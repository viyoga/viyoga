~/how to read this map

Blood runs down, marriages run across. Each panel is one branch, ordered oldest generation at the top. A thin solid line is a parent–child tie; a line with a small diamond is a marriage; a dashed line is a secret, a claim, or a parentage the world has wrong; a dotted line is a bond of oath, mentorship, or chosen family rather than blood.

Names are marked † when the character is dead by chapter 155, and gods and divine beasts carry a small ◇. Faded cards are cross-references to a person whose full entry lives in another panel. Nothing here goes past the end of chapter 155.

the board at chapter 155

The crown

Sol rules in all but name. Lilith is dying, her homunculus lifespan measured in weeks, and only the Phoenix trial and Nirvana may save her.

The conspiracy

Gorfard is broken and demoted; Loki and Leonard hang. Gerald is exiled. The Holy Sword is gone, taken by the Wings of Freedom.

The war

Lupus keeps Wratharis at war. Setsuna, its rightful princess, is in Lustburg — a claimant, a guest, and possibly a royal concubine.

What comes next

In six days the Astral realm opens. Sol must win Isis’s contract, survive the Phoenix court, and decide what to do about Camelia.
