<p>No sign was posted, so Shiwoo retraced his memories while staring at the photographs for a long time.</p>

<p>Then, at some point, the blurred landscapes and scenes rushed back to him with vivid clarity, as if soaked in fresh paint. The building in the photo was the exterior of the karaoke bar he had visited with Mom not long ago. As the memory surfaced, Shiwoo felt a dull, throbbing ache in his head.</p>

<p>While flipping through a few more pages of documents, another commercial building that felt strangely familiar appeared. Shiwoo narrowed his eyes, peering into the photo and scouring his mind once more. Soon, the images hovering in his mind solidified into a precise shape.</p>

<p>That commercial building was the very same one that housed the room cafe they had visited for a group blind date with Sehee Nuna and her friends. Shiwoo confirmed that the owner of that building was also EST Inc.</p>

<p>It felt as if he had been struck in the back of the head; his mind went blank for a moment. Countless spaces he had passed through without a second thought were all intertwined under the name of EST. Reality and memory tangled into a confusing mess, and every familiar place began to feel utterly alien.</p>

<p class="scene-break">· · ·</p>

<p>Passing the file containing the real estate title deeds, documents containing account details and asset valuation reports revealed themselves. Numbers crowded the pages... copies of several bankbooks showing balances in the hundreds of millions of won from just a few months ago. Documents with the sharp logos of securities firms were packed tightly together.</p>

<p>Shiwoo flipped through the papers in order, checking the numbers one by one. Unfamiliar units of currency, strange stock names, and nonchalantly recorded rates of return. Then, Shiwoo's hand stopped in front of one particular valuation report.</p>

<p>The moment he saw the total appraised value written there, Shiwoo gasped involuntarily.</p>

<p>An amount in the hundreds of billions...</p>

<p>That sum was not just a mere number. It was money that could rewrite someone's life several times over.</p>

<p>Company Name: EST Corporate Form: Stock Company (Unlisted) And... Owner: Yu Jin-seong (100% Shareholder)</p>

<p>Shiwoo's eyes widened as he saw Dad's name listed as the owner of EST.</p>

<p>Held Assets: 12 Academy buildings within Seoul and the metropolitan area. 3 Complex officetel buildings. 3 Hospitals and health centers. Other commercial buildings and shares in nationwide franchise businesses.</p>

<p>Given that Dad was in a position to set the rules of the Gathering and manage the participants, Shiwoo thought he would have more than just simple authority, but he never imagined Dad owned the entire company... and a massive one at that, commanding various real estate holdings including academies and hospitals, along with nationwide franchises.</p>

<p>Facing this unbelievable reality, Shiwoo's mind went white for a moment, and his consciousness felt distant. A complex surge of emotions—confusion about the father he thought he knew and a sense of awe—washed over him.</p>

<p class="scene-break">· · ·</p>

<p>Shiwoo continued to examine the documents. What came next was a paper with the title</p>

<p>"Will"</p>

<p>printed at the top. The name of the testator was 'Yu Jin-seong'... It was none other than Dad's will.</p>

<p>At the top of the document, his father's name 'Yu Jin-seong' was written along with his date of birth and address. Looking at the date of preparation, it seemed to have been written long before his father passed away. Below that, details regarding the inherited property were written in dense detail.</p>

<p>The inherited property included all the real estate recorded in the registry Shiwoo had just checked, along with liquid assets such as bank deposits and stocks. Furthermore, the rights as a Master over all the slaves listed in the Slave Ledger were also explicitly stated.</p>

<p>Following that, the section designating the heir was located at the end of the will. The typeface was mechanically clear, but the meaning contained within shook Shiwoo's mind with complexity.</p>

<p>The name written there was not his own, which he had naturally expected to see. In the heir column designated by Dad, a sentence he never anticipated had been written.</p>

<p>'Heir: Among the children sired by the decedent Yu Jin-seong, the youngest child, regardless of gender, is designated as the heir.'</p>

<p>In an instant, the image of the pregnant Sejin Nuna flashed through his mind.</p>

<p>'The youngest child among the children sired by Dad, regardless of gender...'</p>

<p>Currently, the person who fit that description was Sae-a, Sejin Nuna's daughter.</p>

<p>Shiwoo read the sentence over and over as if chewing on the words. But no matter how many times he read it, the content written on the document did not change. With trembling hands, Shiwoo turned to the next page of the file.</p>

<p>The document that followed immediately after made Shiwoo feel suspicious once again. This time, it did not seem to be a document written by Dad. This was because the date of preparation was recorded as being after Dad had passed away.</p>

<p>The title of the document was 'Confirmation of Renunciation of Inheritance Rights.' It was a document containing Seyoung's name and signature...</p>

<p>'I, Yu Seyoung, as a child of the decedent Yu Jin-seong, hereby renounce all inheritance rights.'</p>

<p>And on the back page, a document with the same title written by Sua appeared.</p>

<p>'I, Shin Sua, as a child of the decedent Yu Jin-seong, hereby renounce all inheritance rights.'</p>

<p>Shiwoo dropped his gaze onto the documents with a hollow expression, looking down as if he had lost the thread of his emotions. Come to think of it, if it was a will written by Dad several years ago, it was long before Sejin Nuna became pregnant with Sae-a. At that time, the person who met the condition of the heir was Seyoung. And when Seyoung renounced the inheritance, the next person to meet that condition was Sua.</p>

<p>The fact that Seyoung and Sua wrote the confirmation of renunciation almost immediately after Dad passed away... What on earth was going on? Why did Seyoung and Sua renounce the inheritance? What was Dad thinking? Was there intervention from the Academy? Or was there some undisclosed circumstance?</p>

<p>With this unexpected information and the jumbled chronological order, Shiwoo's mind became as tangled as a knotted ball of thread. He had no choice but to stare blankly at the documents for a long while.</p>

<p class="scene-break">· · ·</p>

<p>Shiwoo closed his eyes and took a deep breath, as if to clear his head. He exhaled a long, quiet breath, pushing away the cluttered thoughts. Then, he silently gripped the corner of the next page and slowly turned it over. That was the only thing Shiwoo could do right now.</p>

<p>What Shiwoo faced on the next page was a bundle of several documents neatly organized within the file. A stack of papers held by a sturdy metal clip, designed to be easily flipped... On the very first page, the title 'Articles of Incorporation of EST Inc.' was clearly printed.</p>

<p>Article 1 (Name and Purpose of the Company) The name of the company shall be 'EST.' This, when combined with the English notation for a 'Stock Company' (Inc.), carries the underlying meaning of 'incest.' The company is established for the purpose of power succession based on the maintenance of incest and bloodlines, and the execution and management of new master-slave contracts between relatives, while also engaging in related businesses and their incidental operations.</p>

<p>From the very first part, where the company's name and purpose were written, a cold and heavy shock surged into Shiwoo's chest. Those Articles of Incorporation seemed to be a document containing the explanation and basic operating rules for the company called EST. The document systematically organized matters regarding the company's stock, regulations and procedures for the general meeting of shareholders and the board of directors, and so on.</p>

<p>...</p>

<p>Article 10 (Number and Composition of Directors) The number of directors shall be nine, and that number shall always be maintained.</p>

<p>Article 11 (Term and Appointment of Directors) The initial directors shall be appointed by the Representative Director at the inaugural general meeting. The term of a director shall be for life unless there is a separate dismissal or resignation. The appointment of a director shall be carried out according to the succession method of the existing director. Specific procedures regarding the succession of directors shall be determined by the Board of Directors.</p>

<p>Article 12 (Convocation and Resolution of the Board of Directors) The Representative Director may convene the Board of Directors when necessary. If a majority of the directors request it, the Representative Director must convene the Board of Directors. Resolutions of the Board of Directors shall be decided by the attendance of a majority of the registered directors and the affirmative vote of a majority of the directors present.</p>

<p>...</p>

<p>Article 20 (Amendment of Articles of Incorporation and Internal Regulations) Amendments to the Articles of Incorporation and internal regulations shall strictly require the attendance of a majority of the registered directors and the affirmative vote of more than two-thirds of the registered directors.</p>

<p>Article 21 (Establishment of a Mediator) A Mediator shall be appointed to facilitate smooth communication between the directors and the company.</p>

<p>Article 22 (Appointment of the Mediator) 1. The Mediator shall be directly designated and appointed by the Representative Director. 2. In the event that the Mediator is incapacitated or the position becomes vacant, a new Mediator must be appointed without delay.</p>

<p>Article 23 (Qualification Requirements for the Mediator) A person who is a direct blood relative of the Representative Director or a shareholder. A person who has no concerns regarding a conflict of interest with the company.</p>

<p>Article 24 (Role of the Mediator) The Mediator performs the role of managing and coordinating the communication of intentions and meeting procedures between the directors and the company.</p>

<p>Article 25 (Mediator's Duty of Confidentiality) The Mediator shall not disclose to the outside or unfairly utilize any information regarding the company and the directors learned in the course of their duties. This duty continues to apply even after the Mediator's term has ended.</p>

<p>The Articles of Incorporation certainly seemed to be a document organizing the basic rules for operating a company. However, strangely, the further he read down the documents, the more it felt as though some hidden discipline was permeating every single clause.</p>
