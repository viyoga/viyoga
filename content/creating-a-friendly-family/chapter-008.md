<p>Sehee Nuna's scolding ended, and we sat at the dining table, eating the ramen Sinbi Nuna had cooked together.</p>
<p>"We all went and got it done today~"</p>
<p>Sinbi Nuna's gentle voice.</p>
<p>Listening to her, it seemed the tattoo Sehee Nuna got was a friendship tattoo, currently popular among the female students at the advanced academy.</p>
<p>Each of them had the English spelling of their favorite person of the opposite sex or a celebrity's name, designed like a picture in the same font, etched onto their thighs.</p>

<p>Sinbi Nuna, using her unique soothing tone that eased tension, explained even things Shiwoo hadn't asked about in detail.</p>
<p>Shiwoo nodded while eating his ramen.</p>
<p>According to Sinbi Nuna's words, it seemed both Sehee Nuna and Sinbi Nuna had someone they liked.</p>
<p>He couldn't tell if they were dating the person whose initial it was, or if they just harbored feelings, but he didn't particularly feel like asking either.</p>
<p>"Want me to show you mine?"</p>
<p>Sinbi Nuna, who was sitting next to him, turned her knees towards Shiwoo and asked.</p>
<p>Shiwoo, eating his ramen, coughed abruptly as if he had choked on Sinbi Nuna's sudden words.</p>
<p>"Cough... gasp... Wh-what did you say?"</p>
<p>"Don't you want to see it? Aren't you curious?"</p>
<p>Sinbi Nuna lightly lifted the top of her skirt with both hands, making a slow, creeping motion upwards.</p>

<p>'Gulp!'</p>

<p>"Your eyes have gotten lewd, haven't they?"</p>

<p>"You really thought I'd show you, didn't you~ Puhahaha!"</p>
<p>Sinbi Nuna's teasing voice and laughter echoed through the house.</p>
<p>Shiwoo was flustered for a moment.</p>
<p>"Your little brother is so cute~ Did you see his face turn red?"</p>
<p>Sinbi Nuna, with a playful expression, said to Sehee Nuna.</p>
<p>Sehee Nuna, sitting across from them, watched the scene silently, as if feeling sorry for him.</p>
<p>"Were you perhaps... expecting it?"</p>
<p>Sinbi Nuna hit the nail on the head, teasing Shiwoo relentlessly.</p>
<p>"Ah, damn it~ No, I wasn't!!!"</p>
<p>Shiwoo, displeased with the situation where even Sinbi Nuna, whom he trusted, was teasing him, replied with annoyance.</p>
<p>"Kkareureureu~"</p>
<p>As if radiating the vitality of a female advanced academy student, Sinbi Nuna held her stomach and laughed for a long time.</p>
<p>While eating ramen, Shiwoo thought,</p>
<p>"Men will keep changing, so you Nunas will definitely regret it later," or</p>
<p>"Well, you could just choose to meet men with the same initials," or</p>
<p>"Or maybe changing the name of the man you're dating is a way?" He was trying to recover his lost dignity and standing by making these half-hearted curses, not quite curses, after being teased.</p>
<p>But each time, thanks to Sinbi Nuna's coaxing and soothing tone like, "Yes, that would work," such attempts didn't have much effect.</p>
<p>"But Sehee Nuna, if Mom finds out about that, won't it be a really big problem?"</p>
<p>This time, Shiwoo asked Sehee Nuna in a genuinely worried tone.</p>
<p>"As long as you keep your mouth shut, there'll be no problem, got it?"</p>
<p>Sehee Nuna was still cold towards Shiwoo.</p>
<p>"Are you not going to the bathhouse or the swimming pool with Mom anymore?"</p>
<p>Shiwoo said to Sehee Nuna, still sounding worried,</p>
<p>"So, I'm telling you, as long as you keep your mouth shut, there won't be any problem!"</p>
<p>Sehee repeated the same words, answering Shiwoo coldly.</p>
<p>"Ah, damn it, do you think I'd tell Mom something like that~?!"</p>
<p>Shiwoo, too, was slowly starting to get annoyed by his Nuna's attitude.</p>
<p>He decided to stop talking to his Nuna now.</p>
<p>It seemed Shiwoo's joke yesterday morning had touched a raw nerve with his Nuna, making her thoroughly angry.</p>
<p>After their father passed away, amidst the family's distress, her breasts had grown, leading her to quit track and field, which she had been diligently pursuing with good results.</p>
<p>Although he had heard it from Sinbi Nuna, if that was the reason, it might have been embarrassing for her to reveal it to her family.</p>
<p>Sehee had never once considered sports as a profession, so she wasn't particularly distressed about quitting the activity itself.</p>
<p>However, the situation where she knew it would cause worry but couldn't openly state the reason was what secretly upset her.</p>
<p>Shiwoo had touched upon that very sensitive point.</p>
<p>Sinbi Nuna, who was watching from the side, perhaps feeling sorry for Shiwoo, started teasing him again.</p>
<p>"Shiwoo, do you have a girlfriend?"</p>
<p>"No, I don't."</p>
<p>Shiwoo answered immediately without much thought.</p>
<p>"Have you ever dated before?"</p>
<p>"No."</p>
<p>"Have you ever kissed anyone?"</p>
<p>"I told you I've never dated, so how could I have kissed anyone!!"</p>
<p>Shiwoo said, feeling embarrassed.</p>
<p>"Can't you kiss someone even if you're not dating?"</p>
<p>"I said no!!"</p>
<p>Shiwoo was a little surprised. To say you could kiss without dating—it wasn't wrong, but shouldn't it usually be between lovers? he thought.</p>
<p>-Fantasy separate, reality separate-</p>
<p>"Are you not interested in things like that?"</p>
<p>Sinbi Nuna's face moved slightly closer to Shiwoo.</p>
<p>"No, it's not that I'm not interested, but it's weird to answer something like that."</p>
<p>Shiwoo, unable to meet her eyes, grew increasingly flustered.</p>
<p>"Want Nuna to teach you how to kiss?"</p>

<p>"...."</p>

<p>"Ah~ You thought about it for a moment, didn't you? Kissing Nuna."</p>

<p>"Oh, stop it!! What are you even saying!!"</p>
<p>"Shiwoo really is cute, I'm telling you! Kkareureuk~"</p>
