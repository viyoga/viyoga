<p>"Yes... when I was carrying you, Shiwoo... that photo taken with Gyuri and the other pregnant women... Gyuri gave it to you, right?"</p>
<p>Mom checked Shiwoo's reaction, her face flushing with a hint of awkwardness as she asked.</p>
<p>"Huh? Ah... yeah... that photo... the one from when the prenatal marriage oath was made with Gyuri before I was born..."</p>
<p>Shiwoo answered, feeling that strange sense of dissonance from back then bubbling up again.</p>
<p>"Most of the women gathered there at that time... each of them served a different master..."</p>
<p>"But the truth is... the father of all the children in those wombs was only one man..."</p>
<p>"That man... your father, Shiwoo..."</p>
<p>Mom spoke while submerged in a bizarre silence and a sense of heightened emotion, as if gauging Shiwoo's response.</p>
<p>"What?"</p>
<p>Shiwoo's mind went blank from the shock. He could only stare dazed at his mother's lips, his speech failing him.</p>
<p>"At that time, the only people who knew exactly what was going on were me, Yu Ye-na, and your father..."</p>
<p>Mom spoke while hanging her head, taking shallow, trembling breaths like someone confessing every single one of their sins.</p>
<p>The pregnant women in that photo included the mothers of the participants who had been called to the police station on the day of the last meeting. If what Mom said was true, then those women had been carrying Shiwoo's half-siblings?</p>
<p>Shiwoo couldn't keep up with the swirling vortex of truth. He blinked his eyes, their focus lost, looking entirely soul-drained.</p>
<p>"Back then, Sunju, who was taking the photo, and Gyuri, who was in the photo with me, probably had no idea..."</p>
<p>"That I was playing such a role in the club..."</p>
<p>"The two of them probably didn't even know such a role existed..."</p>
<p>Mom let out a hollow, dry breath, caught between the misery of passing the weight of a lifelong secret to her son and the liberation of finally laying down that heavy burden.</p>
<p>She had finally confessed to Shiwoo the secret she had carried alone, a secret she had never been able to tell anyone, and the painful past she had intended to keep buried in her heart had now become her son's burden.</p>
<p>In the midst of his confusion, Shiwoo recalled the slave contract for Aunt Gyuri that he had first received from Chul-jin. And the information on the lessee written in the lease agreement attached behind it—his father's name...</p>
<p>"Wait a minute, then... what about the lease agreement and the lessee information attached to Gyuri's slave contract?"</p>
<p>"The one Chul-jin's dad kept hidden in his old briefcase... I saw it clearly! No, with the photo Chul-jin sent me..."</p>
<p>"My dad's name was written right there on the slave lease contract, and it even said 'twins' in the remarks column."</p>
<p>"If Gyuri was leased to my dad for a long time, there's no way she didn't know she was pregnant with his child!"</p>
<p>Shiwoo asked, pressing her as if he couldn't suppress the chaos in his mind. Mom took a deep sigh and looked at Shiwoo with a gaze where pity and coldness were intricately intertwined.</p>
<p>"Depending on the client's request or circumstances, there were cases where long-term leases or exchanges of slaves were handled directly..."</p>
<p>"For example, if an infertile client in a master-slave relationship temporarily delegated ownership of their slave to another master for the purpose of pregnancy..."</p>
<p>"It's true that before you were born, Shiwoo, Gyuri came to our house and lived with us for a while under the pretense of a lease."</p>
<p>"The path was different, but Gyuri was definitely the slave of a client who fell into that category..."</p>
<p>"But in the case of leased slaves, they were meant to be held by not just the lessee, but by countless men during that lease period..."</p>
<p>"The main purpose of the lease itself was pregnancy... and since your father took the lease to act as a proxy... it's not like your father was someone who couldn't enjoy that, either..."</p>
<p>"Just like in the photo Gyuri gave you... we were holding meetings in various places long before we ever got pregnant."</p>
<p>"Sunju and I were protected because we were your father's property... but Gyuri had already been having relationships with various men for the sake of pregnancy."</p>
<p>"So, even if she was leased to your father and they slept together, she couldn't have been certain that the child in her womb was his."</p>
<p>"Besides, slave contracts are designed so the parties involved can't check them again once they're written..."</p>
<p>"It's the same whether it's the master's contract or the slave's. I heard they're all collected and strictly managed by the 'Meeting' side..."</p>
<p>"And slaves, in particular, aren't given those kinds of rights... All rights are extinguished the moment the contract is signed..."</p>
<p>"If that contract you saw made its way back into the hands of Gyuri or Chul-jin's father, it can only mean someone exposed it with a specific purpose in mind."</p>
<p>"And very recently, at that. Deliberately. Because the Meeting would have never allowed such a thing while your father was alive..."</p>
<p>Mom's voice was now rustling like a fallen leaf stripped of all moisture, piercing the silence.</p>
<p>"Maybe... maybe they all know by now... Gyuri... Sunju... haaa..."</p>
<p>"But at the time, they definitely didn't know. That I was doing those kinds of things..."</p>
<p>Mom continued with a low mutter that reached no one, her voice fading into a whisper.</p>
<p class="scene-break">· · ·</p>
<p>"So Mom was the only one who knew everything back then? Even the fact that Chul-jin was my father's blood...?"</p>
<p>"And that Seul-gi and Chul-jin were twin siblings...?"</p>
<p>"Then that means you've been acting like you didn't know anything in front of Gyuri all this time, even though you knew which family Seul-gi was adopted into?"</p>
<p>Shiwoo poured out questions, trying to dig through the overwhelming truth. But he soon bit his lip, as if gauging the weight of the years Mom had to endure alone.</p>
<p>"But in Gyuri's case, the client who bought her was her husband in the first place, so why was Chul-jin the only one left while Seul-gi was sent somewhere else?"</p>
<p>Shiwoo asked again in a low voice, searching for the end of the tangled thread as he watched Mom's expression.</p>
<p>"It didn't matter who the client was... the Meeting's rules applied equally to all participants..."</p>
<p>Mom answered with a voice full of resignation, as if she already had all the answers.</p>
<p>"All children born within the rituals or contracts of the Meeting become the property of the Meeting..."</p>
<p>"From the beginning, the Meeting's protocol only allowed for one child per client..."</p>
<p>"But as fate would have it, Gyuri happened to get pregnant with twins during that time..."</p>
<p>She buried her face in Shiwoo's chest with difficulty, as if reciting a sorrowful confession that had been a lump in her heart for years.</p>
<p>"The most basic rule of the Meeting is not to interfere in the affairs of other participants or slaves..."</p>
<p>"And issues regarding adopted children were particularly sensitive matters..."</p>
<p>"Furthermore, at that time, Seul-gi and the other girls who were left behind weren't recorded as normal adoptions..."</p>
<p>Mom continued, her voice barely squeezed out through her thin, trembling breaths.</p>
<p>"Seul-gi and the other girls who were left behind...? They weren't recorded as normal adoptions? What does that mean?"</p>
<p>Shiwoo asked again in a low voice, his brow furrowing as if he couldn't understand Mom's words.</p>
<p>"Usually, clients have a strong tendency to prefer boys."</p>
<p>"Most of the clients who joined the Meeting were people who got interested through clinics or counseling..."</p>
<p>"So the families for the boys were decided in advance, but in the case of the girls who were left behind, once the client quotas were filled, ownership reverted to the Meeting..."</p>
<p>"I heard that the Meeting runs orphanages or foster facilities, but..."</p>
<p>"Telling Gyuri anything wouldn't have solved or changed a single thing..."</p>
<p>Mom spoke while keeping her head bowed deep into Shiwoo's embrace, as if crushed by layers of accumulated guilt.</p>
<p>"When you do that kind of work, you start to see the relationships between people I originally knew and others..."</p>
<p>"I mean the countless relationships being formed secretly in places I've never seen or heard of..."</p>
<p>"Eventually, you reach a point where you can't even tell what you can say to who anymore..."</p>
<p>"So I had no choice but to pretend I didn't know. In front of Gyuri, in front of Sunju... and in front of you children too..."</p>
<p>Mom let out a silent sob, buried in Shiwoo's chest. Shiwoo squeezed the hand resting on her breast firmly, as if trying to soothe her anxiety.</p>
<p class="scene-break">· · ·</p>
<p>"Then what was the promise Mom broke? The one Mom was supposed to trade with Gyuri!"</p>
<p>"The day I went to Gyuri's house and we had sex, she told me that..."</p>
<p>"She said Mom would have nothing to say even if she entered an extramarital affair with me..."</p>
<p>"It seemed like she resented Mom quite a bit?"</p>
<p>Shiwoo asked her in a low, murmuring voice, as if trying to resolve the last remaining curiosity.</p>
<p>"Haaaaaaaa...."</p>
<p>Mom let out a long breath mixed with a groan, as if forcibly pulling out a thorn embedded deep in her heart.</p>
<p>"Gyuri probably resents me for quitting as a slave..."</p>
<p>"Because she probably felt like I vainly blew the chance for her to see her daughter again..."</p>
<p>Mom spoke with her lips trembling, as if reliving the despair of her friend, Aunt Gyuri.</p>
