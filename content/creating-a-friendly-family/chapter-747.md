<p>"Was it really okay up to that point? Enough that you could just brush it off by saying you were troubled?"</p>

<p>Shiwoo continues to press her with questions, as if determined to drag her true feelings into the light.</p>

<p>Mom remains silent under the weight of his persistent interrogation, her face contorting in a pained, troubled grimace.</p>

<p>"Did you really never have an affair, Mom? If I were you, I would've felt so wronged in that situation that I'd have cheated right back~ Hehe."</p>

<p>Shiwoo speaks with a playful, teasing lilt, but his words dig deep, unearthing the silence she is trying to maintain.</p>

<p>"Why would I ever have an affair?! And is that something you should be saying to your mother right now? My goodness!"</p>

<p>Mom draws a firm line, her expression one of utter disbelief as she lets out a sharp, exasperated sigh.</p>

<p>She pours more wine into her empty glass and stares at it for a moment. Then, as if a foul, unpleasant memory had suddenly clawed its way to the surface of her mind, she brings the glass to her lips and drinks it down, beol-keok, beol-keok .</p>

<p>"Haaaaaaa~"</p>

<p>After draining the glass, she takes a long, deep breath to steady herself and sets the wine glass back down on the table.</p>

<p>"That bitch Seonju needs to be punished a lot more for what she did to me! Even after what she went through with you this morning, I bet she still hasn't come to her senses."</p>

<p>As she speaks, she bites her lower lip as if swallowing her rage, her fingertips tapping a rapid, nervous rhythm against the edge of the table.</p>

<p>"What?"</p>

<p>Shiwoo blinks, watching her as if he's momentarily taken aback by the sheer intensity of her reaction.</p>

<p>Even though he had been joking, seeing his mother casually spit out the word 'bitch' while referring to Aunt Seonju felt strange and unfamiliar.</p>

<p>At the same time, seeing her deeply hidden emotions suddenly burst to the surface made Shiwoo's chest tickle with an inexplicable sensation.</p>

<p>"Back then and even now, that Seonju girl knew exactly what I was going through, yet she was the only one having a grand old time!"</p>

<p>Mom continues to vent, her voice a mix of irritation and whining, though it's unclear if she's talking to Shiwoo or just to herself.</p>

<p>"How does she always manage to pick the exact things that will trouble me and act so spitefully?!"</p>

<p>Her voice, uncharacteristic of her usual self, continues to flow out in short, petulant bursts. There is a strange trembling and instability woven into the end of every sentence.</p>

<p>"Why? Did something happen with Seonju in the past?"</p>

<p>Shiwoo asks with a satisfied smirk, his curiosity clearly piqued.</p>

<p>"It's not that 'something' happened—she's always been like that! Even when your Dad was alive, even though he never gave her a specific order, she'd send the kids to pull strange pranks on me without any warning..."</p>

<p>"Do you think I'm some kind of lesbian or bisexual? Do you think I was doing that because I enjoyed it? And you too, Shiwoo! I'll let it slide for Sejin Nuna since she was still in the middle of doing that stuff back then, but you even tainted Sehee and Seyoung and ended up making them do that kind of play!"</p>

<p>The wine seems to have hit her quickly, as she pours out a disorganized stream of complaints, her resentment directed at no one in particular.</p>

<p>"Even when I was trying to end that kind of relationship with your Dad, she'd make it so obvious that the two of them were meeting behind my back! She turned my insides out just to taunt me...!"</p>

<p>"Even now, she knows perfectly well that I'm uncomfortable having sex with you, so she's egging you on just to make things even more difficult for me!"</p>

<p>Mom finally goes as far as to utter the word 'sex' right in front of Shiwoo, vomiting out all the pent-up resentment and complex emotions she had kept buried regarding Aunt Seonju.</p>

<p>"I told her to be so careful, but then she went and got pregnant with Sua!"</p>

<p>"Fine, if she's pregnant, she has to give birth, but why is she so desperate to drag her own daughter into these things?!"</p>

<p>"And if you look closely, Sua might stay quiet and act reserved, but she's a total fox just like her mother!"</p>

<p>"She pretends to greet me shyly while hugging me, but she's touching me here and there! I caught her and scolded her a few times, so she doesn't do it anymore, but I'm certain Seonju was the one who told her to do it."</p>

<p>Mom continues to pour out her grievances, retracing the bitter memories intertwined with Aunt Seonju.</p>

<p>"Hearing you talk like that, you must have suffered a lot because of Seonju without me knowing~"</p>

<p>"That's why I punished her this morning! I told you, I'm not going to let Seonju mess with you anymore!"</p>

<p>"That's not enough! Do you think I didn't try anything on my own to fix your Dad and Seonju's habits?"</p>

<p>Mom is now gesturing with her arms, appealing to him with a sense of frustration and injustice.</p>

<p>"Alright, alright! I swear, from now on, if it even looks like Seonju is treating you poorly, I won't leave her alone!"</p>

<p>"I'll put a blindfold and handcuffs on Sua and lock her in a dog cage, and even if I have to starve her for days, I'll fix Seonju's habit somehow. So don't worry anymore!"</p>

<p>Shiwoo speaks with a serious expression, looking directly at his mother.</p>

<p>Under Shiwoo's piercing gaze, Mom suddenly stiffens, um-jjil , as if her breath has been caught in her throat.</p>

<p>"N-not... that far..."</p>

<p>Only then does she seem to realize who she is talking to. Her face flushes red as she avoids his gaze and straightens her posture.</p>

<p>Feeling embarrassed at having exposed her weaknesses so nakedly in front of her son, she clears her throat and nervously strokes her cheek.</p>

<p>Just then, as if perfectly timed, the prepared dishes begin to arrive.</p>

<p>"I'll serve the herb scallop salad first~"</p>

<p>"Shall we eat then?"</p>

<p>"Yeah..."</p>

<p>Mom is still staring into space with a flushed face, but she responds to Shiwoo's words by quietly picking up her fork and beginning to eat the scallop salad.</p>

<p>Shiwoo dips a scallop into the sauce pooled at the bottom of the plate and pops it into his mouth.</p>

<p>The scent of herbs brushes past his nose, and soon the tenderly sliced scallop slowly melts away on his tongue.</p>

<p>Behind the faint acidity of the marinade, the characteristic sweetness of the scallop's juices spreads, and the sauce quietly holds onto that lingering aftertaste.</p>

<p>"I thought you heard everything from Seonju... Haaaa..."</p>

<p>"I don't know! I'm still not even used to having this kind of conversation while eating with you! How would I know?!"</p>
