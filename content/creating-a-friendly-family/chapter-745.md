<p>"Yeah. It's pretty delicious here! It's served as a course meal, so you don't even have to pick the menu; it just comes out on its own. We just have to order the drinks separately..."</p>

<p>Shiwoo followed his mother's lead, pretending as if nothing were wrong while explaining the style of the restaurant he had recommended.</p>

<p>"Isn't a place like this expensive? If it's a restaurant course meal, the price is going to be quite high, isn't it?"</p>

<p>Mom peered through the glass display case next to the entrance, looking into the restaurant with a worried expression regarding the cost.</p>

<p>"Considering it includes everything from the course meal to the seafood, I don't think it's as expensive as you'd think. Was it around 60,000 or 70,000 won per person? So about 140,000 won for the two of us?"</p>

<p>Shiwoo spoke to his mother while scanning the menu board, acting as if it were no big deal, as if he were accustomed to such things.</p>

<p>"What?! 140,000 won for the two of us? That's incredibly expensive! With that much money, I could buy groceries and cook seafood dishes for our whole family for a week. Do you come to places like this often with your friends? Where does an academy student get that kind of money...!"</p>

<p>As if shocked by the price, Mom began to pour out a string of words, nagging Shiwoo.</p>

<p>"At this price, of course I can't come with friends. An academy student barely has enough money for the PC bang; why would they come here?!"</p>

<p>Shiwoo shrugged his shoulders and retorted as if he were used to his mother's nagging tone, playing along.</p>

<p>"I came here with Dad before. I've also been here a few times with Seonju-imo..."</p>

<p>Shiwoo brought up the stories of Dad and Aunt Seonju as if tossing out a casual memory, speaking as if revealing his past experiences of visiting the restaurant.</p>

<p>"Long before all this happened... back when I didn't know anything..."</p>

<p>He then added, as if leaving room for an implication regarding the facts he and his mother shared.</p>

<p>Mom tightly shut her mouth as if it were uncomfortable to hear Dad's name come out of Shiwoo's mouth.</p>

<p>Shiwoo watched his mother's reaction—her falling silent at the mention of Dad—with interest...</p>

<p>"Well... shall we go in?"</p>

<p>Shiwoo opened the restaurant door and gestured as if guiding his mother inside.</p>

<p>Mom entered the restaurant through the door Shiwoo held open, her head bowed low.</p>

<p>Shiwoo soon followed behind his mother into the restaurant.</p>

<p class="scene-break">· · ·</p>

<p>"Welcome. Is it for two? Do you happen to have a reservation?"</p>

<p>As they stepped inside, a female staff member wearing a neat navy shirt and a tidy apron stopped in her tracks and spoke to them.</p>

<p>A single button was left loosely undone so she didn't look stifled, yet her overall appearance was impeccable and orderly.</p>

<p>Her lower half was clad in dark slacks; every time she took a step, her legs crossed smoothly without a single rustle of fabric.</p>

<p>A thin silver nametag attached to her clothing above her breast and a brooch engraved with the restaurant's logo shone faintly, adding to her clean impression.</p>

<p>Her voice was a calm, steady tone, neither high nor low.</p>

<p>The overall atmosphere felt less like a restaurant employee and more like the composed presence of a luxury hotel concierge...</p>

<p>"Ah, yes, two people. We didn't make a reservation, but we can eat, right?"</p>

<p>Shiwoo spoke with a smile, using a polite tone and expression.</p>

<p>"Yes, dining is possible. Then, would you like to come this way?"</p>

<p>She nodded slightly and led Shiwoo and Mom toward a table in the corner.</p>

<p>Shiwoo took a look around the surroundings and slowly moved his steps along with his mother in the direction she guided them.</p>

<p>Perhaps because it was a little past lunchtime, the restaurant wasn't very crowded.</p>

<p>Customers sat in sparse groups at each table, quietly continuing their conversations.</p>

<p>The sound of glass dishes clinking against each other echoed faintly, mingling with the sound of voices.</p>

<p>From the kitchen area, the thick scent of cooking seafood wafted out, savory and warming the air inside the restaurant.</p>

<p>The seat the mother and son were guided to was a window seat in the corner opposite the entrance.</p>

<p>It was a place where the interior of the department store was faintly visible through the glass coated with a deep amber film, like a landscape in the fog...</p>

<p>It was appropriately distanced from the surrounding tables, and partitions of a height that prevented eyes from meeting were placed between the tables.</p>

<p>It seemed like a place where he could have an intimate conversation with his mother without being disturbed by others.</p>

<p>A place where they didn't look like a mother and son...</p>

<p>Was it the staff member's consideration for a suspicious couple exuding the atmosphere of an illicit affair?</p>

<p>Shiwoo sat down facing his mother in the seat guided by the staff member.</p>

<p>The female staff member who guided Mom and Shiwoo to their seats checked both of their faces once with a soft gaze.</p>

<p>"If you wait just a moment, I will bring you drinks immediately and help you with the menu guide"</p>

<p>Then, she bowed her head forward in a polite greeting and turned back toward the hall.</p>

<p class="scene-break">· · ·</p>

<p>"Come to think of it, Mom, you don't seem to talk about Dad much in front of us. Why is that?"</p>

<p>Shiwoo spoke while quietly watching the staff member's back as she returned to the counter, then turning his gaze to his mother.</p>

<p>"What do you mean, why? What kind of stories would I tell you? It's not like we don't know each other... Your father is just your father..."</p>

<p>Mom replied while looking out the blurry window.</p>

<p>She was trying to calmly brush off Shiwoo's question, but her expression seemed to hold layers of accumulated memories and longing.</p>

<p>"Since you gave birth to four children, it seems like your relationship as a couple or the bond between you was good..."</p>

<p>"There must be something, right? Stories worth telling us... For example, your first threesome..."</p>

<p>Shiwoo spoke with a mischievous smile on his lips, observing his mother's reaction with a face full of curiosity.</p>

<p>Mom turned her head as if startled by Shiwoo's question and hurriedly scanned the surroundings.</p>

<p>After confirming that no one was listening, she shut her mouth tightly as if dissatisfied and let out a large breath through her nose.</p>

<p>"But thinking about it, wasn't Dad having sex with Seonju all that time back then?"</p>

<p>"Seeing as he brought me here separately, I wonder if this was also a place where those two went on dates?"</p>

<p>"Since you didn't know, maybe the two of them were having a secret affair? Aside from the sex, I mean..."</p>

<p>Shiwoo continued to speak as if teasing his mother.</p>

<p>"How would I know that? If they did it, they did it!"</p>

<p>Mom reacted as if flared up by Shiwoo's continuous teasing.</p>

<p>It seemed that Mom didn't try to get deeply involved or know about the deep relationships between Dad and the other slaves.</p>

<p>"Then... I will help guide you through the course meal menu"</p>

<p>"The meal is prepared focusing on seasonal ingredients we have carefully selected, and will be provided in order according to the pre-arranged course sequence."</p>
