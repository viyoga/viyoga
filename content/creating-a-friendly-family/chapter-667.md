<p>Article 26 (Dismissal of the Mediator) In the event that the Mediator falls under any of the following categories, the Company and the Board of Directors may dismiss the Mediator. 1. Failure to perform duties faithfully. 2. Violation of the obligation to maintain confidentiality. 3. When it is judged that neutrality has been significantly compromised.</p>

<p>Article 27 (Neutrality of the Mediator) The Mediator must perform their duties from a neutral standpoint to ensure they are not biased toward either the Directors or the Company and must not represent the will of any specific Director.</p>

<p>Article 28 (Maintenance of Identity Secrecy and Communication) The identities of the Representative Director and the Directors shall be kept secret, and all opinions or intentions of the Directors can only be conveyed to the Company through the Mediator.</p>

<p>Article 29 (Acting Authority in Case of Accident or Vacancy of the Representative Director) In the event that the Representative Director is incapacitated or the position becomes vacant, a successor previously designated by the Representative Director shall act on their behalf. The Representative Director may designate a lineal descendant, a lineal ascendant, or another suitable person as the successor, which shall take effect upon the Representative Director's absence. If no successor is designated or if there are reasons making them unsuitable to exercise authority, the Mediator shall be delegated the authority to temporarily perform the duties of the Representative Director.</p>

<p>The temporary authority of the Mediator shall last until a successor Representative Director is appointed, and the appointment of the Representative Director shall follow the resolution of the Board of Directors. The Mediator must obtain prior approval from the Board of Directors regarding major management matters.</p>

<p>Article 30 (Acting Authority in Case of Accident or Vacancy of a Director) In the event that a Director is incapacitated or the position becomes vacant, the Representative Director may appoint a person to act on their behalf from among the said Director's lineal ascendants, lineal descendants, spouse, or a person previously designated. In this case, after receiving approval from the Representative Director, the authority as the acting deputy is finalized through a resolution of the Board of Directors. The acting deputy may temporarily exercise the authority of the Director until a successor Director is appointed.</p>

<p>The Representative Director shall take necessary measures to finalize the Director's authority and status of the acting deputy or to appoint another suitable person.</p>

<p>Shiwoo rubbed his eyes as he read through all the articles of the bylaws, which stretched out in a long, dizzying sequence. Looking at the complex and abstruse content made his mind go distant, but he calmed his heart and struggled to understand it somehow. However, upon seeing the content that followed immediately after, cold sweat began to soak into Shiwoo's back.</p>

<p>Article 31 (External Use of Titles) Hereafter, externally, the Mediator shall be commonly referred to as the 'Administrator', the Director as the 'Sponsor', and the Representative Director as the 'Person in Charge'. General clients of the contract, who do not hold ownership over the Company's meetings or public property, shall be referred to as 'Participants'.</p>

<p>In an instant, Shiwoo's pupils shook unsteadily. The terms 'Administrator', 'Sponsor', and 'Participant' were names he had certainly heard somewhere before. The strangely familiar words overlapped as if clicking into place with uncomfortable fragments within his memory. Shiwoo felt as if adrenaline were surging through every corner of his body. His heart began to beat two or three paces faster than usual. Matching the thumping rhythm of his heart, Shiwoo hurriedly moved his eyes to scan the bylaws and began flipping through the remaining pages.</p>

<p>While turning several pages like that, a page with an unfamiliar feel, made of pink A4 paper, suddenly caught his eye.</p>

<p>'Private Supplementary Provisions'</p>

<p>A bold and clear title was stamped at the top of the paper, and beneath it, a heavy yet resolute sentence was written.</p>

<p>'This chapter is not included in general registration documents and is sealed and stored separately according to internal company regulations.'</p>

<p>Shiwoo held his breath for a moment before that sentence. It was a private document hidden so that just anyone couldn't see it, but it was clearly a document stored so that someone would definitely be able to see it. With trembling fingertips, Shiwoo carefully read down, tracing the letters written in the document one by one.</p>

<p>Article 1 (Title and Significance of the Slave Contract) A 'Slave Contract' refers to a contractual relationship regarding a status transition established by the designated person voluntarily surrendering their subjective rights through a vow and accepting subordination under the authority of the contracting party. This contract is distinguished from labor contracts or service contracts and may include unilateral hierarchical relationships such as ownership status and the right to command. Furthermore, this contract is interpreted as a ritualistic and symbolic agreement that transcends simple legal consensus, where the designated person transfers their identity and proclaims their will to submit.</p>

<p>Article 2 (Types of Slave Contracts) Slave contracts are classified into 'Exclusive Slaves' and 'Public Slaves' according to their affiliation and the scope of usage authority. An 'Exclusive Slave' refers to a general form of slave contract where the slave is exclusively attributed to a specific individual contractor, signifying a one-on-one subordinate relationship where they follow only the commands and control of said contractor without interference from third parties. A 'Public Slave' refers to a person registered as the collective asset of the entire organization according to the approval of the Representative Director or the Board of Directors, and all members may use or command them under the control of the Board of Directors.</p>

<p>However, the scope of use and command may be restricted according to internal regulations or the judgment of the Administrator.</p>

<p>Article 3 (Requirements for the Establishment of a Slave Contract) An 'Exclusive Slave' is established by drafting a slave contract according to voluntary agreement between the parties, and the relationship between the said contract and the parties takes effect after undergoing the Company's notarization procedures. A 'Public Slave' can be registered by 'designating them as a sacrifice' from among those who have entered into an 'Exclusive Slave' contract, based on conditions of exchange in the contract, specific reasons, or punitive measures.</p>

<p>A person whose status has been transitioned to a 'Public Slave' has the obligation to temporarily take the 'Administrator' as their Master and obey their authority and commands.</p>

<p>Article 4 (Management of Slaves and Slave Contracts) When a slave contract is concluded, two copies of the contract with identical content are drafted. One copy is held by the contracting party designated as the 'Master', and the remaining copy is filed and stored by the Company in the form of an official ledger. The ledger is stored in a separate secure area under the responsibility of the Representative Director, and unauthorized inspection is prohibited. The slave themselves does not possess the original contract and may only inspect it under the approval of the Representative Director when necessary.</p>

<p>Forgery, loss, or external leakage of contracts and ledger records shall be considered a grave violation, and the handling thereof shall follow the decision of the Board of Directors.</p>

<p>Article 5 (Obligations of the Slave and Scope of Submission) Exclusive Slaves and Public Slaves must unconditionally submit to all commands of the Master from the moment of contract and designation. The scope of submission includes the entirety of physical, mental, and social acts and shall not exceed the limits separately specified in the contract. Failure to follow commands, refusal, or acts of interference may be grounds for punitive measures. In addition to the Administrator, Public Slaves must also obey the commands of authorized users, and the scope of submission follows the judgment of the Administrator.</p>

<p>Article 6 (Authority and Role of the Master) The Master has absolute right of direction over the Exclusive Slave within the scope of the contract, and those commands must be implemented immediately. The Master may issue physical, mental, and social commands to the slave, and the content thereof cannot exceed the scope specified in the contract. In the case of a Public Slave, the Administrator acts on behalf of the Master's authority and, if necessary, conducts reporting and consultation with the previous Master.</p>

<p>Article 7 (Exchange and Lease of Slaves) An 'Exclusive Slave' may be exchanged with another Master under certain conditions upon agreement between Masters and approval from the Company. In this case, the reason and conditions for the exchange are documented and attached to the contract as a supplementary document. A 'Public Slave' may be leased to a specific Master or institution for a specific period according to the Administrator's judgment, and the lease period and conditions must be clearly stated.</p>

<p>The leased slave must obey the commands of the lessee during the lease period, and the Administrator periodically checks for pregnancy, childbirth, physical changes, or abnormalities during that process. Exchange and leasing acts are ultimately approved by the Company to prevent abuse for commercial profit, and in case of violation, contract annulment or punitive measures may be implemented.</p>

<p>Article 8 (Pregnancy and Childbirth of Slaves) If an Exclusive Slave or Public Slave becomes pregnant, that fact must be immediately reported to the Administrator, and the Administrator must record this and notify the Master and the Company. If a slave's pregnancy is confirmed, labor reduction or a certain level of protective measures may be provided for a certain period before and after childbirth, which follows the Administrator's judgment or the contract. The status of the child born is determined according to the contract conditions, the Master's request, and the Company's policy; unless there are special regulations, a female child born is registered as a 'Preliminary Slave'.</p>

<p>Deliberate concealment of the fact of pregnancy may be grounds for punishment, and the Master and Administrator have the authority to investigate and dispose of the matter. Matters related to the slave's childbirth must be handled with attention to medical and psychological stability, and the Administrator maintains those records privately at a medical institution linked with the Company.</p>

None of those were words that had their feet planted in reality. And yet, the shock and the realism of the fact that such a document actually 'existed' and that such words were being thoroughly 'managed' seemed to seep vividly into the very marrow of Shiwoo's bones.</p>
