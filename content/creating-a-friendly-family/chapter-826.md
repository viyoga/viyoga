<p>"We recognized each other's faces... and though we pretended not to, we could immediately tell that something was terribly wrong..."</p>
<p>Kang Haeun's voice was trembling faintly.</p>
<p>"At first, I thought our secrets regarding the gathering had leaked, and the investigative authorities were looking into those involved... I was wondering what we should do."</p>
<p>"But... before long, I realized the situation was flowing in a completely different direction."</p>
<p>Kang Haeun calmly retraced her experiences, as if untangling the threads of her memory one by one.</p>
<p>"You realized right away that the secrets of the gathering hadn't leaked? How on earth~?"</p>
<p>Shin Yuna asked in a low, curious tone, showing her interest.</p>
<p>"Be... because inside the police station... there was a scent drifting in the air that I had definitely smelled before..."</p>
<p>Kang Haeun swallowed hard, her throat burning, as she spoke cautiously.</p>
<p>"A scent? What kind of scent are you talking about? Where did you smell it? When, and from whom?"</p>
<p>Shin Yuna pushed the camera lens right up to her face, asking back with keen interest.</p>
<p>Her voice carried a relentless tone, as if she already held the entire truth in the palm of her hand but wanted to confirm it once more through the other woman's own mouth.</p>
<p>"It was the scent of the perfume worn by my daughter's biological father... I mean... the man who impregnated me during that blackout ritual..."</p>
<p>"The moment I smelled it, I knew. It was far too distinct to be dismissed as a mere coincidence."</p>
<p>Kang Haeun panted heavily, her voice trembling as she confessed how and why she had become so certain.</p>
<p>"How do you know it was him who impregnated you? It was an orgy, wasn't it? It must have been a ritual where you mingled your bodies with countless people in a dark place where you couldn't even see each other's faces."</p>
<p>Shin Yuna pried relentlessly, her tone carrying a cynical sneer as she brought up the chaotic scene as if she had witnessed it herself.</p>
<p>"I-it was called an orgy, but he was the only one I had sex with that day... He relentlessly pushed me over and over again... and in the end, he finished with a-a creampie inside me..."</p>
<p>"And right after having sex with him, without even a moment to catch my breath, I was guided away, almost dragged, to another place by someone... Haa..."</p>
<p>Kang Haeun answered as if calmly reflecting on the bizarre certainty that bound her, even as she gasped for air.</p>
<p>"If it was another place, where~? Are you saying it wasn't the place where the ritual was held?"</p>
<p>Shin Yuna asked, her voice betraying an even stronger curiosity, as if she had uncovered a new point of interest.</p>
<p>The camera was now capturing a close-up of Kang Haeun's trembling lips and her faintly shaking eyes.</p>
<p>"I... I don't know exactly. Like a theater stage, incomprehensible dialogue and bizarre moans were echoing from all over..."</p>
<p>"I think I wandered around, changing locations several times in the pitch-black darkness."</p>
<p>"I feel like I went up and down the stairs a few times too, but I was so out of it... I don't remember clearly..."</p>
<p>"The place I finally arrived at was somewhere with a light so faint that you could barely make out any shapes."</p>
<p>"A few women who seemed to be performing the ritual had already arrived and gathered there."</p>
<p>"I couldn't recognize their faces, but I could instinctively sense that all of them had probably been held by the same man."</p>
<p>"Because that same distinct perfume scent from him was faintly drifting from their skin as well."</p>
<p>"The woman who led me there was also completely naked, just like us."</p>
<p>"Seeing how skillfully she guided us through the dark, she seemed to be someone who knew the system and internal structure of the gathering very well."</p>
<p>"From then on, until the ritual ended, we had to take turns holding and caressing the woman she paired us up with."</p>
<p>"Even though I didn't have those kinds of preferences, I felt like I had to do it to protect myself in that place."</p>
<p>Her eyelashes trembled as she confessed her shameful memories of the past.</p>
<p>"I only found out by chance later on, but the women gathered in that same place back then were the participants of the fetus exchange group that was formed afterward."</p>
<p>"There was a rule forbidding us from speaking about what happened that day, so we couldn't talk to each other in detail about what went on during the blackout ritual."</p>
<p>"But not just me—simply by looking into each other's eyes and reading the atmosphere, everyone must have instinctively known we shared the same fate. Haaaa..."</p>
<p>She let out a long, held-in breath, spinning her tale as if barely piecing together the fragments of sensation dredged up from an unseen abyss.</p>
<p>"So you all already knew~? That you were carrying the child of the same man..."</p>
<p>Shin Yuna asked slyly, raising her tone to a lighter pitch as if probing a secret crack.</p>
<p>The relentless gaze of the camera seemed to wander slowly over Kang Haeun's face, intent on exposing every single trace of her agitation.</p>
<p>"Yes... probably..."</p>
<p>A dark shadow hung over Kang Haeun's trailing response.</p>
<p>"But you said there was a rule forbidding you from speaking about it?"</p>
<p>Shin Yuna's voice coming through the monitor screen carried a relaxed yet chilling tone, like a gambler who had just grasped a winning hand.</p>
<p>"Pardon? Yes..."</p>
<p>Kang Haeun shrank back awkwardly as she answered.</p>
<p>"So you've gone ahead and broken that rule by spilling everything to me~?"</p>
<p>Shin Yuna murmured softly, finding it amusing.</p>
<p>"...I-I'm sorry..."</p>
<p>Kang Haeun apologized, swallowing a heavy breath as if she had just realized the mistake she had made.</p>
<p>"There's no need to apologize now~ After all, isn't that why you came to find me?..."</p>
<p>"Besides, I'll make sure you pay the price for it later~! Heheh, humph!"</p>
<p>The low humming coming from off-camera mixed with the faint static of the speaker, distorting strangely.</p>
<p>A cruel vitality surged in her voice, as if she were thoroughly enjoying the game of cornering and shaking her target like a cat with a toy mouse in a dead end.</p>
<p>"Anyway, so that's how you came to vividly remember the scent of the perfume worn by the man who impregnated you all, right?"</p>
<p>"And that exact same scent was drifting inside the police station... and from a boy you'd never seen before, who looked about your son's age..."</p>
<p>Shin Yuna continued her questioning, shaping the scattered facts and truth while relentlessly capturing her subject's reactions through the camera lens.</p>
<p>"Huu... yes..."</p>
<p>As Shin Yuna's relentless barrage of questions poured down like a heavy punishment, trying to wrap up the situation, Kang Haeun barely nodded in resignation and answered.</p>
<p>"So what kind of change did that trigger in your hearts? What does it mean to you that the scent of the man who impregnated you during that blackout ritual is drifting from another man?"</p>
<p>Shin Yuna pried relentlessly, like someone determined not to miss a single one of Kang Haeun's expressions, digging out the emotional reality hidden beneath the truth.</p>
<p>Kang Haeun parted her lips hesitantly several times, unable to easily find the words.</p>
<p>As if the moment she let the truth escape her mouth, the threads of fate would spin in an irreversible direction...</p>
<p>After hesitating for a long time, she finally relaxed her expression like someone who had given up, and spoke.</p>
<p>"I thought... we had been found out. That they used the children... to gather us in one place... and send us a message..."</p>
<p>Kang Haeun's voice trembled precariously, as if it might break at any moment.</p>
<p>"What were you found out about?"</p>
<p>Shin Yuna asked immediately, firing the question like a shot.</p>
<p>"The fact that we reported the children conceived through the ritual as miscarriages, and smuggled them elsewhere to escape the gathering's eyes..."</p>
<p>An anxiety, as if rising to the surface, leaked through her trembling breath.</p>
<p>"What kind of message?"</p>
<p>Shin Yuna asked back coldly, her voice unwavering, as if refusing to give her any room to escape.</p>
<p>"A warning message that the gathering has already figured out all the facts regarding that matter..."</p>
<p>Kang Haeun swallowed with difficulty, her voice trailing off as she answered.</p>
<p>A silence fell, as if the air had grown heavily stagnant.</p>
<p>"And?"</p>
<p>Shin Yuna urged her on, short and firm, in a dry tone.</p>
<p>Kang Haeun hesitated for a moment, as if sorting through her complex emotions.</p>
<p>Then, realizing she could no longer run away, she opened her mouth.</p>
<p>"And... I thought... they might have already figured out everything... even the matters regarding his death..."</p>
<p>She hung her head with a look of despair, like someone who had committed an irreversible sin.</p>
<p>As if scenes from the past were flashing through her mind, her tightly closed lips trembled guiltily.</p>
<p>"And what else?!"</p>
<p>Shin Yuna, still unsatisfied, did not stop there, repeating her question like someone determined to drag out the hidden truth from her subject.</p>
