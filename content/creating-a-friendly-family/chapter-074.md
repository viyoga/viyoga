<p>The words 'contraceptive pill' were written on Shiwoo's palm by Sinbi Nuna. For a while, Shiwoo, lost in a confused state, couldn't speak, only staring blankly at Sinbi Nuna.</p>

<p>Various unorganized questions swirled in Shiwoo's mind.</p>

<p>Was there any particular reason for Sehee Nuna to keep her taking contraceptive pills a secret from Shiwoo?</p>

<p>Was there a reason she had to take it as if flaunting it in front of Shiwoo, something she was supposedly trying to keep secret?</p>

<p>If she had truly intended to hide it, she wouldn't have called Sinbi Nuna to the house in the morning to receive the pills in front of Shiwoo.</p>

<p>Shiwoo's mind became a tangled mess, perplexed by Sehee Nuna's incomprehensible and contradictory actions.</p>

<p>Then, a single word suddenly surfaced in Shiwoo's mind.</p>

<p>It was...</p>

<p>'Sex Diet'</p>

<p>At the ominous word that came to mind, Shiwoo's chest felt heavily pressed, as if suffocating.</p>

<p>In a corner of Shiwoo's mind, the words 'No way...' had taken root, as if unconsciously repeating.</p>

<p>But Sehee Nuna's inconsistent actions seemed mostly explained by that single word.</p>

<p>Something that had been suffocatingly knotted in Shiwoo's chest melted down again, settling into a heavy sediment.</p>

<p>"...Did you decide to do it?"</p>

<p>Shiwoo cautiously asked Sinbi Nuna, as if confirming the answer.</p>

<p>Sinbi Nuna nodded silently.</p>

<p>"Sinbi Nuna too?"</p>

<p>Shiwoo's voice was tinged with what sounded like despair and resignation.</p>

<p>Sinbi Nuna, with a pitifully smiling expression, blushed shyly and nodded once more.</p>

<p class="scene-break">· · ·</p>

<p>Shiwoo and his Nunas finished preparing and left the house to go to the academy.</p>

<p>Sehee Nuna and Sinbi Nuna walked ahead affectionately, exchanging conversation.</p>

<p>Topics about places to hang out after school or things they wanted to eat...</p>

<p>And sometimes, secret conversations exchanged quietly so Shiwoo couldn't hear...</p>

<p>Shiwoo followed behind, watching the two of them, feeling as if he were alienated.</p>

<p>Shiwoo hadn't been able to say anything since hearing that Sehee Nuna and Sinbi Nuna had decided to go on that diet.</p>

<p>Sinbi Nuna's last words, 'This time, it really has to be a secret~!!?'</p>

<p>Those words seemed to be spoken knowing that Shiwoo had flaunted the secrets Sinbi Nuna had secretly told him before, to Sehee Nuna on the subway yesterday.</p>

<p>If so, what was the point of keeping it a secret now?</p>

<p>Wasn't Sehee Nuna trying to inform Shiwoo of the secret through Sinbi Nuna in this manner from the beginning?</p>

<p>Or was it that she didn't care whether Shiwoo knew or not?</p>

<p>Again, questions chasing each other's tails...</p>

<p>Why, of all ways, was she trying to inform Shiwoo in this manner?</p>

<p>Because even if she tried to persuade him with kind words, Shiwoo probably wouldn't listen?</p>

<p>As her own kind of consideration, to sort out their relationship without even leaving room for misunderstanding for her naive younger brother?</p>

<p>To say that, the oral sex Sehee Nuna had given him in the changing room this morning was far too stimulating.</p>

<p>Last night's new secrets between Shiwoo and Sehee Nuna didn't seem to have been conveyed to Sinbi Nuna yet.</p>

<p>Perhaps...</p>

<p>Perhaps Sehee Nuna might even be hoping that the new secret with Shiwoo gets conveyed to Sinbi Nuna through Shiwoo.</p>

<p>Messy thoughts, impossible to grasp, were stirring Shiwoo's head.</p>

<p>"Hey~ Yu Shiwoo, aren't you coming quickly~!!? If you dawdle like that, I'll just leave you on the street~!!"</p>

<p>Sehee Nuna, who was walking ahead, glanced back and spoke to Shiwoo.</p>

<p>"Uh...yeah~!!"</p>

<p>Shiwoo's awkward reply.</p>

<p>After answering like that, Shiwoo ran to where his Nunas were.</p>

<p>The initiative in their relationship, which Shiwoo had held last night, now seemed to have completely passed to Sehee Nuna.</p>

<p class="scene-break">· · ·</p>

<p>Before they knew it, the three of them arrived in front of the Nunas' academy.</p>

<p>As they were about to say goodbye and part ways, Sinbi Nuna called out and stopped Shiwoo.</p>

<p>Sinbi Nuna approached Shiwoo.</p>

<p>"Don't forget what you promised to do for me~!! You know, right~!!?"</p>

<p>Sinbi Nuna, smiling playfully, spoke to Shiwoo in a small voice, quiet enough not to be heard by Sehee Nuna.</p>

<p>It was Sinbi Nuna's request that he had agreed to grant in exchange for telling Sehee Nuna's secret.</p>

<p>Before Shiwoo could say anything, Sinbi Nuna turned around and quickly ran towards Sehee Nuna.</p>

<p>-It seemed she had no intention of waiting for Shiwoo's answer-</p>

<p>In the end, Sinbi Nuna didn't tell him what her request was.</p>

<p>Only confirming the fact of the promise, she moved away from Shiwoo.</p>

<p>Sinbi Nuna and Sehee Nuna entered the academy gate and blended in with the other students, disappearing from sight.</p>

<p>With complex feelings, Shiwoo slowly moved his steps.</p>

<p class="scene-break">· · ·</p>

<p>Shiwoo arrived at the academy and entered the classroom.</p>

<p>Before homeroom.</p>

<p>As soon as he entered, the voices of Cheoljin and his friends, busily chattering, could be heard inside the classroom.</p>

<p>"They opened an academy in front of the station~ It was in a building, you know?~"</p>

<p>Surrounded by classmates, Cheoljin was busily chattering about something, like someone selling goods.</p>

<p>On Cheoljin's desk, several photos were placed.</p>

<p>"In this industry, she seems quite famous~ Not only are her skills outstanding, but her looks are also exceptional~ so she seems to be quite popular."</p>

<p>Cheoljin, like someone unpacking a bundle and selling goods, was eagerly explaining about someone to his friends, adding trite rhetoric.</p>

<p>With curiosity, Shiwoo approached Cheoljin's seat.</p>

<p>Looking closer, the photos on the desk captured a close-up of a beautiful woman performing a magic show.</p>

<p>"Yo~ Shiwoo, you here?"</p>

<p>Seeing Shiwoo approach, Cheoljin greeted him.</p>

<p>"What's all this?"</p>

<p>Shiwoo, instead of greeting, pointed at the photos on Cheoljin's desk and asked.</p>

<p>"Ah~~!! Want to see too?"</p>

<p>Cheoljin showed Shiwoo the video on the smartphone he was holding as he spoke.</p>

<p>Cheoljin, who had been caught secretly taking photos of Sehee Nuna and had been hit so hard by Shiwoo that his molar broke.</p>

<p>-From his perspective, it seemed he did it thinking of Sehee Nuna, but anyway, since that incident, he seemed to have stopped following Sehee Nuna-</p>

<p>Recently, what he was doing and where he was going seemed to be causing a lot of worry for Gyuri Ajumma, and he seemed to be engrossed in finding a new object for his hobby.</p>

<p>"You know that new magic academy that opened a few months ago in front of the station? It even advertises on TV sometimes... The director teacher there~!! Isn't she pretty?"</p>

<p>Cheoljin, for some reason, was chattering about the director teacher of the magic academy that Shiwoo would visit today.</p>

<p>"Her name is Yu Yena! Her age is early to mid-30s? According to friends interested in magic, she was quite famous from her first appearance."</p>

<p>Cheoljin, as if giving a presentation, was excitedly chattering about the story he had prepared to Shiwoo, who had approached with curiosity.</p>

<p>"Is that so?"</p>

<p>Seeing the excited Cheoljin, Shiwoo deliberately replied as if uninterested.</p>

<p>"She seems to have a lot of novel magic tricks~ But no matter how much I investigate, I can't find information like her family relations, education, or where she lives, you know?! This person is completely shrouded in mystery! And she's such a beauty~!! She seems fluent in foreign languages, and with her exotic looks, she seems mixed-race too..."</p>

<p>Perhaps to draw the attention and interest of the surrounding kids, Cheoljin continued speaking, mixing in exaggerations.</p>

<p>The 'investigation' Cheoljin was doing was probably limited to secretly following people and taking photos or searching the internet.</p>

<p>Shiwoo thought that even if she was famous, there was no way personal information like family relations or education of an ordinary person who is an academy director would be publicly available online.</p>

<p>According to what he heard from Seyoung, Yu Yena Teacher also served as an after-school teacher at the elementary academy Seyoung attended.</p>

<p>Someone supposedly shrouded in mystery couldn't possibly be an after-school teacher at an elementary academy.</p>

<p>"Though it's hard to believe, there are rumors she's a real psychic~ There are also whispers that she's a famous wealthy man's mistress~ and talk of her brainwashing academy kids and using them for prostitution..."</p>

<p>Cheoljin suddenly lowered his voice and spoke, as if secretly divulging secrets that shouldn't leak out.</p>

<p>At Cheoljin's absurd exaggeration, Shiwoo felt like he should start interjecting.</p>

<p>Whether that Yu Yena Teacher was a psychic hiding her powers or a famous wealthy man's mistress wasn't Shiwoo's concern, but if she was the director teacher of the academy Seyoung would now attend, then it was a different story.</p>

<p>Moreover, prostitution using children, no less...</p>

<p>It seemed Cheoljin had crossed a line...</p>

<p>If he just listened to these occult enthusiasts' nonsensical chatter, and strange rumors about the academy spread, it could become a troublesome matter for him as Seyoung's older brother.</p>

<p>"Hey~! Hold on, hold on~! I'm going to that academy today for a guardian interview for my younger sister~!! And that Yu Yena person is Seyoung's elementary academy's after-school teacher~!!! Stop with the weird talk~!!!"</p>

<p>At Shiwoo's words, as if shouting a rebuke, the kids gathered around began to murmur.</p>

<p>"Kids who should know better, why are you listening to such ridiculous talk~!!! That's the academy my younger sister attends, so stop with the absurd talk now!! Come on~! Come on~!!"</p>

<p>Saying that, Shiwoo squeezed into the gap between Cheoljin and the gathered kids and began to disperse them.</p>

<p>He felt sorry for Cheoljin and the kids, but Shiwoo thought it was unavoidable for Seyoung's sake.</p>

<p>At Shiwoo's interference, the kids returned to their respective seats...</p>

<p>Feeling slightly sorry, Shiwoo looked at Cheoljin.</p>

<p>"Today, to your younger sister's magic academy, you're going for a guardian interview?"</p>

<p>But Cheoljin, instead, seemed to feel strong interest in Shiwoo's words and was looking at Shiwoo with sparkling eyes.</p>

<p>Roughly following this flow, for today's after-school guardian interview, Cheoljin also ended up visiting the academy with his younger siblings.</p>
