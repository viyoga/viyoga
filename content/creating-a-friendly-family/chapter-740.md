<p>There was still plenty of time left before his scheduled meeting with Mom.</p>

<p>Shiwoo planned to take advantage of that time to slowly wander through the department store, looking around wherever his eyes landed. And since he had come straight from the academy, he was still wearing his academy uniform; therefore, Shiwoo was also planning in his heart to change into clothes suitable for a date.</p>

<p class="scene-break">· · ·</p>

<p>Even though it was a weekday and only lunchtime, it was quite crowded.</p>

<p>Shiwoo used the passage inside the subway station to enter the department store building, as if swept along by the crowd. A mega-department store equipped with not only an amusement park, aquarium, movie theater, and exhibition halls, but also various auxiliary facilities...</p>

<p>Not much time had passed since he had come here with Seyoung and Soa to buy supplies for the puppy, Sae-a, but the noise inside the department store and the movement of the people still felt unfamiliar to Shiwoo.</p>

<p>Shiwoo first headed to the fashion hall, where famous brand stores were lined up, to purchase clothes.</p>

<p>As he entered the fashion hall through the internal passage of the department store, a variety of clothes and elegant accessories unfolded before his eyes. Luxury brand logos, which he usually didn't even pay attention to, were sparkling, and the lighting and music inside the stores seemed to delicately mix and stimulate Shiwoo's senses.</p>

<p>Shiwoo was looking around, checking the shop windows for clothes he liked.</p>

<p>Then, a clean, casual combination of clothes worn by a mannequin in one brand store caught his eye. It was a look consisting of a loose-fitting light blue cardigan knit, a white T-shirt, slim-fit gray jeans, and white sneakers.</p>

<p>Shiwoo stopped his steps and stared at the clothes on the mannequin for a moment.</p>

<p>An outfit that exuded a simple yet sophisticated feeling overall.</p>

<p>With this, he felt he could leave a comfortable yet clean impression on his date with Mom today. It seemed like it would harmonize naturally with the white dress he had told Mom to wear earlier, creating a balanced atmosphere.</p>

<p>Shiwoo solidified his decision in his heart, entered the store, and purchased the displayed clothes. The clothes could be altered within the store, so it seemed he could wear them today. However, it looked like it would take about 30 minutes to alter the length of the pants.</p>

<p>After paying for the clothes with Mom's card, Shiwoo decided to leave the store and look around the surrounding area a bit more while the clothes were being altered.</p>

<p>Coincidentally, a passage leading to the jewelry hall was connected nearby.</p>

<p>Shiwoo moved his curious steps and carefully went inside.</p>

<p>The jewelry hall Shiwoo entered had various brand booths spread out wide and long across a large hall. Glass display cases were placed around each booth, and various casual metals were shining under the soft lighting.</p>

<p>One side of the passage was against a wall, and the other side led to a wide pedestrian space where people could naturally come and go while looking. The staff moved inside each booth, arranging displays or guiding customers, without standing out excessively.</p>

<p>Shiwoo carefully moved his steps from one booth to another, scanning the crafted metals inside the display cases one by one.</p>

<p>Then, Shiwoo's gaze was stolen by a store display case where yellow and white metals, neither too flashy nor too plain, were sparkling regularly.</p>

<p>Shiwoo stood in front of the booth's display case for a while, scanning the rings while imagining a ring placed on Mom's finger. The small, delicately crafted designs of the rings seemed to make Shiwoo's heart flutter secretly.</p>

<p>"Hello Is there a design you're interested in?"</p>

<p>A female employee approached Shiwoo with a polite tone and expression and spoke to him.</p>

<p>"...Uh, I'm, I'm thinking of a couple's ring, so I was wondering if there's anything good..."</p>

<p>Shiwoo spoke with a slightly nervous expression, looking at the female employee.</p>

<p>"Ah Are you looking for a couple's ring to match with your girlfriend?"</p>

<p>The female employee continued to maintain her smile while asking Shiwoo.</p>

<p>"...Ah, yes, that's right! My girlfriend!"</p>

<p>Shiwoo answered while reflexively thinking of Mom.</p>

<p>"Did you happen to check your girlfriend's finger size in advance?"</p>

<p>The employee asked if he knew his girlfriend's finger size while making friendly eye contact.</p>

<p>"Ah, no... not yet..."</p>

<p>Shiwoo answered with a flustered expression.</p>

<p>"It's okay Since the size can be adjusted later anyway... There are many cases where people buy the size as is and then come back to adjust it after trying it on. Is your girlfriend very thin by any chance?"</p>

<p>The employee nodded kindly and added a natural question so that Shiwoo could answer comfortably.</p>

<p>"No, she's not that thin... Maybe about the size of an average adult?"</p>

<p>Shiwoo spoke as if gauging Mom's finger.</p>

<p>"Yes, then you can buy a size slightly more generous than the average size, have her actually try it on, and then come back to adjust it slowly later. About how much are you thinking for the price?"</p>

<p>She was skillfully asking about the price range Shiwoo was considering while observing his reaction.</p>

<p>"Ah... that... under 200,000 won..."</p>

<p>Shiwoo answered the employee as if he were embarrassed.</p>

<p>He could have chosen a more expensive ring, but as an academy student, he was worried that if he chose a ring that was too expensive, he might receive strange suspicion from the staff, so he answered with an appropriate price range...</p>

<p>"Under 200,000 won seems appropriate. If you gift a couple's ring that's too expensive from the start, your girlfriend might feel burdened."</p>

<p>"Then how about this design? Would silver or other materials be okay?"</p>

<p>The employee pointed to a corner of the display case and asked carefully as if introducing the rings inside.</p>

<p>"Yes. Well... as long as the design is good... it doesn't matter..."</p>

<p>Shiwoo answered as if he felt relieved by the employee's skillful tone and attitude.</p>

<p>"Well then, would you like to pick one? These are designs that are selling well lately for under 200,000 won."</p>

<p>The employee spoke while neatly placing the rings, case and all, on the display stand with smooth hand movements.</p>

<p>On the display stand, three styles and rings of different materials were neatly displayed.</p>

<p>Shiwoo picked up the ring case in the very center and examined the design.</p>

<p>The ring was a simple band-type design that wrapped around the finger with a neat yet softly shining curve. On the woman's ring side, a single small cubic decoration was embedded on the outer surface, serving as a point that secretly caught the eye without being excessively flashy.</p>

<p>"I'll go with this one!"</p>

<p>Shiwoo spoke clearly with a face of conviction, as if some internal grain had aligned perfectly.</p>

<p>Shiwoo had ended up picking out a ring to give to Mom at a shop in the jewelry hall he had happened to stop by.</p>

<p class="scene-break">· · ·</p>

<p>After purchasing the ring with Mom's card, Shiwoo returned to the fashion hall store to pick up his clothes.</p>

<p>Shiwoo looked at his reflection in the mirror, looking satisfied after changing into the well-altered clothes. A young man who had shed the clunky feeling of a young child and looked much more neatly arranged stood before the mirror.</p>

<p>Shiwoo felt strength naturally entering his shoulders.</p>

<p>Just then, a text message arrived from Mom.</p>

<p>[What did you buy so much of? How are you going to walk around after buying all that before even meeting me?]</p>

<p>It seemed Mom was contacting him after receiving the card usage details via text.</p>

<p>[It's just a new set of clothes. I was in my academy uniform because I came straight from the academy I can't walk around in an academy uniform on my first date with Mom.]</p>

<p>Shiwoo spoke as if making an excuse to Mom, who was about to start nagging via text.</p>

<p>[You should have stopped by the house for a bit then, there was no rush, so why go out and spend money on something like that!]</p>

<p>Mom spoke in a scolding tone as if she were appalled by Shiwoo's excuse.</p>

<p>Even so, she didn't seem to particularly deny the words</p>

<p>"first date."</p>

<p>[If I went home with the way I'm feeling now, Mom, you wouldn't be able to go out, you know? By the time you snapped out of it, it would probably be the next morning or afternoon.]</p>

<p>[Seyoung is the one making dinner, and Mom, you probably wouldn't even be able to walk for a day? Hehe.]</p>

<p>Shiwoo threw a mischievous sexual joke as if teasing Mom.</p>

<p>[You need to know how to restrain yourself! Why is this kid acting like he's in heat all day long!]</p>

<p>Mom spoke in a slightly annoyed tone, yet she was secretly playing along with Shiwoo's sexual joke.</p>

<p>[Do you think that's easy in front of Mom?! In my heart, whether we're eating, watching TV, or showering, I want to live with my thing inserted inside Mom at all times.]</p>

<p>[If you were born a man, Mom! If you lived with a mother like yourself, you'd probably be thinking the exact same thing as me right now!]</p>

<p>Shiwoo continued the messages to Mom, revealing his heart with a playful yet subtly provocative joke.</p>

<p>[Ugh! Stop talking about such weird things! Do you think everyone in the world is like you? Anyway, I'm getting in a taxi now and they say it'll take about 20 minutes, so just know that!]</p>

<p>Mom restrained Shiwoo's playful joke with her characteristic tone and added that she was starting out now by taxi.</p>
