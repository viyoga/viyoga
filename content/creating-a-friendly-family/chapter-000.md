<p>A parallel world similar to ours. Here, people who are not blood relatives live as a family. Minors and animals do not exist. Adults are acting as if they are minors, or as if they are animals.</p>
<p class="scene-break">· · ·</p>
<p>Father passed away in a sudden accident. The family crying at the funeral. Mom Two older sisters And a younger sister Shiwoo's family was not prepared. A sudden death. The children were too young to accept their father's death. The eldest, Sejin-nuuna, a university student. The second, 170-year-old Sehee-nuuna. The third, 150-year-old Shiwoo, and the 120-year-old youngest sister Seyoung-i. Was there ever a good age to accept any death, to begin with? Even 400-year-old Mom Seonmi was not much different from the children. The death of the husband she had relied on for half her life was particularly shocking and overwhelming for her. With the help of relatives and acquaintances, the funeral was able to be completed without her even knowing how anything was handled. But with his death, she didn't know how she would cope with the family's secrets and tasks left behind.</p>
