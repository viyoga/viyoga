<p>Knock... knock... knock...</p>

<p>Owen turned his head at the sound of knocking coming from the front door.</p>

<p>&quot;Who could it be? It’s almost night, so I don’t think it’s appropriate for anyone to visit at this hour.&quot; Owen stood up and walked slowly to the door.</p>

<p>&quot;Coming...&quot; he responded. He was starting to mimic the gentle way Arielle always answered whenever he came home.</p>

<p>Click... creak.</p>

<p>&quot;Oh, young flower boy, where is crybaby Arielle?&quot; an old woman spoke, massaging her hunched back. She had clearly walked the entire way to their house.</p>

<p>&quot;Oh, hello Grandma, good day. Mom is sleeping soundly now... she worked really hard today.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;Is that so?&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;Yes, Grandma. Please come inside... I can’t just let you stand out here on the porch.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;Hoho, what a respectful youth...&quot; She was delighted to receive such a warm invitation.</p>

<p>Owen signaled for the elderly woman to enter the room. It was basic courtesy to treat the old with respect.</p>

<p>Elders were the foundation for the youth, after all, and as a young man, he felt it was his duty to honor them.</p>

<p>’I don’t know what’s happening right now... But this is completely new,’ he thought.</p>

<p>He was entirely blank regarding the old woman’s sudden appearance.</p>

<p>This storyline of the game had never popped up before.</p>

<p>The elder hadn’t even appeared in the game, which had followed a strict mapping plan focused entirely on game quests...things like taming, time management, and specific conversations to spice things up.</p>

<p>There had been no mention of an old woman whatsoever...a new thing to him since that old woman popped.</p>

<p>That was why Owen had been so surprised earlier when he ran into her on his way to the barn.</p>

<p>&quot;Thank you...&quot; But I don’t plan to stay, young flower boy,&quot; the elder said, waving her hands as she politely declined his hospitality.</p>

<p>’What a good boy... He is so considerate to offer. I guess the younger generation isn’t completely lost...’ she thought, satisfied to see such a gentle soul among a modern crowd of spoiled youths.</p>

<p>Crybaby Arielle had raised her son incredibly well.</p>

<p>He was a good fellow who would surely look after his mother in the future.</p>

<p>&quot;Oh... why is that, Grandma? I don’t think Mom can help you right now since she’s already exhausted.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;Well... I just wanted to let her know that a sudden event will be taking place in the village over the next three days. And it’s perfect timing, because you are here, young flower boy.&quot; She smiled gently, pausing for a moment to gasp for air.</p>

<p>Speaking in long sentences was a real physical effort for someone her age.</p>

<p>&quot;Oh... is it related to me or Mom, Grandma?&quot; Owen’s ears perked up. The flow of events was now completely diverging from the game now.</p>

<p>There had never been a festival or event related to the protagonist like this.</p>

<p>’Now, this is getting interesting...’</p>

<p>&quot;You really are a wise young man. Can I sit here?&quot; The village elder gestured toward a chair outside on the veranda.</p>

<p>&quot;Of course, Grandma... let me get you some snacks first.&quot; Owen guided her over.</p>

<p>He couldn’t help but wonder if this really was about him. What kind of event would even require his presence?</p>

<p>&quot;Then I won’t reject such a kind offer, flower boy.&quot; She smiled, settling into the seat. Never in her wildest dreams did she expect to receive such the utmost respect from a young man.</p>

<p>&quot;Okay... just wait a moment, Grandma.&quot; Owen walked inside to fetch some drinks and snacks.</p>

<p>’I greatly respect you, Grandma... I respect all elders, but do you really need to keep repeating that &quot;flower boy&quot; thing? Owen was a bit lost for words, his mind momentarily occupied by the silly nickname.</p>

<p>Though it wasn’t terrible, he wasn’t going to complain about receiving compliments.</p>

<p>...</p>

<p>&quot;Now, let’s continue what I wanted to say, young flower boy...sip...&quot; the old woman said, taking a draft of the tea Owen had provided.</p>

<p>&quot;Say whatever is on your mind, Grandma. I’m all ears.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;Hoho, that’s why I like you, flower boy. You can definitely help us.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;What kind of help, Grandma?&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;Then listen carefully to what I have to say... and decide if you will aid us,&quot; she replied, her expression turning serious.</p>

<p>This was about to become a massive deal, a secret kept for ages. The women of the village were finally ready to take action, and it was time to let the matter loose.</p>

<p>&quot;Hmm...&quot; Owen nodded, his eyes sparkling with anticipation.</p>

<p>&quot;You see, young flower boy, we need a man like you for the upcoming opening ceremony of the festival,&quot; she explained, setting her teacup down on the table.</p>

<p>She looked just like a wise sage out of a fantasy novel.</p>

<p>&quot;Hmm... okay...&quot; Owen simply listened, waiting for her to get to the point.</p>

<p>&quot;Are you willing to help the village, young flower boy?&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;Uhh... what exactly do I need to do? If it’s impossible, I can’t agree, Grandma. You know Mom is quite strict when it comes to me.&quot; Owen didn’t have a single clue what she was talking about.</p>

<p>If the favor involved displaying his manhood to everyone... Well, he would gladly reject (or maybe accept) the offer. But if it wasn’t anything scandalous, why not?</p>

<p>Granted, he wouldn’t partake in anything that might bring harm to his divine cock.</p>

<p>Having a metaphorical guillotine hanging over his head while helping them out was a hard ’no.’ However, if it were a heavenly blessing, Owen would gladly jump at the chance.</p>

<p>&quot;Oh... you see, young flower boy, the passions of the women in this village are dwindling.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;Yes, so how is that related to me, Grandma?&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;I want you to help satisfy them...&quot; she said with deep regret, her emotions surging with a sense of profound loss.</p>

<p>She wasn’t just an ordinary old woman; she was the village chief.</p>

<p>The upcoming festival was turning her mind into a complete mess because the local women desperately needed some attention.</p>
