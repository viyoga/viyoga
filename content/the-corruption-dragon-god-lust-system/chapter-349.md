<p>“All right, bring the food.” Tai’Ren declared, clearing his throat.</p>

<p>Even though eating was basically useless for someone like him, he still enjoyed the taste of meat and alcohol.</p>

<p>As soon as his voice echoed, a dozen servants entered the large dining hall, bringing with them a myriad of spirit beast meats, as well as rice, plenty of liquor and other accompaniments common among cat people.</p>

<p>The servants served everyone, but no one began to eat, waiting for permission from Tai’Ren, who stood up.</p>

<p>“Since my grandfather, the eyeless tyrant, died thirty thousand years ago, we’ve started to get back on our feet.</p>

<p>We still have a long way to go, but with a young man as talented as Long Qingyi, I’m sure this path will be much shorter.</p>

<p>This toast is to my new son-in-law.”</p>

<p>The moment Tai’Ren raised his goblet full of liquor, everyone followed soon after.</p>

<p>All except two people.</p>

<p>Lioran and his father.</p>

<p>“Is there a problem, Elder Lu?” Tai’Ren downed his liquor, watching the grumpy old man coldly.</p>

<p>“Patriarch, should we really hand over such a promising young woman who can still bear so many children, to an outsider? He’s not even part of the dragon people and will be leaving our lands soon!</p>

<p>Linyue deserves a man of our people, one who will give her many seeds for future talents!”</p>

<p>Lioran, standing next to his father, couldn’t help but puff out his chest slightly.</p>

<p>Yes… if it was his father speaking, there was a chance of putting sense into that uneducated brute’s head that he was the son-in-law he deserved.</p>

<p>“Hm…” Tai’Ren scratched his chin, his frown deepening. “So you’re asking me, Tai’Ren, to go back on my word?”</p>

<p>Tai’Ren honestly didn’t like Elder Lu very much.</p>

<p>Firstly, because the old man was already on the verge of advancing to the ancestral emperor realm, at only 2,000 years old, he had a high chance of reaching the peak of the ancestral emperor realm if his advancement in a few hundred years’ time was successful.</p>

<p>With the possibility of achieving such power, Elder Lu had already started plotting.</p>

<p>“Not at all, patriarch! I just want you to understand what’s best for our people! Linyue needs a man to match!” Elder Lu spoke, bringing his fists together.</p>

<p>Before Tai’Ren was even able to reply, he saw a figure rise up beside him.</p>

<p>It was his eldest son, who had just advanced to the realm of the Immortal Emperor, one of Tai’Ren’s greatest prides.</p>

<p>“Father, I agree with Elder Lu. What will become of us if we hand someone like Linyue over to some outsider?”</p>

<p>“Hmm…” Tai’Ren grunted with dissatisfaction, his anger leaking out slightly as he stood up.</p>

<p>Now that he had taken a real liking and respect for Qingyi, naturally hearing his own son say that left him with a bitter taste in his mouth.</p>

<p>Tai’Ren was about to give his verdict when he turned his face to his son-in-law, observing his eyes full of coldness, their purple color glowing softly, as if they could penetrate a man’s soul.</p>

<p>“Long Qingyi, do you have anything to say?” Tai’Ren asked.</p>

<p>“Yes, father-in-law.” Qingyi stood up, staring at Elder Lu coldly.</p>

<p>“For Elder Lu to judge me like this, I suppose he’s completely sure that I’m not worthy, isn’t he?”</p>

<p>“You’re not.” Elder Lu smiled.</p>

<p>“Well then, duel me, suppress your cultivation to my level, no, to a level above mine and fight me.</p>

<p>If you defeat me, I’ll leave immediately and never set eyes on Linyue again, but if you lose…” Qingyi’s voice suddenly lowered, turning into a growl dripping with murderous intent. “Your son must be castrated.”</p>

<p>“What insolence!” Elder Lu slammed his hand against the table.</p>

<p>His son, Lioran, couldn’t help but shudder, feeling a chill run through his private parts.</p>

<p>Qingyi really wasn’t thinking of doing something like that, was he? Did he think a cultivator at the peak of the Immortal Emperor was anything?</p>

<p>Even Linyue felt her heart plummet, her lips trembling softly.</p>

<p>Just when she was starting to like Qingyi… why on earth wouldn’t he just let her father sort it out?</p>

<p>Linyue cast a hopeful glance at her father, hoping that he wouldn’t allow such a proposal.</p>

<p>Unfortunately, that wasn’t the case – quite the opposite.</p>

<p>Her father had an expression of pure delight on his masculine, brutish face, the powerful muscles in his arms tensing as he clenched his fists.</p>

<p>‘THIS! This is how a man should be, fighting his own battles, protecting his wife’s honor and fearing no enemy, regardless of the difference in cultivation! I truly have an exemplary son-in-law!’</p>

<p>Tai’Ren repeated proudly in his mind.</p>

<p>Obviously, he didn’t let it show, taking the position of an impartial leader.</p>

<p>“Elder Lu, would you agree to such a duel?” Tai’Ren asked, clearing his throat.</p>

<p>“Of course I would, this boy needs to be taught some manners!”</p>

<p>“Very well, we’ll interrupt dinner and the duel will take place right now, but I will personally seal Elder Lu’s cultivation and this duel will be to the death.” Tai’Ren declared.</p>

<p>That was honestly good even for him, especially if Qingyi was able to kill Elder Lu.</p>

<p>Tai’Ren would be rid of an annoying fly and the position of his son-in-law that gave him so much pride would be completely secure.</p>

<p>Naturally, everyone there shuddered when they heard those words, especially Elder Lu.</p>

<p>Duel to the death… Was Tai’Ren really so confident in that young man?</p>

<p>Elder Lu hesitated for a brief moment, but in the end just swept those thoughts from his mind.</p>

<p>It was impossible… he was in the Immortal Emperor realm, below only the patriarch and a handful of elders.</p>

<p>Even if Qingyi’s lineage was as powerful as Lioran said it was, Elder Lu was still absolutely certain of his victory.</p>

<p>In the end, he too had a powerful lineage, as well as thousands of years of experience.</p>

<p>His black tail moved gently from side to side as an evil smile appeared on his aged face.</p>

<p>“All right, young man, if you want to die that badly, I’ll give you death!”</p>
