<p>Qingyi felt dozens of powerful auras rise up, quickly surrounding him</p>

<p>as the space changed dramatically.</p>

<p>“Space lock…” He growled in irritation.</p>

<p>There were at least six twelfth ring experts and another hundred eleventh ring experts.</p>

<p>The situation was becoming dangerous.</p>

<p>“Husband… please don’t fight with Father!” Sapphire shouted through the connection between Qingyi and Ruxue, causing the young man’s expression to sour.</p>

<p>He opened his mouth to respond to his wife, but soon fell silent, focusing his gaze on something else.</p>

<p>He was certain that there was a Dantian in the emperor.</p>

<p>But why?</p>

<p>Did he have some connection to the demonic cultists?</p>

<p>Had the power of the Heavenly Demon spread that far?</p>

<p>Honestly, Qingyi didn’t doubt it.</p>

<p>After all, the Heavenly Demon was a peak being, even in the Celestial Heaven, where someone like the emperor was just an ant.</p>

<p>If he was able to take control of a subordinate on the verge of death and speak to Qingyi, he probably had the ability to contact the emperor.</p>

<p>Gritting his teeth, Qingyi made a decision.</p>

<p>The emperor had to die. He needed to root out this corruption.</p>

<p>But not now.</p>

<p>Qingyi closed his eyes, focusing his spatial Qi.</p>

<p>“Trying to break through the spatial blockade? That’s useless!” The emperor smiled, attacking with force, his mana exploding toward Qingyi.</p>

<p>He was already relishing the idea of crippling that young man, of finding out everything he knew about Khaedryss and what his connection was to that damned dragon.</p>

<p>Unfortunately for him, his power struck nothing but emptiness.</p>

<p>The spatial blockade surrounding them collapsed, and the world around them was engulfed in a huge tremor as the attack landed with a bang.</p>

<p>For dozens of kilometers, everything was swept away, with hundreds of thousands dying in a single second.</p>

<p>Still, the emperor didn’t care, his face going through hundreds of emotions.</p>

<p>He turned to a figure who appeared beside him, one of his many advisors.</p>

<p>“Declare my bastard daughter, Sapphire, a traitor, as well as the Hero Long Qingyi. Announce that he allied himself with the dragon elves to kill the other three heroes, using the power of demonic cultists!” The emperor declared firmly.</p>

<p>“Right,” the advisor bowed. “Should we send soldiers to Valemont?”</p>

<p>“No,” the emperor shook his head. “We need all our forces here. Soon, we will show death to those damned Vaeldrinn!”</p>

<p>“Yes, Your Majesty.”</p>

<p>***</p>

<p>Qingyi appeared miles away, feeling countless powerful auras lock onto him.</p>

<p>Teleporting once more, he escaped all those auras.</p>

<p>“Sapphire…” He called out in his mind as he ran nonstop. “Are you</p>

<p>okay?”</p>

<p>“Yes…” She replied, her voice clearly tearful.</p>

<p>“She’s not okay,” Ruxue replied over her, eliciting a sigh from Qingyi.</p>

<p>He had expected the bishop to be a demonic cultist, that perhaps only a few high-ranking figures had been bought off by the followers of the Heavenly Demon.</p>

<p>But honestly, he was being foolish.</p>

<p>On the eastern continent, even Astral Body Realm cultivators, such as the matriarch of the fox people, had been affected.</p>

<p>Why would it be any different here?</p>

<p>“Ruxue… this Heavenly Demon, how powerful was he compared to the Dragon God of Corruption?” he asked.</p>

<p>“Hm… I would say they were pretty much equivalent. It took countless sacrifices to defeat the Corruption Dragon God, with over a thousand unmatched experts dying. A similar sacrifice would be necessary to kill the Heavenly Demon.</p>

<p>After all, he is the most powerful among the seventy-two Heavenly Demon Lords. He is not someone you can underestimate, not even my</p>

<p>creator could underestimate him.”</p>

<p>Hearing that, Qingyi nodded silently, feeling the bitterness in his</p>

<p>heart.</p>

<p>The heavenlyn Demon not only hurt his Sapphire, but also mentioned</p>

<p>Ruyan…</p>

<p>That asshole was really courting death, huh?</p>

<p>Shaking his head, Qingyi just continued to run.</p>

<p>He would arrive at Valemont, make sure everything was fine, and then</p>

<p>set off for the Enlightened Sky Empire.</p>

<p>When he met with Meilin’s father and resolved the fox people’s</p>

<p>problem, he would take the time to clean up everything that remained</p>

<p>of the Heavenly Demon in this world.</p>

<p>Closing his eyes and calming his breathing, Qingyi finally stopped</p>

<p>teleporting.</p>

<p>He had used about ten percent of his Qi and was already thousands of kilometers away from the capital.</p>

<p>Sitting on a tree branch, he entered the world of the mind, rushing towards Sapphire’s room.</p>

<p>The beauty was locked inside, with Aeryn and Celestia trying to reach</p>

<p>her.</p>

<p>“Husband… what happened?” Celestia asked, her eyes full of concern as she threw herself into Qingyi’s embrace.</p>

<p>“Nothing much… just a bad encounter with her father.”</p>

<p>Hearing that, Celestia’s confusion turned to anger.</p>

<p>“Ugh… that bastard! He’s always been a pain in the ass! Hubby’s going</p>

<p>to kick his ass, right? Big sister Isabel and big sister Ruyan said that</p>

<p>hubby always kicks assholes’ asses.”</p>

<p>Qingyi paused for a moment before bursting out laughing.</p>

<p>“Yes, I will.” He kissed Celestia on the lips, then knocked on Sapphire’s</p>

<p>door and entered immediately.</p>

<p>The beauty was alone in a corner, her eyes teary as she hugged her</p>

<p>knees.</p>

<p>Why did things have to be this way?</p>

<p>She felt Qingyi’s warm touch, pulling her onto his lap.</p>

<p>“I… I’m sorry for putting you in danger… there wasn’t even anything</p>

<p>important to take there…” Sapphire whispered, rubbing her face against Qingyi’s chest.</p>

<p>“Yes… and what danger was that? I don’t fear your father or his</p>

<p>armies. In fact, this encounter was good for me,” Qingyi smiled, stroking Sapphire’s long blue hair.</p>

<p>It was strange to see her like this.</p>

<p>She was usually a confident and provocative beauty, always pushing</p>

<p>Aeryn towards his cock and making carefree jokes. “Ah…” Qingyi sighed, remembering his relationship with his own father during his life on Earth.</p>

<p>He was a hard-working man, but cold and detached. They had had many fights and even exchanged punches.</p>

<p>Deep down, he understood how Sapphire felt.</p>

<p>“Do you want me to kill him?” Qingyi asked, his eyes meeting</p>

<p>Sapphire’s.</p>

<p>He was already certain that he had to kill the emperor, but he wouldn’t do anything without Sapphire’s permission.</p>

<p>She swallowed her tears, bit her lower lip, and then filled her face</p>

<p>with determination.</p>

<p>“Yes…”</p>
