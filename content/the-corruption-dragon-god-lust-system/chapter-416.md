<p>The Belgrath’s official who had thrown the spear at Qingyi shuddered.</p>

<p>He was an expert of the eighth ring, equivalent to a False Immortal, and an extremely powerful individual with great chances of reaching the ninth ring.</p>

<p>Being one of Belgrath’s favorite officials, he had received a magic scroll carrying that spear, imbued with one of his leader’s strongest attacks and surrounded by stealth arrays.</p>

<p>He had aimed exactly where Qingyi’s mana heart should have been, going into ecstasy when he realized that his attack had worked.</p>

<p>However, that ecstasy soon turned to sheer terror as Qingyi casually pulled the spear from his chest, throwing it to the ground.</p>

<p>The dread intensified as the hole created by the attack closed immediately, even the internal wounds healing in an instant.</p>

<p>“You’re very courageous. Unfortunately, courage without power is worth as much as a brain without a body.” Qingyi smiled.</p>

<p>The officer tried to retreat, but it was too late.</p>

<p>Qingyi was already upon him, swinging his hand in a gentle slap. With a single movement, the officer’s head exploded like a watermelon, his decapitated body falling to the ground.</p>

<p>Qingyi took a step towards the surviving Belgrath soldiers surrounding him.</p>

<p>They immediately retreated, running in all directions, the main guard escorting Belgrath already many kilometers away.</p>

<p>Qingyi considered chasing them, but in the end just shook his head, focusing his gaze on the battle between the royal forces and the orcs.</p>

<p>The green skins hadn’t even noticed the death of their leader and were still fighting voraciously.</p>

<p>They had already killed more than two thousand royal soldiers.</p>

<p>Although the green skins had suffered more casualties, this was only because they had been the biggest victims of the clash between Qingyi and Zharuk; now, they were beginning to turn the tide of the battle.</p>

<p>With one smooth movement, Qingyi activated the fourth form of the Tempest Monarch Sword Art.</p>

<p>Blades began to appear around him: dozens, hundreds, thousands. The number grew until he decided it was enough and let them fall on the orcs.</p>

<p>Like rain, the steel came down, cutting through skin, bone, fat, flesh and muscle.</p>

<p>Horrific sounds filled the battlefield as the entire orc front line collapsed immediately.</p>

<p>But it didn’t stop there.</p>

<p>Qingyi’s blades changed direction, spinning in the air before flying into the rear lines, continuing to cut without stopping.</p>

<p>The ground was bathed in blood, the screams of pain and despair falling on deaf ears under Qingyi’s cold gaze.</p>

<p>With a sigh, he shook his head, keeping Draconic Qi in his brain and eyes as he analyzed the golden artifact in his hands.</p>

<p>It was a circular golden pendant, adorned with a thin silver cord.</p>

<p>Its center bore the figure of a smiling woman’s face, slightly worn by time, its shape barely distinguishable.</p>

<p>Although it looked complete in a way, the pendant still had small fittings on its sides, as if it were just a fragment of a whole.</p>

<p>[Name: Golden Convergence Pendant</p>

<p>Description: An item created in an ancient forge many millennia ago, by the hands of the most talented craftsman of his time.</p>

<p>One of five items made in honor of the Goddess of Light and Justice, Auranys, created to rally heroes and defeat an ancient evil.</p>

<p>Presenting it to the Divine Church of Auranys will have you declared a hero of a generation. It can grant power to any wearer, even if not accepted by the pendant yet.</p>

<p>Grade: Astral]</p>

<p>The moment he saw that description, Qingyi’s eyes shone deeply.</p>

<p>So that was where Zharuk’s power came from and, above all, that was the item that made Lucien a chosen hero.</p>

<p>The handsome young man was unable to hide his smile as he descended from the skies towards the waiting soldiers, shouting their</p>

<p>names.</p>

<p>“Lightning Prince! Prince of Storms! Lightning Prince, Prince of Storms!”</p>

<p>They shouted in unison, their eyes filled with an admiration that almost outweighed the pain of losing their comrades.</p>

<p>After all, many of them were veteran soldiers and death was part of their daily lives. They couldn’t spend too much time mourning the</p>

<p>dead.</p>

<p>“Your Highness.” The general of the ninth ring immediately stood in front of Qingyi, kneeling down. “Should we continue the pursuit? Belgrath’s troops can’t be far behind.”</p>

<p>“No.” Qingyi shook his head. “Their morale is shattered, their leader crippled and their forces in tatters; just one breath and they’ll</p>

<p>collapse. We have time to rest.”</p>

<p>With a big smile, Qingyi ordered his troops to clear the battlefield and</p>

<p>finish organizing the camp for the night.</p>

<p>When everything was settled, he glanced at his status screen.</p>

<p>[Name: Long Qingyi</p>

<p>Age: 21</p>

<p>Cultivation: Third Stage Of The False Immortal Realm</p>

<p>(500,000,000,000 lust points to upgrade)</p>

<p>Affinities: Lightning: 1500 – Sword: 1000 – Fire: 1500 – Ice: 1500 – Charm: 1500 (can be upgraded in the lust store)</p>

<p>Spiritual roots: Immortal-level Primordial Creation Seed spirit root</p>

<p>(Lightning, sword, fire, ice) (10T lust points to upgrade, new roots can</p>

<p>be bought in the lust store).</p>

<p>Lineage: Blood of the Corruption Dragon God (Transcendental grade, 10T lust points to upgrade.)</p>

<p>Constitution: Primordial Dragon Body (Transcendental grade, 10T lust points to upgrade.)</p>

<p>Lust points: 123,341,236,930]</p>

<p>His lust points were naturally quite low, since he didn’t have much</p>

<p>time to have sex with his wives, but fortunately he would soon have plenty of time for that.</p>

<p>First he needed to get to the True Immortal Realm and decide what to do with that pendant, but those were thoughts for another time.</p>

<p>Now, Qingyi had soldiers to reward.</p>

<p>With a wave of his hands, he ordered an area to be opened in the</p>

<p>center of the army and then opened the system store, buying an almost endless list of high-quality food.</p>

<p>Cakes, delicacies, snacks, rare beast meats, luxury wines, beers,</p>

<p>liqueurs and rum.</p>

<p>Qingyi spared no expense, ensuring that his soldiers ate the best, buying enough food to feed an army of sixty thousand men.</p>

<p>For the rest of the night, they just ate and feasted, preparing for the</p>

<p>next day, when they would finally march out to let their fury fall on</p>

<p>Belgrath.</p>
