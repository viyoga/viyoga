<p>“Oh… is dinner ready yet?” Xueyao’s cheerful voice rang through the kitchen as she stepped inside, her snow-white, fluffy tail wagging behind her.</p>

<p>Right behind her, Celestia also walked in. Her beautiful blue eyes immediately found Rou’er across the room.</p>

<p>It didn’t take long before the three of them gathered at the counter, heads leaning close as they gossiped about the world outside… and, more importantly, about that new sister Qingyi had his eye on.</p>

<p>The gossip circle grew bigger the moment Linyue padded in, her orange tail wagging happily as she heard everything.</p>

<p>Without hesitation, the cat-girl pressed herself against Qingyi’s side, the soft, plush warmth of her body molding to his arm as she rubbed her cheek along his shoulder like a needy kitten.</p>

<p>Qingyi chuckled and ran his fingers through her orange hair, weaving slowly through the silken strands.</p>

<p>She melted at his touch, a low, contented purr rumbling from her throat.</p>

<p>It didn’t take long for the atmosphere in the world of the mind to grow lively.</p>

<p>Qingyi devoured Elize’s meal, barely pausing to breathe between bites.</p>

<p>When the other girls asked about the secret ingredient that made the meat soup taste so divine, Celestia, Auranys, and Elize only exchanged blushing glances among themselves.</p>

<p>It was a little too embarrassing to share. So they kept the tiny secret among themselves and their beloved husband… who, after all, already drank the “special ingredient” straight from the source every single day.</p>

<p>Once the long meal drew to a close, Qingyi withdrew to one of the temple’s many cultivation rooms, sealing the door behind him. [Name: Long Qingyi</p>

<p>Age: 23</p>

<p>Cultivation: First Stage of the Celestial Ascension Realm (30 G Lust Points to upgrade)</p>

<p>Mana Manipulation: Fourteenth Ring (100 G Lust Points to upgrade)</p>

<p>Affinities: Lightning: 9500 – Sword: 9500 – Fire: 9500 – Ice: 9500 – Space: 9500 – Light: 9500 – Shadow: 9500 – Charm: 6000 (can be upgraded in the Lust Store)</p>

<p>Spiritual Roots: Origin-level Primordial Creation Seed Spirit Root (Lightning, Sword, Fire, Ice, Space, Light, Shadow) (100 G Lust Points to upgrade).</p>

<p>Spiritual Roots2: None/Origin-level Primordial Creation Seed Mana Heart (100 G Lust Points to upgrade).</p>

<p>Lineage: Blood of the Corruption Dragon God (Origin grade, 250 G Lust Points to upgrade.)</p>

<p>Constitution: Primordial Dragon Body (Origin grade, 250 G Lust Points to upgrade.)</p>

<p>G Lust Points: 321.7]</p>

<p>Over three hundred lust points. Practically every last one of them earned through his perfect Rou’er.</p>

<p>The big upgrades were still out of reach, but acquiring new spiritual roots and strengthening his cultivation were much within his grasp. Only one question remained. Which spiritual root should he buy? ‘I only have one of the five basic elements… the other four are dirt cheap. They’d probably make a good purchase,’ Qingyi tought, scratching his chin.</p>

<p>Part of him longed to snatch something flashier, like time, but such elements were as dangerous as they were costly, far beyond anything he could currently afford.</p>

<p>With a small sigh, he settled for the basics.</p>

<p>The cost was so trivial that not even 0.1 G Lust Points was taken from his balance.</p>

<p>The young man’s eyes narrowed the moment the transaction completed, his breath seizing as a searing pain flooded every inch of his body.</p>

<p>Cheap elements or not, nothing dulled the agony of having his meridians torn apart and rebuilt from the ground up.</p>

<p>His Dantian quaked violently. From within that turbulent core, new Qi surged into being. Four fresh strands wound themselves around the fifth that already dwelled there: earth, fire, wind, metal, and water.</p>

<p>A long, ragged sigh escaped his lips.</p>

<p>[You have successfully united the five primordial elements.</p>

<p>An unknown change is occurring in your dantian.]</p>

<p>“Huh?” Confusion flickered across Qingyi’s face.</p>

<p>He extended his Qi senses throughout his body, probing deep into his dantian.</p>

<p>His new elements felt strange. they were intertwined. Yet not in a chaotic way.</p>

<p>Instead, they were neatly fused, one supporting the next like a foundation of a building.</p>

<p>Curious, Qingyi circulated all five Qi at once. Immediately, an intense, rainbow-hued aura erupted around his body, radiating a power far greater than any of them could have achieved on their own.</p>

<p>At that sight, his lips curled higher still, and his thoughts raced back to his strongest technique: the Heaven-Shattering Divine Slash.</p>

<p>What if he fused this rainbow Qi, his bloodline Qi, and the flames of primordial chaos all into a single strike?</p>

<p>Anyone who overheard such a thought would have called it madness.</p>

<p>Combining so many Qi was a recipe for death. A painful, explosive death. At best, the cultivator attempting it would end up crippled.</p>

<p>But Qingyi was no ordinary cultivator.</p>

<p>He carried within him the seed of primordial creation. Even if every element of Qi that existed throughout the Celeatial Heaven were united inside his Dantian, he would not collapse.</p>

<p>A broad smile split his face.</p>

<p>In a blink, Qingyi departed the world of the mind and, in his bedroom, stepped into the sea of the mind, where he raised one hand and a sword materialized in his grip.</p>

<p>Recalling the foundation of the Heaven-Shattering Divine Slash, he drew in a deep breath.</p>

<p>First, he wove together sword Qi and lightning Qi within his blade, then layered in spatial Qi, all forged into a single keen edge.</p>

<p>On the right side of the weapon, light Qi. On the left, shadow Qi. That alone was enough to make the entire sea of mind shudder. So many Qi bound into a single blade was not something natural. It was not something the primordial laws had ever been built to handle. Qingyi grunted through a sharp stab of pain, finally summoning the flames of primordial chaos and the last two remaining types of Qi. The metal of the blade trembled, a high-pitched whistle slicing through the air as the weapon threatened to disintegrate in his hand.</p>

<p>Across the beautiful young man’s body, an aura took shape. Every color at once, and no color at all. It blazed bright enough to blind any onlooker, and yet it was black as the deepest abyss.</p>

<p>Qingyi exhaled. Took a single step forward. And then he unleashed the Heaven-Shattering Divine Slash.</p>

<p>For a moment, there was only silence.</p>

<p>Then everything trembled.</p>

<p>From the sea of his mind to the world beyond.</p>

<p>The entire Cosmic Void Sect shuddered, not from a specific aura or</p>

<p>Qi, but from the collapse of all primordial laws around it.</p>
