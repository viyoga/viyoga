<p>News of what had happened in the palace of the council of elders spread like wildfire.</p>

<p>Unlike the outer region of the Blue Moon Continent, where Qingyi was already very well known for his achievements and talent, his name in the Orthodox Alliance was basically non-existent.</p>

<p>A rookie who impressed all the squad leaders and won first place during the selection.</p>

<p>These achievements were quickly forgotten amid the stressful routine of the alliance’s warriors.</p>

<p>But now that had changed.</p>

<p>His squad leader’s report stated that he had killed the current heir to the leader of the demon cult in a one-on-one fight. Something that was quickly confirmed by reports from other spies in the demon cult.</p>

<p>That alone would have raised his prestige to incomparable levels, but it didn’t cause as much shock as the news that followed.</p>

<p>Tianyu had challenged Qingyi to a duel to the death, and not only had Qingyi accepted this duel, but Yanxiao himself, the guardian of Qingyi’s origin sect, had given permission for it to happen.</p>

<p>With that, Qingyi’s fame exploded through the roof, his name on the lips of every orthodox warrior on every street corner.</p>

<p>“Ah… I should have taken another route…” Qingyi lowered his head, feeling intense stares focus on him as he walked through the orthodox alliance.</p>

<p>It sure was uncomfortable when his wives weren’t there for him to brag about what an amazing husband they had.</p>

<p>With a weary sigh, he hurried on, arriving in an area completely separate from the orthodox alliance; a cluster of luxurious pavilions protected by powerful defensive formations.</p>

<p>That place was home to the residences of the transcendents who founded the orthodox alliance.</p>

<p>Without hesitation, Qingyi walked down the wide street that separated these many pavilions, arriving in front of a large mansion with flaming red walls.</p>

<p>Through the huge double doors, clearly not made for humans, Qingyi could see a figure lying on a gigantic cushion, watching him with interest.</p>

<p>“It took longer than I expected.” Yanxiao’s feline body rose, his large, sharp claws being briefly revealed as he stretched.</p>

<p>“Greetings, guardian. May I ask what you wish with me?” Qingyi asked, bowing and respectfully clasping his fists together.</p>

<p>The gentle smile on Yanxiao’s huge face suddenly disappeared as he sensed Qingyi’s cultivation.</p>

<p>The day before, Qingyi was at the eighth stage of the Nascent Soul Realm, but now, he was already a single step away from the Rebirth Realm.</p>

<p>Even seeing it with his own eyes, Yanxiao still found it difficult to understand Qingyi.</p>

<p>“Qingyi.”</p>

<p>“Yes, Guardian?”</p>

<p>“How long have you been cultivating?”</p>

<p>That question made Qingyi shudder slightly, thinking of an answer.</p>

<p>After a moment’s hesitation, he decided to tell the truth.</p>

<p>He was in no position to lie about his talent, not when he had proven it so many times.</p>

<p>“My master started training me when I was 10, but I only embarked on my cultivation journey for good a little less than two years ago.”</p>

<p>“Less than two years…” Yanxiao whispered. “Qingyi, do you know what the talent curve is?” The tiger turned its face, its feline eyes focusing on the horizon.</p>

<p>“No, guardian.”</p>

<p>Sighing, Yanxiao explained: “The talent curve is what defines a cultivator’s limit and how quickly a they reaches their limit, it’s steeper at first, but then it starts to straighten out.</p>

<p>It only took Tang Biyue twenty years to reach the peak of the Nascent Soul Realm, but she’s already been in the Rebirth Realm for over two hundred years.</p>

<p>As a mythical beast, my lifespan is much longer than humans and I reached the rebirth realm after 100 years of cultivation.</p>

<p>Even so, it took me more than four thousand years to finally reach the realm of transcendence.</p>

<p>That’s the talent curve, when you reach the end of it, your cultivation slows down, the annual breakthroughs start to take decades, centuries or even millennia.”</p>

<p>Qingyi listened attentively, a doubt soon rising in his chest. “Thank you for your teachings, guardian. But why are you telling me this?”</p>

<p>“Because your talent curve seems to have no end, it’s not slowing down. On the contrary, it’s getting faster.” Yanxiao replied. “You’re an anomaly that I’ve only seen a few times in my life.”</p>

<p>“Few times? Has the guardian ever seen other people like me?” Qingyi tilted her face slightly.</p>

<p>“A few, sometimes every few millennia, sometimes every few tens of millennia.” Yanxiao walked around Qingyi, his claws tapping the ground.</p>

<p>“They have two futures: either they die early, or they ascend to the immortal heaven. I’ve never seen any of them who have stayed on this plane for more than a decade. Either they ascend before they’re thirty, or they die before they’re thirty.”</p>

<p>Yanxiao brought his face close to Qingyi’s, sniffing him.</p>

<p>A breath of boiling air left his huge nostrils and made Qingyi’s long hair flutter.</p>

<p>He wondered which of these Qingyi was?</p>

<p>Unfortunately, that wasn’t what Yanxiao was there for.</p>

<p>Looking into the future of someone like Qingyi was dangerous, the heavens didn’t like such things and no divination master would have the courage to accept such a request.</p>

<p>“Qingyi.” Yanxiao forced even more seriousness into his voice.</p>

<p>It was time to deal with the matter for which he had called Qingyi.</p>

<p>“I put the sect leader under house arrest, do you know why I did that?”</p>

<p>“I heard rumors that she was controlled by a being from the immortal heaven, her original soul erased and exchanged for a fragment of that being’s soul.”</p>

<p>“Oh… You’re better informed than I thought.” Yanxiao sniffed Qingyi once more.</p>

<p>“You’ve never had any contact with her, right?” Yanxiao asked.</p>

<p>“No, and honestly, I don’t understand where the guardian is going with this.” Qingyi shook his head.</p>

<p>“If you’re lying to me, the consequences will be severe.” For the first time since Qingyi and Yanxiao had met, the huge flaming tiger unleashed its aura on the young man.</p>

<p>The world around them seemed to fall silent, Yanxiao’s Qi so overwhelming that space seemed about to collapse, unable to withstand so much power.</p>

<p>“Tell me, why is there a smell so similar to hers on you? Did she give you the means to advance so quickly?”</p>
