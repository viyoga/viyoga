<p>Qingxue left her cultivation cave, her beautiful, serene blue eyes closing instinctively in the intense daylight.</p>

<p>“Congratulations on your breakthrough, Master!” Qingyi appeared in front of Qingxue, admiring her beauty for a brief moment before taking her in his arms.</p>

<p>His right hand rested on her slender waist while his left went up to her long silver hair.</p>

<p>“Did you miss your disciple?” he asked.</p>

<p>Qingxue’s beautiful face blushed intensely, her usual coolness melting into Qingyi’s tight embrace.</p>

<p>Her mind ran through many possible answers, but in the end, she could only swallow her pride and raise her vision, her eyes meeting his.</p>

<p>“Yes, I missed you and… thank you for everything, husband…”</p>

<p>“Good,” Qingyi smiled. “I missed you too.” He slapped her ass, gripping the voluptuous flesh tightly.</p>

<p>“What do you say we do one last master and disciple practice before we leave for the Nine Peaks Basin?”</p>

<p>“Nghnn” Qingxue let out a pained moan before hesitantly nodding.</p>

<p>She was also curious to test her new power as a Burning Blood Realm expert and was eager to see how much Qingyi had improved.</p>

<p>They soon found themselves in the arena of her mansion.</p>

<p>It was clearly insufficient to withstand the power of both of them, but it would still do.</p>

<p>Two sharp whistles echoed as Qingxue’s blades slid against her scabbard, both seized by a powerful wind and sword Qi as the surrounding space shook.</p>

<p>Unlike the Iron Body Realm, which focused entirely on strengthening the body, the Burning Blood Realm concentrated on strengthening the meridians.</p>

<p>As such, Qingxue’s Qi was much more powerful than before.</p>

<p>It was far from enough for Qingyi to feel threatened, but enough for her to be confident that she could defeat hundreds of the previous version of herself in a single move.</p>

<p>Qingyi drew the heaven-defying thunder sword, his body filled with a thunderous aura and the ground beneath his feet cracking with the pressure.</p>

<p>A cherry leaf floated through the air and the moment it hit the ground between them, they both lunged forward.</p>

<p>A deafening bang exploded when the two blades met.</p>

<p>Qingxue’s wind Qi painted the world behind her green while Qingyi’s lightning Qi ripped through the air behind her, making it boil.</p>

<p>After a moment of struggle, Qingxue stepped back, feeling her arms tremble.</p>

<p>“You’ve reached the eighth stage of the Iron Body Realm?” she asked, shocked.</p>

<p>“Master has good senses.” Qingyi smiled as he retracted his blade.</p>

<p>Hearing those words, Qingxue could only shake her head.</p>

<p>With that breakthrough, she believed she would finally be able to surpass Qingyi, but the gap between them had only grown even wider.</p>

<p>She really couldn’t help but feel a little disappointed with herself.</p>

<p>“You’re thinking too much.” Qingyi said, grabbing Qingxue’s face gently.</p>

<p>“Your husband still has one more gift for you.” Qingyi said, opening the system store and going to the lineages and constitution category.</p>

<p>He had 40.000 lust points to spend, enough at least for a rare constitution and lineage for his master.</p>

<p>After a few moments searching, he finally found what he wanted.</p>

<p>[Item: Storm Phoenix bloodline.</p>

<p>Description: A powerful bloodline of a mythical beast whose life was long ago taken over by a coalition of experts from the ancient era.</p>

<p>Grade: Rare</p>

<p>Price: 20,000]</p>

<p>[Item: Constitution of the Heart of the Four Winds</p>

<p>Description: A powerful constitution born of the four primordial winds. It used to be almost invincible even in the celestial heavens, but the ages have dissolved much of its power.</p>

<p>Rank: Rare</p>

<p>Price: 22,000]</p>

<p>“Master, do you trust me?” Qingyi asked.</p>

<p>When Qingxue nodded in confirmation, he grabbed her hands, leading her into the cultivation cave and pressing his hands against her delicate back.</p>

<p>“Be strong, master.” Qingyi spoke before purchasing the constitution and bloodline, imbuing both into Qingxue’s body.</p>

<p>It was a long and painful process.</p>

<p>The confusion on Qingxue’s face turned into painful, muffled screams.</p>

<p>Sweat dripped down her pale skin as every fiber of her being seemed to be ripped apart and remade.</p>

<p>After long hours, the pain ceased and Qingxue’s body finally fell wearily onto Qingyi’s chest.</p>

<p>“Ugh… what have you done to me?” Qingxue asked, panting.</p>

<p>She could feel the difference in her body and smell the horrible odor of a body reconstruction, but she was simply unable to understand what Qingyi had done to her and why it was so powerful.</p>

<p>As she stood up, her body felt much lighter, as if it were floating in the air.</p>

<p>Her sensitivity to Qi had increased tremendously, as had the power of the wind Qi that ran through her meridians.</p>

<p>Studying her Dantian, she felt a new thread of Qi in her Qi Core.</p>

<p>It was strangely familiar, but still distant, like something that had never existed and was there all the time at the same time.</p>

<p>“Is that?” Her eyes widened as she ran out of her cave.</p>

<p>“Wait!” Qingyi hurried after her.</p>

<p>Stopping in front of the courtyard of her mansion, she accessed that Qi, pushing it towards her meridians.</p>

<p>A powerful aura ran through her body as her appearance changed.</p>

<p>Her blue eyes became even deeper, shining with a light of their own while ethereal feathers subtly appeared on parts of her body.</p>

<p>She had never felt anything like it, but she had read countless books on the subject and had seen the sect leader himself demonstrate it to her once.</p>

<p>Activating her movement technique, she took a step forward.</p>

<p>A bang echoed as her body broke through the sound barrier and covered a distance of hundreds of meters in the blink of an eye.</p>

<p>“Did you like my gift for you?” Qingyi asked, observing the trembling expression on the silver-haired beauty’s face.</p>

<p>With tears in her eyes, she deactivated her bloodline ability and threw herself into Qingyi’s arms.</p>

<p>“Thank you husband… thank you for everything…” she said.</p>

<p>Those were tears of happiness, running down her delicate face and staining Qingyi’s clothes.</p>

<p>“This small place is no longer suitable for us, love.” Qingyi kissed her, her plump, rosy lips untouched by the dirt of the body reconstruction.</p>

<p>Parting his lips from Qingxue’s, he smiled at her. “Let’s take a shower, I’m pretty sure the sect leader wants to send us away as soon as possible, hahaha.”</p>
