<p>The last competition between the demonic and orthodox forces had ended in a veritable massacre, leaving a trail of fallen geniuses in its wake.</p>

<p>Even so, it had been an organized event, with judges, observers, and strict rules meant to keep the chaos in check.</p>

<p>The main reason for such a staggering death toll was simple: the stubborn refusal of the young geniuses on both sides to surrender.</p>

<p>Virtually every battle became a fight to the death, each combatant pouring out everything they had, often without allowing the judges even a moment to intervene.</p>

<p>The orthodox faction had suffered more defeats, but even the demonic side had seen dozens of unparalleled geniuses fall during that event.</p>

<p>Many had commented that this was the central reason they had suffered so heavily from Long Wujin's descent into madness.</p>

<p>After all, they had lost nearly half an entire generation of great geniuses only a few tens of thousands of years before the war.</p>

<p>Of course, Tianjin didn't believe those lost geniuses would have made much of a difference, but that mattered little now.</p>

<p>His face wore a stern expression, eyes fixed on the letter as his fingers clenched lightly around the paper.</p>

<p>This was no simple proposal for an organized duel between the demonic and orthodox forces.</p>
