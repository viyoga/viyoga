<p>After Lingxue left, Qingyi remained alone and silent, sending subtle pulses of Qi around him, sensing everything.</p>

<p>About an hour passed, and once he was certain that Lingxue had already removed all the fox women from the danger zone, Qingyi finally acted.</p>

<p>He walked over to one of the many roots and pressed his hands against the firm, rough wood.</p>

<p>Qingyi closed his eyes, sending out a pulse of Qi and processing each response.</p>

<p>“I found you.” Qingyi smiled, his body disappearing, swallowed up by a spatial fold.</p>

<p>When he reappeared, he was in a strange place.</p>

<p>Everywhere he looked, he saw nothing but light. A field of white grass, a white sky, and white walls.</p>

<p>A gentle breeze caressed his long black hair as he walked through this place.</p>

<p>“This must be the heart of the tree of life…</p>

<p>His eyes scanned the area until they finally stopped in the center, where he found what he was looking for.</p>

<p>It wasn’t exactly a seed. A cocoon would be a better way to describe</p>

<p>it.</p>

<p>It was a pulsating red, perhaps two or three meters tall.</p>

<p>Although covered only by a thin transparent membrane, the thick liquid still hid what was inside.</p>

<p>Qingyi pointed a single finger, and an orb of crimson flames appeared at its tip.</p>

<p>He could feel that frightening demonic Qi growing stronger, consuming all the energy of the fox people’s Tree of Life.</p>

<p>If it weren’t for his powerful constitution, he was sure he would have already been poisoned by this Qi.</p>

<p>This couldn’t go on.</p>

<p>He sighed, filled his lungs with air, and then released it.</p>

<p>A terrifying ray of heat exploded at speed, hitting the cocoon with full force.</p>

<p>The world before him split in two, as did his attack, a barrier like a sharp blade appearing in front of the cocoon.</p>

<p>Qingyi’s attack ceased, revealing the cocoon intact between two huge cuts in the ground.</p>

<p>A few moments passed until, finally, something happened.</p>

<p>The cocoon shook, undulating violently before collapsing.</p>

<p>Pure red liquid spilled out in deep veins, corrupting everything it touched. A figure accompanied this spill, falling to the ground.</p>

<p>It was clearly a female figure, but drenched in that liquid. Qingyi was unable to see her facial features.</p>

<p>A pair of double horns, which appeared to be covered in skin, and long white hair were revealed, contrasting with the blood red that covered her.</p>

<p>The figure took a moment on the ground, seeming comfortable until, under Qingyi’s gaze, it ceased to be so.</p>

<p>Her eyes opened, pure and deep, like those of a child who had just seen the world for the first time.</p>

<p>Her fingers grabbed the white grass, now stained red.</p>

<p>Her first reaction was not anger, aggression, or even an attempt to hide her nakedness.</p>

<p>She simply plucked the blade of grass, observing it carefully on her fingertips.</p>

<p>The reddish liquid disappeared completely, evaporating into the air and revealing the grass in all its beauty.</p>

<p>“What is this?” she asked, sniffing the blade of grass.</p>

<p>So sweet and so perfect.</p>

<p>“Spiritual grass of some kind…”</p>

<p>“Hm…” Her lips curved. “I like that.”</p>

<p>“Are you here to kill me?” she continued, focusing her eyes on</p>

<p>Qingyi’s.</p>

<p>“Yes…” He nodded. “What are you?”</p>

<p>She shuddered, immediately retreating.</p>

<p>“I am a thing… a bag to gather energy for my master…”</p>

<p>Something akin to fear flashed in her eyes, lasting only a brief moment before being replaced by curiosity.</p>

<p>“Why do you want to kill me?” she asked again, standing up and squeezing the grass between her curious, delicate fingers.</p>

<p>“Because you will hurt people I love.”</p>

<p>“Hm… but… I don’t want to hurt anyone.”</p>

<p>“And does that change anything?”</p>

<p>“No…” She lowered her head, intertwining her fingers anxiously.</p>

<p>“I can’t sense the energy reserves prepared by my creator. Nor the</p>

<p>Daughter of the Icy Moon.”</p>

<p>“They are far from here. Now, it’s just you and me…” Qingyi said, a strange expression taking over his face.</p>

<p>Daughter of the Icy Moon? Was she talking about Xueyao? What was the Heavenly Demon’s interest in Xueyao?</p>

<p>“I know…” The seed spoke once more, her voice almost a whisper.</p>

<p>There was pain in her eyes, resignation, and above all, hesitation. She looked at her hands and then at Qingyi again.</p>

<p>In the next moment, she disappeared. The ground beneath her feet turned into nothing but pure dust.</p>

<p>Qingyi turned his face away, his eyes cold and focused as his hair</p>

<p>fluttered.</p>

<p>He felt a crimson liquid run down his cheek. A thin cut revealed itself for a brief moment before disappearing, the blood evaporating into a</p>

<p>reddish vapor.</p>

<p>“Hm… I hit you… things die when they are hit… are you already dead?”</p>

<p>“No…” Qingyi turned, watching her standing behind him.</p>

<p>What was wrong with that woman? She was really like a newborn.</p>

<p>He closed his eyes and calmed his breathing.</p>

<p>She had to die.</p>

<p>For the sake of the fox people.</p>

<p>If she got close to them, she could awaken the energy within them</p>

<p>and consume them.</p>

<p>He wouldn’t take that risk.</p>

<p>Qingyi gathered all his Qi in his fist, his eyes filled with murderous</p>

<p>intent as he punched.</p>

<p>The woman seemed confused at first, watching the attack approach</p>

<p>at extreme speed.</p>

<p>She blinked and when she realized what was happening, her body was</p>

<p>torn from the ground, a barrier appearing around her as she was</p>

<p>thrown backward.</p>

<p>The white walls surrounding them held her for only a brief moment before collapsing, the woman’s body thrown into a world she had</p>

<p>never seen before.</p>

<p>It was beautiful. Endless forests, birds singing, and a pure, burning</p>

<p>sun.</p>

<p>If she could, she would just watch that for the rest of her existence.</p>

<p>She turned, watching the huge tree trunk from which she had been thrown drift away.</p>

<p>An intense flaming aura emerged from the hole she had made in the trunk, rapidly approaching.</p>

<p>She crossed her arms, and immediately, a pair of blood-red blades</p>

<p>appeared in her hands.</p>

<p>She looked at the blades, as if she had formed them by instinct rather</p>

<p>than her own desire.</p>

<p>Her beautiful eyes focused. Then, she slashed forward.</p>

<p>Qingyi’s attack split in two with that slash.</p>

<p>The resulting fireball completely engulfed her, the flames continuing</p>

<p>for kilometers and destroying everything.</p>

<p>Trees, birds, animals, spirit beasts, and even oxygen.</p>

<p>Nothing remained but oppressive white smoke.</p>
