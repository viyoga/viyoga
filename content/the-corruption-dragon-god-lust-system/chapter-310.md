<p>Margareth was truly a powerhouse of the sixth ring, probably second in power only to enlightened soul realm cultivators with bloodlines.</p>

<p>Margareth passed through the large double doors of the lord’s mansion, stopping suddenly.</p>

<p>Her fingers brushed a lock of golden hair out of her face as she watched a small army gathered outside.</p>

<p>Around five hundred men in total. There were a few cultivators among them, but the vast majority were mana users.</p>

<p>Naturally, she didn’t have time to spend on any pomp. Those pirates were like rats, constantly changing their hiding places, never staying in one place for more than a few days.</p>

<p>They needed to act as quickly as possible.</p>

<p>It didn’t take more than thirty minutes before they left the protection of the Eagle Peak Valley walls, marching in two separate lines along the closed trails.</p>

<p>Qingyi walked silently alongside the soldiers, his eyes filled with nostalgia.</p>

<p>He wasn’t marching in formation, nor was he obliged to do so, but standing next to them, he couldn’t help remembering his life on earth.</p>

<p>The three years he’d spent in the army had been a living hell, but at least he’d learned a lot.</p>

<p>“You’re new here, aren’t you?” A voice echoed next to Qingyi, coming from a young soldier, one of the locals who had converted to the Auranys faith.</p>

<p>“Yes,” Qingyi replied, “I come from the capital.”</p>

<p>“Oh…” The young man’s eyes twinkled softly. “I’ve heard that the capital is huge. Is it true that there are millions of people there? Are you here to convert to Auranys too? Her light reaches us all!”</p>

<p>“Well…” Qingyi sighed, shaking his head.</p>

<p>A religious fanatic. Honestly, what else could you expect? Even cultivators were fanatical in a way, but they worshipped the heavens, not a specific deity.</p>

<p>And this worship of the heavens also took place in a very different way.</p>

<p>“Maybe if she was hot…” He whispered, imagining the appearance of such a goddess.</p>

<p>She wouldn’t be ugly, would she?</p>

<p>Qingyi barely had time to look away before he was forced to dodge a punch, the soldier’s fist passing inches from his face.</p>

<p>“What a blasphemy! How dare you!” The soldier punched again, stopping those marching behind.</p>

<p>“Kid…” A touch of anger appeared on Qingyi’s face as he parried the young man’s fist. A thin thread of murderous intent left his body.</p>

<p>“If you do that again,” Qingyi brought his face close to the young soldier, squeezing so hard that a grunt of pain echoed, “I’ll make you see your goddess much faster than you can imagine.”</p>

<p>“Is there a problem?” One of the knights overseeing the march approached, his horse slowing down, his powerful mana falling on them both.</p>

<p>The soldier almost fell to his knees while Qingyi just shook his head, continuing to walk.</p>

<p>“N-no, Sir Sillas…” The soldier also resumed walking, holding his aching wrist as he struggled to erase that frightening image of Qingyi from his mind.</p>
