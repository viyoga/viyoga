<p>Qingyi’s eyes shifted, resting on the bedroom door.</p>

<p>Slowly, it swung open, revealing Ruxue standing in the doorway.</p>

<p>Over the past few days, she had been... well... far from her best.</p>

<p>Her long hair was less neatly arranged than usual, and faint shadows marked the skin beneath her eyes.</p>

<p>She trusted Qingyi, of course. But ever since the announcement that the Heavenly Demon planned to abuse her sister and her master in public after reviving her, she had sunk into a deep depression.</p>

<p>She tried to tell herself that everything was fine, that Qingyi, as always, would sort everything out.</p>

<p>He always had.</p>

<p>Why would it be any different now?</p>

<p>Still, repeating those words was much easier than erasing the despair lodged in her chest.</p>

<p>For tens of thousands of years, she had dreamed of the day she would return to the arms of her original master and her sister. Annoying, yes, but someone she truly loved.</p>

<p>But... what if the Heavenly Demon broke her?</p>

<p>Knowing her master as she did, Ruxue knew full well that she would take her own life the very moment that monster touched her.</p>

<p>And even if he stopped her from killing herself, it would do little good.</p>

<p>If she were rescued, she would still end her own life at the first opportunity she had.</p>

<p>She was a proud woman.</p>

<p>A thousand deaths wouldn’t be enough to wash away the shame and despair of losing her purity to the Heavenly Demon.</p>

<p>That worried her more than anything.</p>

<p>Besides that, of course, there was the danger.</p>

<p>Qingyi’s plan was suicidal.</p>

<p>He intended to use his absurdly high spatial affinity to invade the Heavenly Demon’s castle, break through several spatial blockade formations, and then escape with her master.</p>

<p>Ruxue knew the power of his affinity well.</p>

<p>She had seen with her own eyes the tests he’d conducted with Tianjin a few days earlier.</p>

<p>He easily broke through the spatial blockade of more than five Celestial Emperors.</p>

<p>It wasn’t until the number rose to ten that he began to struggle—and that wasn’t even the limit of his affinity.</p>

<p>In a few more months, before putting the plan into action, he would likely be capable of breaking through the blockade of dozens of Celestial Emperors.</p>

<p>With such power, the chances of success were extremely high, especially since the Heavenly Demon would never believe that someone with Qingyi’s abilities could exist.</p>

<p>"Is everything okay?" Qingyi’s soft voice pulled her back from her thoughts.</p>

<p>"Yes..." Ruxue nodded.</p>

<p>Her voice came out weaker than usual, lacking her usual pride.</p>
