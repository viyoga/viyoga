<p>In one of the many luxurious pavilions of the Palace of Ten Thousand Beasts, a woman of extraordinary beauty rested: Bai Yunxi.</p>

<p>The only daughter of the Myriad Beasts Sect’s leader, She was quite different from her brother, Bai Hu Lie.</p>

<p>Her face bore no feral features. In place of a beastly jaw, she had full pink lips and a small, refined nose. Her round face carried an adorable, almost angelic charm.</p>

<p>Her blue eyes shimmered like clear gemstones, and white locks of hair tumbled in soft waves over her slender shoulders.</p>

<p>Two striped ears stood alert atop her head, while behind her, a white tail marked with the same dark patterns swayed lazily through the air.</p>

<p>Her skin, too, was unique. It resembled a kind of oily latex, devoid of the typical coat of a tigress yet boasting an incomparable softness, without so much as a single strand of fur to be found upon it.</p>

<p>The tone was paper-white, streaked with dark stripes that traced a path down her voluptuous thighs, curved across the generous swell of her wide hips, and climbed her slim torso to frame the full, heavy weight of her breasts.</p>

<p>She reclined languidly upon a pile of silk cushions, wearing a dress that covered only the bare essentials, the thin fabric wrapped tight around her body.</p>

<p>The cloth ran down the valley between her huge tits, the material straining to contain the soft, paper-white mounds beneath. A black sash cinched the dress at her slender waist, before the fabric slid down to slip between her thighs.</p>

<p>At that very moment, her eyes took on a disinterested expression, while an arrogant young master, his gaze brimming with passion, presented himself before her.</p>

<p>“Young Miss Bai Yunxi, I, Feng Wuji, the unstoppable White Sword, the greatest genius of this generation, am here to take your hand in marriage and free you from that weakling, Long Qingyi!” he declared, chest puffed out with pride.</p>

<p>Bai Yunxi did not respond right away.</p>

<p>She sank her teeth into the chicken thigh in her hand, taking a greedy bite before lifting her gaze to Feng Wuji with pure contempt.</p>

<p>She had not the slightest interest in marrying just any human.</p>

<p>Unlike her father and brother, she wasn’t obsessed with strength, yet she still belonged to the race of beasts.</p>

<p>Humans weren’t weak in general, but when compared to a worthy dragon?</p>

<p>It was hard to put them on the same level.</p>

<p>“Are you done yet?” she asked, opening her mouth wide and tearing off another generous portion of meat.</p>

<p>There was a certain charm in the way she ate. Still, next to a woman like Rouxi, she seemed little more than just another glutton.</p>

<p>Well, a beautiful and elegant glutton.</p>

<p>“If I’m done? I’m Feng Wuji, your original betrothed! And the agreement between our sects…”</p>

<p>“Hm… I don’t care about that agreement,” she said, drawing each greasy finger between her lips and licking it clean with indifference, her pink tongue lingering at every digit.</p>

<p>“It was your father who signed it!”</p>

<p>“Okay…”</p>

<p>“My master has signed it too!”</p>

<p>“He seems like a very supportive master.”</p>

<p>Feng Wuji nearly went mad.</p>

<p>“Don’t you understand the weight of a stellar sovereign’s word?”</p>

<p>“I understand…”</p>

<p>“Then why—”</p>

<p>It was at that moment that Bai Yunxi finally let go of the chicken leg, turning her gaze directly to Feng Wuji.</p>

<p>“Do I need to have my guards throw you out? I don’t want to be seen next to another man while I wait for the husband my big brother chose for me.”</p>

<p>“You… you really don’t care about marrying a man you’ve never even met in your life? What if he’s a pathetic bastard?”</p>

<p>“If he were a pathetic bastard, my brother would never have chosen him. Why do you think Bai Hu Lie ignored you completely all these years, treating you like an insect?”</p>

<p>Bai Yunxi smiled, and at that very moment, Feng Wuji’s fury exploded.</p>

<p>He had endured such disrespect for far too long.</p>

<p>His hand shot up to the hilt of his sword, only to freeze there in midair.</p>

<p>“Draw that sword.”</p>

<p>Yunxi’s eyes sharpened, and in the span of a single heartbeat she rose and loomed before Feng Wuji, her huge tits bouncing heavily from the sudden motion, the soft mounds rolling against the strained fabric of her dress.</p>

<p>Her mouth opened to reveal human teeth that swiftly lengthened into sharp fangs.</p>

<p>Her fingers clamped down on the wrist of the hand resting against the weapon.</p>

<p>“I dare you, you little bitch. Draw that sword right in my face. Show me you’ve got the guts!”</p>

<p>Her voice rumbled out like a deep growl rising from the depths of her chest, and her fingers tightened with brutal force.</p>

<p>Feng Wuji shuddered, feeling as though his own wrist were about to crumble to dust beneath the crushing pressure of that grip.</p>

<p>To many, she was just a more delicate version of her brother, but at the end of the day, she was still a tigress, the daughter of a tiger.</p>

<p>How could such a creature be just a little kitten?</p>

<p>Another thing many people didn’t know was that, despite being treated as Bai Hu Lie’s little sister, Bai Yunxi was actually far older than him.</p>

<p>Basically, she only allowed him to call her little sister because she enjoyed the treatment, just as she enjoyed being seen as a delicate young lady.</p>

<p>Feng Wuji trembled as Bai Yunxi’s terrifying aura descended upon him like a storm.</p>

<p>That crazy woman was at least an entire cultivation realm above him!</p>

<p>For a moment, his eyes filled with sheer terror.</p>

<p>His hand throbbed with excruciating pain, and he could feel that just a little more pressure would snap it clean in two.</p>

<p>With an irritated grunt, Bai Yunxi simply kicked him away.</p>

<p>The young master of the Divine White Sword Sect tore through the air at terrifying speed, slamming into the wall with a muffled thud that sent dust raining from the rafters and shook the entire pavilion to its foundations.</p>

<p>“Get out of my sight, pig,” she spat, her once sweet and lovely voice now laced with pure murderous intent.</p>

<p>Feng Wuji cast aside every shred of arrogance he had been carrying, scrambling off with his tail between his legs.</p>

<p>During the journey to the palace, he had arrogantly declared that he would kill Qingyi with a single move, but such arrogance had earned him nothing.</p>

<p>Obviously, he hadn’t given up yet. Quite the contrary.</p>

<p>His eyes held a murderous glint.</p>

<p>He would isolate himself and train until the day of the tournament.</p>

<p>If she wanted that Qingyi so badly, he would deliver him to her, broken and crippled!</p>
