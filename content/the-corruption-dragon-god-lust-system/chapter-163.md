<p>Twelve people stood in front of the statue of a dragon whose scales were scarlet red.</p>

<p>One of them was the powerful figure of Qing Shuyin next to the curvaceous silhouette of his daughter, Qing Ruyan.</p>

<p>Next to them were another elder and disciple of the Serene Cloud Sect, along with the two elders and then disciples of the Burning Heart Sect.</p>

<p>Qingyi and Qingxue were on the left of the group, next to the elder Chen Wutian and the direct disciple Feng Zhenhai from the Ascending Dragon Sect.</p>

<p>In total, there were six disciples from the peak of the Golden Core realm and six elders from the peak of the Iron Body realm.</p>

<p>That was a power big enough to take down a medium-sized sect, but it seemed small compared to the figures surrounding them.</p>

<p>“You look beautiful today, elder Qingxue,” Wutian smiled at the silver-haired beauty, but she didn’t even turn towards him, her eyes focused straight ahead, cold and serene.</p>

<p>The old man could only focus on the statue in front of him as well, gritting his teeth.</p>

<p>Qingyi obviously realized this, his fists clenched tightly, but soon relaxed.</p>

<p>That damned old man was obviously begging for death, but Qingyi couldn’t go around killing everyone for a simple compliment.</p>

<p>As long as the old man didn’t cross the line, he wouldn’t either.</p>

<p>Hiding the anger in his heart, he turned to the figure of the leader of the scarlet dragon elders.</p>

<p>“Greetings, young disciples and respectable elders,” he spoke, imbuing his voice with Qi and causing it to spread throughout Scarlet Dragon City.</p>

<p>His speech was short, brief words of encouragement followed by a few instructions.</p>

<p>With a big smile on his lips, he snapped his fingers.</p>

<p>The next moment, the statue moved, seized by an intense Qi as its huge head lowered and its mouth opened, revealing a blood-red portal.</p>

<p>Shuyin was the first to step forward, Ruyan following soon after.</p>

<p>Only when their bodies disappeared into the portal did the others follow.</p>

<p>“Let’s go,” Qingxue spoke as she took a light, elegant step forward, her hand resting on the hilt of her swords.</p>

<p>The moment they passed through the portal, they felt the air around them change.</p>

<p>Qingxue’s long, silky silver hair fluttered as she was hit by a strong, humid breeze.</p>

<p>She took a step back, her head hitting Qingyi’s broad chest and making her shiver and blush softly.</p>

<p>“That’s a hell of a view, hahaha,” one of the elders of the Burning Heart Sect, a burly and honest man, smiled as he looked at the horizon.</p>

<p>They were at the top of a rock formation, high above the clouds.</p>

<p>A waterfall fell above them, descending into a green abyss.</p>

<p>No one there could deny that it was a beautiful sight, their eyes lost in the great forests that stretched to the horizon of clouds made golden by the setting sun.</p>

<p>“We have to run,” Qingyi said, activating his draconic vision.</p>

<p>“Uh? What do you mean? Junior Brother Qingyi-” Ruxue stopped her own words, her eyes twitching.</p>

<p>Just ahead of the sun, she saw a small dot rapidly approaching.</p>

<p>This small dot soon turned into dozens and then hundreds.</p>

<p>They were flying beasts.</p>

<p>The sheer number didn’t frighten any of the elders, but the moment they felt the cultivations of those creatures, they couldn’t help but feel their hearts sink.</p>

<p>Each of those beasts was at the Iron Body realm, with their leader, an eagle with a wingspan of more than twenty meters, only a single step away from the Burning Blood realm.</p>

<p>“Run!” One word and all those proud elders abandoned their dignity.</p>

<p>Chen Wutian was the first to run, activating his movement technique at maximum speed as he passed under the waterfall.</p>

<p>“There’s a door over there!” He said, without even realizing that Qingxue and even Qingyi had already passed him.</p>

<p>The silver-haired beauty passed through the door, waiting for everyone to enter. The last to arrive were Chen Wutian and his disciple.</p>

<p>The old man was forced to grab Zhenhai by the collar of his shirt and throw him towards the living room.</p>

<p>Zhenhai couldn’t hide a startled cry as he was thrown, but that cry was soon replaced by a bright look as he realized where he was going.</p>

<p>That route… would Elder Qingxue catch him?</p>

<p>He closed his eyes, hoping to reach the elder’s soft, snowy peaks, but unfortunately for him, Qingyi stepped aside at the last moment.</p>

<p>What Zhenhai hit was a masculine chest, broad and firm.</p>

<p>With Qingyi contracting every one of his muscles, the sensation Zhenhai experienced was not unlike hitting a concrete wall.</p>

<p>“Is everything all right, young master Zhenhai?” Qingyi flashed a broad smile.</p>

<p>Honestly, as well as defending his wife’s dignity, he had also saved Zhenhai’s life.</p>

<p>He was pretty sure that idiot would be cut into a thousand pieces if he came into contact with Qingxue.</p>

<p>“Y-yes,” Zhenhai stood up hastily, unable to hold back a silent curse.</p>

<p>“Close the door, quick!” Chen Wutian roared as he passed through the steel door.</p>

<p>Shuyin acted quickly, his powerful muscles almost jumping out of his shirt as he closed the door with a bang.</p>

<p>At that moment, they finally had time to look around.</p>

<p>They were in an armory of sorts, surrounded by shelves full of ancient weapons and armor.</p>

<p>Qingxue picked up a sword curiously, but to her surprise, the blade fell apart in her fingers.</p>

<p>Everything there seemed to have been consumed by time.</p>

<p>“You’ve got good eyesight, kid, and fast feet, hahaha!” Shuyin finally relaxed, not feeling the creatures outside anymore.</p>

<p>They seemed to have given up following them as soon as the door closed.</p>

<p>“The marriage proposal with my daughter still stands,” he smiled, slapping Qingyi hard on the shoulder.</p>

<p>“Dad!” Ruyan called out, blushing deeply.</p>

<p>Qingyi was about to reply when he felt his master’s piercing gaze on his back.</p>

<p>’Is that woman jealous?’ he thought, unable to stop himself from smiling softly at the possibility.</p>

<p>After a moment’s thought, he just ignored the other men’s envious glances.</p>

<p>This was no time for jokes, they had to continue with the secret realm.</p>
