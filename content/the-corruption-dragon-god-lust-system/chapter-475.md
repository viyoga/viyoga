<p>The first of the creatures appeared: a giant wind-horned wolf.</p>

<p>It was barely three meters long, but its power in a pack was something even Lucas couldn’t ignore.</p>

<p>Of course, to Qingyi, it wasn’t much.</p>

<p>They were just insignificant base predators looking for easy prey, the most powerful of them barely reaching the level of a false immortal.</p>

<p>Qingyi jumped out of the carriage, ready to cut down the first ten in front of him.</p>

<p>Unfortunately, he had to stop at the last second.</p>

<p>His feet sank into the muddy ground and an intense heat rose up behind him, rapidly approaching.</p>

<p>It was Lucas’s dragon.</p>

<p>Its huge mouth was open while its master flashed a sadistic smile, orange, burning flames falling onto the ground.</p>

<p>This was no accident; Lucas was aiming at Qingyi.</p>

<p>Qingyi just formed a Qi barrier around himself, realizing that the beasts were already surrounding the carriage.</p>

<p>With a single movement of his eyes, his body disappeared.</p>

<p>When he reappeared, he was already above the carriage, exactly ten blades of Qi leaving his body and falling on the wolves.</p>

<p>Groans and growls full of agony echoed as severed paws, tails, and canine heads fell to the ground.</p>

<p>Just ahead, Lucas’s dragon’s flame swallowed more than half of the pack.</p>

<p>“Ugh… better get rid of these damn things first,” Qingyi whispered irritably, as his body was swallowed by another spatial fold.</p>

<p>He didn’t truly care about the death of those miners, but if he could defend them without losing anything in return, why wouldn’t he?</p>

<p>The lives of those who were not his enemies were worth more than a minor inconvenience to him.</p>

<p>In a single second, he appeared beside the remaining wolves.</p>

<p>Flying blades cut through flesh with ease, exterminating them as if they were nothing more than ants.</p>

<p>When the last one fell to the ground, Qingyi couldn’t help but smile with satisfaction, lazily cracking his neck.</p>

<p>He had shown more power than he wanted to and, most certainly, than was necessary.</p>

<p>But for him, what mattered most was that he had gotten a good warm-up.</p>

<p>He turned toward the miners, just in time to see, with his guard down, the flames of Lucas’s dragon engulfing them as the beast tore through the air.</p>

<p>“Die, you bastard!” Lucas roared, not caring about the consequences.</p>

<p>They were just a few random commoners.</p>

<p>As long as there were no witnesses, nothing had happened, right? A smile full of amusement dominated his sadistic face as his dragon landed, unleashing its flames toward Qingyi without stopping.</p>

<p>Neither of them noticed that, during all this, Qingyi was completely still.</p>

<p>His expression was relaxed, and only a thin layer of Qi protected his clothes as he was engulfed by the flames.</p>

<p>“Hm… this is a very pleasant sauna…” Qingyi grunted, an annoyed smile taking over his handsome masculine face.</p>

<p>“Khaedryss, how about an extra hearty meal today?”</p>

<p>“Uh?” Lucas’s eyes widened upon hearing those words.</p>

<p>He barely paid attention to what was said, shocked only by the fact that Qingyi was alive.</p>

<p>By the time he finally had the capacity to realize what was happening, it was already too late.</p>

<p>His dragon turned, taking to the skies with desperate flaps of its wings.</p>

<p>Where its flames fell, a thick veil of white smoke and steam from the muddy ground began to rise, hiding Qingyi within.</p>

<p>“What are you doing? Stay and fight!” Lucas shouted, slapping his dragon’s neck.</p>

<p>He didn’t even notice when the white smoke covering Qingyi parted with a bang, black scales like the night glistening in the sunlight.</p>

<p>A jaw, so large that Lucas’s dragon was barely enough to fill it, opened.</p>

<p>A powerful, armored neck stretched out as endless rows of sharp teeth closed around Lucas and his mount.</p>

<p>Only then did Lucas feel the pain, a pain he had never felt in his entire life.</p>

<p>As he lost feeling in his legs and was thrown away, still clinging to his mount’s head, he understood why his mount was fleeing.</p>

<p>Behind him was a huge beast, one he only recognized from statues and stories of past glory.</p>

<p>The creature’s roar caused extreme terror in the young elf’s eyes.</p>

<p>Khaedryss barely paid attention to Lucas, voraciously devouring his dragon.</p>

<p>When she was finished, she lowered her head, her jaw opening to let out a long, angry growl.</p>

<p>“You know, brat…” Qingyi jumped on top of Khaedryss’s head, watching Lucas.</p>

<p>There was no more arrogance on his face, and his legs had been replaced by nothing but a bloody mess, devoured by the black dragon.</p>

<p>“You could have had a long life, enjoying your family’s countless riches, fucking beautiful whores, and drinking fine wine,” Qingyi said.</p>

<p>Khaedryss’s lips parted, revealing sharp teeth, and a strange black light emerged from the depths of her throat.</p>

<p>Lucas despaired, his pants dampening with urine as he screamed and begged.</p>

<p>For the first time in his entire life, he was in mortal danger. Real mortal danger.</p>

<p>Unfortunately for him, Khaedryss did not listen to pleas.</p>

<p>Her flames engulfed everything: clothes, weapons, even the decapitated head of his mount.</p>

<p>Everything was vaporized into nothing but ashes by the flames of the butcher of empires and slayer of immortals.</p>

<p>When she was done, Khaedryss raised her face and then opened her jaws.</p>

<p>Her thunderous roar echoed for countless kilometers, the surrounding forest shaking.</p>

<p>She could taste that air, taste that blood, one she hadn’t tasted in many, many years.</p>

<p>She was back home.</p>

<p>“Good girl…” Qingyi smiled, patting Khaedryss on the neck.</p>

<p>She had more than doubled in size compared to when he first found her, reaching ninety meters in length and weighing close to a hundred tons.</p>

<p>It was even a little comical for Qingyi, so small in comparison to her, to stroke her scales.</p>

<p>Still, she enjoyed it, feeling the warm, affectionate touch of her rider.</p>

<p>“Return to the world of the mind. Soon the time will come for you to bathe in the blood of your enemies and devour Karlan’s heart.”</p>

<p>As he spoke those last words, Qingyi noticed a unique excitement take hold of Khaedryss’s face, one that he found strangely amusing. Apparently, she was also excited about the idea of eating Karlan’s heart.</p>
