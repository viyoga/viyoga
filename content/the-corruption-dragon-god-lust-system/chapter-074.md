<p>Bluey trotted lightly along a short but well-tended trail.</p>

<p>Yueli, still panting and flushed, allowed herself to sink into Qingyi’s arms, her head resting against his chest.</p>

<p>Her body felt strange, but not in a bad way. A subtle warmth seized her chest while her lips were unable to hide their shy smile.</p>

<p>Qingyi’s hands circled her slender waist, flowing down to her lean, delicate belly before moving up to her breasts.</p>

<p>He grasped the twin peaks with both hands, feeling the soft, creamy skin as his fingers sank into the soft flesh.</p>

<p>“Mmm…” She sighed softly and closed her eyes.</p>

<p>It was comfortable. She wouldn’t mind staying there for the rest of her life.</p>

<p>“What would you do? If you really managed to conquer the lightning spirit?” she asked, breaking the silence between them.</p>

<p>Qingyi thought for a moment before answering: “I would dominate the continent before transcending to the upper heavens.”</p>

<p>“Upper heavens? You mean the immortal heaven?” Yueli’s eyes twitched.</p>

<p>“Yes, and what’s above it.” The handsome young man smiled. “What, do you think your husband is too ambitious?”</p>

<p>“Hm… no, I like ambition…” Yueli returned his smile, but her face soon turned serious.</p>

<p>“The master has always dreamed of ascending, of going beyond the mortal sky and spreading the cult of the spirit of lightning throughout the world…” Her voice lost a little of its strength.</p>

<p>“Ah…” Qingyi exhaled heavily as he hugged her even tighter.</p>

<p>“You have no resentment? Of the lightning spirit, of what it did to your master and to… well, all the others who tried to conquer it.”</p>

<p>“Sometimes…” Yueli snuggled a little closer, her beautiful blue eyes misty.</p>

<p>“But I know there’s a reason behind it, that she would never hurt us if she had a choice…”</p>

<p>“I understand…” Qingyi shook his head and didn’t go any further.</p>

<p>It was something painful for her, he didn’t need much to realize it.</p>

<p>The journey didn’t take long, with Bluey hurrying her steps as the trail opened up into a wide road.</p>

<p>Soon, Qingyi saw a cluster of houses on the horizon.</p>

<p>It was a small village, with no more than a few hundred inhabitants, surrounded by golden fields of rice.</p>

<p>The qi was even thinner than in the temple, to the point that just standing there was enough to make a cultivator uncomfortable.</p>

<p>“Oh… Lady Qin!” An old woman sitting in front of the village gate stood up, her eyes widening at the sight of Qingyi just behind Yueli.</p>

<p>Everyone in the village knew Qin Yueli.</p>

<p>She visited the place once every few months, bought tons of supplies and then disappeared.</p>

<p>But she had always been alone.</p>

<p>Everyone knew about the temple, an isolated place where only women entered, but even those women never accompanied Yueli there.</p>

<p>Yet there she was, accompanied by a handsome man who gripped her hips as if she were everything to him.</p>

<p>Yueli ignored all the strange looks, continuing on with Qingyi until they finally reached the only shop in the village.</p>

<p>“Oh… esteemed cultivator, what can I do for you?” The store owner, a sly-looking old man with gray hair, bowed slightly as he clasped his hands together.</p>

<p>While Yueli shopped, Qingyi walked around the store.</p>

<p>It was a poor place, the products were mostly grains and some low-quality drinks.</p>

<p>As he walked past the shelves, something finally caught his eye.</p>

<p>It was a bottle of wine which, even closed, exuded a strong, alcoholic smell.</p>

<p>Taking it in his hands, Qingyi studied it.</p>

<p>It was clearly a high-quality wine, unlike the cheap liquors that the old man sold in gourds.</p>

<p>“I see you have good eyes, young cultivator! This is royal wine, I got it more than ten years ago when a prince visited our village! It’s been waiting for an owner ever since, hehehe.”</p>

<p>The old man laughed, his eyes filling with greed as he looked at Qingyi’s clothes.</p>

<p>They were clean, made of a soft, pure silk. Qingyi must have had a lot of money!</p>

<p>“How much?” the handsome young man asked, studying the bottle for a few more moments.</p>

<p>“Just 15 gold coins, hehehe!”</p>

<p>Hearing that price, Qingyi shuddered.</p>

<p>15 gold coins? That damned old man was trying to rob him in broad daylight!</p>

<p>He could buy spirit wine, of a quality hundreds of times better, for 12 gold coins in the City of Golden Clouds!</p>

<p>“Oh… has the price gone down?” Yueli’s eyes lit up at the old man’s words.</p>

<p>“Do you want me to buy it for you? There’ll still be a bit left over after I’ve bought the supplies for the girls.”</p>

<p>“No need…” Qingyi shook his head, a little awkwardly.</p>

<p>He had forgotten that he was no longer in the Ascending Dragon Sect.</p>

<p>Now, he was in an even more isolated place, where the ordinary wines he could find in the sect would be priceless treasures.</p>

<p>Sighing, he bought a few gold coins from the system, handing them to the old man before taking the wine.</p>

<p>As he walked back to Yueli’s side, he observed the supplies being placed on the floor by the store staff.</p>

<p>Tons, not just of grains and fruit, but mainly of liquors.</p>

<p>“Do you really need that much?” he asked, looking at the barrels and barrels of liquor piling up.</p>

<p>“Honestly, it’s a bit lacking…” Yueli smiled awkwardly.</p>

<p>The priestesses at the temple drank a lot, and she couldn’t deny that she was partly to blame for that.</p>

<p>When the employees had finished putting everything away, Yueli took out a small bag from her robes, opening it and shaking it gently before a huge suction power came out of its entrance, pulling all the supplies inside.</p>

<p>“All set?” Qingyi asked, little surprised.</p>

<p>That was a space bag, very similar to space rings, only of a much lower price and quality.</p>

<p>“Yes…” Yueli ducked her head as they left the store and headed out of the village.</p>

<p>She didn’t like the place or the people there very much.</p>

<p>They weren’t honest and the men always looked at her disgustingly.</p>

<p>Sighing heavily, her beautiful eyes focused on Qingyi as she grabbed Bluey’s reins.</p>

<p>Her lips opened slightly, but stopped the next second.</p>

<p>Qingyi’s face distorted and a powerful aura took over his body as he advanced towards the forest like a blur.</p>

<p>“Fuck!” A painful grunt echoed as Qingyi’s hands closed around a man’s neck.</p>

<p>He was bald, deep-set and wearing a red jacket.</p>

<p>“The Red Claw gang!” Yueli recognized him immediately.</p>
