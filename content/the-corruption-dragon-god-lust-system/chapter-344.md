<p>“Ah… are you a cat or a vampire?” Qingyi asked; he wasn’t angry, just a little frustrated, his voice dripping with amusement.</p>

<p>Raising his arms, he saw a girl with short orange hair and bright eyes clinging to him.</p>

<p>Her nails left red marks on his skin as her fangs sank into his embrace.</p>

<p>Her back was arched, her ass rising gloriously as Qingyi’s arm sank into the valley between her breasts.</p>

<p>“Nyan? What’s a vampire?” Linyue asked, stopping biting Qingyi’s arm.</p>

<p>She left some deep marks, but he didn’t care much.</p>

<p>Qingyi just flexed his muscles slightly, pumping Qi into his arm. A single second, and all the marks disappeared.</p>

<p>“Creatures of the night, bloodsuckers,” he said, patting Linyue on the ass, grabbing it for a brief moment.</p>

<p>They were now in the palace of the cat people’s patriarch and, fortunately for Qingyi, there wasn’t much ceremony.</p>

<p>Of course, the wedding hadn’t taken place yet and they seemed to be planning quite a big party, but he honestly wasn’t worried about that.</p>

<p>He took the time to talk to the cat girl, introduce her to his other wives and to the forests of the world of the mind, where she would soon be able to hunt freely.</p>

<p>Ruxue even said that, if he could gather enough of her fragments, he could raise his own spirit beasts in the world of the mind, using the place basically as a breeding ground.</p>

<p>Naturally, this would be good for both Linyue, who loved to hunt, and Khaedryss, who needed to eat to get strong.</p>

<p>‘Speaking of Khaedryss…’ Qingyi closed his eyes for a brief moment, entering the world of the mind and observing a large figure on the horizon, just outside the temple.</p>

<p>Her black scales were bathed in blood as she flipped the steel-shelled turtle onto its back, tearing open the lower shell which was hard, but still far inferior to the upper one, surrounded by steel.</p>

<p>Khaedryss raised her head slightly, watching Qingyi for a brief moment before going back to eating.</p>

<p>The handsome young man just sighed.</p>

<p>That was the best prey Khaedryss had eaten since he had tamed her. He was really interested to know how much her power would increase after absorbing it all.</p>

<p>Of course, perhaps giving the carcass to Feiyan to make pills would be even better, but at the moment, Qingyi’s focus was on increasing Khaedryss’ power.</p>

<p>She was still barely comparable to a flowing meridian realm cultivator, something that was simply unacceptable to Qingyi.</p>

<p>After observing her for a brief moment, he left the world of the mind, immediately grabbing Linyue and pulling her into his embrace. He stroked her hair, listening to the soft purr coming from her lips.</p>

<p>Unfortunately, they couldn’t stay like this forever and soon, Qingyi asked Linyue to show him some cultivation rooms he could use here.</p>

<p>Obviously, a family as powerful as hers had a multitude of high-level cultivation rooms, many with multipliers of up to 100 -fold.</p>

<p>Considering the basic density of the Qi in this place, it was simply absurd.</p>

<p>After locking himself in that place, Qingyi immediately sat down cross-legged, looking at her status screen.</p>

<p>[Name: Long Qingyi</p>

<p>Age: 21</p>

<p>Cultivation: First stage of the flowing meridian realm (15,000,000,000 lust points to upgrade)</p>

<p>Affinities: Lightning: 248 – Sword: 257 – Fire: 234 – Ice: 223 – Charm: 1000 (can be upgraded in the lust store)</p>

<p>Spiritual roots: Sky-level three-colored spirit root (Lightning sword, fire, ice) (10,000,000,000 lust points to upgrade, new roots can be bought in the lust store).</p>

<p>Lineage: Blood of the Corruption Dragon God (Gold grade, 10,000,000,000 lust points to upgrade.)</p>

<p>Constitution: Primordial Dragon Body (Gold grade, 10,000,000,000 lust points to upgrade.)</p>

<p>Lust points: 21,302,289,541]</p>

<p>Looking at those figures, Qingyi could only exhale heavily.</p>

<p>It was truly absurd, Linyue gave more than three times as much as Margareth when he took her purity.</p>

<p>After thinking for a brief moment, Qingyi set himself three objectives for this cultivation session: to prepare his base for the next breakthrough, to improve his lineage and, above all, to improve all his affinities to level 500.</p>

<p>After reflecting briefly, he decided to take the first step, clicking to improve his constitution and lineage at the same time.</p>

<p>[Initiating lineage improvement…]</p>

<p>[Initiating constitution improvement…]</p>

<p>Two notifications reached Qingyi simultaneously, followed by a terrible, deep pain, this time totally focused on his eyes.</p>

<p>He lost his sight, his mind entering a world of endless darkness as terrible discomfort gripped his eyes.</p>

<p>He could feel rivers of blood running down his cheeks, bathing his clothes as the pain became more and more intense, spreading to the rest of his meridians.</p>

<p>The strands of draconic Qi in his dantian multiplied again, reaching just over two thousand, their density so high that counting them was simply no longer efficient.</p>

<p>Only after about ten minutes did Qingyi finally manage to see again, his vision still remaining slightly blurred for a few brief seconds before focusing.</p>

<p>[Lineage improved! Gold grade -&gt; Earth grade]</p>

<p>[Constitution improved! Gold grade -&gt; Earth grade]</p>

<p>[Draconic eyes reached maximum level, the inheritance has been completed…]</p>

<p>A flurry of notifications reached Qingyi, but he paid no attention to any of it, focusing his gaze on the furthest part of that room, more than fifty meters away.</p>

<p>He could see it clearly. A grain of dust, small, difficult to detect even centimeters away.</p>

<p>Yet he could see it clearly, as if it were resting on his fingers.</p>

<p>Even the small imperfections in the black bricks, which had previously seemed perfectly smooth, were perfectly visible.</p>

<p>Not only that, at that very moment, his eyes were filled with draconic Qi, bit not the draconic Qi of his Dantian.</p>

<p>This was a Qi that seemed to be imbued innately in his eyes, with only a tenuous connection to the one in his Dantian, just like the Qi that flowed naturally in his meridians.</p>

<p>With a little effort, Qingyi slowly got used to the sight, needing little more than two minutes to finally understand how it all worked.</p>

<p>It was honestly like a game, a mental switch where he could change the form of his vision, keeping it normal or enhanced, now without having to actively move his Qi to do so.</p>

<p>Focusing his vision, he observed the lines of Qi that cut through the air and even those visible beyond the walls.</p>

<p>It was much better than before and he could see the Qi lines clearly, even from miles away.</p>

<p>With a sigh, Qingyi closed his eyes, calming his breathing.</p>

<p>Now it was time to start improving his affinities.</p>
