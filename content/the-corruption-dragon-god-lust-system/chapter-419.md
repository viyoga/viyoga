<p>A few weeks after Belgrath’s death and the recapture of the great fortress in the north, the kingdom finally began to stabilize.</p>

<p>Soon, the wedding date between Qingyi and Isabel was set.</p>

<p>Naturally, such an announcement brought much celebration to the entire capital, but that was not what Qingyi was concerned with at the moment.</p>

<p>Above one of the largest and most prosperous port cities in the entire kingdom of Valemont, Qingyi’s figure floated subtly, his purple eyes sweeping over the city below him.</p>

<p>At his side was Meilin, her beautiful dark eyes shining as her elegant, noble face was filled with interest.</p>

<p>“What do you think of this place?” Qingyi asked, wrapping his hands around her delicate waist and moving down slightly, giving her perfect, plump bubble-butt a hard slap.</p>

<p>“Nghnn~~ it’s good… Meilin replied, blushing softly. “If we can connect it to the central pavilion in Eagle Peak Valley and start flowing eastern goods here and western goods there…”</p>

<p>Just thinking about it was enough to make Meilin salivate.</p>

<p>There was simply no supply of imported goods from the western continent to the eastern one, nor vice versa, at least not outside the capitals of the great empires that ruled each continent.</p>

<p>The elite of both lands would surely spend fortunes to get their hands on such exotic items.</p>

<p>If she could expand further into the kingdom of Valemont, she would likely have enough products to supply entire nations on the eastern continent.</p>

<p>Seeing the expression that took over Meilin’s face, Qingyi couldn’t help but laugh slightly. “Did you like your husband’s gift?!”</p>

<p>“Yes, hubby is the best!” Meilin exclaimed, throwing herself into Qingyi’s arms and kissing him.</p>

<p>Honestly, she had never thought much about it, but she could already imagine the faces of her other siblings when she visited her father with Qingyi.</p>

<p>They would probably be dying of envy, especially of the gifts she would receive from her father for such an achievement.</p>

<p>He was the kind of man who loved to reward his children’s competence.</p>

<p>Pushing that thought out of her mind, she focused her eyes. “Can we take a look at the territory ceded by His Majesty David Valemont now?”</p>

<p>“Yes.” Qingyi nodded and, with a smooth movement, flew toward the city’s pier area, landing next to one of the few empty lots in that area.</p>

<p>It was large, with just over twenty thousand square meters of free space, more than enough for the establishment of a commercial building for a large chamber of commerce.</p>

<p>Naturally, such land would cost a fortune, but Qingyi received it as a gift from the royal family, just as he received the full funds for the construction of the pavilion itself and for hiring staff.</p>

<p>Despite this, on this last point, Meilin preferred to bring people from the eastern continent.</p>

<p>Of course, she needed employees from this continent as well, but in the end, she would not be here to micromanage operations and needed to bring some of her trusted subordinates to run everything there.</p>

<p>Exchanging glances, Qingyi and Meilin prepared to leave when a distant voice interrupted them.</p>

<p>Their arrival had already caused some commotion among the commoners and workers who came and went along the wide street</p>

<p>where the couple had landed.</p>

<p>Their strange clothes and transcendental beauty naturally attract attention.</p>

<p>This commotion only intensified when, on the horizon, a figure appeared: a middle-aged woman of ordinary and noble appearance, surrounded by soldiers in silver armor.</p>

<p>She was Duchess Mary, from the prosperous port city of Bluestorm,</p>

<p>where Qingyi and Meilin were.</p>

<p>“Your Highness… to what do I owe your visit to my lands?” Mary asked, bowing gently.</p>

<p>“You know why I am here.” Qingyi smiled.</p>

<p>“Yes, I do, hahaha.” Mary returned Qingyi’s smile.</p>

<p>Honestly, she had a lot of admiration for this young man, especially</p>

<p>since her husband had been one of those who died in the fight</p>

<p>against Belgrath.</p>

<p>Knowing that he had avenged her husband certainly made her see him in a much more positive light.</p>

<p>“His Majesty has already contacted me, and I have arranged everything necessary to begin construction, but we would need your signature for a few things, in addition to, of course, drawing up the plans for the building,” Mary said, her eyes shifting from Qingyi to</p>

<p>Meilin.</p>

<p>She didn’t know that Qingyi had other wives and couldn’t help but feel a little uncomfortable about it.</p>

<p>Qingyi was openly with another woman just a few days before his marriage to Isabel?</p>

<p>Both Qingyi and Meilin noticed the discomfort, but didn’t pay much attention to it.</p>

<p>Neither of them could care less about Mary’s opinion.</p>

<p>Shortly after this brief conversation, Qingyi and Meilin accompanied Mary to the mansion of the Lord of the city, where the daughter of the King of Gold was able to create a design for the construction of the main building of her chamber of commerce.</p>

<p>She had to, of course, adapt the architecture considerably to create something that the builders of this world would be capable of, while preserving part of its original appearance and, above all, its</p>

<p>functionality.</p>

<p>Under the impressed eyes of Duchess Mary, Qingyi, and other city government officials, Meilin finished quickly.</p>

<p>Her sketch, which took only a few minutes, was superior to what any engineer in the kingdom could do in days.</p>

<p>Talent was truly a miraculous thing.</p>

<p>After arranging everything for construction, Meilin simply used a long-range communication talisman to send a message to the main branch, giving a set of instructions to the manager so that they could finally begin to integrate the branch that would be built in Valemont.</p>

<p>In addition, she also needed materials and training masters to build cultivation rooms, so she needed to ensure the masters’ journey to</p>

<p>that region.</p>

<p>When they finished organizing all of this, Qingyi and Meilin returned to the capital, where the handsome young man immediately locked</p>

<p>himself in a meditation room.</p>

<p>Glancing at his status screen, he smiled.</p>

<p>Naturally, he hadn’t been idle during the few days after killing</p>

<p>Belgrath, and now it was time to advance to the True Immortal Realm.</p>
