<p>Two system notifications arrived at Qingyi, his lust points being rapidly drained while a twinge of pain surged through his body.</p>

<p>He was, at this very moment, in an isolated region in the land of beastmen, thousands of kilometers away from the nearest settlement. <p>But now, it was strange.</p>

<p>Before, it was just an ethereal and transparent form, made only of Qi. But not anymore.</p>

<p>Mountains were turned to dust and molten magma rose, everything within a twenty-kilometer radius being taken over by nothing but pure destruction.</p>

<p>Three hundred meters long and with overwhelming power, the black dragon of Qingyi’s bloodline projection looked more like a god of war who had descended upon the earth.</p>

<p>“That’s…” Qingyi turned, watching his bloodline projection.</p>

<p>It seemed alive, right there, with so much power and strength that it was hard even for him to believe.</p>

<p>Unlike other breakthroughs, there was hardly any pain. At least not at a level that could make him even grunt.</p>

<p>And even if there was, the pain couldn’t bother him less in the face of that terrifying power.</p>

<p>Turning his face, he pointed a single finger toward a huge and powerful mountain, the only one that had resisted the destruction of his lineage breakthrough.</p>

<p>He extended the finger, gathering powerful thunderous Qi at its tip before releasing it all.</p>

<p>Accompanying this attack was another roar from the black dragon, even louder than the previous one. Its hellish suppression fell upon the mountain, turning it to dust as the horizon lit up with the explosion.</p>

<p>It was as if a second sun had just been created on earth.</p>

<p>Of course, despite everything, Qingyi was not the only one preparing for something big.</p>

<p>In the brightly lit imperial capital, it was a calm and quiet night, with most of its inhabitants asleep.</p>

<p>But the emperor’s palace, on the other hand, was more awake than ever.</p>

<p>“How is the emperor’s hand?” A voice asked, belonging to a woman sitting on a throne that appeared to be made of pure gold, her beautiful face covered by a mask.</p>

<p>The man beside that woman was equal, of equal power and wearing an almost identical mask.</p>

<p>These were the Empress and the Enlightened Emperor, the most powerful man and woman on the eastern continent.</p>

<p>“Well, my sovereign. We have finished the maintenance and prepared everything for a long journey, long enough to cross the sea of eternal blue,” a man replied, bowing below the two thrones.</p>

<p>His clothes were rich, his face was noble, and despite the hierarchy, he treated the monarchs of that empire with relative casualness. He was the greatest master builder of flying boats in the whole world, being primarily responsible for the design of the emperor’s hand. Although he was officially a member of the five-color pavilion and an employee of the king of gold, he had accumulated so much wealth and so much trust from the high imperial nobility that his position had long surpassed that of a mere employee.</p>

<p>If it weren’t for the Gold King’s direct funding for many of the major projects he had in mind, he would have already formed his own chamber of commerce focused solely on shipbuilding.</p>

<p>“Very well, you will be well rewarded for this.” The emperor smiled broadly, then stood up and walked over to the master builder. “Hahaha, I always am, my sovereign. Now tell me, why do you need the emperor’s hand in such a state?</p>

<p>Are you going to send it to deal with the resistance of the northern barbarians again? Or a foreign invasion?”</p>

<p>“Oh… nothing much, really, not even anything that is in our territory. You may leave now, I have things to settle with my wife.” The emperor patted the master builder on the shoulder.</p>

<p>His tone of voice was calmer and friendlier than before.</p>

<p>Still, the master builder shuddered, leaving immediately, his steps quicker than when he entered.</p>

<p>He had known the emperor long enough to know that this was not a good sign. What the hell were they planning with the Hand of God?</p>
