<p>With Qingyi's attention finally won over, Yuehan could at last focus on teaching.</p>

<p>"Do you know what earned me the title of Divine Sword Fairy?" she asked, forcing her voice to stay firm while fighting back the blush that insisted on rising to her cheeks.</p>

<p>"All I know is that it has something to do with your sword resonance," Qingyi replied.</p>

<p>Not that he could have known much more.</p>

<p>He came from the Mortal Heavens.</p>

<p>He probably knew less about the legend of the Divine Sword Fairy than any child growing up in the many cities of the Alliance. After all, those children spent their entire childhoods listening to stories about her.</p>

<p>"You're not wrong, but it wasn't simply because my sword resonance was very powerful," Yuehan said, shaking her head slightly.</p>

<p>"In truth, the vast majority of Celestial Emperors—even great sword cultivators like an Azure Blade Emperor—perceive resonance in a way similar to yours."</p>

<p>She raised her hand and lifted two fingers toward the sky.</p>

<p>At the tip of her index finger, a flame appeared—small, but hot enough to make the surrounding air tremble.</p>

<p>At the tip of her middle finger, a bolt of lightning crackled softly, leaving a faint scent of ozone in the air.</p>
