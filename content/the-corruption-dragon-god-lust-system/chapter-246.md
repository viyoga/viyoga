<p>Wuhen and Qingyi walked on in silence, the handsome young man gazing thoughtfully at the horizon.</p>

<p>He was worried, he wanted answers, but there were too many things on his mind.</p>

<p>The demon cult, Biyue’s situation, his own power.</p>

<p>With a sigh, he glanced at his status screen.</p>

<p>[Name: Long Qingyi</p>

<p>Age: 19</p>

<p>Cultivation: Eighth Stage of the Nascent Soul Realm (1.300,000 lust points to upgrade)</p>

<p>Affinities: Lightning 98 – Sword: 97 – Charm: 1000 (can be upgraded in the lust store)</p>

<p>Spiritual roots: Mortal-level three-colored spirit root (Lightning sword, fire, ice) (1,000,000 lust points to upgrade, new roots can be bought in the lust store).</p>

<p>Lineage: Blood of the Corruption Dragon God (Mythical grade, 10,000,000 lust points to upgrade.)</p>

<p>Constitution: Primordial Dragon Body (Mythical grade, 10,000,000 lust points to upgrade.)</p>

<p>Lust points: 18,653,093]</p>

<p>He already had almost enough to raise his lineage to the mortal grade, the highest grade of the mortal sky, the peak of the peak.</p>

<p>A few minutes or hours of dual cultivation with that voluptuous, cute little fox would get him that amount easily.</p>

<p>But beyond that, he wanted to improve his current affinity and also get affinities with fire and ice.</p>

<p>Unlike when he fused the lightning and sword roots, he simply didn’t receive any affinity for fire or ice.</p>

<p>Something he was naturally already feeling.</p>

<p>His efficiency in using these two types of Qi was at least a dozen times worse than with the Qi of the lightning sword.</p>

<p>Qingyi didn’t keep those thoughts in his mind for long. First, he had to meet Tang Biyue.</p>

<p>After making sure she was all right, he would isolate himself and prepare to reach the realm of rebirth and improve what he could.</p>

<p>“Looks like we’ve arrived.” Wuhen stopped his steps, turning to Qingyi. “I have my own things to do, good luck.”</p>

<p>“Same to you, senior.” Qingyi nodded.</p>

<p>Until recently, he had a rather negative view of the Zhao family and even considered them undeclared enemies.</p>

<p>But Wuhen had earned his respect.</p>

<p>He would live a long life if he continued this path.</p>

<p>Sighing wearily, Qingyi entered the palace of the council of elders.</p>

<p>The atmosphere was obviously not pleasant.</p>

<p>Hundreds of guards protected the entire main hall, while a few elders chatted in its center.</p>

<p>One of them looked over his shoulder, noticing Qingyi.</p>

<p>“Greetings, elder.” Qingyi bowed, drawing a smile from the old man.</p>

<p>“Oh… junior Qingyi, Yedu has already sent us a report on your mission! Tell me, did you really kill the heir to the demon cult?” The elder asked.</p>

<p>Those words immediately provoked gasps of shock from everyone there.</p>

<p>Especially from a tall man with golden eyes and beauty only slightly inferior to Qingyi.</p>

<p>It was the leader of the dragon battalion, Tianyu.</p>

<p>“Impossible!” He exclaimed, jumping down from the staircase connected to the central hall.</p>

<p>His body landed in front of Qingyi, the fine marble beneath his feet cracking.</p>

<p>“You didn’t beat that bastard! Even I’m no match for him!” Tianyu exclaimed, checking Qingyi’s cultivation.</p>

<p>Of course, seeing how much and how quickly Qingyi had evolved made his heart tremble, but he attributed it all to luck, nothing more.</p>

<p>“Don’t confuse your incompetence with my weakness, dragon battalion leader.” Qingyi didn’t back down, even all the murderous intent released by Tianyu was far from being able to make him tremble.</p>

<p>“Now, if you’ll excuse me, I have business with my battalion leader, Tang Biyue.” Qingyi took a step to the side, but Tianyu blocked in front of him.</p>

<p>“Do you really think she’ll want to meet you? She didn’t even allow me to meet her, what makes you think that-” Before Tianyu had even finished his sentence, a cold voice interrupted him.</p>

<p>“Young master Qingyi, Lady Tang requests that you go up to the war council room.” The owner of that voice was an elite guard from the Tang family.</p>

<p>He was dressed in long black leather robes encrusted with steel plates, his face covered by a dark black mask.</p>

<p>He was one of the Tang family patriarch’s personal guards, but in the current situation, he was assigned to protect possible heirs.</p>

<p>“How?” Tianyu paused as Qingyi walked past him.</p>

<p>“Battalion leader Tianyu, I ask you to restrain yourself.” The elder grabbed Tianyu’s shoulders, who, overcome with jealousy, ignored him completely.</p>

<p>“Don’t make me have to send a letter to your sect, asking for a new commander for the dragon battalion.” The elder continued, but was still ignored.</p>

<p>“Long Qingyi, I challenge you to a duel to the death!” Shouted Tianyu, his thunderous voice echoing throughout the orthodox alliance, the world around him shaking as the figure of a white dragon with golden eyes appeared on his back.</p>

<p>“Accept if you have the courage.”</p>

<p>The handsome young man stopped his steps.</p>

<p>He closed his eyes.</p>

<p>He calmed his breathing.</p>

<p>And then he finally turned around, not hiding even a little of his Qi either.</p>

<p>The figure of a black dragon appeared on his back, its power no less than that of Tianyu.</p>

<p>Naturally its size had been reduced since they were in an enclosed space, but its presence was still oppressive.</p>

<p>“If you wish to die such a death in front of one of your juniors, very well, I will grant it to you.”</p>

<p>“I don’t accept that, neither does the council of elders!” The elder stepped forward, placing himself between the two and releasing his Rebirth Realm Qi, trying to push them apart.</p>

<p>Fortunately, the elder’s intervention wasn’t necessary.</p>

<p>The lineage projection of both of them was suddenly broken, a disruptive Qi entering their bodies and stopping their circulation immediately, sending them to the ground.</p>

<p>At the large double doors of the hall, they saw the huge figure of a tiger, its flaming eyes focused on the two of them.</p>

<p>It was Yanxiao, guardian of the Eternal River Sect.</p>

<p>“Do you wish to fight to the death? Very well. I will grant you that chance and authorize this duel,” Yanxiao declared.</p>

<p>“Master Yanxiao, please reconsider, they are two of the most important fighting forces in the orthodox alliance.”</p>

<p>“They’ll just be more meat for the demon cult’s meat grinder when the real battle begins. Better they kill themselves now than during the war.” The large tiger shook his head.</p>

<p>His eyes focused on Tianyu, “The duel will be in a month, be prepared.” The tiger spoke, turning to Qingyi.</p>

<p>“Sort out everything you have to sort out with battalion leader Tang Biyue and then come and meet me, I have important matters to discuss with you.”</p>

<p>“Yes, guardian!”</p>
