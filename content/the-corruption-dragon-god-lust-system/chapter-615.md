<p>Guo Tianhao.</p>

<p>He wanted to advance to the Celestial Origin realm as quickly as possible and begin his race to become a core disciple. Only then could he, once and for all, take his beloved for himself.</p>

<p>But first, he needed a lot of contribution points.</p>
