<p>“Shit…” Sapphire growled, rising into the sky as the ground beneath her feet ceased to be solid.</p>

<p>Even Lucien couldn’t help but be taken by surprise, his eyes widening in shock as everything below him disappeared.</p>

<p>Qingyi’s blow created deep furrows in the earth and a crater that nearly swallowed the entrance to the mausoleum whole.</p>

<p>The defensive arrays collapsed and when the dust settled, Qingyi was more than twenty meters deep, his feet sinking into a bloody mess shaped like a woman’s head.</p>

<p>Her body convulsed, her soul destroyed by Qingyi, as well as her brain and, of course, her beautiful face.</p>

<p>Qingyi simply withdrew his foot, rising into the air.</p>

<p>“Ugh… did you really have to do it in such a disgusting way?” Sapphire asked, looking away from the blood-covered young man.</p>

<p>“No.” Qingyi shook his head. A slight smile spread across his lips as he stretched, watching the other cultists, who were in deadly silence.</p>

<p>Honestly, they were lucky.</p>

<p>If there had been even one Immortal Emperor there, they would have been exterminated in a single moment.</p>

<p>‘That’s not all…’ Qingyi repeated to himself.</p>

<p>It was impossible that such a miserable level of power would be enough to worry the emperor and even the church.</p>

<p>“This must have something to do with this procedure of being able to support a mana heart and a Dantian at the same time,’ Qingyi thought to himself, lifting his face.</p>

<p>“What are you waiting for? Let’s slaughter these bastards!” Beside him, Lucien’s roar echoed, his blade slashing dozens of golden cuts</p>

<p>toward the enemy.</p>

<p>Celestia knelt down, casting a small blessing on her allies, while Aeryn also moved, her body glowing gold, taken over by the aura of the priestess’s blessings.</p>

<p>Sapphire, right behind them, simply raised her hands in the air, hundreds of ice spears appearing behind her before she hurled them toward the enemies.</p>

<p>Soon, all the demonic cultists were overcome by nothing but the deepest chaos.</p>

<p>The Jiangshi ran wildly toward their opponents, barely resisting for a single moment before being destroyed by the heroes’ joint attacks.</p>

<p>Qingyi, naturally, did not stand idly by.</p>

<p>He eliminated any undead or cultists that came too close to Sapphire and Celestia, drenching the Heaven-Defying Thunder Sword in blood as he reaped one enemy after another.</p>

<p>Barely a minute of battle had passed before the cultists began to flee desperately. Unfortunately for them, Sapphire would not allow it.</p>

<p>She placed her hands on the ground, gritted her teeth, and then moved all her mana toward her palms while whispering in an ancient language.</p>

<p>Instantly, the world around her changed.</p>

<p>A powerful blue barrier surged from her body, expanding violently and encapsulating all the cultists before finally ceasing to grow and transforming into a physical barrier.</p>

<p>“Eliminate them quickly! I won’t be able to hold them back for long!” Sapphire roared to her companions.</p>

<p>Qingyi just nodded, receiving a second blessing from Celestia, feeling his body become much lighter and his mind much clearer.</p>

<p>That was… very useful.</p>

<p>Without further hesitation, he just advanced, using his draconic eyes to detect all enemies and, in a single moment, exterminate them</p>

<p>completely.</p>

<p>When they were finally done, they gathered in front of the mausoleum.</p>

<p>It was almost completely destroyed, with only a small entrance leading to a bloody staircase, visibly damaged by the attacks.</p>

<p>The heroes just exchanged glances before cautiously entering the staircase.</p>

<p>It was dark and stuffy. A putrid, sickening smell permeated</p>

<p>everything.</p>

<p>The moment Qingyi’s feet stepped at the end of the staircase, he stopped, a strange crack echoing. His boots sank into a strange, brittle</p>

<p>texture.</p>

<p>When he looked down, his eyes widened.</p>

<p>Those were bones. All mortal bones, bearing terrible torture marks.</p>

<p>Looking to the side, he saw cells, hundreds of them, each filled with dozens of frail, broken figures. Their eyes were empty, and their</p>

<p>bodies bore deep signs of torture.</p>

<p>From children to the elderly, and even pregnant women.</p>

<p>No one had been spared.</p>

<p>“W-we need to free them immediately! Celestia exclaimed beside him,</p>

<p>clenching her hands and calling for a silent prayer, immediately</p>

<p>casting a healing spell on those in one of the cells.</p>

<p>To her surprise, there was no relief in their condition; quite the</p>

<p>contrary.</p>

<p>Their lips opened in miserable screams, their bodies writhing as their</p>

<p>eyes widened.</p>

<p>Celestia shuddered, taking a step back and burying her face in</p>

<p>Qingyi’s chest.</p>

<p>She did not watch as the bodies exploded.</p>

<p>“Come on, we’ll rescue them later…” Lucien said through gritted teeth.</p>

<p>He wasn’t angry at the state of those people; quite the contrary.</p>

<p>What made him angry was seeing Celestia like that, clinging to Qingyi</p>

<p>as if he were everything in the world.</p>

<p>Why couldn’t it be him?</p>

<p>No… why the hell was this even happening?</p>

<p>Celestia was the Sacred Priestess, she shouldn’t be corrupted by</p>

<p>Qingyi’s disgusting touch like this!</p>

<p>Lucien’s lips parted, almost releasing a protest, before he finally just</p>

<p>looked ahead and nothing but ahead.</p>

<p>He would take care of these things when they returned to the</p>

<p>imperial capital. If Qingyi had truly dared to take Celestia’s purity…</p>

<p>Lucien could hardly imagine what would happen, his body trembling</p>

<p>with pleasure.</p>

<p>Yes, that would be a great day!</p>

<p>Soon, the group found themselves walking again, going deeper and</p>

<p>deeper.</p>

<p>They encountered no enemies, only absolute darkness until they finally reached a location.</p>

<p>It was a huge pocket at the lowest part of the cave. The ceiling was</p>

<p>clearly over a kilometer high, while the stony walls seemed to hold firm despite the tens of kilometers separating them.</p>

<p>The place was lit by candles on the floor, revealing tens of thousands</p>

<p>of corpses.</p>

<p>In its center stood a single man.</p>

<p>Tall, with long white hair, blood-red eyes, and a handsome</p>

<p>middle-aged face.</p>

<p>His eyes focused on the heroes who had just arrived, and his Qi</p>

<p>spilled out, along with his mana, falling upon them.</p>

<p>From his back, dozens of tubes connecting his body to a blood reservoir broke, and he smiled.</p>

<p>Qingyi took a step back, feeling his blood burn.</p>

<p>That man was powerful, more powerful than any enemy he had faced</p>

<p>in a long time.</p>
