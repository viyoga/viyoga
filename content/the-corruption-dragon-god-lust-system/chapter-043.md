<p>“Begin!” Elder Tian’s voice echoed through the arena.</p>

<p>“Please instruct me well, Senior Brother Xiaohu.” Qingyi clenched his fists, his body filled with a cloud of electric sparks before he advanced.</p>

<p>Xiaohu responded with a smile, his body surrounded by a sea of burning flames.</p>

<p>’He also has a movement technique?’ Qingyi blinked in surprise.</p>

<p>That kind of technique was rare, usually only inner disciples possessed it. He hadn’t expected to see someone with an ability similar to his own.</p>

<p>Unfortunately, he didn’t have much time to think about it.</p>

<p>Xiaohu’s body disappeared in an explosion of embers as he ran around Qingyi, his speed no slower than the lightning steps.</p>

<p>The next moment, a fireball appeared in his hand, tearing through the air towards Qingyi.</p>

<p>Qingyi stopped his steps momentarily, feeling a wave of heat hit his face before brandishing his sword.</p>

<p>The Thunderclouds Sword’s blade cut through the fireball, causing the flaming Qi to spread out in sparks that narrowly missed his body, falling a few meters behind and raising a thick curtain of smoke.</p>

<p>Taking advantage of the smoke cover, Qingyi activated his draconic eyes and could clearly see Xiaohu launching a relentless sequence of attacks at him.</p>

<p>“That bastard’s Qi is infinite?” Qingyi grunted, halting his steps and activating the lightning steps.</p>

<p>He launched himself towards Xiaohu while turning his body and swinging the thundercloud sword.</p>

<p>“You’re fast!” Xiaohu laughed, lowering his body.</p>

<p>Qingyi’s sword passed inches from his back, tearing only a piece of fabric.</p>

<p>“You too,” Qingyi’s eyes cooled. “But it’s time to get serious.”</p>

<p>Without waiting for an answer, Qingyi activated the lightning steps, channeling all his Qi into his legs.</p>

<p>His body turned into a blur, the Qi of the thundering sword dancing around him as he easily overcame Xiaohu’s movement technique.</p>

<p>“Shit!” Xiaohu tried to dodge, but it was too late.</p>

<p>Qingyi’s sword hit his shoulder and he was thrown violently to the ground.</p>

<p>Qingyi did nothing more, just stepped on Xiaohu’s chest and directed his gaze at Elder Tian.</p>

<p>The disciples reacted with astonished murmurs as they watched the scene.</p>

<p>Xiaohu, the most powerful fire cultivator among the sect’s outer disciples, had been defeated by Qingyi, who was two cultivation levels below and supposedly had far inferior talent.</p>

<p>How could that even be possible?</p>

<p>“Xiaohu loses, Qingyi advances,” declared Elder Tian with an approving smile.</p>

<p>Qingyi then returned to Feiyan’s side.</p>

<p>After their second battle, things flowed smoothly.</p>

<p>In his third fight, Qingyi faced a beautiful ice cultivator, but he wasn’t fooled by her delicate appearance.</p>

<p>With a precise and powerful kick, he threw her out of the arena without hesitation.</p>

<p>In the fourth battle, he fought against a cultivator wielding a heavy, large axe.</p>

<p>Qingyi dueled with him for long minutes until he managed to drive his sword into his opponent’s belly, ending the fight.</p>

<p>By the time he had reached the fifth battle, he had gained total respect among the sect’s outer disciples.</p>

<p>A young man with no background who had received the golden token, conquered an incomparable beauty and was now successively defeating opponents with a higher cultivation than his.</p>

<p>“I surrender!” The young swordsman in front of Qingyi declared, bowing his head before leaping out of the arena.</p>

<p>He had only gotten this far by luck, facing weak opponents.</p>

<p>It was better not to fight Qingyi now and simply focus on the battle for the last remaining spots after the semi-finals.</p>

<p>Qingyi ignored the young man and just waited for the semi-finals. His eyes briefly met those of Tianming, who had just finished his own battle.</p>

<p>It wasn’t long before it was Qingyi’s turn to fight again in his sixth battle.</p>

<p>Her opponent was an ordinary-looking girl of delicate stature, carrying an ornate golden fan.</p>

<p>’An enchantment arts cultivator…’ Qingyi smiled slightly.</p>

<p>He had seen this girl fight before, using a strange pinkish Qi to deceive her male opponents’ senses and sneak up on them.</p>

<p>“Senior Brother Qingyi, please have mercy,” she smiled seductively, revealing her cleavage as she assumed a seemingly fragile position.</p>

<p>’Good tactic… but that won’t work on me.’ Qingyi laughed, and the girl’s face distorted in astonishment as he advanced rapidly towards her.</p>

<p>He didn’t even draw his sword; the scabbard would suffice.</p>

<p>A crash echoed as he thrust towards her face, hitting one of her cheeks and sending her flying with a few teeth missing from her mouth.</p>

<p>Many male disciples cast angry glances in Qingyi’s direction as they watched the delicate girl being taken to the sect’s medical pavilion.</p>

<p>Didn’t that man respect beautiful women?</p>

<p>Returning to the stands, Qingyi exchanged glances with Bao Zhang.</p>

<p>“Then I’m the cruel one,” Bao Zhang shook his head. “At least I had a good reason.”</p>

<p>Qingyi laughed at those words. He had indeed been cruel; he could have aimed for the belly or the chest.</p>

<p>Going straight for the face had certainly been merciless.</p>

<p>But he didn’t think about it for long, his gaze taking on a cold expression.</p>

<p>“Be careful of Tianming. He’s promised to kill me, and the mere fact that you’re by my side could make him take aim at you too. Surrendering isn’t a bad idea,” Qingyi warned Bao Zhang.</p>

<p>“Worry more about yourself, junior brother. I can handle myself,” Bao Zhang replied, sprinting towards the arena.</p>

<p>He had seen Tianming fight and, although he wasn’t sure of victory, he trusted that he would at least be able to protect his own life against a one-eyed man.</p>

<p>“Are you a friend of Long Qingyi’s?” Tianming asked, his voice calm and composed.</p>

<p>A faint smile took over his cold face, his black robes fluttering as his remaining eye twinkled softly as he stared at Bao Zhang.</p>

<p>“We’re acquaintances,” Bao Zhang replied, taking up a battle stance.</p>

<p>“Good!” Tianming’s smile intensified, his voice almost animated before changing completely, taking on an icy tone charged with murderous intent. “It’ll be quick, don’t worry.”</p>

<p>The moment Tianming’s voice fell silent, Bao Zhang’s eyes went wide.</p>

<p>He seemed to be floating…</p>

<p>Why could he see his own body?</p>
