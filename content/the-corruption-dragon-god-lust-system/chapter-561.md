<p>“Pathetic? Young man, my technique is perfect, hahaha!” The Heavenly Demon laughed loudly.</p>

<p>“You should see her, the most perfect woman in the world… It was good to see her beg for her daughter’s life while I slit her throat.”</p>

<p>The Heavenly Demon’s eyes rolled back. It was as if just imagining the scene was enough to give him an orgasm.</p>

<p>[Disgusting!] Ruxue shouted in Qingyi’s mind, who nodded.</p>

<p>He might be a pervert, yes, but the Heavenly Demon? He was a complete and repulsive sicko.</p>

<p>A repulsive man who didn’t even deserve the air he breathed.</p>

<p>“His daughter, where is she?” Qingyi asked coldly, immense fury rising from the depths of his soul.</p>

<p>It came from himself as well, but its central source was his bloodline, screaming and begging for the death of that damned demon.</p>

<p>“Oh… I would tell you if I were in the celestial heavens, but…” The Heavenly Demon’s eyes turned deadly cold. “You won’t live past today anyway.”</p>

<p>The moment the voice fell, the entire world changed, in a way very different from before.</p>

<p>That was…</p>

<p>Qingyi’s eyes widened, and he raised a single hand.</p>

<p>Drops fell from the sky, but it was not rain. What gathered in the palm of his hand was blood, red, vibrant, and burning.</p>

<p>Qingyi clenched his fist tightly.</p>

<p>The blood that touched him turned to vapor immediately, without even having time to stain his robes.</p>

<p>His eyes fell, focusing on the ground below him.</p>

<p>It had only been raining blood for a few seconds, but it had already been enough for a sea to form as far as the eye could see, engulfing everything in endless red.</p>

<p>“Ah… Do you see the beauty?” The Heavenly Demon stretched.</p>

<p>“This is my domain, the eternal blood sea, molded with the blood of billions of enemies.”</p>

<p>“And you will soon be a part of it.”</p>

<p>The moment the words fell, he lunged forward, but not just he.</p>

<p>The entire sea of blood followed his movement. Waves turned into crimson spears and beasts that rose into the skies, all focused on Qingyi.</p>

<p>“Annoying…” The handsome young man gritted his teeth, flying as high as he could.</p>

<p>Below him, a blood serpent opened its jaws filled with razor-sharp teeth, its gigantic serpentine body stretching hundreds of meters above the sea.</p>

<p>Qingyi stopped and punched, using the air pressure to destroy the serpent.</p>

<p>He barely had time to relax before he realized: the Heavenly Demon was nowhere to be found.</p>

<p>His eyes scanned everything around him until they fell on the collapsing body of the blood serpent, a figure emerging from within it with its sword already aimed at Qingyi’s chest.</p>

<p>The handsome young man dodged, turned his body, and</p>

<p>counterattacked.</p>

<p>The entire sea shook, the blood being pushed away in waves as the shockwave spread.</p>

<p>The two stood there for a moment, staring face to face, their powerful</p>

<p>bodies vying for the upper hand in that clash of brute force.</p>

<p>“Have I told you how ugly you are up close?” Qingyi taunted with a bloodied smile, feeling some internal injuries surface.</p>

<p>“Oh… look who’s talking!” The Heavenly Demon pushed Qingyi back before roaring.</p>

<p>“Let’s end this once and for all, brat! I don’t have all day to deal with your insignificant life!”</p>

<p>He was doing his best not to show it, but inside, he was desperate.</p>

<p>His Qi was already at its limit.</p>

<p>If he didn’t want to die there and waste tens of thousands of years of planning, he needed to end this now.</p>

<p>He needed to recover and absorb the base of his Dantian as quickly as</p>

<p>possible.</p>

<p>With an angry growl, the celestial demon opened as much space as possible between himself and Qingyi.</p>

<p>He had been avoiding her most powerful techniques, but he realized that holding back would only lead to his death.</p>

<p>Qingyi, who was also holding back, was unable to disagree with that request.</p>

<p>After all, his internal injuries were accumulating, and his enormous reserves of mana and Qi were being rapidly drained with each</p>

<p>exchange of blows.</p>

<p>The two stared at each other for a brief moment before beginning.</p>

<p>The Heavenly Demon withdrew his hand and threw away the sword</p>

<p>he was holding.</p>

<p>He took a deep breath, exhaled heavily, and then summoned another sword, with a blade slightly larger than the previous one.</p>

<p>His eyes grew cold. He chanted something in his mind, and a barrier</p>

<p>of blood appeared around him, sucking in the entire sea that</p>

<p>surrounded him.</p>

<p>Strangely, no matter how much blood it absorbed, the barrier did not grow, it just became more and more powerful.</p>

<p>At the same time, Qingyi summoned a spear of fire, using the Art of the Nine Spears of Heavenly Fire.</p>

<p>Without hesitation, he threw the first spear, which collided with the</p>

<p>barrier with a bang.</p>

<p>It wasn’t enough to cause even a tremor, but Qingyi didn’t care,</p>

<p>calling forth a second spear.</p>

<p>Watching the sea level drop noticeably, he threw the second, then the third, fourth, fifth, sixth, seventh, and eighth.</p>

<p>Soon, the ground was revealed, still covered with a few bloody puddles, the barrier displaying little more than a few small cracks. “Boy… I’ll make you regret being born!” A roar echoed and the blood barrier collapsed, gathering around the Heavenly Demon sword like flames.</p>

<p>They were unstable and trembling, but frighteningly powerful.</p>

<p>Qingyi finally threw the last spear before grabbing the thunder sword that defies the heavens, preparing for the final confrontation. He gathered all his Qi into the blade and watched as the Heavenly Demon easily broke the ninth spear, advancing relentlessly. The blood sword left a crimson trail behind it, like a snake following</p>

<p>its swing.</p>

<p>The moment before it reached Qingyi, the handsome young man used the Heaven-shattering Divine Slash again, but this time, it was</p>

<p>different.</p>

<p>He did not imbue ordinary flames into his blade. He imbued the very flames of primordial chaos.</p>

<p>The two swords met, and then there was silence.</p>

<p>It lasted only a brief moment, almost imperceptible, followed by an incomparable explosion that engulfed the bodies of both.</p>

<p>Bones were broken, limbs torn off, flesh ripped apart, and meridians</p>

<p>lacerated.</p>

<p>But neither of them stopped, the flames of primordial chaos continuing to devour everything they touched.</p>
