<p>The moment Rouxi was expelled from the mind world and appeared in Qingyi’s pavilion, all the girls emerged with her.</p>

<p>Even those who were far from the temple noticed the anomaly and didn’t hesitate to leave.</p>

<p>Every one of them wore a tense expression, but none compared to Qingyi’s.</p>

<p>He turned to Rouxi. Feiyan had already laid her on the bed, while Celestia washed her hands, sleeves rolled up, preparing for the birth.</p>

<p>Qingyi crossed the room in a few strides and knelt at the bedside, taking her hand between his.</p>

<p>"Everything will be all right, my love..." he said with a sweet smile.</p>

<p>"I know it will..." Rouxi managed to return it, even as pain contorted her face. Her fingers tightened weakly around his.</p>

<p>Even for a Stellar Sovereign, her water breaking was no pleasant experience.</p>

<p>In the next moment, Qingyi’s eyes—which had overflowed with tenderness only a moment before—filled with rage.</p>

<p>He gently released Rouxi’s hand and stood. Without a word, he stepped out into the courtyard of their mansion and raised his gaze to the heavens.</p>

<p>Until a short while ago, those heavens had been blue, the sun hanging high in a sky created by the artificial atmosphere of the Celestial Orthodox Martial Alliance.</p>

<p>Now, those same skies had sunk into darkness.</p>

<p>Black clouds swallowed everything, a sea of lightning hammering down without pause, testing the strength of the Celestial Orthodox Martial Alliance’s arrays.</p>

<p>Each strike made the firmament tremble. Thunder rolled in deafening waves, and the smell of ozone soaked the air.</p>

<p>Then, without warning, the arrays shut down.</p>

<p>Qingyi sensed the exact moment the auras of more than seventy Celestial Emperors erupted, all of them rushing toward his pavilion.</p>

<p>None of them reached him. The heavens had already moved, establishing a barrier that stretched for tens of kilometers around Qingyi.</p>

<p>The Celestial Emperors could only hover outside, staring at that invisible dome with clenched fists.</p>

<p>"T-the Celestial Court..." one of them stammered, his voice trembling as he watched a point of light emerge from that sea of darkness.</p>

<p>That wasn’t a Celestial Tribulation! That was the descent of a General of the Celestial Court!</p>

<p>"That means a Child of Destiny is about to be born... but how?" another Celestial Emperor murmured, his eyes falling upon Qingyi’s solitary figure in the courtyard.</p>

<p>Children of Destiny.</p>

<p>No one knew much about them.</p>

<p>The moment one was born, the Celestial Court immediately took the child away. The Court itself was a mysterious group, one that descended only once every few hundred thousand years.</p>

<p>Many said they came from a higher plane. Others claimed they were merely representatives of the heavens themselves, ensuring the world’s balance.</p>

<p>It was said that the Children of Destiny possessed such extraordinary talent that, whenever one was born, a General had to descend personally to fetch her and ensure she was properly nurtured.</p>

<p>For most families, having a Celestial General descend for a child was an immense honour. The Court often showered these families with gifts capable of producing dozens of Celestial Emperors.</p>

<p>Three of the five most powerful families and sects in the Celestial Realm owed their power to the gifts bestowed at the birth of a Child of Destiny.</p>

<p>Qingyi, of course, knew nothing of this. And even if he had, it would have made little difference.</p>

<p>The expression on his face was nothing but the purest fury.</p>

<p>Pigs courting death, endless pigs!</p>

<p>"How were you able to force Rouxi out of the mind world, you dog?" Qingyi’s voice echoed, deep and laden with rage.</p>

<p>Of course, he already knew the answer.</p>

<p>Mind worlds were, after all, governed by the heavens.</p>

<p>They were created beneath the heavens and existed only with their permission.</p>

<p>The heavens were far from holding absolute authority over mind worlds, but they still possessed certain minimal capabilities.</p>

<p>Forcing Rouxi out of the mind world had likely come at an extremely high cost. It wasn’t the sort of thing the heavens would do routinely.</p>

<p>"WHAT GIVES YOU THE COURAGE?!"</p>

<p>Qingyi wasn’t even a Celestial Emperor, but his roar carried enough power to make even Tianjin flinch slightly.</p>

<p>His murderous intent engulfed the entire Orthodox Martial Alliance.</p>

<p>"Courage... huh?"</p>

<p>The moment Qingyi spoke, a voice replied.</p>

<p>It was young, beautiful, and soft as the fluttering of a heron’s wings.</p>

<p>To many there, it was the noblest voice they had ever heard in their entire lives.</p>

<p>Some of the Celestial Emperors lowered their heads without even realizing it.</p>

<p>"Do you know who you’re speaking to? Inferior."</p>

<p>The heavens suddenly parted, an explosion of light bursting forth from within the sea of black clouds.</p>

<p>The light—white and sacred—fell upon Qingyi like a divine bolt of lightning illuminating a demon.</p>

<p>And from within it, surrounded by dark clouds, a figure emerged.</p>

<p>That figure’s cultivation was unfathomable, yet it had been suppressed to the same level as Qingyi’s.</p>

<p>It was a young man, clad in battle robes that shifted between white and blue.</p>

<p>His hands were clasped elegantly behind his back, his face bearing boundless arrogance, his tied-back black hair fluttering in the wind.</p>

<p>"I am Qing Xuanyu, the Seventeenth Celestial General of the Celestial Court, Eternal Representative of the Celestial Heavens. Long Qingyi, your crimes against the heavens are beyond compare, but your child is a Child of Destiny, worthy of being adopted by the Celestial Court." Xuanyu’s eyes fell upon the pavilion behind Qingyi.</p>

<p>"Hand her over willingly, and I will merely cripple your cultivation. Perhaps I will even bring you to a fair trial, where you may be accepted into our order and have your crimes forgiven. With your talent, that is not impossible.</p>

<p>"Now, make way. The Celestial Court will oversee the child’s birth."</p>

<p>In the next moment, a notification from the System reached Qingyi.</p>

<p>[I don’t know much about the Land of the True Gods, but I am familiar with the Celestial Court.</p>

<p>They are the power that controls the ascent from the Celestial Heavens to the Land of the True Gods, a force that usurped the heavens of that region after the death of Tianjin’s great-grandfather.</p>

<p>They are likely the ones holding that old bear’s daughter captive, and they are the ones I fear. When you reach the Realm of the Celestial Emperor and I can no longer hide from them, they will—]</p>

<p>"No... They’ll all die."</p>

<p>Qingyi cut off the System’s worried voice.</p>

<p>His own was calm and low.</p>

<p>"For all of them...</p>

<p>...</p>

<p>...</p>

<p>Death."</p>
