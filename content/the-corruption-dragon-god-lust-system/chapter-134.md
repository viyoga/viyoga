<p>“He’s going after…?” One of the inner disciples opened his mouth, his eyes wide as he watched the arrogant young man approach Qingyi.</p>

<p>“Shhhh, let him try, I’ll want to see that!” Another whispered.</p>

<p>Everyone there had heard of Qingyi’s achievements. Everyone except that poor fellow, apparently.</p>

<p>The arrogant young man approached Qingyi in confident strides, placing his hands on the handsome young man’s shoulders.</p>

<p>“Fall to your knees, before I have to break them.” He growled, unleashing his full Qi of the second stage of the Golden Core realm, forcing even the distant cultivators to retreat.</p>

<p>The marble under Qingyi’s feet cracked, resisting for a brief moment before breaking, causing the handsome young man to sink to his heels.</p>

<p>But despite all that power, Qingyi didn’t even flinch.</p>

<p>His face held a gentle, humorous smile, with no trace of offense in his beautiful, piercing black eyes.</p>

<p>It didn’t take long for the arrogant young man to realize his mistake and hastily try to retreat. Unfortunately, it was already too late.</p>

<p>Qingyi moved his hands, grabbing both of his opponent’s wrists. Without giving the young man time to react, he strengthened the grip of his fists.</p>

<p>A heartbreaking scream echoed as the arrogant young man’s bones were crushed in loud, despairing cracks.</p>

<p>Qingyi didn’t even use his Qi, it wasn’t necessary. Only his brute physical strength was enough to deal with those insects.</p>

<p>“Argh… FUCK! MY HANDS!” The arrogant young man whimpered, falling to his knees.</p>

<p>“I only broke the bones, I didn’t destroy any of your meridians. It should be completely healed in a few days with a good healing pill.” Qingyi patted the young man on the back, whose watery eyes filled with terror.</p>

<p>With what little strength he had in his legs, he ran, retreating from the arena.</p>

<p>Watching the young man’s back move away, Qingyi shifted his gaze to Elder Xu Qingxue.</p>

<p>He noticed her plump rosy lips curving into a subtle smile, lasting just over a second before she suppressed it.</p>

<p>“The others, you may leave,” she said, her eyes fixed on Qingyi’s.</p>

<p>“But elder, we haven’t even taken part in your test yet!” one of the disciples complained, shrinking under the silver-haired beauty’s cold gaze.</p>

<p>“It’s not necessary.” She shook her head, taking a step towards Qingyi.</p>

<p>The other inner disciples hesitated, but in the end just bowed and withdrew. The moment that monster had gotten there, they had all lost.</p>

<p>At least they wouldn’t be going home with some broken bones.</p>

<p>After a few moments, Qingyi and Qingxue finally found themselves alone, her long silver hair fluttering in the gentle breeze.</p>

<p>“Long Qingyi greets elder Xu Qingxue!” Qingyi bowed, clasping his fists respectfully.</p>

<p>After a moment of silence, Qingxue asked: “Did the sect leader instruct you to become my direct disciple?”</p>

<p>Qingyi couldn’t help but hesitate at that question. Was that a good thing or a bad thing?</p>

<p>“Yes, elder, I was instructed by the sect leader to become your disciple and take part in the scarlet dragon competition with you.” He decided to be honest.</p>

<p>“Mmm” Qingxue exhaled softly as she took another step towards him.</p>

<p>Soon, they were face to face, her warm, sweet breath invading Qingyi’s nostrils as she studied him.</p>

<p>“Draw your sword,” she moved away, turning her back on Qingyi.</p>

<p>The handsome young man’s eyes focused on her shapely hips as she walked away, swaying gently with each step.</p>

<p>“I will test your blade. If it’s worthy, you’ll be my disciple. If not, I believe you should report your failure to the sect leader.”</p>

<p>She turned, her double swords sliding out of their sheaths with loud, sharp whistles.</p>

<p>Her Qi exploded, her dress fluttering as her body was covered in wind Qi, the fabric rising and revealing a brief glimpse of her white panties sinking into the rift between the fat, fluffy mounds of her virgin femininity.</p>

<p>Qingyi couldn’t help but shudder at the sight, feeling his dragon awaken.</p>

<p>If he had the courage, he would certainly thank her for allowing him to see such a beautiful scenery.</p>

<p>“I won’t disappoint the elder!” Qingyi bowed one last time, controlling his thoughts and focusing his mind.</p>

<p>“Ugh… poor thing, one more to fall into the hands of that damned womanizer!” Ruxue’s voice sounded in Qingyi’s mind, her sweet, seductive laugh echoing in his head.</p>

<p>Qingyi ignored her, drawing the heaven-defying thunder sword.</p>

<p>His lightning sword Qi immediately took over the blade, shining brightly, with electric sparks exploding from it and making the air around him boil.</p>

<p>Qingxue couldn’t hide a flash of surprise on her beautiful, delicate face, but it didn’t last long.</p>

<p>“Come,” she ordered.</p>

<p>Qingyi activated the lightning steps, his body exploding in speed as he used the first form of the Stormy Monarch sword art.</p>

<p>Qingxue didn’t even move, just casually raising her sword.</p>

<p>A loud clang echoed and Qingyi immediately regretted having made a frontal advance. It was like hitting a wall of cast steel, a painful tremor spreading through his arms.</p>

<p>“It’s not bad,” Qingxue’s serene, cold voice hit Qingyi’s eardrums and he tried to back away, but it was too late.</p>

<p>She raised her right leg, her foot hitting Qingyi’s chest with a thud.</p>

<p>The handsome young man was thrown backwards, bouncing on the ground and being forced to drive the heaven-defying thunder sword into the ground, tearing through it for almost a hundred meters before finally coming to a stop.</p>

<p>Luckily, his constitution was extremely powerful; if it had been up to his strength alone, he would surely have broken all his ribs.</p>

<p>His eyes rose, watching Qingxue, whose leg was still raised.</p>

<p>Her dress slid down smoothly, revealing even more of the pale, smooth, perfect skin of her thick, luscious thighs, a thin trickle of sweat running down between them.</p>

<p>It was a beautiful sight, but Qingyi couldn’t enjoy it for long.</p>

<p>Qingxue was already upon him, her sword flying towards his neck at an absurd speed.</p>

<p>“Use all your power, you’ll hurt yourself if you keep holding back.”</p>
