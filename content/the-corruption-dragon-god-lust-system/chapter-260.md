<p>There were many kinds of dragons.</p>

<p>Some were kind, others evil; some barely cared about those below them while others cared too much.</p>

<p>But there was one thing that united all dragons: their arrogance and ego.</p>

<p>Even the gentlest of dragons had the arrogance to defy the heavens and the ego to think themselves above them.</p>

<p>How could Qingyi’s words not affect Tianyu? A man with a draconic bloodline, overcome with jealousy and with an ego as fragile as butterfly wings?</p>

<p>“You…” Tianyu growled, his voice more like the grunt of a beast.</p>

<p>Above him, the figure of the white dragon grew even larger, its serpentine body flying over the arena, stretching for more than two hundred meters, a golden light bathing everything below it.</p>

<p>One of the transcendents watching acted quickly, moving his Qi around the arena and protecting the weaker spectators.</p>

<p>“You’ll pay for those words!” Tianyu pointed his sword at Qingyi. The Qi surrounding him fell to the ground like molten gold, spreading out around his feet.</p>

<p>‘No weaker than Biyue before I helped her evolve her bloodline and constitution to mortal grade…’ Qingyi thought, readying his blade and watching Tianyu.</p>

<p>Whatever he was planning, it was powerful, but Qingyi didn’t bother to stop it.</p>

<p>After almost ten seconds, an eternity for cultivators of that level, the golden Qi finally stopped falling to the ground, forming a lake of gold that stretched for twenty meters around Tianyu.</p>

<p>“This is the secret technique I inherited from my master. I’ll give you one last chance, take this battle seriously! Die with some honor!” Tianyu declared.</p>

<p>“I’m treating this battle as seriously as it deserves.”</p>

<p>The moment Qingyi’s voice fell silent, the world around Tianyu changed.</p>

<p>The golden lake beneath his feet shuddered, moving gently before turning into a violent whirlpool, swirling around Tianyu and swallowing him up completely.</p>

<p>The golden Qi surrounding him shone more brightly than ever, blinding many of the spectators as the whirlpool expanded further and further.</p>

<p>It wasn’t long before the formations protecting the arena began to fail, the ground beneath Tianyu’s feet sinking as everything within a radius of hundreds of meters around him collapsed.</p>

<p>Only the part where the audience stood remained intact, protected by the power of the transcendents.</p>

<p>“DIE!” Tianyu’s roar echoed as he advanced towards Qingyi.</p>

<p>The handsome young man reacted immediately. Sixteen blades of lightning appeared around him, flying towards Tianyu and breaking the sound barrier, accompanied by the heaven-defying sword of thunder.</p>

<p>The golden whirlpool covered Tianyu completely, but with his draconic eyes, Qingyi had little difficulty in perceiving Tianyu’s movements, even with the absurd density of Qi in front of him.</p>

<p>Covering himself with black scales, Qingyi broke through the golden whirlpool, his sword hitting Tianyu’s, the other sixteen swords stopping a few centimeters before they were able to pierce his body from all sides.</p>

<p>With the exception of the power of his lineage projection, Qingyi didn’t hold back, putting all his strength into that clash.</p>

<p>The thunderous rays of his lightning sword Qi joined with the golden Qi, destroying everything around.</p>

<p>With both the sides and the top of the arena completely blocked by the transcendents’ Qi, there was only one place where all that power could go: down.</p>

<p>The stone beneath both their feet turned to dust, their bodies sinking into an ever-deeper crater.</p>

<p>“You’ve got a good sword there. I think it’ll be a good test.” Qingyi flashed a big smile, his calm, serene eyes focused on Tianyu’s, terribly distorted in hatred and frustration.</p>

<p>Without hesitation, Qingyi moved his sword Qi, forming an even sharper edge in front of the heaven-defying thunder sword, tenuously using his fire Qi with his lightning Qi to raise the temperature as much as possible.</p>

<p>It was time to finally test the heaven-defying thunder sword’s new power after its breakthrough to mortal grade.</p>

<p>“Impossible!” The leader of the ancestral dragon sect suddenly stood up, his eyes wide open.</p>

<p>The sword Tianyu was using was a personal gift from him, a mortal grade item and one of his sect’s most powerful treasures.</p>

<p>How great was the horror that gripped his face as Qingyi’s sword sank into Tianyu’s?</p>

<p>Suddenly, the Qi of both of them stopped, the thunderous clashes of their auras being replaced by a strange silence.</p>

<p>A metallic clang echoed as the melted tip of Tianyu’s sword fell to the stone floor below them, who now found themselves in a crater more than two hundred meters deep.</p>

<p>Tianyu looked at his own sword, or at least what was left of it in his hand.</p>

<p>Tears of hatred streamed down his cheeks and he fell to his knees, a terrible gash tearing through his chest.</p>

<p>“Ah…” Qingyi sighed, gently turning his face.</p>

<p>Among so many shocked looks was Biyue, her rosy lips curved into a suppressed smile, her pale cheeks blushing softly.</p>

<p>“B-bastard – Blergh!” Tianyu vomited a large gulp of blood, being forced to move his hands to the ground, trying not to collapse.</p>

<p>“Was it worth it?” Qingyi asked, pressing his blade against Tianyu’s neck, a little disappointed.</p>

<p>“Truly a waste of talent on such a disgusting, insignificant insect…” Qingyi retracted the heaven-defying thunder sword before swinging it with full force.</p>

<p>An explosion of blood stained the ground behind Tianyu, his head being thrown into the air.</p>

<p>Qingyi closed his eyes briefly, calming his breathing.</p>

<p>Fighting Tianyu without using his lineage projection was difficult.</p>

<p>If Tianyu had been just a little less arrogant, he would probably have managed to at least injure Qingyi.</p>

<p>Unfortunately for him, he was too blind.</p>

<p>Qingyi’s body floated above the crater, his victory finally being announced, with tens of thousands of spectators shouting his name.</p>

<p>Almost all of them except those from the dragon battalion, of course.</p>

<p>Their battalion leader had just died, defeated in a miserable fight in which his opponent of lesser cultivation didn’t even take him seriously.</p>

<p>How could they be happy about that?</p>

<p>It was impossible, their heads were down and their eyes were shaking.</p>
