<p>The patriarch of the draconic elves appeared in a strange place, his feet stepping on damp ground, the sound of splashing water reaching his ears.</p>

<p>He was not in front of the gate through which he had entered, no.</p>

<p>He was miles below the earth, in a chamber so deep and narrow that not even he or his father knew exactly where it was.</p>

<p>All they knew was that this gate led to that chamber.</p>

<p>He took a step forward, observing a long corridor that stretched to an even larger chamber. Its ceiling rose miles above the floor, its length extending as far as the eye could see.</p>

<p>At the end of that chamber was the heart of Karlan.</p>

<p>The patriarch’s pupils focused as he saw a group of figures, twelve in total.</p>

<p>They were the draconic priests and guardians of the remains of fallen heroes.</p>

<p>His most trusted advisors and, above all, the beings responsible for the ritual that would help RedDeath receive the power of Karlan’s dragon.</p>

<p>After all, RedDeath was not like Khaedryss; he could not absorb the power of anything just by devouring it.</p>

<p>“What are you waiting for?” The patriarch smiled, the ground beneath his feet shaking as the Red Death took a step forward. “Let’s get started-“</p>

<p>The patriarch’s words died in his throat, the world around him changing as darkness swallowed everything.</p>

<p>He barely had time to react before he saw the hooded priests being torn in half by a wave of power, a hellish heat rising behind him.</p>

<p>He shielded himself with a thick layer of mana as RedDeath turned, his jaws opening to reveal endless rows of sharp, twisted teeth.</p>

<p>Its roar did little to drown out the explosion.</p>

<p>RedDeath’s scales held, but the ground beneath his feet did not; his enormous body was thrown backward as his claws tore into the hard stone beneath him.</p>

<p>“Who the hell are you? Filthy pig! Reveal yourself!” The patriarch roared as he was also thrown toward the enormous chamber, a sea of flames leaving the hallway where they stood, illuminating the entire dark chamber.</p>

<p>A few moments later, even Red Death was thrown out, his enormous body landing on the stony ground with a thud.</p>

<p>“Filthy pig? Is that how you treat your son-in-law?” A voice full of disdain echoed, followed by a long, booming laugh.</p>

<p>The Patriarch’s eyes rose, the huge beast above him roaring at a young man in the air.</p>

<p>He was handsome, with long black hair and piercing purple eyes, watching them with contempt.</p>

<p>That was one of the champions chosen in the last selection, the one who had been chosen by Seraphine!</p>

<p>The Patriarch gritted his teeth, unable to hold back his anger.</p>

<p>“So that’s why that little bitch was confident enough to finally leave her damn hiding place? Hahahaha!” The Patriarch laughed wildly.</p>

<p>Suddenly, his laughter died and his eyes turned serious.</p>

<p>He could sense the mana leaking from Qingyi…</p>

<p>Tenth ring…</p>

<p>He had been thrown back like that by a bastard who was barely in the tenth ring?</p>

<p>Such a thought made the patriarch’s blood boil with rage.</p>

<p>He couldn’t accept that at all!</p>

<p>“Don’t interfere!” He ordered RedDeath, who only shook his snout in annoyance.</p>

<p>In the next moment, the patriarch charged forward, his body turning into a blur as he broke the speed of sound, a long, elegant saber</p>

<p>appearing in his hands.</p>

<p>The moment the saber and the thunder sword that defied the</p>

<p>heavens met, the world around them seemed to collapse, the patriarch’s crimson mana coming into contact with Qingyi’s thunder Qi and Mana.</p>

<p>A reddish aura engulfed the entire enormous chamber, its stone walls shaking violently as millions of defensive arrays activated at once, preventing it from collapsing.</p>

<p>“Die!” The patriarch’s roar sounded more like the desperate cry of a beast as he unleashed the full strength of an eleventh ring expert, the entire world around him changing.</p>

<p>‘Is that… a domain?’ Qingyi’s eyes widened as he realized this change.</p>

<p>From the floor and walls, millions of tiny scarlet flowers began to</p>

<p>sprout, dominating everything around them.</p>

<p>Controlling his Qi became difficult, and all the mana surrounding him seemed to be violently sucked away.</p>

<p>He retreated, allowing the patriarch to win that clash, throwing him towards one of the chamber walls with a powerful crash, the</p>

<p>defensive arrays nearly collapsing.</p>

<p>“Ugh… damn..” Qingyi gritted his teeth.</p>

<p>Even breathing was difficult, and his body felt much heavier than</p>

<p>before.</p>

<p>His eyes fell on the world surrounding the patriarch, unlike anything he had ever seen before.</p>

<p>The small flowers had turned into giant roses, one by one blooming, hundreds of small thorns sprouting from each one.</p>

<p>“What’s the matter… surprised?” The patriarch revealed a sickly smile, powerful roots sprouting from among the flowers, surrounding him</p>

<p>completely.</p>

<p>“That’s what you get for messing with the wrong person, brat!”</p>

<p>The roots moved toward Qingyi, forming sharp points as the flowers released all their thorns at once.</p>

<p>Immediately, Qingyi felt a chill run down his spine, a sense of danger he had never felt before overwhelming his being.</p>

<p>He shuddered, gritting his teeth, his body quickly covered in powerful</p>

<p>black scales.</p>

<p>It made no sense to fight in the domain of the dragon elf patriarch; it</p>

<p>was a losing battle.</p>

<p>Qingyi had to find a way to break through it, or at least weaken it.</p>

<p>Gritting his teeth, he simply moved his draconic Qi, the enormous figure of a black dragon appearing behind him, its roar engulfing the world around him.</p>

<p>Red Death, who seemed uninterested in the fight, suddenly raised his head, roaring back at the dragon on Qingyi’s back, his crimson eyes</p>

<p>filling with murderous intent.</p>

<p>Under Qingyi’s bloodline suppression, the patriarch’s momentum dropped significantly, but not enough.</p>

<p>The speed of the attack was still absurd, and its power had barely been reduced by ten or twenty percent.</p>

<p>A domain was, without a doubt, much more powerful than he had</p>

<p>expected.</p>

<p>“This is going to hurt…” The handsome young man gritted his teeth, preparing for the impact.</p>
