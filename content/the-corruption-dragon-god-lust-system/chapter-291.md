<p>After leaving Meilin’s office, Qingyi had a brief chat with old Tianxu before leaving.</p>

<p>He walked around the city until he found a cultivation pavilion with links to the Five Colors Pavilion, something that wasn’t too difficult.</p>

<p>He specifically sought out this location because, as a talent recognized by the pavilion, he was entitled to five free days in low-level cultivation halls per month, a benefit his wallet certainly appreciated.</p>

<p>Presenting the token he had received from Meilin, Qingyi followed the attendant to his cultivation room, similar to those in Mortal Heaven.</p>

<p>Black walls, illuminated by blue Qi crystals.</p>

<p>The only difference was the density of Qi. What had once seemed miserable to his mortal-level spirit root was now simply suffocating.</p>

<p>Sitting cross-legged, Qingyi glanced at his status screen.</p>

<p>[Name: Long Qingyi</p>

<p>Age: 20</p>

<p>Cultivation: Second Stage of the Transcendence Realm (20,000,000 lust points to upgrade)</p>

<p>Affinities: Lightning: 248 – Sword: 257 – Fire: 234 – Ice: 223 – Charm: 1000 (can be upgraded in the lust store)</p>

<p>Spiritual roots: Mortal-level three-colored spirit root (Lightning sword, fire, ice) (1,000,000 lust points to upgrade, new roots can be bought in the lust store).</p>

<p>Lineage: Blood of the Corruption Dragon God (Mortal grade, 100,000,000 lust points to upgrade.)</p>

<p>Constitution: Primordial Dragon Body (Mortal grade, 100,000,000 lust points to upgrade.)</p>

<p>Lust points: 36,250,000]</p>

<p>After pondering for a moment, he focused his gaze on his spiritual roots.</p>

<p>Mortal-level spirit roots were comparable to the ninth level in the Nine Peaks Basin, the lowest of what was considered a genius.</p>

<p>It was decent, enough for a pill capable of improving them to be worth millions, but nothing comparable to the true geniuses of that place.</p>

<p>Without hesitation, he clicked to improve them.</p>

<p>His body was immediately seized by the usual intense pain of his meridians being torn apart one by one, rebuilt to withstand much more Qi than before.</p>

<p>[Your spiritual roots have been improved! Mortal level -&gt; Silver level]</p>

<p>Qingyi exhaled as the pain in his body calmed down, immediately clicking to improve his cultivation.</p>

<p>This advance was much smoother than the improvement of his spiritual root. A slight burning spread through his body, quickly replaced by a soft refreshing sensation.</p>

<p>Standing up, he felt power flowing through his meridians. His spirit roots were absorbing so much Qi that the formations supplying the cultivation room shook gently, at the limit of their Qi supply capacity.</p>

<p>When he saw how much the price for improvement had increased, Qingyi felt his heart bleed, but he quickly put it out of his mind and devoted the rest of the night to cultivating and stabilizing his cultivation base.</p>

<p>Early in the morning, he left the attendant a small tip before heading to one of the city’s many squares.</p>

<p>A large caravan from the Five Colors Pavilion was parked there, surrounded by powerful armored guards. Even the weakest was at the peak of the Flowing Meridian Realm, with two false Immortals between them.</p>

<p>“So, who is it? Is he here already?” A high-pitched, irritating voice echoed as a woman with golden hair, above-average beauty and a slim body bounced around Meilin, whose face was as red as a tomato.</p>

<p>The night before, Meilin had had the bright idea of visiting Mei Xiu, one of her best friends who had accompanied her on her trip to inspect some of the Chamber of Commerce’s properties.</p>

<p>She wanted to talk about what had happened to her, about how she felt, about Qingyi… It didn’t take her more than a few moments to regret it.</p>

<p>“I told you it was nothing… I hardly know him,” Meilin said, blushing and looking away.</p>

<p>“Oh… come on… do you really want to be one of those crazy eternal virgin experts? Do you know how many men would die for your fat, sexy ass? I think you-”</p>

<p>Mei Xiu’s voice died in her throat as her eyes flashed, focusing on a man of transcendental beauty who was approaching on the horizon, passing through the security that surrounded them.</p>

<p>“Long Qingyi,” Meilin stepped in front of her excited friend. “Follow the head guard, he’ll show you where-”</p>

<p>“You’re coming with us, handsome!” Mei Xiu shouted excitedly, brushing past Meilin and grabbing Qingyi’s arm, pulling him towards the main carriage.</p>

<p>She turned her face, giving Meilin a teasing giggle. It was as if she was saying, ‘If you don’t want the gold, I’ll take it.</p>

<p>Meilin stopped, closed her eyes and calmed her pounding heart. She really shouldn’t have said anything to that damned girl!</p>
