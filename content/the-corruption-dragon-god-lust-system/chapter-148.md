<p>Qingyi woke up to a gentle breeze caressing his black hair.</p>

<p>He felt long, delicate fingers run across the muscles of his chest as his face sank between two huge, heavy and soft snowy peaks.</p>

<p>“Ugh…” He sniffed, inhaling a sweet, bewitching fragrance he knew well.</p>

<p>Opening his eyes, he saw a lush green world in front of him.</p>

<p>As far as the eye could see, there were hills, forests, streams, snow-white clouds and even a few floating islands.</p>

<p>They were now in the shade of a tall fruit tree next to a temple extremely similar to the Temple of the Lightning Spirit, but much more magnificent.</p>

<p>“Are you awake?” Ruxue asked, bringing her face close to his.</p>

<p>“Yes.” Qingyi nodded and left the beauty’s arms, his confused eyes meeting hers.</p>

<p>“What is this place? We’re not back in the sect, are we?”</p>

<p>“This is my world of mind. It’s still far inferior to my original world of mind, but it’s better than that damn absolute darkness I felt when I entered here.”</p>

<p>“A world of the mind? Like a sea of mind?” Qingyi’s eyes widened.</p>

<p>Was that all fake?</p>

<p>No… how could something like that be reproduced so vividly? It had to be real.</p>

<p>“It’s similar, but not the same.” She shook her head as she stood up too, her enormous breasts swaying heavily under the thin fabric.</p>

<p>“Worlds of the mind are rare, even in the heavenly heavens. It takes more than simply being at the peak of cultivation.</p>

<p>I only have a world of mind because my creator imbued it during the creation of my blade.</p>

<p>Honestly, it’s a miracle that I can even maintain it for more than the next few months without needing a ton of spirit crystals. If I bring anyone else in here, it could collapse completely.”</p>

<p>Ruxue’s last words immediately captured Qingyi’s attention.</p>

<p>“Is it possible to bring other people here? How many?”</p>

<p>“It depends on my power level. At my peak, millions. Now… no more than a few dozen, but space is still very unstable.”</p>

<p>“I see…” Qingyi scratched his chin, his eyes focusing on the temple.</p>

<p>“That’s the place where we used to put the temple’s talents to grow faster.</p>

<p>With the density of lightning Qi here being very high, it’s like a paradise for them,” Ruxue explained, noticing Qingyi’s interested gaze.</p>

<p>He couldn’t stop his mind from filling with ideas. This place had so much potential. Unfortunately, there was no time for that.</p>

<p>“How do I get out of here?” he asked.</p>

<p>“Hm… it’s like moving your fingers, just do it.”</p>

<p>Qingyi couldn’t help raising an eyebrow at that vague explanation, but in the end, he just followed it.</p>

<p>Closing his eyes, he tried to follow the instructions. After a few failed attempts, he finally felt his mind being pulled.</p>

<p>The cool, pleasant breeze accompanied by Ruxue’s sweet perfume was replaced by the stale, smelly air of the cave.</p>

<p>Qingyi forced his aching body to stand, picking up the Heaven-Defying Thunder Sword and looking up.</p>

<p>He could hear heavy footsteps and the shouts of soldiers.</p>

<p>The guard sent from Icy Flower City seemed to have arrived.</p>

<p>“Argh… damn… did I die?” Juechen’s pained voice echoed as he forced himself up.</p>

<p>His eyes focused on Huntao’s fallen figure before finally settling on Qingyi.</p>

<p>Apparently, he was alive.</p>

<p>“What happened? Where’s Ruhu? And that damned old woman?” Juechen asked.</p>

<p>“We killed her,” Qingyi replied.</p>

<p>“Luckily, I had a talisman with the master’s power. Unfortunately, Ruhu couldn’t resist.”</p>

<p>“Crap!” Juechen gritted his teeth, jumping into the dry lake and kneeling over Ruhu’s body.</p>

<p>Ruhu was an annoying bastard and Juechen would be lying if he said his heart was heavy over his death.</p>

<p>He had already completed hundreds of missions in the sect, but this was the first time a disciple under his command had died. That’s what made him feel so bad.</p>

<p>“Qingyi…” He stood up. “I won’t question you about Ruhu’s death, but did you find out anything about what they wanted to do with so many people?”</p>

<p>“Yes… they were trying to summon a demon, a true demon.” Qingyi said, noticing a look of confusion appear on Juechen’s face.</p>

<p>Fortunately, Juechen didn’t ask any more questions, he just approached Huntao and picked him up.</p>

<p>“I’ll report your deeds to the sect master. You will receive a reward to match.”</p>

<p>***</p>

<p>“The footprints end here, my lord,” one of the soldiers reported to the officer Qingyi and his group had met when they arrived in the city.</p>

<p>“Shit… keep looking, they can’t be far.” The officer’s words died in his throat as he felt the ground beneath his feet give way.</p>

<p>With a startled gasp, he jumped away, watching a powerful hand emerge from the snow, grabbing where it could and dragging a powerful body out.</p>

<p>It was Qingyi and Juechen, who were carrying the unconscious Huntao and Ruhu’s corpse.</p>

<p>As soon as they left the cave, the snow collapsed completely, revealing the interior.</p>

<p>“Young master Juechen!” The officer approached, noticing the blood-soaked clothes of the sect disciples.</p>

<p>He looked over them, his stomach turning the moment he saw inside.</p>

<p>“They’re all dead. We didn’t make it in time, I’m sorry,” Juechen said, slapping the trembling officer on the back.</p>

<p>The man lowered his head, gritting his teeth before ordering: “Prepare ropes and ladders! We must at least recover the bodies of our own!”</p>

<p>Juechen and Qingyi didn’t stay there much longer.</p>

<p>They had accomplished their mission and now it was time to finally return to the sect.</p>

<p>The journey back was silent.</p>

<p>Huntao had suffered severe damage to his meridian and was cultivating in a separate carriage while Qingyi and Juechen went in the other.</p>

<p>It wasn’t long before the snowy forests were replaced by tundra and soon by lush, dense green forests.</p>

<p>After a few more days of travel, they finally reached the sect.</p>

<p>Ruhu’s corpse being taken away by the sect naturally caused quite a stir.</p>

<p>Ruhu was not only one of the most talented disciples, but also the apprentice of one of the most powerful elders.</p>

<p>His death came as a shock to everyone.</p>

<p>Qingyi just ignored those looks, saying goodbye to Juechen and paying a brief visit to Feiyan before going to the girls in the temple.</p>
