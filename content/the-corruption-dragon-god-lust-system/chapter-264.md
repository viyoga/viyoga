<p>About a week after the duel between Qingyi and Tianyu, the leader of the ancestral dragon sect took over the dragon battalion.</p>

<p>He reorganized its tens of thousands of members and even provided weapons and armour from his sect’s personal arsenal.</p>

<p>All the other sects and families did the same, armies of tens of thousands of disciples being gathered, marching towards the orthodox alliance.</p>

<p>Even recruiting stations were set up in free cities, recruiting any cultivator above the Burning Blood realm willing to fight against the demon cult.</p>

<p>It was impossible to deny that war was approaching, even the lowest commoners knew it.</p>

<p>Qingyi himself didn’t stop working either.</p>

<p>During these two months, he did nothing but dual cultivation, devoting himself to not only increasing both his own cultivation and that of the girls, but also to accumulating lust points.</p>

<p>In the first week, he had already reached 15 million lust points, a figure that was quickly consumed by his progress in the rebirth realm.</p>

<p>From the second week onwards, this amount remained stable at between 10-15 million per week.</p>

<p>This was an amount he still considered absurd, even taking into account that his life was reduced to nothing but sex and good food during this period.</p>

<p>It was hard work, for sure, but Qingyi couldn’t deny that it was worth it.</p>

<p>His own cultivation had reached the peak of the rebirth realm, with all the girls being at least at the fourth stage.</p>

<p>With the current power of their lineage, each was easily capable of fighting a weaker transcendent.</p>

<p>His eyes shone, flowing to his status screen.</p>

<p>[Name: Long Qingyi</p>

<p>Age: 20</p>

<p>Cultivation: Ninth Stage of the Rebirth Realm (10,000,000 lust points to upgrade)</p>

<p>Affinities: Lightning: 248 – Sword: 257 – Fire: 234 – Ice:223 – Charm: 1000 (can be upgraded in the lust store)</p>

<p>Spiritual roots: Mortal-level three-colored spirit root (Lightning sword, fire, ice) (1,000,000 lust points to upgrade, new roots can be bought in the lust store).</p>

<p>Lineage: Blood of the Corruption Dragon God (Mortal grade, 100,000,000 lust points to upgrade.)</p>

<p>Constitution: Primordial Dragon Body (Mortal grade, 100,000,000 lust points to upgrade.)</p>

<p>Lust points: 44,413,193]</p>

<p>He still had more than enough to jump straight to the transcendence realm, but he held back, listening to Ruxue’s advice.</p>

<p>At his current level, he was probably already stronger than any cultivator at the peak of the transcendence realm, and advancing to this realm was much more complicated than he had imagined.</p>

<p>He needed time, time to feel the primordial laws, time to feel the heavens; only then would he be ready for a breakthrough.</p>

<p>Even the system might not guarantee that he would succeed in creating a powerful law otherwise.</p>

<p>“System… I need defensive artifacts for all the girls, powerful enough to detect danger and immediately transport them to Ruxue’s world of the mind, what do you recommend?”</p>

<p>[Item: Void Veil Ring</p>

<p>Description: A space ring covered in powerful defensive formations, capable of detecting any danger to the wearer, activating automatically and transporting the wearer to safety. It has a storage space of 500 cubic meters</p>

<p>Grade: Mythical</p>

<p>Value: 250,000 lust points].</p>

<p>Looking at that value and the description, Qingyi nodded.</p>

<p>It was a small, golden ring, adorned with a single, elegant jade stone at the top.</p>

<p>For the price and functionality, it was cheap.</p>

<p>Closing his eyes, Qingyi entered the world of Ruxue’s mind, immediately gathering the girls together and handing them each a ring.</p>

<p>Naturally, they were all extremely happy; it wasn’t every day that their husband gave them presents, especially such expensive ones.</p>

<p>In total, he had spent 7.5 million lust points on 30 void veil rings, a considerable sum, but nothing compared to their security.</p>

<p>After having lunch with them, Qingyi finally returned to the outside world.</p>

<p>He was at Biyue’s residence, more specifically, in her personal cultivation room.</p>

<p>With a soft sigh, he stood up, every muscle in his body tensing as he felt his own power.</p>

<p>All those breakthroughs had brought him to a level where he could proudly declare: there was no enemy under mortal heaven that he dared not challenge.</p>

<p>Suddenly, a slight tremor ran through Qingyi’s body and he walked to the balcony of the residence.</p>

<p>Raising his head, he looked up at the sky, his heartbeat accelerating.</p>

<p>Qingyi was feeling strange. Something in his chest, a line; thin, subtle, almost imperceptible, but it was there, rising up to the heavens, touching them.</p>

<p>He had heard stories in ancient books of the heavens above mortals.</p>

<p>In those places, the primordial laws were so powerful that a name could make the world tremble, that every challenge to the heavens was answered immediately; provided, of course, that the heavens deemed the challenger worthy.</p>

<p>That was also the reason why transcendents were the only ones who could ascend to mortal heaven.</p>

<p>As well as having a law of their own, capable of modifying the primordial laws around them, transcendents also formed a connection with the heavens.</p>

<p>It was from this connection that they could challenge the heavens and, if they won, they could ascend.</p>

<p>Qingyi already feeling this connection only made it clear how powerful he truly already was.</p>

<p>Turning around, he got dressed, taking one last look at the horizon before heading down to the lower levels of the snake battalion’s administrative pavilion.</p>

<p>Things were chaotic down there, people running around, piles of documents piling up on the desks of the attendants who were desperately trying to register all the new warriors who would be under the snake battalion in the next war.</p>

<p>Qingyi ignored all this, happy that it wasn’t his problem.</p>

<p>Stepping outside, he turned his face towards the great wall on the horizon, his eyes focusing on the figure of a woman under one of the many towers that protected it.</p>

<p>Her beautiful green eyes watched the sun set over the horizon, her long black hair dancing in the gentle breeze.</p>

<p>With a gentle push, he flew towards her, his face showing a big smile as he hugged her from behind.</p>
