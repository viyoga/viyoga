<p>[Item: Nine Paths of Creation Reversal Art</p>

<p>Description: ???</p>

<p>Rank: ???]</p>

<p>Qingyi stared at the status screen floating before his eyes, his eyebrows furrowing slightly in confusion.</p>

<p>"What is this...?"</p>

<p>[A supreme cultivation technique, created by the Great Creator himself when he forged the cultivation path. You'll understand better if you manage to study it.]</p>

<p>The system's response made Qingyi sink into a heavy silence for a long moment.</p>

<p>He had never imagined he would need a cultivation technique, not with the system always by his side. But the moment he heard the origin of that technique, his heart trembled involuntarily.</p>

<p>A technique created by the being who could be defined as the sole absolute in the entire universe...</p>

<p>Why on earth was something like that here, displayed as a mere tournament prize?</p>

<p>Even the great orthodox sects of the central region would tear each other apart, forgetting the very existence of the Heavenly Demon, for a technique of that caliber.</p>

<p>[The Nine Paths of Creation Reversal Art is more than just a cultivation technique. It is natural that no one below the land of the true gods would be able to recognize it. You must be very careful and, at all costs, claim it for yourself.]</p>

<p>"This book may seem ordinary..." The Peacock Patriarch's voice cut through the air, echoing throughout the arena and drowning out the system's warnings.</p>

<p>"Many of us thought the same for a long time. But after years of study, we discovered not only that it is indestructible and impossible to open, but also that, when placed in a cultivation chamber, it possesses the ability to increase the density of Qi by up to ten times over what the arrays themselves provide!"</p>

<p>The instant those words echoed across the arena, the crowd's eyes lit up with raw, unbridled greed.</p>

<p>The most expensive type of formation in the northern region of the Celestial heaven could barely raise the Qi density in an area by a hundredfold, yet that book, on its own, multiplied that density by another tenfold? That was a thousandfold multiplication factor!</p>

<p>The mere idea of cultivating in an environment so saturated with Qi was enough to make every cultivator present drool with desire.</p>

<p>Ignoring the system's furious curses, which called those people pigs and monkeys incapable of discerning true treasures, Qingyi merely let out a restrained sigh, the corners of his lips lifting slightly.</p>

<p>He had never seen the system react that way before, and it only further inflamed his curiosity.</p>

<p>'Nine Paths of Creation Reversal Art...' he repeated to himself, eyes narrowing as Auranys' voice echoed in his mind through the connection with Ruxue.</p>

<p>[The Celestial Father has already mentioned this cultivation technique... It serves to cultivate both mana and Qi, and it was by using just a small fragment of it that the Father was able to create us.</p>

<p>This book appears to be complete, so do your best to obtain it! Since you possess both Qi and Mana within you, you should be able to open it and read its contents.]</p>

<p>Hearing that, Qingyi nodded solemnly in his mind, keeping his expression impassive.</p>

<p>A technique so powerful that just a fragment of it was enough to create gods...</p>

<p>How could one not be excited?</p>

<p>"Father-in-law." Qingyi turned his face toward the sect leader, who was practically drooling already, imagining using that book to amplify the Qi density in a chamber and fill it with geniuses, forging unparalleled elites for the sect.</p>

<p>"Uh, yes?" The leader choked slightly, snapping his face toward Qingyi.</p>

<p>"I will reach first place and obtain this book, but I cannot share it with the sect. I have my own use for it." Qingyi spoke coldly, leaving no room for questions.</p>

<p>The moment those words hit his ears, the Cosmic Dawn Sect leader's face fell.</p>

<p>He almost tried to argue, but in the end, he merely let out a resigned sigh.</p>

<p>He might not know Qingyi as well as his own daughter did, but at least he knew that his son-in-law wouldn't waste a resource of that caliber.</p>

<p>If Qingyi wanted it just for himself, he certainly had a good reason.</p>

<p>Seeing that his father-in-law wouldn't use his position to take the item by force, Qingyi smiled with satisfaction and refocused his eyes on the Peacock Patriarch.</p>

<p>Theoretically, that reward was private, intended exclusively for the best fighter, but in reality, it was the sect that decided its final fate.</p>

<p>"This book will be handed over to the tournament winner, along with the chance to choose any treasure from within the Myriad Beasts Sect's vault, and even a cultivation session guided by the sect's ancestor herself!"</p>

<p>The instant the Peacock Patriarch's voice fell silent, the audience erupted into an even greater frenzy, enthusiastic murmurs sweeping through the arena like a tide.</p>

<p>The Peacock Clan's Star Sovereign smiled at the commotion, his handsome features revealing just how much he loved being the center of attention.</p>

<p>With a gentle flap of his wings, his human form rose higher into the air.</p>

<p>"Now, we will draw lots for the first match!" the Peacock Patriarch declared, and in the next instant, he snapped his slender fingers.</p>

<p>The mana around him flickered, and on the screen floating above the arena, a thin beam of light began to scan through hundreds of names at breakneck speed, until it finally settled on two that stood side by side.</p>

<p>"Bai Hu Lie versus Lou Qingyao!"</p>

<p>The Peacock Patriarch's voice had barely finished echoing when an imposing figure burst out of the box belonging to the Myriad Beasts Sect's leader.</p>

<p>He landed in the arena with a sharp thud that sent a tremor rippling through the ground, dust scattering around his feet.</p>

<p>Raising his head with a wide smile, the tiger bared sharp fangs that glinted under the light.</p>

<p>From another box, a trembling figure slowly emerged, already cursing his rotten luck through clenched teeth.</p>

<p>His pale face stood in stark contrast to his opponent's confident expression.</p>

<p>Wasn't it enough to have to face Bai Hu Lie? Did he also have to be the unlucky first to enter the arena?</p>

<p>"Don't worry, little human…" Bai Hu Lie crossed his arms. "I'll save a little of you for your funeral."</p>
