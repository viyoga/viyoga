<p>The largest orc army ever assembled in tens of thousands of years, more than forty million strong, with ten of these orcs in the twelfth ring.</p>

<p>Naturally, such an army brought panic to all the regions surrounding the swamps of the Green Skins.</p>

<p>Especially the kingdom of Valemont, which usually only received attacks from border orc armies, infinitely inferior in power to that one, where a single warlord would be enough to exterminate the entire kingdom.</p>

<p>That army, in fact, was the main reason David was losing so much sleep.</p>

<p>But fortunately, it wouldn’t last long.</p>

<p>From afternoon to the darkness of night, and at dawn, two figures continued to fight relentlessly, draining their energy as they eliminated horde after horde.</p>

<p>When they finished, shortly before noon, some piles of corpses reached hundreds of meters in height.</p>

<p>The few remaining orcs, cowards in front of their own people, ran to the hills.</p>

<p>“Ah… this is good…</p>

<p>Lucios flashed a big smile, feeling his blood boil as he sat on the corpse of an ogre over eight meters tall, enslaved by one of the orc warlords.</p>

<p>The smell of blood was terrible and putrid, but neither of them seemed to pay attention to it.</p>

<p>They were already used to that smell. They had killed too many to not be.</p>

<p>Humans, orcs, spirit beasts. They all rotted in the end.</p>

<p>Lucios pulled a metal bottle from his blood-soaked robes.</p>

<p>With a big smile, he took an enthusiastic swig and then threw the bottle to Qingyi.</p>

<p>“Hm… is that rum?” The handsome young man asked after also taking a sip, feeling the honeyed liquid slide down his throat.</p>

<p>“Yes… do you know it? It’s quite common among human pirates in the southern tropical islands.” Lucios smiled. “It’s hard to import, but it’s worth it.”</p>

<p>“Hm… most definitely.” Qingyi sat down next to him, sharing the drink.</p>

<p>“If the Rose Empire were in full condition, it should use this chance to attack these lands and behead those Green Skins once and for all.” Lucios said, looking at the destruction that surrounded them.</p>

<p>Goblins, kobolds, and other green skins were basically useless without them.</p>

<p>To recreate such a force would take thousands of years, but the Rose Empire would likely need no more than a few centuries to recover almost entirely from all its losses.</p>

<p>At that moment, a few million soldiers and a couple of experts from the twelfth ring were enough to completely exterminate that plague from the world.</p>

<p>“Ah…” The handsome young man stretched, looking up and seeing the sun high in the sky.</p>

<p>“I think it’s time to go back. The girls must be worried.” Qingyi stood up, reaching out his hand to Lucios.</p>

<p>Feeling the firm handshake, the old man smiled, and then, the two were swallowed up by a space fold again.</p>
