<p>Slowly, the figures watching Qingyi revealed themselves.</p>

<p>They were just like ordinary humans, the only real difference being the presence of sharp claws, a pair of feline ears and a fluffy tail, as well as eyes not different from those of an ordinary cat.</p>

<p>They wore clothes made from different types of animal skins and held spears, bows and throwing knives.</p>

<p>“They look like catfolk, they come from the same land as Xueyao’s people.”</p>

<p>Ruxue’s voice echoed in Qingyi’s mind, who nodded, still not letting his guard down.</p>

<p>There were around twenty opponents. Most of them were in the False Immortal realm, with only one whose cultivation was so deep that Qingyi could barely sense him.</p>

<p>Strangely, he wasn’t among the group that surrounded Qingyi, just watching silently from afar.</p>

<p>His eyes showed surprise when he saw Qingyi’s gaze focused on him.</p>

<p>“This one looks strong…”</p>

<p>“Would he be good prey for the great hunt?”</p>

<p>“I don’t know… he looks like a human, isn’t the great hunt only for beasts?”</p>

<p>Murmurs echoed among the cat people surrounding Qingyi.</p>

<p>They were all young boys, the eldest appearing to be no more than seventeen, despite his profound cultivation.</p>

<p>“Brats…” Qingyi lost his temper when he heard those words, taking a Qi recovery pill while calling on the little bit of bloodline Qi that had already recovered.</p>

<p>“One more word, and you’ll be the prey!” He roared.</p>

<p>The figure of an enormous dragon appeared on his back, its bestial roar echoing for miles, shaking the forest.</p>

<p>The young men backed away in fright and the expert watching the scene shook his head, sighing before leaping towards them, landing in front of Qingyi.</p>

<p>He was a middle-aged man in the True Immortal realm, clearly at the limit of his talent, unlike those young cat men.</p>

<p>“Young dragon, I beg your pardon for the disrespect of the younger ones, but I ask you to control your aura. After all, you’re on our tribe’s territory.”</p>

<p>The expert spoke, his feline eyes scanning Qingyi curiously as his tail moved gently across his back.</p>

<p>The handsome young man hesitated for a brief moment before retracting his bloodline Qi. This was no time for that kind of fight, not in his current state.</p>

<p>Besides, unlike the young men, that man didn’t seem to have any hostile intent towards Qingyi, treating him with a strange respect.</p>

<p>“Junior Long Qingyi salutes senior,” Qingyi clenched his fists, but didn’t bow. “May I ask what you mean by ‘young dragon’? I am a human.”</p>

<p>“A human?” The True Immortal sniffed the air, his feline ears rising slightly before he shook his head.</p>

<p>“You don’t smell like a human… no, there are traces of human scent on you, but it’s almost gone.”</p>

<p>“I…” Qingyi thought for a brief moment, looking at his own hands.</p>

<p>He remembered Xueyao saying something similar after he improved his lineage and, honestly, he was confused.</p>

<p>Lineages shouldn’t change this kind of thing, not naturally and without using specific lineage techniques that allowed them to take on forms linked to their ancestors.</p>

<p>Qingyi had one of these techniques, the black dragon scales, but this technique should only affect him while he had it active.</p>

<p>The rest of the time, he was supposed to be like any other human, only with a much more powerful body and senses.</p>

<p>“Ah… it doesn’t matter.” The true immortal spoke, turning to the youngsters. “What are you waiting for, brats? Let’s go back to the tribe! We have a guest.”</p>

<p>“Yes, elder!” The young men shouted before disappearing into the forest, leaping through the trees with surprising agility.</p>

<p>After everyone had left, the True Immortal turned to Qingyi.</p>

<p>“Our tribe isn’t generally hostile to visitors, quite the opposite, but you still smell of human blood. I need to know the reason for that.”</p>

<p>“Of course, senior, this junior was in a battle before he ended up here after falling into a space trap, but I can assure you that I have no bad intentions. In fact, I intend to leave as soon as possible.”</p>

<p>Qingyi spoke, drawing a gentle smile from the True Immortal.</p>

<p>“I believe you. It’s rare that we have young dragons among us, so please accompany me. At least stay to watch the great hunt.”</p>

<p>“Great hunt?” Qingyi asked, searching for that term in his mind.</p>

<p>“It’s nothing anyone outside the lands of the, as you call us, ‘beast men’, should know.” The True Immortal chuckled.</p>

<p>“In short, the youngest princess of our tribe has reached maturity and, as a great huntress, has asked for her husband to be chosen in a great hunt involving any young man who has the courage to try his luck and catch the best game.”</p>

<p>“Ah… I understand…” Qingyi scratched his chin, walking alongside the immortal on the same trail that the other cat men had followed.</p>

<p>In the end, this was, or at least appeared to be, an extremely primitive civilization, although powerful enough to have so many young people in the realm of the false immortal.</p>

<p>Qingyi could only imagine how powerful the cultivation of the leader of this tribe would be. Perhaps an immortal emperor? Or something even more powerful than that?</p>

<p>Naturally, Qingyi was excited to meet such a powerful being.</p>

<p>“Won’t you join in too?” The true immortal turned his face, his white feline ears twitching slightly as he sensed Qingyi’s emotions.</p>

<p>His hearing was sensitive enough to sense Qingyi’s heart racing slightly, his senses being sharper than Qingyi was even capable of imagining.</p>

<p>In the end, that was the cat people’s main strength: incomparable reaction time and senses, barely inferior to those Qingyi gained from using his draconic eyes.</p>

<p>“Can outsiders join in too?” Qingyi asked.</p>

<p>“Yes, in fact, anyone whose cultivation is below the False Immortal realm and age is under 25 can join, regardless of their origin.</p>

<p>This is a competition to choose the most capable of the younger generation, after all.”</p>

<p>“Well… I’ll think about it…” Qingyi said, scratching his chin.</p>

<p>He couldn’t make a decision without seeing this woman, could he? What if she was like the princess of the Blue Sea Kingdom?</p>

<p>Qingyi could even imagine having to run away from a crazy whale in the shape of a cat woman.</p>

<p>He didn’t want that kind of headache in his life.</p>

<p>After a few more minutes of walking, the true immortal stopped his steps, as did Qingyi.</p>

<p>They already seemed to have reached their destination.</p>
