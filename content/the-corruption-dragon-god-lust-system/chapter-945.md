<p>The Black Diamond’s cockpit was spacious, its interior entirely white, just like the rest of the spaceship interior.</p>

<p>The only exception was the enormous windshield, which loomed before them like a wall of deep black.</p>

<p>Their footsteps echoed softly across the immaculate floor. The air inside was cool and carried a faint, clean scent.</p>

<p>The moment they stepped inside, the panel lit up.</p>

<p>The blackness of the glass began to fade from the edges toward the center, thinning in a matter of seconds until it vanished completely and revealed the world outside.</p>

<p>The cabin sat just over one hundred meters behind the tip of the spacecraft, which itself stretched for about two hundred and fifty meters.</p>

<p>The front of the ship tapered into the perfect shape of a black sword, narrowing to a faintly silvery tip cut like a diamond.</p>

<p>The cabin, meanwhile, rose gently from the main fuselage in a modern, oval shape.</p>

<p>It must have measured four or five hundred square meters, with roughly a hundred retractable seats scattered throughout, most of them stowed away in the floor.</p>

<p>Qingyi walked to the front of the cabin and stopped right up against the glass. From there, he had a clear view of the ship’s beautiful body stretching out before him, the black fuselage so polished it looked mirrors.</p>

<p>A few meters ahead, he could make out two sections of the fuselage marked by thin lines—clearly covers that could open. They likely concealed the ship’s small-caliber weapons.</p>

<p>Further ahead, right in the center, was a larger compartment, also sealed, hiding the main armament inside.</p>

<p>Qingyi caught a whiff of Ziyue’s scent, faint and floral, a moment before he heard her voice—sweet and understated—as she came to a stop beside him.</p>

<p>"Those are the small caliber weapons." She pointed to the two hatches with a delicate finger.</p>

<p>"They can fire up to two hundred rounds per minute, each carrying the full power of a Cosmic Emperor. One recharge of the Qi crystals that power them should be enough for about one hundred thousand shots."</p>

<p>She sent a faint pulse of Qi into the ship. The compartments responded instantly, the metal sliding sideways with a low hiss as two ninety-millimeter cannons rose from within the fuselage.</p>

<p>Each was about three meters long, its color ranging from obsidian black to gold, the hue shifting as the light played across the metal.</p>

<p>At that very moment, a small reticle appeared on the glass, a thin ring of pale light hovering over the empty space ahead.</p>

<p>The two cannons rotated a few degrees with a faint hum until they were perfectly aligned with it.</p>

<p>"The reticle calculates distance and trajectory on its own," Ziyue continued, leaning her hip lightly against the console, the fabric pulling snug over its curve.</p>

<p>"All you have to do is use your own vision to place it over a target. The cannons automatically adjust the firing angle, the power, and even the projectile velocity."</p>

<p>She gave a little smile, as if saving the best part for last.</p>

<p>"And it can also assess a cultivator’s power level, adjusting the firing power accordingly—up or down."</p>

<p>Once she finished explaining, Ziyue held out her hand. Resting between her fingers was a small obsidian token, smooth and cold. The ship’s keys.</p>

<p>She placed it in Qingyi’s palm, her fingertips brushing his skin for a brief, cool moment.</p>

<p>He closed his hand around the token and infused his Qi into it.</p>

<p>The response came in the blink of an eye: a strange sensation of expansion, as if his mind had gained a new, enormous, metallic body.</p>

<p>He could feel the cold skin of the hull, the idle thrum of the engines somewhere far behind him, the Qi circulating through countless circuits like blood through veins.</p>

<p>Within a second, he was fully connected to the ship’s central computer, feeling each system as an extension of himself.</p>

<p>"Ah! Dadda!" Lingxi let out an excited squeal, tugging at the hem of his shirt and pointing outside with a tiny finger.</p>

<p>Qingyi had just taken control of the cannons with his own Qi, and they now moved in sync with his eyes.</p>

<p>He tested them, sweeping his gaze from side to side.</p>

<p>The two cannons swiveled at once, following every flick of his eyes and locking onto whatever he focused on, a short whir accompanying each adjustment.</p>

<p>Lingxi burst into delighted laughter, bouncing on her toes as her wide eyes chased the cannons back and forth.</p>

<p>To actually fire, though, he would need the joystick in front of the pilot’s seat.</p>

<p>It had two triggers: one for the main cannon and another for the secondary cannons.</p>

<p>"You’re a quick learner," Ziyue said jokingly, though there was genuine surprise behind her light tone, one brow arching ever so slightly.</p>

<p>Qingyi had needed only a single second to fuse his Qi with the ship’s.</p>

<p>She then turned toward the compartment directly ahead of the cockpit and gave a slight wave of her hand.</p>

<p>The metal slid open to the sides, and from within rose a gigantic five-hundred-millimeter Qi cannon.</p>

<p>It was as thick as a tree trunk and long enough to make the secondary cannons look like toys, yet it rose without making a single sound, its shadow crawling across the polished fuselage as it climbed.</p>

<p>Lingxi let out a long "oooh," her small mouth hanging open as her head tilted back to follow the barrel.</p>

<p>"This one can’t be moved." Ziyue crossed her arms beneath her chest, her tone growing a little more serious. "It can fire one shot every thirty seconds, and each shot has the power to wound a mid-level Celestial Emperor."</p>

<p>She raised a finger.</p>

<p>"But it can only fire one hundred shots before the ship enters energy-saving mode. When that happens, every other combat system shuts down. Only the shields, the engines, and the life-support systems remain active.</p>

<p>You can use your own body as a battery in extreme cases. But I don’t recommend it."</p>

<p>Qingyi listened to everything in silence, his eyes still fixed on the enormous cannon outside, taking in every detail.</p>

<p>"It truly is a beautiful and powerful spaceship." He nodded slowly, and then a faint smile tugged at his lips. "I’m afraid all the treasure I stole from the Heavenly Demon won’t be enough to pay for it."</p>

<p>Obviously, he was exaggerating.</p>

<p>The ship was amazing, yes, but it still fell short of a true Celestial Emperor. And he had stolen enough resources to finance the birth of dozens of Celestial Emperors.</p>

<p>"Fufufu~, don’t worry about the price, dear." Ziyue covered her lips with her fingers, letting out a sweet, feminine, and elegant laugh. "I owe Tianjin and little Yuehan’s father many favors. I could never charge them for something like this."</p>

<p>She pushed away from the console and walked toward the pilot’s seat,.</p>

<p>"Come on, take the pilot’s seat. I have a space helmet that can let the little one aim and fire a few shots too." She winked at Lingxi. "She’ll love it."</p>

<p>Lingxi’s eyes went wide, her little hands clenching tighter on Qingyi’s clothes as she looked up at him, as if asking whether she had really heard right.</p>

<p>Ziyue patted the back of the pilot’s seat.</p>

<p>White, wide, and upholstered in something that looked comfortable just to look at, it sat on a swivel base right in the center of the cockpit.</p>

<p>In front of it was the joystick that controlled the ship and also housed the triggers for the weapon system. To the right sat the throttle. To the left, a digital control panel, its screen glowing a soft blue, waiting for a pilot.</p>
