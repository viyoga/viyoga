<p>For Yuehan, it was a little hard not to feel frustrated.</p>

<p>She had first read about the possibility of bodily resonance in one of the many lost books kept in the private library in Tianjin.</p>

<p>At the time, she had been sixteen. She'd spent the next fourteen years practicing those concepts, one by one, until she finally mastered fragmented bodily resonance.</p>

<p>At fifty—twenty years later—she had finally achieved perfect and complete bodily resonance.</p>

<p>And yet, she still hadn't passed the technique on to anyone. Because, well… she still didn't think she'd mastered it at a high enough level to teach it.</p>

<p>Qingyi, in less than a minute, had already reached a level where he seemed to have perfect control over it!</p>

<p>Wasn't that a little too unfair?</p>

<p>Yuehan let out a long, defeated sigh, and that finally caught Qingyi's attention.</p>

<p>"Don't be so surprised." Qingyi smiled, deactivating the bodily resonance.</p>

<p>"My cultivation technique already incorporated concepts of bodily resonance, but I'd never noticed it. I'd never fully comprehended the passages where it was described, either. When I saw you use it, I had an epiphany."</p>
