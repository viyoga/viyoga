<p>” Dammit!” Hei Hunli roared, throwing another empty bottle, the glass shattering as it hit the wall.</p>

<p>His palace was dark and, by his own orders, not even his closest guards dared to disturb him.</p>

<p>The leader’s powerful body sank into his throne, his trembling eyes closing.</p>

<p>He still held his power, his fortune and many of his soldiers, but his heart was in pieces.</p>

<p>All this had been built up by him and his brothers, by a promise they had made to their mother: never to bow their heads to anyone beneath the heavens.</p>

<p>What was the point of going through with that promise without them there?</p>

<p>“Those bitches… this is all their fault!” He clenched his fists hard enough for his nails to sink into his palms and draw blood.</p>

<p>Hunli wanted revenge, he wanted all their heads decorating the walls of his fortress. But he simply couldn’t act.</p>

<p>With each bottle of liquor he finished, he set off for the next.</p>

<p>He hadn’t slept for days. His vision was blurred and his steps staggered as he opened his personal cellar, where his decades-old collection of wines and liquors should have been.</p>

<p>But it was empty – he had already drunk all he had to drink.</p>

<p>“Shadow…” He called out to his most loyal servant, but there was no answer.</p>

<p>“Shadow, where the hell are you? Answer when your master calls-” Hunli’s voice suddenly died away.</p>

<p>Shadow was also in that attack and had died with his brothers.</p>

<p>He gritted his teeth, his face filling with hatred as he gripped the mighty axe displayed above his throne.</p>

<p>He was going to attack that damned temple right now!</p>

<p>He reached the mighty double doors of his throne room and prepared to open them, but froze at the last moment.</p>

<p>The entire fortress of the Red Claw Gang shook with a thunderous explosion.</p>

<p>His eyes widened and his heart almost leapt out of his chest as an aura fell over him.</p>

<p>It was weaker than his own, certainly belonging to a cultivator of the second stage of the Foundation Realm, but the density of the Qi was much greater, charged with a murderous intent that made every hair on his body stand on end.</p>

<p>He breathed deeply.</p>

<p>The shouts of his men soon reached his ears and he forced himself to take a step out of the fortress.</p>

<p>In the center of the courtyard stood a young man. His handsome face displayed a cold smile as his sword tore through the air around him, a blade of Qi forming from its slash and heading towards the bandits coming out of their tents.</p>

<p>The men’s angry eyes were soon filled with terror as the second form of the Tempest Monarch’s Sword Art reached them, torn bodies piling up on the ground.</p>

<p>Hunli felt his blood run cold. A single attack and the Red Claw Gang stronghold fell silent.</p>

<p>The invader obviously hadn’t killed everyone, but none of the survivors had the courage to wait for the second attack.</p>

<p>Qingyi ignored them. They were just small fish – with Elize and Yueli’s current power, they would have no problem dealing with them if they tried to cause trouble.</p>

<p>His eyes rose, focusing on the powerful but trembling figure.</p>

<p>Hunli took a step back under that gaze, but soon realized something.</p>

<p>That boy was identical to the description of the man who killed his brothers, given by his escorts.</p>

<p>At that moment, all the fear in his heart was erased, replaced by a deep hatred.</p>

<p>“You bastard!” Hei Hunli roared, his feet sinking into the ground as his huge body was catapulted towards Qingyi.</p>

<p>“Ugh…” Qingyi grunted as he defended himself.</p>

<p>All four Hei brothers were cultivators with flame spirit roots, but Hunli’s fire Qi was incomparable to that of his brothers.</p>

<p>Qingyi was forced back two steps, the fire Qi hitting his lightning sword Qi and burning everything behind him.</p>

<p>“You will pay for my brothers’ deaths!” Hunli put even more force into his axe, pushing every drop of his Qi towards the young man.</p>

<p>But even his seventh stage foundation cultivation was no match for Qingyi’s current power.</p>

<p>Sighing heavily, the handsome young man turned his body, letting Hunli’s axe pass through him before aiming for the criminal leader’s unprotected flank.</p>

<p>Gathering all his Qi into his sword and arms, he used the first form of the Tempest Monarch’s Sword Art.</p>

<p>Hunli barely managed to retract his axe in time, clumsily parrying Qingyi’s blow.</p>

<p>His legs gave way and he coughed up blood, retreating until his back hit the fortress wall.</p>

<p>’How can this bastard have so much Qi? And how can this Qi be so powerful?’ he asked himself, but Qingyi gave him little time to think.</p>

<p>The young man activated his Lightning Steps, covering his sword with a dense layer of bluish Qi, surrounded by fine silver threads.</p>

<p>His body exploded with speed, his sword striking Hunli’s axe.</p>

<p>A crash echoed and the wall shook, giving way under the bandit’s back as he was once again catapulted backwards, his body flying dozens of meters out of the fortress.</p>

<p>His eyes trembled as he tried to understand what was happening.</p>

<p>That young man was clearly at the second stage of the Foundation Realm. How powerful did his cultivation base have to be to cross so many stages?</p>

<p>Intense terror took over his face, but soon that expression changed completely</p>

<p>It was impossible for that young man to be so powerful with just his cultivation base – there had to be some artifact with him! A very powerful one!</p>

<p>That thought immediately dominated his mind as he reached into his robes and pulled out a red pill dotted with black spots.</p>

<p>He would kill that young man, take whatever artifact he had and then go after those damn priestesses before rebuilding his gang.</p>

<p>The death of his brothers would not be in vain.</p>

<p>With a cruel smile, he opened his mouth wide before throwing the pill in, but his smile soon disappeared as he looked down at his wrists.</p>

<p>The pill had fallen to the ground, covered in blood.</p>

<p>’Where are my hands?’ His eyes widened.</p>
