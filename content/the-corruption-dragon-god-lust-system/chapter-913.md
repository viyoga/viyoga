<p>"This is aged wine infused with the venom of the White-Scaled Divine Snake! I heard the Heavenly Demon nearly died trying to kill that snake just to make this wine." The Divine Blacksmith spun the bottle between his fingers, admiring the pearly blue liquid swirling inside.</p>

<p>With a simple flick of his wrist, he popped the cork loose. A strong, sweet aroma instantly spread through the hall.</p>

<p>The Divine Blacksmith didn't hesitate. He tipped the bottle back, and took a long swig.</p>

<p>A satisfied sigh rumbled out of him before he tossed it back to Qingyi.</p>

<p>Qingyi caught it in midair and took a sip as well. His eyes fluttered the instant the wine touched his tongue.</p>

<p>The taste was mediocre compared to Elize's cooking, but the effects were simply incredible.</p>

<p>"Ah… that son of a bitch really did have a lot of good stuff… I should've waited a little longer and tried to steal his personal spatial ring too, hahaha." Qingyi laughed, handing the bottle back to his father-in-law.</p>

<p>Of course, he wasn't crazy enough to actually try that.</p>

<p>Even with his immense spatial control, if he got anywhere near the Heavenly Demon with the power he had now, a single second would be enough to turn him into dust.</p>
