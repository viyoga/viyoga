<p>“hm… nyan~~” A soft sigh echoed from the rosy lips of a beauty with short orange hair and bright green eyes.</p>

<p>If Qingyi had been there, he would have recognized her as Linyue, youngest princess of the Cat Folk tribe.</p>

<p>She was in her bedroom, primitive for many other peoples, but a large and luxurious residence for the Cat People.</p>

<p>Lying on her bed, she turned over as she stretched, arching her back slightly, her large tits squeezing against the bed as her ass rose majestically.</p>

<p>After another moment like that, she sighed, got up and walked to another room, where the corpse of a huge spirit beast she had hunted a few hours ago lay.</p>

<p>It was a giant fire wolf over twenty meters long from tail to snout, a true monster in size and power.</p>

<p>Approaching, Linyue gave a gentle push and jumped onto the corpse of the beast, flipping in the air and landing elegantly on top of it.</p>

<p>“Ah… you were a pretty tough bastard to catch, huh?” She smiled, holding out her hands as her nails lengthened into sharp claws.</p>

<p>Her voice was sweet, high and soft.</p>

<p>In the blink of an eye, she made two movements, and ten small lines of wind Qi spread across the carcass.</p>

<p>In the next second, the skin split open, revealing the bloody flesh underneath.</p>

<p>Obviously, Linyue didn’t need to do things like that. Even with the primitive appearance of her tribe, they were still a people of incomparable wealth, with coffers containing sums so large that a nation like the Blue Sea kingdom could barely imagine.</p>

<p>Even so, she worked carefully, ensuring that not a single part of that beast would go to waste.</p>

<p>The eyes could be used for medicine, the fur for clothing and defensive artifacts, the Qi core to power arrays, the bones to make weapons and the blood for alchemy.</p>

<p>After a few hours of work, Linyue finally finished, giving a proud smile as she looked at everything she had managed to take from the wolf.</p>

<p>She was truly the one destined to be the best hunter in her tribe.</p>

<p>“Ah… why does Dad want me to get married so soon… I should just go straight into the competition and show those bastards what a real hunter is like!”</p>

<p>A big smile took over Linyue’s beautiful, delicate face as an idea popped into her mind.</p>

<p>Her next husband would be the one who got the best hunt, but no one could get the best hunt if she had already taken the best hunt, right?</p>

<p>After a short moment, Linyue prepared to go out and tell her father that she would also be taking part in the great hunt, but fortunately, it wasn’t necessary and soon, a powerful figure entered the place.</p>

<p>He was a cat man, but honestly, he looked more like a lion, over two meters tall, with a physique more suited to pure brute power than speed and agility. His broad, powerful shoulders wore the skin of a tiger as a cape.</p>

<p>That was Tai’Ren, the patriarch of the cat folk, a man whose cultivation was so deep that a single punch from him could erase a mountain.</p>

<p>He was at this moment totally suppressing his energy, but if he wasn’t, any observer would only need an instant to realize: that was an ancestral emperor.</p>

<p>“Father!” Linyue ran excitedly up to him, her eyes shining with excitement.</p>

<p>“You cheeky girl, I know that smile, what are you up to, eh?” Tai’Ren questioned, but there was no irritation in his voice. On the contrary, he watched Linyue with intense pride.</p>

<p>“Simple, I’m going to take part in the great hunt! The best hunter will become my husband, right? Well, if I’m the best hunter, there won’t be anyone to be my husband!”</p>

<p>Tai’Ren heard those words, his face taking on a slight frown before he sighed.</p>

<p>He even thought about speaking out against it, but he’d been that girl’s father long enough to know that it was no use.</p>

<p>The moment she put that idea into his head, that was exactly what she would do.</p>

<p>“If you manage to defeat every one of our young hunters, I’ll think about your case,” Tai’Ren said, watching his daughter’s face take on a deep glow.</p>

<p>“Now get ready, the event will start soon, you’ll do the opening slaughter.”</p>

<p>“Okay dad!” Linyue said, wiping the blood from her body, changing her clothes and finally running to her room, where her servants began the preparation for the opening slaughter.</p>

<p>Her body was covered in a soft, shiny paint, which strangely made her look even more sensual than before.</p>

<p>Her hair was carefully decorated, as were her robes and even her feline ears.</p>

<p>When she had finished, she gave a big smile, looking at herself in the mirror before following the servants.</p>

<p>Her appearance had to be covered by a veil and she was quickly taken from the city to the large central arena where the start of the event would be announced.</p>

<p>There were still a few hours to go, so she had to wait a while, spending her time throwing knives and eating some snacks she had prepared beforehand.</p>

<p>When the arena finally filled up and Linyue’s sharp hearing picked up the sounds of the crowd outside, she stood up, preparing herself for a short moment before leaving the small place where she was standing.</p>

<p>She appeared on the large central platform of the arena, which seemed to have been carved out of stone, covering more than five square kilometers.</p>

<p>Although the great hunt only had around a thousand participants, that arena was now filled with more than two hundred thousand people, almost half of all the citizens of the Cat People tribe.</p>

<p>“Uh… Nyan…” Linyue inhaled before exhaling heavily, her eyes sweeping over those watching.</p>

<p>She could see many familiar gazes falling on her, from the rich young masters of her tribe to the powerful elders, all watching her with interest.</p>

<p>Suddenly, her brief gaze stopped, filled with a deep tremor as she caught the gaze of one of the many young men in the audience.</p>

<p>It was a burning, penetrating gaze, beautiful dark eyes that seemed to see beyond her soul, adorning a face of incomparable beauty.</p>

<p>Linyue looked away, a soft red spreading across her cheeks.</p>
