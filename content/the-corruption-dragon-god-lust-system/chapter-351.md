<p>A heartbreaking scream echoed through the sky and a gush of crimson blood spurted from Elder Lu’s left arm, now reduced to nothing but a bloody stump.</p>

<p>Lioran’s eyes filled with terror as a resounding laugh reached his eardrums.</p>

<p>Had his father lost? Truly lost?</p>

<p>“Well done, son-in-law! Well done! Hahaha.” Tai’Ren punched his eldest son in the shoulder. “See? That’s what I saw in that young man, hahaha.”</p>

<p>Tai’Ren’s eldest son just bowed his head.</p>

<p>Honestly, he was a little regretful of having defended Elder Lu. He had disappointed his father for absolutely nothing.</p>

<p>Well, there was no need to cry over spilt milk.</p>

<p>“Are you finished, little cuckold Lu?” Qingyi smiled contemptuously as Elder Lu went down.</p>

<p>The old man coughed up another gulp of blood, squeezing a few pressure points on his arm to stop the bleeding.</p>

<p>He had made subtle attempts to break the seal on his cultivation, to kill Qingyi quickly with a single blow and then deal with the consequences afterwards.</p>

<p>Unfortunately for him, this wasn’t working.</p>

<p>Still, he calmed his mind. The battle wasn’t over yet.</p>

<p>To Qingyi’s surprise, Elder Lu didn’t despair or run away, he just flashed a bloody smile, despite the deep despair in his mind.</p>

<p>In the next moment, the air around him changed completely. He raised his remaining hand towards Qingyi, his body moving like that of a feline.</p>

<p>On his back, an image appeared – an enormous leopard, growing without stopping.</p>

<p>Ten meters, twenty meters, a hundred meters.</p>

<p>Shortly after one hundred and twenty meters from snout to tail, his lineage projection finally stopped growing. Its tremendous power fell on Qingyi, who just smiled.</p>

<p>“If that’s how you want to play.”</p>

<p>The moment Qingyi’s voice fell silent, he also released his bloodline Qi in full force.</p>

<p>An enormous black dragon appeared on his back, its roar making Elder Lu’s leopard look like a domestic kitten.</p>

<p>Its purple eyes glowed as its sharp teeth bit into the empty air angrily.</p>

<p>“Shit…” Elder Lu paled, trying to retreat as quickly as possible, using his strongest movement technique and natural feline agility to try to escape.</p>

<p>Qingyi obviously didn’t allow it.</p>

<p>He activated the lightning blink, his body turning into bluish-silver lightning as he chased after Elder Lu.</p>

<p>“Get lost!” Elder Lu suddenly turned around, sending a flurry of claps towards Qingyi, who smashed them one after the other.</p>

<p>Just before his blade penetrated Elder Lu’s neck, the old man bent his body strangely, taking a small scroll from his spatial ring and tearing it open.</p>

<p>Qingyi barely had time to react when a flash of light blinded him, followed by a powerful roar.</p>

<p>An enormous flaming tiger of the flowing meridian realm appeared in front of him in a flash of light, its sharp claws already slashing towards him.</p>

<p>He was thrown back dozens of meters, breaking a few trees in half before coming to a stop.</p>

<p>“A familiar beast… uh?” Qingyi smiled, spitting blood onto the ground.</p>

<p>He hadn’t managed to protect his body properly from that attack, so he had broken a few ribs and his left lung was collapsed, nothing too serious.</p>

<p>Raising his face, he saw the tiger advancing towards him, eyes filled with a crazed light as a blazing roar burst out towards Qingyi.</p>

<p>The handsome young man had a single second to ponder his next move before grabbing the heaven-defying thunder sword with force.</p>

<p>“Unfortunately for you, I have mine too.”</p>

<p>The second before the tiger closed its jaws around Qingyi, the heaven-defying thunder sword flashed.</p>

<p>A pair of even larger jaws appeared in the air, closing around the huge tiger, large, sharp teeth sinking into its neck.</p>

<p>The appearance of Khaedryss brought shock to everyone there, especially Elder Lu, whose face filled with terror as he realized that he couldn’t even sense the beast’s Qi.</p>

<p>The tiger obviously tried to fight back. It twisted and struggled, trying to free itself from Khaedryss’ grip, but nothing was enough.</p>

<p>Now that Khaedryss had completely consumed the steel-shelled turtle, its power had reached the equivalent of the peak of the flowing meridian realm, its body reaching fifty meters in length.</p>

<p>Qingyi ignored the horrible sounds as Khaedyrss tore through the tiger’s flesh and devoured it alive, just walking calmly towards Elder Lu.</p>

<p>Of course, he could handle it alone, without involving Khaedryss, but he didn’t think much of it.</p>

<p>There was no one there from the Continent of Eternal Light and the chances of getting into trouble because of Khaedryss were almost zero.</p>

<p>Elder Lu turned around, his lineage projection disappearing, his eyes filled with terror.</p>

<p>“Patriarch, I made a mistake, stop the fight!” He shouted, but Tai’Ren just shook his head. “I beg you, please! I was wrong!”</p>

<p>Elder Lu even tried to reach out to the other elders, begging them to intervene, to end the battle or at least release their cultivation, but none acted.</p>

<p>Elder Lu had agreed to the patriarch’s terms, a duel to the death.</p>

<p>If they intervened, it would be like slapping the patriarch in the face.</p>

<p>“Come here.” Qingyi stepped on the back of the exhausted Elder Lu and pressed the heaven-defying thunder sword against his neck.</p>

<p>Using his Qi of lightning, he slowly slashed up, destroying bone, flesh and muscle, bleeding Elder Lu like a pig before beheading him.</p>

<p>No one there was shocked.</p>

<p>This was the world of cultivation, cruel and ruthless. Many of them had done much worse.</p>

<p>Elder Lu’s soul escaped into the skies, trying to flee, but was stopped by one of the translucent swords of the fourth form of the Tempest Monarch sword art, erased by the explosion of the fifth form.</p>

<p>“Ah…” Qingyi sighed, approaching Linyue and placing a soft kiss on her lips.</p>

<p>“Your husband’s amazing, isn’t he?” he asked, but soon realized that, despite her blush, Linyue’s eyes were more focused on Khaedryss than anything else.</p>

<p>“Ah… I’ll let you play with her later,” he sighed, turning his face towards Lioran and flashing a big, gentle smile.</p>

<p>“I guess now it’s time for your father to fulfill his part of the bargain, huh? I have full permission to castrate you.</p>

<p>Are you ready to become a woman?”</p>
