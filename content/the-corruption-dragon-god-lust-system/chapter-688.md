<p>The moment Ruxue’s words reached him, Qingyi’s eyes flashed, and he burst forward toward the ship whose hull had been partially</p>

<p>scorched by his flame spear’s attack.</p>

<p>A fluid sweep of his hands pried the outer hull apart, exposing the cargo it had been built to protect.</p>

<p>Ten sealed boxes rested inside, each one inlaid with gold and jade, their lacquered surfaces catching the starlight that poured through the torn opening.</p>

<p>Ignoring the other nine, he fixed his attention on the one at the center.</p>

<p>A thread of Lightning Qi crackled from his fingertips, shattering the seal with a sharp snap and exposing the treasure within.</p>

<p>A small metallic fragment lay inside, no larger than a finger, its silver steel etched with pieces of ancient script and pulsing with a soft purple glow.</p>

<p>Without hesitation, he pressed the fragment against the</p>

<p>Heaven-Defying Thunder Sword. The blade drank it in at once, and a blinding radiance swept across its length.</p>

<p>Qingyi’s eyes widened as an invisible force wrenched the sword from his grip. Above him, the skies churned, thick and heavy clouds rolling in at an alarming pace.</p>

<p>Was this a heavenly tribulation?</p>

<p>[No… these are the tribulations of tempering. That fragment carried my sword aura, the aura that made me so feared!]</p>

<p>Ruxue’s voice drifted through his mind, as if she already knew what he was thinking.</p>

<p>Her sweet, sensual voice brimmed with nothing but a deep, growing excitement.</p>

<p>With every passing day, she moved one step closer to finally drawing blood from that accursed Heavenly Demon!</p>

<p>Inside the Heaven-Defying Thunder Sword, Ruxue’s eyes lit up</p>

<p>with</p>

<p>eager anticipation, while from the heavens above, dozens of lightning bolts came roaring down upon the beautiful blade.</p>

<p>Explosions thundered in rapid succession, shockwaves rippling outward as a seething plasma orb swallowed the sword whole.</p>

<p>The heat radiating from it was so fierce that even Qingyi felt a prickle of danger crawl up his spine.</p>

<p>At the same time, he realized just how violently Ruxue’s power was surging.</p>

<p>The lightning continued to fall without pause, the black skies unleashing everything they had onto the sharp blade below, the air roaring with each strike.</p>

<p>When it was finally over, the heavens cleared and the plasma orb collapsed in on itself, unveiling a weapon wholly transformed before Qingyi’s eyes.</p>

<p>It was stunning, far more stunning than before. The runes tracing its metallic body glowed brilliantly, a vivid purple set against gleaming silver, beautiful and elegant in equal measure.</p>

<p>Violet silk wrapped the hilt in several loops, the binding tapering at its end into a small tassel that was slowly deepening into a rich crimson. Qingyi smiled, reaching out and drawing the blade back into his grasp.</p>

<p>His fingers closed around the warm hilt, the silk soft beneath his touch, smooth as Ruxue’s breasts and strangely similar in feel.</p>

<p>[I made a few modifications with my perverted hubby in mind… if you</p>

<p>dare let any man touch the hilt, I promise I’ll blow myself up!]</p>

<p>At her words, Qingyi laughed heartily, cutting a few gentle arcs through the air and savoring the terrifying, razor-sharp aura radiating from the blade.</p>

<p>He then summoned his full draconic scales, sheathed the sword in a layer of Qi, and without hesitation brought it down against his own</p>

<p>arm.</p>

<p>To his astonishment, even stripped of Qi reinforcement, the edge was so keen that it sliced straight into his flesh, piercing through the</p>

<p>scales and biting into bone.</p>

<p>That was… ridiculously op!</p>

<p>If Ruxue had been there in the flesh, he would have kissed her until</p>

<p>he could no longer breathe. Still, this was fine.</p>

<p>He would simply slow his ship down a little and let the journey</p>

<p>stretch on.</p>

<p>With a gentle swing, he slid the weapon into the sheath at his waist, the design of the scabbard shifting completely as the reborn steel slipped through the elegant leather.</p>

<p>Calling for his ship, the Dragon’s Pride, he stepped inside.</p>

<p>No sooner had he dropped into the pilot’s seat than a beauty was already climbing into his lap, eager to put his vitality through its</p>

<p>paces.</p>

<p>The spaceship tore through the cosmos at a slightly gentler pace than before, the cabin filling with nothing but obscene, breathless moans.</p>

<p>It was a peaceful journey.</p>

<p>***</p>

<p>Countless light-years away, in the central southern region of the celestial heavens, a man stood within a castle of shadows and</p>

<p>blood-red flames.</p>

<p>Tall, with ashen gray skin and twin black horns rising from his brow, he was the Fdemon.</p>

<p>His eyes were deep and distant, lost in some thought as he gazed at the corpse before him. A woman, unmistakably so, lay atop a jade platform, her hands above her chest.</p>

<p>Her left hand rested around the hilt of a sword, one whose design mirrored the Heaven-Defying Thunder Sword in many ways yet remained entirely its own, as if the two had been forged as sisters, each shaped to be wielded in a different hand.</p>

<p>The heavenly demon reached out to touch the blade, only for a searing heat to lance through his wrist. The sword resisted him with every ounce of its will, unleashing a terrible wave of murderous</p>

<p>intent.</p>

<p>He merely smiled, drawing his hand back, his gaze lingering on the corpse once more.</p>

<p>She wore a wedding dress, the delicate fabric draped over her flawless curves, even her face veiled from view. Only her full,</p>

<p>blood-red lips remained exposed.</p>

<p>She had been dead for over a hundred thousand years, and yet her body still seemed to hold a faint warmth.</p>

<p>She had been the greatest swordswoman ever to walk the celestial heavens, daughter of the Divine Blacksmith of the Orthodox Celestial</p>

<p>Martial Alliance.</p>

<p>“Its okay, darling… soon, I’ll take your sister and resurrect your master as my slave. The three of you will serve me together, hahaha.”</p>

<p>A lascive, disgusting laugh spilled from his throat as he licked his lips</p>

<p>with a long, forked tongue, toying with a small orb cradled in his</p>

<p>palm, a flickering soul trapped inside.</p>

<p>He had already waited so long; a few more years would hardly matter.</p>

<p>Rising to his feet, he returned the orb to its pedestal and turned his gaze toward the two purplish-silver fragments resting on a shelf beside the chamber.</p>

<p>He was about to take his leave when he suddenly froze.</p>

<p>A familiar aura was spreading through the cosmos, and the fragments trembled on their shelf as if answering its call.</p>

<p>His eyes widened, and he cast his senses outward, searching for the</p>

<p>source of that aura, only to find nothing.</p>

<p>His hand rose slowly, coming to rest upon the terrible scar carved</p>

<p>across his chest, a wound he had failed to heal even in over a hundred thousand years.</p>

<p>His lips curled into a wide smile. He would find her, no matter the</p>

<p>cost!</p>
