<p>“I didn’t think you’d have the guts to show yourself here, filthy demon.”</p>

<p>A voice echoed, powerful and dignified, spreading through the void of space using the surrounding Qi particles to propagate.</p>

<p>From the void of the cosmos, a golden tear ripped through the fabric of the universe itself.</p>

<p>A figure emerged, illuminated by the pulsating red star behind him.</p>

<p>He was clad in pure white robes. On his head, long golden horns rose, shining like stars at dusk.</p>

<p>His golden eyes held nothing but relentless coldness.</p>

<p>He was Long Tianjin.</p>

<p>Father of Long Qianyao. The immortal golden dragon, founder of the Peerless Celestial Academy, leader of the Celestial Orthodox Alliance, and ruler of countless armies.</p>

<p>Publicly recognized as one of the Seven Great Supreme Emperors, the most powerful experts in all of the Celestial Heavens.</p>

<p>Before him, another man appeared.</p>

<p>Or rather, a demon.</p>

<p>Grayish skin, a muscular abdomen exposed by a robe that clung to his imposing body, which stood over three meters tall.</p>

<p>Among his black hair, horns of the same color rose majestically. His blood-red eyes overflowed with amusement.</p>

<p>The Heavenly Demon.</p>

<p>The first among the seventy-two Demon Lords. The most hated, even among his peers. A despicable man.</p>

<p>“Ah… brother, do you really think you have the right to call me such a derogatory name? Especially right after handing your beloved daughter over to the dragon god of corruption?</p>

<p>The most disgusting being in all of the Celestial Heavens… laying his hands on your beloved Qianyao… I despise you for handing her over to such a terrible fate, you know?”</p>

<p>“You are the most disgusting being in all of the Celestial Heavens, you pig. If I don’t kill you, my son-in-law will. You’ll pay for everything you did to my friend, for the madness that took over his mind!” Tianjin roared, making no attempt to hide his murderous intent.</p>

<p>“Oh… yes… yes… yes, don’t you think he’s pathetic for breaking so easily? I killed his wife, yes, but she’s just a woman, and one who put up quite a fight. Too bad she sent her daughter away…</p>

<p>Tianjin’s face contorted with disgust, every fiber of his being filled with agony as vomit rose in his throat.</p>

<p>The cosmos itself trembled as his rage exploded, spreading from the celestial north to the demonic south.</p>

<p>The fury of a celestial emperor wreaked havoc in every corner of the universe. Stars quaked and nearby planets collapsed under the pressure.</p>

<p>Fortunately, this was an isolated region, with no habitable worlds nearby.</p>

<p>Just imagining his beloved Rou’er, that sweet child he hadn’t seen in so many hundreds of thousands of years, in the hands of that accursed demon…</p>

<p>Tianjin’s heart bled with incomparable pain.</p>

<p>Fortunately, her mother had been able to send her away and spare her from that terrible fate.</p>

<p>“Oh… how could I possibly hurt such an adorable little thing? Hahaha” The Heavenly Demon cackled with contempt. “You orthodox types are far too sensitive.”</p>

<p>“Anyway, if you find Qingyi before I do, tell him that I’ve already found the soul of that girl, the original master of the sword he wields.</p>

<p>She’s still unconscious, and I haven’t fused the soul with her corpse yet, nor have I touched her.</p>

<p>But he’d better hurry, or perhaps I might lose control and take her primordial Yin without even reviving her, hahahaha.”</p>

<p>The Heavenly Demon smiled.</p>

<p>He wanted Qingyi to hurry and come running into his arms. Knowing how much that young man loved his wives, he was certain it would work.</p>

<p>The disgust Tianjin felt only intensified.</p>

<p>He knew exactly who the demon was talking about.</p>

<p>The daughter of the divine blacksmith of the Celestial Orthodox Alliance.</p>

<p>The most beautiful and talented swordswoman to ever set foot in the entire celestial realm, the one who received the title of most beautiful under the heavens after the death of the wife of the Dragon God of Corruption.</p>

<p>It had always been a great stain on the Orthodox Alliance that she had not only been poisoned, but also kidnapped by the Heavenly Demon right before his eyes.</p>

<p>Alongside the death of the Dragon God of Corruption, the fact that she had been condemned to such a terrible fate was one of the greatest sorrows in Tianjin’s heart.</p>

<p>He was so powerful and respected, but in his heart, his failures tortured him every day.</p>

<p>He just wanted his daughter back by his side. He wanted to protect her, just as he wanted to protect Qingyi and Rou’er, if she were still alive.</p>

<p>But to do that, he needed to kill the Heavenly Demon.</p>

<p>He needed to ensure that every fragment of his will was wiped from the face of the earth.</p>

<p>Only then could he sleep peacefully.</p>

<p>Only then could he prepare to be a grandfather, for the day his daughter gave birth to Qingyi’s child, and care for her for all eternity.</p>

<p>A golden spear appeared in Tianjin’s hands.</p>

<p>With a single movement, he attacked.</p>

<p>His power surged forth.</p>

<p>The star on his back was swept away like dust, collapsing into a black hole as the pressure exploded all around.</p>

<p>The spear tore through the void toward the Heavenly Demon, carrying a power so profound that even Qingyi would be unable to imagine it.</p>

<p>The cosmos lit up with an intense golden light as the power of two incomparable Celestial Emperors collided.</p>

<p>A laugh full of contempt echoed, and the spear met nothing but emptiness.</p>

<p>“Hahahaha, I’ll be on my way now, my old friend. Don’t worry, I’ve already warned the elders of the Orthodox Alliance about your connection to the heir of the Dragon God of Corruption.”</p>

<p>In just an instant, Tianjin’s power ceased.</p>

<p>Soon, he found himself alone in the void.</p>

<p>His eyes, once filled with rage and revulsion, calmed as he closed his eyelids.</p>

<p>So much power… and for what?</p>

<p>He couldn’t even avenge his friend, nor protect his daughter, nor remove an obstacle in the path of his dear son-in-law.</p>

<p>“Ah… I have a lot to explain to the Orthodox Alliance…” Tianjin sighed, recalling the Heavenly Demon’s last words.</p>

<p>Now that the great elders of the Orthodox Alliance knew the heir of the Dragon God of Corruption was in the Celestial Heavens, he needed to act quickly.</p>
