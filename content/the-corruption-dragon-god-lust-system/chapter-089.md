<p>“Ugh…” Qingyi grunted as the beast unleashed all its Qi, its dark fur bristling and turning a deep blue as it backed away.</p>

<p>Qingyi took two steps back, his sword trembling in his hand as he watched the giant lightning fox.</p>

<p>Despite all the improvement of his bloodline placing him far above most ninth stage foundation realm cultivators, the difference between spirit beasts and humans was still too great.</p>

<p>Unfortunately for the beast, Qingyi was also far superior to any ordinary human cultivator.</p>

<p>His eyes flashed as he activated his draconic eyes, his body being covered by a thunderous cloak as he advanced, the steel platform shaking with every step he took.</p>

<p>The fox did not stand still either. With a leap, it rose dozens of meters, hovering above Qingyi for a single moment before curling its legs and beginning to spin.</p>

<p>Its body seemed to transform into a spear covered in thunder as it fell toward Qingyi, its jaws opening to reveal large, sharp teeth.</p>

<p>Qingyi did not move or defend himself, and the creature’s eyes flashed as it finally reached him.</p>

<p>It would enjoy that meal that he so humbly allowed himself to be devoured.</p>

<p>Its colossal jaws closed around Qingyi’s torso, the platform sinking under the weight of its fall and a burst of dust spreading through the air.</p>

<p>The spirit beast was waiting for the metallic, salty taste of human blood, which it hadn’t tasted in a long time. But that wasn’t what it tasted.</p>

<p>A sharp pain shot through its jaw, and it felt its teeth shatter.</p>

<p>The giant lightning fox roared in pain, its claws tearing at the ground as it tried to retreat, but Qingyi didn’t let it.</p>

<p>His body was covered in black scales, only a slight scratch marking the spot where the fox had bitten.</p>

<p>“You’re not going anywhere!” He growled as he grabbed its snout and thrust his sword into its neck.</p>

<p>He showed no mercy, his sword Qi cutting through as his lightning Qi destroyed everything inside it.</p>

<p>A gush of blood spurted from the beast’s half-open mouth as Qingyi’s sword finally reached its windpipe, bathing hin in crimson liquid rich in spiritual energy.</p>

<p>After long moments of struggle, the beast finally succumbed to its injuries, its eyes becoming dull and its limbs limp.</p>

<p>“Ah…” Qingyi sighed as he let the beast fall to the ground, looking at his clothes, now red with its blood.</p>

<p>“Will I never come out of a battle clean?” He muttered, but soon shook his head.</p>

<p>It was his fault and his fighting style. He could have come out of that battle much cleaner if he had simply fought in a more reserved manner.</p>

<p>But well… what would be the fun in that?</p>

<p>His eyes soon shifted from his clothes to his skin, still covered in dragon scales.</p>

<p>The resistance was much greater than any armor he could buy in the system.</p>

<p>He still didn’t have the arrogance to claim that he could withstand a blow from a golden core realm cultivator—the difference between the golden core and the foundation realm was too great—but he was very close to it.</p>

<p>The idea of crossing an entire cultivation realm made his blood boil.</p>

<p>If he reached that level, probably only the geniuses of the Nine Peaks Basin would be able to stand up to him.</p>

<p>A slight creak snapped him out of his thoughts as the platform vibrated gently.</p>

<p>At its center, he saw the ground opening up and a large gate slowly rising, still closed, small lines forming around it and flowing toward the center.</p>

<p>’This must be the waiting time Laosan spoke of…’ Qingyi scratched his chin. He was about to walk toward the gate when he remembered something.</p>

<p>His eyes focused on the beast’s corpse, and he felt the Qi-rich blood burning on his skin, no longer protected by the black dragon’s scales.</p>

<p>Now that he had a spatial ring, he no longer needed to leave these good things behind.</p>

<p>The giant lightning fox was a high-quality beast, far superior to the giant horned wolf he had killed for Lord Zhang.</p>

<p>They were so rare and hunted that even one in the foundation realm like this could be worth a fortune.</p>

<p>Of course, it didn’t have much use for Qingyi himself, but for an alchemist like Hua Feiyan, a body like that fox’s would be a gift from the heavens.</p>

<p>Qingyi raised his index finger, the space ring sucking the corpse inside. Time did not pass in his inner space, so he did not need to worry about anything spoiling.</p>

<p>’I wonder if this will be enough as an apology gift for her…’ Qingyi thought. ’No, it’s not even close…’ He concluded.</p>

<p>Knowing her well, he knew that she would probably use that corpse to make pills for him, not for herself.</p>

<p>How could something like that be a worthy payment?</p>

<p>His thoughts were soon diverted from the tanned beauty as he sensed dozens of auras approaching on the horizon.</p>

<p>He walked calmly and sat above the gate.</p>

<p>A few moments later, a crowd of foundation realm cultivators appeared, divided into three distinct groups.</p>

<p>Luckily, Young Master Shen was not among them. Qingyi did not want to deal with the arrogant young master, at least not yet.</p>

<p>The watchful eyes of the foundation realm cultivators scanned the black steel platform, searching for the spirit beast.</p>

<p>But they found nothing but pools of blood and a handsome young man sitting casually on the gate, bathed in crimson liquid.</p>

<p>Their faces were filled with confusion as the more powerful ones sensed Qingyi’s cultivation.</p>

<p>Third stage of the foundation realm…</p>

<p>That fox should be at least in the eighth stage of the foundation realm. How could she be defeated by someone like this?</p>

<p>’Six in the ninth stage of the foundation realm, fifteen in the eighth stage… the rest are trash.’ Qingyi thought as he studied them.</p>

<p>A cold smile took over his face as he questioned them, “Are you all going to come at once? Or would you rather line up to die?”</p>
