<p>The tests proceeded calmly after Elder Tian’s demonstration. No one dared question anything further.</p>

<p>Those who had passed gathered behind the elder, while those who had failed left with their heads down.</p>

<p>Some sniffled with tears in their eyes, others let long sighs escape, but all remained silent.</p>

<p>Soon, more than two hundred faced the test, but only forty passed.</p>

<p>“Next!” Elder Tian’s voice rang out, as a tanned beauty stepped forward with large steps, her fertile hips swaying in a smooth roll and the two large, plump masses of flesh that formed her perfect, heart-shaped ass jiggled with every step.</p>

<p>Qingyi had to admit, she was even more attractive from the back than she was from the front.</p>

<p>Stopping in front of the pillar, she gracefully raised her hand and touched the stone structure, which immediately shook. A vibrant brown aura spread and, in the next moment, seven lights lit up on the pillar.</p>

<p>“Seventh level earth spirit root, approved,” the elder spoke, his eyes barely sweeping over Hua Feiyan’s body before calling for the next one.</p>

<p>Fifty more were called until Xu Tianming stepped forward. He seemed to have calmed his mind, but still flinched with anger whenever he noticed Qingyi’s gaze.</p>

<p>Stopping in front of the pillar, he took a slow breath and pressed his hand, releasing his Qi.</p>

<p>’This bastard had a breakthrough?’ Qingyi was taken aback.</p>

<p>He had felt Tianming’s Qi the day before, and he was a long way from being able to advance, with a very unstable base.</p>

<p>How on earth had this bastard stabilized his base and broken through in such a short space of time?</p>

<p>But then Qingyi remembered: Tianming was rich, filthy rich.</p>

<p>His family probably had a fortune so great that it would make even the Ascending Dragon Sect jealous.</p>

<p>’Ugh… damn rich bastards, using their wealth to take advantage of us lowly, unsupported cultivators!’</p>

<p>Fortunately, he didn’t have time to reflect on how absurd it was for him, of all people, to complain about wealth.</p>

<p>The pillar was surrounded by a silvery aura, trembling as the lights slowly came on.</p>

<p>Elder Tian’s eyebrows rose slightly, but soon a smile took over his face.</p>

<p>“As expected of Young Master Xu. Seventh level sword spirit root, approved.”</p>

<p>As he joined the rest of the passers, Xu Tianming cast a teasing glance in Qingyi’s direction.</p>

<p>’Let’s see if this pretty face can beat that!’ Tianming thought, trying to force some confidence into his face.</p>

<p>He stood next to Feiyan, but she barely spared him a glance, which made his heart burn even more.</p>

<p>’Who the hell does this woman think she is to ignore me like that?’ He gritted his teeth, trying to control his murderous intent, but a little still leaked out, all focused on Qingyi.</p>

<p>’What a pathetic little man…’ Qingyi shook his head. That level of murderous intent was barely enough to touch his skin, but Elder Tian sensed it perfectly.</p>

<p>The old man’s eyes focused on Tianming for a few brief moments before he sighed. His expression looked disappointed as he called for the next person to be tested.</p>

<p>Soon, it was Qingyi’s turn.</p>

<p>He stood in front of the pillar and touched it lightly. A bluish light took over the structure, and exactly five lights came on.</p>

<p>Tianming’s eyes shone more brightly than ever, his body trembling as a triumphant smile dominated his face.</p>

<p>Elder Tian opened his mouth to give his verdict, but something changed. His aged eyes twitched slightly as he took a step closer.</p>

<p>From the blue aura surrounding the pillar, a silver one emerged, dancing before merging into a beautiful blue covered in silver threads. Sharp electric rays streaked violently through the air.</p>

<p>’A two-colored spirit root? No… it’s a lightning sword spirit root!’ The elder exhaled heavily.</p>

<p>In over hundred years of recruiting for the Sect, he had only seen two-colored spirit roots a handful of times, but fused spirit roots? It was unheard of for him.</p>

<p>He hesitated for a moment.</p>

<p>The requirements were sixth-level spirit roots as standard, but he couldn’t force himself to refuse that young man.</p>

<p>Raising the level of a spirit root was expensive, but it was still a possibility. The Sect had plenty of pills for that.</p>

<p>But a spiritual root base? That was almost impossible to change. If you were born with a bad element, you were probably doomed.</p>

<p>From the aura he sensed exuding from Qingyi’s body, he could already tell that, despite his level, his spiritual root power was no lower than a level seven. So the young man would probably have no problem standing out.</p>

<p>If he could cultivate well enough to obtain a spirit root enhancement pill… he would probably become part of the Sect’s backbone in the future.</p>

<p>“Fifth level lightning sword spirit root, approved,” Elder Tian finally announced his decision.</p>

<p>Tianming’s face crumpled as he clenched his fists tightly.</p>

<p>Feiyan’s excited voice only made him even angrier. How could nothing go wrong for that bastard?</p>

<p>Why was the woman who was supposed to be his throwing herself into his arms like that?</p>

<p>“Junior Brother Qingyi really is amazing! It’s the first time I’ve seen a fused spirit root!” Feiyan smiled encouragingly at Qingyi.</p>

<p>She wanted to throw herself into his arms to stoke Tianming’s jealousy even more, but Elder Tian’s stern gaze made her abandon that idea.</p>

<p>“Don’t get too cocky, there’s still the combat test,” Tianming growled between his teeth.</p>

<p>The rest of the tests were less exciting. Lots of failures and a few seventh level spirit roots, nothing more.</p>

<p>“Good,” Elder Tian smiled as he looked at the crowd.</p>

<p>The numbers had dropped considerably – the six hundred who had passed the will test had been reduced to just one hundred and twenty.</p>

<p>The quality was lacking, but the numbers were acceptable.</p>

<p>“Follow me, we’re going to the Sect arena to start the combat test,” Elder Tian’s voice echoed as he started walking.</p>

<p>Everyone had anxious and curious expressions on their faces as they entered the large central gate.</p>

<p>Even if they were rejected, just seeing the inside of the Sect would be enough for some there.</p>
