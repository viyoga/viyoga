<p>“The normal thing is to be expelled immediately from the secret realm upon completion, but since you haven’t been expelled yet, I believe you still need to do something.”</p>

<p>“Do something?” A doubt appeared on Qingyi’s face, his eyes soon focusing on the dragon’s corpse.</p>

<p>Right, the prize wasn’t just the lineage of the scarlet dragon, there was also the body, which in itself was an incomparable treasure.</p>
