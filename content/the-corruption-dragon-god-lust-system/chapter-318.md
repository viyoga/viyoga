<p>Early in the morning, the Burning Red Pavilion returned to its normal operation, as if nothing had happened.</p>

<p>At its gate, a figure arrived, clearly not a cultivator.</p>

<p>He was a tall, burly man, dressed in long white robes. On his head, he wore a gold-plated mitre, common among members of the high clergy of the Church of Auranys.</p>

<p>This man paused for a moment, his round face and scruffy beard showing contempt as he signaled for the guards in silver armor to open the gate.</p>

<p>He was already a well-known figure in the city, the bishop of the Eagle Peak Valley cathedral, a cruel, lustful man more akin to a demon than a man of faith.</p>

<p>Entering the luxurious main building, he was immediately and respectfully attended to by an ordinary-looking attendant.</p>

<p>“Greetings, Your Holiness. What can I do for you?”</p>

<p>“Where is Lady Margareth?” he asked, flashing a fake kind smile.</p>

<p>“She’s meeting with Young Master Qingyi upstairs, shall I call her?”</p>

<p>The attendant bowed, but before the bishop could answer, a sweet, mature voice echoed: “That won’t be necessary.”</p>

<p>Turning his face, the bishop saw the beautiful and elegant figures of Meilin and Margareth descending the grand staircase that led to the upper floors.</p>

<p>His eyes sparkled at the sight, but the sparkle soon disappeared when he saw Qingyi standing between the incomparable beauties, his hands resting on their slim, delicate waists.</p>

<p>The bishop’s face was filled with envy and jealousy, but he soon suppressed this feeling, or at least stopped showing it.</p>

<p>Of course, he was unhappy at the sight.</p>

<p>He was already cursed enough to have been sent to that damned place, losing his position as bishop in a small but wealthy city in the divine kingdom of Auranys.</p>

<p>And now, he even had to watch Margareth rub up against an infidel heretic.</p>

<p>She might not be a member of the clergy, but she was still a genius in whom the church had invested a fortune, even sponsoring her entry into the Red Tower in the capital of the Rose empire.</p>

<p>She should at least have the decency to marry a good man, a follower of Auranys, someone like him. Not just any pretty face she met on the street.</p>

<p>“What does your holiness need?” Margareth asked, leaving Qingyi’s arms, the seductive smile on her face disappearing, replaced by a cold but respectful scowl.</p>

<p>“Oh… some serious things happened during the last night and since Lady Margareth wasn’t in her office, I took the liberty of visiting her to discuss it.”</p>

<p>“Serious things?” Margareth frowned softly.</p>

<p>“Yes, I’ve just received a transmission from one of my men in the capital. Apparently, the king is dead and wasn’t able to leave a clear heir before he passed away.”</p>

<p>The moment he heard those words, Qingyi raised an eyebrow as Margareth and Meilin’s hearts sank.</p>

<p>The king dead and leaving no clear heir… that was the worst of all possibilities and meant only one thing: civil war.</p>

<p>“Are you sure about this, Your Holiness?” Margareth clenched her fists tightly.</p>

<p>Why on earth did something like this have to happen right now, when things were calm and she was finding time to get back to her magical research?</p>

<p>“Unfortunately yes, Lady Margareth,” the bishop said, shaking his head, before turning away. “Please come with me, I have something else to show you.”</p>

<p>Margareth hesitated for a moment, exchanging glances with Qingyi before sighing and following the bishop.</p>

<p>The handsome young man followed right behind, also curious.</p>

<p>They left the Burning Red Pavilion, walking towards the port of Eagle Peak Valley, which had been completely emptied. Around fifty powerful soldiers stood guard and prevented anyone from entering.</p>

<p>But these were no ordinary soldiers. They wore armor so deep white that it reflected the sunlight with enough intensity to momentarily blind mortals.</p>

<p>These were paladins in training, future members of the fifty-thousand-strong army of the Church of Auranys.</p>

<p>An army so powerful that even its weakest member was equivalent in power to a false immortal.</p>

<p>“What is the meaning of this? Why is the port closed?” Margareth asked.</p>

<p>The church had no rights to that port and closing it naturally hindered Meilin’s business.</p>

<p>“Oh… no big deal…” The bishop smiled, watching a ship dock at the port.</p>

<p>After a few moments, a figure left the ship.</p>

<p>It was a young man, no older than 17, with a noble face and short golden hair.</p>

<p>On his armor, he bore the symbol of Auranys and, on his handsome face, a gentle smile.</p>

<p>“Lady Margareth, long time no see…” The young man approached, trying to take her hand and kiss it.</p>

<p>To his surprise, Margareth immediately stepped back, withdrawing her hand and moving towards Qingyi.</p>

<p>She knew that young man. He was a golden child candidate, one of the most promising talents the church was investing in, more promising even than herself.</p>

<p>Reaching the fifth ring at only seventeen years old, Lucien was naturally a talent that deserved respect.</p>

<p>“Lucien will be spending a few weeks training on this continent and, with the possible civil war, I think it would be a good idea for him to be your personal escort, at least for a while,” the bishop said.</p>

<p>“I recognize that Miss Margareth is powerful, but no amount of protection is enough for someone so important, right?”</p>

<p>“I see…” Margareth gritted her teeth, visibly unhappy.</p>

<p>Now she knew exactly what the bishop wanted.</p>

<p>Lucien had a long relationship with him, the bishop being the one who had given him the chance to become a candidate for the golden son.</p>

<p>Now, he probably wanted to use Lucien to keep an eye on her and gather information about her.</p>

<p>Was that old son of a bitch really thinking of making a move?</p>

<p>Margareth couldn’t help becoming anxious, turning her face slightly and staring at Qingyi.</p>

<p>“I have a husband and I don’t need any protection other than his. If Lucien manages to defeat him, I would take this possibility into consideration.”</p>
