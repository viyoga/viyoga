<p>“Damn it!”</p>

<p>Intense pain overwhelmed Qingyi as he was hit by thousands of projectiles at once.</p>

<p>The roots summoned by the patriarch moved like snakes toward him, striking his chest with powerful impacts.</p>

<p>The black scales withstood the initial blow, but soon he felt fatigue setting in.</p>

<p>He gritted his teeth so hard that his gums bled as he gathered his power.</p>

<p>With an explosion, he unleashed everything, shattering all the projectiles for dozens of meters.</p>

<p>“Oh… so you’re not just an ordinary human, huh?” The patriarch laughed loudly, watching Qingyi’s transformation.</p>

<p>He had barely finished his sentence before Qingyi was already upon him, his fist covered in black scales tearing through the air with full power.</p>

<p>The patriarch was struck. His nose sank into his face, his body spinning one hundred and eighty degrees before being thrown backwards like a cannonball.</p>

<p>He hit the ground with a thunderous explosion, powerful tremors engulfing everything around him.</p>

<p>Qingyi advanced toward him again, preparing the Heaven-Defying Thunder Sword, ready to tear him in two.</p>

<p>Unfortunately, the patriarch had already recovered. Dozens of powerful roots shot out of the ground around him, their tips as sharp as razors.</p>

<p>“Tell me, boy, what gives you such confidence to kill me?” The</p>

<p>patriarch roared, preparing his own sword.</p>

<p>He watched with amusement as Qingyi easily cut through his countless attacks, the Heaven-Defying Thunder Sword slicing through everything around him.</p>

<p>Beneath the anger of having his priests killed and his plans delayed, his blood boiled. The powerful suppression of Qingyi’s lineage only made him more excited.</p>

<p>It had been a long time since he had fought an enemy that forced him to sweat like this.</p>

<p>He charged forward, his body turning into nothing but a violent blur.</p>

<p>“Answer me, what did Seraphine give you? Become my subordinate, and I’ll give you ten times more! I’ll even make her your sex slave.”</p>

<p>The patriarch’s blade struck the Heaven-Defying Thunder Sword with a deafening crash. Metallic sparks engulfed the air around them as the pressure rapidly increased.</p>

<p>“Oh… it’s not just because of Seraphine that I’m killing you, filthy cuckold.” Qingyi flashed a genuine smile.</p>

<p>He pushed the patriarch back before attacking again, his blade sliding past his enemy’s and striking the guard of his sword.</p>

<p>“Ugh… FUCK!”</p>

<p>A pained cry echoed, followed by exactly three severed fingers flying through the air, the patriarch’s sword slipping from his hand.</p>

<p>His eyes widened, and he quickly drew a second sword from his spatial ring, grabbing it with his few remaining fingers and preventing Qingyi from decapitating him at that very moment.</p>

<p>How the hell did that bastard have so much physical strength?</p>

<p>Exchanging blows with him was like hitting a wall of solid steel.</p>

<p>“Not just because of Seraphine? What the hell do you mean by that?” The patriarch questioned, his face filled with pure hatred despite the</p>

<p>big smile that dominated it.</p>

<p>Qingyi laughed at the question. He swung the Heaven-Defying Thunder Sword, the movement wiping the blood from the blade and scattering it around in a crescent shape.</p>

<p>“Try to guess. Another daughter of yours, whom you abandoned and tried to kill like the pathetic, filthy cuckold that you are.”</p>

<p>The moment those words echoed, the smile on the patriarch’s face disappeared completely.</p>

<p>His domain shook. His fists clenched hard enough for his nails to tear the skin on his palms.</p>

<p>“Elize… so it’s because of that damned cursed child… I knew it, she</p>

<p>would bring doom to my people!”</p>

<p>The patriarch gritted his teeth. His words sounded more like the growl of a hungry beast, his handsome face contorted into grotesque</p>

<p>expressions.</p>

<p>“No…. you brought your own doom! I am merely the executioner who will deliver this doom to your pathetic little life.” Qingyi corrected</p>

<p>him.</p>

<p>The patriarch’s red eyes flashed with murderous intent as he roared.</p>

<p>“Red Death… devour him!”</p>

<p>Naturally, RedDeath couldn’t be more excited to fulfill a request from his rider.</p>

<p>The pain from that blow he received upon Qingyi’s arrival was still clear in his mind, as clear as the shame of not even being able to react</p>

<p>to such an attack.</p>

<p>His sharp claws tore into the ground for support as his jaws opened toward Qingyi.</p>

<p>The nearly hundred-ton beast of steel-hard scales and pure muscle</p>

<p>mass was ready to devour him.</p>

<p>Qingyi didn’t move, the corners of his lips curving gently.</p>

<p>“How about a little fun too, girl?” he asked.</p>

<p>In the next moment, RedDeath froze, stopping its momentum and</p>

<p>trying to retreat.</p>

<p>dragon’s soul.</p>

<p>With a powerful thrust, she continued to push the dragon back, feeling her teeth sink into the scales, a burning, powerful blood</p>

<p>entering her throat.</p>

<p>massive body, using that momentum to pull Khaedryss off his neck. Free, he quickly retreated, just in time to take a breath of black, burning flames in his face.</p>
