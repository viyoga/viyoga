<p>“You bitch!” one of the bandits roared as he swung his sword in Elize’s direction.</p>

<p>The purple-haired beauty defended herself gracefully, taking only one step back before counterattacking.</p>

<p>Her blade sliced through the bandit’s shoulder, destabilizing him before ripping through his belly.</p>

<p>At the same moment, another bandit appeared behind her, his sword opening a deep gash in the young woman’s delicate back.</p>

<p>“Ghn” Elize grunted painfully before kicking backwards, throwing her aggressor away and quickly retreating.</p>

<p>More than twenty corpses already lay under her feet, but the number of enemies didn’t seem to be decreasing one bit.</p>

<p>“Cough! Cough!” She brought her hands to her lips and looked at the trembling palm bathed in blood.</p>

<p>Her vision blurred and she stumbled, almost falling.</p>

<p>’What’s happening, have I been poisoned?’ she thought, feeling the burning cut on her back.</p>

<p>No… this couldn’t be happening.</p>

<p>“Just a little bit more,” she roared, her aura exploding with more power than ever.</p>

<p>Whatever was in her blood boiled as she pushed all her lightning Qi out of her Dantian.</p>

<p>She wasn’t ready to die, not yet.</p>

<p>How could she accept dying without even presenting the child she would have with her beloved to her mother?</p>

<p>But although she didn’t want to die, she wasn’t willing to back down and leave her sisters behind either.</p>

<p>She refused to be a coward, she didn’t want to lose her sisters like she had lost her mother and her master.</p>

<p>With a heavy heart and panting breath, she felt her meridians tremble violently.</p>

<p>“She’s trying to make a forced breakthrough!”</p>

<p>“Stop her!”</p>

<p>The higher-ranking bandits roared, directing their swords, machetes and axes at Elize. If she advanced to the foundation realm now, they would be in serious trouble.</p>

<p>“Die!” Elize’s voice echoed like a bestial scream.</p>

<p>The ground beneath her feet cracked as she channeled all the energy from the breakthrough into her sword. The metal shook, but didn’t break.</p>

<p>The first bandit reached her, his weapon coming within centimeters of the beauty’s delicate neck before she defended herself.</p>

<p>Her blade found his torso and sliced through bone and muscle until it came out the other side.</p>

<p>Without wasting time, she advanced to the next one, a decapitated head falling to the ground while her sword cut through the air.</p>

<p>“Keep attacking, don’t let her finish her advance!” shouted a bandit who was a single step away from the foundation realm before having his throat torn out.</p>

<p>Slowly, the bodies under Elize’s feet increased.</p>

<p>From twenty to thirty, from thirty to forty and soon, more than fifty bloody corpses covered the ground.</p>

<p>Her body was marked by dozens of cuts, some deep enough to reach her bones.</p>

<p>She was in the worst pain she had ever felt in her life, but she didn’t cry or even wince.</p>

<p>“Shit! She’s almost finished advancing to the foundation realm!” The bandits tried to retreat, feeling the Qi around Elize change drastically.</p>

<p>Forced advances were dangerous, even when successful, they usually left deep wounds and an unstable base.</p>

<p>Even so, they couldn’t help but shrink back in fear in front of that woman.</p>

<p>Their leaders had lied, saying that they were all weak and fragile women!</p>

<p>“You dared to come here, so you’ll stay here for the rest of eternity!” Elize roared, her voice hoarse as she swung her sword in fury.</p>

<p>But then her momentum suddenly stopped.</p>

<p>A metallic clang echoed as her sword shattered and she was thrown more than ten meters.</p>

<p>“Ugh… blergh…” She fell to her knees, vomiting blood and raising her gaze to a shadowy figure.</p>

<p>He wasn’t one of the leaders of the Red Claw gang, but he was still a Foundation Realm cultivator.</p>

<p>Her breakthrough had been a success and she could already feel the Qi core in her Dantian, but she was too injured to fight another cultivator of the same level.</p>

<p>She tried to get up, but her body didn’t respond.</p>

<p>Even using her Qi, all she managed was to shudder slightly before collapsing onto what was left of her sword.</p>

<p>“Ah… you gave us a lot of trouble, huh? But the leader likes women like you. I’m sure you’ll give him many powerful heirs once we’ve sorted out this mess.”</p>

<p>The shadowy figure laughed, approaching her in quick steps, his eyes sweeping over the woman’s body lustfully.</p>

<p>Even with all her wounds, she was still an incomparable beauty.</p>

<p>“Husband… where are you?” She closed her eyes as his hands approached.</p>

<p>She prepared to bite her tongue and commit suicide, but stopped the last moment, hearing a heartbreaking scream and feeling a splash of blood on her face.</p>

<p>“Elize!” Yueli’s worried voice reached her ears and she opened her eyes.</p>

<p>Yueli hugged her tightly as she watched the handsome, powerful figure standing in front of her, holding the dark figure’s neck.</p>

<p>With a single squeeze, the handsome young man’s fists met, and the dark figure’s head was crushed like a ripe tomato.</p>

<p>“Don’t anyone dare move a single step,” he ordered, and no one did.</p>

<p>Not out of obedience, but because they couldn’t.</p>

<p>Their bodies were crushed by a powerful aura, their knees already buckling as their hearts seemed about to explode.</p>

<p>Not even their leader had such overwhelming murderous intent… no, their leader’s murderous intent was like a child’s tantrum at what they felt exuding from that young man’s body.</p>

<p>Qingyi ignored them, his heart sinking as he looked at Elize’s bloodied body.</p>

<p>His hands were shaking as he bought the most expensive recovery pill he could get from the system.</p>

<p>His lust points reached zero and continued to drop until they reached two thousand negative points, but he didn’t care.</p>

<p>[I’ll allow this purchase, but if you don’t recover the points you’ve spent within 24 hours, the punishment will be severe].</p>

<p>Ignoring the system’s threats, he knelt down in front of Elize.</p>

<p>“H-husband…” she whispered, her voice weak as she watched Qingyi.</p>

<p>Her eyes slowly closed.</p>

<p>“I’m sorry, honey… I made you suffer…” He put the pill to his own lips and kissed Elize, forcing the pill down her throat.</p>

<p>Yueli obviously didn’t mind it, placing her hands on Elize’s back and helping her to calm her turbulent energy.</p>

<p>The effect was immediate.</p>

<p>Elize’s complexion soon became pinker and the wounds covering her body quickly closed up.</p>

<p>This was a pill no inferior to what cultivators of the nine peaks basin used, leaving not even a single scar behind.</p>

<p>Qingyi stood up and took a step towards the invaders.</p>

<p>There were still almost a hundred and fifty of them, but these numbers didn’t even make him blink.</p>

<p>Drawing his sword, he used the second form of the Tempest Monarch’s sword art</p>
