<p>“Ah… this day just won’t end…”</p>

<p>A long sigh slipped past the receptionist’s lips, the very same woman who had handed Qingyi that mission only hours earlier.</p>

<p>She knew full well it was impossible for him to finish so quickly, yet her eyes refused to leave the door for even a single moment.</p>

<p>Just in case, you know?</p>

<p>Just as hope began to drain from her and she started rising from her chair, ready to end her shift for the day, her movements froze the very instant her gaze flickered toward the entrance.</p>

<p>There, calmly strolling through the door, was Long Qingyi, unscathed, with nine boxes floating behind his back.</p>

<p>She rushed over with an excited look, her lips already parting before she could even close the distance between them.</p>

<p>“There are ten boxes. Where’s the last one? And the traitor?” she asked, her eyes glimmering with pure excitement.</p>

<p>“I only found nine boxes there. The traitor is dead,” Qingyi replied with a smile, handing everything over to her. Her eyes shone like coins in the sun as she received the boxes.</p>

<p>Well, it didn’t matter if one box was missing. Some loss had been expected from the start.</p>

<p>The important thing was that the traitor was dead and at least some of the boxes had been recovered!</p>

<p>Carefully tucking everything away, she prepared the necessary documents, skimmed off her hefty commission, and handed the rewards to Qingyi, who smiled immediately and asked for another mission.</p>

<p>Unfortunately for her, this time, he requested missions focused purely on speed, the kind anyone could complete quickly. Naturally, she preferred giving him difficult yet fast missions rather than cheap ones. After all, she still wanted to squeeze out as much profit as possible, and, well, she was almost certain he could handle anything.</p>

<p>Qingyi saw through her little scheme right away.</p>

<p>She was trying to rake in fat commissions from missions that had been gathering dust in the pavilion for ages, but he didn’t bother protesting, since he too benefited from the harder tasks.</p>

<p>In the end, for the remainder of those few weeks, he kept taking on missions without rest, finishing one and rushing off to the next, quickly reaching ten missions and earning that greedy attendant over two hundred spirit crystals in commission.</p>

<p>Now, she strutted around flaunting expensive jewelry around her neck, while her fellow female disciples could only watch with obvious envy burning in their eyes.</p>

<p>That envy quickly turned to amusement, though, when she tried to slip Qingyi yet another extremely difficult mission, only for him to refuse.</p>

<p>He had already completed the ten required missions. Now he needed to focus on what truly mattered.</p>

<p>Soon, Qingyi left the mission pavilion and secluded himself in a cultivation room, sitting cross-legged on the mat and glancing at his status screen.</p>

<p>A few months had passed, and now only one week remained until the day he could finally claim Rouxi, the master of the Celestial Fairy Pavilion and a powerful Stellar Sovereign, as his mommy.</p>

<p>How could he not be excited?</p>

<p>But first, of course, he needed to grow stronger.</p>

<p>Well, it wasn’t exactly a necessity. His strength was already more than enough, but better safe than sorry.</p>

<p>By the sacred name of Rou’er! Even though he had only met her a few times in the last few days, he was rich, filthy rich!</p>

<p>Qingyi’s lips curled into a satisfied smile as he tapped to improve his cultivation, again and again.</p>

<p>In the blink of an eye, seven hundred and thirty-five G Lust points vanished from his status screen, propelling him to the peak of the Celestial Ascension realm, just one step away from advancing into the Celestial Origin realm.</p>

<p>He even considered taking that final step right then and there, but in the end, he shook his head.</p>

<p>First, he had to stabilize his cultivation. Then could he continue climbing without worry.</p>

<p>And so, for the rest of the week, Qingyi practiced in silence.</p>

<p>Meanwhile, outside, the Celestial Fairy Pavilion buzzed with excitement.</p>

<p>Hundreds of the sect’s most talented disciples had gathered, debating the requirements in hushed yet eager voices, many already at the early stages of the Celestial Origin Realm, all hoping to seize the chance of becoming the pavilion master’s adopted son or daughter.</p>

<p>Ten completed missions within the sect, cultivation at the Celestial Ascension Realm, and a rigorous evaluation of each candidate’s morality and power were demanded, along with, of course, the strict condition of never having had a family.</p>

<p>As such, only orphaned disciples could participate in the competition.</p>

<p>Some might even try to deceive the pavilion master, lying through their teeth that they had no family in truth.</p>

<p>But for that, it took courage. A lot of courage. Soon, over a hundred entries filled a massive registration list, a number that ballooned to five hundred by the day before the selection.</p>

<p>Qingyi’s name, despite him being in secluded cultivation, mysteriously appeared at the very top of the list, even though he had never gone to register.</p>

<p>Naturally, Rou’er had taken care of this.</p>

<p>Knowing the requirements perfectly, she was already extolling Long Qingyi’s virtues, explaining to Rouxi in great detail just how righteous and pure he was.</p>

<p>If Qingyi knew what she was up to, he would probably burst out laughing at the image of that adorable petite beauty singing his praises to Rouxi.</p>

<p>In the end, it wasn’t until the morning of the competition that Qingyi’s eyes slowly opened, the power of his cultivation humming in his muscles.</p>

<p>He rose to his feet, feeling the cool air of the room brush against his skin, and then smiled.</p>

<p>It was time to become the adopted son of a hot milf!</p>
