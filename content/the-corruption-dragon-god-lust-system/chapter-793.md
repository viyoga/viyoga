<p>"Young Master Qingyi, this area has been marked as a possible crime scene and is under investigation. Please don’t—" The voice of the guard stationed outside Chen Wei’s room echoed down the empty hallway, hesitant and cut off mid-sentence.</p>

<p>"I’m going to count to three. If you don’t get out of my way, there won’t be anything left for your family to hold a funeral for." Qingyi declared, an overwhelming murderous intent radiating from his body in suffocating waves, making the air around him feel as dense as lead.</p>

<p>The guard hesitated, his face turning pale in a matter of seconds.</p>

<p>He thought about contacting his superior, but honestly, he didn’t know what to do in the face of that overwhelming pressure.</p>

<p>"One."</p>

<p>A single number was enough.</p>

<p>The guard, drenched in cold sweat and crushed by that terrible murderous intent, cleared the way instantly, his body trembling as he gasped for air.</p>

<p>Qingyi entered Chen Wei’s room without so much as a second glance.</p>

<p>The place was perfectly preserved, as if someone had slept there just the day before.</p>

<p>A heavy silence hung in the air, mingled with the musty smell of a long-abandoned room.</p>

<p>Even the mess left behind remained untouched, every object exactly where it had been dropped, every layer of dust firmly in its place.</p>

<p>Qingyi’s eyes narrowed. He knelt down slowly, placing his open palm on the worn wooden floor.</p>

<p>He could feel it.</p>

<p>There were ashes there, almost invisible to the naked eye, as if a piece of paper had been reduced to dust at that exact spot, leaving only tiny remnants among the wood fibers.</p>

<p>Running his hand carefully over the floor, he gathered those tiny particles between his fingers.</p>

<p>Without hesitation, he used the Nine Paths of Creation Reversal Art.</p>

<p>The ashes scattered across the floor began to float slowly toward his hand, one after another, gathering into a small pile that soon became enveloped in an intense, silvery glow.</p>

<p>When the glow faded, all that remained between Qingyi’s fingers was a single piece of paper, perfectly restored, as if it had never been reduced to nothing.</p>

<p>Without wasting a second, he read it. And with every word, his eyes filled with even more rage.</p>

<p>It was a very common technique used in threatening letters.</p>

<p>The recipient would read the message, and about twenty seconds later, the letter would disintegrate into dust, leaving not the slightest trace.</p>

<p>[Chen Wei, your arrogance knows no bounds, doesn’t it, you brat?</p>

<p>I can’t lay a finger on you inside the sect, since everyone knows you’re friends with that filthy son of a bitch, Long Qingyi. But your wife is traveling alone on a mission of her own, isn’t she?</p>

<p>Don’t worry, I won’t rape her. At least not if you show your face here and fight me like a man. I, Su Beifeng, will wash away the shame you put my younger brother through!]</p>

<p>Qingyi tried with all his might to control the rage rising in his chest.</p>

<p>But with every word he reread, he felt his heart burn more and more, a fire threatening to consume all his reason.</p>

<p>The entire place had been sealed off over the past few months.</p>

<p>Fortunately, due to the lack of cleaning, everything he needed was still there, preserved by the very fact that it had been abandoned.</p>

<p>Qingyi turned his gaze to the signature on the back of the letter, studying the coordinates engraved just below it.</p>

<p>They were the coordinates of a distant, frozen moon—one of the most remote in the entire solar system where they were located.</p>

<p>Technically, that region should have been under the control of an inner disciple of the sect—Su Beifeng himself—but it was an area that received little attention.</p>

<p>Many even spread rumors that Beifeng was hiring bandits to consolidate his power there and gather resources, all with the goal of returning to the sect and becoming a core disciple.</p>

<p>Since Beifeng had connections to an elder—who, coincidentally, was also in the same region at that very moment—these rumors were quickly quashed by him.</p>

<p>Qingyi stood up slowly, the paper still firmly clenched between his fingers.</p>

<p>Their plan would have been nearly perfect, had Qingyi not possessed his current cultivation technique.</p>

<p>That letter would never have been discovered, and the sect’s investigation—thwarted by the elder himself—would never have gone anywhere.</p>

<p>Unfortunately for them, they had targeted the wrong person’s friend.</p>

<p>Qingyi let out a soft sigh, satisfied to realize that when he reconstructed the paper, the arrays meant to destroy it had not been restored along with it.</p>

<p>In other words, he now held in his hands a letter confirming Beifeng’s crime, signed with his own name, and with his current location recorded right below it.</p>

<p>With eyes filled with anger, Qingyi left the room, his gaze falling on the guard who was still trembling in the hallway.</p>

<p>"Deliver this letter to Elder Shen. Tell him I went to handle the matter on my own," Qingyi said, handing over the letter along with some spirit crystals—the equivalent of about a full year’s salary for that guard.</p>

<p>"I hope you know what the consequences will be for disobeying me."</p>

<p>"I-I won’t let you down, Young Lord Qingyi!" the guard declared shakily, nearly letting the crystals slip through his fingers.</p>

<p>Immediately afterward, he hurried off, running down the hallway to deliver the letter to Elder Shen.</p>

<p>He wasn’t a fool. He knew that disobeying someone as powerful as Qingyi was sheer madness, especially when the matter involved the life and death of someone important to him.</p>

<p>Watching the guard disappear down the hallway, Qingyi remained silent for several long seconds, his gaze lost in the distance.</p>

<p>[You’re too angry—get a hold of yourself, love... everything will be all right.] Ruxue’s sweet, honeyed voice echoed softly in his mind, and he nodded with a bitter smile curving the corners of his lips.</p>

<p>"Ah... I’m sorry. I think I’ve become a little sensitive to the deaths of friends after Meilin’s grandfather’s passing."</p>

<p>[You know you can bring him back using your cultivation technique once we return to the Immortal Heaven, don’t you? Someone of his power level would take at least a thousand years for his consciousness to be erased.]</p>

<p>"I know." Qingyi nodded, focusing his mind. "But I won’t let it happen again."</p>
