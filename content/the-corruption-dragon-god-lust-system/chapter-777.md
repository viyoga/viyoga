<p>Three breathtaking beauties stepped into the most luxurious hall of the Myriad Beasts Sect.</p>

<p>They walked side by side, with Yunxi in the center, Rou’er on the left, and Xue Hua on the right.</p>

<p>The moment their figures appeared before the guests, all attention—which had previously been focused on Qingyi—shifted at once, entirely captivated by the three of them.</p>

<p>They were beautiful.</p>

<p>That had never been a matter of debate.</p>

<p>Even those who had only heard their names, without ever having seen them up close, were already familiar with the renown of their beauty.</p>

<p>Three of the most beautiful women in the northern region of the Celestial Heaven, each incomparable in her own way.</p>

<p>Their red, pink, and white dresses had been carefully arranged into elegant, graceful styles, every strand falling in perfect order over their bare shoulders.</p>

<p>Their eyes—beautiful enough to make a man lose himself in them with a single glance—held both longing and deep love, while their lips curved gently into subtle smiles.</p>

<p>They walked down the wide central aisle of the hall with slow, measured steps.</p>

<p>Their voluptuous bodies were clad in immaculate white silks, a striking contrast to Qingyi’s black robes that, somehow, complemented them just as perfectly.</p>

<p>The fine fabric hugged every one of their curves with a modest grace, tracing the lush figures beneath without ever revealing too much.</p>

<p>The firm clicks of their high heels echoed through the silent hall; each step coaxed a gentle sway from their hips and a soft, rhythmic undulation from their full breasts beneath the clinging silk.</p>

<p>Step by step, they advanced, until at last they came to stand before Qingyi.</p>

<p>Their beautiful eyes rested upon him, meeting the passionate gaze that awaited them.</p>

<p>"You all look beautiful..." Qingyi’s lips curved gently, his soft voice carrying genuine love.</p>

<p>Only Rouxi was missing to make his happiness complete.</p>

<p>For now, however, he would be content with the three standing before him.</p>

<p>"You’re handsome, too..." the three replied almost in unison, their sweet voices ringing shyly through the hall, their cheeks flushed with bashful warmth.</p>

<p>With one more step, they took their places beside Qingyi and, together with him, turned toward the altar.</p>

<p>The priest, who had been watching them with a certain fondness, simply motioned for them to step forward.</p>

<p>The delicate feet of the three beauties and Qingyi’s powerful stride met the first step in perfect unison.</p>

<p>"Those who seek eternity together bow to the earth." The priest’s voice echoed through the sacred hall.</p>

<p>The four bowed at the same time and then took the next step.</p>

<p>"They bow to the heavens."</p>

<p>They bowed once more.</p>

<p>"They bow to their fathers, mothers, and ancestors."</p>

<p>They bowed one last time and, immediately afterward, reached the fourth step, which led them to the golden top of the altar.</p>

<p>"Long Qingyi." The priest’s eyes rested on the young dragon. "You are the Supreme Emperor of the Ten Thousand Blades, the greatest talent of your generation, the eternal and incomparable Black Dragon."</p>

<p>The priest’s gaze swept over those watching the ceremony as he raised a cup of spiritual liquor.</p>

<p>"Does anyone question his dignity to become the eternal husband, lover, and protector of these women?" The priest’s voice rang through the hall.</p>

<p>Naturally, no one dared utter a single word.</p>

<p>No one there wished to be torn to pieces by the leader of the Myriad Beasts Sect, whose imposing presence loomed over the hall.</p>

<p>The priest held the cup aloft for a single moment before lowering it; the cold metal met the gold of the small table before him with a faint clink, the spirit liquor inside swirling gently.</p>

<p>His thin lips curved into a faint smile as he continued.</p>

<p>"Xue Hua, the Black Rose, do you promise to love and serve Long Qingyi for the rest of your life?" the priest asked, dipping a single finger into the spiritual liquor.</p>

<p>"I do," she replied with a little more enthusiasm than she intended, her pale cheeks flushing an even deeper shade of red.</p>

<p>The old priest let out a soft chuckle, withdrawing his index finger from the elixir and flicking a single drop toward Xue Hua’s forehead.</p>

<p>Clearly, this was no ordinary liquid.</p>

<p>The moment the drop touched her forehead, a faint ripple of Qi spread across her skin, a soft glow blossoming there like a small orb of light.</p>

<p>The old man then turned his face toward Bai Yunxi.</p>

<p>"Bai Yunxi, the White Tigress, do you promise to love and serve Long Qingyi for the rest of your life?" the priest asked, dipping his finger into the liquor once more.</p>

<p>"I do!" Bai Yunxi declared with obvious excitement, her radiant smile lighting up her entire face.</p>

<p>The priest once again drew a single drop of the liquor, flicking it toward her pale forehead, marked by the inhuman features of her feline lineage.</p>

<p>The beautiful tigress received the drop gracefully, her full, rosy lips curving into a gentle smile, her blue eyes sparkling like polished sapphires.</p>

<p>Her ample breasts trembled softly beneath the thin fabric of her dress, the subtle quiver mirroring the excitement that surged through every inch of her body.</p>

<p>"Long Rou’er, the Pearl of the Cosmic Dawn Sect, do you promise to love and serve Long Qingyi for the rest of your life?" the priest asked, dipping his finger into the liquor one last time.</p>

<p>"Yes, I do!" Rou’er was the one who declared it with the greatest enthusiasm, her sweet and gentle voice ringing through the hall like a heavenly melody.</p>

<p>Even stellar sovereigns felt their hearts melt upon hearing that voice.</p>

<p>Rou’er also received a drop of spiritual liquor on her forehead; the small orb of light shone on her fair skin like a tiny star.</p>

<p>Finally, it was Qingyi’s turn.</p>

<p>"Long Qingyi, Supreme Emperor of the Ten Thousand Blades, the Black Dragon of the Divine Fist. Do you promise, for your entire life—whether it be eternal or not—to love, care for, and protect these women? To give your life for them, to live for them, and to die for them?"</p>

<p>Qingyi did not hesitate; his powerful, serene voice rose with the dignity and nobility befitting an emperor. "I promise."</p>
