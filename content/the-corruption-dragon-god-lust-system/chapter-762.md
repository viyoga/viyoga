<p>The moment those words rang in his ears, Qingyi’s heart gave a violent lurch inside his chest.</p>

<p>Without wasting a second, he opened her information screen, and his eyes widened at what flashed across it.</p>

<p>That bear was no mere Stellar Sovereign... She was a Celestial Empress! And not just any Celestial Empress, but one as ancient as Auranys herself.</p>

<p>[She is the wife of the golden dragon who slew the Celestial Father and founded the Celestial Heavens... be very careful... Do not reveal my existence to her!]</p>

<p>"Oh... Auranys, I’m just a senile old woman on the brink of death. Don’t worry, my dear, I pose no danger to you," the bear’s voice carried a gentle, almost maternal warmth. "I’ve even forgotten the battles of the past.</p>

<p>I just want to catch up with some old enemies and friends before I go. Come, step out of this weapon’s mind world—let me get a good look at you.</p>

<p>I must confess, I never imagined you’d fall into the hands of the heir to the Dragon God of Corruption, hahaha."</p>

<p>At those words, the cold gripping Qingyi’s heart only deepened.</p>

<p>That bear not only knew what the Heaven-Defying Thunder Sword was, but also his origin, and even his lineage.</p>

<p>Worse still, she somehow had the means to listen in on every conversation he held with his women inside the mind world of the Heaven-Defying Thunder Sword.</p>

<p>She was dangerous. Far too dangerous.</p>

<p>Qingyi hesitated, his eyes gradually filling with a sharp murderous intent. In silence, he began to prepare the token capable of annihilating even a celestial emperor.</p>

<p>The bear was dangerous, yes, but he, too, had his own trump cards.</p>

<p>"Oh... don’t be so hostile toward me, young one," the bear shook her enormous head, her beastly face taking on a hurt expression.</p>

<p>"I was just excited to see an old opponent from the past again. We fought ten times, and it all ended in a tie—five wins for me and five for her. You don’t need to worry so much about this dying old woman."</p>

<p>Qingyi wasn’t so easily fooled. He kept his gaze firmly fixed on the beast before him. "Do you know what the book I won from the tournament is?"</p>

<p>"I do," the bear nodded slowly, her enormous eyes shining with a nostalgic light.</p>

<p>"The Nine Paths of Creation Reversal Art. When my husband sacrificed himself to forge the heavens, he left it behind. It was by studying just a small fragment of it that he managed to create everything he did."</p>

<p>"This thing is already old, and, honestly, I have no further use for it. It’s much better off in the hands of a young person like you."</p>

<p>Qingyi’s eyes narrowed, but his mouth remained closed for long moments, each of her words echoing in his mind.</p>

<p>"Why? If I master the Aether, I’ll bring about the fall of the heavens."</p>

<p>"The heavens won’t fall—they’ll simply gain a new ruler," the bear replied calmly.</p>

<p>"My little daughter must be very lonely in that cold place, and I know that if you manage to break the heavens’ current dominion, you’ll be able to set her free."</p>

<p>Qingyi watched her in silence for a long time.</p>

<p>No matter how hard he looked, he couldn’t find even the slightest sign of a lie on her beast-like face.</p>

<p>"How did you know who I was to make this book the tournament’s reward?" Qingyi asked once more, his voice still heavy with caution.</p>

<p>"I didn’t know," the old bear shook her head, her lips curving into a small smile. "I just had a hunch—nothing more."</p>

<p>In the end, Qingyi could only let out a heavy sigh, his eyes filled with complicated thoughts.</p>

<p>"I know that a cultivation session led by me would be one of the rewards promised to you, but I can’t help you with anything beyond that. Would you like me to just send you back to the sect?"</p>

<p>"I can sense that you’re uncomfortable, and this old woman here just wanted to take a look at you—nothing more."</p>

<p>"That would be... good," Qingyi replied, returning to his human form.</p>

<p>His robes reappeared on his body, and the Heaven-Defying Thunder Sword materialized once more at his waist.</p>

<p>"Very well," the old bear smiled gently, waving one of her enormous claws.</p>

<p>A small talisman materialized in the air and flew toward Qingyi.</p>

<p>"Keep this by your side at all times. If it detects the presence of the Heavenly Demon around you, it will automatically teleport me to your location."</p>

<p>"I will protect you with my very life, for I have complete confidence that you will be able to shatter the heavens and rescue my dear little daughter."</p>

<p>"Thank you... Senior, I will never forget this!" Qingyi bowed deeply, clasping his fists in a sign of respect.</p>

<p>With a slight wave of her claws and a gentle smile on her face, the she-bear teleported Qingyi back to the sect.</p>

<p>When the handsome young man disappeared, leaving her alone in that silent space, the bear let out a long sigh, her eyes growing heavy.</p>

<p>A few months ago, she had sensed the presence of an old acquaintance of hers—one who, in theory, should have been dead long ago, but who, somehow, had been reborn.</p>

<p>"Ah... I think it’s time to pay her a little visit and find out what her interests are regarding this young man," the she-bear murmured, sighing once more.</p>

<p>Then, her enormous body dissolved, vanishing silently into the void.</p>

<p>Silently, Qingyi appeared at the heart of the Myriad Beasts Sect.</p>

<p>He pondered the old bear’s words at length, but in the end, he pushed them all out of his mind.</p>

<p>For now, what he needed most was to grow stronger.</p>

<p>It was time to improve his bloodline and study that new cultivation art!</p>

<p>The moment he entered a cultivation room, Qingyi didn’t hesitate for even a single instant. His eyes lit up with excitement as he sat down cross-legged.</p>

<p>Finally, he had some time to himself, and he intended to make the most of it.</p>

<p>With a big smile, he opened his status screen, clicking to improve his lineage and constitution once again.</p>
