<p>The atmosphere at the Sect of the Cosmic Void was festive.</p>

<p>Today, the sect’s secret realm would finally open its doors to a hundred lucky young masters, who would venture into a world full of dangers, but also of riches.</p>

<p>Perfect arrays for soul cultivation. Legacies of cultivators lost in the past. Soul artifacts of unparalleled quality. Spiritual herbs capable of aiding in the birth of stellar sovereigns.</p>

<p>There was everything in that secret realm.</p>

<p>Only the sect’s hundred most talented members could enter, and all depended entirely on their protectors to claim the riches within.</p>

<p>In total, one hundred talents for one hundred protectors. Ninety-nine protectors, in this specific case.</p>

<p>One of the sect’s talents had been replaced by Rou’er, and her protector was accompanying two people.</p>

<p>Naturally, preparations for such an important event began early. With the sect leader’s announcement of the date and time of the secret realm’s opening, the elders erected defensive formations around the entire area.</p>

<p>Eight stellar sovereigns took their positions, encircling the region in an impenetrable perimeter to ensure nothing would go wrong.</p>

<p>That secret realm opened only once every few decades. On some occasions, it was capable of giving birth to a new stellar sovereign, or dooming an entire generation.</p>

<p>No one there was willing to make mistakes.</p>

<p>Minutes before the secret realm opened, in a luxurious office, an irritated voice echoed.</p>

<p>“The Guo family contacted me. They were questioning the shame you put your fiancé through…” The sect’s grand elder, Xue Hua’s father, crossed his arms as he stared at his daughter. “You know very well how important this agreement is to me, don’t you?”</p>

<p>“Shame him?” Xue Hua let out a dry laugh. “That pig has nothing left to be ashamed of; he’s lucky I even look at him. If we met in the secret realm, I guarantee I’d order Qingyi to kill him.”</p>

<p>The Grand Elder ignored his daughter’s initial disrespect, but the moment he heard those final words, his face froze.</p>

<p>“Girl… don’t test my patience.” The great elder rose with a growl, his powerful aura bearing down on Xue Hua. “Don’t make me show you what terror is.”</p>

<p>“Show me that, you senile old man.” Xue Hua didn’t back down a single step. “I dare you to hurt me. I dare you to test whether the talisman I received from my mother still works.”</p>

<p>The great elder froze at those words. His face twisted with rage, but in the end, he simply sat back down.</p>

<p>“You really are just like your mother. Beautiful and bold, worthy of being my daughter.” A bitter smile appeared at the corner of his lips. “Very well. If you kill Longwei in the secret realm, I’ll pretend I didn’t hear any of this, but I guarantee, you’ll marry a man far worse.”</p>

<p>“You won’t marry me off to any worse man, Father. Mother is coming, and you’re afraid of her.” Xue Hua laughed contemptuously before turning, walking toward the door without looking back.</p>

<p>“Try not to die before she can rip your balls off.” Xue Hua’s voice echoed one last time before disappearing down the hallway.</p>

<p>In the end, the great elder could only sigh in defeat.</p>

<p>He wasn’t unhappy. Quite the opposite.</p>

<p>He was proud.</p>

<p>He had raised an incredible daughter, hadn’t he? What other woman in her cultivation realm would face the aura of a star sovereign with such arrogance and courage?</p>

<p>Of course, there was her mother, who was a figure from the central region, but that was beside the point.</p>

<p>He wished his daughter had been a little more respectful, especially because of that last sentence, which truly hurt him.</p>

<p>But it was fine. It had been his fault.</p>

<p>If Longwei died, at least he wouldn’t have to deal with the headache of calling off the wedding.</p>

<p>“Ah… my dear, our baby has grown up. Her old father can no longer control her… The elder’s eyes drifted beyond the office window, his voice heavy with longing. “When will you come back to me?”</p>

<p>The great elder’s voice echoed once more in that empty office.</p>

<p>He could only hope that Xue Hua would have good luck in the secret realm, and that that young dragon, Long Qingyi, would help her conquer the many incomparable and forbidden treasures within.</p>

<p>***</p>

<p>Honestly, having Xue Hua would be good for his lust points, since he could take her purity more easily than Rou’er’s, who wanted to wait until marriage.</p>

<p>But there was also a problem.</p>

<p>With Xue Hua, he would have to actively seek out fortunes in the secret realm. Unlike Rou’er, with whom he would only need to wait for time to pass, since she had no interest in any treasure.</p>

<p>All things considered, he’d gain much more by fucking her and his wives in the mind world than by seeking opportunities inside. All of this was quite problematic, but in the end, it was all right.</p>

<p>It would be worth it, and one way or another, Rou’er was also excited to explore the secret realm.</p>

<p>He could spend quality time by her side while also securing fortunes for Xue Hua.</p>

<p>Obviously, he wouldn’t let Longwei, with that tiger, amass more fortunes than the woman by his side.</p>

<p>After a few minutes of waiting, Xue Hua’s figure finally appeared.</p>

<p>She was beautiful as always. The pale skin of her breasts revealed in a provocative neckline, her black lips curved into a soft, sweet smile. Qingyi wasn’t fooled by that smile, but he still admired her in silence.</p>

<p>It was hard to deny that she deserved her place as one of the region’s three great beauties.</p>

<p>She wasn’t as beautiful as Rou’er, but still, she was perfection in the form of a woman.</p>

<p>Approaching, Xue Hua held out her hand. “This is a map of the secret realm. The functionality of our spatial rings will be quite limited inside; we can store things, but we can’t retrieve them, so keep this safe.”</p>

<p>Qingyi said nothing, taking the map and tucking it into his robes.</p>

<p>His eyes slowly rose as a loud bell echoed in the distance, its deep sound reverberating throughout the sect.</p>

<p>It was beginning.</p>
