<p>Elder Shen had expected the young master Guo Tianhao to be the one to step forward, but to his surprise, he merely looked away arrogantly, ignoring Qingyi’s presence.</p>

<p>The other nine inner disciples exchanged glances, and after a moment, one of them stepped forward.</p>

<p>“Due to the difference in cultivation, the inner disciple will not use Qi, only his brute strength and senses.</p>

<p>Do your best to win, but if you lose, it doesn’t matter.” Elder Shen declared, gesturing to Qingyi and the inner disciple before him.</p>

<p>The two young men exchanged formal greetings and then stepped forward.</p>

<p>To the inner disciple’s surprise, Qingyi also did not dare to use Qi.</p>

<p>“This bastard in the Astral Body Realm wants to compete in brute strength against someone in the Celestial Ascension Realm?” The inner disciple was unable to hide his shock.</p>

<p>He wasn’t particularly powerful, but wasn’t Qingyi underestimating him too much?</p>

<p>Neither of them drew their weapons; fists flew freely toward one another.</p>

<p>The punches collided in the air, which collapsed into a powerful shockwave, the dust of the arena rising in a whirlwind around the</p>

<p>two.</p>

<p>To everyone’s surprise, Qingyi took only three steps back while the inner disciple took one.</p>

<p>It looked like a victory for the inner disciple, even considering the difference in cultivation between them.</p>

<p>Wasn’t that a bit absurd? Even without the inner disciple using his full</p>

<p>power, it was still hard to imagine such a scenario.</p>

<p>“Uh… I’ve always heard about the strength of black dragons… it wasn’t a lie.” The inner disciple sighed, calming his heart.</p>

<p>He could have ended the fight right there, Qingyi had passed. But…</p>

<p>He wanted a little more.</p>

<p>Exchanging glances with Qingyi and realizing that the handsome young man felt the same, the disciple smiled.</p>

<p>Elder Shen covered the arena with his Qi as the bodies of the two young men vanished.</p>

<p>In just a second, their fists collided dozens of times, thunderous explosions engulfing everything around them, the arena floor reduced to dust.</p>

<p>No one dared make a single sound, the other participants in the selection watching that exchange in shock.</p>

<p>Without even using Qi, yet causing such destruction and shockwaves…</p>

<p>Were they really on the same level?</p>

<p>It certainly didn’t seem so.</p>

<p>In the end, after just over thirty exchanges, the two reappeared, both panting, soft smiles on their faces.</p>

<p>“Approved, junior brother.” The inner disciple turned his back.</p>

<p>He had won every single exchange, and if he had been using Qi, he could have easily crushed Qingyi.</p>

<p>But this time, he swallowed his pride and admitted defeat.</p>

<p>“Thank you for the teachings, senior brother.” Qingyi clasped his fists before returning to Chen Wei’s side, who was watching him with shining eyes.</p>

<p>“What’s up?” Qingyi asked, stretching.</p>

<p>“Even if that inner disciple was holding back, do you know how strong an inner disciple is? He must have even received direct training from an elder for some time!”</p>

<p>Qingyi didn’t pay much attention to those words, simply relaxing.</p>

<p>Yes, he was powerful, and it had been a good fight, but he didn’t dwell</p>

<p>on it for long.</p>

<p>For now, his only priority was to grow stronger without his lineage of the Corruption Dragon God being discovered.</p>

<p>Receiving silence in response, Chen Wei just sighed.</p>

<p>He was among the first to pass the test, so it would soon be his turn to fight as well.</p>

<p>The following fights were far less interesting.</p>

<p>Chen Wei and Young Master Luo fought one after the other, both receiving approval.</p>

<p>The young man who had finished the trial right after Qingyi was also approved, despite having faced Young Master Guo Tianhao.</p>

<p>In the end, after long minutes of duels, Peng Tao fought, and was thrown out of the arena with a single slap, receiving a failing grade.</p>

<p>With teary eyes and a flushed face, he returned to his young master Chen’s side.</p>

<p>Of the slightly more than sixty youths, fifteen received approval from the inner disciples.</p>

<p>The others could still take up combat positions, but were better suited as training masters or in other production roles within the</p>

<p>sect.</p>

<p>Gathering the youths in a corner, Elder Shen led them out of that place, which was immediately swallowed by a concealment array, erasing it completely.</p>

<p>That location was used only for recruitment every few years,</p>

<p>spending most of its time hidden.</p>

<p>“Stay in formation and wait.” The elder rose into the air, looking up.</p>

<p>On the horizon, among the clouds, a vessel tore through the skies toward them, propelled by powerful Qi thrusters and covered by a thin barrier that seemed to serve both to keep oxygen inside and to</p>

<p>regulate gravity.</p>

<p>‘A spaceship… Qingyi’s heart raced, nearly leaping out of his chest. He had seen so much, defeated so many enemies.</p>

<p>But still, seeing that ship tearing through the skies toward them, the heat from the thrusters making his long black hair flutter…</p>

<p>Faced with that, he was like a child.</p>

<p>‘Ruxue… how much would it cost for me to build my own spaceship?”</p>

<p>Qingyi asked, barely able to hide his excitement.</p>

<p>[Hm… when we have some free time, I’ll take you to one of the shipyards belonging to my creator’s family… some people there still owe me favors, if they’re still alive.]</p>

<p>‘Thank you, love…’ Qingyi flashed a genuine smile.</p>

<p>Arriving before them, the spaceship, just over two hundred meters long and with room for thousands of disciples, landed.</p>

<p>Dust swirled up, fluttering everyone’s robes; a terrifying pressure overwhelmed them for an instant before subsiding.</p>

<p>The ship’s hatch, bearing the sect’s symbol, slowly opened, revealing</p>

<p>the interior.</p>

<p>The elder stepped inside, and the disciples immediately followed him.</p>

<p>“Country bumpkins.” Young Master Luo growled through clenched teeth, watching as Qingyi and Chen Wei gazed at the spacecraft in</p>

<p>awe.</p>

<p>Quickening his pace, he stood side by side with Guo Tianhao, who gave him a gentle smile.</p>

<p>Fortunately for Young Master Luo, Tianhao, whose background was incomparable to his own, had shown him great goodwill.</p>

<p>So what if Chen Wei was friends with Qingyi? He had Tianhao by his</p>

<p>side! That alone was enough to guarantee him glory and wealth that even someone as powerful as Qingyi could never even begin to imagine.</p>
