<p>For the first time in a long, long time, Qingyi felt at real risk of death, his mind running through millions of possibilities as he raised his sword.</p>

<p>What on earth could he do against an ancestral emperor? He couldn’t even cross enough cultivation realms to fight a true immortal!</p>

<p>For a brief moment, he opened the system store, looking to buy an escape talisman, but it was too late.</p>

<p>Jinrui’s father had already drawn his sword, all his Qi falling on Qingyi as he attacked.</p>

<p>At that moment, something changed.</p>

<p>Qingyi felt a powerful, heavy figure land next to him, a higher Qi than Jinrui’s father surrounding him.</p>

<p>It was Tai’Ren, his body enveloped in intense green Qi, his muscles expanding as his entire being blazed with power.</p>

<p>The whole thing lasted less than the fraction of a second, but in Qingyi’s mind, it seemed to have lasted many minutes.</p>

<p>Jinrui’s father’s blade stopped a single centimeter from Qingyi’s face, all remaining energy being completely eliminated by Tai’Ren’s Qi.</p>

<p>Even without hitting Qingyi directly, that residual energy was powerful enough to seriously injure even immortal emperors.</p>

<p>“Looks like losing just one hand wasn’t enough, huh?” Tai’Ren growled, retracting his fist before punching.</p>

<p>Jinrui’s father’s eyes widened as he tried to defend himself, but it was too late.</p>

<p>Tai’Ren’s fist hit his face with all the force of one of the most powerful ancestral emperors in the entire immortal heaven and, in the next moment, there was silence.</p>

<p>Jinrui’s father’s body disappeared completely.</p>

<p>The reason for this silence was not the shock of the audience, but the total lack of any means for sound to propagate; every molecule of air had been pushed away, leaving nothing but an intense vacuum.</p>

<p>In the next moment, this vacuum collapsed and the bang was finally heard, a shockwave sweeping across the arena, with the ancestral emperors acting quickly to protect the audience.</p>

<p>Qingyi, still in shock, turned his face towards the horizon, observing a huge mountain, one of the largest in the land of beastmen, which towered majestically over the capital of the dragon people.</p>

<p>It was against it that Jinrui’s father’s body had been thrown, hitting its side at absurd speed, sinking into the resistant rock and creating a crater kilometers deep.</p>

<p>The whole mountain shook, the tremors spreading all the way to the capital, making the ground beneath everyone’s feet tremble.</p>

<p>“Fuck! Blergh…” Jinrui’s father vomited a gulp of blood. His clothes were torn, his face burning and his body sunk in miles of earth.</p>

<p>It only took him a brief moment to regain his composure and realize what had just happened.</p>

<p>His eyes filled with even more fury and he roared.</p>

<p>“TAI’REN, YOU SON OF A BITCH!” Jinrui’s father’s icy aura engulfed everything around him, his Qi exploding the sea of rocks, sending the thousands of tons of rock above him into the air.</p>

<p>The mountain that had once stood tens of kilometers high, bigger than anything Qingyi had ever seen on earth, was now reduced to just half its original size.</p>

<p>“Ah… looks like someone hasn’t learned yet, hahahaha!” Tai’Ren laughed, ready to throw himself at his old enemy and settle their differences.</p>

<p>Unfortunately, he had to stop at the next moment.</p>

<p>Tianjin, the patriarch of the dragon people, one of only two astral body realm cultivators among the beastmen, finally made a move.</p>

<p>He raised a single hand, his beautiful golden eyes sweeping over everyone there.</p>

<p>“Enough.” A single word and Tai’Ren’s Qi collapsed, as did Jinrui’s father’s, even dozens of kilometers away.</p>

<p>They both fell to their knees, coughing up gulps of blood.</p>

<p>“You can kill yourselves another time. For now, the most important thing is that the divine beast tournament goes ahead as normal.” Tianjin spoke, two guards appearing at his side.</p>

<p>They bowed, receiving orders to put Jinrui’s father under house arrest.</p>

<p>Having tried to kill Qingyi was a serious act of disrespect, and Tianjin would deal with it later.</p>

<p>“Ugh… that sucks.” Tai’Ren stood up, turning his face to Qingyi. “What’s up, son-in-law? You’re not going to break just with that wimp’s aura, are you? Hahaha.”</p>

<p>“Not at all, father-in-law, hahaha,” Qingyi replied, getting up and wiping the blood from his lips.</p>

<p>His beautiful purple eyes immediately met Qianyao’s, and he smiled.</p>

<p>“Young Miss Qianyao, are you ready? I’m still excited for us to finish that duel,” Qingyi said.</p>

<p>The golden-eyed beauty seemed to reflect for a brief moment, her delicate fingers removing a lock of silver hair from in front of her beautiful face.</p>

<p>“You’re hurt…” she whispered, jumping into the arena and walking towards Qingyi.</p>

<p>Her fingers searched for something in her space ring, and a single golden pill appeared between them.</p>

<p>Without hesitation, she pushed that pill into Qingyi’s mouth, whose eyes widened in surprise.</p>

<p>“I surrender…” She blushed, looking at Qingyi’s handsome face one last time before turning to her father, who was completely calm, unlike the shock carried by all the others watching the scene.</p>

<p>With one smooth movement, Qianyao’s feet left the ground and landed next to her father.</p>

<p>“Ah… my son-in-law really is incomparable, hahaha… both in talent and in… well… attractiveness, HAHAHAH” Tai’Ren patted Qingyi on the shoulder.</p>

<p>“I’m sure this pill is rare. Go on, start healing your body, you don’t want to waste any of it.”</p>

<p>Hearing those words, Qingyi just gave a bitter little laugh and sat down cross-legged, starting to circulate his Qi.</p>

<p>He wasn’t happy with the outcome. He wanted a serious fight with Qianyao, to test his power even more.</p>

<p>Jinrui had been strong and pushed him to the limit, but that wasn’t the limit Qingyi wanted to face.</p>

<p>“Ah… I’ll still have a chance…” The handsome young man sighed, watching the guards fly off into the sky with Jinrui’s angry father in their arms.</p>

<p>Soon after, a group of doctors arrived to pick up Jinrui himself, who was unconscious, his dantian completely destroyed.</p>

<p>It served them both right.</p>

<p>May they be less horrible people in their next lives.</p>
