<p>It didn’t take more than a few days for the entire kingdom of the Blue Sea to be engulfed in chaos.</p>

<p>The walls of the capital were temporarily sealed, and all the armies under the rule of the princes were forced to retreat beyond the royal domain.</p>

<p>The second prince settled in the Fortress of the Nine Winds to the northwest, while the third settled in the city of the Crimson Blade to the south.</p>

<p>Both gathered armies of tens of thousands of soldiers.</p>

<p>To cause even more chaos, the third prince announced the marriage of the first princess to the current young master of the Five Colors Pavilion, Jin Tianshi.</p>

<p>This was naturally only possible because of the cancellation of her former marriage arrangement with Wang Haoran, who had been killed by a mysterious expert many months ago.</p>

<p>Such a decision caused a rift between the third prince and the great sects, who despised the presence of the Gold King Chamber of Commerce branch in the region.</p>

<p>After much infighting, the decision by the major sects to withdraw their support for the third prince echoed like thunder throughout the kingdom.</p>

<p>Naturally, the Eagle Peak Valley also had to meddle in these discussions.</p>

<p>Especially Margareth and Meilin, who received requests for support from both princes.</p>

<p>Qingyi, on the other hand, was focusing his mind on something else: reaching the Flowing Meridian Realm.</p>

<p>It had been about two weeks since they had received an ultimatum from the princes.</p>

<p>During this time, he had done nothing but dual cultivation with his wives, dedicating more than ten hours a day to Meilin and Margareth alone.</p>

<p>He managed to earn just over fifteen billion lust points and advance to the peak of the Ethereal Body realm.</p>

<p>Now, he finally felt ready to advance to the Flowing Meridian realm.</p>

<p>Sitting cross-legged, he clicked to improve his cultivation.</p>

<p>Ten billion lust points were wiped from his account, his meridians immediately being seized by intense pain.</p>

<p>This was one of the final refinements before being considered a true immortal; a tempering of the meridians, widening them and preparing them to be the receptacles of an immortal soul alongside the body.</p>

<p>Naturally, this process was painful, but Qingyi barely reacted. Only faint grunts escaped his lips as he struggled to keep his Qi stable.</p>

<p>During his time using the system, he had developed an absurd resistance to pain. That kind of suffering was nothing to him anymore.</p>

<p>[Your cultivation has been improved! Third stage of the Ethereal Body realm -&gt; first stage of the Flowing Meridian realm].</p>

<p>Qingyi’s eyes, firmly closed, suddenly opened. A shockwave spread through the cultivation room in a dry rumble.</p>

<p>“Ah…” He sighed, looking down at his hands and standing up.</p>

<p>After a moment’s hesitation, he used his law, the Primordial Chaos Flames.</p>

<p>His hand was immediately filled with gray flames of extreme heat.</p>

<p>Keeping them alight, he carefully controlled them to avoid damaging the cultivation room around him.</p>

<p>After about thirty seconds of this, he finally felt his meridians begin to burn. After fifty seconds, he was forced to stop, sighing heavily.</p>

<p>Fifty seconds was the limit by which he could use that law, at least without suffering damage to his meridians.</p>

<p>After thinking for a brief moment, Qingyi just shook his head and left the cultivation room.</p>

<p>He soon found Meilin and Margareth in the city lord’s mansion.</p>

<p>“Have you decided?” He asked as he entered the golden-haired beauty’s office.</p>

<p>He placed a soft kiss on each of their lips before pulling them onto his lap and sitting down on a sofa in the corner of the office.</p>

<p>His hands went up to the two beauties’ breasts, squeezing gently.</p>

<p>“Mghn~~ Yes…” Meilin replied. “Both princes make a lot of threats, but honestly, we’re surrounded by supporters of the third prince.</p>

<p>If we declare support for the second, we’ll be surrounded on all sides.</p>

<p>Besides, the kingdom’s sects have switched their support to the second prince. My father has problems with sects, both those here and those in the Empire of the Enlightened Sky. I don’t want to help them gain even more power.”</p>

<p>“What about your brother?” Qingyi turned his face slightly.</p>

<p>He didn’t consider himself a dumb person, but he also knew that in terms of intelligence, he was far from Meilin, Margareth or even other wives like Biyue and Ruxue.</p>

<p>So he respected their decision, although he was still a little worried about Meilin.</p>

<p>“There’s not much you can do. In the end, father’s interests are more important. We can deal with him when we have time.”</p>

<p>Hearing those words, Qingyi nodded.</p>

<p>If that was Meilin’s decision, he would help her and kill whoever stood in her way.</p>

<p>Turning to Margareth, he received basically the same opinion: the third prince, even at a military disadvantage, was still one of the best choices for them.</p>

<p>The Church of Auranys itself, which had the final say in the matter, also agreed.</p>

<p>The bishop provided around ten holy guards from the cathedral to accompany the force of just over a thousand men that Margareth was able to assemble.</p>

<p>The vast majority of this army was made up of locals who had come for work but had not yet converted.</p>

<p>After another week of preparation, they were finally ready to leave. A large convoy formed around the Burning Red Pavilion.</p>

<p>The price was astronomical, but she paid no attention, preparing a perfect contract for the third prince to sign.</p>

<p>If they won the war, the royal government would be responsible for paying back the loan equivalent to the purchase price of the weapons plus interest of thirty-five percent a year.</p>

<p>If they lost, the third prince’s private residences could be auctioned off to pay the full amount of the loan.</p>

<p>Since he would be dead if he lost, the third prince had no reason to refuse that contract.</p>

<p>Of course, that wasn’t an absolute guarantee. The second prince could simply seize the third prince’s residences and refuse to pay, but even in this case, Meilin had a solution.</p>

<p>The contract would be signed with the Empire of the Illuminated Sky as the main guarantor that it would be fulfilled, something she could easily achieve with her father’s help.</p>

<p>With a legally valid contract recognized by such a power, there was nothing the second prince could do…</p>

<p>“I’m a genius, hehehe…” Meilin gave a muffled giggle as she rested in Qingyi’s embrace in a luxurious carriage.</p>

<p>“Yes, you are.” The handsome young man laughed, patting Meilin on the butt before turning his face away, realizing that the carriage had started to move.</p>

<p>It was time for him to finally meet the third prince in person.</p>
