<p>Qingyi’s words weren’t much, but they were enough to completely destabilize Xue Mo.</p>

<p>“Don’t you dare put my sister’s name in your mouth, you filthy worm!” The demon cult’s young lord roared, his eyes filled with enough hatred to melt steel.</p>

<p>Without holding back, Xue Mo swung his blade in a horizontal slash.</p>

<p>Qingyi raised the heaven-defying thunder sword defensively, but soon regretted not dodging.</p>

<p>It was like being hit by a truck at top speed; his feet were knocked off the ground and his arms shook as he was catapulted backwards.</p>

<p>His body tore through the dense vegetation, his back breaking through thick trunks like toothpicks, leaving a trail of destruction.</p>

<p>He only came to a stop after almost five hundred meters, sinking his heaven-defying thunder sword into the ground for support.</p>

<p>Raising his gaze, he saw Xue Mo whose body had already changed completely, a crimson aura filled with darkness spreading dozens of meters above him.</p>

<p>From this aura, a male figure formed, his eyes blood red, black horns rising from his bald head, his presence powerful and sickly enough to make the plants around him die.</p>

<p>Qingyi felt nothing from that lineage.</p>

<p>Even with the difference in cultivation, it was simply too far from being comparable to the blood of the dragon god of corruption.</p>

<p>All it managed to do was lessen the lineage suppression that Xue Mo was feeling.</p>

<p>“So that’s the heavenly demon?… Quite disappointing.” Qingyi smiled.</p>

<p>“SHUT UP, INSECT!” Xue Mo shouted as he advanced, his crimson blade breaking through the air with frightening speed.</p>

<p>Qingyi didn’t dare let his guard down as a blade of demonic Qi formed in the air, flying towards him.</p>

<p>Stepping hard on the ground, a wall of ice formed in front of him, more than ten meters thick and rising almost thirty meters high.</p>

<p>Even so, it did little to stop Xue Mo’s blow, only slightly reducing its power before shattering with a crystalline crash.</p>

<p>Although the effect was small, Qingyi didn’t care.</p>

<p>His body was covered in black scales, his lightning sword aura shining with more power than ever as he attacked, using the first form of the Tempest Monarch sword art.</p>

<p>When the attacks met, the world around them seemed to completely lose color.</p>

<p>A shockwave capable of bursting eardrums from kilometers away spread through the Forbidden Forest as the world around them shattered into pieces.</p>

<p>’where does this son of a bitch get so much strength?’ Qingyi gritted his teeth, feeling his muscles scream in agony.</p>

<p>His bloodline was much more powerful than Xue Mo’s, but clearly not powerful enough to overcome the difference in cultivation.</p>

<p>The moment he managed to fend off Xue Mo’s blow, Qingyi found himself having to retreat another hundred meters.</p>

<p>“Get him, hubby! Blow his balls off and make him regret being born!” Ruyan’s voice echoed in Qingyi’s mind; she seemed to be watching the battle quite excitedly.</p>

<p>That immediately filled Qingyi with power. He couldn’t be humiliated in front of his women, right?</p>

<p>With a soft grunt, he propelled himself into the air, raising his hand.</p>

<p>He wondered – how many of his flaming spears would Xue Mo be able to withstand? Would he be able to reach the ninth and most powerful of the spears?</p>

<p>’There’s only one way to find out…’ Qingyi muttered as a sea of flames spread from his hands, forming the figure of a long, ethereal spear, its crimson handle covered in dancing flames.</p>

<p>“What trick do you plan to use now, worm? Just hand over your life and I promise I’ll only torture you for a single eternity! Maybe I’ll even give your soul a rest when you die.” Xue Mo said.</p>

<p>“Let’s see if I will.”</p>

<p>Without hesitating any longer, Qingyi finally threw his first spear, which didn’t even have the strength to reach Xue Mo, being shattered by his demonic Qi in mid-air.</p>

<p>“Is that all? Where do you think you’re going with this pathetic display? You filthy servant!” Xue Mo cursed, but was soon forced to swallow his words as a second spear formed in Qingyi’s hands.</p>

<p>It was much more powerful than the previous one, much more intense.</p>

<p>“I can do it too!” Xue Mo leapt into the air, his body also changing.</p>

<p>’Is that something similar to the black dragon’s scales?’ Qingyi wondered.</p>

<p>For the first time since the fight began, he felt a hint of real danger, but that didn’t discourage him.</p>

<p>It had been a long time since he had bled.</p>

<p>Basically all his enemies were just ants that he could blow up with a single attack.</p>
