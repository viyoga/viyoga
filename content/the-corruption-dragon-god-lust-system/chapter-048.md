<p>A small stone was thrown towards the face of a handsome young man.</p>

<p>“Ugh…” Qingyi grunted and his eyes fluttered slightly, but he didn’t get up. His mind was clouded, his body heavy, and his eyelids even more so.</p>

<p>It was only when a slightly larger stone hit him that he finally woke up.</p>

<p>“Fuck…” Qingyi opened his eyes, massaging his head painfully as he stood up.</p>

<p>He was in a dark room of a simple, rustic hut.</p>

<p>“Are you awake, princess?”</p>

<p>Qingyi heard a voice, soft and pleasant, indistinguishably male.</p>

<p>Another stone hit his head and he turned, his eyes meeting those of a man in handcuffs.</p>

<p>“You’re…” Qingyi’s eyes widened and the man smiled.</p>

<p>“Oh… so you remember me? I thought I was totally unconscious when my little sister showed up.”</p>

<p>“The bastard who stopped me from killing that son of a bitch!”</p>

<p>The two of them spoke at the same time, and Tang Yandu’s face darkened.</p>

<p>“No… I mean, yes, but that’s not the point. I’m Tang Yandu!”</p>

<p>“Oh…” Qingyi scratched his chin, remembering the battle against Tianming.</p>

<p>He really didn’t remember anything after he broke through Tianming’s final technique.</p>

<p>That man had suddenly appeared, broken his sword and after that, darkness.</p>

<p>After thinking for a moment, something clicked in his mind and he looked up.</p>

<p>’Surname Tang… a poison cultivator… he said something about a little sister… could he be Tang Biyue’s brother?” Qingyi thought.</p>

<p>That was the best theory he could think of.</p>

<p>It all made sense, why on earth would such a high-level cultivator be in a miserable place like that?</p>

<p>If Tang Yandu was her brother, it would all be explained.</p>

<p>Feeling the Qi around him, he realized that Yandu’s cultivation had been sealed.</p>

<p>There was still a doubt in his chest.</p>

<p>Why on earth was he standing there, next to that man?</p>

<p>“Honestly… I don’t even know why Biyue bothered to save you and treat you so carefully.” Yandu said.</p>

<p>“She even left her dear brother trapped with you, without a single way of protecting himself! Doesn’t that girl love me anymore?” He sniffled, his voice dramatic and tearful.</p>

<p>“Biyue?” Qingyi raised her face, feigning surprise.</p>

<p>“Yes, Tang Biyue, she’s my sister, the most talented cultivator in the Tang family in five thousand years.</p>

<p>The future of our family and one of the few cultivators in the whole world who has a chance of reaching the Transcendence Realm!”</p>

<p>Tang Yandu declared proudly, but paused for a moment, his eyes turning cold as he studied Qingyi.</p>

<p>“I wonder what she saw in you…” He turned his face away and Qingyi shuddered slightly.</p>

<p>Even with his cultivation sealed, Tang Yandu was still a Nascent Soul Realm cultivator.</p>

<p>A being so powerful that he could probably kill hundreds of cultivators like the leader of the Ascending Dragon Sect with a single thought.</p>

<p>His gaze carried an intense and natural pressure.</p>

<p>After a few moments watching Qingyi, Yandu’s face lit up.</p>

<p>Qingyi was a deadly handsome man, but he was sure that his little sister wouldn’t fall for something so simple.</p>

<p>After a moment’s thought, something finally dawned on him.</p>

<p>“You have a bloodline, don’t you? One that would make my little sister interested enough in you to interrupt her path just to study you.”</p>

<p>Yandu’s words made Qingyi stand up abruptly. How the hell did he know?</p>

<p>’System… can they detect my lineage?’</p>

<p>[No, your lineage is totally hidden, that was probably just a clever guess]</p>

<p>Hearing the system’s words, Qingyi heaved a sigh of relief.</p>

<p>His lineage was not something very orthodox, and he would prefer that no one knew about it.</p>

<p>“Don’t be anxious about it, kid, it’s no big deal. My little sister herself has a bloodline!” Tang Yandu laughed.</p>

<p>“Uh?” Qingyi obviously already knew that, but had to feign surprise so as not to arouse any suspicion.</p>

<p>“Yes, the Emerald Abyss Winged Serpent Bloodline… is so powerful that she managed to kill an Iron Body Realm cultivator with a single blow while still in the Foundation Realm at the age of 11.”</p>

<p>Those words shocked Qingyi.</p>

<p>Reaching the Foundation Realm at the age of 11 was already an absurd feat, but to kill an Iron Body Realm cultivate with that cultivation?</p>

<p>He had heard of people being able to cross entire realms, but to cross two realms and kill an opponent with a single blow was impossible to believe.</p>

<p>Even with poisonous Qi, that shouldn’t be possible.</p>

<p>Of course, Qingyi also had to take her cultivation into account.</p>

<p>With each realm, it became more and more difficult to cross stages and realms with pure combat power.</p>

<p>Now, she shouldn’t be able to face opponents more than three or four stages above her.</p>

<p>Something that was still almost unheard of for cultivators in the Rebirth Realm on the Blue Moon Continent, but which was, in a way, much more believable.</p>

<p>Thinking about his own power and the current power of his bloodline, Qingyi could only shudder.</p>

<p>How many levels of cultivation would he be able to cross if he raised it to mortal grade?</p>

<p>Tang Yandu noticed Qingyi’s face light up and his smile widened even more.</p>

<p>“I can teach you everything I know about lineages and constitutions, even help you awaken your true power…”</p>

<p>“And what do you want in return?” Qingyi asked, not in the least seduced by that proposal.</p>

<p>It was worth nothing to him, he had the system.</p>

<p>“Just release me, I have a technique that will allow you to release my sister’s seals, even with your miserable cultivation.</p>

<p>The moment I regain my power, I’ll get us both out of here. What do you think?” Tang Yandu explained, his face full of anticipation.</p>

<p>Qingyi seemed to think for a moment, but in the end just shook his head.</p>

<p>“No, I’m not as dumb as Tianming.”</p>

<p>“Tianming was an idiot, of course, I killed him myself because of that! But you’re clever, you’re not going to force me to kill you, will you?”</p>

<p>Being ignored by Qingyi, a bit of urgency emerged in Yandu’s voice.</p>

<p>“I’m serious, I have secret manuals that I stole from my family. Do you know how much they’re worth?</p>

<p>You could buy an entire country with them in this miserable place! I’ll pass on all the knowledge I have!”</p>

<p>“That won’t be necessary.” A voice echoed and a beauty with black hair and piercing green eyes entered the room.</p>

<p>Her voice was soft and cold, like the pleasant breeze of a spring morning.</p>

<p>Her beautiful eyes studied her brother with disappointment before focusing on Qingyi.</p>

<p>“Come with me.”</p>
