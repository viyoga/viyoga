<p>The capital of the Golden Sun Kingdom was one of the largest cities on the Blue Moon Continent, with almost five million inhabitants and teeming with life even at night.</p>

<p>Its streets were well lit. The brothels, taverns and even teahouses were still overflowing with customers, even though it was late at night.</p>

<p>Away from the busy main streets, two figures walked in the darkness.</p>

<p>Both were covered in cloaks of spiritual silk, only their faces barely visible in the dim light that met them.</p>

<p>The first had an aged face and a gentle smile.</p>

<p>The second possessed incomparable beauty, her mature, tanned features dotted with subtle, elegant make-up, blood-red lips and beautiful, piercing brown eyes.</p>

<p>But those beautiful eyes were shaky, as were her steps.</p>

<p>“Master…” Hua Feiyan’s lips opened slightly, drawing Madame Xue’s attention.</p>

<p>“What is it, dear?” Madame Xue stopped her steps, her aged eyes scanning her dear disciple’s worried expression.</p>

<p>“Are you sure about this woman?” Feiyan asked, her fingers anxiously intertwined.</p>

<p>“Ah…” Madame Xue exhaled heavily before looking up at the sky, the stars dimmed by light pollution.</p>

<p>“I once had a husband… I was terribly in love with him, to the point where my heart would break just by spending a night away from each other…”</p>

<p>“During the war between the three great sects and the Kingdom of the Golden Sun a hundred and fifty years ago, he disappeared.</p>

<p>For more than twenty years, I searched for him…” Madame Xue’s words died away briefly.</p>

<p>There was pain in them, a pain long suppressed but still there.</p>

<p>“It was this woman who helped me find his body and give him a proper burial.”</p>

<p>Hearing those words, Feiyan shuddered, lowering her face.</p>

<p>What if she found out that her Qingyi was actually dead?</p>

<p>What if all she got was a place where she would find a sun-dried corpse?</p>

<p>It made her breathing uneasy and her heart bleed.</p>

<p>But she controlled herself, calmed her mind and took a step forward.</p>

<p>She had already cried everything she had to cry, nights without being able to sleep and days without being able to eat.</p>

<p>She was tired of it.</p>

<p>She knew that her Qingyi wouldn’t like to see her like this.</p>

<p>“Let’s go.” Madame Xue smiled and they soon arrived at a small, shabby house.</p>

<p>“You must enter alone. A drop of blood is all the payment she needs.”</p>

<p>Hua Feiyan nodded silently before entering the house.</p>

<p>Her steps were a little uncertain.</p>

<p>Why would someone with such peculiar power hide in such an inappropriate place?</p>

<p>But the moment she stepped through the door, she realized that this was no ordinary place.</p>

<p>Her mind was taken over by a calming aura, her eyes lost their worry and her steps stopped shaking.</p>

<p>“Have you lost your beloved, my dear?” A voice called out to her, old and hoarse, carrying a maternal gentleness.</p>

<p>Feiyan immediately felt her heart tremble, her eyes focusing on an old woman sitting casually, her hands resting on an orb on a table.</p>

<p>How did she know that?</p>

<p>“Sit down, honey. I believe you’re in a hurry, aren’t you?”</p>

<p>Feiyan paused for a single moment, but in the end bit her lower lip before stepping forward and sitting down in a chair opposite the old woman.</p>

<p>Without hesitation, she brought her index finger to her teeth, tearing the delicate skin before raising it over the orb. A single drop of blood dripped from the wound.</p>

<p>A bright smile came over the old woman, who grabbed Feiyan’s hands, placing them under the orb and closing her eyes.</p>

<p>“Tell me, darling… who was this man to you?”</p>

<p>“He was my everything.”</p>

<p>“And how far would you go for him?”</p>

<p>“Until my soul is gone.”</p>

<p>“Hm…” The old woman looked surprised. Both answers were the absolute truth.</p>

<p>That kind of customer was rare. Most always had a small lie among their statements.</p>

<p>“And how far do you think he would go for you?” she asked.</p>

<p>“I know that not even the heavens would stop him.”</p>

<p>“You know? And what gives you that confidence?”</p>

<p>“Because he’s my Qingyi.” Feiyan replied, and the old woman’s smile intensified.</p>

<p>Another truth.</p>

<p>“Close your eyes, dear. I can feel your beloved.” The old woman instructed and Feiyan obeyed without question.</p>

<p>The next moment, she felt a strange energy enter her hands.</p>

<p>It wasn’t like the Qi she was used to. It was thin, subtle, almost as if it had no real presence.</p>

<p>And then she felt her obscured vision light up. For a brief moment, she saw it.</p>

<p>Standing among a trail of destruction, a handsome young man with long black hair and piercing eyes.</p>

<p>His sword was drawn, but there was no enemy around. He was just casually practicing.</p>

<p>It all lasted only a single second before Feiyan’s vision was drawn back to her body.</p>

<p>Her heart almost burst out of her chest and tears ran down her cheeks.</p>

<p>Her Qingyi was alive, his body without a single injury, to the point where he could even train in a relaxed manner.</p>

<p>She had never felt so relieved in her entire life, as if she had been carrying a cargo ship on her chest and that ship had suddenly become a small toy boat.</p>

<p>“Thank you very much-” Feiyan suddenly stood up, ready to say thank you, but as the words left her lips, she realized that she was already outside.</p>

<p>The house had disappeared, replaced by an ordinary commercial establishment.</p>

<p>Her watery eyes scanned everything around her, soon meeting those of her master.</p>

<p>“Good news?” Madame Xue laughed, realizing that the tears in her disciple’s eyes were not tears of sorrow.</p>

<p>They were tears of happiness.</p>

<p>“Thank you, master! I promise to train hard and reach the golden core realm before the alchemy tournament!” Feiyan threw herself into Madame Xue’s arms, hugging her tightly.</p>

<p>Her Qingyi was alive and well.</p>

<p>She was sure of that now. Even if they couldn’t meet right now, she was still happy.</p>

<p>She knew he would be back soon.</p>
