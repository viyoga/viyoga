<p>Months passed in the blink of an eye, and the mood among the sect’s outer disciples was festive.</p>

<p>Even some core disciples had descended from their pavilions on the mountain to watch the inner disciples selection.</p>

<p>The outer disciple arena slowly filled up as the elders made preparations, adjusting the spirit arrays and ensuring that nothing would go wrong in the duels that would begin in a few hours.</p>

<p>Qingyi was oblivious to all of this.</p>

<p>His eyes were tightly closed as he circulated his Qi, already ready for the Foundation Establishment realm.</p>

<p>Unlike breakthroughs within the same realm, breakthroughs between realms were much more difficult.</p>

<p>The Foundation Realm itself was one of the most challenging bottlenecks for a cultivator to break through.</p>

<p>A young talent could reach the ninth stage of the Qi Refinement Realm before the age of eighteen and be considered a great genius, but even then, they could easily spend their entire life just trying to break through to the Foundation Realm.</p>

<p>That was the challenge of advancing to the Foundation Realm, and it was only the beginning.</p>

<p>In this realm, cultivators who usually had their cultivation spread throughout their Dantian would gather all of their energy into a small core, while every muscle and meridian in their body would be rebuilt to withstand this denser and more powerful energy.</p>

<p>With a sigh, Qingyi opened his eyes and calmed his breathing before clicking to purchase a realm breakthrough, his last five hundred lust points disappearing.</p>

<p>[Starting breakthrough to the foundation realm.]</p>

<p>The moment after the system notification, Qingyi felt his body tremble as he sat cross-legged, pushing all of his Qi into his dantian.</p>

<p>First, all of the lightning sword Qi gathered together, forming a core slightly larger than a grain of rice in the center of the Dantian.</p>

<p>Despite its size, it was enough to make the black walls of the cultivation room tremble. The Qi became violent and Qingyi’s robes fluttered with the pulsing energy.</p>

<p>When this core calmed down, the two strands of dragon Qi in Qingyi’s Dantian finally moved. First, they gently circled the darkened core before being slowly broken apart.</p>

<p>The dragon strands split into smaller filaments, which were then fragmented and consumed by the core, which grew by a single millimeter before stabilizing again.</p>

<p>Qingyi gritted his teeth as he felt as if his Dantian had been crushed.</p>

<p>His eyes trembled, and the moment he felt his Dantian calm down, the condensed energy in his Qi core exploded.</p>

<p>His body began to levitate, the Qi in the room nearly depleted as it flowed toward him like a relentless torrent.</p>

<p>His skin turned red, electric rays dancing around him, every impurity being expelled from within him as a wave of steam left his lips and nose.</p>

<p>In the next moment, everything stopped.</p>

<p>A dry explosion echoed and the walls of the cultivation room cracked, with parts of the ceiling collapsing. Sunlight hit his face, hurting his eyes accustomed to darkness.</p>

<p>“Damn…” Qingyi grunted as he stood up. His body was much lighter than before, the Qi core in his Dantian pulsing slightly.</p>

<p>When he tried to circulate his Qi, his eyes flashed.</p>

<p>Before, it was as if he was using a slightly closed faucet to circulate, always needing to force the Qi through. But now, it was as if he had opened the valve completely.</p>

<p>His Qi flowed like a calm and gentle sea, and even when he forced his meridians, there were only a few ripples.</p>

<p>Qingyi then looked at the hole in the cultivation room, a hint of worry appearing on his face.</p>

<p>Fortunately, in the next moment, the broken pieces began to float and slowly merge back into the original structure.</p>

<p>Exhaling heavily, he stepped outside his residence. There was little time left before the selection of inner disciples began, but first, he wanted to test his new power.</p>

<p>He approached a tree, closed his eyes for a brief moment, and pushed all his Qi toward his right leg.</p>

<p>He kicked.</p>

<p>The air around him shook violently as an explosion of wood fragments flew in all directions, the tree split in two, its trunk falling with a loud thud.</p>

<p>His leg, covered in the Qi of the lightning sword, trembled slightly as he pulled it back, Qingyi couldn’t help but let out a breath of awe at his own power.</p>

<p>He had been able to knock down a tree with a single blow before, but that was by forcing his meridians to their limit and channeling all his Qi into the blade of his sword.</p>

<p>If he had tried to use his bare hands instead, he would have probably drenched the tree in blood before even reaching the middle of the trunk.</p>

<p>He wasn’t entirely sure, but he could easily bet that he could fight against cultivators of the third or even fourth stage of the Foundation Realm.</p>
