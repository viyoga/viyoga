<p>“I’m sorry about the girls, young master Qingyi… they’re a bit… eccentric,” Qin Yueli apologized as she sat next to Qingyi, watching the priestesses train.</p>

<p>Strangely, they seemed even more excited now that Qingyi was staying with them.</p>

<p>“They were taken in by master when they were still very young. Elize and I failed to teach them common sense….” Yueli’s eyes swept over Qingyi’s body.</p>

<p>“It’s okay, they’re cute,” Qingyi replied with a low laugh.</p>

<p>And it wasn’t as if Yueli or Elize were great sources of common sense either, far from it.</p>

<p>The handsome young man kept a gentle smile on his lips, his clothes disheveled by hugs and tugs, even marked with lipstick.</p>

<p>That wasn’t exactly common treatment in a closed and conservative society, but who was he to judge them?</p>

<p>He was blessed with transcendental beauty and possessed a system that made him even more irresistible, especially to lower-level cultivators who were more susceptible to the lust system.</p>

<p>“Cute?” Yueli’s lips twitched as she hugged her own legs, her head resting lazily on her arms.</p>

<p>Her beautiful blue eyes studied Qingyi with interest.</p>

<p>“Ah… I mean, they’re… well…” Qingyi was speechless.</p>

<p>How on earth could he explain himself?</p>

<p>“Hahaha, that’s okay, it’s true, they’re cute…” Yueli laughed, her sweet, soft voice echoing through the temple and briefly catching the girls’ attention.</p>

<p>But her expression soon turned serious and her laughter died down.</p>

<p>“Master always told me to keep you away from the horrors of this world, that one day a hero would come to take the spirit of lightning and lead our temple to the top of the world again.”</p>

<p>“I wonder where this hero is…”</p>

<p>Hearing those words, Qingyi’s face also lost its smile.</p>

<p>A lightning spirit linked to a powerful sword that was once an artifact of the heavenly sky.</p>

<p>Honestly, that was perfect for him.</p>

<p>There were three main types of weapon.</p>

<p>The first were ordinary weapons, made of metals of different strengths and resistances.</p>

<p>Then came spirit swords, such as the Thunderclouds sword.</p>

<p>These were weapons made of spiritual materials that carried unique elements and could increase the power of a specific type of Qi by a wide margin.</p>

<p>And then there was the third type.</p>

<p>These were also spirit swords, but with one simple difference: they had their own egos.</p>

<p>Unlike ordinary and spiritual swords, a cultivator needed to dominate a sword with an ego and be accepted by it.</p>

<p>Failure would often result in death.</p>

<p>’System… what is the cheapest spiritual sword with an ego that I could buy?’ Qingyi asked mentally, his heart sinking at the sight of the prices.</p>

<p>It was simply absurd.</p>

<p>An common grade sword cost thousands of lust points.</p>

<p>“Miss Yueli… where is this sword?” He asked, feeling the handle of the borrowed sword in his hands.</p>

<p>It was a good blade, and he could easily buy one suitable for his level with the amount of lust points he had.</p>

<p>But wanting more isn’t a sin, is it?</p>

<p>“Are you thinking of trying to be accepted by it?”</p>

<p>“Yes,” he nodded.</p>

<p>The blue-haired beauty shuddered at his answer.</p>

<p>“Qingyi…” His lips opened, a soft tremor running through his voice. “My master was killed trying to be accepted, and not just her. Every year, thousands die trying to be accepted by that sword.”</p>

<p>She paused for a brief moment and her eyes focused on the ground, avoiding Qingyi’s intense gaze.</p>

<p>Why did he always have to look at her like that? Her cheeks were overcome by a faint blush.</p>

<p>“You’ve been very good to me and my sisters. They seem to really like having you here, I don’t want you to die like that… the way the master died…”</p>

<p>“Ah…” Qingyi exhaled, drawing his sword as he stood up, the silver steel sliding into the scabbard with a sharp hiss.</p>

<p>“What are you doing?”</p>

<p>“Why don’t we train a bit more? I’ve just had a breakthrough and I wanted to test out some of my new power,” Qingyi interrupted her.</p>

<p>“A breakthrough?” Yueli stood up in surprise.</p>

<p>Qingyi didn’t seem to be close to a breakthrough.</p>

<p>Only now did she realize that no matter how much she focused her senses; she couldn’t sense his cultivation.</p>

<p>She hesitated for a brief moment, but in the end just shook her head, following him to the middle of the courtyard.</p>

<p>She felt a slight discomfort in her chest at Qingyi’s lack of response about trying to be accepted by the lightning spirit.</p>

<p>But she quickly pushed it aside. Thinking too much wouldn’t solve the problem.</p>

<p>Yueli moved her delicate hands, grasping the scabbard before sliding her silvery blade out.</p>

<p>Her gaze swept over her sisters, who quickly stopped their training, surrounding them with curiosity.</p>

<p>“Let’s get out of the courtyard, it’s dangerous,” Elize grabbed the girls’ shoulders, gently pulling them away, her beautiful purple eyes studying Qingyi with concern.</p>

<p>She had heard their conversation at a glance and couldn’t help but feel her chest tremble.</p>

<p>Her master was at the Golden Core realm and still ended up dead.</p>

<p>How could Qingyi stand a chance?</p>

<p>With the entire central courtyard cleared for them, Yueli finally released her aura.</p>

<p>Thunderous lightning streaked across her silver blade, her beautiful blue eyes shining brightly.</p>

<p>“Are you really fully recovered?” she asked.</p>

<p>She had seen Qingyi kill dozens of men in a minute, chopping off the hands of a fourth-stage Foundation Realm cultivator as if he were a simple farmer.</p>

<p>She knew well that there was no point in holding back if he had already fully recovered.</p>

<p>“Yes,” Qingyi nodded, releasing his own Foundation Realm aura. “I’ll give you the first move,” he smiled, not thinking it would take more than that.</p>

<p>Yueli thanked him, her eyes becoming sharp as the ground beneath her feet shook.</p>

<p>In a split second, she cut the distance between them, her sword tearing through the air and scattering sparks of lightning.</p>

<p>Qingyi only raised his sword slightly, parrying Yueli’s.</p>

<p>A bang echoed, and their surroundings shook.</p>

<p>High-pitched screams reached his ears as the girls’ dresses were swept away by the shockwave of the collision.</p>

<p>And then, a sword flew out.</p>

<p>“H-how?” Yueli shuddered, looking down at her delicate, trembling hands.</p>

<p>She could feel that Qingyi had barely used a fraction of his Qi, yet it was as if he had hit an impassable barrier…</p>

<p>Her eyes rose slightly, meeting his.</p>

<p>“Have more faith in me,” he smiled, moving his right hand and stroking her head.</p>

<p>“Uh?” Yueli froze, vibrant red spreading across her cheeks.</p>

<p>“Oh…” Qingyi quickly withdrew his hand.</p>

<p>He forgot that Yueli was a thirty five year old woman.</p>
