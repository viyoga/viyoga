<p>The palace of the leader of the demonic cult was an incomparable place, surrounded by crimson rivers and boiling gas geysers.</p>

<p>Throughout a region of more than a hundred kilometers, demonic beasts from the realm of rebirth patrolled.</p>

<p>The last ten kilometers were patrolled directly by the demon lord’s personal guard, the Crimson Guard.</p>

<p>In a secluded room in the depths of this palace was the clinic of the celestial demon doctor.</p>

<p>He was an old man with a kind face and calm steps, with a long, well-kept gray beard.</p>

<p>“Ugh… that damned Tang patriarch!” The demon cult leader gritted his teeth as he sat on a cold steel platform, his powerful body revealing horrible wounds.</p>

<p>They were like a thousand tiny needles covering his body, forming purple circles where they had been lodged.</p>

<p>“You were hasty, little Mochen, it’s not just because I said that perhaps the Tang patriarch had a solution to the chief strategist’s problem that you should advance on the Tang family without having any self control.”</p>

<p>The demon doctor spoke, placing acupuncture needles around each of the poisonous marks on Mochen’s body.</p>

<p>This was the poison that came from another transcendent; naturally, it was difficult to get rid of it, even for Mochen.</p>

<p>Only the demon doctor was capable of dealing with that kind of injury.</p>

<p>Despite the doctor’s apparently disrespectful words, Mochen didn’t care.</p>

<p>He would kill anyone else who dared to refer to him like that, even his son. But there were two exceptions.</p>

<p>The demon doctor was one of them, while the other…</p>

<p>Mochen looked at the other steel platform next to him, where a man with noble features and a gentle smile was lying, also receiving the demon master’s treatments.</p>

<p>This man was the chief strategist of the demonic cult, a genius of diplomacy and strategy and above all, Mochen’s personal friend.</p>

<p>Unfortunately, his life was running out and, after an assassination attempt, he ended up in that state, between life and death.</p>

<p>“How much longer do you think he’ll last?” Mochen asked, a little worry creeping into his voice.</p>

<p>He had lost his son a month ago, had been betrayed by one of his daughters, but none of that made him worry.</p>

<p>They were disposable things that he could give birth to at any time.</p>

<p>But the chief strategist was different.</p>

<p>“If he doesn’t wake up in a month… I truly doubt I’ll be able to keep him alive any longer.”</p>

<p>“A month…” Mochen raised his aged but dignified face, the black, broken horns on his head shining next to his eyes.</p>

<p>“Fucking orthodox hypocrites… Pray that he wakes up in a month.”</p>

<p>Mochen didn’t know who had ambushed the chief strategist.</p>

<p>It could be some internal faction of his cult, some mysterious external power or the Orthodox alliance itself.</p>

<p>But whoever it was, the world would bleed for his friend’s death.</p>

<p>***</p>

<p>In the Orthodox alliance, things weren’t looking good either.</p>

<p>Any member of the alliance could only leave the territory with special permission.</p>

<p>Anyone who left without this permission and without a valid justification ran the risk of being investigated as a traitor.</p>

<p>Against this scenery, Qingyi cut through the skies of the orthodox alliance, landing in the center of the zone controlled by the snake battalion, entering the administrative center.</p>

<p>“Where’s Tang Biyue?” Qingyi asked the attendant, who immediately recognized him.</p>

<p>“I beg your pardon, young master Qingyi, but I have no information about Lady Tang Biyue. Only the council of elders can help you with that,” the attendant replied respectfully as she bowed.</p>

<p>“Shit…” Qingyi grunted, preparing to leave the building when he heard a male voice, coming from a noble and dignified-looking man, stroking his well-groomed white beard.</p>

<p>“Greetings, young man. Are you going to the council palace? I can accompany you if you like.”</p>

<p>Qingyi watched the man for a brief moment.</p>

<p>He was an outside visitor, at the peak of the rebirth realm, something made clear by the small badge on his chest.</p>

<p>“Zhao Wuhen… Where have I heard that name before?” Qingyi murmured, a gleam of realization taking over his face.</p>

<p>That man was the great elder of the Zhao family, Yulie’s father, whom he had killed some time ago.</p>

<p>“Do you know me?” Qingyi asked.</p>

<p>“Yes, I do.” Wuhen kept a calm smile on his face. “Why don’t we talk on the way? Without being able to fly, it’s a long way to the council.”</p>

<p>Without even waiting for an answer from Qingyi, Wuhen started walking.</p>

<p>The handsome young man thought for a moment, but in the end just followed him.</p>

<p>After a moment of silence, Qingyi said, “I killed your son.”</p>

<p>“Yes, you did.” Wuhen nodded.</p>

<p>“Aren’t you angry?”</p>

<p>“I was angry for a few minutes; losing a son is still losing a son. But he deserved it. I read the report prepared by that explosive little woman, Rongyan.</p>

<p>I couldn’t deal with the shame of having a son who had worked with the demon cult to kill a fellow disciple.”</p>

<p>“That…” Qingyi paused for a moment, not used to receiving that kind of response.</p>

<p>He really expected Wuhen to throw himself on his sword the first chance he got, begging to die.</p>

<p>That was the usual behavior of damned old men and he rarely saw it any different.</p>

<p>“It makes sense,” Qingyi bowed his head in defeat.</p>

<p>Men like Wuhen were dangerous, he knew that well.</p>

<p>“I’m glad you understand. I asked for you at the alliance, they said you were on a mission. You probably don’t know that the guardian has ordered the sect leader’s house arrest, do you?”</p>

<p>Wuhen commented, watching Qingyi’s face fill with shock.</p>

<p>“They say she’s been controlled by an immortal heaven being for over twenty-nine years, originating from the rift that occurred in the heavens a few decades ago. Truly frightening.”</p>

<p>Qingyi just listened in silence.</p>

<p>As a Nascent Soul Realm cultivator, his mind was naturally much faster than any mortal mind, but that speed wasn’t even necessary for him to reach a conclusion.</p>

<p>Elize had arrived in that world when she was 6 years old; she’s 35 now, so that was 29 years ago.</p>

<p>The rift also took place around 29 years ago, just as the sect leader created the secret realm of the divine beast 28 years ago, probably putting Khaedryss inside.</p>

<p>It was hard not to think that they had some connection.</p>
