<p>As the Orthodox Alliance was on the opposite side of the Nine Peaks Basin to the sect, the journey took a long time and only after almost a month of travel did they finally spot it on the horizon.</p>

<p>In the center was a large eastern palace, its main tower rising hundreds of meters high and its walls forming hexagons around the central palace, dividing the Alliance into seven parts that together amounted to dozens of square kilometers.</p>

<p>Those seven parts represented the seven battalions of the Alliance.</p>

<p>The Azure Battalion, the Red Battalion, the Supreme Saber Battalion, the Dead Moon Battalion, the Ascending Sun Battalion, the Snake Battalion and, finally, the Dragon Battalion.</p>

<p>Each of those battalions was led by Rebirth Realm cultivators, divided into squads led by Nascent Soul Realm cultivators.</p>

<p>“Greetings, fellow disciple.” A strong hand fell on the shoulder of Qingyi, who was sitting next to Qingxue on one of the many rows of seats on the sect’s flying ship.</p>

<p>Turning around, Qingyi saw a man with a sharp smile and penetrating gaze, watching him animatedly.</p>

<p>He was a Nascent Soul Realm expert, and a powerful one.</p>

<p>“You’re new here, aren’t you? Have you chosen which battalion you’re going to join? I can help you choose if you like, hehehe.” He spoke and without even asking permission, sat down next to Qingyi.</p>

<p>“Oh… where are my manners, I’m Wei Longhu, a veteran and leader of the Dragon Battalion’s thirty-seventh squad.</p>

<p>I’m not going to force you into anything, but I really recommend that you choose us, you certainly won’t regret it, hehehe.” Longhu laughed.</p>

<p>“Thanks for the advice, senior brother Longhu. I’m Long Qingyi, I’ll take your words into consideration when I choose my battalion.” Qingyi replied, a little uncomfortable with Longhu’s gaze.</p>

<p>That guy looked like a snake and his breath wasn’t good.</p>

<p>One of them was a tall, grumpy man while the other was someone Qingyi knew well – Rongyan.</p>

<p>The red-haired beauty had been on the same ship as them, but as she was considered a public servant of the sect-ruled White Peak City, she ended up having to be transferred to the Orthodox Alliance in a slightly different way to them.</p>

<p>“One hundred and fifty, that’s a good number.” He said, preparing his throat.</p>

<p>His aura became even more intense, the space around him coming close to collapsing before he eased off, allowing everyone there to breathe.</p>

<p>“Everyone must leave this ship immediately and report to the central courtyard of the Orthodox Alliance fortress. There, you will take part in a friendly competition and then you will be able to choose which battalion you wish to join.”</p>

<p>Speaking those words, the man turned and left immediately; Rongyan cast one last glance in Qingyi’s direction before leaving too, her beautiful face subtly blushing.</p>

<p>The handsome young man only sighed as he stood up with Qingxue, walking towards the exit of the flying ship.</p>

<p>In the center of the courtyard was an arena, the stone platform that represented it spreading almost a thousand meters, powerful barriers preventing the fight from moving away from its interior.</p>

<p>Raising his gaze, Qingyi saw the battalion leaders sitting on a luxurious platform.</p>
