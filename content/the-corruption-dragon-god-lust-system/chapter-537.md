<p>Slowly, the white smoke cleared, revealing the female figure surrounded by a sea of burning hell.</p>

<p>She shuddered. She was unharmed; the barrier had prevented any damage from reaching her. But that mattered little to her now.</p>

<p>Her eyes focused on the ground beneath her feet, observing the destruction and death.</p>

<p>A beast, slightly stronger than the others, barely escaped alive from the outer radius of Qingyi’s attack.</p>

<p>The creature crawled, roaring in pain as its legs were turned to ash. It struggled for a brief moment before collapsing.</p>

<p>“What is that?” she asked, with innocent curiosity. Her hands flowed to her own chest. It was heavy, heavier than usual.</p>

<p>“Death.” A clear and cold answer came from behind her, like the growl of a lord of death. “Don’t worry, you’ll be joining it soon.</p>

<p>Before she could react, she was struck again.</p>

<p>The Heaven-Defying Thunder Sword tore through the air with terrifying power, delivering a downward slash against the barrier.</p>

<p>The demonic spawn was thrown towards the ground. The protection covering her body absorbed all the damage, both from the slash and the impact.</p>

<p>Qingyi didn’t even react, just watching the world around him open up under the impact of the demonic spawn’s body.</p>

<p>A cloud of dust and boiling air rose from the ground, everything within a ten-kilometer radius swallowed up by pure destruction. “But… I don’t want to die…”</p>

<p>Qingyi shuddered when he heard that sweet, symphonic voice, his draconic eyes revealing the demonic spawn still intact among the rubble and dust.</p>

<p>In the blink of an eye, before Qingyi realized it, a blade was at his neck.</p>

<p>He turned his face, covering himself with black scales, but nothing stopped the blow, the steel clanging as his scales shattered.</p>

<p>The defense protected him enough to prevent decapitation, but his throat was slashed, forcing him to spit blood.</p>

<p>“But… I need to die to fulfill the master’s wish… right?” She repeated, placing her hand on her chest again.</p>

<p>It was even heavier. Her eyes trembled, as did her body.</p>

<p>[Don’t let your guard down. She is the result of more than twenty thousand years of absorbing the energy of the Tree of Life. She is learning, and fast.]</p>

<p>Ruxue’s voice echoed in Qingyi’s mind, and he nodded coldly.</p>

<p>It was good that he hadn’t allowed Lingxue to stay by his side; he would have been unable to protect her.</p>

<p>“Ah… I should have come earlier,” Qingyi growled, preparing the Heaven-Defying Thunder Sword.</p>

<p>He should have taken Lingxue earlier. He should have advanced to the Astral Body Realm and raised his bloodline and constitution to the maximum level.</p>

<p>Had he forgotten the lesson he learned against Qingxue’s brother in the selection of core disciples of the Burning Dragon Sect?</p>

<p>The only way to safely deal with an enemy was with brutal, superior power.</p>

<p>That demonic seed had grown too strong.</p>

<p>“Ah…” He exhaled, closed his eyes, and advanced.</p>

<p>The demonic spawn raised a hand, strengthening the barrier in front</p>

<p>of her, the steel of the Thunder Sword that defies the heavens</p>

<p>clanging as it struck her, sending her flying away again.</p>

<p>She gritted her teeth. A single tear appeared in her eyes as she pointed her hands at Qingyi. Dozens of swords appeared and exploded at speed toward the young man.</p>

<p>Qingyi did not stand still and immediately summoned the fourth form of the Storm Monarch Sword Art.</p>

<p>Thousands of thunderous blades surrounded his body, flying against the blood blades as lightning took over the sky.</p>

<p>His domain fell upon the world. Seas of flames and magma dominated everything. Lightning struck the ground chaotically, destroying everything it touched.</p>

<p>Qingyi felt his reserves of Qi and mana being swept from his body at a frightening speed. All his power was focused on a single point: the steel of his sword.</p>

<p>He inhaled, closed his eyes, calmed his mind, and advanced.</p>

<p>The world ahead opened up with the cut of his blade. The primordial laws collapsed, and the very foundation of that place was shaken.</p>

<p>Outside, hundreds of kilometers away, the fleeing fox women felt the Tree of Life tremble, an overwhelming power engulfing its roots and the realm it guarded.</p>

<p>“Die.” The young man growled, watching the final expressions on the demonic spawn’s face.</p>

<p>Instinctively, she threw hundreds of blood blades, aiming them all at Qingyi in a last, unconscious attempt to live.</p>

<p>Qingyi felt those blades tear through his flesh. His internal organs collapsed and his body was bathed in blood, but he did not stop.</p>

<p>The sound of breaking glass reached his ears as the barrier finally collapsed, the demonic spawn’s body hit with full force.</p>

<p>“Ugh… damn it!” He vomited blood. A huge sense of relief overwhelmed him as his body lost strength, barely able to stand.</p>

<p>He had truly put everything into that last blow; his Qi and Mana reserves were below 1%.</p>

<p>With a bitter smile, he turned away, ready to call for Lingxue, but stopped when he heard a distant, pained voice.</p>

<p>“I’m cold… so cold…” The demonic spawn whimpered, bringing her hand to her left breast, which had been severed, her body covered in terrible wounds, and her organs scattered on the ground.</p>

<p>“What is this?” She asked, touching her own wounds.</p>

<p>“It’s pain… The same pain you would make those women feel if I let you live,” Qingyi replied, still cold.</p>

<p>“But… I don’t like this… I don’t want to hurt…” Tears streamed from her</p>

<p>eyes without stopping. Her body trembled as she tried to understand</p>

<p>what was happening to her.</p>

<p>“Does it hurt?” She pointed to the wounds covering Qingyi, the Heaven-defying Thunder Blade already under her chest.</p>

<p>“Yes,” Qingyi said, but he didn’t push the sword in, a tremor</p>

<p>overtaking his eyes.</p>

<p>Why was he hesitating? Why was his heart so heavy?</p>

<p>‘End this, damn it!’ He shouted at himself, but he couldn’t.</p>

<p>“I… I’m sorry… I don’t want to hurt…” She grabbed the blade of the</p>

<p>sword.</p>

<p>Her face carried emotions that Qingyi couldn’t decipher.</p>

<p>“I don’t want to die… I don’t want to feel this…” Her eyes turned to the</p>

<p>blade at her chest. Her small, delicate hands squeezed the sharp steel, and blood spilled out endlessly.</p>

<p>“Can you make it stop?” she asked honestly, begging. “Please…”</p>

<p>Qingyi’s hands finally gave way, and the Thunder Sword fell to the ground. He strengthened his resolve, ignoring Ruxue’s words in his mind.</p>

<p>“Tell me… if you weren’t a thing, if you weren’t a bag of energy for your master… what name would you choose for yourself?”</p>
