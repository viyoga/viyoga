<p>“Ruxue, what is a world of mind?” Qingyi asked, unable to hide the faint smile on his lips.</p>

<p>He knew the concept of a sea of mind, unlocked when a cultivator reached the Nascent Soul Realm, but a world of mind was a completely new term for him.</p>

<p>“I’ll explain later, get rid of that damned old woman!” Ruxue replied, her voice slightly hurried.</p>

<p>She still seemed to be having trouble fully synchronizing with her fragment.</p>

<p>Qingyi swung the heaven-defying thunder sword, using the movement to wipe away the disgusting blood that permeated the beautiful, elegant blade.</p>

<p>“N-no… impossible…” The old woman muttered, barely feeling the cold steel pressing against her neck.</p>

<p>Qingyi pressed down, his lightning sword qi flashing as his blade sank into the aged flesh.</p>

<p>“IMPOSSIBLE!” The old woman screamed, her aura of the third stage of the Iron Body Realm exploding from her body.</p>

<p>“Ugh… how does that wretch still have so much power?” Qingyi gritted his teeth as he was forced to take a step back.</p>

<p>He activated the third form of the Storm Monarch’s sword art, a dry explosion echoing as the blade tore through the air.</p>

<p>“THAT WAS A GIFT FROM MY LORD, YOU’LL PAY FOR IT!”</p>

<p>The old woman raised her body, opening her mouth and sinking her rotting teeth into the heaven-defying thunder sword.</p>

<p>Qingyi gritted his teeth and kept pushing, his meridians seeming to scream in agony as he pushed all his Qi towards the blade.</p>

<p>’Shit… it’s still not enough!’ Qingyi opened the system store, ignoring the pain spreading through his body as he pressed on to improve his cultivation.</p>

<p>[Your cultivation has improved! Sixth stage of the Golden Core Realm -&gt; Seventh stage of the Golden Core Realm]</p>

<p>The momentum of this breakthrough was all he needed.</p>

<p>His Qi, which had previously only been on the same level as the old woman’s, finally beat her completely, the difference in cultivation between them reduced to nothing.</p>

<p>“Die!” Qingyi roared, his lightning Qi turning the old woman’s jaw to dust as the sword Qi cut through the back of her head, splitting her face in half.</p>

<p>The moment the top of the old woman’s head touched the ground, her body blasted Qingyi away.</p>

<p>His body was thrown hundreds of meters until it hit a wall on the other side of the lake.</p>

<p>“What the fuck…” The handsome young man shuddered, feeling a few broken ribs and coughing up a gulp of blood.</p>

<p>Raising his gaze, he saw the decapitated body dragging itself along, its sickly old hands sinking into the earth as it tried to reach the lake of blood.</p>

<p>“Don’t let her touch you-” Ruxue’s voice echoed in his head, but it was too late.</p>

<p>The old woman’s sickly body sank into the lake of blood, which immediately shook.</p>

<p>A deep demonic Qi spread as the blood was sucked towards the old woman’s body.</p>

<p>“That bitch wants to use her own body as a vessel for the demon!” Ruxue said.</p>

<p>“How can I stop it?”</p>

<p>“It’s impossible, not with your current cultivation. Don’t go near her and try to escape!” Ruxue warned and Qingyi looked up, preparing his lightning steps.</p>

<p>“You’re not going anywhere.” A voice rang out, making Qingyi’s blood shiver softly.</p>

<p>That wasn’t the aged, crazed voice of the old woman.</p>

<p>It was masculine, soft and composed.</p>

<p>Qingyi’s eyes focused on the now empty pool of blood. A man was standing in the center of it.</p>

<p>His face was beautiful, adorned with two crimson horns, but his body was distorted, covered in pulsating tumors.</p>

<p>“Ugh… these damn mortal heaven bugs can’t even find a decent host. What am I supposed to do with that?”</p>

<p>The man complained, his beautiful red eyes focusing on Qingyi.</p>

<p>“You’re a good host, mortal.” He smiled, his eyes fixed on the heaven-defying thunder sword. “Why don’t you leave that disgusting item behind and give yourself to me? I’ll give you endless pleasures!”</p>

<p>Qingyi couldn’t stop his face from distorting in disgust as he took a step forward, feeling the Qi exhaled by the demon man.</p>

<p>That man was stronger than the old woman, but not strong enough.</p>

<p>With a sigh, he activated the lightning steps, his body exploding in speed as he used the first form of the Stormy Monarch sword art.</p>

<p>“Futile resistance of a useless insect,” the man raised a single finger, sensing Qingyi’s power.</p>

<p>To his surprise, the blade didn’t stop immediately as he had expected.</p>

<p>A painful grunt escaped his lips as the Qi from Qingyi’s lightning sword tore through his hand, sinking into his chest.</p>

<p>“How?” His eyes filled with shock, but Qingyi gave him no time to react.</p>

<p>Taking out the talisman he had received from his master, he took a step backwards as he tore it open.</p>

<p>At that distance, it was impossible to dodge.</p>

<p>A powerful wind and sword Qi spread across the dry lake, turning into hundreds of slashes that exploded towards the man.</p>

<p>“Ugh… you bastard, you’ll pay for this!” The man gritted his teeth, his body melting as his crimson horns turned into red lightning, flying towards the sky.</p>

<p>“Ah… was that a demon? What happened to it?” Qingyi asked, falling to his knees.</p>

<p>“Yes, fortunately that wasn’t a complete form. If they had used my fragment instead of the old woman’s body, the entire kingdom – no, the entire Blue Moon Continent would have been completely wiped off the map in a single breath.”</p>

<p>Qingyi couldn’t help but shudder at those words.</p>

<p>He could hardly imagine what would have happened if that ritual had been a success.</p>

<p>With a weary sigh, he closed his eyes.</p>

<p>Ruhu was the first of the four to wake up, stumbling towards the banks of the now dry lake.</p>

<p>His eyes fluttered as he saw Qingyi’s kneeling figure.</p>

<p>“That bastard managed to kill that monster all by himself? How is that possible?” Ruhu was unable to stop his heart from sinking gently.</p>

<p>How could Qingyi be so strong?</p>

<p>He felt ashamed, but that shame soon turned to hatred.</p>

<p>He gripped his sword tightly. This was his chance.</p>

<p>He approached, aligned the blade with Qingyi’s neck and swung.</p>

<p>A metallic clang echoed as the sword bounced off Qingyi’s skin, covered in black scales.</p>

<p>“You’re pretty bold, aren’t you?” Qingyi stood up.</p>

<p>“M-monster!” Ruhu fell on his butt, hastily trying to retreat.</p>

<p>“Know your place, worm.” Qingyi pressed his foot against Ruhu’s head, only stopping when he felt the loud crack of bones breaking and brains being crushed.</p>
