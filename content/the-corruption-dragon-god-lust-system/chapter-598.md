<p>Qingyi looked at the orb in his hand.</p>

<p>In an instant, it shifted from a point of light into characters that formed words.</p>

<p>[Celestial Fairy Pavilion.]</p>

<p>“What is this?” He turned to Chen Wei, who appeared to have received the same scriptures.</p>

<p>“Hm… it is one of the nine pavilions of the Cosmic Dawn Sect. The pavilion masters are, like the sect leader, experts of the Stellar Sovereign realm, though much weaker than him.” Chen Wei replied.</p>

<p>His eyes were shining brighter than ever.</p>

<p>Turning his face slightly, Chen Wei saw that the young master Luo had also been assigned to the Celestial Fairy Pavilion, which immediately brought him disappointment.</p>

<p>But he did not show his dissatisfaction, simply accepting that perhaps that was fate.</p>

<p>The Celestial Fairy Pavilion was currently the most powerful among the nine pavilions, second only to the central palace, led by the sect leader.</p>

<p>With great geniuses like Guo Tianhua among its members, it was everyone’s dream to join the Celestial Fairy Pavilion.</p>

<p>Unfortunately, besides being the strongest, it was also the one that accepted the fewest talents, generally taking only five percent of the new recruits and leaving the other ninety-five percent for the other eight pavilions.</p>

<p>“What’s so special about this Celestial Fairy Pavilion?” Qingyi asked, watching everyone who received orbs with the same inscription celebrating, while the others seethed with envy.</p>

<p>“Oh… it’s simple, hehehe.” Chen Wei leaned toward Qingyi, lowering his voice. “The mistress of the Celestial Fairy Pavilion is an incomparable, untouched beauty who has kept her purity intact for over a hundred thousand years.</p>

<p>Now, strangely, she seems to be looking to adopt someone to be her heir.</p>

<p>Having the mistress of the Celestial Fairy Pavilion as your mommy… isn’t that a dream?”</p>

<p>Qingyi took a step back, afraid Chen Wei would drool on his robes.</p>

<p>Noticing the expression on his own face, Young Master Chen quickly pulled himself together, clearing his throat.</p>

<p>“Don’t worry, Senior Brother Qingyi, we still have many months before she chooses the man or woman who will become her son or daughter… As long as you train hard, I’m sure you’ll have a great chance!”</p>

<p>Qingyi didn’t reply, merely flashing a gentle smile.</p>

<p>Well… being adopted by a Stellar Sovereign wasn’t a bad thing.</p>

<p>Even if she was weaker than the sect leader, she was still at the pinnacle of power in that region and brought with her many opportunities.</p>

<p>’Hm… I wonder how many lust points a woman like that would earn me…’ Qingyi scratched his chin, deep in thought.</p>

<p>He had to agree with Chen Wei and start preparing.</p>

<p>“So, Senior Brother Qingyi, what do you say? Are you going to try your luck?” Chen Wei asked, his eyes full of hope.</p>

<p>“What do you think?” Qingyi turned, his lips curving into a huge smile.</p>

<p>“Hehehe… I knew I could count on you!” Chen Wei puffed out his chest with pride.</p>

<p>They had to wait a few more minutes until they were divided into nine groups by the elders.</p>

<p>Qingyi’s group, now much larger and still escorted by Elder Shen, left the courtyard through the east gate.</p>

<p>“Did you break through to the Astral Blood Realm during the journey?” Elder Shen asked, glancing at Qingyi out of the corner of his eye.</p>

<p>“Yes, Elder. I received a small enlightenment and managed to break through.” Qingyi replied curtly, drawing a satisfied smile from the elder.</p>

<p>“Very good, I can sense that your foundation is strong and stable. I’m sure that if you continue like this, it won’t be long before you reach the Celestial Ascension Realm. Just don’t rush to accept missions.”</p>

<p>“Thank you, Elder. But why shouldn’t I accept missions?”</p>

<p>“You’ll understand soon enough, but generally speaking, disciples who accept missions immediately after reaching the Astral Blood Realm don’t come back alive. Be patient, cultivate your power, and only when you’re ready should you venture out on these missions.”</p>

<p>Qingyi didn’t reply, remaining silent.</p>

<p>Strangely, they had only been walking for a few seconds, but it felt as though they had already traveled dozens of kilometers.</p>

<p>’Probably some kind of spatial distortion formation…’ Qingyi thought, observing the arches that surrounded the jade road they were following.</p>

<p>“Ah…” Elder Shen sighed.</p>

<p>He truly hoped that Qingyi would take his advice and not simply throw himself to certain death.</p>

<p>“In any case, you will be introduced to the master of the Celestial Fairy Pavilion. After that, you will begin your lives as outer disciples of the Cosmic Dawn Sect. Do not disappoint me.” The elder turned his face toward the hundreds of disciples following him.</p>

<p>“We won’t let the elder down!” The disciples replied in unison, drawing a hearty laugh from him.</p>

<p>“Very well, that’s the spirit. We’ve arrived at our destination.”</p>

<p>The elder stopped, his eyes rising to a massive mountain east of the patriarch’s palace.</p>

<p>It was a mountain about five kilometers high and covering an area of over two hundred square kilometers, surrounded by streams, woods, and a great wall, with thousands of pavilions of varying sizes scattered across its entire expanse.</p>

<p>Outside the wall, a large civilian city spread out, home to another two million civilians, in addition to the more than eighty thousand disciples of the Celestial Fairy Pavilion.</p>

<p>“Stay with me. You’ll be free to roam the city after the pavilion master takes a look at you. It’s just a formality required by the sect leader, so don’t worry.”</p>

<p>Elder Shen’s firm voice echoed as they entered the city, drawing curious glances from the entire population.</p>

<p>With every step toward the Celestial Fairy Pavilion, the density of Qi increased, and they could sense more terrifying auras surrounding them and watching them at all times.</p>

<p>They were likely studying the auras of each disciple, searching for possible spies from the demonic faction.</p>

<p>When they reached the gates of the wall surrounding the mountain, everyone stopped, their eyes looking up.</p>

<p>At the top of the wall, reclining on a celestial silk seat, was a figure.</p>

<p>The moment she revealed herself and her beautiful eyes fell upon the newly recruited disciples, Qingyi used the system to check her status.</p>
