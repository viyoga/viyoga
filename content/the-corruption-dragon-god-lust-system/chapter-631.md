<p>“I… honestly don’t know,” Auranys replied, her fingers sliding across Qingyi’s muscular abdomen while her enormous pale tits pressed against his right ribs in a tight, warm embrace.</p>

<p>It was funny.</p>

<p>She was once known as one of the most intelligent deities, a representative of justice and light, with a mind that rivaled even that of the goddess of strategy.</p>

<p>Now, she barely knew what to do.</p>

<p>“Don’t you have any ambition to regain your powers?” Qingyi asked, looking at her.</p>

<p>“I do, but… I’m fine like this. I want to relax a little longer with Celestia and Isabel, learn to cook better, and, well…” The goddess’s lips trembled, unable to find the right words. “To be by your side for more</p>

<p>time…”</p>

<p>“I think mortal life isn’t so bad after all.”</p>

<p>Qingyi didn’t press her.</p>

<p>To him, the best path she could take was the one that made her happiest.</p>

<p>If she wanted help regaining her powers, great.</p>

<p>If she didn’t, that was great too.</p>

<p>As long as she was happy, he truly didn’t care.</p>

<p>With a soft sigh, he turned his gaze to Khaedryss, whose growth had been considerable with the strengthening of the beasts in the hunting grounds around the temple.</p>

<p>They, too, had been greatly blessed by his arrival in the celestial heavens.</p>

<p>Khaedryss only needed to kill and devour to grow stronger and improve her talent, so Qingyi rarely had to invest much in her.</p>

<p>Kissing the two beauties and bidding them a brief farewell, Qingyi left the mind world.</p>

<p>He wanted to stay a little longer and make the most of their company, but outside, he could feel a commotion brewing.</p>

<p>What the hell was going on?</p>

<p>With quick steps, he stood up and gripped the Heaven-Defying Thunder Sword before heading to the bedroom door, where Chen Wei was pounding urgently.</p>

<p>“Senior Brother Qingyi! Senior Brother Qingyi!” He repeated several times, the urgency evident in his voice. “Open the door, it’s important!”</p>

<p>Letting out a weary sigh, Qingyi opened the door, revealing a breathless and desperate Chen Wei.</p>

<p>His eyes were teary, and he was visibly injured, with scratches on his face and ragged breathing.</p>

<p>“Calm down, brother. Tell me what happened.” Qingyi grabbed Chen Wei’s shoulders and gave him a firm slap to bring him back to the real world.</p>

<p>His expression hardened as his heart beat slightly faster.</p>

<p>What on earth could have happened to leave Chen Wei in such a</p>

<p>state?</p>

<p>“Guo Tianhao’s men ambushed us while we were returning from class with Old Cang! They captured Peng Tao and said that if you don’t show up in the Blue Herb Valley by midnight tomorrow, they’ll start dismembering him!”</p>

<p>Hearing that, Qingyi’s eyes grew cold.</p>

<p>Peng Tao was Chen Wei’s servant. Honestly, Qingyi didn’t have much affection for him.</p>

<p>He was just a servant who loyally accompanied his master, an average man with no great ambitions.</p>

<p>That was all that could be said about him.</p>

<p>Even so, the fact remained that Peng Tao had been kidnapped because of Qinyi, and they likely chose him instead of Chen Wei precisely because of his low status.</p>

<p>The Cosmic Dawn Sect was, after all, a sect that valued justice, yet was still ruled by power.</p>

<p>The powerful did as they pleased, and the weak simply accepted it.</p>

<p>“I’ll handle this. Cultivate and don’t let a demon of the heart take root within you.” Qingyi spoke firmly before dismissing Chen Wei and returning to his room.</p>

<p>On his handsome face, there was a pensive and slightly weary</p>

<p>expression.</p>

<p>Guo Tianhao had likely met with Rou’er again, and apparently, it</p>

<p>hadn’t been a happy encounter for him.</p>

<p>“Ah… that girl. She isn’t even my wife yet and she’s already causing me nothing but trouble.” Qingyi laughed heartily, shaking his head.</p>

<p>He wasn’t too worried.</p>

<p>He knew Tianhao wouldn’t consider him a threat significant enough to send someone truly powerful after him, and he was far too</p>

<p>arrogant to act personally.</p>

<p>That gave him a comfortable margin of safety.</p>

<p>Even the choice of location puzzled him.</p>

<p>The Blue Herb Valley lay about five hundred kilometers north of the</p>

<p>sect, named for a type of bluish spiritual herb that grew throughout</p>

<p>the valley, widely used in healing pills.</p>

<p>It was a region prized by alchemist disciples and practically</p>

<p>completely ignored by warriors.</p>

<p>With a sigh, Qingyi opened his status screen and clicked to improve</p>

<p>his cultivation twice.</p>

<p>[Initiating cultivation upgrade…]</p>

<p>[Initiating cultivation upgrade…]</p>

<p>A gentle warmth spread through his body, coursing through every meridian like a living current, and when it ended, a massive surge of power erupted from his core, spreading through the room in a shockwave that made the walls vibrate subtly.</p>

<p>[Congratulations, you have advanced in your cultivation! Second stage of the Astral Blood Realm -&gt; third stage of the Astral Blood Realm.]</p>

<p>[Congratulations, you have advanced in your cultivation! Third stage of the Astral Blood Realm -&gt; fourth stage of the Astral Blood Realm.]</p>

<p>Qingyi loved advancing within the same realm.</p>

<p>There was none of the hellish pain of having his body rebuilt from one realm to another, only the pure pleasure of feeling his cultivation grow and his power expand. It was honey without the sting of the</p>

<p>bee.</p>

<p>Stretching contentedly, he focused his gaze.</p>

<p>“What do you think, Ruxue?” he asked with a calm sigh.</p>

<p>“About what? Those pigs trying to kill you?” Ruxue replied, her tone</p>

<p>clearly anxious. “Why overthink it? Just crush them and don’t die.”</p>

<p>“Hahaha, I’m not going to die.” Qingyi laughed.</p>

<p>Despite his confidence, he still used the last few Lust points he had to acquire some offensive and defensive artifacts.</p>

<p>A cautious man died of old age, after all. If shit hit the fan, he wanted</p>

<p>to be ready.</p>
