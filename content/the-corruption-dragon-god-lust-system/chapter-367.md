<p>The young lion took to the air, flying just a few meters off the ground. His arms opened wide as his body changed, taken over by bestial features.</p>

<p>A dense fur covered his skin, his muscles expanded and his claws and fangs grew.</p>

<p>His axe was seized by an increasingly powerful flaming Qi, every drop of his being being poured into that single attack.</p>

<p>“Do you want to finish this with just one strike?” The young lion asked, the air around him boiling with power.</p>

<p>“Hm… yes.” Qingyi smiled in response, turning his face briefly, his beautiful purple eyes meeting Qianyao’s golden ones.</p>

<p>The beauty was totally focused on his sword, her already bright golden eyes becoming even more intense.</p>

<p>After a brief moment, Qingyi focused on his opponent again, finished preparing his attack and then advanced.</p>

<p>“Don’t dodge, young dragon!” The lion roared, raising his axe over his head and dropping it towards Qingyi.</p>

<p>A lion of fire followed his movement, its body stretching like a serpent. Its silent roar silenced all onlookers.</p>

<p>The power was terrifying, but Qingyi didn’t even flinch, confident in his own attack. His sword cut through the air in a downward swing.</p>

<p>When the blades met, a dry crack took over the arena, followed by a cloud of dust and a powerful shockwave. The metallic clang echoed in everyone’s ears.</p>

<p>The spectators without high cultivation or cultivation techniques leaned in to get a better look, while the faces of the strongest were filled with shock.</p>

<p>“I-impossible…” The young lion’s hoarse, powerful voice echoed as he fell to his knees, looking at the blade of his axe.</p>

<p>The blade had been cut perfectly in two. Not the wood handle, the blade.</p>

<p>The metal at the site of the cut was molten, taking on a vibrant, luminous red. The drops of blood that left the young lion’s open chest fell onto them and immediately began to boil.</p>

<p>“Truly worthy of the… slayer of immortals, hahaha…” A last muffled laugh echoed from the young lion’s lips before he leaned on the handle of his weapon and simply declared defeat.</p>

<p>He could keep fighting, but honestly, it wasn’t worth it.</p>

<p>As well as cutting his axe, Qingyi’s sword had also opened a deep cut in his chest and damaged some important meridians; that needed to be dealt with as soon as possible.</p>

<p>Sheathing the heaven-defying thunder sword, Qingyi just watched as the young lion was led away.</p>

<p>A sigh of defeat escaped his lips.</p>

<p>Had the nickname ‘slayer of immortals’ really come all that way?</p>

<p>“Ah… it could be worse…” Qingyi sighed and, with a smooth movement, jumped back to where the other competitors were waiting, landing next to Qianyao.</p>

<p>“A very interesting technique, what’s its name, young master ‘slayer of immortals’? Fufufu~~” The golden-eyed beauty gave a short, low and elegant laugh, covering her mouth with her small, delicate hand.</p>

<p>She could feel Long Jinrui’s eyes piercing her back, all his murderous intent falling on Qingyi, but she just ignored him.</p>

<p>For both of them, treating him like an insignificant cockroach was far better than actively responding to him.</p>

<p>Qingyi returned Qianyao’s laugh before replying: “I don’t know yet, just something new I’ve just tried out, I don’t even think it can be considered a technique, hahaha.”</p>

<p>He scratched the back of his head, his mind wandering elsewhere as his eyes passed Qianyao’s cleavage, admiring her huge, beautiful, heavy pale tits.</p>

<p>He didn’t even notice her blush as she hugged her own chest, her fingers sinking into the voluptuous flesh as she looked away.</p>

<p>No matter how long she stayed by Qingyi’s side, she simply couldn’t get used to it.</p>

<p>It was so… strange.</p>

<p>‘I think I can take some time to refine this and maybe create my first technique…’, Qingyi thought, finally taking his eyes off Qianyao’s breasts, watching the next battle with disinterest.</p>

<p>The result had been much better than he had expected.</p>

<p>If he took a few days to enter his sea of mind and cultivate that skill without stopping, perhaps he could increase his potency even more.</p>

<p>With Ruxue’s help, he was sure that he would be able to create a technique of his own that was no less powerful than his strongest ones today, one that was totally focused on cutting through metal and destroying his enemy’s weapons.</p>

<p>‘Hm… I like this idea…’, Qingyi thought.</p>

<p>Unfortunately, he didn’t have much time to organize everything and soon it was Long Jinrui’s turn to fight.</p>

<p>Finally, the young man from the dragon people managed to unleash some of his anger against his poor opponent, leaving him on the brink of death.</p>

<p>If Elder Yu hadn’t interrupted, the young white dragon would certainly have killed his opponent.</p>

<p>Soon after his battle, another ten fights followed until it was Qianyao’s turn, who again, with a single blow, defeated her opponent.</p>

<p>She swung her huge sword with an elegance and power that simply didn’t seem to suit her.</p>

<p>The same went for all her opponents after that, as Qingyi destroyed blades one after the other, forcing his enemies to surrender.</p>

<p>Some retreated at the mere presence of the black dragon.</p>

<p>Jinrui, on the other hand, continued to beat his opponents almost to death, brutally spanking them.</p>

<p>He even chose to be the one to take on more than one battle in the same round to help adjust the numbers of players.</p>

<p>That tournament didn’t have perfect numbers because its creator, many hundreds of thousands of years ago, made a mistake when defining the terms of the tournament that would decide those who could receive the blessing of the rare fox people.</p>

<p>Since no one dared to change this ancestor’s words, they had to adapt to those rules.</p>

<p>Of course, now it didn’t matter anymore.</p>

<p>The numbers of competitors had been drastically reduced after dozens of fights and soon, Qingyi’s name was called.</p>

<p>His challenger was already standing in the arena, which was slowly regenerating from the previous fight.</p>

<p>That would be the last fight before he could finally face Jinrui.</p>
