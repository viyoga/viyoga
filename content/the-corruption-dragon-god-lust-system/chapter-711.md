<p>Qingyi lost track of time in that place.</p>

<p>He had already explored everything—more than ten million square kilometers—every part of the once-lush forest now reduced to ashes.</p>

<p>Volcanoes erupted nonstop, painting the blue sky with thick clouds of smoke, and a vibrant, hellish red was already devouring the blackness of the horizon.</p>

<p>At his feet, nothing remained but dragon corpses. Thousands. Tens of thousands.</p>

<p>He himself was no better off. One of his wings had been torn from his shoulder, as had his right eye, a deep gash made by sharp claws tearing his face in half.</p>

<p>Every inch of his body bore terrible marks from claws and bites, the vibrant red blood dripping between his black scales and forming pools on the scorched ground.</p>

<p>The pain was hellish, but he didn’t mind it. Quite the contrary.</p>

<p>[Integration of original form “Incomplete Primordial Dragon God” successfully completed.</p>

<p>You can assume your original form in the same way you activate the Draconization ability.</p>

<p>You have lost the “Draconization” ability.]</p>

<p>[Your lineage has been upgraded! Eternal Rank -&gt; Divine Rank]</p>

<p>[Your constitution has been upgraded! Eternal Rank -&gt; Divine Rank]</p>

<p>A huge, inhuman grin spread across his beastly face as his vision darkened at the edges.</p>

<p>When he came to, he was already back in his humanoid form.</p>

<p>That draconic form wasn’t his true final form. No.</p>

<p>That was the form of a beast, not that of a god.</p>

<p>But he was excited.</p>

<p>Just a few more steps and he would become a true primordial dragon god, with a genuinely divine body.</p>

<p>What kind of cultivator wouldn’t feel their blood boil at such a possibility?</p>

<p>Without hesitation, Qingyi also enhanced his spiritual roots, spending another two thousand lust points in the process.</p>

<p>Then, with an excited smile on his lips, he summoned his own status screen.</p>

<p>[Name: Long Qingyi</p>

<p>Age: 23</p>

<p>Cultivation: First Stage of the Celestial Origin Realm (1,000 G Lust Points to upgrade)</p>

<p>Mana Manipulation: Fifteenth Ring (2,500 G Lust Points to upgrade)</p>

<p>Affinities: Lightning: 9,500 – Sword: 9,500 – Fire: 9,500 – Ice: 9,500 – Space: 9,500 – Light: 9,500 – Shadow: 9,500 – Wind: 9,500 – Metal: 9,500 – Water: 9,500 – Earth: 9,500 – Charm: 12,000 (can be upgraded in the Lust Store)</p>

<p>Spiritual Roots: Divine-level Primordial Creation Seed Spirit Root (Lightning, Sword, Fire, Ice, Wind, Metal, Water, Earth, Space, Light, Shadow) (10,000 G Lust Points to upgrade).</p>

<p>Spiritual Roots²: None / Divine-level Primordial Creation Seed Mana Heart (10,000 G Lust Points to upgrade).</p>

<p>Lineage: Blood of the Corruption Dragon God (Divine grade, 50,000 G Lust Points to upgrade.)</p>

<p>Constitution: Primordial Dragon Body (Divine grade, 50,000 G Lust Points to upgrade.)</p>

<p>G Lust Points: 11,145]</p>

<p>“Huh? My charm has doubled?”</p>

<p>Qingyi’s eyes narrowed, and he looked down at his own body, only then realizing he was drenched in a black, viscous slime, all the impurities expelled through his pores.</p>

<p>He shuddered slightly, casting a mana spell to cleanse his body before pulling a mirror from his spatial ring.</p>

<p>There was little left to improve on his handsome, masculine face, but even so, upon observing himself after the bodily reconstruction, he looked like a completely different being.</p>

<p>And it wasn’t just his face.</p>

<p>There was a divine aura around him, strange and unfathomable, that reminded him of the one surrounding Auranys.</p>

<p>[Even incomplete, the divinity of your body is beginning to manifest.]</p>

<p>Upon hearing that, Qingyi nodded with satisfaction.</p>

<p>He was happy with the change, as well as eager to test the power of his original form in a real battle.</p>

<p>Maybe he should take one of those bandit-extermination missions from the sect and handle the whole job by himself?</p>

<p>The image of a mighty black dragon descending upon a bandit stronghold, bringing death to thousands of them with every fiery breath, formed in his mind.</p>

<p>It was fucking badass, he had to admit.</p>

<p>Without delay, he shed his ruined clothes, putting on a fresh set before finally leaving the cultivation room.</p>

<p>To his surprise, both Rouxi and Rou’er were already waiting for him on the other side of the door.</p>

<p>The beauty with pink hair and heterochromatic eyes planted her hands on her hips the moment he appeared, her cheeks puffing out adorably.</p>

<p>“Big Bro Qingyi, do you know how long we’ve been waiting for you? We’re already getting ready to land at the Myriad Beasts Sect!”</p>

<p>The irritated little voice was laced with adorable anger, warming Qingyi’s heart immediately.</p>

<p>Not caring that Rouxi was watching them, he pulled Rou’er into his arms and kissed her.</p>

<p>“I’m sorry, love. I was in the middle of an important breakthrough.”</p>

<p>“A-all right.”</p>

<p>Rou’er blushed at the sudden kiss, her anger melting away in an instant.</p>

<p>“I-I’ll let it slide this time.”</p>

<p>She puffed out her chest proudly, crossing her arms, the gesture drawing a chuckle from Qingyi.</p>

<p>Soon, his eyes fell on Rouxi, who was also blushing, a slight tremor running through her body.</p>

<p>“Greetings, adoptive mother… I beg your forgiveness for taking so long.”</p>

<p>“Don’t worry, sweetheart.”</p>

<p>Rouxi’s lips curved into a gentle smile.</p>

<p>“How has your cultivation been? You don’t seem to have advanced, but your aura is much more powerful.”</p>

<p>“Oh… yes, I’ve been working on refining my foundation a bit,” Qingyi replied, scratching the back of his neck.</p>

<p>Rouxi’s eyes narrowed at those words.</p>

<p>How could a simple refinement of the foundation provoke such a violent burst of power?</p>

<p>She didn’t dwell on the thought.</p>

<p>Qingyi was a young man full of surprises, and, to her, what really mattered was the fact that he was well.</p>

<p>“Let’s go. The sect leader is waiting for us.”</p>

<p>She said, turning her back on the young couple and walking ahead.</p>

<p>Qingyi followed her eagerly, a slight tinge of anxiety pulsing in his chest.</p>

<p>Soon, the spaceship began its descent, a long creaking sound echoing as the hatches opened to reveal the thousands of cultivators inside.</p>

<p>The moment she saw the sect of the myriad beasts before him, Qingyi’s eyes lit up.</p>

<p>Finally, it was time to meet Bai Hu Lie’s sister!</p>
