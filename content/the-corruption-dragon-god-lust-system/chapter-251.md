<p>“So… what did the husband want?” Feiyan asked as she melted into Qingyi’s embrace.</p>

<p>Their quickie had already turned into more than ten rounds before she finally felt even remotely satisfied.</p>

<p>“Nothing much, just that you make some pills for me, but for that, you’ll need something.”</p>

<p>Qingyi smiled as he opened the system store, looking for the mortal-grade furnaces that Feiyan would like.</p>

<p>After a moment of searching, he finally found something, spending 1 million lust points to buy it.</p>

<p>The tanned beauty suddenly stepped away from Qingyi, her eyes shining as an object appeared in front of her.</p>

<p>It was an alchemy furnace easily three meters high.</p>

<p>Its main body was silver, trimmed with pure gold rings and jade pillars at each of its corners, so rich and luxurious that it would make even the furnace that Feiyan got from the royal family of the Golden Sun Kingdom look like complete garbage.</p>

<p>“I-is this for me?” She asked, looking at Qingyi.</p>

<p>“Yes.” Qingyi nodded, delighting in the excited sounds that left Feiyan’s lips as she studied every piece of that furnace.</p>

<p>It was good to see her happy.</p>

<p>With a nod, the four scrolls he had bought earlier appeared in his hands, taking Feiyan’s attention again.</p>

<p>Qingyi spent another few hundred thousand points so that she would have all the materials she needed and then kissed her one last time before returning to his cultivation site.</p>

<p>Feiyan would make those pills for Qingyi first and after that, he would buy 4 other recipes for Wind, Earth, Poison and Blood, ensuring that neither of his wives would be short of anything.</p>

<p>For now, he had another goal: to change the lightning steps.</p>

<p>It was a powerful technique, but only up to the Golden Core realm, and from the Iron Body realm onwards, there were already experts with stronger movement techniques than his.</p>

<p>At the rebirth realm, he had to deal with enemies whose blows broke the sound barrier, his own movements not much slower than that.</p>

<p>Lightning steps simply could no longer keep up with such power efficiently.</p>

<p>After thinking for a moment, Qingyi decided that at least for the time being, he would stick to a lightning Qi movement technique.</p>

<p>Firstly because it was the strongest spiritual root he had and the one he mastered best, and secondly, because lightning was simply fast.</p>

<p>Fortunately, Qingyi didn’t have to look for long before he finally found something he liked.</p>

<p>[Movement Technique: Lightning Blink</p>

<p>Description: A lightning movement technique, so fast that a single blink is all you need to lose sight of it.</p>

<p>Grade: Mortal</p>

<p>Price: 900,000]</p>

<p>After thinking for a moment, Qingyi bought it.</p>

<p>It was expensive, even for a mortal grade technique, but he thought little of the price.</p>

<p>His eyes fluttered as a flood of information flooded into his mind, accompanied by horrible pain.</p>

<p>It was nice to skip the weeks or months it would take to fully read a scroll with such complex techniques, but it was still uncomfortable.</p>

<p>When his vision calmed down and his mind became lighter, Qingyi stared into nothingness, still trying to understand that technique.</p>

<p>It took him a while to organize all the information he needed, but once he did, he realized: it was much more powerful than he had thought.</p>

<p>Qingyi closed his eyes, feeling the lightning Qi in his dantian and circulating it throughout his body.</p>

<p>When his eyes opened, he activated the ability and the world around him became a blur, everything distorting as the air hit his face at absurd speeds.</p>

<p>For Qingyi it was instantaneous, appearing 100 meters ahead of where he had left.</p>

<p>But any observer would see something different: the beautiful young man’s body changing, turning into lightning and tearing through the air at an absurd speed.</p>

<p>Qingyi then tried again, this time with his draconic eyes activated.</p>

<p>As he expected, it wasn’t a Blink technique like the ones he knew from RPGs in his previous world and it didn’t teleport him.</p>

<p>Carefully using the time deceleration he received from the draconic eyes, Qingyi was barely able to control his trajectory slightly while using the lightning blink.</p>

<p>He made a sharp turn to the right, losing control shortly afterwards, hitting a tree with a thud.</p>

<p>“Shit… it’s going to take a while to master this.” Qingyi sighed.</p>

<p>Fortunately, using the 100-fold time difference in his sea of mind, there was plenty of time.</p>

<p>With a sigh, Qingyi began his training routine.</p>

<p>He trained in his sea of mind, occasionally spending a few hours in the real world to test the Qi consumption of his abilities before returning.</p>

<p>He also paid daily visits to the girls at the temple, cultivating with them and collecting as many lust points as possible.</p>

<p>In the meantime, Feiyan had already prepared some pills for him, and he started using them, slowly increasing his affinity.</p>

<p>The effect with ice and fire was honestly far superior to anything Qingyi had expected, the first 20 affinity points reducing his use of Qi by half and greatly increasing the speed of its use.</p>

<p>The next levels reduced this increase by a large margin, but it was still very relevant, especially considering how little he spent on them.</p>

<p>After more than three weeks of uninterrupted training, Qingyi finally stopped for a moment.</p>

<p>[Name: Long Qingyi</p>

<p>Age: 19</p>

<p>Cultivation: First Stage of the Rebirth Realm (5,200,000 lust points to upgrade)</p>

<p>Affinities: Lightning 248 – Sword: 257 — Fire: 234 — Ice:223 – Charm: 1000 (can be upgraded in the lust store)</p>

<p>Spiritual roots: Mortal-level three-colored spirit root (Lightning sword, fire, ice) (1,000,000 lust points to upgrade, new roots can be bought in the lust store).</p>

<p>Lineage: Blood of the Corruption Dragon God (Mythical grade, 10,000,000 lust points to upgrade.)</p>

<p>Constitution: Primordial Dragon Body (Mythical grade, 10,000,000 lust points to upgrade.)</p>

<p>Lust points: 20,153,093]</p>

<p>20 million lust points, that’s what was left, even after buying recipes for blood and poison affinity pills.</p>

<p>Just a little more than enough to finally advance his lineage and constitution to the mortal grade.</p>
