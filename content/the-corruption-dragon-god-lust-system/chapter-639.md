<p>Both the sect leader and Ma Zhenwu paused for a moment.</p>

<p>How long had it been since Qingyi arrived at the sect?</p>

<p>A short time. A very short time.</p>

<p>He was still at the peak of the Astral Body Realm when he arrived, but now, less than three months later, he was already at the peak of the Astral Blood Realm, with his solid foundation bringing him dangerously close to a breakthrough.</p>

<p>Both were old and had seen a lot throughout their lives, but such rapid cultivation was something they had never heard of.</p>

<p>“Ah… it seems he’s a better match for your talented granddaughter than for my daughter, hahahaha!” The sect leader roared with laughter, watching Old Ma’s expression darken by the second.</p>

<p>No… that couldn’t be true.</p>

<p>Was fate playing tricks on him? Even his friend, his dear friend, whom he himself had held in his arms as a baby, was saying something like that.</p>

<p>The sect leader’s laughter faded, replaced by a look of suspicion that lasted only an instant.</p>

<p>Whatever that damned old man was planning, it clearly wasn’t going well for him.</p>

<p>In the end, the sect leader merely shook his head and turned his eyes to Qingyi.</p>

<p>The handsome young man looked a bit confused, watching the two old men in silence.</p>

<p>What the hell was going on there?</p>

<p>After a moment of hesitation, he bowed, clasping his fists.</p>

<p>“Outer disciple Long Qingyi greets Master Ma and the sect leader!”</p>

<p>The two old men motioned for Qingyi to rise. Ma Zhenwu, weary, hid the turmoil in his heart and offered the young man a gentle smile.</p>

<p>“How has your cultivation improved so much in such a short time?” Zhenwu asked, with genuine curiosity.</p>

<p>“I merely had a fortunate enlightenment, Master Ma. I am unworthy of your praise.” Qingyi bowed once more.</p>

<p>“Nonsense. A cultivator’s fortune is also part of their talent, and you’ve helped me immensely. Those thousand celestial spirit crystals you received don’t even come close to what I truly owe you. Tell me, what can I offer you?” Ma Zhenwu asked.</p>

<p>“Well…” Qingyi hesitated for a moment. “I’m in need of a personal ship to facilitate my missions.”</p>

<p>“That’s no problem.” It was the sect leader who replied, pulling a small token from his robes. “Go to the sect’s shipyards and choose whatever you want. It’s on me.”</p>

<p>He paused, his expression growing more serious.</p>

<p>“The fact that you overcame that damned secret realm after so many years and helped a new star sovereign be born within the sect is not something a mere spaceship can repay.”</p>

<p>Master Ma Zhenwu, who just a moment ago had been beaming with relief that Qingyi had asked for something so simple, couldn’t stop his expression from darkening.</p>

<p>That damned junior! A spaceship was more than enough!</p>

<p>What if Qingyi asked for something he wasn’t willing to give? What if…</p>

<p>Before Ma Zhenwu’s thoughts could even finish, Qingyi already seemed to have made a decision.</p>

<p>After a brief moment of reflection, the handsome young man’s lips parted.</p>

<p>“I know that Master Ma Zhenwu has a relative in the central regions.”</p>

<p>Ma Zhenwu’s heart sank.</p>

<p>‘SON OF A BITCH!’ he screamed inwardly, rolling his eyes to the heavens in resentment.</p>

<p>“I’ve always dreamed of visiting the central regions, and if possible, I’d like to accompany Master Ma when he goes there someday.”</p>

<p>“Of course! The sect has ships capable of reaching the central regions, and Zhenwu always uses one of them when he goes to visit his granddaughter. I guarantee you’ll be on the next trip!” The sect leader roared with laughter, giving his old friend a hearty slap on the back.</p>

<p>“Well, young man, you’ve already proven your talent, and…” The sect leader glanced at his adopted daughter, clinging to Qingyi as if he were everything in the world.</p>

<p>It was rare to see that girl so attached to anyone other than Rouxi, and that made him happy.</p>

<p>A happiness that didn’t last long.</p>

<p>Soon, he would have to make a difficult choice.</p>

<p>“You’ve truly given me a fine recommendation, Zhenwu.” The sect leader smiled. “It seems the sect’s fate isn’t entrusted solely to Tianhao.”</p>

<p>Zhenwu merely nodded in agreement, struggling not to let his shoulders visibly slump.</p>

<p>In his heart, however, a plan was taking shape.</p>

<p>He simply wouldn’t visit his granddaughter anymore!</p>

<p>Not forever, of course, just as long as Qingyi’s request still held.</p>

<p>The young man might change his mind over time, couldn’t he? Unless his granddaughter asked directly, he wouldn’t set foot in the central lands for anything!</p>

<p>As Ma Zhenwu bid farewell to the sect leader and Rou’er, departing with a faint smile, at the center of the cosmos, there was a beauty. Around her, a luxurious garden stretched wide enough to be mistaken for a park, with spiritual carps gliding through pure, clear waters while birds sang the most beautiful melodies among the branches. A place whose beauty was second only to that of Ruxue’s world of the mind, and also second only to that of the woman herself who was present there.</p>

<p>Her eyes were closed, and both her curves and her beautiful features were shrouded in elegant white veils that rippled gently in the breeze.</p>

<p>Suddenly, heavy footsteps echoed in the distance.</p>

<p>When she opened her eyes, a man was approaching the pavilion where she rested.</p>

<p>He was tall and imposing, with noble features and a sharp face that bore a stern, tense expression.</p>

<p>“What is it?” she asked, her green eyes resting on the item the man carried in his hands.</p>

<p>She leaned forward slightly, her arms drawing inward along either side of her huge tits.</p>

<p>The movement pressed her full breasts together, the soft, pale mounds swelling and spilling upward beyond the neckline of her veil in a generous, captivating arc.</p>

<p>A magnificent sight, and one that the man, with his lowered gaze, could not see.</p>

<p>“Senior Sister Ma, after many trials, I have managed to retrieve the jade from the fallen goddess’s tear and would like to offer it to you as a gift, so that at the Divine Maiden Ceremony, two years from now, you may become my wife.”</p>

<p>The moment he finished speaking, a powerful aura crashed down upon the arrogant young master like a mountain.</p>

<p>“Get lost!” A thunderous voice echoed through the garden, and he was hurled far away from the pavilion.</p>

<p>Alone, she let out a slow sigh.</p>

<p>“Hm… I hope Grandpa comes to visit me at the Divine Maiden Ceremony. If he doesn’t come, I’ll go get him by force!”</p>
