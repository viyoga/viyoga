<p>“Young Master Wei! The Hidden Moonlight Secret Realm is about to open; we have to run faster!” A tired, anxious voice echoed.</p>

<p>Its owner was a young man no older than twenty, with a cultivation level in the early stages of the Ancestral Emperor Realm.</p>

<p>In the Mortal Heaven, he would be an unparalleled genius, one in a trillion births.</p>

<p>But here in the Celestial Heavens? He barely qualified as mediocre.</p>

<p>“I know, just keep running, Peng Tao!” Chen Wei shouted to the young man following him, increasing his own speed as well.</p>

<p>He was Chen Wei, the young master of a small regional family in the Fallen Moon World.</p>

<p>The young man accompanying him was Peng Tao, his most loyal servant.</p>

<p>Gritting his teeth, Chen Wei quickened his pace even further, his eyes fixed on the horizon.</p>

<p>His face was smeared with blood, and his sword dripped with the red liquid without ceasing.</p>

<p>It was obvious that he had encountered some trouble along his path.</p>

<p>Ever since he began training in martial arts, he had received a single mission from his father: to become a great man and join the Cosmic Dawn Sect.</p>

<p>Even if he became merely an inner disciple, he would still have the power to elevate the Chen family to a level their ancestors could never have imagined.</p>

<p>No longer just a family of regional merchants.</p>

<p>Unfortunately, things didn’t always go as planned.</p>

<p>“Where do you think you’re going, young Master Chen?” A voice came from the right, and Chen Wei spun immediately, assuming a defensive stance.</p>

<p>A silver dragon tore through the air alongside a blade, striking him with full force and hurling his body to the ground, leaving a crater. “Young Master Chen!” the servant shouted, his eyes filling with terror.</p>

<p>“I’m fine… damn it! Why do the heavens conspire against the Chen family?” Chen Wei stood up, sensing dozens of auras surrounding him.</p>

<p>He himself was in the Astral Body Realm, a considerable talent for that region, since he had reached that level at only twenty years of age.</p>

<p>His attackers were also at the same level, though they were already old, possessing a talent that had allowed them to reach this realm only after centuries.</p>

<p>They were the henchmen of the Luo family, who were also sending their children to the secret realm of the hidden moonlight, used as a talent selection camp for the Cosmic Dawn Sect.</p>

<p>Sworn enemies of the Chen family and possessing considerable military power, they would never let them have the chance to join the Sect.</p>

<p>“It’s nothing personal, young master.” The group’s leader smiled.</p>

<p>He was a rugged man with a long beard and dual axes.</p>

<p>“To be honest, we don’t even need to kill you. We don’t want all-out war with the Chen family. Just let us escort you back to your family’s residence, as good friends.”</p>

<p>“Friends? You kill all our guards and still talk about friendship?” Peng Tao roared beside the young master.</p>

<p>The leader’s eyes turned cold, fixing on Peng Tao. “I’m not talking to you, worm.”</p>

<p>He picked up a small stone and hurled it at the servant, with enough force to rip his head off.</p>

<p>Peng Tao crouched immediately, trembling, the stone whizzing over his head at a terrifying speed.</p>

<p>With that force, it should have flown for kilometers, but it didn’t go more than a few meters before being stopped.</p>

<p>“What a welcome…” A voice sounded from behind a bush.</p>

<p>It was masculine, soft, and serene, caressing the ears of anyone who</p>

<p>heard it.</p>

<p>From behind the bush, a young man emerged.</p>

<p>Horns black as night, purple eyes, and a youthful face of transcendent</p>

<p>beauty.</p>

<p>His powerful muscles were exposed through torn clothing, and his body was covered in wounds that were slowly healing.</p>

<p>It was, of course, Long Qingyi.</p>

<p>The handsome young man looked around with curiosity.</p>

<p>The connection he felt with the stars was much stronger than before, and above all, the primordial laws around him also seemed more</p>

<p>intense.</p>

<p>It was like the difference between heaven and earth.</p>

<p>“Who the hell are you to dare interfere in the affairs of the Luo family?” the leader roared, drawing his weapon.</p>

<p>“Oh… I’m not interfering. I just want directions, where am I?” Qingyi asked, with more respect than he believed that man deserved.</p>

<p>They looked more like common thugs than members of a noble family.</p>

<p>“Where is he? Is this son of a bitch crazy?” A thin, ugly-looking man spoke beside the leader. “Just kill him already.”</p>

<p>The leader clenched his teeth and lunged forward, swinging his axe</p>

<p>down toward Qingyi’s head.</p>

<p>“Hm… is that all?” Qingyi raised two fingers, catching the blade</p>

<p>between them.</p>

<p>He was disappointed by that power, but after thinking for a moment,</p>

<p>he merely shook his head.</p>

<p>They were just common bandits, and yet, they had enough power to easily dominate the entire immortal haven.</p>

<p>He shouldn’t be disappointed by them.</p>

<p>Qingyi applied a little force, and a metallic clang echoed as the axe</p>

<p>blade shattered like glass.</p>

<p>The leader didn’t have time to react.</p>

<p>By the time terror filled his eyes, his head had already been sent</p>

<p>flying into the air.</p>

<p>His soul tried to flee, but Qingyi quickly extinguished it with a wave of</p>

<p>his hand.</p>

<p>Silence.</p>

<p>Chen Wei, Peng Tao, all the bandits around them, no one dared make</p>

<p>a single sound.</p>

<p>The thin man who had ordered Qingyi’s death took a step back,</p>

<p>feeling the young man’s eyes rest upon him.</p>

<p>“I…” He prepared to flee.</p>

<p>“Oh… I’m sorry,” Qingyi’s lips curved into a friendly, gentle smile, “but</p>

<p>I don’t leave loose ends.”</p>

<p>His words were a death sentence, but his face and voice made it seem</p>

<p>as though he were chatting with old friends.</p>

<p>The bandits tried to flee in all directions, their faces twisted with</p>

<p>terror.</p>

<p>None of them managed to take more than ten steps before collapsing</p>

<p>to the ground.</p>

<p>Ignoring the thugs lying in pools of their own blood, Qingyi fixed his gaze on Chen Wei.</p>
