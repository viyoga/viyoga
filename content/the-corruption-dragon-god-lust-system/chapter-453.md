<p>Qingyi crouched in front of the demonic cultist.</p>

<p>His beautiful purple eyes were cold as he watched him struggle desperately.</p>

<p>The damage to the man’s body had been catastrophic. More than half of his bones were broken, his legs reduced to a mess, and his mouth was filled with blood.</p>

<p>Of course, that was far from enough to kill a True Immortal. But still, the pain was absurd.</p>

<p>“You’re going to have to answer me a lot of questions…” Qingyi said.</p>

<p>He opened the system store and bought one of the highest quality healing pills available.</p>

<p>The cultist tried to resist, but Qingyi didn’t care. He forced the man to open his mouth and shoved the pill down his throat.</p>

<p>“Ugh… son of a bitch…”</p>

<p>The cultist’s words were cut off before they were even finished. A punch tore through the air at an absurd speed, broke his jaw, and sent him to the ground, just in time for the pill to take effect.</p>

<p>Even without actively circulating energy through the body, it was still a high-quality pill. On its own, it was almost enough to revive the dead.</p>

<p>Immediately, the cultist’s wounds began to heal. A last gulp of blood left his lips as his broken jaw snapped back into place, his bones cracking painfully.</p>

<p>Even though he was a True Immortal thousands of years old, he couldn’t stop the groans of agony, his red eyes widening.</p>

<p>That damn thing healed too fast without him being able to control it! “Ah… uff… uff…” The cultist gasped heavily, trying to get up.</p>

<p>He crossed his knees, pushed himself up, and then fell to the ground. He was exhausted.</p>

<p>“You know…” The cultist flashed a bloody smile, raising his hands. “We have a lot of assholes like you among us. I thought we could even be good friends… why don’t we talk more amicably? A talented young man like you must be very ambitious, I can help you reach a level never seen before.”</p>

<p>Qingyi did not respond.</p>

<p>He knew well how unorthodox cultivators operated.</p>

<p>There were those who were unorthodox by the nature of their cultivation. And there were those who, in addition to the nature of their cultivation, had rot in their souls.</p>

<p>They were snakes. No, worse than snakes.</p>

<p>They hid among orthodox cultivators like cockroaches; they lied and they manipulated.</p>

<p>He was sure that this cultist had maintained the identity of a pure and honorable noble for centuries, using it for the most terrible acts.</p>

<p>Qingyi was not a good person and would never convince himself otherwise. Still, compared to those corrupt pigs, he was an angel incarnate.</p>

<p>“You have a mana heart and a Dantian too. How is that possible?” Qingyi questioned, eliciting a laugh of contempt from the cultist.</p>

<p>“Ask my…”</p>

<p>The cultist’s words were interrupted again.</p>

<p>But this time, it wasn’t by a punch.</p>

<p>Qingyi pressed a single finger against one of the cultist’s newly healed feet, moving his demonic Qi.</p>

<p>“ARGH! DAMN IT! Y-you… UGHN…”</p>

<p>The cultist writhed. His lips opened in a guttural scream that spread throughout the forest, stirring it for a brief moment.</p>

<p>The pain was horrible. The smell of burning flesh was accompanied by whitish smoke as Qingyi’s lightning Qi destroyed everything it touched.</p>

<p>Blood spurted onto the handsome young man’s face, but he didn’t care.</p>

<p>“Do I need to repeat the question?”</p>

<p>Qingyi’s lips lifted slightly. Blood slid down his cheeks, and his eyes were filled with a strange cold gentleness.</p>

<p>He didn’t even seem to recognize that he was in the midst of torture, and this only terrified the cultist even more.</p>

<p>Unfortunately for the prisoner, he had taken too long to answer.</p>

<p>Qingyi’s fingers flowed to the other foot, and then came the pain.</p>

<p>The terrible smell of burning flesh, smoke, and blood. Everything again, with the same intensity as before.</p>

<p>“I can make this much slower than it needs to be,” Qingyi murmured, looking at the cultist, whose face was already drenched in tears.</p>

<p>It was… pathetic.</p>

<p>A being whose power rivaled that of a True Immortal, bearer of a mana heart and a Dantian, being reduced to tears so easily.</p>

<p>Qingyi still remembered many years ago, when he tortured the King of the Heavenly Thunder Kingdom, who had tried to take Ruxue from him.</p>

<p>Even that Golden Core Realm cultivator endured the same torture with much more dignity than this cultist. And that king was barely a fraction of what this man was.</p>

<p>Qingyi moved his hand toward the cultist’s knee, who trembled.</p>

<p>“W-wait! I-I really can’t talk! If I talk, the heartworm in my chest will kill me immediately!”</p>

<p>He hoped that the excuse would work, that Qingyi would at least give him a quick death.</p>

<p>Unfortunately, he didn’t know Qingyi.</p>

<p>The handsome young man placed his hand on the cultist’s chest and used his spatial Qi.</p>

<p>In the next moment, the space between his fingers distorted. The cultist’s eyes filled with shock as he felt a weight lift from his heart, while at the same time a small red worm appeared in Qingyi’s hands.</p>

<p>“Go ahead. Start talking.” Qingyi crushed the worm.</p>

<p>The cultist gulped. His lips parted, but no words came out.</p>

<p>Only after a moment of hesitation did he finally begin:</p>

<p>“In the far west, there is a Dark Master, a powerful Qi user… He was the one who initiated me. He helped me become a noble and gave me a Dantian! I don’t know how the procedure works, I swear! I-I just fell asleep, and when I woke up, my body had completely changed.”</p>

<p>“Hm…” Qingyi scratched his chin.</p>

<p>A Dark Master in the far west, with a connection to mana and Qi.</p>

<p>“Why did you attack me?” he asked.</p>

<p>He needed to know if the attack had occurred because he had already been recognized as one of the Five Heroes.</p>

<p>If that was the case, things could get a little dangerous.</p>

<p>“D-debt… t-to pay off a debt! The master ordered us to capture as many people as possible for a great sacrifice!”</p>

<p>“Hm…” Qingyi exhaled thoughtfully.</p>

<p>“P-please… no more, that’s all I know… I-I’m telling the truth,” the cultist whimpered.</p>

<p>“I know you are.”</p>

<p>Qingyi smiled. With a gentle movement of his hand, the cultist’s head flew through the air.</p>
