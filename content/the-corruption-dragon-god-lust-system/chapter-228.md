<p>“Repeat what you said…” A loud, hate-filled voice left the lips of a man of youthful beauty, short black hair and piercing red eyes, a pair of horns rising from his head.</p>

<p>“Lady Ruyan has taken a servant, Y-young lord, a new recruit. She seems to have locked herself in her mansion with him and avoided going out as much as possible.”</p>

<p>The servant’s words were answered by a loud crack as the glass goblet in the man’s hands shattered, fragments scattering across the floor.</p>

<p>That man was the young lord of the demon cult, the demon lord’s rightful and only heir.</p>

<p>“Who is that? Who dares approach my sister?” he roared, the whole building shaking as he stood up, his demonic aura expanding uncontrollably.</p>

<p>“He is-” The servant tried to speak, but was interrupted when the door to the private room they were in opened, revealing a powerful, dignified-looking man wearing imposing crimson armor.</p>

<p>“What do you want? I’m busy.” Young lord Xue Mo growled as he turned around.</p>

<p>That man was a member of the Crimson Guard, a group of a hundred private protectors of the demon lord, each one a cultivator at the peak of the Rebirth Realm.</p>

<p>Even so, Xue Mo showed him no respect.</p>

<p>“Young master Xue Mo, your father requires your presence immediately.” The guard spoke and the anger on Xue Mo’s face finally disappeared.</p>

<p>“What does father want?”</p>

<p>“I dare not presume on our lord’s thoughts, young master.”</p>

<p>“Ah… fuck.” Xue Mo got up and left with the guard, the servant following right behind.</p>

<p>***</p>

<p>Qingyi stopped in front of one of the richest and most luxurious establishments in the city that surrounded the demon cult.</p>

<p>A building that rose more than twenty stories, its façade seemed to have been dipped in gold and on its door, a large, bright sign read: Dead Moon Pavilion.</p>

<p>That was where the signal from Ruxue’s fragment was coming from.</p>

<p>Qingyi hesitated for a brief moment before entering the main entrance , where he was immediately greeted by an attendant in long, elegant robes.</p>

<p>“Greetings, honored customer. What can I do for you? Do you need a bed for the night? Food? Cultivation rooms? The Dead Moon Pavilion has it all!” She spoke, flashing a professional smile.</p>

<p>“I-” before Qingyi had even finished his sentence, a commotion gripped the entire building, a powerful demonic aura exploding for a brief moment before ceasing.</p>

<p>A few moments later, three figures appeared: a guard in crimson armor, an ordinary-looking servant and finally, a man with a handsome, youthful face.</p>

<p>“Qingyi, my fragment is with one of those three!” Ruxue’s voice echoed in Qingyi’s mind, but he ignored it, focusing his eyes on one of the three figures.</p>

<p>His appearance perfectly matched the description of the young demon cult lord given to Qingyi by Ruyan.</p>

<p>While observing the three, Qingyi’s eyes met those of the servant, who immediately shuddered.</p>

<p>“Y-young lord…” He whispered to Xue Mo, who completely ignored him.</p>

<p>Gathering his courage, he finally grabbed the young lord’s sleeve. “Y-young lord, this is-”</p>

<p>“Did I give you permission to touch me? Worm.” Xue Mo suddenly stopped his steps, turning to the servant.</p>

<p>“N-no, young lord, it’s just that-” The servant’s head exploded under Xue Mo’s bloodthirsty gaze, his murderous intent locking everyone in place as the headless corpse fell to the ground.</p>

<p>Even Qingyi shuddered.</p>

<p>It was powerful, far beyond his current power.</p>

<p>Qingyi immediately realized that he would need to advance to the Nascent Soul Realm to fight on an equal footing with that guy.</p>

<p>“Fucking worms… you give them a bit of responsibility and suddenly they think they have power over something.” Xue Mo spat on the corpse before turning around, his eyes finally meeting Qingyi’s.</p>

<p>From his clothes, he was a newcomer to the cult and from his apparent age and cultivation, he had a miserable talent, mediocre at best.</p>

<p>Xue Mo seemed to think for a brief moment before waving his hands, a small token appearing from the servant’s clothes and floating over to Qingyi.</p>

<p>“You will be my new personal servant, report to my palace in three days at dawn.” Without giving Qingyi time to reply, Xue Mo left with the guard.</p>

<p>The handsome young man remained frozen in place for long moments, still unable to process everything that had just happened.</p>

<p>“Ah… there’s nothing I can do.” Qingyi shook his head.</p>

<p>Well, at least now he had an idea of Xue Mo’s power and was almost certain that he was the one carrying Ruxue’s fragment.</p>

<p>He also knew that someone had reported the fact that Ruxue had taken him to her mansion.</p>

<p>Perhaps one of the other three recruits or the masked elder himself who had recruited him into the demon cult.</p>

<p>Whoever it was, Qingyi would keep it in mind.</p>

<p>It wasn’t long before the corpse was cleaned up and the attendant approached Qingyi, this time even more respectfully.</p>

<p>“Dear customer, can we get you something?”</p>

<p>The handsome young man thought for a moment before nodding, asking for some wine and boiled mutton.</p>

<p>He had a slight headache and really needed to fill his stomach.</p>

<p>He was sure that Elize would be more than happy to cook for him now, but she was cultivating with the other girls and he didn’t want to disturb her.</p>

<p>After eating and paying, Qingyi set off in the direction of the tavern where he would meet his companions.</p>

<p>It was a remote place in the outskirts of the demon cult city.</p>

<p>Hesitating for a brief moment, Qingyi entered.</p>

<p>“You’re late, I thought you were dead.” A tired voice caught Qingyi’s attention and he immediately turned around.</p>

<p>At a far table, two men were sitting.</p>

<p>It was Yedu and fattie Heidu.</p>

<p>“I’ve had a few annoyances, where’s Mojie?” Qingyi asked as he sat down at the table.</p>

<p>Apart from them, the place was completely empty, surrounded by sound suppression and concealment arrays.</p>

<p>“Dead.” Yedu replied, clenching his fists.</p>

<p>“We were at the selection together, I tried to protect him but I ended up between two cultivators at the peak of the Nascent Soul Realm. I couldn’t do much.”</p>
