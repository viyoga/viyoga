<p>The four hundred and ninety-nine disciples had understood one thing about Rouxi’s selection process for her next child.</p>

<p>It was combat-based and only one would remain standing.</p>

<p>But in doing so, they had forgotten something far more important.</p>

<p>“W-why?” he asked again, his eyes clouded with anger and indignation. His body trembled as he stayed on his knees, his palms pressed flat against the cold stone of the pavilion floor.</p>
