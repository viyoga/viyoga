<p>Yuehan looked away, her full rosy lips pressed into a thin line.</p>

<p>Qingyi had heard about ninety percent of what Yuehan, Ruxue, and Huoyue had discussed after she was rescued. Sometimes, that conversation had strayed into details that were… well, a little inappropriate.</p>

<p>Yuehan clenched her teeth, the tips of her ears growing hot. Just remembering it was embarrassing enough.</p>

<p>She pushed the thought aside and drew her blade.</p>

<p>Flames followed the movement.</p>

<p>A blade similar to Ruxue's—but reddish silver, covered in flaming patterns—slid from its sheath with a metallic whisper.</p>

<p>Qingyi drew the Heaven-Defying Thunder Sword in turn.</p>

<p>A sharp crackle of electricity ran along the blade the moment it touched the open air.</p>

<p>"Have you ever heard of Sword Resonance?" Yuehan asked, taking a slow, deep breath to focus her mind, her chest rising with it.</p>

<p>She was here to train with Qingyi and test his strength. That was all... right?</p>

<p>"A little, but I don't know much about it," Qingyi replied.</p>

<p>Among his wives, some had already achieved Sword Resonance with Ruxue's help.</p>
