<p>The Sect of the Myriad Beasts was certainly a unique place.</p>

<p>Divine beasts, mythical beasts, and any kind of creature capable of speech and complex thought gathered there, regardless of whether they could take human form or not.</p>

<p>The sect itself was located on a tropical planet, spanning four distinct biomes: frigid, desert, forest, and marine. Each was home to its own unique types of beast disciples.</p>

<p>All of this, naturally, surrounded the Palace of the Ten Thousand Beasts, the residence of the sect leader. From there, a winding path led to the shadowy Valley of Eternal Death, where the sect’s current ancestor, a mighty giant bear, rested.</p>

<p>The moment Qingyi stepped out of the cold ship, a tropical breeze swept through his long black hair, carrying with it the salty scent of the sea and the distant cries of exotic creatures.</p>

<p>He observed the many pavilions rising up around him, their rooftops gleaming beneath the bright tropical sun.</p>

<p>It was a pleasant place, evoking a feeling similar to the one he’d had upon arriving in the land of the cat people, back in the immortal heavens.</p>

<p>Qingyi, of course, hadn’t forgotten about bringing his loved ones from that place.</p>

<p>He wanted that day to come as soon as possible, but to make it happen, he needed to focus his mind on just one thing: lust points.</p>

<p>“Look, big brother Qingyi! Bai Hu Lie is coming!” Rou’er said with a bright look, waving toward the enormous tiger on the horizon.</p>

<p>Bai Hu Lie, as always, was terrifying.</p>

<p>Especially now, after advancing to the Celestial Origin Realm and awakening his bloodline by killing every one of his male siblings.</p>

<p>His sharp claws were stained with fresh blood, and he wore black leather combat armor that hugged his powerful chest.</p>

<p>He looked as if he had just stepped out of a fight, his breathing heavy and ragged, hot steam escaping from his maw with every exhalation.</p>

<p>Seeing Rou’er wave at him, the tiger smiled, baring rows of fangs.</p>

<p>His powerful body—over three hundred kilograms of pure muscle, his stature intimidating even next to Qingyi—hurried toward them.</p>

<p>“I’ll let the two of you have your moment alone. Come on, Rou’er, we have to find the leader of the Myriad Beasts Sect,” the leader of the Cosmic Dawn Sect said.</p>

<p>Rou’er pouted, but being accustomed to such duties as the sect’s little princess, she simply followed him along with Rouxi and dozens of other elders.</p>

<p>Alone now, Qingyi walked toward Bai Hu Lie.</p>

<p>“Qingyi, my brother-in-law!” Bai Hu Lie roared excitedly, pulling Qingyi into a bone-crushing bear hug, not even bothering to worry about staining his clothes with blood.</p>

<p>“It’s good to have you here! Come on, come on! I’ll take you to my private arena, hahaha!”</p>

<p>Qingyi merely nodded, pulling away from the tiger and exchanging a firm handshake with the mighty predator.</p>

<p>The ground beneath their feet cracked, fine webs of fissures spreading outward as the sheer pressure of their physical strength forced many of the weaker ones to stagger back.</p>

<p>Many sects were arriving at that place, and naturally, many disciples as well. For them, the mere sight of Qingyi and Bai Hu Lie side by side was enough to send shivers down their spines.</p>

<p>Although the two were only in the early stages of the Celestial Origin Realm, no one doubted that both were unmatched within that realm.</p>

<p>Certain of each other’s strength, they smiled and soon found themselves walking side by side through the pavilions.</p>

<p>“You should be careful of that damn Feng Wuji,” Bai Hu Lie said, his tone laced with contempt. “He was obsessed with my sister and gave up some rather important resources to try to win her hand.”</p>

<p>“Ah… another frustrated cuckold?” Qingyi roared with laughter. “We killed the Guo brothers, and now another one shows up right after, begging for a beating.”</p>

<p>“Hahaha, yes. It’d be good if he attacked you first. That way, you’d have a solid excuse to crush him. Unfortunately, that Feng Wuji is at least smart enough to wait until the tournament starts to pick a fight. He must be throwing himself at my little sister’s feet right now.”</p>

<p>Bai Hu Lie looked toward the horizon, his golden eyes fixed on his father’s palace. Without hesitation, he motioned for Qingyi to follow him.</p>

<p>The handsome young dragon wasn’t the least bit worried about Feng Wuji.</p>

<p>He had already heard of that man, the disciple of the leader of the most powerful sword sect in the entire central region.</p>

<p>Since Feng Wuji possessed a powerful Eternal-grade draconic bloodline and cultivation at the peak of the Celestial Origin Realm, Qingyi was curious to test his strength, but nothing more than that.</p>

<p>Alongside Bai Hu Lie, he walked through the Myriad Beasts Sect, observing the most unique creatures, all with humanoid forms to varying degrees.</p>

<p>Some were like Bai Hu Lie, bipedal, yet retaining much of their beastly forms.</p>

<p>Others were essentially complete beasts, possessing only human intelligence behind their wild eyes.</p>

<p>They walked for a few more minutes before finally arriving at the sect leader’s palace. There, Bai Hu Lie first led Qingyi to the main arena.</p>

<p>The guards initially moved to block them, since the arena was being renovated and prepared for the tournament. However, the moment they recognized Bai Hu Lie, they shuddered and quickly made way.</p>

<p>“Look, Brother Qingyi.” Bai Hu Lie spread his arms wide as soon as they stepped out onto the stands.</p>

<p>He pointed to the arena that stretched over ten square kilometers, fortified with formations powerful enough to withstand battles between star lords.</p>

<p>“This will be our stage. Before a million spectators, we’ll spill blood and hunt down enemies of unmatched power and glory!” Bai Hu Lie puffed out his chest with pride.</p>

<p>“You, as my sworn brother and future brother-in-law, must be ready to face me in the finals, hahaha! None of those puny humans should stand a chance against us.”</p>

<p>Qingyi nodded, smiling at Bai Hu Lie’s confidence and excitement.</p>

<p>He could clearly sense that the tiger was itching to start the slaughter, but unfortunately, they had to wait.</p>

<p>There was still quite a while before the tournament began.</p>
