<p>“Who would have thought that, once again, I would fight alongside you.” An old, dignified voice echoed.</p>

<p>If Qingyi had been there, he would have recognized that voice as Lucios, who had officially become the patriarch of the dragon elves.</p>

<p>In front of him stood another man, clearly a high elf.</p>

<p>He was tall, almost six feet tall, equipped with armor that appeared to be made of glass crystals, beautiful and elegant.</p>

<p>His cheeks were high, and his blue eyes were always focused.</p>

<p>That was the king of the elves and Aeryn’s uncle.</p>

<p>“Don’t be so grumpy, Lucios. See this as an opportunity.” The elven king smiled, watching the armies they had gathered.</p>

<p>Five experts from the astral realm and over a thousand ancient emperors, accompanied by nearly two million foot soldiers.</p>

<p>A frightening power, marching toward the capital of the divine kingdom of Auranys.</p>

<p>“Opportunity…” Lucios focused his mind.</p>

<p>He hated to admit it, but his cousin was right.</p>

<p>His people had been fighting for too long, and now they had made a decision.</p>

<p>They would attack the forces gathered in the holy capital before they could behead them one by one, leaving the other isolated.</p>

<p>All they could do was pray that it wasn’t a trap.</p>

<p>“What about the flying ship above the holy capital? Did your escorts say anything about it?” Lucios asked, changing the subject.</p>

<p>“No.” The elven king shook his head. “It belongs to those foreigners from the east, that’s all I know. They don’t seem to be allies of the</p>

<p>tightly.</p>

<p>“But, Lord Lucios-” A knight riding a crimson beast tried to question, but received only a single, cold, short word.</p>

<p>“Retreat, now.”</p>

<p>Without further hesitation, the dragon knights began to turn around, flying toward the horizon.</p>

<p>“You too.” Lucios brought his face close to the muzzle of the bronze beast, his eternal companion.</p>

<p>“Go. I will follow you soon.”</p>

<p>The bronze dragon looked at Lucios with complicated eyes before taking to the skies, retreating as well.</p>

<p>At that moment, as the bloodshed of that battle increased more and more, something changed.</p>

<p>“It’s moving!” Lucios exclaimed, watching the flying ship slowly raise its stern.</p>

<p>It wasn’t aiming the cannons at the armies below, nor the side cannons, nor the powerful front cannon.</p>

<p>The ship’s stern continued to rise even higher until it was finally perfectly perpendicular, pointing toward the skies.</p>

<p>“What the hell are these bastards planning to do?” The old man shuddered, gritting his teeth and taking a step back.</p>

<p>He felt dozens more creatures appear inside the emperor’s hand before being immediately erased, as if they had been consumed by something.</p>

<p>Lucios didn’t know it, but each of those demonic seeds had power equivalent to that of a peak astral body realm expert.</p>

<p>If released into the world, they would be capable of easily leveling entire nations. But now, they were being used for one thing only: to fuel the emperor’s hand’s main cannon.</p>

<p>A single shot with the combined power of fifty peak astral body realm experts…</p>

<p>That was an unimaginable amount of power.</p>

<p>The flying ship slowly repositioned itself, a pulse of Qi running through its wood, the front cannon glowing with an intense and deadly crimson light.</p>

<p>Even from kilometers away, Lucios sensed the danger. Every part of him screamed one thing: ‘Run away, immediately!</p>

<p>Obviously, he didn’t run away, struggling to calm his mind as he tried to understand what was happening.</p>

<p>In the moments before the shot, Qingyi’s figure also appeared, a few kilometers away from Lucios, his eyes immediately falling on the emperor’s hand as he activated his draconic eyes.</p>

<p>He barely had time to study the situation when, finally, the emperor’s hand fired.</p>

<p>Qingyi’s hair fluttered, his body being thrown backward as the shockwave spread, turning everything below into dust.</p>

<p>Even more than fifty kilometers away, trees were uprooted and everything was leveled.</p>

<p>No one below the true immortal realm survived the shockwave.</p>

<p>Qingyi’s eyes followed the Qi projectile fired by the flying ship, rising</p>

<p>toward the heavens with terrifying power.</p>

<p>‘Would I be able to withstand a direct hit?” He asked himself, but</p>

<p>quickly dismissed the thought.</p>

<p>No…</p>

<p>Not even his blood would remain.</p>

<p>The shot finally stopped rising, striking the skies with a terrifying</p>

<p>boom.</p>

<p>For a brief moment, the skies opened with a deep tear, as if a sharp</p>

<p>claw had just sunk into the very fabric of space-time.</p>

<p>The tear immediately began to close, but before it closed completely,</p>

<p>a terrifying sight fell upon those who had survived.</p>

<p>Two pairs of hands, each the size of a mountain.</p>

<p>They grabbed the edges of the tear and forced it to stay open, an oppressive aura falling upon the entire immortal sky. “Something big is coming…” Qingyi gripped the sky-defying thunder sword tightly.</p>
