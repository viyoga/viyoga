<p>Alone in his room, Qingyi sighed tiredly as he sat on his bed.</p>

<p>He had fucked Elize until the moon appeared in the sky, and then continued fucking her until their naked bodies collapsed on top of each other.</p>

<p>Now, at dawn, he wanted to have her in his arms, his face sinking into those huge breasts.</p>

<p>Unfortunately, he had to hold back.</p>

<p>The other women still didn’t know that he had taken Elize and, honestly, he preferred to proceed more slowly.</p>

<p>He could easily take them right now, as he had done with Elize. The difference in cultivation was too great for them to resist his lineage.</p>

<p>But in the end, he just shook his head. He didn’t want to force anything, at least not yet.</p>

<p>Pushing those lustful thoughts from his mind, he focused on his status screen.</p>

<p>[Name: Long Qingyi</p>

<p>Age: 18</p>

<p>Cultivation: Second stage of the foundation realm (700 lust points to upgrade)</p>

<p>Affinities: Lightning 60 – Sword: 60 – Charm: 1000 (can be upgraded in the lust store)</p>

<p>Spiritual roots: Seventh level lightning sword root (1000 lust points to upgrade, new roots can be bought in the lust store). )</p>

<p>Lineage: Blood of the Corruption Dragon God (Rare grade, 10,000 lust points to upgrade.)</p>

<p>Constitution: Body of the primordial dragon (Rare grade, 10,000 lust points to upgrade.)</p>

<p>Lust points: 3137]</p>

<p>His fun with Elize had earned him just over a thousand lust points, enough to make him comfortable.</p>

<p>He could continue improving his spiritual root, but he rejected the idea immediately.</p>

<p>His root was already not much inferior to a ninth level one and, with the density of Qi in this region, he would hardly gain much from a new spirit root.</p>

<p>So there were three things he could improve.</p>

<p>The first was obviously a good sword that could withstand all his foundation realm power.</p>

<p>Right after that, a new movement or combat technique.</p>

<p>The first option was something Qingyi was resisting doing, he was still interested in the spirit of lightning and the sword it inhabited.</p>

<p>It wasn’t worth spending a fortune on a good sword only to replace it when he managed to conquer a spirit sword with an ego, far more powerful than anything he could buy now.</p>

<p>Movement techniques were also something he didn’t want to change just yet.</p>

<p>Lightning steps could easily last him until he reached the golden core realm, perhaps even beyond that.</p>

<p>It was a very reliable technique, even though it was only a rare grade.</p>

<p>And then there were the combat techniques.</p>

<p>These were probably what Qingyi needed most at the moment.</p>

<p>Since he did not had any other combat techniques, this would immediately increase his fighting capacity by a long margin, while at the same time bringing no risk of redundancy.</p>

<p>Of course, there were still other things like artifacts, spiritual robes and attack and defense talismans, but none of them would represent as big a leap in Qingyi’s power as a proper combat technique.</p>

<p>Making his decision, Qingyi opened the system store, looking at the almost infinite list of techniques.</p>

<p>“Ah…” he exhaled.</p>

<p>The amount of techniques was staggering, at a level where even filtering, there were still tens of thousands.</p>

<p>’Where does all this stuff come from?’ he thought, his eyes skimming through the descriptions of dozens of techniques.</p>

<p>After long minutes of searching, something finally caught his eye.</p>

<p>[Combat technique: Tempest Monarch Sword Art Fragment</p>

<p>Grade: Rare</p>

<p>Description: An ancient sword technique that uses lightning Qi, created by a dynasty of lightning cultivators from the ancient era, is divided into twelve forms. This fragment contains the first and second forms.</p>

<p>Price: 2500 lust points]</p>

<p>“Hm…” Qingyi scratched his chin.</p>

<p>Honestly, it was hard to tell how good those techniques were from the descriptions alone.</p>

<p>But one thing caught his eye about the Tempest Monarch’s sword art.</p>

<p>Although it was only a fragment, it was a technique from the ancient era, also known as the golden age of martial arts on the Blue Moon Continent, more than a hundred thousand years ago.</p>

<p>“System, filter only for rare grade techniques from the ancient era,” Qingyi ordered and, in the next moment, the list of tens of thousands of techniques was reduced to just a hundred.</p>

<p>The Storm Monarch Sword Art Fragment was the cheapest of these hundred, with the most expensive ones costing tens of thousands of lust points.</p>

<p>But Qingyi was well aware that this price was not excessive.</p>

<p>Ancestral techniques were on a completely different level; a mortal-grade technique could give you an empire, but a mortal-grade ancestral technique could give you the heavens.</p>

<p>Removing any doubt from his heart, Qingyi moved his fingers, pressing buy on the technique.</p>

<p>2,500 lust points disappeared from his status and, in the next moment, he felt a sharp pain, an almost endless stream of information flooding into his head.</p>

<p>It was similar to what he felt with the lightning steps, but much more intense, as if the lightning steps were a short poem and the Tempest Monarch Sword Art Fragment was an endless epic.</p>

<p>It was only after almost five minutes of suffering that Qingyi stood up, sighing with relief.</p>

<p>Without wasting any time, he immediately left his room, leaping over the temple walls and taking up a distance where he wouldn’t wake the girls.</p>

<p>His long black hair danced lightly in the cool early morning breeze as he looked up, the moonlight bathing his handsome face.</p>

<p>With a smooth movement, he drew his sword, the steel cutting through the air before being taken over by a thunderous aura.</p>

<p>But this was no ordinary aura, it was much more intense.</p>

<p>The air around him seemed to boil as Qingyi used the first form of the Tempest Monarch sword art.</p>

<p>A boom echoed as he struck, lightning leaving the tip of the sword and sweeping over everything more than ten meters in front of Qingyi.</p>

<p>The ground shook as the trees in front of him fell to the forest floor.</p>

<p>Their trunks were burning, but not in flames – they were melting.</p>
