<p>The Heavenly Demon stopped, his eyes coldly scanning Tianjin.</p>

<p>His eyebrows furrowed, and slowly, the hatred etched into his face gave way to an expressionless frown.</p>

<p>He knew very well what Tianjin wanted.</p>

<p>The golden dragon wanted him to charge forward, to attack like a madman blinded by rage.</p>

<p>Hatred gnawed at the Heavenly Demon from within, but he was no fool.</p>

<p>He was fully confident he could defeat Tianjin with ease—but in this situation, one-on-one, with no chance for preparation—the fight would boil down to brute force, Qi, and technique.</p>

<p>The Heavenly Demon was indeed among the most powerful Celestial Emperors, but his true strength lay in his endless schemes, not in his individual combat prowess.</p>

<p>Tianjin, on the other hand, was an aberration only slightly inferior to Long Wujin himself—a monster who would be considered a prodigy even in the land of the true gods.</p>

<p>The Heavenly Demon growled in anger, swinging his sword in a sharp arc.</p>

<p>Putrid crimson energy dripped from the black blade with the force of the movement, like blood being shaken off a freshly used thread.</p>

<p>The droplets hissed as they struck the void, corroding the very fabric of space before dissolving into nothing.</p>

<p>"Where is Long Qingyi?" he asked, narrowing his eyes.</p>

<p>He still wasn’t sure who was responsible for this, but when he stopped to think about it... who else could it be?</p>

<p>Only that wretched little dragon had the power to fuck up his plans like this, time and time again.</p>

<p>And knowing that Qingyi also wielded the Heaven-Defying Thunder Sword, the Heavenly Demon could think of no one else.</p>

<p>It was him.</p>

<p>It could only be him.</p>

<p>"Long Qingyi? Why would someone as haughty, powerful, honorable, and renowned as yourself be interested in a junior who hasn’t even reached the Stellar Sovereign Realm?" Tianjin tilted his head slightly, an almost imperceptible smile dancing at the corner of his lips.</p>

<p>The taunting tone only further soured the Heavenly Demon’s already grim mood.</p>

<p>"Don’t test my patience, golden lizard!"</p>

<p>He swung his blade, and a crimson wave followed the sharp edge, tearing through the void toward Tianjin’s chest with a shriek like torn metal.</p>

<p>The golden dragon moved.</p>

<p>A gentle flick of his spear, and the crimson wave collapsed, shattering into thousands of tiny fragments of red light that dissipated into the emptiness.</p>

<p>Tianjin stared intently at his opponent, his battle intent surging like an unstoppable tide.</p>

<p>He knew the Heavenly Demon was planning an escape. He also knew that, if that were not the case, an all-out battle between them would be devastating to everything around them.</p>

<p>Still, he was ready to fight.</p>

<p>If he killed the Heavenly Demon right here, everything would be settled once and for all.</p>

<p>Once again, golden spear and black sword tore through the void of the cosmos, clashing in the space between dragon and demon with a thunderous crack that rippled across the stars.</p>

<p>The surrounding space visibly trembled, and an explosion of Qi swept through the void, nearly causing it to collapse as golden and crimson currents collided in a violent, seething maelstrom.</p>

<p>A blinding blast bloomed from the point of impact, and the two retreated, the shockwave from their attack triggering terrible tremors on distant planets and stars.</p>

<p>Mountains crumbled. Oceans boiled. Entire civilizations felt the earth beneath their feet groan under the weight of a battle fought light-years away.</p>

<p>By the time the operation to rescue the Divine Sword Fairy began, Tianjin had already secretly issued the order to launch a new one.</p>

<p>All around him, dozens of inhabited planets had been evacuated by various Celestial Emperors with spatial affinity.</p>

<p>He was ready for this battle.</p>

<p>Tianjin gripped his spear tightly, his eyes as cold as eternal ice.</p>

<p>Around the shaft, a golden dragon emerged, coiling along the length of the weapon like a serpent, its jaws clenched around the tip of the blade.</p>

<p>Every scale gleamed with the light of a thousand suns, and its breath came out in low, resonant growls that shook the void.</p>

<p>Around the Heavenly Demon’s sword, a crimson serpent manifested, exuding a putrid, oppressive Qi that caused the void itself to rot.</p>

<p>Where its coils passed, reality blackened and withered, as if the cosmos itself recoiled from its touch.</p>

<p>They stared coldly at one another for another moment.</p>

<p>And then, they advanced.</p>

<p>The golden dragon and the crimson serpent collided, and everything within a radius of hundreds of light-years turned to dust almost instantly, the primordial laws themselves collapsing under the weight of the impact.</p>

<p>Keeping one hand on his spear, Tianjin struck out with the other, unleashing the Divine Fist of the Golden Dragon—one of the most powerful techniques he possessed.</p>

<p>The Heavenly Demon clenched his teeth, stepping back and parrying the blow with the flat of his blade.</p>

<p>The impact traveled through the black metal and hurled him backward with crushing force, his body tearing through the cosmos in a crimson and golden trail as the dragon from Tianjin’s fist pursued him with its jaws wide open, its roar shaking the very bones of the heavens.</p>

<p>"Ah... fuck!"</p>

<p>The Heavenly Demon spun through the void, slicing the golden dragon clean in half.</p>

<p>The projection dissolved into scattered particles of light, and in the next instant, his body vanished as well.</p>

<p>He reappeared beside Tianjin, his black sword already descending in a devastating slash.</p>

<p>Once again, spear and sword clashed, and the entire celestial heavens shook.</p>

<p>With the blades locked together, sparks of Qi leaping from the point of contact, Tianjin and the Heavenly Demon stared each other down with absolute coldness.</p>

<p>"You damned golden lizard! How dare you steal from me?" the Heavenly Demon roared, his fury causing the crimson Qi around him to bubble and hiss.</p>

<p>"Steal from you?" Tianjin thrust his spear forward, forcing the Heavenly Demon to retreat. "Listen to your own words, you son of a bitch!" he growled.</p>

<p>The Heavenly Demon did not respond.</p>

<p>His eyes were cold, his face grim.</p>

<p>He swallowed his anger whole, stifling it until nothing remained but pure calculation.</p>

<p>"If you want a war, very well. You’ll have a war."</p>

<p>The Heavenly Demon didn’t hesitate. He turned his back on Tianjin and, in the next instant, vanished into the void.</p>

<p>A small talisman materialized in his hand. With a low growl, he brought it to his lips.</p>

<p>"Very well... I accept your deal. Help me bring death to all my enemies."</p>

<p>From the talisman, only a cold, amused laugh echoed in reply.</p>

<p>A darkness greater than the Heavenly Demon himself was about to awaken.</p>
