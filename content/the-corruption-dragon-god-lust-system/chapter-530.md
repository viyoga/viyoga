<p>The Gold King and his wife, Yunfeng, remained above Meilin.</p>

<p>Meanwhile, the Emperor and Empress left the audience in shock, positioning themselves beside Qingyi.</p>

<p>Even for those who had heard Jin Hao’s request for the Emperor and Empress to act as Qingyi’s absent family, the scene was enough to elicit gasps.</p>

<p>In their long lives, those present had never seen the monarchs act this way. The gesture was unprecedented, even considering the deep ties between the Gold King and the imperial family.</p>

<p>Qingyi felt uncomfortable with the idea of kneeling before them, especially given the possibility that they were demonic cultists. Now, however, that didn’t matter.</p>

<p>This was Meilin’s special day. He would not allow anything or anyone to spoil the moment. The young couple closed their eyes and bowed their heads in respect.</p>

<p>“Long Qingyi,” the Emperor’s voice echoed. It was cold, calm, and laden with dignity.</p>

<p>“Do you promise, before me, before the heavens, and before whatever values are dear to you, that your words are true and honest?”</p>

<p>“I promise,” Qingyi replied.</p>

<p>Hearing the confirmation, the Empress took a step forward. Her long, delicate fingers reached Qingyi’s head, touching exactly between his beautiful black horns.</p>

<p>Her voice was different from the Emperor’s. It was soft, sweet, and serene, though it carried the slight signs of age.</p>

<p>“And do you accept Jin Meilin as your wife? Do you promise to respect and honor the Jin family, loving and protecting Meilin in sickness and in death?</p>

<p>“I promise,” Qingyi repeated firmly.</p>

<p>Beside him, Jin Hao also opened his mouth to speak.</p>

<p>“Jin Meilin, my daughter, before me, the heavens, and the blood of your parents, do you promise that your words are true and honest?”</p>

<p>“I promise, Father,” Meilin nodded, struggling to contain her excitement as she felt her mother’s hands touch her head.</p>

<p>“And do you promise to accept Long Qingyi as your husband? Do you promise to respect, love, and serve him, both in sickness and in death?”</p>

<p>“Yes, I promise!” Meilin exclaimed excitedly and then raised her bright eyes, meeting her mother’s gaze.</p>

<p>The young couple then stood up.</p>

<p>Their fingers intertwined under the gaze of the Gold King, the White Heron, and the two monarchs of one of the most powerful empires in the immortal heavens.</p>

<p>There was a moment of absolute silence.</p>

<p>Meilin could clearly hear the beating of her own heart as the sweet symphony of Jin Hao’s band entered her ears.</p>

<p>“Then so be it.”</p>

<p>The instant the words were spoken, Meilin turned and threw herself into Qingyi’s arms, planting a passionate kiss on his lips.</p>

<p>With the kiss, the symphony became vibrant. The guests rose en masse, applauding the couple enthusiastically.</p>

<p>Finally, Meilin, the most talented and beloved daughter of the Gold King, was married.</p>

<p>Even political enemies clapped, displaying bitter, forced smiles. The Gold King had not only secured the support of a great talent, but also demonstrated the strength of his bond with the imperial family.</p>

<p>The couple’s lips parted, and they turned to the excited audience.</p>

<p>Qingyi’s other wives, above all, celebrated with shining eyes, focused intensely on Meilin, shouting congratulations to her.</p>

<p>“Hahaha! Let’s begin the banquet, honored guests! This party is just getting started!” Jin Hao laughed loudly beside Yunfeng, hugging Qingyi and Meilin.</p>

<p>The doors of the great hall opened and the Gold King’s servants entered carrying tables, followed by an endless succession of special</p>

<p>delicacies.</p>

<p>There was everything from the meat of spirit beasts from the Ancestral Emperor’s Realm to fish from the frozen lakes of the North. Even a dish of roasted phoenix was served exclusively to the most fortunate guests.</p>

<p>Qingyi and Meilin, unfortunately, still could not give themselves over to each other and had to face the tiresome work of socializing, ensuring that everything went perfectly.</p>

<p>Fortunately, everything comes to an end. As the food and drink satisfied those present, the guests gradually began to leave.</p>

<p>Shortly after midnight, the place was almost empty. Qingyi’s wives had already returned to the world of the mind, and he now carried</p>

<p>Meilin in his arms like a princess.</p>

<p>“Are you comfortable?” he asked as they arrived at the room prepared for their wedding night.</p>

<p>“Yes, thank you, hubby…” A shy smile spread across Meilin’s face. She closed her eyes, pressing her face against her husband’s chest.</p>

<p>With a smile, he lay down on the bed, and Meilin quickly nestled into</p>

<p>his arms.</p>

<p>“Husband, you seem pensive. What’s wrong?” Meilin asked, resting her hand on his chest.</p>

<p>“I…” Qingyi’s lips parted, but he immediately fell silent.</p>

<p>He shouldn’t disturb Meilin with these bad matters, not now.</p>

<p>Meilin’s face showed a subtle initial strangeness, but she decided not</p>

<p>to insist.</p>

<p>If Qingyi wouldn’t talk, it was because he didn’t think it was important enough at the moment.</p>

<p>Her eyes closed, letting exhaustion take over. That night, she didn’t want sex; she just wanted to smell and feel the warmth of her</p>

<p>husband’s protective embrace.</p>

<p>When Meilin finally fell asleep, a voice echoed in Qingyi’s mind.</p>

<p>[What’s wrong?] Ruxue asked.</p>

<p>[I sensed your emotions when you met the Emperor. What happened</p>

<p>between you?]</p>

<p>Qingyi thought for a moment before sighing mentally.</p>

<p>“The Enlightened Emperor has a mana heart, just like the Empress. Apparently, some members of the guard also have it!</p>

<p>[Are you assuming that…?]</p>

<p>‘Yes. He’s probably a demonic cultist. The influence of that damned</p>

<p>Heavenly Demon is much greater than we imagined…</p>

<p>[Ugh… that filthy pig! I’ve had so many chances to slit his throat. Why didn’t my first bearer ever do it? That stubborn woman…]</p>

<p>Qingyi felt Ruxue’s burning anger.</p>

<p>Although she tolerated and even had affection for Ruayn, a half-demon, her hatred for the demon race had not diminished. “Ah… I need to investigate this,” Qingyi repeated to himself silently.</p>

<p>If, in fact, the imperial family had already been corrupted by the celestial demon…</p>

<p>[Be careful, those demonic pigs don’t let their guard down easily]</p>

<p>Ruxue said, eliciting a sigh from Qingyi.</p>

<p>‘I’ll be careful.’</p>
