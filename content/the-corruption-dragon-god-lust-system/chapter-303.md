<p>‘Son of a bitch…’ Qingyi cursed mentally, clenching his fists, his eyes filled with a fury that hadn’t seized his body for a long time.</p>

<p>That look, full of contempt and arrogance.</p>

<p>He would rip it off Wang Haoran’s face, skin and all.</p>

<p>[Just to make sure you don’t chicken out and run away, duel me after the auction if you want this item!] A sound transmission reached Qingyi.</p>

<p>It was from Haoran, who expected to see Qingyi shudder and cower.</p>

<p>But that’s not what happened, quite the opposite.</p>

<p>Qingyi’s lips opened in a big smile, a weight being lifted from his heart.</p>

<p>He had already forgotten Haoran’s challenge.</p>

<p>He hadn’t lost anything, quite the opposite. He would beat an asshole to death and on top of that, he would win a new Ruxue fragment.</p>

<p>And he wouldn’t have to spend a single penny for it!</p>

<p>He was truly a man blessed by heaven, both with his wives and with his enemies.</p>

<p>‘Don’t worry, Brother Haoran… I’ll read fate to find out who your future wife is and sleep with her after I’ve finished with you, I’ll be sure to repay your kindness…’ Qingyi declared mentally, his heart filled with gratitude.</p>

<p>What a noble and kind man Wang Haoran was…</p>

<p>Shaking his head, Qingyi focused his eyes back on the auction.</p>

<p>It didn’t take long for the first items to be sold, with the second batch soon arriving.</p>

<p>These were already items of much higher quality, some selling for hundreds of thousands.</p>

<p>An earth-grade sword went for over two million to the third prince, and a space ring capable of holding up to five thousand cubic meters was bought for four million by the Wang family.</p>

<p>“This will go to my son, in commemoration of his recent breakthrough to the ethereal body realm, one of the youngest of his generation!” The family patriarch declared loudly, handing the ring to Wang Haoran.</p>

<p>Qingyi watched it all, lowering his head slightly.</p>

<p>Even a space ring for him… was there no end to the Wang family’s kindness? How did they know he needed a new space ring?</p>

<p>Qingyi sighed, unable to make any promises worth all the kindness the Wang family was showing him.</p>

<p>In the end, all he could do was lean back in his chair, feeling Meilin’s squeeze against him grow even stronger.</p>

<p>Time passed quickly as hundreds of items were sold and soon, it was time for the Feiyan pills.</p>

<p>Qingyi even regretted a little that he hadn’t brought the tanned beauty with him.</p>

<p>Knowing how much she loved money, he was sure she would love this auction.</p>

<p>“For the last item in the penultimate batch, we have something that is sure to excite many here…” The auctioneer flashed an elegant smile.</p>

<p>“Pills, but not ordinary pills. Pills capable of raising spiritual roots, affinities and even lineages and constitutions!</p>

<p>Their value alone isn’t much, but their purity is so extreme that the process of reverse engineering could easily lead to the discovery of their production method.</p>

<p>Think of an army of geniuses, just yours.”</p>

<p>The moment the auctioneer’s voice ceased, the whole auction erupted in murmurs.</p>

<p>Even the king’s box became slightly louder as the pills were unveiled.</p>

<p>Mortal-grade talent wasn’t much, even though they were already considered geniuses. But the purity of those pills changed everything.</p>

<p>Between 92 and 95%, with such a high purity, the chances of reverse engineering those pills skyrocketed.</p>

<p>“The opening bid is one hundred-”</p>

<p>“Five hundred thousand!” Before the auctioneer’s voice had even finished, one of the richest nobles in the kingdom stood up.</p>

<p>“One million!” The second prince stood up immediately afterwards.</p>

<p>“Two million!” The third prince also stood up.</p>

<p>If they could get those pills and the methods to mass-produce them… their armies would be unstoppable.</p>

<p>It didn’t take more than a few moments for the amount to pass five million, each bid more aggressive than the last.</p>

<p>Many nobles wanted it for personal reasons, while the princes mainly wanted it to stop it falling into each other’s hands.</p>

<p>It was only when the bids passed ten million that they suddenly stopped, an ancient voice echoing through the auction room.</p>

<p>“Fifteen million.”</p>

<p>The moment that bid echoed, everyone bowed their heads.</p>

<p>It came from the king himself. Even those who had the wealth to contest it decided it was better not to.</p>

<p>Slowly, the pills left the platform, floating up to the royal box.</p>

<p>The auctioneer didn’t lose her professionalism, her hips swaying gently as she unveiled the last batch, containing exactly five items.</p>

<p>“Don’t be sad about missing out on the last item… not when you still have the chance to get these rare pills to create great geniuses.” She revealed a small box with two golden pills inside.</p>

<p>The moment the first experts felt the aura exuded by those pills, before she even announced the price, the first bid was placed.</p>

<p>“Ten million!” The patriarch of the Wang family stood up, his bid quickly being covered by the patriarch of another wealthy family.</p>

<p>Qingyi glanced at Meilin’s beautiful face from the corner of his eye, watching her eyes light up as the bids passed thirty million.</p>

<p>Even considering the ability to create an earth-grade genius, she hadn’t imagined they’d get that much money.</p>

<p>“Thank you husband! I’ll make you the richest man in the entire immortal heaven.” Meilin hugged Qingyi, kissing him passionately.</p>

<p>“Even richer than your father?” The handsome young man smiled, slapping Meilin’s plump, shapely ass, feeling the voluptuous flesh ripple with shock.</p>

<p>“Nghn second richest…” Meilin blushed.</p>

<p>“Hahaha!” Qingyi laughed, the bidding for his item stopping just short of forty million, being snapped up by the King again.</p>

<p>With enough wealth to help Meilin get on her feet, now all Qingyi had to do was knock a few teeth out of that young master of the Wang family, also accepting the gifts that they had so kindly bought for Qingyi.</p>

<p>Jin Tianshi, Meilin’s brother, naturally noticed her happiness at seeing that bid, his face distorting as he called for a servant.</p>

<p>“Find out who put those pills up for auction, now!”</p>

<p>“But young master… your father forbids it-”</p>

<p>“I don’t care, I’m the one who pays your salary, so fucking obey!”</p>

<p>The servant bowed his head and left.</p>
