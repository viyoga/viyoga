<p>Mana Manipulation: Sixteenth Ring (25,000 G Lust Points to upgrade)</p>

<p>Affinities: Lightning: 20,500 - Sword: 20,500 - Fire: 20,500 - Ice: 20,500 - Space: 20,500 - Light: 20,500 - Shadow: 20,500 - Wind: 20,500 - Metal: 20,500 - Water: 20,500 - Earth: 20,500 - Charm: 50,000 (can be upgraded in the Lust Store)</p>

<p>Spiritual Roots: Celestial-level Primordial Creation Seed Spirit Root (Lightning, Sword, Fire, Ice, Wind, Metal, Water, Earth, Space, Light, Shadow) (100,000 G Lust Points to upgrade).</p>

<p>Spiritual Roots²: None / Celestial-level Primordial Creation Seed Mana Heart (100,000 G Lust Points to upgrade).</p>

<p>Lineage: Blood of the Corruption Dragon God (Celestial grade, 500,000 G Lust Points to upgrade.)</p>

<p>Constitution: Primordial Dragon Body (Celestial grade, 500,000 G Lust Points to upgrade.)</p>

<p>G Lust Points: 1,545]</p>

<p>Long Qingyi’s lips parted in a deep sigh. Almost at the same instant, he sensed dozens of powerful auras gathering around his cultivation chamber.</p>

<p>Massive pressures bore down from every direction, as if invisible beasts had assembled just to watch him.</p>

<p>They probably noticed the damage to the cultivation chamber and came to repair it.</p>

<p>When he opened his eyes, he realized the room hadn’t collapsed outright, but its condition was far from ideal.</p>

<p>Fine cracks snaked across the obsidian walls—fissures branching out like dark veins as the lingering Qi continued to tear through the space.</p>

<p>After a few moments, the aura of the place began to stabilize.</p>

<p>The cracks gradually faded, the stone regenerating on its own as deep waves of Qi coursed through it, mending each fissure with a soft, bluish glow.</p>

<p>"Ah..." Qingyi let out a satisfied sigh, slowly rising to his feet and rolling his neck from side to side.</p>

<p>This was a chamber built to withstand cultivators at the First Step of the Extinction Realm.</p>

<p>The fact that he—still only at the Cosmic Domain Realm—had managed to inflict this much damage on it was something even he hadn’t expected.</p>

<p>A faint smile pulled at his lips. He was happy. Excited.</p>

<p>His bloodline hadn’t awakened any new abilities, but that was to be expected.</p>

<p>Even before this latest upgrade, he had known that his bloodline possessed two distinct paths of evolution: grades of ascension and grades of stabilization.</p>

<p>This had been a grade of stabilization. Thus, though he hadn’t gained any new techniques, his bloodline had improved tremendously, and with it, so had his body.</p>

<p>His former self, before this improvement, couldn’t have withstood even a single punch from the current Qingyi.</p>

<p>With a soft sigh, Qingyi turned his gaze toward the white-covered book hovering silently a few meters above the ground. Its pages quivered faintly, as if breathing on their own.</p>

<p>It was time to open it, wasn’t it?</p>

<p>[I recommend you do this far from the sect’s territory, as there’s a chance you could trigger a celestial tribulation with this book.]</p>

<p>Another system notification echoed in his mind. Qingyi simply nodded in silence.</p>

<p>Under different circumstances, the idea would have discouraged him a little, and he would have preferred to wait until he returned to the Cosmic Dawn Sect.</p>

<p>It would take at least a few weeks aboard his ship to put enough distance between himself and the sect to feel safe attempting something of this magnitude. And that, of course, would delay his wedding to Rou’er, Xue Hua, and Bai Yunxi.</p>

<p>Fortunately, now that his affinity with spatial Qi had surpassed twenty thousand points, everything had changed.</p>

<p>He could feel his spatial senses extending far beyond their previous reach, every fragment of space around him crisp, sharp, almost tangible.</p>

<p>Qingyi closed his eyes, and his body was swallowed by an endless sea of shadows, dissolving silently into the air like ink washed away by water.</p>

<p>When his eyes opened again, they held a far more intense glow than before. An instant later, his body was engulfed by a powerful spatial distortion—an almost invisible rift that tore through the reality around him.</p>

<p>The entire Cosmic Dawn Sect was riddled with powerful spatial blockade formations, capable of detecting even stellar sovereigns and preventing anyone from teleporting without proper permission.</p>

<p>Still, for Qingyi, a single thought was enough.</p>

<p>He broke through the spatial barriers as if they didn’t even exist, emerging far beyond the planet, into the most distant regions of the solar system.</p>

<p>He continued teleporting, leap after leap, traversing vast stellar distances until twenty percent of his Qi had been consumed.</p>

<p>When he finally came to a halt, he had already crossed beyond the territory of the Myriad Beasts Sect, into a wild region claimed by no specific power.</p>

<p>Qingyi’s gaze swept across the abandoned solar system, searching for a region with a decent density of Qi.</p>

<p>The only one he found was the system’s own star, a white dwarf pulsing silently in the vacuum, exuding an incredibly dense and powerful Qi and mana.</p>

<p>Qingyi didn’t hesitate. He stopped a few kilometers from the star, floating above its surface, the cosmic heat lapping at his skin without causing him the slightest discomfort.</p>

<p>Cultivating using a star...</p>

<p>That was something he’d only ever dreamed of his entire life—at least until now.</p>

<p>He crossed his legs, his face hardening into an expression of pure determination.</p>

<p>Then, slowly aligning his own energy with the one radiating from the star, he retrieved the book Nine Paths of Creation Reversal Art from his spatial ring.</p>

<p>The white book materialized in his hands, and he gripped it tightly, sensing the powerful aura it exuded.</p>

<p>Before he could even try to open the book, a system notification made him stop.</p>
