<p>Qingyi suddenly stopped, closing his eyes and controlling the anger burning in his heart.</p>

<p>He didn’t want to be a hypocrite. He had done cruel things before, he had tortured men to the point of madness.</p>

<p>But he had never intentionally hurt anyone who hadn’t put his life or the lives of his wives at risk.</p>

<p>He had never killed an innocent by choice.</p>

<p>But for Xue Mo, killing those innocent people was little different from crushing an insect.</p>

<p>How could anyone be so unnecessarily cruel?</p>

<p>“Oh… he’s hesitating! Looks like your servant isn’t so good, hahaha!” The voluptuous woman laughed, approaching Qingyi and ignoring Xue Mo’s bitter face.</p>

<p>She reached out her hand, trying to grab him, but her movement stopped at the last moment.</p>

<p>Her eyes widened and a splash of blood hit her face.</p>

<p>Her other siblings had the exact same look of shock as their sister’s severed hand fell to the ground.</p>

<p>That had been a surprise attack and she hadn’t even had time to defend herself with her Qi, but even so, it shouldn’t have happened.</p>

<p>The sword of any other cultivator of Qingyi’s level would have bounced off her skin or, in the worst case, stopped before it even reached the bone.</p>

<p>How sharp was that blade?</p>

<p>“AH! You son of a bitch! I will-” The voluptuous woman’s words were interrupted when the heaven-defying thunder sword tore through her lips and jaw, surrounded by a thunderous aura covered in silver threads of sword Qi.</p>

<p>The blade broke through the inside of her throat, coming out the back of her head with a spray of crimson blood.</p>

<p>Qingyi had expected a lot of reactions from Xue Mo, but not the one she got.</p>

<p>“Ugh… killed by a Nascent Soul Realm cultivator. Trash.” The young lord spat on the corpse, waving to his brothers still in shock. “What are you waiting for, get rid of that little shit.”</p>

<p>The two exchanged glances, hesitating for a brief moment before advancing towards Qingyi.</p>

<p>’Good… that makes things a lot easier.’ Qingyi smiled as he took a defensive stance, watching the two advance towards him.</p>

<p>As children of the demon lord, they naturally had access to better techniques, something that became very clear when they activated their movement techniques.</p>

<p>Qingyi briefly lost sight of them, only being able to see them clearly when he activated his draconic eyes.</p>

<p>At that moment, they were already upon him, their weapons covered in demonic Qi as they sliced through the air.</p>

<p>The handsome young man barely managed to dodge as the two blades passed inches from his face.</p>

<p>He activated the lightning steps, retreating more than a hundred meters in the blink of an eye.</p>

<p>The shockwave from the brothers’ joint attack reached him immediately afterwards, causing his clothes to flutter.</p>

<p>“I’ll kill you for touching our sister, you insolent worm!” The burly brother roared, his great sword filled with a demonic aura as he raised it over his head.</p>

<p>“Don’t let your guard down, idiot, this is no simple enemy!” The smallest of the brothers spoke, but his words were unnecessary.</p>

<p>Deciding it was time to put an end to it, Qingyi touched his draconic Qi, using his lineage projection.</p>

<p>Xue Mo seemed uninterested in the battle, being more annoyed at having to change his personal servant again than at the fact that his sister had just died.</p>

<p>Even the fact that that servant had lightning sword Qi along with fire and ice Qi caused him little shock.</p>

<p>But all that disinterest vanished the moment he noticed the change in Qingyi’s body, his blood-red eyes focused on the black figure that appeared behind the handsome young man’s body.</p>

<p>It was a dragon; or rather, the head of a dragon. Big enough to swallow a small house, each sharp fang easily the size of a man.</p>

<p>Xue Mo trembled under the penetrating gaze of that purple-eyed beast, something that immediately filled him with shock.</p>

<p>’I-I’m afraid?’ Xue Mo looked at his own hands, feeling the lineage of the celestial demon inside him.</p>

<p>No… It wasn’t just him who was trembling.</p>

<p>His bloodline was trembling!</p>

<p>It was being suppressed!</p>

<p>The thought immediately made his heart sink.</p>

<p>How could that be possible?</p>

<p>Qingyi was completely oblivious to Xue Mo’s thoughts, focused on the other two brothers who had been completely suppressed by the image of the black dragon on their backs.</p>

<p>“Ready to die?” Qingyi flashed a broad, strangely gentle smile, as if he were meeting old friends, not preparing for a massacre.</p>

<p>Out of the corner of his eye, he could see the merchants fleeing.</p>

<p>From the distance they were already, he could fight at full strength without the danger of them falling into the crossfire.</p>

<p>“You bastard!” The burly man finally mustered the strength to bring his blade down in a descending attack, a sea of demonic flames flying towards Qingyi, consuming everything around him.</p>

<p>The handsome young man was undeterred, readying his sword.</p>

<p>With a soft sigh, he activated the lightning steps, using the first form of the Tempest Monarch’s sword art.</p>

<p>The corpulent man’s already unstable Qi shattered under Qingyi’s attack, his eyes filling with terror as the handsome young man’s blade tore through his chest.</p>

<p>Without wasting a single moment, Qingyi struck again, using the second form of the Tempest Monarch sword art against the other brother, whose decapitated body soon fell to the ground with a deafening thud.</p>

<p>Soon, only Qingyi and Xue Mo remained.</p>

<p>’Ruxue, are you sure your fragment is with him?’ Qingyi asked, just to confirm.</p>

<p>“Yes,” Ruxue replied.</p>

<p>“You…” Xue Mo took a step forward while gripping the handle of his sword.</p>

<p>A sharp hiss echoed as the crimson blade was revealed, shimmering softly.</p>

<p>“Who the hell are you?” Xue Mo growled, his eyes filled with murderous intent.</p>

<p>“I…” Qingyi seemed to think for a brief moment before grabbing his mask and pulling it off.</p>

<p>His face of transcendental beauty soon revealed itself, the strands of demonic Qi in his Qi disappearing completely.</p>

<p>“I’m Long Qingyi, your sister’s man. She asked me to deal with a disgusting, incestuous pig.”</p>
