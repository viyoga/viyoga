<p>“Long Qingyi, my only disciple, I need you to remember my teachings.” An ancient voice echoed through a small hut.</p>

<p>The owner of that voice was sitting on a stone pedestal, his aged hands pressed the back of a young man.</p>

<p>“That bloodline will try to corrupt you, it will promise you the power to dominate all affinities and…” The old man paused, scratching his chin.</p>

<p>“All the beauties in the world…” He concluded, turning his flushed face away.</p>

<p>“But never fall prey to these temptations. Don’t give in to these seductive words.” He put some more strength into his voice.</p>

<p>“I’ve abandoned the path of cultivation and so must you. I know it’s difficult, but you must sacrifice your future for the good of the world.”</p>

<p>He continued, not noticing Qingyi’s expression darkening at every word.</p>

<p>“Keep your chastity intact and, when you are ready to leave this world, find a worthy disciple, willing to follow this path and prevent this profane bloodline from falling into the wrong hands.”</p>

<p>“Yes, master, I will not let you down and I will not allow the world to be corrupted!” Qingyi spoke, his tone was pure and gentle, drawing a smile from the old man.</p>

<p>he had really found an admirable disciple.</p>

<p>Thinking for a brief moment, the old man shook his head.</p>

<p>He wanted to train Qingyi more, to allow his esteemed disciple to reach at least the foundation realm before forcing him to abandon everything, but time was running out.</p>

<p>On the verge of death, he needed to transfer his bloodline and constitution as soon as possible.</p>

<p>“Let’s begin. It will hurt a little, but you have to be strong.” The old man said</p>

<p>“I will be strong, master!” Qingyi nodded.</p>

<p>In the next instant, the young mang felt the cold, wrinkled hands on his back warm up.</p>

<p>Suddenly, his cultivation of the sixth stage of the Qi refinement realm shattered and every cell in his body screamed in agony.</p>

<p>Qingyi tried to open his mouth, he wanted to scream and cry, but he couldn’t.</p>

<p>The Qi in the room exploded, the ground shook and his body levitated, all the old master’s power flowing into him.</p>

<p>“Stay conscious, don’t faint!” His master’s thunderous voice pulled him out of the pain and he clenched his teeth, resisting with all his strength.</p>

<p>Hours passed until the air around him calmed down and he collapsed on the floor.</p>

<p>“Crap…” Qingyi groaned as he stood up.</p>

<p>He soon noticed his clothes tightening around his body, almost tearing.</p>

<p>With a sigh, he looked outside the hut.</p>

<p>They had started the in the afternoon, but it was already dawn the next day.</p>

<p>Turning around, Qingyi came across the motionless body of his old master. His eyes were open, but empty, with no sign of breathing on his chest.</p>

<p>Hesitating briefly, Qingyi picked up a stick from the ground.</p>

<p>“Master… are you alive?” he poked the corpse with the stick, first in the chest and then in the face.</p>

<p>He pressed a little harder and felt his heart shudder as the old man’s body toppled backwards.</p>

<p>“He’s dead…” Qingyi covered his mouth, his face overcome with shock before bursting into uncontrollable laughter.</p>

<p>“Fucking finally! I’m free!” He roared, wiping away the tears that had built up in his eyes. Twelve years of suffering were finally over!</p>

<p>His life was worse than living on the streets or being an office worker on Earth, working like a slave and living under constant surveillance.</p>

<p>That damned old man even came close to castrating him just because he had looked at an senior sister a few moments too long!</p>

<p>Qingyi was only ten years old at the time! How on earth could that old man see malice in a child’s gaze at his gentle older sister?!</p>

<p>Just because his eyes were on those huge tits didn’t mean he was a pervert! Where else could he look?</p>

<p>Those behemoths were massive! Not even the best implants in his past life were as perfect as her natural breasts!</p>

<p>Qingyi had to spend months trying to prove to his master that he was a pure young man, not a perverted transmigrant from another world.</p>

<p>It was hard work, he suffered a lot, but it was worth every effort.</p>

<p>Standing up, he examined his reflection in a bronze mirror.</p>

<p>’God… I’m even more handsome than the master told me I would be!’ he exclaimed mentally.</p>

<p>He had grown more than twenty centimeters, reaching a height of one meter ninety. His eyes took on a sharp look, and his face, although undeniably masculine, displayed delicate features.</p>

<p>His dark eyes matched with the black hair that now reached his shoulders. Underneath his clothes, he found powerful sculpted muscles.</p>

<p>Qingyi wasn’t ugly before the bloodline transfer ritual, but now, he displayed a beauty that would dominate any woman’s heart with a single smile.</p>

<p>With a confident smile, Qingyi glanced between his pants, admiring the size of what he found.</p>

<p>“It’s almost doubled in size!” he thought in amazement.</p>

<p>Walking excitedly, he changed his clothes, putting on a pure white cloak, like his master’s.</p>

<p>He wanted to change that color, but all the clothes in that hut were like that, some nonsense that his master used to say: “pure men should only wear white.”</p>

<p>Qingyi carefully collected everything he thought was useful and prepared to leave the hut.</p>

<p>Before leaving, however, he looked at the old man’s body.</p>

<p>The corpse was in a strange position, fallen from the platform where they were sitting, with its head pressed against the ground, its back on the platform and its bottom raised high.</p>

<p>“I should give him a proper burial…” he sighed before grabbing the body, trying to move it.</p>

<p>“Ugh… why is this bastard so heavy?” He complained after a few unsuccessful attempts.</p>

<p>He then felt the aged flesh give way under his fingers and quickly stepped back, disgusted.</p>

<p>Although his master had died just that night, his body appeared to have been decomposing for days.</p>

<p>With an expression of revulsion, Qingyi shook his head.</p>

<p>He had only made matters worse.</p>

<p>The old man was now completely on the floor, face down, his face sinking into the damp boards while his bent knees suggestively raised his bottom.</p>

<p>“Ugh… I think it’ll do…” Qingyi fought the nausea and simply picked up a blanket, throwing it over his master’s body as he looked away.</p>

<p>“This disciple thanks the master for all his teachings.” He clenched his fists in a respectful gesture before leaving.</p>

<p>When he took a step out of the hut, Qingyi was greeted by two translucent screens carrying texts in a strange language, but which he seemed to be able to understand perfectly.</p>

<p>Observing them, he smiled. His suffering was finally over…</p>
