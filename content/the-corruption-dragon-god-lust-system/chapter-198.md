<p>“Fuck…” Qingyi closed his eyes, an intense pain taking over his mind and forcing him to clench his teeth.</p>

<p>It was as if his very brain was slowly tearing itself apart, every drop of his being screaming in agony.</p>

<p>It was horrible, but he resisted, moving his draconic Qi and forcing his bloodline to activate.</p>

<p>The moment the colossal figure of a black dragon’s head appeared on Qingyi’s back, the thread of Qi holding that demonic cultivator’s mental attack together broke, crushed by Qingyi’s draconic Qi.</p>

<p>“Is that… bloodline projection?” The demonic cultivator questioned as he took a step back, feeling a shiver run down his spine.</p>

<p>He didn’t lock up like all his men, but he still felt his Qi severely suppressed and his body shuddered gently.</p>

<p>“Ah…” Qingyi sighed, his eyes sweeping over the demonic cultivators around him as he counted. “100… 300… 400…”</p>

<p>“Khaedryss… looks like you’re going to have a full meal today.” Qingyi flashed a bloody smile.</p>

<p>“Damn, what the hell are you talking about? You’re going to die here!” The demonic cultivator exploded as he released as much of his Nascent Soul Realm aura as he could.</p>

<p>The ground beneath his feet cracked, a long saber appearing in his hands as he advanced towards Qingyi.</p>

<p>The handsome young man didn’t even blink at that power and the heaven-defying thunder sword flashed brightly.</p>

<p>The moment the demonic cultivator got within ten meters of Qingyi, he realized his mistake.</p>

<p>A colossal jaw appeared in front of him, sharp teeth flashing menacingly as they closed around his abdomen.</p>

<p>He tried to scream and struggle, but stopped when a torrent of fire poured between Khaedryss’ clenched teeth, cooking him alive before swallowing him.</p>

<p>“Good girl.” Qingyi patted her muzzle, turning to the other demonic cultivators. “What are you waiting for? Take care of them!”</p>

<p>The moment Qingyi’s voice ceased, Khaedryss lunged forward, her jaw opening in a bestial roar as her flames consumed everything in front of her.</p>

<p>Her rider had truly prepared a great feast for her and she had to admit that little Elize chose her men very well.</p>

<p>“So… now it’s just you and me.” Qingyi smiled at young master Zhao, who immediately tried to run away.</p>

<p>’I should have listened to Dad, damn it, why the hell didn’t I listen to Dad?’ He repeated in his mind.</p>

<p>Unfortunately, it was too late to whine or repent.</p>

<p>Qingyi was already in front of him, his eyes filled with a murderous intent so powerful that it was almost palpable.</p>

<p>“You came after me and not my wife, so you’ll die quickly.” Qingyi kicked young master Zhao Yulie, knocking him to the ground and keeping him there.</p>

<p>“P-please, I made a mistake! I-I didn’t mean to-”</p>

<p>“Yes, you meant to.” Qingyi pressed his foot against Yulie’s chest even harder, eliciting a pained cry from him as his ribcage cracked.</p>

<p>“And you’re going to tell me exactly what those demon cultivators wanted.”</p>

<p>Qingyi completely ignored Yulie’s cries as he questioned him, ignoring the cries of despair from the demon cultivators who were trying to escape from Khaedryss.</p>

<p>Unfortunately, Yulie didn’t know much beyond the fact that this was a war band of demonic cultivators operating in the vicinity of White Peak City and that they were interested in the box as a catalyst for a ritual.</p>

<p>It wasn’t exactly a valuable item in the grand scheme of things, at least not for the truly powerful sects, but it still had its value.</p>

<p>With nothing left to take from Yulie, Qingyi simply swung the heaven-defying thunder sword, decapitating him.</p>

<p>By this time, Khaedryss had eaten all she could eat, her body burning with power as her stomach dissolved her victims.</p>

<p>That was Khaedryss’ greatest strength – even without being able to cultivate, she could still get stronger by eating her victims.</p>

<p>Stroking the underside of her neck, Qingyi turned his face away, focusing on the fight on the horizon.</p>

<p>The sky was filled with Rongyan’s flames as she punched, the air shuddering for hundreds of meters around her with every blow exchanged.</p>

<p>Normally, what had happened below them wouldn’t escape the senses of cultivators of their level, but they were too focused on each other to notice anything else.</p>

<p>” You bastard!” Rongyan roared, her fists blazing with flames as she punched towards the shadow cultivator.</p>

<p>He just dodged, his body melting into a sea of shadows before reappearing behind Rongyan, kicking her in the back and sending her flying for hundreds of meters until she stabilized, coughing up a gulp of blood.</p>

<p>That was one hell of an opponent to fight against, especially for her whose entire power was based solely on destructive explosiveness.</p>

<p>“Why don’t you give up, little beauty?” The shadowy figure spoke, his dark eyes roaming over Rongyan. “I’ll put that body to good use after this.”</p>

<p>“Fuck you!” She snarled, gathering all her power, her aura expanding uncontrollably as her hair fluttered.</p>

<p>She had made a decision; if she couldn’t hit him, she would blow up everything around her.</p>

<p>Her power continued to increase unabated, her meridians screaming in agony.</p>

<p>“You’re going to die today!” She roared, finally releasing all that power.</p>

<p>A fireball consumed everything within a radius of two kilometers before disappearing, leaving an exhausted Rongyan behind.</p>

<p>She looked down, searching for Qingyi, but was soon stopped by an annoying, smiling voice.</p>

<p>“An incredible attack, little beauty… You almost hurt me!” The shadowy figure laughed as he pointed his hand towards Rongyan’s delicate neck.</p>

<p>But before he even got close to her, he stopped, looking down with his eyes wide with terror.</p>

<p>He had no time to defend himself, Khaedryss’ jaw closing around his body and devouring him.</p>

<p>Only his hand remained, thrown away in an explosion of blood.</p>

<p>“Is that…” Rongyan whispered, overcome by shock as she watched the huge beast that Qingyi was riding.</p>

<p>“Is everything all right, senior? I’ve already dealt with the pigs down there.” Qingyi spoke as Khaedryss controlled her flight so that he was at the same height as Rongyan.</p>

<p>The beauty opened her mouth to reply, but no words came out.</p>

<p>Her vision suddenly blurred and she collapsed.</p>
