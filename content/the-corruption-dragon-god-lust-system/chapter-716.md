<p>Bai Hu Lie and Qingyi spent nearly four hours in the company of Bai Hu Tian, alongside the leader of the Cosmic Dawn Sect, Rou’er, and Rouxi.</p>

<p>During those four hours, they drank some of the strongest liquor the leader had to offer, and a thoroughly drunk Rou’er nearly tore Qingyi’s clothes off in the process.</p>

<p>She wanted to stay by his side rather than attend another boring meeting with Rouxi. Even so, Qingyi and Bai Hu Lie eventually managed to slip away.</p>

<p>Straightening his clothes, Qingyi let out a long sigh.</p>

<p>He had no problem with Rou’er accompanying him. Quite the opposite.</p>

<p>He’d rather have her by his side at all times, but in the end, she was the princess of the Cosmic Dawn Sect, and her diplomatic responsibilities couldn’t be ignored.</p>

<p>Fortunately, Rouxi was right beside her, along with her adoptive father at the peak of the Stellar Sovereign Realm, and Bai Hu Tian himself, the strongest cultivator in the entire region.</p>

<p>She was well protected, that was for sure.</p>

<p>“Ah… my sister must be waiting, let’s hurry!” Bai Hu Lie said, taking the lead with eager strides.</p>

<p>Finally, it was time to introduce his sister’s new husband to her!</p>

<p>It was a bit strange that she would be the last to meet her own husband, but in the end, none of them thought much of it.</p>

<p>After a few hours of walking through the sect’s endless corridors, they finally arrived at Bai Yunxi’s pavilion.</p>

<p>“It looks like there was a fight here,” Qingyi observed, his eyes tracing a long crack that split nearly the entire front wall of the pavilion.</p>

<p>It had clearly been caused by a powerful impact from the inside. The edges of the crack still emanated a faint residue of energy, making the air hum softly.</p>

<p>“Hm…” Bai Hu Lie sniffed the air and then burst into loud laughter.</p>

<p>“It was that stupid white swordsman, hahahaha! Looks like he had to learn the true terror of my little sister the hard way!”</p>

<p>Without wasting a moment, Bai Hu Lie stormed into the pavilion, kicking the gates open with a deafening crash that rattled the surrounding pillars.</p>

<p>Puffing out his chest, he roared at the top of his lungs.</p>

<p>“LITTLE SISTER YUNXI! YOUR DEAR, VENERABLE, AND BELOVED BIG BROTHER HAS BROUGHT YOU YOUR FUTURE AND ETERNAL MAN-”</p>

<p>Bai Hu Lie didn’t finish his roar.</p>

<p>A book tore through the air at absurd speed and slammed straight into his face with a powerful thud, the impact echoing across the entrance hall.</p>

<p>The tiger shuddered violently, toppling backward with his eyes spinning in dizzy spirals.</p>

<p>“You damn brat! Haven’t I told you not to shout in my residence?!”</p>

<p>Bai Yunxi, sitting casually in the shade of a tree, roared back at her brother, the veins bulging on her forehead.</p>

<p>The anger on her face, however, lasted only until she saw Qingyi.</p>

<p>The handsome young man also froze upon seeing her.</p>

<p>Her appearance was… unique.</p>

<p>Her skin was clearly not human, a deep, pure white that resembled freshly pressed paper.</p>

<p>Her eyes were a crystalline blue, and her delicate face was adorned with soft pink lips and a small, dainty nose.</p>

<p>Her voluptuous body was covered in black stripes that seemed to dance across her pale skin with every breath, like living tattoos.</p>

<p>Qingyi turned his head slightly, catching the title of the book Bai Yunxi had thrown.</p>

<p>Was it… a romance novel for teenage girls?</p>

<p>He didn’t have time to dwell on it, for Bai Yunxi was already standing right in front of him.</p>

<p>Her plump white tits squashed against his chest, the soft mounds molding to his form as the beauty leaned in even closer, her face mere inches from his while she studied his features with sharp curiosity.</p>

<p>“So you were my brother’s choice, huh?” She narrowed her crystalline eyes, her sweet, savage breath invading Qingyi’s nostrils.</p>

<p>Qingyi met her gaze, admiring the soft, refined features of her face and her long white hair, so voluminous it almost resembled a lion’s mane.</p>

<p>Combined with her round, gentle face, she looked surprisingly adorable.</p>

<p>“I approve of you!” Bai Yunxi finally spoke, a bright smile spreading across her lips. “Let’s make babies now—I want at least two on our first try!”</p>

<p>Inside Ruxue’s world of mind, things were calm.</p>

<p>Night had already fallen, and after finishing their daily chores, without their husband to keep them company, the girls had retreated to their rooms, cultivating or resting.</p>

<p>Ruyan was one of them.</p>

<p>Lying on her back, she wore a sweet, relaxed expression, her crimson eyes fixed on the ceiling with a bored gleam.</p>

<p>“Ah… my hubby should come visit me soon… my butt doesn’t feel right after going too long without a spanking!” Ruyan complained with a soft grunt, but soon laughed at herself.</p>

<p>Well, she’d been with him long enough not to care about those embarrassing things anymore.</p>

<p>Not that she’d ever cared much about them to begin with.</p>

<p>She’d always been naughty, and their first real interaction had been Qingyi spanking her butt.</p>

<p>“Ah~ hubby spanked me so hard that day!” she exclaimed, jumping up from her bed with an excited hop.</p>

<p>A big smile played on her blood-red lips, and a mischievous gleam shone in her eyes of the same color.</p>

<p>But all of that stopped suddenly.</p>

<p>Her eyes widened, and she looked down.</p>

<p>There was something strange…</p>

<p>“My demonic energy…” She pressed a hand to her own Dantian, feeling it leaking strangely through her meridians.</p>

<p>It was as if it were beyond her control, responding to a will that wasn’t her own.</p>

<p>Ruyan staggered, her legs buckling beneath her weight.</p>

<p>Her face, usually full of nothing but mischief and fun, quickly paled. Her eyes fluttered shut as she nearly collapsed to the floor.</p>

<p>With shaky steps, she stumbled out of her room, just in time to spot Nianxue in the courtyard, in exactly the same state, her Qi leaking nonstop as if something were sucking it out of her.</p>

<p>Ruxue was the first to notice it, her eyes filling with worry as she teleported to the courtyard.</p>

<p>All the other girls caught on soon after, rushing toward the two demons.</p>

<p>“What’s happening?” Ruxue asked, carefully spreading her Qi senses over the two of them.</p>

<p>“I don’t know, it’s just that… Blergh.”</p>

<p>Blood streamed down Ruyan’s lips in a crimson torrent, staining the fine fabric of her robes a dark red as her cultivation nearly collapsed, sending her crashing toward the ground.</p>

<p>Fortunately, the girls were there to catch her, their expressions twisting with even deeper concern.</p>

<p>None of them had ever seen the lively Ruyan looking so pale and battered.</p>

<p>Tears welled in the demonic beauty’s eyes as she clutched her own chest, each breath rasping out shakily between her blood-stained lips.</p>

<p>Nianxue, who had been holding up a little better, soon nearly collapsed as well, and the girls rushed to catch her as quickly as possible.</p>

<p>What they didn’t know was that across a certain region of the planet controlled by the Guo family, every demon was suffering the exact same reaction.</p>

<p>Something big was about to happen.</p>
