<p>After the emperor left, Qingyi had to endure a seemingly endless series of rituals.</p>

<p>Finally, they confirmed what was necessary: the pendant had accepted him.</p>

<p>In reality, Qingyi had been rejected in almost all of these rituals.</p>

<p>However, all it took was a slight trace of his bloodline Qi to suddenly solve the entire problem.</p>

<p>Apparently, even magical arrays recognized that, sometimes, it was better to give in so as not to be shattered.</p>

<p>After all this, Qingyi had a long conversation with the bishop.</p>

<p>There, he learned more about the current political situation of the Rose Empire and his true mission.</p>

<p>Of course, the bishop did not say too much.</p>

<p>All Qingyi knew was that, in some way, it had something to do with the ancestral spirit that he and Aeryn had eliminated in that forest.</p>

<p>After all that, Qingyi was released.</p>

<p>He even received his own residence in the capital, decided by the church itself.</p>

<p>Their goddess must have been fuming to see her followers treating so well someone she hated so much.</p>

<p>But honestly, it wasn’t exactly a luxurious place.</p>

<p>Still, it was certainly more than enough for an imperial middle-class family to live in.</p>

<p>It was a two-story concrete and wood building.</p>

<p>The house had two bathrooms and two bedrooms, as well as a kitchen</p>

<p>and a nicely furnished living room.</p>

<p>It was one of many houses that the church kept ready for mid-level officials and priests.</p>

<p>After making sure everything was in order and checking that there were no magical spying devices, Qingyi left.</p>

<p>But now, he was accompanied.</p>

<p>At his right side was Jin Meilin, whose noble face and beautiful black eyes showed deep impression.</p>

<p>She really couldn’t believe that one day in her life she would be able to see a city whose glory, size, and beauty were equivalent to that of the Illuminated Imperial Capital.</p>

<p>Margareth, on the other hand, had lived in the capital for a long time.</p>

<p>Right now, she kept her eyes focused on one of the many magical towers surrounding the city.</p>

<p>When she left this region, she had barely reached the fifth ring.</p>

<p>Although talented, she was simply not comparable to the true great geniuses.</p>

<p>She was very lucky to have even been selected by the church to manage those lands in the east.</p>

<p>But now, everything had changed.</p>

<p>She had recently reached the ninth ring, just before Isabel Valemont reached the seventh ring.</p>

<p>But that wasn’t all.</p>

<p>Although Feiyan couldn’t make pills for her, Qingyi still bought enough pills for her to raise her mana heart to the transcendent level.</p>

<p>She was no longer just an above-average talent.</p>

<p>Qingyi noticed her gaze and brought his hands to her long golden hair, caressing it.</p>

<p>“If you want, we can go there soon. I haven’t been given any</p>

<p>responsibilities yet,” Qingyi said.</p>

<p>“But first, we have to go to the Five Colors Pavilion. Your husband needs to cultivate a little, hahaha.”</p>

<p>Qingyi laughed loudly, planting a kiss on Margareth’s cheek.</p>

<p>Ignoring the envious glances, he simply walked toward the huge building on the horizon.</p>

<p>The Five Colors Pavilion branch in the Rose Empire was simply incomparable to the branch in the Blue Sea Kingdom.</p>

<p>It was easily twice the size and was guarded by an Ancestral Emperor.</p>

<p>The moment Qingyi, Margareth, and Meilin entered there, the air</p>

<p>around them changed.</p>

<p>From the darkness that took over the floor, a figure emerged.</p>

<p>That was one of the Shadows of the Gold King.</p>

<p>“Young Miss Meilin, it is an honor to have you here.”</p>

<p>The shadow bowed, turning its face toward Qingyi.</p>

<p>‘So that was the man Young Miss Meilin had chosen, a True Immortal despite being only in his early twenties. That wasn’t bad at all.’</p>

<p>“Young Master Qingyi.” The shadow bowed again.</p>

<p>“I want to talk to my father, are the communication arrays working?” Meilin asked, stepping forward.</p>

<p>“Certainly, young lady.”</p>

<p>The shadow spoke and quickly guided them to the upper floors and</p>

<p>then to his office.</p>

<p>There wasn’t even a hint of displeasure on his face as he gave up his chair to Jin Meilin.</p>

<p>At the end of the day, he was a servant of the Gold King, but also a</p>

<p>servant of the Gold King’s children.</p>

<p>Unless it was directly against an order from the Gold King himself, the</p>

<p>word of his children was law.</p>

<p>Of course, this didn’t apply to everyone.</p>

<p>In the end, only those whom the shadows saw value in could be</p>

<p>treated so well.</p>

<p>The shadows’ only obligation to the rest was to keep them alive and breathing, nothing more.</p>

<p>While Meilin and Margareth made contact with the other continent,</p>

<p>the shadow guided Qingyi to one of the many cultivation rooms in</p>

<p>that huge building.</p>

<p>There, the handsome young man locked himself in.</p>

<p>Honestly, the Qi density wasn’t good.</p>

<p>This was probably due to the lack of spirit veins and the fact that this</p>

<p>cultivation room was only maintained by superior Qi crystals.</p>

<p>But Qingyi didn’t think much of it.</p>

<p>It was, at least, infinitely superior to cultivating in an environment</p>

<p>where the Qi around him was depleted with every breath he took.</p>

<p>With a soft sigh, Qingyi knelt down, opening his status screen.</p>

<p>[Name: Long Qingyi</p>

<p>Age: 21</p>

<p>Cultivation: First Stage Of The True Immortal Realm (5T lust points to</p>

<p>upgrade)</p>

<p>Affinities: Lightning: 1500 – Sword: 1000 – Fire: 1500 – Ice: 1500 –</p>

<p>Space: 1500 Light: 1500 Shadow: 1500 – Charm: 1500 (can be</p>

<p>upgraded in the lust store)</p>

<p>Spiritual roots: Immortal-level Primordial Creation Seed spirit root</p>

<p>(Lightning, sword, fire, ice, space, light, shadow) (10T lust points to upgrade, new roots can be bought in the lust store).</p>

<p>Lineage: Blood of the Corruption Dragon God (Transcendental grade,</p>

<p>10T lust points to upgrade.)</p>

<p>Constitution: Primordial Dragon Body (Transcendental grade, 10T lust points to upgrade.)</p>

<p>Lust points: 65,562,736,930]</p>

<p>Those numbers didn’t exactly make him excited, but he could only</p>

<p>shake his head.</p>

<p>Sure, he could have a few nights of sex with his wives, which would</p>

<p>be more than enough to make him rich again.</p>

<p>But Qingyi preferred to take care of the most urgent matter for him</p>

<p>first.</p>

<p>He had acquired new spiritual roots, but he had not acquired any techniques that utilized these new roots.</p>
