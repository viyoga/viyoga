<p>Qingyi raised his blade, protecting himself from Jiantian’s attack. His feet sank into the ground as an explosion of metallic sparks filled the air.</p>

<p>He gritted his teeth and used the first form of the Tempest Monarch’s sword art, pushing Jiantian back with a thunderous, powerful thrust.</p>

<p>Taking a moment to himself, Qingyi inhaled deeply.</p>

<p>Even being suppressed, the power of a cultivator at the peak of the false immortal was still frightening, but fortunately, it wasn’t enough.</p>

<p>With a gentle smile, Qingyi attacked again. His body split into afterimages as his blade flew towards Jiantian’s neck.</p>

<p>The Wang family patriarch barely managed to dodge, a thin trickle of blood running down his neck.</p>

<p>“What’s the matter, little cuckold Wang Jiantian? Don’t you want to face your grandpa here directly?” teased Qingyi, bursting into laughter.</p>

<p>“Don’t you dare call me that even once more!” roared Patriarch Wang, charging towards Qingyi.</p>

<p>“Or what?” The provocation in Qingyi’s voice died away abruptly, drowned out by the clang of blades meeting and the roar of the shockwave that spread through the forest.</p>

<p>The soft ground beneath their feet sank into a crater, with cracks spreading for dozens of meters.</p>

<p>Suddenly, Jiantian shuddered. The suppression he felt from Qingyi’s bloodline grew even stronger, accompanied by an intense murderous intent.</p>

<p>“Tell me, little cuckold Jiantian, what are you going to do? Piss your pants like your son did before he died?”</p>

<p>Qingyi asked again, putting more strength into his grip and sending Jiantian away.</p>

<p>He watched Jiantian’s face contort in fury, his Qi exploding with full force around his body, licks of flame taking over everything.</p>

<p>“You… fucking son of a bitch!” roared Jiantian, readying his sword, a hurricane of flames surrounding the blade.</p>

<p>“You’ll pay for this with a thousand – no, TEN THOUSAND DEATHS! YOUR SOUL WILL NEVER REST!”</p>

<p>Despite the threats, Qingyi hardly cared. In fact, he was happy.</p>

<p>The less self-control Jiantian had over his own Qi, the easier his fight would be.</p>

<p>He retracted his heaven-defying thunder sword. Exactly thirty-two ethereal, thunderous blades surrounded his body, their powerful lightning Qi making the surrounding air boil.</p>

<p>In that brief moment, their eyes met and they both attacked.</p>

<p>From Jiantian’s sword, a thread of flame emerged, slashing towards Qingyi, offering no resistance to the thirty-two thundering blades.</p>

<p>Qingyi surrounded his body with a wall of ice, which held off the attack for a brief moment before giving way, forcing him to hold back the blow with only the black dragon’s scales, driving his sword into the ground.</p>

<p>Jiantian’s face was filled with ecstasy as he watched Qingyi being engulfed in flames, narrowly dodging the many swords that flew towards him.</p>

<p>He barely noticed when they suddenly stopped flying, the ethereal metal that made them up swelling before exploding into balls of lightning, swallowing everything within dozens of meters.</p>

<p>When the explosions caused by both attacks stopped, they revealed two figures.</p>

<p>Qingyi was almost intact, except for a few burns on the black dragon scales.</p>

<p>Jiantian, on the other hand, was on his knees, his clothes torn, his hair reduced to burnt ends and his eyes filled with deep shock.</p>

<p>He was a false immortal, destined to become a true immortal and live for at least fifty thousand years.</p>

<p>How could he not get rid of that little bug with ease? How could he be suffering against Qingyi?</p>

<p>He wouldn’t just die, would he? Without even avenging his son’s death?</p>

<p>Such a thought immediately filled Jiantian with horror, more horror than even that huge dragon caused him.</p>

<p>His beloved Haoran… he had to avenge him, no matter what.</p>

<p>Wang Jiantian’s Qi changed once again, this time powerful enough to make Qingyi take a step backwards, a pillar of flame rising up to the heavens.</p>

<p>It only took Qingyi a single moment to realize what was happening, his heart sinking slightly.</p>

<p>That was a forced breakthrough, and powerful enough that the residual Qi alone was capable of tearing his skin beneath the black dragon’s scales.</p>

<p>“Brat… today you die,” Jiantian’s voice echoed. His body floated a few centimeters off the ground, seized by an absurd amount of power, his eyes replaced by nothing but glowing flaming orbs.</p>

<p>The dragon on Qingyi’s back roared at Jiantian, who shuddered but didn’t lower his head.</p>

<p>Even with his Qi being suppressed, he was sure that this battle was won.</p>

<p>He didn’t know if he would survive that advance, but it didn’t matter.</p>

<p>He already had the power of a true immortal, even if his body and soul exploded. All he had to do was kill Qingyi.</p>

<p>“Ah… hahaha.” A bitter laugh escaped Qingyi’s lips.</p>

<p>The heaven-defying thunder sword was surrounded by a new kind of power: gray flames, the heat of which made the flames of Jiantian’s advance look like fake fire in children’s toys.</p>

<p>“It’s been a long time since I’ve had a battle this difficult… Don’t let me down,” said Qingyi, spitting a gout of blood onto the ground.</p>

<p>At that moment, they both attacked. Jiantian used his family’s most powerful technique, his sword turning into a flash of light, while Qingyi used no technique at all.</p>

<p>The moment these blades met, the world split between the gray flames of Qingyi and the blazing flames of Jiantian, whose face immediately filled with terror.</p>

<p>He was pushing everything towards Qingyi, all the Qi generated by that forced breakthrough, all the energy from the heavens that entered his body.</p>

<p>Yet nothing was enough – no, quite the opposite, his Qi seemed to be being devoured by those flames, like dry leaves used to put out a fire.</p>

<p>It didn’t take more than a moment for Jiantian to notice the flames climbing up the handle of his blade and spreading to his hand, reducing flesh to bone. Intense pain ripped a muffled scream from his throat.</p>

<p>His Qi ceased and the melted tip of his blade fell to the ground, the heaven-defying thunder sword plunging into his chest.</p>

<p>“So this is how I die… but I’m not going alone!” Jiantian pointed his remaining hand towards Qingyi, finally using his law.</p>
