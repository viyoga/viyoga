<p>“Ah…” The old woman sighed heavily as Hua Feiyan disappeared from her office.</p>

<p>Alone, an evil smile played across her face, her eyes focusing on Feiyan’s blood.</p>

<p>There was something powerful about it, the kind she had never experienced in centuries of cultivating divination arts.</p>

<p>She moved her aged fingers across her orb, the crimson liquid sticking to her pale skin.</p>

<p>Hesitating for a brief moment, she popped it into her mouth and her body shuddered.</p>

<p>Her face was overcome by a deep ecstasy as her body arched. How could such a taste exist?</p>

<p>Not even the most powerful experts who had ever offered their blood to her had such intense flavors.</p>

<p>It was powerful and pure, too pure for any mortal woman to possess.</p>

<p>“I…” The old woman’s lips slowly opened.</p>

<p>She wanted more, her eyes were filled with murderous intent and greed, but she held back.</p>

<p>From the depths of that blood, she felt something else, even purer than Feiyan’s blood.</p>

<p>Strangely, her body seemed incapable of breaking down and absorbing it, even with her cultivation of the Iron Body realm.</p>

<p>It was simply impossible.</p>

<p>“This… has something to do with that brat?” Her eyes twitched, remembering the figure she had shown Feiyan.</p>

<p>That was a common divination technique, which used strong feelings to briefly pick up on someone’s presence in the world.</p>

<p>High-level experts could easily foil these techniques, but cultivators like Qingyi, without any kind of protection, were a perfect target for divination masters.</p>

<p>“I wonder…” A sickening smile appeared on his wrinkled face, and a powerful golden Qi took over his crystal orb. “What will his fate be?”</p>

<p>Seeing someone’s fate was a dangerous technique for most divination masters; they only used it when very necessary.</p>

<p>But she ignored these risks.</p>

<p>Wealthy clients asked to see fate all the time. Once, she even saw the fate of a young boy who was destined to be a Rebirth Realm expert.</p>

<p>Yet all she suffered was a nosebleed.</p>

<p>What were the chances of that young man having a fate that came even close to that?</p>

<p>Calming her breathing, she focused on Feiyan’s blood.</p>

<p>“As I imagined…” she smiled, sensing two interlocking barriers. One of them was Feiyan’s fate, the other Qingyi’s.</p>

<p>Without hesitation, she forced her Qi onto the barrier.</p>

<p>As it was based on the target’s cultivation level, it wasn’t difficult to break through.</p>

<p>And then she saw Qingyi’s fate.</p>

<p>A star, purple, shining, above everything and everyone, beyond anything her mind could comprehend.</p>

<p>The pressure of the star immediately fell on the image she had formed of Qingyi’s fate.</p>

<p>Her eyes widened and she immediately tried to back away, but it was too late.</p>

<p>Her meridians shuddered as all the Qi she hadn’t used to break the barrier collapsed.</p>

<p>Her eyes were filled with an explosion of blood and her skin turned red.</p>

<p>She tried to struggle, to calm her energy, but nothing helped.</p>

<p>After a few more moments of suffering, her body collapsed onto the ground, her aura slowly disappearing.</p>

<p>***</p>

<p>“Uh?” Qingyi looked up, feeling a slight strangeness in his chest.</p>

<p>“Ah…” He sighed before shaking his head and circulating his Qi, calming his mind.</p>

<p>He had trained throughout the night, slowly perfecting the first two forms of the Tempest Monarch Sword Art.</p>

<p>He was still far from mastering them, but at least he had enough control for them to be useful in combat.</p>

<p>Qingyi stood up, hearing a soft knock on the door.</p>

<p>When he opened it, his vision immediately met a mature beauty with purple hair and gentle eyes.</p>

<p>Realizing that no one was looking, he grabbed her slender, delicate wrists, pulling her into an embrace.</p>

<p>“Nghn Not now…” Elize pushed against his chest, but couldn’t resist.</p>

<p>“And why not?” He asked, his hands moving up from her delicate waist to her huge, soft twin mountains, feeling them give way under his fingers.</p>

<p>“S-sister Yueli… she’s waiting for you in the throne room…”</p>

<p>Hearing these words, Qingyi controlled himself, his hands returning to her waist before sinking his lips to hers.</p>

<p>“You’re even hotter today, you know that?” A subtle smile took over Qingyi’s face as their lips parted.</p>

<p>Elize didn’t reply, just sinking her face into Qingyi’s chest, accepting his embrace, her body warm against his.</p>

<p>“Sister Yueli is in a hurry, she still needs to go buy supplies in the village…” She murmured softly.</p>

<p>Elize just wanted to melt into Qingyi’s arms and stay there for the rest of eternity, but she knew she couldn’t, at least not yet.</p>

<p>After a few more moments of caressing, Qingyi finally sighed heavily before letting go of the purple-haired beauty.</p>

<p>His eyes held a bit of sorrow as he left his room and looked towards the large pavilion in the center of the temple.</p>

<p>He hadn’t yet entered the temple’s throne room and was quite curious. That place was different from all the rest of the temple; its construction wasn’t rustic and archaic.</p>

<p>On the contrary, its pillars were made of white marble surrounded by gold threads and its large double doors were a deep purple.</p>

<p>He raised his hand to knock on the doors, but it wasn’t necessary.</p>

<p>With a creak, they opened, revealing a luxurious interior, large enough to fit more than a hundred people.</p>

<p>The floor was covered in a carpet as soft as the most comfortable bed Qingyi had ever slept in, second only to Feiyan and Elize’s bosoms.</p>

<p>And then, far in the background, he could see Yueli’s delicate back, her beautiful blue eyes focused on a large throne that was more like the armchairs of Earth, covered in a cushion as soft as silk.</p>

<p>“Is everything all right?” Qingyi asked, a little uncertain.</p>

<p>“Yes…” Yueli lowered her head before standing up, her eyes meeting Qingyi’s.</p>

<p>“Your night seems to have been fruitful, congratulations on your breakthrough.” Qingyi smiled, noticing that Yueli’s aura was much deeper.</p>

<p>She had advanced to the second stage of the foundation realm.</p>

<p>“Thank you, you also seem to have had some level of comprehension.” Yueli returned his smile, but her expression soon turned serious.</p>

<p>“Young master Qingyi…” She spoke, hesitating for a single moment before continuing. “Are you sure you want to go after the lightning spirit?”</p>
