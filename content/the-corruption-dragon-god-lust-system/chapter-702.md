<p>It was… pathetic.</p>

<p>Guo Tianhao had arrived with all the arrogance in the world, declaring he would defeat Qingyi.</p>

<p>He had used one of his most powerful attacks, and even swallowed a strange pill that made his power erupt like a volcano.</p>

<p>And yet, there he was, kneeling in the dust.</p>

<p>The cloud slowly settled, revealing a bloodied figure. Nineteen swords pierced his body, and a twentieth floated in front of his face.</p>

<p>Qingyi hadn’t even bothered to get up to deal with him.</p>

<p>That was the difference in strength between the two.</p>

<p>In that generation, among all the geniuses he knew, the only one against whom Qingyi knew he could only win in a bitter, bloody battle was Bai Hu Lie.</p>

<p>Without a doubt, the tiger could kill him with a single misstep.</p>

<p>Tianhao was nothing compared to Bai Hu Lie, especially now that the tiger had awakened his bloodline.</p>

<p>“I-impossible…” Tianhao growled, blood rising up his throat and streaming endlessly from his trembling lips.</p>

<p>“W-wait, Young Master Qingyi, we can come to an agreement!”</p>

<p>A powerful man—Tianhao’s uncle and the head of relations between the Guo family and the Cosmic Dawn Sect—hurriedly stood up, addressing Qingyi respectfully.</p>

<p>The handsome young man did not respond.</p>

<p>With a simple swing of the twentieth sword, a sharp whistle cut through the air, and Tianhao’s head flew in a red streak, his soul snuffed out in that very instant.</p>

<p>With that, the two heirs of the Guo family met their end.</p>

<p>With that, the two heirs of the Guo family met their end.</p>

<p>“NO!” Tianhao’s uncle rushed desperately toward his nephew’s body, his previously relaxed expression now twisted by utter terror.</p>

<p>“Sect Leader, Tianhao consumed a strange pill during the battle, and afterward, his power erupted uncontrollably. Everyone saw it. We must examine his body!” Elder Shen, seated around the arena, stood up firmly.</p>

<p>He immediately received a look laden with murderous intent from Tianhao’s uncle, whose power was similar to his own.</p>

<p>The sect leader pondered this for a moment.</p>

<p>That pill was strange. Truly strange.</p>

<p>It carried no aura at all, neither demonic nor orthodox, as if it were just any ordinary medicinal pill.</p>

<p>Many had even ignored it, simply because they couldn’t sense anything emanating from it.</p>

<p>Yet, the moment Tianhao devoured it, his power exploded to an incomparable level.</p>

<p>One only needed eyes to realize that something was wrong.</p>

<p>The sect leader thought for a long moment, scratching his chin thoughtfully.</p>

<p>Finally, his lips parted.</p>

<p>“It won’t be necessary. Let the Guo family at least perform their child’s funeral rites with dignity.”</p>

<p>Hearing that, Tianhao’s uncle thanked the sect leader deeply before withdrawing with his nephew’s corpse in his arms.</p>

<p>“Ah…” The sect leader sighed. With a casual wave of his hand, the destroyed arena rebuilt itself, even Guo Tianhao’s blood vanishing without a trace.</p>

<p>The old man leaned back in his chair and, indifferent to Tianhao’s death, announced:</p>

<p>“It was a good opening match, but now, we need to get to the main event!” The sect leader declared, motioning for the young talents to enter the arena and introduce themselves.</p>

<p>Qingyi remained completely indifferent to Tianhao’s death, as if he had just crushed some annoying insect.</p>

<p>To him, honestly, it wasn’t that different.</p>

<p>Tianhao was annoying. He was an insect. And he had crushed him.</p>

<p>Why overthink it?</p>

<p>With a bright smile, Rouxi, who was standing next to him, pulled Qingyi into her embrace, drawing him back into the valley of that sensual cleavage.</p>

<p>His face sank into the warm depths of those enormous breasts, the soft, pillowy flesh parting around his cheeks before slowly molding back against him, plump and perfumed.</p>

<p>He sighed in comfort, feeling the heat of her bosom radiating through his skin.</p>

<p>It was as if there were some kind of magnetism between them.</p>

<p>He always ended up there.</p>

<p>Not that he was complaining, of course. Quite the opposite.</p>

<p>He savored the long seconds of her embrace, letting his cheek sink deeper into that scented softness until he was finally released, Rouxi’s delicate hands resting gently on his shoulders.</p>

<p>“Very good, dear! I’m so proud to have such a talented son!” Rouxi planted a lingering kiss on Qingyi’s cheek, drawing envious glances from most of the men present.</p>

<p>Who wouldn’t want to be pampered by those two incomparable beauties and even receive kisses from them?</p>

<p>In the end, Qingyi simply relaxed.</p>

<p>When Rouxi released him, he received a look of approval from both the sect leader and the elder of the Myriad Beasts Sect.</p>

<p>“You truly never cease to amaze me, young man! Hahaha!” The old crow laughed heartily.</p>

<p>“Cultivate well and reach the Celestial Origin Realm. You will be the sect’s representative at the celestial tournament of the great sects of the North.” The sect leader nodded to Qingyi, his eyes soon focusing on the fights that were beginning.</p>

<p>With their blood boiling after witnessing the clash between Qingyi and Tianhao, the sect’s young talents fought with renewed determination.</p>

<p>Soon, powerful battles erupted throughout the arena.</p>

<p>The tournament, though still a few months away from beginning, required them to depart in no more than two weeks.</p>

<p>Thus, Qingyi was given very little time to make his breakthrough.</p>

<p>Even so, the sect leader had complete confidence in him.</p>

<p>Qingyi was the star who would lead them to glory, and the possibility of failure never even crossed the sect leader’s mind.</p>

<p>Qingyi did little more than eat, chat, and learn a bit more about the tournament that would take place in about four months, where he could finally fight Bai Hu Lie once again.</p>

<p>He was tired of these weaklings. Now, with his lineage far more powerful, he wanted to fight truly challenging enemies.</p>

<p>At the celestial tournament of the great northern sects, which would gather prodigies from over thirty-two great sects, ten realms, and even guests from the central region, he would have his perfect chance for that.</p>

<p>And so, soon, the grand banquet came to an end.</p>

<p>Many rewards were bestowed, and before Qingyi left, the sect leader took him aside to a secluded corner.</p>

<p>“This is a central disciple token, but not an ordinary one. With this, you may enter any region of the sect, open any door, command any guard, retrieve any items from the sect’s vaults, and gain access to the sect’s divine library. You know what that means, correct?”</p>

<p>The sect leader handed a gleaming token to Qingyi, whose eyes shone brightly.</p>

<p>That was a promise.</p>

<p>A promise that, as long as he remained in the Cosmic Void Sect, he would have the sect leader’s full and unquestioning support.</p>

<p>What was the value of a Stellar Sovereign?</p>

<p>He smiled and, holding the golden token in his hands, bowed in respect.</p>

<p>That battle between Qingyi and Tianhao had not only cemented his title as the Supreme Emperor of the Ten Thousand Blades, but had also earned him the sect leader’s full support.</p>

<p>Even the raven elder seemed to have shining eyes, ready to recount everything he had seen there to his own sect leader.</p>

<p>Given the proud personality of the leader of the Myriad Beasts Sect, he would certainly be more than happy to marry his daughter to a young man of such renown in that region.</p>

<p>The fact that this reputation had been built in just about a year made the shock of everyone there even deeper.</p>

<p>If Qingyi survived until he reached maturity, everyone was already certain he would become a tidal wave that would make the entire celestial sky tremble.</p>

<p>It wouldn’t be long before his fame reached the central regions.</p>

<p>Soon, when everything was over and only the excited rumors of Qingyi’s exploits filled the sect, he made his way to a cultivation room in Rouxi’s palace, isolating himself in absolute silence.</p>
