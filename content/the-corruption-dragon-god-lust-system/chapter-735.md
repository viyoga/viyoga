<p>“Tell me, have you mentioned this young man to any of my direct subordinates?” the old man asked, his low, measured voice breaking the silence of the cultivation chamber.</p>

<p>“The six Stellar Sovereigns who accompany us? No.” Long Aoxue replied after a brief pause, his brow furrowing slightly as he searched his memory.</p>

<p>The Stellar Sovereigns, much like the old man himself, rarely left this place.</p>

<p>Lower-level servants were assigned to the direct protection of the two young men, so they only emerged from seclusion when necessary.</p>

<p>Knowing that Long Aoxue had come straight to him without going through any intermediaries drew a relieved smile from the old man.</p>

<p>The chances that those six knew anything about Long Qingyi were minimal, but he was the sort of man who simply didn’t like to take risks.</p>

<p>“Good. I’ll handle this matter regarding Qingyi personally. For now, just make sure your cultivation remains stable, and don’t disappoint the Orthodox Alliance in the tournament.” The old man said, giving Aoxue a gentle pat on the shoulder.</p>

<p>Aoxue nodded eagerly, his eyes shining with a mixture of gratitude and anticipation.</p>

<p>A broad smile plastered across his face, the young man left the master’s cultivation room.</p>

<p>‘Long Qingyi… you bastard… I’m going to seclude myself in cultivation now, so it’s a shame I won’t get to see your crippled body after the master deals with you… but I’ll gladly take Ma Zhenwu’s granddaughter as my wife!’</p>

<p>Soon, he stepped into an isolated cultivation room, closing the door behind him with a muffled thud.</p>

<p>In his mind, he was already absolutely certain that the old man would go straight to crippling Qingyi.</p>

<p>Alone in the vast cultivation chamber, the old man merely shook his head in defeat before he projected a sound transmission to the Stellar Sovereigns under his command.</p>

<p>He would be investigating a personal matter, and until further notice, every one of them was to remain isolated in cultivation.</p>

<p>None of the Stellar Sovereigns found this strange. Their master was an eccentric man, notoriously averse to being disturbed.</p>

<p>They themselves, not being much different in temperament, simply obeyed the order without questioning a single word.</p>

<p>With everything in order, the old man took a single step forward, and the air seemed to bend around him.</p>

<p>In the blink of an eye, he vanished into nothingness, leaving not even a ripple in space.</p>
