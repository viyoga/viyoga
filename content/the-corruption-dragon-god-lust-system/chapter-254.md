<p>“Husband… do we really need to do that?” Xueyao asked, already outside the world of the mind, her tail curling nervously around her legs.</p>

<p>Despite Qingyi’s confidence, Xueyao still hesitated.</p>

<p>She may have seemed like an adorable woman with almost no knowledge of the outside world, and she really was.</p>

<p>But she still knew her own strength well.</p>

<p>After a moment’s thought, she finally circulated her Qi.</p>

<p>Qingyi prepared himself, obviously not planning to use his sword.</p>

<p>Although he called it training, he just wanted Xueyao to attack him so that he could get a better idea of her power.</p>

<p>Xueyao’s eyes changed, their blue losing the excited, curious gleam they had always had.</p>

<p>A soft, sweet sigh escaped her lips as she pointed a single finger towards Qingyi, all that Qi gathering at the tip.</p>

<p>The next moment, she launched it.</p>

<p>The ice wall barely lasted a moment and the scales covering his body crackled as he was thrown backwards, the ice quickly spreading across his body.</p>

<p>“Shit… what kind of icy Qi is that?” Qingyi wondered, surrounding his body with flames.</p>

<p>“It’s probably related to the law that Xueyao dominated when she became a transcendent,” Ruxue’s voice echoed in Qingyi’s mind.</p>

<p>“Ah… how is that even possible?” The handsome young man asked, struggling to get up.</p>

<p>It was only when he called upon his lineage projection that he finally managed to overcome Xueyao’s Qi, the ice rapidly melting.</p>

<p>“Transcendent laws are complicated things, each cultivator has just one and carries it with them for their entire life.</p>

<p>Some have abstract laws, some have laws that can increase their physical resistance, others have laws that can fuse elements or even change their compositions completely..</p>

<p>If you still plan to try to fuse fire and ice, focusing the awakening of your law on that is the way to go.”</p>

<p>Qingyi listened to Ruxue’s explanation before sighing.</p>
