<p>Qingyi settled into the head of the table with Lingxi snuggled in his arms, the little one’s curious eyes wandering around the room.</p>

<p>Linyue took the seat to his left, while Xueyao slipped into the one on his right.</p>

<p>The cat girl and the fox girl nuzzled against him affectionately, their warm, soft bodies pressing snugly to his sides.</p>

<p>Their tails swayed gently in the air, keeping Lingxi entertained with playful, teasing swishes.</p>

<p>Lingxi, just like her father, was a refined connoisseur — not only of plentiful milk supplies, but also of beast girls, and, of course, their fluffy tails.</p>

<p>Her big round eyes sparkled brightly as her chubby hands reached out to grab the tails of the two women — one as white as fresh snow, the other orange and striped like living fire.</p>

<p>She hugged both tails to her chest with all the strength her little arms could muster, letting out a squeal of pure delight.</p>

<p>The two women smiled, utterly enchanted, pressing their faces against Qingyi’s broad shoulders and soaking up his masculine warmth with soft, contented sighs.</p>

<p>"You know... hubby, we both have a little surprise for you too..." they whispered in unison, their tails curling gently around Lingxi to keep her snug, while their delicate fingers sought out Qingyi’s hands.</p>

<p>Each pulled one of his hands, guiding his open palms until they came to rest on their still-flat bellies, the soft warmth of their skin radiating through his fingers.</p>

<p>For a brief moment, a shadow of confusion flashed across Qingyi’s eyes, but it soon dissipated like mist under the morning sun.</p>

<p>His gaze took on a new sparkle, and he caressed both of their bellies tenderly, a radiant expression of happiness spreading across his handsome, masculine face.</p>

<p>As he looked up, he found Khaedryss staring at him with fiery eyes, the golden glow within them crackling with barely contained anticipation.</p>

<p>The beautiful black dragoness kept her arms crossed beneath those perfect, heavy breasts, the gesture involuntarily pushing them upward. Her black dress hugged every curve of her sculptural body, the deep neckline revealing a tempting cleft between her two generous mounds.</p>

<p>Her forearms sank naturally into the squishy, obsidian-colored flesh, her heavy breasts molding around them like two warm, plush cushions. On her high, noble cheeks, a soft pink flush blossomed.</p>

<p>"That pill you used to get Rouxi pregnant... don’t think you’re going to get away with it. I want one, too!" she exclaimed, looking away with a stubborn pout on her full lips.</p>

<p>The abrupt motion sent her voluminous tits wobbling against her crossed forearms, the heavy, obsidian mounds rippling with a slow, weighty tremor before settling once more into place.</p>

<p>Qingyi let out a tender chuckle. "All right, love... we’ll have a little dragon warrior, too."</p>

<p>He was genuinely curious to discover what the draconic lineage of his child with Khaedryss would be like.</p>

<p>Both were black dragons of extremely pure bloodlines, but from completely different origins, which made the mix all the more unpredictable and fascinating.</p>

<p>"Dadda?" Lingxi finally let go of the two women’s tails and turned her tiny face toward Qingyi.</p>

<p>Babbling adorably, she stretched out her little hands until they touched her father’s cheeks, as if asking what was going on.</p>

<p>"You’re going to get some new little sisters to play with, sweetheart." Qingyi smiled, hugging her tightly and covering her chubby cheeks with loving kisses.</p>

<p>He was happy — he just didn’t know if his heart could handle the combined cuteness of two more Lingxis... well, actually, three more.</p>

<p>Khaedryss also wanted hers right away, and he had no intention of keeping her waiting, especially now that his other wives were already carrying his children.</p>

<p>Luckily, only those three were in a hurry. The rest still had their own goals to achieve before embarking on the path to motherhood.</p>

<p>If dozens of little Lingxis were to appear all at once, Qingyi would never be able to distribute his love equally among them all, and for him, that would simply be unacceptable.</p>

<p>Qingyi kept Lingxi nestled in his arms, bestowing gentle kisses on Linyue and Xueyao, until his eyes fell upon Yuehan and Huoyue.</p>

<p>The two watched him with clearly impressed expressions.</p>

<p>Having babies at such a high cultivation and talent level was extremely difficult.</p>

<p>Many experts devoted centuries — sometimes thousands of years — to focusing solely on conceiving a single child.</p>

<p>If they wished to speed up the process, they would need countless rare and complex resources.</p>

<p>Even so, success could take years to achieve.</p>

<p>Qingyi was only twenty-four years old. A monstrous talent, without a doubt, yet he had already impregnated three women, two of them without resorting to any special resources.</p>

<p>That was a bit too absurd in the eyes of those two women. But, come to think of it, everything about Qingyi was absurd.</p>

<p>His spatial talent, his origins, his strength.</p>

<p>He was the kind of aberration that the heavens produced only once every million years.</p>

<p>Qingyi ignored the two women’s astonished stares, letting them mingle with his other wives, while he himself turned his full attention to playing with Lingxi.</p>

<p>And that was how the time passed until dinner.</p>

<p>When Elize finally filled the table with delicious delicacies and refined dishes, Lingxi was gently taken from his arms to be fed by her mother.</p>

<p>Normally, a child her age would still rely almost exclusively on porridge and milk. But Lingxi was remarkably advanced for her age, and her little teeth were already well developed.</p>

<p>She grabbed a piece of meat nearly as big as her own face, letting out adorable sounds — like the growls of a small, hungry beast — and hugged the delicacy to her chest, sinking her tiny teeth into it excitedly.</p>

<p>She got her entire little dress dirty in the process, drawing a resigned sigh from her mother, who simply shook her head and let her have her fun.</p>

<p>Qingyi turned to his own meal, unable to hide the broad smile plastered on his face.</p>

<p>When the meal came to an end and Lingxi, exhausted by all the commotion, was given a thorough bath before being carried off to bed, Qingyi finally had some time to spare for himself and his wives.</p>

<p>And he knew how to make the most of every second.</p>

<p>Only Yuehan and Huoyue were left out, in the company of Ruxue. The three had much to discuss, especially regarding the new bond that united the first two with Qingyi.</p>

<p>Unfortunately, he couldn’t linger long. Soon enough, he was already making his way toward a secluded and heavily guarded cultivation chamber deep within the Tianjin Palace.</p>

<p>Qingyi maintained a serene expression as he settled into the lotus position.</p>

<p>Finally, the time had come to advance to the Stellar Sovereign realm!</p>
