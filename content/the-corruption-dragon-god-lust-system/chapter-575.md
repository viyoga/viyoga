<p>“Take Auranys as your wife?” The god of war repeated, those words overcoming the shock of having lost a contest of strength to Qingyi.</p>

<p>What the hell was that damn brat talking about? Him, a mortal, taking a goddess as his wife?</p>

<p>“Who the hell do you think you are? Arrogant brat!” The god of war growled, readying his axe again.</p>

<p>“Me? I’m just the one who’s about to kill you and then fuck the woman you dream of having” Qingyi flashed a broad smile, one that made the god of war’s heart sink with hatred.</p>

<p>Without another word, he grabbed his axe and vanished.</p>

<p>Qingyi did the same with his heaven-defying thunder sword.</p>

<p>Both were too fast for any human eye to follow, just blurred figures tearing through the air and meeting again and again.</p>

<p>“You’re fast!” Qingyi smiled, meeting the god of war once more, both being forced to retreat more than twenty steps before advancing again.</p>

<p>The shockwaves from their previous encounters still formed around them, hundreds of craters appearing throughout the city, entire neighborhoods reduced to dust.</p>

<p>When they finally stopped to face each other, both were drenched in blood, every small mistake made had turned into a deep wound.</p>

<p>‘This is annoying…! Qingyi stepped back slightly, extinguishing a crimson fire burning his long black robes.</p>

<p>Those flames were only slightly inferior to the flames of primordial chaos, and worse still, they seemed to slow down the healing effect of the seed of life.</p>

<p>With a soft grunt, he stared coldly at the god of war.</p>

<p>There was no longer blind hatred on the enemy’s brutal face. His exposed muscles burned with power, but they also carried a certain amount of self-control.</p>

<p>“I have led armies so large that your mortal mind would not even be able to comprehend them.” The god of war whispered, rising into the sky.</p>

<p>For the first time since arriving in this world, he showed something other than sickening lust and anger.</p>

<p>In his voice, there was power, and also dignity.</p>

<p>“The heavenly father himself gave me control of all his armies when that damned golden demon rose from the depths of hell, a monster with horns like yours!”</p>

<p>Qingyi did not react.</p>

<p>He knew that it was a golden dragon that had destroyed the reign of the ancient gods and forged both the heavens and the primordial laws.</p>

<p>“But this time, I will not be defeated.” The god of war declared arrogantly, raising a single finger.</p>

<p>Suddenly, the sun in the sky trembled. Thousands of small, scorching golden threads appeared, converging toward the tip of his finger.</p>

<p>‘That’s… he’s absorbing the sun’s energy! Qingyi took a single step back and, for the first time since the beginning of that battle, felt real danger.</p>

<p>The orb of golden flames, which looked like a miniature sun, had barely reached the size of a basketball, but it already carried enough terrifying power to freeze Qingyi’s blood.</p>

<p>“Good…” He wasn’t scared. Quite the opposite.</p>

<p>Black scales engulfed his body as he smiled in anticipation.</p>

<p>This would be his last battle in the immortal heavens, the last enemy he would need to defeat before finally ascending.</p>

<p>It couldn’t be too easy, right?</p>

<p>A dragon roared behind him, its sharp black claws tearing at the ground beneath his feet.</p>

<p>“I wonder…” Qingyi looked at his bloodline projection, which had taken physical form beside him. “If I can use you to attack.”</p>

<p>After a moment of hesitation, and seeing the golden fire orb reach over five meters in diameter, Qingyi moved his draconic Qi.</p>

<p>Not toward his eyes, nor his muscles, nor his mind. He moved everything toward the bloodline projection, whose jaw opened, revealing endless rows of sharp teeth.</p>

<p>“I am the god of war, the god of destruction! The god of pride! Today, you demonic son of a bitch, I will kill you, rip out your guts, and then force that filthy whore to suck my cock until her throat tears!”</p>

<p>The moment the god of war’s voice fell, he threw the golden orb of flames toward Qingyi.</p>

<p>The handsome young man did not respond with words, only pointing one of his hands toward his opponent.</p>

<p>From the depths of his lineage projection’s throat, a sea of purple flames surged forth like a death laser, colliding with the golden orb.</p>

<p>The flash of the explosion was bright enough to erase any shadow and blind any animal, mortal or immortal, within a radius of more than</p>

<p>a hundred kilometers.</p>

<p>Qingyi did not stand still either.</p>

<p>He moved the Heaven-Defying Thunder Sword in a lunge, quickly being swallowed by the sea of purple flames from the dragon behind</p>

<p>him.</p>

<p>He could feel his body burning just from being close to the golden orb, the pressure enough to collapse his lungs.</p>

<p>With each gust of wind, black scales were torn from his body and his</p>

<p>flesh was ripped apart.</p>

<p>The god of war, in a position not much better, roared with more anger</p>

<p>than ever.</p>

<p>He was close… so close…</p>

<p>Why the hell did that damn brat have to show up?</p>

<p>Why did he have to get in his way like this?</p>

<p>A glimmer of happiness appeared on the god of war’s face when he</p>

<p>saw Qingyi’s body being swallowed by his attack.</p>

<p>But this happiness did not last long.</p>

<p>From the golden orb, Qingyi emerged, his sword finding the god of</p>

<p>war’s chest.</p>

<p>“Die.” A single word sounded, cold and calm.</p>

<p>In the next moment, the god of war was pushed away and engulfed</p>

<p>by purple flames.</p>

<p>When the calm returned, there was not a single building left standing</p>

<p>in the hundreds of kilometers that the city stretched across.</p>

<p>The only exception was the golden palace.</p>

<p>The flying island seemed about to collapse, and on the horizon,</p>

<p>Qingyi saw a figure fall to the ground.</p>

<p>The beautiful young man was already destroyed, his clothes reduced</p>

<p>to dust and his nakedness covered only by what remained of his torn</p>

<p>pants.</p>

<p>The figure on the ground trembled and, for a moment, seemed powerless, but that soon changed.</p>

<p>Golden flames engulfed the body of the god of war as he slowly</p>

<p>floated, his wounds healing rapidly.</p>

<p>[Shit… he wants to burn his own divinity to make a final attack!] Ruxue</p>

<p>shouted in Qingyi’s mind.</p>

<p>No god in his right mind should have the courage to do such a thing.</p>

<p>What the hell was that bastard thinking?</p>

<p>“Oh… I’m sorry.” Qingyi said, slashing with the Heaven-Defying</p>

<p>Thunder Sword and using the Heaven-Shattering Divine Slash directly on the enemy’s neck.</p>

<p>“But I don’t have time for this bullshit.”</p>

<p>The head of a fallen god was thrown into the sky, and the world was</p>

<p>enveloped in silence.</p>
