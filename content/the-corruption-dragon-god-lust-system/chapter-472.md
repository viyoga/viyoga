<p>Despite being known as the land of the draconic elves, the Grand Duchy of Vaeldrinn is not a land inhabited solely by them.</p>

<p>Quite the contrary.</p>

<p>It would be more accurate to call it a territory ruled by elves who do not even have a good relationship with their kin in the Kingdom of Naelion.</p>

<p>In the distant past, before elven rule, this was an imperial territory.</p>

<p>It was conquered after a counterattack in response to the Empire’s attempt to exterminate an isolated people in the mountains.</p>

<p>It was this war that resulted in Khaedryss being unleashed upon the Empire, killing countless emperors and burning millions of imperial civilians.</p>

<p>This earned her the nickname Slayer of Immortals and Butcher of Empires.</p>

<p>Had she not lost interest and returned to her deep slumber, the Rose Empire of today would likely not even exist.</p>

<p>“It’s quite a story…” Qingyi repeated to himself as he arrived at a border town of the Grand Duchy of Vaeldrinn.</p>

<p>Its walls were black and tall, and its double gate was open, allowing merchants to enter freely.</p>

<p>This was the city of Nighthall, one of the most powerful and important in the Grand Duchy, with a population of just over three million.</p>

<p>The moment he passed through the main gates, mingling with the ordinary merchants, Qingyi’s eyes rose.</p>

<p>A few hundred meters ahead, the central avenue split into two streets, surrounding a small park.</p>

<p>In this park, among trees and streams, stood the huge statue of a black beast, one that Qingyi knew well.</p>

<p>“Khaedryss…” A soft smile spread across the handsome young man’s face as he studied the statue’s features and watched the peasants kneeling beneath it, praying silently.</p>

<p>It was strange to see it here as a symbol of reverence for these people, when in his mind, she was just a sleepy, hungry beast. “Ah…” He shook his head and suppressed his smile as his eyes lifted. He needed to reach DragonPeak, the capital of the Grand Duchy of Vaeldrinn. but from here on out, the journey would not be so simple. An escort was required to enter the capital, and powerful detection formations were always active.</p>

<p>Even with Qingyi’s shadow and spatial roots, invading the city unnoticed would be difficult. Not without a lot of preparation or, at least, without greatly improving his affinities.</p>

<p>“Speaking of which…”</p>

<p>Qingyi opened his status screen.</p>

<p>Naturally, during the trip, he took plenty of time to be with his wives and had also earned many Lust Points.</p>

<p>It was time to not only improve his affinities, but also finally reach the Immortal Emperor Realm.</p>

<p>cultivate.</p>

<p>Unfortunately, there was nothing specific for cultivators there, but there were many meditation rooms in schools and martial arts shops</p>

<p>in this land.</p>

<p>Without hesitation, Qingyi entered the first one he saw, renting a room for about seven days and then isolating himself.</p>

<p>Immediately, he clicked to improve his cultivation, ten trillion Lust Points leaving his wallet.</p>

<p>A gentle pain took over his body, his meridians expanded, and then a strong feeling of refreshment came.</p>

<p>Qingyi even thought about also improving his Mana Heart to reach the Tenth Ring, but he soon gave up.</p>

<p>The price to raise his cultivation to the Immortal Emperor Realm</p>

<p>reached fifty trillion points, and he still wanted to improve his</p>

<p>affinities.</p>

<p>Taking a moment to calm his Dantian and prepare himself, Qingyi finally clicked to improve his cultivation.</p>

<p>His body trembled with excitement.</p>

<p>The Immortal Emperor Realm would be one of the last steps before he</p>

<p>had another great breakthrough; in the Ancestral Emperor Realm: The</p>

<p>formation of a Domain.</p>

<p>It was something so powerful that unless Qingyi achieved such a domain, his power would rarely go beyond the initial level of</p>

<p>Ancestral Emperor.</p>

<p>‘I wonder what domain I will achieve…! Qingyi thought to himself as</p>

<p>the first twinge of pain surged.</p>

<p>Just one more realm and he would finally be there.</p>

<p>[Initiating breakthrough!]</p>

<p>The moment the system notification arrived, Qingyi’s lips parted in a</p>

<p>painful groan.</p>

<p>His Dantian moved strangely, and the Qi core at its center, still barely the size of a few grains of rice, suddenly expanded.</p>

<p>Qingyi kept his eyes closed, letting the changes in his Dantian take</p>

<p>place.</p>

<p>In just a moment, his Qi reserves had doubled, but that wasn’t all.</p>

<p>stopped.</p>

<p>power.</p>

<p>[Your cultivation has increased! Third stage of the True Immortal Realm -&gt; First stage of the Immortal Emperor Realm.]</p>

<p>heavily.</p>

<p>He stretched, the muscles of his abdomen even more prominent as he</p>

<p>moved his powerful body.</p>

<p>A single punch, without Qi, just pure and casual physical power.</p>

<p>Still, the whole place seemed about to collapse.</p>

<p>Yes… that power wasn’t bad.</p>

<p>It was time to improve his affinities as well.</p>
