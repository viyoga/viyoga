<p>Shortly after leaving the entrance to the Hidden Moonlight Secret Realm, the elder and the ten inner disciples accompanying him appeared in a strange place.</p>

<p>It was a cave that closed in the shape of a dome, with a huge arena at its center and an opening at the top, through which the midday sunlight streamed in.</p>

<p>Surrounding them were thousands of doors, each connected to an individual spatial pocket.</p>

<p>In the Immortal Heaven, a formation so powerful and capable of holding so many experts from the Astral Body Realm would cost the fortunes of entire empires, but in the Celestial Heaven, it was nothing.</p>

<p>The Secret Realm of the Hidden Moonlight, as it was called, was barely considered a low-level formation.</p>

<p>Relaxing, the elder of the Cosmic Dawn Sect sat in a comfortable armchair, a small book appearing between his fingers.</p>

<p>“Is anyone of interest to you?” He turned his eyes slightly, gazing at the inner disciple to his right.</p>

<p>Among all those who had accompanied the elder to the selection, he was the most talented.</p>

<p>Dark eyes, light blue hair, and a masculine, strong face with a refined beauty.</p>

<p>Not only was his appearance arrogant, his background was as well.</p>

<p>The son of a noble family that held complete control over an entire planet, his wealth, status, and talent placed him among the top candidates to become a core disciple in the future, and the potential fiancé of the patriarch’s daughter, the precious pearl of the Cosmic Dawn Sect and one of the greatest beauties under the heavens.</p>

<p>It was no exaggeration to say he was the future of the sect. A young man who had passed that test in just one hour, the fastest time in all of history.</p>

<p>Guo Tianhao was his name.</p>

<p>At the eighth stage of the Celestial Ascension Realm, he was the star of the powerful and wealthy Guo family.</p>

<p>Even though his marriage to the sect patriarch’s daughter wasn’t confirmed, he still arrogantly declared himself her man, with some agreements clearly flowing behind the scenes.</p>

<p>“Hm…” Guo Tianhao scratched his chin at the elder’s words.</p>

<p>“If Elder Shen wants honesty, that young master Luo seemed decent. His family are honorable warriors, though they’re just a few insignificant ants.” Tianhao replied, drawing a surprised look from Elder Shen.</p>

<p>“And that young black dragon?” Elder Shen shifted in his seat, raising an eyebrow.</p>

<p>“Dragons have incredible vision and unmatched talent. Don’t you think there’s a chance he might break your record?”</p>

<p>“That pretty face?” Tianhao’s lips curled up in deep contempt and amusement.</p>

<p>No chance. Not even in a million years.</p>

<p>He could sense Qingyi’s aura, so faint it barely seemed to exist.</p>

<p>That could mean two things.</p>

<p>Either the owner of this aura was truly that weak, or it meant that his</p>

<p>control over Qi was so superior that the observer was simply unable to read it accurately.</p>

<p>In Tianhao’s view, the second alternative was impossible. Only the first remained.</p>

<p>Elder Shen sighed, disappointed in Tianhao.</p>

<p>He had expected more insight from someone with such talent.</p>

<p>‘I just hope he doesn’t break under the trials that are about to come…’ Elder Shen thought, looking at one of the many doors.</p>

<p>Transcendental geniuses often had a fatal flaw; they had never known failure.</p>

<p>Their arrogance was too great, and they knew how to deal with frustration only through anger, never with calm or patience.</p>

<p>Many broke down the moment they realized they had been surpassed</p>

<p>by someone they deemed inferior.</p>

<p>The door that had caught the elder’s eye cracked open and then</p>

<p>began to swing wide.</p>

<p>“Impossible… the tests started less than five minutes ago!” one of the</p>

<p>inner disciples shouted, eyes wide.</p>

<p>Tianhao was also in shock. The only one who remained calm was</p>

<p>Elder Shen.</p>

<p>One hour had been the shortest time in the entire history of the sect, hundreds of thousands of years, with Guo Tianhao being the only one</p>

<p>to reach that mark.</p>

<p>But the door had opened in less than five minutes. Twelve times</p>

<p>faster than the time Tianhao had taken.</p>

<p>The young master’s eyes narrowed, and from the depths of his chest,</p>

<p>an intense bitterness began to rise.</p>

<p>From the open door, a figure emerged.</p>

<p>A face of such beauty that it made even Tianhao, known as a</p>

<p>handsome man, shudder. A powerful body, long black hair, and horns</p>

<p>like obsidian.</p>

<p>His steps were firm, carrying the dignity of an emperor and the</p>

<p>caution of a tiger.</p>

<p>Qingyi stepped out of the doorway, walked up to Elder Shen, and</p>

<p>bowed, clasping his fists.</p>

<p>“Long Qingyi greets the Elder!”</p>

<p>The elder’s lips curved into a brilliant smile, and he stood up, placing</p>

<p>his hands on Qingyi’s shoulders.</p>

<p>“Very well, young dragon! I am truly impressed, hahaha! A talent like yours is rare.” The elder roared with laughter.</p>

<p>He had already earned great rewards by recruiting Tianhao, and now, returning to the sect with Qingyi, a dragon of extraordinary talent, he knew full well that his prestige would skyrocket.</p>

<p>After all, there was competition among the elders of the sect’s external recruitment pavilion.</p>

<p>“The elder flatters me too much. This humble one was merely lucky.”</p>

<p>“Luck does not yield such a perfect result, young man. Only talent, strength, and insight do.” The elder turned, his eyes passing over Tianhao, who seemed to be stewing inside.</p>

<p>‘Don’t give in to anger, envy, or jealousy. Use this opportunity to</p>

<p>temper your mind and learn to deal with the frustration of being surpassed! The elder repeated in his own mind, hoping Tianhao would understand his intention.</p>

<p>The young master met the elder’s gaze and held his head high.</p>

<p>He was Guo Tianhao. The most talented inner disciple of the Cosmic Dawn Sect and son of a wealthy tycoon, heir to an entire planet. That damned dragon had just gotten lucky; he was certain of it.</p>

<p>No one from that cursed region was capable of surpassing him!</p>
