<p>Qingyi felt a little strange, but also happy.</p>

<p>One more level, and the complete bloodline of the Dragon God of Corruption would fully settle within his body, restored to the level it had once reached in the past.</p>

<p>A primordial-grade bloodline—so elevated that, throughout the entire celestial realm, only a handful of beings were believed to possess it.</p>

<p>Honestly, in his case, it was even rarer than that.</p>

<p>Even within the same grade, the gap in power between the weakest and strongest bloodlines was enormous.</p>

<p>And his stood at the very pinnacle of the primordial grade, infinitely superior even to that of the Heavenly Demon, for example.</p>

<p>"There’s still a long road ahead..." he whispered to himself, shaking his head slightly.</p>

<p>Strangely, even after clicking to upgrade his bloodline, nothing happened.</p>

<p>"System, is everything okay?" he asked, furrowing his brow.</p>

<p>[Yes. Be patient.]</p>

<p>"Okay." He nodded, letting his shoulders relax.</p>

<p>After a few moments of silence, a question surfaced in his mind, and he looked up.</p>

<p>"The final step to improve my bloodline... I’ll have to take it on my own, won’t I?"</p>

<p>He wasn’t referring to the primordial grade, but rather to the one above it: the grade of creation.</p>

<p>[Yes.]</p>

<p>"Well... and then?" he asked, crossing his arms casually.</p>

<p>It was a question that still lingered in his mind.</p>

<p>He could no longer raise his own cultivation through the system, and he would soon reach the peak of his talent.</p>

<p>The only function left to the system was the store, allowing him to acquire resources.</p>

<p>[I’ll continue to help you with knowledge, the system store, and affinities. Soon after, I’ll begin the shutdown process and—]</p>

<p>"And get a brand-new body from your future husband, right? Hehehe." Qingyi let out a mischievous chuckle, a playful smirk tugging at the corner of his lips.</p>

<p>The system froze for an instant, incredulous.</p>

<p>[You perverted womanizer...] For a brief moment, once again, the system’s voice lost its neutrality.</p>

<p>It was high-pitched and feminine, as lovely as Rou’er’s.</p>

<p>There was amusement in it, yes, but also a faint anxiety... and... pain.</p>

<p>Hearing that, Qingyi burst out laughing, but the laughter soon died on his lips.</p>

<p>[Qingyi. None of the items you purchase are stored in any kind of spatial warehouse. They are all acquired through the manipulation of fate.</p>

<p>When you advance to the Celestial Emperor Realm, I will no longer be able to hide from the heavens.]</p>

<p>The system’s voice echoed once more, this time returning to its usual coldness.</p>

<p>Qingyi’s expression hardened.</p>

<p>"I will be strong enough to stop the heavens."</p>

<p>[I know.]</p>

<p>Qingyi let out a heavy sigh.</p>

<p>"It’s practically certain that the heavens will attack me with a powerful celestial tribulation the moment I advance to this Realm," he said, his tone firm.</p>

<p>"If you’re planning to initiate your self-destruction during my advancement, hoping to prevent a second tribulation from falling on me right after the first, you know me well enough to realize it won’t work."</p>

<p>[Those who will try to exterminate me won’t just be the heavens—]</p>

<p>"The heavens, the great creator himself... I don’t care. I’ll shatter them all, if necessary."</p>

<p>Faced with those words, the system fell silent.</p>

<p>[Everything is ready. Focus your mind now.]</p>

<p>Qingyi nodded, closing his eyes.</p>

<p>This was no time to let his attention wander.</p>

<p>He sensed the exact moment the refinement of his bloodline began. Strangely, however, he felt no pain at all.</p>

<p>His body became as light as a feather, and his eyes, which had been closed, slowly opened.</p>

<p>He found himself in a strange place, an endless white void.</p>

<p>Gradually, a blue sky spread across that infinite white, stretching above his head, dotted with clouds of untouched purity.</p>

<p>Beneath his crossed legs, a vast sea of grass unfurled, and from that endless green, small purple flowers sprouted, swaying in the gentle breeze.</p>

<p>Slowly, Qingyi rose to his feet.</p>

<p>His eyes swept across the vast grasslands until, suddenly, they settled on something.</p>

<p>At the edge of a cliff, with his back turned, a man stood.</p>

<p>He had a powerful build, clad in greenish robes that revealed strong, well-defined arms. From his hair—also black with a faint bluish tint—two dark horns emerged.</p>

<p>Qingyi watched the man’s back for a moment and then walked toward him, his feet sinking slightly into the soft grass.</p>

<p>He stopped beside him, his eyes wandering across the horizon with a complex expression.</p>

<p>It was... beautiful.</p>

<p>As far as the eye could see, mountains, rivers, forests, and lakes stretched into the distance.</p>

<p>Every part of the landscape was lush and vibrant, like a Renaissance painting coming to life before his eyes.</p>

<p>"Are you the Dragon God of Corruption?" Qingyi asked, turning to the man beside him.</p>

<p>There was no answer. Instead, the man turned his face, and his purple eyes came to rest on a woman.</p>

<p>’Is that... Rou’er’s mother?’ Qingyi’s eyes widened for a moment.</p>

<p>A single glance at that woman’s beauty was enough to make his heart skip a beat.</p>

<p>She had long, pink hair that cascaded softly over her shoulders, a gentle smile playing on her lips, and an immaculate white dress that delicately hugged a body of beautiful curves.</p>

<p>’That’s a memory,’ Qingyi concluded, his gaze still fixed on the woman’s figure.</p>

<p>"Wujin, why are you here so early?" she asked, her voice sweet, pure and melodious.</p>

<p>"Yuelan..." Wujin offered a gentle smile to his beloved, reaching a hand toward her. "Nothing, my love. I was just enjoying the view."</p>

<p>He took her hand and pulled her into a firm embrace, his arm curling around her slender waist as his lips met hers in a gentle kiss, full of tenderness.</p>

<p>Yuelan melted into her husband’s arms, and when they finally parted, a slight blush colored her cheeks, her eyes brimming with love.</p>

<p>"Come have some tea and breakfast," she said with a sweet smile, taking Long Wujin’s hand and gently guiding him along.</p>

<p>Watching the couple, Qingyi realized he was in the courtyard of a vast mansion.</p>

<p>His eyes followed the two of them to a small table in the garden, where various treats had been laid out.</p>
