<p>The Gold King couldn’t believe his ears or his own body, his knees buckling immediately.</p>

<p>What was that damn pressure? That immeasurable brute power?</p>

<p>He felt his bloodline scream in horror, every part of him repeating one thing: “Run.”</p>

<p>When Jin Hao realized it, he was already on his knees before Qingyi, his eyes trembling and his breathing rapid.</p>

<p>He was the King of Gold. A man who had never knelt before anyone, not even before the enlightened emperor.</p>

<p>But there he was on his knees, breaking more than fifty thousand years of holding his head high. Didn’t life play funny tricks?</p>

<p>As quickly as it came, that pressure ceased. Qingyi reached out his hand, ignoring the darkening skies above him.</p>

<p>The heavens had heard his challenge and were not happy.</p>

<p>Jin Hao hesitated before grabbing his son-in-law’s hand and standing up.</p>

<p>He should have been angry at being forced to kneel like this, but strangely, not a shred of arrogance arose in him.</p>

<p>As he remembered the dragon’s eyes on Qingyi’s back, nothing but humility could reveal itself.</p>

<p>What was that young dragon?</p>

<p>“I’m sorry for the disrespect, father-in-law. But if this was a test, I hope I passed…” Qingyi said.</p>

<p>Jin Hao’s eyes filled with shock and surprise as the young man bowed to him.</p>

<p>The arrogance to challenge the heavens and the humility to bow and apologize to his elders…</p>

<p>Jin Hao was unable to stop the excitement from taking over his face.</p>

<p>‘I’ve found the perfect son-in-law, haven’t I? All the strengths of a dragon, but none of its flaws…</p>

<p>“Son-in-law…” The Gold King held Qingyi’s shoulders tightly before laughing. Opening his arms and roaring, he pulled Qingyi into a bear hug. “Welcome to the family!”</p>

<p>Above them, the skies seemed to calm down.</p>

<p>They would not descend upon Qingyi. Not today.</p>

<p>Immediately, Jin Hao turned to the double doors behind him, tightly closed.</p>

<p>The Shadows had obviously sensed that frightening Qi and were ready to storm the place at any moment.</p>

<p>But they soon relaxed, receiving a sound transmission from their master.</p>

<p>“Miss Meilin, your father is waiting for you.”</p>

<p>“Okay” Meilin nodded, stepping forward eagerly. Her eyes immediately focused on her father’s face.</p>

<p>He was happy. His face was taken over by a smile so wide and deep, second only to the smile he gave on the day she was able to break through the formations of that door.</p>

<p>“Come to your father, brat.” Jin Hao moved with Qingyi still in his arms, hugging Meilin tightly as well.</p>

<p>When he finally let them go, he smiled. “I’ll start preparations for the wedding soon, but first…”</p>

<p>Jin Hao’s eyes fell on the blade at Qingyi’s waist.</p>

<p>“That’s a very interesting weapon you carry, son-in-law,” Jin Hao said, studying it carefully.</p>

<p>“Hm? Do you know it?” Qingyi asked, confused.</p>

<p>It had been a long time since anyone had noticed the Heaven-Defying Thunder Sword.</p>

<p>“Yes. In fact, I bought it at an auction a few tens of thousands of years ago. But when I tried to wield it, it almost severed my hand and flew away.” The Gold King sighed, massaging a scar on his right hand.</p>

<p>He had never been able to heal it properly.</p>

<p>But it was good that Qingyi had that sword with him.</p>

<p>The Gold King had been thinking for a long time about what would be the most appropriate gift for his son-in-law. Now he knew exactly what it was.</p>

<p>The moment the Gold King opened his spatial ring, Ruxue’s sweet and mature voice echoed in Qingyi’s mind.</p>

<p>[Husband… I can feel one of my fragments, but not only that…]</p>

<p>Ruxue herself couldn’t believe her eyes as she watched the items that appeared in the Gold King’s hand.</p>

<p>In one of them, a fragment of steel. In the other, her scabbard. The original, forged by its creator over a hundred and fifty thousand years ago.</p>

<p>It was, of course, incomparable to the scabbard she had made with Qi when she first met Qingyi.</p>

<p>A powerful suction force spread out, snatching the fragment from the Gold King’s hands, which was quickly absorbed by the blade.</p>

<p>[Put me in my sheath, quick! It will help me absorb the fragment better.] Ruxue shouted in Qingyi’s mind, full of excitement.</p>

<p>She could imagine the power she would gain after absorbing the last of her fragments in the Immortal Heaven.</p>

<p>Just three more fragments, and she would finally be at the peak of her power again.</p>

<p>With a soft sigh, the handsome young man replaced the sheath on his waist, storing the Heaven-Defying Thunder Sword in it.</p>

<p>Immediately, he felt a change in the blade; its power seemed to increase many times over just by being in that sheath.</p>

<p>‘What does this sheath change? Its appearance is identical to the previous one!</p>

<p>[I’ll tell you after I’m done, fufufu] Ruxue’s laughter echoed, sweet and symphonic.</p>

<p>“All right, son-in-law, daughter, let’s eat and talk a little. We still have a lot to plan for your wedding.” The Gold King smiled, pulling another seat for Meilin beside the lake.</p>

<p>The handsome young man sat down next to his father-in-law and wife, his eyes flowing to his status screen.</p>

<p>He had amassed a fortune of Lust Points during the trip. Now, it was time to become an Ancestral Emperor and finally unlock his own domain.</p>
