<p>In the Eternal River Sect, chaos had taken over.</p>

<p>Disciples and elders ran in all directions as a column of light broke through the roof of the sect leader’s palace, vaporizing tiles and pillars before piercing the clouds above.</p>

<p>No one dared approach. Not even the most powerful elders, who were now flying in the opposite direction.</p>

<p>In the center of this palace, a woman was on her knees, her beautiful blue eyes burning with pain.</p>

<p>She raised a hand to her face, observing the pale disfigured skin for a brief moment before bringing it to her chest, feeling her heart.</p>

<p>“Just a few more years… Is that too much to ask?” She looked up at the black, thunderous sky, her sweet, symphonic voice discordant with her body’s current appearance.</p>

<p>She had failed in her goal. She wanted to stay, she wanted to fight more, she wanted to search more. But her time was up.</p>

<p>Being there was against the will of heaven, she knew that, she had always known that.</p>

<p>Even if it was only a part of her soul, that part was something whose existence the primordial laws of that world would never accept.</p>

<p>The fact that she had lasted this long was a miracle.</p>

<p>With a sigh, she closed her eyes, waiting patiently.</p>

<p>Suddenly, a roar woke her up, one she knew all too well.</p>

<p>Her trembling body stood up, her eyes wide as she watched something on the horizon.</p>

<p>Scales as black as night, deep purple eyes and a powerful body that stretched more than forty meters, its large wings tearing through the air at an absurd speed.</p>

<p>The dragon descended with a crash that cracked the ground, its claws opening deep grooves in the stone floor as it advanced towards the throne room.</p>

<p>“Khaedryss…” She whispered.</p>

<p>The light that devoured her body dimmed momentarily as her gaze fell on the couple riding on the beast’s back.</p>

<p>One was a man she knew, one of the most talented young men to have set foot in that sect in generations.</p>

<p>But it wasn’t this man who caught her eye, not compared to the woman next to him: purple hair cascading down her back, the same color as her gentle eyes now filled with tears.</p>

<p>Elize slid off Qingyi’s lap and jumped to the ground, taking a hesitant step towards that figure.</p>

<p>It was strange. That face didn’t exist anywhere in her memory. And yet she knew, deep down in her heart, that this woman was someone important to her.</p>

<p>“Little Elize… is that you?” The woman’s voice trembled.</p>

<p>She stepped out of the center of the pillar of light for a brief moment. Immediately, the decay of her body accelerated, glowing cracks spreading across her skin.</p>

<p>She raised her trembling hands, trying to reach Elize’s face, but her fingers never got there.</p>

<p>“Big sister…” The word finally escaped Elize’s throat. Tears streamed down her cheeks as she threw herself into the woman’s arms.</p>

<p>“Is it really you? Sniff…” The words came out between sobs.</p>

<p>“Yes, little sister…” The woman ran her hands through Elize’s hair, stroking it tenderly. “Look how much you’ve grown… Mom would be so proud, if only…”</p>

<p>She closed her eyes, remembering painful things.</p>

<p>In a secluded valley, stone roads snaked up to a monumental city, surrounded by walls of reinforced concrete and solid steel.</p>

<p>Tall towers with spiral roofs were guarded by sentries clad in hardened steel armor, only their eyes visible through the slits in their helmets.</p>

<p>In the center, or more precisely, above the city, stood an imposing castle, resting on a floating mountain attached to the ground by colossal chains.</p>

<p>Each link of this chain weighed tons, and each chain was anchored to one of the four corners of the city.</p>

<p>The castle was built of dark gray stone, a fortress capable of sheltering thousands.</p>

<p>The mountain on which it stood had its own forests and valleys, with a massive waterfall plummeting towards the ground, forming a river that cut through the city from side to side.</p>

<p>In one of these forests, a middle-aged man was resting by a stream. Long black hair framed a dignified middle-aged face, with pointed ears and curved red horns emerging from his skull.</p>

<p>His red eyes watched the running water as he wiped his muscular body with a towel.</p>

<p>“Seraphine…” He called out without turning his head, calmly covering himself.</p>

<p>Above him, a large dragon with red scales marked by horrible scars descended. Where there should have been a right eye, there was only a flaming void, the remaining eye focused on a figure approaching from the trail behind.</p>

<p>It was a tall woman, dressed elegantly. A dress the color of her eyes and decorated with black, purple and white roses hugged her curvy body.</p>

<p>A side slit revealed thick thighs and the accentuated curve of her voluptuous, rounded ass, covered by a black bodystocking that also appeared at the wide cleavage of her large, firm breasts, hiding the creamy skin underneath.</p>

<p>They were small compared to boobs like Elize’s, but still big enough to more than fill the hands of a man like Qingyi.</p>

<p>Her delicate, rosy shoulders were exposed, thin strips of fabric snaking elegantly down her slender arms and legs.</p>

<p>Deep purple eyes matched her long hair of the same color, while black horns and pointed ears adorned her pale-skinned, porcelain-perfect face.</p>

<p>That woman was Seraphine Vaeldrinn, one of the most beautiful and talented young women in her generation.</p>

<p>The man only gave her a quick glance before turning to the dragon, patting the scales on the creature’s snout affectionately.</p>

<p>“What does my dear daughter want with me at this hour?” His voice came out calm and unconcerned, a casual smile occupying his face.</p>

<p>“You know exactly why I’m here, Dad.” Seraphine growled low. “Where’s Mom? And little Elize? What have you done with them?”</p>

<p>He paused, closed his eyes and considered his next words before answering.</p>

<p>“I did what was necessary.” The smile disappeared from his face as he turned to face her directly.</p>

<p>“You know that as well as I do. She is the child of that prophecy. The cursed child. Born with a body different from ours, without a mana heart, without our bloodline. I saved our family.”</p>
