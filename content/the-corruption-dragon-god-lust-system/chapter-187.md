<p>Long Qingyi appeared in the center of a dark forest and in the next moment, was greeted by the sounds of battle on all sides as powerful auras from the Burning Blood Realm took over the forest.</p>

<p>“Looks like they’re trying to diminish the competition.” A bitter smile appeared on Qingyi’s face.</p>

<p>It was obvious that they would try to do that.</p>

<p>He worried about Qingxue for a brief moment, but when he remembered the power of her bloodline, he brushed that worry aside.</p>

<p>Probably no one below the crystalline core realm could compare to her speed.</p>

<p>Qingyi stepped forward, ready to activate his lightning steps, but stopped suddenly.</p>

<p>His eyes glowed a deep purple and the heaven-defying thunder sword slid out of its sheath with a sharp hiss, immediately enveloped in a thunderous aura.</p>

<p>In the next instant, a rumble echoed and the whole forest shook.</p>

<p>“It’s strong…” Qingyi gritted his teeth as his body was ripped from the ground, his feet tearing through the soil for dozens of meters, only stopping when his back hit a large tree, breaking it in half.</p>

<p>“So you’re the little shit who messed up my business…” A growl full of murderous intent echoed as Yulei halted his steps.</p>

<p>“Do you understand the situation you’re in now?”</p>

<p>He didn’t even bother to raise his guard, watching Qingyi like an insect.</p>

<p>“Ugh… Yes, now I understand.” Qingyi flashed a bloody smile, observing the look of shock on Yulei’s face as he closed the distance in a single movement, his fist already clenched.</p>

<p>Yulei let out a painful grunt as Qingyi’s punch sank into his face.</p>

<p>The young master of the Zhao family was catapulted backwards, his body bouncing on the ground for dozens of meters before coming to a stop, falling onto his knees.</p>

<p>“S-shit.” Yulei spat out a gulp of blood, his panting breath revealing a few broken front teeth.</p>

<p>“How dare you!” he shouted, his voice trembling as his Qi exploded and thunderous lightning took over his body. “I’ll make you pay for this a thousand times over!”</p>

<p>He was also a cultivator with a lightning spirit root.</p>

<p>Qingyi only smiled in response, but didn’t dare let his guard down.</p>

<p>Without using his bloodline projection, he doubted very much that his combat capabilities would be able to go beyond the second or third stage of the crystalline core realm.</p>

<p>He had only landed that first blow because Yulei was too arrogant.</p>

<p>In the end, the number of cultivation stages that could be crossed decreased with each realm and as Qingyi advanced, his achievements would also be relatively smaller.</p>

<p>Yulei stretched out his hand, a sword appearing in his grip as he roared, advancing like an enraged beast.</p>

<p>Qingyi took up a defensive position, using his black dragon scales to protect his vital regions and his draconic eyes to keep up with Yulei’s speed.</p>

<p>Nothing remained around them when their swords met.</p>

<p>The ground sank into a crater as the lightning that danced around their blades exploded everywhere, knocking down trees and making the air around them boil.</p>

<p>The stalemate lasted only a second before they both retreated.</p>

<p>Qingyi was forced to take more than fifty steps before stopping, while Yulei only took five.</p>

<p>Even so, the young master’s face was filled with shock.</p>

<p>Five steps.</p>

<p>He had just been forced back five steps by a cultivator two realms below his?</p>

<p>Shock soon turned to shame, and that shame immediately turned into enough anger to make him tremble.</p>

<p>“I was only planning to cripple you, but now I’m going to kill you, and then I’ll go after that silver-haired whore who accompanies you… with her beauty…”</p>

<p>Yulei’s face was filled with lust. “She’ll make a good wife – no, a good sex toy for me and all my men!”</p>

<p>Yulei readied his blade, ready to use his family’s strongest offensive lightning technique, but before he could even manifest it in his blade, his whole body froze.</p>

<p>His Qi stopped responding and his legs failed him.</p>

<p>Qingyi closed his eyes.</p>

<p>Yulei’s first words had already made his heart fill with rage.</p>

<p>But the last ones? That changed everything.</p>

<p>“I’d really… really just make you toothless and maybe give you some broken fingers.” Qingyi tried and failed to contain his fury.</p>

<p>Even without his command, his draconic Qi moved.</p>

<p>A colossal figure appeared on his back, its guttural, bestial growl echoing through the forest and making every hair on the body of every cultivator within a kilometer radius stand on end.</p>

<p>“But I’ve changed my mind. Pigs like you should be castrated.”</p>

<p>“W-what?”Yulei was unable to hide the tremor in his voice and the terror in his eyes as he watched the beast behind Qingyi.</p>

<p>There was no nobility nor dignity, nothing but the purest representation of fear and fury.</p>

<p>Beyond lust and desire, that was what a black dragon stood for.</p>

<p>“D-don’t come any closer! D-do you know who my father is?” Yulei asked, taking trembling steps backwards.</p>

<p>“Hm… let me see… to give birth to a son of a bitch like you…”</p>

<p>Qingyi closed the distance between him and Yulei in a single movement, a blade of Qi appearing in his fingers.</p>

<p>“He’s certainly a disgusting cuckold.”</p>

<p>Following Qingyi’s words, a heartbreaking scream echoed throughout the forest as Yulei fell to the ground, an endless stream of blood flowing down between his legs.</p>

<p>Qingyi ignored the screams, kicking Yulei in the face and knocking out the rest of his front teeth.</p>

<p>Qingyi was ready to finish the job when a powerful, dignified voice echoed.</p>

<p>“you’ve already turned him into a woman, kid, you don’t need to give him a full facial reconstruction as well.”</p>

<p>A colossal figure suddenly appeared, its flaming eyes focusing on Qingyi’s lineage projection with interest.</p>

<p>That was one of the most powerful lineages he had ever seen in his life, but he was still a transcendent.</p>

<p>All it took was a single thought and the flow of Qi that fed the projection was broken, causing it to disappear.</p>
