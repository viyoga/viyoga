<p>Qingyi waited patiently outside the cave.</p>

<p>After a few moments, the first figure appeared.</p>

<p>It was a thin, tall man, his face riddled with scars as a powerful flaming Qi surrounded him.</p>

<p>He was clearly an expert of the crystalline core realm.</p>

<p>“You, who the hell are you?” The skinny man questioned, his eyes filled with murderous intent as he saw his men lying dead on the ground.</p>

<p>Dozens more figures appeared behind him, surrounding Qingyi completely.</p>

<p>“Brat…” The skinny man growled, his crystalline core realm aura exploding, taking in everything in a radius of more than ten meters. “Better start explaining yourself.”</p>

<p>“Bao.” Qingyi said a single word, but it was enough to completely shatter the skinny man’s composure.</p>

<p>A soft, gentle smile took over Qingyi’s face, clearly not matching his words. “I would have liked to kill your little brother myself, but my wife took the honor.”</p>

<p>“You son of a bitch! You’ll pay for this!” The skinny man finally exploded as he roared, his voice more like that of an animal than a man.</p>

<p>A fire tornado danced around his fists, the flames rising tens of meters high, the ground beneath his feet turning black under the heat.</p>

<p>He lunged forward.</p>

<p>Qingyi was more than a hundred meters away, but he only needed a fraction of a second to get in front of the handsome young man.</p>

<p>“DIE!” The skinny man punched.</p>

<p>A deafening bang echoed as the Qi of fire and lightning sword met.</p>

<p>The shockwave destroyed everything within a radius of fifty meters, knocking down the weakest trees and defoliating the sturdiest ones.</p>

<p>A cloud of dust and steam rose up, blinding the men of the Blue Crow gang who were watching the fight with excited looks on their faces.</p>

<p>It was rare to see their leader fight and everyone wanted a good show.</p>

<p>When the dust began to settle, they expected to see their boss beating up that pretty face, but unfortunately for them, it wasn’t that sight that greeted them.</p>

<p>Qingyi was standing still, having taken a single step backwards.</p>

<p>The heaven-defying thunder sword had passed through the skinny man’s defenses as if they were nothing, ripping his fist in half, the purplish-silver blade reaching all the way to his wrist.</p>

<p>“Argh… fuck!” The skinny man’s eyes widened as the pain woke up his hate-drunk mind.</p>

<p>He tried desperately to retreat, but Qingyi didn’t allow him to.</p>

<p>The heaven-defying thunder sword tore through the air with a sharp hiss, striking the skinny man’s legs and slicing through flesh, and bone, tearing off both legs in a single movement.</p>

<p>” Damn, the leader’s screwed!” One of the bandits shouted, making the few neurons in his companions work.</p>

<p>This was not an enemy they could defeat.</p>

<p>“Fucking run!” They split up, running everywhere.</p>

<p>“I haven’t given anyone permission to move here.” Qingyi’s cold, soft voice echoed.</p>

<p>In the next moment, his draconic Qi exploded out of his Dantian, the air around him becoming heavy as a colossal figure stood at his back.</p>

<p>Its huge mouth full of sharp teeth opened in a bestial grunt as large purple eyes fell on the criminals.</p>

<p>Nobody moved, not that they didn’t want to.</p>

<p>Their Qi and their bodies simply refused to respond.</p>

<p>Raising their vision, they saw the figure of the black dragon’s head above Qingyi and their hearts immediately filled with terror.</p>

<p>“I was hoping to use you to try out a new technique in real combat, but I understand that I shouldn’t expect too much from filthy insects like you.”</p>

<p>“W-what the hell did we do to you? What do you want? Ugh… SHIT!” The skinny man whimpered, using his only functional hand to grab one of his severed legs.</p>

<p>Qingyi knelt down in front of him.</p>

<p>“I’m after a couple of merchants you kidnapped. A middle-aged man with a birthmark on his neck and a blonde woman with a small scar on her chin.”</p>

<p>Despite hearing Qingyi’s words, the skinny man didn’t reply immediately, confining himself to muffled sniffles as he tried to crawl away from Qingyi.</p>

<p>“Answer me.” The handsome young man punched, breaking a few of the skinny man’s teeth.</p>

<p>“I don’t fucking know! There must be hundreds of people there, I don’t know everyone’s face.”</p>

<p>“Then you have no use.” Qingyi swung his sword, decapitating the leader of the blue crow gang.</p>

<p>Looking at the rest, he used the second form of the Tempest Monarch’s sword art.</p>

<p>No matter how many pleading looks he received, he felt no pity.</p>

<p>He knew well that each of them had killed at least a dozen people and if left alive, they would kill many, many hundreds until they were finally killed.</p>

<p>A blade of thunderous Qi rose as Qingyi’s sword cut through the air, leaving a trail of destruction wherever it went.</p>

<p>For more than a hundred meters around him, tree trunks fell heavily to the forest floor, accompanied by the corpses of all the members of the blue crow gang.</p>

<p>Ignoring the scene in front of him, Qingyi merely stowed the skinny man’s head in his space ring before entering the stuffy cave.</p>

<p>He was immediately greeted by the sight of a corridor leading to a large pocket where more than five hundred people were mining non-stop.</p>

<p>’This is going to be harder than I thought…’ Qingyi sighed inwardly before shouting, taking the workers’ attention.</p>

<p>“Your captors are dead, I have released them.” He showed the decapitated head of the leader of the Blue Crow mercenaries.</p>

<p>The workers stopped mining immediately, casting glances in the direction of the handsome young man.</p>

<p>At first they watched them suspiciously, but that suspicion soon turned to relief as their pickaxes and buckets of spirit crystals fell to the ground.</p>

<p>“I need your help to find someone.” Qingyi exclaimed, before asking all the miners, men, women and even children to line up.</p>

<p>Turning on his draconic eyes, he observed the faces of each of them until he finally stopped at a tired-looking couple.</p>

<p>With a smooth movement, Qingyi jumped onto the platform where they stood, a smile playing on his lips as he spoke. “Little Yao misses you.”</p>
