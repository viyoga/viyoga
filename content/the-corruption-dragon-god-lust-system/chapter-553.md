<p>Jianming even approached slightly, thinking of trying to defend the marquis, but soon retreated.</p>

<p>It was too late.</p>

<p>His eyes were already empty, and his body was just a dry shell. “Ah…” Jianming inhaled heavily before his lips broke into a beastly roar. “COME TO ME, PIGS! MEET YOUR DEATH!”</p>

<p>And immediately, the demons responded, throwing themselves toward him like a crazed mob of transcendent power, blades, axes, and blood spears appearing in their hands.</p>

<p>The entire center of the capital was immediately reduced to dust by the impact between them and Jianming.</p>

<p>The only exception was the five-colored pavilion, protected by powerful defensive arrays.</p>

<p>Jin Hao had to retreat hastily, throwing himself back into the protection of the arrays, just in time to avoid becoming mincemeat.</p>

<p>For the first time in many years, Jianming, now with only one functioning arm, decided it was time to use his domain.</p>

<p>He gripped the sword with all the strength he had in his left hand, sending the demons flying, and then descended to the ground, plunging the blade into the earth.</p>

<p>Immediately, an explosion echoed, the black clouds surrounding the capital suddenly parting, crimson light falling upon the illuminated imperial capital.</p>

<p>Above everyone, through the hole in the black clouds, a red moon, or to be more specific, the projection of a red moon, hovered, blocking out the sun.</p>

<p>Its light, though frightening, struck the experts fighting like a</p>

<p>protective embrace, causing them to turn their faces toward the horizon.</p>

<p>The general of the Crimson Eclipse Saber seemed to finally be fighting seriously!</p>

<p>“Father!” A woman left the palace of the gold king, watching Jianming ascend to the skies again.</p>

<p>Jianming looked up, feeling more and more demons surrounding him, the number quickly passing thirty.</p>

<p>Then he turned his face toward his daughter, flashing a pained smile.</p>

<p>“Tell Meilin I’m sorry… I won’t be able to hold my great-grandchildren in my arms.”</p>

<p>“Father, no! Go back inside, let’s teleport away!” Tears welled up in the white heron’s eyes, but Jianming didn’t listen to her.</p>

<p>Run away and abandon what remained of the people of the imperial capital to certain death?</p>

<p>No.</p>

<p>About one thing, the marquis was correct. Once part of the imperial army, always part of the imperial army.</p>

<p>Jianming would protect the empire until his last breath, no matter what it cost him.</p>

<p>A long sigh escaped his lips, and then, all the demons surrounding him advanced at once again.</p>

<p>The old man did not feel threatened. Quite the contrary.</p>

<p>His eyes flashed and his body disappeared.</p>

<p>Those demons were powerful and immortal, yes. But in the end, they acted only like crazed, bloodthirsty beasts.</p>

<p>“Be glad you can see this before you die, filthy creatures!</p>

<p>Jianming’s saber flashed with his words, the metal whistling as it cut</p>

<p>through the air around him at a perfect angle, so aligned and sharp that it seemed capable of cutting through atoms and the very fabric of the universe.</p>

<p>And that, Jianming’s saber did.</p>

<p>The demons surrounding him barely reached five meters before they froze.</p>

<p>The movement of his blade was slow and easy to dodge, but still, none of them managed to react.</p>

<p>“Broken Reality Slash…” Jianming’s voice echoed, and then the world around him changed, as if split in two, a gap appearing in everyone’s vision.</p>

<p>The upper half of the world seemed to slide over the lower half, the gap lasting for a brief moment before the primordial laws corrected</p>

<p>everything.</p>

<p>Everything except the demons surrounding Jianming.</p>

<p>Their bodies exploded into a bloody mist, split into pieces, the largest of which were barely the size of a grain of sand.</p>

<p>Even for creatures like them, recovering from that was impossible.</p>

<p>Jianming smiled, feeling his body lose strength as he turned to his daughter.</p>

<p>That attack sucked a lot of Qi, but it had been worth it.</p>

<p>“FATHER!” The white heron roared, held back by her husband, who prevented her from throwing herself toward her dying father.</p>

<p>Her eyes were filled with tears as she watched a blade of blood tear through the air, finding Jianming’s back.</p>

<p>He tried to dodge, of course, but nothing was enough, a powerful Qi from the astral body realm falling upon him.</p>

<p>That was the only one of the demons who was at the peak of that level and who had now consumed the power of dozens of experts</p>

<p>from the ancient emperor realm.</p>

<p>Jianming turned, feeling the blade pierce through his chest, destroying his heart and flying towards the defensive arrays of the</p>

<p>five-color pavilion, shattering against the protective barrier.</p>

<p>“Don’t think I’ll die without a fight!” Jianming roared, slashing toward the demon, but he didn’t even come close to hitting it.</p>

<p>A new blade of blood cut through the air, and then a pained cry</p>

<p>echoed.</p>

<p>Jianming’s left hand fell to the ground, dropping along with his saber.</p>

<p>He tried to turn his face, tried to look into his daughter’s eyes one last time, but it was already too late.</p>

<p>He could already see his own body, a spray of blood dominating his vision as his head flew into the air.</p>

<p>“NO!” His daughter’s cry of pain and despair was the last thing he heard before darkness.</p>

<p>There was silence among the experts surrounding the five-colored</p>

<p>pavilion, all watching in shock as the demon fed on Jianming’s body, absorbing his power.</p>

<p>The general of the Crimson Eclipse Saber, one of the most beloved and respected experts in the enlightened sky empire, one of the pillars that kept the empire standing.</p>

<p>He had just died. That was a lie, right? Their eyes were deceiving them.</p>

<p>It was all a bad joke. Soon, they would wake up from a terrible</p>

<p>nightmare! right?</p>

<p>“What are you waiting for? DO SOMETHING!” The white heron clung to her husband’s robes, who was just as shocked as she was.</p>

<p>Jin Hao did not react, the bloody grip on the handle of his axe quickly</p>

<p>losing strength.</p>

<p>He remembered Qingyi’s words once again and, for the first time in a long time, felt something he thought he would never feel again.</p>

<p>Regret.</p>

<p>Jin Hao felt the terror of his wives and children inside the palace, and with a sigh, he strengthened his resolve.</p>

<p>The demon had finished off Jianming and now had its eyes focused on</p>

<p>him.</p>

<p>The beast studied the protective barrier for a brief moment before smiling, preparing a spear of blood with frightening power.</p>
