<p>Elder Yuelan was a woman of few words and left shortly after handing each disciple a small star-shaped pin, which was to be worn on the chest and served as proof of their status as sect members.</p>

<p>With her departure, the elders divided themselves among the groups, explaining basic rules to the disciples and handing out manuals that would serve as the foundation for their initiation into the sect.</p>

<p>Soon after, they led the disciples to their dormitories. Each one had twenty beds and a bathroom.</p>

<p>The bathrooms were insufficient for so many people, but since the journey wouldn’t be long and cultivators didn’t really need to use them, being able to use Qi to keep themselves clean, that didn’t pose any major problems.</p>

<p>What truly surprised those young people was not the accommodations, nor the meal served shortly afterward.</p>

<p>But rather the fact that one of the young recruits among them, who was supposed to be just like everyone else, had been given a private dormitory, the kind reserved only for internal disciples.</p>

<p>They didn’t know if it was favoritism on Elder Shen’s part, but during meals, while talking with disciples recruited by him, they understood everything perfectly.</p>

<p>Qingyi had completed the test in less than five minutes.</p>

<p>With that in mind, no one dared to complain.</p>

<p>Even as an Ascended with mediocre spiritual roots, the sheer level of perception required to finish the test so quickly was enough to make him any formation master’s dream.</p>

<p>Many disappointed sighs echoed when they discovered that Qingyi seemed more interested in taking on a combat role than a production one. But in the end, he was a black dragon. They were beings of war and carnage.</p>

<p>It would be strange to see a black dragon content to become a formation master.</p>

<p>‘Hm… I wonder if I should study arrays…’ Qingyi’s lips curved slightly as he bit into a chicken drumstick.</p>

<p>He was eating in the cafeteria mainly out of respect, but right now he just wanted to lock himself in his room and go into the world of the mind.</p>

<p>Honestly, formations weren’t really his thing.</p>

<p>Some of his wives, like Auranys, had extensive knowledge on the subject, as did Ruxue.</p>

<p>But he himself barely knew the basics needed to identify them.</p>

<p>Whenever necessary, he broke them with brute force, so he had never needed to learn anything about them.</p>

<p>“Senior Brother Qingyi has already become a star in the sect…” Chen Wei sat down next to him, holding a plate of food. His servant settled right beside him.</p>

<p>“Aren’t you proud? Formation masters would likely throw themselves at your feet if you decided to become a production disciple.” Chen Wei turned his face, noticing the indifferent expression on the other’s face.</p>

<p>“Too much attention is problematic…” Qingyi stretched, turning his gaze toward Guo Tianhao, seated at a separate table for inner disciples.</p>

<p>Despite doing his best to ignore Qingyi, every time young Master Guo heard that name, a thread of murderous intent would escape him, visibly struggling to control his anger.</p>

<p>When he was recruited, he was the one everyone was talking about. About how he had conquered the hidden moonlight secret realm in less than an hour, a record never seen before.</p>

<p>“This is going to be annoying.’ Qingyi shook his head, finishing his meal.</p>

<p>“Oh… that guy!” Chen Wei muttered, frowning.</p>

<p>Young Master Luo, his rival, clung to Guo Tianhao like a leech. Chen Wei’s lips parted, but he stopped himself from speaking right away.</p>

<p>There were things better said in Sound transmission, where they could ensure no one overheard them.</p>

<p>[He must have a massive ego. Everything points to the sect master accepting his marriage proposal…]</p>

<p>‘Hm…’ Qingyi didn’t respond immediately.</p>

<p>He had only been there a short while, but he had already heard a great deal about the sect leader’s daughter.</p>

<p>Many called her the jewel of the far north, saying that even powerful experts from the central regions had visited the sect and offered entire star systems in exchange for her hand in marriage.</p>

<p>Strangely, the sect master had never accepted any of them, and no one had ever dared to take her by force.</p>

<p>[Is the sect leader’s daughter really that beautiful?] Qingyi asked, scratching his chin.</p>

<p>[Honestly, “beautiful” doesn’t even begin to describe her. My father once saw her from afar when he had the chance to visit the sect’s main base.</p>

<p>My mother said that, for a whole month, he just stared into space, his heart deeply moved, even though he’d only caught a glimpse of her from afar.]</p>

<p>Chen Wei paused briefly, taking a bite before continuing.</p>

<p>[Besides, they say that when she reached the astral body realm, she also passed through the hidden moonlight secret realm. The news was quickly suppressed, but her time is estimated by many to be less than a minute.]</p>

<p>The moment he heard that, Qingyi nearly choked.</p>

<p>[How reliable is this information?]</p>

<p>[It came from an elder, who quickly isolated himself and suppressed everything. I’d say about 80%.]</p>

<p>With that in mind, Qingyi’s eyes stared into the void.</p>

<p>To cover the hundred meters of the Qi manipulation test, he had spent about ten seconds.</p>

<p>He highly doubted that woman was capable of beating his time on the second test, but on the first, where he had spent a full four minutes and had even received a little help from Ruxue?</p>

<p>That was entirely possible.</p>

<p>How long had it taken her to clear that door? Ten seconds? Twenty?</p>

<p>Just thinking about it made Qingyi shudder.</p>

<p>That girl’s eyes couldn’t be inferior to his for her to have done something like that.</p>

<p>[Chen Wei… can you keep a secret?] Qingyi turned around, gripping his new friend’s shoulders.</p>

<p>[Hm? About what?] Chen Wei looked confused at first, but soon caught on to Qingyi’s train of thought, letting out a soft chuckle.</p>

<p>What do I need to do to me et the sect leader’s daughter?</p>

<p>[Oh… that’s no secret at all, senior brother, don’t worry, we all dream of getting the chance to meet her one day! Hehehe.] Chen Wei puffed out his chest.</p>

<p>Qingyi had saved his life. Now, it was time to repay him with all his great wisdom about the local beauties.</p>
