<p>"Death?" The Celestial General seemed genuinely confused by Qingyi’s words, as if he’d just heard something absurd.</p>

<p>A laugh full of contempt escaped his lips. "Didn’t you hear me, inferior? I am—"</p>

<p>"A pig of the Primordial Chaos, the Land of the True Gods. I know it very well." Qingyi grunted, without the slightest interest.</p>

<p>It was frustrating not to be able to use the Flames of Primordial Chaos.</p>

<p>He wanted to see despair on Qing Xuanyu’s face—the same despair he had seen in that dragon elder’s eyes.</p>

<p>The Flames of Life worked, of course, but their destructive potential was far too small. Using them to attack would be a waste.</p>

<p>Xuanyu froze at such disrespect.</p>

<p>The confusion vanished. His eyes went cold, and he held out a hand.</p>

<p>The power of the heavens—the same power that kept the Celestial Emperors at bay—descended, enveloping Qingyi’s pavilion in a protective bubble.</p>

<p>Qingyi made no move to stop it.</p>

<p>It was obvious that, at least for now, the Celestial Court wanted his child unharmed.</p>

<p>Fine. He could kill Xuanyu first. Then he would settle his scores with the heavens.</p>

<p>Qingyi floated upward, passing through the barrier, the wind tugging at the sleeves of his robes.</p>

<p>He lifted his gaze to Xuanyu, purple eyes brimming with frost.</p>

<p>"Come down. It hurts my neck—and my pride—to have to look up at a worm I’m about to kill."</p>

<p>Xuanyu’s face twisted in disdain, and a dry laugh rasped in his throat.</p>

<p>His right arm rose, the fabric of his celestial robes stretching taut over powerful muscles.</p>

<p>He opened his hand.</p>

<p>From the heavens, a golden bolt of lightning descended, tearing through the air with a sharp hiss until it struck his open palm. The smell of ozone burned in its wake.</p>

<p>An explosion engulfed his arm. When the flash and the roar faded, a golden spear remained in his grip.</p>

<p>His fingers clenched around the shaft, Qi flooding the weapon. The spear’s gold coiled over his hand and slithered up his arm like a living serpent, fangs parting before sinking into his shoulder.</p>

<p>Qingyi stared at the weapon, unimpressed.</p>

<p>It was clearly a mana weapon, strangely converted to function as a Qi weapon.</p>

<p>And its power was no less than that of the Heaven-Defying Thunder Sword.</p>

<p>"You really are a sickening guy, aren’t you? I’m too young to have known that Long Wujin, but my master spoke of him. He truly was—"</p>

<p>"I don’t care about you, your cuckolded master, or how much you two sucked Wujin’s dick in the Land of the True Gods."</p>

<p>Qingyi cut him off without ceremony.</p>

<p>"You’re here, your cultivation limited to the same level as mine, and your weapon no less powerful than mine. Very well. Bring that disgusting head over here so I can rip it off your shoulders."</p>

<p>Xuanyu fell silent.</p>

<p>He wasn’t surprised that Qingyi knew about the Land of the True Gods. The entire Celestial Court already knew the man was Wujin’s heir.</p>

<p>What truly filled his mouth with a terrible bitterness was the disrespect.</p>

<p>He would have liked to come with his cultivation at full strength. He couldn’t.</p>

<p>The Celestial Court had built its entire dominion over the Celestial Heavens to secure access to every "Child of Destiny"-level talent in that wretched region.</p>

<p>That was how they had secured many of the most powerful experts in the current court.</p>

<p>Still, there were far too many limitations on how they could act.</p>

<p>Xuanyu swung his spear. The golden tip ripped the air apart with a crack like a whip.</p>

<p>Qingyi gripped the Heaven-Defying Thunder Sword at his waist. The blade slid free of its sheath with a long metallic ring.</p>

<p>Small arcs of lightning danced along the edge, crackling softly.</p>

<p>The two stared each other down.</p>

<p>The air between them seemed to sink, heavy, as if the heavens themselves were holding their breath.</p>

<p>Their cultivation was locked at the very same level.</p>

<p>Xuanyu was a genius born of the Land of the True Gods. Qingyi, of the Mortal Heaven.</p>

<p>There was no excuse for either of them.</p>

<p>Xuanyu braced his foot against the void, dark eyes burning with an intense golden light.</p>

<p>He leaned forward, leveled the spear at Qingyi, and kicked off the void behind him.</p>

<p>Space crackled under the thrust, and his body shot forward, a streak of gold splitting the sky.</p>

<p>Three snakes wreathed in golden flames spun around the tip of his spear, whirling, cleaving the air ahead.</p>

<p>Behind him, a gigantic serpent emerged, jaws gaping wide, trailing his charge as if ready to devour Qingyi along with the strike itself.</p>

<p>At that very moment, Qingyi’s bloodline projection awoke at his back.</p>

<p>A dragon’s roar engulfed the entire Celestial Orthodox Martial Alliance.</p>

<p>It was the same roar that had once made many of the Celestial Emperors now watching this battle tremble.</p>

<p>The Heaven-Defying Thunder Sword and Xuanyu’s spear collided. Lightning bit into gold, and the shock of the impact rammed up both their arms.</p>

<p>In the same breath, the golden serpent and the black dragon slammed together, the serpent’s body coiling around the dragon’s neck while the dragon’s fangs sank deep into serpentine flesh.</p>

<p>For complete primordial lineages, the projection was more than just an ethereal form.</p>

<p>Both the dragon and the serpent were there, physically. Their bodies were real. Scale ground against scale, muscle against muscle.</p>

<p>For a moment, there was only silence.</p>

<p>In the next instant, the explosion came.</p>

<p>The shockwave from their clash swept across the world below. Shielded by the barrier, Qingyi’s pavilion stood untouched. Around it, destruction spread like a tide, carving a crater that stretched on for kilometers.</p>

<p>Dust and rock rose like a curtain, and the smell of churned earth and scorched air flooded everything.</p>

<p>And even then, the destruction did not stop.</p>

<p>The gale that followed uprooted entire trees. Ancient trunks toppled one after another, with crashes that, on any other day, would have been deafening.</p>

<p>That day, the sound of the clash between those two swallowed everything.</p>
