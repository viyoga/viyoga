<p>On the horizon, endless ranks of soldiers stretched as far as the eye could see, a cloud of blackened red dust rising as they marched towards the Forest of the Fallen Moon.</p>

<p>suddenly, their march stopped, the eyes of those in the vanguard widening in terror.</p>

<p>The leader of the Demon Cult.</p>

<p>The Celestial Demon.</p>

<p>The most powerful transcendent who had ever set foot on this earth in the last fifty thousand years.</p>

<p>At this very moment, he was lying on the ground, sunk in a smoking crater.</p>

<p>His right hand rose shakily to his face and he spat.</p>

<p>In his bloodied palm, a broken tooth, a subtle pain spreading through his jaw.</p>

<p>His red eyes slowly rose, meeting the icy gaze of a handsome young man.</p>

<p>Mochen and Qingyi had last met a few months ago. Back then, the young man could barely bear a casual movement of the demon cult leader’s fingers.</p>

<p>But now… now he possessed enough strength to hurt Mochen. Of course, Mochen had his guard down, not expecting such a strong attack.</p>

<p>Still, he could count on the fingers of a single hand how many transcendents had the ability to hurt him like that in mortal heaven.</p>

<p>“Biyue.” Qingyi turned his face, watching his other wives approache.</p>

<p>“You can unleash your anger on the demon army with the others. I promise i’ll make him pay for what he did to your father.”</p>

<p>Hearing those words, Biyue lowered her head.</p>

<p>Her eyes flicked from the advancing army to the reserve forces, who were desperately trying to reorganize themselves.</p>

<p>“Let’s go, Sister Biyue.” The poisonous beauty felt Elize’s delicate hands grasp hers.</p>

<p>Turning her beautiful face, she saw the excited faces of her sisters, all Qingyi’s wives, all eager to finally test the fruit of countless months of cultivation and training.</p>

<p>“Ah…” She sighed, using sound transmission to give some orders to her officers before accompanying the girls.</p>

<p>Their bodies exploded in power as the suppression of their powerful mortal-grade bloodlines fell upon the demonic army.</p>

<p>Biyue, Rongyan and Ruyan took the lead; poison, fire and blood falling on the Crimson Guard.</p>

<p>Hundreds of them, all in the Realm of Rebirth, the pride of the Demon Cult, were ripped to shreds.</p>

<p>Even the weakest of the priestesses could defeat the strongest among them with ease.</p>

<p>“Die, you filthy sluts!” A roar echoed as a man broke through the formation, his Qi exploding violently, making the world around him tremble.</p>

<p>Although Mochen was the only transcendent within the Demon Cult, there were still many other transcendents among the non-orthodox sects who had been forced to join them under threat of annihilation.</p>

<p>This man was one of those transcendents.</p>

<p>His hate-filled eyes focused on Elize, apparently the most delicate of the women. How could she fight efficiently with those massive, heavy things on her chest?</p>

<p>The great axe in his hands glowed with a dark, corrosive Qi. His sclera took on a black hue, red dots glowing in the center of his eyes.</p>

<p>Raising his weapon over his head, he advanced, swinging it towards Elize’s delicate, voluptuous figure with all his might.</p>

<p>The purple-haired beauty didn’t take a single step back.</p>

<p>Quite the opposite, her body was covered in the lineage projection of an enormous electric dragon, its lightning Qi tearing through the air with a bang.</p>

<p>“Ughn…” She grunted, her sword meeting his axe.</p>

<p>The ground beneath her delicate feet was turned to dust as the shockwave swept everything around.</p>

<p>“‘Yield!” The transcendent roared, his powerful muscles burning as he poured all his strength into that attack.</p>

<p>But nothing happened.</p>

<p>Elize stood firm, despite the trembling in her arms.</p>

<p>“I’ll help you, sister!” Yueli shouted, passing Elize in a blur, her sword flowing towards the transcendent’s unprotected neck.</p>

<p>He despaired, tried to retreat, to activate his most powerful movement technique.</p>

<p>Unfortunately for him, it was too late.</p>

<p>His throat was torn open by the blade of the blue-haired beauty and his axe slipped out of his trembling hands, giving Elize the freedom to pierce his heart.</p>

<p>His mouth opened in a terrified scream, a burst of light leaving his lips as his soul left his body, desperately trying to flee to the skies.</p>

<p>“You’re not going anywhere.” Yunfei, the girls’ master, acted. Her blade sliced the soul through the air, destroying it completely.</p>

<p>Exchanging satisfied glances, they smiled before moving on to their next victim.</p>

<p>In the distance, the leader of the Demon Sect rose from the ground.</p>

<p>His body floated up to the same height as Qingyi, both of them hovering in the sky.</p>

<p>“I have to admit, young man, you’ve truly surprised me.” Mochen laughed, turning his face to watch the one-sided massacre of his army.</p>

<p>“And I thought I was a man blessed by the heavens… With such talent, and with women of such caliber by your side, you are indeed incomparable. Far beyond what the reports I read suggested. Hahaha!”</p>

<p>A moment of silence followed Mochen’s laughter. His eyes regained their usual cruelty soon after.</p>

<p>“Even that damned little fox…” He spat, watching that snow-white fox freeze hundreds of his soldiers with a single attack.</p>

<p>From his waist, a crimson saber emerged, glowing menacingly.</p>

<p>“My daughter has never been this talented. And I don’t think any of those women are that talented either – not by birth.”</p>

<p>Mochen’s eyes filtered Qingyi from head to toe. “Someone granted them that power.”</p>

<p>He tilted his head, smiling cruelly.</p>

<p>“Tell me, boy. Do I have to cut you open like a frog and rip such a secret out of your guts? or will you obediently hand it over?”</p>

<p>The handsome young man didn’t answer immediately. He just shook his head slightly, his face dominated by a soft, confident smile.</p>

<p>The Heaven-Defying Thunder Sword slid out of its sheath with a metallic hiss, pointing straight at Mochen’s chest.</p>

<p>“For someone who is about to die…” Qingyi declared arrogantly. “You truly have plenty of time to talk.”</p>

<p>“Come on. Show your granddaddy here that you’re a filthy cuck less useless than your pathetic son.”</p>
