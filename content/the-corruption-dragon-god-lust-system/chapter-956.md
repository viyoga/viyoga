<p>The primordial star of creation wasn’t referring to the actual prison, of course.</p>

<p>The real prison was located in the Land of the True Gods, the only place capable of withstanding the presence of a being of Celestial’s caliber.</p>

<p>Even so, there were three entrances.</p>

<p>One of them was in the Primordial World, the Great Creator’s first creation, bathed in the light of the Primordial Star of Creation.</p>

<p>The second was in the heart of the Land of the True Gods.</p>

<p>The third was in the southernmost region of the Celestial Heaven, so heavily swarming with frenzied beasts that even Celestial Emperors had to exercise great caution when approaching it.</p>

<p>Suddenly, Qingyi stopped.</p>

<p>His footsteps fell silent in the middle of the empty corridor.</p>

<p>Was the Celestial Father wounded by his older brother?</p>

<p>Wait a minute...</p>

<p>Qingyi knew, from his conversations with Auranys, that the Celestial Father had set out on a mysterious journey and, upon his return, was gravely wounded.</p>

<p>Upon realizing the severity of those wounds, the ancestor of Tianjin—the original Immortal Golden Dragon Emperor—murdered his own ally and sovereign, sparking the war that killed nearly all the gods.</p>

<p>That had always been strange.</p>

<p>The Celestial Father was as powerful as Qingyi would be if he were in the realm of the Celestial Emperor, with the most powerful and steadfast foundation possible.</p>

<p>What on earth could injure someone of that caliber to the point that he couldn’t withstand an assassination attempt, even though he was so strong?</p>

<p>But if it had been his older brother, the first son of the Great Creator...</p>

<p>It all made sense now.</p>

<p>A chill ran down Qingyi’s spine.</p>

<p>[Recently, I could sense a presence approaching Celestial’s prison... and that presence received a portion of his power.]</p>

<p>"Do you believe it’s..."</p>

<p>[The one you call the Heavenly Demon. For Celestial to be able to grant power to someone like that, the seal on his prison must be weakening... I can’t check it myself, but I don’t think you have more than a hundred years before he returns.]</p>

<p>For a moment, Qingyi simply closed his eyes.</p>

<p>The hum of the formations on the walls seemed to grow louder in the silence.</p>

<p>The Heavenly Demon had received power from Celestial, likely the most powerful being in this universe, second only to the father whom Celestial himself had poisoned.</p>

<p>Wasn’t that a little too unfair?</p>

<p>How the hell was he supposed to deal with this?</p>

<p>Qingyi sighed, rubbing his forehead with his fingers before resuming his walk.</p>

<p>He needed to reach the Celestial Emperor’s realm as quickly as possible.</p>

<p>He was confident in Tianjin’s current strength, which was more than capable of killing up to ten Heavenly Demons at once, but... what about a Heavenly Demon with the power of a Celestial?</p>

<p>That was an entirely different matter.</p>

<p>Qingyi needed to have a serious talk with Tianjin about this, so they could begin preparing contingency plans as soon as possible.</p>

<p>Meanwhile, aboard the largest ship in the Demon World—equivalent to the Celestial Spear in power and glory—was a man.</p>

<p>The hall where he rested was hot and humid, illuminated by a faint red light seeping from the walls. The air carried a sweet scent of incense mixed with something heavier, resembling iron... or blood.</p>

<p>He was tall—perhaps three meters, perhaps a little more.</p>

<p>He was naked, his body submerged in a pool of spiritual energy, his arms outstretched and resting casually on either side of the edge.</p>

<p>The energy there was so dense it resembled a thick, silvery liquid, swirling slowly around his broad chest and rising in wisps of vapor.</p>

<p>He wore a gentle expression on his face as one of his handmaidens approached, her footsteps so light they made no sound on the floor.</p>

<p>She knelt a few meters from the pool, her head bowed.</p>

<p>"Lord Heavenly Demon, it has been confirmed that the Heavenly Spear is about to leave the Heavenly Orthodox Martial Alliance. In a week or two, they should be here." The servant stated in a calm, robotic voice.</p>

<p>The Heavenly Demon nodded silently and dismissed her with a lazy two-fingered gesture, turning his gaze to the figure seated on the other side of the pool.</p>

<p>It was the Demon of Eternal Slaughter, the Second Demon Lord—his younger brother and, in terms of raw power, the strongest among the seventy-two Demon Lords.</p>

<p>"Do you think Long Qingyi will manage to win this battle for those orthodox pigs?" the Demon of Eternal Slaughter asked his older brother, twirling the wine cup between his fingers.</p>

<p>"Yes... honestly, I’d be surprised if this war lasted more than a week before our young talents are completely wiped out..." The Heavenly Demon laughed.</p>

<p>The younger brother laughed as well, thinking it was some silly, unfunny joke.</p>

<p>Unfortunately, the laughter died in his throat as soon as he realized his older brother wasn’t joking.</p>

<p>"What’s the matter?" The Heavenly Demon took a sip of wine, savoring it slowly.</p>

<p>"Do you really think Qingyi would be worthy of my attention if he couldn’t even do that? But don’t worry, brother—the deaths of all these young talents serve a greater purpose! Soon, I’ll bring death to every one of them. To Qingyi, to his wives, to Tianjin, to every orthodox pig."</p>

<p>The Heavenly Demon stood up.</p>

<p>Spiritual energy flowed through his massive body, dripping back into the pool in heavy, gleaming drops.</p>

<p>"We, together, make this damned universe bleed."</p>

<p>The Demon of Eternal Slaughter shuddered.</p>

<p>That "together" from the Heavenly Demon wasn’t about him.</p>

<p>His older brother’s eyes weren’t even on him. They were lost somewhere in the distance, far beyond the walls of the ship.</p>

<p>The Heavenly Demon was talking about someone else.</p>

<p>But who?</p>

<p>The face of the demon of the eternal massacre hardened for a moment, but in the end, he sighed.</p>

<p>He sensed that his brother was different and knew there was a strange power within him, but what could he do?</p>

<p>If his brother was confident he could win that war, then they had already won.</p>
