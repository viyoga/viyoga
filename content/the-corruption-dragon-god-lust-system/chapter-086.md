<p>With Laosan’s words, Qingyi finally understood why the Serene Cloud Sect was there.</p>

<p>Secret realms were naturally rare and lucrative.</p>

<p>A secret realm like the Tempest Secret Realm, which only allowed cultivators of the Golden Core Realm and below to enter, wouldn’t usually attract the attention of a powerful sect like the Serene Cloud, at least not in such a remote location.</p>

<p>Especially one with a death rate as high as this.</p>

<p>But a broken realm was different. All its resources were exposed and experts of any level could enter freely.</p>

<p>Although the Tempest Secret Realm was in decline, at its birth it allowed even Nascent Soul Realm cultivators to enter. Even for a powerful sect, the value of such a realm breaking down was inestimable.</p>

<p>If they found just a single artifact from a Nascent Soul Realm cultivator, it would be enough to raise their power by a long margin.</p>

<p>’The bastards probably want to take advantage of the weakening of the realm to get the Lightning Spirit…’ Qingyi gritted his teeth.</p>

<p>If all they wanted to do was simply take the resources from breaking the secret realm, someone like Young Master Shen wouldn’t be there.</p>

<p>They would probably get in his way, but in the end, Qingyi just shook his head.</p>

<p>With his current power, he didn’t fear anyone below the Golden Core Realm. Even if Young Master Shen had dozens of guards, Qingyi would finish them all off.</p>

<p>A subtle smile played on his lips. It had been a long time since he had humiliated an arrogant young master.</p>

<p>The queue went smoothly after young master Shen left.</p>

<p>After a quick check, they finally reached the wide central street of the city.</p>

<p>Qingyi’s eyes swept over the various establishments until they finally focused on a pavilion selling cultivation items on the horizon.</p>

<p>It was a large and luxurious building, even by Mainland standards.</p>

<p>“Where can I find you?” he asked, causing Laosan to turn his head.</p>

<p>“Well… we’ll be at the entrance to the secret realm early in the morning…” He stroked his thinning beard, his eyes widening as Qingyi jumped out of the carriage.</p>

<p>“I’ll meet you there!” Qingyi declared and soon his back disappeared over the horizon, leaving the group speechless.</p>

<p>Generally, their contractors would like to keep a close eye on them, especially to prevent them from running off with the money.</p>

<p>Qingyi didn’t think much of them, hurrying his steps towards the pavilion.</p>

<p>He still had a fortune in Lust points, and it was time to finally improve his lineage even further.</p>

<p>“Good morning, young master, what can I do for you?” An attendant greeted him respectfully.</p>

<p>“I need a cultivation room, the highest level you have.”</p>

<p>Hearing those words, the attendant’s eyes lit up as she searched for something in a notebook in front of her desk.</p>

<p>That was one rich customer for sure!</p>

<p>“That’ll be 150 gold coins a night, young master, with a 70-times increase in Qi density and Foundation Realm training dummies!”</p>

<p>The 70-times increase in Qi density sounded very attractive, but the base Qi in the region was so scarce that Qingyi doubted whether it could even be half of what his private cultivation room in the sect offered.</p>

<p>He paid and was guided by the attendant to his cultivation room.</p>

<p>It was large, about a hundred square meters, surrounded by obsidian walls and illuminated by spirit crystals.</p>

<p>Qingyi stopped in the center of the room, feeling isolated from the outside world as the Qi density increased.</p>

<p>He breathed in deeply, feeling his cultivation move slightly.</p>

<p>Finally being away from the suffocation of such a low Qi density was truly liberating.</p>

<p>Focusing his mind, he opened his statuses.</p>

<p>[Name: Long Qingyi</p>

<p>Age: 18</p>

<p>Cultivation: Second stage of the foundation realm (700 lust points to upgrade)</p>

<p>Affinities: Lightning 60 – Sword: 60 – Charm: 1000 (can be upgraded in the lust store)</p>

<p>Spiritual roots: Seventh level lightning sword root (1000 lust points to upgrade, new roots can be bought in the lust store). )</p>

<p>Bloodline: Blood of the Corruption Dragon God (Rare grade, 10,000 lust points to upgrade.)</p>

<p>Constitution: Body of the primordial dragon (Rare grade, 10,000 lust points to upgrade.)</p>

<p>Lust points: 25637]</p>

<p>He was a little confused about what to do with so many Lust points.</p>

<p>He wasn’t ready to increase his cultivation yet, but one thing was certain: his bloodline and constitution were priorities, especially to fight with limited cultivation against a powerful enemy like the Lightning Spirit.</p>

<p>Preparing himself for the pain, he clicked on upgrade constitution first, trying to stay conscious while his body was gripped by a deep agony.</p>

<p>[Initiating constitution improvement…]</p>

<p>A muffled grunt escaped his lips as his muscles twitched, breaking down and being rebuilt in the next second.</p>

<p>His skin reddened before slowly becoming paler and smoother than before, his panting breaths gradually calming down.</p>

<p>[Constitution improved! Rare Grade -&gt; Epic Grade]</p>

<p>Qingyi barely gave himself time to look at that notification, immediately clicking on improving the bloodline.</p>

<p>[Initiating bloodline improvement…]</p>

<p>His eyes felt like they were about to explode and his blood boiled. But not only that – this time, even his skin reacted, slowly breaking and bathing his clothes in blood.</p>

<p>It was painful, but the pain wasn’t the worst part, the agony was. When the pain finally stopped, his body was completely covered in blood.</p>

<p>[Bloodline improved! Rare Grade -&gt; Epic Grade]</p>

<p>[You have unlocked a new lineage skill: Black Dragon Scales (lower)]</p>

<p>[A thread of draconic Qi awakens in your Dantian]</p>

<p>Noticing those notifications, Qingyi recovered from the shock of the pain immediately, spending a hundred Lust points on a pill to calm his mind before looking at his hand and focusing his draconic Qi on it.</p>

<p>His skin changed instantly, covering itself with black scales, glowing a faint purple under the light that illuminated the room.</p>

<p>His eyes glittered as he stood up, picking up his sword and swinging it with all his might towards his arm.</p>

<p>A metallic clang echoed as the sword bounced off his skin. Even using his lightning sword Qi, the effect was the same.</p>

<p>There was no improvement in his draconic eyes, but he didn’t care – that was even better than an improvement in his draconic eyes!</p>
