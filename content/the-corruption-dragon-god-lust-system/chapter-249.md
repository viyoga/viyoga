<p>Yanxiao still remembered the night that little girl was born. She wasn’t afraid of him, she didn’t cry when she saw him.</p>

<p>For tens of thousands of years, he had guarded the dynasty that ruled the Eternal River Sect, the heirs of the sect’s founder and Yanxiao’s best friend.</p>

<p>A position that was soon taken by that girl.</p>

<p>For 3,000 years, they had been best friends, until, on that day, she changed.</p>

<p>She was no longer the best friend Yanxiao once had.</p>

<p>His eyes were filled with fury, fury enough to completely blind him from reason, fury that fell upon Qingyi.</p>

<p>The handsome young man resisted, his knees trembling but not giving way, unlike the ground beneath his feet, which soon swallowed up his calf.</p>

<p>Even so, he didn’t give in, a drop of blood dripping from his clenched fists as his fingernails sank into the palm of his hand.</p>

<p>“I had no contact with the sect leader, I was born and raised in the outer region and never received any outside help for my cultivation!”</p>

<p>Roared the handsome young man, and the fire in Yanxiao’s eyes grew even stronger, growing until a loud voice interrupted him.</p>

<p>“Yanxiao, what the hell do you think you’re doing?” A figure appeared on the horizon, its Qi no less than Yanxiao’s, taking over the mansion hall and enveloping Qingyi, protecting him.</p>

<p>Accompanying that voice came a crash, Yanxiao’s face being thrown to the side, his eyes wide.</p>

<p>He had just been slapped.</p>

<p>The figure in front of him was a large moon tiger, a mythical beast, just like him, guardian of the Hidden Moonlight Sect.</p>

<p>Its eyes were a serene blue and its thick, soft fur an elegant grayish white.</p>

<p>“I’ve lost control, I’m sorry.” Yanxiao lowered his head, large, sharp teeth being revealed as he contracted his facial muscles, trying to control the scowl on his face.</p>

<p>“You can go now, little one, I’ll knock some sense into that senile old fool.” The moon tigress spoke, her eyes focused on Yanxiao, who shuddered slightly.</p>

<p>There were certain rumors about Yanxiao and the guardian of the hidden moonlight sect being lovers, but few believed them.</p>

<p>How could such high beings surrender to a forbidden relationship?</p>

<p>But seeing how that tigress looked at Yanxiao, Qingyi had to admit that such a rumor didn’t seem to be a lie.</p>

<p>He obviously left immediately, controlling his trembling steps and spitting out a gulp of blood.</p>

<p>That wasn’t pleasant…</p>

<p>Even though he understood Yanxiao, Qingyi was unable to hide a twinge of anger rising in his heart.</p>

<p>But he suppressed it.</p>

<p>“A scent similar to the sect leader on me… what could he mean by that?” muttered Qingyi.</p>

<p>Everything led to one answer: that woman had come to this world with Khaedryss.</p>

<p>Was she related to Elize’s family in some way?</p>

<p>Qingyi wasn’t sure, but he knew he had to investigate this.</p>

<p>The moment he was powerful enough to challenge Yanxiao, he would try to meet the sect leader.</p>

<p>“For now… I need to get stronger, I need to reach the top of the top,” said Qingyi, looking at his status screen.</p>

<p>Cultivation: Ninth Stage of the Nascent Soul Realm (2.000,000 lust points to upgrade)</p>

<p>Affinities: Lightning 98 – Sword: 97 – Charm: 1000 (can be upgraded in the lust store)</p>

<p>Constitution: Primordial Dragon Body (Mythical grade, 10,000,000 lust points to upgrade.)</p>

<p>His wealth had increased even more, to a level where he no longer feared the huge increase in the price of the next cultivation realm.</p>

<p>Before secluding himself for cultivation behind, Qingyi met up with Biyue, handing her some talent and lineage enhancement pills, enough to raise everything to mortal grade.</p>

<p>After giving her a little more love, he headed towards one of the many forests surrounding the orthodox alliance, looking for a good place to train.</p>

<p>After a bit of searching, he finally found it: a deep valley exactly between two mountains, forming a natural barrier with a single entrance.</p>

<p>Sitting cross-legged, Qingyi clicked away to improve his cultivation.</p>

<p>Similar to the realm of the iron body, the realm of rebirth was a complete reconstruction of the body, one even more violent than the previous one.</p>

<p>Qingyi felt every piece of skin on his body burn, his eyes injected with blood as a painful grunt escaped his lips.</p>

<p>His body arched in a strange way as his bones broke and his muscles twitched until they tore.</p>

<p>At the same time, four separate Qi’s exploded from his body, which floated a few meters off the ground.</p>

<p>On one side, his lightning sword Qi cut trees in half, powerful lightning tearing through the ground for hundreds of meters.</p>

<p>On the other, its flames set fire to the forest, burning everything that its ice Qi didn’t freeze.</p>

<p>If anyone had seen that advance, they would have realized something: Qingyi had managed to cause a projection, not of lineage, but of his own cultivation, to tear through the skies.</p>

<p>Something no less rare than a tribulation.</p>

<p>When the outer shell of skin finally shattered, revealing Qingyi’s pure, rebuilt body underneath, the world around him had already changed completely.</p>

<p>Destruction spread for miles, the entire valley reduced to a black floor of burnt and destroyed trees, the mountains fortunately protecting the rest of the forest from the power of his breakthrough.</p>

<p>He was even glad that he had chosen this place rather than any other cultivation room, he was sure that few in the alliance would be able to withstand such power.</p>

<p>With a soft sigh, Qingyi retracted his hand.</p>

<p>He didn’t use Qi, either externally or internally. Only his physical power was needed.</p>

<p>With a gleam in his eye, Qingyi punched and the air in front of him shattered, a shockwave spreading as the punch created a vacuum around him, pushing everything forward and opening a tear for dozens of meters.</p>

<p>All this with pure physical force.</p>

<p>No Qi, no special techniques.</p>

<p>Yes… that power was good…</p>

<p>But that wasn’t the end of Qingyi’s training.</p>

<p>He needed new affinities and his lightning steps were already lagging behind the skills of other experts of his level.</p>

<p>Qingyi really needed a new movement technique.</p>
