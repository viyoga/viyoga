<p>"Ah... that tiger is a complete lunatic! I hope I never have to see him again!" The fattie shuddered from head to toe just thinking about Bai Hu Lie swinging that poor disciple around like a club.</p>

<p>"Well... the sect is pretty big. I’ll just avoid him, hahaha." Xiaohu let out an awkward chuckle, watching from afar as Bai Hu Lie left the arena, accompanied by a few elders.</p>

<p>"Afraid of some hick from the Northern Region?" Qingyi laughed.</p>

<p>"You’re a hick from the Northern Region too, and look what you did to Zihao." The chubby young man shook his head as if pointing out the obvious.</p>

<p>He then puffed out his chest with all the pride in the world.</p>

<p>"I am Ling Xiaohu! Coward, liquor lover, and expert at staying alive!" He pounded his fist against his chest, announcing each word as if it were a glorious title.</p>

<p>"I may have been an orphan, but only because my father kicked me out of the family! He was a coward too, just like my father’s father, my father’s father’s father, and my father’s father’s father’s mother... who, actually, was a father."</p>

<p>Xiaohu raised his hand before Qingyi could even open his mouth.</p>

<p>"But well... the poor fellow was cursed after eating a strange herb and ended up turning into a woman. Thus was born the second matriarch of our family, who ruled for a glorious thirty minutes before suffering a heart attack."</p>

<p>The corner of Qingyi’s mouth twitched. Xiaohu, however, remained as serious as a monk reciting scriptures.</p>

<p>"It doesn’t seem like much, I know, but that was twenty-five minutes longer than the first matriarch to rule our family. She died five minutes after taking office... right after showing courage in the face of a demonic cultivator."</p>

<p>"The women of our family have never had much luck, and, well..." Xiaohu shook his head, brushing away the memories. "It was a strange time, but that doesn’t matter!"</p>

<p>"What matters is that, after her, we embraced a vow of cowardice! And in the last twenty generations of my family, every single one of our leaders has died of old age!" He jabbed a finger at the sky, reciting it as if it were the clan’s sacred motto.</p>

<p>"Cowardice can’t move mountains, but it ensures the mountain stays in place!"</p>

<p>A few passing disciples turned to stare. Xiaohu’s chin only rose higher.</p>

<p>"Caution ensures the mountain stays in place. Cowardice ensures you’ll never be able to protect it," Qingyi replied calmly, then let out a chuckle. "But it’s truly impressive. Your family must be incredible."</p>

<p>He wasn’t joking.</p>

<p>The Celestial Realm was a violent place.</p>

<p>In a world where cultivators died every day over treasures, territories, and wounded pride, twenty consecutive generations dying of old age—even without high cultivation and engaging only in minor disputes—was still a great achievement.</p>

<p>"Anyway, his name is Bai Hu Lie." Qingyi steered the conversation back to the original topic.</p>

<p>"Let’s go to the Competitors’ Hall. He should be there." He pointed toward an entrance leading to the back of the arena, a subtle smile appearing on his face. "Don’t worry, he won’t be interested in a weakling like you."</p>

<p>"Uh? Bai Hu Lie? Weakling? Oh... hahaha! Sure, you’re just trying to scare me, aren’t you?" Xiaohu waved his hand, laughing half-heartedly.</p>

<p>"All right, he’s a lunatic—probably famous in the Northern Region—but you’re a refined man. How could you possibly be friends with him?"</p>

<p>His voice was brimming with disbelief.</p>

<p>"So, who are we really going to meet? A girlfriend of yours who couldn’t come along? You’re the heir to the Dragon God of Corruption, and with your good looks, you must have hundreds throwing themselves into your arms!"</p>

<p>Qingyi just shook his head and kept walking.</p>

<p>The Competitors’ Hall greeted them with a buzz of voices, the clinking of glasses, and warm air heavy with the smell of liquor and the bitter scent of medicinal herbs.</p>

<p>The chubby young man scanned the room.</p>

<p>Refined young men lounged everywhere, some wrapped in bandages and still bearing the marks Bai Hu Lie had left on them, others completely unscathed.</p>

<p>Stunning beauties stared at the two of them with interest, whispering to one another behind their sleeves. Or, to be more specific, they were staring at Qingyi.</p>

<p>And, of course, there he was.</p>

<p>That enormous tiger, nearly three meters tall and weighing over six hundred kilograms of pure muscle, chugging from a twenty-liter barrel of liquor as if it were an ordinary glass.</p>

<p>Xiaohu realized they were heading straight toward the tiger, and for a moment, his heart sank.</p>

<p>No...</p>

<p>He didn’t want to get anywhere near that tiger!</p>

<p>Xiaohu was just one step away from the Stellar Sovereign Realm. His cultivation far surpassed Bai Hu Lie’s and was the highest in his family’s history, despite his young age.</p>

<p>Still, he was no warrior.</p>

<p>He was a poet, a refined dancer, a connoisseur of liquor, wine, and beauty!</p>

<p>"Oh..." Bai Hu Lie’s eyes suddenly locked onto Qingyi. His lips peeled back into a savage, fanged grin before he let out a roar of laughter.</p>

<p>"Long Qingyi, brother-in-law!" Bai Hu Lie closed the distance between them in the blink of an eye, the floor quaking beneath his weight.</p>

<p>Qingyi was a tall man, standing at two meters now. Still, it was hard not to look like a child next to Bai Hu Lie, who towered a full meter above him.</p>

<p>The tiger yanked Qingyi into a tight bear hug, all but swallowing him up in those thick, furry arms.</p>

<p>Then he shoved Qingyi aside and snatched Xiaohu up in the same crushing embrace.</p>

<p>The air burst from the chubby man’s lungs in a wheeze, and he felt his soul nearly leave his body.</p>

<p>"Uh... this one smells like fear. He’s not your friend, is he?" the tiger asked, turning the chubby man’s head from side to side. "He’s got a lot of fat. I prefer meat with more muscle, but I think he’d still make for a good barbecue."</p>

<p>Xiaohu let out a strangled sound, something between a nervous laugh and a cry.</p>
