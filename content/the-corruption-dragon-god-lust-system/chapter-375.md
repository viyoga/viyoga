<p>–</p>

<p>[Name: Long Qingyi</p>

<p>Age: 21</p>

<p>Cultivation: First Stage Of The Enlightened Soul Realm (50,000,000,000 lust points to upgrade)</p>

<p>Affinities: Lightning: 1000 – Sword: 1000 – Fire: 1000 – Ice: 1000 – Charm: 1500 (can be upgraded in the lust store)</p>

<p>Spiritual roots: Transcendent-level Primordial Creation Seed spirit root (Lightning, sword, fire, ice) (250,000,000,000 lust points to upgrade, new roots can be bought in the lust store).</p>

<p>Lineage: Blood of the Corruption Dragon God (Earth grade, 100,000,000,000 lust points to upgrade.)</p>

<p>Constitution: Primordial Dragon Body (Earth grade, 100,000,000,000 lust points to upgrade.)</p>

<p>Lust points: 19,202,689,741]</p>

<p>Qingyi immediately opened his status screen, looking at his new spiritual roots.</p>

<p>He had expected many things, but a perfect fusion of the four elements was certainly not one of them, especially fire and ice.</p>

<p>Raising a single hand, he called for fire and ice at the same time and then, for the flames of primordial chaos, his law.</p>

<p>The resistance between fire and ice was null, unlike before, where there was always conflict.</p>

<p>What’s more, he suffered far less damage when he summoned the gray flames, his body now able to withstand them for longer and suffering less damage.</p>

<p>This time, he was able to keep it burning for a full three minutes, quite different from before, when it barely lasted a single minute.</p>

<p>Although he should have been jumping for joy, there was still one thing that Qingyi couldn’t get out of his mind: the name of that spiritual root.</p>

<p>Primordial creation seed, a type of spirit root made to absorb all kinds of Qi.</p>

<p>Above the immortal heaven, no name was just a name, everything had a meaning.</p>

<p>To have such a great name in the immortal heaven, that spirit root had to have an incomparable origin.</p>

<p>“Ruxue, have you ever heard of the spiritual root of the primordial creation seed?” Qingyi asked, answered by nothing but a long silence.</p>

<p>“I… don’t know. But the primordial creation seed-” Ruxue suddenly shut her mouth, drawing a defeated sigh from Qingyi.</p>

<p>If she couldn’t speak, he wouldn’t force her.</p>

<p>‘System, what can you tell me?’ Qingyi asked.</p>

<p>[Access denied.]</p>

<p>‘Ugh… do I really have to reach the celestial heavens to know that?’ Qingyi sighed in defeat.</p>

<p>[Before the heavens ruled everything below the celestial heavens, this world was very different. The seed of creation is from before this time, before the heavens were even forged.</p>

<p>Don’t speak It’s name in vain, not beneath the heavens, just mentioning it could cause heavenly tribulation].</p>

<p>“I see…” Qingyi sighed, scratching his chin.</p>

<p>[That goddess from the Western Continent can tell you more about it, she’s from that era].</p>

<p>The system explained, and Qingyi just nodded.</p>

<p>Well, that didn’t matter now, not in the face of the supreme fact: he had achieved a technique of his own and now it was time to name it.</p>

<p>Looking at the countless notifications received from the system, he focused on a specific one.</p>

<p>[You’ve just created your own combat technique! Do you want to name it?]</p>

<p>Qingyi looked at that prompt for a brief moment, thinking about the name. He needed something that matched the technique.</p>

<p>Perhaps “Blade Destroyer slash”? No… it was too small. That technique was more than that.</p>

<p>‘Heaven-shattering slash… Qingyi remembered what he saw in his mind’s world, the surrounding space breaking into a deep cut.</p>

<p>If he were powerful enough, he could cut through the immortal heavens like that too, couldn’t he? Like he did in the mortal heavens.</p>

<p>“Yes, Heaven-Shattering Divine Slash will do.” Qingyi smiled, choosing that name.</p>

<p>[You’ve created a new technique!]</p>

<p>[Combat Technique: Heaven -Shattering Divine Slash</p>

<p>Description: Forged by an incomparable young talent, it’s a technique capable of cutting through the heavens themselves, its heat and sharpness unrivaled.</p>

<p>Rank: Astral (can be improved with future enlightenment)]</p>

<p>That last notification took Qingyi by surprise.</p>

<p>He knew the technique would be powerful, but astral grade, the rarest grade of techniques and items in the immortal heaven? That was something he really hadn’t expected.</p>

<p>Now I had not only an incomparable spiritual root, but even an incomparable technique…</p>

<p>How could he not be happy about that?</p>

<p>Qingyi whistled excitedly as he stood up, stretching his body, feeling his new power.</p>

<p>His meridians had more than tripled in capacity to move Qi, as had his dantian, which could now easily hold four times as much Qi as before.</p>

<p>Of course, that wasn’t all.</p>

<p>The fusion of his spiritual roots and the increase in his affinity meant that he was able to demonstrate much more power using much less</p>

<p>Qi.</p>

<p>That had been a simply absurd leap in power and now, Qingyi was sure: he was a match for true immortals.</p>

<p>Flashing a big smile, Qingyi stood up and left the cultivation room, heading straight for the great golden palace that rose majestically on the horizon.</p>

<p>He had won the divine beast tournament and, as well as the chance to be blessed by the fox people, he would also receive a gift in person from the patriarch of the dragon people, Long Tianjin.</p>

<p>The moment he appeared on the grand golden staircase, he was greeted by two draconic guards and was then guided through the palace to a luxurious throne room.</p>

<p>There, he immediately saw two figures.</p>

<p>Long Tianjin was sitting on his throne, reading an ancient scroll. Long</p>

<p>Qianyao, his daughter, was beside him, reading another scroll.</p>

<p>Noticing Qingyi’s arrival, Qianyao immediately raised her head, brushing a wild lock of silver hair out of the front of her beautiful face, her full lips displaying a rosy smile.</p>

<p>“Long Qingyi greets senior Long Tianjin,” Qingyi bowed, watching that golden dragon’s eyes shift from the scroll to him.</p>

<p>“Long Qingyi, get up, don’t be so polite, hahaha,” Tianjin said, flashing a tired smile and then walking over to Qingyi. “Tell me, young man, what do you plan to do after receiving the blessing of the fox people?”</p>
