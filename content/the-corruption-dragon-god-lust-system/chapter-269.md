<p>“Cuck? Granddaddy?” Mochen repeated, still unable to comprehend the young man’s boundless audacity.</p>

<p>Did Qingyi really believe that, simply because he’d knocked out a tooth, they were on the same level?</p>

<p>Suddenly, Mochen’s heart was filled with a hatred so deep that it was no less than what he felt when his best friend perished.</p>

<p>“You’re from the Eternal River Sect…” Mochen growled, “Your guardian is that damned tiger, Yanxiao… I fought him a few centuries ago. He barely lasted twenty exchanges before running away with his tail between his legs.”</p>

<p>“I wonder… how many can you handle?” The instant Mochen’s voice ceased, his saber tore through the air.</p>

<p>A slash of crimson demonic Qi flew towards Qingyi, expanding violently.</p>

<p>The handsome young man didn’t dare hold back, not in front of someone like the leader of the Demon Cult.</p>

<p>Gritting his teeth, he summoned the Eternal Wall of the Icy Lord, forming a thick barrier of crystalline ice in front of him.</p>

<p>Mochen’s attack landed with a thud, tearing through half the thickness of the wall before finally losing its momentum.</p>

<p>‘Now’s my chance!’ Qingyi repeated in his mind.</p>

<p>His draconic Qi rushed through his meridians, the huge figure of a dragon slowly materializing on his back.</p>

<p>Its thunderous roar echoed through the skies, paralyzing Mochen for a brief instant.</p>

<p>The leader of the Demon Cult retreated slightly, his eyes fixed on the black-scaled, purple-eyed dragon hovering behind Qingyi.</p>

<p>He could feel every hair on his body stand on end. His own bloodline hid in the depths of his gut, trembling at the sight of Qingyi’s bloodline.</p>

<p>Was it… afraid of that boy? No… that wasn’t possible!</p>

<p>Mochen refused to accept that reality.</p>

<p>His lineage was the lineage of the Celestial Demon, the one destined to be the Emperor of Heaven, to kill the gods themselves!</p>

<p>That was the prophecy of his people, and he would fulfill it.</p>

<p>Qingyi just watched coldly as Mochen activated his own lineage projection.</p>

<p>The image of a huge demon with blood-red eyes and long twisted horns manifested on his back.</p>

<p>His body also began to change, not unlike the transformation his son’s body had undergone before being killed by Qingyi.</p>

<p>No. It was even more intense.</p>

<p>His clothes tore. His muscles swelled. Its broken black horns flickered majestically as its body reached more than five meters high, towering above Qingyi.</p>

<p>“I’ll rip out your tongue and shove it up your ass!” Mochen roared, attacking again with unbridled fury.</p>

<p>“Oh? Where did you learn such vulgar words? Don’t make your granddaddy have to spank you!” Qingyi narrowly dodged Mochen’s massive body.</p>

<p>His long black hair fluttered as Mochen’s blade passed inches from his face, severing a few strands.</p>

<p>“SILENCE!” Mochen completely lost control, attacking like a crazed beast.</p>

<p>Every movement of his sword sent out waves of Qi that destroyed everything in a radius of hundreds of meters, opening up the earth into deep fissures, causing powerful tremors.</p>

<p>‘It’s getting dangerous.’ Qingyi parried Mochen’s sword.</p>

<p>The Lightning Sword’s Qi and the demonic Qi collided, the surrounding air boiling. The temperature rose high enough to instantly annihilate anything that dared to approach them.</p>

<p>With a grunt of effort, Qingyi broke away from Mochen.</p>

<p>Activating his Lightning Blink, his body transformed into purple lightning, circling around Mochen.</p>

<p>The leader of the Demon Cult did not allowed himself to be left behind. A scream full of hatred escaped his lips as he transformed into a cloud of demonic Qi.</p>

<p>His movement technique was in no way inferior to Qingyi’s in sheer speed.</p>

<p>The speed of both continued to increase, breaking through the sound barrier, becoming nothing but blurs of light before colliding in the center of the battlefield.</p>

<p>A single, short, almost imperceptible flash. But their swords clashed more than fifty times in that instant before they separated, their bodies appearing a few dozen meters apart.</p>

<p>A whole second of absolute stillness followed before the effects of their attacks manifested themselves.</p>

<p>Fifty deafening bangs exploded simultaneously. Trees were uprooted. The ground within a radius of three kilometers turned to dust, creating a massive crater.</p>

<p>Mochen was panting, his eyes bloodshot, a deep gash tearing his chest from side to side.</p>

<p>Qingyi was only a little better, coughing up a gulp of blood, the black dragon scales on his abdomen visibly damaged, crimson liquid dripping down and staining his clothes.</p>

<p>“What? Tired already?” Qingyi teased. “You’re not going to let your granddaddy down now, are you?”</p>

<p>He saw the expression on Mochen’s face intensify dangerously, his demonic Qi flowing unchecked like an overflowing river.</p>

<p>The Demon Cult leader closed his eyes, trying to control his anger. But a voice he knew very well broke through that attempt.</p>

<p>“Get him, hubby! Blow his balls off just like you did to brother!” Ruyan roared, imbuing Qi into her voice to make sure it reached both of them.</p>

<p>Qingyi only laughed lightly, turning his face towards that naughty little demoness.</p>

<p>Her body was drenched in blood as she killed non-stop.</p>

<p>“Ah…” The leader of the Demon Cult sighed deeply.</p>

<p>His fists closed on the hilt of his sword tighter than ever.</p>

<p>“Do you know what my Law is, brat?” Mochen asked, his voice suddenly calm.</p>

<p>“No, and i don’t think it will save your pathetic life.” Qingyi spat.</p>

<p>“We’ll see.” Mochen growled lowly, his eyes filled with a strange energy.</p>

<p>“Get away from him, quickly!” Ruxue shouted in Qingyi’s mind</p>

<p>Qingyi’s eyes immediately noticed the anomaly and all the girls’ protective rings activated at the same time, pulling them into the World of Mind.</p>

<p>No… it wasn’t just that.</p>

<p>Qingyi activated his Lightning Blink, moving away from Mochen quickly.</p>

<p>He could see clearly now.</p>

<p>All around Mochen, from the few living plants under his feet to the demon cultivators fighting tens of kilometers away, everything began to wither.</p>

<p>All the vital energy was being violently absorbed by Mochen’s body.</p>

<p>The Demon Cult leader readied his blade, retracting it towards his body and aiming it at Qingyi’s chest.</p>

<p>His body now glowed with a sickly crimson light, all that stolen vital energy converging on his sword.</p>

<p>“The name of this technique is the Heaven-Piercing Demon Sword.” Mochen declared. “Created by our ancestor so that he could defeat the Heavenly Tribulation and ascend to the immortal heavens…”</p>

<p>His red eyes shone with murderous intent.</p>

<p>“Be grateful for having the honor of being killed by it!”</p>
