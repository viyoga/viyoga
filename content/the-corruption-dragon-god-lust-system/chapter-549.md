<p>A single bolt of lightning, but one of power greater than anything Qingyi had ever faced in his entire life.</p>

<p>The sharp blade of Qingyi’s sword tore the lightning of the heavenly tribulation in two, his own lightning continuing into the heavens and striking the eye of the storm.</p>
