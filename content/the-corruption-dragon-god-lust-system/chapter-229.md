<p>“I’m sorry.” Qingyi lowered his gaze.</p>

<p>He didn’t know much about Mojie, but he didn’t seem like a bad person.</p>

<p>“It’s natural,” Yedu shook his head. “People always die, that’s how this line of work works.”</p>

<p>After these words, there was a moment of silence before Yedu regained his composure.</p>

<p>“We need to organize some things now, get ready to contact the targets and confirm this information.” Yedu said, taking out a document and handing it to Qingyi.</p>

<p>The handsome young man took the document, reading it calmly.</p>

<p>That calm didn’t last long, soon turning to deep shock.</p>

<p>That document was a report from another undercover group, claiming that the chief strategist was seriously ill and that the demon cult was planning an all-out attack on the orthodox alliance, trying to decapitate it in a single battle.</p>

<p>How could that be real?</p>

<p>The demon cult should need at least a few more decades to prepare for an all-out war against the orthodox alliance and from what Ruyan told him, the chief strategist shouldn’t be ill.</p>

<p>“Are you sure about this information?” He asked, putting the document under the table.</p>

<p>“No, that’s what we’re here for.” Yedu said, turning to Heidu.</p>

<p>“You managed to get in touch with one of the great elder’s servants, didn’t you? Did you find out anything?”</p>

<p>“Not much,” the fattie shook his head.</p>

<p>“I tried using some soul techniques to scan his memory, but nothing worked, as if he had a blockage.”</p>

<p>“Hm…” Yedu scratched his chin, wondering what to do.</p>

<p>“I also had contact with one of the targets.” Qingyi said, placing the token he had received from Xue Mo on the table.</p>

<p>Yedu looked confused for a brief moment, but that confusion soon turned to shock as he grabbed the token.</p>

<p>Studying it carefully, he raised his face. “How did you get this? Do you know what this token represents?”</p>

<p>“No. All I know is that it was with Xue Mo’s personal servant.</p>

<p>After he angered the young lord and was killed, Xue Mo gave it to me, perhaps thinking I was a suitable servant.”</p>

<p>“Ah… each of the demon cult leader’s children receives a personal servant who should accompany them throughout their lives; this is Xue Mo’s personal servant token, one of the most powerful items in the sect, having it makes you no less than a core disciple.” Yedu spoke, still unable to believe his own eyes.</p>

<p>After a moment’s thought, he focused his eyes on Qingyi again. “You approach Xue Mo. Heidu and I will try to get close to the great guardian.</p>

<p>The chief strategist is almost always with the cult leader, so we’ll only approach him if necessary.”</p>

<p>After chatting some more and settling some other less important matters, the trio split up.</p>

<p>Qingyi was thinking of going straight back to Ruyan’s mansion, but after thinking for a moment, he decided it was time to make a breakthrough.</p>

<p>It was a challenge to find a suitable cultivation room.</p>

<p>Almost all the cultivation rooms there only had demonic Qi, with the few offering totally pure Qi being absurdly expensive.</p>

<p>With pain in his heart, Qingyi paid the price before entering the cultivation room and calling up his status screen.</p>

<p>[Name: Long Qingyi</p>

<p>Age: 19</p>

<p>Cultivation: Ninth Stage of the Crystalline Core Realm (500,000 lust points to upgrade)</p>

<p>Affinities: Lightning 98 – Sword: 97 – Charm: 1000 (can be upgraded in the lust store)</p>

<p>Spiritual roots: Mortal-level three-colored spirit root (Lightning sword, fire, ice) (1,000,000 lust points to upgrade, new roots can be bought in the lust store).</p>

<p>Lineage: Blood of the Corruption Dragon God (Mythical grade, 10,000,000 lust points to upgrade.)</p>

<p>Constitution: Primordial Dragon Body (Mythical grade, 10,000,000 lust points to upgrade.)</p>

<p>Lust points: 3,803,093]</p>

<p>After a moment’s hesitation, Qingyi clicked to improve his cultivation.</p>

<p>[Beginning advancement to the Nascent Soul Realm, the host must take care when building his Sea of Mind].</p>

<p>A notification from the system arrived and before Qingyi could even react, he was seized by intense pain, but this time in a different way.</p>

<p>His mind seemed to be torn from his body, everything becoming a messy blur as the space around him distorted.</p>

<p>When he realized it, he was naked, in a strange and totally dark place, his feet sinking up to his heels in water.</p>

<p>He couldn’t see anything, not even with his draconic eyes.</p>

<p>The reason he couldn’t see anything there was simple: there was nothing there.</p>

<p>That was his world of the mind; empty, untouched and ultimately non-existent.</p>

<p>“First, bring your dantian here.” Ruxue’s sweet, mature voice echoed in Qingyi’s mind and he felt her appear beside him.</p>

<p>This time, she wasn’t in her human form, taking on the appearance of a strange being of light, floating a few meters off the ground, filled with a thunderous aura.</p>

<p>“That’s my real form, ignore it and focus on forming your sea of mind.” Ruxue warned and Qingyi nodded.</p>

<p>Bringing up his dantian there… how the hell was he supposed to do that?</p>

<p>Qingyi asked, but no answer came.</p>

<p>He closed his eyes, thought, reflected and then just meditated.</p>

<p>After long hours, he felt his dantian respond, subtly, gently, almost non-existently.</p>

<p>It was only after a whole day that a firm response finally came and the world around him changed as a small orb of light left his abdomen, floating tenuously above the ground.</p>

<p>This orb completely swept away the darkness that surrounded him, a bluish sky appeared above his head as the orb shot towards the sky at an absurd speed, shining brighter than ever.</p>

<p>Soon, Qingyi found himself in a totally different place.</p>

<p>As far as his eyes could see, he saw an endless ocean, but one whose deepest places were barely twenty centimeters deep.</p>

<p>The sky above him was a deep blue, painted with ethereal white clouds and illuminated by his dantian, which shimmered above it all.</p>

<p>“Did I succeed?” Qingyi asked, turning to Ruxue who had now managed to take on her human form.</p>

<p>Her face was filled with shock, one hand covering her mouth as she stared into Qingyi’s sea of mind.</p>
