<p>The Heavenly Demon was more than just a tyrant and schemer.</p>

<p>Above all else, he was an actual ruler.</p>

<p>At this very moment, he sat upon his throne of black bones, receiving numerous guests from every corner of the demonic realm. He was the supreme judge of countless matters.</p>

<p>Disputes between sects, trade agreements, wars between nations, laws governing contact between mortal demons and cultivating demons... All of that, and much more, fell within the scope of his governance.</p>

<p>It was an ordinary day, and he listened with bored patience as two vassal demonic nations argued over control of a spiritual mining planet.</p>

<p>The trial was nearing its end, and the Heavenly Demon was about to declare which of the two would claim the planet.</p>

<p>His lips parted, but no words came out.</p>

<p>His eyes—with black sclera and bright red pupils—shuddered violently, and he suddenly rose from his throne.</p>

<p>His gigantic figure, with gray skin and long black horns, exuded such brutal pressure that everyone present collapsed to their knees, bodies trembling against the polished obsidian floor.</p>

<p>The vassals thought they had said something to anger their sovereign, but that was not the case.</p>

<p>The Heavenly Demon slowly turned his face, and in the next moment, vanished from the hall without leaving a trace.</p>

<p>When he reappeared, he stood inside a gigantic vault embedded in the deepest recesses of his palace.</p>

<p>Once filled with riches piled up to the ceiling, the chamber was now empty, silent, and hollow.</p>

<p>The Heavenly Demon felt his heart sink, one hand rising to his chest as if trying to hold back something that was shattering inside.</p>

<p>"No... no, no, no, no, no, no, no, no, no, no, no, no, NO!!!"</p>

<p>He repeated the word over and over, each utterance louder than the last, until the final one twisted into a bestial roar that echoed off the vault’s walls.</p>

<p>He ignored it all.</p>

<p>All that fortune—the accumulation of millions of years—stolen in a single instant—didn’t even cause his heart to flutter.</p>

<p>His gaze locked on the black steel door at the back of the vault, and he could not tear it away.</p>

<p>His pupils trembled uncontrollably, and in his mind, only one thought hammered away ceaselessly: ’That couldn’t be possible... how could that be possible?’</p>

<p>His teeth ground against one another as he closed the distance to the door, passing straight through it in a single second.</p>

<p>When he saw the interior of that circular chamber, completely empty, he could take it no longer.</p>

<p>The Heavenly Demon, one of the most powerful experts in all of the celestial heavens, lost control.</p>

<p>His lips parted in a terrifying roar, and every defensive array of the black palace activated at once, glowing desperately to prevent the entire place from collapsing under the weight of his fury.</p>

<p>He didn’t care about the trillions in stolen Qi crystals, nor about all those treasures that an ordinary sect would spend ages accumulating.</p>

<p>He was old. About twenty million years, to be exact.</p>

<p>He had walked the Land of the True Gods and fought side by side with a Primordial Empress. His ancestral sword, alone, was worth more than all the wealth of the entire celestial realm combined.</p>

<p>To him, that wealth was insignificant. But there was something in that chamber that was not.</p>

<p>His memories, his trophies, his future wives.</p>

<p>That—he could not accept losing.</p>

<p>The Heavenly Demon’s body vanished, reappearing outside the black palace, his divine senses spreading ceaselessly in all directions, sweeping the cosmos in search of any trace.</p>

<p>Where... where was it?</p>

<p>His face was so contorted with rage that it resembled that of a wild beast, gliding through the cosmos in search of the slightest clue.</p>

<p>He found only one trail and followed it relentlessly, his oppressive aura descending upon a cluster of asteroids right on the border separating the central region of his empire from the rest of the demonic territory.</p>

<p>The next moment, that aura vanished completely. After that, nothing.</p>

<p>The Heavenly Demon scoured every inch of the region but found no sign whatsoever, except for a faint remnant of Qi permeating a collapsed asteroid—a trail that led directly to the Celestial Orthodox Martial Alliance.</p>

<p>He did not hesitate.</p>

<p>His body shot forward at blinding speed, tearing through the cosmos like a blade cuts through fabric, stars and planets swept into nothingness in his wake.</p>

<p>He would kill those swine, every last one of them!</p>

<p>Even for a Celestial Emperor, traversing such a vast distance in such a short time was an almost impossible feat.</p>

<p>But the Heavenly Demon was no ordinary Celestial Emperor.</p>

<p>The very space around him collapsed as he moved, a black, bloodstained sword materializing in his hands, pulsing with hunger.</p>

<p>At this moment, he wanted nothing more than to kill.</p>

<p>He wanted only to wrest an explanation from those pigs of the Celestial Orthodox Martial Alliance, even if it meant making them scream for it.</p>

<p>The Heavenly Demon reached the border between the demonic world and the orthodox world in a blink.</p>

<p>A single swing of his sword there, and hundreds of billions of lives would be wiped out across all the star systems surrounding him.</p>

<p>He stopped, raising his sword above his head, his immense body floating imposingly in the cosmic void, his aura staining the nearby stars a sickly red.</p>

<p>"Die."</p>

<p>His sword came crashing down.</p>

<p>That was a dangerous attack, and it would most certainly start a war. A war in which he would be at a disadvantage, now that he had lost all the treasures from his vault.</p>

<p>He didn’t care.</p>

<p>He just wanted to kill as many humans as possible.</p>

<p>From his blade, a black, crescent-shaped slash expanded outward, rapidly reaching hundreds of millions of kilometers and continuing to grow, devouring everything in its path.</p>

<p>In the instant following his attack, however, the frenzied smile brimming with rage on his face faded, giving way to an expression as cold as the Void itself.</p>

<p>A golden spear tore through the Void, colliding head-on with his attack and shattering it into countless fragments of golden light that scattered across space.</p>

<p>"Heavenly Demon... what does this mean? Did you lose something in our lands?"</p>

<p>Tianjin’s figure slowly emerged from the void, his hands elegantly clasped behind his back, his face adorned with a polite smile.</p>
