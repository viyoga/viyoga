<p>Yedu was naturally very happy with Qingyi’s response, his deep green eyes twinkling slightly as he placed a thin hand on the young man’s shoulder.</p>

<p>Of course, Qingyi wasn’t exactly suited to the kind of mission the snake battalion was doing, but his talent made up for it.</p>

<p>They soon left the dormitories, following the wide central street that divided the snake battalion zone in half.</p>

<p>As Yedu’s squadron was considerably wealthy, they had their own rented residence, separate from the communal dormitories.</p>

<p>It wasn’t exactly a luxurious place, being in the far northern corner of the snake battalion wall, but it was a spacious pavilion, with fifty-fold Qi density-increasing cultivation rooms and private bedrooms.</p>

<p>It was poison, and a powerful poison at that.</p>

<p>Yedu immediately covered his nose as he circulated his Qi, trying to stop the poisonous Qi from reaching Qingyi.</p>

<p>To his surprise, the handsome young man didn’t even flinch, as if that poisonous Qi was nothing in front of him.</p>

<p>“Fuck, what are you doing? I told you not to let that shit leak out!” A thin, sickly figure ran through the pavilion, entering a door on the side of the main building that led to the basement of the residence.</p>

<p>It was a tall man with green hair and thin features, not unlike Yedu himself.</p>

<p>Now that Qingyi thought about it, those features were quite common among poisonous cultivators.</p>

<p>Perhaps a side effect of their poisons or something, although not all of them were like that.</p>

<p>Behind the sickly man, another young man appeared, a chubby man with a tearful look, his face covered in bruises.</p>

<p>“Ah… Sorry about the sight, Junior Brother Qingyi. The fattie is Heidu, our illusionist, he’s a bit clumsy, so be careful with delicate things near him.</p>

<p>The other one is Mojie, my brother.” Yedu introduced the two while guiding Qingyi through the pavilion.</p>

<p>They didn’t spend much time there.</p>

<p>Yedu only briefly introduced Qingyi to the other two squad members before heading off to the office to officially register him.</p>

<p>“Why are there so few members in your squad, senior brother Yedu?” Qingyi asked.</p>

<p>Normally, a squadron would have around 12 members, including auxiliaries and field members.</p>

<p>A squad with only three members was rare.</p>

<p>“Our last mission was difficult, we lost almost all the members in a fucked-up infiltration.</p>

<p>“I’m not scared.” Qingyi shook his head, not even a single hint of fear appearing on his face.</p>

<p>“Well, let’s use an item to temporarily camouflage our Qi, change our appearances and then use inside contacts to infiltrate.</p>

<p>It’s nothing too difficult, we just need to achieve a certain level of prestige and get close to some powerful figures.”</p>

<p>“I understand.” Qingyi replied, scratching his chin thoughtfully.</p>

<p>He had heard of many types of concealment and illusion techniques, but techniques and artifacts capable of changing someone’s Qi were rare, especially those capable of fooling experts.</p>

<p>Such an item couldn’t be cheap.</p>

<p>After a few more minutes of walking, they finally reached the administrative office where Qingyi went through another long battery of tests, signing dozens of documents before finally being able to breathe a sigh of relief.</p>

<p>He had to spend almost thirty minutes just reading and signing documents and contracts.</p>

<p>How on earth did such a place survive with so much bureaucracy?</p>

<p>Fortunately, it was all over.</p>

<p>“Come on, Junior Brother Qingyi, I’ll buy you a drink to celebrate you joining our squadron.” Yedu gave Qingyi a firm slap on the back as they turned away.</p>

<p>Suddenly, they stopped.</p>

<p>Behind them, the attendant suddenly raised her head, as if receiving a sound transmission.</p>

<p>“Uh… young master Qingyi, Commander Biyue has requested your presence in her quarters,” she said, her face filled with strangeness.</p>

<p>That wasn’t exactly common…</p>

<p>Yedu exchanged glances with Qingyi, also surprised.</p>

<p>Tang Yedu obviously knew Tang Biyue, she wasn’t the kind of woman who would ask to meet a man like that alone for no good reason.</p>

<p>“Uh… Should I go too?” Yedu asked and the attendant shook her head. “No, just Young Master Qingyi.”</p>

<p>In the end, Yedu could only leave, leaving Qingyi standing there alone, wondering what that woman wanted.</p>

<p>Standing up and bowing respectfully, the attendant led Qingyi up a wide staircase to the central tower of the administrative palace, stopping in front of the door to the only room on the top floor.</p>

<p>The attendant bowed one last time before turning and leaving, leaving Qingyi alone behind.</p>
