<p>No words Qingyi could recognize escaped from the elven woman’s lips.</p>

<p>Only a strange, fast, and beautiful chant, before an intense sense of danger overwhelmed the handsome young man’s body. Immediately, he bent his entire body to the side.</p>

<p>The elven woman’s hands became nothing more than a blur. Her blade flashed as she slashed at a speed that Qingyi was only able to follow with his draconic eyes.</p>

<p>There was no sound, no destruction. Only a quick, silent movement, followed by a cold breeze.</p>

<p>In the next second, the iron bars behind Qingyi opened. The same happened to trees miles away, their huge trunks split by a perfect cut, falling to the ground.</p>

<p>“Ah… it’s been a long time since I’ve received such a warm welcome… Qingyi flashed a gentle smile.</p>

<p>However, the elven woman’s eyes did not linger on him for even a second longer; she soon turned to Ruxue.</p>

<p>To Qingyi’s surprise, she immediately lowered her head.</p>

<p>“I salute the divine spirit… this little one is Aeryn Silverveil.” She spoke, her voice low, noble, and gentle.</p>

<p>It was exactly the voice Qingyi expected from an elven beauty: sweet, feminine, and composed, but not in an exaggerated way.</p>

<p>Every word was perfect, from her pronunciation to her simple tone.</p>

<p>With a gentle look, he opened her status screen.</p>

<p>[Name: Aeryn Silverveil</p>

<p>Age: 23</p>

<p>Corruption: 0/100</p>

<p>Chastity: Intact</p>

<p>Lust: 3%</p>

<p>Talent: Great Genius</p>

<p>Reward: Extremely high</p>

<p>Spiritual roots: None/Immortal Level Mana Heart</p>

<p>Mana Manipulation: Ninth Ring</p>

<p>Traits: Faithful, high elven royal blood, arrogant noble, daughter of nature, non-human common sense, unique sense of humor.]</p>

<p>As he expected, she was a true and arrogant high elf beauty. With a sigh, he just turned to Ruxue.</p>

<p>The spirit beauty was a little surprised by how respectful this woman, whom she had never seen in her life, was being.</p>

<p>She was even more surprised by the fact that Aeryn knew she was a spirit. Of course, she knew that elves worshipped spirits, especially nature spirits like her, but still…</p>

<p>Ruxue was about to respond when Aeryn’s lips parted again.</p>

<p>“Is this human man your enslaver, divine spirit? Should I kill him and set you free?”</p>

<p>“I told you this aunt was scary…” The little girl muttered into Ruxue’s chest, immediately hiding her face from Aeryn’s gaze.</p>

<p>The moment Ruxue heard those words, she was unable to stop a laugh from escaping her lips. Aeryn’s face was immediately filled with confusion.</p>

<p>“He is my husband, not my enslaver. Besides, I wouldn’t exactly consider him human.” Ruxue said, wiping her eyes.</p>

<p>“Not human?” Aeryn’s eyes focused on Qingyi again.</p>

<p>She stopped in front of him, tilting her face toward his shoulder. Qingyi didn’t think much of it, just allowing Aeryn to examine him, his eyes falling over her shoulders.</p>

<p>As her torso leaned forward, her buttocks protruded.</p>

<p>In that position, he could see perfectly: two large, round, perfect globes, the stretchy fabric demarcating them perfectly. Her slim waist and the curve of her hips made them look even bigger.</p>

<p>Aeryn’s face stopped inches from Qingyi’s chest. She sniffed once before realizing.</p>

<p>Qingyi was, in fact, not a human.</p>

<p>“You’re a dragon…” Aeryn whispered.</p>

<p>Her pale, high cheeks were taken over by a soft, reddish blush, her noble features curving in satisfaction.</p>

<p>Of course, how could an honored divine spirit stay with just any human?</p>

<p>Qingyi merely enjoyed Aeryn’s reaction, her eyes adorably avoiding his. There was still arrogance in her, but not the total indifference of her first glance.</p>

<p>“What’s going on here?” Suddenly, an old female voice caught everyone’s attention.</p>

<p>From inside the church, an old woman emerged. She was no less than eighty years old, Clearly a nun, carrying a cross on her chest.</p>

<p>“Mother!” Alice squirmed in Ruxue’s arms, her little feet tapping excitedly on the floor as she threw herself into the nun’s arms.</p>

<p>The old woman was not Alice’s real mother, but the leader of that church, which also served as an orphanage. But that didn’t matter much to the little girl.</p>

<p>“Alice, where were you, dear? I told you not to run away like that!” the old woman said.</p>

<p>Another group of children soon appeared at the church door, watching the newcomers with curiosity.</p>

<p>“I was just-” Before Alice could even finish her sentence, she was met with another barrage of scolding.</p>

<p>Soon, with her head bowed, she was forced into the orphanage.</p>

<p>Of course, her discouragement was short-lived.</p>

<p>Soon, Qingyi could hear her excited voice, telling her friends how she was attacked by a monster and how Qingyi exterminated them in a single moment.</p>

<p>The old nun just sighed, her eyes focusing on Aeryn. Honestly, she didn’t like having that woman there.</p>

<p>She was a chosen hero, yes, but she was not a true follower of Auranys.</p>

<p>Just like every other damn elf, she was an infidel. How could she become one of the chosen heroes?</p>

<p>Slowly, the nun’s eyes shifted to Qingyi. To be more specific, to the necklace he wore.</p>

<p>Aeryn had noticed this, but paid little attention to it.</p>

<p>Of course, the nun couldn’t react so coldly, her face filling with the deepest shock.</p>

<p>“I-is that… Impossible!” The old woman said, approaching Qingyi.</p>

<p>She grabbed the item she carried on her own chest, pointing it toward Qingyi’s chest.</p>

<p>Immediately, there was a reaction, the item glowing brightly.</p>

<p>It was real… Qingyi was the last of the five chosen heroes! The one who would save them from calamity!</p>

<p>The old woman clutched her own chest tightly, turning to Aeryn.</p>

<p>“Didn’t you see that this man is also one of the chosen heroes?” The nun asked.</p>

<p>“Hm… is he?” Aeryn turned her face slightly before answering.</p>

<p>She spoke with all the force of someone who was absolutely not there for political reasons and who really cared about Auranys: “Cool.”</p>

<p>The old woman was unable to stop her mouth from dropping.</p>

<p>Cool?</p>

<p>Why the hell was she the only one there treating this matter with the seriousness it deserved?</p>
