<p>“So… did hubby sleep well? Fufufu~~” Ruyan giggled softly when she noticed Qingyi’s eyes opening.</p>

<p>“What do you think?” Qingyi returned her smile as he stood up and looked outside.</p>

<p>He had slept for almost a whole day, which wasn’t so much when considering that he had almost two hundred days of accumulated mental fatigue.</p>

<p>But it had all been worth it.</p>

<p>Qingyi’s greatest weakness was still his lack of combat experience, especially when compared to experts with hundreds or thousands of years.</p>

<p>With those two hundred days, he had focused all his transcendental talent on solving this and the result was noticeable.</p>

<p>He had cut the time he spent using a skill by almost half, his battle senses becoming even sharper.</p>

<p>To be better than that, only if he were able to create a clone of himself in the seaf of the mind, something that, according to Ruxue, might be possible in the future.</p>

<p>That would make it much easier to train and gain real combat experience.</p>

<p>But now was not the time to think about that.</p>

<p>” Contacting your brother is part of my mission, I got his servant token.” Qingyi spoke, those words making Ruyan’s eyes light up.</p>

<p>“Uh? Is the hubby going to kill him now? Blow his balls off?” She stood up excitedly.</p>

<p>“Hahaha… yes, I’m going to blow his balls off, but not now. I have a mission to complete first.”</p>

<p>Ruyan looked a little disappointed, but soon recovered her sparkle.</p>

<p>Exchanging a passionate kiss with her one last time, Qingyi put the mask prepared by Heidu on his face and activated the concealment arrays of the three-bladed monarch set before leaving.</p>

<p>As the most talented son of the demon cult leader, Xue Mo naturally had his own private residence, a mansion no smaller in size than a small village.</p>

<p>Unlike Ruyan’s mansion, this one was completely surrounded by hundreds of guards, the weakest in the Nascent Soul Realm.</p>

<p>Another thousand servants looked after the mansion’s every need, ensuring that everything was perfect.</p>

<p>“Halt there, disciple, do you have permission to be here?” One of the guards protecting the main gate asked, stepping forward.</p>

<p>Qingyi immediately reached into his robes and pulled out a small token.</p>

<p>The moment they saw that token, the look on the guards’ faces changed completely and they greeted Qingyi with respect, allowing him to enter.</p>

<p>“You must be the young lord’s new personal servant.” A middle-aged, elegant-looking servant approached Qingyi. “What’s your name?”</p>

<p>“It’s Zhao, miss, I don’t have a surname.”</p>

<p>“I see, Mr. Zhao, come with me.” The servant bowed before entering the mansion.</p>

<p>“Do you know why young Lord Xue Mo chose you to be his new personal servant?” The servant asked, looking at Qingyi out of the corner of her eye.</p>

<p>“Because I was lucky and because the last servant made a mistake.”</p>

<p>“Exactly.” The servant nodded, her thin lips curving into a soft smile.</p>

<p>“Then don’t make any mistakes.” She stopped in front of a door, giving it a gentle knock to open it.</p>

<p>Inside, a room resembling a research office, shelves full of jars with tiny red worms taking over.</p>

<p>“What’s that?”</p>

<p>“Curiosity also kills.” The servant answered Qingyi’s question.</p>

<p>“These are heartworms, they will lodge in your heart after you eat it. Any thought of betrayal and the worm will devour your heart and destroy you from the inside.”</p>

<p>Qingyi shuddered at those words.</p>

<p>As an avid reader of Xianxia novels in his past life, he naturally knew about those worms.</p>

<p>But the thought of having one inside his own chest immediately made him rethink his life choices.</p>

<p>[The host has nothing to fear, his bloodline will destroy the worm as soon as it enters his body]</p>

<p>The system’s words made him relax a little, but not completely.</p>

<p>He still had to swallow that disgusting thing.</p>

<p>The servant took one of the jars, opened it and took out the worm.</p>

<p>“Open your mouth,” she ordered as the little creature squirmed in her fingers.</p>

<p>Qingyi closed his eyes.</p>

<p>Was there still time to abandon the mission? Was riding on Khaedryss, grabbing Ruyan and getting the hell out of there an option?</p>

<p>That was exactly what Qingyi wanted to do.</p>

<p>Unfortunately, the servant didn’t seem to be very patient.</p>

<p>Grabbing Qingyi’s cheeks, she shoved the worm into his mouth.</p>

<p>The handsome young man had to fight the urge to vomit, his body seized by slight spasms as the creature made its way into his viscera.</p>

<p>Fortunately, this didn’t last long.</p>

<p>His draconic Qi soon began to move, burning the heartworm to pieces.</p>

<p>The servant just made sure that Qingyi had swallowed everything before turning away. “Come with me, I’ll take you to young lord Xue Mo.”</p>

<p>The handsome young man only coughed a few times before, with trembling steps, accompanying her to one of the courtyards of the mansion, where two men could be seen sitting.</p>

<p>One of these was the young Lord Xue Mo, his handsome, youthful face bearing a big smile as he chatted with another man.</p>

<p>This was an obese man, easily over two hundred kilos of pure fat, devouring a bottle of wine as if he were drinking a glass of cold, refreshing water.</p>

<p>He was the grand elder of the demon cult, Xue Mo’s uncle.</p>

<p>“Ah… this is good, young Lord Xue Mo, hahaha! Truly blessed by the most divine heavenly demon!” The great elder laughed, letting out a great burp as he placed the wine on the table between them.</p>

<p>“But you didn’t call me here just to spoil me with wine of this quality, did you?”</p>

<p>“Indeed not, uncle, I have a favor to ask of you.” Xue Mo spoke awkwardly, pulling out another bottle of wine and handing it to his uncle.</p>

<p>“Speak up, nephew, we’re family. Don’t be ashamed.”</p>

<p>Despite those words, Xue Mo still hesitated for a brief moment before finally speaking, “I’m going on a hunt in the forbidden forest, when I get back from that hunt, I plan to ask my father to make Ruyan my concubine, I want uncle’s support in this matter.”</p>
