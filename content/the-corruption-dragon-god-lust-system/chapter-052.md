<p>“At that time, there was still no division between the orthodox and non-orthodox world, but when the war broke out, everything changed.</p>

<p>All the kingdoms and families had to take a side, support the powerful demon lord, or the declining orthodox sects, the Tang family was no different.”</p>

<p>“And which did you choose?”</p>

<p>“Both,” Yandu shook his head.</p>

<p>The war lasted 15 grueling years, millions died and ended with a coalition of the leaders of the most powerful sects and families of the time storming the palace of the demon sect and beheading the demon lord.”</p>

<p>Yandu paused for a moment, observing the curious expression on Qingyi’s face before continuing.</p>

<p>“But that wasn’t the end of it. The remaining clans gathered a large army and advanced against the orthodox sects, to take revenge and put an end to that war.”</p>

<p>“And the demon cultivators lost,” Qingyi commented, drawing a sigh from Yandu.</p>

<p>“Yes, but they weren’t the only losers.”</p>

<p>“The great elder who had joined the demon lord used our forbidden technique. Filling the Nine Peaks Basin with poison and killing hundreds of millions, reducing the countless powerful sects that existed at the time to a mere handful.”</p>

<p>Yandu’s voice shone, as if he were talking about the most incredible feat of all mankind, but he stopped at the last moment.</p>

<p>His eyes became opaque and a flicker of anger appeared on his face.</p>

<p>“That was the moment for our family to take control of this world, to forge a world under the power of poison, but do you know what they did?”</p>

<p>Yandu gritted his teeth, his voice turning into a growl.</p>

<p>“They begged for forgiveness! Our patriarch knelt to the heavens, vowed never again to use our power for any means other than noble and orthodox, destroying and scattering most of our secret techniques.</p>

<p>We’re a family of poisonous cultivators, born killers, but still, those damned old men cling to stupid ideals, refusing to face an enemy unless it’s head-on.”</p>

<p>Yandu took a deep breath, controlling his anger.</p>

<p>“Don’t you think that’s pathetic?” he asked, turning his face to Qingyi.</p>

<p>“Clinging to these useless traditions, accepting being just one of the most powerful families on the Blue Moon Continent instead of embracing our true power and going to the horizon and beyond?”</p>

<p>Qingyi lowered his head, thinking for a brief moment.</p>

<p>It was an incredible story that he had never heard of.</p>

<p>Neither his master nor even the sect’s books had any information about this war beyond its name.</p>

<p>Knowing all this really broadened his horizon, but even so, there was something that made him uncomfortable.</p>

<p>Thinking about his words for a moment, Qingyi raised his head again. “Why are you telling me this?”</p>

<p>At those words, Tang Yandu smiled.</p>

<p>That was exactly the question he had been waiting for.</p>

<p>“Why don’t you come with me? Become my disciple. Together, we can rediscover our family’s lost techniques and shape this world in our own image!” Yandu exclaimed, looking at Qingyi expectantly.</p>

<p>“Just imbue a little of your Qi into my body, and I alone can free myself from my sister’s restrictions. I have a spatial technique to get us out of here in the blink of an eye.”</p>

<p>Qingyi paused for a moment. That was a tempting proposition.</p>

<p>Despite his youthful appearance, Tang Yandu was an old master who had lived for over four hundred years and a great expert of the Nascent Soul Realm.</p>

<p>For someone with no future, following his plan would probably be the best course of action.</p>

<p>Unfortunately for him, Qingyi was not someone without a future.</p>

<p>The system was more than anything Tang Yandu could ever achieve.</p>

<p>“I’m sorry,” Qingyi smiled. “As I said, I’m not as dumb as Young Master Xu.”</p>

<p>Without even waiting for an answer, he turned his back on Yandu and walked out of the hut.</p>

<p>“Wait, we can think of something, I have a lot of hidden riches-” Tang Yandu’s voice was cut off by the slamming of the door.</p>

<p>“Ah…” He sighed. In his centuries of life, it was the first time he had met someone like this.</p>

<p>“Couldn’t my little sister have found a simpler man?” He gritted his teeth, but in the end just smiled bitterly.</p>
