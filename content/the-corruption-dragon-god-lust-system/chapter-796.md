<p>"Y-you son of a bitch, I’m going to—"</p>

<p>Beifeng’s words died in his throat as Qingyi’s boot came crashing down on his skull with a wet, sickening thud.</p>

<p>Qingyi didn’t even glance back at the body. Beifeng wasn’t worth a single thought anymore, much less the luxury of prolonged torture.</p>

<p>All of his attention was now focused on the words of that demonic cultivator.</p>

<p>[Be careful with this guy. He’s a celestial emperor concealing his cultivation level, a servant of that woman for at least a few million years.</p>

<p>Among the demonic race, he must be only weaker than the Heavenly Demon himself.</p>

<p>The Heavenly Demon was—and probably still is—nothing more than a fly compared to the Primordial Demon Empress at the height of her power, before she was poisoned.]</p>

<p>Auranys’s voice echoed in Qingyi’s mind.</p>

<p>She had already crossed swords with that demon before, during the bloody war between the cultivators and the followers of the Celestial Father.</p>

<p>At the time, he had been considerably weaker than the Heavenly Demon, but even so, he ranked among the most powerful Celestial Emperors of that era.</p>

<p>Now, much older and infinitely more experienced, his power had almost certainly multiplied several times over across the millennia.</p>

<p>"Your Majesty, upon her resurrection, the first thing the Empress did was send us to this region with two missions."</p>

<p>"The first: to recover an artifact capable of masking her demonic aura while she infiltrates the Orthodox Martial Alliance, so she can recover her power before taking revenge against the Heavenly Demon!"</p>

<p>"The second was to order us to report any information regarding Long Qingyi, master of primordial chaos... her destined husband!"</p>

<p>Shuoming’s voice trembled with a mixture of reverence and urgency, his eyes briefly darting toward Beifeng’s shattered body before snapping back.</p>

<p>"You are the master of the flames of primordial chaos, and your name is Long Qingyi! By your deeds, you can only be the destined prince consort!"</p>

<p>Qingyi let out a dry laugh at the absurdity, shaking his head slowly.</p>

<p>It was hard to believe something so insane, but Shuoming’s eyes burned with absolute seriousness.</p>

<p>More than that, there was a fervent devotion in them—something that far surpassed the mere loyalty of a servant toward a superior.</p>

<p>"If you are truly as loyal as you claim..." Qingyi took a step forward, his eyes cold, "cripple your own cultivation."</p>

<p>Shuoming’s eyes filled with sheer terror, his entire body shuddering involuntarily.</p>

<p>"I did not give you permission to hesitate."</p>

<p>Faced with Qingyi’s icy voice, Shuoming reacted without hesitation, driving his own palm into his chest and crushing his Dantian with a wet, nauseating crunch.</p>

<p>Waves of chaotic energy erupted throughout his body. The cultivation he’d accumulated over millions of years began bleeding out of him like blood pouring from a mortal wound, distorting the air around him with violent, unstable pulses.</p>

<p>He didn’t even have time for the blood to rise to his throat before Qingyi had already unleashed the Nine Paths of Creation Reversal Art upon him.</p>

<p>"Ah... w-what?..." Shuoming muttered through clenched teeth, his eyes wide with disbelief as his cultivation flowed back into him, filling the yawning void his Dantian had left only moments before.</p>

<p>Without a word, Qingyi turned to Chen Wei and his wife.</p>

<p>"You know what will happen if anyone besides us finds out about this, don’t you?"</p>

<p>Both nodded at the same moment, not daring even to lift their eyes from the floor.</p>

<p>"Good, since you didn’t give in to Beifeng’s torture even after all this time, I’m sure you’re loyal." He gave a slight smile, turning his face toward Shuoming.</p>

<p>"Come on, get up," he ordered, and Shuoming sprang to his feet at once, still gasping for breath. "I acknowledge your loyalty."</p>

<p>Without delay, Qingyi mentally accessed the system store, purchasing a primordial-grade artifact at a cost of nearly two hundred thousand lust points.</p>

<p>Shuoming had no idea that Qingyi was capable of restoring his lost cultivation, but even so, he—a celestial emperor in his prime—had crippled his own cultivation at a single word.</p>

<p>That, in and of itself, was all the proof Qingyi needed to trust him.</p>

<p>After all, who would throw millions of years of effort down the drain for a simple order, if not someone whose loyalty was absolute?</p>

<p>Qingyi held out the artifact to Shuoming, who examined it for a brief moment before his eyes widened in sheer disbelief.</p>

<p>It was even superior to what they had been trying to obtain for so long!</p>

<p>"Thank you very much, Your Majesty! I will report your presence to the Empress right away; she wishes to see you urgently!"</p>

<p>"No. I will meet her myself when I go to the central region. Tell her I’ll be in the care of the great-great-grandson of the venerable immortal Dragon Emperor—though it won’t be long before my name becomes known there as well."</p>

<p>Qingyi looked up toward the horizon, sensing energy signatures approaching in the distance.</p>

<p>"Now leave immediately. The Pavilion of Celestial Justice will be here in a few minutes."</p>

<p>Shuoming didn’t dare question the orders, bowing deeply before activating a spatial teleportation artifact.</p>

<p>In a burst of silver light, he and his more than one hundred men vanished without a trace.</p>

<p>Qingyi let out a long sigh.</p>

<p>Unfortunately, he hadn’t managed to have a good battle here, but that didn’t matter much in the end.</p>

<p>A few minutes later, five elders from the Celestial Justice Enforcement Pavilion arrived, accompanied by hundreds more disciples.</p>

<p>Among them was Elder Shen himself, who, despite belonging to the Recruitment Pavilion, had insisted on coming along anyway.</p>

<p>The moment he saw Qingyi safe and sound, Elder Shen’s face lit up with pure relief and excitement.</p>

<p>As for the fact that Beifeng had been executed without permission, he hardly gave it a second thought.</p>

<p>After all, Qingyi was the sect’s young lord.</p>

<p>Theoretically, he was second only to the sect leader himself, since, by right of inheritance, he was next in line for succession.</p>

<p>He wouldn’t be punished even for killing a disciple without any justification, and would only face real trouble if he committed an indiscriminate massacre.</p>

<p>As for Beifeng... although he was an important disciple, in the end he had been killed with more than enough justification.</p>

<p>"Ah... Long Qingyi, you really never cease to amaze me, hahahaha!" Elder Shen roared with laughter, pulling Qingyi into a bear hug and patting him vigorously on the back.</p>

<p>"Ah... it’s a shame that—"</p>

<p>Elder Shen’s words died in his throat when, in the very next instant, an overwhelming, suffocating aura crashed down upon everyone present.</p>

<p>"Who dared to lay a hand on my Beifeng?!"</p>

<p>The arrogance in that aura lasted only the briefest moment, for its owner needed but a single heartbeat to realize just how fucked he was.</p>
