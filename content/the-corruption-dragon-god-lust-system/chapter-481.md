<p>The more Lucios watched the two, the more his thin, old lips curved, a huge smile taking over his wrinkled face.</p>

<p>He waved his hands, and then two orbs of light hovered over the two young men.</p>

<p>Qingyi observed his opponent with interest before climbing into the arena, cracking his knuckles and holding the Heaven-Defying Thunder Sword firmly.</p>

<p>“You have a beautiful sword, stranger. My name is Lux, what is yours?” The young man asked, extending his hand.</p>

<p>“Qingyi.” Qingyi replied briefly and coldly, accepting Lux’s handshake.</p>

<p>The moment he extended his hand, however, he sensed danger.</p>

<p>His left foot sank slightly into the ground, his vision blurring as he changed direction in the blink of an eye.</p>

<p>At the same time, the saber Lux carried at his waist became nothing more than a golden blur, his right hand moving at an absurd speed.</p>

<p>A sharp crack echoed, and then a powerful slash tore the arena in half, missing Qingyi by inches.</p>

<p>“Oh… that’s powerful, at least for an ant.” Qingyi smiled disdainfully, watching Lux’s shocked face.</p>

<p>Turning his body, the handsome young man kicked out, his boots hitting Lux’s face with a thud and sending him crashing into the barrier.</p>

<p>“Ugh… son of a bitch.” Lux groaned painfully as he fell to his knees, coughing up blood and a few broken teeth onto the floor.</p>

<p>His nose was red, clearly broken.</p>

<p>“This is going to be interesting, hehehe…” Old Lucios laughed, the long, sharp nail of his index finger tapping against the black steel of his throne.</p>

<p>Lux took only a single moment before recovering, clutching his chest and then roaring.</p>

<p>“You’re fast, but don’t think speed alone will be enough, foreign pig!” Lux punched the ground, his body exploding with a golden aura, any sign of humility on his face replaced by pure arrogance.</p>

<p>In the blink of an eye, he covered the distance between him and Qingyi.</p>

<p>His blade struck the Heaven-Defying Thunder Sword with a powerful crash, his roar growing even louder as he pressed harder and harder.</p>

<p>Qingyi grunted softly as his feet tore into the concrete floor, holding back Lux’s powerful momentum.</p>

<p>He wasn’t even bothered to use his Qi, relying solely on his mana and physical strength.</p>

<p>That was enough to deal with that insect.</p>

<p>Around the two of them, gravity seemed to briefly reverse, small stones from the destroyed arena flying into the air.</p>

<p>Qingyi’s lightning-covered mana and Lux’s golden Qi carved deep grooves into the concrete.</p>

<p>“Yield!” Lux roared, giving himself another burst of power as he prepared a spell in his left hand, one of the few he had mastered.</p>

<p>“I’m sorry, but I’ve seen enough.” Qingyi finally pushed back with full force, planting his foot on the ground and then moving his sword, thunder, and fire toward his opponent’s blade.</p>

<p>In a single moment, steel sank into steel and molten metal fell to the ground.</p>

<p>Lux’s eyes widened as Qingyi’s sword passed through his and the weight in his hands grew lighter.</p>

<p>Before he was even able to react, Qingyi’s fist was already tearing through the air toward his face, covered in a powerful flaming aura.</p>

<p>Many closed their eyes at the thunderous sound of Qingyi’s fist landing.</p>

<p>Lux’s body spun in the air, his feet hitting the ground before he was violently thrown against the protective barrier.</p>

<p>Under Qingyi’s feet, everything turned to dust, and the arrays surrounding them shook violently.</p>

<p>Even though they were able to contain most of the power shock of an Immortal Emperor like Qingyi, they still couldn’t contain it all, and small cracks spread across the ground.</p>

<p>Mixed reactions took over the faces of all the spectators.</p>

<p>Many family and faction leaders stood up from their thrones, looking at that young man with endless greed.</p>

<p>They wanted to take him as their champion right now, brainwash him, train him, and turn him into an unmatched blade against their enemies.</p>

<p>But none of them dared to say anything, waiting for old Lucios’ reaction.</p>

<p>The guard sent by Seraphine, Viola, was no different, although now her mind was focused on something else.</p>

<p>‘A man who doesn’t seem to be of this world… Viola repeated in her mind, her eyes focusing on Lux.</p>

<p>For a moment, she thought it was because of his beauty, but seeing him like that, with his jaw broken and his face turned into a bloody mess, made her dismiss this thought.</p>

<p>Her young lady would never pay attention to someone like that.</p>

<p>So Viola focused on Qingyi.</p>

<p>Much more handsome than Lux, with strange clothes, strange habits, and even a strange way of walking.</p>

<p>Still, accompanying him was extreme power.</p>

<p>“Boy…” Lucios’ voice echoed, snapping Viola out of her stupor.</p>

<p>“I was only looking for a champion, a warrior who would serve my family forever and who would one day ride a dragon by my side.” Lucios rose from his throne, puffing out his chest.</p>

<p>Usually, they would only begin choosing their champions after the last battle; those were the rules.</p>

<p>But he couldn’t give a fuck about the rules.</p>

<p>Among the draconic elves, even the patriarch obeyed him.</p>

<p>The other eleven great families trembled at his power.</p>

<p>“But I’ve changed my mind, become my apprentice-no, my godson! If you want, I’ll even marry you to one of my granddaughters and make you my heir!” Lucios roared, opening his arms as if to pull Qingyi into an embrace.</p>

<p>Those words caused even more shock among the thrones, but no one dared to say anything.</p>

<p>Qingyi thought for a moment, scratched his chin, and then shook his head.</p>

<p>The smile on Lucios’ face disappeared, but he didn’t care.</p>

<p>“I beg your pardon, senior, but this junior came here with the sole intention of serving Lady Seraphine. I will not accept any invitation that does not come from her faction.”</p>

<p>The shock caused by Lucios’ words was incomparable to the shock caused by Qingyi’s refusal.</p>

<p>“What an insolent brat!”</p>

<p>“How dare you refuse Lord Lucios?”</p>

<p>“Do you know who you’re talking to?”</p>

<p>Qingyi ignored everyone, his eyes focused only on Viola.</p>
