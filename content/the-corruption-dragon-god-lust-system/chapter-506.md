<p>Initially, things in the capital of the Rose Empire were lively.</p>

<p>The story of the heroes’ success on their first mission spread like wildfire, and the people celebrated.</p>

<p>But then, someone noticed something.</p>

<p>None of the five heroes had revealed themselves.</p>

<p>Not Lucien, not Aeryn, not Celestial, not Sapphire. Not even the strange foreigner.</p>

<p>Everything was quiet, and the church was no exception.</p>

<p>Many questions arose and were ignored, which only further increased the apprehension of the people of the imperial capital.</p>

<p>The emperor ordered the site to be cleaned up, compensated the families of the dead, and then, silence.</p>

<p>The same was true for the Blue Tower and the elves, who had great talents participating in that mission.</p>

<p>No one said anything about the situation of the chosen heroes.</p>

<p>Naturally, as he walked through the capital, his aura hidden and his body swallowed by a sea of shadows, Qingyi had noticed the current situation.</p>

<p>“It seems I’ve caused quite a mess for them,” the handsome young man chuckled. “Sapphire, Margareth, what do you still want to do here?” he asked.</p>

<p>“I just want to pick up some belongings at the Red Tower,” Margareth said, followed by Sapphire, who had the same desire.</p>

<p>Many of her personal belongings had been left behind, and she didn’t want to lose anything.</p>

<p>“Okay,” Qingyi nodded, moving his spatial Qi.</p>

<p>In a single second, he was no longer on the streets, appearing inside one of the many magical towers that rose above the imperial capital.</p>

<p>That was the Red Tower, ruled by one of the empire’s greatest magicians and inhabited by hundreds of students.</p>

<p>There were more than a hundred floors in total, forming a huge maze of stairs and passageways.</p>

<p>Despite the students’ talent, none of them noticed Qingyi, who was hidden by the Qi of shadows.</p>

<p>It wasn’t difficult to find Margareth’s room, which had been sealed for years.</p>

<p>There, she took some books, research materials, and personal items before returning to the world of the mind.</p>

<p>Closing his eyes, Qingyi followed Sapphire’s instructions and then disappeared again.</p>

<p>He reappeared in the Blue Tower, just below a huge staircase.</p>

<p>“Just go up… my room is on the second-to-last floor.”</p>

<p>Silently, Qingyi followed, passing many students until he reached the second-to-last floor.</p>

<p>If he went up one more, he would come face to face with the master wizard of the Blue Tower, a terrifying expert with over twenty thousand years of history.</p>

<p>Qingyi was curious, but he restrained himself, entering Sapphire’s</p>

<p>room.</p>

<p>He had barely taken a single step into the room before he stopped, the door closing behind him.</p>

<p>Sapphire appeared beside him, her eyes falling on a man sitting on her bed.</p>

<p>He was tall, with powerful muscles pressing against his robes, his face hidden by a bronze mask covered in marks.</p>

<p>“Father…” She whispered, clinging to Qingyi’s robes.</p>

<p>What the hell was that man doing there?</p>

<p>“Your mission was a success…” The emperor stood up, walking toward Sapphire, who cowered behind Qingyi.</p>

<p>The two stood face to face.</p>

<p>Qingyi’s cold, purple eyes stared into the emperor’s piercing, incomprehensible blue eyes.</p>

<p>“Do you know why I am here?” The emperor murmured, speaking to</p>

<p>his daughter, even though he was looking into Qingyi’s eyes.</p>

<p>He released a thread of aura, which should have been enough to crush the young man like a cockroach against the ground.</p>

<p>To his surprise, Qingyi didn’t even react, remaining steadfast. How powerful could this young man have become in such a short time?</p>

<p>Beneath his mask, the emperor’s face changed, and he put a little more power into it, slowly releasing his aura.</p>

<p>“If you want to die, be direct, old man. I will give you a quick and merciful death in the name of my wife, but if you insist…” Qingyi growled, calling upon the flames of Primordial Chaos. “I can make you</p>

<p>feel a little pain.”</p>

<p>Immediately, the emperor took a step back, feeling those strange gray flames consume his mana.</p>

<p>He paused for a moment, strange crackles escaping from inside his mask before he finally burst into laughter.</p>

<p>“You’re good, young man…” The emperor turned his back on Qingyi.</p>

<p>He walked to one of the few windows in his daughter’s room, looking</p>

<p>out at the city below.</p>

<p>From the poorest slums to the luxurious palace he called home.</p>

<p>Every citizen, every loyal guard, every enemy of the crown.</p>

<p>He could see and feel each and every one of them.</p>

<p>With a wave of his hands, they would all be dead.</p>

<p>“It’s a shame… that you have such a disgusting aura about you.” The</p>

<p>emperor turned, staring coldly at Qingyi.</p>

<p>He could feel it well, impregnated in that young man’s body, an aura</p>

<p>he could never have forgotten.</p>

<p>It was a dark power that had brought so much suffering to his people.</p>

<p>Qingyi took a step back, realizing he had made a mistake.</p>

<p>Every touch between experts left a mark of their aura, and unless it was washed away or hidden, it would remain there.</p>

<p>For someone like the emperor, a twelfth-ring warrior and one of the most powerful on the continent, sensing it was as easy as breathing. “Khaedryss… slayer of immortals and butcher of empires… I haven’t forgotten the day she devoured my father… I was just a boy, but I remember. Even your eyes and horns are the same as hers…”</p>

<p>“Father… no!” Sapphire exclaimed, clutching Qingyi’s robes. Before her lips even closed, she had already been sent to the world of</p>

<p>the mind.</p>

<p>The last thing she was able to feel was her father’s aura exploding.</p>

<p>Just below the tower, the eyes of many citizens rose, watching the</p>

<p>skies with curiosity that soon turned to terror.</p>

<p>The entire second-to-last floor of the Blue Tower exploded, engulfed</p>

<p>by a sea of flames.</p>

<p>A figure left the sea of fire with his arms crossed, launched toward the wall with an explosion.</p>

<p>“Damn, this old man is strong…”</p>

<p>Qingyi smiled, his back hitting the outer wall of the capital.</p>

<p>He passed through dozens of meters of steel and concrete, continuing to sink for kilometers.</p>

<p>“You senile old man, what the hell do you think you’re doing in my tower?” Another powerful aura from the twelfth ring exploded.</p>

<p>An old man in blue robes appeared beside the emperor.</p>

<p>Qingyi watched the two from afar.</p>

<p>He was about to advance and test his new power when he suddenly</p>

<p>froze.</p>

<p>Why the hell did he sense such a strange energy coming from the emperor’s mana?</p>

<p>No… that wasn’t pure mana.</p>

<p>Qingyi gritted his teeth and used his draconic eyes.</p>

<p>Now that the emperor was using his power freely, he realized it.</p>

<p>That was… Qi?</p>

<p>Did the emperor have a Dantian?</p>
