<p>Hours later, the moon hung like a pale, judging eye over the Starlight Estate, but inside the manor, the atmosphere was thick with something far more suffocating than darkness.</p>

<p>Rex pushed open the heavy oak doors to the master suite. This had once been the sanctum of Helena and Leonidas, a place of shared laughter, whispered plans, and the warm glow of a family that believed itself untouchable.</p>

<p>Now, the air in the room felt like liquid lead, heavy with the copper tang of dried blood and the ionized sting of spent magic.</p>

<p>Rex walked slowly through the room, his fingers trailing along the polished mahogany furniture.</p>

<p>"I have to admit," he mused, his voice echoing off the vaulted ceiling, "there is something poetic about this."</p>

<p>"I saved this room for last, just so that I wanted it to remain... pristine."</p>

<p>"Untouched by the chaos outside."</p>

<p>He stopped before a gilded mantlepiece lined with silver-framed photographs. He picked up a portrait of Helena and Leonidas standing before the castle gates, young and brimming with unearned confidence.</p>

<p>"Still as clear as ever," he whispered, his thumb brushing over their smiling faces. "The memories haven’t faded..."</p>

<p>"How tragic that you won’t be able to remember them much longer."</p>

<p>In the center of the room, the three prisoners were arranged like grotesque museum exhibits.</p>

<p>Helena, Leonidas, and Evelyn were forced onto their knees in a perfect triangle. They weren’t merely restrained; Rex had woven a lattice of invisible [telekinetic chains] around them.</p>

<p>These weren’t physical shackles, but crushing bands of pure force that pinned their muscles, locked their joints, and pressed their spines into rigid lines. They could not shift an inch.</p>

<p>They could not even slump from exhaustion. They could only exist as conscious observers of their own humiliation.</p>

<p>Evelyn’s condition was the most pitiable. Her body was a patchwork of bandages and dried gore, held together by sheer magical will.</p>

<p>Every breath she took was a wet, rattling struggle.</p>

<p>Rex turned away from the photographs, his shadow stretching long across the carpet as he approached them.</p>

<p>A slow, hungry smile began to spread across his face, a smile that didn’t reach his eyes, which remained cold as arctic ice. This wasn’t just victory; this was the culmination of years of simmering resentment, a debt being paid in flesh and spirit.</p>

<p>"Do you feel it?" Rex asked, stepping into the circle of their forced kneeling position. "The silence? The way the world outside has stopped breathing for you?"</p>

<p>"Kill us..." Helena rasped, her voice breaking. "Just finish it, you fucking monster...!"</p>

<p>"Don’t play these games anymore...!"</p>

<p>"Games?" Rex laughed, a low, vibrating sound that seemed to make the very air tremble. "This isn’t a game, Helena..."</p>

<p>"A game implies uncertainty, but this is... inevitability."</p>

<p>He reached out, his hand hovering inches from Leonidas’s face. The warrior’s jaw was clenched so tightly that the tendons in his neck stood out like steel cables.</p>

<p>"You taught me that power was something to be earned through lineage and divine right..."</p>

<p>"You taught me that some bloodlines were inherently superior."</p>

<p>Suddenly, Rex’s hand snapped forward, not striking, but gripping Leonidas’s chin with crushing force.</p>

<p>CRUNCH.</p>

<p>The sound of cartilage grinding against bone echoed in the silent room.</p>

<p>"AHHH!" Leonidas’s scream was cut short as Rex’s telekinesis constricted around his throat, muffling the sound into a pathetic, gurgling wheeze.</p>

<p>"But I’ve learned something new," Rex whispered, leaning closer until his breath fanned Leonidas’s sweat slicked forehead. "Power isn’t inherited... but power is taken..."</p>

<p>"And I am here to collect."</p>

<p>He turned his attention to Evelyn. He knelt before her, his face inches from hers.</p>

<p>"And you, Evelyn... you... who believed your truth could shield you from the consequences of your arrogance."</p>

<p>With a flick of his wrist, he increased the pressure on her broken ribs.</p>

<p>SNAP. CRACK.</p>

<p>"Nnngh Aaaahhh! Haa... haa..." Evelyn’s back arched involuntarily, her body straining against the invisible chains.</p>

<p>A high-pitched, involuntary moan escaped her lips, a sound born of agony so intense it bordered on something else, a twisted sensory overload that made her hips twitch helplessly against the floor.</p>

<p>"Does it hurt?" Rex asked, his voice dropping to a husky, almost seductive purr. "Or does the pain feel like the only real thing left in this fake world you built?"</p>

<p>He began to circle them like a shark circling wounded prey, his presence filling every corner of the room. The tension was unbearable, a coiled spring of violence and psychological torment.</p>

<p>The only sounds were the labored breathing of the broken, the distant howl of the wind, and the terrifyingly calm heartbeat of the man who had destroyed them all.</p>

<p>Rex circled the kneeling trio like a vulture savoring the scent of decay. The silence in the Master Suite was brittle, broken only by the wet, ragged sounds of Evelyn’s breathing and the faint, rhythmic clicking of Leonidas’s teeth as he fought the urge to vomit.</p>

<p>Rex stopped directly in front of Leonidas, looming over him like a monument of vengeance.</p>

<p>"You’re holding it together, aren’t you, Leonidas?" Rex asked, his voice dripping with false admiration. "That legendary Starlight composure..."</p>

<p>"The stoic lion who never flinches."</p>

<p>Rex turned his head slightly toward Helena, whose eyes were burning with a hatred so pure it seemed to radiate heat.</p>

<p>"But look at her," Rex gestured toward Helena. "She’s pretending..."</p>

<p>"She’s trying to be the pillar for you..."</p>

<p>"She’s trying to prove that her spirit is as unbreakable as your armor."</p>

<p>Rex leaned down, his face inches from Leonidas’s ear. "But I wonder... if I start with her, how long before that mask slips?"</p>

<p>"How long before she’s screaming for mercy instead of cursing my name?"</p>

<p>Leonidas’s stomach lurched. The nausea was a physical wave, cold and acidic, rising in his throat.</p>

<p>The psychological cruelty was worse than the broken bones; Rex wasn’t just attacking their bodies, he was systematically dismantling their dignity.</p>

<p>"You’re insane," Leonidas choked out, his voice thick with bile. "You think this makes you strong?!"</p>

<p>"You’re just a child throwing a tantrum with the power of a god!"</p>

<p>Rex’s smile didn’t waver. "Is that so?"</p>

<p>"Then let’s test the limits of your courage."</p>

<p>"Because if you think Helena is tough... then you never see what I’ve done, to the point that I already break Valentina and Morwenna first before her."</p>

<p>"I want to see if her devotion to your precious legacy can survive what I’m about to do to this family..."</p>

<p>"Let’s use our heads now... see the strongest woman," they said, like Valentina and Morwenna had already ended a broken bitch who can’t resist me..."</p>

<p>"But what would... happen... to your precious Helena, eh...?"</p>

<p>Leonidas felt a cold terror pierce through his nausea. The thought of his other daughters, Valentina and Morwenna, being subjected to Rex’s whims made his vision swim.</p>

<p>Especially Leonidas already saw that once with Valentina.</p>

<p>The fear became unbearable. In a desperate, futile attempt to reclaim some semblance of control, to drown out the terror with noise, Leonidas began to scream.</p>

<p>"YOU MONSTER! YOU FILTHY, PATHETIC COWARD! "</p>

<p>"COME ON THEN! KILL ME!"</p>

<p>"KILL US ALL! YOU’LL NEVER OWN US!"</p>

<p>"YOU’LL NEVER—AAAAHHHHH!""</p>

<p>Leonidas screamed until his vocal cords felt like they were tearing, his face flushing a deep purple, his veins standing out like cords on his neck. He screamed to fill the room, to push Rex away with sheer volume.</p>

<p>WHAM.</p>

<p>Rex’s fist connected with Leonidas’s jaw with the force of a battering ram.</p>

<p>CRACK.</p>

<p>The sound of bone fracturing was sickeningly wet. Leonidas’s head snapped sideways, blood spraying across the expensive carpet.</p>

<p>Before Leonidas could even register the pain, Rex grabbed him by the hair and hauled him upright, only to drive another fist into his solar plexus.</p>

<p>THUD.</p>

<p>"Guhhk...!" Leonidas collapsed forward, gasping for air that wouldn’t come.</p>

<p>Rex didn’t stop. He became a whirlwind of controlled brutality.</p>

<p>A left hook to the ribs SNAP followed by a brutal downward strike to the back of Leonidas’s neck. Leonidas’s body flopped like a fish on ice, his face repeatedly slammed into the hardwood floor.</p>

<p>THWACK. THWACK.</p>

<p>Blood smeared across the floorboards in crimson streaks.</p>

<p>"STOP IT! STOP IT!" Helena shrieked, her telekinetic restraints straining as she tried to lunge forward, her muscles convulsing against the invisible force. "Rex, please!"</p>

<p>"Leave him alone! Take me instead!"</p>

<p>Evelyn could only watch through blurred vision, her own body convulsing in sympathetic agony.</p>

<p>"Please..." she whimpered, her voice breaking into a high-pitched scream. "Don’t... don’t do this..."</p>

<p>Leonidas’s head finally came to rest against the floor, his cheek pressed into a pool of his own blood. His eyes were unfocused, rolling back in his skull.</p>

<p>He tried to open his mouth to scream again, to curse Rex one last time, but Rex was faster.</p>

<p>Rex raised a hand, fingers splayed. The air around Leonidas’s throat shivered with violet energy.</p>

<p>SHHHH!</p>

<p>The telekinetic grip slammed shut around Leonidas’s larynx. The screaming stopped instantly, replaced by the terrifying sound of a man trying to breathe through a sealed pipe, a wet, desperate clicking sound.</p>

<p>Leonidas’s eyes bulged, veins standing out on his temples as he struggled against the invisible vise that had silenced him.</p>

<p>Rex stood over him, breathing evenly, looking down at the broken warrior with utter contempt. He turned back to Helena and Evelyn, who were watching in paralyzed horror.</p>

<p>"There," Rex said softly, wiping a speck of Leonidas’s blood from his cheek. "Much better."</p>

<p>"Now that we’ve eliminated the noise... shall we discuss what comes next?"</p>
