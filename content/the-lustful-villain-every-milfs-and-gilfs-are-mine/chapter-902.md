<p>The atmosphere in the hall had shifted from awe to something far more primal and far more terrifying. The spectators no longer saw a spar; they saw a predator dismantling its prey.</p>
<p>Rex had stopped fighting like a soldier and started fighting like a force of nature, and for the first time in her long, storied life, Aurelia Morr Nightwing was being overwhelmed.</p>
<p>Rex didn't just attack; he orchestrated a symphony of violence. He moved with a terrifying new rhythm, a sequence of strikes that defied traditional martial arts.</p>
<p>WHOOSH CRACK!</p>
<p>Aurelia lunged with a desperate jab, but Rex didn't retreat. He stepped into the strike, catching her wrist with his left hand while simultaneously driving his right elbow upward in a vicious arc.</p>
<p>The elbow connected with the underside of her jaw with a wet, grinding sound.</p>
<p>CRUNCH.</p>
<p>Her head snapped sideways, and before she could even register the pain, Rex unleashed his first unique combo.</p>
<p>He spun low, driving a devastating roundhouse kick into the side of her thigh to destabilize her stance.</p>
<p>THWACK.</p>
<p>As she stumbled, he didn't wait for her to recover; he launched into a 'Viper' sequence: a lightning-fast left jab to her nose to blind her, followed immediately by a spinning back fist that caught her temple, and finishing with a brutal knee strike that drove upward through her sternum.</p>
<p>BAM. WHACK. OOF.</p>
<p>Aurelia was thrown backward, her boots skidding across the blood-soaked floor. Her breathing was ragged, each gasp sounding like broken glass rattling in her throat.</p>
<p>"Damn you..." she wheezed, blood streaming from her nose and mouth, but Rex was already there.</p>
<p>He was a shadow of violence. He moved with terrifying speed, closing the distance before she could even raise her guard.</p>
<p>Rex grabbed both of her wrists in one massive hand, twisting them painfully behind her back to expose her torso.</p>
<p>"Is this the legacy?" he growled into her ear, his breath hot against her bloodied skin. "Is this all the Nightwing blood provides?"</p>
<p>Then came the onslaught. Rex unleashed a relentless barrage of short, piston-like punches to her ribs and kidneys.</p>
<p>THUD. THUD. THUD. THUD.</p>
<p>Each strike landed with the sound of a hammer hitting wet clay. Aurelia's body jerked with every impact, her ribs groaning under the relentless pressure.</p>
<p>He didn't stop there. He released her wrists only to drive a palm strike into her solar plexus, sending her reeling, then immediately followed up with a 'Thunderclap' combo: two rapid-fire hammer fists to her shoulders that forced her to drop to her knees.</p>
<p>WHAM. WHAM.</p>
<p>"Rex... stop..." she managed to choke out, but there was no plea in her voice, only a desperate, defiant challenge.</p>
<p>But Rex wasn't listening. He was in the zone, a state of pure, unbridled dominance.</p>
<p>He grabbed her by the hair, forcing her head back to expose her throat, and delivered three vicious knee strikes to her midsection in rapid succession.</p>
<p>THUMP. THUMP. THUMP.</p>
<p>Aurelia's eyes rolled back momentarily as the air was systematically punched out of her. She collapsed forward, but Rex caught her before she hit the ground, holding her upright only to drive a brutal headbutt into her forehead.</p>
<p>CRACK.</p>
<p>The sound echoed like a gunshot through the silent hall.</p>
<p>Diana watched from the sidelines, her hands shaking. She saw the blood, she saw the brutality, but she couldn't look away.</p>
<p>Rex was terrifying, yes, but he was also magnificent. He was breaking the untouchable matriarch, proving that even gods could bleed.</p>
<p>Her heart raced with a dangerous mix of fear and intoxicating fascination.</p>
<p>Theo, meanwhile, looked like he was about to vomit.</p>
<p>"This isn't fighting," he whispered, his voice cracking with genuine horror. "This is... this is slaughter."</p>
<p>Rex ignored them all. He pulled Aurelia up by her collar, their faces inches apart.</p>
<p>Both were drenched in crimson, their sweat mixing with the blood that dripped from their wounds. Rex's eyes were cold, focused, and utterly merciless.</p>
<p>"You wanted to see what I could do?" Rex hissed, his voice a low rumble of menace. "I'll show you everything."</p>
<p>He unleashed another devastating combo: a spinning crescent kick that caught her across the ribs, followed by a series of brutal, short-range uppercuts that snapped her head back repeatedly.</p>
<p>WHACK. WHACK. WHACK.</p>
<p>Each blow sent fresh sprays of blood across the floor.</p>
<p>Aurelia was broken, her body swaying dangerously, her defenses completely shattered. She was a queen reduced to a wounded animal, but she still refused to fall.</p>
<p>Rex saw this, and instead of showing mercy, he intensified the assault. He wanted to see how much she could take before the Nightwing flame finally flickered out.</p>
<p>The hall was filled with nothing but the sounds of impact, the wet gasps of the dying air, and the relentless, terrifying rhythm of Rex's unstoppable violence.</p>
<p>The training hall had become a cathedral of carnage. The air was so thick with the copper tang of blood and the sulfurous heat of exertion that it felt like breathing liquid fire.</p>
<p>Rex was no longer merely fighting; he was conducting a massacre of technique, systematically dismantling the legendary Aurelia Nightwing.</p>
<p>Rex moved like a predator that had already tasted victory. He caught Aurelia's desperate spinning kick midair with a forearm that sounded like a tree trunk snapping.</p>
<p>THWACK.</p>
<p>Without breaking stride, he pivoted, driving a series of devastating elbow strikes into her guard.</p>
<p>CRACK. CRUNCH. WHACK.</p>
<p>Each elbow landed with bone-deep authority, forcing Aurelia's head to snap back repeatedly. Rex followed up with his most brutal signature: the 'Iron Tempest.'</p>
<p>He unleashed a flurry of low sweeps to her ankles, followed by a rapid-fire succession of gut punches that sounded like sledgehammers hitting a wet mattress.</p>
<p>THUD THUD THUD THUD.</p>
<p>Aurelia's knees buckled, her breath coming in ragged, wet gasps that made the guards flinch.</p>
<p>On the sidelines, Theo's voice rose to a shrill, hysterical pitch. "This is sacrilege! Look at her!"</p>
<p>"The matriarch of our house is being treated like common street trash!"</p>
<p>"This man... this animal is desecrating our very existence!"</p>
<p>"Diana, say something! How can you stand there watching this atrocity?"</p>
<p>Diana's head turned slowly. Her eyes weren't filled with the horror Theo expected; they were blazing with a cold, contemptuous fire.</p>
<p>She turned fully toward him, her lips curling into a sneer that made Theo recoil.</p>
<p>"You truly are pathetic, aren't you, Theo?" Diana's voice was quiet, but it carried like a whip crack through the silence of the hall. "Every word out of your mouth is a confession of your own cowardice."</p>
<p>"I... I am defending our honor," Theo stammered.</p>
<p>"Honor?" Diana stepped into his personal space, her gaze piercing through his soul. "Honor isn't something you inherit like a silver spoon, you spoiled child."</p>
<p>"Honor is earned in blood and fire."</p>
<p>"Rex saved Aethelgard; he stood in the gap when your precious nobility was busy cowering behind castle walls."</p>
<p>"He proved his worth when it mattered most."</p>
<p>She gestured violently toward the center of the ring where Rex was currently driving a knee into Aurelia's ribs.</p>
<p>OOF.</p>
<p>"And right now, he is doing something you couldn't achieve in ten lifetimes: he is showing us what true strength looks like."</p>
<p>"He isn't desecrating the Nightwing legacy, Theo..."</p>
<p>"He's the only one here worthy enough to test it."</p>
<p>"You? You're just a spectator in a house that's already passed you by."</p>
<p>Theo stood frozen, his face draining of all color. The humiliation burned worse than any physical blow; he was being dismissed not just by a noble but by the very symbol of his own family's future. He stood there, impotent and small, while the world moved on without him.</p>
<p>Back in the pit, the climax arrived.</p>
<p>Rex saw Aurelia's opening, a moment of hesitation as she struggled to draw breath. He didn't hesitate.</p>
<p>He launched into his final, devastating sequence.</p>
<p>He feinted a high punch, drew her guard up, then dropped instantly into a devastating leg sweep.</p>
<p>WHOOSH.</p>
<p>As she stumbled forward, he rose like a coiled serpent, delivering a thunderous uppercut that connected squarely with her chin.</p>
<p>BOOM.</p>
<p>The sound reverberated through the entire fortress. Aurelia's entire body lifted off the floor for a fraction of a second before she crashed down.</p>
<p>Rex stood over her, his chest heaving, his skin painted crimson. Blood dripped from his knuckles and his nose, but as he looked down at the fallen matriarch, a slow, predator's smile spread across his face—a smile of pure, unadulterated triumph.</p>
<p>"That," Rex rasped, sweat streaming down his face, "is what real power looks like."</p>
<p>Aurelia lay on the blood-slicked stones, her ribs screaming in pain, her vision swimming. But as she looked up at Rex through one swollen eye, there was no anger.</p>
<p>There was no shame. The humiliation she should have felt, the shame of being beaten by an outsider, was nowhere to be found. Instead, she felt something she hadn't felt in decades: genuine, bone-deep respect.</p>
<p>She saw in him the future. She saw a strength that could shield their house from the coming storms.</p>
<p>A broken smile touched her lips as she stared at the man who had caused her pain.</p>
<p>"You..." she coughed, tasting blood, "you are... exactly what we need."</p>
<p>Rex's smile widened, not out of cruelty, but out of acknowledgment. He had not just beaten a warrior; he had earned the respect of a legend.</p>
<p>The hall fell into a stunned, reverent silence as the new era of the Nightwing bloodline began in a pool of crimson.</p>
