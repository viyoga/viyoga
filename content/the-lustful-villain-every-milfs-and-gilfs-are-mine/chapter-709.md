<p>The rage in Apollo’s heart flared. It wasn’t just anger anymore; it was a divine fury.</p>
<p>The light around him didn’t just shine; it pulsed with the rhythm of a beating heart.</p>
<p>"Shut... up..." Apollo whispered, his voice trembling with a power that made the very air hum.</p>
<p>"What was that?" Rex sneered, stepping forward. "Speak up, little light!"</p>
<p>"SHUT UP!" Apollo screamed.</p>
<p>The explosion of light was staggering. It wasn’t a blast; it was a revelation.</p>
<p>The gold and white energy erupted from his body in a perfectly controlled sphere. It wasn’t the uncontrolled explosion of a dying star but the precise, focused might of the Apostle of Life.</p>
<p>The light didn’t just burn; it reclaimed. Where the light touched the stone, the stone became vibrant and alive. Where it touched the blood stains, the blood seemed to dance.</p>
<p>Apollo stood at the center of the maelstrom, his eyes no longer just glowing but burning with the intensity of a sun. His aura was no longer a shield; it was a living extension of his will.</p>
<p>This was the true power of the Apostle of Life: the ability to command the very essence of existence.</p>
<p>"You speak of death, Tremor," Apollo said, his voice now a calm, terrifyingly steady roar that drowned out the echoes of the cavern. "But you have forgotten the most important truth!"</p>
<p>"Life does not just endure! Life conquers!!!"</p>
<p>As he spoke, the light around him began to shape itself into wings of pure radiance, and the pressure in the room shifted from the crushing weight of death to the overwhelming presence of creation. The battle was no longer just a fight; it was a clash of the very foundations of reality.</p>
<p>The cavern was no longer a cave; it had become a crucible of celestial fury and earthen defiance. Apollo, fueled by a rage so pure it bordered on madness, unleashed a relentless onslaught of luminous destruction.</p>
<p>SHBOOM!</p>
<p>Apollo vanished, reappearing instantly above Rex in a blur of golden light. He descended like a falling star, his fist wreathed in a concentrated sphere of photonic energy.</p>
<p>"SOLAR CRASH!" he bellowed.</p>
<p>The impact emitted a shockwave through the cavern that sounded like a mountain splitting in two. Rex barely managed to brace himself, digging his gauntlets into the floor to keep from being pulverized, but the sheer force sent him skidding backward, leaving a deep, glowing trench in the bedrock.</p>
<p>Rex grunted, his teeth bared in a snarl. "Is that all, Apollo?!"</p>
<p>Apollo didn’t answer with words. He answered with a maelstrom of light.</p>
<p>He began to move in a series of hypersonic zigzags, his body leaving trails of afterimages that made him appear as a dozen gods attacking at once.</p>
<p>VROOOOM! ZAP! CRACK BOOM!</p>
<p>Every strike was a masterpiece of destruction. Apollo unleashed a barrage of light lances, piercing the ground around Rex to cage him in a perimeter of searing heat.</p>
<p>Each lance hit with the force of a cannon blast, exploding into shards of brilliant white energy. Rex was forced into a frantic dance of survival, parrying with his gravity manipulation, but the sheer volume of the attacks was overwhelming.</p>
<p>"LIGHTNING RAIN!" Apollo roared.</p>
<p>He thrust his hands upward, and a torrent of blinding bolts descended. They weren’t just bolts; they were pillars of pure judgment.</p>
<p>The sound was a continuous, deafening roar of thunder that shook the very foundations of the world.</p>
<p>KRA KOOOM! KRA KOOOM!</p>
<p>Rex was battered, his skin scorched, his breath coming in ragged gasps. He managed to catch one of the blasts with a gravity shield, but the sheer intensity forced him to his knees.</p>
<p>The ground beneath him was turning to molten glass.</p>
<p>"You’re... losing... control!" Rex spat, coughing up a mouthful of blood as he looked up at the radiant god above him. "You’re becoming the very chaos you hate!"</p>
<p>Apollo’s eyes flared with a dangerous white light. He was on the brink.</p>
<p>The power was surging through him like a raging river, threatening to drown his very consciousness. He felt the old instincts returning: the urge to simply erase everything in his path, to leave nothing but a void of light.</p>
<p>"APOLLO! STOP!"</p>
<p>The scream pierced through the thunderous roar. It was Kaelira, her voice echoing from the entrance of the cavern, her face pale with terror.</p>
<p>"APOLLO! DON’T LET THE LIGHT CONSUME YOU! REMEMBER WHO YOU ARE!" she cried, her voice trembling. "DON’T BECOME LIKE YOURSELF WHEN YOU FOUGHT THE UNDEAD BRINGER!"</p>
<p>"DON’T LOSE YOURSELF AGAIN!"</p>
<p>The mention of the Undead Bringer acted like a bucket of ice water. Apollo’s vision, which had been a blur of white heat, suddenly flickered.</p>
<p>For a split second, the man within Apollo emerged, separating him from the god. He felt the weight of his own power, the danger of the uncontrolled flame.</p>
<p>He gasped, his body trembling as he fought to suppress the unbridled instincts. He was suppressing the primordial explosion, pulling the energy back into a tight, controlled vortex.</p>
<p>It was an exhausting struggle, a constant battle of will against will.</p>
<p>But he didn’t stop because he couldn’t. He had to finish this.</p>
<p>With a strained, controlled grunt, Apollo redirected the energy. Instead of a wild explosion, he channeled the power into a singular, focused form.</p>
<p>He became a blade of pure light.</p>
<p>He lunged. Not with the uncontrolled fury of a storm, but with the surgical precision of a god.</p>
<p>SHRING!</p>
<p>The sound was like a thousand swords being drawn at once. He moved through the air like a streak of golden light, striking Rex with a rapid succession of blows that seemed to occur all at once.</p>
<p>BAM! BAM! BAM! BAM!</p>
<p>Each hit was a calculated blow, targeting Rex’s weak points, his joints, and his center of gravity. He was no longer just attacking; he was dismantling.</p>
<p>Rex tried to raise his gauntlets, but Apollo’s speed was too overwhelming. Apollo’s fists, coated in a dense layer of solidified light, slammed into Rex’s ribs, his shoulders, and his chest with the force of a falling moon.</p>
<p>Rex was thrown back, his body a mess of bruises and scorched flesh, but he was still standing, a grimace of pain on his face. He had survived the uncontrolled burst, but he was drowning in the controlled might of the Apostle of Life.</p>
<p>Apollo stood there, his chest heaving, his body glowing with a steady, controlled radiance. He was breathing hard, but his eyes were clear. The man was still there, but the god was in command.</p>
<p>"Now," Apollo said, his voice a low, deadly rumble. "Let’s see how much more you can take."</p>
<p>Rex let out a sound that was half laugh, half growl, a raspy, guttural-sounding thing that echoed off the cavern’s walls. He wiped a streak of blood from his chin, his eyes gleaming with a terrifying-looking satisfaction.</p>
<p>"That’s it!" Rex roared, his voice filled with a manic glee. "That’s the fucking spirit!"</p>
<p>"Control the fucking storm, Apollo!"</p>
<p>"Show me the strength that can actually rival a god!" He stood tall, despite the bruises and the scorched flesh, his presence as heavy as the earth itself. "Don’t just explode like a fucking firework! Fight me!"</p>
<p>Apollo didn’t respond with words. He responded with a movement so fast it left a vacuum in its wake.</p>
<p>WHOOSH!</p>
<p>The battle reignited, but this time, it was different.</p>
<p>It wasn’t a chaotic brawl; it was a dance of divine precision and earthen weight. Apollo moved like a bolt of lightning-fast light, his movements fluid and purposeful.</p>
<p>He began a relentless combo, a sequence of strikes that defied the laws of physics.</p>
<p>THUD! CRACK! BOOM!</p>
<p>Apollo launched a lunge, his fist wreathed in a spinning vortex of light.</p>
<p>"SOLAR IMPACT!" he bellowed.</p>
<p>The blow slammed into Rex’s chest, the sound of the impact like a mountain splitting in two. Rex was sent skidding back, his feet carving deep grooves into the stone, but he held his ground, a grin still plastered on his blood-stained face.</p>
<p>Before Rex could even recover, Apollo was already there, descending from above. He unleashed a flurry of punches, each one a concentrated burst of photonic energy.</p>
<p>BAM BAM BAM BAM!</p>
<p>The air itself seemed to shatter with every strike. The sound was a rhythmic staccato of explosions, a brutal symphony of violence.</p>
<p>KRA KOOM! KRA KOOM!</p>
<p>Rex tried to retaliate, throwing up gravity walls to deflect the blows, but Apollo was too swift. Apollo would strike, then leap back, then dive in again, his body a blur of brilliant gold.</p>
<p>He was using his knowledge of the world’s rhythm to find every gap in Rex’s defense.</p>
<p>"Is that all?" Apollo taunted, his voice a low rumble. He landed a heavy kick to Rex’s ribs, the sound of the impact like a thunderclap.</p>
<p>CRACK!</p>
<p>Rex gasped, the air forced from his lungs, but he didn’t break. He laughed again, a bitter sound.</p>
<p>"More! Give me more!"</p>
<p>Apollo’s eyes narrowed. He decided to end it. He felt the energy building in his core, a pure, concentrated mass of life force.</p>
<p>He began to gather every ounce of his power, the light around him becoming so intense that it blinded the very eyes of the observer. He was preparing his ultimate finisher.</p>
<p>"This is the end, Rex!" Apollo screamed, his voice a divine command. "DIE!"</p>
