<p>The guest room at the Starlight household was draped in that particular shade of pre-dawn gray, a liminal space where the world felt suspended between the exhausted silence of night and the inevitable intrusion of day. The light was weak and uncertain, filtering through the curtains like ash.</p>
<p>On the left side of the bed, Elizabeth slept. She possessed the focused stillness of someone who didn’t merely rest but recovered with military efficiency.</p>
<p>Her breathing was deep, rhythmic, and measured the steady pulse of a body that had been pushed to its limits and was now methodically rebuilding itself.</p>
<p>Rex sat on the right side of the bed, a shadow among shadows.</p>
<p>He remained motionless, monitoring his own internal state. The mark on his wrist hummed at its baseline, steady and quiet, the frantic energy of the divine domain visit having receded like a retreating tide.</p>
<p>In the background of his consciousness, his Presence Manipulation was working silently, a passive engine of signature management that shaved off the jagged edges of his aura. For the first time since the Sable Island integration, Rex could feel the difference; he was no longer broadcasting a signal to the world but absorbing it, becoming a ghost within his own skin.</p>
<p>His eyes drifted to the translucent glow of the system’s notification queue hovering in his peripheral vision.</p>
<p>[SYSTEM NOTIFICATION]</p>
<p>[PREGNANCY CONFIRMED Elizabeth Von Starlight.]</p>
<p>[FREE SKILL SELECTION UNLOCKED.]</p>
<p>[BUT YOU ALREADY CHOSE PRESENCE MANIPULATION.]</p>
<p>Rex stared at the words. ’Pregnancy confirmed... good shit...’</p>
<p>’Better late than never... I guess...’</p>
<p>A cold, pragmatic calculation flickered through his mind. This wasn’t about sentiment; it was about the brutal mathematics of power.</p>
<p>He looked at Elizabeth’s sleeping form, the woman who held the keys to the Starlight legacy, and then back at the screen. The cost of advancement had always been blood, sweat, or something far more intimate. He had paid that price. Now, it was time to collect.</p>
<p>With a deliberate swipe of his mental interface, he bypassed the standard skill trees and dove straight into the restricted catalog. His eyes searched for the one prize that would turn the tide against the gods.</p>
<p>’Let me check it...’</p>
<p>[DIMENSIONAL MANIPULATION FULL POTENTIAL]</p>
<p>[Complete navigation and operation across all known dimensional registers.]</p>
<p>[Enables: Detection of dimensional boundaries and layer transitions; controlled crossing of dimensional thresholds without standard teleportation anchors; operation within dimensional layers outside the primary physical plane; creation of localized dimensional pockets for storage or containment; and identification of dimensional workings applied to an area or target.]</p>
<p>[COMBINATION WITH TELEPORTATION: Extends teleportation’s navigation to all dimensional layers, including divine domains and their adjacent spaces.]</p>
<p>[PASSIVE FUNCTION: Constant awareness of the dimensional composition of the current environment detects temporal, spatial, or divine workings applied to the environment before their effects are felt.]</p>
<p>[KEY APPLICATION: Navigation to and from divine domains without divine invitation or sponsorship.]</p>
<p>[FREE SELECTION APPLIED AT NO COST.]</p>
<p>[DO YOU WANT TO FEEL IT?]</p>
<p>’Fuck yeah...’</p>
<p>The moment he committed to the selection, the world didn’t change; it unfolded.</p>
<p>It began as a searing pressure behind his eyes, followed by an invasive, terrifying expansion of his senses. It felt as though his consciousness was being stretched thin across a canvas that was far larger than he had ever imagined.</p>
<p>Then, the pressure snapped into clarity.</p>
<p>The integration was violent in its precision.</p>
<p>Suddenly, the room was no longer just four walls and a ceiling. His perception tore through the physical matter, revealing the hidden architecture of reality.</p>
<p>It was as if he had been blind to the very air he breathed, and now, suddenly, he could see the wind.</p>
<p>Through the new lens of his passive awareness, the room became a complex tapestry of overlapping strata.</p>
<p>First, there was the obvious: the physical plane of wood, fabric, and flesh. But beneath that, like veins beneath skin, he could see the shimmering, sickly residue of the temporal workings, the fading echoes of Kaeleth’s manipulation that still clung to the island like radioactive dust.</p>
<p>Even after Lustia’s counterfrequency had attempted to scrub it clean, the scars remained visible to him now.</p>
<p>And deeper... still deeper than time itself, he saw the foundation. The raw, grinding dimensional architecture of the island’s substrate, the ancient bones upon which this reality was built.</p>
<p>Rex didn’t flinch. He simply observed.</p>
<p>His mind, already hardened by years of tactical warfare, began to catalog the new data with ruthless speed. He wasn’t just seeing the room anymore; he was reading the blueprint of existence.</p>
<p>He read it.</p>
<p>The foundational layer’s dimensional composition was not some simple mineral map or a geological readout of bedrock. That was peasant-level perception.</p>
<p>What Rex saw now was something far more profound and unsettling; it was the skeletal framework of reality itself. He was looking at the specific dimensional architecture that eight hundred years of divine maintenance had woven into the island’s very base.</p>
<p>It was a cathedral of spacetime, constructed and reinforced by celestial hands.</p>
<p>And there, bleeding through the immaculate design like an infection in a clean wound, was the foreign signature.</p>
<p>It was the temporal working. Even after Lustia’s counterfrequency had attempted to sterilize the area, the residue remained a jagged, unnatural distortion that didn’t belong to the island’s natural rhythm.</p>
<p>It was a scar across the fabric of existence.</p>
<p>’Kaeleth’s work,’ Rex thought, his mind cataloging the anomaly with cold, surgical precision. ’A foreign imposition... An artificial graft onto a divine body.’</p>
<p>He didn’t recoil from the wrongness of it. Instead, he leaned into the new sensation, noting the specific frequency of the distortion, its phase shift, and its residual decay.</p>
<p>He filed it away in the deepest vaults of his memory.</p>
<p>Now, he wouldn’t just be affected by temporal anomalies; he would see them coming before they even touched his skin. He would see the trap before the trigger was pulled.</p>
<p>The coldness of his tactical mind retreated momentarily as he turned his gaze toward Elizabeth. The dimensional layers continued to swirl around her: the physical flesh, the spiritual aura, and the subtle displacement of space where her body met the mattress.</p>
<p>He looked at her with the quiet, focused attention of a predator taking inventory of something precious not out of sentimentality, but out of a fierce, possessive need to protect the assets he had claimed.</p>
<p>He leaned down, his movement fluid and silent, and pressed his lips to her temple. The contact was feather-light, a ghost of a touch that wouldn’t disturb her fragile equilibrium.</p>
<p>"Sleep," he murmured against her hair.</p>
<p>His voice was a low vibration, carrying a rare, genuine warmth and a vulnerability he never permitted himself to show the world. This wasn’t a performance for an audience; it was the truth he only allowed himself in the dark.</p>
<p>"You earned it twice over last night."</p>
<p>Elizabeth stirred, a soft, unconscious sound escaping her throat, a small noise of comfort that acknowledged the presence of warmth without breaking the veil of sleep. Rex lingered for one heartbeat longer, letting the image of her peace anchor him before he straightened, the mask of the soldier snapping back into place.</p>
<p>He dressed with the mechanical, lethal efficiency of a man who operated on a different clock than the rest of humanity. The grey morning light meant nothing to him; his internal gears were already grinding toward the next objective.</p>
<p>The door opened with a soft click.</p>
<p>The hallway was not empty. Lily and Diana were both standing there, positioned in the corridor like sentinels.</p>
<p>Whether it was coincidence or the uncanny sixth sense that develops in people who have learned to read the shadows of those they care about, Rex didn’t care. Their presence alone spoke volumes.</p>
<p>They knew. They could feel the charged atmosphere clinging to him like smoke from a battlefield.</p>
<p>Their eyes locked onto his.</p>
<p>"You look like you’ve been awake for three hours longer than you should have been," Lily said, her voice carrying an edge of concern she couldn’t quite disguise.</p>
<p>Rex’s expression remained impassive, his eyes reflecting the hollow exhaustion of someone who had just traversed dimensions.</p>
<p>"I have been awake for a different amount of time than either of us can easily calculate," he replied.</p>
<p>It wasn’t a deflection; it was the literal truth. His subjective experience had been fractured by the divine domain, stretched and compressed by forces they couldn’t comprehend.</p>
<p>Diana didn’t ask about his sleep. She didn’t ask about the time.</p>
<p>She simply watched him with that flat, terrifyingly perceptive stare, the look she used when she knew the surface story was a lie designed to protect the deeper wound. She could see the way his shoulders held tension that wasn’t just physical but existential.</p>
<p>"Are you... fine?" she asked, her voice quiet but carrying the weight of an interrogation.</p>
<p>Rex didn’t offer her the comfort of a lie. He didn’t have the time for it.</p>
<p>The new power thrumming in his veins demanded action, and the pieces of the puzzle were still scattered across the board.</p>
<p>"Yes," Rex said, his voice cutting through the morning stillness like a blade. "I need information."</p>
