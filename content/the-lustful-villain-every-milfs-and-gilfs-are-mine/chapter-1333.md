<p>Rex did not pause to admire his handiwork. He did not give her a moment to grieve the loss of her divine authority.</p>

<p>He was a hurricane of focused, methodical brutality.</p>

<p>Using [Telekinesis]telekinesis, he reached into the twisted wreckage of the clock tower.</p>

<p>With a violent, wrenching motion, SKREEEEE tore Evelyn from beneath the tons of stone like a child pulling a doll from a grave. Before she could even gasp for air, he slammed her downward.</p>

<p>"Gravity Manipulation: World Crushing Pressure!"</p>

<p>The impact was seismic. He didn’t just throw her; he anchored her to the earth with a hundred times the planet’s own weight.</p>

<p>The stone beneath her didn’t just crack; it liquefied. A fresh crater exploded outward, sending shrapnel of granite flying like bullets.</p>

<p>CRUNCH.</p>

<p>The sound of her vertebrae compressing under the sudden, impossible force echoed through the plaza.</p>

<p>He was relentless.</p>

<p>He tore jagged spears of bedrock from the earth with a flick of his wrist, lifting her broken body into the air only to sear her with [Lust] the violet fire that burned not just the flesh but the very nerve endings themselves.</p>

<p>Her screams were cut short by the wet gurgle of blood filling her throat.</p>

<p>KRAK.</p>

<p>His knee connected with her sternum. The last intact rib in her chest snapped like a dry twig, sending bone fragments piercing inward toward her lungs.</p>

<p>Every blow was a calculated atrocity. Every strike was measured with surgical precision.</p>

<p>He wasn’t trying to end the fight; he was trying to dismantle her. He stopped every punch millimeters before it would stop her heart; he stopped every kick inches before it would shatter her skull.</p>

<p>He wanted her conscious. He wanted her to feel every microsecond of her own unraveling. He wanted her to be fully present for the moment her world died.</p>

<p>"I gave you forty minutes," Rex said, his voice dropping to a conversational murmur as he stepped over her broken form.</p>

<p>He planted his boot directly onto her wrist just as she desperately tried to summon a single, dying spark of golden lightning.</p>

<p>CRUNCH.</p>

<p>The bones in her arm ground together under his heel. "I gave you the chance to play hero..."</p>

<p>"To try and balance the scales with righteous fury."</p>

<p>He leaned down, his face inches from hers, his eyes burning with a terrifying, triumphant clarity. "Now you can finally see the difference between us."</p>

<p>His grin was razor sharp. "You fight for a concept, Evelyn..."</p>

<p>"For ’Justice.’ For ’Truth.’ But those are abstract things..."</p>

<p>"I fight for what is real... I fight for the people who breathe because I allow them to."</p>

<p>He stood upright, looking down at her as if she were nothing more than an interesting insect caught in amber. "You have exactly one way to stop me, little angel!"</p>

<p>"And you can’t use it... Because to use it, you have to become me."</p>

<p>Evelyn’s despair arrived not as a scream but as a slow, cold drowning.</p>

<p>First, the lightning died.</p>

<p>The golden glow in her fingertips flickered and faded into gray ash.</p>

<p>Then, the protective circle of [Open Court] began to contract.</p>

<p>The hundred-meter barrier shriveled to fifty, then twenty, then ten, before finally collapsing into a tiny, pathetic ring no wider than her own body.</p>

<p>The divine protection that had defined her existence was being strangled out of her. The mark on her chest, the symbol of her covenant, flickered weakly, dimmed, and then went dark.</p>

<p>In the gathering darkness behind her eyelids, as her vision began to tunnel, she heard it.</p>

<p>A voice.</p>

<p>It wasn’t Rex’s.</p>

<p>It wasn’t her own.</p>

<p>It was faint, thin, and sounded as though it were echoing from the bottom of a deep, ancient well.</p>

<p>’The scales do not ask you to win with force, Evelyn.’</p>

<p>’They ask you to say what is true.’</p>

<p>She lay in the crimson-soaked crater, the copper taste of blood flooding her mouth, and she let those words sink past the pain. Past the broken ribs, past the shattered jaw, past the terror.</p>

<p>She realized Rex was right. She could not kill him.</p>

<p>His anchors made him an immortal plague. She could not even beat him; he had adapted to her very essence.</p>

<p>But she saw the flaw in his perfect equation.</p>

<p>His power was built on his anchors on the women who stood by him. But their loyalty was a product of his will, of his seduction, of his absolute control.</p>

<p>It was a bond forged in shadow, not in truth.</p>

<p>She gathered every fractured ounce of her remaining consciousness. She pulled from the very core of her being, not from her magic, but from the fundamental truth of her soul.</p>

<p>She gathered the last breath her punctured lungs could manage, lifted her blood-streaked head, and prepared to speak.</p>

<p>She would not speak as a warrior.</p>

<p>She would speak as a witness.</p>

<p>"LISTEN TO ME!"</p>

<p>Evelyn’s voice did not merely carry; it detonated.</p>

<p>It was not a human sound. It was the sound of a dam breaking, of a thousand years of suppressed truth erupting from a single, broken throat.</p>

<p>BOOOOOOOOOOOM!</p>

<p>A shockwave of pure, incandescent gold erupted from her body. The [Open Court] domain, which had been shriveled to a pathetic ring around her broken form, exploded outward with the force of a supernova.</p>

<p>The golden ring tore through the plaza, slammed into the ancient city walls with a thunderous CRACK, and refused to stop.</p>

<p>It raced up the avenues like molten lightning, surged over the rooftops like a tidal wave of light, and poured down into every dark alleyway in Aethelgard.</p>

<p>The entire city was suddenly bathed in an unforgiving, divine radiance that left no shadow unturned.</p>

<p>Rex reacted with the instinct of a cornered god. His face contorted into something feral as he lashed out with [Telekinesis].</p>

<p>Invisible fingers of crushing force wrapped around Evelyn’s throat, seeking to strangle the truth before it could finish its sentence.</p>

<p>"Shut. Up." he hissed, his eyes blazing.</p>

<p>But the [Verdict] would not be silenced.</p>

<p>As his telekinetic grip closed around her windpipe, the golden light of the domain reacted to his malice. The authority of the Court recognized him not as a judge, but as the accused.</p>

<p>The divine energy surged against his grip, brushing his telekinetic field aside as easily as a breeze brushing past a sleeve.</p>

<p>The recoil was physical, a shockwave of holy rejection that shoved Rex backward, forcing him to take two full, stumbling steps across the cracked stone.</p>

<p>Evelyn gasped for air, blood frothing at the corners of her mouth, but she did not stop.</p>

<p>She leaned into the agony, using her pain as fuel.</p>

<p>"All of you whose hearts are tied to Rex Rexilion!" she screamed, her voice cracking with the strain of her shattered ribs, yet carrying with supernatural authority. "All of you who believe his lies!"</p>

<p>She turned her gaze toward the sky, toward the heavens that had watched this atrocity unfold. "To Lord Xerollion! To Tremor, the Undead Bringer! Know this truth... THEY ARE THE SAME MAN! "</p>

<p>The revelation hung in the air like a guillotine blade.</p>

<p>"He killed Apollo!"</p>

<p>"He slaughtered Veylor and Calivara and Eryndra and Theo... AND THE OTHERS!" Her words struck like physical blows.</p>

<p>"He tore this city to pieces with his hands, and he has the audacity to call it saving you!!!"</p>

<p>"Look around you!" She gestured wildly to the smoking ruins, the corpses, and the blood-soaked stones. "Look at the devastation he leaves in his wake!!!"</p>

<p>"Remember who you were before he entered your lives!!!"</p>

<p>"Remember the peace you had before his touch poisoned your souls!!!"</p>

<p>Her voice dropped to a raw, desperate plea that pierced through the golden light. "That is not love!"</p>

<p>"That is not devotion!"</p>

<p>"It is a leash!"</p>

<p>"It is a shackle wrapped in silk and honey!"</p>

<p>"WAKEEEEEE UUUUUP!"</p>

<p>The [Verdict] swept through the city like a holy wind, carrying her words directly into the minds and souls of every living creature.</p>

<p>In the plaza, Lily collapsed to her knees, her eyes blown wide with terror as the telepathic link in her mind flickered and sparked like a short circuiting wire.</p>

<p>Diana clutched her head with both hands, gasping as if a thick, choking fog had suddenly been burned away from her brain.</p>

<p>Talyra, Aisella, Nerith, and Mireya froze mid-step, their faces draining of all color until they looked like marble statues.</p>

<p>Cassandra clenched her fists so hard her knuckles turned white, her breath coming in ragged, terrified gasps.</p>

<p>Gorvasha let out a low, guttural growl of confusion and pain, one clawed hand clutching desperately at her own chest as if her heart were betraying her.</p>

<p>Valentina covered her mouth with both hands, tears streaming down her face. The horror of her complicity washed over her; every choice she had made in the last few days suddenly felt like a mountain of lead pressing down on her shoulders.</p>

<p>Far across the city, in the warm, domestic safety of the Silver Rest Inn’s kitchen, Mara and Marceline froze.</p>

<p>Their hands remained pressed against their swollen bellies, their faces pale with a sudden, inexplicable dread.</p>

<p>In the hallowed, silent halls of the Temple of Life, Amelia Brightsoul fell to her knees. The names of the dead, the names of those Rex had butchered, raced through her mind like poisoned spears.</p>

<p>And Elizabeth.</p>

<p>Elizabeth stood straighter than all of them. Her spine was unyielding, her gaze cold and sharp.</p>

<p>She did not flinch. She did not waver.</p>

<p>Because Elizabeth had never been blind; she had watched the darkness grow, and she had chosen to stand in it with her eyes wide open.</p>

<p>The city of Aethelgard held its breath.</p>

<p>The truth had been spoken.</p>

<p>The leash was visible.</p>
