<p>"Eight minutes," Morwenna hissed, her fingers clawing weakly at the stone, her nails splintering as she tried to find purchase in a world that was trying to flatten her into a smear. "Eight fucking minutes of absolute, unmitigated hell... and you’re not even winded."</p>
<p>"I’ve fought Apostles, gods, and monsters... Most of them couldn’t last eight minutes in a real scrap with me without begging for mercy..."</p>
<p>"And here you are, standing there like we just had a casual fucking conversation over tea."</p>
<p>"We did just have a conversation," Rex said, his grin widening into something predatory, beautiful, and utterly terrifying. "And a damn good one at that."</p>
<p>"You’re the best fight I’ve had all morning, Morwenna. And trust me..." He leaned down slightly, his shadow swallowing her whole, "...I’ve had a few."</p>
<p>"That’s a fucking insult... where the hell am I standing?" she muttered, closing her eyes for a second to stave off the violent vertigo of the gravity field.</p>
<p>"No," Rex agreed, his voice dropping an octave, vibrating with a dark, heavy resonance that seemed to echo in her very bones. "I suppose it is."</p>
<p>The heavy, rhythmic thud of approaching footsteps tore through the suffocating silence of the ruined plaza. Rex didn’t even bother to turn his head; his geological awareness had registered the vibrations of their arrival long before they breached the crater’s edge.</p>
<p>The earth, his domain, had whispered their approach in a symphony of dread.</p>
<p>Aurelia emerged from the dust and debris of the south, her presence a crushing mass of pure, unbridled combat potential. Behind her, Iris trailed like a silent, lethal shadow, her eyes scanning the devastation with a cold, calculating intensity.</p>
<p>Rex turned slowly to face them, his mind instantly shifting into a high-level tactical assessment. He was already managing a three-front war, and now, he was adding a fourth.</p>
<p>’Oh...? Here she is... someone that I’ve been waiting for because she’s going to be the next girl I’ve fucked so fucking hard.’</p>
<p>’But now... I have to teach her some lesson.’</p>
<p>His foresight mapped them in a flash of precognitive data. And then he found Iris, where she was a scalpel, an assassin-class energy signature built for precision, speed, and the surgical removal of vital organs.</p>
<p>She was a knife aimed at the heart. Aurelia, however, was a warhammer.</p>
<p>Her energy profile was massive, a direct combat powerhouse designed for overwhelming force and absolute, unrelenting dominance.</p>
<p>Both women froze. Their eyes darted from the broken, bloody form of Morwenna pinned against the shattered cobblestones by a localized, crushing field of gravity to the unbothered, godlike figure of Rex.</p>
<p>"You... you actually put her down," Aurelia said.</p>
<p>Her voice wasn’t filled with disbelief; it was filled with a sudden, violent realization. It was the sound of a woman undergoing a mental recalculation so drastic it threatened to shatter her composure.</p>
<p>The world they knew had just changed.</p>
<p>"I did," Rex said, his tone casual, almost bored.</p>
<p>He glanced down at Morwenna, who was coughing up a thick, bubbling spray of crimson. "She lasted longer than the stories suggested."</p>
<p>"I’ll give her credit for that," Rex laughed. "She’s got a hell of a lot more spine than I expected."</p>
<p>Aurelia’s jaw tightened, her knuckles whitening as her hand drifted instinctively toward the weapon at her side. "Iris told me you were dangerous."</p>
<p>"She told me you were a goddamn menace judging by how you murder those innocent reincarnators and apostles!"</p>
<p>Rex locked onto her, his eyes piercing through her, stripping away the bravado. "And?"</p>
<p>"And I told her she was being dramatic," Aurelia admitted, her voice strained and brittle. "I told her you were just another high-tier anomaly to be managed, cataloged, and then put down."</p>
<p>Rex’s expression didn’t change, but the air around him began to hum. The ground beneath their feet groaned, a low, subterranean rumble that vibrated up through their boots and into their marrow.</p>
<p>The very molecules of the air seemed to thicken, turning heavy and oppressive.</p>
<p>"She wasn’t being dramatic," Rex said, his voice dropping to a cold, lethal whisper that cut through the wind. "She was being fucking polite."</p>
<p>SNAP!</p>
<p>A nearby shard of broken granite exploded into dust as the gravity field expanded slightly, a warning shot that sent a shockwave of pure force through the plaza.</p>
<p>"But I think it’s time we stop being polite," Rex continued, his eyes gleaming with a predatory light. "Come on then!"</p>
<p>"Let’s see if you two can do any better than she did."</p>
<p>Aurelia didn’t wait for a formal invitation to violence. She exploded forward, her aura erupting in a shockwave of pure, unadulterated kinetic force that sent a cloud of pulverized stone screaming outward.</p>
<p>She was a juggernaut, a force of nature that didn’t just move; she conquered the space she occupied.</p>
<p>She came in first, a direct, devastating lunge designed to shatter everything in her path.</p>
<p>Her strikes were precisely what her designation promised: sheer, overwhelming structural impact. Each punch carried the weight of a falling monolith, intended to pulverize bone and dent steel.</p>
<p>Rex met the onslaught with the terrifying efficiency of his martial arts mastery. He didn’t just block; he redirected, using the momentum of her own massive force to slide past her guard, his movements a fluid, lethal dance of redirection and counterpressure.</p>
<p>He was managing the high-intensity engagement with Aurelia in close quarters, all while maintaining the crushing, localized gravity field on the broken Morwenna, ensuring the old woman remained a pinned, bleeding spectator to her own demise.</p>
<p>BOOM! CRACK!</p>
<p>As Aurelia hammered at him, a blur of gold and fury, Iris moved. She was the silent shadow, the lethal variable.</p>
<p>She utilized the chaos of Aurelia’s heavy engagement as a veil, circling wide to find the blind spot, the exact angle required for a precision strike that would end the fight before it truly began.</p>
<p>Rex’s Foresight flared. He saw the trajectory before Iris even tightened her grip.</p>
<p>He saw the glint of the blade at the seven-second mark, the specific, murderous arc she intended to take. Without breaking his rhythmic, brutal exchange with Aurelia, Rex flicked a finger, a microburst of telekinetic gravity snapping toward the incoming steel.</p>
<p>WHIP SNAP!</p>
<p>The knife didn’t just stop; it was seized mid-flight by an invisible hand, its momentum violently inverted. The blade hissed through the air, returning to Iris’s hand at a sharp, unnatural angle.</p>
<p>She caught it with a sharp, metallic "CLACK," her eyes narrowing as she stabilized her stance.</p>
<p>"You weren’t even looking at me," Iris said, her voice a low, dangerous silk.</p>
<p>"I didn’t need to be," Rex replied, his voice steady despite the thunderous impact of Aurelia’s fist slamming against his forearm.</p>
<p>THUD!</p>
<p>He didn’t even flinch. "I’ve watched you fight before!"</p>
<p>"You throw with your wrist angled slightly inward on the release, a tiny, microscopic habit."</p>
<p>"Most people wouldn’t catch it, but it leaves a signature in the air." He glanced at her, a dark, knowing smirk playing on his lips. "I caught it the first time I saw you spar with Theo, actually."</p>
<p>"Never told anyone. Seemed rude to spoil the surprise."</p>
<p>"I fucking knew it... Underlayer people are assholes for eavesdropping and even eyeing us like that!"</p>
<p>A heavy silence descended for a heartbeat, punctuated only by the ragged, wet breathing of the pinned Morwenna.</p>
<p>"You’ve been cataloguing my technique since then," Iris said.</p>
<p>It wasn’t a question; it was a cold realization that settled in her gut like lead. Her voice had gone deathly still, the predatory calm of a hunter who realized she was being hunted.</p>
<p>"I catalogue everyone’s technique," Rex said, his eyes snapping back to Aurelia as she lunged again, her fist whistling through the air like a cannonball.</p>
<p>WHOOSH!</p>
<p>"It’s not personal. Well... it wasn’t, at the time."</p>
<p>The battle reignited with a ferocity that turned the air into a war zone. Rex was now operating at the razor’s edge of his capacity.</p>
<p>He was solving a complex, high-speed mathematical equation of violence: maintaining the crushing gravity hold on Morwenna to keep her out of the fray, parrying Aurelia’s direct, bone-shattering assaults, and simultaneously tracking Iris’s lethal, shifting positioning.</p>
<p>CRUNCH! SMASH! SHLUCK!</p>
<p>Blood sprayed from a shallow cut on Rex’s cheek as Iris’s blade grazed him, and the ground groaned under the weight of Aurelia’s relentless pressure. He was managing a three-front war of attrition, a masterpiece of resource distribution where a single millisecond of distraction would mean a blade in his throat or a fist through his chest.</p>
<p>He was dancing on the edge of a precipice, and the view was magnificent.</p>
