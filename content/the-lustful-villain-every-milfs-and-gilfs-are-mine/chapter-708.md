<p>The light around him began to condense, forming a swirling vortex of pure photonic energy. The shattered remains of the pit were illuminated in a harsh, unforgiving glare, showing every drop of blood and every fragment of bone in vivid, terrifying detail.</p>
<p>Apollo wasn’t just angry; he was ascending into a state of pure, unadulterated wrath. The light was no longer just light; it was a weapon, a judgment, and a promise of total annihilation.</p>
<p>The air in the cavern didn’t just vibrate; it screamed. Apollo had transcended mere anger; he had become a living supernova.</p>
<p>His eyes were no longer human; they were two pitted wells of blinding, white-hot radiance that seemed to burn the very shadows out of the corners of the room.</p>
<p>"You think you’ve won, Tremor?" Apollo’s voice was a tectonic rumble, layered with a godlike resonance. "You think you can break the world and walk away?"</p>
<p>Without waiting for an answer, Apollo exploded into motion. He didn’t run; he teleported through sheer velocity of light.</p>
<p>"SOLAR FLARE: FIRST BURST!"</p>
<p>Apollo launched a series of condensed light bolts, each one moving at a fraction of the speed of light. They weren’t just beams; they were slabs of pure thermal destruction.</p>
<p>They tore through the air with a sonic boom that shattered the remaining stone walls.</p>
<p>Rex, sensing the lethal intent, reacted with the instinct of a cornered predator. He threw up a barrier of compressed gravity, a dense shell of dark force. The light bolts slammed into the gravity shield with the sound of exploding stars.</p>
<p>BOOM! BOOM! BOOM!</p>
<p>The impacts shook the ground, but Rex moved with a predatory grace, sliding backward to avoid the direct concussion of the blasts.</p>
<p>"Is that all, you glorified torch?!" Rex taunted, his voice rasping through the smoke.</p>
<p>He lunged forward, trying to close the distance, but Apollo was already gone. Apollo reappeared ten feet above Rex, descending like a fallen star.</p>
<p>"PHOTONIC STRIKE!" He swung a fist made of solidified light. Rex twisted his body mid-air, the strike grazing his shoulder and melting the very stone beneath him into molten glass.</p>
<p>"You’re fast, Tremor," Apollo mocked, his voice dripping with a cold, divine arrogance. "But you’re still just a man playing with dirt!"</p>
<p>"You’re fighting the sun itself!"</p>
<p>Rex roared, throwing a massive wave of gravity-crushing force, trying to pin Apollo to the ground. But Apollo simply ascended, his body becoming a blur of luminous motion.</p>
<p>He was dancing through the chaos. Every time Rex tried to strike, Apollo was already somewhere else, leaving only a trail of burning light in his wake.</p>
<p>"Come on!" Apollo challenged, his voice echoing like a god’s command. "Show me the strength that broke those women!"</p>
<p>"Show me the power that thinks it can defy the heavens!"</p>
<p>Rex, driven to the brink of fury, unleashed a desperate burst of all his power. He used his Earthen Authority to launch a barrage of jagged obsidian shards, each one infused with gravity wells to drag Apollo down. It was a storm of stone and force.</p>
<p>But Apollo was in the zone. He moved with a mathematical precision.</p>
<p>He parried the shards with shards of hardened light; his movements were a symphony of violent elegance. He dodged the gravity wells by shifting his mass in midair, defying the very laws of physics.</p>
<p>"Too slow!" Apollo shouted, appearing suddenly behind Rex. He delivered a palm strike of pure radiance directly into Rex’s spine.</p>
<p>CRACK!</p>
<p>The impact sent Rex skidding across the bloodstained floor, his body smoking from the sheer heat. Apollo landed softly, his feet barely touching the ground, his aura blazing so bright that the shadows in the room were erased.</p>
<p>"You’re not a god, Tremor," Apollo said, his eyes glowing with a terrifying, unyielding light. "You’re just a bug waiting to be burned."</p>
<p>Apollo didn’t give Rex a moment to breathe. He saw the opening and he took it with the ferocity of a starving predator. He became a whirlwind of incandescent violence, a literal storm of light and heat that turned the cavern into a furnace.</p>
<p>"HEAVENLY CHAIN: LIGHTNING LANCE!" Apollo roared. He thrust his hands forward, and from the very air itself, bolts of pure white-hot plasma erupted like lances, lashing out at Rex from every conceivable angle.</p>
<p>Rex danced through the storm. He was a shadow in the glare, a phantom of grit and gravity.</p>
<p>He moved with a rhythmic, brutal efficiency, parrying the light lances with gravity wells and dodging the shattering shockwaves of Apollo’s footsteps. He was letting the attacks land not because he was weak, but because he was studying.</p>
<p>He was absorbing the rhythm of the god light, feeling the heat, and measuring the intensity.</p>
<p>"Is that all the divinity you have?" Rex taunted, his voice a low growl amidst the roaring flames.</p>
<p>He took a heavy blow to the shoulder, the impact sending a spray of sparks flying, but he didn’t even flinch. He just grinned, a bloody, manic grin.</p>
<p>Apollo didn’t even answer. He transitioned into a deadly combo.</p>
<p>He launched a succession of blinding flashes, followed by a crushing burst of photonic pressure. He was a blur of gold and white, his attacks coming in a relentless cadence.</p>
<p>Flash! Boom! Crackle!</p>
<p>The ground beneath them was turning to glass. The air was screaming.</p>
<p>Apollo was using every ounce of his power, unleashing a maelstrom of light magic that should have obliterated any mortal. He was a conqueror of the void, a bringer of judgment.</p>
<p>But as the dust settled from the last massive explosion, Rex didn’t fall. He didn’t even stumble.</p>
<p>Rex stood there, his body covered in scorch marks and blood, but his eyes were glowing with a dark triumph. He began to laugh.</p>
<p>It wasn’t the manic laugh from before; it was a deep, resonant, terrifying laugh that seemed to vibrate in the very marrow of Apollo’s bones.</p>
<p>"Impressive," Rex said, his voice dripping with a cold, calculated pride. "Truly impressive!"</p>
<p>"You have the fire! But you lack the soul."</p>
<p>As he spoke, a strange sound echoed through the chamber. A faint, crystalline cracking.</p>
<p>A jagged line appeared across the middle of his face. It wasn’t a wound, but it was a fracture.</p>
<p>The mask he had been wearing, the visage of the monster, the mask of the villain, began to crack. It wasn’t flesh breaking; it was a shell.</p>
<p>A porcelain-like surface began to peel away in shards of black stone.</p>
<p>The mask split.</p>
<p>As the shell fell away, it revealed a face. But it wasn’t a legendary warrior’s face.</p>
<p>It wasn’t the face of a demon or a god. It was the face of a man.</p>
<p>A completely ordinary, unremarkable man. A man with average features, a plain expression, and eyes that held no divinity, only a chilling, unsettling emptiness.</p>
<p>He was a nobody. A random soul plucked from the depths of the Underlayer.</p>
<p>A man who should have been nothing, now standing at the center of a god’s wrath.</p>
<p>The mask had fallen, revealing that the monster was nothing more than a man, and the man was something far more terrifying than a monster. He was a void.</p>
<p>"Who...?" Apollo stammed, his light flickering for the first time. "Who are you?"</p>
<p>Rex simply smiled, a cold, human smile that was more frightening than any demon’s snarl.</p>
<p>"Just a man," Rex replied, his voice flat. "Just a man who decided to play god."</p>
<p>The silence that followed Rex’s revelation was heavier than the gravity he manipulated. The man standing there wasn’t a beast or a demon; he was a void in the shape of a human, a hollow shell that had stolen the power of the earth itself.</p>
<p>"Don’t look so confused, little light bringer," Rex said, his voice now devoid of its manic edge, replaced by a terrifying, calm steadiness.</p>
<p>He wiped a smear of blood from his lip, his eyes locking onto Apollo’s with a predatory stillness. "I already introduced myself, right...? The name is Tremor... But you can call me the Architect of the Ruin now."</p>
<p>"I am the one who found the Earthen Apostle’s secrets!" Rex clenched both his fists. "And I am the one who mastered the crushing weight of the world!!!"</p>
<p>He stepped forward, the ground groaning under his presence. "You fight for honor? For justice? Those are just fictions."</p>
<p>"There is only weight, and there is only the will to carry it."</p>
<p>Without warning, Rex moved. He didn’t lunge; he erupted.</p>
<p>"Earth Shatter Counter!"</p>
<p>He slammed his fist into the ground, but instead of a shockwave, the ground itself rose up like a living serpent. A massive pillar of jagged stone shot toward Apollo, not just to hit him, but to constrict him.</p>
<p>Apollo dodged, a burst of light propelling him backward, but Rex was already there, his hand outstretched. A wave of gravity slammed into Apollo’s chest, momentarily stealing his breath.</p>
<p>"Think of them, Apollo!" Rex taunted, his voice echoing through the cavern. "Think of the two women you tried to save!"</p>
<p>"The way their bones snapped! The way their eyes went dull! They died screaming for a god who was too slow to save them!"</p>
<p>Apollo’s eyes widened, a flash of agony crossing his face. The memory of the women’s screams, the wet sound of their breaking bodies, surged back into his mind.</p>
<p>"They were nothing!" Rex roared, launching a barrage of stone shards that moved like bullets. "They were just meat and bone! Just like you will be!"</p>
