<p>SHLICK!</p>
<p>Rex wiped a stray bit of dust from his shoulder, his expression turning cold and predatory. "You wasted your most precious energy on a vanity project."</p>
<p>"You thought you were the storm? You were just a gust of wind trying to catch a lady’s eye."</p>
<p>Veylor’s face flushed a deep, humiliated crimson. His hands shook, not from exhaustion this time, but from a stinging, soul-crushing shame.</p>
<p>The "heroic" moment he had envisioned was being torn to shreds by the very man he thought he had broken.</p>
<p>"It wasn’t... it wasn’t just for her..." Veylor whispered, his voice cracking.</p>
<p>"Liar!" Rex bellowed, his aura suddenly flaring with a dark, suffocating intensity that made the air feel heavy enough to drown in.</p>
<p>The ground beneath him began to crack and sink, not from wind, but from the sheer, concentrated mass of his presence.</p>
<p>CRACKLE! RUMBLE!</p>
<p>Rex’s eyes turned a deep, abyssal black, and the temperature in the plaza seemed to drop as his gravity began to warp the very light around him. The mockery vanished, replaced by a terrifying, singular purpose.</p>
<p>"You’ve had your fun, little performer of a fucking clown," Rex growled, his voice dropping to a lethal, quiet hiss that carried more weight than his roar. "You’ve shown the world your tricks."</p>
<p>"Now... it’s time the world sees the truth of what a real god looks like."</p>
<p>Rex planted his feet, his stance widening, his muscles bulging to the point of tearing. The earth beneath him didn’t just crack; it liquefied under the pressure.</p>
<p>"BRACE YOURSELF, BOY!"</p>
<p>"GRAVITY SINGULARITY: EVENT HORIZON!"</p>
<p>Rex didn’t just move; he collapsed the space between himself and Veylor. He became a black hole of pure, kinetic intent.</p>
<p>The air was sucked into the vacuum of his movement, creating a deafening, roaring howl of a thousand dying stars.</p>
<p>VREEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEE!</p>
<p>He launched himself upward, not as a man, but as a concentrated point of infinite mass. The sheer force of his ascent shook the plaza, which sent Aurelia and Iris tumbling backward.</p>
<p>BOOOOOOOM!</p>
<p>Veylor, seeing the darkness approaching, felt a primal, ancient fear scream in his mind. This wasn’t a flurry of wind or a flashy trick.</p>
<p>This was the end of all things. The predator wasn’t just coming for him; the universe itself was coming to crush him.</p>
<p>"HAAAAAA!" Rex’s roar echoed through the heavens as he closed the distance in a heartbeat, his fist aimed not at Veylor’s body, but at the very center of his existence.</p>
<p>The collision was not a sound; it was a cataclysm.</p>
<p>KRA THOOOOOOOOOM!</p>
<p>When Rex’s fist met Veylor’s desperate, last-ditch wind barrier, the impact didn’t just break the shield; it shattered the very concept of space. The air exploded outward in a violent, concussive ring of pressure that sent Iris and Aurelia skidding across the dirt.</p>
<p>WHOOOOOOOSH!</p>
<p>Veylor didn’t fly backward; he was driven. Rex’s fist, backed by the weight of a collapsing mountain, punched through Veylor’s energy, through his ribs, and slammed him into the ground with such ferocity that a crater twenty meters wide erupted instantly.</p>
<p>CRUNCH BOOM!</p>
<p>Veylor lay in the center of the wreckage, a broken heap of blue and white. His breathing was a wet, rattling sound.</p>
<p>"Gurgle... huff... huff..." He tried to lift his head, but Rex was already there, standing over him like a dark god of ruin.</p>
<p>"Still trying to stand, little bird?" Rex sneered.</p>
<p>He didn’t use a flashy technique this time. He used raw, brutal, unadulterated violence.</p>
<p>WHACK!</p>
<p>Rex’s heavy boot descended on Veylor’s stomach.</p>
<p>CRACK!</p>
<p>The sound of Veylor’s diaphragm collapsing was sickening. Veylor’s eyes bulged, a spray of crimson blood erupting from his lips.</p>
<p>PHTOOO!</p>
<p>"Stop... please..." Veylor wheezed, his fingers clawing at the dirt.</p>
<p>"Stop? We’re just getting to the encore!" Rex laughed, a cruel, jagged sound.</p>
<p>He reached down, grabbed Veylor by his hair, and hauled his head up.</p>
<p>SHLICK!</p>
<p>The scalp strained, the pain white hot and blinding. "Look at her, Veylor! Look at your precious Iris! See how she watches her ’hero’ turn into a pile of bloody meat!"</p>
<p>BAM!</p>
<p>Rex delivered a short, piston-like punch to Veylor’s jaw.</p>
<p>CRACK!</p>
<p>Veylor’s head snapped to the side, his jaw audibly dislocating.</p>
<p>POP!</p>
<p>BAM! BAM! BAM!</p>
<p>A relentless barrage of heavy, gravitational strikes rained down on Veylor. Rex wasn’t just hitting him; he was using his gravity to pull Veylor’s head into the fist, amplifying the impact.</p>
<p>THUD! CRUNCH! SPLAT!</p>
<p>Each blow was a masterpiece of misery. Veylor’s face became a mask of swelling purple bruises and weeping lacerations.</p>
<p>His nose was flattened, his eyes swollen nearly shut, and his beautiful, flowing hair was matted with a thick, dark slurry of blood and grit.</p>
<p>"NO! STOP IT! STOP ITTTTTTTTT!"</p>
<p>Iris’s scream tore through the air, a sound of pure, unrefined agony. She was crawling toward the crater, her hands bleeding as she dragged her broken body across the stones.</p>
<p>"Tremor! Please! He’s done! He can’t take any more!"</p>
<p>"PLEASE, HAVE MERCY!"</p>
<p>Rex didn’t even look at her. He grabbed Veylor by the throat, his massive fingers sinking into the soft tissue, and lifted him high enough that Veylor’s legs dangled uselessly, kicking the air in a rhythmic, dying spasm.</p>
<p>FLAP FLAP FLAP!</p>
<p>"Mercy?" Rex mocked, his voice a low, terrifying growl.</p>
<p>He leaned in close to Veylor’s ear, so close that Veylor could feel the heat of his murderous breath. "Mercy is for the weak!"</p>
<p>"Mercy is for people who haven’t earned the right to die," Rex laughed. "You? You haven’t even finished your performance yet."</p>
<p>Rex slammed Veylor face-first into a jagged pillar of stone that remained standing in the crater.</p>
<p>KRA THOOM!</p>
<p>The stone shattered, and Veylor’s face followed suit.</p>
<p>SPLAT!</p>
<p>Blood sprayed across the ruins like a macabre painting.</p>
<p>"Look at him, Iris!" Rex roared, turning his head slightly toward her, a sadistic glint in his eyes. "Look at your savior!"</p>
<p>"He looks like a crushed insect!" Rex laughed. "Is this the man you wanted to protect you?!"</p>
<p>"A man who can’t even hold his own head up?"</p>
<p>Veylor let out a long, pathetic moan, a sound of pure, broken spirit. "Uuuuugh... hhh..."</p>
<p>A thick stream of blood leaked from the corner of his mouth, dripping onto his tattered tunic. He was a wreck, a shivering, bleeding, humiliated mess of a man.</p>
<p>"Please... Tremor... kill him if you must, but don’t... don’t do this to him..." Iris sobbed, her tears carving clean tracks through the dust on her cheeks. "Don’t make him suffer like this!"</p>
<p>Rex let out a long, satisfied hum, his fingers tightening around Veylor’s throat just enough to make the boy’s face turn a terrifying shade of blue. He wasn’t going to kill him.</p>
<p>Not yet. Death was too quick, and death was too kind.</p>
<p>He wanted Veylor to feel every ounce of his failure. He wanted Veylor to live long enough to realize that his "heroism" was nothing more than a joke in the face of true, primordial power.</p>
<p>"Don’t worry, little asssassin," Rex whispered, his grin widening to show his teeth. "He’s not going anywhere."</p>
<p>"He’s going to stay awake for every single second of the nightmare that’s coming next."</p>
<p>He dropped Veylor like a piece of trash.</p>
<p>THUD.</p>
<p>Veylor slumped into the dirt, his body twitching involuntarily, his eyes staring blankly at the sky, seeing nothing but the shadow of the monster looming above him.</p>
<p>The air in the plaza didn’t just smell of dust anymore; it smelled of copper, of iron, of the raw, primal scent of a slaughterhouse. The atmosphere was thick and cloying, heavy with the mist of atomized blood.</p>
<p>Rex was no longer just fighting; he was dismantling. He was a sculptor of agony, and Veylor was his clay.</p>
<p>"You want to be a hero, Veylor?" Rex’s voice was a dark, rhythmic chant, punctuated by the wet sounds of violence. "Then let’s see how much a hero can lose before he stops being a man!"</p>
<p>CRACK THOOM!</p>
<p>Rex grabbed Veylor’s left arm, his fingers digging into the bicep until the muscle fibers groaned under the pressure. With a sudden, violent twist of his hips, he wrenched the limb.</p>
<p>SKREEEEEEE CHRRRRK!</p>
<p>The sound of the humerus snapping was like a dry branch breaking in a silent forest, followed by the sickening, wet tear of tendons being pulled from their sockets.</p>
<p>"AAAAAAAAAAAAAGGHHHHHHHH!"</p>
<p>Veylor’s scream was a jagged, soul-tearing thing. It wasn’t a warrior’s cry; it was the high-pitched, primal shriek of a wounded animal.</p>
<p>Blood erupted from the wound, spraying in a rhythmic pulse with every frantic beat of his dying heart.</p>
<p>SPLAT! SPURT!</p>
<p>"Look, Iris! Look at his arm!" Rex laughed, holding the limp, mangled limb up like a trophy. "He can’t even hold his sword!"</p>
<p>"He can’t even hold his own dignity!"</p>
<p>Iris was hyperventilating, her hands pressed against her mouth to stifle the sounds of her own retching. "STOP! STOP IT! TREMORRRR, YOU MONSTER!"</p>
<p>"TAKE ME INSTEAD! PLEASE!"</p>
<p>But Rex was in a trance of cruelty. He dropped the arm and drove a heavy, gravity-laden knee directly into Veylor’s chest.</p>
<p>KRA KAAAA BOOOOM!</p>
<p>The impact was so violent that Veylor’s ribcage didn’t just break; it collapsed inward, the jagged bone shards piercing his lungs and heart.</p>
<p>SQUELCH!</p>
<p>Veylor coughed, and instead of breath, a fountain of dark, clotted gore erupted from his mouth.</p>
<p>GURGLE... SPLASH!</p>
<p>He was drowning in his own life force.</p>
<p>"Still breathing? Let’s fix that!" Rex growled. He reached down and gripped Veylor’s jaw and the top of his skull.</p>
<p>With a terrifying, slow-motion heave, he began to pull.</p>
<p>CREEEEEEEAK... RRRRRIP!</p>
<p>The skin of Veylor’s face began to tear away from the bone, the subcutaneous fat and muscle glistening in the sunlight.</p>
<p>SHLICK!</p>
<p>"GYAAAAAAAHHHHHHHH! HEEEEEELP! IRIS! HEEEEEEEELP!"</p>
