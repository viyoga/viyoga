<p>The first light of dawn came through the window and painted the room in soft gray and pale gold. Rex lay on his back and looked at the ceiling, feeling all satisfied because he finally cleared one of his goals.</p>
<p>His breathing had finally calmed down after the night of excitement. Lily was curled up next to him, and her hair was a messy cascade across the pillow.</p>
<p>She was completely at ease and peaceful in her sleep, which only happens when you’re totally worn out and trust someone.</p>
<p>Rex held her cheek and then smirked, "She really is beautiful when you’re looking at her this close... and now she’s all mine."</p>
<p>"There’s not going to be a single man getting close to her other than me, but still... I needed more girls to fuck." Rex said while trying not to laugh. "Oh well..."</p>
<p>Rex could still feel the echo of her love, how she had given herself to him completely without thinking twice or feeling bad about it.</p>
<p>It was exactly what he needed, and the system didn’t take long to confirm it.</p>
<p>[ACHIEVEMENT COMPLETE: LILY VON STARLIGHT MAXIMUM DEVOTION]</p>
<p>[MAXIMUM DESIRE BONUS CLAIMED: 1000 DESIRE ENERGY]</p>
<p>[TOTAL ENERGY AVAILABLE: 1650 POINTS]</p>
<p>The notification came up right after it was over, and Rex felt a rush of happiness that had nothing to do with the act itself.</p>
<p>Rex had earned a thousand points, indicating that he had sufficient energy to activate his next powerful ability, in addition to the ones he already possessed.</p>
<p>"Fuck yeah! Now that is what I’ve been waiting for." Rex clenched his right fist. "It came just in time for the perfect plan of slowly destroying this town!"</p>
<p>He turned his attention inward and easily pulled up the system interface.</p>
<p>"System, let’s fucking do it now! Unlock Full Potential Foresight." He said while snapping his fingers.</p>
<p>[ABILITY PURCHASE CONFIRMED]</p>
<p>[FORESIGHT (FULL POTENTIAL) - 800 POINTS]</p>
<p>[REMAINING ENERGY: 850 POINTS]</p>
<p>[ABILITY DETAILS: FORESIGHT ALLOWS USER TO PERCEIVE POTENTIAL FUTURES UP TO 60 SECONDS AHEAD.]</p>
<p>[CLARITY INCREASES WITH FOCUS.]</p>
<p>[MULTIPLE TIMELINE BRANCHES VISIBLE SIMULTANEOUSLY.]</p>
<p>[COMBAT APPLICATIONS: PREDICT ENEMY MOVEMENTS, AVOID FATAL STRIKES, IDENTIFY OPTIMAL ATTACK WINDOWS.]</p>
<p>Rex felt a change as soon as the ability became part of his mind. Initially, the ability felt like a faint whisper on the periphery of his consciousness, but upon focusing, the world appeared to fragment into scattered images.</p>
<p>He could see himself lying in bed, but he could also see himself sitting up in three seconds or turning to check on Lily in five.</p>
<p>The timelines split and came back together, showing him what would happen right away if he did each of the things he was thinking about.</p>
<p>Rex thought, "This is fucking crazy," as he pushed the limits.</p>
<p>He saw his hand move before he actually moved it. The prediction was spot on, even to the tiniest twitch of his fingers.</p>
<p>"If I’m able to utilize this, then I’m sure that, in the near future... I could just fight my way in against everyone until I get everything I want!"</p>
<p>With Foresight on, fighting would be almost too easy. He could see attacks coming before they even started, dodge blows that weren’t even thrown yet, and get ready for counterattacks with superhuman accuracy.</p>
<p>Along with his telekinesis, he was getting to be very dangerous.</p>
<p>"System, recommend me the next ability I should get to be even more overpowered." Rex asked in his head.</p>
<p>[NEXT RECOMMENDED ABILITY: INFINITE REGENERATION (FULL POTENTIAL)]</p>
<p>[COST: 3000 DESIRE ENERGY]</p>
<p>[ABILITY DETAILS: COMPLETE CELLULAR REGENERATION. FATAL WOUNDS HEAL WITHIN SECONDS. LIMB REGROWTH POSSIBLE. IMMUNITY TO POISON, DISEASE, AND AGING EFFECTS. STAMINA RECOVERY INSTANTANEOUS.]</p>
<p>Rex felt his eyebrows raise when he saw that the cost was three thousand points. That was more than three times what Foresight had cost and almost four times what he had right now.</p>
<p>He would need to have a lot of maximum devotion targets or have a lot of sessions with the ones he already has to get that much, but still he can utilize the multiplier that comes from a threesome or maybe soon... a foursome.</p>
<p>"Noted," he muttered. "But that’s a problem for future Rex."</p>
<p>"But still... it’s fucking expensive, but I ain’t going to complain because getting desire energy is easy, and soon I’ll need to be more creative to make a huge amount of desire energy every single day."</p>
<p>At least he had foresight for now, and that was all he needed. He allowed the ability to relax into a passive state, which kept him aware of what was going to happen in the next few seconds without overwhelming his senses.</p>
<p>It was like having a sixth sense, a quiet warning system that would let him know when trouble was coming before it happened.</p>
<p>He turned his head to look at Lily, who was still sleeping soundly next to him. She looked younger when she was sleeping, weak, and completely at peace.</p>
<p>A small part of him, the part that wasn’t completely focused on strategy, felt something like affection. She had never doubted his motives and had given him everything she had.</p>
<p>Rex thought, "You really are too good for this world," as he reached out to brush a strand of hair from her face. "And that’s why you’re perfect for me."</p>
<p>He lay back down on the pillow and closed his eyes for a moment. The Foresight ability hummed at the edge of his mind, showing him bits and pieces of the near future.</p>
<p>For the next minute or so, nothing really interesting will happen. It was a quiet morning, and Lily was still sleeping as the sun rose higher.</p>
<p>Then the vision changed.</p>
<p>Rex saw flames sixty seconds ahead. There was also many screaming came from villagers and the sound of wood breaking.</p>
<p>He quickly opened his eyes and focused harder on the Foresight, pushing it to show him more details.</p>
<p>The ambush by the bandits. It was happening now, just as he had told Kruger to do it three days ago.</p>
<p>"Ahh yes... Perfect fucking timing," Rex thought with a grim sense of satisfaction. "Right when everyone is still waking up and confused."</p>
<p>He sat up slowly so he wouldn’t wake Lily up. There was no reason to scare her before it was necessary.</p>
<p>He quickly put on his clothes, moving with perfect accuracy because the Foresight showed him exactly where each piece of clothing was before he reached for it.</p>
<p>And then...</p>
<p>BAAAMMMMM!</p>
<p>The first explosion shook the village as he was finishing getting ready.</p>
<p>The sound was like thunder, a deep boom that shook the inn’s walls and sent dust flying from the ceiling beams. Lily woke up right away, her eyes wide with fear and confusion.</p>
<p>"WHAT WAS THAT...?!" she gasped as she held the blanket to her chest.</p>
<p>"Stay here," Rex said in a calm but firm voice. "After I leave, lock the door. Only let me or your family in, okay?"</p>
<p>"R-Rex... what’s going on?" Lily looked concerned when she asked that.</p>
<p>But then a second explosion, this time closer. The window shook in its frame, and a woman screamed outside.</p>
<p>BAAAMMMMM!</p>
<p>"Bandits," Rex said simply. "They’re attacking the village."</p>
<p>Lily’s face turned white. "We have to help! We have to—"</p>
<p>"You need to stay safe," Rex said as he walked to the window. "Your family will take care of the main force."</p>
<p>"I’ll make sure no one gets to the inn."</p>
<p>"And... can you even walk, though?" Rex asked with a smile on his face.</p>
<p>"I..." Lily felt a wave of embarrassment as she realized Rex was right.</p>
<p>It was because of yesterday’s intense session; she felt sore all over her body, and even her legs refused to respond to any command.</p>
<p>He didn’t give her a chance to argue. He could see the next few seconds clearly because of his foresight.</p>
<p>But still... Lily would try to get out of bed, realize she was still naked, and then rush to find her nightgown. That would give him enough time to get out and look around.</p>
<p>"Don’t try anything reckless when I’m out." He gave another clear answer to her.</p>
<p>Rex opened the window and climbed out onto the narrow ledge. Then, with his telekinesis, he pulled himself up onto the roof.</p>
<p>As soon as he got past the edge, he could see the whole attack.</p>
<p>There was a lot of chaos happening in the village. Three different places had fires, and black smoke was rising into the sky in the morning.</p>
<p>There were many bandits in the streets, at least thirty or forty of them, all of them armed with swords, axes, and other crude weapons.</p>
<p>They were attacking without thinking, breaking windows, kicking down doors, and killing anyone who tried to fight back.</p>
<p>Rex’s improved vision found Kruger near the middle of the raid. The huge bandit leader shouted orders and swung a war hammer that smashed a merchant’s stall with one blow.</p>
<p>His men were attacking in a coordinated way, which showed that they were following a plan and not just stealing things at random.</p>
<p>Rex thought, "Good."</p>
<p>"They’re doing exactly what I told them to do..."</p>
