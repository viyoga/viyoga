<p>Rex began to laugh again. At first, it was a low, private sound, a rumbling chuckle that started deep in his chest, making his shoulders shake beneath his blood-stained armor.</p>

<p>But then the sound swelled, growing louder and more unrestrained, until it rang through the broken streets of Aethelgard like a death knell.</p>

<p>"HA HA HA HA HA HA!"</p>

<p>The laughter echoed off the shattered facades of the buildings, bouncing through the empty alleys and piercing through the smoke.</p>

<p>For Cassandra, watching from the periphery, the sound made her skin crawl; it felt like something oily and wrong sliding over her nerves.</p>

<p>It was the laugh of a man who had just watched his opponent play their final card, only to realize the game had never been fair to begin with. He wasn’t just winning; he was enjoying the futility of her resistance.</p>

<p>Wiping a speck of blood and mirth from his eye, Rex straightened his posture. His expression shifted from sadistic amusement to cold, mechanical focus.</p>

<p>With a casual flick of his wrist, he drew the system interface open in front of him. The holographic text glowed with an eerie violet light, casting sharp shadows across his face.</p>

<p>[Free Skill Selection available: 1]</p>

<p>[Select any skill from the full catalog (no cost, no prerequisites).]</p>

<p>Rex began to scroll. His fingers danced through the air, swiping through centuries of accumulated power, forbidden arts, and cosmic abilities.</p>

<p>He hummed to himself a soft, tuneless melody like a man browsing a dinner menu while the world held its breath.</p>

<p>The silence of the city was absolute, broken only by the wet, ragged breathing of Evelyn in her crater and the distant crackle of burning timber.</p>

<p>He passed over the entries Lustia had warned him about the terrifying, abstract conceptual masteries that could unravel reality itself.</p>

<p>He glanced at them with interest but kept moving. He wasn’t looking for raw destruction; he had plenty of that.</p>

<p>He was looking for something more... surgical.</p>

<p>Something to seal the cracks Evelyn had so desperately tried to pry open.</p>

<p>As he reached the bottom of the catalog, the interface flickered. A new section had manifested, pulsing with a deep, bruised purple light.</p>

<p>The system had recognized his evolution: the moment he had copied [Wrath], he had transcended the singular nature of his original sin.</p>

<p>He was no longer just a vessel of desire; he was a convergence of conflicting, primal hungers.</p>

<p>The system had noted that a bearer of two Sin Authorities now qualified for an upper extension of his primary power.</p>

<p>Rex didn’t hesitate. He didn’t read the description.</p>

<p>He didn’t need to. He could feel the pull of it in his own blood, a siren song that promised to turn the city’s newfound clarity into something even more dangerous.</p>

<p>His finger tapped the glowing text with finality.</p>

<p>[Idolatry Dominion (Mark of Lust, Extension Tier): ACQUIRED]</p>

<p>[Effect: Converts desire bond resonance into worship.]</p>

<p>[Targets within the radius of influence perceive the user as a divine protector.]</p>

<p>[Applies to all soul registers in range, with strength scaled to closeness of resonance.]</p>

<p>[The user may exclude chosen targets.]</p>

<p>A sudden, violent pulse of violet energy erupted from Rex’s body. It wasn’t a shockwave of force but a wave of something far more insidious: a psychic fragrance, a spiritual perfume that swept through the plaza like a poisoned mist.</p>

<p>The [Idolatry Dominion] did not assault the mind like magic; it rewrote the reality beneath their feet.</p>

<p>It worked in a way that the [Open Court] was fundamentally incapable of touching.</p>

<p>There was no sorcery in its composition for Evelyn to detect, no divine channel for the Goddess of Justice to grasp and sever.</p>

<p>It was not a spell cast upon the world; it was an infection of the world itself. Because Rex’s [Earthen Divine Authority] had been woven into the very bedrock of Aethelgard for weeks, the power had a perfect, pre existing highway.</p>

<p>It rode through the northern corridor that connected the island to the Underlayer, flowing along the veins of power he had meticulously designed.</p>

<p>THUMMM THUMMM.</p>

<p>The tremor began not in the air, but in the marrow. It ran up from the ancient stone into the bones of every living thing, from the bones into the nerves, and from the nerves directly into the primal part of the human psyche, the part that knows how to kneel.</p>

<p>Because this power originated from a Forbidden God, it existed entirely outside the covenant of the divine. The Goddess of Justice had no jurisdiction here; she was a queen whose laws stopped at the edge of a black hole.</p>

<p>The massive, golden circle of Evelyn’s [Open Court], the last bastion of divine truth, began to spiderweb with cracks.</p>

<p>CRACKLE SHATTER.</p>

<p>Without a single blow being struck, the holy barrier disintegrated like glass struck by a sound frequency it couldn’t hear. The golden light died, swallowed by a creeping, violet shadow rising from the cracks in the pavement.</p>

<p>Rex stood in the center of the devastation, looking less like a man and more like a monument to something ancient and hungry. He lifted one hand toward the darkening sky, and the sigil burned upon his wrist with the intensity of a dying star.</p>

<p>"Kneel," he commanded.</p>

<p>It wasn’t a shout; it was an inevitability.</p>

<p>The light did not spread through the air like fire.</p>

<p>Instead, it sank into the ground, plunging into the fissures of the plaza. It ran through the cracks like arterial blood through veins.</p>

<p>It raced beneath the marble floors of the Grand Academy, surging through the foundations like a subterranean tide.</p>

<p>It flowed beneath the Silver Rest Inn in the southern district, threading through the ruins of the Starlight household, and coiled around the barracks of the Underlayer troops perched on the edge of the cliffs.</p>

<p>VROOOOOOOM.</p>

<p>The energy pulsed once, a single, violet beat that synchronized with the rhythm of a vast, cosmic heart.</p>

<p>Every living soul on the island felt their ribcage shake with the impact. It was a physical pressure that demanded submission, a spiritual weight that made breathing feel like an act of defiance.</p>

<p>In the plaza, Evelyn watched in horror as the violet veins reached her crater, the light licking at her broken form like a lover’s touch.</p>

<p>And then, they changed.</p>

<p>The terror that had sparked in their hearts moments ago—the righteous anger, the dawning clarity, and the grief for their lost loved ones—did not vanish.</p>

<p>Instead, it twisted.</p>

<p>The anger became a desperate need to be forgiven.</p>

<p>The grief became a need to be comforted by the only hand strong enough to hold them.</p>

<p>The clarity became a terrifying realization: that they had been wrong and that the monster was the only thing that could make sense of the chaos.</p>

<p>The resistance died.</p>

<p>The truth became unbearable.</p>

<p>And in its place, something far more dangerous took root.</p>
