<p>Rex then came back to his senses to focus on the demon, and he would use this opportunity to at least bond with a demon. This fantasy world has many things, and it would be a shame for him not to use all of them.</p>
<p>"Thank you..." Lilith said softly and with respect. "Thank you...! Thank you...! Thank yooooouuuu!!!"</p>
<p>"Y-You saved my life... I thought I was going to die, and you came to me like a guardian angel, but not an angel because angels are terrible assholes."</p>
<p>"You came to me like a dark savior who protects the unworthy, and I owe you everything that I have right now!" The demon bowed down. "I owe you my life, my service, and whatever else you want because you saved me when no one else would."</p>
<p>"My loyalty towards the demon king has been replaced by the one and true dark savior..." She tried to lick his boots, but then he dodged it fast.</p>
<p>"Stop it with that." Rex looked disgusted, but the kind of disgust he loves when seeing a woman act lowly toward him.</p>
<p>"You owe me a name for now," Rex said, cutting him off and keeping his voice low and firm. "Spill it."</p>
<p>The mask changed it a little, adding an inhuman tone that made it clear he wasn’t human, even though he looked like a person.</p>
<p>"Lilith!" She said right away, "Lilith Nocturne!"</p>
<p>"I’m Lord Mordecai Vrael’s personal maid, but I guess that doesn’t mean much if I’m dead and alone."</p>
<p>"But now! You saved me, so I’m alive and can still be his personal maid... but still, I owe you everything, so maybe I should be your personal maid instead." Lilith said with her heart-shaped tail moving freely.</p>
<p>"I can be your good maid! How’s that?! Do you want one?!" Lilith keeps getting closer to him. "I’m very good at maid duties! I can clean, organize, gather information, serve tea, and even have sex—"</p>
<p>Rex cut through the nervous chatter and said, "You serve Mordecai, right?"</p>
<p>"Ah~! Yes, I am!" Lilith nodded quickly. "I’ve been his personal assistant for three years."</p>
<p>"I take care of his home, plan his schedule, handle his mail, gather information from his various businesses, and make sure his estate runs smoothly."</p>
<p>"But... he’s odd. Even though he has everything, including all the monster women, he doesn’t want to give them all the pleasure they need... and I needed." Lilith said with a sad look.</p>
<p>"Great to hear that, then..."</p>
<p>It’s better than Rex had hoped. Not only was she connected to Mordecai, but she was also very involved in his personal business.</p>
<p>And the fact that she just said something personal made Rex think that Mordecai was a reincarnator that became a demon lord, and he must be a wimp to not fully take advantage of his privileges.</p>
<p>Rex reached out and took her hand, but she shook her body with her tail raised, feeling very tense.</p>
<p>"Ahh~!" She wrapped her smaller fingers around his right away.</p>
<p>Rex said. "You’re going to take me to him right now. I have some personal business with him."</p>
<p>Lilith nodded. "My pleasure—"</p>
<p>He used his teleportation power again, and Lilith came with him. He had slightly overreached himself with Lustia’s explanation.</p>
<p>He couldn’t handle many people, but one person was fine. The spell structure changed to fit two bodies instead of one, and then they were walking through space together.</p>
<p>They were on a rooftop several blocks away from the eastern wall, far enough away from the main battle that they wouldn’t be seen right away but still close enough to see what was going on.</p>
<p>"I say again... take me to Mordecai," Rex said again. "Right now."</p>
<p>Lilith’s eyes were wide with what looked like a mix of fear, awe, and excitement. "Yes, of course, I can!"</p>
<p>"I have teleportation magic that is much stronger than what you just did and can cross hundreds of miles in an instant, but... Lord Mordecai isn’t alone..."</p>
<p>"He’s with his full inner circle at the staging ground twenty miles north of here, and they were coordinating the attack and waiting for news on how it went."</p>
<p>"Then we go there," Rex said. "Now."</p>
<p>"But you need to understand," Lilith said, her voice getting more serious even though she was clearly in love. "Lord Mordecai has six other personal attendants besides me."</p>
<p>"We all worked together, and if I show up alone with a stranger, especially after the attack failed, they’ll think I’ve been compromised or turned traitor."</p>
<p>"I need to get back together with the others first so we can present you properly, or at least explain the situation before you meet him."</p>
<p>Rex’s foresight saw the branches. If he pushed the issue and went straight to Mordecai now, there would be a fight right away.</p>
<p>There is a chance of winning, but there are also problems that could stop his plan to get in. If he listened to Lilith and met with the other attendants first, he would have to wait, but he would have a much better chance of making himself look like someone Mordecai would want to hire instead of kill.</p>
<p>"Okay then," Rex said. "First, take me to the others."</p>
<p>Rex whispered. "But if this is a trap, you’ll die before your plan can work."</p>
<p>"And I’ll use your body first before I murder you."</p>
<p>Lilith’s body tensed. "T-that sounded hot..."</p>
<p>"Ehem! It’s not a trap, I promise!" Lilith said immediately. "I owe you my life, remember?!"</p>
<p>"I would never betray someone who saved me!" Lilith shook her head. "Demons don’t work that way."</p>
<p>"When we bond, we bond completely! That means... you’re mine now, I mean, I’m yours, I mean, we’re connected, and I would rather die than see you hurt."</p>
<p>Rex noticed that Lilith was already falling for him, which was a good sign because he would soon be able to experience a relationship with a demon after completing his initial plan as the Lustful Villain.</p>
<p>Her eyes were full of real emotion, and Rex could see that she was seventy percent committed to him. It was going exactly as Lustia had said it would.</p>
<p>Demons were loyal to power and control in a way that humans couldn’t be because they didn’t have the same moral issues getting in the way.</p>
<p>"Then let’s not waste any more time and move," Rex said.</p>
<p>Lilith nodded and started to cast a teleportation spell that was much more complicated than Rex’s simple one. With his new energy manipulation sense, he could see the energy patterns forming: layers upon layers of spatial folding and anchoring that would let him travel long distances with great accuracy.</p>
<p>"Hold on to me," she said, and Rex held on tighter.</p>
<p>This time, the world folded in a more planned and controlled way. This was more like being pulled through a tunnel that made distance into a single moment instead of the instant step through space that his own teleportation gave him.</p>
<p>As they emerged, magical lights illuminated a clearing in the woods. There were six other demons around them, all female and wearing different kinds of maid uniforms.</p>
<p>They all turned to look at Lilith and Rex with different looks of surprise.</p>
<p>One of them screamed, "LILITH!" with a tone of relief in her voice.</p>
<p>"We thought you were dead or captured!"</p>
<p>"I almost was," Lilith said as she pulled Rex closer. "But then this charming dark prince saved me."</p>
<p>"This is my savior, my protector, my dark guardian who came out of nowhere and killed the Apostle of Justice, who was hunting me like it was nothing."</p>
<p>"He really killed him without a trace, and it’s amazinngggggg~~~!!!"</p>
<p>The other maids were all staring at Rex now, taking in his masked face and the powerful aura that surrounded him.</p>
<p>"So...? Can I meet with Mordecai and ask some personal things of him?" Rex asked while crossing both his arms.</p>
<p>...</p>
<p>Somewhere, not too far away.</p>
<p>Rex didn’t know, because he couldn’t see it from where he was standing in the clearing, that a group of four reincarnators had seen the teleportation arrival about half a mile away through the trees.</p>
<p>They had been following Lilith since the fight, hoping to find out where Mordecai was. They were now observing as Lilith brought a masked figure, who was clearly a demon, to a location that unmistakably served as a demonic gathering place.</p>
<p>The leader of the group, a woman with black hair and cold eyes, signaled to her companions.</p>
<p>They would follow at a distance. They would observe.</p>
<p>And if the opportunity presented itself, they would strike.</p>
<p>The hunt was on.</p>
