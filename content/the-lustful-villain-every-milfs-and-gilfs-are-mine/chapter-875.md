<p>Rex stared at her, his eyes distant, as if he were already standing in the center of a divine storm, calculating the wind resistance of his own soul. He wasn’t arguing with Lustia anymore; he was rehearsing a ritual of slaughter.</p>
<p>"I already have Infinite Regeneration," he said again, but the words were quieter now, stripped of defiance and replaced with the cold, mechanical precision of a general reviewing battle plans.</p>
<p>"I’m not questioning you... I’m stacking."</p>
<p>"Full Potential Adaptation layered over Infinite Regeneration gives me a body that a temporal god cannot damage through anything he has wielded twice."</p>
<p>"The first time he strikes, I bleed, but then... the second time he strikes, he finds I am no longer made of the same substance he intends to break."</p>
<p>His fingers flexed unconsciously, imagining the grip on a weapon. "Dimensional manipulation provides the breach; it gets me through the door and into his sanctum."</p>
<p>"And Presence Manipulation..." A dark, predatory hunger flickered in his gaze. "Presence manipulation keeps him blind."</p>
<p>"It keeps him arrogant and ensures he doesn’t even realize the predator has entered his lair until my blade is already halfway through his throat."</p>
<p>"That is the correct architecture," Lustia said, her voice carrying a rare note of genuine surprise. "You have constructed a fortress of survival in real time, Rex."</p>
<p>"You built it faster than I anticipated all within the span of a single conversation."</p>
<p>"I’ve had plenty of practice building architecture around things that want me dead," Rex countered, a bitter smile ghosting across his lips. H</p>
<p>His inner monologue was a relentless engine of war: ’Build the shield, sharpen the sword, find the throat.’</p>
<p>’Repeat until the god falls.’</p>
<p>"This isn’t a new discipline... It’s just a new opponent." Rex grinned. "The math changes, but the cruelty remains the same."</p>
<p>Lustia studied him for a long moment. The air between them grew heavy, charged with the static of two powerful entities recognizing a monster in the making.</p>
<p>There was something in her expression, a quiet, unsettling respect. She wasn’t looking at a mortal struggling against fate; she was watching a predator assemble a trap that could catch a star.</p>
<p>Rex closed his eyes for a second, visualizing the dominoes.</p>
<p>"The sequence is clear," he said, his voice dropping to a lethal register. "Presence manipulation first..."</p>
<p>"I need to disappear before I become a target."</p>
<p>"Then Iris and Aurelia... I need the anchors, the life force required to fuel the next two unlocks."</p>
<p>"And then, once the foundation is set, I claim Dimensional Manipulation and Full Potential Adaptation..." Rex punches his own fist. "After that... I’ll fucking hunt my prey."</p>
<p>"That is exactly the sequence I would recommend," Lustia said, nodding approvingly. "The presence manipulation serves a dual purpose."</p>
<p>"It also addresses a critical operational gap you have been carrying since the Sable Island integration."</p>
<p>Rex’s eyes snapped open, sharp as flint. "Which gap?"</p>
<p>"Your Earthen Authority’s geological anchor," Lustia explained, her tone turning clinical. "It is a paradox..."</p>
<p>"It is one of your most significant power sources, but it is also a screaming beacon."</p>
<p>"To anyone with sufficient divine perceptive capability, that anchor reads as a divine class signature, a flare in the darkness that is completely inconsistent with your chosen cover identities."</p>
<p>"You are trying to walk through a den of wolves while carrying a torch that announces your name."</p>
<p>Rex’s jaw tightened. He had felt the subtle, nagging sensation of being watched, of his power feeling too ’loud’ against the fabric of the world.</p>
<p>"Presence Manipulation allows you to mask that signature," Lustia continued. "You can make it read as something mundane, something harmless, or you can make it vanish entirely, depending on what the situation demands."</p>
<p>"It turns your greatest vulnerability into a ghost."</p>
<p>Rex didn’t respond. His gaze drifted downward, falling on the Mark etched into his skin.</p>
<p>It pulsed with a faint, ominous light, a reminder that every power he gained came with a price in flesh, in blood, and in the lives of those he would claim to fuel his ascension. The war had begun, and he was already measuring the distance to the first kill.</p>
<p>"The system notification," Rex said, his voice cutting through the charged silence like a blade. "The free skill selection... It’s still pending."</p>
<p>"Take it now," Lustia commanded, her eyes fixed on him with urgent intensity. "Don’t hesitate."</p>
<p>"The sooner presence manipulation is woven into your fundamental code, the sooner that screaming beacon of yours becomes a whisper."</p>
<p>"Every second you delay is another second you’re exposed."</p>
<p>Rex didn’t need to be told twice. With a sharp mental flick, he tore open the system’s skill catalog, the ethereal interface bleeding into his field of vision.</p>
<p>[PRESENCE MANIPULATION FULL POTENTIAL]</p>
<p>[COMPLETE CONTROL OVER THE SPECIFIC SIGNATURE THAT YOUR EXISTENCE PRODUCES ACROSS ALL DETECTION REGISTERS SIMULTANEOUSLY.]</p>
<p>[REGISTERS COVERED: PHYSICAL MASS AND HEAT SIGNATURE, ENERGY AND SYSTEM SIGNATURE, DIVINE ANCHOR SIGNATURE, TEMPORAL PRESENCE SIGNATURE, AND SOUL LAYER SIGNATURE.]</p>
<p>[ADJUSTABLE FROM FULL AMPLIFICATION (SIGNATURE READS AS SIGNIFICANTLY LARGER THAN ACTUAL CAPABILITY) TO COMPLETE ABSENCE (NO SIGNATURE IN ANY REGISTER).]</p>
<p>[INTERMEDIATE SETTINGS: CAN MIMIC SPECIFIC SIGNATURES OF KNOWN ENTITIES OR PRODUCE ENTIRELY FABRICATED SIGNATURES.]</p>
<p>[PASSIVE FUNCTION: CONSTANT LOW-LEVEL SIGNATURE MANAGEMENT THAT PREVENTS CASUAL DETECTION OF PRIMARY IDENTITIES ACROSS COVER OPERATIONS.]</p>
<p>[ACTIVE FUNCTION: DELIBERATE SIGNATURE MANIPULATION FOR SPECIFIC TACTICAL APPLICATIONS.]</p>
<p>[COMBINATION WITH SHAPESHIFTING: PRODUCES COMPLETE IDENTITY REPLACEMENT AT BOTH PHYSICAL AND SIGNATURE LEVEL.]</p>
<p>[COMBINATION WITH ENERGY MANIPULATION: EXTENDS SIGNATURE MANAGEMENT TO OBJECTS AND CONSTRUCTS IN CONTACT RANGE.]</p>
<p>[DIVINE APPLICATION: AT FULL MASTERY, SIGNATURE CAN BE COMPRESSED TO MATCH A MORTAL BASELINE, PREVENTING DIVINE PERCEPTIVE FUNCTIONS FROM IDENTIFYING THE PRESENCE AS A DIVINE CLASS ENTITY.]</p>
<p>[FREE SELECTION APPLIED AT NO COST.]</p>
<p>Rex didn’t pause to marvel at the sheer, terrifying scope of the power. He simply locked onto the selection and slammed it home. Execute.</p>
<p>The integration was unlike anything he had experienced before. When he had acquired his other skills, it felt like adding new rooms to a house, a physical sensation of expansion, of weight, of new muscle fiber knitting into his existing frame.</p>
<p>But presence manipulation felt like a violation of a different kind. It felt like a subtraction.</p>
<p>It was as if he had been standing naked in a spotlight his entire life, unaware of the searing heat on his skin, and suddenly, the light began to dim. He felt the raw, pulsing energy of his Earthen authority, the geological weight that made him feel like a mountain beginning to retract, folding inward.</p>
<p>The Mark of Lust, which had always throbbed with a frequency that felt like a heartbeat in the astral plane, began to muffle, its divine resonance dampened. The accumulated ’noise’ of his other full potential skills, the dimensional static, and the temporal echoes all began to collapse.</p>
<p>He sat there in the heart of Lustia’s domain, eyes closed, grimacing as his very essence underwent a violent recalibration. Inside his mind, it was a chaotic storm of data: ’Compress. Muffle. Suppress. Erase.’</p>
<p>He could feel the system forcibly grinding down his divine profile, crushing the jagged peaks of his power into something smooth, something manageable, something quiet.</p>
<p>Finally, the pressure receded. He opened his eyes.</p>
<p>He felt smaller. Not weaker, far from it, but he felt less obvious.</p>
<p>His Earthen Authority was still there, coiled like a sleeping dragon beneath his skin, but the ’smell’ of it was gone. His divine frequency had been compressed into a profile that would now read, to any casual divine observer, as nothing more than an above-average reincarnator with a decent skill set.</p>
<p>He was no longer a flare in the dark; he was a shadow among shadows. Not unremarkable, but precisely calibrated to avoid triggering a god’s defensive paranoia.</p>
<p>"Good," Lustia said, her voice carrying a note of satisfaction as she watched the residual energy dissipate from his body.</p>
<p>"How do I look?" Rex asked. His voice was rough, and there was something almost dry in it—the closest he had come to humor since he had woken up bleeding on her floor.</p>
<p>"Quieter," Lustia replied. "Which, coming from me, is the highest compliment I can offer."</p>
<p>"I have spent centuries listening to apostles broadcast their existence across three different registers like desperate children screaming for attention."</p>
<p>"You have never once wanted to be noticed by the wrong audience..."</p>
<p>"This skill simply allows your instincts to finally catch up to your ambitions."</p>
<p>Rex’s mind immediately pivoted back to the mission. The hunger for the kill was still there, but now it was tempered by a cold, professional patience.</p>
<p>"The Iris and Aurelia timeline," he said, his voice hardening. "How soon can we move?"</p>
<p>"As fast as you can manage without compromising the operation’s other requirements," Lustia said, her gaze turning serious. "The foundation must be flawless, Rex."</p>
<p>"If you rush the fuel, the engine will shatter before you ever reach God’s doorstep."</p>
