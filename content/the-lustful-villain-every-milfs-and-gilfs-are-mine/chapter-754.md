<p>ZZZT CRACKLE!</p>
<p>The energy raced through the ground, a web of white-hot mana following the very cracks Zane had feared.</p>
<p>"You told Celestina everything," Apollo said, his voice turning cold, the combat turning into a psychological interrogation. "You told her about the Apostle network."</p>
<p>"You told her about the Reincarnator population on the island."</p>
<p>"You gave her the blueprint of the Academy’s internal structure."</p>
<p>"I gave her the truth," Zane said, his Void working frantically, scanning the ground, trying to predict where the next surge of energy would erupt from the cracks.</p>
<p>"Did you tell her about Lady Valentina?" Apollo asked, his voice dropping to a lethal, quiet whisper.</p>
<p>The question hit Zane harder than any physical blow could have.</p>
<p>For a fraction of a second, the absolute, terrifying precision of his Void working faltered. His displacement didn’t just stutter; it glitched.</p>
<p>The spatial fabric around him tore unevenly, leaving him caught in a momentary, nauseating lurch of half existence.</p>
<p>VWOOM SCHLICK!</p>
<p>Apollo didn’t miss the opening. He surged through the spatial gap before Zane could even stabilize his form.</p>
<p>He didn’t go for a killing blow—not yet. He didn’t need full output; he needed the leverage of proximity.</p>
<p>He drove into Zane’s personal space, forcing the Void’s minimum range to become a claustrophobic, suffocating reality.</p>
<p>"You didn’t tell her about Lady Valentina," Apollo hissed, his voice a jagged edge of accusation. He pressed the advantage, his body a wall of kinetic intent. "You had fourteen months of observation."</p>
<p>"Fourteen months of meticulous, surgical reporting. And in all that time, you never once mentioned her name."</p>
<p>Zane’s eyes flared.</p>
<p>SHHH BOOM!</p>
<p>In a desperate bid to regain breathing room, Zane forced a Void pulse outward. It wasn’t a focused strike but a violent, spherical expansion of repelling force, a desperate, omnidirectional burst of pure spatial pressure.</p>
<p>WHUMP!</p>
<p>The pulse slammed into Apollo, forcing him back three heavy, sliding steps. Apollo felt the edge of the void wave wash over him, the sheer pressure tugging at his skin like a physical weight, but his life affinity passive field absorbed the brunt of the shock.</p>
<p>He skidded to a halt, his boots carving shallow grooves in the stone, his eyes never leaving Zane’s.</p>
<p>"She was not a Reincarnator," Zane countered, his voice regaining its cold, analytical steel, though a bead of sweat rolled down his temple, carving a path through the dust on his face.</p>
<p>"Neither were most of the students in the academy!" Apollo roared, the frustration finally boiling over. "You mapped the Academy’s entire hierarchy!"</p>
<p>"You detailed the dormitories, the training halls, and the faculty rosters!"</p>
<p>"You included the very bones of the institution in your reports... but you left Lady Valentina in the dark!"</p>
<p>"She was not operationally relevant to the specific intelligence Celestina required!" Zane snapped back, the calm mask finally cracking to reveal the intensity beneath.</p>
<p>"She is the Headmaster of the most powerful magical institution in Aethelgard!" Apollo stepped forward again, his voice dropping to a low, vibrating growl that seemed to shake the very air between them. "She is the lynchpin of every piece of intelligence regarding this island!"</p>
<p>"To leave her out wasn’t an oversight, Zane. It was a lie of omission!"</p>
<p>"You left her out on purpose."</p>
<p>The silence that followed was deafening. Zane stood his ground, the dark aura of the Void swirling around his feet like a restless tide.</p>
<p>He looked at Apollo across the short, tense distance, his expression unreadable, his silence a heavy, damning thing.</p>
<p>"You knew exactly what the Legion would do if they had a clear target in the Academy’s leadership," Apollo continued, his eyes boring into Zane’s soul. "You made a unilateral decision."</p>
<p>"You decided what was ’relevant’ and what was ’noise,’ and you didn’t even bother to consult Celestina." Apollo gritted his teeth. "You played god with the truth."</p>
<p>"Intelligence selection is a judgment call in any high-stakes field operation!" Zane finally exploded, his voice echoing off the broken walls of the plaza. "The operative on the ground possesses a context that the principal can never truly grasp!"</p>
<p>"Selecting for relevance is not the same as concealment; it is the essence of efficiency!"</p>
<p>"Then tell me," Apollo challenged, his stance widening, his energy humming with a lethal, expectant tension. "What was your ’judgment’ regarding Lady Valentina’s relevance?"</p>
<p>Zane took a breath, his chest heaving. He spoke the next words with a chilling, clinical clarity, the way a man admits to a crime he has long since justified to himself.</p>
<p>"Including her would have triggered an operational response that was unpredictable and, more importantly, unmanageable," Zane said.</p>
<p>The words were heavy, laden with the weight of a secret kept for a year. "Celestina’s history is clear: when she identifies a high-value target in an administrative position, she doesn’t seek to influence them."</p>
<p>"She seeks to remove them. I judged that the cost of losing Valentina, the cost to the island’s stability, would far outweigh whatever tactical advantage Celestina would have gained from her death."</p>
<p>Apollo stared at him, the realization settling in like a cold blade in his gut. The fury in his eyes didn’t vanish, but it transformed into something sharper, something more profound.</p>
<p>"So you protected her," Apollo said.</p>
<p>It wasn’t a question. It was a revelation.</p>
<p>"I made a calculated judgment call regarding operational priority," Zane corrected, though the defense sounded hollow even to his own ears.</p>
<p>"You protected her," Apollo said again.</p>
<p>This time, there was no anger in his voice. There was only the terrifying, quiet weight of the truth, placed squarely between them like a corpse.</p>
<p>The air between them had become a suffocating pressure cooker of mana and raw, unadulterated violence.</p>
<p>What followed was not a dance; it was a brutal, grinding war of attrition. It was the specific, desperate kind of combat that occurs when a long-range specialist is forcibly dragged into the mud by an opponent who has realized that the specialist’s greatest strength, their distance, is their only lifeline.</p>
<p>Zane’s Void working was a tool made for the smooth, curved shapes of moving through space, needing a certain amount of distance to work properly.</p>
<p>Apollo, however, was acting as a human anvil. He was intentionally staying inside that "dead zone," refusing to give Zane the room to breathe, to shift, or to vanish.</p>
<p>He was accepting every blow, every jagged edge of spatial pressure, in exchange for denying Zane the geometry his system required to survive.</p>
<p>THWACK! CRACK!</p>
<p>Zane’s close-range combat was a frantic, ugly thing. He wasn’t a brawler, but he was a survivor.</p>
<p>He unleashed a flurry of strikes, his hands shimmering with the unstable, dark energy of the Void. One strike caught Apollo in the ribs. CRUNCH, the sound of bone yielding to spatial pressure. Another hit his shoulder, a sickening THUD that left a blooming bruise of violet energy.</p>
<p>Apollo’s restored reserves were formidable, but they were not infinite. He was running at a fraction of the power he had displayed during the twenty-minute Tremor engagement, and the toll was visible.</p>
<p>Blood began to smear across his jaw, and his breathing was a ragged, rhythmic growl. The designation’s passive restoration was working overtime, knitting muscle fibers and sealing capillaries at a frantic, almost visible rate, but it was a stalemate.</p>
<p>The damage he took was being repaired almost as quickly as Zane could inflict it. The fight wasn’t getting better for him, but it wasn’t spiraling into a defeat either. It was a grinding, bloody equilibrium.</p>
<p>VWOOM! SHHH! CRACK!</p>
<p>Zane’s void absorption was a frantic cycle, flickering between an offensive pulse to push Apollo back and a defensive shield to absorb the kinetic impact of Apollo’s relentless forward momentum.</p>
<p>"You could... disengage!" Zane spat, the words forced out through gritted teeth as he parried a heavy, mana-saturated jab from Apollo.</p>
<p>CLANG!</p>
<p>The sound of the impact vibrated through their very bones. "You have enough reserve... for a sustained withdrawal!"</p>
<p>"You don’t have enough... for a sustained engagement at this intensity!"</p>
<p>Zane’s eyes were wide, flickering with the frantic calculation of a man watching his clock run out.</p>
<p>"You have less than I do!" Apollo roared back, stepping into a punishing strike, his fist whistling through the air.</p>
<p>WHOOSH!</p>
<p>He ignored the stinging pain of a spatial pulse catching his forearm, the skin there rippling as if struck by an invisible hammer. "The Tremor fight... it gutted you more than it did me!"</p>
<p>"Your void working is sluggish, Zane! It’s cycling slower than it was when we started!"</p>
<p>Apollo lunged again, his eyes cold and terrifyingly analytical despite the blood dripping from his nose.</p>
<p>"Your absorption delay is running at approximately a third of a second," Apollo shouted over the roar of their colliding energies, "instead of a quarter! You’re losing your window!"</p>
<p>Zane’s eyes narrowed, a flash of grim respect cutting through his exhaustion. "You are... measuring my cycle time?"</p>
<p>"I am measuring everything!" Apollo countered, his voice a guttural snarl as he drove his weight forward, forcing Zane back into the broken stone.</p>
<p>SKREEEE!</p>
<p>"That is what you do when you are in a fight you cannot win quickly."</p>
<p>"You measure every breath, every drop of blood, every millisecond of delay... and you wait for the one number that finally matters!"</p>
