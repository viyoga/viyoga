<p>The air in the training hall didn’t just feel still after Aurelia’s intervention; it felt vacuum-sealed, as if the very oxygen had been sucked out by the sheer force of the humiliation that had just transpired.</p>
<p>The metallic tang of blood—Theo’s blood—hung heavy in the nostrils of every noble present, mixing with the sharp, acrid sting of spent magical energy.</p>
<p>Theo leaned into Aurelia, his body a ruined marionette of shattered cartilage and torn muscle. His ribs felt like jagged glass grinding against his lungs with every shallow gasp, and his vision was a kaleidoscopic smear of crimson and gray.</p>
<p>Inside his mind, there was no screaming. There was no weeping. There was only a terrifying, glacial quiet, the sound of a furnace cooling after a catastrophic meltdown.</p>
<p>The rage had burned itself out, leaving behind something harder, something colder, and something infinitely more dangerous than mere anger.</p>
<p>He steadied himself, forcing his broken limbs to obey. He peeled himself away from Aurelia’s supporting arm, swaying momentarily before finding his footing.</p>
<p>His face was a grotesque mask: one eye swollen into a purple slit, his nose crooked and weeping blood, and his lips split to the point of exposing raw nerves. But his eyes, those eyes were horrifying.</p>
<p>He stood and looked at Rex with the specific flat expression of someone who has processed what has just happened and is currently in the specific state of someone deciding what to do with the processing.</p>
<p>He wasn’t looking at a rival. He was looking at a variable that needed to be corrected.</p>
<p>"The position is yours," Theo said. His voice was startling devoid of the hysterical cracking from moments before.</p>
<p>It was level, hollow, and utterly devoid of inflection. It was the voice of a dead man speaking from the bottom of a well.</p>
<p>Whatever was simmering beneath that calm was not visible to the naked eye, but it radiated off him like heat from a tombstone.</p>
<p>Without waiting for a rebuttal, without acknowledging the blood-soaked nobles or the mercenary who had just dismantled his dignity, Theo turned.</p>
<p>Each step was a calculated agony, his boots squelching wetly in the pooling blood as he walked off the floor. His gait was stiff, mechanical, the movement of a man who had severed his connection to pain in order to survive the walk.</p>
<p>Aurelia watched him go, her pale features unreadable, though her hands trembled slightly where they were hidden beneath her sleeves. She waited until the echoes of Theo’s retreating footsteps died down before she turned her attention to the room.</p>
<p>The predator had been replaced by the matriarch.</p>
<p>She looked at Rex, her gaze sharp enough to draw blood.</p>
<p>"Rex Rexilion," she announced.</p>
<p>Her voice rang through the hall, formal and absolute, carrying the crushing weight of a household head speaking to her domain. "By the authority vested in me during the absence of the primary authority, you are hereby appointed as the acting combat director for the Nightwing unit training program until their return."</p>
<p>The twelve elite fighters standing in the periphery didn’t hesitate. In unison, they performed the formal acknowledgment, a synchronized strike of palms against breastplates that sounded like a single, thunderous heartbeat.</p>
<p>CLANG.</p>
<p>The sound reverberated off the vaulted ceilings, sealing the decree.</p>
<p>Rex stood in the center of the training hall. Beneath his calm exterior, he felt a surge of something primal: the deep, cold satisfaction of a predator whose trap had not only sprung but had been sprung exactly as designed.</p>
<p>This wasn’t luck. This was the exquisite precision of a plan executing at the level it was intended to, the clean, mathematical quality of sustained work finally yielding its inevitable result.</p>
<p>He did not show it. His face remained a neutral canvas.</p>
<p>Instead, he offered Aurelia a bow, a controlled gesture that conveyed warm, slightly surprised gratitude. He played the role of the honored subordinate perfectly, masking the fact that he had just orchestrated the systematic psychological annihilation of the House’s heir.</p>
<p>Hours later, the hall had surrendered to the evening shadows. The spectacle was over.</p>
<p>Iris had departed to seek sustenance; Diana and Elaris remained in low conversation near the entrance like distant ghosts, and Aurelia was occupied with the grim task of debriefing her standard setters.</p>
<p>Rex stood alone in the dead center of the training hall. The silence here was different now; it was heavy with the memory of screams and the phantom pain of broken bones.</p>
<p>He closed his eyes and summoned the only truth that mattered.</p>
<p>The system interface flickered to life before his mind’s eye, glowing with a cold, ethereal light.</p>
<p>He navigated past the trivialities, past the combat techniques and elemental augmentations, straight to the forbidden architecture of his ambition. He moved toward the section he had been constructing since the blood-soaked fields of Lustia’s domain, the capability that would transform him from a mere combatant into something that could rewrite the fundamental laws of existence.</p>
<p>He found it. The text didn’t just appear; it burned into his consciousness.</p>
<p>[SOUL MANIPULATION FULL POTENTIAL]</p>
<p>[COMPLETE OPERATION WITHIN THE SOUL REGISTER OF BIOLOGICAL ENTITIES.]</p>
<p>[ENABLES: READING THE SOUL’S CURRENT STATE AND RECENT HISTORY; COMMUNICATION WITH RECENTLY DEPARTED SOULS WITHIN THE TEMPORAL WINDOW OF THEIR TRANSITION; TEMPORARY ANCHORING OF A DEPARTING SOUL TO THE PHYSICAL TIMELINE; MODIFICATION OF SOUL LAYER BINDINGS, INCLUDING DESIGNATION TIES AND DESIRE BONDS; AND DETECTION OF SOUL-LEVEL WORKINGS APPLIED BY EXTERNAL FORCES.]</p>
<p>[COMMUNICATION WINDOW FOR DEPARTED SOULS: APPROXIMATELY 30 DAYS FROM THE DATE OF DEPARTURE. AT FULL POTENTIAL, BEYOND THIS WINDOW, SOULS HAVE COMPLETED THEIR TRANSITION AND ARE NO LONGER ACCESSIBLE THROUGH THIS SKILL.]</p>
<p>[ANCHORING FUNCTION: TEMPORARILY PREVENTS SOUL DEPARTURE FOR UP TO 72 HOURS, ALLOWING EXTENDED COMMUNICATION OR STABILIZATION OF CRITICALLY INJURED TARGETS.]</p>
<p>[MODIFICATION FUNCTION: CAN ADD, ALTER, OR REMOVE SOUL LAYER BINDINGS, INCLUDING APOSTLE DESIGNATIONS, DESIRE BOND EFFECTS, AND TEMPORAL ANCHORING WORKINGS APPLIED BY EXTERNAL ENTITIES.]</p>
<p>[COST: 11,200 POINTS]</p>
<p>Rex stared at the number. It was a king’s ransom in energy. His fingers invisible to the physical world hovered over the selection.</p>
<p>His mind flashed to the plans with Iris, to the complex web of loyalties and divine designations he needed to unravel. This was the scalpel he needed to dissect destiny itself.</p>
<p>[Current Energy Balance: 13,150 points]</p>
<p>There was no hesitation. No doubt.</p>
<p>Only the cold calculation of a man who had already decided what kind of god he would become.</p>
<p>He selected it.</p>
<p>[PURCHASE CONFIRMED.]</p>
<p>[SOUL MANIPULATION FULL POTENTIAL HAS BEEN INTEGRATED INTO THE USER’S SOUL REGISTER.]</p>
<p>[Current Energy Balance: 1,950 points]</p>
<p>The integration arrived differently from every other skill integration he had experienced because the soul register was not a register that any of his previous integrations had accessed. The closest analogue was the Emotional Insight’s passive read, which touched the emotional surface of the people around him.</p>
<p>Soul manipulation went deeper than that, below the emotional surface into the specific layer where the things that made a person a person rather than a body were located.</p>
<p>He stood in the training hall and felt the world change texture.</p>
<p>Not dramatically. The floor was still the same.</p>
<p>The hall’s ambient sounds were still the same. But the people in it now had a depth that they had not had thirty seconds ago—not the acquisition of strength for its own sake, but a visible extension below the physical and energetic registers into the specific quality of whatever it was that made each of them the specific person they were.</p>
<p>Aurelia’s soul signature was the specific quality of a person who had built her identity around the cultivation of strength, not the acquisition of strength for its own sake but the specific commitment to being capable enough to protect the things she valued.</p>
<p>It had the warmth of someone who had been doing this for decades and had found it genuinely sustaining.</p>
<p>Iris’s soul signature had the flat, sharp quality of someone in acute grief who had not yet reached the stage of integrating that grief. The fresh quality of loss was still actively affecting her.</p>
<p>Rex felt the Veylor connection through Iris’s soul register without looking for it, the specific soul-layer bond that a genuine relationship left even after one of the parties had departed, a thread that ran from Iris’s present state into the direction that recent departures went.</p>
<p>The thread was there.</p>
<p>It was approximately thirty-six hours old.</p>
<p>It was accessible.</p>
<p>Rex filed this with the flat satisfaction of confirmed intelligence and closed the skill catalog.</p>
<p>He looked at the empty training hall.</p>
<p>He looked at the number in his energy balance, which was 1,950 points remaining after the Soul Manipulation purchase, and he considered the specific constraints that this number imposed on his operational options in the near term.</p>
<p>He was not concerned.</p>
<p>He was in the Nightwing household.</p>
<p>He had a month.</p>
<p>He looked at the training hall’s ceiling, the high stone arch that the Nightwing family had built specifically for a space where the ceiling did not need to limit what was done beneath it.</p>
<p>"Let the one month of fun," Rex said, to the ceiling, in the flat, private register of someone speaking to themselves with complete honesty, "begin."</p>
<p>He walked out of the training hall into the rear courtyard of the Nightwing household, where the morning light was now bright, the grey sky replaced by the distinct quality of a day that had chosen to be itself, and Rex moved through it with the ease of someone who knows exactly where they are going and is in no particular hurry to get there.</p>
<p>He was, after all, the acting combat director of the Nightwing household’s training program.</p>
<p>He had things to teach.</p>
