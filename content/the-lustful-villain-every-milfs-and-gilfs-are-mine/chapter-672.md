<p>Once the initial rush of the integration subsided, he turned his attention back to the shimmering interface. He had one more gift to claim. One more way to ensure that when the world finally woke up to face him, they would have no choice but to kneel.</p>
<p>He bypassed the heavy hitters of the upper tier, moving deeper, looking for the scalpel to match his new hammer.</p>
<p>The second skill didn’t roar with the cosmic violence of gravity; instead, it drifted into his mind like a heavy, intoxicating fog, silent and insidious. It was a subtle power, a quiet predator that waited for the moment of vulnerability.</p>
<p>He found it three tiers below the first:</p>
<p>[SLEEP MANIPULATION FULL POTENTIAL]</p>
<p>[INDUCES GENUINE NEUROLOGICAL SLEEP IN DESIGNATED TARGETS WITHIN RANGE.]</p>
<p>[THIS IS NOT SEDATION OR UNCONSCIOUSNESS, BUT TRUE SLEEP PHYSIOLOGY.]</p>
<p>[DEPTH IS FULLY ADJUSTABLE: FROM LIGHT, SEMI-AWARE SLUMBER TO TOTAL, SENSORY-DEPRIVED UNCONSCIOUSNESS.]</p>
<p>[DURATION IS COMPLETELY CONTROLLABLE, FROM A FEW SECONDS TO INDEFINITE MAINTENANCE.]</p>
<p>[RANGE: CONTACT UP TO 50 METERS. BUT YOU HAVE THE FULL POTENTIAL TO MAKE THE WHOLE CONTINENT FALL ASLEEP WITH YOUR PEAK PHYSIQUE.]</p>
<p>[SYNERGY: DIRECTLY COMPLEMENTS DREAM MANIPULATION. TARGETS UNDER SLEEP MANIPULATION ARE FULLY ACCESSIBLE TO DREAM-BASED INTERFERENCE.]</p>
<p>[USERS REMAIN COMPLETELY UNAWARE OF THE INDUCED STATE, ATTRIBUTING IT TO NATURAL CAUSES.]</p>
<p>[SELECTIVE APPLICATION TO MULTIPLE TARGETS IS POSSIBLE. NO PAIN, NO DISCOMFORT, NO EXTERNAL AWARENESS. FREE SELECTION APPLIED AT NO COST.]</p>
<p>A slow, dangerous smile spread across Rex’s face, hidden behind the unyielding mask. "Something that isn’t too broken for me to actually conquer Aethelgard."</p>
<p>"And still... that is deliciously wicked," he breathed, his voice a silken thread in the dark. "Gravity to break their bodies... and sleep to break their minds because I’ve come up with some fucked-up shit about my new manipulation."</p>
<p>He leaned back, his eyes gleaming with the intellect of a master manipulator. The synergy was perfect.</p>
<p>He could plunge an entire kingdom into a dream from which they would never truly wake, all while believing they were simply resting after a long day. He could rewrite their loyalties, their memories, and their very souls in the quiet sanctuary of their own subconscious.</p>
<p>He wouldn’t just be a conqueror of land; he would be a conqueror of reality itself.</p>
<p>"The ultimate gaslighting tool," he chuckled softly. "They’ll thank you for the ’peaceful rest’ while you’re busy tearing their world apart."</p>
<p>With a mental click, he claimed the prize. "Yeah, let’s take it..."</p>
<p>"I’m already strong enough."</p>
<p>[SLEEP MANIPULATION FULL POTENTIAL acquired via free skill selection.]</p>
<p>The integration was seamless. He felt a new, quiet layer of awareness settle over his consciousness, a soft, velvet veil that he could cast over the world at a whim.</p>
<p>He could feel the "rhythm" of the room now: the heavy, deep, rhythmic pulses of the two Queens and the smaller, frantic little heartbeat of the succubus on the floor.</p>
<p>He took a moment to survey his kingdom. Cassandra was lost in the profound, unresponsive depths of a post-battle recovery, her body demanding every ounce of rest to mend the strain of the Blood Oath.</p>
<p>Beside her, Gorvasha was a fallen titan, her primal fury spent, her biology reclaiming itself with the brutal, unthinking efficiency of an Orc queen who had given everything to the fray.</p>
<p>Then, his gaze drifted down to the floor.</p>
<p>Lilith.</p>
<p>She had moved in her sleep, drifting from her initial, somewhat pathetic position to something slightly more comfortable, though no less undignified. She had somehow scavenged a decorative cushion, wedging it under her cheek with a contented little sigh that she would surely deny if she ever woke up.</p>
<p>Her wings were fully extended, a sprawling, leathery expanse of relaxation that reached up, the very tip of one wing brushing against the edge of Cassandra’s side of the bed.</p>
<p>Rex looked at the scene, a sense of profound, dark irony washing over him. He was the Lustful Villain, a man who had just rewritten the fundamental laws of his own existence, a man who held the power of gods in his hands and a fortune in energy points in his coffers.</p>
<p>He was also a man who had just sowed the seeds of dynasties in the wombs of queens.</p>
<p>And yet, here he was, looking down at a sleepy succubus who was using a cushion as a pillow and a wing as a blanket for a queen.</p>
<p>"You really are a ridiculous little thing, aren’t you, Lilith?" he whispered, his voice dripping with a mixture of genuine amusement and dominant condescension.</p>
<p>He slid off the bed, his movements fluid and silent, the new gravitational awareness making his descent feel weightless. He moved to the foot of the bed and then slowly, deliberately, crouched down beside her.</p>
<p>The shadow of his massive, muscular frame loomed over her small, sleeping form, a predator hovering over a particularly interesting morsel.</p>
<p>He crouched down, his eyes fixed on her face.</p>
<p>Rex leaned in, his shadow swallowing her whole. The silence of the room was thick, heavy with the scent of sleep and the lingering, intoxicating musk of the night’s conquests.</p>
<p>He stared at the succubus, his eyes cold and unyielding behind the mask.</p>
<p>"Lilith," he said, his voice a low, commanding rumble.</p>
<p>Nothing. Not even a twitch of a wing.</p>
<p>"Lilith," he said again, this time injecting a sharp, impatient edge into the name.</p>
<p>She responded with a soft, rhythmic mumble, the sound of someone drifting through a dream of silk and honey, and her wing shifted, settling right back into that same, infuriating position, brushing against the queen’s bed like a lazy, leathery curtain.</p>
<p>Rex’s jaw tightened. He looked at the wing, then at the cushion, then back at her smug, sleeping face.</p>
<p>"You absolute, useless little shit," he muttered.</p>
<p>He didn’t reach for her hand. Instead, he reached for the fundamental laws of the universe.</p>
<p>He focused his new awareness on the decorative cushion wedged under her cheek. With a surgical flick of his will, he applied a precise, minimal gravitational adjustment.</p>
<p>He didn’t crush her; he simply increased the weight of the cushion by fifteen percent. It went from soft and inviting to slightly dense, slightly lumpy, and decidedly uncomfortable.</p>
<p>Lilith shifted. Her brow furrowed in a tiny, subconscious frown, but her eyes remained clamped shut.</p>
<p>"Still nothing?" Rex whispered, a dark, teasing smirk tugging at his lips. "Fine."</p>
<p>"Let’s try a little more ’pressure’ on the situation."</p>
<p>He bumped the gravity to twenty percent. The cushion now felt like a slab of mildly compressed foam.</p>
<p>Lilith let out an irritated, muffled groan, her tail lashing once against the floor in annoyance.</p>
<p>Rex sat there, perfectly still, watching her with the predatory patience of a man who had all the time in the world and all the power in the room. He waited.</p>
<p>He counted the seconds.</p>
<p>Forty.</p>
<p>Forty-five.</p>
<p>Finally, with a dramatic, long-suffering sigh, one eye cracked open. It blinked blearily at the floor, squinting against the dim light, until it landed on Rex.</p>
<p>He was crouching there, looming over her like a dark god of disappointment, his expression flat and utterly unimpressed.</p>
<p>Lilith didn’t even try to sit up. She just stared at him with one half-lidded eye.</p>
<p>"I’m asleep," she croaked, her voice thick with slumber.</p>
<p>"You’re not," Rex countered, his voice dry.</p>
<p>"I’m trying to be," she corrected him, her tone dripping with the unearned sass of someone who felt safe in her exhaustion. "There is a profound, fundamental difference, Master."</p>
<p>"Get your lazy ass up," Rex commanded, the teasing edge vanishing into pure, dominant authority. "We have a throne room appearance in two hours."</p>
<p>"You need to be cleaned, dressed, and presentable before we walk in there."</p>
<p>"You can’t exactly parade around looking like you crawled out of a laundry basket."</p>
<p>"I am clean," she insisted, pulling a shred of dignity from the air.</p>
<p>It was the kind of dignity a stray cat might have after rolling in the dirt—technically true by succubus biological standards, perhaps, but a stretch by any civilized metric.</p>
<p>"You’re on the floor, Lilith," Rex pointed out, gesturing to the rug beneath her. "Like a fucking dog."</p>
<p>"I am well aware of my coordinates," she snapped, her eyes still mostly closed. "I chose the floor."</p>
<p>"The floor was available, ergonomically accessible, and entirely appropriate given the current spatial arrangement of the available sleeping surfaces."</p>
<p>Rex stared at her. He felt a sudden, violent urge to either laugh or slap the sleep out of her.</p>
<p>"The spatial arrangement?" he repeated, his voice rising in disbelief.</p>
<p>"The bed was occupied," she said with the clinical, administrative precision of a bureaucrat defending a failed budget. "It was a matter of logistical necessity."</p>
<p>"The bed was occupied because of a legendary engagement that produced two simultaneous maximum bonds and a species passive bonus!" Rex hissed, leaning closer until his mask was inches from her nose. "It wasn’t a ’logistical necessity’; it was a goddamn conquest!"</p>
<p>Lilith’s eyes snapped wide open, both of them finally focusing on the man looming over her.</p>
<p>"O-ohh!"</p>
<p>The tension in the room shifted instantly. It was no longer the heavy, tranquil silence of a post-conquest slumber; it was the sharp, electric friction of a subordinate realizing she had been mathematically insulted.</p>
<p>Lilith’s eyes narrowed, focusing on Rex with the singular, lethal intent of someone who had just found a glaring error in a contract.</p>
<p>"A species passive bonus," she repeated, the words coming out like a slow-burning fuse.</p>
<p>"One hundred and fifty points," Rex said, his voice maddeningly calm, almost conversational.</p>
