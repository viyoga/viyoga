<p>The tension in the plaza was no longer just a feeling; it was a physical weight, a suffocating pressure that made the very air feel thick with the scent of ozone, pulverized stone, and the metallic tang of blood.</p>
<p>As Apollo transitioned into his close-range adaptation, the fight shifted from a clash of macro forces to a brutal, intimate struggle of physics and will. Rex felt the shift immediately. The life affinity wasn’t just a light source; it was a predatory force.</p>
<p>As his armored forearm pressed against Apollo’s chest, the stone armor began to react. The designation’s entropy reversal wasn’t just healing Apollo; it was actively reknitting the stone itself.</p>
<p>The cracks in the armor began to hiss and glow, the stone plates fusing back together as if the very matter were being commanded to remember its unbroken state.</p>
<p>"You heal fast," Rex grunted, his voice a low rumble of approval. "Faster than the canyon. I’m noting that down."</p>
<p>"Glad to be of service to your research," Apollo spat, his eyes burning with a ferocity that matched the heat of the clash.</p>
<p>"And it seems you really looked at me well enough!" He was already moving, a blur of motion that defied the heaviness of the atmosphere.</p>
<p>But Rex was not alone in the chaos. High above, Eryndra’s astral signature descended like a silent predator.</p>
<p>She unleashed an astral working, a frequency designed to shatter the very structure of his constructs. But Rex was a master of the field. With the calculated precision of a god, he didn’t just deflect it; he captured the frequency.</p>
<p>Using his energy manipulation, he mirrored her own signature, throwing her own weapon back at her with a three-second delay.</p>
<p>The result was a visceral catastrophe. The astral frequency hit Eryndra’s form, and the medium of her own power turned against her.</p>
<p>She fell from the sky like a broken bird, hitting the ground with a sickening thud that echoed through the plaza.</p>
<p>Before the dust could settle, Calivara was already there. She was a whirlwind of lethal intent.</p>
<p>She bypassed the golems and the stone, her eyes locked on the vulnerabilities of the man. She used a powerful attack that targeted the blood vessels of her living opponent, aiming to damage their internal fluids.</p>
<p>It was a strike meant to burst veins and shatter organs.</p>
<p>But it hit the unyielding wall of the earthen authority. The attack washed over the stone armor like water over a mountain.</p>
<p>Rex turned his head, his gaze cold and analytical.</p>
<p>"Blood pressure working," he observed, his voice devoid of strain. "Against a stone target."</p>
<p>Calivera didn’t flinch. Her face was a mask of intense focus, her breath coming in short, sharp bursts.</p>
<p>"That was a diagnostic," she countered, her voice tight with the adrenaline of the fight.</p>
<p>"What did you learn?" Rex asked, his tone almost teasing, as if he were testing a student rather than fighting for survival.</p>
<p>"You have biological material inside the armor," she replied, her hands still dancing through the air as she shaped her next attack. "The designation’s circulatory targeting found nothing to grip."</p>
<p>"Your internal systems are shielded."</p>
<p>"Correct," Rex said, a faintly arrogant smirk playing on his lips. "But the stone breaks."</p>
<p>"It does," Calivara snapped, her eyes flashing. "I saw the cracks, and the chest plate gave."</p>
<p>"And you’re walking me through this," Rex noted, his voice dripping with the swagger of a man who was entirely in control of the carnage. "Why?"</p>
<p>"Because you’re going to be wrong about what comes next!" she shouted, her voice a war cry.</p>
<p>Rex let out a short, dark laugh. "Because you’re going to be wrong about what comes next, regardless."</p>
<p>"And frankly, watching you work through the problem is more interesting than just winning."</p>
<p>The arrogance was staggering. He was standing in the middle of a warzone, surrounded by blood and shattered stone, and he was treating the entire engagement like a philosophical debate.</p>
<p>Calivara didn’t respond with words. She responded with violence.</p>
<p>She unleashed a new application of her power, a technique so unorthodox and so intense that it defied the standard taxonomy of her affinity. It wasn’t the crushing pressure of blood flow, nor was it the corrosive acid of her previous strike.</p>
<p>It was something else entirely, a visceral, bloody surge of energy that seemed to throb with a life of its own.</p>
<p>The air itself seemed to bleed as she poured everything into the strike, her eyes burning with the determination to shatter the man who dared to mock her intellect with his calmness. The clash was no longer just a fight; it was a collision of wills, a bloody dance of death and defiance.</p>
<p>The air in the plaza didn’t just vibrate; it groaned under the weight of a dimensional pressure. The atmosphere itself felt thin, as if the presence of the astral summons was sucking the very oxygen out of the lungs of the living.</p>
<p>Eryndra, sprawled in the dust and tasting the copper of her own blood, forced her spirit upward. She didn’t just summon; she tore the veil.</p>
<p>Seven massive, ethereal entities materialized at the boundary of reality, monstrous, translucent shapes that defied the laws of light and shadow. They were titans of the astral plane, looming over the battlefield like ghosts of gods.</p>
<p>But the unseen did not intimidate Rex.</p>
<p>He stood amidst the carnage, his eyes glowing with a predatory intellect. He didn’t even flinch.</p>
<p>He watched the seven entities with a cold, calculating gaze. Using the depths of his energy manipulation, he parsed their frequency, and he saw the rhythm of their existence.</p>
<p>"Too loud," Rex muttered, his voice a low thrumming growl.</p>
<p>He didn’t just counter them; he erased them. He channeled his elemental magic into a new frequency, a destructive interference pattern.</p>
<p>It wasn’t a strike; it was a mathematical execution. He unleashed a wave of cancellation resonance.</p>
<p>The seven entities didn’t explode; they simply ceased. One moment they were towering monsters of energy; the next, they were nothing but a flicker of starlight before dissolving into the void.</p>
<p>Eryndra let out a sound, a hollow gasp that was half sob and half exhale, as her essence was drained of its glory. She collapsed, her body limp, her spirit spent.</p>
<p>"You killed them," Kaelira whispered from the shadows, her eyes wide.</p>
<p>"They were fighting," Rex replied, his voice devoid of emotion, as if he were merely commenting on the weather.</p>
<p>He didn’t look back. He was already moving, his gaze fixed on the next target.</p>
<p>Apollo stepped forward, his face tight with a growing dread. "They are alive, but--"</p>
<p>"They are alive," Rex interrupted, his voice cutting like a blade. "Eryndra is depleted, and Calivara is making a choice."</p>
<p>"That is all that matters."</p>
<p>He began to walk toward Calivara. Every step he took sounded like the hammering of a blacksmith.</p>
<p>Calivara stood her ground, her breath ragged, her body coated in the dust of the plaza and the mist of her own blood. She looked at him with the eyes of a warrior who knew she was walking into a maw.</p>
<p>"You’re not going to give me an out," she said, her voice steady despite the tremor in her limbs.</p>
<p>Rex stopped exactly four meters away. The tension between them was a physical force, a cord stretched to the breaking point.</p>
<p>"You have been analyzing my defenses since the start," Rex said, his voice dripping with a dark charisma. "You found the vulnerability and you found the limitations."</p>
<p>"You are a lethal intelligence, Calivara."</p>
<p>He leaned forward slightly, his presence crushing. "But what comes next has nothing to do with your skill... It has everything to do with your debt."</p>
<p>"Everything has a cost," Calivara spat, her hands trembling as she began to weave the next working. "I knew that the moment I chose a side."</p>
<p>"Then you understand," Rex said, a cruel smirk playing on his lips. "The cost is coming."</p>
<p>"It is," she replied, her eyes burning with a defiant fire. "And I will pay it."</p>
<p>In that moment, the plaza went silent. The only sound was the hiss of escaping steam and the heavy thud of Rex’s heartbeat.</p>
<p>Calivara began to assemble her ultimate technique. It was a masterpiece of blood and will. She drew the very fluid from her veins, her skin turning a deathly pale as she channeled her life force into a concentrated surge of pure biological power.</p>
<p>The workings she built were a monstrosity of math and muscle, a shimmering vortex of crimson energy that ripped through the air with a whistling scream. It was the most sophisticated assault Rex had ever faced, a strike designed to shatter not just the armor but the man himself.</p>
