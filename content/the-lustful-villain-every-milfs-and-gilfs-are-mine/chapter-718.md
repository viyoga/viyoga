<p>He slammed his hands into the earth, sending a dozen massive, spiked pillars of granite erupting in a frantic, chaotic pattern around her. "DIE, YOU FUCKING WITCH!"</p>
<p>"SKREEEEEEEEE!" The stone pillars tore through the air, seeking to impale her.</p>
<p>Morwenna’s eyes narrowed. This was where her true terrifying power manifested.</p>
<p>She didn’t just dodge; she manipulated the flow. As the pillars closed in, she performed a series of rapid-fire hand gestures, her fingers dancing through the air like a conductor.</p>
<p>"REVERSE!" she commanded.</p>
<p>The space around the pillars began to warp and fold. The momentum of the massive stones was suddenly inverted.</p>
<p>Instead of traveling toward her, they were sucked into a localized spatial vortex, spinning around her in a dizzying, lethal orbit.</p>
<p>WHIRRRRR WHIRRRRR!</p>
<p>Rex watched, mesmerized and enraged, as the very weapons he sent to kill her became a swirling shield of destruction around her.</p>
<p>"You think you can control the world?" Morwenna said, her eyes glowing with a cold, ancient light as she stepped through the spinning vortex of stone. "The world is just a tool."</p>
<p>"And you, Tremor... you’re just a clumsy child playing with a hammer."</p>
<p>She launched herself through the vortex. She was a blur of white and silver, a comet of pure, disciplined intent.</p>
<p>She struck him with a flurry of blows—palm, elbow, heel, fingertip—each one timed to the millisecond, each one finding the exact moment his defenses flickered.</p>
<p>BAM! CRACK! THUD! SNAP!</p>
<p>Rex was being dismantled. Not by strength, but by geometry.</p>
<p>He was being hit in the gaps, in the seams, in the tiny, infinitesimal moments of vulnerability that only thirty years of perfection could exploit. His skin was a map of bruises and shallow cuts; his breathing was a ragged, desperate struggle.</p>
<p>But as he felt his ribs groan and his vision blur, a dark, triumphant joy surged through him. He was being pushed. He was being broken.</p>
<p>And in the breaking, he felt more alive than he ever had in his entire godlike existence.</p>
<p>"HAHAHA... HA!" Rex coughed, wiping a thick smear of blood from his mouth, his eyes locked onto hers with a terrifying, manic intensity. "Come on, Grandma... show me... show me what else you’ve got!"</p>
<p>Rex’s laughter died into a low, guttural growl. The playful edge was gone; the time for testing was over.</p>
<p>He wasn’t just fighting a master combatant anymore; he was dismantling a legend.</p>
<p>"Enough," Rex whispered, his voice vibrating with a sudden, crushing weight.</p>
<p>He snapped his fingers.</p>
<p>CRUSH!</p>
<p>The gravity in a twenty-foot radius around Morwenna didn’t just increase; it became absolute. The ground beneath her feet vanished, replaced by a localized singularity that dragged her violently downward.</p>
<p>KRAA THOOM!</p>
<p>She slammed into the ruined cobblestones, the impact sending a shockwave that cracked the foundation of the nearby buildings.</p>
<p>Before she could even gasp for air, Rex was on her. He didn’t fly; he fell with the weight of a thousand mountains.</p>
<p>SPLAT!</p>
<p>His heel descended like a falling star, driving her shoulder deep into the crater. Morwenna shrieked, a sound of genuine agony as her clavicle snapped with a sickening</p>
<p>POP CRACK!</p>
<p>But Rex wasn’t finished. He grabbed her by the throat, his massive fingers digging into her windpipe, and launched her upward with a burst of gravitational repulsion.</p>
<p>VREEEEEE!</p>
<p>As she soared, Rex slammed his fist into the air.</p>
<p>SKEWER!</p>
<p>From the ground, five obsidian spikes erupted with the speed of bullets. Morwenna twisted in midair, her reflexes still sharp, but the gravity field was fluctuating wildly, making her movements erratic.</p>
<p>One spike caught her in the thigh, tearing through the muscle and pinning her leg to the ground.</p>
<p>SHLUCK!</p>
<p>"AAAAAAGH!" she screamed, her voice rasping as she tried to pull herself free.</p>
<p>Rex landed beside her, his face a mask of cold, calculated fury. He didn’t give her a second to recover. He grabbed her arm and slammed it back against the ground.</p>
<p>KRRRRR SPLAT!</p>
<p>The sound of her radius and ulna shattering was a dull, wet thud.</p>
<p>"You’re a relic, Morwenna!" Rex roared, his voice booming over the sound of the dying plaza. "A fucking museum piece!"</p>
<p>"Let’s see how much of you is left when I’m done!"</p>
<p>He raised his hand, and the very ground beneath her began to churn. The broken stones and dust swirled into a vortex, a grinding maw of geological debris.</p>
<p>GRIND!</p>
<p>The stone shards didn’t just hit her; they acted like a thousand tiny saws, shredding her clothing and slicing into her skin. Morwenna gasped, trying to conjure a spatial shield, but Rex’s gravity field was a chaotic, fluctuating mess, disrupting her ability to focus.</p>
<p>SCRRREEEEEE!</p>
<p>He reached down and seized her by the hair, jerking her head up to face him. Her eyes were wide, filled with a mixture of shock and a lingering, stubborn defiance.</p>
<p>Rex grinned, a cruel, jagged expression.</p>
<p>"You’re tough," he admitted, his fingers tightening. "But I’m the god of this fucking rock."</p>
<p>He slammed her face first into a protruding slab of granite.</p>
<p>CRACK!</p>
<p>Blood sprayed from her nose and mouth, splashing across Rex’s chest. He didn’t let go.</p>
<p>Instead, he used his Earthen Authority to fuse the stone slab with her hair, welding her to the ground in a grotesque, living sculpture of pain.</p>
<p>"Let’s see how you handle a million tons of pressure," Rex whispered, his voice right in her ear. He placed his hand on her back and concentrated. "COMPRESS!"</p>
<p>The air around her solidified into a dense, crushing shell. Morwenna’s chest constricted, her ribs groaning under the mounting weight.</p>
<p>GURRRRRK!</p>
<p>She clawed at the ground, her fingers digging into the dirt as she tried to find leverage.</p>
<p>Rex leaned in, his expression a mix of fascination and sadism. "Do you feel it?"</p>
<p>"The earth is trying to eat you alive... and you’re becoming part of the landscape, Morwenna."</p>
<p>"How does it feel to be a fucking rock?"</p>
<p>He suddenly released the pressure and, in the same breath, launched a series of rapid-fire stone projectiles.</p>
<p>WHACK! SMACK! THUD!</p>
<p>Each one hit a different part of her body: her ribs, her stomach, her temple. She was tossed around like a ragdoll, her body bouncing off the debris of the plaza.</p>
<p>BOOM!</p>
<p>The final strike was a massive boulder that caught her midair, slamming her back into the crater. She lay there, broken and bleeding, her breathing a ragged, wet wheeze.</p>
<p>Rex walked toward her, his boots crunching on the rubble. He looked down at her, his eyes dark and empty of any mercy.</p>
<p>"You’re still breathing," he observed, his voice devoid of emotion. "Impressive."</p>
<p>"But look around you, Morwenna!"</p>
<p>"This is what’s left of your world!"</p>
<p>"This is what I’ve built from the ruins."</p>
<p>He stood over her, the shadow of his massive frame eclipsing her. "I’m not going to kill you yet~!"</p>
<p>"I want you to watch, and I want you to see everything you love turn into dust!"</p>
<p>...</p>
<p>The eight minutes of combat had ceased to be a mere exchange of blows; they had become a violent, tectonic collision that had rewritten the geography of the plaza. What was once a bustling center of commerce was now a cratered, hellish wasteland of pulverized granite and slick, dark crimson.</p>
<p>The air itself tasted of ozone, dust, and the metallic tang of spilled life.</p>
<p>Rex’s will had not only shattered the stalemate but also pulverized it under its relentless pressure.</p>
<p>Morwenna lay broken against the shattered cobblestones, her body pinned by a localized, suffocating field of Gravity Manipulation. The force was monstrous, six times the standard gravitational constant, pressing her into the earth with the weight of a collapsing mountain.</p>
<p>CRUNCH.</p>
<p>Every shallow, desperate gasp she took sounded like dry parchment tearing as her lungs fought the invisible hammer trying to flatten them. Her spatial distortion, that terrifying tool of predictive disruption that had once made her untouchable, was bled dry; the air around her shimmered weakly, a dying ember of the powerhouse it had been moments before.</p>
<p>She was experiencing the soul-crushing, visceral exhaustion of a mortal who had poured every drop of her essence into a sustained war against an opponent who hadn’t even begun to sweat.</p>
<p>Rex stood over her, a dark, looming silhouette that blotted out the sun. He wasn’t panting.</p>
<p>He wasn’t trembling. His skin, though splattered with her blood, looked as unbothered as if he had merely finished a light stroll through a manicured garden. He looked like a god who had just finished a casual afternoon of recreation.</p>
<p>Morwenna tilted her head back, her vision swimming. Her face was a mask of grit, bruises, and a thick trickle of blood running from the corner of her mouth, staining her chin.</p>
<p>Even through the haze of agony, her eyes held a razor-sharp, predatory intelligence.</p>
<p>"You..." she wheezed, the words forced out through the crushing weight of the gravity, sounding like grinding stones. "You’re not... the Undead Bringer."</p>
<p>Rex tilted his head, a dark, amused glint dancing in his eyes as he watched her struggle. "No..."</p>
<p>"Not quite."</p>
<p>"You’re something else entirely," she spat, a grimace of pure, unadulterated pain twisting her features.</p>
<p>"GURRRK!" She coughed, a spray of red hitting the stone near his boot. "Something... fucking unquantifiable."</p>
<p>"You weren’t in any of our goddamn assessments..."</p>
<p>"You’re a variable... a fucking anomaly we didn’t account for."</p>
<p>"Assessments are just guesses made by people too afraid to look at the truth," Rex replied.</p>
<p>His voice was smooth, terrifyingly calm, and lacked even a hint of the exertion required to keep her pinned to the dirt. "People love their little charts and their predictable little numbers."</p>
<p>"But the earth doesn’t care about your numbers."</p>
