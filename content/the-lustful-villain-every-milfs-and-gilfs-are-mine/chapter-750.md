<p>She descended upon him like a falling star, her movements no longer just powerful but impossibly fast. She began a relentless, rhythmic execution of martial and magical fury.</p>
<p>WHAM!</p>
<p>A heavy, energy-infused kick caught him in the solar plexus.</p>
<p>OOF!</p>
<p>Rex’s body folded like parchment.</p>
<p>CRACK! THUD!</p>
<p>Before he could recover, she spun, her heel slamming into his jaw with the force of a wrecking ball.</p>
<p>KRA KOOM!</p>
<p>She didn’t stop. She became a blur of gold and white heat, a continuous loop of devastation.</p>
<p>WHAP WHAP WHAP WHAP!</p>
<p>A flurry of palm strikes rained down on his chest, each one sounding like a heavy mallet hitting wet clay.</p>
<p>SPLAT!</p>
<p>A punch to his gut sent a spray of crimson erupting from his mouth.</p>
<p>PHTOO!</p>
<p>VREEEEEEEEE!</p>
<p>She channeled her energy into her fingertips, firing rapid bolts of concentrated solar energy into his torso.</p>
<p>ZAP! ZAP! ZAP! ZAP!</p>
<p>Each bolt punched a smoking hole through his clothes and flesh, the smell of cauterized meat filling the air.</p>
<p>Sssssss!</p>
<p>She grabbed his head, her fingers digging into his temples, and slammed him downward with a telekinetic burst.</p>
<p>BOOM!</p>
<p>As he tumbled, she caught up to him, delivering a brutal knee to his spine.</p>
<p>CRUNCH!</p>
<p>The sound of his vertebrae grinding against each other was sickeningly loud in the thin air.</p>
<p>"Die!" she screamed, her voice a cacophony of divine fury. "Die! Die! DIE!"</p>
<p>KRA KOOOOM!</p>
<p>She unleashed a final, desperate combo: a spinning kick that shattered his ribs, followed by a double palm strike to his chest that shook his entire frame, and finally, a massive, overhead hammer fist aimed directly at his skull.</p>
<p>WHAM!</p>
<p>The impact was deafening.</p>
<p>THUD CRACK!</p>
<p>Rex was sent hurtling back toward the earth, a broken, bleeding wreck of a man, his body battered, bruised, and seemingly beyond the point of no return. He looked like a corpse caught in a hurricane.</p>
<p>Ignivara hovered in the air, her chest heaving, her hands trembling from the sheer output of her rage. She watched his descent, waiting for the final, silent impact.</p>
<p>She was ready to declare victory. She was ready to be done.</p>
<p>But the impact never came.</p>
<p>Midfall, Rex’s eyes snapped open.</p>
<p>They weren’t glazed anymore. They were piercing, cold, and terrifyingly lucid.</p>
<p>VREEEEEEEEEEE!</p>
<p>In a sudden, violent burst of telekinetic energy, Rex halted his descent, freezing in midair just meters above the rooftops.</p>
<p>Ignivara, still caught in the momentum of her final strike, lunged forward to finish him, her hands outstretched to crush his throat. But she didn’t hit soft, broken flesh.</p>
<p>She hit something that felt like solid granite.</p>
<p>CLACK!</p>
<p>With a movement so fast the human eye couldn’t even register it, Rex’s hands shot upward. He caught both of her wrists in a crushing grip.</p>
<p>CRUNCH.</p>
<p>Ignivara gasped, her eyes widening in shock. She tried to pull away, to flare her energy, to do anything, but she was anchored.</p>
<p>She was caught.</p>
<p>And then, she saw it.</p>
<p>The wounds were closing. The blackened, scorched flesh on his face was knitting itself back together in real time, the skin turning smooth and flawless as if the damage had never happened.</p>
<p>Sssssss...</p>
<p>The broken ribs snapped back into place with a series of wet, rhythmic pops.</p>
<p>POP! CRACK!</p>
<p>The massive bruises on his chest faded into nothingness, the blood that had been leaking from his mouth being reabsorbed into his body as his cells multiplied at an impossible, godlike rate.</p>
<p>His Supreme Healing and Infinite Regeneration were working with a terrifying, silent efficiency. Within seconds, the man who had been a broken wreck was once again the pristine, muscular titan she had first encountered.</p>
<p>Rex looked up at her. A slow, dark, and utterly predatory smile spread across his lips, a smile that promised a very different kind of end.</p>
<p>"You’ve had your fun, Ignivara," Rex said, his voice low, calm, and vibrating with a new, overwhelming power.</p>
<p>He tightened his grip on her wrists, his fingers feeling like unbreakable steel bands. "But the data is in."</p>
<p>"You’ve exhausted your variables."</p>
<p>He leaned in closer, his eyes glowing with an intense, terrifying light.</p>
<p>"This ends now..." he whispered, the air around him beginning to hum with a sudden, massive gravitational pressure. "...my turn."</p>
<p>Rex didn’t just move; he ceased to exist in one spot and materialized in another.</p>
<p>VWOOP!</p>
<p>Ignivara swung her claws at where he had been, but Rex was already behind her, his hand striking the back of her neck with the force of a falling building.</p>
<p>CLACK!</p>
<p>Her head snapped forward, and before she could recover, he was gone again.</p>
<p>VWOOP!</p>
<p>He reappeared at her flank, driving a concentrated burst of gravity into her side.</p>
<p>KRA BOOOOM!</p>
<p>The impact sent her spiraling, her body twisting violently in the thin air.</p>
<p>"What’s the matter?" Rex’s voice echoed from everywhere and nowhere. "Thought you had me figured out?"</p>
<p>VWOOP!</p>
<p>He was above her now. He fell like a stone, his knee crashing into her jaw with a sickening thud.</p>
<p>CRUNCH.</p>
<p>As she recoiled, he didn’t let up. He teleported again, appearing beneath her and delivering a precise kick to the small of her back.</p>
<p>THUD!</p>
<p>VWOOP!</p>
<p>He was in front of her again, his fingers digging into the pressure points of her shoulders.</p>
<p>SQUEEEEE!</p>
<p>Ignivara let out a sharp, agonizing shriek as he applied a crushing grip, a single, precise burst of telekinetic force that threatened to snap her clavicles.</p>
<p>"You’re out of time, Ignivara," Rex whispered, his voice cold and devoid of mercy. "This isn’t a fight anymore!"</p>
<p>"It’s an eviction!"</p>
<p>VWOOP!</p>
<p>He vanished and reappeared behind her, delivering a brutal, short-arc elbow to her kidney.</p>
<p>GURK!</p>
<p>She gasped, the air leaving her lungs in a ragged, bloody spray.</p>
<p>VWOOP! VWOOP! VWOOP!</p>
<p>He became a ghost of violence, a flicker of silver and steel.</p>
<p>WHACK!</p>
<p>A blow to the ear.</p>
<p>CRACK!</p>
<p>A palm strike to the bridge of her nose.</p>
<p>THUD!</p>
<p>A devastating knee to her stomach. Each time she tried to guard, he was already somewhere else, striking with a precision that turned her own momentum against her.</p>
<p>She was caught in a loop of endless, agonizing impacts, her body bouncing from one blow to the next like a pinball in a machine of pain.</p>
<p>"STOP IT!" she screamed, her voice a guttural, desperate sound.</p>
<p>In a final, desperate bid for survival, Ignivara’s body shuddered and expanded. With a deafening RRRRRRRUMBLE, she reverted to her massive dragon form in a split second, the sheer force of her transformation creating a shockwave that pushed Rex back.</p>
<p>FSSSSS BOOM!</p>
<p>Bellowing in primal rage, she lunged, her massive jaws snapping shut around Rex’s torso.</p>
<p>CHOMP!</p>
<p>Her teeth sank deep into his shoulder and chest, her powerful neck muscles tightening as she tried to crush him into oblivion.</p>
<p>Rex didn’t panic. He didn’t even flinch.</p>
<p>As the dragon’s teeth sank deeper, Rex reached up and slammed both palms against her upper and lower jaws.</p>
<p>CLANG!</p>
<p>His hands were like iron pylons, arresting her bite with a sudden, jarring halt.</p>
<p>SQUEEEEE!</p>
<p>The dragon’s eyes widened in shock; she had never encountered a mortal who could physically hold her maw open.</p>
<p>"Wrong move," Rex hissed, his eyes glowing with a lethal light.</p>
<p>He began to channel a massive, swirling vortex of energy in his palms, a fusion of gravity and telekinetic force. The air around them began to singe and warp, a high-pitched VREEEEEEEEEE that threatened to burst her eardrums.</p>
<p>"This is the end of the line."</p>
<p>KRA BOOOOOOOOOM!</p>
<p>He unleashed the fusion wave directly into her open maw. A beam of pure, blinding white energy roared down her throat, a concentrated pillar of destruction that bypassed all defenses.</p>
<p>Ignivara’s eyes rolled back as the energy surged through her, expanding and exploding from the inside.</p>
<p>BOOOOOMMMM!</p>
<p>The blast sent her reeling, her massive form convulsed by the internal detonation. But Rex wasn’t finished.</p>
<p>He surged forward, climbing her side, his fists becoming a blur of focused, brutal precision.</p>
<p>WHACK!</p>
<p>A right hook to her snout.</p>
<p>KRAK!</p>
<p>A left jab into her eye socket.</p>
<p>BOOM!</p>
<p>A double palm strike that shook her jaw.</p>
<p>CRACK! WHACK! SMASH!</p>
<p>He unleashed a devastating combo, his fists striking her face with the speed of a machine gun, each hit sounding like a thunderclap.</p>
<p>BOOM! BOOM! BOOM!</p>
<p>The dragon’s head whipped back and forth, blood spraying from her snout as her skull began to crack under the sheer pressure.</p>
<p>"Stay... down!"</p>
<p>Rex spun, gathering all his remaining momentum into one final, singular strike. He drove his fist deep into her soft underbelly, a punch that carried the weight of a mountain.</p>
<p>KRA BOOOOOOOOOOOOM!</p>
<p>The impact was catastrophic. The dragon’s eyes went blank as she was launched backward.</p>
<p>She became a golden streak, a falling star of flesh and scale, hurtling away from Aethelgard at impossible speeds.</p>
<p>VREEEEEEEEE!</p>
<p>She breached the sound barrier, a sonic boom echoing across the island.</p>
<p>BOOOOM!</p>
<p>She soared over the landscape, a doomed projectile, until she finally slammed into the uninhabited, desolate wasteland miles away from the city.</p>
<p>KRRRRRRRRRRAAAAAAAASH!!!!</p>
<p>The ground shook as she crashed into a remote canyon, raising a massive plume of dust and debris. The impact crater was immense; the earth itself shattered by the force of her fall.</p>
<p>Rex stood alone in the sky, his chest heaving, his clothes tattered. He watched the distant plume of dust settle, his expression unreadable.</p>
<p>He had won, but the cost was written in the blood and bruises covering his body.</p>
<p>"Checkmate," he whispered to the wind.</p>
