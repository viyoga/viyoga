<p>Rex’s head settled back into place with a slow, deliberate motion. A single droplet of blood trickled from the corner of his mouth where his teeth had pressed into his cheek, but his eyes remained glacial.</p>
<p>The momentary hope that had flickered in Theo’s shattered mind didn’t just die; it was incinerated.</p>
<p>Rex’s lips curled into something that wasn’t quite a smile. It was something far more predatory.</p>
<p>"My turn," Rex said.</p>
<p>The two words were quiet, but they carried the weight of a death sentence.</p>
<p>The shift in atmosphere was instantaneous. The casual mockery of the crowd vanished, replaced by a hungry, predatory tension.</p>
<p>They knew what was coming. They knew the mercy had expired.</p>
<p>Rex moved.</p>
<p>He didn’t charge; he exploded. Before Theo could even register the movement, Rex was inside his guard.</p>
<p>BAM!</p>
<p>A fist to the solar plexus folded Theo like parchment. The air left his lungs in a wet, strangled gurgle.</p>
<p>Before he could double over, Rex grabbed him by the throat and drove him backward into the stone pillar.</p>
<p>CRUNCH.</p>
<p>The impact reverberated through the hall as Theo’s spine collided with the ancient masonry.</p>
<p>"You wanted to play with fire, Prince?" Rex growled, his voice a guttural rasp.</p>
<p>WHAM.</p>
<p>A brutal uppercut caught Theo under the chin, snapping his head back so hard his teeth audibly collided.</p>
<p>CLACK.</p>
<p>"Then let’s see how you handle the furnace."</p>
<p>Rex unleashed a torrent of violence. This was no longer a demonstration; it was an execution of dignity.</p>
<p>Each strike was precise, devastating, and delivered with the intent to inflict maximum pain without the release of unconsciousness.</p>
<p>THUD. CRACK. WHACK.</p>
<p>A left hook to the ribs CRUNCH.</p>
<p>A spinning back kick to the kidney THUMP.</p>
<p>A series of rapid-fire jabs to the face that turned Theo’s features into a crimson mask.</p>
<p>"This is for your arrogance!" Rex roared as he drove a knee into Theo’s groin.</p>
<p>OOF.</p>
<p>"This is for your weakness!"</p>
<p>WHAM.</p>
<p>A hammer fist to the collarbone. "And this... this is for thinking you could ever be worthy of standing in my presence!"</p>
<p>Theo was no longer fighting back. He couldn’t.</p>
<p>He was a human punching bag, a broken vessel receiving the punishment he had earned. He could only flail weakly, his hands clawing uselessly at Rex’s armored forearms as he was systematically dismantled.</p>
<p>And then, something unexpected happened. The nobles, the very people who had been whispering in disgust, began to change.</p>
<p>The disgust turned into something darker. Something primal.</p>
<p>The cruelty of the scene tapped into something ancient and vicious within them. They weren’t cheering for the Nightwing heir; they were cheering for the spectacle of his destruction.</p>
<p>"Yes! Break him!" the Duke shouted, his face twisted in a grotesque grin.</p>
<p>"Show him what a real warrior looks like!" another bellowed, clapping his hands in time with Rex’s strikes.</p>
<p>CLAP. CLAP. CLAP.</p>
<p>"Crush the disgrace!" the crowd began to chant. "CRUSH THE DISGRACE! CRUSH THE DISGRACE!"</p>
<p>The chant grew louder, more feverish, feeding off Rex’s brutality. The training hall had transformed from a place of learning into a Roman colosseum.</p>
<p>The nobles were no longer observers; they were participants in Theo’s humiliation.</p>
<p>Rex seemed to feed off the energy. His strikes became faster and more vicious.</p>
<p>He grabbed Theo by his hair, forcing his head up to face the chanting crowd.</p>
<p>"Look at them, Theo!" Rex screamed over the roar of the spectators. "This is your kingdom now!"</p>
<p>"They don’t want a king! They want a victim!"</p>
<p>WHAM.</p>
<p>Rex drove a fist into Theo’s nose, sending blood spraying across the front row of the nobles. They didn’t flinch; they laughed.</p>
<p>"Look at the pretty prince bleeding for us!" someone shrieked with delight.</p>
<p>Theo’s eyes were rolled back in his head, his breathing nothing more than shallow, wet rattles. His magic had burned out, leaving him hollow and exposed.</p>
<p>Every time he tried to speak, more blood spilled from his lips.</p>
<p>"Please..." he wheezed, but the word was drowned out by the thunderous chant of the crowd.</p>
<p>Rex didn’t stop. He grabbed Theo by the belt and threw him across the floor like refuse.</p>
<p>SLIDE.</p>
<p>As Theo scrambled to find purchase on the slick stones, Rex descended upon him again.</p>
<p>BAM. BAM. BAM.</p>
<p>Three consecutive punches to the temple. Theo’s body convulsed, his limbs twitching spasmodically.</p>
<p>Rex stood over him, his shadow stretching long and monstrous across the broken boy.</p>
<p>"I’m not done ruining you, Theo," Rex whispered, though the crowd’s roar made it seem like he was shouting. "I’ll make sure that when you finally close your eyes for good, the last thing you see is the face of the man who proved you were nothing."</p>
<p>Rex raised both fists high above his head, the muscles in his shoulders coiling like serpents. The crowd went silent for one heartbeat of pure anticipation.</p>
<p>"DIE, YOU USELESS SHIT!" Rex bellowed.</p>
<p>BOOM.</p>
<p>Rex didn’t just want to hurt Theo; he wanted to erase him. The crowd’s bloodlust had become a tangible thing in the air, a poisonous fog that Rex inhaled to fuel his own cruelty.</p>
<p>Every time Rex’s fist connected with flesh, the hall erupted in a new wave of savage applause.</p>
<p>CRUNCH.</p>
<p>Rex drove a boot into Theo’s ribs, sending him tumbling sideways across the marble. The prince’s body hit a decorative pillar with a hollow THUD, leaving a smear of crimson on the white stone.</p>
<p>Before Theo could even attempt to draw breath, Rex was already there, his shadow falling over the broken boy like a shroud.</p>
<p>"You’re still breathing?" Rex asked, his voice dripping with mock disappointment. "After all that magic, after all that screaming?"</p>
<p>"You’re tougher than you look, little prince... Too bad toughness doesn’t make you competent."</p>
<p>WHAM.</p>
<p>Rex’s fist descended in a brutal hammer strike to Theo’s back.</p>
<p>OOF.</p>
<p>Theo’s face was pressed hard against the cold floor, his cheek grinding into the stone.</p>
<p>"Yes! Harder!" a baroness shrieked from the sidelines, her fan fluttering wildly. "Make him unrecognizable!"</p>
<p>"Break the Nightwing spine!" a knight roared, slamming his fist against his breastplate in approval.</p>
<p>CLANG. CLANG.</p>
<p>Rex grabbed Theo by the collar of his shredded tunic, hauling him upright just enough to deliver a series of devastating body blows.</p>
<p>THUD. THUD. THUD.</p>
<p>Each strike sounded like a butcher’s cleaver hitting meat. Theo’s legs flailed uselessly, his boots scraping against the floor as he struggled to maintain any semblance of balance.</p>
<p>"Look at him!" Rex shouted to the spectators, holding Theo aloft like a trophy of war. "This is your heir!"</p>
<p>"This is the future of the House of Nightwing!"</p>
<p>"A screaming, bleeding child who can’t even land a punch without begging for mercy!"</p>
<p>The humiliation was absolute. Theo wasn’t just losing a fight; he was being stripped of his humanity before the eyes of his entire world.</p>
<p>His eyes were swollen shut, his breathing was a series of wet, gurgling spasms, and his pride had been ground into the blood-soaked marble.</p>
<p>SMACK.</p>
<p>Rex delivered a stinging slap across Theo’s bloodied face, more for the insult than the damage. "Wake up, Prince... The show isn’t over until I say it’s over."</p>
<p>Rex pulled back his fist for what looked like the final, skull-crushing blow. The crowd’s chanting reached a deafening crescendo.</p>
<p>"CRUSH HIM! CRUSH HIM! CRUSH HIM!"</p>
<p>"ENOUGH!"</p>
<p>The command cut through the chaos like a blade through silk. The voice was cold, authoritative, and laced with an unmistakable tremor of restrained fury.</p>
<p>The hall fell silent instantly. Even the nobles froze mid-cheer. Rex paused, his fist cocked back, inches from Theo’s shattered face.</p>
<p>He turned his head slowly to see Aurelia walking down the center aisle. Her face was pale, her eyes fixed on the carnage, but her stride was steady.</p>
<p>She ignored Rex entirely as she approached the crumpled form on the floor. She knelt beside Theo, her expensive silk robes soaking up the blood pooling around him.</p>
<p>"Mother..." Theo’s voice was barely a whisper, a broken rasp that barely carried past her ear.</p>
<p>Without saying a word to Rex or the spectators, Aurelia reached down. Her movements were gentle, almost reverent, as she slipped one arm beneath his shoulders and the other behind his knees.</p>
<p>With surprising strength born of desperation, she began to help him up.</p>
<p>Theo’s body was limp, a sack of broken bones and bruised flesh. He didn’t resist; he couldn’t.</p>
<p>He allowed himself to be pulled upright, leaning heavily against her, his head lolling forward. His blood dripped steadily onto her pristine dress, but she didn’t seem to notice.</p>
<p>The silence in the hall was now different; it wasn’t the silence of anticipation but the uncomfortable silence of witnesses who had seen too much.</p>
<p>Aurelia steadied him, holding him upright as he struggled to find any semblance of equilibrium. Theo’s breathing was labored, each inhale a visible struggle against his broken ribs.</p>
<p>Slowly, painfully, he lifted his head. His one functional eye flickered open, blood streaming down his temple.</p>
<p>He didn’t cry. He didn’t scream. He didn’t curse.</p>
<p>He stood and looked at Rex with the specific flat expression of someone who has processed what has just happened and is currently in the specific state of someone deciding what to do with the processing.</p>
<p>"You know what you need to say to him now..." Aurelia said.</p>
