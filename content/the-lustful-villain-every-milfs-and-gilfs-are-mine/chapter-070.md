<p>"Who’s the one making that?" Rex asked, curious as always.</p>

<p>"What kind of a fucking demon lord is called Mordecai Vrael...? That sounded like a human’s name from my world!" Rex thought to himself. "Don’t tell me he’s a fucking reincarnator...?"</p>

<p>Diana, who had been sitting next to Iris in a way that made it seem like she wanted to stay close to her for a long time, made a noise that she quickly tried to hide as a cough.</p>
