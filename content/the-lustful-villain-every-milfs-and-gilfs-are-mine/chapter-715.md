<p>While the screams of Veran provided a rhythmic backdrop of agony, Rex turned his predatory gaze toward the market square of Caldmere. There, the air was thick with the scent of pulverized limestone and the metallic tang of blood.</p>
<p>Brant Hollow was a different beast entirely. He wasn’t a philosopher or a silent observer; he was a fighter, a man of action who met the chaos with a ferocity that actually made Rex’s blood sing with anticipation.</p>
<p>Brant was currently a whirlwind of steel and grit, his blade whistling through the air as he carved a path through the debris, his eyes locked on the massive, monolithic golem that had turned the market into a slaughterhouse.</p>
<p>Brant lunged, his movement a blur of practiced lethal intent. He slid beneath a swinging stone limb, the wind of the blow ruffling his hair, and drove his blade upward with a guttural roar.</p>
<p>SHIIIIING!</p>
<p>The steel bit deep into the golem’s thigh, a massive chunk of granite shearing off in a spray of grey dust and magical essence. Brant didn’t stop; he spun, using the momentum to deliver a devastating kick to the golem’s "knee," the impact sending a vibration up his own leg that felt like hitting a mountain.</p>
<p>Rex, watching through the golem’s sensory input, felt a surge of genuine excitement. This wasn’t just a victim; this was a challenge.</p>
<p>He decided to stop merely commanding the golem and start inhabiting the fight. He wanted to feel the impact of Brant’s steel against his stone skin. He wanted to taste the struggle.</p>
<p>"Not bad, Brant Hollow!" Rex’s voice erupted from the golem, not as a rumble, but as a thunderous, mocking shout that shook the very stalls of the market. "You’ve got more spine than the last one!"</p>
<p>"You actually think you can chip away at a god?"</p>
<p>Brant didn’t answer with words. He answered with a flurry of strikes, a lunge, a parry, and a heavy overhead chop that sent sparks flying as steel met stone.</p>
<p>CLANG! CLANG! CRACK!</p>
<p>But Rex was done playing defensive. He wanted to feel the "vibe" of the kill.</p>
<p>"You fight like a man who thinks there’s a point to it!" Rex roared, the golem’s massive arm suddenly lashing out with a speed that defied its bulk.</p>
<p>It wasn’t a slow, crushing movement; it was a whip crack of geological fury. Brant tried to parry, but the sheer kinetic energy of the stone limb was overwhelming.</p>
<p>The blade caught the edge, but the force sent Brant spinning backward, his boots skidding through the bloody dust. Before he could find his footing, the golem was on him.</p>
<p>It didn’t just strike; it hunted.</p>
<p>The golem lunged, its massive weight causing the cobblestones to erupt in its wake. Brant rolled, narrowly avoiding a stomp that pulverized a nearby merchant’s table into splinters, but the golem was already swinging a jagged forearm spike.</p>
<p>WHIP CRACK!</p>
<p>The spike caught Brant in the side. It didn’t pierce him clean; it tore.</p>
<p>The jagged edge of the stone hooked into his ribs, the sound of bone snapping CRACK SCHLICK echoing through the square. Brant let out a choked, pained grunt, blood spraying from the wound as he was yanked toward the golem’s massive chest.</p>
<p>"Look at you, scrambling in the dirt!" Rex laughed, the sound a terrifying, booming vibration that seemed to come from the earth itself. "You fight so hard, so desperately!"</p>
<p>"For what, Brant? For a ’cause’? For ’people’? Look around you!"</p>
<p>"The people are screaming! The cause is dying! There is nothing left but the weight of my will!"</p>
<p>The golem grabbed Brant by the throat with a hand of crushing granite. Brant’s feet dangled off the ground, his hands clawing uselessly at the unyielding stone.</p>
<p>His face turned a frantic, bruised purple as the air was squeezed from his lungs.</p>
<p>"GURGLE... HAAA... HAAA..."</p>
<p>"You’re... just... a... tyrant..." Brant wheezed, a single, defiant tear of blood trailing down his cheek.</p>
<p>"A tyrant?" Rex’s voice dropped to a low, sadistic purr, the golem’s grip tightening just enough to make the vertebrae in Brant’s neck groan. "No, Brant."</p>
<p>"A tyrant wants to be obeyed, but a god wants to be felt..."</p>
<p>’And by the time I’m done with you, you’re going to feel every single ounce of the earth’s hatred!"</p>
<p>With a violent, mocking jerk, the golem slammed Brant face-first into a stone pillar.</p>
<p>THUD CRUNCH!</p>
<p>The impact was sickening, the sound of a nose shattering and teeth clattering against the ground. Brant slumped, a broken mess of blood and bruised pride, as the golem loomed over him, its shadow a dark, suffocating shroud.</p>
<p>Rex didn’t want a quick death for Brant. Brant was a warrior, and warriors deserved to be broken before they were shattered.</p>
<p>The golem didn’t crush Brant’s skull. Instead, it used its massive, jagged fingers to pin Brant’s arms wide against the stone pillar, stretching his chest taut.</p>
<p>"Let’s see how much of that ’warrior spirit’ is left when we start peeling it away," Rex hissed through the stone.</p>
<p>The golem’s hand, a lumpy mass of fused obsidian, descended toward Brant’s midsection. It didn’t strike; it pressed a single, razor-sharp shard of rock into Brant’s stomach and began to rotate it.</p>
<p>SKREEEEEEEEE...</p>
<p>The sound of skin tearing and muscle being shredded was sickeningly slow. Brant’s scream was a ragged, desperate thing, a sound of pure, unadulterated agony that tore through the market square.</p>
<p>"AAAAAAAGGGGHHHH! TREMOORRGGHH! YOU... FUCKING... MONSTER!"</p>
<p>"Monster? No, Brant. I’m the inevitable," Rex mocked.</p>
<p>He commanded the golem to pull the shard upward.</p>
<p>RRRRIIIIIP!</p>
<p>The stone tore a jagged furrow through Brant’s abdomen, exposing the glistening, pulsing reality of his insides. Blood cascaded down his body in thick, hot rivers, pooling in the cracks of the cobblestones.</p>
<p>Rex watched the light in Brant’s eyes flicker, the sheer pain forcing the man into a state of semi-conscious delirium. Then, with a sudden, violent burst of sadistic glee, Rex commanded the golem to bring both massive fists down on Brant’s head simultaneously.</p>
<p>KRA KOOOOOM!</p>
<p>The sound was catastrophic. Brant’s skull didn’t just break; it disintegrated.</p>
<p>A spray of grey matter, bone fragments, and crimson fluid erupted outward, painting the surrounding stalls in a grotesque, wet mural of death. The warrior was gone, reduced to a shapeless heap of gore.</p>
<p>But the carnage was only beginning.</p>
<p>As Rex stood atop his throne of carnage, his consciousness expanded, sweeping across the battlefield. He saw them, the Reincarnator and the Apostles.</p>
<p>They were throwing everything they had at his golems. Magic flared, divine light clashed with earthen fury, and the air hummed with the desperate energy of a dying world.</p>
<p>He watched as the golems began to fail. The Apostles were powerful, their strikes cracking the stone titans, their light searing through the magical essence that held the golems together.</p>
<p>One by one, the massive constructs began to crumble, falling in heaps of lifeless rock. The tide was turning.</p>
<p>The "heroes" were overwhelming his army.</p>
<p>But Rex didn’t feel fear. He felt a dark, intoxicating anticipation.</p>
<p>He pulled his consciousness back, withdrawing from the golems and surging into his true, physical form. The earth groaned and buckled as Tremor manifested.</p>
<p>He didn’t just appear; he erupted from the ground, a towering, muscular god of tectonic fury, his tan skin glistening with the sweat of battle and the blood of his enemies.</p>
<p>He stepped into the center of the fray, his presence so heavy it felt like the gravity itself had increased. He walked straight toward the epicenter of the struggle, where Apollo and Kaelira were struggling to stand.</p>
<p>They were a wreck. Kaelira, once a vision of divine grace, was barely recognizable. Her armor was shattered, her skin was a map of deep, bleeding lacerations, and one of her arms hung limp and useless at her side.</p>
<p>Apollo was breathing in ragged, wet gasps, his divine light flickering like a dying candle.</p>
<p>Rex stood before them, a massive, dark silhouette against the chaos. He looked down at their broken forms, at the blood pooling around their feet, and a sound began to rise from his chest.</p>
<p>It wasn’t a roar. It was a laugh.</p>
<p>A deep, booming, melodic, and utterly deranged laugh that echoed even across the entire Underlayer. It was the laugh of a man who had watched the world burn and found the flames beautiful.</p>
<p>"HAHAHAHAHAHA! LOOK AT YOU!" Rex bellowed, his voice vibrating in their very bones. He leaned down, his massive, muscular frame casting a shadow that seemed to swallow them whole. "The great Apollo! The Berserker Kaelira!"</p>
<p>Look at the ’saviors’ of this world! You’re nothing but broken toys!"</p>
<p>"You’re bleeding, you’re dying, and you’re utterly, fucking hopeless!"</p>
<p>He stood tall, his eyes burning with a manic, godlike light, his laughter drowning out the sounds of the dying world around him.</p>
<p>"HAHAHAHA! WELCOME TO MY WORLD! WELCOME TO THE AGE OF TREMOR!"</p>
