<p>CREAK!</p>
<p>He felt the tension in his bones, the terrifying sensation of her will battling against his own mass. "You’re not attacking my defenses directly, and you’re not trying to break my skin or crush my bones with raw force."</p>
<p>A predatory, appreciative grin broke through his strained expression. "You’re attacking the thing that lets me see you coming."</p>
<p>"You’re blinding my foresight by warping the very light it relies on!"</p>
<p>"That’s fifty years of insight if I’ve ever seen one, Headmaster!"</p>
<p>The compression intensified. A piece of jagged masonry, caught in the perimeter of the field, was accelerated toward Rex like a railgun slug.</p>
<p>WHIZZ BOOM!</p>
<p>It struck his shoulder with a sickening "CRUNCH!" the impact sending a spray of crimson blood across the grey stone. Rex didn’t flinch, though the bone groaned under the sudden, violent stress.</p>
<p>Valentina’s eyes remained fixed on him, glowing with a fierce, violet luminescence that seemed to pierce through the very distortion she had created. She looked less like a mage and more like a conductor directing a symphony of destruction.</p>
<p>"I told you," Valentina said, her voice cutting through the roar of the collapsing space with chilling, razor-sharp clarity. "Angles, Tremor..."</p>
<p>"You’re not the only one who pays attention to them."</p>
<p>The air in the courtyard was no longer a gas; it had become a pressurized, screaming medium of violence. The convergent field was a hungry maw, pulling everything toward that invisible center with a relentless, structural greed.</p>
<p>Rex felt the strain in his very marrow. To maintain his anchor, he was pouring massive amounts of mana into the [Earthen Authority], but the spatial compression was fighting him, trying to fold his very existence into a single point.</p>
<p>He realized with a cold, calculating clarity that his [Foresight] was still flickering, the light paths bending like warped glass.</p>
<p>’If I can’t see the strike, the strike will kill me,’ he thought.</p>
<p>He made a split-second tactical decision. He reached into the core of his [Elemental Mastery], pulling the air working upward to create a localized, high-frequency atmospheric stabilization.</p>
<p>He was essentially "smoothing out" the medium of the air around him to counteract the compression’s warping effect.</p>
<p>VREEEEEEEEE!</p>
<p>The light paths stabilized. The "vision" returned. But the cost was immediate and brutal. To stabilize the air, he had to divert a massive portion of his energy reserves away from his [Gravity Manipulation] and the constant, high-speed data stream of the [Golem Relay].</p>
<p>For a heartbeat, his defensive aura flickered. And for another heartbeat, he was vulnerable.</p>
<p>Valentina saw the shift. She didn’t just notice it; she felt the microscopic dip in the gravitational pressure.</p>
<p>SHHHHH BOOOOM!</p>
<p>She unleashed the second work. It wasn’t a single spell but a terrifyingly efficient combined elemental application.</p>
<p>She wove frost and kinetic force into a single, lance-like projectile of compressed matter. It wasn’t as raw or as massive as Rex’s full potential output, but it was something far more dangerous: it was perfect.</p>
<p>She was producing eighty percent of his raw power with only half the resource requirement, a feat of pure, distilled magical physics honed by fifty years of relentless refinement.</p>
<p>The elemental lance slammed into Rex’s chest.</p>
<p>CRACK! THUD!</p>
<p>The sound was sickening: the wet, heavy thud of magical force meeting muscle, followed by the sharp, terrifying "SNAP" of a rib fracturing under the pressure. A spray of bright, arterial red erupted from his mouth, splattering the stones in front of him.</p>
<p>The force sent him skidding backward, his boots carving deep, jagged trenches into the earth as he fought to keep from being folded in half.</p>
<p>Rex spat a mouthful of blood, the copper taste filling his mouth, and wiped his chin with the back of a hand. His eyes, however, weren’t filled with pain but with a dark, manic delight.</p>
<p>He was bleeding, his ribs were screaming, but he was laughing.</p>
<p>"You’re efficient," Rex rasped, his voice thick with blood but steady with admiration.</p>
<p>He adjusted his stance, his gravity aura surging back to life to stabilize his broken form. "Genuinely..."</p>
<p>"I don’t say that lightly, Headmaster..." Rex grinned. "Most people with abilities like yours... they just get bigger."</p>
<p>"They also get louder and they never bother to refine the blade past the point of being ’effective.’"</p>
<p>He took a step forward, the blood dripping from his chin onto the scorched earth. "But you?"</p>
<p>"You went past ’effective’ a long time ago..." Rex started to do a little stretch. "You’ve turned your magic into a scalpel."</p>
<p>Valentina’s face remained a mask of focused, lethal intent, though her breathing was heavy, her mana swirling in violent, exhausted eddies.</p>
<p>"Save the compliments, Tremor," she snapped, her voice a whip of frost and fire. "I didn’t come here to be appraised by a monster."</p>
<p>"I don’t need your validation."</p>
<p>Rex’s grin widened, becoming something truly unhinged, a predator admiring the sharpness of the teeth that were trying to tear him apart.</p>
<p>"Oh, they’re not for you," Rex said, his eyes gleaming with a terrifying, intellectual hunger. "They’re for me."</p>
<p>"I like understanding exactly what I’m fighting."</p>
<p>"It makes the sensation of breaking it so much more... enjoyable."</p>
<p>The air didn’t just vibrate; it shattered.</p>
<p>Rex didn’t wait for her to reset. He lunged, his body a blur of kinetic energy as he ignited the [Earthen Authority] beneath his feet.</p>
<p>BOOM!</p>
<p>The ground exploded outward as he propelled himself forward like a cannonball, his fist encased in a dense, rotating sphere of hypercompressed granite.</p>
<p>Valentina didn’t flinch. As Rex closed the distance, she whispered a single, guttural syllable that seemed to pull the very heat from the air.</p>
<p>VREEE KRA!</p>
<p>She didn’t block his punch; she altered the space in front of it. A [Spatial Shear] manifested a razor-thin plane of distorted reality that acted like a microscopic guillotine.</p>
<p>Rex’s granite fist hit the plane, and instead of a blunt impact, there was a sickening "SHLICK!" The compressed stone was sliced clean through, the force of the impact sending a spray of grey dust and pulverized rock flying like shrapnel. The edge of the shear caught Rex’s forearm, slicing a shallow but stinging red line across his tanned skin.</p>
<p>"Not quite there yet!" Rex roared, his voice a mixture of pain and exhilaration.</p>
<p>He spun in midair, using his momentum to transition into a gravitational sweep.</p>
<p>WHIRRRR THOOM!</p>
<p>He slammed his hand toward the ground, attempting to create a localized gravity well to pin her to the earth.</p>
<p>Valentina responded by showcasing the true, terrifying breadth of her mastery. She wasn’t just a mage; she was a weaver of the fundamental forces.</p>
<p>"AETHERIC LATTICE: PHASE ONE!" she commanded.</p>
<p>Suddenly, the very air around her crystallized. Not into ice, but into shimmering, translucent geometric structures of pure mana. These were [Magical Energy Crystalline Geodes], floating fractals of solidified magic.</p>
<p>As Rex’s gravity well tried to pull her down, the geodes acted as anchors, spreading the gravitational load across a dozen different points in space, rendering his technique nearly useless.</p>
<p>"Is this the best the ’Underlayer’ can offer?" Valentina’s voice rose, echoing with a strange, multi-tonal resonance. "Let me show you why the heavens tremble at my name!"</p>
<p>She began a rapid-fire sequence of high-tier workings, a choreography of destruction that left Rex struggling to even process the data.</p>
<p>"FLUX STRIKE! "</p>
<p>She flicked her wrist, and one of the floating geodes accelerated toward Rex, not as a projectile, but as a localized distortion. It didn’t hit him; it passed through his defensive aura, momentarily reversing the polarity of his gravity.</p>
<p>CRUNCH!</p>
<p>Rex felt his own weight suddenly double, then triple, slamming his knees into the cracked pavement with a bone-jarring thud.</p>
<p>THUD!</p>
<p>"KINETIC REVERSION!"</p>
<p>As Rex tried to push himself up, Valentina snapped her fingers. The kinetic energy he was using to rise was instantly harvested and redirected.</p>
<p>The energy flowed back into him, not as a push, but as a violent, internal shockwave.</p>
<p>KABOOM!</p>
<p>Rex’s eyes bulged as the force erupted from his own chest, throwing him backward. A thick spray of crimson painted the ground as he coughed, his lungs feeling crushed by a hydraulic press.</p>
<p>"Hah... hah..." Rex wiped the blood from his lips, his eyes dancing with a manic, terrifying light. "Yes! Yes! Show me more! Show me the limit of the world!"</p>
<p>Valentina didn’t answer with words. She entered the final stage of her grand design.</p>
<p>The violet light around her intensified until it was blinding, a supernova contained within a human silhouette.</p>
<p>"SINGULARITY VEIL: THE SEVENTH GEOMETRY!"</p>
<p>The battlefield transformed. The ground beneath them ceased to be solid; it became a swirling sea of violet energy and fractured space.</p>
<p>Massive pillars of crystalline energy erupted from the earth, lancing toward the sky like the ribs of a dead god.</p>
<p>SHRRRRRR KRAAAAA!</p>
<p>She moved through the chaos like a phantom. She wasn’t running; she was folding space to step from one point to another.</p>
<p>She appeared in front of Rex in a flash of violet light. Her hand was outstretched, her fingers curled as if grasping the very essence of his soul.</p>
<p>"VOID POINT COLLAPSE!"</p>
<p>She struck his chest, but there was no physical contact. Instead, a point of absolute nothingness erupted at the site of the impact.</p>
<p>VREEEEE POP!</p>
<p>The sound was a vacuum, a sudden, violent implosion. The air was sucked into the point, and Rex felt his very ribs groan, the bone structure creaking under the impossible pressure of a miniature black hole.</p>
<p>CRACK! SNAP!</p>
<p>A rib gave way, the sharp pain a white-hot needle in his side.</p>
<p>Rex was sent hurtling through a crystalline pillar, the structure shattering into a million glowing shards as he passed through it.</p>
<p>KRA SHAAAA!</p>
