<p>Rex did not wake gradually. There was no slow drift from the abyss of unconsciousness, no hazy transition from the realm of dreams to the waking world.</p>
<p>Instead, he burst into consciousness the way a diver breaks the surface of a dark, pressurized ocean at terminal velocity, the air hitting his lungs before his brain had even realized it was thirsty.</p>
<p>For a frantic, agonizing three seconds, his nervous system revolted. His body underwent a violent, involuntary spasm, a reflexive twitch of a man who had been physically dismantled and reassembled by a cosmic sledgehammer.</p>
<p>It was the visceral, shuddering reaction of a predator whose cells were still screaming from the trauma, a delayed reaction of the Infinite Regeneration attempting to patch the microscopic tears in his muscle fibers and the bruising of his very soul before his mind could even grasp his location.</p>
<p>His hands slammed flat against the ground, his fingers clawing at the floor as his lungs burned with the sudden, desperate need for oxygen. His breathing was shallow, rhythmic, and terrifyingly controlled—the calculated respiration of a warrior who knew that a single gasp of panic was a wasted resource, a leak in the vessel of his survival.</p>
<p>’Where... where is the heat?’</p>
<p>’Where is the scent of her?’ His inner voice was a low, guttural growl, still thick with the afterglow of the madness he had shared with Elizabeth.</p>
<p>The surface beneath his palms was not the soft, sweat-soaked silk of the guest room. It was cold, unyielding, and ancient.</p>
<p>’Warm stone... heh... familiar...’</p>
<p>Rex forced his eyes open.</p>
<p>The domain was familiar, but in a way that felt predatory. It was the kind of familiarity one develops with a beautiful, lethal beast, a space you recognize not by sight, but by the way the air feels against your skin.</p>
<p>The light here was a strange, amber luminescence that seemed to bleed out of the very atmosphere, a source-less glow that cast long, dancing shadows into the vast, unseen corners of the hall. The air was heavy, denser than the world above, pressing against his skin with a comforting, velvet weight that felt less like atmospheric pressure and more like a physical embrace.</p>
<p>’Fucking hell... this is that fucking lustful bitch’s domain.’</p>
<p>He sat up, and the movement triggered a cascade of sensory data that made his teeth ache. The pain wasn’t the sharp, jagged sting of a fresh wound; he could feel his regeneration humming, a low-frequency vibration in his bones as it mended the invisible fractures.</p>
<p>No, this was the heavy, throbbing residue of a system pushed beyond its breaking point. It was the deep, bone-deep ache of a machine that had been overclocked until its gears nearly melted, now recording the sheer magnitude of the load as historical data.</p>
<p>’My head... feels like it was split by a god’s axe,’ he thought, a grimace twisting his handsome, tan features.</p>
<p>He could still feel the phantom sensation of those glowing symbols, the temporal runes, and the ticking clocks burning themselves into his retinas.</p>
<p>As he braced himself, a new sensation began to claw at his attention. It wasn’t the ache in his skull or the stiffness in his thighs; it was a frantic, rhythmic thrumming coming from his left wrist.</p>
<p>The Mark of Lust was behaving erratically. Usually, during a visit to this domain, the Mark was a calm, pulsing ember, a steady reminder of his power.</p>
<p>But now, it was screaming. It was a rapid, insistent, and violent vibration, a staccato rhythm that felt less like a heartbeat and more like a frantic warning signal, a drumbeat of impending doom that had been sounding for an eternity, yet he had been too deafened by pleasure to hear it.</p>
<p>It felt like a trapped bird beating its wings against his skin, desperate to get his attention. It was a warning that the equilibrium had been shattered, that the act of his recent conquest had rippled through the very fabric of the Underlayer.</p>
<p>Rex turned his gaze downward, his eyes narrowing as he focused on the glowing, frantic sigil.</p>
<p>He looked at it.</p>
<p>The Mark of Lust was no longer the comforting, rhythmic glow of a steady flame. The usual warm, honeyed gold had bled away, replaced by a violent, frantic, almost blinding white light. It was a cold, sterile radiance the kind of light a star emits just before it collapses under its own gravity.</p>
<p>It looked like a fire that had turned inward, consuming itself, burning through something it was never designed to touch. It wasn’t just pulsing; it was stabbing at his skin, a jagged, electric sensation that made his teeth rattle.</p>
<p>’Fuck,’ Rex thought, his jaw tightening until the muscles in his temples bulged. ’What the hell did I do?’</p>
<p>’Did that bitch Elizabeth’s reaction trigger a goddamn feedback loop?’</p>
<p>The ambient light of the domain began to thicken, swirling like cream in coffee, coalescing into a shape that was both beautiful and terrifying. Lustia didn’t just "enter" the room; she manifested, her essence weaving itself out of the very shadows and golden hues of her realm.</p>
<p>She stepped into the light, her presence commanding the space, but as her eyes met his, Rex felt a jolt of genuine unease.</p>
<p>She looked worried.</p>
<p>It wasn’t the practiced, ethereal concern of a goddess looking down upon a mortal apostle, the kind of soft, performative pity used to soothe a child. This was raw.</p>
<p>This was the frantic, exhausted worry of a commander who had been holding a crumbling dam together with her bare hands, watching the water rise until her fingers bled. There was a heaviness in her gaze, a shadow of fatigue that even her divine aura couldn’t fully mask.</p>
<p>"Thank fucking lust... you are finally back here," she said, her voice lacking its usual melodic perfection, sounding instead like a weary sigh.</p>
<p>"Clearly," Rex snapped.</p>
<p>His voice was a jagged wreck, a low, gravelly rasp that felt like he’d been swallowing broken glass. He sat up fully, the movement causing a sharp, biting ache in his spine, a reminder of the temporal trauma he’d endured.</p>
<p>"Don’t state the obvious, you fucking bitch."</p>
<p>"It’s fucking annoying." He wiped a hand across his face, his eyes burning with a restless, predatory energy. "How long? Give it to me straight NOW."</p>
<p>"Long enough that pulling your consciousness into this realm required more effort than the laws of this domain should allow," Lustia replied, her eyes searching his face. "The external influence on the island... it was tearing at the fabric of the connection."</p>
<p>"It was making the bridge between your world and mine unstable, like a rope fraying under a mountain’s weight..."</p>
<p>"I have been fighting to keep you anchored."</p>
<p>Rex felt a surge of irritation, a hot prickle of anger beneath his skin. "Fighting...? Fighting what?"</p>
<p>"The fucking wind?" He stood up, his massive frame casting a long, intimidating shadow against the warm stone. "Give me a fucking number, Lustia."</p>
<p>"I didn’t come here for a goddamn lecture on the weather."</p>
<p>"How long have I been out?!"</p>
<p>"AND WHAT’S GOING ON!?"</p>
<p>"I do not have a clean number to give you, Rex!" she countered, her voice rising with a rare flash of frustration. "And that is not an evasion."</p>
<p>"It is the goddamn truth! Time is no longer a straight line here."</p>
<p>"It is a shattered mirror... Since the moment you... since the moment the chaos began, the flow has been erratic."</p>
<p>"It has been moving even less predictably than usual."</p>
<p>Rex let out a sharp, cynical bark of a laugh, though there was no humor in it. He paced the stone floor, his heavy footsteps echoing like hammer blows.</p>
<p>’Fuck’s sake... I go in for a fucking good time and wake up in a temporal nightmare...’</p>
<p>"So let me get this straight," Rex growled, turning back to her, his eyes flashing with a dangerous, pissed-off light. "You dragged me into your own sanctuary, into your fucking home, and you’re telling me you can’t even tell me how much time has bled away while I was unconscious?"</p>
<p>"You’ve got the power of a goddess, and you’re as lost as a blind man in a storm?"</p>
<p>Lustia stepped toward him, her expression softening but her eyes remaining intense. "Hear me out just for a sec..."</p>
<p>"I can tell you that to me, it felt like the passage of most of a night," she said, her voice dropping to a serious, weighted tone. "But I can also tell you that even that feeling is a lie."</p>
<p>"The sensation of time is unreliable right now, Rex."</p>
<p>"The very foundation of ’duration’ is warping. And that is exactly what you need to understand before we go any further."</p>
