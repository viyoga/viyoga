<p>The air in the plaza grew thick with the scent of ozone and the heavy, metallic aroma of blood. The rhythm of the fight shifted. It was no longer three separate engagements; it was a synchronized symphony of destruction.</p>
<p>The sisters were no longer fighting as individuals; they were a single, multiheaded hydra of lethal intent.</p>
<p>"HRAAAAH!" Aurelia let out a primal roar, her aura flaring into a blinding, golden corona.</p>
<p>She didn’t just lunge; she surged. She transitioned from a direct assault into a devastating flurry of high-tier brawling.</p>
<p>Her fists became a blur of kinetic energy, each strike accompanied by the sound of a sonic boom.</p>
<p>BOOM! BOOM! BOOM!</p>
<p>She unleashed a heavy combination: a gut-wrenching liver blow followed by a sweeping roundhouse kick that carried enough force to shatter a granite pillar.</p>
<p>Rex felt the impact of the liver shot.</p>
<p>"OOF!" The air was driven from his lungs, and for a split second, his vision clouded with white heat.</p>
<p>A grin, sharp and jagged, split his face. He wasn’t a masochist, but he was a man who lived for the friction of life, and this was the ultimate friction. The pain was a teacher, a sharp, electric reminder that he was truly alive.</p>
<p>He ducked under Aurelia’s sweeping kick, the wind of her movement whistling past his ear with a terrifying WHOOSH!, but he didn’t move away. He moved into the opening.</p>
<p>That was the trap.</p>
<p>As Rex stepped into Aurelia’s guard to deliver a counterstrike, Iris materialized from the periphery like a ghost woven from shadows. She didn’t just strike; she flowed.</p>
<p>Using the momentum of Aurelia’s heavy lunge as a smokescreen, Iris launched herself upward, her body a coiled spring of lethal grace.</p>
<p>SHING!</p>
<p>Iris’s daggers were no longer just tools; they were extensions of her very soul. She moved with a terrifying, silent speed that contrasted Aurelia’s thunderous violence.</p>
<p>She aimed for the carotid artery, her movement a surgical line of intent.</p>
<p>Rex’s Foresight screamed. The world slowed.</p>
<p>He saw the golden arc of Aurelia’s fist coming for his jaw and the silver flash of Iris’s blade aiming for his throat.</p>
<p>"GRRR..." Rex gritted his teeth, a low sound of intense focus.</p>
<p>He couldn’t dodge both. He chose to absorb.</p>
<p>He pivoted his torso at the last microsecond, a feat of impossible core strength. Aurelia’s massive fist caught him squarely in the ribs.</p>
<p>CRACK! THUD!</p>
<p>The sound of his ribs groaning under the pressure was sickening, a dull, wet snap that sent a jolt of white-hot agony through his nervous system. He felt the bruise forming instantly, the internal pressure mounting.</p>
<p>But the pivot worked. It tilted his head just enough.</p>
<p>Iris’s blade hissed past his ear, so close he could feel the cold steel’s temperature.</p>
<p>SST!</p>
<p>The tip of the dagger grazed his cheek, carving a thin, red line that wept blood down his jaw.</p>
<p>"Too slow!" Rex barked, a laugh bubbling up through the pain.</p>
<p>He didn’t wait for them to reset. He slammed his palm into the ground.</p>
<p>"EARTHTHROB!"</p>
<p>The ground beneath the sisters didn’t just shake; it buckled and surged upward in a violent, jagged wave of stone.</p>
<p>KRRR SHOOOM!</p>
<p>The sudden upheaval forced them to break their synchronization; the sudden loss of footing disrupted the perfect flow of their sisterly coordination.</p>
<p>Aurelia stumbled, her heavy footing betrayed by the shifting earth, and Iris was forced to leap backward to maintain her balance.</p>
<p>Rex stood in the center of the chaos, his chest heaving slightly, blood dripping from his chin and his side. His ribs were screaming, and his cheek was stinging, but his eyes were burning with a terrifying, ecstatic light.</p>
<p>He wasn’t just fighting them; he was savoring the struggle.</p>
<p>"That’s it!" Rex shouted, his voice echoing against the ruined walls of the plaza. "Don’t hold back!"</p>
<p>"Give me everything you’ve got! Show me the strength of the blood you share!"</p>
<p>He raised his hands, and the very gravity in the crater began to pulse like a dying heart.</p>
<p>THUMP... THUMP...</p>
<p>The pressure increased, making the blood in their veins feel heavy, pulling them back toward the center of his killing field. The real slaughter was only just beginning.</p>
<p>...</p>
<p>The plaza had transcended the realm of a battlefield; it was now a churning cauldron of elemental fury and human grit. The air was so thick with dust and the metallic scent of blood that it felt like breathing liquid iron.</p>
<p>Aurelia was the first to recover from the earth’s upheaval. She didn’t just regain her footing; she reclaimed it with a roar of defiance.</p>
<p>"HRAAAAAH!" She surged forward, her golden aura intensifying until it looked like molten sunlight.</p>
<p>She wasn’t just a brawler anymore; she was a storm of kinetic devastation.</p>
<p>She unleashed a "Meteor Hammer" sequence, a brutal, continuous chain of heavy impact strikes.</p>
<p>BOOM! CRACK! WHAM!</p>
<p>Her fists moved in a rhythmic, pulverizing cadence. She drove a heavy left hook toward Rex’s temple, followed instantly by a crushing straight right to his solar plexus.</p>
<p>Rex parried the hook with a forearm block. CLANG! The sound of bone meeting concentrated energy, but the sheer momentum of the second strike caught him in the gut.</p>
<p>"OOF!" He was lifted inches off the ground, the air whistling out of his lungs in a ragged burst.</p>
<p>But Rex was a predator who thrived in the teeth of the storm. As he was pushed back, he didn’t retreat; he spun.</p>
<p>He used the force of the gut shot to fuel a centrifugal rotation, his heavy boot sweeping out in a wide, punishing arc.</p>
<p>WHOOSH THUD!</p>
<p>The kick caught Aurelia in the midsection, a heavy, blunt impact that forced her to skid backward across the grit.</p>
<p>"NOW, IRIS!" Aurelia bellowed, her voice a command that bridged the gap between sisters.</p>
<p>Iris didn’t need the prompt. She was already there.</p>
<p>She moved through the dust like a needle through silk, her silhouette blurring as she utilized a "shadow step" technique. She wasn’t running; she was flickering in and out of existence.</p>
<p>FIP! FIP! FIP!</p>
<p>Rex’s foresight was working overtime, his mind a frantic, high-speed processor. He saw the golden trail of Aurelia’s recovery and the silver shimmer of Iris’s approach.</p>
<p>He was being squeezed between a hammer and an anvil.</p>
<p>Iris launched a "Thousand Sting" flurry. Her daggers became a shimmering web of steel.</p>
<p>SHLICK! SHLICK! SHLICK!</p>
<p>The blades whistled past Rex’s throat, his ribs, and his thighs. He was forced into a desperate, high-speed defensive dance, his hands moving in a blur of martial arts mastery to deflect the razor-sharp edges.</p>
<p>TING! TING! CLACK!</p>
<p>Each deflection sent a vibration of stinging pain up his arms, a frantic staccato of near misses.</p>
<p>"You’re dancing on a razor’s edge, Tremor!" Iris hissed, her voice appearing from everywhere and nowhere at once.</p>
<p>"Then let’s see if you can cut me!" Rex countered.</p>
<p>He slammed his hands together. "GRAVITY WELL!"</p>
<p>A sudden, violent spike in gravitational pressure erupted in a sphere around him.</p>
<p>VREEEEE!</p>
<p>The sudden increase in weight acted like a physical barrier, momentarily slowing Iris’s lightning-quick lunge and forcing Aurelia to brace her entire body to keep from being crushed into the dirt.</p>
<p>Taking advantage of that microsecond of hesitation, Rex went on the offensive. He lunged at Aurelia, his fist shrouded in a dense, earthen coating that made it as hard as diamond.</p>
<p>KRA THOOM!</p>
<p>The punch connected with her shoulder, a sickening "CRUNCH" echoing through the plaza as the impact shook her entire frame.</p>
<p>Aurelia grunted, her teeth bared in a snarl of pain, but she didn’t back down. She caught his arm, her fingers digging into his muscle like iron clamps.</p>
<p>"GOT YOU!"</p>
<p>She pulled him inward, intending to deliver a devastating headbutt, but Rex was ready. He tilted his head, letting her forehead slam into his cheekbone with a wet</p>
<p>THWACK!</p>
<p>The pain was exquisite, a blinding flash of white that made his ears ring, but it allowed him to get close enough to her face.</p>
<p>SPLAT!</p>
<p>He drove a short, brutal elbow into her nose, breaking it instantly. Blood sprayed across both their faces, a crimson mist in the moonlight.</p>
<p>As Aurelia recoiled, clutching her face, Iris struck from above. She had used the distraction to leap off a rising pillar of stone.</p>
<p>SREEEEEE!</p>
<p>She descended like a falling star, her daggers held in a reverse grip, aimed straight for the gap in Rex’s guard.</p>
<p>Rex didn’t look up. He felt the shift in the air, and he felt the weight of her descent.</p>
<p>CRUNCH!</p>
<p>He slammed his heel into the ground, causing a jagged spike of obsidian to erupt directly beneath Iris’s landing zone. It didn’t hit her, but it forced her to twist in midair to avoid being impaled.</p>
<p>The sudden, unnatural rotation threw her off her lethal trajectory.</p>
<p>Rex turned, his eyes wild and glowing with a terrifying, primal energy. He was covered in blood, his own and theirs; his clothes were torn; his ribs ached with every breath; his skin was bruised and battered.</p>
<p>But he wasn’t defeated. He looked like a man who had finally found a worthy way to die, or a god who had finally discovered a reason to kill.</p>
<p>"Is that all?" Rex roared, his voice a guttural, earth-shaking sound that seemed to come from the very core of the planet. "THE SISTERS OF THE BLADE AND THE HAMMER? SHOW ME THE REAL YOU! SHOW ME THE BLOOD THAT MAKES YOU LEGENDS!"</p>
<p>He raised both hands, and the ground began to liquefy, turning into a swirling vortex of sand and stone. The very atmosphere began to groan under the weight of his escalating power.</p>
<p>The true climax was approaching, and the plaza was ready to swallow them all.</p>
