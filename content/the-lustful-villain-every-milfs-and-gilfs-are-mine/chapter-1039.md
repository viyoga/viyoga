<p>Rex rolled his shoulders, feeling the satisfying click of vertebrae realigning, and summoned the system interface. The translucent blue display flickered to life before his eyes, casting a cold glow across his sharp features. His first priority was assessing his war chest.</p>
<p>[Current Energy Balance: 50,000 points (Lustia’s bonus) + base encounter accumulation]</p>
<p>Three days ago, that number had appeared like a thunderclap. Lustia hadn’t visited his dream domain this time; she hadn’t bothered with the pleasantries of a full manifestation.</p>
<p>Instead, she had bypassed the passive communication channels and forced the message directly through the Mark, using the specific, urgent frequency she reserved for things that were too brief for a visit but too monumental to ignore.</p>
<p>Her message had been terse, almost reverent: "I counted... You are insane. Here is what insane earns."</p>
<p>Fifty thousand points. A staggering sum that represented more than just currency—it was a reward for audacity, a tribute to his willingness to tear apart the established order of Erosyne.</p>
<p>He had stared at that number for thirty seconds, not in shock, but in cold calculation. He didn’t celebrate victories; he weaponized them.</p>
<p>"Time to invest," he muttered, his voice low and hungry.</p>
<p>He bypassed the standard purchases and dove straight into the skill catalog. He had two slots open, two opportunities to rewrite his fundamental nature without spending a single point of his accumulated energy.</p>
<p>These were the rewards from the pregnancies of Aurelia and Iris, the physical manifestation of his dominion over the Nightwing bloodline.</p>
<p>His fingers danced across the interface, navigating past trinkets and minor buffs. His mind was already locked onto the two skills he had identified as his absolute necessities since his encounter with Lustia’s domain.</p>
<p>[FULL POTENTIAL ADAPTATION FULL POTENTIAL]</p>
<p>The description carried a weight that seemed to distort the very air around him. This wasn’t just a defense; it was evolutionary supremacy.</p>
<p>[ON FIRST CONTACT WITH ANY HOSTILE ABILITY, ENVIRONMENTAL EFFECT, OR DIVINE WORKING, THE SYSTEM ANALYZES THE MECHANISM AND INTEGRATES A COMPLETE DEFENSE AGAINST IT.]</p>
<p>[ALL SUBSEQUENT CONTACTS WITH THE SAME OR SIMILAR MECHANISMS ARE FULLY NEGATED.]</p>
<p>[ADAPTATION IS PERMANENT, CUMULATIVE, AND DOES NOT REQUIRE ACTIVE ATTENTION.]</p>
<p>Rex read through the terrifying scope of the skill. It covered everything: elemental magic, forbidden divine expressions, spatial distortions, and most importantly, soul-level workings that bypassed biological defenses.</p>
<p>The temporal clause sent a cold shiver of satisfaction through him: ’Time Stop, Time Loop, Time Reset all negated after first contact.’</p>
<p>’It won’t make me invincible immediately,’ he thought, a predatory smile touching his lips. ’The first hit will still hurt.’</p>
<p>’The first strike will still draw blood. But the second strike?’</p>
<p>’The second strike will be meaningless.’</p>
<p>Combined with his [Infinite Regeneration], he was becoming a paradox: a being who could endure any atrocity only to become immune to it.</p>
<p>[FREE SELECTION APPLIED NO COST]</p>
<p>[SKILL ACQUIRED: FULL POTENTIAL ADAPTATION]</p>
<p>The integration was instantaneous. He felt a subtle shift in his cellular structure, a new layer of metaphysical armor settling over his soul.</p>
<p>"Next."</p>
<p>He checked his inventory. His previous free selection, which he earned through Elizabeth’s pregnancy, had already granted him the skill [Dimensional Manipulation].</p>
<p>That gap was closed. He needed to find the second piece of the puzzle, the skill that would allow him to see the threats before they could even manifest.</p>
<p>He scoured the catalog, his patience thinning. There was nothing else at this tier that offered the strategic advantage he required.</p>
<p>He already had [Presence Manipulation] to control the battlefield and [Dimensional Manipulation] to control space.</p>
<p>Now, he needed to control information. He needed to see through the lies of gods.</p>
<p>There.</p>
<p>[DIVINE PERCEPTION FULL POTENTIAL]</p>
<p>[COMPLETE AWARENESS OF DIVINE SIGNATURES, DESIGNATIONS, WORKINGS, AND PRESENCES WITHIN RANGE.]</p>
<p>[PASSIVE FUNCTION: CONTINUOUS READ OF ALL DIVINE CLASS ACTIVITY IN THE ENVIRONMENT.]</p>
<p>[ACTIVE FUNCTION: TARGETED ANALYSIS OF A SPECIFIC DIVINE PRESENCE OR WORKING TO PRODUCE FULL OPERATIONAL INTELLIGENCE.]</p>
<p>The implications were staggering. This would allow him to track Apostle designations, identify patron signatures clinging to enemies, and most crucially detect temporal workings regardless of whatever masking techniques they employed.</p>
<p>No god could hide from him. No assassin could approach unseen.</p>
<p>He would see the ripple in reality before the blade even moved.</p>
<p>[RANGE: PASSIVE 500M, ACTIVE UNLIMITED WITHIN LINE OF SIGHT OR ESTABLISHED TRACKING ANCHOR.]</p>
<p>[COVERS: APOSTLE DESIGNATIONS, PATRON SIGNATURES, DIVINE DOMAINS, AND TEMPORAL WORKINGS.]</p>
<p>This was the eye of the storm. This was the ability to read the blueprint of the universe while everyone else was still trying to understand the rules.</p>
<p>[FREE SELECTION APPLIED NO COST]</p>
<p>[SKILL ACQUIRED: DIVINE PERCEPTION]</p>
<p>As the skill locked in, Rex felt his consciousness expand. The world didn’t change visually, but his perception of it fractured into a thousand new layers of data.</p>
<p>The air itself felt thick with invisible signatures, the faint hum of divine energies vibrating in the space between atoms.</p>
<p>He was no longer walking through a world of flesh and stone; he was walking through a web of power, and for the first time, he could see every single strand.</p>
<p>The integration of [Divine Perception] didn’t just change how Rex saw the world; it shattered his understanding of reality. It was as if he had lived his entire life in a silent film, only to suddenly be thrust into a roaring, multidimensional symphony of power.</p>
<p>The world around him didn’t just become richer; it became terrifyingly transparent. The invisible threads of divinity that stitched the fabric of Erosyne together were suddenly laid bare, glowing with a cold, analytical clarity that rivaled his own emotional insight.</p>
<p>He didn’t just see the Nightwing household; he read its divine signature like a text. He could feel the ancient, consecrated weight of the estate’s protection, a stubborn claim of ownership written in the language of the gods.</p>
<p>His gaze shifted outward, deeper. His mind peeled back layers of reality to read Aethelgard itself.</p>
<p>He saw the ambient divine presence, the accumulated, suffocating weight of eight hundred years of divine maintenance woven into the island’s foundational substrate.</p>
<p>It was a thick, invisible atmosphere of sanctified power. Running through it like veins of molten light, he identified the specific signatures of the Apostle network: distinct, colored threads of patron deities, each one a tether connecting mortal puppets to their celestial masters.</p>
<p>Then, he caught something darker. A scar in the divine register.</p>
<p>The residue of Kaeleth’s temporal working flickered in his vision, a ghostly, distorted fossil clinging to the island’s divine layer. Even after Lustia’s counter frequency had attempted to scrub it clean, the wound remained, a fading echo of something that had once torn at the throat of time itself.</p>
<p>’Everything leaves a trace,’ Rex thought, a cold smile touching his lips. ’Nothing is truly hidden anymore.’</p>
<p>With his new senses humming in his skull, Rex turned and walked toward the household’s outer gate.</p>
<p>The morning possessed that peculiar, melancholic quality of a month’s final day—the same sun, the same breeze, but carrying the subjective weight of an era closing. A month of conquest, of blood, of rewriting destinies.</p>
<p>He exited through the rear gate, stepping into the street that hugged the massive Nightwing outer wall. His boots crunched on the cobblestones as he turned toward the city center.</p>
<p>His mind was already shifting gears; the administrative duties of an acting combat director didn’t vanish simply because a month had passed. Power required maintenance, and he had a city to oversee.</p>
<p>He was approximately forty meters from the household’s main gate when the world suddenly screamed.</p>
<p>It wasn’t a sound; it was a violation.</p>
<p>The passive function of [Divine Perception], running at its baseline environmental read, suddenly spiked. The steady hum of ambient divinity was drowned out by a sharp, violent alarm that tore through his consciousness.</p>
<p>This wasn’t the background noise of history or the fading echo of a past spell. This was something raw.</p>
<p>Something immediate.</p>
<p>Something hungry.</p>
<p>An active divine signature. Not a trace, not a memory, but a live, pulsing presence that was tearing through the local reality.</p>
<p>His pupils dilated as the system’s read flooded his mind with data:</p>
<p>[WARNING: ACTIVE DIVINE SIGNATURE DETECTED]</p>
<p>[DESIGNATION: TEMPORAL]</p>
<p>[STATUS: ACTIVE / MANIFESTING]</p>
<p>The air around him began to vibrate with a nauseating frequency. The light itself seemed to stutter, catching on unseen gears that shouldn’t exist.</p>
<p>Time wasn’t just passing; it was being manipulated, twisted by an external force that was forcing its way into the present moment.</p>
<p>Rex stopped dead in his tracks. His muscles coiled instinctively, his heart rate steady despite the metaphysical earthquake threatening to unbalance his soul.</p>
<p>The tension in the air was so thick it felt like walking through water, each breath becoming a conscious effort against the pressure of a collapsing moment.</p>
<p>’Someone is playing with the clock,’ he thought, his jaw tightening. ’And they aren’t trying to be subtle.’</p>
<p>The hair on his arms stood up as the temporal distortion intensified, the very cobblestones beneath his feet seeming to age and decay in micro seconds before snapping back to their original state. The threat wasn’t approaching; it was already here.</p>
<p>Rex tilted his head back, his eyes narrowing as he looked up into the sky, where the fabric of reality was beginning to fray like burning silk.</p>
