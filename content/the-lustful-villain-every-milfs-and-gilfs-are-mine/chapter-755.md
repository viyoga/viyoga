<p>Apollo didn’t just attack; he detonated.</p>
<p>He abandoned the balanced, measured grace of his canyon training and tapped into something far more primal. He unleashed the Apostle of Life’s designation not as a healer but as a force of pure, unadulterated kinetic devastation.</p>
<p>He moved in a terrifying, overlapping pattern of boundary conditions, a rapid-fire sequence of spatial compressions designed to overwhelm any defense.</p>
<p>WHUMP WHUMP WHUMP!</p>
<p>The air itself seemed to scream as the overlapping fields collided. Zane’s eyes were frantic, his mind a blur of high-speed calculus.</p>
<p>One... two...</p>
<p>He managed to catch the first two overlapping conditions by using a desperate, wide-spectrum void absorption technique. The dark energy he was manipulating flared, swallowing the first two kinetic waves with a sickening intensity.</p>
<p>VWOOM! SHHH!</p>
<p>But the third one... the third one was the killer.</p>
<p>It was the lynchpin of the pattern, a concentrated spike of mass and momentum that Zane’s sluggish cycle couldn’t catch.</p>
<p>CRACK BOOM!</p>
<p>The third condition slammed into Zane’s chest like a falling star. The sound was visceral: the sickening, wet THUD of a heavy impact combined with the sharp, terrifying SNAP of ribs yielding to the pressure.</p>
<p>Zane’s breath was driven from his lungs in a spray of crimson mist.</p>
<p>GURGLE!</p>
<p>He went down hard, one knee slamming into the broken plaza stone with a bone-jarring CRUNCH. Before the stars could even clear from his vision, Apollo was on him.</p>
<p>He didn’t just follow; he descended like a predator.</p>
<p>Apollo drove his weight forward, pinning Zane to the earth. He applied his full Apostle-enhanced mass, pressing the Void working’s available surface area directly into the jagged, unyielding stone.</p>
<p>It was a masterstroke of tactical cruelty. By pinning the spatial field against the ground, Apollo left Zane with no place to escape.</p>
<p>The Void had no room to breathe, no room to expand, and no room to escape.</p>
<p>"Nowhere to go, Zane!" Apollo roared, his voice thick with the heat of the fray.</p>
<p>THWACK!</p>
<p>Apollo delivered a brutal, short-range hammer fist into the side of Zane’s head. Zane’s head snapped to the side, a fresh gout of blood erupting from his ear as the pressure caused a shockwave to pass through his skull.</p>
<p>SKREEEEEE!</p>
<p>Zane roared in a mix of agony and defiance, his fingers clawing at the dirt, trying to find a single millimeter of spatial slack. He managed to trigger a violent, localized void burst directly beneath them.</p>
<p>KABOOM!</p>
<p>The ground erupted in a spray of stone and dust, momentarily breaking the pressure. Zane used the explosion to roll away, but he was stumbling, his movements heavy and uncoordinated.</p>
<p>He was a wounded animal, lurching through a sea of debris.</p>
<p>Apollo didn’t give him a second to recover. He lunged through the settling dust, his hand catching Zane by the collar of his combat gear.</p>
<p>RIP!</p>
<p>With a guttural yell, Apollo swung Zane like a ragdoll, slamming him spine-first into a standing pillar of ruined masonry.</p>
<p>CRACK! THUD!</p>
<p>The sound of the impact was nauseating, the sound of vertebrae compressing under immense force. Zane’s head lolled back, his eyes rolling, and a thin trickle of blood leaked from the corner of his mouth.</p>
<p>"Is this the ’judgment’ you spoke of?" Apollo demanded, his face inches from Zane’s, his eyes burning with a terrifying, holy light.</p>
<p>He grabbed Zane’s forearm, twisting it with a sudden, violent jerk.</p>
<p>POP!</p>
<p>The sound of the shoulder joint partially dislocating was sharp and unmistakable. Zane let out a strangled, guttural scream that tore through the silence of the plaza.</p>
<p>"You thought you could manage the chaos..." Apollo hissed, his grip tightening, his knuckles white. "You thought you could hide the truth in the gaps of your reports... but there are no gaps left, Zane."</p>
<p>"Only the impact."</p>
<p>Apollo pulled back his fist, the energy around it swirling into a dense, vibrating sphere of pure life affinity force.</p>
<p>He wasn’t aiming to heal. He was aiming to shatter.</p>
<p>WHIRRRRRRRRR!</p>
<p>Zane looked up, his vision swimming in red, seeing the impending doom of the Apostle’s strike. He tried to raise his hands, to weave one last, desperate shield, but his muscles were screaming, his energy was spent, and his bones were already broken.</p>
<p>"Fight!" Apollo commanded, a roar of pure, unbridled will. "Don’t you dare die before you tell me the truth!"</p>
<p>SHATTER!</p>
<p>The sphere of energy around Apollo’s fist hummed with a frequency so high it made the very air vibrate, a killing blow designed to turn Zane’s remaining structural integrity into dust. He pulled back, his muscles coiled like a predator’s, ready to unleash the full, devastating weight of the Apostle’s wrath.</p>
<p>WHOOOOOOOOOM!</p>
<p>The pressure in the plaza was at a breaking point, the tension thick enough to choke on. Zane could only stare up, his face a mask of blood and grit, waiting for the final, shattering impact.</p>
<p>"APOLLO! STOP!"</p>
<p>The voice sliced through the roar of the battlefield like a silver bell through a storm. It wasn’t just a shout; it was a command that carried the weight of intimate familiarity and a sudden, sharp pang of heartache.</p>
<p>Apollo’s fist froze mere inches from Zane’s shattered chest. His movement’s sheer momentum caused the mana to ripple outward in a harmless, shimmering wave, but he stayed the killing blow.</p>
<p>SHHHHHHHH!</p>
<p>The violent hum of his energy began to subside. Apollo blinked, the predatory light in his eyes flickering as he turned his head toward the source of the voice. Standing at the edge of the debris, her hair windswept and her eyes wide with a mixture of awe and terror, was Kaelira.</p>
<p>"Don’t go down that path!" she cried out, her voice trembling but resolute. "Apollo, look at him!"</p>
<p>"He’s already broken! If you keep going like this... you won’t be an Apostle of Life anymore!"</p>
<p>"You’ll just be the very thing you swore to destroy!"</p>
<p>Apollo stood paralyzed. The heat of the combat, the adrenaline-fueled rage, and the singular focus on the ’truth’ all began to drain away, replaced by a sudden, jarring sense of self-awareness.</p>
<p>He looked down at his hands, hands that were currently stained with Zane’s blood, hands that had just been seconds away from committing a most brutal execution.</p>
<p>He looked back at Zane, who was slumped against the masonry, gasping for air, his body a roadmap of bruises and fractures. The sight of the man not as a target but as a person hit Apollo with the force of a physical blow.</p>
<p>"A butcher..." Apollo whispered to himself.</p>
<p>The word tasted bitter.</p>
<p>He took a long, shuddering breath, forcing the violent, overlapping patterns of his combat style to dissolve. The jagged, aggressive aura of his life affinity smoothed out, transitioning from a raging forest fire into the steady, warm glow of a sunrise.</p>
<p>FSSSSSSSHHHH...</p>
<p>The heavy, oppressive pressure in the air lifted. Apollo stepped back, giving Zane space, his movements losing their predatory sharpness and regaining a measured, dignified grace.</p>
<p>He didn’t lower his guard; the fight wasn’t over, but the intent had fundamentally shifted.</p>
<p>He reached out, not to strike, but to stabilize. As he moved, he channeled the Apostle’s power differently.</p>
<p>Instead of the crushing, kinetic force he had used to break bones, he projected a soft, rhythmic pulse of energy.</p>
<p>THUM THUM... THUM THUM...</p>
<p>It was the rhythm of a heartbeat.</p>
<p>"Forgive me," Apollo said, his voice no longer a roar but a low, resonant calm.</p>
<p>He moved in closer, his touch now precise and careful. He used his boundary condition not to compress, but to wrap around Zane’s wounded form, providing a stabilizing field that eased the throbbing pain of his broken ribs.</p>
<p>He was no longer trying to shatter the man; he was trying to hold him together long enough to finish the interrogation.</p>
<p>Zane let out a long, pained groan, but the sheer agony had subsided into a dull, manageable ache. He looked up at Apollo, seeing a man who had mastered his own tempest.</p>
<p>"You... you listened," Zane wheezed, a ghost of a smirk touching his bloody lips.</p>
<p>"I heard her," Apollo replied, his eyes softening as he glanced back toward Kaelira, a silent promise of restraint in his gaze.</p>
<p>He leaned in, his presence now steady and grounding. "Now, let’s try this again. And this time, Zane... let’s talk without the bloodshed."</p>
<p>Zane looked up from his battered, kneeling position, his vision swimming in a haze of crimson and violet. Apollo’s hands were clamped onto his shoulders like iron manacles, but the sensation was no longer one of crushing weight.</p>
<p>Instead, it was a searing, overwhelming warmth. The Apostle’s Life affinity field was running at maximum close contact range, a suffocatingly intense cocoon of vitality that seemed to vibrate against Zane’s very skin.</p>
<p>HUMMMMMMMMM...</p>
<p>The energy hummed through Zane’s bones, a rhythmic, pulsing heat that fought against the cold ache of his fractured ribs. He didn’t even attempt to trigger a spatial displacement.</p>
<p>He was too seasoned and too pragmatic to commit a tactical error of that magnitude. At this range, with Apollo’s boundary condition field occupying every cubic centimeter of the immediate space, there was no "elsewhere" to go.</p>
<p>The Void had no room to fold, no vacuum to exploit. Any attempt to displace would be like trying to push a mountain into a pebble; it would simply waste the precious, dwindling reserves of his mana on a geometry that didn’t exist.</p>
<p>Zane swallowed a mouthful of copper-tasting blood and met Apollo’s gaze.</p>
<p>"You planned the three-hit overlap... from the very start of this exchange," Zane rasped.</p>
<p>His voice was a broken thing, a dry rattle in his chest, but the statement was delivered with the chilling precision of a man reading a post-action report. It wasn’t a question; it was an indictment.</p>
