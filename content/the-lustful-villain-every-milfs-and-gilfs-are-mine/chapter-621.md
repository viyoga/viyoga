<p>She lunged, the tip of her blade inches from the center of his mask, her entire weight behind the strike.</p>
<p>That was the moment. The moment Rex stopped playing.</p>
<p>The air around him suddenly grew heavy, the gravity increasing a thousandfold. The laughter died instantly, replaced by a terrifying, silent focus.</p>
<p>As her blade approached, Rex didn’t dodge. He didn’t block. He simply wound back his right fist.</p>
<p>The energy didn’t just gather; it condensed. A sphere of pure, compressed kinetic force formed around his knuckles, glowing with a terrifying, white-hot intensity.</p>
<p>"My turn."</p>
<p>BOOOOOOOOOM!!!</p>
<p>The punch didn’t just land; it erased the space between them.</p>
<p>The impact was a sound beyond comprehension, a thunderclap that felt like the world was splitting in half. The shockwave sent a ring of dust and debris exploding outward for miles.</p>
<p>Cassandra didn’t just fly back; she became a projectile of pure kinetic trauma.</p>
<p>WHIZZZZZZZ BOOOOOOOOM!</p>
<p>She tore through the air like a comet, a blur of red and black, screaming as the sheer force of the blow crushed the air from her lungs. She flew across the battlefield, a streak of light, until she slammed into the massive, jagged mountain range in the distance.</p>
<p>The impact was cataclysmic.</p>
<p>KRA THOOOOOOM!!!</p>
<p>The mountain didn’t just crack; it detonated. A massive explosion of stone, earth, and divine energy erupted from the peak, a mushroom cloud of debris rising into the sky as the very summit of the mountain was pulverized into dust.</p>
<p>As the dust settled, Rex stood in the center of the crater, his fist still smoking, his mask perfectly intact, and a calm, terrifyingly badass aura radiating from his muscular frame. He looked toward the smoking ruins of the mountain, a small, predatory smirk playing on his lips.</p>
<p>"Don’t worry, Cassandra," he called out, his voice cool and steady. "I’ll come pick up the pieces."</p>
<p>The playfulness was dead. The mockery had evaporated, replaced by a cold, suffocating pressure that made the very air feel like lead.</p>
<p>Rex stood in the center of the scorched wasteland, his silhouette dark against the rising dust.</p>
<p>Slowly, deliberately, he raised both hands.</p>
<p>CREEEEEEAK!</p>
<p>The sound of his gauntlets was not just metal on metal; it was the sound of tectonic plates grinding together. As he clenched his fists, the divine energy within the gauntlets hummed with a low, predatory growl.</p>
<p>The air began to ripple around him, distorted by the sheer density of his intent. The "game" was over.</p>
<p>Now... there was only the hunt.</p>
<p>"You’ve had your fun, Cassandra," Rex’s voice drifted across the battlefield, no longer a roar but a calm, terrifying whisper that seemed to vibrate inside her very bones. "Now... let’s see how much you can truly endure."</p>
<p>He didn’t move a muscle, but the world around the shattered mountain began to scream.</p>
<p>VROOOOOOOOOM!</p>
<p>Using his telekinesis, Rex didn’t just pull her; he seized her. From the wreckage of the mountain, Cassandra was yanked forward by invisible, iron-hard chains of psychic force.</p>
<p>She was dragged through the air, her body jerking violently as if being hauled by a cosmic fisherman.</p>
<p>SKREEEEEEEEEEEEE!</p>
<p>"NO! STOP! AAAAAAAAGH!" her scream tore through the sky, a jagged, agonizing sound of a goddess being humbled.</p>
<p>But Rex wasn’t finished. He reached out with his Earth Sovereign power.</p>
<p>CRACK THUD!</p>
<p>The ground beneath her didn’t just move; it became her torturer. Massive, jagged pillars of hard, jagged obsidian rock erupted from the earth, impaling her limbs and pinning her midair.</p>
<p>SQUELCH! CRUNCH!</p>
<p>The sound of stone piercing divine flesh was sickeningly wet. Blood sprayed in violent, rhythmic jets, coating the rising pillars in a glistening, crimson sheen.</p>
<p>Rex clenched his left fist, and the earth began to constrict.</p>
<p>GRRRRIND! SNAP!</p>
<p>The rock pillars began to shrink, tightening around her thighs, her waist, and her arms like the coils of a massive, stony serpent.</p>
<p>"AAAAAAAHHHHHHHH! MY BONES! THEY’RE BREAKING! STOP! PLEASE!" Cassandra’s screams reached a pitch that shattered the remaining stones in the area.</p>
<p>CRACK! POP! CRACKLE!</p>
<p>The sound of her ribs snapping under the pressure of the earth was like a volley of musket fire. Rex watched with cold, unblinking eyes.</p>
<p>He clenched his right fist, and the telekinetic pressure intensified. He began to manipulate the very gravity around her, creating a localized vortex of crushing force.</p>
<p>WHIRRRRRRR BOOM!</p>
<p>She was being squeezed from the outside by the earth and crushed from the inside by the telekinetic weight. Her skin began to bruise instantly, turning a deep, sickly purple before bursting open under the sheer tension.</p>
<p>SPLUTTER!</p>
<p>A geyser of blood erupted from her mouth as her internal organs were compressed. The ground beneath her became a meat grinder; the earth rose in tiny, needle-like shards, vibrating at high frequencies, and began to shred her skin like a thousand microscopic saws.</p>
<p>SHHHHHHHHHH</p>
<p>"GODS, HELP MEEEEEEEE!" she shrieked, her voice breaking into a guttural, sobbing wail.</p>
<p>Her eyes rolled back in her head, the white of her eyes stained red by the pressure.</p>
<p>Rex stepped forward, his boots crunching on the blood-soaked gravel. He looked up at her, a broken, mangled mess of divinity and gore, suspended in a cage of crushing stone and invisible weight.</p>
<p>He clenched both fists one last time, the gauntlets glowing with a blinding, apocalyptic light.</p>
<p>KRA THOOOOOOM!</p>
<p>The pressure spiked to an impossible level. The earth pillars exploded inward, the telekinetic force imploded, and the sound of Cassandra’s final, lung-shattering scream was swallowed by the roar of a thousand breaking bones.</p>
<p>She was a whirlwind of blood, stone, and agony, a masterpiece of suffering created by a man who had finally decided to stop being kind.</p>
<p>"Don’t scream like a bitch now... I still have a lot more~!"</p>
<p>The air didn’t just ripple; it tore.</p>
<p>In a flash of instantaneous, violent displacement, Rex vanished from the ground and reappeared directly above the mangled, suspended form of Cassandra. He didn’t descend; he plummeted like a meteor of pure, unadulterated malice.</p>
<p>BOOOOOOOM!</p>
<p>He brought his heavy, armored boot down with the full weight of his divine mass and telekinetic enhancement. The sound was a sickening, wet CRUNCH as his heel met her midsection, driving her through the stone pillars and slamming her deep into the pulverized earth.</p>
<p>The shockwave sent a spray of blood and dirt outward in a perfect, gory circle.</p>
<p>But he didn’t give her a second to breathe. He didn’t even let her scream before he was on her.</p>
<p>Rex descended upon her like a god of war, his gauntlets glowing with a predatory, pulsing light. He grabbed her by the hair, forcing her head up, and then the onslaught began.</p>
<p>BAM! CRACK! SPLAT!</p>
<p>He began to punch her face. It wasn’t a duel; it was a demolition.</p>
<p>LEFT!</p>
<p>A gauntlet smashed into her cheekbone, the sound of shattering bone echoing like a hammer hitting a melon.</p>
<p>RIGHT!</p>
<p>The second gauntlet caught her jaw, sending a spray of teeth and blood flying into the air.</p>
<p>LEFT!</p>
<p>A heavy, overhead strike pulverized her nose, turning it into a pulp of cartilage and crimson.</p>
<p>BAM! BAM! BAM! BAM!</p>
<p>The rhythm was relentless. Squelch. Crack. Thud. Splat. Every strike was a masterpiece of carnage.</p>
<p>He was punching her with such ferocity that her head bounced off the ground like a heavy stone, her divine essence leaking out of her eyes and ears in thick, dark torrents.</p>
<p>The battlefield, once a place of grand strategy and epic warfare, fell into a deathly, terrified silence. The clashing of swords, the roar of magic, and the cries of the dying—it all vanished.</p>
<p>The entire army, from the lowest grunt to the highest commander, stood frozen. They weren’t watching a battle anymore; they were witnessing a slaughter.</p>
<p>They were watching a monster unmask itself.</p>
<p>The sheer, primal wildness of Rex’s assault was intoxicating and terrifying. He wasn’t fighting for a cause; he was fighting for the sheer, brutal joy of breaking something beautiful.</p>
<p>On the command ridge, the atmosphere was even more suffocating. Mordecai, a man who had seen a thousand wars and faced a hundred terrors, felt a cold sweat prickling his skin. His hands, usually steady, were trembling.</p>
<p>He stared at the distant, bloody crater where Rex was still raining blows down on the broken goddess, the sound of the impacts carrying across the silent plains like a heartbeat of doom.</p>
<p>Mordecai turned to Pavellia, his voice uncharacteristically small, cracked with a genuine, primal fear.</p>
<p>"Pavellia..." he whispered, his eyes never leaving the carnage. "How... how do we stop this?"</p>
<p>"It’s been going on for too long..."</p>
<p>"He’s not fighting her anymore... he’s erasing her."</p>
<p>He looked at the carnage, the sheer, unbridled violence of a man who had transcended the need for mercy. "If he finishes with her... what’s left for the rest of us?"</p>
<p>In the crater, Rex didn’t hear them. He only heard the rhythmic, wet thud of his gauntlets meeting Cassandra’s face and the fading, pathetic whimpers of a goddess who had finally learned the true meaning of terror.</p>
<p>He was a whirlwind of blood and iron, a god of destruction lost in the beautiful, gory music of his own making.</p>
