<p>It was a quiet kind of destruction the kind that doesn’t make a sound because it has already consumed everything that could make noise.</p>

<p>Evelyn lay paralyzed by the realization that her defeat was not merely physical. Her bones were shattered, and her lungs were weeping blood, but those were wounds that could not heal.</p>

<p>This was worse. She had weighed him honestly; she had stripped away every lie and offered the raw, naked truth to the heavens.</p>

<p>And the only result had been to provide him with the blueprint to make an entire world stop listening. She had given him the tools to silence the truth forever.</p>

<p>Far above, in the celestial realm where the laws of cause and effect held sway, the Goddess of Justice did something unprecedented: she withdrew her gaze.</p>

<p>The divine mark on Evelyn’s chest flickered and dimmed another degree, fading from a brilliant gold to a sickly, pale amber.</p>

<p>It wasn’t revoked; it simply fell silent, like a deity who had run out of arguments, a god who had realized that some monsters simply cannot be argued with.</p>

<p>Rex began to walk toward her. His gait was leisurely, almost casual, with his hands shoved deep into his pockets as if he were merely taking a stroll through a garden.</p>

<p>The last echoes of his laughter had settled into his features, leaving behind a smile that was wider, more serene, and infinitely more terrifying.</p>

<p>Then, the true end began.</p>

<p>It was not a fight; it was an execution of spirit.</p>

<p>What followed was a systematic, methodical breaking of everything Evelyn Astralight was. Rex didn’t use weapons.</p>

<p>He didn’t need them.</p>

<p>He used the raw, terrifying application of his will.</p>

<p>WHAM.</p>

<p>With a casual flick of his fingers, he activated [Telekinesis]. Evelyn’s body was yanked upward as if suspended by invisible wires.</p>

<p>Before she could even gasp, Rex stepped forward and drove a straight fist into her solar plexus with the force of a battering ram.</p>

<p>"GAK!" A spray of crimson arterial blood erupted from her lips, painting the cracked cobblestones.</p>

<p>He didn’t give her time to fall. As her body slumped, he spun, his leg cutting through the air in a devastating arc.</p>

<p>CRACK.</p>

<p>The sound of her left radius snapping was like a dry branch breaking in winter. She screamed a raw, animal sound that was immediately swallowed by the chanting of the city.</p>

<p>"HAIL REX REXILION!"</p>

<p>He slammed her down.</p>

<p>Real hard.</p>

<p>He didn’t just drop her; he amplified the gravity around her body until it felt as though the entire world’s weight was concentrated on her spine.</p>

<p>CRUNCH.</p>

<p>The marble beneath her back split open, spiderwebbing outward as the stone itself surrendered to the pressure.</p>

<p>Desperation flickered in her dying eyes. With a final, agonizing effort, she tried to summon a spark, a last vestige of holy lightning, to sear his flesh.</p>

<p>Sssssss...!</p>

<p>Rex’s boot came down on her outstretched palm. He didn’t crush it immediately; he simply pressed, his full weight grinding her bones into the grit.</p>

<p>The golden fire hissed and sputtered beneath his heel like water on a hot skillet before dying completely.</p>

<p>Every blow landed in perfect synchronization with the city’s chant.</p>

<p>THUD. HAIL!</p>

<p>THUD. HAIL!</p>

<p>The rhythm was hypnotic, brutal, and absolute.</p>

<p>Finally, he reached down and wound his fingers roughly through her matted, blood-soaked hair. He yanked her head back, forcing her to meet his gaze.</p>

<p>Her face was a ruin of bruises and dried blood, her eyes unfocused and swimming with pain.</p>

<p>"You said you’d kill me," Rex said, his voice conversational, almost intimate, as he studied her broken features. "And I’ll be honest, Evelyn... for a moment there, I actually thought you meant it."</p>

<p>He let out a soft, dismissive hum. "But you’re too precious to kill..."</p>

<p>"You’re the only one who still remembers what it’s like to hate me... And I need that."</p>

<p>With a violent sweep of his arm, he unleashed a wave of telekinetic force.</p>

<p>FWOOOM.</p>

<p>Evelyn was flung across the plaza like a broken doll. Her body sailed over the heads of the kneeling soldiers, their eyes fixed on Rex in worship, oblivious to the carnage, and bounced twice off the unforgiving paving stones with wet, sickening impacts.</p>

<p>She tumbled through the dust and blood, finally coming to a stop, rolling helplessly at the feet of three figures who stood motionless at the edge of the plaza, where the violet ring of power had finally come to rest.</p>

<p>The three figures at the edge of the plaza stood like statues carved from grief and ecstasy.</p>

<p>Helena and Leonidas had both woken, whether by the fading embers of the [Verdict] or the residual healing of Aisella, but they were prisoners in their own flesh.</p>

<p>Helena was not alone. She was trapped in a cocoon of grotesque affection.</p>

<p>Valentina and Elizabeth held her arms, each gripping her tight, their faces wet with tears that refused to stop flowing.</p>

<p>But those tears didn’t convey sorrow; their mouths were curved into serene, unshakeable smiles that would not fade, no matter how much Helena struggled.</p>

<p>"Let me go!" Helena shrieked, her voice cracking. "Mother! Lady Valentina!"</p>

<p>"Please, look at me! Look at what he’s doing!"</p>

<p>But Valentina only squeezed tighter, her eyes glazed with violet light. "Shh, darling..."</p>

<p>"Don’t be afraid... The storm is over."</p>

<p>"He’s here now."</p>

<p>A few feet away, Leonidas was enduring a similar nightmare. Lily and Diana had descended upon him like devoted acolytes.</p>

<p>Each girl held one of his arms with both of theirs, their grip bruising, their bodies pressed against him.</p>

<p>They weren’t restraining him with malice; they were cradling him like something sacred, sobbing and chanting along with the city’s thunderous roar as if their grief had been transformed into a perpetual prayer.</p>

<p>"HAIL REX REXILION!"</p>

<p>Of the three, only Elizabeth maintained a shred of her former self. Her eyes stayed sharp, piercing through the haze of tears.</p>

<p>She had bowed her head by her own choice, not because the power demanded it, but because she possessed the cold pragmatism to know that to resist now would be to invite a cruelty beyond comprehension.</p>

<p>She watched, her jaw clenched so tight it threatened to shatter, as the world she knew dissolved.</p>

<p>THUD.</p>

<p>Evelyn came to a stop between them, landing face down on the blood-slicked stones. Her broken body convulsed once, a wet cough rattling in her chest as she spat blood across the boots of Leonidas.</p>

<p>They watched it all. Helena, Leonidas, and Elizabeth—the three chosen observers—were forced to witness the complete annihilation of their reality with perfectly clear, unclouded eyes.</p>

<p>Helena stared at Lily. Her daughter was screaming Rex’s name, her face flushed with a terrifying, radiant worship.</p>

<p>Something in Helena’s chest didn’t just break; it imploded.</p>

<p>"Lily..." Helena whispered, her voice a broken shard of glass. "Please... my baby..."</p>

<p>Lily turned to her mother. Her expression was terrifyingly beautiful, a smile as gentle as a summer evening, as calm as a still lake.</p>

<p>"Mother, don’t fight it," Lily said, her voice dripping with honeyed certainty. "The war is over..."</p>

<p>"The pain is gone... He’ll protect all of us."</p>

<p>"We’re safe now."</p>

<p>Leonidas let out a guttural snarl, a sound of pure, impotent rage. He jerked his body violently against Diana’s grip, trying to tear his arm free, but the last embers of the [Mark of Wrath] guttered and died in his chest.</p>

<p>The divine fire that had once fueled his fury had been smothered by the violet tide. He looked down at Diana, who was clinging to him with such desperate tenderness, her tears running down her cheeks while her eyes shone with a terrifying devotion.</p>

<p>The emptiness inside him swallowed what was left of his anger.</p>

<p>He felt hollow. A husk.</p>

<p>There was no sorcery left in his veins.</p>

<p>There was no sword to raise.</p>

<p>There was no voice left in the world that would listen to his warnings.</p>

<p>He was a ghost standing in the middle of a carnival.</p>

<p>"Lily," he said, his voice hoarse, cracking with the weight of his despair. "Diana... Look at me."</p>

<p>"Look at what you’re doing! Look at what he has made you!"</p>

<p>Diana didn’t flinch. She didn’t even blink.</p>

<p>She simply leaned closer, her breath warm against his arm, her voice trembling with an emotion that was both holy and horrifying.</p>

<p>"We see you, Father," Diana said softly, her eyes locked onto his with an intensity that burned. "We see everything."</p>

<p>"We see the truth now."</p>

<p>"He doesn’t want to hurt us... He only wants to look after us."</p>
