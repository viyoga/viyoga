<p>In the center of the ruined plaza, standing amidst the blood, the smoke, and the divine afterglow of Evelyn’s desperate gambit, the air itself seemed to fracture.</p>

<p>Rex froze. Not out of fear, but because the very fabric of his reality had just been interrupted by a cascade of data.</p>

<p>A column of stark, luminous text materialized in the air before him, scrolling downward with mechanical indifference.</p>

<p>[WARNING: DESIRE LEVELS DECREASING]</p>

<p>[Lily Von Starlight: 100 → 96]</p>

<p>[Diana Von Starlight: 100 → 93]</p>

<p>[Talyra Skydancer: 100 → 91]</p>

<p>[Aisella Moonbloom: 100 → 90]</p>

<p>[Nerith Sylvarune: 100 → 88]</p>

<p>[Mireya Snowlith: 100 → 82]</p>

<p>[Cassandra Vexmoor: 100 → 85]</p>

<p>[Gorvasha Bloodtusk: 100 → 87]</p>

<p>[Lilith Nocturne: 100 → 92]</p>

<p>[Mara Kesson: 100 → 94]</p>

<p>[Marceline Bancey: 100 → 90]</p>

<p>[Elizabeth Von Starlight: 100 → 98]</p>

<p>[Valentina Von Starlight: 100 → 61]</p>

<p>[Morwenna Nightwing: 100 → 68]</p>

<p>[Amelia Brightsoul: 100 → 40]</p>

<p>[...AND SO ON, TWENTY NAMES SCROLLING DOWN THE SCREEN...]</p>

<p>’Oh shit... I didn’t know that can be done.’</p>

<p>The numbers fell with the cold, brutal logic of arithmetic. The system didn’t care about sentiment; it only cared about the strength of the metaphysical tether.</p>

<p>Those whose love had grown naturally those who had found something genuine amidst his chaos their numbers dipped only slightly.</p>

<p>But those who had been bound by pressure, by fear, by the exquisite torment of punishment, or by the desperate need to fill a void left by loss they hemorrhaged.</p>

<p>Their numbers plummeted.</p>

<p>Amelia Brightsoul, who had just been shattered by the realization that the man whose child she carried had been the murderer of her own flesh and blood, fell the deepest.</p>

<p>Her number bled out like an open wound.</p>

<p>Yet, none fell to zero.</p>

<p>None lost their pregnancies, and none lost their place among the twenty anchors.</p>

<p>The system was too perfect for that; to sever a bond completely would have triggered a catastrophic failure in the very mechanism that had just stitched Rex’s corpse back together.</p>

<p>The anchors held, but they were fraying. The leash was stretching.</p>

<p>Rex read every single line without so much as a flicker of surprise. His breathing remained steady, each inhale slow and controlled despite the carnage surrounding him.</p>

<p>He didn’t curse. And he didn’t rage.</p>

<p>Instead, the corner of his mouth lifted just a fraction into something that was terrifyingly close to a genuine smile.</p>

<p>He had known. He had known since that night in the canyon, when he first witnessed a single digit drop on his peripheral vision, that desire was not a static constant.</p>

<p>It was a variable. He had long anticipated this moment the moment someone would finally find the courage, or the madness, to make them see clearly.</p>

<p>He turned his head slowly, looking down at Evelyn. She lay broken in the center of her own crater, her body a map of violence.</p>

<p>Her chest rose and fell in shallow, wet gasps, each breath a struggle against punctured lungs and splintered bone. Her eyes remained open wide, bloodshot, and still burning with the defiant light of everything she had sacrificed to speak the truth.</p>

<p>"That was well done, Evelyn," Rex said.</p>

<p>His voice was soft, almost gentle, carrying a tenderness that was more unsettling than any roar. "Truly... The cleverest thing you could have done."</p>

<p>"You didn’t attack me; you attacked the foundation."</p>

<p>He tilted his head to the side, studying her broken form with the detached curiosity of a scholar examining a fascinating specimen.</p>

<p>"But you’ve forgotten something fundamental about human nature, little angel." His smile widened, revealing the predatory confidence of a man who had already calculated every possible outcome.</p>

<p>"You think that when something breaks, it’s gone... And you think that doubt equals defeat."</p>

<p>He took a single step closer to the edge of her crater, his shadow falling over her like a shroud.</p>

<p>"But you’re wrong."</p>

<p>"The strongest bonds aren’t the ones that never waver..."</p>

<p>"They aren’t the ones that stay perfect and untouched." His eyes gleamed with a dark, twisted understanding. "The strongest bonds are the ones that break, shatter, and bleed and then find their way back together anyway."</p>

<p>"Pain creates a different kind of memory... And I will make them remember me through that pain."</p>

<p>The drops followed a cold, inexorable logic. The system was not cruel; it was simply honest. Those whose bonds had grown from genuine connection, however twisted, fell the least.</p>

<p>But those whose devotion had been forged in the furnace of trauma, those bound by psychological pressure, by the threat of punishment, or by the hollow ache of loss they hemorrhaged.</p>

<p>Their numbers bled away like arterial spray.</p>

<p>Amelia Brightsoul fell the deepest of all. In a single, devastating stroke of revelation, she had learned that the man whose child she carried was the same monster who had slaughtered her son.</p>

<p>Her number didn’t just drop; it collapsed.</p>

<p>Yet, none fell to zero. None lost their pregnancies, and none lost their place among the twenty anchors.</p>

<p>The mechanism was too perfect, too self-preserving; to sever a bond completely would have triggered a systemic failure in the very resurrection engine that had just stitched Rex back together from carrion.</p>

<p>The chains remained, but they were fraying, sparking with the friction of newfound truth.</p>

<p>Rex read every line of the scrolling data without so much as a flicker of surprise. His breathing remained steady, each inhale slow and controlled despite the carnage surrounding him.</p>

<p>Instead of rage, a dark amusement settled over his features. The corner of his mouth lifted slowly, curling into something that was less a smile and more a predator’s grin.</p>

<p>He had known. He had known since that night in the canyon, when he first witnessed a single digit tremble, that desire was a variable.</p>

<p>He had long anticipated this moment—the moment someone would finally find the courage to tear away the veil of his seduction.</p>

<p>Then, Rex threw his head back and laughed.</p>

<p>"HA... AHAHAHAHAHA!"</p>

<p>It was a thunderous, mocking sound that echoed off the broken buildings, drowning out the distant cries of the city.</p>

<p>The laughter was cruel, rich with condescension, as he looked down at Evelyn, who lay broken in the center of her own crater.</p>

<p>"Oh, Evelyn," he said, wiping a stray tear of mirth from his eye. "That was adorable... Truly."</p>

<p>His laughter died down into a venomous chuckle. "You really thought you did something, didn’t you?"</p>

<p>"You thought you could play at being a savior, wielding your precious little ’Truth’ like it was a holy sword."</p>

<p>His expression hardened, his eyes narrowing as he began to roast her with the precision of a surgeon.</p>

<p>"Look at you... Look at what your ’Justice’ has bought you."</p>

<p>"You’ve managed to wake them up, yes..."</p>

<p>"You’ve given them a moment of clarity. But what does that change?"</p>

<p>"Look at the crater you’re lying in..."</p>

<p>"Look at the blood in your mouth... You’re a broken girl clinging to a dead ideal, screaming at the wind while the world burns around you."</p>

<p>He leaned over the edge of the crater, his shadow swallowing her whole. "You think you’ve weakened me?"</p>

<p>"You think these numbers matter?"</p>

<p>"You’ve just proven my point... You’ve shown them that even when the truth hurts, even when the lie shatters, they still belong to me."</p>

<p>"You haven’t broken the leash, Evelyn... you’ve just shown them how tight it’s been pulled."</p>

<p>His voice dropped to a whisper, dripping with contemptuous pity. "Again... I have to say this more than one time..."</p>

<p>"That was WELL... DOOONEEEE... EVEEEEE... LYYYNNN..." He said it with full sarcasm this time.</p>

<p>"The CLEVEREST... THING! You could have done as a fucking useless pig."</p>

<p>"You’ve managed to make them suffer more." He tilted his head, studying her shattered form. "But you’ve forgotten something fundamental..."</p>

<p>"You think strength comes from purity..."</p>

<p>"You think a bond that never wavers is the strongest."</p>

<p>His grin returned, wider and more terrifying than before. "Again... something that I should repeat myself again to get through your thick skull!"</p>

<p>"You’re wrong..."</p>

<p>"The strongest bonds aren’t the ones that never waver."</p>

<p>"They’re the ones that break, shatter, and bleed and then find their way back together anyway."</p>

<p>"I don’t need their blind obedience anymore... I’ll take their brokenness."</p>

<p>"I’ll take their regret! And I’ll make them love me for it!!!"</p>
