<p>Iris watched Aurelia walk away, but she didn’t follow immediately. Instead, she remained in the corridor, the silence of the hallway feeling thicker than before. A strange, restless energy seemed to prickle at her skin.</p>
<p>Without thinking, she raised both hands, interlacing her fingers behind her head and leaning her weight against the opposite wall. The movement was casual, almost languid, but there was something deeply unsettled in her posture.</p>
<p>"You know," Iris began, her voice dropping to a lower, almost contemplative register that cut through the lingering tension. "There is something else we have to acknowledge..."</p>
<p>"Something that makes this particular collapse almost inevitable."</p>
<p>Aurelia stopped mid-stride. She didn’t turn around immediately, but her shoulders stiffened.</p>
<p>"What are you talking about, Iris?"</p>
<p>Iris exhaled sharply through her nose. "The noise... The... performance."</p>
<p>"Rex and Diana." She paused, choosing her words with a precision that felt forced. "I have been hearing them... For days."</p>
<p>Aurelia finally turned, her expression unreadable, though her eyes flashed with something akin to disgust. "That is none of our concern... it’s their private lives."</p>
<p>"Private?" Iris cut her off with a dry, humorless laugh. "Aurelia, when the wet slap of flesh and the screaming echoes through the ventilation shafts and vibrates through the floorboards of the training quarters, it ceases to be private."</p>
<p>"It becomes a tactical disruption." She shifted her weight, her elbows jutting out as she maintained her relaxed pose, though her fingers pressed harder against her scalp. "I will admit it, even if it grates against my sensibilities; they are being... extraordinarily wild..."</p>
<p>"Shameful, even. There is no restraint in what they are doing... and no dignity."</p>
<p>Iris’s mind flashed involuntarily to the sounds from two nights ago, the raw, guttural demands. Rex had made, the way Diana’s voice had gone completely feral, stripped of all the composure she usually maintained during briefings. The memory of that unhinged, desperate wetness seemed to linger in the air between them.</p>
<p>"They need to be stopped at some point," Iris continued, her words coming faster now. "Not because of morality; we both know that’s a luxury we don’t afford ourselves, but because the sheer lack of control is dangerous."</p>
<p>"Rex is supposed to be demonstrating discipline, yet he’s using his authority to turn the household into his personal brothel."</p>
<p>"He holds the authority for the Nightwing household right now," Aurelia said quietly, her voice carrying a bitter edge of resignation. "Until the Council intervenes, his word is law."</p>
<p>"His appetites are his own."</p>
<p>Iris opened her mouth to respond, but something caught her eye. She froze.</p>
<p>Aurelia was silent, but her face was betraying her. The usual pale, composed mask of the Commander was cracking.</p>
<p>A deep, unmistakable flush had crept up Aurelia’s neck, spreading across her cheekbones until her entire face was stained a vivid crimson. She looked less like a hardened military leader and more like someone who had been caught listening to something she shouldn’t have—something that had stirred something primal and uncomfortable.</p>
<p>Iris blinked, the awkwardness of the moment hitting her like a physical blow. The silence stretched, becoming charged with an odd, uncomfortable energy.</p>
<p>"Aurelia?" Iris asked, her voice wavering slightly. "Your face..."</p>
<p>Aurelia snapped her head away, staring fixedly at a painting on the far wall as if it held the secrets of the universe.</p>
<p>"It’s hot in this corridor," she snapped, though the lie was transparent. "The heating system must be malfunctioning."</p>
<p>Iris cleared her throat, feeling a rare flush of her own creeping up her neck. She forced herself to relax, to lean further back against the wall, trying to regain her characteristic detachment.</p>
<p>She needed to steer this back to something professional, something clinical, before the implications of their conversation became too heavy to breathe.</p>
<p>"Right... The heating," Iris said, though she didn’t believe it for a second. She took a breath, steadying her pulse. "Regardless, we must rationalize it."</p>
<p>"We cannot let this affect our assessment of the situation..."</p>
<p>"After all..." She paused, searching for the most pragmatic angle. "They are young... Biologically speaking, their hormones are at their peak."</p>
<p>"What they are doing... the intensity of it... it’s a release of pent-up aggression."</p>
<p>"In a way, you could argue it’s a form of stress management..."</p>
<p>"A crude, unrefined part of their conditioning. Almost like a form of... primal training."</p>
<p>She tried to make it sound academic, as if she were discussing muscle fatigue rather than the obscene sounds of two bodies colliding in the dark. "High-stress environments require high-intensity outlets."</p>
<p>"It’s normal for their age... We shouldn’t let their lack of decorum cloud our judgment of their capability."</p>
<p>Aurelia didn’t respond, but the crimson on her cheeks didn’t fade. The two women stood in the hallway, caught between the professional necessity of their roles and the undeniable, raw reality of the chaos Rex and Diana had unleashed upon their household.</p>
<p>The crimson flush on Aurelia’s cheeks didn’t fade with Iris’s attempt at clinical rationalization. Instead, it seemed to harden, transforming from a sign of embarrassment into something colder and more dangerous.</p>
<p>The softness of her maternal concern evaporated, replaced by the glacial detachment of a commander who had seen too many soldiers die because they hesitated when they should have struck.</p>
<p>She turned fully to face Iris, her eyes narrowing until they were chips of flint. The heat in her face remained, but it was no longer shame; it was fury.</p>
<p>"Let’s stop pretending this is some biological inevitability, Iris," Aurelia said, her voice dropping to a deadly, quiet register. "Let’s stop wrapping it in the language of ’stress management’ and ’conditioning.’"</p>
<p>"We both know exactly what this is."</p>
<p>She took a step toward Iris, the authority in her stance undeniable. "Theo isn’t just grieving... He’s ashamed."</p>
<p>"And frankly? So am I."</p>
<p>Iris raised an eyebrow, recognizing the shift. "You blame him?"</p>
<p>"I blame his weakness," Aurelia snapped, the words cutting through the air like a whip. "I look at him behind that closed door, and I don’t see a victim."</p>
<p>"I see a man who watched what was his, what he should have protected, be taken by a stronger predator and did nothing but witness it."</p>
<p>"He stood there while Rex dismantled him, both physically and psychologically, and he allowed it."</p>
<p>The hallway seemed to grow colder as Aurelia continued, her voice gaining a bitter edge. "In this world, Iris, there is no room for sentimentality."</p>
<p>"There is no trophy for the man who feels the most pain."</p>
<p>"There is only the strong who stand tall and the weak who bow down... Diana chose the stronger hand."</p>
<p>"Rex took what he wanted because he had the will to claim it."</p>
<p>"Theo’s failure isn’t that he lost her; it’s that he lacked the spine to make her stay."</p>
<p>Iris watched her, studying the raw honesty in Aurelia’s expression. For a moment, the commander’s cruelty mirrored the harsh reality of the Nightwing household.</p>
<p>Finally, Iris nodded slowly, her own expression turning grim.</p>
<p>"You’re right," Iris admitted, her voice devoid of judgment. "The hierarchy is absolute."</p>
<p>"But if we’re discussing the reality of the situation, we have to address the... collateral damage of Rex’s dominance." She paused, a flicker of something unreadable crossing her features. "It isn’t just the noise."</p>
<p>"It’s the humiliation of the entire unit."</p>
<p>"Diana has no concept of discretion. She doesn’t just scream; she broadcasts."</p>
<p>"She makes it a point to remind everyone—every guard, every servant, every junior officer—exactly what Rex is doing to her."</p>
<p>"The way she speaks about him, about his... capacity..."</p>
<p>Iris trailed off, but the implication hung between them like smoke. The memory of Diana’s voice, shameless and ecstatic as she praised Rex’s virility in ways that would make a courtesan blush, resurfaced.</p>
<p>Both women felt the heat rising again, not from anger this time, but from the sheer, obscene vividness of the image.</p>
<p>"She makes it sound as though..." Iris started, then stopped, clearing her throat audibly. "As though he is a god among men..."</p>
<p>"The vulgarity of it is... unprecedented."</p>
<p>The silence that followed was thick with an uncomfortable, charged energy. The mental image of Diana’s vocal surrender, the graphic details she had screamed into the night air, momentarily paralyzed both women. by the mental image of Diana’s vocal surrender and the graphic details she had screamed into the night air.</p>
<p>For several seconds, a shared, involuntary flush crept up the necks of the two most powerful women in the household, uniting them.</p>
<p>Aurelia was the first to break. She straightened her uniform with unnecessary violence, her fingers fumbling slightly with the fabric.</p>
<p>The commander was back, but the mask was slipping.</p>
<p>"Enough," Aurelia commanded, though the word came out slightly too fast, betraying her agitation. "We are wasting time discussing the hysterics of a woman who has lost her mind and a man who has lost his restraint."</p>
<p>"This conversation is beneath us."</p>
<p>She turned away abruptly, her boots clicking sharply against the stone floor as she began to stride toward the training hall with renewed urgency. "We have briefings to prepare for..."</p>
<p>"The unit needs to be ready for tomorrow’s drills, not distracted by the... indiscretions of others. Let’s move."</p>
<p>Iris lingered for half a heartbeat, watching Aurelia’s retreating back, before smoothing her own expression into one of professional neutrality.</p>
<p>"Of course, Commander," she said quietly, falling into step behind Aurelia, leaving the haunted silence of Theo’s door and the echoes of the night behind them.</p>
