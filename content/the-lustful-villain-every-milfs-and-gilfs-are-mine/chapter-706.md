<p>The ground wasn’t just shaking anymore; it was screaming. The tectonic plates of the island were groaning under the weight of the approaching chaos.</p>
<p>Rex stood over the broken Apollo, his eyes glowing with a predatory fury. He wasn’t satisfied with just winning a fight; he wanted to erase the very concept of his opposition.</p>
<p>"You think you’ve seen the limit?" Rex roared at the sky, his voice a thunderclap that drowned out the settling dust. "You haven’t even seen the beginning!"</p>
<p>He planted his feet, his muscles bulging like braided steel cables. He reached upward, not with his hands, but with his will.</p>
<p>The Earthen Authority didn’t just even out the ground; it reached into the heavens. The gravity wells he had been manipulating began to pull, to condense, to drag the very debris of the shattered sky downward.</p>
<p>"DIE!" he screamed, his voice cracking with a manic intensity.</p>
<p>From the clouds, a mountain of stone and compressed matter began to descend. It wasn’t a rock; it was a meteor. A massive, burning, jagged mass of geological violence coalesced from the dust and debris of the battle above.</p>
<p>It was a celestial hammer, a crushing weight of millions of tons aimed directly at the shattered remains of the plaza. It was meant to be the final nail, the ultimate execution.</p>
<p>But just as the meteor began its final, terminal descent, two streaks of light slashed through the smoke.</p>
<p>SHINK! SHINK!</p>
<p>Rex’s eyes widened. His Foresight flared red hot.</p>
<p>He sensed the sudden shift in the air pressure. He twisted his body, a violent, unnatural snap of his torso, throwing himself to the left just as the first strike arrived.</p>
<p>BOOM!</p>
<p>The meteor didn’t hit the ground. It hit the space where he had just been.</p>
<p>The impact sent a shockwave that nearly tore his eardrums.</p>
<p>He looked up, his teeth bared in a snarl. Eryndra and Calivara had arrived.</p>
<p>They didn’t just land; they slammed into the earth like divine sentinels, standing shoulder to shoulder in front of the fallen Apollo and Kaelira. They were a shield of flesh and will, their eyes burning with a defiant, unyielding fury.</p>
<p>Rex stood there, his chest heaving, his hair matted with sweat and blood. He looked at the two women, then at the shattered plaza, and then, he began to laugh.</p>
<p>It wasn’t a heroic laugh. It was the sound of a man who had lost his tether to humanity, and it was a jagged, hysterical, manic sound that echoed off the cracked walls.</p>
<p>"Are you fucking kidding me?!" Rex spat, his voice dripping with a venomous rage. "Every time! Every single time! You little bitches just won’t fucking stay down!"</p>
<p>He wiped a smear of blood from his lip, his eyes turning a dark, unhinged shade of malice.</p>
<p>"You think you’re heroes? You think you’re protecting them?" He pointed a trembling, bloodstained finger at the defenders. "You’re just more meat for the grinder!"</p>
<p>"You’re just more obstacles in my way!"</p>
<p>He took a step forward, his aura exploding in a burst of unstable gravity and dust. His face, once the mask of a stoic warrior, was now the visage of a madman.</p>
<p>"Fine!" he screamed, his voice a raw howl of pure insanity. "If you want to play the shields, then I’ll just have to break you first!"</p>
<p>"I’ll tear the life out of your throats and use your bones to pave the way!"</p>
<p>"You’re all just fucking in the way!"</p>
<p>He crouched down, his body coiling like a viper ready to strike. The arrogance was gone, replaced by a primal, unadulterated lust.</p>
<p>"I’m going to fucking shred you!" he roared, his eyes wide and bloodshot. "I’m going to turn your guts into the dust of this plaza!"</p>
<p>Apollo struggled to prop himself up, his voice a broken, wheezing rasp. Blood bubbled and leaked from his lips, staining his chin in a dark, viscous smear.</p>
<p>"Run..." he choked out, his eyes pleading. "Get out of here... now! He’s... he’s going to kill you both!"</p>
<p>Eryndra didn’t budge. She plants her feet firmly in the cracked stone, her blade reflecting the dull, dying light of the ruined plaza.</p>
<p>"I don’t run," she said, her voice hard as iron.</p>
<p>Calivara stood beside her, her fingers trembling but her grip on her staff tightening, her face a mask of fierce devotion.</p>
<p>"We protect our own," she spat, glaring at Rex. "You’re not touching him."</p>
<p>Rex didn’t answer. He didn’t have time.</p>
<p>Eryndra exploded into motion. She didn’t just attack; she unleashed a barrage of astral summons.</p>
<p>From the void between dimensions, translucent, jagged spirits tore through reality, shrieking with a thousand discordant voices. They descended on Rex like a tide of ghostly razors, pouring out waves of astral magic that turned the air into a searing vacuum.</p>
<p>Rex didn’t even flinch. He raised his hand, his palm open.</p>
<p>"Boring," he spat.</p>
<p>He slammed his hand down, and the ground didn’t just shake; it screamed. The Earthen Authority surged, not as a wall but as a living, sentient parasite.</p>
<p>The stone beneath the astral summons didn’t just rise; it liquified, turning into a churning maw of molten slag and jagged teeth. The astral summons were caught in the sudden vortex, sucked down into the crushing pressure of the earth.</p>
<p>There was a sound of glass shattering as the spirits were annihilated, their essence absorbed by the hungry stone.</p>
<p>"My turn," Rex grinned, and it was a look of pure, unadulterated sadism.</p>
<p>"I’m going to show you what ’creative’ really means," he whispered, his voice low and poisonous.</p>
<p>He slammed his fist into the ground, sending a pulse of focused gravity into the bedrock. Suddenly, the ground beneath Eryndra erupted. Not in a spray of dust, but in a spear of jagged, black obsidian.</p>
<p>It shot upward with the speed of a bullet. Eryndra danced back, the stone narrowly missing her, but Rex was already moving.</p>
<p>He appeared in front of her, his hand snapping out in a blur. He grabbed her by the throat, his fingers digging into her windpipe.</p>
<p>With a guttural roar, he slammed her face first into the stone wall behind them. The sound of her skull meeting the rock was a sickening crack.</p>
<p>"How’s that for a shield?" Rex snarled.</p>
<p>He didn’t let go. He lifted her up, her legs kicking uselessly, and then slammed her back down again.</p>
<p>And again. Her nose shattered; blood sprayed across the ground in a grotesque splatter.</p>
<p>Calivara screamed, launching a desperate bolt of energy at Rex’s head. He caught the blast with his free hand, the energy boiling his skin, but he didn’t flinch.</p>
<p>He turned his gaze toward her, his eyes cold and empty.</p>
<p>"You want to save him?" Rex asked. "Watch this."</p>
<p>He dropped Eryndra, who fell in a heap, gasping and choking on her own blood. Rex slammed his foot down, and the ground beneath Calivara simply vanished.</p>
<p>She plummeted into a pit of jagged stone spikes. There was a sharp, wet sound, the sound of flesh being impaled.</p>
<p>Calivara shrieked as a jagged, obsidian shard tore through her calf, pinning her to the bottom of the pit.</p>
<p>"Look at her!" Rex roared, stepping toward the pit. "Look at your ’champion’!"</p>
<p>He reached down, grabbing Eryndra by the hair and dragging her toward the pit. "You think you’re noble?"</p>
<p>"You think this is a fucking fairy tale?" He threw her down beside Calivara, the two of them colliding in a tangle of broken limbs and torn clothing.</p>
<p>Rex stood at the edge of the pit, looking down at them with a twisted, manic grin. He raised his hand, and the ground began to close back in.</p>
<p>The jagged rocks began to squeeze together, crushing the space around the two women.</p>
<p>"I can feel your fear," Rex chuckled, his voice echoing with a dark, twisted delight. "It smells like copper and sweat."</p>
<p>"Oh~! I love the smell of fear~!"</p>
<p>"It’s the only thing in this world that’s actually real."</p>
<p>He closed his fist, and the pressure increased. The sound of bones snapping filled the air, a brutal symphony of destruction.</p>
<p>"How much of you is left before you stop being a person and start being a stain on the pavement?" He laughed, a wild, jagged sound. "Let’s find out together!"</p>
<p>Rex stood at the edge of the pit, his chest heaving, eyes wide with a manic brilliance. The air around him began to distort, his own gravity warping the very space he inhabited.</p>
<p>Then, he slammed his fists together.</p>
<p>CLANG.</p>
<p>The sound was not a human noise; it was the collision of two continents. From the swirling dust and sparks, his gauntlets materialized as massive, blackened Sunder Gauntlets of the Earthen Authority.</p>
<p>They weren’t just armor; they were instruments of extinction, forged from the core of a dead world, pulsing with a malevolent, rhythmic cadence. Rex flexed his fingers, the metal groaning under the sheer pressure of his strength.</p>
<p>"Time for a lesson in anatomy," Rex sneered.</p>
<p>He didn’t jump down; he descended like a falling star. He hit the bottom of the pit with a force that shattered the remaining stone, creating a crater of debris.</p>
<p>Before Eryndra could even scream, Rex’s right fist collided with her jaw.</p>
<p>The impact was sickening. There was no grace in the strike, only raw, unmitigated violence.</p>
<p>Her head snapped back, her jaw unhinging with a loud, wet pop. Teeth and fragments of bone sprayed across the dirt, and she was flung against the wall of the pit like a ragdoll.</p>
<p>"AAGGHHHH!!!"</p>
<p>"STOP IT!!!" Apollo screamed.</p>
