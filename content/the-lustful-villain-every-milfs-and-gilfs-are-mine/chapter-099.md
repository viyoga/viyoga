<p>"Just one second," Rex smirked. "There’s some trash that could bring me to your lord even closer."</p>
<p>He moved without thinking, raising one hand and pointing a single finger at the source of the threat. A huge fireball, at least the size of a carriage, was flying through the air toward the clearing, right at the group of demon maids.</p>
<p>Rex’s new Energy Manipulation power reached out and grabbed the fireball’s structure, instantly figuring out what it was made of. Fire elemental energy that has been compressed and held together by binding spells is meant to explode on impact with a lot of force.</p>
<p>He flexed his power, and the fireball stopped in mid-air, about ten feet from the nearest maid. Then he changed the energy patterns, switched the direction of the components, and added his own power to the structure that was already there.</p>
<p>The fireball shot back the way it had come, but it was now twice as big and much faster.</p>

<p>"My package had been seen to its coordinates, huh?" Rex laughed.</p>

<p>She also carried a staff that glowed with leftover magical energy. Her face showed a mix of grim determination and fear that was hard to hide.</p>
<p>There were three other people behind her. A man with short black hair and armor that looked like it belonged to a samurai, holding a katana that looked like it had been used a lot.</p>
<p>A woman with pink hair styled in fancy curls and wearing robes that showed she was a mage or healer.</p>
