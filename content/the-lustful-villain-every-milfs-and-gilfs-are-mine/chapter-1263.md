<p>A cold, quiet satisfaction settled deep in Rex’s chest, a feeling so profound and absolute that it required no outward expression, no smirk, no triumphant declaration. It was simply the stillness that follows a successful conquest.</p>
<p>He looked down at her again, his gaze lingering on the pale column of her throat. This was Valentina Von Starlight.</p>
<p>For decades, she had been more than a woman; she had been an institution. She had sat atop the mage rankings for longer than most kingdoms had drawn breath, an immovable force of nature.</p>
<p>She had been untouchable not merely because others had failed to climb her peak, but because the world had lacked the audacity to even consider the attempt. She was a mountain, ancient and unyielding.</p>
<p>Now, that mountain had been hollowed out. Her silver hair was splayed across his pillow like spilled mercury, and her breathing once sharp and commanding was as soft and vulnerable as that of a sleeping child.</p>
<p>Rex felt a surge of dark amusement.</p>
<p>If any of the Apostles, those fanatics who had once knelt before her in reverent terror, could see her now broken, claimed, and carrying his seed, they would not believe their own eyes. Their goddess had been reduced to a vessel.</p>
<p>The silence of the room was suddenly punctured by something internal. A faint hum resonated at the back of his skull, a subtle vibration like a fingertip tapping against the inside of his cranium.</p>
<p>His eyes sharpened. The system was stirring.</p>
<p>It had been waiting for him to wake, waiting for the moment the conquest reached its logical conclusion.</p>
<p>The first notification unfurled in his field of vision, clean, bright, and utterly invisible to the sleeping woman beside him.</p>
<p>[Valentina Von Starlight: Desire Level 100/100. Maximum Bond achieved.]</p>
<p>[First Maximum Bond Bonus: +1,000 pts]</p>
<p>Rex watched the numbers flicker. The ’Desire’ wasn’t just lust; it was the complete surrender of her will, the point where her resistance had fused with her need to be dominated.</p>
<p>A second notification chased the first, appearing with clinical efficiency.</p>
<p>[Pregnancy and Psychological Dominance confirmed: Valentina Von Starlight.]</p>
<p>[Bonus: +1,000 pts.]</p>
<p>[Free Skill Selection now available]</p>
<p>Rex let the words hang in the air of his mind. He didn’t need to surprise himself; he had anticipated this outcome with the inevitability of a predator.</p>
<p>He pictured her waking up the moment the fog of exhaustion cleared and the terrifying reality of her condition settled into her bones.</p>
<p>A sixty-eight-year-old headmaster, a matriarch who had raised some of the most powerful women in Aethelgard, now carrying the child of the man who had systematically dismantled her soul.</p>
<p>He wondered how many hours it would take for the realization to dawn on her.</p>
<p>Would she weep? Or would she simply break further?</p>
<p>Then came the third notification, the one that truly mattered. The tally of his cruelty, translated into raw power.</p>
<p>[Energy accumulated since punishment period: +12,000 pts.]</p>
<p>[Source: 30 sessions with Valentina Von Starlight.]</p>
<p>[High-tier target multiplier applied: x2 per session (400 pts each)]</p>
<p>He mentally reviewed the past week: the sweat, the screams that eventually turned to whimperings, and the way her body had bucked and broken under him thirty times over.</p>
<p>Each session had been a payment, each violation a deposit into his growing arsenal. The system didn’t care about morality; it only cared about the intensity of the experience, and Rex had given it an unprecedented feast of torment and dominance.</p>
<p>[Previous balance: approximately 37,600 pts]</p>
<p>[Current balance: 51,600 pts]</p>
<p>Fifty-one thousand points. The power at his fingertips was intoxicating.</p>
<p>He wasn’t just a man anymore; he was becoming something that could rewrite the laws of this world. He looked back at Valentina, his eyes dark with a new kind of hunger.</p>
<p>The conquest of the Headmaster was complete, but the reign of Rex was only just beginning.</p>
<p>Rex stared at the numbers. He read them twice, not out of uncertainty, but because he savored them.</p>
<p>He liked the way they gleamed in the darkness of his mind—sharp, cold, and undeniable.</p>
<p>’Fifty-one thousand six hundred.’</p>
<p>The figure carried a weight that few in this world could comprehend. His mind instinctively began to run the comparative calculations.</p>
<p>After the brutal, bone-crushing confrontation with Kaeleth, a fight that had left him tasting copper and feeling every fractured rib, and the fifty thousand Lustia had bled into his account after a month of relentless, transactional labor, his balance had hovered around forty-nine thousand.</p>
<p>But power was an expensive addiction. Soul Manipulation had devoured eleven thousand points like a starving beast; Domain Creation had drained another fifteen thousand in a single, reality warping instant.</p>
<p>For weeks, he had been operating on a razor’s edge, fighting a war of attrition where every spell cast was a gamble against bankruptcy.</p>
<p>Now, the total sat higher than it had ever been since the day the system had first awakened inside his skull.</p>
<p>He remembered that day with a detached sort of amusement, waking up on a threadbare cot in a cramped room above a fishing town, the smell of salt and rotting fish clinging to the walls. Back then, his balance had been a humiliating fifty points.</p>
<p>Fifty.</p>
<p>He had been a nobody, a desperate man who had been forced to seduce a married woman in three days just to scrape together enough points to survive another week.</p>
<p>’I have come a long way from the Broken Wheel Inn,’ he thought, a dark amusement flickering behind his eyes.</p>
<p>The journey from that pathetic, starving boy to the man who now held the headmaster of Aethelgard captive in his bed was written in blood, sweat, and broken wills.</p>
<p>Two free skill selections hung suspended in his peripheral awareness, the one earned through Morwenna’s surrender and the one Valentina had just paid for with her dignity. They sat there like unspent ammunition, like divine gift cards waiting to be redeemed.</p>
<p>With the current balance, he could have purchased three or four major, world-altering skills without breaking a sweat.</p>
<p>Yet, he felt no urgency. A man who squanders his fortune the moment it touches his palm is a man who doesn’t understand the value of leverage.</p>
<p>Rex had spent too much time lately being blindsided by the world’s cruelty, being forced to adapt to threats he hadn’t seen coming.</p>
<p>He would not make that mistake again. He would analyze, he would observe, and he would wait until he understood exactly what shape his next conquest would take before he committed his resources.</p>
<p>With a dismissive flick of his consciousness, he closed the notifications. The glowing text vanished, leaving only the dim morning light filtering through the curtains.</p>
<p>Moving with the deliberate grace of a predator, he began to slide his legs out from beneath the tangled sheets. He moved with excruciating slowness, mindful of every muscle twitch, ensuring the mattress remained as still as a grave.</p>
<p>As he shifted, Valentina made a small, broken sound in her sleep, a soft whimper that seemed to carry the echoes of the screams she had unleashed the night before. She instinctively burrowed deeper into the pillow, seeking comfort in the very fabric that had witnessed her undoing.</p>
<p>Rex froze. He waited, breath held, watching the rise and fall of her shoulders until her breathing smoothed out into that eerie, deep rhythm once more.</p>
<p>It was a strange sensation: being careful with her.</p>
<p>A week ago, he would not have given her a second thought; he would have rolled out of bed without caring if he woke her or crushed her ribs in the process. But as he watched her sleep, he realized his restraint wasn’t born of compassion.</p>
<p>There was no tenderness in his restraint, no flicker of guilt or protective instinct. He wasn’t being gentle because he cared for her well-being.</p>
<p>He was being gentle because a rested Valentina was more useful than an exhausted one. A broken tool is difficult to work with; a broken woman was far more entertaining when she was still capable of feeling the full weight of her surrender.</p>
