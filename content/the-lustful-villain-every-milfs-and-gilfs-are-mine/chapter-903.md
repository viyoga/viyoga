<p>The heavy, suffocating silence that had gripped the training hall shattered not with a scream but with a single, thunderous clap that echoed off the vaulted ceilings.</p>
<p>Then another.</p>
<p>And another.</p>
<p>Slowly, the shock dissolved into a roaring crescendo of approval. The guards, the lesser nobles, and the seasoned warriors who had witnessed the carnage didn't see a massacre; they saw a revelation.</p>
<p>In a world where threats were becoming more monstrous and unpredictable, seeing two titans push each other to the brink of death was more than entertainment; it was a reassurance.</p>
<p>This was what survival looked like.</p>
<p>This was the preparation required for the true horrors awaiting them beyond the walls.</p>
<p>"Magnificent!" someone bellowed from the balcony. "That was true steel!"</p>
<p>"Gods, what a display of power!" another voice joined in.</p>
<p>The applause became a wave, a physical force that washed over the blood-soaked arena.</p>
<p>Rex, still breathing in ragged gasps, wiped a smear of blood from his forehead with the back of his hand. His muscles burned with a fire that felt like molten lead, but his eyes were clear.</p>
<p>He turned toward Aurelia, who still lay sprawled on the stone. The predatory smile faded, replaced by a look of grim acknowledgment.</p>
<p>He reached down, offering a massive, scarred hand.</p>
<p>"Come on, Matriarch," Rex said, his voice rough like grinding gravel. "The floor's getting cold."</p>
<p>Aurelia stared at his hand for a long moment. Then, with a grunt of pain that sounded more like a challenge than a complaint, she gripped his palm.</p>
<p>Rex hauled her up with effortless strength, steadying her as she wobbled on unsteady legs. They stood together, two blood-soaked monuments to resilience, and for a moment, the hall seemed to hold its breath before erupting again.</p>
<p>The crowd began to converge. The circle broke as the women of the inner circle rushed forward, drawn by the magnetism of Rex's raw dominance.</p>
<p>Elaris was the first to reach them, her usual composed demeanor replaced by wide-eyed fascination. "That was... beyond anything I've ever witnessed, Rex Rexilion."</p>
<p>"Both of you." She touched Aurelia's shoulder gently before turning to Rex, her gaze lingering on the way his bloodied chest rose and fell. "You've changed everything today."</p>
<p>Iris followed close behind, her face flushed with an adrenaline high that mirrored his own. "I've never seen Aurelia pushed like that!"</p>
<p>"Rex, your technique is unlike anything in our archives! It's beautiful in its own brutal way."</p>
<p>Then came Diana. She didn't stop at the edge of the circle; she pushed through, her eyes locked onto Rex's with an intensity that bordered on worship.</p>
<p>When she reached him, she didn't hesitate. She placed a hand on his arm, her fingers lingering on his heated, sweat-slicked skin.</p>
<p>"You proved it," she whispered, loud enough only for him to hear. "You proved everything I said."</p>
<p>The celebration was a whirlwind of congratulatory touches, heated debriefs about specific strikes, and the shared euphoria of witnessing something legendary. The Nightwing household was united in this moment, bound by the awe of Rex's ascension.</p>
<p>But far behind the circle of light and laughter, standing in the deepening shadows of the corridor entrance, was Theo.</p>
<p>He was a ghost at his own family's feast. From his vantage point, he watched them all—his mother, his sister, and the noblewomen—all circling this mercenary as if he were some returning god.</p>
<p>He saw the way Diana looked at Rex, a look she had never once given him, and it felt like a dagger twisted in his gut. He saw the respect his mother showed the man who had humiliated her, and it made his blood boil with a poisonous heat.</p>
<p>The applause sounded to him like mocking laughter. The cheers sounded like nails being driven into his coffin.</p>
<p>His hands were clenched so tightly in his pockets that his nails drew blood from his own palms. He knew that no matter how many battles Rex won, no matter how many lives he saved, the hatred would never leave him.</p>
<p>He was a prince of a legacy that now belonged to a stranger, and as he watched the light of the hall fade behind him, he realized with terrifying clarity that his resentment was the only thing he truly owned.</p>
<p>The celebration within the hall began to mellow from a roaring frenzy into a warm, reverent hum as the adrenaline started to recede, leaving behind the raw ache of exhaustion. Aurelia, still leaning heavily on Rex for support, raised a bloodied hand.</p>
<p>The gesture was not one of command but of something far more potent: it was an acknowledgment.</p>
<p>The crowd fell silent as she steadied herself, her breathing still labored but her voice carrying the unmistakable authority of a matriarch who had seen the face of death and refused to flinch.</p>
<p>"Listen to me," Aurelia commanded, her eyes scanning the faces of her kin, her guards, and the assembled nobles. "Today, we witnessed more than a fight."</p>
<p>"We witnessed the breaking of old limitations..."</p>
<p>"We witnessed the birth of a new standard!"</p>
<p>She turned her head slightly to look at Rex, whose scarred face remained stoic despite the blood streaming down his jaw. "The threats gathering at our borders do not care for our titles or our ancient traditions."</p>
<p>"They care only for strength. And until my husband returns from the northern campaigns to reclaim his mantle, this house cannot afford to rely on the methods of the past."</p>
<p>A murmur rippled through the hall: uncertainty, fear, and confusion.</p>
<p>Aurelia pressed on, her voice hardening like tempered steel. "Therefore, I am making a declaration."</p>
<p>"From this moment forward, the training regimens, the tactical doctrine, and the fundamental discipline of the Nightwing household will be placed under the direct supervision of Rex."</p>
<p>"He will not merely be an instructor; he will be the architect of our new strength!"</p>
<p>"He will forge you as he forged himself!" Aurelia raised her fist. "Every strike, every stance, every instinct will be rebuilt through his methods."</p>
<p>A collective gasp filled the chamber. To hand over the sacred training of the Nightwing bloodline to an outsider, even one as proven as Rex, was unprecedented.</p>
<p>It was a seismic shift in their entire social and military hierarchy.</p>
<p>"He has earned this authority through blood and sweat," Aurelia continued, her gaze fixed on the horizon beyond the hall's windows. "He has proven that his vision of survival is superior to our comfort."</p>
<p>"Accept this, and we become unbreakable!"</p>
<p>"Reject it, and we become relics waiting to be shattered!"</p>
<p>The silence that followed was deafening. Diana's eyes shone with fierce pride; Elaris nodded slowly, already calculating the tactical advantages; Iris looked exhilarated, eager to begin the transformation.</p>
<p>Even the seasoned guards exchanged glances of grudging respect. They saw the logic in the blood on the floor.</p>
<p>But at the periphery of the celebration, where the torchlight failed to reach, the atmosphere was different.</p>
<p>Theo stood motionless in the gloom. His face was a mask of pale fury, his features twisted into something unrecognizable.</p>
<p>The words 'new standard' and 'architect' rang in his ears like funeral bells. To him, this wasn't progress; it was an execution.</p>
<p>An execution of his family's dignity, his heritage, and his own future. He felt the walls of the hall closing in, the eyes of his mother, grandmother, and aunt now complicit in this betrayal burning into his back.</p>
<p>The hatred he had been nurturing since Rex first stepped foot in Aethelgard had finally calcified into something absolute.</p>
<p>As the crowd began to murmur their reluctant, awestruck acceptance, Theo took one step forward out of the shadows, his voice shattering the sacred silence like a shattering blade.</p>
<p>"No."</p>
