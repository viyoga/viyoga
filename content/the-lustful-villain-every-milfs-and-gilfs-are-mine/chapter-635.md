<p>He was covered in a cocktail of his own crimson blood and the creature’s black ink, his tan skin glistening under the carnage.</p>
<p>He swung a wide, sweeping arc with his gauntlet, a blade of compressed air and fire slicing through the air.</p>
<p>SHIIIIING!</p>
<p>The strike caught the Void Eater across its torso, carving a deep, jagged trench through its muscular chest. The creature’s flesh peeled back in violent ribbons, exposing the glowing, silver core of its stolen essence.</p>
<p>More blood sprayed a frantic, pulsing fountain of black liquid that coated the ground in a slick, deadly mire.</p>
<p>The survivors, huddled in the shadows of the ruins, watched with wide, trembling eyes. They were paralyzed by a primal, existential dread.</p>
<p>To them, the battle wasn’t a fight; it was the collision of two gods. They saw Rex take a hit that should have decapitated a man, a massive, obsidian claw slamming into his shoulder with a sickening CRUNCH of bone and muscle, only to see the wound erupt in a golden light and vanish in a heartbeat.</p>
<p>They saw the Void Eater be torn apart, only to stitch itself back together like a nightmare made of sewing needles.</p>
<p>"They aren’t even human anymore..." one mage whispered, her voice trembling as she watched a chunk of Rex’s cheek fly off, only to be replaced by new flesh before the blood could even hit the ground.</p>
<p>"Heck... they’re even worse than us monsters..." One demon whispered back.</p>
<p>Rex was a whirlwind of carnage. He lunged forward, his foresight guiding him into the creature’s guard.</p>
<p>He caught the Void Eater’s massive arm, twisted, and with a roar of pure, unadulterated ego, he snapped it.</p>
<p>SNAP!</p>
<p>The sound was deafening. The obsidian limb shattered into jagged shards, spraying black blood in every direction.</p>
<p>Rex didn’t stop; he used the momentum to drive his glowing fist straight into the creature’s eye socket.</p>
<p>SQUELCH!</p>
<p>The eye burst in a spray of silver fluid and dark gore. The Void Eater let out a scream that felt like it was tearing the very sky apart, a sound of pure, unbridled fury.</p>
<p>It grabbed Rex by the waist, its claws sinking deep into his abdomen, tearing through muscle and organ. Rex gasped, a fountain of bright red blood erupting from his mouth, coating his chest in a crimson mask.</p>
<p>But even as he hung there, impaled and bleeding, Rex was laughing. A bloody, manic, terrifying laugh.</p>
<p>"Is that all you’ve got?!" he screamed into the creature’s face, his eyes burning with a golden, predatory fire. "I’ve felt worse from a goddamn breeze!"</p>
<p>He slammed his palms against the creature’s chest, and a massive, localized explosion of elemental energy detonated between them.</p>
<p>BOOOOOOOM!</p>
<p>The blast sent them both flying in opposite directions, leaving a massive, smoking crater where they had been clashing. The ground was a graveyard of broken obsidian and cooling blood, a testament to a struggle that defied the very laws of life and death.</p>
<p>The battlefield had ceased to be a landscape; it was now a churning, visceral sea of gore and pulverized stone. The sheer volume of blood, Rex’s bright, arterial crimson, and the Void Eater’s thick, ink-like black ichor had turned the courtyard into a slick, steaming slaughterhouse.</p>
<p>CRASH! BOOM! SPLAT!</p>
<p>Rex lunged through the mire, his movements a blur of golden light. He wasn’t just fighting anymore; he was conducting a symphony of destruction. He caught the Void Eater mid-leap, his gauntlets glowing with a violent, unstable violet hue.</p>
<p>He drove both fists into the creature’s solar plexus with the force of a falling mountain.</p>
<p>KRA KOOOOOM!</p>
<p>The impact didn’t just dent the obsidian; it shattered the creature’s entire torso into a spray of jagged shards and black fluid. The shockwave was so intense it sent a wall of blood flying outward, drenching the terrified survivors like a macabre rain.</p>
<p>But as Rex pulled his hands back, his eyes narrowed in a flash of frustration. The silver essence within the creature was already screaming, swirling into the shattered cavity like a frantic whirlpool.</p>
<p>Before the shards could even hit the ground, they were being pulled back into the mass, the flesh knitting together with a sickening, wet slurp.</p>
<p>"Damn it!" Rex roared, wiping a smear of black blood from his forehead. "It’s like trying to punch a goddamn liquid!"</p>
<p>The Void Eater didn’t give him time to contemplate. It lashed out with a whip-like tail of condensed mana, catching Rex across the chest.</p>
<p>WHAP CRACK!</p>
<p>The blow tore through his pectoral muscle, ripping a jagged canyon of flesh from his shoulder to his hip. A fountain of red sprayed the air, but Rex merely grinned through the agony.</p>
<p>His Supreme Healing was working overtime, the golden light of his regeneration clashing with the dark spray of the monster’s blood.</p>
<p>He was satisfied. The sheer scale of the carnage was feeding his ego; he had never felt more alive, more dominant.</p>
<p>But his mind, sharpened by the foresight of a conqueror, was racing. He knew brute force was a stalemate.</p>
<p>He was fighting a cycle of infinite destruction versus infinite reconstruction.</p>
<p>’Think, Rex... think...’</p>
<p>He dodged a massive, crushing overhead strike that turned the ground beneath him into a crater.</p>
<p>THOOM!</p>
<p>As he rolled, his foresight flickered. He didn’t see a way to break the creature; he saw a way to overload it.</p>
<p>"You can’t heal what’s been turned into nothingness," Rex muttered, his voice dropping into a low, dangerous growl.</p>
<p>He began to change his rhythm. Instead of singular, massive strikes, he began a relentless, high-frequency assault. He became a golden streak of lightning, landing dozens of precise, stinging blows every second.</p>
<p>TAP TAP TAP CRACK!</p>
<p>He wasn’t aiming for the limbs or the torso anymore; he was aiming for the nexus, the core where the silver essence gathered to perform the regeneration.</p>
<p>He struck the creature’s chest, not with a heavy smash, but with a concentrated, vibrating pulse of elemental energy.</p>
<p>VREEEEEEE!</p>
<p>Every time the creature tried to seal a wound, Rex was there, hitting the exact same spot with a microscopic, high-pressure burst of kinetic force, disrupting the flow of the silver essence.</p>
<p>The Void Eater began to panic. For the first time, its movements lost their grace.</p>
<p>It swung wildly, its massive claws missing Rex by mere inches, the air whistling with the force of its desperation. It let out a distorted, gargling scream as Rex’s relentless strikes began to fray the edges of its very existence.</p>
<p>Rex’s eyes burned with a predatory brilliance. He could see it now: the silver essence was becoming unstable, and the "glue" was losing its grip because he was hitting it faster than it could settle.</p>
<p>"Almost there..." Rex hissed, his muscles trembling with the effort of maintaining the tempo.</p>
<p>He was pushing his own regeneration to the limit, his body a frantic engine of healing and striking. "I’m going to burn the very concept of ’healing’ out of your veins!"</p>
<p>He gathered every ounce of mana he had left, his gauntlets beginning to vibrate so violently they hummed with a terrifying, high-pitched whine. He wasn’t just preparing a punch; he was preparing a molecular execution.</p>
<p>He was going to find the frequency of its soul and shatter it.</p>
<p>"HRAAGGHHHH!!!" Rex lunged toward the Void Eater again.</p>
<p>BAM! BAM! BAM!</p>
<p>The battlefield had transcended mere combat; it was a cosmic meat grinder. The ground was no longer earth but a viscous, steaming sludge of pulverized stone, Rex’s bright crimson blood, and the Void Eater’s thick, obsidian ichor.</p>
<p>KRA KOOOOOM!</p>
<p>Rex was no longer just fighting; he was dominating. He had found the rhythm of the monster’s heartbeat, and he was breaking it.</p>
<p>Every time the Void Eater attempted to regenerate, Rex was there, his foresight predicting the exact microsecond the silver essence would begin to flow. He didn’t just hit the wound; he hit the flow.</p>
<p>With a roar that shook the very foundations of the Underlayer, Rex launched a sequence of strikes so fast they appeared as a continuous golden halo of violence.</p>
<p>BAM! BAM! BAM! CRASH!</p>
<p>He hammered the creature’s core with a concentrated, vibrating frequency of elemental energy. It was like hitting a bell with a sledgehammer; the vibrations didn’t just bruise the flesh; they shattered the molecular bonds of the silver essence itself.</p>
<p>"You’re too slow!" Rex screamed, his face a mask of blood and sweat, his eyes glowing with a terrifying, divine arrogance. "Your ’immortality’ is just a delay!"</p>
<p>The Void Eater let out a sound of pure, existential terror. Its regeneration was stuttering.</p>
<p>The wounds weren’t sealing; they were fizzling. Instead of knitting back together, the stolen energy was being agitated into a chaotic, unstable state by Rex’s high-frequency kinetic pulses.</p>
<p>Rex saw the opening. The creature’s chest was a churning, unstable mess of silver and black, unable to decide whether to heal or explode.</p>
<p>"Now... DIE!"</p>
<p>Rex didn’t just punch. He channeled every drop of his infinite energy into a single, catastrophic point.</p>
<p>He lunged into the center of the creature’s mass, his gauntlets erupting in a blinding, supernova white light. He drove his hands deep into the unstable core, his fingers literally grasping the essence of the stolen summons.</p>
<p>VREEEEEEEEE BOOOOOOOM!!!</p>
<p>He didn’t just strike; he detonated the core from the inside out. The kinetic energy traveled through the creature’s entire nervous system, vibrating every cell at a frequency that defied the laws of physics.</p>
<p>The silver essence didn’t just fail to heal; it turned against the host, exploding outward in a violent, internal chain reaction.</p>
<p>The Void Eater’s body swelled, its obsidian skin stretching to the breaking point, its eyes glowing with a frantic, dying light. Then, with a sound like a thousand glass mountains shattering at once, the creature simply disintegrated.</p>
<p>SHATTERRRRRRRRR!</p>
