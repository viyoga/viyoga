<p>Behind him, sprawled across the cracked and blood-slicked masonry, Morwenna stirred. Her world was a spinning kaleidoscope of agony and ringing silence.</p>
<p>BEEEEEEEEEEEE...!</p>
<p>The high-pitched whine in her ears drowned out everything else as she forced one eye open, each movement feeling like hot glass sliding through her veins.</p>
<p>Through the haze of pain and the copper tang of blood filling her mouth, she saw him. She saw the silhouette of the man who held Valentina aloft like a broken doll.</p>
<p>A sound tore itself from her throat—not a battle cry, not a scream, but something far worse: a raw, wordless sound of pure, unadulterated grief.</p>
<p>It was a protest aimed at the universe, at the cruelty of fate, and at the man she had shared the intimacy of skin and breath with only days earlier.</p>
<p>’How?’ The question screamed in the silence of her mind. ’How could you be this monster?’</p>
<p>’How could you be him?’</p>
<p>Every instinct she had forged through thirty years of brutal warfare screamed at her to rise, to draw steel, to die on her feet. But her body was a traitor.</p>
<p>Her skull throbbed with a dull, distant agony where it had met the stone, and her limbs felt like they had been replaced with lead. She could not move fast enough to matter.</p>
<p>She could only lie there, a helpless spectator to a slaughter, watching the man she thought she knew become the architect of her destruction.</p>
<p>GASP... HHHH... CRACKLE.</p>
<p>Valentina’s struggle was growing weaker. Her face, once flushed with the fire of spellcasting, was now fading to a sickly, pale lavender.</p>
<p>Her fingers clawed uselessly at Xerollion’s forearm, her nails leaving faint red crescents in his skin that healed almost instantly. Her grimoire lay several feet away, dark, silent, and tantalizingly out of reach—a tombstone for her power.</p>
<p>The realization settled over Valentina with a terrifying, detached clarity: ’This is how it ends.’</p>
<p>Her vision began to tunnel, the edges of the world fraying into blackness.</p>
<p>Xerollion’s free hand began to glow.</p>
<p>VROOOOOOOOM.</p>
<p>A sphere of violet black energy, dense as a dying star and crackling with malevolent intent, began to coil in his palm. The air around his hand warped and screamed as the magical pressure intensified.</p>
<p>He was preparing to deliver the coup de grace, a spell designed to erase whatever remained of Valentina’s consciousness.</p>
<p>"Die with the dignity you fought so hard to maintain," Xerollion murmured, his voice like grinding stone.</p>
<p>WHAM!!!</p>
<p>Just as the energy reached its crescendo, a blur of motion erupted from the periphery. It wasn’t a spell; it was pure, kinetic fury.</p>
<p>SHHHH THWACK!</p>
<p>A strike, impossibly fast and carrying the force of a battering ram, slammed into Xerollion’s extended arm. The impact was so violent that the bone in his forearm audibly groaned under the stress.</p>
<p>CRUNCH.</p>
<p>The magical sphere detonated prematurely in a harmless shower of sparks, and the sudden shock forced his fingers to spasm open.</p>
<p>Valentina dropped like a stone.</p>
<p>THUD.</p>
<p>She collapsed onto her hands and knees, retching and gasping, her lungs burning as she drank in air in desperate, convulsing gulps.</p>
<p>COUGH. WHEEZE.</p>
<p>Her vision swam as she looked up through tear-blurred eyes.</p>
<p>The attacker didn’t retreat. The figure stepped forward with deliberate, measured steps, moving directly into the kill zone.</p>
<p>The stranger planted their feet between the gasping headmaster and the towering threat, shoulders squared, hands ready, radiating an aura of unmistakable, unshakable protective intent.</p>
<p>The dust settled around the newcomer’s boots. The light from the dying torches caught the glint of armor and the steady rise and fall of a chest that knew no fear.</p>
<p>Rex Rexilion had arrived.</p>
<p>"That’s enough."</p>
<p>The words didn’t carry the desperate scream of a survivor or the frantic command of a soldier. They were measured.</p>
<p>They were heavy. They possessed the kind of absolute certainty that could cut through the chaos of a collapsing corridor like a blade through silk.</p>
<p>Rex Rexilion stood there, his silhouette framed by the flickering torchlight and the swirling dust of the magical devastation.</p>
<p>His face was a mask of carefully constructed courage, a perfect, almost theatrical blend of righteous anger and unshakable resolve.</p>
<p>To any observer, he was the archetype made flesh: the hero who had finally decided to stop watching from the sidelines and claim his destiny.</p>
<p>"I won’t let you hurt them any further," Rex declared, his voice carrying across the stone chamber with the authority of a king.</p>
<p>His hands clenched into fists at his sides, the muscles in his forearms cording like steel cables.</p>
<p>A stunned, deathly silence descended upon the remaining soldiers.</p>
<p>"Is that... Rex?" one whispered, the words barely audible over the crackle of residual lightning. "He’s actually... he’s standing up to him."</p>
<p>A wave of astonishment rolled through the ranks; they watched with bated breath as the man they had known as a stalwart companion finally stepped into the light as their protector.</p>
<p>Across the carnage, Xerollion did not attack. He did not snarl.</p>
<p>Instead, he simply stood there, his masked head tilting to the side with a slow, predatory curiosity.</p>
<p>’Another plan... huh... it seems like I had to play the drama like the usual.’</p>
<p>Behind that impassive mask, Xerollion’s mind was racing at speeds no mortal could comprehend. His Divine Perception wasn’t just seeing Rex; it was dissecting him. He felt something distinctly... wrong.</p>
<p>A signature of energy was bleeding off the newcomer, a resonant frequency that felt disturbingly familiar. It was like hearing a melody played on two different instruments: one perfect, one slightly off-key.</p>
<p>The energy reading carried an almost identical quality to his own power, but it fell just short of perfection. It was a mirror that had been slightly warped.</p>
<p>"What are you?" Xerollion wondered, his interest piqued far more than his anger. "A shadow? A construct? A memory given flesh?"</p>
<p>The tension between them became a physical thing, a live wire stretched to the breaking point. The air between them hummed with the latent energy of two apex predators recognizing one another.</p>
<p>Xerollion took a single step forward, the sound of his boot grinding against broken stone echoing like a gunshot.</p>
<p>CRUNCH.</p>
<p>He lowered his voice, dropping it to a conspiratorial whisper that seemed to slice through the ambient noise of Valentina’s labored breathing and Morwenna’s pained whimpering.</p>
<p>"Interesting," Xerollion said softly, the word dripping with a cold, analytical amusement. "Standing here to protect them, Rex Rexilion."</p>
<p>"Playing the part of the stalwart guardian... The noble savior."</p>
<p>He paused, his masked gaze boring into Rex’s eyes as if trying to peel back the skin and read the soul beneath.</p>
<p>"I’m curious," Xerollion continued, his voice growing colder, more dangerous. "I’m curious exactly how far you’re willing to carry this role you’ve been playing."</p>
<p>"How long can you maintain the illusion before the mask cracks and shows me what’s truly underneath?"</p>
