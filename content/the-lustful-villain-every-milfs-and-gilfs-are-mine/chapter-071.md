<p>Rex nodded and stood up to leave, pleased with how well that had gone. He had taken about three steps toward the hallway when a hand grabbed his wrist and pulled.</p>
<p>He turned around and saw Helena, who had apparently been in the room the whole time without him noticing. This was impressive because he usually paid a lot of attention to where Helena was.</p>
<p>She took him into the side hallway and let go of his wrist. Then she crossed her arms and looked at him with an expression that was displeased and wanted a real answer.</p>
<p>"Telekinesis," she said.</p>
<p>’What...? She fucking knew...?!’ Rex thought about his choices for about two seconds.</p>
<p>His Foresight showed him a few ways to go. In most of them, he tried to change the subject, but Helena called him out with the accuracy of someone who had spent years figuring out when her kids were lying to her.</p>

<p>"Because I was new and you didn’t know me, and walking into a group of skilled fighters and saying you have an invisible force power usually goes one of two ways." Rex kept his voice steady. "Neither of them makes the other people feel at ease around you."</p>

<p>"That was the plan."</p>
<p>"And you kept it a secret because you were worried about how it would go over."</p>
<p>"More or less."</p>
<p>She stared at him for a long time, and he held her gaze and waited. At this point, his Foresight had shown him the greatest change.</p>
<p>What Helena did with this information would have effects on the next few weeks that were too many to fully predict.</p>

<p>"Your answers really made me think that I should’ve trusted you more, and maybe... I’m going to need more help from you in the future." Helena smiled motherly.</p>
<p>Rex didn’t expect that answer from an interrogation, which was useful information in and of itself. Even when she caught him lying, Helena’s initial reaction was to focus on the applications rather than the concealment.</p>
<p>She was the type of person who made decisions based on results.</p>
<p>And that would matter in the future.</p>
<p>"Don’t thank me yet," Rex said. "We’re not in the capital yet."</p>
<p>"No," Helena said, and the small smile she gave him as she turned away made it clear that she was looking forward to what came next.</p>
