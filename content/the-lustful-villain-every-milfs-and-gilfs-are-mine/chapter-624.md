<p>The air in the Underlayer was no longer oxygen; it was a thick, suffocating soup of copper-scented mist and the scorched aroma of incinerated flesh. Rex was no longer merely fighting; he had entered a state of transcendental carnage. The "performance management" was gone.</p>
<p>The restraint was dead. He was a god of the slaughter, and the rhythm of his destruction had become a frantic, beautiful madness.</p>
<p>"HAHAHAHAHAHA!"</p>
<p>The laugh erupted from Rex’s throat, not a joyful sound but a jagged, manic peal of pure, unadulterated ecstasy that sliced through the screams of the dying. It was the laugh of a man who had looked into the abyss and found it hilarious.</p>
<p>A massive, armored troll, its skin like weathered granite, stumbled toward him, its eyes wide with a terror it couldn’t comprehend. The beast fell to its knees, its massive hands trembling as it reached out toward the blood-drenched sovereign.</p>
<p>"Mercy... Great Lord... please... mercy!" it bellowed, its voice cracking with a pathetic, desperate hope.</p>
<p>Rex didn’t even slow down. He moved through the troll like a hot knife through lard.</p>
<p>SHLICK!</p>
<p>With a casual, sweeping motion of his telekinesis, he didn’t just strike the troll; he gripped its very essence. He twisted.</p>
<p>CRACK RRRRRRIP!</p>
<p>The creature’s spine didn’t just break; it spiraled out of its body in a grotesque, fleshy corkscrew. The troll’s plea was cut short by a wet, gurgling GLUG, as its lungs were crushed into a bloody pulp.</p>
<p>"Mercy?" Rex roared, his eyes wide and gleaming with a terrifying, manic light.</p>
<p>He stepped over the twitching corpse, his boots squelching in the gore. "You wanted mercy when you were charging?"</p>
<p>"You wanted mercy when you were screaming for your ’gods’ to save you? Now is the time for consequences!"</p>
<p>He turned his attention to a group of retreating Demonettes, their dark wings fluttering in a frantic, useless attempt to take flight. Rex didn’t let them escape.</p>
<p>He raised his hands, and the Elemental Mastery reacted to his frantic, soaring heartbeat. The very air around them began to vibrate at a lethal frequency.</p>
<p>HUMMMMMMM VREEEEEE!</p>
<p>The air became a vacuum of jagged sonic blades.</p>
<p>SH SH SH SH SH SH!</p>
<p>The Demonettes were caught in a localized cyclone of sound and pressure. Their wings were shredded into black ribbons, and their bodies were flayed layer by layer as the sonic waves tore through their skin.</p>
<p>They didn’t just die; they were disintegrated into a fine, dark mist of blood and shadow.</p>
<p>SPLAT!</p>
<p>Rex’s movements became increasingly erratic and more violent, fueled by a dark, intoxicating adrenaline. He was a blur of crimson and gold.</p>
<p>He caught a charging Orcish Berserker by the jaw and, with a burst of Physique and Earth Authority, he slammed the creature’s head directly into the ground with such force that the earth itself cratered.</p>
<p>BOOOOOM!</p>
<p>The orc’s skull didn’t just crack; it exploded like a ripened fruit, sending brain matter and bone shards flying into the eyes of the nearby soldiers.</p>
<p>SQUISH!</p>
<p>"More! GIVE ME MORE!" Rex screamed, his voice cracking with a manic fervor.</p>
<p>He was laughing again, a wild, breathless sound that sent a wave of pure dread through the survivors.</p>
<p>He saw a group of goblins huddled together, weeping, their tiny hands clasped in prayer to gods that had long since abandoned them. They were begging, their high-pitched whimpers a chorus of absolute submission.</p>
<p>Rex approached them, his shadow looming over them like a shroud of death.</p>
<p>"Please! We surrender! We submit!" the lead goblin shrieked, pressing its forehead into the blood-soaked dirt.</p>
<p>Rex leaned down, his face inches from the trembling creature. The manic grin on his face was wider than the horizon.</p>
<p>"Submission comes after the conquest," he whispered, his voice a terrifying, melodic caress. "But you... you’re just late."</p>
<p>He didn’t use a punch. He used his Creation Magic to manifest a dozen microscopic, razor-sharp needles of solidified gravity.</p>
<p>ZING! ZING! ZING!</p>
<p>The needles rained down upon the huddle of goblins. They didn’t just pierce; they pulled.</p>
<p>The gravitational force of each needle dragged the Goblins toward one another with such violent intensity that the creatures were crushed together into a single, mangled ball of screaming, overlapping limbs and broken bones.</p>
<p>CRUNCH SQUELCH!</p>
<p>He stood in the center of the massacre, chest heaving, his skin steaming from the heat of the blood and the elemental friction. He was a whirlwind of red, a deity of the genocide, and as he looked out at the remaining, trembling masses of the Underlayer, his laughter rose once more, a terrifying, triumphant sound that promised that the night was far from over, and the harvest had only just begun.</p>
<p>...</p>
<p>The battlefield had transitioned from a war to a slaughterhouse and finally to a hunting ground. The grand armies of the Underlayer were gone, reduced to a scattered collection of terrified, hiding animals.</p>
<p>The air was no longer filled with the roar of combat but with the heavy, suffocating silence of dread, broken only by the wet, rhythmic sounds of Rex’s continuing madness.</p>
<p>Rex stood amidst a literal sea of viscera, his breathing heavy but his eyes burning with a frantic, unquenchable light. The "targets" were gone.</p>
<p>There were no more phalanxes to break, no more champions to duel. Yet, the manic grin on his face didn’t fade; it sharpened.</p>
<p>He wasn’t looking for soldiers anymore; he was looking for life.</p>
<p>"Where are you?" Rex called out, his voice a jagged, melodic lilt that echoed through the ruins of the castle.</p>
<p>He began to laugh a high, breathless, terrifying sound that seemed to vibrate in the very marrow of the survivors’ bones. "Don’t tell me the great resistance and coalition are just... hiding?"</p>
<p>"Is this the end of the rebellion? Tucking your tails and praying the dark swallows you?"</p>
<p>"But... don’t you all fucking worry because we can move on to the next game..." Rex smirked. "Hide... and kill..."</p>
<p>He began to move, not with the heavy tread of a conqueror, but with the prowling, predatory grace of a wolf. He wasn’t just walking; he was haunting the landscape. He used his telekinesis to peel back the layers of the world.</p>
<p>SKREEEEEEE!</p>
<p>He ripped massive slabs of stone from the earth, tossing them aside like parchment to reveal the terrified Orcs and Goblins huddling in the dark crevices beneath.</p>
<p>CRUNCH!</p>
<p>He didn’t even look down as he brought his heel down on a hiding Goblin scout, the sound of the creature’s skull flattening like a ripe melon echoing in the quiet.</p>
<p>SPLAT!</p>
<p>"Found you," he whispered, a dark, delighted chuckle escaping his lips.</p>
<p>As he hunted, he noticed a subtle shift in the atmosphere. The few of his own forces, the elite survivors of his initial purge, were no longer standing in awe.</p>
<p>They were catching his fever. Inspired by his absolute, unhinged dominance, they began to fan out, their eyes reflecting the same bloodlust. They became his hounds.</p>
<p>A group of his soldiers, driven by a primal, infectious madness, began to descend into the trenches and the dark tunnels. Rex watched with a smug, satisfied tilt of his head as they began to drag screaming survivors into the light.</p>
<p>SHLICK! THUD!</p>
<p>The sounds of their uncoordinated, brutal killings provided a rhythmic percussion to Rex’s manic laughter.</p>
<p>"Yes!" Rex roared, throwing his head back as he watched a soldier impale a surrendering demon on a jagged piece of rebar. "Don’t let them breathe!"</p>
<p>"If they have a pulse, they are an insult to my victory!"</p>
<p>He turned his attention to a massive, hollowed-out fallen titan, a creature whose corpse had become a makeshift bunker for dozens of retreating soldiers. They were huddled in the dark, praying, weeping, believing the massive carcass would shield them from the god of destruction.</p>
<p>Rex’s eyes narrowed. A slow, predatory smirk spread across his face.</p>
<p>"A fortress of meat," he mused. "How poetic."</p>
<p>He didn’t just attack the titan; he manipulated the very elements within it. He plunged his hands into the earth and channeled his Elemental Mastery directly into the ground beneath the titan.</p>
<p>VROOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOM!</p>
<p>The ground didn’t just shake; it liquefied into a boiling, pressurized sludge of mud and magma.</p>
<p>GLOOP! HISS!</p>
<p>The soldiers inside the titan began to scream as the floor beneath them turned into a churning, scalding cauldron.</p>
<p>SCREEEEEE!</p>
<p>But Rex wasn’t finished. He used his telekinesis to seize the very air inside the titan’s hollowed body, creating a sudden, violent vacuum.</p>
<p>WHHHHHOOOOOOOOOP!</p>
<p>The immense pressure differential caused the soldiers’ lungs to literally implode, sucking their bodies toward the center of the vacuum with sickening force.</p>
<p>POP! CRUNCH!</p>
<p>As the titan’s carcass finally burst open from the internal pressure, a spray of blood, bone, and half-melted armor erupted into the air like a macabre fountain.</p>
<p>KRA SPLAT!</p>
<p>Rex stood in the center of the carnage, his arms spread wide, his chest heaving as he bathed in the spray of the dying. He was laughing harder now, a loud, unhinged, maniacal cackle that signaled the complete dissolution of his sanity into the pure joy of genocide.</p>
<p>He was no longer a man, nor a king, nor even a god; he was the personification of the end of all things, a beautiful, terrifying nightmare that refused to let the world die quietly.</p>
