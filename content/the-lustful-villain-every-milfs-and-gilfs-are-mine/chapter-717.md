<p>There was a chilling, weary quality to her voice, the sound of a woman who had heard that exact same boast from a hundred different "gods" over three decades and had found every single one of them wanting.</p>
<p>The exchange was a nightmare of high-speed violence. For eight agonizing minutes, the plaza became a theater of the impossible.</p>
<p>Space bent and snapped; stone was pulverized into fine mist; blood sprayed in rhythmic, violent bursts as Morwenna’s precision strikes found the gaps in Rex’s defenses. It was a dance of gods and masters, a collision of absolute power and absolute experience, where a single millisecond of hesitation meant a shattered bone or a severed limb.</p>
<p>The air was thick with the sound of grinding rock, the hiss of displaced space, and the heavy, rhythmic breathing of two predators locked in a struggle for total dominance.</p>
<p>The eight minutes that followed were not a fight; they were a localized apocalypse. To any onlooker, the two combatants were nothing more than a blurred strobing of light, shadow, and flying debris. The sheer speed at which they moved meant that the human eye could only perceive the aftermath—the craters, the blood, the shattered stone—rather than the actual exchange.</p>
<p>BOOM! CRACK THOOM!</p>
<p>Rex initiated the onslaught by slamming his palms into the ground. The entire plaza didn’t just shake; it heaved.</p>
<p>A massive, jagged wave of granite, twenty feet high and sharp as a serrated blade, tore through the earth toward Morwenna.</p>
<p>"DIE!" Rex roared, his voice a guttural command to the very tectonic plates.</p>
<p>Morwenna didn’t retreat. She sprinted into the wave.</p>
<p>As the stone wall reached her, she performed a microadjustment of her spatial field. The air in front of her rippled like heat haze on a desert road.</p>
<p>SHHH LINK!</p>
<p>The massive wave of stone hit the distortion and didn’t crash; it slid. The kinetic energy was diverted upward, the massive wave curling over her head like a harmless ribbon of water, the wind of its passage nearly stripping the skin from her face.</p>
<p>Before the stone could settle, she was already on his guard.</p>
<p>WHIP CRACK!</p>
<p>Her movement was so sudden it sounded like a sonic boom. She appeared in Rex’s personal space, her palm striking his chest.</p>
<p>It wasn’t a heavy blow, but it was perfectly timed with a burst of her spatial energy. Rex felt his lungs compress as the air was literally sucked out of his chest cavity.</p>
<p>"GURK!" He coughed, a spray of crimson hitting the ground, but he was too fast to be caught off guard.</p>
<p>He used his earthen authority to instantly erupt a pillar of basalt directly beneath his own feet, launching himself backward to create distance.</p>
<p>THUD CRUNCH!</p>
<p>As he ascended, he sent a volley of stone shards, hundreds of them, each the size of a dagger, spiraling toward her like a swarm of furious hornets.</p>
<p>SKREEEEE!</p>
<p>The shards whistled through the air.</p>
<p>Morwenna danced through the storm. It was a terrifying display of economy; she didn’t dodge with wide, frantic leaps.</p>
<p>Instead, she performed tiny, precise tilts of her head and microscopic shifts of her hips. She moved through the gaps in the projectiles with the grace of a needle threading silk.</p>
<p>"Too slow, you bastard!" Rex yelled, his eyes glowing a deep, volcanic orange.</p>
<p>He clenched his fist, and the very air around Morwenna seemed to thicken. He was using his authority to increase the local gravity, trying to pin her down.</p>
<p>"GRRRRHNNN!" Morwenna grunted, her knees buckling for a fraction of a second as the weight of the world pressed down on her shoulders.</p>
<p>The cobblestones beneath her boots shattered under the sudden pressure.</p>
<p>CRACKLE!</p>
<p>But she didn’t stay pinned. She used the very pressure Rex was exerting against her.</p>
<p>She channeled the gravitational force into a singular, downward burst of her own energy, essentially "falling" faster than the gravity could hold her, turning the downward pressure into a horizontal launch.</p>
<p>"FUCK!" Rex hissed as she breached his line.</p>
<p>She was a blur of lethal intent. Her hand caught the edge of his jaw, her fingers digging into the muscle.</p>
<p>CRACK!</p>
<p>The sound of his mandible straining was sickening. She followed the strike with a knee to his solar plexus, a blow so precise it bypassed his muscular armor and sent a shockwave straight into his internal organs.</p>
<p>"OOF—HAA!" Rex staggered, his vision swimming with white spots.</p>
<p>He didn’t wait to recover. He reached out, his hand catching her ankle as she tried to pull away.</p>
<p>"GOTCHA!"</p>
<p>"SHATTER!" Rex commanded.</p>
<p>The ground beneath Morwenna didn’t just rise; it exploded upward in a series of violent, jagged spikes.</p>
<p>"ARRGH!" Morwenna let out a sharp, rare cry of pain as a stone spike grazed her thigh, tearing through her clothing and carving a deep, bloody furrow in her skin.</p>
<p>SPLAT!</p>
<p>A spray of her blood hit Rex’s face.</p>
<p>But even as she was lifted, she was attacking. She twisted in mid-air, her body coiling like a spring.</p>
<p>She brought her heel down on Rex’s forearm with the force of a falling hammer.</p>
<p>KRA KOOOOM!</p>
<p>Rex’s arm buckled, the bone groaning under the immense pressure.</p>
<p>"GODDAMN IT!" he snarled, the pain fueling his madness.</p>
<p>He released her ankle and slammed both hands onto the ground, creating a massive, localized earthquake that sent Morwenna spiraling into the air.</p>
<p>BOOM! BOOM! BOOM!</p>
<p>He followed up by launching massive, fist-sized boulders from the earth, timed to intercept her while she was airborne. The fight moved from the ground to the sky in a chaotic, vertical ballet of violence.</p>
<p>CLANG! SMASH! WHACK!</p>
<p>Morwenna used the falling boulders as stepping stones, her feet hitting the moving stone with impossible precision. She was a predator navigating a shifting landscape of death.</p>
<p>She leaped from one boulder to the next, closing the distance to Rex, who was now conjuring a shield of dense, compressed, diamond-hard stone.</p>
<p>"BREAK!" she screamed, her palm hitting his shield.</p>
<p>The impact was a deafening KRA THOOM! that shook the entire plaza, shattering every remaining window in the surrounding buildings.</p>
<p>The two of them collided in midair, a whirlwind of limbs, magic, and raw, unbridled fury. For the remaining minutes, the plaza was a vortex of dust and blood.</p>
<p>They were moving so fast that the sound of their combat became a continuous, terrifying roar, a symphony of breaking bones, tearing flesh, and the thunderous percussion of the earth being torn asunder. It was a relentless, high-octane exchange where every millisecond was a gamble between a killing blow and a fatal mistake.</p>
<p>Rex was a force of nature, a tectonic god throwing the very foundations of the world at his opponent. But Morwenna... Morwenna was something far more terrifying. She was the master of the moment.</p>
<p>Despite the decades etched into the subtle lines around her eyes, the mark of a woman who had lived through eras of blood, she moved with a terrifying, predatory lightness that defied her age. She wasn’t just a combatant; she was a grandmaster of the fundamental laws of reality.</p>
<p>"TAKE THIS, YOU OLD BITCH!" Rex roared, his eyes burning like dying stars.</p>
<p>He slammed his fists together, and the air itself seemed to solidify.</p>
<p>CRUSH!</p>
<p>A massive, invisible hammer of pressurized gravity slammed down on Morwenna’s position. The ground beneath her didn’t just crack; it pulverized into a fine, grey powder.</p>
<p>KRA THOOOOM!</p>
<p>Rex grinned through the sweat and the stinging spray of blood on his face. He loved this.</p>
<p>The sheer, unadulterated intensity of it, the way his bones vibrated with every impact, the way the adrenaline made his blood feel like liquid fire. It wasn’t that he sought the pain, but he craved the friction of a fight that actually pushed him to the brink of annihilation.</p>
<p>But Morwenna didn’t collapse.</p>
<p>As the gravity hammer descended, she didn’t fight the weight; she threaded it. With a movement so fluid it looked like water flowing around a stone, she twisted her torso.</p>
<p>The gravitational force, meant to flatten her, instead caught the edges of her spatial field and was spun outward in a violent, centrifugal burst.</p>
<p>WHIRRRRR SNAP!</p>
<p>The pressure that should have crushed her ribs was instead redirected into the ground, creating a massive crater that spiraled away from her in a perfect circle. Before the dust could even settle, she was moving.</p>
<p>She didn’t run; she flickered.</p>
<p>FWOOSH!</p>
<p>She appeared behind Rex, her hand moving in a precise, whiplike motion. Her fingers, calloused by decades of mastery, struck the nerve cluster at the base of his neck.</p>
<p>THWACK!</p>
<p>"AGH!" Rex gasped, his entire left side momentarily going numb.</p>
<p>It wasn’t a heavy blow, but it was a perfect one. The precision was insulting, and it was the difference between being hit by a sledgehammer and being pierced by a needle.</p>
<p>"Is that all?" Rex hissed, spinning around with a feral grin, his arm already conjuring a jagged blade of obsidian. "You’re getting slow, Morwenna!"</p>
<p>"Slow?" Her voice was a calm, chilling whisper amidst the roar of the battle. "You’re just loud, boy."</p>
<p>She stepped into his guard, her movements a masterclass in efficiency. As Rex swung the obsidian blade in a wide, murderous arc, VREEEEEE! she didn’t retreat.</p>
<p>She stepped into the swing, her shoulder dipping just enough to let the blade whistle past her ear by a fraction of an inch.</p>
<p>CRUNCH!</p>
<p>Her elbow connected with his ribs. It wasn’t a brute-force strike; it was a concentrated burst of kinetic energy focused into a single point.</p>
<p>Rex felt the sickening sensation of a rib snapping, the sharp, white-hot flare of pain radiating through his chest.</p>
<p>"HAAAH!" He spat a mouthful of crimson, but the pain only made him laugh harder.</p>
<p>"YES! YES! DO IT AGAIN!" he bellowed, his madness rising.</p>
