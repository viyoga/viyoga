<p>Rex didn’t just select the skill; he unleashed it. The moment his finger pressed the conceptual trigger, the world around him seemed to fracture.</p>

<p>The system interface didn’t merely vanish; it shattered like glass, sending shards of blue light dissolving into the smoke-choked air.</p>

<p>The agony was unlike anything his Infinite Regeneration had ever encountered. It wasn’t the pain of a blade or the burn of fire; it was the sensation of his very essence being forcibly rewritten.</p>

<p>It felt as though molten lead was being poured directly into his marrow, each nerve ending screaming in a chorus of agony that threatened to unravel his consciousness.</p>

<p>’Hold it together,’ Rex roared internally, his teeth grinding so hard a molar cracked with a wet snap. ’Don’t let it consume you.’</p>

<p>’Command it.’</p>

<p>Through the haze of excruciating pain, he lifted his right hand. His fingers trembled, blood dripping from his split knuckles as he pointed directly at the restrained Leonidas.</p>

<p>In that moment of transcendent suffering, Rex fused his capabilities. Soul Manipulation and Divine Perception spiraled together in a single, desperate motion of absolute will.</p>

<p>He didn’t just look at Leonidas; he looked through him.</p>

<p>His consciousness plunged past the flesh, past the shattered ribs, past the bleeding organs, diving deep into the metaphysical architecture of the man’s being.</p>

<p>He followed the silent, invisible threads that stretched from Leonidas’s tortured soul toward the source of his power. It was like plunging his mind into a pressurized abyss, following a cable through freezing, ink-black water.</p>

<p>At the far end of that connection, he felt it: the Weight. A vast, smoldering presence that existed somewhere beyond the veil of the world, an entity both indifferent and incomprehensibly enormous.</p>

<p>It was a cosmic furnace of pure, unadulterated rage. Rex did not attempt to seize it, for trying to grab such a thing would be like trying to catch a supernova in a butterfly net, but he did not recoil either.</p>

<p>Instead, he became a mirror.</p>

<p>Using the precision of his Soul Manipulation, he began to trace the exact geometric pattern of the divine corruption.</p>

<p>He mapped every jagged edge, every pulsing vein of the Wrath authority, weaving its twin directly into the fabric of his own soul register. He wasn’t stealing Leonidas’s fire; he was learning how to forge his own.</p>

<p>Then came the physical manifestation.</p>

<p>A dark red emblem began to burn through Rex’s skin, erupting from his sternum directly over his heart. The jagged, angry lines crawled outward across his chest like living parasites, searing through muscle and sinew.</p>

<p>The heat was unbearable. As the new mark spread, it collided with the violet sigil of the Mark of Lust already burning at his wrist.</p>

<p>The two divine authorities met inside his body like two warring gods. Inside his chest, it felt like a white-hot fist was closing around a block of ice.</p>

<p>The two powers recoiled against each other, Lust’s insatiable, cold craving clashing violently against Wrath’s consuming, volcanic fury.</p>

<p>KRA KOOOOM!</p>

<p>The garden shuddered violently. The earth groaned as if the world itself were breaking under the strain of hosting two conflicting sins.</p>

<p>A massive shockwave rolled out from Rex’s body in a visible distortion of air, bending the scorched grass flat for a hundred yards in every direction and sending debris flying like shrapnel.</p>

<p>For three heartbeats, the conflict threatened to tear Rex apart from the inside out.</p>

<p>Then, with a wet, grinding sound that echoed in his bones, they settled. Like two apex predators reluctantly sharing the same den, the two powers stabilized.</p>

<p>The strange, ethereal violet aura that had always surrounded him began to bleed, mixing with a new, viscous crimson. The colors swirled together, forming a slow, hypnotic double spiral of amethyst and blood red energy that curled around his limbs like living smoke.</p>

<p>The silence returned, heavier than before. The telekinetic threads holding Leonidas still flickered with the new electromagnetic interference of Rex’s transformed state.</p>

<p>Rex’s head lifted slowly. His pupils were no longer human; they were twin vortices of violet and red fire.</p>

<p>He opened his eyes.</p>

<p>The transformation wasn’t complete until the psychology of the power took root.</p>

<p>As the crimson mark fused with his soul register, a tidal wave of alien emotion crashed into Rex’s consciousness. It wasn’t just power; it was a foreign consciousness.</p>

<p>Anger flooded his mind—not the hot, impulsive anger of a child, but a vast, ancient, oceanic tide of rage.</p>

<p>For a terrifying moment, Rex’s vision turned pure scarlet. He understood, with a clarity that made his stomach turn, what Leonidas had been carrying.</p>

<p>It was the grief of a broken man, the accumulated fury of someone who had watched every cherished memory, every loved one, and every hope be torn apart piece by bloody piece.</p>

<p>The weight of that grief was staggering.</p>

<p>For half a second, Rex felt himself drowning in it, his own identity threatening to be smothered by the sheer magnitude of Leonidas’s suffering.</p>

<p>Then, something twisted inside him. Something cold and calculating.</p>

<p>Rex let out a laugh, a short, sharp, bitter sound that cut through the smoke like a blade.</p>

<p>HA!</p>

<p>"Is that all?" he whispered, his voice dripping with a terrifying amusement.</p>

<p>He realized then how absurdly simple this was. The mistake Leonidas had made was allowing the emotion to drive the power.</p>

<p>Leonidas was a slave to the rage; he was the fuel being consumed by the fire. But Rex was not a man who allowed emotions to dictate his reality.</p>

<p>He had never truly been angry, not in the way this power demanded. His anger was always clinical, always purposeful.</p>

<p>’Wrath in the hands of a furious man is a bonfire,’ Rex thought, feeling the crimson energy coil around his ribs like a serpent. ’Wild, consuming, and ultimately suicidal.’</p>

<p>’But Wrath in the hands of someone who feels nothing?’</p>

<p>’That is not a fire. That is a scalpel.’</p>

<p>He didn’t let the rage consume him; he harnessed it. He didn’t become the storm; he became the eye of it.</p>

<p>He grabbed the raw, chaotic energy of the Wrath authority and forced it into a shape of his own choosing. He didn’t let the power pull him forward; he pulled the power.</p>

<p>[Mark of Wrath Authority: ABSORBED (Resonance Copy)]</p>

<p>[Warning: Soul integration complete.]</p>

<p>[Dual Sin Authority active: Lust, Wrath]</p>

<p>Across the devastated garden, the effect on Leonidas was instantaneous and catastrophic.</p>

<p>The Mark carved into Leonidas’s chest shuddered violently. The brilliant, white-hot glow that had been his lifeline for the last hour suddenly flickered and stuttered like a candle caught in a hurricane.</p>

<p>Rex had not stolen the power, but he had achieved a deeper, more terrifying resonance with the same divine frequency. In the presence of Rex’s new, amplified authority, Leonidas’s flame began to shrink.</p>

<p>The crimson lines on Leonidas’s skin drained of their light, fading from white hot to a sickly blood red, then down to a dull, dying ember glow.</p>

<p>The supernatural strength that had been propelling him forward ebbed away. His muscles slackened, his spine bowed, and the terrifying aura that had made him seem like a god began to dissipate like mist in the sun.</p>

<p>Leonidas’s eyes widened. A raw, primal horror flooded his expression as he felt his very essence being hollowed out, not by force, but by being outshone.</p>

<p>"No," Leonidas rasped, the word breaking in his throat. Blood spattered his lips as he coughed. "No... I won’t...!"</p>

<p>"I won’t stop...! I will not let you—"</p>

<p>His defiance was cut short by the sheer impossibility of what he was seeing. The man who had been struggling to survive was gone. In his place stood something far more predatory.</p>

<p>Rex didn’t walk. He didn’t run.</p>

<p>He simply was there. One moment he was twenty meters away; the next, the space between them had been erased by a burst of violet and crimson speed that defied human perception.</p>

<p>Rex was already in front of him.</p>
