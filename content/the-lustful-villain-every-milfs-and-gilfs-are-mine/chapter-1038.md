<p>The amber gold radiance of the domain suddenly intensified, surging upward like a solar flare. Inside Theo’s chest, something fundamental shifted.</p>
<p>The temporal designation wasn’t just sitting within him like a foreign object; it was nesting. He could feel it in his soul register a new, cold, and infinitely precise tool orienting itself.</p>
<p>It was the sensation of a blade finding its balance in a warrior’s grip, probing the boundaries of its own lethality, mapping out the parameters of what it could reach and what it could shatter.</p>
<p>It was hungry. And Theo was the hand that would feed it.</p>
<p>He didn’t hesitate. He reached inward, grasping for the most immediate, most visceral application of this new divinity.</p>
<p>The designation responded instantly, presenting its primary function to his consciousness with the surgical clarity of a weapon being unsheathed.</p>
<p>’Time Stop.’</p>
<p>It wasn’t the grand, universe-altering freeze of a god; it was something more intimate, more dangerous. A local rupture.</p>
<p>A brief, controlled severance of causality within a limited radius. But to Theo, it felt like holding the throat of reality itself.</p>
<p>’Test it,’ his mind commanded. ’See if you can break the flow.’</p>
<p>He willed it. He shoved his newfound authority outward.</p>
<p>The domain screamed not audibly, but temporally. The amber gold symbols that had been dancing in their eternal, flowing patterns suddenly jerked and locked.</p>
<p>The motion died. The universe didn’t go dark; it simply became static.</p>
<p>The symbols froze mid-spiral, suspended like insects trapped in ancient resin. Even Kaeleth, the eternal constant, found himself ensnared in the eerie stillness of a being caught between one moment and the next.</p>
<p>The God was still there, but the flow that allowed him to act had been severed.</p>
<p>’One second.’ The silence was absolute, a deafening void where even the concept of sound had ceased to exist.</p>
<p>’Two seconds.’ Theo felt the strain, a sharp, metallic tang in the back of his throat, the sensation of holding back an ocean with a single finger.</p>
<p>’Three seconds.’</p>
<p>Then, Theo released it.</p>
<p>The collapse of the stop was not a crash but a seamless reintegration. Reality rushed back in with a terrifying fluidity, as if the paused moments had never been interrupted but merely folded into the continuity.</p>
<p>The symbols resumed their dance; Kaeleth remained unruffled, though his eyes held a flicker of something new recognition.</p>
<p>Theo stared down at his hands.</p>
<p>In the physical world, they looked the same scarred, pale, human. But as he focused through the temporal register, he saw them for what they truly were: the soul signed physical extensions of a predator.</p>
<p>He saw the way his very atoms now hummed with the signature of the Apostle. In the soul register, the temporal designation wasn’t just present; it was integrated.</p>
<p>It was a living part of his intent, already coiled and ready, already orienting itself toward the singular purpose he had carved into his own heart.</p>
<p>He wasn’t just Theo Morr Nightwing anymore. He was the glitch in the system.</p>
<p>He was the error that would delete Rex Rexilion from existence.</p>
<p>He lifted his gaze to Kaeleth. His eyes were no longer those of a man seeking answers; they were the eyes of a man who had become the answer.</p>
<p>The emptiness he had discovered earlier had hardened into a diamond sharp focus. There was no more room for fear, no more room for doubt. There was only the mission.</p>
<p>"Send me back," Theo commanded, his voice carrying the absolute authority of a man who had already decided how the story would end.</p>
<p>...</p>
<p>Objectively speaking, the last month had been the most productive period of Rex Rexilion’s existence since his arrival in Erosyne.</p>
<p>It had been a month defined by conquest not merely of territory or power, but of the very souls of the women who defined the Nightwing legacy.</p>
<p>He stood in the rear courtyard of the Nightwing household as the first pale rays of dawn bled across the horizon. The morning air was crisp, carrying the faint scent of dew and damp earth, but Rex didn’t feel the chill.</p>
<p>He stretched with the slow, deliberate thoroughness of a predator who had been running a sustained, high-output operation and was now granting his body the grim acknowledgment of its own efficiency.</p>
<p>Every muscle coiled and released with flawless precision, a testament to the absolute control he now wielded over his own physicality.</p>
<p>Behind his eyes, the system’s notification queue hummed with the residual energy of his recent successes. The queue had been active since the previous night, pulsing with the confirmation of his most recent claim.</p>
<p>[PREGNANCY CONFIRMED Iris Morr Nightwing. Free Skill Selection unlocked.]</p>
<p>The first notification had arrived twelve days prior, a different kind of victory:</p>
<p>[PREGNANCY CONFIRMED Aurelia Nightwing. Free Skill Selection unlocked.]</p>
<p>Rex raised his arms above his head, staring up at the shifting colors of the sky. His mind wandered back through the month, dissecting his methods with the cold detachment of a strategist.</p>
<p>The training sessions with Aurelia had been... genuine. That was a rare commodity in his world.</p>
<p>He didn’t need to exert himself; his martial arts mastery made the performance of flawless, world-class instruction as effortless as breathing, but the value had lain in her reaction.</p>
<p>Aurelia was not some naive girl to be manipulated with empty theatrics. She possessed a perceptive function that predated any system ability: twenty more years of accumulated assessment, a lifetime of watching masters and frauds, and knowing exactly what real effort looked like.</p>
<p>She would have recognized a performance within seconds.</p>
<p>So, he hadn’t performed. He had given her real training.</p>
<p>He had pushed her body to its limits, watching the sweat drip from her brow and the fire ignite in her eyes as she grasped the true mechanics of combat. She had responded like an apex student, seizing the instruction with a hunger that matched his own.</p>
<p>Iris, however, had been a different challenge entirely. Her process was not one of technical mastery but of navigating the jagged wreckage of grief while simultaneously maintaining the deadly discipline of an assassin.</p>
<p>She was a woman who moved through the world with a blade in one hand and a memory in the other.</p>
<p>Rex hadn’t tried to push her grief aside; he had integrated it. His soul manipulation had revealed that Veylor’s soul anchor remained accessible within the communication window, a fragile, spectral bridge between the living and the dead.</p>
<p>Eleven days ago, when the window’s optimal conditions aligned, Rex had facilitated the impossible.</p>
<p>He had acted as the technical intermediary, the cold conduit through which Iris could reach across the veil. For twenty minutes, he maintained the soul level connection with the flat, professional precision of a surgeon.</p>
<p>He held the bridge steady while Iris spoke to the ghost of the person she had lost, allowing her to receive the words that grief had kept suspended in the air like poison gas.</p>
<p>’I didn’t listen,’ he reminded himself.</p>
<p>The thought brought no guilt, only satisfaction. He had granted her privacy because privacy was the currency of authenticity.</p>
<p>If it had been a clinical procedure, it would have been meaningless. By refusing to eavesdrop, he had made the experience real for her.</p>
<p>When she emerged from that conversation, she wasn’t the same woman. She was something new, someone who had taken what she needed from the dead and was now deciding how to wield it in the world of the living.</p>
<p>The system had quantified it immediately afterward. Her desire level had spiked to 78/100.</p>
<p>It wasn’t the absolute maximum, but it was a profound reading of the specific signature of someone whose deepest wound and deepest connection had been tended to by the same hand.</p>
<p>And that number had been climbing steadily ever since, tracked by the Desire Authority System as Rex continued his sustained, genuine engagement with her.</p>
<p>Two days ago, the climb had ended. The system had confirmed the maximum bond during a session that had been as intimate as it was transformative.</p>
<p>And last night, the system had delivered its final, undeniable proof of his dominion.</p>
<p>Rex let out a slow exhale, watching the steam of his breath dissipate into the morning air. The Nightwing bloodline was changing. It was being rewritten by his hand.</p>
