<p>The six demon maids stood in a loose semicircle around Rex. Their body language told him everything he needed to know about how they felt about the situation at the time.</p>
<p>Even though Lilith gave them a warm welcome, they were suspicious, cautious, and more than a little hostile.</p>
<p>Rex takes a good look at them, one by one, and they look rather unique for his taste. And it makes him think even more that Mordecai was a stupid virgin reincarnator that didn’t take any chances on them.</p>
<p>"Now I’m fucking curious to take a look at this fraud demon lord for making all these demon maids still feel pure in my eyes," Rex thought to himself. "Maybe I should use psychological dominance on him to see if I can harvest a lot of energy."</p>
<p>The first person, well, a demon, to talk was a maid with dark purple hair styled in twin tails and yellow eyes that sparkled with a cunning intelligence. Her uniform was more complicated than the others, with extra frills and what looked like functional armor pieces built into the design.</p>
<p>"I know that Lilith always talks a lot of nonsense, but feeling your weird presence is rather..." She crossed her arms and gave Rex a look that said she didn’t believe him.</p>
<p>"So you want us to believe that you, a stranger in a suspicious mask, just happened to save Lilith out of the goodness of your heart?" Her voice was sharp and accusatory. "Right... before our lord’s attack fails and she conveniently brings you to our meeting place?"</p>
<p>"That’s not suspicious at all."</p>
<p>"Awww~! Come on now, Nyx, he really did save me!" Lilith protested, but Nyx grabbed her shoulder and pulled her back forcefully.</p>
<p>"Your worthless nympho mind has breached your security," Nyx flatly stated. "Everyone knows that succubi can be easily manipulated by mind control or charm magic."</p>
<p>"Judging by his presence... he probably used one of those on you."</p>
<p>Another maid, this one with red eyes and silver hair like Lilith’s, nodded in agreement. "She’s theoretically correct!"</p>
<p>"This whole thing smells like a trap... For all we know, he’s an apostle of life in disguise trying to get into Lord Mordecai’s inner circle."</p>
<p>Rex didn’t answer when the maid just said ’apostle of life.’ For now he just wants to see where this conversation will lead him, while also studying them all.</p>
<p>A third maid, who was smaller than the others and had cat-like ears and a tail, hissed softly. "It’s the safest option to kill him now and ask questions later."</p>
<p>Rex watched the proceedings happen with a detached sense of humor. The maids were doing exactly what excellent employees should do when they think there might be a security breach.</p>
<p>They were protecting their lord and their organization by defaulting to suspicion instead of trust.</p>
<p>Unfortunately for the maids, their lord did not have time for lengthy explanations or trust-building activities.</p>
<p>"Are you all fucking stupid bitches that shared one brain cell or what?" Rex said, "One of your allies, Lilith, told you I saved her life."</p>
<p>The mask changed his voice so that it sounded like something not human. "You don’t believe her when she says I killed an apostle of justice without any trouble because you think she’s been compromised or tricked."</p>
<p>"It’s really the truth, isn’t it? All demon bitches only share one brain cell." Rex said with a mocking tone.</p>
<p>They looked pissed when he said that and almost attacked him, but he had already made a force field with his telekinesis so that no one could get close to him.</p>
<p>He stopped for a moment, letting the silence last. "Okay, I’ll prove it..."</p>
<p>"Who wants to see if I can do what she said I could do?"</p>
<p>"I’ll just fight any of you to prove it." Rex raised his right hand to taunt them one by one. "I can just beat you without trying."</p>
<p>Nyx, the maid with the purple hair, stepped forward right away. "Okay then, hot shot. It seems like you really went overboard with your worthless words."</p>
<p>"I’ll check your claims myself. If you’re as strong as Lilith says, then you probably won’t mind a simple test."</p>
<p>"Come at me then. Make sure to fight with your killing instinct as a demon."</p>
<p>"Ohh... you really did it this time!"</p>
<p>She moved like a predator, pulling out a pair of nasty-looking daggers from her uniform. "Show me this incredible power that is said to have turned an apostle into liquid meat."</p>
<p>Rex’s Foresight made it very clear what would happen in the next sixty seconds. Nyx would attack with the real goal of killing him, testing both his reflexes and his willingness to fight back.</p>
<p>The other maids would watch closely and judge how he reacted. If he showed any weakness or doubt, they would all attack him at once.</p>
<p>"Fucking great... This is another perfect chance for me to prove my worth to those demons."</p>
<p>"Enough talk," Rex said simply. "Just come at me right now before I get bored."</p>
<p>Nyx lunged, and even by demon standards, she was very fast. Her daggers came at her opponent from two different angles at the same time, a practiced move that made them choose which attack to block while leaving themselves open to the other.</p>
<p>Rex’s telekinesis stopped both blades in mid-strike, holding them so tightly that they felt like they were stuck in concrete. Nyx’s eyes got bigger as she tried to pull back, but the daggers wouldn’t move.</p>
<p>"W-What...?!" Nyx looked shocked. "T-That’s impossible...! What level of telekinesis is this!?"</p>
<p>"Full fucking potential, bitch."</p>
<p>After that, Rex showed off his power.</p>
<p>He moved the telekinetic grip from the daggers to Nyx herself and lifted her off the ground like someone would pick up a piece of paper. Her feet left the ground, and invisible hands held her body three feet in the air.</p>
<p>"Urgghhhh~!" She made a startled sound. "Fuck...! I underestimate him...!"</p>
<p>"Let me show you and all your bitches a good demonstration of trusting a masked man like me," Rex said.</p>
<p>Then he pushed her down to the ground with full force.</p>
<p>The force of the impact caused a crater in the ground and left her breathless with an explosive gasp. Rex picked her up again and threw her sideways into a tree trunk before she could get better.</p>
<p>The wood broke when it hit Nyx, and her body crumpled against it before sliding down to the ground.</p>
<p>"Stop!" one of the other maids yelled, but Rex didn’t listen.</p>
<p>He picked Nyx up again and spun her through the air like a ragdoll before throwing her back down to the ground from a different angle. Then it hit another tree.</p>
<p>Then they fell face-first into the dirt.</p>
<p>Then it hit a boulder in the back, which broke under the force of the impact.</p>
<p>His Foresight told him exactly how each crash would happen. Hard enough to hurt, hard enough to show complete control, but not hard enough to hurt for good.</p>
<p>He was trying to make a point, not hurt her other than by making a fool of herself.</p>
<p>"Isn’t this embarrassing? You, out of all demons, felt like a puppet!" Rex laughed. "Is this the quality of all the demons that Mordecai leads?!"</p>
<p>Rex finally picked Nyx up again after the seventh or eighth hit and held her there, about six feet off the ground. Then his telekinesis wrapped around her neck, and an invisible hand held it just tight enough to make it hard for her to breathe.</p>
<p>"Let’s see if you can last through this one..."</p>
<p>Nyx’s hands instinctively went up to claw at the force that was choking her, but there was nothing to grab. Her legs kicked around in the air for no reason, and the pressure and lack of oxygen made her face turn red.</p>
<p>The other maids stood still, horrified and fascinated at the same time. Lilith looked torn between being worried about her friend and being pleased that Rex was proving her right.</p>
<p>And then something happened that was not planned at all.</p>
<p>Nyx’s face changed. The fear and panic went away and were replaced by something else.</p>
<p>Her struggles got less desperate and more trying. Her eyes, which had been wide with fear, now had a very different kind of intensity until a heart symbol appeared.</p>
<p>And she cried out. "Hyaahhhnnnn~!"</p>
<p>It wasn’t a sound of pain or worry. It was a moan of pure pleasure that didn’t belong in any other context.</p>
<p>"What the fuck...?" Rex was caught off guard seeing her moan out of nowhere.</p>

<p>’Masochist... that explains why.’</p>
<p>Rex’s grip on her throat got a little looser, enough for her to breathe but not enough for him to let her go. Nyx’s moan got louder.</p>
<p>"Ahhhhnnn~! Harder~!"</p>
<p>Her face was red now, but she was getting enough oxygen. Her body had gone from fighting to nearly melting in his telekinetic grip.</p>
<p>"Oh demonnns," she gasped, her voice breathless and full of desire. "Yes~! More~! Choke me harder~! Please don’t stop, masked man~!"</p>
<p>The other maids were staring now, and their faces showed a range of emotions, from shock to understanding to barely hidden amusement.</p>
<p>"Of course Nyx would like this," one of them said under their breath.</p>
<p>"She’s been looking for someone who could really control her for years."</p>
