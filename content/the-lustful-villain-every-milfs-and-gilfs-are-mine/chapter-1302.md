<p>A bolt of lightning that did not come from the sky erupted in the center of the garden. It wasn’t weather; it was a raw, electrical scream of pure mana that tore through the oppressive atmosphere.</p>

<p>The invisible fist of Rex’s gravity shattered like glass under a sledgehammer, the magical hold on the ruined hall bursting apart in a cascade of violet sparks and concussive shockwaves.</p>

<p>Leonidas had gambled everything. He had taken every ounce of residual strength, every scrap of mana left in his marrow, and detonated it in a single, suicidal burst of liberation.</p>

<p>FWOOSH...!</p>

<p>He vanished from his position. In that same microsecond, a streak of silver steel flashed through the air like a comet.</p>

<p>The longsword didn’t just strike; it roared. It intercepted Rex’s descending fist with a bone-jarring CLANG that shook the very foundations of the garden.</p>

<p>The impact sent a visible ring of dust and debris rolling across the lawn like a tidal wave, the sound echoing off the stone walls like a hammer hitting an anvil.</p>

<p>Leonidas materialized between the monster and his wife. He stood there in a deep, strained guard, his blade vibrating from the force of the parry.</p>

<p>With his free arm, he had hooked beneath Helena’s limp shoulders, hauling her up to shield her body with his own. His chest heaved in ragged, desperate gasps; his hair was plastered to his forehead with sweat and blood; his skin burned a raw crimson from the magical backlash.</p>

<p>But his eyes burned into Rex’s with a hatred so focused, so crystallized, that it transcended rage. It was the calm, cold hatred of a man who had already accepted his death and chosen to spend it killing his enemy.</p>

<p>"Enough," Leonidas rasped.</p>

<p>His voice was low, unsteady, vibrating with the seismic force of the emotions he was barely containing. "You have touched my family... you have broken my home... you have taken my children’s peace."</p>

<p>His grip on the sword tightened until his knuckles cracked audibly. "You have touched them for the last time."</p>

<p>Rex didn’t flinch. He didn’t recoil from the near miss that could have severed his arm.</p>

<p>Instead, he slowly lowered his hand, the violet glow fading from his palm like dying embers.</p>

<p>There was no anger in his expression. No wounded pride.</p>

<p>Instead, a slow, spreading smile—something hungry and ancient—stretched across his face. The violet aura around his body gave off a single, deep pulse of dark energy, a heartbeat of pure anticipation.</p>

<p>He took one deliberate step back, then another, testing the space, before lifting two fingers in a lazy, mocking invitation.</p>

<p>"At last," Rex whispered, his voice carrying across the silent garden with terrifying clarity. "The legendary Leonidas."</p>

<p>"The second strongest mage in Aethelgard... I thought you might never wake up." His smile widened, revealing teeth that seemed too sharp in the twilight. "I’ve been waiting for you."</p>

<p>The entire garden froze. The weeping stopped.</p>

<p>The wind itself seemed to die. Even the psychic echoes of Lily’s screams seemed to muffle as the world held its breath, suspended on the razor’s edge between two collapsing stars.</p>

<p>Leonidas didn’t answer. He couldn’t.</p>

<p>His entire being was channeled into the sword and the man in front of him. With movements that were agonizingly careful, as if the slightest tremor might shatter the woman he held, he lowered Helena to the grass.</p>

<p>He laid her down on the soft earth with the tenderness of a priest handling a relic, ensuring her head was supported, before turning fully to face the abyss.</p>

<p>It was a small, quiet action, performed with a reverence that seemed to defy the carnage surrounding them. Without a sound, Leonidas knelt beside Helena one last time.</p>

<p>He slid his arm from beneath her shoulders with excruciating gentleness, lowering her head onto the turf as if the grass were spun from fragile glass.</p>

<p>With one unsteady hand, he unfastened his traveling cloak, shook the dust from its heavy fabric, and spread it over her broken form. He tucked the edge carefully under her chin, creating a cocoon of wool against the encroaching cold.</p>

<p>For one suspended heartbeat, his palm rested against her cheek, his thumb brushing away a smear of drying blood from her temple. It was a silent vow, a promise that he would return or die trying.</p>

<p>Then, he stood.</p>

<p>The tenderness of those few seconds served only to sharpen the terror of what came next. When Leonidas turned to face Rex, the transformation was complete.</p>

<p>The father, the husband, and the protector they had all retreated into a singular, terrifying purpose. A hush fell across the garden so absolute that even the wind seemed to recoil, leaning away from the coming storm.</p>

<p>Leonidas had been the Shield of Aethelgard for more than three decades. He was not merely the second-ranked mage in the city; he was its living history.</p>

<p>His combat style was a rare, deadly synthesis: the ancient Starlight sword forms precise, economical sweeps designed to end lives before they could react, fused with a sorcery that did not manifest as bolts or flames.</p>

<p>Instead, his energy flowed like liquid mercury down the length of his blade, each stroke carrying the devastating, unseen weight of compressed magical force.</p>

<p>In the border wars, veterans spoke of his terrifying calm. They said Leonidas looked bored even as he cut through enemy ranks, his composure never breaking until the last throat was slit.</p>

<p>Nobody in Aethelgard had ever seen him lose his nerve. But behind the stone wall, Valentina gasped, her hand flying to her mouth.</p>

<p>This was not the calm of a strategist; this was the stillness of a collapsing star. His eyes were bloodshot, his skin pale beneath the grime, and the veins in his neck stood out like corded steel.</p>

<p>"You will not leave this garden whole," Leonidas said.</p>

<p>His voice was quiet, almost level, but it carried a vibration that made the pebbles on the ground tremble. "I do not care what god you claim to serve..."</p>

<p>"I do not care how many monsters kneel at your feet, or how many faces you have worn to deceive us." His grip tightened on the hilt, and the air around the blade began to warp with shimmering heat. "I will carve the truth out of you."</p>

<p>Rex didn’t respond with words. He simply smiled that hungry, predatory grin and beckoned again with two fingers, a lazy gesture that insulted every drop of blood Leonidas had shed.</p>

<p>FSHHH...!</p>

<p>The sound of energy igniting filled the air as Leonidas moved.</p>

<p>He didn’t run; he exploded. The ground beneath his boots shattered as he launched himself forward, a streak of silver light cutting through the twilight.</p>

<p>His sword whistled through the air, each swing leaving behind a trail of starlight that hummed with lethal intent.</p>

<p>Rex raised a hand to meet him, but Leonidas was already inside his guard.</p>

<p>CLANG SHHHH RING!</p>

<p>The steel met gravity with a sound like a mountain splitting apart. Leonidas’s first strike was a horizontal slash aimed at Rex’s throat, the blade wreathed in a corona of white-hot magical energy.</p>

<p>Rex twisted his wrist, summoning a localized gravity well to deflect the blow, but Leonidas anticipated the resistance. He pivoted on his heel, using the momentum of the deflection to spin into a devastating uppercut.</p>

<p>The sword caught Rex under the chin.</p>

<p>CRACK.</p>

<p>The sound of cartilage tearing echoed across the lawn as the enchanted blade bit deep, sending a spray of crimson droplets across Rex’s metallic face.</p>

<p>Rex stumbled back, but his laughter echoed through the pain. "Yes! Show me!"</p>

<p>Leonidas didn’t give him an inch. He became a whirlwind of controlled violence.</p>

<p>Slash. Thrust. Parry.</p>

<p>His movements were a flawless choreography of death. He struck with the precision of a surgeon and the force of a siege engine.</p>

<p>Every time Rex attempted to crush him with a burst of gravitational pressure, Leonidas countered with a burst of Starlight mana, creating a temporary pocket of space where his own momentum remained untouched.</p>

<p>BOOM. CRUNCH. WHIP.</p>

<p>Blood sprayed across the grass as Leonidas’s blade found purchase. A shallow cut across Rex’s shoulder; a brutal thrust that grazed his ribs; a spinning kick that sent a shockwave through the air.</p>

<p>The two combatants became a blur of violet energy and silver steel, clashing in the center of the garden while the very earth beneath them groaned and buckled from the sheer magical output.</p>
