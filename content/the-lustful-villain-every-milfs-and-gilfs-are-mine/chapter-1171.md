<p>The silence that followed Rex’s revelation was not merely an absence of sound; it was a physical entity. It was a vacuum that seemed to suck the very oxygen out of the domain, leaving Apollo gasping in a space that had suddenly become impossibly small and infinitely hostile.</p>
<p>Apollo stared up at Rex. Whatever was happening on his face could no longer be described as grief.</p>
<p>Grief was human; grief was something that could be mourned. What was surfacing now was something older, darker, and far more dangerous.</p>
<p>The tears still tracked through the blood and grime on his cheeks, but underneath them, something ancient was rising through the wreckage of his psyche.</p>
<p>The trembling that had been born of heartbreak moments ago had transformed. It was no longer the shuddering of a broken man; it was the violent, tectonic vibration of something about to detonate.</p>
<p>"You," Apollo said.</p>
<p>His voice had dropped to a register so low it felt like a vibration in the bones rather than a sound. It was rough, serrated, and shaking with a lethal intensity.</p>
<p>"You... touched... my mother."</p>
<p>"I did considerably more than touch her, Apollo," Rex answered.</p>
<p>There was no regret, no hesitation, only the obscene enjoyment of watching the last vestiges of Apollo’s humanity burn away. "Touch is for strangers..."</p>
<p>"But for lovers... there is something much more... thorough."</p>
<p>"And I assure you, she was happier about it than any of the others."</p>
<p>"She found a comfort in me that your ’righteousness’ could never provide."</p>
<p>"Say it isn’t true," Apollo whispered. He wasn’t shouting.</p>
<p>That was somehow worse than the quiet desperation of a man clinging to a single thread of reality before it snapped. "Look at me... Look me in the eye and tell me you’re lying about her!"</p>
<p>"You’ve admitted to every atrocity, every betrayal, every murder today!"</p>
<p>"Lie about this one thing... Just this one thing, Rex...!"</p>
<p>"Lie to me, and I swear to whatever gods are left that I will believe you...!"</p>
<p>Rex didn’t blink. His violet eyes remained fixed on Apollo with predatory calm.</p>
<p>"She’s about four months along, Apollo..."</p>
<p>"She’s been keeping it quiet, playing the part of the grieving, exhausted High Priestess."</p>
<p>"The Temple thinks she’s been unwell, that the divine light is taxing her body... They have no idea they’re worshipping a womb for my legacy."</p>
<p>Something behind Apollo’s eyes finished dying. The last spark of the boy who believed in heroes, the last fragment of the man who believed in loyalty, vanished into a black abyss.</p>
<p>The golden light around Apollo didn’t just brighten; it fractured.</p>
<p>It was not the controlled, harmonious aura he had carried throughout their entire confrontation. This was raw, unmediated output.</p>
<p>It was energy screaming out of his body without direction, without shape, flaring in jagged, violent pulses that threw harsh, demonic shadows across a floor that had never known darkness before. The sound was a sickening, continuous, low-frequency hum that made the teeth ache and the ears bleed.</p>
<p>CRACK.</p>
<p>Fresh fissures split outward from beneath Apollo’s knees, spider webbing through the pulsing violet surface in a radial web three meters wide.</p>
<p>The domain itself gave a low, guttural groan of protest, a sound of structural agony as its young, unanchored reality registered a magical pressure it had never been designed to contain.</p>
<p>The air around Apollo began to distort, shimmering with heat haze as his power began to cook the very atmosphere.</p>
<p>"You took everyone I love," Apollo said.</p>
<p>His voice began to climb, gaining terrifying force with every syllable. Each word carried the weight of a collapsing star, echoing through the emptiness with a devastating power that made the domain tremble.</p>
<p>"You killed my friends like they were nothing more than insects..."</p>
<p>"You destroyed Theo’s entire life, breaking his mind until he had no choice but to throw himself off that cliff..."</p>
<p>The gold light intensified, turning from amber to a blinding, searing white that began to char the edges of the violet floor. Blood began to weep from Apollo’s nose and ears as his own power began to tear his body apart from the inside.</p>
<p>"You put your hands on my mother," he roared, the sound cracking like thunder. "And you did all of it while smiling at me..."</p>
<p>"While pretending to care... While letting me call you brother..."</p>
<p>Rex did not flinch. He did not retreat in fear. Instead, he stood up with a slow, deliberate grace and stepped back several paces, maintaining a measured distance.</p>
<p>He moved without any sense of urgency, his movements fluid and unhurried, as if he were merely adjusting his position to get a better view of a gallery painting.</p>
<p>His violet eyes remained fixed on Apollo, watching the transformation with the focused, clinical fascination of a scientist observing a volatile chemical reaction finally reaching its critical point.</p>
<p>This was the culmination. This was the entire purpose.</p>
<p>The grief had been delicious—a fine wine to savor—but grief was passive. Grief was the aftermath.</p>
<p>What Rex craved was this: the exact, exquisite moment where a good man’s morality burns away like dross in a furnace, leaving behind whatever primal, terrifying thing had been buried beneath the surface all along.</p>
<p>As Apollo’s power swelled, Rex felt something strange. The domain began to pulse with a foreign distress.</p>
<p>It wasn’t a conscious feeling, but a distant, systemic shudder that vibrated through his own magical essence. It was as if a limb he owned was suddenly experiencing phantom pain.</p>
<p>The system’s warnings began to flicker at the periphery of his consciousness, unbidden and insistent.</p>
<p>[Warning: Domain stability degrading.]</p>
<p>[Anchoring principle undefined.]</p>
<p>[Structural integrity at seventy-one percent and falling.]</p>
<p>Rex glanced at the notification and dismissed it with a flick of his mental attention. He felt no fear.</p>
<p>If the domain collapsed, it collapsed. They would simply be thrown back into the reality of the corridor, surrounded by ten thousand witnesses to their confrontation.</p>
<p>It would be inconvenient, yes, a messy complication in his perfect design, but survivable. He refused to interrupt this moment to go and repair his own architecture.</p>
<p>He would not break the spell now. Not for anything.</p>
<p>"I am... not going to forgive you," Apollo said. His voice was no longer a scream; it was something worse.</p>
<p>It was quiet, absolute, and carried the finality of a death sentence.</p>
<p>Apollo began to rise. The movement was agonizing to watch.</p>
<p>His body was a ruined vessel beneath the divine light, muscles torn, bones cracked from the sheer pressure of his own ascending power, and ribs grinding against one another.</p>
<p>But he forced himself up, each inch of movement a battle against gravity and pain. When he finally stood fully upright, the air around him seemed to solidify.</p>
<p>The golden light had stabilized, but it was no longer a soft glow. It had compressed into a hard, tight sheath of searing energy that burned millimeters from his skin, crackling with the sound of superheated metal.</p>
<p>"Not now... Not later... Not ever," Apollo continued, his eyes locked onto Rex’s with a hatred so pure it felt like it could melt stone. "You can kill me here, in this void, where no one will ever hear my bones break..."</p>
<p>"You can erase me from existence, and you might succeed..."</p>
<p>"But I refuse to die quietly knowing that something like you is still walking around in a world I was sworn to protect."</p>
<p>Then, the energy began to corrupt.</p>
<p>It started as a tremor in the light. The pure, sanctified gold began to bleed.</p>
<p>Something dark, viscous, and arterial red started to thread through the golden veins. It moved like living blood through arteries, pulsing in a slow, sickly rhythm that was completely out of sync with the divine cadence of his power.</p>
<p>Where the gold touched the floor, the cracks attempted to seal, the instinct of life affinity seeking to repair the damage. But where the red touched the floor, the domain screamed.</p>
<p>CRUNCH.</p>
<p>The red energy didn’t heal the fractures; it fed them. The cracks spiderwebbed outward with violent force, widening from three meters to six, then ten, tearing through the violet consecrated surface like teeth through flesh.</p>
<p>The pattern was no longer random damage. The fractures began to arrange themselves into something deliberate, something geometric and grotesque, spreading outward from Apollo’s feet like something ancient and hungry was growing out of the very foundation of reality.</p>
