<p>Rex didn’t just activate his powers; he detonated them.</p>

<p>[WRATH: OVERDRIVE]</p>

<p>The crimson mark above his heart erupted. It wasn’t just light; it was a hemorrhage of pure, unadulterated rage that threw jagged shadows across the ruined plaza.</p>

<p>The veins in his neck bulged like writhing serpents, his skin stretching to the breaking point as his blood pressure spiked to impossible levels.</p>

<p>"I WILL NOT BE JUDGED!" he roared, though the sound was half strangled by the blood pooling in his lungs. "I AM THE ONE WHO DECIDES!"</p>

<p>Using [Earthen Authority], he tore the island’s very bones apart. Massive slabs of bedrock screamed as they were wrenched upward, forming concentric circles around him.</p>

<p>Seven layers of stone walls, each as thick as a city’s fortified gate, rose in a spiraling fortress. They were stacked one inside the other, a geological cocoon designed to withstand a god’s hammer.</p>

<p>Then, he turned his gaze upward.</p>

<p>"Gravity Manipulation: Absolute Singularity!"</p>

<p>He reached toward the descending golden scale with invisible hands. He grabbed the very concept of its descent and fought back with everything he had.</p>

<p>He applied pressure with a force equivalent to a hundred times the world’s gravity, trying to crush the light before it could crush him.</p>

<p>Simultaneously, he activated his most stable creation: the deep, ringing violet frequency. The sound was a low, bone-deep hum, VUUUUUUUMMM,VUUUUUUUMMM that made the teeth of everyone in Aethelgard ache.</p>

<p>He wrapped that vibration around his body like a second skin, a harmonic armor designed to phase his atoms away from any incoming strike.</p>

<p>For three heartbeats, he held. The air screamed.</p>

<p>The stone walls groaned under the impossible tension.</p>

<p>Then, the darker pan of the scale tipped.</p>

<p>The verdict had been delivered.</p>

<p>A spear of white gold lightning, a hundred meters long and impossibly slender, tore free from the celestial scale.</p>

<p>It didn’t fall like a projectile; it descended like a fundamental law being enforced. It didn’t fight Rex’s defenses; it ignored them.</p>

<p>CRACK BOOOOOOOOM!</p>

<p>The first wall of stone didn’t just break; it shattered into fine powder as the lightning struck, the sound echoing like a mountain splitting in half.</p>

<p>The second wall dissolved, not from impact, but from sheer energetic overload. Rex’s gravity field, designed to hold the world, buckled.</p>

<p>The spear carried a metaphysical weight; it weighed exactly the sum of every atrocity Rex had ever committed. No amount of physical strength could lift the weight of a thousand sins.</p>

<p>"NO—!" Rex’s inner voice screamed as his violet frequency armor burned away to nothing in a microsecond.</p>

<p>The light didn’t care about his harmonics; it consumed them as if they were mere shadows.</p>

<p>The spear passed through his final layers of stone like a needle through water. There was no resistance.</p>

<p>There was only the wet, horrific sound of divine energy meeting mortal flesh.</p>

<p>SQUELCH. SHHHH THWACK.</p>

<p>The lightning spear punched through his sternum, tore through his heart with the wet crunch of breaking bone, and erupted out of his spine in a fountain of superheated blood and sparks.</p>

<p>Rex’s body jerked violently. His head snapped back, his spine arching in a grotesque parody of ecstasy.</p>

<p>For three seconds, he remained standing. The spear stayed lodged in him, glowing white hot through his torso.</p>

<p>His eyes were wide, pupils blown to pinpricks, his mouth slightly parted.</p>

<p>A final, unspoken thought flickered behind those eyes: a realization of the terrible irony that he had spent his life trying to become a god, only to be executed by one.</p>

<p>The two marks that defined his power, the violet at his wrist and the crimson above his heart, flickered weakly. They wavered like dying embers in a storm, then went black.</p>

<p>[SYSTEM ERROR: CORE INTEGRITY ZERO]</p>

<p>[ADAPTATION FAILED]</p>

<p>His knees folded. The defiance left his body as suddenly as it had arrived. He collapsed onto the stone with a wet thud, then rolled onto his side.</p>

<p>The sound of his broken, spent body hitting the ground carried across the plaza with a hollow, devastating finality, like a sack of meat dropped from a great height.</p>

<p>The lightning faded. The golden scale retreated into the heavens.</p>

<p>The city went silent.</p>

<p>It was a silence more absolute than anything the world had ever known. It wasn’t merely the absence of noise; it was a physical presence, a vacuum that swallowed the wind, the distant sounds of the city, and the very air itself.</p>

<p>The world seemed to have been hollowed out, leaving only the wreckage of the plaza and the corpses of two gods.</p>

<p>Then, the silence broke.</p>

<p>Lily screamed.</p>

<p>The sound didn’t travel through the air; it tore through the telepathic link, a raw, psychic laceration that Rex felt screaming inside his own skull in his final moments of consciousness.</p>

<p>It was a sound of pure, unadulterated agony—the sound of a connection being ripped apart.</p>

<p>SNAP.</p>

<p>The link severed like a taut rope parting under impossible tension, leaving a psychic wound that bled static.</p>

<p>Cassandra’s sword slipped from her nerveless fingers, clattering against the broken stones with a lonely, ringing CLANG that seemed to echo forever.</p>

<p>She didn’t move. And she didn’t blink.</p>

<p>She simply stared at the blood-soaked crater where a man had just been executed.</p>

<p>Lilith collapsed to her knees, both hands pressed hard against her mouth as if she could physically hold back the horror spilling out of her.</p>

<p>Valentina closed her eyes tight, tears streaming down her face in steady rivulets; she couldn’t have told you if those tears were for the boy who died or for the monster who had been unleashed, or perhaps a twisted cocktail of grief and relief.</p>

<p>Diana turned her back to the carnage, pressing her forehead against the cold, unfeeling stone of the protective ring, seeking any anchor to reality.</p>

<p>And Elizabeth... Elizabeth stood as still as a statue carved from ice, her gaze fixed on the spot where Rex lay, her expression unreadable even in the face of annihilation.</p>

<p>Evelyn remained standing in the center of the wasteland, her hand still raised toward the heavens.</p>

<p>Above her, the great golden scale began to dissolve. It didn’t vanish instantly; it faded slowly, the way the resonant hum of a great bell fades into nothingness after it has been struck.</p>

<p>When the last golden particle finally dissolved into the atmosphere, she lowered her arm with a slow, shuddering exhale, the movement of someone who had been carrying the weight of the entire world on their shoulders for an eternity and had only now been permitted to set it down.</p>

<p>For three seconds, there was nothing.</p>

<p>No breathing.</p>

<p>No heartbeat.</p>

<p>No wind.</p>

<p>It was not like falling into sleep, nor was it like descending into darkness. It was a void without floor, without ceiling, without edges.</p>

<p>It was a sensory deprivation so absolute it felt like nonexistence.</p>

<p>[Divine Perception: OFFLINE]</p>

<p>[Foresight: OFFLINE]</p>

<p>[Full Potential Adaptation: OFFLINE]</p>

<p>The three pillars of Rex’s consciousness, the abilities that had been his constant companions since the moment he woke in this alien world, went out simultaneously.</p>

<p>It was like three candles being snuffed by the same breath.</p>

<p>No light. No sound. No sensation of flesh, no pain, no blood, no gravity.</p>

<p>Just the endless, terrifying black.</p>

<p>In that infinite stillness, a single thought flickered. It didn’t come from a mind; it came from somewhere deeper, something ancient and detached.</p>

<p>It was clear, calm, and carried a faint, almost cruel amusement.</p>

<p>’Interesting.’</p>

<p>Then, piercing through the absolute dark like a blade of pure data, a line of text ignited in the center of his vision.</p>

<p>[Bond Anchor: 20/20 active. All anchorholders are alive.]</p>

<p>[Death condition met: Subject Rex has ceased vital functions.]</p>
