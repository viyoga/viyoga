<p>"The area expression," Rex murmured.</p>
<p>His voice didn’t carry to Theo; it was a private cataloguing, a cold, clinical assessment delivered in the flat register of a predator analyzing the mechanics of a trap. "Broader range."</p>
<p>"Drastically reduced precision."</p>
<p>"The tradeoff is precisely what you’d expect from a power source operating at overload."</p>
<p>Rex didn’t hesitate. He didn’t seek cover.</p>
<p>He began to walk not away from the chaos, but directly into the eye of the storm. He marched toward the epicenter where the temporal distortion was most violent, where reality was being shredded into a thousand screaming fragments of past and future.</p>
<p>Inside his mind, the machinery of the Full Potential Adaptation roared to life. The first contact analysis surged through his consciousness like liquid mercury, scanning the mechanism of the distortion.</p>
<p>It registered the anomaly: this wasn’t the surgical precision of Theo’s previous temporal stops or the gradual drag of his temporal slows. This was a new category, an uncontrolled, omnidirectional area expression.</p>
<p>Because it was new, the adaptation’s protective coverage had not yet extended to it.</p>
<p>’First contact will deliver full effect,’ the system warned.</p>
<p>Rex felt it immediately. The distortion slammed into his biological system like a physical assault.</p>
<p>It wasn’t just pain; it was the nauseating horror of existential uncertainty. His timeline began to flicker.</p>
<p>For terrifying micro-moments, his bones felt like they were both ancient dust and unborn marrow. His blood felt as though it were flowing backward through his veins before surging forward again.</p>
<p>His consciousness stuttered, his mind grasping at a present moment that refused to stay solid. His flesh screamed as cells attempted to exist across multiple temporal states simultaneously, the sheer friction of time tearing at his molecular continuity.</p>
<p>The adaptation ran its analysis through the agony.</p>
<p>’Three point four seconds.’</p>
<p>For three and a half seconds, Rex existed in a state of quantum torment, his body pushed to the absolute brink of dissolution.</p>
<p>Then, with the silent click of a completed equation, the adaptation’s coverage snapped into place. The temporal distortions continued to howl around him, and the buildings continued to flicker and decay in the background, but the effect on Rex simply... stopped.</p>
<p>The distortion was still running at full power, but it was no longer touching him. He had become a foreign object in the timeline that the storm could not grasp.</p>
<p>Rex steadied his breathing, his eyes locking onto the broken figure above.</p>
<p>"That one took longer," Rex said, his voice cutting through the temporal static with terrifying calm. "New category."</p>
<p>Theo stared down at him, his face a mask of pure, unadulterated horror. His chest heaved, blood frothing at his lips, his eyes wide with the shock of a god who had just discovered he was mortal.</p>
<p>He had poured everything he had, every ounce of his rage, every fragment of his power, into that blast, and he was watching it fail to do anything more than annoy the man below.</p>
<p>"How..." Theo gasped, the word breaking into a wet cough. "How the fuck... how are you still standing?"</p>
<p>"Adaptation," Rex said.</p>
<p>The word was simple. Final.</p>
<p>"Everything you have is going to work exactly once, Theo."</p>
<p>"The first time, I learn. The second time? The second time belongs to me."</p>
<p>Rex didn’t wait for a reply. He didn’t give Theo the chance to regroup or scream again.</p>
<p>He rose.</p>
<p>There was no dramatic flourish of wings, no shimmering display of flight magic. Instead, Rex activated the Gravity Manipulation’s personal gravity reduction.</p>
<p>The world seemed to drop away beneath him as he inverted his own relationship with mass. In less than two seconds, he shot upward from the street level, a human bullet streaking through the distorted air.</p>
<p>He covered forty-five meters with the flat, terrifying efficiency of a predator who had already calculated the distance and was simply moving to close the gap.</p>
<p>Theo didn’t wait for Rex to reach him. Panicked, his instincts screaming that his godhood was being stripped away, he lashed out.</p>
<p>He threw a temporal acceleration working at point-blank range, a desperate, concentrated burst of warped time designed to force Rex’s body to move faster than its own nervous system could handle, tearing muscle from bone through sheer velocity.</p>
<p>Rex took the full, brutal first contact effect.</p>
<p>For a moment, his body became a prisoner of its own speed. His heart hammered against his ribs like a trapped bird, each beat occurring at a frequency that threatened to shatter his sternum.</p>
<p>His synapses fired with such violent intensity that his vision blurred into a streak of meaningless colors. It was a jarring, sickening dissonance, the feeling of a machine being forced to run at ten times its rated capacity while the environment around it remained stagnant.</p>
<p>His blood felt like molten lead racing through his veins.</p>
<p>’New subexpression detected: Temporal Acceleration,’ the Adaptation hummed in his mind. ’Analyzing parameters...’</p>
<p>Four point one seconds.</p>
<p>Rex’s internal clock snapped back to standard. The acceleration was still howling around him, but it no longer had purchase on his biology.</p>
<p>He was a still point in a rushing river.</p>
<p>Rex closed the distance in a blur of motion.</p>
<p>He hit Theo.</p>
<p>This wasn’t a tactical strike. This wasn’t a calculated probe.</p>
<p>This was a fucking execution. Rex channeled the full, devastating output of his martial arts mastery, amplified by the forty-five meters of momentum he had built during his ascent.</p>
<p>He used telekinesis as the delivery mechanism, a focused lance of invisible force, and his own enhanced physical strength as the payload.</p>
<p>The impact sounded like two freight trains colliding. A wet, crunching sound reverberated through the air as Theo’s ribs shattered under the concentrated pressure.</p>
<p>Theo was launched backward through the sky like a broken doll, a trail of crimson blood spraying from his mouth as he hurtled away from the Nightwing household.</p>
<p>He sailed through the air for sixty meters before the temporal designation’s physical enhancement kicked in, forcing his body to absorb the kinetic energy in a managed force collapse.</p>
<p>He didn’t crash; he simply stopped, suspended in midair by his own power.</p>
<p>Then, Theo screamed something that wasn’t a word; it was a command to reality itself.</p>
<p>’Temporal Rewind.’</p>
<p>This was the crown jewel of Theo’s power. Not the crude destruction of the area distortion, not the simple stagnation of the stop, but the sophisticated, terrifying ability to force a targeted system to reverse its own history.</p>
<p>He aimed it directly at Rex, attempting to unravel the last few seconds of Rex’s existence, to revert his body to a state before the damage, before the movement, and before the confrontation.</p>
<p>Against most, it was a death sentence.</p>
<p>Against Rex, it was a paradox.</p>
<p>The Rewind tore into Rex’s timeline, seeking purchase to reverse his state. But Rex’s Infinite Regeneration was already running at maximum output, knitting flesh together faster than time could unmake it.</p>
<p>The Rewind located a target that was already constantly rebuilding itself. It was trying to undo something that refused to stay undone.</p>
<p>The two powers grinded against each other in a silent, metaphysical war, producing nothing but a null result, an attempt to erase a shadow that refused to disappear.</p>
<p>Inside Rex’s mind, the adaptation screamed warnings. This was the most technically sophisticated expression of the temporal class it had ever encountered.</p>
<p>The analysis was painstakingly slow, incredibly complex, and utterly deadly.</p>
<p>’Analysis time: Six point two seconds.’</p>
<p>Six point two seconds of vulnerability.</p>
<p>Theo, sensing the window, didn’t waste a heartbeat. He knew the Rewind was failing to erase Rex, so he used the application time to launch a physical assault.</p>
<p>Emboldened by his divine designation, Theo tore through the air, closing the gap with inhuman speed.</p>
<p>His fist connected with Rex’s jaw with the force of a sledgehammer. The sound of bone splintering was audible even over the temporal howling.</p>
<p>Rex’s head snapped sideways, blood erupting from his split lip and torn cheek. A moment later, another strike, a kick to the ribs, sent a fresh spray of crimson across the hovering debris.</p>
<p>The damage was undeniable. It was raw and brutal.</p>
<p>But as fast as the blood flowed, it stopped. As fast as the bones cracked, they fused.</p>
<p>The Infinite Regeneration consumed the damage as quickly as Theo could inflict it, the flesh knitting shut in real time beneath the bruises.</p>
<p>Rex emerged from the six-second window with his jaw set and his eyes colder than the void. The adaptation was complete.</p>
<p>His coverage now included the ability to express temporal rewinds.</p>
<p>Rex rose in the air, stabilizing himself thirty meters away from the panting, blood-soaked Theo.</p>
<p>"Last category," Rex said. His voice was flat, devoid of anger, carrying only the terrifying certainty of an inevitable conclusion.</p>
<p>"How many times do I have to fucking say it?" Theo shrieked.</p>
<p>The controlled, measured delivery of his earlier grievances was gone. This was the raw, unhinged voice of a man who had been stripped of every defense, standing naked before a truth he couldn’t comprehend.</p>
<p>"He is not what you think he is! He is not what any of you fucking think he is!"</p>
<p>Theo’s eyes were wild, pleading, and murderous all at once.</p>
<p>Rex looked at him, his gaze unblinking.</p>
<p>"I know what he is," Rex said.</p>
