<p>His foresight grew longer, and it became more focused as it sifted through different options that he could use for this situation.</p>
<p>He was looking for a certain kind of demon that would help him reach his real goal towards the core point. He didn’t look for a mindless grunt or a combat expert, but someone who is smart, has connections, and is useful to reach Mordecai’s group.</p>
<p>The fight went on for hours. The sky went from full night to the first light of dawn, but the demons kept coming.</p>
<p>However, the situation began to shift. The defenders were holding their ground, and even more importantly, they were starting to push back.</p>
<p>The walls stayed the same, and there were surprisingly few casualties given how big the attack was. And the demon armies were clearly getting smaller as more and more of them were killed.</p>
<p>Finally, Rex’s foresight showed him what he had been looking for.</p>
<p>"There it is... something that I can use for this perfect plan."</p>
<p>He saw a vision very clearly in his mind. A female demon wore what looked like a maid’s uniform.</p>
<p>The elegant attire was a striking contrast to the harshness of the battlefield. She was running away from the main fight, moving through the shadows by the eastern wall.</p>
<p>And following her was a reincarnator, someone Rex’s system recognized as a low-level apostle of justice, one of the minor god’s champions who had come for the gathering.</p>
<p>In the vision, the reincarnator caught the maid demon and killed her by stabbing her in the back while shouting, ’In the name of justice.’</p>
<p>However, that vision was not final, allowing Rex to completely alter it.</p>
<p>He smiled in the dark and then clenched the fist that has the mark of lust until it responded to his desire.</p>
<p>"It’s time to put this baby on good work."</p>
<p>The transformation of the Lustful Villain proceeded smoothly, much like breathing. The mask appeared over his face, beautiful and scary, covering all of his features except for his eyes, which were visible through glowing red slits.</p>
<p>His clothes changed, becoming something darker and more flowing, like a living shadow taking shape. And most importantly, he changed in every way.</p>
<p>The aura of power that had been carefully hidden in his Rex Rexilion identity now shone brightly, making him seem dangerous and different.</p>
<p>His traits got better right away, thanks to the desire that had built up from his relationships with Lily and Mara and the partial connections he had made with Helena, Diana, Evelyn, Iris, and Elaris.</p>
<p>He could tell that his strength, speed, and durability were all better than they usually were.</p>
<p>"Holy shit... it far exceeds my expectations." Rex looked at his arms and legs. "I’m going to enjoy every bit of this..."</p>
<p>"And if this double life is addicting, then I’ll just have to stick with the Lustful Villain until I’m satisfied." Rex closed both his eyes to focus on his teleportation.</p>
<p>Rex used his new teleportation power to focus on the place that his foresight had shown him. He focused on the area next to the eastern wall, where the maid demon was likely hiding in the shadows.</p>
<p>He could see the spell structure taking shape in his mind, simple and beautiful, and then he stepped.</p>
<p>Reality bent around him when the teleportation activated.</p>
<p>He was on a roof near the Silver Rest one minute. The next thing he knew, he was standing in a small space between two buildings near the eastern wall, with shadows pooling around him like they were alive.</p>
<p>And there was the demon maid, just like his foresight had shown him.</p>
<p>She was beautiful in the way that many demons are, with features that were almost human but just a little bit off in ways that made people feel uneasy.</p>
<p>Her hair was silver-white and styled in a complicated way that somehow stayed perfect even though everything was going wrong.</p>
<p>Her skin was pale with a hint of blue underneath. Her eyes were bright red and wide open with fear right now.</p>
<p>And her outfit was a real maid’s uniform, black and white with many frills, and it looked great even though she was running away from a battle.</p>

<p>[CLASSIFICATION: SUCCUBUS VARIANT - MAID CLASS]</p>

<p>"Succubus...?!" Rex was shocked and also interested right away. "Fucking great..."</p>
<p>Just what he had been looking for. Someone who could get to Mordecai directly and act as his way into the demon lord’s group and, of course... for other things that led to more of his energy regeneration.</p>
<p>And right behind her, exactly on schedule, was the reincarnator who’d been hunting her.</p>
<p>The man was young, maybe twenty-five, and had a serious face that made it seem like he really believed in what was right and wrong. He wore light armor with symbols of his god on it, and he held a short sword that glowed with holy energy.</p>
<p>"Stop running, you cowardly demon!" he yelled, raising his sword. "Even if you are a woman! Your corruption stops here!"</p>
<p>"I serve justice for its purpose and uphold gender equality at the same time..." The reincarnator said that and then laughed. "Hahaha~! Damn, I sounded so cool and sigma!"</p>
<p>Lilith fell, and her back hit the wall of the building behind her. It was intriguing that her fear was solely due to the imminent threat of a human’s death.</p>
<p>Most of the demons Rex had met so far had been either aggressive or fearless. It was clear that this one was made for sneaking around, not fighting.</p>
<p>The reincarnator lunged, his sword aimed for Lilith’s heart.</p>
<p>"This is the end...!"</p>
<p>But then Rex intervenes.</p>
<p>His telekinesis reached out and caught the blade in the middle of a strike, stopping it in mid-air just inches from Lilith’s chest. The sudden resistance made the reincarnator trip, and he looked confused as he tried to figure out why his attack had failed.</p>
<p>"W-What the...!? H-How!? This sword is a gift from the god of justice itself!" He said with a panicked look.</p>
<p>Then he looked up and saw Rex standing there in his Lustful Villain form. The confusion turned into pure fear right away.</p>
<p>"W-what?" the person who had been reincarnated said. "Who... who the FUCK are you?!"</p>
<p>"Why are you protecting a demon? Don’t tell me you got seduced by the succubus’ powers!" He gritted his teeth. "You fucking weak! Why did you give in to your desire like that!?"</p>
<p>Rex didn’t bother to respond, just because talking to someone who was about to die was pointless.</p>
<p>His telekinesis flexed, and an unseen force wrapped around the reincarnator’s body from all sides at once. "W-What the...!? S-Stop this instant...!"</p>
<p>The man opened his mouth to scream, but Rex tightened his grip, stifling any sound before it could escape.</p>
<p>The result was brutal and complete. Bones shattered, organs ruptured, and blood vessels burst.</p>
<p>In just three seconds, the reincarnator’s body was crushed from the inside out. When Rex released his hold, what fell to the ground was not a corpse but a puddle of blood and liquefied tissue, barely contained by torn clothing.</p>
<p>"Apostle of Justice..." Rex landed in the puddle of blood. "What a fucking joke."</p>
<p>There was nothing left that could help find him. There was no face, no recognizable features, just a jumbled mess that could have come from any number of people who died in battle.</p>
<p>Rex saw Lilith, who was looking at him with a changed expression. Her red eyes were wide open, but not because she was scared, but because she was fascinated.</p>
<p>In admiration. There was a sense of reverence in the atmosphere.</p>

<p>"From nothing to seventy in a single move... that was astonishing, even considering what I had previously accomplished with Marcus through the psychological dominance bonus."</p>
<p>Then he thought about what Lustia had said. Demons worked on rules that were different from those of people.</p>
<p>They weren’t limited by the same moral codes or emotional issues. Demons responded right away to power and dominance because that was the language their whole culture was based on.</p>
<p>He had just demonstrated complete control over life and death, the ability to protect someone weaker, and such immense power that he turned a divine champion into liquid meat without even trying.</p>

<p>If there were any doubts about his abilities, her desire level would likely be higher.</p>
