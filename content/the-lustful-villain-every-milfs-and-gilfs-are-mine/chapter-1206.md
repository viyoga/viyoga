<p>The violence escalated from a clash of steel and muscle to something far more fundamental: a war of reality itself.</p>
<p>Rex Rexilion’s next move was a cataclysm. His hands blurred as he wove together two of the most devastating disciplines in existence.</p>
<p>VROOOOOOM CRACKLE!</p>
<p>With his left hand, he summoned the fury of Elemental Mastery, conjuring a torrent of superheated plasma that screamed through the air like a dragon’s breath.</p>
<p>Simultaneously, his right hand twisted in a complex gesture of Gravity Manipulationgravity manipulation.</p>
<p>The air around the plasma stream didn’t just burn; it collapsed inward, creating a localized gravitational well that dragged the flames into a spinning, hyperdense vortex.</p>
<p>WHOOOOOOOM!</p>
<p>The attack roared toward Xerollion, a spiraling lance of elemental destruction and crushing force. But Xerollion did not flinch.</p>
<p>He didn’t even adopt a defensive stance. Instead, he met the vortex head-on with the terrifying nonchalance of a god meeting a child’s tantrum.</p>
<p>Because he was not merely fighting an opponent; he was fighting a mirror image of his own genius. Every technique Rex unleashed was a technique Xerollion had perfected, refined, and understood down to its atomic structure.</p>
<p>He knew the precise harmonic frequency at which the plasma would destabilize. He knew exactly how much gravitational pressure would cause the construct’s telekinetic shield to buckle.</p>
<p>SHHHHH BOOM!</p>
<p>Xerollion raised a single finger. A microburst of countergravitational energy met the vortex, canceling out the momentum in a spectacular explosion of displaced air and sparks.</p>
<p>KRA KOOOOM!</p>
<p>The shockwave sent dust and blood-stained debris cascading across the corridor floor like shrapnel.</p>
<p>For several minutes, the corridor became a kaleidoscope of impossible phenomena: elemental bursts of crimson and azure collided in midair, canceling each other out in blinding flashes of white light, while overlapping gravitational fields twisted the very light around them, making the two fighters appear to warp and stretch like phantoms.</p>
<p>To the soldiers watching, paralyzed by awe, it looked like an impossible stalemate between two gods locked in an eternal dance of destruction.</p>
<p>But the illusion of balance was rotting from within.</p>
<p>WHACK. CRUNCH. THUD.</p>
<p>Xerollion began to close the distance. His movements became sharper and more economical, each parry more devastating than the last.</p>
<p>He wasn’t just deflecting Rex’s attacks anymore; he was redirecting them back at the construct with lethal intent.</p>
<p>"You know the biggest problem with fighting your own reflection?" Xerollion’s voice rang out through the thunder of clashing magic, cold and utterly amused.</p>
<p>He caught Rex’s wrist mid-swing GRIP and twisted, sending a jolt of pain through the construct’s simulated nervous system. "I already know exactly what you’re going to do next."</p>
<p>He stepped inside Rex’s guard, his elbow connecting with the construct’s jaw with a wet, bone-deep CRACK.</p>
<p>"Because I thought of it," Xerollion whispered, his eyes burning behind the mask, "before you were ever created."</p>
<p>The tide turned with brutal efficiency. Xerollion stopped playing.</p>
<p>The curiosity faded, replaced by the cold satisfaction of a predator dismantling a trap. Every attack Rex unleashed was met with a counter that exploited a microscopic flaw in the construct’s programming.</p>
<p>When Rex attempted a telekinetic shield, Xerollion struck the exact pressure point where the energy was most vulnerable, shattering it like glass.</p>
<p>SHATTER CRACK!</p>
<p>THUD!</p>
<p>A devastating roundhouse kick caught Rex square in the ribs.</p>
<p>Even though Rex was an energy-based construct, the magical feedback was so precise that his form began to manifest physical damage; the simulated bones splintered, and a spray of synthetic, blood-red energy spattered across the stone floor.</p>
<p>SPLAT.</p>
<p>CRUNCH.</p>
<p>Xerollion followed up with a brutal uppercut that lifted the ’hero’ off his feet, sending him staggering backward. Rex’s breathing, the simulated, labored gasping of a human, became more ragged, more desperate.</p>
<p>Blood or whatever crimson essence the construct bled streaked across Rex’s face, painting the picture of a dying hero.</p>
<p>The soldiers’ hope began to waver as they saw their savior falter, bleeding and broken under the relentless onslaught of the monster who seemed to know his every heartbeat before he even felt it.</p>
<p>The violence took on a grotesque, uncanny quality. As Xerollion dismantled the construct, the damage appeared not as random trauma but as a deliberate anatomical dissection.</p>
<p>Bruises bloomed across Rex’s skin deep, violet contusions that shouldn’t have been possible on something made of light and data and blood welled from lacerations that followed the exact surgical lines of human vulnerability.</p>
<p>Every cut was a statement: ’I know where you are fragile.’</p>
<p>CRACK THUD.</p>
<p>Xerollion drove a fist into a specific point along Rex’s ribcage. It wasn’t a random strike; it was the exact nexus where the construct’s magical circulatory system maintained its equilibrium.</p>
<p>SPLAT.</p>
<p>A spray of crimson essence erupted from Rex’s mouth as his ribs simulated but terrifyingly convincing buckled inward with a wet, grinding sound.</p>
<p>Rex staggered backward, his knees buckling, his simulated breathing coming in ragged, wet gasps that made the horrified soldiers flinch.</p>
<p>Yet, the construct did not falter. It could not falter.</p>
<p>Rex Rexilion threw himself back into the fray with a relentless, mechanical fury. For several agonizing minutes, the fight descended into something almost hauntingly beautiful, a macabre ballet of perfect symmetry.</p>
<p>Two identical figures moved through techniques so intertwined that watching them felt like witnessing a man engaged in a schizophrenic argument with his own shadow.</p>
<p>WHOOSH SHINK!</p>
<p>A spinning crescent kick, a move Xerollion himself had once used to shatter Kaeleth’s fragments, tore through the air. Instantly, the construct mirrored it with an identical counter.</p>
<p>Both fighters adjusted mid-motion with the same instinctive correction, the same subtle shift of weight on the balls of their feet, and the same microscopic pause before committing to the strike. It was the ghost of Rex’s muscle memory manifesting in a puppet.</p>
<p>Xerollion caught a lightning-fast jab on his forearm.</p>
<p>THUD.</p>
<p>The shockwave traveled up his arm, vibrating through his bones. A thin smile touched the corners of his mouth beneath the mask.</p>
<p>"You fight well," Xerollion admitted, his voice carrying over the crackle of residual magic, "for something built from behavior data rather than lived experience."</p>
<p>His grip tightened on Rex’s wrist as they locked together, foreheads nearly touching. "But there’s a difference between knowing how to move and knowing why you move."</p>
<p>"And that difference... that’s where you die."</p>
<p>Inside the construct’s core, a silent, cold intelligence flickered a cascade of data processing at impossible speeds.</p>
<p>[SYSTEM ALERT: Energy signature analysis complete.]</p>
<p>[Construct confirmed.]</p>
<p>[Behavioral fidelity: 97.3%.]</p>
<p>[Combat dataset current as of last synchronization.]</p>
<p>[WARNING: Structural integrity compromised.]</p>
<p>[Deviation increasing under sustained load.]</p>
<p>[Neural magical pathways destabilizing.]</p>
<p>Rex Rexilion could not process fear, but his programming demanded escalation. He answered Xerollion’s taunt with the only language he possessed: violence.</p>
<p>VROOOOOM!</p>
<p>Rex lunged forward, driving a palm strike aimed directly at Xerollion’s sternum. The strike was wrapped in a razor-thin shell of condensed telekinetic force, vibrating at a frequency designed to shatter magical barriers.</p>
<p>It was the exact technique Rex had deployed weeks earlier against Kaeleth’s fragments—the same lethal precision, the same devastating follow-through.</p>
<p>Xerollion didn’t dodge. He met it.</p>
<p>BOOOOOOOOOM!!!</p>
<p>Xerollion met the palm with his own, palm to palm. The collision was catastrophic.</p>
<p>A perfect circle of compressed air exploded outward from the point of contact, forming a visible ring of pressure that tore through the remaining air in the corridor.</p>
<p>CRACKLE CRUNCH.</p>
<p>The floor beneath them couldn’t withstand the simultaneous release of opposing gravitational and telekinetic energies; the ancient stone shattered in a spreading web of fractures that spiderwebbed outward from their feet, sending chunks of masonry flying like shrapnel.</p>
<p>For a heartbeat, they remained locked in that impossible embrace, two mirrors facing each other, one cracking under the pressure of reality while the other remained unyielding as stone.</p>
