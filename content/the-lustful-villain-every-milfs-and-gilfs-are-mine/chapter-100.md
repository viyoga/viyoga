<p>Rei’s eyes narrowed, and she started to cast a spell that was more complicated. It took longer for this one to form because energy was building up at the tip of her staff in a swirling vortex of different elements.</p>
<p>"I really need to let it all out to beat all of you at once," Rei shouted. "I got nothing to lose starting from this point on...!!!"</p>
<p>All of the magic of fire, ice, lightning, and force is combined into one powerful attack, and Rex could see that it was a powerful merge of magic from her own energy manipulation. But still, it’s not enough to stop Rex’s powerful telekinesis.</p>
<p>"I will not move from this spot, so let it all out—"</p>
<p>"ELEMENTAL MERGEFUL FORCEEEE!!!" She let it go with a shout, and the spell shot forward like a tiny comet, leaving behind burned air and a trail of colorful energy.</p>
<p>Rex’s Foresight had shown him exactly how this would go down. He waited until the spell was about six feet away, then he used his energy manipulation to break it up.</p>
<p>The carefully woven structure of the spell disintegrated like a thread unraveling from a sweater, transforming the once devastating attack into harmless wisps of magic that gradually faded away.</p>
<p>"What a pathetic puny attack... is that all you got after saying you got nothing to lose?" Rex asked, "I thought an apostle of balance would do more."</p>
<p>Rex saw that the rest of her friends were targeting the demon maids, and he needed to help them build their trust even further. He wasn’t going to let Rei’s three friends move forward on the demon maids as ordered.</p>
<p>His telekinesis reached out in three different directions at once, wrapping around Takeda, Kagura, and Ayame and slamming them face-first into the ground with full force on their backs and the tops of their heads.</p>
<p>Then he pushed down harder and harder until he felt pressure. Not enough to kill them right away, but enough to stop them from moving.</p>
<p>"Urrggghhhh...! I can’t... move!" Kagura tried to resist.</p>
<p>"My body... it feels all tired and weak!" Takeda gritted his teeth.</p>
<p>Ayame said. "This is probably one of his powers...!"</p>
<p>They fought to resist the force from the telekinesis, their arms and legs flailing around uselessly, but it was like they were trying to lift a mountain off their backs.</p>
<p>"Your friends are busy there with the burden on their backs." Rex turned his attention back to Rei and said, "Now you have my full attention... and I hope you can make this more interesting."</p>
<p>Rei was losing her composure. He could tell she was thinking about retreating by the way her hands shook a little on her staff, the way her eyes widened, and the way her weight shifted slightly.</p>
<p>"Ohh...? "What’s this?" Rex noticed and then taunted him instead. "Have you lost all of your bravado from earlier?"</p>
<p>"Was it worth it to charge at someone that’s stronger than you, lowly scum?"</p>
<p>"There’s no way in hell I would run away like you cowards!" Rei starts to get frustrated hearing his taunt.</p>
<p>She didn’t run, which is a good thing. Instead, she planted her feet and started sending out power on a level that Rex had never seen before.</p>
<p>The air around her was full of energy, and the ground beneath her feet started to glow with complicated magical circles.</p>
<p>"BALANCE BREAKER!" she shouted, letting go of everything she had.</p>
<p>The spell was really cool, even for Rex. It used both offensive and defensive magic in a way that should have been deadly.</p>
<p>It made a wave of destructive force that tried to destroy everything in its path while also keeping the caster safe from attack.</p>
<p>Rex’s Foresight showed him 17 different ways to fight it. He picked the one that was the least polite.</p>
<p>He walked straight into the wave of energy, moving slowly and steadily. The spell struck him with the force of a tidal wave, engulfing him in a moment of intense, bright light.</p>
<p>"Masked man!" Lilith was shocked seeing Rex make that unpredictable move.</p>
<p>After that, the light went out, and Rex was still standing where he had been before, unharmed. And there’s not even a scratch on his mask, and that surprised everyone there.</p>
<p>Rei’s expression looked shocked and scared at the same time. "No way... that was my most... powerful move!"</p>
<p>"Powerful...? More like garbage," he said. "Now it’s my turn!"</p>
<p>His telekinesis reached out and grabbed Rei by the waist, lifting her off the ground. She tried to fight back, cast spells to protect herself, and do anything else she could think of to get free, but it was like trying to fight against gravity itself.</p>
<p>Rex started to have fun with her.</p>
<p>At first, he spun her slowly through the air, but then he sped up and up until she was a blur of motion. Then he suddenly stopped her, and the whiplash made her gasp.</p>
<p>He lifted her higher, then let her fall almost to the ground before catching her at the last second. He squeezed a little, not enough to break ribs but enough to make it hard to breathe.</p>
<p>His foresight showed him her growing fear, her realization that she was completely outmatched, and her dawning understanding that she was going to die here.</p>
<p>"Please," she finally managed to beg, "please stop it...!"</p>
<p>"I... I give up! I’ll do anything... just let me go!"</p>
<p>Rex thought about it for exactly one second. Then he looked over at Takeda, Kagura, and Ayame, who were still pinned to the ground and couldn’t move. T</p>
<p>Their faces were turned toward their leader, and they looked scared and hopeless.</p>
<p>Kagura said. "Please stop...! We gave up!"</p>
<p>"Gave up? Come on now, we just started!" Rex felt disappointed.</p>
<p>"Well, no one cares anyway."</p>
<p>"Pay close attention," Rex told them. "This is what happens to people who go against me."</p>
<p>Then he made a fist.</p>
<p>The telekinetic force around Rei pushed in from all sides at the same time. There was a sound like many things breaking at once, a wet crunching that echoed through the clearing, and Rei’s scream stopped suddenly.</p>
<p>CRACKKKKK!</p>
<p>When Rex opened his fist and let go of the telekinetic grip, what fell to the ground looked like it had been human at one point, but now it isn’t.</p>
<p>"There you go. Fast and easy, to the afterlife she goes to meet her god," Rex said while clapping.</p>
<p>Her body had been crushed so badly that her bones had turned to dust, her organs had burst and merged, and the only thing holding it all together was the remains of her armor and clothes.</p>
<p>Kagura was screaming. Takeda made sounds like he was trying not to throw up. Ayame had turned completely white and was silent, shock taking over all other reactions.</p>
<p>Rex looked at them, but his face was blank behind the mask. "You have two choices."</p>
<p>"Fight me and join her, or give up and live."</p>
<p>"Choose now," Rex raised his fist.</p>
<p>Kagura’s scream stopped right away. Takeda’s hands, which had been reaching for his fallen sword, stopped moving. Ayame just stared at him with eyes that were empty from fear.</p>
<p>"Y-YES...! W-We give up," Takeda was able to say while peeing his pants. "P-P-Please...! We give up for good! Spare us all...!"</p>
<p>Rex let go of the pressure that was holding them down, but he kept his telekinesis ready in case they did something dumb.</p>
<p>And of course, they didn’t.</p>
<p>They just lay there on the ground, shaking and broken.</p>
<p>He looked back at the demon maids, who had all had different reactions to the show. It was clear that Nyx was turned on again, because that’s how she is.</p>
<p>Sera looked impressed and a little scared. Some were interested and some were disturbed, while Lilith was thrilled.</p>
<p>Rex asked, "Is this enough proof?"</p>
<p>A sound cut through the clearing before anyone could answer. A deep, resonant horn sound came from the direction of Aethelgard.</p>
<p>The signal that demon armies used to tell each other to back off when a battle was going badly.</p>
<p>"That sound... the attack has failed," Sera said in a matter-of-fact tone. "Lord Mordecai will be calling all troops back to the staging grounds and then back to headquarters."</p>
<p>"Right on time," Rex said.</p>
<p>He pointed to the three reincarnators who were still alive. "Bind those three."</p>
<p>"They’re proof of my worth to your lord." Rex crossed his arms. "Three captured apostles and reincarnators, delivered directly to him."</p>
<p>"That should prove my worth to face him."</p>
<p>The maids swiftly drew ropes and bound spells from their supplies with a familiar ease. Takeda, Kagura, and Ayame were all tied up and gagged in less than a minute, and their weapons were taken away.</p>
<p>"You’re smarter than you look," Nyx said, getting up even though she was still a little shaky. "Taking prisoners shows that you are thinking strategically instead of just killing everyone you meet."</p>
<p>"Lord Mordecai likes that kind of thinking."</p>
<p>"Then let’s not waste any more time," Rex said.</p>
<p>"Bring me to him."</p>
