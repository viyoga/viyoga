<p>The courtyard of the Nightwing estate was no longer a place of training; it was a consecrated ground for a massacre. The air itself felt brittle, as if the atmosphere had been crystallized by the sheer force of Theo’s lingering temporal distortion.</p>
<p>Rex Rexilion stood in the center of the training circle, his movements precise, almost hypnotic. He moved through the mastery’s martial forms, a flowing sequence of deadly transitions designed to maintain the body’s combat memory.</p>
<p>Each strike was silent, each shift of weight absolute. He looked less like a man practicing and more like a predator maintaining its edge.</p>
<p>Theo watched from the shadows of the colonnade. His breathing was ragged, each inhale feeling like he was swallowing broken glass.</p>
<p>His knuckles were still split and weeping blood, but he didn’t feel the pain. All he felt was the white-hot, pulsing hatred that had become his new heartbeat.</p>
<p>His mind was a fractured mirror, reflecting only one image: Rex’s face.</p>
<p>"I know what you are," Theo spat.</p>
<p>His voice didn’t carry across the courtyard; it seemed to tear through the silence like a blade.</p>
<p>Rex completed a devastating circular transition, his palm stopping millimeters from an imaginary throat, before he settled into a perfect stance. He didn’t turn immediately. He allowed the silence to stretch, a deliberate provocation.</p>
<p>"You said that earlier, Theo."</p>
<p>"I mean it differently now," Theo said, stepping into the light. His eyes were bloodshot, pupils dilated into pinpricks of murderous intent. "Before, I was an accusation."</p>
<p>"A child crying out against an injustice."</p>
<p>"Now?" He let out a laugh that sounded like bones grinding together. "Now, I am a statement of fact."</p>
<p>"I am the consequence you created."</p>
<p>Rex finally turned. His expression was unnervingly placid, the flat, patient attention of a god observing an insect.</p>
<p>"And what is this statement?"</p>
<p>"That I am a vector," Theo snarled, stalking forward. "As long as I carry this designation, as long as Kaeleth’s voice screams in my skull, I am the weapon you forged to destroy yourself."</p>
<p>"I am the plague you invited into your house."</p>
<p>"Yes," Rex said simply.</p>
<p>The casual acceptance was like salt in a wound.</p>
<p>"And you are going to address that." Theo’s voice rose, cracking with restrained insanity. "You’re going to take it."</p>
<p>Rex’s eyes narrowed almost imperceptibly. "Take what?"</p>
<p>"The designation!" Theo screamed, the temporal energy around him flaring, causing the pebbles at his feet to levitate and shatter. "You think I don’t see it?!"</p>
<p>"You think I’m too broken to understand your fucking calculus?!"</p>
<p>"You’ve been hunting Kaeleth since before this month began!"</p>
<p>"You’ve been playing this long game, sacrificing families, sacrificing souls, sacrificing my mother!"</p>
<p>Theo’s fist slammed into a stone pillar beside him, the impact sending a shockwave through the courtyard. "The designation I was given was temporal mechanics, the navigational component—it was the last piece of your puzzle!"</p>
<p>"You didn’t want to kill me; you wanted to study me!"</p>
<p>"You wanted to adapt to the designation’s mechanics so you could navigate to Kaeleth’s domain!"</p>
<p>"You needed the key, and you used my trauma to forge it!"</p>
<p>Rex said nothing. His silence was a vacuum, sucking the air out of Theo’s lungs.</p>
<p>"You want to use dimensional manipulation and presence manipulation to breach his realm," Theo continued, his words coming faster now, fueled by a psychotic frenzy. "And you need the temporal designation’s navigational data to find the coordinates."</p>
<p>"You need to bridge the gap between our reality and his."</p>
<p>"Hohhh...?" Rex smirked. "Busted, huh?"</p>
<p>"But I don’t care as long as he’s aware of me trying to get to his ass," Rex laughed. "Then this is indeed going to make everything interesting."</p>
<p>Theo stopped inches from Rex’s face. The hatred between them was so potent it felt like a physical barrier.</p>
<p>Theo’s breath smelled of copper and rage. "You want the power of a god to go to war with a demon. So take it. Take it from me."</p>
<p>Rex’s hand moved in a blur of motion that Theo barely registered and gripped Theo’s throat. It wasn’t a killing blow but a test of will.</p>
<p>"You think you can simply hand over something that has rewritten your very existence?"</p>
<p>Theo didn’t flinch. He leaned into the grip, his eyes burning with a terrifying smile. "I’m not handing it to you, you bastard."</p>
<p>"I’m forcing it on you. I am giving you the very thing that will consume you."</p>
<p>Rex’s grip tightened, his fingers digging into the muscle of Theo’s neck. "You want to die for this? To be a hollow shell so I can have my war?"</p>
<p>"I want you to feel it," Theo whispered, his voice dripping with venom. "I want you to feel the weight of every soul you’ve crushed."</p>
<p>"I want you to take this designation and feel Kaeleth’s fingers tightening around your throat!"</p>
<p>"I want you to take it over my dead body."</p>
<p>The tension in the courtyard reached a breaking point. The air screamed as the two forces, Rex’s cold, calculated ambition and Theo’s raw, chaotic hatred, collided.</p>
<p>"Then come and claim it," Rex said, his voice dropping to a lethal register. "Try to take it from me."</p>
<p>Theo’s response was not words. It was a roar of pure, unbridled violence as he swung a fist imbued with the power to shatter time itself.</p>
<p>The collision was not merely physical; it was a cataclysmic rupture in the fabric of the present. When Theo’s fist connected with Rex’s forearm, the sound wasn’t a wet thud; it was a CRACK BOOM, like a lightning strike trapped inside a bell.</p>
<p>The temporal shockwave rippled outward, pulverizing the stone tiles beneath them into fine white powder.</p>
<p>Theo didn’t stop. He couldn’t stop.</p>
<p>He was a hurricane of grief and chronological fury.</p>
<p>’FUCK YOU!’ Theo’s mind screamed, drowning out the divine whispers of Kaeleth. ’FUCK EVERYTHING YOU’VE BUILT!’</p>
<p>He unleashed a torrent of temporal energy, his movements becoming a series of staccato blurs. To any observer, Theo would seem to be teleporting, but in reality, he was simply accelerating his own personal timeline to impossible speeds.</p>
<p>He struck Rex with three consecutive blows: a left hook, an uppercut, and a spinning backfist, each hit landing with the force of a freight train.</p>
<p>THWACK CRUNCH!</p>
<p>Rex’s head snapped back from the first blow, blood spraying from his split lip in a crimson arc. But as Theo pivoted for the second strike, Rex moved.</p>
<p>He didn’t dodge; he simply shifted his presence, his body flickering like a flame in a draft. Theo’s fist passed through the space Rex had occupied, finding only empty air.</p>
<p>"Is that all your hatred provides, Theo?" Rex’s voice came from behind him, cold and mocking. "Raw, directionless momentum?"</p>
<p>"SHUT UP!" Theo roared.</p>
<p>He spun, his hand glowing with a sickly gold light. He grabbed the air itself and pulled.</p>
<p>VROOOOM SHATTER!</p>
<p>The space around Rex fractured. Time slowed to a crawl within a three-foot radius of Rex, forcing his movements to become sluggish as if he were moving through molten lead.</p>
<p>Seizing the opening, Theo drove his knee into Rex’s ribs.</p>
<p>CRACK.</p>
<p>The sound of Rex’s ribcage splintering was sickeningly clear. Blood coughed from Rex’s mouth, spattering Theo’s face. But Rex didn’t collapse.</p>
<p>He grabbed Theo’s throat with both hands, his fingers like iron bands.</p>
<p>"You think pain is a currency I respect?" Rex growled, his eyes burning with a cold, predatory light.</p>
<p>Rex unleashed his own power, a surge of dimensional manipulation that twisted the space between them. Suddenly, Theo felt as though he were being pulled in two directions at once.</p>
<p>His left arm felt like it was stretching toward next week, while his right arm felt anchored in yesterday. The vertigo was nauseating.</p>
<p>"AUGH GAAAAH!" Theo screamed as his tendons strained against the temporal shear.</p>
<p>But the pain only fed the furnace. Theo’s eyes turned entirely gold.</p>
<p>"YOU WANT THE DESIGNATION?" he shrieked, his voice echoing with the layered distortion of Kaeleth’s power. "THEN FEEL THE FULL WEIGHT OF IT!"</p>
<p>Theo detonated his power outward in a sphere of chronological decay.</p>
<p>KRA KOOOOM!</p>
<p>The explosion threw Rex backward twenty feet, sending him skidding across the courtyard, leaving deep furrows in the earth.</p>
<p>Theo didn’t give him a moment to breathe. He charged, his footsteps sounding like rapid-fire gunshots: tap, tap, tap, tap, tap!</p>
<p>Each step brought him closer to Rex in a series of microteleports.</p>
<p>Rex rose to one knee, wiping blood from his chin, his breathing heavy but controlled. As Theo closed the distance, Rex raised both hands.</p>
<p>"Presence Manipulation: Absolute Anchor."</p>
<p>The air around Rex became impossibly dense. When Theo swung a devastating overhead strike, it felt like he was trying to punch through solid diamond.</p>
<p>The impact sent a shockwave up Theo’s arm that threatened to shatter his own radius and ulna.</p>
<p>KRA THOOM!</p>
<p>"Too slow," Rex whispered. He drove a palm into Theo’s solar plexus.</p>
<p>The impact wasn’t just physical; it was a dimensional displacement that momentarily displaced Theo’s internal organs, sending him spiraling backward in agony.</p>
<p>Theo hit the ground hard, gasping for air that felt too thin to breathe. His vision swam.</p>
<p>His ribs felt like broken shards of glass grinding against each other. But through the haze of pain, he saw Rex walking toward him, methodical and inexorable.</p>
<p>’Don’t stop,’ Kaeleth whispered, the voice now a seductive purr. ’Break him.’</p>
<p>’Break the world.’</p>
<p>’Let time bleed until there is nothing left but the void.’</p>
<p>"I’m not... done..." Theo wheezed.</p>
