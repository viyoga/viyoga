<p>The Open Court had established a fundamental rule of existence within this circle: ’No murder shall touch the Guardian of the Innocent.’</p>

<p>Every attack he launched carried the undeniable signature of murderous intent, and because of that intent, the universe itself refused to let the attack connect.</p>

<p>His own malice was being weaponized against him by the very air he breathed.</p>

<p>A cold, calculating clarity washed over Rex as the implications of the Law sank in. He couldn’t fight her with anger.</p>

<p>He couldn’t fight her with intent. To kill her was to fail by the very physics of the arena.</p>

<p>’Fine,’ he thought, a predator’s grin splitting his bloodied lips. ’If I can’t be the hunter, I’ll be the trap.’</p>

<p>He shifted his entire strategy mid-motion. He lowered his stance, his hands open instead of balled into fists.</p>

<p>He stopped initiating. He stopped throwing the first blow.</p>

<p>He stopped every magical assault that carried the unmistakable stench of assassination.</p>

<p>He became passive, almost vulnerable, waiting for her to commit.</p>

<p>He let her attack him first.</p>

<p>The moment Evelyn lunged, Rex’s entire existence became a reactive weapon. When she struck, he didn’t counterattack; he defended with such flawless timing that her own momentum became his ammunition.</p>

<p>He would parry a blow, not to push her away, but to redirect her force into his own counterstrike. Because his movements were now framed as defensive responses, mere reactions to her existing violence, the Court’s Law found no purchase.</p>

<p>The ’intent to murder’ was stripped away, replaced by the ’intent to survive.’</p>

<p>His counters began to land. Each one carried the redirected fury of her own divine strikes, amplified by his [Wrath] and [Martial Arts Mastery].</p>

<p>Every strike she threw at him came back with something worse behind it than anything he would have started himself.</p>

<p>BOOM. CRACK. SHATTER.</p>

<p>The battle escalated beyond the confines of the earth. The plaza could no longer contain them.</p>

<p>Evelyn drew a thin, razor-sharp line of lightning through the air above the plaza, stitching the sky with gold.</p>

<p>With impossible grace, she stepped onto the bolt as if it were solid ground. She ascended past the half fallen clock tower of the Academy, a streak of ascending divinity.</p>

<p>Rex didn’t use magic to follow. He didn’t need it.</p>

<p>He pushed his [Peak Superhuman Physique] to its absolute breaking point, his muscles tearing and knitting in real time as he leapt from crumbling masonry to floating debris, a dark, relentless shadow rising through a sky choked with smoke and ash.</p>

<p>They collided in midair beside the shattered windows of the Great Hall. The impact was cataclysmic.</p>

<p>The clouds themselves seemed to catch fire around them, ignited by the friction of their clashing energies, swirling in pale gold halos.</p>

<p>WHAM!</p>

<p>Evelyn’s boot connected with Rex’s chest in a devastating roundhouse kick. The force sent him plummeting, a human meteor crashing through the ancient roof of the main assembly hall.</p>

<p>CRASH!</p>

<p>The structure groaned as he tore through layers of slate tiles and ancient oak beams, sending a rain of splinters and debris cascading down like deadly confetti.</p>

<p>Rex rolled through the wreckage, blood streaming from his ears, but he didn’t stop. He launched himself back upward like a coiled spring, catching Evelyn mid-flight.</p>

<p>He wrapped his arms around her waist and threw his entire body weight sideways, driving her toward the edge of the floating island with the force of a runaway train.</p>

<p>KRA KOOOOOM!</p>

<p>They slammed into the cliff’s edge. The impact was so violent that a massive plate of rock the size of an entire parade ground cracked free from the island’s foundation.</p>

<p>The earth shrieked as the foundation gave way, and the enormous slab began to tilt precariously over the endless abyss below.</p>

<p>Rex froze.</p>

<p>For a heartbeat, the violence stopped. The world seemed to hold its breath.</p>

<p>It was a small thing, only a fraction of a second, but in that moment, Rex’s instincts overrode his bloodlust. His mind flickered to the island itself, to the stone that held the people he called ’his.’</p>

<p>He reached out, his fingers splayed. "Earthen Authority: Absolute Seal."</p>

<p>The power didn’t flow like a weapon; it flowed like a lifeline. His connection to the island’s bones surged outward.</p>

<p>The cracked stone groaned as if in pain, but then the fissures began to close. The massive slab stopped its deadly tilt, shuddering as Rex’s will forced the rock back into its original position.</p>

<p>The edges fused together, sealed by sheer geological stubbornness. The island stood whole again.</p>

<p>The silence that followed was heavier than any explosion.</p>

<p>Evelyn had not attacked during that second. She hung suspended in the air a few meters away, her golden aura dimming slightly.</p>

<p>Her bloodied face was unreadable, but her eyes, those terrifying gold eyes, were fixed on him with something approaching confusion.</p>

<p>"You’re protecting your kingdom," she said.</p>

<p>Her voice was flat, almost hollow, echoing strangely in the sudden stillness. "Even in the middle of this..."</p>

<p>"Even while you try to destroy me."</p>

<p>Rex stood on the newly sealed edge, his chest heaving, blood dripping from his chin onto the unmoving stone.</p>

<p>He looked at the island, then back at her, his expression hardening into something ancient and possessive.</p>

<p>"It’s mine," Rex said, his voice carrying a terrifying certainty.</p>

<p>He pressed the last of the crack closed with the flat of his blood slicked hand. "I look after what’s mine."</p>

<p>It was the absolute truth, and in the brutal calculus of combat, truth was nothing more than another vulnerability. Rex knew this better than anyone.</p>

<p>The island did not simply float; it was suspended by an ancient, divine gift, anchored by the temporal authority of Kaeleth, a power that now hummed like a living nerve inside Rex’s own soul.</p>

<p>He would not unleash it; to touch that power would be to rewrite the very history of the sky, and he was not ready for that cost. But the knowledge of it was a cage.</p>

<p>He understood now that every time Evelyn struck at the cliff’s edge, every time her divine fury threatened to fracture the island’s foundation, he was forced to split his focus.</p>

<p>He couldn’t simply attack; he had to defend the very ground they stood on. He had to hold his kingdom together while she sought to dismantle it.</p>

<p>Every strike she aimed at the perimeter forced him to pour his energy into [Earthen Authority] to prevent a catastrophic collapse.</p>

<p>She had nothing to guard but her own life; he had to guard his entire world. It was a constant, draining tax on his power, a small but relentless advantage that had allowed her to push him onto his back foot twice before.</p>

<p>The fight ceased to be a duel; it became an endurance of gods.</p>

<p>Time lost all meaning. The violence became rhythmic, a horrific dance of breaking bones and screaming energy.</p>

<p>Twenty-three minutes after the first pillar of golden light had touched the plaza, the last bell in the Academy tower gave one final, mournful CLANG before its rusted support snapped.</p>

<p>The bell tumbled from its perch, crashing through ancient rafters before striking the ground with a deadening THUD that echoed through the carnage.</p>

<p>By forty minutes, the sun had begun its slow descent toward the western horizon. The shadows of the two fighters grew long and distorted across the blood-soaked stones, stretching like grasping fingers across the cratered plaza.</p>

<p>Both were transformed. Rex’s skin was a map of fresh lacerations and old burns; Evelyn’s golden aura flickered like a dying candle, her breathing ragged and wet.</p>

<p>Rex’s eyes suddenly flared with a new, sickly light. He had been experimenting, testing the boundaries of the Court’s Law, and now he had found a crack in the divine armor.</p>

<p>"Resonance Frequency: Violet Rupture."</p>

<p>He didn’t throw a fist or a bolt. Instead, he wove a new frequency out of thin air, a pulsing, subsonic wave of violet energy that made the very atoms of the air throb.</p>

<p>It wasn’t an attack aimed at her body; it was an attack aimed at the space she occupied. The pulse tore through the air with a sound like tearing silk—SHHHHHHHH VUM—and struck Evelyn from the inside out, designed to make her own organs vibrate against each other.</p>

<p>Evelyn’s eyes widened as her ribs groaned under the harmonic pressure. But the Court was not so easily fooled.</p>

<p>"JUDGMENT!"</p>

<p>CRACK BOOM!</p>

<p>A lance of Judgment Lightning tore through the violet pulse. The blessing didn’t care about resonance or frequencies; it didn’t understand physics or harmonics.</p>

<p>It only understood one thing: Intent. It read the murderous hunger behind the violet wave and struck it down with righteous fury.</p>

<p>The lightning collided with the resonance wave, creating a secondary explosion that threw both fighters backward.</p>

<p>Evelyn recovered instantly, her training overriding her agony. She reached into the air and pulled, condensing the surrounding light into a solid form.</p>

<p>"Lightning Blade: Severance."</p>

<p>A sword of living, crackling gold materialized in her grip. With a scream of effort, she lunged, slashing a horizontal arc aimed at Rex’s torso.</p>

<p>The blade hummed with enough voltage to vaporize steel.</p>

<p>Rex didn’t dodge. He couldn’t afford to.</p>

<p>"Gravity Field: Singularity Shield."</p>

<p>A sphere of hyperdense gravitational force snapped into existence inches from his chest. As the lightning blade connected, the gravity field didn’t block it; it warped it.</p>

<p>The blade struck the invisible curve of the field and bent sideways with a screeching SKREEEEEE, the energy struggling to follow the impossible geometry.</p>

<p>The strain was too much; the blade shattered like glass, sending shards of solid light embedding themselves into the surrounding rubble.</p>

<p>"Damn you..." Rex hissed, tasting blood.</p>

<p>"I am... the answer," Evelyn gasped, her voice cracking.</p>
