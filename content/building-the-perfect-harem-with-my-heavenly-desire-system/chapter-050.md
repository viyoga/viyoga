<p>The man tried to speak, but all that came out at first was a broken gasp. His lips trembled as he struggled to form words.</p>

<p>&quot;I... I don’t know&quot; he stammered, but when he noticed the chilling look in Alex’s eyes, he immediately spilled everything.</p>

<p>&quot;Okay! Okay, I’ll talk!&quot; The man spoke frantically. &quot;The boss is still at his villa, expecting the beauty any minute, while the others just went out to get some food... That’s why I... I just wanted to use that opportunity as they were gone, to sleep with the woman over there. Please it was the work of the devil... Spare me sir, I promise I won’t ever look at any woman in my life... In fact I’ll even become a monk. J... just spare me.&quot;</p>

<p>Alex tilted his head slightly and gave him a look of pure disgust. What he absolutely loathed were scums like this, who bullied the weak with passion and feared the strong. If he hadn’t been strong enough to protect Olivia, if he hadn’t been strong enough to instill fear into the coward’s heart, wouldn’t he have just watched a repeat of his first life, but this time in person.</p>

<p>&quot;Why did you guys go after a poor defenseless woman, who has never done a bad thing to anyone in her life? Why did your boss want her?&quot; Alex asked. Though he already knew the reason, he just wanted proof, so that dealing with the others, especially the boss, would be justified.</p>

<p>&quot;It was... it was her son! Zack! Yes, Zack!&quot; The man squealed, desperate to shift the blame to someone. &quot;F-from what I know, the idiot happened to sleep with one of the boss’s girls, so when we tried looking for the brat and couldn’t find him anywhere. The boss ordered us to target his mother instead, hoping to use her to lure him out, but we didn’t know that his mother was even more beautiful than the boss’s girls. So when the boss saw her photo, he immediately wanted her as revenge... After we captured the woman, I had been secretly observing her, because she was just too beautiful, so much so that I couldn’t stop thinking about her.. I swear I just wanted a taste, even if it was only once. It was the devils fault. Please have mercy on me!&quot;</p>

<p>Hearing all that crap, Alex decided that there was no need sparing such a scum, who in reality, was really nothing more than a stain to society... Not like he even cared about that, all he knew was that anyone who touches his woman, must either die or live a life worse than death.</p>

<p>Meanwhile, Olivia on the side had forgotten about the intense pain racking through her foot, as she stared in shock at Alex’s action. She wondered how much he had really changed, from being Zack shy, quiet friend, to becoming a handsome young man who seemed indifferent to violence, and would not hesitate to strike when the need arises.</p>

<p>Truth be told, she was scared of this new version of Alex, but deep down she also felt touched by his dominance and gentle touch. As for her idiotic son, Zack. She was beginning to grow more and more disappointed with him. After her ex-husband left them, she had struggled fearlessly to take care of him, and reached where they currently were without breaking any morals. She had loved her son with all her heart, but he ran away after doing something so bad, without even caring about his own mother.</p>

<p>Olivia sneaked a peek, at Alex who was still interrogating the burly man. After the last time that they had made out and Zack had almost caught them, she began to hate her self for almost sleeping with her son’s best friend. But she didn’t know when she began to crave his warmth, his touch, she stayed up most nights thinking about what would have happened, even though she wanted to forget that moment. She didn’t even know why the first chance she had to call someone for help, it was Alex that first flashed through her mind.</p>

<p>She had thought that he wouldn’t answer, that maybe he also regretted that night... But still he came all alone, just to save her. That single gesture broke down the walls she had so desperately built around her heart. But she shook her head. Now wasn’t the time to think about all that. It was still really dangerous here, especially with the other thugs still out there. She was sure that Alex, no matter how much he could fight now, won’t be able to resist a dozen people.</p>

<p>Before Alex could deliver a finishing strike straight for the man’s back, the sound of multiple footsteps suddenly rang out, filling the burly man with a faint sense of hope. Alex turned his head toward the direction of the sound, and narrowed his eyes slightly.</p>

<p>Good, it seemed like the other idiots had all arrived. That meant there was no need trying to look for them. Soon a dozen men poured into the dilapidated warehouse with ferocious looks on their faces. But Alex just took a step toward Olivia and covered her disheveled body that was revealing some skin with his jacket, he didn’t want the idiots seeing her beautiful body.</p>

<p>As he turned to face the thugs, Olivia clutched his clothes tightly, and frantically shook her head to forbid him from taking another step. It was clear that she didn’t want to see him hurt in the slightest.</p>

<p>But Alex just gave her a reassuring gaze and stroked her light brown hair.</p>

<p>&quot;Don’t worry. I can handle these fools without breaking a single sweat. So just trust me.&quot; He paused, then smiled mischievously to diffuse the tension. &quot;But after this, later today when you’re fully healed, we’ll continue from where we stopped last time. This time there will be no interruptions.&quot;</p>

<p>Olivia blushed heavily and cursed him under her breath. How could the fool be so shameless in such a serious situation? So gritting her teeth, she pushed through the pain, looked around and grabbed a wooden stick, the size of a man’s hand.</p>

<p>With determination written all over her face, she pushed herself off the ground and stood beside Alex. Olivia absolutely refused to watch him get hurt just because of her. At least she’ll do what she can do to help him, even if her leg currently hurts like hell.</p>

<p>Alex stared at Olivia with shock and something close to genuine respect. He never expected that the warm motherly woman with gentle vibes would one day pick up a stick fatter than her delicate arm, all in the name of helping him. Inside he felt deeply touched, as he watch the delicate woman beside him try to stand strong despite the pain. Right then and there he promised himself to always protect this woman, even if she was Zack’s mother.</p>

<p>The leader of the thugs, a burly man around two meters tall, with rippling muscles under his dark skin and a steel pipe in his hand, stepped forward and sneered.</p>

<p>&quot;Would you look at that? The brat and bitch think they can stop us, especially after beating Jeffery to a bloody pulp... Boys! after we beat this annoying pretty boy over here to death, what do you say about enjoying this woman over here, before delivering her straight to the boss. But... no one should tell the boss about what we did. Do you get that!&quot; He barked.</p>

<p>The others all laughed with a lecherous grin on their face and nodded their heads. Alex, thoroughly disgusted by them, dashed forward like a hurricane and unleashed a terrifying set of attacks that could leave anyone horrified with fear.</p>

<p>He first broke the first man’s jaw, sending him straight into another thug, who wanted to stab him with a broken bottle of beer. Alex then dodged the swing of a blonde haired thug with a casual tilt of his head and delivered a crushing kick straight into the man’s groin. Immediately a guttural, high-pitched scream echoed throughout the room, filling all who heard it with fear.</p>

<p>The crowd momentarily became confused, causing Alex to capitalize on their confusion. Everywhere he went the sounds of bones cracking rang out. The leader, noticing that his men were beginning to get scared and disorganized, immediately roared out.</p>

<p>&quot;What are you waiting for you piece of shit?! Attack the damn bastard! He is just one guy. Just how hard is it for you guys to hit him...? In fact, whoever takes him down first, gets to sleep with the beauty first.&quot;</p>

<p>His words seemed to rile up the disoriented crowd of thugs, and served as a shot of energy. The thugs immediately attacked with frenzied attacks, not caring whether they hit one of their brothers or the perpetrator in their midst.</p>

<p>Though everywhere looked rowdy and extremely dangerous, Alex remained calm and composed as he easily dodged their attacks. These fools couldn’t compare to the various abominations he had fought in the training realm during his training.</p>

<p>He grabbed a man’s outstretched hand and instantly twisted it backward, with a sickening snap, before he turned back and delivered a powerful round house kick to an incoming thugs midsection, knocking the daylight out of him. Another tried to stab him in the stomach but he used another man as a shield while flashing Olivia a reassuring wink.</p>

<p>Meanwhile, Olivia stood there with her mouth wide open in awe. Since when did Alex become capable of holding his own against a group of brutish thugs? She snapped out of it, the moment she saw the gang leader sneak up behind Alex with a steel pipe in his hand.</p>
