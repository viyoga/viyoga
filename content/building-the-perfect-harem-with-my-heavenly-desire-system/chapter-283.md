<p>&quot;But.... If I remember correctly, the Boss did tell me how to break it.&quot;</p>

<p>Alex turned his head toward her. &quot;She did? Then what are you waiting for? Spill it.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;She didn’t exactly give me every detail, but she did explain enough for me to understand what we’re going to be dealing with. So you know the barrier around the island is not just some ordinary defensive barrier, right? And that It has been designed to keep even powerful Awakened from forcing their way inside. Well according to the Boss, its defensive strength is roughly twice that of a Master Realm expert.&quot;</p>

<p>Doris crossed her legs and rested her hands on her knee. &quot;Meanimg only one Master Realm expert wouldn’t even be close to enough to break it. Even two would have a very hard time, especially if the barrier is being actively maintained from inside. To destroy it before the people protecting the island can even think of reacting, we would need at least three Master Realm experts working together.&quot;</p>

<p>She paused, allowing the information to settle before continuing.</p>

<p>&quot;That means you and I should be able to do it, provided we can find a third Master Realm expert willing to help us. Sigh. If only Jade were here now, we would already have our third person. Unfortunately, she’s not. And contacting her might prove more difficult than before. I haven’t heard from the Boss in a while, so I don’t know whether they can receive messages from outside the island.&quot;</p>

<p>Alex rubbed his chin. The situation was more troublesome than he had expected. He had assumed that reaching the island would be the simple part and that his strength would be enough to deal with whatever obstacles stood between him and Nyx. Apparently, the Von Aurelians seemed to take their security very seriously. But in a way it was understandable.</p>

<p>With them being a Great Family their security naturally has to be top notch. But building a protective force field of that scale would definitely have caused quite a huge fortune. Not that it might even feel anything to a family operating in the trillions tiers</p>

<p>&quot;But since she isn’t, our only hope right now is getting little miss frosty fingers over there to help us.&quot;</p>

<p>Alex looked at her. &quot;Celine?&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;Who else?&quot; Doris gave him a knowing smile. &quot;She’s already a Master Realm Awakened, and she has a close &quot;relationship&quot; with you. With that sweet mouth of yours if you talk to her, I don’t see why she would refuse.&quot;</p>

<p>Alex gave a thoughtful hum. Celine was not exactly the easiest of persons to convince. She had a stubborn streak, and she disliked being told what to do. Still, he had spent enough time with her to know that she wasn’t unreasonable. Since he once helped her with the Marcus problem then maybe she’d agree to help him. If worse comes to worse, he’ll just use preparing her favourite food to win her over.</p>

<p>&quot;Hmm. I’ll talk to her.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;Good. We should avoid wasting too much time. The sooner we bring the Boss and Jade back, the better.&quot;</p>

<p>Doris pushed herself off the mattress and stretched her arms above her head. Her expression then changed, and she glanced toward the door as though she had suddenly remembered something important.</p>

<p>&quot;Anyway, I should get going. My Chinese food delivery just arrived, and I’ve been dying to try it.&quot;</p>

<p>Alex stared at her for a moment, before shaking his head with a quiet laugh. &quot;well.then go before your food gets too cold.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;yes I’m going.&quot; She walked toward the door, then glanced back at him. &quot;Remember, don’t forget what we discussed.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;I won’t, now hurry off.&quot;</p>

<p>Doris huffed and then left, closing the door behind her, leaving Alex who remained on the bed, returning his gaze back to the ceiling. For a while, he considered the problem she had just presented. Three Masters would be needed to break the barrier, and although he and Doris could provide two, they still needed another person.</p>

<p>Asking Celine shouldn’t be too difficult, right?</p>

<p>He could already imagine her narrowing those beautiful obsidian eyes and asking why he was dragging her into another troublesome situation. She might complain, call him a pervert, or accuse him of making decisions without considering her opinion. That was simply how she was. But once he enticed that second foodie with his signature dish she would definitely agree.</p>

<p>At least, he hoped she would.</p>

<p>He would speak to her later. There was no point worrying about it before he had even asked.</p>

<p>Another thought soon came to him, and Alex shifted slightly against the pillow. The auction Celine had mentioned was scheduled to take place tomorrow. He still had the two genuine Ascendant Realm Primordial Cores to sell, and he also wanted to see what other treasures would be available during that day.</p>

<p>There might be something worth purchasing, who knows he might even see a rare treasure there.</p>

<p>The auction also offered an opportunity to meet people with influence, not that he cared about all that, but there was no harm in observing the people who would attend. If the host family could provide a safe way to sell the cores, he would be satisfied.</p>

<p>For now, the plan was simple. He would attend the auction, sell the two cores, and see whether anything caught his interest. After that, he would deal with the problem of reaching the Von Aurelian island and bringing Nyx and Jade home.</p>

<p>....The following day arrived sooner than expected.</p>

<p>By around the hour after the sun had set, Alex was standing near the entrance of a luxury cruise liner docked at a private harbor. He had to say that these enormous vessel certainly looked more like a floating palace than an ordinary ship, especially with all those several decks of polished glass, white walls, and golden decorations.</p>

<p>Its name was written in elegant lettering along the side, while a line of expensive cars ranging from flashy Ferraris to Mercedes Benz and other world class cars, continued to arrive near the boarding area.</p>

<p>When Celine had first told him about the auction, he had imagined a large hotel ballroom or perhaps a private convention center. He hadn’t expected the organizers to hold the event on a cruise liner of all places.</p>

<p>Apparently, it seems the host family wanted their guests to enjoy a little luxury while conducting business.</p>

<p>....</p>

<p>Ever since getting here, the system had been chiming no stop. It seems being extremely handsome had it perks.</p>

<p>Alex adjusted the cuff of his dark suit and glanced toward the people arriving around them. There were wealthy businessmen, influential socialites, politicians, and several faces he recognized from television. A few famous actors and musicians were among the guests, each arriving with a partner or walking alone while surrounded by assistants and security personnel.</p>

<p>It was an impressive gathering, even by the standards of the wealthy.</p>

<p>Some guests wore expensive suits, while others had chosen elegant dresses and jewelry that probably cost more than what an ordinary person would earn in their lifetime. The atmosphere was very lively indeed, that he couldn’t deny, but there was also an unmistakable sense of exclusivity that filled the air.</p>

<p>Every person entering the ship had to present an invitation or a ticket, and the security staff were checking each guest carefully.</p>

<p>Since Alex had the ticket that allowed him to bring one companion. After considering his options, he ended up deciding to bring Celine along. Apart from the fact that she knew more about the Awakened world and its auction customs, he didn’t want to attend an event like this alone.</p>

<p>Celine stood beside him in a beautiful black dress that fitted her curvy figure without being overly revealing. Her long dark hair fell over her shoulders, and her pale face looked calm as she observed the guests. She had chosen a simple necklace and matching earrings, but even without expensive accessories, she still managed to attract plenty of attention.</p>

<p>&quot;With the way they’re staring at you, I’m afraid someone might truly steal you away from me.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;Says the man who has been gawked at by at least more than half the women since we arrived here.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;So you’ve been counting. Does that mean you’re jealous?&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;Oh please. They can gawk at you for all I care.&quot;</p>

<p>With that they continued toward the entrance, where a staff member stood beside a small registration desk. Alex reached into his jacket and produced the invitation ticket which the staff member checked before returning it with a polite smile.</p>

<p>&quot;Welcome aboard, sir. The auction hall is on the upper deck. You may proceed inside.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;Thank you.&quot;</p>

<p>Alex stepped forward with Celine, but before they could reach the entrance, his extraordinary senses picked up the feeling of someone watching them.</p>

<p>Alex subtly turned his attention toward the source and found the perpetrator which was a young man being accompanied by a powerful looking bodyguard.</p>

<p>The guy looked like he was in his late-twenties, with pale blonde hair that was neatly brushed back and a pair of dark eyes that gave his aristocratic looking face a charming look. The suit he wore looked like it was clearly expensive, and the way he carried himself suggested that he was most likely someone who was accustomed to generally having people obey him.</p>

<p>His attention was not at him but was instead directed on Celine.</p>

<p>It wasn’t unusual for people to look at Celine. After all, with her kind of beauty, anywhere she went she was bound to attract attention. However, the gaze the young guy was giving her carried a different intention.</p>

<p>It was one which Alex had seen countless times before. In words, it was the type of gaze all men knew about whenever they saw a drop dead beauty. The young man made little effort to hide his interest, which made Alex eyes grow cold and dangerous.</p>

<p>Is this bastard truly tired of living? Staring at his woman with that perverted gaze of his, it seems he truly no longer wants that life of his.</p>

<p>What happened next made him mentally put the young man on his list. The young man leaned slightly toward the bodyguard standing beside him and spoke in a low voice.</p>

<p>&quot;Find out who the man accompanying that woman is. I want to know his background, and whether he has any powerful connections. Don’t dare make a scene here. Just investigate him quietly.&quot;</p>

<p>The bodyguard followed his gaze and looked directly at Alex.</p>
