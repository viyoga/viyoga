<p>Before he could continue thinking, a familiar ripple of blue light suddenly appeared beside him. The next second, the tiny chibi materialized in mid-air.</p>

<p>Her blue hair swayed lightly as she floated cross-legged. Unlike earlier, her expression was cheerful... Very cheerful, maybe even suspiciously cheerful, causing Alex to raise an eyebrow.</p>

<p>’What?’</p>

<p>The chibi grinned.</p>

<p>[What? Can’t I be happy?]</p>

<p>’No, it’s just that you only smile like that when you’re planning something.&quot;</p>

<p>The tiny girl gasped dramatically.</p>

<p>[Alex!]</p>

<p>[How could you accuse me like that?]</p>

<p>’You literally look guilty.’</p>

<p>[That’s slander]</p>

<p>Alex rolled his eyes. The system then floated closer, widening her smile even further.</p>

<p>[Anyway. Congratulations on surviving tonight like a champ]</p>

<p>’Thanks.’</p>

<p>[You know you were nearly this close to death]</p>

<p>’Yeah I noticed.&quot;</p>

<p>[Several times... Proving that you really are an Indomitable cockroach]</p>

<p>’Can you really stop calling me that.’</p>

<p>The system giggled.</p>

<p>[Well it is one of your titles, right next to the newest one. Sexual predator]</p>

<p>&quot;...&quot;</p>

<p>[But still I have to commend you for managing to save your woman]</p>

<p>A faint smile appeared on Alex’s face. Yeah. That part alone made everything worth it. Nyx was safe from that perverted man. They had escaped. And the icing on the cake, Annie was captured.</p>

<p>Objectively speaking, tonight had ended far better than it could have. But then the system suddenly tilted her head.</p>

<p>[Speaking of Nyx]</p>

<p>Alex blinked, confused.</p>

<p>&quot;What about her?&quot;</p>

<p>The chibi floated lazily through the air.</p>

<p>[You know... In all that chaos. You never actually checked something important.]</p>

<p>Alex frowned.</p>

<p>’What is it?’</p>

<p>The system’s grin instantly turned smug.</p>

<p>[The rewards! Rings a bell]</p>

<p>Alex froze before his eyes slowly widened slightly. Right. Because of everything that had happened afterward, he had completely forgotten.</p>

<p>Earlier tonight, in order to cure the drug’s effect, he and Nyx had had sex. Which meant his desire rewards like his Desire Points and Desire Energy. He hadn’t checked any of it yet.</p>

<p>The realization immediately pushed every other thought out of his mind. Alex straightened slightly in his seat while looking toward the floating system.</p>

<p>’Okay show me what I got?&quot;</p>

<p>[With pleasure]</p>

<p>[Total Rewards Calculating...]</p>

<p>[+250 Desire Points Gained For Successfully Sleeping With Nyx]</p>

<p>[Total Points: 939 desire Points]</p>

<p>[+70 Desire Energy Gained For Successfully Sleeping With Nyx]</p>

<p>[Total Desire Energy: 300 Desire Energy]</p>

<p>[+3 boost has been added to all your physical stats]</p>

<p>&quot;Alright, now let’s see my full stats.&quot;</p>

<p>[Yeah, just hold on a minute]</p>

<p>[Status — Updated]</p>

<p>[Name: Alex Walker]</p>

<p>[Age: 21]</p>

<p>[Body Condition: Super human]</p>

<p>[Vitality: Strong]</p>

<p>[Strength: 114→117]</p>

<p>[Speed: 112→115]</p>

<p>[Agility: 117→120]</p>

<p>[Perception: 110 →113]</p>

<p>[Charm: High]</p>

<p>[Baseline Charisma: High]</p>

<p>[Libido: High]</p>

<p>[Desire points: 939 points]</p>

<p>[Desire energy: 300/600]</p>

<p>[System Rank: C ]</p>

<p>[Skills:]</p>

<p>• Aura Of Seduction (Lv.1)</p>

<p>• Profit Instinct</p>

<p>• Spatial Storage Space</p>

<p>• Infinite Skill Absorption</p>

<p>• Deathfrost Bloom (Thirty Shards Limit With Fifty Percent Resistance To Cold)</p>

<p>• Eye Of Chronos (7.5 seconds usage period)</p>

<p>[Physical Development: high]</p>

<p>[Current length: 10 inches]</p>

<p>[Harem Slots: 5 / ∞]</p>

<p>[Titles Earned:]</p>

<p>• The Indomitable Cockroach</p>

<p>• Harem Predator</p>

<p>Alex stared at the floating status screen for several long seconds before a slow grin gradually spread across his face.</p>

<p>&quot;Not bad.&quot;</p>

<p>Actually, not bad was underselling it.</p>

<p>A couple of months ago, he had been nothing more than a completely ordinary human with no powers, no special abilities and no special future waiting for him.</p>

<p>But Now?</p>

<p>Just his strength stat had reached one hundred and seventeen. His speed had climbed to a decent one hundred and fifteen. His agility had broken into one hundred and twenty. Even his perception had now crossed one hundred and thirteen.</p>

<p>If his earlier self had seen those numbers, he’d probably never believe any of it.</p>

<p>The system folded her tiny arms smugly.</p>

<p>[You can thank your beautiful and hardworking system for that]</p>

<p>Alex snorted.</p>

<p>’Did you just say hardworking?’</p>

<p>[Absolutely. You heard me right]</p>

<p>’Oh please. You spend half your time teasing me.’</p>

<p>[That’s called being emotional supportive, buddy]</p>

<p>’Well, It is not.’</p>

<p>[Well, it is]</p>

<p>Alex shook his head helplessly before returning his attention back to the rest of his status screen. Three hundred Desire Energy. Nine hundred and thirty mine Desire Points. Everything was looking great.</p>

<p>Well, at least for a few seconds. Then his smile slowly weakened, causing even the system to immediately notice it.</p>

<p>[What’s wrong? Why does your mood feel a bit down?]</p>

<p>Alex leaned back in his seat.</p>

<p>’It’s just...’</p>

<p>His eyes drifted toward the Desire Energy section.</p>

<p>Three hundred. Only three hundred out of six hundred.</p>

<p>The system’s smug expression faded slightly.</p>

<p>[Ah]</p>

<p>’Yeah.’ he replied mentally while rubbing his head.</p>

<p>Although the reward from Nyx had been incredible, they still weren’t enough. He was only halfway there.</p>

<p>Halfway.</p>

<p>All of a sudden the number felt much smaller than it had initially looked. According to the system’s requirements, six hundred Desire Energy was needed before he could finally advance to the next rank.</p>

<p>And that next rank wasn’t just any rank. It was B Rank. The equivalent of Ascendant Realm. The Realm that had nearly beaten him to death.</p>

<p>The Realm were energy or essence could be projected outward to improve both defense and apply pressure on weaker opponents. And finally the damn Realm where true monsters began appearing.</p>

<p>Alex couldn’t help remembering the fight with the Chinese fighter the moment he suddenly broke through to Ascendant Realm. The overwhelming speed probably way past the speed of sound.</p>

<p>The terrifying power to cause destruction on a large scale with only pure strength. The sonic booms. And the helpless feeling of being completely outmatched.</p>

<p>Even now, that memory annoyed him. The only thing that saved him was that he was lucky to have enough Desire Points to activate Time Rewind, if not he would have been a dead man.</p>

<p>Now, three hundred Desire Energy.</p>

<p>He still needed another three hundred Desire Energy. Hmm. The realization made him sigh.</p>

<p>The system floated closer and said softly.</p>

<p>[C’mon cheer up. Don’t look so depressed]</p>

<p>’I’m not depressed.’</p>

<p>[Well you look depressed to me. You can’t fool this eyes]</p>

<p>’I’m really not depressed. I’m just thinking.’</p>

<p>[Yeah... that’s just depression with extra steps]</p>

<p>Alex rolled his eyes, making the tiny chibi giggled before poking his shoulders.</p>

<p>[Besides you’re forgetting something important]</p>

<p>’And what is that if I may ask?’</p>

<p>[Don’t forget that you still have several very promising women]</p>

<p>Alex blinked. Then his eyes slowly lit up. The system wasn’t wrong. Actually... She was completely right. What was he overthinking himself for?</p>

<p>There was still Jade, the college goddess who represented a huge opportunity. Then there was Olivia. After everything they had already gone through together, their relationship was steadily growing stronger, even if she refuses to admit it.</p>

<p>Sofia was another possibility. And then... Alex gaze unconsciously drifted toward the uncomfortable assassin lying nearby. Annie.</p>

<p>A dangerous smile slowly appeared on his face. That woman had nearly blown him to the afterlife. The least she could do was compensate him. Generously.</p>

<p>[Wow. Alex with that smile on your face, I’m beginning to feel sorry for the poor woman]</p>

<p>Alex smirked before ignoring her as another name appeared in his mind.</p>

<p>Celine.</p>

<p>A chuckle escaped his lips. The memory was still amusing. That woman while wearing his clothes had practically sworn she would find him again.</p>

<p>Something about revenge. Something about making him pay. Honestly, he wasn’t completely sure anymore. But if she truly intended to keep that promise, then perhaps their next meeting would be very interesting.</p>

<p>Hehe.</p>

<p>The more Alex thought about it, the better his mood became. Three hundred Desire Energy wasn’t actually that bad. At least not when he looked at the countless opportunities ahead.</p>

<p>At this rate, reaching Ascendant Realm wasn’t some impossible dream anymore. It was only a matter of time.</p>

<p>The system nodded approvingly.</p>

<p>[There we go. Now that’s the spirit.]</p>

<p>Alex smiled.</p>

<p>’You’re right. Thanks.’</p>

<p>[Of course I’m right]</p>

<p>The tiny girl said while placing both hands on her hips.</p>

<p>[I’m always right]</p>

<p>Before Alex could argue with that ridiculous statement, his stomach suddenly growled loudly. The noise echoed throughout the cabin.</p>

<p>Both Alex and the system blinked before his stomach growled again. This time even louder than the last.</p>

<p>&quot;...&quot;</p>

<p>[&quot;...&quot;]</p>

<p>For several seconds, neither of them spoke. Before Alex then remembered that it was the side effect of the Body Recovery Water acting up.</p>

<p>The water had repaired practically every injury across his body. But healing required energy. And by energy he meant a lot of energy.</p>

<p>His face scrunched up in pain as the full force of the hunger slammed against him like a tidal wave. It was so damn unbearable. It felt as though he hadn’t eaten anything in weeks.</p>

<p>Without wasting another second, Alex mentally opened his storage space. Fortunately, it was a good thing he had developed a habit of storing food whenever possible. And tonight that habit was about to save his life.</p>

<p>A moment later...</p>

<p>Three extra large pizza boxes appeared on the table in front of him. Then came several turkey sandwiches. Followed by a large cake he had purchased days ago and forgotten about. Then two big Macs and finally, an extra large bottle of ice cold cola.</p>

<p>The entire table was instantly covered, causing the system to stare at both him and the table with shock.</p>

<p>[Good grief]</p>

<p>Alex first grabbed a slice of pizza and bit into the cheesy surprise with relish, before reaching for the big Mac, and taking a big bite out of it.</p>

<p>&quot;Oh my God! So good!&quot;</p>

<p>Several minutes later, the scene at this moment looked less like a meal and more like a natural disaster.</p>

<p>Pizzas vanished like they were nothing. Sandwiches ceased to exist. The cake— may it rest in perfect peace— suffered a brutal and immediate death. The big Macs lasted less than thirty seconds before joining its fallen comrades.</p>

<p>Even the extra large cola bottle was almost empty. Alex ate with the determination of a man fighting for survival. Because in a way.... He was.</p>

<p>The recovery water had turned his stomach into a black hole. At one point even the system looked horrified.</p>

<p>[Where is it all going?]</p>

<p>Alex swallowed another mouthful before responding.</p>

<p>’I have no idea.’</p>

<p>[You’re terrifying, host]</p>

<p>’I’m just hungry.’</p>

<p>[You’re consuming enough food to feed a small family]</p>

<p>Alex casually pointed toward the empty pizza boxes.</p>

<p>&quot;Correction. Consumed.’</p>

<p>The system stared at him before slowly floating away from him, lest he adds her to the meal.</p>

<p>[Host. I’m suddenly afraid of you]</p>

<p>Time passed. Eventually the mountain of food gradually disappeared, and the terrible hunger that once plagued him finally began weakening. Though not completely.</p>

<p>But it was enough that Alex felt human again. He leaned back into his seat. Satisfied. Comfortable. And considerably less likely to devour the entire food in the private jet</p>

<p>The system placed her tiny hand on her chest and sighed in relief.</p>

<p>[Thank goodness. The black hole of a man is finally satisfied]</p>

<p>Alex stretched lazily, enjoying the calm of the quiet aircraft. After a minute he reached toward the final remaining pizza box.</p>

<p>Only one slice remained. A survivor and the last warrior standing. He grabbed it and just as he was about to take a bite of the good stuff, a cold voice suddenly interrupted him.</p>

<p>&quot;Now how about we talk about that ability you’ve been hiding from me.&quot;</p>
