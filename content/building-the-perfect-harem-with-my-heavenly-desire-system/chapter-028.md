<p>Since he had been drinking with her this whole time, then suddenly acting drunk shouldn’t look strange at all... Right?</p>

<p>In fact, Alex knew that with the way he looked like a typical mundane college kid who had probably never touched a bottle of liquor in his life—Nyx would most likely believe his act immediately.</p>

<p>And if he happened to lose his balance...</p>

<p>Alex’s lips slowly curled upward. Then suddenly falling on her by accident wouldn’t look suspicious at all, he mused. Plus no matter how brief the contact ended up being. The aura would still be able to do its work in that brief expanse of time.</p>

<p>But there was a small problem though. If Nyx manages to see through the act... Then he might not leave this room with all his bones intact.</p>

<p>Alex took one slow, final deep breath, mentally bracing himself. If he was going to do this, then it had to look real. Not just clumsy—no. It had to be an all out, full blown, one hell of a fall.</p>

<p>&quot;Everything okay over there? You look like you’re fighting a war in your head,&quot; Nyx remarked, her voice a bit slower than before. She was watching him over the rim of her glass, with a slightly glazed pair of eyes that seemed to be getting dimmer with each passing minute.</p>

<p>&quot;I’m... I’m fine,&quot; Alex stammered, putting on his best performance of a drunk man who was pretending to be sober. &quot;I’m just a bit... Hot in here. Is the AC off? Oof it’s just so damn hot.&quot;</p>

<p>He stood up abruptly, making the glass table rattle after him. took one step, then two, before deliberately letting his right ankle fold underneath him.</p>

<p>&quot;Whoa...!&quot;</p>

<p>Alex’s body lurched forward like a puppet whose strings had suddenly been cut. His foot slid awkwardly across the floor as his balance gave out completely.</p>

<p>Thump!</p>

<p>His body crashed down awkwardly on top of Nyx. A light gasp escaped her lips as his shoulders collided with her torso, the impact forced both of them back into the soft couch.</p>

<p>&quot;S-sorry... &quot;He mumbled weakly, his words slurring slightly. &quot;Everywhere just felt... blurry. And the floor just sorta moved. I swear.&quot;</p>

<p>Nyx stared at him with a stunned expression on her usually aloof face. The faint blush from earlier had now deepened more than before. And for the briefest of seconds, her fingers came up to caress Alex’s face. But then, suddenly, her eyes sharpened as she instantly regained her composure.</p>

<p>&quot;... Are you done?&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;You have exactly three seconds to remove your body from me, if not, I’ll make sure you won’t be able to move it. Permanently.&quot;</p>

<p>So instead of scrambling away like a terrified subordinate, he ignored her, and continued to rest on top of her.</p>
