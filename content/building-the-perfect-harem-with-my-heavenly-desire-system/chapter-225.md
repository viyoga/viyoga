<p>By the time they finally left the apartment, Olivia looked completely satisfied with herself, while Alex had already accepted that his peaceful day was over.</p>

<p>Somehow, the woman who had later coaxed him after the sex that they were only going out for a little shopping had turned the simple trip into what felt like a military campaign on steroids. Alex followed beside her through the large shopping center, carrying several bags in both hands without showing the slightest sign of effort.</p>

<p>He glanced down at the bags hanging from his fingers and then at Olivia, who was currently standing in front of another clothing display, examining a dress with the kind of concentration one would give something very important.</p>

<p>Alex sighed inwardly. Shopping and women really are like five and six. They are both completely inseparable. He had already lost count of how many stores they had entered.</p>

<p>Olivia, however, seemed to be getting happier with every one.</p>

<p>&quot;This one is beautiful,&quot; she said, holding up a dark blue dress against herself.</p>

<p>Alex looked at it and then said without thinking.</p>

<p>&quot;Yeah, I guess it looks good.&quot;</p>

<p>Olivia turned her head and eyed him.</p>

<p>&quot;Hmm?&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;I said it looks very good. Truly top notch material, especially on you. I don’t think even a model will be able to pull it off like you.&quot;</p>

<p>Her lips curved into a pleased smile.</p>

<p>&quot;That was better.&quot;</p>

<p>Alex patted his chest and gave a small sigh of relief.</p>

<p>He was already beginning to understand that women did not simply go shopping to buy clothes. They went shopping to receive compliments, admire themselves, compare colors, change their minds, buy something they had already decided they didn’t need, and then return to the first store because they suddenly remembered a dress they had liked twenty minutes ago.</p>

<p>And this woman standing not too far from him, was no exception. In fact, she even seemed particularly excited today. Maybe it was because she was finally happy.</p>

<p>Alex watched her move from one rack to another, her eyes lighting up whenever she found something she liked. There was something oddly satisfying about seeing her this way. She was not thinking about loneliness, family problems, or anything else that had once weighed heavily on her.</p>

<p>She was simply enjoying herself. Alex smiled faintly. Seeing that alone made carrying all the bags worth it.</p>

<p>Olivia suddenly pulled another dress from the rack.</p>

<p>&quot;Alex, look at this.&quot;</p>

<p>He turned and looked at what she was holding. It was a beautiful white dress that looked elegant and fitted, with a simple design that somehow made it look more expensive than it probably was.</p>

<p>&quot;It looks good too,&quot; he admitted.</p>

<p>Olivia couldn’t help agreeing and decided to add it to the list of things she was buying.</p>

<p>Soon two good hours had passed and this woman still looked so full of energy. Alex was exasperated by how women could keep shopping so much without getting even the least bit tired. He himself was already starting to get tired from staying here.</p>

<p>Just as he was about to tell her that hadn’t they done enough shopping for today? he suddenly remembered the first rule of Women 101. Never tell a woman to stop shopping while she’s shopping. So he silently closed his mouth.</p>

<p>A few minutes later, Olivia disappeared into the changing room with another dress in her hands.</p>

<p>&quot;This one is for something important,&quot; she said before closing the curtain.</p>

<p>Alex raised an eyebrow. Something important? He wondered what she was planning. But he didn’t ask.</p>

<p>Experience had taught him that when a woman said she needed to try something on for something important, the safest response was to wait until she decided to explain it herself.</p>

<p>Alex stood outside the changing room, surrounded by shopping bags. While waiting, Alex casually shifted the bags in his hands and looked around the shopping center. The place was still bustling despite the fact that it was already well into the afternoon, with people moving between the various stores and employees standing near entrances while trying to attract customers.</p>

<p>He didn’t pay much attention to any of them.</p>

<p>That was until he suddenly noticed someone staring at him. At first, Alex ignored it and continued standing there with his back against the wall, patiently waiting for Olivia to finish changing. He was already used to stares like this from people everyday.</p>

<p>However, after several more minutes, he began to feel increasingly uncomfortable.</p>

<p>The person staring at him was a woman, who looked like she was most likely in her late twenties to early thirties. She had neatly styled brown hair, a fashionable cream-colored blouse, and a small black handbag resting against her side.</p>

<p>There was really nothing particularly strange about her appearance. If anything it looked pretty good actually.</p>

<p>The thing was that the way she was staring at him without blinking, was beginning to bother him. Not to be a narcissist or anything, he knew he was handsome and all, but this was just too much.</p>

<p>Five minutes passed. Then ten followed suit. Eventually, Alex began wondering whether there was something strange about his face that he hadn’t noticed.</p>

<p>By the time nearly twenty-five minutes had passed, he finally couldn’t take it anymore.</p>

<p>What made the entire situation even stranger was the fact that the woman had started mumbling to herself while staring directly at him.</p>

<p>Alex couldn’t hear everything at first, but his enhanced perception easily picked up her quiet voice from several meters away.</p>

<p>&quot;Really exceptional facial proportions... Honestly, it’s almost unfair.&quot;</p>

<p>Alex’s eyebrow twitched. The woman continued studying his face as though she were inspecting an expensive piece of merchandise.</p>

<p>&quot;Striking forest-green eyes that remind one of nature. Hmm... sharp but also naturally harmonious features.&quot;</p>

<p>She tilted her head slightly.</p>

<p>&quot;Jawline is perfection incarnate.&quot;</p>

<p>Alex’s expression became increasingly strange.</p>

<p>&quot;And the skin is just magnificent. It looks even better than most top models I’ve seen.&quot;</p>

<p>At that point, Alex finally pushed himself away from the wall. He turned toward her and stared directly into her eyes.</p>

<p>&quot;Excuse me?&quot;</p>

<p>The woman suddenly froze. Alex gestured toward his own face and couldn’t help asking.</p>

<p>&quot;Is there something on my face that’s making you so fixated on it? Because you’ve been staring at me for the past twenty-five minutes.&quot;</p>

<p>The woman blinked several times before realizing that she had been caught.</p>

<p>&quot;Oh! I’m so sorry!&quot;</p>

<p>She hurriedly straightened herself and gave him an apologetic smile.</p>

<p>&quot;I didn’t mean to make you uncomfortable. I was just... surprised.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;Surprised?&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;Yes.&quot;</p>

<p>She looked at his face again, though this time she quickly forced herself to look away.</p>

<p>&quot;To be completely honest, I was just surprised that someone could actually be this good-looking.&quot;</p>

<p>Alex stared at her. For a moment, he didn’t know how to respond. Was she seriously hitting on him?</p>

<p>He glanced toward the changing room before looking back at the woman.</p>

<p>&quot;Are you flirting with me?&quot;</p>

<p>The woman’s face immediately turned awkward.</p>

<p>&quot;What? No!&quot; She hurriedly waved both hands in front of herself. &quot;No, no, that’s not what I meant!&quot;</p>

<p>Alex raised an eyebrow.</p>

<p>&quot;Because it certainly sounded like it.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;I understand why you would think that.&quot;</p>

<p>She gave an embarrassed laugh and adjusted the strap of her handbag.</p>

<p>&quot;The truth is, I’m actually a modeling scout.&quot;</p>

<p>Alex’s expression changed slightly.</p>

<p>&quot;Oh, a modeling scout?&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;Yes.&quot;</p>

<p>She reached into her handbag and pulled out a small business card.</p>

<p>&quot;I work for a modeling company called Luxury Swan. We’re still small compared to other major agencies in the city, but we’re still growing.&quot;</p>

<p>She held the card toward him.</p>

<p>&quot;I’ve been searching for new faces around the city, and when I saw you standing here, I honestly couldn’t believe my luck.&quot;</p>

<p>Alex looked down at the card before taking it from her. The woman continued speaking with growing enthusiasm.</p>

<p>&quot;You have exactly the kind of appearance we’re looking for. Your facial proportions are incredibly balanced, your eyes are distinctive, your skin is excellent, and your overall presence is something that photographs extremely well.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;Honestly, you could probably walk into a professional photoshoot tomorrow and make half the people there wonder why they didn’t notice you sooner.&quot;</p>

<p>Alex couldn’t help chuckling.</p>

<p>&quot;That’s quite the sales pitch.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;I’m serious.&quot;</p>

<p>He looked at the card again. Luxury Swan.</p>

<p>The name immediately triggered a memory from his previous life.</p>

<p>Back then, almost nobody had heard of this company. It had been a small modeling group struggling to establish itself in a market dominated by much larger agencies.</p>

<p>But that wouldn’t remain the case forever.</p>

<p>Years later, Luxury Swan would end up becoming a major name in the fashion and modeling industry, working with famous models, luxury brands, and international campaigns.</p>

<p>Alex looked at the card with a strange expression. Who would’ve thought this pie would fall right into my lap in this life?</p>

<p>It was certainly an interesting opportunity.</p>

<p>Unfortunately, modeling wasn’t really his thing.</p>

<p>He already had enough responsibilities on his shoulders. Between helping with Nyx’s businesses, taking control of assets inherited from Marcus’s former empire, dealing with matters connected to the Bureau, and managing his own growing harem and abilities, he barely had enough free time as it was.</p>

<p>Adding modeling to that list sounded exhausting.</p>

<p>&quot;I’m flattered,&quot; Alex said politely, preparing to hand the card back. &quot;But I’m afraid....&quot;</p>

<p>Before he could finish, something caught his attention. Several meters away, a man was standing near one of the racks.</p>

<p>At first glance, there was nothing particularly suspicious about him. He just looked like a guy buying something for his lover. The man was even holding a phone against his ear while speaking quietly.</p>

<p>&quot;...Yes, honey, I’ll be home soon. Don’t worry about dinner. I’ll pick up something nice on my way back.&quot;</p>

<p>However, there was still something about him that immediately made Alex uncomfortable.</p>

<p>Behind the ordinary smile and relaxed expression he was showing right now, there was something deeply wrong. Hidden deep inside his dark brown eyes was a sort of madness that brought a strange chill down his spine.</p>

<p>And aside from that, Alex was able to pick up a faint ticking sound coming from underneath the man’s shirt.</p>

<p>It was so quiet, maybe almost silent enough that an ordinary person would never even notice it even if standing close to the guy. But Alex’s enhanced hearing was able to pick it up clearly...</p>
