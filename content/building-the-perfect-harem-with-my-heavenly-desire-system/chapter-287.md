<p>Just as he was wondering why, a trace of the mist leaked out, making even him feel this warm, inexplicable gentle force suddenly wash over him. The sensation came without any form of warning whatsoever, only spreading all the way from his skin into his muscles and bones as the bluish-green mist slowly drifted through the auction room.</p>

<p>To describe the sensation, it was not really overwhelming or anything.... but if he had to put it into words, he’d say it was soothing, almost like stepping into a warm bath after spending an entire day surviving an icy blizzard. He could feel as a pleasant vitality surged through his body, nourishing him from the inside and easing the barely noticeable exhaustion he had not even realized he was carrying this whole time.</p>

<p>Alex’s eyes widened slightly.</p>

<p>The mist lingered for only a few seconds before it began moving in the opposite direction as Brenda immediately closed the lid, causing that gentle sensation to disappear along with it.</p>

<p>Alex leaned forward a little, staring at the plant in surprise. Now he suddenly understood why every person in the room had reacted so strongly. Even just that brief contact had left him feeling noticeably better. So much so that he couldn’t help wishing that it would have lasted a little longer.</p>

<p>What a profound plant! Just the nourishment the mist provided felt almost close to half the recovery he would normally have experienced from using the Body Recovery Water. This is seriously insane from only the mist of a plant.</p>

<p>’But come to think of it, if the mist alone can do all this, what would end up happening if someone used the plant itself?’</p>

<p>He was still considering the possibilities when a loud voice rose from the crowd.</p>

<p>&quot;What a fine plant! An exquisite plant indeed!! Just the mist alone has rid me of that head splitting headache I’ve been nursing for days!&quot;</p>

<p>Alex glanced toward the direction of the voice and found out that the speaker was a bald middle-aged man seated several rows away. He rubbed his shining forehead with amazement, still unable to believe that the persistent pain that had troubled him for days was finally gone after the intervention of that mist. Just what kind of sorcery is this?!</p>

<p>His words stirred the entire room.</p>

<p>Several people confirmed that they truly felt refreshed more than before, while others exchanged excited whispers. Even a man who couldn’t perform properly felt like he was just injected with strength and couldn’t resist taking his mistress to their room to go have fun.</p>

<p>The eyes of many people burned with greed, much like wolves watching an easy meal. Some of them suffered from no sickness, so that meant the plant’s effects might not be of much use to them. But that didn’t mean they had no reason to want it.</p>

<p>All of them were people with brains if not they wouldn’t be where they were currently! A resource like this which was more than capable of curing illnesses could very well be used to build connections with very powerful people.</p>

<p>For example they could offer it to a powerful family in exchange for protection, while someone from an organization could use it to secure a favor from an important figure. Even a person who could not benefit personally might find countless ways to turn such a treasure into an advantage. Who knows, maybe this could even be the lottery ticket to getting one of the great families of America as their backer.</p>

<p>Brenda watched all of their reactions with a soft smile. In truth, she was quite satisfied with the result. Even though she knew it would cause quite a stir, this still exceeded her expectations.</p>

<p>&quot;Alright, since everyone here appears interested, allow me to explain what we know about this extraordinary item.&quot;</p>

<p>She said while gesturing toward the sealed container.</p>

<p>&quot;This is a strange plant discovered by one of our people near the Bermuda Triangle not too long ago. Its exact origin remains unknown, and our researchers don’t exactly know how long it has existed, it could be a million years or even more. However, we have confirmed several of its effects.&quot;</p>

<p>She paused briefly, allowing the guests to focus on her.</p>

<p>&quot;As you all felt a minute ago, even just a wisp of the mist it naturally releases can cure minor ailments and restore a person’s vitality. As for the plant itself, its effects are far more astonishing. It can cure diseases that ordinary medicine considers incurable, and also possesses the ability to regenerate lost flesh.&quot;</p>

<p>A wave of murmurs swept through the room.</p>

<p>&quot;Regenerate flesh? Are you really saying it can restore a missing limb?&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;Isn’t that what she just said a minute ago, or is the old age finally affecting you, chairman Steele?</p>

<p>&quot;Then what about damaged organs?&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;If it can regenerate limbs then it’s only natural that it can also heal damaged organs.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;Uhmm, do you think it might have any side effects that we should be aware off?&quot;</p>

<p>The questions came from different directions, but Brenda did not seem surprised. She raised one hand, and the staff beside her waited until the room became quiet again.</p>

<p>&quot;According to the information available to us, its regenerative properties are genuine. We cannot promise that it can restore every kind of injury, because we have not conducted enough tests to establish its limits. Nevertheless, the results have been remarkable. And no it does not have any side effects. I hope I’ve answered all your questions?&quot;</p>

<p>Alex’s expression became thoughtful.</p>

<p>The plant was certainly valuable. Its ability to cure diseases and regenerate flesh could make it priceless to ordinary people, particularly those suffering from conditions that even the best hospitals could not treat. It would also be useful to Awakened who sustained serious injuries.</p>

<p>However, he already possessed something that could perform a similar function.</p>

<p>The Body Recovery Water in his Desire Shop was capable of restoring his body, although its effectiveness had diminished after repeated use. But spending billions of dollars on it simply because it was rare did not seem sensible.</p>

<p>’Maybe unless it has some hidden property that the auction house hasn’t discovered yet, buying it would be a waste since I already possess Body Recovery Water.’</p>

<p>He leaned back in his chair, deciding to observe the bidding rather than participate. Brenda waited until the excitement had settled down before announcing the starting price.</p>

<p>&quot;The starting bid is one billion dollars, with each of it’s increase set at a minimum of ten million.&quot;</p>

<p>She had barely finished speaking when a voice shouted from the audience.</p>

<p>&quot;Two billion dollars!&quot;</p>

<p>The sudden bid drew several glances. The bidder was a fat flamboyant looking man wearing a gold watch, and from the look of things he appeared determined to secure that plant at any cost. With that plant he would finally be able to heal the sick mother of a big shots investor from France.</p>

<p>A woman seated nearby sneered.</p>
