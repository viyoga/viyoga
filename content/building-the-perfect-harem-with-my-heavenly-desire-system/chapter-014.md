<p>Alex felt a chill crawl down his spine. Fair?! There was absolutely nothing fair about any of this?!</p>

<p>This woman was absolutely crazy.</p>

<p>But what choice did he have? It was either he agreed to her absurd demand or lose his finger and tongue. And right now, he simply wasn’t strong enough to resist her.</p>

<p>If only he were stronger... None of this would have been happening.</p>

<p>He clenched his fists tightly. Strength. Everything came down to strength. Without it, even breathing felt like a privilege granted by someone else.</p>

<p>The chibi, who had been sitting comfortably on her favorite spot—his shoulder, and was thoroughly enjoying the situation, suddenly stood up with a serious expression on her cute little face and said.</p>

<p>[Listen carefully, crying about strength won’t magically make you stronger]</p>

<p>[Since she wants you, take advantage of it and grow under her protection then surpass her]</p>

<p>[Do I need to remind you that you literally have a system that helps you grow strong, faster than others... well ahem as long as you gain more romantic like emotions from women]</p>

<p>Alex nodded. What the chibi said was true. He just needed to pretend to work for her for a while, then when he grows stronger he will definitely break out of her hold and get his revenge.</p>

<p>He took a deep breath then turned to the casino owner with a serious expression on his face and agreed to her demands.</p>

<p>&quot;Fine, I agree to work for you.&quot;</p>

<p>’though not forever.’ he said the last sentence mentally.</p>

<p>The casino owner’s face lit up with a satisfied smile.</p>

<p>&quot;Smart choice, kid. From today onward your life belongs to me.&quot;</p>

<p>She gestured to the blonde baby-faced girl, signalling that it was time to leave. Halfway gone, she suddenly stopped, as if remembering something, and turned back.</p>

<p>&quot;oh, before I forget. I need you to take care of that injury by your side, I don’t want my newest asset dying before he even starts working for me. And I will need you for an important assignment later so don’t even think of hiding because as long as you are alive you can’t hide from me.&quot; She said with a smile on her face. But to Alex it felt like the smile of the devil himself.</p>

<p>&quot;Run? Where would I even run to? I’m sure a person like you can find me whenever you want to.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;Good then.&quot;</p>

<p>After saying that, the mysterious casino owner turned and walked away without sparing him another glance.</p>

<p>After Alex made sure she was really gone, he cursed for a bit to vent the pent up emotion in his heart, but then winced in pain, and remembered that he was still seriously injured.</p>

<p>Alex pressed a hand to his side and hissed in pain. His mind immediately drifted and remembered an item in the desire shop.</p>

<p>&quot;Hey, system... I almost forgot, in the shop wasn’t there an item for healing?&quot;</p>

<p>The chibi stared at him, then facepalmed herself.</p>

<p>[Yes or have you forgotten. You literally saw it when you were buying the profit instinct]</p>

<p>She sighed dramatically</p>

<p>[Seriously, how do you survive with that kind of brain?]</p>

<p>Alex looked at the system in exasperation, then gestured for the chibi to show him the store. Immediately, a soft familiar ding! echoed inside his head, as a translucent blue panel unfolded in the air.</p>

<p>[Desire Shop: unlocked]</p>

<p>• Minor Luck Boost for twenty four hours — 50 Desire Points</p>

<p>• Charm Amplifier Lvl.1 (Temporary increases attraction towards the opposite sex) — 80 Desire Points</p>

<p>• Random Gacha spin — 100 Desire Points</p>

<p>• Body Recovery Water (Heals injuries) — 50 Desire Points</p>

<p>• Profit instinct (active skill) — 200 Desire Points</p>

<p>• Aura Of Seduction Lv.2 — 500 Desire Points</p>

<p>• X-ray Vision — 500 Desire Points</p>

<p>• Time rewind (Only within one minute) — 700 points</p>

<p>Alex almost cried out. After spending two hundred points on profit instinct he was still going to spend his remaining hard earned points on the body recovery water.</p>

<p>&quot;Sigh. I will just buy the body recovery water.&quot;</p>

<p>[Good]</p>

<p>His points immediately dropped down from 55 desire points to 5 desire points. Then a small bottle the size of a tea cup with an intricate design appeared in his palm.</p>

<p>The water inside looked sparkling and pure. Immediately he drank from it, a wave of pleasure instantly washed over his body. It felt like dipping in a cold pool during summer.</p>

<p>The sharp, stabbing pain at his side began to dull almost instantly. Then he felt as the torn flesh at his side began to wriggle together. Within seconds the pain and exhaustion that haunted him was completely gone.</p>

<p>Alex slowly removed his hand from his side and looked down. The wound that had nearly crippled him moments ago had now vanished without a trace. Not even a scar remained.</p>

<p>He twisted his waist carefully but there was no pain. His eyes widened with joy.</p>

<p>&quot;This is completely insane. Okay I take it back this item was worth every point spent.&quot;</p>

<p>The chibi crossed her arm and smirked proudly.</p>

<p>[Of course it is. Did you think the Desire shop sells fake products?]</p>

<p>Alex ignored her smug expression and clenched his fist while looking toward the direction that the casino owner had left earlier.</p>

<p>&quot;Enjoy while you can, because I won’t work for you forever. I’ll use you as a stepping stone to establish myself and then I’ll make sure I crush your pride and turn you to one of my women. Just you wait.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;Sigh. But for now I need to become much stronger. That means I’ve got to up my game and conquer more women.&quot;</p>

<p>[Yay finally I don’t have to stay stuck with a weak host. Ahem, did I say that out loud, sorry about that]</p>

<p>Alex didn’t bother with her, instead he pulled out his phone and checked the time. It was already late and he still had classes tomorrow. He put the phone back into his pocket, glanced around at the people still lying on the ground, then walked away without any care.</p>

<p>***</p>

<p>The night air felt cooler now. Alex adjusted his collar and walked down the street calmly. He was almost close to his apartment.</p>

<p>Honestly, after all that just happened today he really needed a good night’s rest. Even though the healing water had healed his body he was still mentally tired. The chibi perched on his shoulder suddenly interjected.</p>

<p>[Oh... I almost forgot to tell you something. that recovery water...]</p>
