<p>[Ding! Nyx is now eligible to be added to your harem slot. Do you wish to add her?]</p>

<p>For several seconds, Alex simply remained silent while trying to process what the system had just said. Here he was worrying about how he was going to face tomorrow. And now this... Audacious system was asking whether he wanted to add Nyx to his harem slot or not.</p>

<p>Finally, he dragged a hand slowly down his face and let out a long tired breath.</p>

<p>&quot;You’ve got to be kidding me. Here I am worrying about tomorrow... And all you have to say is this?&quot;</p>

<p>He leaned his head back against the couch and groaned softly.</p>

<p>&quot;God... I’m tired. Like, really tired.&quot;</p>

<p>[Hey it’s not my fault that I was designed to ask you this question. And besides, You should know that after every fucky fucky act with a woman. She gets automatically recognized by the system, so that’s why I asked whether you were willing to add Nyx to your harem slot or not]</p>

<p>[Plus. Remember that you have only one woman currently in the slot, so I’ll advise you to add Nyx]</p>

<p>Alex really wanted to pick No. after all, who in their sane mind would willingly choose to add a woman like Nyx to their harem? Unless, of course the person was completely tired of living.</p>

<p>But when he glanced down at the woman lying on his chest, sleeping peacefully with a faint satisfied smile on her face. He began to have second thoughts</p>

<p>For all her cruelty and the terrifying reputation she carried in the underworld, at this moment she looked nothing like the cold queenpin everyone feared. She simply looked like a woman who had finally allowed herself to relax.</p>

<p>Alex sighed quietly. No matter how harsh she had been toward him or how likely it was that she would try to kill him tomorrow, the truth remained that she was still human beneath that icy mask. And whether he liked it or not, he had taken her first time tonight. That fact alone made things far more complicated than he wanted to admit.</p>

<p>He ran a hand through his hair and stared at the glowing prompt floating in front of him.</p>

<p>Add Nyx to harem slot?</p>

<p>Yes or No.</p>

<p>&quot;This is a terrible idea,&quot; he muttered under his breath. Still, after a long moment of hesitation, he reached out and chose Yes.</p>

<p>[Desire Energy Bar Is Fully Filled Up. You Can Now Level Up To The Next Rank]</p>

<p>After she finished speaking, a surge of intense warmth suddenly exploded from deep inside his chest. The sensation instantly spread through his entire body. It felt like his entire body was dipped into a warm pool during the coldest of winters.</p>

<p>He could feel his muscles tightening involuntarily as the wave of warmth flowed through his body, rushing from his chest into his shoulders, down his arms, and all the way to the tips of his fingers.</p>

<p>Soon, the sensation ended as fast as it had begun, leaving Alex in a daze state. After a minute Alex slowly regained himself and looked at his body which was brimming with power. He felt like his sight had turned sharper, capable of seeing things from far distance. His ears clearer, capable of catching sounds from distances that a normal person would find difficult. Hell, even his body felt stronger... Faster and strangely lighter. With how much his strength had increased by leaps and bounds, Alex was confident that he could handle twenty men for now. Maybe even more.</p>

<p>Wanting to test his newfound strength, Alex gently lowered Nyx’s head away from his chest onto the couch, and stood up. He clenched his fist tightly, punched the air and felt just how much faster and stronger it had gotten.</p>

<p>With a satisfied smile on his face he nodded at the tiny chibi, who had been watching this foolish man punch the air like an insane person with no goal in life.</p>

<p>&quot;Dammit. This is really amazing! I can literally feel stronger. Oh my gosh with this strength I should be a match for that baby faced bodyguard. Shit this is amazing.&quot;</p>

<p>[I know right? Now stop yelling before you go ahead and wake miss queenpin over here]</p>

<p>&quot;Sorry about that. I was just so excited with this newfound strength.&quot;</p>

<p>Then remembering about the new skill he decided to give it a try.</p>

<p>&quot;Hey system, about that spatial storage stuff. How do I use it? What I mean is how do I activate it.&quot;</p>

<p>[I understood it the first time you said it. Anyways, are you dumb or just pretending to be this dumb? I literally said you can activate it through mental command. Like just look at the object you want to store and think about storing it. It’s as simple as that. It’s not exactly rocket science]</p>

<p>Without paying attention to the systems insult and only focusing on the important part of her speech. Alex finally understood how the spatial storage skill worked. Then he looked around the room, searching for an easy object to test the skill on. It’s not like he thought the skill was defective or any of that. He just wanted to see how it works.</p>

<p>Finally he sighted the empty glass cup lying on the small glass like table beside the couch.</p>

<p>&quot;...Alright. this should do.&quot;</p>

<p>Alex focused on the glass cup, trying really hard to imagine it vanish into the strange hollow part that had settled deep within his mind. Without a sound, the glass cup folded inwardly. <p>&quot;What the hell?&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;...Holy shit! Ok that was crazy.&quot;</p>

<p>[I know right? Told you it was pretty cool, but a fool over here was wishing for super strength or laser eyes...]</p>

<p>&quot;...&quot;</p>

<p>He knew that he had been too hasty to judge the skill without considering how beneficial it actually was. With this skill he could rob banks... I mean help world hunger by storing food.</p>

<p>&quot;Alright. I take it back. This is better than laser eyes. But hypothetically could I put a person in here?&quot;</p>
