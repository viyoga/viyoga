<p>With a bit of uncanny luck on their side, Alex and Celine managed to meet the person in charge of the auction not long after boarding the cruise liner.</p>

<p>The person in charge turned out to be a beautiful woman who introduced herself as Brenda Sinclair. As he later found out, she so happened to be from the Sinclair family, the very same family who hosted the auction in the first place. From what Alex had also gathered while listening to the conversations around the ship, the Sinclairs were among the five top-tier elite families in Washington.</p>

<p>Apparently, their family history stretched several centuries back, with influence extending deep into business, politics, and the Awakened community. They truly possessed deep roots and unfathomable resources, making them a family that even the Kade family would not dare offend unless they were truly afraid of not dying.</p>

<p>During their meeting, Brenda herself seemed to carry that type of confidence naturally. She was polite and professional, but there was this subtle authority in the way she spoke and handled the staff around her.</p>

<p>After briefly exchanging greetings, Alex placed the two genuine Ascendant Realm Primordial Cores on the table which made even someone like her change her expression in shock the moment she saw them. Wasn’t these Awakened cores? And judging by how exquisite they looked, they were most likely nothing lower than Vanguard cores, maybe even.... gasp... Ascendant core!</p>

<p>Staring at them solemnly, she did not reach for the cores immediately. Instead, she just continued to study the two glowing spheres carefully before looking back at Alex with deeper respect.</p>

<p>&quot;These are genuine Ascendant Realm cores?&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;That’s right, I would like to have them included in today’s auction if that’s possible, provided we can both agree on the terms.&quot;</p>

<p>Brenda gave a small nod and called over one of the specialists responsible for examining the items. The man used a small device and several methods Alex did not recognize to check the cores. The process took a little while, but in the end it was confirmed that both were authentic, and their quality was excellent.</p>

<p>The shocked specialist quietly informed Brenda of his findings before stepping away, leaving her staring at Alex again, this time with more interest than before, when she didn’t really put him in her eyes. Someone who could casually bring two Ascendant level primordial core was someone not to be trifled.</p>

<p>&quot;These are valuable items. We can certainly arrange for them to be auctioned, although I have to inform you first that there will be a commission deducted from the final sale which will be set aside for the Sinclair family.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;Ok I can work with that, after all, no person would choose to help auction for free. But I assume your family has a standard arrangement for outside consignors.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;We do, young expert. The Sinclair family will naturally receive fifteen percent of the final selling price, while the remaining eighty-five percent will go to you. I hope it’s fine with you?&quot;</p>

<p>Alex considered the offer briefly. It was a reasonable split, especially considering the reputation of the host family and the kind of buyers gathered aboard the ship.</p>

<p>&quot;Yeah, that works for me.&quot;</p>

<p>Brenda smiled and extended her hand.</p>

<p>&quot;Then we have an agreement, Mr. Walker. I’ll make sure the cores are included in the catalogue for tonight’s event.&quot;</p>

<p>Alex shook her hand. &quot;I appreciate it.&quot;</p>

<p>Once the paperwork had been completed and the cores were taken away by the auction staff, Brenda personally arranged for them to be shown to the upper-tier auction room.</p>

<p>Alex and Celine left the private lounge and followed another staff member through a wide corridor. As they walked, Alex noticed that the atmosphere around this part of the ship was quite different from the lively lower decks. There were fewer people moving about, and the security was noticeably tighter.</p>

<p>He glanced at Celine. &quot;I thought an auction of this scale would attract more people. Why does it seem so quiet up here?&quot;</p>

<p>Celine looked at him as if the answer couldn’t have been any clearer.</p>

<p>&quot;It’s because the auction is divided into two grades. The lower grade is where most celebrities, business moguls, and other wealthy guests whose net worth are usually in the hundreds of millions, sometimes more, gather. The items offered there mainly include mudane things like estates, private lands, luxury properties, expensive jewelry, and other things ordinary wealthy people would be interested in.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;While this section is mainly reserved for heirs and heiresses from various powerful families, Awakeneds, major organizations, and the elites among the elites. If you must know, most of the people here have net worths ranging from tens of billions to well over a hundred billion dollars. Some might not even care about money as much as they care about extremely rare resources worth a fortune.&quot;</p>

<p>Alex nodded slowly. That explained the smaller crowd and the heavier security. But if there was one thing he had to admit that was Marcus had really gone all out to secure an upper-grade ticket. Considering how much effort the man had put into obtaining access to this event, he must have been very desperate.</p>

<p>Unfortunately for Marcus, he would never get the chance to enjoy the benefits in this life.</p>

<p>Alex followed Celine into the auction room. Glancing around, he noticed that the place was spacious as hell, with rows upon rows of comfortable seats arranged toward a raised platform. The decorations here were tasteful rather than excessive, and the entire place gave off a kind of atmosphere that plainly screamed wealth and quiet power.</p>

<p>It looked like they were not the only ones to come early since there were already several guests already seated inside. Some of them spoke in low voices that he couldn’t be bothered to pay attention to, while others remained silent, only waiting for when the auction would finally start.</p>

<p>Alex even noticed the presence of a few Awakeneds thanks to the little gremlin who looked excited as a little kid after taking twenty cotton candy. Majority of them were Early Realms and Vanguard, but there were also a few Ascendant Realm Awakeneds. Surprisingly, there were even one or two Master Realm experts other than him and Celine.</p>

<p>Thanks to the help of a kind hearted staff who led Alex and Celine to their seats near the middle of the room, they were finally able to sit down. Once they settled he heard a woman’s excited voice coming from behind.</p>

<p>&quot;So, are all the people here really that powerful, brother?&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;Most of them are. But don’t assume that everyone here is powerful just because they have money. Some families have enormous businesses but only a handful of Awakened protecting them. Others have less wealth but possess powerful fighters. The balance between money and strength isn’t always straightforward.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;I see.&quot;</p>

<p>Before she could ask anything else, the lights in the auction room dimmed slightly, and a moment later, a stunning woman stepped onto the platform.</p>

<p>Brenda had changed from her previous dress into a stunning purple dress that hugged her curves, with a high slit that revealed one elegant leg whenever she moved. Her face was beautiful enough to belong on the cover of a fashion magazine, and her confident posture only added to her charm.</p>

<p>With the way she appeared, her temperament was in no way inferior to that of Celine’s, although in terms of appearance, Alex felt she fell just a little short.</p>

<p>Brenda waited until the room had quieted before smiling at the audience.</p>

<p>&quot;Good evening, ladies and gentlemen. On behalf of the Sinclair family, I welcome you all to today’s auction. We have prepared several rare items for tonight, and I hope everyone finds something worthy of their interest. Without further delay, let us begin.&quot;</p>

<p>The first item was brought onto the stage.</p>

<p>It was a red stone about the size of a clenched fist. Even from his seat, Alex could see a faint, fiery glow pulsing beneath its surface with various runes patterns that felt profound. The large screens even displayed its details for everyone to see.</p>

<p>Brenda gestured toward it and continued with a small smile.</p>

<p>&quot;Our first item is a rare Fire Element Essence Stone discovered in a volcanic region of Africa. Its purity has been carefully examined, and its elemental energy remains remarkably stable. As most of you know, It is especially valuable to Awakeneds whose abilities are connected to fire. As for the starting bid for an item like this, it begins at five million dollars with nothing less than a ten thousand increment.&quot;</p>

<p>A low murmur spread through the room.</p>

<p>Several people exchanged surprised looks, and some even immediately began discussing the stone with their companions. Alex noticed that even a few guests who had seemed uninterested at first were now all paying close attention.</p>

<p>If the organizers were presenting something this valuable first, then the later items might be even more impressive.</p>

<p>Alex leaned toward Celine and lowered his voice. &quot;What exactly does that stone do for Awakeneds with fire related ability? And why does it look like so many people are very interested in it?&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;That’s because this stone you’re seeing right there is an elemental stone. From what I know, those are rare resources that contain concentrated elemental energy. In this case, it’s fire. An Awakened with a fire-related ability can use its energy to strengthen their fire ability. The actual improvement depends on the purity and quality of the stone.&quot;</p>

<p>She studied the red glow for another moment.</p>

<p>&quot;And Judging by its appearance and the energy it’s releasing, I’d say this one could increase a fire ability by around ten percent or so. Though I’m not too sure.&quot;</p>

<p>Alex’s eyes brightened upon hearing that.</p>

<p>Ten percent?</p>

<p>That was certainly not a small benefit. Though a ten percent increase might seem small, it was in no way little. One had to know that in a life or death battle that seemed equal, a ten percent increase could drastically tip the scale in the persons favour.</p>

<p>Aside from that, his World Erasing Fire was already a terrifying ability, and although he had only recently acquired it, he had already begun to understand just how much potential it possessed. If a stone like this could strengthen it further, then obtaining it would be worth every penny.</p>

<p>Even if he decided not to use it himself, he could always keep it as a gift for Claire in the future. An elemental resource like this would suit her perfectly.</p>

<p>He looked back at the stage, his interest in the auction increasing considerably.</p>

<p>&quot;Five million five hundred thousand.&quot; One middle aged man shouted while raising his paddle high. His son was a fire type Awakened. With that stone, his strength would definitely grow stronger!</p>
