<p>In a small-sized room with old light blue curtains that swayed softly due to the breeze coming from an old squeaky ceiling fan tracing lazy circles in the air.</p>

<p>A young man with pale porcelain like skin and a slender yet well-built body lay sprawled on the messy bed. His thick black slightly wavy and naturally tousled hair fell carelessly over his forehead due to his constant shifting while asleep.</p>

<p>Under the dim light, his face looked surprisingly beautiful and almost unreal. Capable of easily rivaling those of top handsome boy stars on TV.</p>

<p>Alex was in the middle of a beautiful yet weird dream where he was about to screw the mysterious beautiful casino owner, when all of a sudden...</p>

<p>BEEP! BEEP! BEEP! BEEP!</p>

<p>His eyes immediately snapped open. He groaned, still wishing he could continue the dream. Like why was it at the moment he was about to fully conquer her that the alarm saw it fit to wake him up?</p>

<p>The annoying alarm clock on the bedside table continued to scream at him mercilessly.</p>

<p>BEEP! BEEP! BEE...</p>

<p>&quot;Will you just shut the hell up!&quot; He growled while slamming his palm down on it. After recovering from the outburst, he ran his finger through his dark messy hair, making it even more messier.</p>

<p>&quot;Why couldn’t that alarm have woken me up after I did the deed. Sigh. You know what? forget it.&quot;</p>

<p>[Ohhh? So this is what my host fantasizes about when he sleeps? I can’t believe you dreamt of making out with the mysterious beautiful woman. I guess boys will always be boys]</p>

<p>Alex froze.</p>

<p>&quot;Wait, you saw that?!&quot;</p>

<p>[Hello... I can literally read your mind so what makes you think that I can’t gain access to your dreams. And by the way, it was so interesting. Alas I just wished I had a camera to record it]</p>

<p>&quot;Y-you better delete that memory. I don’t care how you do it, just wipe it off or something.&quot;</p>

<p>[Wait let me think about that. Nope!]</p>

<p>Alex groaned and threw a pillow over his face. This was the worst! There was nothing worse than having your perverted system watch your dream like it was a movie.</p>

<p>Even though he knew that it was only him who could see and talk to the system, which meant that, there was no risk of the midget blabbing it to people or worse to that annoyingly terrifying woman. He still felt like it was an invasion of his privacy, but then again it looked like he better get used to it.</p>

<p>Alex let out a long breath before removing the pillow from his face and muttering.</p>

<p>&quot;You know what? Fine, it’s not like you can even tell her about it in person.&quot;</p>

<p>[Hmph! What a petty human being.]</p>

<p>Then the system suddenly exclaimed after remembering something.</p>

<p>[Oh! Aren’t you forgetting something? You were supposed to be preparing for school, afterall wasn’t that the reason your alarm clock was ringing]</p>

<p>Alex’s eyes widened immediately. &quot;School!&quot; He almost forgot about it. Immediately he bolted upright and headed straight for the bathroom to take a quick shower.</p>

<p>After finishing, he dried himself off and changed into his good jeans and a black hoodie before topping it off with a pair of white sneakers, then nodded in satisfaction when he checked himself in the mirror. After checking the time, he breathed a sigh of relief and nodded.</p>

<p>&quot;At this rate I think I should be able to make it to class in time.&quot;</p>

<p>[Yeah... Maybe]</p>

<p>After making sure that everything was in place, Alex locked the door and then hurriedly headed down the stairs. As he was about to walk out of the building, a cold voice suddenly called out his name, startling him.</p>

<p>With apprehension, Alex looked around carefully, then spotted the owner of the voice. It was the cold baby-faced girl that followed the mysterious casino owner, and judging from the way she was looking at him... something told him that this wasn’t a casual visit.</p>

<p>He suppressed a groan and walked toward her, wondering what the hell she needed from him.</p>

<p>He just hoped that whatever she needed or wanted him to do wasn’t now, because he had an important class today. And call him narcissistic or not, he wanted to see people’s reaction when they saw his improved appearance.</p>

<p>Plus, he wanted to find a new target to add to his harem. After all from his last encounter with her boss, he realized that only with strength could a person really be free to live the life he wants, without being shackled.</p>

<p>Soon he reached where she was and casually forced a nonchalant expression on his face.</p>

<p>&quot;So to what do I owe this visit early in the morning?&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;The Boss wants to see you now. So wherever you were headed before would have to hold for now.&quot;</p>

<p>Alex tried to protest.</p>

<p>&quot;Right now? But I have school, and it is really important. Can’t I just meet her after my class.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;No. Now get in the car, and don’t keep the boss waiting.&quot;</p>

<p>She said with no room for objection, then stepped aside and gestured toward a sleek black luxury SUV parked across the street.</p>

<p>Alex wanted to protest again but when he saw the icy look in her eyes, he immediately closed his mouth and complied.</p>

<p>[Ohhh, I never thought that you could be this submissive. You should really try it once in a while it fits you]</p>

<p>’Shut up’ he thought coldly.</p>

<p>He hated this feeling. He hated the fact that whenever she wanted him on a whim she could just command him to come even if he had something important doing. This only reinforced his desire to grow stronger.</p>

<p>After sitting in the back seat, the car door closed with a click, then the engine started. As the car pulled away from the curb, Alex leaned back against the seat and finally decided that he might as well enjoy how it felt to ride in a luxurious car.</p>

<p>...after a while Alex noticed that the car had begun to slow down. Driven by curiosity, he stared in awe at the large elegant villa in front of them.</p>

<p>It was a massive villa with cream colored walls trimmed with dark stone. Floor to ceiling glass panels that reflected the morning sun and a well manicured lawn that stretched on both sides. Everything here screamed wealth and power. Hell even the marble statues here looked like they were able to pay for his rent for a long time.</p>

<p>Even though he was still mad at the mysterious casino owner he had to admit that the woman certainly had good tastes.</p>

<p>As the car came to a smooth stop in front of the entrance. The baby-faced girl immediately got out and gestured for him to follow her, then walked ahead without looking back again. Alex nodded, then slipped his hands into his pocket and followed her at a relaxed pace, refusing to look intimidated.</p>

<p>Inside, the room looked even more impressive than outside. There was a crystal chandelier above and by the walls there were different expensive paintings framed with gold. After walking for a while, due to how large the villa was, they finally stopped in front of a pair of tall double doors.</p>
