<p>Brenda looked around the room, waiting to see whether anyone wished to challenge the offer. After counting to three, she brought down the small auction hammer.</p>

<p>&quot;Fifty million dollars! Sold to the gentleman in the middle section.&quot;</p>

<p>Alex lowered his paddle with a satisfied smile. Yes, he might have paid a little more than what the stone was worth, but the improvement to his ability made the expense worthwhile.</p>

<p>Besides, money was technically not something he lacked now. With all the money he had collected from generous idiots, and from Marcus he was practically swimming in wealth. A rare resource like that, that could strengthen World Erasing Fire was not something he would encounter every day.</p>

<p>Celine leaned closer and whispered, &quot;You really wanted that stone, didn’t you? But I don’t seem to recall you ever having a fire type ability, or did you have one but have been hiding it all this while?&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;Maybe, maybe not&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;Still spending a whole fifty million on that stone, don’t you think it is a little excessive? You could have kept that to bid for more precious thing later on.&quot;</p>

<p>Alex smiled. &quot;Don’t worry. I can afford it. Have you forgotten that I literally own all of Marcus Assets, so your man is definitely not poor.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;Which stinky man?! You better put down those ideas inside your mind if not I guarantee you’ll be looking like a sponge very soon.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;Are you denying that I’m not your man. After all, we did do what boyfriend and girlfriends do on the bed, more than once?&quot; Alex said with a shameless smile, and immediately scrunched his face in pain after feeling Celine’s finger pinch his waist.</p>

<p>&quot;You dog man! I ought to teach you a lesson one of this days.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;Ow ow ow, okay I’m sorry, baby... Ouch!&quot;</p>

<p>The next item was a shield made entirely of condensed energy. It had a smooth, pale surface, with an outline that shimmered faintly beneath the lights. According to Brenda, the shield could block attacks from an Early Realm Awakened and could even withstand roughly half the force of a Vanguard Realm fighter’s punch.</p>

<p>The moment she finished explaining its defensive ability, several guests showed interest.</p>

<p>For most Early Realm Awakened who lacked strong defensive skills, such an item could be extremely useful. It might even save their lives in a dangerous battle. The bidding quickly became fierce, with several people raising their paddles in succession.</p>

<p>After a prolonged contest, the shield was finally sold for ninety-three million dollars to a lanky-looking man who appeared to be an Early Realm Awakened.</p>

<p>Soon more items followed suit. There were rare medicinal ingredients, weapons, defensive artifacts, and several resources collected from dangerous ruins. Some were useful to Awakened, while others were valuable mainly because of their rarity. The prices varied greatly, but the guests continued bidding with enthusiasm.</p>

<p>Eventually, the two Primordial Cores Alex had submitted were brought onto the stage.</p>

<p>Their appearance immediately attracted attention. Genuine Ascendant Realm Primordial Cores were not items that wealthy collectors encountered every day, and several Awakened in the room leaned forward as soon as Brenda introduced them.</p>

<p>The bidding started aggressively and continued for what felt like several minutes. Powerful families and organizations competed for the two cores, each apparently unwilling to let the other obtain such a valuable resources.</p>

<p>By the time the auction ended, the pair had both sold for a staggering six hundred million dollars.</p>

<p>Even Alex was somewhat surprised by the final amount. After the Sinclair family’s fifteen percent commission, he would still receive an enormous sum. He leaned back in his chair, quietly pleased with the result.</p>

<p>The next item, however, drew his attention for an entirely different reason.</p>

<p>A long weapon was carried onto the platform. It was a halberd as black as the night, with a faint blue sheen running along its surface that made it appear like some kind of peerless artifact. Even from a distance, it seemed to radiate a sharp brilliance, as though its edge could tear through anything that stood in its path.</p>

<p>Brenda, without wasting any time introduced it as a rare weapon discovered on an island. According to the information provided by the auction house, the halberd possessed the terrifying ability to cut through even the terrifying defense of an Ascendant Realm Awakened.</p>

<p>Its starting price was five hundred million dollars.</p>

<p>The moment the guests heard its description, the atmosphere changed. Powerful families and Awakened began raising their paddles almost immediately. A weapon capable of penetrating an Ascendant’s defenses was an extremely valuable treasure, especially for those who regularly faced opponents of similar strength.</p>

<p>Even Alex felt tempted. Unfortunately, the weapon’s demonstrated ability only reached the Ascendant Realm. If it could also cut through a Master’s defense, he might have considered bidding for it himself. But as things stood, it would offer him little advantage.</p>

<p>The bidding continued until the price reached three billion dollars. In the end the young aristocratic man Alex had noticed earlier finally raised his paddle and secured the halberd. He was the same blonde-haired man who had been staring so brazenly at Celine when they arrived.</p>

<p>Alex glanced at him briefly with a cold light flickering behind his eyes before looking away. After that, Brenda announced the successful bid, and the staff carried the halberd away.</p>

<p>Then, after a short pause, another special item was brought onto the stage.</p>

<p>This time this one arrived inside an elaborate box with a small red tart placed on top as decoration. With a calm smile full of meaning, Brenda removed the tart, revealing a transparent container beneath it. The container looked incredibly sturdy with even a passcode.</p>

<p>Judging by how sturdy the glass looked, Alex wouldn’t be surprise if even a missile couldn’t cause even a single crack.</p>

<p>Inside was a strange bluish-green plant, with delicate leaves that seemed to glow faintly, while a thin layer of what looked like aquamarine mist swirled around its stem and branches. The sight made it look almost divine. Like a plant that did not belong to this world.</p>

<p>For a moment, the entire room fell silent, and even the most composed guests leaned forward in their seats. Alex looked around and noticed that every single person’s eyes in the auction room lit up, including those two Master Realm experts, just as he was wondering why, a trace of the mist leaked out, making even him....</p>
