<p>’What do you mean?’ Alex repeated his question in his mind, with his eyes darting between the floating little gremlin and jade.</p>

<p>The system did not answer right away. She kept scratching the back of her head, with her tiny brow furrowed as she looked over some invisible panel only she could see.</p>

<p>She muttered something inaudible under her breath, turning around in small circles in the air like a little fairy. Then, all of a sudden, she clapped her two hands together and exclaimed,</p>

<p>[Ah! Now I get it!]</p>

<p>Alex’s eyebrow twitched as he watched Doris shift her gaze between him and Jade. He had to keep his face normal so Doris wouldn’t think he was losing his mind.</p>

<p>’Get what?’ he snapped. ’Stop leaving me hanging.’</p>

<p>The system chibi flew closer to his ear, and said.</p>

<p>[She is a double Awakened, Alex! That’s why she broke through much earlier than expected after sleeping with you!]</p>

<p>Alex almost frowned as his thoughts turned into a complete mess of confusion. Huh? He stared at the little floating girl, wondering if his system was finally turning coo-coo from watching too much television.</p>

<p>’And?’ he asked, completely lost. ’What the hell do you mean, after sleeping with me? How does having sex with me make someone break through to the next Realm?’</p>

<p>The chibi let out a long, dramatic sigh, crossing her tiny arms over her chest as if she were a teacher dealing with a slow student.</p>

<p>[Okay, so you know how you have the Infinite Skill Absorption? The one that allows you to copy an Awakened woman’s ability after you’re intimate with them? Well, it turns out that specific ability works both ways. It doesn’t just steal from them, it actually allows the Awakened woman to advance to the exact same Realm as you!]</p>

<p>Alex’s jaw almost dropped wide open, if he hadn’t bitten the inside of his cheek in time, to keep his expression normal in front of Doris.</p>

<p>’Wait... What?’</p>

<p>[Let me finish!] The chibi huffed, waving her hand to bring up a status screen in front of him.</p>

<p>[The ability has a permanent passive effect. If an Awakened woman is added to your harem slot, she will automatically level up whenever you level up. This means any woman who is a whole Realm lower than you will automatically match your exact Realm after sleeping with you and being added to your harem slot]</p>

<p>[Think about it for a second. Didn’t Doris just tell you that she entered the Vanguard Realm the exact same day you and Nyx both left for South Korea? And what did you two do the night before you left? Both of you slept together!]</p>

<p>A massive wave of realization washed over Alex’s mind. The pieces of the puzzle were finally clicking together.</p>

<p>He remembered Doris’s sudden, explosive speed, a speed only Vanguard Realm Awakened could possess, before she then literally confirmed to him that she was now a Vanguard Realm.</p>

<p>[Exactly!]</p>

<p>The system chibi continued, nodding her head proudly.</p>

<p>[Even your hot assassin, Celine, should now be in the Vanguard Realm because of this. And if you ever enter the B Rank which should be soon, both Doris and Celine, who are currently sitting in your harem slots, will automatically enter the Ascendant Realm right along with you. But since Jade isn’t actually in your harem slot yet, her break through right now is just a one time thing from the sex you two just shared earlier. But if you later decide to add her to your harem slot, she will also automatically enter any new Realm that matches your Rank in the future]</p>

<p>The chibi did a little spin in the air, looking incredibly pleased with herself.</p>

<p>[So, infinite Skill Absorption doesn’t only benefit you by copying their cool abilities, but it also allows them to completely sync with your power and reach the exact same Realm as you. Isn’t it totally awesome or what?]</p>

<p>Alex took a slow, deep breath to calm his racing heart. The implications of all this were absolutely massive. He was basically a walking, talking cheat code for any Awakened woman who joined his side.</p>

<p>’So let me get this straight,’ he carefully said in his mind, wanting to be absolutely sure that he wasn’t misinterpreting anything. ’Any Awakened woman I sleep with who is in a lower Realm than me will automatically reach the same Realm. And if I add the said woman to my harem slot, then whenever I level up, they automatically level up to match the exact same Realm as me?’</p>

<p>The system nodded rapidly, with her large anime-like eyes sparkling brightly.</p>

<p>[Uhh, yeah! I’m pretty sure I just made it completely clear. You’re basically a cultivation booster for women]</p>

<p>Alex couldn’t help but smile slightly. It was a really good thing. A spectacular thing, actually. Having his women instantly match his strength meant he wouldn’t have to constantly worry about them being left behind or become easy targets for his enemies.</p>

<p>But then, his smile slowly faded, and a small frown formed on his face as he remembered the rest of his women. He thought about Nyx, Sofia, and Olivia, who were currently occupying his harem slots. They were normal women, not Awakened.</p>

<p>He had been with Nyx for some time now, and had even slept with her after he got the Infinite Skill Absorption, she hadn’t awakened any magical powers.</p>

<p>He turned his attention back to the chibi, his mental voice turning a bit solemn.</p>

<p>’So I’m guessing the Infinite Skill Absorption won’t help ordinary women, even if they are in my harem slot?’</p>

<p>The system chibi’s expression softened, and she shrugged her shoulders with a look of pity.</p>

<p>[Sadly, that’s true. The ability only works on existing primordial essence . It can’t just create power out of nothing for an ordinary human body. It only amplifies what is already there]</p>

<p>Alex let out a silent sigh in his mind, feeling a deep sense of regret that this incredible passive effect couldn’t affect his ordinary wifeys.</p>

<p>He wanted them to be safe and strong more than anyone else. But before he could dwell on the melancholy thought, a sharp ding sound echoed in his head.</p>

<p>The chibi suddenly jumped up, clapping her hands.</p>

<p>The moment the system messages finished rolling, a sudden rush of warm energy flowed through his body, instantly clearing away the remaining fatigue from his long night. Ah. The stat boost was really good.</p>

<p>Right then, the small gust of wind in the room finally began to die down. He watched as the sparkling blue electricity in her eyes flashed one last time before disappearing as her ocean blue eyes came into existence.</p>

<p>The breakthrough was now officially over.</p>

<p>Jade stood perfectly still for a moment. She clenched her fist, staring down at her palm in surprise as she felt the massive pool of essence far above that of an Early Realm Awakened, now circulating through her core.</p>

<p>Her mind was reeling from how fast and seamless the process had been, but true to her nature, she quickly suppressed her shock. Her beautiful face turned back to its usually indifferent expression that would probably say don’t talk to me.</p>

<p>Doris looked at her with her large eyes wide with amazement before she let out a bright, cheerful smile. She walked over and smiled slightly.</p>

<p>&quot;Wow, welcome to the club, Jade! Now you’re also a Vanguard Realm Awakened. I guess the training tonight really paid off for you.&quot;</p>

<p>Jade gave a slow nod of her head, and said with her voice calm and steady.</p>

<p>&quot;Yes. It seems I finally broke through the barrier.&quot; She then shifted her ocean blue eyes toward Alex, a strange, unreadable glint passing through them. &quot;Can I spar with Alex after you’re done sparring with him? I want to test my new limits.&quot;</p>

<p>Doris smiled, but it wasn’t her usual kind of smile. This one was a slightly mischievous smirk as she glanced at Alex.</p>

<p>&quot;Sure, you can have him right after me.&quot;</p>

<p>Alex couldn’t resist the urge to sigh. Why did he feel like everyone always wanted to fight him lately?</p>

<p>&quot;Alright, let’s get to it then. Just make sure to control your strength so that we don’t end up destroying this entire building.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;Oh no worry, I’ll make sure to control my strength, but I’ll still make sure to beat you just like how I did some while back.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;Oh let’s see about that.&quot;</p>

<p>Without wasting another second, Alex and Doris resumed their spar. To keep the facility from being completely demolished, they mutually agreed not to use their full strength and abilities just yet.</p>

<p>Even without their full strength and abilities, the fight was nothing short of brutal. Alex completely dominated the exchange from the very beginning.</p>

<p>Thanks to all the hard earned stats boost, he was significantly stronger and faster than any vanguard Realm Awakened in terms of physical prowess.</p>

<p>Doris found herself struggling heavily within the first few exchanges and had accidentally caused tiny craters around them. Every time her fist managed to collide with Alex, she felt like she was striking a solid brick wall.</p>

<p>Her baby face contorted with effort as she ducked under a sweeping kick that literally tore through the air with a loud whistle like sound, or something close to that.</p>

<p>Loud, heavy thuds resounded through the training room as they traded blows. And small craters and cracks quickly formed on the ground from the sheer force of their footwork and impacts.</p>

<p>Doris leaped backward, her breath already turning shallow as she noted in her mind how much of a freakish monster Alex actually was. No way a normal Vanguard Realm should possess this kind of raw overwhelming physical suppression.</p>

<p>Suddenly, Doris slid to a halt near the edge of the room. She wiped a stray bead of sweat from her forehead and looked at him with a fiery, competitive spark in her eyes.</p>

<p>&quot;How about we crank things up a bit,&quot; Doris suggested, her small lips curving into a sharp grin. &quot;And actually use our ability?’</p>

<p>Alex stopped his advance, lowering his hands slightly as a confident smirk played on his lips. He wanted to see how far her ability had improved in Vanguard Realm.</p>

<p>&quot;Sure. Show me what you’ve got.&quot;</p>
