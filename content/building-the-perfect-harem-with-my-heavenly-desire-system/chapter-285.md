<p>He had to get that stone at all costs. A stone like that was something his son needed. Even if the bidding climbed higher than expected, he was prepared to spend as much as he could to secure it.</p>

<p>Before he could raise his paddle, however, a mocking voice he knew all too well like the back of his hand, came from the other side of the room.</p>

<p>&quot;Well, aren’t you being a little stingy, Old Carter? Bidding that much for a stone as rare as this. Aren’t you afraid of being called cheap. I bid six million dollars!&quot;</p>

<p>The middle-aged man who had made the previous offer turned sharply toward the speaker. And his expression immediately darkened when he saw the person, Declan that shameless old dog! He didn’t even know when his fingers began tightening around the bidding paddle. He didn’t even need this stone!</p>

<p>&quot;Declan You! You shameless old dog!&quot;</p>

<p>The other man only laughed, looking entirely pleased with himself. It was obvious that the two had some sort of history with each other, and the way they glared at each other suggested that they were not on good terms with each other.</p>

<p>Old Carter’s face turned red. How could he not recognize what this old fogey was doing! So he raised his paddle again and shouted, &quot;Seven million!&quot;</p>

<p>He came here intending to obtain that stone for his son, who so happens to possess a fire attribute ability. So there was no way he was going to let this irritating fellow snatch it away simply to embarrass him.</p>

<p>Just then, another voice rang out.</p>

<p>&quot;I bid ten million dollars.&quot;</p>

<p>It was a mature woman’s voice, rich with charm and authority that made one know that the owner of this voice was no simple person. Even though the words were not particularly loud, they still carried clearly across the auction room, making almost everyone turned toward the source.</p>

<p>There, a beautiful mature looking woman, about forty or so, sat several rows away, dressed in an elegant evening gown that complemented her beauty.</p>

<p>&quot;As expected from Lady Rosé of the Montgomery Family, even Old Carter from the Carter family and Patriarch Declan from the Adams family can’t be put in her eyes,&quot; another guest replied quietly.</p>

<p>As Alex later found out from a man who was explaining about it to his mistress. The Montgomery Family was also one of the top five powerful families in Washington, on the same par with the Sinclair family. And that Lady Rosé’s was also known as the undisputed Matriarch of the Montgomery Family, who rumors also had was secretly the mistress to a main family member of one of the two most powerful families in America, House of Blumonté. That’s why no family dares mess with them.</p>

<p>Even Alex was shocked after hearing such news! But he didn’t dwell much on it.</p>

<p>However, the competition did not stop there. A few guests still wanted the stone, and the price continued climbing from Twelve million! To Fifteen million! To even Eighteen million!</p>

<p>The bids came one after another. Before long, the opening item had attracted far more attention than anyone might have ever thought expected.</p>

<p>Meanwhile Alex just watched the entire proceedings with interest. He had initially thought the stone would sell for a little more than its starting price, but the competition was becoming surprisingly fierce.</p>

<p>The price rose to twenty-five million, then thirty million, and finally forty million. The people still bidding were clearly wealthy enough to spend such sums without worrying about their everyday expenses.</p>

<p>In the time it took to burn a candle, the price had reached an astonishing forty-three million dollars. It was at that time that Alex finally made his move and lifted his paddle.</p>

<p>&quot;I bid fifty million dollars.&quot;</p>

<p>For what felt like a brief moment, the entire room fell silent, with several guests turning toward him to see who made such a bid, while others looked at one another in surprise. Even Celine glanced sideways at him, wondering whether he had suddenly lost his mind.</p>

<p>Fifty million dollars for a single elemental stone was an enormous offer. Although the stone was rare and all, its usefulness was after all limited. One had to know that only Awakened with fire-related abilities could properly benefit from it. For everyone else, it was little more than an expensive collectible. Based on its quality and likely improvement, its reasonable market value should have been around ten million dollars.</p>

<p>It was already surprising that the bidding had climbed so high. Now someone had offered five times that amount.</p>

<p>The guests were not necessarily unable to afford fifty million. For many of them, the sum was hardly worth mentioning. However, this was only the first item, and the auction catalogue contained many other treasures. Spending too much on an item with such a narrow use could leave them with fewer resources when something truly exceptional appeared.</p>

<p>Old Carter stared at Alex as though he wanted to burn a hole through him.</p>

<p>He had wanted the stone for his son, and God knows he’d been willing to spend a substantial amount to obtain it. But he was not foolish enough to keep raising the price without considering what else might appear tonight. There were still several important items waiting to be presented, and he needed to preserve his funds.</p>

<p>In the end, he could only grit his teeth and give up.</p>

<p>However, before looking away, he memorized Alex’s face. He might not be able to compete for the stone today, but he certainly would not forget the young man who had ruined his plans.</p>

<p>He’d just let the young man keep the stone for a while before sending people to collect it later, after first investigating the young man’s background just in case. It was good to be cautious at times.</p>

<p>Lady Rosé also studied Alex for a moment, before shifting to Celine who was sitting beside him. Her expression showed curiosity after her Ascendant Realm bodyguard whispered something to her, but she did not raise her paddle again.</p>
