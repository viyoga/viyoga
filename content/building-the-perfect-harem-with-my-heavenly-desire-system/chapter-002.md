<p>[The heavenly desire system initializing]</p>

<p>Suddenly, Alex gasped for air as his eyes flew open. He pushed himself from the bed too fast and almost fell, but quickly caught himself against the wall. Then he reached for the back of his head and froze when he didn’t feel any trace of blood.</p>

<p>With a confused expression he looked around and noticed the strangely familiar small room with old light blue curtains, and the squeaky ceiling fan, tracing lazy circles in the air.</p>

<p>That’s when it suddenly clicked. He knew this place. It was his old college apartment. A place he hadn’t seen in over twenty four years. So why was he back then?</p>

<p>Still confused, Alex stumbled into the bathroom and flicked the light switch on, then stared at the mirror. A younger version of him stared back.</p>

<p>&quot;Is t-that me?&quot;</p>

<p>He leaned closer to the mirror and touched his face, before exclaiming.</p>

<p>&quot;Yes! I’m sure of it. That’s really me from twenty four years ago...&quot;</p>

<p>Alex heart hammered against his chest, as his mind raced with different thoughts.</p>

<p>&quot;What the hell is going on? Am I not suppose to be dead? So how then did I suddenly get transported to my old college apartment after hearing a strange voice&quot;</p>

<p>The air suddenly vibrated as the familiar female voice echoed in Alex head again. Then blue light suddenly flooded the bathroom mirror, as floating screens unfolded in front of his eyes.</p>

<p>[Congratulations Alex. The heavenly desire system has now been bound to you]</p>

<p>Alex quickly took a step back, and bumped his foot against the bathroom door in panic.</p>

<p>&quot;W-what is this? How do you know my name&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;Wait! I think I’ve seen this in a movie before.</p>

<p>Yes! You must be some kind of AI that wants to enslave humanity.&quot;</p>

<p>A female chibi suddenly materialized and facepalmed herself.</p>

<p>[Really? That’s the best you could come up with. An AI?]</p>

<p>[Well sorry to burst your bubble, but I’m not some stupid AI]</p>

<p>His mouth went dry, then he stole a few quick glances at the tiny chibi to make sure that what he was seeing was real. But just to be sure he opened his mouth to confirm whether it was real or just a hallucination.</p>

<p>The chibi spoke, as if it knew exactly what he was about to say.</p>

<p>[No Alex you’re not hallucinating. This is all real]</p>

<p>Alex ran a hand through his hair as he struggled to make sense of what was going on. A minute ago, he was killed by his bitch of a wife. Now a floating game screen was talking to him in his own bathroom. If he were to tell anyone about this, they would probably think that he had lost his mind and send him to a psychiatric hospital.</p>

<p>[Anyways, welcome back to your prime, Alex]</p>

<p>[Your first mission will now be issued]</p>

<p>A loading bar suddenly appeared in mid air. And his eyes followed it without blinking.</p>

<p>25%... 46%... 72%... 99%...</p>

<p>Then it stopped, and a text appeared, causing Alex eyes to widen.</p>

<p>&quot;...You want me to do what?!&quot;</p>

<p>The chibi answered instantly with a light, playful smile on its lips.</p>

<p>[Calm down Alex, your heart rate just sped up by 41%]</p>

<p>&quot;Well I think that’s pretty normal... Especially for a guy who just got resurrected and sent back in time into his younger body, then gains a floating system that tells him to...&quot;</p>

<p>He stopped talking as his gaze drifted back to the mission text, causing heat to creep up his face as his thoughts stalled somewhere between shock and disbelief.</p>

<p>The chibi’s tone turned teasing.</p>

<p>[Yes, host]</p>

<p>[I am asking you to do exactly that]</p>

<p>&quot;I can’t do that. That’s insane, that’s immoral. That’s...&quot;</p>

<p>Alex stopped talking as the memory of Emily’s betrayal flashed through his mind. Then he nodded his head to show that he agreed. The chibi excitedly clapped her hand as she spun around him with joy.</p>

<p>[Good, good]</p>

<p>[So now I’ll just give you a quick run up. The power you need to level up is called Desire Energy]</p>

<p>[Desire Energy is produced when a woman feels emotions like love, lust, obsession, jealousy and crushes towards you]</p>

<p>[So in total the stronger the emotion, the more energy you collect]</p>

<p>Finally understanding what the system said, he nodded seriously. Then realizing something, he stared at the chibi and said with a chuckle.</p>

<p>&quot;So that basically means your a pervert system&quot;</p>

<p>The chibi froze for a second, then her tiny face scrunched up in offense.</p>

<p>[No!]</p>

<p>She crossed her arms and turned her head away with a huff before peeking back at him through narrowed eyes.</p>

<p>[I am a high growth assistant. And if what I said makes me a pervert in your primitive forty five years old brain, then fine. I’m a pervert who can keep you from dying like an idiot]</p>

<p>Silence instantly flooded the room as Alex expression changed. He couldn’t believe that he just got roasted by a tiny being. Exhaling slowly he asked the question that had been on his mind.</p>

<p>&quot;So why me? It’s not like I’m the most special person in the world&quot;</p>

<p>For the first time, the chibi stopped teasing and looked at him with a calm expression as it’s voice grew almost human like.</p>

<p>[You died with extreme despair after losing your family, work and life]</p>

<p>[But your desire did not die]</p>

<p>[You qualified]</p>

<p>&quot;Qualified for what exactly? He asked with a raised eyebrow.</p>

<p>[Revenge, rebirth, power and fun]</p>

<p>&quot;Revenge huh&quot; Alex said repeating it under his breath. His thought drifted back to the past where he was a good person but still got fired by his boss. And how even though he loved Emily with his entire heart he still got betrayed.</p>

<p>Making up his mind he said in a firm voice.</p>

<p>&quot;I agree. I don’t want to be weak anymore. I want to be someone the world will look at with respect, and I don’t care what I have to do to gain that respect. I will do it, even if it means I have to become shameless and cruel&quot;</p>

<p>[Good]</p>

<p>A panel instantly opened.</p>

<p>[System tutorial — begin]</p>

<p>• You are twenty-one again.</p>

<p>• Time rewinded twenty-four years into the past.</p>

<p>• Only your soul remembers.</p>

<p>• Body reset to base level.</p>

<p>• Power gained through Desire Energy and System Tasks</p>

<p>&quot;What about them? I mean Emily and Zack.&quot;</p>

<p>[Well they’re also young again. The rewind reset everything. So they’ve not betrayed you yet]</p>

<p>&quot;So they don’t even know what they’ll become?&quot; He said with a dry, bitter edge in his voice</p>

<p>[Only you remember]</p>

<p>...Then Alex expression quickly turned sad as he turned his gaze to the ceiling and thought of his children. Since he had been sent back to the past, that meant they did not exist yet.</p>

<p>Even though they weren’t really his biological children, They had still called him dad. That made Alex unable to hate them, but also unable to love them either.</p>

<p>He let out a sigh and forced his mind away from that topic.</p>

<p>A new window suddenly appeared.</p>

<p>[Stat Window — Host]</p>

<p>Name: Alex Walker</p>

<p>Age: 21</p>

<p>Body Condition: Weak adult male</p>

<p>Charm: Below average</p>

<p>Vitality: Low</p>

<p>Libido: Dormant</p>

<p>Desire Energy: 0</p>

<p>Desire Points: 0</p>

<p>System Rank: F (Locked)</p>

<p>Skills: None</p>

<p>Titles: None</p>

<p>Harem Slots: 0/∞</p>

<p>&quot;...Hey! Why is my charm so low?&quot;</p>

<p>[Reality]</p>

<p>&quot;...&quot;</p>

<p>Another message appeared.</p>

<p>[Beginner gift pack unlocked]</p>

<p>[Congratulations Alex, you have obtained: Aura of Seduction]</p>

<p>Immediately, Alex felt a strange, warm current flowing through his body, making him feel extremely comfortable, like taking a warm shower in winter.</p>

<p>After the sensation ended he lifted his hands, expecting them to glow or something, but they just remained the same.</p>

<p>&quot;What did you just do to me?&quot; He asked with a bewildered expression.</p>

<p>The chibi purred while rubbing her cute, puffy cheeks.</p>

<p>[Oh nothing dangerous. You’re now very difficult to ignore]</p>

<p>[Active Skill: Aura of Seduction (Lv.1)]</p>

<p>• Women become subtly aware of you</p>

<p>• Attraction grows faster at close range</p>

<p>• Physical contact amplifies effect</p>

<p>• Strong-willed women resist longer</p>

<p>• Lonely or insecure targets fall faster</p>

<p>Alex brows knit together as he rubbed his chin while thinking for a moment.</p>

<p>&quot;So that means the closer I get, the faster woman are affected?&quot;</p>

<p>[Correct]</p>

<p>&quot;And if I touch them...?&quot;</p>

<p>[Even faster]</p>

<p>[Hope you remember your first mission? Just in case you’ve forgotten, I will show you again]</p>

<p>A translucent screen popped up.</p>

<p>[•Mission: You have 24 hours to create a genuine romantic or lustful desire in at least one woman.</p>

<p>•Reward: +50 Desire Energy</p>

<p>+150 Desire points</p>

<p>•Bonus Reward: full appearance upgrade</p>

<p>•Failure: System ridicule and emotional embarrassment]</p>

<p>Alex stared at the words for a long second.</p>

<p>&quot;Wait, so you are going to punish me with humiliation if I fail?&quot;</p>

<p>[Yes, I have already prepared several insults and jokes]</p>

<p>He clicked his tongue.&quot;Sadistic system.&quot;</p>

<p>[Why thank you]</p>

<p>A digital clock appeared. 24:00:00</p>

<p>It began ticking down...23:59:59... 23:59:58...</p>

<p>Alex hurriedly stood up from the bed and began pacing around the room, desperately thinking of who to try and seduce first. He needed to act, and he needed to act fast, but rushing in blindly would only get him into trouble.</p>

<p>He ran a hand through his hair, then stopped near the window, staring at nothing in particular as he searched his memory for a name. Someone vulnerable, someone who could actually work. But who?</p>

<p>Then a familiar name suddenly flashed into his mind causing him to slowly let out a smirk.</p>

<p>Of course. The answer had been staring him in the face all along.</p>

<p>’Olivia Carter—Zack’s hot, lonely mom.’</p>
