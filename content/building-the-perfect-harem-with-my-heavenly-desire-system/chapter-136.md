<p>Alex looked surprised for a second, not expecting her to be so stubborn.</p>

<p>&quot;Are you really sure about that?&quot;</p>

<p>Alex looked at the stubborn woman for several seconds, before eventually, he shrugged.</p>

<p>&quot;Alright then.&quot;</p>

<p>The exact moment those words left his mouth, Jade suddenly made her move. She exploded forward without any warning. Then a perfectly precise roundhouse kick sliced through the air toward his head.</p>

<p>Alex just calmly leaned his head backward, and watched as the insane kick, which was most definitely strong enough to easily break through a solid wall, passed inches away from his face.</p>

<p>But Jade didn’t stop there. The exact instant her foot touched the ground, she seamlessly flowed into another unexpected attack at a tricky angle, aiming right for his neck.</p>

<p>Alex waited until it was just about to touch him, and grabbed her leg. He could have easily dodged it like the first, by either bending his head low, or just stepping backward, but he needed that small contact to increase the damn Aura’s effect, since the desire meter was moving way too slow for his liking.</p>

<p>Just as his grip was a little bit loose, because he wanted to release her leg, Jade pulled her leg free and pushed herself backward, before suddenly rushing toward him again.</p>

<p>In short, she launched various kinds of attacks that, if combined with her void step ability, would make her a formidable threat to any other Early Realm Awakened out there, maybe unless the person had the ability to see seconds into the future like Doris.</p>

<p>Jade’s movements were incredibly fluid like flowing water, yet at the same time they carried tons of force, which was enough to smash clean through a two or maybe three meter boulder.</p>

<p>That was roughly the size of a car for crying out loud!</p>

<p>But Alex moved smoothly, dodging every single strike thrown at him with minimal effort. The more the fight went on the more Alex realized that most techniques or skills were truly useless when the Realm of the opponent was higher</p>

<p>That is, unless a person uses cheap shots like poison or maybe temporarily blinding the eye with sand, like how he had done to that monster in the training realm.</p>

<p>Ah! The training realm. He would definitely go there one of these days, to practice and maybe even perfect all the abilities he would have copied by that time. Yeah. He’ll definitely do that.</p>

<p>As they kept moving across the spacious room at remarkable speed, Alex made sure to occasionally let his hands make direct contact with her body.</p>

<p>Whenever he blocked a punch because he was too lazy to continue dodging, he would redirect her arm, or step past her side, making his fingers brush against her skin or her waist. Just to speed the Aura’s effect up.</p>

<p>Alex took a quick step back and glanced at the less than subtle Desire Meter in his mind.</p>

<p>[Desire Meter]</p>

<p>• Lust: 46%</p>

<p>• Attraction: 51%</p>

<p>• Resistance: 17%</p>

<p>All because what was previously a small strange heat suddenly increased and began pooling around her inner thighs, making her entire body feel weak and heavy.</p>

<p>And apart from that, the overwhelming feeling of wanting to throw herself straight into the delicious looking man in front of her increased until it was beginning to drive her crazy.</p>

<p>Jade didn’t exactly understand what this strange feeling was, since she was known as the school ice goddess who always liked to stay alone, completely away from men and relationships. So she had never once in her entire life felt anything like this before.</p>

<p>Alex noticing her completely confused expression and the way her body was trembling, suppressed the satisfied smirk that was making its way to his face.</p>

<p>[+5 Desire Energy Gained]</p>

<p>• Lust: 49%... 54%... 59%...</p>
