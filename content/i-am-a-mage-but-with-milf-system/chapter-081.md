<p>While Julian was undergoing his intense breakthrough in the sea of consciousness, the outside world was experiencing a phenomenon of apocalyptic proportions. The sky that was once calm was suddenly engulfed by a surge of violent lightning storms.</p>

<p>Bolts of electricity crackled across the heavens illuminating the world below with flashes of light so intense they seemed to split the sky in half. Thunder roared like a beast shaking the ground beneath and the people whether in the Ares Kingdom or far beyond all looked up in terror at the unfolding spectacle.</p>

<p>This wasn’t confined to one region. Across continents, from bustling cities to remote villages. The sheer intensity of the lightning made it appear as though the heavens themselves were in revolt. Entire kingdoms were paralyzed in awe and fear as the once clear skies turned into a chaotic dance of storm and fury.</p>

<p>Scholars, mages and kings alike began to speculate about the cause as this was no ordinary storm. It seemed to defy the natural order, an omen that hinted at something monumental happening beyond human understanding.</p>

<p>In the Ares Kingdom where Julian’s family and allies resided, the atmosphere was especially tense. People knelt in prayer fearing that the gods were angry while others rushed to secure their homes from the overwhelming forces of nature.</p>

<p>The Easvil estate itself became a focal point of whispers as those who knew Julian wondered if this phenomenon was tied to him. The ground trembled while the air crackled with power and though no one could see Julian’s transformation firsthand they all felt the shift in the world.</p>

<p>The royal family and the dukes across the Ares Kingdom were gripped by fear as they witnessed the cataclysmic event unfolding in the sky.</p>

<p>In the royal palace, the king and queen stood at the highest balcony as they stared at the endless flashes of lightning with their pale faces. Advisors and mages were summoned in haste but none could offer a satisfactory explanation for the overwhelming storm that seemed to defy all magical understanding.</p>

<p>Even the most powerful mages of the royal court felt helpless unable to trace the origin of such an intense display of elemental fury.</p>

<p>In the estates of the noble dukes, similar scenes of panic were unfolding. Duke Alden Easvil and Regina who were already tense after Julian’s breakthrough sensed that this was not a natural storm. Their worry for their son deepened suspecting that he might somehow be at the center of this unprecedented event.</p>

<p>Other dukes like the Duke of Norish and the Duke of Ethwer were equally shaken. Each wondered if this was a divine warning or some great power being unleashed that they could not comprehend. No one dared to step outside as the crackling air and roaring thunder seemed like a manifestation of nature’s wrath.</p>

<p>Despite their status and influence the royal family and the dukes felt powerless in the face of the unknown. Fear spread among their ranks as they pondered whether this storm marked the end of an era or the beginning of something far more dangerous. Whispers of ancient prophecies and legends filled the halls but no one could predict what would come next or if the kingdom itself was at risk.</p>

<p>As the storm raged outside, Augustus, Gregoria, Regina, Alden, Eva, Eleanor and the other key members of the Easvil family gathered in Julian’s room. They had tense expressions filled with anticipation.</p>

<p>They had rushed there sensing something extraordinary happening, but Julian was nowhere to be seen. The room was eerily quiet except for the distant rumble of thunder that seemed to echo their own anxiety.</p>

<p>Just as Regina was about to voice her concern, a sudden ripple of energy passed through the room and in the blink of an eye, a figure materialized in front of them.</p>

<p>There stood Julian, though he was almost unrecognizable. His once blonde hair had transformed into flowing, crackling white strands, cascading down his back like threads of lightning. His skin was glowing faintly with a silver glow and he had no signs of clothing.</p>

<p>It didn’t seem to matter as the aura of power he exuded made even his nudity feel like a display of the raw and uncontainable energy coursing through him. His eyes were now pure white with lightning as they scanned the room as if they were seeing beyond the material world.</p>

<p>The family stood in stunned silence their disbelief evident. Augustus, the powerful Grand Duke was the first to recognize what had happened.</p>

<p>“Julian…” he whispered, his voice mixed of both awe and concern.</p>

<p>Regina’s eyes filled with a mix of shock and maternal worry while Alden’s expression darkened trying to comprehend the magnitude of what his son had become. Eva and Eleanor exchanged wide-eyed glances realizing that the brother they once knew had ascended to something far beyond them.</p>

<p>Julian who was still glowing with the remnants of his breakthrough stood tall as the power of lightning itself embodied within him.</p>

<p>Julian stood in awe of his own body marveling at the surge of power that flowed through him. The authority of lightning imbued in every fiber of his being.</p>

<p>With a sudden determination, he took to the air as he soared out of his room and into the open sky above the castle. The air crackled around him and his family hurriedly followed, their hearts racing with a mix of excitement and trepidation. As they gathered outside, they looked on in disbelief at the spectacle unfolding before them.</p>

<p>Raising his finger toward the tumultuous sky, Julian seemed to command the very forces of nature. His family watched with wide eye unable to comprehend the magnitude of his actions.</p>

<p>Alden trying to mask his growing concern turned to Augustus and asked, “Father what is he doing?”</p>

<p>The Grand Duke’s brow furrowed as he replied, “I don’t know.”</p>

<p>Just as confusion rippled through the crowd, the atmosphere shifted dramatically.</p>

<p>To everyone’s astonishment, the once violent storm began to calm, the swirling clouds dissipating as if they were yielding to Julian’s silent command. The ominous thunder that had filled the air with dread now faded into a gentle rumble replaced by a serene stillness.</p>

<p>It was as though the sky itself was listening to Julian’s unspoken order responding to the authority he had recently acquired. The Easvil family exchanged stunned glances as realization dawned upon them that their son had not only gained power but had become a being capable of altering the very fabric of their world.</p>

<p>Julian’s heart swelled with pride as he felt the connection between himself and the lightning knowing that he had truly become a force to be reckoned with.</p>
