<p>After some time, Julian and Eleanor made their way to the grand dining hall for breakfast. The room was filled with the aroma of mouthwatering dishes.</p>

<p>As they approached the table, Julian immediately noticed the familiar figures of his family seated together, engaged in light conversation.</p>

<p>His eyes swept across the table and locked onto a striking figure beside Regina. His heartbeat quickened.</p>

<p>Aunty Alice, he thought instantly recognizing her.</p>

<p>Her beauty rivaled even that of his mother, Regina, with the same serene grace and captivating presence.</p>

<p>However, unlike the more formal attire of other female nobles, she wore a stunning kimono.</p>

<p>It was a beautiful blend of green and blue, which symbolized her status as a healer.</p>

<p>She had long, flowing black hair and mesmerizing green eyes that carried both kindness and strength.</p>

<p>Despite her composed grace, the fabric of her kimono struggled to contain her big, mature breasts. It only added to her allure and charm.</p>

<p>As Julian and Eleanor approached, all eyes turned toward them. The soft murmur of conversation stopped, and it was Alden who broke the silence first.</p>

<p>“Julian, you are back,” he said with a nod, his eyes lighting up.</p>

<p>Julian bowed respectfully. “Yes, Father. I hope you have been well.”</p>

<p>Alden nodded in acknowledgment, smile forming on his face.</p>

<p>Julian then turned his attention to Regina. “Mother, I hope you have been well too.”</p>

<p>Regina nodded as well, but she could not hide the blush that appeared on her face.</p>

<p>Finally, Julian’s gaze settled on Alice. He offered a charming smile. “Aunty Alice, you have arrived.”</p>

<p>Alice’s eyes softened, a gentle smile curving her lips. “Julian, it is good to see you.” Her voice was smooth, and it carried a hint of affection.</p>

<p>“You have grown so much since I last saw you. And from what I hear, you have been quite busy.”</p>

<p>Julian chuckled softly. “You can say that,” he replied confidently.</p>

<p>“I have been exploring here and there, seeing what the world has to offer.”</p>

<p>Alice laughed, “Ah, the adventurous spirit of youth. It reminds me of our own adventure.”</p>

<p>She turned her gaze to Alden, her eyes sparkling with mischief. “Do you remember how we used to sneak out of the castle to explore the duchy? Those were the days.”</p>

<p>Alden, who rarely opened himself, let out a loud laugh, “Oh, I remember it well. Especially when Father caught us and hung me upside down in the garden for half a day. That was quite the lesson.”</p>

<p>Laughter erupted around the table, filling the grand dining hall with warmth. The memories shared by Alden and Alice painted a vivid picture of the strong bonds between them.</p>

<p>As the laughter subsided, Alice turned toward Regina with genuine admiration.</p>

<p>“Regina, you are truly fortunate. To have given birth to such a powerful son, it’s no small feat.”</p>

<p>Regina smiled and simply nodded in response. She had secrets that only she knew, and she would take them to her grave.</p>

<p>Julian and Eleanor settled into their seats, and he turned his attention to Alice with a curious smile. “Aunty, how’s Grandfather doing?” he asked.</p>

<p>Alice nodded reassuringly. “He’s recovering well,” she said. “He pushed his cultivation too far and faced a backlash, but with time and rest, he will heal naturally.”</p>

<p>Julian’s eyes flicked toward Gregoria. “Thank the heavens for that,” he said with a teasing smile playing at the corners of his lips.</p>

<p>Gregoria shifted slightly in her chair, her challenge with Julian flashing in her mind. She looked away from him but could still feel the weight of his gaze.</p>

<p>With that, the breakfast continued, and they all began feasting on the delicacies laid before them. After some light conversation, the breakfast finished, and the family dispersed, each returning to their respective tasks.</p>

<p>Julian stepped out into the garden. The scent of blooming flowers and the soft rustling of leaves greeted him as he walked through the peaceful atmosphere.</p>

<p>He took a deep breath and found a spot beneath a tall tree with red fruits hanging from its branches. He sat down and leaned against the trunk. He closed his eyes and concentrated on the mana around him.</p>

<p>The mana got sucked into his body, nourishing and easing his fatigue.</p>

<p>As he concentrated further, the familiar sensation washed over him, and the garden around him transformed into swirling pixels.</p>

<p>Julian’s breathing slowed as he adjusted to the sight before him. Let’s try unraveling more of this mystery, he thought to himself.</p>

<p>He focused harder, concentrating on the overwhelming numbers of pixels that represented seven different elements. Slowly, he directed his attention, trying to isolate a single element.</p>

<p>After a moment, he narrowed his awareness until only the lightning element remained.</p>

<p>The sight that greeted him was the same as before. The purple pixels of the lighting elements were tightly packed in a web-like pattern, with some small gaps between them.</p>

<p>He shifted his focus further, attempting to isolate a single pixel.</p>

<p>As time passed on, Julian’s mind raced with different strategies, yet no matter how intensely he concentrated, the single pixel refused to come into focus.</p>

<p>It was as if it mocked him, remaining just out of reach.</p>

<p>The strain on his mind grew unbearable, a sharp pain rushing through his body as he tried to zoom in further.</p>

<p>Just as he was on the verge of giving up, a deafening roar echoed in his mind—a sound like a drumbeat. His heart raced, and his entire body tensed in fear.</p>

<p>With a jolt, he snapped out of the trance. His body was drenched in sweat, and blood dripped freely from his eyes and nose.</p>

<p>This time, he listened closely to the sound, searching for its source. It was faint at first, but as he concentrated, it became clearer. The sound was coming from the pixels themselves.</p>

<p>With caution, he moved toward the source of the roar. As he continued walking, something extraordinary happened—the strain on his mind began to lessen.</p>

<p>The sharp pain faded, and for the first time since he began his focus, he felt a wave of relief wash over him.</p>

<p>What is going on? he thought in surprise, continuing towards the source. It’s like a path.</p>

<p>“This… this energy,” Julian whispered in awe.</p>
