<p>Kain stood in the doorway, his chest heaving as he caught his breath. The normally stoic man’s face was etched with a fear that Julian had never seen before.</p>

<p>“Rael, we need to leave. Now!” he barked, his voice hoarse with urgency. “Something… something impossible is happening out there!”</p>
