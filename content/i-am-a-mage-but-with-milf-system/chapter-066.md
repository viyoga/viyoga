<p>Julian took a deep breath, focusing his energy as he wielded the Cosmic Spear of Death firmly in his grip. He closed his eyes for a moment, channeling the surge of mana that flowed through him, feeling the electric energy building up within.</p>

<p>“Lightning Trident!” he called out, his voice echoing through the training grounds.</p>

<p>Although lightning trident was a spell and not a weapon, it had properties of weapon and acted like one.</p>

<p>As the words left his lips, the air crackled around him, and the familiar shimmering trident of lightning materialized beside him.</p>

<p>Sparks danced and crackled, illuminating the area with a brilliant light. The two weapons pulsed with power, resonating together as if they were meant to be united.</p>

<p>With unwavering determination, Julian aimed the spear forward, envisioning the moment when he would unleash this combination of cosmic and electrical might.</p>

<p>As he prepared to merge their powers, the atmosphere around him thickened with anticipation, and he felt the energy coursing through his body intensify, ready to be unleashed in a spectacular display of strength.</p>

<p>As the aura of the Lightning Trident drew nearer to the Cosmic Spear of Death, an unexpected surge of energy erupted between the two weapons.</p>

<p>Instead of merging, the opposing forces clashed violently, sending a shockwave rippling through Julian’s body. The raw, electric energy from the trident lashed out, burning his arm where he held the spear.</p>

<p>Julian winced, feeling the searing pain, but he quickly steadied himself, refusing to let the setback deter him.</p>

<p>He realized that the power within these two legendary weapons was too immense to simply combine without careful balance. The moment ignited a determination within him to master the balance between their energies rather than force them together in a reckless attempt.</p>

<p>With a fierce resolve, he took a step back, assessing the situation.</p>

<p>“I need to control this,” he muttered under his breath, strategizing how to harmonize the powers of the spear and the trident without succumbing to their chaotic clash.</p>

<p>The pain in his arm served as a painful reminder of the challenges he faced, but it also fueled his ambition to push beyond his limits.</p>

<p>Just then the system warned,” Be careful host, you might loose your life if you take on wrong step”</p>

<p>Julian paused, the system’s warning echoing in his mind.</p>

<p>“I know, System,” he replied, taking a deep breath to steady his nerves. “But I can’t let fear hold me back. This power has the potential to change everything.”</p>

<p>He looked at the Cosmic Spear of Death, its aura still shimmering with untapped energy, and the Lightning Trident, crackling with raw electricity. The stakes were high, but the reward could be even greater.</p>

<p>“I just need to find a way to control both,” he thought, determination flooding his veins.</p>

<p>“Thanks for the reminder,” he said with a wry smile. “But I’m not done yet.”</p>

<p>With renewed focus, he began to strategize how to fuse their powers effectively while keeping himself safe. Each attempt would require precision, and he was ready to face the challenge head-on.</p>

<p>Julian stepped back, contemplating the chaotic energy that surged between the Cosmic Spear of Death and the Lightning Trident.</p>

<p>The two weapons, each a formidable force had their own dominance. They both resisted his attempts to merge their powers. It was as if he were trying to combine two raging storms, both refusing to yield to the other.</p>

<p>“This isn’t just about strength; it’s about balance,” he mused, rubbing the burn on his arm. “I need to respect their individual powers while finding a way to harmonize them.”</p>

<p>He envisioned the unique properties of each weapon, the destructive force of the spear and the volatile energy of the lightning trident, both essential but opposing.</p>

<p>With a newfound sense of strategy, Julian decided to focus on understanding their natures rather than brute force.</p>

<p>“What if I approach this differently?” he thought.</p>

<p>Instead of trying to forcibly merge them, he would seek a way to connect their energies, allowing them to complement each other rather than clash.</p>

<p>“I need to be the conductor, not just the wielder.”</p>

<p>Taking a deep breath, he began to focus on channeling his mana more gently, hoping to find a middle ground where both powers could coexist.</p>

<p>“Rampaging Domain”, Julian called.</p>

<p>As Julian summoned the Rampaging Domain, the chaotic energy around him intensified, wrapping him in a cocoon of vibrant lightning.</p>

<p>He felt the elements respond to his will, creating an environment where the powers of the Cosmic Spear of Death and the Lightning Trident could be tempered.</p>

<p>The energy swirled around him, a wild dance of electricity and raw force, but this time it felt different, more controlled, more focused.</p>

<p>“Although it may not be much, I can suppress their dominance even if just a little,” he thought, a confident grin spreading across his face.</p>

<p>As he concentrated, the aura of the spear and the trident began to resonate with the energy of the domain, their fierce tendencies tempered by the surrounding chaos.</p>

<p>The initial resistance they had shown started to diminish, as if they were acknowledging the authority of the domain he had created.</p>

<p>With this newfound synergy, Julian could feel a connection forming between the spear and the trident. He gripped the spear tightly and attempted to merge their energies once more.</p>

<p>This time, the process felt smoother, as if the weapons were beginning to dance together rather than fight against one another.</p>

<p>The glowing light of the spear and the crackling electricity of the trident started to intertwine, creating a breathtaking display of power that filled the training grounds with a bright light</p>

<p>Julian’s heart raced with excitement as he realized he was on the verge of achieving something extraordinary.</p>
