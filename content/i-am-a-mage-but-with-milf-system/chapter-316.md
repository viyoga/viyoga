<p>Each bolt that hit the castle obliterated everything in its path—stone, metal, and ground—all disintegrated under the raw force of the lightning.</p>

<p>By the tenth strike, nothing remained. No walls, no towers, no ruins—only dust and scattered debris.</p>

<p>Raphael stood frozen, watching in complete awe as Julian effortlessly tore the castle apart with nothing but his will.</p>

<p>His mouth opened wide, unable to fully comprehend the magnitude of what he was witnessing.</p>

<p>This is beyond anything I have ever seen.</p>
