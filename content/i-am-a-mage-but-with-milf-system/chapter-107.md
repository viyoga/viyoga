<p>Julian approached Raphael, who was skillfully training with his spear in the training yard. The sound of metal clashing and the sight of Raphael’s focused expression made it clear he was eager for battle.</p>

<p>“You really are excited for war,” Julian remarked, watching his knight’s intense training.</p>

<p>“Yes, my lord,” Raphael replied, pausing for a moment to catch his breath. “This is what I’ve been waiting for. I’ve trained for years, preparing for a chance to fight the war. I want to protect you and show my loyalty on the battlefield.”</p>

<p>Julian nodded, appreciating Raphael’s dedication. “I have no doubt you’ll make us proud. Just remember, it’s not just about strength; strategy is key.”</p>

<p>Raphael smiled, determination shining in his eyes. “I will give it my all, my lord. I’ll be ready to follow your lead, no matter what comes our way.”</p>

<p>Julian felt his heart flutter as he searched the training camp for Rose and her group, but he couldn’t find anyone.</p>

<p>Raphael noticed Julian’s distracted demeanor and asked, “My lord, are you searching for someone?”</p>
