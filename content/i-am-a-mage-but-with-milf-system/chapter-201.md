<p>Guard One bowed as it spoke in a deep, robotic tone. “Understood.”</p>

<p>Julian couldn’t help but laugh at the response of the guardian. It was so mechanical, yet there was something oddly cute about it.</p>

<p>With a smile, Julian turned his focus back to the task at hand. “Harem Manifestation.” he called.</p>

<p>In an instant, a wave of energy enveloped the area, and the figures of Isabel, Eleanor, and Lisa materialized before him. The sudden change left them stunned. They looked around, their eyes wide with disbelief.</p>

<p>The landscape before them was breathtaking—fields of vibrant green, rivers sparkling under the sun, and towering hills in the distance.</p>

<p>However, the most striking feature was the magnificent castle at the center of it all. The sight felt like something out of a fairy tale, the kind they had read about as children but never imagined they would see in person.</p>

<p>They stood in awe, their minds racing to understand the scene they had been suddenly pulled into.</p>

<p>Eleanor blinked, her voice trembling slightly as she looked around. “Where are we?” she asked.</p>

<p>Lisa’s gaze locked onto the grand castle and spoke with awe. “This looks like heaven,” she murmured, her voice filled with wonder.</p>

<p>Julian chuckled at their reactions. He turned to Guard One who was standing beside him.</p>

<p>“Well, you better get busy,” Julian said with a smirk, before teleporting himself into the sky.</p>

<p>In an instant, Julian found himself under the purple sky, high above the stunning landscape. He closed his eyes for a moment, focusing his senses on the entire world.</p>

<p>He could feel everything around him, the flowing rivers, the whispering winds, the lush forests, the vibrant flowers, and the violent clash of lightning—but something stood out.</p>

<p>He sensed strange, unusual movement from the land below. A group of small, snake-like creatures crawled across the land. Their bodies were long, with scales shimmering faintly under the light.</p>

<p>As Julian increased his focus, the details of the creatures below became clearer. But what caught Julian’s attention were the horns on their heads that glowed with sparks of lightning.</p>

<p>Fascinating, Julian thought, intrigued by the presence of these elemental beings.</p>

<p>His focus then shifted to a small fox-like creature nearby. Its fur was scarlet that blazed with fire. It was as though the creature itself was born of flames.</p>

<p>Julian then turned his attention to the river. Beneath the water’s surface, he could sense something moving. They were the creatures of the water element.</p>

<p>Their bodies flowed with the current and their forms were sleek and agile, perfectly adapted to their aquatic environment.</p>

<p>As his senses expanded further, Julian noticed other creatures scattered across the land. Some embodied the seven elements while others seemed completely normal. He could feel the diversity of life growing in the world.</p>

<p>The balance between the creatures who could manipulate the elements and those who could not, intrigued him.</p>

<p>As Julian continued to wander his senses over the creatures, he came across an unusual pattern of the distribution of life. Although there are different creatures, he thought, their numbers are quite small. It’s as if the world is testing them.</p>

<p>Only those who could adapt and evolve would survive. The others, weaker or less capable of harnessing the elements, would eventually perish. The world though beautiful and filled with potential seemed cruel and unforgiving.</p>

<p>He pointed his hands towards the land below and the entire geography of the land began to shift and change. The creatures below, sensed the sudden change in their surroundings and panicked.</p>

<p>The rivers once calm and clear, were now filled with dense vegetation, transforming the environment into something more complex and challenging.</p>

<p>The lush greenery spread rapidly, changing the landscape into something both beautiful and wild.</p>

<p>With a concentrated effort, Julian manipulated the terrain further. Fire-element creatures were transported to an area filled with volcanic mountains, where the intense heat and constant eruptions would test their strength and endurance.</p>

<p>Wind-element creatures were sent to a region with constant tornadoes and powerful storms, providing a brutal environment for those who thrived on the element of wind.</p>

<p>The dark creatures found themselves in a land shrouded in eternal darkness, where the sun never rose and the air was thick with mystery and danger.</p>

<p>He smiled, watching the changes unfold. “Since I have now given you ideal places to live,” Julian said, “let’s see which of you can evolve and claim your own territory.”</p>

<p>Julian raised his hands again, filling the lands with abundant fruits, plants, and other items essential for the survival of the creatures. The world quickly transformed into a place where the creatures could feed and sustain themselves, at least for now.</p>

<p>This is just an initial method to help them satisfy their hunger, he thought, observing the creatures below. Soon, they will have to hunt each other and become self-sufficient.</p>

<p>The creatures soon began to adapt. Some ran wild on the new landscapes while some started feeding on the fruits. They all were excited and happy about the sudden change of the world around them.</p>

<p>Julian took a final glance at the transformed world before making his way toward the castle. He decided not to teleport this time, wanting to take in the surrounding beauty and enjoy the calm atmosphere.</p>

<p>He walked around the surroundings for a while, taking in the serene beauty of the world he had created. Once he had enough of the peaceful landscape, he teleported back to the castle.</p>
