<p>Julian immediately teleported to his world. The familiar surroundings of the Easvil castle walls was now replaced by the expansive and beautiful world that he had created.</p>

<p>His world was developing rapidly. The once unstable and lacking mana was now stabilizing. It was slowly being filled with all seven elements, forming a rich and balanced environment.</p>

<p>The creatures within his world had undergone trials of survival, and only a few had managed to endure. However, these survivors were multiplying at an astonishing rate, filling the vast expanse.</p>

<p>Meanwhile, Julian, who had just appeared out of nowhere, collapsed onto the grass. His body trembled violently as unbearable pain surged through him. It was so intense that he found himself wishing for a swift, merciful death.</p>

<p>The pain continued, and it felt as if his very organs were fighting to be released from the confines of his body.</p>

<p>The atmosphere of his world shifted dramatically. The vibrant and lively surroundings were suddenly replaced by a dark and gloomy chill that seemed to seep into the very air.</p>

<p>The mana within the world formed a chaotic vortexes. It was wild and untamed, as it rushed toward Julian’s trembling form with terrifying force. The creatures in Julian’s world sensed the sudden change and scattered in panic, their instincts telling them to flee from the surging chaos.</p>

<p>Julian who was trembling with pain on the ground, managed to shout, “Sys… temm… what… is happpening?”</p>

<p>“Host, this is the result of your continuous mana absorption and the use of the Lightning Milf Technique. The pleasure you experienced recently exceeded the technique’s handling capacity. As a result, the mana you obtained has become unstable and is attracting the wild mana from the outside world.”</p>

<p>(Lightning MILF Technique: Every time you have intercourse with a MILF, you can absorb their mana and make it your own)</p>

<p>Julian shouted in pain, the explanation doing little to ease his agony as the vortex of mana grew larger and more chaotic around him.</p>

<p>He gritted his teeth and forced himself upright, sitting in a lotus position. Each movement of his body was followed by a fresh wave of pain.</p>

<p>“Let me try… absorbing the mana,” he said, his voice barely audible over the loud vortex around him.</p>

<p>He closed his eyes and began to focus, summoning every ounce of willpower to ignore the pain swirling inside him. The violent mana surged into his body causing his internal organs to tear apart,</p>

<p>The overwhelming overflow threatened to break him apart from within.</p>

<p>Despite the heavy pain, his resolve didn’t waver. His body trembled uncontrollably, but his mind tried to find the solution that would quench his pain.</p>

<p>The chaotic mana pushed harder, and he did his best to try controlling the mana.</p>

<p>As Julian endured the torture, flashbacks of his past emerged in his mind. The memory of obtaining the Authority of Lightning played out vividly, where his body was caught in the similar, continuous torture.</p>

<p>Each surge of mana was powerful enough to shatter his body, only for it to heal itself moments later.</p>

<p>The regeneration was agonizingly slow, each recovery leaving him more exhausted but slightly stronger. His breath was ragged and his body was soaked in sweat.</p>

<p>The cycle continued and soon the landscape around him also began to shift and change. The vibrant and beautiful greenery that once symbolized the flourishing of his world began to fade.</p>

<p>The plants and vegetation withered, and the world slowly turned into a barren, desolate wasteland.</p>

<p>The creation of this world was the result of his power, and when his own energy fluctuated dangerously, threatening his very existence, the damage was also reflected to the very world.</p>

<p>The battle was not only internal, and now the vitality and well-being of his world depended upon his ability to create a miracle and change his fate.</p>

<p>Julian’s heart raced with every breath, What should I do? he thought.</p>

<p>If this internal chaos continues, my entire world will turn to ruin. I have to do something and fast.</p>

<p>Suddenly, a new idea began forming in his mind. Maybe I can use death and cosmic energy to to use and control this mana for my benefit.</p>

<p>He focused on the mana even more as it split into pixels. It was a sight of a chaotic mess with no clear pattern or rhythm.</p>

<p>The new pattern felt stable but Julian knew that while the mana was now under his control, it still retained its chaotic nature. Determined to find a solution, he turned inward and began channeling his cosmic energy.</p>

<p>A golden light enveloped him and radiated outwards in waves. As the cosmic energy surged through his body, it met the chaotic mana head-on.</p>

<p>The golden glow seemed to suppress the wild energy, taming it until the mana’s unpredictable rhythm no longer posed a deadly threat.</p>

<p>He immediately felt the effects of the cosmic energy stabilizing his internal state. His eyes narrowed as he observed the balance. The rate of destruction is now slower than the rate of healing.</p>

<p>Julian took a moment to catch his breath before channeling his death energy. Dark, ominous tendrils of energy began to flow from his body, wrapping around now-controlled but chaotic mana.</p>

<p>To his surprise, the death energy began to eat the chaotic mana effortlessly, swallowing it completely. After absorbing the mana, it retreated back into Julian.</p>

<p>Julian’s shock was visible as the mana absorbed by the death energy transformed into a nourishing force, providing him with the supply of calm mana.</p>

<p>His eyes flicked open with a mix of shock and curiosity sweeping over his face.</p>

<p>“What…?” he gasped. “This mana… it’s… nourishing. Far more than normal mana.”</p>
