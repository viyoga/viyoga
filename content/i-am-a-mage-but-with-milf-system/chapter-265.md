<p>The feast soon ended and Hanu eagerly guided Julian to his room.</p>

<p>“Big brother, this is my room,” he said with excitement, pointing to a small but cozy space. “You can sleep on the bed. I’ll take the floor.”</p>

<p>Julian chuckled, shaking his head. “No need, Hanu. I’ll sleep on the floor. The bed is yours.”</p>

<p>Despite Hanu’s protests, Julian insisted and settled onto the floor, which had been arranged with warm, soft fabric to provide some comfort. The room was quiet except for the faint sounds village outside.</p>

<p>As they lay in silence, the calm of the night was broken by Hanu’s low voice. “Big brother…” he said.</p>

<p>Julian opened his eyes in the darkness, turning slightly towars the boy. “Yes, Hanu?”</p>

<p>There was a pause for a moment, as if Hanu was carefully choosing his words. Then he asked softly, “Big brother, what is the purpose of life?”</p>

<p>The unexpected depth of the question startled Julian and for a moment, he was silent.</p>

<p>How could such a young boy ask something so profound? he thought.</p>

<p>As Julian lay on the floor, staring up at the ceiling, Hanu’s question lingered in the air. After a moment of silence, Julian turned his head toward the young boy.</p>

<p>“That’s not the question you should be asking, Hanu,” Julian said, his voice calm and thoughtful.</p>

<p>Hanu was surprised and he sat up slightly. “Why, Brother Julian? What is wrong with asking about life?”</p>

<p>Julian’s gaze turned distant, “The question you should be searching the answer for isn’t the purpose of life,” he explained, his tone serious, “but rather the purpose of your life.”</p>

<p>Hanu blinked, clearly trying to process the difference between the two. He frowned, unable to grasp Julian’s words.</p>

<p>“What’s the difference?” he asked. “I don’t understand.”</p>

<p>“The life that you lead is hugely different from others, Hanu,” Julian explained. “Your surroundings, your environment, your lifestyle, your thinking—everything differs from someone else’s. Not many can walk the path that others have walked, and they do not need to. Each of us has our own path, a unique journey that we must find and walk.”</p>

<p>“I’m still learning, just like you. But if I have to say, I think leading a life that welcomes me with a fine morning and soothes me with a fine night is enough.”</p>

<p>Hanu laid back, contemplating Julian’s words. He didn’t fully understand it completely, but the simplicity of Julian’s answer felt strangely profound.</p>

<p>Julian’s smile faded slightly, and his tone grew more serious. “Hanu,” he said, “don’t focus too much on how things are. Don’t live in the uncertain future, and don’t let your past haunt your present. The future is unknown, and the past is behind you. What matters the most is your present.”</p>

<p>Hanu listened intently, his expression softening as Julian’s words began to sink in. He hadn’t thought about it that way before.</p>

<p>“Now go to sleep, Hanu,” Julian said softly. “There is a fine morning waiting for all of us tomorrow.”</p>

<p>Hanu smiled, his heart lightened by Julian’s words. They felt unnaturally embracing and soothing, as if they wrapped him in a gentle comfort. He quickly prayed to the god of his tribe before closing his eyes.</p>

<p>“What do you mean?” he asked.</p>
