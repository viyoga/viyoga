<p>Julia stepped forward, her voice steady yet pleading. “Father, it’s okay,” she said.</p>

<p>“Julian didn’t mean to do that. We were just exchanging moves, and I was the one who became overconfident, thinking I could take him.”</p>

<p>Her words hung in the air, a fragile bridge attempting to mend the tension. She looked at Julian, her eyes softening, as if to convey her understanding.</p>

<p>“It was all in good fun. I underestimated his power, and now we know better.”</p>

<p>Julia’s calm demeanor seemed to diffuse some of the anger in the room, her father’s expression softening slightly as he considered her words.</p>

<p>Duke Ethwer’s gaze bore into Julian, a mix of warning and authority in his voice.</p>

<p>“You better be careful, boy. Recklessness gets you nowhere,” he said, his tone firm yet measured.</p>

<p>The weight of his words settled in the air, a reminder of the fine line between confidence and overreach. He paused, allowing the gravity of the situation to sink in before continuing.</p>

<p>“This is not just a game. Lives are at stake.”</p>

<p>The intensity in his eyes reflected a lifetime of experience. The lesson was clear: in the world of magic and power, even the smallest mistake could have dire consequences.</p>

<p>Julian nodded, sensing the weight of his words. “I understand, Duke.”</p>

<p>As the echoes of their voices faded, the air grew still in the training ground. The dukes and duchesses, their expressions a mixture of concern and authority, departed, leaving Isabel, Julia, and Julian standing in the aftermath of the confrontation.</p>

<p>Julia let out a breath she hadn’t realized she’d been holding. “Well, that was intense,” she said, a nervous laugh escaping her lips.</p>

<p>Isabel shook her head, still in shock. “You really went all out there, Julian. It was… impressive, but also terrifying.”</p>

<p>Julian rubbed the back of his neck, feeling the weight of their gazes. “I didn’t mean for it to escalate like that. I just wanted to have some fun.”</p>

<p>Julia crossed her arms, a teasing smile forming on her face. “Fun, huh? Maybe next time, we should keep the fun less… explosive.”</p>

<p>Julian chuckled, the tension easing. “Yeah, no more summoning lightning tridents. I promise.”</p>

<p>Isabel smirked. “And maybe you can explain the game rules a little better next time?”</p>

<p>With a shared laugh, the three of them began to shake off the remnants of the encounter.</p>

<p>Julian grinned, crossing his arms with a sense of victory.</p>

<p>“Well, looks like I win this round, Julia. Next time, maybe don’t underestimate me,” he said, a playful glint in his eye as he reveled in the triumph of their earlier exchange.</p>

<p>Julia rolled her eyes, the remnants of fear still lingering in her expression.</p>

<p>“Well, no shit, Julian! You nearly killed me with that last move,” she exclaimed, her tone a mix of irritation and disbelief as she tried to shake off the adrenaline that still coursed through her veins.</p>

<p>Julian turned to Issabel with a mischievous smile, his confidence still brimming.</p>

<p>“So, do you want to have a go, Lady Issabel?” he asked, his tone teasing as he gauged her reaction.</p>

<p>Issabel shook her head vigorously, wide-eyed and a bit shaken.</p>

<p>“No, sir Julian, I don’t want to die!” she replied, her voice trembling slightly as the memory of the earlier display loomed over her.</p>

<p>Julian burst into laughter, the tension of the earlier confrontation fading away.</p>

<p>“I guess you both lose!” he exclaimed, a playful glint in his eye. “Do you still remember our deal?” He leaned in closer, clearly relishing the moment, as he awaited their reactions.</p>

<p>Julia frowned, still shaken from the earlier encounter. “Seriously, Julian? You can’t just put us in danger like that and expect us to just… lose clothes as if it’s nothing!”</p>

<p>Issabel nodded, her expression a mix of disbelief and amusement.</p>

<p>“Yeah, I’d rather keep my clothes on, thank you very much. I like being alive!”</p>

<p>Julian grinned mischievously. “Come on! It was all in good fun! Besides, I think you both look stunning, but now I get to see just how brave you really are!”</p>

<p>Julia rolled her eyes. “Brave? More like reckless! You could have really hurt me.”</p>

<p>Issabel chuckled nervously. “Okay, maybe I don’t want to find out just how brave I am right now.”</p>

<p>Julian laughed again and with a sly smile said. “Alright, fine! No pressure. But just know, I’m always up for a challenge clothes or no clothes!”</p>

<p>Julia smirked, a playful glint in her eye. “You really want us to strip, don’t you, Julian? What kind of mage are you, trying to turn this into a show?”</p>

<p>Issabel raised an eyebrow, trying to suppress a giggle. “Yeah, Julian. Is that your secret plan?”</p>

<p>Julian shrugged, feigning innocence. “Hey, I’m just a spectator here! If you two want to put on a show, who am I to stop you?”</p>

<p>Julia laughed, shaking her head. “You’re impossible! But I suppose if it means we get to see just how powerful you really are, it might be worth it.”</p>

<p>Issabel joined in, “Or at least it could be entertaining! Just don’t forget, we might end up getting you back for this later.”</p>
