<p>Gregoria’s heart pounded like crazy. She searched his gaze, expecting to find raw, animalistic hunger, but instead found something softer—something she didn’t want to name, something that twisted her insides with a sickening pull.</p>

<p>“Julian…” she managed to whisper, her voice barely audible. Her eyes wavered, torn between the shame screaming this is wrong and a deeper, treacherous whisper murmuring this is us.</p>

<p>He held her gaze, his thumb caressing her cheek, smearing the mess gently.</p>

<p>“You see it, don’t you?” He murmured, his voice strangely hypnotic. “It’s more than lust, Grandma. I want you…every piece of you. That’s how my love is.”</p>

<p>His words hung in the air—filthy, possessive, yet filled with a strange tenderness.</p>
