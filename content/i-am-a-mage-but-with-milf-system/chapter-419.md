<p>“This rare potion allows your dantians to expand beyond natural limits. Drink it… and your body will be able to store more Qi than others at your level — perfect for both combat and cultivation!”</p>

<p>Gossips filled the room instantly:</p>

<p>“That’s a top-tier treasure for Core Formation!”</p>

<p>“Even Nascent Soul cultivators would kill for that boost!”</p>

<p>“I heard just one drop can help during bottleneck breakthroughs!”</p>

<p>Fi Feng raised one hand for silence, then smiled sharply.</p>

<p>“The starting price is… ten mid-grade spiritual stones!”</p>

<p>Hands shot up immediately.</p>

<p>“Eleven mid-grade stones!”</p>

<p>“Twelve!”</p>

<p>“Thirteen and a fire essence pill!”</p>

<p>“Fourteen and one Spirit Tempering Manual!”</p>

<p>The hall exploded into action.</p>

<p>Many bidders nervously looked up toward the Sapphire Lobby but seeing Julian unmoved, they sighed in relief and continued fighting among themselves.</p>

<p>Fi Feng grinned widely, watching the excitement grow.</p>

<p>“Fifteen stones and one Profound Metal shard!”</p>

<p>“Seventeen!”</p>

<p>“Twenty!”</p>

<p>The bid was climbing rapidly.</p>

<p>“Twenty-five mid-grade stones!” a voice boomed confidently.</p>

<p>A man in plain robe stood up proudly from the center, his chest puffed.</p>

<p>Fi Feng turned toward him with a wide grin and began the countdown.</p>

<p>Some disciples exchanged glances, whispering among themselves.</p>

<p>“The potion is good…” one murmured.</p>

<p>“But is it really worth twenty-five mid-grade stones?”</p>

<p>“Overpriced,” another muttered. “That guy just wanted to show off.”</p>

<p>Fi Feng raised his hand.</p>

<p>“One… Two… Three…!”</p>

<p>No one else raised their hand.</p>

<p>He clapped once.</p>

<p>“Congratulations! The Heavenly Potion of Qi now belongs to you!</p>

<p>You can redeem it later at the treasure desk after the auction concludes.”</p>

<p>The proud disciple sat back down smugly as polite applause followed.</p>

<p>The two seductive maids bowed gracefully, then carried the tray back behind the curtains.</p>

<p>Fi Feng wasted no time.</p>

<p>“With that out of the way,” he said smoothly, “bring in the next item!”</p>

<p>Immediately, two new maids stepped through the side entrance.</p>

<p>They were equally alluring as the last pair and between them, they carried a black-fabric covered tray.</p>

<p>Fi Feng stepped forward dramatically and pulled off the fabric.</p>

<p>A dagger lay beneath, its edge glowing with a strange dark glow, like it was made of darkness.</p>

<p>Even from a distance, one could sense a dangerous aura leaking from it.</p>

<p>Fi Feng gripped the hilt and raised it high.</p>

<p>“This,” he said</p>

<p>“is the Dragon’s Breath Dagger. It is said… this blade was forged from the tooth of a dragon that once roamed our world.”</p>

<p>The moment the name “Dragon’s Breath Dagger” was spoken, the crowd buzzed wildly.</p>

<p>“Did he say… dragon?”</p>

<p>“A real dragon’s tooth?”</p>

<p>“Impossible! Aren’t they extinct?”</p>

<p>“No wonder it looks like it’s alive…”</p>

<p>Murmurs turned into full-on whispers, the entire auction hall filled with disbelief.</p>

<p>Fi Feng basked in the attention, spinning the dagger slightly in his hand.</p>

<p>“This dagger… is not only forged from the tooth of a true dragon…”</p>

<p>He paused, letting the anticipation rise.</p>

<p>“But its edge… carries a curse.”</p>

<p>The room fell silent.</p>

<p>Fi Feng smiled wider and continued,</p>

<p>“Any wound inflicted by this blade will never heal naturally. Not by pills. Not by medicine. Not even by high-grade healing techniques.”</p>

<p>Gasps echoed through the chamber.</p>

<p>Fi Feng spun the blade once more and stabbed it lightly into the table — the wood rotted and blackened where it touched.</p>

<p>A chilling silence followed.</p>

<p>Everyone in the room knew: this item wasn’t just rare — it was dangerous.</p>

<p>“And this…” Fi Feng said,</p>

<p>“starts at 1,000 high-grade spiritual stones.”</p>

<p>Eyes widened, and some cultivators physically jolted in their seats.</p>

<p>“A thousand?!”</p>

<p>“That’s a sect elder’s annual salary!”</p>

<p>“That’s… that’s robbery!”</p>

<p>Fi Feng laughed.</p>

<p>“Oh, come now. Let’s not pretend this dagger is a common treasure. This is an unhealable wound in your hands.</p>

<p>So to everyone who has been saving their entire life for something…</p>

<p>this is your moment to empty your pockets.”</p>

<p>He leaned forward slightly.</p>

<p>“After all… if it ends up in the hands of someone you hate—”</p>

<p>His tone darkened,</p>

<p>“—they’ll have the advantage against you forever.”</p>

<p>The hall fell into a tense silence.</p>

<p>Everyone looked around at each other, calculating their next move.</p>

<p>Then, from the VIP section on the lower floor, a voice shouted—</p>

<p>“One thousand and fifty!”</p>

<p>A grey-robed elder stood, his posture both tense yet excited.</p>

<p>Then another voice cut in sharply,</p>

<p>“Eleven hundred!”</p>

<p>It came from a young man in golden robes, the badge of a Ten great sect on his chest.</p>

<p>“Eleven-fifty!”</p>

<p>“Twelve hundred!”</p>

<p>Julian watched with amusement as the bidding war began to heat up.</p>

<p>Below, even the confident voices were filled with nervous tension — no one wanted to overspend, but none wanted to let it fall into a rival’s hands either.</p>

<p>Fi Feng laughed joyfully.</p>

<p>“Wonderful! Let the bloodless battle begin!”</p>

<p>“Thirteen hundred!” shouted a voice from the far-right balcony.</p>

<p>Heads turned — it was Elder Wu from the Iron Ghost Sect, one of the ten great sects, his face already red with tension.</p>

<p>“Fourteen hundred!” came immediately after, from the left side — Elder Gao of the Thunder Flame Pavilion, one of the ten great sects.</p>

<p>The crowd tensed.</p>

<p>These two had history.</p>

<p>“You fucking snake!” Elder Wu barked, pointing a finger at Gao.</p>

<p>“I should have crushed your sect twenty years ago when your whore of a daughter begged to sleep in my courtyard!”</p>

<p>Gasps exploded across the hall.</p>

<p>Elder Gao smirked and snapped back,</p>

<p>“You old fart! I’ll fuck your mother and your grandmother! You think I’m scared of your weak techniques?”</p>

<p>“Fifteen hundred!” Elder Wu roared, face tomato with fury.</p>

<p>“Fifteen-fifty!” Gao shouted back, veins bulging.</p>

<p>Disciples below lowered their heads as spiritual pressure began leaking from both elders.</p>

<p>Fi Feng stepped in quickly, laughing but serious.</p>

<p>“Esteemed guests, esteemed guests—let’s keep the auction orderly! Save the physical violence for after, hmm?”</p>

<p>“Sixteen hundred!” shouted Wu, ignoring him entirely.</p>

<p>“You want this dagger so bad?” Gao barked,</p>

<p>“Are you scared I’ll stab your limp old cock next time you challenge me?!”</p>

<p>The entire hall was in chaos — gasps, laughter, and shocked silence all clashed together.</p>

<p>Just as Elder Wu opened his mouth to throw another curse, a calm voice rose from the center of the crowd.</p>

<p>“Twenty thousand.”</p>

<p>Silence.</p>

<p>The entire auction hall froze.</p>

<p>Heads snapped toward the source of the voice like puppets.</p>
