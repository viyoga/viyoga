<p>“Does it hurt a lot?”</p>

<p>“It’s manageable,” he lied. “The medicine Father gave me is helping. I just need rest.”</p>

<p>Olivia didn’t look convinced. She stayed seated beside him, her hand still resting lightly on his hair. The room was quiet except for the faint sound of his labored breathing.</p>

<p>After a long pause, she spoke again, her voice barely above a whisper.</p>

<p>“When I saw the light from the training ground… when I felt the clash of those two powers… I was terrified. For a moment, I thought something terrible had happened to you.”</p>

<p>Julian opened his eyes and looked up at her.</p>

<p>“Father had hopes of me,”</p>

<p>He said quietly.</p>

<p>“I needed to do it. Father and Commander Aldric were already suspicious of me. If I had suddenly backed out and avoided the duel, it would have been even more suspicious.”</p>

<p>Olivia remained silent.</p>

<p>She knew he was right. Only she knew the truth behind her son’s sudden and terrifying rise in power.</p>

<p>To anyone else, especially to her husband, Kraven’s explosive growth from a mediocre mage to an Arch Mage in just a few months was nothing short of suspicious.</p>

<p>After a few long moments, she looked down at Julian again, her expression softening with genuine worry.</p>

<p>“I lost too much of my mana during the fight with the Commander.”</p>

<p>That excuse sounded very decent, and it seemed to do its job. Olivia’s concern deepened immediately. Her voice became softer.</p>

<p>“I know…” she whispered.</p>

<p>Olivia looked at his pale face, the bandages wrapped around his chest and shoulder, and the faint bloodstains that had seeped through. Her heart tightened.</p>

<p>“You can… take some of my mana.”</p>

<p>The offer came out of nowhere.</p>

<p>“You gave me so much today. You strengthened my foundation and even restored my youth. If my mana can help you recover even a little… then you should take it. I don’t want to see you suffer like this.”</p>

<p>She finally turned her gaze back to him.</p>

<p>“I know it might feel… strange. But right now, you are hurt because of me as well. Let me do something for you.”</p>
