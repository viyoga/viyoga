<p>The incoming aura struck the barrier, sending out a blinding explosion. For a moment, everything around Julian seemed to blur as the raw power of the energy collided with his protective shield.</p>

<p>The force of the blast shook the world around them, making everything crack and tremble.</p>

<p>Julian’s barrier held strong at first, easily absorbing the initial onslaught. However, as the power continued to pour into the shield, Julian’s expression shifted.</p>

<p>The sheer power of the energy was beyond anything he had anticipated. Even with my Arch Mage mana, I can’t handle this, he thought, his focus deepening as he tried to maintain control.</p>

<p>The cracks began to form in the barrier’s surface. Each new attack of energy caused the cracks to widen, and the barrier began to show signs of strain under the relentless pressure.</p>

<p>The villagers, who had initially been in awe of Julian’s display of power, now felt a new wave of fear wash over them.</p>

<p>So even he can’t save us, they thought, watching in growing panic as the barrier began to break down.</p>

<p>Julian’s heart pounded rapidly as he observed the barrier weakening further. Should I use another energy? he thought, his mind racing to find alternatives.</p>

<p>The cracks were spreading faster than he could keep up with. His fingers trembled slightly as his hands clenched into a fist.</p>

<p>But what if Preservation recognizes it? The thought lingered, making sweat run down his face.</p>

<p>“I have no other choice,” he muttered to himself, steeling his resolve.</p>

<p>With a deep breath, he closed his eyes and began to channel his cosmic energy. As the energy swirled within him, it began to creep outward from his body, flowing to the barrier.</p>

<p>The golden energy enveloped the barrier, healing the cracks and reinforcing the shield. The barrier began to stabilize, growing thicker and stronger.</p>

<p>The villagers who had been watching in terror shielded their eyes from the blinding light, their mouths wide as they witnessed Julian’s transformation.</p>

<p>Julian finally felt a sense of control return. The overwhelming aura from the cavern continued to attack, but now it was met with a force that seemed equally powerful.</p>

<p>Aria’s eyes widened in disbelief. “What is this…? This is not mana,” she whispered.</p>

<p>Her voice trembled with awe and confusion as she watched the golden barrier before her, withstanding the attack of the energy that belonged to their deity.</p>

<p>Julian took a deep breath, the weight beginning to lift from his shoulders as the onslaught of energy from the cavern no longer seemed to affect him.</p>

<p>As time passed, the cosmic energy started to interact with the cavern’s aura in ways Julian hadn’t anticipated.</p>

<p>His brows furrowed in confusion. What is this? he thought, watching the strange interaction unfold.</p>

<p>The villagers looked at him in confusion, unsure of what was happening but too fearful to speak. The golden energy from Julian’s body seemed to be repelling the cavern’s energy, but it did not seem to be dominating it.</p>

<p>As he focused even more intently, Julian realized that the cosmic energy wasn’t just repelling or blocking the cavern’s force—it was actively shielding it, creating a protective aura.</p>

<p>The cavern’s energy seemed to slowly calm down as it merged with the cosmic energy.</p>

<p>What is cosmic energy doing? Julian wondered as the two energies began to coexist, the overwhelming pressure starting to dissipate.</p>

<p>As the final remnants of the wild energy dissipated into the air, the atmosphere that had once been filled with intense pressure and chaos slowly returned to normal.</p>

<p>The ripples in Julian’s barrier gradually calmed, and the golden light from his cosmic energy slowly faded.</p>

<p>The villagers, who had been holding their breath in fear, finally exhaled in relief. The world around them grew silent, the atmosphere light and peaceful.</p>

<p>But then through the silence a clear and loud voice rang out, filling the entire surrounding, “Creatus…”</p>

<p>At the sound of that word, time seemed to slow, as if the entire world was pausing for a brief moment. It was as if the voice was calling for someone, someone close.</p>

<p>The villagers stood frozen, glancing at one another, trying to understand what the word they had just heard meant.</p>

<p>Julian remained silent, his mind swirling with questions. That was preservation’s voice, but what did he mean by Creatus? he thought</p>

<p>He paused for a moment searching for possible explanation. Creation maybe? The thought sending a shiver down his spine. Did he notice me using cosmic energy?</p>

<p>He shook his head, getting rid of the thought. There is no use thinking about it. Explore stories on empire</p>

<p>He then laid on the ground, letting his body finally release the tension that it had been holding for what felt like an eternity.</p>

<p>His gaze locked on the peaceful sky above, filled with countless stars and he took a deep breathe, Its finally over.</p>

<p>He closed his eyes with a smile tugging at his lips. The weight of the events had left him exhausted, but he felt a deep sense of relief.</p>

<p>Around him, Aira, Dakota, and the villagers stood in quiet awe, their gazes locked on Julian. They all smiled, noticing the rhythmic breathing of Julian.</p>

<p>“He slept,” one of the villagers murmured softly.</p>

<p>“Yes, he protected us all,” another added with a deep gratitude playing in his words.</p>

<p>Aria, observing the scene, spoke thoughtfully, “He was hiding his power all along. I never imagined…” he said, her mind flashing the image of strange energy Julian had used earlier.</p>

<p>Dakota nodded gently, her eyes softening as she looked down at him. “Go back, Mother,” she said. “I will accompany him.”</p>

<p>Aria gave a knowing nod, understanding Dakota’s desire to stay with Julian. She turned away and signaled the villagers to head back as well.</p>

<p>They soon left, leaving Dakota alone by Julian’s side. She carefully sat beside him, her hand resting lightly on his, feeling the warmth of his presence.</p>

<p>She stayed there, her gaze drifting to the night sky, grateful for the peace that had finally returned after the storm.</p>
