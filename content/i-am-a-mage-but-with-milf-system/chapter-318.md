<p>Some stumbled over one another, some collided and fell, while some were stepped to their death as they tried to escape the fate they knew was closing in on them.</p>

<p>Arberus shouted in fury, “You fuckers! Where are you running to?!”</p>

<p>But his words were like dropping a bottle of water into an ocean—completely meaningless. The soldiers didn’t listen.</p>

<p>They were already too far gone, lost in their panic, and it was clear that their resolve had shattered.</p>

<p>Lian watched with a mixture of frustration and defeat. He shook his head. “Forget about it, Arberus. Those are just bandits and criminals. Who would expect loyalty from them?”</p>

<p>The words seemed to hang in the air as the last remnants of his army fled into the distant forest.</p>

<p>Lian’s gaze remained fixed on the now-empty battlefield, and for the first time in a long while, he felt the weight of defeat crushing down on him.</p>

<p>Julian had won—not just the battle, but their very will to fight.</p>

<p>Lian fell to his knees, his hands raised in surrender. “We surrender,” he said, the words heavy with defeat.</p>

<p>Arberus hesitated, his gaze flickering between Lian and Julian before finally following. He, too, dropped to his knees, his hands raised.</p>

<p>Julian’s lips curled into a smile. “Wise choice,” he said, his tone both mocking and satisfied.</p>

<p>He turned to his soldiers and issued a command with a cold, calculated gaze. “Go and chain them.”</p>

<p>Without a word, his men jumped into action and surrounded Lian and Arberus. They swiftly bound the two men with chains, treating them like criminals.</p>

<p>Lian and Arberus, once commanders feared in their own right, now sat shackled like prisoners awaiting their fate.</p>

<p>Julian’s gaze shifted to the distant forest, his eyes narrowing as a sinister smile curled on his lips.</p>

<p>He took a deep breath before shouting to his soldiers, “The hunt starts now. Every head is worth 100 gold. You all know what to do.”</p>

<p>For a moment, there was a chilling silence. Then, with a sudden loud sound, the army surged forward. Laughter and mocking shouts filled the air as the soldiers raced toward the forest, eager to claim their prize.</p>

<p>Lian and Arberus could only shiver in fear. They could hear the mocking laughter of the soldiers as they closed in on their prey.</p>

<p>The fate of everyone who had stood against Julian now sealed.</p>

<p>Lian’s voice trembled as he whispered, “What have we done?”</p>

<p>“Julian, go. We can’t stay like this any longer. I trust you to handle this. Do what you must.”</p>

<p>Julian turned to face Alden. “I will, Father,” he said, his eyes glowing with fierce determination.</p>

<p>But before Julian could move, a voice interrupted. The commander, standing near the doorway, stepped forward.</p>

<p>“Your grace, we can’t act without a proper plan. We should not rush in blindly.”</p>

<p>Julian narrowed his eyes, staring at the commander. There was something about him that didn’t sit right.</p>

<p>Alden, sensing the growing tension, let out a heavy sigh.</p>

<p>“We can’t afford to stay like this any longer,” he said, his voice carrying the weight of authority.</p>

<p>The commander seemed to panic, his hands trembling slightly as he spoke hurriedly.</p>

<p>“Let’s wait for an hour, Your Grace. If there is no news by then, we can proceed as you wish.”</p>

<p>Julian’s gaze remained cold. He knew that the longer they waited, the harder it would be to take control of the situation. However, he had no other choice than to wait.</p>

<p>After a minute or so, the commander swiftly slipped out of the room. Julian immediately noticed, and he called for the Domain of Isolation.</p>

<p>The commander seemed cautious and was always looking around to check if anyone was following him.</p>

<p>Julian smiled coldly. So this fucker was the one leaking our information.</p>

<p>The commander reached a secluded hallway, where a maid stood with a soldier nearby. Without hesitation, he approached the soldier and drew a dagger from his belt.</p>

<p>With a swift motion, he slit the soldier’s throat, killing him instantly. The maid let out a silent gasp, but before she could react, the commander turned his attention to her.</p>

<p>He gave her a smirk before ignoring her completely. He reached into his robe and took out a small crystal.</p>

<p>With a dark grin, he began channeling his mana into the crystal. The crystal flickered with an eerie light, responding to the commander’s call.</p>

<p>Julian’s eyes narrowed. This is what he’s been up to. He has been selling us out all along.</p>

<p>“It’s fine for now, but I can’t handle them anymore. You only have one hour to prepare everything,” he said with frustration.</p>
