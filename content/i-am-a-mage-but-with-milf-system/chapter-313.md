<p>As they continued their journey, they came upon a vast snowy domain, where the landscape was covered in a thick layer of ice and snow.</p>

<p>In the distance, they saw a massive creature. Its form resembled a wyvern, but it was much larger than any they had encountered before. Its body was snowy blue, and it almost blended perfectly with the frozen surroundings.</p>

<p>The wyvern’s breath sent a wave of icy mist into the air, freezing the ground beneath.</p>

<p>Alice’s eyes were wide in both awe and fear as she watched the massive creature. She shivered and instinctively took a step back.</p>

<p>“Let’s not get near it,” she said.</p>

<p>Julian chuckled softly. “Don’t be scared,” he said with a grin, clearly unfazed by the imposing creature.</p>

<p>Without waiting for a response, he flew towards the wyvern.</p>

<p>The creature turned its head to meet Julian’s gaze, and for a moment, there was a silence in the air.</p>

<p>It seemed to recognize Julian, and it let out a roar. It wasn’t a sound of threat, rather of acknowledgment.</p>

<p>To everyone’s surprise, the wyvern lowered its head in a bow, as though submitting to Julian’s presence.</p>

<p>Julian chuckled again, pleased by the creature’s response. He raised his hand to pat the wyvern’s head. The wyvern seemed pleased, and its icy scales glowed faintly.</p>

<p>“Good boy,” Julian said, giving the wyvern another gentle pat.</p>

<p>Then, with a swift movement, he jumped onto the wyvern’s back.</p>

<p>The creature’s powerful wings unfurled with a tremendous gust of wind, and it took off into the air, soaring above the snow-covered landscape.</p>

<p>Regina and Alice watched in stunned silence as Julian flew higher and higher, disappearing into the vast sky. Eleanor, still holding the fire creature in her arms, smiled softly.</p>

<p>“He never fails to surprise me,” Alice whispered, her eyes still wide with amazement.</p>

<p>After a moment, Julian and the wyvern descended gracefully to the ground.</p>

<p>Julian turned toward the three women and gestured for them to join him.</p>

<p>The women exchanged hesitant glances, especially Alice, who hesitated for a moment longer than the others.</p>

<p>She took a deep breath and carefully approached the wyvern.</p>

<p>One by one, they climbed onto the wyvern’s back and sat beside Julian.</p>

<p>With the four of them now on the wyvern, the creature let out a low roar before unfurling its wings again.</p>

<p>“Hold on tight,” Julian said loudly. “We are going for a ride.”</p>

<p>With a powerful flap of its wings, the wyvern lifted off once again. The wind grew stronger as they ascended, but the view from above was breathtaking.</p>

<p>Below them, the snowy domain stretched endlessly. There were tall, snow-covered mountains that sparkled by reflecting the sunlight.</p>

<p>There were equally mesmerizing but unique snow creatures that were playing and walking in groups.</p>

<p>An amused smile played on Alice’s lips as she took in the mesmerizing view.</p>

<p>“This… this is incredible,” she said, her voice barely above a whisper.</p>

<p>Meanwhile, Regina and Eleanor were enjoying the flight, glancing around the scenery in awe.</p>

<p>Eleanor, who was still holding the fire creature, glanced at Julian. “This wyvern… It seems to really trust you.”</p>

<p>Julian nodded. “It does…”</p>

<p>Just then, he caught a sight of something interesting. He patted the wyvern gently, “Stop.”</p>

<p>The wyvern obeyed instantly, and its massive wings flapped slowly as they hovered above the strange sight.</p>

<p>Regina looked down curiously. “What is it?” she asked.</p>

<p>Julian smiled. “Look below,” he said.</p>

<p>The women all leaned forward, their eyes scanning the landscape below.</p>

<p>At first, it seemed like an ordinary scene—green hills, patches of grass, and trees—but then they saw them: figures moving through the grass.</p>

<p>They were standing tall and walking in a manner they had never seen before in Julian’s world.</p>

<p>Alice’s eyes widened. “Is that… humans?” she whispered.</p>

<p>Eleanor nodded slowly, her gaze fixed on the figures below. “They look like one,” she murmured.</p>

<p>Julian’s voice grew confident. “Yeah, they are the first wave of humans to evolve here.”</p>

<p>The group watched in awe as the humans below moved through the terrain, unaware of the presence of the wyvern and its companion.</p>

<p>The humans below moved with surprising coordination. They were naked and stealthy, and it seemed that they were in the middle of hunting.</p>

<p>Soon, they came across a large bear-like creature. The humans stopped instantly and signaled to one another in low whispers.</p>

<p>Julian’s interest was piqued. “Let’s see how they will…”</p>

<p>But before he could finish his sentence, one of the humans raised their hand and threw a fireball towards the bear.</p>

<p>The creature roared in pain as the fireball hit its side. Its delicate fur was easily caught by the fire.</p>

<p>Julian’s eyes widened in shock. “They can use mana?” he asked in disbelief.</p>

<p>The other women looked at him, equally stunned. Julian’s mind raced as he processed what he had just witnessed. This was far beyond what he expected.</p>

<p>The humans below had already unlocked the ability to use mana. Their potential was far greater than Julian had imagined.</p>

<p>“This is… unexpected. They are using mana to hunt,” Eleanor whispered.</p>

<p>The bear rolled around on the ground in pain but was unable to escape as the humans moved swiftly behind it.</p>

<p>Another individual raised his hands and surrounded the bear in a web of water.</p>

<p>The water condensed and solidified, forming a cage around its body.</p>

<p>The first human, who had cast the fireball, approached the trapped beast. With one brutal slash, he severed the bear’s head, and its body collapsed to the ground, lifeless.</p>

<p>The group of humans following closely behind the two immediately erupted into cheers and laughter. They quickly began to cut the creature and carried the pieces on their shoulders.</p>

<p>Julian was fascinated at the sight. “This… this is impressive,” he murmured.</p>

<p>“They are already using mana spells and working as a unit.”</p>

<p>Regina leaned forward. “They are evolving faster, it seems,” she said. “They are already developing skills that would take most civilizations centuries to learn.”</p>

<p>Eleanor nodded. “And they’re not just using fire and water. They somehow have learned to use their environment, hiding in tall bushes, caging it.”</p>

<p>Meanwhile, Alice was speechless for a moment before finally she spoke. “So… will they not be a threat to us?”</p>

<p>Julian simply chuckled at Alice’s words.</p>

<p>As he observed the humans below, he saw them suddenly freeze. Their eyes widened in awe, and before he could even react, they all kneeled and bowed deeply.</p>

<p>Julian nodded, acknowledging their worship with a small smile on his lips.</p>

<p>“They know their place.”</p>

<p>With that he turned his attention away from the humans. He gestured the wyvern to take flight again, and it complied immediately.</p>

<p>Unlike the humans created by the supreme beings for specific purposes, the humans here have evolved naturally with no aim or purpose, so they are capable of using mana on their own.</p>

<p>It was not given to them as a gift and were simply born with the talent to control it. It’s like a part of their body to them. It’s fascinating how the world is shaping them.</p>

<p>It was an unexpected development but one that Julian found intriguing. I wonder how far they’ll go…</p>

<p>They’ll be stronger than the mages of the outside world, that’s for sure, he thought.</p>

<p>I’ve truly created something remarkable.</p>

<p>They continued exploring the world for a few more hours, discovering new sights and creatures.</p>

<p>The landscape was ever-changing, and each new discovery only deepened their awe of the world.</p>

<p>However, as the evening approached, they made their way back to Easvil Castle.</p>

<p>Once inside the castle, the group split off to their respective rooms, each exhausted from the adventure.</p>

<p>Julian lay down on his bed with many things running through his mind. The world was evolving rapidly, and so was his power and influence.</p>

<p>He closed his eyes, shaking off his thoughts, and drifted off to sleep.</p>
