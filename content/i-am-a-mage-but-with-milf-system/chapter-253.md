<p>“It’s time you let it go, Raphael. Let her go.” Julian said, his voice warm and gentle. “Bury her deep within you, somewhere safe, where she cannot chain you down anymore.”</p>

<p>“Sometimes, letting them go is the most precious act of love you can offer. You do it not out of hate, but because you want them to live their life, just as you deserve to live yours.”</p>

<p>Raphael’s heart fluttered at Julian’s words. They were so simple, yet so beautifully profound. A slow smile crept onto his face as he looked up at Julian.</p>

<p>“You make even letting go sound beautiful, my lord,” he said, his voice calmer than before, filled with a new sense of clarity.</p>

<p>Julian chuckled softly. “Every moment you spend living is beautiful,” he replied.</p>

<p>Raphael felt a weight lift from his chest, as if the burden he had been carrying for so long had finally been acknowledged and understood.</p>

<p>“Maybe all I needed was someone who could listen to me,” he said, his words carrying a deep sincerity. “Thank you, my lord,” he added, bowing his head in respect.</p>

<p>Julian waved off Raphael’s gratitude with a flick of his hand, his grin widening into something more mischievous. “Forget about that, Raphael,” he said. “Let’s go. You’ll finally be a man today.”</p>

<p>Raphael blinked in surprise, his brow furrowing as he stepped back slightly. “What do you mean, my lord?” he asked.</p>

<p>Julian’s smirk only deepened. “You’ll see soon enough,” he replied.</p>

<p>He then suddenly teleported, arriving at an unknown location along with Raphael. The sudden shift in space overwhelmed Raphael, and he fell to the ground, clutching his stomach.</p>

<p>“Ughhh… Rughgh…” Raphael gagged, vomiting uncontrollably.</p>

<p>Julian raised an eyebrow, crossing his arms. “Really, Raphael? You’re acting like this is your first time teleporting.”</p>

<p>Raphael wiped his mouth, glaring weakly at him. “My lord… huff… you didn’t even warn me! Where… where are we?”</p>

<p>Julian smirked, looking around the mysterious, dimly lit surroundings. “Somewhere interesting, that’s for sure.”</p>

<p>Raphael looked around, his eyes narrowing as he took in the dim surroundings. The ground was made up of stones, and the air felt suffocating.</p>

<p>“Is this… a cave?” he asked.</p>

<p>Julian glanced at him. “Save that for later. Compose yourself and follow me.”</p>

<p>Raphael sighed, wiping the remnants of vomit off his mouth and quickly straightening up. He brushed off his clothes, trying to maintain his composure.</p>

<p>“As you command, my lord,” he muttered as he quickly followed behind Julian, who had already started walking deeper into the cave.</p>

<p>They reached the end of the cave, and Raphael frowned in confusion. “My lord, it’s a dead end.”</p>

<p>Julian smirked and stepped forward, tapping the wall twice. The sound echoed softly, and suddenly, the wall split open.</p>

<p>Raphael stumbled back, his eyes wide with shock. He immediately summoned his spear, getting into a defensive stance.</p>

<p>“My lord, what is this?!” He shouted, scanning for danger.</p>

<p>Julian laughed at Raphael’s reaction. “Relax, Raphael. Do you think I would bring you somewhere without knowing what lies ahead?”</p>

<p>Raphael’s grip on his spear loosened slightly, though he kept his guard up. “I would rather be cautious, my lord. Whatever is hidden behind walls like these… can’t be simple.”</p>

<p>“You’ll see soon enough.” Julian said. With that, he stepped confidently into the opening.</p>

<p>As soon as they entered, Raphael’s eyes widened in disbelief. Before them stretched an entire underground city, bustling with life. It was massive.</p>

<p>Ancient pagodas rose high, glowing faintly under the soft, flickering light of magical lanterns that hovered like fireflies. Stone pathways covered the ground, and the air buzzed with chatter and activity.</p>

<p>Raphael’s gaze froze on a massive gate ahead, adorned with intricate gold designs and faint inscriptions. At the top, in bold letters, it said—Pleasure District.</p>

<p>His jaw dropped as he muttered, “My lord… what is this place?”</p>

<p>Julian smirked, clearly enjoying Raphael’s shock. “Surprised, are you? It’s an underground paradise… though not many know about it.”</p>

<p>Raphael glanced back at him, struggling for words. “But this… a city under a cave… how is it even possible? And—”</p>

<p>He hesitated, pointing toward the gate. “That… Pleasure District?”</p>

<p>Julian chuckled, placing a hand on Raphael’s shoulder. “This is the place where you’ll finally become a man today, Raphael.”</p>

<p>Raphael froze, his face turning red. “But my lord…” he stammered, playing with his spear like a shy maiden.</p>

<p>Julian’s smirk widened. “Why are you being shy, Raphael? Be a man. Aren’t you a soldier? Act like one.”</p>

<p>Raphael opened his mouth to protest further. “But my—”</p>

<p>“Shut up and follow me,” Julian cut him off sharply, grabbing his arm and dragging him toward the towering gate of the Pleasure District.</p>

<p>They both entered the towering gate and found themselves standing in a long queue for the entrance.</p>

<p>Raphael, still processing the bizarre sight of this underground city, looked around with a mix of unease and disbelief.</p>

<p>When their turn finally arrived, two armored guards stepped forward, blocking the path. “Your name and fee,” one of the guards demanded.</p>

<p>Julian stepped forward and flashed a confident grin. “Ah, yes, of course. Name’s… Diddy, Sean Diddy,” he said smoothly, pulling the name straight out of his ass.</p>
