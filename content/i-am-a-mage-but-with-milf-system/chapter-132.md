<p>Julian could not help ask “But what do you mean by ‘gods’?” he pressed, “Who exactly are they? Where are they?”</p>

<p>The system’s tone shifted, becoming more serious. “Host, I cannot reveal anything about the gods just yet, not until you grow stronger. However, all you need to understand at this point is the concept of the Trinity.”</p>

<p>Julian’s brow furrowed with a look of confusion spreading across his face. “The Trinity?” he asked, his voice tinged with curiosity. “What is that?”</p>

<p>The system’s voice remained firm. “I cannot answer your question now, Host. It is something you need not concern yourself with just yet.”</p>

<p>Julian nodded as the weight of the system’s words sank in. Although there was many things ahead of him, all that mattered to him was power.</p>

<p>Julian walked toward the newly formed castle. The grand structure stood before him. The air around it was still glowing with mana.</p>

<p>As he approached the entrance, the massive gates swung open as though it was welcoming its new ruler.</p>

<p>Inside, the castle was just as magnificent as the outside. There were tall and imposing walls adorned with intricate designs.</p>

<p>In the center stood the main castle, so tall and grand that its building seemed to touch the very sky itself. It was crafted from shimmering gold.</p>

<p>Beside it, a collection of smaller castles stood like a cluster of jewels. Each one was grand in its own right but was pale in comparison to the towering central castle.</p>

<p>The scene before him was nothing short of a dream, an ethereal landscape and the fact that it was created by him, in his own world made it even more special.</p>

<p>Julian stepped forward, and as if responding to his presence, the massive doors of the main castle swung open. He entered and witnessed that the interior was even more magnificent than the exterior.</p>

<p>The grand hall stretched high above him, its ceiling adorned with intricate golden chandeliers. The walls were crafted from the finest jewels, that seemed to shine with an otherworldly light.</p>

<p>At the far end of the hall, a grand throne sat on a raised platform. The floors were polished, smooth and reflective, and along the edges of the hall there were detailed designs of stars and planets emphasizing Julian’s connection to the divine powers.</p>

<p>Every corner of the castle was designed to reflect his immense power.</p>

<p>Julian sat down on the throne. As he settled into the throne he felt immense connection and to the world he had created. He focused on absorbing the surrounding mana.</p>

<p>He closed his eyes and he tried to absorb the mana into himself, but as he did, a frown slowly formed on his face.</p>

<p>The mana in this world was noticeably thinner than in the outside world and it was less abundant. The concentration of lightning energy was far more than the other elemental forces. They were almost nonexistent.</p>

<p>This imbalance left the mana in this realm weaker and its quality diminished compared to the outside world.</p>

<p>”</p>

<p>Since this world is primarily made up of lightning energy, it’s lacking in other elemental energy,</p>

<p>” he said to himself. ”</p>

<p>That’s why the mana here feels so much weaker, both in quantity and quality.”</p>

<p>He had created this world to reflect his power, to house his own domain, yet it wasn’t fully developed in the way the outside world was.</p>

<p>With time, as his world grew more developed, the mana would begin to stabilize. He knew that it would evolve to match his needs but it would take time.</p>

<p>“I’ll need to master the other elements if I’m going to make this world a paradise,”</p>

<p>he said to himself.</p>

<p>Julian’s thoughts were interrupted by a system, “Host you have following new abilities as the ruler of House of Harem”</p>

<p>HOUSE OF HAREM</p>

<p>Harem Manifestation:</p>

<p>Ability to summon and manifest your harem within the realm. They can serve, support, and interact with the world you have created.</p>

<p>Harem Assignation:</p>

<p>You can assign a particular castle to whoever you want in your harem. They will be in control of that castle, managing it and all that it entails.</p>

<p>Harem Cultivation:</p>

<p>The members of your harem will experience increased cultivation speed while residing in their assigned castles, allowing them to grow stronger at an accelerated rate.</p>

<p>Harem Multiplier:</p>

<p>You can massively increase the number of your harem by impregnating your wives. Their pregnancy period will be halved, accelerating the growth of your harem.</p>

<p>Incest Blessing:</p>

<p>The closer the blood relation between you and your partners, the more offspring can be produced. Additionally, your offspring will inherit the ability to produce even more offspring, continuing the cycle of growth and power.</p>

<p>Guardian Manifestation:</p>

<p>Summon guardians who represent the element. These guardians can embody specific elements that you control, aiding in defense or offense when required.</p>

<p>Disability Purifier:</p>

<p>All disabilities in your offspring will be reduced and purified, ensuring they are born completely healthy and without any impairments.</p>

<p>Population Increase (Passive Ability):</p>

<p>The more the population of your domain increases, the more powerful your abilities become. As your harem grows and your world develops, your overall strength and control will increase, enhancing all of your powers.</p>

<p>Mana Infusion:</p>

<p>Ability to slowly infuse the elemental energy of the outside world into your domain. This can bring a balance of elements into your world over time.</p>

<p>Julian looked at the abilities in front of him and a confident smile crept onto his face.</p>

<p>“Harem Manifestation, Assignation, and Cultivation will help me manage my harem and make them stronger, each of them playing a vital role in the growth of this world.”</p>

<p>He paused, letting the significance of the next abilities sink in.</p>

<p>“With Incest Blessing, Disability Purifier, Multiplier, and Population Increase, I can develop this world at an unimaginable pace. As the population grows, so too will my strength. This will allow me to reach new heights faster than I ever expected.”</p>

<p>Julian’s eyes lingered on the Mana Infusion ability and a new glimmer of realization sparkled within him. ”</p>

<p>This might be the most useful ability I have right now,</p>

<p>” he thought.</p>

<p>“By infusing mana from the outer world into this one, I can develop the mana of this world. This will solve the mana issue this world faces and allow me to balance the elemental energies.”</p>

<p>He felt a surge of excitement at the thought.</p>
