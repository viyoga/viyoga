<p>“You are not like your father,” she said.</p>

<p>Julian met her eyes. “I’m not.”</p>

<p>“Explain that to me.”</p>

<p>“My father,” Julian said, his voice unhurried, “is a man who endures. Every insult, everything Liam and his men do—he takes it in, thinks about it, and makes careful calculations before moving on. He is very good at enduring.”</p>

<p>He paused.</p>

<p>“I am not interested in enduring.”</p>

<p>“When that soldier said what he said about you,” Julian continued, “my father heard it politically. What it meant for the duchy and what responding would cost.” He looked at her steadily. “I heard something different.”</p>

<p>“What did you hear,” she said quietly.</p>

<p>“A man who believed he could say anything about you in this castle without consequence,” Julian said. His voice remained calm throughout, which made it even more intense. “I heard that clearly. And I decided it was the last time anyone in this building would make that calculation.”</p>

<p>The room held the words.</p>

<p>Olivia’s hands had gone still completely.</p>

<p>“You cannot do this every time someone says something,” she said, her voice trying to sound composed but failing miserably at it.</p>

<p>“I can, mother,” Julian said. “I absolutely can.”</p>

<p>She stared at him.</p>

<p>“And I will,” he continued, his voice carrying no heat. “If the same words came from the King’s own mouth, I would burn him where he stood. The crown changes nothing about what I would do.”</p>

<p>The silence that followed was total.</p>

<p>Olivia’s composure nearly fractured, replaced by horror and actual disbelief. She leaned forward slightly and her voice dropped to something urgent and low.</p>

<p>“Kraven, control your words—right now. If anyone in this castle heard what you just said, our entire family would hang for treason. Not just you—all of us. Your father. Vanessa. Me.”</p>

<p>Her eyes were fixed on him with an intensity that had nothing composed about it.”Do you understand what I’m telling you?”</p>

<p>Julian stood.</p>

<p>He rose from his chair to his full height, and Olivia instinctively pushed hers back. The fear had not vanished yet.</p>

<p>Ignoring her internal warfare, Julian turned and walked to the window.</p>

<p>He stood with his back to the room, gazing out over the duchy below. Night had fully fallen, swallowing everything in darkness. The streets were quiet at this hour, and the buildings appeared only as faint silhouettes against the night sky.</p>

<p>“I understand perfectly,” he said to the glass and the dark beyond it. “I understand the treason law… what hanging means for a family.”</p>

<p>He paused.</p>

<p>“I simply find that there are things I am no longer willing to organize my behavior around.”</p>

<p>“That is an extraordinarily dangerous thing to be,” Olivia said behind him.</p>

<p>“Yes,” Julian said. “It is.”</p>

<p>“Then why are you telling me this? Why say it at all?”</p>

<p>“Because you were in the room,” he said. “And I don’t intend to lie to you.”</p>

<p>Another silence.</p>

<p>Longer this time.</p>

<p>He heard her breathe, the kind of nervous breathing one did when they were unsure of where this was leading.</p>

<p>“I am not a woman who needs protecting,” she said.</p>

<p>Julian turned from the window.</p>

<p>The candlelight sat between them, casting a soft glow over the space that separated them. She sat with her back straight, her eyes fixed on him with full intensity.</p>

<p>He crossed the room slowly and stopped close enough that the conversation shifted into something entirely different from what it had been only moments before.</p>

<p>“Yes,” he said, looking down at her. “I know you’re not.”</p>

<p>She looked up at him without moving back.</p>

<p>“I know exactly what you are and what you are not,” he continued. “You have run this household and managed this family through years of my father’s careful observation and my own childish behavior.” He paused. “You did all of that without anyone in this house acknowledging it for a single day.”</p>

<p>Olivia said nothing.</p>

<p>Her hands on her knees had tightened slightly. He could see it.</p>

<p>“I also know,” Julian said, “that you are not a tool. You are not an asset.” He held her eyes steadily.</p>

<p>“And I want to be very clear with you about something. That is never going to happen. Not while I am in this castle. Not while I am alive in any form anywhere in this world.”</p>

<p>The room was completely still.</p>

<p>Olivia was looking up at him and the composure was simply gone now. What lay beneath it was not weakness, but something far more complicated—the expression of a woman who had just heard something she had stopped expecting years ago, and did not immediately know what to do with it now that it had finally come.</p>

<p>“Your father,” she said, her voice quieter than it had been at any point in the conversation, “never once said anything like that to me.”</p>

<p>Julian said nothing.</p>

<p>“Not once,” she repeated. The words came out with the weight of years behind them. “In all the years of this marriage.” She paused. “He never once looked at me and said what you just said.”</p>

<p>“No,” Julian said. “He wouldn’t.”</p>

<p>Her eyes sharpened slightly. “What does that mean.”</p>

<p>Julian stepped back and moved to the window again, but this time he turned to face the room rather than the glass.</p>

<p>“There’s a difference,” he said, “between acquiring and yearning.”</p>

<p>Olivia’s brow drew together.</p>

<p>“My father acquires,” Julian said. “He is very good at it. He acquired this duchy, his position, his marriage, and his family. Once something is acquired, you protect it. You maintain it. You make the decisions that preserve it as an asset.”</p>

<p>He held her gaze.</p>

<p>“Possession do not give rise to the kind of reality I just described,” he continued. “Throughout your entire marriage, this family has controlled your role, your place, and your life within it.”</p>

<p>Olivia looked at him.</p>

<p>Her breathing had become more uneven, her chest rising and falling as though his words were reaching her in a way she could not easily ignore.</p>

<p>“And yearning?” she asked.</p>
