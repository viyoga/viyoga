<p>He wasn’t just standing in some random world. He was in his own world — a space entirely separate from the outer world, a world made of his own sea of consciousness.</p>

<p>And even within that world, he had created an entire universe, one formed by the eternal clash of his creation energy and death energy.</p>

<p>To find anything that even stood in front of his will was already a stretch. But to find this — something capable of disturbing his core? Impossible.</p>

<p>And not out there… not in some other world… but here, inside my own domain?</p>

<p>It was too much.</p>

<p>Too overwhelming.</p>

<p>So overwhelming that Julian instinctively stopped his thoughts — not because he didn’t want to know…but because he couldn’t afford to.</p>

<p>There was only one explanation.</p>

<p>And Julian was afraid to accept it.</p>

<p>Did the Supreme Being of Death… infiltrate here?</p>

<p>Another chill crept down his spine, colder than anything he had ever faced before.</p>

<p>He shook his head sharply, forcing the thought away.</p>

<p>That’s not important, he told himself. First… I must get my hands on this.</p>

<p>His previously carefree demeanor — the calm, uninterested and lazy expression was gone, replaced now by urgency.</p>

<p>He leaned forward slightly, still suppressing the death energy.</p>

<p>Down below,</p>

<p>Fi Feng gestured grandly toward the tray.”And this,” he said with an overly cheerful tone,”is a pendant!”</p>

<p>There was a pause.</p>

<p>Then murmurs of disappointment.</p>

<p>“Just a pendant?” one voice whispered.</p>

<p>“That’s it?”</p>

<p>Someone else scoffed, “What do you mean ‘is a pendant’?!”</p>

<p>A louder voice surged through the crowd, filled with anger.”Fuck is this shit?! Wasting our time with junk!”</p>

<p>Laughter, and curses followed.</p>

<p>Fi Feng, ever the performer, laughed along with them.”Dear bidders, dear bidders, don’t lose heart so soon! This is merely a small checkpoint before the heavier treasures arrive!”</p>

<p>But even as he tried to please the crowd with charm, some voices didn’t give up.</p>

<p>“What checkpoint?!” someone snapped.”That’s not even a good accessory!”</p>

<p>Another voice called, “Just toss it down here, we’ll sell it for scrap!”</p>

<p>Fi Feng’s eyes narrowed slightly, and this time, his tone turned serious.</p>

<p>“The starting price,” he said clearly, cutting through the noise,”is ten mid-grade spiritual stones.”</p>

<p>The room quieted for a moment.</p>

<p>And then—</p>

<p>“Ten mid-grade?!”</p>

<p>“Are they serious?”</p>

<p>“What a scam!”</p>

<p>“They’re not even trying to hide it anymore!”</p>

<p>Suspicion, anger, and mockery filled the room.</p>

<p>But before the situation could escalate—</p>

<p>A calm voice echoed from the crowd below.</p>

<p>“Fifty mid-grade spiritual stones.”</p>

<p>Silence.</p>

<p>It came from the same young disciple who had dominated every bid before.</p>

<p>The moment the number left his lips, the entire hall hushed.</p>

<p>Some cultivators shifted uncomfortably in their seats.</p>

<p>“Fifty?””For that pendant…?””Is he mad, or does he know something we don’t?”</p>

<p>Confusion spread fast.</p>

<p>And then it got worse.</p>

<p>A second voice rang out. From above.</p>

<p>“One hundred mid-grade spiritual stones.”</p>

<p>The tone alone was enough to chill the room.Because everyone knew where it came from.</p>

<p>The Sapphire Lobby.</p>

<p>Gasps filled the hall.</p>

<p>“What?! Even the VIP joined in?”</p>

<p>“That’s the same person Fi Feng bowed to earlier!”</p>

<p>“Why the hell are two monsters bidding on a pendant that looks like junk?!”</p>

<p>A storm of murmurs followed, voices rising in pitch and shock.</p>

<p>“Wait—what is this treasure, really?”</p>

<p>“Could it be sealed?”</p>

<p>“A cursed item?”</p>

<p>“No… it must be something deeper… something hidden.”</p>

<p>The hall, once filled with hate for the simple pendant, now buzzed with a different energy.</p>

<p>The common cultivators didn’t dare move.The rich ones hesitated.The smart ones realized: they didn’t understand anything about what was happening.</p>

<p>Meanwhile,</p>

<p>Julian’s gaze slowly drifted from the pendant to the disguised Sect Leader, his head tilting slightly.His expression shifted from tension to curiosity.</p>

<p>He couldn’t tell.</p>

<p>Is she truly capable of sensing the death energy within it…? Or is she just bidding blind — caught in the heat of competition?</p>

<p>She gave nothing away. Her face was calm, focused as if she was waiting for this moment.</p>

<p>Then her voice rang out again:</p>

<p>“One thousand mid-grade spiritual stones.”</p>

<p>Gasps erupted instantly from every corner of the auction hall.</p>

<p>“What?!”</p>

<p>“One thousand?!”</p>

<p>“He has gone mad—”</p>

<p>“No, no one throws away that kind of wealth unless they’re certain. What the hell is that pendant?!”</p>

<p>Even Fi Feng’s eyebrows twitched slightly before he forced a grin, hiding his shock.</p>

<p>But above them all, Julian… chuckled.</p>

<p>How cute.</p>

<p>He slowly raised one hand, eyes still on the “young master” below.</p>

<p>“One thousand high-grade spiritual crystals.”</p>

<p>Time stopped.</p>

<p>The auction hall went silent.</p>

<p>Truly silent.</p>

<p>Even the sound of breathing seemed to vanish.</p>

<p>Not stones.</p>

<p>Crystals.</p>

<p>Not one thousand mid-grade stones — but one thousand high-grade crystals.</p>

<p>The kind that could fund a city for a decade.</p>

<p>The kind no sane person would throw away for a “simple” pendant.</p>

<p>“Did… did he say crystals?” someone whispered.</p>

<p>“High-grade…?”</p>

<p>“I-I don’t even know how much that’s worth…”</p>

<p>Fi Feng stared up at the Sapphire Lobby, his body tense, sweat trickling down his brow.He quickly cleared his throat, trying to recover his composure.</p>

<p>“One… one thousand high-grade crystals offered by our honored VIP!” he declared.</p>

<p>But no one clapped.</p>

<p>They couldn’t.</p>

<p>Because every single person now had the same terrifying thought in their heads:</p>

<p>What in heaven’s name is that pendant… and who the hell is this VIP?!</p>

<p>Meanwhile, even the disguised Sect Leader couldn’t hide her shock.</p>

<p>Her eyes widened slightly as she looked up at the Sapphire Lobby, where Julian sat in silence — a faint smirk curling at the corner of his lips.</p>

<p>Her hands clenched tightly.</p>

<p>She knew who the VIP was. Luoshi had brought him in — that strange monk with an odd charm and eerie calmness. She had suspected he was powerful… but this level of wealth?</p>

<p>Even for her, it was shocking.</p>

<p>Just who is this monk…?</p>

<p>Anger raged in her chest. But beneath it, a sharp stab of shame.</p>

<p>She was the Sect Leader of the Heavenly Moon Sect.</p>

<p>One of the Ten Great Sects.</p>

<p>A Saint.</p>

<p>She had cultivated for centuries. Commanded armies. Conquered forbidden realms.</p>

<p>And yet, here she was — being overwhelmed by a smiling monk who hadn’t even stood up from his chair.</p>
