<p>He wasn’t easily shaken, but even he had to admit—this was on a whole different level.</p>

<p>He leaned back in his chair, rubbing his chin as he reread the tasks. After a few moments of silence, he let out a sharp exhale and shook his head.</p>

<p>“What the fuck, bro? You did not hold back,” he said both in disbelief and admiration.</p>

<p>“But these cuckolding ones? Now that’s bold.”</p>

<p>The system responded, its tone carrying a hint of amusement. “Good luck, Host. This will be quite the challenge.”</p>
