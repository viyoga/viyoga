<p>Julian sat back, his gaze falling on Annie still asleep behind him. His heart steadied as he whispered, “Mother… I’ll see you soon.”</p>

<p>He took a long breath and closed his eyes once more. The room faded into silence until only the faint hum of Heaven’s energy surrounded him. He raised his palm and focused his mind, visualizing a lightning forming in his grasp.</p>

<p>Slowly, the energy began to stir. Threads of golden light drifted toward him, actively responding to his intent. They gathered around his hand, swirling lazily at first—then, as his concentration deepened, the light condensed.</p>

<p>crackle crackle</p>

<p>To Julian’s surprise, the golden light suddenly changed form, transforming into a bolt of lightning that crackled with energy between his fingers.</p>

<p>He studied it with quiet wonder. “Hm,” he murmured. “So Heaven’s energy can shift its nature depending on the wielder. It becomes what I will it to be.”</p>

<p>The realization made him smile faintly. This realm wasn’t rejecting him anymore—it was adapting to him. Accepting him.</p>

<p>With renewed focus, he shut his eyes again and let the lightning fade. He directed his awareness inward, diving deep into his consciousness, searching for the familiar connection of his own world. For a long moment, there was only stillness. Then, faintly, he felt it—the heartbeat of something vast.</p>

<p>A rush of energy surged through him, and suddenly his surroundings changed.</p>

<p>When he opened his eyes, he was no longer in the quiet inn room but standing beneath an endless sky floating with violet clouds.</p>

<p>The world before him was even more magnificent than when he’d left it.</p>

<p>Rolling hills stretched endlessly toward the horizon. Crystal-clear rivers wound through the valleys, their waters so pure that they reflected the violet sky above. Ancient trees—far taller than any he remembered planting—rose from the ground.</p>

<p>The air itself seemed vibrant.</p>

<p>Julian could feel a warm and welcoming sensation tingling in his skin, as if the very atmosphere recognized its creator.</p>

<p>Flowers he had never seen before bloomed wildly, their petals releasing fragrances that stirred something nostalgic in his memory.</p>

<p>But what truly surprised him were the inhabitants.</p>

<p>New creatures roamed the fields below. A group of four-legged animals with furs that shimmered like galaxies grazed peacefully near one of the rivers. Their movements were graceful, almost hypnotic, and occasionally one would lift its head to reveal eyes that glowed with an intelligence far beyond any common beast.</p>

<p>Julian’s eyes widened as he watched a massive bird-like creature soar overhead, its wings easily thirty feet. Its feathers changed colors—deep blue fading into purple, then gold, then back to blue again. When it cried out, the sound resonated through the air like a massive bell.</p>

<p>He closed his eyes and extended his consciousness toward the creatures below, feeling their energy.</p>

<p>“They’ve broken through… above Grand Mage realm,” he murmured, genuinely impressed.</p>

<p>The creatures had evolved beyond what should have been possible in his absence. Each one radiated a cultivation base that the humans from his original world were unable to reach even in thousands of years.</p>

<p>How long had he truly been gone?</p>

<p>His gaze swept across the landscape and found something even more unexpected: settlements.</p>

<p>Huts and buildings dotted the hillsides. They weren’t wild shelters thrown together for survival—these were homes. Some were built from smooth stone while others were constructed from wood, their surfaces carved with glowing runes.</p>

<p>Smoke rose from chimneys. Gardens flourished beside doorways. Paths had been formed between structures.</p>

<p>Julian rose silently into the air, his body moving without conscious thought. He made no sound as he floated above the largest village.</p>

<p>The last thing he wanted was to disturb whatever had grown here in his absence.</p>

<p>Humanoid creatures moved below, going about their daily activities. A group gathered around what appeared to be a well, drawing water in clay vessels that looked expertly crafted. Two others worked together to repair the roof of a building, moving with practiced coordination. Near the edge of the village, several younger ones—children, perhaps—played some kind of game that involved tossing a glowing sphere between them.</p>

<p>Each one radiated power that would put most mages to shame.</p>

<p>Julian descended slightly, close enough to observe details but still hidden to not aware them of his presence.</p>

<p>The beings below appeared human at first glance—two arms, two legs, faces with familiar features. But as he watched more carefully, he noticed the differences.</p>

<p>Their skin were unusual. Some had faint markings along their arms and necks—not tattoos, but patterns that seemed to be part of their very flesh, glowing softly.</p>

<p>One of them paused in their work and looked up, as if sensing something. The being’s eyes, scanned the sky for a long moment before returning to their task.</p>

<p>They were aware. Not just alive, not just intelligent, but truly conscious in a way that spoke of enlightenment.</p>

<p>For the next several minutes, Julian explored throughout his world, moving like a ghost through the realm he had created. Each new discovery left him more amazed than the last.</p>

<p>In the northern regions, he found beings that were fully human in appearance, their features so perfect they might have been sculpted by a master artist. They lived in larger settlements, with buildings that reached several stories high. He watched as one of them raised their hand and summoned fire with a casual gesture, using it to light a forge where another worked with a metal.</p>

<p>“Enchanted smithing,” Julian observed quietly. “They’ve developed crafts.”</p>

<p>To the east, near a great lake that sparkled like a diamond, he discovered beings that bore human features mixed with fish. Their skin was covered in scales. Some had fins along their forearms and backs, and when they dove into the water, they moved with the speed and grace of creatures born for the water.</p>

<p>In the western forests, he found beings with features that suggested both human and beasts ancestry. They moved through the trees silently, their large eyes capable of seeing small movements in the forest.</p>

<p>Their settlements were built among the branches, connected by bridges woven from vines.</p>

<p>Julian paused to watch a group of younglings being taught by an elder—the lesson appeared to be about cultivation techniques, judging by the way they sat in meditation while energy visibly circulated around their small forms.</p>

<p>“They’re teaching each other,” he breathed. “They’ve formed societies. Cultures.”</p>

<p>Everywhere he looked, life had flourished. His world hadn’t just survived in his absence—it had evolved, grown, become something far more complex than the simple realm he had left behind.</p>
