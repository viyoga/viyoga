<p>“So,” he whispered hesitantly. A shiver coursed through his body as he realized the truth.</p>

<p>“Those orbs… they weren’t just some strange phenomenon… they were the consciousness of the Supreme Beings.”</p>

<p>He paused, feeling his heart beating rapidly in his chest. “If they exist within the pixels—the very foundation of reality—then that means they are present everywhere… and are continuously monitoring everything in the universe.”</p>

<p>The words felt heavy as they left his lips. A flood of questions and uncertainties swirled within him.</p>

<p>What did it mean for his life? His freedom and his destiny? Would he remain an ant under their gaze?</p>

<p>He clenched his fists in determination. If these beings are watching me, then I will make them see my rise.</p>

<p>The fury in his eyes matched the fury of the lightning that had become his signature.</p>

<p>“The golden glow…” Julian continued, remembering the peaceful and calm golden circle that he had seen in the plane.</p>

<p>Its power was beyond his comprehension, and it seemed like the center through which the three energies of creation, preservation, and destruction were flowing.</p>

<p>“Could it be… the Mother of Everything?” he whispered. “The source that birthed it all…”</p>

<p>He stepped outside the castle and lifted his gaze to the sky. The morning sun was shining peacefully, but the world around him seemed unusually heavy.</p>

<p>A strange weight pressed on his shoulder, each step heavier than the last.</p>

<p>He glanced around and immediately sensed unseen eyes upon him, tracing his every move.</p>

<p>Goosebumps formed on his skin, and he fought the urge to turn around and confront an invisible watcher. The sensation grew stronger with each passing second, filling him with certainty that he was no longer alone.</p>

<p>He tried calming his racing heart, but no matter how much he tried, the feeling refused to fade.</p>

<p>“System,” he called out, but there was no response.</p>

<p>A tense silence filled the air. Ever since his encounter at Nu Village, it felt as though the system had shut down completely.</p>

<p>Just as he was about to give up, a familiar voice echoed in his mind.</p>

<p>“Hey, host.”</p>

<p>Julian blinked in surprise before a warm smile spread across his lips. “Took you long enough, System,” he said with a hint of relief in his tone.</p>

<p>“Well, host, you gave me quite a hard time barging into the den of one of the Supreme Beings like that,” the system said in a tone of exhaustion.</p>

<p>Julian chuckled, shaking his head. “You should have warned me.”</p>

<p>“Would that have changed anything!” the system shot back. “You were too busy being… you. Charging into danger headfirst, without considering what might be lurking behind the veil.”</p>

<p>“That’s part of my charm,” Julian teased, crossing his arms. “Besides, I survived, didn’t I?”</p>

<p>The system sighed. “Barely. If you keep tempting fate like that, you might not survive the next time.”</p>

<p>Julian grinned, clearly enjoying the banter with the system. “Good to have you back. Now, let’s talk about that plane I saw… and those orbs.”</p>

<p>The system hesitated for a moment before replying, “That’s not a simple matter, host. You have touched on forces beyond the ordinary… things that even I have limited data on.”</p>

<p>“You must know something, right?” Julian said with a smirk.</p>

<p>The system sighed, “Since you have already become part of this, I might as well let you in on some things I have data on.”</p>

<p>Julian’s smile widened with excitement. “Go on.”</p>

<p>The system’s voice grew more solemn. “Let’s start from the beginning,” it said.</p>

<p>“Before creation, there was nothing but infinite emptiness—a plane with no time, no space, no importance, and no meaning.”</p>

<p>My dream… Julian thought, immediately recalling the familiar scene from his dreams.</p>

<p>It was in that dream where he stood in a void that matched the system’s description.</p>

<p>The system continued, “Even in that vast emptiness, a strange flame flickered continuously. It burned with no fuel, no source, and no purpose. For an eternity, it simply existed.”</p>

<p>“And then?” Julian asked, leaning forward in interest.</p>

<p>“One day,” the system said dramatically, “that flame suddenly expanded and exploded. The infinite emptiness condensed and concentrated into a single, impossibly dense orb—an orb radiating a golden glow.”</p>

<p>Julian’s eyes widened as he pictured the scene vividly. “The orb… was that the source?” he asked.</p>

<p>“Yes,” the system confirmed. “It was the mother of everything. It then further split into three distinct forms of energy. You, Host, are quite familiar with them: the golden, the dark blue, and the red energies.”</p>

<p>Julian nodded slowly. He was well acquainted with these energies and was also using two of them, death and creation.</p>

<p>The system continued, “These energies reacted with and complemented one another, giving rise to the creation you now witness around you.”</p>

<p>It paused for a moment. “However, the creation in its infancy was not vast or complex like the world you inhabit today. It was small, simple, and devoid of life.”</p>

<p>Julian’s heart pounded rapidly in his chest as he absorbed each word.</p>

<p>“For millions of years, the energies coexisted, striving to evolve and shape a higher existence. But their efforts were fruitless. More millennia passed, and still, there was no breakthrough.”</p>

<p>Before Julian knew it, he was already clenching his fist in anticipation. “What happened next?” he asked.</p>

<p>The system paused, and its tone fell low, almost in respect. “In their persistence, each energy began to develop its own consciousness. A mind and a will of its own.”</p>

<p>They became more than raw power—they became entities. Beings of creation, preservation, and destruction.</p>

<p>Julian’s eyes narrowed. “Supreme beings?”</p>

<p>The system sighed as if sensing Julian’s rising tension. “Yes, Supreme Beings.”</p>

<p>“Each fulfills a unique role in the reality of higher existence. Creation exists solely to bring stability and order. Destruction exists solely for instability and chaos. Preservation creates a bridge between these two forces, maintaining balance and allowing for growth.”</p>

<p>Julian’s thoughts raced as he processed the enormity of what he had learned. “So, these three entities… they’re basically the most powerful?”</p>
