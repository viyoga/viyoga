<p>“And yearning?” she asked.</p>

<p>“Yearning,” Julian said, “is when another person’s existence starts to matter to you. When they are no longer just part of a calculation, not a piece or a pawn.”</p>

<p>He held her gaze.</p>

<p>“It is what makes you place someone above you.”</p>

<p>The candle between them flickered once and then settled again.</p>

<p>“That is either,” Olivia said carefully, “the most honest thing anyone has said to me in this house or the most sophisticated manipulation I’ve ever encountered.”</p>

<p>“Which do you think,” Julian almost smiled.</p>

<p>She stood. The movement brought her to her full height and closed some of the distance between them.</p>

<p>“I don’t know,” she said. “And that is the part that frightens me.”</p>

<p>“I frighten you,” Julian said.</p>

<p>“You have always frightened me,” she said, with a directness that clearly surprised her. “But before… I knew what I was afraid of. I knew exactly what you were, what you wanted and what you were capable of… Now I am not sure.”</p>

<p>She looked at him for a long moment.</p>

<p>Julian chuckled.</p>

<p>“Don’t think about it too much, mother,” he said.</p>

<p>She looked at him with an expression that suggested this was the least useful advice she had ever received.</p>

<p>“How,” she said. “How do I not think about it after everything you just said.” She stopped.</p>

<p>Something moved through her face — a brief, internal collision — and then the words arrived before she had fully controlled them.</p>

<p>“My own son telling me he yearns for me. What do I do with that information. Where do I put it.”</p>

<p>The room went quiet.</p>

<p>Julian said nothing.</p>

<p>He let the silence continue for long moment, and it did exactly what silence did when given enough room — it turned back on the person who had spoken. He watched as Olivia slowly realized what she had just said.</p>

<p>She quickly looked away. a</p>

<p>Her jaw tightened once and a faint color bloomed in her face.</p>

<p>She was embarrassed.</p>

<p>Julian watched this happen and said nothing for several long moments.</p>

<p>Then, quietly: “Mother.”</p>

<p>She looked at him. Her eyes were direct despite the embarrassment.</p>

<p>“I have always yearned for you,” Julian said. “That is true and I won’t pretend otherwise. It cannot be changed or undone or made into something it isn’t.”</p>

<p>Olivia stood very still.</p>

<p>“But what was true before,” he continued, “was that I didn’t see the lines. I didn’t acknowledge the boundaries that exist for reasons and those lines don’t bend just because I want them to.”</p>

<p>He held her gaze.</p>

<p>“That person is gone,” he said. “I am very aware of the limits now. I know precisely where they are and I know precisely why they exist and I have no intention of crossing them.” He paused. “So you don’t have to be afraid of me. That specific fear — you can put it down.”</p>

<p>Olivia looked at him. Her mind was racing right now.</p>

<p>“With time,” Julian said, more quietly, “it will pass. Like everything passes.”</p>

<p>Olivia said nothing for a long moment.</p>

<p>She looked at the candle on the table. At the window where the duchy sat in its complete dark. At the floor between them.</p>

<p>When she finally looked back at him, her expression had settled into something more comfortable then before.</p>

<p>“You confessed that very easily,” she said finally.</p>

<p>“You deserved the truth more than you deserved my comfort,” Julian said.</p>

<p>She nodded.</p>

<p>“That is either wisdom,” she said, “or cruelty dressed as wisdom.”</p>

<p>“Which do you think,” Julian said.</p>

<p>She looked at him for a long moment.</p>

<p>“I think,” she said carefully, “that you are the most complicated thing that has ever walked into this family. And this family has produced some genuinely complicated things.”</p>

<p>Julian almost smiled.</p>

<p>She smoothed her dress, making sure there were no creases and looked at him one final time.</p>

<p>“Do not get yourself killed,” she said.</p>

<p>“The duchy needs you,” she continued. “Your father needs you, even if he doesn’t know how to say it. Vanessa needs you.” She paused. “And whatever this is—”</p>

<p>She gestured slightly between them.</p>

<p>“Whatever complicated thing this is — it is worth considerably less if you are dead.”</p>

<p>Julian looked at her.</p>

<p>“I don’t intend to die,” he said.</p>

<p>“Kraven never intended anything bad either,” she said quietly. “He simply did it.”</p>

<p>She held his gaze for one final moment.</p>

<p>Then she turned, walked to the door and stepped out without pausing. The door closed behind her and her footsteps moved down the corridor until they gradually faded.</p>

<p>Julian sat in the dark room for a long time without moving. a</p>

<p>That was more taxing than I expected.</p>

<p>The political conversations were simple enough. The confrontation with the soldiers, the exchange with Kraven’s father, Vanessa— all of it required attention to not sound out of place. Julian was well capable of keeping it under control</p>

<p>But this was different.</p>

<p>He walked to the bed, fell down on the soft mattress and stared at the ceiling he couldn’t see in the complete dark. The candle had long since burned out, leaving the room empty and blind.</p>

<p>For a while, his thoughts drifted without direction.</p>

<p>Then, slowly, something began to take shape.</p>

<p>Kraven’s soul is gone.</p>

<p>Julian had seen it himself. He watched the last trace of it fade away when he had filled the vacant space with his own will. He was certain of it.</p>

<p>I replaced him.</p>

<p>He was certain of that.</p>

<p>And yet—</p>

<p>Again and again, something felt… wrong.</p>

<p>Kraven kept interfering.</p>

<p>Not in a way Julian could see or name clearly. It wasn’t a voice or memory. It was faint but persistent, like something beneath the surface refusing to stay buried.</p>

<p>Why is this happening?</p>

<p>His thoughts sharpened.</p>

<p>It shouldn’t be possible. There’s nothing left of him.</p>

<p>He went over it again, step by step, as if reexamining a calculation.</p>

<p>The soul is what carries identity. Thought. Desire. Intention.</p>

<p>And Kraven’s soul was gone. So what was this?</p>
