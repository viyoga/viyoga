<p>The calmness with which the voice spoke, the chilling authority, and the temptation all wrapped around Julian, making the heat in his body rise.</p>

<p>His gaze fell once more on Aria and Harith, then drifted back to the villagers as their acts continued. The aura in the room pulsed with a magnetic energy, and he felt its pull more strongly now.</p>

<p>“Join us,” the voice whispered again.</p>

<p>Julian calmed himself, not giving in to the temptations. He glanced around, but he could not find anything unusual. His heart raced, and he tried to focus, pushing aside the rising panic.</p>

<p>“Concentrate,” he told himself.</p>

<p>He closed his eyes, took a deep breath, and then focused his mind as intensely as he could. When he opened his eyes again, the surroundings had transformed, filled by the tiny, overwhelming number of pixels.</p>

<p>He desperately searched the space, hoping to see anything out of the ordinary, but all he saw were black pixels, swirling and pulsing with a dark glow.</p>

<p>Then, as his gaze swept back to the center of the cavern, his eyes widened in disbelief. Previously, the shadow clinging to Amir was nothing but a concentration of some weird energy, but now, it was different.</p>

<p>Julian could faintly see the seven distinct pixels within it, swirling and interacting with each other.</p>

<p>He stumbled back as his mind struggled to grasp what he was seeing. “What is that? How can this be?” His voice was barely a whisper, his legs trembling as he fought to stay upright.</p>

<p>Am I hallucinating? Am I dreaming? he thought, his hands shaking as he reached for something to hold onto.</p>

<p>He inhaled deeply, trying to calm himself, but his mind refused to settle. His eyes snapped back to the shadow, and the strange pattern of seven pixels now seemed even more clear to him.</p>

<p>Why does that shadow have all seven pixels? he thought, the question clouding his mind as it refused to believe it. And why… why are they existing in harmony?</p>

<p>He watched in stunned silence as the seven distinct elements—fire, water, earth, air, light, darkness, and lightning—coexisted peacefully, each maintaining its presence without disrupting the balance of the others.</p>

<p>Why does it seem like it has unified all seven elements?</p>

<p>To Julian, whose one of the ultimate goals was to unify the seven elements, this hit harder than he could have ever expected. He had researched the concept and even experienced firsthand how difficult—no, impossible—it was to achieve the equilibrium between the seven elements.</p>

<p>The complexity of balancing the chaotic forces of the seven elements made the task seem beyond the reach of even the greatest mages. But now, as he stood before the shadow, the truth stared back at him, and he could not deny it anymore.</p>

<p>The seven elements were merged, existing not as separate entities but as a single, unified energy.</p>

<p>What is this ritual? His voice cracked and his composure completely shattered.</p>

<p>And then, it hit him. The realization was so powerful, so shocking, that it left him frozen in place. The being that’s possibly being summoned…</p>

<p>The words formed in his mind, each one a puzzle piece falling into place. A being that could control all the elements as a single, unified power…</p>

<p>God… Julian thought, his heart racing. Creation. Destruction. And… the final piece. It’s him.</p>

<p>The realization struck like a thunder shock. The ritual wasn’t simply summoning a god—it was summoning something that could reshape the very fabric of existence, something that could wield the very elements as one.</p>

<p>The power that was about to be unleashed was beyond anything Julian had ever encountered, and his body trembled in both fear and awe at the scale of what was unfolding.</p>

<p>“This energy…” Julian whispered, his voice shaking with awe and fear. It’s not just any energy.</p>

<p>He turned his gaze back to the platform, where the energy surged violently. Goosebumps crawled over his body as the full scope of its power began to settle in his mind. It’s the unification of all elements.</p>

<p>No wonder I was discovered. The voice, the strange force that had reached into his mind, wasn’t some random entity; it had been waiting and watching his every move.</p>

<p>It’s as if my fate was already sealed the moment I stepped into this village. His goosebumps intensified. No… he paused. Maybe it was the moment I stepped into this forest.</p>

<p>He thought of running, escaping out of this cage, but the thought quickly dissipated. Futile resistance, he thought, shaking his head.</p>

<p>Whatever was about to happen, he was already a part of it, whether he wanted to be or not.</p>

<p>The voice echoed in Julian’s mind again, more insistent this time. “Come forward; be part of this ritual.”</p>

<p>Julian stood there silently, taking in the overwhelming revelation. After a brief moment, he opened his mouth.</p>

<p>“What is this about? What exactly is this ritual?” He asked, “I will join only after you tell me what’s happening.”</p>

<p>The voice let out a low, almost mocking laugh. “It’s been a while since I have seen a demigod argue with me. You sure have balls.”</p>

<p>Julian frowned in confusion. “Demigod? What is that?” he asked. He had never heard of such a thing, at least not in any context that made sense to him.</p>

<p>The voice laughed again, this time with a hint of amusement mixed with pity. “You don’t even know who you are, do you now?”</p>

<p>Julian’s breath caught in his throat as the weight of the words hit him. What does that mean? His mind searched for clarity, but everything around him felt like it was slipping further into an incomprehensible maze.</p>

<p>Desperate for answers, he reached out mentally and tried contacting the system that had always guided him.</p>

<p>“Hey system, are you there?” he said, but there was nothing, no reply or response.</p>

<p>He reached out again, but still, no response. It was as if the connection with the system had been severed.</p>
