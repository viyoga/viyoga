Instead of taking the scroll, Alex poured himself some tea and while doing so, asked, "It's for me?"

"No." Lucifer said it straight. "It's for me. Go through it."

Alex put the teapot down and taking hold of the teacup, asked calmly, "Having some trust issues with her on something?"

Being asked two questions in a row and not doing what Lucifer had asked to do would incite a major reaction from her.

Today, Lucifer simply shook her head and said, "No, I just want you to go through it."

Alex's calm gaze scanned through the scroll's content and a surprised shimmer flashed in the blue oceanic eyes of his.

It didn't go unnoticed by Lucifer and a small smirk formed on her face.
