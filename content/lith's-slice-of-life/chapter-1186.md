The VSOL universe:




Here you have it, at last, in a clear manner, what the universe of VSOL exactly is.




I've not been able to dive into world building because of writing the war plot.




Honestly, I very much like world building and lore and I just can't wait to share it with you guys.




These don't have any spoilers by the way, so you can read them without worries.




Now, let's unpack what the VSOL universe has.




The VSOL universe is divided into two parts: Upper and Lower. That's it. Really.




Since the start of the novel, I've kept things relatively simple and the Vsol universe is just as simple too.




However, here comes the complex part.




You all know about the 12 elements, right?




They were Fire, Water, Earth, Wind, Lightning, Light, Life, Death, Dark, Destruction, Space, and Time.




The upper realm has a single plane in which these twelve elements are present in "zones".




Now, if you think of zones, there should be some boundary or something at the center, right?




There's indeed something important at the center.




These zones are present around this important center but in a very complex geometrical topography.




One would think that one can only find Dark element in the Dark zone, but well, at the edge of the Dark zone, as it shares boundary with the Death zone, you can find Dark and Death both.




Now, what if I tell you, the dimensions are so mind boggling and complex of these zones that you can even find overlap not between one or two of these zones, but all twelve at certain places!




Dimension is a very weird thing and deciphering it is very strange too.




Anywho, that's the world building of the upper realm. It wasn't a spoiler because the plot revolves around that "center" I talked about. It has a name. It has an importance. You'll find out in later chapters once we are done with this arc.




And also, all living beings of the upper realm reside strictly in these zones.




The "center" is empty.




Why?




You'll find you.




And I can't dive much deeper into it, because a scholar(me) once said:
