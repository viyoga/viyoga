Lith had no idea how to react. Should he be sad or angry knowing that his mother almost got raped in the past, be happy that she was strong or be shocked that she could kill Supreme ranks so easily.

His mind was in turmoil, today he was getting shocks after shocks. He never would’ve believed that his mother had such a past and his own story of being born would be like this.
