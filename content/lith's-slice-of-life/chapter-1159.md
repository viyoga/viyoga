"Everybody around him calls him that, so I felt I should too. He's quite older and it feels like I'm talking to an ancient being, so calling him brother Noman doesn't feel right." Hyunsuk answered frankly.

Sylvia was a quiet person like Keith, but occasionally with Fei, she would argue and speak a bit.
