"Continuing on with the broadcast, the church has some more important news." The white beard man said.

"However, with this broadcast, the church is sure that the devil will take an active effort to change himself. So, it matters not if the image is seen or not. Hence, here's the image…"

The scene on the fountain changed and what the people saw was a man with black hair and red eyes, showing a serious face.

Something wasn't right.

There was no way the church was this advanced here to find Saint Rank beings so easily. Not to mention keeping them chained.

Lastly, Ralph was chained and being tortured by these fools.
