The axe swung and beat the hardwood, chopping a good part of it away.

It took time and effort to chop down trees to make a kayak and a paddle.

Lith was almost done chopping down the wood and all that was left was to shape it.

Meanwhile on Mayzin's side, beads of sweat formed on her forehead as she used all her strength into chopping trees.

Her body had great strength, no doubt, but by being suppressed like this and suddenly getting magic taken away, things were slightly difficult.

Lith had somewhat of an advantage over Mayzin due to this and was leading.

It took her ten minutes to carve out the kayak and it slightly was off the mark as Mayzin had never seen a kayak before and made one from whatever rough idea she had.

She had to race Lith down the water stream and knowing that was enough to give Mayzin an idea as to what shape a boat should be made of.

"Alright… 3… 2… 1, go!"
