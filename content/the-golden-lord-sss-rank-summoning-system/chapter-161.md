<p>"I don’t want to have to kill someone so young," Aerick muttered, calming his aura.</p>
<p>"And I don’t want to have to kill a man who doesn’t deserve to die," Ethan replied. "But it’s a cruel world, isn’t it?"</p>
<p>A dry, bitter laugh escaped Aerick’s lips at those words.</p>
<p>He glanced over his shoulder, noticing that his wife had finally reached the wall.</p>
<p>Her tear-filled eyes were fixed on him, her hands clenching the wood as if she meant to crack it.</p>
<p>"Yes. It’s a cruel world."</p>
<p>The two Lords exchanged one last glance, and then they advanced.</p>
<p>The metallic clang of swords began to echo around them without ceasing, while Ethan’s two wives and Aerick’s wife watched, their eyes trembling slightly with every powerful impact.</p>
<p>Naturally, none of them wanted to lose their man in a duel like this.</p>
<p>"I’m sorry, young man, but I can’t die yet!" Aerick roared, his blade scraping along Ethan’s breastplate and tearing off a sliver of metal with a piercing shriek.</p>
<p>"I say the same, old man!" Ethan shouted back, twisting his body and swinging his sword toward Aerick’s neck.</p>
<p>Realizing the danger, Aerick raised his left hand, channeling every drop of his aura into the gauntlet and strengthening it with everything he had.</p>
<p>Ethan’s sword sank into the metal and the chainmail beneath, yet only managed to draw a single drop of blood.</p>
<p>The two men leaped back.</p>
<p>Their faces were covered, only their eyes visible through the slits in their helmets, staring coldly at one another across the gap.</p>
<p>Aerick ignored the throbbing pain in his arm, taking stock of his aura reserves. He shouldn’t have more than eighty percent left, perhaps less.</p>
<p>Ethan did the same, confirming that he still carried about ninety percent of his own aura.</p>
<p>He took a step forward, his eyes shining with the most intense murderous intent he could muster.</p>
<p>Aerick responded in kind, his eyes ablaze with hatred.</p>
<p>Their swords met once.</p>
<p>And then again.</p>
<p>Again.</p>
<p>Again.</p>
<p>With each strike, the air around them expanded in muffled explosions, their auras colliding ceaselessly, sparks of aura dancing between the blades.</p>
<p>It was only on the twentieth strike that Aerick managed to hit Ethan, his sword tearing through the helmet and drawing a spurt of warm blood.</p>
<p>The next moment, however, he realized his mistake as Ethan moved.</p>
<p>His arm was pinned in place, and a powerful punch crashed into his face, knocking the helmet from his head with a hollow clang.</p>
<p>A second punch nearly crushed his jaw before he managed to stumble back, shoving Ethan away with all the strength he had left.</p>
<p>Pain spread through every inch of his face.</p>
<p>If not for the strength of a third-stage warrior, he would already be disfigured.</p>
<p>Aerick planted his feet on the ground, which cracked beneath his weight, sinking into a crater a few centimeters deep, the fractures spreading outward for about five meters.</p>
<p>His aura exploded, and behind him, to Ethan’s shock, something emerged.</p>
<p>A dragon, made of nothing but aura, yet carrying a terrifying power, like a guardian.</p>
<p>But it was not a Western dragon, with four legs.</p>
<p>It was an Eastern dragon, a horned, whiskered serpent, roaring toward Ethan with a sound that made the very air vibrate.</p>
<p>"I’m sorry, master, but I’ll have to reveal your secret technique to save my insignificant life..."</p>
<p>"It’s incomplete." Ethan’s words echoed across the twenty or so meters between them, and Aerick nodded.</p>
<p>The dragon had started strong, but was already losing its power, its form on the verge of shattering into luminous fragments.</p>
<p>"My master did not give me permission to use this ability unless I reached the fifth stage."</p>
<p>"It’s impossible with your talent."</p>
<p>Aerick nodded again.</p>
<p>"But it doesn’t matter."</p>
<p>His body turned into a blur, his strength, once inferior, now matching Ethan’s.</p>
<p>But that was hardly enough.</p>
<p>He didn’t understand who Ethan was.</p>
<p>The handsome young man planted his feet with calm.</p>
<p>His eyes grew cold, and then, from beneath his feet, a sea of blood surged forth, spreading across the earth in crimson waves.</p>
<p>"Aerick!" The scream tore from Aerick’s wife’s throat.</p>
<p>His short black hair danced in the wind.</p>
<p>His cold eyes locked onto Ethan’s.</p>
<p>His blade sliced through the air with the power of a dragon, leaving a bluish trail behind it, but that was not enough.</p>
<p>His feet touched the ground, and then Ethan raised a hand.</p>
<p>A spear of blood appeared, sinking into Aerick’s belly, tearing through his armor and lifting him into the air like a dry leaf.</p>
<p>The dragon disintegrated into fragments of light, its aura still striking Ethan with a terrible heat.</p>
<p>Perhaps, if he had known what was coming from below, Aerick might have defended himself.</p>
<p>But that ability, clearly originating from the continent beyond the seas, consumed a terrifying amount of mental strength.</p>
<p>He had forced his eyes onto Ethan and only Ethan, completely forgetting everything else.</p>
<p>With a simple gesture, Ethan dissolved the spear of blood.</p>
<p>Aerick’s legs gave way, and he fell to the ground, his knees sinking into the broken earth.</p>
<p>His eyes rose to Ethan as he clutched his bloodied stomach with trembling hands.</p>
<p>He wanted to cover his ears. He wanted to stop hearing his wife’s cries.</p>
<p>But he didn’t.</p>
<p>Slowly, Ethan removed his helmet, revealing the face where Aerick’s sword had slashed.</p>
<p>There wasn’t a single wound.</p>
<p>"Are you afraid of dying? Of leaving your wife and son behind?" Ethan asked, his gaze drifting to the boy in Aerick’s wife’s arms.</p>
<p>Aerick himself was a Rank A talent, but the boy was a Rank S talent.</p>
<p>His value was inestimable, and if allowed to grow, he could become a major combat force for Goldenveil.</p>
<p>"What does that matter?" Aerick used his remaining strength to tear at the straps holding the breastplate in place.</p>
<p>When the metal fell to the ground with a heavy thud, his belly lay exposed, his torn guts glistening wetly in the dim light.</p>
<p>Even for a third-stage warrior, without a great healer or an unparalleled healing potion, this was death.</p>
<p>"I’m already dead anyway."</p>
<p>"You’re not dead." Ethan cut him off, pulling one of the divine healing potions from his spatial ring and holding it out to Aerick.</p>
