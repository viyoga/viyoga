<p>It was a wolf.</p>
<p>But not just any wolf.</p>
<p>No... it was more like a demon, as heavy as a bull, with claws the size of daggers and teeth so sharp they looked like needles.</p>
<p>It was a mana beast, and one of the worst kind: a corrupted mana beast.</p>
<p>Mana beasts weren’t naturally vile creatures, but when corrupted, they became the embodiment of demons on earth, capable of little more than killing and killing.</p>
<p>Why on earth was a beast like that there?</p>
<p>The young warrior felt his heart leap into his throat, his legs trembling beneath him.</p>
<p>If it were his father standing there, a single slash would have been enough to solve the problem.</p>
<p>But he wasn’t his father.</p>
<p>He was just a foolish, impulsive young man.</p>
<p>He tried to grab his sword, but before his hand could even reach the hilt, the beast bit down.</p>
<p>Blood spurted through the air and his body convulsed, everything above his chest torn away by the creature’s bite.</p>
<p>The beast was second-stage, just like him, so he might have had a chance to fight if he’d been prepared.</p>
<p>Maybe he could even have fled, using his companions as bait, since the beast would go after the easier prey first.</p>
<p>Unfortunately, fear overcame him, and he couldn’t even react.</p>
<p>That was the difference between a trained, experienced soldier and a young noble who had never seen the world.</p>
<p>The other four froze in terror, their eyes wide.</p>
<p>They were older and had seen a lot, but a corrupted mana beast? No.</p>
<p>The wolf’s jaws snapped open, letting out a satisfied growl, like the snarl of a demon come to drag them into the depths of hell.</p>
<p>The beast paused for a moment, writhing, and then lunged forward with its jaws wide open.</p>
<p>Its teeth closed around the torso of another of the four men, not stopping until they met at the center.</p>
<p>His legs fell to one side of its jaws. His head, shoulders, and arms, to the other.</p>
<p>A single bite, and there wasn’t much left to bury.</p>
<p>"R-run! Everyone in a different direction!"</p>
<p>The remaining three finally reacted, bolting in desperation.</p>
<p>Whatever that thing was, they were fucked, too fucked for any other option.</p>
<p>The beast howled, its body trembling with excitement, lunging after the fattest one first.</p>
<p>A scream filled with pain echoed. With its front paws, it struck him in the back, its claws tearing through the flesh with ease; its weight crushing him against the ground with a dull thud.</p>
<p>It wasn’t killing out of hunger. It was having fun.</p>
<p>Finishing off the fat one, the wolf raised its snout, sensing the scent of the other two, before charging at the maximum speed a second-stage mana beast could reach.</p>
<p>"Shit..." One of the adventurers gasped, filled with terror, as another scream echoed behind him.</p>
<p>The chubby one had died, and now another.</p>
<p>Which meant he was the last one.</p>
<p>He ran with everything he had, channeling every drop of aura into his legs. Behind him, heavy footsteps echoed closer and closer, shaking the ground.</p>
<p>When he finally felt the beast’s hot breath on his back, he knew it: it was over.</p>
<p>He closed his eyes and threw himself forward.</p>
<p>The beast leaped forward as well, its jaws opening wide, ready to devour its prey.</p>
<p>’Damn... why does it have to end like this?’</p>
<p>The man had barely finished the thought when he heard a different sound.</p>
<p>Not the sound of fangs tearing flesh, but the clang of steel against teeth.</p>
<p>When he opened his eyes and turned, there was a man between him and the beast, sword in hand, the blade sunk between the wolf’s teeth.</p>
<p>"Captain Doran!"</p>
<p>Another soldier appeared, freezing the moment he saw the wolf.</p>
<p>"Get back!" Doran roared, and with a surge of superhuman strength, he hurled the wolf backward.</p>
<p>His body was engulfed in a fiery red, his attribute points rising rapidly.</p>
<p>The wolf stopped a few meters away, growled, and then charged again.</p>
<p>"Shit..." Doran grunted as the beast passed him, biting at empty air.</p>
<p>With no room to use his sword, he punched, his fist crashing down on the beast’s skull with full force.</p>
<p>A crash echoed and the wolf staggered away, slamming into a tree. It was clearly dazed, its vision spinning as its brain rattled inside its head.</p>
<p>Doran already possessed very high Strength compared to his other base attributes, just slightly above Ethan’s Strength, despite his total base attributes being much lower.</p>
<p>With the Burning Blood body-enhancing technique, that Strength multiplied even further, reaching one hundred and thirty.</p>
<p>That sight brought immediate shock to the man on the ground.</p>
<p>That guy had nearly knocked out that cruel beast with a single punch!</p>
<p>He was saved! Saved!</p>
<p>Unfortunately, he didn’t know that Doran didn’t share the same excitement.</p>
<p>"Call for the Lord." He growled at the guards accompanying him, gripping his sword firmly.</p>
<p>The beast had already recovered, and from now on, it would no longer treat Doran as just another easy prey. That made him anxious.</p>
<p>Mana beasts were intelligent and naturally far more powerful than humans. If that one fought carefully, he would be dead.</p>
<p>The wolf circled Doran with sharp eyes, calmly sizing him up.</p>
<p>Doran wasn’t a sheep; he was like the beast, a predator, or a shepherd, to be more precise, and the beast realized this quickly.</p>
<p>"I received a grand gift from my lord this morning, and I won’t waste it on a pointless death, so don’t think you’ll defeat me easily, foul beast!" Doran roared, even though he knew a beast of such a low stage wouldn’t understand human speech.</p>
<p>The wolf growled in irritation and then turned away, casting one last glance, as if warning that it would return.</p>
<p>"Ah... shit." Doran gave a bitter laugh, sheathed his sword, and turned to the man sitting on the ground behind him.</p>
<p>About twenty-six years old, he had a well-groomed beard, dark eyes, and an expression still filled with terror.</p>
<p>"Come with me. You have much to report to my lord," Doran said coldly.</p>
<p>That beast couldn’t be allowed to roam freely around Goldenveil. It needed to be killed.</p>
