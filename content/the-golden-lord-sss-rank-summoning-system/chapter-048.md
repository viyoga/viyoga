<p>Ethan Goldenveil wasn’t sure yet whether the system would consider Alaric a worthy opponent for a fair duel, since he was injured.</p>
<p>But he wanted to test it anyway.</p>
<p>Ethan was also wounded, and by the end, both of them had expended a great deal of Aura.</p>
<p>’I should have enough to use the Burning Blood Body Enhancement Skill and a single Aura Slash,’ he thought, his eyes fixed on Alaric’s.</p>
<p>Just behind the captain of the Goldenveil guard, if he could still be called that, one of his followers tried to flee.</p>
<p>He barely took three steps before having his throat slit by one of the summoned guards.</p>
<p>"Where did you get these men?" Evelyn asked, impressed.</p>
<p>She was already relaxed, despite the initial confusion that had overtaken her face.</p>
<p>"A man has his secrets." Ethan chuckled. "Now rest a bit. I’ll deal with this pig."</p>
<p>Under Ethan’s gaze, Alaric hesitated.</p>
<p>Nobles didn’t duel to the death against commoners.</p>
<p>At least not normally.</p>
<p>For nobles, a duel to the death was a divine judgment.</p>
<p>Winning meant, in a way, being declared the chosen one of the gods. After all, the gods wouldn’t hand defeat to one who carries the truth in his heart, would they?</p>
<p>A trial by combat was, too, a judgment by the gods.</p>
<p>How could a noble, someone whose blood was blessed by the gods themselves, put himself in the position of dueling a commoner?</p>
<p>And yet, there was Ethan, offering Alaric the chance of a duel.</p>
<p>Wasn’t that madness?</p>
<p>A flood of thoughts washed over Alaric’s face, but in the end, all he could do was sigh and prepare his sword.</p>
<p>That wasn’t necessarily a bad thing...</p>
<p>That might be the only chance he had!</p>
<p>If he killed Ethan, everything would be settled, wouldn’t it?</p>
<p>Alaric’s eyes flashed. He didn’t even care that his men were dying one by one as they tried to flee, their screams of agony echoing all around.</p>
<p>Ethan’s summoned guards showed no mercy, but neither would Alaric.</p>
<p>Gritting his teeth, he raised his sword.</p>
<p>"If you want to die that badly, brat... I’ll show you how the real world works!"</p>
<p>"Very well, come on!"</p>
<p>The two roared at the same time, and, stepping forward, Ethan activated the burning blood Body Enhancement Skill, swinging his sword to parry Alaric’s descending blow.</p>
<p>A metallic clang echoed as the two blades met, dust rising around them, steel sliding against steel.</p>
<p>"You... what kind of sorcery is this?" Alaric took a step back, feeling a metallic tremor spread through his arms.</p>
<p>Ethan, standing before him, glowed with a terrifying crimson aura, exuding murderous intent.</p>
<p>"A dead man needs to know nothing." Ethan advanced again, stomping hard on the ground before gripping his sword with both hands and launching an upward slash.</p>
<p>Alaric leaned back, striking his sword against the side of Ethan’s, deflecting it to the side and opening the boy’s guard.</p>
<p>"Shit..." Ethan turned to the side as Alaric drew a dagger from his waist with his free hand, maintaining control of Ethan’s sword with the other.</p>
<p>The dagger aimed for the heart but struck the shoulder, drawing a pained grunt from Ethan, who planted his right foot on the ground and kicked toward Alaric’s face.</p>
<p>A crash echoed and Alaric’s head was sent flying backward, Ethan’s sword sliding past his as Ethan stepped back.</p>
<p>"Ah... son of a bitch..." Alaric shifted his aura, clearing his vision blurred by the impact and spitting blood onto the floor, two teeth coming out along with the crimson liquid.</p>
<p>He wiped his bloodied mouth with his shirt sleeve, keeping a cold stare on Ethan.</p>
<p>’90 seconds...’ Ethan counted down the time remaining until the Burning Blood body enhancement technique wore off.</p>
<p>His mana reserves were half gone, and his stamina wasn’t much better either, but fortunately, the same was true for Alaric.</p>
<p>If they were both screwed, then they were both even, right?</p>
<p>The two stared each other down for a moment before charging forward at the same time, blades aimed at each other’s necks.</p>
<p>Alaric attacked with a downward slash from left to right while Ethan swung his blade in the opposite direction.</p>
<p>Evelyn, who was watching from the sidelines, couldn’t help but close her eyes for a brief moment as the sharp sound of steel meeting steel echoed, a burst of metallic sparks scattering in the space between them.</p>
<p>Once again, neither of them managed to gain the upper hand.</p>
<p>’Don’t you dare lose...’ She clenched her perfect, pearly teeth, her fist clenched so tightly that her own fingernail tore the skin of her palm.</p>
<p>Blood dripped onto the floor beneath her as she steeled her resolve.</p>
<p>Suddenly, she understood perfectly how Alice felt.</p>
<p>It was hard to imagine losing a man like Ethan, and even harder to imagine what her life would be like if he were to die there.</p>
<p>Planting her feet firmly on the ground, she prepared to intervene as soon as necessary.</p>
<p>She wouldn’t go home without her man!</p>
<p>Ethan felt Evelyn’s burning gaze on his back.</p>
<p>He knew what she was thinking, a soft smile spreading across his lips.</p>
<p>It was time to end this fight.</p>
<p>"You’re strong, kid, but this sorcery won’t last forever! Sooner or later, you’ll go back to being just another pathetic first-stage!" Alaric roared with all the strength in his lungs.</p>
<p>Even as a second-stage warrior, practically every type of aura technique was completely out of his reach, just as Goldenveil’s poverty was.</p>
<p>How could he see that technique that made Ethan’s body explode with power and be engulfed by a crimson aura, if not as witchcraft?</p>
<p>"You talk too much, worm." Ethan shook his head, checking his mana reserve.</p>
<p>One hundred and twenty points.</p>
<p>It wasn’t much, but it was all he needed.</p>
<p>With a long sigh, he took a step forward, the blade slicing through the air and leaving a crimson trail behind, like a crescent-shaped cut spreading toward Alaric, aiming for his chest.</p>
