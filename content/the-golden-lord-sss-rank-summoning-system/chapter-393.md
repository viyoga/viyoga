<p>Ethan wanted Lord Fallenwinter alive.</p>
<p>He believed Zael Fallenwinter was a capable leader who, if handled well, could become one of the pillars supporting Goldenveil’s future.</p>
<p>If that proved impossible, however, Ethan would not hesitate to kill him.</p>
<p>The young lord let out a low growl, his grip on the sword firm as he climbed onto a small platform where the five mages who would participate in the initial phase of the battle were already positioned.</p>
<p>His blue eyes were cold as steel, and his fingers tightened around the hilt of the God-Slayer.</p>
<p>A sharp hiss echoed as the blade slid free of its sheath, revealing the gleaming metal bit by bit.</p>
<p>Ethan’s eyes flashed gold, his lips parting. "Shields!" he roared.</p>
<p>The soldiers did not hesitate, raising their shields to form an almost instantaneous wall of metal, the coordinated thud echoing across the field like a single clap of thunder.</p>
<p>In the next instant, from the forest below, a single spear—clearly hastily and clumsily fashioned—sliced through the air with a harsh whine.</p>
<p>Following it, thousands more.</p>
<p>They rained down upon the shields of the Goldenveil army in a deafening downpour, shattering against the sturdy metal in a chaotic symphony of shards and splintered wood.</p>
<p>Even those few that slipped past the shields could do nothing more than leave faint scratches upon the armor.</p>
<p>It wasn’t that the spears lacked force, but their quality was, well... just poorly crafted sharp pieces of wood.</p>
<p>Ethan swung his sword in a fluid motion, slicing a spear aimed at him clean in half. The two halves fell uselessly at his feet.</p>
<p>Scattered neatly across the ground among the soldiers lay javelins as well—short shafts of carefully polished black wood tipped with gold, sharp enough to cut through the air itself.</p>
<p>"Ready your javelins!" the centurions shouted from within their ranks, the order echoing from shield to shield.</p>
<p>The soldiers crouched in practiced movements, picking up the weapons from the ground while keeping their swords sheathed at their sides.</p>
<p>At a second command from the centurions, their arms rose in unison and the golden tips pointed toward the forest.</p>
<p>In the next instant, the first green skin burst forth from among the trees, charging madly toward them, its yellow eyes gleaming with a thirst for blood.</p>
<p>Following the first came a second, then a third, and in a matter of moments, a large mass of them poured out onto the plain.</p>
<p>Orcs, trolls, goblins, kobolds.</p>
<p>The greenskins hurled themselves against the base of the hill in animalistic desperation, guttural roars and grunts mingling with the heavy squelch of thick feet sinking into the mud.</p>
<p>The run was about five hundred meters to the top, up a gentle slope that, thanks to all that mud, had become hellish.</p>
<p>The vanguard, as expected, consisted entirely of orcs. More than five thousand of them, most in the second stage, with a few from the third stage scattered among them.</p>
<p>They were walking mountains of muscle and murderous intent, yellow eyes gleaming with a thirst for blood.</p>
<p>But those mountains wouldn’t be of much use on such a climb.</p>
<p>Although the slope wasn’t particularly steep, the very first few steps were enough for one of the orcs to slip.</p>
<p>His powerful body crashed to the ground with a wet thud, sliding backward and dragging three of his comrades down with him. They tumbled together in a tangle of flailing arms and clattering axes until they slammed into a bush and finally came to a stop.</p>
<p>Roars of rage rolled down the hillside, mingling with the frustrated grunts of those still fighting to regain their footing.</p>
<p>At the top of the hill, the soldiers of Goldenveil kept their javelins firmly aimed at the horde. The front lines crouched, clearing a line of fire so that the rear ranks could also take aim at their targets.</p>
<p>When the first orc crossed the 100-meter mark, Laine and Theron roared in unison, ordering the soldiers to release their javelins.</p>
<p>The front ranks didn’t hesitate for even a second.</p>
<p>Each soldier’s aura flowed through their arms to their fingertips, channeling into the shafts of their weapons, which left their hands with sharp cracks and tore through the air.</p>
<p>As soon as the first two rows threw their javelins, they crouched, making room for the comrades behind them to do the same.</p>
<p>A veritable deluge began to rain down on the greenskins—a shower of steel that darkened the gray sky for a moment before plummeting violently to the ground.</p>
<p>Those weapons were of exceptional quality, forged from the finest steel available, and each soldier had mastered powerful throwing skills that nearly doubled the natural speed of the javelin.</p>
<p>The leader of the vanguard, a third-stage orc—tall and powerful enough to make his green skin look as if it were carved from stone—swung his axe with a roar that shook the damp air.</p>
<p>The blade struck a javelin in mid-flight, clipping it with a metallic snap. But there were too many.</p>
<p>A second javelinssank deep into his abdomen with a wet sound, wrenching a hoarse grunt from his throat. A third pierced his shoulder, dark blood spraying in a hot arc through the air.</p>
<p>He still managed to parry the fourth with a desperate swing, but a fifth found its mark squarely, punching through his skull.</p>
<p>The colossal body collapsed onto the muddy ground, falling onto its side with a heavy thud.</p>
<p>Under the crossfire of eight thousand javelins raining down simultaneously, more than a thousand orcs died in the first minute of battle. They hadn’t even managed to cover half the distance to the top of the hill where Goldenveil’s soldiers awaited.</p>
<p>Bodies riddled with javelins fell heavily, sliding down the slick slope and knocking down those coming right behind them, creating a growing chaos of twisted limbs, broken weapons, and blood mixing with the earth.</p>
<p>If they were humans, this battle could already be considered a complete disaster.</p>
<p>But these were greenskins, and greenskins knew no retreat.</p>
<p>The orcs and trolls halted their advance, roaring orders for the goblins and kobolds to take the front line.</p>
<p>The goblins surged forward, a wave of greenish skin and red or sickly yellow eyes, rotten teeth bared, faces distorted by grotesque genetic abnormalities. They charged madly through the mud, screeching like starving beasts.</p>
<p>Close behind came the kobolds, slightly larger than the goblins, with hairy bodies and lizard-like heads, their jaws full of sharp teeth that gnashed with bloodthirst.</p>
<p>Through the treacherous mire, it was these creatures who formed the new vanguard, their small, disposable bodies serving as a makeshift floor so that orcs and trolls could advance over them without slipping.</p>
<p>Of course, the hell descending upon them was still only just beginning.</p>
<p>Behind Goldenveil’s army, the five mages were already chanting their most powerful destruction spells, hands raised to the sky as mana gathered around their voluptuous bodies in a vibrant, crackling aura.</p>
