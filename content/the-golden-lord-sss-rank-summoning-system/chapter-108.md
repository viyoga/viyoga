<p>While searching the bodies of the corrupted orcs for anything useful, the Goldenveil guards stumbled upon a pleasant surprise: most of the creatures possessed magical cores.</p>
<p>The corruption, however, ran far too deep and purification was impossible.</p>
<p>Still, though humans couldn’t absorb them, the cores remained an excellent source of energy for magical arrays and so, the guards carefully tucked each one away.</p>
<p>The battle had been brief, yet extremely fruitful.</p>
<p>Ethan and Evelyn were now approaching level forty-nine, while Alice had already reached level fifty-three. It wouldn’t be long before she was ready to advance to the third stage.</p>
<p>The other guards had also leveled up significantly.</p>
<p>Those in the second stage had gained two levels each, while those in the first stage had already climbed to level twenty-five.</p>
<p>Under Doran’s guidance, it wouldn’t be long before those with talent above Rank D reached the second stage.</p>
<p>It was a shame Cain wasn’t there, but the boy was already at level twenty-three and wouldn’t take long to reach the second stage himself.</p>
<p>With a soft sigh, Ethan simply motioned for Doran to get ready.</p>
<p>A few minutes later, the group resumed their march, entering a tunnel at the end of the vast chamber.</p>
<p>The floor sloped downward at a gentle incline, almost imperceptible underfoot.</p>
<p>Every now and then, a lone orc would leap from the shadows to attack them, but nothing that posed a real problem.</p>
<p>The creatures were growing stronger the deeper they went, and some even looked well-fed, though their numbers had dwindled considerably.</p>
<p>After a little over twenty minutes of walking, they finally came face-to-face with an orc in its prime.</p>
<p>The creature stood nearly three meters tall, with intense blood-red eyes and a powerful body covered in scars; over five hundred kilograms of pure muscle.</p>
<p>The orcs they had faced at the cave entrance had barely weighed over a hundred kilograms and a single glance at that monstrosity was enough to make Ethan freeze in place.</p>
<p>Most orcs weren’t quite that colossal, generally not exceeding two hundred or two hundred and fifty kilograms.</p>
<p>Still, it was terrifying to imagine an entire army made up solely of brutes like this one.</p>
<p>The Eternal Forest was infested with both orcs and goblins, and he considered himself lucky not to have stumbled upon an orc camp until that moment.</p>
<p>Stopping in his tracks, Ethan tightened his grip on his sword.</p>
<p>[Name: Greater Corrupted Orc</p>
<p>Description: An orc in an advanced state of corruption. Once a glorious creature, with the potential to reach the third stage, but now nothing more than an empty shell.</p>
<p>Level: 50]</p>
<p>Standing before a massive door of rotten wood, the orc watched them coldly before its jaws peeled open in a furious growl.</p>
<p>The creature seized the colossal axe leaning against the door behind it, the iron head grinding against the stone with a low rasp.</p>
<p>It took a heavy step forward, and the ground shuddered faintly beneath its feet.</p>
<p>Ethan didn’t hesitate, activating both the Burning Blood Body Enhancement Technique and the Lost Crimson Blade Technique.</p>
<p>Behind him, Alice cast a powerful buff spell, strengthening him even further.</p>
<p>Confident, Ethan planted his feet firmly on the ground.</p>
<p>"RRRRAKH!"</p>
<p>The orc let out a deafening roar before swinging the axe at him, the rusted blade crashing against the beautiful elven sword.</p>
<p>A shrill metallic clang tore through the cavern, bright sparks scattering across the walls as the very air between them buckled under the pressure of their colliding auras.</p>
<p>"Damn..."</p>
<p>Ethan grunted, a thin trickle of blood running down the corner of his lips.</p>
<p>His eyes flashed. With a powerful thrust, he sent the orc’s weapon flying upward.</p>
<p>Had he been alone, this would have been a grueling battle, but fortunately, that was not the case.</p>
<p>The monster staggered forward, its eyes widening just in time to see Evelyn’s axe descending swiftly toward its skull.</p>
<p>A wet, sickening thud echoed as the skull-crusher’s sharp edge bit violently into bone and pulped the creature’s brain in a single stroke.</p>
<p>The orc’s body convulsed, its thick muscles spasming, and its colossal axe slipped from its fingers to clatter against the stone floor.</p>
<p>"Ah... a fitting death for a filthy pig."</p>
<p>Evelyn laughed, trying to pull the weapon out. The blade didn’t budge a millimeter, and the orc still seemed alive, despite its brain having been reduced to pulp.</p>
<p>"Shit!"</p>
<p>Evelyn released the handle and stepped back as the orc’s powerful hands lunged forward in a attempt to seize her and crush her with sheer brute force.</p>
<p>Fortunately, Ethan had already recovered.</p>
<p>His blade swept upward in a clean, rising slash, slicing cleanly through the creature’s wrists.</p>
<p>The first strike sent two enormous greenish hands crashing to the ground; the second, a head.</p>
<p>"Ah... come on, we have to take out the boss soon."</p>
<p>Ethan barely gave the body time to fall before he was already ripping open the creature’s chest to extract the powerful magical core.</p>
<p>As he worked, the Blood Emperor’s Ring absorbed yet another point of the enemy’s strength attribute.</p>
<p>Mentally, he counted the seconds remaining before the Burning Blood Body Enhancement Technique wore off, his eyes still shining as he ignored the throbbing pain spreading through his body.</p>
<p>The clash of power against the orc had left him with a minor internal injury, but nothing serious.</p>
<p>"Wait, you’re hurt!"</p>
<p>Alice interrupted him, pressing her delicate hand against his armor, her beautiful face filled with concern as she cast a healing spell.</p>
<p>A cool freshness spread through his chest, and the dull ache began to dissipate.</p>
<p>Ethan smiled softly.</p>
<p>He could easily heal the wound with the Blood Emperor’s Ring, but it felt too good to receive such care from that beauty.</p>
<p>"Thank you, darling."</p>
<p>With that, he turned and pressed his hand against the door.</p>
<p>Slowly, the heavy wooden slab gave way, a creak and a loud crack echoing through the passage as the path ahead revealed itself.</p>
<p>Ethan took a single step, and then froze in place.</p>
<p>Alice’s light vanished behind him. When he turned around, a dense, impenetrable mist had sealed off the path he had just entered through.</p>
<p>His heart sank in his chest, and he slowly turned back around.</p>
<p>All around him, purple-flamed torches flared to life one by one, and a system notification reached him.</p>
<p>[You have entered the lair of the Fallen Knight Princess, a heroine of the past and a skilled duelist who succumbed to necromantic powers.</p>
<p>Defeat her in a duel and prove yourself worthy.]</p>
<p>"Shit..."</p>
