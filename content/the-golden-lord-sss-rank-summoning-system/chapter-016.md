<p>Ethan examined the quality of his own sword.</p>
<p>It was only a D-grade item, without a name of its own.</p>
<p>A decent quality sword, crafted by a great blacksmith.</p>
<p>Considering how much his father had paid for it, he had really expected something better, but that was okay.</p>
<p>In the end, the main difference between a high-quality weapon and a low-quality one was the amount of Aura or Mana it could absorb.</p>
<p>With Ethan’s current power, even all of his Aura pushed into the metal of that blade would barely be able to make it tremble.</p>
<p>"I really need to train." He repeated to himself.</p>
<p>Fortunately, Evelyn was more than willing to help him with that.</p>
<p>Sighing, Ethan removed the Skull-Crushing Axe from the system’s storage.</p>
<p>His hands closed around an elegant darkened wooden handle, long, but not too long, with perfectly balanced weight.</p>
<p>The head of the axe was divided into two parts: a sharp, curved silver blade and, on the opposite side, a hammer-like structure covered with small metal spikes.</p>
<p>That was probably where the name "Skullcracker" came from.</p>
<p>"It’s a beautiful weapon..." Ethan murmured, swinging the axe a few times before looking up at Evelyn.</p>
<p>Her beautiful silver-gray eyes were shining, fixed on that axe as if it were everything to her.</p>
<p>Ethan shook his head and threw the axe in her direction.</p>
<p>Immediately, Evelyn moved, catching it with both hands.</p>
<p>"What the hell do you think you’re doing? Do you know how much a weapon like that costs?" she asked, her mature voice laden with indignation.</p>
<p>"I know. It’s yours now." Ethan smiled, turning his back on her.</p>
<p>Evelyn’s eyes floated between the weapon and Ethan repeatedly, until she bit her lower lip.</p>
<p>[Evelyn’s opinion increased by 5 points]</p>
<p>[Evelyn’s loyalty increased by 5 points]</p>
<p>Two system notifications reached Ethan, whose lips curved even further.</p>
<p>That was just the beginning. Before acting as the rightful lord of these people, he needed to win their loyalty.</p>
<p>His father had taught him that early on; even with all the money in the world, a leader without the loyalty of his people couldn’t even build a chicken coop, every step would be taken with the resistance of a thousand blades at his neck.</p>
<p>Ethan didn’t want that for himself.</p>
<p>"Hey, where are you going?" Evelyn ran after him as he walked, her huge breasts bouncing rhythmically with each step.</p>
<p>Ethan didn’t answer, continuing onward.</p>
<p>Maybe having her by his side was a good thing.</p>
<p>As soon as he left the mansion, his eyes fell on Ulric and his son, Alaric, gathered with the rest of the guard under a tree a few meters away.</p>
<p>The moment he noticed Ethan’s presence, Ulric’s face lit up with a glow of false friendliness.</p>
<p>"My lord, to what do I owe your esteemed presence and..." Ulric’s words died in his throat as he bowed.</p>
<p>Where the hell had that woman come from? He hadn’t seen her anywhere before.</p>
<p>"My lord, who is that woman?" Ulric asked, slightly concerned.</p>
<p>"Evelyn. A friend of mine," Ethan replied coldly.</p>
<p>Behind Ulric, Alaric also stepped forward.</p>
<p>"That’s not enough, my lord. Do you know the dangers that inhabit this region?"</p>
<p>Each subsequent word that escaped Alaric’s mouth caused a new vein to appear on Evelyn’s forehead.</p>
<p>"What if she’s a monster in disguise? I need to have control over anyone who,-"</p>
<p>"Try to control your own filthy ass first, you damn dog face. I can smell you from here." Evelyn growled, her beautiful, sensual voice laced with venom.</p>
<p>"Besides, shouldn’t you be on your knees while speaking to your Lord? I would kill you myself if I had such useless servants."</p>
<p>Alaric clenched his jaw so tightly that his teeth seemed ready to break, every part of him screaming in agony and rage.</p>
<p>"My lord..." Alaric turned his face to Ethan.</p>
<p>"My father would break the legs and rip out the tongue of any servant who treated him as an equal. You’d better get down on your knees." Ethan said.</p>
<p>His father wasn’t that strict about noble etiquette, but that didn’t matter now.</p>
<p>The fact was, Ethan couldn’t show weakness. Not at all.</p>
<p>Alaric shuddered and glanced at his father out of the corner of his eye.</p>
<p>He was almost begging to just kill that damn golden lord right there.</p>
<p>Fuck the Church. Fuck that priest.</p>
<p>They had the power!</p>
<p>Still, his father didn’t move, maintaining a stern gaze.</p>
<p>In the end, Alaric simply bent down, one knee sinking into the muddy ground. "Forgive my disrespect, my lord!"</p>
<p>[Alaric’s Loyalty dropped 5 points! Negative Loyalty.]</p>
<p>[Alaric’s Opinion dropped 5 points! Negative Opinion.]</p>
<p>"Good." Ethan nodded silently, motioning for Alaric to stand up, then focusing his gaze on Ulric.</p>
<p>"It’s time to inspect the settlement’s warehouses. Things aren’t looking good, correct?"</p>
<p>Hearing those words, Ulric, who had also knelt, stood up, ignoring his son retreating behind him.</p>
<p>"Well... they’ve been better, my lord. We had a long and difficult winter, and we were still forced to pay a..." Ulric answered honestly, but fell silent at the last part, starting to walk.</p>
<p>The village was small, so the warehouse wasn’t far away, and its size was certainly not impressive.</p>
<p>"Hm..." Ethan scratched his chin, stopping in front of the warehouse. "We must have at least one large field, right?" He asked.</p>
<p>"Yes, my lord. Would you like to inspect the fields as well?"</p>
<p>"After the warehouses." Ethan replied, opening the double doors of the place.</p>
