<p>The last fifteen kilometers to Goldenveil were the most grueling walk of Theron’s life, his muscles screaming in protest with every movement.</p>
<p>His knees threatened to give out at any moment, and his eyes, heavy with exhaustion, could barely focus on the trail ahead.</p>
<p>Dried blood caked his torn clothes, while the biting wind cut into his open wounds like tiny blades.</p>
<p>Finally, he began to feel the magical density in the air increase.</p>
<p>Yes, Goldenveil had always had a strangely high magical density.</p>
<p>Theron quickened his pace, forcing his trembling legs to obey.</p>
<p>He needed to reach Goldenveil and find a healer soon, or he wouldn’t live to see the next dawn.</p>
<p>He didn’t have much money on him, but it would be enough to pay for a healer doctor. At least, that’s what he hoped.</p>
<p>"Ugh... isn’t the density a little too high?" he muttered to himself, spitting out a thick strand of blood as he kept moving forward.</p>
<p>It was then that he caught sight, through the mist dancing before his weary eyes, of the first sign of human civilization: a road!</p>
<p>Theron ran with relief, until, finally, through the veil of pain clouding his mind, he realized: that road was too new and too perfect.</p>
<p>Made of some kind of black brick—perhaps basalt—each piece was meticulously fitted to the next.</p>
<p>For a moment, Theron hesitated, wiping a trickle of blood running down his cracked lips with the back of his hand.</p>
<p>His eyes narrowed in suspicion, and he slowly turned his head, following the line of the road with his gaze.</p>
<p>"Shit..." Finally, he saw what he was looking for. A city.</p>
<p>That had to be Goldenveil. He was almost certain his mental maps were correct, but...</p>
<p>How the hell could he believe that?</p>
<p>What Theron saw was a massive, snow-white wall, the basalt road stretching all the way to a wide gate.</p>
<p>Towers rose along the wall, each crowned by banners fluttering in the wind.</p>
<p>He hesitated for a moment, but in the end, he pressed on.</p>
<p>He was too exhausted to think about anything else.</p>
<p>Step by step, he dragged his bloodied body toward the gates, each movement draining what little strength he had left.</p>
<p>Ten armed soldiers stood guard at that gate.</p>
<p>The moment they noticed him, and the pressure of his faltering aura reached the guards, they all shuddered—especially the third-stage officer commanding the platoon.</p>
<p>His subordinates could only tell that this man was a powerful warrior, despite his injuries. But the officer immediately realized that this man was a fourth-stage warrior!</p>
<p>Theron took a few more unsteady steps forward before collapsing, his body finally succumbing to exhaustion.</p>
<p>One of the guards rushed over immediately, catching him before he hit the ground, his hands gripping the man’s bloodied shoulders to support his weight.</p>
<p>"L-Lieutenant, what should we do?" the guard asked, turning his pale face toward the officer.</p>
<p>"Go get Lord Ethan! We must take him to the barracks’ medics immediately!" the officer ordered, pulling the powerful specialist from the soldier’s trembling hands in one fluid motion.</p>
<p>Those were the last words Theron heard before darkness engulfed him completely.</p>
<p>He didn’t know how long he slept.</p>
<p>He only felt his own body relax, the pain gradually fading, and a strange peace taking hold of his tired and confused mind.</p>
<p>Even the terrible wounds on his body—caused by the forced advance and which should have practically left him crippled for the rest of his life—began to heal.</p>
<p>When Theron’s eyes finally opened again, he was in a clearly military room—an officer’s room, judging by how comfortable the bed was.</p>
<p>His body was no longer covered in bandages, as they were no longer necessary.</p>
<p>All the open wounds had been closed; only the most severe ones left scars behind.</p>
<p>His eyes stared at the ceiling for a brief moment before he slowly turned his face to the side.</p>
<p>There, sitting in a chair beside the bed, was Ethan Goldenveil.</p>
<p>The young lord had a calm expression on his face and his arms casually crossed over his chest.</p>
<p>He stared at Theron for an instant, his eyes shining as he looked at the status screen floating before him.</p>
<p>[Name: Theron</p>
<p>Age: 53</p>
<p>Talent: Rank SS</p>
<p>Level: 125/180</p>
<p>Opinion: 70</p>
<p>Loyalty: 85%</p>
<p>Class: Swordsman]</p>
<p>In his entire life, this was the first time he’d seen someone with such a high base loyalty—and better yet, a fourth-stage expert!</p>
<p>Arwen came pretty close, but even she only had a base loyalty of eighty.</p>
<p>"You’ve been through some terrible things, haven’t you? What led you to perform a forced advance? My wife has literally divine healing magic, but even she barely managed to save your aura heart," Ethan said in a calm voice.</p>
<p>It was true.</p>
<p>It took Arwen nearly ten hours to repair Theron’s aura heart, and by the time she finished, she was on the verge of collapsing from exhaustion.</p>
<p>Forced advances were an incredible way to save lives in lost battles, but few used such a method unless they wanted to kill the enemy at all costs or were truly desperate.</p>
<p>The chance of surviving a forced advance, even with great talent, was less than twenty percent. And even if they survived, their aura hearts would be terribly damaged.</p>
<p>If Ethan didn’t have Arwen and her incredible magical abilities, Theron would live for no more than ten years and would hardly be able to demonstrate power beyond that of a second-stage.</p>
<p>Fortunately, she was a priestess of the goddess of life herself.</p>
<p>It was difficult, but she managed to repair all the damage.</p>
<p>Theron placed his hand on his chest, feeling his aura heart pulse in harmony with his blood heart.</p>
<p>All the damage had vanished, both from his aura heart and from his body.</p>
<p>Theron forced himself to stand, looked at Ethan, and immediately fell to his knees.</p>
<p>He didn’t hesitate for even a second, declaring in a powerful voice.</p>
<p>"Lord Ethan, my father always told me that I—just like all the men and women of our families—was born to be the sword that cuts down the enemies of a great man!</p>
<p>For saving my life, please let me be your blade and slit the throats of those who stand against you!"</p>
