<p>As the lord of a small settlement, Ethan was more than just a ruler and administrator.</p>
<p>He was also the sword of the law and the judge.</p>
<p>Without a justice system or a legal code, the lord’s word was the law itself, and it was this lord’s duty to judge who was innocent and who was guilty.</p>
<p>From land disputes to murders and accusations of infidelity.</p>
<p>As Lord of Goldenveil, Ethan had a duty to judge all these cases.</p>
<p>Shortly after getting himself together, with his sword at his waist, he walked out to the front of his mansion.</p>
<p>His steps were heavy.</p>
<p>His ears picked up the wailing of a woman and the screams of a young man who seemed to be on the verge of being lynched to death.</p>
<p>Ethan approached, his heart growing heavier with every step.</p>
<p>There was a body on the ground, blood splattered across the mud, children crying as they screamed for their father.</p>
<p>Three in total. The youngest was six, the oldest, seventeen.</p>
<p>The mother, now a widow, clung desperately to her husband’s slit throat, trying to staunch the bleeding.</p>
<p>Well... there was nothing left to stop.</p>
<p>He had been dead for many minutes.</p>
<p>Alice, who had already dressed and was standing beside Ethan, joined the widow, casting healing magic with tearful eyes, even though she knew it was useless.</p>
<p>Humans are funny creatures.</p>
<p>Ethan opened his mouth, but no words came out.</p>
<p>His eyes shifted, focusing on the young man on the ground, whose body was already covered in bruises.</p>
<p>He was a familiar figure in Goldenveil, even before Ethan arrived.</p>
<p>But he had never been a serious problem, sometimes he’d steal bread, sometimes a chicken. Things that could be resolved with a few lashes, stern words, and a bit of hard labor.</p>
<p>Now, it was different.</p>
<p>He had killed a man. A father and a husband.</p>
<p>"My lord... the trial must be swift, since the guards will have to leave soon." Ulric spoke, and Ethan’s eyes fell upon the old man.</p>
<p>In the settlement’s square, the guard had already set up a wooden throne, something Ethan didn’t even know existed.</p>
<p>"Remove the body from the street and prepare a coffin as quickly as possible..." Ethan turned away. "The expedition will be delayed a little."</p>
<p>He didn’t want to conduct a trial while the widow was still weeping desperately and the dead man’s body remained sunk in the mud.</p>
<p>They were better than that.</p>
<p>"Yes, my lord." Ulric bowed, shouting orders to some of the village men.</p>
<p>As he waited, Ethan’s eyes met the young man’s.</p>
<p>There was pain, despair, and guilt—his hands stained with blood and his face pressed into the mud by the heavy body of Goldenveil’s master builder.</p>
<p>That young man was talented. One of the few Rank C talents in the settlement.</p>
<p>At seventeen, if he enlisted in the guard, he would be a great talent who would quickly reach the second stage, and who had a small chance of reaching the third.</p>
<p>Unfortunately, that chance no longer existed.</p>
<p>Ethan went to the throne and waited.</p>
<p>An hour. Two hours.</p>
<p>Soon, the body was pulled from the mud, the blood washed away, and the widow was given a more appropriate place to mourn her loss.</p>
<p>"What are his possessions?" Ethan asked, his eyes shifting from the young man on the ground to the dead man’s eldest son.</p>
<p>From what he’d heard from Alice, the two young men were good friends and were always together.</p>
<p>Now, from the eyes of the eldest son, filled with hatred and a thirst for revenge, it was obvious that the friendship was over.</p>
<p>"He has his dead father’s old house, and the sword as well, the one he used to kill," Ulric replied.</p>
<p>"Hm... bring the sword to me." Ethan motioned to Alaric, who obeyed.</p>
<p>A few minutes later, the captain of the guard returned with the sword in his hands.</p>
<p>"No! That’s mine!" The young man on the floor roared, his eyes filled with tears and rage. "It’s all my father left me... please..."</p>
<p>Ethan paid no heed to those words. He took the sword, which had already been cleaned of blood, and studied the blade.</p>
<p>It was a good sword, only slightly inferior to the one he himself used, in terms of quality.</p>
<p>"Why did you kill him?" Ethan asked.</p>
<p>"Because he was a son of a bitch!" The young assassin spat on the ground. "Just like you!"</p>
<p>"Ah..." Ethan turned his face toward Ulric and Alaric, waiting for an answer from father and son.</p>
<p>"He was caught stealing and, in desperation, ended up using the sword," Alaric said. "Or at least that’s what the widow said."</p>
<p>"Hm..." Ethan scratched his chin, looking into the young man’s eyes once more.</p>
<p>There was guilt, but not the guilt of someone who was remorseful.</p>
<p>It was more like the guilt of a partner who betrays his own companion. He felt guilty because he’d been caught, not for what he’d done.</p>
<p>"You killed a man... a father and a husband." Ethan drew his sword from its sheath, walking toward the murderer.</p>
<p>The man holding murderer’s down didn’t hesitate, immediately exposing his neck to Ethan.</p>
<p>"In the name of his soul and the peace of his now-widowed wife, I, Ethan Goldenveil, Lord of Goldenveil, ruler by divine right and by the blessing of the Nine, sentence you to death."</p>
<p>Ethan gave the young man no time to scream.</p>
<p>With a small piece of wood positioned beneath the assassin’s neck, he brought the sword down.</p>
<p>A pained groan echoed. A head rolled across the floor.</p>
<p>Ethan did not look at the decapitated corpse. His eyes fell upon the victim’s eldest son.</p>
<p>"All of the accused’s properties shall be handed over to you. Your mother, now more than ever, needs a man to help her raise your younger siblings. Be strong." Ethan handed the sword to the young man, glancing briefly at his status screen.</p>
<p>A Grade D talent, still, a level Ethan would have liked to have in his guard.</p>
<p>The young man took the weapon that had been used to kill his father and that had now served to deliver justice.</p>
<p>He looked at the corpse of the boy who had once been his best friend and at the blood on the blade.</p>
<p>There were many thoughts in his mind, but in the end, he only strengthened his determination.</p>
<p>"Thank you, my lord... my name is Cain, and I will serve you until death!"</p>
<p>He sheathed the sword, bowed to Ethan, and then turned his back.</p>
