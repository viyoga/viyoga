<p>Ethan had to fight hard to resist the urge to start studying that technique right away.</p>
<p>Reluctantly, he put the book back on the table and then pulled out the gacha tickets he had saved up.</p>
<p>First, the seven free tickets.</p>
<p>He didn’t expect much from them, so he just tore them open casually.</p>
<p>[You used: 7x Free Gacha Tickets]</p>
<p>[Spinning...]</p>
<p>As expected, he didn’t win much of real use.</p>
<p>Aside from an F-grade blacksmith’s forge, which he would certainly put to good use, the rest was just a few miscellaneous tools and a single E-grade sword.</p>
<p>"Not bad..." Ethan smiled.</p>
<p>At least compared to last time, when he spun nearly a hundred free tickets, the result had been quite satisfactory.</p>
<p>Shaking his head, Ethan took out the other twenty-seven gacha tickets he had in his possession, twenty common and seven premium.</p>
<p>First, he tore open the common ones, saving the premium ones for last.</p>
<p>[You used: 20x Common Gacha Tickets]</p>
<p>[Spinning...]</p>
<p>[Congratulations! You received: 1x Mana Core (Rank E)]</p>
<p>[Congratulations! You received: 5x Minor Stamina Potion (Rank E)]</p>
<p>[Congratulations! You received: 1x White Steel Pickaxe (Rank F)]</p>
<p>[Congratulations! You received...] ...</p>
<p>A flood of notifications flooded Ethan’s mind, but he focused only on the first two.</p>
<p>Five stamina potions and a mana core.</p>
<p>The mana core wasn’t very useful to him, but it was perfect for Alice, who was a mage.</p>
<p>The stamina potions, however, were wonderful for Ethan.</p>
<p>This type of potion was less rare than healing potions, of which Ethan already had two, both low-rank.</p>
<p>But despite being less rare, it was still very useful, bringing with it the ability to instantly regenerate the stamina of an exhausted warrior.</p>
<p>If used in large quantities, it would cause great harm to the body, but when used in moderation, it was an incredible and reliable potion.</p>
<p>With a sigh, Ethan took a look at the other items he had gained.</p>
<p>As always, hardly impressive.</p>
<p>Even less impressive than what he had gained from the common gacha tickets the system had rewarded him with after his integration.</p>
<p>Tools, two sets of leather armor, and even a recipe for roast chicken.</p>
<p>Not exactly one of Ethan’s priorities at that moment.</p>
<p>With a bitter smile, Ethan removed only the chicken recipe from his inventory, which he would give to Alice.</p>
<p>"At least you have to give me something useful, right?" Ethan pulled out the seven premium tickets.</p>
<p>The system had no mercy.</p>
<p>He knew full well that if the system was in a bad mood, there was a chance he’d get seven pieces of grass, one after another.</p>
<p>[I have far less autonomy than you think.]</p>
<p>Ethan ignored that response, maintaining a suspicious expression as he tore up the seven premium tickets.</p>
<p>[You used: 7x Premium Gacha Tickets]</p>
<p>[Spinning...]</p>
<p>[Congratulations! You received: 1x Wolf Tooth Greatsword (Rank D)]</p>
<p>[Congratulations! You received: 1x Intermediate Alchemy Workshop Blueprint (Rank C)]</p>
<p>Right after the second item, an intense golden light engulfed Ethan’s office, his eyes widening.</p>
<p>An A-Rank item!</p>
<p>[Congratulations! You received: 5x Long Teleportation Scroll (Rank A)]</p>
<p>[Congratulations! You received: 1x Refined Cold Steel Ingot (Rank C)]</p>
<p>[Congratulations! You received: 1x Dawn Bow (Rank D)]</p>
<p>[Congratulations! You have received: 5x 70% XP Bonus Potion (Rank B)]</p>
<p>After the Rank A item, Ethan really wasn’t expecting anything much better than that.</p>
<p>But when he saw the seventh item, his lips curved from ear to ear, a huge smile taking over his face.</p>
<p>Alaric was fucked. So fucked.</p>
<p>Ethan controlled his emotions, taking a look at the three items that caught his attention the most.</p>
<p>[Name: 70% XP Bonus Potion</p>
<p>Description: A potion capable of temporarily increasing all XP gained by 70%. Each bottle can be divided into twenty doses; the effect can be amplified or diluted depending on the user’s power level.</p>
<p>Effect: +70% XP</p>
<p>Duration: 7 days.]</p>
<p>That item was, without a doubt, the one that surprised Ethan the most.</p>
<p>Five vials that could be divided into twenty doses each, a total of over seven hundred days with a 70% XP bonus.</p>
<p>Even if the effect diminished starting from the second stage, it was still absurd.</p>
<p>[Item: Intermediate Alchemy Workshop Design</p>
<p>Description: A design for creating an alchemy workshop. A master of formations is required.</p>
<p>Rank: C]</p>
<p>[Item: Long-Range Teleportation Scroll</p>
<p>Description: An item that, when torn, allows the user to teleport to a desired area within a radius of two thousand kilometers. Rank: A]</p>
<p>They were very useful items, but their utility would only have real value in the future.</p>
<p>Ethan simply kept them stored away for now.</p>
<p>Finally free, he picked up the book that was on his desk, The Principles of the Burning Blood Body Enhancement Skill.</p>
<p>After a moment’s hesitation, Ethan opened the book, his eyes falling on the first page.</p>
<p>Skill books in this world were not like those in games, even with the arrival of the Gaia System.</p>
<p>To learn a skill, it was necessary, first and foremost, to understand the intent of its creator.</p>
<p>Whether it was an internal skill, focused on circulating mana internally for physical enhancement or healing.</p>
<p>Or an external skill, like Aura Slash, the only skill Ethan had learned that could be used to launch a long-range external aura attack.</p>
<p>He didn’t use it much in the past, since it consumed a lot of aura and his talent didn’t help much. But now, with his superior aura and talent, he could already use it with a little more freedom.</p>
<p>Extending a single finger, Ethan moved his aura, forming a small line, and then flicked his finger.</p>
<p>An aura slash appeared, flying through the air and striking the wall in front of him, leaving only a small mark.</p>
<p>That tiny strike had already consumed five aura points.</p>
<p>If he used his sword to create a powerful strike, he would need at least a hundred.</p>
<p>With a soft sigh, Ethan simply began reading the book, focusing on his new ability.</p>
<p>With those two abilities and the seventh item he had earned with the premium gacha tickets, he was certain of one thing.</p>
<p>Alaric was completely fucked in his hands.</p>
