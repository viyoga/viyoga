<p>Ethan’s sword met the hobgoblin’s axe.</p>
<p>Sparks burst from the point of impact, dancing like fireflies the air.</p>
<p>The ground between them cracked beneath the monstrous pressure, and both warriors took a single step back, eyes brimming with murderous intent before they hurled themselves at one another once more.</p>
<p>"Ah... has anyone ever told you how bad your breath stinks? Even with your pathetic attempt to look like a human king, you’re nothing but a pig in a crown!"</p>
<p>Ethan taunted, his blue eyes locked on the hobgoblin’s blood-red gaze as their swords remained crossed.</p>
<p>The hobgoblin answered with a guttural roar. "I’ll rip out your tongue, hooman!"</p>
<p>Ethan smiled and ducked low, narrowly avoiding decapitation.</p>
<p>The blade of the hobgoblin leader’s axe scraped across his helmet with a sharp hiss of metal grinding against metal.</p>
<p>He had realized immediately that this hobgoblin possessed human intelligence.</p>
<p>The angrier the creature got, however, the more that intelligence crumbled away.</p>
<p>Just two sentences, and the beast was no longer trying to contain its aura.</p>
<p>The young lord had read about this in an ancient manual—a perfect technique for dealing with hobgoblins, orcs, any greenskin endowed with human intelligence.</p>
<p>Good old ragebaiting.</p>
<p>And the fact that it worked made everything so much easier for him.</p>
<p>"Come on, pathetic green skin!"</p>
<p>"SILENCE! Skrak... SHUT UP!" The hobgoblin kept roaring as he attacked frantically, the handsome young man slipping beneath blow after blow with mocking ease.</p>
<p>Finally, the perfect opening for a counterattack revealed itself.</p>
<p>Ethan’s eyes flashed with murderous intent. He let the hobgoblin’s axe whistle over his head, crouching low and slashing upward in a single fluid motion.</p>
<p>The rank A elven sword struck the hobgoblin squarely in the groin, continuing its merciless ascent until it tore through the creature’s chin.</p>
<p>Greenish blood stained the blade and splattered onto the dirt floor.</p>
<p>The creature tried to launch one final desperate attack, but Ethan obviously wouldn’t allow it.</p>
<p>He spun his body in a swift, circular motion.</p>
<p>His blade severed both of the hobgoblin’s wrists and, with one last swing, took its head as well.</p>
<p>Ethan smiled as the severed head hit the ground with a wet thud, rolling for several meters before coming to rest in the dirt.</p>
<p>[You killed a third-stage hobgoblin!</p>
<p>+120% XP]</p>
<p>[You leveled up! 66 -&gt; 68</p>
<p>+6 free attribute points]</p>
<p>With this advancement, the number of Ethan’s unallocated attribute points reached twenty-nine, and his lips curled in satisfaction.</p>
<p>His gaze fell upon the hundreds of goblins hurling themselves against Goldenveil’s shield wall as if leaping into a meat grinder.</p>
<p>Without hesitation, he charged forward, activating Golden Steps and tearing through the goblin rear ranks like a gleaming storm.</p>
<p>With their master fallen and Ethan striking from the rear, the goblins didn’t last long.</p>
<p>The Goldenveil army soon broke through the goblin "formation" and executed the third form of the Golden Emperor Dragon’s fencing.</p>
<p>In that instant, with shields lowered and heavy footsteps thundering across the bloodstained ground, Goldenveil’s troops pressed forward in their slaughter.</p>
<p>The battle lasted about twenty minutes. All told, just over a thousand goblins fell, while only a few dozen managed to escape, fleeing deep into the forest.</p>
<p>When it was over, both Ethan and Evelyn were drenched in dark green blood, as was the entire front line of the army.</p>
<p>The exhausted men gradually broke formation, weary boots stepping over twisted goblin corpses as they gathered the bodies of their own fallen comrades.</p>
<p>Three Goldenveil guards had died. Five were seriously wounded, and another twenty needed immediate medical attention to keep their conditions from worsening.</p>
<p>All things considered, three human deaths in exchange for a thousand and one goblins didn’t seem like a bad toll.</p>
<p>Even so, watching the corpses being carried away, Ethan couldn’t contain the pain and rage squeezing his chest.</p>
<p>Three Goldenveil deaths for a hundred human enemies? That he could easily accept.</p>
<p>But for goblins?</p>
<p>Not even a million corpses at his feet would bring him peace. That was how little value he placed on the lives of those damned creatures.</p>
<p>He looked at Alice, who was already casting healing spells on the many wounded soldiers, and let out a heavy sigh.</p>
<p>Alice had reached level seventy-two, while Evelyn had hit seventy. He himself was now also at level seventy.</p>
<p>"Aerick." Ethan called the vice-captain of the guard, the man responsible for protecting Alice and who had barely seen any combat.</p>
<p>"Gather the hundred most rested men and go exterminate the female goblins and their babies. Collect all the loot." Ethan ordered, then summoned Mireya with a flick of his hand.</p>
<p>"Accompany Aerick, but try not to use up too much of your strength. It may be needed should any trouble arise." He instructed the death knight princess.</p>
<p>Mireya merely pouted, ignoring the surprised stares she drew from the soldiers around her.</p>
<p>Her cheeks puffed out in pure indignation, and she brushed a strand of misty white hair from her face with a dismissive flick of her fingers.</p>
<p>"Couldn’t you have just used me to kill those disgusting creatures earlier? Your army wouldn’t even have had to tire itself out." She said, inflating her chest with pride.</p>
<p>The motion thrust her generous bosom forward, her heavy globes straining against the dark fabric of her armor..</p>
<p>Having absorbed countless souls during the massacre, Mireya had already reached level seventy-five, and she was eager to put her new power to use.</p>
<p>"Don’t worry. When we get home, I’ll have a private battle just for you." Ethan smiled, letting a hint of teasing slip into his voice.</p>
<p>Mireya didn’t catch on right away. But the moment she caught the mischievous glances Evelyn and Alice exchanged, her cheeks flushed a deep red, and she followed the order she’d received in silence.</p>
<p>’Or maybe I should ignore his orders? Those delicious spanking slaps and—’</p>
<p>Realizing the lewd thoughts invading her mind, Mireya clenched her teeth tightly.</p>
<p>Look what that bastard had done to her, a princess!</p>
<p>She would make him pay!</p>
