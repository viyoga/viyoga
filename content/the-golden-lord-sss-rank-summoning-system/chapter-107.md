<p>The moment Alice’s light engulfed the chamber, agonized screams tore through the air.</p>
<p>Lining the perimeter of the circular room stood creatures even taller than hobgoblins, their hulking forms reaching nearly two and a half meters in height.</p>
<p>Their bulging eyes gleamed with a hollow, vacant emptiness, and thick strands of black slime dripped ceaselessly from mouths lined with protruding lower fangs.</p>
<p>Orcs.</p>
<p>They should have been formidable creatures, with bodies packed with dense muscle, yet these were gaunt and wasted, as though they hadn’t eaten in months.</p>
<p>"Ugh... these are already in a very advanced stage of corruption," Ethan muttered, "Doran, get the men ready. No one is to die today!"</p>
<p>His voice had barely faded when one of the creatures lunged, hurling itself at him in a desperate charge.</p>
<p>Ethan didn’t hesitate.</p>
<p>He activated the lost technique of the crimson blade, and the elven sword in his hands erupted into flames as red as fresh blood, carving a diagonal arc through the air.</p>
<p>The orc made no attempt to defend itself, charging forward like an irrational beast, its powerful jaws snapping open in a ravenous snarl.</p>
<p>It didn’t get far.</p>
<p>The blade bit into its ribs and sliced clean through, cleaving bone, flesh, and organs before emerging on the other side.</p>
<p>The orc’s severed torso slammed against Ethan’s steel armor before tumbling to the ground with a wet, heavy thud.</p>
<p>He had no time to step back and reset his stance. Three more orcs were already closing in from behind, their clawed feet scraping against the floor.</p>
<p>Without a moment’s pause, Ethan activated the Blood Emperor’s ring, aiming his open palm at the creatures’ chests.</p>
<p>A bloody mist unfurled from beneath his feet, and from within it, spears of crimson erupted upward, piercing the beasts’ chests in a single motion.</p>

<p>Four kills, and he had already leveled up twice, gaining more attributes than he would have from slaughtering hundreds of goblins.</p>
<p>Of course, most of the XP came from the Rank B supreme XP bonus potion that he and the others had drunk before entering, but still, the results were wonderful.</p>
<p>He only snapped out of his daze when he felt two ice spears whistle over his head, slamming into the still-blinded orcs and detonating in bursts of frost.</p>
<p>Shards of frozen flesh and bone scattered across the chamber floor.</p>
<p>Alice stood at the center of it all, her eyes glowing a brilliant white.</p>
<p>Her robes billowed around her voluptuous frame, her grimoire floated in midair, pages flipping non stop.</p>
<p>"You look amazing when you use magic like that," Ethan said, a grin tugging at the corner of his lips.</p>
<p>Alice’s cheeks flushed a soft pink, her lips parting slightly before she quickly refocused on the battle.</p>
<p>Not far from her, Evelyn had already taken down three corrupted orcs and she din’t want to get behind.</p>
<p>The number of orcs grew rapidly, swelling into the dozens, spilling forth from the shadowed corridors beyond.</p>
<p>Had these been ordinary orcs, the party would have been doomed.</p>
<p>Even five or six healthy orcs would have been enough to carve a terrible massacre through the guards.</p>
<p>But these creatures were far too weakened, and their minds could barely process the most basic logic.</p>
<p>Orcs weren’t known for their intelligence, but they were still capable of speech, of organizing themselves into societies and of planning.</p>
<p>The things before them, however, were nothing more than beasts driven by raw instinct.</p>
<p>Generally, the higher a beast’s degree of corruption, the greater the danger it posed. Fortunately, these orcs were in too advanced a state of decay to manifest the true terror of their corruption.</p>
<p>With a broad smile, Ethan advanced, his blade weaving around his body.</p>
<p>Enhanced aura slashes rained down on the orcs without pause, each arc of crimson light cleaving through flesh.</p>
<p>After just over five minutes of relentless carnage, his sword finally stopped swinging.</p>
<p>Beneath his feet, massive corpses, pools of blood, and tangled viscera lay scattered across the stone floor.</p>
<p>With a wide smile, Ethan extended his hand toward the bodies of the corrupted orcs.</p>

<p>With a pleased grin, Ethan raised his agility and vitality to 100, then poured the remainder entirely into intelligence.</p>
<p>Although it seemed like a less important stat for combat, intelligence also increased the brain’s processing speed, sharpening the senses just as much as agility.</p>
<p>Feeling his mind grow lighter and his thoughts flow faster, Ethan’s eyes drifted toward a corridor yawning open on the far side of the chamber.</p>
<p>"Come on. These orcs can’t be all there is. There must be even more of them on the lower floors." He swung his sword in a crisp arc, flinging the blood from the blade with the motion.</p>
<p>It was time to farm even more XP and attributes.</p>
