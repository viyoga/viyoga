<p>The aura is the power imbued within oneself.</p>
<p>From the blood to the bones and the flesh.</p>
<p>While mana is the spirit and the mind, the aura is the body itself. Strength and determination.</p>
<p>Mana represents the brain and rationality.</p>
<p>The aura represents the heart and the will.</p>
<p>Only those who master the will are capable of seeing the truth of the burning blood.</p>
<p>***</p>
<p>Ethan had already lost track of time while reading that book.</p>
<p>They were simple, familiar concepts, over a hundred pages devoted solely to them.</p>
<p>It was, without a doubt, different from other abilities he had studied, but not in a bad way.</p>
<p>The Burning Blood enhancement ability was, generally speaking, simple.</p>
<p>Too simple.</p>
<p>The foundation of its mastery was understanding one’s own will and using it as a means to fill the blood vessels with aura, bringing the blood to a boil.</p>
<p>Of course, that was a rather simplified explanation, but it was good enough.</p>
<p>Standing up, Ethan left the room and walked out to the garden of his mansion.</p>
<p>It was already night, and in a few hours it would be the time he had agreed upon with Evelyn to go out.</p>
<p>Closing his eyes, he circulated his aura, recalling the instructions from the skill book.</p>
<p>"Injection, and then..." His eyes opened as he directed his aura toward his meridians. "Combustion."</p>
<p>The moment his voice fell, his body changed; his aura began to drain as his power exploded.</p>
<p>[You have used the body enhancement skill "Burning Blood"</p>
<p>Effect: +50% to all basic attributes, +70% pain reduction.]</p>
<p>As Ethan’s body temperature rose, a reddish aura enveloped his body, glowing in the darkness.</p>
<p>He looked at the massive tree in the garden, planted his left foot firmly on the ground, and then kicked.</p>
<p>The tree shook, Ethan’s foot sinking into its trunk.</p>
<p>It wasn’t enough to split it in half, but if he kept kicking, he was sure he’d be able to bring it down before long.</p>
<p>With an excited smile, he deactivated Boiling Blood, his body returning to normal.</p>
<p>It was a skill with a cooldown, so he’d have to wait at least five hours before using it again without suffering severe damage to his body.</p>
<p>With a soft sigh, Ethan turned around, two figures emerging from inside the mansion.</p>
<p>"Ethan... are you okay? I heard an explosion!" Alice exclaimed, approaching quickly.</p>
<p>"I’m fine, I was just training a little."</p>
<p>He gave a smile, remembering something before pulling an item from his inventory.</p>
<p>"Uh? What’s that?" Alice looked at the item that appeared in her hands with interest.</p>
<p>It was bluish, the size of an egg, exuding powerful mana.</p>
<p>"A mana core," Ethan replied, watching her eyes fill with a deep gleam.</p>
<p>Mana cores were the primary source of power for many mages in training, and from the description he’d read, they could even provide XP.</p>
<p>In short, the ability to train and level up without putting oneself in danger.</p>
<p>Wasn’t that wonderful?</p>
<p>Alice clutched the item tightly in her hand.</p>
<p>She had already read about mana cores.</p>
<p>They were expensive, rare even for noble families.</p>
<p>But Ethan was just giving it to her?</p>
<p>"My lord, this is far too expensive..." Alice replied, blushing and looking away.</p>
<p>"It isn’t. And I’m your man, not your lord." Ethan flashed a gentle smile and embraced Alice, his hands resting on her fat, perfect ass.</p>
<p>"Now train hard; I’ll try to get you a mana manipulation manual..." he said, his fingers closing around her ample flesh.</p>
<p>Although Alice knew a healing spell and a fireball spell, her mana manipulation was terrible.</p>
<p>She could only use magic because of her absurdly high talent.</p>
<p>Alice hesitated for a moment before nodding, pressing her lips against Ethan’s, and then running inside the house.</p>
<p>Mana cores not only affected power and mana reserves, they also affected appearance, improving the skin and the body’s overall health.</p>
<p>She was excited not only to become stronger, but also to become more beautiful.</p>
<p>Not that there was much to improve on her, her celestial beauty being technically human perfection incarnate.</p>
<p>Evelyn watched the exchange from behind, arms crossed and lips curled.</p>
<p>She had received the Skull-Crushing Axe as a gift from Ethan, so it was only natural that Alice should receive something valuable as well.</p>
<p>"I think we can go in the morning, Alice will probably be busy absorbing the mana core for a few days." Ethan said, stretching and pulling a book from his robes.</p>
<p>Evelyn wore a strange expression at first, but her eyes lit up as soon as she read the title on the cover.</p>
<p>It was a skill book, and an extremely powerful one at that!</p>
<p>With a big smile, she also went back inside the mansion, locking herself in her room.</p>
<p>"Ah... looks like I’ll be sleeping in the cold tonight..." Ethan shook his head.</p>
<p>Alice was absorbing the mana core in her room, and to do so, she needed to meditate.</p>
<p>That was something that could take up to a week, and during that time, she couldn’t be interrupted.</p>
<p>"Sleeping in the office shouldn’t be that bad..." Ethan scratched his back in defeat.</p>
<p>He couldn’t disrupt Evelyn’s training, nor Alice’s meditation.</p>
<p>What would become of him without those two?</p>
<p>With a grunt, he went to the office, pulled an item card from his inventory, and tore it open.</p>
<p>[Item: Leather and Steel Armor Set</p>
<p>Description: A set of leather armor with patches of steel plates protecting vital organs. Lightweight and durable.</p>
<p>Rank: D]</p>
<p>A full set of armor fell in front of Ethan, complete with steel greaves, gauntlets, and shoulder guards.</p>
<p>The rest was leather, with a fine chainmail shirt to go underneath it all and a breastplate with two steel plates patched onto the front and back.</p>
<p>He arranged the armor carefully, studying its quality.</p>
<p>It was good, even compared to what his father’s soldiers wore, though far inferior to that of a knight.</p>
<p>With a soft sigh, Ethan sat down in the chair and closed his eyes, beginning to meditate.</p>
<p>Tomorrow would be a long day.</p>
