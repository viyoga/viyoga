<p>"Ah... what a man must sacrifice for some noble titties? Ethan settled into the carriage provided by his father, his face contorting in boredom.</p>
<p>Technically, that noblewoman’s enormous tits weren’t to blame for his situation.</p>
<p>But it wasn’t as if the system was going to take any responsibility, right?</p>
<p>It had been over ten hours since the journey began, but he felt as if he had been traveling for weeks.</p>
<p>With his eyes fixed on the carriage ceiling, he muttered, "System, are you there?"</p>
<p>Talking to the system was a habit Ethan had developed to combat boredom in a world where he didn’t have all the distractions of his previous life.</p>
<p>Most of the time, he got no response, and when he did, the words were usually incoherent, as if obscured by strange interference.</p>
<p>After a few seconds of silence, he insisted, "Do you have any plans to give me real rewards, or are you just going to keep giving me missions for nothing?"</p>
<p>[Integration with Gaia system incomplete, unable to access main systems...]</p>
<p>[Calculating rewards... unable to calculate rewards, integration with Gaia system incomplete.]</p>
<p>[Attempting forced integration...]</p>
<p>[Failure, Gaia system did not respond, world not yet integrated with Gaia system...]</p>
<p>[Calculating estimated integration time...]</p>
<p>[Gaia system integration will occur in approximately five days.]</p>
<p>"What?" Ethan stood up abruptly, hitting his head on the carriage ceiling.</p>
<p>’Shit...’ He massaged the sore spot, his eyes fixed on the system messages.</p>
<p>"Gaia system? Not yet integrated with this world? What do you mean?" He asked, but there was no answer.</p>
<p>"Ah... I don’t even know if I’ll be alive in five days, you bastard..." He sighed heavily, leaning back on the uncomfortable seat.</p>
<p>He knew the Eternal Forest well enough to know that his father had basically sentenced him to death.</p>
<p>But still, a little hope wouldn’t kill anyone, right?</p>
<p>Shaking his head, Ethan just closed his eyes.</p>
<p>The trip to the Eternal Forest took just over four days.</p>
<p>Normally, it would be a journey of weeks or even months without horses, but luckily, they used a teleportation station to a town on the edge of the forest.</p>
<p>As he stepped out of the carriage, Ethan looked from side to side.</p>
<p>They were in the middle of nowhere, and on the horizon, a dense forest of dark foliage stretched as far as the eye could see.</p>
<p>The trees were the tallest he had ever seen, forming an almost impenetrable wall of wood and leaves.</p>
<p>"Young Master Ethan." The knight who was escorting him approached, giving a respectful and formal salute.</p>
<p>"The count only gave us permission to take you this far. You will have to make the rest of the journey alone."</p>
<p>The slight smile Ethan had been wearing as he stretched disappeared instantly, and he stared at the knight in disbelief.</p>
<p>"This is... a joke, right?" he asked.</p>
<p>"These are your father’s orders, young master. We are not allowed to enter the Eternal Forest."</p>
<p>"It can’t be..." He clenched his teeth, his gaze turning to the dark interior of the forest.</p>
<p>Ignoring his expression, the knight handed him the reins of a horse and a small piece of yellowed metal.</p>
<p>"It’s incense to ward off monsters. It’s not entirely effective, but it will help," he explained, pointing to the object.</p>
<p>Without waiting for a response, he returned to the carriage and left with the guards, leaving Ethan alone.</p>
<p>"Shit..." He sighed heavily, looking at the horse, his only remaining companion.</p>
<p>He was as good as dead, right?</p>
<p>Ethan even thought about running away, but he knew his father well enough to know that the old man had put a tracking device on him somewhere.</p>
<p>He watched the sun on the horizon. It was morning, and he needed to hurry if he didn’t want to spend the night in the forest.</p>
<p>Luckily, he had brought few belongings: a suitcase and two backpacks with some personal items and clothes, including his sword.</p>
<p>The weapon, with its long silver blade and black hilt encrusted with gold and diamonds, looked more like an ornamental piece than a functional one.</p>
<p>Its value probably exceeded a small fortune.</p>
<p>But despite its sumptuous appearance, it had been forged by one of the greatest blacksmiths in the kingdom.</p>
<p>Its blade, made of a type of magical steel known as white steel, had an unmatched ability to withstand mana and aura.</p>
<p>Not even the blow of a third-stage warrior would be able to break it.</p>
<p>Ethan fastened the sword to his waist before stowing the bags on the horse’s back.</p>
<p>Mounting up, he tapped the reins lightly, and the animal began to trot toward the forest.</p>
<p>Although he was not a great warrior, in reality only a first-stage aura user, it should still be enough to put up some fight.</p>
<p>***</p>
<p>The Eternal Forest was dark and uninviting.</p>
<p>Following a map he had received when leaving his family’s castle, Ethan advanced through a long-abandoned trail.</p>
<p>The Count had not sent men to that region for some time, and undergrowth had already taken over the path.</p>
<p>Even so, the absence of dense trees on the trail provided enough direction to guide him.</p>
<p>Luck smiled on him, as no monsters crossed his path, although distant roars and the constant feeling of being watched kept him on alert.</p>
<p>And so he continued.</p>
<p>Minutes turned into hours, hours turned into days, and soon the system’s deadline began to shrink.</p>
<p>In the middle of the afternoon on the penultimate day, Ethan decided to take a break by a crystal-clear stream.</p>
<p>His horse was tired, and so was he.</p>
<p>With his eyes alert to his surroundings, he remained beside the animal as it drank from the stream, one hand always on the hilt of his sword.</p>
<p>After thirty minutes of rest, he prepared to resume the trail.</p>
<p>However, something stopped him at the last moment.</p>
<p>His eyes scanned the forest as the steel of his sword slid through its leather sheath.</p>
<p>The horse whinnied anxiously, retreating to the edge of the stream, increasingly agitated.</p>
<p>Ethan let go of the reins, allowing the animal to flee while he faced whatever was lurking.</p>
<p>Suddenly, a creature emerged from a nearby bush.</p>
<p>Small and with green skin, its red eyes fixed on him and its disproportionate jaw full of rotten, sharp teeth twisted into a grotesque smile.</p>
<p>"A goblin!" Ethan hissed, sensing the nauseating miasma exuded by the creature.</p>
<p>The goblin leaped toward him, its small rusty cleaver striking his sword.</p>
<p>Ethan pressed on, and the goblin’s body was thrown backward, hitting a tree with a crash.</p>
<p>He barely had time to catch his breath when two other creatures, armed with makeshift wooden spears, appeared, a third behind him.</p>
<p>"More than I expected..." he muttered, spinning to dodge the attack and delivering a fatal blow to the goblin’s neck.</p>
<p>Before the goblin’s decapitated head even hit the ground, Ethan took off, the aura exploding in his legs as he catapulted forward.</p>
<p>He could feel at least a dozen surrounding him.</p>
<p>Although he was seen as a talentless brat by his father and the nobles of his age, since arriving in this world, Ethan had still been trained by the most skilled swordsmen in the kingdom.</p>
<p>He knew how to deal with a rabble of goblins.</p>
<p>He spun his body as another goblin advanced toward him, his feet sinking into the damp ground as his sword flowed in a downward strike, sinking into the creature’s shoulder blade, turning its rib cage to dust and piercing its lungs.</p>
<p>He barely had time to react when a second goblin appeared from behind, the blade tearing a deep gash in Ethan’s leg.</p>
<p>The young man spun around, kicking the small creature, breaking a few ribs and sending it crashing into a tree.</p>
<p>The moment he put his injured leg on the ground, he felt a jolt of pain.</p>
<p>He forced himself to ignore it, cutting toward a third and then a fourth goblin.</p>
<p>In just a single second, he had already taken down five.</p>
<p>But their numbers seemed to keep growing.</p>
<p>An arrow whistled through the air and lodged itself between Ethan’s ribs, missing his lungs by just a centimeter.</p>
<p>Ethan gritted his teeth and ran without stopping, his aura preventing him from collapsing under his injured leg.</p>
<p>At that moment, a nearby bush shook and another goblin lunged at him, its rusty blade aimed at his neck.</p>
<p>"Shit, there’s no time!" he exclaimed, trying to raise his sword, but the goblin’s blade was already inches from his throat.</p>
<p>Suddenly, intense heat filled the air.</p>
<p>Ethan instinctively closed his eyes as a fireball fell on the creature, engulfing it in flames.</p>
<p>The creature collapsed, screaming in agony as the flames engulfed it.</p>
<p>Falling to his knees, Ethan struggled for a few seconds to compose himself and understand what had just happened.</p>
<p>"A mage!," he exclaimed as he stood up, his eyes finding a figure sitting not far away.</p>
<p>She was a woman of stunning beauty: carefully tied blonde hair, clear blue eyes, framed by a delicate and harmonious face.</p>
<p>Her voluptuous body was covered by a long white dress, the neckline revealing a deep valley where her huge, soft, pale tits met.</p>
<p>The woman stood up and ran towards Ethan, whose eyes did not leave the sway of her breasts.</p>
<p>So big... so beautiful...</p>
<p>"Are you all right?" she asked, her voice as soft and melodious as the finest elven flute.</p>
<p>She crouched down in front of him, hesitated for a moment, and then pulled him towards her.</p>
