<p>Right after the first ten spins, Ethan’s eyes lit up.</p>
<p>A golden light engulfed the entire office, washing over the desk, the stacks of documents, and the bookshelves in a vibrant glow. He sat up straight in his chair at once.</p>
<p>An SS-Rank item!</p>
<p>[Congratulations! You have received: 1x Queen Mother’s Sap Spring (Rank SS)]</p>
<p>He didn’t even have time to open the description. The spins kept rolling one after another, and after that item came thirty more uninteresting ones.</p>
<p>He barely bothered to read them, just skimming the names while drumming his fingers on the desk.</p>
<p>Until the office lit up once more, and his fingers went still.</p>
<p>[Congratulations! You have received: 1x Frozen Heart of a Fourth-Stage Beast (Rank A)]</p>
<p>[Congratulations! You have received: 1x Item Crafting Manual: Rich Harvest Pendant (Rank B)]</p>
<p>Ethan gave a small nod as he read the second name.</p>
<p>That was the first item he’d chosen from the secondary lottery system: a pendant designed for his farmers to wear, one that could be mass-produced.</p>
<p>Nothing flashy, but exactly what he wanted.</p>
<p>The spins that followed kept turning up useful items.</p>
<p>[Congratulations! You have received: 1x Large-Scale Talent Enhancement Card (Rank B)]</p>
<p>[Congratulations! You have received: 1x Beast Growth Bonus Card (Rank A)]</p>
<p>[Congratulations! You have received: 1x 2,500 High-Level Pops Card (Rank B)]</p>
<p>After exactly ninety spins, the second item he’d chosen from the lottery system finally appeared: a manual for a mass-produced space ring.</p>
<p>[Congratulations! You have received: 1x Medium-Capacity Space Ring Creation Manual (Rank B)]</p>
<p>The ring’s generic name didn’t surprise or disappoint Ethan.</p>
<p>If anything, it made him happy.</p>
<p>He didn’t need a legendary treasure for this. He needed something with decent capacity that wouldn’t be hard to produce for tens of thousands of soldiers.</p>
<p>A generic medium-capacity ring was perfect.</p>
<p>The next nine spins went by in a blur he barely registered.</p>
<p>Then his eyes locked onto the hundredth spin, and he leaned forward, elbows on the desk.</p>
<p>It was time to receive his Knights’ Order Creation Token!</p>
<p>An intense light flooded the office. A deep, dense reddish-gold, far stronger than any glow before it. It spilled across the walls and swallowed every shadow in the room.</p>
<p>He closed his eyes for a moment. When he opened them again, the notification was waiting for him, and he couldn’t help but smile.</p>
<p>[Congratulations! You have received: 1x Unique Divine-Grade Knights’ Order Creation Token (Rank SSS)]</p>
<p>[Name: Unique Divine-Grade Knights’ Order Creation Token</p>
<p>Description: A divine-grade Knights’ Order creation token.</p>
<p>A divine-grade order of knights is the highest-grade order that can be created in an intermediate world.</p>
<p>A divine-grade order provides significant buffs to morale, loyalty, XP, attributes, and talent for its knights and squires.</p>
<p>Creating an order requires a fortress to serve as its base, along with a minimum initial payment of one hundred thousand gold coins.</p>
<p>Upon creation, the user may pay an additional five million gold coins to have a Grand Master, knights, and squires automatically generated along with the order:</p>
<p>1 Grand Master at the peak of the fourth stage</p>
<p>2 Vice-Masters at the beginning of the fourth stage</p>
<p>100 knights at the peak of the third stage</p>
<p>1,000 squires at the peak of the second stage</p>
<p>All are created with their own mounts and a base initial loyalty of 90%, as well as their own weapons, armor, and techniques, which vary based on the order’s name and emblem.</p>
<p>If the five million payment is not made, the user must designate at least one Grand Master, ten knights, and one hundred squires to start the order.</p>
<p>Rank: SSS]</p>
<p>"Ah... for someone as rich as me, this really is way too OP." Ethan shook his head with a soft chuckle and leaned back in his chair.</p>
<p>Five million gold coins? He still had tens of millions stashed away, and in a week or two at most, Ron would be back with even more money for him.</p>
<p>That was next to nothing compared to the real value of just the Grand Master and the two Vice-Masters, all three of them at the fourth stage.</p>
<p>Ethan let out a satisfied sigh and turned his attention to the other items he’d earned, starting with the spring.</p>
<p>[Name: Queen Mother’s Sap Spring</p>
<p>Description: The Queen Mother was a queen bee of the Giant Empress breed, an extinct breed of bees that grew to over fifteen centimeters in size.</p>
<p>They were hunted to extinction for their honey’s magical properties, as well as their adorable appearance, peaceful nature, lack of a stinger, and the value of their wings in the making of luxury clothing.</p>
<p>In the final years of their existence, Giant Empress bees survived only as luxury pets for wealthy families.</p>
<p>The Queen Mother was the last of her kind living in her natural habitat. She received a blessing from the Goddess of Life, which created an extremely rare source of sap that served as food for her young.</p>
<p>The Queen Mother’s Sap Spring produces about one hundred drops of sap per day.</p>
<p>The sap has miraculous healing effects. With the right ingredients, a single drop dissolved in one thousand drops of pure water can be turned into up to one hundred divine-level healing potions.</p>
<p>A single pure drop, consumed by a pregnant woman, will significantly increase the initial basic attributes of the child in her womb, as well as its talent and health.</p>
<p>It can also be used in the production of high-quality wines, though it is recommended to dissolve it in large quantities of water to spread the effect.</p>
<p>When the spring is placed, the original Queen Mother’s hive will come with it, consisting of one queen bee and ten worker bees.</p>
<p>The spring must be placed in a region with a high density of life energy. Its production may rise or fall depending on the level of care it receives.</p>
<p>While the hive is active and alive, it provides bonuses to all plants nationwide.</p>
<p>If the hive dies, sap production will be cut in half and the effects will be nullified.</p>
<p>Effects (applied to flora nationwide): +50% disease resistance, +50% fertility rate, +50% resistance to climate changes.</p>
<p>Rank: SS]</p>
