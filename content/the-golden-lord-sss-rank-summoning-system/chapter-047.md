<p>Ethan was confused, as if in a trance.</p>
<p>His sword cut without him controlling it, growing faster with every drop of blood it spilled.</p>
<p>Was this what they called combat enlightenment?</p>
<p>He didn’t know, but he was certain he wanted to make the most of it.</p>
<p>[You have struck terror into the hearts of the goblins of the Eternal Forest; you have received a heroic title: "The Goblin Slayer"]</p>
<p>[Title: The Goblin Slayer</p>
<p>Effect: +1 Prestige, +20% Fear among greenskins. (Upgradable) 0/100 goblins until the next title level.]</p>
<p>[You were the first Golden Lord to obtain a heroic title! +5 premium gacha tickets, +1 prestige.]</p>
<p>[A global announcement has been sent to all Golden Lords.]</p>
<p>Ethan couldn’t care less about those system notifications, though he was a bit confused by the message stating he was the first Golden Lord to obtain a heroic title.</p>
<p>Was there a difference between this title and the one he had before?</p>
<p>In the end, he just ignored it.</p>
<p>A quick glance was enough to bring a faint smile to his lips, but he did nothing but continue killing goblins, taking down six more before stopping.</p>
<p>The creatures, once numbering eighty, had been reduced to just a few stragglers, the survivors quickly scattering and fleeing as fast as they could.</p>
<p>With a sigh, his eyes focused on the guard, who quickly cleared the goblins surrounding Alaric, allowing him to fight the hobgoblin freely.</p>
<p>Neither of them was in good shape.</p>
<p>Alaric had a wound on his leg, and the hobgoblin chieftain had lost his right eye, screaming in agony like a wounded beast filled with rage.</p>
<p>Ethan approached from behind with quick steps, barely giving the guard time to react.</p>
<p>His sword pierced the air in a downward arc, aimed at the hobgoblin’s back.</p>
<p>The beast stopped the club mid-swing, its brutal barrage of blows toward Alaric ceasing abruptly.</p>
<p>"Coward... hooman..." The beast’s eyes rolled back, a blade protruding from its chest, clearly piercing its heart.</p>
<p>Its legs gave way. With a bloody gurgle and a sound that sounded more like a song from hell, the creature fell to its knees and then collapsed to the ground.</p>
<p>[You killed: 1x hobgoblin, +50% XP]</p>
<p>[XP bar full, accumulated XP will be added after stage advancement.]</p>
<p>Ethan looked at that notification, a soft smile spreading across his face as he gripped his own sword.</p>
<p>There was a burning wound on his right arm and the blood flowed unceasingly, the blade’s hilt nearly slipping from his bloodied grip.</p>
<p>It hurt like hell, but he didn’t want to show it.</p>
<p>Evelyn, who had also leveled up while killing the goblins, quickly took her place beside him, gripping the handle of her axe tightly.</p>
<p>"So... are you going to show your true colors, or are you just going to keep exuding that fucking murderous intent?" Ethan laughed, tapping the ground lightly with his sword.</p>
<p>The metallic clangs echoed, each one making the frown on Alaric’s face deepen even further.</p>
<p>"You die today, brat. It doesn’t matter if you have some pathetic plan or if you’re just a bastard with more balls than brains." Alaric growled. "Now get out of my way; I’ll take care of you after I kill that whore by your side."</p>
<p>"I’m not your mother, pig." Evelyn didn’t even bother to retort, merely lifting her face arrogantly.</p>
<p>’Ethan... if you really have a trick up your sleeve, you’d better reveal it now!’ She glanced at him out of the corner of her eye, worried.</p>
<p>She could easily kill Alaric in a one-on-one fight, but would Ethan be able to handle the other guards?</p>
<p>And then there were those archers—wait!</p>
<p>Evelyn’s eyes widened as she looked toward the archers.</p>
<p>They were calm and relaxed, knowing their leader’s plan had already succeeded.</p>
<p>One of them, sitting quietly, stood up, grabbed his bow and arrow, and then aimed at Evelyn’s ample, luscious cleavage.</p>
<p>"It’s a shame for such perfect beauty to die like this." The archer smiled, ready to release the arrow.</p>
<p>But just then, he felt a chill run down his spine, a voice that wasn’t his companion’s echoing behind him.</p>
<p>"A shame, indeed."</p>
<p>The archer didn’t have time to scream. A powerful hand covered his mouth, and a dagger pierced his back several times.</p>
<p>Grunts of pain echoed, his eyes rolling back as he watched his companion meet the same fate.</p>
<p>Following Evelyn’s gaze, Ethan smiled, remembering the seventh item he had won from those premium gacha tickets.</p>
<p>[Name: Low-Rank Soldier Summoning Scroll.</p>
<p>Description: Summons a platoon of twenty level-fifteen soldiers, led by a level-thirty captain.</p>
<p>Their talent ranges from rank E to rank D, their initial loyalty is 60%, and they are already equipped with basic weapons and armor.</p>
<p>Rank: E]</p>
<p>It was a considerably low-rank item, but its use was extremely efficient.</p>
<p>Twenty trained soldiers and a second-stage captain, all at least partially loyal to Ethan.</p>
<p>Wasn’t that exactly what he needed for Goldenveil’s defenses?</p>
<p>"You..." Alaric spun around suddenly, sensing dozens of auras surrounding them.</p>
<p>The strongest possessed power no less than his own, exuding a powerful murderous intent.</p>
<p>"You... contacted outside forces?" Alaric roared. "You treacherous bastard."</p>
<p>"They aren’t outside forces; they’re my men, loyal to me and no one else." Ethan spat, stepping forward. "Unlike you."</p>
<p>"Now tell me, what is your plan? What gave you the confidence to keep Goldenveil standing after killing me?"</p>
<p>As he spoke, the guards he had summoned in the forest a few nights earlier approached.</p>
<p>Their leader was a man with cold eyes and a composed face, no older than thirty. He carried a sword and a shield, his black beard always neatly trimmed.</p>
<p>It was Doran, a second-stage warrior and captain of the small guard Ethan had summoned.</p>
<p>"My lord, we’ve dealt with the archers. Shall I dispose of these?" Doran asked, drawing his sword.</p>
<p>"No..." Ethan shook his head, savoring the look of fear that was finally beginning to appear on Alaric’s face.</p>
<p>"Alaric, I believe the wound on your leg isn’t too bad, is it?" Ethan’s lips curved into a smile. "Fight me, and I’ll give you the chance to die by my blade."</p>
