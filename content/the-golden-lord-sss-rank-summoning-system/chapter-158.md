<p>Ethan and Doran left Windmare with a very positive impression of both its Lord and its people.</p>
<p>Of course, Ethan wasn’t about to abandon his plans over something so trivial.</p>
<p>Still, he would prefer to spare the Lord’s life and that of his family, keeping him as a loyal subordinate.</p>
<p>Silverbrook, however, was a different story.</p>
<p>The village welcomed adventurers and kept its gates open, much like its neighbor, housing a population of just over a thousand and fifty souls.</p>
<p>The pair offered the guards a slight nod as they crossed beneath the gates, only to freeze in place a heartbeat later.</p>
<p>The subtle smiles on their faces vanished the instant a loud, shrill scream tore through the air.</p>
<p>In the middle of the street, right in front of the stately residence, stood the Lord of Silverbrook.</p>
<p>Well, whether such a pig deserved the title of lord was highly questionable.</p>
<p>He carried the powerful aura of a third-stage Awakened, but that was the only thing respectable about him.</p>
<p>He was nearly naked, wearing nothing but a filthy pair of underwear, staggering drunk while bellowing without pause.</p>
<p>His large, protruding belly swayed like a sack of lard, and his right hand clutched a half-empty wine bottle so tightly that his knuckles had gone white.</p>
<p>Below him, sitting on his butt in the mud, was a young man in tears.</p>
<p>His eyes were swollen from the punches he’d taken, and his cracked lips dripped blood.</p>
<p>"Ugh... you fucking asshole! Who do you think you are to deny me something? Tell me, where is your sister?"</p>
<p>The Lord roared the question, then drove a brutal kick into the young man’s ribs.</p>
<p>The blow landed with a muffled thud, sending him sideways into the dirt with a strangled gasp.</p>
<p>A crowd had gathered around them, watching the terrible scene in silence. Many clenched their fists at their sides, but none had the courage to intervene.</p>
<p>The beating only ceased when the young man hung at the edge of consciousness, his face reduced to a bloody mess.</p>
<p>"I’ll wait another week, you bastard. I’d better have your sister in my arms by then."</p>
<p>He delivered one final kick to the young man on the brink of death, then staggered away.</p>
<p>Ethan and Doran watched it all with cold eyes.</p>
<p>They could have killed that pig right there. Ethan didn’t think it would take more than two moves to be done with him.</p>
<p>But in the end, they restrained themselves.</p>
<p>His time would come.</p>
<p>Naturally, unlike Windmare with its pleasant atmosphere, Silverbrook held no charm for them.</p>
<p>Even in the tavern there were hardly any adventurers, and the villagers wore cold, hopeless expressions on every face the two passed.</p>
<p>It was a miserable little place, inhabited by miserable people.</p>
<p>After buying a few drinks and discreetly scouting the village’s defenses, the two finally set off. But not toward Goldenveil.</p>
<p>Consulting the map, Ethan decided to first pay a visit to the medium-sized goblin camp, which lay about twenty kilometers away.</p>
<p>The other two smaller camps weren’t far either, but after a moment’s thought, he chose to leave them to the regular escorts. Night was already falling, after all.</p>
<p>Soon, Doran and Ethan found themselves descending into a deep valley just past Silverbrook.</p>
<p>At the bottom, surrounded by hills on all four sides, sprawled a gigantic goblin settlement.</p>
<p>Tall palisades of rough wood encircled it, and dozens of yellow-eyed sentinels patrolled the perimeter.</p>
<p>The hills weren’t high enough to grant a clear view over the wall, so the two climbed a sturdy tree, hiding among the dense foliage.</p>
<p>That place was, without a doubt, very different from the goblin camps they had destroyed near Goldenveil.</p>
<p>The basic structure was the same, but this one had three leaders.</p>
<p>Three large huts stood at the center of the settlement, where the leaders bred and hoarded their treasures, surrounded by hundreds of smaller huts inhabited by the common goblins.</p>
<p>"It won’t be easy..." Doran whispered, mentally counting the creatures swarming below.</p>
<p>Two, three thousand? Maybe a little more?</p>
<p>It was only a rough estimate, but seeing so many creatures gathered like that drew a heavy sigh from his lips.</p>
<p>This was the goblins’ greatest strength.</p>
<p>If three thousand goblins lived in that camp, then there were three thousand warriors who wouldn’t hesitate to rush headlong to their own deaths.</p>
<p>They were weak, yes, but that mattered little in the face of such absurd numbers.</p>
<p>Worse still, the pair counted about twenty hobgoblins among them, all strong and healthy.</p>
<p>Those alone could carve terrible damage into Goldenveil’s formations, since, like it or not, they were far stronger than human warriors of the same level.</p>
<p>Of course, that was without considering special skills and human intelligence.</p>
<p>"It shouldn’t be that hard..." Ethan’s lips curved into a smile.</p>
<p>Goblins, as a rule, were idiots who only cared about food and violence.</p>
<p>Hobgoblins, on the other hand, were idiots who cared about food, violence, and above all, their own pride.</p>
<p>What if a few goblin corpses turned up in places where they shouldn’t, bearing marks that should never be on them?</p>
<p>The Eternal Forest was held together by a tenuous balance built on respect, fear, and violence.</p>
<p>Those smaller goblin camps existed precisely because of that balance.</p>
<p>If those lines were broken, a crushing retaliation would fall the very next moment.</p>
<p>Doran, catching his Lords’s drift, nodded in confirmation.</p>
<p>The more of those damned goblins they eliminated, the easier the attack would be.</p>
<p>Finally, their gazes fell upon a cave situated at the northernmost edge of the camp, likely where the creatures stored their food reserves for the winter.</p>
<p>They couldn’t afford to wait much longer. They needed to strike before the goblins began consuming those reserves.</p>
<p>With Doran jotting down everything important about the camp, the two men finally set off back toward Goldenveil.</p>
<p>By the time they arrived, the night was already deep.</p>
<p>Both were dirty and exhausted, having walked nonstop for over a hundred kilometers.</p>
<p>Even with superhuman strength, exhaustion was inevitable.</p>
<p>Fortunately, both had beautiful wives waiting at home.</p>
<p>Upon returning to his mansion, Ethan sank into a hot bath, letting the steam draw the soreness from his muscles.</p>
<p>Afterward, he settled into Alice’s loving arms, her soft body warm against his own as her gentle fingers traced slow, soothing paths across his hair until sleep finally claimed him.</p>
<p>He would need to be well-rested when morning came. Tomorrow would mark the day Goldenveil began to prepare its first conquest campaign.</p>
