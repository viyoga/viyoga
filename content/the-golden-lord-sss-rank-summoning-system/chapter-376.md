<p>Ironically, Raynar was less concerned about the deal itself and more about that green fairy wine.</p>
<p>The current head heiress of the Drakenoir family was not only a talented businesswoman and a beauty without equal, but also a passionate lover of rare wines.</p>
<p>Raynar sincerely feared she would leap onto the first available ship and race straight to Goldenveil the very moment the first drop of that wine touched her lips.</p>
<p>In the end, he could only push such thoughts out of his mind and focus on finishing everything he still had to do in the city.</p>
<p>At dusk, Ethan finally watched the Drakenoir ships set sail. Their dark sails stood silhouetted against a sky tinged with orange and purple, while the warm harbor breeze carried the salty scent of the sea, mingled with the sweet aroma of the city’s fruit trees.</p>
<p>He had offered the merchants rooms in several of Goldenveil’s empty hotels and residences for the night, but Raynar was in a hurry. The stop in the city had been unexpected, and he still had a deadline to meet.</p>
<p>Recognizing the merchant’s responsibilities, Ethan did not press the matter, simply sending him on his way with a few bottles of wine as a gift, along with a single fruit from the Tree of Life.</p>
<p>Just a small token of the friendship and future cooperation between Goldenveil and the Drakenoir family.</p>
<p>With a weary sigh and a space ring stuffed full of gold, Ethan accompanied Doran and Theron through Goldenveil’s bustling streets. The magical streetlights flickered to life one by one atop their lampposts as night settled over the city.</p>
<p>"How did trade with the common people go?" Ethan asked, since the Drakenoir family had set up a few small temporary stalls, buying and selling goods directly to Goldenveil’s residents.</p>
<p>Commander Doran and Thalion, the second-in-command of Goldenveil’s army, had been put in charge of overseeing these sales alongside the many officers of the Drakenoir family.</p>
<p>"Everything went smoothly, my lord. The total amount of gold traded was about forty thousand coins," Doran replied, drawing a satisfied smile from Ethan.</p>
<p>He was pleasantly surprised by the figure—far higher than he’d expected for a single day of informal trade.</p>
<p>"And Cain? How’s his training going? He and Vaelith are already close to advancing to the third stage, aren’t they?" Ethan asked, shifting his gaze between Doran and Thalion—the latter being Vaelith’s father.</p>

<p>"Very well. If they work hard enough, I’ll soon promote them to the rank of legate, making both of them into legion commanders," Ethan declared casually.</p>
