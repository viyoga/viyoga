<p>“You can send me two hundred and fifty purebloods and one noble. I’m interested in founding a combat unit called the Night Corps and…”</p>
<p>Ethan explained to Carmilla, calmly and without haste, exactly what he planned to do with that specialized combat unit.</p>

<p>The Darkwall family itself had units similar to the Night Corps he intended to found in Goldenveil.</p>
<p>Of course, they operated in a far less structured manner and didn’t even have an official name. Most of the time, they were simply called the Shadows of the Ancestor.</p>
<p>They were ten high-level knights, wholly devoted to fighting behind enemy lines, assassinating key figures, spying, and sabotaging.</p>
<p>Still, they didn’t exist as a formal group. And the Darkwall family, as was common in the Kingdom of the Burning Ice, didn’t maintain permanent professional armies, unlike Goldenveil.</p>
<p>They had their family troops, guards, men-at-arms, and knights, but their numbers were minuscule. Almost the entire army of the family consisted of peasants conscripted in times of war.</p>
<p>With Goldenveil’s Night Corps, Ethan would create something different: a formally trained group—in secret, of course—but one with clear guidelines and vast experience, thanks to the Hell Soldier’s Portal.</p>

<p>The cathedral in the capital of the Kingdom of the Burning Ice, also known as the Diamond Cathedral, was a gigantic structure.</p>
<p>It wasn’t as large as the Cathedral of Arwen had been in its prime, and it was far from being as beautiful.</p>
<p>Still, it was enough to draw sighs from anyone who passed through its doors.</p>
<p>The name came from the immense white stone housed within.</p>
<p>On certain sunny days, with the blessing of Justiciar, God of Light and the Sun, the stone shone like a rainbow, bathing the faithful of the Nine in its divine light.</p>
<p>Blindness, cancer… everything could be cured by that light.</p>
<p>That was why, every year, hundreds of thousands of the faithful visited the capital, bringing with them fortunes in donations that the Church of the Nine, of course, never refused.</p>
<p>Inside the cathedral lay the prayer hall. And above everything and everyone, at the top of a staircase as white as snow and trimmed with gold, stood the throne of the Archbishop of the Kingdom of the Burning Ice.</p>
<p>He was an old man of divine appearance.</p>
<p>His eyes looked like molten gold. His robes were of silver and gold, threaded with precious stones that formed intricate patterns, sparkling in the sunlight that streamed through the stained glass windows.</p>
