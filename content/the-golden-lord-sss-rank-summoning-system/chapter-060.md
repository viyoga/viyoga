<p>[You have used 10 Premium Gacha Tickets]</p>
<p>[Spinning...]</p>
<p>As soon as the first notification appeared, Ethan held his breath and covered his eyes with his hands, as an intense golden light flooded the office and momentarily blinded him.</p>
<p>It clearly wasn’t an S-Rank item, but it was still amazing!</p>
<p>[Congratulations, you have received: 1x Random Character Selection Card (Rank A)]</p>
<p>[Item: Random Character Selection Card</p>
<p>Description: An item that allows you to generate a random character of a specific profession. This character will have a Rank A talent, as well as unique skills for their profession.</p>
<p>Rank: A]</p>
<p>Ethan’s eyes sparkled so brightly that he barely paid attention to the other items, already imagining what kind of character he might summon.</p>
<p>There were so many professions essential to the basic functioning of a society and that simply didn’t exist in Goldenveil.</p>
<p>That reward wasn’t Rank S, but it was just as wonderful!</p>
<p>[Congratulations! You have received: 1x Subordinate Talent Enhancement Potion! (Rank B)]</p>
<p>[Congratulations! You have received: 1x Three-Step Poison Vial (Rank C)]</p>
<p>[Congratulations! You have received: 1x 50kg of Lunar Green Grass Spirit Seeds (Rank D)]</p>
<p>[Congratulations! You have received: 1x Random Mana Skill Book (Rank C)]</p>
<p>[Congratulations! You have received: 1x High-Quality Weapon Kit (Rank D)]</p>
<p>[Congratulations! You have received: 1x Underground Prison Blueprint (Rank D)]</p>
<p>[Congratulations! You have received: 1x Dawn Blade Dagger (Rank B)]</p>
<p>[Congratulations! You have received: 1x Recipe for Rising Red Flower Wine (Rank C)]</p>
<p>Right after the ninth item, which Ethan had greatly appreciated, he wasn’t expecting anything else good and was already ready to focus on his Rank A item.</p>
<p>But he soon noticed something: the pause between spins was much longer than before.</p>
<p>The floor beneath his feet was shaken by a gentle tremor, and a golden light appeared, much more intense than the one that had accompanied the Rank A item.</p>
<p>It was as if a second sun had risen inside the office.</p>
<p>When the light faded and Ethan’s vision cleared, he could barely contain his excitement.</p>
<p>An S-Rank item, finally!</p>
<p>[Congratulations! You have received: 1x Supreme Mana Beast Egg (S-Rank)]</p>
<p>[Name: Supreme Mana Beast Egg</p>
<p>Description: The egg of a high-level mana beast, accompanied by formations that will immediately connect it to the host during hatching.</p>
<p>The beast’s race is unknown, and it will hatch in one year and four months. Rank: S]</p>
<p>"A mana beast..." Ethan’s lips curled up so high they seemed about to reach his ears.</p>
<p>It was a mana beast, and one that would be tamed from birth, to boot.</p>
<p>’System... what are the odds of it being a dragon?’</p>
<p>[A dragon’s rank wouldn’t be lower than SSS]</p>
<p>"Damn..." Ethan cursed under his breath, defeated.</p>
<p>He wanted a dragon, he wanted a dragon so fucking bad! A dragon that could take human form and had huge tits!</p>
<p>What kind of man would he be if he lived his whole life without even a single dragon mommy to call his own?</p>
<p>What would he have to do? Marry an ugly woman and name her his dragon?</p>
<p>Well... that would be stupid and unnecessarily cruel.</p>
<p>There was no way that beast would be born as some kind of stupid worm that would only serve as a pet, right?</p>
<p>Nah... no way.</p>
<p>Life wouldn’t be that cruel to him.</p>
<p>In the end, even though that beast was still many years away from becoming useful, Ethan didn’t care.</p>
<p>It was an S-Rank item. His first S-Rank item.</p>
<p>It wasn’t as good as it would be if it were a unique S-Rank heroine, but it was still very good.</p>
<p>Besides, Ethan also discovered that there were items beyond S-Rank, going all the way up to SSS-Rank.</p>
<p>It wasn’t something important to know, but it was still interesting.</p>
<p>With a sigh, he focused on the other three rewards that interested him.</p>
<p>A Rank B Talent Enhancement Potion, which he would give to Evelyn. A blueprint for an underground prison project, which would be important for Goldenveil’s future. And, of course, the character card.</p>
<p>The latter, though not comparable to an S-Rank item, was still what Ethan wanted most.</p>
<p>The reason was simple.</p>
<p>Goldenveil didn’t have a blacksmith, nor anyone capable of working with metal.</p>
<p>The guards knew a little about maintaining and repairing their own weapons, but that was the limit, and weapons weren’t all they needed.</p>
<p>Ethan’s horse, for example, which was being cared for by a local hunter, already needed new shoes, and the tools the system provided simply weren’t enough for the entire population of Goldenveil.</p>
<p>They already had a forge ready, right in the old blacksmith’s house.</p>
<p>It was a fully equipped forge, built by the Darkwall family, who planned to make the settlement as self-sufficient as possible.</p>
<p>It had been abandoned for a long time and would finally have a new owner.</p>
<p>With a quick motion, Ethan tore up the letter, immediately greeted by a system screen with hundreds of professions to choose from.</p>
<p>After scrolling through the screen a bit, he finally found what he wanted: blacksmith.</p>
<p>Without hesitation, he clicked.</p>
<p>[Do you wish to summon a random character with Rank A talent and the blacksmith profession?</p>
<p>The character will be a veteran in the role, aged between thirty and fifty, and at the second stage.]</p>
<p>Ethan didn’t hesitate to click yes.</p>
<p>The moment he selected the option, the torn letter shifted, transforming into hundreds of tiny fragments of light that gathered before him, forming a male figure.</p>
<p>If Ethan had a way to describe him, it would simply be: rough.</p>
<p>He was tall, even taller than Ethan himself.</p>
<p>He was burly, but most of that bulk was muscle rather than fat, dressed in brown pants, a white shirt, and a gray-green apron.</p>
<p>The man’s eyes, dark and serene, were seized by a momentary shock.</p>
<p>His whole body shuddered, and vomit seemed to rise in his throat.</p>
<p>He held it back, staring at the younh man in front of him, a ton of information flooding his mind all at once.</p>
<p>It was hard to process so much happening so fast.</p>
