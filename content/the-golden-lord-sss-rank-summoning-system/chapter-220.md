<p>It was still early morning when Ethan left the mansion, fully armed and with his armor on.</p>
<p>His footsteps sank into the nearly completely melted snow, tiny crystals shattering beneath his boots with every step.</p>
<p>It was curious. Only ten days had passed since the end of winter, yet the temperature was already rising visibly.</p>
<p>The trees glowed a vibrant green, and amid the last remnants of snow, thick grass was reviving with vigor.</p>
<p>That was heart of the forest beat, pumping mana and aura brimming with life force to every corner of that place.</p>
<p>In just five more days, the Eternal Forest would cease to be a frozen wasteland and transform into a warm, lush tropical forest.</p>
<p>It was strange to wake up every morning during that transition and find the world radically different from the day before.</p>
<p>Honestly, Ethan could only feel happy about it.</p>
<p>The faster the change, the sooner farming in Goldenveil could begin.</p>
<p>Just thinking about all the cultivation speed bonuses he had, an excited smile was already forming on his lips.</p>
<p>Letting out a satisfied sigh, he quickened his pace toward the barracks.</p>
<p>Doran was already waiting for him there, alone since with the arrival of summer, Aerick had left to lead various escort missions around Goldenveil.</p>
<p>"My lord." Doran greeted him respectfully, rising from the desk. "Will you be entering the Hell Soldier’s portal today?"</p>
<p>"Yes. We have two hundred and thirty-two lower-grade magic cores in total, plus three medium-grade ones.</p>
<p>That should be enough to fulfill my objectives and still leave plenty for Goldenveil’s upkeep for a few months."</p>
<p>Doran nodded and walked alongside Ethan across the barracks, passing the many guards training inside.</p>
<p>Most of them were young, but all wore determined, controlled expressions.</p>
<p>Their breathing remained calm even during the rapid-fire duels against their comrades, blades clashing in a steady, ringing rhythm.</p>
<p>Ethan watched the scene with satisfaction, nodding in acknowledgment to yet another member of the Goldenveil guard who had reached the third stage.</p>
<p>Soon, once the guard had organized itself into a more structured military force, that specialist would easily assume the post of captain of a few hundred men.</p>
<p>After a few minutes’ walk, Ethan finally reached the Hell Soldier’s portal.</p>
<p>He opened the interaction menu, adjusted various scenarios, and set the temporal distortion rate to three hundred sixty-five, compressing an entire year of training into a single day.</p>
<p>Fortunately, he could leave at any time, so he didn’t worry too much about the possibility of not being able to endure that long in continuous combat.</p>
<p>"Elena will be acting as regent while I’m away. Any matters should be addressed directly to her or to Ophelia," Ethan said, though none of that would likely be necessary.</p>
<p>After all, it was only one day.</p>
<p>"Yes, my Lord." Doran nodded in agreement, and Ethan let out a faint smile.</p>
<p>"Good." Without further hesitation, the young Lord activated the Hell Soldier’s portal and stepped through with firm strides.</p>
<p>A powerful spatial distortion engulfed his body, dragging him through the fabric of reality.</p>
<p>His vision darkened for an instant, his senses lost in the void.</p>
<p>When his sight returned, he found himself standing in the midst of a battlefield.</p>
<p>A hellish heat enveloped him at once, suffocating and heavy, scalding the air in his lungs with every breath.</p>
<p>Hundreds of arrows sliced through the air without ceasing, whizzing past like a furious swarm, while more than ten thousand soldiers clashed in a bloody battle.</p>
<p>The acrid stench of blood, sweat, and burnt iron invaded his nostrils, and screams of pain and fury mingled with the incessant clanging of blades, forming a chaotic symphony of carnage.</p>
<p>It took him only a few seconds to orient himself amid the chaos. By the time he did, his sword was already slicing through the air in a fluid upward arc, executing Moonclaw Severance at full power.</p>
<p>As the blade completed its arc, three powerful bursts of aura erupted from his feet, splitting the earth open into deep fissures that surged toward the soldiers in front of him.</p>
<p>The poor wretches didn’t even have time to raise their weapons.</p>
<p>Their armor shattered like wet paper under the golden claws that swept over a hundred meters, brutally tearing through flesh and bone in a spray of crimson.</p>
<p>Severed arms, exposed guts, crushed legs, and torn-off heads tumbled onto the blood-soaked ground with sickening thuds.</p>
<p>More than seventy men died in that single instant, while just as many writhed across the dirt, mutilated, screaming desperately amidst their own torn flesh.</p>
<p>"Ah... it’s more real than I imagined," Ethan murmured, tilting his neck to the side to dodge a blade aimed straight at his jugular.</p>
<p>"You bastard!" the leader of the enemy soldiers roared, his sword barely grazing the steel of Ethan’s armor as the young Lord calmly took a step back.</p>
<p>The man’s fury seemed too real for a mere creation of a simulated universe, but Ethan didn’t care.</p>
<p>He responded immediately with a precise thrust at his opponent, who wore powerful black armor.</p>
<p>The man reacted without hesitation, parrying the tip of the blade as the powerful auras of two third-stage warriors collided with a muffled crash that shook the air around them.</p>
<p>It was then that Ethan grinned.</p>
<p>His sword bore, alongside the blade itself, the image of a golden dragon, representing the fourth form of the Golden Dragon Emperor’s Fencing.</p>
<p>The true danger of that technique lay in the invisible attacks that accompanied it, perceptible only to senses of aura or mana.</p>
<p>By the time the enemy realized it, it was already too late.</p>
<p>A clean hole opened in his armor, sinking into his chest and piercing his aorta.</p>
<p>Ethan merely took a step back, watching the blood trickle through every crack in the enemy’s carefully sealed steel armor.</p>
<p>Honestly, it was much better than Cain had described.</p>
<p>It was as if he had just stepped out of a real battle.</p>
<p>With blood boiling in his veins, Ethan plunged headfirst into the slaughter.</p>
<p>This was the perfect opportunity to gain the experience of a thousand battles and hone his skills to the utmost.</p>
<p>He had memorized everything up to the sixth form of the Golden Dragon Emperor’s Fencing, but had only fully mastered the first four.</p>
<p>How could he allow himself to fall behind in a sword art that had been created in his own honor?</p>
