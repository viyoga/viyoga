<p>"Captain, what does the Lord want?" Cain asked, settling onto a bench in Doran’s office.</p>
<p>Most of the guard was still looting the corpses outside, leaving only the two of them in the barracks.</p>
<p>"I don’t know." Doran shook his head, sitting at his desk as he organized a list of the barracks’ inventory. "He seemed excited, so it must be something good. Remember to show your gratitude to the Lord."</p>
<p>"I will." Cain nodded.</p>
<p>Few people in this town felt more gratitude toward Ethan than he did.</p>
<p>Ethan was the man who had put an end to his father’s killer. The man who would raise Goldenveil to unparalleled glory!</p>
<p>Cain’s eyes sparkled just thinking about it.</p>
<p>He was already at the peak of the first stage and soon, he would reach the second.</p>
<p>Given the age at which he had reached this level, he should have a chance at the third stage, shouldn’t he?</p>
<p>The thought alone made his chest swell with anticipation.</p>
<p>His lips parted to say something to Doran, but before he could, a soft knock echoed at the door.</p>
<p>Without waiting for a response, the door swung open, revealing Ethan’s clearly weary figure.</p>
<p>"My lord." Both men stood at the same time, bowing toward him.</p>
<p>"Sit down." Ethan gestured with a slight smile. "How is your strength? Ready to advance to the third stage?"</p>
<p>The question was directed at Doran, who merely shook his head with a bitter expression. "No, my lord, not even close... I’m already at the peak of the second stage, but I don’t see even a single path forward."</p>
<p>Ethan said nothing more.</p>
<p>These were the limits of a Rank C talent.</p>
<p>The second stage was almost guaranteed, but the third, which required the formation of one’s own aura, was nearly impossible.</p>
<p>That wouldn’t be a problem for Ethan himself, since he already possessed a Rank B talent and, along with the advancement pearl, had also received a pearl imbued with the intent of the sword, which could be used to form his own aura.</p>
<p>Similar to the aura Mireya had used to freeze all those first-stage warriors without even moving.</p>
<p>Unfortunately, for everyone else, they had to find their own aura and, with it, their own path.</p>
<p>That was the greatest divide between those who had talent and those who did not.</p>
<p>Reaching the peak of one’s own stage was easy, merely a matter of repetition and time. But advancing to the next was a different beast entirely.</p>
<p>This, of course, varied from talent to talent.</p>
<p>An S-Rank talent could break through the third stage in a single night and perceive far more than any C-Rank ever would.</p>
<p>But upon reaching the fifth stage, the difficulty began, and by the sixth, even centuries might yield nothing.</p>
<p>This was a world where talent was everything.</p>
<p>The truths of the universe opened themselves to the gifted, and those who were not blessed could only sink into an eternal struggle.</p>
<p>With a sigh, Ethan glanced at their status screens.</p>
<p>[Name: Cain</p>
<p>Age: 18</p>
<p>Level: 25/25</p>
<p>Class: Swordsman</p>
<p>Talent: B</p>
<p>Opinion of You: 100</p>
<p>Loyalty: 100%]</p>
<p>[Name: Doran</p>
<p>Age: 30</p>
<p>Level: 60/60</p>
<p>Class: Swordsman</p>
<p>Talent: C</p>
<p>Opinion of You: 100</p>
<p>Loyalty: 100%]</p>
<p>Both were absolutely loyal and held, deep in their hearts, a firm determination to grow powerful.</p>
<p>Without a doubt, in the future, they would be the spearhead of the Goldenveil army.</p>
<p>Ethan closed the screen and retrieved two golden pills from his inventory.</p>
<p>The pills rested in his palm, their surfaces gleaming under the dim light of the office, a faint warmth radiating from within.</p>
<p>Doran and Cain looked at them with interest, but neither could tell what they were.</p>
<p>Ethan said nothing, simply handing one to each of them.</p>
<p>"Eat them, then circulate your aura. Both of you should begin your breakthrough only after the pill takes effect."</p>
<p>Doran and Cain exchanged puzzled glances but didn’t hesitate, swallowing the pills.</p>
<p>The next instant, they froze.</p>
<p>Doran, the stronger of the two, was the first to feel the change. His eyes went wide. His heart hammered against his ribs.</p>
<p>Everything was so... clear.</p>
<p>He could see everything. Feel everything.</p>
<p>His strength surged through his body, every tiny movement of mana around him becoming visible as never before.</p>
<p>The air itself seemed alive, currents of energy flowing in patterns he had never been able to perceive.</p>
<p>He remembered a book he had read years ago, when he was still an instructor in the Fallen Sun Empire.</p>
<p>It was a book aimed at training instructors, explaining the difference between how a genius sees the world and how a mediocre person sees it.</p>
<p>A mediocre talent sees the painting. The genius sees the brushstrokes.</p>
<p>With just a glance, they understand exactly how everything was constructed. Not merely observing the final product but unraveling the foundation beneath it.</p>
<p>That was what Doran was feeling now.</p>
<p>As if he were unraveling the foundation of the very aura that surrounded him. Every layer, every thread of energy, laid bare before his senses.</p>
<p>He even remembered when he first taught imperial fencing to a young prodigy.</p>
<p>He himself couldn’t get past the first three forms, while the genius needed only a brief glance to master the first six.</p>
<p>That was how he felt now. As if, even without realizing it, he could already master those six forms.</p>
<p>The movements that had once seemed unreachable now felt as natural as breathing.</p>
<p>His talent had improved. Once again. And by an absurd margin.</p>
<p>Doran’s gaze shifted to Cain, who had clearly undergone the same transformation.</p>
<p>The young man’s body erupted with aura as he advanced to the second stage without the slightest difficulty.</p>
<p>An aura heart formed inside his chest as naturally as a river finding its course.</p>
<p>"M-my lord..." Doran’s knees buckled. His eyes shut for a brief moment before snapping open, burning with an intense glow.</p>
<p>That was the lord he had been blessed with the chance to follow, the Lord who would lead Goldenveil to eternal glory!</p>
