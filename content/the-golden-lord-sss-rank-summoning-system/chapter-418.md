<p>Seo-ah, for her part, cast a slightly hesitant glance at Ethan, her fingers giving his sleeve a gentle squeeze.</p>
<p>He simply nodded, his hand still resting firmly on her waist.</p>
<p>That was all the confirmation she needed. She took a step forward, reached out her hand, and paid the gold coins to found her guild.</p>
<p>The moment the guild was created, Seo-ah didn’t hesitate for even a second.</p>
<p>Her fingers were already gliding across the system’s translucent window, searching for the name of a friend of hers—a famous and beautiful Korean idol.</p>
<p>The two had talked a few hours earlier, and Seo-ah had already extended the invitation for the position of co-leader.</p>
<p>When her friend’s nickname appeared on the screen, her cheeks took on a faint pink hue.</p>
<p>She stared at that name for a long moment, as if she could change it with the power of her mind alone. In the end, she just let out a sigh and confirmed the invitation.</p>
<p>The response came almost immediately. Her friend accepted without a second thought, and the system registered her as the guild’s co-leader.</p>
<p>Meanwhile, a faint notification chime rang in Ethan’s ears. Then another. And yet another.</p>
<p>System windows began piling up before his eyes, one after another.</p>
<p>[The "Golden Amazons" guild has been successfully created in Goldenveil! Standard rights have been assigned to it!</p>
<p>Use the guild management menu to set the rights for Goldenveil guilds.]</p>
<p>[The first player guild in Goldenveil has been created! You can designate this guild as a protector guild.</p>
<p>A protector guild will be directly linked to a region’s governance and will serve as an official, recognized military organization, with the ability to manage fortresses and receive a military budget.</p>
<p>In decentralized nations, every Lord with the title of Count or higher can designate a protector guild. In Goldenveil, only one protector guild can be designated. To designate more than one, decentralize your government and grant more power to the ruling Lords.</p>
<p>Protector guilds receive a 0.01% fee on all XP earned and on every monetary transaction between players in their region.]</p>
<p>[In twenty-four hours, Goldenveil will be unlocked as a spawn and respawn point for non-criminal players.]</p>
<p>[Guild: Golden Amazons</p>
<p>Rules: (Competitive) (Female players only)</p>
<p>Current Leader: SnowBreath</p>
<p>Co-Leader: BustyViper</p>
<p>Guild Level: 1</p>
<p>Guild Renown: 12</p>
<p>Guild XP: 0/100 (earned from hunting, PvP, and quests completed by members)</p>
<p>Number of Members: 2/1,000</p>
<p>Average Level: 112</p>
<p>Guild Region: Goldenveil</p>
<p>Guild Fortresses: 1/1</p>
<p>Guild Cities: 0/0</p>
<p>Guild Villages: 0/1</p>
<p>Guild Businesses: 0/5 (any business that generates profit: hotels, hunting firms, item production, caravan routes, merchant ships, etc.)</p>
<p>Guild Wealth: Low</p>
<p>Requirements to level up the guild: 2/25 members, 15,000 gold coins, 0/100 guild XP, 12/100 renown.]</p>
<p>The young lord read through each line with a satisfied smile on his face, his thumb idly tracing small circles at Seo-ah’s waist as he went.</p>
<p>But that smile turned into something else entirely the moment his eyes landed on the name of her co-leader.</p>
<p>"Who is BustyViper?" he asked, turning toward her and trying, without much success, to hide the little smirk already taking over his expression.</p>
<p>"O-oh? C-can you see that?" Seo-ah stammered, her shoulders jerking in surprise. Under his palm, Ethan felt her whole body go stiff.</p>
<p>She cleared her throat, trying to regain her composure, but the blush spreading all the way to the tips of her ears gave her away.</p>
<p>"I-it’s nothing. Just a friend of mine with some... strange tastes. You don’t need to worry about her." The answer came out far too quickly, and she averted her gaze to anywhere but his face.</p>
<p>"She should be here in about two or three weeks," she added.</p>
<p>Just then, a small message notification flashed in the corner of her vision. She turned to read it, silently thanking the universe for the distraction.</p>
<p>[Yo, Seo-ah! I’m still in that damn Dungeon! When I get there, I want you to introduce me to your hot NPC husband!]</p>
<p>The distraction was short-lived. Seo-ah read the message, closed her eyes for a second, and let out a sigh of pure defeat.</p>
<p>Honestly, no matter how much she complained, BustyViper would be the backbone of her entire guild.</p>
<p>She’d need talent recruitment bases scattered all over the world, and she couldn’t use her father’s assets for that.</p>
<p>Well, she could, actually. She just didn’t want to.</p>
<p>That’s where her friend came in. As one of the three biggest stars in Korean pop, BustyViper was filthy rich and would handle the entire process on Earth. Meanwhile, Seo-ah would use Ethan’s wealth to take care of everything in Estria.</p>
<p>All of it without a single cent passing through her father’s hands, and without the two of them having to rely on the black market for gold.</p>
<p>The devs had already announced they were creating an official channel for exchanging gold for real money, but that would still take a few months. And she didn’t want to wait that long.</p>
<p>Players had already founded over a hundred guilds, and that number was going to multiply quickly. She had no intention of falling behind.</p>
<p>Turning her gaze back to her friend’s message, Seo-ah felt her face flush all over again. Her fingers moved quickly, tapping out a reply.</p>
<p>[I’m going to show him that picture of your boobs, bitch.]</p>
<p>A photo that, incidentally, more than lived up to her nickname.</p>
<p>The reply came in less than two seconds.</p>
<p>[H-hey, that was just a photo between friends! D-don’t you dare! I’m as pure as the first ray of morning sunlight! The only man who’s ever seen my body was the doctor who helped my mom give birth to me.]</p>
<p>[Don’t worry, my NPC husband will take care of fixing that.]</p>
<p>Seo-ah typed with a venomous little smile on her face, and before her friend could finish replying, she silenced her with a single tap.</p>
<p>[Crazy bitch, I’m going to—]</p>
<p>They’d talk later.</p>
<p>With a smile still playing on her lips, she turned her attention back to Ethan.</p>
<p>He had noticed her typing away rather hurriedly, but he didn’t give it too much thought.</p>
<p>Soon, he’d ask Ophelia about this "BustyViper."</p>
<p>After all, a nickname like that had, naturally, piqued his interest.</p>
