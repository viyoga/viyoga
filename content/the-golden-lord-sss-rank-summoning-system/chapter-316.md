<p>"Is everything set for the announcement about our baby, honey?" Alice asked, walking lightly beside Ethan, her slender, delicate arms intertwined with his powerful, protective ones.</p>
<p>"Yes... the players have already delivered all the beasts, and they’re now locked up in the Arena’s dungeon," Ethan replied, a confident smile curling on his lips. "It’s going to be a great show for our people... but we might have to delay it a little."</p>
<p>"Huh? Why?"</p>
<p>"My mother is coming, and I don’t want to start anything without her here."</p>
<p>The moment she heard those words, Alice flinched slightly, her face immediately clouded by a shadow of anxiety. Her fingers curled a touch tighter around his arm.</p>
<p>Veronica, walking a step behind, showed a similar reaction, though far more restrained. As a concubine, the pressure weighing on her was considerably lighter.</p>
<p>"Haha, don’t worry." Ethan chuckled softly, sliding his hand down the graceful curve of Alice’s back before letting it drift lower still, his palm cupping the plumpness of her rear and delivering a light, affectionate pat.</p>
<p>Beneath his fingers, the soft flesh sank pliantly, then rebounded in a slow jiggle.</p>
<p>"My mother loves anyone willing to take care of me... and she’ll love you, especially when she finds out you’re carrying my heir."</p>
<p>Alice nodded, a warm blush spreading across her cheeks and creeping up to the tips of her ears. Only then did she realize they had already reached their destination.</p>
<p>She and Veronica came to a halt, standing side by side as they watched Ethan continue on toward the empty lot ahead.</p>
<p>The young lord studied the terrain for another brief moment, then activated his instant-construction ability.</p>
<p>His eyes sparkled as thousands of magic tower options appeared before him, floating in the air, their golden icons spinning slowly as they awaited his choice.</p>
<p>He still had nine million gold coins to his name, and after the magic tower, only the Divine Forge would remain.</p>
<p>After setting various filters and specifically prioritizing fragmented magic towers, it didn’t take long before he found something that made his eyes shine even brighter.</p>
<p>It was the main magic tower of the fallen God-Emperor’s empire!</p>
<p>[Name: The Golden Tower</p>
<p>Description: The great Magic Towers are ancient, as old as history itself. The Golden Tower was the main magic tower in the service of the powerful God-Emperor, and one of many that fell and disappeared.</p>
<p>The Golden Tower was destroyed by the God of War during his descent, following the death of the God-Emperor.</p>
<p>The Golden Tower, like the Brown Tower, was dedicated to the study of all types of magic, housing several tower masters for each element under a Supreme Archmage who coordinated them all.</p>
<p>The Golden Tower has a base area of one hundred thousand square meters, but the main building area is only twenty thousand square meters, with one hundred floors of the same area, totaling two million one hundred thousand square meters across the occupied area and the one hundred floors.</p>
<p>At its peak, the Golden Tower was capable of supporting the training of seventh-stage mages, but it is now limited to the sixth stage.</p>
<p>This blueprint is fragmented, containing only 1/5 of the Golden Tower’s fragments.</p>
<p>In times of crisis, the Golden Tower has the ability to erect a shield around the city in which it was built.</p>
<p>The Golden Tower can accommodate up to 2,500 mages at full capacity, with a large portion of the tower’s space dedicated to training, the production of magical herbs, and research.</p>
<p>The Golden Tower contains laboratories for the research, development, and testing of magical artifacts, potions, spells, etc.</p>
<p>The Golden Tower contains several spatial pockets that can be used for the simulated training of young mages.</p>
<p>Effects: +300% magical density in Goldenveil, +150% chance of new magical talents being born in Goldenveil, +700% attractiveness of Goldenveil to mages.</p>
<p>Price: 4,000,000</p>
<p>An additional 900,000 gold coins can be paid to ensure that the Magic Tower’s libraries are already fully stocked.]</p>
<p>The price was steep, but Ethan couldn’t bring himself to dwell on it. His eyes sparkled as his fingers glided, almost of their own accord, straight to the purchase option.</p>
<p>In total, connecting the Magic Tower to the rest of Goldenveil’s facilities cost him another hundred thousand gold coins.</p>
<p>After spending the full five million, he stepped back at once, returning to the women’s side.</p>
<p>Around the tower, walls of snow-white concrete and golden bars sprouted from the ground, flanked by a wide double gate.</p>
<p>Beyond the gate, a marble path cut through a small grove that had sprung up as if by magic around the tower, its leafy trees emerging from nowhere and yet looking as though they had always stood there.</p>
<p>The marble-brick path ended right at the heart of the grove, encircling an area of just over twenty thousand square meters.</p>
<p>At the center of that area, a tower rose floor by floor, its architectural style unmistakably echoing that of the fallen God-Emperor’s palace, every detail meticulously constructed.</p>
<p>From the first floor—a gigantic reception hall—upward, each level was dedicated to living quarters, research stations, sprawling laboratories, dining halls, and classrooms.</p>
<p>The first eighty floors were reserved for students, while the top twenty belonged to the Magic Tower’s highest-ranking mages.</p>
<p>Crowning them all, the very top floor was essentially a small palace for the Supreme Archmage, containing everything necessary to train in magic, live in luxury, and, of course, care for one’s apprentices.</p>
<p>The tower’s entire structure mirrored that of the fallen God-Emperor’s palace—white concrete gleaming beneath the sunlight like polished gold, rising in a perfect circle with forms both uniform and distinct at once.</p>
<p>Magical runes wreathed the entire structure, its majesty unmatched, while massive defensive artifacts hovered silently overhead.</p>
<p>Ethan, Veronica, and Alice watched it all with wide eyes, their jaws slightly agape at the grandeur rising before them.</p>
<p>A brief silence lingered before Alice’s lips finally parted.</p>
<p>"What would the hubby think about making the Golden Magic Tower accept only women?..."</p>
<p>"Hm? Wouldn’t that be a little unfair to others—" Ethan’s eyes widened for a moment.</p>
<p>Alice wasn’t trying to imitate Arwen and Evelyn... was she?</p>
<p>Well... he was certainly more comfortable with the idea of his wives interacting only with other women.</p>
<p>He could always choose a military complex with magical facilities to train the men of Goldenveil as battle mages already integrated into the army... but still...</p>
<p>Ethan smiled bitterly...</p>
<p>So that was why they’d designed the uniforms with such short skirts.</p>
<p>Two hundred elves, one hundred and fifty guards, and now dozens more beautiful mages soon to be accepted as apprentices at the Goldenveil Magic Tower...</p>
<p>Would he have enough semen for them all?</p>
<p>Fortunately, he’d soon find a way to set up temporal distortion formations in his harem palace. Otherwise, giving love to so many concubines would be an impossibility.</p>
