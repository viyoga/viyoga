<p>Before starting to play with the gacha system, Ethan spent some time browsing through the countless screens of the system, unable to contain a certain impression.</p>
<p>It had everything: resource overview, city warehouses, quality of each building, beauty, and even a general summary of the village, with specific and detailed tabs for the economic, military, and social areas.</p>
<p>[Name: GoldenVeil</p>
<p>Type: Medium settlement</p>
<p>Population: 67</p>
<p>Population loyalty: 30%</p>
<p>Houses: 97 (23 occupied)</p>
<p>Prosperity: Miserable</p>
<p>Attractiveness: Miserable</p>
<p>Population increase: +3/Week (Increase attractiveness and prosperity to receive more inhabitants)</p>
<p>Lord: Ethan GoldenVeil</p>
<p>Knights: 0</p>
<p>Average level: 0</p>
<p>Guard: 10 light infantry</p>
<p>Average level: 14</p>
<p>Guard loyalty: 23%</p>
<p>State of ’miserable wooden walls’: 243/654</p>
<p>Chests: 3 gold coins (+0.01/month)</p>
<p>Upgrade requirements: 97/30 houses, 67/100 inhabitants, 5 gold coins, Lord of knight level or higher]</p>
<p>"Wow... it’s just like in a game!" Ethan exclaimed internally, trying to contain his shock.</p>
<p>He had lived in this world for over eighteen years, and seeing things like this being treated as game mechanics brought a genuinely strange feeling to his chest.</p>
<p>How many others like him existed? Had his father also had access to a similar system?</p>
<p>Why did the Church of the Nine Gods personally act to warn the settlement’s population about the arrival of this system?</p>
<p>As he pondered, a few more notifications arrived, taking his attention.</p>
<p>[You have received a new mission! The Road to Glory]</p>
<p>[Quest: The Road to Glory.</p>
<p>Description: Raise your village to the level of a large settlement within two months.</p>
<p>Rewards: 1x premium gacha ticket, 2x gacha common tickets, +100% XP, +5 prestige, unlock a random basic skill from the Golden Lord’s Ring.</p>
<p>Failure penalty: none]</p>
<p>Ethan looked at the notifications for a brief moment before leaning back in his chair and relaxing.</p>
<p>Had the system learned to be kinder to him?</p>
<p>"No..." He shook his head.</p>
<p>He couldn’t let his guard down.</p>
<p>A single misstep and that system would force him to throw every ounce of his dignity in the trash.</p>
<p>He’d rather die as a hidden pervert than live as a degenerate known to all!</p>
<p>Not that his preferences mattered much to the system.</p>
<p>"System, how can I use a gacha ticket?" Ethan asked mentally, stretching.</p>
<p>[Just tear them in half. There should be no resistance. Tickets are stored in the system’s storage indefinitely. Rewards can be stored for up to one year.]</p>
<p>"Right..." he muttered, opening the system storage and removing a single ticket.</p>
<p>It was a free ticket, the kind generated every twenty-four hours or received via easy quests.</p>
<p>Made of plain paper and covered in strangely familiar writing, there was something almost nostalgic about it.</p>
<p>After a moment of observation, Ethan tore it in half.</p>
<p>[You used: 1x free ticket]</p>
<p>[Spinning...]</p>
<p>[Congratulations, you received: 1x Common Woodcutter’s Axe (Rank G)]</p>
<p>Ethan’s eyes lit up as he accessed the warehouse again and took out the item.</p>
<p>The next moment, he felt the unmistakable weight and texture of a wooden handle.</p>
<p>It wasn’t a high-quality item; the handle already showed small cracks despite being new, and the iron head was full of impurities.</p>
<p>Still, compared to what the woodcutters there had access to, it was excellent.</p>
<p>Unable to contain his excitement, he took all the remaining free tickets and tore them up at once.</p>
<p>[You used 91x free tickets]</p>
<p>[Spinning...]</p>
<p>[Congratulations, you received: 1x Aged Scythe (Rank G)]</p>
<p>[Congratulations, you received: 1x Steel Scythe (Rank F)]</p>
<p>[Congratulations, you received: 1x Common Steel Sword (Rank F)]</p>
<p>[Congratulations, you have received: 1x Low-Quality Wheat Seeds (Rank G)]</p>
<p>[Congratulations, you have received: 1x Common Steel Axe (Rank F)]</p>
<p>[...]</p>
<p>The system inventory was quickly filled with dozens of items.</p>
<p>Seeds of various types, weapons, and especially farming tools.</p>
<p>Only in the last few items did something above Rank F appear.</p>
<p>[Congratulations, you received: 20x Common Pops (Rank E)]</p>
<p>[Item: 20x Common Pops.</p>
<p>When redeemed, twenty common inhabitants of random talent are generated to join your settlement in the next week, with an initial loyalty of 70%.]</p>
<p>"Good!" Ethan exclaimed, punching the table excitedly.</p>
<p>The tools and seeds were great, of course, but nothing compared to twenty new citizens with high loyalty.</p>
<p>"If at least five of them are at full combat capacity..." He could already imagine himself creating his own guard and eliminating the risk of waking up decapitated by Alaric.</p>
<p>’Luckily, they don’t seem to want to kill me yet...’ he thought, quickly grabbing his common tickets, 35 in total.</p>
<p>From what he had discussed with the system, these had a chance of generating items up to grade A. With that in mind, he grabbed them firmly and tore them.</p>
<p>[You used 35x common tickets]</p>
<p>[Spinning...]</p>
<p>[Congratulations, you received: 1x High-Quality Corn Seeds (Rank E)]</p>
<p>[Congratulations, you received: 1x White Steel Sword (Rank E)]</p>
<p>[Congratulations, you received: 1x Compound Bow (Rank E)]</p>
<p>Right after the third spin, a different light appeared alongside the notification. Not the usual white, but a deep green that blinded Ethan for a moment.</p>
<p>[Congratulations, you have received: 1x Leather and Steel Armor Set! (Rank D)]</p>
<p>Before he could even react, another intense green light dominated everything around him.</p>
<p>[Congratulations, you have received: 1x Book: The Basics of Blacksmithing and Ore Extraction. (Rank C)]</p>
<p>Dozens of other items arrived in quick succession, including a few more Rank D items.</p>
<p>But Ethan focused on just one.</p>
<p>He opened the storage and took out a large, heavy book, easily over eight hundred pages.</p>
<p>"Ugh... is this just the basics?" he wondered, opening the first few pages.</p>
<p>It took only a moment to realize: he had found pure gold.</p>
<p>The book not only taught how to make axes, pickaxes, and swords, but also included mining techniques and, above all, construction techniques.</p>
