<p>Doran’s firm, commanding voice echoed across the courtyard as he marched back and forth before the new recruits, each step reverberating off the stones.</p>
<p>The men’s shoulders trembled, their eyes shut tight, swords raised before their faces in white-knuckled grips.</p>
<p>Beads of sweat trickled down their temples and chins, dripping silently onto the stone floor.</p>
<p>A single glance was enough for Ethan to realize these men had been standing in that position since early morning.</p>
<p>Their bodies were exhausted, and they truly seemed just one step away from collapse.</p>
<p>He sighed, then approached.</p>
<p>The recruits noticed the lord’s presence immediately, but no one dared to break formation.</p>
<p>"Ah... aren’t they going to end up dying if they keep training like this?" Ethan asked with a gentle smile, catching Doran’s attention.</p>
<p>"My lord," Doran bowed slightly. "No, they can handle much more than that."</p>
<p>A confident smile appeared on Doran’s lips, and Ethan returned the gesture.</p>
<p>Well... it wasn’t a lie.</p>
<p>With the Tree of Life and the dense magical concentration of Goldenveil, a sacred city, it was nearly impossible to completely deplete the stamina of any Awakened, even those at the third stage.</p>
<p>"Hmm... you’re dismissed for today," Ethan said to the recruits, waving his hand to signal them to relax.</p>
<p>The moment his voice fell, all their bodies slumped against the stones of the courtyard, swords slipping from their exhausted hands.</p>
<p>"Rest well. Once you’ve mastered the basics of the Golden Dragon Emperor’s fencing, you’ll enter a special training regimen that will forge you into true warriors," Ethan said, motioning for them to leave.</p>
<p>"Yes, Lord Ethan!" the recruits replied in unison, mustering what little strength they had left to stand up and file out in a line.</p>
<p>If it weren’t for the prospect of glory and the generous pay, many of them would have given up long ago.</p>
<p>After all, everyone there dreamed of becoming great experts, and the Goldenveil army was the perfect path to that goal.</p>
<p>Doran wasn’t annoyed by the interruption of training; he knew his Lord would never stop it without a good reason.</p>
<p>"Did something happen?" he asked, turning to Ethan.</p>
<p>There was a slight hint of concern in his voice.</p>
<p>"Nothing major," Ethan smiled.</p>
<p>His eyes swept across the now-empty courtyard, and from the system’s inventory, he retrieved an item: a summoning card.</p>
<p>Holding the silver card between his fingers, he tore it in half with a firm motion and took a step back.</p>
<p>A thousand golden lights burst from the torn card with a low hum, rising into the sky like a swarm of stars before cascading down onto the barracks’ stones.</p>
<p>Each luminous particle transformed as it landed, taking the shape of a fully equipped soldier. Exactly one thousand men.</p>
<p>At first, they seemed confused, looking around as if trying to figure out where they were.</p>
<p>But it took only a moment, and as soon as their eyes fell on Ethan, every one of them dropped to their knees in a single, synchronized movement.</p>
<p>With a broad smile, Ethan gave Doran a gentle pat on the shoulder. "With these, we’ve reached two thousand five hundred soldiers. We’re just five more cohorts away from completing our first legion.</p>
<p>Train them well."</p>
<p>***</p>
<p>While Ethan and Doran spoke, in a remote region surrounded by icy mountains, stood the Blue Magic Tower, one of the most powerful magical organizations on the entire continent.</p>
<p>In one of its highest chambers, surrounded by still-smoldering magic circles and blue candles that danced without a breath of wind, a pair of eyes slowly opened.</p>
<p>They were blue eyes, as deep as Ethan’s, and the long golden hair falling over the woman’s shoulders shone softly in the candlelight, exactly the same shade as her son’s.</p>
<p>Her eyes held an immediate concern as her delicate fingers reached for the pendant hanging over her chest.</p>
<p>Upon seeing it glow with an intense, vivid green, she let out a long sigh of relief.</p>
<p>Her baby was fine.</p>
<p>Not that she really needed to worry that much, though.</p>
<p>She had hidden, with the utmost care, dozens of magical arrays all over his body.</p>
<p>Should Ethan ever be in real danger, the arrays would activate instantly, forming a shield around him while simultaneously opening spatial portals so her servants could provide immediate assistance.</p>
<p>"Selene," she called softly, rising slowly to her feet.</p>
<p>She brought her hands to the tight neckline of her dress, which strained to contain the pair of full, heavy breasts pressed within.</p>
<p>Gripping the hem, she pulled it up with a firm gesture. Her breasts jostled from the motion, the heavy mounds bouncing softly before settling perfectly into her dress.</p>
<p>A soft sigh escaped her lips.</p>
<p>A few seconds later, the air in front of the woman rippled, a spatial fold opening to reveal the figure of another woman.</p>
<p>This second woman’s body was tall and curvaceous, her hair as black as night falling over her bare shoulders, her green eyes shining with a cold gleam.</p>
<p>Judging by the energy rippling around her figure, she was clearly a space mage.</p>
<p>"Master... was your advance a success?" Selene asked, her eyes already sensing the weight of the new energy emanating from her mistress.</p>
<p>It was still a bit unstable, wavering here and there, but the power behind that aura was clearly terrifying.</p>
<p>"Yes," the golden-haired woman replied with a faint smile. "You’ve reached the fourth stage, as I ordered. Have your sisters achieved the same?"</p>
<p>"Yes, master," Selene nodded, keeping her head bowed in respect.</p>
<p>"Hm... very well."</p>
<p>The golden-haired woman’s lips curved even further in satisfaction, and she snapped her fingers. A small talisman materialized between them.</p>
<p>"Now, gather your sisters and go after my Ethan. I’ll go as soon as I finish stabilizing my foundation," she ordered, handing over the talisman.</p>
<p>Selene didn’t hesitate; she bowed her head and withdrew, her figure dissolving into another spatial fold.</p>
<p>Alone again, the golden-haired woman let out a long sigh.</p>
<p>She had wanted to send Selene much earlier, but she simply couldn’t interrupt Selene’s training—not when she was so close to reaching the fourth stage.</p>
<p>With another soft sigh, she sat back down, her eyes closing as she began to circulate mana through her body once more.</p>
<p>"Just a little longer, love... just a little longer and mommy will be there with you..."</p>
