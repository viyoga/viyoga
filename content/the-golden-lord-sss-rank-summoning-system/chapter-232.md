<p>[Your assumption is correct.]</p>
<p>A deep, gravelly male voice echoed, as if it had sprung from within Ethan’s own mind.</p>
<p>The young Lord’s face contorted, and a dry, inhuman laugh vibrated through the air around him.</p>
<p>[That young man was a most delicious source of golden souls, but now that you’ve taken him from me, I think you should compensate me, don’t you?]</p>
<p>The demon’s voice echoed, wavering between interest and boredom.</p>
<p>Out of the darkness, Ethan saw a pair of blood-red eyes open, but he did not let himself be frightened.</p>
<p>"If you have a wife, send her to me and I’ll take care of her for you as compensation. Otherwise, Fuck you." He growled, not showing the slightest interest in the demon.</p>
<p>[Hahaha, you’re quite an interesting guy, you know? That Ulric spoke very badly of you. Too bad he failed the mission I gave him.]</p>
<p>At those words, Ethan’s face hardened even further.</p>
<p>So that was it. Ulric had been following that demon’s orders the whole time.</p>
<p>That explained why he and Alaric had been so eager to kill him.</p>
<p>He was about to respond with another taunt when his system’s voice echoed in his mind.</p>
<p>[The souls of the Golden Lords are blessed and considerably strong. Because of this, they are a real feast for demons. Don’t be fooled by a single word he says.]</p>
<p>Ethan silently nodded to the system, his face cold as stone.</p>
<p>[Now...] The demon’s voice came again. [Why don’t we make a deal? Alistair promised me the souls of ten Golden Lords in exchange for my power, but he managed to deliver only four. If you accept what I offer, simply provide me with the remaining six souls.]</p>
<p>The moment the demon fell silent, a system screen appeared before Ethan.</p>
<p>[You have been given the chance to make a pact with a demon. Do you wish to accept it?</p>
<p>Conditions: Provide six souls of Golden Lords to the Demon of Cruelty.</p>
<p>Reward: The demonic ability "Skill Theft"]</p>
<p>[Yes] [No]</p>
<p>’Hm... so that’s where Alistair got the ability to steal Golden Ring’s skills from others.’ Ethan’s lips curved into a cold smile, and without a second thought, he clicked "No."</p>
<p>For a moment, there was only silence. Then, from within that silence, a roar overflowing with fury erupted.</p>
<p>[You damned human brat! You break my toy and still think you’ll get away without paying me?!] The demon’s voice exploded like thunder, already beginning to fade into the air.</p>
<p>[I curse you! I curse your lineage and all those you have ever loved!]</p>
<p>[The Demon of Cruelty attempts to cast a curse upon you.]</p>
<p>[The Goddess of Life turns her curious eyes toward you. With a simple gesture of her delicate hands, a blessing descends from above and envelops your being.]</p>
<p>[You have received a temporary blessing from the Goddess of Life. For the next year, you and your entire family will be immune to all types of disease, poison, curses, and the like.]</p>
<p>[You have resisted the curse: "Eternal Necrosis"]</p>
<p>The moment he read that last notification, Ethan’s heart trembled.</p>
<p>Eternal Necrosis. A cruel curse that slowly killed the body from within, reducing the afflicted to a mass of black, rotting flesh, eternally alive in its own suffering.</p>
<p>He raised his eyes to the heavens, his gaze filled with reverence, and let out a heavy breath.</p>
<p>If he ever built a church in Goldenveil, the Goddess of Life would have absolute priority. Beside her, of course, would stand the Goddess of Beauty and the Goddess of Death.</p>
<p>As for the male gods... they would still need to prove their worth before being worthy of the reverence of the people of Goldenveil.</p>
<p>Shaking his head slightly, Ethan pushed those thoughts from his mind and refocused on what was happening around him.</p>
<p>The battle did not last long after Alistair’s death.</p>
<p>Two third-stage warriors managed to break through Goldenveil’s formation and, along with five hundred other soldiers, escaped down the flank. The rest were left behind, doomed to slaughter.</p>
<p>In just over twenty minutes, Ethan found himself surrounded by a mountain of corpses, the ground beneath his boots soaked in blood.</p>
<p>His eyes swept the field until they settled on Evelyn.</p>
<p>The warrior beauty kept one foot firmly planted on the crushed head of a third-stage warrior, her golden armor entirely bathed in crimson that ran down the plates and dripped slowly onto the muddy ground.</p>
<p>Right beside her stood Mireya, with purple skin and silvery-white hair.</p>
<p>Her appearance was considerably cleaner, though the number of enemies slain by her hand was no less than Evelyn’s.</p>
<p>The two of them, alone, had slain three third-stage warriors and over three hundred ordinary soldiers.</p>
<p>Ethan also recognized other familiar faces scattered across the field, and a quiet breath of relief slipped from his lips as he confirmed that everyone had survived.</p>
<p>Adam, Cain, and Vaelith.</p>
<p>The three were the greatest geniuses of the new generation of the Goldenveil Guard, and all had reached the pinnacle of the second stage thanks to that battle.</p>
<p>It wouldn’t be long before they reached the third stage as well.</p>
<p>Alice and Veronica, who had been fighting on the other side of the field, hurried across the river toward him.</p>
<p>Upon confirming that her husband was unharmed, Alice stood on tiptoe and pressed a gentle kiss to his lips, paying no mind to the blood that streaked his face.</p>
<p>Then she hurried off to tend to the wounded soldiers. Her powerful healing magic was capable of saving even those who seemed on the brink of death.</p>
<p>Ethan exhaled slowly, stretching his tense shoulders and allowing his body to finally relax after all the adrenaline pulsing through his veins.</p>
<p>Extending his open hand toward the ground, Ethan activated the Blood Emperor’s Ring.</p>
<p>In that very instant, several system notifications appeared, not only for him but for every participant in that battle.</p>
<p>They had won. And now, it was time to reap the rewards.</p>
