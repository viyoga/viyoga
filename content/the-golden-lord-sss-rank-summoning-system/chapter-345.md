<p>[NT: I know these geopolitical issues are sensitive (especially the real-world ones), but, well, this is a kingdom-building novel. Geopolitics is part of the package.</p>
<p>Sarah Paine is the primary source for my statements regarding the difficulty of creating a nation from scratch versus rebuilding a fallen one.]</p>
<p>***</p>
<p>In defeated Germany and Japan, modern institutions already existed. Damaged, yes, but still standing.</p>
<p>All it took was aligning those institutions, investing, and making sure every penny of that investment landed in the right place.</p>
<p>That was one of the reasons the German and Japanese reconstructions worked so well while Afghanistan failed completely, collapsing the very moment U.S. forces left the country.</p>
<p>Even devastated after World War II, Japan and Germany had preserved almost everything that characterized a modern state: a civil service, national security forces, an established national identity, banks, businesses, and courts.</p>
<p>Afghanistan, on the other hand, was a largely tribal society where almost all of those institutions had to be created from scratch.</p>
<p>That was Ethan’s struggle.</p>
<p>Founding or annexing new cities wasn’t enough.</p>
<p>He needed to create an entire nation from scratch—from courts and businesses to a unified national identity.</p>
<p>In Goldenveil, he had succeeded with ease, since the population treated his every word as the decree of a god.</p>
<p>But what about the rest of the Eternal Forest? And the other human settlements he would conquer in the future?</p>
<p>For that, he would need an absurd amount of money, manpower, and power.</p>
<p>Ethan had no intention of building a nation that would collapse the second he turned his back, as had happened with the Afghan government.</p>
<p>Leaning back on his comfortable chair, he pondered a while longer before turning his attention to the premium gacha tickets, the system’s bluish screens floating silently before him and washing the office in pale light.</p>
<p>Now he could select seven primary items and fourteen secondary items.</p>
<p>Since the quality of the fourteen secondary items was capped at Rank A, he didn’t waste much time on them.</p>
<p>Rank A items weren’t bad, obviously.</p>
<p>A Rank C sword would be a treasure for a commoner family, and a Rank A could easily become the heirloom of a noble house.</p>
<p>The Darkwall family’s sword, for example, was probably Rank S at best.</p>
<p>With that in mind, a Rank A sword was an incredible item, capable of letting a fourth-stage warrior wield their full power. To Ethan, though, it held little value.</p>
<p>The blacksmiths at Goldenveil’s Divine Forge were already turning out items of that caliber with considerable consistency. Even his lowest-ranking soldiers wore armor of at least Rank B, pieces classified as magical artifacts.</p>
<p>After a moment’s thought, Ethan settled on what he needed most right now: a trained workforce.</p>
<p>Housing, at least, was already handled. His wealth was enough to build another twenty thousand homes and still have money to spare, not to mention food—more than enough for everyone.</p>
<p>It was time for Goldenveil to experience its greatest population boom since its founding.</p>
<p>As for the seven main items, Ethan already had three in mind.</p>
<p>An artifact to guarantee that Alice’s pregnancy would proceed flawlessly and that the child would be born with the greatest possible talent.</p>
<p>A life-saving artifact to keep his wives and concubines from dying.</p>
<p>And a communication artifact, specifically to stay in touch with Goldenveil’s other cities and, of course, with Ron.</p>
<p>He already relied on Ophelia’s intelligence network to keep him informed of everything happening across the rest of the continent, but he still wanted more.</p>
<p>One could never have too much information.</p>
<p>Sighing, he opened the Luck System and set up the next seven cycles of one hundred items each, leaving the main slots of the last four cycles empty.</p>
<p>After these first three hundred premium gacha tickets, he would decide what to do with the remaining four hundred.</p>
<p>Ethan adjusted the system’s filters so it wouldn’t notify him of any drop below Rank A, then clicked to open the three hundred tickets.</p>
<p>[You used 300x Premium Gacha Tickets.</p>
<p>Spinning...]</p>
<p>The screen flickered, and a cascade of icons began to spin, blurring into streaks of blue light.</p>
<p>After the first ten spins, the first Rank A item appeared.</p>
<p>[Congratulations, you have received: 1x Slayer’s Axe (Rank A)]</p>
<p>[Congratulations, you have received: 200x Talent Boost Potions (Rank A)]</p>
<p>[Congratulations, you have received: 1x "Blue Rose" Special Building Creation Card (Rank A)]</p>
<p>Sixty spins passed in a single second, yielding only three Rank A items. Terrible luck.</p>
<p>Ethan clicked his tongue.</p>
<p>Fortunately, the tide turned soon after. Two of the items he had been aiming for appeared, and his luck began to climb.</p>
<p>[Congratulations, you have received: 1x High-Level 3,000 Pops Card (Rank A)]</p>
<p>[Congratulations, you have received: 1x Supreme-Level 5,000 Pops Card (Rank S)]</p>
<p>[Congratulations, you have received: 1x Special Military General Summoning Card (Rank S)]</p>
<p>A smile tugged at the corners of his lips. That was much better.</p>
<p>Thirty more spins flew by in the next second until, at last, Ethan received the item he’d been hoping for most.</p>
<p>The spinning icons froze.</p>
<p>An orange-gold light burst from the screen and engulfed the entire office, tinging the walls with amber.</p>
<p>Ethan’s eyes sparkled.</p>
<p>An SSS-Rank item, and a clearly very powerful one!!</p>
<p>[Congratulations, you have received: The Grace of the Ancestral Goddess of Life (SSS-Rank)]</p>
<p>[Name: Grace of the Ancestral Goddess of Life</p>
<p>Description: A magical artifact created by the first entity that could be called the Goddess of Life, the Mother of All, who predated the current gods of Estria.</p>
<p>It was a gift to her most loyal follower, created after the man lost his wife and, devastated, never married again. The item ensured he would never again lose a woman he loved.</p>
<p>It allows the user to bind wives, concubines, and children.</p>
<p>The artifact must be connected to a magical vein to function.</p>
<p>Whenever one of the bound targets dies, their body is automatically teleported into the artifact and revived within it.</p>
<p>Rank: SSS]</p>
