<p>"Stop working? You’re crazy! My men never stop working—they—" Garrick’s words died in his throat.</p>
<p>In the next instant, he felt the courtyard beneath his feet expand.</p>
<p>The forges around them split apart and multiplied before his eyes.</p>
<p>Stones ground against each other with a low rumble, walls slid silently aside, and the entire layout of the barracks reshaped itself to make room for everything.</p>
<p>When the transformation was finally complete, Garrick found himself standing at the center of a sprawling complex containing three hundred identical forges, each already equipped with every tool and implement a blacksmith could possibly need.</p>
<p>Ethan smiled with satisfaction, then strode over to the refined ore storage area, a vast chamber wide enough to easily hold several thousand metric tons.</p>
<p>Once there, he deposited roughly two hundred and fifty metric tons of steel—the highest-quality material stored in his system inventory.</p>
<p>The lower-grade steel would be distributed among other, less important blacksmith shops, used to forge common tools that meet the far less demanding needs of the general population.</p>
<p>Alongside the steel, Ethan added the ten thousand kilograms of mithril he had obtained with the premium gacha tickets.</p>
<p>Steel, of course, wasn’t all that was needed to produce those armors and weapons.</p>
<p>Fortunately, everything else—especially the cloaks—would be produced directly by a small, newly built elven cooperative that specialized in fabrics infused with elven magic.</p>
<p>Ethan hadn’t visited them in person yet, but he planned to subsidize them and make sure they were under his complete control.</p>
<p>In time, he wanted to turn that cooperative into the hub for the production of textiles, artifacts, and elven silver items across the entire continent.</p>
<p>After all, as skilled as the dwarves were with steel, the elves excelled with silver, artifacts, and textiles.</p>
<p>Ethan let out a soft sigh, pulling two more items from his inventory.</p>
<p>The first was the dwarf summons card. The second was the SS-Rank buff that would boost their work rate for an entire month and multiply each item produced by ten.</p>
<p>Garrick was already practically drooling as he stared at the gleaming mountain of mithril.</p>
<p>His heart nearly leapt out of his chest when more than two hundred and fifty dwarves suddenly appeared in the courtyard, materializing out of thin air.</p>
<p>For a moment, all of them looked around in confusion, bewildered expressions on their faces, thick fingers scratching at unkempt beards.</p>
<p>Then a wave of pain struck their heads as the system flooded their minds with everything they needed to know.</p>
<p>"They are your new subordinates," Ethan said, exchanging glances with the dwarves, who were taking in their surroundings with curiosity and admiration etched onto their faces.</p>
<p>"I’ll leave them in your care. Feed them, bathe them, and then put them all to work." Ethan let out a soft chuckle. "Their starting wage will be ten gold coins a month, but it will increase in the future."</p>
<p>Without delay, Ethan retrieved five thousand gold coins from his storage ring and handed them over to Garrick.</p>
<p>"That should be enough to pay the wages and cover the rest of the lower-value items. Focus on using up the mithril reserves first, then the steel. You need to produce as much as possible by next month."</p>
<p>For a moment, Garrick simply nodded, his eyes wide, still trying to process it all. Soon, however, he turned away abruptly.</p>
<p>"What do you mean, feed and bathe them?! I’D RATHER DIE THAN—"</p>
<p>Ethan simply walked out of the barracks, pretending Garrick’s furious complaints didn’t even exist, a small smile playing at the corner of his lips.</p>
<p>He hated having to integrate system-summoned people into Goldenveil, but fortunately, he now had someone to do that work for him.</p>
<p>Doran and Garrick would manage just fine.</p>
<p>With a soft sigh, he picked up the second item he’d taken from the system’s inventory and tore it between his fingers.</p>
<p>[You used: SS-Rank Work Efficiency Bonus on blacksmiths at the Goldenveil barracks!]</p>
<p>[The work speed of all blacksmiths at the Goldenveil barracks will increase significantly while the bonus is active!</p>
<p>While the bonus is active, for every item produced by the blacksmiths at the Goldenveil barracks, ten will be deposited into your system inventory.]</p>
<p>Ethan’s lips curved into a satisfied smile, and he finally let his shoulders relax.</p>
<p>By next month, without a doubt, he would have enough armor to equip one—perhaps even two—entire legions.</p>
<p>Now, all that was missing were the soldiers.</p>
<p>He quickened his pace.</p>
<p>It was a shame his wealth had dwindled so much, but it was all right.</p>
<p>Ron would be back soon, and he could, once again, drain every last coin out of him.</p>
<p>If anything went wrong, he was ready to simply travel to a nearby city and auction off a few artifacts.</p>
<p>Gold was already a very minor problem for Ethan.</p>
<p>"Ah..." He sighed, stretching his arms as he reached the gates of his own palace.</p>
<p>He crossed the wide marble path that connected the gate to the staircase, then suddenly stopped in his tracks.</p>
<p>He looked up and, there, at the base of the staircase, saw a woman.</p>
<p>She was beautiful, without a doubt.</p>
<p>She had dark, deep eyes, and her full breasts pressed tightly against a steel breastplate, pushing upward as if struggling against the restrictive armor. The soft, plump flesh spilled temptingly over the metal’s rim.</p>
<p>Her waist was slim and delicate, tapering elegantly before giving way to generous hips, with thick, shapely thighs that filled her attire with a natural sensuality.</p>
<p>Her skin was milky white, glowing beneath the sunlight, and her cheeks were softly flushed, a shy blush tinging her delicate face.</p>
<p>Back on Earth, Ethan had seen many beautiful Korean women, especially idols.</p>
<p>None of them came even close to the woman standing before him.</p>
<p>"You’re... Snowbreath, am I remembering correctly? Did you come for that drink?" Ethan said with a gentle smile on his lips.</p>
<p>She nodded in confirmation, the small gesture shy, her dark eyes flickering away from his gaze for just a moment.</p>
<p>"Well... I... it’s just that..." She hesitated, drawing a nervous breath, the blush on her cheeks deepening even further.</p>
<p>"It’s okay, come with me. Let’s have a drink and talk," Ethan said, offering his arm to Seo-ah with noble, respectful courtesy.</p>
