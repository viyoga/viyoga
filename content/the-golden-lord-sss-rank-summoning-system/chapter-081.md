<p>[You have used 7x Premium Gacha Tickets!]</p>
<p>[Spinning...]</p>
<p>[Congratulations, you have received: 3,000 kg of Common Steel (Rank D)]</p>
<p>[Congratulations, you have received: Small Military Arsenal]</p>
<p>[Congratulations, you have received: 50x Common Pops (Rank C)]</p>
<p>[Congratulations, you have received: 50kg Bag of Various Magic Seeds (Rank C)]</p>
<p>[Congratulations, you have received: 5x Superior Healing Potion Vials (Rank B)]</p>
<p>[Congratulations, you have summoned an epic heroine: Elena the Great (Rank A)]</p>
<p>Ethan’s eyes widened, and he was already ready to jump for joy at having summoned a new heroine, but his expression changed completely before he could.</p>
<p>An intense, powerful golden light filled his office, and soon a system notification appeared.</p>
<p>An S-rank item? No...</p>
<p>The golden light transformed, taking on a deep red hue unlike anything he had ever seen before.</p>
<p>A tremor rippled through the air, and Ethan’s face was filled with sheer shock.</p>
<p>An SSS-rank item...</p>
<p>[Congratulations, you have received: 1x Blood Emperor’s Ring (Rank SSS)]</p>
<p>[Item: Blood Emperor’s Ring</p>
<p>Description: A ring created by the Blood Empress as a gift for her destined husband, the one who was to help her revive her race. With the Blood Empress falling into eternal slumber, the ring was lost to time.</p>
<p>It can be used to absorb the blood of enemies. The amount of blood absorbed depends on the enemy’s power and race.</p>
<p>Effect 1: Uses the absorbed blood to heal the user. In the event of a fatal wound, the stored blood is automatically consumed.</p>
<p>Effect 2: With each absorption of blood, it extracts part of the opponent’s talent and basic attributes.</p>
<p>Effect 3: The user can transform into a mist of blood, becoming temporarily immune to physical and magical attacks. (Cost of five blood points per second)</p>
<p>Effect 4: Stored blood can be transformed into weapons or condensed into a powerful attack. (The power of the attack and of the generated weapon varies with the amount of blood used.)</p>
<p>Effect 5: The user will not age while wearing the Blood Emperor’s Ring.</p>
<p>Effect 6: When reunited with the Blood Empress’s Ring, the item’s grade will be upgraded.</p>
<p>Level: 1/10</p>
<p>Upgrade Requirements: 1x Low-grade Item Upgrade Stone.</p>
<p>Grade: 1 (Increase by upgrading the item to level ten and using an Ascension Stone)</p>
<p>Rank: SSS</p>
<p>Blood Reserve: 5/1000]</p>
<p>[You have unlocked a new system feature: Item Upgrade]</p>
<p>[Global Announcement: Weapon Upgrade Stones can be obtained by hunting monsters or mined from magical core mines, and can be used to upgrade weapons, armor, and tools.]</p>
<p>Ethan stood motionless for a long while, his eyes fixed on the screen before him.</p>
<p>He had been so lucky in the first six spins that he was barely looking forward to the last one, but... holy hell.</p>
<p>An SSS-rank item.</p>
<p>And not only that, now he could even upgrade his items.</p>
<p>Wasn’t that just wonderful?</p>
<p>With an eager look, Ethan retrieved the item from his inventory, cradling it in the palm of his hand.</p>
<p>For a moment, it looked like an ordinary ring, with a golden band, some strange inscriptions engraved on the surface, and, at the top, a small blood-red stone, resembling a ruby.</p>
<p>He slipped it onto the index finger of his right hand, right next to the Ring of the Golden Lord.</p>
<p>Immediately, he felt a prick on his skin as the ring drew a single drop of his blood.</p>
<p>[Blood Emperor’s Ring bound!</p>
<p>Bound items are linked to the user and cannot be stolen or forcibly removed.]</p>
<p>Ethan barely glanced at that notification, already drawing the sword at his waist and making a quick cut across his own palm.</p>
<p>Warm blood trickled between his fingers, dripping onto the floor in small scarlet splatters, and he growled softly in pain.</p>
<p>He focused his mind and activated the ring’s first ability.</p>
<p>The effect was immediate. The blood was sucked back into the wound, even the drops that had dripped onto the floor returning clean, without any impurity.</p>
<p>It was as if the cut had never existed, the skin even more perfect than before.</p>
<p>The healing had consumed two blood points, leaving only three in reserve.</p>
<p>Honestly, that first ability was already absurd on its own, but the second? The ability to steal attributes and talent from his enemies?</p>
<p>It was exactly what Ethan needed, especially considering his terrible talent.</p>
<p>He needed to improve this talent and didn’t want to rely solely on system missions.</p>
<p>With the Blood Emperor’s ring, he could actively work to improve his talent, without waiting for the system</p>
<p>Every new effect he read made him more excited than the last, the excitement growing in his chest like a flame.</p>
<p>"Ah... life is good! Hahahaha!"</p>
<p>Ethan let out a mighty laugh and slumped back in his chair, arms spread wide and a broad smile on his face.</p>
<p>He wanted to take a look at Elena, the newly summoned heroine, but after thinking it over, he decided to leave her for last, checking his other rewards first.</p>
<p>[Item: 50x Common Pops</p>
<p>Description: When used, fifty common inhabitants with talent above Rank D are generated to join your settlement next week.</p>
<p>Rank: C]</p>
<p>It was hard to resist the urge to use that item right then and there, but when he thought about Goldenveil’s housing problem, Ethan hesitated.</p>
<p>It wasn’t the right time. Fifty homeless people would only bring him headaches.</p>
<p>First, he would build at least another twenty houses, and only then would he use the item.</p>
<p>After all, population growth was extremely important.</p>
<p>To turn Goldenveil into a barony, he would need at least a thousand people, the same requirement for elevating it to the level of an average village, which, who knows, might finally trigger an improvement in Goldenveil’s prosperity and attractiveness.</p>
<p>With a soft sigh, Ethan glanced at the other items.</p>
<p>They were all very useful, especially the three tons of refined steel.</p>
<p>If Roderik worked hard, wouldn’t he even be able to create armor for the entire Goldenveil guard?</p>
<p>No...</p>
<p>Maybe if he had a team working with him, yes, but alone? It would take months to produce that many full sets of plate armor.</p>
<p>Honestly, simple breastplates would suffice; Goldenveil wasn’t in a position to equip a full guard with a type of armor meant for knights.</p>
