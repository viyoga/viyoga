<p>"We’re doomed..." Ethan repeated to himself, looking at the empty interior of that warehouse.</p>
<p>How much food was there? Five pounds of dried meat... about twenty pounds of grain.</p>
<p>Of course, many citizens had their own personal warehouses, and his own mansion stored some food.</p>
<p>But even so, with that miserable amount...</p>
<p>No, it wasn’t possible.</p>
<p>Ethan turned his face to Ulric, noticing the calmness on the old man’s face, and even on the face of the attendant in charge of the warehouse.</p>
<p>No... that was wrong.</p>
<p>"I’m going to check the fields now," Ethan declared, a thin thread of murderous intent running through his bones.</p>
<p>Honestly, he was confused as to why Ulric was using these tactics to undermine his power, even at the risk of killing everyone there.</p>
<p>Perhaps because of the priest who warned them of the arrival of the Golden Lords?</p>
<p>Yes... that was possible.</p>
<p>Ulric might be acting so cautiously because, even in this isolated region, an order to exterminate from a priest of the Church would be devastating.</p>
<p>If he wanted to get rid of Ethan, he would have to do it in a way that left no loose ends.</p>
<p>"As you wish, my lord!" Ulric laughed, his face full of amusement as he watched his lord’s bitter expression.</p>
<p>Silently, Ethan and Evelyn followed him.</p>
<p>Most of the houses there had their own personal cultivation areas, but even so, when the Darkwall family formed the settlement, they cleared a large area, creating a field of about 100,000 square meters.</p>
<p>This field was in a relatively remote part, with a small stream cutting through it and a few workers tending it.</p>
<p>The size was insufficient to provide enough food for a small village for the entire winter on its own, but, together with fishing, hunting, and gathering herbs in the forest, it should be enough.</p>
<p>Especially with how small the current population of the settlement was.</p>
<p>"Are these all the workers?" Ethan asked, counting three adult men.</p>
<p>Everyone stopped working when they saw the new lord arrive, watching him with curiosity and a hint of hostility.</p>
<p>"Currently, yes, my lord. There should be more people here, but we have so few hands and the harvests have been so poor that it’s simply not worth the energy," Ulric replied.</p>
<p>"Hm... Gather all the citizens of the village," Ethan ordered.</p>
<p>"Uh? My lord, it’s work time, everyone is-"</p>
<p>"Now," Ethan repeated, cutting the old man off.</p>
<p>Ulric gritted his teeth, turning to the workers in the field.</p>
<p>"What are you waiting for? You heard the Lord, gather everyone here!"</p>
<p>Nodding, the workers ran toward the houses, passing the news along.</p>
<p>Soon, more than sixty people surrounded the field, watching Ethan and Evelyn with suspicious looks.</p>
<p>With a sigh, Ethan took an item out of the system’s warehouse.</p>
<p>[Name: Growth Bonus Card.</p>
<p>Description: A powerful growth card imbued with magical energy. Its area of effect is small, but it improves the growth rate of plants absurdly, reducing the need for nutrients and sunlight.</p>
<p>Effect: +350,000% growth bonus in a two hundred by two hundred meter area around the user.</p>
<p>Duration: 48 hours. ]</p>
<p>"Hm... the area and duration aren’t great, but the effect..." Ethan almost drooled.</p>
<p>It was simply incredible, even if the area covered less than half of that entire field.</p>
<p>The golden card in Ethan’s hands quickly caught everyone’s attention as he walked to the center of the field.</p>
<p>Kneeling down, he took a single corn seed from his storage and planted it.</p>
<p>"My lord, the soil isn’t ready for planting yet!" One of the farmers protested, but Ethan ignored him.</p>
<p>With a smooth motion, he grabbed the bonus card, and then tore it up.</p>
<p>Immediately, the world around him began to change.</p>
<p>Mana spread across the field in an explosion, and the black earth was overtaken by a subtle golden glow.</p>
<p>The villagers covered their mouths in shock, retreating with frightened steps as the golden glow dominated everything.</p>
<p>Children threw themselves into their mothers’ arms, and even Ulric’s previously calm face changed completely.</p>
<p>What the hell had happened there? What kind of profane magic was this?</p>
<p>Ethan stood up, looking down. Everyone’s eyes followed his.</p>
<p>Beneath his feet, a sprout appeared, growing at a speed visible to the naked eye.</p>
<p>In just a few seconds, it had already reached five centimeters, and it kept growing.</p>
<p>There was total silence as the minutes passed.</p>
<p>The corn stalk grew taller and taller, soon reaching Ethan’s waist, then his chest. Three fruits appeared on the stalk, developing at an equally frightening speed.</p>
<p>After about thirty minutes, the growth stopped completely.</p>
<p>Ethan turned his face toward the people, watching a man break through the crowd.</p>
<p>Over ninety years old, he was the oldest among them, his entire life dedicated to nothing but agriculture.</p>
<p>He approached, grabbed one of the fruits with his calloused hand, and peeled it.</p>
<p>The corn underneath was flawless. Perfect. A yellow so pure it looked like gold.</p>
<p>It was the largest and healthiest cob he had ever seen in his entire life, and there were three of them on the same stalk.</p>
<p>Opening his mouth, he used what few teeth he had left to take a bite.</p>
<p>The taste of raw corn wasn’t good, but to him, it was like the most perfect delicacy.</p>
<p>Where had such perfection been hidden for so long?</p>
<p>His eyes filled with tears. And he fell to his knees.</p>
<p>"I salute Lord Goldenveil!" He roared, prostrating himself repeatedly, and the entire population of the settlement followed suit.</p>
