<p>After leaving Amelia in a veritable paradise for mages, Ethan headed straight for his office, closing the door behind him before sinking into the high-backed leather chair.</p>
<p>Finally, it was time to get his sword.</p>
<p>He had been very specific with the system’s filters, targeting the sword of the Fallen God-Emperor—likely an SSS-Rank item.</p>
<p>Normally, it was impossible to determine an item’s exact rank in the Luck System, but there was still a way to get around that.</p>
<p>The system defined each item based on various keywords, and in this world, an item’s name was directly tied to its power.</p>
<p>When he narrowed down an item by its tags or description, the system would try to find the closest match to those parameters.</p>
<p>An item generated with the "divine" tag could never be lower than Rank S, and in most cases, not even lower than Rank SS.</p>
<p>Because of this, his confidence was sky-high.</p>
<p>Opening the system’s inventory with a simple thought, he retrieved exactly forty lucky tickets. Each one floated above the desk, spinning gently and glowing with a soft golden light.</p>
<p>In the next instant, he activated them all at once.</p>
<p>A burst of intense, brilliant light flooded the entire office, blinding his blue eyes for a moment as it ricocheted off the polished stone walls in dazzling streaks.</p>
<p>Ethan blinked a few times until his vision adjusted to the glare, then ignored every item below Rank A, focusing only on the ones that truly mattered.</p>
<p>[Congratulations! You have received: 100x Talent Boost Potions (Rank S!)]</p>
<p>...</p>
<p>...</p>
<p>...</p>
<p>[Congratulations! You have received: 2,500x High-Level Pop Summoning Cards (Rank A)]</p>
<p>[Congratulations! You have received: 1,000x Intermediate-Level Spirit Horse Eggs (Rank A)]</p>
<p>...</p>
<p>...</p>
<p>...</p>
<p>[Congratulations! You have received: Management Skill: Regional Planning (Rank SS)]</p>
<p>[Congratulations! You have received: 100x Shattered Chaos Arrows (Rank A)]</p>
<p>[Congratulations! You have received: 10,000x Mass XP Boost Potions (Rank A)]</p>
<p>[Notice: You can link the "Regional Planning" skill to the "Instant Construction" skill.]</p>
<p>[Reach the Kingdom level to unlock the "National Planning" feature.]</p>
<p>A slow smile curved the corner of Ethan’s mouth. Every one of those items was absolutely incredible—four Rank A items, one Rank S, and one Rank SS.</p>
<p>Still, he didn’t dwell on them for long, since he had already reached item number thirty-nine...</p>
<p>Upon seeing the reward appear on the screen—just an ordinary Rank C sword with nothing special about it—Ethan finally shifted his gaze to the fortieth ticket, the last one yet to be revealed.</p>
<p>A reddish light streaked with golden veins flooded the office in a violent surge, and the floor beneath his feet trembled with a deep, reverent hum.</p>
<p>Before him, a status screen materialized in the air.</p>
<p>[Congratulations! You have received: God Slayer (Rank SSS)]</p>
<p>[Name: God Slayer</p>
<p>Description: A legendary sword, forged by the very hands of the Fallen God-Emperor with a single purpose: to bring the fury of men into the divine realm.</p>
<p>With this blade, he slew several lesser gods and gravely wounded the God of War.</p>
<p>It was declared a profane item after the Fallen God-Emperor’s downfall and was lost to time.</p>
<p>It is capable of withstanding direct blows from gods and directly wounding the divinity of transcended beings.</p>
<p>While wielding this weapon, your attacks deal 550% holy damage to corrupted enemies.</p>
<p>While wielding this weapon, your aura consumption is reduced by 70%.</p>
<p>While wielding this weapon, the potency of all your abilities is increased by 300%.</p>
<p>While wielding this weapon, your stamina consumption is reduced by 90%.</p>
<p>While wielding this weapon, you can summon the God-Emperor’s Armor.</p>
<p>Enemies killed by this weapon leave behind something called divinity. Every being possesses a certain level of divinity, but the amount required to become truly divine is astronomical.</p>
<p>The amount of divinity absorbed depends on the talent of the target you defeat. Targets from the darkness leave behind double the amount of divinity.</p>
<p>Smaller amounts of divinity can be fused and transformed into talent pills and many other useful items.</p>
<p>Current Divinity: 0</p>
<p>Rank: SSS]</p>
<p>Ethan’s blue eyes sparkled as he scanned each line of the description. This was far beyond what he had expected.</p>
<p>[Warning! This is a weapon banned by the Nine. Accepting it will severely damage your relationship with the gods. Do you wish to decline it? A random new item of equivalent level will be generated.]</p>
<p>[Accept] [Decline]</p>
<p>Ethan didn’t hesitate for even a second, clicking "Accept" with a contemptuous look in his eyes.</p>
<p>Fuck the gods. He only needed his three goddesses.</p>
<p>[The God of War glares at you with fury]</p>
<p>[The God of the Sun and Justice narrows his eyes with murderous intent]</p>
<p>[The God of Wisdom lets out a dry, cruel laugh]</p>
<p>[The Goddess of Life looks at you with curiosity]</p>
<p>[The Goddess of Beauty looks at you with interest]</p>
<p>[The Goddess of Death seems excited by your virile personality and your lack of fear of the gods]</p>
<p>...</p>
<p>...</p>
<p>...</p>
<p>[A curse has been placed on you]</p>
<p>[You have received a blessing from the Goddess of Life]</p>
<p>[You have resisted the curse]</p>
<p>[A curse has been placed upon you]</p>
<p>[You have received a blessing from the Goddess of Beauty]</p>
<p>[You have resisted the curse]</p>
<p>[A curse has been placed upon you]</p>
<p>[You have received a blessing from the Goddess of Death]</p>
<p>[You have resisted the curse]</p>
<p>Ethan calmly ignored the notifications, the corner of his mouth curving into a smile.</p>
<p>As for the three goddesses who had protected him, he was grateful, even though it was entirely unnecessary.</p>
<p>The truth was that the gods’ curses had a very weak effect on those who didn’t directly follow them, especially in Goldenveil, where the worship of any deity other than the Three was strictly illegal.</p>
<p>A single night of circulating his aura would be more than enough to rid himself of them.</p>
<p>[Warning: Starting at the fifth stage, angels may be sent to eliminate you.]</p>
<p>"As long as it’s a hot, busty angel, I don’t mind," Ethan said with a mischievous chuckle, finally retrieving the God Slayer from his spatial ring.</p>
