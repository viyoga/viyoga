<p>"Oh... look, Dad, Mom, it’s so big!"</p>
<p>The sweet, excited voice of a little boy echoed down the street.</p>
<p>His tiny finger pointed at the imposing barracks and the luxurious mansion that loomed on the horizon.</p>
<p>His parents, Aerick and his beautiful wife, froze in the same instant, unable to believe their eyes.</p>
<p>Stone streets. Perfectly constructed sidewalks, adorned with trees, benches, and even a streetlight system.</p>
<p>And it wasn’t just that.</p>
<p>The imposing barracks stood too large to have been built by human hands in such a short time, and the mansion, smaller in size, carried an incomparable luxury.</p>
<p>All of it was wrapped in a mana density at least double that of Windmere.</p>
<p>’You’ll understand when you arrive in Goldenveil.’ Aerick mentally repeated Ethan’s words.</p>
<p>Yes, now he understood why Ethan had decided to integrate his people into Goldenveil rather than take them as vassals.</p>
<p>Goldenveil was barely thirty percent urbanized, and most of the houses already built stood completely empty.</p>
<p>He glanced back, stepping onto the sidewalk beside Ethan, watching his own people and those of Silverbrook pour into the city.</p>
<p>In total, two thousand two hundred and six souls, bringing Goldenveil’s population to three thousand four hundred and fifty-seven.</p>
<p>As they entered the beautiful village, the inhabitants of the two conquered villages couldn’t hide the sparkle in their eyes.</p>
<p>Many among them had lived their entire lives in the eternal forest.</p>
<p>They had never laid eyes on such beautiful streets, such a grand mansion, or such a tall and mighty wall.</p>
<p>Those above the second stage reveled in the density of aura and mana hanging in the air, some nearly moved to tears.</p>
<p>The people of Goldenveil welcomed the newcomers with love and open arms.</p>
<p>Some, like Adam and his sister Veronica, received a residence immediately.</p>
<p>Others took part in a lottery to decide who would stay in their own homes and who would have to wait at the guard barracks, a place that wasn’t exactly bad either.</p>
<p>After all, Goldenveil had only a little over six hundred houses, but it would need at least a thousand to shelter so many people.</p>
<p>"Doran." Standing beside Aerick and his family, Ethan called out to his trusted captain of the guard.</p>
<p>"Ensure that the entire integration process goes smoothly. Aerick will be your new vice-leader.</p>
<p>Take good care of the guard and ensure that the men from Windmere and Silverbrook are well integrated with ours."</p>
<p>With the absorption of the two new guards, the number of soldiers in Goldenveil would reach three hundred and two, nearly the perfect number Ethan wanted to maintain.</p>
<p>Ten percent of the inhabitants as guards, and thirty percent as a reserve militia for desperate situations.</p>
<p>"Yes, my lord." Doran bowed, gave a respectful nod to Aerick, and set off to carry out the orders.</p>
<p>"He is a trustworthy man," Aerick remarked, to which Ethan nodded.</p>
<p>"Yes... it’s rare to find men with as much wisdom and loyalty as he has. It’s hard to believe he was just an ordinary instructor in life... well, in his first life."</p>
<p>"First life?" Aerick’s eyes filled with puzzlement, and Ethan let out a low laugh.</p>
<p>"Start training the guard alongside him and find out." Ethan turned his back and simply motioned for Aerick and his family to follow him to the mansion.</p>
<p>The former Lord of Windmere had many questions about Ethan.</p>
<p>Especially about this place, about how he had transformed a miserable Darkwall settlement into a prosperous and wealthy Goldenveil.</p>
<p>But he chose silence.</p>
<p>It didn’t matter.</p>
<p>None of it mattered.</p>
<p>He just wanted his son to live well and his wife’s heart to be at peace.</p>
<p>Ethan took the lead, waving to the two gardeners tending the gardens of Goldenveil.</p>
