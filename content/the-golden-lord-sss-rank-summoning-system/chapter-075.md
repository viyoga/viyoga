<p>"What did you do to us?" Evelyn asked, looking at Ethan with a puzzled expression.</p>
<p>He was acting strange...</p>
<p>"Nothing much, just trying out some new things," Ethan smiled in response, letting go of them as the sweet smell of roasted meat filled the hallway.</p>
<p>Predator meat wasn’t usually the most delicious, but mana beasts had extremely rich meat, with a unique flavor very different from that of ordinary animals.</p>
<p>Furthermore, even though it was a mana beast, the meat had the ability to restore both mana and aura, which further increased its value.</p>
<p>Sitting in a chair, Ethan simply watched as Alice removed the large piece of roasted meat from the oven, the hot, greasy aroma filling the room.</p>
<p>Seeing Ethan and Evelyn standing side by side, visibly drooling with hunger, made Alice very pleased with her culinary skills.</p>
<p>It felt good to make her husband and her best friend happy.</p>
<p>With a big smile on her face and her pale cheeks slightly flushed, Alice carved the meat and served herself, Ethan, and Evelyn.</p>
<p>Evelyn was the first to take a bite, her eyes sparkling as the sweet, juicy flavor flooded her palate.</p>
<p>Even though it was a corrupted beast, the corruption was practically limited to the mana core, leaving the meat completely free of impurities caused by demonic mana, and the result was simply delicious.</p>
<p>Ethan also tasted it, his eyes shining just like Evelyn’s, but for very different reasons.</p>
<p>[You have consumed beast meat rich in aura and mana; your aura reserves increase slightly. Aura: 950 -&gt; 1000]</p>
<p>Immediately, he took another greedy bite, but to his disappointment, no further improvement appeared.</p>
<p>It was kind of obvious. It had probably just been a stroke of luck, and to repeat the feat, he would need meat of superior quality or an absurd amount of it.</p>
<p>Still, Ethan was satisfied.</p>
<p>He was still far from Alice’s mana reserve, which was likely around three thousand, but little by little, he was building his own.</p>
<p>Right after eating, Ethan went out for a walk around town.</p>
<p>He inspected the training of the new guards, made sure everyone was well-armed, and then went to visit Goldenveil’s blacksmith, Roderik.</p>
<p>With a devoted woman now living with him, the once-abandoned and dusty blacksmith’s shop had taken the form of a legitimate establishment under her care.</p>
<p>Naturally, with his spirits high, Roderik worked much harder, his eyes filled with gratitude whenever he saw Ethan, the one who had given him a second chance.</p>
<p>When Ethan entered the smithy, Roderik immediately stopped working and bowed.</p>
<p>"My lord. What brings you to my humble establishment?" Roderik flashed a bright smile as Ethan motioned for him to rise.</p>
<p>"Just taking a look... is this the shield design Doran chose?" Ethan asked, noticing a finished shield in the corner of the room.</p>
<p>It was a simple design, made of wood and teardrop-shaped, with small metal rods reinforcing the sides and center, creating a firm grip for the hand.</p>
<p>Large enough to defend against arrows, yet light enough to be handled with ease in both large formations and one-on-one combat.</p>
<p>It wasn’t made from any special mana-conducting material, but even so, it was undoubtedly a high-quality, Rank D shield.</p>
<p>Considering it was equipment intended for mass use in an army, this was excellent.</p>
<p>"I should be able to produce about two hundred with the current amount of steel, but it’ll take quite a while."</p>
<p>"You don’t need that many; about sixty should suffice for now," Ethan replied.</p>
<p>He wanted to expand the guard to fifty soldiers before the goblin attack, which would take place in three months.</p>
<p>But after that, if there was no danger in sight, he would halt the guard’s expansion entirely, and the reason for this was simple.</p>
<p>Even if guards could take on civilian work occasionally, that was not, at heart, their duty.</p>
<p>They needed to train, patrol, and sharpen their weapons.</p>
<p>In a professional, standing army, soldiers were soldiers, not farmers, and this created a serious problem.</p>
<p>With Goldenveil’s current population, fifty guards meant that twenty-five percent of the inhabitants would be warriors, something entirely unsustainable, especially since most of the younger men were joining the guard.</p>
<p>For now, it was possible to maintain this ratio thanks to the large food reserves they had, but all it would take was one winter for that to change completely.</p>
<p>Goldenveil’s population would continue to grow, and by the following year, their dry grain stores would likely already be empty.</p>
<p>When that time came, Ethan wanted to keep the number of soldiers at no more than five percent of the population, even lower in times of peace, if possible.</p>
<p>For now, however, he needed every man capable of fighting, with those not joining the guard still receiving limited training to assist in the toughest battles, serving as a militia that could be called upon when necessary.</p>
<p>"Ah... being a Lord is hard." Ethan shook his head, motioning for Roderik to get back to work.</p>
<p>There were so many accumulated problems, and he still needed to establish a functional economy if he wanted any chance of building a prosperous society in the future.</p>
<p>Forming a strong army was easy: gather men, hand out weapons, train them, and secure their loyalty.</p>
<p>Building a functional economy and society, on the other hand, required much more than just men.</p>
<p>He would need merchants, infrastructure, systems for tax collection and wage payments, as well as a central governing body.</p>
<p>All of this was far beyond Goldenveil’s current capabilities.</p>
<p>Perhaps, if only there were someone with solid administrative and legislative knowledge to help him resolve these issues, everything would be much simpler.</p>
<p>Unfortunately, even among the characters he had summoned, all were focused exclusively on combat.</p>
<p>’Ah... I’ll have to take a closer look at the newcomers from now on... there must be at least one with administrative skills.’</p>
<p>With a sigh, Ethan simply left the forge and went to check on John’s work with the wine.</p>
<p>If everything went according to plan, he’d be filthy rich.</p>
