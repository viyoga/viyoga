<p>Merida's nose flared but she turned to Caius.</p>
<p>"Do you know that by performing a Full submersion, which she should not have been able to do by the way, she ruined your life? She ripped you out of your reality and merged with one that isn't your own. Doomed you to live and die by the rules of that world… rules she thought up arbitrary and most often made no real sense!"</p>
<p>"Arbitrary?" Samantha repeated with a little mirthless laugh, "I always thought things through."</p>
<p>"No, you didn't. And you still don't. As proven by what you have done with his life," Merida said to Samantha with a thumb gesture at Caius.</p>
<p>"I didn't force him. I gave him a choice. He chose to help me," Samantha said.</p>
<p>"That's true," Caius said, "I did agree to help."</p>
<p>Merida let out a breath.</p>
<p>"And you left nothing behind that will make you regret that decision? Family, aspirations?" She asked with a raised brow.</p>
<p>Caius pursed his lips and said nothing.</p>
<p>Merida puffed out a breath through her nose, seemingly satisfied that she had stumped him.</p>
<p>"Turning your back on an entire life, an entire reality is never quite so easy. Once the drive for adventure and the lust to indulge in fictional characters inevitably wear off…"</p>
<p>"If they are fictional characters, so am I," Caius said with a shrug, "I just happen to be aware of truths that surpass the realm of their— our world. Our reality.</p>
<p>Do I regret leaving my life behind in my old world? Yes. But not in a way that makes me want to return.</p>
<p>The truth is, no one in that world cared about me. And no one I cared about. I drifted aimlessly. The regret I feel is that I don't feel regret. The regret I feel is that if Samantha hadn't chosen to reply to that little rant I made…"</p>
<p>"Little?" Samantha laughed.</p>
<p>Caius smiled and continued.</p>
<p>"If she hadn't, I'd have continued to drift, aimless. Trying to figure out what could possibly be next?</p>
<p>Novels were my life. I let myself into the worlds crafted by the authors, hoping to escape my reality. It just so happened that escaping my reality was actually possible.</p>
<p>So when Samantha offered me the chance, of course I took it. When she told me what it would cost and how it would end any possibility of returning, I was doubly excited. I regret that I was so ready, but I was still.</p>
<p>In this world, in the world Samantha has crafted, I have felt all that eluded me in my past reality. I'm grateful for that."</p>
<p>"You hear that," Samantha said proudly, taking a stance that made her tits jiggle. "He's grateful."</p>
<p>"Of course, he is. I've seen what he's been up to," Merida said with another sneer.</p>
<p>"Hmm," said Jared the horny Editor still shrouded in darkness.</p>
<p>"Fortunately, what you have enjoyed in that world has no bearing on this hearing.</p>
<p>You have interrupted the story structure and acquired an ability that was never once used in the original canon. The way this will change canon is unprecedented.</p>
<p>If the story structure is this much in flux, the entire story might as well be pruned from the Archive."</p>
<p>"Just go ahead," Samantha said with an annoyed wave of her hand.</p>
<p>Caius turned to her.</p>
<p>"What?" He asked, "Shouldn't you be asking her not to do that?"</p>
<p>Samantha folded her arms beneath her breasts.</p>
<p>"Merida is stubborn. She's the one who insisted that Caius should die and when I tried to offer alternatives, she moved to threats.</p>
<p>She wants me to grovel and beg."</p>

<p>"I won't. Because while it will stoke her ego, probably even get her off, she'll still prune my story. She likes to exert her power wherever she can."</p>
<p>Merida smirked.</p>
<p>"That's quite the assessment of me. But I'm glad you understand there's no changing my mind. The hopelessness in your eyes is so sweet—"</p>
<p>"It was you," Caius said then.</p>
<p>Merida's face turned to him.</p>
<p>"What?"</p>
<p>"You set Brogan Kraxia on me. You specifically, I mean."</p>

<p>"How could I not have seen it? The attack on Athenodora, that was you as well.</p>
