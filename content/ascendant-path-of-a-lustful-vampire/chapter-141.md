<p>The last view anyone had of Kerwin Leopold was of him unleashing his canopy of water and now his form was completely shrouded in the darkness of the Black Hand spell as well as the dust that had billowed out from the powerful contact the spell had made with the ground.</p>
<p>And then the darkness began to fade and the cloud of dust began to disperse to reveal Kerwin on one knee with his head bowed and one hand raised. It was the hand he had used to cast his defensive spell.</p>
<p>Kerwin’s eyes rolled up in his sockets and he looked at Caius who was calmly observing him. He bowed his head over again and his raised hand fell to his side, dampening any hopes there might have been that he was about to counterattack.</p>

<p>Kerwin had put his all into that final defensive spell. Every inch that the Black hand descended had sapped him of more and more of the little bit of physical energy he had left.</p>
<p>Now he had managed to remain on one knee rather than get flattened against the ground but he didn’t even have the strength to keep his body in that position or do anything at all, really.</p>
<p>The hearts of the Council members sank even further as Kerwin’s body went limp and he fell with no control over the direction of his descent.</p>
<p>*Thud*</p>
<p>His back hit the ground.</p>
<p>The Duel was over.</p>
<p>The chain of their Vow melted away from around Caius’s heart then as he no longer had any obligations or stipulations to abide by.</p>
<p>On the other hand, the chain transformed around Kerwin’s heart turning from just a deterrent against breaking the vow he had made and into something of a leash. A leash that had been handed to Caius as per the terms that had been agreed upon.</p>
<p>Caius felt it then.</p>

<p>He looked at the Council members and could feel their seething at this outcome. And then he felt their acceptance. There was no resistance they could make. Divine power that had made their existences possible compelled them to accept their fates.</p>
<p>Caius retracted his Noble Authority now. He was nearing his limit and no longer had a use for it. Not against this bunch anyway. Whatever strength they possessed, from this moment on, would serve his goals and nothing else.</p>
<p>With the Noble Authority gone and the Duel over, the Lord’s Prerogative that had limited Kerwin Leopold to the Third Circle was finally lifted. He regained his actual Magical and Physical strength and lifted himself off the ground by sheer force of will. No spell required.</p>
<p>Kerwin didn’t get to his feet. With a sullen expression, he fell to one knee with one arm across the leg whose knee was not touching the ground, bowing his head at Caius.</p>
<p>The Council Members followed his lead. The only one not to take a knee was the one Vampire woman who had been struck by Tenth Circle Quality Mana and was still comatose by the wall.</p>
<p>Caius smiled.</p>
<p>For the first time in the days since he had acquired the title, he finally, truly felt like a Vampire Lord.</p>
