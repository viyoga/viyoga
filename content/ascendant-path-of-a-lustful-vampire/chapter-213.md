<p>It’s why Vampires make for very good close combat fighters and why some Vampires who are so ingrained in the traditional mage ways of keeping their distance from an opponent try to include some Mage-Knight fighting techniques into their repertoire.</p>
<p>This is such a sound advantage that it is said that, should a Vampire get within a foot of a target, the target is as good as dead. This adds even more terror to the existence of Vampires especially with people who are not quite learned about vampire facts.</p>
<p>Of course, this advantage is not the end-all be-all. A Vampire must still invest in its evolution to get the opportunity to make use of that advantage.</p>
<p>In fact, despite close combat being in their favor because of this advantage, Mage-Knight Vampire Hunters who know all about Vampires still readily engage in close combat battles, parrying claw strikes to kill Vampires all the same.</p>
<p>You only have to fear the fangs and claws if the Vampire actually gets the chance to get either of the two in direct contact with your body.
