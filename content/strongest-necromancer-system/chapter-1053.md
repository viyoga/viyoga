<p>The leaders of these worlds wondered if there was really no way to take down this Celestial rather than making all their subjects or people migrate to another world! But Jason was very clear about the dangers of Death, and he told them that even trying to put up a fight against Death would immediately lead to them dying as well!</p>
<p>That was enough for them to realise just how serious things were, and many of them realised that they were completely fucked if they didn’t leave! So they simply gathered their people, and they began opening massive Dimensional Tears all across their world to move them towards another world!</p>
<p>By the time the day was over, Jason had visited no less than a hundred and fifty worlds and warned them about the coming disaster. The evacuation efforts were still ongoing, and Jason received reports that only two worlds were almost completely evacuated! At this pace, they might only be able to evacuate five hundred to a thousand worlds by the end of the week, and Jason knew without a doubt that it would not be enough!</p>
<p>Death was going to be here in six to seven days, and it would kill them all.</p>
<p>But Jason couldn’t stop no matter what! Jason knew that there was nothing else that they could do right now. If they didn’t evacuate, then they would simply be waiting for Death to come upon them, so Jason would rather do what he could as quickly as possible! Jason supervised the evacuation on Earth, and he made sure that people from all over the world were getting evacuated. Jason hadn’t given the humans a lot of information about what was going on since he didn’t want to cause a panic, but Jason told them that they were leaving Earth because of an approaching disaster, and that was enough to make all of them listen to him.</p>
<p>There were a few countries where the rich and powerful were manipulating things so that they would leave before the poor commoners, and although Jason did his best to make sure no one was neglected or left behind, he couldn’t be everywhere at the same time, so there was a lot of discrimination! The worst moment of discrimination had been when Jason found out that certain superhumans were demanding payments of hundreds of thousands of dollars to allow people to use the inter-world travel rune! Jason had nearly killed those superhumans when he found out, and from that moment on, no other superhumans dared to try something so fucking stupid!</p>
<p>Jason had put his and Gina’s family members in his mansion in Riverdale as a precaution! Jason knew that he could move all of them through multiple worlds very quickly if he needed to, so for now, he simply told them to wait in Riverdale while he worked on evacuating the rest of the world!</p>
<p>Stella and Valeria were also in Riverdale with Jason’s family members, and Harold was still unconscious even after a full day. Jason hadn’t been home all this while since he was so busy, but he was glad that Stella was there to take care of the family members!</p>
<p>After the first two days, when Jason was certain that the evacuation efforts on Earth and the other worlds in the Sixth Domain had fallen into a sort of autopilot mode where everyone knew exactly what they were meant to do and they were simply doing it as quickly as possible, Jason finally made his way towards the world of Nox in the Eighth Domain where Lamina and the other Vympirs were waiting for him!</p>
<p>Jason appeared in a large hall in Lamina’s mansion where a meeting was taking place between hundreds of Vympir elders about the evacuation efforts, and everyone in the room felt their eyes widen in shock when they saw Jason appearing in front of them! All the members of the elder board were wielders of Blood Oaths just like the previous Queen of the Vympirs, Gillian. They all ruled their own individual elements, but as the strongest of them all, Gillian had been given the authority to rule over them as the wielder of the Blood Oath of Fire.</p>
<p>So as the inheritor of Gillian’s Blood Oath, all the Vympirs knew that Jason was meant to be the one on the throne of Nox, and the only reason why they had accepted Lamina as their leader was because Jason said that it was okay for her to continue ruling! All the elders stood from their seats and bowed to Jason respectfully, and Jason waved his hand once to say that it was okay. Jason told them to sit before he spoke up while looking towards Lamina.</p>
<p>"How are the evacuation plans going?"</p>
<p>Lamina stood up again, and she answered that they were almost finished with evacuating the worlds in Death’s path. Luckily for them, Death was only going to cross over the Western section of the Eighth Domain, so they only had to move the races from the worlds in the Western section towards the East, and they would no longer be in any danger! The real problem was with the world of Nox itself.</p>
<p>"We also fall in the Western section, and we’ve done our best to move the Vympirs who can survive outside of this world, but the lower-ranked Vympirs are waiting on your help, my lord."</p>
<p>Lamina bowed respectfully once she was done talking, and Jason put his hand in his coat pocket and let out a calm sigh as he asked them if they already had a world in mind. One of the elders at the side, a mature-looking woman with short black hair and a dangerous vibe around her, spoke up.</p>
<p>"There is a world known as Lunaris, a few hundred Dimensional jumps east of here. It is very similar to the conditions of Nox since it only has twenty-four hours of daylight and three months of nighttime. The world is currently in the middle of its lunar cycle, so if we move there, I believe there should be no issues for the lower-ranked Vympirs."</p>
<p>Jason nodded to the woman, and he ordered the elders to gather all the lower-ranked Vympirs.</p>
<p>"Make sure they’re all in one place in the next two hours. I have to get back to my world, so I want to finish this as soon as possible."</p>
<p>That was all Jason told them before he vanished from the meeting room, and the elders immediately began to move to complete Jason’s order!</p>
<p>Jason stood at the top of a large mountain that overlooked the main settlement of Nox, and he couldn’t help but appreciate how beautiful it was. Nox was a world that existed in constant night! The sun never got to this world, so all sorts of Vympirs could live here without worrying about getting roasted by a powerful sun!</p>
<p>The full moon in the sky shone its bright silver light down on Jason’s form and cast him in cascading shadows that highlighted his features. There was a gentle breeze blowing across the top of the mountain, and Jason’s face was set in a blank, emotionless look as he watched the people below him while also keeping an eye on everything happening in the Sixth Domain.</p>
<p>Currently, most of the people of Nox were moving around like chickens as they gathered as many of their belongings as possible. Jason already told them that they wouldn’t be able to bring their belongings with them, but it was obvious that the Vympirs were a lot like the humans. They just don’t listen! But it didn’t matter at the end of the day. Death wouldn’t be able to affect nonliving objects, so after they leave their luggage behind, they can just come back for it when they return!</p>
