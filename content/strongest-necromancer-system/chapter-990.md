<p>Jason knew that it would be a good thing for the entire Primordial Expanse to also become stronger! With the way things were going right now, there was a small chance that a new Primordial war would soon break out across the Primordial Expanse. And even though the chances were low, if it did happen, Jason didn’t want the mortals to be helpless against the much stronger Primordials!</p>
<p>"I plan to call a meeting of the council soon to discuss the pill and what it could do for us. If we come to an agreement about payment and distribution, then we can spread it to the rest of the Primordial Domain."</p>
<p>Hesabeth nodded calmly from Jason’s side, and she told Jason that she would look forward to it. Hesabeth was also an Overlord, so she naturally had an interest in the Mortal Severance Pill as well since the pill could help the worlds in Hesabeth’s domain a lot! It didn’t matter if they would have to pay for the pill or not, because at the end of the day, whatever cost they would pay for the pill would be less than what they would have to spend if they tried to raise trillions of mortals to the realm of Immortals using regular training! So long as the pill didn’t have any significant side effects or negatives, then Hesabeth would be more than happy to spread it across her domain!</p>
<p>"What did you do to the Jamani?"</p>
<p>Riyala spoke up curiously when she saw that Jason and Hesabeth were done talking. Riyala had an interest in the mercenaries because those same mercenaries had been blocking some of the trading routes that the Radagan traders were using to move around the Primordial Expanse. Riyala really hoped that Jason had gotten rid of them once and for all! Jason hummed when he heard Riyala’s question, and he narrated the story about how the Jamani had attacked one of the superhumans, and he had to eradicate them for it. Riyala smiled happily when she heard this, and Hesabeth nodded in approval as she spoke.</p>
<p>"That was the right choice to make, darling. I once faced problems with mercenaries in my domain as well, and I had to move across various worlds to force them out once and for all. It was a very tiring process, but I believe it was more than necessary. Mercenaries are nothing but pests and parasites that feast on whatever they can get their claws into."</p>
<p>Hesabeth had a look of genuine irritation on her face when she was done, and Jason could tell that the Mercenaries must’ve been an absolute menace in this domain! Jason put a hand around Hesabeth’s shoulder and pulled her in for a short kiss, and Hesabeth’s frown suddenly vanished into a small smile. Jason grinned back at Hesabeth before he continued his story. This time, Jason spoke about the explosion that nearly took out the Northern Alliance Academy as well as the Primordial that had been attracted to the exposed Primordial Corpse.</p>
<p>"You fought against the Phantasmal Lightning Dragon!? That is one of the higher beasts of nature! Gina, why didn’t you tell us about this?"</p>
<p>Hesabeth was stunned to hear what Jason had gone through, and Hesabeth had no idea how Jason was even still alive right now! The Phantasmal Beasts were seen as some of the most powerful creatures in the Primordial Expanse! They all possessed a strong bond with nature that allowed them to call on one or more elements during their battles! Defeating something like that would automatically put Jason in the top ten percent of the most powerful mages in the world!</p>
<p>Gina had told the other girls that Jason fought against a Primordial, but she never said it was the Phantasmal Lightning Dragon!</p>
<p>"Is that what you were talking about with Jason earlier?"</p>
<p>Riyala asked from the side, and Gina nodded. Gina didn’t think it was necessary to tell the other girls about what sort of beast the Lightning Dragon was. Gina had already told them that Jason fought a Primordial, so she didn’t think there was a need to do more than that! Jason rubbed his arm along Gina’s back, and he told the girls that there was no need to worry since he was fine.</p>
<p>Riyala decided it was time for them to change the topic, and she spoke up with stars in her eyes.</p>
<p>"So, you entered a deal with the Northern Alliance Academy? Do you plan to set up academies in all the major worlds in the sixth domain?"</p>
<p>Jason hummed in confirmation and said that this was exactly what he planned to do.</p>
<p>"Most of the worlds need to learn how to use magic properly, so we’re creating a plan for the best places to put the academies so it’ll benefit everyone. There’ll be major and minor academies on all the worlds so we can accommodate all the students, but I’m also thinking of using this as an exchange program to improve the relationship between worlds. Since there’ll be an Academy on Earth, we can take in students from all the worlds parallel to us, even Radagan. And we can send students from Earth to the parallel worlds as well."</p>
<p>Riyala’s eyes shone even more, and she hugged Jason happily when she heard this! That was an amazing idea! Riyala already knew that this academy would be beneficial to her world, but with Jason’s plan, the academy would become more than just a school for children to learn how to use magic! The Academy was also going to be a place for the children to grow closer and form bonds! This means that the people from Earth and the People from Radagan would most likely have a very friendly relationship in the future, and it would benefit both their worlds a lot!</p>
<p>A round of applause echoed through the opera house as the actors on the stage completed one of their plays, and Jason and his girls turned to look at the bowing actors for a moment! Most of the actors were looking up at the balcony where Hesabeth was sitting with Jason, and it was obvious that they were all waiting for Hesabeth’s approval! Hesabeth calmly clapped along with the others in the hall, and the actors’ faces all bloomed into happy smiles as they bowed to Hesabeth again!</p>
<p>Jason could tell that Hesabeth had a lot of influence on the people in this world! Just her approval alone was able to make most of them feel like they had accomplished a great feat, and Jason couldn’t really blame them for feeling that way! Hesabeth’s bias was an unpredictable power, but the one thing that everyone was certain of was that Hesabeth’s bias worked very closely with her emotions! If Hesabeth was happy with you, then her bias would most likely favour you, but if Hesabeth was not happy with you, then her bias would be against you as well!</p>
<p>To these people, gaining Hesabeth’s approval was the equivalent of gaining the approval of the entire world!</p>
<p>Once the actors were done with their greeting, they left the stage and a stage crew came out and began to prepare the stage for another play. The night was still young, so there would be two more plays tonight before the theatre would close! The people sitting below the balcony all began to move out of their seats to visit the toilet or get something to eat! The entire scene reminded Jason a lot of the movie theatres back home, and Jason couldn’t help but smile as he saw how similar Earth and Minori were!</p>
<p>"I believe it’s time for us to go."</p>
<p>Jason hummed in surprise when he heard that, and he turned towards Hesabeth calmly and asked her why they had to leave.</p>
<p>"Aren’t you enjoying the play?"</p>
