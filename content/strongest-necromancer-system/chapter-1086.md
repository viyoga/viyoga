<p>If Agiel tried to do anything to ruin Jason’s relationship with Gina and the others, then Agiel knew she would lose Jason.</p>
<p>So Agiel simply sighed to the side and decided to accept Jason’s conditions. Before Agiel could get together with Jason, she would talk to the other girls and try to convince them to accept her! Jason sensed Agiel’s acceptance through their link, and he smiled at Agiel when she looked up at him with a pompous huff.</p>
<p>"Fine, I’ll try my best to hold back. But can I at least get a hug?"</p>
<p>Agiel said this while opening her arms and pouting cutely, and Jason chuckled softly as he walked forward and pulled Agiel into a hug. Agiel wrapped her hands around Jason’s waist and put her head on his chest, and imagined that it was just the two of them standing alone in the whole world. In that moment, Agiel felt like she could do anything as long as she could remain right here beside Jason! It didn’t matter how long she had to wait, and she didn’t care who she had to convince, but Agiel swore that she would make sure she became a part of Jason’s family no matter what!</p>
<p>....</p>
<p>A few minutes later, Jason and Agiel finally finished dressing up, and they headed out to meet with Farlow and the other elves.</p>
<p>"Are you sure there’s nothing else you need?"</p>
<p>Farlow spoke up in a worried tone as he stood in front of Jason and Agiel. Currently, a large group made up of Farlow’s family was standing outside their castle, and they were seeing off Jason and Agiel before they headed towards the ninth domain. Farlow’s wife, Camille, had a stressed aura around her as she watched Jason closely, and even Farlow’s children were very worried after they learned about what Jason was about to do!</p>
<p>Jason and Agiel had both changed into new clothes. Jason was still wearing his black artefact jacket, but he was now wearing a grey shirt and black trousers that he had gotten from Farlow, and Agiel had completely changed out of her gown, and she was now wearing one of the military uniforms that she took from the Arandelle military barracks! Agiel had been trapped in gowns and skirts for the past few weeks since she went to Jadar, and Agiel had missed the feeling of being in military attire badly!</p>
<p>Right now, Agiel was in a white military shirt and trousers, and her white hair was tied up into a long ponytail that fell down her back. There was a confident look on her face that had been missing for a long time! One of her hands was in her pocket, and she was holding Jason’s left hand with her other one. Agiel hadn’t let go of Jason for more than a few minutes since they formed their soul bond, and Agiel didn’t have any intention of letting go of him for a very long time! Agiel felt like the most powerful person in the world just standing beside Jason!</p>
<p>Jason shook his head at Farlow’s worried look, and Jason told Farlow that he would be fine.</p>
<p>"I know this isn’t normal, but I have a feeling that it’ll work. We’ll be meeting with Kalum in the ninth domain, so he can provide anything we need."</p>
<p>Camille stepped forward beside her husband and spoke up worriedly:</p>
<p>"Yes, but what you’re going to face is not a normal beast, is it? There’s no physical force that can –"</p>
<p>"You don’t need to worry, Lady Camille. As long as Jason and I are together, there’s nothing in the world that can defeat us. I only ask that you help me look after my world until I return."</p>
<p>Agiel finally spoke up with a confident look in her eyes, and Jason huffed lightly when he felt a surge of confidence flowing into his body through their Soul Link. Although Agiel still didn’t fully understand their Soul Bond and Agiel knew that this mission would rest completely on Jason’s understanding of death, Agiel still did not have any doubt in her mind that they would win! Agiel felt that no matter what happened from here on, so long as she was standing beside Jason, then they would be fine.</p>
<p>Camille was obviously not willing to accept a declaration like that! Just saying you would be okay wasn’t enough reason for you to walk into the claws of death itself! But Farlow put his hand on his wife’s shoulder, and Farlow smiled at Camille before shaking his head and saying it’s alright.</p>
<p>"I trust Jason knows what he is doing. If they say they will be alright, then we can only give them our support."</p>
<p>Camille let out a long sigh before she walked forward and grabbed both Jason and Agiel in a hug! Jason was surprised by the show of affection, but Agiel was already used to Camille’s touchy nature since Agiel used to live here when she was a little girl. Agiel patted Camille on the back softly, and Camille pulled back a few seconds later and spoke softly.</p>
<p>"I just want you both to be alright. And... just know there is no shame in running away if you ever realise that there is nothing you can do."</p>
<p>Once she said this, Camille stepped back, but both Jason and Agiel didn’t bother taking her words seriously. There was no chance that they would be running away from this. Jason gave a nod to Farlow and the other royal family members before he and Agiel fell into the shadows and vanished.</p>

<p>[The Ninth Domain]</p>
<p>[The World of Voltax]</p>
<p>Voltax was a fourth-ranked world located in the territory of Overlord Kalum. As a fourth-ranked world, it contained some of the most magically advanced races in the ninth domain, and it was normal to see multiple TITAN Ranks within this world! But right now, most of the world was completely devoid of any form of life!</p>
<p>Due to the evacuation notice by Overlord Kalum, all the major races in this world had been moved towards parallel worlds in order to try and escape from the Celestial of Death! Voltax was a world filled with multiple kingdoms and towns, but all those kingdoms had been emptied, and the towns were nothing but ghost towns! The only kingdom where you would find any sign of life was the largest kingdom, which had been turned into a temporary military barracks by Kalum’s army.</p>
<p>There were tall soldiers with wings moving across the castle, and more soldiers were standing at the top of tall towers as they kept an eye on the large cloud of black smoke approaching them from a distance. The overlord of the ninth Domain, Kalum, was inside the castle at this moment, and he was talking to his secretary while waiting impatiently for Jason and Agiel to finally show up!</p>
<p>"My Lord, you need to calm down. They sent a message saying they are already on their way."</p>
<p>Kalum’s beautiful secretary spoke up when she saw Kalum staring at the wall and tapping his foot impatiently, but Kalum just waved her off with a frown!</p>
<p>"It’s been hours since the last message, and we still haven’t seen them. If the Celestial of Death manages to leave this world, it will enter the world of Vinra that’s still under evacuation. It could kill hundreds of millions before we stop it."</p>
<p>Kalum’s secretary let out a soft sigh as she realised that the overlord was correct! This entire operation depended a lot on Jason and Agiel stopping this beast here before it could get any further through their domain! But the secretary knew that there was something else that had been disturbing Kalum all this while as well. None of them knew if Jason and Agiel were actually capable of stopping the Celestial of Death!</p>
