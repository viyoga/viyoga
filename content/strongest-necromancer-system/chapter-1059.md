<p>"All Elder Worlds that are in charge of those sections of the domain are to send requests for anything they might need to the finance department of Arandelle. We will provide you with artefacts, transportation vehicles, and storage devices for all your worlds. Remember how hard we have worked to create such a peaceful world. We must ensure it is not destroyed by this mad creature."</p>
<p>The Elders all nodded in agreement as they accepted their leader’s words, and Farlow was about to speak again, but he stopped as someone came through the door. It was Farlow’s wife, Queen Camille, and she entered with a graceful elegance and greeted all the elders with a smile before moving towards her husband.</p>
<p>Farlow put his hand out towards his wife and touched her waist as she came close, and he leaned toward her as she bent to speak into his ear.</p>
<p>"You have a message, darling. It’s from Lord Jason."</p>
<p>Farlow blinked in surprise, and he collected the letter that Camille handed to him. Farlow smiled at her thankfully as she left, and Farlow opened the letter to see what was inside. The letter only had two sentences written in it, and the moment Farlow read it, Farlow knew that there would be a lot of trouble in the future!</p>
<p>[I know you told me to, but I just can’t ignore it. I’ll deal with the consequences later.]</p>
<p>Farlow put a hand to his forehead and groaned in annoyance! This bastard was going to send him to an early grave!</p>
<p>’Why does he have to be so much like his father!?’</p>
<p>Farlow already knew what Jason was about to do, and Farlow honestly couldn’t believe that Jason was doing something like this in the middle of such a dire period!</p>
<p>"My Lord, what is the matter?"</p>
<p>The centaur spoke up when he noticed Farlow’s distressed look, and Farlow just waved him away and said it was nothing. Farlow folded the letter and put it away in his robe calmly. Although Farlow did not like Jason acting up like this, Farlow knew that Jason was a smart man, so Jason would have a good reason for doing what he was doing!</p>
<p>If Jason managed to stop the marriage and get Agiel out of that world, there would be a lot of consequences for it. Especially since Jason is an Overlord of another domain entirely. But Farlow decided not to allow Jason’s actions to distract him from what they were doing right now. Farlow could only deal with Jason if they managed to live through this disaster!</p>
<p>"Instruct every world to begin immediate evacuation plans. This is a direct order from the Overlord. You are dismissed."</p>
<p>The Elders were all surprised when they heard Farlow’s hard tone. Didn’t Farlow want them to wait for a few more days before they began the evacuation plans? But the Elders knew better than to argue with their Overlord, so the Elders simply nodded their agreement before they began opening Dimensional Tears to return to their worlds. Farlow frowned, and he finally stood up to begin his own plans as well. Farlow knew the evacuation wasn’t meant to start this soon, but Farlow had his reasons for pushing it up so suddenly. Farlow just hoped that this would be enough to help Jason a little more.</p>
<p>....</p>
<p>[The Third Domain]</p>
<p>[The World of Jadar]</p>
<p>For the past few days, the world of Jadar had been in a very festive mood! The news of the wedding between the former princess of Jadar and the current King raised the morale of all the people because they all knew that this wedding would bring an end to the bloody war that had taken the lives of millions of their people! There had been festivals and celebrations taking place every single day leading up to the wedding! But recently, those celebrations had begun to die out!</p>
<p>The news of the Celestial of Death passing over the Primordial Expanse had finally reached Jadar, and the people all felt a deep terror in their hearts when they realised that they were one of the worlds that the Celestial would pass through! The evacuation order had already come from Overlord Farlow, and the people had immediately begun gathering their belongings and preparing to leave this world so they wouldn’t get caught by Death, but all of that had been stopped by the current King of Jadar!</p>
<p>The King told the people that no one was going to be leaving the world until after the wedding ceremony between him and the princess! Although the Overlord had already sent out a command telling the people to evacuate, the King had ignored that message! Instead, the King moved the wedding so that it would happen sooner, and the King’s right-hand man, Vinaris, informed the people that the Celestial of Death was still in the Ninth Domain, very far away from where they were. So they would have more than enough time to carry out the evacuations!</p>
<p>Right now, the people were all gathered in the kingdom square right below a massive high stage, and they were waiting for the ceremony to start. Although this was meant to be a celebratory function, there was a wave of nervousness going through everyone there, and it was obvious that they all wanted this wedding to end as quickly as possible so they could finally evacuate this world!</p>
<p>....</p>
<p>[Central Wing of the Royal Palace]</p>
<p>In a room deep within the royal palace, the current King of Jadar was throwing a tantrum!</p>
<p>"That fool! How dare he bring up an evacuation so close to my wedding!? He believes he has more authority over my world just because he is the Overlord! If I get my hands on him, I will tear him apart!"</p>
<p>The King of Jadar was a large, handsome man, with short black hair and green eyes. His name was Tigal, and he was a TITAN Rank II being and the strongest warrior in Jadar! King Tigal had a long scar running across the right side of his face, and he was currently dressed in a smart-looking military suit that showed off his strong figure! Before he became the ruler of this world, Tigal had been a general in the Jadite army who did not approve of the Queen’s choice to marry the previous King. Tigal knew that he was a far better candidate for king, so rather than allowing the failure of a Queen to rule them, Tigal had taken matters into his own hands, and he staged a coup that overthrew the previous government in barely five years!</p>
<p>Now, Tigal was ruler over Jadar, and in order to make sure that his rule was cemented, Tigal was planning to marry the daughter of the previous Queen. Tigal needed to show the people that he had truly gained full control over this world, and after this wedding, Tigal was sure there would be no more complaints about his rule!</p>
<p>At the side, the right-hand man of the King, Vinaris, was standing with his hands joined in front of him silently. There was a coy smile on Vinaris’ face, like there was a massive comedy being played somewhere and he was the only one who could see it, but no one dared to say anything about Vinaris’ strange tendencies since they knew that this was simply how he was!</p>
<p>Vinaris watched with pure white eyes as the King threw a tantrum about the Overlord’s order, and Vinaris’ gaze became slightly condescending as he watched the childish act. In truth, although King Tigal was a very powerful man, Vinaris knew that Tigal was not very smart. For the past ten years, Vinaris was the one who had been pulling the strings of the kingdom from behind the scenes!</p>
