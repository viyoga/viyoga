<p>"If you want to appraise and sell those drops, then you can approach the SMD Appraisal Department tomorrow. There will be an official test in a few days to register your ability for the Superhuman Database, so you should expect a call."</p>
<p>Jason nodded. He was about to leave, but Liam suddenly spoke up again to stop him in his tracks. Jason turned to Liam, and Liam smiled apologetically before talking.</p>
<p>"I'd appreciate it if you heard me out for a few more minutes."</p>
<p>Jason nodded while leaning against the wall, and Liam continued.</p>
<p>"We at the SMD are not independent like the rest of the guilds in the country. We don't go into Gates or fight monsters on a daily basis. But what we do here is just as—if not more—important. We keep order among the Superhumans and ensure the lives of the regular humans are not affected by the presence of Superhuman beings. Why am I telling you all this? It's simple. I think you would make a good addition to the SMD. Your skill set and power align with our values, and we will work well together. As far as monetary compensation goes, your salary will equal or surpass the average salary of your rank in guilds across the country. It's very favorable working conditions."</p>
<p>Once Liam finished his speech, he waited patiently for a response. Just like all the other guild representatives who were waiting outside for Jason to appear, Liam was also interested in Jason's power, as well as how powerful he would be in the future. Awakening as an A-Rank meant that Jason had the potential to become just as strong as the Five Rulers, and that was not something any recruiter could overlook.</p>
<p>It was just that Liam had more power than the others, which allowed him to talk to Jason before anyone else.</p>
<p>There were many benefits that came with being a part of the government!</p>
<p>But the moment he finished talking, Liam could already see Jason's reaction plainly on his face. At that moment, Jason had the same look that many others who were asked to join the SMD had at one point or another. The look of someone who desired more in life.</p>
<p>Whenever people hear about joining the SMD, they immediately assume that they're signing up to become office workers or slaves for the government. There was a bias surrounding them that would never be removed, and Liam could tell that Jason also held the same bias.</p>
<p>Liam smiled as Jason spoke.</p>
<p>"Sorry, but I'm not thinking of joining any organizations right now. Especially the government. There's too much protocol to observe."</p>
<p>Liam hummed. He already expected as much, so he wasn't surprised.</p>
<p>"Well, if you ever change your mind, the SMD will always be available to you. We'll contact you at a later date for your assessment. Oh, and before I leave, you should keep in mind that you will be required to pay reparations for the damage you did to the city. This is only your first offense, so the payment will suffice, but on future offenses, there will be more consequences. The use of Gifts are prohibited outside of the Gate for the safety of the civilians. I'm sure you understand..."</p>
<p>Liam stretched his hand out for a handshake, and Jason took it in a firm hold and nodded before he watched Liam leave.</p>
<p>Jason didn't really have any issue with paying for the street repair. He was going to make so much money selling the Magic Crystals that he wouldn't even know what to do with it all.</p>

<p>She was nowhere near as endowed as Gina, but that didn't mean she wasn't sexy.</p>

<p>"I'm fine, Jason. I should be the one asking you if you're okay. You were in that Gate for so long, I thought..."</p>
