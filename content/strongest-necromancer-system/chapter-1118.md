<p>Jason steeled his mind and hardened his heart as he walked through the world of Ventag! Jason knew that there would be a lot of people around the Mind Apostle who were only being controlled by his Arcane Art, but Jason didn’t have the liberty of showing any mercy. Jason planned to take the Mind Apostle and all his minions out in one move, and that was exactly what Jason would do!</p>
<p>As Jason walked through the deserted town, the shadows all around him slowly began to move. The Toulli were a very cautious race, and they would usually run away the moment they saw any strangers, but the Toulli had no idea that for the past month, they had been living in the midst of thousands of extremely dangerous creatures! The shadows bulged and grew as thousands of Legion subordinates began to emerge! The Legion subordinates were the ones Jason sent out to look across the Sixth Domain for the Mind Apostle. After finding the Mind Apostle in Tarisavia, the Legion subordinates had all hidden in the shadows and waited there for the past few weeks so that Jason would arrive!</p>
<p>There were giant bears and massive iron-bladed falcons. Five dragons spread their wings wide and flapped them as they rose into the air, and thousands of other Bishop and Rook-rank subordinates all prepared for battle! Jason put his hand out and patted the head of Fenrir, his most loyal Moonlight Wolf.</p>
<p>CAW!</p>
<p>The crimson phoenix, Helios, soared through the air above Jason’s head before it swerved down and landed on Jason’s shoulder.</p>
<p>’My King. The Dimensional Tear is open.’</p>
<p>Jason turned to his left to see one of his mercenary subordinates kneeling before him, and Jason looked past the subordinate to see that there was a massive Dimensional Tear open in the distance. That was the Dimensional Tear that led to the world of Tarisavia, and it had taken more than two full days before they were able to open it. When they first tried to open it, Jason noticed that there was a magic protection around the world of Tarisavia that prevented them from entering. But thankfully, the magic protection was not very complicated, so it wasn’t difficult for Azrael to break through it! Jason could have simply forced his way through the magical barrier, but Jason had made Azrael take time to break through it slowly because Jason didn’t want to alert the Mind Apostle to his presence! Jason wanted this attack to be a surprise for the bastard!</p>
<p>Jason immediately told all his subordinates to enter his Alternate Dimension before Jason walked through the tear and entered the world of Tarisavia.</p>
<p>....</p>
<p>Jason appeared in the sky high above the mountain where Theosis’ hideout was located!</p>
<p>DOON!</p>
<p>The moment Jason appeared above the mountain, there was a sudden pressure that travelled across the world as Jason’s power pressed down on every organism in this region! Every beast in this world was under Theosis’ control, and they all stopped moving the moment they sensed the danger that had appeared!</p>
<p>Inside Theosis’ hideout, all the slaves froze in the middle of their tasks as well, and they looked up in shock when they felt the PRIMORDIAL mana! Even Theosis jolted into attention inside his room as he realised that something had changed!</p>
<p>Jason put his hand out and activated two powers at the same time!</p>
<p>First, a rune on his palm came to life with a blue glow!</p>
<p>[Extreme Distortion]!</p>
<p>This was a second-grade, one-time-use rune that Jason had created together with Azrael! It had taken them more than a week of constant work to craft it, and the rune was so high-level that it couldn’t maintain its own structure under the weight of its power. This meant that the moment the rune was activated, it would disintegrate, and you would need to take time to craft a new one in order to activate it again!</p>
<p>Jason could say without a doubt that it was the single greatest rune he had ever crafted! The [Extreme Distortion] rune allowed Jason to create an alternate dimension within a set radius. And within this alternate dimension, Jason would bombard the mana particles in the air with so many destructive frequencies that any organism that wields magic in this set region would be incapable of functioning! Thinking, moving, and breathing would all cease due to the destructive interference, and it would remain this way until the rune breaks!</p>
<p>Jason had thought long and hard about how he was going to combat the Mind Apostle and his allies all this time. Jason knew that the Mind Apostle wasn’t working alone. There was someone else working with the Mind Apostle who was capable of opening Dimensional Tears using a Space Arcane Art. This meant that there was no way Jason could use a regular artefact or spell to try and stop them from escaping! Jason needed to think of another method that could allow him to affect not only the Mind Apostle, but also the Space Apostle as well!</p>
<p>And after thinking about it for a long time, Jason finally came to a realisation! Even though these people wielded Arcane Arts that they could use without magic, Jason knew that they also wielded regular magic!</p>
<p>The Mind Apostle was capable of casting regular spells, and whoever wielded the Space Art would also be capable of using regular magic! And so long as they had even the tiniest atom of magical energy inside them, then Jason could attack them using the Distortion Rune!</p>
<p>The [Extreme Distortion] rune was so effective that no mage within a set radius would be able to move for at least one minute, but although it was very effective, it was also not ideal because in order to maintain the rune properly, Jason needed to be inside the cast radius of this rune, and that meant that Jason would also be affected by his own Distortion! Jason would’ve had Azrael activate the rune for him, but the strength of the rune was heavily dependent on the strength of the mage that casts it, and since Jason didn’t know how strong the Space Apostle was, Jason decided to cast this himself so that it would have the highest chance of working!</p>
<p>But it didn’t matter that Jason would be affected by the rune, because Jason had already accounted for it! That was why Jason had activated two abilities at the same time! The first ability was the [Extreme Distortion] rune to disrupt every mage within the region, but the second ability was a single drop of pure Resonance!</p>
<p>DOON!</p>
<p>The moment [Extreme Distortion] activated, every mage within a five-mile radius held their heads in pain and immediately fell to their knees! Theosis grunted as he felt the magic in his body vibrating like it was trying to tear itself apart, and most of the women in the mansion who were under [Mind Corruption] were screaming while they fell to the floor in pain! Up in the higher floors of the mansion, Bathseda, the Space Apostle, was holding onto a table while grasping their head in agony, and Bathseda stumbled and almost fell to the ground as their magic slammed against their body dangerously!</p>
<p>Bathseda had already felt Jason’s mana earlier, and Bathseda had been gathering things so that they could escape! Bathseda knew that they had to get out of here before Jason got to them! But there was no way for Bathseda to move while they were in so much pain!</p>
<p>And it was while everyone was suffering the pain from the Extreme Distortion that they felt the pressure of Resonance!</p>
