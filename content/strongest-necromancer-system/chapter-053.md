<p>The ants had heard a fight happening here, so they expected to see their brother killing another creature. They knew that their brother wasn’t stupid, so he wouldn’t attack a creature that he wasn’t sure he could handle.</p>
<p>The ants immediately suspected that something was wrong and one of them moved closer to the corpse while the others began moving around the forest to see if they could find what had killed their brother.</p>

<p>The anthill was an ecosystem that had been self-sustaining for a long time. The Adamantine Ants were omnivores, and they possessed strong mandibles made from adamantine that allowed them to cut and carry tree logs and carcasses of stray animals around with them.</p>
<p>The ants that went out for these tasks were the worker ants. These ants were wingless and they possessed a smaller body than the soldiers and the scouts, which made it easy for the soldiers to keep them in line even though they were far fewer.</p>
<p>There were four entrances to the maze-like hill, and each of the entrances had its own workers and soldiers who were in charge of carrying food and protecting that area from intruders.</p>
<p>The worker ants were currently scavenging for food all around the hilltop with some soldiers flying around them. The soldiers were twice as large as the Scouts, but they possessed a much stronger body and mandibles that looked like silver daggers. They were flying with an aura of danger around them and anytime they saw a stray beast or monster in their domain, they would immediately attack it with their powerful mandibles, tearing it to pieces and then carrying it back to the tunnel entrance so that the workers could take it to their Queen.</p>
<p>There were ten soldiers and close to fifty workers in this one space and Jason watched them from a distance with his [Predator’s Gaze]. <p>Jason was trying to come up with a proper plan of action in his mind. The ten soldier ants had formed a matrix of protection around the entrance. They weren’t moving away from the entrance because the scouts were the ones in charge of looking for enemies and they would only move if the scouts reported back that there was something for them to kill.</p>
<p>So once Jason begins his plan, he was sure that the soldiers would all move away from the entrance to go and attack the wolves. And when they leave, Jason would use that opportunity to attack the worker ants and gain more subordinates!</p>
<p>Jason had gained more than five hundred kill points from the system after killing the nine scouts so far, so he decided to use the five hundred kill points to evolve one of his Pawn Rank II Adamantine Ant Scouts into a Pawn Rank III.</p>

Jason then transferred the points into one of the ants in his Alternate Dimension and the ant’s body changed and grew into a larger version of itself. Its wings grew larger and the metallic skin grew stronger. Its mandibles also doubled in size and Jason could see the similarities between the Pawn Rank III scout and the Soldier ants that were at the tunnel entrance.</p>
<p>’Okay, the forces should be evenly matched now. There are ten soldier ants, so I’ll send twenty subordinates to fight them. Four of them will be Pawn Rank III and then sixteen Pawn Rank II. I’ll send the rest of my subordinates to block the entrance and make sure the workers can’t escape into the tunnel before killing them too.’</p>
