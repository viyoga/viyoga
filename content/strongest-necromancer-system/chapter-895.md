<p>Various Legion subordinates were flying through the air, and many of the Adamantine Ants and dragons grabbed any Bengari that they could reach and held them in place so that Gina’s attack could land more easily! The leader of the Bengari air forces was a woman with short red hair and a nasty glare in her eyes! She wore streamlined armor that made it easier for her to move quickly, and she had on a pair of shoe artefacts that allowed her to fly quicker than the others! The woman had twin swords in her hands, and she was flying past all the Legion subordinates and soldiers to try and get to Gina from behind! The swords wielded by the leader of the air force were special poison artefacts retrieved from the stinger of a giant Redtail Poison Scorpion, so one slash was all she needed to bring Gina down!</p>
<p>The leader reduced her presence and sped up even more as she tried to get to Gina before Gina would notice her, but the leader was shocked when a loud laugh echoed from somewhere to her right!</p>
<p>"HAHAHAHA!"</p>
<p>The leader looked to the side, and she saw a flaming man flying towards her like a comet, and before she could even react, the man slammed her out of the sky!</p>
<p>BOOM!</p>
<p>Stragga laughed again as he felt a thrill go through his body, and he grabbed the leader by the neck before slamming her into the ground and creating a massive crater where they landed! The leader tried to break free from Stragga’s hold, but Stragga was far too strong, and the leader immediately lashed out with one of her poison swords! But Stragga lifted her off the ground and tossed her into the distance before he pulled out a massive sword from a spatial space beside him and sent it flying after her! The leader slammed into a mountain with a deep grunt, and at the next moment, the flaming sword stabbed through her stomach and pinned her to the mountain! The leader gasped from the pain and held the sword tightly to try and pull it out, but Stragga appeared on the mountain again and pulled his sword back before blasting off the mountain and heading back into the fight without giving the leader another glance!</p>
<p>Stragga let out another laugh, and all the Bengari soldiers around couldn’t help but look at him like he had gone mad, but Stragga didn’t care! Stragga had felt it! He actually felt it just now! For more than ten years, Stragga had been traumatized by what he had seen in the Green Gate and had been wondering if it would be possible for any human to challenge the PRIMORDIALS! Was a level of power like that something that humanity could ever hope to achieve!? But just now, Stragga had felt a power that was similar to what he had felt that day! It wasn’t as strong as what he felt, and Stragga knew that the two powers were like night and day, but that didn’t mean they were worlds apart anymore! Jason had just released a power that was comparable to the Primordials, and Stragga could feel a surge of hope rising from deep inside him as he realised that it was possible! Humanity could actually reach the heights of the Primordials!</p>
<p>Multiple Bengari women charged after Stragga, but a green light glowed around Stragga’s body, and Stragga let out another laugh as his entire body lit up in flames! Stragga’s sword burned with a fire that was hotter than the sun, and he planted his feet and slashed his sword through the air to unleash a blast that sent everyone flying back! Stragga was one of the first superhumans to break out of the realm of mortals and become a TITAN, but Stragga knew that his power alone wouldn’t be enough to cause this much damage to the Bengari, and the boost in Stragga’s strength was thanks to the other ruler who had also broken into the realm of TITANs, Katarina!</p>
<p>Katarina was standing on a mountain a far distance away from the battle, with multiple subordinates surrounding her as protection, and her eyes were closed as she sent out all of her mana to support as many of the fighters as she could!</p>
<p>Right now, Katarina was supporting all the superhumans as well as more than a thousand subordinates at the same time, and she was managing to raise the power level of the weaker subordinates by nearly a hundred percent while giving the stronger fighters a boost of around seventy percent! As the strongest supporter in existence, this was the current limit of what Katarina could do! But thanks to that boost, Stragga had enough power flowing through his body to challenge even TITAN Rank IIs, and when you add in the excitement Stragga was feeling because of Jason’s ascension into the realm of Primordials, you’d understand why Stragga was laughing like a madman while charging through the battlefield!</p>
<p>In the icy regions of the battlefield, Francis let out a cold breath as he faced off against multiple Bengari warriors with Legion subordinates supporting him! Francis’ eyes glowed with a red light, and he used his gift to mentally take hold of the metal armor worn by the Bengari women, and Francis psychically threw all the warriors under his control towards their own comrades to serve as a distraction so that the Legion could attack! Along with Stragga and Katarina, Francis was the third member of this team that had finally broken through to the TITAN Ranks, and the moment he transcended the realm of mortals, his gift also evolved in a way that allowed him to control any form of metal with his mind! Francis’ gift had been limited before, and that was why he had only been able to control the ten bands on his hand, but now that limitation was gone!</p>
<p>"ARRRGGH!"</p>
<p>A Bengari warrior shouted as she charged at Francis, but Francis’ eyes glowed red, and he swung his arm! The Bengari’s sword was immediately torn out of her hand, and the Bengari found herself flying back and slamming into the ground with intense force! The Bengari’s sword floated up into the air, and the Bengari let out a scream as the sword dropped and stabbed her into the ground! Francis frowned as he looked toward his mana reserves, and he saw that he was already at half capacity. Along with the increased power, Francis also realised that he was consuming a lot more mana and stamina during this fight, and he knew he had to get out of here and recuperate before coming back to continue fighting!</p>
<p>BUOOOHHHH!</p>
<p>All of a sudden, Francis turned his head to the left in shock as he heard the loud bellow of a monster! The [Beast of the Apocalypse], Arcticus, walked over the landscape slowly, causing massive tremors with each step! The Legion subordinates all passed messages between each other as they felt Arcticus’ mana reaching out to them, and they quickly abandoned their battles and dived into the shadows! Francis was surprised by what happened, but he didn’t have time to complain as one of the subordinates appeared behind him and pulled him into the shadows as well! The subordinates understood that Arcticus was about to join the battle, and it would be best for them not to be here when he did! The subordinates quickly began to leave the battlefield, and the Bengari women were left behind as Arcticus finally unleashed his aura!</p>
<p>WOOOSH!</p>
<p>A wave of icy cold air travelled across the landscape! Even though this region was already extremely cold, the aura unleashed by Arcticus was so imposing that it actually began to freeze the snow and ice into a layer of pure silver! All the Bengari women who saw the aura heading in their direction felt a surge of fear in their hearts, and they all began running to escape the battlefield! But there was nothing they could do to escape nature itself, and the aura washed over them all and froze them into human-sized ice sculptures with looks of anguish on their faces!</p>
