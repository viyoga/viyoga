<p>Jason was still calling out the locations calmly while his Phantoms tore through the potential bombers, but Jason’s mind wasn’t entirely on the current situation. Even though Jason had gotten the information about this attack from the New Order member that he killed, there had also been more information that the man had. And the most important information that Jason got from the man was the location of one of the New Order bases over in Australia. Jason had come back to America with Agiel to complete this mission, but he sent fifty subordinates, led by Jaraht, the dragonborn General, to take care of the base. And Jason just got a message from Jaraht that they had found and killed all of the New Order members that were hiding in the base. Jason acknowledged the message and told Jaraht that he did a good job before he activated the skill.</p>
<p>[Remote Resurrection] had been activated.</p>
<p>Jason used the [Eye of the King] skill to focus on one of the New Order members who looked important, and he made this one man the target of his resurrection. Mana surged out from Jaraht’s body, and it latched onto the man’s soul before dragging it out from his body and into the world of the living. Once Jason had the man’s soul, he received an entire wave of new memories, and Jason frowned when he learned that there were ten more New Order bases in Germany, Australia, and even in America.</p>
<p>’I’ll leave Jaraht and some other subordinates to deal with them. They aren’t a threat right now, but I shouldn’t leave them to do whatever they want.’</p>
<p>Jason called on a hundred Rook Rank subordinates in his Legion, and he gave them an order to go and eradicate the rest of the New Order bases around the world. Jason knew the locations already, so he just divided the subordinates into ten groups of ten before sending them off! There were thousands of New Order members in all those bases, but Jason wasn’t worried about the numbers since he knew that most of the members were either regular humans or weak superhumans. Jaraht alone would’ve been enough to deal with all of them, but Jason just wanted to make sure no one escaped, so he sent enough subordinates for the mission.</p>
<p>——</p>
<p>New Mission: Eradicate the New Order cult</p>
<p>A cult called New Order that aims at the eradication of all superhumans has grown rapidly over the past few months. The user must eradicate all the major executive members of the cult as well as all lower-class members in order to complete this mission.</p>
<p>Rewards:</p>
<p>+40,000 Free Kill Points</p>
<p>The user will gain the title [Neighbourhood Hero]</p>
<p>——</p>
<p>Jason ignored the sudden message from the system, and he told Jaraht and the other subordinates to be as thorough as possible in clearing out the New Order bases. If they saw anyone in there that looked like they were prisoners or they had been kidnapped, then they should drop them off at any SMD headquarters in that country, but other than that, everyone else should be killed! Jaraht responded that he understood, and Jason finally turned away and refocused on what he had been doing.</p>
<p>"You’ve been silent for a while. Is everything okay?"</p>
<p>Agiel walked up to Jason and put her hand on his back while saying this, and Jason glanced back to see her standing very close to him. Jason raised a brow at her, and Agiel smiled confidently as she refused to move from her position. Jason just shrugged as he answered.</p>
<p>"It’s fine. I’m just monitoring something else. There are some other New Order bases around the world, so I’m taking care of those ones as well. I should be done here soon, there are only about thirty—"</p>
<p>Jason suddenly stopped in the middle of what he had been saying, and Agiel raised a brow when she saw his face twist into a furious look! Agiel took a step back from Jason in slight fear when she saw the intense anger that came over him, and she asked Jason what was going on! Did something happen with the mission? Jason didn’t even bother to respond to Agiel’s words as he felt an intense anger rising from somewhere deep inside his heart! Jason had just gotten a message from one of his subordinates who had been protecting his sisters, and the subordinate told Jason that his sisters and Gina had been attacked.</p>
<p>[King’s Substitute] has been activated.</p>
<p>Jason immediately activated this skill and vanished from Core City, and Agiel was shocked when she saw a Rook Rank Phantom suddenly appearing in front of her. The Phantom looked at Agiel for a second before he slowly bled into the shadows below him and vanished from sight. Agiel could only wonder what the fuck just happened!</p>
<p>...</p>
<p>[Los Angeles]</p>
<p>[The Vincent Thomas Bridge]</p>
<p>"We need medical attention over here! Are there any healers! We need your help!"</p>
<p>A woman shouted loudly over the bridge as she pressed down on the bleeding wound of an unconscious man. The entire Vincent Thomas Bridge was in absolute chaos after the attack from the leader of the New Order. The bridge had fallen from its position high up above the river, and it took all of the cars and people that were on it when it plummeted to the river below! And the only thing that prevented everyone here from drowning were the giant Swamp Vipers keeping the bridge afloat! There were many people who got injured or killed after the bridge dropped, and currently, all the superhumans who had been on the bridge were trying their best to help those who were trapped under cars or injured!</p>
<p>The woman who called out for a healer was Madam Rose, the leader of the B-Ranked Hornet Guild. Madam Rose had a pinched look on her face as she looked around for any healer that wasn’t already working on someone else, and she cursed as she looked down at the man who was bleeding under her hands. The man had fallen unconscious in his car after getting stabbed by a giant rod, and even though she managed to get him out of the car, she wasn’t able to stop the bleeding.</p>
<p>’I’ll have to burn it to keep it cauterized. It’ll leave a nasty scar, but it’s better than leaving him to die here.’</p>
<p>Madam Rose had an ability that allowed her to coat her hands in fire and use them as weapons in a fight, but she had learned a bit of first aid from when she was in Core City, and she knew that she could use her flames to prevent this man from dying. Madam Rose quickly activated her Gift, and the smell of burning flesh rose through the air as she began to burn the man’s wound!</p>
<p>"ARGGGHH!"</p>
<p>The man suddenly burst back to consciousness and screamed at the top of his lungs when he felt the pain from the burns, but Madam Rose kept him pinned with her superior strength, and she didn’t stop burning the wound until she was sure that she had covered it properly! Once she was done, the man passed out again, and Madam Rose checked to make sure that he was still alive before she stood up and looked around her. There were people crying and screaming as they mourned their loved ones, and Madam Rose felt a surge of anger rising from deep inside her when she realized that there was nothing she could do for those who had been killed. She was a superhuman, not a miracle worker. But Madam Rose couldn’t help but feel responsible for all this. There was no doubt as to why this bridge had been attacked. Although Madam Rose didn’t know how anyone found out about the convoy of superhuman leaders that were being moved today, this was one of the only times when more than twenty leaders of various guilds would all be at the same place at the same time, and it was the perfect opportunity for any terrorist organization to take out a majority of the guild leaders in one move. This had all happened because of them.</p>
<p>Madam Rose looked up in anger to look at the person responsible for all this, but Madam Rose knew that she didn’t have to worry about handling the terrorist because someone was already giving him the fight of his life!</p>
<p>BOOM!</p>
<p>A supersonic boom echoed through the area as two large typhoons slammed into each other, and Yuselv shouted as he froze the typhoons into ice and sent them towards the woman opposite him!</p>
<p>SLASH!</p>
<p>But a large blade of wind easily tore the ice apart, and Gina appeared from within the falling ice and shot towards Yuslev! Yuselv quickly formed another rain of ice and fired it at Gina, but Gina put her hand out and unleashed a ball of wind that tore the ice apart like they were made of nothing! Yuslev felt shock pass through him when Gina easily tore through his attack again, and he screamed at Gina in pure fury!</p>
<p>"WHAT THE FUCK ARE YOU!?"</p>
