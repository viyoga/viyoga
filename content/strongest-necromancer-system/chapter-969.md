<p>The girl suddenly put her hand over his face and began laughing! The girl’s body shook as she threw her head back from the sheer intensity of her laughter, and the superhuman immediately took a few steps back from the laughing girl while wondering what was going on!</p>
<p>But he was even more shocked when the girl’s body began to change! The girl grew taller, and her shoulders extended! Her clothes changed to a pair of black trousers and a shirt, and her hair shortened into a more masculine look! By the time her transformation was done, the girl had completely transformed into a man, and the man looked at the superhuman with amusement dancing in his eyes!</p>
<p>"They were right about you humans! You’ll really fall for the crying little girl every time! What a bunch of sympathetic fools!"</p>
<p>The superhuman pulled back in shock, and he put his hand to his ear and immediately started calling for support! The superhuman could feel an immense pressure coming from the man in front of him, and he knew that this shapeshifting man was far stronger than him! But before the superhuman could call for help, the shapeshifter’s hand flashed through the air, and a dagger suddenly flew straight into the superhuman’s chest!</p>
<p>Squelch!</p>
<p>The superhuman gasped in pain and looked down at his chest as blood began to flow freely down his body, and in the next moment he stumbled and fell to his knees!</p>
<p>The shapeshifting man crouched beside him with an amused laugh!</p>
<p>"You fool! I can’t believe all it took was a weak appearance and a smiling face, and you actually walked right into your death!? Hahaha! Fools like you who call themselves good people are the best! I don’t even have to work for the kill; you literally bring yourself to my blade – Eh?"</p>
<p>The man’s rant was suddenly cut short as he saw various Machina emerging from the shadows. Some of them moved towards the superhuman with green healing circles glowing on their hands, and the others immediately dashed towards the shapeshifting man threateningly!</p>
<p>’Guess it’s time to go!’</p>
<p>The shapeshifting man grinned as he had this thought, and he stood quickly and took multiple steps back before melting into the wall with a playful laugh!</p>
<p>The man already knew how much damage this one action would cause to the entire Jamani mercenary group, and in truth, the man didn’t care. There was only one person in this world that was interesting enough to meet, and the man would use the upcoming carnage to his advantage to finally speak to the great Overlord of this domain.</p>
<p>Up in the sky, above the royal city of L’horm, a storm was gathering! Dark clouds formed over the city and lightning arched across the sky as loud thunder echoed through the air. The commoners who lived in the city surrounding the main castle all looked up in surprise when they felt the storm approaching, and before they could even ask any questions, they all felt an intense pressure slam down on them! The unimaginable pressure forced every single person in the city to their knees, and they all wondered what in the world was going on! None of them could guess that this storm was a result of one man releasing his power! Jason stood in the air, high above the L’horm castle with a calm look on his face. On the outside, anyone who looked at Jason would never be able to tell what Jason was feeling, but on the inside, there was an intense fury flowing through Jason’s body, and his anger revealed itself in the way his mana flowed across the entire world and forced the sky to darken with a storm!</p>
<p>After Jason saw the superhumans being attacked by the shapeshifter, Jason had taken a look into the room that the little girl had been crying in front of, and he discovered that the room was the L’horm treasury filled with hundreds of millions of gold coins that the Jamani were secretly moving into their transportation ship behind the castle. Jason had already been looking for a reason to get rid of the Jamani right from the first moment he arrived in this world! Not only did Jason hate the fact that the Jamani mercenaries were in his domain, but Jason also hated the fact that the Jamani were pretending to be good people who had helped liberate the people of L’horm when they were actually just crooks looking to plunder this world dry! People like the Jamani were twisted thieves who only cared about how much they put in their pocket! You could never trust a mercenary!</p>
<p>But Jason wasn’t a psychopath, and he wasn’t the sort of person to go out of his way to kill people without a good reason. That was why Jason sent the superhumans out to try and find out what the Jamani’s true intentions were! And from what the superhumans found, Jason realised that the Jamani had been orchestrating a kingdom takeover all this time.</p>
<p>The Jamani helped Makin to take the throne from his brother, but after the battle ended, the Jamani realised that there was no longer a working army in this world, and that meant there was no one to stop them from taking over L’horm! The Jamani had been secretly stealing all the gold and artefacts in the L’horm treasury, and if Jason hadn’t come when he did, then the Jamani would’ve plundered L’horm until there was nothing left! Now that Jason knew all this, Jason had enough reason to no longer show mercy! Jason was going to get rid of every single one of them!</p>
<p>’Azrael. Block all exits.’</p>
<p>Azrael appeared from Jason’s Alternate Dimension, and he raised his staff into the air. A massive rune sequence formed across the sky and glowed red, and a shield began to form over Azrael’s head and expand across the entire castle! Once it formed, the shield pulsed with a red light before solidifying into a stable structure that blocked anyone from passing through! This shield was a replica of the [Covenant Cube] shield that the Machina had used to trap Jason on Vratax when they tried to capture him with a group of criminals. Jason had studied that cube, and he worked with Azrael and Vana to create a high-level rune that had the same properties of allowing people to enter a certain area, but not allowing anyone to leave either physically or magically!</p>
<p>Jason put his hands behind his back as he floated down to the ground and landed in front of the large gates leading into the castle. Jason’s eyes glowed with a blue light, and all the living creatures inside of the castle were suddenly illuminated as blue figures against a black background! There were thousands of people in the castle, and more than half of them were Jamani mercenaries who were running quickly towards the back of the castle to try and escape in their airship! The mercenaries all felt Jason’s mana, and they also knew that one of their men had attacked a superhuman, so they knew that Jason was here to kill them!</p>
<p>Jason gave the Legion a simple order.</p>
<p>"Kill every mercenary you see."</p>
<p>The castle grounds pulsed with raw magic as thousands of soldiers appeared from Jason’s Alternate Dimension! All the soldiers were in the Bishop Class or higher, and the pressure in the air shot up even more as their mana mixed with Jason’s own! Jason couldn’t use the dragons or his giant beasts here since he did not want to cause any significant damage to the castle and the workers in it, so the only Legion members Jason called out were the humanoid members, like the Machina and the Bengari! They all rose from the grounds around Jason like demons from the shadow realm, and they drew their weapons and headed into the main castle!</p>
