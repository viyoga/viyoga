<p>Jason watched as Rosa took Riyala, and they both fell into the shadows. Once they were gone, Jason turned back to the fight, and he watched as some Iron Bladed Falcons created a distraction that allowed Gina to fire a wind-arrow into the eye of one of the dragons! The dragon recoiled from the attack, and that gave Azrael the opening to fire another blast of lightning towards the dragon’s wings to send it to the ground! Gina held on tighter as the Skyking performed a spinning manoeuvre in the air that allowed them to avoid an attack from one of the dragonriders! Gina looked behind her, and she grinned when she saw that some of her subordinates were coming from behind the dragonrider!</p>
<p>Gina told the Falcons to provide enough of a distraction, and the Falcons began flying past the dragonrider at high speeds! The dragon rider was falling through the air since his dragon was already gone, but he was still able to stabilize himself, and he swung his sword around him expertly as he tried to get the Falcons away from him! This particular dragonrider was only an A Rank, so he wasn’t as strong as some of the monsters in Jason’s Legion. But he was still a trained soldier, so he possessed enough skill and intelligence to attack the monsters in a way that would cause them the most damage! The dragonrider activated [Blazing Embers] and his sword erupted in fire as he grabbed hold of one of the Falcons and stabbed it in the neck! The dragonrider expected to see blood spraying, but he was shocked when the Falcon simply vanished from his hands!</p>
<p>’What sort of sorcery is this!?’</p>
<p>The dragonrider was shocked to the core, but he didn’t have time to analyze what had just happened as another Falcon slammed into him from the sky and forced him up into the air! The dragonrider spun around uncontrollably, and he held his sword tighter as he tried to get his feet back under him! He needed to get to the ground so he would have a stable foundation for his sword arts!</p>
<p>WOOSH!</p>
<p>The dragonrider heard the unmistakable sound of a projectile heading for him and he cursed since he knew that there was no way for him to defend in his current position! The dragon rider brought his hands up to try and protect his head, and he let out a shout as Gina’s wind arrow slammed into his back! More and more wind arrows followed the first one and continued to slam into him in a continuous volley and Gina relentlessly released more attacks as she pushed her magic to the very limit! Gina wanted to find out just how much stronger she had become! When she was only a C rank, Gina was capable of creating five arrows at the same time, and each one of them could move fast enough to tear through metal! But now that she was strong enough to be regarded as an A rank, her powers had also increased by leaps and bounds!</p>
<p>Gina created twenty-five arrows around her and sent them forward to slam into the dragonrider! The man slammed into the ground from the force as the arrows continued striking him and Gina created even more arrows that flew forward like a Gatling gun! The dradongirder couldn’t put up a defence because Gina wasn’t giving him any opening, and after a while, his body went limp as he just lay on the ground. Gina stopped after she was sure that he wasn’t going to get back up, and she sent one of the Falcons to see if he was still alive. The Iron Bladed Falcons went down and poked the dragonrider in the head with its beak, and after making sure that he was dead, the falcon flew off again. Gina looked at her hand as she let out a breath of exertion. Gina had fired close to two hundred arrows just then, and each of them was stronger than anything she had ever made before. With this much power, Gina knew that she could fight the boss of an Orange Gate all on her own!</p>
<p>’What in the world did Jason do to me? My mana hasn’t even gone below fifty percent.’</p>
<p>Gina felt a strange smile spreading on her lips when she realized how much more powerful she was, and she knew that she had every opportunity to get even more powerful in the future. Gina turned her attention towards another dragon that was still struggling against Azrael and she narrowed her eyes as she commanded the subordinates to attack! The Skyking let out a cry as it charged forward, and Jason felt a small grin appear on his face when he felt Gina’s excitement through their mental link. Gina didn’t show it often, but Jason could tell that she really enjoyed fighting a lot! She was like a fucking Amazon!</p>
