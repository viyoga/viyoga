<p>Azrael raised its magic staff from behind Hesabeth, and the sky began to darken with a magical attack! There was a giant of a man who had the features of the Dragonborn standing to the side, and Hesabeth watched as he pulled out his longsword and shot forward with multiple other swordsmen to attack Jason!</p>
<p>ROARRRR!!!</p>
<p>BZTTT!!</p>
<p>Up in the sky, hundreds of large dragons appeared from seemingly nowhere, and they unleashed their fire on Jason. At the same time, the dark clouds that Azrael had called on immediately sparked with electricity and unleashed a blast of lightning on Jason! And in the midst of all this, Jason didn’t even bother to move! Jason seemed completely unbothered as he waited with his hands in his pockets, and there was even a small smile on his face as he allowed the lightning and fire to slam into him!</p>
<p>BOOM!</p>
<p>A massive explosion went off as Jason tanked the explosion without any issue! Jason turned around and brought his hand up to catch a sword swing from the Sovereign, and the air split in half from the intensity of the swing! But Jason didn’t even flinch, and he just dodged out of the way as three more swings came from various directions! Jason jumped back as a loud screech suddenly echoed from above him, and no more than a second later, the Skill unleashed a sonic blast where Jason had just been!</p>
<p>SCREEECHHH!!</p>
<p>The blast destroyed the entire battlefield, and Jason could sense Arcticus pleading with him to let it come out from the Alternate Dimension and fight as well, but Jason denied it and told it to just wait in there. Hesabeth was out here, so if Arcticus came out and unleashed its powers, there was no doubt that it would hurt Hesabeth, and that would just draw the world’s attention again.</p>
<p>’Speaking of Hesabeth... where the hell did she go?’</p>
<p>Phwoom!</p>
<p>Jason didn’t have the time to find Hesabeth since he noticed a light shining on the horizon and realised that it was his Bishops lining up in a proper formation! The Bishops all raised their hands as they aimed at him, and in the next moment, they unleashed the [Mana Blast] skill! Jason looked down, and he saw large vines wrapping around his legs as his Dryad subordinate tried to hold him in place, and even though Jason knew that he could easily dodge this, he wanted to see just how much damage this much firepower could actually do to him! Jason’s mouth split into a grin as he simply stood there and waited for the attack to land!</p>
<p>KA-BOOOM!!</p>
<p>The entire world rumbled and shook from the impact, and a massive storm in the sky grew even more intense from the shockwave! Azrael raised his staff as he gathered enough mana for his most destructive attack because he knew for a fact that the King couldn’t be harmed by anything less, and Jason’s laugh echoed through the area as he reappeared from the massive dust cloud created by the Bishops’ attack! There wasn’t even a single injury on Jason’s body yet, and Azrael wasted no time as he activated a giant rune that had been carved into the ground!</p>
<p>DOON!</p>
<p>Jason looked down as the ground under him glowed with a bright purple light, and the sky suddenly rumbled with thunder as Azrael unleashed a TITAN level attack!</p>
<p>[Wrath of The Shattered Sky]!</p>
<p>TOON!</p>
<p>Multiple bright purple beams of light suddenly shot down from the sky and began to slam into the ground all around Jason like rain! Jason could feel an insane amount of power behind each one of those light beams, and Jason knew that any one of them would’ve been able to kill any TITAN Rank I easily! But Jason didn’t even seem to register the abilities, and he easily walked through the rain of pure energy without a care in the world! Ragnar appeared from the side and opened its mouth with a loud growl, and a magic circle formed in front of Ragnar’s open mouth before it sparked with a red flash and finally erupted in pure fire!</p>
<p>BOOM!</p>
<p>Ragnar’s attack shot straight towards Jason like a bullet of fire, and it slammed into Jason with enough force to eradicate a small world from existence! But the beams suddenly split down the middle as Jason easily deflected them, and they flew towards different parts of the world before they erupted in a massive fireball that sent shockwaves for miles in every direction! Trigon appeared, and he tried the same attack as Ragnar, and the multiple dragons in the sky also unleashed their attacks as they tried to cause even the tiniest harm to their King! But Jason just stood within the massive fireball from the combined destructive forces, and even in the midst of all this madness, the subordinates could still hear their king’s low laugh as he simply withstood the full brunt of their attacks!</p>
<p>Up in the sky, Hesabeth watched from on top of a dragon as Jason’s subordinates did everything in their power to try and hurt him, and Hesabeth could only smile and shake her head in disbelief as Jason’s laugh echoed through the area.</p>
<p>’I already had an idea of how powerful he was, but this is absurd. He’s a damn monster.’</p>
<p>Hesabeth couldn’t help but grin in excitement as she saw Jason tanking all of the attacks from his different subordinates like they were nothing. All this time, Hesabeth had been trying to find an opening to use the [Spear of Heaven] to launch an attack, but there were literally no openings at all in Jason’s stance! Even though Jason was just walking around with his hands in his pockets, Hesabeth’s instincts told her that the moment she put mana through this spear, Jason would know exactly where she was, and he would avoid it easily! Hesabeth frowned as she saw Jason reach to the side and grab a plain-looking sword from his Alternate Dimension. The sword looked similar to what she had seen the dragonborn soldiers of Radagan using in the past, and Hesabeth wondered what in the world Jason was planning to do with that.</p>
<p>Jason suddenly turned around, and in one smooth move, he launched the sword right at her! Hesabeth’s eyes widened in shock! This bastard!</p>
<p>"Move!"</p>
<p>ROARRR!</p>
<p>The dragon under Hesabeth immediately swerved to the side to avoid the sword, and Hesabeth wasted no time as she commanded the dragon to unleash its most dangerous attack! The dragon roared as it released a blast of fire towards Jason, and Hesabeth pulled her hand back and poured all the mana she could spare into the spear! Hesabeth thought that Jason would at least try to move out of the way, but Jason didn’t even seem bothered by the fact that she was about to attack him, and for some reason, this only pissed Hesabeth off!</p>
<p>’See if you can smile after this!’</p>
<p>KA-TOOOM!!</p>
<p>Hesabeth launched the spear like a javelin, and the spear immediately transformed into a beam of light that shot forward like the judgment of heaven! Jason felt his heart skip a beat as the spear approached him, and Jason realised in that moment that there was more power in that spear than any SS Rank should be able to produce!</p>
<p>’Did the world add extra power to her throw? Honestly, no matter how many times I see it, her Bias is just too overpowered.’</p>
<p>Jason realised that he would actually have to block this particular attack since the world had made it strong enough to actually hurt him, and Jason brought one hand out from his pocket and put it forward while unleashing a stream of magic that surrounded him like a shield!</p>
<p>BOOM!</p>
<p>The spear slammed into the mana shield with immeasurable force, and the spear began to shake as it tried to tear through the shield!</p>
