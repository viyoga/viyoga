<p>In the chapel located at the center of the mansion, Katarina knelt before the crucifix with her hands joined in prayer. She had heard the sounds of fighting and battle echoing through the mansion, and she could tell that her subordinates had been clashing against the Enforcer, but the loud echoes had stopped a few minutes ago and now there was nothing but silence! Anyone else would think that this meant the fighting was finally over, but Katarina knew better! Even though there were no more explosions, the battle still raged on!</p>
<p>Katarina sent out her mana towards all her subordinates, and she was using every bit of power she had to support them and ensure they gained as much of a power boost as possible. But even with her support, she could feel the lives of her subordinates being snuffed out one by one! It was happening so quickly, and Katarine couldn’t help but feel saddened about the number of people that she was sacrificing just to extend her life for as long as possible. Was this really worth it? She always knew a day like this would come, so why was she fighting the inevitable end? Why was she still afraid to face the promised King?</p>
<p>No, Katarina couldn’t die yet. She had done too much and come too far to stop now! Katarina made a lot of mistakes in her life, and she knew that no amount of apology would fix the mistakes she made. She destroyed the minds and lives of so many people just for her own selfishness. She had even taken her brother and made him into a puppet that lived only to do her bidding, just to ensure that she lived for a moment longer. This wasn’t something you could simply apologise for. Katarina had no choice but to see this through to the end.</p>
<p>Katarina could feel through her magic that her brother still lived. She knew the magic signatures of every single person she supported, so she was sure that the brother was somewhere out there! And Katarina also knew that he was likely creating a plan to escape so he could come and save her from this mess. That was what she had programmed him to do, after all.</p>
<p>Katarina felt a deep sadness fill her heart when she had these thoughts and she stood up slowly as she turned around with a more serious expression! Behind her were five women dressed as nuns. They were all members of Katarina’s guild, and they possessed enough power to be regarded as A Ranks. These five nuns were the subordinates whom Katarine had blessed the most out of all her guild members. With the amount of boosts she had given them, they were powerful enough to even take on low-level S Ranks! And since none of them had any regard for their own lives due to the fact that Katarina’s [Mana Shots] had molded them into loyal slaves just like Tobias, the nuns were willing to lay down thier lives to ensure Katarina was safe.</p>
