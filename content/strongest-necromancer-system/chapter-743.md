<p>[The World of Braseal.]</p>
<p>[Two Dimensional Leaps Away from Eden.]</p>
<p>[Sixth Branch of the Great Holy Eden Magic Academy.]</p>
<p>Within the Sixth Branch of the Eden Magic Academy, a tall man wearing a pure white three-piece suit walked calmly through the hallways of the Academy with a calm look on his face. The man had pure white hair that reached all the way down to his lower back, and his eyes were a bright blue that seemed to pierce straight through everything around him. Behind him, he had two large white wings that grew from his lower back and folded behind him beautifully, and above his head was a golden halo that shone with a brilliant light. This man was named Mik’hael and he was a Seraphim of Eden, as well as one of the highest authorities in the second Domain. Beside Mik’hael, a beautiful woman with the same angelic features walked with her hands held in front of her. The woman did not have the same halo that Mik’hael had, and her wings were significantly smaller than Mik’hael’s wings to show that she was of a lower class of Angel, but her hair was pure white, and she carried herself with a grace that would put her above anyone else who dared to stand beside her! The woman had a black cloth tied around her eyes, but she moved around with precision and ease to show that the blindfold didn’t inhibit her in any way.</p>
<p>This woman was named Jaeral and she was Mik’hael’s personal assistant as well as one of the Cherubim of Eden. As the two Angels walked through the halls of the Academy, they passed many researchers and students, and Mik’hael gave smiles and waves to the shocked students who all hurried to bow to him respectfully! The students in this academy were all from different races in this world, but the fact that they were here meant that their skill and intelligence in the art of magic had been recognized by the school board! The students all wore long black robes that had the symbol of the Eden Magic Academy – a pair of angel wings on either side of a golden wand – sewn into the chest. Along with the robes, the students also wore scarves of four different colors that showed the different departments within the Magic Academy. There was red for the Department of Magical Combat, green for the Department of Magical Artefact Research, blue for the Department of Magical Beast Research, and finally yellow for the Department of Runic Arts. This Academy was one of the many branch Academies that had been set up by Mik’hael’s sister and the current Overlord of this domain, Rak’hael, as a way to ensure that all the worlds in her domain were well educated in the use of magic.</p>
<p>Currently, there were more than two hundred branches of the Eden Magical Academy all across the second domain, and Mik’hael couldn’t believe that Rak’hael was somehow managing to take care of all two hundred branches by herself! It was an impossible task, but Mik’hael knew that if there was anyone who could handle something like this, then it was his sister!</p>
<p>The two Angels continued on their way through the Academy, and Mik’hael spotted a few students who had been enrolled recently. There were quite a lot of anomalies this year, and there were one or two that Mik’hael could see a lot of potential in! But Mik’hael just smiled as he continued on his way. Only time would tell if those students made use of that potential or not! If they managed to tap into their potential and grow into the mages that Mik’hael knew that they could become, then there was a chance they could join the upper ranks of the Academy as Lead Researchers or Beast Hunters, and he would get a chance to meet them again.</p>
<p>’Ah, who am I kidding...’</p>
<p>All of a sudden, Jaeral turned in surprise as she sensed Mik’hael moving away from her and heading towards the group of newly enrolled students, and Jaeral let out a tired sigh as Mik’hael suddenly stopped the students with a smile! The shorter children all looked up at Mik’hael with wonder in their eyes, and many of them bowed to him immediately since they recognized his rank! But Mik’hael just waved all of that away, and he leaned down to the two students whom he had seen the most potential in! Mik’hael gave the little boy and girl a wide smile and began to speak to them.</p>
<p>"You two have quite a lot of potential, so listen closely. You boy, you have chaotic mana, and that’s a good fit for the combat class. Make sure you ignore the other departments. The fire and lightning attributes will mesh well with your mana, so use those as your primary and take a secondary from one of the other elements. You girl, you have calm mana, and that means you’ll make a good runecrafter since it’s easier to feed your mana into runes..."</p>
<p>The two young children were looking up at Mik’hael like he had grown a second head, and Jaeral sighed as she waited for her master to finish his instructions. This was just the sort of person Mik’hael was. When Mik’hael saw an opportunity to help powerful people realise their potential, he didn’t hesitate to help them out. Mik’hael was an absolute busy-body, and even though Rak’hael had warned Mik’hael about talking to the students like this many times because it always caused a big scene, Mik’hael just never listened! Jaeral waited for a few more seconds for Mik’hael to give a few more instructions before she finally cleared her throat to let Mik’hael know that they had to leave!</p>
<p>"Your Majesty, we must be on our way."</p>
