<p>[The Upper Districts of Riverdale.]</p>
<p>The Riverdale landscape was very similar to that of many other cities in America. Although there was no rampant poverty in the city, there was still a great divide that showed where the higher and lower-class civilians could be found, and that divide created the two distinct upper and lower districts of Riverdale. The lower district was where the less privileged members of the city lived. Here, you would see both the middle class, who usually rented or bought small apartments, as well as the lower class, who were too poor to stay in apartments and were either living in shared shelters or actively carrying out criminal activities in order to survive. The crime rate in the city had been very low before the discovery of Gifts and Magical Beasts, but ever since that first Gate appeared and superhumans began selling Magical Crystals and Beast corpses to the government for fortunes, there had been a great increase in the prices of houses, food, and other amenities, and this had led to many people in the lower class having to resort to criminal activities to survive.</p>
<p>On the other side of the city was the upper district, and this was where the rich and powerful members of the city lived. In this district, there were hardly any apartment buildings. Instead, it was filled with various estates that housed very wealthy people, as well as high-end restaurants and large skyscrapers that formed a large cluster of buildings in the eastern part of the city. This cluster of buildings was seen as the most economically important part of Riverdale because it contained every single major office building for the businesses that ran the city! This was the Riverdale Business Sector!</p>
<p>Just a few months ago, there had only been about thirty or so businesses that were involved in the Riverdale economic market since Riverdale hadn’t been a crucial part of the country’s economy, but over the past few days, there had been a recent boom in the number of businesses that were coming into the city! There were new skyscrapers being put up, and the office spaces were being sold off at such an insane rate that the prices had tripled in less than a week due to the increase in demand! It was as though every single businessman suddenly wanted to become a part of Riverdale’s growing economy, and the reason for this was simple.</p>
<p>The Monarch Guild had chosen Riverdale as their base of operations.</p>
<p>In one of the large skyscrapers in the middle of the business sector, Juliet Rahpa stood at the window and looked down into the city. There were dark circles around her eyes, and she looked very tired, but she couldn’t get rid of the smile on her face. Juliet was currently standing inside a large office that had a very minimalistic design. The office walls were a pure white, and the floors were tiled with a dull grey. There were couches and a TV on one side of the office, and a large mahogany table with a high-end swivel chair that could turn around to view the city skyline through the large window.</p>
<p>This office was what Juliet had chosen for Jason since she knew that he wouldn’t want something flashy or fancy, and since Juliet was Jason’s personal assistant, she would also use this office when Jason wasn’t around. Juliet had been working hard for the past few days to try to make big changes in the way the world saw the Monarch Guild, and she was happy to see how well things were working out! After the horrible interview that Griselda had with the press earlier, Juliet thought that things would become more difficult since people would think that Griselda wasn’t fit to run the guild, but it turned out that she was wrong! Rather than things becoming worse, it was almost like no one even cared about the interview at all, and things had only gotten better for the guild! Right now, the Monarch guild was growing faster and pulling in more attention than any other guild in the world!</p>
