<p>An intense force slammed into Jason as Agiel’s skill eradicated everything around them! But even after all this, Jason still didn’t move a single inch, and the fighting spirit felt her eyes widen as Jason suddenly lashed out and grabbed her by the face before tossing her into the air!</p>
<p>WOOSH!</p>
<p>CRASH! CRASH!</p>
<p>BOOM!</p>
<p>The spirit slammed through three buildings like a bullet and let out a scream as she crashed into a skyscraper! She quickly tried to get up, but Jason appeared from the air with his hand pulled back and unleashed a punch that slammed straight into her stomach!</p>
<p>BOOM!</p>
<p>The punch completely rocked Agiel’s world, and Agiel’s mouth opened in shock as she was sent back into the wall like a rocket!</p>
<p>BOOM!</p>
<p>The entire building exploded like someone had just detonated a bomb inside of it, and it came down like a house of cards right on top of Agiel and Jason! The pile of rock and stone began to shake as a strange pressure erupted under it, and in the next moment Jason rocketed out from under the rocks as he was blasted through the air by Agiel! Agiel flashed after Jason with a massive grin on her face, and she pulled her hand back and unleashed a punch straight towards Jason!</p>
<p>[Rupture]!</p>
<p>The air in front of Agiel immediately went through a destructive interference, and a beam of pure energy slammed into Jason! And since Jason was flying through the air, he didn’t have any way to block the attack, and he was immediately sent flying down to the ground like a rocket! Jason crashed into a cluster of buildings and completely destroyed every single one of them at once! The entire region exploded in dust and rocks as skyscrapers fell on top of one another, but Agiel didn’t have any time to rest as she saw a massive truck fly out from the dust!</p>
<p>WOOSH!</p>
<p>Agiel quickly created an air platform that she used to dodge to the side and avoid the truck, but all of a sudden, more and more cars were being thrown out from the dust like rockets, and Agiel had to speed up drastically to try and avoid all of them! But it was impossible to avoid them all, and Agiel had to eventually create a platform above her that she used to shoot herself back down to the ground!</p>
<p>BOOM!</p>
<p>Agiel slammed into the ground in a crouch before rising back up to her feet, and she looked around the deserted streets with a critical gaze while waiting for something to happen!</p>
<p>"On your left."</p>
<p>Agiel’s eyes widened, and she turned to her left quickly, only for her to suddenly be blindsided as Jason swung a car towards her like a baseball bat!</p>
<p>BOOM!</p>
<p>Agiel was sent flying through the air, and Jason didn’t give her any time to rest! Jason flew after her and used the same car to slam her into the ground, and then he threw the car down at her and followed after it! Agiel slammed into the ground with a grunt, and the car slammed into her before Jason tore through the car and slammed his fist into Agiel’s chest!</p>
<p>BOOM!</p>
<p>Agiel was nearly split in half from that punch, and a mad scream echoed through the city as red-hot pain tore through Agiel’s body! But Agiel wasn’t done yet! Agiel powered through the pain, grit her teeth, and swung her leg up!</p>
<p>[Rupture]!</p>
<p>Agiel unleashed a sonic blast with her feet that sent Jason flying back, and Agiel blasted off the ground and flew after him! Agiel slammed her fist into Jason’s chin, and Jason tanked it easily and slammed his own fist into her face! Agiel saw stars from the punch, but she didn’t dare to retreat! Agiel charged forward and started trying to trade blows with Jason! She would swing at him using her superhuman strength as well as her [Rupture], and Jason would swing back with even more force! Agiel’s entire face was battered and bruised, and she was bleeding from everywhere! But even in the midst of all this pain and suffering, Agiel was still grinning! She had the happiest look on her face as she traded blows with Jason, and Agiel felt more alive in this one moment than she had in her entire life.</p>
<p>And somehow, without even noticing it himself, Jason had also gained a grin on his face. Jason slammed his fist into Agiel’s face and sent her flying to the ground, and he felt a thrill of enjoyment going through him as Agiel charged straight back at him and slammed her feet into his stomach! Jason was thrown back into a building, and he had to immediately move out of the way as Agiel grabbed and threw a skyscraper towards him! The entire building crashed down like a meteor, throwing dust and smoke everywhere, but Jason was already on the move again!</p>
<p>Jason flew up into the air, and he decided that it was time for him to stop playing around! Jason raised his hand and activated [Flash Freeze], and the entire world around Jason was immediately encased in a wave of pure ice! The ice travelled straight towards Agiel, but Agiel only grinned as she put her hand forward! Agiel had already learned how to match the frequency of elemental magic during her fight against the dragons months ago, and after that fight, Agiel was able to master how to match the frequency of all the other elements as well! So the moment the ice tried to reach her, Agiel slammed her palm into it and activated her skill!</p>
<p>[Rupture]!</p>
<p>All the ice in front of Agiel was immediately destroyed at the molecular level, and it turned into billions of shiny blue dust particles that drifted through the air! Jason’s grin widened even more when he saw this, and he put his hands to the side as he took this as a challenge! Jason tapped into multiple skills within his arsenal and spoke in a calm voice.</p>
<p>"Try your best to survive this. I’ll do my best to hold back, but I can’t guarantee anything."</p>
<p>Agiel narrowed her eyes at Jason, and she was about to say something, but at the next moment, the sky opened up, and millions of massive arrows began to fall upon the earth like the barrage of a heavenly army!</p>
<p>[Wind Arrow]!</p>
<p>Jason poured his Primordial Mana into the skill, and he was able to break the skill and turn a simple A Rank skill into a force of nature! The arrows fell upon the earth like millions of battering rams, and Agiel immediately began to move! Agiel ran through the streets and jumped through buildings as she tried her best to avoid the attacks! There were an uncountable number of arrows, but Agiel was just one person, so she knew that it would be hard for Jason to land an attack as long as she kept moving! Agiel lashed out and used her skill to destroy a few of the arrows that tried to hit her, and she jumped into a skyscraper and hid behind some columns to catch her breath!</p>
<p>BOOM!</p>
<p>An arrow immediately shot through the air and slammed into the skyscraper like a ballistic missile! The building erupted in dust and stone, and Agiel felt her lips split into a wider grin as she hurried out to continue the fight!</p>
<p>[Heart of the Ocean]!</p>
<p>Agiel felt the air pressure drop significantly, and she looked up in shock as the sky darkened and it began to rain! For a moment, Agiel wondered what the hell Jason was trying to do, but then she saw a massive shadow falling over her, and she raised her head to see that Jason had created a towering tsunami from the water that was heading right towards her! A deep, intense surge of amusement rose from somewhere inside Agiel as she looked up at the wall of water! With power like this, how could Jason be anything but a god in human flesh?</p>
<p>Agiel planted her feet as her smile grew even wider! Who cared if Jason was a god!? Agiel had never backed down from a fight, and she wasn’t going to start now! Agiel put her hand out towards the water and activated her skill!</p>
<p>[Rupture]!</p>
<p>BOOM!</p>
<p>The water was immediately torn asunder as Agiel matched its frequency with her mana! The intense pressure threw Agiel back, but her Rupture skill worked quickly, and she managed to destroy the water before it could cause too much damage! Agiel landed on the ground in a wet heap and tried to catch her breath, but Jason wasn’t going to give her any time to recover! Jason immediately activated another skill!</p>
<p>[Lightning Bomb]!</p>
<p>This time, it was a PRIMORDIAL Ranked skill, and the entire city lit up in pure white light as lightning sparked across the air and began to form a massive ball in the sky!</p>
