<p>[The Ashenvalley Habitat of the Dragons and Wyverns]</p>
<p>The Ashenvalley was a large expanse of land that had been claimed by the dragons and wyverns more than a millennium ago. No one knew where the dragons came from, and they didn’t know how long they had really been in this world for, but there was a folklore that was told to every dragonborn child the moment they were old enough to understand spoken words. According to this folklore, the Ashenvalley was more than just the resting place of the dragons; it was a mystical land that the dragon gods had blessed thousands of years ago. The first dragon god had arrived in this world as a single being, and from its fire, it created a large valley that cut across the entire western peninsula of Radagan. This dragonfire didn’t leave the land burnt or scorched; instead, it brought natural healing and growth to the land, and the land began to grow and prosper as a result of it. The dragon god took the mud and sand that it had created using its fire, and from that mud, it crafted the very first dragon. This dragon was known as Rumunaga, and it was the oldest and wisest of all the dragons.</p>
<p>Rumunaga was given the wisdom of the gods and blessed with the eternal flames, and with those flames, Rumunaga ruled over the dragons that existed in the Ashenvalley and brought about an age of prosperity for them. But it wasn’t long before the dragon god introduced a new creature to the Ashenvalley. This creature was different from the dragons. It stood on two legs, and it had no hair on its body. The dragons called this creature a Tumani, and they treated it as a companion since they had all come from the dragon gods. But the dragons noticed that the Tumani were weak creatures. They did not have any scales to protect them from attacks, and they did not have fire to hunt. They were creatures that would not survive in the wild! So rather than sending them out to hunt, the Tumani became the scholars and workers in the Ashenvalley. Because although they were lacking in strength and fire, they more than made up for it in intelligence.</p>
<p>Life was good and peaceful between the dragons and the Tumani for a long time, and it would have continued in this way for the rest of time. But the dragons and the Tumani fell out of favour with each other when a Tumani made the mistake of falling in love with the daughter of the dragon king, Rumunaga. The moment Rumunaga heard of this sacrilegious attraction, he grew angry and immediately ordered the execution of the Tumani! But what Rumanga did not expect was that his daughter had also fallen in love with the Tumani, and once she heard about what her father was going to do, she rushed to warn her beloved of the impending death, and together they both vanished.</p>
