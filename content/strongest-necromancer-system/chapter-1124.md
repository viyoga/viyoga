<p>Enkidu knew that doing this much was necessary to make sure that Jason was caught off guard! It had only been a few minutes since the Court of Apostles learned of Jason’s actions here. Sicario and Enkidu had already been preparing to come to Tarisavia and get Theosis so that they could begin the second part of their plan, but then Bathseda had come to their headquarters and told them that Theosis was under attack. Sicario immediately mobilised their forces to come help Theosis, and Sicario sent Enkidu ahead to hold Jason off until the rest of them could arrive. They still had use for Theosis, so they couldn’t let Jason kill him yet!</p>
<p>Enkidu continued to maintain the structure, but meanwhile, inside the mansion, Jason had already gotten his footing back, and he immediately created a black platform under his feet and blasted forward as he headed towards Theosis again!</p>
<p>A Dimensional Tear opened in front of Jason, and a woman jumped out of it and put her hand forward.</p>
<p>[Reality Shaping]!</p>
<p>The air in front of Jason immediately opened up like someone had ripped the sky apart, and Jason was sent falling through the hole! Jason shot out of another hole in the sky high above the mansion, and Jason looked around with a shocked frown as the sky closed back up with clouds! Ulysses, the Apostle of Reality, turned sharply towards Theosis’ body and narrowed her eyes in annoyance as she activated another of her Arcane Arts!</p>
<p>[Reality Distortion]!</p>
<p>’I can’t believe I’m actually trying to save this pathetic bastard.’</p>
<p>Ulysses was obviously not happy at the moment, but there was nothing Ulysses could do about this situation! Sicario had given them their orders, and they had no choice but to listen. The swords around Theosis immediately transformed into water and fell to the ground like a pool, and Ulysses was about to grab Theosis, but a sudden pressure slammed into Ulysses and froze her in place! Jason’s mana spread across the world rapidly, and Jason put his hand towards the mansion and unleashed a PRIMORDIAL Rank skill!</p>
<p>[Phantasmal Lightning]!</p>
<p>White lightning arced across the sky before striking down on the giant tree and destroying it! The lightning raced towards the mansion and completely tore through the structure, and the mansion crumbled to pieces and sent everyone inside falling through the sky! Jason sent out an order to his subordinates, and they immediately charged for Ulysses and Theosis!</p>
<p>ROARRRR!</p>
<p>The giant Phantasmal Lightning dragon immediately roared as it charged out from Jason’s Alternate Dimension, and Ulysses’ eyes widened in shock when she saw the sheer number of beasts that were coming towards her and Theosis! What in the world is this!? Does one man really have this much power!?</p>
<p>[Absolute Dominion of Nature]!</p>
<p>Enkidu put her hand up, and thousands of Dimensional Tears suddenly burst to life behind her! And from those Dimensional Tears poured out millions of creatures from SSS Ranks all the way up to the TITAN Ranks! Enkidu’s Arcane Art was specially crafted to give her an extremely close relationship with nature! She was the literal embodiment of all living things, and her second Attribute [Absolute Dominion of Nature] allowed Enkidu to summon creatures from all across the Primordial Expanse to come and aid her in battle!</p>
<p>The creatures charged towards the Legion subordinates, and they both clashed in the sky in a display of pure savagery! Just like the Legion subordinates who didn’t care for their lives, the animals that Enkidu had summoned were exactly the same! Neither army was bothered about death, and all they cared for was carrying out the instructions given to them by their masters, so there was no tactic or reason to their battle! All they wanted was to cause as much damage to the other side as possible!</p>
<p>ROARRR!</p>
<p>The Primordial Lightning Dragon let out a roar as it charged through hundreds of animals, but the creatures were relentless, and they dived towards the dragon’s eyes and wings to try and slow it down! None of Enkidu’s creatures were strong enough to stop any of the Legion subordinates since the creatures that Enkidu summoned were too weak, but it was enough to slow the Legion down, and this would give the Apostles time to escape!</p>
<p>Ulysses put her hand out and activated [Reality Shaping] before she reached out and grabbed the sky in front of her and dragged it forward like a curtain to cover her and Theosis! The two Apostles were immediately transported a far distance away, and Ulysses grabbed Theosis by the arm and shouted for Bethseda to open a Dimensional Tear, but Jason suddenly clapped!</p>
<p>Clap!</p>
<p>[Resonance Surge]!</p>
<p>Jason’s Resonance energy shot through the entire region like a wave of pure destruction, and both Ulysses and Theosis were thrown through the sky like ragdolls once the energy slammed into them! Ulysses managed to stop herself from flying back after a few seconds, and she grabbed her temple as she felt her head shaking in pain! Ulysses struggled to get herself back quickly, and she cursed as she saw that Theosis was falling! Ulysis immediately dived down to catch Theosis before he could crash into the ground!</p>
<p>WOOSH!</p>
<p>"!"</p>
<p>A feeling of pure dread suddenly came over Ulysses, and she instinctively activated [Reality Shaping] and transformed the air in front of her into a wall!</p>
<p>BOOM!</p>
<p>Jason’s fist slammed into the wall with enough force to cause the sky to shake! Jason had been planning to take Ulysses’ head off just now, and if that attack had hit, there was no doubt in Ulysses’ mind that she would already be dead! Ulysses activated [Reality Distortion], and she vanished from in front of Jason and appeared beside Theosis to grab him before he could hit the ground! Jason turned angrily to see Ulysses running away again, and Jason immediately activated a skill!</p>
<p>[Lightning Bomb]!</p>
<p>The PRIMORDIAL level skill came to life beside Jason, and everyone turned to look at Jason in shock when they felt the sheer power inside the attack! The bomb was strong enough to eradicate this continent from the map, and if Jason let it loose, then they were all going to die! Two Dimensional Tears immediately opened up in front of Ulysses and Enkidu for them to escape, but Jason had no intention of letting them leave!</p>
<p>[Land of Lost Shadows]!</p>
<p>Jason finally activated his own Arcane Art, and the aura wrapped around all four of them as they were quickly transported into the Alternate Dimension with the Lightning Bomb floating between them! This was Jason practically sentencing all of them to death, and with how quickly the shadow world was moving, both Ulysses and Enkidu knew that there was nothing that they could do to stop this! A feeling of dread came over the two women as they were being consumed by the dark shadow, and they tried despreately to think of a way out. But before the bomb could go off, a voice spoke up from the side.</p>
<p>"That’s quite enough."</p>
<p>The voice was calm and nonchalant, like someone who was not interested in fighting at all. Jason turned sharply when he heard that voice, and his eyes narrowed when he recognised the person immediately! Jason spoke up with a sneer!</p>
<p>"Sicario."</p>
<p>The leader of the Court of Apostles put his hand up and activated his own Arcane Art.</p>
<p>[Lord of Time]!</p>
<p>The entire world slowed to a crawl as a large holographic clock appeared behind Sicario’s back! The clock had been moving forward, but it slowed down gradually until it finally came to a halt, and everything around Sicario froze in place as well. And then the clock began to run in reverse! Everything and everyone around Sicario seemed to be moving at 2x speed as they retraced all of their steps backwards!</p>
