<p>After walking for another five minutes, Jason could feel his Qi reserves running out, and he frowned as he realised that he would have to turn off the Qi technique for a few minutes so he could recharge his Qi. But Jason also knew that the moment his technique was dispelled, every single monster around him would sense his mana. Jason’s mana was too powerful for him to suppress completely, so there was always residual mana leaking from him. Right now, Jason was only about five miles away from his destination, and Jason simply said fuck it! The beast would sense him once his Qi was gone, so why should he stand here and wait for the beast to come after him? Jason was going all out.</p>
<p>Jason made up his mind to turn off his camouflage, and he asked the system to point out the quickest route to the Dimensional Circle. The system immediately brought up a prompt!</p>
<p>[The user is currently within the territory of a dangerous TITAN-Rank beast. The system does not advice the user to attract the attention of the beast -]</p>
<p>’I know, just tell me how far away I am, dammit!’</p>
<p>Jason shut off the system’s warning immediately, and he crouched into a running stance! Jason already knew all the bad things that could happen, but he didn’t care! The heavy wind from the rising snowstorm slammed against Jason’s body, and Jason narrowed his eyes in concentration as he glared into the distance. The system immediately got rid of its warnings and brought up another tab.</p>
<p>[The user is five miles away from the location of the next Dimensional Tear. At his full speed, and taking the density of air and gravity into account, the system believes the user should take the North East route to reach the Dimensional Tear in exactly thirty seconds.]</p>
<p>’Alright, let’s go.’</p>
<p>Jason could feel his Qi finally fading away from his body, and the moment his presence concealment technique went down, it felt like a million eyes turned in his direction at the same time! Jason was like a beam of light standing in the centre of a dark room, and every single monster in this territory had turned towards him with predatory eyes!</p>
<p>ROARRRRR!!</p>
<p>BUUUOOOHHH!!</p>
<p>Jason felt the first strings of what felt like fear pulling at his heart, but Jason cut off the feeling immediately, and he pulsed his mana to full power! Jason’s body roared with magic, and his strength increased to unbelievable levels! The intense gravity that had been holding Jason down seemed to vanish, and the air parted for him and gave him a clear path forward! And in the next moment, Jason took off!</p>
<p>BOOM!</p>
<p>Jason was like a bullet shooting through the air with how fast he was going, and he covered more than two miles in less than ten seconds! At the eleventh second, Jason had to dodge into a roll as a massive tail slashed across the spot where his head had just been, but Jason didn’t bother to wait and see what sort of monster that was as he got up and continued running! Another large monster tried to tackle Jason to the ground, but Jason pulled out the [Death’s Mirage] and delivered a slash that cut the beast in two easily!</p>
<p>Slash!!</p>
<p>Ding! Ding! Ding!</p>
<p>Jason got multiple notifications in his system after killing the monster, but he was moving so quickly at that moment that he didn’t have the time to look at them! All Jason knew was that he had suddenly gained a sudden increase in power, and he could feel the soul of the beast he just killed connecting to him, so he knew he gained it as a subordinate. Jason sent a mental command to the monster to buy him time, and Jason felt the beast release a loud screech as it took off to meet with the Beast of the Apocalypse.</p>
<p>[The user is half a mile away from the Dimensional Tear. To open the gateway, the user must release 25,000 Magicules of mana in the specified location.]</p>
<p>SCREECH!</p>
<p>BOOM!</p>
<p>[The user’s subordinate [Skrill] has been killed]</p>
<p>’Isn’t that the new beast I just resurrected?’</p>
<p>Jason felt his eyes widen when he realised that his newest subordinate was already dead! From what Jason felt from that subordinate, he could tell that it was at least a low-level TITAN, so the fact that it was killed in almost no time at all was shocking! But Jason didn’t bother trying to resurrect the beast since he was already at the Dimensional Tear, and he released a loud pulse of mana and watched as the space in front of him tore open into a Dimensional Tear! And as Jason jumped through the tear to reach the next world, he could hear the furious roar of the Beast following behind him.</p>
<p>BUUUOOOHHH!!!</p>
<p>——</p>
<p>The user has killed [1] TITAN Rank I Skrill</p>
<p>+30,000 Kill Points</p>
<p>——</p>
<p>[Remote Resurrection] has been activated!</p>
<p>——</p>
<p>The user has gained [1] Bishop Rank II subordinate</p>
<p>+250,000 MP</p>
<p>+8% Strength</p>
<p>+10% Stamina</p>
<p>+5% Durability</p>
<p>+5% Agility</p>
<p>The user has gained the SSS Rank skill [Skrill Scream]</p>
<p>——</p>
<p>[Skrill Scream: This skill allows the user to unleash a loud sonic scream that disorients enemies and provides a debuff of [1] Rank to all defensive artefacts and skills below TITAN Rank.]</p>
<p>This skill can be evolved.</p>
<p>——</p>
<p>[The world of Ja’ral]</p>
<p>[Rank III]</p>
<p>Jason appeared from the other side of the Dimensional Circle, and he cursed as he felt himself falling through the air before splashing into a large ocean below! Jason emerged from the water, and he rose above the ocean on a dark shadow disk and took a look around him. The ocean stretched out as far as the eye could see in every direction. Even with his advanced vision, Jason couldn’t see any land, and he couldn’t help but wonder if this was a completely aquatic world.</p>
<p>[The world of Ja-ral is home to various aquatic species, but it still possesses various land-based creatures that live on different islands around the world. The location of the final Dimensional Tear is thirty-seven miles south of the user’s current location.]</p>
<p>Jason read through the message quickly before he immediately turned and began to move. Jason already knew that the beast was being held back by the other monsters in Gerani, but it would only be a matter of time before the beast defeats them and comes after him again. Jason called on various Rook Rank subordinates, and he told them to spread out across the world and go ahead of him to find the Dimensional Tear. Meanwhile, Jason called on Ragnar, and the large dragon flew out from the Alternate Dimension and landed on a large black shadow platform. Jason got on its back before commanding it to take off, and Ragnar let out a deep roar before he shot off into the sky.</p>
<p>This time, Jason managed to travel for close to ten minutes without any incidents. This world was Rank III, so there wasn’t anything here that could cause Jason any harm, and Jason just tried to put as much distance between himself and the Beast as possible! But it wasn’t long before Jason felt the unmistakable pressure from the Beast pressing down on him, and with a loud bellow, the Beast of the Apocalypse appeared in the world of Ja’ral. Jason immediately felt the intense chill from the beast’s power wash over him, and he let out a cold breath as a snowstorm began to gather in the sky. The large ocean froze rapidly, and Jason felt a stab of guilt in his heart as he realised that everything that lived inside this ocean would likely die from the freezing cold. But Jason didn’t have time to worry about that, and he urged Ragnar to go faster when he felt the beast’s mana approaching from behind.</p>
<p>Jason sent a message out to his other subordinates and asked them how far they had gone, and he grinned when a subordinate sent back a message that it had reached its destination. Jason immediately told Ragnar to stop, and Jason came down and stood on a black shadow platform before telling Ragnar to return to his Alternate Dimension.</p>
