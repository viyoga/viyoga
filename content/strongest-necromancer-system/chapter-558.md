<p>All of a sudden, loud, piercing screeches began to travel towards them from all over the world! Freya’s sensitive ears could pick up high-pitched sounds from miles away, and it was easy for her to hear the screeches coming from all over the place! The rabbits in this town had agitated the rabbits in various other places, and they were all suddenly screeching at the same time! Freya had no idea what these rabbits were screeching for, but she decided that it couldn’t be anything good, and she quickly put her hand out and shouted out to her men while activating her cannon blaster!</p>
<p>"Kill them! Kill them all!"</p>
<p>The Machina opened fire on the rabbits and began to kill them, but their action came a little too late. The rabbits in their town had already agitated the rest of the rabbits all across the world, and the screeching only grew louder and louder as more rabbits joined in on the circus!</p>
<p>All the way on the other side of the world, the Machina Generals, who had been trying to circle around the town and reach Jason, also felt shocked when they noticed all the rabbits around them screeching loudly! The soldiers were already only a few miles away from Jason, but they were forced to stop and look around in shock from the sudden screeching! What the hell was that!?</p>
<p>[Sir! What should we do?]</p>
<p>One of the soldiers in the air carriers shouted to the general for his orders, and the General immediately told them to fire at the rabbits! If the rabbits continued screeching, then they would announce their presence to Jason! The air carrier began to fire at the rabbits, but there was no way they could kill the thousands of rabbits that occupied this world, and the screeching only grew louder as more and more rabbits joined in!</p>
<p>Jason turned around in shock when he heard the loud screeching coming from the town behind him! What the fuck was going on back there!? Jason knew that the rabbits would only scream if they had found intruders in this world, so that could only mean that they had seen something! And Jason didn’t need to think for long before he realised that it was the Machina! Jason squinted against the snow, and he could just barely make out the shape of the Machina air carriers as well as the intense blue light from their cannon blasters as they fired at the rabbits! Those fucking idiots! What the fuck did they think they were doing!?</p>
<p>Jason frowned in irritation as he heard more and more rabbits screeching from all around him, and Jason turned around sharply when he heard an intense screech coming from behind him! Thankfully, it was just a single rabbit, and Jason easily killed it with a sword to the head before he decided that he was going to try and get some distance between him and the Machina! From the memories Jason got from his rabbit subordinate, the Beast of the Apocalypse would always appear close to where the screeching was coming from, so as long as Jason wasn’t close to the Machina, he was sure that he wouldn’t run into the beast! In fact, Jason might be able to get off this world before the Beast even knows he is here!</p>
<p>DOOON!!!</p>
<p>But Jason had to stop and find his footing as the entire world suddenly shook with an intense tremor! A fierce vibration travelled across the world, and Jason felt a sudden surge of power press down on everything in the area! The ice began to crack and splinter from the force, and for the first time in his life, Jason felt a grunt escape him from the intense power pressing down on him! The moment Jason felt that power, he realised that this wasn’t some random monster! This wasn’t a power that any regular beast could release! It was the power of a TITAN! It had to be the Beast of the Apocalypse! This meant that the Beast had been attracted by the screeches of the rabbits, and it was coming to this world! Another intense rumble echoed through the ground, and Jason saw a massive Dimensional Tear opening in front of him! The Dimensional Tear was almost a kilometre away from Jason’s location, but it was so large that it looked like it was right in front of Jason! And from within that Dimensional Tear came a storm that nearly threw Jason off his feet!</p>
<p>WOOOOSSSHHH!</p>
<p>An intense wind spread out over the world as a snowstorm raged to life! Jason put his hand up to his face as the winds suddenly tripled in intensity! Snow and ice began to shoot through the air as the wind grew into a massive typhoon, and Jason was shocked when the intense winds forced him to take a step back! Jason knew just how powerful he was, and he was certain that there weren’t a lot of storms that would be able to make him move at all! So the fact that this storm was literally threatening to take him off his feet was unbelievable! If Jason was experiencing something like this, then he could only imagine what the Machina soldiers were going through!</p>
<p>And Jason was absolutely right about what was about to happen! The intense storm from the Dimensional Tear lashed across the world at such a rapid pace that no one was able to even put up a mediocre defence before they were hit by the intense wind! The air carriers that the Machina had been using to scan the world were the first to experience the wind, and the moment the metal constructs were slammed into by the ice cold typhoon, they were thrown back like ragdolls! The pilots screamed as their aircraft was thrown about and they tried to control it desperately! But the air had already grown ice-cold, and the air carrier engines began to freeze over!</p>
<p>[We’re going down! We’re going down!]</p>
<p>A Machina pilot screamed at the top of his lungs, and at the next moment, the carrier slammed into the ground and immediately exploded in a massive fire that was quickly snuffed out by the cold wind! On the ground, the General and the rest of the soldiers were trying their best not to be pushed back by the wind, but none of them stood a chance! The wind had literally pushed Jason back a single step, and he was a TITAN with enough power to withstand unbelievable force, so the S and SS-Rank Machina didn’t even stand a chance!</p>
<p>"AHHHH!!!"</p>
<p>"I can’t hold on!"</p>
<p>"Use the grappling hooks to hold down!"</p>
<p>"The hooks are frozen! I- AHHHHHH!"</p>
<p>Before the Machina could continue screaming, their entire bodies began to freeze over and the General could only watch in shock as his men were transformed into silver statues that were eternally stuck with looks of shock on their faces! The frozen Machina soldiers were lifted into the air and carried away by the intense wind, and if the General had been in another situation, then he might have tried his hardest to save some of his men! But at that moment, the General was also trying his best to fight for his life! He had never felt winds like this before! The General’s feet were planted to the ground with all his strength, and he was crouching low and gripping the ground so that the winds wouldn’t carry him away! There was already ice gathering on his face, and his mechanical arms were creaking as they were slowly weakened by the intense cold! The General was an SSS-Rank, and even he wasn’t immune to the sheer power of this typhoon! He felt like the moment he released his grip even a little, he was going to die! Far off in the distance, the General could see the blue highlight of the Dimensional Tear that had opened, and he knew without a doubt that that Dimensional Tear was the reason behind all this! What in the world did they unleash upon themselves!?</p>
