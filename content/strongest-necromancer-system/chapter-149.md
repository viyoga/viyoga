<p>’I’m tired of living in the shadows! I need to get away from this bastard!’</p>

<p>"We will now be heading in for your assessment, and you will be divided into groups according to a predetermined standard. Please, try to remain quiet and orderly in the building. <p>Naomi turned to give Jason a hard look when she finished talking, and she narrowed her eyes at him when she saw him shrug! Jason’s arrival here had already made a bad impression on her, and from the way he was dressed, Naomi could tell that Jason was one of those rowdy bad-boy types who liked to strut around and cause issues for people with their very presence! Naomi already knew they were going to have issues with him before the assessment even started, so she would definitely keep her eyes on him!</p>
<p>The teenagers were murmuring and whispering when they heard Naomi say that there were going to be Enforcers in the building! Even though they were new to the superhuman world, the teenagers had also heard about the infamous Enforcers and the insane level of skill that was needed in order to become one of them! Other than this assessment, there was another separate assessment that the students had been told about. That assessment was far more difficult than this one and was meant for those superhumans who wanted to take a step higher in the superhuman world and become Enforcers.</p>
<p>Only one in a hundred superhumans ever pass that test and become Enforcers, but once you pass it, there was no doubt that you would become one of the Elites!</p>
<p>Naomi cleared her throat at the students, and their whispering finally calmed down as they all turned to her attentively. She looked over all of them and smiled slightly when she saw the young, naive, eager faces, but her smile died a brutal death when she turned to Jason’s mature, bored look. Naomi frowned at him before shaking her head and turning around.</p>
<p>"Come on then, children. It is time for your assessment."</p>
<p>The doors leading into the building opened, and the teenagers followed after Naomi like ducklings following their mother. As they walked through the pristine-looking SMD building, the children couldn’t hide their awe at how enormous it was! The grand-looking hall and the numerous SMD agents and Enforcers walking around drew their attention, and they became more and more excited the further they walked!</p>
<p>Jason, on the other hand, was bored. He had already been here before, so Jason didn’t have any interest in looking around. <p>Jason scoffed and turned away, but his eyes immediately caught the eyes of another woman who was looking back at him while walking away. She was swinging her hips seductively, and Jason raised a brow while watching her go. Not bad.</p>
<p>The employees in the SMD building all recognized Jason from when he came here the last time, as well as from all the online pictures and videos they had seen of him, so there was hardly anyone in the building who didn’t know him!</p>
<p>"Boss, are you seeing this!? The women are all looking at me!"</p>
<p>Darren whispered while leaning over to Jason with wide, disbelieving eyes, and Jason scoffed again! Darren was standing right beside Jason, so whenever a woman looked at Jason, it would look like she was also staring at Darren! Jason didn’t want to burst Darren’s bubble by telling him he was only imagining things, so he just ignored the boy and allowed him to enjoy his fake popularity for the time being.</p>
<p>"Hehehe~ Look at those boobs."</p>

<p>"Shit, boss! What was that for!?"</p>
<p>"Stop getting distracted by women, you idiot. Come on."</p>

Jason knew that there were many women out there who would be more than willing to sleep with him if he showed even the slightest bit of interest. But Jason believed that you shouldn’t allow yourself to get distracted by every woman you see.</p>
