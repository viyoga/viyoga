<p>[The World of Minori]</p>
<p>[Central Region of the Fifth Domain.]</p>
<p>Gina’s vacation with Riyala and Hesabeth was turning out to be far more enjoyable than she ever thought it would be! When Hesabeth had suggested the vacation a few months ago to allow all three wives to grow closer, Gina already knew it was a good idea. The three women had never really spent that much time getting to know each other, and Gina knew that if they were going to be sharing one man, then they needed to know a lot more about each other so they don’t get into pointless fights in the future! So that was why Gina was happy to leave Earth and travel down here at the start of the month to see Hesabeth and Riyala!</p>
<p>At first, Gina thought that this vacation would just be about them spending time in the castle and talking, but Gina couldn’t have been more wrong! Hesabeth had planned one of the most elaborate vacations that Gina had ever been on, and at this point, Gina was starting to believe Hesabeth might just be the richest woman in the entire Primordial Expanse! Hesabeth had prepared houses for every single person that came to Minori, and she provided food, entertainment, and anything else that the women wanted!</p>
<p>Hesabeth planned her vacation across seven different worlds in her domain for both Riyala and Gina as well as all the other women that came with them! But since most of the women already went back to their own worlds and jobs, the three sister-wives were now spending time alone. Hesabeth had been carrying Gina and Riyala around ot multiple tourist attractions across multiple worlds, and Gina was loving every second of it!</p>
<p>Right now, the three women were seated at the upper balcony of an opera house in a minor kingdom known as Carin in the world of Minori, and they were watching a play by some actors on stage. The play was about a young boy who was on a journey to find an ancient magical antidote to cure his mother’s illness, and apparently, it was seen as one of the classic tales on Minori – something like the Journey to the West back on Earth.</p>
<p>Gina, Hesabeth, and Riyala were seated on a long couch on the balcony as they enjoyed the show, and Stella was standing at the edge of the couch with her hands joined in front of her dutifully. From how close the three women were sitting on the couch, anyone would be able to tell that they had really grown to like each other! Gina was sitting in the middle of the couch with a small smile on her face, and Riyala was lying down and resting her head on Gina’s lap. Gina stroked Riyala’s long hair calmly, and Riyala’s eyes slowly started to droop as she drifted into sleep! On Gina’s other side was Hesabeth!</p>
<p>The Prideful Queen was seated in her usual regal position with her hands positioned perfectly on her lap! Hesabeth was the perfect picture of a Queen, but there was a sense of calmness around her that she only showed to those that she was truly close to, and she allowed her usual high guard to drop slightly since she was around her sisters.</p>
<p>But all of a sudden, Hesabeth let out a small hum, and she spoke to Gina in a low voice.</p>
<p>"Don’t you think Jason would love this play?"</p>
<p>Gina turned to Hesabeth curiously while wondering what Hesabeth was trying to say, but Gina smiled when she saw the longing in Hesabeth’s eyes. It was obvious that Hesabeth just wanted to talk about Jason. Gina shook her head with a small laugh.</p>
<p>"Jason always hated soap operas. He never watched them unless he was with me. I think he’d be falling asleep like Riyala if he was here."</p>
<p>Hesabeth laughed softly at that, and she turned towards Stella as Stella said:</p>
<p>"I believe Master Jason’s reason for disliking them was because they were too ’cheesy’ and unrealistic. Although I do not know why he feels that way. I believe they are wonderful."</p>
<p>Both Hesabeth and Gina chuckled, and Hesabeth couldn’t really blame Jason for thinking like that. Soap Operas all had over-the-top acting to try and pass the message, so most men wouldn’t appreciate it. Hesabeth couldn’t stop herself from smiling as she imagined Jason dozing off as he tried and failed to pay attention to the dramatic movement and singing from the actors!</p>
<p>’I wish Jason was here.’</p>
<p>Hesabeth let out a calm sigh as this thought crossed her mind before she turned to Gina and asked:</p>
<p>"What did Jason say when you last spoke to him? Did he settle his business on L’horm yet?"</p>
<p>Gina nodded, and she said that Jason had already left L’horm.</p>
<p>"He said he was headed home now. I don’t know everything that happened, but Jason seemed excited about setting up some new Academies in the sixth domain. I think he found a way to raise the power level of our world even more, but I’m not sure exactly what he meant."</p>
<p>Gina said all this with a small smile of pride, and Hesabeth hummed curiously. So that was the path Jason wanted to take. Hesabeth knew that every overlord chose a different path to try and improve the lives of the creatures in their domain. Queen Rak’hael of the second domain chose education, King T’arigan of the dwarves chose industrialization, and Baron Skarr of the dragons chose militarization. Hesabeth had chosen agriculture for her own domain since she knew that the worlds in her domain had some of the most fertile land in the entire Primordial Expanse, and it seemed like Jason had chosen to go into education as well!</p>
<p>Hesabeth knew that it would be difficult to set up the schools and advance them to the point where they could run independently, so Jason would be really busy for a long time. But Hesabeth was sure that Jason could do it.</p>
<p>"It won’t be easy to set up academies on so many worlds. If he needs help with organisation, then I’ll be happy to step in."</p>
<p>"Oh, is that so? I’ll appreciate any help I can get."</p>
<p>"!"</p>
<p>All the women suddenly jolted in shock as they heard a voice from behind them, and they turned around quickly to see Jason standing there with a smile on his face!</p>
<p>"JASON!"</p>
<p>Gina was getting up before she even knew what was going on! But she had completely forgotten that Riyala was resting on her lap, so the poor dragon priestess was thrown to the ground and jolted awake with a yelp! Gina looked at Riyala apologetically, and she leaned down to help Riyala off the ground. And that moment allowed Hesabeth to get up from her seat and jump at Jason immediately!</p>
<p>"Darling!"</p>
<p>Hesabeth hugged Jason around the neck and hung off him happily, and Jason laughed as he lifted Hesabeth off the ground and spun her around! Hesabeth had the largest smile on her face, and she leaned down and kissed Jason deeply, like a dying man who just found water, and Jason could feel how much she missed him through this kiss alone! Jason ran his hand through Hesabeth’s long hair as he kissed her back with even more passion to show her that he missed her too!</p>
<p>Gina finally helped Riyala off the floor, and Riyala’s face brightened in joy once she saw that Jason was there! The two girls headed towards Jason as well, and Jason put Hesabeth down after one final peck before he spread his hands out to his other two girls. They both came forward, and Jason kissed Riyala softly with his hand around her waist before he turned to Gina with a smile.</p>
