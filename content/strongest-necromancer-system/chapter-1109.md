<p>First, Jason reached out for his Qi, and he felt it flow through his body at a steady, even pulse. Jason then reached for his mana and brought the two of them together!</p>
<p>[The User is attempting to combine two major power sources]</p>
<p>[The combination of Qi and Mana is considered detrimental to the body of any mage.]</p>
<p>[The user has bypassed the warnings]</p>
<p>[The user has achieved Resonance]</p>
<p>DOON!</p>
<p>The entire forest shook with a tremor as Jason’s power surged through the air! Jason felt a feeling of pure adrenaline coursing through his veins, and he had to concentrate to make sure he didn’t lose control of it! Jason’s body began to glow with a red and blue light, and all the Qi Matrices on his skin pulsed as they were fed with the new power source!</p>
<p>Phwoom!</p>
<p>CAW!</p>
<p>Up in the air, Helios let out a massive cry as he soared over Jason’s head, and Helios landed on a large branch a few hundred meters away and watched Jason with a curious look in its eyes! Helios knew that this wasn’t Qi, but at the same time, this power had a familiar Qi-like sensation that allowed Helios to sense it easily! Although the air was filled with power, there was still a lot of power inside of Jason that would easily destroy this entire city if Jason ever lost control of it! Helios always knew that his master was strong, but the Phoenix couldn’t believe his master was this strong!</p>
<p>Jason let out calm breaths as he allowed the power to slowly flow from one part of his body to another. Back when Jason had not yet gone through the Qi awakening and his Resonance was weaker, Jason’s power had only gone through a slight boost with each Resonance activation, and Jason was only able to use about fifteen percent of the power before he would lose control! But now that Jason’s Qi was a lot more stable, Jason could feel that he had far more control over it!</p>
<p>With this new Resonance, Jason’s power had grown to the peak of PRIMORDIAL Rank I, and Jason hadn’t felt any strain on his body yet, even though it had been more than ten minutes now!</p>
<p>’I should be able to hold it for about twenty minutes now. This is good. With this much power, I won’t have to worry about anything when I enter the Uncharted Zone.’</p>
<p>’Hohoho! My student has truly grown! I’m going to add some more training exercises to your routine! We can’t allow such promising Qi to go to waste!’</p>
<p>Esdath spoke up from within Jason’s Alternate Dimension, and Jason just huffed! This bastard had been silent for a long time now, and Jason had been wondering if Esdath died when he entered the Celestial Cloud earlier. Unfortunately, the bastard was still alive.</p>
<p>’Oi! What do you mean by unfortunately!? How dare you wish death upon your teacher!?’</p>
<p>Esdath waved his fist up at Jason, but Jason just grinned in amusement.</p>
<p>’Yeah, yeah, sorry about that. I’ll come in later for training.’</p>
<p>Esdath huffed pompously and turned away before Jason closed down the connection. Jason had really warmed up to Esdath in the past few months since Jason knew that Esdath meant well for him, so Jason was tolerating Esdath’s eccentric attitudes a lot better now. Jason refocused on his resonance, and a smile slowly formed on his face as he felt the intense power surging through him. Jason honestly wanted to train and see just what this power could do, but Jason knew that it would be a really bad idea to test his power on Earth! Before his awakening, a single drop of Jason’s Resonance was capable of destroying entire cities, so now that Jason was awakened, Jason knew that a single drop of his Resonance might even destroy this entire country!</p>
<p>’Maybe I should go do it in the Sahara Desert?’</p>
<p>Jason hummed with a calm smile as this idea came to him, and Jason was very close to actually doing it, but Jason felt his concentration slip when he heard a voice echo in his head.</p>
<p>’You’re going to burn the forest down, Jason.’</p>
<p>Jason quickly deactivated his Resonance, and he allowed his power to settle slowly before he finally opened his eyes and looked around him to see that all the trees and grasses were blackened and burnt! There hadn’t been any fire at all, but Jason’s aura was so intense that just the simple wave he had been letting out was hot enough to cause a lot of damage! Jason let out a calm sigh as he heard Helios cry proudly from up above, and Jason finally turned towards the person who spoke.</p>
<p>Agiel was standing at the edge of the clearing and watching Jason with a smile on her face. Her hands were folded across her chest, and she was leaning against one of the trees gently. Since Agiel didn’t have any clothes to wear here, she had borrowed some of Gina’s clothes, and although they were a bit big for her around the chest, they fit her well enough. Agiel was wearing a pair of tight shorts and a crop top that Gina would normally wear around the house when it was just her and Jason, but Agiel was a lot bolder with her body than Gina, so Agiel didn’t mind wearing the clothes outside.</p>
<p>As Jason looked Agiel over, the first thing he noticed was that she had gotten stronger. Jason didn’t know how it was possible, but in the last few days of training, Agiel had gone from a common SSS rank to a TITAN Rank I without any effort at all. When Jason had asked Agiel about this a few days ago, Agiel had said that it was a result of their Soul Bond.</p>
<p>Agiel had been training for a very long time without seeing an improvement in her magic because she hadn’t formed the Soul Bond yet, but now that she had gotten the Soul Bond, her body was finally starting to catch up to the actual strength of her soul! And besides, Agiel was already at the very peak of SSS Rank, so it shouldn’t be surprising that she broke into the TITAN Ranks so easily. It might take a few more months, but Agiel knew she would soon reach TITAN Rank II.</p>
<p>Jason had accepted this because Jason knew that Agiel was actually meant to be stronger. Jason remembered being a bit disappointed with Agiel’s strength when he met her a few months ago, and the Agiel that Jason had fought inside of Agiel’s mindscape was already around TITAN Rank III, so Jason knew that was what Agiel’s actual power level was meant to be. It would be great if Agiel could reach that strength soon!</p>
<p>"I thought everyone already went to bed. Did I wake you?"</p>
<p>Jason spoke up calmly while leaning back on his hands, and Agiel found her eyes trailing down Jason’s exposed chest. There was a sudden surge of desire that went through Agiel’s body as she stared at Jason’s tattooed body, and Jason raised a brow and called out:</p>
<p>"Agiel."</p>
<p>"!"</p>
<p>Agiel suddenly snapped back to her senses, and she felt her cheeks heat up as she realised that Jason had felt what she just felt! Agiel quickly pushed off the tree and began walking forward while talking.</p>
<p>"N-No, you didn’t wake me. I was just looking for you to discuss our plans for tomorrow. I checked inside, but Stella said you came out to train."</p>
<p>Jason smiled in amusement when Agiel said this! It was obvious that Agiel was trying to ignore what happened, and Jason decided to also ignore it since she was so embarrassed.</p>
