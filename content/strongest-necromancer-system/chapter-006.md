<p>Jason whispered to the boys!</p>
<p>"Hey, get up! Get up and move!"</p>
<p>Jason tried to tell the boys to get to their feet, but the boys were being difficult as they still writhed on the ground like worms, and Jason decided that he had done everything he could! He wasn't going to risk his life trying to save people who didn't want to be saved! Jason immediately trained to the side as silently as possible and hid behind a large tree!</p>
<p>He tried his best to calm his breathing so his heart would stop beating so loudly, and he watched as a pack of three giant wolves appeared through the foliage of the forest.</p>
<p>They were as tall as a grown man and had white fur with black streaks across their bodies. Their teeth gleamed in the moonlight whenever they growled, and their claws looked sharp enough to tear through a man in one slash! They calmly stalked through the foliage like apex predators and looked around dangerously, and it wasn't long before they caught sight of the three boys on the floor!</p>
<p>Jason gritted his teeth as he heard one of them howl loudly before all three of them pounced on the boys! The boys screamed in fear when they saw the wolves and they finally tried getting to their feet so they could run, but they weren't able to go anywhere as the wolves descended on them immediately!</p>
<p>The sound of screams filled the night and were followed by the crunching of bones and tearing of flesh! The wolves didn't hold back as they tore through the boys easily, and Jason risked looking behind the tree to make sure that none of the wolves were nearby!</p>
<p>Once he was sure that there weren't any wolves, he quickly tried getting to his feet! The wolves weren't going to feast forever! When they were done with the boys, they would turn around and look for more food! He couldn't be there when the wolves finished! He had to get out of there!</p>
<p>Jason began to creep his way through the forest desperately! If he could find a place to hide, like a cave or even a thick bush where he wouldn't be seen, then he could lay in waiting until the Superhumans got there! He just had to survive somehow!</p>
<p>Jason was as silent as possible as he moved through the forest. The sound of the wolves feeding behind him assured him that he wasn't being followed, and as the sound grew further away, he became more and more confident in his steps as he realized that he was far enough to move faster!</p>
<p>Jason looked around to find a place to hide and he saw a large shrub at the other side of the forest that was the perfect size for him to crawl into! Jason moved towards it and he parted the shrub.</p>
<p>Growl~!</p>
<p>And Jason came face to face with a giant white wolf baring its fangs at him!</p>
<p>At that moment, the first thought that crossed through Jason's mind, unsurprisingly, was the fact that he was fucked.</p>
<p>Jason was a calm person, and he always handled situations with as much logic as possible, but there was something about coming face to face with a beast of this size that completely eradicated all forms of logic from the mind of a man! It did strange and incomprehensible things to a man and made him act in ways that couldn't be explained with science no matter how much you tried!</p>
<p>And that was why, the moment Jason saw the beast, he didn't bother running away from it. Instead, he jumped toward it with all his strength!</p>
<p>Jason and the beast tumbled to the ground in a tackle of limbs, teeth, and claws! The beast was nearly as tall as Jason and it had far more strength than him as well, but Jason was being pushed forward by the strength of a thousand men at that moment! The adrenaline surging through his body would have been enough to make an elephant drop dead!</p>
<p>It was like Jason was possessed!</p>
<p>Jason pulled out the knife that he had taken from the boy earlier and he lashed out with it with a growl! He didn't even care what he was attacking; all he knew was that he wanted to cause as much hurt to this beast as possible!</p>
<p>And through sheer luck, the blade ended up scratching across the beast's eye, tearing a large gash over its face and making blood fly across the air! The beast growled in anger at the attack and at the next moment, Jason felt his entire body jolt as a massive gash was torn across his chest by the beast's claws!</p>
<p>Jason was thrown back to the ground as blood pooled all around him, and the beast growled in anger as it started to stalk over to finish him off.</p>
<p>Jason's blood poured out of his chest in a river, and completely enveloped the pendant that he had been wearing since his mother gave it to him. The pendant suddenly cracked down the middle and the beast stopped as a bright purple light lit up the shrub that they had been fighting in, making the beast turn its eyes away to avoid being blinded. The bright purple light enveloped Jason as it slowly began to seep into his body, and Jason blinked tiredly as he saw a line of purple text appear in front of him.</p>
<p>[System Initializing...]</p>
<p>[Host discovered... Analyzing host....]</p>
<p>[Analyzing...]</p>
<p>[Analyzing...]</p>

<p>[Analyzing...]</p>
<p>[It has been discovered that the Host has suffered a major injury and is incapable of rational thought. As the only conscious party, the system holds all rights to Decision Making.]</p>
<p>[The system has decided to follow through with the Bonding]</p>
<p>[During the Bonding, the host stands a 55% chance of passing away due to his injuries. Therefore, the system shall expand energy to heal all injuries to the Host.]</p>
<p>'What... what is all this?"</p>
<p>Jason had no idea what was going on. He was delirious and he could feel the blood leaking out of his chest and making him lose consciousness. It was almost like he was hallucinating due to the blood loss! But all of a sudden, he felt a strange sensation spreading across his chest. The wounds that had been hurting suddenly began to dull and he could feel energy returning to his limbs quickly!</p>
<p>No, not just energy! He felt even better than when he got injured! It was like he was filled with twice as much power as before!</p>
<p>Jason slowly sat up and touched his chest with wide eyes. The wounds that had once scarred him were all gone and he immediately understood that it was because of the message that appeared in front of him! The 'system' healed him!? What the hell was going on!?</p>
