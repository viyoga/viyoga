<p>It had become common knowledge among those travelling through the Hyperspace to make sure that they avoided going into the world of Capri. That world was far too dangerous, even for the mercenaries or travellers that were used to being in the midst of danger all the time.</p>
<p>But all the creatures and Pirates in the world of Capri had no idea that they were about to get a visitor that would completely throw their entire world upside down.</p>
<p>"We’re ten miles off from the Dimensional Tear leading to Capri. We should be there in about ten minutes."</p>
<p>Valeria spoke up as she typed something into one of the computers in the cockpit, and Jason hummed as he steered the massive ship straight towards the point marked out on the radar. Jason had just read through all the information on Capri from the system, and Jason couldn’t help but think that this was strange. Jason knew that the Zeroth Domain was a lawless place. There were no overlords there to make you do what they wanted, and the council had given up on this domain, so you didn’t have to worry about the Primordial Treaty either. That meant that most pirates shouldn’t have any issues living in this domain freely. So then, why were the Pirates on Capri so hellbent on living in a world filled with so many monsters?</p>
<p>Jason asked this question to the system, and the system immediately brought up a tab to explain.</p>
<p>[The user is correct regarding the situation at Capri. The Pirates that took up residence in that world did so because they believe there is a treasure hidden somewhere in the world of Capri. They refer to it as the Unix Treasure. The greatest Pirate Loot that will ever be discovered. According to legend, it is believed that the legendary Pirate known as Egor the Dominator had hidden his great treasure in Capri just before his death. The Pirates have been looking for it for a very long time, and they were even willing to fight against the dangerous monsters there to try and acquire it. But this battle has been going on for hundreds of thousands of years, and now there are many pirates who don’t even believe that the treasure exists anymore, and they only continue to stay in Capri for their own selfish reasons.]</p>
<p>’What the hell is this? One piece?’</p>
<p>Jason scoffed at his own joke once he was done reading through the system message. Jason should’ve known that those fucking pirates wouldn’t be wasting their time on a world filled with monsters for no reason. The fact that they were so desperate to stay in monster-infested waters and lands showed that there had to be some big reward that they were looking for. But Jason wasn’t interested in any of that. Jason had his own reason for going to this world, and there was nothing that would stop Jason from getting exactly what he wanted.</p>
<p>Jason had looked through the power levels of the inhabitants of Capri, and from what Jason deduced, Jason knew that the three strongest fighters on that world - the sea kraken, the golden dragon, and the Pirate King - were all in the PRIMORDIAL Rank I. There were also other PRIMORDIAL Rank Is in Capri as well, but the biggest difference between these three powerhouses and the other PRIMORDIALS was the fact that these three had exceptionally pure PRIMORDIAL cores. Their cores were filled with very pure Essence, and this allowed them to extract far more power from their cores than the other PRIMORDIALS. Jason wanted those cores. It had been a long time since Jason took a core from another PRIMORDIAL to advance his own growth, and Jason made up his mind right then that these three would be his very first victims in this domain.</p>
<p>"Master Jason!"</p>
<p>A young voice called out from behind and pulled Jason out of his thoughts. Jason turned around calmly, and he saw the young boy, Arte, running into the cockpit from behind with his sister, Tsara, running after him with a worried expression on her face.</p>
<p>"Arte! Don’t run inside here! They’re busy!"</p>
<p>The girl tried to stop her little brother, but Arte refused to listen at all and quickly ran up to Jason and climbed up the large captain’s chair. Arte took a seat right on Jason’s lap, and Jason chuckled as he patted Arte on the head calmly. Tsara came up to them and bowed to Jason with a worried look on her face.</p>
<p>"I’m very sorry, master Jason! He just refused to stay away from the cockpit!"</p>
<p>Tsara looked like she wanted to pull Arte away to go and discipline him, but Jason just waved her worries away calmly. It had been a few days since Jason found and released the two siblings, and things had been going well for them since then. It had been a bit difficult to get them to open up to the other Avarans at first. Although Arte had convinced Tsara that Jason did not lie to them at all in their previous conversation, Tsara was still a little worried about being on a ship with strangers, and she had been rightfully cautious for the first three days or so.</p>
<p>But as Tsara and Arte spent more time with Stella, they began to understand that the new crew were not bad people at all. Stella had taken the two children under her wing, and she taught them whatever they wanted to know about the people on this ship. The one person that Stella spoke the most highly of was Jason, and she praised Jason so much that the two siblings began to look up to Jason as some sort of perfect being.</p>
<p>Arte had taken a very intense liking to Jason, and it became normal to see the little boy sitting with Jason on the captain’s seat and watching the vast Hyperspace pass by as they travelled between worlds. Tsara was still a little bit reserved with Jason, but that was mainly because of the sort of person she was. There had been men who had tried to force themselves on Tsara in the past due to her magical allure, so Tsara was now very cautious around men.</p>
<p>But Tsara had been shocked when she discovered that Jason wasn’t affected by her powers, and she’d watched Jason discreetly for many days to see if he would look at her in the same lustful ways that many men had done in the past. But Jason never looked at her like that, and Tsara now knew that Jason was one of the only men capable of withstanding her abilities. This had risen Jason extremely high in Tsara’s mental evaluations, and although Tsara now trusted Jason a lot, she was still doing her best to stay reserved around him because she didn’t want to risk making him lose control by getting too close.</p>
<p>Jason had spoken to Arte and Tsara about their situation and what he planned to do for them, and the two children were very happy when they heard Jason say that he would eventually take them home. Jason had a plan that he needed to complete first. But once he was done with that, Jason would bring them back to their own world in the third domain. But for now, Tsara and Arte would be a part of Jason’s crew.</p>
<p>Jason patted Arte on the head calmly, and Arte chuckled before Arte spoke up curiously.</p>
<p>"Are we there yet?"</p>
<p>Jason hummed.</p>
<p>"Just a few more minutes. How are you two holding up? Is everything going alright?"</p>
<p>Jason turned to look at Tsara as he asked this, and Tsara nodded with a reserved smile.</p>
<p>"We are doing well, master Jason. I’m grateful that you took us in."</p>
<p>Tsara bowed gratefully, and Arte looked up at Jason with a playful smile.</p>
<p>"Tsara thinks you’re like the handsome princes from her storybooks."</p>
<p>"ARTE!"</p>
