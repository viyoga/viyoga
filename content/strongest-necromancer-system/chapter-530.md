<p>[This is Favin, leader of Squad Zero from the Zeus Settlement. Backup has arrived.]</p>
<p>The Mecha soldiers all looked up when they saw multiple figures appearing in the sky, and they saw fifty helicopters carrying large Zen Mechas towards the battlefield! Inside the Zen Mechas in the sky, the teenage pilots couldn’t believe what they were witnessing! The entire region was covered in a black blanket of monsters, and it didn’t look like there was ever going to be an end to them! Doubt began to creep into the hearts of the teenagers, but Favin narrowed his eyes and spoke up again. Favin was the leader of Squad Zero, the strongest squad in this world, and the squad that had been fighting against the Lynx for the longest, so everyone listened when they heard him speak.</p>
<p>[You all heard what Haruto said. This is what we’ve been trained for all our lives. This is our destiny! I wish to see you all on the other side of this battle, safe and sound, but if your life is taken, then make sure you do not die a simple death! Rain down carnage upon those who will threaten your existence and drag them screaming and shouting into the afterlife! We will live and die as warriors, because that is what gives our lives meaning! We are the final defence for our planet! The Zen Warriors! And we will give them hell.]</p>
<p>Chink!</p>
<p>The helicopters released the harness holding the Zen Mechas, and all fifty Mechas began to fall from the sky like giant balls of destruction! The pilots in the other Mechas felt their confidence boosted from Favin’s speech and they narrowed their eyes and pulled out their weapons as they fell towards the blanket of darkness below them!</p>
<p>BOOM!!</p>
<p>The first person to land was Favin and his partner, and Favin immediately stabbed his sword into one of the Lynx before spinning around and unleashing multiple bullets on the Lynx around him with a massive shout! The fifty new Mechas caused the tide of the battle to shift heavily in favour of the Varigan army again, and they began to rush into the horde of Lynx to finish the battle as quickly as possible!</p>
<p>Ratatattata!</p>
<p>Behind the Mechas, the soldiers were fighting their own battle as they shot diamond-tipped armour-piercing rounds at the Lynx’s heads! The Lynx were basically S-Rank in strength, but their skin was not as strong as an S-Rank on Earth, and that was why they could still be hurt using diamond-tipped bullets! But in order to do any significant damage, you had to make sure every shot was a headshot!</p>
<p>Bang! Bang! Bang! Bang!</p>
<p>The soldiers continued to play their part, and Jason watched the entire battle unfolding from on top of Ragnar’s back! Ragnar was flying Jason and Gina across the battlefield so that they could reach the Pharynx quickly! Jason had given orders to his subordinates not to let any of the child soldiers die in this battle, so the subordinates were doing everything they could to make sure that the Lynx didn’t overwhelm the Zen Mechas! Jason’s subordinates were taking care of the larger portion of monsters, and leaving the rest to the Zen Mechas, and they didn’t have to worry about the Mechas since it was obvious that each and every Zen pilot was more than capable of holding their own in a battle! Jason watched as one of the Mechas slit a Lynx down the middle before slamming its gun into the mouth of another Lynx and unleashing a bullet that splattered its brains across the floor! So fucking cool! Jason still couldn’t get over how fucking cool those Mecha suits were, and Jason had to turn away and focus on the Pharynx when he sensed that they were close to it.</p>
<p>’Damn, that thing is huge.’</p>
<p>Jason had already noticed the sheer size of this beast when he looked at it through the [Eye of the King] earlier, but there was nothing that could have prepared him to see it in real life! The Pharynx was larger than any monster that Jason had ever seen in his life! In fact, the only thing that still managed to be larger than it was Rumunaga in his dragon form, and that was only by a little! The Pharynx managed to make a few of Jason’s subordinates look like tiny rats in front of its size, but Jason wasn’t worried about the size of the beast! Big? Small? It didn’t matter. As long as it could be killed, then every beast would fall to him eventually! The Pharynx was currently trying its best to crush the Sovereign and Jaraht under its large tentacles! Those two had taken the role of the frontline fighters and they were running towards the beast with fire burning on their weapons! The Sovereign slashed his sword across one of the Pharynx’s tentacles, and his attack tore the tentacle in half and caused the Pharynx to cry out in pain as it used another tentacle to slam the Sovereign to the side! The Sovereign crashed into the ground, but Jaraht continued forward, and he reached the Pharynx’s main body before pulling back his flaming fist and slamming it into the beast’s side!</p>
<p>BOOMM!!</p>
<p>A tremor echoed through the region, but Jason saw a pulse of energy travel across the Pharynx’s body, and Jason knew that the Pharynx had absorbed the impact! The Pharynx brought its tentacle down on Jaraht and crushed him to death, and Jason hummed as he got a notification in his system to resurrect Jaraht.</p>
<p>’So we can’t hurt it with blunt weapons. That’s fine. I have more swordsmen than brawlers, so this works for me. Those without bladed weapons, switch to distraction. Draw its attention and make space for the others to attack.’</p>
<p>Jason quickly passed an order to his subordinates before he turned to Gina and spoke seriously.</p>
<p>"Take the Skyking and command the air forces to distract the monster. Don’t try to take it down yourself, it’s too strong for you to handle, but make sure it doesn’t have any rest. Don’t worry about any of the subordinates dying. I have enough mana to resurrect them."</p>
<p>Gina nodded, and she went up to her toes to kiss Jason’s cheek before she jumped off Ragnar’s back! The Skyking appeared from the Alternate Dimension with a loud caw, and it swooped under Gina and took her into the air to start their attack! Jason watched as hundreds of his flying subordinates followed after Gina, and Gina pointed to one side of the Pharynx and commanded the dragons to unleash fire!</p>
<p>ROARRRR!</p>
<p>KA-BOOM!</p>
<p>A massive blast echoed through the air as over ten dragonbreaths exploded at once, and the Pharynx screeched as it began raising its tentacles in the air to try and take Gina and the dragons out! But they were far too high for it to reach, and this distraction allowed the Sovereign to come in with his sword and release another flaming slash that took off a second tentacle!</p>
<p>SCREEEEECHHH!!!</p>
<p>Jason’s eyes narrowed as the Pharynx suddenly opened its mouth wide and unleashed hundreds of Lynx on the battlefield! The Lynx charged straight for the Sovereign and Jason noticed that the battlefield was slowly becoming saturated with the black monsters. Jason had to even the odds.</p>
<p>Jason put his hand out towards the battlefield, and a pulse of mana suddenly surged through the region! The people fighting on the ground looked up when they sensed the sudden rise in power, and they were shocked when they noticed a large magic circle forming under their feet! Jason released his mana towards all the dead monsters on the ground, and he activated a skill!</p>
<p>[Necrotic Reanimation] has been activated.</p>
<p>ROARRRRR!</p>
