<p>Jason already had to deal with enough problems here in the Primordial Plane! He really didn’t want to have to deal with fucking gods as well!</p>
<p>’I couldn’t even move when I was in front of it! That thing is hundreds of times stronger than me!’</p>
<p>[The system suggests that the user focus on gaining as many subordinates as possible and raising his power level to even greater heights. It will not be easy to survive the coming future at the user’s current level.]</p>
<p>Jason released a calm sigh after reading the system’s message. The system didn’t need to tell him that since he already knew it. If Jason didn’t begin making some serious moves to raise his power, then he would eventually meet someone strong enough to kill him without any effort at all. Jason knew he was far stronger than he had been only a few weeks ago, but the Primordial Expanse was too dangerous for him to be content with this level of power! Jason needed to—</p>
<p>"Jason...?"</p>
<p>A voice suddenly spoke up in surprise, and Jason felt his eyes widen in shock when he heard it! Jason turned down slowly to look at the woman in his arms and felt a feeling of agonizing joy rising from deep inside him when he looked into Gina’s blood-red eyes. Jason had never cried for any reason before, but he could feel his eyes getting wet as he felt a smile spreading on his face.</p>
<p>"Hey, Gina."</p>
<p>That was the only thing Jason could bring himself to say at that moment, and he watched as Gina raised her hand up to her face with wide, disbelieving eyes. Gina touched her face nervously before her hand went down to feel her heart beating through her chest. Gina was obviously shocked by the fact that she was alive at that moment, but it didn’t take long before tears began to fall from her eyes! Gina smiled brightly as she looked up into Jason’s eyes, and a choked laugh escaped from her lips! Gina had no idea where she was or what Jason had done to bring her back to life, but none of that mattered at all! The only thing that mattered at that moment was that Jason was here in front of her! The man she loved had torn through reality itself to bring her back to life!</p>
<p>Gina’s shaky hand rose to cup Jason’s cheek, and she brought him down for a heated kiss. Jason leaned into the kiss with every ounce of desire inside his body as he held Gina close, and he pressed his forehead to Gina’s own when they separated a few seconds later. Gina caressed Jason’s cheek as her tears continued to fall, and she spoke in a voice filled with love and adoration.</p>
<p>"I’m so proud of you, Jason. Thank you! Sniff~ Thank you so much!"</p>
<p>Gina wrapped her hand around Jason’s neck and hugged him tighter as her emotions threatened to overwhelm her! Gina could only imagine what Jason must have gone through to bring her back to life, and she knew that she would never be able to repay him for what he just did! Jason wrapped his hand around Gina’s head and held her close as her sobs became even more intense! Jason allowed her to cry her heart out from happiness and he waited till she had finally begun to calm down before he pulled back slightly and used his thumb to wipe the tears falling down her face. Jason’s lips split into a small smile as he spoke in a low, amused voice.</p>
<p>"I guess this means I have to ask you to be my girlfriend all over again, doesn’t it?"</p>
<p>Gina felt amusement bubble up from her chest when she heard Jason’s words, and it wasn’t long before both of them fell into laughter as they held each other close.</p>

<p>"What in the world are you saying!?"</p>
<p>Griselda and Riyala were kneeling on the floor in a close huddle, and Griselda felt like pulling her hair out as she stared at her phone! For the past hour, Griselda had been trying to figure out what language Riyala was talking in! Griselda’s translator app had a special function that allowed it to detect and translate more than five hundred different languages from all over the world, and Griselda knew that it was one of the best in the market! Even some of the most unheard languages were registered on the app! But for some fucked-up reason, the language detector couldn’t even tell Griselda what country Riyala’s language was from!</p>
<p>Riyala was sitting beside Griselda with a slightly confused expression, and she watched Griselda getting increasingly frustrated with the translator app! At first, Riyala had been afraid of Griselda because of how intense and dramatic Griselda always was, but after hanging out with her for some time, Riyala realized that Griselda was actually a very courteous person. Griselda had a lot of mannerisms that reminded Riyala of the Riyal family in Radagan and Riyala couldn’t help but wonder if Griselda wasn’t a member of some royal family here on Earth! The only thing that showed Riyala that Griselda was a perfectly normal girl was how expressive she always was. There weren’t a lot of princesses who would show such intense emotions on their face all the time!</p>
<p>"Here, try to say something."</p>
<p>Griselda put her phone towards Riyala with an expectant look on her face, and Riyala nodded as she came forward and spoke into the phone.</p>
<p>"@#!#!@."</p>
<p>Riyala had only said ’Hello’ in her language since that was one of the words that she knew in both English and the Radagan tongue, and Griselda quickly turned to her phone and watched in anticipation as it loaded the language and tried to translate it. But it didn’t even take ten seconds before it showed an error and declared that it couldn’t find the language!</p>
<p>Griselda cursed at the phone!</p>
<p>"Useless trash! I paid fifty dollars for this translator app, and it’s useless!"</p>
<p>Riyala recoiled in shock at Griselda’s loud voice, and Griselda sighed tiredly as she apologized. Riyala understood ’sorry’ in English, so she just nodded to Griselda and smiled kindly. Griselda smiled back at her, and then she frowned. It was obvious that Riyala’s language was just too much for the translator app to handle, so they would have to wait for Jason to come back so they could ask him what country Riyala was from.</p>
<p>’Speaking of Jason...’</p>
<p>Griselda frowned as she finally stood up and turned to look at Juliet. Juliet was sitting on the floor with her back against the wall and staring at her phone with a nervous expression on her face. Griselda could tell that there was something wrong, but for some reason, Juliet didn’t want to say anything about it. Juliet only told them that Jason had gone somewhere to finish some business, and he didn’t want them to leave the house till he got back.</p>
