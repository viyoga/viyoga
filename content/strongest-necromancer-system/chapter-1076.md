<p>Agiel was smiling happily with an excited light in her eyes! Even though Jason said that things were complicated, the mere fact that Jason didn’t want to see her getting married to anyone else made Agiel feel like there was really a chance here! Agiel knew that Jason cared for her. And for Agiel, that was more than enough.</p>
<p>Agiel finally stopped the dance, and she took Jason’s hand and led him out towards a large door at the side. The door led to a massive balcony that had a perfect view of the night sky with a full moon shining down on them, and Agiel hugged Jason’s arm to her chest and watched Jason’s face lovingly as Jason appreciated the silver moon.</p>
<p>"It’s nice, isn’t it?"</p>
<p>Jason hummed when Agiel said this, and he turned to Agiel as she continued.</p>
<p>"This was where my mother first met your father. She fell in love with him right here, and she confessed to him the moment she realised he was her soul bond. Your father was so freaked out that he thought she was crazy."</p>
<p>Agiel laughed softly when she said this, and Jason chuckled as well! Jason could only imagine how shocking it would be for someone to confess to you on the first day you meet them! Jason understood why his father would think Agiel’s mother was crazy!</p>
<p>Agiel sighed as she stared up at the moon wistfully, and then she finally turned to Jason and looked up at him with a serious face.</p>
<p>"I love you, Jason."</p>
<p>Jason blinked in surprise, and he turned to look down at Agiel as she said this. There was a small smile on Agiel’s face at that moment. Agiel understood that she was just repeating history by doing this, and there was a chance that she and Jason might turn out exactly like their parents, but Agiel didn’t care. Agiel wasn’t going to allow her fears to stop her from reaching out for what she wanted.</p>
<p>Jason wanted to say something, but Agiel put her hand on his chest and shook her head to tell him to stop! Agiel wanted to finish first. Jason calmed down, and he just listened as Agiel continued.</p>
<p>"I know your heart isn’t mine alone. I didn’t find you quickly enough, and you met other women that took parts of you away from me. I know you can never stop loving them just to be with me, but I don’t care. I’ve seen the sort of life I would live if I decided to leave you, and I hated it so much, Jason. I hated every minute of it, and I kept thinking I would rather be dead than live the rest of my life like that. So I decided that I’d rather share you and have a piece of your heart than lose you for the rest of my life. Do you... Do you have any space in your heart for me?"</p>
<p>Agiel placed her hand over Jason’s heart as she asked this, and she looked up at Jason with an expression filled with anxious anticipation! Agiel was not going to force Jason into anything, and if he refused her here, then she would accept that this was just not meant to be. But Agiel found herself wishing and praying that there would be a chance! Agiel wanted this more than she had ever wanted anything in her life!</p>
<p>Jason looked down at Agiel with a calm look on his face, but his mind was racing. Jason knew that this was the moment. If Jason wanted to take the bond with Agiel, then he would have to accept her here. And if he did not want it, then he would simply say no. Jason let out a soft breath as he asked himself: what did he want to do?</p>
<p>Jason knew that Agiel did not like his other wives. Agiel had been competing with Gina for a long time to try and win him over, and even when they last met, Agiel had told Jason that she did not like the fact that he left her for other women! One of the reasons Jason never fully opened his heart to Agiel was because he thought she would never accept to join the sort of relationship he had. But now, Agiel was putting herself out there on her own! Agiel had come to understand that there was nothing she could do to get rid of the love Jason had for his other wives, and Agiel was willing to accept them just to be with Jason.</p>
<p>Jason still didn’t know what exactly he felt for Agiel. But as Jason thought about it in this moment, Jason realised that although he didn’t love Agiel yet, what he felt for her was still far greater than just ordinary affection. Jason knew that this emotion could definitely become love, and Jason was willing to explore it to the end. Jason smiled down at Agiel, and he stroked her cheek softly while speaking in a calm voice.</p>
<p>"Alright, Agiel. If you’re willing to accept everything that comes with this relationship, then I’m willing to accept you as well. Let’s try together."</p>
<p>Agiel felt an excited feeling surge through her entire body once she heard this, and for a moment Agiel was too stunned to even speak! Tears gathered in Agiel’s eyes as she felt her emotions beginning to overwhelm her, and Jason chuckled softly and gently rubbed his finger against Agiel’s cheek to wipe away the tears.</p>
<p>Agiel sniffled, and she hid her face in her hands as she laughed at herself.</p>
<p>"I can’t believe I’m crying."</p>
<p>Agiel said this when she noticed that the tears wouldn’t stop coming, and Jason just smiled at her. Agiel looked up at Jason with pure devotion, and she gently pulled Jason down toward her while going up on her toes, and Jason held her close as the two lovers kissed under the silver moonlight.</p>
