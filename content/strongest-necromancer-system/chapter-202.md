<p>Ten Falcon guards had responded to Jason’s charge, but Jason had more than thirty adamantine ants with him, so when the Falcons started to ascend, the Adamantine Ants were able to overwhelm the Falcons with their numbers and force them to give up their plan to attack! The Falcons immediately began to shoot their sharpened feathers toward the ants with deadly accuracy! The feathers flew true and began to slam into the ants like flying swords, and many of the ants that were not Rook Subordinates suffered major injuries that almost took them out of the fight! The Rook Subordinates, on the other hand, were able to shrug off most of the attack since their skin was much tougher! And since they had the unique skill [Stonewall] that helped them to reduce the damage from ranged attacks by half, the feathers didn’t even leave any scratches on them at all! They dashed after the Falcons and didn’t give them any room to escape, and they began to grab hold of the Falcons’ feathers to prevent them from being able to fly!</p>
<p>The Falcons flapped their wings while crying out loudly as they started falling from the sky, and Jason commanded Ragnar to get ready! Ragnar tucked its wing in and fell into a dive so that it was directly below the falling Falcons! The Falcons were stunned to see Ragnar there, and they were all shocked when Ragnar suddenly flipped in the air so that he was facing them! Ragnar opened its mouth, and a bright yellow light shone with power from inside it and the Falcons immediately tried to get the ants off them since the ants were preventing them from flying out of the way! But the ants refused to let go! The ants latched onto the Falcons’ wings and bodies, and they allowed their bodies to act like anchors that brought the Falcons down!</p>
<p>Ragnar reared its head back, and the Falcons all cried out as he unleashed a wave of pure hellfire upon them!</p>
<p>PHWOOM!</p>
<p>The flames were scorching hot even before they had reached the Falcons, and the Falcons looked on in fear while waiting for the exact moment when the ants would let go so that they could fly away! The Falcons believed that there was no way the ants would keep holding them down after seeing the flames that were about to scorch them! The ants had to let go eventually!</p>
<p>But the Falcons had no idea what they were dealing with! If these had been regular beasts, then they would have been right in thinking that the ants would let go! But these weren’t regular beasts! These were members of the Legion who had dedicated their lives to their King! Their lives didn’t matter to them at all! The ants held on tight until Ragnar’s attack reached them, and the Falcons could do nothing as the fire washed over them!</p>
<p>WOOSH!</p>
<p>Ragnar’s flames burned for more than five seconds, and everything caught within it was cooked beyond recognition! The Falcons tried to defend themselves by sending mana into their wings to make them stronger, but no level of strengthening would be capable of closing the gap in level between them and Ragnar! The flames cooked them from the inside out, and they were dead before they even knew it!</p>
