<p>Jason lashed out with sharp claws and cut across the chest of a Machina! Blood arched through the sky, and Jason wasted no time as he reached out and grabbed the neck of another Machina before ripping his head clean off! There was a prickling at the back of Jason’s head, like a sensation of having someone whisper silently to you, and as Jason watched the blood flying through the air, he realised that it was his vympiric side telling him that he needed to drink!</p>
<p>The next Machina that attacked Jason found himself being pulled in by strong arms, and Jason wasted no time as he sank his teeth into the man’s neck and drank! The man screamed in pain as Jason drank him of all his blood, and Jason felt the whisper at the back of his head growing stronger as he gained more energy! The whisper finally grew into a massive scream, and Jason pulled back from the man’s neck with a wide smile, and he activated his innate Vympiric skill!</p>
<p>The concept of a Vympiric skill was something every Vympire was familiar with. For any Vympire that was turned, there was a unique skill that you gained as a result of your heritage! This skill was always something related to blood and a natural element, and most of the time, the Vympiric skill gained by a Changeling was a similar element to the Vympiric skill held by the Dead Apostle that bit them! Gillian’s Vympiric Skill was called [Blood Bomb], and it allowed her to detonate the blood of any person she injured. And since Jason was her Changeling, it was obvious that his Vympiric skill would also be something similar to fire! Jason felt this mana pour from his body towards the men he had injured, and he called out.</p>
<p>"Crimson Hellfire."</p>
<p>PHWOOMMM!</p>
<p>A red flame suddenly erupted to life around Jason as all the men began to burn with an everlasting fire, and loud screams could be heard from those who had been rushing towards Jason as they were captured in the flame and burnt to ashes! The skill [Crimson Hellfire] allowed Jason to create an eternal fire from the blood of those that he had injured! This fire would burn for as long as Jason wanted it to, and since the fire could easily spread from one person to another, anyone who dared to come close would be engulfed in the flames as well! Jason threw his head back in pure ecstasy, and his lips split into a bloody smile as he listened to the screams and cries of those who dared to charge into his flames! Jason’s eyes turned towards the large figure of Alexander as he flew through the sky, and the skin on Jason’s back bulged and rippled with a disgusting cracking sound, before a pair of large bat wings suddenly erupted from behind him! Jason beat the wings and quickly took off after Alexander!</p>
<p>BOOM!</p>
<p>A massive explosion went off on the side, and Jason turned to see that Gillian had blown up all the men who were trying to block her way! Gillian had also called on her bat wings, and she was moving so fast that she rocketed past Jason at supersonic speeds! Jason quickly increased his own speed, and he followed behind her as they headed straight for Alexander! The moment they began heading towards him, Alexander could sense their power, and he turned around with an angry roar! Alexander was only a few hundred meters away from the central control building, but with how fast Jason and Gillian were moving, he knew that he wouldn’t make it to the building before they caught up to him! Alexander immediately turned back around, and he transformed his head from the beautiful appearance of a female lamia into a scaly and dark dragon head with red eyes and sharp teeth! Alexander opened his mouth wide, and a bright red light ignited at the back of his throat as he unleashed a massive flame breath!</p>
<p>TWOOONN!!</p>
<p>The flaming breath was very similar to the ray of superheated fire that Ragnar and Trigon normally used for their attacks, but this one was so much more dangerous than anything Ragnar or Trigon could produce, and it flew forward with enough force to carve a large valley in the ground! All the buildings that the beam passed were immediately eviscerated into dust, and Alexander watched with a vindictive look on his face as the beam slammed into the Central control building and erupted in a massive explosion!</p>
<p>KA-BOOM!</p>
<p>TWOOON!</p>
<p>The sky above the world of the Machina rippled and shook with static, and Alexander noticed that the forcefield blocking interdimensional travel had just come down!</p>
<p>Alexander gained a bloodthirsty grin that looked even deadlier on his dragon face, and he was about to release enough mana to travel out of this world and make his way towards Radagan, but all the fighters suddenly stopped as a strange power appeared from the sky! Alexander had been the head of the Machina for a very long time, and he knew about all the secret projects and magical weapons that the Machina had been creating to protect themselves against enemies! So the moment Alexander felt that power, he immediately knew what it was!</p>
<p>’The doomsday sequence!’</p>
<p>A giant ball of pure metal that was at least as large as a city suddenly began to appear from behind the clouds! The ball looked like a massive moon, but there were various red dots all around its body like millions of tiny eyeballs! Alexander immediately released a ton of mana to create a dimensional tear, but before he could pass through it, he was rammed by an impossible force that threw him out of the air and straight to the ground! Gillian pulled her hand back like a blade, and Alexander shouted towards Gillian in anger!</p>
<p>"Stop fighting and listen to me! There’s an attack coming that could kill all of us! We need to get out of here!"</p>
<p>The large dome in the sky whirled with an inexplicable power, and this time even Jason stopped what he had been doing to look up in surprise! The power he could feel within that orb was greater than anything he had ever felt before! It was higher than any of their individual powers, and even greater than that!</p>
<p>’The power of a primordial?’</p>
<p>"That orb is a Death Star! It was designed to kill fucking Primordials! If we don’t get out of here, we’ll all die!"</p>
<p>Alexander was absolutely rabid as he tried to get out from under Gillian’s power, but Gillian didn’t even look like she cared! Gillian shot her hand forward and stabbed Alexander in the heart, and Alexander roared in pain as he quickly transformed his back into its slimy form and flowed away from Gillian! The large orb began to spin in the sky, and Gillian turned and chased Alexander down with a furious look in her eyes, but before she could grab him, Jason appeared from the side and grabbed her by the arm before he quickly pulled her down into the shadows!</p>
<p>And in the next moment, the world erupted in red!</p>
<p>TWOON!</p>
<p>TWOON!</p>
<p>TWOON!</p>
<p>TWOON!</p>
<p>Millions of red lasers suddenly blasted out from the Death Star, and the moment the lasers touched the ground, they erupted in massive explosions!</p>
<p>BOOM!</p>
