<p>Gina looked down as Gerael came over as well, and Gina put the princess down before she kissed the little maid on the hair with a wide smile. Gerael blushed happily as Gina rubbed her hair affectionately, and Gina finally put her hands on her knees and faced the two little girls before asking.</p>
<p>"So, what are you two cuties doing out here? I hope you aren’t trying to stop me from training today. King Farlow made it very clear that we can only hang out when I’m done with my training, so he’ll be very angry if you try to keep me from training. But you see, just between the three of us, if two cute little girls happen to kidnap me and take me away for a full day, then there’s absolutely nothing I can do about that. I’ll be powerless to stop you."</p>
<p>Gina lowered her voice and whispered the last part conspiratorially, and the two little girls laughed as Gina gave them a playful wink. Heriet spoke up with a wide smile!</p>
<p>"You just want to play hooky, don’t you!?"</p>
<p>Gina put her hand on her chest and gasped in shock!</p>
<p>"How dare you? I would never do such a thing! I’ll have you know, I’m the most dedicated student your father has ever had! It’s not my fault that I’m weak to your devilish cuteness!"</p>
<p>Gina reached over and pinched Heriet’s cheek, and Heriet chuckled cutely! After a few seconds, Heriet finally told Gerael to show what they brought, and Gerael reached into a pocket dimension behind her and produced a small box wrapped in cloth. Gina blinked in surprise when she saw it, and she spoke in a low voice.</p>
<p>"Is that... food?"</p>
<p>"Mhmm! Mhmm! Gerael and I wanted to thank you for hanging out with us yesterday! We know you were really tired from your training, but you were so much fun! So, so, we went to the kitchen, and we made you something really yummy! Well, Gerael made most of it, but I helped cut the vegetables, so I helped too! We... we hope you like it!"</p>
<p>Gina’s face was set in a look of absolute disbelief as she slowly collected the box of food from Gerael. Gina stared down at the box, and she had no idea what to think at this moment. There was a pure feeling going through Gina’s heart as she looked at the meal box, and as Gina looked over at the two girls, she felt that feeling intensify until it nearly consumed her! Heriet saw the shocked look on Gina’s face, and her face dropped slightly as she spoke.</p>
<p>"Uhm, d-do you not like it?"</p>
<p>Gina looked towards Heriet in shock once she heard this, and she realised that Heriet thought she didn’t like it! Gina immediately dropped to her knees, and she put the food to the side before she dragged both girls in for a tight hug! Gina released a shuddering breath as she felt her heart beating rapidly! There were just far too many emotions in Gina’s heart at this moment, and she didn’t really know how to express all of them at the same time! But the one thing Gina knew was that she absolutely loved this! If this was what it would feel like to have kids, then Gina couldn’t wait to be a mother!</p>
<p>"I love it so much. Thank you for doing this."</p>
<p>The girls hugged Gina back happily, and Gina squeezed them once more before she kissed both girls on the cheeks and finally pulled back.</p>
<p>"I’ll make sure not to leave a single crumb behind! You can count on that!"</p>
<p>Heriet and Gerael both giggled, and they thanked Gina with one more hug before Gina watched as they took off from the ground and flew away while waving goodbye! Gina picked up the box of food again, and she stared at it with a motherly smile on her face as she headed off to the garden. There were two guards standing on either side of the entrance, and they both bowed to Gina as she passed them. Gina gave them a bow and a greeting in return as she took off her shoes and vanished into the garden. Once she was gone, the guard on the left sniffled and used a hand to rub his eye, and the guard on the right turned to look at him with a deadpan expression.</p>
<p>"Oh come on, don’t tell me you’re actually crying right now."</p>

<p>"Sniffle Hah, you don’t have any daughters, so you wouldn’t get it. That was... that was so beautiful."</p>
