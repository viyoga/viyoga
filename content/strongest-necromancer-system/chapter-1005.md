<p>"I was just thinking of the quickest way to reach the Eastern Mountains. Do you know about the Interdimensional Transit Line?"</p>
<p>Stella’s face squeezed in confusion when she heard that term, and Stella thought for a few seconds before her eyes widened and she snapped her fingers in realisation!</p>
<p>"Yes, I remember, master! I heard about it once from a passing wanderer on B’Nazin. I think it helps people travel across domains quickly. But it’s very expensive, so only wealthy people can use it."</p>
<p>Jason hummed, and he told Stella that they would be using it to get to the fifth domain. Stella was surprised by this, and she asked Jason why they couldn’t just fly there.</p>
<p>"It’ll take too long to fly. I don’t want to be gone for so long. Come on."</p>
<p>Jason began to fly quicker, and Stella immediately sped up to keep up with him! Jason sent another message to the system asking it where the transit line was located, and once Jason had the location, he changed his direction and began heading towards it!</p>
<p>....</p>
<p>[The World of Mionia]</p>
<p>[Ten Dimensional Leaps Away From Minori]</p>
<p>The interdimensional transit line was seen as one of the greatest pieces of magical engineering to ever be created in the Primordial Expanse. It was located on a world called Mionia that was ten dimensional leaps away from Minori. Mionia was one of the most developed worlds in the fifth domain, and it was made up of multiple megacities filled with massive skyscrapers and very advanced technology. There were flying cars and robots moving across the city streets, and people from many different worlds and diverse races could be seen walking together. Jason was currently in the largest megacity known as City A, and he was standing on top of a tall skyscraper and looking down on the city calmly.</p>
<p>The city was extremely beautiful from up here, and Jason could easily tell that nearly everyone in this city was rich. There was just an intense sophistication about all the people walking on the streets and moving around inside the buildings, like they didn’t have any worries at all! If Jason had to compare this city to anywhere in America, then Jason would say that this city was very similar to Silicon Valley!</p>
<p>From where he stood, Jason could see a massive circular structure where the interdimensional transit line was located in the distance. The transit took up more than two hundred acres of land, and the rest of City A was built around the transit line in concentric circles going outwards! Jason studied the transit line for a few minutes, and Jason realised he wouldn’t be able to use his shadows to sneak into the transit line like he planned! There was a massive magical shield protecting the entire transit structure, and a line of people were paying a toll fare at a gate before they would be allowed to pass through the magic shield and enter the transit line. If Jason tried to pass the magical shield through the shadows, he would trigger every alarm in the transit line, and that would most likely delay his trip by several days for no reason!</p>
<p>’The best choice is to find a disguise. My mana is already too high for any of the people here to notice it. They won’t be able to tell I’m a PRIMORDIAL if I suppress the pressure I release. A disguise will be a lot more reasonable than using the shadows.’</p>
<p>Jason turned to his side, and he saw Stella looking at the transit line structure with a small frown. Jason had already told Stella about his plan to sneak onto the transit line, but it was obvious that Stella had her doubts about getting past the magical structure as well! Jason spoke up:</p>
<p>"What are you thinking, Stella?"</p>
<p>Stella turned to Jason quickly, and she was silent for a few seconds before she said:</p>
<p>"I don’t think we can pass through the shield undetected, master. Its level is too high."</p>
<p>Jason hummed, and he told Stella that he had been thinking the same thing. The best choice they had now was to use a disguise to get into it, but Jason had never had to use a disguise before, so he didn’t know where to go for something like that! But Jason just raised a brow when he saw Stella’s eyes light up happily, and Jason realised that he had forgotten who he was talking to! Stella was the woman who had a skill for everything, so there was no way she wouldn’t have a skill for disguises as well!</p>
<p>"You have a skill that can help me hide my identity?"</p>
<p>Stella nodded immediately!</p>
<p>"It’s a skill called [Astral Projection]. It allows me to assume the identity of anyone I’ve killed, and I can use it on my allies as well. Although it is a physical skill, so it won’t hide your mana from detection."</p>
<p>Jason just waved Stella’s worries away, and Jason told her that his mana was already undetectable to people below the PRIMORDIAL Rank. And from what Jason could feel, there were barely up to ten people in this entire world that had TITAN Rank mana, so there shouldn’t be a problem getting through undetected! The only thing most of these people would feel was that Jason was really strong, but they wouldn’t know how strong!</p>
<p>Stella nodded to Jason’s deduction, and she asked Jason what appearance he would like to have. Jason frowned for a moment before he said.</p>
<p>"Maybe an older man. Something that doesn’t look human. Give me a different colour skin and make me look skinnier if possible. That should make it harder for them to know it’s me."</p>
<p>Stella immediately began going through the memories of all the people that she had killed since her time as a soldier, and it took her nearly a full minute before she finally found someone that she could use for what Jason wanted! It was an old general from a world known as Carisa in the first domain. The people of Carisa had red skin with dangerous-looking black lines running along their entire body, and this general was one of the most dangerous members of their race. But unfortunately for the general and his army, he had gone up against the Avarans, and they had lost horribly!</p>
<p>Stella hesitatingly reached out to try and touch Jason’s face, and Jason just rolled his eyes and put Stella’s hands on his cheeks! Stella blushed once she made contact with Jason, and she did her best not to think about how close they were standing before she activated the skill.</p>
<p>[Astral Projection]!</p>
<p>Jason didn’t feel anything different about his body after the skill activated, but Jason looked down at his hands, and he hummed in surprise when he saw that they were now a light red colour with black fingernails. Jason told Stella to give him a mirror, and Stella produced one from her pocket dimension and handed it to Jason so he could see what he looked like! And once Jason saw his appearance, he nodded in approval!</p>
<p>Jason now looked like a dangerous warrior who had lived through many wars. Jason’s skin was red, and there were two black lines going over his eyes, down his neck and into his shirt. His hair had turned grey, and it was braided behind his head. Jason had a full grey beard that gave him a mysterious look, and when he smiled, there were two golden teeth that shone back at him.</p>
<p>’I look like the leader of some biker gang.’</p>
