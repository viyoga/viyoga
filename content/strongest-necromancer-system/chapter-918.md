<p>Jason made his way to the world of Falic, and he saw that the rain there had also stopped, and the water was quickly clearing out. As Jason moved through the world, Jason noticed a caravan with a foreign mana signature travelling a small distance away, and Jason went to meet with the travellers.</p>
<p>The caravan was made up of multiple carriages pulled by horses that had flames on their hair and tail, and the people driving it all had red skin with blindfolds around their eyes and flames burning on their heads. Jason immediately recognized them as the same race as the man who came along with Ragran to visit him from the Elder World earlier, F’yame. They were from a world called Soroch, and they were all flame users whose trade route had been blocked by Varina for a long time. But now that Varina was dead, they were able to move freely once more!</p>
<p>The moment the Sorochs saw Jason on their path, a few guards rode forward on their flaming horses, and they were about to demand Jason move away, but one of the guards there was lucky to recognize Jason from a picture of the Overlord he had seen once, and he immediately jumped down from the horse!</p>
<p>"STOP THE CARRIAGES! STOP NOW!"</p>
<p>The man shouted to all the carriages and made them stop, before he immediately went down on a knee!</p>
<p>"Lord Jason! It’s an honor to be in your presence!"</p>
<p>All the guards who hadn’t recognized Jason before immediately felt their eyes widen when they heard his name, and they all got down from their horses and went to their knees as well!</p>
<p>"LORD JASON! IT’S AN HONOR TO BE IN YOUR PRESENCE!"</p>
<p>The greeting echoed through the road, and even the caravan drivers all stood respectfully to greet Jason! The guards couldn’t believe that they would meet Jason on a random road like this, and they could only wonder what Jason was doing here! The guards had confirmed that the witch’s influence on this world had been taken care of by Jason a few days ago, and they had immediately started moving their goods once again, but they thought that Jason would have already returned to his world by now!</p>
<p>Jason raised a hand to tell everyone to calm down, and he spoke to the guard in front.</p>
<p>"What are you transporting?"</p>
<p>The guard responded in a booming voice!</p>
<p>"We are moving a cargo of dissected flame-mud Dragon organs to the Gregarious Research Institute in the world of Fayanard! We’ve had trade agreements with the Research Institute for more than fifty years now, but the recent actions from the Witch of Calamity have prevented us from delivering our goods for a long time. We thank the Overlord for helping us take care of the wicked witch!"</p>
<p>Jason’s smile strained slightly when the guard called Varina a wicked witch, but Jason knew he couldn’t fault him for thinking like that. One person’s hero was someone else’s villain, and to these Sorochs, the witch had been the devil that prevented them from selling their goods and making money. Jason simply nodded, and he told the guards that they could leave.</p>
<p>"Send my regards to F’yame when you return to your home. Tell him I recieved his invite and I’ll honor it when I am not so busy with work."</p>
<p>"YES, LORD JASON! IT’LL BE OUR GREATEST HONOR TO HOST YOU!"</p>
<p>The guards all spoke up in loud voices as they heard Jason would be coming to their world sometime in the future, and from the looks on their faces, it was obvious that they were looking forward to it!</p>
<p>’Wow, they’re like puppies. So damn excitable.’</p>
<p>Jason remembered that even F’yame had been like this as well, and Jason could only wonder if this was something common among the Sorochs. Jason waved his hand to tell the Sorochs that they could continue on their journey, and the Sorochs all saluted as Jason melted into the shadows and vanished from this world!</p>

This woman was named Enkidu, and she was the Apostle wielding the Arcane Art of Nature! Enkidu hovered in the air and looked around at the peaceful garden before her eyes finally trailed down to the man resting at the edge of the hot springs below.</p>
<p>The man had long white hair that fanned out around him in the water and a strong, sturdy build with muscles in all the right places! This was Sicario, and he was the leader of the Court of Apostles! The moment Enkidu appeared above him, Sicario opened one eye and looked up at the younger girl.</p>
<p>"You seem irritated, Enkidu. Did you have another run-in with Ulysses?"</p>

As Enkidu grew, Sicario had come to see her as his only daughter, but unfortunately for him, Enkidu didn’t think the same way at all, and she saw him as more of a romantic interest than a father!</p>

<p>"There’s something on your mind. What is it?"</p>
