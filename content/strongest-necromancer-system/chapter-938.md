<p>The new Queen of the palace despised the King with every fiber of her being, and she had tried to kill herself many times to try and get away from her husband!</p>
<p>The Queen’s maids managed to prevent her from going through with suicide every time, but the worst incident had occurred when the Queen found out that she was pregnant. No one knew exactly what the Queen drank that day, but it had caused such a big scene that multiple healers had to be brought in, and they worked for nearly three days straight just to make sure the Queen didn’t die! At the end, the child in her stomach did not survive, and it was declared that the Queen could no longer bear children. The King had whipped the Queen publicly as punishment for her actions, but even with the painful scars on her back and the blood dripping down her body, the Queen was happy that she would never have to carry this madman’s child.</p>
<p>At this point, everyone in the kingdom knew it was only a matter of time before the king took another woman as his wife, but things had taken a sudden turn for the worse when the younger brother appeared once again after he somehow managed to cure his paralysis. And this time, he came with an army! A group of savage mercenaries known as the Jamani had been hired by the younger brother, and he declared war against the current King for the right to the throne!</p>
<p>The war that took place over the next few months was unlike anything their world had ever seen! Battles raged across the entire world for days without end! Blood soaked the soil, and massive tremors travelled across the land as the powerful Jamani faced off against the fearsome L’horm warriors! The warriors of L’horm were loyal to the crown, and they all fought fiercely to protect their king, but the mercenaries possessed powers that the people of L’horm couldn’t even hope to match! Every member of the Jamani mercenary group was in the TITAN Ranks, so even though there were only about a hundred of them, they were still powerful enough to take over the entire world in just a few days!</p>
<p>The younger brother had pushed his older brother to the very edge of the world, and he was determined to kill him once and for all. But the L’horm warriors had defended their king to the very last man, and when the soldiers saw that they couldn’t win, a dedicated group had taken the royal family away from L’horm and passed through multiple Dimensional Tears to escape from the mercenaries!</p>
<p>The world of L’horm finally experienced its first moment of peace in more than a month since the battles began, but even though the people celebrated the peace, they knew it was only a matter of time before the King returned to try and reclaim the throne once more.</p>
<p>But what none of them would have expected was how the battle between the two brothers would affect their lives, and in time, the situation would get so serious that even Jaen Trush, the headmaster of the Northern Alliance Magic Academy, would be forced to act just to try and protect his world from the evil trying to consume it.</p>

<p>[Earth]</p>
<p>[The Seraphina Restaurant]</p>
<p>The Seraphina was a five-star restaurant located in the heart of Riverdale! As one of the highest-rated restaurants in the entire country, it had served many politicians and heads of state, and it was regarded as the ideal outing spot for important members of society! But today, the owners and staff of the Seraphina restaurant were far more tense than ever before!</p>
<p>"Make sure everything is perfect! If I see a speck of dust, I’ll end your life myself! Why are the floors not sparkling!? Clean them again! Take those plates and shine them till they glow! Who’s responsible for cleaning the chandelier!? Someone get up there and clean it!"</p>
<p>The head of the restaurant was a tall man with a handsome face and stern appearance, and he was currently standing in the middle of the restaurant and shouting out orders to multiple employees who were running around and trying to do everything immediately! The restaurant would be opening in a few minutes, and this was usually the time the staff would take to relax and make preparations to serve the customers properly! But there was no time for them to relax today at all! Because just a few hours ago, the head of the restaurant had received a call from the Director of the Monarch Guild, Juliet, and he was given the most shocking news he had ever received!</p>
<p>Tonight, they would be hosting the leader of the new world along with his entire family! Jason Marc was coming to Seraphina Restaurant!</p>
<p>When the Manager received the news from Juliet, he had almost ordered the entire restaurant to be closed down for the day so that they could focus on serving only Jason! This was the most important man in the world, so there was no need for them to host anyone else tonight! But Juliet had told the Manager not to bother with something like that. Jason and his family planned to eat upstairs, so they only needed to make sure there were no guests upstairs, and they could do whatever they wanted downstairs! So right now, the manager was in the upstairs hall, and he was doing his best to make sure this area was spotless for the family!</p>
<p>On the other side of the city, Jason and the rest of his family were being driven in a limo towards the Seraphina restaurant. It had already been a full week since Fiona and Betsha came to visit Earth with their families, and it was almost time for them to return to their own worlds, but the three mothers decided that they wanted to go out and eat with their children before they finally returned. The Seraphina was one of the highest-rated restaurants in the entire country, and Jason knew that only high-class people were welcomed into the restaurant, so Jason decided to bring everyone here since they didn’t want to be bothered by the other guests while eating.</p>
<p>Jason and Gina were currently sitting together on one side of the limo and talking through their mental link. Gina was leaning against Jason’s side, and Jason had a hand on her knee as he laughed at something she said. Everyone was dressed formally, so Jason had on a black suit that fit him perfectly, and Gina wore a long red gown that matched her hair and eyes with a white jacket over it. At the side, Stella was in a white gown as she helped Janet to fix up her blue gown and makeup while Georgia and Hendrix were making sure that Kai and Maya sat still.</p>
<p>Georgia looked a little uncomfortable in the black gown she was wearing since she wasn’t used to it. But Gina had bought it for her, and Georgia felt it would be disrespectful not to wear it at least once. Hendrix was also in a black suit just like Jason, and it became very obvious to everyone that the two men were brothers when they stood beside each other in their suits! Jason might be the son who looked the most like their father, but Hendrix definitely took a lot from their father’s looks as well!</p>
<p>The three mothers, Mira, Betsha and Fiona, were all dressed beautifully as well, and they all couldn’t help but smile when they saw all their children looking so good together! The three mothers were seated at one end of the limo, and they spent most of the time just admiring their beautiful children!</p>
