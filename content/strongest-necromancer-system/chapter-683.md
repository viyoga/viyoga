<p>[The World of Teram]</p>
<p>[One Dimensional Leap Away From Machina]</p>
<p>[Blood Bomb]!</p>
<p>BOOM!</p>
<p>A massive explosion rang through the sky of the Rank III world known as Teram! This world was home to a diverse number of local tribes that survived by hunting and gathering, and even though they were already aware of magic, they were still very technologically stagnant! The three TITAN Rank fighters fell through the sky as they made their way out of the Dimensional Tear, and Alexander roared as he sent sharp tendrils out like swords to try and cut the two Vympirs to pieces!</p>
<p>Clang!</p>
<p>Slash!</p>
<p>But before the swords could connect, Jason’s hand glowed with the blue glow of a Qi sword, and he deflected Alexander’s attack before lashing out with an attack of his own that sent blood flying across the air! Jason put his hand out towards Alexander and activated his innate skill!</p>
<p>[Crimson Hellfire]!</p>
<p>A red fire erupted to life around Alexander, and Jason’s smile widened as Alexander let out a maddening screech of pure pain! Hundreds of tendrils suddenly shot out from Alexander’s body as he began to attack everything around him blindly, and Jason pulled back a little to allow Alexander to exhaust himself, but Gillian didn’t seem to care as she charged forward again! Gillian easily dodged around all the tendrils, and she activated a Blood skill as a long scythe appeared in her hand! Gillian pulled back her scythe to split Alexander in half, but Jason noticed that Alexander was about to stab Gillian in the back with his tendrils and he charged forward and slammed into both Alexander and Gillian, and they all fell into the large river below in a massive splash!</p>
<p>Crackle!</p>
<p>Almost immediately, the world of Teram began to freeze over! The river turned into ice at a rapid pace, and the ice travelled over the land and began to freeze the two large towns that were located close by! The people in the towns had no idea what was going on, and they could barely even run before they were immediately turned into silver statues!</p>
<p>BOOM!</p>
<p>A massive force broke free from the frozen river as the three TITANs emerged, and Alexander’s head transformed into a strange robotic appearance that had a massive gun attached to the front! The gun began to glow with a bright red light, and he unleashed a blast of red energy towards Jason and Gillian! Jason crossed his hands and slammed his feet into the ice before he was hit with the attack, and he grunted as he felt his arms burning! But Gillian simply put her hand forward, and the attack slammed into her and split to either side harmlessly!</p>
<p>TWOOOM!</p>
<p>The attack flew across the landscape and tore a massive trench into the ground before it travelled into the horizon and exploded in an enormous fireball that destroyed another three towns! The three TITANs had only been in this world for less than two minutes, but in that time, they had killed hundreds of thousands of people and caused enough collateral damage to set this world back for centuries! The attack from Alexander continued for at least a minute as he tried his best to kill Jason and Gillian, but once he noticed that the attack wasn’t working, he quickly sprouted his angel wings and took off again! Gillian immediately shot off after him, and Jason activated a skill!</p>
<p>[Time Dilation] has been activated!</p>
<p>BOOM!</p>
<p>Jason suddenly vanished from where he had been standing with a massive shockwave, and he appeared right in front of Alexander before lashing out with a punch that echoed through the world like an explosion! Alexander flew down like a rocket, and he cursed as he realised that he was flying straight towards Gillian! Gillian’s lips split into a mad grin, and she pulled back her fist and lashed out with a punch!</p>
<p>KA-BOOM!</p>
<p>A shockwave suddenly rippled through the world as the sound barrier was broken multiple times, and Alexander flew back like a bullet! Alexander spun through the air rapidly as he tore through a large town, and he spread his wings out forcefully to stop his flight! Alexander quickly sent enough mana to open a Dimensional Tear behind him, but Alexander’s eyes widened as he noticed both Gillian and Jason flying towards him like demons straight out of hell! Jason and Gillian slammed into Alexander, and they all flew through the Dimensional Tear together!</p>
<p>This was how the three TITANs went on a battle spree across the Primordial Domain for the next few hours! The three fighters were some of the strongest beings in this entire Primordial Domain, so there was no chance that anyone would be capable of standing in the way of such a large-scale battle, and yet the damage that they caused in their fight was nearly immeasurable! In just the first hour alone, more than ten worlds had suffered millions of gold coins’ worth of damage! There had been hundreds of thousands killed in these worlds, and many of them had suffered massive natural disasters caused by one attack or another! In the next two hours, that number of worlds had tripled, and it wasn’t long before that number began to approach the hundreds! Normally, the council of Nine and One would be the ones responsible for dealing with situations like these! The Council member in charge of overseeing the affairs of a particular domain would always interfere if there was a situation that was affecting so many worlds at the same time! But right now, that same Overlord that was meant to watch over this world was involved in the destruction!</p>
<p>BOOM!</p>
<p>BOOM!</p>
<p>BOOM!</p>
<p>The sound of explosions and destruction was prevalent across the many worlds, and people were running and hiding in their homes while praying to their gods that they wouldn’t be killed by these forces of nature! Finally, after more than a hundred dimensional jumps, Jason felt something stir in his heart!</p>
<p>’Give me back my body.’</p>
<p>"!"</p>
<p>Jason’s heart skipped a beat as a sudden voice echoed in his head, and for a split second, there was a flash of black that crossed over his eyes! Jason shook his head, and his eyes turned back to red, but that singular moment of distraction was enough for Alexander to lash out with an attack that slammed into Jason! Jason was thrown back like a bullet, and he grunted as he felt his heart skip another beat!</p>
<p>’I said, give me back my body. You don’t belong here.’</p>
<p>"Shut up and let me fight!"</p>
<p>Jason slammed his feet into the ground and ran forward before blasting off the ground and charging right back into the fight! The voice in Jason’s heart went silent all of a sudden, and Jason was grateful for that, but just as Jason reached the battle, he felt his eyes widen when he saw that Alexander had stabbed Gillian in the stomach with multiple tendrils!</p>
<p>"MISTRESS!"</p>
<p>Jason screamed at the top of his lungs, and he quickly flew to catch Gillian as Alexander threw her back! But in the middle of his flight, Jason’s heart suddenly skipped another beat, and this time, Jason wasn’t able to stop his eyes from turning from red back to black!</p>
<p>"Urk!"</p>
<p>Jason caught Gillian as blood spilt from his mouth, and he felt his entire body fighting against him as his eyes started to flicker between red and black constantly! This was Jason’s soul that was fighting back for its true place! All this time, the Jason that had been acting was the servant of Gillian, was the one who had been created after the Toxin separated Jason’s soul from his body! Normally, Jason’s true self would never be able to fight back like this because the Grim Reaper would have taken his soul away, but since Jason managed to secure his soul inside his magic, he was able to put up a serious fight against the control of the Changeling!</p>
