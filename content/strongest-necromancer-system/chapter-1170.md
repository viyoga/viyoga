<p>The sky began to darken as the lightning dragon gathered mana from the surroundings, and lightning arched through the sky like millions of blue snakes. The golden dragon let out a mighty roar towards the sky as it tried to get up and escape, but it was forced back to the ground as thousands of subordinates climbed onto its body and held it down.</p>
<p>The golden dragon was shocked. Don’t these fools know that they would also die if the attack hit? The golden dragon didn’t understand what these enemies were doing. But it was obvious that the enemies didn’t care about their lives at all. It didn’t matter if they were hit by the attack! And it didn’t matter if they died! So long as they could fulfill the wishes of their King, they would gladly give up their lives!</p>
<p>The lightning arched through the sky like the bellows of a vengeful god, and congregated into a swirling mass of blue light that created a spinning vortex over the body of the golden dragon, and in the next moment, all the lightning fell.</p>
<p>BZZZZTTTT!</p>
<p>Phantasmal lightning was completely different from regular lightning. Jason had felt the full brunt of that attack once before, so Jason understood fully well what a fully charged Phantasmal lightning was capable of doing to a person. But even Jason didn’t expect the absolute carnage that suddenly erupted across the island. The lightning completely eviscerated the island and sent all the weaker subordinates flying through the air with electric burns travelling across their bodies. The Golden Dragon let out a massive roar as it was cooked from the inside out by the skill, and the entire region seemed to be constantly glitching with electric current flying through the air.</p>
<p>It took almost a full minute for the electricity to finally die down, and once that was done, Jason noticed that the Golden Dragon was alive but seriously injured.
