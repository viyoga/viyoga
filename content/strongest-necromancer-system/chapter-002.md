<p>The gate that the Five Rulers closed down was colored Green and it was seen as the highest possible Gate that could open in the world.</p>
<p>Gates were color graded based on how dangerous they were, with the weakest being the Red Gate, followed by the Orange Gate, Yellow Gate, and then finally, the Green Gate. People speculated that there were far stronger gates than the Green Gate, but no one had ever seen one before, so humanity as a whole accepted that green was the highest level a Gate could reach.</p>
<p>After the Five Rulers, there was a surge in the number of superhumans all over the world. Scientists believed that it was due to the sudden influx of mana from the Gate that the humans were developing such abilities, but no one could really explain it since it was so sudden.</p>
<p>The superhumans who gained Gifts would develop one ability or another. Some of them gained the ability to use fire-based attacks. Others gained the ability to control objects with their minds, or the ability to heal any wound instantaneously.</p>
<p>But after a lot of superhumans emerged, the abilities in existence were eventually boiled down to five major divisions.</p>
<p>Brawlers - Those with physical-based abilities or augmentations.</p>
<p>Emitters - Those with elemental attacks or mana emissions.</p>
<p>Supporters - Those with abilities that boost or reduce the strength of others.</p>
<p>Healers - Those with abilities that can aid in healing wounds.</p>
<p>Special - Those with abilities that cannot be classified into any of the other four categories.</p>
<p>All the abilities in the world could be put under one of these five categories, and the government began a system where they would register anyone who developed an ability and make sure that they were following strict rules so they didn't misuse their powers.</p>
<p>The world rapidly changed after Gifts and Gates appeared.</p>
<p>It was discovered that the only way to grow stronger as a superhuman was by defeating the monsters found in the Gate. The monsters granted the superhumans a boost in power based on their potential, allowing them to surpass their limits and become even stronger if they killed enough of them.</p>
<p>It was also found that the monsters would leave behind something known as a Drop. The monsters found in the Gates were known to always leave behind a Magic Crystal as well as other less common magic items. This magic crystal contained the mana essence of the beast and it could be used to produce energy, or to enhance the weapons created for the superhumans.</p>
<p>And the bodies of the creatures could also be used to craft weapons and magic artifacts like magic staffs or healing items.</p>
<p>The amount of energy or mana that could be retrieved from these crystals made it a precious commodity amongst humans, and it wasn't long before people began offering huge sums of money to anyone who would go into the Gate to retrieve these crystals, throwing the world into a new era of Gate Diving.</p>
<p>The Five Rulers created their own guilds, cementing their place in legend as the five strongest guilds in the world. Then, many superhumans began to create their own guilds as well and they tried to recruit many superhumans and band together to head into the Gate.</p>
<p>These guilds registered with the government for a license and they became the only viable way for a superhuman to legally enter a Gate, reinforcing their place as the cornerstone of the new world.</p>
<p>They were graded in the same way superhumans were graded, from F Rank guilds all the way up to S Rank guilds who were at the very top of the food chain.</p>
<p>Right now in America, there were more than twenty guilds operating at the same time and fighting for jurisdiction and permission to enter the Gates. Out of these guilds, three of them were seen as the apex guilds.</p>
<p>They were the A Rank Green Hornet Guild, the B Rank Snow Storm Guild, and the S Rank BloodRaven Guild.</p>
<p>And since the BloodRaven was being led by one of the Five Rulers, the woman hailed as the strongest Supporter in the world, they were regarded as the strongest in America.</p>
<p>Jason didn't really care much for superhumans or what they did. He knew that most people in the world saw them as heroes who were risking their lives to save others, but Jason didn't feel the same way. As far as Jason was concerned, they were only doing all this because of the money it would make them.</p>
<p>Being a superhuman was now regarded as the highest paying job in the world, with some of the highest paid superhumans raking in hundreds of millions every month. It was abysmal to realize just how much money people were willing to pay for Magic Crystals.</p>
<p>On the screen, the news channel showed the members of the C Ranked Black Falcon Guild arriving on a large truck.
