The Wind Arrows struck various Imperial Guards, and some of them were even thrown off their dragon, and their dragons were forced to break out from the chase to go and catch them! <p>[100%]</p>
<p>It was like a rush of power suddenly rose from somewhere deep inside Gina! The power filled her entire body and surged forward like a tsunami as it slammed into an invisible wall! Gina tried to reach for that power, but for some reason, she couldn’t! There was an invisible wall standing between her and that power, and there was no way for her to reach it, no matter how hard she tried! Gina knew that this power was going to make a massive difference and she was almost desperate to reach it! If she could just reach, Gina knew that she could change the layout of the entire fight!</p>
<p>’Jason! Jason, I need help!’</p>
<p>Gina called out to Jason in desperation! She had tried all this time not to call out to Jason because she knew that he might be in the middle of a fight, and she didn’t want to distract him, but the desperation inside Gina forced her to call out for her lover! <p>’I need more power!’</p>

Jason smiled as he realised what had happened! Gina felt her power increasing, but she couldn’t reach that power because he hadn’t added the upgrade points that she needed to go to the next level!
