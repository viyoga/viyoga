<p>Ding!</p>
<p>[The system has noticed an increase in the user’s Qi levels. The combination of Qi and mana has been known to be extremely destructive. The system does not advise the user to combine these two power sources for his own safety.]</p>
<p>’Hey, what the hell are you doing!? Why are you calling on your Qi when you’re still using mana!?’</p>
<p>Esdath’s shocked voice echoed from Jason’s Alternate Dimension, but Jason ignored him as he felt his entire body heating up from the sudden power that filled every single cell! It was like Jason had drunk an entire gallon of adrenaline, and he realised that he needed to pour out all this energy before it completely destroyed him! And there was only one place where it could go! Jason shot all of the energy down into the [Death’s Mirage], and at the next moment, Jason’s vision faded to white!</p>
<p>On the other side of the battlefield, Sicario felt an intense, unbelievable surge of power flowing out from Jason’s sword, and he didn’t even need to think before he turned and flew off like a rocket! And then the world exploded!</p>
<p>KA-TOOOOOOMMM!!</p>
<p>BOOOOOOMMMM!!</p>
<p>It was like a clap of thunder echoed through the entire world before a sudden fireball the size of a miniature moon rose up and blasted through the clouds like a beacon of destruction! The entire world vibrated as the intense explosion shook it to the very core, and even though he had already flown far away, Sicario was still not able to get away quickly enough to avoid the shockwave that slammed into him! The intense wind pressure threw him back with intense force, and he cursed as he put his hand up to his face to block the wind! But before he could even get used to that, a massive lava tsunami suddenly rose high into the sky and began to surge across the world in a wave of pure destruction! Sicario knew that the lava wouldn’t kill him since he was a TITAN, but he was still not interested in being burned by the intense heat, so he quickly flew up to make sure the tsunami missed him, and he watched on in shock as the tsunami continued on its path across Helion and swallowed Jason and the Beast in its wave.</p>
<p>’If things are this bad after the explosion, I wonder what happened to Jason and the beast. Did Jason survive this, or is the limit of Jason’s fate?’</p>
<p>Sicario quickly took off towards the origin point of the explosion to see if Jason was alright or not. But even as he went, he couldn’t help but think that there was a chance that Jason actually died in there! An explosion of this magnitude would kill nearly anyone!</p>
<p>’I suppose we’ll see just how strong your fate truly is, Jason Marc. Are you destined for greater things, or is it your destiny to die in this molten graveyard?’</p>
<p>....</p>
<p>GASP!</p>
<p>Gina’s eyes shot open as she felt an intense surge of emotions passing through her mental link with Jason, and she immediately sat up in bed! She put her hand up to her forehead as an intense headache pounded against her skull, and she looked to the other side of the bed with a worried frown. Jason was still not back from his mission, and Gina could feel the first strings of extreme worry wrapping around her heart! What happened to Jason?</p>
<p>’Jason... Jason, are you there? Jason?’</p>
<p>Gina tried to reach out to Jason through their mental link, but no matter how much she called out, there was no response! Gina immediately tried contacting one of Jason’s Legion members who was with him, but she could not get through to them either. Gina knew that the only reason she could talk to the other Legion members was because she used Jason’s mind as a sort of connector that allowed them to communicate, so the fact that the subordinates were also not answering her just made the fear inside her grow even more. What the hell did Jason really get himself into? Didn’t he say that this was just a simple mission that would be over before even an hour? It’s already been several hours, and Gina still hasn’t heard from him.</p>
<p>Gina knew that Jason wasn’t dead. She could still feel his life force deep inside her magic, and their connection was as strong as ever. But for some reason, he just couldn’t respond. That could only mean that something had gone wrong, and Gina immediately stood up and began to change into something more suitable! Gina already knew that Jason had been going to Yortsed, so she decided that she would search for him there first. Once Gina put on her trousers and a shirt, she jumped out the window of the room and fell through the sky! The Skyking immediately appeared from the shadows and caught her in the air, and it released a loud cry as it began heading for the location of the next dimensional tear!</p>
