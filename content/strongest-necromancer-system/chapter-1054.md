<p>The gathering of the Vympirs did not take that long at all. Since all the Vympirs here were under the direct control of one of the elders on the Vympir council, the elders simply had to send a direct order, and all the Vympirs gathered at the meeting place! Before even an hour, Jason got a message from Lamina informing him that the Vympirs had gathered, and Jason vanished from the mountain and reappeared in the massive open field where hundreds of thousands of Vympirs had gathered!</p>
<p>The Vympirs all recognized Jason as the wielder of the Blood Oath of Fire, and they all fell to one knee and greeted Jason with the respect he deserved! Jason raised a hand and told them to get to their feet before he put his hand out and opened the door leading into his Alternate Dimension.</p>
<p>"You’ll all stay within my artificial space until we arrive at Lunaris. Make sure you don’t cause any issues with the beasts living in here, and ignore the old man living alone in a mansion. He’s crazy."</p>
<p>’Hey! Who are you calling crazy!? Show some respect for your teacher!’</p>
<p>Jason huffed in amusement when Esdath shouted at him, and Jason just told the Vympirs to begin heading in. The Vympirs walked through the portal in front of Jason, and Jason could feel them appearing one by one in his Alternate Dimension. Thankfully, there was an immeasurable amount of space in this Alternate Dimension, so even though there were thousands of giant beasts living in there, Jason had more than enough space for all the Vympirs to stay!</p>
<p>Just like Jason had predicted, some Vympirs attempted to bring some unnecessary things inside with them, and Jason told them that they didn’t need to carry any luggage! This evacuation was simply to make sure that the Celestial of Death didn’t take any lives! All their luggage and belongings would still be here when they returned.</p>
<p>This made a lot of people put their luggage away, but there were some who simply did not want to leave their luggage behind, and Jason only allowed them through because he didn’t have time to bother about them at all! By the time Jason was done, only Lamina was left behind, and Lamina bowed to Jason deeply as a surge of gratitude poured out of her!</p>
<p>"Thank you for aiding us in the evacuation, Lord Jason. We would’ve surely lost a significant portion of our population if not for you."</p>
<p>Jason smiled at her and told her that it was okay.</p>
<p>"Gillian left them under my care, so they are my responsibility. It is only right that I help you when you really need it. You should be fine traveling across worlds with me, right?"</p>
<p>Lamina nodded seriously. As Jason’s subordinate, Lamina was a Blue Sun Vympir, so she could survive the yellow or red suns of most worlds without any issue! Jason told Lamina to lead the way, and the two Vympirs took off towards Lunaris and left the deserted world of Nox behind!</p>

<p>[Radagan]</p>
<p>[With Riyala]</p>
<p>Riyala had a frown on her face as she walked through the hall of the royal castle. Beside her, two attendants were giving her information about the recent events happening across the Primordial Expanse. Riyala had only arrived back in Radagan a few days ago, and she thought that she would be able to get right back to work now that she had relaxed on vacation for a while. But Riyala never expected something like this to happen!</p>
<p>"The Celestial of Death had already crossed the midpoint of the Ninth Domain. We believe it will enter the Eighth Domain within the next two days."</p>
<p>One of the attendants spoke to Riyala in a hurried voice, and Riyala felt the fear in her heart growing even more! There weren’t many things that could terrify Riyala in this world! Especially since the only personal connections she had were to her dragon and Jason. But the mere mention of the Celestial of Death put an undeniable fear into her heart!</p>
<p>As the priestess of Radagan, Riyala had inherited the memories of all the previous priestesses as well as their experiences, and after looking through all of them, Riyala was able to retrieve the memories of the priestess who lived during the era of the Celestial Hunt. According to the memories, the Celestial of Death was the single most terrifying thing to ever pass through the Primordial Expanse! Nothing was safe while the Celestial was free. Mortals, Titans, Primordials, Spirits, Ghosts, even undead creatures! They could all be killed the moment the Celestial passes over them! So right now, Riyala was one of the only people in the Primordial Expanse who understood just how serious this situation was!</p>
<p>"How are the evacuation plans going? Have we been contacted by the Elder World yet?"</p>
<p>Riyala asked the attendant on her other side, and the man nodded and said they had. The King of Radagan had been in contact with the leader of the Elder World for a few hours now, and they had already finished the evacuation discussions!. So people were already preparing to move from all the cities on Radagan. Since Radagan was in the direct path of the Celestial of Death, they all needed to evacuate to make sure they didn’t fall victim to Death’s touch.</p>
<p>Riyala let out a long breath, and in that moment her thoughts went towards Jason and her sister-wives. Riyala had spoken to Jason a few hours ago, and he told her that Gina and Hesabeth were both safe on Minori, but Riyala couldn’t help but worry about Jason. As the Overlord, Jason was responsible for making sure that all the worlds in this domain were taken care of, and Riyala knew that Jason might even have to join the battle efforts to handle the Celestial of Death later! Jason might be strong, but Riyala knew that even someone like Jason wouldn’t be able to resist the hand of Death!</p>
<p>If Jason goes anywhere close to the black cloud, then he would also be killed!</p>
<p>Ba-Dump!</p>
<p>A sudden tremor went through Riyala’s heart when she thought about Jason dying, and Riyala put her hand over her chest and took in multiple breaths to calm herself! No, she couldn’t think like that. Jason was alive and well, and he would survive this! Riyala just had to focus on evacuating her own people first!</p>
<p>"Priestess! There is an issue! The dragons refuse to listen to us!"</p>
<p>A loud voice suddenly called out from the front, and Riyala turned to see one of the high-ranking generals coming towards her. The woman pointed towards the Flaming Forest and spoke.</p>
<p>"The free dragons do not wish to leave their nest behind. We’re managing to evacuate all the contracted dragons, but we can’t do anything about the others."</p>
<p>Riyala’s frown deepened once she heard this! Riyala already knew that the dragons in the Flaming Forest would be an issue, and in truth, Riyala couldn’t blame them. That was their home, and they had been there for multiple generations, so there was no way they would agree to leave just because someone asked them to! Riyala told her attendants to continue keeping records of what was happening before two black wings tore out from her back and she took off towards the Flaming Forest!</p>
<p>At the edge of the Flaming Forest, Riyala saw multiple soldiers who were being attacked by a large male dragon! The soldiers had tried to get into the forest, but the dragons immediately attacked them and chased them out to protect the females! Riyala floated down to the ground as the largest male dragon let out an earth-shattering roar, and Riyala raised her hand towards the dragon.</p>
