<p>The giant Skrill was more than a hundred times larger than Jason, and it had a wingspan that was wide enough to cover a small island! So when it appeared, it managed to easily block Jason from the sunrays, and Jason took in a deep breath as he felt his power rising up to its regular level once more! The power that came with it was amazing, but Jason couldn’t help but think that being a Vympir fucking sucked!</p>
<p>ROARRR!!</p>
<p>A loud roar suddenly echoed from somewhere in the distance, and Jason turned around sharply to see a large dust cloud heading in his direction! Jason’s eyes bled to red as he tried to get a better look at what was coming towards him, and he cursed when he saw an unbelievable horde of monsters charging in his direction! There were millions of them, and there was a desperation on their faces that showed they weren’t heading towards Jason because they wanted to attack him! The monsters had all felt the intense heat from the sun, and they knew that their world was coming to an end soon, so they were running out of sheer desperation to try to escape their fate! Jason grit his teeth in annoyance, and he just decided that this didn’t concern him! It wasn’t his fault if the world was willing to destroy an entire species just because of the whims of one woman!</p>
<p>’Let’s go! I need to get out of here before the sun explodes! Make sure you keep up with me and block me from any sunrays!’</p>
<p>Jason gave an order to the Skrill before he blasted off, and the Skrill let out a loud screech as it took off after Jason! The Skrill managed to easily block Jason from the sun, and since it was a TITAN Rank beast, it easily withstood the intense heat that was spreading through the world as the sun reached its imminent destruction. Jason glanced at the system and he saw that there were still about four minutes before the system matched the mana frequency, so all he needed to do was stay ahead of the approaching stampede and stay out of the sun.</p>
<p>BOOM!!</p>
<p>BUOOHHHH!</p>
<p>A loud sound echoed from the side, and Jason looked over to see a giant tentacle reaching out to try to grab him! Jason avoided the tentacle, but unfortunately, his Skrill wasn’t so lucky, and it was caught by the monster and dragged into the sand below! The monster that appeared looked like a giant octopus, but its skin was dry and it could use its tentacles to move through the sand like it was water! The moment the Skrill was dragged away, Jason cursed as smoke started to rise from his skin, and he immediately called on his Legion!</p>
<p>[Mana Blast]!</p>
<p>BOOM!</p>
<p>The world shook as the Skrill unleashed its attack on the giant octopus, and the Octopus let out another loud bellow as it tried to swallow the Skrill, but multiple Rook Rank subordinates appeared and blew up in a massive [Kamikaze], and the attacks finally forced the octopus to let go of the Skrill! The Skrill quickly went back to protecting Jason as they travelled across the desert! The sun in the sky had now turned completely blue, and Jason knew that getting exposed at all would completely fry him! Jason glanced at his system tab at the side and he cursed when he saw that there were still two minutes before the mana synchronization was complete! But Jason didn’t have any more time left, and the heat in the world suddenly spiked to an absurd level before the sky lit up in a massive fireball as the sun exploded!</p>
<p>The explosion was extremely quiet, and if you weren’t looking up, you would never know that the sun had just blown up, but the shockwave that tore through the world a second later was like a wave of pure destruction! The monsters that had been chasing Jason were immediately swept up by the intense wind! Jason grunted as the Skrill was also pulled away by the wind, and he felt a burning sensation travel across his body as the sunrays slammed into him! Jason’s skin started to burn as smoke rose from his body, and he quickly called on multiple dragons to fly overhead and protect him! The dragons formed a protective cover, and Jason glanced back to see a massive wave of blue sunfire racing in his direction!</p>
<p>’I need that Dimensional Tear right now!’</p>
<p>Jason shouted to the system as he realised that the blue flames were moving far too quickly, but the system only said that it needed another minute so Jason cursed as he called on the Beast of the Apocalypse! The Beast appeared from the Alternate Dimension and stood between Jason and the flames, and its cold aura filled the air as it tried to fight back against the flames! But the flames were literal sunfire, powered by the raw mana of the world itself, and they easily tore through the cold aura and washed over the beast! But thankfully, the beast’s body was able to withstand the flames for a while, and that bought Jason a few extra seconds as he used the beast’s body as a shield! The dragons that had been protecting Jason were immediately incinerated by the flames since they were so weak, and Jason called on both Ragnar and Trigon immediately! Jason’s face and arms had already turned black from the sunfire, and Jason grit his teeth as he looked over at the system tab showing a timer!</p>
<p>[0:00:35]</p>
<p>[0:00:30]</p>
<p>[0:00:20]</p>
<p>[0:00:15]</p>
<p>’Come on, come on, come on, come on...’</p>
<p>Jason already had his mana ready as he waited for the exact moment when the countdown would end, and just as the countdown was about to hit zero, the entire world rumbled as a giant hand pulled itself out from the desert sand!</p>
<p>ROARRRR!!!</p>
<p>[0:00:00]</p>
<p>[The system has completed the synchronization.]</p>
<p>’Finally!’</p>
<p>Jason didn’t even bother trying to figure out what sort of monster was about to appear, and he simply poured out enough mana to create a Dimensional Tear and jumped right through!</p>
<p>....</p>
<p>Jason stumbled out of a Dimensional Tear on another world and he immediately felt his energy levels rising as he enjoyed the warmth of the yellow sun on his skin. Fuck, that was terrifying. Jason let out a harsh breath and then he looked around and saw that he was in the middle of a large city. There were tall creatures with green skin and large builds that were similar to Orcs walking around. Every now and then, one of the Orcs would turn around to stare at Jason, and Jason couldn’t blame them since he looked absolutely terrible. His clothes were burnt, and there were black patches on his skin from the blue sun. There was smoke rising from his hair, and he had sand everywhere. Jason coughed out some smoke and then he clicked his tongue as he looked around for any sign of danger. Jason knew that the world could track him to any part of the Primordial Domain, so he didn’t have any hopes of hiding in this city. Jason needed to get out of here before the world did something that would end up with this city being destroyed! Jason took off as he started heading out of the city, but a strange alarm suddenly rang through the air, and a lot of the civilians standing around immediately moved away from Jason while pointing to him in shock.</p>
<p>"He’s using mana in the city!"</p>
<p>"Doesn’t he know that’s illegal! The Mana Force will be here soon!"</p>
<p>"Come on, let’s get out of here before a fight breaks out!"</p>
<p>In this world, foreigners were restricted from using mana, so the Orcs were shocked to see Jason floating in the air.</p>
