<p>Jason was going to have to be very careful to make sure he survived against the monsters he would be meeting in the future! Jason let out a calming breath, and he put a hand on his chest as he felt the surge of mana flowing through him calmly. Somewhere in the middle of Jason’s chest, just under his heart, Jason could feel a new power source that hadn’t been there before. It was like a ball filled with a thick jelly-like substance, and Jason didn’t have to think for long to realise that this was Primordial Essence. Every time Jason absorbed mana from the environment, the mana would first go through this part of his body before it would spread to the rest of him.</p>
<p>[There have been new additions to the user’s system tab]</p>
<p>——</p>
<p>Primordial Essence Rank: 1 (50%)</p>
<p>Primordial Essence Purity: 10%</p>
<p>——</p>
<p>[In order to advance from [PRIMORDIAL Rank I] to [PRIMORDIAL Rank II], the user must absorb enough Primordial Essence to increase the Primordial Essence Rank to 100%. But the user must raise the purity of his Primordial Essence if he wishes to become stronger than other PRIMORDIAL beings in the same rank.]</p>
<p>——</p>
<p>Jason read through the new information quickly, and he understood it easily enough. Now that Jason was a Primordial, simply killing TITANs and mortals wouldn’t be enough for him to rise through the ranks anymore! If Jason wanted to get stronger, then Jason would need to specifically target other Primordials so that he could absorb their Primordial Essence and increase the purity and rank of his Essence! Jason let out a huff as he finally got to his feet, and he looked out into the distance! At that moment, Jason came to the absolute realisation that this world would no longer be enough to support him! Jason loved Earth, and he wanted to help them as much as possible, but Jason also knew that staying on Earth for longer than necessary was only going to stagnate his growth! If Jason really wanted to grow, then he needed to go out into the Primordial Expanse more often and look for powerful creatures to fight!</p>
<p>Jason hummed calmly, and he decided that he would just keep his plans on hold for now. Jason promised himself that he would stay on Earth for at least a year so that he could help them to go from a Rank II world to a Rank III world, and Jason was not going to change his mind about that! But once that year is over, Jason would begin a new journey around the Primordial Expanse! And this time, Jason’s mission wouldn’t be to just grow as a Primordial! Jason would enter the world of the Primordial Beings to find out exactly where his father was, and Jason would make sure that before long, he would surpass the Primordials and enter into the realm of Celestials!</p>

<p>[With the Legion and Superhumans]</p>
<p>The battle against the Bengari had been going on for more than twenty minutes now! Ever since the Bengari appeared from the Dimensional Tear, they immediately began to launch attacks against the Legion that nearly decimated every one of them! Although there were many subordinates in the Legion, most of them were still Rook Rank I’s and II’s, and that meant that they were only as strong as SSS Ranks! But the Bengari army was made up of thousands of TITAN Rank soldiers who were willing to lay their lives down on the battlefield! The sheer intensity of the Bengari’s attack was enough to decimate most of the Legion!</p>
<p>But even though the weaker members of the Legion were struggling against the warrior women, the stronger Legion members didn’t have that issue at all!</p>
<p>ROARRR!</p>
<p>Ragnar roared loudly as it soared over the battlefield and unleashed a massive blast of fire towards a group on the ground! There were Bengari soldiers as well as Legion subordinates in that group, but Ragnar didn’t care since he knew that the Legion subordinates could simply revive after they died! The battlefield lit up in a massive explosion as Ragnar’s mana landed, and the Bengari were incapable of catching their breath as multiple other dragons appeared from behind Ragnar and unleashed blasts of their own! On another side of the battlefield, the Sovereign, as well as other Dragonborn subordinates, were fighting against a group of Bengari who were using the spear as their primary weapon! Since the Bengari were a warrior race, they had various divisions in their ranks, and each division worked as a well-oiled machine to take care of any enemy on the battlefield! The Bengari spear soldiers were one of the elite groups within the army, and they each had power that was on par with a high-level TITAN Rank I! But even with this immense power, they were still no match for the Sovereign! The Sovereign was already a Bishop Rank II, and that meant he was at least as strong as a TITAN Rank II!</p>
<p>The Sovereign’s sword slashed across the neck of a Bengari warrior and took her head off! The Sovereign dodged the thrust from another warrior, and he lashed out with a kick that sent the Bengari flying into a mountain in the distance!</p>
<p>BOOM!</p>
<p>A massive dragon appeared from the sky and let out a spray of flames, and the Sovereign simply stood and allowed the fire to wash over him as the Bengari screamed in pain! As a dragonborn, the Sovereign was more resistant to fire, so he used the opportunity and urged all the other dragonborn forward to kill as many enemies as possible! A loud scream echoed from behind the Sovereign as a Bengari warrior lashed out with a spear, but the Bengari was blindsided by a Machina who fired his blaster at her and sent her flying into the distance! The Sovereign looked around at all the dead Bengari, and he decided that this number was enough for him to finally use the Legion skill!</p>
<p>[Remote Resurrection] has been activated!</p>
<p>DOON!</p>
<p>A surge of mana travelled across the area, and the Bengari warriors who had been killed were suddenly pulled out from their bodies and into the real world! All the other warriors felt their eyes narrow in anger as they saw their sisters joining the Legion’s number, and the Sovereign simply raised his sword towards the Bengari and sent a mental order for the Legion to attack!</p>
<p>ROARR!!</p>
<p>The entire army charged forward as one, and the two armies slammed into each other like massive waves!</p>
<p>Up in the sky, Gina was currently eradicating every single Bengari around her! Gina was being held up by a massive tornado swirling beneath her, and her eyes were glowing with a green light as she called on all the mana the world could give her! As a Sage, Gina didn’t have to ever worry about mana consumption, and the only people Gina had to worry about in a battle were those who were at least one rank higher than her! If Queen Diane had also been on this battlefield, then things would’ve been far harder for Gina, but the Bengari warriors here were only made up of TITAN Rank I and TITAN Rank II soldiers, and since Gina was a Rank II TITAN as well, she knew that no one here was her match! Right now, Gina was a one-woman army with enough firepower to turn the tide of this war by herself!</p>
<p>Massive blades of air constantly formed in the air and fired in every direction around Gina! The blades slashed through any Bengari that dared to come close to her, and the Bengari were forced to start using defensive artefacts to avoid getting shot out of the air!</p>
