<p>[The World of Radagan]</p>
<p>The people of Radagan had come a long way from the period when they were ruled by the Sovereign! The battle against the Sovereign had destroyed a significant portion of the main city of Radagan, and many of the smaller cities had suffered serious infrastructural damage that would take a very long time to repair! But even after they suffered such a serious setback, the dragonborns of Radagan didn’t allow it to hold them back! Over the past few months, the dragonborns of Radagan had been working very hard to try and rebuild their cities and replenish the money that the Sovereign had wasted from their treasury! The main source of income for the dragonborn was the dragonstone that they mined from the Yellow Ash Mountain in the Eastern plains, and every able-bodied person in Radagan was working hard to retrieve as much dragonstone as possible!</p>
<p>But even though the dragonborns were all working hard to mine the dragonstone, they had run into a problem with their sales. Radagan sold most of their dragonstones to five worlds within the sixth domain, and that was their main source of income for the stones, but the sudden increase in the number of dragonstones didn’t mean that the other worlds would be interested in buying more dragonstone than they needed, and the dragonborns ended up having a massive inventory of dragonstone lying around in Radagan that they couldn’t sell! For the past few months, Riyala had been working hard to try and secure more trade deals with worlds in this domain so they could make more money, and although she was making progress, Riyala could tell that she would need to make many more deals before they started making profits from the dragonstones.</p>
<p>So when Hesabeth told Riyala that she was willing to buy a significant amount of dragonstone for her processing plants in Minori, Riyala had been over the fucking moon! Hesabeth’s deal with Riyala was very beneficial to the dragonborns because, as the Overlord of the seventh domain, Hesabeth could also help Riyala refer the dragonstone to other worlds in her domain! All Riyala had to do was make sure she did a good job in providing Hesabeth with all the dragonstones that she needed, and Riyala was sure that Hesabeth would recommend the dragonstone to anyone else who needed a fire-based mana stone!</p>
<p>"Priestess, we’ve sent out the first shipment to Minori, and it should be arriving within a few weeks. The miners have already prepared the second shipment, and we plan to send it in a few days so the two will arrive around the same time."</p>
<p>A dragonborn woman spoke quickly while following behind Riyala as they walked through a large processing plant on the outskirts of the Radagan central city. The processing plant was located in an enormous building that took up more than a thousand acres of land! The entire building was exceptionally hot with hundreds of furnaces blasting scorching dragon fire at the raw dragonstone that had been mined from the mountains! The freshly mined stones were dirty and rough, and they needed to be heated up and cleaned before they would be ready for shipment. In the past, the process had been done by a group of dragons in a valley far away from any city, but after seeing the developments on Earth, Riyala had brought up a plan to create this factory, and she got help from a few engineers on Earth to make sure it was perfect!</p>
