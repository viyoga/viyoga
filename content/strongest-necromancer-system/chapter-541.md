The Machina tried their best to fight back against Jason’s Legion, but even though the Machina had been augmented with mechanical parts, they were still mortal beings underneath all the machines, and this meant they were creatures that still feared death! Because the Legion did not fear death, and the only way you could face the Legion on even footing was by abandoning the fear of death completely. But Jason also knew that using up so much mana at once was going to leave him very vulnerable. If Jason just used up all two million mana points that he had, that would drop him all the way down to zero at once!</p>
<p>’I’ll have to use one of my Mana Storage Runes.’</p>
<p>This thought crossed Jason’s mind before he told Azrael to go ahead with the anti-rune Key and tell him when it was done. Jason had been creating a lot of runes over the past month, and one of the runes that he made the most of was Mana Storage Runes for himself and Gina. These were Third-Rank Runes that were created by someone named Desire Fluer from an unknown world, and they could siphon a steady flow of mana from a mage’s body and hold onto that mana until it was needed. The moment you deactivate the rune, all its stored mana would be released, and this would either replenish the mana of the mage that drew the rune or act as a catalyst in a magical reaction.</p>
