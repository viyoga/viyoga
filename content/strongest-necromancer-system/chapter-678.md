<p>The Grand General’s body slowly began to transform from its Machina appearance into the appearance of another giant slime! The sound of bones cracking and breaking echoed through the room as the slime grew, and once it was large enough, it jumped on the second slime and the two of them began to merge!</p>
<p>The power in the building suddenly skyrocketed to new levels, and both Jason and Gillian turned their attention towards the central building as they sensed the new power that suddenly erupted from there! Gillian’s entire face twisted in anger as she recognized that mana signature immediately, and she spread her wings as she quickly shot through the air and began heading straight for the central building! Jason could sense the anger radiating off Gillian, and he knew that whatever was inside the building was what Gillian wanted to kill the most, so he quickly took off after his Queen to support her!</p>
<p>BOOOM!</p>
<p>But before either of them could reach the building, it suddenly exploded outwards with a mighty sound, and a tall giant appeared from the ruins and slammed its feet into the ground! This giant was one of the creatures that Alexander had consumed a long time ago! It was known as a Red Troll, and it stood at more than a hundred meters tall with large tusks and dark red skin! Its muscles were bulging and powerful, and its innate ability was an impervious skin that couldn’t be pierced by any sort of blade! The troll roared at Gillian, but Gillian didn’t even seem to give a damn about the size of the creature, and she immediately rammed into it with all her strength and sent it tumbling to the ground!</p>
<p>BOOM!</p>
<p>The entire world of the Machina shook from the impact, and Gillian raised her shadowed hand and tried to stab the troll in the head, but the troll swiped its hand to the side and smacked her away from it! The troll opened its mouth towards Jason and unleashed a massive blast of fire, and Jason easily twisted to the side to dodge it before he sent a blast of ice towards the monster! The troll immediately responded with another flaming breath!</p>
<p>BOOOM!</p>
<p>Another massive explosion of fire and ice echoed through the region, and the troll’s back bulged and rippled as two massive angel wings sprouted from behind it! The angels were another race that Alexander had managed to consume a long time ago, and since they were one of the fastest races in the Primordial Domain, he was going to use them to absolutely blitz his attackers! Alexander shot forward with speed, and he was about to slam into Jason, but Jason quickly activated [Dispersion], and his body vanished into millions of particles! The troll flew through the particles and slammed through multiple buildings on the other side, and Jason quickly formed back into his regular self before he watched as Gillian slammed into the giant troll again! Gillian used her [Hands of Providence] to lift the troll into the air, and she spun him around with a roar and tossed him into the distance! The troll flew like a bullet and destroyed more than five hundred buildings as he crashed into the ground! The entire world of the Machina had already entered into a state of crisis as they tried desperately to fight against the multiple subordinates that had appeared, and the current fight between three TITAN Rank beings was only causing them more damage!</p>
<p>RATATATTATATA!</p>
<p>Multiple air carriers appeared from the side and began to open fire on all the fighters! The soldiers had no idea that the giant troll had once been their Overlord, and right now, the only thing they could think about was to kill everyone involved in trying to destroy their world!</p>
<p>ROARRR!</p>
<p>The troll roared in anger, and it raised its hand and transformed it into a cannon blaster before firing at the air carriers!</p>
<p>BOOM!</p>
<p>A massive explosion rang across the city, and the troll had to quickly turn back around as Gillian appeared again! This time, Gillian had formed her shadow tendrils into a large spear, and she was about to stab the troll through the head, but the troll brought up its hand, and the spear only managed to scratch the skin of its palm and draw a few drops of blood! But that one cut was enough for Gillian to attach her mana to the troll, and Gillian immediately activated [Blood Bomb]</p>
<p>BOOM!</p>
<p>The Troll’s right hand exploded in a massive spray of blood, slime, and fire, and the Troll roared as it brought its cannon blaster forward and used it to drive Gillian away with some rapid fire! The troll got to its feet as the damaged arm quickly began to heal again! As a slime, Alexander had a very high healing constitution, and as long as an injury wasn’t lethal, then there was nothing that he couldn’t reverse! Alexander’s entire body began to change again, and this time, his skin transformed into a diamond matrix material that had very high defences! Jason came from the side and attacked with the [Desert Fang] artefact in his right hand!</p>
<p>A massive windstorm suddenly surged to life as the Desert Fang’s innate skill activated, and the troll raised its hand to its face to protect itself from the sand! This brief moment of blindness gave Jason the chance to pull out the [Death’s Mirage], and he poured mana into it to create a massive blade! But Jason was shocked when he looked down and saw that the blade didn’t form!</p>
<p>’What...!?’</p>
<p>BOOM!</p>
<p>The troll lashed out with a massive swipe and slammed Jason through the air like a rag doll! Jason smashed into a building more than ten miles away, and he growled as he pulled himself up and saw that he had landed in a residential area! There were dead bodies all around Jason, and the stench of blood and metal was thick in the air, but Jason didn’t care about any of that! Jason looked down at his blade, and he cursed as he didn’t feel any connection to it! Was this because he became a Vympir? The [Death’s Mirage] had been crafted by connecting it to Jason’s soul, so it was possible that Jason couldn’t access it since his soul was no longer in his body! Jason just decided to ignore it, and he tossed the weapon back into the Alternate Dimension and exploded off the ground to get back in the battle!</p>
<p>The battle between the three TITAN Rank mages raged on for a long time, and in that time, the world of the Machina suffered so much damage that it was nearly unrecognizable from the beautiful paradise that it had once been! All three fighters possessed power that was either equal to or greater than TITAN Rank II, and since even a single TITAN Rank mage was capable of destroying an entire world if they wanted to, there was nothing that the individual soldiers could do against them! But that doesn’t mean that the Machina were willing to go down without a fight! Within the military facility in the eastern region of the world, the screams and explosions of a massive battle could be heard! Jason’s subordinates had made their way here according to Jason’s orders, and now they were going through the military facility and killing every single soldier! But the soldiers were a lot fiercer than the civilians, and so they were fighting back with everything that they had! But since most of the Generals had been killed by Alexander, the only soldiers here were the SSS Rank lieutenants along with other weaker soldiers, and they didn’t stand a chance against the might of the Beast of the Apocalypse!</p>
<p>BWOOOHHH!!</p>
