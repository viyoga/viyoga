That was going to be very useful in the future when he goes to fight against the other entrances!</p>
<p>Jason sat on the floor and made sure that his subordinates were well spread out to watch the environment properly. <p>’If I could gain more than ten percent from each subordinate, then I would be able to grow faster. Well, I shouldn’t be worried about that. I’ll just work towards gaining more kill points so I can upgrade the soldier ants into Rooks and use them to kill stronger beasts.’</p>
<p>Jason nodded to this thought, and once he thought this, he suddenly remembered that he had gained more than enough kill points to finally level up one of the Pawn Rank III subordinates to the Rook Class!</p>
<p>Jason gained a wide smile on his face once he realized this and opened the tab immediately! Jason had been wondering for a while now what it would be like to have a Rook Class subordinate! All the monsters that Jason had killed so far were all Pawns, and Jason knew that even though they were strong, they wouldn’t even hold a candle to the other classes!</p>
