<p>Madam Palina’s ability [Allsight] didn’t work in the same way most magic did! You couldn’t just force yourself to see the future using [Allsight]! Instead, the skill activates whenever the world decides that it wishes to give you a vision of an event that is yet to happen! The only thing Madam Palina could do to control the sort of vision she receives was to put all her focus towards certain people so that she would only get visions about them!</p>
<p>And after entering into a deal with her, Madam Palina now kept the Marc family close to her heart to make sure that the world would always give her visions regarding the future of this family! Harold understood just how important it was for him to have access to those visions, and that is why he was willingly paying her insane prices!</p>
<p>Madam Palina leaned forward, and Harold felt the tension in the room suddenly rise as Madam Palina’s milky white eyes pinned him in place! The old woman simply stared at him without blinking for close to a minute, and anyone else under that stare would’ve gotten uncomfortable immediately! But Harold kept his cool, and he did his best to focus as Palina finally spoke in a low, raspy voice!</p>
<p>"A vision has come from the great beyond. A vision of what is to come. Of darkness and death. Destruction. I see blood in the river, bodies on the land. I see parents crying for their children. I see billions dying without cause. A Creature of myth and legend has been awoken. A destroyer of worlds has been freed. A disaster cometh from beyond the Primordial veil. A paragon goes forth at your family’s heed."</p>
<p>Madam Palina stopped here and began to cough desperately, and Harold suddenly jolted as he was brought back to the present! Harold had been completely engrossed in what Madam Palina was saying, so he had zoned out, but now that he was back in the present, he could feel the dread of those words starting to settle inside of him. When Harold had been summoned here by Madam Palina, he already expected to hear something serious, but nothing could’ve prepared him for this!</p>
<p>’Isn’t this a prophecy about the end of the world!? Is someone from the family planning something that serious!?’</p>
<p>Harold didn’t know what to think! He desperately wanted to ask Palina if what she was saying was true or if she was just playing around, but Harold knew that it was pointless to ask something like that! The [Allsight] was an absolute skill, and it did not lie! Magic itself had revealed this to Madam Palina, so it would certainly happen! Somewhere in the distant future, there would be a great disaster that would kill billions of people across worlds, and it would all be his family’s fault.</p>
<p>Madam Palina coughed a few more times to clear her throat, and Harold waited patiently for her to be done before she finally spoke up again.</p>
<p>"But I see a way. A chance, a sliver of salvation. The youngest prince of the strongest king and she who is to share her soul with him. In there, you will find a power that will return the beast to its cage and stop its devastation."</p>
<p>Harold’s eyes narrowed!</p>
<p>’Jason?’</p>
<p>Harold didn’t even need to think for long before he realised exactly who Madam Palina was talking about! The strongest king could only mean their father. There was no other king in the entire Primordial Expanse that could match their father’s strength. That meant the youngest son was Jason! If that was the case, then who the hell is the girl who is to share her soul with him!?</p>
<p>’Jason has three wives, doesn’t he? Is it one of them?’</p>
<p>Harold had done a lot of investigation into Jason, just like the rest of the family, and Harold knew that Jason was in a relationship with three women! But Harold didn’t know if any of these three women was the one that the prophecy was talking about! And even if he finds the woman, how in the world were they supposed to stop this disaster from happening?</p>
<p>Madam Palina had already stopped talking, and Harold frowned as he asked her if there was anything more:</p>
<p>"Did the vision give a location, or maybe any other clues?"</p>
<p>Madam Palina stayed silent, and Harold let out a long breath as he realised that this was the end of the prophecy!</p>
<p>’I need to get in contact with Jason, and I have to find out who this girl is. I’m sure there are some clues somewhere. I also need to talk to Valeria. If this prophecy is true, then we need to deal with it first before dealing with Gregory.’</p>
<p>Harold frowned as these thoughts ran through his head, and he realised just how much he had to do! If Harold wanted to stop this disaster, then he needed to get moving! Harold stood up, and he threw his hood back over his head.</p>
<p>"Until next time, young Harold. May the threads of magic guide you on your path."</p>
<p>Madam Palina gave her standard greeting, and Harold nodded once in thanks before he turned and left the store behind.</p>
