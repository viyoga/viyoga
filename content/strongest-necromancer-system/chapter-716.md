Doesn’t that just mean that he was impervious to any sort of attack! That was awesome! And the weapon was something that Jason had been thinking about for a while now! <p>’That’s the weapon that killed the prisoner after Hesabeth grew angry with him. I’ve been thinking about the runes on that weapon for a long time. I’ll study it once I get the chance.’</p>
<p>Jason made up his mind about that, and he finally turned his attention towards the final reward. The title was insanely overpowered! By surviving against the world, the system had basically made Jason a one-man army against the world itself! Any natural disaster or natural phenomenon created by the world would have its effects reduced by eighty percent once it came in contact with Jason! That meant Jason could walk through a literal earthquake like he was walking on regular ground while this skill was active!</p>
<p>These were some amazing rewards, and Jason was more glad than ever that he managed to survive against the world back then! Jason didn’t know if he would be willing to fight against the world again since he knew that the world had been holding back in their previous fight. But at least with this title, Jason could put up a better fight if he ever had to do that again!</p>
<p>Jason finally turned his attention to the next notification, and he hummed when he saw the mission that had been cancelled. So the system also realised that the Overlord had been killed before all of this happened. But now that Jason knew all this, he finally understood why he felt so strange whenever he was in front of the Overlord in the past. There was always this weird feeling that Jason would have when facing the Overlord, and it felt like the Overlord’s magic was slightly different from all the other Machina that Jason had faced before. But no one else could feel what Jason felt, and that made it seem like Jason was just being paranoid!</p>
<p>But Jason hadn’t been paranoid at all! Jason’s senses were very sharp, and this incident only made his trust in his own instincts grow even more! If his instincts could pick up the difference between the Overlord’s mana and the mana of the other Machina when no one else could, then Jason would trust any strange feeling his instincts warned him about in the future. Jason looked back at the rewards that he was meant to pick out from, and it didn’t take long for Jason to realise that there was only one good choice there. Jason already had more than a hundred million kill points at this time, so two hundred thousand wouldn’t do anything for him, and the increased reputation with the Radagans, as well as the council, could be worked on some other time!
