<p>Gina felt how the subordinates in the Alternate Dimension had been dying and coming back to life constantly, and Gina knew it was likely that Jason had used his [Soul Mate] skill to make sure that he didn’t die. But the fact that the subordinates had been killed so many times meant that Jason was in a situation that should’ve killed him multiple times as well, so there was no way Gina wouldn’t be worried!</p>
<p>Jason could almost feel the anxiousness flowing off Gina’s body through their mental link, and Jason decided to change the topic.</p>
<p>’How’s your vacation going? Are you having fun?’</p>
<p>Gina let out a small sigh when she heard this. Gina knew that Jason was only trying to distract her so she wouldn’t be worried, and Gina decided to drop the previous topic as she smiled and answered.</p>
<p>[Yes, babe, it’s so much fun. Hesbeath had been showing us around the popular sights in Minori, and we’ve been eating some exotic food. Did you know that the plants on Minori are magical potions? Some of them can cure illnesses, and there’s a particular one that can boost your mana output for a certain amount of time. They are all so expensive.]</p>
<p>Jason laughed lightly as he heard this. Jason already knew that Minori was one of the most successful agricultural worlds in the Primordial Expanse, so Jason wasn’t really surprised! The land of Minori was so fertile that it could grow practically anything!</p>
<p>’Say hello to Hesbeath and Riyala for me, alright. And don’t be in a hurry to get back. We’ll have the wedding soon, so take your time and enjoy yourself. I’ll come see you later.’</p>
<p>Gina’s face brightened once she heard Jason say this!</p>
<p>[Really!? You’ll come see us!?]</p>
<p>Jason’s grin widened!</p>
<p>’Yeah, babe. I’ll take a small leave and come see you once I settle things in L’horm. Just make sure you’re taking things easy for the baby.’</p>
<p>Gina nodded happily, and she said she wouldn’t stress herself at all!</p>
<p>[Hesbeath has so many servants that we don’t really have to do anything. I’m surprised we haven’t gotten fat from how much food we’ve been eating.]</p>
<p>Jason actually laughed at that, and he finally told Gina that he had to go and make sure everything was okay on L’horm.</p>
<p>[Alright, babe. Just take care of yourself, alright. Love you.]</p>
<p>’I love you too, babe.’</p>
<p>Jason finally closed down the connection, and he let out a calm sigh as he looked up at the massive blue Gate in the sky. For some reason, the fact that Gina and the other girls were on Minori right now gave Jason a feeling of calmness that he just didn’t have before. Jason knew that Minori was one of the most protected worlds in the Primordial Expanse, and Hesbeath’s bias only increased the protection even more. If there was one place that Jason would want his family to be, then it was on Minori!</p>
<p>’I need to deal with all this madness before the baby gets here. I don’t want to be fighting against the Court of Apostles or my brother while taking care of my baby. I won’t do it!’</p>
<p>Jason’s face hardened as he made up his mind, and Jason decided that he would do his best to deal with the entire situation concerning the Court of Apostles in the next few months. The Moonlight Wolves were still tracking the couple that Jason fought, and once they found them, Jason would kill them and resurrect their soul to learn more about what was really going on.</p>
<p>But first, Jason needed to return to L’horm and make sure there wasn’t too much damage done to the academy. Jason opened his Alternate Dimension, and he took the body of the dead Lightning dragon into it before he called on the resurrected Lightning dragon. The dragon put its massive head down and allowed Jason to float up to its neck before it raised its head and let out a massive roar!</p>
<p>ROARRR!</p>
<p>Rumble!</p>
<p>The sky rumbled with thunder and sparked with lightning, and the dragon flapped its massive wings before taking off into the air! The dragon headed straight for the massive blue gate in the sky, and since it was so quick, it didn’t even take two minutes before it crossed through and reappeared above the Northern Alliance Academy!</p>
<p>Jason looked down at the Academy, and he hummed when he saw that they had already erected a new barrier over the building. The roof was still missing, but Jason could no longer sense the Primordial Essence from the Primordial corpse, so he knew that the academy was well protected again! The researchers in the academy all took multiple steps back in fear when they saw the giant dragon appearing above them, but Jaen raised his hand and told them all to relax! Once the researchers saw that Jason was riding on the back of the dragon, they felt a sense of awe rising from their hearts! Jason had actually defeated such a mighty dragon!?</p>
<p>"I thought the dragon was stronger than him. How did he kill it so quickly?"</p>
<p>"It’s also a Phantasmal Beast. It’s not easy to defeat a beast like that!"</p>
<p>Jason ignored the murmurs from some of the scientists as he looked around at the destruction caused by the recent fights, and Jason knew it would take a while for this world to recover everything that had been lost.</p>
<p>.....</p>
<p>Over the next few days, Jason supervised the rebuilding of the destroyed world. Since most of the damage to the world had been done by the Lightning dragon, there were a lot of buildings that had to be rebuilt, and the palace and magical academy also needed many repairs. Jason put the Jamani subordinates in his legion to work doing all of this! The Jamani were the ones who were responsible for bringing this much chaos to this world in the first place, so it was only right that they did most of the work in repairing what was destroyed!</p>
<p>At the Northern Alliance Academy, Jason and Jaen had spoken for a long time about what would be happening with the Mortal Severance Pill and the research they were doing here. It was obvious that they could no longer continue performing their research on L’horm since every single Primordial in the Primordial Expanse now knew about this place. If they stayed here, then it wouldn’t be long before someone came to try and take the Primordial Corpse for themselves.</p>
<p>Jaen already had a number of other Academies around this domain where he could go, but Jaen was surprised when Jason offered to allow him to stay on the world of the Machina! Jaen honestly never expected Jason to make an offer like that! The world of the Machina was seen as one of the most well-protected worlds in this entire domain, and setting up their Academy there would allow them to perform their research without ever having to worry about outside interference again!</p>
<p>It was a very good offer, but Jaen knew that there had to be a catch to it, and he asked Jason what Jason wanted in return for offering them something like this. Jason’s answer was simple.</p>
<p>"I want an Alliance between Earth and the Northern Alliance Academy."</p>
<p>Jaen blinked in surprise when he heard this, and Jaen could see from Jason’s expression that he was dead serious. Jaen asked Jason what exactly the alliance would be like, and Jason gave Jaen a detailed description of how he wanted things to go from there.</p>
<p>Jason wanted the Alliance Academy to provide Earth with a steady supply of the Mortal Severance pill to allow them to boost multiple mages into the TITAN Ranks, and in exchange, Jason would give the Alliance Academy a significant discount on their future purchases of Aqualith stone as well as enough protection to ensure they are never touched in this domain again.</p>
