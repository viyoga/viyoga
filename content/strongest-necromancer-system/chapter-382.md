<p>"I also have a few suggestions regarding possible trade deals with Radagan. Are you okay with talking about this later?"</p>

Luthor thanked Jason when he saw him nod before he watched as a large mature dragon appeared from the horizon.</p>
<p>The dragon’s arrival was met with screams and cheers from the people of Radagan. It didn’t take long for Riyala to appear from the shadows with Jason and Gina. <p>....</p>

Many of the dragons and wyverns turned to look at him curiously, and Jason saw many small dragons poking their cute heads out to stare at him. <p>’You could probably defeat them if you took my offer to learn my techniques.’</p>

Was it one of the benefits that came with being the Priestess of Fire? The other dragons all recognized Riyala once she appeared, and they bowed their heads in respect as she passed them. <p>"You look very beautiful. I didn’t know you could do this."</p>

<p>Gina came through the foliage like an ethereal creature born from the forest itself. There were blue lilies in her hair that made her red eyes shine like sapphires, and a veil had been used to cover her hair, and it was dragging over the ground behind her. Oh, so this was what a faery looked like.</p>
<p>Jason took in a shallow breath and released it in one long sigh when he saw Gina. She looked absolutely breathtaking, and Jason smiled when he saw her giving him a shy look. So this was what they went to do? Jason had to admit that it was more than worth it. <p>"You look breathtaking."</p>
