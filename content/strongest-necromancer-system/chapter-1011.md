<p>The transit line stopped high up above the transit line station before it began to slowly lower itself down to the ground! The vehicle finally came to a stop inside the station, and a loud bell rang five times across the entire station to inform the travellers that they had arrived at the fifth domain!</p>
<p>Jason and Stella had already gotten all their things ready, and they turned as one of the conductors on the train came to open their room door and bowed to them.</p>
<p>"Sir, madam, we have arrived at your destination. May I take your luggage?"</p>
<p>Stella shook her head and said there was no need for that, and the conductor nodded before he gestured for them to follow him.</p>
<p>"Please come this way; I’ll guide you to the exit. We ensured no one would bother you as you make your way out."</p>
<p>Stella and Jason followed behind the conductor as he took them down the vehicle from the VVIP area towards the common area where the normal passengers were seated. There were other people who were also waiting to get off, but everyone had been told to stay in their seats and wait until Jason and Stella got off since they were VVIPs! Jason noticed a lot of people staring at them with curious gazes while wondering who they were, and Jason was just glad he decided to use this disguise.</p>
<p>As they walked, Jason saw the old woman who had caused the commotion when Jason was boarding the transit line sitting at the side. The woman’s eyes widened once she saw Jason and Stella, and she quickly looked away with her head bowed like she was trying to hide inside a shell! Jason had no idea what the president had done to her, but from what he could see, it was obvious that she didn’t want to have anything to do with them ever again! Jason just ignored that as he continued following behind the conductor, and they eventually made their way out of the transit line and into the station! There was a long line of people waiting outside the station and buying tickets to get into the transit line, and Jason couldn’t help but wonder where the transit line on his domain was.</p>
<p>’I looked through all the documents from the Machina, and I didn’t see any mention of the transit line in any of them. Don’t we have something like this in the sixth domain?’</p>
<p>Jason frowned as he had this thought, and he decided that he would have to look into it!</p>
<p>But what Jason didn’t know was that they did have a transit line in the sixth domain and the transit line used to be very popular among the rich and wealthy, but the reason why the Machina didn’t have any documents on it was because Rubik decided that he was too lazy to take care of the line properly, so he neglected it and allowed bandits and mercenaries to occupy the world where the transit line station is located!</p>
<p>These mercenaries always tried to rob the many wealthy people who came to the sixth domain through the line, so the transit line stopped going to the sixth domain completely! After Jason started taking care of the mercenaries, the number of robberies in the sixth domain transit line went down significantly, and it wouldn’t be too difficult for Jason to get the transit line running again once he takes care of the remaining bandits!</p>
<p>Jason and Stella were led by the conductor to a futuristic-looking floating taxi, and he spoke up while gesturing towards the car.</p>
<p>"The station has taken the liberty to provide you with transportation that will take you wherever you need to go within this world. There is no need for you to pay anything, and we wish to use this as a means of apologising for the disrespect shown to you in the seventh domain transit line. I hope you will forgive our rudeness."</p>
<p>The conductor bowed to Jason and Stella respectfully, and Jason just waved his hand and told him that it was alright! Jason didn’t even want things to get this serious in the first place, but Jason was just glad that they could put all this behind them.</p>
<p>"Tell the president of Mavina that there’s no problem. I’ll put in a good word with Overlord Hesabeth so he doesn’t need to worry."</p>
<p>The conductor bowed once again.</p>
<p>"Thank you for your generosity. We are forever in your debt."</p>
<p>Jason and Stella got on the taxi, and Jason was surprised when he didn’t see any driver. The taxi had a holographic image of a pretty woman floating on the dashboard, and she waved at Jason and Stella with a smile.</p>
<p>[Hello, my name is Fora, and I’ll be your driver today~! Where are you heading to~?]</p>
<p>The hologram spoke in the official Izagi language, but since both Jason and Stella had translation abilities, they were able to translate and understand her easily enough. Jason turned towards Esdath and asked him where they were meant to go.</p>
<p>’You’re going to the world of Diran. I don’t know how to get there from Izagi though.’</p>
<p>Jason hummed, and he asked the system for directions.</p>
<p>[The world of Diran is three Dimensional Leaps from Izagi. The first Dimensional Gate is located fifteen miles north-east of the transit line station on the edge of the floating ocean bridge.]</p>
<p>Jason thanked the system and then passed the message on to the hologram. The holographic woman saluted playfully before the taxi floated off the ground and began to fly towards their destination!</p>
<p>....</p>
<p>[The World of Diran]</p>
<p>[Three Dimensional Leaps From Izagi]</p>
<p>Diran as a world was very different from many of the other worlds in the Primordial Expanse. Unlike most other worlds that focused on the use and development of mana and magic, Diran had developed in a unique way that prevented it from being able to produce mana. Instead, Diran produced a different form of energy that was very well connected with life itself! People call it Qi!</p>
<p>Qi was a form of energy that had even more potential than magic. When comparing an exact amount of Qi and Mana, researchers found that Qi not only possessed more energy than Mana, but also had much higher destructive power than Mana! But although Qi was such an extraordinary power source, there was a reason why Qi was not the common source of energy in the Primordial Expanse, and the reason for that was simple: Qi was far too difficult to harness!</p>
<p>Qi as a power source was directly connected to the soul of all living things, and this meant that every living thing within a certain world had the ability to manipulate the Qi present in that world. But the fact that Qi was so deeply connected with the souls of creatures meant that it was also very easy for the power to be corrupted by the emotions those creatures experienced! Good feelings like happiness and joy would make the Qi in the world peaceful and gentle, but the moment people began to release negative emotions like anger and hatred, their Qi and the Qi of those around them would become corrupted!</p>
<p>This constant dependence on emotions prevented the world from ever fully relying on Qi, and there were very few worlds whose inhabitants evolved to use Qi over mana! Diran was one of those worlds that managed to somehow master their Qi!</p>
<p>The world of Diran was what you would call a "cultivator’s world"! It contained multiple continents with hundreds of different cultivation sects existing in the cities on those continents! The largest continent was known as Yang, and this was where the largest and most powerful cultivation sects lived in massive cities.</p>
