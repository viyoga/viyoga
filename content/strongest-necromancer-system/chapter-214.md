<p>[Jason’s Apartment]</p>
<p>Jason’s eyes slowly opened as the sound of new notifications flooded his system. The light coming through the window cast a half shadow across the floor of his new apartment, and for a second, Jason wondered where the hell he was. But then everything returned to him, and he calmly blinked himself back to the present. He put his hand to his neck and cracked it to the side to wake himself up.</p>
<p>"Mmh..."</p>
<p>A low voice came from beside him and Jason looked to his side to see that Riyala had come down to the living room at some point during the night. She was sitting on the floor, and her body was angled toward him in a way that made it easy for her to rest her head on his shoulder. Jason could remember when she came down, but he had been too tired to tell her to go back upstairs, so he remembered just letting her do whatever she wanted. Jason released a calm sigh and ignored her before turning back to his system tabs. Jason could tell that his subordinates were making quick work of the monsters in the Orange Gate, and he was glad they were able to fight properly even though he wasn’t there. The notification about them killing the Gate Boss had not yet come in, so Jason knew they weren’t done with the Gate, but he could feel that a few of his subordinates were dead.</p>
<p>Jason immediately activated the [Eye of The King] so he could see through Raya’s eyes and he hummed as he saw that she was currently fighting against a giant black snake. The snake was at least seventy feet long and had sharp fangs that looked thick enough to tear through a building! Its eyes were a piercing blood red, and it fought with a ferocity unlike anything Jason had ever seen!</p>
<p>But even though it was strong, it was nowhere near as strong as Riyala! Riyala had taken charge of the battle and she was dealing with the giant snake all on her own while the rest of the Legion were handling the multiple smaller snakes around the area.</p>
<p>’There shouldn’t be a problem since all the Rooks are still alive. They can handle the Boss and his subordinates on their own.’</p>
<p>Jason changed his view to that of one of the Pawn Rank II subordinates who was guarding the superhumans who had been trapped in the Gate, and Jason hummed in surprise when he saw Madam Rose there. Jason remembered her from the brief interaction they had when he first got his powers, and he was surprised that a B Rank guild managed to go into an Orange Gate. The system said that there were two guilds inside, so Jason was sure that at least one of them would be an A Rank guild. It was suicide for two B Rank guilds to even think of entering an Orange Gate alone!</p>
<p>Jason just made sure that all the guild members there didn’t need any immediate medical attention before he ignored them and turned back to the fight between Raya and the large Viper Boss. The boss had wrapped its long body around Raya and was trying to squeeze her to death, but Raya was strong enough to create enough space for her to move her head and hence she used her mandibles to tear through the Viper’s side! The Viper hissed in pain as it retreated, but Raya didn’t give it any chance to escape as she immediately pounced on it to continue the fight!</p>
<p>Jason turned off the [Eye of the King] once he was sure Raya was not going to lose the fight and he was about to wake Riyala up when he heard a knock at the door.</p>
<p>Knock! Knock! Knock!</p>
<p>Jason turned sharply towards the door, and his eyes immediately narrowed. He tapped Riyala gently while telling her to wake up, and Riyala’s red eyes fluttered open in confusion before widening once she realized where she was!</p>
<p>"I’m sorry, I didn’t—"</p>
<p>Jason immediately put his hand over Riyala’s mouth and told her to be quiet. Riyala’s blush increased when Jason touched her, but she could see the seriousness in his eyes and she finally nodded calmly. Jason looked towards the door again before he told one of his subordinates to go and see who was there. The Dragonborn soldier in Jason’s Legion moved through the shadows and appeared outside the front door to give Jason a good view, and Jason felt his brow rise in surprise when he saw the people there.</p>
<p>’What the hell?’</p>
<p>Jason stood up and went to open the door. He gave the two people there a curious look and wondered what the hell they were doing here. One of them was Juliet Rapha. She was wearing a clean black suit and carrying a bag in front of her, and she had an apologetic smile on her face as she looked up at him. But the other person there was someone he didn’t expect to see for a long time.</p>
<p>"Griselda?"</p>
<p>Griselda, the girl from Jason’s school who had been asking him to join her future guild, was standing beside Juliet with a confident look on her face. She was carrying a folder in her hands and Jason could already tell she would give him a headache before she even opened her mouth! Jason understood why Juliet would be there since she was Jason’s agent. It was obvious that they would have been looking for him after everything that happened yesterday. But Jason had no idea what Griselda was doing there. Jason looked over at Griselda and spoke with narrowed eyes.</p>
<p>"What the hell are you doing here? And how the fuck did you even know where I was?"</p>
<p>Griselda immediately walked forward confidently while saying,</p>
<p>"I heard about your exploits on the news, and I had to come see you before any other guilds tried to take you in! After everything that happened, I’m finally ready to take you in as a member of my guild!"</p>
<p>Jason’s expression shifted to one of absolute confusion before he finally allowed his gaze to shift over to Juliet. Jason’s expression told Juliet that he was demanding an explanation for this craziness!</p>
