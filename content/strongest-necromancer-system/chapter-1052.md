<p>"Did you speak to darling? Is he alright?"</p>
<p>Gina nodded.</p>
<p>"Yes, he’s fine. But the news was a surprise to him too. He’s working on evacuating the worlds in his domain."</p>
<p>"Well, of course it was a surprise to him. Even I did not expect something like this to happen so suddenly. Darling is a very capable man, so I am sure he will do his best for his people. Come, you need to go lie down and get some rest. You look very stressed."</p>
<p>Hesabeth’s face twisted in worry when she saw the exhausted look on Gina’s face, and Hesabeth immediately knew that something like this was not good for the baby. Gina had been very relaxed for most of the time she spent on Minori, and it felt like she had been on vacation, but it was as though all of that relaxation was immediately being destroyed by the stress that came from this sudden news! So Hesabeth decided that she would do her best to make Gina relax again! But Gina shook her head.</p>
<p>"No, I want to know what’s going on out there. You’re about to speak to your Elder Worlds, aren’t you?"</p>
<p>Hesabeth nodded. Since there was nothing that the two of them could do while they were trapped in here, Hesabeth was going to make a call to the Elder Worlds around Minori to warn them about the coming disaster and give them instructions while they carried out the evacuations. Gina told Hesabeth that she wanted to stay for the meeting, and Hesabeth was a little skeptical about it.</p>
<p>Hesabeth was going to have to make some difficult choices during this meeting. Just like Jason figured out before, Hesabeth also knew that she couldn’t save everyone, so she was going to tell the Elder Worlds to focus more on saving the worlds that had higher value first! Hesabeth didn’t want Gina to have to see all of that if it would stress her out!</p>
<p>But Gina just told Hesabeth that it was fine! Gina couldn’t bear to remain in the dark while hoping that things were going fine. Whether things went well or not, Gina just wanted to know what was going on! Once she heard this, Hesabeth finally decided that it wasn’t that big of a deal, and she simply told Gina to come with her as they headed out of the room.</p>
<p>....</p>
<p>All around the Primordial Expanse, the news of the approaching Celestial was finally beginning to spread rapidly across every world! All of the most powerful mages in the Primordial Expanse who did not have any attachment to the lesser mortals had already migrated to safer worlds where the Celestial of Death would not be able to reach them, so right now, it was only the lesser mages who needed to move.</p>
<p>On hundreds of thousands of worlds, large congregations of mortals could be seen leaving everything behind as they headed towards massive Dimensional Tears that were being held open by powerful TITAN Rank warriors! The congregations moved forward slowly as they headed towards other worlds, and although there were hundreds of billions of people being evacuated, everyone knew that there would not be enough time to help every single person!</p>
<p>The powerful Angels of Eden had begun moving towards the Academies all around their world and giving evacuation notices to the students there! Unlike most other worlds that wanted to save everyone, Eden understood that the students of the Academy were their future, and so they were prioritizing the evacuation of the students before anything else!</p>
<p>Farlow had called on the Elder Worlds around his domain, and they were moving billions of people rapidly towards worlds on the outskirts of the Third Domain! The same thing was going on in every single domain that had been marked as a passageway for the Celestial of Death! This one monster had completely destroyed the harmony of the entire Primordial Expanse with its appearance, and it was now up to the mortals to try and do as much damage control as possible!</p>
<p>In the Sixth Domain, Jason had arrived on Earth a few hours ago, and he immediately got to work informing the Elder Worlds about what was coming and creating plans with them on how they would be carrying out the evacuation. According to what C’thlyanu said, the places that they needed to evacuate first were the Eastern and Northern Pathways, and it just so happened that Earth was right in the middle of the Eastern Pathway, so Jason needed to focus on evacuating this world before anyone else.</p>
<p>Thankfully for Jason, there were many worlds outside of the Eastern and Northern Pathways that would be suitable for people to temporarily live in until the entire situation calmed down. One of the main worlds out there was the world of the Machina! Machina was in the Western Corridor, far outside the path of the Celestial of Death, and since Jason had already killed most of the Machina in that world, there was more than enough landmass to occupy most of the humans!</p>
<p>The other worlds around Machina also had a lot of space that allowed them to accommodate many refugees, and Jason told the Elder Worlds of the Sixth Domain to begin moving the people in the worlds around their domain to these distant worlds in the Western Corridor! The Elder Worlds immediately began doing as they were told, and Jason focused on helping Earth as well as the other worlds that were around him. The first world that Jason visited was Radagan! Riyala had been in Minori just a few days ago, but she had returned to Radagan because of her duties, and there was a part of Jason that hoped she would’ve just remained in Minori for a few more days so she wouldn’t be out here!</p>
<p>Minori was currently the safest place to be, and Jason would’ve had a lot more peace of mind if he knew that all three of his girls were safe behind the protection of the world! But Jason did not allow that to affect him much, and he simply went to tell Riyala what was going on. Riyala had obviously been shocked when she heard about this, and Riyala began to wonder how in the world she was going to make the dragons leave their forest! The Radagan dragons in the Flaming Forest were very territorial, and they were the sort of creatures that never left a region once they made that region their home! But now Riyala had to not only take them out of the Flaming Forest, but she also had to make them leave Radagan completely!</p>
<p>"Riyala, you need to get them to move. If they don’t, then they are all going to die. The Celestial of Death will not spare any of them."</p>
<p>This was what Jason had said to Riyala when she told him about her problem, and Riyala could see in his eyes that he was very serious. Riyala immediately nodded to tell Jason that she understood, and she decided that she would just use her authority as the priestess to make the dragons move! The dragons would be a little hesitant, but they always listened to the priestess, so Riyala telling them to move from the Flaming Forest should be enough to make them leave!</p>
<p>Jason thanked Riyala, and he told her that he had to leave to warn the other worlds in this part of the domain. Riyala gave Jason a kiss and told him to be careful out there!</p>
<p>This was how Jason moved across more than a hundred worlds in less than a day and informed them about what was coming! Many of the leaders of these worlds were even more stunned than Riyala had been.</p>
