<p>Jason deactivated the Adamantine armor around his body and felt his cloak flutter in the dry wind as he walked forward to stare down at the prone body of the Immortal Lich.</p>
<p>Jason couldn’t help but admit that the Immortal Lich had truly earned its title, because even after releasing such a massive blast that killed both Ragnar as well as other Rook rank subordinates, the Immortal Lich was somehow still alive. The Lich was lying on the ground since it could no longer move at all. Its clothes had been completely destroyed, so its skeletal figure was now exposed and there were large cracks travelling across it’s entire body. Jason glanced toward the light in the center of the Lich’s chest and saw how it was flickering like a dying ember. It was obvious that the Lich was already on its final leg.</p>
<p>The Lich’s eyes trailed up slowly when Jason stepped in front of it, and it met Jason’s eyes with a low chuckle. So even this wasn’t enough to defeat this monster? The Lich already knew that the people of the Avaran race were demons that possessed unimaginable power, but he had hoped that he would at least be able to claim that he took down one of the Avarans in his lifetime.</p>
<p>"You... monster..."</p>
<p>The Lich spoke in a low, raspy voice, and Jason didn’t even bother to respond. Jason went to one knee and stabbed his hand into the Lich’s chest before dragging out the crystal with a heavy pull, and the Lich grunted in pain! The light in its eyes and chest finally dimmed out and the last thought the Lich had just before it died was how much it regretted not being able to complete it’s research. The Immortal Lich was no more.</p>

A notification appeared in front of Jason, and Jason felt a smile spread across his lips as he read through it.</p>

<p>——</p>
<p>Jason heard the sound of all the notifications appearing on his system, but Jason ignored them for now! Jason looked down at the crystal in his hand and felt a surge of happiness spreading out from inside him as he realized that he had actually done it! He finally got everything he needed for Gina’s resurrection! Jason wasn’t normally the sort of person to express his emotions loudly or intensely, but at that moment, Jason didn’t bother trying to stop himself as he squeezed his hand into a fist and shouted into the sky!</p>
<p>"I FUCKING DID IT!!"</p>
<p>Jason’s voice echoed through the entire world in his excitement, and he smiled widely while staring down at the large Magical Crystal in his hand! Jason felt a feeling of pure excitement fill his heart, and he laughed before releasing a hard breath! Even though Jason had set his mind toward completing this task, he still knew that there were many things that could have gone wrong! The fact that he was here right now was only because he made sure everything was done perfectly!</p>
<p>Jason finally decided to read through the notifications that the system just sent to him, and he felt shocked when he saw the new Legion skill that he had gotten. The ability to share his soul amongst his subordinates was an amazing advantage, and Jason could immediately think of many ways to use something like this to his advantage! Jason read through the rest of the skill description and he frowned slightly when he saw that he would have to be careful when choosing the subordinates. If he chose the wrong one, then he would suffer some damage if they were killed in battle.</p>
<p>’That means I have to pick the strongest subordinates that are less likely to be killed. If I choose a weak one, then I might end up being distracted in the middle of an important battle when they’re killed.’</p>
<p>Jason released a calm sigh before shaking his head and turning toward the artefact that the system gave him. Jason looked down at the dead Lich and saw something glint from within the it’s body. He leaned down to pick it up, and hummed in surprise when he saw what it was. It was a simple hilt that you would normally use to wield a longsword. It had a pommel made from silver, and the hilt itself fit in Jason’s hand like it had been made specifically for him. There was no blade attached to the pommel and Jason felt a frown form on his face as he activated [Appraisal].</p>
