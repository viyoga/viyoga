<p>Jason would go to fight the Sovereign since he knew his subordinates couldn’t handle him, and he would try to either hold him off until his Legions win the other battles or kill him so he could make things easier for the rest of the Legion.</p>
<p>’But it’ll be nice if we can get some sort of reinforcements from the people in Radagan. I’m sure that not all of them are in support of the Sovereign. It was a coup, so there had to be a revolution that fought against him. Did the Soverign manage to kill everyone who opposed him?’</p>
<p>’Actually, there is something else, my king.’</p>
<p>One of the Radagan soldiers suddenly spoke up, and Jason turned to him and hummed when he saw that it was Grinot. Grinot bowed to Jason in respect, and Jason gestured for him to speak.</p>
<p>’Although there has been no backlash from the citizens and nobles, mainly due to their fear of the Sovereign’s power, there has been a significant backlash from the dragons and wyverns living in the Ashenvalley. Every year, there is a ritual carried out by the young children in Radagan where they go to the Ashenvalley Forest and try to bond with one of the young dragons or wyverns living there. It is something that has been happening since the dawn of our world, and it is the only way a dragonborn child can find a lifelong companion. But after the coup, the dragons and wyverns in the Ashenvalley grew hostile against all the Radagan children who went to try and bond with them. The adult dragons and wyverns blocked the path into the Ashenvalley Forest, and the children haven’t been able to gain any companions ever since. Some of the nobles believe that this is the work of the dragon gods who are angry at the Sovereign for his actions, but the Sovereign refuses to believe this, and he claims that he would kill all the adult dragons and wyverns if they continue to act hostile.’</p>
<p>Jason’s brow furrowed as he listened with rapt attention to the words from his subordinate, and Jason’s mind began to run at even faster speeds as he considered the implications of such a situation. This... could be useful. Jason had no idea that the dragons and wyverns on Radagan were so independent. Jason always thought that the people in Radagan were in control of the Magical beasts, and the Magical beasts just did whatever they wanted.</p>
<p>’That means the dragons that attacked us only did so because they formed a bond with the Dragonborn. The free dragons have their own beliefs and thoughts, and they don’t like the fact that the Sovereign took over the kingdom. If I can get the dragons to fight with us... wouldn’t that even the odds?’</p>

<p>’Riyala.’</p>
