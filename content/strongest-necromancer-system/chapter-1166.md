<p>As Jason moved through the air, he noticed a few Pirates who were in the TITAN Rank III, but there were no PRIMORDIALS, and Jason knew that all the PRIMORDIALS might be gathered in the central city. Since the only way for a TITAN Rank III to rise up to the level of the PRIMORDIALS was by killing a PRIMORDIAL, Jason knew that it would be a lot harder for the Pirates to rise through the ranks. The only reason there were so many PRIMORDIAL Rank monsters was because they were unintelligent beasts who attacked on instinct, and many of them just got lucky enough to kill a PRIMORDIAL and gain the power.</p>
<p>So Jason knew he wouldn’t be finding any PRIMORDIALS among these nobodies. Jason passed through the port town, and as he passed over other towns, he could see many pirates making their way towards the Port Town as the news about the Kraken spread very quickly. Jason kept his senses sharp for any sign of a PRIMORDIAL, but there were none, and it wasn’t until Jason finally got to the main Central City a few minutes later that he finally sensed a PRIMORDIAL Rank being. In fact, there were a lot of them.</p>
<p>At the gate leading into the central city was a man wearing an elaborate robe and looking over the guards manning the gates. The second Jason laid eyes on him, Jason knew that this was a PRIMORDIAL. But he wasn’t the only one. Deeper inside the city were about three more PRIMORDIALS. Two of them were extremely weak, and it was obvious that they had only killed one PRIMORDIAL in their lives to just break through from TITAN to PRIMORDIAL, but the third person was powerful. He wasn’t as strong as Jason, but he had at least 50% Core Essence Quantity and 50% Core Essence Purity. That had to be the Pirate King, and he would be Jason’s next victim.</p>
<p>The moment Jason’s dragons appeared from the sky, every single person in the central City was on guard. The guards at the city gate all looked up in shock, and the PRIMORDIAL Ranked guard immediately raised his voice and told them to sound the alarms.</p>
<p>GONG!</p>
<p>GONG!</p>
<p>A massive wooden beam slammed into what looked like a rusted church bell, and the sound echoed across the entire city. The pirates around all poked their heads out from pubs and whorehouses as they tried to figure out what was going on. Jason spoke to Valeria through their mental link as he began sending his subordinates towards the ground to begin the fight.</p>
<p>’I’m going to take care of the PRIMORDIAL. You can lead the Legion and take care of any other Pirate that gets in the way. I’ve told them to follow your orders. I’ll be back soon.’</p>
<p>Valeria nodded, and she began heading toward the centre of the city while Jason headed straight down towards the PRIMORDIAL Rank guard on the ground.</p>
<p>CAW!</p>
<p>Helios let out a cry as it followed after Valeria, and Jason told it to watch over her properly.</p>
<p>’If anything happens to her, I’ll turn you into a feather coat.’</p>
<p>CAW!</p>
<p>Helios let out another loud cry in annoyance, and Jason couldn’t help but laugh when he realised that Helios had just cursed at him. This bastard was growing up fast. Jason turned his attention back to the fight in front of him, and Jason watched as the PRIMORDIAL ranked guard pulled out two swords from scabbards on either side of him. The swords glowed with immense power, and in the next moment, he shot off to meet Jason in the sky. The guard was someone who had lived his whole life as a pirate. Battle and conquest were a part of his everyday life, and he was more than confident that he could take on one attacker with their dragon.</p>
<p>DOON!</p>
<p>But Jason suddenly unleashed the full might of his mana on the people below him, and many of the weaker TITANs immediately fell to their knees and passed out. The stronger TITANs groaned as they were almost pushed to the ground, and the PRIMORDIAL Rank guard was thrown out of the air! The guard slammed into the ground in shock as he realised that he was facing something far stronger than he thought, and he turned around and immediately took off to try and escape from Jason. The guard might be a PRIMORDIAL Rank, but he wasn’t a fool. When faced with a danger far above their strength, a pirate would choose the cowardly option of running away rather than facing it every time. Jason wasn’t surprised by this at all, and Jason simply reached into his alternate dimension and pulled out the [Spear of Heaven].</p>
<p>The golden spear shone with a brilliant radiance as it was unleashed, and Jason pulled back and threw the spear towards the PRIMORDIAL.</p>
<p>WOOSH!</p>
<p>The spear flew true, and before the pirate could even take ten more steps, he was stabbed through the back and pinned to the floor.</p>
<p>SQUELCH!</p>
<p>——</p>
<p>The user has killed [1] PRIMORDIAL Rank Caprian</p>
<p>+650,000 Kill Points</p>
<p>——</p>
<p>The other pirates who were watching the fight were shocked when they saw the guard die so easily. The guard hadn’t even used any of his abilities to try and fight back even once. The other pirates couldn’t understand why things had ended up like this, but the guard already knew that running away was literally the only thing he could do in that situation. The guard was the only one who could feel Jason’s mana, and the guard knew that Jason was already close to filling up his Primordial Core Quantity and Purity. A monster like this couldn’t be taken down using brute force.</p>
<p>Jason ignored all the TITANs around him, and he put his hand out and said:</p>
<p>"Resurrect."</p>
<p>The guard’s body immediately jolted before a hand shot out from his body, and his soul slowly climbed its way out into the real world. The other TITANs around watched this happen with looks of absolute disbelief on their faces, and they were left even more stunned when the resurrected guard looked towards them with a hostile look on his face. It was obvious that the guard was more than ready to kill them at that moment. But Jason told the guard to forget about them, and he ordered him to head towards the location of the Pirate King. Jason didn’t come to this world to kill lower-ranked TITANs; he came here to get as much Primordial Essence as he could.</p>
<p>The guard immediately started heading towards the eastern side of the city, and Jason first looked towards the central region to check on Valeria. Valeria was currently in the middle of a bloody battle between hundreds of Legion subordinates and pirates. She had come down from her dragon, and she was using her skills and raw strength to tear through the TITANs like they didn’t exist. Although Valeria was just a TITAN Rank II, she was already very close to becoming a TITAN Rank III, and Jason knew that she might get there after this battle.</p>
<p>’I can’t believe she said she didn’t enjoy this. What a fucking liar.’</p>
<p>Jason huffed in amusement as he saw the expression on Valeria’s face, and he turned his dragon around and began heading towards the Pirate King. Although Valeria had told Jason in the past that she only became a mercenary because she wanted to avenge her mother, Jason could easily see that Valeria enjoyed the thrill of the fight far more than she was willing to admit. There was a huge smile on Valeria’s face as she stabbed and tore her way through multiple TITANs, and she wasn’t bothered by any of the blood spilling around her. It was obvious that Valeria had been born for the battlefield.</p>
