<p>[The User is attempting to combine two major power sources]</p>
<p>[The combination of Qi and Mana is considered detrimental to the body of any mage. The system advises the user against this.]</p>
<p>[The user has bypassed the warnings]</p>
<p>[The user has achieved Resonance]</p>
<p>[Resonance: 10%]</p>
<p>This was Jason’s current maximum, and Jason decided he wouldn’t go any higher for now! Ten percent resonance was more than enough to take care of this bitch! Jason let out a tense breath as he felt his mana and Qi beginning to merge! The process of combining the two power sources had become a lot easier than it had been in the past! After training his Resonance so much, the process was almost second nature to Jason now! But the end result from that combination was just as powerful as ever! Jason’s mana suddenly vanished, and the Weeping Angel turned in Jason’s direction in surprise, but in the next moment, a new power surged to life within Jason, and Jason felt every cell in his body begin to vibrate like he had just pumped them full of raw adrenaline!</p>
<p>DOON!</p>
<p>The battlefield trembled as a purple wave of energy washed over the area, and the Weeping Angel felt shocked when it was pushed back by the new power! This strange combination of Mana and Qi wasn’t something the angel was familiar with, and the angel was shocked that this power from a mere TITAN could affect a PRIMORDIAL like it! But the Angel didn’t allow the surprise to stop it, and it brought its sword forward and unleashed another devastating horizontal attack!</p>
<p>SLASH!</p>
<p>A wave of pure white energy flew straight for Jason, and this time, Jason put his hand out and created a shield from his mana! Normally, Jason’s TITAN mana would’ve never been able to stand up to the PRIMORDIAL attack, but the fact that Jason was using Resonance mana gave him an insane boost in power, and Jason planted his feet and grunted as the white mana slammed into him! The mana poured over Jason with all its wrath, but it did nothing, and Jason simply threw his shield down and charged forward once the attack died down!</p>
<p>BOOM!</p>
<p>Jason shot into the air and pulled the [Death’s Mirage] back for an attack! The sword began to grow as Jason poured more of his mana into it, but before Jason could even get close, he was blindsided by a massive white shield that the Angel had pulled out of nowhere!</p>
<p>BOOM!</p>
<p>Jason crashed into the ground like a meteor, and Jason had to quickly move out of the way as a massive light spear flew through the air and slammed into where he was!</p>
<p>BOOM!</p>
<p>Jason narrowed his eyes as the Angel pulled its arm back to throw another light spear, and Jason activated [Flash Freeze]! Jason already knew that the skill he was about to activate would be a lot more powerful since it was being powered by his resonance rather than just his mana, but even with that knowledge, there was nothing that could’ve prepared Jason for the sheer intensity of cold that shot through the air at that moment! Everything for miles in every direction immediately froze solid! There was no warning at all, and nothing was spared! One moment, the Angel had released its spear, and in the next, the spear had been frozen in place just a few meters away from Jason! Behind the spear, the Weeping Angel had also been frozen solid, but the Angel unleashed its mana, and a loud sound echoed through the area!</p>
<p>Crack! Crack!</p>
<p>Shatter!</p>
<p>The ice holding the Angel in place broke into millions of pieces, and the Angel turned to try and find Jason again, but Jason was already on the move! Jason ran across the landscape quickly while watching multiple of his subordinates gather around the Angel! All these subordinates were the weaker Rook Rank I’s who Jason hadn’t upgraded yet, but Jason knew they would be more than enough for what he wanted to do! The Angel brought its sword down towards one of the Rooks, and Jason immediately activated [King’s Substitute] and transported to a Rook behind the Angel! Jason’s sword glowed with the purple light of resonance, and he slashed it across the Angel’s back and clipped one of its wings!</p>
<p>SCREEECCHHHH!</p>
<p>For the first time since the fight began, the angel let out a scream of pain, and the sound was so intense that Jason felt his eardrums shaking! Jason grimaced as he almost lost control over his resonance, but Jason managed to keep his focus, and that was the only thing that saved him from the sudden sword slash the Angel sent his way! The Angel turned around sharply while swinging its sword towards Jason, and Jason had to quickly use [King’s Substitute] to get out of the way!</p>
<p>WOOSH!</p>
<p>The blade tore through empty air, and Jason added even more mana to his sword as he swung it toward the Angel’s neck! The sword grew till it was nearly fifty meters long as it travelled in a horizontal slash, but the Angel brought up one of its wings and used that to block the attack! But the moment the resonance mana touched the angel’s wing, the entire wing began to decay at an alarming rate, and the Angel let out another screech as it pushed Jason back and took a large leap away! The angel created a large magic circle and activated [Angel’s Wrath]! A massive ball of energy appeared from the magic circle and began to travel straight for Jason, and Jason threw the [Death’s Mirage] back into his Alternate Dimension before he pulled out the massive Raygun Artefact! Jason planted his feet on the ground and held the artefact with both hands at his waist like he was about to fire off a massive machine gun! The gun sparked and rippled with energy as Jason loaded it to the brim with his Resonance mana, and Jason saw a message pop up from the system!</p>
<p>[The user is attempting to release a significant amount of Resonance energy. The system believes this could cause serious damage to the user’s body!]</p>
<p>’Who cares!?’</p>
<p>Jason grit his teeth as the energy in the Raygun tripled in intensity! The world around Jason began to shake and rumble as the power grew to even greater heights, and Jason could feel his internal organs tearing as they were flooded with the Resonance mana! But Jason knew that this was it! With this attack, Jason was certain he could take down his first PRIMORDIAL, so Jason simply grit his teeth and bore the pain as he finally shifted the gun into firing mode and unleashed all the gathered energy!</p>
<p>KA-BOOMM!!</p>
<p>A sound like a nuclear explosion tore through the area as a purple light ripped through the space between the two fighters! The [Angel’s Wrath] that had been flying towards Jason was immediately decimated as the resonance energy tore a hole straight through the attack and travelled towards the Angel! The Angel realised immediately just how dangerous this attack was, and it brought its remaining wings forward to try and form a shield! The Resonance mana slammed into the Angel’s wings, and for a split second, it looked like the Angel was actually going to stop it! But then, a massive rune on Jason’s arm shone with a brilliant red light, and Jason activated [Pyromatic Combustion]! The world exploded!</p>
<p>KA-BOOOMMM!!!</p>
<p>An explosion that was large enough to tower over the entire island resounded across the region! A massive column of fire rose into the air and split the clouds for miles in every direction, and all the trees and mountains in the area were immediately reduced to cinders as the sheer intensity of the explosion washed over them! Even the frozen mountains on the other side of the island began to melt from the heat, and the fighters all had to stop whatever they were doing as the shockwave slammed into them!</p>
