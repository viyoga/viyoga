<p>Jason couldn’t stop his shock when he saw the sheer size of the monster hiding within the dust cloud! Not even Arcticus had been this large! From where Jason was standing, he noticed that the Snake boss was long enough to wrap around a mountain multiple times, and it had coiled its body around more than twenty times so that it would fit properly into the mountain! But once the mountain was destroyed, the serpent uncoiled itself to reveal a gigantic monster that rivalled the Leviathans Jason had only heard about in stories! The giant serpent slashed its tail across the air to get rid of the smoke, and Jason finally got a good look at it!</p>
<p>The serpent had a golden head with two silver crosses painted over where its eyes should be! Its body was covered in strange black scales that seemed to move and shift with a mind of their own, and Jason’s eyes narrowed as he saw multiple Sky Serpent mobs slithering out from the giant snake’s scales like they were being born from an abyss! Was this how the Sky Serpents were created? No wonder there were so many of them! The Sky Serpents that slithered out from the boss immediately charged towards Jason, and Jason wasted no time as he called on Arcticus!</p>
<p>BWOOOOOHHH!</p>
<p>Arcticus appeared from Jason’s Alternate Dimension and immediately unleashed a blast of cold breath that enveloped the entire region! The weak Sky Serpents were immediately frozen into solid blocks, and they fell from the sky and shattered against the ground like sculptures! But before Arcticus could continue its attack, a giant tail suddenly flashed across the air and slammed into Arcticus’ mouth! Arcticus was thrown to the ground from the impact, and Jason whistled at the impressive feat before he quickly moved out of the way as the tail came back for him! The massive tail missed Jason by only a few centimeters, and Jason felt a smile appear on his face as he looked down at the giant monster!</p>
<p>Jason couldn’t believe it! This snake was a TITAN Rank III! There was no way a beast this size would have this much strength and power unless it was at least that strong! Jason saw Arcticus getting up from where he had fallen, and Arcticus opened its mouth to unleash another ice attack, but this time the snake was completely ready for it, and it opened its mouth and created a magic circle that suddenly unleashed a massive blast of super-compressed wind! The wind slammed into Arcticus like a fucking bullet train, and Arcticus was thrown off his feet and crashed into the ground again!</p>
<p>’So this is a Blue Gate boss.’</p>
<p>Jason felt his lips splitting into a smile as the boss let out another massive roar while slamming its tail into the ground! There was an electric feeling going through the boss’ body, almost like a predator that was on the verge of winning against other predators for territory! The boss had a feeling that it could win this fight, and honestly, Jason couldn’t have asked for anything else! Jason had been hoping desperately that the boss would be at least this strong and have at least this much intelligence. Because now, Jason knew that the boss wasn’t going to die from just one punch! For the first time in a long time, Jason could finally stretch his legs and fight properly! Jason reached to the side, and his hand vanished into his Alternate Dimension, and Jason pulled out the Death’s Mirage! Jason poured mana into the weapon, and it glowed with a bright blue light as it formed a meter-long blade from pure mana!</p>
<p>Bat wings erupted from Jason’s back as Jason’s eyes turned blood red, and Jason immediately dashed to the side as the boss released another super-concentrated wave to try to take Jason down!</p>
<p>BOOM!</p>
<p>The wave of air blasted into a mountain in the distance and completely obliterated it, but Jason didn’t care! Jason glided across the air quickly as he made his way towards the boss’s head, and Jason swerved out of the way as the boss swung its tail towards him even faster than last time! Jason spun his body in a perfect barrel roll, and he easily dodged around the serpent tail before he activated [Hands of Providence]! Thousands of shadow hands erupted from around Jason and grabbed hold of the Serpent’s tail as it passed by, and Jason used it as a launchpad to catapult himself forward!</p>
<p>The serpent roared as its scales rippled, and Jason’s eyes widened when he saw three massive snake mobs being born from the scales! The snakes immediately charged towards Jason, and Jason swung his sword in a perfect arc towards one of them!</p>
<p>Slash!</p>
<p>The sword easily took off the snake’s head, and blood splashed across the air like a geyser! Jason immediately activated his [Blood Art], and he grabbed hold of the blood and swung it towards the other snakes! The blood splashed across their bodies, and Jason tucked his wings in and dropped from the sky just as one of the snakes was about to take a bite out of him! The two snakes immediately charged at Jason, and Jason snapped his finger!</p>
<p>[Crimson Hellfire]!</p>
<p>PHWOOM!</p>
<p>SCREEECHH!!</p>
<p>The snakes all let out pained screams as Jason ignited the blood from the snake he had killed, and a deep red flame erupted across their bodies and began to burn them to cinders! Jason spread his wings once again and flew to the side as he continued heading towards the large boss! The boss let out another intense cry, and Jason cursed as it birthed more serpents from its scales! Jason called on his subordinates, and all the Rook and Bishop Ranks in Jason’s Alternate Dimension appeared in the real world as they quickly went to confront the beasts! Thousands of Machina! Hundreds of dragons and dragonborn all charged straight for the snakes, and massive explosions echoed through the sky as the battle began immediately!</p>
<p>Jason ignored the fighting and continued making his way towards the large boss! The boss noticed Jason’s approach, and another magic circle appeared in front of its mouth! For a moment, Jason was ready to dodge out of the way since he thought it would fire another wave of super-concentrated air, but Jason was shocked when he felt a force pulling him instead! The magic circle that the boss just used was a giant gravity pull, and the skill immediately grabbed hold of everything around the boss and pulled it in like a black hole! Jason cursed as he felt his momentum shift forward as he headed straight towards the boss’ open mouth, and Jason quickly activated [Hands of Providence]! Multiple shadow hands erupted around Jason and grabbed hold of the boss’ body, and Jason used that to pull himself out of the way before the boss clamped its mouth down hard!</p>
<p>BOOM!</p>
<p>Jason landed on the boss’ strange vibrating scales, and he quickly dashed forward as a massive snake erupted from the scales to try and consume him!</p>
<p>[Black Fog]!</p>
<p>Phwoom!</p>
<p>Jason activated a skill to block the snakes from seeing him, and a giant cloud of black smoke immediately erupted across the air! The snakes all charged into the black smoke, but none of them could find Jason, and the giant boss roared in anger as it released a wave of air to get rid of the smoke! The smoke was blasted away by the attack, and it revealed Jason in a ready stance with his hand pulled back! Jason was gripping the long golden [Spear of Heaven], and just from looking at it, the giant boss could tell that this weapon was dangerous! The boss immediately unleashed another wave to try to push Jason away, but Jason’s body erupted in blue Qi that he sent towards the spear! The Spear was covered in the powerful energy and began to glow with a bright blue light before Jason slammed his feet down and threw the spear towards the boss’ mouth!</p>
<p>TWOOM!</p>
