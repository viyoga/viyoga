<p>The great wall shattered into millions of pieces as the ice shards rained down on them, and the Rooks were immediately decimated! The ice shards continued on as they flew towards Jason, but they had already lost a lot of their power after striking the massive Great Wall, so the defensive artefacts that Jason activated were strong enough to stop them completely! Jason felt the Beast finally turn its attention towards him again, and at that moment, there was a sudden light that sparked in the Beast’s eyes, almost like it had come to a realisation!</p>
<p>Jason didn’t know it, but the Beast had realised in that moment that it had been fighting the wrong battle all along! All the other enemies that had been surrounding it weren’t important because Jason was the one who was controlling all of them! That meant that the moment it managed to kill Jason, it would have won this battle! Jason felt the intense pressure from the Beast slam down on him, and the winds suddenly sharpened into a massive typhoon that sent a deep chill into Jason’s bones! The Beast stood like a creature of myth within the flashing white winds, and its glowing white eyes shone with a deep malevolence as they fixed themselves on Jason! Jason kept his gaze fixed on the beast, and he watched it with bated breath as he waited to see what it would do next!</p>
<p>The Beast suddenly vanished into the wind, and Jason quickly called a Rook Rank III dragon out from his Alternate Dimension and activated [King’s Substitute]! Jason vanished towards the other side of the battlefield, and at the next moment, the Beast’s feet slammed down on the dragon and crushed it with its massive weight!</p>
<p>[A Rook Rank III dragon has been killed...]</p>
<p>BUOOOHHHH!!!</p>
<p>The beast let out another bellow as it chased Jason down, and Jason coated his body in Qi and dashed into the sky before sending a message to Ragnar! Ragnar appeared from the side and let out another massive [Mana Blast] towards the Beast’s head, but the Beast completely ignored the attack since it knew that there was nothing that Ragnar could do to hurt it! The Beast continued to run Jason down, and Jason immediately summoned another Rook from his Legion and activated [King’s Substitute]! Jason vanished from where he had been, and he reappeared behind the Beast’s head before he pulled out the SSS-Rank Artefact [Raygun]! The artefact was a giant Cannonblaster that shone with a brilliant silver, and the moment Jason activated it, a bright blue light lit up across the region before it unleashed a blast of pure energy towards the Beast’s head!</p>
<p>BOOOOOMM!!!</p>
<p>BUUUUUOOOHHH!!</p>
<p>This time, the scream that the beast let out was one of true pain as it felt the energy blast burn the back of its head! The [Raygun] was designed to absorb and amplify the mana of whoever fires it, so Jason’s TITAN-Rank mana had been absorbed and amplified to a level that could actually cause damage to the beast! Jason clicked the gun again as he prepared to fire a second shot, but he frowned when nothing happened!</p>
<p>’Fuck, that’s right! It has a cooldown of thirty seconds!’</p>
<p>This thought crossed Jason’s mind, and Jason quickly called on Braga and Yelena and told them to give the beast hell! The two Machina Bishops appeared from Jason’s Alternate Dimension, and they aimed their massive cannon blasters at the beast before suddenly unleashing pure destruction!</p>
<p>BOOOM!</p>
<p>The Beast let out another bellow as it swiped its head through the air to get rid of them, and Jason immediately shouted out to Azrael and asked him how much time was left!</p>
<p>’Just one more minute, my king! The rune is almost finished!’</p>
<p>Jason grit his teeth as he watched the Beast swiping its hand through the air to try and grab Braga and Yelena! Stalling for another minute wasn’t going to be easy at all! Jason could tell that the Beast was already starting to catch on to all the tricks that Jason was using, and Jason had no idea what else the Beast had up its sleeve, so there was a chance that he would be hit by an attack that he never even saw coming. Jason landed back on the ground a couple hundred meters away from the Beast, and he watched as the Beast suddenly swung its arm towards Braga! Braga managed to move out of the Beast’s reach, but Jason was shocked when a blue club made from ice suddenly appeared in the Beast’s hand, and this gave it the extra reach it needed as it slammed into Braga with extreme force! Braga was thrown to the ground and crashed into the ice, and the Beast turned its eyes towards Jason once again!</p>
<p>[The subordinate [Braga] has been killed...]</p>
<p>The ice club in the beast’s hand grew larger as it began to stalk Jason down, and Jason took a step back. Jason was about to keep moving again, but he suddenly heard Gina speak through their mental link!</p>
<p>’Babe! I’m here! I’m at the location of the Dimensional Tear!’</p>
<p>The moment Jason heard that voice, he felt an intense surge of emotion pour out from his heart, and Jason’s lips split into a vindictive smile as he realised that he had already won this battle! The Beast ran towards Jason with its club raised for a hit, and Jason knew that he could simply switch places with a Rook right now and escape from this world! But Jason refused! Jason had suffered at the hands of this bastard, and Jason wasn’t going to leave this world until he left his own mark on the Beast’s skin! Jason spoke to Azrael as he jumped back and pointed the raygun up at the Beast’s head!</p>
<p>’Send me your location!’</p>
<p>Azrael was surprised by this order, and he told Jason that the rune was not ready yet!</p>
<p>’I just need a few more seconds before –’</p>
<p>’I don’t care, you bastard! Send me your location and continue with the rune! I’ll drag the beast there myself, so you’d better be finished by the time we get there!’</p>
<p>Jason cut Azrael off quickly, and Azrael realised Jason’s plan immediately! Azrael quickly sent the location to Jason, and Jason grinned even wider as he clicked the raygun and unleashed hell on the beast!</p>
<p>BOOOOOM!</p>
<p>A blast of pure mana surged up towards the beast’s head, and the Beast raised its club and used it as a makeshift shield to try and block the attack!</p>
<p>KA-BOOOM!!</p>
<p>But the Beast had seriously underestimated the sheer destructive power of the blast, and it was caught off guard as the attack absolutely destroyed the club and sent ice shards raining down on its face! The Beast raised its hands to try and protect itself, and once it was sure that there were no more ice shards, it turned towards Jason and let out a furious roar! Jason saw true anger simmering within the glowing white eyes of the Beast, and Jason laughed as he wrapped his body in Qi and immediately took off! The next few seconds turned into an intense game of cat and mouse as Jason raced across the stormy blizzard with the wild Beast right on his tail! Jason hadn’t used his Qi at all for the past few minutes, so he had more than enough to carry him across the landscape without any issue at all! Jason could hear the tremors from the Beast’s rampage behind him, and Jason knew that if he dared to look back at all, then he was going to get caught immediately! But the location that Azrael had given Jason wasn’t that far away, and it wasn’t long before Jason could see Azrael in the distance.</p>
