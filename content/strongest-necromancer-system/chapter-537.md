<p>For a moment, Braga felt a sudden fear come over him at the mere thought that Jason might be a TITAN, but that only lasted for a moment, and in the next moment, Braga’s fear was immediately replaced by an intense surge of anger! Braga refused to acknowledge this power! Who the hell did Jason think he was to talk to the Machina this way! They were the leaders of this domain! The superior race! Jason was only a goddamn criminal from a backwater world, and he dared to speak to them like this!? Braga realised that he needed to make a statement here! He needed to show Jason that the Machina were not afraid of his empty threats! The Machina could do whatever the fuck they wanted, and there was nothing Jason could do about it!</p>
<p>"You think you can threaten the Machina, you peasant!? Someone from a dirty, useless world like yours could never understand the superiority we possess! I was fighting wars before you even learned the meaning of the word, and I have killed more creatures than you will ever know! Do you want to know what your greatest crime was!? It was daring to lay your filthy fingers on our people!? Your crime was thinking that you had the right to go against our Overlord! And for that crime, we are going to kill every single person in this pathetic world! I will make sure to keep you alive long enough to watch this world burn! And when we’re done with this world, we’re going to return to your dirty, disgusting backwater world and eradicate it for your foolishness! KILL THEM ALL!!"</p>
<p>Braga’s order rang out over the battlefield like a war cry, and Jason watched as all the giant robots around him suddenly began to shift! The large domes on the robot’s chest that had been shining with bright lights suddenly began to rotate quickly, and Jason realised that they were about to fire at him! Jason called on every single Pawn in his Legion, and they formed multiple tight circles around the group of Varigans and immediately activated the [Stonewall] skill! And at the same moment, the large robots suddenly unleashed a massive blast of energy straight at Jason and the Varigans!</p>
<p>BOOOM!!!</p>
<p>The attack had been empowered using high-level mana crystals from SSS-Rank beasts, so it possessed enough power to injure any mage in the SSS-Rank, and it could easily kill those in the lower ranks! Jason could sense the power behind the attacks, and that was why he made his subordinates form multiple [Stonewalls] rather than just one! But even with the multiple layers of defence, the moment the beam slammed into the first line of Pawns, it immediately eradicated them, and the beam moved onto the second row and slammed into them as well before eradicating them just as easily! Jason realised that the Pawns weren’t going to be enough, and he immediately called out his Rooks!</p>
<p>Jason’s Rooks flashed out from the Alternate Dimension, and they immediately formed another protective circle behind the final line of Pawns before activating the Legion skill [Great Wall]!! A towering wall of pure mana erupted to life around the Varigan soldiers, and Jason could feel the air vibrate with power as the large light beams broke through the last of his Pawns and slammed into the [Great Wall]! The beams immediately lost ninety percent of their power due to the innate power of the defensive skill, and this was enough to finally stop the beams in their tracks!</p>
<p>The rest of his Rooks quickly charged straight for the giant robots with the Bishop-Rank Machina Yelena leading them! The moment the Rooks appeared, the soldiers and tanks on the ground immediately opened fire like they had been waiting for this very moment! Some of the Rooks began to return the attacks, and Jason told all the dragons to head towards the giant robots and destroy them, while the other subordinates should focus on returning fire at the soldiers! Gina and the Skyking also came out from the Alternate Dimension, and they began to head for the air carriers that were trying to join the battle, and Jason warned Gina to be careful when she got up there!</p>
<p>’I’ll be careful, babe!’</p>
<p>Gina responded quickly as she continued heading towards the first carrier! Gina was currently buzzing with adrenaline from the Rank Promotion she just had, so her body was vibrating with a ton of energy that she needed to release! Jason noticed Gina’s excitement, and he told Ragnar to look out for her and make sure she didn’t push herself too far! Ragnar roared as it followed after Gina, and Trigon led the rest of the dragons and headed straight for the first giant robot before unleashing a massive blast of fire that slammed into the robot’s head!</p>
<p>BOOM!</p>
<p>Jason watched to see how much damage that attack would do, but he narrowed his eyes when the smoke cleared to show that there was hardly any damage at all! A defensive forcefield shimmered around the robot’s body and Jason realised that there was some sort of magical protection around all the giant robots! Jason cursed. This could make things a lot harder! The giant robot that Trigon attacked suddenly raised its hand and unleashed more than a dozen rockets towards Trigon, and Trigon had to immediately fly out of the way to dodge the attacks!</p>
<p>BOOM!</p>
<p>Another attack from the air carriers suddenly slammed into the [Great Wall] and Jason clicked his tongue in annoyance. The [Great Wall] was the only thing standing between the Varigans and certain death, and Jason knew it couldn’t hold up forever. The Aegis was strong, but there was no way it would stand up to multiple SSS-Rank light beams! Jason’s hand twitched as he thought about joining the battle, but he remembered Azrael’s words from before, and he decided not to rush things. Jason looked at his tab himself to check how much mana he had, and he frowned when he saw that he was at just about half capacity!</p>
<p>[Mana Points: 1,534,232/3,460,956]</p>
<p>Jason had used up a lot of mana to kill the Pharynx earlier, and that was why his mana was so low! But even though Jason was trying to preserve mana, he knew that he didn’t need to use too much mana to reanimate dead monsters, and Jason looked towards the body of the dead Pharynx and sent out a surge of mana!</p>
<p>Doon!</p>
<p>Jason’s mana surged out from him in a wave of pure power, and he activated a skill!</p>
<p>[Necrotic Reanimation] has been activated!</p>
<p>SCREEEECHHH!!!</p>
<p>A loud screech echoed across the battlefield as the dead Pharynx suddenly came back to life, and the Machina cursed as they realised that they had another beast to worry about! Some of the Machina soldiers immediately switched their attention towards the Pharynx and began firing at it, but the Pharynx was an SSS-Rank beast, so their weapons barely even scratched it! Jason gave the Pharynx an order, and the beast immediately turned towards the giant robots at the side and opened its mouth to unleash a blast of pure mana!</p>
<p>TWOOOOMM!!</p>
<p>At the same time, Trigon unleashed another blast of fire towards the same robot, and Jason hummed when the attacks slammed into the defensive shield around the robot! The defensive shield shimmered and cracked from the intense force before it suddenly shattered into a million pieces! Once that happened, Jason knew that the robot had become more vulnerable to attacks, and he immediately instructed all his subordinates to focus all their attacks on that one robot!</p>
<p>TWOOOOM!</p>
<p>Multiple dragon breaths flew straight towards the robot, and the large Pharynx unleashed another blast of mana! The robot tried to bring up some defences, but it wasn’t able to withstand the pressure of so many attacks, and its entire upper section was suddenly blown off by a massive blast!</p>
<p>BOOM!</p>
