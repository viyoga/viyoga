<p>All it took was one hit from the beast, and a Bishop-Rank was crushed to paste! Jason put his hand forward and called on his Rooks, and multiple Rook Rank II subordinates appeared in the air around the beast! The beast turned around sharply and let out a bellow when it saw the Rooks, and Jason wasted no time as he commanded them to activate a Legion skill.</p>
<p>[Kamikaze] has been activated!</p>
<p>KA-BOOOM!</p>
<p>A massive explosion echoed through the sky, and Jason immediately dashed to the side as he felt a pressure gathering in front of him! The beast appeared in a spray of white snow and slammed its hand down on where Jason had been, and Jason noticed that there was a large bruising on its chest from the explosion. The bruise was barely even there, and Jason could see that it was already healing, so it was obvious that the beast hardly even felt the attack. Jason cursed as he flipped through the air and slashed across a large building with the [Death’s Mirage]! The building was split horizontally by Jason’s attack, and Jason grabbed the large building as it fell and threw it towards the beast! The building was already frozen solid, so it flew forward like a bullet, and the beast was forced to raise a hand to block the attack. Jason dashed to the side and called on many more Rook Rank subordinates before telling them to provide cover for him!</p>
<p>BOOM!</p>
<p>BOOM!</p>
<p>BOOM!</p>
<p>Massive explosions echoed through the world of Yortsed as the Rooks constantly killed themselves to try and provide support for their king! The giant beast was roaring like a wild beast as it swung its arm around erratically to try to kill all the Rooks! The winds grew in intensity, and there was a sudden spike in the chill around the city that made the explosions weaker, but Jason didn’t stand still to see what was going on. Instead, he was in a serious conversation with his system.</p>
<p>’I need heat. Lots of it. This beast thrives in the cold, and it’s strong enough to transform any world it arrives in into its territory. I need to go to a world that has enough heat to fight back against the cold.’</p>
<p>Jason knew that the main reason things were so difficult for him was due to this cold. Without this cold, not only would Jason be able to bring all his subordinates out of the Alternate Dimension so that they could put up a proper fight, but Jason would also be able to focus on the fight without freezing his ass off! Jason told the system to give him directions to a world that could withstand the intense heat of the beast, and Jason cursed at the message he got back.</p>
<p>[The closest world that might provide enough heat to withstand the permafreeze of the Beast is a Rank III world known as Ha’lez. But the route leading to Ha’lez passes through the Rank IV world Gerani. The system advises the user not to consider stepping foot in a Rank IV world at his current power level.]</p>
<p>BWOOOHHH!</p>
<p>BOOM!</p>
<p>Another massive explosion went off from behind Jason, and Jason called on Ragnar and Trigon and told them to go and make sure the beast didn’t catch up to him! The dragons went off to do as they were told, and Jason dived back into his conversation with the system. At the rate things were going, Jason didn’t think he could follow the system’s advice.</p>
<p>[The subordinate [Trigon] has been killed]</p>
<p>[Would the user like to...]</p>
<p>’Stop sending the message and just do it!’</p>
<p>Jason responded in irritation, and he felt some mana leave his body as Trigon and Braga were both revived. Jason activated his Arcane Art and fell into the shadows before he quickly started making his way through this world while thinking about the system’s warning and whether or not there was anything else he could do to kill this beast. Taking the beast to that Rank III world was the best plan Jason had come up with as of yet, and Jason was sure that it would even the playing fields a lot more, so wasn’t it worth taking a risk and passing through that Rank IV world? Jason immediately asked the system to give him all the information it had on the Rank IV world, and the system brought up another warning.</p>
<p>[The system does not advise the user to -]</p>
<p>’I know! Just give me what I asked for!’</p>
<p>Jason cut off the message as he felt something approaching from above, and Jason quickly shot out from the shadows and narrowly dodged another wild swing from the Beast! The beast had escaped from the Rooks, and it managed to track Jason down even though Jason was hiding in the shadows and limiting his mana output! There was a look of fury painted on the beast’s face, and Jason could only wonder how in the world this bastard managed to track him!</p>
<p>’Is this how it was able to find me even though I already went through more than twenty Dimensional Leaps. Its mana sensing abilities are insane!’</p>
<p>Jason knew that hiding in the shadows always masks a portion of his mana from people around him, so the fact that the beast could track him using only that tiny amount of stray mana was impressive! The beast squeezed both hands into fists and raised them over its head like a hammer before bringing them down on Jason in a heavy swing that shattered the earth and sent a massive tremor traveling across the world! Jason had activated [King’s Substitute] at the last moment before the attack hit, and he appeared on top of a building a few miles away and watched as the beast eradicated one of his Rooks. That would have hurt like a bitch. Jason let out a tired breath, and he blinked when he heard Gina’s voice coming through their mental link.</p>
<p>’Babe, is everything okay? I thought you were just taking care of something small, but you’ve been gone for a while.’</p>
<p>Jason hummed back at her in a tired voice, and he made sure to catch his breath before he spoke.</p>
<p>’I’ll explain everything to you once I get back, I promise. Just wait there for me and protect the royal family, okay?’</p>
<p>’What? Babe, what’s going on? Did something happen on the mission? Do you want me to come-?’</p>
<p>’No, just stay there. I’ll explain everything later, I promise. Just let me focus for now.’</p>
<p>Jason cut off the mental link, and he narrowed his eyes as he saw the beast turn around to look for him again. Jason knew that Gina might be useful in this fight, but Jason didn’t want to risk losing her against a beast that could kill a Bishop-Rank in one hit. Jason wasn’t worried about the other bishops dying since they were just dead souls that followed all his orders, but Gina was a real person with a mind of her own, and dying in this battle might do far more damage to her mental and physical state. Even if there was a high chance Jason would be able to revive Gina after she died, he just didn’t want to risk it. The system started bringing up the information that Jason had asked it for, and Jason read through it quickly while moving into the shadows and making his way out of the city.</p>
<p>[The Rank IV world of Gerani is known to be home to multiple TITAN-Rank beings. The air pressure in that world is several million times higher than Earth, and the Gravity is several thousand times stronger than even the strongest Rank III world. Every region within Gerani is a territory belonging to one or more TITAN-Rank beasts, and there is a high likelihood that the user will be attacked the moment he steps foot within the territory of these beasts. The user is already trying to defeat one TITAN-Rank beast, therefore the system does not believe it is wise to gain even more enemies.]</p>
