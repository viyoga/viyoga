<p>’Fuck. I can’t ignore this anymore.’</p>
<p>Jason realised that he could no longer turn a blind eye to what his oldest brother was doing. Jason honestly did not want to get involved with Gregory at all, and if Jason had a choice, he would rather live his life without having to go against his brother since the family feud had been between Gregory and their father, but it was obvious that Gregory was not interested in leaving Jason alone! Jason needed to deal with this before it became something more serious!</p>
<p>’And what was he saying about birthright? Is he talking about the system?’</p>
<p>Jason still remembered how he had gotten this system in the first place. The necklace his father gifted him on his eighteenth birthday was what awakened it, and Jason couldn’t help but feel like that necklace was a lot more special than he thought! Was this system meant to be some sort of birthright?</p>
<p>’Did Dad give me something he was meant to give to Gregory?’</p>
<p>Jason frowned as this thought crossed his mind, but Jason eventually shook his head and decided not to let it bother him. Regardless of who was meant to get this system in the first place, it had ended up with him, and that was all that mattered. Even if Gregory claimed that it was his birthright, the bastard could go fuck himself! Jason decided right then that he would have to send out some spies to start looking into Gregory and the Avarans he was gathering! If Jason continued to ignore these bastards, then Jason was sure that they would eventually grow into something even more dangerous! Stella was one of the most capable warriors that Jason had ever seen, but when compared to the rest of the Avarans, Stella herself admitted that she was only a low-level guard at best. Jason didn’t even want to imagine what an entire army of high-level Avarans would be like!</p>
<p>DOON!</p>
<p>Jason suddenly stopped and turned to the side sharply as he felt a massive surge of mana spread through the air! The mana was coming from the opposite end of the kingdom, and Jason suddenly remembered something that Jack the Ripper had said!</p>
<p>’He left behind a gift?’</p>

<p>A massive explosion lit up the sky, and Jason immediately activated [Eye of the King] and saw that the explosion had taken place at the Northern Alliance Academy! <p>But unfortunately for these False Primordials, many of them were very far away in other domains, and they knew that they wouldn’t be able to get to L’horm quickly enough to claim the essence for themselves, so most of them simply stopped and watched to see what would happen. <p>DOON!</p>
