<p>Beside Jason, Riyala put her hands over her face as she felt tears coming to her eyes. She didn’t know if there was anything she could say or do to make things better! All this destruction and death were just because she came here! But Jason just took her by the hands, and he easily broke the handcuffs that were still on her wrists. Riyala looked up at Jason, and Jason smiled at her calmly as he spoke.</p>
<p>"I already told you that you don’t need to apologise for anything. We decided to help you because you’re our friend, and that’s all that matters. Besides, you didn’t tell them to do any of this. <p>Riyala looked up at Jason in surprise, and she felt heat rising on her cheeks when he smiled at her. At that moment, Riyala didn’t know what she must’ve done to deserve meeting someone like Jason! If Riyala hadn’t stumbled into that Orange Gate and met Jason, then she would’ve regretted it for the rest of her life! <p>"Jason is right, Riyala. None of this is your fault. You should really stop trying to put the weight of the entire world on your shoulders all the time."</p>
<p>Riyala turned around very sharply once she heard the familiar voice! The person was speaking in the Radagan tongue, but Riyala knew who that voice belonged to! There was no way she could forget! But from what Riyala remembered, there was no way that person should know how to speak her tongue! Riyala’s face was set in a look of absolute disbelief, and there was a small part of her heart that prayed to every god she knew that she hadn’t just been hearing things! <p>"I’m so sorry, Gina! I couldn’t do anything at all! I just watched you die! I was so useless!"</p>
<p>Riyala was apologizing in between heavy sobs, and Gina just patted her back calmly as she allowed her to let it all out. Gina knew that Riyala blamed herself for what happened, so she already expected a reaction like this.
