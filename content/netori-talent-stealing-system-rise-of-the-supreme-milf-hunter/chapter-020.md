<p>Dorian’s eyes widened.</p>
<p>He reached out with a wrinkled hand and carefully grasped the spear, his fingers closing firmly around the icy shaft.</p>
<p>The moment he felt the weapon’s weight settle into his palms, his breath caught in his throat.</p>
<p>The spear began drawing the Qi out of his body almost immediately, sucking it in with a terrifying force that made his eyes widen even further.</p>
<p>Simply wielding this spear would be enough to raise his combat power by twenty percent. Of that, he was absolutely certain!</p>
<p>For someone like him, who had already reached the middle stages of the Spiritual Transformation Realm, such an increase was nothing short of absurd!</p>
<p>For a brief moment, the old leader of the Fallen Sword Sect felt greed burn in his chest. But the feeling didn’t last long.</p>
<p>His mind drifted back to the day he’d had the privilege of wielding the sect’s ancestral sword.</p>
<p>That legendary blade was, without a doubt, inferior to the spear now resting in his hands. And that realization was far from encouraging.</p>
<p>Dorian carefully extended his senses, probing the spear’s aura first, then Kael’s cultivation.</p>
<p>As someone who had lived for two thousand years, he recognized at once that neither the boy’s cultivation technique nor the spear in his hands carried any trace of demonic origin.</p>
<p>That left only two possibilities.</p>
<p>Either Kael had stumbled upon some random stroke of luck and was lying about having a master.</p>
<p>Or this so-called master was indeed real.</p>
<p>The Fallen Sword Sect had existed for twenty thousand years.</p>
<p>Dorian didn’t dare bet that legacy on the first possibility. He wasn’t willing to take that kind of risk.</p>
<p>For this master to hand over such a precious item without batting an eye, he would have to be, at the very least, a Nascent Soul Venerable.</p>
<p>Perhaps even a Void Soul Venerable or a Perfect Soul Venerable—the three cultivation realms above the Mortal Tribulation Realm, where the sect’s own ancestor still resided.</p>
<p>What if that Venerable returned in a few years and discovered that the sect itself had confiscated his disciple’s belongings?</p>
<p>Dorian was smart enough to know he could never bear the consequences of something on that scale.</p>
<p>He stared intently at Kael for a few long seconds. Then, holding the spear out with both hands, he returned it to the young man with a slight nod.</p>
<p>His fingers lingered on the shaft a heartbeat longer than they should have before he finally let go.</p>
<p>He wanted that weapon with every fiber of his being, but he wasn’t willing to die like a fool and drag his entire sect into annihilation over his own greed.</p>
<p>If Kael’s master truly was an expert of the Venerable realm, then maintaining a good relationship with the boy was, by far, the wisest choice.</p>
<p>"G-Grandfather?" Sol stared at Dorian with wide eyes, his voice faltering. "What are you doing?"</p>
<p>Dorian slowly turned his head, his icy eyes settling on Sol like two sharp blades.</p>
<p>The young man shuddered under the weight of that gaze, swallowing the words still trying to escape his throat.</p>
<p>Still, the fury in Sol’s eyes hadn’t dissipated. Killing intent continued to smolder deep within them, and his hands curled into trembling fists at his sides.</p>
<p>Kael sensed that hatred the instant his fingers closed around the shaft of his returned spear, its familiar chill seeping into his palm. A faint smile curved the corner of his lips.</p>
<p>As a huge fan of xianxia novels, he already had a very clear idea of what would come next.</p>
<p>An arrogant young master suffering from "two-neuron syndrome" would never be able to swallow a defeat of that magnitude!</p>
<p>"Young Kael..." Dorian’s voice echoed through the hall, a feigned kindness settling over his wrinkled face.</p>
<p>"It is truly a blessing for our sect that you have not only regained your cultivation but have also secured a powerful master."</p>
<p>Dorian rose from his throne.</p>
<p>"Your grandfather is proud of you. The Celestial Treasure Pavilion will organize all the resources owed to you, and within a week, everything will be delivered into your hands."</p>
<p>"Thank you, Grandfather!" Kael declared, clasping his fists in front of his chest and bowing respectfully.</p>
<p>He slowly raised his head, taking in Sol’s furious expression before shifting to Lucian Purewind, whose face was filled with contempt.</p>
<p>Kael answered that contempt with a provocative smile, one he directed specifically at the woman standing beside Lucian—his wife.</p>
<p>When she noticed Kael’s gaze on her, the woman frowned in apparent displeasure but kept her lips pressed tightly together, her fingers tightening around the fabric of her sleeve.</p>
<p>Without letting his smile fade, Kael turned to the side.</p>
<p>There stood his stepmother, Selene, her eyes shining with determination as she took a firm step forward, the generous swell of her chest giving a slow, heavy sway beneath her robes.</p>
<p>"Father-in-law, now that the origin of little Kael’s power has been properly clarified, don’t you think it’s time we demanded compensation for the assassination attempt he suffered?"</p>
<p>Selene’s sweet voice echoed like thunder through the throne room, silencing everyone present in a fraction of a second.</p>
<p>Shocked faces turned toward her in unison.</p>
<p>Selene raised her arm and pointed a finger straight at Seraphine, her eyes flashing with pure murderous intent.</p>
<p>"You cheap whore, step forward and take responsibility!"</p>
