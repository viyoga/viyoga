<p>Kael waited, sitting in silence while Seraphine took all the time she needed to make her breakthrough.</p>
<p>As for the fact that she would become stronger than him afterward, Kael didn’t really care.</p>
<p>Her acceptance was already close to one hundred, and by that point, Seraphine had fully accepted herself as his woman.</p>
<p>What kind of coward would he be if he were afraid of having a woman stronger than him?</p>
<p>In reality, his system practically demanded that he pursue women stronger than himself.</p>
<p>After all, only such women attracted arrogant young masters powerful enough to yield truly enormous returns.</p>
<p>Leaning against a corner of the room, Kael remembered the mystery boxes he hadn’t opened yet. One Rank S, one Rank A, one Rank B, and one Rank C.</p>
<p>Taking advantage of the fact that Seraphine was still cultivating, he decided to open all four at once.</p>
<p>[Opening mystery boxes...</p>
<p>Congratulations, you have received: Green Wing Nirvana Pill (Rank C)</p>
<p>Congratulations, you have received: Secret Imprint of a Cultivator of the Void Soul Venerable Realm with 3% of the original power (Rank B)</p>
<p>Congratulations, you have received: Great Beast-Devouring Cauldron (Rank A)</p>
<p>Congratulations, you have received: Ten Thousand Colors Sweet Herb (Rank S)]</p>
<p>[A spatial ring has been detected. Items have been automatically stored.]</p>
<p>Kael raised his eyebrows.</p>
<p>All of those items were incredibly valuable, but the secret imprint stood out among them.</p>
<p>Even though it possessed only 3% of that cultivator’s original power, it was still absurdly strong.</p>
<p>It could probably kill even the current ancestor of the Fallen Sword Sect with a single strike.</p>
<p>As for the other three items, Kael wasn’t quite sure what to make of them.</p>
<p>The Nirvana Pill was good for entering a state of enlightenment, and the Ten Thousand Colors Sweet Herb had no use for him, though it could be sold for a fortune.</p>
<p>The Great Beast-Devouring Cauldron, however, was something he could only test later.</p>
<p>With a sigh, Kael simply made sure the imprint was placed in his dantian, ready to be activated at the first sign of danger.</p>
<p>As for the true owner of that imprint sensing his presence, he only relaxed after receiving confirmation from the system that it’s owner was almost certainly dead, since the imprint was nearly one hundred thousand years old.</p>
<p>If the owner were alive, he would surely sense his own soul imprint inside a cultivator as weak as Kael.</p>
<p>Fortunately, to have lived for so long, that cultivator would need to be at a level where even the powers of the Divine Light Continent would bow before him.</p>
<p>What were the odds that this random venerable had reached the Immortal Ascension Realm and was still alive after a hundred thousand years?</p>
<p>Fourteen hours later, already well into the next day, Kael heard an excited little squeal and felt a powerful pulse of Qi burst out from inside the cultivation chamber.</p>
<p>The walls trembled slightly, and a breeze thick with spiritual energy swept down the hallway, tugging at his hair and robes.</p>
<p>Kael pushed himself off the wall and stood up just in time to see the chamber door swing open and to feel Seraphine’s Golden Core Realm cultivation explode outward.</p>
<p>[Name: Seraphine Purewind</p>
<p>Age: 24</p>
<p>Cultivation: Fourth Layer of the Golden Core Realm</p>
<p>Race: Human (Half-divine, sealed)</p>
<p>Spiritual Roots: Divine Spiritual Roots (Rank ???) (sealed)</p>

<p>Acceptance: 90/100</p>

<p>The other two Golden Core Realm cultivators present in the secret realm were only at the first layer.</p>
<p>Compared to Seraphine, who was far more talented, they would hardly stand a chance against her.</p>
<p>"You..." Seraphine murmured, watching Kael with a complex expression.</p>
<p>She lowered her gaze to her own body, feeling the power rush through her veins like a newly unleashed river.</p>
<p>"What did you do to me? What I just felt was far superior even to the most powerful barrier-breaking pill I’ve ever taken!"</p>
<p>Kael didn’t answer right away, merely shrugging.</p>
<p>But after thinking for a moment, a mischievous smile crept onto his lips.</p>
<p>"You know... I have a special power. I can grant power to women by being smothered by their boobs," Kael declared, puffing out his chest with pride.</p>
<p>For a moment, Seraphine didn’t know what to say.</p>
<p>Truly...</p>
<p>Not a shred of shame on his face.</p>
<p>In the end, all she could do was throw herself at him, grabbing his head with both hands and yanking his face straight into her chest.</p>
<p>"You won’t survive this time, you perverted bastard!" she declared forcefully, squeezing even tighter.</p>
<p>Soon, Kael found himself fighting against that cruel fate once again.</p>
<p>Drowning between tits so perfect, large, and fragrant.</p>
<p>What man could withstand that?</p>
<p>Unfortunately, their playful moment didn’t last long.</p>
<p>Soon, Seraphine pulled away and stood up, hurriedly pulling her dress—which had slipped down to her waist—back up to cover her now-bare breasts.</p>
<p>The heavy globes swayed once as they slipped free of his face, then settled behind the fabric as she tugged it into place.</p>
<p>Her cheeks had taken on a rosy hue similar to that of her hair as she avoided Kael’s gaze, tucking a stray strand behind her ear.</p>
<p>As for getting a real answer from him... that could wait until they left the secret realm.</p>
<p>"Let’s try going down to the lower regions... all the recorded cases in the history of the Golden Alliance Sect’s recruitment token encounters took place there," Seraphine said, her voice already firmer.</p>
<p>Kael nodded.</p>
<p>Everything beneath that palace was a gigantic network of tunnels and labyrinths, a dangerous place known for something very particular: the door leading there opened only once, for five minutes.</p>
<p>After that, it could only be opened from the inside, and it wouldn’t open again until the next secret realm.</p>
<p>That was why even those Golden Core Realm cultivators formed groups, making it easier to explore its interior.</p>
<p>If Kael and Seraphine went in first and then emerged laden with riches, they would most certainly become prime targets.</p>
