<p>A gentle smile curved Selene’s lips as her gaze swept across the sun-drenched arena.</p>
<p>It settled on Kael, who trained alone amid the scattered straw dummies and weathered weapon racks, the sharp whistle of his strikes cutting through the still midday air.</p>
<p>His bare chest glistened beneath the noon sun, drenched in sweat that traced glimmering paths down the hard ridges of his stomach.</p>
<p>The muscles beneath his skin had grown far stronger and more defined than they had once been, sculpted by relentless effort.</p>
<p>Seeing him so healthy filled Selene’s chest with warm joy.</p>
<p>"Miss Selene." Kael paused mid-form, lowering his arms, and dipped his head in a slight, respectful bow.</p>
<p>Selene shook her own head in return, a soft sigh escaping her lips as she feigned mild disapproval.</p>
<p>"Hm... silly boy, why don’t you just call me Mom? I’ve insisted on it so many times already."</p>
<p>She closed the distance between them with smooth, gliding steps.</p>
<p>She didn’t break stride as her slender fingers slipped into the deep, warm valley between them.</p>
<p>The plump, milky flesh parted around her hand before closing in on it from both sides. Those heavy mounds swallowed her wrist whole, engulfing it in a cushion of soft, perfumed skin that hugged her like the finest silk pillows.</p>
<p>The huge globes quivered gently every time she shifted her wrist deeper into the fragrant, shadowy cleavage, the pale upper slopes wobbling with each small movement of her buried hand.</p>
<p>A white handkerchief emerged a moment later, carrying with it the sweet, intoxicating warmth of her skin.</p>
<p>The moment her hand slid free, the soft flesh closed back together, the two mounds settling against each other with a slow, heavy bounce.</p>
<p>She came to a stop directly in front of Kael, whose eyes refused to tear themselves from that voluptuous cleavage, and smiled with tenderness.</p>
<p>She pressed the warm, perfumed handkerchief against his sweat-slick chest, wiping downward in slow, careful strokes.</p>
<p>Her hands came together at the very center of his pecs, her arms squeezing those huge, heavy globes inward from both sides until the cleavage deepened into a pale, bottomless canyon of flesh.</p>
<p>The dress strained beneath the pressure, its delicate seams groaning in quiet, threatening protest.</p>
<p>Soft, creamy flesh bulged obscenely over the neckline, two swollen crescents of skin spilling upward.</p>
<p>Kael’s breath caught in his throat, his pulse hammering against his ribs as he very nearly surrendered to impulse.</p>
<p>But in the next instant he noticed Selene’s hands go still against his chest.</p>
<p>"What happened, miss—" Kael caught himself just in time, swiftly channeling Qi into his cheeks to force them into a crimson flush, then continued with feigned, bashful embarrassment. "Mom..."</p>
<p>Selene’s heart nearly leapt clean out of her chest at those long-awaited words, her fingers tightening around the handkerchief as her entire face lit up with pure, unguarded excitement.</p>
<p>Finally, after twenty long years, her stepson had called her mother!</p>
<p>"Nothing, my dear. Just a few little pigs who don’t know their place in the world."</p>
<p>Selene smiled sweetly and rose onto the tips of her toes, tugging Kael’s head down so she could reach.</p>
<p>Her massive chest pressed flush against his sweat-slick torso, the soft mounds flattening against the hard planes of his muscles as she laid a tender kiss on his damp forehead.</p>
<p>She lingered there for a heartbeat longer than was strictly necessary before finally settling back down onto her heels.</p>
<p>Kael said nothing, merely offering a small nod before turning his face toward one of the two iron-bound gates that led into the residence.</p>
<p>With a sharp crack of wood striking against stone, the gate swung wide open, revealing ten figures advancing in tight, deliberate formation.</p>
<p>The leader was someone Kael knew all too well.</p>
<p>Sol Ashen, his cousin, hailed as the most talented young master of this generation of the Ashen Family.</p>
<p>Beside him stood Lucian Purewind, husband of Seraphine Purewind, while just behind the two, eight lesser figures rounded out the obedient entourage.</p>
<p>The warmth drained from Selene’s face in an instant.</p>
<p>"I hope you have a good reason for entering my residence without permission," she demanded, her voice dropping into a dangerously cold register.</p>
<p>She kept her aura carefully restrained, considering it beneath her dignity to crush insignificant ants with the full weight of her power.</p>
<p>"It’s nothing major, Senior Selene. Just matters among the younger generation."</p>
<p>Sol Ashen stepped forward, puffing out his broad, powerful chest. His shimmering golden hair caught the sunlight, and his striking sapphire-blue eyes settled on Kael with open contempt.</p>
<p>He couldn’t sense a trace of Kael’s cultivation, and so he simply concluded the other boy was too weak for his strength to even register on the spectrum.</p>
<p>Even if Selene had been actively concealing her stepson’s cultivation, it hardly mattered.</p>
<p>Sol himself stood just one step away from the Qi Foundation Realm.</p>
<p>He had nothing to fear from a pampered mama’s boy who had never once tasted the bitter sting of real death.</p>
<p>"Kael Ashen, Heir Disciple of the Ashen Family, you who have disgraced our name with your pitiful talent! I, Sol Ashen, challenge you to a martial duel for the position of Heir Disciple!"</p>
<p>His voice rang across the arena, and the eight behind him straightened their backs, chins lifting in unison.</p>
<p>Kael’s lips quirked upward in a barely contained grin, and he was on the very verge of shouting ’Hell yeah!’ when Selene cut sharply in.</p>
<p>"No, absolutely not! My baby isn’t going to fight you, you filthy worm!" she roared.</p>
<p>Her chest heaved with the outburst, the heavy globes lurching once against the strained fabric.</p>
<p>Even without releasing a single thread of her Qi, an overwhelming, suffocating power rode on her voice, sending faint tremors rippling through the stone tiles beneath her feet.</p>
<p>Loose straw skittered across the ground, and the wooden dummies rattled on their posts.</p>
<p>Had any mortals been present, their eardrums would have ruptured and their eyes filled with blood from the sheer intensity of that pressure alone.</p>
<p>Sol clenched his teeth at her words, jaw tight enough to ache, but soon realized he wouldn’t even need to respond.</p>
<p>A new presence was already approaching through the open gate.</p>
<p>"Yes, he will fight."</p>
<p>The voice echoed across the courtyard, and Selene’s eyes snapped to the side, fixing coldly on the newcomer.</p>
<p>He looked like an older, uglier version of Kael, his lips curled into a confident, arrogant smile.</p>
<p>Luke Ashen, Kael’s father, stood with calm, calculating eyes and his hands crossed behind his back.</p>
<p>"Come on, kid. Show me you’re more than a waste of oxygen."</p>
<p>"You!—" Selene was about to lose control when she felt Kael’s hand brush lightly against hers, his warm fingers transmitting an unexpected, grounding calm.</p>
<p>"It’s okay. I’m confident." He smiled, that smile carrying all the purity and kindness she knew only her stepson to possess.</p>
<p>Selene battled a storm of emotions raging inside her, anger, fear, and anxiety all warring at once.</p>
<p>Yet as she gazed into Kael’s handsome, resolved face, she found herself reluctantly taking a step back.</p>
