<p>Ttuk...!</p>
<p>The moment she saw the first raindrop fall on the Building rooftop, Fujigasaki Rinka spewed out the Abyss.</p>
<p>-Shinkaiyatsude Heukmu (Black Mist).</p>
<p>It was a technique that seizes a wide area by spewing out the Abyss like fog, but because she was Fujigasaki Rinka, whose absolute amount of Abyss had significantly decreased after having her Abyss absorbed during the Takakusaki Gengo battle, she couldn't spew out the fog of Abyss as if seizing the surroundings, and instead used Heukmu as if thinly wrapping it around her skin.</p>
<p>Although she was wearing a cyber Suit, she used a shield once more, thinking it would be better not to be hit by those black raindrops.</p>
<p>The remaining Fragment of All-Knowledge scramblers also, perhaps feeling it, were each using protection skills or equipment. Except for the Werewolf Chief, who was consumed by savagery.</p>
<p>The Werewolf Chief's beautiful silver Hair, continuously aiming for the Fragment of All-Knowledge and attacking Fujigasaki Rinka, began to turn black as if fallen into dirty mud.</p>
<p>"Keureureung...! Kihyaaa!"</p>
<p>The Werewolf Chief, completely drenched by the Rain of Lamentation, suddenly stopped his attack towards Fujigasaki Rinka, suffering in pain, and his body began to mutate.</p>
<p>The already large Size of the werewolf grew even larger, and with a Udeudeuk sound, arms made of flesh began to sprout from his back. And the flesh all over his body split, and bloodshot eyes began to pop out; it was a mutation that could no longer be called a werewolf.</p>
<p>"Keuheong!"</p>
<p>After the mutation ended, the monster werewolf, now swinging Claws that were 1.5 meters long, struck towards the Fragment of All-Knowledge held by Fujigasaki Rinka.</p>
<p>Fujigasaki Rinka, due to Heukmu, extended her similarly blackened Legs and kicked the monster werewolf's Chin. Both Fujigasaki Rinka and the monster werewolf were blackened, but the feeling of the two was distinctly different.</p>
<p>Fujigasaki Rinka's black Color was like looking into an infinitely deep pit, giving an unknown Fear, while the monster werewolf's black Color was a disgusting black, as if all the Filth of the World had gathered to become black.</p>
<p>Fujigasaki Rinka had her Sensitive Nerves on edge. She had to watch the monster werewolf and the Black Sage floating in the sky at the same time. What was fortunate was that the Lizardman Warlord and the SPF New York Branch ganbu were attacking the monster werewolf, and the others were attacking the Black Sage, providing Help.</p>
<p>"Stained by ugliness, you despicable born! Die!"</p>
<p>The SPF New York Branch ganbu, wearing a silver shield, swung his great Sword. Forged from Mithril and silver, it was a weapon with extreme Compatibility against werewolves, so he had no doubt that this single blow would inflict a Fatal Wound, but the Rain of Lamentation, thoroughly drenching the monster werewolf, entangled the SPF New York Branch ganbu's great Sword and received the Authority of exorcism instead of the monster werewolf. Peusyu...!</p>
<p>The Rain of Lamentation, burning on the silver and Mithril, raised smoke, but the liquid of lamentation scattered as smoke was not consumed due to the continuously falling black rain and was quickly replenished.</p>
<p>Almost simultaneously, the Lizardman Warlord, clad in a Golden Light curtain, created a Sphere of Lightning at the tip of his trident and shot it towards the monster werewolf.</p>
<p>Regardless of the Rain of Lamentation, since it was already thoroughly drenched in this liquid, he thought the Lightning attack would be effective.</p>
<p>But the Lizardman Warlord's thought was wrong. The Rain of Lamentation was, on the contrary, an insulator that neutralized Lightning.</p>
<p>Hit squarely by the electric Sphere, the monster werewolf nonchalantly, making a Keuheong! sound, charged at Fujigasaki Rinka.</p>
<p>"This Guy, why is he only attacking me?!"</p>
<p>Fujigasaki Rinka shouted and struck the werewolf with her fist. Of course, Fujigasaki Rinka also knew. What the monster werewolf was aiming for was not Fujigasaki Rinka herself, but the Fragment of All-Knowledge she was holding.</p>
<p>At that time, in the sky, the Black Sage, the Vampire Grand Duke, and Magicians were engaged in battle.</p>
<p>"If there were only Corpses or blood bags around, I would have swept them all away!"</p>
<p>The Vampire Grand Duke, grumbling, pouring a Bottle of red blood potion into his mouth, created blood Arrows in the empty space and shot them.</p>
<p>For human Targets, excluding Vampire Hunters, he was a very strong Vampire, but if there was no blood to use, he would consume his own blood and rapidly weaken, which is why he was grumbling. Of course, for a Vampire Grand Duke of his level, there were several ways to replenish his vitality, nevertheless, due to the frustration of not being able to exert his full power, he couldn't help but grumble.</p>
<p>This area, due to the destruction and slaughter from the Fragment of All-Knowledge scramble, had almost no living humans, and quite a lot of blood flowed on the street, but for some reason, after the Rain of Lamentation fell, the blood on the street was completely unaffected by the Vampire Grand Duke's Domination.</p>
<p>The Magicians had no restrictions on using blood, so they were able to continue a relatively stable battle, but from the unfurled Book of Lamentation, due to the continuously heard wailing sounds, their minds were shaken, and their spells were often interrupted midway.</p>
<p>Of course, since it was a multi-on-one battle, the Black Sage also was busy defending against simultaneous incoming attacks.</p>
<p>It was true that the Black Sage was the most outstanding among those gathered here, but all those gathered here were outstanding beings who had broken through a certain Realm, so even the Black Sage could not let his guard down.</p>
<p>However, due to the Book of Lamentation, seizing the moments when the Magicians' spells were interrupted, he was launching dangerous counterattacks.</p>
<p>Because the Black Sage's Counterattack was so strong, the Magicians each time had no choice but to switch from Offensive to defensive.</p>
<p>Fujigasaki Rinka glanced at the undecided battle above and decided to deal with this troublesome monster werewolf first. If she dealt with the werewolf and everyone attacked the Black Sage, she judged that they could win.</p>
<p>For a while, because she had watched the SPF New York Branch ganbu and the Lizardman Warlord attack the werewolf, she roughly knew how to Raid it.</p>
<p>Currently, most attacks were being neutralized by the Rain of Lamentation thoroughly drenching the monster werewolf.</p>
<p>If so, she had to use a method of attacking the Interior rather than the exterior, and that was the attack method that Fujigasaki Rinka's Shinkaiyatsude was best at.</p>
<p>Having already made her judgment, Fujigasaki Rinka, without delay, approached the werewolf. And immediately, as if stamping a Seal, she extended her Palm.</p>
<p>-Shinkaiyatsude Shintōbanin (Penetrating Disk Seal)-Man'nami (Ten Thousand Waves).</p>
<p>It was Fujigasaki Rinka who further enhanced Shintōbanin, a signature infiltration technique of Shinkaiyatsude that channels the Abyss into the body, under the name Shintōbanin -Man'nami.</p>
<p>As its name Man'nami (Ten Thousand Waves) suggests, the Abyss Energy entered the body, causing it to continuously emit waves. It was a technique that inflicted continuous damage, and with a single strike, it could deal fatal, sustained damage.</p>
<p>"Kyaooo! Kaekkaeng...! Kkaeng...!"</p>
<p>At first, it seemed merely a little uncomfortable, but as the Abyss waves destroying its body's inside continuously spewed out, the monster werewolf whined Like a Dog. As the monster werewolf continuously vomited black blood, Fujigasaki Rinka, confident of Victory, turned her Head towards the sky where the Black Sage was, and the Black Sage's Voice was heard.</p>
<p>"It's getting boring.</p>
<p>The Black Sage, who said it was boring, swung his staff, emitting a shimmering wave of purple Fire that flew around him and attacked, repelling the Vampire Grand Duke and Magicians. And he raised the Book of Lamentation and began to activate another magic.</p>
<p>"Lamentation Resonance."</p>
<p>As the spell ended, with the Black Sage as the vertex, the empty space at both ends of the triangle tore open, and mouths appeared. And from those mouths, wailing sounds, no different from Screams, began to be heard.</p>
<p>As the Book of Lamentation unfurled, the wailing sounds amplified and began to attack everyone's minds. Even the Vampire Grand Duke and Lizardman Warlord, who had endured well until now, were staggered by this mental Attack.</p>
<p>Wrapped entirely in Heukmu, only Fujigasaki Rinka, neutralizing the Lamentation Resonance, was calmly looking at the Black Sage. But the Black Sage's magic was not over.</p>
<p>"Lamentation Extreme Pain."</p>
<p>Ureureung!!</p>
<p>As he summoned the Rain of Lamentation, thunder was heard from the gathered clouds, and Lightning began to strike towards the Fragment of All-Knowledge scramblers. Again, it was not ordinary Lightning, but extremely ominous black Lightning.</p>
<p>At its threatening Appearance, each shook off the mental Attack from Lamentation Resonance and avoided the black Lightning, but one Magician who couldn't avoid it was struck by that black Lightning.</p>
<p>For a Moment, Darkness covered the Surroundings, and when the Moonlight illuminated the World again, the Magician was gone, and only a monster, whose entire body was drenched in the Rain of Lamentation, remained. Its body was thoroughly immersed in black tar, looking like a bloated Corpse, and its Appearance was utterly gruesome.</p>
<p>-Geueoeo!</p>
<p>The Magician struck by the black Lightning, becoming a monster, began to unleash magic indiscriminately at his colleagues. Of course, the mutated Magician's magic was threatening, but what was more terrifying was the power that could instantly turn a Magician who had reached a Realm into a monster devoid of reason, a power close to an ominous Authority.</p>
<p>"There's no other Crazy Bastard like him."</p>
<p>Fujigasaki Rinka, giving High Praise to the Black Sage with Cursing, began to gather the Abyss. If things continued like this, one by one, they would turn into monsters, and without any other variables, they would head straight for Defeat. Before that happened, she thought she had to settle the Match.</p>
<p>However, Fujigasaki Rinka, who was concentrating on gathering the Abyss, did not realize that the monster werewolf, whom she thought was dead, was rising. Rather than the monster werewolf rising, the Rain of Lamentation covering the monster werewolf was moving the monster werewolf's body like an exoskeleton.</p>
<p>The SPF New York Branch ganbu and the Lizardman Warlord, enduring the Lamentation Resonance, concentrating on the Black Sage, also missed the monster werewolf's movement for a Moment.</p>
<p>-Kyaoooo! The werewolf howled and swung its Claws. Fujigasaki Rinka stopped concentrating on the Abyss and dodged, but it was already too late.</p>
<p>The werewolf's sharp Claws cut through the Seal's chains, which had been tightly wrapped around the Fragment of All-Knowledge, as they passed.</p>
