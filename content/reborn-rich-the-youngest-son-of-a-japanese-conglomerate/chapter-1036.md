<p>"Prey? Ha...!"</p>
<p>When the Mexican wrestler, who called himself an Ogre, referred to her as prey, Seizawa Shii wore an expression of utter absurdity.</p>
<p>Considering he participated in the Fragment of All-Knowledge scramble and had just fought well against dozens of people, it was clear that the wrestler Ogre was strong, not weak, but treating Seizawa Shii as prey was excessively arrogant.</p>
<p>"Alright, let's see who becomes the prey." "Kwahahaha!! Yes, that attitude is good! I would have been greatly disappointed if you had tucked your Tail between your legs...!"</p>
<p>At Seizawa Shii's Words, the Ogre rejoiced loudly and charged forward like an assault tank. Then, he leaped into the air and delivered a dropkick.</p>
<p>'Fast...!'</p>
<p>She had thought it before, but he was indeed no ordinary pro wrestler. His dropkick was launched like a missile.</p>
<p>But no matter how fast, it was a large, linear Attack, and there was no way Seizawa Shii couldn't dodge it. She easily moved to the Side, and the Ogre's dropkick struck the wall at the end of the alley with a Kwang! The wall crumbled.</p>
<p>It was a sturdy wall that hadn't collapsed even when Seizawa Shii threw a human missile, yet it crumbled in a single blow like this. Its power was also extremely threatening.</p>
<p>"Hmm.... Big techniques won't work."</p>
<p>The Ogre, dusting off the dust as he stood up, muttered something and slowly approached Seizawa Shii.</p>
<p>In contrast to how she had been fighting haphazardly with brute Strength until now, Seizawa Shii took the stance of the traditional martial art, Seizawa Eishin-ryu, searching for the Ogre's openings.</p>
<p>"Hup!"</p>
<p>The Ogre charged with one Arm outstretched. It was a clothesline struck with his extended forearm, moving at an incredible speed as if folding space.</p>
<p>No matter how fast, Seizawa Shii was already watching the Ogre, so she was able to duck her Head and dodge his clothesline.</p>
<p>Huuung!</p>
<p>She dodged it, but felt an immense power that made the air hum. Seizawa Shii, still crouched, formed her Hand into a Hand Blade and swung it.</p>
<p>-Seizawa Eishin-ryu Mugenzan.</p>
<p>"Kuh-heo-eok!"</p>
<p>Seizawa Eishin-ryu Mugenzan was a technique that swung the Hand Blade at immense speed to cut the opponent, but the Ogre's flesh was tough, and it didn't cut at all. However, he seemed to have taken a considerable shock, clutching his belly and writhing in Pleasurable Ache.</p>
<p>"Hmph! Nothing special...!"</p>
<p>Seizawa Shii swung her fist at the Ogre, who was writhing in Pleasurable Ache. But the Ogre, as if he had never been in pain, dodged Seizawa Shii's fist, grabbed her forearm, and lifted her.</p>
<p>"H-hey, let go?!"</p>
<p>Seizawa Shii struggled, but the Ogre simply lifted her above his Head and then slammed her onto the concrete floor. It was a Powerbomb, one of the pro wrestling techniques.</p>
<p>Kwaaang!</p>
<p>Seizawa Shii, thrown down so hard that the floor cracked, felt an immense Pleasurable Ache.</p>
<p>Even if her cyber Suit had absorbed some of the shock, it was no different from her entire Body hitting the concrete floor, and she received such a massive shock that the sky spun before her eyes.</p>
<p>"Kwahaha, good! Hurting like this is part of the showmanship!"</p>
<p>The Ogre laughed loudly, watching Seizawa Shii stumble to her Feet. She didn't know if he was truly a former pro wrestler, but he seemed to prioritize showmanship, just like his attire suggested.</p>
<p>Seizawa Shii shook her Head, regained her mind, then leaped backward to create distance. She admitted that she had let her guard down because of her opponent's ridiculous attire and attitude. In Japan, she had trained with Naruse Shiyo, who used pro wrestling techniques, and later received training from Fujigasaki Rinka while in the USA, so if she utilized everything she had learned, she would be able to defeat the Mexican wrestler before her.</p>
<p>"You should fight properly...! It'll be disappointing if you run away, female Ogre!"</p>
<p>The Ogre charged again, just like before. Charging with his Shoulder forward, it seemed like he was using the pro wrestling technique, Spear.</p>
<p>"Pro wrestling isn't Martial Arts. It's just entertainment. I'll show you what Actual combat Martial Arts is."</p>
<p>Seizawa Shii dodged the charging Ogre's Body. Then she recalled Fujigasaki Rinka's first teaching.</p>
<p>-No matter how sturdy and strong an opponent is, if they are human, there are unavoidable weaknesses due to their structure.</p>
<p>Remembering Fujigasaki Rinka's first teaching, Seizawa Shii quickly moved behind the Ogre and kicked the Back of his Knees.</p>
<p>As Seizawa Shii kicked the popliteal fossa behind his knee, the Ogre couldn't withstand the pushing power and knelt on one knee. His Body lowered, and naturally, the Ogre's Temple came within Seizawa Shii's range.</p>
<p>-Seizawa Eishin-ryu Eiu-chi </p>
<p>Seizawa Shii spun her Body and struck the Ogre's Temple with her Elbow. For an ordinary person, it would have been a blow that would dent the Temple or shatter the skull, but the Ogre endured it.</p>
<p>But Seizawa Shii's Attack wasn't over. She continuously struck the Ogre's philtrum, Chin, and Solar Plexus-known human vital points-continuing her assault.</p>
<p>"Kuaaaah!"</p>
<p>At Seizawa Shii's Attack, the Ogre roared and flailed his Hands. It was a blow that would have killed an ordinary person ten times over, but the Ogre endured it all. Then, as if his anger had reached its peak, he grabbed Seizawa Shii's Arm and threw her.</p>
<p>Perhaps because he threw Seizawa Shii haphazardly without even using wrestling techniques due to his anger, Seizawa Shii regained her Balance in empty space and landed without injury.</p>
<p>Seizawa Shii prepared for a continuous barrage of attacks, but no Attack came from the Ogre. What was happening? She looked, and the Ogre was tearing apart and eating the Corpse of the Obsidian Syndicate gang members Seizawa Shii had killed.</p>
<p>Seizawa Shii had seen all sorts of people while working as a fixer, but this was the first time she had seen someone cannibalizing during combat. She was so flustered that she momentarily missed her timing to Attack.</p>
<p>The Ogre, having devoured one person's thigh in a few bites, turned his Head and glared at Seizawa Shii.</p>
<p>"Krrr...!"</p>
<p>Listening to the sounds emanating from his mouth and his eyes shining like a Wild animal, it seemed his humanity had almost vanished.</p>
<p>The Mark of Seizawa Shii's fist on his Solar Plexus disappeared as he recovered, and at the same time, Hair began to grow long. His mouth protruded further and further, tearing his luchador Mask, and he transformed into the appearance of a werewolf seen in movies or novels.</p>
<p>"A werewolf?"</p>
<p>Otagi Yuka, a Vampire, was also her friend, and having encountered various pieces of information, she had heard stories of werewolves, but this was her first time seeing one in person.</p>
<p>As Seizawa Shii knew, werewolves possessed tremendous physical Ability, thick Leather, and excellent regeneration, making them difficult to defeat in melee combat. Their weakness, like Vampires, was silver, but Seizawa Shii currently had no silver weapons.</p>
<p>"I'll have to ask Ryuta-nim to make me a mithril Knuckle...." "Kuaaaang!"</p>
<p>Perhaps thinking Seizawa Shii's muttering was a signal for Attack, the Ogre, no, the werewolf, charged at Seizawa Shii. He was definitely much faster, and without using unnecessary pro wrestling techniques, his movements were streamlined.</p>
<p>The werewolf's Claws, glinting in the crescent moonlight, swung and left deep wounds on the wall. Seizawa Shii dodged the Claw Attack, but seeing the trace left on the wall, her Hair stood on end. Even wearing the highest-grade cyber Suit, an Attack of that caliber felt like it could sever her Body. But despite her fear, Seizawa Shii dodged the werewolf's Claw Attack and punched him in the Solar Plexus.</p>
<p>Puk!</p>
<p>She had clearly landed a solid blow, yet perhaps due to his Hair and Leather, it didn't have the same satisfying impact as before. In that case, she had no choice but to use Fujigasaki Rinka's second teaching.</p>
<p>-If attacking the weakness doesn't break through the opponent's Defense, you must destroy the opponent's inside, not just the outside.</p>
<p>Fujigasaki Rinka's second lesson, learned in the USA, was about the Inner Heavy Hand Method, which targets the opponent's interior.</p>
<p>The Reason Fujigasaki Rinka reached the pinnacle of the Martial Arts world with a Woman's Body was precisely because she was a master of the Inner Heavy Hand Method.</p>
<p>While in the USA, Seizawa Shii learned the secrets of the Inner Heavy Hand Method from Fujigasaki Rinka, and after diligent Effort, she was able to create and add a new Martial Arts Form to the Seizawa Eishin-ryu, which previously lacked Inner Heavy Hand Method techniques.</p>
<p>"Kyaoooh!"</p>
<p>Huung!</p>
<p>The werewolf's Claws swished past where her Head had been. Seizawa Shii quickly ducked to dodge, then extended her Palm. Seizawa Shii's gently extended Palm adhered to the werewolf's breast.</p>
<p>-Seizawa Eishin-ryu Eishinbaku.</p>
<p>The Martial Arts Form Eishinbaku, created jointly by Seizawa Shii and Fujigasaki Rinka, was a technique that unleashed Fa Jin into the opponent's heart, delivering a shock to the heart.</p>
<p>Immediately, the werewolf's movements, which had been about to counterattack by swinging his Claws at Seizawa Shii's Attack, stopped. And blood began to drip from his eyes, nose, and Sensitive Ear.</p>
<p>The blood, which had initially flowed drop by drop, began to pour out in a Jureureuk, a stream that perfectly matched the sound.</p>
<p>No longer could the Wild savagery and Killing Intent of the wolf that devoured humans be felt in his eyes. What filled his Gaze was the Fear of Death and Despair.</p>
<p>"To be a werewolf and call yourself an Ogre.... I don't particularly like the nickname Ogre, but it seems too good for you."</p>
<p>Seizawa Shii swung her Palm once more. This time, it was his Head. As Seizawa Eishin-ryu Eishinbaku exploded on his Head, turning his Brain into pulp, the light vanished from the werewolf's eyes.</p>
<p>Seizawa Shii, having killed the werewolf, leaned against the wall with her exhausted Body and gazed at the sky. A crescent moon hung in the night sky.</p>
<p>"If it had been a full moon, I might have been defeated." Since werewolves' Strength varied with the size of the moon, Seizawa Shii felt Fortunate that it was a crescent moon night.</p>
