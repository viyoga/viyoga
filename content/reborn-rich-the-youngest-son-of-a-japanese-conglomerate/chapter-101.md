<p>Han Yu-gyeong was a celebrity at Korea University. She entered Korea University as the top student in the Business Administration Department and was also the daughter of a chaebol family ranked within the top 10 in the country, though not the absolute highest. But more than anything, what made Han Yu-gyeong famous was her beauty.</p>
<p>The contrast between her glossy black hair, ink-drawn black eyes, and fair but lively skin highlighted each other, and the features of her eyes, nose, and mouth were all beautiful and harmonious. Han Yu-gyeong was more beautiful than most celebrities.</p>
<p>Although she wasn't very active, Han Yu-gyeong was already a celebrity beyond Korea University. Her SNS account, which she barely updated once a week, already had over a million followers, and photos of Han Yu-gyeong were often posted in online communities with titles like "Korea University Goddess." Sometimes, people even posted photos of her in her high school uniform, so she was an incredibly famous figure within Korea University.</p>
<p>Perhaps because of this, Han Yu-gyeong received a considerable number of direct and indirect advances, but she coldly blocked all of these men. The reason for her rejections was always the same.</p>
<p>,I have a boyfriend."</p>
<p>Most men couldn't approach her after hearing that one sentence from Han Yu-gyeong and fell away. There were some men who stubbornly clung on, but they were blocked by the bodyguards Han Yu-gyeong brought with her and couldn't even get close.</p>
<p>The most recent guy to be rejected, Park Sang-beom, came up with a new method. He planned to investigate the boyfriend that Han Yu-gyeong claimed to have.</p>
<p>He thought of using the power of his father, Park Il-soo-ssi, the second son of the BS Group Chairman, to dig up dirt on the boyfriend. Park Sang-beom was Park Il-soo's only child, born late in life, and Park Il-soo cherished Park Sang-beom so much that he would grant him any request. No matter who the boyfriend was, there wouldn't be a person who didn't have at least a speck of dust to be found. And he intended to use that dust to blackmail Han Yu-gyeong into leaving him.</p>
<p>However, there was no trace of the boyfriend that Han Yu-gyeong claimed to have. Neither on her SNS, which she occasionally updated, nor after following her for several days, could he find any trace of a man.</p>
<p>Recalling something, Park Sang-beom smiled slyly. ,That bitch Han Yu-gyeong is just pretending to have a boyfriend to get rid of guys! If I reveal this fact, what excuse will she use to reject me then?</p>
<p>In Park Sang-beom's eyes, he was the perfect man. A muscular body of 180 centimeters, a face that was decently handsome, his father's wealth, and the immense power he had gathered with that wealth... He didn't feel inferior to anyone except for a few people. That's why he couldn't even imagine that Han Yu-gyeong would reject him. Even though he had already been rejected once with the excuse that she had a boyfriend.</p>
<p>,You will eventually become my woman."</p>
<p>There was even obsession mixed in with Park Sang-beom's muttering. After that, Park Sang-beom spread rumors that Han Yu-gyeong was lying about having a boyfriend. He even went so far as to suggest that she might have a delusional disorder.</p>
<p>He said that Han Yu-gyeong's infrequent SNS updates were because she was afraid of revealing her personal life, and he even claimed that her being the top student in the Business Administration Department was a lie, driving everything about Han Yu-gyeong into falsehood. The fact that she was the daughter of a chaebol family was so well-known that he couldn't push that as a lie, but if Shinkang Group had been even slightly less powerful, he would have made her out to be a delusional patient who even lied about her family.</p>
<p>Even Han Yu-gyeong, who didn't pay attention to others, began to feel stressed. Even friends who she thought were close asked her if the story about her having a boyfriend was a lie. Park Sang-beom's claims were gradually polluting her surroundings. Unable to endure it any longer, Han Yu-gyeong met Park Sang-beom to settle things once and for all. Park Sang-beom wore a despicable expression, looking as if he had already won.</p>
<p>,Now, tell me honestly. You don't have a boyfriend, do you? You're just annoyed by men coming after you, right? Then let's do this. I'll become your real boyfriend. Then you can comfortably reveal that you have a real boyfriend. Good, right?"</p>
<p>Han Yu-gyeong frowned as she watched Park Sang-beom speak like a machine gun without even waiting for an answer. In Han Yu-gyeong's eyes, Park Sang-beom was the worst kind of man. The fact that he wielded his father's power instead of his own, the muscles he had built only for show, and, most of all, his face wasn't her type. It was then that Han Yu-gyeong realized that she was quite picky about faces.</p>
<p>Han Yu-gyeong had yet to see a man who surpassed Jin Yong-tae. Jin Yong-tae, with his perfect body and handsome face, was truly Han Yu-gyeong's ideal type. And he even possessed a tool that far exceeded the Korean average... Park Sang-beom couldn't even be compared to Jin Yong-tae.</p>
<p>,It's okay. I really do have a boyfriend."</p>
<p>,Don't lie!"</p>
<p>Kwang!!</p>
<p>Park Sang-beom, having been rejected again, slammed the table and shouted. Park Sang-beom's eyes were filled with unpleasant heat, murderous intent, and lustful desires.</p>
<p>,It's true. My boyfriend is in Japan, but he's coming to see me now that it's vacation."</p>
<p>,Japan? Keukeuke! Another lie! You have a talent for making things sound plausible!"</p>
<p>Park Sang-beom, who had obtained most of what he wanted and had rarely experienced rejection, couldn't accept the fact that he had been rejected. So, he focused on the fact that Han Yu-gyeong was lying and vented his anger on that.</p>
<p>He wanted to forcibly grab Han Yu-gyeong and violate her, but that wasn't easy because of the huge bodyguard protecting Han Yu-gyeong's back.</p>
<p>,...Then let's do this. Bring your boyfriend to the end-of-semester party on Friday. If you do, I'll believe you and won't approach you anymore."</p>
<p>,Why should I do that?"</p>
<p>,Or I'll tell my father to thoroughly investigate Shinkang Group."</p>
<p>In truth, Han Yu-gyeong didn't have much affection for Shinkang Group, but she couldn't help but nod because she thought that if Shinkang Group were greatly harmed due to Park Sang-beom's request, even by a slim chance, the veil of being the daughter of a chaebol family would disappear, and as a result, other men would cling to her even more. Until she could stand on her own, she needed the position of being the youngest daughter of Shinkang Group. And she didn't want to make Park Sang-beom, the son of Park Il-soo-ssi who was vying for the position of the next Chairman of BS Group, into an enemy just yet.</p>
<p>,Okay. I'll bring my boyfriend to the end-of-semester party. If I bring my boyfriend, you really won't talk to me or even acknowledge me."</p>
<p>,Alright. But if it's revealed that you don't have a boyfriend, you'll have to meet me."</p>
<p>Han Yu-gyeong frowned again at the absurd demand. Han Yu-gyeong looked at Park Sang-beom with contempt.</p>
<p>,If I don't have a boyfriend, why would I have to meet Sunbae?"</p>
<p>,Since I'm the first person to reveal your lie, shouldn't I get some kind of reward?"</p>
<p>,I don't want to. My boyfriend might not be able to come because he's busy, but I can't meet Sunbae just because I couldn't show him to other people. If those are the conditions, I'd rather not make the promise."</p>
<p>At Han Yu-gyeong's firm attitude, Park Sang-beom raised both hands and said "Okay, okay," taking a step back. But his attitude was relaxed. He seemed confident that Han Yu-gyeong didn't have a boyfriend.</p>
<p>,Fine, I'll be satisfied with just revealing your lie."</p>
<p>Park Sang-beom disappeared with a sly smile. Soon, what Park Sang-beom's sly smile meant was revealed in a KakaoTalk message that Park Sang-beom posted in the Business Administration Department's group chat for the class of '53.</p>
<p>Park Sang-beom '52: This year's end-of-semester party for the class of '53 will be held at Lime Tree at the Korea University intersection! And Han Yu-gyeong from our department says she's bringing her boyfriend, so I hope everyone can attend. There are no dues. Please indicate if you will be attending.</p>
<p>Jo Gi-taek '53: I will attend.</p>
<p>Choi Seong-hoon '53: I will attend!</p>
<p>Ok Hye-min '53: Attending~~</p>
<p>Lee Da-on '53: I will attend.</p>
<p>Kim Ji-yeon '53: Wow~ No dues? I'm definitely attending 11111</p>
<p>No one knew when Park Sang-beom, from the class of '52, had joined the group chat for the class of '53, but whether it was because there was no reason to refuse a gathering with free dues in real-time, or to see the boyfriend that Han Yu-gyeong claimed to have, those who didn't have any other unavoidable prior engagements or family events all attended, showing an incredible turnout.</p>
<p>Park Sang-beom was part of the student council, but it seemed like he was determined to gather people by making all the dues free. It was somewhat rumored that Park Sang-beom's family was a third-generation chaebol from BS Group, so everyone knew that this wasn't a big deal for him. It was laughable to Han Yu-gyeong, who was also the daughter of a chaebol family, but still.</p>
<p>Han Yu-gyeong fell into deep thought. That thought continued until she returned to her studio apartment that night. Soon, having made a big decision, Han Yu-gyeong turned on her phone.</p>
<p>Han Yu-gyeong: Yong-tae, are you asleep?</p>
<p>It was a KakaoTalk message sent after making a big decision. She worried that she wouldn't get a reply because it was a bit late, but a reply came less than 30 seconds after Han Yu-gyeong sent the message.</p>
<p>Jin Yong-tae♥: I'm not asleep yet. What's up?</p>
<p>Han Yu-gyeong: I was wondering if you could come to Korea this weekend...</p>
<p>She felt sorry for suddenly saying this. It was obvious that there wasn't enough time to prepare for a trip, and she also felt sorry for asking Jin Yong-tae to come all the way to Korea just because of a strange bet.</p>
<p>Jin Yong-tae ♥ : I can go. If you're suddenly asking me to come like this, there must be a reason. Can you tell me?</p>
<p>However, despite Han Yu-gyeong's worries, Jin Yong-tae said that he would come to Korea without any hesitation. Han Yu-gyeong looked at her phone and unconsciously wore an excited expression. It was as if she had already met Jin Yong-tae.</p>
<p>Han Yu-gyeong: The thing is...</p>
<p>In response to Jin Yong-tae's question, she unconsciously told him everything that had happened. Jin Yong-tae said that he absolutely had to go if it was something like this, and that he would take a day off on Friday and Monday so he could be in Korea for Friday, Saturday, Sunday, and Monday.</p>
<p>After finishing the conversation, Han Yu-gyeong hugged her phone. And she muttered softly.</p>
<p>,I love you, Yong-tae."</p>
