<p>"Actually, the Sugawara family is a combat force that operates several PMCs. Even if it wasn't for Master's mission, from Taira's point of view, Minamoto Risa and Sugawara Chohei joining forces is something that must be prevented."</p>

Of course, the Sugawara family isn't that big of a force to feel threatened, but from Taira's perspective, having a neutral force with military power siding with Minamoto is why preventing this marriage proposal was a good idea.</p>
<p>"If it's a family that owns several PMCs, approaching them will be more difficult."</p>
<p>"Sugawara Chohei is the second son, so his security isn't that strict. After reading what Asada-ssi investigated, it seems he usually only takes one secretary with him. The secretary is from the Self-Defense Forces, but there shouldn't be any major problems."</p>
<p>"Is that so?"</p>
<p>Having only one bodyguard is good. What Minamoto Risa and I talked about was using my psychokinesis to make it look like an accidental death, and based on that, I discussed with Sakagami Rena where to handle it, looking at Sugawara Chohei's schedule.</p>
<p>And I thought about fabricating an alibi in case I was suspected of a crime.</p>
<p>"I'll transform into Master's appearance with illusion magic and get caught on CCTV a lot."</p>
<p>With Sakagami Rena's level of illusion magic, she could even deceive the CCTV of a regular store, so even if someone judged that Sugawara Chohei's death wasn't an accident but a murder, I wouldn't be a suspect. Even if they thought of me as a suspect, they couldn't easily indict me as long as there was CCTV evidence.</p>
<p>"Then I don't have to worry about getting caught?"</p>
<p>"That's right! I'll run around diligently!"</p>
<p>"No, just run around moderately."</p>
<p>If I ran around too much in my appearance, acting differently from usual, people might think, 'What's wrong with him?' so moderation was important. At my request, Sakagami Rena smiled and replied, "Ehehe, I understand, Master."</p>
<p>"Since everything is decided, how about loosening up a bit before doing a big job today?"</p>
<p>"Loosen up?"</p>
<p>Why is she suddenly telling me to loosen up? But seeing her take off her clothes with a *seseuk* sound, I realized what Sakagami Rena meant by loosening up.</p>
<p>"Okay. Let's relieve some tension in my body before going."</p>

<p>"Bye~! Ryuta-cchi!"</p>
<p>"See you tomorrow, Sensei!"</p>
<p>"You... see you tomorrow."</p>
<p>I received the students' greetings and came outside. Minamoto Risa also decided to play with the students in the clubroom as an alibi.</p>
<p>I arrived home quickly and dyed my hair black with a one-time hair dye spray that lasts about 12 hours. Then I put on sunglasses and wore a rider suit that I don't usually wear.</p>
<p>I went straight outside and headed into the house across the street. The reason why so many students have been coming and going from my house without being noticed by the surroundings is that the house across the street is also being used as a safehouse by the Taira information team.</p>
<p>Sometimes, to hide that it was a safehouse, it was used as the home of a friendly family's daughter attending St. Francisco Girls' Academy, but recently this house had been empty. It was because Taira Kanna and Minamoto Risa were attending St. Francisco Girls' Academy, so they didn't know when they would need a safehouse, so they kept it empty. I occasionally used it as a place to store things I needed for my missions, and today, a burner motorcycle under a homeless person's name was parked in the mansion's parking lot.</p>
<p>I stroked the plain black bike that wasn't easily noticeable. It's well placed as requested. I rode the bike straight to Tokyo, where Sugawara Chohei was.</p>
<p>The Sugawara family is a family in southern Mie Prefecture, so why is Sugawara Chohei in Tokyo? It's because most of the Sugawara family's PMC headquarters are in Tokyo. As expected, Tokyo, the center of Japan, is good for making contracts, so the troops are in southern Mie Prefecture, and they rent a few offices to sign contracts in Tokyo.</p>
<p>Sugawara Chohei was in charge of operating one of those PMCs, and there were rumors that he was surprisingly good at management.</p>
<p>Of course, what was more famous than his management skills was his outrageous behavior, so Sugawara Chohei was over 40 years old but still unmarried.</p>
<p>No matter how much marriage between prestigious families is a union of families, no parents would like their daughter to marry a sex offender, and if they brought in a sex offender as a son-in-law, the family's honor would also fall, so Sugawara Chohei was still single and freely enjoying the nightlife in Tokyo.</p>
<p>Going to Southeast Asia tomorrow was to do things that he couldn't even do in Tokyo, which was infuriating no matter how I looked at it. It wasn't Sugawara Chohei who was infuriating, but Minamoto Ushin, the head of the Minamoto family, who was infuriating.</p>
<p>When I asked if Minamoto Ushin knew about the marriage proposal, Minamoto Risa's sad face is unforgettable.</p>
<p>Minamoto Ushin likes Minamoto Risa the most among his children, but the family's affairs take precedence, so he didn't oppose this marriage proposal.</p>
<p>Rather, Minamoto Ushin emphasized the importance of military power to Minamoto Risa and even advised her that taking control of the Sugawara family, which has several PMCs, would be good for Minamoto Risa's future.</p>
<p>Of course, if you look at it coldly, if Minamoto Risa takes control of the military power of the Sugawara family, she can get a foothold in the Minamoto Group's succession structure, but it was clear that Minamoto Risa would not be happy if she did that.</p>
<p>"This is all because of that sex offender bastard, so I have to kill him."</p>
<p>I muttered and increased the speed of the bike. I'm getting angry for no reason, damn it.</p>

<p>'Did our paths cross because he's a customer at the same restaurant? Am I being too nervous?'</p>

<p>"Huh?"</p>
