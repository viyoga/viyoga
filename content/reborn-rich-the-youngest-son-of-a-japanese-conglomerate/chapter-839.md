<p>'Why isn't Athena Mannerheim learning the Mumatsu Anya martial arts system and is instead learning other martial arts?'</p>
<p>This was what Athena Mannerheim was most curious about. Athena Mannerheim was not learning Mumatsu Anya, which was created by stealing Shinkai Yatsude, the martial art that formed the backbone of Anyaji.</p>
<p>She was learning a fist technique that used a completely new power of white light, and although Athena Mannerheim was not my opponent at the Time, so I couldn't see it properly, she was using a martial art with a completely different system from Mumatsu Anya.</p>
<p>First of all, the energy source used was an unidentified white light, not the Abyss, and when I heard the story from Shimokawabe Airen, who fought Athena Mannerheim, she said that the form of the martial art was different from Mumatsu Anya. In other words, it was not a Shinkai Yatsude-based martial art.</p>
<p>'Just call me Athena. It's awkward to be called Mannerheim.... To answer your question first, I didn't learn the martial arts of my father, no, Takakusaki Gengo, as you said. I was told that I couldn't learn Takakusaki Gengo's martial arts because of my constitution. I learned from one of the Guardians who is now deceased. He was a defeated North Chinese Person, and Takakusaki Gengo brought him to Japan.'</p>
<p>So there are Guardians in Anyaji. It's not like some martial arts group. By the way, a pretty important keyword has come out. Constitution.... Is it related to using white light?</p>
<p>'Constitution.... Are you perhaps talking about that white Aura that you, Athena, use?'</p>
<p>'Yes. To be exact, it can't be seen as a constitution.... I heard that the Aura that Takakusaki Gengo uses to scatter the Abyss is latent in me. For reference, I don't know even if you ask me about that Aura.'</p>
<p>I was going to ask a little more in detail, but Athena Mannerheim took the initiative and said that she didn't know the details either. I'll have to ask Lizhael through Urabe Konoka when Urabe Konoka comes later.</p>
<p>Lizhael would likely know. She had roughly guessed the identity after hearing the story before, so if she saw it directly, she would know more details.</p>
<p>'Maybe, although it's not accurate, it's highly likely that your Parents were targeted because of that constitution. Otherwise, why would a Japanese Person go all the way to Finland to try to assassinate them?'</p>
<p>'I guess so...?'</p>
<p>Athena Mannerheim nodded in agreement to my words.</p>
<p>'I don't know how you found out, but knowing that the Aura you possess is the opposite of the Abyss, it seems that they tried to get you in order to fight my Sabu Fujigasaki Rinka.'</p>
<p>'I didn't even want this power.... It's a bit depressing that my mom and dad died because of it. But I'll cheer up and take Revenge.'</p>
<p>Having talent is a good thing, but if precious people around you die because of the talent you have, it will be thought of as a curse rather than a talent. But Athena Mannerheim did not despair. She was trying to take Revenge on Takakusaki Gengo with the power she had cultivated.</p>
<p>'Then. Do you know anything about Genbaki, no.... Genba Kiyohira?'</p>
<p>Otaki Yuka and Sakagami Rena, who had just contacted Urabe Konoka and were coming in, had puzzled Expressions at my question.</p>
<p>The two knew that Genbaki was my friend, but they seemed curious as to why I was asking Athena Mannerheim about it.</p>
<p>'Ah, what happened was....'</p>
<p>I told them the story of how I smelled Anyaji on Genbaki so that the two could easily understand.</p>
<p>'So? You're saying that it was revealed that he learned Anyaji martial arts after watching the match scene?'</p>
<p>'Yeah, I don't know exactly if it's Mumatsu Anya, but he was using a fist technique similar to Kaiju Kakan, who I fought.'</p>
<p>I nodded in response to Otaki Yuka's question.</p>
<p>'If it's Kaiju Kakan.... He had great Loyalty to Takakusaki Gengo, but he is actually a disciple of one of the Guardians. He was furious that you killed him, so you should be careful. He may come to take Revenge directly. If your friend used a similar form of fist technique to Kaiju Kakan, you can probably think that he learned that Person's fist technique, right? Takakusaki Gengo went to the American Continent to recreate the Four Heavenly Kings, so he's probably trying to entrust the Four Heavenly Kings to your friend.'</p>
<p>There was several Information mixed in this answer.</p>
<p>To organize it a little, first of all, Kaiju Kakan was not Takakusaki Gengo's direct disciple, and the Guardian, who was Kaiju Kakan's Sabu, held a grudge against me.</p>
<p>And Takakusaki Gengo went to North America to fill the two Four Heavenly Kings who were lost after attacking us, and finally, Genbaki was selected as the Four Heavenly Kings and learned Kaiju Kakan's fist technique.</p>
<p>I thought it was a little fortunate after hearing Athena Mannerheim's story. I thought that if Genbaki didn't learn Mumatsu Anya, there would be no need to kill him. Because my Sabu and I's target for assassination are the Guys who learned Mumatsu Anya, which stole Shinkai Yatsude.</p>
<p>Of course, I'm thinking of giving him a gift of an injury of about 8 weeks to wake him up, though.</p>
<p>'Lastly, I'm curious, what is the organizational structure of Anyaji like?'</p>
<p>In fact, before meeting Athena Mannerheim today, I was just guessing that it was made up of the leader Takakusaki Gengo, the Four Heavenly Kings below him, the general disciples below that, and the Friends they were trying to kidnap and make disciples.</p>
<p>But a new word, Guardian, appeared from Athena Mannerheim's Mouth, so I became curious about how Anyaji exists.</p>
<p>'As you Guys know, the leader of Anyaji is Takakusaki Gengo.... Below him is the Guardian Court. It's a Place where there are old monsters that Takakusaki Gengo has traveled around the world to subjugate or build friendships with. In fact, they have tremendous force, but they are not in a relationship where they unilaterally receive Takakusaki Gengo's orders.'</p>
<p>Listening to Athena Mannerheim's explanation, it didn't seem like the Guardian Court was in a Master-Slave Relationship. It seemed to be a gathering of those who had been defeated by Takakusaki Gengo, who were always preparing to rebel against Takakusaki Gengo.</p>
<p>Of course, even if it's Takakusaki Gengo's gathering of losers, since it has the grand name of Guardian Court, they would all be strong. I thought that they were probably as skilled as Yoda, the old man who was Kujo Kina's Sabu.</p>
<p>'How many of those Guardians are there?'</p>
<p>'I don't know because my Sabu died and I hardly go there, but as I remember, about fifteen? Except for my Sabu, they were all healthy, so if it increased, it increased, but it wouldn't have decreased.'</p>
<p>At least fifteen.... That's pretty tough? Of course, Athena Mannerheim said that they were not in a relationship where they listened to Takakusaki Gengo's orders, but we always had to assume the worst-case scenario.</p>
<p>If you think that at least fifteen martial arts Masters and the newly recruited Four Heavenly Kings could attack.... Hmm, I think it might work out?</p>
<p>On our side, there are super Masters like Fujigasaki Rinka Sabu and Home Economics Teacher Shimokawabe Airen, and although I haven't seen her fight directly, there is also Taira Kanna, who overwhelmed the entire Ryuta Rescue Team.</p>
<p>If we borrow some Yin-Yang Masters from the Naruse Family and my PMC, including East Weapon, step up, we might be able to win easily.</p>
<p>The Problem was that if I operated a PMC in Miyashi and got caught, I could fall into Narak even if I was a Taira.</p>
<p>In short, it meant that it was doable if I excluded the PMC, and it was easy if I included the PMC. I'll have to decide this in a full meeting later. I haven't even heard all of Athena Mannerheim's story yet, so I'll have to listen to everything and have a meeting.</p>
<p>'And Takakusaki Gengo has the Four Heavenly Kings and their Anya disciples directly under him, and the Elders also teach their martial arts to their favorite Anya-affiliated martial artists and have a group of disciples. Besides that... there is the Gold Hall (金堂), which manages finances, and the Action Hall (行堂), which dispatches Anya martial artists...'</p>
<p>Sakagami Rena draws an organizational chart on the tablet she is holding according to Athena Mannerheim's explanation.</p>
<p>By the way, Takakusaki Gengo's interest is all in martial arts, or he was too lazy to name them, so except for the military force led by the Four Heavenly Kings and the Guardian Court, all the departments ended with the word Hall.</p>
<p>However, these seemed to be organizations that had to exist for any organization to exist, regardless of military force.</p>
<p>Of course, the Gold Hall, which manages money, and the Action Hall, which dispatches Anya martial artists, were said to have their own military force to protect money or to dispatch when there was a shortage of manpower.</p>
<p>The rest were mostly company workers who did Administration. Of course, due to Anyaji's circumstances, they couldn't hire Laypersons, so those among Anyaji's martial artists who didn't have the talent for martial arts took on this role. According to what I heard from Athena Mannerheim, they were treated as dropouts and were greatly despised.</p>
<p>'Thank you for the Information for now. If you wait a little, someone will come to check if you have a Jusul on you.'</p>
<p>'Yes. I'll wait a little.'</p>
<p>No sooner had Athena Mannerheim finished speaking than footsteps were heard. And the door opened. Urabe Konoka wasn't a saint either, and appeared as soon as I talked about her.</p>
