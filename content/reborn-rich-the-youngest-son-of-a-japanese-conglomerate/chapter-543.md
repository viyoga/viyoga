<p>,Ah, Yamashiro-sensei.</p>
<p>On the way to the infirmary, I ran into Otawa Masato, the history teacher for the first period on Mondays. It was kind of funny how he forced himself to act like he knew me with that rotting expression. I mean, if we run into each other in the hallway like this, I have to say hello, but isn't he making it too obvious that he hates me?</p>
<p>Before, he apparently used to hit on Kijimoto Jurie-ssi and then rode Ishidera Kosuke's line, acting all high and mighty at the academy. I heard he even asked Kijimoto Jurie-ssi out on dates more aggressively, but that didn't last long.</p>
<p>That's because after the temporary academy director Ishidera Kosuke went missing, he became quiet as if he'd never been a problem. He probably knew what that 'missing' meant. Daigo Terue-ssi told me that Otawa Masato was the one who received the most accusations among the teachers who rode Ishidera Kosuke's line.</p>
<p>Apparently, he subtly hit on not only Kijimoto Jurie-ssi but also other teachers and students. I heard that with Daigo Terue-ssi's return and a major purge, he'll be retired by the end of this semester. The conclusion is that there are only a few months left to see this man's face.</p>
<p>,Good morning, Otawa-sensei.</p>
<p>I greeted him with a refreshing smile and entered the infirmary. I heard Otawa Masato grinding his teeth as he passed me, but I didn't give a shit. There was no need to pay attention to someone who would be fired soon. Of course, I was willing to make him disappear at any time if we met in a secluded place.</p>
<p>,Huh? Yamashiro-sensei, what's wrong?</p>
<p>Kijimoto Jurie-ssi seemed to be working, sitting at her desk and writing something. She looked at me with an expression that seemed to say, 'You were with me until dawn, and now you're here to see me again?' so I shook my head as if to say no.</p>
<p>,It's not what you're thinking, Kijimoto-sensei, I have something to ask about Naruse Shiyo.</p>
<p>,Ah, Shiyo-yo?</p>
<p>At my words, Kijimoto Jurie-ssi wore a mixed expression of relief and disappointment. Well, doing it in the infirmary has its own unique flavor, so she might be disappointed. The thrill of possibly getting caught by students gives another kind of pleasure.</p>
<p>,The kids say they haven't been able to reach her over the weekend, and she didn't come to school today either. It's the first time she's been sick for three days in a row. But she always contacted me when she couldn't come to school until now, but today there's no contact. I was wondering if you've heard from her, Kijimoto-sensei.</p>
<p>,Ah, she usually contacts me, but there's no contact today? And she hasn't been sick for three days in a row very often... I'm worried.</p>
<p>Even if she's not feeling well, I know she doesn't have any special underlying diseases, so being sick for three days in a row like this seems pretty serious.</p>
<p>,I'll try contacting her home.</p>
<p>I said, reassuring the worried Kijimoto Jurie-ssi, and left the infirmary. I went straight to the education preparation room and looked up Naruse Shiyo's parents' phone number. There's no mom's phone number, only dad's.</p>
<p>I called Naruse Koyou-gun, Naruse Shiyo's father. But it just kept ringing and he didn't answer.</p>
<p>This time, I contacted the house where Naruse Shiyo lives. Again, only the ringing tone was heard, and no one answered.</p>
<p>Hmm... is this a bit dangerous? The sick daughter isn't answering the phone, and neither is her father, nor anyone at home. Even if the sick Naruse Shiyo or Naruse Shiyo's father couldn't answer, there were servants at the house. Not answering the phone was impossible. Even if they couldn't answer, a callback should come right away, but my phone remained silent.</p>
<p>I took out the student files that the Taira Group investigation team had investigated, hidden in a corner of the filing cabinet.</p>
<p>Actually, I read them once at the beginning of the semester, but I only paid close attention to Minamoto Risa-ssi, who was the target, and a few others, and I was busy organizing the Korean classes, so I didn't remember the contents well.</p>
<p>Naruse Shiyo: Daughter of Naruse Koyou, the second son of the Naruse family. She is congenitally weak and often absent.</p>
<p>Only this much was written about Naruse Shiyo, so it was natural that I didn't remember the contents. Next, there was information about Naruse Koyou and the Naruse family, and there was quite a bit of new information, whether I just skipped it or read it carelessly.</p>
<p>Reading the data, there was content that Naruse Shiyo's father, Naruse Koyou, was originally a collateral line of the Naruse family and was adopted by Naruse Hirohide, the head of the Naruse family, and became the second son. Hmm... suspicious.</p>
<p>,It's definitely suspicious.</p>
<p>I heard a voice, and Sakagami Rena-ssi was looking at the Naruse family data with me. How did she get out during class?</p>
<p>,How did you get out?</p>
<p>,I put an illusion on my seat. Konoe Akina-ssi or Otagi Yuka-ssi might recognize it, but the others will think I'm there.</p>
<p>Ah, she said she was learning illusion magic from the scroll back then, and it seems like she's made some progress. Even if her future is decided as my information team, is it okay for her to wander around like this? Isn't she telling the teacher very confidently that she's skipping class?</p>
<p>,It seemed like Master was worried about something, so I came out, is it because of Shiyo?</p>
<p>,Yeah, at first I was worried because I hadn't heard from her for three days, but when I called, she didn't answer. So I was wondering what was wrong and was looking at the data...</p>
<p>,You mean you're starting to see some suspicious circumstances?</p>
<p>,Yeah.</p>
<p>First of all, what was suspicious from the beginning was that the prestigious Naruse family was almost neglecting Naruse Shiyo's treatment. Even if she had surgery after graduation, there were vacations for the students. I kept thinking that it would be better to have surgery sooner rather than neglecting her illness for so many years.</p>
<p>,And it's also suspicious that they adopted a man from a collateral line when they have a son.</p>
<p>,That's right.</p>
<p>They already have a son to inherit the family, but they still adopt another son? It didn't make sense. Rather, if they had a son, there were quite a few cases where they would adopt a daughter to sell to another family in the name of marriage, but adopting a son was rare.</p>
<p>Of course, Naruse Hirohide might just be a good person or just wanted to have another son, but I wondered if the head of a prestigious family would really do that. Moreover, establishing a second son could only cause disputes over the succession issue, so the other senior members of the family would have opposed it. So, the fact that the senior members of the family didn't oppose adopting Naruse Koyou was something that couldn't help but be suspicious.</p>
<p>There was one more page of Naruse family data, so I turned to the next page. Sakagami Rena-ssi was also looking at the data intently, as if curious.</p>
<p>I remembered seeing the second page of data before. It was written about the history of the Naruse family, stating that they had accumulated considerable wealth from being an Onmyoji family in the Heian period to a trading family that traded with the West in modern times. They currently run a trading company and are a well-known company in their respective field.</p>
<p>,Hmm, an Onmyoji...</p>
<p>,Could it be related?</p>
<p>At Sakagami Rena-ssi's words, I shook my head as if to say I didn't know. Since they were an Onmyoji family a long time ago, there was a higher possibility that it had nothing to do with the present.</p>
<p>,Maybe Shiyo's father is just an adopted child from a collateral line, so they're not giving her treatment?</p>
<p>,That's the most likely possibility.</p>
<p>Well, this was something I couldn't do anything about. It was different from the case of Saionji Diana-ssi, whom I helped. Saionji Diana-ssi was completely abandoned by the Saionji family and was only provided with school fees and a house, and was treated like trash with only 20,000 yen for monthly living expenses including allowance. So they didn't care whether I treated her or not, but Naruse Shiyo was still being given minimal attention by the family, so me intervening would be interfering in another family's affairs.</p>
<p>,Sakagami, you need to find out a bit. Find out how the Naruse family is treating Naruse Shiyo and how her treatment will be in the future.</p>
<p>,Right away?</p>
<p>Sakagami Rena-ssi seemed very excited to be asked to find out. Is it because she doesn't have to go to class? My intention was for her to investigate after class...</p>
<p>,I'll write you a leave pass.</p>
<p>I couldn't break her excitement when she was so excited. I just decided to write her a leave pass. Sakagami Rena-ssi seemed to find investigating much more fun than class. Soon the bell rang, and Sakagami Rena-ssi waited for Otawa Masato, the history teacher for the first period, to pass by and then returned to the classroom with light steps to pack her things.</p>
<p>I couldn't just stand still while at school, so I called Gocho Kikuhito, the 8th secretary of Taira Danjo-nim, who oversees St. Francis Girls' Academy.</p>
<p>-Young Master, what's the matter?</p>
<p>,I'd like to request a higher level of information about the Naruse family.</p>
<p>-... I understand. I will organize the information and send it to you by email within fifteen minutes.</p>
<p>I didn't think I would be rejected because he easily granted requests for information about other families, not money or manpower. As I hung up the phone and waited, Sakagami Rena-ssi, who had packed her things, peeked her face in and greeted me.</p>
<p>,I'll be going, Master. I'll contact you as soon as I get new information.</p>
<p>,Yeah, I'll let you know if I get new information too.</p>
<p>,Yes</p>
<p>Sakagami Rena-ssi slightly bowed her head to me and closed the door. After waiting a bit, the Taira information team sent me an email with high-level information about the Naruse family, which had been newly organized with a higher security level.</p>
