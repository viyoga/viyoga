<p>-An incident occurred where the Cheonan factory of BS Precision was completely destroyed by fire. The police are investigating the incident as arson. The fire resulted in the deaths of Factory Manager Jeon -ssi and other factory executives at the Cheonan factory ....</p>
<p>"Turn off the radio."</p>
<p>Park Mun-seok, the president of Base Technology, said to his driver with a displeased expression. The driver quietly turned off the radio. Base Technology appeared to be an independent company, but in reality, it was a secret affiliate of the BS Group.</p>
<p>Therefore, he felt uncomfortable when bad news about the BS Group came on the radio. Moreover, the situation became difficult because the body of Park Sang-beom, the son of Park Il-soo who was vying for succession of the group, was found near the main gate of Base Technology last night.</p>
<p>It was something completely unrelated to him, but since the body was found in the bushes near the main gate of Base Technology, he became one of the prime suspects. Fortunately, Park Sang-beom's body was full of traces of being killed by a wild animal, so he was able to get off the suspect list, but Park Il-soo's side was still looking at him with suspicious eyes.</p>
<p>Base Technology was in a position to develop technologies that the BS Group had risks in developing directly, so technology could not be given or not given depending on the faction. Therefore, according to the order of the current Chairman Park Seong-wook, Base Technology was directly under Park Seong-wook.</p>
<p>So, he was in a neutral position, not belonging to the forces of Park Il-soo or Park Il-man, but as the current Chairman Park Seong-wook gradually stepped down from management due to old age, the pressure on Park Mun-seok, the president of Base Technology, was gradually increasing. He was threatened that he had to decide on his affiliation before Park Seong-wook retired.</p>
<p>Park Mun-seok had similar thoughts. The treatment of someone who joined from the beginning of the succession war and someone who joined when a certain force became advantageous could not be the same. Park Mun-seok also intended to decide on a course before the war between the heirs broke out.</p>
<p>Park Mun-seok thought that even if Park Seong-wook retired or was forced to retire, there would still be a war between brothers, but that was too naive. The war had begun since Park Sang-beom's body was found yesterday. Or perhaps the succession war had been going on since before, and Park Sang-beom may have been one of the victims of that war.</p>
<p>He was very confused because of that, but he had to solve the immediate task first. In order to successfully launch the security company newly planned by the BS Group, he had to bring down Vanguard, which had a good reputation in the market. The recent theft incidents were all staged by Base Technology. Base Technology was using this incident to find fault with Vanguard.</p>
<p>"But Vanguard's president is quite audacious. Asking us to come directly...."</p>
<p>Yoon Yeong-cheol, the driver and secretary, said while driving. Han Yu-gyeong, the president of Vanguard, had been drawn to them as they had planned until now, but now the situation had changed a bit.</p>
<p>-If we go through the main gate full of blood stains, the evidence will be damaged, so please come here this time.</p>
<p>Even now, the police were searching, making it difficult for Base Technology employees to come to work. He could go to work through the back gate, but the atmosphere in the company was not good at all. In that situation, if even the Vanguard people with a hostile atmosphere came and went, the situation would be even worse, so Park Mun-seok obediently followed Han Yu-gyeong's suggestion.</p>
<p>"Given our situation, she must have used it smartly. But she's still a fool who doesn't know our purpose."</p>
<p>"Still, isn't it laughable? That the youngest daughter of the Shinkang Group is so audacious."</p>
<p>"She probably can't even imagine that our goal isn't money, but Vanguard's reputation."</p>
<p>Vanguard was a well-known company in the industry, so its headquarters were located in a bustling area of Gangnam. Park Mun-seok, who felt twisted inside at the sight, clicked his tongue once. If Base Technology, which had excellent technical skills, had not focused only on secret technologies, it would have been much more famous and bigger than such a security company.... It was a pity.</p>
<p>"We've arrived, Mr. President."</p>
<p>At Yoon Yeong-cheol's words, Park Mun-seok got out. Park Mun-seok, who got out in the underground parking lot, took the elevator and arrived on the 6th floor, where the promised conference room was located.</p>
<p>Yoon Yeong-cheol was ostensibly a driver, so he could not accompany Park Mun-seok. Besides, Park Mun-seok thought that he alone was enough to deal with a mere freshman girl. After all, he was just pretending to negotiate to buy time, so there was no need to use a lot of people and waste labor costs. Of course, he was planning to submit documents stating that he had brought lawyers and pocket the lawyer fees.</p>
<p>Ding! 6th floor.</p>
<p>Park Sang-beom, who got off the elevator, was guided by the female receptionist standing in front of the elevator to what appeared to be a conference room. Two burly men stood on either side of the door. Park Mun-seok snorted inwardly at the sight.</p>
<p>'Hmph! Is she trying to intimidate me with something like this? How childish. She's really childish because she's a young girl....'</p>
<p>Park Mun-seok opened the door and entered the conference room.</p>
<p>"!!"</p>
<p>Inside, Han Yu-gyeong and Vanguard executives, whom he had seen until now, were sitting. But their appearance was different from the atmosphere until yesterday. They were all emitting some kind of pressure, and at Han Yu-gyeong's feet, who was sitting in the seat of honor in the center, a huge black panther was lying down. It was almost the size of a bull, so its overwhelming presence was enormous.</p>
<p>'It's, it's probably a decoration?'</p>
<p>Park Mun-seok's thought changed in an instant. That's because the eyes of the panther statue (?) flashed open as Park Mun-seok took a step closer. Park Mun-seok took a step back without realizing it.</p>
<p>"Heeik! What, what is this?!"</p>
<p>"What do you mean, 'this'? This is my pet cat, Nero."</p>
<p>"Who would see that as a cat?"</p>
<p>"What are you talking about? Nero, meow. Meow!"</p>
<p>Han Yu-gyeong said in a mocking voice. The black panther called Nero seemed to be in a bad mood at the sound of being called a cat, so it raised its head and growled softly. It was the sound of a beast that was far from a meow that anyone could hear.</p>
<p>"Don't worry about my cat and sit across from me."</p>
<p>"Hmm...!"</p>
<p>As Park Mun-seok sat down, the female employee who had been guiding him until just now brought tea. But Park Mun-seok was not interested in the tea. As soon as Park Mun-seok sat down, the black panther lightly jumped up and lay down in front of the conference room door, as if to block his escape route. And it began to watch Park Mun-seok with sharp eyes.</p>
<p>"Th, that panther...."</p>
<p>"No, Nero is a cat. You're not going to leave right away anyway, are you? You don't have to worry about Nero because you have to do the negotiations that the president of Base Technology likes so much. I'll take it away when the negotiations are all over."</p>
<p>Grrr...!</p>
<p>Another cry was heard from behind his neck. Park Mun-seok suddenly remembered Park Sang-beom's cause of death, which he had heard in passing. Death from injuries caused by a wild animal...! Could it be...!</p>
<p>"Well.... Our proposal has changed."</p>
<p>Han Yu-gyeong handed Park Sang-beom a document. The document was part of a slush fund ledger that Park Sang-beom had sent to congressmen and relevant ministers in order to develop illegal technologies. How, how did she get this?</p>
<p>"Our proposal is."</p>
<p>Han Yu-gyeong's voice became even colder. Park Mun-seok felt cold sweat flowing. His back became wet and his legs trembled. It was a feeling he was experiencing for the first time in his life, but Park Mun-seok knew what that feeling was.</p>
<p>Killing intent.</p>
<p>The energy to kill the opponent no matter what. That was pouring from Han Yu-gyeong and everyone here to Park Mun-seok.</p>
<p>"Our proposal is that Base Technology be loyal to me, not the BS Group."</p>
<p>"What! D, don't say such ridiculous things! And our Base Technology has nothing to do with the BS Group!"</p>
<p>It was ridiculous. Even if Han Yu-gyeong had the slush fund ledger, how could she swallow Base Technology whole with just her words? Han Yu-gyeong also sensed a rebellious spirit in Park Mun-seok's tone that he would never accept the proposal, so she smiled and handed over another document.</p>
<p>Park Mun-seok trembled as he received the document. This time, it was data on illegal human experiments conducted at Base Technology. Even if he could get away with bribing, he could never get away with such illegal experiments. If this fact became public, it was obvious that the BS Group would immediately cut ties with Base Technology.</p>
<p>As if that wasn't the end, Han Yu-gyeong smiled and handed over another document. This time, it was not a document related to Base Technology. It was a document about Park Mun-seok himself. Several photos were printed on the document, most of which were scenes of Park Mun-seok and a young woman rolling naked in bed.</p>
<p>"I'll deliver the last document to your family myself."</p>
<p>"That...! My relationship with my wife is over anyway. I'll have to pay some alimony, but you can't threaten me with this."</p>
<p>That was certainly true. His old wife was nothing to Park Mun-seok himself. So, he couldn't be threatened with these photos.</p>
<p>"As the investigation confirmed, your relationship with your wife is not good. But how about this?"</p>
<p>The fourth document that Han Yu-gyeong handed over contained a profile of his son. It detailed where he went to college, what his school schedule was, what his friendships were like, and what clubs he was in.</p>
<p>"I heard your son joined a mountaineering club. He might meet a wild animal in the mountains."</p>
<p>"Ar, are you threatening me now?"</p>
<p>"Threatening? You're talking as if I can control wild animals?"</p>
<p>Park Mun-seok gritted his teeth at Han Yu-gyeong's brazen expression. No matter how negligent he was as a husband, he was a father. It was natural to cherish his only son. Han Yu-gyeong was now threatening him with his only son.</p>
<p>"Even if you tell your son not to go to the mountains, will your son listen to his father who cheated and divorced?"</p>
<p>"!"</p>
<p>So, she's using the third document in this way.... Park Mun-seok was horrified by Han Yu-gyeong's meticulous plan.</p>
<p>"So now...."</p>
<p>The pressure started again. The panther was growling behind his neck, and everyone in the room was sending killing intent towards him.</p>
<p>"What are you going to do?"</p>
<p>Han Yu-gyeong's face, asking with a bright smile, was very beautiful, but it looked more frightening to Park Mun-seok than anyone else. Even more than Park Seong-wook, the chairman of the BS Group whom he served.</p>
