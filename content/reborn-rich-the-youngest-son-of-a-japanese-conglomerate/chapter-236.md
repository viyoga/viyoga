<p>"As Muyeonggwi-nim knows, our Black Moon Company's New York branch wasn't in the best shape."</p>
<p>As a branch of the Black Moon Gang whose main business was real estate, New York was a good place for them to operate. Redevelopment was quite active, and just buying land in the slums, restoring order with the Black Moon Gang's forces, and then selling it, as they used to do, was a good way to make money.</p>
<p>But the problem was that this city was New York. With so many forces intertwined, the Black Moon Gang's traditional strategies didn't work. There were already many competitors doing the same thing as the Black Moon Gang, and even if the Black Moon Gang bought land, the opponents they had to restore order against were big-shot mafias or gangs, so the sacrifices were great. They could make money, but their power diminished.</p>
<p>In order to pay tribute to the Black Moon Gang headquarters, they had to carry out various operations, but these operations reduced their power, leading to a contradictory situation where they lost the momentum to pay the next tribute. Because of this, the head of the New York branch had to devise a new method, not the original Black Moon Gang way.</p>
<p>Or rather, it could be said that they returned to a more traditional method. The Black Moon Gang, like the old mafia, began to take control of New York's Chinatown and collect protection money.</p>
<p>Although it didn't make as much money as openly working in the slums, they stretched their hands into everything that made money based on Chinatown, such as drugs, human trafficking, and illegal arms dealing. As a result, they made more money, and their power began to grow. Just as Jackson Sha was seriously considering whether to become independent without sending money to headquarters, things began to happen.</p>
<p>There were no problems with the protection money, but problems began to arise with all the other illegal activities that the Black Moon Gang's New York branch was doing.</p>
<p>It started with the disappearance of a low-level thug doing a simple job of delivering drugs, which was a common occurrence. Accidents during drug deliveries were common, but finding the thug's corpse mummified with all the blood drained was not common at all.</p>
<p>After that, Black Moon Gang members in drug warehouses, human trafficking prisons, and unlicensed weapons factories began to die one or two at a time.</p>
<p>By the time Jackson Sha realized something was wrong, more than 30 percent of the Black Moon Gang's New York branch's forces had already been killed by this unknown monster. The subordinates began to flee in fear, and naturally, the illegal activities of the Black Moon Gang's New York branch were taken away by other gangs, and the control of Chinatown gradually began to shrink.</p>
<p>So, they couldn't pay tribute for several months, and naturally, the Black Moon Gang headquarters judged that they had cut off the tribute. Cutting off the tribute meant committing an act of independence, that is, rebellion against the Black Moon Gang, so Muyeonggwi's execution squad was dispatched.</p>
<p>In addition, by chance, the department in charge of communication between the headquarters and the New York branch was ousted due to internal factional strife within the Black Moon Gang, so all contact with New York was cut off. <p>"We'll investigate first, and if your story is true, we'll spare you and let you keep your position as the head of the New York branch. But if it's a lie, I'll make you regret not dying here for the rest of your life."</p>
<p>Muyeonggwi, pointing at Jackson Sha with Daegyeom, soon went outside. Jackson Sha sighed.
