<p>The Anya Branch Kwon Masters, who had been running behind their leader Kaiho Kyoichi, suddenly realized they were in a dense forest. They had clearly been climbing the well-maintained stairs of the shrine until now, but suddenly they were in the middle of a dense forest, so they couldn't help but be bewildered.</p>
<p>"Where did the leader and the others go...?"</p>
<p>In an instant, their leader and about ten comrades in front of them disappeared, and as everyone looked around in bewilderment, Piiing! with a sound that tore through the air, an Arrow flew between the trees. The Arrow shot from the forest accurately hit one of the Anya Branch Kwon Masters.</p>
<p>"Kueuk!"</p>
<p>Seeing their comrade fall after being hit by an Arrow near the breast, the Anya Branch Kwon Masters each tensed up and looked around. But as if the Arrow had never been shot, the forest was silent.</p>
<p>"It's too quiet."</p>
<p>It was Spring, the season when all things awaken, but the forest was strangely quiet. The sounds of insects or Birds that should be heard in a forest were completely absent. The Anya Branch Kwon Masters unconsciously broke out in Cold Sweat at the ominous Atmosphere of the forest, where only their own Breathing Sound could be heard.</p>
<p>What broke this ominous Silence was again the sound of Arrows tearing through the air. This time, three Arrows were shot simultaneously and three Kwon Masters fell, unable to dodge the Arrows.</p>
<p>"Everyone, hide behind the Trees!"</p>
<p>Although they had been Brainwashed, they were not fools, so the Anya Branch Kwon Masters all quickly began to move to dodge the Arrows.</p>
<p>Although four had already fallen, sixteen people still remained. If they could find the Enemy shooting a Bow from afar, they felt they could get Revenge for their fallen comrades.</p>
<p>Piiing!</p>
<p>"Kueuk...!"</p>
<p>"Ugh...!"</p>
<p>This time, two people fell. But because they were thoroughly prepared, the Anya Branch Kwon Masters were able to pinpoint the Position from which the Arrows were shot.</p>
<p>"It's over there! Run!"</p>
<p>"Before the Archer draws an Arrow! We must approach as much as possible!"</p>
<p>The Anya Branch Kwon Masters ran through the forest, relying on the dim Moonlight. Even while running, Arrows kept flying, and two or three people at a time were hit by Arrows and fell. But because they had completely identified the Position from which the Arrows were coming, it seemed they could quickly find the Archer and get Revenge.</p>
<p>"Found you!"</p>
<p>As they ran, they saw a Woman in Shrine Maiden attire drawing a Bow in a clearing in the forest. The Shrine Maiden with a red headband on her Head opened her Mouth as she saw the Anya Kwon Masters emerge from the bushes.</p>
<p>"That's what I should be saying. Found you!"</p>
<p>The headband Shrine Maiden released the bowstring at the same time she said, "Found you!" With a 'tung' sound, three Arrows flew and embedded themselves in the torsos of the Anya Kwon Masters. Because it was Marksmanship at such close range, the Anya Kwon Masters fell without even a moaning sound. Now only two remained.</p>
<p>The remaining two, while their comrades fell, the Anya Kwon Masters had already approached close to the headband Shrine Maiden, so the headband Shrine Maiden had no Time to shoot an Arrow.</p>
<p>""Die!""</p>
<p>The Anya Kwon Masters simultaneously extended their Fists towards the headband Shrine Maiden. The headband Shrine Maiden, without panicking, stabbed one man's Shoulder with an Arrow and then parried the other's Attack with her Bow. The Anya Kwon Master stabbed by the Arrow fell helplessly, and the headband Shrine Maiden leaped back as if performing a Back Dash in a Game, hiding behind a large Tree.</p>
<p>A one-on-one standoff.</p>
<p>Still, it wasn't a bad Situation for the Anya Kwon Master. Because there was a Tree between them, there was no Worry about being hit by an Arrow.</p>
<p>As the Anya Kwon Master was feeling Sweet Relief, a 'tung!' sound of a bowstring snapping was heard.</p>
<p>Shuuuung peok!</p>
<p>The Arrow shot by the headband Shrine Maiden curved around the obstructing Tree and lodged itself in the Anya Kwon Master's Shoulder.</p>
<p>"Cu-curve shot...?!"</p>
<p>The headband Shrine Maiden, who still looked young, had demonstrated the art of the curve shot, where the Arrow's Path was not straight but drew a Sensual Curve, avoiding Obstacles and hitting the Goal.</p>
<p>"Kueuk...!"</p>
<p>The Anya Kwon Master endured the Pleasurable Ache and pulled out the Arrow. He had expected immense Pleasurable Ache, but the Arrow came out surprisingly easily. This was because the arrowhead was blunt, not sharp enough to kill.</p>
<p>Then why did all his comrades fall with just One Shot of an Arrow? The Moment he had this Question, he felt his Head spin and the Space in front of his Eyes distort.</p>
<p>'Poison...!'</p>
<p>The Last remaining Anya Kwon Master felt the Floor surge up before his Eyes and collapsed. The headband Shrine Maiden's Voice reached the Whispering Ear of the falling Anya Kwon Master.</p>
<p>"Perhaps because it was an Arrow that had been used once, it held out a little...."</p>
<p>The Arrow used on the Last Anya Kwon Master was the same Arrow that had stabbed the previous Anya Kwon Master, so it seemed the anesthetic coating was a little insufficient. Other Anya Kwon Masters had fallen as soon as they were hit by an Arrow, judging by the fact that he had held out even a little.</p>
<p>"As Athena-ssi requested, I tried my best not to kill them.... They didn't die, right?"</p>
<p>The headband Shrine Maiden, Urabe Konoka, muttered as she called the Transport team that had been prepared in advance to move the fallen Anya Kwon Masters.</p>
<p>Originally, she could have dealt with them faster, but at Athena Mannerheim's Request, she controlled her Power not to kill them and made an Effort not to hit vital points, which is why it took Time.</p>
<p>"You've splendidly defeated them all."</p>
<p>"Yes, Hanakame-nim."</p>
<p>Seeing Hanakame appear in the forest with ten unconscious Anya Kwon Masters, Urabe Konoka replied with a Smile.</p>
<p>"All the Attackers on the shrine side have been dealt with, so now only the Enemy's main force remains. They'll defend well, right?"</p>
<p>"Everyone has prepared thoroughly, so they will defend well."</p>
<p>Hanakame nodded his Head at Urabe Konoka's Question. Since they already knew the Enemy was coming, they could defend even if several times more Enemies came.</p>
<p>To definitely block the Attack that occurred while Taira Ryuta was absent was everyone's common Thought. As everyone had made a strong Decision, Taira Gensuke would be severely Punished and forced to flee.</p>
<p>"The rear Impact team must have made Entry, right?"</p>
<p>At Taira Gensuke's Words, his buha, Davi Mendes, who had been with him since Brazil, nodded his Head.</p>
<p>Davi Mendes was a man who looked like a Latin gang member wearing sunglasses, and along with Álvaro Ramos, he was active as Taira Gensuke's closest confidant.</p>
<p>The Reason Davi Mendes wore sunglasses even on a dark Night was because he was equipped with cybernetic Eyes. Even dark sunglasses could not obstruct the Field of Vision of the cybernetic Eyes which had various functions, so he always wore sunglasses.</p>
<p>"The Mansion is quite spacious. It seems they built it ignorantly large because the Land was cheap. Perhaps because he's a bastard, he thinks as long as it's Vulgar and big, that's all that matters."</p>
<p>"As Boss said, the Mansion is so spacious that once we Breakthrough the Entrance, we'll have to split into several teams."</p>
<p>At Taira Gensuke's sneering Words, Davi Mendes replied dryly. Taira Gensuke muttered that Davi Mendes was a boring bastard and slapped his Shoulder.</p>
<p>"Alright, as you said, let's split into teams and depart. We'll divide into Gensuke's team and Mendes's team, right?"</p>
<p>"Understood."</p>
<p>At Taira Gensuke's Words, Davi Mendes nodded his Head and sent Hand Signals to the buha waiting behind him, and they split into two teams in perfect Order.</p>
<p>"Let's depart."</p>
<p>"Understood, Boss."</p>
<p>With Taira Gensuke's departure Signal, everyone began to move quickly. The Surroundings around the Mansion were an open field with nothing else, so Taira Gensuke's Attack team headed towards the Main Gate as quickly as possible.</p>
<p>"Even here in Neo Tokyo, it seems they didn't even consider the Thought of being attacked. A guy of this Level as the Boss of a Taira Affiliate Company...!"</p>
<p>There was no Security Guard at the large Main Gate. It wasn't even locked, so they could easily open the Door.</p>
<p>"What's the Thought?"</p>
<p>"It could be a Trap, Boss."</p>
<p>Although it was an Act of attacking the bastard Taira Ryuta whom they had ignored, they had come with some Preparation in their Mind, but the Breakthrough of the Main Gate was too easy, making them suspicious.</p>
<p>"That bastard, how would he know I was coming and set a Trap?"</p>
<p>"Indeed.... If it's not a Trap, it would be best to use this lax Security and execute the Operation as quickly as possible."</p>
<p>"Good. Let's go quickly."</p>
<p>The two, who had decided to Attack as quickly and decisively as possible, easily passed through the Main Gate.</p>
<p>"If you go straight this way, it's the main building. Taira Ryuta will probably be here too. I'll go around this way and Attack."</p>
<p>"Good. Clean up the rest and join me at the main building. Because I'll show you an interesting Scene."</p>
<p>That's how Taira Gensuke and Davi Mendes split, each with their own teams. Taira Gensuke ran towards the main building, the largest Mansion visible in the distance. But he couldn't reach the main building.</p>
<p>Between Taira Gensuke and the main building, someone was waiting. A Woman with Silver hair, almost White hair, and a small Group were standing, blocking the Road.</p>
<p>"Who are you?!"</p>
<p>"Such a Question shouldn't it be asked by the Head of Household or a Security Guard?"</p>
<p>Taira Gensuke, seeing the Silver-haired Woman blocking his Path, his Eyes gleamed. It was a familiar Face. It was clearly Takakusaki Athena, the adopted daughter of Taira Gensuke's martial arts teacher/master Takakusaki Gengo, glaring at him.</p>
<p>Urabe Konoka, Athena B-cuts~!</p>
<p>Konoka first~!</p>
<p>Athena~!</p>
