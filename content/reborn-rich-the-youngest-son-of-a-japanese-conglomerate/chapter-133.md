<p>"You are the customers who made reservations at our Inn, aren't you?" A Voice came from behind the people who had just disembarked from the boat without a sound. Everyone turned around in surprise to see a small Grandmother, just under 140 cm tall, standing there. Judging by her Kimono and the Apron she wore over it, she seemed to be an Inn employee.</p>
<p>"Ah, we made a reservation as the Kanto University team, and this is...."</p>
<p>"I made a reservation under the name Taira Ryuta."</p>
<p>"Hehehe, I am Hamahata Motoko, the Master and Master Chef of Chōten Inn. Everyone, please follow me." The Inn owner and Master Chef... Is that why she's wearing an Apron? Despite being a Grandmother, the Inn owner was spry, confidently leading the way to guide us to the Inn.</p>
<p>"You may go to your respective reserved rooms. Lunch and dinner will be brought to your rooms, hehehe...." The Inn was a classic Japanese Ryokan itself. The exterior, surprisingly, was very well-maintained. I had expected a somewhat shabby Inn, given its remote Island location, so it was a surprise. Moreover, since Chōtenjima was a volcanic Island, Chōten Inn was a Ryokan that offered hot springs.</p>
<p>There was a public hot spring, but I had no intention of enjoying a hot spring bath while looking at naked men, so I reserved the most expensive room, which came with a private open-air bath. If it had been a mixed-gender bath, my thoughts might have been different, but mixed-gender baths were almost nonexistent in this era. My room, as expected of the most expensive room, was clean and tidy. Opening the window to the outside, there was an open-air bath right there, offering a great view of the Sea.</p>
<p>I decided to take a hot spring bath later and grabbed my folding bike to head Outside the Inn. First, I decided to take a tour around Chōtenjima, as I didn't know what might happen. Then, after lunch, I planned to visit the shrine where the ritual was said to take place tomorrow.</p>
<p>"Where are you going, martial artist-nim?"</p>
<p>"I'm just going to take a tour around the Island." Uchidate Kanimi spoke to me. Perhaps because I had demonstrated a straight punch from an ancient martial art on the boat, she called me a martial artist.</p>
<p>"Can I come with you?"</p>
<p>"You can ride on the back, but there isn't much space, so you'll have to hold on tight." I said, pointing to the back seat, which could carry light Luggage. Uchidate Kanimi smiled and got on the back. Then she gripped my waist tightly.</p>
<p>"I'm holding on tight, hehe." Hmm, what was that? No matter how handsome I was, this was the first time I'd encountered such an assertive Woman. Reconnaissance was the priority, so I just nodded and started the electric bike. As we set off, I noticed that the boat that had brought us had already departed. First, we headed north. The north was a Cliff area, so there were few private houses. As it was a Cliff area, the uphill road continued. We rode to the very top of the Cliff, stopped the bike, and gazed at the Sea for a moment.</p>
<p>The Sea viewed from a similar height and the Sea viewed from above felt somehow different. I couldn't explain it precisely, but the Scenery was different. Watching the Waves break rhythmically, I felt an unusual sense of emotion.</p>
<p>"Doesn't the Sea look a little different from what we saw on the boat?"</p>
<p>"It's just the same Sea." Uchidate Kanimi replied, seemingly uninterested in my Words. What kind of dry Woman is this? I got back on the bike and continued driving along the coastal road, past the Cliff area. The road was adjacent to private houses, and I saw People working in vegetable gardens and others maintaining boats. As we passed, the villagers stopped what they were doing and stared at us with a somewhat empty Gaze. Perhaps it was because I saw them while riding the bike, but their Gaze was empty, as if hollow.</p>
<p>"Are they wary of Outsiders? Their Gaze is strange."</p>
<p>"I wonder." It was Uchidate Kanimi again, answering perfunctorily. This was a completely different Feeling from her assertive Attitude before we set off. Why did she ask for a ride then? Since the Conversation ended in such short answers, I also felt a little Frustrating and stopped talking, just driving silently. By the time we had circled the Island, raindrops were falling from the sky. Huh? There was definitely no rain forecast.</p>
<p>"It's raining, so I'll speed up a bit."</p>
<p>"Yes." It seemed I would get completely soaked at this rate, so I sped up a bit more and returned to the Inn. By the time we arrived at the Inn, the rain was getting heavier. It looked like a heavy rain would pour down any moment now.</p>
<p>"Thank you for the ride." Uchidate Kanimi greeted me with her usual charming Smile. She had been so perfunctory earlier, what was up with this Woman? When I looked at her with a slightly strange Expression, she opened her mouth as if making an Excuse.</p>
<p>"My Attitude was a bit strange, wasn't it? Actually, the bike and the Cliff were a bit scary...."</p>
<p>"Oh, is that so? You seem to get a bit stiff when you're scared."</p>
<p>"Yes, hehe...." Is that it? I acknowledged it and headed to my room. Since there was still a little time before lunch, I turned on my Smartphone and checked the weather forecast again. The forecast had already changed to rain. It would be raining until at least tomorrow evening. After checking the weather forecast and browsing the internet, I heard a knock on the door.</p>
<p>"Lunch is served. May I open the door?"</p>
<p>"Yes." The door opened, announcing lunch. The main dish was braised fish in soy sauce, accompanied by a few pieces of sashimi, seasoned seaweed, pickled vegetables, and seasoned wild herbs. The person who brought the food was a young waitress, and it seemed to be the first time I had seen a young Woman my age in this village. Well, I had only seen the villagers while circling the Island on my bike. Anyway, the waitress was a Beautiful Woman, even hard to find in the City. Perhaps she had a delicate Feeling about her?</p>
<p>If Uchidate Kanimi felt like a glamorous female college student from the City, this waitress felt like a wild flower blooming modestly on a Countryside road.</p>
<p>"Hello? I am Hamahata Konoka, and I will be Attending you during your stay."</p>
<p>"Yes. Please take good care of me." Her surname was the same as the Inn owner Grandmother, so they seemed to be Blood Kin. Given her Age, she was probably the Granddaughter... It seemed that for the most expensive room, they even assigned someone to attend. Hmm...! After tasting it, the lunch was all delicious. The inn owner, Grandmother, seemed to be a good cook. Hamahata Konoka watched me eat lunch a bit awkwardly and even refilled my glass when I finished my tea.</p>
<p>Actually, since I ate everything with my own Hands, there wasn't much for her to attend to. About halfway through my meal, a Scream suddenly came from Outside.</p>
<p>"Kyaaak!!!" What was that? Hamahata Konoka and I left the Inn room at the Scream and ran towards the source of the sound. We arrived at the public open-air bath. And it was the women's bath. Many People had already gathered, drawn by the Scream, and peeking through the crowd, I saw a man lying naked. He was one of the two men from Kanto University whom I hadn't been introduced to, and he seemed to have slipped in the open-air bath, lying sprawled on the stone floor.</p>
<p>"Wano, what do you think?" Professor Honmura Koga asked Wano Haruto. Wano Haruto, with his characteristic gloomy Expression, put on Gloves and began to examine the Corpse of the dead male student.</p>
<p>"What happened?"</p>
<p>"Ah, that Wano Haruto is a famous Genius at Kanto University. He's minoring in the police Department and criminal psychology. So, by looking at the condition of the Corpse, he can tell if it was Murder or if he really just fell and died."</p>
<p>Professor Honmura Koga answered my Question. So, while pursuing a Ph.D. in folklore, he's also studying police Department and criminal psychology to the point of being able to use it in Actual combat? I understood why Wano Haruto's default Expression was gloomy. He must be a slave to three professors (a graduate student), which would explain that Expression.</p>
<p>"It's Murder..."</p>
<p>"!"</p>
<p>"!" Everyone was surprised by Wano Haruto's weak Words. Wano Haruto continued to speak, regardless of their surprise.</p>
<p>"Judging by the slight rigor mortis starting in the temporomandibular joint and cervical vertebra, the time of death was one hour and thirty minutes ago." One hour and thirty minutes ago was almost immediately after we arrived at the Inn. The time I was roughly organizing my Luggage and preparing my bike to go out was precisely the time of the Murder.</p>
<p>"Why do you say it's Murder?" When Professor Honmura Koga asked, Wano Haruto began to explain in his usual weak Voice.</p>
<p>"If you look at the floor, it's a stone floor, but where the Blood is, it's flat. However, if you look at the Corpse's Head, it looks like it was struck by something with an edge. Since the place he fell and the wound on his Head don't match, it's Murder."</p>
<p>"This can't be...! There's a murderer among us?" As one person muttered, as if on cue, the sky brightened, and Kwarrreung! Lightning struck. The Women screamed, "Kyaaak!"</p>
<p>and the men also Flinched slightly. It was a sign of a Storm.</p>
<p>"We can't keep Morisuke-gun here, so let's take Photos of the Corpse and its Surroundings, and put it somewhere that won't decay easily."</p>
<p>"There's a cool place in the Inn's Underground; we can put it there." The owner Grandmother said. The men from Kanto University and I, as instructed by the Grandmother, carried the Corpse in a long box to the Underground.</p>
<p>"How convenient, there's a coffin-like box." One of the two I hadn't been introduced to muttered. When I looked at him, the man introduced himself as Kimura Atsuhiro. I also told Kimura Atsuhiro my name.</p>
<p>"It's a custom for Seongin on Chōtenjima to prepare their own coffins in advance. We're probably using the coffin that the Inn owner Grandmother prepared beforehand."</p>
<p>Wano Haruto replied in a gloomy Voice. Ah, so there's such a custom. How interesting. The Underground seemed to be connected to a natural Donggul, and there were a few jars, perhaps for storing soy sauce or soybean paste.</p>
<p>"It really is cool, just as the owner Grandmother said." As Kimura Atsuhiro said, the warehouse, which was a modified Donggul, was cool enough to feel Han'gi. We left the Corpse as it was and came back up. When we reached the Jeesang, everyone had grim Expressions, and seeing us, Professor Honmura Koga opened his mouth.</p>
<p>"Due to the sudden rain, the police can't come...." The nearest police Department was said to be on Hachijōjima Island, and it seemed they couldn't come due to the rain. I had a Feeling something would happen when it started raining, but it's going exactly according to Cliche. I let out a Sigh.</p>
