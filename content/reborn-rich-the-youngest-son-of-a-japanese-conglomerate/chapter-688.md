<p>During the Third World War, Tokyo had been bombed. As it was a fierce Jeonjaeng befitting the name 'World War', a City being bombed was not unusual, and it was common for the City's Appearance to Change due to that Bombardment.</p>
<p>When Tokyo was bombed, a part of Akihabara was also bombed, and during post-Jeonjaeng reconstruction, its old Appearance disappeared. Befitting the Otaku holy land, it transformed into an area where large, futuristic Design shopping districts gathered.</p>
<p>There were many old Otaku who longed for Akihabara before the Jeonjaeng, but since the buildings had already disappeared due to the Bombardment, there was nothing to be done. There was no choice but to Adapt to the new Appearance.</p>
<p>Of course, Minamoto Risa and I, who had never been here before, didn't know the old Appearance well, so without anything to long for, we simply walked around, admiring the buildings made with futuristic Design.</p>
<p>"It's a spaceship-shaped building, but with Christmas decorations, it looks a little strange."</p>
<p>"I think it looks fine?" We entered the spaceship-shaped building and headed to a Store that dealt with Animation, Comic, and their merchandise from the 2000s to the 2030s.</p>
<p>"Wow...!" As soon as we entered the Store and went to the ○-Punch Man section, Minamoto Risa's Eyes sparkled like the Milky Way. Indeed, befitting an Otaku holy land, there was a lot. Even for an old Comic and Animation, there were quite a lot of Items.</p>
<p>"Look at this, you~! It's a Borus spaceship Figure...!" She grabbed my Hand and pulled me, pointing at the space Battleship in the Glass display case. But not only Borus, even the Borus spaceship is made into a Figure? Its Size was large, and its Design was quite precise, so the Price was considerable, around 230,000 yen.</p>
<p>"Lisa, calm down for a Moment."</p>
<p>"No, how can I calm down after seeing these?!" In fact, from a connoisseur's perspective, one couldn't help but be dazzled. I also stopped trying to calm Minamoto Risa down, and we decided to look around the Store separately.</p>
<p>Before Possession, I was a light fan who only read Comic books, but now that I was in a Store full of merchandise, I saw a few things I wanted to buy.</p>
<p>Oh.... The Dragon Ball Shenron Figure is cool, isn't it? I walked around, checking a few Figures and merchandise that caught my eye. If, after going around once, my Mind hadn't Changed and I was still drawn to them, I intended to buy them.</p>
<p>As I walked around, I came across a corner labeled 'Excavated Items ' with 'All 500 yen!' written on it. Curious about what it was, I called over a passing clerk and asked.</p>
<p>"What is this Excavated Items corner?"</p>
<p>"Ah.... These are Items found in the Ruins of post-Jeonjaeng Akihabara. There are some valuable Items, but many are also wounded from the Bombardment, so the Price is low."</p>
<p>"Is that so?" It's been 8 years since the Jeonjaeng ended, yet there's still an Excavated Items corner. It seemed unlikely there would be many good Items left by now. Or perhaps they might be selling Defective products in the Excavated Items corner.</p>
<p>I thought there wouldn't be anything useful, but out of Intrigue, I looked around and indeed, as I first thought, there were no good Items. Most of the Figures had one of their Arms or Legs missing, or were heavily scratched, making many of them without commodity Value.</p>
<p>Oh, there's the Cloaked Baldy that Minamoto Risa likes. Of course, as expected of an excavated Item, the Cloak part was half torn off, but the rest of its Limbs and Head were intact, so it could be considered a good quality Item for an excavated piece.</p>
<p>Moreover, the torn Cloak gave the impression of being ripped during Actual combat, making it look like the Munchkin Character, Cloaked Baldy, was struggling, which seemed quite good.</p>
<p>Since Minamoto Risa would likely like it, I put it in my basket. I wondered if there was anything else, but there were no other special Items. There were only Figures with missing Limbs or crushed merchandise boxes whose contents were unknown if they were intact.</p>
<p>"Huh?" As I was looking through the excavated Items, an Item Info Window suddenly popped up, startling me and making me involuntarily exclaim. Items with options usually display an Info Window, but for a Figure or merchandise?</p>
<p>I pushed aside the pile of excavated Items to confirm the identity of the Item that had triggered the Info Window. That Item was a single iron Glove, mechanically designed, except for a transparent, glassy gem like quartz embedded in the Knuckle part of the fist.</p>
<p>◆Jeonsugap Eul-005 Prototype "Narrative Equipment/Glove/Weapon": A prototype energy weapon created by Zhanlang, a now-defunct North China weapons development company. During the Third World War, while conducting actual combat experiments with the prototype and en route to China, its transport plane crashed within Japan's air defense network, and it was lost.</p>
<p>Consumes Electricity to fire a powerful Energy Blaster. Energy consumption: 45</p>
<p>The name is quite complex, isn't it? Jeonsugap Eul-005 Prototype.... Is it because it's a prototype? For a Battle Weapon left behind during the Jeonjaeng to end up in an Otaku merchandise Shop under the name 'excavated Item' is quite plausible. After all, there are many model Guns too.</p>
<p>Moreover, since this Jeonsugap was a mechanical Glove, not a firearm, the People who searched the Ruins of post-Jeonjaeng Akihabara probably thought it was merchandise.</p>
<p>By the way, China was also developing Energy Weapons. Seeing that it was defunct, not acquired, it seems North China was defeated in the Third World War and it was buried. I should tell Eve to try and get Information about this company called Zhanlang.</p>
<p>I first put the Jeonsugap in my basket, then contacted Eve and explained The Situation.</p>
<p>EVE: "So you've discovered an Energy Weapon prototype made by a defunct North China company called Zhanlang?"</p>
<p>Ryuta: "Yes. It seems to have been lost during Actual combat testing in a Jeonjaeng with Japan."</p>
<p>EVE: "Then we should investigate that company called Zhanlang, shouldn't we? If any useful data comes our way, we'll take it. Even if not, it would be good to dispose of it so other companies can't get it."</p>
<p>As expected, it was Eve. She understood what needed to be done without me having to give direct Commands.</p>
<p>Ryuta: "Yes. If someone already has Zhanlang's data, there's nothing we can do, but if not, it would be good for us to get it."</p>
<p>EVE: "Lara is incredibly intrigued. I'll assign Lara and some Agents to be dispatched."</p>
<p>Indeed, since Lara Sinclair's purpose was to make the Energy Weapon originating from her Father into a US military Drill Weapon, she would clearly be very intrigued if a new Energy Weapon appeared. The more the Weapon was developed, the higher the Probability of it becoming a US military Drill Weapon.</p>
<p>Ryuta: "I'll bring this Glove to the Tokyo Branch later."</p>
<p>EVE: "Yes~! I'm incredibly excited~!😘😘😘😘"</p>
<p>Ryuta: "And...."</p>
<p>After adding one more Request, I ended the chat with Eve. As I put the merchandise I had checked while walking around earlier into my basket (excluding a few in case my Luggage became too much), I returned to where Minamoto Risa was likely to be, and she was still sparkling-eyed, happily worrying.</p>
<p>"Haven't you finished choosing yet?"</p>
<p>"Have you already chosen everything?"</p>
<p>We asked Questions that seemed to indicate we didn't understand each other. Indeed, if you go to a department store with a Woman, they walk around a lot choosing Clothes, so thinking of it that way, it made sense.</p>
<p>The only difference was that Minamoto Risa was worrying over Otaku merchandise, not Luxury Brand items from a department store.</p>
<p>"You've chosen quite a lot too. Can I take a look?"</p>
<p>"First, choose all of yours, then we'll see. If you look at these and choose, it seems it will take more Time."</p>
<p>"...Understood." At my Words, Minamoto Risa, as if resolved, called the Clerk. The Clerk, who had been glancing at Minamoto Risa for a while, quickly rushed over. It seemed they had been paying attention because Minamoto Risa, a rare Beauty, was in the Store. Our Lisa is indeed pretty.</p>
<p>"Please give me everything in the ○-Punch Man section, excluding duplicates."</p>

An Ordinary Person like me had excluded a few things I liked in case my Luggage became too much, but it seemed the real deal thought, 'If the Luggage is going to be a lot anyway, just buy everything.'
