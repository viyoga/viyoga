<p>"She's quite bold."</p>
<p>Han Byeong-jae, the eldest of Shinkang Group, opened his mouth as if muttering while sipping a glass of cognac. The second, Han Woo-min, slammed the table nervously while gulping down vodka.</p>
<p>"Isn't she just Seong-jae's daughter with no real backing? We can just take it away...."</p>
<p>It was Han Woo-min, the most hot-blooded of the brothers, who said it, but everyone gathered here felt similarly. They thought they could just take away a company built by a mere child through luck.</p>
<p>"There's no way, hyung-nim. We have no legal authority to do so, and even if we try to use force, Yu-gyeong has Vanguard, so that's impossible too. In fact, the only thing we can do is coax her into spitting out that company called TG Tech, but Yu-gyeong doesn't own all the shares, and Jeil Electronics is also involved, so it's impossible right now."</p>
<p>Han Gyeong-jae, who was quick to grasp the situation and was sharp, spoke. Internally, the three were fighting to become the next Chairman of Shinkang Group, but when an external enemy appeared, they joined forces. Seeing them join forces to acquire Han Yu-gyeong's TG Tech, it seemed that these three did not consider Han Yu-gyeong an internal member of Shinkang Group.</p>
<p>"Right, she had Vanguard too. Amazing, unlike Seong-jae's daughter. If she were a son, it would have been quite a problem."</p>
<p>Because Shinkang Group had a rule of not entrusting important tasks to women, the three brothers gathered here did not consider Han Yu-gyeong a competitor. That's why they were so eager to take away Han Yu-gyeong's TG Tech and increase their own power. Han Woo-min, who was fully intending to take it by force, gritted his teeth upon hearing that Han Yu-gyeong was the owner of Vanguard.</p>
<p>"Rather, Jeil Electronics isn't much of a problem. We can negotiate with someone other than Cheon Ha-rin. The problem is that even if we persuade or work on Yu-gyeong, we still don't have enough shares in TG Tech."</p>
<p>Han Byeong-jae nodded at Han Gyeong-jae's words and spoke. It was too early now. Stimulating Han Yu-gyeong recklessly today was a mistake. They should have stayed still and struck like a storm when the conditions were right.... Han Byeong-jae regretted his mistake.</p>
<p>"How about targeting Jin Yong-tae, the co-representative?"</p>
<p>"He's in Japan, and his whereabouts are unknown, so it's difficult to contact him."</p>
<p>Han Byeong-jae shook his head at Han Gyeong-jae's suggestion. Judging by the fact that he knew about Jin Yong-tae, he seemed to have already investigated him. Han Gyeong-jae, who had made the suggestion, felt a slight chill at Han Byeong-jae's words. He was afraid of Han Byeong-jae's ability to have already reviewed and investigated his suggestion.</p>
<p>"Shall we release the kids to find him?"</p>
<p>"Don't do it, it'll provoke Japanese companies. They're nothing special except for Taira and Minamoto.... But it's always good to be careful."</p>
<p>Han Byeong-jae shook his head at Han Woo-min's words. He didn't think it was necessary to escalate a family feud into an international issue.</p>
<p>"By the way, why didn't Father say anything today?"</p>
<p>Han Gyeong-jae looked at his two older brothers and asked. It was strange that Han Mun-gang, who usually had a fiery temper, didn't say anything even after seeing his granddaughter Han Yu-gyeong and his children raising their voices at each other.</p>
<p>"Well, he must think it's okay no matter what happens."</p>
<p>"What do you mean, hyung-nim?"</p>
<p>Han Woo-min asked as if curious at Han Byeong-jae's words.</p>
<p>"It means that it's good if we oppress her and TG Tech becomes our property, and it's also good if Yu-gyeong takes charge and grows it further. He was quiet because he thought it was okay no matter what the outcome was today. No, he probably thought the latter was even better."</p>
<p>Because they were sons, the three brothers knew their father's abilities well. It was Han Mun-gang who turned a small, insignificant company into one of the top 10 conglomerates in Korea, and there was always a reason why Han Mun-gang was quiet.</p>
<p>"He already knew about the parts we thought were problems."</p>
<p>"Right, Father probably thought we should push Yu-gyeong and increase her shares in TG Tech."</p>
<p>The other two brothers nodded at Han Byeong-jae's words.</p>
<p>"Then the method is...."</p>
<p>"There's a way to talk to Seong-jae."</p>
<p>They would call Han Seong-jae, Han Yu-gyeong's father, who would be slightly sulky for missing the brothers' meeting again today, and ask him to work on Han Yu-gyeong. They thought that as Han Yu-gyeong's father, he would listen to them better than his uncles.</p>
<p>###</p>
<p>The black hem of the dress reflected the gloomy blue moonlight and fluttered. The girl in the Gothic Lolita dress wore a black mask that covered below her eyes, concealing her identity as she looked at the city's night view.</p>
<p>A voice leaked from the mask of the Goth Loli masked girl standing on the rooftop of a building.</p>
<p>"Geez! I was so busy with my schedule that I couldn't even change my clothes. This skirt is uncomfortable...."</p>
<p>The Goth Loli masked girl grumbled to herself and tied a rope to the railing. Then, she began to climb down the railing. Since the target was the penthouse on the top floor, the girl only had to go down a little. Despite grumbling that the skirt was uncomfortable, the Goth Loli masked girl skillfully climbed down the rope like a special forces soldier.</p>
<p>Before she knew it, the Goth Loli masked girl arrived at the penthouse window and placed her palm on the glass, causing spiderweb-like cracks to appear. The glass, making small cracking noises, crumbled like sand.</p>
<p>The Goth Loli masked girl went straight through the window that had disappeared like sand. As befitting a penthouse, the house was spacious. It seemed like no one was there, but her sharp senses felt something. The Goth Loli masked girl strode around the house as if it were her own, and saw a closet in the study.</p>
<p>"There must be a secret door here...."</p>
<p>After searching for a while, the Goth Loli masked girl muttered, "Whatever?" and hit the bookshelf as it was.</p>
<p>Kwaaang!</p>
<p>With a tremendous roar, a hole the size of a person was created, revealing a secret space behind the bookshelf. It was a place with a strong smell of blood. Women's arms and legs were cut off, and taxidermied faces were on display. It was like a serial killer's murder site.</p>
<p>"Hmm...! There's a reason why he asked to be killed so badly."</p>
<p>The Goth Loli masked girl frowned slightly as she looked at the surrounding scenery. Perhaps it was thanks to the girl's voice, but the man who was about to cut off someone's head regained his senses and stood up, looking at the Goth Loli masked girl.</p>
<p>This man was the notorious Kiruoni (Cutting Ogre), who had recently carried out a series of murders in the Tokyo area. He left almost no evidence and disposed of the limbs, excluding the head, in places like parks, which led to rumors that he might be a psychic.</p>
<p>There were already more than ten victims, and because of that, it was difficult to find people on the streets of Tokyo at night recently.</p>
<p>"How did you get here?"</p>
<p>"It's not a name I like, but.... Do you know someone called Ogre?"</p>
<p>Ogre. An assassin who appeared two years ago and was presumed to have tremendous power. He never used firearms and crushed parts of his opponent with tremendous force, which is why he was nicknamed Ogre.</p>
<p>He was famous as an assassin with his own standards in the police and assassination industry. Ogre had a standard of not killing innocent people. He was different from ordinary assassins who would kill anyone for money.</p>
<p>So, those who knew Ogre sometimes described him as an anti-hero or a dark hero.</p>
<p>"Ogre...? Then are you?"</p>
<p>The serial killer Kiruoni looked at the Goth Loli girl as if he couldn't believe it and asked again. Naturally, the name Ogre conjured up an image of a huge man, but the woman claiming to be Ogre was wearing a delicate-looking Gothic Lolita dress, so it was natural to be puzzled.</p>
<p>"Geez, I told you I don't like that nickname."</p>
<p>"I guess the rumors about Ogre were false. To give such an undeserved nickname to such a young girl."</p>
<p>Kiruoni laughed at Ogre's appearance and aimed the saw blade he was holding to cut the lying woman.</p>
<p>"I told you I don't like that nickname!"</p>
<p>Ogre instantly stood in front of Kiruoni as if teleporting and swung her fist. Kiruoni also swung his saw blade without backing down.</p>
<p>Kakakaka!</p>
<p>A sound that was hard to believe came from the collision of the saw blade and the fist, and sparks like those from a steel mill began to rise and spread around.</p>
<p>"You're pretty good? Are you really a psychic as the rumors say?"</p>
<p>Ogre asked, seeing Kiruoni's considerable physical abilities and his ability to handle the saw blade freely. Kiruoni didn't answer and kicked Ogre's abdomen as it was.</p>
<p>Kwaang!</p>
<p>Ogre, who flew away in an instant, crashed into the wall of the secret space, denting it. But Ogre, who didn't seem to be shocked at all, came flying back again, shook off the flying saw blade, and placed five fingers on Kiruoni's chest.</p>
<p>Ssuuk!</p>
<p>Ogre's fingers easily dug in as if putting fingers in mud.</p>
<p>"Kuaaaa!"</p>
<p>Kiruoni, in sudden pain, thrashed around wildly, swinging his saw blade, but Ogre grabbed the wrist holding the saw blade and broke the bone as it was. With a chilling sound of kwajik, the broken humerus pierced through the skin.</p>
<p>Even in the meantime, Ogre's fingers dug into Kiruoni's chest, and already two knuckles of her fingers had entered. Ogre began to feel something throbbing at the tip of her fingers.</p>
<p>"Is this the heart?"</p>
<p>"Plea, please... Sa, save...!"</p>
<p>"You didn't save me even when I begged you to save me. I got an advance payment, so I have to kill you. I'm sorry, but die~☆"</p>
<p>Ogre didn't even listen to Kiruoni's words asking to be saved and crushed his heart to death. Then, she pulled out her hand and wiped the blood-stained hand on Kiruoni's hem.</p>
<p>"Are you alive over there?"</p>
<p>"...Yes."</p>
<p>The woman who was lucky enough to survive thanks to Ogre's appearance trembled and answered Ogre's question.</p>
<p>"I saved you, so please don't talk about my appearance if possible."</p>
<p>"I understand. Th, thank you for saving me... Heueuk, heuk...! Heuk."</p>
<p>The surviving woman began to cry. It was impossible to know whether she was crying because she was alive, or because the situation just now was scary, or because Ogre, who she was talking to now, was scary.</p>
<p>"It's quite noisy, so the police will be here soon. Well, I'll be going~! Bye~☆"</p>
<p>Ogre greeted the surviving woman brightly and came out of the secret space. She went to the window she came in through and climbed up to the roof on the rope. Then, she sent a text message to someone saying that the mission was complete. Seeing the money coming in right away, she smiled refreshingly and looked at the moon floating high in the sky.</p>
<p>"I did a good job."</p>
<p>Ogre, who was muttering for a moment, discovered Kiruoni's blood on her Gothic Lolita dress reflected in the moonlight.</p>
<p>"Ah~! I have to go to the laundromat again. I hope I don't meet Ryuta-jji this time either?"</p>
<p>Ogre, who was grumbling for a moment, quietly disappeared, melting into the darkness.</p>
