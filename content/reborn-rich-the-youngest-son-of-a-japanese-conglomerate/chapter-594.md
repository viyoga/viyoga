<p>Due to this incident, apart from South China, the place most in chaos due to the New Nanjing city virus outbreak was none other than Japan. The prestigious St. Francis Girls' Academy, where daughters of renowned families attend, was trapped in New Nanjing, and Uenozono Karen, Japan's pride as an idol, was among them.</p>
<p>The parents immediately convened a parents' meeting. With only a few months left until graduation, they had gone on a graduation trip, and a virus had broken out, so the parents were extremely worried. It wasn't even within Japan but in a foreign land across the sea, making it impossible to rescue them directly, which made the situation even more frustrating.</p>
<p>Since all those parents were members of Japan's prestigious families, the entire Japanese society was closely watching the situation in New Nanjing. Even ordinary people were keeping an eye on the situation because of Uenozono Karen, so it was no exaggeration to say that the entire nation of Japan was watching the news.</p>
<p>,If this goes wrong, consider our dealings with the Tsujio family over...!</p>
<p>As the Tsujio family, who had strongly advocated for South China as the graduation trip destination, received unfavorable glances from everyone, the door to the parents' meeting room suddenly slid open with a *deureureuk* sound.</p>
<p>It was the arrival of Taira and Minamoto, Japan's two major powers. From the Taira clan came Taira Kokoro, the eldest daughter of Taira Danjo, and from the Minamoto clan came Minamoto Youken, the third son of Minamoto Ushin. Both were influential figures. Taira Kokoro, an heir of the Taira lineage, and Minamoto Youken, who oversaw the Minamoto Group's business strategy department thanks to his exceptional skills, were both impressive personalities.</p>
<p>"...."</p>
<p>Although they were all parents who had left their children in a deadly situation, the parents at the parents' meeting could only watch the two who had just appeared. That was how high the status of Taira and Minamoto was.</p>
<p>,My father said he would explode if he saw you all fidgeting, so he sent me.</p>
<p>,Haha, it's similar for me. He said he felt like throwing something at the person who recommended the trip to South China in particular.</p>
<p>Taira Danjo and Minamoto Ushin, who were unmatched in their fiery tempers in Japan, would have caused a huge uproar if they had come themselves, as their children said.</p>
<p>So, they sent their children, Taira Kokoro and Minamoto Youken, but their atmospheres were quite different. Taira Kokoro had a mixed expression of anger and worry because her younger sister, Taira Kanna, as well as Uenozono Karen, who belonged to a company under Taira Entertainment, albeit only within Japan, were involved. On the other hand, Minamoto Youken just looked annoyed.</p>
<p>Minamoto Youken had no affection for Minamoto Risa, so it felt like he was just taking on and handling troublesome work outside of his own duties.</p>
<p>,Still, the good news is that according to the latest WHO report, it's a kind of computer virus, so the students of St. Francis Girls' Academy gathered here won't turn into ugly zombies.</p>
<p>They couldn't rest assured that their children wouldn't be infected by the New Nanjing zombie virus. Even if half of the over twenty million people in New Nanjing had cyber implants, the number of zombies was still a whopping ten million. Even if they weren't infected by the virus, they were still worried about being torn to death by cyber zombies infected with the virus.</p>
<p>,Just wait a little. Taira and Minamoto are working together on this matter and negotiating with the South Chinese government, so there will be good results.</p>
<p>The parents' expressions brightened a little at Taira Kokoro's words. A collaboration between Taira and Minamoto...! It was almost unheard of, but it seemed they were cooperating when it came to their children's affairs.</p>
<p>,Even if the negotiations don't go well, we'll hire a private military company (PMC) separately to rescue the students, so don't worry.</p>
<p>Minamoto Youken reassured the parents with a friendly smile. The parents knew Minamoto Youken's nickname: The Smiling Prince of Minamoto. Because Minamoto Youken always smiled no matter what happened, they couldn't feel relieved even by his smile. Moreover, since communication was cut off, they couldn't know the current situation in New Nanjing at all, so the parents were even more uneasy.</p>
<p>###</p>
<p>After a sticky morning sex with Otagi Yuka, Otagi Yuka smiled seductively, kissed me on the cheek, and unfolded her mist flower to return to the special class dormitory. She's so sly. Since everyone had agreed to meet in the dining hall in the morning, I washed up, left the room, and headed to the next room.</p>
<p>*Cheolkeok.*</p>
<p>The door opened, and the black reinforced plastic bags that were still left were visible in the next room. The cyber suits had all been given to our class students, but quite a lot of firearms and energy packs were still left. It was fortunate that Eve had foreseen this situation and packed a lot of weapons.</p>
<p>I grabbed a few bags and headed to the dining hall. I didn't need to carry everything myself. I could just tell them to come and get them later.</p>
<p>,Good morning, sensei?</p>
<p>,Yeah, I was so tired that I fell asleep as soon as I lay down.</p>
<p>I sat down at a table while receiving greetings from the students. As if showing the horrors of yesterday, guests were lying dead, and some of the tables were covered with food and bloodstains. We sat at a relatively clean table, and soon some students and teachers brought breakfast.</p>
<p>,The students and teachers who didn't participate in the battle got together and tried to make a meal. The electricity hasn't been cut off, so the ingredients in the refrigerator are all fine.</p>
<p>Kijimoto Jurie said as she brought food to us. It seemed that the non-combatants who had some energy left had prepared the meal. Breakfast was scrambled eggs, morning bread, grilled bacon, and onion soup, and knowing that I had a large appetite, they brought me a lot. It looked like almost four servings....</p>
<p>Still, I had consumed a lot of calories yesterday, so I had to eat this much. Everyone was hungry from yesterday's hardships, so even though it was breakfast right after waking up, only the clattering sound of plates and utensils could be heard, and no other sounds were heard.</p>
<p>After the quiet mealtime ended, the homeroom teachers and class presidents and vice-presidents from each class gathered. Except for the homeroom teacher of Class C, the only victim of St. Francis Girls' Academy in this incident, the students and teachers who could be called the leaders (?) of all classes gathered.</p>
<p>,What should we do from now on?</p>
<p>Communication was still cut off, and we were trapped in a hotel in the city center, so we had to decide on a direction somehow.</p>
<p>There was enough food in the hotel to provide for the numerous guests, so we could survive for a week just by holding out, but that wasn't a good choice.</p>
<p>Cyber robot zombies were roaming around, destroying buildings in New Nanjing, and the hotel was located in the city center with definitely over millions of cyber zombies, so staying isolated wasn't good. Moreover, even apart from the youngest son of Megacorp's event, there was a 30 percent chance that the South Chinese government would bomb New Nanjing under the pretext of purifying the virus, so if we just held out, we might not be rescued and could be swept away by the explosion and disappear.</p>
<p>,We just need to get out of New Nanjing. If only people with cyber implants have turned into zombies, shouldn't we not have to worry about being chased by the infected if we get out of New Nanjing?</p>
<p>I proposed getting out of New Nanjing. Some refused my proposal out of fear of running through the zombies again, while others nodded as if agreeing.</p>
<p>Those who agreed were all the influential figures of the academy students, such as Taira Kanna, Minamoto Risa, and Nakanoin Rina, and the teachers were the grade head, Matsunaga Yuzuki, Shimokawabe Airen, and Kijimoto Jurie. So, the minority opposing opinions didn't gain traction. Only the homeroom teacher of Class B, a physics teacher, and about two students opposed, but the argument to hold out in the hotel was rejected due to the majority vote.</p>
<p>,There are enough guns to distribute to each class, so don't worry too much.</p>
<p>I said, putting the boxes on the table. The opposition was worried because they didn't have a means to protect themselves, so I showed them as if to reassure them.</p>
<p>,Those who prepared breakfast this morning should pack lunch boxes to eat for lunch and dinner, and some should search the hotel to see if there's anything useful. Let's leave in an hour... thirty minutes.</p>
<p>Taira Kanna said as if organizing and got up from her seat. Food is definitely important. After the meeting, as directed by Taira Kanna, some went to the kitchen to pack lunch boxes, and some equipped firearms and searched each room of the hotel.</p>
<p>After dealing with a few cyber zombies who were trapped in the hotel rooms, what we found were a few tourists hiding in fear and two hotel employees who didn't have cyber implants.</p>
<p>They were all South Chinese, so a female student who knew Chinese spoke to them, but they looked at us strangely when we said we were leaving the hotel even though there were no zombies in the hotel. They seemed to have an expression that we would be rescued if we held out here. They didn't try to stop us or go with us, perhaps because they could take over all the food in the hotel if we left.</p>
<p>I found a satellite phone, but it didn't work properly, as if they had blocked the communication. Of course, I knew in advance that the satellite phone wouldn't work, but I tried calling just in case, and I was disappointed that no one answered.</p>
<p>After the promised 1 hour and 30 minutes, we gathered again in the parking lot. It was to decide on an escape route before leaving.</p>
<p>,How about going towards the zoo we went to once? It's on the outskirts of the city, so if we keep going, we might arrive at another city?</p>
<p>I didn't know the geography accurately because I didn't have a navigation system, but I had a map that I brought just in case, so we started discussing while looking at the map. We even called a South Chinese hotel employee who knew the geography of New Nanjing and asked him, and after considering various factors, we decided to go back to the zoo as suggested earlier. It was close to other cities, and we had driven the zombies from the zoo to here while coming to the hotel, so it was calculated that the road to the zoo would be deserted without zombies.</p>
<p>,We don't know the way, so we have no choice but to go that way.</p>
<p>Class B, whose bus was destroyed, decided to take the safari bus they had come on, and we each took our own buses and set off. The destination was the zoo again.</p>
