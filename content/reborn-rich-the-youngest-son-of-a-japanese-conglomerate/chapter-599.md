<p>Of course, while some enterprises raised their brand value, others naturally saw theirs fall. While TG Tech was making a name for itself in the world, Phoenix Technology Company and its parent company, SCSMC (South China Semiconductor Manufacturing Corporation), faced a massive Stock market crash and an onslaught of public opinion.</p>
<p>Following the WHO's statement that the recent cyber zombie virus incident was due to open-source cyber implants, the Stocks of numerous cyber implant manufacturers and related companies plummeted. However, Phoenix Technology Company and SCSMC were hit the hardest, as statistics showed that 85 percent of New Nanjing City's cyber implant Users employed Phoenix Technology Company's models.</p>
<p>Just when it seemed to be over, the Red Revolution Front (红色革命阵线), an extremist Terrorist organization in North China, issued a statement claiming responsibility for spreading the virus.</p>
<p>That alone wouldn't have been a Problem, but the statement declared that the virus, created by exploiting vulnerabilities in Phoenix Technology Company's operating system, had crushed the Arrogance of the despicable South Chinese. This effectively revealed that the entire incident was due to Phoenix Technology Company's technical flaws.</p>
<p>As a result, Phoenix Technology Company was destined to be embroiled in numerous lawsuits: civil lawsuits for causing half of New Nanjing City's residents to die by zombies and turning the other half into zombies, and criminal lawsuits for failing to properly Guarantee the Safety of its consumers.</p>
<p>SCSMC and Phoenix Technology Company complained that while they had made mistakes, the ultimate Problem lay with the Red Revolution Front, which had spread the virus.</p>
<p>However, as other cyber implant companies, which had been affected by the incident without significant issues, pointed out Phoenix Technology Company's lax security, Phoenix Technology Company and SCSMC were forced to pay astronomical compensation.</p>
<p>Ultimately, SCSMC chose to abandon Phoenix Technology Company to save itself. By expelling the SCSMC Chairman's Eldest son, who held the largest Share in Phoenix Technology Company, SCSMC asserted that their Relationship with Phoenix Technology Company was merely one of Cooperation, not Servitude, and that SCSMC bore no Responsibility for the incident.</p>
<p>- SCSMC Chairman Wang Chang (王常): "SCSMC bears no responsibility for this incident. The only person from our family who held shares in Phoenix Technology Company was a son who had been expelled from our family. However, we are willing to provide humanitarian aid to the affected citizens." "It's flowing exactly as expected, I see."</p>
<p>I muttered, lying down and reading the article on my phone. Based on my previous Playthroughs, SCSMC would eventually lose Phoenix Technology Company, and its company Value and influence would significantly diminish, but it would ultimately overcome this incident and survive.</p>
<p>Logically, it made no sense for a company to remain unscathed after an incident that harmed twenty million people, but this World was one where it was difficult to survive without a "megacorp sensitivity." Later, news would break that SCSMC had paid enormous Bribes to high-ranking South Chinese officials, including National Assembly members, the President, and judges, causing another Noisy uproar, but that was a Matter for the future.</p>
<p>I only needed to secure my own profits here. Phoenix Technology Company, which had to pay astronomical compensation, would eventually be torn apart and enter liquidation. Since several of its patents were useful, I planned to use a portion of the immense profits I had gained from shorting cyber implant companies and related enterprises to purchase them.</p>
<p>It had now been two days since we were quarantined in the Auditorium. The Party from St. Francisco Girls' Academy had given their statements about the events of the past two days and undergone several examinations.</p>
<p>As it became known that this incident was caused by a computer virus that infected cyber implant operating systems, not humans, the fact that our quarantine was practically meaningless was revealed.</p>
<p>Dissatisfaction about when we would be released was slowly brewing, but since there were rumors that the final examination results would be out in an hour and we would be sent Home today, everyone reacted by saying they would wait a little longer. So, I was comfortably lying on a makeshift bed, reading the article.</p>
<p>Having left New Nanjing City, where communication was jammed, and with communication now possible, I received several Contact messages over the past two days.</p>
<p>While others received Contact from all sorts of people, I only received messages from a select few who knew I had gone to New Nanjing City as Yamashiro Ryuta, so I didn't get many calls.</p>
<p>Han Yu-gyeong, Mimata Chieri, Yukiko, a few from Seryu Military Company, Cheon Ha-rin, and Nagumo Sian, I suppose? Thinking about it, for a Secret Mission, quite a lot of people know. Still, given how many people know my Secret and it hasn't leaked yet, they must all be trustworthy.</p>
<p>Knock knock.</p>
<p>"Please gather, as the examination results are out."</p>
<p>A Knocking Sound came from outside the Door, along with a request to gather. I replied, "Understood," and got up from the bed. Since the Special Class bus that had carried our Luggage was intact, I kept the South Chinese Military Uniform as a souvenir and put on the Clothes I had brought. So, I was now dressed in comfortable-looking Shorts and a T-shirt.</p>
<p>Minamoto Risa, claiming it was for a souvenir, again snapped countless Photos of me in my South Chinese Military Uniform and my comfortable Appearance, and since numerous Students were watching, I had to pose for Photos like a signboard at a scenic photo spot.</p>
<p>"Ah, Ryuta-jji is out too?"</p>
<p>"Yeah. I came out because they told us to gather."</p>
<p>When I came out, Seizawa Shii was the first to greet me. While we were happy to have escaped safely, the South Chinese side was in a rather somber Atmosphere due to the numerous casualties, so even the Soldiers managing the shelter had grim Expressions.</p>
<p>It had been two days since we arrived here, and only two people had escaped New Nanjing City since then. Of course, there might have been others who escaped alive through routes other than the Shanghai direction, but compared to New Nanjing City's large population, it was less than a speck of dust.</p>
<p>After standing in the Auditorium for a while, military doctors in medical uniforms and Soldiers came carrying Paper. It seemed the examination results were written on that Paper.</p>
<p>"First, no infectious diseases have been detected in anyone. Those who came from Japan, please pack your Luggage immediately. An Airplane has been arranged at Shanghai Airport."</p>
<p>Everyone murmured at the sudden instruction to pack, but soon, delighted by the news that they could return Home, they began packing their Luggage.</p>
<p>As a teacher, I went to the soldiers to ask for more details. They said we would go to Shanghai Hongqiao International Airport, one of the two airports in Shanghai and the one closest to our quarantine facility, take a waiting airplane, and fly to Fujikawa Airport, the closest airport to Miyashi.</p>
<p>After hearing the explanation, I went to the room I was using alone and packed my Luggage. When I came out after quickly packing, it seemed the Students had also packed their Luggage at an incredible speed. That meant they wanted to return Home just as quickly.</p>
<p>"Everyone finished packing?"</p>
<p>"Yes~!"</p>
<p>Everyone answered Matsugana Yuzuki, the third-year Class Teacher, with spirited voices. At the announcement that Preparation was complete, we boarded the waiting vehicle. We rode in the back of a military transport truck, not the bus we had arrived in, and headed towards the Airport.</p>
<p>After driving for about 40 minutes, the Airport came into view. We were able to board the waiting Airplane directly, without any special procedures.</p>
<p>Naturally, there was no one else to board besides those affiliated with St. Francisco Girls' Academy, so the moment we got on, it departed immediately. The seamless, natural flow of everything indicated that both the South Chinese and Japanese sides had prepared quite well.</p>
<p>After two hours of receiving lavish hospitality from the stewardess, we arrived at the Airport to find the Students' Parents and reporters waiting.</p>
<p>Several familiar faces were visible, including Headmaster Daigo Terue and Taira Kokoro, who seemed to have come as Taira Kanna's guardian. Taira Danjo and Minamoto Ushin did not appear to be at the Airport. They were probably waiting for their Daughters at Home.</p>
<p>The hall was quite Noisy with Parents calling out to and embracing their Daughters, and Students shedding Tears. Reporters were Filming, seemingly intent on capturing every single moment of this reunion.</p>
<p>However, Otagi Yuka, who had broken ties with her Family, and Saionji Diana, who had been practically abandoned by her Family, seemed a bit awkward in the current Situation because their Parents hadn't come. I squeezed both their Hands, as if to say it was alright.</p>
<p>After a brief Commotion, and with the reporters having left the Airport, Daigo Terue took the Microphone.</p>
<p>"I apologize that such a Situation arose during an academy event. However, it is Fortunate that all the Students have returned safely."</p>
<p>In truth, the graduation Travel destination was decided by the parent committee, so it wasn't St. Francisco Girls' Academy's Mistake. However, since the graduation Travel event itself was held under the name of St. Francisco Girls' Academy, Daigo Terue, the Headmaster of St. Francisco Girls' Academy, offered an apology.</p>
<p>"For the Students who must have been quite shocked by this incident, I will grant a week of special Vacation. We will ensure there is no disadvantage to their attendance days, so please all recover well and return."</p>
<p>If the Students rested, I, as their Class Teacher, would naturally also get a week of Rest. This is good, isn't it? Now, we had to return to Miyashi, but there were no separate vehicles prepared by the academy for the Students, so everyone decided to return in the cars their Parents had arrived in.</p>
<p>"Thank you, Teacher-nim...!"</p>
<p>"You took good care of our Daughter, we are truly grateful!"</p>
<p>Before leaving the Airport, the Parents of the Special Class Students each thanked me for taking good care of their Daughters, offering Handshakes and expressing their gratitude. It seemed the Students had told their Parents about my contributions during the two days of quarantine.</p>
<p>Of course, Minamoto Youken, Minamoto Ushin's third son, who apparently didn't like Minamoto Risa at all, merely offered a soulless "Thank you for helping a Minamoto person." Anyone could tell there was no gratitude in his Emotion. I had previously heard Minamoto Risa say to avoid everyone from her Family, and seeing this, it was truly understandable why Minamoto Risa would dislike him.</p>
<p>"Yamashiro Sensei, thank you. I will definitely repay this Favor."</p>
<p>"It's naturally what I should do as a Class Teacher."</p>
<p>Finally, Daigo Terue greeted me. After finishing all the greetings with the Parents, we went to the Airport Parking Lot. Since a vehicle had been prepared to transport the Teachers, I took the Special Class Students whose Parents hadn't come and boarded the vehicle prepared by St. Francisco Girls' Academy. For reference, the vehicle prepared by St. Francisco Girls' Academy was a mini bus.</p>
<p>"Alright, let's go Home now."</p>
<p>"Yeah, Sensei-ssi."</p>
<p>"Thank you for taking care of us, Teacher-nim."</p>
<p>"It's nothing.... All the other Teachers also worked hard."</p>
<p>"Yamashiro Teacher-nim also worked hard."</p>
<p>We exchanged Words of appreciation for each other's hard Work and rode the mini bus towards Miyashi.</p>
