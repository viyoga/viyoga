<p>,"Did you sleep well, you drunks?"</p>
<p>After spending the night with Ha Ju-yoon until she begged me to stop, and sleeping for about two hours, I returned to the hotel where the St. Francis students were staying. As I was eating breakfast, the students came down one by one.</p>
<p>Since there were troublemakers who drank and acted crazy at the chicken place yesterday, I asked with a smile. Except for Seizawa Shii, who just kept laughing, everyone showed off a black history-level of drunken behavior, so they all quietly sat down without saying a word.</p>
<p>Sakagami Rena, Otagi Yuka, and Saionji Diana, who helped me send the drunk guys to their hotel rooms yesterday, chuckled at my greeting, but the other students who didn't know the whole story tilted their heads, wondering what was going on. Alik sat next to me and asked with an innocent expression.</p>
<p>,"Ryuta drank?"</p>
<p>,"No, I don't drink much because drinking makes me lose muscle."</p>
<p>,"Yeah, Alik likes vodka. It makes me warm...."</p>
<p>As expected of a Russian girl, Alik liked vodka. But isn't she still a minor? She shouldn't be drinking, especially not vodka....</p>
<p>,"Alik. Minors shouldn't drink. Got it?"</p>
<p>,"Yeah, Alik only drank when I was cold, before bad people caught me. Egawa Mama told me not to drink vodka either, so I didn't."</p>
<p>Listening to the story, it seemed that she drank vodka to overcome the cold when she was wandering around Manchuria before being caught by the slave hunters. Seeing that Alik's adoptive mother, Egawa Shiho, also banned Alik from drinking vodka, she must know this story.</p>
<p>,"Did Alik do well?"</p>
<p>,"Yeah, I did well."</p>
<p>,"Then Ryuta, pat Alik's head."</p>
<p>I don't know what she did well, but since she said she did well, Alik brings her crown to me as if asking me to pat her. I ruffled Alik's hair and opened my mouth to the special class students sitting around me.</p>
<p>,"Everyone knows today's morning schedule, right?"</p>
<p>,"Yes."</p>
<p>Since we had quite a long free sightseeing time yesterday, I thought the students would be tired, so today's morning schedule was a bit static, with a visit to the museum. So, I boldly decided to leave the museum tour to Kijimoto Jurie and decided to sleep on the bus... but I couldn't resist the students' urging and ended up going to the museum with them.</p>
<p>Now that it's come to this, should I take a look around the museum to see if there are any useful items...? I looked around, but they were all historically important items, and there weren't any items with special abilities attached. Nothing is going according to plan today.</p>
<p>After spending a leisurely morning at the museum, I got on the bus for the afternoon schedule. The afternoon schedule was a strange one for a graduation trip, which was a tour of Cheil Electronics.</p>
<p>Of course, it would be a strange schedule for a regular school, but our school is St. Francis Girls' Academy, where daughters of prestigious families attend. During graduation trips, they include these kinds of tours with the aim of broadening their horizons by looking at the industries of other countries.</p>
<p>Cheil Group is currently the best conglomerate in Korea. I wonder how they negotiated so well. By the way, if it's Cheil Electronics, that woman will be there.... Surely I won't meet her on a high school tour like this.</p>
<p>,"First, we'll have lunch at the cafeteria when we arrive, so line up and follow me."</p>
<p>,"I have to compare it to the Hyper Enter cafeteria I ate at yesterday~!"</p>
<p>,"It was delicious yesterday...."</p>
<p>As it was time for lunch, the tour started with a meal at the cafeteria. When the bus arrived and we got off, we saw a very large parking lot and several buildings.</p>
<p>,"Welcome, students of St. Francis Girls' Academy~!"</p>
<p>When I got off the bus, a beautiful woman with almost silver-white hair greeted us in fluent Japanese. Ah.... Why is she here?</p>
<p>,"I'm Cheon Ha-rin, an executive director at Cheil Electronics. I'll be introducing you to Cheil Group today."</p>
<p>Cheon Ha-rin, an executive director at Cheil Electronics. She was a very important figure even in the youngest son of Megacorp. She was a genius who entered a prestigious American university at the age of 15, graduated early, and even obtained a doctorate, and she was an ambitious woman who aimed for the throne of Cheil Group, the best in Korea.</p>
<p>The player could either bring Cheon Ha-rin into an alliance or antagonize her, and as you can see, she was an incredibly beautiful woman, so many people wanted to ally with her. However, since Cheon Ha-rin herself was highly capable, the conditions she demanded from her allies were also strict. Therefore, to become Cheon Ha-rin's ally, you had to have good specs as well as gain her favor.</p>
<p>,"Everyone must be hungry from coming all the way here. Shall we go to the cafeteria?"</p>
<p>It was as if she was treating us like kindergarteners, but with a strange sense of intimidation, all the students followed Cheon Ha-rin and a few escorts without any complaints.</p>
<p>,"Lunch time is one hour, so please have lunch and rest at the cafe in front."</p>
<p>The cafeteria was quite large. There was a buffet with quite a lot of menus prepared, and they all looked delicious. I skipped breakfast this morning, so I was quite hungry.</p>
<p>,"I have something to tell the supervising teachers, so could you please come this way for a moment?"</p>
<p>I was about to get a tray and line up behind the students when I heard Cheon Ha-rin's words, so I put aside my disappointment and followed Cheon Ha-rin with the homeroom teachers and the teachers who came to help with the escort.</p>
<p>The place where I followed Cheon Ha-rin was a small meeting room next to the cafeteria.</p>
<p>,"I'm going to tell you about the precautions during the tour before the meal. The Industrial Espionage Prevention Act has become stricter these days, so we don't want any unfortunate events to happen after coming on a tour, right?"</p>
<p>Ah, I see. It seemed that she called us in to give us a warning in advance because there might be sensitive matters during the tour. As technology developed, the Industrial Espionage Prevention Act became more and more thorough and strict in each country to prevent this kind of technology leakage, but on the other hand, it was because there were so many attempts to steal technology.</p>
<p>,"The place we're touring isn't a very important facility, so most of the filming is allowed, but I'll tell you in advance where filming isn't allowed. Then, please control the students well."</p>
<p>It seemed that she called us in to ask the teachers to control the students because Cheil Electronics couldn't control all the students. It's nothing special. Usually, it's allowed, but if it's not allowed, they'll tell us it's not allowed, so tell the students well at that time. It wasn't a very difficult request because it was about that much.</p>
<p>,"Then you can go to eat now. I'll see you in front of the cafeteria in an hour."</p>
<p>When the teachers were about to go outside at the word that we could go eat, I heard Cheon Ha-rin's voice from behind.</p>
<p>,"Ah, tanned male teacher. You dropped this."</p>
<p>I'm the only tanned male teacher, right? I turned around and looked at Cheon Ha-rin. She was holding something that looked like a wallet. That's not my wallet, though? While I was making a puzzled expression, all the teachers left, and only Cheon Ha-rin and I were left in the room.</p>
<p>,"I lied for a moment to be alone with you, Yongtae-ssi."</p>
<p>Cheon Ha-rin put the wallet she was holding in her inner pocket and said. To be alone with me.... How does she know me? Besides, she knew my name exactly. If she knows my real name, that means she knows a lot about me.... How does she know?</p>
<p>Click.</p>
<p>I closed the door. It was because I thought the sound of our conversation shouldn't leak outside.</p>
<p>,"Why do you want to be alone with me? I think this is our first time meeting...."</p>
<p>,"It might be your first time, Yongtae-ssi, but it's not my first time."</p>
<p>The eyes looking straight at me sparkle mysteriously. Mysterious eyes where purple and red light intersect.... It seemed that she had eye-type characteristics.</p>
<p>Unlike other named NPCs whose characteristics are usually fixed, Cheon Ha-rin's characteristics were different each time she played, but it seemed that she had acquired eye-type characteristics in this world. What ability does she have to recognize me with her eyes? Is it a clairvoyance type? Or....</p>
<p>,"There's no need to think complicatedly. I was just looking for an opportunity to talk to you alone because I wanted to form an alliance."</p>
<p>Cheon Ha-rin, who was so picky, wanted to form an alliance as soon as she saw my face.... I was a little embarrassed. Is an alliance this easy? But since I'm not leading my forces alone, I couldn't rashly decide on such an alliance on my own.</p>
<p>,"I'm not leading my forces alone, so I'll have to talk to my colleagues about such a big thing as an alliance."</p>
<p>,"Hehe, I didn't think an alliance would be formed right away at our first meeting either. Here's my business card. I'll be waiting for Yongtae-ssi's call."</p>
<p>Cheon Ha-rin hands me a business card. It was a simple business card with Cheon Ha-rin, Executive Director of Cheil Electronics, written on it, and a phone number and email address on the back.</p>
<p>,"This is my business card."</p>
<p>Since I received a business card, I couldn't not give my business card out of courtesy. I handed over the disguised business card for St. Francis Girls' Academy that I had.</p>
<p>,"I want to receive a real business card instead of this."</p>
<p>How much does this woman know? There are few people who know my identity even in Japan, but the fact that a woman in Korea who has nothing to do with me knows me so well was a bit uncomfortable.</p>
<p>,"That's all I have right now. Anyway, it's not a fake contact number, so you can contact me at the place written on the business card."</p>
<p>I wasn't carrying around a business card with Taira Ryuta or Jin Yongtae written on it because I was worried that I might accidentally get caught while I was with the students.</p>
<p>,"Then.... Let's go eat, Yamashiro-sensei."</p>
<p>She even knows the teacher's name properly. I nodded sullenly and headed to the cafeteria.</p>
