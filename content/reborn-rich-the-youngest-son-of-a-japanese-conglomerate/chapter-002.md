<p>For those who have recently started reading or are following along, "The Youngest Son of a Japanese Conglomerate" accepts AI illustration requests~!</p>
<p>If you make an illustration request, the requested illustration will be uploaded at the end of the latest chapter, hehe.</p>
<p>Requests should be made in the latest chapter where the author frequently checks comments, or if you are still reading, you can leave them in this notice.</p>
<p>However, there must be rules for requests, right?</p>
<p>The rules are as follows:</p>
<p>1. I will not accept requests that are too explicit. EX) Sex scenes or requests involving genitals.</p>
<p>2. I will limit it to about 2 requests at a time.</p>
<p>3. I will not accept abstract requests. For example, "Alik's seduction" or "anything." Please make specific requests so that a picture can come out to some extent.</p>
<p>4. I will not accept illustrations with multiple people appearing at the same time due to technical + time limitations.</p>
<p>5. If requests are flooded or there is a sudden AI program error, the request may be delayed to the next chapter or the chapter after that.</p>
<p>6. In addition, illustrations may not be produced due to the limitations of the AI program or the author's lack of ability. Also, the old feeling may not be achieved due to program updates, etc.</p>
<p>7. I will not accept spoiler-like scenes that will appear in the future, as judged by the author. EX) An affection scene of a character who has not yet been captured...</p>
<p>8. I will not accept duplicate requests that are similar to previously received requests.</p>
<p>9. Since it takes a very long time, I will not accept requests such as B technique appearing in work A~!</p>
