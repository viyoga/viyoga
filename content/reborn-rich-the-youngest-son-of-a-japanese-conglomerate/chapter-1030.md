<p>"Are you throwing water balloons? Blood Wall"</p>
<p>Otagi Yuka stretched out her Hand and created a wall of hardened blood. The Blood Wall was a Blood Sorcery that blocked physical attacks. As the Gastric Acid Explosion magic, blocked by the blood wall, exploded with a chiiiii, instantly melting the blood wall, Otagi Yuka was startled and dodged sideways.</p>

<p>With a chilling sound, the dagger embedded itself in Otagi Yuka's abdomen. Although she was wearing a sturdy cyber suit, the dagger, as if magically treated, tore through the cyber suit and pierced Otagi Yuka's flesh as well.</p>
<p>"How's that? It's a silver dagger that Vampires love to die for. I was convinced that the Vampires of New York would target the Fragment of Omniscience, so I prepared it in advance."</p>
<p>'Were there other Vampires...?'</p>

<p>"...."</p>
<p>Hertha Vogel stood still, waiting to see Otagi Yuka suffer in pain from the silver dagger, but Otagi Yuka showed no reaction whatsoever to the silver. Rather, far from suffering, she wore an Expression of wonder.</p>
<p>"Just as Ryuta-ssi said, my silver resistance seems to have risen tremendously because of my Clan Lord Trait."</p>
<p>Otagi Yuka muttered, smiling at Hertha Vogel. In fact, no matter how much of an ambush it was, and even if Hertha Vogel's attack was un-witch-like fast, a Swordsman of Otagi Yuka's level should have been able to dodge this attack.</p>
<p>However, Otagi Yuka had deliberately taken the dagger hit, both to test her silver resistance and to quickly end this stalling battle.</p>
<p>"Checkmate? A fight isn't chess, witch."</p>
<p>Otagi Yuka said, looking at Hertha Vogel's Hand, which was covered in her own blood. Subsequently, as Otagi Yuka said, "Boom!", the blood on Hertha Vogel's Hand caused an explosion, and a blood squirt gushed up from Hertha Vogel's wrist, where she had lost her Hand.</p>
<p>That blood squirt instantly became sharp shards of blood due to Otagi Yuka's Blood Energy Domination and embedded itself in Hertha Vogel's Body. Because most were fatal vital points, Hertha Vogel collapsed in the alley with an Expression of disbelief.</p>
<p>"I was going to capture her alive and ask various things, but still, I can't let her live. Her face and Figure are equally good, so Ryuta-ssi might try to win her over with his body."</p>
<p>Otagi Yuka muttered, holding Golden Blood and beheading Hertha Vogel as a coup de grâce.</p>
<p>She wasn't an easy opponent to capture alive, and Otagi Yuka, worried that Taira Ryuta, with his personality of never refusing a woman who comes to him, might draw this witch to his side, killed Hertha Vogel.</p>
<p>"This grimoire... and the witch's hat and chains look quite useful, don't they?"</p>
<p>The grimoire was such a rare item, and the other items Hertha Vogel was equipped with also looked quite good, so Otagi Yuka decided to take them.</p>
<p>"Come to my side here. There are items I need to collect."</p>
<p>Otagi Yuka contacted the transport team via radio. Since she couldn't carry these items, she intended to tell the transport team to collect them.</p>
<p>-We will send a transport team to your location.</p>
<p>The Jo Seodang agents at the command post, who were on standby for radio, immediately replied. They would probably arrive within 2-3 minutes and take the items.</p>
<p>"The witch's blood is a bit unsettling...."</p>
<p>Even though she had killed Hertha Vogel, Otagi Yuka's Expression was still pale. If she was a witch who had prepared for Vampires with a silver dagger, she didn't know what she might have done to her own blood.</p>
<p>Ultimately, Otagi Yuka decided not to consume the witch's blood. She drew out Hertha Vogel's blood, poured it down the sewer, and focused her mind on the necklace she was wearing around her neck.</p>
<p>It was the ruby necklace Taira Ryuta had given her as a gift at the airport. It was a necklace that could recover Blood Energy, a very useful item for a Vampire.</p>
<p>Otagi Yuka absorbed the Blood Energy imbued in the ruby necklace. Then, her pale Face began to regain its color.</p>
<p>"If these are the items I obtained today, it would be a small repayment, right?"</p>
<p>Otagi Yuka smiled broadly, imagining Taira Ryuta rejoicing at the witch's items she had collected.</p>
