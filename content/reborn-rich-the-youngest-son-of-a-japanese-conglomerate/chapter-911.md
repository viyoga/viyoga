<p>Pa-at! As if to prove that it had merely been letting itself be hit until now, Endless Despair switched to the offensive. Perhaps because each muscle fiber was as thick as a thumb, it boasted immense power and speed. I dodged Endless Despair's punches by a hair's breadth; if I hadn't been wearing the Black Knight Armor, the pressure alone would have ripped off my cheek flesh. It was faster than I expected, but not to the extent that my eyes and senses couldn't follow.</p>
<p>That was because it wasn't just me; Fujigasaki Rinka and Shimokawabe Airen were also here. When one of us was attacked, the other two could attack and block Endless Despair's assault. -Mu-matsuan-ya (Endless Dark Night) Pain Point - Consecutive (Tsuuten-Ren). Endless Despair continuously used the Fist Technique Martial Arts Form that emitted black Gi, which it had just used against my Creation Chaos Style Star Breaker. It was as if its arms had multiplied into dozens, simultaneously attacking me, Fujigasaki Rinka, and Shimokawabe Airen. Woong.</p>
<p>I raised my Creation Chaos Gi, deflecting attacks that could be deflected and blocking strikes aimed at vital areas. The Armor's defense option was considerable, and especially because it had an option to additionally block Divinity damage, I didn't receive as much shock as I expected. Though I couldn't confirm with my eyes due to lack of focus, there were no sounds of impacts or moans, indicating that the others also defended well against the attacks.</p>
<p>-You defend well. Now, I will raise the gear one more stage. Endless Despair declared it would raise the gear one more stage, with an attitude as if a cat were toying with a mouse. Raise the gear? Of course, Endless Despair seemed to have plenty of reserve power left. -Mu-matsuan-ya (Endless Dark Night) Dansue-seu (Death Throes). This technique didn't seem like an ordinary Fist Technique. As it swung its fist, instead of a sonic boom, the Screams of many people echoed, unsettling the mind.</p>
<p>Even I, with high Will Stats, felt my mind slightly waver, indicating that it was using the Authority it gained from becoming an Outer God this time. In addition to the mental Attack, the Fist Technique itself was extremely complex and lethal, truly befitting the Leader of Anyaji.</p>
<p>"Aaaagh!" As Shimokawabe Airen stumbled slightly from the mental Attack, Endless Despair's fist plunged into her Abdomen. With a 'paang!' sound, Shimokawabe Airen's Cyber Suit on her back burst open. As expected, since Mu-matsuan-ya's base martial art was Shinkaiyatsude, which utilizes Infiltrating Force, a phenomenon typically seen in Infiltrating Force or Through-the-Back Fist, where the back bursts even though the Abdomen was struck, seemed to be occurring.</p>
<p>The most dangerous thing right now was Shimokawabe Airen's Mithril Sword, so it seemed to have shaken her mind with the previous technique before attacking her. Endless Despair followed up that strike, attempting to finish off Shimokawabe Airen, but was forced to evade my and Fujigasaki Rinka's attacks.</p>
<p>"Airen, are you alright?!"</p>
<p>"I'm still fine." To Fujigasaki Rinka's Question, Shimokawabe Airen calmly nodded her Head. She must have suffered considerable Internal Injury, but instead, Shimokawabe Airen's Fighting spirit burned even brighter as she raised her Sword and aimed it at Endless Despair.</p>
<p>"Since I have Defense, I will stick to it as much as possible, so please focus on attacking, both of you." It seemed we would inevitably lose if this continued, so trusting in my Armor and regeneration, I decided to draw aggro from Endless Despair.</p>
<p>"Haaat!" I closed in on Endless Despair with immense speed, using psychokinetic Flight in addition to my running speed. Then I raised my Index Finger and thrust it straight out. -Creation Chaos Style Iljeom Gaebyeok (Single Point Genesis). I unleashed a technique that gathered Creation Chaos Gi at my fingertip to cause an explosion, but Endless Despair struck my Forearm upwards, Neutralizing my Attack.</p>
<p>I brought the raised Arm straight down to attack, and Endless Despair spun its Body to dodge my Attack while simultaneously striking my Temple with its Elbow. I raised my other Arm, the one I hadn't brought down, to block Endless Despair's Elbow. It was a scene like a well-choreographed Fighting sequence one might see in a movie, but it was a fight where even a slight misstep could result in a serious Injury. Endless Despair Neutralized not only my attacks but also those of Shimokawabe Airen and Fujigasaki Rinka, who had aimed for openings.</p>
<p>It blocked Shimokawabe Airen's attacks by using me as a Shield, and suppressed Fujigasaki Rinka's attacks with the power of Compatibility, using the White Light it had stolen from the White Light Fist Masters. Shimokawabe Airen calmly sharpened her Eyes to seek another opening, and Fujigasaki Rinka bit her Lips tightly, as if frustrated that she couldn't display her true Skill due to the stolen Abyss.</p>
<p>Apart from this battle, which felt like being subjected to Mockery by Endless Despair, after fighting like this for a long time, my Creation Chaos Style gradually began to become more proficient. When I first learned Creation Chaos Style, the long Training I underwent with Lizhael was not learned physically, but mentally. Since I hadn't engaged in such intense combat after acquiring Creation Chaos Style, my Skill was increasing in real-time. Of course, Endless Despair, my Opponent, also keenly felt my rapidly rising Skill.</p>
<p>Endless Despair's Expression hardened, then it pushed me back with a push-kick and soared into the empty space. -Let's end the Play here. Kukukuku. After Endless Despair declared an end to the Play, the Tentacles that had been emerging from the pile of Corpses fully burst forth and clung to Endless Despair's back. It was as if numerous snakes were flying through the empty space and attaching themselves to a person's back. Soon, wings made of Tentacles unfurled from Endless Despair's back.</p>
<p>The Members of this battle, who had been wrestling with the Tentacles, looked at Endless Despair, their expressions bewildered. Endless Despair flapped its Tentacle wings once, then began to emit black Gi from the tips of the Tentacles. And as it thrashed its wings as if shooting out wind, the black Gi formed a vortex and swept over my Students. Lizhael's Blessing, received by Urabe Konoka and Hanakame, blocked the black vortex like a Shield, but it seemed precarious, on the verge of shattering.</p>
<p>Especially, the black vortex blew an even stronger wind at me, Fujigasaki Rinka, and Shimokawabe Airen, who had been fighting Endless Despair until just now, making it impossible for us to counterattack. Endless Despair flapped its wings again as if shooting out a vortex, then this time, it shot towards Shimokawabe Airen with immense speed. Huwoong! With the sound of wind, Shimokawabe Airen was instantly sent tumbling across the Ground.</p>
<p>Shimokawabe Airen blocked Endless Despair's hand attacks, but she couldn't block the Tentacles emerging from its wings. Tentacles pierced her Body, leaving finger-sized Holes from which Blood was flowing. But Shimokawabe Airen didn't just take it. Seizing that Moment, she slashed Endless Despair's Side with her Mithril Sword. Although it received an Attack from the Mithril Sword, which inflicts a fatal Dibeopeu, Endless Despair did not let out a painful Scream or avoid combat.</p>
<p>Instead, Endless Despair, as if unsatisfied with this, immediately flew towards Fujigasaki Rinka. Fujigasaki Rinka looked at me and opened her Mouth.</p>
<p>"Ryuta, don't close your Eyes and watch closely. I will show you the Pinnacle of Martial Arts. Due to my Body's condition, I think I can only show it once." Fujigasaki Rinka awaited Endless Despair's Attack in a ready stance for a straight punch. Endless Despair's Tentacle wings began to glow with White Light. Even with the White Light, which was its opposite Compatibility, before her Eyes, Fujigasaki Rinka maintained a calm Expression, her Eyes half-closed. As Endless Despair swung its Tentacle wings, engulfing Fujigasaki Rinka.</p>
<p>Fujigasaki Rinka opened her half-closed Eyes and unleashed a straight punch. Peo-peo-peo-peong! And Endless Despair's Tentacle wings burst one by one. An ideal straight punch where Physical Body, mind, and Gi were united. That punch, where Sim, Gi, and Che were in Union, Neutralized Endless Despair's Attack and destroyed its wings and Body.</p>
<p>As if a single slash from the Mithril Sword wasn't enough, Endless Despair's Body was reconstructing itself again, but the right wing, which had taken Fujigasaki Rinka's strike head-on, was not regenerating at all. Teol-sseok. With a single strike, Fujigasaki Rinka seemed to have exhausted her strength, as she immediately knelt on the Cave Floor. Fujigasaki Rinka knelt and collapsed, but the straight punch she had shown me was not in vain at all. This was because a small Enlightenment had come to me after witnessing that strike.</p>
<p>As I was realizing something after seeing Fujigasaki Rinka's strike, Endless Despair raised its relatively intact left Tentacle wing to finish off Fujigasaki Rinka. The wing, imbued with White Light, was swung with immense speed. Just before the White Light Tentacle wing struck Fujigasaki Rinka, I embraced Fujigasaki Rinka and rolled my Body to dodge. Kwaang! Dust billowed up, and the Ground was deeply gouged.</p>
<p>Fortunately, I was able to dodge, but Endless Despair approached me, holding Fujigasaki Rinka, as if to say, 'This is the end.' -I will make good use of your power. Endless Despair's Tentacle wings began to spread wide. It was as if countless snakes were raising their Heads and looking at me. It felt as if no matter where I dodged, one of the countless Tentacles would follow me. -Die with your Sabu. Ssyae-ae-ae-aek! A strike from the net-like Tentacle wings, spread wide and dominating all directions.</p>
<p>I tightly embraced Fujigasaki Rinka to protect her and gather Creation Chaos in my fist for a Counterattack, but a sudden, unexpected Situation prevented me from counterattacking. Kwaaaang! Suddenly, the Cave's ceiling burst open and began to collapse.</p>
