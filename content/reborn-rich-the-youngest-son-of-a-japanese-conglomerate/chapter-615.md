<p>I drove to the location Fujigasaki Rinka sent me, which turned out to be a dilapidated house near Aokigahara forest at the foot of Mount Fuji. It didn't even have an address, probably because it was built without registration. I had to input the coordinates to find it, and Kujo Kina, upon seeing it, was delighted, saying it was just like the abode of a hidden master.</p>
<p>"Still, he said he'd have a few disciples... We should at least get a container building as a temporary measure..."</p>
<p>Kujo Kina was constantly thinking about something for the disciples kidnapped by Anya-ji who would be arriving tomorrow. By the way, they're not even officially disciples yet, and she's already calling them fellow disciples. Since Kujo Kina was the youngest of the Chairman's direct line, she seemed to be enjoying the feeling of having younger siblings.</p>
<p>"It's impossible to go any further, Agassi."</p>
<p>"I'll get off here."</p>
<p>From the start, it was far from a paved road, so we couldn't approach any closer. Since I got in the car directly from the Kujo residence, it wasn't my car but a vehicle with a driver attached to the Kujo mansion. I was glad I didn't bring my own car because the road conditions were terrible.</p>
<p>"Come to think of it, I saw a ghost near Aokigahara once."</p>
<p>"Aii! Don't tell me stories like that, Sensei. It's scary...!"</p>
<p>Kujo Kina was horrified when I brought up the story that suddenly came to mind. Contrary to her appearance, she seemed to be afraid of ghost stories. She looks like she could destroy a ghost with one blow, though.</p>
<p>Kiiiik...!</p>
<p>Suddenly, the dilapidated wooden door of the old house opened with an unpleasant sound. Kujo Kina let out a small kyaa at the creepy noise and clung to me. Hmm, even a muscular woman has soft breasts. I enjoyed the sensation of Kujo Kina's chest against my arm as I approached the old house.</p>
<p>"Hehe...! Are you the ones who said you'd be visiting my abode today?"</p>
<p>The one who came out of the old house was a small, Yoda-like old man. Is that Kinu-i Tachio? But, he's a martial artist, right?! I heard he traveled all over Japan in his youth, challenging dojos, so I was expecting a burly, intimidating, stern martial artist...</p>
<p>"Yikes!"</p>
<p>Thwack!</p>
<p>Yoda, no, Kinu-i Tachio, rushed at me with incredible speed, kicking me in the shin.</p>
<p>"Kyak...!"</p>
<p>It was a blow that made me scream, something I rarely do. It looked like a simple kick, but it hurt like hell? As expected, a master is a master. To inflict this much pain while bypassing my defense...</p>
<p>"W-What was that? Since when do you kick someone's joints on first meeting?"</p>
<p>"You're the one who's out of line calling me Yoda on first meeting, you bastard!"</p>
<p>Did I say the word Yoda out loud? Or does he have telepathy...?</p>
<p>"The thought of Yoda is all over your face, you bastard!"</p>
<p>Ah, I see. It seems the thought of 'Isn't that Yoda?' was written all over my face. Kinu-i Tachio must have seen that expression countless times, so he recognized it immediately.</p>
<p>"Hmph, just like that woman's disciple."</p>
<p>I don't know if being called Fujigasaki Rinka's disciple is an insult or a compliment. Judging from what just happened, it's definitely an insult.</p>
<p>"I'm not the important one here. This is Kujo Kina, a candidate to be the old man's disciple."</p>
<p>It didn't seem like anything good would come from talking about me. If the topic of being Fujigasaki Rinka's disciple kept coming up, there was a high chance Kujo Kina would find out my identity, so I introduced Kujo Kina to Kinu-i Tachio, putting her forward.</p>
<p>"Hmm...!"</p>
<p>Kinu-i Tachio looked at Kujo Kina, circling around her and checking her physical condition. Soon, with a satisfied expression, Kinu-i Tachio nodded approvingly.</p>
<p>"Good bones. Is this young lady the one who suffered from Anya-ji's Rampage?"</p>
<p>Judging by the fact that he knows about Rampage, Kinu-i Tachio seems to know about Anya-ji as well. Well, that's probably why he's trying to take in the children kidnapped by Anya-ji.</p>
<p>"Yes, I found and eliminated the Rampage early on."</p>
<p>"Indeed...!"</p>
<p>Kujo Kina was standing there with a nervous expression, watching to see if Kinu-i Tachio would accept her as a disciple or not.</p>
<p>I am Kinu-i Tachio. I once traveled all over Japan to master the fist, and now, using that experience, I have created a martial art called Hakei-ryu (Destruction Style), and I have started taking on disciples to pass down this martial art.</p>
<p>Hakei-ryu... A martial art that destroys forms, huh? Just from the name, it sounded like a pretty overbearing martial art.</p>
<p>"I believe that the master has the right to choose a disciple, but the child who will become a disciple also has the right to choose a master. The relationship between master and disciple is not a light one, so first, I will show you my martial art."</p>
<p>The disciple also has the right to choose a master... That's actually true. Unless it's kidnapping like Anya-ji, you need the disciple's consent to pass on martial arts.</p>
<p>Kinu-i Tachio stood in front of a tree and struck it with a small shout of Hap! At first, there was no change, so I looked at the tree with a 'what?' expression, but soon the tree started to crack and creak, and then it burst out with a bang!</p>
<p>It was so destroyed that you couldn't even find its original form, just like the name Hakei-ryu suggested. Roughly estimating from the phenomenon that occurred after the punch, Hakei-ryu seemed to be a type of Internal Energy Heavy Hand technique that not only vibrated the opponent's internal organs from the inside but also caused them to explode.</p>
<p>"Wow...!"</p>
<p>Kujo Kina's eyes sparkled with admiration at Kinu-i Tachio's demonstration. Kinu-i Tachio looked at Kujo Kina, who was admiring him, with a satisfied expression.</p>
<p>"How about it, will you become my disciple?"</p>
<p>"I will!"</p>
<p>Kujo Kina answered without hesitation for even 0.1 seconds to the question of whether she would become Kinu-i Tachio's disciple. Kinu-i Tachio's demonstration must have been impressive.</p>
<p>"Alright, what did you say your name was?"</p>
<p>"Kujo Kina desu!"</p>
<p>"I see. Kujo Kina, you are this Kinu-i Tachio's second disciple."</p>
<p>Second disciple? Did Kujo Kina have a Sunbae? Kujo Kina thought she would be the first disciple, but she looked slightly disappointed at the mention of a Sunbae.</p>
<p>"Then, what about my senior disciple...?"</p>
<p>"He died. At the hands of Anya-ji."</p>
<p>Wait, why is the atmosphere suddenly turning somber like this? Even a disciple of a martial artist of this caliber died, so Anya-ji's disruption was beyond expectations.</p>
<p>"You also suffered at the hands of Anya-ji, so we have a common enemy."</p>
<p>"I see. I've certainly heard that I was cursed by that group called Anya-ji as well."</p>
<p>"Hmm, let's not talk about heavy things for now. I have to accept another new disciple tomorrow, so we can talk about it all at once then... More importantly, you said you could solve the problem of your disciples' residence?"</p>
<p>Kinu-i Tachio turned his head to me and asked, as if he hadn't received the exact details from Fujigasaki Rinka.</p>
<p>"It's not me, but a new disciple will solve it."</p>
<p>"Master, my disciple is rich. My fellow disciples are coming tomorrow, so I'm going to install a temporary container building right away, and then buy land to build a dojo and even construct the dojo."</p>
<p>"W-What? You're going to build a dojo?"</p>
<p>Kinu-i Tachio was extremely surprised at the mention of building a dojo, as if he didn't know how rich Kujo Kina was.</p>
<p>"There was a reason that woman was so confident..."</p>
<p>Kinu-i Tachio muttered quietly and smiled. Judging by the fact that he was happy about the mention of building a dojo, it didn't seem like he was living in a dilapidated house in Aokigahara because he liked it.</p>
<p>Well... He must have traveled all over Japan challenging dojos his whole life, so there's no way he'd have money. On the other hand, I also thought that he was able to create a powerful new martial art because he traveled all over the country like that. There's a world of difference between being taught a martial art and creating a new one.</p>
<p>"Master, how about we have some meat to commemorate our connection as master and disciple? I know a high-end steakhouse around here."</p>
<p>Trying to win over her master with money... Kujo Kina was surprisingly shrewd for her appearance. And Kinu-i Tachio was swallowing hard, saying, "W-Wagyu?" Just how poor has this Yoda grandpa been living? Is his small stature due to poor nutrition?</p>
<p>"You bastard! You were thinking Yoda, weren't you?!"</p>
<p>"No, are you some kind of monster?"</p>
<p>Has this old man developed telekinetic abilities that only react to the thought of Yoda? Kinu-i Tachio rushed at me. I was caught off guard the first time and got hit, but I couldn't just let him hit me this time, so we had a brief boxing match.</p>
<p>Fists collided with fists, shaking the trees with tremendous vibrations and causing leaves to fall like rain, and the shockwave from legs colliding with legs caused the door of the dilapidated house where Kinu-i Tachio lived to fall off. After bickering for a while, we stopped fighting, keeping our distance. It was because I thought Kujo Kina, who was watching from the side, would get caught up in it if we continued like this.</p>
<p>"...Hmph! You're strong, just like that woman's disciple!"</p>
<p>"Let's go eat meat, Yoda grandpa."</p>
<p>I was also a little sulky, so I decided to call him Yoda outright now. Kinu-i Tachio huffed for a moment at my words, but his expression soon softened again at the thought of eating meat.</p>
<p>Kujo Kina was staring at the chaotic surroundings, which looked like a natural disaster had struck, with enraptured eyes. She seemed very excited at the thought that she could show this kind of power herself, although it would be difficult to achieve in a short period of time.</p>
<p>We took the car we came in to a nearby meat restaurant. It was a steakhouse, as Kujo Kina had said.</p>
<p>"Please give us six 1-kilogram steaks."</p>
<p>Kujo Kina ordered a lot of steaks with good sense. It seemed like she ordered one each for Kujo Kina and the driver, and two each for me and Kinu-i Tachio.</p>
<p>Yoda grandpa Kinu-i Tachio was counting the price of the steak on the menu, one, ten, hundred, thousand, ten thousand... And his eyes were shaking because it was more expensive than he thought. He had an expression that said, 'Am I even allowed to eat something like this?'</p>
<p>While sitting down and waiting for the steak, my smartphone rang with a jiing. I checked my phone, wondering who it was, and it was Minamoto Risa.</p>
<p>-Minamoto Lisa: You, let's do what we promised.</p>
<p>What we promised? Could she be talking about a date?</p>
