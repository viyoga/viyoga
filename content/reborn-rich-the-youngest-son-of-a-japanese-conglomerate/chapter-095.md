<p>"By the way, what kind of person is Nagumo Sian-nim? I heard the Chairman-nim personally scouted her...."</p>
<p>I asked Matsuhira Ginichiro about Nagumo Sian-nim on the way to the restaurant. I asked not to get on the wrong side of Nagumo Sian-nim, even if I couldn't get on her good side like the other three successors.</p>
<p>"Hmm...! To put it in a nutshell, Nagumo Sian-nim is like a monster."</p>
<p>What is this old man saying? If you're the president of a Taira Group subsidiary, you could blow away the head of the Human Resources Development Center branch with just a puff of breath, but a monster? Isn't that a bit much?</p>
<p>Don't look at me like that. I don't mean it in a weird way, but I don't know her exact age. Her face looks exactly the same as it did twenty years ago when she ran a small company called Kanmido, fifteen years ago when she became the president of Taira Foods, and even now with gray hair. She doesn't age at all. That's why Nagumo-nim is nicknamed the Monster of Akita. Of course, she hates it if you call her that to her face...</p>
<p>Ah, she doesn't age? Nagumo Sian-nim must have eaten a Juangwa. If she hasn't changed in 20 years, the effect of the Juangwa must be pretty amazing.... As someone who fed it to my woman and ate one myself, I was curious.</p>
<p>We took the rental car we came in, and Matsuhira Ginichiro drove according to the navigation. Matsuhira Ginichiro didn't stop talking even while driving. He told me a lot of information I didn't know, but it wasn't very helpful.</p>
<p>The restaurant we arrived at wasn't that fancy. In fact, the sushi restaurant we went to for lunch looked fancier.</p>
<p>According to Matsuhira Ginichiro, Nagumo Sian-nim likes popular restaurants more than fancy restaurants. It's because she's the president of a food company that studies the tastes of the public. So, he said he booked a restaurant between a popular restaurant and a fancy restaurant for this dinner.</p>
<p>Fortunately, we were able to arrive at 7:20, ten minutes before the appointment time of 7:30. We went into the restaurant and sat at the reserved seats. Nagumo Sian-nim didn't seem to have arrived yet; there was no one there.</p>
<p>Our group sat, each occupying one side of the square table. About five minutes after we sat down, the restaurant door opened and a woman walked in. She was a beauty with glossy gray hair wearing a high-class Kimono. She looked like she was in her late twenties or early thirties, but the woman who just came in was probably Nagumo Sian-nim.</p>
<p>Matsuhira Ginichiro said that Nagumo Sian-nim had an incredibly youthful appearance for her age, but he didn't say she was this beautiful. She was a beauty with grace and a somewhat seductive aura, and her figure was as explosive as Yukiko's.</p>
<p>"Hello, Nagumo-nim. I haven't seen you in a while, and I don't know how much I've missed you. I don't think Nagumo-nim knows how happy I am to meet you like this because of the audit of the Human Resources Development Center. And I...."</p>
<p>"Stop it. You're still talkative, Matsuhira. And you.... You're Saburo's son."</p>
<p>"Hello, Nagumo-nim. I'm Taira Ryuta."</p>
<p>Nagumo Sian-nim, who cut off Matsuhira Ginichiro's words, soon looked at me and recognized me. Do I really look like Taira Saburo that much? Personally, I thought I looked like Jinmija Yeosa, but oh well.</p>
<p>"And this person is...?"</p>
<p>"I'm Suzuki Chisami, Deputy Team Leader of Education Support Team 2 at the Human Resources Development Center."</p>
<p>Suzuki Chisami also greeted her so it wouldn't be awkward. Nagumo Sian-nim looked at me with warm eyes and opened her mouth.</p>
<p>"As you may know, I'm Nagumo Sian. I'm the president of Taira Foods. Don't believe this idiot Matsuhira. He's too talkative, exaggerates well, and is a big liar."</p>
<p>Nagumo Sian-nim glared at Matsuhira Ginichiro, as if she didn't like him very much, but Matsuhira Ginichiro cheerfully laughed and said, "You're too much, Nagumo-nim!" and moved on.</p>
<p>"Saburo's matter was unfortunate. I've known him since fifteen years ago, so I've seen your father since he was in his mid-twenties. You really look like Saburo back then."</p>
<p>"Is that so? I thought I looked more like my mother, but everyone says I look like my father."</p>
<p>I shouldn't ruin the mood by answering, 'Actually, I can only see my father as a corpse, so I don't really know.' So, I answered as I usually thought.</p>
<p>"You may not know, but you look similar to Saburo in his younger days. Of course, you're much better looking...."</p>
<p>"He is definitely handsome."</p>
<p>We chatted and enjoyed our meal. Nagumo Sian-nim skillfully led the dinner, even making the taciturn Suzuki Chisami happy.</p>
<p>As we became more cheerful, we drank a few glasses of alcohol at the dinner, but I refused, saying I had to drive, but Nagumo Sian-nim said she would provide a driver and made me drink. I have a high resistance stat, so I didn't get drunk, but Suzuki Chisami and Matsuhira Ginichiro got so drunk from the constant exchange of drinks that they couldn't even stand up straight. Nagumo Sian-nim seemed very excited that I kept up with her drinking capacity.</p>
<p>After some more time passed, when Matsuhira and Suzuki Chisami collapsed on the table and passed out, Nagumo Sian-nim opened her mouth.</p>
<p>"By the way, didn't Matsuhira say anything?"</p>
<p>"He was talkative, but I don't remember anything in particular. Ah, he called Nagumo-nim the Monster of Akita."</p>
<p>In exchange for talking until my ears bled, I told Nagumo Sian-nim a few things that Matsuhira Ginichiro said that she would dislike.</p>
<p>"Hmm...! That's not the story I expected.... Matsuhira's problem is that he's too loose-lipped. I'll tell you in case you're surprised. Something will happen to Matsuhira's personal affairs tomorrow or the day after. You and your party won't be hurt, so don't worry."</p>
<p>I thought he was being too talkative; it seemed he was blabbing the secrets of the Taira Group here and there. I don't know exactly what he'll go through, but I found out that Matsuhira Ginichiro will go through something while we're here.</p>
<p>The Taira Group was a scary company. To actually do something just because you said something wrong. On the other hand, I thought about how much Matsuhira Ginichiro must have said for the Taira Group to do something like this.</p>
<p>"Let's get up now. Come alone next time. These two are too weak to drink, so it's not fun."</p>
<p>When Nagumo Sian-nim clapped her hands, two men in sturdy suits came in. One carried Matsuhira Ginichiro on his shoulder and left the restaurant, and the other stood in front of me.</p>
<p>"I'll drive you to the hotel."</p>
<p>"I'll be going, Big Sister Nagumo."</p>
<p>"Big Sis? Hohoho! You're good at flattering."</p>
<p>Rather than flattering, it was because it was too awkward to call her Nagumo Grandmother because she looked too young, so I called her Aunt, but it seemed like I scored quite a few points. Would she have liked it better if I called her Sister?</p>
<p>"I was going to call you Sister because you look so young, but I held back."</p>
<p>"Hohoho! I didn't know you'd make me laugh like this. Then, I'll see you tomorrow."</p>
<p>Nagumo Sian-nim laughed happily and went outside. I also supported Suzuki Chisami and got into the rental car that Nagumo Sian-nim's driver had started and was preparing.</p>
<p>"Please go to Iceville Hotel."</p>
<p>It wasn't as much as Hokkaido, but Akita Prefecture was also famous for its heavy snowfall. That's why many hotel names had themes like ice or snow, such as Iceville. It was close to the Human Resources Development Center's Kitatohoku branch, so it was close to the restaurant.</p>
<p>We moved for about five minutes and parked in the hotel parking lot, and we got out.</p>
<p>"Here's the key."</p>
<p>"Thank you."</p>
<p>The driver gave me the key, bowed his head, and disappeared. What is that man going to ride back? More than that, I was more worried about Suzuki Chisami, who I was supporting. Suzuki Chisami came and we didn't have a company dinner, so I only now learned about Suzuki Chisami's drinking capacity. She's a weak drinker.</p>

<p>"I have a reservation under Taira Ryuta."</p>
<p>"Yes, we'll help you to room 703."</p>
<p>Huh? Why are they only giving me one room number? As the hotel employee was about to guide me kindly, I hurriedly opened my mouth.</p>
<p>"Wait a minute! I.... I booked two rooms?"</p>
<p>"Yes? Please wait a moment."</p>

<p>"I'm sorry, sir! Due to our mistake, only one room was booked."</p>
<p>"Is the bed in the reserved room big enough for two people to sleep in?"</p>
<p>"Yes, yes! It's big enough for two people to sleep in."</p>
<p>"Then I'll just sleep there."</p>
<p>I was a little tired, so I didn't argue and said I would just sleep in one room, and the receptionist bowed at a 90-degree angle and thanked me.
