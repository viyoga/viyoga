<p>The auditorium was quite luxurious, befitting a VIP Resort. There was a conference table made of ebony and comfortable-looking high-end chairs, as well as a hologram projector.</p>
<p>Since I had to show the Corpse photos first after arriving at the auditorium, I stepped forward.</p>
<p>'I didn't really think anything would happen, so I didn't go around the building Outside with any expectations.... The first thing I discovered was this photo.'</p>
<p>As I spoke, I activated the hologram projector connected to my phone, and the Corpse of the male university student appeared.</p>
<p>"Kyaaah!!"</p>
<p>"T, Tanaka!"</p>
<p>"Norio-gun...!"</p>
<p>"W, what is this...."</p>
<p>The university students who saw the photo were all astonished. By the way, it seems the dead university student's name is Tanaka Norio.</p>
<p>'The cause of death, as shown in the photo, is the sword stuck in his neck.'</p>
<p>Once the university students' reactions had subsided to some extent, I showed them photos taken from different angles and close-ups, saying,</p>
<p>'And.... the employees were also all Murdered. Just like Tanaka-gun, they were all stabbed in the neck with a sword.'</p>
<p>I showed them the Resort employee Corpses in the incinerator, trash can, and buried in the snow, saying.</p>
<p>The auditorium was instantly filled with silence. Except for the Room 301 Students I had informed in advance, no one had imagined that the missing university student and employees would be found as Corpses.</p>
<p>'Hmm.... I know who the culprit is.'</p>
<p>It was Seizawa Shii's sudden remark. She figured out the culprit just by looking at the Corpses? As we looked at Seizawa Shii with surprised Expressions, Seizawa Shii waved her Hand and opened her mouth.</p>
<p>'No, no, I don't mean I know who among the people here it is, but everyone was stabbed in the neck, and the wounds look exactly like one of the data I received during vacation.... Everyone knows Kotowazuki, right? I mean the famous serial killer recently. The wounds that guy leaves behind seem similar.'</p>
<p>Ah, all the Corpses were stabbed in the neck, so I felt a sense of deja vu, and it was similar to the story of the serial killer Kotowazuki that I saw on my smartphone while coming to the Resort.</p>
<p>Seizawa Shii would have received various data from Eve while working as a fixer in Tokyo during vacation, so she must have seen photos of Corpses who had fallen victim to Kotowazuki.</p>
<p>She had said that she tried to catch him even in the helicopter coming to the Resort, but she stopped because there was a lack of clues.</p>
<p>'I don't know if it's appropriate to ask this in this Situation.... They're stabbing necks, so why do they call him Kotowazuki (Beheading Demon)? They're not cutting off Heads.'</p>
<p>'I'll explain that. When they discovered the Corpse in the early stages, the police made a Mistake while handling the Corpse, so the Head fell off, and that's why he's called by that nickname. He stabs the sword so deep into the neck that the Head might fall off if you're not careful.'</p>
<p>Shido Tsubaki, who knew this kind of thing well, spoke in response to Kujo Kina's question. I was also wondering why he was called the Beheading Demon when he was stabbing necks, but it was a nickname that originated from a police Mistake.</p>
<p>'Then.... does that mean Kotowazuki is inside here?'</p>
<p>One of the university student pack said with a terrified Expression.</p>
<p>'I don't know. That serial killer could be among us, or they could have committed the Murders and left the Resort, or they might not be among us and be hiding inside the Resort.'</p>
<p>Urabe Konoka said with a cold Expression. As expected, those are the only three possibilities.</p>
<p>'Urabe's Words make sense, but the second one isn't true.'</p>
<p>Minamoto Risa shook her Head and said. The second one is the Hypothesis that Kotowazuki committed the Murders and left the Resort, but she meant that it was impossible.</p>
<p>'You know I've been worried about the weather, right? When I saw a living employee last night, the snowstorm had already started Outside. The closest place from here is the ordinary Resort of the same Shiga Kogen affiliate, which takes ten minutes by bus, but ten minutes by bus would take much longer on foot. Especially in this snow, it would take even longer.'</p>
<p>Minamoto Risa's Words were an argument that the culprit could not have left the Resort because of the weather. It made sense.</p>
<p>However, if the serial killer had some kind of strange ability, there was no way they couldn't leave the Resort. Even if they only had Telekinetic Flight ability like I do, they could go somewhere else in an instant. Of course, they would have to endure the cold, though.</p>
<p>'Then, let's assume that the serial killer is inside the Resort and talk.'</p>
<p>Shido Tsubaki said, but I didn't bother to interject. I also didn't think that the serial killer would have both the ability to move and the ability to avoid the cold.</p>
<p>'First, let's hear the stories of the people who looked around each floor. There might be some clues.'</p>
<p>Unlike the university students who were still in a panic, the special class Students had gone through many things, so they began to handle things calmly. Right, now that they've seen my data from looking around Outside the Resort, they should look at the photos of the places they investigated.</p>
<p>'First, I'll start with us who looked around the first floor.'</p>
<p>Shido Tsubaki stood up from her Seat and plugged her phone into the hologram projector, saying.</p>
<p>'Actually, there wasn't anything special. We didn't discover a Corpse like Teacher-nim found. But....'</p>
<p>The photo that Shido Tsubaki showed was a photo of blood droplets falling on the window frame.</p>
<p>'It seems that some of the Corpses abandoned in the snowfield were killed Inside and thrown Outside through the window. I think they thought the Corpse would be quickly covered up because of the heavy snow.'</p>
<p>Aha...! I was wondering why the Corpses were lying on the road, but they just threw them out, trusting in the heavy snow. The sound of the Corpse falling wouldn't have been heard well because of the already accumulated snow.</p>
<p>'And we saw that a few kitchen knives had disappeared from the restaurant where we ate this Morning. Also, judging from the blood droplets falling on the back door of the kitchen, it seems that a Murder also took place in the kitchen.'</p>
<p>If a Murder took place in the kitchen, the Corpse in chef's attire that I found in the trash can seemed to be the protagonist of those blood droplets. They killed him in the kitchen, put him in the trash can as-is, went out the back door, and threw him in the trash dump.</p>
<p>'And the employee accommodation was a bit messy, but I didn't know if it was messed up because they fought with the serial killer or if it was just messy from the start, so I just took a photo.'</p>
<p>It seemed that there was nothing special on the first floor besides the bloodstains. Of course, those bloodstains were quite important evidence proving where the Murders took place.</p>
<p>After Shido Tsubaki's explanation, Naruse Shiyo of Room 302, who had investigated the second floor, came out. Naruse Shiyo showed the messed-up CCTV room.</p>
<p>'We couldn't confirm who moved because all the disks of the CCTV were physically damaged. That's all we confirmed. Except for Athena-ssi's room, all the guest rooms were locked, so there wasn't much to see. The few public conference rooms and this auditorium were clean.'</p>
<p>It seemed difficult to confirm because the guest rooms that were not in use from the second floor were locked. As expected of a famous serial killer, they didn't leave behind any video evidence like this.</p>
<p>Seizawa Shii from Room 303, who had investigated the third floor, came out. Seizawa Shii had some experience as a fixer, so I thought she might have found something out.</p>
<p>'First, I looked around all of our special class guest rooms. Of course, nothing came out. And I looked around the university students' rooms, but I didn't find anything special, but Yuka-cchi found one thing. Look closely at this window frame. It's slightly red, right? They wiped away the blood, but there are still bloodstains left. And if you look below, below....'</p>
<p>Seizawa Shii showed it in a video, and below the window with the faint bloodstains was the Corpse of Tanaka Norio that I had dug up. The culprit must have wiped away the bloodstains, but they couldn't fool the eyes of the Vampire Otagi Yuka.</p>
<p>'T, then does that mean Tanaka was Murdered inside the room?'</p>
<p>'Yeah, he died inside the room and was thrown Outside the window.'</p>
<p>There would be three people in the room besides the deceased, so how could no one have noticed the window opening and closing? Even if one of those three was the culprit, shouldn't the other two have noticed?</p>
<p>My question was not resolved, and it immediately moved on to the fourth and fifth floors. However, if you exclude the fact that one CCTV on the fourth floor was broken, there were almost no clues found there.</p>
<p>'Then.... shall we piece together the clues we've obtained?'</p>
<p>We weren't detectives, but the police wouldn't be able to come because of the heavy snow (the university students had already reported it, but they said they could only come after the snow stopped due to the severe snowstorm), and everyone thought it would be better to think about who the culprit was rather than just staying still.</p>
<p>While everyone was restless and unable to guess who the culprit was, Kotowazuki, who was mixed in with the university student pack, was secretly smiling.</p>
<p>'No one will be able to figure out my True Identity with evidence like bloodstains, right? If I take care of them one by one while they're in such a state of confusion....hehehe.'</p>
<p>Last night was Ecstasy. It had been a long time since he had killed so many people in one day. He had killed all the club members he didn't want to see and the Resort employees who were getting in the way, so he had unknowingly reached Orgasm.</p>
<p>Now, if he slowly kills them one by one....</p>
<p>'Then.... does that mean we have to stay trapped inside the resort until the snow stops and cook our own rice?'</p>
<p>The woman called Seizawa Shii said with a flushed Face, as if she was angry. It would be good to make that woman the next Target. He didn't like Seizawa Shii, who had been acting like a fixer since earlier. He just thought she was an attention seeker who was bluffing about being a fixer.</p>
<p>Kotowazuki was about to think about what kind of pleasure it would give him to kill the bluffing Gyaru of a prestigious family when Seizawa Shii shouted again.</p>
<p>'Damn it! Today's schedule was my turn...!'</p>
<p>Kwang!</p>
