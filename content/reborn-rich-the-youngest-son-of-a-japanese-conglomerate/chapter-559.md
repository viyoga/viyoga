<p>After finishing buying souvenirs, we had lunch. Although there were Mot Burgers or Jetria in the amusement park, we could eat franchise burger joints anytime, so we had lunch at a restaurant in the amusement park.</p>
<p>,Hmm...! It's not very good, just like I remember~☆,</p>
<p>As expected, it wasn't that delicious because it was the amusement park's own restaurant. The special class students had already been here in middle school and knew the taste, so they all ate it, saying it was a taste of memories. Of course, even if it's a taste of memories, it doesn't mean it's all delicious, but everyone seemed to have burned a lot of calories riding the roller coaster, so even though they said it wasn't good, they ate it all without leaving any.</p>
<p>The special class girls, who had regained their energy with lunch, scattered back into the amusement park and began to enjoy the rides, and I had to ride each ride once with the female students in turn, like some kind of rite of passage.</p>
<p>Almost all of the rides at Fuji-Q Highland were outside, so they didn't operate at night. As soon as it hit 6 PM, it was over, so I kept moving from one student to another without a break, constantly riding roller coasters.</p>
<p>Since I couldn't feel any thrill from roller coasters, instead of just sitting in the roller coaster seat and wasting time meaninglessly, I did some training that I could do even in this situation. I floated a pebble smaller than my fingernail next to me and trained to keep it floating stably next to me even when the roller coaster was running.</p>
<p>It turned out to be quite difficult training. It was quite draining on my willpower to follow the roller coaster's movements and keep it at an exact distance from me at the same time. After spending time like that, the ride experience with all the students was over, and it was getting close to Fuji-Q Highland's closing time, so we decided to move to the company dinner venue.</p>
<p>When we came to the amusement park, there were two students missing, so the inside of the car was quite spacious, but now it felt cramped. First of all, there were the souvenirs that everyone had bought, and above all, Minamoto Lisa's ○Funman giant dolls, which she had received as a gift from me, were taking up a seat.</p>
<p>,Lisa-ttang.... I feel like I can't breathe because it's so cramped!,</p>
<p>,...Sorry.,</p>
<p>Minamoto Lisa blushed and apologized to Seizawa Shii's joke. Now that all her classmates knew her taste, she seemed quite embarrassed.</p>
<p>,Ah, there it is.,</p>
<p>I muttered as I saw a mountain lodge that looked like it was made of wood. For the evening company dinner, we just decided to grill meat together. If we went to a restaurant, the young ladies of prestigious families wouldn't be able to eat much or talk much because they would be concerned about appearances, so we just booked a barbecue area in a pension-like place and decided to grill a lot of meat, sausages, mushrooms, shrimp, etc.</p>
<p>Of course, as it was a barbecue party for young ladies of prestigious families, all the beef was top-grade Wagyu, the pork was the finest product made by crossbreeding Duroc and Kumamoto black pork, and the shrimp were expensive ones that cost 5,000 yen per 100 grams. It was an excessively expensive price for a dozen people to eat at once, but I had a lot of money. I also had Danjo-halbae's corporate card, so I bought the barbecue ingredients with the corporate card.</p>
<p>After a short preparation, we sat around a huge grill table, put the meat and various ingredients we would eat in front of us, and waited for them to cook. The delicious smell of meat and shrimp cooking over charcoal stimulated our noses.</p>
<p>We prepared not only salt and pepper but also other seasonings and herbs so that each person could sprinkle them according to their taste.</p>
<p>,Hmm~! It's definitely delicious because lunch wasn't very good!,</p>
<p>,It's true that lunch wasn't very good when I came to Fuji-Q Highland on a middle school picnic.,</p>
<p>,Memories~!,</p>
<p>,Memories!,</p>
<p>While everyone was reminiscing about their middle school memories, Alik, who only had memories of being sold around to slave traders at that age, was diligently grilling and eating meat.</p>
<p>,Ryuta, ah~.,</p>
<p>Alik brought a well-grilled piece of meat to my mouth. I ate the meat that Alik grilled. Hmm, it's well-grilled meat. The meat sprinkled with the few seasonings and herbs that Alik chose was quite delicious.</p>
<p>,Is it delicious...?,</p>
<p>,Yes, it's very delicious.,</p>
<p>Alik makes a proud expression at my words. Next, he peels a shrimp and puts it in my mouth. When I say it's delicious again, he puts mushrooms and then sausages in my mouth. After putting one of each type of grill in my mouth, Alik nods his head with a satisfied expression as if he has accomplished something and begins to focus on his own meal.</p>
<p>,Alik, it's my turn next.,</p>
<p>Otagi Yuka, who was sitting across from me, held out meat that was rare and blue, with blood dripping from it. As expected from a vampire, she seemed to prefer it almost raw, with only the surface lightly seared. I ate the meat that Otagi Yuka offered. Actually, I usually don’t like it that rare, but the meat she prepared was delicious. She seemed to know the perfect sauce for it, probably because she often ate dishes like tataki, which are only seared on the outside. The combination of meat and sauce was really good.</p>
<p>,Oh, is it delicious?,</p>
<p>,It's Yuka's special sauce! It goes well with meat in this blue rare state.,</p>
<p>When I accepted Otagi Yuka's grill, the students who were focused on their meals looked at me, and after that, everyone fed me meat, shrimp, mushrooms, etc., as if it were a cooking contest and I was the judge. I was a little embarrassed that even Shimokawabe Airen and Kijimoto Jurie fed me meat.</p>
<p>It was convenient because they grilled the meat deliciously and put it in my mouth, but it was a bit burdensome. Still, I couldn't accept some and not accept others, so I accepted the grills that everyone gave me. I didn't say it out loud, but the dish of mushrooms and meat grilled together made by Shimokawabe Airen, who often cooks as a home tutor, was the most delicious. The rest also tasted above a certain level because the ingredients were expensive, so there was no chance of getting a bad dish and ruining my appetite.</p>
<p>After giving everyone a delicious rating, I got up from my seat when the prepared meat had decreased to some extent.</p>
<p>,Eh, Ryuta-zzi is a glutton. Did you already eat it all?,</p>
<p>Seizawa Shii looked at me with a puzzled expression as I got up from my seat. Of course, I didn't eat it all.</p>
<p>,No, since I'm a Korean language teacher, I'm going to make at least one Korean dish on this occasion.,</p>
<p>,Eh.... Ryuta-zzi always orders something. Can you cook?,</p>
<p>Seizawa Shii looks at me with a surprised expression. I was curious about what kind of person I was in Seizawa Shii's head, but I also cook sometimes. The dish I'm going to make this time isn't that difficult either....</p>
<p>,I can't do anything complicated, but when you eat meat in Korea, you usually eat cold noodles or stew, but today I'm going to make cold noodles.,</p>
<p>I was thinking of making cold noodles to refresh my greasy stomach because I had eaten a lot of top-grade Wagyu with a lot of fat. In fact, the broth was a ready-made product and the noodles were a product that only needed to be boiled, so it was more like ramen than cooking, but everyone looked at me with sparkling eyes as if they were looking forward to me cooking.</p>
<p>It's going to be a problem if you expect too much.... I told the students to wait while eating meat and boiled water. While the water was boiling, I divided the cold noodle broth into each bowl and boiled the noodles. I washed the quickly cooked noodles in cold water, then transferred them to each bowl and put a boiled egg on top. It would be nice to put thinly sliced boiled pork on top, but there's a lot of meat on the grill, so this much will be enough.</p>
<p>,Here. Eat it and add vinegar or mustard to your taste.,</p>
<p>,This time we'll evaluate Ryuta-zzi's cooking!,</p>
<p>In fact, I never said that I would evaluate the female students' noodles, but Seizawa Shii ate the cold noodles with an expression that showed she intended to pay her debt. There were also Morioka cold noodles made by Korean residents in Japan, but they were a bit greasy, so they were different from Korean cold noodles. There are also cold ramen noodles like Hiyashi Chuka, but they, too, are different from Korean cold noodles, making this dish unique. The students widened their eyes at the unexpectedly cool and refreshing taste.</p>
<p>,My mouth feels refreshed!,</p>
<p>,If this happens, I can eat more meat. Ryuta-zzi, are you trying to make us pigs?,</p>
<p>The cold noodles were well received. As expected, you have to eat cold noodles after eating meat. All meat after-meals like soybean paste stew, kimchi stew, or ramen, excluding cold noodles, were heretical.</p>
<p>I ate the meat wrapped with my cold noodles, looking at the students eating well with satisfaction.</p>
<p>After eating the cold noodles and finishing the meal, everyone patted their stomachs happily. When our meal was over, the mountain lodge owner brought out soft ice cream for dessert, so we ate dessert. We talked while eating dessert, and after various topics came up, the story of the school trip came up soon.</p>
<p>,This school trip will be the last one.,</p>
<p>,We're not going on a school trip, we're going on a graduation trip.,</p>
<p>,Where should we go this time?,</p>
<p>,We went to Europe in the first semester, so this time it will be Asia or America.,</p>
<p>St. Francisco Girls' Academy is a rich school, so they go on school trips twice a year, once in the first semester and once in the second semester, and the second semester school trip was in mid-November. This school trip was the last school trip, that is, the graduation trip, for the third graders.</p>
<p>When I checked the PMC certification exam that I was studying these days at the students' words, it was also in mid-November, but fortunately, the graduation trip was on the Monday after the weekend when the PMC certification exam was over. I'm glad the schedule doesn't overlap.</p>
<p>,Ryuta-zzi! Do you know where we're going on the graduation trip?,</p>
<p>,Well....,</p>
<p>In fact, I knew that there were several candidates. The school trip agenda is usually decided at the parents' meeting rather than the school. They have meetings such as, "My child hasn't been here, so I hope they can go," or "This place is not safe, so let's go somewhere else," and the final decision is made. I think the destination will be decided sometime next week.</p>
<p>After talking about the school trip for a while and then talking about another story, it was time for the company dinner to end. We paid extra money for a contract that the mountain lodge would do the dishes and cleanup, so we didn't have anything to do separately.</p>
<p>,Ryuta, I had fun today.,</p>
<p>In fact, this amusement park + company dinner was decided because Alik said he wanted to go to the amusement park, so it was enough if only Alik was satisfied.</p>
<p>,Let's come again next time.,</p>
<p>,Okay...!,</p>
<p>I stroked Alik's head and got into the driver's seat. It was time to go back to Miyashi.</p>
