<p>The crazed Jinildang-nim leader wrapped all the Jinildang-nim combatants approaching him with vine tentacles, absorbing their life force. As he did so, his size increased, reaching about 5 meters. This... does he get bigger the more life force he absorbs? Seriously scary.</p>
<p>"...!"</p>
<p>Kurayamada Kirie couldn't close her gaping mouth. She seemed incredibly shocked by the scene of her father killing all of his comrades. He's already completely dominated by that plant-like thing, so wouldn't it be impossible to call him her father anymore?</p>
<p>This wasn't good. That monster vine tentacle werewolf looked to be at least a mid-boss level. He might be weaker than Famine, the strongest being I've fought so far, but he seemed stronger than Dang So-hwa.</p>
<p>Of course, if Dang So-hwa's poison worked well, she might be able to win due to type advantage, but that werewolf vine monster's true form was a werewolf. Werewolves are naturally very resistant to poison and have excellent regeneration, so she would be similar to me in relation to Dang So-hwa. On top of that, he had the tentacles that absorbed life force from eating the roots of an unknown earthquake, and the earthquake power that shook the ground with every step, so it would be quite difficult for Dang So-hwa to win.</p>
<p>Then, could I beat that monster? I thought about all the abilities I had. Fist techniques, telekinesis, and the Abyss. If I were to classify and divide my combat abilities, it could be seen as largely consisting of three things.</p>
<p>Fist techniques and telekinesis are very useful and powerful forces, but they don't exert much power against that monster that's over 5 meters tall, only being effective against enemies the size of humans.</p>
<p>Of course, if fist techniques and telekinesis reached their peak, I could easily defeat that monster, but I wasn't quite there yet.</p>
<p>Then what's left is the Abyss. Only the powerful force of an Outer God could eliminate that monster. In my opinion, that monster over there also received the power of an Outer God from a different lineage than the Abyss and became like that...</p>
<p>Still, now, when I haven't had the power for long, was the opportunity. I had to eliminate him before he became completely accustomed to those vines and continued to absorb people's life force, growing even larger.</p>
<p>-Hun...gry...!</p>
<p>It had already become a mind-shaking sound that couldn't be called a human voice. Everyone seemed dazed by the sound, staggering.</p>
<p>"Everyone, snap out of it!"</p>
<p>I shouted to wake everyone up, then ignited the Abyss like flames all over my body. Right now, I probably look like black flames are fiercely erupting from black armor. I had never used the Abyss to its limit while fighting until now. Even though it was an energy source that allowed me to use techniques, I had used it with a feeling of infinity, without any shortage. But this time, I felt like I had to use it to the limit.</p>
<p>"Everyone, step back and attack from a distance!"</p>
<p>I told everyone except me to stay away, because if they went into close combat and had their life force sucked by the vines, it would give the enemy more buffs.</p>
<p>I approached the monster, passing by the withering, crumbling Jinildang-nim combatants.</p>
<p>Chwachwachwachwa!</p>
<p>As I approached, dozens, hundreds of vine tentacles shot at me. The black burning Abyss seemed to have resistance to the monster's life force absorption. Since what the monster was sucking in was life energy, it probably couldn't absorb the Abyss, which was a completely different otherworldly energy.</p>
<p>"Keuk!"</p>
<p>But even if it didn't absorb life force, the vine tentacles' strike was powerful. It was breaking through my armor and dealing physical damage.</p>
<p>I grabbed and tore the vines with my hands, or cut them with my hand blades, and got closer. As expected, the monster was looking at me, thinking that my Abyss energy was unusual. We made eye contact and approached each other.</p>
<p>His eyes, filled with madness, contained only hunger and animosity towards me.</p>
<p>Huuuung!!</p>
<p>Without warning, he swung a huge fist. It was such a fierce blow that I staggered from the wind pressure even though I dodged it. The surrounding trees fell with a roar from the monster's strike.</p>
<p>Shinkaiyatsude (Deep Sea Eight Hands) Fureashitsuyoha (Tentacle Vigor Wave: Tentacle Kinetic Wave)</p>
<p>I approached right under his feet and used the Shinkaiyatsude kicking technique to kick the monster's ankle. When I put in the maximum amount of Abyss, a black explosion occurred and a handful of the monster's ankle flesh fell off. I felt recoil due to the earthquake power, but it wasn't unbearable.</p>
<p>-Kuaaaa!</p>
<p>Apart from screaming in pain, countless root-like things came out of the injured flesh and began to repair the wound. Before I knew it, the injured area was filled with tree roots and became even stronger.</p>
<p>It was similar to regeneration, but with a slightly different feel. Instead of the injured area being restored to its original state, it was regenerating with a tree-like feel, and if he regenerated his entire body like this, he would become a tree monster instead of a werewolf.</p>
<p>"We will help too!"</p>
<p>Nakanoin Rina shouted and fired her gun. Judging by the blue trajectory, she seemed to be using expensive mithril bullets. Nice. Mithril bullets themselves are physically about 30 percent better in damage than regular bullets, but what's even better is the debuff they give when hit by a mithril bullet.</p>
<p>They were valuable bullets that reduced regeneration, weakened supernatural protection, and dealt additional damage to beings from other dimensions, allowing you to catch ghosts and spirits even when you were on a gun tree.</p>
<p>It seemed quite useful because it seemed like regeneration reduction and additional damage would be applied to that monster as well. Konoe Akina and Kurayamada Kirie also seemed to have received guns and mithril bullets from Nakanoin Rina, and were madly shooting mithril bullets at the monster.</p>
<p>"Fire Release! Demon Flame Burst!"</p>
<p>Sakagami Rena had awakened several ninjutsu techniques in her traits, and used fire release techniques that seemed to be weak against the monster, causing blue flames to explode. As the mithril bullets and fire release techniques harassed him in succession, the monster screamed and became increasingly angry.</p>
<p>Of course, I wasn't just standing still while my party members were attacking. I kept moving and attacking the lower body, tormenting the monster.</p>
<p>-Kuaaa! I'll kill you!!</p>
<p>Realizing that his true form was a werewolf, he took out claws from his front paws that were almost as big as Kurayamada Kirie's greatsword and began to swing them. The sharp claws, swung together with the vine tentacles in a well-coordinated attack, were incredibly fast and difficult to avoid.</p>
<p>Kakang!</p>
<p>The armor and claws collided, creating sparks. The speed also increased, and he took out claws that he hadn't used until now, so it seemed like it was phase 2.</p>
<p>As the enemy got stronger, I moved my body faster. I unleashed Shinkaiyatsude's moves in an instant, such as tentacle blades, One Finger Heaven Piercer, and Tentacle Kinetic Wave, and countered the claw strikes.</p>
<p>Peopeong!</p>
<p>The flesh under the monster's skin burst out along with the surrounding air. It seemed to be the effect of linking the techniques in an instant. But the monster also knocked away my claws while taking my attack. Because I was in the middle of attacking, I couldn't dodge, and the left shoulder of my armor broke, scattering the Abyss.</p>
<p>"Keuk!"</p>
<p>It was an amazing power to destroy the armor made by Simyeonui ttal. The armor could be repaired by pouring the Abyss when it was in bracelet form, but I was in the middle of a battle right now. I couldn't repair it right away, so I had to be careful of direct hits to the shoulder from now on.</p>
<p>If the flesh was injured, it would regenerate in an instant, but if my arm was cut off, it wouldn't grow back during the battle. Sufficient food and energy supply would have to come first for it to recover properly.</p>
<p>Still, I continued to attack the monster like a madman, linking Shinkaiyatsude's moves. The monster also disregarded defense and sprayed vine tentacles and sharp claws at me.</p>
<p>After moving for almost fifteen minutes without rest, attacking and dodging, I was very tired. Almost all of the armor except for the helmet was broken, and I could feel the Abyss, which I had thought was infinite until now, starting to dry up.</p>
<p>While I was making countless attacks, I also allowed the monster to attack me several times, one of which was so threatening that it activated the Yeouiju's instant death prevention ability. Now, I really couldn't allow a big hit. Because it would be immediate death.</p>
<p>From the rear, the gunshots had stopped at some point, probably because the bullets had run out. Or maybe it was because I was so close that they couldn't shoot? I didn't think it was the latter. It was a monster over 5 meters tall, so even if I was close, there would be plenty of places to shoot.</p>
<p>I felt while fighting that although the appearance was different, the opponent was similar to me in style. He was similar to me in that he had regeneration close to restoration and strong close-range attacks. Of course, I don't have tentacles like that, but I use tentacles from Shinkaiyatsude techniques, so that was similar too.</p>
<p>"I'll go again! Fire Release...! Demon Flame Burst!"</p>
<p>Even though the bullets had run out, Sakagami Rena's shout came with a burst of flames, as if she still had the power to use ninjutsu.</p>
<p>Since fire attacks dealt considerable damage due to their attribute, the monster did not ignore the fire release technique and neutralized Sakagami Rena's fire release technique by swinging its claws and spraying a sword wind-like energy.</p>
<p>I didn't miss the gap at that time and once again used several moves to pressure the monster. The monster's lower body no longer had the appearance of a werewolf. Only the hardened tree legs that had been regenerated could be seen. Still, the energy wasn't infinite, and the tree bark that was cracked or destroyed by being submerged in the Abyss began to appear.</p>
<p>As I was fighting, mindlessly swinging my fists and feet, I had an epiphany. Do I really need to use one move at a time? I thought that I could use the technique of drawing tentacles from the shadows along with the fist strike, just like that monster was swinging its claws and vine tentacles at the same time.</p>
<p>The sudden realization during the battle was also a clue to victory, but the momentary hesitation caused by the realization revealed a huge flaw.</p>
<p>-Die-!</p>
<p>In an instant, sharp claws grew larger and flew in front of my eyes. I'm going to die...! I felt death and had no choice but to close my eyes tightly.</p>
<p>But I didn't feel the pain that should have been felt right away, so when I opened my eyes, the monster was stopping its hand in the air as if caught in a net. The claws were stopped right in front of my nose, and if they had been swung just a little more, my face would have been split in half and I would have died.</p>
<p>"There's something inside the monster's solar plexus! I think that's the weak point. Attack quickly!"</p>
<p>I heard Konoe Akina's voice. When I glanced over, Konoe Akina was shedding tears of blood and emitting light from her red eyes. She seemed to have used her Mystic Eyes to momentarily suppress the monster's movements.</p>
<p>I stepped on the monster's knee and jumped up to not miss the opportunity that Konoe Akina had created while shedding tears of blood.</p>
<p>And I applied the realization I had just had and simultaneously used the fist technique Wave Breaking Blow, a Seiryuken technique, and Kakushi, a technique that draws tentacles from the shadows to pierce the opponent.</p>
<p>Shinkaiyatsude (Deep Sea Eight Hands) Fusion Secret Technique Jukagefurekanharo (Ten Shadow Tentacle Piercing Wave: Ten Shadow Tentacle Penetrating Wave)</p>
<p>Nine strands of Abyss energy in the form of cephalopod tentacles, poured out from the shadows along with the fist strike, struck the monster's solar plexus.</p>
<p>-Kwaaaa!</p>
<p>The solar plexus and chest were shattered by the Abyss, and a red heart tangled with root-like blood vessels was visible from the inside. It seemed that the red heart was what Konoe Akina had seen with her Mystic Eyes.</p>
<p>But as it had done until now, root-like things came out of the gaping chest wound and began to intertwine with each other, repairing the wound. Because I had used so much Abyss, I needed a few more seconds for the next attack, but it would be completely healed in the meantime.</p>
<p>Damn it...! Is this the end? Just as I was despairing, I heard a voice.</p>
<p>"I'm sorry, Father."</p>
<p>Along with Kurayamada Kirie's voice, her greatsword brushed past my ear and pierced into the heart.</p>
<p>-Kuaaaa! I'll kill you all...!</p>
<p>The monster screams with Kurayamada Kirie's greatsword stuck in its heart after finishing regeneration. The situation was desperate until just now, but it wasn't anymore. Kurayamada Kirie's sword, thrown to liberate her father, who was no different from being captured and killed by the monster, was hope.</p>
<p>Shinkaiyatsude (Deep Sea Eight Hands) Shintoohanin (Penetrating Palm Seal: Penetrating Palm Seal)</p>
<p>I used Penetrating Palm Seal, a type of Penetrating Vigor, to hit the sword with my palm and push it in. The Abyss energy that went through the sword began to burst the heart.</p>
<p>-Daughter.... I'm sorry...!</p>
<p>Before the Abyss energy exploded, the leader of Jinildang-nim, no, the monster, looked at Kurayamada Kirie with sad eyes. But before he could finish his words, the explosion began.</p>
<p>Kukukung!!</p>
<p>An explosion occurred inside the giant monster's torso. I let go of my hand and jumped back. The heart was the energy source that generated that infinite regeneration, so it caused a huge explosion.</p>
<p>With its chest completely pierced, the monster fell, knocking down the surrounding trees and old shrine buildings. I watched the monster fall and sat down on the spot. My legs had given out for a moment.</p>
