<p>"Since I've shown you a long weapon, now I suppose I should show you a relatively short one." What Ichimonji Harugoro took out of the box this time was a straight sword, said to be used by ninjas. If it was a Ninjato, then it was Sakagami Rena's turn for a weapon. Sakagami Rena also received the Ninjato with a bright expression upon seeing her weapon appear. When he drew the sword from its scabbard, a purple blade, like Sakagami Rena's hair, was visible. Its length was shorter than a typical Ninjato, seemingly made a bit shorter with portability in mind. Both the blade and the length were quite unique. "I named it Murasakiichi (Purple One)." ◆Murasakiichi (Purple One) [Epic+ Sword/Dagger] A ninja-style straight sword crafted by the excellent Master Ichimonji Harugoro. Made from a special alloy, its purple blade is impressive. At the request of the client, Sakagami Rena, sorcery ingredients were added to make it a Sorcery Sword that can also be used as a sorcery auxiliary tool, helpful even when using magic. ●Increases final Damage during Ambush. ●Increases Attack Speed. ●Increases Magic Casting Speed. ●Reduces Mental Power consumption of Magic. Oh...! Sakagami Rena had apparently ordered specific options. It seemed she requested these options because Sakagami Rena was a ninja and a magic-user who employed stealth and illusion magic. Of course, Ichimonji Harugoro wasn't a divine Master who could just conjure any options, but he possessed the skill to adjust ingredients and create weapons that closely matched desired options. Even so, this Murasakiichi seemed to have been perfectly crafted for Sakagami Rena, without a single useless option. "Thank you. I like it." "Other blacksmiths wouldn't have been able to make it as per your request." As Sakagami Rena, seemingly pleased, embraced the sword and bowed her waist, Ichimonji Harugoro praised himself, saying he had made it this well precisely because it was him. "Next is... right, the weapon for the Tairaga Daughter." In this cafe, there was only one Daughter of the Tairaga. Taira Kanna looked at Ichimonji Harugoro with a calm expression. Now that I thought about it, when we went to Kofu City to order swords, Taira Kanna had also ordered a secondary weapon. However, unlike Alik or Otagi Yuka, who possessed Relic-grade weapons, the rapier Taira Kanna used as her main weapon was crafted by Peter Jonathan, a blacksmith making a name for himself in the West. Ichimonji Harugoro had felt his pride wounded, challenging, "Just you wait and see who makes a better weapon." The rapier Ichimonji Harugoro took out was quite unique. In fact, with its ornate scabbard, it merely looked like a normal ceremonial Japanese sword, but when the sword was drawn, it was no Japanese sword. From the handle, wrapped in ray skin and bound with Leather Straps, to the guard, it had the appearance of a Japanese sword, but the blade itself was in the form of a rapier. Simply put, it looked like a crossbreed of a Japanese sword handle and a rapier's thin blade, yet it was crafted to blend seamlessly, not appearing alien at all. "Its name is Haritachi (Needle Sword)." Ichimonji Harugoro spoke with utter confidence, offering no further explanation beyond the name. ◆Haritachi (Needle Sword) [Unique Sword] A thin sword crafted with all the heart and soul of the excellent Master Ichimonji Harugoro, made to defeat his rival Peter Jonathan. It is a rapier made from an alloy of precious metals, including Mithril, and forged to be incredibly elastic and sturdy. ●Increases final Attack Speed. ●Increases Critical Probability and Damage. ●Not destroyed by weapons of equal or lower grade. ●Additional Damage proportional to Attack Speed. Looking at Haritachi's description, it seemed the swords he had given to the students until now hadn't received his full effort. After all, the weapon's Info Window description even included the additional phrase, 'made with all his heart and soul.' Moreover, while most weapons until now had been Epic+ grade, this was a Unique grade, one rank higher, serving as proof of how crucial the motivation of rivalry is, even for a Master. Of course, not only the Description Window and grade, but the options were also extraordinary. The last two options, in particular, were incredible. The "not destroyed" option, which rarely appears in thin sword categories, was an extremely rare option for thin sword-type weapons, which are quite prone to destruction due to their thinness, even with the condition of "equal or lower grade." With its high Unique grade, it seemed it would almost never be destroyed. And the additional damage proportional to attack speed was an option that maximized the strengths of most thin swordsmen, who possess high attack speed. In a word, it was a thin sword that compensated for weaknesses and maximized strengths. "...It's amazing." Taira Kanna, who hadn't held any particular high expectations, stroked Haritachi and admired it. "So, will you use this Haritachi as a secondary weapon?" Ichimonji Harugoro made an "ehem" expression while placing his hands on his waist, asking Taira Kanna. It was an action brimming with confidence that his weapon was superior to the one made by Peter Jonathan. "Fufufu, I'll make the weapon I originally used my secondary weapon and use this one." "Of course!" When Taira Kanna said she would use Haritachi, Ichimonji Harugoro clapped his hands and rejoiced. Of course, it was a Match Peter Jonathan knew nothing about, but Ichimonji Harugoro didn't care much. He believed that winning was what mattered. "Then it's the last one. Ah, before that, first..." Ichimonji Harugoro handed a long Bag to Shimokawabe Airen. As everyone looked on, wondering what it was, Ichimonji Harugoro opened his mouth to explain. "Kagiya Haruka's Disciple brought several swords I made and requested signatures, so I specially engraved Ichimonji (One Character) with gold inlay on the scabbard and tang parts before returning them." Ah, that's right. Shimokawabe Airen was Kagiya Haruka's Disciple, and Ichimonji Harugoro and Kagiya Haruka are said to have known each other since childhood. Ichimonji Harugoro spoke as if he and Kagiya Haruka were Lovers, but according to Shimokawabe Airen, Ichimonji Harugoro had an Unrequited love for Kagiya Haruka... Anyway, since their relationship hadn't been bad until now, he must have signed her original swords and even made new ones for her. "Then finally, the name of this sword is... Kamiwa (Divine Harmony)." Ichimonji Harugoro, with a slightly serious expression, took out the last Uchigatana from the box and handed it to Shimokawabe Airen. It was a quite ornate Japanese sword with a red scabbard, a red handle bound with red Leather, and a red hilt. By the way, Kamiwa (Divine Harmony), really...? It's one character each from Kagiya Haruka's (Divine House, Gentle Harmony) family name and given name. Just how much did you love her, Ichimonji old man?! ◆Kamiwa (Divine Harmony) [Unique+ Sword] A sword crafted with all the heart and soul of the excellent Master Ichimonji Harugoro for the Disciple of his old love, Kagiya Haruka (Divine House, Gentle Harmony). Pounding the hammer with the anguish of love and hate, he gained Enlightenment. Ichimonji Harugoro forged it by refining a secret Metal he had kept since long ago, along with Mithril. ●Increases the Damage of Kashimakiichishindoryu and reduces Stamina and Energy consumption. ●Not destroyed by weapons and skills of equal or lower grade. ●Increases final Damage. ●Can cut intangible beings. "...." I was speechless after seeing the options. Looking at Kamiwa, I came to realize that love was stronger than rivalry. Given the explanation that he had gained Enlightenment, it was clear that Ichimonji Harugoro had grown one step further while making this sword. Of course, an already accomplished Master growing one step further was a good thing from the perspective of doing Business together. The options were also as good as, no, even better than the Haritachi I had just seen, as its grade was about half a rank higher. It seemed to have been made exclusively for Shimokawabe Airen, with options that lacked nothing: increasing the Damage of Shimokawabe Airen's school, Kashimakiichishindoryu, reducing consumption, a conditional "not destroyed" option, a final Damage increase that acted as a multiplier, and even an attack function against intangible beings, which is one of the weaknesses of swordsmen who rely on physical force. "Thank you." Shimokawabe Airen, who received the sword, stood up from her Seat and bowed politely at the waist, giving a greeting. As an excellent Swordsman, Shimokawabe Airen seemed to know immediately how excellent the sword she had just received was. "Hehehe... I just made it because I received the request. By the way, when I came into the cafe, what was that talk about an Operation?" It was Ichimonji Harugoro, seemingly embarrassed to receive thanks from Shimokawabe Airen, who needlessly changed the subject. Since Ichimonji Harugoro had entered while we were in the middle of discussing the Anti-Anyaji Operation, it seemed he had overheard some of our conversation. "Well..." I roughly explained the current situation to Ichimonji Harugoro. Of course, because Ichimonji Harugoro had wide connections in the traditional martial arts world, he knew well what kind of organization Anyaji was, so the explanation was easy. "Be careful. I don't know much about Fist Technique practitioners, but Anyaji's Mad dog Nobuchi Tamehito (Wild Abyss, For One) and Sword Beast Senno Yoridake (Recommend Field, Rely on Courage) are incredibly strong Swordsmen. They are among Japan's top Swordsmen, so whoever faces them, it would be wise to be cautious." Ichimonji Harugoro, after listening to my explanation, warned us and spoke about the two Swordsmen. Of course, I had heard of both from Athena Mannerheim, but as Athena Mannerheim hadn't had much interest in Anyaji until now, I didn't know their exact Skill. Listening to Ichimonji Harugoro's words, they seemed like formidable practitioners who could never be underestimated. I also heard their nicknames for the first time-Mad dog and Sword Beast, really? They certainly aren't nicknames given with good intentions. "Of course, we must be careful. As we received the weapons, time has already passed so much. Today, I will treat you all, so let's go eat dinner. We can continue the rest of the Story during the meal." As we received the swords one by one, it was already Mealtime. Since he had generously crafted Epic-grade weapons like Santa Grandfather, I felt I should treat him to a good meal. I stood up from my Seat and, with everyone, headed to the reserved restaurant.</p>
