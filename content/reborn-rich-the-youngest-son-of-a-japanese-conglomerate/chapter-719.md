<p>"Tairaga Industries, which had a bad situation at the beginning of this year, has also recovered its profitability. Well done."</p>
<p>Taira Danjo looked at Taira Ichiro and opened his mouth while they were eating again. It seemed like they were slowly entering the latter half of the meal and were about to evaluate the achievements of the past year, 2053.</p>
<p>"Yes, I'm glad the economy is improving."</p>
<p>"The US and Latin American branches are also doing well."</p>
<p>The US and Latin American branches were managed by Taira Tabenobu and Taira Gensuke, respectively, but since both were Taira Ichiro's sons, the praise was directed at Taira Ichiro. After all, both branches were heavily influenced by Taira Ichiro.</p>
<p>"Other affiliates are also doing well... Do as well this year, '54, as you did last year."</p>
<p>"Understood, Father."</p>
<p>Taira Ichiro bowed his head but bit his lip slightly. Taira Danjo was a man of clear rewards and punishments. If he was truly satisfied with Taira Ichiro's performance this year, he would have given him something, but the fact that he passed without giving anything meant that there was something wrong or something he didn't like.</p>
<p>But if he was showing this reaction despite the clear significant achievements this year, it meant that Chairman Taira Danjo was keeping Taira Ichiro in check.</p>
<p>"Ah, I didn't mention that. Gocho Kikuhito, who was in charge of Shizuoka and Yamanashi, died, so Tsuboya Kanemune, who was under Fumimoto, will take over. If you have any business there, contact Tsuboya and ask."</p>
<p>'This is it...!'</p>
<p>Taira Danjo introduced Tsuboya Kanemune, who would be in charge of the new Shizuoka-Yamanashi information team, as if he was mentioning something he had momentarily forgotten. He acted like it wasn't important information, but Taira Ichiro realized that this introduction of Tsuboya Kanemune was a reprimand to him.</p>
<p>Originally, the information agent that Taira Ichiro had been infiltrating for a long time was supposed to become the team leader as the successor to Gocho Kikuhito, whom he had assassinated by disguising it as a suicide, but that fell through and an agent under Fumimoto Takayuki, a henchman of Taira Danjo, became the team leader.</p>
<p>He didn't know how he found out, but it seemed like he realized that Taira Ichiro was the one who killed Gocho Kikuhito.</p>
<p>'I'll have to go back and discuss it with Kurinami.'</p>
<p>While Taira Ichiro was having many thoughts inside due to the unexpected situation, it was time to evaluate Taira Kokoro, Taira Danjo's second child and also a contender in the succession race.</p>
<p>"Kokoro."</p>
<p>"Yes, Father!"</p>
<p>"I heard that the department store is booming and the entertainment business is doing well. I will officially entrust you with the cosmetics business that I temporarily entrusted to you."</p>
<p>"Thank you! I won't let you down."</p>
<p>Taira Kokoro smiled broadly and bowed to Taira Danjo. As Taira Danjo said, Taira Kokoro's business was doing well this year. Taira Entertainment was also doing well, and she received good reviews for greatly expanding the department store business. In return, she was officially appointed as the president of Taira Cosmetics, which she had been temporarily in charge of.</p>
<p>'Not good.'</p>
<p>Taira Ichiro and Taira Jiro had the same thought in their minds at the same time after hearing Taira Danjo's story. This was a pretty serious matter.</p>
<p>Taira Cosmetics was not a very large business when looking at the entire Taira Group, but it was a company strongly connected to Taira Bio, one of the two companies that controlled all of the Taira Group's shares, Taira Robotics and Taira Bio.</p>
<p>Taira Ichiro and Taira Jiro didn't like being grouped with Taira Kokoro as the three successors. They had been ignoring Taira Kokoro, saying that she was only doing entertainment business.</p>
<p>However, as a reward for the growth of Taira Department Store this time, Taira Kokoro became the president of Taira Cosmetics, taking a half step ahead of the other successors.</p>
<p>Of course, they thought it was only half a step and they could overtake that difference at any time, but they were shocked to be hit by someone they had been underestimating.</p>
<p>While everyone here was thinking about how the succession structure would change in the future, Taira Danjo started evaluating Taira Jiro's performance last year without paying attention to it.</p>
<p>"Jiro, you know your evaluation well, right? You did well in the second half, but you ruined the first half, so it's plus or minus zero."</p>
<p>"I know, Father. I'll do better this year."</p>
<p>Taira Jiro had his management rights of Taira Steel recovered by Taira Danjo because of his past of being played by Minamoto's spy.</p>
<p>However, he was able to get Taira Steel back by succeeding in several tasks assigned by Taira Danjo with great effort in the second half and greatly expanding the remaining businesses.</p>
<p>He got Taira Steel back, but it seemed like he still couldn't be forgiven for what he had suffered from Minamoto.</p>
<p>But Taira Jiro had a smiling expression as if he was satisfied with getting back Taira Steel, which could be said to be his main force.</p>
<p>Of course, he was grinding his teeth inside and vowing to be at the top of the succession structure in 2054.</p>
<p>"Yukiko,"</p>
<p>"Yes, Father-in-law."</p>
<p>"The Human Resources Development Institute is not a place to produce results, so it's a pass just for operating it stably. I believe you will do well next year as well."</p>
<p>"Yes. I will operate it without any problems, Father-in-law."</p>
<p>Taira Yukiko, the widow of the deceased Taira Saburo, was able to continue to hold the position of Director of the Human Resources Development Institute in 2054. In a way, it was natural because she mostly agreed with Taira Danjo's evaluation.</p>
<p>However, what the three successors felt towards Taira Yukiko was a sense of incongruity. She didn't seem like Yukiko, who was always nervous in the main house, but rather had the composure of a big shot.</p>
<p>In fact, the Human Resources Development Institute was a very important organization in the Taira Group, but it was an institution that couldn't exert much power in the succession structure, so it was far from everyone's attention.</p>
<p>In addition, everyone's attention was on Taira Kanna's turn, so the changed atmosphere of Taira Yukiko was quickly forgotten.</p>
<p>"Kanna. Congratulations on becoming an adult."</p>
<p>"Yes. Thank you for the congratulations, Father."</p>
<p>Taira Kanna was not yet involved in the Taira Group, so she was able to escape Taira Danjo's cold evaluation.</p>
<p>But everyone in this place knew. That Taira Saburo died and 70% of Taira Saburo's faction swore allegiance to Taira Kanna.</p>
<p>Taira Kanna was the head of a faction that was among the top five in the Taira Group, even though she had not yet entered the Taira Group. Of course, it was not as big as the other three forces, but all three of them were watching Taira Kanna's actions.</p>
<p>"Yukiko, Ryuta is not here, so please tell him instead. Tell him to finish the mission well."</p>
<p>"Yes, I will definitely tell him."</p>
<p>Everyone here knew that Taira Ryuta had received some kind of mission from Taira Danjo and couldn't come because of that mission.</p>
<p>It was taboo in the Tairaga Family to ask about or interfere with the mission given by Taira Danjo, so everyone pretended not to be interested in Taira Ryuta's mission.</p>
<p>In fact, Taira Ichiro had been receiving information about Taira Ryuta's whereabouts while briefly working with Gocho Kikuhito, and knew that Taira Ryuta was doing something at St. Francisco's Girls' Academy, and Taira Kokoro also happened to meet Taira Ryuta at St. Francisco's Girls' Academy and also knew that he was on a mission at St. Francisco's Girls' Academy.</p>
<p>Both of them were just satisfied with finding out where Taira Ryuta, who had not been seen, was, and didn't pay much attention to Taira Ryuta. They knew that he was running a small business called Hatake Investment, but they knew that it wouldn't affect the big picture.</p>
<p>Among the successors, only Taira Jiro didn't know what mission Taira Ryuta was doing.</p>
<p>Taira Jiro put food in his mouth and slyly rolled his eyes to steal a glance at Taira Ichiro and Taira Kokoro's expressions.</p>
<p>He was the only one who was curious about Taira Ryuta's mission, and both of them were eating peacefully with expressions that showed no interest in Taira Ryuta's mission.</p>
<p>Seeing that, Taira Jiro realized that all the other successor rivals knew what Taira Ryuta was doing, and he was the only one who didn't know about Taira Ryuta's mission.</p>
<p>'Damn it! I was so focused on getting the steel back that I didn't pay attention to the other side, and I fell behind in information. I have to find out somehow. I'll have to join hands with Ryuta in the end...'</p>
<p>Taira Jiro, who was running Taira Steel, was thinking that he would have to join hands with Taira Ryuta to receive Mithril from Taira Mining, which belonged to Hatake Investment Company founded by Taira Ryuta.</p>
<p>In fact, he could have taken away his youngest nephew's small business by force, but he heard that Taira Danjo's current wife, Taira Shizuku, cherished Taira Ryuta.</p>
<p>In addition, there were still 30% of the former Taira Saburo forces who did not follow Taira Kanna, so if he forcibly took the mine from Taira Ryuta, his position in the group would weaken. The remaining 30% of Taira Saburo's forces would never stick to Taira Jiro.</p>
<p>He was going to find out information about the mission because it would be helpful to know what mission his nephew was doing.</p>
<p>"Let's finish the meal now. There are guests waiting."</p>
<p>When Taira Danjo mentioned the dignitaries gathered at the Taira main house and put down his chopsticks, the rest of the family took a small sigh of relief as the annual evaluation of all the family members was over and the tense breakfast was also over. That's how heavy Taira Danjo's charisma was.</p>
