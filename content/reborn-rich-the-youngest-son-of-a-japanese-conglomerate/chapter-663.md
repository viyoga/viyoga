<p>25 minutes ago.</p>
<p>,Wow...! We've already sold 7,000 copies of the Ryuta-tchi version! And over 4,000 of the Karen version... At this rate, we'll have to request additional production, won't we?",</p>
<p>Seizawa Shii, who was checking the remaining stock, said in surprise to Minamoto Risa. It was possible because it was just past ten o'clock and there were over three hundred customers. Since each person bought at least 20 to 60 copies, a total of 10,000 copies were sold.</p>
<p>,The people who were going to buy them in the beginning will have already bought them, so there's no need to order more. But just in case, should we add about a thousand or two thousand sets?",</p>
<p>,I think that would be a good idea. Two thousand sets!",</p>
<p>Minamoto Risa wasn't very shaken because she had expected that these kinds of merchandise would sell incredibly well at the beginning of the festival. She wasn't happy about this early success because it was obvious that these sales figures wouldn't continue and would gradually slow down.</p>
<p>,Two thousand sets of just the A card pack... No, I'll order three thousand sets. How long will it take? Around the end of lunch? Okay.",</p>
<p>Minamoto Risa, who had called the printing company to request only the card packs, hung up and went down to the first-floor cafe.</p>
<p>Otagi Yuka was coming out of the cafe with a heavy-looking large box. Minamoto Risa smiled as she looked at Otagi Yuka.</p>
<p>,Success?",</p>
<p>,Yeah. Sensei-ssi is surprisingly helpful.",</p>
<p>Otagi Yuka took the box to the education preparation room. The box was full of club magazines that had only been unwrapped.</p>
<p>,When Sensei-ssi told me the story about how when sticker bread was popular in the old days, people only took the stickers and threw away the bread, I didn't really believe it. It's a bit of a shame to see the club magazines being thrown away like this.",</p>
<p>,They're not being thrown away, they're being recycled.",</p>
<p>Since homeroom teacher Yamashiro Ryuta had predicted that many people would throw away the club magazines because each person was buying at least 20 copies, they had all discussed it separately and decided to create a trash can specifically for club magazines. Unlike bread, club magazines can be recycled.</p>
<p>Then there would be no need to print the club magazines separately, and they could just be packaged with the card packs that would be delivered later and sold again, which would be financially beneficial and reduce the number of club magazines being thrown away, killing two birds with one stone.</p>
<p>,This can just be left here to be packaged with the card packs when they arrive later... I'll go back up.",</p>
<p>,Okay. I'll go check if there's anything I can help with at the cafe.",</p>
<p>Minamoto Risa entrusted the recycling of the club magazines to Otagi Yuka and headed back to the second floor via the stairs. Suddenly, she noticed some of the people lining up to buy the club magazines, and the first thing she saw was Taira Kanna standing in line with the people who had come to buy the club magazines between the first and second floors.</p>
<p>,Kanna. Are you here to buy the magazine too?",</p>
<p>,That's part of it, but I actually came to see how you're doing business, but I can't cut in line, so I'm going to stand in line.",</p>
<p>Since the St. Francisco Girls' Academy Festival is organized by the student council, it was common for the Chairman to walk around and inspect things, so Minamoto Risa nodded, thinking that was the case.</p>
<p>,Risa, this person is the president of our Tairaga Foods. She's friends with me, so she came to enjoy the festival for a while.",</p>
<p>,Greetings, I am Risa of the Minamoto family.",</p>
<p>When Taira Kanna introduced the beautiful, gray-haired woman standing next to her, Minamoto Risa greeted her politely. Even though the relationship between Minamoto and Tairaga wasn't good, Minamoto Risa couldn't greet her impolitely, so she greeted her politely.</p>
<p>,Hehe, you know, I'm Nagumo Sian. Is Ushin-oraboni doing well?",</p>
<p>Of course, Minamoto Risa knew. Nagumo Sian, who is called the godmother in Akita Prefecture, which can be said to be the base of Tairaga Foods, was called a witch in the Minamoto Group.</p>
<p>She was the one who brought Tairaga Foods, which had been far behind Minamoto Foods, to a similar level, along with her mysterious appearance that didn't age even after decades, so they gave her the nickname witch with hatred and fear.</p>
<p>Even now, it seemed very bizarre that she, who looked like she was in her early thirties or late twenties, was calling Minamoto Ushin "oraboni." Minamoto Risa said, "He's doing well," bowed her head, and went back up the stairs.</p>
<p>But she couldn't walk a few steps and stopped again, this time seeing Sakagami Rena standing in line.</p>
<p>,Sakagami, why are you standing in line?",</p>
<p>,Ah, you said it was okay to leave your seat for a while if you have relatives or personal guests. My master came, so I'm going to show them what we're selling.",</p>
<p>Since it was an open festival, students were allowed to leave their seats if they had personal guests. Of course, this was only allowed when there were people waiting in line.</p>
<p>,Hello, I'm Minamoto Risa, a friend of Sakagami.",</p>
<p>,Ah, yes. I'm Takinogawa, Rena's master.",</p>
<p>This one was also a young woman. Seeing how young she was, Minamoto Risa wondered what kind of master she was, but since she knew that Sakagami Rena was a ninja, she concluded that Takinogawa must be a master in that field.</p>
<p>As she climbed the stairs again, the next student she encountered was Konoe Akina, and this time there were quite a few guests. There was a woman with blue hair and a woman with orange hair, two red-haired women who looked like sisters, and a white woman with purple hair.</p>
<p>,Are they all guests?",</p>
<p>,Yeah. They're the PMC agents who rescued me when I was kidnapped.",</p>
<p>The Konoe family's kidnapping was an incident that caused some suspicion in prestigious families. There were disturbing rumors that the next head of the family's kidnapping was ordered by his uncle. When everyone thought that Konoe Akina had died after a year of no news, Konoe Akina was rescued and returned.</p>
<p>Konoe Akina, who returned in that way, returned to the family after a year, killed her uncle who had usurped the position of head of the family (it is said that he committed suicide externally), and became the head of the Konoe family, which became quite a hot topic.</p>
<p>,Thank you for saving Konoe.",</p>
<p>,It's only natural. I've heard a lot about Minamoto-ssi. I'm Sawayanagi Azuma, the president of Seiryu Military Company.",</p>
<p>Minamoto Risa was only able to return to the club room after exchanging business cards with Sawayanagi Azuma.</p>
<p>,Nothing special going on?",</p>
<p>,A few kids went out for a while because their acquaintances came to visit?",</p>
<p>,They're all standing in line here.",</p>
<p>,Ahahaha, really? They should look around at places that don't have lines first. There's no need to stand in line when there are so many customers.",</p>
<p>Minamoto Risa spoke to Seizawa Shii and entered the integrated management app that she had created separately for this business to check the current sales.</p>
<p>8,000 copies of club magazine A, 5,000 copies of club magazine B, a total of 300 drinks sold... In terms of money, it was about 16 million yen.</p>
<p>,Ahahaha... Was it this easy?",</p>
<p>Minamoto Risa laughed as if she was a little dumbfounded. The reason she planned this club magazine was to pass the Minamoto family's test. She had spent about 900,000 yen on this project, so she had almost passed the Minamoto Group's test, which required her to earn more than 10 million yen with an investment of less than 1 million yen.</p>
<p>Of course, half of the profits from club magazine B, which accounted for a significant portion of the sales, had to be donated, and she also had to pay labor costs to the club friends who helped her, so she hadn't exactly passed the Minamoto Group's test yet.</p>
<p>But she was deeply moved that she could pass the test in just a little over an hour. There was something Minamoto Risa had to do in the Minamoto Group. But Minamoto Risa's feelings didn't last long.</p>
<p>,Yahoo! Hello!",</p>
<p>Before she knew it, the line had shortened, and one of Konoe Akina's group, whom she had greeted earlier, was standing at the counter. Of course, the people she had met so far had been standing in line, so they quickly met again as the line shortened.</p>
<p>It was a woman with orange hair and large sunglasses standing at the counter, but they hadn't introduced themselves yet.</p>
<p>,We're selling club magazines here. You only need to buy one.",</p>
<p>,This is A? Give me 500 copies of this.",</p>
<p>,Huh?",</p>
<p>Minamoto Risa asked again, thinking she had misheard. The orange-haired sunglasses woman laughed, spread out her hand, showed all five fingers, and said again, "500 copies~!"</p>
<p>,Ah... 500 copies, it's 1,200 yen per copy, so it's 600,000 yen.",</p>
<p>,Here you go~!",</p>
<p>The orange-haired sunglasses woman, who had prepared the cash in advance, immediately took out a wad of 10,000 yen bills and paid. Although it was a thin book, 500 copies were heavy, so she had Seizawa Shii take them to the car that the orange-haired sunglasses woman had parked.</p>
<p>,Hello.",</p>
<p>,Ah, Sawayanagi-ssi. How many...?",</p>
<p>,I'm not going to buy as many as the person just now. Just about 100 copies of club magazine A...",</p>
<p>She couldn't understand why the president of a PMC company would want to buy a club magazine with a photo of Yamashiro Ryuta's face as a bonus, but it was less shocking than the 500 copies just now, so she didn't think much of it and gave it to her. Then the red-haired sisters bought 80 copies, and the purple-haired white woman bought 40 copies.</p>
<p>,I bought a card set, so I'm going to look around with my acquaintances?",</p>
<p>,Yeah... Did your acquaintances buy them to help the club?",</p>
<p>,Well, something like that?",</p>
<p>She didn't know why she was putting a question mark in her answer, but Minamoto Risa understood it as her wealthy acquaintances spending money on her close friend's club event.</p>
