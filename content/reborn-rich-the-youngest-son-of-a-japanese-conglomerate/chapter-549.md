<p>Naruse Mansion's Head of family's Hall was in the deepest part of the mansion. While most of Naruse Mansion was built in modern Western style, the Head of family's Hall was a Japanese-style building, so it was easily recognizable. Is Naruse Hirohide's Preference Japanese style...? As Naruse Koyou was currently saying he was conducting an inspection and had summoned all the Security Guards, we were able to approach the Head of family's Hall without any interference.</p>
<p>However, because there were the Head of family's direct Guards, we had no choice but to start the Battle from near the Head of family's Hall. When we got somewhat close, quite a number of Guards armed with rifles began to shoot their Guns.</p>
<p>"Ryutta, can Alik eliminate them?"</p>
<p>"Yes, go ahead." For several years with Slave Traders, and for several years before that in the reverse Western era of the northern Korean Peninsula where Marauders roamed, Alik had lost her mom and wandered alone, so she had no qualms about Murder. When I asked her before, she said her first Murder was at twelve years old. Yet, to grow up so upright and Cute, our Alik was truly the best. As I gave permission, Alik ran ahead.</p>
<p>Countless Marksmanship barrages followed Alik, who suddenly darted out, but all the Bullets that touched Alik's unique Charge Barrier melted and fell to the Ground. Before the molten Bullets fell to the Ground, I floated them in empty space with Telekinesis and shot them back. I kept practicing Telekinesis, so now I can do this much in an instant. Of course, since I shot molten Lead without physical power, its killing power wasn't much, but the Guys who couldn't avoid it and got hit cried "Aht-tteugeo" and were temporarily incapacitated.</p>
<p>This much is enough. The rest, the Kids who came with me, will take care of. While Alik was wildly swinging her sword with an Electricity-infused Impact, becoming a Calamity that caused electrocution and Death simultaneously, Otagi Yuka also unleashed Blood Gi with the Golden Sword, the Vampire's exclusive weapon she obtained in New York. Once a wound was inflicted, it was over.</p>
<p>After causing a Blood explosion with the Authority of Blood on a bleeding Enemy, the Enemies wounded by that Blood explosion also became targets for Blood explosion activation. As expected, it was truly Vampire-like, almost unrivaled against weaklings without such Jusul Protection. Of course, Otagi Yuka was a High Blood, close to the Otagi Head of family's Bloodline, which allowed her to exert this much power, but a normal Vampire could not do this much.</p>
<p>In contrast, Saionji Diana held a Shield and a rifle, set up her Shield beside me, and fired shots. Compared to the other two's performance, it was modest, but with Muscle strength enhanced by cyber implants, she was sweeping the area, holding a Shield in one hand and a light machine Gun in the other. To hold a light machine Gun with one hand.... Cyber implants were indeed good. Saionji Diana also seemed to have decided on her combat style.</p>
<p>With the overwhelming physical Ability unique to cyber-implant Characters, she seemed to be aiming for a style proficient in both long-range and close-range, clearing long distances with heavy but powerful Weapons, and cutting down approaching Guys with Swordsmanship.</p>
<p>Kuung...!</p>
<p>While we were massacring the Head of family's Guards, Naruse Hirohide seemed to have appeared, as a heavy Gi wave swept the Surroundings. From behind the Wall, thousands of Paper Shikigami suddenly burst out like butterflies taking flight, and began to assist the Guards, causing the rate at which the Guards were dying to decrease. After the Paper Shikigami appeared, Yogoe of the Tetsuki class, like the ones I had fought, appeared. The Paper Shikigami seemed to have been Summoned for the purpose of buying some time.</p>
<p>"Teacher-ssi. Leave this to us and go finish off Naruse Head of family!" Otagi Yuka shouted at me, swinging her Blood Gi-blazing Sword. Yes, Naruse Hirohide wields dozens, hundreds of Shikigami, so fighting like this would only delay time. I ignored the pouring Bullets and Paper Shikigami and ran towards the Head of family's Hall. Since I was also wearing the cyber Suit Eve had prepared for me, I could cross the Wall and enter the Head of family's Hall without being harmed by the gunfire.</p>
<p>Huung!</p>
<p>The Moment I crossed the Wall and landed on the floor, a one-eyed Yogoe wearing a Bamboo Hat and a Sword swung its Sword at me. I instantly reached out my Hand, deflected the blade, and struck the Yogoe's Abdomen. Because I struck it with the Abyss fully imbued, the Yogoe was incapacitated in one blow. The more Shikigami of Naruse Hirohide I killed, the fewer Yogoe Naruse Koyou would inherit, so I intended not to kill them if possible, like the Tetsuki I had just fought.</p>
<p>Since I could always get Help from Naruse Koyou once he became Head of family, I should help him maintain strong forces. Of course, if there was no other Method than killing, I would act mercilessly.</p>
<p>"Are you the Cause of this commotion?" It was a middle-aged to elderly man wearing a kariginu. Looking at him like this, he resembled Naruse Ginji very much. I didn't know if he knew about Naruse Ginji's Death or not, but his Gaze at me was very cold.</p>
<p>"No, the Cause of this commotion is you." If he hadn't used Naruse Shiyo to Seal the Dragon in the first place, today's events wouldn't have happened. Of course, if I talked like this, it would go as far as saying Japan shouldn't have traded with the West in the first place, which could be called Forced.... That was none of my business. I was just here as a Teacher to save my Student.</p>
<p>"Hmph, just die." Seeing me being Forced, he seemed to think Words were no longer effective, so Naruse Hirohide instantly Summoned three Yogoe. A giant Yogoe, appearing over 3 meters tall, wearing General Armor and a Helmet, an Immense Size snake that seemed to declare "I am an Imoogi!", and a golden-furred horangi, also boasting considerable Size, were blocking my path. Judging by the Momentum I felt, they seemed to be one Stage higher Shikigami than Tetsuki. If it's just one Stage higher, even if it's 3 against 1, I can handle them alone.</p>
<p>When at a numerical Disadvantage like this, preemptive strike was key. I first rushed in and punched the Furrowed Brow of the golden horangi at the very front with my fist.</p>
<p>-Keuheong!</p>
<p>The horangi, unable to bear the pain, shed a few Tears and was sent flying, and next was the giant Samurai.</p>
<p>Huung!</p>
<p>It was a Swordsmanship Attack of a different Dimension than the Bamboo Hat-wearing Swordsman Yogoe from before. Should I have brought Shimokawabe Airen? She had a bit of a battle-maniac tendency, and I think she would have really enjoyed a Sword fight here. As expected, because it was an Opponent using Technology, it was quite difficult to incapacitate. Especially, I don't know what its Armor was made of, but it was effectively blocking my Fist Technique imbued with the Abyss.</p>
<p>In the meantime, the golden horangi that had been sent flying recovered from its pain and attacked me. Since a three-pronged Attack would make it even harder, I glanced to see what the Imoogi was doing, and fortunately, it was coiled around Naruse Hirohide, protecting him. One was on Defense, and the others were on Attack, it seemed.</p>
<p>-Keu-heo-heong!</p>
<p>The Sole of the foot, appearing almost twice the Size of a human Face, swung and approached my Eyes. I grabbed the horangi's Ankle and brought it against the giant Samurai's Sword, which was slashing at my Back.</p>
<p>Chang!</p>
<p>The blade clashed with the horangi's Claw, and Flame erupted. I swung my Foot at the horangi, whose Ankle I held, and kicked the Internal organs part below its ribs, unprotected by bone.</p>
<p>Shinkaiyatsude (Deep Sea Eight Hands) Hureashitsuyoha (Touching Leg Strong Wave)</p>
<p>As the Shinkaiyatsude Martial Arts Form unfolded, a wave of the Abyss surged out from where I had swung my Foot and struck, sweeping the Surroundings. The horangi was sent flying, crying "Keu-heo-heong," and with that recoil, I too was pushed and flew towards where the giant Samurai was.</p>
<p>Shinkaiyatsude (Deep Sea Eight Hands) Kuroyamamota (Black Mountain Lean)</p>
<p>Using the receding force, I unleashed Kuroyamamota, the Shinkaiyatsude version of Iron Mountain that strikes with the Back and Shoulder, and struck the giant Samurai's Solar Plexus with my Back.</p>
<p>Kwaaang!</p>
<p>An enormous roar was heard, and the Armor burst open. As expected, using the Shinkaiyatsude Martial Arts Form was much stronger than just imbuing with the Abyss and striking. I then turned my Body and used the Kuroyamamota combo Martial Arts Form that strikes with the Elbow.</p>
<p>Shinkaiyatsude (Deep Sea Eight Hands) Kaitenchuhure (Rotating Elbow Touch)</p>
<p>My Elbow dug into the already damaged Armor, which had been hit once by Kuroyamamota. The Armor completely burst open, and the giant Samurai clutched its Belly and knelt.</p>
<p>Chwaaaah!</p>
<p>The Moment I struck the giant Samurai with my Elbow, I heard a sound of Something being shot-it was the Imoogi coiling and uncoiling its Body like a spring, shooting towards me. I slightly dodged the snake's Head shooting towards me, like a bullfighter, and wrapped its Nape with both Arms. Since the snake was already shooting forward, I, holding its Nape tightly, was dragged along with it and crashed into the Wall of the Head of family's Hall.</p>
<p>Kwaang!</p>
<p>The Wall collapsed, and Dust billowed thickly. Whether the Wall collapsed or all the buildings of Naruse Mansion collapsed, it was none of my concern. I kept my Arms tightly constricted. The Imoogi, feeling a press-machine-like Pressure on its Neck, thrashed wildly like a salted loach, but soon the Blood flow to its Brain was cut off, its Rhythmic Motion gradually dulled, and then it became quiet. I released my Hands from its Neck, stood up, and approached Naruse Hirohide.</p>
<p>"Judging by your Body type, you're not Fujigasaki himself, but a Disciple?" Seeing him mention my Sabu's name, he seemed to have recognized my Fist Technique. Yeah, I won't go easy even if you know my Sabu. Still, since he might be an Acquaintance of my Sabu, should I spare his life?</p>
<p>"If you pass on your Shikigami to Koyou-ssi and retire, I will spare your life."</p>
<p>"Are you so arrogant after merely dealing with three of my Shikigami?" Of course, because I had heard from Naruse Koyou, I knew that Naruse Hirohide still had a lot of power remaining. I was just trying to spare his life because he was someone my Sabu knew. To not recognize another's goodwill, it seemed he lacked sensitivity towards the weak, perhaps because he had been the Head of family for too long, and an overwhelmingly stronger Head of family than anyone else in the Family. It seemed he didn't have even 1% of a thought that he could lose.</p>
<p>Of course, I had never once thought that I could lose either.</p>
