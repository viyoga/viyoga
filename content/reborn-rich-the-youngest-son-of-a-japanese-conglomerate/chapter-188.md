<p>Senboku City, Senboku Municipal Middle School.</p>
<p>As it was July, the school was empty due to summer vacation, so the Kurotsukido Hall had nested here without any interference. Currently, Tang Xiaohua, the Northeast Asia Branch Manager of Kurotsukido Hall, who was in charge of the battle against Taira Foods, was sitting in the school library, receiving a report from a subordinate.</p>
<p>"The East Weapon bastards from the western front are invading Kakunodate!"</p>
<p>"Hmph...! With so few of them, and not even all of them, but only the guys on the western front are invading? What are they thinking?"</p>
<p>Tang Xiaohua tilted her head, seemingly not understanding. Although the quality of the lower-ranking soldiers of Kurotsukido Hall wasn't great, they had more numbers, and they had ability users here, and although she hadn't revealed it yet, if she stepped up, she could wipe out all the remaining Taira Foods forces alone. Of course, the opponent didn't know that she, an incredible superhuman, was sitting here in the school library.</p>
<p>The Kurotsukido Hall messenger who was reporting was looking at Tang Xiaohua with a slightly fearful expression. Tang Xiaohua was always wearing a cyber suit, being a battle maniac, and her personality wasn't good either, so he was nervous.</p>
<p>But what made Tang Xiaohua more frightening than her personality was her ability.</p>
<p>Tang Xiaohua, who uses the Poison Art, a secret technique of the Tang family that fell during World War III, uses a powerful poison that can melt people without a trace, so the messenger himself was afraid of being melted away.</p>
<p>Tang Xiaohua had many legends associated with her. Starting as a low-ranking soldier in Kurotsukido Hall, she quickly stood out in the Yangcheon Battle, where she killed a hundred people, and the Tianjin Battle, where she killed 200 with a single poisoning, and continued to demonstrate her skills, rising through the ranks to the position of branch manager, which could be considered an executive position in Kurotsukido Hall. It was unprecedented for a Yang or Xia clan member, who were high-ranking members of Kurotsukido Hall, to become the head of Northeast Asia, let alone other regions.</p>
<p>Tang Xiaohua's poison had been used several times in the political infighting within Kurotsukido Hall, so even her subordinates feared Tang Xiaohua, just like the messenger's attitude now. They were afraid that Tang Xiaohua's poison might be aimed at them as well.</p>
<p>"Okay, tell the kids to be ready to depart at any time so that we can quickly support Kakunodate, even though they have a lot of troops!"</p>
<p>"Y-yes!"</p>
<p>Hearing Tang Xiaohua's words, the messenger bowed and hurriedly left the library. At that moment, the moon, which had been hidden in the clouds, revealed itself, revealing Tang Xiaohua's appearance.</p>
<p>Perhaps due to the influence of the Poison Art, she had bright green hair and radiant green eyes. Her slender body was clad in a cyber suit. What was unique was that she was wearing a tech mask in the shape of a skull's mouth. This skull mask was Tang Xiaohua's trademark, and anyone who knew Tang Xiaohua would run away immediately if they saw someone wearing that mask. The green lights flashing on the cyber suit circuits here and there made her look dangerous to anyone.</p>
<p>"Ah! I'm so bored...! I wish the situation was a bit more urgent...."</p>
<p>Tang Xiaohua stretched alone and muttered. After all, it wasn't Taira's main force, but just a fight with Taira Foods, so she didn't even think about losing. Tang Xiaohua was certain that this fight would end in her victory as well.</p>

<p>Tang!</p>
<p>Sawayanagi Mikiyo was advancing, taking out the scouts on the roadside buildings one by one from the armored transport vehicle. As soon as they departed, they received a call saying that the Daesens were also putting pressure on them by moving north, forcing Kurotsukido Hall to divide their forces.</p>
<p>We were advancing straight along Route 46, and the Daesen main force was moving north along Route 105. The place where the two roads met was Kakunodate.</p>
<p>"We'll arrive at the point we're thinking of in about 5 more minutes, so everyone, get ready."</p>
<p>Sawayanagi Azuma shouted while looking at everyone, and everyone answered, "Yes!" Unlike usual, they were showing respect for Sawayanagi Azuma, the leader, in actual combat.</p>
<p>"Stop for a moment."</p>
<p>Telepath ability user Lianna Holden said to Kiyona Hori, who was driving, and then opened the door on top and sent out a drone. Sawayanagi Mikiyo, who had been sniping from the top of the car until now, came down through that door.</p>
<p>"I've taken care of the reconnaissance around here for now. But they probably noticed we were coming because of the gunshots."</p>
<p>"It's a battle of strength against strength anyway. We can't help it if they notice."</p>
<p>Sawayanagi Azuma answered in a firm voice to Sawayanagi Mikiyo's words. Everyone was silent for a moment, so only the sound of Sawayanagi Shigeru, a speedster ability user who needed a lot of calories, putting something in his mouth could be heard.</p>
<p>"We've arrived."</p>
<p>"Lianna?"</p>
<p>"It looks okay to get off now, according to the drone."</p>
<p>I and Sawayanagi Tobe got up from our seats. It was a simple operation to infiltrate first and then subdue the Kurotsukido Hall in confusion, but it required the premise that we could play a huge role in causing chaos with just the two of us.</p>
<p>Since Sawayanagi Tobe didn't have long-range attack abilities, he wore submachine guns on both thighs, crossed guns on his back in an X shape, and held a gun with a grenade launcher in his hand.</p>
<p>I wore pistols on both thighs and carried a gun with a grenade launcher in my hand, wearing lighter equipment compared to Sawayanagi Tobe.</p>
<p>"Investor-nim! Be careful! I'll give you some instructions through Lianna later, and you must follow the contents of those instructions unconditionally!"</p>
<p>"Yes. I have to follow the operator's instructions."</p>
<p>What future did she predict to tell me this in advance? I nodded first and got out of the car with Sawayanagi Tobe.</p>
<p>We walked slowly, looking around. There were several paths, but to draw aggro, we walked slowly along the largest avenue. There didn't seem to be many enemies in the early part of Kakunodate yet. After walking for about 3 minutes on foot, bullets finally flew.</p>
<p>"Engage!"</p>
<p>I shouted into the radio and ran towards a group of people who were shooting at me. While the helmet and cyber suit deflected most of the bullets, I arrived in front of the group. Sawayanagi Tobe provided covering fire while I ran. It seemed like he was doing exactly as we had decided, with one person rushing in and the other providing cover.</p>
<p>Shinkai Yatsude wasn't even needed for the lower-ranking soldiers of Kurotsukido Hall, who were just given guns to delinquents. I raised the gun barrels with telekinesis and swung my abyss-filled hand greatly, instantly bisecting ten people.</p>
<p>The enemies were flustered when the gun barrels suddenly rose, and they were cut in half without being able to take any defensive action.</p>
<p>"Kuaaa!"</p>
<p>With screams, a group of soldiers was annihilated, and we headed to the next location. Next, bullets poured in from both sides of the alley, but this time, we stood back-to-back and fired bullets to both sides.</p>
<p>Since the opponent would die if hit by just one bullet, and we didn't take much damage, we were able to annihilate them without difficulty this time as well.</p>
<p>But the opponent now realized that we couldn't be dealt with using ordinary bullets, so they attacked with RPG-7s and grenade launchers, but Lianna Holden, who had checked in advance with a drone, warned us, and Sawayanagi Mikiyo, who had climbed onto the roof of the building, sniped them with headshots, providing appropriate defense.</p>
<p>Even if there was someone Sawayanagi Mikiyo couldn't handle, I changed the muzzle with telekinesis to avoid the shelling, and in the meantime, I ran and killed them with a gun. It was too much to use telekinesis from such a long distance, so my head was throbbing, but I thought it was all to improve my proficiency, so it was bearable.</p>
<p>The main force of East Weapon, which had entered behind us, was also engaged in battle, and the sound of gunfire could be heard faintly. I'm sure it's a very advantageous situation because we're at the forefront, drawing aggro and massacring at least close to three digits.</p>
<p>Tatantantantantantan!!!</p>
<p>When we reached almost the end of the Kakunodate downtown area, machine gun fire poured out from both sides of the building with a loud roar. Certainly, the large-caliber bullets of the machine gun pierced through the cyber armor and dug into my flesh. The cyber armor reduced the damage, but the bullets were embedded in my flesh.</p>
<p>Looking at Sawayanagi Tobe next to me, it seemed like he was deflecting the bullets with sparks thanks to his iron skin. Still, if he kept getting hit, his iron skin would be released.</p>
<p>We quickly ran and entered the alley.</p>
<p>"Are you alright-sio?"</p>
<p>Sawayanagi Tobe asked in his unique haoche, worried about me bleeding, but when I showed him the flesh filling up and the bullets falling to the ground, he nodded as if relieved.</p>
<p>"That's fortunate...."</p>
<p>"Let's jump out at once and shoot the grenade launcher at the place where the machine gun is."</p>
<p>"Then I will shoot the building on the right-sio."</p>
<p>Then naturally I'm the building on the left. We counted to three at the same time and jumped out, firing grenade launchers at the buildings we were in charge of.</p>
<p>With a thud, the grenade flew, broke the window where the machine gun was, and went inside. And 1 second later, a kwaang! sound was heard and it exploded.</p>
