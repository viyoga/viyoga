<p>A few days ago.</p>
<p>In a dark room, the only thing shining was the screen of an old CRT TV. Someone was sitting on the sofa in front of the TV, but even in the light cast by the TV screen, they were shrouded in deep Darkness, making it impossible to tell who it was.</p>
<p>-With Black Knight's activities, New York's crime rate is.... What appeared on the TV screen was News about New York, where Black Knight was active. The Person sitting on the sofa silently watched the screen for a while, then soon opened their mouth.</p>
<p>"Now that I think about it, among the Guys who opposed me, there was a Guy called Black Knight. Was that Guy active in New York...."</p>
<p>With a Hoarse Voice, as if scraping iron, the man in front of the TV looked at the Hero Black Knight in black Armor on the screen and spoke.</p>
<p>The man was currently the world's most notorious criminal, with a bounty higher than anyone else's globally, known by the name of the Calamity, Famine, instead of his real name. He was a rare madman who had killed tens of thousands of people, to the point that in the USA, Famine's appearance was described as the most shocking event since World War III.</p>
<p>"Back then, I was busy so I left them alone, but it's about time they paid the Price for interfering with me."</p>
<p>Famine, who first appeared at LA Dignity Health Sports Park, was interfered with by Aitana and Black Knight. Because of that interference, he was able to make a shocking appearance, killing over 50,000 People, but he only managed to kill 25,000, so Famine held a Grudge against both of them.</p>
<p>Attacking Aitana, who belonged to the USA military's superpower unit, was also a stretch for Famine. Even if Famine could face tens of thousands of People, he thought it would be difficult to capture Aitana, who received immense firepower support from the USA military.</p>
<p>Furthermore, even if he tried to Ambush or Assassinate her, the USA military kept Aitana's Position a Secret, so Famine couldn't launch an Ambush even if he wanted to.</p>
<p>But Black Knight's Situation was different from Aitana's. At first, he wondered if Black Knight kept appearing in New York to Seduce him, but that didn't seem to be the case. There were several Reasons why Famine thought that.</p>
<p>First, as Famine, who led the superhuman supremacist Terrorist group Superior, he had his own Information network, and as far as he knew, he hadn't heard any Information about Black Knight's Affiliation. This meant Black Knight was a much easier Prey than Aitana, who was affiliated with the USA military.</p>
<p>Second, if this were a Trap, the USA government would never have unleashed Black Knight in New York. This was because if Famine and Black Knight fought in New York, the human casualties would be so immense that the 25,000 Sacrifices at LA Dignity Health Sports Park would seem like a Prank.</p>
<p>For these two Reasons, Famine was convinced that the Black Knight appearing on TV now was not a Trap.</p>
<p>"I want to handle it myself, but...."</p>
<p>Famine was currently tied to one Place due to some Matter. But as the Master of Superior, he had many Subordinates who could deal with his Enemies in his stead.</p>
<p>"Behemoth and Whisper should be enough to eliminate Black Knight."</p>
<p>Both individuals Famine mentioned were Strong Persons and Executives within Superior's top 10. Those two would be more than enough to handle Black Knight. To convey his will to Superior, Famine picked up the antique corded Phone Call placed on the side table next to the sofa.</p>
<p>To suddenly tell me to come as fast as possible? Did something happen? Of course, even if Eve didn't rush me, I did have a Plan to go to the USA soon. As the nominal Boss of Cyberware, the cyber implant company Eve created, I had Work to do in the USA.</p>
<p>-Ryuta: What's the Matter? Since Eve wasn't one to just tell me to come without a specific Reason, I asked her why.</p>
<p>-EVE: The Federal Superpower Management Department came to Cyberware. They say they absolutely must meet Ryuta-nim.</p>
<p>The Federal Superpower Management Bureau...? I thought they might find me since Cyberware was a company created under my USA name, Dante Aster, given by the Federal Superpower Management Department, but I didn't expect them to find me so quickly after creating Cyberware.</p>
<p>Is there something urgent? I subtly switched the Screen on my phone from chat to world News, but there was no particularly new content.</p>
<p>-Ryuta: Can't they tell me what it is, even roughly? -EVE: No. They say it's quite important Information, so they can only tell Ryuta-nim if they meet him directly. In Japan, they say it's awkward to contact Ryuta-nim directly since he returned to the Taira Group. What kind of Matter is it that they have to meet me in Person to tell me? I thought they could just tell me in Japan, but it seems many People would misunderstand if they contacted me now that I've returned as Taira Ryuta, so they can't meet.</p>
<p>-Ryuta: For now, tell them to wait a bit. I'll figure out what's going on and be there soon. Actually, I didn't have any particularly urgent things to do right now. The major competition was over, and I had done my part as Boss by achieving great Performance with East Weapon. And Taira Food distribution wasn't a company whose Performance would drop depending on the presence or absence of its Boss.</p>
<p>Of course, before going to the USA, I had planned to weave a few conspiracies into the Taira Successor structure, and check the UCT that Yukiko had given me to learn about the Information Taira Shizuku had provided and the individuals from the Taira Saburo line who had left the Taira Family, but it seemed fine to change the order and go to the USA first.</p>
<p>Conspiracies could be woven anytime, and the individuals from the Taira Saburo line who had left the Taira Family wouldn't just disappear somewhere while I was briefly in the USA.</p>
<p>-EVE: Understood. Since you'll be here, you'll also need to handle Cyberware Work, so I'll prepare the necessary Documents~! -Ryuta: Yes~! See you soon.</p>
<p>Although I said I'd go as early as possible, I still needed to talk with my various Staff Officers. First, I should Contact Linxiang, who would be at the company. When I called Linxiang, she answered before the Phone Call even rang once.</p>
<p>-Yes, Boss-nim. Seeing her say "Boss-nim," it seemed there were employees around her. Since it was before lunch, they must be in the middle of Work.</p>
<p>"Linxiang, I'll be going to the USA as soon as possible. For details, check with Eve, and I'll send you data through a secure channel, so understand that and create data to share with the Joseodang."</p>
<p>-The USA? Understood. I'll prepare quickly, Boss-nim. As an AI, Linxiang wasn't particularly surprised by my sudden trip to the USA.</p>
<p>"Before going to the USA, I'll also hold a Meeting at the mansion, so please Contact those who can attend. For those not at the mansion, we'll have a video conference."</p>
<p>-Understood. I'll inform everyone."</p>
<p>"Then, see you at the mansion."</p>
<p>-Understood, Jin Yongtae-nim. Seeing her say "Jin Yongtae-nim," it seemed there were no employees around her. After ending the Phone Call with Linxiang and transmitting the UCT data to Linxiang and Asada Ei, Tsukiyo was looking at me from the Driver's Seat.</p>
<p>"Ryuta Older Brother, are you going to America?" "Probably?" "Then, what about me?"</p>
<p>Tsukiyo asked what she would do as soon as I said I was going to the USA. Unfortunately for Tsukiyo, I wasn't planning to take her this time.</p>
<p>I planned to give Tsukiyo the Mission of guarding my mansion and time to learn about my company Work by following Linling. This was because I thought Tsukiyo still needed Education to become one of my core Staff Officers.</p>
<p>Furthermore, since it hadn't been long since she caught the eye of the Paranormal Phenomenon Management Bureau, going to the USA again might attract unnecessary attention, so I wasn't planning to take Tsukiyo on this trip to the USA.</p>
<p>"Eh...! I want to go too~! Older Brother-aaang!"</p>
<p>Realizing from my Expression that I wouldn't take her, Tsukiyo threw a tantrum, saying she wanted to go together.</p>
<p>"Tsukiyo, I want to go with you too, but...."</p>
<p>I soothed Tsukiyo and told her the Reasons I had just thought of. As I explained each one, Tsukiyo pouted and reluctantly nodded her Head.</p>
<p>Her forced acceptance seemed to indicate that Tsukiyo also thought the Reasons I gave weren't entirely wrong.</p>
<p>As I sat there, kneading Tsukiyo's pouting Cheek while she grumbled "Chit-chit," we arrived at the mansion before I knew it.</p>
<p>"Everyone is gathered in the conference room, Young Master."</p>
<p>As I got out of the car, Tosen Nobumichi spoke. It seemed Linling had informed everyone in the mansion while I was on my way.</p>
<p>"Thank you, Butler. I should head straight to the conference room."</p>
<p>After thanking Tosen Nobumichi for the Information, I headed to the main building's conference room with Tsukiyo. The conference room was set up in a quite spacious room on the second floor, and hologram seats were also prepared so that People who were far away could attend the Meeting.</p>
<p>"Jin Yongtae-nim."</p>
<p>When I opened the conference room door, Linling, who had gathered everyone in the mansion, greeted me. Alik, Shimokawabe Airen, Kijimoto Jurie, Saionji Diana, and the two Gentlemen were seated in the mansion.</p>
<p>Sakagami Rena and Naruse Shiyo were participating in the Meeting via video conference because they were currently working at the Joseodang.</p>
<p>Asada Ei and Jinmija Yeosa, along with Mimata Chieri from East Weapon, Sawayanagi Azuma from Seryu Military Company, Kurayamada Kirie from the Chungrang Unit, and Yamada Kaita from Kōtetsubutai also participated in the video conference, and although she wasn't a formal PMC yet as her license hadn't been issued, Athena Mannerheim was also seated as the head of my subordinate PMC. Dang So-hwa was in Korea for construction work, so she wasn't called to the Meeting. Looking at it this way, even excluding those who went to the USA, the overall power was considerable...? At this level, it seemed I wouldn't be easily dismissed anywhere.</p>
