<p>,Ryuta, Alik, that was fun. Thanks."</p>
<p>As I dropped Alik off at the entrance of the East Weapon headquarters where he's currently staying, Alik gave me a tight hug. I heard a surprised sound from behind Alik, and saw a middle-aged woman in a Kimono looking at Alik.</p>
<p>,Who are you...?"</p>
<p>,Ah, I'm Egawa Shiho, teaching Alik various things. You must be Taira Ryuta-ssi?"</p>
<p>Ah, I heard that a teacher who teaches Alik household chores and etiquette was staying nearby during the training period, so it seemed like this was the woman.</p>
<p>,Yes, I'm Taira Ryuta."</p>
<p>,Ah... My teacher."</p>
<p>So he calls the person who teaches him, "teacher."</p>
<p>,Originally, there was a class this afternoon, but you met Taira Ryuta-ssi."</p>
<p>,I'm going on assignment next week, so I won't be able to see him for a few months. I'm sorry if I interfered with the training."</p>
<p>When I bowed my head, Egawa Shiho waved her hand, looking embarrassed.</p>
<p>,Ah, I wasn't trying to blame Taira Ryuta-ssi. Mimata-ssi told me in advance. I was discussing the training schedule with Mimata-ssi and came down, and that's how we met."</p>
<p>Hmm, so that's how it happened. Since we've met like this, I should ask if Alik's training is going well.</p>
<p>,Is Alik learning well?"</p>
<p>,Yes, he's very smart and learns well as I teach him. He learns complex lists of famous Japanese families and recipes easily."</p>
<p>Oh, he's doing well. Listening to Egawa Shiho speak proudly, I could see that she really cared for Alik and had affection for him. Is she really trying to adopt Alik?</p>
<p>,I'm glad he's learning well."</p>
<p>,Well, but...."</p>
<p>,Yes?"</p>
<p>Could she be about to bring up the adoption story right away? Seeing her trying to talk to me with such hesitation, I thought my guess was probably right.</p>
<p>,Would it be okay to adopt Alik as my adopted daughter?"</p>
<p>,Well, Alik's opinion is more important than my permission, so if I get Alik's permission, I'll agree too. But even if you adopt Alik, I hope you don't force him to do anything in the name of the family...."</p>
<p>Egawa Shiho waved her hand at my words as if to say absolutely not, and said to me.</p>
<p>,Of course! Alik is so cute, and I've grown fond of him as I've continued to teach him."</p>
<p>,That's a relief then. What do you think, Alik?"</p>
<p>When I asked Alik, Alik, who had been listening from the side, opened his mouth.</p>
<p>,Alik, I don't know yet. I like teacher. But...."</p>
<p>In fact, unlike the other children rescued from Krascana, Alik was not abused by his parents. The problem was that his parents died early.</p>
<p>So, unlike the other children, he had good memories of his parents, especially his mother. Alik's nickname was also given by his mother, and the fact that he continued to use that nickname showed that his mother occupied a fairly large part of Alik's heart. That's why Alik couldn't make a decision easily. Still, seeing him worry, it seemed like Alik liked Egawa Shiho to some extent.</p>
<p>,Becoming an adopted daughter doesn't mean betraying your original mother. Think carefully and tell Egawa-ssi."</p>
<p>,Okay, I understand. I'll think about it carefully."</p>
<p>When I stroked his head and spoke, Alik answered, enjoying the touch. Egawa Shiho, who was watching the scene, bowed her head to me.</p>
<p>,Thank you, Taira Ryuta-nim, you are a good person."</p>
<p>,Well, I'm just respecting Alik's opinion."</p>
<p>I entrusted Alik to Egawa Shiho and went to the underground parking lot, got in my car, and headed to the Tokyo branch of Amsang. I had something to discuss with Eve and Han Yu-gyeong about the business, so I decided to meet at Amsang.</p>
<p>Han Yu-gyeong doesn't come to Amsang in Tokyo, but Eve is in both Tokyo and Seoul, and they can have a conversation as if they were next to each other with holograms, so we decided to have meetings like this if we couldn't meet.</p>
<p>As I passed through the entrance of Amsang, which was disguised as Higashiyama Trading, and went down, Eve waved her hand and greeted me. Before, she wore a tight white short dress, which was a bit embarrassing, but today she was wearing a black short dress. Still, it was less embarrassing than before because it didn't feel so tight.</p>
<p>,Hello~! Ryuta-ssi, long time no see."</p>
<p>,Hello, Eve."</p>
<p>I greeted Eve and shook her hand. Today, it was not a hologram but an android's body with substance. It's so amazing. It feels like I'm touching a real hand....</p>
<p>,Ryuta-ssi? How long are you going to keep touching me?"</p>
<p>,Ah, I'm sorry, Eve. It feels so real when I touch it."</p>
<p>,You know how to give compliments quite well, saying it feels real. Please sit here."</p>
<p>Eve sat me at a square table. And when she snapped her fingers, a hologram machine whirred and scanned my body. It's probably scanning Han Yu-gyeong's body in Seoul too.</p>
<p>Soon, the hologram machine in the seat opposite me activated and created a scanned image of Han Yu-gyeong. My image will probably be visible to Han Yu-gyeong opposite her in Seoul.</p>
<p>,Yong-tae~! It's so real...!"</p>
<p>,I was so surprised too."</p>
<p>I was also quite surprised because it felt like Han Yu-gyeong was in front of me. Of course, I can see her with my eyes, but I can't feel Han Yu-gyeong with my other senses, so I can recognize that she's not there, but visually, it seems to be more than 85 percent realized.</p>
<p>,You shouldn't get up and try to touch it because it's amazing. The hologram only works in the seat you're sitting in~!"</p>
<p>Eve stopped Han Yu-gyeong when she got up from her seat to touch me because she was amazed. Ah, so that's why she designated a seat and guided me.</p>
<p>,Is the preparation for TG Tech going well?"</p>
<p>,Yeah, I've almost built a factory with the data Yong-tae got from the US and the Energy-Link Industrial Company data."</p>
<p>Since there was already a lot of data to make things that could be mass-produced, we could start production as soon as the factory was almost finished.</p>
<p>,Is Lara Sinclair helping?"</p>
<p>I asked because Lara Sinclair, the former owner of Energy-Link Industrial Company and a combat android, was also in charge of the position of technical advisor. Eve answered the question about Lara Sinclair instead.</p>
<p>,Lara Sinclair answers us faithfully whenever we ask. But she doesn't take the initiative to help."</p>
<p>Still, it seems like she helps when we ask. She was probably busy exchanging android technology with Eve to upgrade herself.</p>
<p>,First, we should use the media to massively promote that TG Tech will supply equipment to Vanguard and East Weapon as soon as it starts operating."</p>
<p>Vanguard and East Weapon were both famous security companies and PMCs in Korea and Japan, respectively, so if a new company supplied equipment, it would attract a lot of attention.</p>
<p>In addition, if the president is the youngest daughter of the Shin-gang Group, she will be trusted to some extent. Since the Shin-gang Group itself is a chaebol family that has developed into the defense industry, it could create the illusion that the technology will not be lacking.</p>
<p>,How are the psychics reacting in Korea?"</p>
<p>Since one of TG Tech's equipment lines was equipment for psychics, the treatment of psychics in Korea and the world was important. I knew the future, but I still asked Han Yu-gyeong because my actions could have a butterfly effect and change the future.</p>
<p>,The public opinion is still not good... The assemblyman Choi Seok-hoon you mentioned will probably die tonight. Then the public opinion will gradually change."</p>
<p>Han Yu-gyeong's words were strange. It was a nuance that she would probably take action directly. Choi Seok-hoon, a five-term assemblyman, was a representative opponent of psychics, so he was a person who eventually had to disappear for psychics to operate in Korea.</p>
<p>,What about Japan?"</p>
<p>At Han Yu-gyeong's question, I smiled slightly and nodded. I, who was very busy, didn't move directly, but I supported a radical psychic group with money, and as a result, several mysterious deaths of members of the National Assembly were already occurring.</p>
<p>Of course, most of the dead members of the National Assembly were people who had spoken badly about psychics, so everyone didn't say it out loud, but they roughly knew who the culprit was.</p>
<p>After a bit of a dark story, we talked about the company's management policy while looking at various researched data such as market reactions and opinion polls.</p>
<p>,Then the TG Tech business will be smooth~!"</p>
<p>After about an hour, when we finished talking about TG Tech, Eve clapped her hands and said.</p>
<p>,Then shall we talk about the bodyguard pet business? We've finally completed the prototype."</p>
<p>Oh, I heard they were making it at the Tokyo factory, so the prototype is finally complete. Eve brought a box and placed it on the table. Soon, the box opened and a small Yorkshire Terrier walked out. Wow, it looks so real? The glossy fur, the real-looking eyes, the moist nose and tongue, there was no sense of incongruity at all.</p>
<p>,Oh! It looks so real...!"</p>
<p>,Really! It's amazing. Can I buy one too?"</p>
<p>Han Yu-gyeong seemed very surprised even though she couldn't see the real thing and was watching it with a hologram.</p>
<p>,We're running the company together, so you don't need to buy it. I'll just send you one. Just decide what kind of animal you want."</p>
<p>The Yorkshire Terrier that came out of the box walked around and showed off its talents by licking my arm.</p>
<p>,Then let's decide on the price first...."</p>
<p>,Shall we?"</p>
<p>Since we couldn't openly sell the AI bodyguard pet, we had to use a high-end strategy. We decided to advertise and sell it at Amsang as a high-end pet that couldn't be sold legally, and the price would start at 500,000 points. 500,000 points is 500,000 dollars, so the minimum price would be over 600 million won. There are many rich people in the world, so those who want to buy it will buy it.</p>
<p>Since everyone wants different pets anyway, we decided to adopt a system where the price goes up if you choose the specifications, size, and shape because you have to custom-make them. For reference, if you apply the highest-level technology to the appearance of wild animals such as lions and leopards that Middle Eastern royalty like, the price is a whopping 5 million dollars.</p>
<p>,I'll go to Korea soon. In about a month?"</p>
<p>,Okay. I miss you."</p>
<p>,Me too."</p>
<p>,I'll turn off the hologram now~!"</p>
<p>We looked at each other's holograms and said we missed each other, and Eve turned off the hologram, ending our short meeting.</p>
<p>,I'm curious."</p>
<p>,About what?"</p>
<p>Before I left the Amsang Tokyo branch, Eve spoke to me, so I asked.</p>
<p>,The emotion called love."</p>
<p>,Hmm, I don't know either. If you keep wanting to see the other person and want to grant their wishes, isn't that love?"</p>
<p>,Is that so?"</p>
<p>Well, there probably aren't many people who can properly define love. Eve had a strange expression, but she returned to her original cheerful attitude and waved her hand at me.</p>
<p>,Goodbye~! I'm bored, so please visit often, Ryuta-ssi!"</p>
<p>,Okay."</p>
<p>I came out of Amsang, escorted by Eve.</p>
