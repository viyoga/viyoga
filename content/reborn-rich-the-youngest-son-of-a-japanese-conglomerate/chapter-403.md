<p>"Yamashiro-sangbyeong-nim! We need to secure point A!"</p>
<p>Before I knew it, Oofuchi Erika was briefing me, completely immersed in the role of a special forces soldier. Impressive, isn't it? It could be a little embarrassing, but she's practically doing method acting. Anyway, point A... Where is that?</p>
<p>"Try touching the map button on the panel screen on your wrist."</p>
<p>Listening to Oofuchi Erika, I looked at my wrist and saw a fairly large panel. As Oofuchi Erika said, I pressed the Map button on the panel screen with my finger, and a semi-transparent map appeared in the upper right of my vision. A place some distance away was flashing and labeled as point A. Capturing that place would probably end this game in victory.</p>
<p>"Ah, here? Can I go now?"</p>
<p>"Yes. Let's get Yamashiro-sangbyeong-nim's equipment first and then depart."</p>
<p>Listening to Oofuchi Erika, I looked around and saw several large boxes. Approaching the boxes, I saw some guns inside, and I chose a pistol and the rifle I used in the Kurotsuki subjugation battle and equipped them.</p>
<p>"Good choice. Let's depart now."</p>
<p>Oofuchi Erika, carrying a large gun that she would have struggled to lift in real life, dashed out into the alley. I also ran after her with a gun, but it was quite frustrating. As expected, since it's a game, I moved faster than an ordinary person, but it felt far short of my actual physical abilities in reality. Because of that, it felt like I was moving in sand.</p>
<p>Tang, tang, tang!!</p>
<p>Oofuchi Erika was in a gunfight with terrorists dressed all in black. I joined in and fired my gun, but my accuracy wasn't great, probably because I didn't have a good understanding of the game. If Yukiko, who has shooting skills, or Han Yu-gyeong, who has throwing skills, played this game, they would quickly adapt and do really well.</p>
<p>Pfit!</p>
<p>When I got shot in the shoulder, one of the eight life bars in the upper left corner decreased. Wait, I regenerate, so why is it decreasing? Since it's different from reality, I couldn't use my combat style of charging in, trusting in my regeneration ability.</p>
<p>"A- No, Captain Yamashiro!"</p>
<p>How long is she going to call me sangbyeong-nim? Even before the possession, I was discharged as a byeongjang, you know. I decided to think of immersing myself in the role like this as a way to enjoy the game more.</p>
<p>"It's okay, I think I'm starting to get the hang of it!"</p>
<p>I said I was okay and then rattled the gun. I was slowly getting used to shooting and moving. I have more combat experience than anyone else, so there's no way I can lose in a mere game!</p>
<p>HEAD SHOT!</p>
<p>GAME OVER!</p>
<p>The moment I came out of the alley and tried to massacre all the terrorists, red letters appeared and the screen started turning gray. No, this doesn't make sense! I just jumped out of the alley a little, so why a headshot...! If there was a keyboard, I would have gone for a shotgun angle, but this game is a VR-dive game, so I couldn't do that.</p>
<p>"Yamashiro-sangbyeong-nim...! I'll get revenge!"</p>
<p>With Oofuchi Erika's angry words as the end, I exited the game. Taking off the goggles and looking around, I saw several female students holding their stomachs, rolling around, and bursting into laughter.</p>
<p>"Kyahaha! Sensei, is there anything you can't do!"</p>
<p>"Ahaha! Ryuta-jji, why don't you start 'adapting' to death~!"</p>
<p>"Haha!! Sensei-ssi is so funny!"</p>
<p>The laughing girls were Kujo Kina, Seizawa Shii, and Otagi Yuka in that order. Even the seemingly cool-headed Shido Tsubaki and Minamoto Lisa were giggling. Just you wait... you guys.</p>
<p>Shido Tsubaki was also sitting at the table, laughing and typing on the keyboard non-stop, so I glanced at the laptop screen and was shocked! Special Class's Teacher Y, Game Skills Revealed! It said, and she was writing a newspaper article. Below that, it said Shido Tsubaki, guest reporter for St. Francis Girls' Academy Newspaper Club, so it seemed she was still submitting articles to the newspaper club even after resigning as the head of the newspaper club.</p>
<p>"Wait, my gaming skills are becoming newspaper material?"</p>
<p>"Sensei, you're handsome, you know. You don't realize it, but you're super popular in our school. Every article about you becomes news material. I'm giving you these sources one by one to prevent the newspaper club from covering the special class, so don't say too much."</p>
<p>So, Shido Tsubaki is providing my stories instead to prevent the special class from becoming a mess, in order to stop the swarming newspaper club reporters?</p>
<p>"Is the newspaper club that big of a club?"</p>
<p>"We're just a little over 10 members, but the newspaper club has over ten members per grade from middle school to high school, so it's a club with nearly 100 members. Besides, they haven't tasted the bitterness of society yet, so they don't care about the consequences when looking for news material."</p>
<p>Hearing this, it's quite scary. If I get targeted by the newspaper club, it could cause quite a problem for my mission, so I need to be careful. Rather, I should be grateful that Shido Tsubaki is publishing these articles.</p>
<p>"As a former head of the newspaper club, I'm taking charge of Sensei and leaking sources little by little with my authority, so don't say too much."</p>
<p>"Okay. Please keep the newspaper club kids from being interested in the special class in the future. There are quite a few students with scars, after all."</p>
<p>I asked her to block the newspaper club's interest under the pretext of the special class students. Minamoto Lisa is already in the special class, so she's providing a considerable shield, but it would be safer if Shido Tsubaki diverted their interest with appropriate articles separately.</p>
<p>"Wow!"</p>
<p>While I was talking to Shido Tsubaki, I heard a "wow" sound, so I looked over and saw Oofuchi Erika rolling her body on the TV screen and instantly getting headshots on five people.</p>
<p>Her senses are good. If I teach her well, she might be able to do some real combat? Although Daigo Terue, Oofuchi Erika's godmother, would never allow it.</p>
<p>"Whew...! Thank you for your hard work."</p>
<p>Oofuchi Erika said, taking off her goggles.</p>
<p>"Sorry I couldn't enjoy it with you because I wasn't good."</p>
<p>"No, it's natural that you're not good at it since it's your first time. It's my fault for choosing a difficult game."</p>
<p>Hmm, hearing these words is a bit of a blow to my pride... As Oofuchi Erika said, it couldn't be helped since it was my first time. It also felt like my physical abilities were nerfed a lot compared to my original abilities, so I had a hard time adapting.</p>
<p>"Then... is it Sakagami's turn next?"</p>
<p>The last one in the game part was Sakagami Rena. She was still wearing a mask, but instead of the scary mask from before, she was wearing a normal mask, so I was happy that she had accepted my words to some extent.</p>
<p>"...."</p>
<p>Sakagami Rena was still nodding silently, but Konoe Akina told me that she still talks on LINE, so that was a relief.</p>
<p>"Sakagami said to play a simple dart throwing game."</p>
<p>Oofuchi Erika, a VR-dive expert, spoke for Sakagami Rena instead. Dart throwing... At least I won't be embarrassed like I was just now.</p>
<p>When I put on the VR-dive goggles, we were suddenly facing a dartboard. There are several ways to play darts, such as Zero One, Cricket, Half-It, and Count-Up, but we decided to play Count-Up, which is the most common for beginners.</p>
<p>Count-Up was a match where you throw three darts per round, play eight rounds, and the person with the highest score in the final round wins.</p>
<p>Glancing at Sakagami Rena standing next to me, it looked very natural for her to have darts between each of her fingers.</p>
<p>I went first. I aimed for the 50-point Bull's Eye in the center, but I hit the Outer Bull's Eye next to it.</p>
<p>Tuk! Tuk!</p>
<p>"Wow! You hit the center! Amazing!"</p>
<p>The second one missed, but the third one hit the center. It was still pretty good. It seemed to be hitting well because of the sense I adjusted while playing the FPS game earlier. Since I had the spectator mode turned on, I could hear the female students cheering from behind.</p>
<p>Sakagami Rena stood quietly and threw three darts at once. What? Is she just going to give up? As I looked at the dartboard, all of them were stuck right in the center.</p>
<p>"Saka-chan! You're so good!"</p>
<p>"Did you see her throw them all at once?"</p>
<p>"Is it luck?"</p>
<p>I also wondered if it was luck, but Sakagami Rena's skill was not luck. After all eight rounds were over, my score was 430 points, and Sakagami Rena had hit the Bull's Eye in every round, scoring 1200 points. With a score difference of about three times, I had to kneel.</p>
<p>I was defeated too badly, so when I took off my goggles, none of the students were laughing like before. Only Shido Tsubaki was writing an article titled Shock! Teacher Y's Dart Match Defeat!</p>
<p>"It seems like today is not your lucky day, Sensei."</p>
<p>Konoe Akina handed me a cup of orange juice and spoke to me. Thinking about it, I've lost everything since the tennis match with Otagi Yuka today.</p>
<p>"The game is over, so there won't be any more defeats today."</p>
