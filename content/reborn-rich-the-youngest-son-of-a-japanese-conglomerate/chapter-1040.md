<p>"I was looking for someone worthy of being an opponent. You seem to be the very person I've been searching for. Will you not engage in a sword duel with me?"</p>
<p>A Black Person holding a greatsword said respectfully towards Shimokawabe Airen.</p>
<p>Unlike his respectful demeanor, seeing the brutally bisected corpses already strewn around the Black Person, it seemed he was actively participating in the battle for the fragments of omniscience, just like Shimokawabe Airen, to find his opponent.</p>
<p>"Can you satisfy me?" Shimokawabe Airen said as if disbelieving. She had met quite a few Swordsmen until now, but no one had yet satisfied Shimokawabe Airen, the combat maniac.</p>
<p>Even Shimokawabe Airen's Sabu Kagiya Haruka could not satisfy Shimokawabe Airen's combat Longing.</p>
<p>"My name is Isaac Flint, the Swordsman who will end your combat Longing."</p>
<p>Isaac Flint, the Berserker under the Black Sage, recognized Shimokawabe Airen at a glance. That she was exactly the type of Person who gained Thrill from combat, just like himself.</p>
<p>Kashima Kiichi Shinto-ryu (Deer Island Demon One Divine Way Style), Shimokawabe Airen.</p>
<p>Since the opponent had introduced himself, Shimokawabe Airen also introduced herself and pointed her Sword at Isaac Flint.</p>
<p>The two Swordsmen stood side by side, pointing their Swords at each other, frozen like a Sculpture. There was no movement in their bodies, but their eyes were busily searching for each other's openings.</p>
<p>'Quite impressive...!'</p>
<p>Just as Shimokawabe Airen thought, 'Quite impressive...!' Isaac Flint closed the distance and swung his greatsword.</p>
<p>Huuung!</p>
<p>The greatsword, swung with a sound like a massive sheet of Iron cutting through the Wind. Shimokawabe Airen decided to use a Kashima Kiichi Shinto-ryu Martial Arts Form to deflect Isaac Flint's powerful attack.</p>
<p>-Kashima Kiichi Shinto-ryu Sakurakaben-nagashi (Cherry Blossom Petal Stream)</p>
<p>Shimokawabe Airen moved her Sword as if guiding Isaac Flint's greatsword. Isaac Flint's greatsword, which looked more like a thick sheet of Iron than a Sword over 2 meters long, was deflected sideways as if guided by Shimokawabe Airen's thin katana.</p>
<p>Kwaaaang!</p>
<p>Isaac Flint's strike, due to Shimokawabe Airen's deflection, dug into the building Remains and kicked up a huge cloud of Dust.</p>
<p>-Kashima Kiichi Shinto-ryu Kiichi-ryo-cham (Demon One Territory Slash)</p>
<p>Shimokawabe Airen used the vertical Slash attack Martial Arts Form, Kiichi-ryo-cham, towards Isaac Flint, who had an opening from bringing his Sword down. It was one of the basic Martial Arts Forms, but Kiichi-ryo-cham delivered a fatal strike that vertically cut through space.</p>
<p>Isaac Flint, perhaps sensing something unusual, simultaneously pulled out the Sword stuck in the building Remains and leaped backward as if flying.</p>
<p>All these actions happened while Shimokawabe Airen was delivering her strike. Because it was done with pure physical Ability, not by utilizing any special Technique, Shimokawabe Airen admired his tremendous physical prowess.</p>
<p>"Khahahaha! This is it! This is it!"</p>
<p>His initially respectful Attitude was gone, and Isaac Flint's whites of his eyes turned red, becoming increasingly wild. True to his nickname, the Berserker, it seemed Madness was eroding his Brain more and more as the battle progressed.</p>
<p>Huung! Huung!</p>
<p>Based on his immense physical Ability, Isaac Flint swung his Sword like a Storm. His movements lacked any specific form, but the fundamentals of swordsmanship seemed ingrained in his Body, with no illogical movements.</p>
<p>'He has no special Martial Arts Forms, but his fundamentals are solid, and he uses instinctive swordsmanship with immense physical Ability and savagery born of Madness.'</p>
<p>Shimokawabe Airen instantly grasped Isaac Flint's combat method.</p>
<p>Isaac Flint could be described as a Swordsman who had mastered basic forms until they were ingrained in his Body, and during combat, he entrusted his Body to his wild Instinct, a swordsman whose Technique and savagery were well-harmonized.</p>
<p>Furthermore, with the Drugs he created during his researcher days, his gaejo, and even the Black Sage's Jusul, he possessed physical Abilities that surpassed humans, which was why he had found it difficult to find an Enemy until now. Thus, Isaac Flint was always thirsty for battles of life and death.</p>
<p>But today, he had found a female Swordsman worthy of being his opponent. In that Exhilaration and excitement/arousal, Isaac Flint was being eroded by Madness faster than usual.</p>
<p>"Khahahaha!"</p>
<p>As he wildly swung his greatsword, a Sword Wind like a tornado poured from Isaac Flint's Sword. Shimokawabe Airen narrowed her slender eyes and frowned.</p>
<p>Because she realized that such a fierce attack could not be deflected with the Sakurakaben-nagashi Technique she had just used.</p>
<p>-Kashima Kiichi Shinto-ryu (Kashima Kiichi Sacred Way School) Sakurakaben-henkyaku (Cherry Blossom Petal Return)</p>
<p>However, Shimokawabe Airen had Sakurakaben-henkyaku, a superior Technique to Sakurakaben-nagashi. Sakurakaben-henkyaku was not a typical reflection Technique, but one that reflected it back.</p>
<p>Shimokawabe Airen exquisitely swung her Sword and returned the Sword Wind he had unleashed back to Isaac Flint.</p>
<p>Because Sakurakaben-henkyaku was not a typical reflection Technique, but a Technique that added her own power to the opponent's, reflecting it even more strongly, the Sword Wind Isaac Flint had unleashed became even more massive.</p>
<p>"Khaha! My eyes weren't wrong! This is the best!"</p>
<p>Even though his opponent had reflected his Technique back, Isaac Flint was greatly pleased by her Skill. After evading the reflected Sword Wind, he clung to Shimokawabe Airen as if attempting Melee Combat this Time.</p>
<p>Shimokawabe Airen also did not retreat, but charged forward and swung her Sword.</p>
<p>Chaeng! Chaeng! Chaeng!</p>
<p>With metallic sounds that seemed to tear one's Sensitive Ear, sparks of Fire that illuminated the night flew as Sword clashed with Sword. Even clashing with a greatsword over 2 meters long, Shimokawabe Airen's Sword remained unharmed.</p>
<p>Perhaps because it was a sword, Kamiwa (Divine Harmony), crafted with all his heart from mithril by the master craftsman Ichimonji Harugoro, Shimokawabe Airen's sword, Kamiwa, showed no signs of losing its sharp aura, far from breaking.</p>
<p>The two stayed close and continued to swing their Swords. Their Swords were swung so rapidly that only the occasional Sword Light reflected by the surrounding Street Lights was briefly visible.</p>
<p>Due to the battle of the two Swordsmen, the surrounding buildings and structures were destroyed, and the area had become almost flat ground.</p>
<p>Shimokawabe Airen and Isaac Flint, who had been swinging their Swords at each other for a long Time, created distance. They realized that a Match could not possibly be decided through extreme close-quarters combat. Isaac Flint was busy blocking Shimokawabe Airen's complex Techniques, and Shimokawabe Airen found the maenggong stemming from Isaac Flint's powerful body burdensome.</p>
<p>Ultimately, both realized that a grand Technique was needed to decide the Match.</p>
<p>Shimokawabe Airen's eyes sank deeply, and Isaac Flint's Muscles swelled as if they would burst.</p>
<p>"You are the first to receive this Technique."</p>
<p>Isaac Flint said, looking at Shimokawabe Airen with a Gaze mixed half with Madness and half with Fading Reason. Shimokawabe Airen, on the other hand, said nothing. After staring at each other for a moment, both swung their Swords almost simultaneously.</p>
<p>-Flint-ryu (Flint Style) Secret Technique: The Pinnacle.</p>
<p>-Kashima Kiichi Shinto-ryu Kuukan-kire-kaika (Space Slash - Blooming).</p>
<p>Isaac Flint's greatsword thrust forward with incredible speed, breaking the sound barrier and creating a sonic boom, and its shockwave possessed immense power, enough to sweep away everything around it.</p>
<p>On the other hand, what Shimokawabe Airen unleashed was a red, flower bud-shaped Geomgi the size of a Thumb joint.</p>
<p>It was a delicate-looking Geomgi that seemed as if it would be swept away by the immense power pouring from Isaac Flint's Higi, but before long, the red, flower bud-shaped Geomgi was absorbing the power of Isaac Flint's strike and growing larger and larger.</p>
<p>When the red flower bud approached Isaac Flint, the shockwave Isaac Flint had unleashed had already vanished, leaving only the flower bud. And finally, just before reaching Isaac Flint, the flower bud began to bloom. Countless Petals bloomed from the flower bud, and it began to unleash the hidden sword attacks all around. As the Petals unfurled, hundreds and thousands of Geomgi, cutting through space like the Kiichi-ryo-cham that Shimokawabe Airen frequently used, swept through the surroundings.</p>
<p>The Kashima Kiichi Shinto-ryu's space Slash attack, which cut through space, could not be blocked even by Isaac Flint's body, enhanced by gaejo and Jusul. Although it was a body that could block even Bullets without being pierced, Isaac Flint's body could not block the Geomgi unleashed by Shimokawabe Airen and began to be cut and shattered into pieces.</p>
<p>"I have no intention of losing with a Martial Arts Form I used for the first Time against a Person."</p>
<p>Looking at Isaac Flint, whose entire body was split apart, leaving only his Head, Shimokawabe Airen muttered as she sheathed her Sword. Before turning her Body, Shimokawabe Airen left one more Word for Isaac Flint's Head.</p>
<p>"You couldn't satisfy me, but among the Swordsmen I've fought so far, you are the best."</p>
<p>Perhaps he heard Shimokawabe Airen's Words before dying, as the Corner of the Mouth of Isaac Flint, with only his Head remaining, was seen to rise slightly.</p>
<p>Whether it was because he met his end at the hands of the Person who had satisfied his combat Instinct, which he had been searching for until now, or because Shimokawabe Airen had called him the best among the Swordsmen she had fought, only Isaac Flint would ever know. Leaving behind a worthy opponent, Shimokawabe Airen began to move towards the Points she was assigned to cover again.</p>
