<p>Having successfully stolen the data, I left the research complex using the same method as when I came in. I went down to the 4th floor, entered the storage room, went out through the window, and climbed down the wall using my shadow domination ability. Then, using the shadows of each building as a shield, I left the research complex. End.</p>
<p>With the combination of shadow domination and the senses of a master, infiltration and escape were incredibly easy. Of course, it was even easier because this was Pyongyang, where there weren't many CCTVs.</p>
<p>After leaving the research complex, I used my smartphone to find the location of the Dark Merchant Guild's Pyongyang branch. Because I was a Plus member, I could find the location of the Dark Merchant Guild branch at any time using the Dark Merchant Guild app.</p>
<p>Let's see... Fortunately, the Dark Merchant Guild's Pyongyang branch wasn't too far from the hotel I was staying at, although I had to go around a bit.</p>
<p>I slowly walked towards the Dark Merchant Guild branch. I had been there several times in the game, so I wasn't worried about getting lost, and I leisurely strolled, taking in the sights of the cyberpunk city.</p>
<p>The Dark Merchant Guild was using an underground bunker in Pyongyang, built by the old North Korean regime, as their nest. I don't know how the Dark Merchant Guild established their nest here, but they were still in business now, and even in the backward TMC, so it seemed like they had made some kind of deal when the new Pyongyang Technology Special City was established.</p>
<p>The entrance to the Dark Merchant Guild was a plain, small two-story shopping center, but the entire shopping center was used by Dark Merchant Guild members, so they didn't create secret doors in the restrooms like they did in Seoul.</p>
<p>There was just a staircase leading to the basement, and in front of it was a barred gate. If you verified that you were a Plus or higher-level member with the Dark Merchant Guild app on the scanning device on the wall, the barred gate would open automatically.</p>
<p>The barred gate went up with a grating sound, *geugeuk*.</p>
<p>I went straight into the basement. As expected, there were several counters in the basement, and Eve-ssi, with a mix of white and pink hair, was sitting at each counter.</p>
<p>I went straight to an empty counter and sat down. Then, Eve-ssi greeted me. She's made with AI, but still pretty. Eve-ssi greeted me as if she were saluting.</p>
<p>, "Hello, Ryuta-nim. You were in Seoul before, but today you're in Pyongyang?"</p>
<p>, "Yes, I'm traveling."</p>
<p>As expected of artificial intelligence, she remembered my business. Thinking about memory, I don't know how much storage capacity Eve-ssi's data has, but considering the times, it must have a huge capacity, right?</p>
<p>, "What would you like to buy today?"</p>
<p>, "There are things I want to buy... and things I want to sell, but let's look at the catalog first."</p>
<p>, "Yes~!"</p>
<p>The offline items sold at the Dark Merchant Guild branch reflected the characteristics of each city, and the items in the catalog were full of cyberpunk-esque things, befitting the Pyongyang Technology Special City.</p>
<p>There was also the Centipede Spine, a basic cyber implant that was implanted along the spine in the shape of a centipede, and there were many cybernetic eyes and prosthetic hands.</p>
<p>Ah, right. Pyongyang was a paradise for people who went with the modification trait, wasn't it? Since most of the items were cyber implants, there wasn't much I could use without getting modified.</p>
<p>Still... I should buy something since I came to Pyongyang. I found some pretty useful things in the catalog.</p>
<p>Give me a Centipede Spine SA-A091SGF, an artificial eye R-Z001, a pair of cybernetic hands 40F-5044, and a pair of cybernetic legs -09277 too.</p>
<p>Even if I don't use them, there will be a time when I can use them someday. In particular, the Centipede Spine SA-A091SGF, artificial eye R-Z001, and cybernetic hand 40F-5044 were not the top-of-the-line trims, but for some reason, they were cyber implants that were compatible with superpowers, so they were incredibly popular in the game.</p>
<p>Originally, if you got modified to gain tremendous physical abilities or special abilities, there were limitations on the superpower side. If you got modified, you either couldn't use superpowers at all, or something would go wrong.</p>
<p>Of course, you could use superpowers if you got very expensive, top-of-the-line custom modifications, but at that time, it was already the late game, and the trait tree was already set, so the usage was limited. And no one raised the modification and superpower traits at the same time, looking towards the very end, so it was a method that wasn't used.</p>
<p>But one user discovered that the Centipede Spine SA-A091SGF, artificial eye R-Z001, and cybernetic hand 40F-5044 were compatible with superpowers, and the situation changed.</p>
<p>To unleash superpowers among off-the-shelf products that weren't custom-made, the Centipede Spine SA-A091SGF, artificial eye R-Z001, and cybernetic hand 40F-5044 were almost the only options for each part. These three cyber implants played a role in creating countless builds in MegaCorp's youngest son.</p>
<p>There were many builds created that made MegaCorp's youngest son popular, such as a build that combined the artificial eye and pyromancer abilities to fire flames from the eyes like Superman or Cyclops, or a *mussou* warrior build that combined the hardening ability to simply harden the body with the Centipede Spine's acceleration and increased perception, or a *chidori* build that combined the cybernetic hand and electrokinesis abilities.</p>
<p>That's why I bought the three and the decent cybernetic legs. I was thinking of giving cyber implants to disabled superpower users or if my subordinates who were superpower users got hurt someday.</p>
<p>, "Would you like to take the items you bought with you? Or delivery?"</p>
<p>, "Please deliver them to the registered address."</p>
<p>It's better to have them delivered since I don't need them right away. There's no need to carry heavy cyber implants around.</p>
<p>I completed the payment with points and handed over the USB containing the BS Group's AI data that I had put aside. I spent money, so it was time to recharge.</p>
<p>, "This is AI data from the BS Group Research Institute. How much can you give me?"</p>
<p>I spoke quietly to Eve-ssi so that the people around me couldn't hear. Eve-ssi's eyes widened at the words "AI data" and she looked at me.</p>
<p>, "Is it really BS Group's data?"</p>
<p>, "Of course. Are you going to buy it?"</p>
<p>Eve-ssi closed her eyes for a moment at my words. She was clearly calculating how much would be a reasonable amount with her excellent calculation ability.</p>
<p>, "How about 82,724,326 points?"</p>
<p>Roughly taking off the numbers at the end, and 1 point is 1 dollar, so 82.72 million dollars... That's a little over 100 billion won. But it's AI research data from a large company, so isn't 100 billion a bit cheap? Eve-ssi quickly opened her mouth when she saw my worried expression.</p>
<p>, "I don't know the contents. I'll buy it first, review the contents, and give you more points if it's good."</p>
<p>Well, it's a huge amount to offer 100 billion without knowing the contents. Of course, even if I was a scammer, the Dark Merchant Guild's influence would allow them to get that 100 billion back, so that's why they offered it.</p>
<p>, "Hmm, I'll add a bonus to the amount you just offered."</p>
<p>, "What is it? Should I give you another piece of equipment?"</p>
<p>, "Let's shake hands once."</p>
<p>Eve-ssi chuckled at my words. She must be laughing because she knows that Eve-ssi is an AI, but there are sometimes people who try to touch her because of her beautiful appearance. But I didn't have such ulterior motives.</p>
<p>My heart was as clean as a well-polished mirror. I just had a thought while talking to Eve-ssi.</p>
<p>I even did it with a ghost, so can't I touch the AI's hologram? I was also wondering if the ability to influence heterogeneous beings that I had when I was the Golden Pillar might have disappeared when I upgraded to the crimson flesh pillar swimming in ejaculate, so I wanted to check it out.</p>
<p>, "I'm in hologram form, so you can't touch me?"</p>
<p>, "I still want to shake hands."</p>
<p>, "Then you'll sell it to me?"</p>
<p>, "Yes."</p>
<p>, "Here you go."</p>
<p>Eve-ssi smiled broadly at me, thinking it was a huge profit. I also smiled and raised a little abyss, shaking Eve-ssi's hand.</p>
<p>Oh! I can touch her! It was a different feeling than with a ghost. Is it because she's a hologram? Anyway, it seemed that the crimson flesh pillar swimming in ejaculate still had the ability to influence heterogeneous beings that the previous Golden Pillar had.</p>
<p>, "Ah! *Ahheut!*"</p>
<p>A surprised groan escaped Eve-ssi, as if she felt something too. I shook the hand I was holding up and down with a playful smile.</p>
<p>, "This is a handshake. Here's the USB."</p>
<p>, "Yes, yes! No, please plug it into the USB port below."</p>
<p>Since she's a hologram, she can't receive it directly, so she asked me to plug in the USB, so I nodded and plugged the USB into the port on the counter desk.</p>
<p>, "Hmm...! Ah! This is pretty good!"</p>
<p>As expected of an AI that is one of the top three in the world, Eve-ssi was interpreting the data I had obtained while saying, "Hmm...!"</p>
<p>, "This is at least 150 million points worth of data. I'll deposit it for you!"</p>
<p>Along with Eve-ssi's words, an alarm rang on the Dark Merchant Guild app that 150 million points had been deposited. I won't need to recharge points for a while... 150 million points is close to 200 billion won. I would have used all the money if there was an auction, though.</p>
<p>, "And as a reward for giving us such important data, I'll upgrade you to a Diamond-level member."</p>
<p>Oh! As if she read my mind, Eve-ssi gave me Diamond status, which allows me to participate in the Dark Merchant Guild auction.</p>
<p>, "I wanted to participate in the auction anyway, thank you, Eve-ssi."</p>
<p>, "If you're thankful, shake hands one more time."</p>
<p>Huh? Why is she doing this? Is she curious? She may have felt curiosity about the phenomenon that originated from me, just like she was exploring the AI data.</p>
<p>I raised a little abyss again, and this time I reached out my hand first. Eve-ssi looked at my hand for a moment, then took my hand.</p>
<p>, "Is this... what you call touch?"</p>
<p>, "Probably."</p>
<p>, "Thank you."</p>
<p>Hmm, it's certainly difficult for an AI to feel touch like this, even if they know what it is. I received a kind greeting from Eve-ssi and returned to the hotel. Looking at the time, it was around 9:55 PM. I'll have to wait a little longer for that waitress. I'll have to give her an unforgettable night instead of being a little late.</p>
