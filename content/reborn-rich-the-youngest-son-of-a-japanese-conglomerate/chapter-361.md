<p>"Hoo...."</p>
<p>Monday mornings are tough for everyone. Even I, with not much on my plate, dragged my heavy feet to the prep room. Maybe it's from the sparring yesterday, my body feels a bit stiff... Of course, with my regeneration ability, there's no way I'd still be stiff, it's just my mind not wanting to go to work making me feel that way, I guess.</p>
<p>Yesterday, after dinner at Mom's Punch, we all listened to Shimokawabe Airen-nim's advice on martial arts until late. Maybe it's because of the Saint of Fist Arts trait, but Shimokawabe Airen-nim's advice was absorbed like a sponge. Saionji Diana-ssi next to me was listening quite seriously too, so I had to take Saionji Diana-ssi home late at night.</p>
<p>"I came a bit early, should I grab some breakfast?"</p>
<p>I muttered to myself and got up, taking out a pre-made Neapolitan pasta from the fridge and microwaving it. *Bbibik*, the microwave beeped on time as I pressed the button, and the prep room door burst open.</p>
<p>"Teach! I'm gonna have some pudding for breakfast!"</p>
<p>"Excuse me..."</p>
<p>Seizawa Shii-ssi and Minamoto Risa-ssi came into the prep room and opened the fridge without hesitation. Seizawa Shii-ssi was a bit sulky on Friday because of my dirty jokes, but it seemed like she recovered over the weekend. She didn't feel awkward around me at all. That's a relief. She doesn't hold grudges.</p>
<p>"Ah! There's only ice cream left. I guess we ate all the pudding!"</p>
<p>"I can't fill it up every time, so you guys fill it up yourselves."</p>
<p>Seizawa Shii-ssi, disappointed by the lack of pudding, chose a lemon sherbet ice cream, and Minamoto Risa-ssi still went for the green tea flavor.</p>
<p>"Really, we can buy and put stuff in? I thought we weren't supposed to touch it!"</p>
<p>"Yeah. I'll try to fill it up as much as possible, but there might be times when I forget."</p>
<p>With the amount of money these girls have, they could fill this entire prep room every day, not just the fridge, so money won't be an issue. It would be a problem at other schools, but no one related to this school would think it's a bribe to the teacher.</p>
<p>As we were talking, *Ding!* The microwave finished, and I took out the instant Neapolitan pasta.</p>
<p>"Teach, you didn't eat breakfast?"</p>
<p>"Yeah, that's why I came a bit early. But why are you guys here so early?"</p>
<p>Come to think of it, it's still quite a while before the students need to be at school. Considering I came a little earlier to eat breakfast, Minamoto Risa-ssi and Seizawa Shii-ssi came way earlier than usual.</p>
<p>"Ah, that's..."</p>
<p>"Hello, Teacher."</p>
<p>Just as Seizawa Shii-ssi was about to explain, the prep room door opened again, and Konoe Akina-ssi came in. If even Konoe Akina-ssi came early... Ah! Club-related stuff. If it was class-related, then the vice-class president, Shido Tsubaki-ssi, would have come... So, the club president, Konoe Akina-ssi, and the vice-president, Minamoto Risa-ssi, came. Seizawa Shii-ssi probably just followed Minamoto Risa-ssi.</p>
<p>"Did you come early because of club stuff?"</p>
<p>"Ah, yes! We did some remodeling in the clubroom over the weekend, so we came to check it out this morning."</p>
<p>As expected, Saint Francisco Girls' Academy is different. The students just remodel the clubroom with their own money. If it was done over the weekend, it wouldn't be a major renovation, but what did they do? I'm getting a little curious.</p>
<p>"So! Teach, wanna come up with us?"</p>
<p>"Of course, I have to go with you. I'm your homeroom teacher and club advisor. I need to check it out."</p>
<p>"Let's go up, Teacher!"</p>
<p>The kids were urging me to go with them, so I had no choice but to put down the instant Neapolitan pasta I was eating and go up to the second-floor clubroom.</p>
<p>"We just left it to the contractors and we're seeing it for the first time today!"</p>
<p>"I'm kinda excited."</p>
<p>"Teacher will like it too."</p>
<p>Listening to the chattering girls next to me, I soon arrived at the door of the clubroom.</p>
<p>*Cheolkeok!*</p>
<p>Opening the door, the clubroom I knew was gone. It was a large clubroom that felt like some rich person's penthouse. The clubroom itself was large, so it felt even more like that.</p>
<p>The floor and walls, which were no different from a classroom, had all been luxuriously changed, and there was a king-size bed in the corner, as well as a sofa. Of course, both were top-of-the-line.</p>
<p>"Um...! Can I ask what the theme of the clubroom is?"</p>
<p>"I heard that there's something called a 'home date' during dates. So, we all thought about our future boyfriend's room and made it like that."</p>
<p>Konoe Akina-ssi answered my question. I can tell that everyone's imagination is lacking. Besides, if they have boyfriends with rooms like this, they're all from prestigious families.</p>
<p>"We can't do club activities this week because of the picnic, but next week we're going to have a 'dating experience' in here, in the boyfriend's room...! Teacher, you have to attend!"</p>
<p>I nodded at Minamoto Risa-ssi's words. She stuttered a bit on the word "boyfriend," so she seemed a little embarrassed, which was kind of cute. I looked around so Minamoto Risa-ssi wouldn't feel too awkward. Besides the sofa and bed, there was also a place that looked like a kitchen, with a fridge, gas stove, and sink.</p>
<p>"There's a fridge... and a sink and a gas stove?"</p>
<p>"Most of the classrooms designated as clubrooms can use water and gas. There are quite a few cooking clubs at Saint Francisco Girls' Academy."</p>
<p>"And cooking for your boyfriend is a girlfriend's virtue!!"</p>
<p>I see. As daughters of prestigious families, they'll probably do things like bridal classes in clubs, so cooking clubs and tea clubs would be popular. A girlfriend's virtue, I wonder what everyone's cooking skills are like.</p>
<p>"Okay, next week, everyone come up with dates you can have in the 'boyfriend's room' and we'll try them out. I'm going back down to the prep room, are you guys going to stay in the clubroom a bit longer?"</p>
<p>"Of course? It's not even time for school yet, so I'm going to lie down on that bed and laze around!"</p>
<p>"I'm going to carefully check if the remodeling was done properly."</p>
<p>"I'm going to move some of my favorite things from Teacher's fridge to the clubroom first."</p>
<p>Well, except for Konoe Akina-ssi, it's clear that they just wanted to create a place to laze around, not a boyfriend's room.</p>
<p>Konoe Akina-ssi was looking around the room, carefully checking if there was anything wrong, and Seizawa Shii-ssi was yelling "Yahoo!" and rolling around on the bed. Minamoto Risa-ssi was looking at me with eyes that said, "Why aren't you leaving?"</p>
<p>"Okay, let's go down."</p>
<p>Minamoto Risa-ssi and I left the clubroom and went back down to the first floor. Unlike the friendly Konoe Akina-ssi and the energetic Seizawa Shii-ssi, Minamoto Risa-ssi was a bit quiet, so we came down the stairs silently without saying a word.</p>
<p>"Teacher, do you have something like a basket?"</p>
<p>"Use this."</p>
<p>It wasn't the basket Minamoto Risa-ssi asked for, but I still had a shopping bag I used to bring my Korean class materials to the prep room, so I handed it to Minamoto Risa-ssi.</p>
<p>"Ah...!"</p>
<p>As I handed over the shopping bag, our hands touched, and Minamoto Risa-ssi froze up a bit. Of course, the shopping bag also fell to the floor with a *Tak* sound. How little immunity to men does she have to freeze up over something like this?</p>
<p>"Ah, sorry. I didn't touch you on purpose."</p>
<p>"I know. I was just... a little surprised."</p>
<p>Minamoto Risa-ssi was a little flustered as she picked up the fallen shopping bag and opened the fridge to put in ice cream. As expected, even in a flustered situation, she doesn't miss out on the green tea Häagen-Dazs. Seizawa Shii-ssi also picked out a few ice creams that other people might like, and Minamoto Risa-ssi said, "I'm leaving now...!" and hurried out of the prep room.</p>
<p>First of all, to capture Minamoto Risa-ssi, I need to find out why she has this unknown fear or hesitation towards men.</p>
<p>This related information wasn't even in the information recorded by the Taira Information Team, so it was probably something from the very distant past and likely an internal matter for the Minamoto family. I don't know how I'll find out in the future, but at least I have a goal of what to do, so the feeling of frustration has disappeared a bit.</p>
<p>I finished eating the still-warm Neapolitan pasta and started preparing for today's schedule.</p>
<p>###</p>
<p>"Did you all listen well in class today?"</p>
<p>"Yes!"</p>
<p>Before I knew it, it was time for Monday's closing ceremony. Everyone seemed to be looking forward to some event today, their eyes sparkling.</p>
<p>"So, we're going to Miyashi Department Store together today to prepare for the picnic we talked about last week, is there anyone who can't go?"</p>
<p>Idol Uenojo Karen-ssi, who said in advance that she couldn't go to the picnic, didn't come to school today, so no one raised their hand. Only hikikomori Oofuchi Erika-ssi was looking around a bit, hesitating.</p>
<p>"Okay, class president, what did you decide to do?"</p>
<p>"We're just going to go to the department store and the supermarket together right now to buy swimsuits and food and stuff."</p>
<p>I asked Minamoto Risa-ssi, but it didn't seem like she had planned anything in particular. The department store is quite far away, and I can't take the daughters of prestigious families on the bus, so what should I do? As I was thinking, Shido Tsubaki-ssi raised her hand.</p>
<p>"I've prepared the car. I think we can go in that. Teacher, you have a license, right?"</p>
<p>That's a relief. I was getting a headache thinking about taking the kids on the bus. Shido Tsubaki-ssi has a lot of common sense.</p>
<p>"Of course, I have a license. Then let's follow Shido-ssi to where the car is parked."</p>
<p>At my words, the female students got up from their seats, chatting amicably. The picnic we're going to tomorrow is for two nights and three days, so it didn't feel like a picnic, but going to the car together now actually felt like a picnic.</p>
