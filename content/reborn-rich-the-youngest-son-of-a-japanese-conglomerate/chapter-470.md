<p>After arriving in Miyashi in the early morning, I didn't get much sleep and went to work feeling tired. If I slept any longer, I felt like I'd oversleep and be late, so I arrived earlier than Kurayamada Kirie and sat down today.</p>
<p>I was sitting and drinking coffee when I checked the fax that came in at the faculty office, and it said that the midterm exam scores would be released around lunchtime today. There's no hand-grading or anything, so the results are coming out pretty quickly.</p>
<p>There's going to be a big fuss when the exam results come out. We decided to give awards to the top 6 in the class and students whose scores improved a lot, so everyone studied hard for the exam.</p>
<p>Our class was a special class that other students avoided, but there were a lot of talented kids, so I couldn't be sure who would be at the top.</p>
<p>In my prediction, Oofuchi Erika, who is rumored to be good at studying, would be number one in the class, and for some reason, I had a prejudice that glasses characters are intelligence characters, so Shido Tsubaki would be the runner-up. Of course, it was just a rough guess, not a detailed analysis, so there was a high possibility that I was wrong.</p>
<p>Woong! Woong!</p>
<p>I was looking at the fax and predicting the students' rankings when my phone vibrated and I got a text message. When I checked, it was Dang So-hwa.</p>
<p>Dang So-hwa: About what Ryuta-gun asked me to find out. I looked into it a bit?</p>
<p>What did I ask her to find out...? I asked Dang So-hwa for several things, so I don't know what she's talking about.</p>
<p>Taira Ryuta: What are you talking about out of the things I asked you to find out?</p>
<p>Dang So-hwa: Information about Jinildang.</p>
<p>Ah...! That's right. I heard from Sakagami Rena that Jinildang's combat personnel had moved to Yamanashi Prefecture, where St. Francisco Girls' Academy is located, so I was going to ask Dang So-hwa about it too.</p>
<p>Kurotsuki-dono had his own information network in the underworld and back alleys, and Ryuto Construction, its successor, inherited that information network as it was, so it had its own information power.</p>
<p>Dang So-hwa: I don't know the exact reason why Jinildang went to Yamanashi Prefecture. It seems like Jinildang is cracking down on it a lot. But according to what our informants have found out....</p>
<p>Taira Ryuta: According to?</p>
<p>Dang So-hwa: Judging from the way Jinildang's terrorists are moving, it seems like they're looking for something like a relic.</p>
<p>Relic? It was clear that the relic Dang So-hwa was talking about wasn't just a historical relic. She must be talking about an artifact with some kind of power. Since a relatively large terrorist group like Jinildang is looking for it, it was likely to be a very powerful artifact.</p>
<p>I don't know much about Japan's hidden items, so I don't know what relic Jinildang is looking for, but I found out that they're looking for something, so I thought I could start from here. Or I could subtly ask Kurayamada Kirie....</p>
<p>,Good morning."</p>
<p>Kurayamada Kirie must not be a saint either, because as soon as I thought about Kurayamada Kirie, she came into the education preparation room, greeting me good morning. Kurayamada Kirie greeted me with the same glaring eyes as usual.</p>
<p>,Good morning."</p>
<p>,I guess the scores are coming out today."</p>
<p>Kurayamada Kirie glanced at the official document that came by fax and opened her mouth. She seemed subtly curious about who would get good grades.</p>
<p>,Who do you think will be number one?"</p>
<p>,Wouldn't Oofuchi Erika, who has been doing well, be number one? Personally, I hope Minamoto-yang is number two."</p>
<p>She picked Oofuchi Erika for first place just like I did, but the second place was different. Minamoto Lisa also had good grades last year considering she didn't come to school much, so she was definitely a candidate for second place. This is the second exam, not the first.</p>
<p>,Let's go to the morning assembly first."</p>
<p>,Yes."</p>
<p>We left the education preparation room and headed to the special class classroom. The door opened with a deureureuk sound, and I could feel the students looking at me. The students seemed to know that today was the day the scores would be released, and they had slightly expectant eyes. Of course, some troublemakers didn't look forward to it at all.</p>
<p>,Yahoo~☆ Ryuta-cchi~! Good morning!"</p>

<p>,Hello, teacher!"</p>

<p>,The scores will probably come out around lunchtime today. Everyone worked hard, so I hope the scores aren't too bad."</p>
<p>The students' reactions to my story were mixed. Of course, the confident students had expectant eyes, and the insecure students had anxious expressions.</p>
<p>,Ah! The scores are coming out already...?"</p>
<p>,I'm going to get an earful from my older brothers and sisters."</p>

<p>Unlike the noisy reactions of the students who were afraid of the scores coming out, the students who were confident in their scores sat still with confident expressions. It was like they wanted the scores to come out soon.</p>
<p>,Ryuta-cchi~! You'll keep your promise, right?"</p>

<p>That's true, but I'm a teacher, so I can't say that openly. Of course, Seizawa Shii was right. I'm going to walk around with a student and pick out a gift, and it's not just that, I'm going to have a meal with them, so I can't say it's not a date, but as a teacher, I can't directly say it's a date.</p>

<p>,Then go to the secondary analysis site and look for it."</p>
<p>,I understand!"</p>
<p>The subordinate quickly got up and disappeared at Kurayama Kazuma's command. It had been quite a while since Kurayama Kazuma left his family and realized that there was an incredible relic in some of the documents he had taken with him.</p>
<p>However, the documents were made up of extremely difficult ciphers to decipher, so it took a long time to decipher them, and it was only recently that he learned the identity and approximate location of the relic.</p>
<p>The description of the relic written in the deciphered document was as follows.</p>
<p>■■■-shin cut off the root of the earthquake and hid it on the right side of Mount Fuji, so no one could find it. Whoever finds the root of the earthquake can control earthquakes at will...</p>
<p>Kurayama Kazuma is searching for this Shinne, known as the root of the earthquake. Naturally, if others heard this story, they would likely dismiss it as merely a forgotten legend or myth, claiming that the root of the earthquake doesn’t exist. However, Kurayama Kazuma believed that if it were just a folktale, there would be no reason to write this story in code.</p>
<p>And he showed the document to a miko who had the Eye of Truth in Jinildang, and the miko told Kurayama Kazuma that the contents of the document were true and that the root of the earthquake really existed.</p>
<p>The miko with the Eye of Truth had helped Jinildang countless times with her ability to see the truth, so Kurayama Kazuma had absolute trust in the miko.</p>
<p>Finally, Kurayama Kazuma was convinced that this story was true because he had seen the thing from another story in the document.</p>
<p>He had seen with his own eyes a swordsman wielding the sword of the god called ■■■-shin, who cut off the source of the earthquake in this story. It was a tsurugi type weapon from ancient Japan, and it looked exactly like the one described in the document he had. He saw the swordsman slicing through a tank on the battlefield and fully believed the story.</p>
<p>If the root of the earthquake could really control the earthquake as described in the document, he could become the god of Japan.</p>
<p>On the surface, Jinildang was an extreme right-wing terrorist group that supported the emperor's rule of the country, but in fact, it was just a pretext for Kurayama Kazuma to increase his power. Kurayama Kazuma had a secret ambition.</p>
<p>,I should tell Kirie soon. If it's near Mount Fuji, it could be St. Francisco Girls' Academy where Kirie is."</p>
<p>Kurayama Kazuma opened his mouth quietly. It was something he had been hiding from his daughter Kurayamada Kirie, but since he was doing things near Mount Fuji, his daughter had to know about this fact.</p>
