<p>As Kyoken and Kenju's Swords drew almost right in front of her Nose, Shimokawabe Airen opened her Eyes. And she began to swing her Sword like a Storm. In a situation where she would inevitably be hit by one Attack if she blocked the other, the only way was to draw in the attacks as much as possible until both attacks converged to a single point, then parry them at once.</p>
<p>Of course, this Method was almost impossible because one could not predict the Opponent's Sword Path in advance, but because Kyoken and Kenju's Swordsmanship, Katori Kensatsu Shinto Style, had its Roots in the same Shinto Style as the Kashima Kiichi Shinto Style that Shimokawabe Airen had mastered, Shimokawabe Airen could roughly predict the Sword Path and block the fatal blow. Chachang! Shimokawabe Airen, who had parried both Swords at once, did not merely stop at blocking the Attack but continued to swing her Sword.</p>
<p>She launched an Attack, showing Lightning-like movements, similar to her Disciple Alik. However, Kyoken and Kenju, Swordsmen who had stained their Swords with the Blood of countless people, did not show any Gap at the Failure of their Sword strikes and calmly continued to block Shimokawabe Airen's Sword as much as possible. Chaechaechaechaeng! Dozens of times in a row, the sound of Metal clashing with Metal, and the orange Sparks from that clash, spread around like Fireworks.</p>
<p>Their respective Momentum clashed without an inch of retreat, displaying a fierce battle. As she swung her Sword without rest, Shimokawabe Airen's Face gradually became flushed. Seeing that, Kyoken and Kenju thought Shimokawabe Airen was gradually getting tired in the continuous 2-on-1 Match, but that was a Mistake, different from the fact. Although Shimokawabe Airen had fought many times as the hidden Sword of St. Francis Girls' Academy for the past few years, it was the first time she felt so alive as today.</p>
<p>It had been a long time since she had a Match with a Swordsman like herself, but more than anything, the Joy of being able to fight fiercely with a superior Opponent was the Cause of Shimokawabe Airen's Face flushing. Kyoken and Kenju, unaware of that, began to use the Killing Moves of Katori Kensatsu Shinto Style to push Shimokawabe Airen even further. -Katori Kensatsu Shinto Style (Katori Sword Killing Shinto Style) Sword Killing Wall. -Katori Kensatsu Shinto Style (Katori Sword Killing Shinto Style) Sword Killing Point.</p>
<p>As Kyoken swung his Sword, a dark red Aura violently rose, dominating the Surroundings, and as Kenju swung his Sword, the dark red Aura split into dozens, shooting out like blades. It was an exquisite combined Attack technique that followed an area Attack with a surprise fatal Attack. It was clearly a threatening situation, but Shimokawabe Airen smiled. At the same time, blue ghost fire (鬼火) started burning on the blade of Shimokawabe Airen's sword, Kamiwa.</p>
<p>-Kashima Kiichi Shinto Style (鹿島鬼一神道流) Kiichi's Domain Slash (鬼一領斬). -Kashima Kiichi Shinto Style (鹿島鬼一神道流) Sakura Petal Flow (桜花弁流し: Scattering Cherry Blossom Petals). As if the two Martial Arts Forms unfolded almost simultaneously, Shimokawabe Airen's Sword moved with immense speed. After splitting Kyoken's Sword Killing Wall in half with a single blow, she flowed all of the dozens of split dark red Auras, the Sword Killing Points, behind her with Sakura Petal Flow.</p>
<p>"It was very enjoyable, but let's end it now. My Disciple is looking at me with an Expression that wishes for it to end quickly." Shimokawabe Airen glanced at Alik, who was watching the battle with an Expression that screamed, 'I have to go to Ryuta quickly...!', and opened her Mouth towards Kyoken and Kenju.</p>
<p>"Arrogant!"</p>
<p>"Despite being tired, you talk a good game!" When Shimokawabe Airen spoke as if she could finish them both at any time, Kyoken and Kenju erupted in Anger. While continuously exchanging blows, they had been feeling that something was going wrong, but for a female Swordsman less than half their Age, who had wielded Swords for decades, to disregard them, their Anger exploded. They slashed their Swords as if to strike down Shimokawabe Airen immediately, but perhaps due to Anger, their Movements unconsciously became larger.</p>
<p>Shimokawabe Airen did not miss that Gap. -Kashima Kiichi Shinto Style (鹿島鬼一神道流) Spatial Cutting - Yorozuhana (空間切-萬花: Space Slice - Myriad Flowers). Like cherry blossoms swirling in the spring breeze, hundreds of thousands of Sword Flowers (劍花) bloomed from Shimokawabe Airen's sword. The beautiful petals, almost unbelievable in reality, filled the entire surroundings, and each petal carried tremendous power capable of tearing space apart.</p>
<p>"Kraaaagh!"</p>
<p>"Uwaaaah!!" Though it looked incredibly beautiful, Shimokawabe Airen's countless Sword Flowers were slashing Kyoken and Kenju's Bodies. When the numerous Sword Flowers melted and vanished into empty space, Kyoken and Kenju had become Blood-men, their bodies entirely covered in blood. Although they appeared to have no external injuries, the Sword Flowers of Space Severing - Ten Thousand Flowers were so sharp that their wounds were not easily visible.</p>
<p>And due to the unique Space-cutting Aura of Kashima Kiichi Shinto Style, the Wounds would not close, and Blood was continuously flowing out. Shimokawabe Airen slowly walked towards Kyoken and Kenju.</p>
<p>"In fact, it was hard to defeat you easily. But thanks to Alik, you became angry, and because of that Anger, your Movements became larger, allowing me to find a Gap."</p>
<p>In fact, to use a Killing Move like Space Severing - Ten Thousand Flowers, one inevitably had to expose a Gap. Especially, facing Kyoken or Kenju, who had trained their Swords for decades, if she used a big technique, there was a Possibility that she would be attacked before fully deploying the technique. So Shimokawabe Airen deliberately mocked Kyoken and Kenju with a Way of Speaking that implied she could finish them immediately, to make them angry.</p>
<p>Normally, experienced Kyoken and Kenju wouldn't get angry at this level of Provocation, but when they actually thought they might be Defeated by Shimokawabe Airen, who was less than half their Age, they unconsciously became angry. The two couldn't hold back and erupted in Anger; that Anger made their Movements larger and stiffened their Bodies. The Result of that Anger, as Shimokawabe Airen saw, was them kneeling in a pool of Blood, facing Death from excessive bleeding.</p>
<p>"You, who have killed countless people, are not righteous people, but since you gave me Pleasure, I will grant you a peaceful Death."</p>
<p>"Ne-eoh...!"</p>
<p>"Th-this bitch...!" Shimokawabe Airen swung her Sword at the slowly dying Kyoken and Kenju. After a flash of blue Light, Kyoken and Kenju's Necks floated in empty space for a moment, then rolled unceremoniously on the park Floor. Shimokawabe Airen briefly looked at their Corpses, then swung her Sword to shake off the Blood, and sheathed it in its Scabbard. And turning her Body, she saw Alik and Naruse Shiyo waiting for her.</p>
<p>"Let's go now."</p>
<p>"Moka Sabu. Alik has to go to Ryuta."</p>
<p>"Moka Sabu-nim! I did various Experiments in this battle!" Although they left ten Corpses, the three, with an Attitude no different from usual, talking, slowly walked towards the Parking Lot where they had parked their bikes.</p>
<p>"Minobucho Park Group C battle is over!"</p>
<p>"Since it was a Swordsmen's battle, the Scene must be covered in Blood. Send in the cleanup team." At the Words of the Information agent watching the Scene on screen, Asada Ei ordered the deployment of the cleanup team. To prevent the police and several prestigious families from knowing that they had caused a commotion in Miyashi, they had created a separate cleanup team. When the battle ended, they quickly deployed the cleanup team, operating it on a quite large Scale so that no traces of battle or human Corpses would be found.</p>
<p>"Over there...!"</p>
<p>"Don't call out, just tell me what's happening immediately."</p>
<p>"A pedestrian has entered Group C Park!" Even if it was a sparsely populated park due to ghost rumors, there were still one or two people who used the park daily. Unfortunately, one of those one or two daily pedestrians had now set Foot in the park. Asada Ei thought for a moment, then opened her Mouth.</p>
<p>"Tell them to make him Faint. If they clean up neatly while he's unconscious, he'll think it was a Dream or a ghost."</p>
<p>"Understood!" Although she said "make him Faint" simply, if that pedestrian reported it to the police, the Problem would escalate. Asada Ei was watching the Group C screen with a tense Expression. The pedestrian is seen discovering the Corpse and being startled. And as he raises his phone, just then, the cleanup team appeared from behind and choked him unconscious. Even if he wakes up and reports it, since the cleanup team would have already erased all traces, the police would also think the reporting pedestrian made a false report.</p>
<p>"Haaah...! Tell them to clean up quickly before he wakes up." Seeing that the pedestrian had been successfully knocked unconscious, Asada Ei let out a Sigh of Relief. Because of this incident, the urban legend of "Minobucho Park Bloody Corpses" would spread in the urban legend Community, but that was none of Asada Ei's concern.</p>
<p>"Team Leader-nim, please check the G Camera!" No sooner had she let out that Sigh of Relief than the Information agent shouted with an urgent Expression. If it's the G Camera.... it was a Camera secretly installed at the Place where Anyaji's Command post, where Takakusaki Gengo was, was located.</p>
<p>On Anyaji's side, to grasp all Situations and to help in unfavorable Battle Situations, they had rented a commercial office in the middle of where everyone was fighting and were waiting, but Athena Mannerheim had informed them, allowing them to install the Camera in advance.</p>
<p>"...Where on earth is he going?" Takakusaki Gengo, who had been standing still for a while, got up from his Seat and was seen leaving the office. Grasping Takakusaki Gengo's movements was one of the most important Missions.</p>
<p>"With the CCTV around the commercial building, I will try to Track him."</p>
<p>"First, have one team exclusively Track Takakusaki Gengo and continue to Track him. And...." Asada Ei stood up from her Seat. And she opened her Mouth towards the Joseodang combat group sitting on one side.</p>
<p>"It would be good for us to also achieve one Achievement, wouldn't it? Everyone, stand up."</p>
