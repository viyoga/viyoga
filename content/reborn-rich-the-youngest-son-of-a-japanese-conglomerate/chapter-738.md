<p>-Diana Rich: I've arrived in Japan. Let's meet briefly after your work is done.</p>
<p>It was a message from Diana Rich, the Deputy Director of the Federal Psychic Management Department. When did she come to Japan again? I thought she said she had things to do in Korea, but it seems like she's finished with those.</p>
<p>She's probably trying to give me U.S. citizenship. It seems to be happening faster than I thought. I figured it would take a little longer. So, did she come to Japan because of me?</p>
<p>This message came while I was teaching my Korean class. So, I guess I can meet her after class. The class schedule was the same in the third semester, so on Tuesdays and Wednesdays, school was over after Korean class and the closing ceremony.</p>
<p>"Ryuta-jji~! Is this going to be on the test today?"</p>
<p>"If I say it won't be on the test during class, it won't be. What I emphasized will be on it. That part was just glossed over in class, so it probably won't be."</p>
<p>Even though Seizawa Shii's grades aren't bad, she was quite actively asking about Korean while we were walking to the special class building. Seizawa Shii is automatically accepted into St. Francisco Women's University if she's a student at St. Francisco Girls' Academy, so she doesn't even need to try hard on this exam.</p>
<p>"Aren't you going to St. Francisco Women's University anyway? Why are you working so hard?"</p>
<p>"Ah, just because~! I might go to Korea with Ryuta-jji, you know? I want to be able to do some free talking then~☆"</p>
<p>To my question, Seizawa Shii said with a shy expression that she was thinking about a Korean date with me. The special class students taking my class suddenly started pouring out questions about Korean to me.</p>
<p>"You should have worked hard during class~! I have something to do after class today, so let's go back to the classroom quickly."</p>
<p>"Eh? I was going to talk with Ryuta-jji in the club room today...!"</p>
<p>"That's right! Sensei! Don't forget you're still the faculty advisor for the Dating Experience Club~!"</p>
<p>"Woo~! Woo~!"</p>
<p>As expected, when I said I was going straight home after class today, I was met with jeers and accusations. But it can't be helped. I can't change my appointment with Diana Rich.</p>
<p>"I'll finish quickly today without the closing ceremony, so go home and study. We're going to the ski resort next weekend, so you have to work hard before then."</p>
<p>The special class female students and I, who went straight to the special class classroom, finished class for the day without any special closing ceremony. Even if St. Francisco Girls' Academy doesn't emphasize grades, they still had to get them to some extent. It's a matter of the family's face, not just the students' grades.</p>
<p>"Ah...! I don't want to study~!"</p>
<p>"It's the last exam, so just bear with it a little longer."</p>
<p>Kujo Kina grumbled that she didn't want to study, but I comforted her to bear with it a little longer and then left the classroom and headed to the teacher's preparation room.</p>
<p>There was nothing on the fax machine. There were no work emails either. Hmm, I guess I don't have to work overtime.</p>
<p>-Taira Ryuta: I'm finishing up now. Where should I go?</p>
<p>I immediately sent a text message to Diana Rich. Actually, I wasn't in a hurry, but I thought I should deal with annoying things quickly.</p>
<p>-Diana Rich: I'll send you the address. Come here.</p>
<p>I checked the address that Diana Rich linked, and it was a cafe in a corner of Miyashi's downtown area. After looking at the address, I nodded and packed my things.</p>
<p>"Master, do you have an appointment today?"</p>
<p>When I came out of the special class building, Sakagami Rena was waiting for me. Since I said I had an appointment, it seemed like she was waiting because she was curious about what it was, as one of my information team leaders.</p>
<p>"Ah, this."</p>
<p>I subtly showed Sakagami Rena my phone screen. Sakagami Rena, who quickly read the contents of the screen, nodded as if she understood.</p>
<p>"Shall I go with you?"</p>
<p>"No, what could happen?"</p>
<p>When I shook my head and said that to Sakagami Rena's words, Sakagami Rena frowned. She didn't seem to like my answer.</p>
<p>"Didn't you say that Master lost the Abyss yesterday and should be careful? Why are you talking like you have such a safety insensitivity?"</p>
<p>"The American side probably doesn't even think about going up against the Black Knight. They're already having a hard time because of the psychic terrorist group organized by Famine, so they wouldn't try to add me to that."</p>
<p>Sakagami Rena pointed out that I had a safety insensitivity, but I reassured Sakagami Rena, saying that I didn't.</p>
<p>"Okay. But still, be careful, Master."</p>
<p>"Yeah, of course, it's my body, so I'll be careful. Don't worry. Rena will save me if something happens, right?"</p>
<p>"Of course, because you're Master!"</p>
<p>After reassuring Sakagami Rena like that, I stopped by home and took a taxi to the place I promised to meet Diana Rich. At that time, my phone vibrated. I wondered if she was urging me to come quickly, so I looked at my phone, but it wasn't Diana Rich.</p>
<p>-Taira Kanna: Brother~! Could we possibly meet today?</p>
<p>It was Taira Kanna. What's wrong? She's asking to meet today, but I don't know how long my conversation with Diana Rich will last, so today was a bit much.</p>
<p>-Taira Ryuta: I don't think I can because I have a prior engagement today.... Is it urgent?</p>
<p>-Taira Kanna: No~ it's not urgent. Then, would tomorrow be okay? I wanted to treat you to a meal.</p>
<p>It was just a message asking to have a meal. There's no reason to refuse, so it would be good to meet once. Taira Kanna also has power in the Taira Group, so it would be good to be friendly.</p>
<p>-Taira Ryuta: Okay. Then let's meet tomorrow evening.</p>
<p>-Taira Kanna: Yes~! See you tomorrow, Brother!</p>
<p>After finishing the chat with Taira Kanna, I arrived in Miyashi's downtown area. I paid the taxi fare and got off in the downtown area.</p>
<p>The cafe where Diana Rich was waiting was a bit out of the way. I looked at the screen of my smartphone and went into the alley towards the cafe.</p>
<p>After walking for a while, an antique-designed cafe appeared. When I opened the door, the small bell on the door rang, announcing my arrival. A bearded man who seemed to be the cafe owner looked at me while wiping a cup with a dry towel.</p>
<p>"It's here."</p>
<p>Diana Rich raised her hand and called me, as if she had noticed my arrival from the sound of the bell. Diana Rich was sitting at a corner table.</p>
<p>"Give me oolong tea."</p>
<p>I passed the counter seat, ordered from the cafe owner, and went to sit down where Diana Rich was.</p>
<p>"Hello, Dante-ssi."</p>
<p>"Are you calling me that already?"</p>
<p>As I sat down, Diana Rich grinned and called me Dante. Dante was the name Han Yu-gyeong gave me.</p>
<p>"The documents are out, so Dante is a real person."</p>
<p>"So, what will I be called as Dante?"</p>
<p>To my question, Diana Rich smiled and handed me a passport and some documents. First, I opened the passport and checked it.</p>
<p>The passport had my picture, which I don't know when it was taken, and the name Dante Lee Astor was written on it.</p>
<p>"Dante Lee Astor?"</p>
<p>"The Astor family isn't very famous, but it's a loyal family that runs a small business. The middle name is because the Astor family's daughter-in-law, who is your identity's mother, is of Korean descent, so that's how the middle name was chosen."</p>
<p>I see. I briefly took out my phone and searched for the Astor family, and it was a family that ran a mid-sized factory located in Pennsylvania Erie County.</p>
<p>"Well, it's appropriate. Not very famous... not attracting attention...."</p>
<p>"That's right. Of course, don't even think about receiving any inheritance."</p>
<p>"I have a lot of money."</p>
<p>I had trivial small talk with Diana Rich and checked the documents placed under the passport. I could understand to some extent because I was studying English, but I asked Diana Rich about words I didn't know and examined the documents in detail.</p>
<p>There was nothing special. They were documents that needed to be signed to fabricate that a person named Dante Lee Astor existed, and because of the benefits I would get from becoming an American.</p>
<p>"First of all...."</p>
<p>We stopped talking. It was because the cafe owner brought the oolong tea. I said thank you, received the oolong tea, and took a sip. Diana Rich had arrived earlier and was drinking coffee, and she asked the cafe owner for a refill.</p>
<p>"We were talking about 'first of all'."</p>
<p>"First of all, I'll explain the brief things you need to know about Dante Lee Astor. You should know at least the basics, right?"</p>
<p>That's true. If I meet someone as Dante Astor, I'll have to answer questions that I can't refuse, so it's important to know something.</p>
<p>"First of all, you were homeschooled for high school education, and you studied abroad in Korea for university, graduated from Korea Catholic University, and returned to the United States."</p>
<p>Homeschooling and studying abroad, it seemed like they designed my life to make it as difficult as possible for people to recognize me.</p>
<p>"The details are written here in the documents, so please check them. And here."</p>
<p>What Diana Rich handed me was a Driver's license. It was a license with my picture, which I don't know when it was taken, and the name Dante Lee Astor written on it. You can't submit a passport as an ID in the United States, so it's better to submit a license as an ID.</p>
<p>"You've signed everything, so you're an American now."</p>
<p>"Well, please take care of me."</p>
<p>I shook hands with Diana Rich. We said please take care of each other and chatted until I finished drinking the oolong tea, then I packed the documents, passport, and Driver's license in my briefcase and left the cafe.</p>
<p>"I should have just said yes to meeting Taira Kanna."</p>
<p>The conversation ended faster than I thought, so I should have accepted the dinner appointment with Taira Kanna earlier. I can't change the appointment now, so I decided to pack some things to eat in Miyashi and go home.</p>
