<p>"...I hereby declare the 137th Sports Day open!"</p>
<p>Daigo Terue's simple opening speech for the Sports Day ended, and St. Francis Girls' Academy High School Sports Day began.</p>
<p>For reference, the middle school Sports Day was held in the spring, so middle school students had the day off today. Due to the nature of the combined middle and high school, it would be quite crowded if all the middle and high school students came out to the playground, and the difference in physique and athletic ability was so great that the games could end up being too one-sided.</p>
<p>The first event was archery, which was a class-versus-class competition, so there were four classes per grade, making a total of 12 classes competing in a tournament. I wondered how 12 teams could compete, so I looked at today's Sports Day schedule and saw that four teams would receive a bye each to make the tournament bracket. The homeroom teachers of each class came out to draw lots to determine the teams that would receive a bye and the matchups, and I was the fourth in line, starting with the 3rd grade teachers.</p>
<p>Two bye teams came out before me, so the probability was quite low, but since I knew my luck stat well, I didn't expect much.</p>
<p>-Yamashiro Ryuta-sensei of the Special Class, please come forward.</p>
<p>The broadcast called my name, so I walked to the lottery box in front of the podium.</p>
<p>"Sensei, you're so handsome!"</p>
<p>"Woo~!"</p>
<p>As soon as I went out, several shouts were heard from the Special Class. Seriously, just pick one: handsome or booing....</p>
<p>Perhaps because of my class's over-the-top reactions, the atmosphere loosened up considerably, and shouts like "Korean teacher, fighting!" started to be heard from other classes as well.</p>
<p>"Please pick one lottery ball."</p>
<p>As the staff member said, I put my hand in the box and picked one of the balls I grabbed. The number was 5. Since byes were written as "Bye-1" and so on, it was obvious that I wasn't going to get a bye team. When I showed the ball I picked to the staff, they announced my ball number on the broadcast.</p>
<p>-3rd Grade Special Class, Number 5.</p>
<p>I glanced at the bracket and saw that the opposing team had not yet been determined. I slowly walked back to the front of the Special Class, and everyone was giving me a thumbs down and booing, as if disappointed that I didn't get a bye. Of course, they weren't booing seriously, but laughing playfully, so all the students around us were laughing.</p>
<p>"Sorry. But you can do well, right?"</p>
<p>"It's not a big deal even if we have to play one more game."</p>
<p>Shido Tsubaki, the captain of the archery team, said calmly. In fact, there were many events at the Sports Day, so class-versus-class matches couldn't take too long. Therefore, until the final round, each person was only given the opportunity to shoot three arrows, so the game ended with a total of thirty arrows from both teams. The preparation time before shooting was also quite tight, so each game didn't even take ten minutes. Therefore, the pressure from the number of games wasn't that great.</p>
<p>"I won the archery competition at the New Idol Star Athletics Championships! Don't worry, Sensei!"</p>
<p>Uenozono Karen, one of the archery team members, said confidently. Oh, there's a New Idol Star Athletics Championships too? That's kind of interesting. I should search for it later.</p>
<p>After some time, the lottery ended. Our game was the third, against 2nd Grade Class C, who drew number 6. If we won this game, we would play against 1st Grade Class B, who had a bye.</p>
<p>While the lottery was being drawn, the staff members finished installing the targets and the safety barriers behind the targets. Large screens were set up throughout the playground so that people could see the targets and the students shooting the arrows. As expected of a rich school, they installed so many of these screens.</p>
<p>"2nd Grade Class C has the vice-captain of the archery club and several archery club members. They're definitely not a weak team, so don't let your guard down."</p>
<p>Shido Tsubaki, who had already gathered the archery team members, told us about the opposing team's strengths. She's amazing. She's seriously gathering information about the opposing team even at the Sports Day....</p>
<p>As I expected, the games ended quickly. This was because of the method where one person shot all three arrows given to them in a row, but shooting in a row caused concentration to waver, resulting in many ridiculous arrows being shot, so the outcome was easily decided. Meanwhile, calm students showed off their skills by concentrating well, drawing admiration from the viewers.</p>
<p>-3rd Grade Special Class, 2nd Grade Class C, please come up to the arena.</p>
<p>"It's our turn."</p>
<p>"Okay, everyone do your best."</p>
<p>"We'll definitely win!"</p>
<p>Shido Tsubaki, who told us not to let our guard down but didn't seem worried, went to the arena with a nonchalant expression, followed by Uenozono Karen, Oofuchi Erika, Sakagami Rena, and Urabe Konoka with various expressions. However, only Uenozono Karen was waving her hand to the audience and giving fan service with ease, as befitting an idol.</p>
<p>"Waaaah~!! KAREN! KAREN!"</p>
<p>As expected of an idol, the reaction was no joke. Even the opposing team, 2nd Grade Class C, was chanting "Karen, Karen."</p>
<p>The first was Shido Tsubaki. Since Shido Tsubaki didn't get nervous easily, she was perfect for the vanguard in this kind of game. If Oofuchi Erika, who had a history of being a hikikomori, was the vanguard, there was a possibility that she would make mistakes due to nervousness, but if Shido Tsubaki, who stepped up as the vanguard, did well, the students behind her would also be less nervous.</p>
<p>Shido Tsubaki drawing and aiming the large bow unique to Japanese archery. It was 8 points. Even 8 points was quite good compared to the previous games.</p>
<p>As if she had gotten a feel for it, she shot the remaining two arrows in a row, and they were all 9 points. A total of 26 points. This was more than just pretty good.</p>
<p>The next turn was the opponent's, and judging from their skillful posture, it seemed like it was the vice-captain of the archery club that Shido Tsubaki had warned us about.</p>
<p>The score was 9, 10, 8, for a total of 27 points. The wind seemed to have blown quite strongly when they shot the last arrow, which seemed to be the reason for the lower score. Still, they shoot pretty well?</p>
<p>The next turn was Urabe Konoka's. I was also wondering about Urabe Konoka's participation, but since she wasn't from a prestigious family and was an adopted daughter, I thought she wouldn't have many opportunities to come into contact with archery, so I asked Urabe Konoka directly a few days ago.</p>
<p>"Yabusame (horseback archery ceremony hosted by shrines) and archery itself hold religious significance in Shinto, so I've been practicing archery ever since I was adopted."</p>
<p>Urabe Konoka said that she had been doing quite harsh training since she was adopted into the Urabe family, and I was convinced that if she could even do horseback archery, she could probably do something like shooting at the Sports Day.</p>
<p>Urabe Konoka drew her bow and shot her arrows in a row without any sign of nervousness. 10, 10, 9. The surprised expression of the opposing team's vanguard was caught on the screen.</p>
<p>The next opponent was surprised by Urabe Konoka's skill and seemed flustered, and their score wasn't good. 666. A very ominous number came out.</p>
<p>Our team's third shooter was Oofuchi Erika. There were opinions of "Why Oofuchi Erika here?" but due to the characteristics of VR Dive, which was similar to actual training, Oofuchi Erika, who had played numerous FPS VR games, was no joke when it came to shooting skills.</p>
<p>Oofuchi Erika, who only played as Shimada Hanzo in Under Watch, which currently has 10 series, also had considerable knowledge of archery.</p>
<p>The only downside was that she got nervous easily, but since the previous opponent showed a low score of 666, Oofuchi Erika showed her true skills and returned to her seat with a smile with a score of 999, which was 666 in reverse.</p>
<p>The next opponent made a huge mistake, as if they weren't even an archery club member, and shot 7, 5, 2. Unless both of our team's remaining two members completely screwed up, we were guaranteed to win.</p>
<p>Sakagami Rena, who freely handles all kinds of projectile weapons, shot 8, 8, 8 points as if she was giving the opponent a break. Seeing that she wasn't flustered by the low(?) score, I could tell that she deliberately got that score.</p>
<p>The next opponent was an archery club member and put in some effort, shooting 9, 9, 8. Our team's last shooter is....</p>
<p>"KAREN! KAREN! KAREN!"</p>
<p>It was Uenozono Karen, who was carrying a huge wave of support. Since there was already a considerable point difference, Uenozono Karen was guaranteed to win as long as she didn't score less than 9 points in total with three arrows. Even if she scored less than 9 points, we would have to check the opponent's score.</p>
<p>Uenozono Karen, waving her hand to the cheers, carefully drew and released her bow at the shooting line. The arrow was shot out with a *tung* sound and hit 6 points.</p>
<p>It was a slightly disappointing score compared to the previous students, but since we only needed 3 more points to win, no one expressed disappointment.</p>
<p>Perhaps because she was disappointed with her first shot, Uenozono Karen drew her bow with a slightly more serious expression, and with a *tung* sound, she hit 10 points.</p>
<p>-The 3rd Grade Special Class wins!</p>
<p>To confirm the victory without even using all the arrows.... Amazing, isn't it? And perhaps because Uenozono Karen delivered the finishing blow, most of the students cheered at the victory declaration.</p>
<p>The 2nd Grade Class C student who had turns left had to come down from the arena without even getting a chance. This was because there was a rule that the game would end on the spot once the game result was decided due to the tight schedule.</p>
<p>"Good job."</p>
<p>I gave each of our class's students who were coming down from the arena a high five and told them they did well.</p>
<p>"Okay, let's review the game we just played before the next game starts."</p>
<p>Shido Tsubaki clapped her hands and called the archery team members together. She's doing it so seriously. Anyone would think it's a national team game. Surely she's not working this hard because of the excellent team dinner I promised if we won, right? I was slightly afraid of how much they were planning to eat, seeing how hard the students were working.</p>
