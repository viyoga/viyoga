<p>Ryuto Construction Company was a newly established construction firm that hadn't been in existence for long. Despite being called a construction company, it was actually a fairly large-scale enterprise, involved not only in construction but also in real estate, security services, contracting, pharmaceuticals, and even operating a small private military company (PMC).</p>
<p>Although only a few knew the truth, the reason Ryuto Construction Company grew to such a large scale in such a short time was that its predecessor was the power of Kurotsuki-doo in Japan, Korea, and Eastern Russia. Since Kurotsuki-doo itself was a company involved in real estate and construction, it was able to expand its influence quickly.</p>
<p>Because Tang Xiaohua, pronounced Too Kobana in Japanese, had absorbed all the power of the globally vast Black Moon Group's Northeast Asia Branch, Ryuto Construction Company was able to quickly establish a significant foothold. Of course, if it weren't for Tang Xiaohua's personal Charisma and her formidable military might, Kurotsuki-doo, having lost its Domination in Korea and Japan, would have scattered into dozens of smaller organizations, and ultimately, Kurotsuki-doo's influence would have completely vanished.</p>
<p>"Hmph, you betrayed us for a mere thirty million yen?" Dozens of people were gathered at a construction site where a luxury mansion was being built. Amidst the Barren Scene strewn with construction materials like rebar and H-beams, among men in black suits, Tang Xiaohua, with her green Hair, sat on a Chair, gazing at a man kneeling at her Feet. The kneeling man was a mess of bruises and bloodstains, as if he had been severely beaten somewhere.</p>
<p>This man was a middle ganbu of Ryuto Construction Company, caught selling Information to a rival company for Money, and thus brought here.</p>
<p>"Have you extracted all the Information?"</p>
<p>"Yes, we have obtained sufficient information. They say Toyo Construction made contact." At Tang Xiaohua's Question, she asked the man in the black suit standing beside her, and he bowed slightly at the Waist and Answered. The kneeling man was bruised and bloody because he had been beaten countless times to extract Information. Toyo Construction had recently been competing with Tang Xiaohua's Ryuto Construction in a redevelopment Ipchal.</p>
<p>As the construction industry was booming with the recent Neo Tokyo Project, the demand for redevelopment projects also surged, leading to a Situation where numerous construction companies were engaged in an Information war to secure contracts.</p>
<p>"P-please...."</p>
<p>"Please what?" The kneeling man opened his mouth, his Pronunciation unclear due to his mouth being torn up inside. If his Hands hadn't been tied, he would have clasped them together and begged profusely, but his Hands were tightly bound with Rope.</p>
<p>"M-my family is innocent...."</p>
<p>"Do you take us for Yakuza? Your family will just think you've gone missing, and that's the end of it."</p>
<p>"T-thank you...!" Tang Xiaohua, still seated, gazed at the kneeling man with an imperious Expression and pointed her Index Finger. The tip of Tang Xiaohua's Index Finger glowed with a green Light, then shot towards the kneeling man. The kneeling man's skull was pierced by the green Light, and with a chiiik sound, he began to melt. Since he suffered Instant Death the moment his skull was pierced, he wouldn't have had time to feel any Pain. It was a form of Mercy, as a pleasant visit was scheduled for tomorrow.</p>
<p>Before long, the kneeling man completely melted into blood by the Miasma. No one would be able to find this man's Corpse. As Tang Xiaohua said, there was a high Probability he would simply be declared missing.</p>
<p>Yang Xuexi has already gone to Toyo Construction, hasn't she?</p>
<p>"That's right. She moved immediately upon hearing that Toyo Construction was involved in this Incident. She said Toyo Construction touched our company, so they must pay the Price."</p>
<p>Yang Xuexi, Yang Seol-hui, was in charge of Ryuto Construction Company's Korea Branch. Normally, she should have been in Korea, but she had come to Japan to meet a certain man she hadn't seen for a while over the Weekend. While in Japan, she had gone to Attack Toyo Construction, which had provoked Ryuto Construction Company, using the Information obtained today. Tang Xiaohua, after hearing the Report about Yang Seol-hui, let out a chuckle and then stood up from her Seat.</p>
<p>"With Yang Xuexi, there'll be no Worry. Even if Yang Xuexi and I take a Vacation for the Weekend, it won't affect Work, will it?"</p>
<p>"Of course. We can handle any Attack from Toyo Construction." Boss Tang Xiaohua used her Rest freely, but taking a Vacation herself meant she wouldn't be disturbed by anything. Excluding the friction with Toyo Construction that was about to unfold, there would be no new Incidents, so the man nodded his Head.</p>
<p>"Then, we'll disband today and rest from tomorrow, so you all rest too."</p>
<p>"Understood, Boss!" Tang Xiaohua got into the angular Limousine. And she stretched her Hand out the Window, waved, and disappeared from the construction site.</p>
<p>Yang Seol-hui was lying prone on the rooftop of a Building, aiming a suppressed Sniper Rifle. Yang Seol-hui was aiming at a conference room in a Building a full three kilometers away. The conference room Building Yang Seol-hui was aiming at was Toyo Construction's Headquarters. Upon receiving Information that Toyo Construction was up to Tricks, Yang Seol-hui immediately grabbed her Sniper Rifle and came to a Sniping Point with a clear view of Toyo Construction.</p>
<p>Soon, the Door of the conference room opened, and Toyo Construction's ganbu were entering. Most were men of middle Age or older. Yang Seol-hui first aimed at the Elderly Person who appeared to be the oldest. That Elderly Person was probably Toyo Construction's Boss. Peushut! A whooshing sound came from the Gun, and the Glass of the conference room three kilometers away shattered. Almost simultaneously with the Glass shattering, a red dot appeared on the Elderly Person's Furrowed Brow, and he collapsed.</p>
<p>The men inside the conference room stood dumbfounded, as if they hadn't yet fully grasped The Situation. Peushut! Peushut! Two people fell. Only then did Toyo Construction's ganbu Scream and hide under the large table in the conference room or run Outside. Yang Seol-hui began firing indiscriminately at the backs of the fleeing ganbu. Peushut! Peushut! Peushut! Peushut! She couldn't stop everyone from escaping, but a full four more people fell.</p>
<p>With seven of the company's ganbu dead, it seemed they had paid a sufficient Price for daring to provoke Ryuto Construction. Normally, succeeding in such long-range Marksmanship would be incredibly Arousing, but today, her Face was only slightly flushed. Thinking of what would happen with that man starting tomorrow, the Arousal from this Sniping was nothing. This Sniping was merely an appetizer to whet her appetite for the main course tomorrow.</p>
<p>Yang Seol-hui leisurely unscrewed the suppressor, disassembled the Sniper Rifle, and put it into a Guitar Bag. Then, slinging the Guitar Bag over her Shoulder, she began Humming and descended through the rooftop Door.</p>
<p>After spending the night with Sakagami Rena, I woke up early in the morning. It was nice to start early because I had to drive nearly three hours to today's destination, Kisarazu City, Chiba Prefecture. Sakagami Rena had already gone home at dawn, so I woke up alone, washed up, got ready, and got in the car. Kisarazu City was the City where the Taira Group's Headquarters was located, and it was the center of the Neo Tokyo Project, which the Japanese government and the Taira Group were massively promoting.</p>
<p>Naturally, with numerous Buildings rising due to the Neo Tokyo Project, the construction economy was very good, so the Headquarters of Ryuto Construction Company, founded by Tang Xiaohua, was also in Kisarazu City. I heard they were making enormous profits by securing several construction contracts through my, Mimata Chieri's, and Nagumo Sian's connections. At first, I was quite flustered when I heard the name Ryuto Construction. It was a name that combined Ryuta Taira's 'Ryu' and Tang Xiaohua's 'Tang' .</p>
<p>It felt like some kind of love confession. I felt a bit awkward. Since Yang Seol-hui had also agreed to the name Tang Xiaohua had chosen, there was nothing I could say. Even though it would become a business under my umbrella, I couldn't dictate its name. It wasn’t a business I’d started from scratch with my investment; rather, Tang Xiaohua had taken over the Kurotsuki-doo Northeast Asia Branch and set it up, so all I gave was a little financial support. This meant Tang Xiaohua and Yang Seol-hui had a lot of independence.</p>
<p>Trying to excel in both the Underworld and the construction industry meant they were both incredibly busy. Even Mimata Chieri and Han Yu-gyeong, who were only helping a little with Work, saw their Work/Job increase, so both Tang Xiaohua and Yang Seol-hui must have been quite exhausted. To commend them, I had prepared several Gifts and put them in my Luggage, but I wondered if both would be Satisfied. While driving, I stopped at a Taira Group Safe House.</p>
<p>Kisarazu City, being the location of the Taira Group's Headquarters, naturally had many Spies from other Factions. This meant there was a high Probability of my True Identity being exposed. Even if Tang Xiaohua or Yang Seol-hui didn't know, I was also meeting Mimata Chieri, so I had to change vehicles at the Safe House midway. Mimata Chieri was Taira Ryuta's secretary, and her True Identity was already known, so I needed to be careful when meeting her. I parked the sports car I had been driving and, carrying my luggage, got into another car.</p>
<p>This one was a stately sedan, which didn’t really suit me, but there was no choice. I drove from Tokyo to Kisarazu City via the Tokyo Bay Aqua-Line. Ryuto Construction’s headquarters was near the center of Kisarazu City, but Tang Xiaohua’s home was on the outskirts of Kisarazu, near the border with Kimitsu City. True to Tang Xiaohua’s famous status in the underworld, her home was built on a large plot of land.</p>
<p>Building it in central Kisarazu would have been too expensive, and placing a Yakuza mansion in an area full of prominent businesses would certainly have drawn attention, so her home was located on the city’s outskirts. The navigation system announced that I had arrived. It was an Eastern-style Building with a quite high Wall. As if having been informed beforehand, the Security Guard in a black suit, after confirming my Face through the lowered Window, respectfully bowed 90 degrees.</p>
<p>"Greetings, Chairman-nim. We welcome your visit." Hmm, since Tang Xiaohua is the Boss, that must mean I'm the Chairman, right? I got out of the Car and handed the Car keys to the servant who was waiting. The large Door opened, and Tang Xiaohua, wearing a black China Dress, was visible.</p>
