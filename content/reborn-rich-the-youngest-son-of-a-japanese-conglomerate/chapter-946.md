<p>"Alik, you've worked very hard."</p>
<p>I stroked Alik's Head and said. Although I saw Alik's Status Window after a long Time, raising his Level by as many as six in the meantime was still a tremendous Achievement. He worked so hard that he even acquired a Training Skill, so he must have become strong so quickly.</p>
<p>In fact, when I first brought Alik, I didn't expect him to become strong this quickly. Although he was an infamous mid-Boss, I thought it was due to the Modifications of the First group and various Brainwashing, so I expected him to become strong at a somewhat understandable pace, but Alik was doing better than I expected.</p>
<p>"Yes. Alik, worked hard to protect Ryuta...!"</p>
<p>All that Effort was to protect me, which was quite touching.</p>
<p>"I, I also worked hard for Ryuta-nim!"</p>
<p>As I wore a deeply moved Expression, Konoe Akina also, as if not to be outdone, raised her hand and said. In fact, Konoe Akina had indeed worked hard. She wasn't just any Young Lady, but the length of a Family, and going to the Family Security/Guard to receive combat Training was not an easy task.</p>
<p>"Konoe, Alik, Diana, and Yuka, thank you all."</p>
<p>I conveyed my gratitude to those in the vehicle who worked hard for me. Saionji Diana said, 「It's something I naturally have to do,」 and seemed a little burdened, while Otagi Yuka said, 「Why say such things~!」 and wiped her nose with her Index Finger.</p>
<p>As we exchanged pleasant greetings and thanked each other, before I knew it, we arrived at the Joseodang Safe House, which I had visited several times.</p>
<p>Although it looked like an ordinary office, inside, Joseodang agents carrying Guns were walking around, and Strong People like Asada Ei, Sakagami Rena, and Naruse Shiyo were moving about, so it could be called a Den of iniquity.</p>
<p>"How do you do?"</p>
<p>As we entered the Safe House, a Joseodang agent recognized us and greeted us. And then, guided by them, we passed through the office where agents were busy working and entered the conference room.</p>
<p>Seeing the agents working busily, it seemed there was a lot of aftermath to deal with from yesterday's events.</p>
<p>"Ah, Ryuta-nim...! You've arrived."</p>
<p>As I entered the conference room, Asada Ei looked at me and bowed her Head. And Sakagami Rena and Naruse Shiyo, who had arrived earlier as Joseodang executives, also stood up from their seats and welcomed me.</p>
<p>"Liege/Master...!"</p>
<p>"Teacher~!"</p>
<p>"Ryuta-cchii~!!"</p>
<p>The people waiting in the conference room were only the three Joseodang executives and Seizawa Shii. Those who could attend the meeting were either recuperating in hospital rooms, or some, like the Yoda old man, were in a distant alliance-like Relationship and couldn't be called my subordinates, so I didn't call them. Konoe Akina and Seizawa Shii also couldn't be called my subordinates, but they were close allies with whom I would work on various matters in the future, and they were curious about the aftermath of this incident, so they participated in this meeting. Still, today's meeting wasn't about making grand new plans, but rather about dealing with yesterday's aftermath, so not everyone's participation was necessary.</p>
<p>"Alik~! Did something interesting happen?"</p>
<p>As if recognizing Alik's slightly flushed Expression, Seizawa Shii stretched Alik's cheek, which was like a glutinous rice cake, and asked, whereupon Alik nodded his Head.</p>
<p>"Alik, became strong...!"</p>
<p>"Huh? What do you mean?"</p>
<p>"Ryuta checked Alik's Abilities."</p>
<p>Seizawa Shii, who didn't know the Ability of my Glasses from the Ruins, wondered what he meant and kept tilting her Head at Alik's Words.</p>
<p>"I have the Ability to quantify and see the Opponent's Abilities."</p>
<p>"Ah~! It's like a game Status Window?! Ryuta-cchii!! Why didn't you check mine?!"</p>
<p>"After the meeting, I was going to check everyone's."</p>
<p>I was curious about Seizawa Shii's Abilities. With a Woman's Body, she possessed Super Strength. When she came out of the Coin Laundry and had Sex, she had devastated the surroundings, so her power, stamina, and recovery seemed extraordinary. Still, I came here for a meeting, so I can check after the meeting.</p>
<p>"Are you going to check mine too?"</p>
<p>"Yes. After the meeting, I plan to check everyone's."</p>
<p>Naruse Shiyo also, with a very intrigued Expression, asked me to check hers, so I said I would do it for everyone and sat down.</p>
<p>"But this one.... Isn't it Rieko-ppi?!"</p>
<p>Seizawa Shii, as if recognizing Seiwa Rieko, called her Rieko-ppi and waved her hand happily. Otagi Yuka, seeing Seizawa Shii waving her hand, as if it were an opportunity, nudged Seiwa Rieko's arm.</p>
<p>"Ah, how do you do? I am Seiwa Rieko, who will be working under Otagi Yuka Sunbae."</p>
<p>"Have you joined us?"</p>
<p>"Long Time no see."</p>
<p>Sakagami Rena and Naruse Shiyo knew Seiwa Rieko, who was their classmate, and greeted her as if happy to see her. Of course, although she had quit school due to an unpleasant incident, everyone, with good Sense, didn't bring up that Story. Even Asada Ei, who was meeting her for the first Time, greeted her, and everyone began to sit down.</p>
<p>"Now that everyone is seated, I will now begin the meeting."</p>
<p>As we all sat down, Asada Ei changed the subject to the meeting and turned on the screen hanging on the wall. On the screen, the Cost incurred for this incident was written in a Table, quite easy to understand.</p>
<p>"As you can see from the Table, the Amount spent on this incident is about 220 million yen. 45 percent was for bribing local police, 20 percent for recovery costs, and the rest were Labor Costs, operating expenses, data investigation fees, and other miscellaneous expenses."</p>
<p>As expected.... Bribing the police cost the most money. The fact that we fought in Miyashi and also in Tokyo, where An-yaji's headquarters is located, and the police weren't involved, seemed to be thanks to a considerable Amount of money spread among the police. The fact that recovery costs were as high as 20 percent was because of the pension with a barbecue area that was my combat area, and things were broken while fighting Musashi Takuro old man, and the damage was further exacerbated by the sudden appearance of Takakusaki Gengo, so we had to pay not only for recovery but also Silencing fees. If it had been another area, we could have settled it by giving a generous Amount for recovery costs, but the pension where the battle took place was in Miyashi, which was of interest to prestigious Families, so we had to add Silencing fees. If the pension owner made a fuss for no reason, we would also attract attention. For reference, the third highest Labor Costs were the Joseodang agents' overtime pay and Seizawa Shii's employment fee. The Gwonmaeng people participated for their own Revenge, so they didn't receive money, but incidental expenses like meals and travel costs we had to cover, so it couldn't be said to be entirely free.</p>
<p>"The 220 million yen spent on just one day's fight is not a small Amount of money, but what we gained is not insignificant."</p>
<p>Asada Ei switched the screen to the next page. This Time, what we gained was written.</p>
<p>"First, as you can see on the screen, the tangible benefits are various land documents and An-yaji's Account obtained from a secret Treasury revealed by Athena-ssi's tip, and a large Amount of cryptocurrency contained in the UCT (Universal Connection Terminal)."</p>
<p>Aneta Mannerheim revealed to us the existence of a secret Treasury that she could monopolize alone if An-yaji were to fall. A lot of money was spent on this fight, and perhaps thinking that if we backed out, she wouldn't be able to get Revenge for her Parents, she provided us with benefits. Of course, even if there hadn't been the secret Treasury Aneta Mannerheim told us about, we would have settled things with Takakusaki Gengo, but the more money, the better.</p>
<p>"So, how much is it in total?"</p>
<p>"Excluding land documents that are difficult to convert to Cash immediately, if we only combine the Account and cryptocurrency... it's about 1.35274 billion yen."</p>
<p>As the topic of money came up, Seizawa Shii, as if curious, raised her hand and asked. In fact, since cryptocurrency can also be used like Cash, only the liquid assets amount to about 1.35 billion yen, as Asada Ei said. An-yaji was quite rich, wasn't he? I thought he didn't have much money because he focused on Fist Technique, which was useless for making money, but it seemed he had made quite a lot of money. Of course, I knew that An-yaji dispatched his martial monks to various places to do business providing Security/Guard or armed missions, but I wondered if he made so much money from that.</p>
<p>"What should we do with this money?"</p>
<p>Asada Ei asked me about the use of the money. It was a considerable Amount of money, but Asada Ei didn't seem particularly greedy.</p>
<p>"Release some of it as a bonus to the agents who suffered through this incident and use it as Joseodang's reserve fund. There will be many expenses in the future."</p>
<p>Because I was thinking of growing Joseodang more aggressively, I decided to invest the money obtained this time in Joseodang. Since Joseodang's current reach was only covering Miyashi and Tokyo, the first goal now was to turn it into an intelligence organization that covers at least the Kanto, Kansai, and Chubu regions. The second goal is to cover all of Japan. I decided to think about the third goal after achieving the second.</p>
<p>"And, excluding liquid assets, we also obtained many quite useful things. First, excluding the martial monks handed over to Kanna-ssi's side, if we make good use of the approximately 60 An-yaji martial monks taken as Prisoners, we can expect a significant increase in Combat power."</p>
<p>I was reported yesterday that there were 50 An-yaji martial monks taken as Prisoners, but I don't know how many more were captured, and before I knew it, there were a dozen more. I received advice from a few people yesterday that since the An-yaji martial monks were sufficiently trained, if we trained them in firearms and made them combat agents, they would be quite useful, so I was heading in that Direction. However, the Worry was whether to let them officially operate as PMC agents or to let them operate in the shadows like unlicensed Mercenaries, and this seemed to require more discussion. Probably Taira Kanna also has similar thoughts to us. Even if Taira Kanna possessed excellent Combat power, numbers were important in battle, so she must have coveted the trained An-yaji martial monks.</p>
