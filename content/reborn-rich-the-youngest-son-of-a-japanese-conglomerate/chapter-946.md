<p>It hadn't even been a minute since they entered the office. Climbing the stairs took a bit longer, but if you calculated generously, it would be about a minute and thirty seconds. But how could they develop this Photo and leave it out in such a short amount of time?</p>
<p>The leader of the black cyber Suit group looked at the Photo for a moment, then opened his mouth with a serious Expression. It seemed like he had realized something.</p>
<p>-Is it a superhuman...? It's a trap. Everyone, prepare yourselves.</p>
<p>There was a superpower called thoughtography. It was a superpower that could develop Images floating in a superhuman's Mind into Photos or Drawings, and it seemed that this Photo was a thoughtography of a scene after seeing the future and placing it on the desk.</p>
<p>If they knew they were coming, the Opponent would have definitely prepared a trap in advance. The empty office was understandable.</p>
<p>-D-First, Report that our Information was leaked in advance. It seems like there's a precognitive superhuman on the Opponent's side....</p>
<p>The leader, who told the VR diver to Report the current Situation, couldn't finish his Words in surprise. That's because someone was sitting on the window frame of the office, which was now wide open.</p>
<p>The inside of the office was dark, and it was difficult to see who the Opponent was due to the backlight of the city lights pouring in from the Tokyo night View outside the window.</p>
<p>-It's the enemy! Everyone, shoot!</p>
<p>The leader's response was quick. The black cyber Suit attackers reflexively shot at the intruder squatting on the window frame at the leader's Command.</p>
<p>Pit-pit-pit! Pit-pit-pit!</p>
<p>Everyone had high-performance silencers attached to their guns in preparation for being in downtown Tokyo, so they fired with the sound of air escaping, pit-pit, instead of noise.</p>
<p>Tang-tang-tang-tang!</p>
<p>The fired bullets were all deflected with a metallic sound that rang in their Sensitive Ears. They had deflected all the bullets fired at them by swinging a sword.</p>
<p>'!'</p>
<p>Everyone was so surprised that their mouths dropped open, but there was no time to be surprised like that.</p>
<p>Pajik-!</p>
<p>With a flash of light rising from the Darkness, the intruder on the window frame disappeared. And a streak of Light that flashed like Lightning and momentarily illuminated the office. A katana that had become Lightning itself was swung along with the sword light.</p>
<p>Tuk!</p>
<p>One of the black cyber Suit group fell to the Ground with his waist split in half. Blood didn't splatter even though his waist was split in half because the entire cut surface was scorched by the extreme high temperature due to the electric shock, causing the blood to evaporate.</p>
<p>'The Opponent is a young Foreign Country woman. A superhuman who uses a sword.'</p>
<p>The leader of the black cyber Suit group was able to see the Opponent when the office was illuminated by the flash of light, and he saw a Foreign Country woman who looked younger than he thought swinging a sword and cutting off his buha's waist.</p>
<p>Of course, he only saw it for that brief moment, so he might have been mistaken, but he himself was also an enhanced human who had undergone bio-enhancement, so he didn't think that what he saw in that short amount of time was wrong.</p>
<p>Once again, the office is illuminated by a flash of light. This time, the target was VR diver D-First, who was contacting the headquarters Situation room.</p>
<p>Pit-pit-pit!</p>
<p>Only the leader identified the Foreign Country woman's target and fired his Gun, but as the bullet approached the woman, the lead bullet melted with a sound like roasting beans.</p>
<p>-Shi, fuck! How do we fight that!</p>
<p>One of the team members' Voice, muttering with a curse, was heard through the communication device. The black cyber Suit intruders could only helplessly watch D-First's neck being cut off and flying into the empty space.</p>
<p>Kwang!</p>
<p>At the same time, someone kicked open the office Door. A man with a Body made of Metal and a woman with blue Hair appeared with the loud noise of the Door opening.</p>
<p>Kwang!</p>
<p>The man with metallic skin charged at the closest black cyber Suit agent and grabbed his neck, lifting him up and slamming him into the office floor. Of course, they fired at the man with metallic skin who was approaching them, but it only caused a few sparks to fly on the man's skin, without causing any harm.</p>
<p>No matter how much shock-absorbing cyber Suit they were wearing, they couldn't help but take damage if they were thrown to the Ground so hard that the floor cracked.</p>
<p>Pit-pit-pit!!</p>
<p>The woman with blue Hair randomly fired the pistols in both hands without even looking properly, but even though it looked like she was shooting randomly, all the bullets she fired hit their mark.</p>
<p>Normal pistol bullets would have a hard time penetrating the cyber Suit, but the blue-haired woman's bullets or guns seemed to be specially made luxury items, knocking down one Person with each shot.</p>
<p>And she showed a natural way of dodging the black cyber Suit team member's counter-fire as if she knew it 'in advance'.</p>
<p>-Since it's come to this...!</p>
<p>The leader, feeling that the Opponent's Skill was unusual, put his Hand inside his Suit to do something suicidal. There was a small Bomb inside his Suit.</p>
<p>"Let's all die together!!!"</p>
<p>The leader of the black cyber Suit group deactivated the communication mode and shouted so that everyone could hear, pressing the switch on the Bomb.</p>
<p>"...."</p>
<p>"That Bomb, when will it explode?"</p>
<p>Even with the Foreign Country woman's question, the Bomb was silent. At that time, a communication sound was heard in his Sensitive Ear.</p>
<p>-Did you think only you had VR divers? And how can you have a digital signal Bomb that's been released for more than 2 years and is easy to hack?</p>
<p>A VR diver, whose location was unknown, had already hacked this side's communication and even the Bomb.</p>
<p>-It's meaningless, but let me give you some advice: use a completely up-to-date Bomb that's difficult to hack, or carry one with a simple detonating device that can't be hacked at all.</p>
<p>As the VR diver said, it was meaningless advice to him. Because the sword blade with electricity flowing through it was already approaching his Eyes.</p>
<p>Seogeok!</p>
<p>His Field of Vision rose and rotated 360 degrees. And as his Eyes went dark, the leader of the black cyber Suit group's Consciousness disappeared.</p>
<p>"It's all over. Please clean up the office."</p>
<p>As the head of the intruder leader, who was cut down by Alik, rotated and floated in the empty space and fell to the office floor, Sawayanagi Azuma Reported the end of the battle with the wireless device and looked around the office. Sawayanagi Azuma opened her mouth as she looked at Alik, who was squatting down and poking the Bomb that the enemy leader's Corpse was holding with his katana.</p>
<p>"If you poke it a few more times like that, it'll explode."</p>
<p>"Ah, is that so...?"</p>
<p>At Sawayanagi Azuma's Words, Alik widened his Eyes and quickly took his sword away. And put it in its sheath.</p>
<p>"The clean-up team will be here soon, so let's withdraw. It'll be a hindrance if we keep staying here."</p>
<p>Sawayanagi Azuma retrieved the Photo that was placed on the desk, then took Alik and Sawayanagi Tobe and left the building that used to be Hatake Investment company and headed to the next building where the team members were waiting.</p>
<p>Before the battle, Alik, who was waiting in this next building, immediately jumped and infiltrated through the window, and Sawayanagi Azuma and Sawayanagi Tobe waited on the 3rd floor of the Hatake Investment company building before going down to the 2nd floor and entering through the office Door.</p>
<p>"There was nothing for me to do."</p>
<p>As the three People who had gone into battle returned, Sawayanagi Mikiyo, who was in charge of the sniper at Seryu Military Company, said as if she was disappointed. Originally, she was going to blow off the wrist holding the Bomb when the enemy leader was about to detonate the Bomb, but she missed the opportunity to play an active role at the signal that Sawayanagi Sinya's hacking was successful.</p>
<p>"By the way, Alik~! You've gotten so strong?!"</p>
<p>Rianna Holden, who was standing next to Sawayanagi Mikiyo, hugged Alik tightly and said. Alik nodded with his unique Expressionless face and said, Alik, got stronger. If Taira Ryuta, who calls himself an Alik expert, saw it, he would say that Alik was making a proud Expression, but to the average Person, it felt like his usual Expressionless face.</p>
<p>"By the way, how was the thoughtography? I couldn't see the reaction at the time because I was busy hacking the signal of the Opponent's VR diver."</p>
<p>In the early stages of the Opponent's invasion, Rianna Holden was also busy hacking the signal of the Opponent's VR diver in the Situation room. They had to cut off communication before revealing this side's True Identity. That Work was successful, so the connection with the Opponent's Situation room was completely cut off after the Opponent entered the office.</p>
<p>The thoughtography that surprised the Hatake Investment company invaders this time was a Ability that Rianna Holden had recently awakened. She shared Sawayanagi Azuma's future sight with telepathy and developed the scene of the Opponent invading with thoughtography.</p>
<p>"The Opponent was a little surprised, but it seems like it ended up letting them know that we have a superhuman on our side."</p>
<p>"Hmm.... Is it difficult to use in battle? Where do we use this?"</p>
<p>At Sawayanagi Azuma's Words, Rianna Holden nodded her Head as if she was disappointed. As more than half of the Seryu Military Company members were superhumans, it was easy to see them sharing their concerns about these new Abilities and discussing how to utilize them.</p>
<p>For reference, Seryu Military Company had recently recruited a few more superhumans, so the number of People had increased a little more. Currently, the Seryu Thai team members who were rescued from Korea were acting as seniors and training the newcomers.</p>
<p>"Seeing how strong Alik has become, shouldn't we also accept the education curriculum of St. F... what's-it academy?"</p>
<p>"Don't say stupid things, Holden. Alik got stronger because he worked hard on his own, not because of that all-girls academy education."</p>
<p>When Sawayanagi Azuma, who roughly knew what kind of training Alik had undergone, scolded Rianna Holden, Rianna Holden became sullen and muttered, 'Hmph, I was just saying it as a joke...' and let Alik go.</p>
<p>And she closed the laptop that was spread out on the table and started gathering various items. Sawayanagi Azuma smiled at the sight and helped her pack her Luggage, opening her mouth.</p>
<p>"Let's empty this Situation room too. The Opponent might come to check it."</p>
<p>With that, Seryu Thai and Alik, who had succeeded in this Operation, erased all traces and disappeared like ghosts.</p>
