<p>"Ryuta-tta, ah...!"</p>
<p>As soon as I sat down at the club room's dining table, Alik put curry rice into my mouth. I ate the curry rice Alik put in my mouth, looking at the food laid out before me.</p>
<p>"Uh...."</p>
<p>Is this a club room or a buffet? Seeing over 10 dishes laid out before me, I was speechless. And who's the Guy who ordered a whole Pizza in front of me?</p>
<p>"I don't know how much of a glutton you all think I am, but I can't eat 10 servings at once, can I? And isn't a whole Pizza at least 4 servings?"</p>
<p>"Ryuta-cchi, you can't do this much?"</p>
<p>Seizawa Shii looked at me with a slightly disappointed Expression, as if genuinely believing I could, saying, "Eh? You can't do this?" I don't know about the others, but Seizawa Shii seemed to genuinely think I was a food fighter.</p>
<p>"You can just eat it; we'll pack up the leftovers."</p>
<p>"Understood."</p>
<p>At Minamoto Risa's sharp remark, I sat down and began to eat dinner. Since it was late evening, everyone could eat deliciously.</p>
<p>"Ah, we were originally supposed to come back tomorrow, which is a shame. How about we just keep playing until tomorrow?"</p>
<p>Kujo Kina spoke idly, saying, "Ah, we were originally resting until tomorrow." While traveling, playing until tomorrow would have been fine, but now that we had already returned to Miyashi, playing was out of the question.</p>
<p>"Sorry, but no. Exams start the day after tomorrow. Even though we had to come back early, now that we're back, we have to study."</p>
<p>Of course, it wasn't a particularly important exam for the Female Students who would advance to St. Francis Women's University, but for those advancing to other universities, it was quite important, so from a teacher's perspective, I had to tell them to study.</p>
<p>In fact, only Minamoto Risa, Oofuchi Erika, and Shido Tsubaki were aiming for universities higher than St. Francis Women's University, so the rest could actually play. Of course, they still had to pass the cut-off line to avoid retaking the exam. Since no student had received a Failing Grade in the last exam, I firmly believed there would be none this time either.</p>
<p>In fact, St. Francis Women's University also offered escalator advancement for students from St. Francis Women's Academy, but it required considerable grades for other students not from the academy to enter, so it could be considered a prestigious University.</p>
<p>"Ah, I also have something to say."</p>
<p>Minamoto Risa raised her Hand. What does she have to say? Surely she's not going to ask who the Woman I was with is, right?</p>
<p>"Since we caught Kotowazuki this time, we'll be splitting the bounty placed on Kotowazuki from the Dark Market."</p>
<p>Ah, that's right. I hadn't even thought about the bounty. I asked Minamoto Risa the question I was most curious about.</p>
<p>"So, how much is the bounty?"</p>
<p>"1.22 billion yen."</p>
<p>Oh... The bounty is more than I thought. Of course, it doesn't compare to the astronomical bounty on Gaoshang, the leader of the Red Revolution Front whom I caught, but Gaoshang was the Master of a world-class terrorist organization that caused 10 million victims, and Kotowazuki is merely a serial Killer in Japan, so it was natural for the bounties to differ. Besides me, the other special class Students were also surprised to hear about the bounty. After all, even for Young Ladies of prestigious families, 1.2 billion yen is a large sum of Money.</p>
<p>"Kotowazuki is just a serial killer, so why is it so much?"</p>
<p>"Then each person will get a little less than 100 million yen, right?"</p>
<p>Including me, there were 13 people on this trip, so 1.22 billion divided by 13, minus commission, would probably be around 90 million yen.</p>
<p>"Since he went around killing people in wealthy neighborhoods like Minato-ku, it seems the rich put up a lot of bounty money."</p>
<p>From what I heard, of course, the victims' families put up the most bounty, but it seemed there were also people who, even if not related to the victims, put up bounties with a feeling of, "This Bastard is messing around in our neighborhood, so deal with him."</p>
<p>"Are there any kids who haven't joined the Dark Market?"</p>
<p>Most of our class had joined the Dark Market. Students who decided to follow me after Graduation were already members of the Dark Market by default.</p>
<p>Besides them, Minamoto Risa and Seizawa Shii joined through my introduction, Shido Tsubaki, true to her former role as Club President of the newspaper club, already knew about the Dark Market, Oofuchi Erika learned about it through Shido Tsubaki's introduction, and Kujo Kina, as expected of a Kujo Family Daughter, said she already knew.</p>
<p>"They said the money would be deposited as soon as the Information is confirmed, so just wait a bit."</p>
<p>"Then, shall we turn on the TV for a moment? If it's on TV, the Information will be confirmed, and the Money will come in right away, won't it?"</p>
<p>As Seizawa Shii said, I turned on the TV to a news channel, and just then, a Scene of Kotowazuki in Handcuffs descending from a helicopter and going to the police station was being filmed. A breaking news Phrase read, 'Serial Killer Caught by St. Francis Women's Academy Female Students!', and the content included a reporter's statement that the Female Students of St. Francis Women's Academy, including Minamoto-ga's Young Lady, Lisa-ssi, had caught the serial Killer.</p>
<p>"Sorry. It seems our group's PR team moved on their own. We all caught him together, but..."</p>
<p>Minamoto Risa, her Face flushed at the article's content, which blatantly showed the influence of the Minamoto Group, apologized to the special class Students.</p>
<p>"It's fine. That's just how Family matters are, you know."</p>
<p>Konoe Akina smiled, saying it was fine. Perhaps in the local media where each Family resided, articles featuring the names of the special class Students from that region were being published instead of Minamoto Risa's.</p>
<p>Only the Taira side ended up being a shame, I guess. If it had been a Situation where I could reveal my name, they would have published an article saying, 'The special class, whose Class Teacher is Ryuta-ssi, Scion of the Taira Family, caught Kotowazuki!'</p>
<p>Ttiring!</p>
<p>Everyone's phone rang while they were watching TV and talking about this Incident. Checking my phone, I saw an alarm from the Dark Market.</p>
<p>-Kotowazuki's bounty of 641,383 Points has been deposited. Please check your Points Account.</p>
<p>Since 1 dollar is 1 Point, it seemed to have come in roughly correctly. That's about 920 million Korean Won. Calculating it, the commission for the total of 1.22 billion yen seemed to be roughly 10 million yen.</p>
<p>"Oh! The Money's in~! I won't have to Work for a while. Hehe~☆"</p>
<p>Seizawa Shii smiled cheerfully, looking at the Money deposited in her Dark Market Account. She had been saying she was running a deficit after foolishly acquiring a coin laundry, but now that she had earned Money this time, she seemed to be in a good Mood.</p>
<p>"Everyone's finished eating, right?"</p>
<p>As I asked, intending to break up the gathering since the Students had finished eating and received their Money, the Students, seeing the still-unopened food packages in front of me, spoke up.</p>
<p>"Teacher-nim, didn't you finish eating?"</p>
<p>"Huh, Ryuta-cchi, why did you leave so much food?"</p>
<p>"Ryuta-tta, weren't you hungry...?"</p>
<p>The Students criticized me for not being able to eat more, seeing that I had left the dishes that would still taste good reheated and eaten the rest. No, but I still ate 5 servings. Even Alik looked at me with a "Are you sick?" Gaze, making me wonder if even Alik thought I was a glutton, but I figured it was all because I had eaten too much.</p>
<p>"5 servings is a lot already. I'll take the rest Home and eat it, so don't worry."</p>
<p>At my Words, the Students, saying "Is that so?", put their own trash into the Trash Can.</p>
<p>"Ryuta-cchi, what are you going to do when you get Home?"</p>
<p>"I have to wash the Clothes I wore on this trip."</p>
<p>At Seizawa Shii's Words, I was about to answer that I would just rest, but then I thought I should ask about Minamoto Risa, so I subtly sent a signal that I would go to a coin laundry.</p>
<p>Seizawa Shii, as if she understood, narrowed her Eyes with a "Hmm," then grinned and nodded her Head.</p>
<p>"Everything's tidied up, right? Then let's go down."</p>
<p>Having successfully sent the signal to Seizawa Shii, I looked around at the Students and spoke. All the Luggage for this trip had been left in the 1st floor corridor, so we just had to leave the club room.</p>
<p>I took the food I hadn't finished eating downstairs and put it into the now empty ice Box.</p>
<p>"Everyone worked hard. It was a fun trip, but the end was a bit disappointing."</p>
<p>"Don't think that way. You can just think of it as a trip where we all worked together to catch a Villain."</p>
<p>At my Words that the end was disappointing, Minamoto Risa shook her Head and said. Indeed, what high school students could have gone on a ski resort trip and caught a serial Killer? Unless they were people like Conan or Kindaichi from manga or anime, that is.</p>
<p>"Exams are on Monday, so study a little. See you on Monday."</p>
<p>"Eh, no way~!"</p>
<p>""Yes!""</p>
<p>"Do I have to pass the cut-off line this time too...?"</p>

<p>Even Saionji-ga's vehicle, which had almost neglected Saionji Diana, and Sakagami Family's vehicle, which had not paid attention to her with a feeling of "she's independent, so I trust she'll do well," had arrived. I hadn't realized the ripple effect of this Incident would be so great...?
