<p>"This is Jin Yong-tae."</p>
<p>-Long time no see, Yong-tae-ssi.</p>
<p>Diana Reach's voice was heard over the phone. It's been a while. Diana Reach, who can speak Korean for me, who is not good at English, contacted me.</p>
<p>I've been studying English in my spare time while living at St. Francis Girls' Academy, but I'm not yet at the level where I can have a free conversation with an American.</p>
<p>"It's been a while, Diana-ssi. What brings you to call?"</p>
<p>-Of course, I called to talk about the Black Knight.</p>
<p>As I expected, the United States seemed to be reacting to my killing Gao Xiang. There are superpower haters in the United States who think superhumans are dangerous, or twisted patriots who treat non-Americans as dangerous elements if they have strong powers, so I was curious to see what they would say.</p>
<p>"I'm curious to know what was said about me."</p>
<p>-I don't think this is a light topic to talk about over the phone, so shall we meet and talk first?</p>
<p>"Sure. When shall we meet? I can decide whether to meet in Korea or Japan depending on the date we meet."</p>
<p>From her voice, it didn't seem like a serious matter, so I said I would meet her. I was planning to stay in Korea for about two days after the New Year and then return to Japan, so the meeting place would change depending on when I met Diana Reach.</p>
<p>-I'm in Seoul right now. If you're not busy, let's meet right away.</p>
<p>Oh, that's fast? At this rate, it means my video became a hot topic and she left for Korea within two days, so it seemed that the United States was taking this incident seriously and making a quick decision.</p>
<p>"Sure. Where shall we meet?"</p>
<p>-I'll go to your TG Tech headquarters. TG Tech's energy weapons are also being considered as weapons for the Federal Superpower Management Agency's agents, so let's check their power.</p>
<p>It seemed that TG Tech might be able to make a new contract as well as my story. I hope it goes well.</p>
<p>"Okay. I'll tell my secretary and the reception desk and wait."</p>
<p>-Okay, I'll be there in 10 minutes.</p>
<p>She says she'll be here in 10 minutes... Diana Reach seems to have a pretty impatient personality because she is of mixed Korean heritage. She gets things done so quickly.</p>
<p>"10 minutes? Then I'll get ready."</p>
<p>-Okay, see you then.</p>
<p>"Who is it?"</p>
<p>When I hung up the phone, Han Yu-gyeong, who was listening next to me, asked who it was.</p>
<p>"The United States wants to see me because of the Gao Xiang incident. I don't know what it is yet. But apart from that, the people coming are from a place called the Federal Superpower Management Agency, and they want to see some of TG Tech's products."</p>
<p>"Oh, really?"</p>
<p>Han Yu-gyeong had a slightly complicated expression on her face at my words. She was worried about what attitude the United States would take towards me, but at the same time, she had expectations that a new sales outlet could be a department of the United States.</p>
<p>"Don't worry. They called in advance and are coming to see me, so it won't be a bad thing."</p>
<p>"Yeah. They said they'll be here in 10 minutes, so shouldn't we tell the people downstairs in advance?"</p>
<p>"Oh, that's right. I was going to tell them. I'll tell them."</p>
<p>I forgot to tell them about Diana Reach in advance because I was explaining it to Han Yu-gyeong for a while. But Han Yu-gyeong didn't forget what she heard next to me and told me, so I called my secretary and told them to bring Diana Reach to my office.</p>
<p>As Diana Reach said, exactly 10 minutes later, a report came up that Diana Reach had arrived at the TG Tech headquarters building.</p>
<p>After waiting for a while for Diana Reach to come to the CEO's office, the secretary opened the door and brought Diana Reach in.</p>
<p>"Long time no see, Yong-tae-ssi."</p>
<p>"It's been a few months."</p>
<p>Diana Reach, a beautiful woman with ebony-like glossy black skin, came into the room and greeted me, and I accepted her greeting.</p>
<p>"Hello, I'm Diana Reach, Deputy Director of the Federal Superpower Management Agency."</p>
<p>"I'm Han Yu-gyeong, co-CEO of TG Tech."</p>
<p>Diana Reach and Han Yu-gyeong, who were meeting for the first time, shook hands and greeted each other, and we all sat down.</p>
<p>-Is it okay to talk about the Black Knight in front of Han Yu-gyeong-ssi?</p>
<p>At that moment, Diana Reach used some kind of power and asked me in a way that only I could hear. As far as I know, Diana Reach was an aerokinesis (atmospheric manipulation) ability user, but Han Yu-gyeong didn't hear those words, so it seemed that she used the ability to block sound.</p>
<p>When I nodded as if to say yes, Diana Reach took out a few documents from her bag.</p>
<p>"The Red Revolution Front is an organization that has carried out several terrorist attacks in the United States. On behalf of the U.S. government, I would like to thank Yong-tae-ssi."</p>
<p>Not long after the war ended, the victorious countries were attacked by various terrorist groups that could not accept defeat, and one of them was the Red Revolution Front.</p>
<p>Because of that terrorism, Asians were discriminated against in the United States for a while. Of course, the terrorist groups became quiet because they were suppressed in their own countries due to strong protests that were no different from threats from the victorious countries, but it was true that the Red Revolution Front caused damage to the United States, so she was thanking me.</p>
<p>"So, what I'm proposing is..."</p>
<p>Both Han Yu-gyeong and I focused on what Diana Reach would say.</p>
<p>"Yong-tae-ssi, please become an American."</p>
<p>"Excuse me?"</p>
<p>Suddenly, she wants me to become an American... Could she be talking about naturalization?</p>
<p>"When you say become an American... are you telling me to naturalize?"</p>
<p>"That's right."</p>
<p>To my question, Diana Reach showed me the documents she had prepared. It was a document that kindly stated "Naturalization Proposal" in Korean.</p>
<p>I roughly read it and it explained what benefits there are if I become an American and what benefits the U.S. government can give me.</p>
<p>I have several identities, but the only identity that allows me to naturalize is Jin Yong-tae, a Korean resident in Japan. Japan is not a country that allows dual citizenship, so Taira Ryuta or Yamashiro Ryuta would have to give up their Japanese nationality to become American. The head of the Taira Group was one of my goals, so I couldn't give up Taira Ryuta's Japanese identity, and I couldn't give up Yamashiro Ryuta's identity either because he is currently in charge of the mission of being a teacher at St. Francis Girls' Academy.</p>
<p>Of course, Korea also requires me, who has not served in the military, to give up my Korean nationality to obtain U.S. citizenship, but there were quite a few benefits that I had obtained and would obtain as the CEO of TG Tech with my Korean nationality, so I didn't want to give up my Korean nationality either.</p>
<p>"But why is there talk of naturalization?"</p>
<p>First, I asked about the background of this story.</p>
<p>"As a result of analyzing the video, it was said that the Black Knight's power is excessively strong. It was said that that power could be a threat to the United States."</p>
<p>I thought about whether I was that much of a threat, but I couldn't come up with an answer. I'm not yet at the level where I can catch a famine, and there are at least ten boss-level superhumans who are much stronger than me, as far as I know, so the U.S. government is picking on the Black Knight because the Black Knight is too famous and has become a hot topic this time.</p>
<p>"Wait a minute. You're saying that just because Yong-tae is strong, he's a threat? Isn't that too much?"</p>
<p>Han Yu-gyeong got angry at Diana Reach's words. It was very harsh to be subject to U.S. surveillance or sanctions just because she was strong, but the United States has always been like that.</p>
<p>"That's the U.S. for you."</p>
<p>Diana Reach shrugged her shoulders as if she couldn't help it, even though she was an American herself. In fact, Diana Reach was in a position as Deputy Director of the Federal Superpower Management Agency, but she was only in a position to receive orders from above, so it was meaningless to say anything to Diana Reach.</p>
<p>"Then how about this instead of naturalization?"</p>
<p>After thinking for a while, I decided to make a new proposal to Diana Reach. The only way I could think of to maintain all my identities was one.</p>
<p>"Instead of naturalization, just create a new American identity for me. Keep the benefits of naturalization as they are."</p>
<p>Anyway, I already have three identities, so adding one more wouldn't cause me any big problems, so I just asked for a new identity. Diana Reach, who heard my words, seemed to think for a few seconds and then nodded.</p>
<p>"...That would be good too."</p>
<p>"Is it okay to nod so easily?"</p>
<p>"With the power of our Federal Superpower Management Agency, we can easily give you a new identity. Maybe the side Yong-tae-ssi proposed would be cleaner."</p>
<p>I asked back as if I was dumbfounded at Diana Reach's quick agreement, but Diana Reach didn't seem to care.</p>
<p>"Naturalizing Yong-tae-nim would leave many traces, so if Yong-tae-nim's identity is revealed, we don't have to hear that we stole a superhuman from an allied country, which is good. It's also attractive that we can design Yong-tae-nim's identity and growth process to our liking."</p>
<p>She seemed to have thought a lot during that short period of time. She was saying that giving me a new identity was better than naturalizing me, judging from the reasons she was giving.</p>
<p>"I can decide my name, right?"</p>
<p>"You can decide your name. Then you agree to give you a new identity?"</p>
<p>"Yes, of course, you know that I can't give up my current identity, right?"</p>
<p>"That's right. Instead, let's make the identity of 'Black Knight' the identity we give you."</p>
<p>In the end, they were trying to make a profit by making the Black Knight, not Jin Yong-tae himself, an American. Anyway, I was planning not to reveal the identity of the Black Knight as much as possible, and I didn't care if they thought of me as an American.</p>
<p>"Then I'll report to the higher-ups first and then check TG Tech's products."</p>

<p>"Yong-tae-ya."</p>

<p>"What's wrong?"</p>
<p>"What are you going to name your American name?"</p>
<p>"I don't really have anything in mind, I just asked if I could do it as I please."</p>

<p>"Then can I name Yong-tae's name?"</p>
<p>Han Yu-gyeong was looking at me with a very excited expression. Is it that exciting to name my name? I answered "Uh-huh" without realizing it.</p>
<p>"Give me a few candidates so I can choose at least."</p>
<p>"Okay! I'll give you a cool name, so wait a minute...!"</p>

<p>"We'll have to ask Diana Reach exactly when she comes. Just think about the name for now."</p>
