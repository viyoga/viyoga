<p>Each time Saionji Diana swung her sword, invisible blades of wind swept around her. Two hundred people were shooting at her, but the cyber suit and cyber implants on her arms and legs were blocking the bullets. Where there were no cyber implants, she used the Wind Blade Technique, a swordsmanship style that harnessed wind, to create wind blades and deflect the bullets. Saionji Diana was single-handedly defending against the SPF's attacks without a single scratch.</p>
<p>Ordinary Wind Blade Technique wouldn't be able to deflect bullets, but Saionji Diana also had a trait called the Kamaitachi Dance, and the combined effect was strong enough to block bullets.</p>
<p>Of course, since she was fighting against as many as 200 enemies alone, Saionji Diana was mostly focused on defense rather than offense. There weren't many opportunities to attack while blocking the relentless barrage of bullets. Of course, the SPF side, seeing Saionji Diana blocking bullets from 200 soldiers alone, must have been even more frustrated. If they weakened the fire net even a little to help the other executives, Saionji Diana's wind blades would cut off their limbs, so they couldn't stop shooting at Saionji Diana.</p>
<p>Also, Sakagami Rena and Eve were helping with sniper fire from a distance, so Saionji Diana was becoming more and more advantageous as time passed. As the number of soldiers decreased, Saionji Diana's attack frequency increased, and whenever Saionji Diana attacked, at least one person's neck or limbs fell to the ground.</p>
<p>Tang! Tang!</p>
<p>Some people noticed the snipers and fired back at the flashing gun muzzles, but Sakagami Rena and Eve were focusing on the enemies who were shooting at them. It was a shooting strategy that reduced enemy numbers while keeping themselves safe.</p>
<p>The situation changed in an instant. Alik, who had first defeated an enemy named Angel Ramirez, scattered electricity and swung the lightning-covered katana, Azure Thunderbolt, to support Saionji Diana. Soon after, Shimokawabe Airen joined the battle, showing off her skill of deflecting bullets and shooting them into the enemy's necks.</p>
<p>Saionji Diana also switched from defense to offense in response to her comrades' participation, and began to send wind blades in all directions. Saionji Diana's Wind Blade Technique was specialized in wide-area attacks with wind blades, so with the help of the two participants, the SPF's forces noticeably decreased rapidly as Saionji Diana switched to offense.</p>
<p>Every time the three swordswomen swung their swords, at least four people fell, so before long, all the SPF soldiers were bleeding on the shrine floor, their bodies growing cold.</p>

<p>Alik approached and asked, seeing me holding Otagi Yuka. In fact, I didn't have any medical knowledge, and Otagi Yuka was a vampire, not a human, so I didn't know what condition she was in even more. It was certain that her condition wasn't good right now.</p>
<p>"I don't know."</p>
<p>"Yuka..."</p>
<p>I couldn't take her to the hospital because, as I just said, Otagi Yuka was a vampire, not a human. She might not be completely different from humans, but there would still be some differences, so I doubted whether they could treat her properly. Also, if I took her to the hospital and the existence of vampires was revealed to the world, that would be a problem in itself, so there wasn't much I could do.</p>
<p>Eve had formed an alliance with Otagi Yuka and helped each other when Otagi Yuka was fighting alone in New York, so Eve might know something. So, right now, the only thing I could do was wait for Eve, who was at the sniper point, to come here.</p>
<p>While waiting for Eve, I looked at my phone and heard that St. Francisco Girls' Academy had been attacked, so Saionji Diana and Shimokawabe Airen interrogated the surviving survivors. According to the survivors, the people gathered at the shrine were some SPF agents and a group of small and medium-sized PMCs hired by the SPF Tokyo branch, but even when asked about the attack on St. Francisco, the survivors answered that they knew nothing about the group that attacked St. Francisco Girls' Academy.</p>
<p>Attacking St. Francisco Girls' Academy was something that even Taira Jiro would have to do carefully, so it seemed that he had thoroughly hired a completely different group.</p>
<p>"I heard that the resistance from the St. Francisco attackers was so strong that they were all killed. They were using cloaking devices, so it wasn't easy to capture them alive."</p>
<p>Shimokawabe Airen told me as if she had heard information from somewhere. She had been living at the academy longer than I had, so she could have easily obtained information from somewhere. If all the attackers were dead, it would be more difficult to find out who was behind it... But just in case, I should call Taira Danjo-halbae later and ask him to scold them.</p>
<p>"What's the damage to St. Francisco Girls' Academy?"</p>
<p>"One teacher and fifteen guards died, and two students died and five were seriously injured."</p>
<p>From the academy's point of view, the death of a teacher and fifteen guards wasn't a big problem, but the death of two students was a big problem. The size of the problem would be determined by which family those students came from, but no matter who died, it wouldn't be a small ripple.</p>
<p>"Did anyone we know die?"</p>
<p>Even though people had died, I was still hoping that it wasn't someone we knew. Of course, I had asked Kurayamada Kirie and Konoe Akina to take care of the special class, so our class would be fine, and I would have contacted Taira Kanna in advance to prepare, so there would be no deaths.</p>
<p>"One of the 3rd grade teachers passed away. The calligraphy teacher..."</p>
<p>Ah, I thought it was Kurayamada Kirie who died while blocking the enemies because she was a 3rd grade teacher, but it wasn't. It was a strange feeling that I didn't know whether to be relieved or not. Still, I should be relieved that the person who was asked a favor by me didn't die.</p>
<p>"Ryuta-nim~!"</p>
<p>"My lord!"</p>

<p>"That's... I don't really know. She fought a vampire hunter one-on-one, and when I finished my fight and went to help her, the vampire hunter was dead and Otagi Yuka was also collapsed."</p>

<p>"It should be inside the shrine."</p>
<p>"Wait a minute, there's something I need to check...!"</p>
<p>Eve hurriedly ran into the shrine as if the answer to why Otagi Yuka was collapsed was in the vampire hunter's body. It was rather fortunate that she had something she suspected rather than not knowing anything at all.</p>
<p>"I'm sorry to make you work right after you came back, but Sakagami, can you get information from the guys we captured?"</p>
<p>"I didn't even fight directly. Leave it to me, my lord!"</p>
<p>Since she's a ninja who specializes in extracting information from the opponent, Sakagami Rena will get information that we don't know yet. Now, when Eve comes back from the shrine building, I can focus on saving Otagi Yuka.</p>
<p>"I found out why Yuka collapsed."</p>
<p>Eve, who had gone to the shrine, came back and opened her mouth. That's good. If we know the cause, we can remove the cause and treat her quickly.</p>
<p>"Vampire hunters drink something called Mercury Elixir before battle. It has the basic effects of strengthening muscles and increasing agility, but the most important effect is to prevent vampires from using their blood even if they bleed during battle."</p>
<p>At Eve's words, I immediately realized the cause of Otagi Yuka's current serious injury. The most difficult thing when fighting a vampire is that once you bleed, the vampire immediately uses that bleeding to inflict additional damage up to the 2nd, 3rd, and 4th times.</p>
<p>The SPF has been making Mercury Elixir with an alchemical recipe that has been passed down since ancient times, and if you take that Mercury Elixir, the vampire will suffer great damage because the blood energy is contaminated when vampires use bleeding to launch other attacks.</p>
<p>Humans were just food for vampires from ancient times to the Middle Ages, but it was no exaggeration to say that humans were able to fight vampires on equal terms since the development of Mercury Elixir.</p>
<p>"That body..."</p>
<p>"Yes, there was almost no blood."</p>
<p>Otagi Yuka used the vampire hunter's bleeding for revenge, not caring about the damage she would take. She killed the nanny who had raised her since she was a child, so she would have wanted to take revenge by any means. As a result, she suffered great damage from the contamination of blood energy, which is the source of vampires.</p>
<p>"What's the treatment?"</p>
<p>"Um... The only way is to absorb a huge amount of blood to minimize the proportion of contamination itself, or to eliminate the contamination with magical treatment."</p>
<p>Neither method could be used right now. It might be possible if I could get help from the Otagi family, but Otagi Yuka was currently estranged from the family.</p>
<p>To get a huge amount of blood, I would have to kill a lot of people, but I couldn't do that, and no one knew how to do that magical treatment.</p>

<p>It's not my style to violate a woman who's unconscious like this, but I had no choice if I wanted to save Otagi Yuka.</p>

<p>"Yes, Ryuta-nim. Please save my friend."</p>
<p>"Please take good care of my former disciple, Ryuta-gun."</p>
