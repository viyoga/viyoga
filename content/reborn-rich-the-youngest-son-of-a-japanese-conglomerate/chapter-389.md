<p>The berserk Iron Helmet Wolf, whose fur had turned orange-red as if heated iron, began sweeping through its surroundings like crazy. Of course, the first to be attacked were the agents who had harmed it: the agent who fired the anti-tank missile and the agent who fired the grenade launcher. The Iron Helmet Wolf, as if disappearing before one's eyes, moved at an incredible speed, and burned all the agents it passed. Its orange-red glowing fur was emitting ultra-high temperatures, to the point where just passing by would set things ablaze.</p>
<p>Tangtangtangtang! The lead bullets fired by the BS Group agents couldn't even reach due to the high temperature emitted by the Iron Helmet Wolf, turning into molten metal and falling to the ground. Kwadeuk! As if nothing could stop it, the Iron Helmet Wolf, which ran in a straight line burning everything, bit off the head of the agent who fired the anti-tank missile as if swallowing an apple in one bite. After that, moving like crazy, it tore at the agents holding grenade launchers and burned its surroundings.</p>
<p>In less than a minute, among the 130 BS Group agents, nearly a hundred lost their heads or suffered fatal burns and fell to the ground. And the Iron Helmet Wolf's reddened fur returned to a wire-like glossy black. The berserk state had ended. As expected, exactly one minute. No matter if it was a boss of an event, it was still in the early to mid-stages, so it was unreasonable for a berserk state that strengthened it to that extent to last for several minutes beyond one minute.</p>
<p>"I'll go out now and deal with that wolf-like guy, so join me at an appropriate time." "Understood, be careful, Ryuta-ya."</p>
<p>I immediately came out from where I was hiding and charged towards the Iron Helmet Wolf, which seemed slightly exhausted after its berserk state ended. While the Iron Helmet Wolf couldn't react, with a fist full of Abyss, I struck the shoulder part of the Iron Helmet Wolf's foreleg.</p>
<p>"Kya-aeng!" It yelped, seemingly feeling considerable pain, but I instinctively felt that I hadn't dealt proper damage. The densely growing wire-like fur had absorbed most of the impact from my fist. I followed up with punches and kicks, but while I could inflict pain, I couldn't deliver a proper impact. As expected, with just random punching, it's hard to catch an event boss. I'll have to use skills to catch it. The Iron Helmet Wolf glared at me with eyes burning with anger, and began to bite me with its maw covered in blood.</p>
<p>But I was not an agent of BS Defender who would be helplessly defeated. Watching the Iron Helmet Wolf's teeth until the end, I calmly dodged and began to use the Martial Arts Forms of Shinkaiyatsude. Shinkaiyatsude (Deep Sea Eight Hands) Shokushujin (Tentacle Blade: Tentacle Blade) Sharper than anything in the world, with the blade of the Abyss, I cut through the obstructing wire fur and left a wound on its body.</p>
<p>Shinkaiyatsude (Deep Sea Eight Hands) Kakekushi (Shadow Skewer: Shadow Skewer) With a skewer that shot out from the Iron Helmet Wolf's own shadow, I pierced its leg, depriving it of mobility. In pain of a different dimension than before, the Iron Helmet Wolf whimpered and let out a mournful cry. Hearing that sound, an enormous number of mutated wild dogs and enhanced wild dogs, to protect their leader, abandoned the BS Defender agents they were fighting and began to charge at me like crazy. Ah, how annoying.</p>
<p>I need to finish off the Iron Helmet Wolf quickly, so its berserk cooldown time doesn't come. Just then, as if reading my thoughts, Han Yu-gyeong stepped forward. Fourth-Tier Magic. Espada Estrellada (Star-Light Sword) Instantly, the sky darkened as if it had become night, and changed into a starry night sky that would never be seen in Seoul. And without distinction between mutated wild dogs, enhanced wild dogs, or BS Defender agents, stars began to fall like swords.</p>
<p>After a short time, the sky brightened again, and the star-light swords disappeared. Most were lying collapsed, suffering with blood holes pierced through them, and those who unluckily took a star-light sword to a vital spot were in a state of death. If Han Yu-gyeong's level had been a little higher, or her proficiency in star magic had been higher, she might have been able to annihilate them completely.</p>
<p>Still, since most were incapacitated, there was no one to hinder me from finishing off the Iron Helmet Wolf, so I spread my palm and struck the Iron Helmet Wolf's breast. Shinkaiyatsude (Deep Sea Eight Hands) Shintōbanin (Penetrating Disk Seal: Penetrating Disk Seal) Shintōbanin was a typical penetrating technique that struck the outside to wound the inside. It was a skill used to deal with creatures with high defense like the Iron Helmet Wolf. The Abyss's aura that permeated the Iron Helmet Wolf's body by Shintōbanin directly burst its heart.</p>
<p>The Iron Helmet Wolf vomited blood and breathed its last.</p>
<p>"Shall we deal with all the remaining guys?" Since most were hit by Han Yu-gyeong's magic without any preparation, all that was left was to slit the throats of the fallen guys. Han Yu-gyeong summoned Nero and White Bear. Since it would take considerable time if they tried to deliver the finishing blows by themselves, Nero and White Bear also had to lend their strength. Drawing out the Abyss long, and making it like a long skewer, regardless of whether they were mutated wild dogs or BS Group members, I went around stabbing their vital spots.</p>
<p>After about ten minutes of stabbing, a level-up window appeared. LEVEL UP! Please select a Trait. From dawn until today, having caught numerous mutated wild dogs and even the boss, the Iron Helmet Wolf, my level-up was fast. Han Yu-gyeong must have leveled up twice, right? While thinking about the level-up window, I felt a presence in my senses. I first dismissed the level-up window and ran at an incredible speed towards where I felt the presence. Had I gone about three hundred meters?</p>
<p>There was a man with a telescope and a filming camera around his neck, looking at me, surprised by my sudden appearance in front of him. Ah, I instinctively thought, 'This guy must be the surveillance soldier who reported me as a superhuman to the BS Group.' "Did you report?"</p>
<p>"Y-yes? I tried to contact them to report, but they didn't answer the call for some reason...." Seeing that they weren't answering calls, it seemed Vanguard was successfully carrying out the attack. That's good news.</p>
<p>"Did you upload the video to the cloud or the internet?"</p>
<p>"Ah, not yet. Team Leader-nim's command hasn't been given yet.... It's true! Th-there's a lot of sensitive information, so I often delete it without uploading it directly to the cloud! I also sent the video I filmed during lunch today to the Boss-nim and deleted it."</p>
<p>If it's Boss-nim, he's probably referring to Park Yeong-beom, the boss of BS Defender. Having heard all the necessary information, I dealt with the surveillance soldier, dragged the corpse, and went to where Han Yu-gyeong, Nero, and White Bear were diligently slitting the throats of the fallen guys as if digging for sweet potatoes. Perhaps because there were so many of them, the time it took to end everyone's lives took longer than the time spent fighting.</p>
<p>If they hadn't died from excessive bleeding from the blood spilled from where they were pierced by the star-light swords, it would have taken even longer. With the collection syringe gun I had prepared, I collected a sample from the Iron Helmet Wolf, left a note saying [Jellyfish Feed] on top of the collected corpses, and offered it as a tribute to Simyeonui ttal.</p>
<p>From the ground, a black maw opened wide, ravenously devouring the corpses I had gathered and disappearing, the empty lot of the Divinity Group's secret laboratory, which had been overflowing with corpses, instantly became clean. If there are no corpses, it's a perfect crime. Now, the 130 BS Defender agents had become complete missing persons. No one would ever see them again.</p>
<p>"It's always amazing to see. Where on earth do you send them?"</p>
<p>"I send them to the sea as jellyfish food. Shall we go to the laboratory now and look for data?" I had collected samples, and now, if I only obtained research data from the laboratory, I would have gotten everything there was to get from Bukhansan National Park. Although there would still be widely spread mutated wild dogs, it was too troublesome to climb mountains and wander around to level up, so I decided to leave them. I should tell Han Yu-gyeong to use them as a leveling field for Vanguard agents. We entered the laboratory.</p>
<p>Since all the doors were broken, it was convenient to move around freely. Although there was a quite thick iron door, there was a hole large enough for a person to pass through, melted by immense heat. The Iron Helmet Wolf probably melted the iron door with the ultra-high heat of its berserk state, causing the mutated wild dogs to leak out of the laboratory. Since I roughly knew the layout, I immediately headed to the laboratory where the data was.</p>
<p>Sharing with Han Yu-gyeong, we pulled out the computer's hard drives and gathered important-looking medicines/drugs. Other places would only have broken cages anyway, so there was nothing else to take besides research data.</p>
<p>"Now that we've gathered all the research data, all that's left is to finish up with BS Defender."</p>
<p>"Okay, I'll call Dickson." As Han Yu-gyeong called Dickson, Dickson quickly answered the phone. Perhaps he was very worried, because as soon as he answered, Dickson's voice, asking "Are you okay?", could be heard from beyond the phone.</p>
<p>"I'm fine. But how are things on your end?" -"It's going smoothly. Most of the troops went towards the Boss-nim's side, so we entered BS Defense without significant harm and captured the boss, Park Yeong-beom."</p>
<p>Oh, that's good. During the BS Defender event, if things went wrong, they would escape through a secret passage. If they were captured without even being able to escape, it meant there weren't enough troops to stop Vanguard. Because someone needs to buy time for them to escape.</p>
<p>"It seems we don't need to go. Park Yeong-beom tried to kill us, so deal with him properly. You can even make it look like Park Il-soo's doing." Han Yu-gyeong spoke coldly and hung up the phone. Come to think of it, all the successors of the BS Group died by our hands. Now, Park Il-man, who lost his son, will rampage. Just like Park Il-soo, who blew up a building after Park Seong-beom died before. Now, it's only a matter of time before the BS Group collapses.</p>
<p>"Let's go to the research building where Lara is waiting, and deliver the samples as well."</p>
<p>"Yeah, I'm hungry. Shall we buy something to eat and go?" I hadn't eaten much even at lunch due to the sniping, and now it was slowly approaching evening, so my stomach was rumbling. It seemed perfect to get dinner takeout from a good restaurant.</p>

<p>As expected, my Future Eye (未來眼) was right. The white-haired woman made an unknown sound, thinking of someone. After observing 'him,' her Future Eye began to draw a new future. "I don't know when we'll meet, but I look forward to it." The white-haired woman, who had muttered to herself, began to descend the stairs with the clack-clack sound of high heels. And she took out an employee ID from her pocket and hung it around her neck. The employee ID read 'Cheon Ha-rin, Managing Director of Jeil Electronics.'</p>
