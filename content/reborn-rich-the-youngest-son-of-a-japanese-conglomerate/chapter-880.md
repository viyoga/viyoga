<p>Naturally, those who came to capture Fujigasaki Rinka were not just the White Light Fist Practitioners. The White Light Fist Practitioners acted as a type of debuffer, suppressing Fujigasaki Rinka's Abyss abilities and inflicting continuous damage, while those who faced Fujigasaki Rinka were old Masters affiliated with Anyaji's Guardian Hall.</p>
<p>Three Anyaji Guardians passed by the White Light Fist Practitioners surrounding Fujigasaki Rinka and stood before her. And the Guardian standing in the middle, who looked the youngest, opened his mouth.</p>
<p>"Long time no see, Rinka."</p>
<p>"Serihara -sir, were you also with Anyaji?" Fujigasaki Rinka knew the true identity of the Anyaji Guardian right from the start. Under the name Serihara Tamijiro, he was an existence who could be called the best friend of Soda Haruto, Fujigasaki Rinka's Sabu. After Takakusaki Gengo stole the essence of Shinkaiyatsude, Soda Haruto had been missing for several years, and since then, Fujigasaki Rinka had never seen Serihara Tamijiro. But the fact that Serihara Tamijiro appeared as an Anyaji affiliate meant....</p>
<p>"You and Takakusaki Gengo have been together since long ago."</p>
<p>"I merely gave Gengo advice. That to get Revenge, his current martial arts alone wouldn't be enough." Fujigasaki Rinka realized that the one who manipulated Takakusaki Gengo from behind to steal Shinkaiyatsude was none other than Serihara Tamijiro. That meant that Serihara Tamijiro was involved in the disappearance of her Sabu, Soda Haruto.</p>
<p>"Then Sabu also...?"</p>
<p>"Hehe, Haruto, that guy, although he was a Fist Technique practitioner with few rivals, he couldn't withstand Gengo's and my combined Attack." At Serihara Tamijiro's Words, Fujigasaki Rinka's Fading Reason snapped. Because the Cause of her Sabu's disappearance, or Death, was right before her Eyes, she couldn't suppress her burning Anger.</p>
<p>"Die!" Woooong! Gathering the Abyss's dark Aura in her fist, Fujigasaki Rinka, who was rushing towards Serihara Tamijiro, had to first face the white Light of the White Light Fist Practitioners.</p>
<p>"Kueuk!" Upon being hit by the Light, Fujigasaki Rinka's Internal Organs were shaken, and she vomited Blood. Each individual Light from the White Light Fist Practitioners was weaker compared to the original, Takakusaki Athena, but instead, they compensated for the Light's weakness with sheer numbers.</p>
<p>"Indeed, my Disciple, that guy, must have struggled." Fujigasaki Rinka, who had leaped back, muttered. She somewhat understood the Words of her Disciple, Taira Ryuta, who had made such a Fuss about the danger over the Phone Call.</p>
<p>"But... I should be able to overcome this much. When I meet him later, I'll have to scold him." -Shinkaiyatsude (Deep Sea Eight-Armed) Black Fog. Fujigasaki Rinka drew out the Abyss from her entire Body like fog. Fujigasaki Rinka, who had already reached the Climax Level in handling the dangerous energy source known as the Abyss, instantly created a technique suitable for the current Situation and covered the vicinity with Abyss Fog.</p>
<p>The white Light emitted by the White Light Fist Practitioners now began to appear blurry, obscured by the black fog. Although it couldn't be said that its influence had completely disappeared, it wasn't enough to make her vomit Blood like before. If so, there was almost nothing the White Light Fist Practitioners could do now. Kwadeuk! Fujigasaki Rinka struck a White Light Fist Practitioner's neck bone with her Elbow, instantly bringing down one White Light Fist Practitioner.</p>
<p>Because his neck bone was broken with a single strike, if not quickly treated, this White Light Fist Practitioner would die. And Fujigasaki Rinka had no intention of doing anything for the fallen White Light Fist Practitioner. And next was another White Light Fist Practitioner, who was also creating an Encirclement from the Side and emitting white Light. No matter how much Light the White Light Fist Practitioner emitted with a Fearful Expression, it was useless. Because the Light was swallowed by the Abyss Fog, emitting only a blurry glow.</p>
<p>Kwaang! This time, half of his Head was blown off by a kick. The White Light Fist Practitioners, emitting Light in the Darkness, were easy Prey. Rushing to where the blurry Light was emitted, and swinging either a Fist or a Foot, what ultimately connected was the Body of a White Light Fist Practitioner. Of course, even without Light, because Fujigasaki Rinka could perceive everything within this black fog as if she saw it clearly, it could be said that the swift elimination of the White Light Fist Practitioners was perhaps inevitable.</p>
<p>'Good, isn't it?' Although it was a new technique created from the White Light Fist Practitioners' Attack, Fujigasaki Rinka felt Satisfaction.</p>
<p>"Haap!" Fujigasaki Rinka, feeling Satisfaction with her new technique, again rushed to where the blurry Light was shining and swung her Fist. -Shinkaiyatsude Kakuumidashoo (Striking the Sea to Hit the Reef). What Fujigasaki Rinka used this time was not a typical straight punch, but Kakuumidashoo, a type of the Gyeoksantau (Striking the Mountain to Hit the Ox) technique. She struck the White Light Fist Practitioner to Attack the Anyaji Guardian waiting behind him.</p>
<p>"Kwaaaah! How could...!" Since the Anyaji Guardian was not a fool either, he could easily notice Fujigasaki Rinka attacking the White Light Fist Practitioners shining in the Darkness. So, he had been waiting around the White Light Fist Practitioners, intending to launch an Ambush when Fujigasaki Rinka attacked them. But what they didn't know was that Fujigasaki Rinka's combat Sensation within the Abyss Fog was almost omniscient. Because she already knew they were preparing an Ambush, she attacked with Choksimpagong.</p>
<p>Of course, even so, since her Opponent was a Guardian affiliated with the Guardian Hall, he didn't become combat-incapable with a single Attack. Fujigasaki Rinka also knew that fact, so she immediately closed in and began to pummel his Body as if mincing it. Peopeopeopeopeopeok! The sound of hitting a meat bag in the Darkness spread around. Fujigasaki Rinka, who had thus turned her Opponent into a chunk of minced meat, instantly removed the remaining White Light Fist Practitioners. And then she retracted the Black Fog.</p>
<p>What remained in the arena were only three people in total: Fujigasaki Rinka, Serihara Tamijiro, and one Guardian from the Guardian Hall. The remaining Anyaji Attackers had all lost a part of their Body, fallen, and their Breath had ceased, and especially the Anyaji Guardian who had hidden behind the White Light Fist Practitioner, preparing an Ambush, was dead in a way that 'horrific' was an understatement.</p>
<p>Fujigasaki Rinka, who had shaken off the Blood clinging to her Fist by swinging it, pointed at Serihara Tamijiro, who stood with an Expression of half Astonishment, half Fear.</p>
<p>"You, if you run, I'll kill your entire family." Fujigasaki Rinka, who had known Serihara Tamijiro since long ago and knew he had a family, after Threatening Serihara Tamijiro not to run, sprinted towards the remaining Anyaji Guardian. Of course, the Anyaji Guardian also didn't just bear the name 'Guardian' for nothing, he tried to resist as much as possible.</p>
<p>But no matter how plausible the title of 'Guardian' he held, ultimately, he was beneath Anyaji's Chief, Takakusaki Gengo, and was no match for Fujigasaki Rinka, who had possessed Skill equal to Takakusaki Gengo since High School. It seemed they exchanged Fists a few times, but he gradually began to be at a disadvantage and started allowing Fujigasaki Rinka's Attacks. Because those Attacks were not ordinary strikes, but Shinkaiyatsude's Murder Martial Arts Form, the moment he allowed one Attack, it was safe to say the Match was already over.</p>
<p>Collapse...! The Last strike was Choksimpagong, which Fujigasaki Rinka's Disciple, Taira Ryuta, was said to have learned in a Dream. Its power was so immense that his Belly was completely pierced, to the point where his back was visible. It looked as if his Belly had been pressed and pierced by a hydraulic Press. The nameless Anyaji Guardian, with his Belly pierced, fell to his Knees and died. Now, all that remained was Serihara Tamijiro, who could be called the Enemy of Fujigasaki Rinka's Sabu, Soda Haruto.</p>
<p>"Lest you misunderstand, I'm not just waiting quietly because you Threatened me." Even though his Colleague had died before his Eyes, with a Head-sized Hole pierced through his Belly, Serihara Tamijiro's Expression was calm. With an Expression that conveyed his conviction of his own Survival, Serihara Tamijiro opened his Mouth.</p>
<p>"Your Sabu, Soda Haruto, is not dead. If you kill me, you'll never be able to find him, will you?" He had an Expression filled with the conviction that Fujigasaki Rinka could never kill him. He was letting Fujigasaki Rinka know that to save her Sabu, she would instead have to kneel and beg.</p>
<p>"...." Even after hearing Serihara Tamijiro's Words that her Sabu was not dead, Fujigasaki Rinka glared at Serihara Tamijiro without a single Word. Glaring with her Clothes and Face covered in Blood, her Appearance was quite terrifying.</p>
<p>"A long time ago, I watched a Movie with Sabu." Fujigasaki Rinka, who had been glaring at Serihara Tamijiro in Silence for a while, opened her Mouth.</p>
<p>"I don't remember what Movie it was. But I still remember one Scene. It was a Scene where someone was taken Hostage, and the protagonist was worrying whether to let the Villain go."</p>
<p>At that Moment, Serihara Tamijiro realized that the Movie Scene Fujigasaki Rinka was talking about was quite similar to the current Situation.</p>
<p>"As a child, I couldn't possibly know what to do in that Situation, so I asked Sabu. Back then, Sabu was a know-it-all who seemed to know everything. Do you know what Sabu said when I asked what I should do?"</p>
<p>"Haruto, what, what did he say?" As Serihara Tamijiro asked with an Expression engulfed in Fear, Fujigasaki Rinka, instead of the glaring Expression she had just moments ago, showed her usual Smile and took one Step forward. Although the distance between them was quite far, as Fujigasaki Rinka took one Step closer, they became so close that it was as if their noses could touch. Fujigasaki Rinka's smiling Face filled Serihara Tamijiro's Field of Vision.</p>
<p>"Rather than Subjugating to the Enemy, kill them before the Hostage is killed, or if it's a Situation where the Hostage's life cannot be guaranteed, it's better to kill the Enemy and get Revenge, that's what he said was the correct Answer."</p>
<p>Kwang! Serihara Tamijiro, hit by a Fist whose swing he hadn't even perceived, was sent flying an immense distance as if he had been hit by a car. Fujigasaki Rinka, after leaping with such immense Power that the Floor seemed to cave in, caught up to the rebounding Serihara Tamijiro. And then she opened her Mouth again.</p>
<p>"You will either die a Pleasurable Ache today, or you will tell me where Sabu is." Serihara Tamijiro, Fujigasaki Rinka's Emotionless Words felt like a Prophecy that would soon come true.</p>
