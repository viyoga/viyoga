<p>In the Tairaga, this was the second funeral. Following the funeral of his father, Taira Saburo, which he experienced not long after coming to this World, it was now the funeral of his cousin, Taira Kitsumasa, which he was attending.</p>
<p>In fact, he had only seen Taira Kitsumasa once, at Taira Saburo's funeral, so the news of his Death didn't particularly move him. Come to think of it, if counting encounters until today, they were all at funerals.</p>
<p>"I think it was a good idea to leave a little early. The Cars are starting to get backed up."</p>
<p>Tsukiyo, who was driving, said. Looking Outside the window, stately black Sedans, similar to the one they were in, were lined up, heading towards the Taira Main family. As expected, there were more customers when the Successor's Son died than when the Successor himself died.</p>
<p>"Next time, should we take a Helicopter in this kind of Situation?"</p>
<p>Suddenly, feeling that the time wasted on the Road was a shame, he mentioned buying a Helicopter. Originally, he had enough funds, and with the Behemoth bounty earned in the USA this time, he had enough Money to purchase several Helicopters, so there was no worry about funds.</p>
<p>"That sounds good too~! Then.... I'll have to get a Helicopter license, won't I?"</p>
<p>Tsukiyo seemed quite intrigued by my proposal. Seeing as she looked ready to get a Helicopter license right away.</p>
<p>She loved learning so much that even a Helicopter license seemed like quite a challenge to her.</p>
<p>"That would be fine. Yongtae-nim's time is important, after all."</p>
<p>Linxiang also nodded her Head with a serious Expression, agreeing to my proposal. If even Linxiang agreed, I'd have to seriously consider it.</p>
<p>"Now that the Main family is starting to come into view, let's seriously consider the Helicopter Matter later.... I need to sharpen my Sensitive Nerves."</p>
<p>Arriving at the Taira Main family Mansion, which was practically a Den of iniquity, he had to be careful with his actions. Even if he was Taira Danjo's Youngest Grandson.</p>
<p>"We've arrived~!"</p>
<p>"Okay. Thanks for your hard Work today too."</p>
<p>"Don't worry. I'll be downloading and looking at the Helicopter Textbook."</p>
<p>He was worried about Tsukiyo, who would be alone guarding the Car again today, so he asked her to bear the trouble, but Tsukiyo seemed to have already entered study Mode. Seeing as she was already searching for a Helicopter control Textbook on her phone.</p>
<p>"First.... Let's go over there."</p>
<p>The place they parked was the Taira Family's exclusive Parking Lot, and since they had decided to meet Taira Yukiko there and go to the Main family together, they decided to wait on a bench next to the Parking Lot.</p>
<p>After walking a bit, they saw Taira Yukiko sitting on a bench, and Taira Yukiko's secretary, Ikehara Koyuki, standing beside her. It seemed they had arrived earlier and were waiting.</p>
<p>"Ryuta, are you here?"</p>
<p>"Mother, you were waiting."</p>
<p>Normally, he would call her Yukiko-ssi, but since this was the Main family with many Sensitive Ears, he decided to call Taira Yukiko "Mother" today.</p>
<p>"Ufufu, I left quite early, knowing the Cars would be backed up."</p>
<p>They had left early too, but Taira Yukiko seemed to have left even earlier. I rummaged through my Embrace and handed Yukiko the accessory I had purchased at the USA black market auction.</p>
<p>"What is this?"</p>
<p>"I bought it when I went to the USA, it just came to Mind."</p>
<p>In fact, it was an ordinary accessory with no special options, but I bought it because it somehow seemed to suit Taira Yukiko. It was a luxurious earring with pink Gold and pink Diamonds, and at the time of the black market auction, most of the customers were Magicians, so even when pretty accessories came up, there was no particular reaction, allowing me to buy it at a surprisingly low Price.</p>
<p>"Oh my...♥ It's pretty. I'll have to try it on later. Thank you, Ryuta."</p>
<p>Being pink Gold and pink Diamond, it suited her current Appearance and would also suit her Succubus form. Since it was a funeral, she couldn't wear it immediately, but Taira Yukiko had a very satisfied Expression.</p>
<p>"Let's go in now before it gets too late."</p>
<p>After chatting for a while, we entered the Main family before it got too late. Since it was a funeral, we had to be a bit careful inside the Main family building, which is why I had a brief conversation with Taira Yukiko on the Parking Lot bench.</p>
<p>As expected, just like at Taira Saburo's funeral last year, a sign reading 'Kichu' , indicating that a funeral was taking place, was placed on the gate. Seeing it, the reality of a funeral hit me once again.</p>
<p>Upon entering the Main family, Taira Jiro and his Eldest son, Taira Yuuichi, were seen in the distance, greeting guests. Around them were the affiliate company Bosses and cooperative company Bosses of Taira Jiro's Pabeol, politicians, and prominent Family members, giving the feeling of flaunting their power.</p>
<p>Of course, as it was the Main family where Taira Danjo resided, there were no troops, but seeing it anew like this, it felt like a force capable of rivaling Taira Ichiro, the most likely Successor candidate. Excluding Taira Foodstuff and Taira Yukiko's Talent Development Institute, which I was not officially supporting, there was no one supporting me, so in terms of influence, there was no comparison. Well, I don't think I'm inferior in terms of military might, though. Of course, not everything can be solved by Power, so I should gradually build up my own influence.</p>
<p>"Uncle Jiro, may the deceased rest in peace."</p>
<p>"Thank you."</p>
<p>When he offered his condolences to Taira Jiro, Taira Jiro, as the chief mourner, gave a polite Answer rather than informal speech. His large Size (though smaller than mine) was unchanged, but his Face looked quite gaunt, and it seemed he was suffering greatly in his Mind, perhaps from losing his Son.</p>
<p>"There's much to talk about.... But now I must greet the guests, so let's do it later."</p>
<p>"Yes. Please bear the trouble."</p>
<p>At Taira Jiro's Words, I nodded my Head and moved aside.</p>
<p>"Yuuichi-hyung, may the deceased rest in peace."</p>
<p>"Thank you."</p>
<p>I heard they were quite close Siblings, but he also looked to be in quite a bad state.</p>
<p>"I heard you're coming back to Japan this time?"</p>
<p>"Yes, that's right. We'll be seeing each other often from now on."</p>
<p>Hearing his Answer, it seemed his return to Japan was official. Even if he came to Japan, I didn't think we'd see each other often. We were both busy, and if I met Taira Jiro's Son, Taira Yuuichi, often, there was a high Possibility of misunderstanding from those around us, so I wouldn't seek him out first.</p>
<p>Next, it was my Turn to offer condolences to Urata Rie, Taira Jiro's ex-Wife. Although she was the ex-Wife, she was the biological Mother of the deceased Taira Kitsumasa, so she seemed to be holding the Seat instead of the current Wife, Taira Moko. And since Taira Moko was also unwell, it was a Situation where she couldn't hold the Seat.</p>
<p>Urata Rie was a middle-aged Woman who resembled the deceased Taira Kitsumasa more than Taira Yuuichi, whom I had just greeted, and looking at her current state, it seemed her Soul was almost half-gone. Even after divorcing Taira Jiro, she had maintained a close relationship with her children, so the shock of her Son's Death seemed considerable.</p>
<p>"May the deceased rest in peace."</p>
<p>"...Thank you...."</p>
<p>Since I had nothing particular to say to Urata Rie, whom I was meeting for the first time, I merely offered my condolences and passed the Turn to Taira Yukiko, who was next.</p>
<p>"Yukiko-ssi.... Heueuueuk!"</p>
<p>Upon seeing Taira Yukiko, whose Face she recognized, Urata Rie seemed to be overcome with emotion and began to shed Tears.</p>
<p>Taira Yukiko, without panicking, embraced Urata Rie and comforted her, patting her gently. Seeing the Situation, it seemed I would have to wait for a while, so I headed towards the gathering of Taira Family members and high-ranking Taira Group executives.</p>
<p>"Hello."</p>
<p>I greeted those around me and took a Seat. They were distant Relatives of the Tairaga and Bosses or Executives of affiliate companies with whom I had little contact, so the Atmosphere was a bit awkward, but I decided to sit for now.</p>
<p>For reference, Linxiang and Taira Yukiko's secretary, Ikehara Koyuki, were in different Seats. Linxiang would naturally be moving around to gather useful Information for us.</p>
<p>"I heard Mr. Ito's Business is doing well these days."</p>
<p>"You flatter me. I heard that...."</p>
<p>Still, I couldn't just sit there doing nothing like a forgotten scarecrow, so I decided to have a Conversation with the affiliate company Bosses. A few Words of Conversation wouldn't make them support me, but building some rapport would still be a good thing.</p>
<p>Jeobeok jeobeok.</p>
<p>As I was talking with the affiliate company Bosses, I heard approaching Footsteps. I glanced over, thinking someone had finished their greetings and was coming to our Seats, and saw that Taira Yukiko was still caught up in Conversation with Urata Rie, and the ones approaching us were an Elderly Person and a young Woman.</p>
<p>"Oh...! Taira Electronics'...."</p>
<p>The affiliate company Boss I was talking to also muttered in surprise, seeing the two approaching. The Elderly Person was a Face I had seen once before at the Taira Grand Meeting. It was Shinonome Zen, the Boss of Taira Electronics, the core of the core of the Taira Non-Faction.</p>
<p>The young, Silver-haired Woman next to Shinonome Zen was dressed in attire that made one wonder if she was really at a funeral: black Platform Boots that reached her Shins, patterned Stockings that reached her Thighs with garter belts, and a Gothic Lolita-style black Dress.</p>
<p>It was an Attire that made one wonder if it was appropriate for a funeral, but since it was a black Dress, it felt a bit ambiguous to point it out.</p>
<p>Seeing her with Shinonome Zen, could it be.... Is it really Shinonome Mio? The Photo I had showed a demure Japanese Woman with ebony-like black Hair, but after her Father's Death, she was said to have become a Cripple, and her Appearance seemed to have changed considerably.</p>
<p>Shinonome Zen immediately went to an empty Seat and sat down, but Shinonome Mio did not. She was looking straight at me and approaching.</p>
<p>"Oh...!"</p>
<p>Shinonome Zen also seemed a little surprised when his Granddaughter approached me. Seeing that stubborn-looking Elderly Person involuntarily make a sound of surprise.</p>
