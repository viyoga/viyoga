<p>"Ah, well...." I explained to Takakusaki Athena, who had entered late. When I told her that one of the University Students had disappeared, and according to the University Students, the resort employees were also nowhere to be seen, her Expression became slightly serious.</p>
<p>"Eh~ So we can't eat Breakfast?" Ah, that's what made her serious?</p>
<p>"Our Students and the female college students are in the kitchen, so you can go to the kitchen and ask for an extra serving."</p>
<p>"Shall I~?" Takakusaki Athena headed to the restaurant with light Footsteps. Perhaps because of her affiliation with Anyaji, she didn't seem to care much about a person's disappearance. It was as if she thought, 'They're not even dead, so what?' "After Breakfast, we'll also take a look around, so don't Worry too much."</p>
<p>"Ah, thank you." The Club President of the International Politics Debate Club looked too worried, so I spoke soothingly. In fact, while I was soothing her, my purpose was more to find the employees than to find the missing University Student. We couldn't go Outside today because of the snow, and if there were no employees, we'd have to prepare our own lunch and dinner, which would be a bit much for guests who came to a VIP resort.</p>
<p>"Have Breakfast~!" There wasn't much to prepare, so Breakfast came out quickly. It was just French toast dipped in egg wash, eggs, cereal, and Milk, so it wouldn't take long. Since it was food we made ourselves, not food prepared by employees, we ate without dissatisfaction. If Breakfast at a VIP resort had been this level, we would have been frustrated, but given the circumstances, it couldn't be helped.</p>
<p>"Ryuta, is it delicious? Alik made the toast."</p>
<p>"Yeah, maybe because Alik made it, it's even more delicious?" Perhaps because she learned cooking from Shimokawabe Airen, the Home Economics Teacher at Egawa Kawa, Alik's French toast was delicious. Even though it was a simple dish, it had a richer and deeper taste. Hearing my Answer, Alik, seemingly satisfied, cut her French toast with slightly joyful Hand movements and put it into her mouth.</p>
<p>"Then.... I'll take a look around. It's dangerous, so should we have one group per room?" If all the employees disappearing was truly the act of a Murderer or a dangerous entity, the Students should not be left alone. Even one group per room was equally dangerous. Especially Room 301, which had Oofuchi Erika, Shido Tsubaki, and Sakagami Rena, only Sakagami Rena was a Combatant, and while Sakagami Rena's Combat power was strong, she felt more like an Information agent than a Combatant. Ah, that's how we'll do it.</p>
<p>"Athena-ssi, go to the 301 group and help them." Although there was a probability that Takakusaki Athena was the culprit, after seeing her a little yesterday, she didn't seem like someone who would kill just anyone at a travel destination, so I entrusted her to the 301 group. I thought that if anything happened, I could make the search area close to me so I could immediately help.</p>
<p>"Then let's decide on a floor, take a look around, and meet back at the restaurant. If anything urgent happens, Contact us by phone."</p>
<p>"Are you okay alone?" At my Words, Minamoto Lisa asked with a worried Expression, so I smiled as if to say I was fine.</p>
<p>"Rather, I wish someone would Attack me if I'm alone. If there's a culprit in this incident, I can catch them. First, I'll check the Outside of the resort, Room 301 will take the 1st floor, Room 302 the 2nd floor...."</p>
<p>Since the resort had a total of 5 floors, including the University Students, we could all go around the resort at once. Also, the 301 group I was worried about, with me Outside and 301 on the 1st floor, were practically on the same level, so if the Students screamed, I could immediately join them.</p>
<p>"Then I'll see you back at the restaurant." I waved to the Students and University Students and went Outside the resort. Snow had accumulated to about a third of the height of the resort Entrance, so I had to force the door open to go Outside. The door opened with the crunching sound of snow being compressed. Hwiiiii~! As soon as I opened the resort Entrance, a blizzard was raging wildly. If it weren't for the Trait that also boosts Attribute Defense, it would definitely be a temperature to get Frostbite.</p>
<p>First, I decided to start with the barbecue area, go around the resort building, and check the hot spring and sauna room. Both at the resort Entrance and in the Surroundings, snow had accumulated almost up to my Thighs. For an ordinary Person, walking would be difficult, but I had Telekinesis. I floated in the empty space, piercing through the blizzard, and headed towards the barbecue area.</p>
<p>As expected, snow was blocking the door, so after clearing it and entering, the area where we had eaten yesterday was tidy, but the spot where the University Students had drunk late into the night was a mess. Did the employees all desert en masse? I thought I should grab the Drinks and snacks inside the barbecue area later as I walked around it. Inside the barbecue area, there was nothing much except the traces of the University Students' Drinking Party.</p>
<p>"The barbecue area is clear for now." I grabbed a Bottle of Cola and came out of the barbecue area. Then I flicked my Finger to open the Bottle cap and drank it with a Swallowing Sound. I hadn't drunk water before coming out, so I was thirsty, and there were Drinks in the barbecue area, just right. Next, the hot spring? With the resort building at the center, the barbecue area was on the right, and the hot spring and sauna room were on the left, so I decided to go around the back of the building to the hot spring.</p>
<p>Behind the resort building, there was a trash disposal area and a warehouse for storing equipment, so I thought it was worth checking there too.</p>
<p>"Oh?" As I floated and flew around the back of the building, I felt something strange. Usually, when it snows, it feels mostly flat, but this wasn't flat; one side felt somewhat convex, like a Grave. Like a Grave.... I approached it and dug through the snow with Telekinesis.</p>
<p>"!" What was revealed as the snow was dug away was a Corpse with a Sword stabbed in its Neck. Seeing the familiar Appearance and that it wasn't employee attire, it seemed to be the University Student who had disappeared.</p>
<p>"Come to the corridor connected to the back of the building." I used the Phone Call to summon the Students from Room 301 who were searching the 1st floor. I didn't call them Outside in this cold, but to the outer corridor. Deureureuk! After waiting a bit, a Window on the 1st floor opened. The first to stick her Head out was Takakusaki Athena.</p>
<p>"Ah, it's cold...! What is it?"</p>
<p>"A Corpse." At the word "Corpse," Takakusaki Athena looked at the Corpse carefully, and Oofuchi Erika and Shido Tsubaki, being Laypersons, seemed quite surprised to see it. Of course, Sakagami Rena was also guessing the state of the Corpse. I had only called the 301 Students who were searching the 1st floor, thinking that the other groups wouldn't be able to come immediately since most of the Windows connected to the back of the building were in guest rooms. I took a Photo of the Corpse to share its Appearance with everyone.</p>
<p>Then I cut a Branch from a nearby Tree with the edge of my Hand and deeply stuck it next to the University Student's Corpse. This was to make it easy to find again even if it kept snowing. I had no intention of bringing the Corpse indoors. I thought it would be more convenient for Corpse preservation to leave it buried in the snow. If I brought it inside and it started to decompose, it would be a nuisance to deal with.</p>
<p>"For now, I've marked it here, so I'll move again. Now that it's clearly a Murder case, the unseen employees probably aren't safe either. I know you're surprised, but please thoroughly search the 1st floor for traces."</p>
<p>I spoke to the University Students who were shedding Tears on the 5th floor and moved again. Moving to the trash disposal area, as expected, a man in resort employee Clothes was found in the incinerator, a cold Corpse with a Sword wound in his Neck. I took another Photo of the employee. Besides the incinerator, in a large trash can, two men in chef attire were amicably crammed in, both with Sword wounds in their Necks. Already four people dead. I thought this wouldn't be all.</p>
<p>After taking all the Corpse photos, I passed the trash disposal area and headed towards the warehouse. Cheolkeok! The warehouse door was locked. I forcibly twisted the door open. Inside the warehouse were repair items, cleaning supplies, and spare cooking tools, but all the Swords among the cooking tools were gone. I wondered why each Corpse had a Sword in its Neck, and then realized the killer must have obtained a large number of Swords from here. I took a picture of the empty Sword rack and then came Outside.</p>
<p>There were no Corpses in the warehouse, so there was nothing more to see.</p>
<p>"Here too." There was another spot that bulged slightly like a Grave. When I dug it up with Telekinesis, this time a Female Employee was lying there with a Sword wound in her Neck. The snow had naturally hidden the Corpse, but from the Murderer's perspective, they probably hadn't considered that someone like me would be directly moving around in such Extreme Cold weather. There were no Corpses near the hot spring and sauna room.</p>
<p>However, I hadn't properly checked the front of the resort Entrance, so I re-checked the resort front and the barbecue area. The Corpses found there were three in total. Two Security Guards in guard uniforms and one employee were the victims. I took Photos of those Corpses and re-entered the resort. In truth, I didn't know how many employees stayed overnight, so I couldn't tell how many Corpses there might be. There was no way for me to know if the Corpses I found were all of them, or if there were more I hadn't found.</p>
<p>Still, I couldn't keep searching for Corpses indefinitely, so I had no choice but to go inside the resort. As I headed towards the restaurant, everyone was gathered in front of the restaurant Door.</p>
<p>"Why aren't you going into the restaurant?" When I asked, Shido Tsubaki proposed going to the Auditorium instead of the restaurant. Such resorts often had auditoriums for academic groups or companies holding workshops to report their Achievements to each other. Indeed.... An Auditorium with a beam projector seemed better than passing around a phone to view all the photos. Because we could all see them at the same time.</p>
<p>"Then let's go to the Auditorium."</p>
<p>"The Auditorium is on the 2nd floor." We took the nearby stairs instead of the elevator and headed to the 2nd floor.</p>
