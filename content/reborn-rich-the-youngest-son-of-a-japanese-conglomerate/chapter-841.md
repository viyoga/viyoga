<p>"How about the profits from this operation?" Inside the small passenger Airplane flying from Brazil to the USA, Takakusaki Gengo asked the man standing next to him. Since only Anyaji personnel were inside the passenger Airplane, no one commented on the man standing next to Takakusaki Gengo. This man, with an 8:2 hair part and wearing a suit, was Hojo Otoji (方丈 音次), an excellent Fist Technique practitioner and also the second-in-command overseeing various tasks for Anyaji. In addition to Fist Technique and management, he was skilled in Jusul and was also the Sabu of Funayama Seigo, one of the Four Heavenly Kings. "...Since we succeeded in the request, 200 million yen will be deposited." "Besides the 200 million yen, what about the profits obtained from the favela?" Anyaji had gone to Brazil not just to recruit a Four Heavenly King, but at someone's request. While carrying out the request in Brazil, they gathered Information and sought out an excellent Fist Technique practitioner, which is how Bernardo appeared on Anyaji's Radar. "We confiscated all the property of the Vila Cruzeiro favela Drugs gang, disposing of all the Gold ingots, Cash, cryptocurrencies, and various ornaments, obtaining about 1.5 billion yen. Of course, if we had thoroughly plundered the hidden assets and Accounts concealed in the mansion, twice that amount would have been possible, but that would have taken too much Time...." The Massacre of the Drugs gang was indeed to acquire Bernardo, a new Four Heavenly King candidate, but Anyaji, as it had always done, did not forget to extort the property of the forces it destroyed. This time, the target of the property seizure was a Drugs cartel involved in the Drugs business, so they earned far more money than from the request itself. It is thanks to the money from requests and seizures that Anyaji, which does not engage in any particular Business, is maintained. Of course, they were running a Business similar to a PMC that dispatched Fist Technique practitioners, but they were being pushed back by heavily armed PMCs and were not achieving significant results. So, borrowing Takakusaki Gengo's reputation in the underworld, they acted as high-priced fixers, usually procuring Money. "So, a total of 1.7 billion, including the request? The remaining Money can be considered given to the local police. Their tacit approval of us is all for those crumbs. If we plunder too much, it will become an international Problem." Takakusaki Gengo waved his Hand as if to say not to Worry. As Takakusaki Gengo said, if the fact that a former Self-Defense Force special unit member committed a Massacre in another country were to spread, an international Problem would arise. To prevent that, they had to take what they could and leave. "But can we entrust Bernardo to Gwangkwon (狂拳)?" Hojo Otoji glanced at the eccentric-looking Elderly Person sitting next to Bernardo, explaining various things. Gwangkwon, or Kamishirato Manzo (上白土 万蔵), known as the Mad Fist, was a madman who traveled the world, studying various martial arts, teaching them to his disciples—whom he called 'Experiment Subjects'—and striving to create the ultimate Fist Technique. Kokubo Shiratsu, one of the Four Heavenly Kings who was killed by the special class students of St. Francis Girls' Academy, was also Kamishirato Manzo's experiment subject, and he achieved the greatest results in training practitioners of Fist Technique who used White Aura, or Baekgwanggi (白光氣), in which Takakusaki Athena took part. Recently, perhaps having realized something during the training of Baekgwanggi Fist Technique practitioners, he immediately flew to Takakusaki Gengo in the USA and offered to take charge of one of the Four Heavenly Kings. Although Gwangkwon Kamishirato Manzo called his Disciples 'Experiment Subjects,' treated them not as Disciples, and subjected them to extremely harsh Training, his Achievements were undeniable, so Takakusaki Gengo decided to entrust Bernardo to him. It was a natural Choice for Takakusaki Gengo, who didn't care about the Process as long as the Result was good. "How long will it take for both of them to become useful?" Takakusaki Gengo muttered. The two Takakusaki Gengo referred to were Kiyohira, who had just been brought in as a Four Heavenly King, and Bernardo, who was currently on the Airplane with him. "Kiyohira won the UMC Openweight class, so he won't easily lose to any Fist Technique practitioner right now. As for Bernardo, with Gwangkwon attached, his Skill will quickly improve." At Hojo Otoji's Optimistic Words, Takakusaki Gengo shook his Head as if that wasn't enough and opened his Mouth. "It's not enough. To promote their Skill improvement, there's no other Method than to make them engage in a lot of Actual combat in Japan." "Whether Swordsmen or Fist Technique practitioners, all Martial artists achieve their greatest growth through Actual combat, so that's true." Hojo Otoji nodded his Head in Consent to Takakusaki Gengo's Words. Soon, the Airplane's altitude lowered, and it arrived at an Airport in the USA. Then the Door opened, and someone boarded the Airplane. Kiyohira, the UMC Openweight Champion, boarded the Airplane with his Sabu, Musashi Takuro. "You found your way here without being late." "Taira Airlines' small passenger Airplane is easy to find. But how can Anyaji use Taira's small passenger Airplane like a private jet?" To Kiyohira's Question, Takakusaki Gengo replied simply, "It's a request." It seemed the current client was a high-ranking Person from the Taira Group. Kiyohira, hearing Takakusaki Gengo's Words, didn't understand how a mere request could allow them to use a Taira Group Airplane like a private jet, but he simply nodded his Head and sat in an empty Seat on the Airplane. Then, feeling a Gaze, he looked to his Side and saw Bernardo staring at Kiyohira with Eyes burning with a challenging Spirit. Rather than Kiyohira, Musashi Takuro was looking at the scene with Displeasure. Musashi Takuro, a somewhat orthodox Martial artist, and Gwangkwon Kamishirato Manzo, a master of various dark arts and unorthodox Methods, did not get along. Seeing Kamishirato Manzo sitting right next to Bernardo, he realized that Gwangkwon's ridiculous 'Experiment Subject' game had begun again. "That guy staring at you will pick a Quarrel at any Time." "It seems like it's now." At Musashi Takuro's Words, Kiyohira stood up from his Seat and muttered as he watched Bernardo approach him. Seeing him approach without hesitation, he seemed very confident. "Hey, you're the UMC Openweight Champion, right? I'm Bernardo." "Did you come to introduce yourself? You must know my name, so there's no need for me to introduce myself." "An introduction, yes, but... let's fight." Indeed, it seemed to be a Quarrel, just as Musashi Takuro had said. "Sparring is always welcome, but this is an Airplane, so let's fight in Japan..." Kiyohira didn't want to cause a commotion on the Airplane, so he tried to postpone the sparring. But he was surprised by Bernardo's incoming Fist and had to tilt his Head back to dodge. "W-what the hell are you doing?!" "Sparring, you say...? I don't know anything about fighting safely inside a ring like that, so let's fight right now." Because Bernardo had fought gangs in the Brazilian favelas with his Maenmom, he possessed a considerably rough nature. This Openweight Champion guy seemed to be a Four Heavenly King like himself, and he wanted to see his Skill. Kiyohira, having been subjected to a preemptive Attack (though he dodged it), seemed unable to tolerate it and stood up from his Seat. Both glared at each other and took up a Combat Posture. Kiyohira's basic Posture was to raise his Fists in front of his Face, while Bernardo's was a Capoeira stance, bending his Body and moving with rhythm, taking Steps. "Stop." It was Hojo Otoji who stopped the fight that was about to begin between the two. "If it were our Airplane, I'd tell you to fight all you want, but it's not our Airplane." As Hojo Otoji was responsible for Anyaji's finances, he couldn't allow Kiyohira and Bernardo to cause a disturbance onboard. If they just waited one more day, they could fight in a spacious martial arts dojo, so there was no need to cause a ruckus here, breaking various things and scattering Money for repair costs. "Even breaking one Chair costs several million yen. Your Bodies might be itching, but bear with it a little. I'll let you fight as much as you want." At Hojo Otoji's Words, both sat down in their Seats. After a brief stop for inspection and refueling, the Airplane took off again. The Airplane began to fly from the USA towards Japan, the hometown of everyone except Bernardo.</p>
