<p>Shimokawabe Airen, the Teacher in charge of Home Economics at St. Francis Girls' Academy, unusually found her busiest period of the year not During the Semester, but in the second week of February, after all semesters had ended.</p>
<p>It was precisely because of Valentine's Day that all the Students in the grades she was in charge of came to the Home Economics Lab.</p>
<p>Shimokawabe Airen, seeing over a hundred pairs of Eyes looking at her, let out a slight Sigh.</p>
<p>Until this year, because they were making chocolate for Family members like their Father, Uncle, or Maternal Uncle, they were Students with little enthusiasm, but this year, something was different. It was as if they were filled with fervor. Their Gaze was incredibly clear, and they were looking at Shimokawabe Airen with Eyes full of Expectation.</p>
<p>'It's probably because of Yamashiro-sensei....'</p>
<p>In Comics, I had seen Female Students treated as Idols at an academy, but the Idol of St. Francis Girls' Academy was none other than Taira Ryuta, the Disciple of her friend Fujigasaki Rinka.</p>
<p>He was tall, had an appearance as handsome as an Actor, and because he didn't often appear from the special class, he even had a mysterious Charm/Allure.... Perhaps the Home Economics Labs of other Home Ec Teachers not in charge of the 3rd grade would also be quite busy with the same passion from Students as now.</p>
<p>Even the younger Students from other grades liked Taira Ryuta, as she had heard in the Cheongsim Hall, where she served as an advisor. I heard he even had two Fan Clubs.</p>
<p>"By the time you're in 3rd grade, there's nothing left for me to teach, is there?"</p>
<p>Shimokawabe Airen, looking at the Students, asked with a Smile. From 1st year of middle school to 3rd year of high school, they had been making chocolate for several days every Valentine's Day season, so the graduating Female Students of St. Francis Girls' Academy could almost be called chocolate pâtissiers without exaggeration.</p>
<p>"Me, please look at me~!"</p>
<p>"I forgot!"</p>
<p>"This year, we have to make it perfect, so please help us, Teacher-nim~!"</p>

So the Students, like 1st-year middle schoolers, asked Shimokawabe Airen for help, raising their Hands.</p>
<p>"I'll look at each of you one by one, so let's start slowly. Anyway, the 14th is Valentine's Day, so today, you can just make them for Practice, right?"</p>
<p>Since there were about Five days left until Valentine's Day, there was no sense of urgency.</p>
<p>"The ones I Practice until Thursday, I'm going to give to dad~!"</p>
<p>At the Words of one of the St. Francis Girls' Academy Students, everyone seemed to agree, and sounds of them saying "That's right, that's right" were heard. Shimokawabe Airen felt pity for the Students' Fathers who would be delighted to receive Practice chocolates as a gift, but she couldn't scold youths for whom love was the top priority.</p>
<p>"Then, shall we start today by Reviewing first? First, cocoa butter and...."</p>
<p>Everything Shimokawabe Airen explained (except for Urabe Konoka, who transferred in the middle of the semester) was already known, but to make perfect chocolate, as quiet as a dead Mouse, they concentrated on Shimokawabe Airen's Words.</p>

<p>Now that there's exactly one week left, the outcome will be decided next week. Although there were many Enemies besides Anyaji, Anyaji was currently the closest Threat to me, so dealing with this could be said to have significant meaning.</p>

<p>Seiwa Rieko (勢和 梨絵子). She dropped out of St. Francis Girls' Academy.</p>
<p>Although a bit timid, Seiwa Rieko, who was a bright girl, began to become unhappy precisely when several Accidents overlapped and she went to the special class, a class for Troublemakers or physically weak Students at St. Francis Girls' Academy.</p>
<p>If it had been another prestigious Family, she might not have gone to the special class. But the Seiwa Family was a small Family barely clinging to the prestigious Families.</p>
<p>Although they sent Seiwa Rieko to St. Francis Girls' Academy for the Family's reputation and connections, her Parents had to make many Sacrifices to allow Seiwa Rieko to attend St. Francis Girls' Academy. Therefore, regarding Seiwa Rieko going to the special class at St. Francis Girls' Academy, they could not strongly Protest. Even St. Francis Girls' Academy, without overlooking Seiwa Rieko's minor Mistakes of occasionally defaulting on tuition, immediately sent her to the special class.</p>
<p>Knowing these facts, Seiwa Rieko endured the inappropriate behavior of her class teacher, Nomata Shigemichi, and attended the academy.</p>
<p>Nomata Shigemichi also had intelligence, so he dared not think of violating a Daughter of a prestigious Family, and only subtly wrapped his Arm around her Waist or brushed her Buttocks, but that day was different.</p>
<p>Having drunk Alcohol, with a heavily flushed Face, he lunged at Seiwa Rieko.</p>
<p>The terrible Alcohol Smell emanating from Nomata Shigemichi's Mouth, his forcefully grabbing Hand, and his bloodshot pupils were bound to be deeply etched into Seiwa Rieko's Brain.</p>
<p>By a stroke of Fortunate, Minamoto Lisa, who had left her Bag behind, opened the Classroom door, so the incident of her Being violated by Nomata Shigemichi did not occur, but Seiwa Rieko received a great wound to her Mind.</p>
<p>Before the Situation could be Handled, because of the scoop broken by Shido Tsubaki, the Club President of the Newspaper Department, Seiwa Rieko felt like she was receiving everyone's invisible Pointing Finger.</p>
<p>Because she had acted in a way unbecoming of a Daughter of a prestigious Family, she felt she was implicitly being pointed at, as if to say she deserved what happened.</p>
<p>Of course, thanks to that, Nomata Shigemichi left St. Francis Girls' Academy sooner, and her Parents received Reward from St. Francis Girls' Academy, but Seiwa Rieko could no longer attend the academy.</p>
<p>Seiwa Rieko, who had dropped out, felt rather relieved. She could avoid the Gaze of her Friends who seemed to blame her, and she didn't have to go to the special class Classroom, which held only dirty Memories.</p>
<p>Furthermore, Seiwa Rieko's Parents no longer had to Work so hard. They didn't have to pay St. Francis Girls' Academy's tuition, and had even received a considerable Reward from this Incident.</p>
<p>Although she decided to drop out, St. Francis Girls' Academy had hired Nomata Shigemichi, and it was a big Mistake not to properly Surveillance Nomata Shigemichi's actions, so they told Seiwa Rieko that if she only took online Lectures, her Graduation from St. Francis Girls' Academy would be recognized.</p>
<p>So Seiwa Rieko stayed at Home and spent her Time listening to online Lectures. And that Time wasn't bad. Without the bullying Class Teacher and the surrounding Gazes, she felt lighter.</p>
<p>While staying at Home, Seiwa Rieko, who was recovering from her mental wound, was shocked again precisely when she went to a Convenience Store and saw Saionji Diana, who had been a friend from the special class in the past.</p>
<p>Saionji Diana, who had lost her Limbs in a car Accident and both her Parents, and whom she thought was more unhappy than herself, was walking perfectly fine, and she saw her laughing and talking with her Friends.</p>
<p>'How can Saionji be so Happy? She should definitely be having a harder Time than me....'</p>
<p>Seiwa Rieko, looking at Saionji Diana, who seemed like an Ordinary high school girl, began to have thoughts darker and deeper than Jealousy, which she would never have had in an ordinary Situation.</p>
<p>Seiwa Rieko returned Home and contacted the internet and her remaining Friends to find out what had happened.</p>
<p>Through her Friends' Stories and by browsing the St. Francis Girls' Academy club bulletin board or the chat rooms where Students gathered, she was able to understand what had happened.</p>
<p>It was precisely because of the special class Class Teacher.</p>
<p>Not Nomata Shigemichi, who had tormented her, but thanks to a Teacher named Yamashiro Ryuta, who had come in as Nomata Shigemichi's successor, she heard that the special class had changed. A perfect Teacher who was handsome and had a good Personality, rumors of a true Teacher who couldn't stand by while Students struggled at School, along with handsome pictures of Yamashiro Ryuta's Face, were flooding the internet. Unlike the ugly Nomata Shigemichi, he was an incredibly handsome man.</p>
<p>Seiwa Rieko, who read that content, was not Happy. On the contrary, more than when she decided to drop out, she felt even more miserable.</p>
<p>'Why do such things happen to me, and you all are Happy...? Why should only I be unhappy? Am I not worthy of Happiness?'</p>
<p>Seiwa Rieko knew that what she was thinking was Delusion of persecution. But even knowing that, she couldn't shake off the thought of being unhappy.</p>
<p>Seiwa Rieko's Delusion of persecution grew larger and larger. After Winter passed, and Spring, preceding Graduation from St. Francis Girls' Academy, approached, a Letter arrived.</p>
<p>The sender of the Letter was Shido Tsubaki, the Club President of the Newspaper Department, who had provided the Cause for Seiwa Rieko's dropout.</p>
