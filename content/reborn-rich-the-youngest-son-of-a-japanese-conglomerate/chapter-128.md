<p>We cross the collapsed torii gate and head towards the shrine. The outside of the shrine was eerily quiet. There was no sound of leaves rustling in the wind, no chirping of birds, not even the sound of insects. Feeling a subtle sense of unease, we muffled our footsteps and circled the outside of the shrine. Going through the main gate would likely get us caught by the fanatics, so we decided to look for a back door.</p>
<p>Fortunately, after looking around, there was a door at the back of the shrine. The shrine had the unique smell of a wooden mansion. The first floor was quiet. But with my master-level senses, I focused and felt presences on the second floor and in the basement. It sounded like the cult members were having some kind of gathering on the second floor, judging by the mumbling. The timing was good. If they hadn't been having a gathering, we would have had to start fighting right away.</p>
<p>,There's probably a way to the basement around here.</p>
<p>,Basement? Sounds a bit scary.</p>
<p>Kazuhiro said, looking slightly disgusted at my words. Kiyohira also looked tense. If we went into the basement and the door to the first floor was blocked, we would be trapped, so it was dangerous for all three of us to go.</p>
<p>,One of us needs to guard the door. Who wants to do it?</p>
<p>,I'll guard it. If Sumikazu-ssi got caught guarding, he'd get beaten to a pulp.</p>
<p>It was dangerous to have only one person guarding, but if there was a commotion on the first floor, I could quickly join them, so Kiyohira decided to guard the door to the basement, and Kazuhiro and I decided to go down into the basement.</p>
<p>We quickly found the way down to the basement and started going down. There was a button to turn the lights on and off near the entrance, so it wasn't dark. The basement wasn't like some kind of dungeon we were imagining. However, there were definitely several people lying in rooms that felt like wooden prisons.</p>
<p>,Hyung-nim, it really looks like they've been kidnapped.</p>
<p>Kazuhiro, who had been taking pictures ever since we entered the shrine, said quietly. Once we were completely down, there were a total of six people lying down. They were all sprawled out haphazardly with empty, hopeless eyes.</p>
<p>,A-Aren't you wearing masks...? Could it be that you've come to rescue us?</p>
<p>One of them, who had been lying down, saw us and spoke. It seemed that the followers were wearing masks even inside the shrine. The man who was lying down staggered to his feet. And then he began to wail.</p>
<p>,Please, get us out of here!! Please!</p>
<p>,Shhh! Be quiet! They'll hear you upstairs and come down!</p>
<p>Kazuhiro said, putting his index finger to his lips, and the man finally quieted down. The others also tried to say something, but stopped themselves at Kazuhiro's action. And they whispered, "I'm sorry, please don't leave us."</p>
<p>The six people trapped inside the wooden bars were three men and three women. The men seemed to be two middle-aged and one our age, and the women seemed to be in their late teens to early twenties.</p>
<p>,Were you all kidnapped in the Jukai Forest?</p>
<p>,I, I was kidnapped outside the forest, I was kidnapped on the outskirts of Kofu City.</p>
<p>,I was kidnapped by men in Gotemba City....</p>
<p>Looking at the people, I suddenly became curious and asked the kidnapped people, and two of them said they had been kidnapped outside the Jukai Forest. The man who had begged us to rescue him had been kidnapped in Gotemba City, and the youngest-looking woman had been kidnapped in Kofu City. She looked like a high school student, her parents must be very worried.</p>
<p>Both Kofu City and Gotemba City were not that far from the Jukai Forest. This meant that the Shirashira cult had some influence outside of Jukai.</p>
<p>,I'll let you out now. Be quiet.</p>
<p>,Thank you, thank you...!</p>
<p>,I'll never think about suicide again...!</p>
<p>Of course, there was no way they would have keys, and the wood was quite thick. But I didn't want to show these people the appearance of handling the Shinkai Yatsude or the Abyss, so I kicked the hinges, the weakest part of the prison, a few times, and the hinges couldn't withstand the impact and fell off. Then the prison door tilted and fell to the floor with a thud.</p>
<p>Since there were two people in each of the three prisons, it took quite a long time to break all the hinges. We were about to take everyone out of the prison and go up when we heard a commotion on the first floor, where Kiyohira was guarding.</p>
<p>It seemed that the gathering on the second floor had ended and Kiyohira had been caught blocking the door to the basement. I went up to the first floor first.</p>
<p>,Kazuhiro, I'll take care of this quickly, so close the door and wait!</p>
<p>I said to Kazuhiro, closed the door, and turned around to see masked men, about thirty of them, drawing their swords and surrounding Kiyohira.</p>
<p>,Kiyohira, you stand behind me too.</p>
<p>,What are you talking about? I'm from UMC....</p>
<p>Even if Kiyohira was a UMC fighter, he had a flesh and blood body that would bleed if he was hit by a sword. So it was easier for me to deal with them if he just stepped back.</p>
<p>,Have you ever killed anyone?</p>
<p>,Wh, what are you talking about?</p>
<p>,Those guys are standing there with swords, ready to kill us. And I'm going to kill those guys too. Watch.</p>
<p>I took a step forward.</p>
<p>,Shirashira-nim said there would be an unpleasant visit today.... So you really came!</p>
<p>One of the masked men said. They knew we were coming? At this point, was there really a person with precognitive abilities on the level of Sawayanagi Azuma in this shrine? If not....</p>
<p>,For Shirashira-nim! I will eliminate these unpleasant intruders for Shirashira-nim!</p>
<p>,Aaaah!! Shirashira-nim!!</p>
<p>,Kkyaaaa! Dedicate your hearts!!</p>
<p>They all screamed and rushed at me with their swords raised above their heads. Their stances looked very clumsy, but all of their clumsy actions were filled with the intent to kill us. I could feel Kiyohira tensing up behind me from that murderous intent.</p>
<p>But I was the one who had fought against PMC soldiers with guns just a few days ago. There was no way I could be afraid of mere sword-wielding cultists.</p>
<p>I focused my mind and used telekinesis to trip the fanatic who was running in the front. Naturally, the fanatics who were following behind tripped over him, and my front became a scene of chaos. Since they all had swords, they ended up cutting and stabbing each other as they fell. I even used telekinesis to force the guys who were trying to jump over them to fall.</p>
<p>Kiyohira behind me probably didn't notice, but I didn't miss the opportunity and subtly adjusted my telekinesis to make slight cuts into deep cuts, and situations where they were stabbed in non-vital areas into situations where they were stabbed in vital areas and died instantly.</p>
<p>In an instant, our one-meter radius became a living hell, filled with screams of pain and splattering blood. I leisurely used telekinesis to block every drop of blood that tried to splatter on me.</p>
<p>After waiting for a while, I slowly approached and kicked the face of a fanatic who was still alive by some stroke of luck. Still, since Kiyohira was my friend, I shouldn't show him the scene of me killing them directly. The fanatics whose faces were kicked by my foot each fainted in one blow.</p>
<p>,What the hell is going on here?</p>
<p>,They tripped over each other, we're lucky.</p>
<p>,Really?</p>
<p>Kiyohira tilted his head as if something was strange, but there was no way he would ever think that I had telekinesis. He would just think that this was just good luck.</p>
<p>I left the confused Kiyohira alone and opened the door to the basement. Then I saw Kazuhiro's face, who was waiting anxiously. Behind him, six pairs of eyes were all staring at my face.</p>
<p>,What happened?</p>
<p>,One guy fell, and then everyone fell and struggled, stabbing each other with their swords and self-destructing.</p>
<p>,What?</p>
<p>,Let's get out of here. Let's get these people out of here quickly.</p>
<p>Kazuhiro, who was about to come out at my words, was startled by the pile of corpses, but soon regained his composure and let the people out. Rather, the people who came out were not surprised by the corpses, but cursed that they had received divine punishment.</p>
<p>,I've been here the longest, and these guys have killed at least ten people...!</p>
<p>,Ptooey! You trashy bastards!! Good riddance!</p>
<p>Kiyohira's face hardened as he looked at the kidnapped people who were cursing. What's wrong?</p>
<p>,She's not here.... The woman who was captured today isn't here.</p>
<p>Kiyohira definitely saw her being captured, but where did that woman go?</p>
<p>,Ah, if that's the case, there's a high probability that she went straight to the second floor. My friend was also kidnapped here with me, but she went straight to the second floor.... And she never came down again, sob...!</p>
<p>A woman who was following Kazuhiro out after hearing our words told us to go to the second floor. I don't know exactly, but it seemed that there was some condition, whether to keep them alive or kill them immediately.</p>
<p>,I'll go to the second floor, so Kiyohira and Kazuhiro, please send these people out of the shrine.</p>
<p>,.... Let's talk later. I'll do as you say for now.</p>
<p>Kiyohira stared at my eyes for a long time, and then nodded, saying he understood, because he didn't feel any fear or sacrifice in my eyes. It seemed that I would have to tell Kiyohira and Kazuhiro the truth about my superpowers after this was over.</p>
<p>While Kazuhiro led the way and Kiyohira guarded the rear, the kidnapping victims went out the exit, and I went up to the second floor alone.</p>
