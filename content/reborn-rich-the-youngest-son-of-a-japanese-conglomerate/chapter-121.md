<p>Pishut!</p>
<p>Snipers started firing from the 2nd and 3rd floors. Luckily, it seemed their aim wasn't perfect due to the flames, and the bullets hit the ground a foot in front of where we were standing.</p>
<p>"Snipers! Take cover behind the car!"</p>

<p>Luckily, it seemed some gasoline went through an open window on the 3rd floor. When Kiyona Mari flicked her hand, the 3rd floor started to catch fire, and only the windows on the 2nd floor caught fire.</p>
<p>Judging by the screams of, 'Euaaak,' it seemed that one unlucky person had caught fire.</p>
<p>"There they are! There aren't many enemies, kill them all!"</p>

<p>"Hata-ssi, could you take Mari-yang over there?"</p>
<p>"Understood."</p>
<p>The place Sawayanagi Sinya pointed to was a container warehouse about 50 meters away. I snatched Kiyona Mari, who was firing a gasoline gun at the guards' path, and ran towards the place Sawayanagi Sinya pointed to. Sawayanagi Sinya also packed his things and ran beside me.</p>
<p>Tang, tang, tang, tang!!!</p>

<p>"When did you install the bombs?"</p>
<p>"Three years ago...!"</p>
<p>Kiyona Mari, surprised by the roar and vibration of the explosion, asked, and Sawayanagi Sinya answered.</p>
<p>Amazing. Did he plant bombs three years ago, thinking that people here might kidnap him?</p>
<p>But the situation didn't end with the bomb. Only about thirty of the fifty people were caught in the explosion, leaving about twenty people outside, and a considerable number of guards were still putting out the fire inside the building.</p>
<p>Just as the surviving guards were regaining their senses and trying to rush at us, Sawayanagi Sinya was already preparing another bomb. <p>"There you are!"</p>

<p>Flames danced around and sprayed fire in all directions, but we had Kiyona Mari. As a pyrokinesist who controlled fire, she was able to concentrate the randomly spewing flames on the guards.</p>
<p>Is this what they call a coordinated attack? Since we've been working together for a long time, it seemed like we knew what to do even in this sudden operation.</p>
<p>"Kuaaak!"</p>
<p>The remaining twenty people were also on fire all over their bodies, rolling on the ground to put out the fire, so we could easily shoot the parts not protected by the tech armor to incapacitate them.</p>
<p>"Let's go back to the entrance for now. If we keep staying at the entrance, the fifty people remaining in the building won't be able to go underground for support."</p>
<p>"Okay, let's go quickly!"</p>
<p>"I'll ask about the situation over there while we go."</p>

<p>◆Gloves Woven with Abyss Thread "Epic Equipment": Gloves woven with the hair of the Daughter of the Abyss. Gives a 150 percent bonus when using Abyss-type techniques and increases the probability of special effects occurring.</p>
