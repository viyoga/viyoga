<p>Students who entered the middle school division could potentially continue all the way to St. Francisco Women's University, a university run by the Daigo Family. Of course, Students with excellent grades would go on to prestigious universities like Edo University, but perhaps because it gathered Female Students from the Upper class and operated on an escalator system, most Upper class Female Students were said to attend St. Francisco Girls' Academy.</p>
<p>"You said it's a closed school, isolated from the outside?" Yes, unlike St. Francisco Women's University in Tokyo, St. Francisco Girls' Academy is located in Miyashi, west of Mount Fuji. Ah, Miyashi. I heard it was a recently established City with a short History, but it was called a Villa City because of the many Villas owned by the Japanese Upper class. That's why I heard the Security was incredibly strict.</p>
<p>It was a City where Outsiders might be stopped and questioned by the police three or four times a day, and it might be even more so because of St. Francisco Girls' Academy. After all, the daughters of the Upper class would be tempting Prey for kidnappers.</p>
