<p>'It's stuffy....'</p>
<p>Alik fidgeted with the fencing helmet he was wearing. The environment was different from when he usually wielded his sword. There was the fencing helmet, and in his hand, he held a thin, light foil instead of the katana he favored. Alik turned the wrist holding the foil this way and that. It felt a bit stiff because of the gloves he wasn't used to wearing.</p>
<p>'But.... I can do it.'</p>
<p>The reason he volunteered for this match was because he had confidence. Even if the shape of the sword or the equipment changed, the essence of swordsmanship remained the same.</p>
<p>-Make my sword reach, and don't let the opponent's sword reach.</p>
<p>This was the essence of the sword that he learned from his master, Shimokawabe Airen-nim. He learned that if he didn't forget this essential part, he could win in any battle.</p>
<p>Alik awkwardly held the foil. And then he looked at his opponent. A skillful stance. The match hadn't even started yet, but he had the momentum to stab Alik right away. It seemed he was quite determined since the victory or defeat would be decided by this match.</p>
<p>"Salut, En Garde! Etês vous Prêts, Allez!"</p>
<p>He didn't know what it meant, but Alik knew that these words from the referee signaled the start of the match. Since Alik had a talent for languages, he was curious about the meaning of each word, but he knew that now was not the time to think about that.</p>
<p>Shuuk~!</p>
<p>The thin tip of the sword aimed for his abdomen and stabbed. In that instant, Alik thought, Fast! But when he controlled the electric current in his body and used neural acceleration, the world slowed down. Therefore, the foil being shot at Alik was also approaching slowly.</p>
<p>Alik blocked the tip of his opponent's sword with the guard of the foil, then flicked the sword as if bouncing it off. It touched. If he did it too hard, a tragedy would occur where he pierced through the fencing uniform and his opponent's body, so it was a strike with considerable power control.</p>
<p>-Bbeeeeeeeee...!</p>
<p>In the slowed-down world, the signal sound acknowledging Alik's score stretched out and was heard. Before that signal sound even ended, Alik swung his sword once again.</p>
<p>It was a movement like cutting, but the tip of the sword moved forward as if stabbing, like a spring being released. Once again, a score. The opponent gritted his teeth and stabbed his sword as if not wanting to lose, but it only looked slow to Alik. Deflect and stab. Deflect and swing. Stab before the opponent even lifts his sword.</p>
<p>At first, it was new, but it quickly became boring. This was because the difference in skill between him and his opponent was too great. But it was already over. By the time Alik had become bored, the score was already pointing to 14 points.</p>
<p>-If you win too overwhelmingly, they might look at you strangely, so give them about 1 point!</p>
<p>Otagi Yuka's words came to mind. Alik puffed up his cheeks inside the fencing helmet. He didn't want to give them 1 point. Because he wouldn't be able to return to Taira Ryuta as quickly as the time it took to give them 1 point.</p>
<p>'Alik is weird anyway.... It'll be okay.'</p>
<p>Alik decided to ignore Otagi Yuka's words. The moment Alik decided to ignore Otagi Yuka's words, Alik's nervous system accelerated like it was burning, and the foil was touching his opponent's chest.</p>
<p>-Bbee!</p>
<p>The world sped up again. Now that the match was over, Alik would be able to return to Alik's 'Ryuta'.</p>
<p>###</p>
<p>"Hoo...! That kid."</p>
<p>"He's got great skills."</p>
<p>Taira Danjo and Minamoto Ushin watched Alik's match and revealed their curiosity. A swordsman with movements like rays of light, with a speed so fast that the signal sound announcing the score couldn't keep up. In this era where firearms were rampant, he was a rare talent. Although firearms were the main thing, swordsmen could also be used effectively depending on how they were used.</p>
<p>"Egawa Alexandra.... The adopted daughter that the Egawa family recently took in."</p>
<p>"Hmph! Has his collecting urge flared up again!"</p>
<p>Minamoto Ushin sneered at Taira Danjo's words. Unlike the Minamoto, which was a traditionally prestigious family, the current Taira could be said to be a rising family. Therefore, compared to the Minamoto, who meticulously recruited talent because they could use the children of prestigious families as manpower, the Taira family had no qualms about accepting outside manpower.</p>
<p>Now that the Minamoto and Taira were on equal footing, the Taira also had the conditions to accept talent from prestigious families, so the talent pool became similar, but in the early post-World War II period when the Taira Group was emerging, they recruited numerous hidden oddballs.</p>
<p>That spirit of the Taira was still present today, and Taira Danjo enjoyed recruiting talent to the point where it was called a collecting urge.</p>
<p>Taira Danjo's intuition was telling him. That young blonde kid is an outstanding talent. He must bring her into the Taira's fold.</p>
<p>"Do you have business with my daughter?"</p>
<p>A graceful woman wearing a Kimono, Alik's adoptive mother Egawa Shiho, approached the place where Taira Danjo and Minamoto Ushin were.</p>
<p>"Oh, it's Egawa-ssi. Your adopted daughter...."</p>
<p>"I want to talk about that too."</p>
<p>When Taira Danjo spoke to Egawa Shiho with a delighted expression, Minamoto Ushin, who had been pretending not to be interested until now, jumped in.</p>
<p>"Didn't you just say that my collecting urge had flared up?"</p>
<p>"I have a desire to prevent Taira Danjo from satisfying his collecting urge."</p>
<p>"Crazy old man."</p>
<p>"I'm thinking that too, so are we self-hating?"</p>
<p>If ordinary people were to witness two giants of the Japanese business world childishly arguing in front of them, they would be flustered, but Egawa Shiho was an educator who had trained numerous brides from prestigious Japanese families. She didn't get flustered easily. She only wore a faint smile as she looked at the two old men.</p>
<p>"I'm grateful that you think so highly of my daughter, but she already has someone?"</p>
<p>"Is that so? That's a shame."</p>
<p>Taira Danjo clicked his tongue at Egawa Shiho's words.</p>
<p>"Why are you disappointed? If it's a child or grandchild's talent, it will eventually be in the Taira's fold anyway."</p>
<p>"Ah, that's right. Kanna, was it.... Our Lisa should have been the student council Chairman, too. That's a shame."</p>
<p>Minamoto Ushin said with regret at Egawa Shiho's words. He knew through the information team that Taira Kanna was taking away the talent from St. Francisco Girls' Academy while serving as the student council Chairman. They weren't talents that could be used right away since they were still high school students, but Minamoto Ushin was a little afraid of Taira Kanna's future.</p>
<p>Minamoto Ushin misunderstood, but Taira Danjo understood the meaning of Egawa Shiho's words. A child or 'grandchild'.... Surely that kid must be Taira Ryuta's talent.</p>
<p>Egawa Shiho probably knew that Taira Ryuta was doing something at this school. So she deliberately told the story so that Minamoto Ushin would misunderstand that Alik was under Taira Kanna. If Egawa Shiho hadn't said anything, while investigating Alik, he would have definitely reached the truth that Yamashiro Ryuta was Taira Ryuta.</p>
<p>'I owe her one.'</p>
<p>Taira Danjo wore a faint smile. There would be a chance to repay this debt someday.</p>
<p>"Wh-wh-wh.... What is that!"</p>
<p>While Taira Danjo was smiling, Minamoto Ushin, who was looking at the sports field, shouted. Taira Danjo, who was curious because Minamoto Ushin was making a fuss, also glanced at the sports field. There, he could see the teachers and students standing for the next event, the three-legged race.</p>
<p>Among them, his grandson Taira Ryuta was standing with his arm around Minamoto Ushin's daughter Minamoto Lisa's shoulder. Seeing that sight, Taira Danjo laughed happily.</p>
<p>###</p>
<p>-Three-legged race contestants, please come forward.</p>
<p>The fencing match, which Alik won with his outstanding skills, was over, and I was stroking Alik's head as he jumped around, celebrating his victory, when the broadcast soon came on. The three-legged race was a game where one teacher and one student from each class competed in a race with a feeling of harmony? I stood with Minamoto Lisa at the starting line with our names on it. As I stood at the starting line, a staff member came and handed us a rope to tie our feet together. It was a thick rope made of a fairly soft material, probably to prevent the students' ankles from getting chafed.</p>
<p>Since the homeroom teachers of each class were a mix of men and women, the classes with male teachers were starting about 5 meters behind, taking into account their physical condition. If you looked at it from the sky, it would look like they were standing in a zigzag W shape.</p>
<p>I knelt on one knee and tied my and Minamoto Lisa's ankles together. Thinking about it now, Minamoto Lisa had slender ankles. However, her calves weren't so thick that her slender ankles stood out. Rather, she was a moderately slim, slender type.</p>
<p>"Did I tie it too tight?"</p>
<p>"No, it's just right."</p>
<p>She said it was okay, so I got up from my seat. I grab Minamoto Lisa's shoulder as we've practiced so far. It would be more comfortable to grab her waist, but I can't wrap my arms around her waist while the parents are watching.</p>
<p>Suddenly, there seemed to be a bit of a commotion in the area where the parents were gathered near the headquarters. When I glanced over, I saw a bald old man causing a ruckus, and it was none other than Minamoto Ushin. Next to him, Grandpa Taira Danjo was chuckling as he watched.</p>
<p>"Oh, man...."</p>
<p>Minamoto Lisa's face turned red, probably because she was embarrassed by her father's antics.</p>
<p>"Just a moment...."</p>
<p>Minamoto Lisa untied the rope tied to her feet and walked towards the parents' section. She probably couldn't stand watching her father's disgrace any longer. If that father was a giant that no one in Japan could stop, then even his daughter would have to step in and stop him.</p>
<p>The headquarters also seemed to know this, as they didn't stop Minamoto Lisa from leaving the stadium and going to the parents' section.</p>
<p>I could hear them talking, but it was noisy and far away, so I couldn't hear them well. After talking for a while, Minamoto Lisa sighed and came towards me.</p>
<p>"What's got him in a bad mood?"</p>
<p>"He doesn't like that you're holding my shoulder."</p>
<p>I didn't even think about that. He was getting angry about that? What a daughter-fool, Minamoto Ushin.</p>
<p>"It's because I was afraid you'd fall...."</p>
<p>"I told him that. He said that after practicing without holding my shoulder, I fell a few times, and then he finally sat down."</p>
<p>Fortunately, it seemed like Minamoto Lisa's excuse was accepted. If Minamoto Ushin had caused more of a ruckus here, it would have been difficult to proceed with the three-legged race.</p>
<p>"But...."</p>
<p>"Yeah?"</p>
<p>It seemed like Minamoto Lisa hadn't finished speaking yet. She was hesitating a bit, was something bothering her?</p>
<p>"He said he'll fire the teacher if we don't get first place in this three-legged race."</p>
<p>Ah, Minamoto Ushin was just as nasty of an old geezer as Taira Danjo. If it's just first place.... Huh? What?!</p>
<p>In fact, until 3 seconds ago, I was confident of getting first place. But I made eye contact with one person, and it was none other than Shimokawabe Airen-nim. Why is a teacher who isn't even a homeroom teacher standing in the three-legged race? Moreover, as a female teacher, she starts 5 meters ahead of me. At this rate, my chances of winning are decreasing.</p>
<p>"Do you really think he'll fire me if we don't get first place?"</p>
<p>"If it was just something he said, I'd let it go, but he just said that in front of numerous parents, that is, people from prestigious families, so I think my father will fire you if we don't get first place because of his reputation."</p>
<p>Damn it, it wasn't just something he said. Is he saying that if I don't produce results, he can't possibly leave the guy who dared to touch his daughter's shoulder as a teacher?</p>
<p>"...I'm sorry."</p>
<p>"It's okay. Instead, if we get first place, let's go to the internet cafe we went to before one more time. There's still a book I haven't finished reading."</p>
<p>When I made a date offer in exchange for my apology, Minamoto Lisa burst out laughing. Then, the atmosphere, which had been a bit heavy until just now, lightened up.</p>
<p>"Ahaha...! You're about to get fired, but you don't even have a sense of crisis?"</p>
<p>"We've practiced a lot. And if worst comes to worst, I'll show you some magic, so don't worry."</p>
<p>Minamoto Lisa looked at me with a puzzled expression at my words of magic, but she couldn't resolve that question because she soon heard the sound of preparation on the broadcast.</p>
