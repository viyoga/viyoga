<p>However, instead of killing everyone, I ordered them to capture some executives alive, and that was because of the armor on Jeon Yong-cheol's cyber implant. <p>It's really tough, huh? Jeon Yong-cheol definitely said it was a mithril alloy, so it must be armor made by combining mithril with several other metals. I was quite greedy for it. If we could get this, TG Tech would have more advantages in the market.</p>
