<p>As we landed on the Naruse Family's helicopter landing pad, Naruse Koyou, the Head of Household of the Naruse Family, along with Naruse Shiyo, Alik, and Shimokawabe Airen, immediately greeted us.</p>
<p>「Benefactors, welcome! Please come into the Naruse Family Home.」 Naruse Koyou greeted us politely. Naruse Shiyo, seeing us, bounced excitedly, shouting 「Teacher-nim~! Everyone~!」 and making a fuss, then, receiving Naruse Koyou's gaze, she also greeted us politely.</p>
<p>「Ryuta...!」 Alik scampered over and grabbed my sleeve. I said, 「Have you been well?」 and stroked Alik's Head. Alik, like a docile Cat, closed her Eyes and quietly accepted my strokes. Alik is indeed cute.</p>
<p>「Was your Training good?」 「Yeah, I played in the Mountain with Naruse and Moka Sabu.」 For Alik, it wasn't Training, but playing. Still, judging by Naruse Shiyo's reaction, it must have been quite intense Training, so both of them must have grown considerably.</p>
<p>「The weather is chilly, so let's go inside for now.」 We were guided by Naruse Koyou and entered the Naruse Family's Main family mansion. As expected, the Naruse Family, being a venerable Family that has existed since the Heian period, had a spacious and grand mansion.</p>
<p>「Shall we have a meal first?」 「Indeed.」 We were guided to the restaurant and seated at a luxurious dining table, and soon, Kaiseki (会席: Japanese course meal) began to be served. As expected of traditional Japanese cuisine, the delicate flavors were excellent. I wondered if they had understood my appetite all the way to the Naruse Main family home here in Kyoto, as twice the amount served to others was placed in front of me, allowing me to eat to my heart's content.</p>
<p>「Has Shiyo's Training gone well?」 Yes, now she has adapted to her suddenly increased muscle strength and agility, so the gap between her thoughts and body movements has disappeared. Her Nagamaki technique is also solidly grounded in the basics. When I asked how the Training went, Shimokawabe Airen told me that the Training had gone well.</p>
<p>Naruse Shiyo, who had become excessively strong after fusing with a Dragon, had managed to control her power in that short Training period; it seems Shimokawabe Airen's know-how from teaching many Students as an advisor at Cheongsim Hall is truly remarkable.</p>
<p>「Teacher-nim, regarding the previous Family matter and this Shin Namgyeong Incident, I am truly grateful.」 After the meal, when the tea was served, Naruse Koyou bowed his Head politely to me and conveyed his gratitude.</p>
<p>「As a Class Teacher, I merely did what was natural.」 「Hahaha, all the Students here will know that it's not natural.」 Indeed, protecting Students from Zombies is not really a Class Teacher's job. I only did it because I had the Ability to, and before the Possession, I wouldn't have been able to muster such Courage.</p>
<p>「Teacher-nim, you said you would choose two Treasures from among those our Family has collected, and what about the others?」 After drinking tea, perhaps intending to give the Reward immediately, Naruse Koyou asked, looking at the Students and Shimokawabe Airen. For reference, Shimokawabe Airen did not participate in the Naruse rescue mission, but she was to receive a gift for her involvement in the Shin Namgyeong Incident and for becoming Naruse Shiyo's Sabu.</p>
<p>「Konoe and Sakagami will receive Shikigami, and the others will receive Treasures.」 「Is that so? Understood. Then, let's go see the Shikigami first.」 To confirm the Shikigami, we moved to the backyard. Upon arriving in the backyard, a person dressed in Onmyoji robes brought a rectangular tray, and on the tray, Talismans were neatly arranged. When I looked, wondering what it was, Naruse Koyou kindly explained.</p>
<p>「These are Talismans that have sealed Yogoe.」 「Ah.」 I see. I had wondered what kind of Talismans they were, but it seemed that Yogoe destined to become Shikigami were sealed within them. When sealed, they are merely Yogoe, but only when they form a contract with an Onmyoji do they become Shikigami.</p>
<p>「Then, for what purpose will the two Agassi use their Shikigami?」 「Konoe would prefer a Yogoe that can provide continuous Security/Guard.</p>
<p>Sakagami, while also preferring a guardian-type, would like one with the Ability to create a Gap for her to escape.」 For Konoe Akina, who has no physical Body Ability whatsoever, a Shikigami that provides continuous protection is good, while Sakagami Rena, who has physical Abilities and high Evasion, as a ninja, when surrounded by Enemies during a mission, needed a Shikigami that could create a momentary Gap for her to escape.</p>
<p>「Hmm, in that case....」 Naruse Koyou, who had heard my explanation, stroked his Chin for a moment, then first picked out two Talismans from the tray.</p>
<p>「Here are Konoe-ssi's Shikigami candidates.」 As Naruse Koyou held the Talisman and muttered an Order, pang, two Yogoe appeared. One was a Yogoe in the form of an Ashigaru (足軽: lowest-ranking infantry in medieval and early modern Japan) with three eyes, holding a Taketaba (竹束: a bamboo bundle shield), a traditional Japanese deployable shield. The other was a small, girl-like Yogoe holding a large umbrella. Konoe Akina looked at me as if asking me to choose quickly, but she was, of course, subtly glancing at the cute, girl-like umbrella Yogoe.</p>
<p>She was asking me to choose, yet already appealing to me with what she wanted.</p>
<p>「The umbrella Yogoe would be good.」 It's a variant of Karakasa (からかさ), and you chose well. It can open its umbrella to defend against attacks. As I thought, the umbrella was for Defense. Konoe Akina, as if saying 「Please take care of me,」 gave the umbrella Yogoe an Eye Smile, and the umbrella Yogoe looked at Konoe Akina and smiled slightly. Next was Sakagami Rena's turn to choose. This time too, with two Talismans, two Yogoe were summoned: one was a hippopotamus-like Guy with an enormous mouth, and the other was a small, pure white weasel.</p>
<p>「Could you explain their Abilities?」 Ah, that big-mouthed guy is a Yogoe called Ooguchi (大口), a Yogoe that immediately lets out a loud noise to confuse people, and that small white weasel is a variant of Kamaitachi (鎌鼬) called Tsuchiitachi (槌鼬). Unlike Kamaitachi, which cuts people with its blade-like wind, Tsuchiitachi is a Yogoe that instantly generates a wind that pushes people away. So, it's either a stun with sound or a push with Wind.</p>
<p>In fact, Sakagami Rena had also entrusted the choice to me, but Sakagami Rena was already looking at the weasel Yogoe Tsuchiitachi with Eyes dripping honey, as if she had fallen in love. It seemed she couldn't help it, as her favorite Animal was a weasel.</p>
<p>「Let's go with that weasel Yogoe Tsuchiitachi.」 She asked me to choose, yet she made it so obvious what she wanted. She made it too obvious what she wanted, so I couldn't help it. Since Sakagami Rena was very pleased after hearing my choice, it seemed that was enough.</p>
<p>"I'll call you Ribbon-chan! Let's get along well~♥ I also have a friend named Hamtaro." Ribbon-chan, huh.... This must also be a hamster name from Hamtaro, like the weasel Hamtaro that Sakagami Rena has. Konoe Akina and Sakagami Rena, who had chosen their two Yogoe, were left in the backyard to perform the contract ritual, and the rest of us headed to the Naruse Family's warehouse. As expected of an old Family, the warehouse was also enormous, and countless Relics would be sleeping within it.</p>
<p>「This is our Family's warehouse. Due to the nature of an Onmyoji Family, there are many dangerous objects, so please do not carelessly touch the displayed items.」 Naruse Koyou issued a Warning and opened the Door. It wasn't an Intention to say 「Don't steal the items,」 but an Intention to say 「Be careful, as it's truly dangerous.」 Indeed, as an Onmyoji Family, cursed objects or items with sealed Yogoe could be hidden within.</p>
<p>As expected of a warehouse, there was a slightly musty smell, but perhaps because it was well-maintained, the smell wasn't too strong.</p>
<p>「Come on, Teacher-ssi, quickly choose something useful for us.」 「Wait. First, I need to check what kind of Treasures there are.」 I had just stepped inside the warehouse and told the impatient Otagi Yuka to wait, then checked the exhibits decorating the Entrance. As expected of an Entrance, historically important Treasures were displayed. There was nothing useful as Equipment.</p>
<p>「Oh? Otagi, that would be good for you to use.」 「How would that Help me?」 As I pointed to a gold earring shaped like a water Bottle with a Floral Pattern engraved on it, Otagi Yuka tilted her Head. Naturally, it looked like an ordinary accessory, but that earring was a perfect item for Otagi Yuka. ◆Vase Earring [Narrative Equipment/Accessory]: An accessory from the Kamakura period. An earring that can hold 1 liter of liquid. The wearer can drink from it whenever they want.</p>
<p>For an ordinary Person, it could only be used to store a mere 1 liter of water or Alcohol, but for Otagi Yuka, a Vampire, if she put Blood or my Semen in it, she could recover her vitality anytime, making it incredibly useful.</p>
<p>「This earring can hold 1 liter of liquid. Otagi, you'll be able to use it very well.」 「Oh, it had that function? That's amazing, I can really use it well?!」 Otagi Yuka, who had heard my explanation, looked at Naruse Koyou and said, 「I'll take this one~!」 Naruse Koyou, who had been listening to our conversation, made a surprised Expression, then soon opened his Mouth to me.</p>
<p>「T-Teacher-nim, do you also possess Appraisal Ability?」 「Well.... I do.」 It seemed he was very surprised that I, who also excelled in combat power, possessed Appraisal Ability as well.</p>
<p>「May I ask a Request of you? In our Naruse Family's warehouse, there are several items that have been sealed for hundreds of years. As Time passed, the records disappeared, so we don't even know why they were sealed. I will give you one more Treasure from the warehouse, so could you appraise the sealed items for us?」 He would let me choose 3 Rewards instead of the original 2 if I appraised his items? It wasn't a difficult Request. As for items, I just needed to concentrate a little and look at them.</p>
