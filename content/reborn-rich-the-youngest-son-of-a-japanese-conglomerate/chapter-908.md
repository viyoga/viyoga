<p>As the Abyss that had escaped Fujigasaki Rinka connected to Takakusaki Gengo, Takakusaki Gengo's Appearance began to change. He curled his Body like a fetus, and an ominous black membrane began to envelop Takakusaki Gengo.</p>
<p>Konoe Akina, seeing that sight, immediately realized the danger. Because Konoe Akina possessed a Mystical eye, she could see what others could not.</p>
<p>The line of Abyss that connected from Fujigasaki Rinka to Takakusaki Gengo was, in fact, a kind of umbilical cord. It seemed he was trying to transform into a new existence, transcending humanity, using Fujigasaki Rinka's Abyss as an Energy source. He might even be trying to become a god, just as Takakusaki Gengo had just shouted.</p>
<p>"Dangerous...!"</p>
<p>"This, we definitely can't just stand by and watch."</p>
<p>At Konoe Akina's murmur, Urabe Konoka, who was beside her, also nodded her Head in agreement.</p>
<p>Konoe Akina, Urabe Konoka, Kijimoto Jurie, and others who lacked direct Combat power were positioned in the rear, while Hanakame, Kujo Kina, and Nakasone Rishin were nearby to protect the rear personnel.</p>
<p>Suddenly, Konoe Akina saw the Bow Urabe Konoka was holding and the Talisman-wrapped Arrows in her quiver. If it was this...</p>
<p>"I can temporarily stop the Glistening Trail of that black Aura with my Mystical eye. Then, can you, Urabe, shoot an Arrow to sever that Glistening Trail?"</p>
<p>Konoe Akina's Mystical eye was a Basilisk's eye, capable of Petrification. Normally, it was a Mystical eye that induced Fear and Petrification in living beings, but by using the Eye-light Amplifier she received as a gift from Taira Ryuta, she could even temporarily stop the Aura of non-living opponents.</p>
<p>So, she proposed this plan, thinking that if she could temporarily stop that ominous, umbilical cord-like Glistening Trail of Abyss, Urabe Konoka could shoot an Arrow imbued with divine power to prevent this dangerous Situation.</p>
<p>"...Alright. Let's try it."</p>
<p>"I shall also lend my strength."</p>
<p>As Urabe Konoka nodded her Head, Hanakame, who was beside her, also said she would lend her strength. Perhaps because they were both Shrine Maidens serving the same god, they seemed to have some Means to add power.</p>
<p>"I'll do it now."</p>
<p>There was no time to make a detailed plan. Nor was it something that required a complex plan. Konoe Akina, wearing the Eye-light Amplifier, made her Eyes glow. The Authority of her Mystical eye activated, stopping the movement of the Glistening Trail of Abyss connecting Fujigasaki Rinka and Takakusaki Gengo, as if even time itself were petrified.</p>
<p>Not missing that Moment, Urabe Konoka, having received even Hanakame's power, drew and released her bowstring. Tung! With the sound of the bowstring snapping, the Arrow flew.</p>
<p>'This is dangerous!'</p>
<p>Watching the Glistening Trail of Abyss connecting Sabu Fujigasaki Rinka and Takakusaki Gengo, I thought. Takakusaki Gengo was curled up like a fetus, enveloped in an ominous black membrane, like an amniotic sac or a cocoon.</p>
<p>Instinctively sensing the danger, I immediately leaped up to stop that mutation. But someone blocked my way: Guardian Hall Master Numagoori Kaneshige. Numagoori Kaneshige was blocking me with an Expression of ecstasy, like a Fanatic worshipping a god.</p>
<p>At that Moment, I realized something.</p>
<p>-Numagoori, that man, is closer to an ascetic than a martial artist.</p>
<p>-Everyone, be proud of yourselves! You will witness the birth of a god!!</p>
<p>Musashi Takuro's Words evaluating Numagoori Kaneshige and Takakusaki Gengo's Words just now flashed through my Head, along with the temple carved into the Cliff, the altar, and the piles of Corpses.</p>
<p>Takakusaki Gengo was trying to become an Outer God himself, stealing Fujigasaki Rinka's Abyss with countless Offerings...! And Numagoori Kaneshige was the priest of Takakusaki Gengo, who would become a new god.</p>
<p>Numagoori Kaneshige, as if he grew stronger with Takakusaki Gengo, had somehow doubled in Size. Not only his Size, but his power and speed also seemed to have increased. Every time he moved, the Ground was deeply indented.</p>
<p>"Everyone, Attack!"</p>
<p>There was no leisure to be caught by Numagoori Kaneshige. That amniotic sac, which was growing more ominous by the second, had to be destroyed as quickly as possible. That's why I gave the Attack order to everyone.</p>
<p>"No one shall pass behind me!"</p>
<p>Numagoori Kaneshige, using a Telekinesis-like Ability that Takakusaki Gengo had just displayed, lifted the Gwonmaeng Fist Technique Masters into the empty space and threw them at us like a catapult, blocking our way. If it were a normal Attack, we would parry it, but since they were allies, everyone was flustered and wasting time catching them.</p>
<p>One person flew at me, the man who had complained about the strange jit when Urabe Konoka and Hanakame received a Blessing.</p>
<p>"Kuek!"</p>
<p>I unconsciously struck him with my hand. The Gwonmaeng Fist Technique Master, who had been slapped on the Cheek by me and tumbled to the floor, lay motionless, as if Faint.</p>
<p>Tung.</p>
<p>As Numagoori Kaneshige was blocking us with all his might, something bounced, and an Arrow passed over my Head with a Tung sound. Feeling the familiar Aura of Creation Chaos, it seemed to be an Arrow shot by Urabe Konoka.</p>
<p>The Arrow was flying towards the Glistening Trail of Abyss connecting Fujigasaki Rinka and Takakusaki Gengo, and it felt as if space-time itself had solidified there, with no flow of Abyss perceptible.</p>
<p>It seemed to be Konoe Akina's Mystical eye Ability, and I wondered if it had developed to this extent. This is because the Petrification Mystical eye's Ability is fundamentally applied to living beings. It might also be thanks to the Eye-light Amplifier I gave her as a gift.</p>
<p>"Not a single Attack can be allowed!"</p>
<p>Numagoori Kaneshige stretched out his hand towards the flying Arrow. It seemed he intended to block the Arrow with his Telekinetic Ability. Although he couldn't completely block the Arrow, as if lifting the Gwonmaeng Fist Technique Masters into the empty space, due to the Aura of Creation Chaos contained within it, Numagoori Kaneshige's desperate efforts to block it resulted in the Arrow's Path being diverted.</p>
<p>While Numagoori Kaneshige stretched out his hand to block the Arrow's movement, we didn't stand idly by either.</p>
<p>Shimokawabe Airen slashed his Shoulder, severing one of his Arms, and Alik, Naruse Shiyo, and Otagi Yuka also slashed Numagoori Kaneshige's massive Body with their Swords. Seizawa Shii, old man Yoda, and Musashi Takuro struck Numagoori Kaneshige with their fists, Sakagami Rena used stealth techniques to attack with Sorcery, and Athena Mannerheim created and threw a light-Sword-like object into the empty space.</p>
<p>"Kwahahaha! No one can stop the descent!"</p>
<p>"That's just what you think."</p>
<p>Numagoori Kaneshige, tattered by everyone's attacks, laughed madly as he looked at the diverted Arrow, but that was because he didn't know I possessed Telekinesis. I immediately stretched out my hand towards the Arrow and controlled it with Telekinesis, hitting its original Goal, the Glistening Trail of Abyss.</p>
<p>Pee-ee!</p>
<p>With an unpleasant sound, like Claws scratching a Blackboard, the Glistening Trail of Abyss connecting Fujigasaki Rinka and Takakusaki Gengo shattered. Because Konoe Akina had solidified the Glistening Trail of Abyss with her Petrification Mystical eye, it seemed to be a phenomenon like a Mirror breaking.</p>
<p>As the Glistening Trail of Abyss was severed, Takakusaki Gengo inside the amniotic sac could no longer control something, and the Abyss no longer drained from Fujigasaki Rinka.</p>
<p>That's Fortunate. I had worried that even if it was severed once, he might be able to re-absorb it, but that worry seemed to have been unfounded.</p>
<p>"Aow!"</p>
<p>Although a tremendous amount of Abyss had been stolen, Fujigasaki Rinka still seemed fine. As the White Light that had been debuffing her disappeared, she used her power to snap the tough tentacles and dropped down beside me.</p>
<p>"Thank you, Disciple. Gengo's White Light was on a different Dimension from what I experienced coming here."</p>
<p>"I think we should save our greetings for later."</p>
<p>Fujigasaki Rinka thanked me, but I murmured, looking at Takakusaki Gengo's amniotic sac, which was cracking with a zzzzt sound.</p>
<p>Bu-wook!</p>
<p>With another tearing sound, a hand burst out of the amniotic sac. It was a hand that looked as if the human skin had been torn away, leaving only the muscle fibers inside, each muscle fiber writhing like an earthworm.</p>
<p>"Kwaaaah!"</p>
<p>Just looking at that sight seemed to inflict damage, as the Gwonmaeng Fist Technique Masters screamed in painful Screams, blood flowing from their Eyes. Fortunately, our side seemed perfectly fine, perhaps due to Lizhael's Blessing.</p>
<p>Bu-wook!</p>
<p>With another tearing sound, the opposite hand burst out. I snapped back to my senses. Why was I just watching this? Although we hadn't suffered damage like the Gwonmaeng Fist Technique Masters, we had clearly been affected somehow.</p>
<p>Now was when Takakusaki Gengo was weakest. Attacking now would inflict the greatest harm on the opponent.</p>
<p>"Everyone, Attack. Just in case, from long range...!"</p>
<p>I shouted, gathering Aura in my fist. As if they heard my Words, everyone prepared to Attack. I extended my fist and released the gathered Aura.</p>
<p>-Creation Chaos Style: Grand Cannon.</p>
<p>An indigo blue Aura and a sparkling, starlight-like Aura of Creation Chaos began to fly towards Takakusaki Gengo's amniotic sac. Indeed, Shimokawabe Airen's long-range Slash attack, Alik's Lightning, Sakagami Rena's stealth techniques, and Athena Mannerheim's light-Sword also headed towards the amniotic sac along with my Grand Cannon.</p>
