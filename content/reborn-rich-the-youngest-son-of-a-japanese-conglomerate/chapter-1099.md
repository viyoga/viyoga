<p>"First of all.... I have this."</p>
<p>I first took out the mental amplifier. Most of Omnicom's researchers already had a rough idea of what it was, as I had mentioned the existence of such an item in advance to headhunt them.</p>
<p>"Does this really affect mental superpowers?" "Yes. But don't use it now. The side effects are severe."</p>
<p>Asada Ei reached out a hand as if curious about the mental amplifier, so I gently stopped her and said. As expected of a Jo Seodang executive, she seemed to have a lot of curiosity about new information.</p>
<p>"It puts a considerable strain on the brain...."</p>
<p>I explained what side effects it had, reading from the item window, so that no one else would use it. Then, everyone nodded their heads with an expression that said, 'So, more research needs to be done.'</p>
<p>"Since the research team will be going to the TG Tech Laboratory, I'll send this to Korea...."</p>
<p>Of course, since there was the researchers' data, they could create new ones based on that, but if there was such a prototype, they could conduct more experiments, so I intended to send it to Korea.</p>
<p>Next, I decided to skip over the items that had been acquired or obtained and then given as gifts by the USA.</p>
<p>Things like the bracelet that materialized space, received by Kurayamada Kirie, Sawayanagi Azuma's necklace containing time, or the phoenix egg replica received by Kiyona Mari.</p>
<p>Since they had already passed to other people, there was no longer any meaning in talking about them.</p>
<p>"Next are these."</p>
<p>I placed several items on the desk.</p>
<p>The witch's hat, chains, and two grimoires Otagi Yuka obtained after defeating Herta Vogel, who was under the Black Sage, the items of the Vampire Grand Duke who died in the battle against the Black Sage, the SPF New York Branch Chief's sword, the Manryeongjuhae received from Arisu, and a chronicle bound in Aether purchased at auction-various pieces of loot appeared before everyone.</p>
<p>Most of them were magical items, and since this event was a scramble for the Fragment of All-Knowledge, a type of magical item, many magical characters had participated and died, so the loot also seemed to be entirely from this category.</p>
<p>"First, this is an item Yuka obtained as loot...."</p>
<p>I pointed at the witch's hat, chains, and grimoires and said. First, as I concentrated my mind on the skull-decorated silver witch's hat, the item options appeared before my eyes as a translucent window.</p>
<p>◆Hat of the Witch of the Black Forest (Die Hexe des Schwarzwaldes) [Unique Armor/Hat]</p>
<p>The witch's hat, called the nightmare of those stranded in the Black Forest, is made from the silk of spiders that feed on metal, and decorated with the skulls of children lost in the forest.</p>
<p>○Only those with a Will stat of 13 or higher can wear the hat. Those with a Will stat of less than 13 become the hat's prey.</p>
<p>●The skulls chant spells together, strengthening the spells.</p>
<p>●When a spell hits, one of the debuffs [Spiderweb] or [Enchantment] is applied to the enemy.</p>
<p>●By transferring the fear of those sacrificed while making the hat, the [Fear] debuff is applied when an enemy's attack hits.</p>
<p>●When wearing the hat, the spell  can be used.</p>
<p>Oh...! The item description and the wearing restriction were quite eerie, but the options themselves seemed quite good. It was a hat that most magician-type characters could use effectively.</p>
<p>It was a very good hat with no wasted options: a spell enhancement option, a debuff applied if my spell hits, a debuff applied even if an enemy's attack hits me, useful options for both offense and defense, and even an option to use a new spell.</p>
<p>The only regrettable thing was that three out of four spell-related options were meaningless if the wearer wasn't a magician character. Well, since I could give it to a spell-using character, it wasn't a major disadvantage.</p>
<p>Our spell users are Han Yu-gyeong, Taira Yukiko, Sakagami Rena, Nagumo Sian, Maya Reed, and others, so it seemed I could give it to one of them.</p>
<p>"It's amazing!" "The options are good, but the shape is a bit...."</p>
<p>When I explained the options, most reacted with amazement, and there were also reactions of not liking the unique wide-brimmed, pointed shape of the witch's hat.</p>
<p>"Next is...."</p>
<p>It was the turn of the handcuffs with chains that Herta Vogel was said to have been wearing.</p>
<p>◆Restraints of the Witch of the Black Forest (Die Hexe des Schwarzwaldes) [Unique Accessory/Bracelet]</p>
<p>The restraints were made by the Witch of the Black Forest, Herta Vogel, from metal melted by the flames that burned the corpses of those stranded in the Black Forest. They cause extreme fear in those who see them.</p>
<p>○If worn by a person with an average mental Stat of 12 or higher, the maximum spell usage energy increases by 50 percent, and they are not affected by the options below.</p>
<p>●When worn, the [Binding] debuff is applied.</p>
<p>●When worn, the [Fear] debuff is applied.</p>
<p>●When worn, the [Slowness] debuff is applied.</p>
<p>●When worn, the [Helplessness] debuff is applied.</p>
<p>...This is a peculiar item, isn't it? It's an item laden with debuffs applied when worn, but it also has conditional options: if the average mental Stat is 12 or higher, the debuffs are not applied, and the maximum spell usage energy increases by 50 percent.</p>
<p>Spell usage energy is a general term for various energies consumed when using spells, such as sorcery power, magic power, or blood energy. An increase of as much as 50 percent in the maximum of all spell usage energy was a very good option.</p>
<p>Of course, in my opinion, Herta Vogel would likely remove the restraints she was wearing when capturing stranded individuals or experimental subjects, attach them to the opponent, and make them riddled with debuffs. All the debuffs felt like they restricted movement, so they were clearly made to prevent escape.</p>
<p>"I quite like this."</p>
<p>Asada Ei said. Jo Seodang also has the role of interrogating captured prisoners, so the Witch of the Black Forest's restraints, which inflict various debuffs, seemed to her liking. Hmm, I should consider handing it over to Jo Seodang.</p>
<p>Next were two grimoires. First, I saw a grimoire with a mouth on its cover. According to what Otagi Yuka told me, if it opened its mouth, it had many teeth and was very disgusting, so I wondered what options it truly had.</p>
<p>◆Internal Organ Praise [Legacy Magic Tool/Grimoire]</p>
<p>A grimoire recording the wicked sorcery of an unknown entity, the [Internal Organ Devourer]. The European Magicians' Parliament designated this grimoire as a forbidden book in 1743.</p>
<p>○The owner must offer human internal organs once a week.</p>
<p>●When an attack spell hits, it inflicts the  curse.</p>
<p>●Spell power increases by 25 percent.</p>
<p>●Upon thorough reading, Knowledge Stat +2, Will Stat -1.</p>
<p>●Magic , , , , .... can be acquired.</p>
<p>This is a bit.... The name "Internal Organ Praise" is strange, and it requires offering human internal organs once a week.... There's clearly a reason it was designated a forbidden book.</p>
<p>The recorded magic itself only contained grotesque things, making it seem like a grimoire difficult to use anywhere. A lot of magic is recorded, but.... What the hell is a Pancreas Wedge?</p>
<p>"This can't be used. Having experienced it myself, the magic power is strong, but it didn't seem useful."</p>
<p>Having heard my explanation, Otagi Yuka shook her head and said. It's said to shoot internal organs from that mouth; just thinking about it was disgusting.</p>
<p>"Older Brother~! If you don't need it, give it to me!"</p>
<p>Everyone gave a verdict that it was unusable, but only Tsukiyo raised her hand. If it's Tsukiyo, a half-Outer God, could she use this eerie forbidden book? Like Lizhael, she might be able to steal the grimoire's power or transform and use the grimoire.</p>
<p>"Are you sure? I don't have to get human internal organs for you once a week, do I?" "I'm Tsukiyo~! I can ignore such restrictions, so it's fine."</p>
<p>Since she was so confident it was fine, it seemed I could hand it over. As I nodded my head, Tsukiyo exclaimed, "Older Brother is the best, as expected~!" and took the Internal Organ Praise.</p>
<p>Now it was the turn of the last piece of loot obtained from Herta Vogel: a grimoire burning with purple flames. Uniquely, it was clearly on fire, but the book showed no damage at all; it looked perfectly fine.</p>
<p>◆Testament of Purple Flame (Purple Flame's Will) [Legacy Magic Tool/Grimoire]</p>
<p>A grimoire that sprang from the flames of a burning pyre one day. According to the stories passed down, it was discovered at a witch's cremation ground.</p>
<p>●When equipped, the proficiency level of curse magic increases by 3, and the power of curses increases by 15 percent.</p>
<p>●Upon thorough reading, flame resistance increases by 20 percent.</p>
<p>●If a living being cursed by the owner dies, 5 percent of the maximum spell usage energy is recovered, and spell power increases by 10 percent for 5 minutes. This effect is stackable.</p>
<p>●Magic , , , , .... can be acquired.</p>
<p>This time, it was a grimoire exclusively for curses. As it was Herta Vogel's item, it was still a wicked grimoire, but unlike the previous Internal Organ Praise, there was no wicked restriction requiring human internal organs once a week.</p>
<p>Of course, the act of cursing an opponent itself is a wicked deed. But this world now is a cyberpunk megacorp world where cursing an opponent is almost aegyo. There's not much difference between shooting an opponent or cursing them. This Testament of Purple Flame seemed like it could be used quite usefully.</p>
<p>"...."</p>
<p>But as it was a grimoire primarily focused on curses, no one rashly stepped forward to say they would use it. I'll keep it for now and give it to someone who seems to need it.</p>
