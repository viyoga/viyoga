<p>,I'm repeating the test firing we did at dawn to see where and how supernatural energy is transferred to the gun."</p>

<p>If we find out how supernatural powers are compatible with guns, we will definitely be able to create an improved version. <p>,Ah! Come to think of it, Eve is also investigating cyber implants that are compatible with supernatural powers, so it will be faster if they exchange information."</p>
<p>,That's right. It seems that the simulation will be faster. Later, Han Yu-gyeong has to confirm whether what we researched is correct."</p>

<p>,Then I'll come again next time, Lara."</p>
