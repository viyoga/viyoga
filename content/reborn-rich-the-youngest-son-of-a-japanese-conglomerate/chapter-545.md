<p>Before leaving, I called the Naruse Mansion once more. Still no answer. Next, I called Naruse Shiyo's father, and this time, he answered.</p>
<p>「Who is this?」 "Ah, this is Yamashiro Ryuta, Naruse-yang's Class Teacher. Her classmates were worried because they hadn't been able to reach Naruse-yang for several days, so I contacted you. Is Naruse-yang alright?"</p>
<p>「...I see. Shiyo is not feeling well right now.... It's a bit difficult for her to take calls.」 Is she very sick? I wondered if Urabe Konoka's and my deduction was wrong, but Naruse Shiyo's father, Naruse Koyou's, way of speaking was a little bothersome. It was a tone that subtly revealed a father's bitter heart, as if he was forcibly hiding something. It seemed certain that something had happened to Naruse Shiyo. I definitely needed to confirm it.</p>
<p>"In that case, I'd like to make a home visit. Would that be possible today?"</p>
<p>「That... would be difficult, Teacher-nim.」 "I see. Understood. I apologize for the unreasonable Request." I hung up the phone. Whether it was Naruse Koyou or the Naruse Family itself, they were clearly hiding something. After hanging up, I immediately called Daigo Terue.</p>
<p>「Yamashiro Teacher-nim, what is it?」 "Headmaster-nim, I have a Request."</p>
<p>「A Request?」 "Yes, could I know when the Daughters of the Naruse Family enrolled and graduated from St. Francis Girls' Academy until now?"</p>
<p>「What? Naruse Family students?」 From Daigo Terue's perspective, I must seem strange for making such a sudden and odd Request. But to confirm my hypothesis, I had no choice. Enrollment and graduation lists weren't secret Information, so there was no reason to hide them; she would tell me.</p>
<p>"Yes, I apologize for the trouble, but I would appreciate it."</p>
<p>「Understood. Our academy's enrollment and graduation records aren't secret Information, so I'll tell you. However, it will take a little time since I need to extract only the Naruse Family students' data.」 "Time is fine. Ah, and please also check if there are any Young Ladies from the Naruse Family among the students on next year's enrollment list."</p>
<p>Anyway, there would be time to travel to the Naruse Mansion, so she would organize and send the data during that period. After finishing the call with Daigo Terue, I first returned Home, changed my Clothes, put on my motorcycle helmet to roughly conceal my True Identity, and then rode my folding motorcycle towards the Naruse Family's Mansion. When the Naruse Family Mansion on the hill appeared small in the distance, Daigo Terue's email arrived. I briefly stopped my motorcycle and checked the email Daigo Terue had sent.</p>
<p>The email contained Daigo Terue's Words, "That's strange," along with an attached Excel Table that clearly showed the enrollment and graduation details of female students from the Naruse Family, which I had requested. The total enrollment of Naruse Family Young Ladies over 160 years was 82. Graduation was 47... The number of graduates was excessively small compared to enrollment.</p>
<p>Although there had been various events like Jeonjaeng or earthquakes over 160 years, the small number of graduates, barely more than half of the enrollment, made me think my assumption was correct. Using Naruse Shiyo as a reference, I began counting the students who had graduated, died, or disappeared in the year immediately before Naruse Shiyo's enrollment, and then those who had graduated, died, or disappeared immediately before that student's enrollment. The total was 30.</p>
<p>Most of them died or disappeared in their final year of high school, or a little earlier in their second year, just before becoming Seongin, and then a new Young Lady of the Naruse Family enrolled the very next year. The Naruse Family had female students enrolling who would die or disappear every 6 years. These were students with a very high Possibility of being a Seal or an Offering, as Urabe Konoka and I had discussed.</p>
<p>Of course, there were Young Ladies of the Naruse Family who didn't fall into this cycle, but it wasn't strange since there would be ordinary Young Ladies of the Naruse Family who weren't Offerings or Seals. The Naruse Family had been sacrificing their Daughters for hundreds of years, perhaps even longer, to protect the Family's Honor. At the end of the email Daigo Terue sent, the name of next year’s Naruse Family enrollee at St. Francis Girls’ Academy was written: Naruse Minori (成瀬 実祈)... This child would probably be the new Offering.</p>
<p>Sakagami Rena must be safe... Normally, I wouldn't worry, but the Naruse Family is an Onmyoji Family. I was quite worried because I didn't know what kind of Trick Sakagami Rena might fall victim to. The Naruse Family Mansion was quite a distance away. And since it was built on a small hill with nothing around it, making it easy to spot anyone approaching, it was tricky to get close, but since my Face was covered, I decided to approach head-on. With Regeneration and Telekinetic Defense, there wouldn't be many who could Harm me.</p>
<p>I rode my folding motorcycle straight down the road connected to the Naruse Family's main gate.</p>
<p>「Stop! Stop! The road you are currently on is private property of the Naruse Family. Any further Entry will be considered Invasion! Stop!」 Someone shouted at me, but I didn't care. As I continued to ride without even pretending to hear the broadcast, the Security Guards tried to block my path with a hail of bullets. Do they think this is the USA, to immediately try to kill someone with a Gun just for ignoring a stop signal? I didn't need Protection, but my folding motorcycle did, so I rode with my Telekinetic Reaction Barrier activated.</p>
<p>I heard 'tik-tik' sounds as the bullets from the enemies' submachine guns bounced off.</p>
<p>"Open the Castle gate!" I shouted, coating the Telekinetic Reaction Barrier with Shadow Domination and the Abyss. The front was momentarily dark, but it was all to destroy the main gate blocking my path. I became a self-propelled siege weapon, running and destroying the Naruse Family Mansion's main gate, entering the Mansion.</p>
<p>"Who are you?!"</p>
<p>"Do you want to die?" Perhaps they had something they believed in, as the Security Guards aimed their Guns and Cursed at me, who had just destroyed the main gate and entered without a scratch. Of course, it was Japanese Cursing like 'Baka-yaro', so it didn't really hit home. At least if I heard Korean Cursing heavily laden with 'ssang-siot' or English Cursing filled with F-words, I'd think, 'Oh, I'm being Cursed at,' but this wasn't even Cursing.</p>
<p>"Just return the ninja you're holding, and I'll leave." Still, since I was a customer, I stated my purpose. I couldn't just kill everyone, so I thought if I spoke kindly (?), they might understand.</p>
<p>"What Nonsense/Bullshit are you talking about? Kill him!" As the Security Guard who seemed to be the leader ignored my Words and gave the Command, Fire flashed from the muzzle, and bullets flew towards me. Watching the incoming bullets, I extended my Palm and stopped them in empty space with Telekinesis. Everyone was surprised by this scene, as if from a movie, and stopped firing.</p>
<p>"I'll say it again. If you return the ninja you're holding, I'll leave. Otherwise... everyone will die." Actually, I thought the Security Guards weren't guilty, but looking at this damned Naruse Family, who sacrificed a girl every 6 years, I felt like they weren't entirely innocent bastards either. They just shoot people for approaching the Mansion, you know. Just as Killing Intent was slowly rising, the Security Guard leader stepped forward.</p>
<p>"J-just a Moment, please...!" His Attitude was quite subservient, considering he had just ordered me killed, but I decided to wait for a Moment as the Security Guard leader said. Sakagami Rena was likely being held by someone other than the Security Guards, so he was probably reporting to them.</p>
<p>"That Person has granted you permission to enter the Mansion. Please follow me." The Security Guard leader began to politely guide me, as if his Cursing and shooting at me had never happened. The Naruse Mansion was a well-maintained Western-style Mansion. It seemed to be a building built during the Japanese Empire period, feeling antique yet not old-fashioned. After admiring it for a while and walking slowly, I saw a man drinking tea on the Grass. The man saw me and smiled, rising from his Seat.</p>
<p>"Greetings." A man wearing glasses and a Kariginu, a traditional Japanese Attire with slits at the start of the sleeves, like an Onmyoji in manga or anime, greeted me with a gentle Expression. My name is Naruse Ginji. Do you know me? I had seen in the morning's Information check that Naruse Ginji was the first Son of Naruse Hirohide, the Naruse Family Head. Even with this man, Naruse Hirohide had adopted Naruse Shiyo's father. This man, like Naruse Hirohide, clearly knew about the Naruse Family's dirty secrets and had contributed to them.</p>
<p>"Your name doesn't Matter. I just need to take the ninja who came here." Since rescuing Sakagami Rena was the priority, I kept repeating the demand to hand over the ninja, like Kim Doo-han constantly shouting "4 dollars."</p>
<p>"Well. Whether it's the ninja or you, from our perspective, you're both Invaders. But if you pass my Test, I'll return that female ninja." He said "female ninja" even though I hadn't specified her gender, which confirmed that Sakagami Rena had been captured.</p>
<p>"What's the Test?"</p>
<p>"Defeat my Shikigami. Isn't that very easy?" Shikigami... Yogoe or Spirit Beasts that move under an Onmyoji's Command were called Shikigami. I remembered there was a Shikigami tree in the Summon build. Perhaps because it was a cyberpunk World where Yogoe were rare, they weren't often seen in the game. It might require a specific Bloodline, or it might be because Korea and the USA were the main Stages.</p>
<p>"Can I kill it?"</p>
<p>"It doesn't Matter. You will die by my Shikigami if you lose anyway." Naruse Ginji spoke as if I were already dead. I thought he said that because he didn't know me well, but his Attitude was a bit Annoying, wasn't it? Tetsuki (鐵鬼), deal with this invader. As soon as Naruse Ginji finished speaking, empty space ripped open with a 'jjeok' sound, and a monster with metallic skin appeared, swinging its claws at me. The Telekinetic Reaction Barrier, which I had unconsciously activated, was torn, and sharp claws came right before my Eyes.</p>
<p>I tilted my Head back, dodging the claws. Although my Proficiency with the Telekinetic Barrier was still low, to tear through the Telekinetic Reaction Barrier that even blocked bullets in a single strike, it seemed like a quite troublesome Enemy to face. I subtly glanced at Naruse Ginji, who was still watching our fight with that gentle smile and Intrigue. That bastard is a bit Annoying.</p>
