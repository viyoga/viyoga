<p>Neo Tokyo is a City built on the Bōsō Peninsula in Chiba Prefecture, which was destroyed in the Third World War. In fact, except for being connected to Tokyo via the Tokyo Bay Aqua-Line, it was a place completely unrelated to Tokyo, but the Taira Group, which was undertaking the Bōsō Peninsula reconstruction project, named it Neo Tokyo, incorporating the name of Tokyo, the most symbolic City that comes to mind when one thinks of Japan.</p>
<p>After the war, there were many things to do, and perhaps due to the debate over granting autonomy to each Enterprise, the Neo Tokyo project started late. Furthermore, perhaps because of the group's unreasonable plan to create an autonomous City all by itself, its progress was sluggish. But nevertheless, development was slowly progressing. From my perspective, rather, that was fortunate. Thanks to it progressing so late and slowly, I was able to catch the last train of Neo Tokyo's development.</p>
<p>Although I bought the land quite expensively, I knew that later I wouldn't be able to buy it even if I wanted to, so I poured a significant portion of my property into buying land and erecting several buildings. Also, in fact, because it was a building erected by Dang So-hwa's Ryuto Construction Company, which can be said to be under my Influence, it was built sturdily without skimping on materials. There were also quite a few Secret Spaces where we could move comfortably, allowing us to respond to unexpected threats.</p>
<p>"This building is where Hatake Investment Company and the PMC company created in Taira Ryuta's name are located. I deliberately made it a bit ordinary."</p>
<p>While in the car, I pointed to the Rectangle building, which was similar in appearance to its surroundings, and spoke. Because I had no desire to put Hatake Investment Company and my PMC prominently, I deliberately made the buildings of Hatake Investment Company and my PMC in a Rectangle shape, devoid of Individuality.</p>
<p>"Still, the Height is quite considerable...!"</p>
<p>Naruse Shiyo muttered while looking at the building. In fact, because most buildings in Neo Tokyo were tall, the Hatake Investment Company building was a 60-story skyscraper. Of course, there were many skyscrapers far exceeding 100 floors among the buildings of various Large Corporations trying to establish themselves in Neo Tokyo. However, to build a building of that scale, we had to spend more Budget than expected. Also, as I just explained, to prevent Hatake Investment Company from attracting attention, I didn't overdo it and made it to the average Height of Neo Tokyo's skyscrapers.</p>
<p>For reference, other buildings were scheduled to house the TG Tech Japan Branch, the Cyberware Japan Branch that Eve and I decided to create, and several company offices disguised by the Joseodang. For the rest, I planned to rent out offices and collect monthly rent.</p>
<p>"We'll stop by the Hatake Investment Company building later.... First, let's go Home."</p>
<p>Originally, I didn't intend to stop by Hatake Investment Company. On the way to the mansion I prepared in Neo Tokyo, there happened to be the Hatake Investment Company building, so I just showed it, saying, "Here it is." Since I also need to unpack my Luggage, I should stop by Home first.</p>
<p>After driving for about fifteen more minutes, a mansion with quite high walls appeared. As we briefly emerged from the forest of tall skyscrapers, a mansion of about four floors, surrounded by high walls, was visible. This mansion was the Home where I and the rest of the family would live.</p>
<p>Originally, this place was a place that had become Ruins due to air raids, and it was a place with strange rumors that ghosts appeared. Naturally, this place too, as it was a Neo Tokyo redevelopment area, several contractors got involved and attempted Development. However, due to mysterious accidents where workers died or were injured, and because of rumors that ghosts appeared, Construction was repeatedly halted and resumed until it was abandoned. One of the development companies that undertook this Business went completely bankrupt, and the others suffered heavy losses, so it came to be regarded as a cursed place.</p>
<p>Therefore, I was able to purchase the land at a considerably cheap Price than the average land Price in Neo Tokyo. I sent the Onmyoji of the Naruse Family and Hanakame to clear out any ghosts or similar entities that might really exist. I heard later that due to the air raids, a considerable number of remains were buried, so Hanakame separately collected the remains and performed a memorial service at a shrine. Perhaps because of that, strange phenomena appeared. Naturally, for a Shrine Maiden serving the Oeisin Lizhael, ghosts were merely beings to be eradicated.</p>
<p>Anyway, I, who had purchased a large plot of land at such a cheap Price where ghosts no longer appeared, made several demands to the construction company. The first was that it should be a sturdy Home, able to respond to enemy Invasion and withstand sudden attacks. The second, naturally, was that it should be comfortable to live in.</p>
<p>The first demand was met with high walls and several attack response systems installed, so it could block sudden attacks. At the main gate, Security Guards hired by the PMC were guarding, so they could respond to the opponent's attacks. Also, several CCTV cameras were installed on the Path leading to this mansion, so we could prepare in advance. The second demand, since it was naturally something that could be solved by pouring Money, I made a considerable Investment in the mansion as well.</p>
<p>Because I wasn't living alone in the mansion, it wasn't just one large building, but a form with multiple buildings, like an Eastern Palace with several pavilions. Of course, the buildings were not Eastern style with tiled roofs, but sophisticated modern buildings. Of course, the main building where I lived was the largest and most splendid, but the other buildings were not far behind. After all, there was no need to make the other buildings shabby. And those buildings, like Branches from a single Root, shared the Underground floor.</p>
<p>Most of the mansion's buildings were 3-4 floors, but the Underground was even deeper. In the Underground, various cultural Facilities, a central control room where all the mansion's systems could be operated, and other Facilities were located, but many places were still empty.</p>
<p>As I parked the car, everyone tumbled out and checked the mansion.</p>
<p>"Wow...! It's quite spacious."</p>
<p>"If it's this much land, it would be similar to our Main family home?"</p>
<p>Because most of them were Young Ladies from prestigious families, if these Young Ladies said "quite" spacious, by other people's standards, it meant it was incredibly spacious. In fact, after my Possession, I had never lived in such a spacious Place. Naturally, not before my Possession either. Right after my Possession, I lived in a small apartment with Jinmija Yeosa. During the Talent Development Institute period, I lived in Taira Yukiko's house inside the Talent Development Institute, but that Place wasn't this spacious. Even when I was assigned to Miyashi as a teacher at St. Francis Girls' Academy, instead of a huge mansion that could arouse suspicion from other families, I lived in a house about the size of the one at the Talent Development Institute.</p>
<p>"Ryuta-ssi! I want to live here too~!"</p>
<p>Seizawa Shii, although she was very close to me, was not under my Affiliation but an independent problem solver, so we had decided not to live together. However, seeing the Appearance of the mansion, it seemed her Mind had changed again. Soon, she had to go to the USA with Sabu Fujigasaski Rinka, so she didn't need a room right now. After returning from the USA, she might again be whimsical and say she didn't need a room.</p>
<p>"I can lend you a room as many as you want, so think carefully."</p>
<p>Lending a room is something I can do anytime, but living in the same mansion as me (though it's so incredibly spacious that it's a bit much to say we live in one house), now that I have revealed myself as Taira Ryuta, could send a Message that the Seizawa Family has joined hands with Taira, so she had to think carefully. Of course, since security is good, if she's careful, she won't be caught, but ultimately, the Choice was Seizawa Shii's. Once I finished talking with Seizawa Shii, I took everyone and headed towards the main building.</p>
<p>In front of the main building, the truck that had departed earlier arrived and was unloading Luggage. In front of the building, a thin Elderly Person wearing Glasses was standing in formal Suit attire. He was the Butler I had requested my Grandmother Taira Shizuku to find. Because this kind of thing is important, I asked my Grandmother, who would do anything for me. Naturally, my Grandmother, the mistress of the Taira Family, found an excellent Butler and allowed him to contract with me.</p>
<p>"Hello, Ryuta Young Master. My name is Tosen Nobumichi."</p>
<p>"Yes, I am Taira Ryuta. Please take good care of me from now on."</p>
<p>As we exchanged greetings, the Servants and Maids brought by Tosen Nobumichi began carrying the Luggage. Because everyone had already decided on their rooms, there was no Problem with moving the Luggage. For reference, Seizawa Shii's Luggage was moved to the guest room.</p>
<p>Most wanted to live in the main building with me, but Shimokawabe Airen decided to stay in the Annex closest to the main building, and Kijimoto Jurie decided to stay separately in a small Annex close to Shimokawabe Airen's Annex. Even if Shimokawabe Airen's Choice was understandable, Kijimoto Jurie, who liked me so much she wanted to taxidermy me, when I asked why she chose the Annex, she said the distance between the Annex and the main building wasn't far anyway, and it was to create a separate space for research. Because she conducts toxicity research, she said it could be dangerous if it leaked.</p>
<p>So, separately next to the Annex, a small Annex was built with various Devices installed, ordered by Kijimoto Jurie. Only this Annex was not connected to the Underground Facilities, because it would be a big Problem if toxic substances flowed into the Underground Facilities, so it was not connected separately to the Underground Facilities. If Kijimoto Jurie wanted to use the Underground Facilities, she would have to go to the adjacent Annex, which would be annoying. But because her Personality was so dedicated to research, it seemed she wouldn't use them much. Even now, the Servants were following her with expressions wondering if they might break her research Items.</p>
<p>"First, let's unpack our Luggage and meet at the restaurant."</p>
<p>I told everyone and followed the Servant carrying my Luggage. The others who came with me also began moving with excited Footsteps to check their respective rooms.</p>
