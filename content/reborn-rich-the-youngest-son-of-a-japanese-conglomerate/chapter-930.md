In Asada Ei's case, being vulnerable to Heat, even in Winter, the indoor heating must have been quite agonizing. Even a Meeting like this must have felt suffocating.</p>
<p>But because her Level had risen in this battle with Anyaji, and she had acquired the Flame Resistance Trait, the indoor temperature seemed to feel more comfortable.</p>
<p>"You've worked hard. Now, you won't have to suffer from normal temperatures anymore."</p>
<p>"I see.... It was hard wearing a special cyber Suit every day.... That's fortunate." Even now, Asada Ei was wearing a cyber Suit. <p>"Well.... Everyone worked hard, so I'm thinking of giving bonuses to both the Jo-seo-dang and the Combatant-nim this time." In fact, we gained more from Anyaji than we consumed in this battle, so giving bonuses wouldn't be a big burden.</p>

<p>When I checked before, it was 14, so she had gained 2 Levels. The lower increase was due to her already high Level, not because Naruse Shiyo hadn't put in Effort. Given that her Stats increased more than the 4 points from two Level Ups, she must have undergone considerably harsh Training until her Stats rose. From what I could see, her relatively low Mind Stats had risen significantly, which suggests that Shimokawabe Airen, Naruse Shiyo's Sabu, primarily focused on Training to increase Mental Power.</p>
<p>Even now, her Physical Body Abilities are high, but since they increase further when she transforms into a Dragonkin, she must have trained her Mind to balance her Physical Body. In terms of Special Ability, as expected, her Luck increased by 2 to 13 due to the Achievement of witnessing Takakusaki Gengo's Outer God transformation, and her Magic Power also increased by 1, reaching an impressive 17. In fact, her Stats were impeccable, except for the slightly low Intelligence and Creativity Stats on the Mind side.</p>
<p>Of course, with her high Magic Power Stat, she could pursue a growth path in Sorcery, but her Intelligence Stat, one of the main Stats for Magic-type abilities, was a bit lacking for primarily focusing on Magic. Ultimately, using Sorcery as a secondary skill and engaging in Hand-to-Hand Combat with the powerful Physical Body of a Dragonkin, a Magic Swordsman path, would be Naruse Shiyo's proper growth direction.</p>
<p>Looking at her Skills, the Naruse Family's Naginata Arts she had been learning had changed into her own Nagamaki Arts called Shiyo Style - Dragon Claw Long Sword Style. She said she was creating it with the intention of founding a new school with Shimokawabe Airen, and even though she had created a Narrative+ grade Skill, she was still not satisfied and was further refining her Skill. And like Alik, it seemed natural that she acquired the Training Skill, having trained under the same Sabu.</p>
<p>Unlike Alik, she acquired a Skill called Camping, which suggests that while Alik was undergoing physical Training, Naruse Shiyo, who relatively didn't need as much physical Training, acquired this Skill by making various preparations outdoors. The last Skill in the Skills section, Flight Combat Arts, seemed to be a Skill she acquired by transforming into a Dragonkin and fighting while flying, and it was a pretty good Skill. Flying itself was a significant advantage, so an increase in the Level of Flight Combat Arts was a welcome development.</p>
<p>She acquired one Trait, which was Dragonkin Sensation. In fact, I hadn't known there were Dragons in this Worldview, so it was a Trait I was seeing for the first time. Curious about its options, I immediately checked the content of Dragonkin Sensation. ◆Dragonkin Sensation "Unique Race Trait": Acquires the Sensation of a Dragonkin, allowing for a better grasp of battle situations. When in Dragon form, can receive more Information through Dragon's Horn, Dragon's Wing, and Dragon's Tail.</p>
<p>Sensitive Nerves Stat's maximum value increases by 1, and its efficiency and range increase. Reading the content, it seemed to be a Trait that sharpened her Senses, but the description was somewhat abstract. Roughly reading it, it felt like it would increase Evasion Rate or Critical Hit Chance during battle. I'll have to check it separately later. Besides Sensation, the maximum value of the Sensitive Nerves Stat increased, and not only its efficiency but also, unusually, its range increased.</p>
<p>If the range of Sensation increases, she could better detect and avoid an Opponent's Ambush or sniper attack, and even launch an Ambush herself.</p>
<p>"My Level hasn't risen as much as I thought."</p>

<p>"Ehehe.... Is that so?" Naruse Shiyo was a little disappointed when I explained that she had only gained 2 Levels, but when I comforted her, she soon smiled brightly. And my Words weren't just to comfort her; they were the truth. According to the game Setting of Megacorp's Youngest Son, although the max Level was only 20 (of course, the maximum Level could be expanded with Traits or Elixirs), reaching max Level was not easy. This is because the Experience Points required increase with each Level.</p>
<p>Therefore, there was no need for Naruse Shiyo to be disappointed that she had only gained 2 Levels since I last saw her at Level 14. I, too, had experienced an enormous number of Incidents during the half-year since I was assigned to St. Francis Girls' Academy, and I was at Level 16, the same as Naruse Shiyo.</p>
<p>"Yes, so don't be too Eager Impatience.... Levels aren't the only important thing; how well you can use your Skills or Traits is also important, so you should work harder on that."</p>
