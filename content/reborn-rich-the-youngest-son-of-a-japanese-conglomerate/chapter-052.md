<p>"Fumimoto, what do you think?"</p>
<p>"Are you referring to Ryuta Young Master-nim?" Taira Danjo questioned Fumimoto Takayuki, his Chief of Staff. Sometimes his questions were vague, causing the other person to reveal what they were currently thinking. But this time, he nodded as if Fumimoto Takayuki's re-questioning was correct.</p>
<p>"Why did he only ask for an Allowance?"</p>
<p>"Well, isn't it because he's afraid of his Uncles and Aunts? He might be being careful, fearing he'll end up like Saburo-nim."</p>
<p>"Hmm, I suppose so." Although the True Identity of the person who instigated Taira Saburo's Death had not yet been revealed, Taira Danjo and Fumimoto Takayuki believed it was instigated by one of the Tairaga's three Successors: Taira Ichiro, Taira Jiro, or Taira Kokoro. This was because they had determined that even the rival Minamoto Group wouldn't gain much by killing Taira Saburo. Taira Danjo's thought was simple. When an Incident occurs, the Person who gains the most from it, or their close associate, is the culprit.</p>
<p>In that case, the three Successors could be said to have gained the most, as their rival had disappeared. Although the investigation into Taira Saburo's Death was ongoing, the fact that the Manpower for the investigation had been halved was evidence of Taira Danjo's thoughts. After all, the personnel investigating the Minamoto side had been reassigned elsewhere.</p>
<p>"That Guy also knows that fifty million yen is a large Allowance."</p>
<p>"Well, Chairman-nim was going to promote Ryuta Young Master-nim, wasn't he? Isn't that Money given with that in mind?" That's right. This time, Taira Danjo had intended to promote Taira Ryuta to Deputy Team Leader of the Education Support Team 2. Although he hadn't yet produced any Work Achievements, Taira Danjo had considered the promotion because he believed Ryuta had won the public's favor by directly fighting Terrorists. Although Taira Ryuta had kicked away that opportunity, Taira Danjo understood.</p>
<p>If Fumimoto Takayuki's Words were correct, then prioritizing his own Life was paramount. In fact, he had asked what he wanted as a formality, but he hadn't expected him to Demand something. Until now, his Sons and other Grandsons had always said it was fine not to give them anything, or that they would accept whatever was given, so hearing him directly state what he wanted was refreshing. It was even more refreshing that it was an Allowance. He had never given an Allowance to anyone until now.</p>
<p>This was because most of the Secretaries in charge of each Child handled their Children's Money problems.</p>
<p>"What will that Guy do with that Money?"</p>
<p>"He's reportedly collecting Stock in an American Enterprise called Miracle Health these days. It seems he's investing there again this time."</p>
<p>"Stock, huh.... If he invests well, it should be fine. Do you think it's a Stock that will rise?"</p>
<p>"This time, new Medicine Development is reportedly almost in its final Stage. I don't know what kind of new Medicine it is, but if it goes well, the Stock price is expected to rise."</p>
<p>"If it goes well, that is. More importantly, he hid the fact that he's a Superhuman well, didn't he?" Taira Danjo received a Report on Taira Ryuta's regenerative Ability and ordered Fumimoto Takayuki to conceal it. From a certain Moment, Superhumans were gradually appearing in the World. Taira Danjo thought this would have a significant Influence on the Future, but his foolish Children, who couldn't see the Future, would not tolerate a Superhuman Nephew/Niece.</p>
<p>Unlike the Taira Saburo Incident, Minamoto would surely think of eliminating Taira Ryuta this time. They would not tolerate the Grandson of a rival Group being a Superhuman. This was because Superhumans were rare and Talented individuals desired by any Enterprise or Nation. It was natural for a rival to desperately try to eliminate a Superhuman if they possessed one.</p>
<p>"Yes, we've manipulated it to feel like a somewhat absurd rumor. No one will know that Ryuta Young Master-nim is a Superhuman."</p>
<p>"Still, since we don't know, keep a close eye on his movements."</p>
<p>"Understood, Chairman-nim."</p>
<p>We had an early Evening meal and immediately set off for Home by car. Taira Yukiko still seemed to be in a bad Mood. First, I thought she'd feel better if she took off that funeral Kimono and put on something else.</p>
<p>"Mother, do you have any Kimonos other than the one you're wearing now?"</p>
<p>"Uh? I probably have a red Kimono I wore when I was a Virgin."</p>
<p>"Then let's go Home, put that on, and go see the cherry blossoms." Taira Yukiko seemed flustered by my sudden Words about going to see the cherry blossoms. By the way, a red Kimono.... Perhaps because I've only seen the funeral Kimono, I can't quite Imagine it.</p>
<p>"Suddenly... why?"</p>
<p>"I don't want to keep seeing Mother down, I'll make you happy." Taira Yukiko remained silent, not saying a Word to my statement. Glancing over while driving, I saw her slightly bowing her Head, seemingly lost in Worry. But the time for thought was short. Since the Talent Development Center and the Main family home were only thirty minutes away by car, we soon arrived Home.</p>
<p>"I'll change my Clothes and wait in the car. Mother, you change your Clothes and come too. Into that red Kimono I mentioned earlier." I made the announcement and went straight up to my Room on the second floor before Taira Yukiko could say anything. Even just Jeans and a white T-shirt would have looked good on my Body, but it was a bit chilly, so I put on a light jacket. Anyway, it would take some time to change Clothes, so should I sit for about five minutes and then leave?</p>
<p>While looking at my phone for a moment, ten minutes passed without me realizing it. I hurried downstairs. As I opened the Front Door, Taira Yukiko stood there, dressed in a red Kimono, with the full moonlight as her backdrop. With the moonlight and the Home's Lighting, Yukiko's Appearance was truly picturesque. Certainly, this was better than the funeral Kimono.</p>
<p>"Ah...!"</p>
<p>"You're beautiful, Mother." Taira Yukiko was at a loss for what to do at my Praise. Has it been a long time since she heard she was beautiful? She must have heard it a lot. Taira Saburo probably didn't express it separately. I took Taira Yukiko's Hand and put her in the car. Of course, I had a thought, so I wasn't planning to go to Yotsukaido General Park, where the Terror Incident had occurred. The Reason I looked at my phone in the Room earlier was to see if there was another Place to see cherry blossoms in this vicinity.</p>
<p>After searching for ten minutes, I found a Place to go. The place we were going to was a park called Utsukushigaoka Kinrin Park (美しが丘近隣公園), about a ten-minute drive east of the Talent Development Center. There were lots of cherry blossoms and a lake in the center, making it a great place for a walk. As soon as we got out of the parking lot, the fluttering cherry blossoms greeted us.</p>
<p>"Beautiful...!" As Taira Yukiko said, the cherry blossoms reflected in the moonlight had a different taste. Taira Yukiko quietly expressed Admiration as she looked at the cherry blossoms. We walked through the Park, admiring the cherry blossoms. After walking for a while, we saw a street stall. It was around 7:30, so it was peak selling time.</p>
<p>"We've lived together for several weeks, but I don't even know what Mother likes. Let's start with street food. What kind of street food do you like?"</p>
<p>"I used to like strawberry candy. But it feels like it's been so long since I've been to a Place like this festival. So I've forgotten the taste." Strawberry candy? Ah, it seemed she was talking about the strawberry tanghulu I couldn't eat before due to the accident and Terror. I had even thought about getting this wrapped for Taira Yukiko then, what a coincidence?</p>
<p>"It's been a while since we had Evening, so shall we buy some and eat together?"</p>
<p>"Y-yes. Let's do that." I led Taira Yukiko by the Hand and ordered two strawberry candies and two green grape candies. As I bit into the strawberry candy, the sugar coating on the outside broke with a crisp, 'wasak'. And the refreshing strawberry flesh was felt. I felt as if this strawberry candy was like Taira Yukiko. A possessor of a delicate Mind, wrapped in a fragile sugar coating. That was the Taira Yukiko I thought of.</p>
<p>Taira Yukiko also ate the strawberry candy deliciously, with an Expression that said, 'Ah, this was the taste!' The green grape candy wasn't bad either, but the strawberry was tastier, so we decided to buy two more strawberry candies and eat them one by one as we moved. After that, we wandered around, enjoying the street stalls and the festival's Play. Hearing the Stories from the Surroundings as we walked around, they said there would be Fireworks around 9 PM.</p>
<p>I had thought this year we'd be watching bombs exploding in the air as Fireworks, so it's Fortunate to be able to see actual Fireworks.</p>
<p>"Let's just watch the Fireworks for a bit and then go in, Mother."</p>
<p>"Let's do that." Before I knew it, Taira Yukiko's Face, which had been full of shadows, was now filled with excitement. This is Filial Piety, indeed. We moved to a Place where the Fireworks would be clearly visible. The Place we found was a bit like a Hill, and there weren't many People. It was good, as we could focus more on the Fireworks. Soon, at 9 o'clock, the Fireworks began.</p>
<p>As the Flames rose like meteors ascending in reverse, exploding in the empty space and embroidering the night sky with beautiful Fireworks, I unconsciously looked at Taira Yukiko. Taira Yukiko was also looking at me. Taira Yukiko's Face grew larger and larger. Was I approaching, or was Taira Yukiko's Face approaching me? Soon, Velvet Lining met Velvet Lining. Pung! Peopung! Pung! Fireworks continued to bloom in the night sky. Hidden beneath the Fireworks, we continued our Kiss. Taira Yukiko's Lips tasted of strawberry candy.</p>
