<p>The next match was against Class 1-B, our group's bye team. Maybe it was because they were first-years with less experience, but we were able to win even more easily. The opposing team didn't seem too upset, probably because they got an autograph from Uenozono Karen. It almost seemed like the autograph was their main goal.</p>
<p>We were able to win the semi-finals easily as well. No matter how many members of the archery club were on the opposing team, all five of us were highly skilled. The cool-headed Shido Tsubaki even saw Uenozono Karen make a mistake in the first match and entrusted the final shot to Sakagami Rena. It seemed like she had faith that Sakagami Rena, the last player, would cover for any mistakes made before her.</p>
<p>The final match was against Class 3-A, a class made up of the archery club president and most of the archery club members. I thought, 'Okay, this is what a final should look like.' Most of them were shooting at least 8 points, averaging 9, and the competition was fierce.</p>
<p>Even Oofuchi Erika, who gets nervous easily, and Uenozono Karen, who made a mistake in the first match, did quite well, scoring a combined total of 27 points.</p>
<p>Even though we were doing so well, the scores were neck and neck. The opponents were also shooting without any major mistakes, so our scores were constantly going back and forth, making it an even fight. Eventually, with the score tied, the ponytail-wearing girl who was the archery club president stepped up.</p>
<p>The archery club president calmly drew and released her bow. *Tung! Tung! Tung!* With the sound of the three arrows being shot, the scores came up: 10, 10, 9. A total of 29 points. Now, the only way we could win was if Sakagami Rena scored 10 points with all three arrows.</p>
<p>Sakagami Rena stepped onto the shooting range with a calm expression. She didn't seem nervous at all.</p>
<p>Sakagami Rena drew and released her bow just like before. However, her movements were so fast that all three arrows hit the target almost simultaneously. It wasn't the *tung! tung! tung!* feeling of the archery club president's shots, but more like *tututung!* - a rapid-fire barrage of incredible speed. And all of those quick shots hit the 10-point mark.</p>
<p>A moment of silence. The archery club president stared at Sakagami Rena with a dumbfounded expression. Sakagami Rena looked at the archery club president and gave a faint smile.</p>
<p>,Waa~! We won!</p>
<p>Uenozono Karen's excited face appeared on the screen, and everyone cheered, congratulating us on our victory.</p>
<p>,Now it's my turn, huh? I'll do my best, Ryuta-cchi!</p>
<p>After archery was the lacrosse match, which was a Red vs. White team competition instead of class-based. Seizawa Shii participated for our class. The truth was, no one in our class was particularly good at lacrosse, so Seizawa Shii, who had only played a few times, stepped up.</p>
<p>Contrary to Seizawa Shii's confident cheering and participation, the Red team, which we were on, was utterly defeated by the White team. The White team had a bunch of regulars from the lacrosse club.</p>
<p>,Hueeng~! We lost, Ryuta-cchi!</p>
<p>Seizawa Shii returned to the class with tears welling up in her eyes, upset about losing the game. After the lacrosse match, the last event before lunch started. It was the horseback riding competition, another Red vs. White event that would be over quickly with just one race.</p>
<p>,I'll dedicate this victory to you, Sensei!</p>
<p>Konoe Akina decided to participate in the horseback riding competition. While the lacrosse match was going on, the staff members were leveling the ground outside the field, which seemed to be for the horseback riding event.</p>
<p>In the horseback riding competition, six members from the Red team and six from the White team would compete simultaneously. The first-place winner would receive 12 points, the second-place winner 11 points, and so on, with the last-place finisher receiving 1 point. The team with the higher combined score would win.</p>
<p>On our Red team, Taira Kanna and the vice-captain of the equestrian club were the notable competitors. On the opposing White team, there was the Public Morals Committee Chair, Nakanoin Rina, the Public Morals Committee Vice-Chair, Muko Neika, who helped us catch the drug dealer this time, and the captain of the equestrian club.</p>
<p>Looking at the members, it seemed like the White team had the advantage, but if Konoe Akina performed well, who knew what would happen.</p>
<p>The horses used in the horseback riding competition were owned by the participating students or their families. Konoe Akina, as the head of the Konoe family, brought an absurdly fast-looking, muscular black horse with an unusually blonde mane.</p>
<p>Konoe Akina rode the black horse in front of our class and opened her mouth with a dignified air.</p>
<p>,Please pray for Ryutaro's victory.</p>
<p>Ryutaro... as its name? Anyone could tell it was named after me. Naturally, all the students in the class burst out laughing at the name Ryutaro.</p>
<p>,Ahahaha! A black horse with a blonde mane... Isn't this basically Ryuta-cchi?!</p>
<p>,It's just like Sensei!</p>
<p>The female students laughed and gathered around Ryutaro, stroking its fur. Ryutaro didn't seem to mind, as it stood still and neighed softly.</p>
<p>,It's just like Sensei, chasing after women.</p>
<p>,Shut up.</p>
<p>Otagi Yuka, who was standing next to me watching, chuckled playfully and muttered. Soon, an announcement was made to prepare at the starting line, and Konoe Akina rode Ryutaro toward the stadium.</p>
<p>Bang!</p>
<p>With the sound of the starting gun, everyone started riding their horses. The sound of the horses' hooves pounding the ground and the cheers of the crowd made the race quite exciting.</p>
<p>As expected of an expensive horse, Konoe Akina's Ryutaro took the lead from the start. Taira Kanna's white horse closely followed behind, and the captain and vice-captain of the equestrian club were running behind them. Nakanoin Rina and Muko Neika were also riding their horses as if they wouldn't be outdone.</p>
<p>Since the remaining riders were all members of the equestrian club, they all rode without falling behind, constantly overtaking each other.</p>
<p>By the second lap, Ryutaro and Taira Kanna's white horse were gradually widening the gap and running ahead. By the third and final lap, Taira Kanna's white horse put on a burst of speed at the end, and the two of them reached the finish line almost simultaneously.</p>
<p>The captain and vice-captain of the equestrian club, Nakanoin Rina, and Muko Neika came in one after another. When the scores were calculated, the Red team had won by a small margin, probably because they had taken both 1st and 2nd place.</p>
<p>However, even though they were on the same Red team, they needed to confirm who had come in first place for the record, so a replay was slowly being shown on the screen. At the end, Taira Kanna's white horse had tried to make a comeback with a final burst of speed, but Konoe Akina's Ryutaro had taken first place by a narrow margin.</p>
<p>Maybe it was because it was named Ryutaro, but I felt pretty good about it taking first place. It felt like I had won first place myself.</p>
<p>,Waa, Ryuta-cchi won~!!</p>
<p>,Why is that horse Ryuta-cchi!</p>
<p>Seizawa Shii, who was watching the race, jumped up and down excitedly and hugged me. I peeled Seizawa Shii off of me, scolding her because there were a lot of eyes watching.</p>
<p>,Sensei, we won!</p>
<p>,Good job.</p>
<p>When Konoe Akina, who had returned Ryutaro, told me excitedly, I stroked Konoe Akina's head. Seizawa Shii, who had been pushed away when she tried to hug me, seemed a little jealous that I was stroking Konoe Akina's head, and glared at me, saying, ,Chii~!. So I stroked Seizawa Shii's head as well. Her expression immediately softened. I really don't know if she has a good personality or if she's just very jealous.</p>
<p>,It's lunchtime now, right? Go have lunch with your families~!</p>
<p>,Yes~!!</p>
<p>,I wonder if my dad's here...?</p>
<p>Lunchtime was longer than usual because there were guests. They gave us a generous hour and a half, so there was no need to rush.</p>
<p>Of course, I didn't have any family here, so I was planning to eat alone. I was going to eat with Kijimoto Jurie, but Kijimoto Jurie said that she had been invited by the Naruse family because she usually takes care of Naruse Shiyo.</p>
<p>,Ryuta, have lunch with me.</p>
<p>Oh, right, I do have family. Taira Kokoro stopped me as I was about to head to the special class building.</p>
<p>,Should I?</p>
<p>If it's Taira Kokoro, she probably packed something delicious, right? I smiled and nodded.</p>
<p>###</p>
<p>Seizawa Eiji arrived at St. Francisco Girls' Academy shortly after the lacrosse match ended and before the horseback riding competition began. Seizawa Eiji is the father of Seizawa Shii and a current member of the National Diet. He is still young and does not belong to any particular faction or wield significant power, but the Seizawa family is a prominent political family, so with experience, he could become a future Prime Minister of Japan.</p>
<p>,Tsk tsk tsk! Just like a politician, he doesn't want to see his daughter lose, huh?</p>
<p>Minamoto Ushin clicked his tongue as he watched Seizawa Eiji sitting in the parents' section. Seizawa Eiji, like a politician, smoothly brushed off Minamoto Ushin's sharp words.</p>
<p>,Haha! How could that be? I had some business to discuss with the Thai side, so I couldn't find the time in the morning.</p>
<p>,You came at the right time. The horseback riding competition is quite a sight to see.</p>
<p>At Taira Danjo's words, Seizawa Eiji smiled and sat down. When he saw Taira Kanna on a white horse on the large screen visible from the parents' section, Seizawa Eiji grinned.</p>
<p>,I've heard rumors that Kanna-yang is quite skilled at horseback riding.</p>
<p>,She's quite useful compared to others her age... No! What is that horse?!</p>
<p>Taira Danjo, who had been modest in responding to Seizawa Eiji's praise, was shocked by the black horse on the screen. As someone knowledgeable about horseback riding and the owner of several horses, Taira Danjo could tell that the horse Konoe Akina was riding was truly exceptional.</p>
<p>,Still, it's fortunate that they're on the same Red team.</p>
<p>,Yes. It looks like the Red team will win.</p>
<p>,The White team should win....</p>
<p>While Seizawa Eiji and Taira Danjo were predicting the Red team's victory, Minamoto Ushin, since his daughter wasn't participating in the race anyway, was hoping that the White team, which symbolized Minamoto, would win.</p>
<p>The race started, and as Seizawa Eiji and Taira Danjo had predicted, the Red team won, causing Minamoto Ushin's expression to darken. Even though it was just a symbol, he felt bitter that the White team, which symbolized Minamoto, had lost to the Red team, which symbolized Taira.</p>
<p>,Ah...!</p>
<p>The screen showed the Red team, the winning team, gathered together, and a scene flashed by where Seizawa Shii was jumping up and down excitedly and hugging the homeroom teacher standing in front of her. Seeing this, Seizawa Eiji was speechless, and Taira Danjo and Minamoto Ushin smiled and opened their mouths.</p>
<p>,Shii is still the same. She's so free-spirited.</p>
<p>,Don't worry too much. She probably just hugged him without any ulterior motives.</p>
<p>The two of them spoke as if they were trying to reassure him, but their expressions were full of teasing, like sly old men. Seizawa Eiji, like a politician, said with a smile, ,It's good to be able to rejoice with the homeroom teacher when you're having fun,, but he couldn't help but have a large vein pop out on his temple.</p>
<p>Kujo Kazuo, who was watching from behind, was anxiously looking at the area where the special class had gathered, wondering if Kujo Kina would hug him too.</p>
