<p>"Do you have something specific to tell me?"</p>
<p>Takakusaki Athena placed the luggage she had brought next to the dining table and then sat down. Takakusaki Athena looked at me with an Expression that seemed to ask, 'What is this person trying to say?'</p>
<p>In truth, even if we were in a truce, it was only for the duration of this ski resort trip; originally, we were adversaries, so it was natural for her to be puzzled when I asked her to sit in front of me, as there shouldn't have been anything specific to discuss. Still, I had a lot to say because I needed to persuade Takakusaki Athena.</p>
<p>"First, I have something to ask. I heard you have a foster father-daughter relationship with Takakusaki Gengo, but under what circumstances were you adopted?"</p>
<p>"I have no reason to tell you, but my Parents died during the War. My Father, Gengo-ssi, came to rescue my parents but was late, so he only saved me."</p>
<p>Ah, so that's what she believed. I had wondered how Takakusaki Gengo, an Assassin, had managed to entice Takakusaki Athena, but it seems he told her a story along the lines of, 'I was part of the rescue team, but I was late.'</p>
<p>"That's what you believe?"</p>
<p>"That way? Are you saying I'm mistaken? It's something I experienced myself. I have no reason to listen to you speak like that."</p>
<p>At my Words, Takakusaki Athena frowned slightly, as if displeased. It was, in fact, quite a Sensitive topic. Since it involved the death of her biological parents and her current foster father, Takakusaki Athena must have thought I was speaking carelessly.</p>
<p>"I spoke that way because what I know and your Story are different. I have objective data."</p>
<p>I took out several documents I had prepared in advance from the resort office last evening and handed them to Takakusaki Athena.</p>
<p>I showed her Takakusaki Gengo's roster from the Japanese Special Mission Unit during the Third World War, the assassination list of the Japanese Special Mission Unit, and the order that was revised before the mission, suddenly placing Takakusaki Athena's father, Elias Mannerheim, and mother, Sofia Mannerheim, on the assassination list.</p>
<p>"This is a secret document from the Japanese government that I obtained with great difficulty. If you read it, you'll understand its true meaning."</p>
<p>Takakusaki Athena meticulously read the documents I handed her. As time passed, her Expression gradually shifted to astonishment.</p>
<p>"I-is this true?"</p>
<p>"I have an agent under me who is a former Self-Defense Force intelligence officer. These are the documents he obtained. If you don't believe me, investigate it yourself."</p>
<p>She still didn't seem to fully trust the documents I presented. Naturally, she wouldn't be able to accept the Story that the person she had believed to be her father for years was, in fact, the one who killed her biological parents.</p>
<p>And she would likely want to believe her foster father more than me, her adversary. She might also consider my documents to be fabricated.</p>
<p>I, too, didn't have the rosy expectation that she would immediately believe my Words. I was satisfied with having planted the seed of doubt in Takakusaki Athena's Mind.</p>
<p>I don't know the Level of Takakusaki Athena's information, but now she'll have to find out for herself.</p>
<p>"Here."</p>
<p>I handed Takakusaki Athena a business card. Takakusaki Athena, whose Mind seemed troubled, looked at my business card and then back at me.</p>
<p>"This is..."</p>
<p>"If you investigate and find that Story to be true, contact me, and I'll help you with your Revenge. And as you know, don't have the foolish thought of asking Takakusaki Gengo directly. You might even be harmed instead."</p>
<p>"I'll handle the information myself. And don't you remember? You already gave me a business card on Sanzokuyaki restaurant street."</p>
<p>Takakusaki Athena reacted sharply to my warning not to ask Takakusaki Gengo directly. Ah, that's right. I had already given Takakusaki Athena a business card. Then there's no need to give her another one.</p>
<p>"Then... make yourself comfortable now. If it's time, I'll provide lunch here too."</p>
<p>"Understood. I should put my luggage in the room I'll be staying in."</p>
<p>After breakfast, investigation, and going to the auditorium, it was already almost lunchtime. Still, I had about an hour of leisure, so I should catch up on some of the Sleep I missed during Dawn.</p>
<p>The university students, who had returned from the auditorium after arguing with the Students of St. Francis's Girls' Academy, first gathered in the room where the male students were staying. The university students had only skimmed the fifth floor, so despite it being their own room, they hadn't personally checked the bloodstains on the window frame.</p>
<p>"There are definitely faint bloodstains here. And outside the window is Tanaka Norio's Corpse..."</p>
<p>Club President Suzuki Soki said, looking at the faint dark red bloodstains on the window frame, opening the window, and looking down. The snowstorm continued, so Tanaka Norio's Corpse, which the blonde, tanned teacher had dug up, was half-submerged in the snow.</p>
<p>"Shouldn't we bring Tanaka's Corpse inside?"</p>
<p>"No, if we touch it, evidence might be contaminated. And if we bring it inside, it might thaw and decompose faster."</p>
<p>At Hiragami Noyu's Words, Itsu Tomohiko shook his Head and said. Usually, Itsu Tomohiko was a swaggering, flashy type, but given the Situation of a club friend's death, he seemed serious and somber.</p>
<p>"For now, everyone, sit over here."</p>
<p>Suzuki Soki closed the window and spoke to his Friends. They had clearly come on a trip with light hearts, but it had turned into a major Incident with one of their club members dead, leaving everyone out of their minds. Still, Suzuki Soki, as the Club President, needed to comfort his Friends and sort out this Situation, which was why he had gathered them.</p>
<p>"Although we just argued with the high school girls who claimed the culprit was among us... circumstantially, it seems correct that the Killer is among us."</p>
<p>The Situation itself pointed to the culprit being among the university students. Above all, with the biggest piece of evidence being the sleeping pill powder settled in the beer, it couldn't be denied.</p>
<p>"But you know what? Didn't they bring that white powder and put it in to make us suspicious?"</p>
<p>Nakamura Shunna, the Vice Club President, said with a dissatisfied Expression. Of course, the probability isn't zero, but if the culprit was among the high school girls, why would they specifically target Tanaka Norio for Murder? As long as that question remained unresolved, it was more likely that one of the club members gathered here was the culprit.</p>
<p>"So, what are we going to do?"</p>
<p>The ordinary-looking man, Shiojima Atsushi, asked Suzuki Soki.</p>
<p>"First, since the probability of the culprit being among us is high, let's not scatter and instead Surveillance each other. The police will arrive tomorrow or the day after, so we just need to hold out until then."</p>
<p>"Wouldn't it be better to scatter and lock our room doors instead?"</p>
<p>At Suzuki Soki's suggestion to stay together and hold out, Uzuka Hikari, the top Beauty of the International Politics Discussion Club, offered a dissenting opinion. Her thought was that it would be better to scatter and lock their doors than to stay with club members who were likely to include a Killer, but Suzuki Soki shook his Head.</p>
<p>"Remember that Kotowazuki, the Killer, murdered all the employees. He definitely has a master key. If we separate, we'll surely be picked off one by one."</p>
<p>"Ah...!"</p>
<p>It was certainly a valid point. If there was a guarantee that the Killer didn't have a master key, it might be advantageous for each person to lock themselves in their room and hide, but for now, it would be better to stay together.</p>
<p>"Then why don't we just join forces with the high school girls? If there are more people, we can take turns resting..."</p>
<p>"Not right now... It'll be lunchtime soon, so let's meet at the restaurant and talk."</p>
<p>Nakamura Shunna shook his Head at Hiragami Noyu's proposal. Since Nakamura Shunna was the one who had just argued, he seemed reluctant to humble himself and approach St. Francis's Girls' Academy.</p>
<p>"...."</p>
