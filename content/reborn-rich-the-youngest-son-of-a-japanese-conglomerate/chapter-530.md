<p>The matches didn't start immediately after lunch. There were performances by several singers invited by the academy. Of course, Serif's performance was among them.</p>
<p>Perhaps because there were performances by various singers, not just Serif, the atmosphere was quite good. Large screens were attached here and there, so the performances were easily visible even from a distance.</p>
<p>After about an hour of performances, there was a class-versus-class match, which was dodgeball. It was a 10-on-10, 10-minute game with the rule that the team that annihilated all opponents before 10 minutes or had more survivors at the end of 10 minutes would win.</p>
<p>Naruse Shiyo and Shido Tsubaki, who were physically weak, and Uenozono Karen, who was tired from just performing, were excluded from the dodgeball game.</p>
<p>Of course, our 3rd-year special class was unstoppable. Seizawa Shii threw the ball with tremendous power, as if to avenge her loss in the morning lacrosse game, incapacitating one person after another (in fact, students were unable to get up after being hit by Seizawa Shii's ball), and Kujo Kina threw the ball while laughing scarily, "Kyahaha." Sakagami Rena, who specialized in throwing techniques, knocked out two or three people with one throw, and Alik threw lightning-fast balls with her unique expressionless face.</p>
<p>Otagi Yuka and Saionji Diana seemed to be blending in appropriately, perhaps because they didn't feel the need to step forward themselves. Nevertheless, the special class was overwhelmingly winning. The academy, which had tightly scheduled the sports, would be quite grateful since each game ended in almost 3 minutes.</p>
<p>In the finals, we met 3rd-year Class C, led by the Public Morals Committee Vice-Chairman Muko Neika, and even though Muko Neika alone showed off her spirit by knocking out a whopping 7 people, in the end, our special class won. By the way, Muko Neika is amazing, isn't she? With this level of skill, she was as good as the students with high combat power in our special class.</p>
<p>I even wanted to recruit her like some of our classmates, but Muko Neika will probably... become one of Taira Kanna's henchmen, along with Public Morals Committee Chairman Nakanoin Rina.</p>
<p>Looking at what Taira Kanna is doing, it seemed like she was steadily gathering the forces she could gather at St. Francisco Girls' Academy. The Public Morals Committee members under the student council were well-armed and steadily gaining combat experience, and from what I heard, the student council members were all talented individuals who excelled in one field or another.</p>
<p>It certainly seemed like she was gathering forces within the fence of the school to take a share in the Taira Group's empire.</p>
<p>,Well done."</p>
<p>,Teacher, we won!"</p>
<p>,Waa~! Ryuta-jji won!"</p>
<p>,Ryutta, won...!"</p>
<p>I hugged the students who rushed at me all at once and congratulated them on their victory. I was conscious of the parents, but still, I couldn't coldly turn away the kids who were so happy as their homeroom teacher.</p>
<p>So, I didn't care about what others thought and let the students be as happy as they wanted.</p>
<p>The next game was a Red vs. White fencing match. Otagi Yuka was going to participate in fencing, but a problem arose. It wasn't our class's problem, but the Red team's problem.</p>
<p>,So, the student who was knocked unconscious by the ball thrown by Seizawa Shii was the fencer for this match?"</p>
<p>The captain of the Red team for the fencing match came to me with a dark complexion. Fencing was a matter of drawing one or two people from each class, but since our class only drew one person, he was saying that we should draw one more person from our class. Since our class was the one that made it impossible for him to participate in the match, we had to take responsibility.</p>
<p>,Okay. Don't worry. I'll send one more person from our class."</p>
<p>,Alik will go, Ryuta."</p>
<p>As soon as I said I would send one more person, Alik, who was standing next to me, stepped forward. Is it okay? Alik is learning a German swordsmanship book that she got from America, but she probably doesn't know much about fencing....</p>
<p>,Alik? Alexandra, right? I'll put your name on the list of participants~!"</p>
<p>The Red team captain for the fencing team was excited about Alexandra's participation and ran to the place where the tournament support office was located. Seeing that it was okay to pick anyone, the student who fainted might not have been very strong.</p>
<p>,Alik, you don't know the basic rules of fencing, do you?"</p>
<p>,Alik, don't know...!"</p>
<p>Is it okay to be so cheerful when you don't know? I sighed and called Otagi Yuka.</p>
<p>,Yes, Teacher-ssi. What's wrong?"</p>
<p>,The player who was supposed to go out for fencing was injured, so Alik is going out instead, can you teach her the rules?"</p>
<p>,That's easy... but since there's not much time left, I have to teach her quickly. Alik, let's go~!"</p>
<p>Since there will be a fencing match right away, it will take quite a while just to teach her the basic rules. Otagi Yuka grabbed the back of Alik's clothes and went to the place where the fencing team was gathered. Alik was quietly carried in Otagi Yuka's hand like a kitten being carried by its mother by the scruff of its neck.</p>
<p>In fact, I think Alik could win even if she doesn't know anything about swordsmanship, let alone fencing, even if she faces all the opponents at the same time, but since there are so many eyes watching, I hope she wears the appearance of fencing. It's a pain in the ass if Minamoto Ushin or Taira Danjo grandpa notice her for no reason.</p>
<p>Regardless of my worries, the fencing match started right away. Fencing is a dangerous game to play in dolphin pants and a white t-shirt, so everyone was well-equipped with fencing clothes. Alik, who suddenly registered, was also wearing fencing clothes that fit her body, whether the fencing club had them or not.</p>
<p>The fencing match was in the foil category, which was a game where you scored points by stabbing the upper body, excluding the arms, head, and legs. I thought that the sabre, which allows cutting and attacking the entire upper body, would be advantageous for Alik, but I sat down, thinking that she would do well in foil as well.</p>
<p>The White team and the Red team each have 9 players and play 9 games, with each game lasting 3 minutes for 2 rounds, and the first to score 15 points wins. If they don't score 15 points during the 2 rounds, the side with the most points wins. Like the archery match in the morning, it was a match where the first to win 5 out of 9 games would win, so the arrangement had to be strategic.</p>
<p>Most of the players were fencing club members, or at least Cheongsim Hall students who knew a little bit about swordsmanship. The White team included Public Morals Committee Chairman Nakanoin Rina, the fencing club president and vice-president, and this side included Alik, Otagi Yuka, and Taira Kanna.</p>
<p>I was wondering if Taira Kanna was good with swords, but I thought she must have come out because she was confident, so I started watching the game as it was. Anyway, Taira Kanna was the vanguard, so my question would be resolved soon.</p>
<p>Since it was a 9-game, 5-win match, the fact that Taira Kanna was in charge of the vanguard meant that she was a considerable powerhouse. For reference, Alik was the last in line. I guess they thought Alik's turn wouldn't come. Even when the captain asked for one more person earlier, he didn't seem to care much about Alik's skills.</p>
<p>,Salut, En Garde! Etês vous Prêts, Allez!"</p>
<p>A woman who appeared to be a referee shouted something. I know that En Garde is preparation, so it probably means something like "Start preparing!"</p>
<p>Taira Kanna raised her sword and stood quietly, looking at her opponent. It wasn't a foil stance at all, but the pressure was no joke. The opponent was the vice-president of the fencing club, by no means a weak opponent. A shout of "Hap!" was heard, and the foil was fired at an incredible speed. Taira Kanna deflected the opponent's sword by swinging the raised sword down and stepped back, stabbing the opponent's chest as it was. 1 point. She scores 1 point in an instant.</p>
<p>,Kuh!"</p>
<p>The fencing club vice-president's flustered expression. As if she couldn't lose like this, she stabs her sword again. This time, she entangles the sword in the stabbing position and bounces it upwards. It was a mystery that I didn't know how to do even after seeing it directly. And in the meantime, a three-hit combo. A beep beep beep sound is heard and 3 points are recognized. Even though the opponent was the vice-president of the fencing club, she was no match for Taira Kanna.</p>
<p>The result of the match was Taira Kanna's victory with a score of 15 to 1. The 1 point that the opponent scored was as if she was conveying the intention that it would be too humiliating if she scored 0 points, so she would give her one point, to the point where the opponent had a desperate expression. I thought it was a bit too much... Well, Taira Kanna must have her own thoughts.</p>
<p>In the next match, the enraged fencing club president stepped up and launched a flawless attack as if to repay Taira Kanna in the same way. The result was 15 to 6. It wasn't as overwhelming as Taira Kanna. The fencing club president came down from his seat with a resentful expression even though he had won.</p>
<p>Following that, our Otagi Yuka and the opponent's Nakanoin Rina each had an overwhelming victory, and the others also shared their wins and losses fairly.</p>
<p>In the end, the score was 4 wins and 4 losses, and the last 9th game became the turning point of this fencing match. The Red team fencing captain, who had only thought of Alik as a member to fill the numbers, sat down on the spot as if in despair. He must have thought there was no chance of winning.</p>
<p>Alik looks like she's asking Otagi Yuka something with a questioning expression. I burst out laughing at the sight. She's probably asking, "Yuka, what's wrong with that kid?" in an innocent way. Otagi Yuka, who heard Alik's words, also smiles like my reaction and puts a fencing helmet on Alik.</p>
<p>Alik slowly climbed onto the arena and held the sword in an awkward position. The opponent was a skilled player who looked like he had been training for several years. Few of the people watching this scene right now would predict Alik's victory.</p>
<p>,Salut, En Garde! Etês vous Prêts, Allez!"</p>
<p>Soon, the referee's shout was heard and the game began.</p>
