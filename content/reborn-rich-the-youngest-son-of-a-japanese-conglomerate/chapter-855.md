<p>Saturday, we gathered at Joseodang's Safe House for a Meeting to counter Anyaji. Although a rough outline had been drawn, we needed to finalize the details today to use Kyoshiro, who was embedded with Anyaji, to leak false Information. So, we decided to determine the exact Date and response by the end of today.</p>
<p>"You mean it's better to fight after Graduation than before, to elicit a definite Reaction from Anyaji?"</p>
<p>"Because the 'St. Francis Girls' Academy Student' label is bigger than you think. If it's a graduate, it's limited to that graduate's individual Family, but attacking a St. Francis Girls' Academy Student feels like attacking a Young Lady of a noble Family, making it an Achievement for the noble Family."</p>
<p>At my Question, Asada Ei answered, adding an appropriate Explanation. Her Words were correct. This could also apply to me. Since Anyaji's Attack was inevitable anyway, there was no harm in our side setting the battle Time after Graduation.</p>
<p>We could avoid the bad reputation of having Students fight, and we wouldn't have to hear stories about St. Francis Girls' Academy being attacked because of us.</p>
<p>"Let's make it the day after. Usually, on Graduation day, Families have a meal together or something.... If we were to gather immediately on that day, they would be suspicious. But if we say we're having a Party or something the day after Graduation, wouldn't they think it's a good opportunity to Attack and come?"</p>
<p>Everyone nodded their Head at my Proposal to make it the day after Graduation. If the Date was too far from Graduation, it would feel strange for the busy Young Ladies of noble Families to remain in Miyashi. And on Graduation day itself, the noble Families would gather in Miyashi with their Guard troops, making it difficult for either our side or Anyaji to carry out an Operation.</p>
<p>The day after Graduation was just right. A time when it wouldn't be awkward for Students to still be in Miyashi, but the noble Families would have left, and their Guards would be absent, leaving them vulnerable. This would be when Anyaji would feel secure and Attack us. We would then exploit that Gap and Counterattack.</p>
<p>"As important as inducing the Attack is scattering the Enemies."</p>
<p>As soon as I finished speaking, Sakagami Rena raised her Hand and spoke. Since we already had Kyoshiro, a broker embedded with Anyaji who could leak false Information to the Enemy, as Sakagami Rena said, we could appropriately leak false Information to divide the Opponent's Attack into several branches and guide their composition to our advantage. Coincidentally, since I also had a PMC Test that day, I thought I could contribute to scattering the Enemy.</p>
<p>"If you check page 12 of the data, it lists Anyaji's key figures sent by Athena Mannerheim-ssi, so let's think about how we can gain an advantage."</p>
<p>Because there was a roster of Anyaji personnel sent by Athena Mannerheim, we could roughly know what kind of Opponents Anyaji had. Not only the Four Heavenly Kings who were active externally, but also the Guardians of the Guardian Hall, who were called Old Monsters, were present, so it was by no means a weak force.</p>
<p>Our side was by no means easy, but I planned to consider the Compatibility with the Opponent and plan the Battle without letting my Guard down.</p>

<p>"Are you only planning to Attack Fujigasaki Rinka's Disciple and Kinui Tachio?"</p>
<p>Takakusaki Athena asked at Takakusaki Gengo's Words. Attacking only two people was easy, but since both had companions, Takakusaki Athena was asking if they would also Attack those companions. And even if Anyaji decided to Attack only those two, their companions would not simply stand by and watch.</p>
<p>"When we attacked Kinui Tachio's Dojang, how many Opponents were there, and who were they?"</p>

<p>Funayama Seigo had attacked Kinui Tachio outside the Dojang, so he didn't know the exact situation inside, but he had heard stories from Anyaji Musung, who had fled after Zant, and Takakusaki Athena, so he intended to explain as the person in charge at the time.</p>
<p>"Excluding Fujigasaki Rinka's Disciple and Kinui Tachio, first, Seizawa Shii, from whom Kyoshiro is getting Information, was on our side at the time, but now that her deception has been caught, she is hostile. <p>A brief Silence followed the name Shimokawabe Airen. In the Japanese ancient martial arts world, Shimokawabe Airen was a Swordsman who was even more famous than Anyaji's Four Heavenly Kings.</p>
<p>"Hehehe! Leave that brat to us."</p>
<p>"Alone, perhaps not, but the two of us can certainly cut down Shimokawabe Airen."</p>
<p>Within Anyaji, there were two middle-aged to elderly Swordsmen from the Guardian Hall who uniquely wielded Swords. The two old Swordsmen were Swordsmen of similar age to Kagiya Haruka, Shimokawabe Airen's Sabu, and their nicknames and names were Kyoken (Mad Dog) Nobuchi Tamehito and Kenju (Sword Beast) Senno Yoridake.</p>
<p>The two, who had been famous in Japan for their exceptional swordsmanship for twenty years, went to China when the Third World War broke out and became known as Kill-Demons for killing at least a four-digit number of people. <p>After the War, they were in Seclusion at Anyaji's Guardian Hall, using their murderous experience to create absolute Swordsmanship. They had recently almost completed their Swordsmanship and were thirsty for slaughter to test it.</p>
<p>The two Swordsmen considered Shimokawabe Airen, who was still in her 20s, to be no match for them. That's why they stepped forward confidently. They already thought she lacked experience compared to them, but now that their new Swordsmanship was complete, they were convinced that Shimokabe Airen was no match for them.</p>
<p>"The ten Enemies we have to face won't all be from one Pabeol, so how are they divided?"</p>
<p>"According to Kyoshiro, there are a total of four factions. Fujigasaki Rinka's Disciple Pabeol, Seizawa Shii's Fixer Pabeol, Kinui Tachio and his Disciple Kujo Kina, and finally, Shimokawabe Airen and her two Disciples."</p>
