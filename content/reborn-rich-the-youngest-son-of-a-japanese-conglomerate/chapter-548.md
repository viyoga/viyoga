<p>"There's one way."</p>
<p>Sakagami Rena said, looking at me. We'd been racking our brains for almost an hour, talking about how to undo Naruse Shiyo's seal, but it seemed she already knew a way.</p>
<p>"Why did you talk for so long if you already knew the solution?"</p>
<p>Otagi Yuka said, sounding a bit annoyed, but Sakagami Rena gave her an apologetic look.</p>
<p>"My method relies on borrowing someone else's power. It would be best if we could solve it ourselves, but since we couldn't find a solution by talking among ourselves, I had no choice but to say it."</p>
<p>"Ah, is that so?"</p>
<p>Otagi Yuka, understanding Sakagami Rena's calm explanation, suppressed her annoyance. Ah... we need to bring in someone else. It seemed she had been quiet until now, hoping we could solve it ourselves, but since we couldn't reach a conclusion, she brought out Plan B.</p>
<p>"Is this person trustworthy?"</p>
<p>"Yes, they're my illusion magic master! They're definitely trustworthy, so they'll help us!"</p>
<p>Ah, she mentioned before that she was going to get advice from someone to learn the illusion magic from the scroll, so it must be that person. If Sakagami Rena, who handles information, trusts them, I should trust them too. Still, I'm curious about the method.</p>
<p>"But what method are you going to use?"</p>
<p>"Um... Shiyo-ssi is under two kinds of rituals: a seal and a sacrifice. It would be difficult but possible to undo one, but the problem is that she's under two spells at the same time. If even one is removed, Naruse-ssi will be in danger."</p>
<p>Sakagami Rena first explained to me, who knew nothing, why the spells on Naruse Shiyo were dangerous. The two spells were in a delicate balance. If the sealing spell was undone, the dragon would tear through Naruse Shiyo's body and burst out, and if the sacrificial spell was undone, it would also release the sealing spell, as the two were closely connected.</p>
<p>But if we just left her alone, she would eventually be eaten by the sealed dragon due to the sacrificial spell, so Naruse Shiyo's future would only lead to death. That's why Naruse Koyou, Naruse Shiyo's father, decided to wait until the sacrificial spell was activated, so that Naruse Shiyo could live a little longer, rather than immediately undoing the seal and causing her death.</p>
<p>"So, I thought, what if we just didn't undo the seal? In the end, what's dangerous is not the dragon's body sealed inside Shiyo-ssi, but the dragon's spirit, which has become extremely violent after being sealed for over a hundred years."</p>
<p>The spirit is scarier than the sealed body... So, is Naruse Shiyo being attacked mentally right now? Well, if the seal isn't broken, there's no chance of the dragon tearing through Naruse Shiyo's body, so the dragon's spirit must be more dangerous.</p>
<p>"What's next?"</p>
<p>"We'll enter Shiyo-ssi's mind and destroy the sealed dragon's spirit. My master will help us with that. In the end, the body moves according to the spirit's commands, so if we destroy the dragon's spirit, it will become quiet."</p>
<p>...Is that so? I'm a layman when it comes to spells, so I don't really know. But Otagi Yuka and Urabe Konoka, who are somewhat knowledgeable about spells, didn't say anything after hearing the explanation, so it seemed like a plausible plan.</p>
<p>"Then ask your illusion magic master if they'll help. I'll call Shiyo's dad."</p>
<p>Sakagami Rena made a call, and I also called Naruse Koyou.</p>
<p>-Yes, Sensei.</p>
<p>"Naruse-ssi. I've found a way to save Shiyo."</p>
<p>-R-Really?</p>
<p>Naruse Koyou seemed very surprised and happy at the same time at my words. But it was too early to be happy. The process of saving Naruse Shiyo would be very difficult. I smiled slightly and opened my mouth.</p>
<p>"Instead, you must become the head of the Naruse family."</p>
<p>###</p>
<p>Naruse Kinji was groaning in a luxurious bed. When a shikigami is destroyed, it inevitably causes tangible and intangible damage to the onmyoji who contracted with that shikigami. Naruse Kinji was also feeling immense pain from the death of his shikigami Tetsuki, which he had inherited from his father.</p>
<p>"Kuh... I'll definitely kill that bastard...!"</p>
<p>He was wearing a helmet, so I couldn't tell who he was, but Naruse Kinji thought that the Naruse family's information network could find out his identity. Once I knew who he was, I could kill him at any time. Even if his power was strong, he would have weaknesses, so I thought I could get rid of him easily.</p>
<p>"Hyung-nim, I brought the medicine."</p>
<p>Naruse Koyou's voice was heard from outside the door. Naruse Koyou was also a weak onmyoji with a shikigami. He seemed to know Naruse Kinji's pain well, so he brought painkillers.</p>
<p>"Come in."</p>
<p>Naruse Kinji used honorifics to strangers, but he spoke informally to his younger brother, Naruse Koyou. From the beginning, this guy and his daughter were like accessories to the family. He didn't see them as the same human beings, so he spoke to them informally, almost disrespectfully.</p>
<p>"Here's the painkiller, Hyung-nim."</p>
<p>"Where's Father?"</p>
<p>Naruse Kinji took the medicine from Naruse Koyou without saying thank you. Naruse Koyou didn't seem to mind the attitude, as if he was used to it.</p>
<p>"Father's meeting at the main house is going to be long, so he'll be back late at night."</p>
<p>"Is that so... I'm going to get a scolding this time. Damn it."</p>
<p>Naruse Hirohide never scolded Naruse Kinji, the next head of the family, no matter how big of an accident he caused, but he wouldn't just let the loss of the shikigami Tetsuki pass. Naruse Hirohide was a man who cherished his shikigami more than his children.</p>
<p>"Who would have known that a man who transcends the strength of Hyung-nim's shikigami would attack? Father will understand that it was an unavoidable situation."</p>
<p>"That's right. It's an unavoidable situation. I hope Father understands that."</p>
<p>Naruse Kinji nodded at Naruse Koyou's words. Yes, it's definitely unavoidable. Who would have known that a disaster in the form of a human would appear? Killing Tetsuki with just punches... Even thinking about it now gave me goosebumps on my arm.</p>
<p>"So... I hope Hyung-nim understands my unavoidable situation too."</p>
<p>"W-What...? Keoeok!"</p>
<p>Naruse Koyou said something incomprehensible and stabbed Naruse Kinji in the neck with a knife hidden in his sleeve.</p>
<p>"If I want to save my daughter, Hyung-nim has to die. I'm sorry, it can't be helped."</p>
<p>"Geureuk, keureureuk!"</p>
<p>Naruse Kinji, unable to leave a will due to the blood constantly flowing from his neck, died in less than a minute.</p>
<p>###</p>
<p>We were heading back to the Naruse mansion. Of course, we were disguised as Naruse Koyou's private soldiers, wearing cyber suits that covered our faces so that our identities couldn't be revealed.</p>
<p>Thanks to Taira Kanna, who had asked the disciplinary committee's field agents to wear the highest-end cyber suits, we wouldn't be injured by the submachine guns used by the Naruse family's guards.</p>
<p>"Sakagami, when will your illusion magic master arrive?"</p>
<p>"They're a bit far away... I think they'll arrive within thirty minutes?"</p>
<p>When I asked Sakagami Rena when her illusion magic master, the most important person to save Naruse Shiyo's life, would arrive, she replied that they would arrive within 30 minutes. It had been about two hours and thirty minutes since I called earlier, so it seemed like they were coming from quite a distance. Judging by the arrival time, they probably lived in eastern Tokyo, Nagano, or Nagoya.</p>
<p>"Welcome."</p>
<p>Unlike before, the guards didn't give us a warning or shoot at us, but politely opened the gate. From the beginning, we had chosen the time when the most guards who supported Naruse Koyou were on duty. That way, we could infiltrate the mansion quietly without causing a commotion.</p>
<p>As we entered through the main gate, Naruse Koyou was wiping his blood-soaked hands and looking at us. The blood on Naruse Koyou's hands was the blood of Naruse Kinji, the next head of the Naruse family, who had annoyed me earlier. Since Naruse Kinji had already lost his shikigami to me, it wouldn't have been too difficult to deal with him.</p>
<p>"You've made a decision."</p>
<p>"Even if he's my Hyung-nim, he's the one who contributed greatly to making my daughter like that."</p>
<p>Naruse Koyou answered with a bitter smile at my words.</p>
<p>"Where is the head of the family?"</p>
<p>"He's probably in the head's hall."</p>
<p>Our plan was simple. Unlike other families, Naruse Koyou would win over the elders of the Naruse family, who couldn't use their power. The Naruse family's elders usually had no political power due to the head's strong authority and power, but they had immense authority in one situation.</p>
<p>That was when the head of the family was critically ill and there was no next head of the family to take over the position, they had the right to decide the head of the family. The next head of the family was decided by the opinions of the elders, and with the help of the elders, they could forcibly transfer the shikigami contract held by the critically ill head of the family.</p>
<p>Usually, there was no chance that the head of the family, who handled many shikigami, would be in danger, and there was almost no chance that the next head of the Naruse family would die, so it was a nominal authority, but today's situation was perfect. Naruse Kinji, the next head of the family, had been killed by Naruse Koyou, and there was a high probability that Naruse Hirohide would also die by my hand today.</p>
<p>While I, Saionji Diana, Otagi Yuka, and Alik, who had high combat power, faced Naruse Hirohide, the head of the Naruse family, Urabe Konoka, Sakagami Rena, and Konoe Akina would protect the sealing formation where Naruse Shiyo was trapped. If Naruse Hirohide was cornered, he might just release Naruse Shiyo's seal and die together, so there had to be people to protect the seal.</p>
<p>If we put the Naruse family head in a near-death state, Naruse Koyou would receive the surviving shikigami of the family head with the consent of the elders and become the family head, and we would enter Naruse Shiyo's mind with the help of Sakagami Rena's illusion magic master and deal with the dragon's spirit, then everything would be completed.</p>
<p>"Okay, let's start the Shiyo rescue as planned."</p>
<p>"Okay, Sensei-ssi!"</p>
<p>"I'll protect Shiyo-ssi well, my lord."</p>
<p>"Ryuta, can I swing my sword?"</p>
<p>"Be careful, Sensei!"</p>
<p>We strengthened our resolve, and the combat team headed to where Naruse Hirohide, the head of the Naruse family, was, and the remaining protection team headed to the magic circle where Naruse Shiyo was sleeping. Naruse Koyou ran to the designated place to subdue the guards who were not in his faction.</p>
