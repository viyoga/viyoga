<p>Taira Ryuta, while preparing for the battle with Anyaji, outwardly adopted an Attitude that he could win, but in truth, he thought he was just a little short on power for Victory. Having newly heard from Athena that there were over a dozen Old Monsters from Anyaji's armed group, the Guardian Hall, even including the St. Francis Girls' Academy Special Class Students, Student Council members, Shimokawabe Airen, and Kinui Tachio, and no matter how much they knew the Opponent's plan, it was a Situation where they couldn't claim a superior power. So, he requested Help from some of the authorized PMC personnel guarding Miyashi's police and wealthy residents, ensuring it wouldn't be perceived as a new PMC or armed group invasion. Among them were Kurayamada Kirie and Hangari Muneo Hanakame.</p>
<p>Kurayamada Kirie was immediately recruited because her Skill was already known, and Hangari Muneo Hanakame was brought into the battle after hearing from Urabe Konoka, during a Conversation with Lizhael, that Hanakame also practiced the Creation Chaos Style.</p>
<p>Hanakame, even before being called Hangari Muneo, had been practicing the ancient martial arts passed down at the Shinkai Hime Shrine on Chooten Island as a Shrine Maiden and shrine keeper, and when Simyeonui ttal metamorphosed into Lizhael, she began to learn Lizhael's martial art, the Creation Chaos Style. Of course, Hanakame had only mastered the Early Stage of the Creation Chaos Style, as she hadn't practiced it for a long period in Lizhael's time-stopped World like Taira Ryuta.</p>
<p>Having originally practiced martial arts for decades and never neglecting her training, Hanakame was a considerable Skill user. And because Lizhael had possessed outstanding physical Ability since her Simyeonui ttal days, influenced by an Outer God, she could become the additional power Taira Ryuta desired. As Lizhael couldn't directly Help Taira Ryuta due to various restrictions, sending Hanakame to Help Taira Ryuta was the best option.</p>
<p>At Urabe Konoka's Words that her friend was in Danger, Hanakame ran at maximum speed. She leaped over all the stairs at once, descending floor by floor, exited the commercial building, and immediately entered the abandoned Orphanage to assess the Situation. Seizawa Shii and Sakagami Rena were barely holding on. No, Funayama Seigo was tormenting them like a Cat playing with a Mouse, not yet killing them, which was why they were barely enduring.</p>
<p>"You...!" Funayama Seigo looked at Hanakame, who was suddenly rushing in with terrifying Momentum. While Funayama Seigo was watching Hanakame, Seizawa Shii and Sakagami Rena widened their distance from Funayama Seigo.</p>
<p>-Creation Chaos Style Grand Cannon. Hanakame immediately extended her Palm, as if Conversation was unnecessary. Lizhael's unique Aura, shifting between deep blue and purple, poured out from Hanakame's Palm like an Energy cannon.</p>
<p>-Sorcery Fist (Curse Fist) Grudge Kill (Resentment Kill) Soul Eruption (Soul Burst) Faced with an attack that came without warning, Funayama Seigo also released a dark red aura from his hand. It was a technique that consumed the soul of a secret Buddhist monk who had given his heart to him, projecting spiritual power from the center of his palm.</p>
<p>Kwaaaah! When the two Auras clashed, an enormous Explosion occurred. But as if unaffected by the Explosion, they rushed at each other and began to exchange Martial Arts Forms. With the sound of air tearing, when a Fist was swung, Funayama Seigo dodged, and with a 'pang' sound of air bursting, when Funayama Seigo's Feet were swung, Hanakame also retreated slightly, dodging the kick. They both did their best to evade, as if knowing the immense power of each other's attacks without having to be directly hit. Thus, the Skill of the two, exchanging many Martial Arts Forms, was equal. Hanakame's precision in technique was higher, but Funayama Seigo's speed and power, enhanced by Sacrifice Sorcery, more than compensated for his lack of technique.</p>
<p>Unfortunately for Funayama Seigo, Hanakame had three colleagues. Sakagami Rena threw Surigeom and sprayed Flame or Ice with her concealment techniques, and Urabe Konoka's Arrows flew in at opportune moments. Seizawa Shii also watched for Funayama Seigo's openings with Killing Intent gleaming in her Eyes.</p>
<p>"Kueuk! Four of you ganging up, how underhanded...!" "Hmph, isn't it more underhanded to possess the power of over a dozen people alone through Sacrifice Sorcery!" Funayama Seigo, his limbs growing increasingly disoriented, shouted that it was underhanded, but Sakagami Rena's sharp retort left him speechless. In truth, having gained power through Sorcery that sacrificed over a dozen secret Buddhist monks, it wasn't really a 4-on-1 Match.</p>
<p>- Fire Release (火遁) Explosive Flame Orb (爆焰球). Sakagami Rena, having retorted to Funayama Seigo, deployed her concealment technique and threw an explosive Flame Orb. As if they had coordinated beforehand, Hanakame and Seizawa Shii dodged, and a huge Explosion erupted in response. Kwakwakwang!! "Kwaaaah!" Amidst the tremendous explosion sound, Funayama Seigo's Scream was heard. Sakagami Rena then activated her Wind Release technique to push away the smoke. They couldn't wait for the smoke and Dust to settle. Sakagami Rena and Seizawa Shii had little Stamina left from the battle so far, so they had to deal with Funayama Seigo as quickly as possible.</p>
<p>As the Wind pushed away the smoke, Funayama Seigo's Appearance was a mess. His entire Body was scorched, and some parts of his flesh were completely burned due to the Explosion. Most notably, an Arrow shot by Urabe Konoka was embedded in his lower back, and a dark red Aura was leaking from that spot as if seeping out. Sakagami Rena had used her full-power explosive Fire Release, and Urabe Konoka had shot an Arrow, seizing the Gap while Funayama Seigo's Gaze was drawn to the explosive orb.</p>
<p>"Keuheok! Everyone, I'll kill everyone!" Swaeeeeeek Thud! As Funayama Seigo declared he would kill them and tried to chant a spell, another Arrow flew and embedded itself in his Body. Seizawa Shii's Party was not one to miss such an Opportunity. Sakagami Rena watched Funayama Seigo, intending to disrupt his spell if it activated, while Seizawa Shii and Hanakame simultaneously rushed at Funayama Seigo and began to stomp him. As long as some ominous Sorcery remained, they had to completely crush him to prevent any further defiance.</p>
<p>Kwadeudeuk! As Seizawa Shii's Fist completely crushed Funayama Seigo's Jawbone, Funayama Seigo's Eyes rolled back, and he began to tremble violently.</p>
<p>"You kept spouting Annoying Words, let's see if you can do it after I rip out your Tongue." Seizawa Shii, still unsatisfied even after completely crushing his Jaw, began to pull out Funayama Seigo's Tongue. With an unpleasant 'ppudeudeuk ppudeudeuk' sound, Seizawa Shii completely ripped out Funayama Seigo's Tongue, then threw it onto the Ground and stomped on it with her Feet as if extinguishing a cigarette, leaving no trace of its form. Funayama Seigo stared blankly at his mangled Tongue, then completely ceased all movement.</p>
<p>Since Seizawa Shii and Hanakame had stomped Funayama Seigo like mincing meat, it was natural that he could no longer endure.</p>
<p>"I promised Grandfather Yoda. That in exchange for yielding Funayama Seigo, I would kill him as painfully as possible." When Sakagami Rena and Hanakame looked at Seizawa Shii with an Expression that seemed to ask, 'Isn't that a bit too much?', Seizawa Shii explained her promise with Grandfather Yoda, Kinui Tachio, to them. Seizawa Shii had received Funayama Seigo as a concession from Kinui Tachio in this fight. It was a considerably large concession, as Funayama Seigo was the one who had killed Kinui Tachio's Disciple.</p>
<p>Of course, Hojo Otoji had planned everything, and Funayama Seigo had merely finished it, but Funayama Seigo had mocked Kinui Tachio with his characteristic polite language, tearing apart the Heart of a Master who had lost his Disciple. Having heard this Story, however briefly, Seizawa Shii had promised Kinui Tachio to kill Funayama Seigo as painfully as possible. Now that the promise was kept, Seizawa Shii began to worry slightly.</p>
<p>Funayama Seigo was such a formidable Existence that they would have been Defeated if Hanakame hadn't appeared. But then, how strong would Hojo Otoji, Funayama Seigo's teacher/master and the second-in-command of Anyaji, be? Seizawa Shii had faced Kinui Tachio during the previous Anyaji Attack, so she roughly knew Kinui Tachio's Skill. She guessed he was similar to her or slightly stronger, but she didn't think such a Kinui Tachio could handle Hojo Otoji.</p>
<p>"If Funayama Seigo is this strong, I think I should go Help Grandfather Yoda." When Seizawa Shii spoke with an urgent Expression, Sakagami Rena smiled as if to say it was alright and shook her Head.</p>
<p>"Of course, we'll know for sure after hearing the Information from our central command center, but that side will probably be fine. Besides Grandfather Yoda and Kina, that side has many colleagues."</p>
<p>Seizawa Shii only needed to focus on her own battle, but Sakagami Rena was one of the few who understood the overall Situation of today's fight, as one of the three Executives of Taira Ryuta's intelligence team, the Jooseodang. Sakagami Rena knew well that Kinui Tachio's team, nicknamed the Yoda Team, had the highest Win Rate in this fight against Hojo Otoji, with the exception of special circumstances.</p>
<p>"Colleagues? What do you mean?" "Well..." In response to Seizawa Shii's Question, Sakagami Rena began to explain kindly. Only after hearing that Explanation could Seizawa Shii feel Sweet Relief.</p>
