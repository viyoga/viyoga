<p>After having a Showdown with the old man Danjo and leaving the restaurant, I met Taira Kokoro and Taira Kanna in the restaurant Lobby.</p>
<p>"Orabeoni!"</p>
<p>"Ryuta, did your conversation with Father end well? And Kanna, calling your Nephew 'Orabeoni'... what is this, 'Orabeoni'...."</p>
<p>Taira Kanna waved her Hand at me and called me 'Orabeoni', while Taira Kokoro questioned me and, at the same time, scolded Taira Kanna about the Address issue.</p>
<p>"Even though he's my Nephew, he's older than me, so he's my Orabeoni. Please just let it slide until Father arrives."</p>
<p>"This girl, really...!"</p>
<p>If I just stood by, Taira Kanna and Taira Kokoro would probably keep arguing until Taira Danjo arrived. If that happened, I might get caught in the crossfire again, so I decided to change the theme.</p>
<p>"First, the conversation ended well. You'll probably see me again at the Taira Group Meeting."</p>
<p>"Hmph.... So you're finally going to formally Work for the Taira Group?"</p>
<p>"I was an employee at the Taira Group Talent Development Center even before this mission, you know?"</p>
<p>She must not have been very interested in me. Did she think I was an Unemployed Person before the mission?</p>
<p>"I didn't know that? Did you receive something from Father?"</p>
<p>She really didn't know. This Aunt seemed to have paid no attention to me at all. Of course, now she at least knows that I'm in charge of Hatake Investment Company, but since it's not an industry that overlaps with Taira Kokoro's, she's probably just pretending not to care.</p>
<p>"Well. Things changed during the mission, so I'm not sure what I'll receive either."</p>
<p>In fact, since today's mission had changed from seducing Minamoto Lisa to preventing the union of the Sugawara and Minamoto families, there was a possibility that the Reward would change, so I didn't know what I would receive at the Taira Meeting in March. Of course, even if I knew, I wouldn't have told Taira Kokoro right away.</p>
<p>"Hmm.... Ryuta, as your Aunt, I'll just say this: be careful of your two Uncles. Those men stop at nothing. We're not very close, but still, I don't want my Nephew to get hurt like Saburo, do I?"</p>
<p>Was this Advice for me, or a Warning not to side with Ichiro and Jiro? Judging by her Expression, it seemed to be a Worry that her insignificant Nephew shouldn't get caught in a whale fight. I suppose I should just accept it as Advice then.</p>
<p>"Yes, thank you for the Advice, Aunt. Well, since I'm still Yamashiro Ryuta, it would be a bit awkward to be seen with Grandfather, so I'll be going. Goodbye, Aunt Kanna."</p>
<p>I didn't know when Taira Danjo would come out, so I cut the conversation short and said my goodbyes. Taira Kokoro nodded her Head as if she understood and waved her Hand, telling me to go, and Taira Kanna chirped, "Orabeoni, see you later!"</p>
<p>I got into the vehicle I had arrived in at the restaurant Parking Lot and headed to the cafe where Saionji Diana and Otagi Yuka were waiting. There was a Parking space in front of the cafe, so I parked the car and went inside to find them sitting and talking.</p>
<p>"I'm here."</p>
<p>"Did the conversation go well?"</p>
<p>As soon as Saionji Diana saw me, she asked, and I nodded my Head as I sat down. Seeing me nod, both of them let out a Sigh of Relief. That's how much the name Taira Danjo had become a Symbol of Awe for Japanese People.</p>
<p>He's just a bad-tempered old man, you know. Oh, of course, if that bad-tempered human possesses the Financial Power and power to rival Japan's best, then he'd naturally be terrifying.</p>
<p>"Yeah, don't Worry about the old man Danjo's matter."</p>
<p>"Could you please stop calling him 'old man Danjo'? It's a little scary, you know?"</p>
<p>"That's right! And when Teacher-nim speaks so casually, it's even more...."</p>
<p>"Even more?"</p>
<p>"You're like a Young Master."</p>
<p>A Young Master.... I chuckled at Saionji Diana's Words. In fact, I am a modern Noble, but it felt somehow unfitting for me.</p>
<p>"What are you going to do now?"</p>
<p>"Me? I have a lot of Work tomorrow, so I was planning to send you two home, let you rest, and then prepare for tomorrow's Work."</p>
<p>"Right now...?"</p>
<p>"There's no one at my Home!"</p>
<p>Both of them were incredibly disappointed when I said I'd send them home. Thinking that all their other Friends would be celebrating Graduation with their families, they seemed to dislike being alone.</p>
<p>"Then, why don't you stay at Our Home until Evening?"</p>
<p>"We should, Teacher-ssi!"</p>
<p>"As expected, Teacher-nim is the best!"</p>
<p>I took the two noisy girls Outside, put them in the car, and drove home. In fact, there was nothing specific to prepare for tomorrow's Battle. I also have a PMC certification Test starting tomorrow morning, but since I already passed it once under Yamashiro Ryuta's name a few months ago, I wasn't nervous at all.</p>
<p>In the end, Otagi Yuka and Saionji Diana tormented me until almost Dawn before returning to their respective homes. I woke up in the early Morning before Dawn had ended, had coffee and toast, showered, and prepared my electric bike.</p>
<p>Since I had charged it yesterday, I would be able to go back and forth from Kofu to Miyashi. After completing all preparations, I packed my writing tools and Identification Card needed for the Test into the bike's Trunk and started the engine.</p>
<p>When I returned to this Miyashi Home, Anya-ji's matter would be settled. I looked around the Miyashi Home, which I would soon be leaving, and then started the electric bike.</p>
<p>Shido Tsubaki packed a gift set and stepped Outside with a nervous Expression. Today was the day she was scheduled to meet Seiwa Rieko.</p>
<p>The person she had unintentionally shocked, Seiwa Rieko, who had even dropped out of school because of it, left a deep trace of Guilt in Shido Tsubaki's mind. This incident made Shido Tsubaki deeply consider the ripple effect her Knight could have.</p>
<p>Seiwa Rieko's Home was quite far away. The Seiwa Family was a family that barely qualified as a prestigious family, so it was impossible for them to find a Home near St. Francis Girls' Academy, where land prices were expensive. So, Shido Tsubaki took a Taxi.</p>
<p>"Hoo...."</p>
<p>Shido Tsubaki got out of the Taxi, let out a nervous Sigh, and pressed the Doorbell of a not-so-large house. Then her phone vibrated, and a Text Message arrived. Checking her phone, it was a Text Message from Seiwa Rieko.</p>
<p>-Seiwa Rieko: The door is open, just come in.</p>
<p>Is there no one else at home? Shido Tsubaki thought that as she opened the door. As per Seiwa Rieko's Text, the door was not locked. She wondered if it was okay to leave the door open like this, even in Miyashi, which was famous for its good Public Order, but Shido Tsubaki took off her shoes at the front door, thinking that even this thought might be rude.</p>
<p>"Rieko...?"</p>
<p>"This way."</p>
<p>As Shido Tsubaki cautiously called her name, Seiwa Rieko's Voice came from somewhere. As she moved her Footsteps towards the Voice, Shido Tsubaki realized that the Inside the House was a bit dark. She wondered if the lights weren't on because it was Morning, and as she moved her Footsteps again, Seiwa Rieko was waiting for Shido Tsubaki in front of a certain room.</p>
<p>"Long time no see."</p>
<p>Seiwa Rieko smiled and greeted Shido Tsubaki. Seiwa Rieko, wearing only an oversized Shirt suitable for comfortable wear at home, had Dark Circles under her Eyes. Shido Tsubaki remembered that even when she was in school, she had Dark Circles because of the former Class Teacher's Sexual harassment, so it seemed her Appearance hadn't changed much, except for being thinner than before.</p>
<p>"Here, a gift...."</p>
<p>Shido Tsubaki held out the gift. The gift was a set of high-quality Western confectionery that Shido Tsubaki had carefully chosen, but Seiwa Rieko received the gift and thanked her without checking what it was.</p>
<p>"Um, your Parents?"</p>
<p>"They don't live here. I've been living here alone since 'that incident.' They come about once a week, though...."</p>
<p>'That incident'.... It was an uncomfortable theme, but it was a Story Shido Tsubaki had to face. So, it seemed Seiwa Rieko was living alone in this house.</p>
<p>"Um...."</p>
<p>"Sorry, but could I change my Clothes? I overslept a bit today, you see."</p>
<p>Seiwa Rieko cut off Shido Tsubaki's Words and spoke with a Smile. Surely, Seiwa Rieko wouldn't want to greet a customer wearing only a Shirt.</p>
<p>"Yes, change your Clothes."</p>
<p>"Could you wait in the living room?"</p>
<p>"Okay."</p>
<p>"Thank you."</p>
<p>Kwang, the door closed.</p>
<p>When she turned and walked a few steps towards the living room, a rather loud deolkeong! sound came from Seiwa Rieko's room. Shido Tsubaki felt an unknown ominousness from that deolkeong sound.</p>
<p>Shido Tsubaki turned and ran to Seiwa Rieko's room. It was only a few steps, but it felt like a very long time.</p>
<p>Cheolkkeok!</p>
<p>The door was not locked. When Shido Tsubaki opened the door, she saw a dark room with the curtains drawn. And Seiwa Rieko was hanging from a Rope around her Neck, slumped.</p>
<p>"No!!!"</p>
<p>Shido Tsubaki involuntarily let out a Scream and grabbed Seiwa Rieko's thigh, lifting her up. Seiwa Rieko was quite thin, so Shido Tsubaki, being a Woman, could hold her up for a moment. Then she looked around for something to cut the Rope with, but it was too dark to see.</p>
<p>She vaguely saw a Chair lying on the floor. Shido Tsubaki held Seiwa Rieko with one Hand and lifted the Chair with the other, placing it under Seiwa Rieko so she could stand on it.</p>
<p>After placing the Chair and catching her Breath for a moment, Shido Tsubaki turned on the room light. Then, she supported Seiwa Rieko with one Hand to prevent her from falling off the Chair again and looked for something to cut the Rope.</p>
<p>Shido Tsubaki found a utility Knife in a dusty pencil case, took it out, climbed onto the Chair Seiwa Rieko was standing on, and cut the Rope.</p>
<p>With a sguk-sguk sound, the Rope was cut, then completely snapped, unable to bear the weight. Shido Tsubaki carefully laid Seiwa Rieko on the bed and untied the Rope from her Neck.</p>
<p>What should she do now?</p>
<p>Seiwa Rieko was dying. Had she become so weak that she would be like this just a few seconds after hanging herself...?</p>
<p>Shido Tsubaki worried. Even if she called 119, it didn't seem like Seiwa Rieko would survive. Calculating the time it would take for 119 to arrive and the time to get to the Hospital, it seemed she would pass away in between.</p>
