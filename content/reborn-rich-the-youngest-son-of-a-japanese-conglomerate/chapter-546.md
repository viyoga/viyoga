<p>Naruse Ginji's summoned Shikigami, named Tetsuki, truly lived up to its name (鐵鬼: Iron Demon) with its metallic skin. It had gleaming, chrome-like silver skin, four arms with sharp, sword-like claws, and its head was adorned with a Japanese samurai helmet, also shining with a metallic luster. "You wear a samurai Helmet, yet you use Claws like a bitch? For a Shikigami used by the next Gaju of the Naruse Family, it's quite pathetic!" This remark was less to provoke the Tetsuki before me, and more to provoke Naruse Ginji, the Shikigami's Master. As expected, perhaps annoyed by my Words, the gentle Smile he had maintained until now stiffened slightly. Huung! Of course, even then, Tetsuki was attacking, swinging its blade-like Claws to tear me apart. Its speed was incredibly fast, with enough Power to grind down infantry armed only with bulletproof vests. But it lacked the logic to contend with martial artists, experts in Rhythmic Motion. Its Attack speed was very fast, but its movements were so large that martial artists who had reached a certain Realm would be able to evade it sufficiently. Of course, whether merely evading could destroy that metallic Body was another Story, but I felt I could certainly do it. First, without using any Martial Arts Form, I swung my Arm with a fist imbued only with the Abyss, then struck its wide-open Side. Jjeong! Being a metallic Body, it was certainly sturdy. For a Layperson, it would have been a blow strong enough to rupture their Internal Organs like a violently thrown water balloon, but it only ended with its Side caving in. "Kueeeoeoeoeh!!" Tetsuki belatedly let out a monstrous scream and swung its Claws at me, but I had already moved away from where its Claws passed. Enraged by the Attack against itself, Tetsuki wildly swung its Claws, and I, too, now began to seriously swing my fists and Legs, smashing Tetsuki. On the contrary, its very hardness made it satisfying to strike. My fist Marks continuously appeared on its metallic Body, and as it took more hits from me, its Stamina seemed to decrease, and Tetsuki's Rhythmic Motion gradually slowed. I gazed at Tetsuki's Body, which was cracking from my punches, then extended a finger and thrust it into a cracked spot. Shinkaiyatsude (Deep Sea Eight Arms) Isshitenkan (One Finger Piercing the Sky) Isshitenkan was a penetrating martial arts technique that delivered a Shock to the Body's Interior by thrusting a finger. The Abyss's ripple, starting from where the finger was thrust, grew larger and larger, destroying Tetsuki's Interior, and the crack on its outer Body also began to widen. Tetsuki couldn't hold out much longer and burst apart with an explosion of metallic fragments. Naruse Ginji's Face turned pale the Moment his Shikigami was destroyed with a gasp of surprise. He must have been incredibly surprised that it suddenly exploded after merely being poked with a finger. "Now, hand over the ninja." This Match, from the start, was to acquire Sakagami Rena. I left the shattered Tetsuki behind and approached Naruse Ginji. It didn't Matter if Naruse Ginji didn't hand over Sakagami Rena. If I threatened Naruse Ginji, the next Gaju, with his life, he would quickly give her up. "Th-that can't be...!" As expected, he didn't immediately obey. Perhaps because his Shikigami had been destroyed, Naruse Ginji took no particular action even as I approached. Since he had already allowed me the distance, I instantly closed in and grabbed Naruse Ginji's Face. "You saw that hunk of metal shatter, didn't you? Shall I crush your Face the same way?" "N-no! Don't...! I'll release her! I'll release her, so...!" "Tell that Security Guard to bring her here." The Security Guard who had brought me here had completely lost his Will to fight after I dealt with Tetsuki. Even if he'd lost his Will to fight, shouldn't he at least point a Gun if the next Gaju is captured? "Th-that... Bring the ninja!" "Y-yes, understood!" Even after Naruse Ginji shouted and the Security Guard disappeared somewhere, I still held Naruse Ginji's Face. I could have released him, but because he had annoyed me earlier, I continued to apply pressure to my Hand, inflicting continuous pain. "I-I've brought her." After waiting a bit, listening to Naruse Ginji's moans, the Security Guard returned, and with him was the man holding Sakagami Rena. I knew that man's Face. It was Naruse Koyou, Naruse Shiyo's Father, whose Information had recently appeared in the Taira Group's data. "Release my older brother." Seeing him call Naruse Ginji "older brother," it seemed he was indeed Naruse Koyou. "If you release her first, I will release him." "Understood." Naruse Koyou immediately released Sakagami Rena at my Words. Sakagami Rena quickly ran to me. "Liege/Master...!" "Are you okay? Are you hurt anywhere?" "Yes.... I ran away as much as I could before being caught, so they didn't have much time to torment me." "That's Fortunate." It seemed she had run for a long time to buy as much time as possible even after being caught. Still, it's Fortunate that she's not hurt.
