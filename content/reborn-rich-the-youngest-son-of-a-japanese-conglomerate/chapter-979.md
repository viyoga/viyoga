<p>Mithril was a Metal with a special Position in this Worldview. It was a precious ingredient that was an artifact of cutting-edge technology and was unconditionally included in top-tier crafting items. Moreover, mines were extremely rare, so its Price was bound to be quite high.</p>
<p>The fact that I supplied such a precious ingredient in quantities sufficient for Taira Steel to use was clearly surprising for my Cousins to hear. Even now, Taira Gensuke, who had been so confident, was staring at me with his Mouth agape.</p>
<p>Of course, while my Cousins learned about me, I also learned something here: that Parents engaged in a Succession War do not share Information with their children. Of course, it might also be that they didn't share information because they didn't consider information about me to be that important.</p>
<p>"You did well to contract with a place that can supply the goods well. Since you did well this time, try managing Taira Railway."</p>
<p>"Thank you!"</p>
<p>Taira Railway was a railway company managed by the Taira Group. Recently, with the Taira Railway-managed Subway also entering Neo Tokyo, it was a company with considerable expected profitability, so it could be said to be quite a large Reward for Taira Jiro. Satisfied with this Reward, Taira Jiro bowed his Head to Taira Danjo with a beaming Expression.</p>
<p>"Next is Kokoro."</p>
<p>Since Taira Jiro's Wife, Taira Moko, was lying in a Hospital Room, he immediately moved on to Taira Kokoro. Now that I think about it, Taira Kokoro is older than Taira Jiro, so why did he do Jiro first? Was it not in order of Age? Or was it simply Taira Danjo's Mistake?</p>
<p>"You're still doing well. The new Cosmetics Business you took on is also doing well.... However, since you failed the mission, there will be no Reward."</p>
<p>Taira Danjo, who initially praised her, then became serious. Taira Kokoro also seemed to have received a mission, like me and Taira Kanna, and failed it. Taira Kokoro nodded her Head with an accepting Expression. Although she failed the mission, the fact that she continues to be recognized by Taira Danjo in Business suggests that her Management Capability is quite outstanding.</p>
<p>Next in Turn was Taira Katsuto, the Son-in-law of the Taira Family and Taira Kokoro's Husband. Although his Expression was shadowed, he was a man with a decent Appearance, but rumors suggested that his relationship with Taira Kokoro was not good.</p>
<p>"Katsuto."</p>
<p>"Yes, Father-in-Law-nim...."</p>
<p>Taira Katsuto looked at Taira Danjo with a slightly intimidated Expression. Taira Kokoro glanced at him, seemingly displeased with her Husband's Attitude, and her Daughter, Taira Kisa, also let out a small Sigh.</p>
<p>"I hope the Movie opening this time does well."</p>
<p>"It... will do well."</p>
<p>Taira Katsuto was the Boss of Katsuto Production, a Movie Producer created with the Taira Group's Capital, but he hadn't had any major successful Movies apart from two or three hits so far. Given that Katsuto Production was a Movie Producer established when he married Taira Kokoro, having only two or three hits over those years would mean that Taira Katsuto's Capability was lacking or the Taira Group's Support was insufficient. But since the wealthy Taira Group's Support wouldn't be lacking, ultimately, what was lacking was Taira Katsuto's Capability. Taira Danjo said nothing more to Taira Katsuto and moved on to the next Turn. Since the Movie hadn't been released yet, he couldn't comment on the Result.</p>
<p>"Yukiko is doing well, and Kanna was mentioned at the beginning, so we'll skip her.... Did Tabenobu not come this time?"</p>
<p>Yukiko, as the head of the Talent Development Institute, seemed to be skipped because it's not a place evaluated by Achievement, and Kanna seemed to be skipped because she received the Taira Driving System as a Reward for her mission, celebrating her becoming an Adult in the early stage.</p>
<p>"He said he couldn't come because Work in the USA was too busy."</p>
<p>Taira Ichiro answered Taira Danjo's Words. Taira Tabenobu, Taira Ichiro's First Child, was responsible for Taira Bussan's North American Branch. He was highly capable and was praised for managing Taira Bussan's North American Branch well at a young Age. Unlike Taira Gensuke, who was large and of Mixed Blood, making his Appearance look somewhat different from Taira Ichiro, he was a Son who strongly resembled Taira Ichiro in Appearance. As the Sons of Taira Ichiro's deceased Wife, Taira Sayo, Taira Tabenobu and Taira Gensuke, who had different Mothers, were rumored to have a very poor relationship. It could be said to be a similar situation to how Taira Ichiro and Taira Kokoro disliked Taira Jiro, who had a different Mother.</p>
<p>"Next is.... Gensuke. As I heard earlier, you significantly increased the market share of the South American Branch, didn't you? Well done."</p>
<p>"Haha, it's all thanks to the Group's Help."</p>
<p>Although he gave a humble Answer to Grandfather Taira Danjo's Praise, Taira Gensuke had a very proud Expression.</p>
<p>"Next quarter, I will increase the Budget for the South American Branch."</p>
<p>"Thank you, Grandfather~!"</p>
<p>As expected, when it came to the Grandson's Faction, the Rewards were a bit stingier. For the Son's Faction, affiliate companies were given as Rewards, but for the Grandson's Faction, it was merely an increase in Budget. Of course, it's natural since what they bear and what they achieve differ according to their generational rank.</p>
<p>"Next up is...."</p>
<p>After Taira Gensuke, there were Taira Ichiro's Daughters, Taira Akane and Taira Reiko, but Taira Akane was helping her Mother with her Travel agency Work, and Taira Reiko was still a University Student. Taira Danjo offered simple words of encouragement to his two Granddaughters, then looked at Taira Kokoro's Daughter, Taira Kisa.</p>
<p>"Kisa, it's quite remarkable that you've achieved this much without significant Help from the Family."</p>
<p>Taira Kisa's company was not affiliated with the Taira Group. She had launched her own Clothing brand company and was doing well. Of course, she received startup Capital from Taira Danjo's Allowance (?) and her Mother's Support, but from Taira Danjo's perspective, he didn't count that as Support, so he seemed to evaluate Taira Kisa quite highly. She was quite close with her Aunt Nae Fitzgerald, who wasn't present. Taira Kisa respected her Aunt, who ran a global Luxury Brand company, and Nae Fitzgerald seemed quite proud of Taira Kisa, who was working hard to succeed without the Family's Help. Taira Kisa also smiled while seated at her Grandfather's Praise.</p>
<p>"I'm happy that Grandfather, the top entrepreneur in Japan, praised me. This year, I think I'll be competing with Mother. I'm thinking of entering the perfume and Cosmetics Business."</p>
<p>She intends to expand beyond the Clothing brand. If Taira Kisa has ambitions for the Taira Group like her Mother Taira Kokoro, she seems quite smart. Because she can increase her own Faction outside and use the surname 'Taira' to enter the Group at any time. If she has no ambition for the Taira Group, then she simply inherited her Mother's excellent Business acumen.</p>
<p>"Alright. Do well in your competition. After the Meal, come find this old man, and I'll give you some Allowance."</p>
<p>"Wow~! Grandfather is the best!"</p>
<p>In fact, Taira Kisa was considerably older than his Daughter Taira Kanna, but because Taira Kanna had a somewhat indifferent feeling except when dealing with me, Taira Danjo pleasantly accepted his Granddaughter's Aegyo.</p>
<p>"Akio, are you studying Photography diligently?"</p>
<p>Taira Akio, Taira Kisa's younger sibling, was a free Soul. He majored in Photography and traveled the World taking Photos, using studying abroad as an Excuse. Perhaps because his Family was Taira, allowing him to live leisurely taking Photos, he seemed to have no worries.</p>
<p>"Yes, I took some Photos to give to Grandfather this time."</p>
<p>"Alright. Show them to me later."</p>
<p>Taira Danjo also didn't particularly tell Taira Akio to do Business. He was strict with his children, Grandson, and Granddaughters who were in charge of the Taira Group's Business, but seemed somewhat more lenient with those who lived independently of Business. No, it should be said he paid less attention to them.</p>
<p>Next was the Turn of Taira Jiro's children, but none of them came today. Taira Yuuichi, Taira Jiro's First Child, was working as a manager at Taira Shipyard in the USA, and his second child, Taira Kitsumasa, was working in finance in Europe. Taira Yue, the Daughter of his current Wife Taira Moko, was working in the medical field, perhaps due to her weak Mother. She was working as a Doctor at Taira Medical Center, but she reportedly didn't have much Interest in the Taira Group's Business, so she rarely attended family meetings like this Meal.</p>
<p>Naturally, the Fitzgerald Family in the USA did not participate in today's Meal, and Taira Kanna was unmarried and had no children, so naturally, the next Turn was mine, the Youngest of the Taira Family.</p>
<p>"Ryuta."</p>
<p>"Yes, Grandfather."</p>
<p>At my Answer, Taira Danjo made an Expression as if wondering what to do with this Guy. No, old man, look at me with a kind Expression like you do my other Grandson and Granddaughters.</p>
<p>"Ryuta successfully carried out the mission I gave him, so I will entrust him with Taira Food distribution."</p>
<p>At Taira Danjo's Words, Silence fell over the restaurant. It seemed even quieter than when Taira Kanna received the Taira Driving System earlier. This time, they seemed surprised not because I was taking over Taira Food distribution, but because I had succeeded in Taira Danjo's mission. Even Taira Kokoro failed Taira Danjo's missions, so they must be thinking, 'How did this greenhorn succeed?'</p>
<p>"May I know what mission Ryuta performed?"</p>
<p>Again, Taira Ichiro asked what mission I had performed. However, it was a Question with a different Feeling than the one he asked Taira Kanna. If the Question to Taira Kanna had a Feeling like, 'What kind of mission did she perform to be entrusted with the Taira Driving System?', this time it was a Question like, 'He succeeded in Father's mission?'</p>
<p>Why are they surprised that I succeeded in the mission? No matter how much I tried to hide my Power and stay quiet, everyone looking at me with a Feeling of 'He did it? He did it?' is a bit Frustrating. Still, I have to endure it for now.</p>
<p>"I will give an Explanation, so make sure not to let the content of the mission leak Outside this time either. Do you understand?"</p>
<p>Everyone nodded their Heads at Grandfather Taira Danjo's Question.</p>
<p>"This Guy, Ryuta, prevented the union of the Minamoto Family and the Sugawara Family. If Minamoto and Sugawara had joined forces, it would have become quite a troublesome Matter, so it can be said that he achieved considerable merit."</p>
<p>At Taira Danjo's Words, everyone looked at me. As everyone was part of the Taira Group, they knew the Story that the marriage Proposal between the Minamoto Family and the Sugawara Family had fallen through because Sugawara Chohei, the second Son of the Sugawara Family, who had a marriage Proposal with the Minamoto Family, unfortunately tripped and died. They seemed quite surprised that I was involved in Sugawara Chohei's Death.</p>
<p>"Despite achieving such merit, he left no trace on our side at all, so taking that into account, I am entrusting Ryuta with the affiliate company, Taira Food distribution."</p>
<p>At Taira Danjo's Words, I rose from my Seat, greeted Taira Danjo, and also greeted my Uncles and Aunts who were looking at me with surprised Expressions. Still, since it was my first step in the Taira Group, I had to show at least minimal Etiquette.</p>
<p>"I was fortunate enough to receive a great Responsibility and Reward from Grandfather. Please take good care of me. Please Help your ignorant Nephew."</p>
<p>Taira Food distribution was a company connected not only to Nagumo Sian, who supported me, but also to Taira Ichiro, who was in charge of Taira Bussan, and Taira Kokoro, who was in charge of Taira Department Store, so I had to make a good impression on my Uncles and Aunts first. Of course, it would be difficult to achieve any significant results with Taira Food distribution, but at the very least, I would have to maintain the status quo to avoid being cursed by Grandfather Taira Danjo. Growth can be achieved outside Taira. Within the Taira Group, I decided to aim for maintaining the status quo for a while to avoid being checked.</p>
