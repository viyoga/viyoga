<p>Now that the name was decided, the next step was to determine Torinedo's affiliation. But that was easily resolved. The information team seemed too powerful for any one person under me to control, so I decided to keep it directly under my command.</p>
<p>Even if Torinedo was directly under me, I didn't have the knowledge or experience in information, so I decided to appoint a few people in charge. But having only one person in charge would defeat the purpose of having it directly under me, so I decided to have three vice-chiefs for mutual checks and balances. A so-called triumvirate.</p>
<p>,One seat will go to Sakagami Rena. As a ninja from the Sakagami family, she's naturally talented in gathering information. Does anyone have recommendations for the other two seats?</p>
<p>I had already decided on Sakagami Rena for one of the vice-chief positions. Although she was weak in broad information, Sakagami Rena, as a ninja, was perfect for deeply investigating one person or one group, so it was natural for Sakagami Rena to take on the role.</p>
<p>,I have a recommendation.</p>
<p>Konoe Akina raised her hand. When I nodded as if to tell her to speak, Konoe Akina carefully recommended someone, and it was an unexpected person.</p>
<p>,I'd like to recommend Naruse Shiyo.</p>
<p>,Naruse? Can you tell me why?</p>
<p>I had only been thinking of Naruse Shiyo as a combatant after seeing her formidable status window, so I was surprised when Konoe Akina recommended her as the vice-chief of Torinedo.</p>
<p>,Torinedo's agents will be learning shikigami magic from instructors dispatched by the Naruse family, so we'll need someone in the leadership who can oversee them.</p>
<p>Even if information wasn't her specialty, I agreed that someone was needed to oversee the Naruse family instructors since they were dispatching them. Naruse Shiyo also had quite high judgment stats, so even if she didn't have information-related traits, she could at least judge whether information was important or not.</p>
<p>,Any other opinions?</p>
<p>Everyone didn't oppose Naruse Shiyo becoming one of the vice-chiefs. Rather, they were taken aback that Naruse Shiyo received a high position as soon as she decided to follow me, but no one opposed, so Naruse Shiyo was decided. One information expert and one person for political considerations, so who should I give the remaining seat to...?</p>
<p>,Umm...!</p>

<p>,I don't know if she's suitable for the high position of vice-chief, but I'd like to recommend my daughter, Asada Ei. <p>Ah, that's right. Asada Ei existed. As the daughter of Asada Fukushi, a former intelligence officer, I remembered being quite surprised when I saw her stats with the relic's glasses. <p>I had temporarily forgotten because of various things. As Asada Fukushi said, she could be said to be the perfect talent for the information team. But....</p>
<p>,Asada-ssi, loyalty is important for the information team, but can I trust Ei-ssi, whom I haven't even properly met yet?</p>
<p>,You'll know if you meet her. Just as I am loyal to Ryuta-nim, who healed my daughter, my daughter also intends to be loyal to the person who healed her.</p>
<p>Asada Fukushi confidently answered my question. It seemed that Asada Ei and Asada Fukushi had already talked about me. She even used the expression "loyalty," so it was certain that she was quite grateful to me.</p>
<p>,I understand. Let's decide on the remaining seat after meeting Asada Ei-ssi.</p>
<p>For now, appointing the people in charge of Torinedo was over, and now it was time to recruit agents.</p>
<p>,The agents of Torinedo will be, for now, the appropriate personnel selected by East Weapon's Mimata-ssi and Ryuto Construction's Tang Sohwa-ssi, and reliable people among Asada Fukushi-ssi's former colleagues.</p>
