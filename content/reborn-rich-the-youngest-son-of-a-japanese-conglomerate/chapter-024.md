<p>Fitzgerald Arisu</p>
<p>The Fitzgerald family was a family with a long history. They were a family that ruled the southeast of Ireland, and they left the British colony during the Irish Potato Famine and immigrated to the developing United States.</p>
<p>After finally escaping the famine and settling down, they began to exert some influence when the American Civil War broke out. The Fitzgerald family realized during the Irish Potato Famine that agriculture was no longer viable. They liquidated all of their family assets and engaged in manufacturing in the northeastern United States, so they naturally supported the northern United States.</p>
<p>Fortunately, supporting the Union Army was successful, and the northern United States won the Civil War against the southern Confederate States, and the Fitzgerald family gained considerable profits. After that, they engaged in various businesses, and after a long period of ups and downs, they now only operate one company called Taron.</p>
<p>As a family with a long history from aristocratic origins, the Fitzgerald family had many traditions and interesting legends passed down, one of which was that the Fitzgerald family was descended from fairies.</p>
<p>Other people would just say, 'Fairies, what an interesting story?' but the people of the Fitzgerald family all believed the story to be true. This was because about one in every one or two generations, a child was born with truly special abilities.</p>
<p>The fairy child of this generation was Fitzgerald Alex, the eldest son of the Fitzgerald family. With the ability called Fairy Footsteps, he gained the ability to easily avoid danger while running, and based on that ability, he is now the best running back in the college football league.</p>
<p>Fitzgerald Alex is a scat back-style running back who is nicknamed Ghost because he is as difficult to catch as a ghost while avoiding the defense's tackles. Due to the nature of running backs, whose prime is short, Fitzgerald Alex is not usually picked in the upper rounds of the draft, but he is being evaluated as a talent who is certain to be picked in the upper rounds. That's how outstanding the Fairy Footsteps ability is.</p>
<p>The Fitzgerald family didn't even know it, but there were two fairy children in this generation. The other one was Fitzgerald Alex's younger sibling, Fitzgerald Arisu. Fitzgerald Arisu also knew that she was a fairy child.</p>
<p>The fairy ability that Fitzgerald Arisu was born with was Gruagach's eyes. Gruagach was a beautiful blonde female fairy who was said to bring good luck if brought into the house. Fitzgerald's innate Gruagach's eyes was the ability to measure the value of an object with light.</p>
<p>Fitzgerald Arisu, who grew up in an environment with many precious heirlooms and antiques, thought the whole world was brilliant. Fitzgerald Arisu didn't know about her ability at first, but she gradually realized that what she perceived was different from other people and checked the family's books.</p>
<p>That's how she learned that she was a fairy child born with fairy abilities and that her ability was Gruagach's eyes. However, she didn't tell anyone about it because the family's books said that the ability of Gruagach's eyes was a powerful ability, and that children born with that ability were overworked by the family's greedy adults and died young many times.</p>
<p>However, the choices made by those short-lived children with Gruagach's eyes were amazing: liquidating assets before the famine and leaving Ireland for the United States during the famine, choosing manufacturing when considering large farms and manufacturing, and supporting the United States instead of the Confederate States during the Civil War were all due to the advice of the child with Gruagach's eyes.</p>
<p>Fitzgerald Arisu, who read the book, didn't want to die young, so she refrained from using Gruagach's eyes as much as possible. Fortunately, it was an ability that could be turned ON/OFF according to the owner's will, so Fitzgerald Arisu usually lived with Gruagach's eyes sealed.</p>
<p>When Fitzgerald Arisu first went to Japan in middle school to see her maternal relatives, she heard that her maternal family ran one of Japan's leading companies, so she wondered what it would be like and checked with Gruagach's eyes.</p>
<p>At this time, Fitzgerald Arisu saw the strongest light she had ever seen. It was the light of her maternal grandfather, Taira Danjo. Gruagach's eyes not only see the intensity of the light, but also feel the personality of the object if the light is a person, so she could know what tendencies the owner of the light had.</p>
<p>Her maternal grandfather, Taira Danjo, had an excessively cold light. If Fitzgerald Arisu had been a little younger, she would have cried because she was scared. It was that cold of a light.</p>
<p>When she was a sophomore in high school, she went to Japan again due to a family matter, but Fitzgerald Arisu was afraid of her grandfather, so she left the hotel in Tokyo where she was staying the day before going to her maternal family's house in Chiba and wandered around the streets.</p>
<p>While wandering around aimlessly, she ran into a group of delinquents in an alley. Fitzgerald Arisu didn't know it, but they were a group called Dragon Head, which was famous for being bad in the area. Just before she was about to be gang-raped by several people, two men appeared. They were a very handsome Japanese boy and a black boy, and the two of them dealt with almost twenty delinquents. In particular, the handsome boy showed the feat of knocking out fifteen people by himself, unlike his appearance.</p>
<p>,You shouldn't come to a place like this alone. I'll go with you to the taxi stand, so don't even go near these back alleys.</p>
<p>,Haha! Hyung-nim, she's obviously an American woman, do you think she'll understand if you speak in Japanese?</p>
<p>,You idiot, just because someone is white doesn't mean they're American. There's also Europe.</p>
<p>The two of them had a strange skit-like conversation and guided her to the taxi stand. Fitzgerald Arisu knew Japanese, but she thanked them without being awkward and took a taxi back to the hotel. When she returned to the hotel, she thought, Ah, I should have asked for their names... and wondered what the boy's light would be like, but it was already too late.</p>
<p>After graduating from high school, she came to Japan right away. The desire to meet that boy again overlapped with the fact that she was affiliated with her aunt's Taira Entertainment and became a model.</p>
<p>She had been working for almost a year when she heard the news that her uncle, Taira Saburo, had died. They weren't very close, but it was the first time a relative had died, so Fitzgerald Arisu was quite shocked.</p>
<p>Fitzgerald Arisu called Taira Saburo, who died this time, Uncle Liar because every time he saw Arisu, he said that he had a son her age. Even when she asked her mother, she only heard that her uncle didn't have any children, so she called him Uncle Liar.</p>
<p>That was the extent of her memories with her uncle. Since all of the Fitzgerald family except for her were in the United States, Fitzgerald Arisu attended the funeral alone, where she met the handsome boy who had saved her. Moreover, that boy was her uncle's son that her uncle kept talking about.</p>
<p>,He's so lucky. He became a direct descendant of Taira in an instant.</p>
<p>Someone said in a dissatisfied voice, but Fitzgerald Arisu didn't hear it. She was the lucky one. After several years, she finally met the boy she had been wanting to see. Fitzgerald Arisu couldn't help but look at Taira Ryuta's light with Gruagach's eyes.</p>
<p>It was brilliant. It wasn't as big of a light as Taira Danjo's, but Taira Ryuta's light was brilliant and warm.</p>
<p>,You're quite handsome, aren't you? You look like a K-pop idol.</p>
<p>,You're pretty too, noona. You look like a Hollywood actress?</p>
<p>She was very happy that he said she was pretty. But the disappointing thing was that she remembered Taira Ryuta, but Taira Ryuta didn't remember her at all. If he did, they would have more to talk about. But she could start making memories from now on.</p>
<p>A week later, it was the day to visit her uncle's grave according to Japanese customs, but for some reason, Taira Ryuta had grown taller and more handsome in just a week. Her cousins were whispering that he had gotten plastic surgery and body modification, but that wasn't the case. Gruagach's eyes could recognize plastic surgery and body modification, but there was nothing like that on Taira Ryuta. Rather, his light had become stronger and more brilliant in just a week.</p>
<p>After the visit, Fitzgerald Arisu was searching on her phone for cousin marriage in Japan while moving to the hotel that her parents had booked together. Fitzgerald Arisu was born in New York State, which allows cousin marriage, so she only needed to check Japan.</p>
<p>,It's allowed! Hehe....</p>
<p>She felt fluffy. The Fitzgerald family itself was from an aristocratic family, so they didn't have much aversion to cousin marriage. Fitzgerald Arisu was already far ahead, thinking about how many children she would have.</p>
