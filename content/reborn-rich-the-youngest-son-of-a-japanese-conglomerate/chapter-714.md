<p>Minamoto Risa's childhood. Minamoto Risa knew that her family was different from others.</p>
<p>Unlike other friends' houses where they lived with their fathers, she saw her father once a week at most, and sometimes once a month. Of course, her father was always good to her, but the question she had since some point never disappeared.</p>
<p>Of course, that question was resolved as Minamoto Risa got a little older and realized that she was not a regular child but an illegitimate child born out of wedlock.</p>
<p>-Mommy is sorry.</p>
<p>It's okay. It's not Mommy's fault.</p>
<p>-Even without Mommy, Risa can do well, right?</p>
<p>No. I can't do anything without Mommy.</p>
<p>As Minamoto Risa's existence could no longer be hidden, Minamoto Risa was entered as the daughter of Minamoto Toyomi, and Minamoto Risa's mother, Sakitani Yaeko, left Minamoto Risa.</p>
<p>I don't know what the adults talked about, but after Minamoto Risa entered the main house, she couldn't see her mother as often as she had seen her father until now.</p>
<p>Instead, as she entered the Minamoto main house, she saw her father more often, but she liked her mother, who had been with her every day, much more than the unfamiliar father she saw occasionally. She wanted to live with her mother even if she never saw her father for the rest of her life, but Minamoto Risa's opinion was not accepted.</p>
<p>-Is that her?</p>
<p>-Mommy told me not to play with her.</p>
<p>-She looks gloomy.</p>
<p>-Hmph...! She's saying I'm the child born to a dirty woman.</p>
<p>No. My mommy isn't a dirty woman.</p>
<p>The newly created siblings did not like Minamoto Risa. The sibling with the smallest age difference was Minamoto Akari, who was 6 years apart, so it was fortunate that they didn't run into each other often.</p>
<p>While attending elementary school at the main house, Minamoto Akari attended St. Francisco Girls' Academy far away, and the older siblings were either working for the Minamoto Group as adults, studying abroad at foreign universities, or attending St. Francisco Girls' Academy or all-boys aristocratic schools in other regions.</p>
<p>So, the day Minamoto Risa hated the most was a holiday. On holidays when siblings gathered at the main house, they severely bullied Minamoto Risa as if trying to make up for what they hadn't been able to do.</p>
<p>In fact, even without her siblings, Minamoto Risa was not happy because of the subtle abuse from Minamoto Toyomi, Minamoto Ushin's legal wife.</p>
<p>Because she was Minamoto Ushin's beloved daughter, he did not directly bully Minamoto Risa, but there were endless ways for Minamoto Toyomi, the mistress of the Minamoto family, to bully Minamoto Risa.</p>
<p>Threats and verbal abuse using her mother, and the neglect and bullying of servants instigated by Minamoto Toyomi....</p>
<p>The reason why Minamoto Risa, who was constantly subjected to mental abuse, did not become severely twisted was thanks to her kind nature, the love she received from her mother in her childhood, and the classic comic books in her father's old study.</p>
<p>As time passed, attending St. Francisco Girls' Academy was a blessing for Minamoto Risa. She became even closer to Seizawa Shii, whom she had been friends with before, and now there was no one to bully her anymore.</p>
<p>Occasionally, there were mansion servants who were instructed by Minamoto Toyomi to monitor or neglect her, but servants alone could not handle Minamoto Risa, who had grown up to some extent.</p>
<p>After entering St. Francisco Girls' Academy, Minamoto Risa pondered how she could live happily with her mother as before.</p>
<p>After much deliberation, Minamoto Risa realized that there was only one way. That is, to gain power in the Minamoto Group.</p>
<p>The only talent that Minamoto Risa, who had no foundation, could find were the female students of St. Francisco Girls' Academy.</p>
<p>So, she recruited Sunbae-nims and friends like Izanaki Tamaho and Iide Sunami to make them her future forces.</p>
<p>When Taira Kanna entered the school, there were considerable problems in recruiting juniors as talent, but juniors were not as good as recruiting Sunbae-nims in that they had to wait until they graduated and grew up, so it was not a big loss.</p>
<p>After finishing the recruitment of students at St. Francisco Girls' Academy, Minamoto Risa went out to create her own independent force, regardless of whether she was absent from school.</p>
<p>Of course, she hid herself by tanning her skin and acting like a gyaru so as not to reveal her ambition to her half-siblings, but eventually a problem arose.</p>
<p>She was held back a year because she was absent from school too often to build her forces. It was something that happened because the Taira side attacked and the Minamoto side did not actively defend.</p>
<p>She felt very sorry that even Seizawa Shii, who had been with her as a friend and bodyguard, was held back with her. Of course, the easygoing Seizawa Shii was happy to be able to do school life that she often missed and couldn't do properly, but she was still sorry.</p>
<p>That's how the time in the 3rd grade special class began. She was ordered to restrain herself by her family and was held back due to insufficient attendance, so she couldn't be absent like before.</p>
<p>The first semester was the worst. Every day, there were strange students gathered in a class with Naruse Shiyo, who was always sick, Saionji Diana, a gloomy girl in a wheelchair, Sakagami Rena, who wore a mask so that no one could know what she was thinking, and Oofuchi Erika, a hikikomori who didn't come to school, and even the homeroom teacher was the worst.</p>
<p>Homeroom teacher Nomata Shigemichi groveled to the point of being unpleasant to students with strong family power like Minamoto Risa and Seizawa Shii, but there was an exception for one student.</p>
<p>For her friend Seiwa Rieko, who was quiet and whose family's power was not very strong, St. Francisco Girls' Academy was hell.</p>
<p>Nomata Shigemichi's blatant sexual harassment, which was only to the extent of brushing or grabbing her shoulder, became increasingly intense, and Seiwa Rieko, who had nowhere to turn, was eventually raped by Nomata Shigemichi in the classroom.</p>
<p>Minamoto Risa saw the scene as she returned to the classroom after leaving her luggage, and Nomata Shigemichi was fired, but it became a great trauma for the victim Seiwa Rieko and even the witness Minamoto Risa.</p>
<p>Because of that incident, Seiwa Rieko dropped out of school, and Minamoto Risa developed a subtle hatred for men.</p>
<p>That's how the first semester, which was stained with dirty stories, ended and the second semester began.</p>
<p>This time, the new homeroom teacher was also a man.</p>
<p>Minamoto Risa couldn't understand. Even though such an unsavory incident had occurred, they were putting a male teacher in charge.</p>
<p>The homeroom teacher's name was Yamashiro Ryuta. He was a handsome young man. With the homeroom teacher, the number of students in the class also increased, and the special class became as crowded as other classes.</p>
<p>The gloomy Saionji Diana became brighter, Oofuchi Erika started coming to school again, Sakagami Rena stopped wearing a mask, and Naruse Shiyo recovered.</p>
<p>She realized that all of this, except for Naruse Shiyo's recovery, was due to the new homeroom teacher.</p>
<p>Minamoto Risa had never met a homeroom teacher who cared so much about students until now.</p>
<p>The homeroom teachers so far had either treated the students in a business-like manner, were indifferent, or were trash like the previous homeroom teacher.</p>
<p>And he was kind to Minamoto Risa as if he didn't care about her family.</p>
<p>'Why is he being so nice when he's just a homeroom teacher?'</p>
<p>That's what she thought at first. She wondered if he was being nice because he saw her family, but she changed her mind when she saw that he was being equally nice to all the students.</p>
<p>Ah, this person really thinks that a homeroom teacher should be like this. Minamoto Risa felt a sense of freshness.</p>
<p>It was new. The homeroom teacher's always bright appearance. The homeroom teacher's appearance of talking happily with the students.</p>
<p>The second semester was a different school life than before.</p>
<p>It wasn't about finding talent or increasing power, but about truly enjoying the short remaining school life.</p>
<p>New clubs, picnics, sports days, graduation trips, and even festivals....</p>
<p>As she spent numerous events with the homeroom teacher, a space for Yamashiro Ryuta, a 'man', grew in Minamoto Risa's heart without her knowing.</p>
<p>Even their hobby of liking classic comics was similar. She made all sorts of excuses to go on dates with the homeroom teacher, and she was very nervous every time. It was her first time dating a man, and her heart wouldn't stop pounding when she thought of Yamashiro Ryuta.</p>
<p>She spent the second semester, which was not long but had many things happen, and Minamoto Risa learned a lot about Yamashiro Ryuta.</p>
<p>That he was a gluttonous eater, that he liked the classic comic ○Piece, and that he was actually a psychic....</p>
<p>The more she knew, the more she liked him. She thought his eating a lot was cute, and she thought his talking about comics was intellectual. When she found out that he had telekinetic powers like Tatsumaki from her favorite ○One-Punch Man, she was very excited at the thought of being able to hug him and fly in the sky.</p>
<p>Even Yamashiro Ryuta didn't just stand by and watch her crisis. He didn't hesitate to get his hands dirty himself in her suddenly arranged marriage.</p>
<p>One winter morning. Minamoto Risa headed to the meeting place through the cold wind, listening to Christmas carols. It was a little early, but waiting was one of the pleasures.</p>
<p>At the appointed time, the window opened from the approaching car. It was him. Yamashiro Ryuta. At that moment, she remembered her mother's happy expression when she met her father once a week or once a month. Am I making a similar expression to my mother at that time?</p>
<p>Minamoto Risa smiled and greeted him.</p>
<p>"Merry Christmas! You're not late, are you?"</p>
