<p>"By the way, what happened to our energy guns?"</p>
<p>When escaping from New Nankyung City, weapon possession was prohibited, so all the guns and swords I had prepared in advance were confiscated. I remembered Eve saying she would take care of it, so I asked.</p>
<p>"Ah, we dispatched an employee from the TG Tech South China branch to retrieve them."</p>
<p>"The military just handed them over?"</p>
<p>I was surprised they even retrieved them. I thought the military would definitely try to hold onto them.</p>
<p>"Of course not. They said there was some procedure and didn't want to return them, so I threatened to sue and publish articles about it. They reluctantly gave them back then."</p>
<p>"They never give anything easily, do they?"</p>
<p>"They seemed so disappointed, so we suggested they just buy them when they're released soon. They made a sour face."</p>
<p>Of course, they wanted to take it apart and see if they could steal any technology, but when we told them to just buy it if they wanted it so badly, they couldn't help but make a sour face.</p>
<p>"Actually, the demand for release is getting stronger because I just uploaded another video. Especially in North America."</p>
<p>Since the country has the most gun enthusiasts in the world, it was natural for them to drool over a new gun that shoots energy bullets. By the way, she said she uploaded another video, so I should check it out later.</p>
<p>The fact that this technology came out before the Third World War and was rejected in the U.S. military's rifle selection is not a well-known fact. The Third World War broke out shortly after the U.S. military selected another rifle as its standard, so this energy bullet technology was forgotten.</p>
<p>"So, how about we speed up the release a bit? Actually, the guns are already fully developed, and it's a bit delayed because of the cyber suits. Instead of releasing them at once, we can release them separately and get attention twice."</p>
<p>"That sounds good too. Let's talk to Yu-gyeong about it first."</p>
<p>Since important matters such as product release dates had to be discussed with the co-CEO, Han Yu-gyeong, I nodded and decided to wait for Han Yu-gyeong's call. Dinner would be over soon, so I would get a call from Han Yu-gyeong.</p>
<p>For reference, I ate two pizzas that Eve prepared while talking to her. As I was chatting with Eve about trivial things, I heard a 'woong' sound, and Han Yu-gyeong's image appeared in a hologram. Since I installed a hologram machine in Han Yu-gyeong's office in Vanguard, where she mainly works, we could now see each other and talk.</p>
<p>The hologram machine above my head would be shining on me, scanning my appearance and transmitting it to Han Yu-gyeong in real time.</p>
<p>"Did you have a good meal with your family?"</p>
<p>-Of course not. Ryuta, I'm stressed out.... Give me a hug~!</p>
<p>"Come here if a hologram is okay."</p>
<p>I smiled at Han Yu-gyeong's grumbling and spread my arms. Han Yu-gyeong made a crying face, saying she couldn't hug me, but she seemed to relieve some stress from my comforting words.</p>
<p>-The meal was as we expected. My eldest uncle....</p>
<p>Han Yu-gyeong's explanation was exactly as we had predicted. We expected Han Yu-gyeong's greedy uncles to ask for TG Tech, and our expectations were met.</p>
<p>As a response, we informed them of the existence of another co-CEO, letting them know that TG Tech could not be controlled solely with Han Yu-gyeong's shares, and we threatened them by mentioning Jeil Electronics. It seemed that our plan worked.</p>
<p>You might ask why Han Yu-gyeong, being an esper, couldn't solve it with force, but Han Yu-gyeong's uncles were important enemies who hindered her from taking over the Shin-gang Group in the game, and two of the three were excellent espers.</p>
<p>She might be able to hold her own in a 1-on-1 fight, but it would be difficult to win in a 2-on-1 situation. Han Yu-gyeong also knew this fact in advance, so she didn't resort to force and instead resolved it with words.</p>
<p>"Good job. We should start thinking about the next step."</p>
<p>All three of Han Yu-gyeong's uncles never let go of prey they've targeted, so they will continue to attack to take away TG Tech.</p>
<p>This time, they tried to forcefully take it away for an absurd reason, so next time they will use a slightly softer approach, and everyone could guess that they would use Han Yu-gyeong's father, Han Seong-jae.</p>
<p>In fact, using Han Seong-jae as a conciliatory measure was the worst possible option. As soon as he returned to the Shin-gang Group after being kicked out, he had affairs with numerous women and separated from Ha Ju-yoon (Ha Ju-yoon did have an affair with me during the separation, but there was talk of divorce at that time). Afterwards, when he divorced Ha Ju-yoon, he tried to completely bury Ha Ju-yoon through the media. No matter how much Han Seong-jae was her father, Han Yu-gyeong couldn't see him in a good light.</p>
<p>Of course, he wouldn't be able to succeed even if he tried to do something to TG Tech through Han Seong-jae. Han Yu-gyeong also promised to contact us immediately if any tricks were used against TG Tech, so we would be able to respond right away.</p>
<p>"Anyway, Eve suggested that we release our guns first, what do you think?"</p>
<p>After the story about Han Yu-gyeong's uncomfortable family dinner was roughly over, I talked about Eve's suggestion to release the guns first. Han Yu-gyeong looked at me with wide eyes.</p>
<p>-Ah! I was going to talk about that too! I was going to tell Ryuta that we should release the guns first because the public's attention is focused on us right now.</p>
<p>As expected, Han Yu-gyeong seemed to have quite the management skills, so she didn't want to miss this opportunity. If all three of us agree, then this matter will proceed smoothly.</p>
<p>It would be even smoother if Lara Sinclair, who is in charge of development, also agreed, but Lara Sinclair will also agree with our decision.</p>
<p>"Lara also agreed. She has a great affection for energy guns, so she seems to want to let the world know about them quickly."</p>
<p>Come to think of it, the condition for merging with Lara Sinclair's Energy-Link Industrial Company was to help make a gun made with the energy gun patent developed by Lara Sinclair's father, Kaiden Sinclair, the U.S. military's standard gun.</p>
<p>"Then we'll have more to prepare, right? We have to do production, distribution, and advertising...."</p>
<p>-We have preparations for production and distribution, so we just need to do advertising.</p>
<p>"Since the U.S. is very interested in our guns right now, the actor should be American.... I have a cousin who is a model and actress named Arisu Fitzgerald...."</p>
<p>-Still, this is a matter that depends on the future of our company, so I want to hold auditions.... I haven't even decided how to advertise yet....</p>
<p>"Okay. But at least let her have a chance to audition."</p>
<p>-Ryuta, can you do that for me~</p>
<p>I was going to push Arisu once, but in the end, she has to pass the audition. Still, I think I've done my part since I put her in the running when she wasn't even a candidate. For reference, Arisu is promoting a movie that she filmed that is about to be released.</p>

<p>"No, I'm sorry that I can't help much. Send me the paperwork I need to do by email. It's our company's work, so I'll work hard too."</p>

<p>Eve said something to ruin the mood when we were about to get all lovey-dovey while saying we missed each other. Of course, we are seeing each other through holograms, so she's right, but....</p>
<p>"The atmosphere has become strange, so I'll go now."</p>
<p>"Ryuta-nim! Don't paint me as the mood breaker~!"</p>
<p>-Okay, I'll text you. Let's be lovey-dovey there♥</p>

<p>"I'll be busy again from tomorrow, so should I unpack today?"</p>

<p>"I need to go to the laundromat."</p>

<p>"Huh?"</p>
