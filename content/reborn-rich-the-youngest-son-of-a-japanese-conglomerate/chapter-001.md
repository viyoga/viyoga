<p>"What the hell is this? Shit!"</p>
<p>I was watching the drama adaptation of the game "MegaCorp's Youngest Son", which I had been enjoying recently, and I threw the remote. I even recommended it to my friends because it was so good, but they backstabbed me like this at the end.</p>
<p>"It's completely different from the original, and what are they even trying to say?"</p>
<p>Annoyed, I went to the viewer forum on the MegaCorp's Youngest Son official site, and the public sentiment was already fucked up and about to explode.</p>
<p>"I'll add my strength to this fucked up public sentiment too."</p>
<p>My furious fingers danced wildly. After posting a 5700-character critique, I felt a little bit of my anger subside.</p>
<p>"I just wasted my time for 16 episodes."</p>
<p>It was late, and suddenly I felt sleepy. Right, I have to go to work tomorrow, so I should sleep.</p>
