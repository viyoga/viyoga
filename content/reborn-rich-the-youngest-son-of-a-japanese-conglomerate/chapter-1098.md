<p>A few days ago. Alabama, USA, Asakura-Taira Shipyard.</p>
<p>Taira Yuuichi was working even past 10 PM. When he first worked as the manager at this Alabama Shipyard, he strongly thought, 'Why am I doing work that isn't even recognized by the Family?'</p>
<p>After hearing from Taira Jiro that the reason he was here was as Taira Jiro's eldest son, to build connections in the USA and to keep an eye on the actions of Taira Ichiro's eldest son, Taira Tabenobu, Taira Yuuichi began to work diligently as the shipyard manager.</p>
<p>Even though the war had ended and the production of warships was rapidly declining, Taira Yuuichi secured orders for large merchant ships and large cruises, and successfully launched the commissioned ships, thereby maintaining the reputation of the Asakura-Taira Shipyard. He also solidified his connections with the American upper class, strengthening his own position.</p>
<p>When he eventually returned to the Taira Family, he didn't want to be remembered as a loser. That was why Taira Yuuichi was working so hard until late.</p>
<p>Ttarrreureung!</p>
<p>As he was reviewing the last document before leaving work, the phone rang. Taira Yuuichi picked up the phone without any particular expression. Because his employees knew he worked late, they often reported things late at night.</p>
<p>"This is Taira Yuuichi, President of Asakura-Taira Shipyard."</p>
<p>-It's your father.</p>
<p>It was Taira Jiro. Taira Yuuichi wore a puzzled expression. Since it was 10 PM here, it would be around lunchtime in Japan. Although it was a good time to call from Japan, Taira Jiro and Taira Yuuichi, father and son, didn't talk on the phone often.</p>
<p>Suddenly, Taira Yuuichi felt that his father's voice was trembling.</p>
<p>"What's wrong, Father?"</p>
<p>-Your younger sibling is dead.</p>
<p>".... What are you saying...?!"</p>
<p>An unbelievable story came from his father's mouth, so Taira Yuuichi asked back, as if unable to believe it.</p>
<p>His father had countless affairs with other women, and eventually abandoned their mother like a worn-out shoe. Because of this, Taira Yuuichi and Taira Kitsumasa, as siblings, believed they had no one to rely on but each other, so their brotherly affection was different from ordinary siblings.</p>
<p>So, even at the sudden news that his younger sibling had died, he didn't easily believe it. But with his father's ensuing silence, Taira Yuuichi knew that the words were the truth.</p>
<p>"How did he die?"</p>
<p>Taira Yuuichi asked calmly. His younger sibling had been healthy just a few days ago.</p>
<p>Now that medicine had developed so remarkably, he wouldn't have suddenly died from an illness. He clearly thought that some external force had caused his death. It must have been a car accident or someone had killed him.</p>
<p>-It was an assassination. You need to prepare for the funeral, so come to Japan.</p>
<p>Assassination.... Taira Yuuichi nodded his Head, thinking his assumption was correct. Since his beloved younger sibling had died, it was natural to attend the funeral.</p>
<p>"Understood. I will prepare quickly and go to Japan."</p>
<p>-You won't be able to prepare quickly. What I mean is for you to move to Japan entirely. It's time for you to work for the Family. I will send your successor soon.</p>
<p>Finally, the time had come for his triumphant return to the Taira Group, which he had dreamed of. But hearing that news along with the news of his younger sibling's death, he wasn't particularly happy.</p>
<p>-However, if you want to go to the position you desire, you must achieve at least some merit.</p>
<p>"Understood. I suppose I'll have to show my achievements to Grandfather this time."</p>
<p>Of course, even if he returned now, he could get a position, but the position Taira Yuuichi desired was at least the president of an affiliate company, like his cousin Taira Ryuta. To get a position of that caliber, he would have to achieve something significant.</p>
<p>Thinking that something like this would happen someday, Taira Yuuichi had prepared something. With that, Taira Yuuichi would be able to obtain the position he desired.</p>
<p>"Then, shall we organize what we've gained from the USA?"</p>
<p>The purpose of this trip to the USA was because the Federal Superpower Management Bureau had called him, but while he was there, he also saved Sajo and handled business-related matters, making it quite a fruitful business trip. Now it was time to discuss those achievements and talk about what to do next.</p>
<p>"First, regarding business matters.... Shiyo, could you summarize and tell us?"</p>
<p>Rather than me speaking, I judged it would be better for Naruse Shiyo, who had been with me as the head of Josodang in the USA, to do it. So I called Naruse Shiyo.</p>
<p>"Yes~! First, the business achievement is that we purchased the patents of Obsidian Pharmaceuticals. Our employees are currently there working diligently, so they should be able to acquire the targeted patents."</p>
<p>Although it would be unfair from their perspective, Obsidian Pharmaceuticals and Obsidian Syndicate were being dismantled, both the company and the gang organization, after being identified as the main culprits of the New Rochelle Incident.</p>
<p>"If possible, we are also trying to recruit alchemists belonging to Obsidian Pharmaceuticals, but the New Rochelle Incident is not a small matter, so there's a possibility it won't work out."</p>
<p>If there was the Dignity Health Sports Park incident caused by Famine in LA in the Western USA, then the New Rochelle Incident in the Eastern USA was an equally significant event.</p>
<p>Of course, although the casualties were fewer than in the LA incident caused by Famine, the New Rochelle Incident was a widespread event that left an entire city in ruins, so its level of attention was much higher.</p>
<p>Given such a situation, extracting the alchemists from Obsidian Pharmaceuticals who supplied drugs to the Obsidian Syndicate would be extremely difficult.</p>
<p>Of course, the Federal Superpower Management Bureau said they would provide some convenience for us, but this matter could only be observed for now.</p>
<p>"Tell the working-level staff not to overdo it."</p>
<p>"Understood."</p>
<p>Because if they tried to steal alchemists for no good reason, we could also get swept up in it, I told them to do it within reasonable limits.</p>
<p>"And the employment of the researchers who created OmniCom's mental amplifier was quite easily accomplished. It seems that because the mental amplifier project was completely scrapped, the head of the faction that led that work was completely pushed out of the company. The researchers were also treated as part of that faction and had poor treatment within the company, so there were no problems recruiting them. Even though they were told they would have to relocate to Korea, everyone readily agreed."</p>
<p>Oh...! So, the headhunting of the mental amplifier researchers went well. Of course, it doesn't end there; we also need to purchase all the rights to the mental amplifier research from OmniCom. I wonder if that's going well.</p>
<p>"What about the purchase of rights for that mental amplifier research?"</p>
<p>"Since a fairly large project failed, OmniCom is currently in a situation where they lack funds. The negotiation team is currently negotiating, but I think it will go well."</p>
<p>Indeed.... The recruitment of researchers was quick because they were dissatisfied with their current environment, but the negotiation for purchasing the rights to the mental amplifier research would naturally take longer. OmniCom, lacking funds, would want to sell it expensively, and we would want to buy it cheaply.</p>
<p>In fact, even without purchasing the research rights, if the recruited researchers worked together with Lara Sinclair and TG Tech's excellent research team, they would be able to create a much more advanced mental amplifier without side effects.</p>
<p>However, because OmniCom could claim a share in the research and demand royalties, we are purchasing the rights to the research data to prevent any future disputes.</p>
<p>"If OmniCom names a high price, tell them we'll purchase it along with other patents to match that price. If you ask Lara Sinclair, she'll tell you which patents are good."</p>
<p>"Yes, I'll convey that to the negotiation team and TG Tech Laboratory."</p>
<p>Since OmniCom is a company with its own technology, there will likely be a few patents useful to us. If they demand an expensive price compared to the value of the research data, we can just buy it by including other patents.</p>
<p>"Next, there are achievements obtained from your aunt's husband, Oliver Fitzgerald-nim. We were able to use Talon's engine miniaturization technology at a low price, and he introduced us to the US military test flight unit in Japan that uses Talon's engines, allowing us to distribute foodstuffs."</p>
<p>He was a grateful aunt's husband. Of course, the engine miniaturization technology was something we paid to use, but he contracted it at a very low price, and he allowed us to distribute foodstuffs because we knew each other. Blood kin really was good.</p>
<p>Since I also received something, I would have to make some concessions when working with Talon later, but I could just return as much as I received.</p>
<p>"Oh, right! Eve contacted us to say that the security contract with Moth Tactical went well."</p>
<p>This went well. It seemed they didn't have much work in the USA due to the Taira successor's checks, but since we offered a long-term contract, they would probably get a little breathing room. I had specifically asked Eve to treat them well, and seeing that the contract was quickly finalized, it seemed she had given them quite generous terms.</p>
<p>"Finally, there's the Mars Burger Japan entry contract, which is currently under negotiation. It's still early in the negotiations, but Mother Mars's side is reportedly quite favorable."</p>
<p>Since I had built a certain level of friendship with Vivian Taylor, the head of Mars Burger, they must be looking favorably upon us. Moreover, I'm not just anyone; I'm a major shareholder holding 7 percent of Mother Mars's shares, so it seems they are giving us a favorable contract.</p>
<p>"This is the first I'm hearing of Mars Burger, is it that delicious?"</p>
<p>"Alik wants to eat it too...!"</p>
<p>"I saw on MeTube that it's popular in the USA, but I'm curious about the taste."</p>
<p>As I talked about Mars Burger's Japan entry project, those in Japan who hadn't tasted Mars Burger were quite curious about its taste. I calmed everyone down, telling them to wait a little longer, as they would be able to taste it once Mars Burger entered Japan.</p>
<p>"So, the business report for the USA side is finished. Looking at it this way, we gained so much, didn't we? For a business trip of less than two weeks, this level of achievement is tremendous, isn't it?"</p>
<p>I was also quite surprised listening to Naruse Shiyo's summary. Organizing what we had gained this way, I realized we had gained more from the USA than I had thought.</p>
<p>But what we gained didn't end there. There were other things besides what we gained business-wise.</p>
<p>"Then, next, let's organize the items we obtained in the USA. We also need to discuss who should receive them to increase our overall strength."</p>
<p>When I mentioned items, everyone's eyes sparkled. 'Is there anything useful for me?' was the expression on their faces.</p>
