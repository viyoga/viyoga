<p>Upon arriving at the restaurant, we first ate lunch. The meal was as delicious as when I had eaten it with Taira Kanna before. Otagi Yuka and Saionji Diana, after grumbling a bit, took a bite and, finding the taste agreeable, began to eat heartily.</p>

<p>"Teacher-ssi, it's really good enough to make you faint while eating."</p>
<p>"Isn't it?"</p>
<p>"As Yuka said, it is delicious."</p>
<p>After finishing the delicious meal, I sent Otagi Yuka and Saionji Diana to a nearby cafe, then, guided by the clerks, headed towards the restaurant's secret room.</p>
<p>"This way to the room, Young Master."</p>
<p>Hearing them call me 'Young Master', it was clear this place was managed by the Tairaga Family. I nodded slightly and opened the sliding door.</p>
<p>"Have you come?"</p>
<p>Inside the room, only Taira Danjo was present. I had wondered if Taira Kanna would also be there, but it seemed she was kept separate, waiting for me, as she only knew I had a mission at St. Francis Girls' Academy but not the detailed content of the mission.</p>
<p>"Yes. It's been a long time, Grandfather."</p>
<p>I bowed my Head slightly, then sat on the Opposite Side of Taira Danjo. As Taira Danjo clapped his Hands, the door soon opened, and clerks brought in tea.</p>
<p>"So. Did the Work succeed?"</p>
<p>Taira Danjo's Question, this 'success', if spoken explicitly, was whether I had Sex with Minamoto Lisa, so it was a bit awkward to Answer.</p>
<p>"Before that, I'd like to hear the Evaluation of my merit in preventing the Sugawara Family and the Minamoto Family from uniting...."</p>
<p>I had mentioned this because, by killing Sugawara Chohei, the second son of the Sugawara Family with whom Minamoto Lisa had a marriage Proposal, I had been told by Fumimoto Takayuki, Taira Danjo's chief secretary, if not Taira Danjo himself, that it was an immense merit.</p>
<p>"Ah, Takayuki told me. The Sugawara are nothing special, but they're a nuisance with their regional Foundation. Yes, that was well done. Shall I deduct one of the missions you're supposed to do for me?"</p>
<p>"Yes. Please deduct it for the Sugawara Matter instead of this current Work."</p>
<p>Taira Danjo asked me playfully, but I nodded my Head resolutely. In truth, I had worried quite a lot about this mission.</p>
<p>In fact, it was a task that only required taking a Photo of Minamoto Lisa sleeping next to me, covered by Entwined Sheets, not a Sex Photo, but the moment I handed this Photo to Taira Danjo, Minamoto Lisa would fall into Narak, and I would become the Minamoto Group's top Target for Attack. <p>Even if the mission succeeded, the moment Taira Danjo used the Photo proving my mission's success to 'teabag' Minamoto Ushin, both Minamoto Lisa and I would be in considerable trouble.</p>
<p>"Are you saying you're abandoning this mission?"</p>
<p>Taira Danjo's Voice noticeably grew colder. Simultaneously, the indoor temperature felt as if it had dropped by about 4 degrees. To exert such an Intimidating Aura on me, hardened by countless fights, he was an incredible old man. My Story must not have pleased him. Instead of Answering this Question, I decided to reply in a different way.</p>
<p>"Grandfather, if, while undertaking a Request 'A', you discovered a better Method 'B' for the Family and Group, and choosing 'B' would mean failing Request 'A', what would you do?"</p>
<p>Taira Danjo must have immediately understood my Words. After all, he was Japan's foremost chaebol.</p>
<p>"Are you saying I ordered you to do useless Work that isn't necessary for the Family and company's development?"</p>
<p>"Honestly, isn't it Work you ordered just to make yourself feel good, rather than for the Family or company?"</p>
<p>You bastard!!</p>
<p>The old man, Taira Danjo, immediately flared up at my blunt truth. His Voice was so loud it seemed it could be heard outside the restaurant, but I figured some Soundproofing measures must have been taken.</p>
<p>"Alright. I don't even need to ask about that Method 'B' you discovered. You probably mean taking control of the Minamoto side through that child, Lisa, or stirring up trouble within the Minamoto side, right?"</p>
<p>Taira Danjo, who had just been shouting, now calmly spoke about the plan I had conceived. As expected, he understood immediately.</p>
<p>"I don't know what you're talking about, Grandfather."</p>
<p>I couldn't Consent to Taira Danjo's Words. The moment I agreed to what he just said, I could be treated as a spy collaborating with the Minamoto, so I had to be careful with my Words.</p>
<p>"Do you think no one has had thoughts like yours during the long time the Minamoto and Tairaga have existed in Japan, vying for first and second place? But as you can see from the current Situation, no one has succeeded in that endeavor until now."</p>
<p>Taira Danjo spoke seriously, seemingly unconcerned by my feigned ignorance. Of course, I had no intention of playing along with this serious Story.</p>
<p>"I truly don't know what you're talking about. If I succeed in this Work and hand over Photos or Videos to Grandfather, Grandfather will use them to infuriate Minamoto Ushin, as intended, won't he? Then I, who have no power or backing, would have to face the Minamoto's Attack at the forefront, which is no different from telling me to die, is it? I'd rather fail the mission; I don't want to die at just twenty-one years old."</p>
<p>"Kukuku, you don't even think you'll die at the hands of the rabble sent to kill you, not the Minamoto main force, yet your Excuse is good."</p>
<p>Taira Danjo chuckled, kukuku, as if not believing my Words. In truth, I wasn't worried about an Attack from the Minamoto Group either. As Taira Danjo said, no matter how angry Minamoto Ushin was, I didn't think the Enemies sent by the Minamoto side to me, who had no power or proven anything, would be strong.</p>
<p>What I was worried about was Minamoto Lisa. Minamoto Lisa, precisely because she had no power or backing within the Minamoto Group, could immediately be in Danger due to the new century.</p>
<p>"Alright, considering the Sugawara incident and the Help you gave with the Gocho Kikuhito Matter, I'll count this mission as a Success for preventing the Sugawara Family and Minamoto from uniting."</p>
<p>Taira Danjo, who had been angry just a moment ago, was now smiling and saying he would do as I asked, as if his previous anger had vanished.</p>
<p>For reference, Gocho Kikuhito was the Miyashi Information Team Leader in the Taira Information Team, known to have committed suicide. Hearing that Story, I felt something was strange and told Taira Danjo-nim's secretary, Fumimoto Takayuki, that it might have been due to Telekinesis rather than suicide.</p>
<p>Seeing that Grandfather Taira Danjo was even considering the Help with the Gocho Kikuhito Matter, it seemed my suspicions at the time were indeed true.</p>
<p>If so, it seemed he had combined the act of killing the Sugawara Family's second son with giving hints about the Gocho Kikuhito suicide Incident to count this mission as a Success; if even one had been lacking, he likely would have just said, 'Let's consider this Work a Failure.'</p>
<p>"Since I've counted it as a Success, I won't nag any further. Then, since you've suffered for several months with this Work, rest until March and participate in the Tairaga Group Grand Meeting in March. We'll decide the Reward for this Work then."</p>
<p>Since the Tairaga Group Grand Meeting was in early March, I could get about a fortnight's Vacation. Of course, I would be ceaselessly busy even during that Vacation period. After all, I had more than one personal Business. Since I hadn't been able to pay attention during the six months of the mission, I had to check them one by one.</p>
<p>By the way, I'm curious about the Reward. Before, he had promised to give me one of the Affiliate Companies, but since I ultimately received a Judgment of Success for other Work, the Reward might have changed. I decided to think about that when the time came, and I bowed my Head as if I understood.</p>
<p>"Understood. Thank you for accepting my Forced request."</p>
<p>"Hmph...! You only know one thing and not the other, you bastard! Fighting with the Minamoto isn't a minus, you bastard! It's all merit!"</p>
<p>Grandfather Taira Danjo seemed to think that even if I succeeded in this Work and became a Minamoto Target, it wouldn't be a bad thing.</p>
<p>He believed that if I fended off the Minamoto Group's Attacks, that too would all be merit. Of course, Taira Danjo's Words weren't wrong, but because Minamoto Lisa was not in Taira Danjo's plan, I couldn't accept this Work. It was because I believed there were things a Man had a Responsibility to do.</p>
<p>"Rather than passively receiving Attacks, I want to Attack myself."</p>
<p>"You reckless bastard, like a naked thunderbolt, who won't lose even a single word!"</p>
<p>"Perhaps because I resemble Grandfather, I don't know how to lose even a verbal fight."</p>

<p>"Kukuku, you're saying you succeeded even without proof, right? Understood. You may go now."</p>
