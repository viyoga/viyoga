<p>The weekend passed without any particular incident, and finally, the PMC qualification exam was upon me. As it was a nationwide exam, there was a test center in each prefecture (with the exception of Tokyo, which had multiple test centers due to its large population). Since Miyashi was located in Yamanashi Prefecture, I had to go to Kofu City, the prefectural capital of Yamanashi Prefecture, to take the exam.</p>
<p>Fortunately, I didn't have any classes on Monday, the day of the exam, so I was able to easily take a day off and go take the test.</p>
<p>I entrusted the role of homeroom teacher for the special class to Kijimoto Jurie, who had become quite free now that Naruse Shiyo was better, and she was actually happy that I was giving her work to do. Or was she happy because I gave her five sex request tickets in exchange for being the temporary homeroom teacher?</p>
<p>The destination for the school trip, which should have been decided a long time ago, was announced on Monday, the very day I was taking the PMC qualification exam, for some reason. Still, since it's St. Francisco Girls' Academy, they'll choose well, right? There are some trap-like travel destinations at this time, so I was a little uneasy, but I decided not to worry about it because the exam right in front of me was more important.</p>
<p>It was roughly a 45-minute drive from Miyashi to Kofu City. Still, it was within the same prefecture, so it only took that long; if the test center was only in Tokyo, I would have had to spend six hours traveling back and forth, which would have been quite a pain.</p>
<p>The test center used several conference rooms in the Kofu City Hall. It wasn't an easy exam, but it was a qualification that would help with employment if you got it, so there were quite a few applicants.</p>
<p>As I parked my car and got out, several people who had arrived around the same time as I did were getting out of their cars or walking towards the city hall with serious expressions. Whether it was because the exam application fee was 35,000 yen, or because it was an exam that only took place twice a year, everyone had a determined look on their faces, as if they couldn't afford to fail.</p>
<p>"Ryuta-ssi!"</p>
<p>As I was leisurely heading towards the city hall, someone called my name and ran towards me. When I wondered who it was, it was Kurayamada Kirie, the cool beauty with the hime cut and long black hair. She had a bright smile, so at first, I kept staring, wondering who it was. Seeing her smiling expression instead of her usual cold expression was quite refreshing.</p>
<p>When Kurayamada Kirie linked arms with me, the men who were going to the city hall to take the exam looked at me with envy. Kurayamada Kirie is indeed a rare beauty, but I wondered if they would still have such envious expressions if they knew that she was the leader of a famous terrorist organization. However, there are quite a few men who think that if a woman is pretty, that's all that matters, so even if they knew that fact, half of them would probably still be envious.</p>
<p>"What's wrong?"</p>
<p>"Well, an old man was bothering me at that entrance."</p>
<p>When I asked her why she was welcoming me so much, I saw a middle-aged man in an expensive suit looking at Kurayamada Kirie with a regretful expression at the entrance of the city hall. Judging from his appearance, he was probably a president or a man from a small prestigious family who applied for the exam because he wanted to have his own PMC. So that's why she wasn't acting like her usual tsundere self, but rather in a friendly manner; it seemed like that old man was hitting on her because of her beauty.</p>
<p>"Kurayamada is quite a prestigious family, isn't it? Why didn't you just reveal your family name?"</p>
<p>"I'm currently out of the family, so I can't sell a family that doesn't exist."</p>
<p>Is that so? I'm always ready to sell my family name. Since Kurayamada Kirie was expected to graduate from university, she had to leave the family if she wanted to do something that the family didn't want her to do. It was a typical characteristic of a prestigious family, so I nodded as if I understood.</p>
<p>"Where's the test center?"</p>
<p>"I'm in A-2."</p>
<p>"Oh, me too?"</p>
<p>"What a coincidence."</p>
<p>We were in the same test center. Since there were a total of three test centers, the probability of being in the same test center wasn't that low, but it was still quite nice to be in the same test center.</p>
<p>"Are we going to have lunch together in between?"</p>
<p>"Are we going to have dinner together after the exam too?"</p>
<p>"Let's do that. I'll buy you something delicious since you worked hard."</p>
<p>As we were walking and talking quite affectionately, we arrived at the entrance of the city hall before we knew it. The man in the expensive suit who had been watching us from in front of the city hall was nowhere to be seen, as if he had already gone inside. If he had any sense, he wouldn't have caused trouble at the test center on the day of the PMC exam.</p>
<p>"The test center is... this way."</p>
<p>We followed the guide arrow and found the test center. Since we had different seats, we separated and sat down, taking out our pens and ID. Come to think of it, Japan is probably the only country that still uses pens for exams nowadays. Most countries provide digital exam tablets and solve the problems just by clicking.</p>

<p>In addition to these, I also received messages from Yukiko and Mimata Chieri, among others who knew that I was preparing for the exam, and I gained strength from looking at the Line messages.</p>
<p>"Okay, everyone, please submit your smartphones or cyber implants that are not permitted by the regulations."</p>
<p>One of the examiners came out and opened his mouth. In fact, these kinds of exams will disappear a lot in just over a dozen years. This is because testing simple knowledge becomes meaningless as you can download knowledge with cyber implants.</p>
<p>Most exams are changed to practical exams, with the written exam simply checking whether the data has been downloaded, but there are still purists who don't use cyber implants, so if such a person applies for a qualification, they sometimes have to take a written exam. Of course, in the future, there will be quite a few qualifications that you can't even apply for without a cyber implant, so being a purist is a considerable disadvantage.</p>
<p>The examiner was going around the room, receiving phones and cyber implants and checking identification. When it was my turn, I handed over my Yamashiro Ryuta identification, and the examiner checked my face and nodded.</p>
<p>With this, I could take the exam again as Taira Ryuta and own two PMCs. Usually, only one PMC ownership is allowed per person, so it was good that I could own multiple PMCs if I had a different identity like me. Of course, I wonder how many cases like mine there are....</p>

However, I could establish a special security company in Korea under the identity of Jin Yongtae, so it wasn't really a disadvantage.</p>
<p>"We will now begin the exam."</p>

<p>"Today is the last day. If we want to prepare for the trip."</p>
<p>"So, let's go with Plan 1!"</p>
<p>"Still, Plan 1 is a bit...!"</p>

<p>"Then how about this? We increase the trip by one day and go to both Plan 1 and Plan 2, excluding some schedules."</p>
<p>"Will the school allow us to increase the number of travel days?"</p>

<p>"Then we need to think about the order. Whether to do Plan 1 first or Plan 2 first...?"</p>

<p>"Let's just vote on this too. Those who want Plan 1 to be first, please raise your hands."</p>

<p>"Then, this 3rd grade graduation trip will be to Seoul, Korea, and then to Xinnanjing City in southern China."</p>
<p>"Hoo.... It's decided. I'll report to the principal."</p>
<p>Matsunaga Yuzuki sighed softly and immediately contacted Daigo Terue. She had a relieved expression, perhaps because the meeting that had been going on for several days had finally ended.</p>
