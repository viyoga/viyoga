<p>I opened my eyes to find we were just about to arrive at the Neo Nanjing City Technology Special Zone Airport. Maybe it was because I hadn't slept at all during the graduation trip, but even though I only slept for a little over an hour on the plane, I felt pretty refreshed. Of course, the reason I couldn't sleep was because of my overly healthy dick, but I still managed to doze off here and there, so I wasn't too tired.</p>
<p>Looking to my side, Kijimoto Jurie, who had been tormented by me all night, was sound asleep, and Uenozono Karen was also sleeping, clutching her GoPro stick.</p>
<p>After landing at the Neo Nanjing City Technology Special Zone Airport, we got off the plane, went through immigration, and boarded the prepared bus.</p>
<p>As I looked at the scenery outside from the moving bus, the cyberpunk atmosphere welcomed us, befitting the world's largest technology city. Neon signs in Chinese, people practically covered in cyber implants, and drones flying in the sky... It was a scene hard to see in Japan.</p>
<p>If Neo Tokyo were to be born, you'd probably see scenes like this. Oh, right. I had something to hand out on the bus. I almost forgot.</p>
<p>,Since Neo Nanjing is quite inconvenient without using the VR network, I'll hand out the VR devices prepared by the school here."</p>
<p>I distributed the glasses-type VR devices to the special class students. Both in Pyongyang and here in Neo Nanjing City, it was quite inconvenient without a VR device, so St. Francisco Girls' Academy had prepared them. Of course, they received support from Neo Nanjing City, but seeing that they were distributed to all third-year students, it was clear that the parents' meeting, which decided on the school trip, had also made a significant donation.</p>
<p>I remembered buying a VR device on the black market and wandering around Pyongyang. It was quite convenient because it provided various explanations and made payments easy, even when buying things.</p>
<p>,The manual is also included, so read it carefully and give it a try."</p>
<p>After receiving the VR devices, all the students seemed to have something to do on the bus, and all I could hear was the sound of them fiddling with the devices. Since they were expensive models with good security, their accounts wouldn't be hacked or anything.</p>
<p>I was too lazy to set up the new VR device, so I decided to just use the VR device I bought in Pyongyang. Unlike the students' devices, it was a sunglasses-type VR device, so it stood out a bit, but being lazy was being lazy, so I didn't even open the packaging of the VR device I received.</p>
<p>,Excuse me, Yamashiro-sensei."</p>
<p>Shimokawabe Airen called me, and when I looked to the side, she was looking at me with a slightly embarrassed expression.</p>
<p>,Yes? What's the matter?"</p>
<p>,Actually, I'm not good with machines... Could you help me?"</p>
<p>The home ec teacher was a tech idiot... She seemed so perfect, but it was refreshing and attractive to see that she had some shortcomings.</p>
<p>,Yes. While linking the device to your smartphone..."</p>
<p>,Ah, like this?"</p>
<p>Shimokawabe Airen said she wasn't good with machines, but she was doing well as I taught her.</p>
<p>,You're doing well?"</p>
<p>,Ah, I'm good at following instructions. Actually, I got close to Renka-san by asking her how to set up my smartphone at a martial arts gathering."</p>
<p>Ah, so that's how she got close to Sabu. I was wondering how two martial artists with different backgrounds became friends, but I didn't know there was such a backstory.</p>
<p>,Yes, press that button there."</p>
<p>,If I use it like this... words pop up!"</p>
<p>Shimokawabe Airen completed the setup according to my instructions, put on the VR device, and was looking out the window with great wonder. The other students and Kijimoto Jurie were also absorbed in looking at explanations like game information windows popping up outside the window.</p>
<p>I also put on my sunglasses, changed the language settings, and looked out the window. When passing a restaurant, ratings and menus popped up, exposing the restaurant, and it also showed the smartwatch model that passersby were wearing.</p>
<p>After driving for a while, we arrived at a splendid Chinese-style hotel. It was a hotel with golden dragon pillars at the entrance, which I personally thought was quite excessive.</p>
<p>At this hotel, we were using an entire floor, and it felt like a penthouse with multiple rooms, so the special class decided to use the same floor. Of course, as a man, I couldn't use the same floor and decided to use a private room on the floor below.</p>
<p>,The accommodation this time is on the 19th floor, so use the rooms as divided beforehand. I'll be in room 1802 on the 18th floor, so come there if anything happens."</p>
<p>,You're not going to wander around outside here either, are you, Ryuta-cchi?"</p>
<p>,That's right! Don't go anywhere and just play with us at night!"</p>
<p>Because of what happened in Seoul, the special class students looked at me with suspicious eyes.</p>
<p>,Unfortunately, I don't know anyone in Neo Nanjing. I'll be staying at the hotel all night, so don't worry."</p>
<p>I answered with a serious expression, indicating that I really wouldn't go out. I really didn't know anyone in Neo Nanjing, so there was no reason to go out at night. As it was a newly built city, there weren't many hidden items, so there was no benefit to exploring. Except for the graduation trip schedule, I was planning to just stay at the hotel in China.</p>
<p>Of course, as it was a cyber city, there were quite a few events where you could get mechanical equipment or parts, but I could get those through Eve.</p>
<p>,Then let's go up, put our luggage down, and meet at the hotel restaurant. The hotel restaurant is on the 5th floor, and St. Francisco Girls' Academy has chartered it, so come there by 1 PM..."</p>
<p>It was 12:30 PM now, so there was a little time before lunchtime. The students each took the elevator up, and I also received a digital key for the 18th floor and went up.</p>
<p>I opened the door to room 1801, which was next to my room, 1802. It was a system where the door recognized and opened when the holder of the digital key stood in front of it. With a click, I entered the opened room and saw large trunks stacked neatly.</p>
<p>,It seems Eve prepared everything well."</p>
<p>In Seoul, there was only the minor (?) incident of dealing with the Yongcheol Faction, so the special class students weren't really involved in any incidents, but the incidents in Neo Nanjing were the kind that you would definitely get caught up in once they happened, so I had asked Eve to prepare things in advance.</p>
<p>After confirming that the items were there, I went into my room, 1802, put my carrier in a corner, and turned on my smartphone while lying on the bed.</p>
<p>I checked to see if there were any unusual news, but there wasn't any yet. That's a relief. Shall I go eat lunch then?</p>
<p>I came out of the hotel room and stood in front of the elevator. When the elevator door opened with a ding, the special class students from the floor above were inside. Naturally, the special class students who found me welcomed me and pulled me into the elevator.</p>
<p>,Ah! It's Ryuta-cchi!"</p>
<p>,Ryuta, get in quickly. Alik is hungry..."</p>
<p>Many of the students hadn't eaten the in-flight meal because they were sleeping, so they seemed to have taken the elevator to eat lunch right away because they were hungry.</p>
<p>The door closed, and when I looked at the elevator, I saw "5th Floor, St. Francisco Girls' Academy Reserved" written in my vision. Even though this was the second time after Pyongyang, I still hadn't gotten used to the VR device.</p>
<p>Ding! 5th floor!</p>
<p>It was an elevator announcement in Japanese, as there were Japanese people on board, according to the VR device. When we got off the elevator, there were arrows on the floor guiding us to where we needed to go, probably because it was reserved, so we could easily go to the restaurant chartered by St. Francisco Girls' Academy.</p>
<p>The restaurant served traditional Chinese food. We had Chinese food for our first meal in Seoul too, so there was something strangely in common. Of course, what we ate in Seoul was jajangmyeon, which could be called Korean food that you can't really see in China, but still, Chinese food is Chinese food...</p>
<p>The menu was a blank white paper, but when I looked at it with the VR device, I could see the menus translated into Japanese. This is amazing.</p>
<p>I ordered Peking duck and beef potstickers. The other students also ordered and ate their own food, and it was quite edible. In particular, Peking duck was said to be the original dish of what we commonly know as Peking duck, but I hadn't actually eaten Peking duck yet, so I couldn't tell the difference.</p>
<p>,What kind of animals do you like, Ryuta-cchi?"</p>
<p>Seizawa Shii, who was eating dim sum, looked at me and asked. She probably asked because the next schedule was the zoo.</p>
<p>,Dogs, bears, or leopards?"</p>
<p>Suddenly, Han Yu-gyeong's summons came to mind, so I answered that way, and the other students also talked about their favorite animals.</p>
<p>,Alik doesn't like dogs, bears, or cats..."</p>
<p>Alik, unlike a Russian girl, didn't like bears. She didn't like dogs or cats either, and when I heard the story, it was because they were competitors for discarded food when she was wandering alone after losing her mother.</p>
<p>Really... She's saying it with such a blank expression, but she fought with stray cats and stray dogs to get food, so I don't know what kind of expression to make every time I hear it. Alik said she liked dolphins. It seemed like she chose a cute animal that wasn't in a food competition (?) relationship.</p>
<p>,I like lions! How about you grow your hair out like a lion's mane, Ryuta-cchi?"</p>
<p>Seizawa Shii said she liked lions and made an unreasonable request to me. Grow my hair out like a lion's mane... Isn't that too much?</p>
<p>,I like ferrets."</p>
<p>Sakagami Rena uniquely chose ferrets. Ferrets are cute too, but when asked about favorite animals, ferrets wouldn't be the first thing that comes to mind.</p>
<p>After that, the students each talked about their favorite animals. Most of them were dogs or cats, and only Minamoto Risa, who liked flying squirrels, and Urabe Konoka, who liked octopuses, remained in my memory.</p>
<p>After finishing the meal and brushing their teeth, the students of St. Francisco Girls' Academy boarded the bus again. Neo Nanjing City was a new city, so there were no beautiful (?) traditions like uniforms at Lotte World or hanboks at Gyeongbok Palace, so the students just wore comfortable casual clothes.</p>
<p>The bus started, and Seizawa Shii's voice was heard as she looked out the window.</p>
<p>,Huh? They're fighting over there...!"</p>
<p>Listening to Seizawa Shii's story, it seemed like someone was fighting on the street. I have a bad feeling about this? Surely it's not the situation I was worried about, is it?</p>
<p>,It's already passed."</p>
<p>Shimokawabe Airen, a battle maniac, looked out the window, but the bus was moving too fast, so she couldn't see the fighting and spoke with a disappointed expression.</p>
<p>The commotion caused by the street fight stopped, and we soon arrived at the zoo. At the entrance, a neon sign that read "Neo Nanjing City Grand Zoo" was translated next to a panda face.</p>
