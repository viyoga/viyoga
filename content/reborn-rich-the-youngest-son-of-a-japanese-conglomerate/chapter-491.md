<p>After eating the assorted cutlets, Minamoto Lisa-ssi and I went to a cafe to have dessert and spend some time. Of course, it was a cafe that Minamoto Lisa-ssi had personally chosen, just like before. A net cafe, a meal, and a cafe course-it felt like an old couple's date course.</p>
<p>We didn't talk much while eating dessert, but it wasn't awkward, surprisingly. I don't know if it's because we watched manga without talking at the net cafe, or if it's because Minamoto Lisa-ssi is just comfortable to be around.</p>
<p>Maybe because we spent time reading books at the net cafe, the sky was already dark when we came out of the cafe.</p>
<p>"Shall we part ways now?"</p>
<p>How cool. Minamoto Lisa-ssi, coming out of the cafe, said to me with a refreshed expression. She seemed to be in a good mood from reading manga for the first time in a while and meeting a like-minded companion. Minamoto Lisa-ssi wasn't a delinquent after all. If she were a delinquent, now would usually be a good time to hang out, but she's trying to go straight home.</p>
<p>"Before we part, let's stop by somewhere for a bit."</p>
<p>I glanced at my phone and grabbed Minamoto Lisa-ssi's wrist. Then, I went to a parcel locker in an alley.</p>
<p>"Huh? You can just pick up packages by yourself, right?"</p>
<p>Minamoto Lisa-ssi looked at the parcel locker, gently stroked the wrist I was holding, and asked me with a puzzled expression.</p>
<p>"It's your present, so it's meaningless for me to pick it up alone."</p>
<p>"I said it wasn't necessary... I had enough fun today."</p>
<p>Even if she had fun playing, it would be a different kind of joy from receiving a gift. Of course, I didn't prepare anything grand because I was short on time. I entered the password and took a box out of the parcel locker.</p>
<p>"I still wanted to give it to you, so please accept it."</p>
<p>"...Thank you."</p>
<p>Minamoto Lisa-ssi, who received the box from me, looked like she was wondering if she could open it, even though she said it was okay, so I nodded. Minamoto Lisa-ssi immediately opened the box and checked the contents.</p>
<p>The contents of the box were a 12-inch figure of Bald Cape, the protagonist of *One-Punch Man*. I knew that the Miyashi underworld branch had a top-of-the-line 53-year-old 3D printer to make simple parts self-sufficient, so I asked Eve to quickly make the figure.</p>
<p>In fact, it was closer to a statue than a figure because it was made entirely of metal with a 3D printer, so there were no joints, but Eve had colored it all, so there was no difference in appearance. It's a concept where a statue goes inside a figure, so it wasn't wrong to say anything.</p>
<p>"Ah...! Thank you."</p>
<p>She seemed very satisfied, judging by how she couldn't take her eyes off it. No matter how much it's made with a 3D printer, the quality varies depending on the skill of the person making it, so the quality of the item made by Eve, who can be said to be the world's best AI, was beyond imagination. The pose was so vivid that it looked like it would move at any moment, and the coloring was also very well done.</p>
<p>It was a great quality considering it was ordered from the *tonkatsu* restaurant and made in an hour and a half. Of course, the price was very expensive because the quality was so high. On top of that, I received a quick service from an underworld agent, so the price was considerable for a figure.</p>
<p>Of course, I had so many underworld points that making this one figure was like scooping out a bucket of water from a lake, so I could ask Eve without much hesitation.</p>
<p>"How did you order this so quickly?"</p>
<p>It was natural to be curious. It was only four hours ago that I went to the net cafe where I could find out Minamoto Lisa-ssi's tastes, and even if I ordered it right away from the net cafe, the delivery wouldn't be this fast.</p>
<p>"You know that place where you misunderstood me before."</p>
<p>Minamoto Lisa-ssi also knew about the underworld. She misunderstood that Saionji Diana-ssi and I were going to a love hotel and introduced me to the Miyashi underworld branch. After that, she didn't think much about the underworld, so she clapped her hands, as if remembering what she had forgotten at my words.</p>
<p>"Ah, that's right. Was it the underworld? It must have cost a lot of money to prepare this so quickly, so it's a very useful place.... Thank you."</p>
<p>"I promised to give you a gift.... I hope you like it."</p>
<p>"Thank you. I like it."</p>
<p>She looks like she's going to say it like a *tsundere*, but she hugs the figure tightly as if she likes it. That appearance is quite cute, but this figure is made of metal, so it must hurt if you hug it so tightly...? Eve made it with a method of emptying the inside, so it was light when I picked up the box earlier, so Minamoto Lisa-ssi, who is a woman, can lift it enough.</p>
<p>"Shall we go home now?"</p>
<p>"Yeah. I had fun today, *anata*."</p>
<p>When I suggested going back because the gift was over, Minamoto Lisa-ssi smiled and answered. By the way, how long are you going to call me *anata*? Minamoto Lisa-ssi seemed to like the title *anata* quite a bit.</p>
<p>"How long are you going to call your teacher *anata*?"</p>
<p>"I'm a Miyashi Municipal High School student now, you know?"</p>
<p>When I asked playfully, Minamoto Lisa-ssi also answered with a playful smile. A usually cold *gyaru* showing this side of her-there's no *gap moe* like this *gap moe*. I was unknowingly a little excited.</p>
<p>"Then, since I'm not a teacher either, let's call today a date."</p>
<p>"A date?"</p>
<p>"We'll have a short date until we go to the taxi."</p>
<p>I grabbed Minamoto Lisa-ssi's hand. This action was like an adventure to advance the relationship. Because Minamoto Lisa-ssi had a weak hatred of men because of her former homeroom teacher, it could be counterproductive, but there is no progress without challenge, so I tried it once.</p>
<p>"...."</p>
<p>Fortunately, Minamoto Lisa-ssi didn't get angry or pull her hand away. Feeling that it was tacit consent, I bought a sweet ice cream near Miyashi Prime Mall, where we first met, and handed it to Minamoto Lisa-ssi's remaining hand.</p>
<p>The two of us walked to the taxi stop, holding hands with one hand and holding and eating ice cream with the other. It was as if we didn't want to arrive at the taxi stop, and our pace gradually slowed down.</p>
<p>No matter how slowly we walked, we couldn't help but arrive at the not-so-distant taxi stop soon. A taxi came right away at the taxi stop, so we couldn't have a special conversation, and Minamoto Lisa-ssi got into the taxi right away.</p>
<p>"Thank you for today...."</p>
<p>"Don't say thank you because we had fun together."</p>
<p>Minamoto Lisa-ssi left a thank you that I didn't know the reason for and went home by taxi. I could have taken Minamoto Lisa-ssi to her house myself, but if I did, the Minamoto information team would quickly spot me and start an investigation.</p>
<p>That's why this mission was difficult. I have to get close to Minamoto Lisa-ssi, but I have to get close while avoiding the eyes of the Minamoto family next to Minamoto Lisa-ssi. The closer I get, the greater the risk of getting caught.</p>
<p>It was a little fortunate that Minamoto Lisa-ssi had a troublemaker image. If she were a real young lady character like a flower in a greenhouse, she would be monitored every move, so I wouldn't be able to touch her.</p>

<p>"What? You want to search St. Francisco Girls' Academy?"</p>
<p>Kurayamada Kirie-ssi looked at her father, Kurayama Kazuma-ssi, and asked again. St. Francisco Girls' Academy was a prestigious school where most of the young ladies of Japan's prestigious families attended. Even she was a graduate of St. Francisco Girls' Academy, so she could say that she knew the school better than anyone else.</p>
<p>It was absurd that Jinildang, a terrorist organization with a high reputation, would search such a St. Francisco Girls' Academy.</p>
<p>Even though Jinildang is a terrorist organization, it has continued to exist until now because of the support of forces with far-right views. But if they knew that their daughters or granddaughters attending St. Francisco Girls' Academy were in danger, they would cut off their support, and then Jinildang would never survive.</p>
<p>Kurayama Kazuma-ssi looked at his daughter's expression and shook his head as if he had misunderstood his words and opened his mouth.</p>
<p>"Who said Jinildang would search? You will do the search. Who would say anything if a teacher at the school walks around the school?"</p>
<p>"Am I not affiliated with Jinildang?"</p>
<p>Thanks to having a father who is the head of Jinildang, she had the position of deputy head, but she still couldn't erase the feeling that she was just floating around. That's because she didn't know anything about the search she was talking about now, and she heard it for the first time now.</p>
<p>In other matters, seeing her father and Jinildang colleagues, who are just deputy heads and do things that she doesn't know at all, she often thought that she was just a nominal existence. Just now, she couldn't erase the feeling of resentment because she spoke as if she wasn't a member of Jinildang.</p>
<p>"Of course you are a member of Jinildang, but the people around you see you as a teacher at St. Francisco Girls' Academy, so it's good for us."</p>
<p>"Okay. So what the hell are you searching for?"</p>
<p>"Didn't I tell you that either? Ah, that's right...! I didn't tell you...."</p>
<p>Her father, whom she had seen in a long time, was a little strange. It was like he was possessed by something. When Kurayamada Kirie-ssi felt a strange sense of incongruity, Kurayama Kazuma-ssi began to speak.</p>
<p>It was the story of the roots of ancient earthquakes and that if Jinildang obtained the roots of the earthquake, they could seize the hegemony of Japan.</p>
<p>And the possibility that the roots of the earthquake are in Miyashi, and more than anything, the possibility that it is on the site of St. Francisco Girls' Academy.</p>
<p>Kurayamada Kirie-ssi was dumbfounded after hearing the story. Of course, it was true that supernatural beings such as psychics were appearing in this era. Even she has the ability to be in the category of psychics.</p>
<p>But earthquakes are natural phenomena that have already been scientifically verified. What is the absurd root of an earthquake? And if you have the roots of that earthquake, you can have the hegemony of Japan? Her father, who was telling a story completely different from Jinildang's ideology until now, suddenly looked unfamiliar. She thought that everything was prioritizing the Japanese imperial family, but it wasn't.</p>
<p>"That's impossible. Earthquakes are just natural phenomena!"</p>
<p>"No, I can feel it. I, the owner of the document, can feel it. We are close to the root of the earthquake...!"</p>
<p>Kurayamada Kirie-ssi felt it. She couldn't persuade her father with words anymore. The problem was the document that her father was referring to. Her father was possessed by the magic of that document.</p>
<p>"This is an order as the head! Investigate St. Francisco Girls' Academy as soon as possible and find a clue to the root of the earthquake!"</p>
<p>Kurayama Kazuma-ssi shouted. Kurayamada Kirie-ssi could only nod sadly at her crazy father.</p>
