<p>-Everyone who has waited for today, greetings from the New Colosseum Arena in Los Angeles! Currently, the time in LA is 8 PM! It must be 12 PM in Japan! I hope everyone watches the UMC Unlimited Class Champion Match before lunch~!</p>
<p>Has it already gotten this late? Looking at the clock, it was exactly 12 PM, just as the caster said. The previous Match wasn't unexpectedly boring, so Time seemed to have flown by quickly.</p>
<p>-I will carry on Fujigasaki Rinka's legend! Japan's New Hope! Genba~ Kiyohira~!</p>
<p>As the Champion Match began, first, the challenger, that Guy Genba Kiyohira, ascended into the ring with his entrance Music. His White hair, maintained since middle school, and fierce Impression were quite familiar. Perhaps to fight in the Unlimited Class, or perhaps he had significantly gained Weight, his Size seemed even larger.</p>
<p>"Every Time I see him, I think he looks fierce."</p>
<p>Seizawa Shii, watching the Screen, muttered. There was a good Reason why that Guy Genba Kiyohira was called Genbaki. He was given that nickname because he resembled the fierce characters in Baki.</p>
<p>Of course, in the Neighborhood, only Kazuhiro and I called that Guy Genbaki by that name because everyone was so afraid of him.</p>
<p>-UMC Unlimited Class Champion! Hunter Herrera, the 'Iron Fist' who pulverizes anything!!!</p>
<p>As that Guy Genba Kiyohira entered the ring, the current UMC Unlimited Class Champion Hunter Herrera appeared. He was a Monster who had undergone both biological gaejo and mechanical gaejo simultaneously, making him incomparable to that Guy Genba Kiyohira in Weight class from the start.</p>
<p>With a Height of over 2 meters and an unbelievable Reach, and the immense Weight from his mechanical gaejo, it seemed impossible for that Guy Genbaki to win based on Specs.</p>
<p>Genba Kiyohira was a former welterweight, with a Height of only around 170s, so in terms of a normal Weight class, there was no comparison.</p>
<p>Of course, since he was now in the Unlimited Class, no one spoke about the Weight class difference. It was common for someone 160 cm tall to defeat a giant over 2 meters.</p>
<p>Even the previous Champion, Fujigasaki Rinka, with a Height of 170 centimeters, which was tall for a Woman, had remained on the throne for several years, so no one cared about Height or Weight.</p>
<p>"Ugh.... I'm Taut Tension!"</p>
<p>Naruse Shiyo, a huge fan of Martial Arts, muttered while watching the Screen. Although she had Bet that Hunter Herrera would win, her Gaze watching the Screen was complicated, as if she still wished for Genba Kiyohira, also a Japanese Person, to win.</p>
<p>The referee informed the players of the precautions/warnings. Even in the Unlimited Class, there were minimum rules. Basic rules like not killing the opponent or not attacking the genitals existed.</p>
<p>After the Referee finished giving the instructions, the bell rang, signaling the start of the match.</p>
<p>That Guy Genba Kiyohira, instead of the mixed Martial Arts Intimate Posture he had practiced until welterweight, was in an Intimate Posture similar to Kaishu Kakan, who had fought me, and was looking at Hunter Herrera.</p>
<p>According to what I heard from Athena Mannerheim, Kaishu Kakan's Fist Technique was the Constantly Victorious Mountain-Shattering Style of the Anyaji Guardian named Musashi Takuro, specializing in powerful strikes that destroy the Opponent's Body. Looking at the Unlimited Class Matches so far, that Guy Genbaki seemed to have inherited it well.</p>
<p>The first Attack was from Hunter Herrera. A light Jab thrown with immense Reach struck Genba Kiyohira's Guard, making a 'jjeok!' sound. It was a Whip-like Attack.</p>
<p>Thus, Hunter Herrera unilaterally attacked, using his superior Reach. Although Genba Kiyohira was defending well against Hunter Herrera's Attacks, each strike, a combination of biological gaejo and cybernetic implant mechanical gaejo, was heavy, even if it was just a light Jab.</p>
<p>Every Time he blocked, a dull 'Kung Kung' sound echoed, and the Asian spectators were looking at that Guy Genba Kiyohira with worried Expressions. But Genba Kiyohira was staring at his Opponent, Hunter Herrera, without any wavering.</p>
<p>-He's blocking, but Genba Kiyohira is continuously allowing Attacks! Even if he blocks, Damage accumulates! Rather than defensive Tactics, he needs to penetrate inward and Attack!</p>
<p>The commentary sounded regretful, but from my perspective, that Guy Genbaki didn't seem to have suffered significant Harm.</p>
<p>Kaishu Kakan had endured my Fists, filled with the Abyss, several times, so no matter that Hunter Herrera was the UMC Unlimited Class Champion, it was hard to see him possessing Destructive Power greater than my own strike.</p>
<p>-PpII!</p>
<p>Round 1 ended. For reference, the UMC Unlimited Class had more Rounds than Weight class Matches. It meant to aim for a decisive Match rather than a Judgment.</p>
<p>"He's not losing, is he?"</p>
<p>"He's not attacking at all...."</p>
<p>Minamoto Risa and Shido Tsubaki, who didn't know Martial Arts, spoke with anxious Eyes.</p>
<p>"No. Round 1 was just a feeling-out Match. Hunter Herrera only used Jabs, and Genba Kiyohira blocked all Attacks without significant Harm, so it's too early to say who gained an advantage."</p>
<p>Otagi Yuka, who was calmly observing The Situation of the Match, gave her assessment of Round 1. Seizawa Shii and Naruse Shiyo, who at least understood physical combat, also nodded in Consent. Alik was watching the Match without a Word.</p>
<p>Since they had been watching the Match since Morning, everyone's Popcorn buckets were empty. They had eaten everything while watching the previous Matches, so there wasn't much left to eat. Anyway, since it would be lunch after this Match, there seemed no need to make Popcorn or Fries again.</p>
<p>-In Round 1, Genba Kiyohira seemed to be slightly pushed back. We hope for a Counterattack in Round 2! Now, Round 2 begins!</p>
<p>As Round 2 began, unlike Round 1, both actively clashed. Hunter Herrera, who had only used Jabs and feints, seemed to think he had an advantage when Genba Kiyohira didn't Counterattack during Round 1, so he pushed even more fiercely. He pressured Genba Kiyohira, using not only Jabs but also various mixed Martial Arts Techniques.</p>
<p>Genba Kiyohira also stepped forward aggressively, as if his defensive Play in Round 1 had been a lie, and this Time, he didn't just block but gradually approached Hunter Herrera.</p>
<p>Pang!</p>
<p>Genba Kiyohira dodged the Jab, then pushed the straight punch that followed with his Palm to change its Path, and burrowed into Hunter Herrera's Body, instantly closing the distance.</p>
<p>Kung!</p>
<p>Simultaneously, both of their Attacks landed. Genba Kiyohira threw a Body Shot, and Hunter Herrera struck Genba Kiyohira's Temple with a Hook as he burrowed in.</p>
<p>But it was Hunter Herrera who fell. While the Referee counted, a Replay was shown, revealing that Genba Kiyohira had simultaneously struck the Abdomen and raised his Shoulder to block Hunter Herrera's Hook. In the end, only Hunter Herrera had taken a direct hit.</p>
<p>"Amazing~! If it wasn't for slow-motion, I wouldn't have known what happened."</p>
<p>Oofuchi Erika muttered in surprise. I had roughly grasped The Situation, but due to Camera angles and broadcast limitations, I couldn't help but be slower to understand The Situation than if I were watching in the Arena.</p>
<p>"Seven...! Eight...!"</p>
<p>Perhaps because he was the Champion and it would be anticlimactic if it ended so quickly, the Referee was counting very slowly. Hunter Herrera stood up and raised his Fist when the Referee called 'Nine'. His Gaze was clear, as if he had been resting while lying down until now. With gaejo, his durability also increased, so he seemed able to withstand one Body Shot.</p>
<p>The Match resumed, and Hunter Herrera, perhaps Frustrating that he had been knocked down, attacked that Guy Genba Kiyohira even more fiercely.</p>
<p>Genba Kiyohira also confronted Hunter Herrera even more aggressively, as if he wouldn't lose. He continuously Counterattacked, blocking each of Hunter Herrera's violent strikes, and even though it was a Match where adrenaline exploded, Hunter Herrera winced, as if Genba Kiyohira's strikes were painful.</p>
<p>"KWAHHH!"</p>
<p>Unable to land proper hits and only taking them, Hunter Herrera, whose Anger had exploded, charged forward. Genba Kiyohira, as if he had been waiting for this Moment, swung his Fist towards Hunter Herrera's iron Fist flying directly at him.</p>
<p>Kwakwakwa!</p>
<p>A sound unlike Fists clashing erupted, and Hunter Herrera's cybernetic implant Fist and Arm were disassembled and shattered.</p>
<p>Genba Kiyohira was not satisfied with just taking off one of Hunter Herrera's Arms. He then crushed Hunter Herrera's Peach bone with a Calf Kick, and as Hunter Herrera fell, bringing his Head within range, he struck Hunter Herrera's Chin with a Palm Strike, knocking him Faint.</p>
<p>Naturally, a Faint Hunter Herrera would not be able to continue the Match, so the Referee declared the Match over.</p>
<p>-Ahh! Kita! Amazing~! He swept in like a Storm, instantly KO'd the former UMC Unlimited Class Champion Hunter Herrera, and seized the throne!! What's even more surprising is that in the short Time of Round 2, he didn't allow a single direct hit!</p>
<p>Along with the caster's High Praise, that Guy Genba Kiyohira was shown receiving the belt. After that, the Camera focused on the spectator stands, and Kujo Kina was seen jumping up and down in place, making all sorts of commotion, causing all the special class Students watching that Scene to burst into laughter.</p>
<p>"Genba Kiyohira won, and I won too~!"</p>
<p>Seizawa Shii jumped up and down, delighted that she had won. When I looked at her with an Expression of 'What did you win?', she looked at me as if I was Stifling.</p>
<p>"Child~! Did you forget? We made a Bet yesterday on who would win, didn't we?"</p>
<p>"Ah, right...."</p>

I had said Genba Kiyohira would probably win, but since I hadn't participated in the Bet, I would just watch the Students' Bet from a safe distance.</p>
