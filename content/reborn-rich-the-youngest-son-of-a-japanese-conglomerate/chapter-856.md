<p>Uuung...! A round table, completely dark all around. Suddenly, a light flickered and flashed, and someone appeared and sat in a chair placed at the round table. What appeared was a cute, deformed Ogre character. The reason a character suddenly appeared was because this space was a VR space. This VR space with the round table was a space created by Oofuchi Erika, and today, some Students had a meeting there. Suddenly, the light flashed once more, and this time, a pink-blonde SD Gyaru character appeared in the chair next to the deformed Ogre.</p>
<p>"Is that Lisa-ttang?" At the deformed Ogre's question, the pink-blonde Gyaru character gave an Eye Smile.</p>
<p>"Yes. Shii, you came early?"</p>
<p>"Huh? How did you know it was me?!" At Minamoto Risa's Words, the deformed Ogre-Seizawa Shii-asked in surprise.</p>
<p>"Only Shii calls me Lisa-ttang."</p>
<p>"Ah, right...! Ehehehe, I was found out immediately." While Minamoto Risa and Seizawa Shii were having a Conversation, the chairs flashed brightly, and the St. Francis Girls' Academy 3rd-year special class Students, with characters they had created as they wished, began logging into the virtual round table they had made in the virtual space. In human form, there was a cute SD Maid Alik, a cute ninja character Sakagami Rena, and an SD Shrine Maiden character Urabe Konoka.</p>
<p>In non-human forms, there was Konoe Akina, whose eyeballs floated, Naruse Shiyo in the form of a Fairy Dragon, Otagi Yuka, a cute bat character wearing a Cloak, and Saionji Diana, a teddy bear sitting in a wheelchair.</p>
<p>"Everyone's here." Finally, Oofuchi Erika, who had created this VR virtual space, appeared. Oofuchi Erika's character had the Appearance of wearing an old CRT computer Monitor like a helmet.</p>
<p>"Since everyone's here, I'll start the special class Valentine's Day Meeting now." Although Oofuchi Erika had prepared this Place, it seemed that Minamoto Risa, as the Class president, was presiding over the Meeting.</p>
<p>"What about Kina, Tsubaki, and Karen?" They said they would give giri choco (obligatory chocolate), so I didn’t invite them to the meeting. Valentine's Day chocolate gifts in Japan were categorized into giri choco (obligatory chocolate), given to classmates or coworkers rather than romantic partners, and honmei choco (true feeling chocolate), given to a boyfriend, crush, or when confessing one's feelings.</p>
<p>The special Valentine's Day meeting held now was a gathering of students who would give sincere chocolate, that is, honmei choco, to their class teacher Yamashiro Ryuta, not giri choco. Kujo Kina and Shido Tsubaki said they would give giri choco, and Uenozono Karen, for whom participating in such a love Event was professionally dangerous, was not even told that there was a Meeting today.</p>
<p>Since next Sunday was Valentine's Day, if they didn't hold a Meeting now, with exactly one week left, their designs might overlap, so a Meeting was absolutely necessary. It could be called the last love-love Event before Graduation, and if their designs overlapped, they wouldn't feel good. Chijijijik! Just as the Meeting was getting underway, one side of the VR space where the special class Students were, suddenly began to distort.</p>
<p>Everyone looked on in surprise, and an Arm popped out from the distorted side of the space, and soon, Taira Kanna's SD character, wearing a Pure White Wedding Dress, appeared.</p>
<p>"Fufufu, to conspire to give Orabeoni honmei choco without me, that's impossible." Although she was an Uninvited Guest, Taira Kanna naturally sat in an empty Seat as if she had been invited. Minamoto Risa frowned at Taira Kanna, but since it was an SD character, it was just cute.</p>
<p>"Haa... Alright, anyone object to Taira Kanna participating in the Meeting?"</p>
<p>"...." There was no particular opposition. Everyone was curious about Taira Kanna's Valentine's Day gift, and if they needlessly kicked her out, she might secretly hack in, just as she had entered the VR space at will, and somehow only their Information would be stolen, and they would only end up providing ideas for the chocolate Taira Kanna would make.</p>
<p>"Since I arrived last, I'll speak first. I'm thinking of giving Orabeoni a three-tiered Pure White chocolate in the shape of a Wedding Cake...!" Taira Kanna said, as if making a Declaration, as soon as she sat down. A three-tiered chocolate in the shape of a Wedding Cake, there was a Reason why Taira Kanna's character was wearing a Wedding Dress.</p>
<p>"Quite impressive. I'm thinking of making three liquor chocolates of different Sizes, infused with Japanese sake." This time, Minamoto Risa spoke, and while ordinary people might not know what Minamoto Risa was referring to, most of the Daughters of prestigious families present here understood the meaning of the chocolate Minamoto Risa was preparing.</p>
<p>She had recreated Sansankudo (a ritual where a couple pledges their marriage by sharing omiki (sake offered to the gods) from three sake cups of different sizes—small, medium, and large), one of the steps in a traditional Japanese wedding ceremony, as Valentine's chocolate.</p>
<p>"A traditional wedding ceremony, huh.... You're quite formidable, Lisa...!" Gooooh! Taira Kanna and Minamoto Risa stood glaring at each other. Sparks flew between them, but Seizawa Shii, who had often seen their bickering since childhood, raised her Hand as if it were nothing.</p>
<p>"It's my Turn now, right? I'm not going to tell you, because I know it won't overlap anyway. No one would think of it as a Valentine's Day gift, so don't Worry about duplication!"</p>
<p>What Seizawa Shii said was, 'I'm not gonna tell you!' Minamoto Risa and Taira Kanna, who had just been having an Eye fight, simultaneously shrieked at Seizawa Shii as if nothing had happened.</p>
<p>"What do you mean, Shii?!"</p>
<p>"Shii-chan! We agreed to tell each other everything!"</p>
<p>"Nooo. It's something Ryuta-cchi and I have Memories of, just the two of us...♥" The Ogre character, pressing both Index Fingers together and speaking shyly, was a bit awkward, but the two of them, who knew Seizawa Shii well, knew that no matter how much they pressed her, Seizawa Shii wouldn't say what shape the chocolate she would make was, so they decided to let it go.</p>
<p>"I haven't thought of anything yet. Just know that I'm going to mix strawberry fluid into white chocolate to make it red. Don't Worry, I won't copy anyone after hearing their Story."</p>
<p>This time it was Otagi Yuka. Like a Vampire, she seemed to be planning to make something with red chocolate. The blood-like Appearance made everyone feel a little eerie.</p>
<p>"Alik is.... going to make Maid Cafe Omelet Rice." True to her SD Maid character, Alik said she would make Omelet Rice. But Valentine's Day is a day for exchanging chocolates. Perhaps because Alik was Russian, she didn't seem to know much about Japanese Valentine's Day culture.</p>
<p>"Um... Alik, Valentine's Day isn't Omelet Rice, it's choco...."</p>
<p>"Alik, Omelet Rice." Konoe Akina tried to explain to Alik, but Alik, with a resolute Expression, just said Omelet Rice.</p>
<p>"R-right. Not Alik Omelet Rice...."</p>
<p>"Alik, Omelet Rice." Other Friends tried to persuade her to make chocolate, but Alik, like a stubborn character, only said, "Alik, Omelet Rice." In the end, the special class Students, who gave up on Persuasion Alik, moved on to the next Turn.</p>
<p>"I won't copy anyone, so.... I'm going to make eye-shaped chocolates." Konoe Akina announced that she would make eye-shaped chocolates, signifying her Maan. Although she didn't elaborate, she was thinking of making the eye shape, representing her Maan, with heart-shaped pupils. Naturally, since no one else was thinking of making slightly eerie eyeball-shaped chocolates as a Valentine's Day gift, everyone inwardly thought, 'I won,' and moved on.</p>
<p>"I'm thinking of making mine in my current Appearance~!"</p>
<p>"I'll make mine heart-shaped to prove my loyalty to my Liege/Master." Naruse Shiyo said she would make chocolate in the shape of her current character, an SD Fairy Dragon, and Sakagami Rena said she would make heart-shaped chocolate to prove her loyalty.</p>
<p>"If it's heart-shaped, isn't it just a regular heart shape?" At Seizawa Shii's explanation, Sakagami Rena silently displayed a Photo of an actual heart. The Heart Sakagami Rena was referring to seemed not to be the heart of love that the Students were thinking of. In fact, Sakagami Rena, who spoke with her, was so strange that Naruse Shiyo, who was making a Fairy Dragon-shaped chocolate with a reptilian form and wings, was also incomprehensible.</p>
<p>"I haven't decided yet because I'm going to make it with the God I serve. O-of course, don't Worry, I won't make it overlap!" Urabe Konoka seemed to have not decided yet. Like a Shrine Maiden, she said she would make it with the God she served, so the God's opinion seemed to be paramount. Gradually, the special class Students looked at each other with fresh Expressions. Most of the Students, seeing that they were preparing strange Valentine's chocolates, felt that they were not normal.</p>
<p>"I'm also thinking of preparing something similar to my current character." Saionji Diana said. Since her Appearance was that of a teddy bear sitting in a wheelchair, they wondered if she would make teddy bear-shaped chocolates. However, it was "similar".... Something bothered them.</p>
<p>"What's similar?" When the deformed Ogre, Seizawa Shii, unable to contain her curiosity, asked, Saionji Diana turned her Head towards Seizawa Shii and looked at her. Since it was a teddy bear with no Expression, despite its cute Appearance, it felt somewhat eerie.</p>
<p>"I'm thinking of making it in the shape of a teddy bear, but without Arms or Legs."</p>
<p>"...." At Saionji Diana's Words, everyone, including Seizawa Shii, fell into Silence.</p>
<p>"Shall we move on to the next? Hmm.... Only Oofuchi Erika is left." Before the Atmosphere turned cold at Saionji Diana's Words, Minamoto Risa pointed to Oofuchi Erika, who was the last one remaining. Oofuchi Erika, with a Head shaped like an old CRT Monitor, displayed a ^o^ Expression on her Screen, then opened her Mouth.</p>
<p>"I've decided to make chick and Chicken shapes." Chick and Chicken? It was a shape that didn't suit Valentine's Day at all. After everyone had stated their Valentine's chocolate shapes, all the Students, except Alik, looked at each other and thought they weren't normal. While thinking that the chocolate they themselves had mentioned was normal.</p>
