<p>In the fixer district of Hussa, the largest building was a black building with a red neon sign that read "Black Tech."</p>
<p>The building was constructed with black, special glass that made the interior almost invisible, and the glass was soundproofed and bulletproofed. Just one sheet was very expensive, but covering the entire building with it showed how much money this company, Black Tech, had.</p>
<p>The man who created such a successful company that could own such a building was Gomisawa Renya.</p>
<p>He was the former vice president of Seryu Military Company. After the president, Sawayanagi Ryuta, died, he took more than half of the Seryu Military Company employees and became independent, creating a company of this size in just six years.</p>
<p>When Sawayanagi Ryuta, the former president of Seryu Military Company, was rescuing the test subject children from the laboratory, Gomisawa Renya secretly backed up all the data while claiming to be destroying the research institute's data.</p>
<p>After becoming independent from Seryu Military Company, Gomisawa Renya sold the unidentified research institute data to two major Japanese groups and several overseas companies for a very high price. In return, Gomisawa Renya had money in his hands that he had never had in his life.</p>
<p>With that money, Gomisawa Renya acquired several promising companies in the fixer district, where there were only small PMCs, and combined them with the personnel from Seryu Military Company who had followed him to create Black Tech.</p>
<p>Black Tech, which had a different scale from other PMCs in the fixer district from the beginning, was on a roll and is now a large PMC ranked around 15th in Japan. This was thanks to the research data Gomisawa Renya stole.</p>
<p>From the beginning, Black Tech had a scientific research team that was rare to see in small and medium-sized PMCs, and that research team was dedicated to interpreting the research data that Gomisawa Renya had stolen.</p>
<p>The research resulted in several combat potions and physical modification results, but further research did not progress because the test subjects in the laboratory were essential for the research.</p>
<p>After receiving a report four years ago that test subjects were needed, Gomisawa Renya began to harass Seryu Military Company. He used tricks to take away the training grounds and equipment owned by Seryu Military Company, and this time, he pressured Sato Tojima, who held most of the debt, to obtain the rights to Seryu Military Company's debt.</p>
<p>,With this much, they won't reject our acquisition offer, right? It's better to have the name Black Tech than to go bankrupt altogether."</p>
<p>Gomisawa Renya was a middle-aged man with slicked-back hair and a mustache. Perhaps because he was a businessman from a military background, his body looked solid and his eyes were very sharp. Next to him stood a man named Tomano Toki, who had been with him since his days at Seryu Military Company, with the title of Chief Secretary.</p>
<p>,Even Azuma, who is so stubborn, will accept it this time."</p>
<p>,I shouldn't have said from the beginning that I needed test subjects."</p>
<p>From the beginning, this was not the first attempt to acquire Seryu Military Company. When he first heard the report, he tried to acquire it, and at that time, the story was progressing smoothly in the beginning. However, the problem was that he had unknowingly mentioned that he needed test subjects.</p>
<p>After that, Sawayanagi Azuma informed all the employees of Seryu Military Company of Black Tech's purpose, making it difficult to persuade them individually, and Sawayanagi Azuma's precognitive abilities allowed her to avoid Black Tech's behind-the-scenes maneuvers, and that's how it has been until now.</p>
<p>,It's better for the family to be in our hands than to be scattered."</p>
<p>Gomisawa Renya smiled grimly. While Gomisawa Renya was smiling, the door to the president's office opened as if it would break, and a man rushed in, panting. He was one of the secretaries working under Tomano Toki.</p>
<p>,S, President-nim! There's a rumor that a woman is asking about Seryu Military Company!"</p>
<p>,What? Have you identified the woman?"</p>
<p>,I'm telling you as soon as I heard this information, so I don't know her identity yet...."</p>
<p>,Find out quickly! Quickly!"</p>
<p>At Gomisawa Renya's shout, the secretary who came to report left the president's office again with a frightened expression. Gomisawa Renya's expression became complicated as he watched the scene.</p>
<p>On the surface, Seryu Military Company looked terrible. Without a systematic work system, they were just occasionally taking on jobs based on the connections that their predecessors had built up, and the facilities were terrible and unremarkable.</p>
<p>Only a very few knew that the personnel of Seryu Military Company were excellent, and only a few of those who knew that knew that the people of Seryu Military Company were espers. Gomisawa Renya was one of those few.</p>
<p>,Toki."</p>
<p>,Yes, President-nim."</p>
<p>,You go and find out. I don't know where she came from, so don't be rude. Find out why she's asking about Seryu Military Company and what she's trying to do to Seryu Military Company."</p>
<p>,Understood."</p>
<p>Gomisawa Renya entrusted the task to Tomano Toki, the chief secretary, thinking that it was not something that the secretary who had just left could handle. Tomano Toki bowed deeply and left the president's office.</p>
