<p>Taira Shizuku said, "Have a good talk with Grandfather," and went elsewhere. Before she left, I asked her, "Aren't you having lunch?" and she replied that she had no Appetite and wouldn't eat lunch. She said she was full from watching me eat just now....</p>
<p>Of course, I did eat so much that merely watching me would make one full. Taira Shizuku kept offering, and it felt rude not to eat. Since I had a lunch meal with Taira Danjo right after, it seemed I would have to seriously hit the Athletics when I returned to the Human Resources Development Center today.</p>
<p>"Ahem...! First, let's have lunch and then talk."</p>
<p>"Yes...."</p>
<p>I had intended to talk first to help Digestion of the wagashi I had just eaten.... I replied with a slightly sullen tone and picked up my chopsticks.</p>
<p>Of course, lunch was delicious. It was Japanese cuisine with light seasoning, so it didn't quite suit my Preference for strong seasoning. Nevertheless, the fact that it tasted delicious was probably due to the exceptional cooking Skill of the Main family's chef.</p>
<p>"You were Attacked yesterday, weren't you?"</p>
<p>"Yes."</p>
<p>Taira Danjo, having finished his meal, started not with the Request but with the Story of yesterday's shooting incident. Certainly, a shooting incident is bigger than a mere Request.</p>
<p>"Do you know who Attacked me?"</p>
<p>"First, let me correct your Words. They did not Attack you. They Attacked your Mother."</p>
<p>They didn't Attack me, but Jinmija Yeosa? Why? As far as I know, my mom is just a company employee working at an Advertisement company.</p>
<p>"To cause you, and by extension, me and the Tairaga Family, pain."</p>
<p>"What? Who...?"</p>
<p>"Look at this."</p>
<p>As Taira Danjo crooked his finger, one of the secretaries standing behind him handed me a document. The document contained a description of a certain person.</p>
<p>Tochimura Kaminjo... Deputy Head of the Planning and Coordination Office at Minamoto Group Headquarters... When Taira Danjo showed me this document, it seemed to be the work of the Minamoto side.</p>
<p>"Is this the work of the Minamoto side?"</p>
<p>"That bastard Tochimura is classified as a radical faction even within Minamoto. What our Group has found out is that this Tochimura is behind it. I will give you an Opportunity. If you are a Man of the Tairaga Family, you must be able to parry the sword that attacks you and stab the Opponent's neck. What do you say, will you seek Revenge yourself?"</p>
<p>"Yes. Please let me handle it with my own Hands."</p>
<p>Taira Danjo nodded his Head, as if understanding my Words.</p>
<p>"I will send a few personnel to help you, so go ahead and do it as you see fit."</p>
<p>"I have a few Questions, Grandfather."</p>
<p>"Yes, you must resolve your curiosities. Ask freely."</p>
<p>"Why are Taira and Minamoto on such bad terms?"</p>
<p>There was one thing I had always been curious about. When playing MegaCorp Youngest Son or the previous work, TMC, it was only explained that Taira and Minamoto were on bad terms, but the Reason was never given. I wondered what had happened in the past to make their relationship so bad.</p>
<p>"Outwardly, the Japanese people believe that Minamoto and Taira are on bad terms because of the Genpei War in the past. But that is not true. Two Families cannot keep fighting over an incident from a thousand years ago."</p>
<p>The Genpei War refers to the civil war that took place between the Minamoto clan of the Genji and the Taira clan of the Heishi during the late Heian period, a conflict that resulted in the Minamoto's victory and the founding of the Kamakura Shogunate.</p>
<p>Of course, I also didn't think that the thousand-year-old lingering resentment between Taira and Minamoto still remained.</p>
<p>"Furthermore, it is unclear whether we are even the Taira of that time. This is because warriors and daimyo without a clear Bloodline, who were ruined by Minamoto, indiscriminately used the surname Taira. Do not speak of this Story anywhere. There are many Guys in Japan who quibble over such legitimacy issues."</p>
<p>Ultimately, the current Taira Group is likely not the Taira of the Genpei War, so there is no way they would harbor the grudge that Japanese people associate with the Genpei War. Moreover, the Minamoto side was the victor, so they would have no grudge.</p>
<p>"Is Minamoto also not the Minamoto of the Genpei period, like us?"</p>
<p>"That side is correct. In fact, they were a distant collateral branch, but they achieved great Business success and, in the middle, I understand they received a Blood Transfusion from the Minamoto main branch."</p>
<p>To buy a Family's Blood with Money, the Minamoto Group are quite something.</p>
<p>You became a Seongin and received the surname Taira, so you haven't had this opportunity. I will briefly tell you the origin of the Taira Group. Taira Seikou, founded by my great-grandfather Taira Kiyotaka in October 1945, after Japan lost World War II, is the beginning of our Taira Group. Taira Kiyotaka, who had lived in the USA before the war, entered postwar Japan and expanded the enterprise by acquiring the land and factories of war criminals, which the US military sold off, using the connections he had built in the USA until then.</p>
<p>That Taira Seikou was the beginning of the Taira Group was a Story I heard for the first time. When I asked Taira Danjo if I hadn't heard of a subsidiary called Taira Seikou, he told me that Taira Seikou had now become Taira Robotics, one of the two major holding companies of the Taira Group. So that's it, it wasn't gone, it just changed its name.</p>
<p>"So, ultimately, the Reason Taira and Minamoto dislike each other isn't because of the Genpei War, but for other Reasons, right?"</p>
<p>"There are several Reasons. The origin of the Taira Group I just mentioned is one Reason."</p>
<p>"Surely, the Factory that the Taira Group acquired from the US military was Minamoto's Possession?"</p>
<p>"That's right."</p>
<p>After that, the two Families clashed over every single Matter. However, thinking that such Quarrels were bad for Business, the two Families decided to reconcile.</p>
<p>The method of reconciliation was a marriage alliance, and after much discussion, a woman from the Minamoto family married into the Taira family. This was during the generation of Taira Danjo's grandfather. However, Minamoto Aono, who married into the Taira family from the Minamoto family, committed suicide in less than a year, turning the two families into sworn enemies.</p>
<p>The Minamoto side claimed that the Taira Family tormented Minamoto Aono, causing her to commit suicide, while the Taira side claimed they had sent a Woman with a Mental illness, but it is said that the Truth of which side was correct is unknown.</p>
<p>"Yes, I believe this sufficiently and neutrally explains why we and Minamoto are on bad terms. Do you have any other Questions?"</p>
<p>"Is Mimata Chieri alright?"</p>
<p>"She's perfectly fine. She should wake up around Evening today."</p>
<p>Even if it wasn't her Torso, she was shot in the Shoulder or Arm, and she'll be up the very next day? Was the Taira Group's medical Skill that amazing? Or there was also the Possibility that Mimata Chieri had undergone Body Modification. It could be both.</p>
<p>"I see. That's Fortunate."</p>
<p>"One of the personnel I'll assign to you is Mimata, so you'll get along well."</p>
<p>To be deployed immediately for a mission was truly astonishing.</p>
<p>"Right. The Attack incident from yesterday is also concluded, so the Wish ticket I gave you remains. What is your Request?"</p>
<p>"I want to acquire a PMC, but there are many Conditions. I would be grateful if Grandfather could recommend me so that I don't need those qualifications."</p>
<p>"Do you know the Price of that recommendation? Do you know the Price your Uncles and Aunt paid to establish or acquire a PMC from me? That Request cannot be granted with just one Wish ticket."</p>
<p>No, old man, what do you mean you won't grant it after giving me a Wish ticket?</p>
<p>I was a little Frustrating, but I understood. From Taira Danjo's perspective, it would be similar to giving a Wish ticket expecting a Snack, but then being asked to buy a house, which he couldn't grant. I had no choice but to study for the qualifications.... Just as I was resigning myself, Taira Danjo opened his mouth.</p>
<p>"You stupid bastard! Even if you acquire a PMC, if your Uncles or Aunt find out, you will immediately become a Target for all three of them. And that's not how you make your Request."</p>
<p>"If not..."</p>
<p>There were two Problems. The Price for me, bearing the Taira Family name, to own a PMC was high, and even if I paid that Price and Taira Danjo granted my Request, Taira Ichiro, Jiro, and Kokoro would all Attack me. In that case...!</p>
<p>"Please send me a Woman who has the qualifications for a PMC without needing a recommendation, as my secretary or in a similar position."</p>
<p>I just needed to set up a Figurehead. Originally, I was going to ask Taira Danjo for a recommendation because obtaining the qualifications was troublesome, but if there was a Talent who didn't need a recommendation, that would suffice. The specific Request for a Woman was so I could secure her Loyalty with my Body.</p>
<p>"I don't know who you take after to be so lustful for Women! I can assign such a Person to you. But can you truly trust the Person I assign to you?"</p>
<p>"Even if it's someone Grandfather assigned, I'll just make her my Person."</p>
<p>"Hmph! Your Confidence is overflowing. Very well, I'll confirm again. So, your Request is to assign a Young Woman in her early twenties to early thirties with PMC qualification requirements as your secretary or in an equivalent position, right?"</p>
<p>Taira Danjo confirmed my Request once more. Hmm, let me think again, there might be a trap I haven't considered.</p>
<p>"Hmm! I'll change it a little. Please assign a 'Young Woman in her early twenties to early thirties' with PMC qualification requirements as my secretary or in an equivalent position."</p>
<p>"What a shame! I was thinking of assigning a 70-year-old Grandmother."</p>
<p>This old man...! Even so, 70 is too much!</p>
<p>"However, this Request is also more expensive than the Wish ticket you possess."</p>
<p>He's acting like he'll grant it, but surely he's not going to refuse again? If he does, I'll really become perverse, you old bastard...!</p>
<p>"Don't worry, I will grant your Request. Instead, you must perform three missions that I will assign later. Will you do that?"</p>
<p>"Three?"</p>
<p>"Yes. I have one in mind, and I'll think of the other two later."</p>
<p>In my opinion, Taira Danjo already had something he wanted me to do, and this Wish ticket usage seemed to be a Trick to solve that as well. But I had no choice. Powerless as I was, I had no choice but to endure the tyranny of the old man Taira Danjo.</p>
<p>"I will do it."</p>
<p>"Now, sign here."</p>
<p>Taira Danjo presented the prepared document. The document stated, "I, Taira Ryuta, will faithfully perform 3 missions as a Condition for Taira Danjo granting my Request." As expected, he had intended to make me do something from the beginning. Cunning old man....</p>
<p>I signed a little roughly and returned it to Taira Danjo. Taira Danjo, receiving the document, gave a satisfied Smile.</p>
<p>"I will send you a Young Woman in her early twenties to early thirties with PMC qualifications, as you requested. Now, you may go."</p>
<p>"If possible, please send a Beautiful Woman."</p>
<p>"Crazy Bastard!"</p>
<p>Finally, I received the Hospital Address where Mimata Chieri was hospitalized and left the Main family.</p>
