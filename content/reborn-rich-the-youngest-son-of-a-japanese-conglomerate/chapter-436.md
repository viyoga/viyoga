<p>Jack Henderson, as I remember him, was an old, one-eyed geezer with slicked-back white hair. In the future, during the TMC era, he was one of the three elders at SPF headquarters, mainly handing out missions that favored the hardliners. If you took a vampire's point of view or opposed the hardline stance, you could fight him as a boss in the SPF event, and I remember him being incredibly strong, as befitting a boss fight.</p>
<p>He's young now, so I didn't recognize him right away, but knowing this guy is Jack Henderson is still a win. I still remember what techniques he uses and what his fighting patterns are, so it'll be helpful in combat.</p>
<p>,Are you happy to see me? You killed everyone in my mansion...!,</p>
<p>Otagi Yuka burst out in anger, but she didn't do anything rash like charging out alone. Still, it seems like the experience she gained fighting Kurotsuki-doo for a year is helping. Looking at Otagi Yuka's condition, I could feel that she was furious in her heart, but her mind was cold.</p>
<p>,Oh, that happened. So what? Humans who help vampires are no different from vampires, so I just did my duty as a vampire hunter and took care of it.,</p>
<p>As expected, he's not someone you can reason with at all. Helping vampires makes you no different from vampires... So, if I help a vampire grandma carry her luggage, does that make me a vampire too? Otagi Yuka must have been thinking the same thing, as she drew her sword instead of answering. Roughly looking around, among the 200-plus guys gathered, the strong-looking ones were Jack Henderson, the fake Blade black guy who caused the tragedy at the Otagi family mansion, a Latino guy who looked like he was from Central or South America holding the latest H&amp;K MP5 submachine guns in both hands, and an Asian guy with a katana at his waist who was probably the leader of the Tokyo branch's support troops.</p>
<p>,I'll take that black guy.,</p>
<p>Otagi Yuka opened her mouth. He was the one who killed her nanny and the servants of the mansion, who were like family, so it was only natural that Otagi Yuka would target him more than anyone else.</p>
<p>,I think that Japanese guy is a swordsman, so I'll take him.,</p>
<p>Shimokawabe Airen immediately chose the Tokyo branch support troop leader, as if she was looking forward to a life-or-death duel between swordsmen.</p>
<p>,I'll take that blonde guy who looks like the leader.,</p>
<p>Alik or Saionji Diana might have been in trouble if they had chosen Jack Henderson, so I chose Jack Henderson. Now there was only one strong guy left, and Alik and Saionji Diana talked for a while, then drew their weapons as if they had made a decision.</p>
<p>,What did you decide to do?,</p>
<p>,Alik decided to fight that Latino guy. I need to fight as many people as possible and get combat data because I had a new cyber implant installed.,</p>
<p>When I asked, it seemed like Saionji Diana was going to fight the 200 goons while we fought the strong guys. You can't know who will have a difficult fight until you actually fight, but there were so many enemies left that Saionji Diana was sure to have a very difficult fight too.</p>
<p>,I'll take care of my guy quickly and come help.,</p>
<p>,I'm not alone, Eve and Sakagami are here too, so don't push yourself too hard.,</p>
<p>Come to think of it, Saionji wasn't alone. Eve and Sakagami Rena, who were sniping, were also there, so there were three of them, so they would be able to hold out longer than I thought. Saionji Diana's voice also sounded confident, so I wasn't too worried.</p>
<p>,Looks like you've decided on your opponents. Shall we fight now?,</p>
<p>Jack Henderson said, taking out a pistol. And then, a moment of silence... The tension gradually increased. Someone couldn't stand the tension and made a rustling noise. Like releasing a rubber band stretched to its limit, everyone ran towards each other. The battle began.</p>
<p>###</p>
<p>The Latino guy, Angel Ramirez, bit his lip. Unlike his team leader, Jack Henderson, or Malik Lieberman, Angel Ramirez was not a hardline SPF member. He wasn't a moderate either, but had a tendency somewhere between moderate and hardline.</p>
<p>So he was against killing the civilians in the Otagi mansion, and he didn't like fighting the small-framed girl who was charging at him now to fight him.</p>
<p>But Angel Ramirez was a veteran SPF agent. Once he entered combat, he had to fight in a cold state, whether he liked the fight or not, and he could fight without hesitation.</p>
<p>Angel Ramirez fired two guns full of silver bullets at the small-framed girl, Alik, who was running towards him.</p>
<p>The moment he fired the gun, Angel Ramirez felt that his bullets would not work on this girl. Angel had a slight precognitive ability in addition to his wide-area invisibility buff ability, so he could sometimes feel the outcome of this battle.</p>
<p>,This battle is going to be tough...!,</p>
<p>But now that the battle had begun, he couldn't run away. At that moment, he saw dozens of bullets fired by Angel being blocked by Alik's electric field and turning into molten metal, falling to the floor of the shrine.</p>
<p>,Guns won't work. Then...!,</p>
<p>The moment Angel realized that guns wouldn't work, he threw away his guns and put his hand into his pocket. He grabbed a silver shrapnel grenade in his hand. Angel threw the grenade at Alik as soon as he grabbed it.</p>
<p>Alik, who was running, must have thought that it would be impossible to just block the grenade with her electric field, so she used neural acceleration to move at an incredible speed to avoid it. It was like she was moving at several times the speed alone in a world moving at 1x speed. Rather, the grenade exploded, sweeping through a wide area and damaging the PMC members and terrorists hired by the SPF. Silver fragments rained down on Alik, but the fragments could not penetrate the electric field.</p>
<p>Alik didn't just avoid it, but continued to maintain her incredible speed and ran towards Angel Ramirez. Electricity crackled and flowed along the path Alik moved. At the end of that path of electricity was Angel Ramirez.</p>
<p>Since the distance was close, he couldn't throw the grenade again. Angel Ramirez gave up throwing the grenade and drew the kukri he was wearing on his waist.</p>
<p>Angel Ramirez, who mainly used firearms and didn't prefer close combat, was in the elite Striking Team 1 of the New York branch, so his skills in handling the kukri were not bad.</p>
<p>,Haaap!,</p>
<p>But Alik couldn't be dealt with by just being not bad. Angel Ramirez swung his kukri with all his might, but he couldn't stop Alik's katana, Cheongcheonbyeokryeok, from being swung dazzlingly and the afterimages of lightning from adorning the sky. It was a very flashy swordsmanship. Angel Ramirez could only accept his defeat and death as he watched his arms and legs flying in the air with the lightning.</p>
<p>###</p>
<p>,I'm Ankoji Satoi. May I know your name?,</p>
<p>Ankoji Satoi, wearing a black suit and holding a pistol in one hand and a katana in the other, asked the female swordsman in front of him for her identity. He couldn't tell who she was because she was wearing a cyber suit and a helmet.</p>
<p>,I'm just a teacher of a student who was harmed.,</p>
<p>,I've heard rumors that there's a great female swordsman at St. Francis Girls' School.,</p>
<p>Shimokawabe Airen didn't want to reveal her name to a corpse who would die soon, but Ankoji Satoi kept talking to her in a smooth manner. Even though she was a teacher at St. Francis Girls' School, Shimokawabe Airen's reputation had spread to some extent. Shimokawabe Airen drew her katana and pointed it at Ankoji Satoi as if she was annoyed by the answer.</p>
<p>,If you don't want to talk, I can't help it.,</p>
<p>Tang, tang, tang!</p>
<p>Ankoji Satoi fired his pistol in a surprise attack. Shimokawabe Airen showed the art of drawing and swinging her katana in an instant to deflect the bullets, just like she did when climbing the stairs. But the moment she deflected them, the bullets exploded.</p>
<p>While climbing the stairs, Ankoji Satoi had confirmed that everyone except the main target, Otagi Yuka, were not vampires, so he had changed the bullets to explosive bullets in advance. He judged that explosive bullets would be more effective than silver bullets because most of the opponents were human.</p>
<p>Ankoji Satoi thought that Shimokawabe Airen would be surprised by the unexpected situation caused by the explosion, and rushed in and swung his katana. But when the smoke cleared, Shimokawabe Airen's expression was not flustered at all, but cold itself.</p>
<p>Shimokawabe Airen, who easily blocked Ankoji Satoi's sword that was swung horizontally to cut Shimokawabe Airen's waist, cut off Ankoji Satoi's neck in retaliation. Ankoji Satoi rolled his body to avoid it because of an unknown ominous feeling that he would block it.</p>
<p>,Your instinctive sense is still alive.,</p>
<p>Ankoji Satoi felt bad about Shimokawabe Airen's words as if she was evaluating him, but he still smiled.</p>
<p>,You have a good body too. Your chest is a little lacking though...,</p>
<p>Ankoji Satoi said that to excite Shimokawabe Airen and disrupt her movements, but he didn't know that Shimokawabe Airen would do her best because of what he said.</p>
<p>Shimokawabe Airen's eyes pierced through her helmet and her sword was swung at an incredible speed. Ankoji Satoi couldn't even think of receiving the attack and had to avoid it once again.</p>
<p>For dozens of moves, Ankoji Satoi had no choice but to keep running around and avoiding. It was such an overwhelmingly tyrannical blow that he thought his sword would break if he blocked it with his sword. Ankoji Satoi was a very skilled person just by continuing to avoid Shimokawabe Airen's sword that was being swung at an incredible speed.</p>
<p>,It's over now.,</p>
<p>Shimokawabe Airen, who had been swinging her sword for a while, left those words and put her katana back into its scabbard. Ankoji Satoi was puzzled by what this meant, but he was soon overwhelmed by the fear caused by the anomaly that occurred around him.</p>
<p>Kashima Kiichishin-ryu Kenkai Kuuzan (Sword World Empty Slash)</p>
<p>The empty spaces that Shimokawabe Airen had been cutting until now suddenly cracked and tore, and katanas poured out of that space and pierced Ankoji Satoi's body. All the sword strikes that Shimokawabe Airen had been swinging until now were not in vain.</p>
<p>Ankoji Satoi died instantly, and Shimokawabe Airen muttered and sighed.</p>
<p>,I had too much expectation because he was a swordsman.,</p>
<p>----</p>
<p>Cyber suit-wearing homeroom teacher.</p>
<p>Unlike what Ankoji Satoi said, her chest is not lacking haha.</p>
