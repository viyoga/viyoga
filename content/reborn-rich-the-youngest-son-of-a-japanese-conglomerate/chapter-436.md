<p>Every weekend, I would leave Miyashi and go to Tokyo or Seoul, but I decided to take a break this week. As October arrived, the exam period was approaching, so I had to start preparing exam questions. I stayed home, reviewing past Korean exam questions to identify key points for creating the exam.</p>
<p>Since I wasn't originally a teacher, I had to check everything one by one, which made it a time-consuming task.</p>
<p>But I wouldn't have to suffer anymore if I just finished it this weekend, so I braved my laziness and sat at my desk, creating questions on Sunday, following Saturday.</p>
<p>Ding-dong~♬</p>
<p>I was biting on my pen and creating questions with a bored expression when the bell suddenly rang. Who could it be? I didn't order any food...</p>
<p>,Coming~!</p>
<p>I got up from my desk and headed to the front door. After looking at the intercom screen installed next to the front door, I couldn't help but wear a puzzled expression.</p>
<p>Kurayamata Kirie was standing on the intercom screen. These days, for security reasons, you can see visitors from various angles in case they're carrying a weapon, and after checking, I saw that she was holding something like a shopping bag in her hand.</p>
<p>It's a bit creepy...? How does she know my address? And why is she standing in front of my house? Various questions flashed through my mind.</p>
<p>I opened the front door with many questions in my mind. Kurayamata Kirie was still standing there, looking at me with sharp eyes. She slightly bowed her head as if it was a greeting when she saw me.</p>
<p>,Kurayamata-sensei, what brings you here...?</p>
<p>,...I wanted to give you a gift.</p>
<p>Kurayamata Kirie answered quietly to my question. A gift all of a sudden? The shopping bag I saw on the intercom must have been the gift. Something's fishy...</p>
<p>,Ah, please come in.</p>
<p>I couldn't keep her standing outside, so I let her in. I sat Kurayamata Kirie on the sofa in the living room and brought her coffee and cookies for a snack.</p>
<p>,Please have some coffee.</p>
<p>,Yes, this is a gift.</p>
<p>Kurayamata Kirie hands me a wrapped gift box. It was a moderately large box, so I don't know what the gift is. I can't open it now, so I'll have to open it later.</p>
<p>,By the way, how did you know my address?</p>
<p>,Ah, I happened to go out on Friday and saw Yamashiro-sensei leaving work....</p>
<p>It was somehow a lie. Kurayamata Kirie left work much earlier, but it was too much of a coincidence that she saw me leaving work much later. I didn't know where Kurayamata Kirie's house was, but there wasn't much around here. The only thing there was a coin laundry.</p>
<p>,I see. I was creating exam questions, but how are you doing with the Japanese classics exam?</p>
<p>I couldn't say anything because I was suspicious, so I asked about the exam questions.</p>
<p>,The exam is originally created by Azusawa-nim, the Japanese classics teacher, and she highlighted what would be on the exam in my textbook. She told me to emphasize it when teaching the special class students.</p>
<p>,Ah, I see.</p>
<p>Well... It's strange to ask a student teacher who just came a day ago to create exam questions. It was right for the teacher who was originally teaching to create the questions. I'm suffering like this now because I'm a formally appointed teacher, not a student teacher.</p>
<p>,I heard that Kurayamata-sensei also attended St. Francisco Girls' Academy?</p>
<p>,Ah.... Yes. So yesterday, I visited the teachers who were there when I attended and gave them gifts.</p>
<p>Ah, it seemed like she gave gifts to the teachers who had been there since her school days and came to give me a gift as well. It wasn't strange that she brought a gift.</p>
<p>After talking about Miyashi and St. Francisco Girls' Academy, there was nothing more to say. Kurayamata Kirie was a bit taciturn, and even when we were talking, she kept looking at me as if she was thinking about something else, so the conversation kept getting cut off.</p>
<p>,I'll be going now. Please take care of me in the future, Ryuta-sensei....</p>
<p>Kurayamata Kirie got up from her seat and suddenly called me by my first name and bowed her head. After Kurayamata Kirie left, I opened the wrapped gift.</p>
<p>It was a chocolate gift set made with Uji matcha, a specialty of Kyoto, where the Kurayamata family is located. Matcha chocolate.... It looks pretty delicious.</p>
<p>Tuk.</p>
<p>I opened the box and was about to eat a chocolate when a rather expensive-looking envelope fell out. What is it? Did she even write a letter? I opened the envelope without thinking.</p>
<p>I opened the envelope without thinking, but I couldn't help but frown at the contents.</p>
<p>To Yamashiro Ryuta-nim.</p>
<p>Thank you for welcoming me as a student teacher. I look forward to working with you in the future.</p>
<p>I am curious about your journey from the Taira Group Human Resources Development Center to St. Francisco Girls' Academy that you experienced.</p>
<p>If it's not too much trouble, I would like to hear the story in detail.</p>
<p>I hope you will listen to my story in the future as well.</p>
<p>From Kurayamata Kirie.</p>
<p>Is this a threat? But how did she know that I was from the Taira Group...? Looking at the contents, it seems like she's saying, ,I know you're from Taira, so listen to me.</p>
<p>I was unexpectedly hit. But seeing that it says ,To Yamashiro Ryuta-nim,, it seems like she doesn't know my true identity yet, even though she knows I'm affiliated with Taira. If she knew that I was Taira Ryuta, she wouldn't have threatened me with just a letter like this.</p>
<p>Ding-dong~♬</p>
<p>The doorbell rings again. It can't be Kurayamata Kirie who left and came back... Who is it this time?</p>
<p>When I checked the intercom, the masked girl Sakagami Rena was standing there. How does she know my address too?</p>
<p>Surely, it's not like you can just search ,Yamashiro Ryuta's house, on Gugol and it comes right up, right?</p>
<p>I opened the door while having silly thoughts.</p>
<p>,Sakagami, what brings you to teacher's house?</p>
<p>,...Excuse me.</p>
<p>Sakagami Rena, who didn't even ask to come in, looked around and entered the house. What's wrong with her again...? She's similar to Kurayamata Kirie in that she's taciturn.</p>
<p>,Sit down.</p>
<p>This time, I brought out coffee with the matcha chocolate that I received as a threat and gift from Kurayamata Kirie. I sat across from Sakagami Rena, unwrapped the matcha chocolate, and put it in my mouth.</p>
<p>I felt the bittersweet taste of matcha along with the sweetness of the chocolate. Sakagami Rena took a sip of coffee and looked at me.</p>
<p>Sakagami Rena's purple eyes were looking at me with a gaze that seemed to pierce through me.</p>
<p>,You may already know, but the Sakagami family is a family of ninjas.</p>
<p>A sudden ninja coming out. By the way, when I looked at the information written by the Taira information team, I thought, ,Isn't it a ninja? because they've been a family skilled in espionage since ancient times, but it was really a ninja family.</p>
<p>Usually, the ninjas of the Sakagami family live with the Sakagami family for their entire lives, but when they become Chunin (中忍: middle-level ninja), they are given the opportunity to choose another lord.</p>
<p>I never thought I would hear the word Chunin in a real conversation. When I urged her to continue with my eyes as if saying, ,So what?, Sakagami Rena opened her mouth again.</p>
<p>,Sakagami Rena, the 31st of the Sakagami family, wants to use her right to choose a lord as a Chunin for Taira Ryuta-nim.</p>
<p>I was drinking coffee, but I almost spit it out without realizing it. This is the second time my identity has been revealed today. Kurayamata Kirie didn't seem to know my exact name, but Sakagami Rena is a ninja, so she must have obtained information and found out my identity.</p>
<p>,Why me?</p>
<p>In fact, I hadn't really had a conversation with Sakagami Rena. Sakagami Rena herself was a very quiet girl and wore a mask to block people's attention.</p>
<p>,You are the only person who has cared about me besides my family. You sincerely worried about me during the picnic too....</p>
<p>If it's the picnic.... It must have been when I told her not to wear a mask at the beach. Was that so touching? Surely, if she's a ninja, I can use Sakagami Rena well.</p>
<p>,If you really won't regret it, I'll let you be my lord.</p>
<p>,Really?</p>
<p>Sakagami Rena, who seemed to not have expected much, widened her eyes and rejoiced when I said something positive. And she knelt down on one knee in front of me and bowed her head so that I couldn't say anything else.</p>
<p>,I, Sakagami Rena, will serve Taira Ryuta-nim as my lord and pledge my loyalty for life.</p>
<p>,I will also become a person who can be a role model as a lord.</p>
<p>I didn't know what to say to Sakagami Rena's oath of allegiance, so I just answered whatever came to my mind.</p>
<p>Should I give her her first order as a lord? Sakagami Rena was looking at me with eyes like a puppy wagging its tail, waiting for me to throw a ball. Suddenly, I noticed the mask that was covering half of Sakagami Rena's face.</p>
<p>That's when she was touched by me, so she said something with her mask....</p>
<p>,I'll give you your first order as a lord. You can't help it when you're working, but don't cover your pretty face and take off your mask in your daily life.</p>
<p>Sakagami Rena took off her mask at my first order. Her sharp nose, which was covered by the mask, was revealed, and her mouth, which was hidden by the mask, was smiling happily.</p>
<p>,Yes, I will follow my lord's command.</p>
