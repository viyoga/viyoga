<p>"By the way, who were you talking to?"</p>
<p>Minamoto Risa asked, glancing at my smartphone. I wondered if she'd noticed me holding my phone during practice.</p>
<p>"Oh, somehow Taira Kokoro-ssi found out and contacted me, telling me not to become a celebrity by joining Hyper Entertainment."</p>
<p>"Hmm...."</p>
<p>At the mention of Taira Kokoro's name, Minamoto Risa narrowed her eyes and looked at me. Was the name Taira Kokoro a bit provocative in front of Minamoto Risa, who is also a Minamoto?</p>
<p>"How long have you been close to the Taira side?"</p>
<p>"Well, to be honest, I wouldn't say we're exactly close. I only saw Taira Kanna briefly when we were young, and I've only seen Taira Kokoro-ssi a couple of times...."</p>
<p>I said that I was close to Taira Kanna because we had agreed to say we saw each other when we were young, and I said that I wasn't very close to Taira Kokoro because I hadn't seen her many times. In reality, I wasn't very close to people with the Taira surname either.... The only person with the Taira name I was close to was Yukiko. Besides, Yukiko only has the Taira surname; she's not actually a Taira relative.</p>
<p>"It might sound weird coming from a Minamoto like me.... Taira Kanna and Kokoro-ssi are okay. But you should be careful of Taira Ichiro, the eldest of the Taira family, and Jiro, the third."</p>
<p>Minamoto Risa seemed to be giving me sincere advice. Does she know something about Taira Ichiro and Taira Jiro? Of course, I also know from some investigation and experience that they're not decent people, but I didn't expect Minamoto Risa to advise me about it. Still, seeing her advise me like this means she cares about me.</p>
<p>"Yeah, I don't really plan on getting close."</p>
<p>"Good."</p>
<p>She smiled slightly at my words that I had no intention of getting close. I suddenly felt a playful curiosity and opened my mouth to ask Minamoto Risa something I was curious about.</p>
<p>"But surprisingly, you said Kanna is okay? I thought you two didn't get along."</p>
<p>"It's true that I don't get along with Taira Kanna. She's a nasty woman with a bad personality and I don't know what she's thinking.... But she's still a much better person than the two I just mentioned."</p>
<p>At this point, I was curious about what Minamoto Risa knew about Taira Ichiro and Jiro. But if I keep asking, it might seem like I'm prying too much, so I should just skip it for today and ask later. Instead, I should ask something else.</p>
<p>"Are there any people on the Minamoto side I should be careful of?"</p>
<p>"Don't even think about getting close to anyone on the Minamoto side except for me."</p>
<p>Minamoto Risa was firmly saying that about the Minamoto side as well. Does that mean the rest of the Minamoto are bad guys? Since Minamoto Risa belongs to the Minamoto family, she probably knows more, so that's why she's saying this.</p>
<p>"I already think I'm close to a Minamoto, so I just shouldn't get close to other Minamoto then."</p>
<p>Minamoto Risa didn't say anything to my subtly thrown words. Her face is already flushed from dance practice, so I don't know if she's embarrassed or not. I continued the entertainment industry story from earlier to change the suddenly quiet atmosphere.</p>
<p>"Anyway, I have no intention of becoming a celebrity, but it's a problem that people keep bothering me about it."</p>
<p>"You really have no thoughts about it?"</p>
<p>"Yeah, not really."</p>
<p>Just as Minamoto Risa was about to say something, the break time ended and it was time for dance practice again, so Minamoto Risa went back to the students.</p>
<p>I watched the students' dance practice for a while, and finally, we filmed Serif's new song dance cover and finished the schedule. The live performance that the Serif members suggested was decided to be done next time due to security issues, and we headed to the hotel.</p>
<p>Before entering the hotel, I called the students and opened my mouth. I should tell them in advance that I can't hang out with them today so they won't be as disappointed.</p>
<p>"I have plans today, so let's hang out tomorrow."</p>
<p>"Okay. Ryuta-jji is a working adult, so he must have plans."</p>
<p>For some reason, the special class students reacted mildly to my words that I had plans. Are they understanding because it's been a while since I've been to Korea? No, definitely.... They have a scheming look on their faces. I should ask Sakagami Rena or Konoe Akina about this.</p>
<p>The special class students and I hid our inner thoughts and smiled, then returned to our hotel rooms for now.</p>
<p>###</p>
<p>"Okay, is everyone ready?"</p>
<p>Some of the special class students from St. Francisco Girls' Academy were wearing hats and masks to cover their faces and were talking to each other.</p>
<p>The students who gathered were Seizawa Shii, Minamoto Risa, Kujo Kina, Oofuchi Erika, and Shido Tsubaki.</p>
<p>"The others didn't notice, did they?"</p>
<p>"Yeah, the rest seem to have decided to just rest today."</p>
<p>Shido Tsubaki answered Seizawa Shii's question. Shido Tsubaki, who has a good eye, had already subtly noticed that the remaining students besides Uenozono Karen, who is not in a position to easily go out with the students here, were under the control of their homeroom teacher, Yamashiro Ryuta, so she only called these students.</p>
<p>"Just going to a meeting, but a chase.... Isn't the teacher going to get angry?"</p>
<p>Kujo Kina opened her mouth with a slightly worried expression, but Seizawa Shii wore a devilish smile.</p>
<p>"But it seems fun, right? It's not like we're interfering with anything big...."</p>
<p>"Yeah, the dadd... teacher who doesn't take care of us as a homeroom teacher is bad!"</p>
<p>Oofuchi Erika answered in a way that Yamashiro Ryuta was bad, rather than Seizawa Shii's playful answer.</p>
<p>Oofuchi Erika, whose father died early, was always craving paternal love, so she seemed to be feeling resentful that Yamashiro Ryuta hadn't been paying much attention to the special class lately. She was unconsciously feeling resentful about him not paying attention to them because he was preparing for his certification and not playing with them at night on the graduation trip, so she was trying to relieve that resentment with a small act of rebellion called a chase.</p>
<p>-Jijik, the target has left the hotel room.</p>
<p>Minamoto Risa, who was waiting near the hotel room, stood up and opened her mouth at the contact from the security guard.</p>
<p>"Let's go."</p>
<p>Minamoto Risa was initially negative about the chase, but after hearing one story from Shido Tsubaki, she joined the chase.</p>
<p>"If we ride the elevator together, we'll get caught, so we'll take the employee elevator."</p>
<p>Following Shido Tsubaki's guidance, the special class chase team (?) students carefully got into the car prepared in the basement.</p>
<p>"Um, can you really drive?"</p>
<p>"Don't worry. I've driven several times when I was working part-time."</p>
<p>Oofuchi Erika asked with a slightly scared expression as Seizawa Shii grabbed the steering wheel. Seizawa Shii, who was already an adult and had a driver's license, could of course drive.</p>
<p>-The target has taken a taxi. The number is....</p>
<p>As another security guard's contact came in from outside the hotel, Seizawa Shii started the car. Before long, the taxi that Yamashiro Ryuta was in came into view.</p>
<p>"Hurry up and say that line."</p>
<p>"Taxi driver! Please follow that taxi!"</p>
<p>Kujo Kina chimed in at Seizawa Shii's words and pointed to the taxi that Yamashiro Ryuta was in, which was visible in front. Everyone in the car burst out laughing at their skit.</p>
<p>The taxi that Yamashiro Ryuta was in didn't go far. He was seen getting out of the taxi in a slightly quiet commercial building.</p>
<p>"He's going into the commercial building."</p>
<p>"Let's park first.... The commercial building's parking lot is over there."</p>
<p>Seizawa Shii parked the car in the commercial building's underground parking lot. Everyone got out of the car and said a word each.</p>
<p>"Shii-jjang drives well."</p>
<p>"Where should we go?"</p>
<p>"We have to search from the first floor. Ryuta-jji is noticeable, so we'll be able to find him quickly."</p>
<p>"It's written here what's in this commercial building. There's a chicken restaurant on the 3rd floor. Should we pack up some Korean chicken to take back later?"</p>
<p>"The teacher is a glutton, so he'll definitely be in a restaurant."</p>
<p>There was an elevator that went directly from the underground parking lot to the commercial building, so the special class troublemakers waited for the elevator.</p>
<p>Ding!</p>
<p>The elevator door opened with an alarm sound. Yamashiro Ryuta was waiting there with a smile.</p>
<p>###</p>
<p>"Aren't you getting in?"</p>
<p>Everyone was flustered and stood still, so I pressed the open button so the door wouldn't close, as if telling them to get in.</p>
<p>"How did you know, Ryuta-jji?"</p>
<p>Seizawa Shii asked as she got into the elevator with a slightly awkward expression.</p>
<p>"I scolded you so much in the morning, but if you suddenly become generous, I can only think that you have some kind of scheme."</p>
<p>At my answer, Seizawa Shii muttered, "Tsk, did I get caught there...?" Actually, I found out with the help of Sakagami Rena and some other students, but even so, I suspected that they would do something with the sudden change in attitude.</p>
<p>"Ahaha...! You're not angry, are you? Teacher? It's just a joke."</p>
<p>"I'm not that angry. There are also things I haven't been able to pay attention to lately, and since you're the special class kids who are a bunch of troublemakers, I expected a chase."</p>
<p>I answered Kujo Kina that I was okay. Sakagami Rena, who is close to Shido Tsubaki and Oofuchi Erika, told me that I thought I hadn't been paying much attention lately, so as a homeroom teacher, I couldn't get angry at such a small prank.</p>
<p>Of course, if I hadn't noticed this small prank, a big deal could have happened where they broadcasted my meeting with Ha Ju-yoon.... But I noticed it in advance, so it's okay.</p>
<p>"I have about an hour to spare until my appointment, so let's eat chicken and talk at the chicken restaurant here. But it's a secret from the other kids?"</p>
<p>"I like chicken, teacher!"</p>
<p>"Ryuta-jji is buying the chicken, right?"</p>
<p>Everyone was excited when I pressed the 3rd floor where the chicken restaurant was. There's no one who doesn't like chicken. If I can lower the dissatisfaction rate of the special class problem children with just the price of chicken, it's a cheap deal, so I don't regret the money at all.</p>
