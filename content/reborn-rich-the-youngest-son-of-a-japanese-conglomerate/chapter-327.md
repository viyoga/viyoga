<p>The accident happened a year ago, before the end of summer vacation. While on a family vacation, they were suddenly hit head-on by a dump truck that crossed the lane, and she suffered severe injuries to her limbs from sharp fragments, which eventually had to be amputated.</p>
<p>In fact, it was a miracle that she survived. The bodies of her father, mother, and younger brother, who were in the car with her, were said to be unrecognizable.</p>
<p>Of course, Saionji Diana was undergoing life-or-death surgery, so she didn't see her parents' faces and didn't attend the funeral. Not being able to be there for her parents and younger brother's last moments gave Saionji Diana an indelible emotional wound.</p>
<p>More than half a year of hospitalization and a similar amount of rehabilitation flew by. Saionji Diana had gone from a lively high school girl to a depressed repeater.</p>
<p>Her kind grandfather no longer looked for her, and Saionji Diana's uncle, Saionji Fusato, whom she suspected of being the mastermind behind the accident, was working to become the successor to the Saionji family, which was currently vacant.</p>
<p>When her rehabilitation was over, Saionji Diana went from being the heiress of the Saionji family, who had everything, to being treated as a surviving burden. All that Saionji Diana was given was a minimum living allowance and tuition for St. Francis Girls' Academy.</p>
<p>Of course, she couldn't even hope for expensive cyber implant limbs. She could only replace her right arm, which she mainly used, with a low-end cyber implant, and had to be satisfied with using prosthetic arms and legs that only had a shape.</p>
<p>She couldn't even practice swordsmanship, which she enjoyed, so she was just going to school day after day without any motivation. It was then that Yamashiro Ryuta, the new homeroom teacher who came in from the second semester after the previous homeroom teacher was fired for a scandalous incident, called her.</p>
<p>With the ominous thought that her grandfather might not have paid her tuition for this month, she headed to the education preparation room where her homeroom teacher was.</p>
<p>The story she heard in the education preparation room was about a sponsorship she had never imagined. Moreover, the conditions that Yamashiro Ryuta set were quite generous. To give her cyber implants for her limbs for just a few years of work... This was the first time someone had come forward so earnestly to help her since the accident. Almost no one was helping her because they were watching her uncle's every move, who would become the successor to the Saionji family.</p>
<p>However, Saionji Diana, whose confidence had plummeted due to the pain of the past year, couldn't readily accept her homeroom teacher's offer. When Saionji Diana was hesitating, the words that Yamashiro Ryuta uttered instantly dispelled Saionji Diana's hesitation.</p>
<p>"I'll help you avenge your parents."</p>
<p>"...Okay."</p>
<p>Yes, if she could get revenge, she could even shake hands with the devil. If it's just her homeroom teacher, who isn't even a devil, it would be even easier to grab him.</p>
<p>Saionji Diana was suffering from survivor's guilt. She was thinking about why she alone survived and whether she should continue to live like this. Yamashiro Ryuta had thrown her a lifeline when she was thinking about suicide.</p>
<p>Saionji Diana, who had decided to receive Yamashiro Ryuta's sponsorship, returned to her quiet home. There was no housekeeper, which would be common in other homes.</p>
<p>She was going to school alone. She ate lunch at the school cafeteria, but she was eating convenience store meals for dinner.</p>

If Yamashiro Ryuta's sponsorship wasn't a lie, these unmoving arms and legs would be replaced with mechanical arms and legs that could move. If she could just walk like before, mechanical arms and legs would be fine.</p>
