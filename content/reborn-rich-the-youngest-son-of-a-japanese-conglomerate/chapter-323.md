<p>Shimokawabe Airen's mornings are early. Every day, she wakes up at 5 AM, breathing the clear air of dawn, practices sword dance and swordsmanship Training, and finishes her morning discipline. Then, she cooks a dish in the St. Francisco Girls' Academy cooking practice room and has breakfast. After the meal, she tidies up, and after washing, styling her hair, and putting on Clothes, her Going to Work was complete.</p>
<p>The fact that putting on Clothes meant Going to Work was complete was because Cheongsim-gwan and the cooking practice room right next to it were her workplaces, so she didn't need to commute separately. During hours without class, she meditates, and if there's class, she teaches Students. This was her usual routine. Since there was no class until lunch today, Shimokawabe Airen was planning to spend the day practicing mental landscape. But things never go according to plan.</p>
<p>Just as she sat in a meditative posture in the center of Cheongsim-gwan and closed her Eyes, she felt a presence approaching this place. She was quite displeased that someone was disturbing her Meditation so early in the morning, but soon, sensing that presence, she smiled faintly. A presence similar to Fujigasaki Rinka, her close friend and fellow Martial artist. The presence of Taira Ryuta, her friend's Disciple whom she had met a few days ago, was approaching Cheongsim-gwan.</p>
<p>"Excuse Me." When I knocked on the door of the Japanese-style two-story Dojang, which had a signboard that read 'Cheongsim-gwan (淸心館)'—old but dignified—a voice from inside said, 'Come in, Ryuta-gun.' Just as I thought, she's incredible. She recognized me just from my presence. I opened the Door of Cheongsim-gwan and entered. In the middle of the quite spacious Dojang, Shimokawabe Airen was sitting in a meditative posture. A sharp Prayer, refined to its utmost, poured towards me.</p>
<p>I subtly stirred the Abyss, suppressing Shimokawabe Airen's Prayer as I approached. Shimokawabe Airen looked at me like that and smiled with Satisfaction.</p>
<p>"How did you know it was me?"</p>
<p>"In St. Francisco Girls' Academy, there's no one but Ryuta-gun who gives off a presence similar to Rinka." Ah, I see. She had approached me at the Gym back then too, saying I had a Fujigasaki Rinka vibe. It seems Masters can sense the Aura of the Abyss. I might need to be a bit careful about this, then? Because even if I were to ambush someone, if they sensed my Aura beforehand, it would all be for naught.</p>
<p>"I've come this time because of Alexandra, whom Shimokawabe-ssi will be teaching."</p>
<p>"Alexandra? Ah, you mean Egawa-yang. Egawa Shiho-ssi, with whom I've had an Acquaintance for a while, made a Request, so I decided to take her on starting today, but...."</p>
<p>"I also have a Relationship with that Egawa-yang, so I came to ask for your kind cooperation." Shimokawabe Airen looked at me with languid, half-closed Eyes. It must be an Expression asking for a little more explanation. I told Shimokawabe Airen everything without hiding anything, from rescuing Alik from the Slave Trader, to what Alik wanted, and even Alik's Talent.</p>
<p>"Hmm.... In a word, Ryuta-gun is Egawa-yang's guardian, right?"</p>
<p>"Yes, it's a coincidence that I came to the same St. Francisco Girls' Academy like this, but since I've become aware, it's Etiquette to greet Alik's Sabu-nim, so I came."</p>
<p>At my polite Words, Shimokawabe Airen nodded her Head with a slightly satisfied Expression.</p>
<p>"I won't be a complete teacher either. I'm just planning to teach the basics, like for the other club members."</p>
<p>"That's enough. After all, everyone has their own Martial Way (budō)." If Alik's talent impresses her, she will likely teach Kashima Kiichi Shintoryu, the sword style of her own school. If not, she’ll only go over the basics, but since Alik has also learned swordsmanship, there will still be significant progress.</p>
<p>"Ah, a customer has come, and I haven't even served tea. Would you mind following me for a moment?" Shimokawabe Airen clapped her Hands as if she had forgotten, then rose from her Seat. Telling me to follow, she went out of Cheongsim-gwan and headed towards the cooking practice room. I also followed Shimokawabe Airen and entered the cooking practice room. The quite spacious cooking practice room was equipped with Facilities for fifty people, two and a half classes, to cook simultaneously, and ingredients were abundant.</p>
<p>Shimokawabe Airen skillfully brewed tea and took out wagashi, and went to the restaurant next to the cooking room to set them out. The restaurant had a luxurious feel, like a fine dining restaurant.</p>
<p>"In St. Francisco Girls' Academy's home economics classes, they learn both cooking and meal Etiquette. That's why there's a place with such a splendid restaurant feel."</p>
<p>Ah, I see. Even if they are Prestigious Families, there are also newly established chaebol families, so it seemed St. Francisco Girls' Academy was also teaching meal Etiquette.</p>
<p>"Since we're drinking tea together anyway, may I ask something I'm curious about?"</p>
<p>"I am a Teacher, but I don't know much."</p>
<p>"I'm going to ask about clubs rather than teaching." If I couldn't avoid becoming a club advisor, I thought I should ask Shimokawabe Airen to gather as much Information as possible and try to make it as convenient for me as possible.</p>
<p>"Clubs?"</p>
<p>"Somehow, the Atmosphere feels like they'll make me an advisor. As a new Teacher and a second foreign language Teacher with not much Work, they say being an advisor is mandatory."</p>
<p>"Ah, that's right. I came to this academy to be both the director of Cheongsim-gwan, which ordinary Teachers cannot oversee, and a home economics Teacher, but Ryuta-gun's Situation is different from mine."</p>
<p>Listening to Shimokawabe Airen's Story, it seemed that long-standing clubs wanted a somewhat qualified Teacher as an advisor. Since an ordinary Teacher couldn't advise Cheongsim-gwan, an ancient martial arts club, it seemed they had invited Shimokawabe Airen.</p>
<p>"Ryuta-gun will probably become the advisor for a club that Students can run among themselves, with almost no role for the advising Teacher." I see.... For example, since I couldn't become an advisor for clubs like ink painting or performance clubs, which require a certain level of culture, it meant I would become a general club advisor.</p>
<p>Other Information I heard from Shimokawabe Airen was that a club needs 5 members and an advising Teacher to be maintained, and if either one is missing, they receive a Warning, and if the deficiency isn't supplemented within fifteen days of the Warning, the club is disbanded. She then unnecessarily scared me by saying that several clubs on the verge of disbandment would be targeting me. I was idly thinking, 'I'm only known a little to the third-year high school Students, surely they wouldn't target me?' "I'll be going now."</p>
<p>"Do drop by often, Ryuta-gun. I've been lonely without any close Teachers." It made sense that she didn't have any close Teachers. If I were another Teacher, I'd probably ostracize someone living for free on school grounds without paying. Just looking at Kasaoka Yuzuki's case, she bought a house near the school, couldn't handle the Debt, and is now working at a delivery health service.</p>
<p>The Headmaster and I know that Shimokawabe Airen is quite helpful for school Security, so we don't think that way at all, but other Teachers, not knowing Shimokawabe Airen's power, objectively or subjectively, must be ignoring and ostracizing her.</p>
<p>"Yes, I'll drop by often. Shimokawabe-ssi, if you come to my side, I'll treat you." There wasn't really anything to treat someone with in the education preparation room where I was. Since I have to treat guests when they come, I should at least get a refrigerator for the education preparation room. I should fill it with lots of ice cream and pudding. I said my greetings to Shimokawabe Airen and returned to the education preparation room where I would be.</p>
<p>Although there was no class today, perhaps because I had been there for two weeks, I felt comfortable, as if the education preparation room was my area. When I returned, it was still class time, but soon the Bell rang, and break time came. Not long after break time began, I heard a knocking sound on the Door of the education preparation room. Knock knock.</p>
<p>"Come in." As soon as I told them to come in, five Students rushed in. One or two of them were faces I had seen among the third-year Students, and a few were unfamiliar, so they seemed to be from other grades.</p>
<p>"""Teacher, please become our club advisor!""" Hmm, five people are looking for a club advisor? All five were Club Presidents, and each of their club names made it clear why they were unpopular clubs.</p>
<p>From the Orange Peel Craft Club, Slime Making Club, and Enka Club - clubs that anyone could tell would be unpopular - to the Club Presidents of the 3rd Flower Arranging Club and the 4th Anime Appreciation Club, which were once large clubs but had split due to various problems, reducing their numbers and putting them on the verge of disbandment, came and requested me to be their advisor.</p>
<p>"Can't the 3rd Flower Arranging Club and the 4th Anime Appreciation Club just merge with their original large clubs?"</p>
<p>"Hmph! Who would do orange peel crafts, outdated slime making, or enka?!" Since I wasn't saying anything, the five of them started fighting among themselves to get an advising Teacher. Both sides sounded right to me, so I didn't particularly want to become an advisor for either. As they were asking me to become their club advisor, a Voice of salvation was heard.</p>
<p>"Ryuta-cchi has already agreed to be our club advisor!" Looking towards where the sound came from, Seizawa Shii and Minamoto Risa, the owners of the Voice, were standing at the Door. The five Club Presidents of the unpopular clubs in the education preparation room made Expressions of Failure. There was no one who could contend with the two delinquent female Students, famous for being troublemakers. Moreover, their Families were incredibly powerful, so they wouldn't even dare to defy them.</p>
<p>"I didn't know you already had an Appointment. I'm sorry for bothering you, Teacher."</p>
<p>"I'll be going too...!" The five Club Presidents left the education preparation room as if the tide was receding. It was awkward, but Fortunate.</p>
<p>"You helped me because I was in a difficult Situation, right? Thank you." When I greeted Seizawa Shii, Seizawa Shii said with an Expression that asked, 'What are you talking about?' "What are you talking about, Ryuta-cchi? We're really going to make a club, you know?"</p>
