I was expecting her to be wearing something unusual today, and she was wearing a Kimono. It was a Kimono with a white background and red flower patterns, which suited Eve's hair color well.</p>
<p>"Ryuta-nim~☆ You're here early?"</p>
<p>"The traffic wasn't bad since it's a weekday afternoon."</p>
<p>We exchanged brief greetings, and I sat down in the seat Eve had prepared for me. Eve was looking at me with an expression that seemed to be begging for a reaction to her outfit, so I smiled slightly and opened my mouth.</p>
<p>"But why a Kimono today?"</p>
<p>"Since you've been in Korea and China, I thought you might miss Japanese style."</p>
<p>Ah, was that it? Actually, when I was in Korea, it hadn't been long since I left Japan, so I didn't think about it, and when I was in South China, there were so many urgent situations that I didn't miss Japan either.</p>
<p>Still, it felt good to see a woman with a great body wearing a Kimono after a long time. Each country's traditional clothing has its own moe points, and personally, I like Kimonos because they have a kind of delicate feeling, or something like that....</p>
<p>"It suits you, Eve. It matches your hair color."</p>
<p>"Thank you for the compliment, Ryuta-nim~☆"</p>
<p>Eve was quite pleased with my compliment. I don't know if it's because of the program or if she's really happy. But I decided to believe that Eve is really happy.</p>
<p>"First of all.... Here you go."</p>
<p>I first took out the data I had hacked from the SCSMC's secret research lab from my pocket and handed it to Eve. Eve received the UCT I handed over with an expression as if she were receiving an amazing gift, and then plugged it into a nearby terminal.</p>
<p>Then, she closed her eyes for a while and checked the data as if savoring a fragrant liquor. Eve soon opened her eyes and smiled at me.</p>
<p>"Good. The data includes methods for synchronizing the movements of pilots and robots, some missile technology, some drone technology, and several autonomous Ai algorithms."</p>
<p>Of course, as Eve said, there are various data included, but we couldn't use it as is. If we used these technologies as is, it would be like advertising that we were the ones who raided SCSMC, so we had to apply these technologies to create advanced or new technologies.</p>
<p>"Ryuta-nim, can we buy Ai algorithms from Am Sang?"</p>
<p>Ah, Eve has been collecting data on Ai for a while. I also stole and sold BS Group's technology in Pyongyang before.</p>
<p>"I understand."</p>
<p>"Thank you, I'll give you a good price~!"</p>
<p>As soon as Eve finished speaking, a notification sound rang from my smartphone. When I checked my smartphone, I saw that 60 million Am Sang points had been deposited.</p>
<p>60 million Am Sang points is.... about 8 billion yen. Because I had invested all my Am Sang points in various investments due to the recent situation in New Nanking City, my Am Sang points were running dry, so I was glad that I could replenish them with this.</p>
<p>"Now that you have points again, you can receive Eve's special service, Eve's recommendation service☆"</p>
<p>Normally, I would have to scroll through the countless items registered in Am Sang from all over the world one by one to see if there was anything good, but Eve knows my buying preferences and sends me a list of items worth buying every day, which is called Eve's recommendation service.</p>
<p>I didn't buy everything she recommended, but she recommended things that seemed useful, so I often bought them.</p>
<p>"Leaving aside the Ai, are there any other useful applications for the other data?"</p>

<p>"I'll have to talk to Lara first, but there are many ways to apply it. In particular, the method of synchronizing the movements of pilots and robots seems to have endless applications. We could apply the synchronization technology to the power suits we're thinking about for our next project, or to cyber implants. Of course, I think we can fully apply the remaining drone and missile technology and digest it into our own technology."</p>
<p>As expected, listening to Eve's explanation, it seemed that the data stolen from the secret research lab of SCSMC, a mega-corp in South China, was quite valuable.</p>
<p>"How's TG Tech doing?"</p>
<p>"As everyone expected, there's a problem with Han Yu-gyeong-ssi."</p>
<p>At Eve's words, I nodded heavily. I had expected that if TG Tech stood out in the recent New Nanking City situation, the Shin Kang Group, Han Yu-gyeong's family, would covet TG Tech, and it meant that it was really happening as expected.</p>
<p>"But Yu-gyeong will do well because there are things we've thought of to respond to that."</p>

<p>As I am in Japan now, all I can do for Han Yu-gyeong is to cheer her on, but I thought she would do well.</p>

<p>Shin Kang House </p>
<p>Originally, it was a small Korean-style house where Han Mun-shin and Han Mun-gang, the founders of the Shin Kang Group, lived and worked together. The pronunciation of Shin Kang House was the same, but it had a different name in Chinese characters.</p>
<p>However, when his younger brother Han Mun-gang took over the company from Han Mun-shin and gained full control, he tore down the original small residence and built a large Western-style house in its place, even changing the character from Shin, representing his older brother Han Mun-shin, to Shin, erasing all connections to his older brother Han Mun-shin.</p>
<p>"As expected of Yu-gyeong! Top of her class at Korea University! I thought she only had a head for studying, but she's got a head for business too!"</p>
<p>The quiet meal time was broken by the words of Han Byeong-jae, the eldest son of Han Mun-gang. Han Byeong-jae was the president of Shin Kang Steel, which supplies steel materials to Shin Kang Defense, the parent company of the Shin Kang Group, as well as several small affiliates, and there were few who did not fear him because of his ruthless and sharp demeanor, which was different from his light words and actions.</p>
<p>"Thank you, Great Uncle."</p>
<p>Because it was a congratulatory greeting with obvious ulterior motives, Han Yu-gyeong gave a short answer that was bland and made it impossible to continue talking about it.</p>
<p>"Congratulations, Yu-gyeong-a. Where did you get that technology?"</p>
<p>This time it was Han Mun-gang's third son, Han Gyeong-jae. Seeing the brothers saying this side by side, it seemed that they had already finished talking to each other. Han Yu-gyeong sighed as if she couldn't help it and opened her mouth.</p>
<p>"I bought an American company that has related patents. I made the gun based on that."</p>
<p>Han Gyeong-jae nodded with a satisfied expression even at Han Yu-gyeong's short explanation. He seemed to think it was even better if she had a patent. They already had an attitude as if they had taken over TG Tech.</p>
<p>"Yu-gyeong-a, leave TG Tech to your father and uncles. It must be quite difficult to go to college and run a company at the same time."</p>
<p>Han Mun-gang's second son, Han Woo-min, who is in charge of Shin Kang Security, said somewhat forcefully. Usually, to make such a proposal, you would have to give something in return, but he was just telling her to leave it to them.... Han Yu-gyeong was dumbfounded.</p>
<p>"Why should I, Second Great Uncle?"</p>
<p>"What, what? Why? You dare!"</p>
<p>At Han Yu-gyeong's bold words, Han Woo-min stood up from his seat and shouted. Han Mun-gang, who was eating at the head table, made a sound as if he was uncomfortable, and Han Woo-min said he was sorry and sat down.</p>
<p>"Yu-gyeong-a, think about it carefully. Wouldn't it be better for a professional manager to manage the company than you, who haven't received proper management training?"</p>
<p>"Among those professional managers that Uncle is talking about, please name at least one person who has achieved better results than me recently."</p>
<p>Han Yu-gyeong replied to Han Gyeong-jae's words with a sharp attitude. In fact, as Han Yu-gyeong said, there were not many young managers who had achieved as much as Han Yu-gyeong recently.</p>
<p>She had created TG Tech, a company that was attracting attention from the world, with her bare hands without receiving any support from the group, so it could be said that she had achieved very outstanding results. So Han Gyeong-jae couldn't name anyone in response to Han Yu-gyeong's words.</p>
<p>"You! What kind of attitude is that towards your uncle?"</p>
<p>Han Mun-gang's fourth daughter, Han Seong-hee, who had been eating quietly, spoke sharply to Han Yu-gyeong. Han Seong-hee was responsible for the cosmetics business under the Shin Kang Group called Yusu, and she was content with the cosmetics business because of Han Mun-gang's belief that important work should not be entrusted to women. However, she felt jealous when Han Yu-gyeong's TG Tech gained a lot of attention.</p>
<p>"Then, Aunt, would you be able to stand still if you were told to hand over the cosmetics business to someone else without any compensation?"</p>
<p>"...."</p>
<p>Han Seong-hee couldn't say anything in response to Han Yu-gyeong's words. Han Yu-gyeong looked around the room and continued to speak.</p>
<p>"And although I am one of the co-CEOs, TG Tech is not solely owned by me. My stake is not more than 50 percent. I won't give my stake to people who didn't help at all while making TG Tech, but even if I did, if the other stakeholders went around saying that someone other than me was the CEO, they would be kicked out soon, right?"</p>
<p>In short, it meant that a puppet set up by her uncle, not herself, could not become the CEO of TG Tech.</p>
<p>Rather than having the relationship with TG Tech completely cut off by trying to set up a scarecrow, it was implied that it would be better for the Shin Kang Group to indirectly gain influence by having Han Yu-gyeong continue to serve as co-CEO.</p>
<p>"Lastly, we are about to sign a contract with Jeil Electronics, but if the CEO is suddenly replaced, will Cheon Ha-rin, the managing director of Jeil Electronics, continue to trade with TG Tech?"</p>
<p>Everyone was silent at Han Yu-gyeong's words. The name Jeil Group seemed to weigh down on everyone here, even though she was just a managing director of one affiliate. Recently, the Shin Kang Group has been steadily increasing its ranking in the business world, but Korea's number one was Jeil Group.</p>
<p>Even if she was just a managing director of an affiliate, she was the managing director of Jeil Electronics, the most important affiliate of the Jeil Group, and she was also the granddaughter of the chairman of the Jeil Group, so Cheon Ha-rin was by no means someone to be ignored.</p>
<p>"This time, this uncle made a mistake. I'm sorry, Yu-gyeong-a."</p>
<p>In the end, this meal at Shin Kang House was concluded with Han Gyeong-jae apologizing to Han Yu-gyeong on behalf of everyone. Of course, judging from the explosive expression of the hot-tempered second son, Han Woo-min, it was impossible to think that the attempt to seize TG Tech would end here.</p>
