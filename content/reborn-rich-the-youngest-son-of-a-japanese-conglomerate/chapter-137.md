<p>A few hours earlier.</p>
<p>I enjoyed a passionate rendezvous with Shinoe Misano in an empty office, as usual, and then put in for a vacation. It was a whopping 43-day vacation, so Shinoe Misano was taken aback when she saw the form I submitted, but she soon gave me a helpless look and accepted the form.</p>
<p>,Isn't 43 days too long of a break?"</p>
<p>,I have a lot of vacation days I received as rewards. Plus, my grandfather gave me 10 extra days this time...."</p>
<p>The legally mandated annual leave for new employees in Japan is 15 days, so I was getting almost three times that amount.</p>
<p>,What are you going to do during your vacation?"</p>
<p>,First, I'm going to go hang out with my friends somewhere. Then, I'll figure it out when I get there."</p>
<p>Once the PMC acquisition was finalized, I was planning to take Kazuhiro and Kiyohira to some island. Since Kazuhiro had decided to transfer to the United States, this year was likely to be the last time the three of us could go on a trip together.</p>
<p>Well, if they both retired, we'd have plenty of time to travel, but that would be at least fifteen years from now, so I decided to go this year. Thanks to the advancement of science and technology, the lifespan of athletes has also increased, so recently they've been retiring in their early forties.</p>
<p>,Do you have to come see me in the middle of your vacation?"</p>
<p>,I practically live here anyway. I'll stop by often."</p>
<p>I got permission from Shinoe Misano. Now, I should rest a bit until the investigation mission I entrusted to Mimata Chieri is over. I went to work briefly to have lunch and get my vacation approved, but I was quite tired because I did quite a lot of strenuous activity yesterday. I met Yukiko, Mimata Chieri, Suzuki Chisami, Shinoe Misano, and even Kawaji Emiko, who came along to exercise, so I was very tired.</p>
<p>In fact, if I told Yukiko about taking a vacation without going through Shinoe Misano, the vacation would be approved immediately, but I still have to protect the pride of Shinoe Misano, the team leader. After all, we've shared bodies.</p>
<p>I said goodbye to Shinoe Misano and headed home. Mimata Chieri would have gone to Fussa-shi from the morning to gather information, so there was a high possibility that she was already compiling the information. Hmm, then I won't have time to rest?</p>
<p>I was thinking about Mimata Chieri when I suddenly got a call from Mimata Chieri. Speak of the devil, and he shall appear; Mimata Chieri must be a cat.</p>
<p>,Hello?"</p>
<p>-Sunbae, if we want to acquire Seiryu Military Company, we need to do it as soon as possible. Black Tech, the largest PMC in Fussa-shi, is trying to acquire Seiryu Military Company.</p>
<p>Black Tech? I've heard of them somewhere.... Ah, that's right! They were a famous unit in the game because all of their members were cybernetically enhanced soldiers. I thought they were cost-effective, but their evaluation dropped because they often suffered from the 'Berserk' status ailment, which made them unable to distinguish friend from foe. Nevertheless, I remember they were quite large, ranking around twelfth in Japan... so they're targeting Seiryu Military Company here....</p>
<p>,When you say as soon as possible, do you mean today?"</p>
<p>-Yes, as soon as I indicated that we were going to acquire them, they tried to stop us with force. I think we need to finish the acquisition quickly.</p>
<p>,I'm going there now. Get a head start on assessing the situation."</p>
<p>-Yes, please be careful coming, Sunbae.</p>
<p>Fussa-shi, where Seiryu Military Company is located, is on the opposite side of Tokyo from Yokosuka City, where the Human Resources Development Center is located, so it will take at least two hours to get there. I entered it into the navigation system, and it said it would take about an hour and 45 minutes. It's not rush hour, so it must be a little shorter.</p>
<p>,I hope nothing happens while I'm on my way?"</p>
<p>I muttered to myself and started the engine.</p>
<p>Today, the accuracy rate of my unconscious thoughts and words was high. I thought about Mimata Chieri and got a call from Mimata Chieri, and I muttered, "I hope nothing happens," and about thirty PMC operatives were surrounding the shabby office of Seiryu Military Company.</p>
<p>,How long has it been like this?"</p>
<p>I got out of the car and asked Mimata Chieri.</p>
<p>,About five minutes? I think."</p>
<p>,Then Black Tech might be showing off their skills and forcing them to sign a contract."</p>
<p>,I guess so?"</p>
<p>For some reason, Black Tech seemed to have a bad relationship with Seiryu Military Company, to the point where they were trying to force the acquisition while showing off their skills. If they had a good relationship, they wouldn't refuse the acquisition of the largest PMC in the area.</p>
<p>,Let's go in too. We have to stop the acquisition from happening."</p>
<p>,What if those thirty people stop us?"</p>
<p>,Shall we bet on who takes down the most?"</p>
<p>,What kind of bet?"</p>
<p>I said let's bet, but I never thought about what to bet on.... Mimata Chieri is getting into sex, so should I bet something lewd?</p>
<p>,The loser has to cosplay as the winner's preference and do whatever they want, in whatever position they want."</p>
<p>,Okay!"</p>
<p>At the same time as she answered, Mimata Chieri took out a pistol from her bosom and shot the PMC operative closest to us. He fell down after getting hit with something between the eyebrows; upon closer inspection, it was an injectable tranquilizer gun.</p>
<p>,What?!"</p>
<p>,Is that a gun?"</p>
<p>While I was approaching the thirty people, Mimata Chieri shot down six of them in an instant. What the hell did she want me to do??</p>
<p>Feeling like I couldn't lose, I started taking down the guys one by one, starting with the bald guy who was trying to draw his gun first. Since the guys who were trying to draw their guns were the first targets, the Black Tech operatives clenched their fists and rushed at me.</p>
<p>I knocked away a fist flying at my chin, hit the bearded guy next to me to get a team kill, and broke his forearm with a palm strike. I could feel the sensation of bone breaking in my palm.</p>
<p>While I was taking down two people, I kicked the chin of the guy approaching from behind to knock him down, and as I took the punches pouring in from both sides, I knocked down seven more. The rest were already knocked down by Mimata Chieri's tranquilizer gun.</p>
<p>,I won, right? Right?"</p>
<p>,Yes, yes...."</p>
<p>Her eyes are gone...? I nodded, fearing what Mimata Chieri would ask me to do.</p>
<p>,Let's take care of the Seiryu Military Company first, Sunbae."</p>
<p>,Ah...! Yes."</p>
<p>I took the lead and entered the office with a sign that said 青竜軍事会社 (Seiryu Gunji Gaisha: Blue Dragon Military Company) in fancy Chinese characters. I put on the most friendly expression I could and spoke.</p>
<p>,I came to visit because I want to acquire Seiryu Military Company."</p>
<p>When I entered the office, a man with slicked-back hair and a mustache, who was standing awkwardly, glared at me. This guy must be Gomisawa Renya from Black Tech.</p>
<p>,It's too late, Seiryu Military Company has decided to join us."</p>
<p>,Is that so?"</p>
<p>When I looked around at everyone except Gomisawa Renya and asked, they all shook their heads.</p>
<p>,I don't know who you are, but why are you telling such an easily detectable lie?"</p>
<p>,Who are you?"</p>
<p>,You don't need to talk to that man. I'm the president of Seiryu Military Company, so you can talk to me."</p>
<p>Interrupting Gomisawa Renya, a woman with a slightly bluish ponytail came in front of me. Ah, she was the president. Her name was probably Sawayanagi Azuma. In MegaCorp's youngest son, she was a useful combat operative with an abnormally high evasion rate due to her precognition passive and an almost 80 percent trap detection ability, but I didn't know she was the president.</p>
<p>,I'm Sawayanagi Azuma, the president of Seiryu Military Company."</p>
<p>,I'm Hata Ryuta, the president of Hatake Investment Company."</p>
<p>I exchanged business cards with Sawayanagi Azuma. I gave her the business card I made yesterday with the name Hata Ryuta, president of Hatake Investment Company, printed on it.</p>
<p>I didn't want to be discovered by other successors if possible, so I made a business card with the name Hata Ryuta, which is the Japanese pronunciation of my original surname, Jin, which I had before receiving the Taira surname.</p>
<p>Hatake Investment Company was also created in a hurry, and it only had the funds to acquire Seiryu Military Company and operate it for a few years as capital. The name was also very casually chosen, since it would be Jin's Investment Company in Korean. It was created quickly because I had the Taira name; otherwise, it would have taken a long time to get approved.</p>
<p>,Ryuta...?"</p>
<p>,Yes?"</p>
<p>Suddenly calling my name? Sawayanagi Azuma had a very surprised expression.</p>
<p>,Oh, I'm sorry. My late predecessor's name was also Ryuta. But the Chinese characters are different."</p>
<p>,Ah, it's okay. What an amazing coincidence."</p>
<p>It's amazing that the predecessor's name was also Ryuta. Now that we've introduced ourselves, it was time to give her the acquisition proposal.</p>
<p>,Here are our terms."</p>
<p>I received the documents from Mimata Chieri and handed them to Sawayanagi Azuma as is. Sawayanagi Azuma received the documents and read them.</p>
<p>,Acquisition price of 300 million yen and the debt is paid separately.... Ah, Gomisawa-nim, would you mind stepping out? We can't leak the contents of the contract outside."</p>
<p>,Hehehe...! Are you mocking me like this? You'll regret this day for the rest of your life."</p>
<p>The middle-aged Gomisawa Renya with slicked-back hair laughed like a madman and left the office.</p>
<p>,Thank you. You came to help us, right?"</p>
<p>,It's true that I came to help because there were a lot of people surrounding you, but it's also true that I intend to acquire you."</p>
<p>,I'm sorry...!"</p>
<p>Sawayanagi Azuma suddenly bowed her head and apologized. Was she going to reject the acquisition?</p>
<p>,If we get acquired, someone else will be the president, right?"</p>
<p>,Is that so?"</p>
<p>,Me and my colleagues can't work under anyone else.... Thank you for your help, but we're planning to dissolve Seiryu Military Company today."</p>
<p>There seemed to be some story behind it. They'd rather dissolve the company than work under someone else.</p>
<p>,Wait!"</p>
<p>Mimata Chieri interjected. Did she have a solution to this problem?</p>
<p>,The problem is that it's an unreasonable condition for someone else to become the president instead of me.... no, the current president, right?"</p>
<p>,That's right."</p>
<p>,Hmm.... Then how about this? Our president buys all the shares of Seiryu Military Company here. And our president appoints Sawayanagi Azuma-ssi, the current president of Seiryu Military Company, as a professional manager."</p>
<p>Can we do that? Is it okay if I own all the shares? Mimata Chieri winks at me when I look at her with a questioning expression.</p>
<p>,Can you give us some time to discuss it with our colleagues?"</p>
<p>,Go ahead."</p>
<p>I also needed time to ask Mimata Chieri what she was thinking, so I nodded at Sawayanagi Azuma's request.</p>
<p>We went outside the office for a while.</p>
<p>,Japanese law can't be that lax, can it? Can you own a PMC in this way through a loophole?"</p>
<p>,Of course not. I'll get the shares, and Sawayanagi Azuma-ssi will be the president. And we'll hide this fact from the Taira family and find a new PMC for me to sit as president. It's an opportunity to be the owner of two PMCs, Sunbae!"</p>
<p>In that case, Mimata Chieri would effectively be the owner of two PMCs, but it didn't matter if I trusted one or two.</p>
<p>Besides, I was drawn to Mimata Chieri's words that I could have two PMCs. It was even better because I could hide one and use it at a critical moment.</p>
<p>,Okay. Please do your best, Sunbae."</p>
<p>,Hehe, will you double my salary?"</p>
<p>,That's easy."</p>
<p>Around the same time our conversation ended, the conversation at Seiryu Military Company also seemed to have ended, and the door opened and Sawayanagi Azuma's face appeared.</p>
<p>,The conversation is over. Please come back in."</p>
<p>,Okay."</p>
<p>Our conditions weren't bad for them either, but I was a little nervous in case they rejected us.</p>
<p>,We also have a few conditions."</p>
<p>,Please tell me."</p>
<p>,We'll take care of the medical side. That means we'll take care of anything that involves getting hurt or getting a health checkup."</p>
<p>Hmm.... This condition seemed to be a measure to hide their identities as psychics. Well, I can do that much.</p>
<p>,Then I'll give you the medical expenses in cash every year."</p>
<p>,Ah, thank you! And the second condition is that it says there's a 10-year ban on retirement from the time of acquisition, but if I step down from being a professional manager, please allow the rest of the people to freely decide whether to stay or leave."</p>
<p>,Sure."</p>
<p>I didn't intend to fire Sawayanagi Azuma in the middle, so I could easily answer this condition.</p>
<p>,Finally, please settle the debt as soon as possible. They said they'll seize the office and warehouse if we don't pay it off by tomorrow."</p>
<p>,That's easy. Where should I send the money?"</p>
<p>,I'll give you the account number when the contract is over."</p>
<p>That's right. It would be foolish to pay it off before the contract is even signed.</p>
<p>,We also have a changed condition."</p>
<p>,Changed?"</p>
<p>,My secretary, not me, will own all the shares. I found out that you need to have PMC ownership qualifications to own shares. So, please just call me an investor."</p>
<p>I don't know why, but Sawayanagi Azuma's eyes were shaking at the word investor.</p>
<p>,In, Investor-nim?"</p>
<p>,You don't have to add -nim, hahaha."</p>
<p>Why is her face red? We signed the newly revised documents. The psychic unit I wanted is finally in my hands. I won't use them right away, but I'll have to level them up through various missions.</p>
