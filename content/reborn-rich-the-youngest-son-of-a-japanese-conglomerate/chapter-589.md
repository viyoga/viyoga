<p>"Kill those two bastards in the room!"</p>
<p>The guys who smashed the door and came in were thugs with huge builds wearing black suits. Judging by the 'kill' part, is there a grudge with Han Yu-gyeong? There's no way I'd be involved with Korean gangsters.</p>
<p>An ordinary woman would be terrified by this commotion and just tremble, but Han Yu-gyeong was no ordinary woman.</p>
<p>"Kuaaaaak!"</p>
<p>She instantly threw throwing daggers from who-knows-where, piercing the foreheads of the two approaching in the front. It was pretty funny to see the two big guys fall and the guys behind them trip and fall over them. I chuckled a bit, but Han Yu-gyeong didn't laugh.</p>
<p>"It's our first date in a while... and you're interrupting?!"</p>
<p>Han Yu-gyeong's anger seemed beyond imagination. Han Yu-gyeong's long black hair fluttered, and eight bluish light spheres soared around Han Yu-gyeong. Seeing her use magic I hadn't seen before, it seemed Han Yu-gyeong's star magic skill level had risen even more. One of them, seeing Han Yu-gyeong's bizarre appearance, put his hand inside his jacket and shouted.</p>
<p>"She's a psychic! Everyone, take out your guns!"</p>
<p>Judging by the sound of gunshots already outside the restaurant, it seemed a gunfight had broken out between Dickson and the attackers as well.</p>
<p>Han Yu-gyeong was already fully prepared to cast her magic, so it was too late to take out guns. Starting with the guy who put his hand inside his jacket, Han Yu-gyeong's spheres of light pierced through their torsos. Like a red-hot iron ball passing through a large pat of butter, Han Yu-gyeong's spheres easily made fist-sized holes in people as they passed through.</p>
<p>"A...a, a monster! Everyone, run away!!"</p>
<p>Seeing the thugs fall in an instant, the remaining comrades were terrified. They turned to run away, but already the shadow panther Nero, the sacred white dog White Bear, and the grizzly bear Teddy were blocking their retreat.</p>
<p>Looking at it like this, Han Yu-gyeong's summoned beasts look incredibly scary. I was sure the spirit of the grizzly, Teddy, was a small baby bear, but now it looked 1.5 times bigger than a brown bear roaming around Russia. It could stand because this was a high-end restaurant with high ceilings, but in a normal house, it would have to stand awkwardly.</p>
<p>"Keureureu!"</p>
<p>"Wang!"</p>
<p>"Kkueoeong!"</p>
<p>I heard the white bear's cheerful "Wang!" in the middle, but the two survivors sat down on the spot, paralyzed by the summoned beasts' loud howling.</p>
<p>"Bring them here. I need to find out who ordered this."</p>
<p>At Han Yu-gyeong's words, the white bear and Teddy brought the suit-wearing thugs in one by one. Nero was somewhat large, but not enough to bite and drag the attackers. So, it sauntered over, found me, jumped up, and landed on my lap. I thought it would hurt when Nero landed since it was so big, but it seemed it adjusted its weight like a shadow panther. As I was petting Nero, the white bear and Teddy, who had delivered the gangsters to Han Yu-gyeong, also approached and looked at me with sparkling eyes as if asking to be petted.</p>
<p>"Who sent you?"</p>
<p>While I was busy petting Han Yu-gyeong's summoned beasts, Han Yu-gyeong quietly took out a throwing dagger and spoke, and the thugs, already mentally broken, spewed out information without hesitation. After all, they're not an information agency, and you can't expect loyalty from organized crime members.</p>
<p>The ones who attacked Han Yu-gyeong and me (more accurately, they were trying to kill Han Yu-gyeong, but I was unlucky enough to be caught up in it) were the Yongcheol Faction, an organized crime group based in Gangnam, which had previously had conflicts with Han Yu-gyeong's Vanguard. Clearly, I had killed one of their branch managers after they attacked us on the request of Bang Sang-beom of BS Group.</p>
<p>Even in the game, if the protagonist didn't pay attention to the underworld, the Yongcheol Faction would take over Seoul and constantly clash and interfere with everything, and here they were clashing with Han Yu-gyeong as well. Is it because we killed one of their branch managers? I clearly killed him without anyone knowing back then.</p>
<p>"Are you alright, Boss?"</p>
<p>Dickson rushes in, panting, as if the battle outside is over as well. Dickson let out a sigh of relief after seeing that most of the Yongcheol Faction members were down and Han Yu-gyeong was safe.</p>
<p>"Take these two and extract more information. And call the cleanup crew to take care of this place."</p>
<p>"I will do so and report back."</p>
<p>"I can't eat here. Yong-tae and I will take a taxi home, so please take care of the cleanup, Dickson."</p>
<p>After finishing all the instructions to Dickson, Han Yu-gyeong looked at me with a very apologetic expression and apologized with her hands together.</p>
<p>"I'm sorry, Yong-tae. Let's just go home and order something."</p>
<p>"It can't be helped with the situation like this."</p>
<p>I nodded as if I understood and got up from my seat. There was no way I could eat dinner in a place full of corpses, so I had to leave. The cleanup crew that Han Yu-gyeong called would come and clean up, but the police would definitely come rushing in because of the gunshots, which would make things even more troublesome.</p>
<p>We came out of the restaurant and took a taxi. On the way to Han Yu-gyeong's house, I looked at Han Yu-gyeong's face. At first, she was caught by delinquents and trembled, but now she had grown to the point where she could quickly deal with the attackers before I even intervened... I felt a sense of pride.</p>
<p>We got out of the taxi and arrived at Han Yu-gyeong's house, and first, we ordered food with a delivery app. Instead of eel, I ordered Samgyetang full of tonic ingredients, and I could feel Han Yu-gyeong's heart trying to nourish me somehow. Of course, she might be planning to feed me a lot of stamina-boosting food and then suck up all that yang energy.</p>
<p>"But how did you get into a conflict with the Yongcheol Faction?"</p>
<p>While waiting for the food, I asked Han Yu-gyeong what I was curious about. I was sure that my identity hadn't been revealed when I attacked the Cheongdam branch back then.</p>
<p>"Ah, that's... Recently, Yonghwa Construction, which Yong-tae-ssi attached, had a problem with the Yongcheol Faction..."</p>
<p>Yonghwa Construction was a disguised construction company created in Korea by Dang So-hwa, who gathered the remnants of Kurotsuki-doo in Korea. At first, I tried to keep it as Ryuto Construction, but then I changed the name to Yonghwa Construction. It was created to support Han Yu-gyeong and TG Tech from the shadows, and I heard that the construction business has been doing well recently.</p>
<p>If you translate Ryuto Construction into Korean, it becomes Yongdang Construction, so the nuance was a bit strange, so I changed it to Yonghwa Construction, which of course included Yong-tae's Yong and Dang So-hwa's Hwa in the name. If Han Yu-gyeong knew the meaning, she would have told me to change the name right away, but it seemed she didn't notice since the name was still the same.</p>
<p>Originally, while Taira Foods and Kurotsuki-doo were at war, the Black Moon Society in Korea also provided a considerable amount of support to Japan, which created a power vacuum in Seoul.</p>
<p>The Yongcheol Faction did not miss that power vacuum and dug in to expand its power, and Yonghwa Construction was originally my area, so they fought each other with the position of 'give it back'.</p>
<p>"How did it go beyond Yonghwa Construction and they knew that Yu-gyeong-ah was the person in charge?"</p>
<p>In preparation for being caught for a crime, I had a figurehead president for Yonghwa Construction, and Yang Seol-hui was in charge of the actual command, but it was strange that the Yongcheol Faction knew that Yonghwa Construction was created to support Han Yu-gyeong beyond that.</p>
<p>"I don't know exactly, but maybe a spy from that side infiltrated Yonghwa Construction while they were expanding their power."</p>
<p>Han Yu-gyeong explained to my puzzled expression. Well... Since both Yonghwa Construction and the Yongcheol Faction were expanding their power, impurities could enter at any time in the process of expanding that power. It seemed that there was a spy sent by the Yongcheol Faction among those impurities.</p>
<p>"Now that I've been exposed and even attacked, I have no choice but to eliminate the Yongcheol Faction."</p>
<p>As Han Yu-gyeong said, now that they directly attacked Han Yu-gyeong, we couldn't just stand still.</p>
<p>"Shall I help you?"</p>
<p>"No, it's okay. Yong-tae is busy too. I can't ask you to take care of the students until dinner and help me with my work at night."</p>
<p>"There's no man who can stand still and watch his lover get attacked in front of his eyes. Just let me help."</p>
<p>Han Yu-gyeong initially refused my help, but when I insisted on helping her until the end, she reluctantly allowed it.</p>
<p>"Thank you, Yong-tae-yah..."</p>
<p>Han Yu-gyeong came to me and said, hugging me. As expected, Han Yu-gyeong's embrace felt good. Before I knew it, the atmosphere became strange, and Han Yu-gyeong's face was getting closer and closer.</p>
<p>Jjing! Jjing!</p>
<p>Just before Han Yu-gyeong's lips touched my lips, Han Yu-gyeong's phone rang, and I received a notification that the delivery was complete.</p>
<p>"Let's do it after dinner next time♥"</p>
<p>Han Yu-gyeong made a seductive expression and went out to receive the delivery food.</p>

<p>"I don't have much to do tonight, so how about buying something to eat at the convenience store and raiding Ryuta-jji's room?"</p>
<p>At Seizawa Shii's suggestion, the special class students nodded as if it was an interesting proposal. Since they had heard that their homeroom teacher, Yamashiro Ryuta, had rented a single room, Yamashiro Ryuta's room, where there were no other teachers, was perfect for raiding.</p>
<p>"But won't the teacher be surprised if we raid him suddenly?"</p>
<p>Sakagami Rena, who knew that Yamashiro Ryuta would be away, subtly expressed her opposition, but Seizawa Shii smiled as if that wasn't the case and opened her mouth.</p>
<p>"We're raiding him because we want to see Ryuta-jji's surprised expression~!"</p>
<p>"Yeah, I want to see his surprised expression once."</p>
<p>"Even if he's a Korean teacher, he won't have anything to do in Korea, so we're playing with him."</p>
<p>Sakagami Rena's opposition could not stop the special class students, who had already adopted the mindset of male students teasing their favorite female students.</p>
<p>"Then, we'll buy what we want at the convenience store and meet in front of Ryuta-jji's room in an hour?"</p>
<p>"Okay!"</p>
<p>Seeing her friends who were already determined to raid Yamashiro Ryuta's empty room, Sakagami Rena sighed.</p>
<p>'I'm sorry, Master. I tried to stop them.'</p>
<p>When the students gathered and opened Yamashiro Ryuta's room an hour later, there was no one in the room.</p>
<p>"Eh...? Is Ryuta-jji away?"</p>
<p>"Then let's play games in the teacher's room until the teacher comes!"</p>
<p>"Shall we?"</p>
<p>The special class students waited while playing board games they had brought from Japan in their homeroom teacher's room, but homeroom teacher Yamashiro Ryuta did not come until the headcount before going to bed.</p>
