<p>Three bikes arrived at the parking lot of Minobucho Park, a park in the old urban area of Miyashi. After parking normally, each of them took their weapons from their bikes and got off. It was Shimokawabe Airen, her disciple Alik, and Naruse Shiyo.</p>
<p>"There's a rumor that ghosts appear here; it's scary." Naruse Shiyo muttered, looking around at the eerie Scenery of the sparsely populated park, even though it was daytime. Recently, a rumor had spread that naked female ghosts and male ghosts appeared, and the park, which already had few people because it was in the old urban area, became completely deserted as that urban legend spread. With only a few occasional visitors for psychic spot experiences, it became a park completely devoid of people on normal days.</p>
<p>"If a ghost appears, I'll just cut it down."</p>
<p>"Alik, I'll make the ghost jjirit jjirit." Shimokawabe Airen and Alik were not agitated at all by Naruse Shiyo's slightly frightened Words, as if the ghost urban legend wasn't scary to them. It wasn't that they didn't believe in ghosts, but they were full of Confidence that even if they appeared, they could defeat them. The three masters and their disciples, walking from the parking lot into the park, soon came across a place where several swordsmen had gathered.</p>
<p>Kyoken (狂犬) Nobuchi Tamehito (野渕 為一), with a chonmage-style haircut, shaved at the front and middle of his head with a topknot like an old samurai, and Kenju (劍獸) Senno Yoritaake (薦野 頼赳), who had long straight hair parted down the middle, saw Shimokawabe Airen's party and their eyes lit up. They began to emit an unpleasant, 'keuheuhheu' laugh.</p>
<p>"Keuheuhheuhheu, it's been a long time since we've seen a Swordsman who doesn't run away even after receiving our Duel challenge."</p>
<p>"Heuhheu, they must have heard our rumor. They know that even if they run, we'll Track them to the end. Still, their courage is worth noting. Because it means they won't die as Cowards, even if they die."</p>
<p>As Kyoken and Kenju said, they were famous for sending Duel challenges and Tracking down and killing those who didn't respond to the Duel to the very end. Of course, even if they responded to the Duel, killing them was the same, but challengers who didn't respond to the Duel were killed painfully and cruelly and used to enhance their notoriety. The reason why Shimokawabe Airen, a fairly famous Swordsman, hadn't received a Duel challenge until now was because Shimokawabe Airen was a teacher at St. Francis Girls' Academy.</p>
<p>No matter how famous Kyoken and Kenju were as mad Swordsmen, they couldn't kill a teacher at St. Francis Girls' Academy, which received Sponsorship from several prestigious families, and an advisor to Cheongsim-gwan, who had considerable Influence. But now, Trouble had arisen with Anyaji, and because she had submitted her resignation to join Taira Ryuta's command, she was no longer affiliated with St. Francis Girls' Academy.</p>
<p>So, through Anyaji's current Attack on Miyashi, Kyogen and Kenju sent a Duel challenge to Shimokawabe Airen, whom they had been eyeing for a long time.</p>
<p>"I don't know what you're talking about. Who said they were afraid of you and ran away?" Shimokawabe Airen, who heard their Words, tilted her Head and spoke. Shimokawabe Airen, a battle maniac unlike her Appearance, couldn't understand the very idea of running away after receiving a Duel challenge or mustering courage. She was just puzzled by the Words of Kyoken and Kenju, who were speaking incomprehensible things.</p>
<p>"Heuhheuhheu, it seems they haven't heard our rumor. Or they know but don't care. Sometimes there are such Swordsmen. True swordsmen who have a good taste for cutting."</p>
<p>"Keuheuhheuhheu! It seems this time we've sent a Duel challenge to a winner." Kyoken and Kenju, who laughed as if satisfied by Shimokawabe Airen's Attitude, soon looked at Alik and Naruse Shiyo, who had come with Shimokawabe Airen.</p>
<p>"They are Disciples. We have an educational philosophy that our Disciples must be trained in Actual combat."</p>
<p>"Naturally, our Disciples also enjoy Actual combat like us." Kyoken and Kenju's Disciples were all eight people. The two Swordsmen seemed to feel no particular Problem, even though their Disciples were four times more numerous than Alik and Naruse Shiyo. In battles where lives were at stake, it was common for the Opponent's numbers to be many or few, so they didn't particularly care. Probably, even if Shimokawabe Airen had more Disciples, they wouldn't have cared.</p>
<p>In fact, the initial number of Kyoken and Kenju's Disciples had exceeded 100 people. The two, in their pursuit of extreme Actual combat practicality, thought it couldn't be helped if their Disciples died in battle, but in numerous battles, their Disciples gradually lost their lives or became disabled, dwindling in number, and the remaining ones were now eight.</p>
<p>"Certainly, you need about eight of your Disciples to be a match for my Disciples." Shimokawabe Airen glanced at Kyoken and Kenju's Disciples and said One Word with an indifferent Expression. Of course, she couldn't know the Opponent's Skill thoroughly just by looking, but Shimokawabe Airen knew that Alik and Naruse Shiyo, whom she had as Disciples, were rare and outstanding Talents not just in Japan, but in the World.</p>
<p>She also knew that her Disciples were not Talents who would kneel to a mere numerical Disadvantage, which was why she could disregard Kyoken and Kenju's Disciples.</p>
<p>"Khahahaha! How arrogant! Alright, let's see what kind of Skill you have, spoken with such Confidence?"</p>
<p>"Disciples! Let's see some Blood!" Kyoken and Kenju, rather, seemed to like Shimokawabe Airen's Words and laughed loudly. And at the Words to see Blood, the eight Disciples of the two mad Swordsmen began to draw their Swords.</p>
<p>Naruse Shiyo, seeing the four swordsmen charging at her, tightly gripped her Nagamaki (長巻) Ryuukiba. She felt its heavy weight and cool blade, which reflected the distinct blue glow of Mithril.</p>
<p>"Haap! No need for questions!" One person shouted 'No need for questions!' and attacked with a vertical slash. Following that, one person Stabbed, another made a horizontal slash, and the last Swordsman took an Iaijutsu stance, as if aiming for a Gap behind Naruse Shiyo's Defense. While shrinking from over 100 people to just 8, their combined attack technique (合擊術) had reached a significant level. It must have been the result of desperately coordinating their hands as a survival strategy.</p>
<p>"That's unfair!" Honestly, although Sabu Shimokawabe Airen had said she believed in her Disciples and that they could win even a 2-on-8 Match, from the Opponent's perspective, it was true that it was burdensome. But since they had already entered Actual combat, saying 'unfair' with her Mouth was meaningless. Naruse Shiyo, with the long Handle of her Nagamaki, struck the blade surface of the thrusting Sword, deflecting its Path, then struck diagonally upwards, simultaneously Neutralizing the horizontal and vertical slashes.</p>
<p>It was an excellent response, but Iaijutsu still remained. Passing through the scabbard, which acted as a launcher, the blade, shot out like a Bullet, cut through the empty space. Naruse Shiyo, who had blocked the Attack with an upward diagonal slash, without delay, rolled her Body and dodged the Iaijutsu. Of course, dodging the Iaijutsu by rolling her Body didn't mean the battle was over. The three who had failed in their previous Attack attempted to Attack again.</p>
<p>Naruse Shiyo's Hands and Body became busy Neutralizing and dodging the Opponent's attacks again, as if returning to the beginning of the battle, but in her Expression, there was no sense of urgency or Sense of Crisis. In the Training she had already received from Shimokawabe Airen, she had also been rigorously trained for situations like the current multi-on-one battle. Of course, compared to Actual combat, even rigorous Training had its shortcomings.</p>
<p>That's why Naruse Shiyo also was parrying the attacks of Kyoken and Kenju's Disciples as if the current Actual combat Situation was an advanced Training session. She deliberately didn't Attack even when a Gap was visible in the Enemy, and was only focusing on Defense. Kyoken and Kenju's Disciples also initially thought they were in a superior Situation, but as situations similar to their initial attacks repeated several times, they realized that the Woman holding the Nagamaki in front of them was Mocking/Debasing them.</p>
<p>"It's not working! Killing Move!" At the shout of one person, everyone began to adjust their Swordsmanship martial arts forms. The essence of Kyoken and Kenju's swordsmanship lay in the use of an aura called Sword Killing Intent (劍殺氣), which naturally accumulated in the body through slaying countless people. It was such a destructive aura that, unless one reached the levels of Kyoken or Kengo, using it would place significant strain on the body.</p>
<p>So the Disciples usually faced Enemies with combined attack techniques, and for Enemies difficult to face with combined attack techniques alone, they had used Sword Killing Intent to take their lives. Naruse Shiyo, as if she herself had become a Sword, looking at the Enemies emitting Killing Intent with their entire Bodies, made a slightly Taut Tension Expression and raised her Nagamaki. Seeing the Enemies who had changed from just now, this time, it was Naruse Shiyo who launched a preemptive Attack.</p>
<p>Because Ryuukiba was a whopping 3 meters long, she could unilaterally force a reactive Attack on an Opponent holding a normal katana. If Naruse Shiyo fought aggressively, it was as if she always held the right to the first Attack. As Naruse Shiyo changed the defensive stance she had maintained until just now into an Offensive, Kyoken and Kenju's Disciples, who were preparing to Attack with Sword Killing Intent, were flustered and had no choice but to abandon their Attack and block Naruse Shiyo's blade.</p>
<p>Naruse Shiyo didn't use special Auras like Sword Killing Intent like them, but her attacks were simply fast and strong. Moreover, since she was using a Mithril Weapon, no matter how much Sword Killing Intent a katana contained, it couldn't damage Naruse Shiyo's Ryuukiba. Kang! Kang! Kang! Kang! Once Naruse Shiyo went on the Offensive and began to Pressure the Enemies with the Power to sweep away long-range attacks and multiple areas at once, all of Kyoken and Kenju's Disciples had no choice but to keep their Hands busy.</p>
<p>If they didn't block, their Arm or Neck would quickly be severed by Naruse Shiyo's heavy blow.</p>
<p>"Keueueut...! It can't be helped! We're moving to Stage 3!" Someone who couldn't endure any longer shouted. The third Stage, following their usual combined attack techniques and Sword Killing Intent, was Doping. Kyoken and Kenju's Disciples, who leaped behind the Nagamaki's range as if to escape, took out and ate ominous red pills from their Embrace. Then, the intangible Sword Killing Intent began to appear as a red, tangible haze, and their Pupils also began to glow, stained red.</p>
<p>As a combat doping agent jointly developed by Anyaji's Jusul team and the medical team, it was named Hyeolpokhwan (血爆丸). It was an item that amplified the user's aura and stimulated their muscles to increase power and speed. Of course, there was a Price. After taking Hyeolpokhwan, they could gain an enormous Combat power increase for 20 minutes, but after that, they would have to recuperate for at least two weeks.</p>
<p>"I was just going to face you normally.... But if you use such a cheat Item, I can't help it either." Naruse Shiyo, looking at the Opponent whose Momentum was clearly changing, spoke incomprehensible Words. And at the same time, Naruse Shiyo's Appearance began to change. Ppudeudeuk! With a sound similar to bones breaking, things that wouldn't exist for a human began to grow. Horns grew from her Head, the back of her Cyber Suit opened, and Wings popped out. Lastly, a long Tail grew from above her Buttocks, and the Transformation was complete.</p>
<p>It was Dragon Transformation, one of Naruse Shiyo's Abilities. In fact, today, to improve her Skill as a Swordsman, she had intended to refrain from Dragon Transformation, but since the Opponent was even using Medicine/Drug like that, Naruse Shiyo couldn't help it either. Kyoken and Kenju's Disciples were surprised by the Appearance of the Opponent in front of them, who had suddenly Transformed, and had their Mouths agape. This was because it was something they had never Imagined.</p>
