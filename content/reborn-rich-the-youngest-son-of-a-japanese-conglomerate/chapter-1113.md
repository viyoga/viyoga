<p>"Do you remember that among the information about An-yaji kidnapping or Brainwashing children and minors, all those who were kidnapped or Brainwashed had considerable Talent?"</p>
<p>Huh? Why that Story all of a sudden...? Of course, I knew that fact when I was a Teacher at St. Francis Girls' Academy, and Kujo Kina was also put under Jusul by An-yaji and went almost to the point of Brainwashing, so I remembered it.</p>
<p>"How on earth did they know the potential beforehand?" I had wondered. I had guessed that Funayama Seigo, the now-deceased Four Heavenly Kings of An-yaji who was in charge of Brainwashing and Kidnapping, possessed that Ability.</p>
<p>I asked Athena Mannerheim, the former Four Heavenly Kings of An-yaji, but Mannerheim said she didn't know much about the Kidnapping and Brainwashing side because she didn't work hard for An-yaji and diligently traveled domestically.</p>
<p>"Yes, I remember. So I judged that there might be a Person with the Ability to know potential, and I thought that was probably the deceased Funayama Seigo."</p>
<p>"Among the employees Gensuke had, there was one who was demoted after being at An-yaji Headquarters, and upon closer inspection, it was someone with the Ability to know the potential of the Opponent."</p>
<p>"Really?!"</p>
<p>Hearing Linling's Story, I was surprised, and my Voice involuntarily grew louder. With the Glasses from the Ruins that I possessed, I could only know the 'current' Status, and I couldn't know how much it would grow in the future.</p>
<p>Of course, I couldn't intuitively know the potential as if looking at a Status Window, but with my game Knowledge, combined with elements of luck and innate factors, I could roughly guess how much it would grow.</p>
<p>My recruitment of Alik, who was like a mid-boss, or Dang So-hwa, was a good example of that. Even if Trait acquisition through Level up was random, as long as there was a destiny line of being a mid-boss, their lower limit was quite high, and they always had innate Traits.</p>
<p>Excluding such special Cases, I couldn't know the potential of the others, so I was surprised that Linling found an Ability User who could discern potential among those she rescued and captured as Prisoners this time.</p>
<p>"Who is that Person, and what is their Ability Level?"</p>
<p>She is a woman named Miyazaki Kirara, and while she may not know all the details, she says she can sense it by color when using her ability. The closer it is to gold, the higher the potential, and the closer it is to black, the lower the potential she feels.</p>
<p>It's not like looking at a Status Window, but an Ability to see by Color.... It didn't seem bad.</p>
<p>"How much can that Ability be used?"</p>
<p>"She says it's a Stack-type, and it can be charged once a day. There's no maximum, so if she accumulates it for 10 days, she can use the Ability ten times, and after a day passes, it charges once again, and so on."</p>
<p>Once a day is a bit regrettable, but it was better than nothing. But why didn't she say it immediately and say it now?</p>
<p>"That seems like quite important Information, so why did you say it now?"</p>
<p>"Actually, when I first heard it, I thought it was absurd Information...."</p>
<p>At my Question, Linling replied, subtly watching my Awareness. Indeed.... The claim that one can see a Person's potential by Color, even in this Era where Superhumans have appeared, is quite believable to be considered absurd false Information.</p>
<p>And since it was such a Rare Ability that even I, who knows games well, heard of it for the first time, I could understand Linling not reporting it to me and moving on.</p>
<p>"But why did you say it now?"</p>
<p>"To verify that Ability, I included her in the Negotiation team and sent her to the USA. A Report came in during the funeral."</p>
<p>Ah, so she tried to verify the Ability by having her work as part of the Negotiation team in the USA. I was quite curious about how the verification was done.</p>
<p>"In what way?"</p>
<p>"They received Information about the alchemists from the Obsidian Syndicate and roughly graded their potential, and it reportedly matched about 80 percent."</p>
<p>80 percent.... If the Woman named Miyazaki Kirara is truly an Ability User of potential, should we follow her Information more than the Obsidian Syndicate's Information?</p>
<p>Come to think of it, we don't know how many we can extract. Even among the 80 percent of personnel whose opinions overlapped, if there were many, we might extract three, if few, none, so there was no meaning in my Worry.</p>
<p>"How many do you think we can extract?"</p>
<p>"Surprisingly, the Story went well. Actually, among the Obsidian Pharmaceuticals employees, there were alchemists who were not involved in Drug manufacturing, so the US government judged it unreasonable to impose criminal charges for the New Rochelle incident on them as well. Therefore, alchemists who feel like general employees not involved in Drug manufacturing can be recruited without restriction. The Problem, however, is...."</p>
<p>Oh...! If they can be recruited if not involved in crime, then we might be able to save a few? Of course, I also guessed what Linling meant by 'Problem'.</p>
<p>"Most of the Skilled individuals are involved in Drug manufacturing, aren't they?"</p>
<p>Drugs are an incredibly efficient commodity compared to Investment. No matter how excellent an alchemist and how much they pursue truth, research funds were necessary. Then they would jump into Drug manufacturing, which is easy to make Money from, and naturally, most of the Skilled alchemists of Obsidian Pharmaceuticals would become entangled in the Drug Business.</p>
<p>"Of course, Skilled individuals who joined from outside, not from the Obsidian Syndicate, are not entangled in the Drug Business, so we are targeting them for recruitment. It seems they weren't trusted because they weren't from the Obsidian Syndicate."</p>
<p>So, the strategy is to target alchemists who purely joined the pharmaceutical company from outside. Then....</p>
<p>"Then, let's do this. Recruit the external Skilled individuals, and among the innocent alchemists who are said to lack Skill, also additionally recruit the Kids recommended by that Woman named Miyazaki Kirara."</p>
<p>"Understood. I'll proceed with that!"</p>
<p>At my Words, Linling nodded her Head as if she understood. She probably sent an email or Text Message to the Negotiation team the moment she nodded her Head. Since the Work was finished for now, I decided to ask what I was curious about.</p>
<p>"But why was Miyazaki Kirara demoted?"</p>
<p>Although there was the disadvantage that the Frequency increased once a day, An-yaji was so sincere about kidnapping children with high potential, given her Rare Ability to discern potential, so why was she demoted from Headquarters to a Branch?</p>
<p>"Miyazaki Kirara is from a martial artist Family. That's why she was affiliated with An-yaji. She thought she would supply promising talents with high potential to An-yaji with her Ability, but she didn't know they would kidnap or Brainwash children."</p>
<p>So, Miyazaki Kirara said that since that Kid had high potential, it would be good to recruit them, and An-yaji used their recruitment methods of Kidnapping and Brainwashing, right?</p>
<p>"Ultimately, Miyazaki Kirara protested and declared that she would not use her Ability for An-yaji. An-yaji then sent Miyazaki Kirara to a Branch. While doing so, they threatened her Family as an excuse to prevent her from leaving."</p>
<p>So, they sent her to a Branch because she was disrupting the Atmosphere at Headquarters, and they threatened her to prevent her from leaving because they knew Miyazaki Kirara's Ability. Still, given An-yaji's Traits, I don't think they would have left Miyazaki Kirara alone....</p>
<p>"An-yaji's schedule after the attack on St. Francis Girls' Academy was precisely Miyazaki Kirara's Brainwashing. She seemed to have a certain level of mental Resistance, so An-yaji also needed Preparation for Brainwashing."</p>
<p>Ah...! If we hadn't brought down An-yaji, Miyazaki Kirara would have gone around scanning the potential of children to be kidnapped for An-yaji again. This is Fortunate.</p>
<p>I was worried because she was from An-yaji, but her nature didn't seem bad. As Linling said at the end, having mental Resistance was also quite likable.</p>
<p>"Are the other personnel recruited from the An-yaji Branch also People with special Abilities like now?"</p>
<p>I remembered Linling saying she had separately recruited a few people from the An-yaji Branch under her, so I asked. I was curious if those few recruited also had Abilities like Miyazaki Kirara.</p>
<p>"Except for Kirara-ssi and one other Person, the rest were recruited because they had good Administration Abilities."</p>
<p>Miyazaki Kirara and one other Person? So, there's one more surprising thing, then?</p>
<p>"One Person? Does that one Person also have some kind of Ability?"</p>
<p>"That one isn't an Ability.... Ufufu, I'll tell you about this Person after the verification is complete. I don't want to make Yong-tae-nim worry unnecessarily."</p>
<p>At my Question, Linling replied with a slightly mischievous Expression, laughing. I found such a Linling annoying, so I approached her and tickled her Armpit, saying.</p>
<p>"No, if you don't tell me, I'll worry even more, you know?"</p>
<p>"Hee-heet, I'll tell you right away!"</p>
<p>Of course, I wouldn't tickle her, but Linling giggled as if she were truly ticklish.</p>
<p>"This one isn't an Ability User, but a Person who was in finance when she was at An-yaji Headquarters. Although she came to the Branch after being pushed out in a factional fight, she holds quite important Information. When An-yaji was brought down, An-yaji's property wasn't as much as we thought, was it?"</p>
<p>An-yaji was a group that was over a decade old. They were involved in various evil deeds and engaged in activities through force, so I thought they didn't have much when An-yaji was brought down, but the Person Linling mentioned seemed to be related to that.</p>
<p>"According to this Person, An-yaji has a separate secret Treasure warehouse. She said she wouldn't open her mouth unless given her share, so we are trying to persuade her. Also, it could be a Lie, so I intended to tell you after grasping as much as possible."</p>
<p>This one isn't an Ability User, but she could be called a Person like a Treasure map. A Treasure warehouse.... It was a Word with a certain romance.</p>
<p>"Try to persuade her well, or... tell her you'll listen to her Words."</p>
<p>"Ufufu.... Understood."</p>
<p>At my Words, Linling replied with a Villain-like laugh. She probably roughly guessed my Intention. Unlike Miyazaki Kirara, who defied evil deeds, this one is merely the losing side in a factional fight. There would be no need to necessarily keep a Promise to such a Person.</p>
<p>"Then the Report is finished! You must be fatigued from staying up all night for the Tsuya, so get some Sleep!"</p>
<p>At Linling's Words, I nodded my Head. Although it was only a one-day funeral, considerable mental fatigue had accumulated, so I felt I needed to take a Rest.</p>
<p>Since the funeral was over, Taira Jiro would begin to move in earnest, and if I didn't take a Rest now, I didn't know when I would be able to rest, so I immediately headed to the Bedroom.</p>
