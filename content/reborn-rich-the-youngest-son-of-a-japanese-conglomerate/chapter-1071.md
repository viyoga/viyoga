<p>When Taira Gensuke attacked Taira Ryuta's mansion's main building, Davi Mendes and his subordinates, who were moving to attack the Annex, were dumbfounded to see three women blocking their path. This was because none of the three seemed to have come out for battle.</p>
<p>Standing on the far left, a white woman with hair somewhere between beige and silver hair was glaring at them, holding a Japanese sword. A Japanese sword in battle now...! She must be either a madwoman or an incredibly skilled person, but there was no possibility that a woman barely over 20, even if he was generous, could be skilled, so it seemed she was indeed a madwoman.</p>
<p>The white-haired woman in the center was even worse; she was wearing a cyber Suit, but held no equipment in her hands. The Gas Bag on her back was curious, but it clearly wasn't combat equipment. It was an even more absurd sight than the woman on the left who at least held a sword.</p>
<p>Lastly.... Standing on the far right, the one who blocked their path was a green-haired girl with metal ornaments on her Head.</p>
<p>She was clearly wearing a cyber Suit designed for battle, but a young child stood there, holding nothing in her hands, without a sword or a strange bag.</p>
<p>In this era where cybernetic implants and biological gaejo were possible, there was no difference in Combat ability based on Gender, but a child was different.</p>
<p>Because they didn't know what might happen during growth, gaejo was rarely performed, and in an era that wasn't an old war using child soldiers, Davi Mendes felt something strange about them sending a child out against their attack.</p>
<p>He also thought that perhaps they had come out for a walk unaware of their attack, but it was late Dawn for a walk, and everyone was wearing cyber Suits not worn in daily life, and especially since the white woman was pointing her sword at them, it could be said that the girl who came with them was also there to block them.</p>
<p>「Are there only three of you?」</p>
<p>「Three are more than enough.」</p>
<p>When Davi Mendes asked, the white-haired woman, Asada Ei, replied. Without any panic or surprise, her Expression was truly calm, so Davi Mendes realized that they had truly come out to block him.</p>
<p>「It's a waste of Time to have a Conversation with madwomen.」</p>
<p>Since they had started their Infiltration in the late Dawn, if they delayed too long, the Sun would rise. To seriously think that just three people, including a child, could block them could only be seen as them being insane.</p>
<p>「Kill them all and pass through.」</p>
<p>Davi Mendes gestured to his subordinates. Although it had only been a few days, they had undergone rigorous Training, so the Anya Branch Kwon Masters had become excellent Mercenaries. Due to the influence of Brainwashing, they also followed Davi Mendes's Commands well.</p>
<p>Chwaak! Chwaak! Chwaak!</p>
<p>As the subordinates aimed their Guns according to Davi Mendes's Command, sprinklers installed to water the Grass suddenly began spraying water around them.</p>
<p>Davi Mendes was surprised, thinking it was a hidden Defense Device inside the mansion, but was relieved that it was just a sprinkler spraying water. Although everyone got very wet, now that Winter had passed and Spring had come, there was no Frostbite Problem. The mansion's Administrator must have pressed something wrong or the sprinkler was broken.</p>
<p>But Davi Mendes's Sweet Relief quickly ended.</p>
<p>As if lightning flashed in the Eyes of the woman holding the Japanese sword, Alik, she suddenly began to close the distance at an incredible speed.</p>
<p>Tatatatatang!!</p>
<p>Naturally, Davi Mendes's subordinates also fired indiscriminately at the rapidly approaching Alik, but Flames burst around Alik like an electric fly swatter, and melted Lead Ammunition fell to the Ground.</p>
<p>And she began to emit electricity from her sword, electrocuting the water-soaked subordinates.</p>
<p>Not only Alik, but also the white-haired woman, Asada Ei, did not stand still. She slid forward like she was skating, charging, and began to emit a white fog at the subordinates.</p>
<p>Because she was emitting a white fog like smoke, Davi Mendes, thinking it was Poison, covered his nose with his Forearm, but this white fog was not a toxic Gas. It was an immense Cold energy that would instantly freeze bodies soaked in water.</p>
<p>The subordinates hit by the electricity Alik shot trembled, foamed at the mouth, and collapsed, while the subordinates hit by Asada Ei's Cold energy had their bodies stiffen and fell to the Floor like frozen tuna.</p>
<p>Activating the sprinklers was all part of the plan. To give synergy to the two women who used electricity and Cold energy, they activated the sprinklers to make electricity conduct better and make them even more vulnerable to Cold energy.</p>
<p>「I'll take Responsibility for you.」</p>
<p>The AI Android Linling, whom Davi Mendes had mistaken for a girl, took a step closer and said.</p>
<p>As if melting cotton candy in water, Davi Mendes, who had been staring blankly as Alik and Asada Ei dealt with his subordinates at an incredible speed, came to his senses and looked at Linling.</p>
<p>He had been thinking that if things continued like this, he too would be electrocuted or become like frozen meat, but Davi Mendes thought he had found a way to survive thanks to that Little One.</p>
<p>If he grabbed that child, took her Hostage, and threatened to kill her, he would be able to escape this mansion alive. Also, if he held that child, they wouldn't be able to shoot electricity or Cold energy at him. Because it was certain that the Little One would be caught in the Attack with him.</p>
<p>「Cha-ap!」</p>
<p>Davi Mendes Jumped and leaped in an Ambush to grab Linling. Seeing Davi Mendes suddenly leap towards her, Linling frowned as if she was very disappointed, because a Laser Trap was spread out just a handspan in front of Linling. It was a Laser Trap that looked like the laser alarm systems in museums or art museums seen in movies, but it was a vicious Trap that would split the Body of a Person who passed through it with confidence.</p>
<p>Chwaang!</p>
<p>Seeing Davi Mendes, whose Upper Body and lower body were separated by the Laser Trap, Linling approached.</p>
<p>「Ah, you can't die yet!! This mansion has many excellent Traps and Defense towers! I wanted to Experiment on a skilled person like you!!」</p>
<p>Linling wasn't disappointed by Davi Mendes's Death, but was disappointed that she couldn't Experiment with the various equipment installed in the mansion against Davi Mendes.</p>
<p>「You... crazy... bitch.」</p>
<p>Davi Mendes looked at Linling, who was desperately shaking his Shoulder, and then drew his last Breath.</p>
<p>'I didn't want to use it if possible....'</p>
<p>Taira Gensuke muttered to himself and activated the Drugs Injection function installed in his cyber Suit. A stinging Feeling as the Injection's Needle pierced his Skin, and Body enhancement Drugs began to be injected.</p>
<p>Taira Gensuke wasn't just working for the Taira Trading Company's South American Branch in Brazil. Free from his Family's Gaze, he was doing several more Businesses in Brazil, where his Mother, Taira Kisa's Family, helped him.</p>
<p>One of them was the pharmaceutical company Força Viva, which superficially produced painkillers or Cold Medicine, but its Goal was to produce combat enhancers and supply them to armies, PMCs, rebels, or Terror groups worldwide.</p>
<p>That research had borne some fruit, and was currently selling combat enhancers to the Brazilian police Special Attack unit and the Brazilian Drugs Cartel.</p>
<p>At the pharmaceutical company Força Viva, they had combined various Drugs considering several situations to create several combat enhancers, which basically had the Effect of increasing physical Body Ability.</p>
<p>The Drugs Taira Gensuke injected into himself also, in addition to the basic function of increasing physical Body Ability, were Luxury items with various Effects such as increased cognitive ability and Dynamic Visual Acuity.</p>
<p>Of course, even if it only increased Combat power for a certain period, there couldn't be no Side Effects. There were several Side Effects such as Muscle rupture, vomiting, Headache, and fever.</p>
<p>Taira Gensuke also, to use in an unforeseen Situation, even though he had installed the Drugs Injection function in his cyber Suit, he thought he would rarely use it due to concerns about Side Effects.</p>
<p>But if he didn't use the combat enhancer in the current Situation, he would inevitably face the worst Result of being captured or Death, so Taira Gensuke used the combat enhancer for his Survival.</p>
<p>-White Light Dragon God Style, First Form, Light Dragon Fang Point.</p>

<p>「Hmph!」</p>

<p>As Athena Mannerheim thought, Taira Gensuke allowed Athena Mannerheim's blow. But Taira Gensuke's Shoulder showed no Reaction, as if she had struck a thick Iron plate.</p>

<p>Kwaaaang!!</p>
<p>Athena Mannerheim gathered as much white light as possible to prepare, but true to Mumatsuanya's Murder Martial Arts Form, it delivered an enormous Shock.</p>

<p>「He's escaping!!」</p>
