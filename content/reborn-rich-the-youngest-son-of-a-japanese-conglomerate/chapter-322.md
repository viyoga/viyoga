<p>It wasn't just Seizawa Shii who thought Alik was cute. Even Minamoto Risa, who always seemed disgruntled and full of complaints, had a faint smile as if she found Alik adorable, and even Saionji Diana, the gloomy wheelchair girl who had lost her limbs, was giving Alik a faint smile.</p>
<p>Only Sakagami Rena, the masked girl, was unreadable. Sakagami Rena changes her mask almost every other day, and today she was wearing a long-nosed Tengu mask. I wondered why, but strangely, there wasn't much information about Sakagami Rena in her student record.</p>
<p>,Alik, go to your seat for now. There's an empty seat over there."</p>
<p>I pointed to the empty seat between Konoe Akina and Otagi Yuka. Alik nodded silently and walked quickly to the seat.</p>
<p>,I heard that our class has the lowest club membership rate. You know our school rules, right? Joining a club is pretty much mandatory. It's possible not to join a club if you have your own business like Uenozono, but usually you have to do club activities."</p>
<p>The parents of prestigious families wanted their children to attend a school where daughters from other prestigious families gathered, so they could socialize and make a wide range of friends. That's why, since its founding, students at St. Francisco Girls' Academy have been required to participate in at least one club activity.</p>
<p>They allow Uenozono Karen not to participate in club activities because she's busy with her idol activities, but the rest have no excuse. They must adhere to the principle of one person, one club.</p>
<p>If you really don't want to join a club, you have to get your parents' signature, but there were hardly any parents from prestigious families who would do that.</p>
<p>,This week is the new semester's club recruitment period, so don't give Shimokawabe seonsaengnim a headache and just join any club. If you don't want to, get your parents' signature."</p>
<p>,Then I'll join the club that Ryuta-jji is the advisor for!"</p>
<p>Seizawa Shii said, raising her hand at my words. Hmm, I don't have a club that I'm advising yet, so does that mean she won't join?</p>
<p>,I want to join Ryuta's club too."</p>
<p>Alik suddenly raised her hand and said. The classroom became quiet again, just like when Alik first entered the classroom. Everyone was looking at me and Alik with expressions that seemed to say, "Are they two acquainted?"</p>
<p>,Ryuta-jji! What's your relationship with our cutie Alik? That's super dangerous...!"</p>
<p>,Could it be... a pervert?"</p>
<p>From the start, Seizawa Shii, who was always making a fuss, was freaking out, saying "Cho yabai!" and Minamoto Risa was looking at me with slightly contemptuous eyes, calling me a pervert and amplifying the misunderstanding.</p>
<p>These guys... Why are they making such a big deal out of this? Even though the previous homeroom teacher was fired for sexual assault. I shouted before the situation got worse.</p>
<p>,Don't make a fuss. Alik and I have known each other for a long time!"</p>
<p>,Hmm...! Was that it? Eheh~☆ I misunderstood for nothing?"</p>
<p>I wanted to pinch and stretch Seizawa Shii's white cheeks as she stuck out her tongue and went "Eheh~☆," but I held back. I thought, "Yeah, it's understandable to misunderstand because of the previous homeroom teacher," and decided to be grateful that the misunderstanding was cleared up.</p>
<p>,And I'm not advising any clubs yet, so don't think about joining a club that I'm advising and try to join a club that suits you."</p>
<p>,Ehh...! That's too bad~! I wanted to join Ryuta-jji's club."</p>
<p>Not only Seizawa Shii, but also Alik, Konoe Akina, and Otagi Yuka seemed to feel disappointed at my words. It's troublesome to be too popular....</p>
<p>,And Alik already has a club she's decided to join, right?"</p>
<p>Come to think of it, Alik has to join the Cheongsim Hall, which is advised by her home teacher, Shimokawabe Airen, right? Because she decided to learn swordsmanship from Shimokawabe Airen. Alik, hearing my words, nodded with an expression as if she had remembered something she had forgotten.</p>
<p>,Ah...! That's right."</p>
<p>After that, we had some small talk, and the morning assembly time ended, so I organized the attendance book. Today, the hikikomori students who don't usually come to school and Uenozono Karen, who is doing idol activities, were absent. Uenozono Karen attended school only about twice a week.</p>
<p>Still, shouldn't I see the faces of the students who don't come to school since the teacher has changed...? The principal seemed to be mumbling something, so I should ask Kasaoka Yuzuki, the head of the third grade, about this matter.</p>
<p>,Make sure to decide what club activities you're going to do this week! See you at the end of the day."</p>
<p>,Bye, Ryuta-jji~!"</p>
<p>,See you later, seonsaengnim!"</p>
<p>I heard the students' greetings and left the classroom. As I turned around to close the classroom door, I saw Seizawa Shii and Minamoto Risa approaching Alik. Fortunately, they didn't seem to have any intention of ostracizing Alik.</p>
<p>Alik transferred to this special class for third graders and caused not a typhoon or a strong wind that would flutter skirts, but a gentle breeze that would make the students feel good. She truly deserves to be called the transfer student of gentle breeze.</p>

<p>,You're Alik, right? So cute~!"</p>

<p>When Alik asked back with a naive expression, Seizawa Shii screamed and hugged Alik with her large breasts.</p>
<p>,Did you really know the homeroom teacher from before?"</p>
<p>,Yeah. Ryuta, saved Alik."</p>
<p>Alik nodded at Minamoto Risa's question. Minamoto Risa was slightly surprised by Alik's straightforward answer.</p>
<p>Certainly, the homeroom teacher was handsome, but he had a bit of a Yankee vibe because he usually dyed his hair blonde and tanned his skin brown, so he didn't seem trustworthy. Not only did he know Alik, but he also saved Alik, so Minamoto Risa blamed herself.</p>
<p>,I'm still not there yet...."</p>
<p>She had decided not to judge people by their appearance, but in the end, she judged the homeroom teacher by his appearance and had a preconceived notion. Even though she herself was doing a gyaru style.</p>

<p>,I'm Minamoto Risa. Nice to meet you, Alik."</p>
<p>Minamoto Risa introduced herself and extended her hand. Alik also grabbed Minamoto Risa's hand, shook it, and waved it. Seizawa Shii put her hand on top of their shaking hands and opened her mouth.</p>
<p>,I'm Seizawa Shii. What do you like, Alik?"</p>
<p>Seizawa Shii introduced herself and asked about Alik's preferences at the same time. At Seizawa Shii's question, Alik thought hard and then opened her small mouth.</p>
<p>,Uhm, Alik likes pizza."</p>
<p>,Then shall we go eat pizza after class?"</p>
<p>Alik was very conflicted by Seizawa Shii's suggestion, but she had no choice but to shake her head because she hadn't forgotten why she came to this St. Francisco Girls' Academy.</p>
<p>,Alik, have to go to club."</p>
<p>,Haa...! I hate clubs, but I guess I have to join one after all. Risa, do you have a club in mind?"</p>
<p>In the end, the problem was the club. The two troublemakers had joined several clubs until now, but they were all uncomfortable. Because there were no friends who approached them because of their excessively high family reputation, and the two ended up being outsiders, so they ended up withdrawing from the club repeatedly.</p>
<p>,Not yet... Or shall we create one ourselves? We can just create a club where we do nothing and rest. We can just call it the Meditation Club."</p>

<p>Before she knew it, Yamashiro Ryuta was going to be the advisor for the tentatively named Meditation Club that Minamoto Risa was going to create. Konoe Akina and Otagi Yuka, who were listening to the two delinquent girls' story, had slightly uneasy expressions.</p>
<p>It was because they thought that the two delinquent girls might ruin the innocent Alik. Konoe Akina and Otagi Yuka made eye contact and nodded.</p>
