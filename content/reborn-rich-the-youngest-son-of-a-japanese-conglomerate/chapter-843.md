<p>Bernardo climbed onto the Sparring Ground and faced Pungshingwan's Disciple. Pungshingwan's Disciple was the Person who had annoyingly bothered Takakusaki Athena, but his Skill was quite good, enough to be the first to challenge this Four Heavenly Kings Match.</p>
<p>"Can he win?"</p>
<p>"If he loses, Pungshingwan's Disciple will become a Four Heavenly King."</p>
<p>Takakusaki Gengo spoke coldly to Takakusaki Athena's muttering-like Question. It could be seen as an Attitude contrary to having just commanded Bernardo to kill everyone who came onto the Sparring Ground, but in a way, it could also be seen as a manifestation of Confidence.</p>
<p>"Since I taught him and made him for over ten hours, there's no way he'll lose."</p>
<p>As if he had been listening to Takakusaki Athena's Words, Gwangkwon Kamishirato Manzo said there was no way he would lose. Can a Person become strong in merely ten-some hours? Takakusaki Athena thought, but before she could ask Kamishirato Manzo, the Duel began.</p>
<p>"Actual combat Pungshinryu, I am Namizato Masao."</p>
<p>"Bernardo. I have no school."</p>
<p>After briefly introducing themselves, the two faced each other and took their stances, their Appearances strikingly opposed. Namizato Masao stood still, extending his Fists, while Bernardo lightly performed Capoeira's ginga step, clearly showing that the martial arts they had learned were distinctly different.</p>
<p>"Cha-ap!"</p>
<p>Namizato Masao made the first Attack. It was a Pungshinryu Technology called Gyeokpung, leaping out like a Bullet and striking the opponent's weak spot in their Defense. Bernardo instantly dodged to the side with a light step and strongly struck Namizato Masao's Shoulder with Capoeira's roundhouse kick, Martelo.</p>
<p>"Kueuk!"</p>
<p>"To me, who can even dodge Bullets, such slow Technology is meaningless."</p>
<p>"What are you saying?"</p>
<p>Because Bernardo had muttered in Portuguese, Namizato Masao couldn't understand his Words. But knowing from his Expression and the Atmosphere that he was being mocked, he fumed with anger and lashed out with his Fists and Feet.</p>
<p>No matter how angry he was, Namizato Masao was an excellent Fist Technique practitioner. Each downward strike and thrusting blow contained no tremor caused by anger. But Bernardo's reflexes and physical Ability were superior. He dodged Namizato Masao's sharp, continuous Attacks with remarkable ease.</p>
<p>"G-great. His Evasion is especially incredible. I understand why Makwon brought that Brazilian...!"</p>
<p>One of the Guardian Hall members muttered, watching Bernardo's Appearance. Takakusaki Athena thought the same. While watching, she had considered them sharp blows in her own way, but she hadn't expected him to dodge them so easily.</p>
<p>Of course, to Takakusaki Athena, the most Masterful among the Four Heavenly Kings, he showed a slightly clumsy aspect that needed a bit more refining, but for a self-taught Fist Technique practitioner, he was quite amazing.</p>
<p>Bernardo, having dodged the continuous Technology, placed his Hands on the floor, performed a handstand, and used Beija flor, a move that strikes down on the opponent's Delicate Collarbone or Crown of the head, to strike down on Namizato Masao's Delicate Collarbone.</p>
<p>Udeudeuk!</p>
<p>At the same time as the sound of a Delicate Collarbone breaking, Bernardo rushed in. Having sufficiently demonstrated Capoeira, it was now his Turn to show Brazilian Jiu-Jitsu, another martial art Bernardo had mastered.</p>
<p>Bernardo instantly entangled Namizato Masao's Body like a snake, lifted and pressed down on the arm with the broken Delicate Collarbone, and simultaneously choked his neck.</p>
<p>"Keu, keu, Kueuk...!"</p>
<p>As his neck was choked, the sharply broken Delicate Collarbone must have pierced the surrounding flesh, inflicting tremendous pain on Namizato Masao.</p>
<p>"S-stop! Isn't this enough?! We've lost...!"</p>
<p>Udeuk!!</p>
<p>In the midst of Pungshingwan's Master shouting to stop him, a truly goosebump-inducing sound of bone breaking was heard. It was the sound of Namizato Masao's neck snapping. Namizato Masao, his neck broken, instantly died, his entire Body sagging limply.</p>
<p>"...."</p>
<p>The hall was enveloped in Silence. No matter how much Anyaji and its subordinate organizations pursued extreme Actual combat, cases of dying in a Duel like this were not common.</p>
<p>"Is there no next challenger who wishes to become a Four Heavenly King? Even if Bernardo gets tired, even if an Arm or a Leg breaks, he will continue to accept the Match."</p>
<p>It was as if Takakusaki Gengo's Words completely dismissed Namizato Masao's Death as if it had never happened. But at Takakusaki Gengo's Words, everyone who had decided to step forward for the Four Heavenly Kings Match began to exchange glances, full of Awareness.</p>
<p>No matter how overwhelmingly Bernardo seemed to have won, such a Match was bound to consume tremendous stamina. Furthermore, if Bernardo fought with the Determination to kill, then they too should fight with the Determination to kill.</p>
<p>As the Possibility of becoming a Four Heavenly King became visible, the remaining People, who originally should have protested alongside Pungshingwan's Master, instead engaged in a silent battle of Awareness, wondering who would step forward first.</p>
<p>"This Time, we will step forward!"</p>
<p>The next to step forward was Chimhapryu Jujutsu, seemingly having come forward after seeing Bernardo use Brazilian Jiu-Jitsu-style Technology. Chimhapryu Jujutsu seemed to intend to drag the Match into a Jujutsu Duel. It meant they were confident in joint locks and ground-based Technology.</p>
<p>The second match began, and Bernardo, who had been fighting entangled with his opponent, shook them off and struck down between the opponent's Furrowed Brow with Capoeira's Gancho, a heel strike, killing them instantly with one blow.</p>
<p>A second Death. Of course, Bernardo was gradually getting tired. His Appearance, exhaling roughly, was clearly exhausted, and furthermore, in this Jujutsu Match, his finger had been broken, and his left thumb was heavily swollen.</p>
<p>As expected, rather than fearing Death, other schools challenged again, thinking they could win since the opponent was tired. Bernardo thus received five more challenges from other schools. As Bernardo, who had repeated Battle after Battle, gradually grew more exhausted, he waged fierce Battles, but ultimately, it was Bernardo who won.</p>
<p>Bernardo, possessing two Styles, attacked the opponent's weak points and achieved Victory, but the strange thing was that the more the fight repeated, his Body seemed to grow faster and stronger.</p>
<p>"This is...."</p>
<p>"Kukuku, the more he fights, the stronger he gets, doesn't he?"</p>
<p>As Takakusaki Athena muttered, Gwangkwon Kamishirato Manzo laughed unpleasantly and said.</p>
<p>"What happened? Getting that strong in merely ten-some hours is impossible."</p>
<p>"Kukuku, curious? That Guy's Style was already complete. Rather than accepting new Technology, it seemed right to develop his physical Ability. So, I taught him Gangshinsul-type Jusul, which I've recently taken an Interest in."</p>
<p>"Gangshin, you say...." Takakusaki Athena softly repeated Kamishirato Manzo's Words. Is it a Technology that possesses a Body with a ghost? She roughly understood the concept, but it was a question/doubt why physical Ability would increase by performing Gangshin.</p>
<p>"Physical Ability can increase with Gangshinsul?"</p>
<p>"Of course, not by ordinary methods. I tested it all on experiment subjects and perfected it. Especially the method of descending a Yogoe into a Person's Body, no one would have thought of that, kikiki...!"</p>
<p>Once again, an unpleasant Playful Laughter. Takakusaki Athena knew that Kamishirato Manzo called his Disciples experiment subjects and didn't care for them at all, but she hadn't known it was to this extent.</p>
<p>"Yogoe, you say...?"</p>
<p>"It's difficult to catch, but I either buy them from an Onmyoji Family or use ones I've captured myself, you see. Since that Guy is a Foreigner, I naturally put in a Yogoe from that region. Do you know of something called a Manticore?"</p>
<p>Of course, Takakusaki Athena knew of the Manticore. It was a Chimera-type demonic beast with the forms of various animals attached. It was usually depicted as having the Body of a lion, the Face of a human, and the Tail of a snake.</p>
<p>"Isn't the Manticore a monster from Europe or the Middle East? That Person seems to be from South America...."</p>
<p>"Shut up. It's the same Foreign Country, isn't it?"</p>
<p>Kamishirato Manzo, who ignored Takakusaki Athena's Tackle with a single Word of "Shut up," looked up at the Sparring Ground with an Expression of utter Satisfaction.</p>
<p>Bernardo, exuding a strongly Dangerous Atmosphere, stood looking down at a single Corpse.</p>
<p>"Is there more?"</p>
<p>No one could Answer Takakusaki Gengo's Words. At the end of a brief Silence, Takakusaki Gengo opened his mouth again.</p>
<p>"With this, there won't be anyone opposing Bernardo occupying one of the Four Heavenly Kings' Seats, will there? With this, let's end today's event. Only Anyaji's ganbu remain, everyone else, leave."</p>
<p>At Takakusaki Gengo's Words, the People affiliated with Gwonyeonghoe, and the schools and martial arts dojos subordinate to Anyaji, rushed out like an ebb tide.</p>
<p>"Seigo."</p>
<p>Takakusaki Gengo, watching the People leave, called Four Heavenly King Funayama Seigo.</p>
<p>"Yes."</p>
<p>"Since Bernardo used his power until just now, this Time, take Genba Kiyohira and take over the schools that came onto the Sparring Ground today. You may deal with those who show defiance as much as you want."</p>
<p>"Understood."</p>
<p>Funayama Seigo took Genba Kiyohira and disappeared somewhere.</p>
<p>"Is there a need to go this far?"</p>
<p>Takakusaki Athena asked. She thought that completely taking over those schools, after just killing their Disciples, was an excessive action.</p>
<p>"Before a big fight, pruning is necessary, Athena."</p>
<p>"Is that so...."</p>
<p>Takakusaki Gengo, who had been familiar until she regained her memory, now felt like a stranger. Takakusaki Athena, not wanting to show her Expression, bowed her Head deeply and answered, pretending to agree.</p>
