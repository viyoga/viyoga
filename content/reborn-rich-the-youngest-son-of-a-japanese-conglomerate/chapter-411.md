<p>Not long after Yamashiro Ryuta finished his Conversation with Eve and left the Secret Chamber, Sakagami Rena came down to the Underground to meet Eve. Since Yamashiro Ryuta had already left, Sakagami Rena also intended to greet Eve before returning Home and then go Home.</p>
<p>"Sakagami-yang, have you investigated thoroughly?"</p>
<p>"...What are you talking about?" At Eve's abrupt Question, Sakagami Rena tilted her Head and asked back. It was a completely incomprehensible Question, asking if she had investigated when she hadn't even requested Information.</p>
<p>"I told you that for Ryuta-nim to become Sakagami-yang's Liege/Master, I needed to know Ryuta-nim well, didn't I?"</p>
<p>"Since that advice, I've been following Teacher-nim constantly, so I believe I know enough." Although it was Sakagami Rena's confident Answer, Eve shook her Head. Her Attitude implied it was far from enough.</p>
<p>"You still haven't obtained the level of Information I had in mind. It's not Information you can learn just by following him around."</p>
<p>"You...!" At Eve's definitive Words, Sakagami Rena tried to get angry, but soon, as if realizing Something, she nodded her Head. She didn't know everything about Yamashiro Ryuta yet. Rather, she felt ashamed that she had been so confident, thinking she knew everything just by following him for a few days. Indeed, she wasn't investigating an ordinary Person. She was investigating about the Liege/Master to whom she would entrust her entire life.</p>
<p>Sakagami Rena seemed to have thought too complacently, having been drawn in by Yamashiro Ryuta's Charm/Allure. Eve had thrown the Question at Sakagami Rena, asking if a mere few days of investigation would be enough. Moreover, because she had implicitly suggested that there was still a lot of other undisclosed Information about Yamashiro Ryuta, Sakagami Rena rather felt grateful to Eve.</p>
<p>"Thank you."</p>
<p>"I haven't done anything, have I? I merely don't want to see Sakagami-ssi looking puzzled when we stand on the same side. And if Sakagami-ssi realizes it belatedly, she'll end up blaming herself."</p>
<p>"That's what I'm grateful for." Eve was also helping Yamashiro Ryuta with his Work, and if they were going to be on the same side anyway, it was clear she wanted to see more of Sakagami Rena's Skill as a Kunoichi.</p>
<p>Sakagami Rena bowed her Head to Eve and stood up from her Seat. What was important wasn't just following Yamashiro Ryuta around. It was important to investigate him from beginning to end.</p>
<p>'It wasn't just about following him; I needed to find traces from the Yamashiro Family where Teacher-nim was born and raised.' After finishing her thoughts, Sakagami Rena stepped out of the Secret Chamber and picked up her phone. She was going to contact her main family after a long time. She was doing it to obtain information as a Chunin (中忍: Middle Ninja) of the Sakagami Family.</p>
<p>"It's Rena. I'd like to Request Information...."</p>
<p>A few days ago, a certain Place.</p>
<p>"Until now, we have overlooked the importance of the special class. Moreover, looking at the faces of the Students, this special class is even more important, but everyone only avoided it to not get covered in poop water, so they missed the position of Class Teacher...."</p>
<p>Daigo Toyotaka, a man with a closely shaved head and a gray beard, opened his mouth as he looked at the gathered crowd. Daigo Toyotaka, as the sujang of the Toyotaka Faction (豊尊派), the second strongest faction of the Daigo Family that dominates St. Francisco Girls' Academy and St. Francisco Women's University, was currently working to expand the influence of St. Francisco Girls' Academy, where Daigo Terue served as Headmaster.</p>
<p>The People gathered now were also from the Toyotaka Faction; they were in the middle of a Meeting to increase their Influence at St. Francisco Girls' Academy.</p>
<p>"Certainly, it's a special class mainly attended by Problem Students and Students with poor health. But this year, its meaning is different. Because most of the Students, including Minamoto Risa of the Minamoto Family, are Young Ladies of outstanding high-ranking Prestigious Families, or affiliated with organizations that cannot be ignored."</p>
<p>One of the seated individuals spoke as if explaining to those around him. Indeed, the current special class was different from previous special classes. Until then, it had been just an ordinary class for delinquent Students, but this year, even a quick glance at the Students' names revealed they were all extraordinary.</p>
<p>From the Daughter of the Minamoto Family, one of Japan's two great powers, the Seizawa Family, a Family that produces Prime Ministers, the Kujo Family, which pursues Minamoto and Taira, the Otagi Family, a Hospital chaebol, the Young Lady of the Saionji Family of Saionji Bank was affiliated with the special class, and even the young Head of Family of the Konoe Family, a Prestigious Family among Prestigious Families, was directly affiliated, so its importance was enormous.</p>
<p>"That's right. We should have blamed Daigo Terue for the former Class Teacher's mistake and appointed a Class Teacher from our side. But that didn't go as well as expected. Especially since the former Class Teacher was connected to our side."</p>
<p>After the former Class Teacher of the 3rd-year special class's sex scandal, the Toyotaka Faction couldn't exert its Power at St. Francisco Girls' Academy. Because the former Class Teacher who caused the accident was a Person recommended by the Toyotaka Faction. As it was, they couldn't even make a move under the influence of the Tadanori Faction, the largest faction of the Daigo Family, and were stuck in meaningless positions like that of a special class Class Teacher.</p>
<p>But even that position was lost due to the former Class Teacher's sex scandal, so the Toyotaka Faction's agitation was enormous. They thought this was finally the time to recruit Young Ladies from various Families and expand their influence, but because of the foolish former Class Teacher, the Toyotaka Faction's plan was shattered.</p>
<p>"Still, this time, there is someone who voluntarily Contacted us and gave us Information and a scheme. They say it's the History Teacher for the 3rd year.... If that Teacher's Words are true, we can plant our influence in the special class."</p>
<p>"Vice President Toyotaka-nim, what is that scheme?" Daigo Toyotaka gave a deep Smile at the Question about the scheme.</p>
<p>"Hehehe, that plan is...."</p>
<p>"What is this, a student teacher?" I, who had gone to Work in the Morning, read the content that had arrived on the fax. It said that today, a student teacher, an Education intern, would come as an temporary assistant Class Teacher for our class. They said it was to alleviate the hardships of the Class Teacher of the difficult special class, which had many Students to care for.... Can something like this be decided so suddenly? Woong...! While I was looking at the fax, a Phone Call came from Daigo Terue. As expected, it seemed she was trying to give me additional Explanation.</p>
<p>"Yes, this is Yamashiro."</p>
<p>"-Yamashiro Teacher-nim, I have something to tell you about the deployment of this Education intern."</p>
<p>"Ah, I was also just surprised that a student teacher-nim was suddenly coming."</p>
<p>"-Actually, due to our school's Traits, we don't usually accept Education interns." That's right. St. Francisco Girls' Academy, due to its academy Traits, is thorough in Information management. So there's no Reason to unnecessarily accept an untrustworthy Education intern.</p>
<p>"-Saying this is close to a disgrace for our Family, but.... The Daigo Family is divided into my Father Daigo Tadanori's Faction and my uncle Daigo Toyotaka's Faction. Although our side's Power is strong as my Father is the eldest son of the Family, that Faction also has the Power to insert at least one Education intern. It seems they orchestrated this because the special class was originally the Toyotaka Faction's territory and they wanted to reclaim it." So, it was an Incident orchestrated by another Faction. It's become a bit troublesome, but since it's already happened, there's nothing I can do. I just had to assess The Situation and deal with it well.</p>
<p>"-Still, I chose the most decent-looking Person from the List they sent. I chose someone who would have the least possible connection with Toyotaka's Faction, so it should be better, but still, be careful."</p>
<p>"Yes, thank you for your concern." As expected, both the Taira Family and the Daigo Family had Factions. It was fortunate that this side only had two Factions. Taira currently had three Factions vying for Succession, and I also intended to participate in that struggle for the throne someday, so it was much more chaotic than the Daigo Family. After ending the Call with Daigo Terue, I continued reading the content written on the fax. It said the Education intern would arrive here before 9 AM and join the Morning assembly.</p>
<p>Since I had come a little early to eat Breakfast in the Education preparation room, I would have to wait a while to find out the True Identity of the Education intern. As I was eating the Sandwich prepared for Breakfast and reading another fax, my phone vibrated, and a Line message arrived. When I checked who it was, it was the Masked Woman, Sakagami Rena.</p>
<p>"-Sakagami Rena: Teacher-nim, I will be absent today due to Family/Household matters." Why is she doing this again? Sakagami Rena had been Held Back a Year before because she had many absences due to Family/Household Work. So since I was appointed as Class Teacher, I had made sure she wouldn't be absent as much as possible. But today, she had Contacted me to say she would be absent for the first time in a while.</p>
<p>According to the data provided by the Taira Group's Intelligence team, the Sakagami Family was said to be a Family specialized in Intelligence. It seemed Sakagami Rena had also been doing that Work since she was young. Seeing that she was absent after a long time, it seemed she had a very busy task. By the way, if it's a Prestigious Family that has been specialized in Intelligence for a long time.... Surely it's not a Family like ninjas, is it? Still, it wasn't an unexcused absence, and she had informed me, so I should take it into account.</p>
<p>Since the special class was created to overlook such minor things a bit and help Students graduate, as the Class Teacher, I wrote it down in the Attendance Book as an absence due to Work. If it wasn't an unexcused absence like Minamoto Risa or Seizawa Shii had done until now, the special class was quite lenient, so there wouldn't be much harm to Sakagami Rena.</p>
<p>"Hello, I am Kirie Kurayamada." Just as I finished eating my Sandwich, the Door opened, and a Voice was heard. It seemed the student teacher had arrived.</p>
