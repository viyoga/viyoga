<p>I didn't rush straight into the battlefield; I took a moment to assess the situation to see what was going on.</p>
<p>First off, the guys wearing St. Francisco Girls' Academy security uniforms seem to be PMC under Minamoto, supporting the Toyotaka faction, who I was originally going to deal with....</p>
<p>Those wearing the latest combat suits, judging by their figures, were all women. Their movements suggested they weren't used to this kind of full-scale slaughter, but considering they were firing mithril bullets-practically throwing money into the air-from combat suits with built-in bulletproof features, I could guess their identities.</p>
<p>More than anything, seeing the woman in a combat suit who seemed to be the leader, holding a jitte in one hand and a submachine gun in the other, confirmed her identity. Among the combatants I know, only Nakanoin Rina, the Disciplinary Committee Chairwoman, uses a jitte as a weapon.</p>
<p>It seems Taira Kanna sent the Disciplinary Committee's combat unit because Taira's side couldn't properly mobilize their forces due to the sudden kidnapping... But even in an emergency, is it right to give a high school girl a gun and send her out?</p>
<p>Okay, I roughly know who the combat suit side is, so looking at the rest... The group with different outfits and no sense of uniformity in their equipment was impossible to identify no matter how I looked at them.</p>
<p>Ah! Looking closely, one person is familiar... It was a one-eyed guy with a mohawk and a black eyepatch; I definitely felt like I'd seen him somewhere.</p>
<p>"Kyahahaha! Die!"</p>
<p>As if knowing I was struggling to identify him, the mohawked one-eyed man even gave me his voice. Hearing his voice, I remembered exactly who he was. It was a Jinildang executive who fought me during the cherry blossom viewing terror incident at the Talent Development Center. I remember struggling quite a bit back then because it was my first time fighting a guy modified with cyberware. I felt like I could beat him much more easily now.</p>
<p>Finally, to summarize the situation, it was a three-way battle with the Toyotaka faction, Minamoto's security forces, the St. Francisco Girls' Academy Disciplinary Committee, and Jinildang, who I don't know why they showed up.</p>
<p>It's quite a chaotic situation, but what I have to do is simple. Except for the Disciplinary Committee, I just need to wreck them all.</p>
<p>"I'll explain the situation..."</p>
<p>I contacted Sakagami Rena and Konoe Akina, who were following behind, told them about the current three-way battle, and then jumped into the middle of the battlefield.</p>
<p>Kung!</p>
<p>A moment of silence. Everyone seemed surprised by the sudden appearance of a huge person in black armor-like combat suit in the center.</p>
<p>"B, Black Knight?"</p>
<p>The security forces from PMC, who are the most informed, recognize my identity. Of course, there was a guy who shot at me without paying attention to my appearance, so the armor that repels bullets rang, tingtingting.</p>
<p>I first ran towards the guy who was shooting at me from a close distance without saying anything and threw a middle kick. It was a guy who seemed to be a Jinildang combatant, and he tried to block my kick by raising his gun. But that was a meaningless act.</p>
<p>Kwadeuk!</p>
<p>With one kick, the metal gun broke in half, and my shin shattered his ribs. The Jinildang member flew into the air from the impact as if he had been in a car accident and crashed to the ground. Judging by the fact that he was twitching and then stopped moving, it seemed that the broken ribs had pierced his major organs, causing his death.</p>
<p>The next thing I attacked was the barrier set up by the security guards with long shields. Each shield had bulletproof glass and gun holes, so they could shoot without much interference and block enemy fire. It was a pretty dirty tactic where they only hit and didn't get hit from the perspective of the opponent.</p>
<p>I have to smash such dirty tactics. I used Shadow Domination and the Abyss to create shadow blades on my fingertips and swung them. In an instant, the shadow of my hand stretched out and began to cut through the shield wall.</p>
<p>"Now! Focus fire!"</p>
<p>While I was cutting the shields, all of Jinildang fired at me. Even though they had been fighting each other until now, they seemed to know that they would be annihilated if they didn't stop me from taking down the security guards.</p>
<p>I spread out my other hand and created a psychokinetic barrier to stop the bullets pouring at me in mid-air. My highest stat, Willpower, was the main stat that determined the power of psychokinesis, and I had been training psychokinesis and Shadow Domination Shinkaiyatsude whenever I had time, so my skill level had also increased considerably.</p>
<p>So, I was able to use a similar technique to the unnamed psychokinetic ability user I struggled with before. I continued to cut the shield wall while simultaneously reflecting the bullets I had stopped in mid-air to launch a counterattack. Unfortunately, the precision was a bit lacking, and the trees in the forest interfered with the bullets' trajectory, and the Jinildang combatants hid behind the trees scattered in the forest, so I couldn't kill many. Only two people who were startled by my psychokinesis and hesitated were hit by the reflected bullets and fell.</p>
<p>The Disciplinary Committee also seemed to realize that I wasn't the enemy, so they were firing their guns avoiding me. The security guards who had lost their shield wall and Jinildang, who were intimidated by surprise, were now in a state of low morale.</p>
<p>"Die!"</p>
<p>The mohawked one-eyed man throws a grenade at me. I stopped the grenade in mid-air with psychokinesis. And just like before, I was about to return the grenade when he gave me a bitter smile.</p>
<p>"I knew you'd do that!"</p>
<p>At that moment when I was concentrating on psychokinesis, the mohawked one-eyed man's hand dropped, and a lens-like muzzle was visible. In an instant, a laser beam was fired at me.</p>
<p>Kwaang!</p>
<p>It was the mohawked one-eyed man's desperate attack, but this armor was made by Simyeonui ttal herself. In combat situations, the Abyss flickers and envelops it like flames, so a laser beam with only cyborg output couldn't penetrate it.</p>
<p>Because I was hit by the laser beam while trying to return it with psychokinesis, the grenade fell to the ground with a thud. Looking at the grenade, it seemed he had thrown it at me without pulling the pin. He had planned to launch a surprise attack when I tried to return the grenade, but unfortunately, the power was too weak to work.</p>
<p>"I'll throw it properly this time."</p>
<p>Geugeugeuk, the safety pin is pulled in mid-air. The grenade flies like a cannonball by psychokinesis. At the same time as the grenade that fell in the middle of Jinildang exploded, I rushed at the mohawked one-eyed man. I felt the fragments of the exploded grenade hitting the armor, but I didn't care because I trusted the armor.</p>
<p>"If I spared you before, you should have stayed put."</p>
<p>"What are you talking about?!"</p>
<p>I was talking about the story of him fighting me during the cherry blossom viewing terror and escaping in a near-death state, but of course, he didn't understand. Back then, he was a Talent Development Center employee wearing a suit, and now he's wearing Black Knight's armor, so it was natural that he couldn't recognize me.</p>
<p>Shinkaiyatsude Kuroyamamota (Black Mountain Lean)</p>
<p>I struck the mohawked, one-eyed man’s solar plexus with my shoulder using Gobeop (a technique of hitting with the shoulder and back). I felt his metal body crumple with an unpleasant sound, kwadeuk. Then I swung my foot at his neck as he recoiled from my Black Mountain Lean.</p>
<p>Shinkaiyatsude Hurekatana Zanshuashi (Touching Sword Decapitation Leg)</p>
<p>As the name of the super-skill Touching Sword Decapitation Leg suggests, my leg covered in the Abyss's blade becomes the Abyss's tentacle and separates the mohawked one-eyed man's body.</p>
<p>"Kuaaa!!"</p>
<p>The mohawked one-eyed man was still alive and screaming, probably because he had a life support device attached even though his head was blown off. I raised my foot and crushed his face as it was. He somehow survived and escaped during the cherry blossom viewing terror, but it seemed he was destined to die by my hands in the end.</p>
<p>Leaving Jinildang, who had suffered considerable casualties due to the grenade explosion, I rushed towards the security guards this time. Actually, I came running to find the kidnapped Oofuchi Erika, so taking down these guys was a priority over Jinildang. But if I left the cyborg mohawked one-eyed man alone, the Disciplinary Committee would suffer considerable damage, so I took down Jinildang first.</p>
<p>There was no one behind the shield wall I had already destroyed. Everyone's morale had dropped, and they were hiding behind the shrine's walls or trees, passively firing. I expanded my senses as much as possible to feel where Oofuchi Erika was.</p>
<p>She was surprisingly close. I felt a presence in the shrine building right in front of me; it seemed like there was one woman and two men.</p>
<p>"Stop!"</p>
<p>"Fire!"</p>
<p>As I headed straight for the shrine building, the security guards, who had only been passively responding, began to fire at me. These guys are quite brave, considering they saw me use psychokinesis?</p>
<p>To save Oofuchi Erika as quickly as possible, I didn't use psychokinesis but used Shadow Domination to pierce the feet of the guys aiming at me. This alone can stop the fire concentrated on me and temporarily incapacitate them.</p>
<p>And if the Disciplinary Committee attacks the guys groaning in pain during that time, I can stop the security guards without having to use much strength. Glancing back, Nakanoin Rina, holding a giant jitte, was shooting a small submachine gun and swinging the jitte to dent the enemies' heads.</p>
<p>It's a non-lethal weapon, the jitte, but the 1.5-meter jitte that Nakanoin Rina uses has become an excellent lethal iron mace that can break heads just by swinging it. Nakanoin Rina's strength to swing that with one hand was also amazing.</p>
<p>While watching Nakanoin Rina's head-breaking show, I was able to arrive at the shrine I was aiming for. I burst open the door and went inside.</p>
<p>"D, don't come any closer! Do you want to see this woman die?"</p>
<p>"Why did Black Knight come here? Is it money? We'll give you several times more money, so how about joining our side?"</p>
<p>Ishidera Kosuke was threatening me by pointing a gun at Oofuchi Erika, and a man who seemed to be from Minamoto was trying to bribe me with money.</p>
<p>"Money? That's a bit interesting?"</p>
<p>I said something that sounded like I was tempted by the money for Oofuchi Erika's safety. I felt a sign of relief, as if my acting had worked. Ishidera Kosuke's hand holding the gun relaxed slightly, and I saw the man from Minamoto smiling slyly.</p>
<p>But because the two were relieved by my words, they didn't notice their shadows lengthening and becoming darker.</p>
<p>"The money I'm supposed to receive is..."</p>
<p>When I paused for a moment, the two focused even more on my mouth. Of course, they couldn't see my mouth directly because I was wearing armor and a helmet.</p>
<p>"Your heads."</p>
<p>Ishidera Kosuke's shadow rises like a blade and drops the hand holding the gun. A shadow skewer pierces the ankle of the Minamoto guy to get information.</p>
<p>"Kuaaaa!"</p>
<p>"Ugh!!"</p>
<p>Ignoring Ishidera Kosuke, who was bleeding profusely from his wrist, I snatched Oofuchi Erika and held her in my arms.</p>
<p>"W, who are you?!"</p>
<p>Sakagami Rena had already entered the shrine using ninja techniques, so I handed Oofuchi Erika over to Sakagami Rena. Now I should finish up the outside matters... Huh?</p>
<p>Suddenly, a rumbling sound and the ground starts to shake. An earthquake at a time like this? What's going on?</p>
