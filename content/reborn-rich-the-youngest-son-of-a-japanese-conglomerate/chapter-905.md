<p>Just then, World War III broke out. Takakusaki Gengo immediately joined the Japanese Special Mission Unit. Takakusaki Gengo, who already had a high reputation as a Fist Technique master, was able to join the unit he desired. Takakusaki Gengo, while undertaking various assassination missions, traveled the world and collected various data. He could also access secret information gathered by the military and the Japanese government. It was then that he discovered a power antithetical to the Abyss.</p>
<p>The power of an Outer God called the White Night Dragon was randomly manifesting across generations in people of a certain Family in the Nordic region, where white nights continued endlessly. Takakusaki Gengo killed everyone in that Family except for the daughter who possessed that power, and adopted that daughter as his own. Naturally, that adoption did not stem from any affection, but merely to research the power of an Outer God.</p>
<p>Takakusaki Gengo, believing he had gathered enough information, left the Japanese Special Mission Unit, using an injury during the war as an excuse, and created Anyaji, named after his martial art, Mumatsuan'ya. He attached the name 'Ji' , meaning Temple, to the martial arts organization because Takakusaki Gengo's martial art had already reached a stage of spiritual cultivation rather than just physical training.</p>
<p>Takakusaki Gengo gathered various martial artists who possessed outstanding skill but were unrecognized, and Monsters who had learned suspicious sorcery and could not integrate into society. He then kidnapped war orphans, whose numbers had increased due to the war, brainwashed them, and slowly expanded his influence.</p>
<p>Takakusaki Gengo created Anyaji to create limbs and hands that would move on his behalf, as he was busy with his training, and to gather information related to Outer Gods and kidnap people born with the power of Outer Gods, like Athena Mannerheim, whom he had adopted as his daughter. With the power of sorcery, the various powers of Outer Gods collected from across the country, and even the power of heretical martial arts possessed by the martial artists who joined Anyaji, Takakusaki Gengo's Mumatsuan'ya gradually began to change.</p>
<p>It transformed into an ominous black Aura, a mixture of all sorts of powers, just as it was when first named. If the Abyss felt like looking into the darkness of an endless hole, Takakusaki Gengo's Mumatsuan'ya felt like looking into a puddle where paints of countless colors were discarded. At some point, this Aura, which differed from Shinkai Yatsude's Abyss, was unshaken even by Athena Mannerheim's white Light. That was why he had kept Athena Mannerheim alive, whom he had considered disposing of because she might harm him.</p>
<p>Since she was no longer a threat to him. At some point, Takakusaki Gengo felt discomfort not in his body, but in his shell. He continuously thought that the wicked power within his human skin could not display its full Destructive Power with his current Physical Body. At that point, Takakusaki Gengo realized his true Goal. Takakusaki Gengo wanted to become an Outer God himself. He wanted to become a being of Fear that even the Magician who killed his Father would not dare to look upon.</p>
<p>Of course, he hadn't intended to become an Outer God from the beginning. But the Outer God he sought to become stronger possessed what he desired. That was infinite Power, transcending humans. Takakusaki Gengo thought that from the moment he first learned of the existence of Outer Gods, he hadn't merely wished to gain the power of an Outer God, but had implicitly desired to become an Outer God himself. From then on, Takakusaki Gengo prepared for his transformation.</p>
<p>He built a shrine deep in Mount Genashi for himself, tapping into the power of the ley lines from Mount Fuji, Japan's most sacred mountain. Using the dark, powerful magic he had amassed over more than ten years, he aimed to shed his human form and gradually ascend into a god beyond the human world, an alien god. The Children who were raised for Anyaji's future became offerings, and those born with the power of an Outer God, whether young Children or older ones, were all kidnapped and their power absorbed.</p>
<p>The attack on Miyashi this time was an Operation to finally absorb Fujigasaki Rinka's Abyss and be reborn as an Outer God. It was luck that he found out Fujigasaki Rinka's Disciple was in Miyashi. Because he could use Fujigasaki Rinka's Disciple to lure Fujigasaki Rinka to the Place he desired. To kidnap the Children they had rescued again, attacking Miyashi, where countless forces were entangled, was a truly terrible Justification, but Anyaji's Subordinates, who already had a well-established system of strict obedience, faithfully followed.</p>
<p>Although the Attack hadn't gone well for Anyaji, Takakusaki Gengo didn't care. Because his metamorphosis was right before his Eyes.
