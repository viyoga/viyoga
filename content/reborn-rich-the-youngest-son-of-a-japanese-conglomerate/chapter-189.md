<p>"Yeah. I'll come back without a single scratch, so don't worry."</p>

<p>Thanks to my regeneration ability, I won't get scolded even if I get hurt. I've been hit by so many bullets, fists, and blades that my regeneration ability has leveled up quite a bit. Now, even if I lose a finger, it'll regenerate in about two hours.</p>
<p>On the way back to Seoul, Han Yu-gyeong-ssi seemed to be receiving a report from I Yeon-ju-ssi. <p>At my words, Han Yu-gyeong-ssi said she would take care of it, as if telling me not to worry. She'll probably send Vanguard agents to destroy the data or something. <p>"Mr. Jin, we've arrived. Be careful."</p>
<p>"Really, don't get hurt, okay?"</p>
<p>"Yeah, I definitely won't get hurt. I'll be back."</p>
<p>I got out of the car. There was a long line in front of the club, with music blaring even outside. I withdrew about 1 million won in cash from the convenience store in front of the club. Then, I walked past the long line and approached the bouncer guarding the door.</p>
<p>"What's the matter? If it's nothing important, just stand in line like everyone else."</p>
<p>The bouncer was a giant, nearly 2 meters tall. He had a menacing look that would make most people shit their pants just by looking at him. At this level, people would listen very well. Of course, I wasn't intimidated by his size. No matter how tall he is, I don't like him looking down on me.</p>
<p>"I came to find someone..."</p>
<p>"Whether it's a person or whatever, if you want to enter our club, stand in line."</p>
<p>I could feel the stinging gazes of the people standing in line, but I chuckled and handed the bouncer the cash envelope.</p>
<p>"I'll really just find the person and leave."</p>
<p>I could knock him out and go in without spending money, but there were too many people watching because the line was so long, so I couldn't leave any evidence. The bouncer glanced at the envelope and then cleared the way for me to enter. I could hear the curses of the people in line behind me.</p>
<p>When I opened the door and went inside, the thumping music became louder, and as I went further into the club, I saw it was filled with psychedelic lights, music, and men and women dancing while indulging in drugs.</p>
<p>The DJ who was DJing on the second floor was a body modification freak who had modified his entire body, but not for combat purposes. He was a crazy bastard who had implanted speakers and neon lights into his body.</p>
<p>There weren't just people dancing; there were also suspicious-looking drug dealers and guys in suits who looked like gangsters.</p>
<p>"If I remember correctly, it was on the 4th floor."</p>

<p>Two Yongcheol gang members were standing in front of the door at the end of the stairs on the 4th floor. There was also a CCTV camera in the corner of the ceiling on the right. <p>"Civilians are not allowed to enter here, so please go down."</p>

<p>"Please tell Hyung-nim Gyeong-tae."</p>

<p>"Yes."</p>
<p>"Moon-shik Hyung-nim must have seen you in a good light. Sending a newbie as a messenger. Do well."</p>
<p>"Thank you."</p>
