<p>"Haa..."</p>
<p>A man sighed while looking at documents. The man was Choi Gyu-hyeon, the president of ISIS, and the document he was sighing at was ISIS's sales report for 2053.</p>
<p>ISIS was a company that researched cooling technology, and its main product was a human cryopreservation package for freezing people with incurable diseases.</p>
<p>When they started the business, they were doing pretty well, but that was only for a short time. Suddenly, World War III broke out, and as time passed, the medical field developed, and many incurable diseases that required freezing disappeared, causing ISIS's cryopreservation package sales to plummet.</p>
<p>Of course, there were other businesses that utilized cooling technology, but in the end, ISIS's main focus was the cryopreservation package, so the deficit continued to grow.</p>
<p>"Haa... In the end..."</p>
<p>Choi Gyu-hyeon sighed and put the documents on his desk. While he was worrying about sales like this, a proposal came along like a lifeline to escape this predicament.</p>
<p>TG Tech, which was famous these days, had made a proposal to acquire ISIS. For Choi Gyu-hyeon, it was a very good opportunity. After confirming each other's intentions for the past few days, only the signature remained.</p>
<p>It was a good opportunity to receive hundreds of billions and get rid of ISIS, which was slowly going bankrupt. However, Choi Gyu-hyeon, who was a conscientious manager rare in this era, couldn't help but worry about the employees who would be left behind.</p>
<p>'I should negotiate in this direction, even if I receive a little less.'</p>
<p>Choi Gyu-hyeon looked at the clock with a firm expression. It was almost time for the TG Tech negotiation team to arrive.</p>
<p>###</p>
<p>"Preparation complete, is everyone ready?"</p>
<p>Lara Sinclair looked around and asked, and several TG Tech researchers and the Vanguard security guards led by Han Yu-gyeong-ssi nodded as if they were all ready.</p>
<p>"The escort personnel will be able to receive their equipment after landing at the North Chinese airport."</p>
<p>No matter how much of an escort it was, they couldn't board the plane with combat equipment, so they had planned to obtain the equipment locally after arriving in North China. Since TG Tech had not yet entered North China, they decided to receive firearms through underground channels.</p>
<p>"As the head of the escort team, I would like to say that even though it is not the case now, a few years ago, it was a hostile country where we pointed guns at each other, so please be careful in your actions."</p>
<p>As Lara Sinclair finished speaking, the man in charge of the escort team said. North China had lost much of its past power after the defeat in World War III, but it was still a powerful country.</p>
<p>If you got caught causing an accident in North China, there was nothing South Korea could say, so it was best to be careful on your own.</p>
<p>The public security officers' habit of doing as they pleased was still there, so Lara Sinclair's group, heading to North China to steal technology, had to be careful at all times. They had to be careful at all times because they didn't know how they would be caught.</p>
<p>Perhaps that's why Lara Sinclair's group, including the escort personnel, were all dressed as tourists. They had even thoroughly prepared a North Chinese disguised guide belonging to the underground organization in North China.</p>
<p>"If this goes well, there will be a considerable bonus. I'm thinking of paying about 200 percent of your salary, so please do your best."</p>
<p>At Lara Sinclair's words, not only the researchers but even the security guards with stiff expressions were agitated. 200 percent of their salary, such a bonus for a mission that lasted less than a week... Everyone realized how important this was.</p>
<p>"Well then, shall we go to the plane?"</p>
<p>Lara Sinclair took the lead with the plane tickets and passports. The others hurried to follow Lara Sinclair.</p>
<p>###</p>
<p>I took a limousine with Han Yu-gyeong-ssi and headed to the place I had promised with the president of ISIS.</p>
<p>ISIS was a fairly promising mid-sized company before the war. With its unique cooling technology and cryopreservation packages that freeze patients with incurable diseases until they can be treated... ISIS became quite a hot topic when it appeared.</p>
<p>-'ISIS' has the cryopreservation technology that I only saw in movies. It announced that it will apply a package that freezes the first 10 customers for 200 years for a certain amount.</p>
<p>...Lee ■-shin-ssi, who will receive ISIS's 200-year package, said, "I hope a cure will be developed when I wake up," revealing his feelings just before being frozen.</p>
<p>I looked at ISIS's data among the prepared materials. It was an article from the 2020s before the war. As I read the article, I became curious. Is this person named Lee ■-shin-ssi still frozen, or did he wake up and receive treatment?</p>
<p>ISIS had received attention from the beginning in this way, but as the war broke out, all attention shifted to the war situation, and after the war, due to the highly developed medical technology and cyber implants, the cryopreservation technology that ISIS was proud of was treated as old technology.</p>
<p>For example, if an incurable disease occurred in a certain internal organ, the entire organ could be replaced with an artificial organ, which greatly reduced the number of incurable diseases that required freezing.</p>
<p>Of course, cooling technology was quite useful, so there were traces of trying to expand the business in various ways, but the large corporations had a firm grip on this area, making it very difficult to break in.</p>
<p>Currently, they were making profits in a few small markets that they had successfully entered, but the popularity of the main cryopreservation package had disappeared, and the deficit was accumulating.</p>
<p>"How much will Taron pay for ISIS if we acquire it?"</p>
<p>"Since Taron is receiving a lot of investment in the Mars industry these days and has a lot of cash, I think we can probably get twice as much."</p>
<p>"They'll give us twice as much?"</p>
<p>"My aunt-in-law said it that way before, so they'll probably do it."</p>
<p>Of course, it might just be words, but since Nae Fitzgerald-ssi is the negotiator on Taron's side, there was a high possibility that she would negotiate in my favor.</p>
<p>"Let's think about selling ISIS after we acquire it first."</p>
<p>Of course, we didn't intend to just hand ISIS over to Taron and only see financial benefits without doing anything. We were going to check what Taron was looking at and why they thought it was a cooling technology that perfectly matched their engine technology, and if there was any useful technology for us, we were going to take that data.</p>
<p>"We have arrived, President, Mr. Jin."</p>
<p>"Thank you for the ride, Dickson."</p>
<p>"Since Yong-tae is in Korea, reporters and intelligence agents may have been attached. The role of the security team is important, so please do your best until the end."</p>
<p>Han Yu-gyeong-ssi and I got out of the limousine and headed to the ISIS headquarters office where we had made an appointment. ISIS was not a very large company, so unlike us, who used an entire building, they only used about half of one floor as an office.</p>
<p>"Hello, I am Choi Gyu-hyeon, the president of ISIS."</p>
<p>When we entered the office, Choi Gyu-hyeon, the president of ISIS, greeted us. Choi Gyu-hyeon was a middle-aged man with glasses and half of his hair was gone, but I didn't know what he was like inside, but his appearance was quite like a kind-hearted uncle.</p>
<p>"I have already reviewed the data in advance. The price we are offering is this much..."</p>
<p>We handed over a document. The contents of the document stated that we would pay 45 billion won in exchange for buying Choi Gyu-hyeon's entire stake in ISIS.</p>
<p>ISIS was not a listed corporation, but the president, Choi Gyu-hyeon, had divided some of his shares among the researchers and long-term employees, so he did not own 100 percent of ISIS's shares. I knew he owned about 74 percent. Of course, to obtain absolute management control, you only needed more than 50 percent of the shares, so we only needed to purchase the shares held by Choi Gyu-hyeon.</p>
<p>"There are two conditions."</p>
<p>President Choi Gyu-hyeon demanded conditions from us with a determined expression. We gave him quite a generous amount of money, but he wanted more, wasn't he too greedy? I was slightly displeased, but I decided to talk to him first.</p>
<p>"Conditions? The 45 billion we offered is already very generous."</p>
<p>"I know that. It's okay to reduce the money I receive, so I would appreciate it if you could listen to the conditions."</p>
<p>What could be the conditions that would make him reduce the money offered to him?</p>
<p>"Let's hear it first."</p>
<p>"First, please guarantee the employment of the employees."</p>
<p>Ah, was that it? It wasn't a condition he set because of his own greed.</p>
<p>If that's the case, it's simple. Since Taron said they would use ISIS's researchers, there would be almost no one to cut. The other businesses were making profits, so there was no need to cut them either.</p>
<p>"It will be impossible to guarantee everyone, but we will ensure that most of the employment is guaranteed while ISIS is under TG Tech."</p>
<p>Choi Gyu-hyeon was surprised that I accepted one of the conditions too easily. In fact, ISIS wouldn't be under TG Tech for very long, so it was a very temporary promise. I was also thinking of setting conditions for Taron to save Choi Gyu-hyeon's face.</p>
<p>"And you must take responsibility for the people who are currently frozen."</p>
<p>Ah, it was worth setting that as a condition too. It would be a big problem if they trusted ISIS and were frozen, but the company that acquired them ignored them. This was a condition that could be accepted because it could later become a legal issue. I nodded and looked at Choi Gyu-hyeon.</p>
<p>"We will definitely take responsibility for that part, even if we discontinue the cryopreservation business after the acquisition."</p>
<p>"T, thank you...!"</p>
<p>"Then... We will reflect the conditions and give you the same amount."</p>
<p>I didn't reduce the money because I was planning to get more than that from Taron no matter how much we bought ISIS for. Choi Gyu-hyeon looked very touched by my words.</p>
<p>He was a rare manager who cared about his employees. But it was also not suitable for the current era. In today's world, it was difficult to survive without mega-corp sensitivity. If we hadn't been there, he would have gradually withered away due to the continued deficit and gone bankrupt at some point.</p>
<p>"This is the contract that reflects the new conditions."</p>
<p>Since there were only two additional conditions, the new document was quickly prepared. I signed the document and handed it over, and Choi Gyu-hyeon had a complex expression with mixed feelings of relief and regret, and then he signed it.</p>
<p>"Thank you for the good deal."</p>
<p>"Yes, thank you for listening to the conditions."</p>
<p>We shook hands with Choi Gyu-hyeon, who had a complex expression of half relief and half regret, and we concluded the ISIS acquisition agreement. After this, there will be things like actually transferring money and receiving shares, but that will also be completed as quickly as possible.</p>
