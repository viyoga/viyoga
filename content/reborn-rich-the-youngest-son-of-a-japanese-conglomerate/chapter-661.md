<p>Standing before me was Kaiju Kakan, the Four Heavenly Kings, a massive man with red hair, towering at 2 meters. He wasn't just tall at 2 meters; his entire body was covered in tremendous muscles, making him look gigantic to anyone who saw him.</p>
<p>"You seem to have done quite a bit of physical training, Rinka's disciple."</p>
<p>Kaiju Kakan opened his mouth after scanning my muscles visible through the training uniform. Judging by the fact that he knew I was Fujigasaki Rinka's disciple, it seemed that Nakasone Rishin, who had been acting strangely, had leaked quite a bit of information to the Anya District.</p>
<p>"You're in pretty good shape too. But we're not just standing here admiring each other's muscles, are we?"</p>
<p>"Right, there's no need for further conversation."</p>
<p>Kaiju Kakan charged at me without another word. He led with his shoulder, like a body slam, and crashed straight into me.</p>
<p>Kwaang!</p>
<p>The dojo echoed with a thunderous sound, but I wasn't pushed back much. Usually, with this much of a size difference, I would have been thrown back as if hit by a truck, but I put as much strength as possible into my feet to resist being pushed back. The dojo's floor was quite dented, but let's just move on since it can be repaired later.</p>
<p>Huoong! Huoong!</p>
<p>We hit each other almost simultaneously. I felt a heavy pain, as if hit by a large blunt weapon. Of course, Kaiju Kakan, who was hit by my fist, also seemed surprised by the power, as I felt him falter.</p>
<p>That hesitation was brief. We continued a haphazard fight, one-touch style, hitting and getting hit. At first, it was just punching, but soon, destructive techniques imbued with the Abyss began to destroy each other's bodies.</p>
<p>Even if we destroyed each other's bodies by attacking, my resistance stat, which governs defense, had broken through its limit, and I also had regeneration, so I could keep recovering. On the other hand, Kaiju Kakan defended himself with something like a shield of the Abyss. I don't know if it was a trait or a technique, but it looked quite useful.</p>
<p>Naturally, as the battle continued, the shield couldn't withstand my punches and shattered, and Kaiju Kakan's body was directly exposed to my attacks, bones breaking and muscles bursting.</p>
<p>In fact, it was natural for me to win. I had already fought against the final boss-level Famine and the avatar of an Outer God. No matter how notorious the Four Heavenly Kings of the Anya District were, I never thought I would lose fighting someone who wasn't even the number one in the Anya District.</p>
<p>I was easily gaining the upper hand with my regeneration, but I was impressed by Kaiju Kakan's mental strength. I could clearly feel his bones cracking with a "ppudeuk" sound in my fist, but he didn't care at all and kept hitting my body with his fists.</p>
<p>Still, something's a bit strange... If this continues, he'll definitely die in a few minutes, so why is he trying to fight so recklessly? Doesn't he have the option to dodge?</p>
<p>I quickly found out the reason for my question.</p>
<p>Kwaaaang!!</p>
<p>The counterattack after I landed a hit wasn't a fist but a tentacle. Before I knew it, one of Kaiju Kakan's dangling arms had become a tentacle, and the rest of his body was gradually transforming. Moreover, its power had increased, making it a powerful blow that jolted me awake.</p>
<p>When did he change like this? Impressive. It seems the side effects of the Abyss work like this, so I need to be careful too. I had received mental transformation side effects from Simyeonui ttal, but not physical side effects, so I had to be even more careful.</p>
<p>Seeing that, it seemed the side effects were quite significant because he was freely transforming Shinkai Yatsude.</p>
<p>The Shinkai Yatsude had been passed down for generations and had a somewhat optimized operation method to reduce side effects, so the higher the proficiency level, the fewer the side effects. But the Anya District didn't have that, so he was showing such a form.</p>
<p>"To think that a trained body would transform into such a hideous form... how unfortunate!"</p>
<p>-There is no need to feel sorry. I will do anything to win....</p>
<p>When I expressed my pity, Kaiju Kakan spoke in a bizarrely echoing voice. Even his voice had already been transformed. It seemed he had changed the inside before the outside, so I wouldn't notice.</p>
<p>While the battle was paused, I glanced around.</p>
<p>Looking at our classmates fighting the white-haired man, Kokubo Shiratsu, they were fighting well, using their numerical advantage. Shimokawabe Airen and Takakusaki Athena were fighting so dazzlingly and spectacularly that it was impossible to tell who had the upper hand.</p>
<p>"Which of the Four Heavenly Kings are you in terms of strength?"</p>
<p>I suddenly became curious and asked. If Kaiju Kakan had joined the special class students, it would have been quite dangerous.</p>
<p>His physical abilities were excellent, and his defense and attack power were good, so even without this transformation, it would have been quite a tough fight? Of course, all the students are not ordinary, so they would have eventually won.</p>
<p>-In terms of pure martial arts skill, I am the second strongest among the Four Heavenly Kings.</p>
<p>The fact that he specifically mentioned pure martial arts skill meant that Funayama Seigo, another of the Four Heavenly Kings who isn't here, is skilled in curses and spells, so if you include that, you can't tell how the fight would go.</p>
<p>Then that means the strongest of the Four Heavenly Kings is either Kokubo Shiratsu or Takakusaki Athena....</p>
<p>Judging by what I felt, that Kokubo Shiratsu seemed to be the weakest of the Four Heavenly Kings, so that meant the strongest of the Four Heavenly Kings was Takakusaki Athena. As expected of Takakusaki Gengo's daughter? Although she's not his biological daughter.</p>
<p>"Then I hope you'll continue to show me the martial arts of the second strongest of the Four Heavenly Kings."</p>
<p>Although he was using the method of exchanging punches recklessly, I was also thinking about how Anya District's martial arts differed from Shinkai Yatsude, and Kaiju Kakan's martial arts felt like a power-oriented, forceful style focused on strength and speed.</p>
<p>When I glanced at Kokubo Shiratsu earlier, he was using quick martial arts, so I could tell that Takakusaki Gengo, the leader of the Anya District, didn't pass on all of his martial arts to his disciples, the Four Heavenly Kings, or only passed on techniques that suited their styles.</p>
<p>-Experience the essence of Anya District's martial arts...!</p>
<p>I gave him some time, and before I knew it, the number of tentacles had increased to four. His arms had turned into tentacles, and two new tentacles were growing grotesquely from his back. His legs were still the same, so he had six limbs like an insect. It's a bit gross....</p>
<p>Sswaeeeeek!</p>
<p>As his arms turned into tentacles, his reach became incredibly long, and they moved freely as if they had no joints, making him very difficult to deal with.</p>
<p>"What essence of martial arts? You're just a monster!"</p>
<p>I shouted at Kaiju Kakan, who was wrapping his tentacles around my body to break all my bones, and swung my fist.</p>
<p>Deep Sea Eight Hands Touching the Abyss, Piercing the Void.</p>
<p>Thinking of dealing with tentacles with tentacles, I used a technique I saw in a dream I had after visiting the Naruse main family.</p>
<p>A giant tentacle made of the Abyss emerged and began to pierce everything in its path.</p>
<p>-There is no such technique in Shinkai Yatsude...!</p>
<p>Kaiju Kakan, with a hole blown through his chest, said in confusion. In fact, someone like Anya District's Four Heavenly Kings would know what techniques Shinkai Yatsude has.</p>
<p>Basically, Takakusaki Gengo, the founder of the Anya District, stole and learned Shinkai Yatsude.</p>
<p>"Yeah, it's the latest update~!"</p>
<p>Actually, it's not really Shinkai Yatsude, but more like a technique from a parallel world.... Kaiju Kakan wouldn't know that, so I just said it as it came out, as if teasing him.</p>
<p>I swung my fist again to aim Touch Abyss Piercing Void at his face, as he didn't seem to be thinking of dying even with a large hole in his chest from how his body had been transformed.</p>
<p>-W-wait a moment...!</p>
<p>"Sorry, but I don't have time to talk anymore. I have to go take care of my students."</p>
<p>Kwaaaaa!</p>
<p>I not only pierced Kaiju Kakan's face but completely obliterated his head. Still, it had increased in power each time I used it, even though I had only used it a few times.</p>
<p>Still, the power in the dream was enough to create holes in space. I was still far from that. I need to get stronger.</p>
<p>After turning Kaiju Kakan into a headless octopus, I first checked on the special class students' battle. They were unilaterally beating Kokubo Shiratsu to a pulp, as if running in a group with a "Waaah~!" in a manga, raising dust.</p>
<p>Sakagami Rena, who had been waiting for an opportunity, used a water-attribute water escape, and Alik swung a sword made of lightning, frying him to death with electricity all over his body. As I expected, Kokubo Shiratsu seemed to be the weakest of the Four Heavenly Kings.</p>
<p>This time, I turned my head to check on the fight between Shimokawabe Airen and Takakusaki Athena, and I saw quite a surprising scene.</p>
<p>Takakusaki Athena was fighting Shimokawabe Airen, whom even I would have difficulty defeating, on equal terms.</p>
<p>Of course, this was a disadvantageous fight for Shimokawabe Airen, a swordsman, who was holding a mop handle in her hand. But a skilled person like Shimokawabe Airen could use even a twig in her hand like a Famous sword.</p>
<p>Unexpectedly, Takakusaki Athena didn't seem to be using the Abyss. If the opponent was using the Abyss, I would feel something resonating, but I didn't feel anything like that at all. Rather, every time the white energy that Takakusaki Athena was using flashed, my chest churned as if I was getting motion sickness.</p>
<p>Could it be... that Takakusaki Gengo created a counter-martial art to capture Fujigasaki Rinka...?</p>
<p>"Uweck...!"</p>
<p>I only watched Shimokawabe Airen and Takakusaki Athena fight, but blood rose from my stomach. The two suddenly stopped fighting at the sound of me vomiting blood, and the special class students approached me, asking if I was okay, sensei?</p>
<p>"Ah, am I the only one left? I'm sorry, but I'll be going first."</p>
<p>Takakusaki Athena, who seemed to have had no time to assess the situation because the fight with Shimokawabe Airen was so intense, smiled slyly as if she had just grasped the situation, and flew out of the dojo like a flash of light and disappeared.</p>
<p>"It's okay. First... let's go to where Old Man Yoda is. It'll be a headache if that woman joins him."</p>
<p>I stopped the special class students who were asking if I was okay and ran out of the dojo with them.</p>
