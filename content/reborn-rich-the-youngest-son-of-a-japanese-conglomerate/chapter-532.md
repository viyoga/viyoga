<p>-Ready.... BEEP!</p>
<p>With the starting signal, Minamoto Risa and I began running as we had practiced. Most others hadn't practiced much, so they were out of sync from the start, quickly leaving behind not only the female teachers' group 5 meters ahead but also the male teachers' group that started with us.</p>
<p>However, Shimokawabe Airen's team was indeed fast. But perhaps due to the limitations of being tied to a female student, the distance between us didn't increase. The initial 5-meter gap remained. If so, the speeds were neck and neck. If we could speed up, we could definitely close that 5-meter gap.</p>
<p>"Minamoto, don't be surprised. And relax."</p>
<p>I whispered to Minamoto Risa as I ran. When Minamoto Risa nodded, I increased my speed and activated my telekinesis.</p>
<p>I was assisting Minamoto Risa's tied legs with telekinesis, matching her to my speed. I felt Minamoto Risa flinch, but she didn't show any surprise and entrusted her legs to my telekinesis. As I ran, increasing speed, I saw the distance between us and Shimokawabe Airen gradually closing.</p>
<p>Shimokawabe Airen glanced at me and also started to speed up, but since she didn't have psychic powers like me, she faltered a bit, which actually slowed her down. So, we were instantly on the same line, and at that time, the remaining distance to the finish line was not much.</p>
<p>Unlike the other groups, who were moving only slightly faster than walking, our group and Shimokawabe Airen's group were running almost at full speed, which seemed to surprise everyone.</p>
<p>Just as I was about to overtake Shimokawabe Airen by speeding up even more, Shimokawabe Airen somehow increased her speed even further.</p>
<p>And we crossed the finish line almost simultaneously. Ah, at this rate, aren't I really going to get fired? I looked at the panting Minamoto Risa.</p>
<p>"Are you okay?"</p>
<p>"Haa, haa, haa.... I'm a little out of breath, but I'm okay."</p>
<p>It was natural to be out of breath since we ran much faster than we usually practiced. The panting sounded a bit sexy, but my mind was entirely focused on the race results.</p>
<p>The large screen in the stadium was showing a very slow replay of which team, ours or Shimokawabe Airen's, had reached the finish line first. It looked almost the same.... After showing it several times, it was clear that Minamoto Risa and I had arrived slightly later by a hair's breadth.</p>
<p>Ah.... Am I getting fired now? As I made a slightly flustered expression, Minamoto Risa quickly opened her mouth.</p>
<p>"I'll somehow plead with my father, so don't worry. I'll make sure you don't get fired."</p>
<p>It was nice of her to plead for me, but I doubted Minamoto Ushin would sacrifice his reputation. And I thought he would want to investigate the man his daughter was begging for even more.... Is this being in a desperate situation?</p>
<p>While I was flustered by the result, all the runners soon crossed the finish line, and an announcement was heard.</p>
<p>-1st Grade Class D has violated Article 3 of the three-legged race rules and will be disqualified.</p>
<p>I don't know where 1st Grade Class D is, but what rule did they break? As I was thinking this, cheers erupted from where our special class was. And suddenly, Minamoto Risa hugged me, and when I looked at her, she was looking at me with a touched expression.</p>
<p>"We won!"</p>
<p>Could it be.... Was 1st Grade Class D Shimokawabe Airen's class? But what rule did they break to be disqualified? As if to answer my question, the screen showed why 1st Grade Class D was disqualified.</p>
<p>From the point where Shimokawabe Airen suddenly made a last-minute spurt, she was running while carrying the student. That's why she was so fast.</p>
<p>I also thought about doing that at first, but after hearing it was prohibited, I concentrated on practicing with Minamoto Risa. Shimokawabe Airen didn't seem to know that rule and looked bewildered. In fact, using telekinesis was no different from carrying a student, but telekinesis wasn't prohibited in the rules, and there was no one who could recognize it, so there was no chance of getting caught and disqualified.</p>
<p>Minamoto Risa and I, who won the three-legged race, went up to the podium, received our medals, and came down. Shimokawabe Airen, who had been disqualified, was waiting below the podium.</p>
<p>"Congratulations, Yamashiro-sensei."</p>
<p>"Thank you. But how did you end up participating when you're not even a homeroom teacher?"</p>
<p>After receiving Shimokawabe Airen's congratulations, I asked what I was curious about. I was curious how Shimokawabe Airen, who was a home economics teacher and not a homeroom teacher, ended up participating in the three-legged race.</p>
<p>"Ah.... The homeroom teacher of 1st Grade Class D is elderly. So, I ended up coming out as a substitute."</p>
<p>I see. Since the elderly often get seriously injured when they fall, it seemed Shimokawabe Airen was running instead.</p>
<p>"I came out in a hurry, so I wasn't familiar with the rules. I'm sorry for making a spectacle of myself."</p>
<p>"Ahaha.... It's okay. We practiced hard in our own way, but I was just surprised at how fast you were."</p>
<p>As I was talking with Shimokawabe Airen for a moment, Minamoto Risa tugged at my sleeve. Ah...! We were still tied together by the ankles.</p>
<p>"I'm sorry. Does your leg hurt or anything?"</p>
<p>I asked Minamoto Risa as I untied the rope that bound our ankles together. Minamoto Risa still shook her head with a flushed expression. She might be okay now, but she would suffer from muscle pain later when she got home. I had made her run faster than usual with telekinesis.</p>
<p>"Can you tell me later?"</p>
<p>"...Okay. It's a secret from the other kids."</p>
<p>It was about telekinesis. The homeroom teacher suddenly moved his legs with telekinesis, so she must have a lot of questions. I decided to postpone this troublesome topic until after the sports day, and I returned to the special class area with Minamoto Risa.</p>
<p>###</p>
<p>A few minutes ago.</p>
<p>Minamoto Ushin was yelling at the top of his lungs, despite the guffaws of Taira Danjo watching from the side. It was because he was furious at the special class's homeroom teacher, who was holding Minamoto Risa's shoulder.</p>
<p>"No! Touching my daughter's shoulder? How dare he, the shooooulder!!!! I'll get rid of that bastard! I'll get rid of him!! Daigo Academy Director! What is the meaning of this!"</p>
<p>His tantrum looked like a typical (?) drunken old man causing trouble, but when that trouble was caused by someone with the name tag Minamoto Ushin, the reaction from those around him was different. Everyone was afraid of getting caught in the crossfire, so they couldn't say anything.</p>
<p>Taira Danjo, who could have stopped him, was just chuckling and laughing at the sight, and Kujo Kazuo was busy filming his younger sister, Kujo Kina, with a home video camera. Seizawa Eiji had lunch with Seizawa Shii and had to return to Tokyo for urgent business, so he wasn't there.</p>
<p>So, no one could stop Minamoto Ushin from yelling at the top of his lungs. Daigo Terue of St. Francisco Girls' Academy was also at a loss and didn't know what to do.</p>
<p>"Father."</p>
<p>It was then. Minamoto Risa had come to the parents' seating area. Minamoto Risa was looking at her father, who was making a scene, with clear eyes.</p>
<p>"Risa, what was that just now? Why is that blonde delinquent touching your shoulder, that's what I'm saying!"</p>
<p>"At first, I practiced without any contact. But I kept falling, so the teacher had no choice but to hold my shoulder to keep me from falling. Do you want me to get hurt, Father?"</p>
<p>Minamoto Ushin closed his mouth for a moment at Minamoto Risa's words. He would become a cold-hearted father who wanted his daughter to get hurt if the teacher didn't hold her shoulder, and Minamoto Ushin didn't want to see that blonde bastard touching his precious daughter's shoulder.</p>
<p>"Hmph...! Fine. I'll allow it. But if your team doesn't win, I'll use whatever means necessary to get that teacher fired."</p>
<p>"Okay. Then please watch me win."</p>
<p>Minamoto Risa accepted Minamoto Ushin's threatening remarks without flinching. She immediately turned around and headed back to the stadium. Taira Danjo burst into a hearty laugh at the sight.</p>
<p>"Your daughter is very assertive. Good quality."</p>
<p>"Hmph! There's nothing to gain even if you praise her."</p>
<p>"But firing the teacher? You're still doing things that will damage your reputation."</p>
<p>"The time for you and me to worry about our reputation has passed."</p>
<p>Taira Danjo hinted that firing the teacher was excessive, but Minamoto Ushin said it as if he didn't care about his reputation at all. It was like the commotion Minamoto Ushin had just caused. No matter what the people of the prestigious families around him thought, Minamoto Ushin freely expressed his emotions. It was a show of power. No one could stop him, no matter what others thought, so he could show off Minamoto's strength and instill a sense of intimidation in the people around him.</p>
<p>While Taira Danjo and Minamoto Ushin were talking, everyone started running across the stadium with the starting signal.</p>
<p>"Oh! They're taking the lead. At this rate, it will be difficult to fire the teacher?"</p>
<p>"Ahem...!"</p>
<p>Minamoto Risa's team took the lead from the start. Whether Minamoto Risa's words about practicing were true or not, they were much faster than the other teams, who were struggling because they weren't in sync.</p>
<p>Minamoto Risa's team maintained the distance with the leading Shimokawabe Airen's team and then sped up to catch up. Still, perhaps because his daughter was running, Minamoto Ushin was unknowingly cheering for his daughter.</p>
<p>Of course, Taira Danjo, who was watching his grandson run, was also cheering for Minamoto Risa's team without showing it.</p>
<p>"Run! Run! Crush your father's high nose!"</p>
<p>"Quiet. You should cheer while worrying about your reputation and those around you."</p>
<p>Minamoto Ushin felt strange when Taira Danjo cheered for Minamoto Risa, who was on the same team, instead of cheering for his grandson. He felt like he was missing something. But before he could think about what that feeling was, the two teams that were competing for the lead crossed the finish line almost simultaneously.</p>
<p>"Wh, who's first?"</p>
<p>"They arrived almost at the same time, so they're doing a camera review."</p>
<p>Minamoto Ushin asked Daigo Terue. Daigo Terue seemed to be talking to the organizing team and then answered Minamoto Ushin's question. The screen was showing a slow-motion replay of the two teams crossing the finish line.</p>
<p>"Ah...! They lost."</p>
<p>Minamoto Ushin unknowingly made a regretful sound. His desire to cheer for his daughter seemed to have grown even more.</p>
<p>Taira Danjo's mind was complicated. He was weighing whether it would be more beneficial to prevent Minamoto Ushin from firing Taira Ryuta or to just let it go when Daigo Terue approached with an excited expression.</p>
<p>"Congratulations. The team that came in first committed a foul and was disqualified. Your daughter won."</p>
<p>"Really?"</p>
<p>Minamoto Ushin's face lit up at the news that his daughter had won. Taira Danjo, who was sitting next to him, also felt better, thinking that he didn't need to put in the effort to protect Taira Ryuta. Soon, an announcement was made that the first-place team had been disqualified, and Minamoto Risa was seen excitedly hugging Taira Ryuta. Minamoto Ushin gritted his teeth again at the sight.</p>
<p>"I still have to fire that teacher."</p>
<p>"If you fire him, your daughter will hate you? She's just happy about the victory, so why don't you let it go instead of acting like a petty old man?"</p>
<p>"Hmph.... I have no choice."</p>
<p>Minamoto Ushin decided to let this go because he also knew the joy of victory. Taira Danjo was subtly siding with that man, but Minamoto Ushin didn't recognize it because of the anger he felt at seeing Minamoto Risa's happy face and his daughter being hugged by another man.</p>
