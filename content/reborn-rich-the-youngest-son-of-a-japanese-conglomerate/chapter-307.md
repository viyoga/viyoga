<p>A week passed quickly. There was class preparation and a lot of other things to prepare. Surprisingly, I was busy even before the semester started.</p>
<p>For a week, I kept going to the school without students every day. It was located in the special class building far from the building with the faculty office, so I wasn't in the faculty office, but as the homeroom teacher of the 3rd-year special class, I greeted the teachers in the faculty office.</p>
<p>I didn't know when I would get involved with teachers of other subjects, so making acquaintances was quite important. Since we could help each other, I, a blonde tanned guy, bought the favor of those around me with the image of a kind new teacher.</p>
<p>In addition, I had to grasp the geography of St. Francisco Girls' Academy and the location of major structures such as CCTV. I have to be careful because there shouldn't be CCTV when I'm doing stupid things.</p>
<p>What was a little unusual was that there weren't many CCTVs in the school. There were no CCTVs in the gym storage where it's good to do bad things related to physical education classes or behind the secluded school. It felt like they weren't going to record even if bad things happened, so I became quite scared of this school. From the perspective of a prestigious family, whether they are the perpetrator or the victim, both were shameful, so they seemed reluctant to leave data.</p>
<p>I heard from some teachers that when bullying incidents occur, the families resolve it among themselves. When I asked, "Then wouldn't it be very difficult if the victim's family is inferior?" they said that in such situations, the perpetrator's side usually just pays for treatment and compensation and continues to attend school. If they don't even want to do that, the Daigo family, a family of St. Fitzgerald Girls' Academy, says something, so they seem to make at least minimal compensation.</p>
<p>,By the way, why aren't you giving me the student roster? I need to know who's there in advance."</p>
<p>I grumbled while packing my bags. In fact, most of the special classes are for cases like Minamoto Lisa, who failed to graduate and was held back, or those who are not in good health, or those who cannot do well in school life because they are busy.</p>
<p>Of course, from the perspective of a prestigious family, they wouldn't want to publicize that their daughter attends a special class as much as possible. It could be considered a disgrace to the family. They don't want to give me information because I'm not officially a homeroom teacher yet before the semester starts.</p>
<p>Since there are still many prestigious families with such old-fashioned ideas, St. Francisco Girls' Academy said they would give me the roster when the semester starts, so I didn't know how many students were in the special class or who they were.</p>
<p>But since today is the opening day, I'll be able to find out when I go to school.</p>
<p>I came out wearing casual clothes that were not exactly a suit that a school teacher would wear, but were still somewhat formal. I even wore glasses without lenses, so even someone who saw me in a photo wouldn't be able to recognize me.</p>
<p>The school attendance time for St. Francisco Girls' Academy is until 8:30 AM. Teachers who need to prepare for class had to come by 7:30 AM. For me, who used to go to work around 8:55 AM while working at the Human Resources Development Institute, going to work early in the morning became quite difficult.</p>
<p>I live 3 minutes away from St. Francisco Girls' Academy on foot, so I'll come a little leisurely, but more than half of the teachers lived quite far away.</p>
<p>Because the prestigious families bought houses near the school so that their daughters could commute comfortably, the house prices rose considerably, so teachers could not afford to buy houses with their salaries. Occasionally, there were families who built huge mansions at a reasonable distance and commuted by limousine, but most students preferred to be close.</p>
<p>Other teachers from prestigious families who came in with recommendations like me seem to live close to the school, but there were more ordinary teachers who didn't have the money to live close to the school.</p>
<p>,Good morning, Amashiro-ssi."</p>
<p>,Good morning, Kozaki-ssi."</p>
<p>The guard greeted me at the entrance, so I greeted him happily. The guard was a young man named Kozaki Koji, and I had become a little close to him while going in and out of the school for the past week. I became friends with him by talking in the school where there were no peers in their 20s.</p>
<p>I have my own mission, so it's not bad to be friends with the guard. After talking a few times, I found out that Kozaki Koji was a guard dispatched from Fuji Military, a representative PMC in Shizuoka Prefecture where Miyashi is located.</p>
<p>I heard that Kozaki Koji and a few guards take turns working day and night shifts, but I have only seen Kozaki Koji so far. Soon the rotation will turn and I'll be able to see other people.</p>
<p>I didn't go to the central building with the faculty office and went straight to the quiet building with the special class. Perhaps because of the students' health problems, there was a forest behind it, and there was a mountain behind the forest, but I had an ominous feeling that there would be many mosquitoes.</p>
<p>Now that I have a body like mine, mosquitoes can't break through my skin. Still, I didn't like my students getting bitten by mosquitoes. They are students I haven't even seen yet, but I have to have the teacher mindset.</p>
<p>I swiped my teacher ID on the education preparation room and went inside. When I checked the fax, a paper with today's schedule and some meeting details was printed.</p>
<p>The school itself is large, so it seems that they send faxes like this to other buildings far away. Fax, it was a very Japanese way of handling things. They could have sent it by email or Line.</p>
<p>When I read the schedule, there was nothing special because it was the opening day. Listen to the principal's opening ceremony speech on TV in the classroom instead of the playground, check if the kids are properly downloading the textbook files they will learn during the semester on their tablets, introduce each other... And that was it. The schedule is simple.</p>
<p>I memorized today's schedule and drank the sandwich and coffee I brought for breakfast while looking at the meeting details. Um, those who want to join a club should join... But it's the last semester of 3rd grade, does the club have any meaning? If they are in 3rd grade, almost all of them would have already joined a club.</p>
<p>In fact, I heard that St. Francisco Girls' Academy can advance to St. Francisco Women's University in Tokyo unless there is a very big problem, so even if they become 3rd grade test takers, they are more relaxed than other schools, but still, they are in 3rd grade. If they want to enter a difficult school like Edodai, they will have to study hard.</p>
<p>The rest of the meeting details were nothing special. It's always the same talk about creating a learning atmosphere. I threw the sandwich wrapper in the trash can and came out of the education preparation room and went into the narrow men's restroom compared to the large women's restroom and brushed my teeth.</p>
<p>I understand that the men's restroom is small because it's a girls' school and there are many female teachers. But shouldn't they make at least two urinals? The men's restroom and washroom had one sink, one urinal, and one toilet. It seemed like a restroom made just for show.</p>
<p>I finished gargling and bumped into someone while coming out of the restroom.</p>
<p>,Kya!"</p>
<p>,Ah! I'm sorry."</p>
<p>When I grabbed her to keep her from falling, she wasn't a student, and she was wearing a white gown, so she seemed to be the school nurse. It's September now, but she wasn't hot, and she was wearing a sweater and a long skirt under her white gown.</p>
<p>By the way, she's quite a beauty. As befitting a school nurse(?), her breasts are also huge...</p>
<p>,Are you the special class nurse?"</p>
<p>,Yes, but who are you..."</p>
<p>Perhaps because a man she had never seen suddenly appeared, she was looking at me with a very wary expression.</p>
<p>,Ah, I'm Yamashiro Ryuta, who will be in charge of the special class from the second semester."</p>
<p>,I, I see. I'm Kijimoto Jurie."</p>
<p>Kijimoto Jurie... St. Francisco Girls' Academy hired her as a special nurse for the special class, so she must be quite skilled. It wouldn't be bad to get close to her.</p>
<p>,I look forward to working with you for the special class."</p>
<p>,Yes, I look forward to it too. I'm sorry for bumping into you just now."</p>
<p>,I didn't look around properly either. Instead, can I ask you questions about things I'm curious about since this is my first time at this school?"</p>
<p>,If it's that kind of thing, as much as you want..."</p>
<p>We apologized to each other, and I headed to the education preparation room, and Kijimoto Jurie headed to the nurse's office. If there's anything I want to ask, I can ask Kijimoto Jurie, who is nearby.</p>
<p>I was sitting in the education preparation room when I heard footsteps. It seemed like a few people were entering the classroom. It was still around 8 o'clock, so they seemed to be diligent students who came to school quite early.</p>
<p>A few people entered the classroom little by little, and at 8:30 AM, the school attendance time, I got up from my seat.</p>
<p>I opened the door of the education preparation room and opened the closed door of the classroom. Judging from the fact that there were a few empty seats, it seemed that not everyone had come to school. As expected, the special class has many troublemakers.</p>
<p>,Nice to meet you, I'm Yamashiro Ryuta, who became the homeroom teacher of the special class from the second semester."</p>
<p>I stood at the podium and introduced myself to everyone. When I glanced at the podium, there was a student roster and attendance book.</p>
<p>I scanned the students once. But... there were two familiar faces.</p>
<p>Konoe Akina, the owner of the Evil Eye and the head of the Konoe family, with black hair and red Evil Eyes, was looking at me with a surprised expression, as if she recognized me. As expected, for those who already know me, it seems that I can't hide my identity with this much disguise.</p>
<p>And Otagi Yuka, a vampire with red hair and golden eyes, also recognized me and was sitting with a smirk. Why are you smiling? It's scary.</p>
<p>When I think about it, both of them are daughters of prestigious families and 20-year-old high school students, so there was enough probability that they would come to the special class of St. Francisco Girls' Academy. It was something I hadn't even thought of, but I had to figure out how to handle it.</p>
<p>Fortunately, judging from their reactions now, it didn't seem like they would reveal my identity right away.</p>
