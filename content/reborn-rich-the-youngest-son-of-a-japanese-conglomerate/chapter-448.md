<p>"If it's a serial killer, there should be a killing cycle. What's the duration of that cycle?"</p>
<p>Arisu, seemingly knowledgeable about serial killers, asked Diana Ritchie about the killing cycle. Serial killers usually have a consistent pattern, so she seemed to be checking if the weekend I'm here falls within that cycle.</p>
<p>"Initially, it was about a month, but the cycle has been shortening, and now they're killing every 2-3 days. It's clear that something will happen this weekend."</p>
<p>Killing every 2-3 days? That's a really short cycle. Shouldn't the U.S. Federal Paranormal Management Bureau be able to prepare and catch them? The location is limited to Hollywood, and the killing cycle is short, so it should be easy to catch them by setting up an ambush....</p>
<p>Ah...! There was a reason they're assigning me this mission. This serial killer is too strong for the U.S. Federal Paranormal Management Bureau agents to hunt, so they need external help. They've probably faced them a few times, but the U.S. Federal Paranormal Management Bureau alone couldn't handle it.</p>
<p>"How strong are they?"</p>
<p>"Huh?"</p>
<p>"The Thousand Mouths. If I have to help, they must be pretty strong."</p>
<p>Diana Ritchie looked at me with a 'How did you notice?' expression, then opened her mouth with a slightly gloomy look.</p>
<p>"...To be honest, we don't know either. All the dispatched agents died. Once it was two lower-level agents, but the second time, a whole squad of eight mid-level agents was wiped out. Strangely, all the filming equipment malfunctioned too."</p>
<p>U.S. Federal Paranormal Management Bureau agents are supposed to be quite strong, but a whole squad died.... Since there are no witnesses, I don't know how strong they are. Filming equipment doesn't work, and psychometry is useless, so there's no way to know anything about the opponent.</p>
<p>"Then it's super dangerous?! What if our Ryuta gets hurt?"</p>
<p>Arisu, after hearing Diana Ritchie's story, put her hands on her hips and started protesting. I can regenerate even if I get hurt, but it's better not to get hurt at all.</p>
<p>"But it'll be okay this time. Taira-ssi is here, and Mr. Murdoch here is an excellent agent.... There are also nearly thirty agents disguised as studio staff infiltrating the set."</p>
<p>"Still...!"</p>
<p>"It's okay. If we leave this killer alone, Arisu could be in danger too, so it's right for me to participate."</p>
<p>I stopped Arisu, who was still hesitant despite Diana Ritchie's explanation. When I said I'd take on the serial killer hunt for Arisu's sake, she seemed touched, with hearts in her eyes.</p>
<p>"Since it's come to this, I can't back down, so I'll help Ryuta too. What's the reward for the mission?"</p>
<p>At first, I thought she meant helping with the battle, but after listening more, it seemed like she meant helping with reward negotiations and such.</p>
<p>Arisu and Diana Ritchie talked for a while, and after finding common ground, they shook hands and started telling me the agreed terms to report(?).</p>
<p>"The reward is one million dollars just for accepting, an additional million if a battle breaks out, two million if Ryuta kills the killer, and three million if he captures them alive."</p>
<p>Arisu negotiated a contract to receive up to five million dollars. It's just for the weekend, but it's a pretty high-paying part-time job. Capturing alive pays the most, so are they planning some kind of experiment? Or....</p>
<p>"Ah! We're here...!"</p>
<p>Arisu shouted, looking out the window. Outside the window was a large, square-shaped building. They spent quite a bit of time negotiating, so it seems we've arrived at the filming studio.</p>
<p>"Rest comfortably. I'll contact you if something happens."</p>
<p>"Please send the additional data to my email right away."</p>
<p>"Yes, don't worry."</p>
<p>Diana Ritchie didn't get out, as the SUV we were in seemed to be acting as a command and control center, and she saw us off. When getting out, I glanced at the screen inside the SUV, and it seemed to be monitoring all the surrounding CCTVs.</p>
<p>Next to us, Arisu's sports car was parked, and an agent who had driven the sports car was waiting with the car keys.</p>
<p>"Thank you."</p>
<p>We received the car keys from the agent and headed to the filming studio.</p>
<p>"Actually, there's almost no outdoor shooting anymore. Mostly VR recordings...."</p>
<p>According to Arisu, movies and dramas these days are more like recordings than filming.</p>
<p>Most filming now takes place inside VR rather than on location, so you can edit with all the scenes without a camera, and post-processing is very easy, so most movies and videos are made with VR recordings.</p>
<p>Some older, master directors continue the tradition of on-location shooting, but the environment has changed so much that even those directors are coming to VR.</p>
<p>There was also a discussion about using AI actors instead of human actors, but after the war, restrictions on AI became severe, and that discussion disappeared. The actors, who were in danger of losing their jobs, were relieved that the war had happened.</p>
<p>"This is the VR room."</p>
<p>There were small rooms attached side by side like coin karaoke rooms, and inside each room were VR connection machines with chairs.</p>
<p>The room wasn't very big (of course, not as cramped as a coin karaoke room), but as befitting a Hollywood studio, air purifiers were running constantly, humidity was adjusted to suit the actors, the latest safety devices were installed, and a doctor was stationed in case of emergencies.</p>
<p>"Nice facilities?"</p>
<p>"Yeah! It's pretty good."</p>
<p>Arisu, who introduced me to the VR room, dragged me along and introduced me to the editing room. The editing room was in a huge office, and many people were working on computers. Some were directly VR diving to edit, and others were checking how the edited screen looked.</p>
<p>"Since it's VR shooting, the editing team is the biggest."</p>
<p>"The editing team is almost the alpha and omega, except for the shooting."</p>
<p>Now that I think about it, there's no need for lighting teams, filming teams, or prop teams, so the editing team seems to be getting bigger.</p>
<p>After looking at the editing team and the sound team, we toured the rest of the set and finally headed to the director's office to meet the director.</p>
<p>"Hello, Director. This is my boyfriend, Yamashiro Ryuta!"</p>
<p>Arisu introduced me to the director, who had a full beard. If I said Taira Ryuta, it would seem strange since I'm Arisu's cousin, so I introduced myself as Yamashiro Ryuta.</p>
<p>"Oh! Nice to meet you! Ms. Fitzgerald's boyfriend! You have quite the visual!"</p>
<p>The director, named Mike Rogers, was a fairly famous director. He had made several big commercial films successful and gave Arisu, who had appeared as a minor role, a fairly significant supporting role in this film.</p>
<p>The director looked at me and made a square shape with his hands like Son Heung-min's goal celebration, putting me inside the square screen at various angles. Then, he nodded in satisfaction and spoke to me.</p>
<p>"Have you ever thought about becoming an actor?"</p>
<p>"No."</p>
<p>I already got scolded a lot by Taira Danjo-halbae for appearing in a music video, so a Hollywood actor.... It was impossible. Hollywood movies have a different level of impact than music videos, and if I appeared, Halbae might remove me from the family register.</p>
<p>"Aww, come on! Just one shot! Can't you do just one shot?"</p>
<p>"You want me to suddenly join the movie you're filming? I've never learned acting, so I'll ruin the movie. I'll only be here for the weekend, so I don't have time either."</p>
<p>I was a bit taken aback because the director suddenly started acting like a child. Even though I've gained some acting skills from lying and living as Yamashiro Ryuta, it's different from actual acting, so I couldn't do it. This is my acting debut, excluding minor roles, and I couldn't ruin it. I don't want to become a movie actor who gets a lot of attention from people.</p>
<p>"I won't do it."</p>
<p>"Is that so.... What a shame."</p>
<p>The director looked very disappointed. He tried to appeal to my sympathy with a look that made him seem ten years older, but I mercilessly ignored him. When the director got too clingy, Arisu, who was smiling with satisfaction because he was praising me so highly, said something.</p>
<p>"Even if Ryuta is handsome, how can you suddenly say something like that?"</p>
<p>"No, I saw the inner side of your boyfriend, not his appearance. He has a pretty face, but there's a beast hidden inside him. There are many handsome actors these days, but they don't have the wildness of your boyfriend...!"</p>
<p>The director seemed to have a good eye. Maybe if I looked with the glasses from the ruins, I could see emotion-related traits.</p>
<p>"Our Ryuta is a bit beast-like! But still, it can't be helped. We're going to eat."</p>
<p>"Even an extra! Even a passerby...!"</p>
<p>We left the director's office, hearing his desperate cries. Arisu smiled awkwardly, as if she was sorry.</p>
<p>"Hehe, sorry...! The director is a bit of an oddball, right?"</p>
<p>"It's okay. I'm grateful because he's praising me highly. But I won't act."</p>
<p>It was still early, but lunchtime was approaching, so we headed to the cafeteria. The cafeteria was also quite large, and the food was buffet-style and quite excellent. Arisu had to manage her figure, so she chose her food lightly, but I knew I would be burning a lot of calories spending time with Arisu today, so I put all the delicious-looking food on my plate.</p>
<p>As we sat down to eat, someone's voice was heard.</p>
<p>"Arisu!"</p>
<p>The one who called Arisu loudly was a very handsome man. He was a pretty famous actor.... Who was he again? I can't remember his name.</p>
