<p>Now, the last remaining Target of boksu for Sato Tsukiyo was Ogata Koji. The two people killed earlier were also malicious, but the same was true for Ogata Koji. At the request of his then-girlfriend, Miyano Yoshimi, he bullied Sato Tsukiyo, tormenting her with the Attitude that all of this was due to his girlfriend's request and he himself was not at fault.</p>
<p>Perhaps because he wasn't bullying her voluntarily, but rather setting up a defense mechanism of 'his girlfriend's request,' Ogata Koji didn't seem to feel any Conscience.</p>
<p>The intensity of the bullying rapidly grew stronger, until finally, in front of her Friends in class, he was cutting Sato Tsukiyo's Clothes with Scissors when he was caught by the student advisor and transferred.</p>
<p>In Sato Tsukiyo's Memory, it was the most shocking incident after the Orphanage Director, probably due to the Goosebumps she felt when the dirty desire pooled in Ogata Koji's Eyes and the cold blade of the Scissors brushed against her flesh.</p>
<p>Tsukiyo, who had taken Cash from the Wallets of the two women she had killed, left the Murder Scene as it was and walked down the stairs. Thanks to dealing with Fuke Michie, who had shown up unannounced, the Streets of Tokyo were already dyed red by the sunset.</p>
<p>"...Please take me here."</p>
<p>Tsukiyo, who had gotten into the Taxi, showed the Address written on her phone Screen and stated her Destination. And she looked Outside the window at the orange Tokyo Streets, bathed in the light of the setting sun.</p>
<p>By the time the orange Streets had turned navy, Tsukiyo arrived in front of Ogata Koji's Home. Tsukiyo paid the Taxi fare and got out.</p>
<p>And standing in front of the mansion where Ogata Koji lived, she held up her phone with a slightly eerie Expression.</p>
<p>Ogata Koji had been bothered ever since Miyano Yoshimi's Contact yesterday. In her Call with Miyano Yoshimi, she had said it couldn't be, but looking at the News article about the A-village Murder Incident and the faces of the samangja, she thought that Sato Tsukiyo might indeed be the culprit, just as Miyano Yoshimi had said.</p>
<p>So after the Call, she tried to Contact Miyano Yoshimi several more times, but Miyano Yoshimi didn't answer.</p>
<p>So when she contacted Fuke Michie, who had been forcibly transferred with her, Fuke Michie also received a missed Call from Miyano Yoshimi and said she would visit the next day, as she couldn't get in touch.</p>
<p>And today, the next day, Fuke Michie, who had left a message saying she was going to Miyano Yoshimi's Home, also lost Contact.</p>
<p>Ogata Koji felt afraid, but at the same time, she wondered if the current Situation might be a Prank by Miyano Yoshimi and Fuke Michie.</p>
<p>"Yes, they're both giggling and watching me be scared. Sato wouldn't really come to kill us, would she? That Sato...."</p>
<p>Ogata Koji smiled awkwardly and thought of this as a Prank by her two Friends. Ogata Koji knew that Sato Tsukiyo was by no means a Person who would commit Murder.</p>
<p>Sato Tsukiyo had been a pretty Child since childhood. But because she was an Orphan, she was a quiet Child, timid, and the Parents of the class Students told them not to associate with an Orphan, so she gradually became alienated.</p>
<p>Even when some Students started bullying her, Sato Tsukiyo still maintained a quiet Attitude without any defiance. And even when the bullying worsened, Sato Tsukiyo silently endured the bullying poured upon her until her third year of High School.</p>
<p>That Sato Tsukiyo killed eighteen people? And among the dead were several men of large Size. No matter how much Sato Tsukiyo's mind suddenly turned to kill, there were people who would be impossible for her to kill, so Ogata Koji couldn't believe it even when Miyano Yoshimi said it was Sato Tsukiyo's jit.</p>
<p>Uuung!</p>
<p>As she lay on the bed with such complex thoughts, the phone she had placed by her Head vibrated. Checking who it was, it was Miyano Yoshimi's LINE. Ogata Koji, thinking, 'She didn't answer all day, and now she's going to say it was a Prank?' checked the content of the LINE.</p>
<p>On the phone Screen, along with a Photo of Scissors, the Message "I'll cut you too." was written.</p>
<p>"It's just a Prank, but it's too much."</p>
<p>She spoke calmly, but Ogata Koji was very angry. There's a time and place for pranks, but playing a Prank with something like this? The angry Ogata Koji immediately pressed the Call Button and called Miyano Yoshimi.</p>
<p>"What are you doing? Why are you playing such a Prank? Am I a joke to you?! We're not even dating anymore, isn't this Prank a bit too much?!"</p>
<p>The Call waiting tone stopped, and as soon as the other party answered the phone, Ogata Koji poured out her anger. After unleashing her anger, she felt a little relieved, but when she heard the other party's Voice that followed, she felt Goosebumps as if she had been doused with Ice water.</p>

<p>Ogata Koji's Head felt like it was spinning with Confusion. But the Confusion was brief; Ogata Koji could only come to one Conclusion.</p>
<p>Sato Tsukiyo killed Miyano Yoshimi and is using Miyano Yoshimi's phone. Fuke Michie probably also fell victim to Sato Tsukiyo.</p>
<p>Ogata Koji immediately tried to call the police, but the phone displayed "Out of Service Area," making a Call impossible. It was an absurd Situation. How could "Out of Service Area" appear in the Middle of Tokyo?</p>
<p>Just in case, Ogata Koji called Miyano Yoshimi again. The Call tone rang, and it connected. Ogata Koji immediately hung up. It seemed like the only phone she could Call was Miyano Yoshimi's.</p>
<p>Ogata Koji bolted up from the bed. And she hurriedly opened the room door, ran, and began to lock the Front Door. After even putting on the door chain to prevent the Opponent from entering, she felt a little Sweet Relief.</p>
<p>"...!"</p>
<p>But that Sweet Relief vanished without a trace at the rustling, scraping sound coming from Behind the Back of Ogata Koji. The eerie sound of Scissors blades rubbing against each other was coming from Behind the Back.</p>
<p>Ogata Koji slowly turned her Head, dripping Sweat. Behind the Back, Sato Tsukiyo was holding the Scissors she had seen in the LINE Photo, smiling so wide her mouth seemed to rip.</p>
<p>"What? Miyano Yoshimi and Fuke Michie have already fallen victim?"</p>
<p>Seido Omina pressed her forehead, receiving the police Report via phone. Seido Omina hung up the phone and looked at her team members.</p>
<p>"I don't know if this is good or bad, but the phone Call just now reduced our Choices to one."</p>
