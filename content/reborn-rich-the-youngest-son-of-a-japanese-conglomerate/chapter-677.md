<p>I hurriedly changed into my martial arts uniform and went outside. A tremendous roar was heard nearby, causing confusion. Everyone was startled by the explosion and frantically stamping their feet in dismay.</p>
<p>"What's going on?"</p>
<p>"There was an explosion on the road passing in front of the dojo."</p>
<p>Sakagami Rena answered my question. If there was an explosion near the dojo, where there aren't many houses, Yoda-halbeoji's (Grandpa Yoda's) light truck might have broken down or there might have been an accident.</p>
<p>"Should I go check it out?"</p>
<p>As I spoke to Kujo Kina, Nakasone Rishin, who had been sitting in a formal posture, suddenly stood up.</p>
<p>I thought, 'Is he going to go himself since he's a disciple?' but the words that came out of Nakasone Rishin's mouth were unexpected.</p>
<p>"I cannot go. You... and I... must spar...?!"</p>
<p>"No, Yoda-yeonggam (Old Man Yoda) has to come anyway to spar, so I'll just go for a bit..."</p>
<p>I tried to explain to Nakasone Rishin, but stopped. His eyes were already rolled back, showing only the whites. And on his exposed skin, mottled patterns like octopus sucker marks were appearing. Probably those patterns were spreading all over his body.</p>
<p>"Sorcery...! It's Anya Order!"</p>
<p>I shouted. Nakasone Rishin's sudden change and the explosion sound, suspected to be Yoda-halbeoji (Grandpa Yoda)... There was only one place that would do such a thing.</p>
<p>Only the Anya Order would make Nakasone Rishin, who was kidnapped by them, like this. They probably have various curses and sorceries besides the Berserker. If I had known this would happen, I would have brought Urabe Konoka too...!</p>
<p>"Otagi! Go to where the explosion happened! Sakagami, contact Urabe and ask her to come!"</p>
<p>"Okay! Sensei-ssi, be careful! I'm suddenly sensing a huge presence!"</p>
<p>Otagi Yuka left me with a warning to be careful and turned into a blood-colored mist, exiting through the window. I was feeling it too. Along with Nakasone Rishin's change, I could sense quite a few people surrounding the dojo.</p>
<p>There are more than a few people here with sensitive senses, but the fact that we didn't notice them until they got this close probably means they also have a spell to conceal a large presence.</p>
<p>"Kujo, protect yourself and the disciples. It seems like the Anya Order has come to take you again."</p>
<p>Kujo Kina also had a history of being attacked by a Berserker, so she could be a target of the Anya Order. That's why I had to tell her to be careful.</p>
<p>"Naruse, help Kujo."</p>
<p>"I understand, Sensei. I won't let anyone lay a hand on her."</p>
<p>At my instruction, Naruse Shiyo clenched both fists tightly and replied as if to say, "Leave it to me." Although she doesn't have a weapon she's trained with so far, Naruse Shiyo has the Dragon Transformation ability, so her defense is top-tier here. She was perfect as a tank to protect Yoda-halbeoji's (Grandpa Yoda's) young disciples.</p>
<p>"Phones and even walkie-talkies don't work, Jugun (My Lord)!"</p>
<p>"Looks like they've installed a signal jammer. Sakagami, watch the entire battlefield and provide support based on your own judgment."</p>
<p>"Yes!"</p>
<p>After giving instructions to Sakagami Rena, I looked at the remaining three swordsmen. Shimokawabe Airen, Alik, and Saionji Diana didn't bring their swords, so their combat power would be reduced, which worried me a bit.</p>
<p>"Everyone doesn't have anything to use as a sword... so be careful."</p>
<p>"Sensei, I have an emergency sword."</p>
<p>Saionji Diana's left thigh clicked open, and she pulled out a sword. Oh... that's so cool? As I was admiring her for a moment, Shimokawabe Airen and Alik stepped forward.</p>
<p>"We'll use these, so don't worry."</p>
<p>"Ryuta, don't worry. Alik fights well with this too. Because Alik is Ryuta's maid..."</p>
<p>Shimokawabe Airen and Alik brought two mops that were standing in one corner of the dojo, pulled out the poles, and held them like swords as they spoke. Alik said something admirable, so I smiled and stroked his head.</p>
<p>"Dangerous things happen a lot when you're with Yamashiro-sensei (Teacher Yamashiro)."</p>
<p>"You seem to enjoy danger, so how about helping me from next year?"</p>
<p>Shimokawabe Airen smiled and spoke to me even in this sudden situation, so I made a move to recruit Shimokawabe Airen.</p>
<p>"I'll think about it positively."</p>
<p>Oh, if she's thinking positively, I might be able to recruit Shimokawabe Airen? Since she's an extreme mania who enjoys combat, I need to emphasize that there are several battles planned.</p>
<p>"Everyone be careful... what?!"</p>
<p>The Anya Order's attack didn't start from outside, but from Nakasone Rishin, who had lost his mind. I don't know if he was only thinking about sparring with me due to the sorcery or if he had been cooperating with the Anya Order from the beginning, but he said in a strange tone, "I'll attack first!" and threw a punch.</p>
<p>Pararak!</p>
<p>With the sound of the martial arts uniform fluttering, a sharp punch flew at me. Indeed, it was a blow with considerable speed and power, but perhaps because his training period was short, I could see a weakness in it.</p>
<p>I grabbed the sleeve of his uniform, pulled him, and slammed Nakasone Rishin to the ground. Nakasone Rishin, who fell to the dojo floor with a thud, got up as if it didn't hurt.</p>
<p>"Snap out of it, you bastard!"</p>
<p>"Snap. Out. Of. It. Let's. Continue. Sparring."</p>
<p>He said he snapped out of it, but his eyes were completely gone. And for some reason, I felt like the red, sucker-shaped patterns all over his body were getting redder.</p>
<p>Paaaaa!</p>
<p>The speed of his fist strike increased even more. The speed was faster, but it was a similar attack to before, so I grabbed his arm again and threw him to the ground with a modified overturning technique.</p>
<p>Kuung!</p>
<p>This time, he couldn't get up in one go. If he were in his right mind, he would have fainted from the feeling that every muscle in his body was being dismantled, but he kept wriggling and trying to get up, so it seemed like he was being controlled by some kind of sorcery.</p>
<p>"Kueueueu..."</p>
<p>Earlier, I thought it just seemed red, but now I could say for sure. The sucker marks on Nakasone Rishin's body were now turning crimson as if someone had painted them with paint.</p>
<p>Shuooo!</p>
<p>And every time those patterns got darker, his speed and power increased. There were many ways to become stronger by burning life force like this, and if the sparring continued, Nakasone Rishin's life seemed to be in danger.</p>
<p>I should just turn his jaw and knock him out. This time, I didn't grab his sleeve and hit his fist with my palm. My palm tingled, indicating that his fist had tremendous power.</p>
<p>And I went straight into Nakasone Rishin's chest and hit his chin with a short uppercut. If it were a blow with all my strength and the addition of the Abyss, it would be a blow that would separate his body and head, but I took away enough power to just knock him out, so Nakasone Rishin collapsed and couldn't get up.</p>
<p>Still, I watched him for a moment in case he was dead, but judging from the fact that his chest was rising and falling, it seemed like he had only fainted and didn't die. As he fainted, the curse seemed to lose its power, and the red sucker-shaped patterns gradually faded and disappeared.</p>
<p>After knocking down Nakasone Rishin, I looked around and saw nearly thirty martial artists wearing black robes rushing in and fighting the special class students and Shimokawabe Airen.</p>
<p>"As expected, the skills of KamiKen's disciples are amazing. A martial artist with the 'Sucker' engraved on them originally exerts about twice their skill."</p>
<p>Just as I was about to rush in to deal with the Anya Order attackers, a voice was heard from the dojo entrance.</p>
<p>A man with white hair, pale skin, and sharp eyes in a black martial arts uniform entered the dojo while talking, followed by a muscular giant with brown skin and red hair who looked over 2 meters tall. This giant was also wearing a black martial arts uniform.</p>
<p>The last one to enter was someone I knew. A beauty with transparent, light hair, Takakusaki Athena, whom I met at the festival and signed my photo card for.</p>
<p>However, what was unique was that, unlike the other two, she was not wearing a black martial arts uniform but a combat cyber suit with a white background and navy blue mixed in.</p>
<p>As expected, Takakusaki Gengo and Athena-ssi had the same last name, so she really was affiliated with the Anya Order.</p>
<p>"Ahaha, we've met before, haven't we?"</p>
<p>"We met at the festival."</p>
<p>Takakusaki Athena, looking embarrassed to meet like this, greeted me. I answered curtly and glared at the three of them.</p>
<p>"I heard that among the Anya Order's Four Heavenly Kings, there is a white-haired man and a red-haired giant. Are you the Anya Order's Four Heavenly Kings?"</p>
<p>"Yes. We are the Anya Order's Four Heavenly Kings."</p>
<p>To my question, the red-haired giant, whom I remember having the name Kaishu Kakan, which was quite a fitting name.</p>
<p>So far, Fujigasaki Rinka had identified all three of the Four Heavenly Kings: the red-haired giant Kaishu Kakan, the white-haired Gokubo Shiratsu, and Funayama Seigo, who wears glasses and trains the Anya Order's disciples.</p>
<p>Seeing Takakusaki Athena with them, it seemed that Takakusaki Athena was the hidden Four Heavenly King who hadn't appeared until now. Kaishu Kakan definitely said 'we' are the Four Heavenly Kings, so it must be true.</p>
<p>"If Takakusaki Athena is also one of the Four Heavenly Kings, where is the remaining one?"</p>
<p>"He's probably where you imagine him to be."</p>
<p>Gokubo Shiratsu said mockingly to my question. As expected, the remaining member of the Four Heavenly Kings, Funayama Seigo, seemed to have gone to deal with Yoda-halbeoji (Grandpa Yoda). Yoda-halbeoji's (Grandpa Yoda's) skills are considerable, but seeing that he's alone, he must have taken another Anya Order helper with him. Otagi Yuka has joined him, so I don't think I need to worry.</p>
