<p>"We'll divide the 300 million yen acquisition fee equally among the ten of us."</p>
<p>Sawayanagi Azuma declared while signing the documents. Dividing 300 million yen by ten meant 30 million yen per person, and everyone seemed overjoyed, exclaiming, "Ooh!" as if they'd never handled that much money before.</p>
<p>"I may be the president, but we've all been running things together. Everyone's been working without getting paid properly..."</p>
<p>Listening to Sawayanagi Azuma's story, even after combat missions, after paying off debts and repairs, they were only left with about 20,000 to 30,000 yen per person.</p>
<p>So, even if they did three missions a month, they were only making 900,000 won? That's incredible, everyone. Is it because they're all still young and don't know the ways of the world, or are they enduring it because they think things will get better after paying off the debt? I couldn't know since I wasn't in their shoes.</p>
<p>"Then we should change the salary system. Would you like to continue with the system of getting paid per mission, or would you prefer a monthly salary?"</p>
<p>Mimata Chieri asked. Since the shares belong to Mimata Chieri, it's right for Chieri to ask. To be honest, I hadn't even thought about the salary system.</p>
<p>"There's also a compromise. A monthly salary, but with extra money for each mission."</p>
<p>The ten of them decided to vote on the salary. Since they were dividing the 300 million yen anyway, there was no need to rush.</p>
<p>"Now that you've signed the acquisition contract, please take care of the debt."</p>
<p>Sawayanagi Azuma said with a desperate expression, handing over the debt-related documents. She probably hadn't even thought about an acquisition, and the debt was a more important issue for Sawayanagi Azuma. To be honest, I didn't really care if this shabby office or warehouse disappeared. I was planning to move to a better building anyway, but I had to pay off the debt since I had to use this shabby office until I bought or built a new building. And it was also unsettling to keep owing Black Tech.</p>
<p>"Then I can just deposit the money into the account listed in these documents, right?"</p>
<p>Mimata Chieri said after receiving and reading the documents. Mimata Chieri said she would take care of it quickly and left with the documents to make a phone call somewhere. It seemed like she was calling the bank. It seemed like she was making sure since the owner of the debt had changed.</p>
<p>"It's completely taken care of. Seiryu Military Company is now free from debt!"</p>
<p>"Waaah~!!"</p>
<p>The six pairs of eyes that had been watching our every move cheered at the news that the debt was gone. Debt definitely has a way of tightening a person's heart. I heard it was a debt inherited from the previous generation, and it had been tormenting Seiryu Tai for almost six years, so it was natural for them to cheer.</p>
<p>"Now, let me introduce our Seiryu Tai colleagues. This is our vice president, Sawayanagi Sinya."</p>
<p>"Thank you, investor-nim! I'm Sawayanagi Sinya."</p>
<p>Sawayanagi Sinya was an ordinary-looking man, but in my memory, he was an excellent net diver and a bomb installation and defusal expert. He was one of the four out of ten without superpowers, but he must have had outstanding qualities to be the vice president.</p>
<p>"This is Sawayanagi Tobe. He's the leader of our Seiryu Tai assault team."</p>
<p>"As a man, I will definitely repay the favor of paying off our debt."</p>
<p>Sawayanagi Tobe was a man with a height close to 2 meters, a sumo wrestler-like build, and a buzz cut. In my memory, he had the ability to turn his skin into steel, called Steel Skin. So, I used him as a tank and assault leader in the game, and he was playing the same role here.</p>
<p>"This cute girl is Sawayanagi Seari."</p>
<p>"Hello!"</p>
<p>A very cute girl who looked about sixteen years old raised her hand and greeted me. Ah, so she's Sawayanagi Seari. In the beginning, I thought she was useless because she only had the ambiguous ability to control wind (useful for tracking, though), but she was actually the most promising talent in Seiryu Military Company.</p>
<p>It's because she has the body of a fairy, like Yukiko, who has the body of a succubus, or me, who has the body of a cambion. In the later stages, her potential explodes, and she can wipe out an entire company by herself, but since playable characters become much stronger if you raise them well until the end, few people bothered to raise her. It's also difficult to use her since she's hired.</p>
<p>Well, I don't have to control her separately here, so she'll grow up well on her own.</p>
<p>"These two are sisters. This is the older sister, Kiyona Hori, and this is the younger sister, Kiyona Mari."</p>
<p>They were two beautiful, similar-looking girls in gothic lolita fashion, but the older sister had breasts and no superpowers, while the younger sister had no breasts but had superpowers. Online, they said Mari traded her breasts for superpowers. Certainly, the older sister had better physical stats and driving skills since she had no superpowers and bigger breasts. On the other hand, Kiyona Mari had pyrokinesis, the ability to create fire and cause chaos. Both had different uses despite their similar appearances, so they rarely acted together in the game.</p>
<p>"Hey, Azuma-chan! Why are you introducing only us at once?"</p>
<p>"That's right, that's right! Even if we're sisters, we're not a set menu~!"</p>
<p>"See? They're like this."</p>
<p>""Azuma-chan!""</p>
<p>The three of them were doing a skit. The introduction was over since the four who hadn't returned from their mission would be introduced later.</p>
<p>"Now that we're family, I'll treat you all to dinner!"</p>
<p>It was obvious that they hadn't been able to eat what they wanted because of the debt and had been saving a lot, so I had to raise their favorability with something delicious.</p>
<p>"Our president-nim has a lot of money, so let's go somewhere expensive and delicious! Everyone, please recommend a place."</p>
<p>Mimata Chieri called me "president-nim" instead of "Sunbae-nim" in front of others. At Mimata Chieri's words, everyone solemnly began to discuss. It was a battle between the meat faction and the sushi faction, and President Sawayanagi Azuma sided with the sushi faction and made a reservation at the most expensive sushi restaurant in Fussa.</p>
<p>Fussa wasn't Tokyo, so I wondered how expensive it could be, but the prices were considerable. When I asked Sawayanagi Azuma, she said that there were many PMCs, so a lot of money came in at once after a mission, but since PMCs risked their lives, many people spent their money lavishly. So, high-end restaurants and entertainment culture naturally developed.</p>
<p>Hmm, so that's the story. We headed straight to a sushi restaurant called Fussa Ichiban Sushi (Fussa Number One Sushi). I ordered a platter of assorted sushi for each person and told them to add more of whatever sushi they wanted after they finished.</p>
<p>"Maissuyo!"</p>
<p>It was a sushi restaurant of surprisingly high quality, enough to surprise Mimata Chieri, a gourmet collector. The people of Seiryu Military Company and I ate sushi to our hearts' content, and for the second round, we went to an Izakaya for a simple drink. It was said to be a regular spot for the Seiryu Military Company people, and it definitely had a more common atmosphere than the sushi restaurant in the first round.</p>
<p>Still, it was quite delicious as it was a long-time regular spot for the Seiryu Military Company people. It was quite funny to see the Seiryu Military Company people looking at me with satisfaction as I ate the snacks deliciously. It was like watching a foreigner eating their country's traditional food with relish.</p>
<p>Seari, who looked young, was actually twenty-three years old, so she could enjoy drinking. However, she was weak at drinking, so she was seen lying down on the table from the middle of the night.</p>
<p>After a pleasant time, it was late at night and time to part ways. We went outside and gathered in front of the store, but one person was missing. President Sawayanagi Azuma was nowhere to be seen. Where did the president go?</p>
<p>"Where is Azuma-ssi?"</p>
<p>Since everyone except the Kiyona Hori and Mari sisters were Sawayanagi, we decided to add "-ssi" to their names when calling them, so I called out "Azuma-ssi" and looked for Sawayanagi.</p>
<p>Sawayanagi Seari, who had fallen asleep in the middle and was being carried on Sawayanagi Tobe's back, woke up and muttered something before shouting at us.</p>
<p>"I asked the wind... Azuma-chan has been kidnapped by five people! If I hadn't fallen asleep, I could have found her right away...!"</p>
<p>At Sawayanagi Seari's tearful words, everyone's faces turned pale as if they had sobered up. Kidnapping all of a sudden...! And if they kidnapped Sawayanagi Seari while she was asleep, it was highly likely that they were people who knew them well.</p>
<p>"First, don't cry, do you know which way they went?"</p>
<p>At my question, Sawayanagi Seari raised her hand and pointed in one direction.</p>
<p>"What's over there?"</p>
<p>"Over there... is Black Tech's secret laboratory."</p>
<p>When I asked Vice President Sawayanagi Sinya, Sawayanagi Sinya shuddered as if he didn't even want to think about it and answered. Is the secret laboratory such a dangerous place?</p>
<p>"First, let's go to the warehouse to get equipment and then go rescue her."</p>
<p>I said in a firm voice to the people who were all in great mental shock at the words "secret laboratory." Hearing my words, the people of Seiryu Tai came to their senses and all ran to the warehouse.</p>
<p>"Sunbae-nim, do you have equipment?"</p>
<p>"It's in the car I drove."</p>
<p>I didn't really need equipment, but I should at least wear a helmet. As I stood in the open space in front of the warehouse with a helmet in my hand, the people of Seiryu Tai, equipped with gear, gathered one by one. When even Mimata Chieri arrived wearing techwear, I opened my mouth.</p>
<p>It's a bit strange for me to act like the leader, but everyone is spaced out, so I have no choice but to step up.</p>
<p>"Let's go!"</p>
<p>We headed to Black Tech's secret laboratory in an old transport vehicle driven by Kiyona Hori.</p>
