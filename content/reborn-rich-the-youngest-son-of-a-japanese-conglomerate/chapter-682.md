<p>"You've arrived?"</p>
<p>As Minamoto Risa opened the door, Minamoto Toyomi greeted her with a smile. Minamoto Risa, who had expected only her father, Minamoto Ushin, to be there, found Minamoto Toyomi and her siblings sitting together in the dining room.</p>
<p>"Yes, I'm here, Mother. Father, have you been well too?"</p>
<p>Because there were siblings with bloodshot eyes, trying to find fault with Minamoto Risa in any way possible, Minamoto Risa greeted them politely and took her seat.</p>
<p>"Yes. I've been well. Today, your mother and siblings have decided to have a meal together to celebrate your passing the Minamoto Group entrance exam."</p>
<p>Ah, so that was the reason. Minamoto Risa nodded at Minamoto Ushin's explanation. Of course, some siblings in charge of foreign branches couldn't come due to the distance.</p>
<p>However, she had been wondering why all of Minamoto Ushin's sons and daughters in the country had gathered, and the reason was that she had joined the Minamoto Group, so she didn't know whether to be happy or not.</p>
<p>"First of all, congratulations on achieving so much during the second semester. Leading the special class as the undefeated top class in the sports festival, something no one had ever achieved in the long history of St. Francisco Girls' Academy, was an honor to the family name."</p>
<p>Minamoto Risa's weakness was that she had been held back a year in school, but with these achievements, that weakness could be covered up a little. Minamoto Ushin also knew this fact, so he spoke about it where everyone could hear.</p>
<p>"Hmph, in the end, it's just high school achievements. Ultimately, what's important is the group's performance, Father."</p>
<p>Minamoto Akari spoke sarcastically. She was Minamoto Ushin's second daughter and Minamoto Risa's immediate older sister, and in fact, the youngest daughter born to Minamoto Toyomi. At the young age of 26, she was a talented individual who was successfully managing the Minamoto Hotel.</p>
<p>"Now that Risa has joined the group, she will also produce results. So, you should also take care of your younger sister."</p>
<p>"If Risa is as outstanding as Father says, she won't need my help."</p>
<p>Minamoto Akari replied sullenly to Minamoto Ushin's words. In fact, she had a slight inferiority complex because she was also from St. Francisco Girls' Academy, like Minamoto Risa.</p>
<p>Minamoto Akari was not interested in studying, so she didn't have top grades like her younger sister Minamoto Risa, so she was in a bad mood when Minamoto Ushin praised her for her grades today.</p>
<p>"Are you still aiming for Edo University?"</p>
<p>"Yes, my grades are sufficient, and from the Minamoto Group's perspective, it would be good to build friendships with the outstanding talents of Edo University, so I'm aiming for Edo University."</p>
<p>"Yes, you should be able to pass."</p>
<p>Recently, it has been fashionable for prestigious Japanese families to send their children to famous foreign universities for education, but the reputation of Edo University, the best university in Japan, was still strong.</p>
<p>"None of our siblings are from Edo University, so if you go, it will be helpful to the Minamoto Group."</p>
<p>Minamoto Kiyotada, Minamoto Ushin's second son, who had been listening quietly, spoke up.</p>
<p>Minamoto Kiyotada is in charge of both Minamoto Securities and Minamoto Steel, and is considered a potential candidate to become the next Chairman of the Minamoto Group, along with the eldest son Minamoto Kazumasa and the third son Minamoto Youken, who are overseas.</p>
<p>In addition, the eldest daughter Minamoto Katsue, who is also overseas, is also mentioned as a potential successor, but it is believed that a woman could never become the Chairman in the conservative Minamoto Group, so she was not included in the succession group.</p>
<p>"Father, what kind of work will Risa do within the group?"</p>
<p>"She will be assigned to a suitable place after going through various verification procedures, just like you all did. If her performance is good there, she will be assigned additional tasks, or her work will be changed to more important tasks."</p>
<p>When Minamoto Shigemune, the fourth son in charge of the Minamoto Educational Foundation, asked Minamoto Ushin, Minamoto Ushin answered as if asking what he was asking. Most people with the Minamoto surname are assigned to a suitable place after going through various aptitude tests, and the next step is determined according to the performance of the initial assignment.</p>
<p>Minamoto Shigemune, who is in charge of the Minamoto Educational Foundation, which is not very important in the Minamoto Group because he did not achieve proper results in the early stages, looked at Minamoto Risa with envious eyes, as she still had a bright future.</p>
<p>"Risa, unfortunately, I heard that Sugawara Chohei-gun, who was supposed to be your marriage partner, has passed away."</p>
<p>"Yes, I heard that there was a secretary dressed like a prostitute next to him. It's better this way. If I had married such a person, I would have tarnished the face of the Minamoto Group."</p>
<p>Minamoto Risa spoke quite sharply to Minamoto Toyomi's words. The atmosphere cooled rapidly at Minamoto Risa's words.</p>
<p>The hidden meaning of Minamoto Risa's words was that Minamoto Toyomi, who had brought a marriage proposal that would tarnish the face of the Minamoto family, was the problem, so the faces of the other siblings hardened.</p>
<p>Only Minamoto Kiyotada, who rarely gets angry, was calmly drinking tea with a peaceful expression.</p>
<p>"Father, since my marriage proposal story has come up, there is something I would like to say."</p>
<p>"Yes, speak."</p>
<p>As if this was a good opportunity, Minamoto Risa opened her mouth, looking straight into Minamoto Ushin's eyes.</p>
<p>"I hope there will be no marriage proposals until I graduate from university. From the Minamoto family's perspective, a daughter who is a graduate of Edo University will be more appealing to other families than a daughter who is a high school graduate."</p>
<p>"Insolent...!"</p>
<p>"How can you say such a thing?!"</p>
<p>"Risa! This kid, really!"</p>
<p>This time, the other siblings started shouting, unable to bear listening to Minamoto Risa's words, which made the Minamoto family out to be a family that sells off their daughters.</p>
<p>Still, unlike the angry siblings, only Minamoto Kiyotada was quietly looking at Minamoto Risa with an interesting gaze.</p>
<p>"Everyone, be quiet!"</p>
<p>""...!""</p>
<p>Everyone shut their mouths at Minamoto Ushin's words. Minamoto Ushin looked at Minamoto Risa for a while and then opened his mouth.</p>
<p>"Before, when you were selected for the undefeated top team in the sports festival, when I asked you what you wanted, you asked me to grant you one request, didn't you?"</p>
<p>"Yes, I did."</p>
<p>"Let's grant that request now. From now on, you can take care of your own marriage. The family will not interfere with your marriage."</p>
<p>"Thank you, Father."</p>
<p>In fact, the fact that the family would not interfere with Minamoto Risa's marriage was by no means a good thing for Minamoto Risa, but Minamoto Risa was satisfied.</p>
<p>In the first place, Minamoto Toyomi, the mistress of the Minamoto family, had full control over these marriage proposals, so she didn't know what kind of trashy man she would attach to her again. Rather...</p>
<p>'Yes, my homeroom teacher is better....'</p>
<p>Yamashiro Ryuta's face came to her mind without her knowing. Minamoto Risa, embarrassed by her own thoughts, took a sip of the tea in front of her with a flushed face.</p>
<p>"Okay. It seems like the story is over, so let's start eating."</p>
<p>As soon as Minamoto Ushin finished speaking, the doors of the dining room opened and maids began to bring in food. As expected of the Minamoto family, who pride themselves on being the best family in Japan, the table began to be filled with the best food.</p>
<p>"Let's eat."</p>
<p>"Enjoy your meal, dear."</p>
<p>""Thank you for the food.""</p>
<p>The meal began at Minamoto Ushin's words. Because the atmosphere was bad until just now, no stories were being told except for the sound of eating.</p>
<p>"So, what are your plans for the vacation?"</p>
<p>Minamoto Ushin broke the silence and asked Minamoto Risa, perhaps because he didn't like the sound of clattering dishes and spoons.</p>
<p>"I've only planned to prepare for university entrance exams in Miyashi and attend the main family gathering on Christmas Day."</p>
<p>"Yes, it's important to study to pass, but it's also important to make plans after passing. Now that you've decided to work for the group, you'll have to plan your university schedule so that it doesn't interfere with the group's work."</p>
<p>When Minamoto Risa told him her plans for the vacation, Minamoto Ushin nodded and told her that she would have to balance university and Minamoto Group work.</p>
<p>"Yes. I'm already checking the Edo University course materials."</p>
<p>"Yes...."</p>
<p>"If it's for Minamoto Group work, even Edo University will approve attendance, Father. I know that my cousin Kumaharu also received attendance approval for group work."</p>
<p>Minamoto Kiyotada joined the conversation by giving the example of his cousin Minamoto Kumaharu. In fact, no matter how much Edo University was the best university in Japan and a national university, it was difficult to ignore the Minamoto family.</p>
<p>Although the silence, which had only been filled with clattering sounds, disappeared with the topic of Minamoto Risa's university classes, all the topics that came up after that were stiff.</p>
<p>Stories were being told about which group had developed what, and which family's daughter had married whom, so the power map had to be redrawn.</p>
<p>If anyone were to only listen to the stories coming out of the table, they would never think it was a family meal. It was just a bleak business story, and the only interest in each other was, "Is this guy going to take away my interests?" That was all.</p>
<p>Even Minamoto Toyomi's children from the same womb were making remarks to check each other, so it was by no means a friendly family meal.</p>
<p>'My homeroom teacher's lunch is more fun and delicious....'</p>
<p>In the stiff atmosphere, Minamoto Risa couldn't properly taste the delicacies in front of her. She envied Yamashiro Ryuta and her classmates, who were probably having a fun lunch.</p>
