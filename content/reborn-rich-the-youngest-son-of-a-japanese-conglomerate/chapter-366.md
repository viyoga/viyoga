<p>"Understood, I'll be leaving as soon as you send the data." Shimokawabe Airen hung up the Phone Call from Daigo Terue. Then, she stood up from her seat and opened the Wardrobe. In the Corner of the Wardrobe hung a black full-body Suit. Though it looked like a thin Leather Suit, it was a Luxury item that assisted Muscle strength, blocked pistol Bullets, protected against Swords, and had heat resistance to shield against Flame. It was named 'Light Combat Suit G-019', but Shimokawabe Airen simply called it her Combat Uniform. Sarreuk, Sarreuk.... Shimokawabe Airen took off the Clothes she was wearing. Because she had eaten Evening Meal and was resting in her Room, she had been wearing a light Short-sleeved T-shirt and short shorts, but she even took off her underwear, becoming a naked body with nothing on. Shimokawabe Airen's Nasin was beautiful. Though her Mommae was not voluptuous, her Nasin, trained as a Swordsman, was enough to Seduce any man. To receive the Muscle strength assistance of the Combat Uniform, she had to wear nothing underneath, which was why she had taken off all her Clothes and underwear. Soon, Shimokawabe Airen, wearing the Combat Uniform, came out of the Room. Shimokawabe Airen, who had come down to Cheongsim-gwan's Training ground, slightly lowered the Helmet of a samurai Armor statue in one Corner. Cheolkeok! After a sound, a Secret Space was revealed without a sound. It was a secret warehouse for storing equipment used for such occasional missions. Weapons used for Murder could not be left where Students could see them, so they were kept in the secret warehouse. "Today, Kurotsuru (Black Crane) would be good." Kurotsuru was a katana primarily used by Shimokawabe Airen when undertaking Work on a dark night like this, characterized by its deep black Blade that reflected no Light. Perhaps because it was so suitable for Actual combat, it was the most frequently used Sword among Shimokawabe Airen's Collection. Shimokawabe Airen packed Kurotsuru, a spare dando, and throwing Weapons like shuriken, then attached the shuriken to various parts of her Suit and tucked the dando beside her Ankle. Having completed her combat readiness, Shimokawabe Airen, holding Kurotsuru, headed towards Cheongsim-gwan's temporary Parking Lot at the back. A black Motorcycle was parked in the Parking Lot. Cheolkeok. Shimokawabe Airen mounted Kurotsuru on the Motorcycle, then got on the Motorcycle. And she entered the Destination into the GPS mounted on the dashboard in the center of the Motorcycle's Handlebars. Shimokawabe Airen, even while wearing a bike helmet that completely covered her face, immediately set off. Her destination was an abandoned factory on the outskirts of Fujinomiya City, south of Miyashi—a remote place no one visited, truly a spot where a sinister group might dwell. Perhaps faithful to its Concept of being an abandoned Factory, the main gate was sealed with rusty chains and a rusty Lock. Anyone seeing the main gate tightly secured with rusty chains and a Lock, showing no trace of use, would not think anyone frequently entered or exited. 'Then there must be a frequently used Backdoor.' Shimokawabe Airen launched the drone from her Motorcycle's Trunk high into the sky to confirm the Scenery of the abandoned Factory. It wasn't just a drone that captured visible scenes; it was a low-noise Tracking drone equipped with limited AI, capable of identifying not only CCTV-like Cameras but also Footsteps on the ground, thus revealing Escape routes and Entry points. 'There's a main door on the west side.' She would just need to take the side path next to the main gate. Shimokawabe Airen retrieved the drone and rode her Motorcycle. As she rode, she threw shuriken, destroying the CCTVs along the way. Because it was Disguised as an abandoned Factory, there weren't many CCTVs. There were only two in total: one next to the main gate and one midway along the path. Checking the entrance primarily used by the Enemies, which she had noted beforehand, it was a gap disguised as a natural collapse. Having come this far, there was no need to destroy the CCTVs. The drone's findings showed no other path used besides this collapsed Entrance, so if she started down this path and killed everyone she encountered, they wouldn't be able to escape. Shimokawabe Airen, holding Kurotsuru, entered the Entrance. She saw a man holding a toolbox, seemingly there to fix the CCTV, and instantly approached him as if folding space, slashing him vertically. The man with the toolbox couldn't even let out a proper Scream and died, split in half as if from a Comic or Movie. As Shimokawabe Airen passed him, the sound of Internal Organs spilling out followed, and the two halves of the Corpse were heard Collapsing in opposite directions. "Hey! Yamada (山田)!! What's the problem with the CCTV?" A man, with a pistol holstered, called the dead man's name as he emerged from the abandoned Factory building and asked. Naturally, what was revealed was a tragedy. The sight of Yamada, split in half and dead, and a Woman wearing a Motorcycle Helmet and a tight black Combat Uniform, holding a katana, was visible. "W-what?! What is this?" The man, seemingly a veteran who had fought in a world war, immediately drew his pistol at high speed upon seeing Shimokawabe Airen and fired at her. But he hadn't even fired a few shots before his Eyes could only widen further. The man had fired a total of five Bullets. Shimokawabe Airen swung her Sword, slashing or deflecting all of those Ammunition. Moreover, the Last of the five Bullets was precisely deflected into the man's Furrowed Brow. Naturally, the man, struck by a bullet in his furrowed brow, had no choice but to collapse. Even though it was a ricochet, it was powerful enough to pierce his skull and destroy his brain. Shimokawabe Airen, who had moved leisurely until now, began to move a little more cautiously and quickly. Since Gunshot had been heard, most of the People inside the Factory would be in combat readiness. Shimokawabe Airen, entering the Factory, immediately rolled her Body and threw a few shuriken. Opponents with Guns could be dealt with by Sword, but those Guys using explosive Firearms like grenade launchers could suffer significant harm even in a Combat Uniform, which was why she threw shuriken at the Guys holding larger cannons than Guns. Tatatatang!! The Flames from Bullets hitting the floor and walls shone as if following the trajectory of her rolling Body, and the Screams of "Kwak!" from being hit by shuriken followed the sound of the Flames. Shimokawabe Airen, hidden behind cover, ran towards the Guy holding a rifle who was standing closest. Tatatang! Dodging Bullets was easy for Shimokawabe Airen. Because her immense physical Ability was combined with the basic Rule that she just needed not to be in a straight Line with the Muzzle, dodging a single person's Bullets was easy. If multiple people spread a net of Fire, it would be difficult to dodge, but then there was another Method for that. As Shimokawabe Airen, who had approached as silently as a ghost, raised her Sword, the man who was firing his Gun tried to block the Sword with his Gun. But Shimokawabe Airen, unconcerned, brought her Sword down. Seogeok! The Body of the rifle, made of hard composite steel, and the man's Neck were both cut by Kurotsuru's Blade. Shimokawabe Airen grabbed the severed Head and threw it into the Place where the most People were gathered. "This Bitch...!" A man, who thought Shimokawabe Airen had merely thrown his colleague's Head to mock them, Cursed at her but soon had no choice but to shut his mouth. It was because he saw the Face of his dead colleague. A Grenade was clenched in his colleague's mouth. "Ah, jenjang...!" Kwaaang! The Grenade exploded along with the Head, and the Enemies within its radius were annihilated. Even while everyone's minds were distracted by the loud noise and Aftermath of the Grenade, Shimokawabe Airen calmly reduced their numbers one by one. After the Confusion caused by the Grenade passed, few People remained. The True Japanese Imperial Guard, which had numbered in the tens, was reduced to only about seven by a single Woman. "Michinnyeon!" "Crazy bitch, you say...? It's just that there was no other Method for Actual combat in Japan if not this way." At the Cursing of one man, Shimokawabe Airen, inside her Helmet, answered with a Smile. No matter how many combat Organizations like PMCs had increased, there were no places that would hire a Swordsman like Shimokawabe Airen. Not until she was hired by Daigo Terue, who knew her Skill. To enter a higher world of Swordsmanship, Actual combat was essential. No matter how excellent a Geomsul Master/Expert, if they didn't experience Actual combat, they would inevitably die from a single pistol Bullet. Shimokawabe Airen wanted to overcome that Limit. "There's not much left, so I'll finish it in One Shot." Because Fear was gradually filling the Enemies' Eyes, Shimokawabe Airen, finding it troublesome to chase after the scattered, fleeing Enemies, decided to use the ultimate technique of her school, Kashima Kiichi Shinto-ryu. Kashima Kiichi Shinto-ryu (鹿島鬼一神道流) Ichijibuntenji (One Character Dividing Heaven and Earth) What Shimokawabe Airen did was merely a horizontal slash. But the Result was different from a typical horizontal slash. In that Moment, a Fantasy as if the sky and Ground were separating appeared before everyone's Eyes, and Blood gushed simultaneously from the waists of the seven Enemies. Now, only one Person remained alive. The man, who appeared to be the leader, had his Waist slashed by the recent technique, but it wasn't enough to kill him. Shimokawabe Airen had measured her Hand to extract Information. "If you Answer the Question I ask, you'll die peacefully." "And if I don't Answer?" Shimokawabe Airen took off her Motorcycle Helmet. Shimokawabe Airen's Eyes, narrowed into slits, shone eerily. And she smiled widely. "You won't be able to die." The leader of the True Japanese Imperial Guard ultimately chose the path of spitting out everything he knew and dying with his Neck severed.</p>
