<p>Since I came all the way to Tokyo, I decided to have dinner at a ramen shop that's currently trending in Tokyo before going back. <p>Since I succeeded, should I contact Minamoto Risa-ssi? You have to be careful about even small things like this to avoid getting caught.</p>
<p>MONA-RISA: Are you not hurt? Thank you. I won't forget this favor.</p>
<p>Minamoto Risa-ssi's secret messaging ID is Mona-Risa. That's kind of cute. It seemed like she made it with a pun.</p>
<p>Ryuta: From the start, the plan was that I wouldn't have to fight, so there's no way I could get hurt. Since it looks like he naturally fell and hit the parking lot curb and died, no one will think it's murder.</p>
<p>MONA-RISA: Considering the sins that guy committed, he should have died more painfully... But since the situation is like this, there's no helping it. Are you coming back to Miyashi now?</p>
<p>Ryuta: I came to a trendy ramen shop in Tokyo. I'm going to eat that and go back ww</p>
<p>MONA-RISA: It'll take a lot of energy to come back without a bike, so eat a lot.</p>
<p>Because I might get caught because of the bike, I was planning to leave the bike in Tokyo and fly to Miyashi using psychokinetic flight. It was also an opportunity to check how long it would take and improve my psychokinesis proficiency.</p>
<p>Minamoto Risa-ssi also knew about this plan, so she chatted to me to eat a lot. Originally, she would have teased me for being a glutton, but it seemed like she was really grateful.</p>
<p>Ryuta: Yeah, see you tomorrow.</p>
<p>MONA-RISA: Thank you. See you tomorrow, you.</p>
<p>As soon as I finished chatting with Minamoto Risa-ssi on the secret messaging app, the ramen came out. Since I ordered one bowl of pork bone broth-based ramen and one bowl of soy sauce-based ramen, I could enjoy the taste alternately without getting tired of it. Delicious. It was indeed a taste worthy of being trendy.</p>
<p>After finishing my meal, I rode my bike back to the outskirts of Tokyo and abandoned it in a corner of an alley. It was already dark, so it seemed like I wouldn't be seen even if I flew around. I wore a black rider jacket because I had planned this from the beginning.</p>
<p>Actually, I didn't have many opportunities to do psychokinetic flight, so it was a bit awkward at first, but I got used to it after flying around for a while. It was quite enjoyable to see the scenery on the ground whizzing by as my speed gradually increased.</p>
<p>Since I was going in a straight line instead of following the road, I would be able to get there much faster. However, it was quite cold since it was December winter and I was flying at a high speed.</p>
<p>When I see other psychokinesis-specialized characters flying well, there must be some method. After flying around for a while and thinking about it, I came up with one method. It was to use the psychokinetic reaction shield among the characteristics I had. I surrounded my body with a psychokinetic reaction shield.</p>
<p>I made the shield streamlined so that I could receive less wind resistance, and when I flew, the speed became faster and I wasn't cold. Of course, the consumption of mental power increased, but since my will stat, which is the main stat of psychokinesis, was so high, I could withstand this level of consumption.</p>
<p>Hmm, experience was indeed important. If I hadn't experienced it directly, I wouldn't have known what was lacking, so I wouldn't have been able to figure out this method. Just realizing this meant that I could say that I gained something from this assassination trip.</p>
<p>After flying for about an hour, I could see Miyashi in the distance. Even though it was far away, I was able to arrive quickly because my speed was quite fast. I landed in an alley that I had found out in advance that had no CCTV.</p>
<p>"I'm definitely tired."</p>
<p>I muttered as I came out of the alley. It seemed that the consumption of mental power was even greater because I was unfamiliar with it and came while doing various experiments.</p>
<p>Still, it was good to be able to cover a distance that would take 2 hours in 1 hour. Although it wasn't twice as fast as a car, the time was shortened much more than expected because there was no need to follow the road or get stuck in traffic at all.</p>
<p>If my psychokinetic flight ability becomes more familiar or related characteristics come out, it will become much faster, so it seemed like I could use it quite usefully. The problem is that flying around during the day is too noticeable, but I'll have to solve this with technology.</p>
<p>I should talk to Eve or Lara Sinclair and develop a flight suit with stealth capabilities.</p>
<p>"By the way, should I report Sugawara Chohei's death to the Taira side?"</p>
<p>Of course, there was no physical evidence that I killed him, but if I requested Sugawara Chohei's information and he died two days later, they could suspect that I did it.</p>
<p>I was curious about what kind of disciplinary action Gocho Kikuhito, who was in charge of the Miyashi area, received, so I decided to contact Fumimoto Takayuki, Grandfather's first secretary.</p>
<p>I thought it would be better to talk to Fumimoto Takayuki, who knew everything, because it was a bit difficult to talk to Gocho Kikuhito, who didn't know what my exact mission was.</p>
<p>-Yes, youngest master. This is Fumimoto.</p>
<p>Fumimoto Takayuki answered the phone as soon as the dial tone rang about once. He was indeed an old man with a calm voice who didn't seem to be shaken by anything.</p>
<p>"I'm sorry for calling you at this late hour, Fumimoto-ssi."</p>
<p>-It's not even 9 o'clock yet, it's okay.</p>
<p>Since he was Fumimoto Takayuki, Taira Danjo Grandfather's chief secretary, I needed to be polite even if I was a Taira. It would be more likely to be seen favorably to be polite than to be arrogant. You could say that Fumimoto Takayuki knew everything within the Taira Group.</p>
<p>"It's information that's a bit difficult to tell Gocho Kikuhito-ssi, so I contacted Fumimoto-ssi."</p>
<p>-What kind of information is it? Is it related to Minamoto Risa?</p>
<p>Fumimoto Takayuki quickly realized why I called him at my words. It was obvious that he would realize it because the only thing that was a bit difficult to tell Gocho Kikuhito was about Minamoto Risa.</p>
<p>"Yes, do you know that Sugawara Chohei, who was Minamoto Risa's marriage partner this time, died?"</p>
<p>-Yes, I just received the report.</p>
<p>"I did it."</p>
<p>-You did, Master?</p>
<p>Fumimoto Takayuki is very surprised at my words. He seemed not to believe that I, who was suddenly in Miyashi, killed Sugawara Chohei in Tokyo.</p>
<p>-According to the information I obtained, it was just an accidental death where he fell while walking down the street... It seems you have a psychokinetic subordinate. I thought it was Ichiro Master's side...</p>
<p>Fumimoto Takayuki was even making inferences. What he's muttering in the back is a bit concerning? Does that mean that there's a psychokinetic ability user on Taira Ichiro's side too?</p>
<p>"!"</p>
<p>At that moment, the keyword psychokinetic ability user and the keyword Taira Ichiro combined, and I remembered the psychokinetic ability user I met in a laboratory during the Kurotsukido subjugation mission a few months ago. He was a very skilled psychokinetic ability user, so I struggled quite a bit.</p>
<p>'If that guy is Taira Ichiro's subordinate...?'</p>
<p>Of course, psychokinetic ability users are common among ability users, so there's a higher probability that it's someone else, but contrary to that probability, I intuitively felt certain that the psychokinetic ability user I met at that time was related to Taira Ichiro.</p>
<p>This is good information. I don't know if it was on purpose or by mistake, but I heard good information from Fumimoto Takayuki.</p>
<p>-Master?</p>
<p>Ah, I was thinking about that unidentified psychokinetic ability user, so I didn't say anything without realizing it.</p>
<p>"Ah, I'm sorry. I was thinking about something else for a moment... Of course, it officially died as an accident, so there won't be any problems, but if I'm suspected as a suspect, you can block it with the power of the Taira Group, right?"</p>
<p>-Of course. This time, you prevented the connection between Minamoto and Sugawara, so the Chairman will give you a great reward.</p>
<p>From the Taira Group's point of view, they have several PMCs and prevented the Minamoto Group from being connected to the Sugawara family, which is a small but local leader, so you could say that I made quite a big contribution.</p>
<p>"I'm looking forward to what kind of reward Grandfather will give me."</p>
<p>-He will probably give you a generous reward.</p>
<p>A generous reward... I'm curious. The reward hasn't been confirmed yet, and I don't know Taira Danjo's inner thoughts, so I decided to ask what I was curious about.</p>
<p>"Has Gocho Kikuhito-ssi been decided to be disciplined?"</p>
<p>-Ah... Haven't you heard? Gocho Kikuhito-ssi has passed away.</p>
<p>Huh? What do you mean passed away? Did he die while doing an information mission? If he's in charge of information in a region, he would hardly have to run around himself...</p>
<p>"He died? Why?"</p>
<p>-He committed suicide. It seems that he committed suicide because evidence was revealed that he was connected to a large foreign company, so he couldn't avoid severe punishment.</p>
<p>Suicide and suddenly a large foreign company...? Miyashi wouldn't have much important information... Also, looking at his attitude when I last talked to him, I didn't think he would commit suicide.</p>
<p>"Is it possible to know which company he was connected to?"</p>
<p>-It's SCSMC in South China.</p>
<p>Ah... If it's SCSMC, I could understand a little. He probably contacted them to check if there was anyone among the students of St. Francis Girls' Academy who stole information during the cyber zombie incident. Gocho Kikuhito wouldn't know much, so I didn't think I would get caught by SCSMC. However, I was a bit concerned that SCSMC contacted Gocho Kikuhito, who belonged to the Taira's core information team. I wondered how SCSMC trusted Gocho Kikuhito enough to contact him.</p>
<p>"Thank you for the information. Then, who will be in charge of Miyashi information...?"</p>
<p>-We have to choose now. We will probably choose within two days. Please keep in mind that there may be some lack of information power during that time.</p>
<p>He hasn't been chosen yet. But something feels off... I had a lot of questions about whether SCSMC really approached Gocho Kikuhito, and whether Gocho Kikuhito really committed suicide. I can't just tell Fumimoto Takayuki that I'm feeling uneasy, so I decided to just say goodbye and end the call.</p>
<p>"Then the report of what I did is over too..."</p>
<p>I realized something while talking. Yes, what I did. It was the act of killing someone with psychokinesis. I suddenly remembered that among the things I talked about with Minamoto Risa, there was also a method to disguise it as a suicide with psychokinesis.</p>
<p>-Master?</p>
<p>"I'm sorry, I suddenly remembered something because of the story that Gocho Kikuhito-ssi committed suicide. Would it have been better to pretend that Sugawara Chohei committed suicide using superpowers?"</p>
<p>I didn't immediately say that Gocho Kikuhito's death was suspicious. But Fumimoto Takayuki, an ability user who is the closest aide to the chairman of the Taira Group, would easily notice what the meaning behind what I was going to say was.</p>
<p>-Since there's no motive for suicide, an accident would be better than suicide... Ah, I see. Thank you, Master.</p>
<p>Fumimoto Takayuki seemed to have realized something as well. Since he suddenly said thank you, I nonchalantly pretended not to know.</p>
<p>"I didn't say anything."</p>
<p>-It's just that I'm grateful that you often contact me, who is just a secretary. I suddenly thought of something urgent, so can I hang up?</p>
<p>"Of course. I don't know if I've kept you for too long when you're busy."</p>
<p>-No. You can contact me anytime, youngest master. Then I'll hang up now.</p>
<p>He's probably busy. The question I raised can just be passed over, but there's always a chance. I arrived home before I knew it while walking while talking on the phone.</p>
<p>It was quite a welcome home, even though it had only been a few hours. I put my phone in my pocket and went inside the house.</p>
