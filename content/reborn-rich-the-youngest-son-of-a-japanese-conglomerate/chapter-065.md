<p>After the hunting incident, we enjoyed a slightly sweeter Atmosphere on our Dijuniland date. After Splash Mountain, it was Fantasy World and Toon World, which, being modeled after Dijuni's classic animations, had many gentle attractions like carousels and spinning teacups. Taira Yukiko happily enjoyed various Princess-themed rides as if she had become a Princess, and I was busy taking Photos for her. Still, because it was Taira Yukiko's figure, all the Photos turned out satisfyingly.</p>
<p>The last, Tomorrow World, with its future Concept, gave off an almost cyberpunk Atmosphere. Especially popular was a ride that allowed you to tour the colonial Mars City streets, a Concept from the recently successful movie 'Princess of Mars,' and we also waited in a long line and rode it. Actually, I hadn't seen 'Princess of Mars,' so I didn't know it was that fun, but Taira Yukiko was really excited.</p>
<p>"Have you seen 'Princess of Mars'?" "Yes, it was an interesting Movie, so I watched it three times." "Would you like to watch it together next time? I actually haven't seen 'Princess of Mars'." At my Words, Taira Yukiko looked at me with an Expression that said, "You still haven't seen that fun thing?"</p>
<p>It was a pitiful Expression, as if she were looking at a savage who didn't know civilization.</p>
<p>"Alright, if you haven't seen it, let's watch it together. It's fun." Taira Yukiko's Eyes were filled with the Will to "I must show this fun Movie to my Son!"</p>
<p>By the way, since 'Princess of Mars' came up, regarding Mars colonization, Alex Musk, the Owner of SpaceZ and Tess, was said to be vigorously developing Technology for Mars terraforming even at the Age of 81. Currently, there were only closed research complexes on Mars, and one couldn't freely go Outside like on Earth yet. Still, because he was the Mister who had actually traveled to Mars and realized "I'm going to Mars," he was still considered a pioneer in space Technology. Of course, he also caused a lot of Trouble, so many people disliked him.</p>
<p>Tomorrow World had the most People, and there was a Reason for that. It was because it had the most rides modeled after the latest New Works. And the ride that impressed me the most was a ride called "Space Fighting" from Space Wars. Space Fighting was a single-person ride where you wore a VR Machine, piloted a space fighter in virtual space, and engaged in battle with the Empire's space fighters. It was more like a Game than a ride. Since it had 100 Seats, even though the line was long, you could enjoy it quickly. Since Taira Yukiko liked Marksmanship, she enjoyed this Space Fighting.</p>
<p>After enjoying Tomorrow World, we returned to Toon World and Fantasy World, enjoyed the rides we couldn't enjoy earlier due to long lines, and then headed back towards the Exit. Since we had enjoyed many rides and spent Time waiting in lines, it was already eight o'clock.</p>
<p>"It's a little late, but let's watch the Fireworks and then have Dinner at a nearby Restaurant, Yukiko-ssi." According to what I had found out earlier, the Fireworks were at 8:30 PM, so we could just watch that and then go Outside for Dinner. We stood at a prime Points where the Fireworks could be seen well, which we had found in advance, and waited for the Fireworks. After a short wait, with the sound of firecrackers rising, Dijuni Castle's white bricks reflected the various Colors of the Flame and shone beautifully.</p>
<p>The Sky was also filled with beautiful Fireworks, and the Surroundings were filled with People's Admiration.</p>
<p>Looking beside me, Taira Yukiko was also sincerely enjoying the Fireworks. I wanted to Kiss her like during the cherry blossom viewing Fireworks, but because this Place was full of Taira Group People due to the Talent Development Center event, I fought off the Lustful Demon and looked up at the Sky full of Fireworks again.</p>
<p>"Let's go back now." Taira Yukiko, having enjoyed all the Fireworks, wore a satisfied Smile and led me by the Hand. Before leaving Dijuniland, we stopped by the Global Bazaar, which we had talked about visiting earlier, to buy some souvenirs. Surprisingly, Taira Yukiko was sincere about buying souvenirs. It took a long Time to choose, and by the Time we came out, it was 9:40 PM. At this rate, a Restaurant would be out of the Question, wouldn't it?</p>
<p>Taira Yukiko also looked at the Time, and her Expression became a little awkward, then she glanced at me, as if checking my Awareness, and opened her Mouth.</p>
<p>"Coming to Dijuniland, which I visited in college, I'm craving something I used to eat often in college. Can I eat that?" I didn't know what it was, but I was extremely hungry, so I nodded my Head.</p>
<p>"I don't know what that is, but if you guide me, I'll go. Is it open at this Time?" "Yes, it's a Store that's open 24 hours...." The place Taira Yukiko, who was sitting in the passenger seat, pointed out was Matsuya, one of Japan's three major gyudon chains. I thought she wanted to eat something extraordinary, but it was just gyudon... Maybe noticing my look, Taira Yukiko said it as if offering an excuse.</p>
<p>"After marrying Saburo-ssi, I couldn't eat it." Indeed, if a daughter-in-law of a Chaebol Family was eating Gyudon alone at a commoner's Restaurant, it would immediately become a scoop in the newspaper or News.</p>
<p>"It's been a while since I've had Gyudon too. Let's go eat quickly." I drove a little hastily and headed to a nearby Matsuya. Japanese People are said to have their preferred Gyudon Franchises, and Taira Yukiko seemed to prefer Matsuya. Jin Yong-tae also preferred Matsuya. It must be because it was close to Home and he went there often, becoming accustomed to it.</p>
<p>"I also prefer Matsuya over other Franchises. What a coincidence." "Really? That's amazing. My Friends around me were mostly Yoshinoya-faction, so it was tough. So if I wanted to eat it, I had to go alone." What, is there a Matsuya-faction and a Yoshinoya-faction, like Yakuza? As we drove, talking about Gyudon, we arrived at Matsuya before we knew it. Since I had searched for a Place with a Parking Lot, we could park right in front and go in. Gyudon must always be eaten in Atamamori (regular rice, extra meat) size.</p>
<p>Taira Yukiko, speaking as if it were a Law, ordered Gyudon Atamamori Size for me too. I'm quite hungry now, and this alone probably won't fill me up....</p>
<p>"If it's not enough, you can just order more, so don't make that Expression." As if she had read my thought, it was Taira Yukiko playfully pressing my Nose slightly. As expected of Japan's representative fast Food, the Meal came out immediately. Perhaps because it had been a long Time since I ate it, it was quite delicious. Taira Yukiko, with Tears welling up, ate it deliciously, saying, "This is it...! This!"</p>
<p>Today was completely Taira Yukiko's Memory trip, since we went to Dijuniland and a Gyudon restaurant she visited in college. I additionally ordered and ate Katsudon and Mapo Tofu Rice Bowl. Hmm.... Perhaps because I had a simple lunch today, I needed to eat about three bowls to feel full. The way back after finishing the Meal was smooth and without traffic. Taira Yukiko, perhaps tired from playing all day, fell into a deep Sleep in the car.</p>
<p>She was still sleeping even after arriving Home, so I carried her in a Princess Carry and laid her on the Bed. Taira Yukiko forced her sleepy Eyes open and looked at me.</p>
<p>"Thank you." "It's nothing to thank me for." I Kissed Taira Yukiko's forehead, then lightly Kissed her Lips, whispered "Good night," and left the Room.</p>
<p>Minamoto Group Conference Room. Several reporters, looking at the Chair turned away towards Outside the window, were standing, sweating profusely.</p>
<p>"So, this is all the Information on Taira Danjo old man's Youngest Grandson, is that right?" The Chair turned around. An Elderly Person with a shiny bald Head was sitting in the Chair. The bald Elderly Person held two thin Documents and asked in a cold Voice.</p>
<p>"An illegitimate Child, graduated from Edogawa High School, joined the Taira Group Talent Development Center.... Is that all?" "Yes, yes! He had no contact with the Taira Group from a young Age and just grew up Ordinary, so there's not much Information." As the profusely sweating reporter answered, the bald elderly man, Minamoto Ushin, clicked his tongue.</p>
<p>"Tsk, tsk, tsk! This is why smart bastards who've had a taste of University don't work out. Bring it!" At Minamoto Ushin's Words, a man in a black suit wearing sunglasses brought the Documents. Minamoto Ushin unfolded the Documents and began to read. Taira Ryuta, before joining the Tairaga, was named Hata Ryuta, Jin Yong-tae. All his elementary, middle, and high school records are documented. His close friends are Kazuhiro Smith, the soccer player... and Kiyohira Genba...</p>
<p>His biological mother is Jin Mi-ja, a second-generation Zainichi Korean. His Tairaga mother is Taira Yukiko. There's a rumor that his relationships with women are scandalous... Look at this! I just gave a few pennies to those private investigators, and they uncovered this much. But! The guys from the Minamoto Group Headquarters Special Strategy Team only managed to get this much information? Minamoto Ushin's Anger was terrifying.</p>
<p>Killing Intent filled the Room, and the bodies of the reporters who felt that Killing Intent trembled like aspen trees.</p>
<p>"This guy is the weakest link in the Taira Group! He's the easiest one for us Minamoto to Attack. But you bring me just this much Document... as a Report? This is your last opportunity. Look at the Information those private investigation bastards, whom you treat like scum, brought, and immediately formulate a Plan. If I like it, I'll let it go this Time, but if I don't, it's your Head immediately." At the mention of "Head," the reporters hastily looked at the Documents sent by the private investigation agency.</p>
<p>The reporters, flipping through the Documents so fast they made a rustling sound, soon seemed to organize their thoughts and began to speak.</p>
<p>"If we consistently gather Information using his Friends, it should be fine." "To confirm the rumor that his Relationships with Women are Obscene, it might be good to find a Woman and attach her to him." "Using his birth Mother, Jin Mi-ja, also...." As the Threat to their livelihood fell upon them, the reporters did their best to formulate a Plan regarding Taira Ryuta. Minamoto Ushin, upon hearing that, nodded his Head with a faint Smile.</p>
<p>"Now, it's finally somewhat acceptable. This Time, based on the strategies you've just thought of, create the most effective Plan. If you mess up like this again, I will never Forgive you." "We will not disappoint you!" As the reporters bowed and left the Conference Room, Minamoto Ushin rose from his Seat.</p>
<p>"Hehe, after Danjo old man's Youngest Son, if his Youngest Grandson also goes wrong, the pain will be great, won't it? Hehehehe....." Minamoto Ushin stood alone in the Conference Room and laughed sinisterly.</p>
