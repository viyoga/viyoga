<p>,But if the enemy has such a dangerous ability, are these agents okay?</p>
<p>,We have an ability user in our Federal Paranormal Management Department who can imbue objects with abilities. They brought a lot of goggles and sunglasses imbued with protective abilities.</p>
<p>Diana Ritchie explains to me while putting on goggles. Come to think of it, the combat agent Manuel Murdock I saw yesterday was wearing sunglasses. I just thought he was wearing them for style, but listening to Diana Ritchie, it seemed like he was wearing sunglasses as equipment to deal with the Thousand Mouths, the enemy this time. By the way, the Federal Paranormal Management Department has a pretty rare enchanter, which is quite impressive, isn't it? If there was an enchanter, they could respond with the enchanter's ability depending on the opponent, so they were quite high-level personnel.</p>
<p>,They're slowly approaching.</p>
<p>Manuel Murdock isn't just anyone either; as I think of him, he comes out of the van parked next to us wearing a gun and various equipment. Seeing him still wearing sunglasses, it seemed like my thought that they were protective gear was correct. If he was wearing them for style, he would have taken off the sunglasses and put on other equipment. Seven more armed agents came out of the van that Manuel Murdock got out of, and armed forces came out of other parked cars as well.</p>
<p>As Manuel Murdock said, I could feel the serial killer getting closer and closer. I was feeling considerable pressure. But it seemed like I was the only one feeling that pressure; the surrounding agents were just watching the CCTV.</p>
<p>Of course, they were aware that the enemy was approaching because the screen that was constantly filming the surroundings with the CCTV was turning off, but it seemed like I was the only one feeling it with my body.</p>
<p>,It's the studio entrance!</p>
<p>Looking at the entrance at Diana Ritchie's shout, a man was standing at the studio's main entrance. As said before, I felt a considerable shock just from seeing him. Because I was wearing Simyeon Bu-gun's combat equipment, I didn't take any damage because I felt like my whole body was covered in the abyss, but I felt pain.</p>
<p>,Kheu-euk...!</p>
<p>I took a look at how the others were doing, and thanks to the defensive ability granted by the enchanter having some effect, their eyeballs weren't exploding like last time, but they were still complaining of considerable pain. Some were wiping the blood flowing from their eyes. Still, it's better to bleed a little than have your eyeballs explode.</p>
<p>At first, I thought he was just a normal man because the entrance and the parking lot were far apart, but as he got closer, I felt that something was different.</p>
<p>He had a human form, but the more I looked at him, the more he gave off a negative feeling, like the uncanny valley? Of course, he noticed us too, so he stood at a certain distance and opened his mouth.</p>
<p>,A tasteless appetizer.</p>
<p>The man's voice was chilling. The sound itself was like metal scraping, and the way he looked at humans as food made me even more convinced that this murderer was not human.</p>
<p>,Did you see inside his mouth?</p>
<p>Diana Ritchie asked me. I nodded heavily. Like the impression I had just felt, the man's overall form was human, but when you looked at him in detail, he wasn't human. When he opened his mouth to speak, his mouth was full of countless wriggling short tentacles instead of a tongue. It looked like a leech.</p>
<p>It feels similar to the physical transformation, which is one of the side effects of the abyss stat. I hope my ominous prediction is wrong, but if it's right, it's highly likely that the enemy is using the power of an existence from outside the dimension, similar to Simyeonui ttal-nim. Perhaps his will or resistance stat to minimize the side effects wasn't sufficient, so he went crazy and his body was transformed at the same time.</p>
<p>Step...! Step...!</p>
<p>As Thousand Mouths, who was standing at the entrance, gradually walked towards the parking lot, a strange murderous aura began to envelop my whole body. I could endure it, but the other combat agents couldn't stand that strange murderous aura and were trembling.</p>
<p>,Aaaah! Die!</p>
<p>Phew phew phew!</p>
<p>One of the agents couldn't stand it any longer and screamed and fired his gun. Because it was in the middle of Hollywood, he was equipped with a silencer to avoid causing a disturbance, so the gunshot wasn't loud, but along with the phew sound of the gun being fired, a massive barrage began.</p>
<p>The shooting started accidentally due to fear, but as Manuel Murdock, the leader of the combat agents, stepped forward to encourage them, they began to form a fire net and shoot as they had been trained. Seeing this, it seemed like Manuel Murdock didn't have great superpowers, but he was good at commanding.</p>
<p>Diana Ritchie pressed her temples as if she had a headache and began to contact someone.</p>
<p>,The battle has begun, so please put up the barrier.</p>
<p>It seemed like they even brought a barrier specialist to completely block the involvement of ordinary people. As soon as Diana Ritchie finished speaking, I felt the space in this area become isolated, unable to interact with the outside world any longer.</p>
<p>The shooting stopped briefly when the barrier specialists set up a barrier at the battle site. And when the gun smoke swirling around Thousand Mouths dissipated, I saw Thousand Mouths, who had been almost turned into rags by the gunfire. His whole body was covered in blood from the bullet holes, but Thousand Mouths gave a bitter smile.</p>
<p>Maggots began to fill the holes and wounds on his body caused by the numerous bullets. Before I knew it, the maggots that had filled his flesh began to regenerate Thousand Mouths' wounds. The speed of regeneration was considerable, almost similar to my own regeneration ability. Mithril bullets are perfect for stopping regeneration, but I wonder if they're prepared?</p>
<p>,It would be good to use mithril bullets since he has regeneration ability.</p>
<p>,I'll pass that on.</p>
<p>At my words, Diana Ritchie sent a radio message to everyone to use mithril bullets. As expected of an organization under the US government, they seemed to be preparing expensive mithril bullets as well. At Diana Ritchie's radio message, all the agents quickly changed their magazines. But Thousand Mouths wasn't going to stand still during the short time it took to change magazines.</p>
<p>He ran towards us with strange movements and stretched out both hands, and fingers grew in enormous numbers on the palms of his hands, like villi, and at the ends of those fingers were mouths with jagged teeth.</p>
<p>It seemed like the cause of the photo of the corpse I saw yesterday, where it looked like finger-sized piranhas had bitten off pieces of his whole body, was these numerous fingers.</p>
<p>The fingers stretched out like tentacles and began to attack the Federal Paranormal Management Department agents who were changing magazines. Seeing this, Diana Ritchie jumped out and began to attack the finger tentacles by creating wind blades with her aerokinesis ability.</p>
<p>While the finger tentacles paused due to the wind blades, the agents who had replaced their magazines with mithril bullets began firing again. Mithril bullets poured down, leaving blue trails.</p>
<p>Unlike the shooting just now, Thousand Mouths felt that this was definitely dangerous, so he began to move at an incredible speed and attack the agents one by one.</p>
<p>No matter how fast he moved, he couldn't avoid all the bullets, so he began to get hit by mithril bullets. But he tore off the parts that were hit by the mithril bullets himself and eventually grabbed one person.</p>
<p>A crunching sound was heard, and blood began to flow from the grabbed part, and the agent began to scream in tremendous pain. I couldn't see the grabbed part, but judging from the sound, it seemed like he was chewing on flesh with the saw-like teeth on the ends of his fingers.</p>
<p>It was clear that chewing on flesh was helping with regeneration, as he began to recover quickly despite being hit by mithril bullets. Thousand Mouths threw away the agent who appeared to be dead and began running towards the next target. The agents felt hesitant at the cruel sight and the screams of their comrade.</p>
<p>If this battle was repeated as it was, it was only a matter of time before the Federal Paranormal Management Department was annihilated. Of course, that was assuming I was standing still, but I wasn't standing still.</p>
<p>,I'll join in too.</p>
<p>Now that I've roughly grasped the opponent's abilities, I can't stand still any longer, so I ran towards Thousand Mouths. While running, I stopped the finger tentacles attacking the agents with telekinesis and gave Thousand Mouths a punch full of the abyss.</p>
<p>Kwaaaang!!</p>
<p>As expected, thanks to the equipment buff, even though it wasn't a Shinkaiyatsude technique but just a regular punch, it had a completely different power than usual. One punch was enough to cave in one side of his chest. It was a blow that would kill an ordinary person dozens of times, but Thousand Mouths didn't die and looked at me.</p>
<p>,Kyaak! This power is...!</p>
<p>Thousand Mouths felt something from my fist and began to scream while looking at me. I had a hunch, but when I felt Thousand Mouths' energy up close, it wasn't the abyss. But it was definitely a power from an alien, similar to the abyss.</p>
<p>,Dieeeeeee!</p>
<p>Die! As he opened his mouth wide, the wriggling leeches inside his mouth popped out and stuck to my armor and helmet.</p>
<p>Chi-ik!</p>
<p>Because it was armor with the flames of the abyss burning, the leeches burned and fell to the ground as soon as they touched it, but the flames got smaller each time I burned a leech, so it seemed like I would be in considerable danger if I kept getting hit.</p>
<p>I dug a little deeper into Thousand Mouths and raised his chin with a palm strike. After making him close his mouth by raising his chin, I immediately used the Shinkaiyatsude technique again and swung my fist.</p>
<p>Shinkaiyatsude (Deep Sea Eight-Hand) Kakekushi (Shadow Skewer)</p>
<p>A spear of the abyss popped out of Thousand Mouths' shadow and pierced his body.</p>
<p>When I first awakened the abyss, it really looked thin and skewer-like, just as the technique's name suggested. But as my level increased, my abyss stats went up, and I even wore Simyeon Bu-gun's combat gear, it turned into a technique where a thick spear, not a skewer, pierced through. Should I start calling it Kakeyari instead of Kakekushi from now on...?</p>
<p>I was having useless thoughts in my head, but my fists and legs kept moving. While being pierced by the spear of the abyss, I unleashed five of the Shinkaiyatsude techniques in an instant and inflicted damage all over his body.</p>
<p>No matter how much he had an energy source equivalent to the abyss I had, this man was like a beast possessed by madness without any combat skills. He couldn't be a match for me, who had trained in the world's best martial arts and trained myself. He tried to attack by moving his fingers with teeth, but it was just a meaningless movement.</p>
<p>Fortunately, no matter how strong his regeneration ability was, it seemed like he couldn't easily recover from the damage he took from the abyss. If he continued to regenerate even after my attack, I wouldn't have been able to inflict fundamental damage even if I just kept hitting him all day.</p>
<p>,It seems like I have to kill him without leaving a corpse because of his regeneration ability.</p>
<p>For some reason, I didn't want to hand over Thousand Mouths' corpse to the Federal Paranormal Management Department. It was because I had a feeling that something unfavorable to me would be achieved if the US government researched this corpse.</p>
<p>,I can't help it. I understand! Get rid of it!</p>
<p>Hearing Diana Ritchie's shout, I unleashed one of Shinkaiyatsude's ultimate techniques, the Ten Finger Demise, and stabbed Thousand Mouths' chest with the tips of ten fingers. The ten streams of the abyss that had seeped into his body swirled inside his body and began to explode like fireworks.</p>
<p>,Kyaaaaaaak!!</p>
<p>Feeling that Thousand Mouths, whose whole body had been torn to shreds by the explosion of the abyss, had lost his life, I used the offering to Simyeonui ttal-nim at the same time to send Thousand Mouths to Simyeonui ttal-nim and gathered all the abyss and struck the ground with my fist.</p>
<p>Kwakwakwang!</p>
<p>Immediately after Thousand Mouths' corpse disappeared, I struck the ground and created a crater about 5 meters in radius. Everyone didn't seem to notice that I had taken Thousand Mouths' corpse because of the dust and smoke.</p>
<p>You might ask why I couldn't use the offering to Simyeonui ttal-nim from the beginning, but in the case of small fries, I could use it however I wanted. But if I used it on someone with a certain amount of power, it would easily scatter the energy of the magic circle around me and easily ruin the magic circle. I couldn't use it at all during the battle, so I used the offering to Simyeonui ttal-nim only after Thousand Mouths died in this battle as well.</p>
<p>,That's amazing. To not even leave a trace...!</p>
<p>Diana Ritchie approached and said while looking at the crater I had created.</p>
