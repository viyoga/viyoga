<p>Unlike other families, the Urabe family didn't have a mansion in Miyashi. Of course, just because they didn't have a mansion didn't mean the Urabe family's female students attending St. Francis Girls' Academy were sleeping on the streets; they stayed at a nearby Urabe family shrine, pursuing both training and academics simultaneously.</p>
<p>So, the daughters of the Urabe family are said to have a different vibe from the other noble young ladies attending St. Francis Girls' Academy.</p>
<p>Unlike the other noble young ladies who are like flowers in a greenhouse, or who live doing everything they want and are like tomboyish aristocratic young ladies, the daughters of the Urabe family, who train and communicate with the gods, are said to feel more like nuns than students.</p>
<p>Of course, Urabe Konoka, who hadn't been training for more than a few months, didn't quite give off that feeling, but I was going to pick her up because she couldn't skip her morning training even on weekends, and also to see the shrine.</p>
<p>Cutting through the cold morning air, as I headed to the shrine, I saw a few worshippers who had come to pay their respects even in the early morning. Since the Urabe family is a famous family of Shinto priests, they own several famous shrines, so it seemed they had many worshippers.</p>
<p>I parked the car in the parking lot in front of the shrine and followed the worshippers into the shrine, where I saw women dressed as shrine maidens walking around.</p>
<p>Urabe Konoka isn't here. The shrine in Miyashi enshrines Susanoo, and in Japanese Shinto, Simyeonui ttal is called Shinkai Hime and regarded as a lower sea god of Susanoo's lineage. That's why Urabe Konoka, the shrine maiden of Simyeonui ttal, was adopted into the Urabe family.</p>
<p>Naturally, the altar dedicated to Simyeonui ttal, not Susanoo, who could be said to be the main deity of the shrine, was set up in a corner, but I had heard from Urabe Konoka in advance where it was, so I headed east of the shrine.</p>
<p>To the east of the shrine, there was a small torii gate, like a shrine within a shrine. Passing through the torii gate and entering a small building, there was an altar dedicated to Shinkai Hime, that is, Simyeonui ttal. In front of it, Urabe Konoka, dressed in shrine maiden clothes, stood with her hands clasped.</p>
<p>Even if she greets Simyeonui ttal with her hands clasped now, it won't be conveyed to Simyeonui ttal, who is cutting off all connections. Even though she's afraid of having her ego completely devoured by Simyeonui ttal, she seemed to be a sincere shrine maiden in her own way.</p>
<p>Urabe Konoka, who had finished praying, turned around and was surprised to find me.</p>
<p>,Oh?! Sensei, you're here? You should have said something when you arrived."</p>
<p>"Yeah, you were so focused that I waited."</p>
<p>"Yes.... I'll go change quickly!"</p>
<p>Urabe Konoka looked great in the shrine maiden clothes, but since it was a date, she probably wanted to change into casual clothes.</p>
<p>Knowing that it takes at least 30 minutes for a woman to prepare for a date, I sat on the shrine's veranda-like area and looked at my phone while waiting.</p>
<p>Armed conflict breaks out in the border area between South and North China!</p>
<p>What is this? I knew the atmosphere was tense between them because of the cyber zombie incident, but an armed conflict broke out? Of course, even after the end of World War III, South and North China, which had a tense atmosphere, often had armed conflicts, but they all ended in small skirmishes.</p>
<p>Even now, it's just a small skirmish in the border area, not a full-scale war, but this time, more than ten million people have been killed by a radical terrorist group in North China, so South China's anger has grown tremendously, and most people thought it would escalate significantly.</p>
<p>Because there were so many victims, world opinion of North China was not good, so it seemed that South China would have more power if they started a war.</p>
<p>In fact, if North China accepted South China's demands, the war wouldn't escalate, but it was questionable whether North China would accept the demands.</p>
<p>South China's demand was simple. Arrest the Red Revolution Front, which committed this crime, and send them to South China. Some argued that sending one terrorist group to the victim country would prevent a war, while others said it was an unreasonable demand from South China that infringed on the country's right to punish.</p>
<p>I knew that the Red Revolution Front was being supported by North China, so I was convinced that they wouldn't send the Red Revolution Front to South China because North China could end up taking all the blame for this cyber zombie incident.</p>
<p>"Sensei~! Did you wait long?"</p>
<p>"No, I was looking at my phone for a while, so I didn't even realize the time."</p>
<p>Urabe Konoka came out after changing her clothes while I was checking the articles on my phone. She was wearing a simple outfit of a long-sleeved polo shirt and jeans, but it suited her quite well because of her beautiful figure. Urabe Konoka's trademark red headband also suited her surprisingly well.</p>
<p>"Let's have breakfast here."</p>
<p>"Shall we?"</p>
<p>Actually, there weren't many places to eat breakfast in Miyashi. Following Urabe Konoka, there was a small restaurant with two servings of neat Japanese food set out.</p>
<p>"I told the chef yesterday to prepare two servings in advance."</p>
<p>"Really? I'll enjoy it."</p>
<p>The breakfast prepared in advance by the Urabe family wasn't particularly special. But it was made up of very comforting foods, so it was a kind and gentle morning.</p>
<p>Sweet wagashi came out for dessert, and we sat and chatted while eating, then brushed our teeth for a while and met again.</p>
<p>"Shall we really go now?"</p>
<p>"Yes~"</p>
<p>I started driving with Urabe Konoka in the car. Our date course this time was the villa district on the outskirts of Miyashi.</p>
<p>Miyashi's wealthy neighborhoods, famous as villa cities, were in two places: the area around St. Francis Girls' Academy, where large mansions were gathered, and the area on the eastern outskirts of Miyashi, where moderately sized villas were gathered.</p>
<p>It was a place where beautiful villas were gathered, giving it the feeling of a European resort city, with many delicious high-end restaurants and cafes with a good atmosphere.</p>
<p>The prices were quite high, targeting wealthy retirees or people who came to rest at their villas, so naturally there weren't many out-of-town customers, and it was known as a place where celebrities secretly went on dates.</p>
<p>"I've only ever gone back and forth between school and the shrine, so this is my first time in the villa district."</p>
<p>"I've never been there either. I came to Miyashi at the end of August, so I've lived here for almost three or four months, but I haven't been there."</p>
<p>I've been on a few dates with students, but this was my first villa date, and I hadn't come because I was being careful in case there were any relatives of the student among the retired noble families.</p>
<p>The students also didn't want their date scenes to be seen by their grandfather-level relatives living here, so naturally the Miyashi villa district wasn't chosen as a date course.</p>
<p>Urabe had recently become an adopted daughter, so I boldly chose this as our date course, thinking that even if there were any retirees from the Urabe family, they wouldn't recognize her.</p>
<p>"Wow...! The buildings are all so pretty!"</p>
<p>"Right?"</p>
<p>As we passed along the road lined with beautiful or stylish villas, Urabe Konoka couldn't take her eyes off the window.</p>
<p>It was definitely beautiful. There were rows of houses that wealthy people had invested in to spend their retirement or take a short vacation, so even if you just took pictures on the street, the atmosphere would be great.</p>
<p>"This is a residential area, and we're going to play in that street over there."</p>
<p>The commercial district, not far from the residential area, also had only beautiful buildings. There were no franchise stores that detracted from the theme of the street, and all the stores were pretty and well-suited to the villa district, making it perfect for a date.</p>
<p>I parked the car in the parking lot and got out. Urabe Konoka was taking pictures of the surrounding scenery with her phone, looking amazed. It was Saturday, and it was also famous as a place where celebrities go on dates, so there were quite a few people.</p>
<p>"Because there are so many people...."</p>
<p>Ssak.</p>

<p>Today's date wasn't just an ordinary date for fun, but a date to find out Urabe Konoka's true feelings, so I was going to be a little more proactive with skinship. On the outside, she just had the face of a beautiful Japanese girl.</p>
