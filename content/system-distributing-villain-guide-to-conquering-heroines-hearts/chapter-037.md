<p>Felix watched Dante’s back as they walked out of the alley. He couldn’t help but let out a sigh as he thought about his condition.</p>
<p>’What a shame.’ He sent a voice transmission to Freya, who was walking beside him.</p>
<p>’Hm? What do you mean?’ Freya turned to look at him and caught him staring at Dante.</p>
<p>’Someone like Sir Dante can’t cultivate. Don’t you think that’s a waste?’ He said with some emotion in his voice. ’If he could, I think he’d be a real pillar for Madam and for rebuilding Eden’s Gate.’</p>
<p>’What?’ Freya’s brow shot up. ’Since when do you think that highly of him? Don’t you remember how he talked to Madam when we picked him up at the bar?’</p>
<p>’I don’t know what happened between them that day, but he’s a completely different person now.’ Felix paused for a moment. ’At the very least, you have to admit he’s more trustworthy than our young master who went missing.’</p>
<p>’That’s... true.’ She admitted. ’I was actually worried about how he’d hold up against awakeners.’</p>
<p>’It doesn’t matter how many items you strap onto a regular human, one good hit from an awakener and it’s over. But he walked in there and dominated the whole room without breaking a sweat.’</p>
<p>’I hope we find a cure for both Madam and him someday.’ He said, his tone heavy.</p>
<p>’We will. It’s not like they’ll stay that way forever.’ Freya said, her chin lifting slightly. ’I’m sure Raven Labs will figure something out at least.’</p>
<p>Neither of them said anything after that as there was no certainty behind her words.</p>
<p>As Dante stepped out onto the street, the night air hit his face.</p>
<p>He took a long breath before climbing into the car. Felix took the driver’s seat and Freya got in the back as usual.</p>
<p>"So," Freya was the first to speak as the car pulled away. "What now? What’s the next move?"</p>
<p>"Next move?" Dante leaned back in his seat with a relaxed expression. "What next move?"</p>
<p>"Oh, come on. You’re seriously going to just sit around and wait?" She asked with a knowing look.</p>
<p>In the driver’s seat, Felix’s ears perked up.</p>
<p>"Why does everyone act like I’m some kind of troublemaker?" Dante put a hand on his chest like he was offended. "They told us to stand down. So I’m standing down. Like a good, law-abiding enforcer."</p>
<p>"You’re lying." Freya said flatly.</p>
<p>"I’m hurt."</p>
<p>"You’re full of shit."</p>
<p>Dante pressed his lips together to keep from laughing.</p>
<p>Freya stared at him through the rear-view mirror and waited, but nothing came.</p>
<p>She didn’t know why she suddenly felt disappointed.</p>
<p>’Hold on. Did I actually expect him to come up with something?’ She thought, and the realization annoyed her more than his silence did.</p>
<p>She was starting to understand what Felix meant earlier.</p>
<p>"I really want to crack your head open and see what’s going on in there." Freya said, crossing her arms.</p>
<p>Dante looked at her and shook his head with a smile. He could tell both of them were itching to know what he was planning next.</p>
<p>But he couldn’t exactly tell them he was going home to scheme against their madam, could he?</p>
<p>Besides, he had already completed what he set out to do.</p>
<p>’Stage one is done.’ Dante thought, watching the streetlights blur past. ’Now I just need to keep the pressure on until the two sides go at each other’s throats.’</p>
<p>He was never planning to finish off the gangs himself. That was the protagonist’s job. Why the hell would he do that bastard’s homework for him?</p>
<p>As a villain, he much preferred to light the fire, step back, and collect everything that fell out of people’s pockets while they burned.</p>
<p>And when the smoke cleared, he would take care of Marcus with his own two hands.</p>
<p>****</p>
<p>Late that night...</p>
<p>Dante sat cross-legged on his bed, fully focused on channeling the mana through his body. But this time, he wasn’t trying to absorb another mana stone.</p>
<p>He had already absorbed more than enough mana to break through. He just had to digest it properly.</p>
<p>The mana rushed through his circuits and his body shook once before something inside him finally gave way.</p>
<p>The wall he had been pushing against for days cracked open like an egg.</p>
<p>’There it is.’ Dante thought, keeping his breathing steady as everything settled into place.</p>
<p>He opened his eyes and pulled up the status panel.</p>
<p>[Name: Dante Ackerman]</p>
<p>[Realm: Mana Awakening Late Stage]</p>
<p>[Bloodline: None]</p>
<p>[Talents: Foresight(EX), Mana Affinity(SS), Scheming Mind(S), Two-Faced(C), Business Sense(D)]</p>
<p>[Luck: Doomed (In Rise)]</p>
<p>[Villain Points: 8,000]</p>
<p>[System Slots: 1/2]</p>
<p>[Bound Targets: Natasha]</p>
<p>[Techniques: None]</p>
<p>[Inventory: 50 Litres of wine, 200ml of medicine, 6 Mana Stones (Mythic)]</p>
<p>"Phew, that took some time." Dante said with a grin. "Not bad for a guy who couldn’t absorb mana a couple of weeks ago."</p>
<p>He then checked the notifications.</p>
<p>[You successfully infiltrated Crimson Hollow and obtained the position of Enforcer while plotting the destruction of a protagonist-affiliated organisation!]</p>
<p>[You have obtained 1,000 villain points!]</p>
<p>"Nice." He nodded with satisfaction. His total was now sitting at 8,000. The extra 500 came from wrecking the two White Tiger bases.</p>
<p>He closed the panel and leaned back against the headboard. He raised his hand and stared at the shiny ring on his finger.</p>
<p>It was a storage ring he got from Phantom Force. The tank, and the gun were developed by Raven Arms in top secret.</p>
<p>"And she actually let me use them however I want after I told her about my plan." He said with a joking smile. "I guess that’s why my school teachers taught me to be honest."</p>
<p>In the novel, mana weapon development would stay pretty limited. They barely worked against awakeners at Circuit Expansion.</p>
<p>Petra was at Circuit Expansion Early Stage.</p>
<p>The second she felt the danger, she attacked without wasting a breath.</p>
<p>For these weapons to actually work, he would either need to catch someone off guard or need them to stay perfectly still.</p>
<p>’Alright. The gang stuff is on hold for now. Petra told us to wait, and I’m happy to let her take her sweet time.’</p>
<p>His eyes narrowed as his thoughts shifted.</p>
<p>’Which means it’s time to focus on the person I’ve been neglecting.’</p>
<p>Natasha’s favourability had stalled after reaching 40, barely increased by 1. The daily cooking missions were keeping the rewards trickling in, but that was it.</p>
<p>He had given her enough time to recover, and it was time for him to act again!</p>
<p>He had already thought through different strategies and mission ideas, and he had already decided on one.</p>
<p>He pulled up the mission tab with a thought.</p>
<p>A grin formed on his face as the blue screen appeared.</p>
<p>[Mission: Let Him Cook!]</p>
