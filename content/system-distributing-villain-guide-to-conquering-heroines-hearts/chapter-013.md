<p>[Triggering Task 3....]</p>
<p>[Chain Mission: Save Dante (3/3)]</p>
<p>[Task 3: Princess Carry Dante to his bedroom and tuck him into bed. Covers included!]</p>
<p>[Time limit: 15 mins]</p>
<p>’Uhm why is the last one so easy? Doesn’t the last one usually have the highest difficulty?’ Natasha thought, feeling surprised.</p>
<p>But she wasn’t complaining, not at all.</p>
<p>’But princess carry? What’s with this weird requirement?’ she tilted her head in confusion and looked at Dante who was sleeping soundly on her chest.</p>
<p>’Actually, nevermind...’ Her eyes softened as she thought back to everything that had happened after Dante came home.</p>
<p>The bond between the two of them had grown a lot in just a few hours.</p>
<p>Natasha felt safe around Dante now that he already knew everything about her. She felt like she could finally drop her guard around him for the first time in years.</p>
<p>’W-why is she looking at me like that?’ Dante watched her through Target Feed while his face was buried in her twin peaks.</p>
<p>His conscience took a direct hit from the soft look in her eyes.</p>
<p>Luckily for him, she didn’t waste any time and got moving.</p>
<p>Natasha stood up from the chair while holding Dante steady.</p>
<p>Then she slid one arm under his knees and the other behind his back, and lifted him off the ground like he weighed nothing.</p>
<p>"He is surprisingly light..." Natasha said with a bit of surprise.</p>
<p>’Light? Master, you are just way too strong.’ Dante was a little speechless after hearing her.</p>
<p>"I should cook more dishes for him," Natasha said after weighing him like a bag of potatoes.</p>
<p>’.....Come to think of it, this new body really is light.’</p>
<p>Dante wasn’t about to argue when food was on the table.</p>
<p>Compared to his cooking, Natasha was basically a master chef.</p>
<p>Natasha grabbed his dangling arm and placed it on her shoulders before walking toward the stairs.</p>
<p>As she climbed a few steps, Dante’s hand slipped off her shoulder and landed on her breast, making her stop.</p>
<p>’It’s not my fault if I’m sleeping, right?’ Dante grinned internally.</p>
<p>Natasha didn’t think much of it and just put his arm back on her shoulder, then kept climbing.</p>
<p>But Dante wasn’t going to stop there.</p>
<p>Every time Natasha took a few more steps, his hand somehow ended up on her chest again, forcing her to pause over and over.</p>
<p>[Remaining Time: 9 mins and 21 seconds]</p>
<p>’No, I can’t waste time like this. Besides, he’s sleeping anyway!’ With that thought, Natasha just grabbed Dante’s hand and placed it on her chest, then climbed the rest of the stairs fast.</p>
<p>Dante finally got a proper feel of her melons with his own hand and was very satisfied.</p>
<p>Being princess carried by a woman was a new experience for him, especially by a woman this gorgeous.</p>
<p>Honestly, he got the appeal now.</p>
<p>He could only thank Felix later. Without that guy, he never would have thought of this.</p>
<p>Above all, he was very happy with how much progress he made in just one day.</p>
<p>’Hehe, Ryan, it hasn’t even been 24 hours and your mom is already carrying me to bed.’ Dante felt a twisted rush of excitement. ’At this rate, you’ll have a little sibling before you even come back.’</p>
<p>A few moments later, Natasha looked down at Dante lying on the bed, sleeping soundly.</p>
<p>She smiled at the sight, wiped the sweat off her forehead, and pulled the covers over him.</p>
<p>The very next second, a bunch of system messages popped up in front of her.</p>
<p>[Task 3 has been fulfilled]</p>
<p>[Congratulations! You have successfully completed the chain mission - Save Dante]</p>
<p>[You have obtained the Phoenix’s Tears]</p>
<p>[Note: Do not share it with others.]</p>
<p>[The item will lose its prowess even if you share a single drop]</p>
<p>’I did it!’ Natasha’s body shook with excitement after seeing the mission complete message.</p>
<p>But she was a little disappointed that she couldn’t even share a single drop of the Phoenix’s Tears.</p>
<p>[Do you wish to collect the reward?]</p>
<p>She was about to say yes right away, but then realised it was better to do it in her own room. She turned toward the door in a hurry.</p>
<p>Right as she took a step, she felt her hand being pulled back, and then Dante’s scared voice reached her ears.</p>
<p>"M-master, don’t leave me behind. Take me with you..." Dante was still pretending to be asleep, looking like he was having a nightmare.</p>
<p>[Do you wish to collect the reward?]</p>
<p>’Now, let’s see what you’re going to do.’ Dante thought with an amused smile internally.</p>
<p>He already knew Natasha cared about him, not just doing all of this for the rewards. But he wanted to see how she would react in this situation.</p>
<p>’I don’t really care what she does,’ Dante thought while watching through Target Feed.</p>
<p>’Actually, it would be good if she did that. I wouldn’t feel bad while manipulating—’</p>
<p>Before he could even finish that thought, Natasha turned back and leaned down toward his face.</p>
<p>And she kissed his forehead.</p>
<p>"Don’t worry, kid."</p>
<p>"Master isn’t going anywhere without you." She said with a gentle smile, then sat down next to him and held his hand. She brushed his hair aside while watching over him.</p>
<p>Dante couldn’t form a single thought as he felt shitty. He pretended to slowly recover from his nightmare.</p>
<p>After making sure he was okay, Natasha stood up from the bed and walked to the door.</p>
<p>Before heading out, she took one last look at him and closed the door.</p>
<p>"Ah... why is this woman so stupidly kind?" Dante opened his eyes and stared at the ceiling. "I guess that’s why she ended up in this situation..."</p>
<p>He was sure of one thing now.</p>
<p>It wasn’t going to be an easy job to assign missions to Natasha and the other heroines while playing dumb the whole time.</p>
<p>***</p>
<p>On the other hand, Natasha quietly closed the door behind her and took a deep breath with her eyes closed.</p>
<p>When she opened them again, she immediately said with a serious expression.</p>
<p>"System, collect the reward."</p>
