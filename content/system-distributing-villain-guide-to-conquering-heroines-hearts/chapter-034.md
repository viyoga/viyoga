<p>"Is this the right place?" Freya asked in a doubtful tone as the trio looked at the rundown bar in front of them.</p>
<p>The Red Eye.</p>
<p>The neon sign above the door was barely holding on, flickering nonstop.</p>
<p>Corbin opened his mouth to explain that it was just a cover, but Dante beat him to it.</p>
<p>"It’s this way, right?" Dante was already standing in front of an alley beside the bar.</p>
<p>’Oh?’ A look of surprise flashed on Corbin’s face for a second before he nodded.</p>
<p>"How did you know?" Felix asked casually.</p>
<p>"Yeah, have you been to this place before?" Freya spoke up, already suspicious.</p>
<p>"Haha, I was just asking. I didn’t expect it to be right." Dante said with a shrug.</p>
<p>’Was it really a fluke?’ The three of them had the same thought, and none of them believed him.</p>
<p>As Corbin led the trio down the narrow alley, a few guys loitering around saw him coming and got out of the way without being told.</p>
<p>"So," Corbin glanced back at them while walking. "You guys got names, or should I start calling you mask number 1, mask number 2?"</p>
<p>"Jester." Dante said.</p>
<p>"Two Punch Man." Felix said.</p>
<p>Corbin waited for the third.</p>
<p>"...Kitty Crusher." Freya forced out like it cost her a year of her life.</p>
<p>Corbin stopped walking and turned around with surprise all over his face.</p>
<p>He clearly wanted to say something, but seeing the murderous aura pouring off her, he wisely kept his mouth shut and kept walking.</p>
<p>"So can I ask why you guys are going after White Tiger’s bases?" He asked in a prying tone as curiosity got the best of him.</p>
<p>Even their spies couldn’t dig up anything useful, and they heard the higher-ups at White Tiger were just as clueless.</p>
<p>"What did the White Tiger Union do to you guys?"</p>
<p>"They killed my brother." Dante said casually.</p>
<p>The trio kept walking while Corbin stopped in his tracks.</p>
<p>He looked at their backs, especially Dante’s, and something about the way he said it so easily made the hair on the back of his neck stand up. A lot of people had similar reasons for joining their organisation, but he had never felt anything like this from any of them.</p>
<p>’W-white Tigers really messed up this time.’ He gulped, feeling secretly grateful that these three weren’t their enemies.</p>
<p>"Hey, wait for me!"</p>
<p>"I’m the one who’s supposed to be showing you the way!"</p>
<p>Corbin caught up to them, slightly out of breath but trying his best to look like he wasn’t.</p>
<p>He straightened his jacket and walked past them to a heavy metal door at the back of the alley. He knocked three times, paused, then twice more.</p>
<p>The door swung open from the inside and Corbin gestured for them to follow.</p>
<p>A set of concrete stairs led straight down.</p>
<p>The further they went, the louder it got.</p>
<p>What started as a faint vibration under their feet turned into muffled shouting, then full-on screaming, and somewhere underneath all of that, the dull, wet sound of fists hitting flesh.</p>
<p>When they reached the bottom, the stairway opened up into a massive underground space.</p>
<p>A caged ring sat in the center with two guys tearing into each other while a mob of people surrounded them, waving cash and screaming like animals.</p>
<p>The smell of sweat, smoke, and blood filled the air.</p>
<p>It was loud, filthy, and completely lawless.</p>
<p>Corbin noticed Freya and Felix looking around, taking note of their surroundings, while Dante just stood there watching the fight in the ring.</p>
<p>’Just who is this guy?’ Corbin couldn’t get a read on him at all. ’Why do I feel like he knows this place better than I do?’</p>
<p>Dante had a smile on his face as he caught the puzzled look on Corbin’s face from the corner of his eye.</p>
<p>He could guess exactly what was running through his head.</p>
<p>’Do I know this place? Of course I do.’ Dante thought with amusement. ’How could I possibly forget about this place?’</p>
<p>In the novel, this was called the Pit. One of Crimson Hollow’s biggest operations and the personal territory of a Captain named Petra.</p>
<p>Petra was one of those rare women in the story who was as beautiful as the heroines.</p>
<p>Ryan actually ran into her during his revenge arc.</p>
<p>Normally, he would have added her to his harem. That was practically his reflex at that point.</p>
<p>But instead, he KILLED her.</p>
<p>Every time they fought, he would always wear gloves and keep his distance from her. She was one of the few female characters he didn’t lay a finger on.</p>
<p>And the reason was so hypocritical it was almost funny.</p>
<p>’He can have a harem but she can’t?’ Dante thought, shaking his head with a smile. ’Someone needs to crack that guy’s skull open and study what’s inside.’</p>
<p>’How can a person be that much of a hypocrite and still believe he’s righteous?’</p>
<p>Personally, Dante didn’t give a fuck about what others did with their lives. It wasn’t his business, and he didn’t have any right to question them.</p>
<p>Maybe it was because he grew up as an orphan. Nobody gave a shit about what he did, so he did the same.</p>
<p>Most of the time, he would only act if someone messed with his interests.</p>
<p>"Oi, you coming or what?" Corbin called out. He was already halfway across the arena.</p>
<p>Dante pulled himself out of his thoughts and followed, with Felix and Freya right behind him.</p>
<p>Corbin stopped at the far end where a set of metal stairs led up to a section above the ring. The glass was tinted dark, but Dante could make out shapes moving behind it.</p>
<p>Two guys stood guard at the bottom, arms folded, faces blank.</p>
<p>They didn’t even look at Corbin. Their eyes went straight to the trio.</p>
<p>Corbin jerked his thumb over his shoulder. "They’re with me."</p>
<p>The guards stepped aside.</p>
<p>As Dante climbed the stairs, he saw the woman who made the protagonist run away.</p>
<p>She had red hair that fell past her shoulders and a pair of violet eyes that gave off a lazy but sharp look. Her face was the kind that could stand next to any of the heroines without losing.</p>
<p>She was sitting in a leather chair with her legs crossed, swirling a glass of wine in her hand.</p>
<p>Four men stood around her.</p>
<p>All of them tall, good-looking, and not a single one was watching the fight below.</p>
<p>They were all watching her. Like dogs waiting for a command.</p>
<p>Dante took one quick look and turned his head before she noticed him. He had zero interest in getting this woman’s attention.</p>
<p>That would only speed up her death.</p>
