<p>In the bedroom, Dante sat on the bed and began to check the system messages.</p>
<p>[Natasha has successfully completed the mission "Let Him Cook" and obtained Path of Recultivation (Super Rare)!]</p>
<p>[Triggering 10x rewards.....]</p>
<p>[Detecting the reward isn’t suited for your situation....]</p>
<p>[Path of Recultivation is designed for those who have lost their cultivation. Since your cultivation was never lost, the manual is incompatible.]</p>
<p>[Do you wish to convert the reward into equivalent value?]</p>
<p>"Of course!" Dante said without hesitation. The manual had zero use to him.</p>
<p>[Converting reward to equivalent value....]</p>
<p>[You have obtained a token to buy an Epic grade manual!]</p>
<p>’Claim.’ Dante collected the reward with a thought and a purple token appeared in his hand.</p>
<p>[Manual Token]</p>
<p>[Grade: Epic]</p>
<p>[Description: Usable only for manuals.]</p>
<p>After checking the token, he scrolled through the rest of the notifications while flipping it between his fingers.</p>
<p>[Natasha’s favourability has increased by 4.]</p>
<p>[Current Favourability: 45 (Crush)]</p>
<p>’Nice! Finally some real progress.’ He thought with a satisfied grin.</p>
<p>If the favourability kept going up by 1, Ryan would come back before it even crossed 60 and he could forget about ever becoming that bastard’s father.</p>
<p>Besides, he and Natasha weren’t the only ones living in this mansion.</p>
<p>Emma also lived with them, mostly because the Raven Group headquarters was near the mansion. She would be busy with work and wasn’t home half the time.</p>
<p>Even now, she was on a business trip outside the Allied States.</p>
<p>’Once Emma comes back, the difficulty is going to shoot up.’ Dante’s expression turned serious. ’I’ll need to focus on her too.’</p>
<p>Natasha knew that Emma liked him. She would definitely use that as an excuse to put distance between them again.</p>
<p>’In that case....’ A scheming grin formed on his face.</p>
<p>’I’ll make her do the opposite.’</p>
<p>Just as he was going through his future plans, his eyes landed on the villain points notifications.</p>
<p>[You touched Natasha inappropriately and gaslighted her into thinking it was normal.]</p>
<p>[+1,000 villain points!]</p>
<p>[You made Natasha think dirty and imagine herself getting spanked by you.]</p>
<p>[+1,500 villain points!]</p>
<p>[You tricked Natasha into sucking the life out of you and made fun of her!]</p>
<p>[+1,500 villain points!]</p>
<p>[Current balance: 12,000 villain points]</p>
<p>"....Can you phrase that last one properly?" Dante had a speechless look on his face.</p>
<p>Technically, Natasha did suck the life out of him. But if anyone read that out of context, they would think of something completely different.</p>
<p>Still, he was happy about the villain points finally crossing five digits. He needed to buy something for the next mission and it would prove very helpful down the line.</p>
<p>’But first, let’s buy a manual.’ Dante opened the villain store and went straight to the techniques section.</p>
<p>After browsing for a while, he finally found what he was looking for.</p>
<p>[Path of Awakeners]</p>
<p>[Grade: Epic]</p>
<p>[A manual written for those walking the path of awakeners. Contains detailed knowledge on mana cultivation and the awakener world. Includes a Super Rare mana cultivation technique and basic combat spells such as fireball, wind blade, and others.]</p>
<p>"It’s basically an all-in-one. And exactly what I needed!" Dante said with a smile. "Searching through hundreds of techniques was really worth the effort."</p>
<p>He could have just bought an Epic grade mana technique and called it a day, but for giving up one grade, the bargain he got was way too good to pass up.</p>
<p>Up until now, he had been going off the knowledge he had from the novel.</p>
<p>The author never went into deep detail about cultivation, so his understanding of mana and how it actually worked was seriously lacking.</p>
<p>To be honest, he had just been absorbing mana and hoping for a breakthrough. His recent one made him realize it wasn’t going to be that easy going forward.</p>
<p>"More importantly, I can’t just keep relying on items!" He wanted to learn some real techniques and build up his own strength.</p>
<p>Besides, an Epic grade mana technique wouldn’t help him much either.</p>
<p>What he really needed was a Divine grade technique.</p>
<p>Even a low-level Divine grade technique cost more than a million villain points. It would take him months to save up that much.</p>
<p>The only way to get them faster was by stacking favourability milestones.</p>
<p>But he had already reserved Natasha’s milestone rewards for a bloodline.</p>
<p>"Well, there is an easier way actually." He said with a grin. "Master did say she was planning to teach me. She’s really serious about making me the successor it seems."</p>
<p>The mana technique of Eden’s Gate, the oldest awakener organisation in the world, had to be a top-level Divine grade at the very least.</p>
<p>"But I also have another option." He added with a devious look. "If the mother is going to give me one, the son should too, right?"</p>
<p>After thinking it through, he used the token to buy the manual.</p>
<p>[You have successfully purchased "Path of Awakeners"!]</p>
<p>[Would you like to absorb it?]</p>
<p>"Yes." Dante nodded as he got into a meditation position and closed his eyes.</p>
<p>The next moment, a vast amount of knowledge poured into his mind. The origin of mana. How cultivation actually worked. What awakeners really were and how they came to be.</p>
<p>Things the novel barely touched on and things the author probably didn’t even know himself.</p>
<p>***</p>
<p>A few hours later, Dante slowly opened his eyes and held his head with a frown. "Ugh... Whoever wrote this wasn’t joking when they said detailed."</p>
<p>"My head is killing me. I probably would have fainted if I wasn’t an awakener." He rubbed his temples, but feeling the mana flowing through his body smoothly for the first time, a smile crept onto his face.</p>
<p>The mana technique he got from the manual was called the Flowing River Art. And it really lived up to its name.</p>
<p>The mana moved through his circuits like it finally had a road to follow instead of him just dumping it in and hoping for the best.</p>
<p>"A Super Rare technique is already this good?" He clenched his fist, feeling the difference in every fiber of his body.</p>
<p>"How broken would a Divine one be?"</p>
