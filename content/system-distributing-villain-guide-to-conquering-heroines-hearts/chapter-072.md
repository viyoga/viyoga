<p>"...The what?"</p>
<p>She rubbed her eyes, thinking she might be seeing things, but the blue screen was still there.</p>
<p>’So it’s real? I awakened a system?’ She read the words on the screen again carefully.</p>
<p>She knew very well about systems.</p>
<p>During her college days, she had read countless novels and even though most of them were romance, a good number of them had systems in them.</p>
<p>"But a bullying system? I have to bully people?" She had a puzzled expression.</p>
<p>[That’s right! You just have to bully the designated target. The more you bully them, the greater your rewards!]</p>
<p>"Target? Who is that?" Emma looked at the screen with more confusion in her eyes.</p>
<p>"And why do I have to bully someone? I’m the CEO of one of the biggest corporations in the world. I don’t bully..." She paused. She wanted to say she didn’t bully anyone but that would be a blatant lie.</p>
<p>She had bulldozed so many people who went against her that she could be crowned a bully queen. But she never went after anyone without a reason.</p>
<p>"I only go after people who offend me! I don’t bully people just because. That’s just a waste of time."</p>
<p>[Indeed. I know you aren’t that free. But the target is someone who has made you cry so many times.]</p>
<p>"Wait. You don’t mean...?" Emma immediately figured out who her target was after that hint.</p>
<p>The system confirmed it by showing Dante’s picture on the screen.</p>
<p>"You want me to bully Dante?" Emma had a mocking smile on her face. "Heh. Get out of my face."</p>
<p>She pulled the covers over herself and turned to the side. She had suffered a lot because of Dante, but she still didn’t want to actually harm him.</p>
<p>The thought had crossed her mind plenty of times, but that was always out of anger and frustration.</p>
<p>She never truly meant any of it.</p>
<p>[Your chat history with the target suggests otherwise. Would you like me to read some of the messages out loud?]</p>
<p>"Don’t you dare!" Emma’s eyes snapped open in panic.</p>
<p>[The word "idiot" has been used 9,998 times.]</p>
<p>[The word "coward" has been used 7,776 times.]</p>
<p>[The word "hopeless" has been used 5,678 times.]</p>
<p>[The word "blockhead" has been used 4,434 times.]</p>
<p>[...]</p>
<p>[...]</p>
<p>[...]</p>
<p>"STOP! I SAID DON’T!" Emma shot up and tried to grab the screen but her hands went straight through it.</p>
<p>She took a deep breath and composed herself.</p>
<p>She was a businesswoman.</p>
<p>Normally, she wouldn’t lose her cool this easily. But she didn’t expect the system to actually pull the receipts on her.</p>
<p>’That was embarrassing.’</p>
<p>"You... those don’t count." She said with a slight blush and coughed. "They’re the nicknames I use for him."</p>
<p>[Fucking idiot.]</p>
<p>"What did you just say?" She asked with veins forming on her forehead.</p>
<p>[Nicknames.]</p>
<p>[N.I.C.K.N.A.M.E.S.]</p>
<p>"....."</p>
<p>Emma didn’t fall for the obvious ragebait. Instead, she looked at the screen with a serious expression.</p>
<p>"What do you want from me? I already said I won’t bully Dante. Just leave me alone!" She held her head, already feeling a headache coming on.</p>
<p>[Aren’t you tired of waiting? How long are you going to wait for Dante to take the initiative?]</p>
<p>[Are you okay with your relationship staying like this forever?]</p>
<p>"Of course not! But how is bullying him going to help with that?"</p>
<p>[Haven’t you read romance novels? The male lead bullies the female lead all the time. He teases her, gets under her skin, makes her flustered.]</p>
<p>[And what happens at the end? She falls for him every single time. You just have to do the same thing but in reverse. Stop waiting around like a side character in your own love story.]</p>
<p>"A-are you crazy? Do you think that stuff actually works in real life? On Dante?!" Emma’s face was burning red as the scenes played out in her head involuntarily.</p>
<p>In her imagination, she shoved Dante onto the bed and climbed on top of him before he could react.</p>
<p>"Emma, what are you doing?" Dante looked up at her with anger in his eyes but his ears were red.</p>
<p>"Big sister." She grabbed his chin and tilted his face up toward hers, a teasing smile playing on her lips. "Call me big sister."</p>
<p>He tried to turn his head away but she held it in place.</p>
<p>"You’re my little brother. I can do whatever I want with you." She leaned down and kissed him.</p>
<p>She held the back of his head and pulled him in, kissing him rough enough to leave a mark.</p>
<p>"THERE IS NO WAY I CAN DO THAT!" She snapped back to reality and kept shaking her head. Her heart was slamming against her chest and her face felt like it was on fire.</p>
<p>[Then are you okay with him being with other women?]</p>
<p>The moment those words hit her ears, Emma’s expression turned ice cold.</p>
<p>The playful embarrassment vanished instantly and what was left was pure murderous intent.</p>
<p>"Don’t even joke about that."</p>
<p>"Besides, do you think Dante can find someone better than me?" There was absolute confidence in her voice.</p>
<p>In her mind, there was no woman on this planet better than her.</p>
<p>And she was right about that.</p>
<p>After all, she was one of the heroines in the novel. There were only equals to her. No one above.</p>
<p>[That is exactly the problem. Don’t you understand?]</p>
<p>[Dante doesn’t want someone "better" than you.]</p>
<p>Emma felt her heart ache the moment those words landed. It was like an arrow piercing straight through her chest. ’Right. That’s the reason for this whole mess.’</p>
<p>She had been sitting around, complacent, thinking Dante still had feelings for her and that things would work out on their own.</p>
<p>But what if some bitch stole him before that happened?</p>
<p>Her blood boiled at the thought and the temperature in the room dropped instantly.</p>
<p>’I would never let that happen!’ She vowed in her heart and reached out her hand toward the blue screen.</p>
<p>[Do you wish to bind?]</p>
<p>[Yes / No]</p>
<p>And... She pressed Yes.</p>
