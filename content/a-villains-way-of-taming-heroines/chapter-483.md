<p>[Day Forty-Seven.]</p>
<p>I witnessed the confrontation between Seraphina and the current pact head, Tyrus Vistidon, the Head of Strength from Lord Flamelle. The sense of oppression, even only witnessing from the outside me's perspective, was overwhelming.</p>
<p>But Seraphina managed to pierce his heart with her fingers... remarkable. This is the pact head chosen by Ansel, her martial prowess is unparalleled.</p>
<p>In this meeting, the outside spoke of matters I had considered long ago. The conflict between the Empress and Lord Flamelle is intensifying, and if they go to war, the consequences will be unimaginable. Ansel must be aware of this. I have always believed… he must have prepared for this.</p>
<p>Because he clearly does not have an urgent need for the pact head, he could spend a year, no, four years slowly taming me, indicating he is not in a hurry to increase his power.</p>
<p>I wonder how he plans to deal with this. Will I have time to participate and offer him assistance?</p>
<p>…</p>
<p>[Day Fifty]</p>
<p>I began to attempt to influence the memories of the outside me.</p>
<p>In three years, the soul fragments created by Lord Flamelle for the outside me are about to dissipate, and her memories will start to uncontrollably synchronize with mine from three years ago.</p>
<p>Due to Lord Flamelle's impeccable creation... as long as enough soul fragments are supplemented, the outside me is indeed myself, a completely independent soul. Even though I have studied numerous soul secrets over these three years, to influence the outside me silently and without Ansel noticing, I had to wait until now.</p>
<p>I succeeded... I can control the order of her memory awakening to some extent and even insert fabricated memories of my own.</p>
<p>Excellent, this way, I can ensure everything proceeds smoothly.</p>
<p>However, something unexpected happened.</p>
<p>Suellen... she approached me, telling me she knows the cause of grandfather's death, and in exchange, I need to help her deal with Evora.</p>
<p>Grandfather...</p>
<p>Ansel, ideals, the Tower of Babel, and grandfather's death... these might be the four things driving me to continue living.</p>
<p>...the outside me would not pass up this opportunity, nor would I now, but could this be Ansel's trap?</p>
<p>...</p>
<p>The outside me confessed this matter to Ansel, the most reasonable and correct choice, but she remained extremely cautious and did not ask Ansel why grandfather died, believing the cost of obtaining this information was too high.</p>
<p>Three years ago, I did not ask either... but now it seems, it was because while living with Ansel, I let go of many obsessions, focusing solely on him and life, no longer troubled by past sorrows and pains.</p>
<p>Not because... seeking answers from Ansel would come at a great cost.</p>
<p>How absurd, yet so reasonable... the outside me and Ansel have completely lost trust in each other.</p>
<p>…</p>
<p>[Day Seventy-two.]</p>
<p>Ansel has a maid... no, should I say an attendant? Secretary? That girl named Marlina, Seraphina's sister, gave my external self a hint.</p>
<p>She said Ansel has some kind of hidden trouble.</p>
<p>Of course, I know this, I am more aware than anyone in this world that Ansel must have hidden troubles, but... why would she tell the outside me this?</p>
<p>Marlina said she learned this from Ansel's mother, Madam Annelisa. Why would Ansel's mother... tell this to an ordinary person?</p>
<p>Fate... are you meddling, deliberately pushing things forward?</p>
<p>Assuming this entity truly exists, I have discovered too many things over these three years that could be caused by It, imperceptible while within, but as an observer, I can vaguely capture the "deliberateness."</p>
<p>If fate is pushing my external self to be tamed by Ansel, deliberately pushing for what both Ansel and I desire, then is it beneficial for fate if Ansel successfully tames the outside me, or is it beneficial for fate… if I succeed?</p>
<p>...Too difficult, even a slight contemplation, and I can feel Ansel's pain.</p>
<p>Facing such a thing, whom can he trust?</p>
<p>He cannot even trust himself.</p>
<p>The outside me, due to Marlina's words, has already sensed the blurriness and fragmentation of memories, further doubting the truth of those years, further falling into Ansel's trap.</p>
<p>I do not know what Ansel will do, nor what fate intends, but I must... I must continue.</p>
<p>…</p>
<p>[Day Eighty.]</p>
<p>I began investigating the cause of my grandfather's death, and Ansel intervened. Was this part of his plan? I do not know.</p>
<p>... Leiden, my father, had visited my grandfather's old residence.</p>
<p>He couldn't possibly be the murderer; neither his motive nor his capability aligns with such an act. So, was he there to pay his respects?</p>
<p>I haven't seen him in so long. To me, he is merely a meaningless term, and even now, I harbor resentment towards him, the outside me's dissatisfaction and hatred for him have only grown.</p>
<p>The outside me conversed with my parents... How strange, I could feel that they seemed to care for me. Their concerns didn't seem feigned, yet their betrayal of my grandfather was real. What is the truth?</p>
<p>I never considered them because they deemed me abnormal, but my grandfather saw me as the hope for the future. How could I possibly acknowledge them under such circumstances? But why would they...?</p>
<p>Ansel interrupted my conversation with my parents. He wouldn't do something so meaningless. Could it be that my parents truly care for me?</p>
<p>No, I must not be swayed by such thoughts. If I want answers, I will confront them once everything is over. They can wait for me, but Ansel cannot wait for anyone else.</p>
<p>... He did it to me again.</p>
<p>The outside me became increasingly emotional after just a few meetings with Ansel. He is melting the outside me, just like three years ago.</p>
<p>Only with him do I truly feel alive.</p>
<p>But... never mind.</p>
<p>Puppet again. What is he thinking? Why place my body on a puppet? If you have the ability—</p>
<p>… No, it won't work. The outside me got ahead, and I can only watch helplessly. I can't accept this.</p>
<p>…</p>
