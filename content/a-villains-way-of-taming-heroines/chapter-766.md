<p>As for who would be blamed as the culprit, it didn't matter - remnants of the Duke of Bloodust's faction, a crazed organization pursuing power, or something else entirely... Regardless, Marlina had made preparations. Whether through fabrication or framing, she could use this opportunity to elevate Hydral's reputation even further.</p>
<p>This farce had come to such a perfect conclusion. Ansel didn't need to pay a great price to obtain everything he needed.</p>
<p>He remained that blazing, bright sun.</p>
<p>Marlina quietly gazed at Ansel's back, her dark eyes filled with deep affection and tenderness.</p>
<p>This should have been the duty of all Pact Heads, but now only she could fulfill it.</p>
<p>However, that didn't matter. For Marlina, there couldn't be better news in this world.</p>
<p>"The end..."</p>
<p>She suddenly heard Ansel murmur.</p>
<p>"But I just told Venna that this is only the beginning. Do you know why, Marlina?"</p>
<p>"..." Marlina, still gazing at Ansel's back, was silent for about two seconds before responding gently, "Because you plan to lead the Empire into a new phase. You're about to usher in a... great era, aren't you?"</p>
<p>"Correct."</p>
<p>Ansel laughed, his laughter bright and cheerful. Marlina hadn't heard him laugh like this in a long time.</p>
<p>The young Hydral turned around, looking at this woman who was willing to sacrifice and dedicate everything for him, and asked:</p>
<p>"Do you know how much lifespan you have left, Marlina?"</p>
<p>"About five months, I suppose."</p>
<p>Marlina supported her cheek with her hand, smiling somewhat helplessly. "I guess I'm still that mediocre me after all. I'm sorry, Mr. Ansel."</p>
<p>"Since you're willing to dedicate everything to me, don't you think it's an unforgivable sin to waste the power of a pact head like this?" Ansel raised an eyebrow slightly. "Or are you confident you can find a way to slow down the erosion in these five months?"</p>
<p>"No."</p>
<p>A confident smile spread across the woman's face, making even those seemingly eerie black markings appear beautiful.</p>
<p>"I'm confident that within five months, I can clear all obstacles for Mr. Ansel and pave your path to the supreme."</p>
<p>"...Now that's a far more audacious idea than extending your life."</p>
<p>"Hmm, it's also possible that..." Marlina placed her index finger on her chin, pondering, "it's because I believe Mr. Ansel certainly won't abandon me."</p>
<p>"I clearly have no need for someone like you," the young Hydral said with a smile.</p>
<p>"Yet you believe I won't abandon you."</p>
<p>The ebony witch glided towards Ansel, raising her left hand, her countenance equally radiant with a clever smile. "Mr. Ansel may not need a foolish woman like me, but surely he requires the Head of Devouring, does he not?"</p>
<p>Her obsidian eyes seemed to convey: Disregard my identity, my significance, my sense of self. Simply... command and utilize me as the pure Head of Devouring.</p>
<p>You need such an individual, and that individual can only be me.</p>
<p>As Ansel met those eyes, he could sense the aether within him surging like an ocean, yet his heart remained tranquil, even finding the situation somewhat... absurd.</p>
<p>He had never imagined there would come a day when he'd be propelled forward by another.</p>
<p>—What choice to make? Since that moment of ultimate despair, Ansel had ceased to hesitate.</p>
<p>He must conquer fate, not merely for himself, not only for his father and mother, not solely for the... happier life he yearned for.</p>
<p>But for the purest form of vengeance!</p>
<p>The fiend from the abyss had never taken to heart such nonsense as "revenge is hollow."</p>
<p>Let go and pursue the life I desire?</p>
<p>But what if this vengeance is an integral part of the life I yearn for?</p>
<p>You want me to choose? Why should I, what right do you have to demand that I submit like a docile lamb, why should I... make a choice at all!</p>
<p>It is you who has always been at the end of your rope, not I. Don't get it twisted.</p>
<p>"Marlina," Ansel suddenly chuckled.</p>
<p>"Do you know what someone just called me?"</p>
<p>Marlina tilted her head. "What?"</p>
<p>"He said I'm a hypocritical monster, a malevolent devil, that I'll forever be a monster from the abyss, never capable of becoming a true human."</p>
<p>After a brief silence, Marlina softly replied, "There are always those who presume to understand you, Mr. Ansel. I can't comment on such ridiculous statements; I only wonder if the one who uttered those words has paid the price."</p>
<p>"Indeed, there are always those who think they understand me," Ansel mused, his gaze fixed on Marlina with an enigmatic smile.</p>
<p>The woman, however, responded with unwavering confidence, "But I will never be one to presume, because—"</p>
<p>Her words suddenly halted.</p>
<p>Because... without warning, Ansel revealed his true form.</p>
<p>The Hydral, now possessing five vibrant heads, stood at the zenith, surveying the world below, exuding an endless, awe-inspiring majesty that made one tremble.</p>
<p>"The recent calamities in the Western Lands have led me to a realization," the overlapping, deep voices resonated across... the entire sky of the West!</p>
<p>"Be it destruction or salvation, all disasters in the Empire stem from and culminate in the extraordinary."</p>
<p>"To end this chaos, the extraordinary must be constrained."</p>
<p>"Someone must judge the extraordinary with laws and rules, someone must restrict the extraordinary with power and violence."</p>
<p>"Someone must become the absolute measure."</p>
<p>"And that someone can only be me."</p>
<p>"Laws, rules, power, violence—all of these must be embodied in me, Ansel of Hydral."</p>
<p>As countless extraordinary beings and mortals raised their heads, gazing bewilderedly at the sky, the sovereign from the abyss impassively issued his decree.</p>
<p>"From this day forth, I shall rule the Empire."</p>
<p>"The extraordinary shall be under my dominion."</p>
<p>As Ansel made this proclamation to the world, he felt an unprecedented exhilaration.</p>
<p>Returning to his human form, he looked at the utterly stunned Marlina, who was at a loss for words, and said with a beaming smile, "Did you anticipate I would do this?"</p>
<p>"You..." Marlina's lips trembled. "Why did you... why would you do this..."</p>
