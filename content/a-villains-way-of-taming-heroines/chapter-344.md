<p>"Your timing is wonderful, Venna, come and assist me."</p>
<p>Anse's voice was light and airy, a stark contrast to the calm he had maintained during his conversation with Annelisa. Ravenna approached silently, noticing that he was meticulously carving an ether circuit.</p>
<p>"I've just conceived a rather intriguing notion."</p>
<p>The young Hydral looked up, glanced at Ravenna with a smile, and said warmly, "But I suppose the practical aspects are not quite my forte; it would be better suited for you."</p>
<p>"You are free to use the equipment here. Let me first explain what I intend to do—I think—"</p>
<p>"Ansel," Ravenna interjected softly, halting his speech.</p>
<p>"Hmm? What is it?" Ansel raised an eyebrow slightly.</p>
<p>"Could you do me a favor?"</p>
<p>Ravenna lifted her gaze, locking eyes with Ansel.</p>
<p>"This is indeed... unusual."</p>
<p>Ansel expressed his surprise, "The cautious you, who would avoid asking for my help for fear of incurring a debt you cannot pay, is now seeking my assistance? But of course... I am more than willing."</p>
<p>He laughed cheerfully, "What is it? Surely it's not about your grandfather's affairs? Be mindful, the cost of that is beyond your means."</p>
<p>"No, not at the moment... it's not necessary," Ravenna shook her head, "I wish for you to design a weapon."</p>
<p>Under Ansel's slightly frozen gaze, miss puppet stated calmly:</p>
<p>"A weapon capable of slaying a deity."</p>
<p>"..."</p>
<p>In the alchemy workshop, a profound silence fell between the two.</p>
<p>After what seemed an eternity, Ansel burst into laughter, "Slay a deity? Can you really craft such a thing, Venna? That's not like the civilian alchemical devices you've made before, nor is it like the firearms that can be mass-produced. The Etheric Academy only managed to create mechanical armor with the support of Her Majesty, possessing the power of a fifth-stage warrior...</p>
<p>What made you think of creating such a thing?"</p>
<p>"And besides, don't you detest making weapons the most?"</p>
<p>"But now, it is necessary," Ravenna replied.</p>
<p>"What necessity?"</p>
<p>"The necessity to kill a deity."</p>
<p>The petite scholar gazed intently into Ansel's eyes, her gaze unwavering.</p>
<p>"If there's a necessity, I will act upon it, and as for whether I can achieve it... for me, it's merely a matter of time."</p>
<p>To slay a god, to kill an emperor.</p>
<p>Such a reckless, absurd, laughable, foolish decision, Ravenna made without a moment's hesitation.</p>
<p>From a rational standpoint, the empress might well be the ultimate barrier to her ideals; emotionally, she had too many reasons to continue standing by Ansel's side.</p>
<p>...Even if he neither needed nor cared for it.</p>
<p>Ravenna did not know whether this was atonement or something else; she only knew that it was the right thing to do.</p>
<p>"You are truly confident…"</p>
<p>Ansel tilted his head slightly to regard Ravenna. Despite her outwardly indifferent demeanor, her resolve was unmistakably clear.</p>
<p>He spoke with a serene tone, "But it's beyond my capabilities to create a weapon that can slay a deity; such an artifact is out of my reach. Although I'm uncertain of your intentions, abandon such delusions, Ravenna."</p>
<p>Ansel was, of course, fully aware of her implications. His casual refusal was simply an affirmation of his stance—I have no need for you.</p>
<p>As he had stated, "Abandon such delusions."</p>
<p>It was only to be expected. Who would readily accept the aid of a traitor? Especially when Ravenna, in her current state, lacked any such privilege.</p>
<p>As for ability, who can truly articulate its bounds?</p>
<p>Ravenna fell silent for a moment before inquiring, "Then, what is it that you require now?"</p>
<p>"..."</p>
<p>Ansel glanced at the object of his recent attention, fell silent for a brief spell, and then a smile unexpectedly graced his features: "I no longer require your assistance. What I need from you is an answer—what did my mother impart to you just now, Venna?"</p>
<p>Ravenna paused, momentarily taken aback. Lawrence's warning still echoed in her ears, and she hadn't anticipated its relevance so soon.</p>
<p>If it's Ansel who's asking, then there's no issue... The woman's voice trailed off into silence before she responded, "Madam informed me that you possess..."</p>
<p>"Unspeakable tribulations, an immense burden, and you find me in a most precarious situation, thus I require your earnest support—is that it?"</p>
<p>The young Hydral spoke with a nonchalant air.</p>
<p>"...Yes."</p>
<p>Ravenna was stunned, not expecting such a reaction from Ansel.</p>
<p>"And what about you?" Ansel countered, "Do you believe what she told you?"</p>
<p>Ravenna gazed into his clear yet unfathomable azure eyes and replied softly, "I have no reason to disbelieve."</p>
<p>"None at all? I think there are plenty. I deceived you in advance, betrayed you, and you are a victim. From that standpoint, you have no reason to empathize with me, do you?"</p>
<p>"Or is it..." Ansel tilted his head slightly, his tone laced with derision, "that the most impossible has occurred? You believe that I am of greater significance than all that you uphold?"</p>
<p>Observing Ravenna's silent demeanor, Ansel spoke with a tinge of boredom, "Of course, the impossible remains just that. If you truly changed in such a way, you wouldn't be Ravenna, would you?"</p>
<p>"But suppose, just suppose, I could be of assistance to you." Ravenna's voice took on an urgent tone, "If the threat looming over you were to be resolved, you wouldn't have to—"</p>
<p>"What do you consider yourself to be, my dear Venna?"</p>
<p>Ansel expressed his astonishment, "A god within the alchemy field capable of surpassing my father? The fifth divine species? With what means do you propose to assist me? And..."</p>
<p>"What right do you have to assist me?"</p>
<p>Ravenna, you forfeited such a right three years ago.</p>
<p>Those eyes imbued with a chilling detachment, etching these harsh words deep into Ravenna's heart.</p>
<p>Ravenna yearned to speak, yet the moment her gaze met Ansel's, all words were choked back, unable to find their way out.</p>
<p>When she was almost without hesitation ready to commit to aiding Ansel, he, with an air of indifference, rejected her.</p>
<p>Was this punishment? No, it was the inevitable cost.</p>
