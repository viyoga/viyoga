<p>The city of Red Frost, and indeed the entire Red Frost territory, had not seen such liveliness in a long time.</p>
<p>This land, though not inherently barren, had been rendered desolate under the oppressive rule of successive lords. Even Red Frost city, the capital, did not seem particularly prosperous.</p>
<p>However, everything changed from that day onwards.</p>
<p>— The day when a headless corpse was hung at the entrance of a mansion beneath the Hydral manor.</p>
<p>From then on, everything in Red Frost city began to fall into place, everything became orderly. The sanitation conditions in the outer city visibly improved, the crowds and traffic in the inner city gradually became incessant, merchants from other territories waited for opportunities, and the nobles of the north began to reassess this land.</p>
<p>"Red Frost is improving," every resident of Red Frost city would agree.</p>
<p>Even the poorest laborers in the outer city began to harbor hopes for the future.</p>
<p>Raul was a stonemason, a skill passed down through his family. Because his village was becoming increasingly impoverished and the young and strong were gradually leaving, he had no choice but to leave his aging mother and young brother to seek a livelihood.</p>
<p>Even a countryman like him knew that in this ravaged land, Red Frost city, stationed under the Hydral mansion , held infinite hope.</p>
<p>Raul crossed the snowfields and traversed the dense forests with great difficulty, finally reaching this city, which once had a notorious reputation in the eyes of the people of Red Frost territory.</p>
<p>When he entered Red Frost city, he found it incredible.</p>
<p>Not because Red Frost city was majestic and prosperous.</p>
<p>On his way to Red Frost territory, Raul passed through several cities. He had no privilege to glimpse the scenes of the inner city or upper city areas. He, who could only stay in the most rundown inns, had seen all kinds of people, most of whom were either cold and dangerous or dark and numb. This was the appearance of the most bottom-level, hopeless people.</p>
<p>But here, in the outer city of Red Frost... these poor people, who were no different from the poor in other cities, wearing tattered cotton clothes, with vivid frostbite on their hands and faces, and dry, cracked skin, were brimming with a…vitality that Raul could not understand.</p>
<p>It was the kind of vitality that only those who are truly alive and still have expectations for life would show.</p>
<p>They may not be friendly, they may not be happy, but they are truly people who will think about the "future".</p>
<p>To think about this, the most luxurious thing for the poor who could freeze or starve to death at any time.</p>
<p>"Is this... the greatness of that lord?" Raul exclaimed with immense admiration and began to look for a small inn to stay in.</p>
<p>He went around and around, almost running through the entire outer city... but couldn't find a single inn to stay in.</p>
<p>— Because they were all full!</p>
<p>"Don't you know?"</p>
<p>The owner of the last inn looked at the dusty young man with some surprise, "Lord Hydral is going to give a speech in the central square today, the inns in the inner city were full yesterday! Some rich people even had to stay in our shabby inns, hahaha--"</p>
<p>The middle-aged owner cheered heartily, "Long live Lord Hydral!"</p>
<p>In the narrow and dirty lobby of the inn, several guys wearing tattered cotton jackets also cheered, "Long live Lord Hydral!"</p>
<p>"But..." Raul smiled bitterly, "Is there really no place to stay? This weather is not something everyone can bear."</p>
<p>The owner shrugged, "Maybe you can try your luck at the church, the Holy Church may not have much influence in the north, but we still have a church here, maybe the priest will take you in for the night."</p>
<p>The owner's suggestion made Raul overjoyed. Although he grew up in the countryside, he had learned about the church from many people who had returned from outside, and thought it was a good idea.</p>
<p>"Thank you! I'll give it a try!" T</p>
<p>he simple and kind young man bowed slightly to the owner in thanks, then quickly turned and ran out.</p>
<p>"Hey, kid, wait! The big cold wave is coming in a few days, don't you want to book a room in my place now--"</p>
<p>"...He runs fast."</p>
<p>Seeing a potential regular customer gone, the owner couldn't help but purse his lips.</p>
<p>"Hey, Lorca."</p>
<p>Sitting on the old sofa in the lobby, a man in a tattered cotton jacket grinned, "Can you give me a room to get through this big cold wave? My house is too drafty."</p>
<p>"Can you afford the coal fee?" Lorca crossed his arms, "If you don't pay, you might as well die outside."</p>
<p>"You heartless bastard!" The man in the tattered cotton jacket cursed, but soon started laughing again, "I'll bring my own coal, you don't have to worry about it, how about it?"</p>
<p>His friends, who all looked quite poor, started laughing together.</p>
<p>"...What do you mean? You didn't steal from that big shot, did you?" Loka became alert, "Don't think about dragging me down!"</p>
<p>"We heard something big from Lambert, you know, the most well-informed Lambert."</p>
<p>The poor man who should have been worried about his life was so confident and happy, "Want to know? If you want to know, leave a room for me!"</p>

<p>Raul entered the spacious church, apprehensively surveying his surroundings.</p>
<p>"Greetings, young man," a deep, amiable voice resonated behind him. "What brings you here?"</p>
<p>Startled, Raul hastily turned around, stumbling over his words, "I... I harbor no ill intentions, I merely—" "</p>
<p>Relax," the black-robed priest smiled. "No thief would cower as you do. Is this your first time in a church?"</p>
<p>"Ah... yes."</p>
<p>Raul's gaze fell upon the pendant on the priest's chest—a serpent biting its own tail, forming a circle.</p>
<p>He recognized it as the emblem of the Holy Church.</p>
<p>"There's no need for formality; we don't demand reverence from everyone."</p>
<p>The priest's voice was soothing. "So, do you require assistance, young man?"</p>
<p>"I..." Raul hesitated, then asked awkwardly, "Might I be permitted to... stay here for the night?"</p>
<p>"Of course."</p>
<p>The priest agreed without hesitation. "Though we have no spare lodgings, God tells me you are worthy of shelter within the church."</p>
<p>Raul was bewildered by the priest's cryptic words but was delighted to have a place to stay. Though unsure of the church's insulation, at least he wouldn't be exposed to the elements.</p>
<p>Moreover, Raul harbored a secret—for certain reasons, he wasn't particularly fearful of snowstorms and frigid temperatures.</p>
<p>Subconsciously touching the pendant on his chest, he bowed gratefully to the priest. "Thank you for your generosity."</p>
<p>"I accept on God's behalf," the priest replied with a smile.</p>
<p>With temporary shelter secured, Raul's spirits lifted. With nothing else to do, he followed the priest, curiously examining the church while inquiring, "Father, is it just you in this vast church?"</p>
<p>"There's also a monk, a nun, and six children," the priest replied.</p>
<p>"However, they've all gone to the central square."</p>
<p>"The central square..." Raul paused, then realized, "Is it because of Lord Hydral's speech?"</p>
<p>"Naturally."</p>
<p>The priest's matter-of-fact tone prompted Raul to nod in agreement. "After all, it's the esteemed Lord Hydral... He's truly an extraordinary figure! I never imagined Red Frost City would be like this, as if everyone has found hope."</p>
<p>"Hope," the priest paused, uttering in a tone Raul couldn't comprehend, "Hope is often merely an illusion crafted by the devil."</p>
<p>"…What did you say?"</p>
<p>Raul's mind stalled for a moment, his expression gradually turning apprehensive.</p>
<p>He didn't want the priest's words to mean what he thought they did.</p>
<p>"Nothing, just a spontaneous remark," the priest chuckled. "</p>
<p>Young man, why don't you go watch the speech? Judging by the time, it should be starting soon."</p>
<p>"What?!"</p>
<p>Raul exclaimed, nearly dropping his luggage. "It's starting? I... I intended to watch it, but I didn't think it would be so soon!"</p>
<p>"Could you please watch my luggage, Father?"</p>
<p>The young man's expression was anxious. After all, who in Red Frost territory wouldn't yearn for the great Lord Hydral?</p>
<p>"Go ahead, and if possible, share your thoughts afterward," the priest took Raul's luggage, smiling.</p>
<p>"God seems to have some interest in this."</p>

She had requested three bottles of energy potions and nutrient fluids from Ansel, enough to keep her physically healthy for a whole month without sleep.</p>

<p>"Is this your plea, Marlina?"</p>

<p>— She was born to bask in the thunderous applause and adoration.</p>

<p>"Hydral!!!"</p>

<p>"Hydral..."</p>

He began to mingle with the self-proclaimed great nobles, picking up and learning the stupid rules he had once despised, following those old, stale, and rotten things day after day, year after year.</p>

If he couldn't even do such small things, how could he sacrifice bigger things and face a more brutal reality?</p>

<p>Ansel looked at the restless commoners, his voice suddenly calm.</p>
<p>"But I won't say—just work hard, and everything will change."</p>

<p>"If fate makes you kneel, you should respond to it in my name—I will never kneel!"</p>
<p>"Because Ansel of Hydral commands me to stand up!"</p>

<p>No one spoke, no one dared to speak again, whether they were fanatics, or those who called him a devil or a god, no one dared to say even a word, make a sound.</p>
<p>Ansel staggered back a couple of steps, somewhat drained, he used his scepter to support his body, after a brief silence, he bowed slightly:</p>
<p>"This is all of me, all of Ansel of Hydral."</p>
<p>He turned around without hesitation and walked down the podium.</p>
<p>The rational him savored his mad declaration just now, and couldn't help but chuckle.</p>
<p>It was... unfortunate.</p>
<p>Just at this time, just at this moment, just at this node, he made such a speech in such a fierce and fanatical manner.</p>
