<p>[July 21st.]</p>
<p>Ansel seems to have reached the limits of his patience.</p>
<p>He is now resorting to a clumsy, indirect manner of questioning, something he would never have done before, to ask whether I will follow him or pursue my ideals.</p>
<p>Ansel... you already know my answer.</p>
<p>Yet, from the beginning, you have not trusted me, nor believed in that possibility.</p>
<p>Have you confined me within some preconceived framework? Stubbornly using the information you possess, what image have you molded of me in your mind?</p>
<p>...No, it is not entirely your fault. If it were not for my presumptuousness, my secret attempts to uncover your mysteries, my desire to help you—if I had confessed earlier, perhaps the image you hold of me would be much better.</p>
<p>But doing so would mean losing my only chance, my sole opportunity to assist you.</p>
<p>Because you trust no one, including me, you would never... reveal your secrets to anyone.</p>
<p>It is too late to confess now; in your eyes, I have already become the person you believe me to be.</p>
<p>I do not know what lies at the end of this path, but I will continue to walk it.</p>
<p>As for the reason, you certainly would not like it now.</p>
<p>[July 25th.]</p>
<p>I feel weary, weary of deceiving Ansel, weary of the truths obtained through this deception.</p>
<p>Now, I realize... Ansel has fallen into a kind of madness.</p>
<p>He clearly craves external help, yet he rejects everyone with extreme prejudice; he longs to pour out his inner suffering, yet he will never speak of it to anyone.</p>
<p>He wants me to be his friend, yet he also desires an absolutely loyal servant.</p>
<p>I can sense his growing torment; perhaps he feels the time for decision-making and revealing his hand to me is approaching.</p>
<p>All of this, perhaps, is just my wishful thinking. Maybe things are not so complicated; maybe if I side with you, you will be much better.</p>
<p>But that would only be temporary... How could something that causes such agony to a divine species be resolved by a mere word or a slight change in my attitude?</p>
<p>Without finding the root of the problem, Ansel will inevitably fall into such pain again.</p>
<p>I lack the courage, the qualification... to bear his entirety.</p>
<p>I am too weak, and besides, the notion of "life and death together" in words does not suit me.</p>
<p>Ansel's madness... perhaps it is akin to my own obsession.</p>
<p>The difference is, I am acutely aware that my obsession is indeed abnormal, yet I am willing to sacrifice everything for it. But Ansel seems never to have realized he is ensnared in such madness.</p>
<p>This madness... must have a source.</p>
<p>I lost my grandfather, but what about Ansel?</p>
<p>Has he suffered betrayal? Could a single betrayal wound Ansel to such an extent? Was it a friend's, a relative's betrayal?</p>
<p>Or perhaps it was not betrayal, but an event unacceptable to him, much like the despair my grandfather's death brought to me.</p>
<p>I too... have not been able to move on for a very long time.</p>
<p>Regardless, I will continue.</p>
<p>I am sorry, Ansel, that even now, I have not been able to do anything for you, merely chasing the truth under the guise of righteousness.</p>
<p>[August 10th.]</p>
<p>Amidst a tempestuous downpour, Ansel confronted me with a stark revelation.</p>
<p>I inquired about his motives, seeking to understand the reasons behind his abandonment and outright disdain for our shared ideals.</p>
<p>His response was "no reason".</p>
<p>He confessed that everything was a fabrication designed to ensnare me: the ideals, the future we envisioned, the camaraderie we shared... all were but lures in his elaborate deception.</p>
<p>He even resorted to previously prepared tactics to underscore this bitter truth.</p>
<p>You needn't have expressed it so, Ansel... You could have, with great anguish, assured me that none of it was a lie, vehemently denouncing my baseness, yet you chose silence.</p>
<p>Even at this juncture... did you wish to spare me from a dilemma?</p>
<p>Ansel...Ansel, I claim to understand you better than anyone else in this world.</p>
<p>Your loves and hates, your joys and angers, your hopes and sorrows.</p>
<p>For nearly half a year, you have been my entire world.</p>
<p>How then, can you expect me to believe that all was mere falsehood?</p>
<p>I am acutely aware, more so than anyone, of the silent suffering you've borne for so long.</p>
<p>Gazing into his eyes, I discerned a chilling void and profound loss.</p>
<p>He must now be… blaming me, wondering why I couldn't concede even slightly, why I never bowed to him, why, in my eyes, he could never measure up to my ideals.</p>
<p>In aiding you, I was merely furthering my own ideal, as if to justify my actions as nothing more than utilitarian, right?</p>
<p>But doesn't this revelation speak volumes...</p>
<p>Aren't you, Ansel, the embodiment of my ideals?</p>
<p>With talents, insights, and visions surpassing mine, sharing the same aspirations, hopes, and desires... why should I chase after the cold and distant unknown, forsaking you, who stands right beside me?</p>
<p>You could have contemplated these thoughts, yet you refrained. You even withheld the reasons for abandoning our shared dream.</p>
<p>This is why I couldn't bare my soul to you.</p>
<p>You trust neither me nor anyone else, relying solely on conclusions drawn from your obsessive reasoning, trusting only what you deem to be objective analysis. Even though I stand with you, even though you consider me a friend, you wouldn't confide the root of your torment to me.</p>
<p>This mistrust isn't your fault, nor are your delusions and obsessions... You are merely a victim, Ansel.</p>
<p>Crucially, even with this knowledge, I am powerless to effect change.</p>
<p>I need... time.</p>
<p>Fortunately, Ansel's proposition, likely a groundwork for subsequent taming efforts, aligns perfectly with my needs.</p>
<p>[August 11th/]</p>
<p>The day of soul division is imminent.</p>
<p>I must make my final preparations.</p>
<p>The version of me that is created... cannot fully possess the memories of my current self, nor the understanding and knowledge I have of Ansel.</p>
<p>For the Ansel who has been with me for the past six months is still a child, but once the storm subsides, he will no longer be... He will become more cold and indifferent due to my betrayal, and more perceptive of the truth.</p>
<p>I may no longer be able to deceive him.</p>
<p>Moreover, only when the part of me that so despises and hates him is completely tamed, will he be truly at ease.</p>
<p>This is absolutely necessary.</p>
<p>Thus... there is only one choice—to erase these memories.</p>
<p>Erase all my thoughts, considerations, concerns, and emotions about Ansel... leaving only hatred and loathing.</p>
<p>Erasing is not difficult; the destruction of memory is indeed simple, but preservation...</p>
<p>The only medium that Ansel will never discover, to preserve these memories, is the nascent, unstable data system.</p>
<p>Once my memories are destroyed within it, everything will be over, and Ansel and I will be locked in a death struggle, with him ultimately gaining a servant of absolute loyalty... which he certainly does not desire.</p>
<p>A gamble... I have never done something so foolish, but I have no choice. Even Lord Flamelle might detect my memory erasure at any moment, but I must proceed. There is no such thing as absolute success in this world.</p>
<p>This is my only chance to uncover the truth, and I must not miss it.</p>
<p>This opportunity, Ansel's plan, has given me time, the capital to grow stronger, and the space to maneuver.</p>
<p>So perfect, perfect as if... it were destined.</p>
<p>It is as if fate has granted me this chance.</p>
<p>Fate...</p>
<p>Though unlikely, and I dare not believe it, but... is it you?</p>
<p>Are you the one tormenting Ansel, destroying his ideals, making him so lonely, so insecure?</p>
<p>To oppose fate is a... numbing endeavor. Can such an ethereal thing truly exist?</p>
<p>And if it does, if my actions are part of your design, it does not matter.</p>
<p>I will fight you in the darkness until one of us is utterly vanquished.</p>
<p>I will not let you destroy my ideals.</p>
<p>I will not let you destroy my Ansel, my friend.</p>
<p>*</p>
<p>In a confined space devoid of anything but a table and bed, Ravenna Ziegler silently set down her glasses, which glimmered with a fleeting light. Beside her lay her most treasured notebook, and on the open page was a string of... inexplicable characters.</p>
<p>These were codes that only she could decipher, serving as the key to unlocking the memories she had encrypted and sealed within the data system.</p>
<p>"So, this is it..."</p>
<p>The woman murmured, "This is the truth."</p>
<p>She had no doubt about the authenticity of these memories and instantly understood her mission here, without any hesitation.</p>
<p>"Three years."</p>
<p>Ravenna walked straight to the wall of the doorless chamber, effortlessly passing through it to the underground library of the Imperial Capital's Hydral Manor.</p>
<p>"You have three years, Ravenna."</p>
<p>Gazing at the countless hidden treasures that could drive the world mad, Ravenna Ziegler whispered:</p>
<p>"Three years is enough."</p>
<p>She retreated back into the secret chamber, where a crystal on the desk continuously projected an image onto the wall.</p>
<p>In the image was another version of herself.</p>
<p>Unaware of everything, yet... crucially important.</p>
<p>This version harbored only suspicion and hatred towards Ansel, lacking the vital memories and emotions...</p>
<p>But precisely this was the "Ravenna" as seen through Ansel's framework.</p>
<p>"It's time to begin."</p>
<p>Ravenna Ziegler cast her gaze towards the future.</p>
<p>Here, she would further unravel the mysteries surrounding Ansel, probe into his hidden pains, and understand the source of his incomprehensible obsession, while also amassing the necessary strength.</p>
<p>Even if it meant enduring three years of solitude in the darkness, unknown and unappreciated.</p>
<p>For Ravenna knew that in this world, the only person who could understand and help Ansel was herself.</p>
<p>She had never once thought of betraying her friend.</p>
