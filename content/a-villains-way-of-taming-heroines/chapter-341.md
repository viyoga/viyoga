<p>Annalisa remained silent, for she could have continued her usual coaxing or spoken oddities to compel Ansel to acquiesce, but she did not.</p>
<p>A mother stood there, silently gazing at her son for a long while, then spoke softly: "Very well, Ans, go and rest... However, might you lend me little Ziegler for a moment?"</p>
<p>"As you wish."</p>
<p>Ansel lowered his head slightly, without hesitation, and turned to depart, leaving Ravenna momentarily bewildered.</p>
<p>"He's still the same... What could be the matter?"</p>
<p>Annalisa, with a sideward tilt of her face, sighed softly: "Ans is troubled by something, and I am utterly unaware. Little Ziegler, do you think... Am I, as a mother, greatly remiss?"</p>
<p>"Madam, sorry, I...,"</p>
<p>"Oh, I'm sorry, asking you such a question would be difficult for you. I forgot."</p>
<p>The woman smiled gently: "Come with me, you must be curious why I kept you here. When I saw you, I recalled many things... I also have some words to share with you."</p>
<p>She unlatched the door and entered, Ravenna following closely behind, squinting instinctively as a burst of light struck her eyes.</p>
<p>Once Ravenna adjusted to the light and slowly opened her eyes, she was astounded by the sight before her.</p>
<p>Upon being transported to the verdant jungle by Evora, Ravenna was not as taken aback as she was now by the overwhelming, beautiful spectacle—a pure, exquisite impact.</p>
<p>Spread before her was an endless sea of flowers.</p>
<p>A true, ocean of blooms.</p>
<p>Diverse shapes and colors of blossoms stretched to the horizon, seemingly endless, and the sun suspended in the sky, initially blinding, now appeared perfectly radiant, illuminating each flower's vibrant hues.</p>
<p>Nearby, a wooden cabin stood, flanked by a long, hanging chair, both blending seamlessly with the surroundings.</p>
<p>So, Ansel resisted... this kind of place.</p>
<p>"In his youth, Mel often did things he believed to be romantic."</p>
<p>Annalisa strolled through the heart of the flower sea, her grand, flowing gown resembling the most vivid and beautiful blooms.</p>
<p>"Sometimes I wonder, did he not feel awkward doing such things?"</p>
<p>This woman, speaking thus, crouched down to inhale the fresh fragrance, hands cupping her chin, laughing softly: "Sometimes I also wonder, how could he be so charming to me."</p>
<p>"Then, I realized my heart was ensnared by him, and there was no escape."</p>
<p>Lady Hydral sighed, her face still sweetly smiling: "Ans inherited many of Mel's virtues—intelligence, confidence, bravery, decisiveness... but also inherited more flaws, among them, toying with girls' hearts, which he has surely mastered."</p>
<p>"Little Ziegler," Annelisa tilted her head towards Ravenna, "Is your heart ensnared by Ansel?"</p>
<p>"I..."</p>
<p>"You are not."</p>
<p>She answered for Ravenna.</p>
<p>As Miss puppet remained silent, Annelisa seated herself amidst the flower field, her fingertips emanating a faint glow. The flowers seemed to come alive upon touching the light, as if endowed with sentient will, joyously swaying around her fingers.</p>
<p>"I can discern, there are matters of greater importance reflected in your eyes."</p>
<p>"..."</p>
<p>"Ah, fear not, for in my eyes, this is a favorable sign."</p>
<p>Lady Hydral smiled, "Ansel's side requires someone like you, a girl who harbors a simple affection for him is utterly futile—though little Seraphina is an exception."</p>
<p>"Madam..." Ravenna, striving to regain her composure and ponder the current situation, whispered, "What are you implying? Is it concerning Ansel?"</p>
<p>"You are far more astute than little Seraphina," Annelisa slightly raised her eyebrows, "You remind me somewhat of little Marlina... Ah, no, there lies a fundamental difference between you and Marlina."</p>
<p>She beckoned Ravenna, "Come, sit beside me."</p>
<p>Ravenna obediently took her place next to Annelisa, who scrutinized her for a moment before gently pinching Miss puppet's cheek with a somewhat ambiguous expression.</p>
<p>"Although I am aware of Ans's wide array of interests, it's hard to imagine... the manner in which he approached you."</p>
<p>Following this jest, Annelisa adopted a more serious tone, softly inquiring Ravenna:</p>
<p>"Little Ziegler, what kind of person do you perceive Ans to be?"</p>
<p>"What kind of... person?"</p>
<p>"Do not ponder too long, share with me your initial impression."</p>
<p>"He is... a genius."</p>
<p>"Go on?"</p>
<p>"A genius, malevolent, adept at manipulating hearts, possessing a strong desire for control, but..."</p>
<p>Ravenna lowered her head, and after a brief silence of a second or two, her voice turned hoarse, struggling to continue:</p>
<p>"But indeed, he is the only one who can perceive the same vision as I do."</p>
<p>"Companion..."</p>
<p>Annelisa's eyes shimmered slightly, "Is this merely your unilateral perception, or has Ans... also acknowledged this?"</p>
<p>"...I do not know, Madam."</p>
<p>"Hmm?"</p>
<p>"Ansel, he refuted everything," Ravenna's voice gradually began to quiver, "He told me it was all a facade, a deception crafted to tame me, but... but I learned that he seems—"</p>
<p>"He seems to have been compelled to forsake... what was most important to him, correct?"</p>
<p>Annelisa gazed into Ravenna's eyes, those magnificent purple pupils brimming with turmoil and struggle.</p>
<p>"Little Marlina...is truly a girl who wholeheartedly considers Ansel's well-being, not inferior to little Seraphina in the slightest," she remarked with a sigh, "She disclosed this matter to you, did she not?"</p>
<p>"...Yes, Madam."</p>
<p>Subsequently, the two were enveloped in an indescribable silence.</p>
<p>Iit was only after an everlasting pause that Annelisa spoke again, her voice breaking the silence, "Little Ziegler, do you know? I never yearned for Ans to take you as his pact head, despite your qualifications, despite your capacity to be acknowledged by him, and your... convictions."</p>
<p>"But you... you betrayed him."</p>
<p>The words light as a feather yet striking Ravenna's heart with the weight of lead.</p>
<p>"Of course, my perspective is undoubtedly subjective. From your standpoint, it would be more accurate to say that Ans betrayed you."</p>
<p>The woman, caressing the petals, whispered softly, "But as a mother, how could I possibly remain impartial?"</p>
<p>"Do you know what I have witnessed, little Ziegler? I have seen nothing but my son, wounded grievously, suffering from a chasm within that beckons him to a deeper hell."</p>
<p>"Six years ago, when Ans was but ten, he underwent some transformation... I cannot articulate it, for the child was too astute, always veiling his emotions so well. All I knew was that he became reclusive… unwilling to trust others."</p>
<p>"It was three years prior when he attempted to subjugate you as his pact head. During this endeavor, that cunning child must have donned a disguise, deceiving you in many ways—a fact I will not deny."</p>
