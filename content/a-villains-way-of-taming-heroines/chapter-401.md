<p>"Leave, Evora," Ansel commanded, lifting his chin slightly, "Do not disturb my game; I am fond of it."</p>
<p>Ansel... he must want to change everything!</p>
<p>In the depths of utter despair, Ravenna Ziegler found the thread of hope that could lead her out of the endless maze, a dawn that pierces through all chaos.</p>
<p>With Ansel, if Ansel is there, then everything is possible—</p>
<p>"Wait... wait! Your Highness, Lord Elder Princess!"</p>
<p>At this moment, a discordant voice intruded upon the perfect situation twisted by Ansel.</p>
<p>Ravenna looked on in shock as she saw a stout man in utter disarray, stumbling towards Evora.</p>
<p>His expression was so excited, so fervent, and not even his charred, battered face could hide his passion at this moment.</p>
<p>Laurel Morlamo, how could he—</p>
<p>When Ravenna saw what the man was holding, her pupils shrinked to pinpoints.</p>
<p>It's the soil enhancement potion he has collected… now nearly depleted!</p>
<p>What is he doing... what is he planning!</p>
<p>"Despicable creature, leave... hmm?"</p>
<p>Evora, initially ready to incinerate the man with a mere glance, brightened up when she saw the bottles in his arms.</p>
<p>"This is..."</p>
<p>"This is the secret to changing everything!"</p>
<p>This man, endowed with exceptional wisdom, ability, strategy, patience... a talent who, as a mere mortal, is destined to excel in any field, remained unflappable even when facing Reginald as a farmer. Now, without hesitation, he kneeled before Evora.</p>
<p>With both hands, he presented the remaining potion, shouting with a trembling voice from excitement:</p>
<p>"Please allow me... please allow me to offer all of this to you!"</p>
<p>He said... what?</p>
<p>Ravenna's vacant, dim eyes reflected the image of Laurel, humble as dust.</p>
<p>He said he wanted to give the remaining potion... to Evora?</p>
<p>"Hahahaha…hahahahaha!"</p>
<p>The Elder Princess, in a fit of rage just moments ago, now laughed heartily with utmost delight:</p>
<p>"Ansel... Ansel! Is this your game? Is this the game you designed to please me?"</p>
<p>Meanwhile, Laurel strived to maintain his composure and added, "I implore your highness... please spare Pelican City, spare the Watson territory."</p>
<p>In Laurel's eyes, his plan was flawless, without any mistakes.</p>
<p>He was unaware of what Evora truly desired; to him, since the conflict between these two powerful figures revolved around "ruining the game," then naturally, Evora's only course of action would be destruction.</p>
<p>By speaking these words, he was not opposing Ansel; on the contrary, he was pleading with Evora.</p>
<p>As for the potion... it was clear that Lord Ansel valued the game itself far more, showing little concern for the potion.</p>
<p>Thus... this was his only, his last, his most perfect opportunity!</p>
<p>"Spare this place? Is that your request in offering this to me?"</p>
<p>Evora released the hand covering her wound, allowing the blood to flow freely, and now, as she smiled, there was a chilling, bewitching beauty about her.</p>
<p>"No, not just that... please forgive my greed, what I desire is..."</p>
<p>Laurel lifted his head, his eyes shining with a pure, brilliant light.</p>
<p>"—I wish to have the opportunity to become an extraordinary being!"</p>
<p>Extraordinary beings...</p>
<p>Ravenna's body swayed slightly.</p>
<p>He would trade the hope that could eliminate hunger, the hope that could satiate the entire empire, the entire world.</p>
<p>All for a chance... to become an extraordinary being?</p>
<p>Like a dog, to trade for such an opportunity?</p>
<p>"How greedy... but, excellent... excellent!"</p>
<p>Evora laughed heartily with satisfaction: "It is this kind of desire that I appreciate the most, the longing for extraordinary, for power... this is what I admire!"</p>
<p>"An... Ansel... no, father... father!"</p>
<p>Ravenna, who had not spoken for what seemed like an eternity, spoke with a rough, hoarse voice, gripping Ansel's arm tightly, her voice no longer possessing its usual indifference and calm.</p>
<p>"You can't... if Evora gets the potion, she will have complete control over the enhancement potion, and then everything will... will..."</p>
<p>Ansel remained silent, looking at her calmly, with pity.</p>
<p>Ravenna looked up into those sea-blue eyes, unable to utter a word.</p>
<p>She numbly released her grip and staggered two steps backward, nearly falling to the ground.</p>
<p>So, this is... his choice.</p>
<p>It was I who gave him the power to decide.</p>
<p>"No... it's not like that..."</p>
<p>It could have changed everything, it could have been good for everyone, Laurel, such a person... such people, they are just exceptions... only exceptions.</p>
<p>Without him, things wouldn't have turned out this way, without him, everything would—</p>
<p>"Ravenna, you once asked me if Laurel was a pawn of mine, and at that time, I did not give you an answer."</p>
<p>The young Hydral gripped Ravenna's neck, forcing her to look at the mortal kneeling on the ground, offering everything for the chance to become extraordinary, and he spoke deliberately:</p>
<p>"I did not, nor have I ever needed to arrange for such a person, do you know why?"</p>
<p>"Arrange... his arranged person? Hahaha, you are truly delusional, poor little puppet!"</p>
<p>Evora laughed heartily as she took all the potions and then casually tossed a bottle of black, viscous potion to Laurel, who clumsily catched it and clutched it to his chest as if it were his own child, bowing incessantly with all his might:</p>
<p>"Thank you for your mercy, thank you for your greatness, thank you for your magnanimity... Thank you, thank you for everything, Your Highness!"</p>
<p>Evora paid no heed to this worthless mortal, instead continuing to mock Ravenna: "If this damned bastard was so understanding, to arrange a character that could make me this happy... I would have cherished him long ago. But you... I really can't understand, how he could value someone as foolish as you, to deliberately arrange such a person... Hehehe... Hahahaha!"</p>
<p>The pinnacle of the extraordinary beings laughed uproariously:</p>
<p>"Any mortal, as long as they have a brain, will inevitably make this choice! Be it him or someone else, no matter who... it's always the same!"</p>
<p>Evora mocked with contempt and pleasure:</p>
<p>"Place these trinkets and the opportunity to become an extraordinary being before mortals, and out of ten thousand people, ten thousand would choose the latter."</p>
<p>"—This world is full of pitiful creatures… who yearn to become extraordinary!"</p>
