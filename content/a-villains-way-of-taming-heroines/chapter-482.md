<p>[Day Twelve.]</p>
<p>The mass production issue of the floating cannon was resolved. The outside me suspected that the previous inability to mass-produce was due to Ansel's interference... I also thought so, believing that Ansel wanted to further my downfall.</p>
<p>But it was too coincidental. I came to the Alchemy Association because of the resolution of the mass production issue, encountered Evora, and was ultimately incinerated... the whole process was too coincidental.</p>
<p>As an observer, I can see that this was Ansel's framing.</p>
<p>But that me couldn't, because she was blinded by the non-existent person... she believed that Ansel wanted to use the Etheric Academy to create mechanical armor, and that the non-existent person intervened to solve the mass production issue of the floating cannon to counter the mechanical armor.</p>
<p>Seeing the construction workshop of the mechanical armor at the Etheric Academy further validated this idea, making her believe even more in the existence of such a person.</p>
<p>Truly... a chilling design. In three years, Ansel has grown this ruthless.</p>
<p>If I were not an observer, the current me would also be unable to escape.</p>
<p>The final result, the outside me suffered Evora's incineration, the soul was damaged again, further solving the memory problem...</p>
<p>Ansel, are you really that interested in the puppet's body? Do you have to manipulate my emotions… in this way?</p>
<p>Although it is indeed effective, it is not likable. You really have no trace of that child left.</p>
<p>… You lewd and wicked thing.</p>
<p>[Day Twenty-Seven.]</p>

<p>Indeed, I was right. If I had sided with Ansel back then, losing the chance I have now, the possibility of seeking the truth, I would be unable to stop Ansel from continuing his descent into deeper cruelty and coldness.</p>
<p>It feels as if, without Ansel, I too am gradually slipping into that abyss of rational indifference and mechanical coldness.</p>
<p>He will destroy himself; I must stop him.</p>
<p>This game... I hope Ansel still retains even a shred of goodness, and does not bring disaster upon the civilians.</p>

<p>Is this the choice I would make?</p>
<p>If I... if I had never met Ansel, if I had just buried myself in research, from the time my grandfather died until now, always closed off, would I have become like her?</p>
<p>... I would.</p>
<p>I know very well that I would become like that, because three years ago, I already had this tendency, though it was far from reaching the current extent.</p>
<p>If not for Ansel, I would certainly have become this kind of... monster.</p>
<p>And Ansel must know that the outside me would make this choice, which is why he deliberately set this condition.</p>

<p>It is you who should save him.</p>
