<p>Whether it was physical cutting striking or magical burning freezing, whether it was soul-shattering or mental torment and erosion... any existence, any damage that could not kill Seraphina in a short time would ultimately become ineffective.</p>
<p>Although this effect would reset after the battle, preventing Seraphina from becoming invincible through self-abuse, it was already enough to cause despair.</p>
<p>And this was just one of the many powers she possessed as a result of her spiritual essence.</p>
<p>This was the spiritual essence that even the emperor praised and acknowledged, pushing the Sky Wolf Emperor to the pinnacle of power, known as —</p>
<p>The Beast King!</p>
<p>The moment her words fell, the fist raised high behind her swept forward, stirring up a spiraling tempest! This was not driven by any element, but merely by physical force, a storm raised by physical reasons, which astonishingly tore a wind path through the frigid storm spewed by the Blazing Ice Viper!</p>
<p>The wind pressure under the fist whipped up a brutal and ferocious destruction, the spiraling storm that even the Blazing Ice Viper, possessing wind elements, could not suppress, roared like a wild beast as it tore through the frosty breath!</p>
<p>In the eyes of the massive creature, a sense of fear emerged at this moment.</p>
<p>Seraphina's fist had not yet touched the belly of the Blazing Ice Viper, this cruel storm that seemed to have turned into a living entity, had already broken open, crushed, torn, and slaughtered the icy crystal body of the Blazing Ice Viper.</p>
<p>This seemingly hard body could not resist the wind of Seraphina's punch at all. Under the successive advances of the Fist of the Beast King, the wind had already drilled a huge crack in the belly of the Blazing Ice Viper, and Seraphina's fist, through this crack, went straight into the body of the Blazing Ice Viper!</p>
<p>In this instant, it seemed as if the world had fallen silent.</p>
<p>Then, there was a mournful scream that stretched for miles, making some of the onlookers unable to sit still.</p>
<p>"Who... is that guy?"</p>
<p>The man with the giant hammer stared blankly into the distance, at the Blazing Ice Viper that was writhing on the ground.</p>
<p>From their perspective, they could see this behemoth, over fifty meters long, almost split in two!</p>
<p>Literally, blasted into two pieces with one punch!</p>
<p>The terrifying power of Seraphina's punch directly hit the body of the Blazing Ice Viper, spreading the ultimate violence and destruction in all directions, almost shattering its entire body from the inside out into two sections!</p>
<p>The revolutionary army, who had been lying in ambush here, preparing to hunt the Blazing Ice Viper at the fastest speed, were not only shocked at the Blazing Ice Viper, which was obviously too powerful, but also at... the tall woman who was still standing proudly in the snow, her face exposed to the frigid breath, yet unharmed, whom no one had ever seen!</p>
<p>When did the Duke of Ironblade or the Duke of Gray Tower have such a person? Planning to defeat the Blazing Ice Viper single-handedly?</p>
<p>In this team, a woman holding a scepter, looking at the lone wolf with white hair in the distance, muttered to herself in disbelief, "This... this is impossible, she can't be..."</p>
<p>"Professor." The man with the giant hammer turned his head to look at her, "Do you know her?"</p>
<p>"She..."</p>
<p>The professor opened her mouth and said in a dazed tone that she herself did not believe, "She looks a lot like, a lot like someone I know."</p>
<p>"That person is called... Seraphina."</p>
<p>"...Seraphina? Is there such a person in the North? Seraphina—"</p>
<p>The man's words paused, then he immediately turned his head and exclaimed in a voice even more incredulous than the professor's:</p>
<p>"That Marlowe? That Marlowe of Calamity that Wendigo said has become the pact head of Ansel of Hydral?!"</p>
<p>"What a joke, that little girl just fought with Nomad a few days ago, she was still at the second stage! Although she was outrageously strong... but she was still at the second stage!"</p>
<p>He had a ghostly expression, "Are you telling me, a second stage can break the Blazing Ice Viper in half with one punch in just three or four days? Is it you who are crazy, or me, or the world?!"</p>
<p>"Although her figure has changed, and her temperament has changed a bit, but... indeed, it looks like, it seems that this is the only answer."</p>
<p>The professor said somewhat bitterly, "Mr. Hammerforce, you said it yourself, she has become... the pact head of Lord Hydral."</p>
<p>"..."</p>
<p>Hammerforce looked gloomy, they had paid a big enough price and were determined to take down the Blazing Ice Viper. There has been no high-level universal material on the market for a full six years, they can't wait any longer.</p>
<p>But, the other party is the pact head of that Hydral, if it is Hydral...</p>
<p>Do they really want to be enemies with that Hydral?</p>
<p>Just as the squad was caught in a quandary, a sudden outcry erupted from one of them:</p>
<p>"Two high-staging forces under the grand dukes have suddenly emerged from the cities of Luminopolis and Sparklens! They are calling for reinforcements!"</p>
<p>"What?!"</p>
<p>Seraphina was oblivious to the events unfolding not far away. All she knew was that she was in the throes of excitement.</p>
<p>"Phew..."</p>
<p>The young girl exhaled, her punch had drained eighty percent of her strength and ether, the consumption was astonishing.</p>
<p>But the effect, well... was even more astonishing.</p>
<p>If you were to boast at the entrance of the Etheric Academy that someone could almost obliterate a fourth-stage peak monster with a single punch at the third stage, the guards wouldn't arrest you, but would skillfully send you to a mental hospital.</p>
<p>"Now is the perfect time."</p>
<p>Seraphina twisted her waist and swung her arms, performing simple warm-up exercises to recover and maintain her condition. She licked her lips excitedly, "You rest, I rest, and after the rest, we continue with the third round!"</p>
<p>She had no intention of taking advantage of the victory. Seraphina was not only fighting for the sake of enjoyment but also to fulfill her promise.</p>
<p>She had promised Ansel that she would let the world know how strong she was, to make this performance... absolutely perfect!</p>
<p>The wolf beast stared intently at the monster trying to recover its massive body, its unmasked hunger and desire almost piercing the monster's confused consciousness, reaching deep into its chaotic soul.</p>
<p>You look... delicious.</p>
