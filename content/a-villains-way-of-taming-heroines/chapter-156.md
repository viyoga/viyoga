<p>In the lounge, the indignant Seraphina and the leg-perched Ravenna found themselves in a standoff. However, given that this was Ansel's home, Miss wolf managed to suppress her urge to seize Ravenna and give her a good beating, anxiously awaiting Ansel's reaction.</p>
<p>"Ravenna."</p>
<p>Ansel's scepter gently tapped the floor, "I have some private dealings with you, but this does not mean that you can repeatedly provoke and insult Seraphina."</p>
<p>He stepped forward, his smile gentle, but a distinct chill surged in his sea-blue eyes:</p>
<p>"If you continue to disrespect her like this, you will certainly pay the price."</p>
<p>"..."</p>
<p>Ravenna's eyebrows slightly furrowed.</p>
<p>"Taking it this seriously..." Her gaze shifted to Seraphina, whose anger had significantly subsided, and the deliberate contempt and provocation in her face disappeared instantly.</p>
<p>That kind of deliberately irritating mood seemed to be just a non-existent hazy veil.</p>
<p>"Miss Marlowe."</p>
<p>Ravenna retracted her leg, her legs together, slightly straightened her waist, and became a bit more serious in her posture.</p>
<p>"From the transportation circle to now, all my performances were just a small test, I hope you don't mind."</p>
<p>"If you do mind." After putting away that false contempt, her expressionless, icy face, coupled with words that were more than ten times more self-centered than Seraphina's, was even more annoying.</p>
<p>"Then continue to mind."</p>
<p>Seraphina's temples throbbed, and she, who could no longer stand it, turned to look at Ansel:</p>
<p>"Ansel, can I beat her up?"</p>
<p>"You can." Ansel shrugged indifferently, "But this is just another alchemical puppet, didn't you smell it?"</p>
<p>"…Huh?!"</p>
<p>Seraphina took a sharp sniff, looking incredulously at Ravenna sitting on the sofa.</p>
<p>Indeed, it was mixed with a similar smell as before... but how did it become so human-like all of a sudden?</p>
<p>"As far as I know."</p>
<p>Ansel said leisurely, skillfully pouring the liquor on the coffee table into the cup: "Miss Ravenna, who has severe paranoia, has prepared no less than twenty alchemical puppets. Where her real body is hidden, only she knows."</p>
<p>"This is data from three years ago."</p>
<p>Ravenna pushed her glasses, "So far, the number of my puppets has increased to fifty-six."</p>
<p>"And, this is not paranoia."</p>
<p>The woman looked up at Ansel, her voice cold:</p>
<p>"Hydral, do you know how many times the Etheric Academy has assassinated me after you left?"</p>
<p>Ansel, who took a sip of the liquor, shrugged slightly and chuckled, "It made you expand the capacity of your puppets nearly three times, hmm... I can imagine the severity."</p>
<p>Seraphina, who didn't quite understand, was led by Ansel to sit next to him in a daze.</p>
<p>"So, Ravenna."</p>
<p>The young Hydral put down his wine glass and said with a smile, "What exactly is it that makes you so eager to find me?"</p>
<p>"…It's just like you."</p>
<p>Ravenna took off her glasses, and her purple eyes contrasted with her cold and abstinent appearance, creating a subtly captivating feeling.</p>
<p>"You noticed that my reaction in the transportation circle was a performance for the manager, didn't you?"</p>
<p>"It's obvious, but... with your usual performance, they would just believe it. Anyway, you put on that kind of expression."</p>
<p>Ansel leisurely stroked Seraphina's soft palm, "You've made some progress in these three years without seeing me, Ravenna."</p>
<p>Although our Miss Seraphina didn't understand at all what these two were talking about, she was very happy to be touched and didn't bother to think about it.</p>
<p>"If there's no progress, I would have died in the hands of those old guys of Etheric Academy long ago."</p>
<p>She threw the glasses she had taken off onto the coffee table in front of Ansel, and her purple eyes, which seemed to carry magic, stared at Ansel:</p>
<p>"Hydral, this is my sincerity, and the price I will pay later."</p>
<p>"In exchange."</p>
<p>The woman looked at Ansel, who was interested in playing with the glasses, and her tone relaxed a bit:</p>
<p>"I need you to provide me with protection for a while, and—"</p>
<p>"Oh... you've actually achieved systematic dataization."</p>
<p>Ansel's tone was quite surprised, "Although it's a bit crude and simple, it's a huge progress."</p>
<p>Ravenna's eyebrows furrowed slightly, "What are you talking about, do you think I can't reproduce this kind of thing when I have a clue?"</p>
<p>She was not pleased with Ansel's praise, on the contrary, a strong dissatisfaction and even disgust was subtly floating in her cold words.</p>
<p>But the cold woman almost instantly suppressed that emotion and continued, "Since you also recognize this thing, then I also need—"</p>
<p>"Well, this little thing is indeed good."</p>
<p>Ansel put the glasses on the coffee table and gently pushed them to Ravenna, smiling brightly:</p>
<p>"But, I refuse."</p>
<p>"..."</p>
<p>Ravenna stared straight at the gray glasses pushed in front of her, then raised her head and stared at Ansel's face.</p>
<p>Her slightly contracted purple eyes silently told her incomprehension.</p>
<p>"Ravenna."</p>
<p>The young Hydral wrapped his arm around the waist of the girl next to him, letting her lie sideways on his leg, leisurely stroking her soft and smooth cheek.</p>
<p>"You know that my evaluation and attitude towards a person never considers those meaningless courtesies and words, but only values as the absolute standard."</p>
<p>"So when you seek cooperation or even protection from me, you didn't make any humble and respectful gestures. Because I am such a person, and you are such a person."</p>
<p>"But you got one thing wrong, Ravenna."</p>
<p>Ansel, who was lowering his head to knead Seraphina's face, had a light and cheerful smile, and although the girl's face was red, she lay on Ansel's leg and let him do as he pleased, enjoying it very much.</p>
<p>He raised his head and said casually, "You think you have a bargaining chip that can make me respond, but in fact—"</p>
<p>Ansel tilted his head slightly and said in a casual tone:</p>
<p>"In my eyes, you have no value at all."</p>
<p>Not contempt, nor indifference.</p>
<p>Just a kind of, don't care.</p>
<p>Under this don't care, Ansel lowered his head and continued to play and laugh with Seraphina, while still saying indifferently, "In the three years I left the imperial capital, I thought a lot, and the conclusion I came to was..."</p>
<p>"Your ideals and talents are worthless to me."</p>
<p>"Moreover, I have found something more precious..."</p>
<p>The wicked Hydral exhaled gently in Seraphina's ear, "Dear Seraphina, isn't that right?"</p>
