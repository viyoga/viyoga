<p>The knocking made the wealthy Mr. Reginald slightly raise his eyebrows, "Come in."</p>
<p>The guard at the door opened it, "Sir, there are two... farmers. They say they want to discuss business with you."</p>
<p>"... Farmers?"</p>
<p>Reginald was momentarily taken aback, then smiled with interest, "Interesting, let them in."</p>
<p>The guard nodded, then turned to shout outside, "The master invites you in!"</p>
<p>Two men in coarse hemp clothes walked in from outside. The tall, handsome young man looked nervous, while the unremarkable, stocky man appeared quite composed.</p>
<p>"You..." Reginald, with his leg crossed, showed no disdain, merely asking lazily, "wish to conduct business with me? Hmm?"</p>
<p>"We've heard," Laurel stared at the man behind the desk, puffing smoke, "Mr. Hegel, you are the wealthiest in Pelican City."</p>
<p>Reginald Hegel laughed heartily, "So what if I am? Do I look like some philanthropist to you?"</p>
<p>"If you were, it would mean you're a wise man indeed."</p>
<p>James shivered at Reginald's laughter, but Laurel remained calm, stating, "Only a man not wise enough couldn't maintain a vast fortune in this damned world."</p>
<p>The merchant removed the cigar from his lips, leaning forward slightly with a grin, and said, "Interesting, do go on."</p>
<p>"And as a person of wiseness, lord, you should be aware of the changes that are about to sweep over the Pelican City… no, the whole Watson domain."</p>
<p>Laurel took a deep breath, his trembling fingertips betraying his lack of the calm composure he presented: "You must already be aware of the fields outside the city, brimming with magic... In just seven days, the harvest can increase four or five fold. This means that, in a short span of time... Pelican City could produce an abundance of crops for export."</p>
<p>He stepped forward, and the guards who had entered with them instinctively reached for their swords, but Reginald shook his head, signaling them to stand down.</p>
<p>"Please... take a moment to 'taste' this wheat, just smelling its scent is enough."</p>
<p>Laurel first wiped his palms on his clothing to dry the sweat, then took a handful of wheat from his pocket and placed it on the desk.</p>
<p>Reginald, driven by curiosity, took a small handful and sniffed it. In an instant, his pupils contracted.</p>
<p>He had never... never before smelled such fragrant wheat! Even the wheat procured from the most fertile fields could not exude such an aroma.</p>
<p>What was initially mere curiosity for Reginald turned into increasingly rapid breathing.</p>
<p>"It seems you now understand the quality of this wheat, lord," Laurel said, "This quality, coupled with its terrifying yield, could in a short time..."</p>
<p>He did not finish his sentence, but Reginald, gasping for breath, said:</p>
<p>"The immense yield could satisfy the common folk, and the high quality could also cater to the elite... If this wheat were to flood the market at low prices, in a short time..."</p>
<p>Reginald spoke with a chilling tone, his expression akin to a ravenous wolf at the height of its hunger.</p>
<p>"...Destruction, then, domination."</p>
<p>He whispered thus, his body trembling slightly, but it was purely out of excitement, an extreme, uncontrollable excitement.</p>
<p>This sent a shiver down the spine of the onlooking James, who began to fear, uncertain of what would come next, but certain that it would not bode well.</p>
<p>Reginald then extinguished his cigar, stood up, and stared intently at Laurel:</p>
<p>"Do you wish to discuss this business with me? To distribute this wheat throughout the entire Watson territory?"</p>
<p>"Yes, I believe you have the power to do so."</p>
<p>Reginald continued to gaze at Laurel with an intensity unbearable to the average person, and after a long moment, he finally said:</p>
<p>"Aren't you afraid... that I will plunder everything you possess?"</p>
<p>"If people like you were to do so, then we should have started with nothing," Laurel replied candidly.</p>
<p>"The fields do not belong to us, the potion is not ours, and even the crops that are grown will not be ours."</p>
<p>"But... somehow, all of it inexplicably belongs to us."</p>
<p>Under James's bewildered gaze, Laurel clenched his fists, his head bowed slightly, his voice hoarse as he said, "So, I am gambling that for some reason... you will not, or cannot, forcibly take away what we now possess."</p>
<p>In the office, there reigned an absolute silence.</p>
<p>James was tense and bewildered, Laurel bowed her head in silence, and on Reginald's desk, the last wisps of cigar smoke rose.</p>
<p>"Hehehe... Hahahaha!"</p>
<p>The merchant laughed heartily, overjoyed: "You're a farmer? You, of all people, a farmer? It's utterly unbelievable... truly beyond belief."</p>
<p>"Marvelous!"</p>
<p>He looked at Laurel with admiration, loudly proclaiming: "This deal, I'm in. All the food you two can provide in seven days, I'll take it all, name your price!"</p>
<p>"We do not sell directly; we seek a partnership."</p>
<p>Laurel, staring intently at the face of the tycoon he never thought he'd meet in her lifetime, spoke deliberately: "I've spoken at length with you, and I hope, you won't consider me... a fool."</p>
<p>Reginald looked at Laurel for a moment, then grinned and asked: "What's your name?"</p>
<p>"Laurel, Laurel Morlamo."</p>
<p>"Very well, Mr. Morlamo."</p>
<p>Reginald took out a whole box of cigars from his desk drawer and handed it to Laurel.</p>
<p>"I appreciate intelligence and prefer to collaborate with the intelligent. You're certainly no fool, and far from a farmer; that title does not suit you."</p>
<p>"For now, at least." Laurel accepted the cigar, emphasizing, "If that farm remains mine, I might well be a farmer for life."</p>
<p>"Ha, no one will try to take what's yours, rest assured."</p>
<p>Reginald came around from behind his desk, not minding Laurel's slightly muddy clothes, and patted him on the shoulder: "This is a gift from fate to you, and no one can take it away."</p>
<p>"A person both fortunate and intelligent... We will enjoy a fruitful collaboration, Mr. Morlamo."</p>
<p>"On that note, I'd like to make an audacious request." Laurel, caressing the cigar box, spoke softly.</p>
<p>"Oh, let's hear it."</p>
<p>"I hope you can provide an advance on the payment," the man said.</p>
