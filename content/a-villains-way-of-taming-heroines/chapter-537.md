<p>Ansel showed no surprise, instead teasingly twirling Seraphina's white hair, "Why would you want to leave me? Are you tired of me?"</p>
<p>"Of course not!"</p>
<p>Seraphina almost jumped up in shock, "How could I ever get tired of you, Ansel? I wish I could be with you every day to have se—"</p>
<p>The girl paused, blushing, "I, I do spend every day with you, having... having..."</p>
<p>"I know, just joking." Ansel stroked her head in the way she liked best, "You want to find an opportunity for a breakthrough, right?"</p>
<p>"Yes." Seraphina nodded vigorously.</p>
<p>"I seem very strong now... but in reality, I'm still the same, neither here nor there, unable to truly help you."</p>
<p>The wolf girl caught Ansel's hand, pressing it to her cheek, and with her eyes closed, she softly said, "The little one can become incredibly powerful, but I can't... In such battles, I would only be a burden to you."</p>
<p>"I want to become stronger. Since I can easily fight those of the fourth stage while being in the third, maybe... when I reach the fourth stage, I can easily fight those of the fifth?"</p>
<p>Opening her eyes, Seraphina's gaze was full of hope and desire, "By then, I won't be a burden anymore, and I can help you with anything!"</p>
<p>"But..." Seraphina hesitated again, "It seems like you need help right now. If I leave, wouldn't that be bad?"</p>
<p>"Not at all."</p>
<p>Ansel gently caressed Seraphina's cheek, "Do what you want to do, Seri, not just for me."</p>
<p>"...Even if I leave for a while, you won't run into trouble?"</p>
<p>"Who could possibly trouble me?" Ansel couldn't help but laugh, "They should be praying that I don't trouble them."</p>
<p>"Indeed..."</p>
<p>Seraphina nodded thoughtfully, then looked at Ansel with bright, hopeful eyes:</p>
<p>"So, can I go?"</p>
<p>Ansel gazed at the girl's radiant expression, a gentle smile playing on his lips: "Of course, Seri. Aren't you looking forward to it? Why hesitate?"</p>
<p>"... Looking forward?" Seraphina touched her face, somewhat bewildered. "Am I really looking forward to it? I... I don't want to leave Ansel!"</p>
<p>Her tone turned anxious, making our wolf lady appear exceedingly adorable. Ansel pinched her soft cheek, his smile deepening: "Leaving me is just the premise. What you truly anticipate is the vast world beyond."</p>
<p>How could the future Beast King ever be content to lie at Ansel's feet like a domesticated dog?</p>
<p>"Seri, nothing can confine you, and I am certainly not your cage."</p>
<p>Ansel's eyes, more enchanting than usual, and his tender words, made Seraphina's heart flutter. She couldn't help but hug Ansel's waist tightly, nuzzling his stomach affectionately, murmuring, "Ansel... you've changed so much."</p>
<p>"Have I? I don't feel any different."</p>
<p>"Yes!" The girl nodded vigorously, her cheeks flushed. "I like Ansel even more! Every day, more than the last!"</p>
<p>She continued to nuzzle Ansel's stomach like a puppy, her movements gradually slowing.</p>
<p>"Ansel..."</p>
<p>Her head lowered, her voice becoming soft, moist, and…lewd, completely opposite to the wolf's exterior "I don't know how long I'll be away from you, so... before I leave—"</p>
<p>"I want Ansel... to fill me up."</p>
<p>She straightened up, biting his belt, and whispered in his ear, her breath hot, "Be rough, like training a dog, okay?"</p>
<p>*</p>
<p>In the disheveled study, the exhausted Marlina lay on the sofa, panting. She could only watch her sister's swaying body with blurred vision, listening to her joyful and blissful sounds.</p>
<p>She too wanted to be like Seraphina, pulled by the collar from behind, forced to the floor, wrists bound by a belt and pressed against the wall. But now, she could only watch, and if this continued, she might not even be able to do that.</p>
<p>Marlina loved that ecstasy, but she knew, and was grateful, that she wasn't hopelessly addicted to it.</p>
<p>"Mr…Ansel...."</p>
<p>The girl's soft murmur of her beloved's name was dreamy and distant. She had even forgotten her purpose for this visit, her thoughts soaring skyward.</p>
<p>What am I truly yearning for?</p>
<p>Besides Mr.Ansel, what else do I desire?</p>
<p>What qualities do I possess that make Mr.Ansel not discard me like the other women who crave him?</p>
<p>Mr.Ansel... do you truly acknowledge me? Am I as important to you as Seraphina?</p>
<p>A clever girl knows many things, but she also knows how to escape reality.</p>
<p>Mr.Ansel... Mr.Ansel.</p>
<p>I will follow you, don't leave me behind.</p>
<p>With these thoughts, Marlina drifted into a deep sleep.</p>
<p>When she awoke, she found herself clean and dressed in fresh clothes.</p>
<p>"Awake?"</p>
<p>The gentle voice echoed in the room, and Marlina immediately sat up, nervously looking at the young man seated at the desk.</p>
<p>"Sorry, I got a bit carried away with Seri and it affected you... Are you alright?"</p>
<p>"No... It's my own weakness that failed to entertain you."</p>
<p>Marlina shyly pressed her still-weak legs together: "I apologize, Mr. Ansel."</p>
<p>"Having a lady apologize for such things is not gentlemanly." Ansel smiled. "So, what brings you here?"</p>
<p>"Ah, Lady Rhine sent me. There are some matters that require your attention..."</p>
<p>After conveying the message and handing over some documents, Marlina struggled to stand from the sofa and bowed slightly: "Then, I shall take my leave, Mr. Ansel."</p>
<p>"Wait."</p>
<p>Ansel called out to Marlina, gesturing for her to come closer.</p>
<p>The girl, unsure of Ansel's intentions but filled with a mix of anticipation and delight from the recent events, approached him.</p>
<p>However, Ansel did nothing more than retrieve a ring from the drawer and hand it to her.</p>
<p>"Did you know that Seri wants to go on an adventure, Marlina?"</p>
<p>"...Yes, I know. Miss Seraphina mentioned it to me." The girl bowed her head respectfully.</p>
<p>Ansel didn't comment on Marlina's formality, instead speaking gently: "The ring contains many essentials for her journey. She tends to leave impulsively, likely without proper preparation. She probably won't see me today, so please give this to her."</p>
<p>"Mr. Ansel, you... prepared this long ago, didn't you?"</p>
<p>"With her transparent nature, who could she possibly deceive?"</p>
<p>Ansel smiled, his eyes radiating pure warmth and affection, which Marlina easily noticed.</p>
<p>"Though I encourage her to pursue her desires," he sighed softly, his eyes lowering, "the thought of her absence makes me feel lonely."</p>
<p>"—Ah, don't tell Seri this, or she won't leave."</p>
<p>"You're overthinking it." Marlina smiled. "I would never be so indiscreet, Mr. Ansel."</p>
<p>"... Indiscreet?"</p>
<p>Ansel glanced at Marlina, lost in thought, but eventually smiled warmly: "Anyway, thank you for running this errand, Marlina."</p>
<p>"Yes, then I shall take my leave."</p>
<p>"Do your best, Marlina. Rhine's report speaks highly of you... It never gives such praise lightly."</p>
<p>Looking at the girl's delicate back, Ansel encouraged, "I told you, you are capable, aren't you?"</p>
<p>Marlina turned slightly, showing Ansel a gentle smile, her profile delicate and beautiful:</p>
<p>"It's all thanks to your grace that I am where I am today, Mr. Ansel."</p>
<p>"I will continue to strive, as you wish."</p>
<p>She bowed one last time and walked out.</p>
<p>Just "capable"…</p>
<p>Just being capable isn't enough.</p>
<p>But does my capability truly matter to Mr. Ansel?</p>
<p>I must become irreplaceable.</p>
<p>Like Seri, I must become irreplaceable to Mr. Ansel.</p>
<p>But how should I… achieve that?</p>
<p>...No, first, I must find a way to transcend to extraordinary.</p>
<p>Being merely "capable" is not enough; I never intended to see those words as my… endpoint.</p>
<p>At some point, Marlina no longer felt ecstatic merely from Ansel's mere praise.</p>
