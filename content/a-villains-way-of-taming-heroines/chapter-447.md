<p>"And what about you?"</p>
<p>Ravenna retorted coldly, "How complete are you, and to what realms can you reach?"</p>
<p>"I cannot achieve it either, but the difference between you and me lies in..."</p>
<p>Helen's carving knife paused slightly as she tilted her head to look at Ravenna, her eyes devoid of light, filled with "self"-loathing.</p>
<p>"I have come to recognize and accept this fact."</p>
<p>She continued her work, adding, "I have realized and accepted that with Father's guidance, I was able to continuously improve the universal synthesizer to the sixth model in such a short time. But after my father left... for three whole years, I have struggled to make any progress."</p>
<p>As Helen spoke, her usually indifferent face softened.</p>
<p>"You are right, Ravenna. Even without those fifteen years of scheme, my heart still yearns to change the world, and even now... I have not completely given it up."</p>
<p>"But this... signifies that Father is the most important presence in my life."</p>
<p>"He is..."Helen's voice trembled with uncontrollable excitement and joy.</p>
<p>"He is the only one who can lead me towards that future."</p>
<p>Ravenna did not look at Helen; under her control, Nidhoggur either subdivided, disassembled, or processed the materials. In the use of this special alchemical tool, she was clearly several levels above Helen.</p>
<p>"I thought... you were going to say something else."</p>
<p>Ravenna, alone for three years, spoke without any change in tone, unmoved by Helen's words.</p>
<p>After Nidhoggur completed a batch of material processing, it quickly divided a large portion, solidifying into a faceless puppet to assist Ravenna in creating an additional production line.</p>
<p>"Much more formidable than I imagined..."</p>
<p>Ansel slightly raised his eyebrows as he leaned forward, peering through the light screen at the faceless puppet.</p>
<p>"It seems you have also incorporated some mechanical armor construction logic... Have you also considered the pursuit of power during these three years?"</p>
<p>Ansel had given Ravenna ample freedom; she could consult any book in the underground library at will. How much Ravenna had grown in these three years, Ansel had no clear idea.</p>
<p>What she had been thinking about these years, Ansel was equally unaware. However, seeing her performance now... it was obviously no different from three years ago.</p>
<p>If there was any difference, it was that the current Ravenna was perhaps ten times, a hundred times more resolute than before.</p>
<p>However, although the object of her pursuit had not changed, the current Ravenna, obviously more thoughtful… than that normally developing Ravenna in the destined future.</p>
<p>If she hadn't studied mechanical armor, she could not possibly have created a puppet mixed with mechanical armor construction concepts so effortlessly.</p>
<p>Ravenna... if you could defeat Helen, what would you want to do? Arm the Tower of Babel?</p>
<p>That's really... You are not at all reassured about me.</p>
<p>The caution and indifference displayed by the former friend did not sadden Ansel much—he had already experienced opportunities given and betrayals faced.</p>
<p>Rather, it was Ravenna's current coldness, aloofness, defensiveness, and even... hostility that proved the preciousness of Helen's… undying loyalty.</p>
<p>Ravenna's nearly extreme confrontational intent made Helen's loyalty appear so close to the perfection Ansel needed.</p>
<p>"Early breakthroughs are common in the initial stages of research."</p>
<p>Ravenna, while manipulating Nidhoggur and the puppet, and simultaneously processing on her own, already displayed the poise of an alchemist master.</p>
<p>"As for the research on the seventh model, it has reached its final stage. It's not that there's no possibility of further progress without him; rather, both he and I have hit a bottleneck."</p>
<p>The detached idealist glanced at her despised self: "You even use this to proclaim his importance to you, which clearly shows... you are indeed beyond help, and my having any hope for you was foolish."</p>
<p>During their conversation, Ravenna had already set up an entire massive platform framework on her workbench, while Helen only held a small component in her hands.</p>
<p>This terrifying disparity, almost not of the same dimension, made Helen's chance of victory seem impossible.</p>
<p>Meanwhile, Ravenna continued:</p>
<p>"Since you want to prove to me how important Ansel is to you... let me also make you realize something." She even had spare energy to use Nidhoggur to create a hydra, which floated in mid-air, and then she said indifferently:</p>
<p>"The reason he uses to crush your beliefs—it's you, me, we have never walked among the mortals, and naturally, there's no reason to have such a strong desire to change the lives of the ordinary."</p>
<p>"I admit it. I admit that, as he said, I desire to change the world more than... wanting to bring them a better life."</p>
<p>"But," Ravenna's expression turned cold, and the hydra shaped by Nidhoggur roared silently.</p>
<p>"You say, everything he ever told me was not a lie. Then please tell me..."</p>
<p>"Ansel of Hydral, born a divine species, a being truly superior to all creatures, where does his belief in changing the world come from, and where does his zeal to free the mortals from their shackles originate?"</p>
<p>The dark hydra disintegrated into nothingness, and Ravenna, with a distant and indifferent voice, told her other self:</p>
<p>"Listen well... this is the proof that everything he said was a lie—Ansel of Hydral is even less likely than you or me to empathize with the people, even less likely to genuinely hope for the world to change."</p>
<p>"As a divine species, he is the heaviest shackle on this world!"</p>
<p>In the alchemy workshop, only the mechanical operations and the sound of the furnace burning could be heard.</p>
<p>And in the sealed study within the manor, Ansel's laughter rang out.</p>
<p>He leaned back in his chair, head tilted back, arms covering his eyes, as if he had heard some marvelous words, laughing so freely, so joyfully, so... liberated.</p>
<p>"Right... you are absolutely correct, Ravenna, you truly are... the smartest."</p>
<p>His arms slowly fell as the laughter subsided, and Ansel once again gazed at the screen, looking at the determined petite sorceress.</p>
<p>As long as the divine species exists, the world remains incapable of true transformation.</p>
<p>To illustrate with a simple example—Eileen spent her entire life, sparing no sacrifice, even that of his descendants, in pursuit of what might be possible with a device known as the Universal Ether Furnace...</p>
<p>For Flamelle, however, it was merely a toy that could be easily crafted.</p>
