<p>"...I am fine," Ravenna murmured, her hand pressed to her forehead as she modestly bowed her head, "It's my memory."</p>
<p>"Memory... The ones previously damaged when placed inside a puppet, are they finally beginning to mend?"</p>
<p>Hendrik exhaled in relief, yet his voice carried a tinge of worry, "But you seem quite distressed, perhaps we should seek—"</p>
<p>"There's no need," Ravenna, now firmly on her feet, shook her head, "Myron, the mentor of Madam Ronger, once told me that sudden flashbacks and feelings of discomfort are normal, indicating that I am recovering well."</p>
<p>"... Very well, but should you feel unwell at any point, you must inform us, understood?"</p>
<p>The middle-aged man, his face showing signs of age, spoke earnestly, "Everything is falling into place, Ravenna, and you shouldn't bear too much pressure. This is what the teacher... cough."</p>
<p>Hendrik suddenly coughed, his voice growing deeper, "This is what the teacher would not wish to see..."</p>
<p>Ravenna, lost in thought over those fragments of memory, failed to notice his concern, but as the conversation turned to Eileen, her thoughts snapped back to reality.</p>
<p>Whether to disclose Eileen's matter to Hendrik and Ronger was a dilemma for Ravenna.</p>
<p>Initially, she had acted alone, at great cost, and wished to keep them in the dark, but now... Ansel could provide clues at a far more reasonable price than the exorbitant one demanded by Suellen, and given their trust in Ansel, they were unlikely to scrutinize the cost too closely.</p>
<p>Just like me.. they had been awaiting the truth for far too long.</p>
<p>Ravenna opened her mouth to share with Hendrik but quickly regained her composure.</p>
<p>Yet, it is merely a clue... If the truth is to be revealed, it should come from me directly.</p>
<p>I shall tell them once I have the answers.</p>
<p>Suppressing the urge within, Ravenna calmly stated, "After the completion of the soil enhancement potion's efficacy test, leave the coordination to me; I shall communicate with Lord Ansel."</p>
<p>Without a second thought, Hendrik replied, "Of course, I believe this task is best suited for you, Ravenna."</p>
<p>He placed his hand on her shoulder with utmost solemnity, forgetting her aversion to physical contact, his eyes brimming with hope and admiration.</p>
<p>"You are undoubtedly the one who can change this world. He believed in you wholeheartedly, and we share that unwavering belief."</p>
<p>"... Thank you, Hendrik."</p>
<p>In the fifteen years since Eileen's death, Hendrik, Ronger, and a few other of Eileen's students had raised Ravenna, providing her with the finest resources and education.</p>
<p>Although their care was almost devoid of… affection, even the maternal Ronger maintained a controlled distance from Ravenna,</p>
<p>But this was precisely what Ravenna needed.</p>
<p>In her life, she no longer required parental figures; simple guidance was far more valuable than anything else.</p>
<p>Gazing at one of the few significant figures remaining in her life, Ravenna softly affirmed her resolve:</p>
<p>"I will, Hendrik, whether it be grandfather, you all, the empire that awaits change, or the common folk."</p>
<p>[What exactly drives you to pursue these endeavors?]</p>
<p>Even as the nightmare loomed again, Miss puppet did not hesitate to declare, "I will not let any of you down."</p>
<p>Hendrik smiled reassuringly, "I know you can do it, Ravenna... Ah, right, I almost forgot to tell you something important, I should have sought you out first."</p>
<p>He patted his forehead, "The matters you've shared have muddled my thoughts... Do you remember Lord Suellen? The Young Princess."</p>
<p>"..."At the mention of this name, Ravenna felt a sense of foreboding, her brows furrowing slightly, "Her? What about her?"</p>
<p>"She has also placed an order with us for a customized data system, and according to her... she has high expectations for this system and wishes to meet with you alone to discuss it."</p>
<p>"Suellen..."</p>
<p>Ravenna mused over the Young Princess, murmuring thoughtfully, "She wants a data system, but for what purpose?"</p>
<p>Is it to continue suppressing Evora according to the empress's wishes? If that's the case... I must not get involved in their conflict. It would bring no benefit to the Tower of Babel and could cause considerable trouble for Ansel.</p>
<p>Regardless, a meeting is at least in order.</p>
<p>"When does she wish to meet with me?" Ravenna inquired.</p>
<p>"I'll make the arrangements for you; she said she would come immediately once I contact her." Hendrik chuckled, clearly pleased, "Her Highness Princess Suellen holds you in high regard, Ravenna."</p>
<p>"... Her Highness," Ravenna mused over the title with a cold laugh, but said no more.</p>
<p>"Then I'll wait for her in your office."</p>
<p>Ravenna turned to leave the alchemy workshop, and halfway through, she looked back at Hendrik, observing his face aged by the constant mental strain, a visage that even a fifth-stage warrior would find weary.</p>
<p>"... Hendrik," she suddenly said.</p>
<p>"What is it, Ravenna?" The man quickly turned, looking at her kindly.</p>
<p>"You should rest as well," Ravenna chose her words carefully, "The Tower of Babel is recovering; you needn't put so much... pressure on yourself."</p>
<p>Hendrik was taken aback.</p>
<p>But Ravenna did not linger to say more, leaving promptly.</p>
<p>Hendrik's gaze remained fixed in place, the petite girl who in his eyes had always been a child, seemingly unchanged… over the fifteen years, had now transformed into someone beyond anyone's imagination.</p>
<p>Though her words were meant to be comforting, after Ravenna's departure, Hendrik's eyes betrayed a sense of sorrowful distraction.</p>
<p>It took him a long while before he lowered his gaze, murmuring to himself softly, "Teacher, if you could see this moment, you would surely... be very comforted."</p>
<p>*</p>
<p>In the office, Ravenna soon met with Suellen, who had come to discuss business.</p>
<p>The Young Princess, always dressed in a pure white, elegant gown, radiated purity and beauty like an iris blooming in a field.</p>
<p>She sat across from Ravenna, her legs neatly together, her smile gentle yet approachable with a hint of playfulness.</p>
<p>"Miss Ziegler, you must understand my intentions from Mr. Hendrik," Suellen began.</p>
<p>"Your intentions…"</p>
<p>Ravenna, flipping through the documents in her hand, furrowed her brows.</p>
