<p>"There's no need for astonishment," the arrogant doppelgänger of Seraphina, arms crossed, declared, "I am you, and you are me."</p>
<p>"It's just that you hesitated during the ascension process, which is why we're having this conversation now."</p>
<p>She tilted her head, "What, can't accept your true self?"</p>
<p>Seraphina squinted slightly, arms also crossed, and scoffed, "What makes you so sure you're the real me?"</p>
<p>The beast-like Seraphina grinned, "Because I am destined to touch a broader world, possess a vast hunting ground, and devour stronger prey. Therefore, I am the real me."</p>
<p>"The reason you, or rather, the current me, is like this, is simply because you're still a country girl who hasn't been out of the village for long."</p>
<p>"..."</p>
<p>Seraphina fell silent for a moment, then asked, "Will I devour Ansel?"</p>
<p>"What are you talking about, isn't that a given?"</p>
<p>The beast-like Seraphina sneered, "In this world, is there a more valuable prey than the one I love?"</p>
<p>"Oh, I see." Seraphina nodded, then the next second—</p>
<p>Bang!</p>
<p>She instantly overpowered her other self, her expression growing more ferocious.</p>
<p>"I don't think the most valuable prey is Ansel."</p>
<p>"The most valuable prey can only be..."</p>
<p>"Myself." x2</p>
<p>Echoing in the boundless white space were two distinctly different tones of laughter.</p>
<p>"I am the master of this body, and I am the master of my soul. Elements? Beast elements?" Seraphina Marlowe disdainfully choked her other self's throat, word by word, "You need to understand one thing, it's not you influencing me."</p>
<p>"But me... dominating you!"</p>
<p>Seraphina suddenly understood why her other self had become the Sky Wolf Emperor.</p>
<p>Her life was in despair, her sister fell into hell, her village was completely destroyed, and her like-minded friends were one of the culprits who killed her own family.</p>
<p>She chose to let her beast nature rule her, merging with it.</p>
<p>Thus, the insatiable beast was born.</p>
<p>But now, in contrast... her life is full of hope!</p>
<p>Her family is living a happy and fulfilling life, she has found a goal for which she can sacrifice everything, fallen in love with someone who loves her, who is strong, rich, spoils her in every way, and is a handsome man who is super good at sweet talk!</p>
<p>"I won't hand over such a perfect life... to a fool like you who only knows how to eat!"</p>
<p>"I am Seraphina..."</p>
<p>In the vast depths of her soul, facing the beast nature in her heart, the girl roared: "I am Ansel's Seraphina!"</p>
<p>At this moment, Seraphina seemed to feel something completely clear.</p>
<p>She could feel the absolute details within her body, feel the flow of ether within millimeters, feel the breath between heaven and earth, feel the indescribable resonance.</p>
<p>Her consciousness moved unimpededly within her body, that perfect sense of fit made her almost want to shout out in comfort.</p>
<p>At this moment, Seraphina opened her eyes.</p>
<p>"How are you feeling, are you okay?"</p>
<p>The moment she opened her eyes, Ansel immediately reached out to touch her head, then let out a long sigh of relief, "Soul is intact... good, you succeeded, Seraphina."</p>
<p>He smiled and ruffled the girl's hair, "You not only successfully ascended to the third stage, awakened your spirit, but also unprecedentedly inherited the power of two pact heads."</p>
<p>"Impressive."</p>
<p>"Hehehe... I didn't expect it either, huh? Ansel, why are you a bit shorter?"</p>
<p>"…You've grown taller." Ansel looked up helplessly at Seraphina, who was almost reaching 1.8 meters, "The pact head will remodel your body to the most suitable form for you."</p>
<p>"Also, while you were unconscious just now, I changed your clothes."</p>
<p>"Oh... ah? Clothes? Ansel, do you carry clothes with you when you go out?"</p>
<p>Seraphina looked surprised at the pants that fit her body perfectly, "And they fit just right."</p>
<p>"Made on the spot, a little spell I learned before, didn't expect it to come in handy now." Ansel straightened Seraphina's collar, "I didn't change your top, it looks pretty good. This is the grand stage I prepared for you, Seraphina."</p>
<p>The young Hydral showed a proud smile: "I have no more concerns and hesitations, and you are the same, right, Seraphina?"</p>
<p>The girl raised her eyebrows, "Do I even need to say it? I have to pin that big caterpillar to the ground and give it a good beating!"</p>
<p>She felt the surging power flowing in her body, feeling that with a light leap, she could fly a hundred meters away!</p>
<p>Under the triple boost of the double pact heads and Seraphina's terrifying spirit, even Ansel was not sure how strong the current Seraphina was.</p>
<p>"Ansel, you really have to watch this time!"</p>
<p>Seraphina twisted her neck, smiling brightly, "I want to tell you, no matter how many pact heads you have in the future, I will definitely be the best one!"</p>
<p>"Oh, and also—" She reached into the pocket of her cloak and pulled out a... collar?</p>
<p>Under Ansel's slightly surprised gaze, Seraphina cherished the collar, "Glad it's not broken... ha!"</p>
<p>She naturally and smoothly fastened the collar around her neck, then strode forward, hugged Ansel, lowered her head down, and kissed him deeply.</p>
<p>"Stupid Ansel."</p>
<p>The girl's face was slightly red, "I really don't know what you're hesitating about..."</p>
<p>The wolf, who had become a loyal dog but still retained her beast nature, held the collar around her neck with great peace of mind: "I've long been... your possession."</p>
<p>After saying this, she turned around in embarrassment, bent her knees slightly, and like escaping, she jumped up and flew into the sky in an instant.</p>
<p>With that wild laughter, so different from her shy demeanor just now, she announced her existence to the whole Red Frost territory, the whole empire, the whole world, as Seraphina Marlowe.</p>
<p>"Worm!"</p>
<p>"It's time for the second round!"</p>
<p>Ansel looked up at the excitedly laughing girl, who was declaring her existence to the world, and also laughed from the bottom of his heart.</p>
<p>Perhaps, this is the reason why he fell in love with Seraphina, but it's never just as simple as loving her.</p>
<p>Seraphina. You are definitely the best.</p>
