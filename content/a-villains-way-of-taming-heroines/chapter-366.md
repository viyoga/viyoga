<p>Upon returning to the Tower of Babel, Ravenna sought confirmation from Ansel once more regarding the matters of the game.</p>
<p>"So, neither you nor I… will intervene or bear responsibility for the operation of this game?"</p>
<p>"Of course," Ansel replied with a smile, "If Helen... ah, I mean Ravenna now, if you wish to attempt to manage the arrangements yourself, I wouldn't mind."</p>
<p>"No, I shall not interfere... with matters beyond my capability."</p>
<p>Ravenna responded calmly, "The management of a domain is far beyond my reach."</p>
<p>She was well aware that venturing into an entirely unknown realm would only invite disaster. How could she foolishly meddle in the affairs of a domain about which she knew absolutely nothing regarding its maintenance or operation?</p>
<p>Her inquiry stemmed from a concern that Ansel might forcefully impose the management of the domain upon her, even finding it peculiar that Ansel would think she harbored any intention to intervene.</p>
<p>Could anyone be so foolish as to meddle in a domain they knew nothing about, based merely on presumption?</p>
<p>"Then... since the outcome is determined based on the development of the two domains."</p>
<p>Ravenna maintained her caution, "Do I need to choose a side?"</p>
<p>Her cautious demeanor amused Ansel, "We're merely playing a game, Miss Ravenna. Do I appear to be someone who cares deeply about winning or losing, to the extent of setting rewards and punishments?"</p>
<p>The young Ansel of Hydral leisurely said, "Whether you choose a side or simply observe the developments, it doesn't matter. The focus is on these two domains, on what they will eventually become. Victory or defeat is their concern."</p>
<p>Without the need for her own arrangements or predetermined stance, and with no penalties involved...</p>
<p>Ravenna meticulously scrutinized every possible trap Ansel might have laid within this game, convinced that Ansel's proposal was not merely for the sake of playing a game, just as in that "father-daughter game," where in less than half a day, he brought her a series of profound impacts.</p>
<p>At this thought, Ravenna immediately halted her contemplation.</p>
<p>That nightmare, which resided deep within her soul, would at any moment, in any place, abruptly swing its scythe—</p>
<p>"What are you truly chasing after?"</p>
<p>A bone-chilling cold spread from Ravenna's soul to her body, causing her to shiver slightly before she forcefully suppressed the thought once more.</p>
<p>This game... Ansel may have set other traps, but from the rules, there is no harm to the two domains and the civilians.</p>
<p>The alchemical devices and props provided will not be plundered by extraordinary beings, and autonomy remains in the hands of the civilians. I am not compelled to make arrangements, nor do I need to place my bets on either side...</p>
<p>There are no issues.</p>
<p>Ravenna exhaled softly and nodded.</p>
<p>"Alright, I have no further questions, Father... Lord Ansel."</p>
<p>With Ansel's assurance, this could potentially serve as an excellent pilot test.</p>
<p>Ravenna's negation of the actions of Eileen's students, such as Ronger, was not a denial of their actions but of their motives—if their ultimate goal is merely to study scattered alchemical devices, then nothing will change.</p>
<p>The Empire needs a revolution, a massive one.</p>
<p>Without this resolve, it is not possible; it is weakness; it is...</p>
<p>["...All an illusion!"]</p>
<p>"!"</p>
<p>Ravenna's body suddenly shook, nearly causing her to fall backward, and her usually indifferent face showed a hint of... fear?</p>
<p>What, what was that just now?</p>
<p>A voice... a real voice, was it... Ansel's voice?</p>
<p>For a moment, she even saw a vision of that youthful face, and the startled Ravenna could not clearly identify it, but in the fleeting glimpse, the anger on that face was so vivid.</p>
<p>Was it... a memory?</p>
<p>"Ravenna, what's wrong?"</p>
<p>A concerned voice arose beside Ravenna, allowing her to temporarily detach from her chaotic thoughts.</p>
<p>The young Hydral raised an eyebrow: "It seems you saw something terrifying. What... have the memories you spoke of returned?"</p>
<p>His tone was casual, as if the matter did not concern him and he was indifferent.</p>
<p>"...Some have."</p>
<p>Ravenna, holding her forehead, spoke in a complex tone: "I saw you, angry."</p>
<p>"Oh, that must have been when we had our falling out."</p>
<p>Ansel's tone remained nonchalant:</p>
<p>"After all, that was the only time I was angry with you."</p>
<p>"..." Ravenna did not speak, nor could she respond to his remark.</p>
<p>Ansel's sacrifice, the lost truth... it made it difficult for her to feel completely antagonistic towards Ansel again.</p>
<p>"Thus, regarding the assistance provided to these two cities..."</p>
<p>Ansel refrained from further troubling Ravenna on that topic, instead proposing, "What do you intend to offer them? Within the Tower of Babel, there must be a plethora of those alchemical devices designed for public convenience that never successfully launched, correct?"</p>
<p>Ravenna's mind was flooded with the conversations she had with Ansel.</p>
<p>Necessities of life...</p>
<p>She was eager to attempt a transformation using extraordinary powers for all aspects of daily life, yet she was acutely aware that the more elements mixed in, the more complex and uncontrollable the situation becomes.</p>
<p>She never forgot that Ansel must have thoroughly prepared for this game.</p>
<p>Even so, if she were to recklessly distribute too many alchemical devices into these two cities, leading to catastrophic outcomes due to unforeseen malfunctions... that would be a loss far outweighing any gain.</p>
<p>Indeed, Ansel's opportunity might very well be a trap, luring you into a deeper abyss, Ravenna.</p>
<p>Should these cities collapse due to her oversight, especially due to the very alchemical devices meant to revolutionize them... Ravenna dared not contemplate what she would become then.</p>
<p>Therefore, it was imperative to mitigate such risks, preferably... by temporarily introducing only one type of alchemical tool to ensure controllability and stability.</p>
<p>The petite scholar pondered calmly.</p>
<p>If it were to be just one, it should be what the common folk most desperately need, the most essential...</p>
<p>Indeed, it could only be food.</p>
<p>"Regarding the soil enhancement potion..." Ravenna responded, "I could inquire with Hendrik. If they are nearing completion, I hope to deploy it into those two cities after confirming its efficacy."</p>
