<p>Ansel didn't lie, he really thought so — although it's far from many things he's eaten, he just thinks it's delicious.</p>
<p>As he said this, Hydral was still examining his cut and separated emotions.</p>
<p>"Really?!"</p>
<p>Seraphina's expression became radiant, it was a visible, not exaggerated "glow", from expression to eyes, from eyes to words, all filled with the purest joy and happiness.</p>
<p>"Um."</p>
<p>Ansel, who was coldly analyzing himself, smiled and said, "Seraphina, I never —"</p>
<p>"Stop!"</p>
<p>Seraphina blushed and stopped Ansel from continuing, "Don't... don't always hang these words on your lips, it's... how do you say it, it's light, frivolous."</p>
<p>"I never claimed to be a decent gentleman."</p>
<p>"…It's a bit greasy, Seraphina."</p>
<p>Ansel touched his cheek, looking at Seraphina with a bit of amusement.</p>
<p>"Don't laugh!" Seraphina said, but seeing the spots of grease on Ansel's face, she couldn't help but laugh.</p>
<p>She moved closer to Ansel, but because there were no rocks to sit on, she simply knelt on the ground, holding Ansel's arm with both hands, and nervously and clumsily stuck out her pink tongue, gently licking Ansel's cheek.</p>
<p>"Like this…" She blushed, whispering, "Is it better now?"</p>
<p>Hydral shook his head, "I don't think so."</p>
<p>Seraphina paused, in her helplessness there was also a hint of unspoken anticipation, she tugged at Ansel's clothes, whispering, "Then, what should I do?"</p>
<p>"Um… I remember there's a spell called Cleanse, but I accidentally forgot how to use it."</p>
<p>Ansel tapped his forehead in frustration, "It would be nice if I could remember."</p>
<p>Seraphina, suppressing the embarrassment that was about to explode in her heart, licked Ansel's cheek again.</p>
<p>"There's still a lot missing, my memory is a bit fuzzy."</p>
<p>"You, you just say it! What should I do!"</p>
<p>Seraphina, kneeling on the ground, hammered Ansel's thigh, extremely embarrassed.</p>
<p>The wicked Hydral laughed and grabbed Seraphina's arm, saying, "Then sit up first."</p>
<p>"Sit… sit up?!"</p>
<p>"Unwilling?" Ansel tilted his head.</p>
<p>Seraphina didn't say anything, she just lowered her head, showing her blushing ears to Ansel, and sat on his lap.</p>
<p>"Turn around." Ansel's breath brushed against the back of the girl's neck.</p>
<p>The slender and soft body trembled slightly, the soft roundness intimately rubbing against Ansel's thigh as she slowly turned around, the wolf girl couldn't help but spread her legs, facing Ansel.</p>
<p>"Let me think…" Ansel stroked his chin, suddenly laughing, "I remembered how to use that spell when I was tickling the belly of a puppy at home, Seraphina, maybe if I do it again, I'll remember."</p>
<p>"Puppy…? There's no—"</p>
<p>"Ansel!!"</p>
<p>Seraphina screamed, hammering his shoulders with both hands, "You're the puppy, you're the puppy!"</p>
<p>"I see."</p>
<p>Ansel raised an eyebrow, "Then I'll go back and tell Marlina that Seraphina, with her mouth full of grease, kissed me, and thought she could lick it clean by licking it—"</p>
<p>"Awww!"</p>
<p>Seraphina made a panicked and cute sound in her throat, she quickly covered Ansel's mouth, and then realized… her hands were also greasy.</p>
<p>Facing the boy's playful gaze, Seraphina felt a heat surging from the bottom of her heart, from her lower abdomen, she lowered her head, grabbed her clothes, and with both shyness and… anticipation, she slowly lifted it up.</p>
<p>"Quick, hurry up." The girl, exposing her tender belly, closed her eyes, "It's a bit cold!"</p>
<p>"Is this what a puppy should look like?"</p>
<p>Ansel placed his hand on her soft and smooth skin, but firm and elastic belly, but didn't move, he just said, "Shouldn't a puppy be very welcoming to being tickled by the person they like?"</p>
<p>The words "the person they like" easily broke through Seraphina's defenses.</p>
<p>The girl weakly slumped into Ansel's arms, resting her head on his shoulder, whispering in his ear in a confused and soft voice:</p>
<p>"Ansel… touch me, please touch me… ah… touch my belly."</p>
<p>Although she seemed very embarrassed, in fact, Seraphina was happily enjoying Ansel's caress.</p>
<p>After all, no girl would dislike the eager desire of the person they admire for themselves, that desire that proves their charm.</p>
<p>So even though Seraphina usually rejects Ansel's actions at the first opportunity, she rarely physically resists Ansel anymore.</p>
<p>But… what about Ansel, who is holding Seraphina now?</p>
<p>Is he really indulging in the girl he is holding so intimately?</p>
<p>The young Hydral listened to the soft gasps in his ear, remaining silent.</p>
<p>He was contemplating something, pondering the words he had not spoken to Seraphina.</p>
<p>That is the primary element that Seraphina holds.</p>
<p>It is almost engraved in the depths of her soul, equivalent to her essence, the element that leads her on the path of the infinite pursuit of dominance.</p>
<p>[Beast].</p>
<p>The greatest element that accomplished the grandeur of the Sky Wolf Emperor.</p>
<p>It allowed her to stride towards strength with endless desire, to walk the path of evolution with a fiery heart.</p>
<p>It symbolized the infinite hunger for life, the infinite desire for power, and the infinite pursuit of a more lofty and grand scenery.</p>
<p>It was something that could not be dominated.</p>
<p>Therefore, the Sky Wolf Emperor was crowned with the name of the emperor and followed the pure path of dominance.</p>
<p>The key point was not that the Sky Wolf Emperor chose the Beast.</p>
<p>But the king at the top of power was the beast itself.</p>
<p>However, this did not mean there was no chance for change — Marlina, who recognized the essence of Seraphina, used her own life as a guide to successfully bind the beastliness rooted in the depths of Seraphina's soul, transforming her from the "Sky Wolf Beast" to the "Sky Wolf Emperor".</p>
<p>Since Marlina succeeded, why would Ansel have any reason to fail?</p>
<p>He held Seraphina's waist, allowing her soft abdomen to press against him, swaying and rubbing.</p>
<p>"Seraphina," the devil whispered, "do you find it interesting to be a puppy?"</p>
<p>"Interesting... you... die!"</p>
<p>The girl said with a slight tremor in her voice, struggling to maintain her last shred of dignity.</p>
<p>Ansel just smiled and didn't say anything more. Now is not the time, and there was plenty of time.</p>
<p>Moreover, he has long been prepared for this.</p>
<p>To bind that beastliness and completely tame this wild beast — that would be the victory of Hydral.</p>
