<p>"Oh, he's dead. I also took care of that butcher next door."</p>
<p>Seraphina pulled down her hood, scratching the hair at the back of her head that had grown somewhat, resembling the fine wolf fur on a wolf's neck. "There's still plenty to do... Oh, find Cliff for me and tell him to meet me at my place. And—I'm starving, I need to find something to eat first."</p>
<p>Waving a hand, she left the stunned young man behind and strode into the excessively luxurious mansion in the lower city.</p>
<p>"Eating like this all the time won't do... I need to find a way to make some money."</p>
<p>Seraphina would distribute all the money she confiscated from villains to the local civilians, keeping none for herself.</p>
<p>Her travel and food expenses were mostly earned by doing odd jobs and taking on commissions, something she was quite accustomed to by now.</p>
<p>"Should I take on some commissions in the upper city... But if I'm not around, they might be scared, and some might start acting out. What should—"</p>
<p>Walking through the corridor, Seraphina suddenly halted.</p>
<p>It seemed she didn't hear the greetings from the lower city residents or her current "subordinates" at all. Her eyes were fixed ahead, on a group of people discussing something not far away.</p>
<p>More precisely, the most conspicuous among them—a young man with black hair, dangerous and sinister.</p>
<p>"This guy."</p>
<p>Seraphina grabbed a passing follower, her voice slightly hoarse. "Who is he? How did you let him in?"</p>
<p>"... Him? Ah, he's an adventurer from the upper city," the caught young man quickly answered. "He took on a commission… looking for missing persons. He thinks it might be related to the Speckled Snake's human trafficking, so he came to us."</p>
<p>Seeing Seraphina's increasingly stern expression, the young man grew more apprehensive. "Boss Fenrir, does he… does he have a problem?"</p>
<p>"No, but…"</p>
<p>Seraphina's eyes were locked onto the black-haired youth, filled with both shock and confusion.</p>
<p>She couldn't see through him… not even the Head of Wind could probe a single detail about this person.</p>
<p>Since leaving Ansel's side, this was the first time Seraphina had encountered such an existence.</p>
<p>Logically, meeting such an opponent should have made her heart race with excitement and battle fervor.</p>
<p>But, but… this resistance and coldness in her heart… what was it?</p>
<p>Was it that she didn't want to fight him… or did she feel she wasn't his match?</p>
<p>Seraphina found it absurd. While people she couldn't probe were rare, those who couldn't ignite her fighting spirit were non-existent.</p>
<p>Even against the Empress, she could muster intense battle fervor... Why did this seemingly familiar stranger evoke no desire to fight at all? Was it some special ability?</p>
<p>After much hesitation, Seraphina approached, closing the distance to the black-haired youth with heightened vigilance.</p>
<p>"Hey, you."</p>
<p>She stopped about three meters away from him, instinctively clenching her fists, her body tense. "Who are you, and what do you want?"</p>
<p>The black-haired youth slightly tilted his head, his gaze falling on Seraphina.</p>
<p>"So, you're the one who replaced the Speckled Snake… Miss Fenrir?"</p>
<p>His tone was indifferent, and his relaxed demeanor suggested he didn't take Seraphina seriously at all.</p>
<p>Such a near-taunting attitude would have usually set Seraphina off, but this time, she felt inexplicably subdued. Holding back her frustration, she said, "Yes, that's me. So, you—"</p>
<p>"I'm looking for someone," the black-haired youth interrupted her without the slightest courtesy. "Fifteen years old, brown hair, about 1.61 meters tall, with a birthmark on the neck. Do you have anyone like that here?"</p>
<p>His blunt interruption and the way he spoke, as if he wasn't asking for help but rather demanding it.</p>
<p>Seraphina's temper flared instantly, quelling the inexplicable unease. She scoffed coldly, "First, address me respectfully as 'Big Boss,' bow, and then politely repeat your question. Only then will I consider answering you."</p>
<p>The black-haired youth merely raised an eyebrow slightly, a faint twitch at the corner of his mouth, before completely ignoring Seraphina and walking directly towards her.</p>
<p>Though he exerted no overt sense of intimidation, Seraphina grew increasingly tense, the veins on the back of her hand bulging, ready at any moment to land a punch on his face.</p>
<p>But... the enigmatic black-haired youth did nothing. Just as Seraphina was on the brink of demolishing the entire mansion, he nonchalantly brushed past her.</p>
<p>"..."</p>
<p>Miss Wolf blinked, her forehead beaded with sweat, her expression bewildered.</p>
<p>Nothing… happened?</p>
<p>Why was he here? Was he really just looking for a missing person?</p>
<p>Why? Why would someone so powerful waste time on such a small matter?</p>
<p>No, someone this formidable shouldn't have trouble finding a missing person?</p>
<p>Instinctively, Seraphina turned her head and noticed a frail, ordinary adventurer trailing behind the black-haired youth. This person, in contrast, was entirely transparent to her, an ordinary individual with some peculiar disguise.</p>
<p>Ha... so his follower is an ordinary person?</p>
<p>Seraphina stared suspiciously for a long time. As the black-haired youth's figure was about to disappear, she suddenly spoke, "Are you truly here to find a missing person?"</p>
<p>Receiving no reply, and seeing him continue walking, Seraphina, more incensed, shouted, "Repeat the description. I'll see if I can help you find them!"</p>
<p>"No need," the black-haired youth said leisurely. "He's not here."</p>
<p>With that, he vanished around the corner.</p>
<p>"…What the hell?!"</p>
<p>Seraphina's eye twitched. "He comes here looking for someone, asks for help, and when I'm willing to assist, he says that someone is not here… This guy deserves a beating!"</p>
<p>Seraphina expected to be furious, but after a brief moment of irritation, she felt no further emotion. Realizing this… she found it increasingly bizarre.</p>
<p>That guy was definitely not simple, and there was undoubtedly something deeply wrong with him!</p>
<p>"Totok, what did you talk about with him? Did he mention anything about himself?" Seraphina asked the people who had conversed with the black-haired youth.</p>
<p>"He... he didn't say much, Sister Fenrir," one of them answered honestly. "He just said he was looking for someone..."</p>
