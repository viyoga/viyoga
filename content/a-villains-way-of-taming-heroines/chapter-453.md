<p>Following the successful taming of Ravenna, a semblance of tranquility seemed to return to the imperial capital, devoid of any significant upheaval.</p>
<p>Yet, this very calmness alerted the astute elites to an underlying anomaly.</p>
<p>Outside Ephesande's palace, Anthicheg, Evora's gaze narrowed as she scrutinized the sealed gates before her.</p>
<p>Her beloved mother had not convened the morning council nor attended to any state affairs for a continuous half-month.</p>
<p>The empire's nobility had grown accustomed to this disarray, preferring the chaos marking the twilight of Ephesande's reign. An emperor who does nothing is deemed the best kind of ruler.</p>
<p>However, for Evora, poised to inherit divine authority, this situation hinted at something profoundly… abnormal.</p>
<p>Common sense dictated that, despite nearing madness, Ephesande should have at least two to three years of life left. Typically, emperors at this stage would either desperately seek a breakthrough from the immense burden borne by Hydral or abandon all efforts for a final bout of indulgence.</p>
<p>Yet, none had secluded themselves within the Source Flame, merely clinging to life… as Ephesande did.</p>
<p>Initially, Evora believed her mother sought to delay the inevitable madness, but as the threshold approached, such actions seemed utterly futile.</p>
<p>Reflecting on the past, Evora reached out to touch the gates of Anthicheg, only for her palm to be instantly engulfed in fierce blood flames. With a slight click of her tongue, she withdrew her hand, but it was already severely charred, revealing blackened bones in mere moments.</p>
<p>Frowning, Evora's one hand turned into a knife, severed her entire other hand at the wrist, allowing a new one to regenerate amidst the flames.</p>
<p>"You're not merely clinging to life..."</p>
<p>The ferocious, unruly flames reminded Evora of the mother she knew in her youth—possessing the absolute power… that looked down upon the world.</p>
<p>Clasping her newly formed hand, she murmured, "Are you... amassing power?"</p>
<p>Evora, inherently cruel and ruthless yet never foolish, quickly pieced together numerous possibilities, her expression darkening with hesitation.</p>
<p>After a brief moment of deliberation, she resolved to act, transforming into a blaze of fire and vanishing from the spot.</p>
<p>Inside Anthicheg, amidst the eternal blaze of the Source Flame, the twilight monarch, who had lain dormant for a full half-month, opened her eyes.</p>
<p>"Such… a troublesome fool, but, no matter."</p>
<p>Those turbid flame-colored eyes were filled with unsettling chaos and madness.</p>
<p>"I have all the time in the world to teach you."</p>
<p>On the other side, Evora, who had instantly arrived in Ansel's bedroom, did not find Ansel there. Instead, she encountered a pitifully frail girl.</p>
<p>"Where is Ansel?" she inquired impatiently, addressing the girl before her who appeared slightly dazed and sported a mane of snow-white hair.</p>
<p>"Princess Evora... Greetings—"</p>
<p>Marlina, coming to her senses, initially greeted Evora, only to be rudely interrupted: "I asked you, where is he."</p>
<p>The irate Elder Princess, in a foul mood, telekinetically grasped Marlina's neck, lifting her off the ground without a trace of emotion: "Can't you understand simple speech?"</p>
<p>"Mr. Ansel is... in... the Empire... Royal Theatre... cough, cough, cough!"</p>
<p>After struggling to answer Evora's question amidst pain, Marlina was harshly thrown to the ground, the vivid burn marks on her neck silently narrating the agony she had just endured.</p>
<p>"The Royal Theatre... Does he still find the leisure and mood to frequent such places?"</p>
<p>Evora mused with a frown, then glanced at Marlina, who lay on the ground, continuously coughing.</p>
<p>"You are the mortal deliberately kept by Ansel's side... Marlina, if I'm not mistaken."</p>
<p>Marlina, struggling to her feet, bowed deeply to Evora in an utmost humble manner, attempting to speak, but her scorched throat was too hoarse to articulate complete sentences.</p>
<p>"How fragile... What use does he have for you? It hardly seems entertaining."</p>
<p>The Elder Princess waved her hand dismissively, and a surge of blood flame passed over Marlina's throat, healing her injuries instantly.</p>
<p>"Thank you... Thank you for your grace, and for remembering me, Your Highness."</p>
<p>Marlina, with hands clasped over her abdomen, bowed deeply, responding to Evora with a tone of respectful humility, devoid of any hint of displeasure.</p>
<p>"Enough of this nonsense, convey a message to Ansel for me—now, immediately."</p>
<p>Although unsure of Evora's intentions, Marlina responded respectfully: "As you command, Your Highness."</p>
<p>"Tell him: That old damn woman might make a move against you and Lord Flamelle, be extra vigilant."</p>
<p>Evora spoke these words nonchalantly, but Marlina's heart skipped a beat. She opened her mouth, unable to speak for a moment, then, overcoming her immense fear with a newfound strong will, she risked further torment from Evora to hesitantly ask: "Could you... repeat that, please?"</p>
<p>"What? You think I'm speaking nonsense?"</p>
<p>Evora scoffed coldly, but did not mistreat Marlina further, merely stating indifferently: "I haven't misspoken a single word, ensure you deliver this message to Ansel exactly as I said."</p>
<p>Then, her figure vanished from Ansel's bedroom.</p>
<p>Marlina stood frozen in place for a good four or five seconds, then, as if panicked, she ran towards the door in disarray. Despite her extensive training in noble etiquette, she was now as frantic as a person unable to coordinate their limbs, nearly tripping over her skirt and falling.</p>
<p>Five minutes later, Evora stood atop the roof of the Hydral Manor, arms crossed, watching as a carriage sped away from the manor's gates, then casting her gaze towards the highest point of the imperial capital, her eyes cold.</p>
<p>She had initially intended to directly inform Ansel of the matter, but upon encountering Marlina, Evora conceived a different plan.</p>
<p>The moment she touched the door of Anthicheg, Evora was certain that she had alarmed Ephesande.</p>
<p>Despite her mother's senility and clouded consciousness, anything concerning her life would undoubtedly awaken her lucidity—after all, it seemed all her thoughts were concentrated on this very aspect.</p>
<p>If Evora's speculation were true, then Ephesande would surely kill Marlina as a warning, unwilling to allow any disruptions to her plans. With Marlina serving as a cautionary example, Ephesande would not direct her wrath towards Evora, sparing her from risking her own safety.</p>
<p>However, if Marlina remained unharmed, it would indicate that… Evora's conjecture was incorrect, or at least, not entirely correct. That old creature might be planning to accumulate power for some other purpose.</p>
<p>Of course, theoretically, there exists another possibility, a terribly dire one.</p>
<p>— That Ephesande is absolutely confident in her victory.</p>
<p>But, this remains purely theoretical.</p>
<p>Evora looked up at the sky, where, at an imperceptible altitude, hung another divine being's sword, strategically placed there.</p>
<p>"Do you truly believe you can triumph over Flamelle, that monster?"</p>
<p>The Elder Princess scoffed disdainfully:</p>
<p>"You haven't lost your mind to that extent, have you, old fool?"</p>
