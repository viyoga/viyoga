<p>"... That's a fair point," Flamelle, stroking his chin, chuckled and shook his head, "I indeed didn't consider as thoroughly as you, Ans. So, you must have prepared in advance, right?"</p>
<p>"Of course," Ansel responded, "I have many things to do, none of which include dying at her hands or those of other fifth stages."</p>
<p>His composure and confidence made Lawrence, who was looking up at him, show an expression of utmost admiration. Even the proud sorcerer Toradon and the emotionally reserved Tyrus bowed their heads slightly in respect.</p>
<p>The pact with Hydral will not be inherited. After Hydral's death, the pact heads won't have much time left. The chaos and madness that they shared with Hydral will not dissipate with Hydral's death, but will intensify. Their lives have already begun the countdown with Flamelle's demise.</p>
<p>But they have followed Flamelle through a life more legendary than legends, with almost no regrets. Some pact heads may choose to die with Flamelle, some may end their lives somewhere after severing their last ties with the mortal world, or choose to die in the most spectacular way.</p>
<p>These legendary figures, their respect for Ansel, is not due to the difference in status, but a sincere admiration for their young lord.</p>
<p>"Well then, I'll take my leave, Father."</p>
<p>Ansel, helping Seraphina to stand up, said, "There are still many things to do."</p>
<p>"Ah, remember to visit your mother... She was upset that we didn't invite her to our chat." Flamelle, smiling, raised his teacup to Ansel.</p>
<p>"... I will," Ansel nodded slightly, and left the courtyard with Seraphina.</p>
<p>Flamelle watched the young man's retreating figure, his radiant golden hair identical to his wife's, his gaze suddenly became somewhat dazed and lost.</p>
<p>But that daze only lasted for a moment, he brought the teacup to his lips and took a gentle sip.</p>
<p>"Saville."</p>
<p>Hydral, who was already in decline, suddenly spoke, "Ansel will become the greatest Hydral, I've told you that, haven't I?"</p>
<p>"Yes," Lawrence, who had climbed onto the tea table, nodded, "Boss, you've said it countless times."</p>
<p>"That many times?"</p>
<p>The man chuckled, "Hmm... I suppose I have."</p>
<p>He stroked the wall of the teacup, his azure eyes, identical to Ansel's, reflected the surface of the tea, and also reflected countless... only he could see, but incomprehensible scenes of madness.</p>
<p>"I often wonder."</p>
<p>Flamelle, who seemed to be looking at something, said softly, "If Ansel wasn't a Hydral, would he have had a greater, more wonderful life?"</p>
<p>The pact heads remained silent. They could speak freely with Flamelle, but they didn't have the right to... express their views and feelings on this matter.</p>
<p>"My son, he had no choice in being born, but I did."</p>
<p>"I chose to bring him into this world... but does he harbor hatred for the blood that flows in him, the curse that binds his soul, and me, who brought him into this world?"</p>
<p>Flamelle drank the tea in the cup, took the wine brewed by Ansel from Saville's hand, poured himself a full glass, and drank it all in one gulp.</p>
<p>"Pfft... cough, cough... I don't know who Ansel learned this from, I clearly never drink."</p>
<p>The man smacked his lips, "How can he drink wine like tea? Well... the taste isn't too bad, I suppose."</p>
<p>Flamelle of Hydral stood up and walked to the center of the courtyard, casting his gaze at the highest point of the imperial city, at the second sun that burned all day.</p>
<p>The one who slumbers in the fire wakes up, and also casts a cold and dangerous gaze at him.</p>
<p>"You, a struggling loser, have no right to look at me like that, Ephesande."</p>
<p>The man in the noble suit, with an elegant and dignified demeanor, revealed a smile — that ferocious, cruel, violent... terrifying smile mixed with all negative emotions, proving that he was not as rational as he appeared in life, everything was like the madness in his sea-blue eyes.</p>
<p>"How about," he said with a smile, "we start now, and let the empire perish at our hands."</p>
<p>Shadow.</p>
<p>At this moment, a shadow that does not exist in reality completely envelops the imperial city.</p>
<p>Despite the clear weather and bright sunshine, all the people living in this huge city suddenly feel an indescribable terror and coldness. They instinctively look up, but they have no idea where this fear from the heart comes from, they can only see the dazzling sunshine.</p>
<p>Flamelle raised his left hand, and a huge cannon appeared at the bottom of the alchemical fortress in the sky, ten thousand meters above the imperial city.</p>
<p>The alchemical fortress, Nostos, excavated by Flamelle from the Zero Realm Enigma, a relic from the Sky Conquering Dynasty era, and then upgraded by him.</p>
<p>Saville bowed his head slightly, but his figure seemed to be between existence and non-existence; Lawrence's tiny red eyes were filled with a deeper blood color; Tyrus silently stood in front of Flamelle, his terrifying body over three meters tall seemed to have grown a bit more; Toradon held his scepter, and the top of the scepter was brewing the purest destruction.</p>
<p>As long as Flamelle has any thoughts of bringing down destruction, they will carry out Flamelle's thoughts without hesitation, starting from the imperial city, and completely destroy the entire empire.</p>
<p>"..."</p>
<p>That gaze was so long, but it seemed... so short.</p>
<p>The aging empress hiding in the Source Flame chose to avoid Flamelle's gaze, no longer looking at him. This act of submission brought the usual gentle smile back to the man's face. He waved his hand, Nostos retracted its destruction, still floating calmly in the sky above the imperial city.</p>
<p>The pact heads also retracted their respective pressures, not caring at all about how many fifth stages in the entire imperial city were scared and almost ran away on the spot.</p>
<p>"She is still so weak, so everything is fine."</p>
<p>Flamelle stretched, "Although Ans has his plans, since it's a problem I should solve... how can I let him worry and work hard to do it."</p>
<p>"As a father, I'm already unqualified enough, if I throw this mess to Ansel, Anne will make me sleep in a separate room."</p>
<p>"Lawrence, Saville," he straightened his collar, preparing to leave the courtyard, and said, "Go and knock on the fifth stages who might be ready to make a move. Since Ephesande has backed down, it doesn't matter if we kill a few more."</p>
<p>"No problem, boss!" "Understood, dear lord."</p>
<p>"As for Tyrus and Toradon... you guys forget it, if you start fighting, you'll tear down the imperial city, and that will be a bit troublesome. I've been looking for the location of another Zero Realm Enigma recently, come with me when the time comes."</p>
<p>"Yes, my lord." "Understood, my lord."</p>
<p>The man nodded, and walked calmly towards the outside of the manor.</p>
<p>Flamelle of Hydral walked on the right path, bathed in warm sunlight, full of brightness.</p>
