<p>Ansel picked up Ravenna on his way back.</p>
<p>Her mood was evidently complex, a mix of bad and good, a nuance only someone as experienced as Ansel could grasp.</p>
<p>"Looking back now, I realize there were many times I could have seen that my father and mother... always cared about me," Ravenna said softly, sitting opposite Ansel in the living room.</p>
<p>"But fate made me miss those moments, forcing me to walk the path… it had set."</p>
<p>"Do you hate it?"</p>
<p>"Not that much," Ravenna shook her head. "Ultimately, I was too foolish, too self-centered."</p>
<p>"That's why you're so hard to deceive," Ansel joked. "If you don't even hate fate that much, how could I have dared to tell you about it back then?"</p>
<p>"..."</p>
<p>Ravenna's gaze gradually sharpened. "I told you, didn't I? You have to pay the price—for your distortions, your malice, your distrust."</p>
<p>"...Yes, I think you did mention that," Ansel replied nonchalantly.</p>
<p>Ravenna's eyes narrowed slightly. "Do you think I can't do anything to you?"</p>
<p>This made Ansel laugh. "I'd like to see what you can do to me."</p>
<p>Ravenna said nothing, just stared at Ansel.</p>
<p>Suddenly, Ansel felt a weight on his back, and a pair of hands wrapped around his neck.</p>
<p>"Do you really think... I can't do anything to you?"</p>
<p>Two identical voices echoed in the living room.</p>
<p>At the same time, the Ravenna behind Ansel bit his ear and whispered, "Father, or… Daddy?"</p>
<p>Even Lord Ansel was momentarily silent for three or four seconds.</p>
<p>Then, he smiled as if everything was going according to plan.</p>
<p>"So, this is the price you want me to pay?"</p>
<p>"I won't lose. Don't forget my current state."</p>
<p>Ravenna pointed to her abdomen, her eyes showing rare defiance. "Even in this form, the shape inside... can be adaptive."</p>
<p>She slowly climbed onto Ansel's lap, joining the Ravenna behind him, tightening her grip on him. "Don't you love... shaping some passages?" she whispered in his other ear.</p>
<p>"You're doing something very foolish," Ansel's eyes turned serious.</p>
<p>"It's not too late to regret, Venna."</p>
<p>"You should be the one saying that."</p>
<p>A taller, more graceful Ravenna appeared beside Ansel, stroking his cheek and narrowing her eyes slightly. "You still have a chance to surrender to me, Ansel."</p>
<p>"Heh heh... heh heh heh..."</p>
<p>Ten minutes later, the door to the living room suddenly opened.</p>
<p>"Ansel, hurry up and pack! When are we—"</p>
<p>The spirited Miss Seraphina stood dumbfounded, staring at the room with one man and three women.</p>
<p>"...Busybody." The smaller Ravenna frowned, "This is my punishment for Ansel, get out."</p>
<p>"You... you!"</p>
<p>Seraphina's blood rushed to her head: "You're asking for it!"</p>
<p>"It doesn't matter if you stay." Another petite Ravenna, lying on Ansel's back, licked his earlobe, "You can only watch, you don't have the ability to join in."</p>
<p>"Hmm..."</p>
<p>The tall Ravenna let out a lazy hum: "That's indeed the case... ah…ha"</p>
<p>The wolf girl lost her mind.</p>
<p>"You think you're the only one who can transform... you think you're the only one who can transform, huh!"</p>
<p>She shouted furiously: "Today, I'll show you just how compatible Ansel and I are!"</p>
<p>With a fluffy wolf tail, furry wolf ears, and a rapidly increasing height…</p>
<p>Looking at the Seraphina lunging at him, Ansel couldn't help but sigh inwardly.</p>
<p>One after another... naughty girls.</p>
<p>*</p>
<p>The young Hydral leisurely buttoned his shirt, looking spirited and full of energy as he approached the map on the living room wall.</p>
<p>Two girls, too exhausted to even make a sound, lay entwined on the soft sofa.</p>
<p>Ravenna's campaign had ultimately ended in failure. Although Seraphina had intervened and caused trouble, in the end, the two of them had joined forces to try to defeat the demon king.</p>
<p>Unfortunately, neither the ever-changing Miss Ravenna nor the strong and tall Miss Seraphina could do more than make the demon king break a sweat.</p>
<p>After the relaxing pleasure, Ansel didn't continue to linger, as he had gone a bit overboard this time. Both girls had fallen asleep, leaving no one to cuddle with.</p>
<p>He gazed at the map of the entire continent, hands clasped behind his back.</p>
<p>"The North, abundant in extraordinary resources, the best place to cultivate extraordinary combat power, and... the revolutionaries."</p>
<p>The young man murmured softly, continuing: "The Western Kingdom, with numerous Zero Realm Enigma entrances, the highest concentration and purity of etheric regions, a land of conflict and opportunity."</p>
<p>He then turned his gaze to the East:</p>
<p>"The Eastern Port, the Lost Sea, trade, abundance, the only sea route connecting to another continent..."</p>
<p>"As for the Southern Territory, with me there, it's the least likely to stir up any trouble."</p>
<p>As he muttered to himself, a still weary voice suddenly sounded from behind him.</p>
<p>"…Just finished that kind of thing, and you're already eager to get back to work?"</p>
<p>Ravenna wrapped her arms around Ansel's waist from behind, slightly poking her head out.</p>
<p>Ansel couldn't help but laugh, "Are you talking about the old you?"</p>
<p>"The current me is the same… I just didn't expect you to be like this."</p>
<p>"I've always been like this. It seems you still don't know me well enough, Venna."</p>
<p>"Hmph."</p>
<p>Ravenna snorted softly, tightening her arms around Ansel.</p>
<p>Ansel placed his hand over hers, turned his head slightly, and said with a radiant smile, "Are you reassured now?"</p>
<p>"…Reassured about what?"</p>
<p>The petite scholar's expression stiffened slightly, and she averted her gaze.</p>
<p>"You're not the type to be anxious about such things." Ansel patted Ravenna's head gently, "Why be so insecure?"</p>
<p>Ravenna remained silent, merely pressing her head against Ansel's back.</p>
<p>After a long while, she whispered, "But I have already… become such a monster. I'm not even a puppet anymore."</p>
<p>Ravenna was actually afraid of this.</p>
<p>Who would have such feelings for a… for a monster that doesn't even have a tangible body, made of some alchemical substance?</p>
<p>So, rather than a punishment, it was more of a cautious test for Ansel. If he showed any displeasure or even resistance, Ravenna would immediately stop.</p>
<p>"What is it?"</p>
<p>But Ansel turned around, unable to suppress his laughter, and hugged her tightly:</p>
<p>"Am I not a monster too?"</p>
<p>Ravenna looked up, staring blankly at the face so close to hers.</p>
<p>Seeing Ravenna looking at him like this, Ansel extended his hands, making a clawing gesture and a playful "rawr" face.</p>
<p>"…Pfft."</p>
<p>"Is it funny?" Ansel looked at his hands shaped like claws.</p>
<p>"No." Ravenna shook her head, stood on tiptoe, and pecked Ansel on the lips.</p>
<p>She gazed into those mesmerizing sea-blue eyes and whispered:</p>
<p>"Very cute, I like it a lot."</p>
<p>"From now on, always make that face for me, Mr. Monster."</p>
<p>Ansel pinched Ravenna's cheek, "As you wish, Mrs. Monster."</p>
<p>They gazed into each other's eyes and smiled.</p>
<p>For they had finally found a brilliant color in their otherwise identical gray lives, a color they would never forget.</p>
<p>(Volume Two, Gray Rhapsody, End)</p>
