<p>"I'm sorry."</p>
<p>Seraphina first cast her gaze at the man who should be manipulating the spatial magic, slightly raising her chin. That already vaguely domineering look in her eyes made the man, who was full of hatred and wanted to settle Seraphina here, feel his hands and feet turn ice cold, his spirit as if hammered.</p>
<p>"Your trick, it's time to end."</p>
<p>The burly man's pupils contracted sharply, and he roared, "Albatross!"</p>
<p>He immediately switched places with the male revolutionary. The next moment, the burly man felt the oncoming... gale!</p>
<p>That was the breath of a brutal beast, the tidal wave stirred up by a monster chasing power!</p>
<p>He saw, under that flying snow, those excited and fanatical crimson eyes, and... a punch without any fancy!</p>
<p>"I've been waiting… for you."</p>
<p>He heard a soft chuckle.</p>
<p>The burly man who had switched places didn't have time to react, his abdomen dramatically caved in under Seraphina's strike. If time were to slow down, one could see his bear-like body arching entirely, his back even exploding from the intense impact of the punch. From his ribs to his internal organs, from his organs to his spine... the continuous explosion almost pierced him through in one strike!</p>
<p>The current Seraphina wouldn't consider the so-called optimal battle situation, she only had one thing to do, and that was to defeat, to shatter the strongest person standing in front of her!</p>
<p>That frail guy, she had no interest in fighting him.</p>
<p>The burly man spat out a mouthful of blood mixed with organ fragments, he roared in shock and anger: "Transfer scroll!"</p>
<p>Seraphina didn't stop them from escaping. Firstly, Marlina had said it was best to let them run, and secondly... that punching bag had lost his will to fight, his confidence had been shattered by Seraphina's punch, and the current Seraphina didn't need such a worthless opponent.</p>
<p>"Phew..."</p>
<p>A strong sense of fatigue swept over her, causing the young girl to stagger a few steps, she had to lean on something to steady herself.</p>
<p>"Is this my spiritual essence?" Seraphina squeezed her sore fist and grinned, "Although I don't know what other effects there are, it feels pretty good."</p>
<p>Not yet reaching the third stage, but being able to forcefully drive the spiritual essence again and again, only a monster like her could do it.</p>
<p>The girl struggled to walk out of the shabby hut, showing a brilliant smile to her worried sister not far away.</p>
<p>"Marli!"</p>
<p>She waved her fist vigorously, shouting excitedly: "I did it!"</p>
<p>Marlina, who was still worried about whether something had happened to Seraphina, was stunned: "Wha, what?"</p>
<p>The girl, whose skin was cracked and bleeding from her arms, cheeks, and thighs, casually wiped it off, then used her blood-stained hands to comb back her short hair. As the snow-white hair was dyed with a striking red, it added a breathtaking, proud, and stunningly beautiful demeanor to Seraphina.</p>
<p>She raised her index finger, pointed to the sky, and laughed brightly: "I want to tell Ansel —"</p>
<p>"I must be, the best one!"</p>
<p>*</p>
<p>Ansel was unusually... perhaps it could be said that it was not unusual, because he had been daydreaming, or possibly contemplating, more and more recently.</p>
<p>Even sitting in the study, he didn't read, but silently stared at the bottle of black liquid placed on the desk, or looked at the snake ring that exuded a violent aura, rolling at his fingertips.</p>
<p>"...Speak, Saville."</p>
<p>Ansel, who had temporarily escaped from his contemplation, said softly: "How are things on your side?"</p>
<p>"As per your instructions, I have ensured that there are no opponents in the Red Frost territory that could seriously fight Miss Seraphina."</p>
<p>The old butler bowed slightly: "The revolutionary army is accumulating strength, their main force will not appear before the Blazing Ice Viper awakens."</p>
<p>Ansel sighed: "I've troubled you, Saville. I know this task is a bit too much, but it's... a variable I have to prevent."</p>
<p>At this point, there were too many things that fate could interfere with. To let Saville run around alone, controlling all the strong people who could threaten Seraphina, was indeed unrealistic.</p>
<p>But Ansel had to do it.</p>
<p>Saville smiled: "Your requests have never been excessive, young lord. It's just..."</p>
<p>His tone changed slightly, with a hint of persuasion: "The Blazing Ice Viper will awaken in three days, letting the current Miss Seraphina face it, is somewhat..."</p>
<p>"...I know."</p>
<p>Ansel replied, placing the ring flat on the table. His sea-blue eyes reflected two objects. "I've also been, prepared."</p>
<p>He said so softly, but his eyes drooped slightly.</p>
<p>Just then, the door of the study was "bang" directly knocked open, Seraphina, who was wrapped in bandages all over her body, like a runaway dog, completely ignoring Marlina's pull, ran to Ansel's desk in extreme joy, propped her hands on the table, blinked her bright eyes and looked down at Ansel, almost sticking to her favorite person:</p>
<p>"Ansel, Ansel!"</p>
<p>The girl announced joyfully: "I found the method! I found that feeling! I can reach the third stage!"</p>
<p>She climbed onto the table again, put her hands on Ansel's shoulders, her eyes full of uncontrollable happiness: "I can become a third stage now! That feeling... that feeling, my impression is too deep, I will never forget it in my life! Wait for me, let me brew, um..."</p>
<p>"...Wait."</p>
<p>Ansel suddenly spoke, for some reason, his voice was a bit hoarse.</p>
<p>The young Hydral half-closed his eyes and waved his hand: "Saville, Marlina, you go out first."</p>
<p>The old butler disappeared as ordered, Marlina also retreated with a slight surprise in her salute, and soon only the two of them were left in the study.</p>
<p>"An, Ansel..."</p>
<p>Seeing that only she and Ansel were left, Seraphina's face unconsciously turned a little red, she moved her body back subconsciously, and said a little coyly: "Why did you call Marli and the old butler away?"</p>
<p>"Because I have something to say to you, Seraphina."</p>
<p>Ansel held Seraphina's face and said softly: "I need you to do something."</p>
<p>The temperature of Ansel's palm quickly rose, Seraphina wanted to pull away, but couldn't help but giggle: "You, you don't have to be so serious. Whatever Ansel says, I will do!"</p>
<p>"Is that so, that's good."</p>
<p>Ansel smiled in relief, and said to Seraphina with an incredibly gentle voice: "Then, don't ascend to the throne, Seraphina."</p>
<p>In the girl's slightly frozen smile, Hydral whispered: "Don't ascend to the throne in your way, I will... help you."</p>
