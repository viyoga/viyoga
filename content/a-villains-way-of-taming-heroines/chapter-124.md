<p>"Sir Sparklens," Marlina calmly stated, "It appears you require some assistance."</p>
<p>"Yes, indeed," the lord of Sparklens City stuttered, then quickly nodded, "My guards have discovered two members of the revolutionary army."</p>
<p>"Following the instructions of Lord Hydral, I, of course, did not harm them, but..."</p>
<p>The noble, who was clearly indulging in forbidden substances, making him look rather frail and languid, whispered, "The trade chain you helped me establish with the Duke of Ironblade's Soul Eater a week ago might be damaged because of this..."</p>
<p>Seraphina clicked her tongue in annoyance, "That's your problem, why involve us?"</p>
<p>Although she was not entirely sure what Marlina had done for the nobles, Seraphina knew her sister well. The Marlina of now was different from the future Lady Violet who would sacrifice everything to climb up; she would not do anything morally reprehensible.</p>
<p>But the thought of cleaning up after the nobles irritated Seraphina greatly.</p>
<p>Baron Sparklens turned to Seraphina, forcing a smile, "You're right, Lord Marlowe. It's just that my abilities are limited, and there's also Lord Hydral's instructions..."</p>
<p>"Enough," Marlina glanced at Baron Sparklens, her voice cold, "Where are they? Seraphina and I will handle it."</p>
<p>Seraphina was taken aback, about to say something, but upon seeing her sister's indifferent and stern profile, she swallowed her displeasure.</p>
<p>*</p>
<p>"Marli, what's the use of helping that noble?" Seraphina, squatting by the roadside with her hands supporting her cheeks, asked her sister in a puzzled tone, "And what strange things has Ansel done again?"</p>
<p>"Most of the poor in Sparklens City died in the great cold wave," Marlina's gaze swept across the desolate street, "As a punishment, he must hand over his city to the revolutionary army."</p>
<p>"... Is that so?"</p>
<p>Seraphina scratched her head, not feeling that the revolutionary army taking over the city was a good thing. Just looking at that mad Snow Beast, she knew that those guys were only slightly better than the nobles.</p>
<p>"What's the situation with the trade chain that guy mentioned, do we have to deal with it?"</p>
<p>A cold smile appeared on Marlina's increasingly mature and pretty face, "Baron Sparklens is short-sighted, a fool addicted to forbidden drugs. I helped him connect with the people under the Duke of Ironblade, and he thought it was a favor from Mr. Ansel, but he never thought... would Mr. Ansel waste resources and time in a city about to change hands?"</p>
<p>Listening to this, Seraphina was even more confused, "So, so what does this have to do with us?"</p>
<p>Marlina patted Seraphina's head and smiled, "This is a gift I want to give to Mr. Ansel, so I need your help, Seri."</p>
<p>Upon hearing this, Seraphina immediately stood up, rubbing her hands together, "You should have said so earlier, Marli. I don't understand those strange plans, as long as I can help Ansel, that's good!"</p>
<p>She excitedly waved her fist, "So, I just need to knock down those two members of the revolutionary army?"</p>
<p>"Let them escape," Marlina said, "No need to detain them."</p>
<p>According to the information provided by Baron Sparklens, the two members of the revolutionary army who infiltrated Sparklens City and acted as scouts and insiders were hiding in a small house across the street. Seraphina rubbed her neck and strode across the street.</p>
<p>Marlina watched Seraphina's back, the smile on her face disappeared, and her expression gradually became worried -— although she had not shown it from just now until now, Marlina was always thinking non-stop, wondering why Seraphina had not shown any signs of reaching the third stage.</p>
<p>"Seri thinks she's hiding it well, but it's absolutely impossible to hide it from Mr. Ansel."</p>
<p>The young girl lowered her eyes and murmured to herself, "Is Mr. Ansel acknowledging its occurrence because it doesn't matter whether Seri advances to the third stage, or is it because..."</p>
<p>"... Mr. Ansel has already anticipated that Seri cannot advance to the third stage, and knows what is hindering her advancement, but... deliberately did not tell Seri?"</p>
<p>Marlina, who lacked key information, could not continue to deduce the matter. She closed her eyes and could only sigh lightly in the end.</p>
<p>"Mr. Ansel will not harm Seri, even if he does this, there must be an important reason."</p>
<p>The slight confusion caused by thinking was instantly dispelled by her, and Marlina's expression returned to calm, staring at the small house across the street.</p>
<p>*</p>
<p>At this time, Seraphina, who had already walked to the front of the house and then kicked the door open, did not know that her sister had thought of something crucial. In her single-threaded thinking, there were only Marlina's words and the results derived from these words.</p>
<p>Marli has a gift for Ansel, so she has to do this → doing this will make Ansel happy!</p>
<p>As long as there is such simple logic, as long as there is such a sense of purpose in action, Seraphina's fists can be filled with power.</p>
<p>"Hello, rodents of the Revolutionary Army."</p>
<p>The girl draped in a black cloak stood in the living room, one hand on her hip, her chin slightly raised. Her defiant and mocking expression made her look like a perfect villain.</p>
<p>"I offer you two choices."</p>
<p>She mimicked the words that the scoundrel Wendigo had once said to her, returning them to the Revolutionary Army.</p>
<p>"First, leave immediately with your tails between your legs. "</p>
<p>"Second, wait for me to break your limbs and throw you out."</p>
<p>The two members of the Revolutionary Army, who had infiltrated the city of Sparklens disguised as a couple, were stunned as they stared at Seraphina —there was no ether fluctuation emanating from her… she's below the third stage?</p>
<p>Their first reaction was: We're surrounded!</p>
<p>Then the second reaction: Using this fool as bait, do they think we're idiots?!</p>
<p>The man and woman exchanged a glance, and without any unnecessary words, they acted in perfect unison. The man dashed towards the window, intending to retreat immediately, while the woman raised her hand, clenched it, and from the living room's flower pots, boxes, and various decorations, countless sharp nails flew out, bombarding Seraphina from all directions!</p>
<p>The rain of nails tore the floor to shreds, but Seraphina, who should have been standing there, had disappeared. The female sorcerer, who had just made a cool move, was now spinning in the air, crashing into the retreating male Revolutionary Army member like a cannonball.</p>
<p>Whether it was the female sorcerer who was sent flying with a punch, or the man who felt like his back was about to break, their third reaction was —</p>
<p>She's not a third stage?!</p>
