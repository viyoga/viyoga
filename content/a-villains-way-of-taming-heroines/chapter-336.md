<p>"Ansel!"</p>
<p>Upon crossing the fiery portal, Seraphina caught sight of Ansel and immediately dashed towards him with exultant cries.</p>
<p>"Stop."</p>
<p>A frigid, haughty female voice compelled Seraphina to stop, as Evora, standing beside Ansel, furrowed her brow.</p>
<p>"You reek too much... calling you Ansel's dog, do you truly have no distinction from the wild beasts in my hunting grounds? Can't you use magic to tidy yourself?"</p>
<p>"You—"</p>
<p>Seraphina's eyes flashed with malice, her burgeoning feral spirit causing her to instinctively radiate a brutal, menacing aura, which Evora easily perceived as hostility.</p>
<p>"Come, Seraphina."</p>
<p>Just as Evora raised her hand, seemingly to chastise Seraphina, Ansel interjected, chuckling softly as he said, "Ignore her. Your scent is far more pleasant than hers."</p>
<p>"..." Seraphina was momentarily stunned, then blinked and let out a light chuckle, joyfully skipping to Ansel's side and making a mischievous face at Evora's back.</p>
<p>Seraphina's body indeed bore the marks of the jungle hunting grounds: bloodstains, mud, and various pungent odors. After standing to Anse's side, she hesitantly sniffed her own arm, only relaxing when she saw no abnormal reaction on Ansel's face.</p>
<p>"Do you always neglect logic when defending your little dog, Ansel?"</p>
<p>Evora crossed her arms, scoffing coldly: "Now you look like a lovesick fool of a young lad, isn't that a bit too embarrassing?"</p>
<p>"Because, compared to somehow overpowering feminine aura, I prefer Seraphina's wild nature."</p>
<p>Ansel ruffled the wolf girl's head, who purred contentedly.</p>
<p>"Rather than saying I'm a lovesick fool..."</p>
<p>Young Hydral, with a slight tilt of his head, gazed at the lavishly dressed, long-legged Evora, unable to suppress a laugh as he said, "It's more like our esteemed Lord elder princess, a dissatisfied, unfulfilled woman ."</p>
<p>Seraphina, who was being petted and enjoying it, quickly pulled Ansel's sleeve, not wanting him to start another argument because of her.</p>
<p>Evora said no more, merely huffing and vanishing in a burst of flames. The grand hall was left with only Ansel, Seraphina, and Ravenna, who stood with her head bowed, her expression vacant and dazed.</p>
<p>Ansel glanced at Ravenna but remained silent, instead turning to Seraphina, holding her hand, and speaking gently, "Did you have fun?"</p>
<p>"So much fun!"</p>
<p>Seraphina nodded vigorously, her face aglow with unwavering joy.</p>
<p>After slaying the Tidecaller, Seraphina had frolicked in Evora's hunting grounds for a while, even killing two fourth-stage monsters and encountering a fifth-stage giant turtle. Our fearless Miss Seraphina bravely punched it, only to find that the creature didn't even bother to retaliate, simply walking away.</p>
<p>In summary, Seraphina had never experienced such a luxurious and perfect birthday, and she indeed had a joyful time.</p>
<p>"Since the days I've been with you, Ansel, every day is wonderful… I feel happier and happier! "</p>
<p>Seraphina, her cheeks flushed with a rosy hue, demurely lowered her gaze, seemingly unable to contain the excitement within her heart. She leaned in for a kiss, only to become acutely aware of the dirt that marred her skin, leaving her head awkwardly suspended in mid-air.</p>
<p>Yet Ansel, undeterred, cradled the back of her head, pressing her tender, slightly wild lips against his own.</p>
<p>"What's the matter?"</p>
<p>He inquired, eyebrows arched in playful challenge as he slightly pulled away. "Do you find me repulsive?"</p>
<p>Caught off guard, Seraphina hesitated for a moment before embracing Ansel with fervor, engaging in a passionate and untamed kiss.</p>
<p>The sound of their mingling breaths, the entwining of lips and tongues, and the girl's expertly skills honed by Ansel…were, to any onlooker, exceedingly intense, enough to induce a blush.</p>
<p>And there was but one onlooker present.</p>
<p>Ravenna's discomfiture and loss of control, stemming from Marlina's words and initiated by Ansel's test, found its culmination in Seraphina's declaration, leading to the crashing of her self.</p>
<p>All the hesitations, confusions, vacillations, and contemplations she had experienced pointed inexorably to the outcome she least desired to confront.</p>
<p>She had considered the possibility that while Seraphina could not deceive her, could Ansel have deceived Seraphina? Was this layer of deceit part of his calculations as well?</p>
<p>Yet, what pained and seemed absurd to Ravenna was the belief that — Ansel would not deceive Seraphina.</p>
<p>Indeed, even if she perceived Seraphina to have been tamed by Ansel's design, the Ansel of now would surely not deceive Seraphina.</p>
<p>— Ansel of Hydral would not stoop to deceive someone he cared for, someone he loved deeply, for something as trivial as the forsaken herself/</p>
<p>Such a cold and harsh reality was inescapable for Ravenna, for her intelligence laid bare the truth, and the answer emerged unbidden before her eyes.</p>
<p>The young couple before her, seemingly indulging in their desires, were in fact exchanging a pure and evident love, a truth so glaring it was almost blinding. To Ravenna, what was blinding was not the love between Ansel and Seraphina, but the irreplaceable… nature of their bond.</p>
<p>—An irreplaceable bond that once felt so near, yet now seemed so distant.</p>
<p>"Alright, if that's what you truly desire, we can discuss it further tonight," Ansel said, steadying Seraphina by her waist, their foreheads touching as he spoke with a light chuckle, "Today, we shall do as you wish."</p>
<p>"Hmm…ha…"</p>
<p>Seraphina, breathing heavily, reluctantly licked Ansel's lips, her inner fervor and strength not diminished by the day's vigorous hunt but rather intensified.</p>
<p>Clinging to Ansel's hand, she nestled against him like a puppy circling its master, her silly grin belying her contentment.</p>
<p>"Take a short rest, you can go back to the village with Marlina later. I won't intrude," Ansel suggested, pinching Seraphina's cheek affectionately.</p>
<p>"But remember to return on time tonight, alright?"</p>
<p>"I know, I know!"</p>
<p>Seraphina responded with glee, planting another kiss on Ansel. "I wish every day could be just like this."</p>
<p>"Hmm? If that's what you wish, Seraphina, it's not out of the question."</p>
<p>"Ah... No, no, no... Let's not," Seraphina hastily objected, shaking her head vigorously at Ansel's seemingly serious consideration.</p>
<p>"That won't do; I would become a complete fool, a useless person! I am, cough, the strongest pact head for Ansel... Ah! Ansel, look, look, this is the Tidecaller! I've taken care of it, hehe!"</p>
