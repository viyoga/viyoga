<p>The large Adventure's Guild stationed at Dispute Fortress comprises three factions.</p>
<p>Namely, the [Hive] led by Queen Bee, Yvaine Silmer; the [Executioners] under the command of the Black Omen, Cabossa Penther; and the [Whistling Legion] of Dispute Fortress's lord, Bryce Palmer Leo, also known as "Whistling Chaos."</p>
<p>Everyone, whether residing in Dispute Fortress or visiting for some purpose, knows one common rule: under no circumstances should they provoke adventurers from these three guilds.</p>
<p>Even the most renowned hereditary nobles must bow their heads if they lack the strength when they come here.</p>
<p>Yet today, someone audaciously affronted two of these guilds simultaneously.</p>
<p>What's more astonishing is that the perpetrator… left behind a "handle."</p>
<p>Queen Bee Yvaine was the first to arrive at Dream Island, the finest brothel in Dispute Fortress, now devoid of any patrons, with an air of chilling danger.</p>
<p>"Is it her?"</p>
<p>Yvaine turned to the tall, slender woman with slightly sunken cheeks beside her, frowning slightly: "You said that person killed two people, then suddenly fled... but left his servant behind?"</p>
<p>She gazed at the head of the largest brothel in Dispute Fortress, her eyes narrowing: "My dear little sister, are you serious?"</p>
<p>Yvaine despised this woman and had always sought an opportunity to kill her.</p>
<p>This self-proclaimed Snake Attendant had arrived in Dispute Fortress about half a year ago and monopolized the brothel market within two months... a market that originally belonged to the Hive.</p>
<p>The profits from the sex industry far exceeded common imagination, and high-end brothels offered many benefits beyond money. Yet, despite fighting on home turf, the Hive was utterly defeated by Snake Attendant. Bryce had not taken a stance, likely because Snake Attendant offered too much.</p>
<p>Exhaling smoke, Snake Attendant glanced at Yvaine: "What about the Worker Bee? Can't he identify her?"</p>
<p>"He needs some rest."</p>
<p>Yvaine sneered, then turned her gaze back to the frail adventurer sitting on the sofa, calmly sipping tea.</p>
<p>She could immediately tell this adventurer was a girl and also discern her absolute composure and calmness.</p>
<p>"He killed someone on your turf, and you still entertain his servant like this?"</p>
<p>This composure made Yvaine's eye twitch slightly. She stared intently at the girl disguised as a boy, her face gradually revealing a dangerous, sinister curve.</p>
<p>"You are being overly polite, Snake Attendant."</p>
<p>"Because he said before he left, let this young girl wait here."</p>
<p>Snake Attendant exhaled a thick cloud of white smoke indifferently and said in a hoarse voice, "If he dares to kill your people, do you think he wouldn't dare to kill me?"</p>
<p>"Ms. Snake Attendant is right."</p>
<p>The girl placed the teacup on the small table beside the sofa and said with a radiant smile, "I hope you also consider this calmly, madam."</p>
<p>"Calmly... consider?"</p>
<p>Yvaine was stunned for two seconds, then burst into laughter. She pointed at Marlina, then turned to look at Snake Attendant, her face showing an expression of great amusement:</p>
<p>"Look, look... my dear sister, we are being admonished by a mortal, being told by a mortal to 'calm down.'"</p>
<p>Queen Bee walked up to Marlina, scrutinizing her as if she were a rare treasure, and said with great emotion, "This is truly... an unprecedented experience."</p>
<p>Snake Attendant shook her cigarette holder expressionlessly: "If you want to do something to her, do it quickly, don't waste my time."</p>
<p>"I didn't ask you to stay."</p>
<p>"But Ms. Snake Attendant must ensure that it was you who made the first move against me, otherwise, how will she explain to Mr. Faust?"</p>
<p>Marlina smiled, her simple, androgynous adventurer attire unable to conceal the charm of her smile.</p>
<p>"..."</p>
<p>Yvaine's hand paused slightly as she reached for Marlina's head, but only for a moment before she continued.</p>
<p>However, she did not do anything to Marlina's head but instead removed the beret from it.</p>
<p>"Such enviable hair," Yvaine remarked as she stroked Marlina's hair, smiling, "What is your name, lovely lady?"</p>
<p>"Margarete Gretchen Müller."</p>
<p>"Very well, I shall call you Miss Margarete."</p>
<p>The mature beauty sat down beside Marlina, intimately close, like an old friend she hadn't seen in years.</p>
<p>"Miss Margarete, you seem... very confident."</p>
<p>Yvaine nonchalantly twirled Marlina's hair around her finger: "Do you not recognize me, or are you simply not very bright?"</p>
<p>Marlina did not respond. Instead, she drew a revolver from the holster at her waist and took a silk handkerchief from her pocket, meticulously wiping the gun.</p>
<p>"This... pfft, hahahahaha—"</p>
<p>The Queen Bee, after a moment of stunned silence, burst into laughter, her shoulders shaking with mirth as she gently pushed Marlina's shoulder:</p>
<p>"Miss Margarete, is this your answer? Is this your confidence? An ether firearm?"</p>
<p>"Mr. Faust believes that I shouldn't be left here with nothing at all."</p>
<p>Marlina smiled shyly: "One must have something to rely on."</p>
<p>She ejected the cylinder, curiously examining its internal structure, as if she had never used such a thing before.</p>
<p>"So... hahahaha... this is your reliance?"</p>
<p>Yvaine grasped Marlina's hand, laughing uncontrollably as she pressed the gun barrel to her own head, still unable to stop laughing: "Go ahead, shoot, Miss Margarete. Let me see how hard and hot the firearm Mr. Faust bestowed upon you is."</p>
<p>"What are you talking about, madam?" Marlina said helplessly, "I haven't loaded the bullets yet."</p>
<p>"Oh, that's true. Then hurry up and load the bullets, dear Miss Margarete."</p>
<p>The Queen Bee giggled as she held the barrel, seemingly eager for Marlina to fire a shot directly at her head.</p>
<p>"Even if I loaded the bullets, I wouldn't shoot you, madam."</p>
<p>Marlina blinked: "I have no reason to do so, do I? Just like..."</p>
<p>She smiled and extended her hand, gently prying Yvaine's fingers off the barrel one by one:</p>
<p>"Just like you have no reason to harm me."</p>
<p>The smile on Yvaine's face gradually faded.</p>
<p>She gripped Marlina's wrist, her fingers slowly tightening, the sound of bones being compressed and faintly cracking emanating from her palm.</p>
<p>"Confidence is a good thing, Miss Margarete."</p>
<p>The woman, who neither used nor deigned to use extraordinary power, looked at the beautiful girl before her, whose face was gradually turning pale, with an even more sadistic smile.</p>
<p>"I have no reason to harm you, hmm?"</p>
<p>She licked her lips, not crushing Marlina's wrist outright, but applying pressure bit by bit, savoring the process of tormenting her.</p>
<p>"Guess if your hand can be saved?"</p>
