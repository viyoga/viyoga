<p>Suellen knew better than anyone that the reign of the Flamefeast was over.</p>
<p>Her unrealistic dreams had been shattered… in such an absurd manner.</p>
<p>She would rather have been killed by Evora, who inherited the power of the Flamefeast, or seen her mother truly find a way to ascend to the seventh stage and achieve eternity. She would have preferred such a failure over losing in such a ridiculous way.</p>
<p>As a member of the Flamefeast, she would rather face punishment from someone who shared her blood than… endure the miserable fate that might await her.</p>
<p>Sorcerers might capture her out of curiosity about the Flamefeast bloodline, or perverse strongmen might take her as a plaything because of her royal status. In any case… her end would be unimaginably tragic.</p>
<p>But why? Why did Suellen not flee immediately like her kin, to wander in exile?</p>
<p>"How long have you been here, dear Princess Suellen?"</p>
<p>...It was because of this.</p>
<p>Because of the person who spoke these words.</p>
<p>"..."</p>
<p>Still wearing a pure white dress, as innocent as an iris, the girl slowly turned around.</p>
<p>Suellen saw the handsome young man she had been waiting for.</p>
<p>Leaning on his scepter, he walked leisurely through the ruins, sighing softly: "This is truly poignant, isn't it? A place where countless great figures once bowed respectfully has become such a desolate ruin, ignored by all."</p>
<p>The young Hydral tilted his head slightly, looking at the delicate and pitiable flower before him: "Your feelings must be very complicated, Princess Suellen."</p>
<p>"Lord Ansel..."</p>
<p>Suellen spoke with difficulty, her voice somewhat hoarse.</p>
<p>"I hope I haven't stirred up painful memories."</p>
<p>Ansel continued forward, the rubble before him crumbling away, as if bowing to a king, clearing a path to the throne.</p>
<p>"After all," he paused, then turned back with a smile: "I was the one who killed the Empress."</p>
<p>This radiant smile sent chills down Suellen's spine, yet... it also ignited an inexplicable warmth in her heart.</p>
<p>"I can see that you miss this place, miss the days when the Empress displayed her supreme authority."</p>
<p>Step by step, he ascended the stairs, clearing the debris that buried the throne. Standing before it, he brushed the dust from the armrest.</p>
<p>"Or perhaps..."</p>
<p>The young Hydral turned, looking down at the delicate princess like a flower. He patted the armrest of the throne, laughing mockingly:</p>
<p>"Do you wish to sit on this throne, even just once?"</p>
<p>At this moment, Suellen, with her head deeply bowed, finally spoke: "No, I have never thought that, Lord Ansel."</p>
<p>"Is that so?"</p>
<p>"Yes, I believe..."</p>
<p>She raised her head, her pure and innocent face showing genuine admiration and tenderness: "Now, only you are worthy of sitting on this throne."</p>
<p>Ansel looked down at her for an indeterminate amount of time before suddenly letting out a soft chuckle.</p>
<p>This chuckle soon became uncontrollable, turning into a hearty, joyful laugh.</p>
<p>"Suellen... Suellen."</p>
<p>Laughing heartily, Ansel shook his head and tapped the throne with his scepter: "Dear Princess Suellen, I have no interest in it, at all."</p>
<p>"Yes."</p>
<p>Suellen immediately lowered her head again: "For you, there is no need to use such a burdensome thing to demonstrate your authority and glory."</p>
<p>"Your astuteness impresses me." Ansel clapped his hands, "But..."</p>
<p>He stepped down from the throne, approached Suellen, and lifted her chin with his scepter.</p>
<p>The young Hydral's expression was cold and indifferent:</p>
<p>"Your deceit displeases me."</p>
<p>"I did not—"</p>
<p>Suellen began to deny it, but under the gaze of those deep sea-blue eyes, she could not continue.</p>
<p>After a brief silence, she responded softly: "Yes... I deceived you, Lord Ansel. "</p>
<p>"I yearn for that power, I wish to sit on that throne and look down upon all beings."</p>
<p>"Isn't that wonderful?"</p>
<p>Ansel smiled reassuringly: "I like honest people."</p>
<p>"...Is that so?"</p>
<p>Saying this, Suellen suddenly took a step forward.</p>
<p>A smile that was both pure and enchanting slowly appeared on her face.</p>
<p>"Then, may I..."</p>
<p>The petite princess in pure white cautiously moved closer, then... pressed herself against Ansel's chest.</p>
<p>"May I reveal... my desires to you?"</p>
<p>She said this, her eyes shimmering with moist light.</p>
<p>Ansel looked at her with a half-smile: "Go ahead."</p>
<p>"Lord Ansel... you must know better than anyone that the Empire always needs a ruler."</p>
<p>Pressed against Ansel's chest, Suellen spoke softly: "Such a kind and great person like you surely does not wish to see the Empire fall apart, with commoners displaced and war raging everywhere... right?"</p>
<p>"Hmm... perhaps."</p>
<p>"Then, you need someone who can temporarily stabilize the situation."</p>
<p>Suellen's breathing became increasingly rapid. She first lifted her skirt, then... guided Ansel's hand to explore her… inner body.</p>
<p>"Someone... with legitimate authority, who will not cause you to be attacked."</p>
<p>"Someone... ah...ha…"</p>
<p>She seemed to want to meld herself into Ansel's body, writhing and rubbing against him, moaning softly:</p>
<p>"Someone who will always obey you, forever and ever... belong to you."</p>
<p>Ansel lowered his head slightly and whispered in her ear: "Then, where is she?"</p>
<p>Biting her lip, Suellen's eyes were filled with seductive charm. When someone usually so pure and elegant displayed such allure, the impact was doubled.</p>
<p>"She is right... in your—"</p>
<p>Suellen's words were abruptly cut off because Ansel had already seized her throat, lifting her off the ground with an expressionless face.</p>
<p>Suellen's words were abruptly cut off as Ansel seized her throat, lifting her off the ground with an expressionless face.</p>
<p>"Princess Suellen, your speech was well-prepared, but you need to understand a few things."</p>
<p>"First,"</p>
<p>Ansel smiled at the terrified Suellen: "I am not kind."</p>
<p>"Second,"</p>
<p>He tossed Suellen to the ground as if discarding trash: "The Empire's division and chaos are inevitable, and I cannot stop it, let alone someone as worthless as you."</p>
<p>"And... third,"</p>
<p>Ansel suddenly flashed a bright smile at the trembling Suellen:</p>
<p>"I do indeed need you to absolutely, absolutely obey me."</p>
<p>"Come."</p>
<p>He tapped the brick in front of him with his scepter: "Lift your skirt like you did before."</p>
<p>"..."</p>
<p>Biting her lip, Suellen showed no sign of distress. Instead, she quickly recovered from her earlier fear, crawling to the spot Ansel indicated like a dog. She sat down in a W position, shyly lifting her skirt again, revealing her pale, naked flowers.</p>
<p>Ansel, however, did not look at her face but gently pressed the end of his scepter against her lower abdomen.</p>
<p>"Ah!"</p>
<p>A miserable scream immediately echoed. The delicate girl fell to the ground, clutching her abdomen in pain, yet she dared not say anything, even forcibly suppressing her cries.</p>
<p>When the pain gradually subsided, she looked down at her abdomen and found... a vivid, jet-black hydra tattooed there!</p>
<p>"From now on,"</p>
<p>Ansel looked down at Suellen, slightly raising his chin: "You are mine. Always remember your place."</p>
<p>Lowering her head, silently pulling down her skirt, Suellen crawled to Ansel's feet.</p>
<p>"I understand, mas..."</p>
<p>Her voice faltered, but she quickly made a decision.</p>
<p>"I understand, Master."</p>
<p>Suellen of the Flamefeast, branded with the Hydra's mark, perhaps the most pitiful, disgraceful, and contemptible Flamefeast in a thousand years, called Ansel her master with such flattery.</p>
<p>"Rest assured." Ansel patted her head, "I don't care about that throne, nor do I need it, but that doesn't mean you have no chance to sit on it."</p>
<p>His lips curled into a slight smile as he saw Suellen's eyes brighten momentarily: "However..."</p>
<p>Gleipnir transformed into a hand cannon with a mechanical twist, and without looking back, the young Hydral fired a shot that shattered the entire throne into pieces.</p>
<p>Under Suellen's stunned gaze, its fragments mixed with the ruins, indistinguishable from the scattered bricks... perhaps just more expensive.</p>
<p>"It's just a seat, after all."</p>
<p>Ansel pinched Suellen's chin, his voice gentle:</p>
<p>"If I want you to sit, you will sit."</p>
