<p>"Should I also consider the hunting essence, which fits with the beast essence, the difficulty is not too complex, and also —"</p>

<p>"Hunting essence? What, have you recently had time to go hunting?"</p>

<p>"...When I'm alone with you, you don't have to use this name."</p>
<p>Ravenna glanced at Ansel, who seemed to be on vacation in the alchemy workshop: "I'm more used to you calling me by my name, Ansel."</p>

<p>"..."</p>

<p>"So... you still have expectations of me? You seem somewhat inconsistent, Venna, declaring that you've seen through my true nature while still entertaining these unrealistic notions about me... Hasn't this happened numerous times already?"</p>
<p>Those hands gradually moved downward, and Ravenna's breathing subtly changed — her flesh could not regulate sensation and was far more sensitive than any puppet's.</p>

<p>"What, the clever Venna can't think of the answer?"</p>
<p>"Of course."</p>
<p>Ravenna slightly lowered her head, her voice a touch hoarse: "Just as I can't fathom, among the words you've spoken to me, how many were lies."</p>
<p>"Hmm?" Ansel's brows lifted slightly, "As I recall, what I told you was... everything."</p>
<p>"Yes, everything." Ravenna, wanting to not press so closely against Ansel's chest but held firmly in place, continued under the somewhat scorching heat, "You told me that everything you said to me was a lie, but I... don't believe it."</p>
<p>Marlina's words had ultimately caused her to waver.</p>
<p>"Ansel, I don't believe you've never told me a single truth."</p>
