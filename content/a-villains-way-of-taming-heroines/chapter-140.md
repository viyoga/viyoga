<p>"Fate."</p>
<p>Ansel opened his eyes.</p>
<p>He detached the tumultuous emotions surging in his heart with indifference, uttering the word he despised the most in a low voice.</p>
<p>Fate, up until now, must have been observing everything.</p>
<p>What he could confirm now was that his feelings for Seraphina were abnormal, surely a delusion fostered by the push and pull of fate.</p>
<p>She was just a girl, a foolish, arrogant, prideful girl who always caused trouble.</p>
<p>Ansel had met others who were gentler than her, more beautiful than her, and equally, if not more, infatuated with him.</p>
<p>He knew countless girls like that, so why should he be attracted to Seraphina, who was inferior to them in every aspect? Why had his feelings for her escalated to this point?</p>
<p>Without a doubt, it was fate playing tricks, Ansel told himself.</p>
<p>Why would it do this? Simply to drive him under the impulse of these emotions, to let go of his fear of the Beast King, to believe in Seraphina's future with a baseless, illusory "love", believing she would not become the self-centered, mad beast.</p>
<p>His feelings for Seraphina were merely a product of fate, but Seraphina's nature was an established fact.</p>
<p>She was a beast yearning for evolution and transcendence, absolutely not allowing any existence to dominate her, a humanoid monster that even surpassed Hydral in the essence of the beast.</p>
<p>One, two, three years... Seraphina's loyalty might last ten years or even longer.</p>
<p>But ten years later, Ansel might be a different person from now, and she, one day, would become the future Sky Wolf Emperor.</p>
<p>At that moment, he would become Seraphina's nourishment.</p>
<p>"So, I won this round."</p>
<p>Ansel murmured so, his figure disappearing from the spot.</p>
<p>He appeared not far from where the Blazing Ice Viper was active, watching the tiny figure expressionlessly.</p>
<p>"Even if you failed to push me impulsively make a pact with Seraphina, your plan wouldn't fail. You always have more preparations, more choices."</p>
<p>The current Seraphina was in a state where she could advance to the third stage at any time. Undoubtedly, fate would not let her die here, which means... Seraphina would gain some insight or opportunity in this battle, just as she said.</p>
<p>"I have to let Seraphina understand this, only then will she give up that naive idea."</p>
<p>Ansel understood Seraphina, for her now, the most unacceptable thing was to step into the control of fate.</p>
<p>She said those words more to move herself, and if the "gift" of fate really came, Seraphina would definitely not accept it, she would realize that her thoughts and ideas were still within the guidance of fate.</p>
<p>In this case, under the disgust of fate, in order to get Ansel's help, she would eventually choose to give up her delusions.</p>
<p>Although after this... the relationship between the two, most likely cannot return to the past, but Ansel did not care.</p>
<p>He watched the girl charge against the rolling cold current, her hair and cloak covered with red frost, her expression unwavering.</p>
<p>Each level of the extraordinary beings represents a leap in change, and the higher the level, the more exaggerated this transformation.</p>
<p>The transformation from the third stage to the fourth can be likened to the difference between a rifle and a top-tier heavy tank.</p>
<p>Without exaggeration, as a peak fourth-stage Blazing Ice Viper, it could freeze Seraphina to death on the spot with just a breath of cold air, and if it were a normal second-stage, he or she would die before even getting to the Blazing Ice Viper.</p>
<p>In fact, Seraphina already felt like she was about to die.</p>
<p>The bone-chilling cold was even more terrifying than the great cold wave, perhaps because other elements were added to the cold current continuously emitted by the Blazing Ice Viper, making the cold piercing and making Seraphina's limbs increasingly stiff.</p>
<p>"This thing... even if I become a third stage, I might not be able to kill it…"</p>
<p>The girl gritted her teeth and muttered, "Ansel, he really knows how to find work for me, but... hehe..."</p>
<p>The lips of the wolf, which had already turned slightly purple, laughed without any resentment or fear in her heart:</p>
<p>"This also shows how much that guy, who never says what he thinks, believes in me!"</p>
<p>Step by step, her original sprint turned into a jog, from a jog to a trek. When she came to this huge creature that was fifty meters long, she could hardly stand, let alone throw a punch.</p>
<p>"...this is, really."</p>
<p>Seraphina, whose face was already covered with frost, murmured in a trembling voice, "What I said just now, it really sounds like a joke, I was too arrogant."</p>
<p>She took a deep breath, clenched her fists, tried to make her cold and stiff muscles swell, let the blood warm up and continue to flow in her body, transmitting power.</p>
<p>"But at least... I can't just stand here —"</p>
<p>"Die without throwing a punch!"</p>
<p>Bang!</p>
<p>The red ice crystals that almost covered Seraphina's body exploded instantly, the veins on the girl's neck and forehead bulged, and her small fist even cracked the skin and burst the blood vessels in this moment. The flying blood instantly turned into ice crystals, and Seraphina's fist hit the crystalline body of the Blazing Ice Viper among the flying ice crystals.</p>
<p>Then, in the crisp sound of bone breaking, nothing happened except for Seraphina's entire twisted arm.</p>
<p>Oh, one more thing happened.</p>
<p>The slowly moving Blazing Ice Viper seemed to feel the scratch on its shell, it slowly turned its huge head, and its cold gaze rested on the incredibly, incredibly tiny ant beside it.</p>
<p>It just glanced at Seraphina and then turned its head back.</p>
<p>The monster was unwilling to waste its ether on such an ant, it didn't even bother to breathe.</p>
<p>So, the mountain-like body casually rolled towards Seraphina.</p>
<p>A few seconds later, a deeper blood color appeared on the vast snowfield covered with ice crystals.</p>
<p>Seraphina collapsed to the ground, looking up at the sky, her pale face and purple lips had no blood color at all.</p>
<p>"It's so cold... there's some good in it."</p>
<p>She trembled slightly and lowered her head, pulling at the corner of her mouth, "I can't... feel any pain."</p>
<p>In this cold, even if Seraphina sensed the impending death crisis in advance, it was a bit too late.</p>
<p>The girl's waist and abdomen below... had been casually crushed into a pool of flesh and bone by the huge monster.</p>
