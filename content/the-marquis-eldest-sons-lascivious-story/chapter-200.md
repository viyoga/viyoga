<p>Vol. 2 Chapter 200</p>
<p>The Blue Land, The Heaven’s Cloud City</p>
<p>Translated by Raizu</p>
<p>Author’s Note: This time, the story will be presented from the perspective of the Adellahan family, one of the five great Noble families of the Kingdom of Levios.</p>
<p>Until a few years ago, there was a school for children of Nobles in the Royal Capital Levios.</p>
<p>The school was called Blue Land Institute, because it consisted of two buildings, the Blue tower, and Land tower.</p>
<p>Blue Land.</p>
<p>The pure earth stretches out to the horizon, illuminated by the cloudless blue sky.</p>
<p>This is one of the slogans raised by the Levios family, and it simply expresses the ideal land that all Nobles should aim for.</p>
<p>The imaginary land dreamed of by the First King Levios could be said to be a symbolic motif of the Noble era.</p>
<p>Blue Land Institute is a system that allows anyone who was born into a Noble family in the Kingdom to study abroad.</p>
<p>Originally, Wilk Quordenze was supposed to attend the Blue Land Institute, spending a lifetime of frustrating masturbation while interacting with beautiful Princesses from all over the world that he couldn’t get his hands on.</p>
<p>But, the emergence of demon beast Yersinia changed all that.</p>
<p>The Levios family’s status as a Noble was greatly lowered as they were unable to keep the monsters in their own territory and spread monster disasters not only to the Kingdom but also to the empire.</p>
<p>The Nobles of the Kingdom then chose to hold tight to their children who survived Yersinia, never to send them to the Royal Capital anymore. Originally, only Nobles who like to stay withdrawn in their territory would make such an excuse not to let their children out.</p>
<p>And thus, Blue Land Institute, after several decades of history, was closed.</p>
<p>However, even if the school itself is no longer operational, the building itself is still open. Many people are still coming in and out, although not as much as in its heyday.</p>
<p>There were quite a few alumni who missed the days spent at this academy and would visit the building when they visited the Royal Capital, and the Blue Land Institute, which could accommodate many Nobles, was also used as a social gathering place in the Royal Capital.</p>
<p>On this day as well, several carriages belonging to Nobles were parked in the parking lot.</p>
<p>Blue Land Institute has annexed buildings that were built by the Levios family members as a hobby. It was simply called the library due to its purpose.</p>
<p>Since it was meant for students of Blue Land Institute, it was not widely used by the general Noble populace, and therefore barely had any parking space for carriage.</p>
<p>On this day, an unfamiliar carriage was parked in the library’s parking lot. A small flag was hoisted on the carriage.</p>
<p>On the small flag was the crest of horses facing each other with a double edged sword standing in between. The carriage belonged to Marquis Adellahan, one of the five Great Nobles of Levios Kingdom.</p>
<p>Inside the library, an old woman, a young man and a woman, as well as three shadows were in the room that had been prepared as a reception room. Of course, each of them has guards and civilians, but no one is aware of their existence.</p>
<p>After a brief greeting to the old woman who will be the host of the tea party, the young man and woman quietly took their seats.</p>
<p>The old woman who was also the director of this library used her fingertips to signal the attendants to prepare the tea, and a wrinkled smile appeared on her face as if to announce the start of the tea party.</p>
<p>[It’s been a while, Novalf-dono.]</p>
<p>Although they had already greeted each other, the old woman dared to say those words.</p>
<p>The only people who knew each other at this tea party were the old woman and this Novalf Adellahan.</p>
<p>[Is it the first time in four years? I am very happy to meet you again in this building.]</p>
<p>[It’s good to see that Teacher is doing well too.]</p>
<p>The Blue Land Institute was run by Levios family, not the Kingdom itself.</p>
<p>That’s why the teachers consisted of retired knights and officers who served the Levios family, selected for their wealth of knowledge, wisdom, and practical experience. However, important positions such as the Director were held by the founder.</p>
<p>The old woman is the granddaughter of the Levios’ First King, and a Highlord of the Levios family.</p>
<p>At Blue Land Institute, she concurrently served as the Vice President and Head of the Library. The old woman glanced at the person sitting next to her former student for a moment.</p>
<p>[I read it in the letter the other day, but you really brought your betrothed here today…]</p>
<p>The reason Novalf came to the library that day was to introduce his fiancée to his former teacher.</p>
<p>He forced the scheduling to visit in the morning after he entered the capital because he really wanted to do this before it became too hectic when the social gathering finally began.</p>
<p>The old woman respectfully bowed to Novalf’s fiancée introduction. Despite being a place where former teachers and students meet face to face, the person who ranks highest here is the next Head of the Adellahan family, Calmia Adellahan, who she has never met before.</p>
<p>In order of strata, Calmia, the next Head of the Noble family, is number one, her husband, Novalf, is number two, and the old woman, who is just a Highlord of the collateral line, is number three.</p>
<p>Calmia was conscious of her own position and spoke with a stronger tone than usual.</p>
<p>[I have been looking forward to meeting you. I always wondered what life was like while Novalf was staying in the Royal Capital.]</p>
<p>She puts on a necessary charade to give off the impression that the next head of Adellahan is perfect. But Calmia’s true feelings were different.</p>
<p>She simply wanted to know what kind of days her beloved fiancée spent in the Royal Capital from a perspective other than the report she received.</p>
<p>I want to know the side of the man I like that I don’t know. It was the honest feeling of a 16-year-old girl.</p>
<p>[As a Nobleman from Adellahan, he acted very splendidly. He worried a lot, learned a lot, and then thought about it again…that could be said to be the way a Noble should be. Doing everything in their power to make things possible.]</p>
<p>Perhaps because Calmia was too good at hiding her emotions, the old woman frankly gave her evaluation of Novalf’s attitude accordingly, befitting a report to the next Head of the Adellahan family.</p>
<p>The Levios family hopes to reopen the Blue Land Institute when things have cooled down.</p>
<p>For that reason, whenever the opportunity arose, the old woman would appeal to the Royal Capital for its excellent educational environment.</p>
<p>For Nobles, child education is extremely important.</p>
<p>A Highlord is physically and magically powerful, but the future of territory still depends greatly on whether the family head is a competent Ruler or not.</p>
<p>In order for Nobles to continue to exist as Noble families, it is essential to educate them to raise good heirs, and it is no exaggeration to say that if they raise incompetent heirs, the family line might end.</p>
<p>Combined with these circumstances, the Blue Land Institute system was not very welcomed by the Royal Nobles from the beginning.</p>
<p>It was an unthinkable idea to entrust the education of an important heir to another family, even if only partly. Even among the Noble households under the Levios family, there weren’t many examples of sending children who would become the next Head of the family to Blue Land Institute.</p>
<p>Most sent children from irrelevant collateral families as a form of act of loyalty to the Levy family. Novalf was the perfect example of that.</p>
<p>Even so, the Levios family continued to operate the Blue Land Institute.</p>
<p>This was because while the theme was to deepen exchanges between the Nobles of the Kingdom, there was another purpose behind it.</p>
<p>The Levios family had a bitter past that caused a civil war with the Milendorva family because they once had a stupid child as the Head of the family.</p>
<p>The Knights of Levios, who were in charge of education, deeply regretted this and proposed the establishment of Blue Land Institute in order to improve their educational skills and know-how.</p>
<p>To put it in a better way, it’s a place where you can receive the cutting-edge education that the Levios family has.</p>
<p>To put it in a bad way, it is a testing ground for the advanced curriculum devised by the Levios family.</p>
<p>This is what the Blue Land Institute was all about.</p>
<p>However, it’s not like only the Levios family who would receive the benefits.</p>
<p>The Royal Nobles made good use of it, such as taking the educational content back to their own territory and applying it to the children who would become the next family Head, taking only parts that are applicable.</p>
<p>At the same time, if the family line of the Royal Nobles’ families stabilized, it would lead to the interests of the Levios Kingdom as a whole.</p>
<p>[Hearing you being chosen as the betrothed of the next family Head, Princess Calmia, and to be recognized and adopted by Duke Crowne, it makes me happy as if you are my own. Knowing my former student reached that height, there is no greater honor as a teacher.]</p>
<p>“I’m glad,” said the smiling old woman, which came from her heart.</p>
<p>Speaking from the Levios family’s point of view, the fact that the spouse of the next head of the Adellahan family is a Blue Land Institute alumni is a very satisfying story. In addition to the achievements of the academy, it is convenient in many ways for a Noble who is familiar with the Royal Capital to appear on the front stage of diplomacy.</p>
<p>However, even without those things, the old woman was happy that Novalf was well received by the Adellahan family.</p>
<p>There is no lie in what the old woman just said. While studying abroad, the teachers responded to Novalf’s greedy desire to learn things head-on.</p>
<p>Originally, she thought that all that would be a waste when thinking about what he would do after he graduated.</p>
<p>He is from a collateral family, and if you think about it normally, there won’t be many opportunities for him to play an active role in the future. It’s human nature to want to prepare a place where you can play an active role that matches your abilities.</p>
<p>For that reason, the old woman thought about bringing Novalf into the Levios family, and even chose a suitable Princess to welcome him as a son-in-law.</p>
<p>But thanks to demonic beast Yersinia, all those plans were for naught.</p>
<p>[…..When he first came to the academy, Novalf-dono seemed to have a lot on his shoulders…]</p>
<p>While talking, the old woman, sensing that Calmia was more interested in Novalf’s private life than her evaluation, gradually shifted the topic towards her former student’s private life.</p>
<p>While the old woman is a teacher, she is also a Noble linked to the Levios family. In order to make this tea party meaningful for the Levios family, she was given several tasks in advance.</p>
<p>One of the tasks was to investigate the person named Calmia Adellahan.</p>
<p>Due to the disaster caused by the magic beast Yersinia, the number of Highlords that make up each Noble family has decreased significantly.</p>
<p>In other words, the order of succession to the family estate has changed greatly as well.</p>
<p>Before and after the Yersinia Calamity, the Quordenze family was the only one that did not see any change to their succession.</p>
<p>In diplomacy between Nobles, the personality of an individual is a bigger factor than you can imagine.</p>
<p>For the Levios family, who were the ones who held the Kingdom together, it was necessary to know who would lead the next generation as well as the current one.</p>
<p>Originally, Calmia was not in the position to inherit the family headship of Adellahan family and had never appeared on the stage until now. Therefore, Levios family marked her as the person with the highest priority to investigate.</p>
<p>[…On days off, he would come to this mansion. Staring at the bookshelves with great interest, wondering if there were any good books. Fufu, the books here are meant to be read on the spot, but his enthusiasm pushed me to lend some books to him several times.]</p>
<p>[Ohh. Really?]</p>
<p>Calmia’s voice softened a little.</p>
<p>The old woman knew from Novalf himself that the books and manuscripts obtained in the Royal Capital were being sent to Calmia.</p>
<p>The reason why the old woman said this was because she wanted to see Calmia’s reaction.</p>
<p>It makes you happy that someone is willing to do something for you. Even more so if it’s someone you like.</p>
<p>Calmia narrowed her eyes and smiled quietly. The old woman decided that it was a movement to hide the change in her expression. If you let your guard down, your face will grin, so you’re overwriting it with a different smile.</p>
<p>The naive reaction of a Princess who had just learned to fall in love made the old woman feel itchy.</p>
<p>Perhaps because his tribute has become a hot topic, Novalf shows a slightly embarrassed gesture. Then, as if teasing his movements, Calmia gently placed her hand on him.</p>
<p>The old woman immediately understood that these innocent fiancés are on very good terms.</p>
<p>[It’s good to know that you two seem to get along very well.]</p>
<p>[When Teacher says that, it makes me feel a little embarrassed.]</p>
<p>Even after marriage, there will be no problems at night. After that, even if this Princess’s womb is infertile…barring that factor, there would be no worries about the heir… The checklist in the old woman’s head, the most important item, is marked with a check mark.</p>
<p>If Novalf, who is strongly influenced by the thoughts of the First King Levios, acts as the de facto Head of the family, it would be desirable for the Levios family. The old woman thought that she could go a little adventurous here.</p>
<p>[Princess Calmia seems to like reading. This is also my hobby, but there are a lot of books stored here. Why don’t you choose the one that suits your taste here? You can borrow it anytime. I will make them available for loan at any time.]</p>
<p>That sounded like a genuine offer. However, Calmia felt a kind of test in it, a speculation to measure her aptitude.</p>
<p>The Head of Noble families, especially those with a strong sense of self-reliance, such as the Adellahan family, do not usually come to the Royal Capital for casual intention.</p>
<p>Understanding that and asking you to come to the library at any time can be interpreted as [Novalf will act as the de-facto Head of the family, right? You are the nominal Head of the family, in other words, just a decoration, so why don’t you stay in the Royal Capital?]. It’s a pretty demeaning way of looking at it.</p>
<p>On the contrary, it can also be interpreted as [I’ll lend it to you via the Adellahan civil officer who is staying in the Royal Capital. Even after you leave, you’ll still have a representative stationed in the Royal Capital, right? Let’s continue to get along well].</p>
<p>The old woman had no intention of setting things up with the next Head of the Adellahan family.</p>
<p>Therefore, her tone sounded very calm and natural. There was almost no way to tell whether it’s sarcastic, friendly, or it’s a remark that doesn’t have any meaning.</p>
<p>[Thank you very much for your kind offer. I have always been instructed to read many good books from the generation before the one before. If you don’t mind, I would appreciate it if you could suggest something to me.]</p>
<p>Calmia’s tone did not fluctuate at all. The old woman judged that her displeased look was just an act of warning.</p>
<p>[Is that so? Then, for reference, would it be possible for you to tell me what kind of books Princess Calmia has read so far? I would appreciate it if you could also tell me what kind of book you are interested in.]</p>
<p>If they have the chance, they will definitely try to read my reading history and impressions… In other words, read my thoughts. That was the answer Calmia expected.</p>
<p>She calmly describes the title of the book she had prepared in her head and what part of it interests her.</p>
<p>Each time she raises the question one by one, the old woman understands what Calmia wants to say.</p>
<p>Because it was a list of books that the Adellahan family’s second generation Head liked.</p>
<p>The Levios family has a great deal of information about the second generation Head of the Adellahan family.</p>
<p>The second Head was sent as hostage to Levios territory when she was young, and received most of her education from the Levios family.</p>
<p>The Levios family, who had their sights set on future diplomacy, kept meticulous records of the personalities and abilities of the second generation Head, the type of men she liked, and even the books that influenced her.</p>
<p>Knowing that, Calmia dared to show it. It’s just that she can see their thoughts.</p>
<p>The old woman’s palms were sweating for the first time since coming here after receiving that petty counterattack.</p>
<p>It seems that the spy’s report that she was a treasured child of the second generation Head was correct. The old woman thought that the reason why Calmia attached the excellent collateral line, Novalf-dono, was to make him the de-facto Head of the family… She started wondering if it was her fault for making such a hasty conclusion.</p>
<p>……Even so, she’s a pretty calm Princess for her age. The old woman could feel some similarities to the Adellahan’s second generation somewhere.</p>
<p>The old woman let out a small sigh on purpose, as if giving the sign of the end of her investigation.</p>
<p>[I see. I feel like I understand a little about Princess Calmia’s preferences. As soon as I’ve finished selecting books, I’ll hand them over to Adellahan’s civil officers.]</p>
<p>[Yes, I’m looking forward to it.]</p>
<p>It is highly likely that the next head of the Adellahan family will be Calmia Adellahan both in name and reality. The spy’s report is probably correct. Continuing the investigation… The old woman conveyed her conclusions to the Levios civil officer with a predetermined sign, and then returned to a harmless chat.</p>

<p>[…Pardon for the sudden intrusion. Would it be alright if one more visitor joins us? I believe that Novalf-dono is acquainted with this guest. In any case, Dunkel wishes to join us by any means…]</p>

<p>The Royal castle and the library are quite far apart, so at least it’s not a place you can casually visit.</p>
<p>It’s possible to decline since it’s actually rude to barge in like that, but there’s too much disadvantage in doing so. It’s not good for Adellahan and Levios, even if the newly arriving person is an unwanted guest.</p>
<p>This place is within the Royal Capital. The advantage is on the Levios side.</p>

<p>[This will be our first meeting, Princess Calmia. Since we’re currently in a private place, let’s save the formal greeting for a later date. Just take it easy here…]</p>

<p>[I’m Dunkel Levios… I fear, I’m the one who somehow ended up inheriting the Levios estate.]</p>
<p>[Young Master!]</p>

<p>But to Novalf, the words were a little sympathetic.</p>
<p>The Levios estate is awe-inspiring――</p>
