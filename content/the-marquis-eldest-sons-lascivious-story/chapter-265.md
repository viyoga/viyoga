<p>Chapter 265: Old Knights</p>
<p>The Marquis’ Eldest Son’s Lascivious Story</p>
<p>It was late afternoon when a visitor arrived at Nex Canfore’s mansion.</p>
<p>Two people, an old man and a young man, got out of the carriage as if they didn’t want to be seen. After confirming this with the servant, Nex moved to the mansion’s entrance.</p>
<p>[Oh, if it isn’t Nex-dono! I’m really happy to have met you!]</p>
<p>It seemed that the guests had already entered the room, guided by the servants, and as soon as the old man saw Nex, he approached with a smile on his face.</p>
<p>The name of that cheerful old man is Seyman Garletta. He was a member of the knight family who served the Count Nongite family, one of the noble families of the Zinkaen Empire.</p>
<p>Seyman’s smooth bald head shows no trace of his younger days. However, Nex felt nostalgic in his exaggerated gestures and clear, high-pitched voice.</p>
<p>[Seyman-dono seems to be doing well, too. We’re both retired, so I never thought we’d meet in the Imperial Capital like this.]</p>
<p>Nex and Seyman are old friends. When they first met, they were both young Lord-class and originally enemies. After that, after many twists and turns, there was a time when they faced each other as knights in the Imperial Capital. There was a relationship there that could not be summed up in the simple terms of enemy and ally.</p>
<p>[No, no, I’m already old! Lately, the interval between magic malfunctions has become narrower and narrower, and if things continue like this, I’ll be reduced to ashes by tomorrow, so I can no longer beat you! Wahahahaha!]</p>
<p>Seyman laughed playfully. There are many people who become weak and withdrawn when they develop a magic power malfunction, but he still seemed to be active and well for an oldman.</p>
<p>Nex turned his attention to the young man standing next to Seyman. He looked to be in his early twenties, neatly dressed, and has a gentle demeanor.</p>
<p>The young man noticed Nex’s gaze and tried to say his greeting, but Seyman spoke first before he could.</p>
<p>[I was in trouble the other day. I was trying my best to give the child a little practice, but as soon as I went out into the garden, my magic ran out. If that happens, I’m just an old man. I wonder when it’ll return? It’s already been half a week …Hey, look! Crane! What are you doing! You shoulda say your greetings early! If you mess around too much, you’ll be exhausted in no time! Also, the way you wrote yesterday’s letter…]</p>
<p>Seyman started nagging the young man while patting him on the back.</p>
<p>The young man missed the opportunity to say his greeting because of Seyman’s constant small talk, but that doesn’t seem to be worth considering. One sermon led to another, which led to a chat. The young man seemed to be wondering how he could stop the barrage and actually say his greeting.</p>
<p>As if noticing his plight, Nex sent him a lifeboat at the right time.</p>
<p>[Knight Garetta, it’s been a while.]</p>
<p>Then, the young man, Crane Garletta, the current head of the Garletta family, softened his expression as if relieved.</p>
<p>[Nex-dono, it’s been a while. Thank you for accepting this sudden visit.]</p>
<p>Seyman was the only one with a dissatisfied look on his face.</p>
<p>[Listen, Crane. Don’t you have enough enthusiasm to overcome the noisy old man in your family? I thought you’d forget your position as a guest and just jump at the favor…no, I don’t want you to behave in such an unsightly manner. It would be really unwise to show them to others. The children nowadays are truly unreliable. This is what someone who has long since retired says, but don’t you think so too?]</p>
<p>[How troublesome, how troublesome], muttered Seyman as he patted his bald head. The surrounding servants and maids relaxed their guard due to the somewhat whimsical atmosphere.</p>
<p>[Unfortunately, there aren’t many young people around here…]</p>
<p>Nex ordered a servant to lead the two to the parlor while trying to make a fuss about the fact that he was stepping back from the front lines of politics.</p>
<p>Not changed at all. That was Nex’s honest impression after meeting Seyman.</p>
<p>Seyman was good at catching people off guard with his charming gestures and speaking skills. Nex had seen his strength several times in the past.</p>
<p>It would be reassuring if he became just a senile old man, but that is an overly optimistic idea. Nex warns himself not to let his guard down.</p>
<p>To begin with, the people scheduled to visit today were “Knight Garetta and one of his aides”. Since that aide was Seyman, he could only assume that they’re plotting something.</p>
<p>When they entered the guest room, Seyman was the first to start making small talk.</p>
<p>If it were a public event, it would normally start with a conversation between Nex and Crane, an active Knight. First of all, today’s Seyman was a free bystander whose name was not on the list of participants, and he should have been sitting quietly. However, this meeting is strictly private.</p>
<p>[I’m really sorry for making this sudden request. I’m old too, and when I found out that you were in the Imperial Capital, I just went ahead with it. No, I’m sorry. There will always be a formal bow…]</p>
<p>As Seyman said, it was indeed a sudden call. As it turned out, it was only yesterday that a messenger from the knight Garletta came to the mansion to convey their request for a meeting.</p>
<p>Nex didn’t want to do anything noticeable right now. To be honest, he would have absolutely refused to welcome a knight from another family to his mansion in the Imperial Capital.</p>
<p>[No, the Count Nongite family had a lot of trouble last year. Compared to this, it’s not a big deal.]</p>
<p>However, there were circumstances that made it difficult for Nex to refuse.</p>
<p>Last year, he visited Galfis Quordenze. The main purpose of that was to get a consensus on the future policy against Spierzeig, and the person he relied on at that time was the Nongite family.</p>
<p>As expected, it is impossible to enter Quordenze territory via Spierzeig territory. Nongite territory is adjacent to the westernmost outer part of Quordenze territory, so it was convenient in many ways.</p>
<p>As for Nex, although he had paid appropriate homage to the Nongite family as he passed by on their territory, it is true that he’s in debt to them. For him, there remains only a small amount of psychological indebtedness. Therefore, he decided to settle the matter by agreeing to this meeting. Considering he’ll be going to Quordenze again someday, he’d rather relent this time.</p>
<p>Nex thought that it was because Seyman understood this that he forced the sudden request for a meeting.</p>
<p>However, Seyman seemed to be aware that he has made an unreasonable request, and has been following up on this diligently. The fact that Seyman came forward and spoke as if pushing Knight Garretta away is probably a form of performance and consideration to emphasize that this is a secret meeting. In this way, the contents of the meeting that disrespected the active Knight will no longer be made public. This is an appeal that he will never tell anyone else.</p>
<p>[Is the Count well?]</p>
<p>Count Nongite must be aware of this matter, Nex asked, implicitly claiming that.</p>
<p>[The old prince is doing well. He is currently in Nongite, but he is always keeping an eye on the Imperial Capital.]</p>
<p>Of course, he replied, and Seyman continued speaking.</p>
<p>[It seems that the old prince is acutely aware of the lack of ability among young people, and lately he has been busy educating them. That’s why I was approached, too, to discipline the children of the family.]</p>
<p>[I see. Did something similar happen to you?]</p>

Nex had overheard several times that due to the change in generations, the Nongite family has become noticeably younger overall. <p>Considering the current state of the Empire’s stagnation, Nex thinks it’s not a bad idea to allow for some mistakes and let the younger players gain experience. <p>[I’m just a dying old man whose only purpose in life is to self-deprecate over the frequency of my magic power malfunction.Even if I show it publicly, I’m sure it’ll just be a nuisance, and the children won’t like it either. It’s making me nervous.]</p>

<p>Although he meant what he said, it was something that Nex did not want to welcome.</p>
<p>There are probably other old people like Seyman who have great abilities but have retired due to magic power malfunctions. If he tries to take advantage of the youths that he met last year, he might just end up being led into a trap.</p>

<p>[However, right now I would rather think about how my children should behave than about myself.]</p>
<p>It seems that the conversation up until now was just a prelude to get to the main topic. It was a conversation that was almost a check to show that his ability was still strong.</p>
<p>[What do you mean by children?]</p>
<p>[As you can see, Crane has inherited the name of Knight Garletta, but from my perspective, he still has a long way to go. I don’t think he has enough experience yet.]</p>
<p>Nex couldn’t exactly agree with that. Even if it’s in a private setting, making a statement that directly criticizes an active Knight from another family will cause unnecessary uproar.</p>
<p>[Is that so? Even if that’s the case, I think it’s a common story among knights these days.]</p>
<p>It’s not that much of a concern, he avoided making a clear statement with vague answers that can be interpreted in any way.</p>
<p>However, Nex understood exactly what Seyman wanted to say. For those who survived the era of turmoil, it often feels like there is a laxity within the current Empire. The only people who are nervous are the people involved with Spierzeig.</p>
<p>He doesn’t think it’s a good idea to have a life where you’re constantly forced to decide whether to live or die, but whether or not you’ve had that experience will have a big impact on how you’d behave.</p>
<p>[Yes, yes, I think I understand that. I have never once felt nostalgic for those rough days when I was faced with the rise and fall of my Master’s family at every opportunity. We are deeply grateful to Emperor Zinkaen.]</p>
<p>Nex felt that there was a hint of relief in his voice as he spoke with great emotion.</p>
<p>[However, I don’t think it’s alright to remain ignorant. I often think that children today misunderstand that bloody feat as just a fun fairy tale…]</p>
<p>Nex couldn’t help but nod at those words.</p>
<p>The unification of the western continent by Emperor Zinkaen’s faction is now treated like a heroic tale. Because Nex was an early member of the group, he was sometimes looked at with a strange twinkle in the eyes by the young officials within the Empire. Since the Yersinia disaster has reduced the number of people who know about the past, it has become even more prominent. This was also one of the reasons why Nex usually distanced himself from the Imperial Capital.</p>
<p>[In that case, it would be better if Seyman-dono himself spoke. The words of an experienced veteran who has endured many hardships as a knight of Nongite should resonate more than anything else.]</p>
<p>That is not rejection, but Nex’s true feelings.</p>
<p>[I see, that’s true too. Thank you for your valuable advice.]</p>
<p>While saying that, Seyman looked at Crane sitting next to him. There was an easy-to-understand fake smile on his face.</p>
<p>[It’s funny when you think about it that I was able to ask for advice like this now. Nex-dono and I were enemies at first. Whether you’re an enemy or an ally, it’s important to know who the person is. This is important. If you can see who you are confronting, you can find a compromise and cooperate with each other. If you are unnecessarily afraid of the meeting, it will only lead to unnecessary conflict, so you have to judge the opponent appropriately… Do you understand, Crane? The problem that you are currently having may be the same.]</p>
<p>Nex could see now. This is where the main point comes from. From the flow of the conversation, he felt that the real reason why he had asked for a meeting was finally going to be revealed, and he mentally braced himself.</p>
<p>[Nex-dono, would you please listen as well? It seems like Crane is concerned about the Quordenze family.]</p>

<p>Perhaps the purpose of the visit was to investigate the internal affairs of the Quordenze family, and Nex returned the question, being careful not to give out any unnecessary information.</p>
<p>[That’s…]</p>
<p>From there, Seyman continued to talk about Crane’s concerns about Quordenze.</p>
<p>However, there is not much content in those words. He was just babbling about innocuous matters, and Nex had no idea what he wanted to ask. There were endless expressions that he didn’t know how to interpret, and words that could be interpreted in any way he wanted.</p>
<p>[…Hmm, Knight Garletta seems to be having a lot of trouble as well.]</p>
<p>Since he did not know what was being asked, Nex could not provide a significant response.</p>
<p>[For Crane, who is still young, the name of the Great Noble Quordenze is too big. You must be well aware of its weight.]</p>
<p>As Seyman said, Nex is quite well-connected to the Quordenze family.</p>
<p>It seems that he was born in a weak noble territory in the western part of the continent. The reason it seems so is because Nex himself has no memory of it. <p>Seyman and the Nongito family are concerned about Wilk Quordenze, the next Head of the Quordenze family. He didn’t know why they suddenly started paying attention to this boy, but he guessed that was the purpose of the meeting.</p>

<p>The battle between Quordenze and Spierzeig broke out. This was an unexpected battle that was not part of the future plans that he had heard from Galfis.</p>

<p>Seyman must have come here to find out whether the battle had been planned, or whether Nex was working behind the scenes in the Imperial Capital in preparation for the move. And Wilk Quordenze is probably very involved in this battle.</p>
