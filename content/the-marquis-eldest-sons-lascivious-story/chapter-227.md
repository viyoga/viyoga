<p>Chapter 227</p>
<p>The Marquis’ Eldest Son’s Lascivious Story</p>
<p>Novalf received the sword from one of the escorts and gripped it tightly.</p>
<p>There hadn’t been any new attacks in the garden since Ephial escaped. It can be said that the impending situation has calmed down now that the escort groups of each Noble family are joining up. <p>[Don’t be nervous.]</p>
<p>[But, Grandmother.]</p>

Fighting, running away, merging with other houses or keeping a distance, they are ready to move no matter which plan they choose. <p>She emitted a very strong hatred the moment she killed one of the attackers. The insane amount of magic power and murderous intent was more than enough to frighten or threaten any Nobles present.</p>
<p>The battle of threat that took place in the Royal Capital was still fresh in memory, but at that time they sensed it from a distance. This time they’re practically in the same garden. Even if the vector was directed toward the assailant, the pressure was immeasurable.</p>

<p>Novalf, who had experience of clashing with a unit led by Orshian, reflexively went into combat mode after sensing the murderous intent. <p>[Do you think Princess Orshian intends to subjugate the second demon beast?]</p>

<p>Crowne also agreed and gave instructions to maintain vigilance. It was clear that the implication was that “If Orshian comes here, do not accept the invitation”. Of course, he had refused once already, so normally people would think that neither Kalmia nor Novalf would join.</p>

<p>Perhaps realizing that it would be a hindrance if only one remained, she kicked the remaining left shoe off.</p>

<p>[It’s more refreshing this way.]</p>

<p>Taking off one’s shoes in front of the masses and leaving a torn dress as it is is the worst behavior for a Noble daughter. If someone ridicules her for walking barefoot on the ground, she can’t complain.</p>
<p>However, there is a sacred atmosphere that must not be violated in her imposing figure that does not waver even though she is receiving the attention of the Royal Nobles. No one could even approach her, let alone ridicule her.</p>
<p>As Orshian took a step forward, the Nobles who were standing in the direction of her movement retreated like a wave. Her red and blue eyes stared at Wilk Quordenze.</p>
<p>[She seems to be intending to ask the Quordenze family, no, Wilk-dono for help.]</p>
<p>The tone of her speech implied the certainties that Orshian will be turned down. Novalf nodded in response to Kalmia’s comment.</p>
<p>[Wilk-dono understands the duties of a Noble…]</p>

The subjugation of monsters in the Levios territory is the responsibility of the Levios family. Perhaps what he dared to say was his desire for the person named Wilk Quordenze to match his own image of nobility, Novalf mocks himself.</p>

<p>[Please wait right there.]</p>

<p>Orshian was distracted for a moment when she tried to approach Wilk while ignoring the maid. It was Wilk’s maid who stood in her way. <p>[Young Master accepted the request to intercede for Princess Orshian. He is preparing, so please wait for a while.]</p>

<p>Orshian was willing to break through and talk to Wilk if he said no, but if she was told that he would come over, she wouldn’t be selfish enough to ignore it. It would be a different story if she stretched out the time too slowly, but at this moment, her intention to forcefully break through was diminished.</p>

<p>And, even though it was temporary, the fact that he handled Orshian’s sudden visit well was worthy of appreciation for Crowne.</p>
<p>[Are you going to stop that? …Kalmia.]</p>
<p>[What is it, Grandmother?]</p>
<p>[Has that girl approached the snake before?]</p>

The reason she was able to catch Wilk moving to a secluded place and Orshian chasing after him was because she was also wandering around the garden to attend the next generation family meeting. <p>[Yes. I believe they have made contact before this.]</p>

<p>[Did the little girl somehow fall for him?]</p>
<p>In diplomacy, love is an element that cannot be underestimated. If a Noble boy or girl of similar age is approaching, one can’t help but pay attention.</p>
<p>Of course, Kalmia understands that as well.</p>
<p>[There was no such atmosphere. From both sides.]</p>
<p>She concluded so. Because she didn’t feel the slightest bit of sweet or bitter love from Wilk and Orshian’s conversation.</p>
<p>[I just felt like it was friendly banter. It’s a little surprising considering the events of the other day.]</p>
<p>Wilk acted like he was the eldest son of a Noble family with a long history, and Orshian acted like a commoner, as if to say that it was impossible to make up for it. Kalmia thought it wouldn’t mix like water and oil, and they wouldn’t be able to understand each other.</p>

At least she felt that Orshian was more in sync with Wilk much more than with Kilorde.</p>
<p>When Kalmia conveyed her impression, Novalf agreed.</p>
<p>[Hou…]</p>

<p>Wilk moved gracefully step by step so as to attract the attention of those around him. His majestic gestures that even felt gorgeous were overflowing with the dignity of a Great Noble.</p>
<p>[Wilk-kun!]</p>
<p>A bright, excited voice resounds high in the morning sky. The sound, which was filled with strong intentions, naturally attracted people’s attention.</p>
<p>A girl in bare feet and a boy in formal attire faced each other. It was a combination that had just happened the other day, albeit in a glaring match of murderous intent.</p>
<p>A sense of anticipation that something is about to begin, as well as anxiety, dominated the garden. Everyone in this place still remembers the threatening waves of that day.</p>
<p>Wilk took a deep breath. Feeling the cool, fresh air of the morning filling my lungs, he made a slightly exaggerated gesture. It is also a declaration that he will start speaking.</p>
<p>Wilk’s every move attracted attention.</p>
<p>[If it isn’t Orshian…]</p>
<p>[I’m going to defeat the monster! Wilk-kun, let’s go together!!]</p>
<p>However, Orshian knew nothing of such Noble customs. There are other things that matter to her.</p>
<p>Some people frowned at her rudeness, ignoring order and one-sidedly telling the other side what to do. However, Wilk, the person who was interrupted, didn’t try to blame her.</p>
<p>Is that reaction a sign of composure? The Nobles were staring intently at the exchange in order to determine the quality of the eldest son of the Great Noble Quordenze.</p>
<p>Wilk didn’t rush. He had long since given up on asking her for courtesy.</p>
<p>[Defeat the demon beast… are you referring to the second demon beast?]</p>
<p>[Huh? That’s it? That. Ah!]</p>
<p>She raised her index finger and repeatedly thrust her hand into the distance as if to violently knock on the door. Toward the huge shadow approaching the Royal Capital.</p>
<p>Wilk continued so as not to get caught up in Orshian’s pace of repeating “that, that, that” over and over again.</p>
<p>[Then, are you saying that the Archduke Milendorva will send out an army? But, the idea of defeating it together is…]</p>
<p>The question was not directed at Orshian.</p>

<p>[Kilorde. Don’t.]</p>

<p>[But, Father!]</p>

<p>[Hmm, that means the only representative from Archduke Milendorva will be Princess Orshian?]</p>

<p>[Snake, what are you thinking…?]</p>
<p>Due to Wilk’s remarks, the Zeldomitra and Adellahan had withdrawn their previous statements and are now in a situation where it is difficult to say that they will send out reinforcements. Similarly, it would be difficult to bring anything other than Orshian out of the Milendorva family. But, that’s it.</p>

The name Cassia Quordenze, the Princess of conspiracies, who had once plunged the Arpeo region into a vortex of chaos, suddenly came to Crowne’s mind.</p>

<p>[These words make us think again about what powerful people should do.]</p>
