<p>"Hmm, this time Grandfather advanced all the way to this area, so on his way back, he might be around here. According to his letter, they've completed the operation without any problem. So I think he'll return through the shortest route. Who knows, perhaps he's already at the entrance of Nyuneri City."</p>
<p>I thought while sliding my finger on the map.</p>
<p>Grandfather was my father's father and the previous head of the house. At present, he'd already handed over the headship, but he hadn't retired yet and was engaging war with other lands.</p>
<p>I somehow had the unified image of a country from my previous world, but that wasn't really the case in this world.</p>
<p>The lords of each territory were governing their own lands as they like, and the strongest among them was the king. That's why most nobles didn't have any loyalty towards the royal family. There were even some nobles who were trying to outsmart the royal family so that they could stand at the top.</p>
<p>If the royal family weakened, the nobles would usually abandon their king and join other countries or start their own together with the other nobles that they have good relations.</p>
<p>Since the royal family didn't have a strong authority towards the lords, it was often the case that some nobles would engage in war with the nobles from other countries.</p>
<p>The closest example would be us, Quordentz. The Quordentz house had been frequently in a war with Jinkaen Empire, an empire located next to Levios Kingdom. Of course, we weren't engaging in war with the whole empire, but only with some of the nobles under it.</p>
<p>Both the Levios Kingdom and the Jinkaen Empire were nothing more than a group of gathered lords. Levious Kingdom was founded around 80 years ago, while Jinkaen Empire only had 55 years of history.</p>
<p>My grandfather, who really hated some of the nobles in the empire, turned over the headship to my father the moment my father became an adult and moved the army that he'd personally trained to provoke a war.</p>
<p>According to the records in the warehouse, our territory had been expanding to the west, taking over the lands from the nobles of the empire. The reason the slavery had been in good shape these past years was also due to Grandfather's actions.</p>
<p>From the perspective of the Levios royal family, they were satisfied and didn't have any issue that my grandfather was stomping the enemy empire. Even during the recent campaign, Grandfather fiercely rampaged around the empire's lands.</p>
<p>"Someday, I want to see the world like Grandfather."</p>
<p>The world that Grandfather was seeing, I think it was a world filled with blood. If Fanny were to see that kind of world, she'd probably die from shock.</p>
<p>"If you want to go outside, then lets go to the town together in the future."</p>
<p>"Wow! are we really going?"</p>
<p>"Yes, I also have some business to do in the town so you can come along. I'll talk to father about it."</p>
<p>Fanny leaned her head on my arms. I patted her head which felt like a silk while saying 'there, there'. Fanny was forbidden to leave the castle, so for her, going to the castle town was a big event. In order to realize my date with my younger sister, I had to do my best to persuade father.</p>

<p>"What's the matter? Since you're coming at this hour, there must be some important matter."</p>

<p>"So Grandfather has returned. Did, did he mention anything about me?"</p>
<p>"No, he only said that he has something to talk about with Young Master."</p>
<p>"I see…"</p>
