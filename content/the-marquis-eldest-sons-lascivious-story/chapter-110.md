<p>Chapter 110 - Continental Map 2</p>
<p>TL Note: This chapter is basically the author talking about the political geography, and contains 3 maps. Its just lore and is irrelevant to the story, so feel free to skip this chapter. The author talks in first person here.</p>
<p>This time there are 3 maps.</p>
<p>This is the influence of the three major nations on the continent of Eruo.</p>
<p>Blue is the Kingdom of Levios, red is the Jinkaen Empire, and green is the area under the influence of the Mamshred Federation. (Not all of them are actually inhabited and ruled by people.)</p>
<p>The areas not specifically mentioned are other powers and places that are inaccessible to people.</p>
<p>The following is a brief explanation.</p>
<p>Kingdom of Levios</p>
<p>One of the three major nations of the Eruo continent. Founded about 80 years ago.</p>
<p>It was founded by the Levios family, a powerful noble family in the eastern part of the continent at the time, who were upset with the arrogant Holy High Society of Zes, and formed a coalition with the surrounding noble families.</p>
<p>There are no churches of the Holy High Society in the territories of the noble families that participated in the Levios Kingdom from the time of its founding.</p>
<p>It is also a place that claims independence from the priests and respects the independence of the noble families.</p>
<p>The Levios royal family does not have absolute power, but is, so to speak, in the leadership position of the coalition.</p>
<p>It is said to be the most stable country on the current Eruo continent.</p>
<p>Recently, the entire kingdom has been in turmoil due to various reasons, but it seems to be calming down.</p>
<p>Jinkaen Empire</p>
<p>One of the three major nations on the Eruo continent. Founded about 55 years ago.</p>
<p>It was founded by Jinkaen, the third son of a weak aristocratic family in the western part of the continent, during a time of warring factions.</p>
<p>The collapse of the second emperor triggered an upheaval in the succession to the throne that wreaked havoc in the empire.</p>
<p>Mamshred Federation</p>
<p>One of the three major states on the continent of Eruo.</p>
<p>The name of a dynasty located in the Mamshred region that has united numerous tribes, as seen from the perspective of the Kingdom of Levios and the Zinkhaen Empire.</p>
<p>The name of the state changes from dynasty to dynasty, and because the dynasties change so often, the inhabitants of the central part of the continent (where the Kingdom of Levios and the Jinkaen Empire are located) call it the Mamshred Federation because it's too complicated to remember.</p>
<p>The current official name of the country is Nagi.</p>
<p>Some of the tribes came to the Kingdom of Levios as refugees during the civil war that occurred three or four decades ago.</p>
