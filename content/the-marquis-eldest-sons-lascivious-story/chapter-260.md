<p>Chapter 260: Forward</p>
<p>The Marquis’ Eldest Son’s Lascivious Story</p>
<p>Thick clouds were piling up in the sky.</p>
<p>It’s nice to be shielded from the hot summer sun, but the sky looked uncertain for the future.</p>
<p>Reisleaf Castle could already be seen in the distance. If I raced my horse seriously, I could get there in no time, but of course I couldn’t do that right now.</p>
<p>It’s because the enemy army welcomed us.</p>
<p>Spierzeig’s second army led by Kindlor.</p>
<p>According to military officers’ estimates, the number of soldiers was 90,000.</p>
<p>[Three times as much as ours?]</p>
<p>[It’s more than that considering the internal circumstances.]</p>
<p>The number of soldiers in the Upper Jaw Army, which I currently lead, was approximately 30,000. However, most of them are locally procured Spierzeig residents.</p>
<p>By the way, the largest number was the territorial militia procured in Dornia City. Dornia City can be categorized as a city in the northern part of the Voistra Plains, but many soldiers were left behind to suppress the affiliated noble families based in the western mountainous region.</p>
<p>[It seems that the raid on Reisleaf is still continuing.]</p>
<p>It was a voice that sounded like a mixture of relief and concern.</p>
<p>The military officers concluded that around 150,000 Spierzeig soldiers would be mobilized for the operation to recapture Reisleaf. In addition to their empirical rules, this value takes into account the confidential information recovered in Weldburg City, so it can be trusted.</p>
<p>If Kindlor came here with 90,000, then Magnis had the remainder 60,000. It’s a simple subtraction.</p>
<p>[It hasn’t fallen yet. That’s good news, but…]</p>
<p>The battle is finally approaching, and tensions are rising whether I like it or not. There was no time to spare unless we were having a conversation.</p>
<p>[…will the siege continue into this period?]</p>
<p>I wonder if Magnis and Kindlor have any plans to lynch me. Well, if both armies actually appear, I’ll switch to hit-and-away tactics and run around in all directions, saving Reisleaf Castle and buying time for reinforcements to invade the northern Voistra.</p>
<p>[It would be psychologically difficult to let up on the attack when the Reisleaf Castle is about to be recaptured. The Great Shield of Voistra is a special asset for the Spierzeig family.]</p>
<p>I’m sure the same is true for Quordenze.</p>
<p>Perhaps sensing that I was nervous, the adjutant continued talking as if to review the situation.</p>
<p>[And if they back off now, the affiliated nobles who are watching the situation will suddenly become noisy.]</p>
<p>If Reisleaf cannot be recaptured with this, there will be people who will see the Spierzeig family as worthless. Considering external relations, giving priority to Reisleaf may be an unavoidable decision.</p>
<p>[It’s hard to maintain face. I guess they’ve been caught up in the eyes of those around them.]</p>
<p>Perhaps because I said it as if it were someone else’s business, the adjutant looked a little troubled, then laughed lightly.</p>
<p>[Wasn’t Young Master one of the people who pushed them too? I’m sure they’ve heard about Meiziode’s incident.]</p>
<p>[Well, how about that?]</p>
<p>From what I heard from the adjutant, it seems like the Spierzeig family would still prioritize Reisleaf Castle even if I didn’t do anything.</p>
<p>[The main purpose is different. Even if it has the effect of pushing them, it’s secondary. Just a bonus.]</p>
<p>Even as I said that, I was relieved that I was able to pull out only Kindlor’s army. I don’t want to deal with two dangerous Highlords at the same time.</p>
<p>Our army and Kindlor’s army gradually approached each other, and the image became clearer.</p>
<p>Soldiers, soldiers, soldiers, soldiers, soldiers, soldiers… There was an incredible number of people, but with detection magic I was able to pinpoint where the enemy’s main force was. At the same time, I felt a little strange.</p>
<p>[…That’s strange. It feels like the main force of Kindlor is nearby.]</p>
<p>[Compared to Operation Fang? At that time, the Highlord’s main headquarters was not among the troops attacking Nambonan City, and they were stationed at the rear. I think it’s natural that it feels closer compared to that time.]</p>
<p>Well, certainly the most memorable battle for me was the defeat of Spierzieg’s army in Nambonan City. Since it was also a strong success experience, I may have unconsciously compared it to that.</p>
<p>[Unlike Verret’s main force, Kindlor’s main force seems to be in the middle of the army.]</p>
<p>[In a decisive battle between armies led by Highlords, this arrangement is the safest. It will make a difference in reinforcement.]</p>
<p>It goes without saying, but body strengthening magic is more effective when used from within the army rather than from a distance.</p>
<p>I understood this not only logically but also by experience. It was none other than myself who led the “Voistra Plains Cities Continuous March Tour” by making full use of body strengthening magic.</p>
<p>[It’s not as easy to break through as it looks.]</p>
<p>[Affirmative. The Lord-class soldiers will be hard and difficult to cut down. And in this case, the enemy will be able to replenish their troops immediately from the rear. Kindlor’s army can always fight in its full strength of 90,000 soldiers.]</p>
<p>The adjutant says that if you defeat 1,000 soldiers in Kindlor’s army, 1,000 replacements should be sent from Magnis’ army. Magnis and Kindlor seemed to have decided that if they had 90,000 soldiers, they would be able to withstand Wilk Quordenze’s assault with ease.</p>
<p>If you think about it the other way around, the Kindlor army doesn’t need any more soldiers. It may be a natural conclusion that they left the rest to conquer Reisleaf.</p>
<p>[That’s a troubling story.]</p>
<p>The Kindlor army has very high defense power, and it’s kinda like a boss character with an automatic recovery effect? Even if you keep attacking, you won’t be able to conquer it forever.</p>
<p>[If the enemy has enough soldiers, we won’t be able to break through. No matter how young you are, you’ll definitely be stopped midway. The wall of 90,000 is too thick as expected.]</p>
<p>I remembered the battle in Nambonan City. The attack of the troops led by the Highlord is unparalleled in power, but it is possible to obstruct the advance with the appropriate judgment of the military commander and accurate use of troops. What’s more, this time the enemy side also has the strengthening magic of a Highlord.</p>
<p>The adjutant declared that even with my amount of magical power, I would never be able to surpass it.</p>
<p>[…It’s too thick, huh?]</p>
<p>[It’s easy to see that they are very wary of Young Master. Honestly, there are more than enough soldiers.]</p>
<p>When I met Magnis in the capital, he probably guessed my amount of magical power. If we hadn’t met, I might have been able to catch him off guard. There’s nothing I can do about it now.</p>
<p>[Over time, our troops will increase, but compared to them, it will be insignificant. There’s no way we can win in a sustained battle. We’ll be wiped out first and reduced to dust.]</p>
<p>At this very moment, the Quordenze soldiers are supposed to be entering one after another from the interior, but it will take a lot of time to reach the front line in Reisleaf. In the first place, the invading main forces were ordered to prioritize invading and suppressing the northern region. We don’t get that many soldiers here as the vanguard.</p>
<p>[Magnis’ army is in the way…]</p>
<p>If only the Kindlor army was there, we could replenish the Quordenze soldiers as much as we wanted via foreign land. However, since Magnis’ army is holding its ground in front of Reisleaf Castle, reinforcements will not be able to reach us in time.</p>
<p>[…But, there’s no point in complaining.]</p>
<p>We succeeded in attracting the Kindlor’s army. If that’s the case, I have no choice but to open myself up and try as much as I can. There are also recovery plans in case of failure.</p>
<p>[I’m going to defeat Kindlor Spierzeig. That’s all I’m thinking about right now.]</p>
<p>If we can do that, Operation Snake’s Jaw will end with a great success. Magnis’ army alone cannot maintain Reisleaf Castle even if it is recaptured. Once the Kindlor army is defeated, Magnis will be forced to retreat.</p>
<p>[Preparations for ambush are already complete.]</p>
<p>It is clear that it is impossible to conquer with a direct attack method. That’s why I was devising a plan.</p>
<p>The Upper Jaw Army that is currently advancing is long and vertical, and I am in the center of it. If you were to compare it to an airplane, it would be the seat near the main wing.</p>
<p>And several military officers were sent to stand in ambush at a point where the wings of this imaginary airplane were extended considerably. They are placed right next to me, on each side. Since they are far away from the Upper Jaw Army, the Kindlor Army probably recognizes them as scout soldiers.</p>
<p>The ambush soldiers also perform reconnaissance, but this is only a secondary mission. Its true role is to launch a surprise attack on Kindlor’s army. They carry Manaral crystals filled with my magical power, waiting anxiously for the moment to attack.</p>
<p>This is a reuse of the Manaral crystals used to cross the Arknoa Contaminated Area. The treasure would be wasted if I left it with Eve who stayed back at the Misty Village, so I made sure to collect it.</p>
<p>[We didn’t use it in the duel in Nambonan City. I never thought we would have such an attack method.]</p>
<p>I did something similar when I had a one-on-one fight with Spierzeig Veret. But I entrusted the Manaral Crystal to Knight Gatoren, telling him to help me if I was about to lose.</p>
<p>In the end, we were able to win without using it at that time, so Spierzeig’s army was not aware of its existence. So, I decided to use it here and now.</p>
<p>[Please draw in the Kindlor army.]</p>
<p>If the Upper Jaw Army and Kindlor Army were to collide head-on like this, we would almost certainly be pushed. After all, we are just a group of people.</p>
<p>[I’ll leave the decision to retreat to you.]</p>
<p>That’s why the retreat of the Upper Jaw army is natural. No one doubts this move. They are simply retreating when they should be retreating.</p>
<p>And if the Kindlor army thinks they have the upper hand, they would come forward to attack the Upper Jaw army. What would happen if the ambush soldiers deployed to the left and right at that time remained in place without moving? Their position would change from being right next to the Upper Jaw Army to being right next to the advancing Kindlor Army. If we can attract them enough, we might be able to get behind them instead of right next to them.</p>
<p>[I’ll pull them all the way down to their throat.]</p>
<p>Of course, Kindlor’s army should be wary of the remaining soldiers who appear to be scouts left and right. However, their wariness should be lesser given that the people at the flank are at most Lord-class.</p>
<p>[The warped poisonous fangs will pierce the enemy.]</p>
<p>Seeing the timing, the ambush soldiers on the left and right will release Highlord-level attack magic towards the Kindlor army. A surprise attack from behind would be shocking. Kindlor’s army will surely fall into chaos.</p>
<p>[In the end, I will claim Kindlor’s head together with the elite 3,000. It will be a very short decisive battle. Once the situation starts, don’t relax for even a second.]</p>
<p>The poisonous fangs pierced through the prey, which caused it to panic from the pain. But, the snake would swallow it all at once.</p>
<p>However, I don’t think everything will go that well. There is a chance of victory, but I don’t think things will go as planned on the battlefield.</p>
<p>[If it is impossible to break through, please advise immediately. In that case, we will retreat without hesitation.]</p>
<p>I won’t say that I will destroy the Spierzeig family even if I stab them in the wrong place.</p>
<p>Because I’m fighting to live.</p>
<p>[Understood.]</p>
<p>[It will be difficult to judge, but I believe in your wisdom and experience.]</p>
<p>So don’t decide to retreat too quickly, don’t give up until the very limit… I implicitly told him this. This is a check that does not allow overprotection. The adjutant nodded respectfully.</p>
<p>[It’s finally time.]</p>
<p>The Kindlor army in front gradually slowed down. Just like us, the enemy was vertically long while moving, but eventually spread out horizontally. The Upper Jaw Army also deployed its troops accordingly.</p>
<p>Both armies had now determined the location of the battle.</p>
<p>The battle began.</p>
<p>When I think about that, I don’t even notice the noise made by the soldiers. A mixture of nervousness and excitement rushed through my body, making my heart race. I felt like my own heartbeat was the loudest sound in this place.</p>
<p>I wondered what the others were thinking as they approached the battle, and when I looked at my adjutant standing next to me, I saw him peering at the enemy with a curious look on his face.</p>
<p>[…What is it?]</p>
<p>[The enemy soldiers are upset.]</p>
<p>It seems that the Kindlor soldiers were not exactly excited when they saw this army. When he said that, the average soldier seemed somewhat dismayed.</p>
<p>Regardless of the Spierzeig military officer who was in a position to obtain information, the ordinary soldiers probably never thought that they, the attackers, would be targeted.</p>
<p>[They can’t seem to get things under control.]</p>
<p>The adjutant continued in a mocking tone.</p>
<p>[It seems that the morale of the ordinary soldiers is not very high. Since this is an interception battle after they have been attacking Reisleaf for days and nights, it is natural for them to feel discouraged.]</p>
<p>I think attacking Reisleaf was based on a rotational system, but if they keep attacking for days on end, they’ll probably run out of motivation.</p>
<p>In that sense, the Upper Jaw Army is similar, but the Dornia City territorial militia recovered on the way were relatively healthy, and the morale of the elite 3,000 or so members was still high.</p>
<p>[…No, that’s not all.]</p>
<p>The people of Spierzeig territory were already exhausted. Tired of the war itself, not just this battle.</p>
<p>[Will we be able to break through this unexpectedly easily?]</p>
<p>[We might be able to put up a good fight in the first clash, but it’s still almost impossible to push through.]</p>
<p>The adjutant reprimanded me by saying that at this level, Spierzeig officers would be able to rebuild without any problems, and that it would be a mistake to expect anything from the low morale of the enemy soldiers.</p>
<p>[Being able to handle even weak soldiers with low morale without any problems…that is the highlight of a military officer’s skills, isn’t it?]</p>
<p>[Affirmative. Indeed it is.]</p>
<p>The adjutant laughed fearlessly.</p>
<p>[Of course, we have no intention of losing either.]</p>
<p>He also told me to look at other people’s behavior and change my own. The problem of weak troops may actually be more serious on this side. After all, what I’m leading is a hastily assembled ragtag force.</p>
<p>Things were working fine for now, but there was a good chance that the morale would collapse over something trivial.</p>
<p>[I trust your skills…But, I also think it’s my role to turn weak soldiers into strong ones.]</p>
<p>One of the General’s jobs is to raise the morale of the soldiers.</p>
<p>[Young Master’s name is widely known in this land. You will be a great support to our soldiers. I think just standing tall will be enough.]</p>
<p>The adjutant said there’s no need for me to worry too much.</p>
<p>I think he’s probably concerned about the morale of the serf soldiers and that it will affect my original job of intimidation and magic activation.</p>
<p>[We’ll just heat things up a little before the war starts. When the time is right, I’ll ask for a signal.]</p>
<p>Even when making a statement, timing is important. After confirming that my adjutant nodded, I started thinking of words of encouragement.</p>
<p>The two armies finally faced each other and were about to start a staring contest. The situation was such that a battle could break out at any time.</p>
<p>Even though there were so many people, their minds were focused on one place. There is a thing called “the moment when the end of war” exists, and it is floating somewhere on this battlefield… Everyone was staring at the void, so much so that they had the illusion.</p>
<p>The adjutant said quietly.</p>
<p>[…Young Master, please give a heroic speech.]</p>
<p>This place was like a stage prepared for me and Kindlor.</p>
<p>The Quordenze soldiers, the Spierzeig soldiers, the Quordenze military officers, the Spierzeig military officers, all of them are performers and spectators.</p>
<p>I will not hand over the leading role to Kindlor. By livening up the atmosphere, I will show all my friends and enemies who is the real main character.</p>
<p>I ordered my adjutant.</p>
<p>[Let the soldiers raise the flag.]</p>
<p>==============</p>
<p>Wilk’s army moved.</p>
<p>All the Spierzeig military officers belonging to Kindlor’s army paid attention to his every move.</p>
<p>[…Is he rallying his own troops?]</p>
<p>That’s what one Spierzeig military officer said. He made that decision after seeing the soldiers of Wilk’s army raise their flags all at once.</p>
<p>Wilk’s army was clearly a motley crew. It is appropriate to raise the flag of one’s own army and raise the fighting spirit of the soldiers.</p>
<p>The military officers immediately understood his intentions. What they didn’t understand were the ordinary soldiers who followed.</p>
<p>[Those flags…!]</p>
<p>That flag was also hoisted at Reisleaf Castle, which they had been attacking for days on end. The coat of arms depicted on both were the same.</p>
<p>[A snake entwined with intersecting spears and water spray…!?]</p>
<p>There was not a single person here who did not know that emblem. It wasn’t supposed to be there.</p>
<p>[It’s the Quordenze army!!]</p>
<p>[Why is the main force of Quordenze here!?]</p>
<p>[It’s an unbelievably large army!!]</p>
<p>[Weren’t we attacking?!]</p>
<p>The general idea among military officers is that as long as ordinary soldiers are given commands and they do a reasonable amount of work, that’s fine. There are many military officers who don’t want to be told unnecessary things and have their soldiers do whatever they want. As a result, little information about Wilk’s army’s attack was conveyed to the Kindlor soldiers.</p>
<p>However, now that the military flag has been raised, all the soldiers will know the true identity of the army they are facing. 30,000 is less than 90,000, but that’s just a military officer’s feeling. From the point of view of the ordinary soldiers actually facing the enemy, 30,000 is just 30,000, and it is nothing more than a large army.</p>
<p>The shock and confusion spread like waves from front to back.</p>
<p>[Quiet!!]</p>
<p>Of course, Spierzeig officers expected that level of turmoil. The scolding voice of the unit commander echoed throughout the Kindlor army.</p>
<p>Before the noisy Kindlor soldiers could be calmed down, the next movement began.</p>
<p>[I am Wilk Quordenze!!]</p>
<p>Wilk’s clear voice echoed as if piercing through the thick clouds. The loudspeaker magic made the announcement echoed throughout the entire battlefield.</p>
<p>After a moment of silence, confusion disappeared from the Kindlor soldiers. What emerged instead was palpable fear.</p>
<p>[The Great Shield Destroyer!!?]</p>
<p>[It’s Nambonan’s Blue Demon!! The Blue Demon has attacked again with a surprise attack!!!]</p>
<p>[Galfis’ grandson!?]</p>
<p>[W, w, w, wee, we are done for!!]</p>
<p>The name of Wilk Quordenze, the grandson of Galfis and the young nobleman who destroyed Voistra’s Great Shield, was well known even to the lowest-rank of soldiers. Furthermore, some of the Kindlor soldiers fought with Wilk’s forces in Nambonan City and were captured. It was no wonder that they were in a state of panic.</p>
<p>Wilk continued to speak with enthusiasm, as if mocking the chaos.</p>
<p>[All of northern Voistra has fallen to Quordenze! Look at my results!!!]</p>
<p>Wilk’s army took down the military flag of Quordenze and raised completely different flags in accordance with the lines. There was not just one type of flag, but multiple types.</p>
<p>Few Kindlor soldiers know all of the emblems depicted on those flags. However, most of them recognized at least one type. This is because it’s familiar to them.</p>
<p>[It’s the flag of Weldburg City!]</p>
<p>[There’s also Balaika City!]</p>
<p>[That’s Meiziode City! And Fanes City too!?]</p>
<p>[T, that’s!! Torsa City…!]</p>
<p>Most of the soldiers who participated in the operation to recapture Reisleaf were citizens gathered from the northern region of the Voistra Plains.</p>
<p>The cities that Wilk conquered were the hometowns where they were born and raised, or the cities closest to their hometowns, places they had heard of at least once if they were alive. Many Kindlor soldiers are familiar with the city emblem painted on the flag.</p>
<p>[Hey! Is it true!? The flags of Balaika City and Fanes City are over there!?]</p>
<p>Some soldiers from Kindlor’s army were brought in from other regions. The soldier who had joined the battle from the center of the Voistra Plains was not very familiar with the flag raised by Wilk’s army. However, if the names of key cities in the north come up one after another from the mouth of a colleague besides them, there was no way they could stay calm.</p>
<p>[Balaika is my birthplace!! There’s no way I could mistake it!!]</p>
<p>The soldiers who came from the central area were stunned by the words of their colleague who was about to break down in tears. Because they knew a little more about geography than the others, they understood the danger of these cities falling.</p>
<p>[All of the northern Voistra has been felled by Quordenze!!]</p>
<p>As if to stir up confusion, Wilk repeated the same declaration.</p>
<p>At the same time, the city flag was lowered and the military flag with the Quordenze crest was raised again. It seemed as if it was a metaphor for the fall of the northern city by the Quordenze army.</p>
<p>In reality, Wilk simply hid this before he realized that only the flags of the Seven Cities, which are important points, existed. However, Kindlor soldiers do not know the behind-the-scenes circumstances. They had begun to believe Wilk’s words that all of the north was under Quordenze control.</p>
<p>[Don’t listen to lies!! Be quiet!! Don’t talk nonsense between soldiers!!]</p>
<p>Spierzeig officers restrained the soldiers with a strong tone. However, there was no one who did not feel anxious when they heard that their hometown had been taken away. The soldiers kept their mouths shut in response to the military officer’s orders, but they remained restless and watched their surroundings for a long time.</p>
<p>How did Quordenze attack? Why did the city that was supposed to be in the rear fall? Why did this situation suddenly occur? Wilk provided answers to the questions that arise in the soldiers’ minds.</p>
<p>[A message to those who fell into the trap of my grandfather Galfis! !]</p>
<p>It was spoken in a way that emphasized the “trap” part very much.</p>
<p>[Regret that you were lured to Reisleaf!! It’s all a trap to make this surprise attack a success!! It’s all a ploy to free Voistra!!]</p>
<p>At Wilk’s brazen and bold lie, Kindlor made a bitter expression on his face.</p>
<p>[Cut the nonsense…!]</p>
<p>Of course, the upper echelon at Spierzeig understood why Wilk had told such a fabricated story and its main purpose.</p>
<p>[As expected, the purpose is probably to hide the lack of magic power.]</p>
<p>The adjutant standing by Kindlor’s side continued speaking indifferently.</p>
<p>[Even if they succeed in defending Reisleaf Castle, it will still be a sign of Quordenze’s decline.]</p>
<p>The Spierzeig family had already assumed that Wilk would come to the rescue of Reisleaf Castle via Nambonan City, so it was a move without any particular problems. This is because they can spread the rumor to the world that “Galfis, who developed a magic malfunction, abandoned the front line and remained miserable in seclusion until his grandson came to rescue him”.</p>
<p>With the success of the Spierzeig army pushing back the Quordenze army all the way to Reisleaf, the surrounding noble families will also be convinced that Galfis has developed a magic malfunction. If that happens, there will probably be a fundamental review of the policy towards the Quordenze family. The tide of noble diplomacy was supposed to change.</p>
<p>[Galfis’s army feigns magic malfunction and retreats, attracting Spierzeig’s army to Reisleaf. Meanwhile, Wilk’s army traverses the old Amelia public road and takes control of the northern part of the Voistra Plains, which has become an empty shell. It was a strategy… By making such a claim, I wonder if he is trying to make General Galfis’ condition ambiguous.]</p>
<p>Wilk chose to go through Amelia-Arknoa. What will happen if Spierzeig’s army fails to capture Reisleaf and he captures the northern part of the Voistra Plains? At that time, the only result that will remain is that “Spierzeig was completely caught up in Quordenze’s plot”.</p>
<p>[There have been reports that similar claims are being made in Dornia City and Meiziode City. Their target is probably the west.]</p>
<p>In the end, people from other families can only judge the situation based on the final results of the battle. If the Quordenze army carried out the operation and obtained the results commensurate with it, the others would give weight to their claims.</p>
<p>Wilk thought there was room to plant doubts there. If the strategy is successful, they can cover the truth with a veil. Among most noble families, they will think “I don’t think the Quordenze family, which was supposed to be in the upper hand, would carry out such a high-stakes strategy. Perhaps, as the Spierzeig family claimed, Galfis had developed a magic malfunction”. However, one cannot be sure of that. This is because the Quordenze family has achieved great results. The doubt that “Galfis may be alive and well” can only be resolved by confronting the person himself. But, the Quordenze family won’t allow it. The truth will always be in darkness.</p>
<p>The illusion of Galfis has a strong deterrent effect. Noble houses would think twice before becoming an enemy to the Quordenze family.</p>
<p>[What a poor ploy…]</p>
<p>A young nobleman who decides to take the heroic action of crossing Amelia-Arknoa should feel the raw enthusiasm and spirit of wanting to surpass the warriors of Galfis. However, what Kindlor felt was Galfis’ grandson’s spiteful tenacity to make use of his grandfather’s military name until the very end.</p>
<p>[…However, I think Magnis understands the kid’s purpose.]</p>
<p>That’s why Spierzeig’s army must take back Reisleaf Castle. The goal was to have a “successful recapture of Reisleaf Castle” that was clearly visible from the outside. If they retreat like this, Wilk’s lie that it was a trap will become true.</p>
<p>The considerations of Kindlor and Spierzeig officers were correct. Wilk’s purpose is to create an illusion of Galfis.</p>
<p>However, that consideration was a little lacking. The people Wilk was trying to convey his message to were not just the “Nobles” of the Zinkaen Empire and the Levios Kingdom.</p>
<p>And the effects of this were now becoming apparent on the ground.</p>
<p>[I knew it! I thought there was no way that the red demon would retreat!]</p>
<p>[We were trapped!!]</p>
<p>The lamenting voices of the Kindlor soldiers resounded across the battlefield.</p>
<p>Even after witnessing the retreat of the front line and the siege of Reisleaf, there were quite a few soldiers who were suspicious of Galfis’s sudden poor condition. They couldn’t believe that the veteran General who had won numerous battles would end up like this. That’s why the lies Wilk told are deeply ingrained in their hearts.</p>
<p>The morale of the Spierzeig Territory Militia, also known as the Kindlor soldiers, was not high to begin with. The despair that “Galfis might be alive and well” quickly eroded their hearts.</p>
<p>Spierzeig officers, who were on the scene and felt the change firsthand, became a little impatient.</p>
<p>[The soldiers are quite shaken.]</p>
<p>[I don’t think I’ll be overtaken, but it’s not a very good situation.]</p>
<p>Kindlor could argue back with Wilk using loudspeaker magic. However, in the end, it became nothing more than an argument. Wilk claimed that it was a plan, and Kindlor claimed that Galfis’s magic is malfunctioning.</p>
<p>It was an eternal parallel line. Only the winner’s claim will be correct. The moment the lines intersect is the moment of battle.</p>
<p>At that moment, an intense magic force filled with murderous intent ran through both armies almost simultaneously.</p>
<p>[Gah….!!!]</p>
<p>The exchange of threats finally began, and Spierzeig officers instinctively braced themselves.</p>
