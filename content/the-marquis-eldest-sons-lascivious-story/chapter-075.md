<p>Map of the Continent (1)</p>
<p>Author Note:</p>
<p>This is sudden but here is a map of the continent of Elluo.</p>
<p>I’ve only named the places that often came up in the story so far.</p>
<p>I’ll add more onto the map as the number of places increases in the story.</p>
<p>This map only shows the rough shape of the continent and large rivers.</p>
<p>Terrain features such as rivers that branch off the large rivers, normal rivers, mountain ranges, lakes are all not there right now.</p>
<p>The yellow-green blocks are the Qualdense’s territory.</p>
<p>In recent years, due to Wilk’s grandfather General Garphis’s efforts in the war of aggression, the Qualdense’s territory has been expanding to the west.</p>
<p>His grandfather is currently working hard to build a castle in the westernmost part of the territory.</p>
<p>This is not recorded on the map, but there is a river that branched off the large river there.</p>
<p>It goes around Mt.Erekthion, then it continues northwest in the mountains.</p>
<p>Some of you might look at the map and go “Mm?” like you noticed something, but there’s no special foreshadowing or anything like that.</p>
