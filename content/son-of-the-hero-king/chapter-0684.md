<p>High in the Astral realm, sitting on a reclining chair with a parasol protecting her pristine skin from the sun, Tiamat looked at a dozen books spread on the table at her right. Though they were numerous, three titles repeated again and again among the lot.</p>
<p>[Bible of the All-Seeing Radiant Lord]</p>
<p>[The Sulfurous Adventure of the Prince of Lust]</p>
<p>[The Tales of the Dragon Emperor]</p>
<p>The gentle sound of the wave filled the air as two Titans were busy frolicking in the sea. Meanwhile, the smallest of the three Titans was sitting on another reclining chair close to Tiamat.</p>
<p>“So, you are telling me the phoenix girl wrote all of this?” Tiamat took the book titled The Prince of Lust, read a few pages, and could not stop herself from whistling. “I did not think you were that daring, Kiyohime.”</p>
<p>“Oh! let me see!” Skuld took a second book with the same title and started reading as well. “Heh, she is a talented writer. This one is particularly detailed. Hahaha.”</p>
<p>Standing on the white sand, clad in a blue swimsuit, Kiyohime could only close her eyes. Her face was red and her breathing was a little hurried. She wished she could jump in the sea and sink in the deepest part of the ocean. Forgotten by everyone forever.</p>
<p>This was a shame of the like she had never felt and the cackles of Tiamat and Skuld did not help her out. Not one bit.</p>
<p>It had been a few days now since Skuld lost most of her powers. Despite going from a powerful Titan to what was essentially a powerless mortal, said Titan did not seem particularly bothered and acted as carefree as ever.</p>
<p>In fact, Kiyohime had a feeling that, until now, Skuld had only been putting on an act and that only now was she truly feeling carefree.</p>
<p>But she was not in the mood to ponder more about this situation and simply answered Tiamat's question.</p>
<p>“Nefertiti is the author; Kaiser climbed up with the books and also shared all the events about the war he witnessed.” She took a recording device and showed it to Tiamat.</p>
<p>“Oh my…” Both Tiamat and Skuld exclaimed simultaneously. This video was different from the previous one they had received. It was a fully compiled and edited recording of all the events that were broadcast by Lustburg.</p>
<p>Looking at the events showing different sides of Sol, Tiamat had to admit that she felt endless admiration in her heart.</p>
