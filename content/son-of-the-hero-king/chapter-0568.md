<p>Echidna had many small goals in the upcoming conflicts and while they were all relatively easy to accomplish she had to make sure everything worked for the better.</p>
<p>The path toward the mastery of her power was slow but incredible.</p>
<p>Using the Earth element as the base, evolving it to gravity and finally transforming it in the absolute destruction that was a black hole by compressing the weight to the utmost degree.</p>
<p>While Nuwa had shown an unexpected talent and managed to directly reach the power of Gravity, there was one individual that intrigued her.</p>
<p>'That maid was interesting.'</p>
<p>Echidna sighed as the next gate was getting destroyed and the pure energy filled her body.</p>
<p>Milia. Nothing more than a cow woman. A weak beast woman with no special qualities or skills. No hidden bloodline.</p>
<p>Even so, this woman was the one who ended up having the highest compatibility with her blood. To the point of creating a zone similar to the path she once used.</p>
<p>The endless shadows.</p>
<p>'I would have loved to observe her more but I had to make a decision.'</p>
<p>In the end, Milia was nothing more than a failed product.</p>
<p>Nuwa meanwhile was the result of years of research.</p>
<p>Using the blood of Asmodeus and her, mixing their divinity and bringing a body that has the potential to evolve limitlessly until up to the demigod level at least.</p>
<p>There was so much potential. So much buried.</p>
<p>'If only that damned Dwarf did not intervene.:</p>
<p>She would have been reborn in the perfect body. She would have grown far faster and by now she would have already regained her prowess as a demigod and much more.</p>
<p>'But it's also good.'</p>
<p>A scientist had to be prepared for the unexpected and use it as a way to further growth.</p>
<p>Her goal had always been the same —- Witnessing the birth of the absolute being.</p>
<p>The creation of a new god. Doing the impossible and breaking the unbreakable wall that was transcendence.</p>
<p>Nothing in this world could compare to the potential Nuwa had — At least this was what she believed until now.</p>
<p>"I came to take a look because of what he said. But I must say — This is truly surprising."</p>
<p>Echidna stopped, for the third time in her life she felt a chill go down her spine, her instincts screaming at her about an incredible danger that could threaten her life.</p>
<p>Even so, she was Echidna, the mother of thousands of monsters.</p>
<p>Her eyes flickered and she finally turned towards the unexpected voice.</p>
<p>Behind her, there should have been nothing other than a void. The results of devouring the previous gate without hesitation and disturbing the equilibrium of this zone</p>
<p>But in the same void —stood a man.</p>
<p>Short golden hair, a handsome face, and a leisurely gate.</p>
<p>But what captured her attention were the striking two blue orbs. Eyes blue like the sky or the surface of the sea, giving an illusion of wideness with no limit.</p>
<p>For the first time in her life, Echidna felt loss.</p>
<p>She knew who it was. Even without looking at his recognizable features, she could deduce who it was from the memories of Nuwa.</p>
<p>But this was why Echidna was even more lost for words.</p>
<p>Sol Dragona Luxuria.</p>
<p>A young boy born from the union of Mars Luxuria and Blaze Dragons. Someone who barely came of ages and who, based on Nuwa's memories, was not even a Duke yet.</p>
<p>"Sol!…"</p>
<p>Many thoughts flew past her mind as her expression morphed into one of genuine joy.</p>
<p>"Do not talk to me in such an endearing way. You are not Nuwa."</p>
<p>The smile froze and her expression cooled down with it.</p>
<p>"To think the Prince would grow so fast."</p>
<p>"I have heard this same expression so many times, it's boring."</p>
<p>He took a step forward, until he was only a few centimeters away from her, forcing her to look up at him.</p>
<p>This was pretty humiliating for Echidna who had been used to be treated as a queen</p>
<p>"I know who you are. I understand the current situation and I honestly have many things to ask you."</p>
<p>Echidna bites her teeth.</p>
<p>"You are pretty calm despite knowing who you are facing."</p>
<p>She forced herself to stand tall but all she received was a mocking smile from the one facing her.</p>
<p>"I am not calm. As I said, I am surprised. To think the great and legendary Echidna would be reduced to nothing but a mere parasite who needs the body of a little girl to survive."</p>
<p>Echidna gritted her teeth and was about to lash out when she saw his finger shining with a golden light as it slowly approached her.</p>
<p>Instantly, her hackles were raised and she tried to move away, only to realize that she was no more in the Zone of the Bai Hu but in a different place.</p>
<p>'This should be his dimension.'</p>
<p>"As I thought, there are no changes. I guess even though you are just a parasite now you are still a demigod, huh."</p>
<p>Echidna tried to accelerate the power of her black hole.</p>
<p>She wasn't just able to absorb the energy from objects and the like. It was also possible for her to destroy and absorb organic matter.</p>
<p>The dimension itself was another thing she could target.</p>
<p>'Even though he has a dimension, the power a Duke can use with his dimension should be abyssal.'</p>
<p>She was forced to stop her prejudice when she realized that no matter how fast the blackhole accelerated, no energy was drawn.</p>
<p>In fact, the young prince was simply observing her acting, with a benign smile, as if looking at a young child acting unreasonably.</p>
<p>"I will be direct. You now have two choices."</p>
<p>The golden light flashed once again, giving her a feeling of danger.</p>
<p>She had the illusion that a giant pair of scissors was hanging above her head, ready to bisect her at any moment.</p>
<p>This was becoming very problematic.</p>
