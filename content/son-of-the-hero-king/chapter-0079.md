<p>The mana user needed six steps in order to reach the summit.</p>
<p>Reinforcement</p>
<p>Cladding</p>
<p>Materialization</p>
<p>Intent</p>
<p>Zone</p>
<p>And finally… Avatar.</p>
<p class="scene-break">· · ·</p>
<p>&lt;&lt;War zone: screams of thousands soldiers&gt;&gt;</p>
<p>&lt;&lt;Tyrant intent: Dragon Fear&gt;&gt;</p>
<p>The intent represented the will of a person to perform an action. Killing intent, fighting intent, Tyrant intent. Since a person could make different choices and perform different actions, one person could use thousands of intents.</p>
<p>But the zone was different. It represented the supreme truth laying in one heart. One person could only have one zone. Even though said zone could change.</p>
<p>As Sol reviewed the information about the zone, he knew why the Duke was showing him this scene.</p>
<p>The zone was the supreme truth. The zone represented the outlook of life of the user.</p>
<p>The zone was not only a supreme weapon but also a supreme weak point since not only it showed everything about the user, but its destruction could bring the user on the path of insanity.</p>
<p>Looking at the scene in front of him, Sol understood more than ever how gruesome war was.</p>
<p>There was no glory in war.</p>
<p>There was no joy in war.</p>
<p>Even the victor had to stand on the bodies of its soldiers.</p>
<p>Children who will never see their parents again.</p>
<p>Parents who will never see their children again.</p>
<p>This was the true ugliness of the thing known as war.</p>
<p>"Your highness, as of now I have lived for three generations."</p>
<p>The voice of the Duke sounded so far away from him. There was none of the usual joy. Only gloom and sadness.</p>
<p>"I fought against Wratharis more times than I can count. I fought against the greedy dwarves. I fought against the cunning demons. I fought against the prideful elves. More than anything-I fought against the incarnations of gluttony. I have fought for so long. More than the number of years you lived."</p>
<p>Sol listened attentively. This was the wisdom of the old.</p>
<p>"Your highness, do you know? I see each and every soldier as my own child. From the foot soldiers to the high and mighty generals. I see all of them as precious children of the kingdom I swore to protect all those years ago.</p>
<p>"Your highness. Let me ask you again. What is war?"</p>
<p>Sol continued to fight back the pressure of the zone with his intent. But inwardly, he asked himself the question.</p>
<p>What is war?</p>
<p>As someone from a modern world he could give many answers.</p>
<p>Literally speaking, war is an intense armed conflict between states, governments, societies, or paramilitary groups such as mercenaries, insurgents and militias.</p>
<p>From an economical standpoint, war was a fight necessary in order to obtain more spaces and resources.</p>
<p>Ideally speaking, war is the place where heroes rise.</p>
<p>From a realistic standpoint, war is death and destruction.</p>
<p>From a cynical standpoint, war is the game of the higher ups and the sacrifices of the lower people.</p>
<p>So many definitions. So many ways. But if you asked Sol to give an answer to this question. Then he could only give one answer.</p>
<p>"I do not know."</p>
<p>The atmosphere stagnated as the zone seemed to vacille a little. The fragile balance seemed to tilt in Sol favor.</p>
<p>The Duke looked at him with curiosity in his eyes as he smiled mysteriously, "You do not know?"</p>
<p>"Indeed. I do not know."</p>
<p>The intent of Sol intensified at those words.</p>
<p>"All my life have been blissful. Even though my parents died, I do not remember them and as such have no particular feeling of sadness over it. I never had to starve. Never had to suffer. Never felt loss and never fought for my life. As such, what could I know about war?"</p>
<p>Sol was someone who always refused to overestimate himself. It was one thing to show pride. It was another thing to change it into arrogance.</p>
<p>The one standing in front of him was a veteran who fought for more years than he lived in this world. Any answer he would give by using his knowledge would be without substance.</p>
<p>In his old world, Sol was nothing more than a random teenager who never lived any particular events. In this world, the greatest extent of his suffering was some harsh training he received from his aunt.</p>
<p>What could he understand about war?</p>
<p>What could he understand about pain and suffering?</p>
<p>"I know nothing and this is exactly why!"</p>
<p>His tone changed from a calm breeze to a raging tornado.</p>
<p>"This is exactly why I need your loyalty!"</p>
<p>His intent was slowly changing even as the words left his mouth.</p>
<p>"A king is the leader of a kingdom. But, a king is in no way omnipotent.</p>
<p>"There's many things I do not know. As such it's necessary for me to surround myself with people who do know!</p>
<p>"What I need are trustworthy comrades who will follow me on the path I am about to follow!</p>
<p>"What I need is a close advisor who will guide me on the path of greatness!</p>
<p>"What I need–are people full of experience and worthy of trust."</p>
<p>Standing up, Sol looked down on the old Duke.</p>
<p>Pointing at him, "I do not know war. But you do."</p>
<p>"I have no experience with war. But you do."</p>
<p>"I do not understand the pain of war. But you do."</p>
<p>"Then, if you are truly the greatest general of this kingdom. If you are truly the one who went through thousands of battlefields but still came out alive.</p>
<p>"I ask you. Right here, right now , are you willing to make an oath!? Will you swear allegiance to me?!”</p>
<p>The zone of the Duke was no more. All around him was golden light so pure and so bright it was blinding.</p>
<p>&lt;&lt;Ruler intent: Dragon Awe&gt;&gt;</p>
<p>Looking at the young prince bathed in such a light in front of him, the Duke could feel his blood boiling.</p>
<p>How long had he waited for such words?</p>
<p>Standing up, he looked deeply in the eyes of Sol and–slowly put one knee on the ground.</p>
<p>"Tyr Highland, do you swear allegiance?"</p>
<p>Putting his left fist over his heart and his right hand behind his back, the Duke answered loud and clear.</p>
<p>"I, Tyr Highland, in front of the goddess do swear that I will be faithful and bear true allegiance to his highness Sol Dragona Luxuria, his heirs and successors, according to law. Until death!"</p>
