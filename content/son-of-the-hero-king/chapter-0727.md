<p>Most of those nicknames were from the era when he was at war against the goddesses and the divinity as a whole. Vilifying the enemy was a proven true tactic after all. This ultimately proved to be quite useless.</p>
<p>After all, Anubis did not need to recruit allies to fight alongside him. He simply had to kill people and then turn them to undeath and he would have their unquestionable loyalty and strength. <p>Next to him was Nephthys. His first wife and the love of his life. She was clad in a small white bikini that contrasted greatly with her chocolate-colored skin and red hair.</p>
