<p>Sol spent the rest of his day with Kiyohime, leisurely enjoying his time in the great forest. This had truly been a very relaxing vacation. So relaxing that, he had made it his go-to vacation spot. In the future, whenever the stress of everything proved too much for him and he had the leeway, he would surely visit this place once more.</p>
<p>In the meantime, Kiyohime helped him get a better understanding of the world of the divine beasts. Not all beasts were equal nor were they friendly and inviting, especially to him.</p>

<p>Sol chuckled, “I feel like everyone is praising and hyping those angels to death. I will be quite disappointed if they are not up to the expectations I have in my mind.”</p>
