<p>Sitting on a dark rock while gazing at the moonlight, Gerald could sense the tension gradually thickening in the air.</p>
<p>Ideally, he wished for the situation to remain unchanged for a longer period. While this might lead to more casualties, it would make Wratharis less prone to deploying their heavy artillery prematurely.</p>
<p>Undoubtedly, it was Wratharis who was currently racing against the clock, not Lustburg.</p>
<p>Regrettably, the other side understood this as well.</p>
<p>The leaders in Wratharis were far from foolish, and they were bound to take action to shift the disadvantageous situation.</p>
<p>And that's when it happened.</p>
<p>ROAR!</p>
<p>Gerald wasn't surprised when he heard the roar of an enraged beast.</p>
<p>"Here they come."</p>
<p>It was ironic how those who held the fortress were the ones launching such a relentless assault, but that was the bitter reality of war.</p>
<p>'She will have to fight again.'</p>
<p>It saddened him to see a gentle soul like Nuwa forced into the bloodshed of battle, but such was the harsh reality of war.</p>
<p>'Be cautious, young one.'</p>
<p>He sighed and looked up, his face bathed in crimson light.</p>
<p>It appeared they wanted to begin with a bang.</p>
<p>ROAR!</p>
<p>The skirmish began with a massive crimson fireball hurtling toward the encampment at breakneck speed, ready to consume everything in its path.</p>
<p>The human soldiers were taken aback; they couldn't believe that the opposing side would launch such a fierce attack without hesitation.</p>
<p>"Mages, prepare the barriers!"</p>
<p>Viktor sprang into action. While some damage was inevitable, they could greatly mitigate it.</p>
<p>What they didn't know was that this was no ordinary fire. Several tanukis had combined their mana to create this potent attack, each sacrificing a significant portion of their lifespan. It was a taxing technique, but it offered efficiency and power, akin to the full might of a typical Duke-ranked mage, and in some cases, even surpassing it.</p>
<p>Watching the fireball descend, Bai Hu clenched his fist. Perhaps they could survive this night.</p>
<p>At least, that was what he initially believed, but—</p>
<p>—they had overlooked one crucial detail. There was no way they could have foreseen what happened next.</p>
<p>Whoosh!</p>
<p>[Smash]</p>
<p>Out of nowhere, a figure could be seen soaring toward the iridescent fireball. Onlookers opened their eyes wide, wondering if this person was simply reckless, attempting to stop the fiery onslaught.</p>
<p>Human soldiers shouted, while those from Wratharis smirked.</p>
<p>But soon, silence enveloped the battlefield as everyone struggled to comprehend the astonishing sight before them.</p>
<p>"Ah..."</p>
<p>Bai Hu let out a humorless laugh, his eyes twitching.</p>
<p>"What a monstrous sight."</p>
<p>This sentiment was shared by all on the battlefield, regardless of their affiliation. They witnessed the maid approaching the fireball, but instead of a cataclysmic clash, something entirely unexpected occurred.</p>
<p>Rather—</p>
<p>"Is she... devouring it?"</p>
<p>His wife's voice trembled as she asked, her eyes wide.</p>
<p>"Yes... Yes, I believe she is."</p>
<p>In front of them all, the gigantic crimson fireball vanished in an instant, leaving only residual heat as proof of its existence.</p>
<p>"Do we... still need to fight?"</p>

<p>Without waiting for his wife's response, Bai Hu leaped into action. His form rapidly transformed as he ascended into the air.</p>
<p>He grew larger and more formidable, with fur enveloping his entire body, a striking mix of black and white that bestowed upon him a majestic appearance. He stood tall as a humanoid tiger, ready to fight to the death.</p>
<p>The moment he touched down, his focus zeroed in on his target—the one who could potentially end his life.</p>
<p>'She's young.'</p>
<p>Despite her youth, the young woman wore a blank expression, her cheeks slightly bloated, as though she had indulged in a hearty meal. It might have been endearing in different circumstances, but Bai Hu knew she was a paradoxical monster, much like Sun Wukong.</p>
<p>Bai Hu didn't bother with words or thoughts.</p>
<p>His mana surged through his body, following a specific path, imbuing him with energy.</p>
<p>[Zone: Late Heaven Trigram.]</p>
<p>A yin-yang circle, black and white, formed beneath his feet, encompassing both him and the young woman. Words appeared at each extremity of the Trigram, opposing each other.</p>
<p>Heaven and Earth, Wind and Thunder, Water and Fire, Mountain and Lake.</p>
<p>"Young lady, I am the Lord of the Tiger Clan. I don't know if I can defeat you, but— you'll regret not attacking me sooner."</p>
<p>[Heaven and Heart Formation.]</p>
<p>In an instant, the world shifted.</p>
<p>A brilliant light flashed, nearly blinding Nuwa. When her vision cleared, she found herself atop a mountain peak engulfed in clouds.</p>
<p>{Well, it seems you should have heeded the old man's advice.}</p>
<p>Nuwa had no words to rebut Echidna this time.</p>
<p>She was loss about what to do when a voice reached her,</p>
<p>[This is the Heaven and Earth formation. One of my proudest achievements. A power I developed especially to face monsters like that I would never be able to win against in a normal situation.]</p>
