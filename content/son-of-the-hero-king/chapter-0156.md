<p>Another two days went by.</p>
<p>Since Setsuna and Lilin were both awake, he would regularly visit them both in the morning and the evening to keep a check on them.</p>
<p>He was glad to see that they were indeed healing fast.</p>
<p>Though, for some reason, Lilin was healing way faster than she should have been able to.</p>
<p>Sol wondered if it had to do with her identity and the blood in her, but she shouldn't have awakened any ability</p>
<p>A matter worth pondering about.</p>
<p>Aside from this, he had been quite surprised when Athena joined the assembly in the place of Tyr.</p>
<p>He, of course, understood what it meant and was happy for her.</p>
<p>He had been even happier when he had learned about the thieves.</p>
<p>Sol wasn't naive enough to believe that corruption could be erased.</p>
<p>As long as he didn't fundamentally change the mind of all the nobles and workers under him, it was impossible to do so.</p>
<p>Still, he believed that there was some limit to not cross and he was happy to see that all his ancestors had the same opinion.</p>
<p>He needed examples to set things straight and make everyone follow the rules. Tacit and explicit.</p>
<p>As such, since a bunch of fools delivered themselves to him, he happily set a decree to punish them by taking away one-fourth of their fortune.</p>
<p>He also condemned them to use another fourth of their fortune to invest in the development of structures to take care of orphans, old people, and wounded soldiers.</p>
<p>After all, since they stole from the family of soldiers, they should give back tenfold what they stole.</p>
<p>He also permitted the black knight to capture the nobles who refused to comply.</p>
<p>Of course, he couldn't use such a heavy hand every time. But as he was now he could easily use his age and inexperience as an excuse.</p>
<p>Then the other Duke would come and pacify the nobles. Good cops/bad cops.</p>
<p>Once the decision about the punishment of the thieving nobles was passed, they began to delve into more pressing matters such as the war.</p>
<p>With winter approaching, they were pretty sure that Wratharis wouldn't attack them.</p>
<p>Winter in this world was extremely brutal, fighting a war during such times was crazy, since the casualties would be three or four times higher than in normal times.</p>
<p>This was exactly why,</p>
<p>"The chances that he attacks us during winter are extremely high ."</p>
<p>Camelia announced suddenly.</p>
<p>Everyone stopped discussing and looked at her.</p>
<p>Sol asked simply, "What gives you such confidence?"</p>
<p>Doubt could be clearly heard in his voice.</p>
<p>Camelia was shocked, the fact that Sol doubted her words left her somewhat baffled.</p>
<p>She also understood just how shallow she had been previously.</p>
<p>Sighing, she continued, "The one who informed me is the Supreme daughter of Patientia."</p>
<p>Sol tapped his finger on the table.</p>
<p>He was weighing the situation. Then asked Milia.</p>
<p>"I forgot to ask, but how is the relationship between the royal family and the church in Wratharis?"</p>
<p>Milia, who stood up behind him bowed and answered, "From our report, since the crowning of the new king, the relationship between those two had been extremely strained."</p>
<p>Sol nodded. Of course, he couldn't simply believe the information given by the crown's shadow either.</p>
<p>So he turned once again toward Camelia.</p>
<p>"What does that supreme daughter want?"</p>
<p>It was impossible to have given such information for free.</p>
<p>If Lustburg won this war, it would be able to claim a large amount of lands and could seriously threaten the safety of Wratharis.</p>
<p>"The death of the king."</p>
<p>A heavy silence fell in the room.</p>
<p>Everyone was surprised by this news and couldn't help but ponder about it.</p>
<p>Sol was shocked as well, but he had access to information that helped him connect the dot.</p>
<p>'They want Setsuna to become the queen.'</p>
<p>Only a Blessed could officially become king or queen.</p>
<p>But what would happen if a ruler died without any crown prince or princess ready to replace them?</p>
<p>Such cases were rare in history, but generally, the goddess would choose the immediate and closest relative.</p>
<p>"Does the Wolf have any children or is there any Royal Blessed aside from him?"</p>
<p>"Not in our knowledge."</p>
<p>‘As I thought.’</p>
<p>The plan was pretty simple. Kill the king. Setsuna becomes blessed. Take her as a queen. Everyone is happy.</p>
<p>… As If such a thing was possible.</p>
<p>There were simply too many unknown variables in this situation.</p>
<p>What's more, this was just a speculation of his. Perhaps they had another candidate in mind.</p>
<p>He needed to ask Setsuna if she had sisters or brothers or cousins.</p>
<p>But he doubted it.</p>
<p>Like all powerful magical beings, the fertility of blue wolves was extremely weak. Getting one child was already impressive enough.</p>
<p>Some did not even manage to give birth in all their life.</p>
<p>"The discussion stops here. Lady Athena, I would like you to inform Duke Tyr and begin to prepare countermeasures for an eventual fight during winter."</p>
<p>Humans were, on average, far weaker than beastmen.</p>
<p>Even though the information was still suspicious, it was better to prepare and avoid bad surprises.</p>
<p>"Understood."</p>
<p>Sol then turned toward Hermes, "Duke Hermes, contact Aunt Theresa. I am free to meet her tonight in the tower."</p>
<p>They needed special engraved weapons for this war. Using cold steel would be dangerous.</p>
<p>What's more, Greed Dike had been pretty pissed and the law they set put them in disadvantage. Thankfully, Theresa was a member of the council and should perhaps be able to work on something.</p>
<p>"Your will shall be done."</p>
<p>After getting Hermes’ answer, Sol faced Arachne,</p>
<p>"Duchess, your talent as a seamstress will be put to test. How many people would you need to help you create enough clothes and cloaks that could protect soldiers from the cold?"</p>
<p>Arachne closed her eyes for a short moment before nodding, "I need none, my golems will be enough."</p>
<p>Sol shook his head, "This is truly good news. But I would like to use this occasion to create more employment. I give you the right to recruit between two to three hundred women or men experienced in tailoring. Of course, you can also use your golems on the side."</p>
<p>Arachne did not seem pleased, but she understood that this would be helpful for the country. So she reluctantly accepted.</p>
<p>"Now then, if you could please leave. I have some matters to discuss with the supreme daughter."</p>
<p>It wasn't that he lacked trust in them.</p>
<p>But he did not wish to share Setsuna's past without her permission.</p>
<p>What's more, he didn't know how they would react if they knew they could have control over the potential future queen of Wratharis.</p>
<p>Sharing glances furtively, the two dukes and Athena nodded and saluted before leaving, followed by Milia.</p>
<p>He was now alone once again with Camelia.</p>
<p>"So, let's discuss shall we?"</p>
