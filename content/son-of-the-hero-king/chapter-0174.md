<p>The sacred ceremony or rather the show-off ceremony was expedited rather fast.</p>
<p>The moment Sol stood in front of Camelia and placed his hand on the crystal sphere, all the people present were astonished once the numbers regarding Sol's capacity were revealed.</p>
<p>All of them had known from the beginning that Sol would have a Tier 1 capacity. This was an inevitability in their minds. Even so, 500 CP was simply too much.</p>
<p>It meant that if he worked hard enough, the kingdom of Lustburg would have an additional 5 potential kings or perhaps more.</p>
<p>Since the ceremony was open to everyone, it meant that even people of different races could enter.</p>
<p>Clearly, in this lot, some of them were spies of other countries.</p>
<p>But this did not matter. From the very start, Sol had never planned on hiding his talents. Doing so was meaningless.</p>
<p>The only reason he did not even reveal to the world that he was a dimensional mage was that it was a trump card that could save his life—which it did.</p>
<p>If Drei had known from the very start that he could teleport in his own dimensions, that night might have gone very differently.</p>
<p>Once he finished showing off and accepted the exclamations of awe and surprise, it was finally time for the most important part of the ceremony to happen.</p>
<p>The opening of the dimensional wall.</p>
<p>A ceremony that could only happen once a year and one that only nobles, church members, or royalty could participate in.</p>
<p>Even though it was unjust, the world was never just in the first place.</p>
<p>If the crown prince was normally the last to be tested, then he was always the first to enter the door.</p>
<p>In the past, the door would send anyone who entered to random coordinates and they could only stay there for the equivalent of one day in the mortal world.</p>
<p>The prince was the sole exception as in, even though he would be sent in a random direction, he could stay as long as he wished before leaving.</p>
<p>This was one of the reasons as to why the crown princes or princesses would always grow much stronger after coming back from the Astral dimension.</p>
<p>After all, they could stay years and search for the best possible partners while training before finally coming back.</p>
<p>With the difference in time axis between the two dimensions, it wouldn't be weird for only a few short weeks to have gone past in the mortal world when they came back.</p>
<p class="scene-break">· · ·</p>
<p>Since the ceremony was extremely important and could only happen once a year, all the unconcerned people needed to be chased out of the cathedral.</p>
<p>The commoners who had shown enough talent today would have to wait for the next year before being allowed to enter.</p>
<p>During that year, they would receive adequate training and would have their loyalty tested.</p>
<p>The training was necessary to optimize their chances to obtain a good partner befitting of their Capacity points.</p>
<p>After all, a contract could never be formed without the consent of the two parties. Since the first contract was for life, high-ranked magical beings would never accept to form a contract with someone who had no future prospects.</p>
<p>Who would sign a contract where they had everything to lose and nothing to gain?</p>
<p>At the same time, it was necessary to warn them about the potential risks.</p>
<p>If they were lucky enough to enter Asmodeus or Gabriel's territories then the chances of them dying were pretty small.</p>
<p>Anywhere else and all bets were off.</p>
<p>Because of this, entering the Astral dimension was something that needed both courage and strength.</p>
<p>Of course, the prince was basically assured protection. If anything happened to a Blessed in their territory, the divine beasts would have a hard time explaining themselves.</p>
<p>Even so, there was always that slight possibility of meeting rebel spirits who were not under the control of the divine beasts or their descendants.</p>
<p>Those natural spirits might not even know what a Blessed looked like or might simply not care.</p>
<p>Of course, until now no crown prince or prince happened to get killed. But it did happen to other royalty.</p>
<p>The worst outcome would be to fall into the part of the Astral world that wasn't controlled by the divine beasts–The abyss.</p>
<p>That place was outside of all laws and all the residents there were beings of horrors.</p>
<p>But Sol was sure that he had nothing to worry about.</p>
<p class="scene-break">· · ·</p>
<p>Currently, Sol was standing behind Camelia as he looked at her kneel and pray.</p>
<p>Since the portal he would be using would be different from the one the others would use. Camelia had decided to open it in another place unreachable by most people.</p>
<p>After all, the one he would use had direct coordinates toward Gabriel's territory.</p>
<p>Once he went through the portal, it would close automatically.</p>
<p>He could only come back by opening a portal from that side.</p>
<p>To assure his security, they had decided to open the portal in the deepest and most guarded part of the church.</p>
<p>This was also the room Camelia had previously used during the Saint Fall and when she activated the Holy territory.</p>
<p>While waiting for the portal to open, Sol was going through the steps he would have to take.</p>
<p>Basically he needed to enter the Phoenix realm and find a Phoenix that suited him.</p>
<p>Phoenixes were elemental creatures of the highest order. The energy part that constituted their bodies was entirely made out of one specific element that varied for each Phoenix.</p>
<p>Fire phoenixes were the most numerous, but there was basically phoenix for every element.</p>
<p>Once Sol found the phoenix that pleased him, he would have to convince him/her or it to form a contract with.</p>
<p>Then, they would have to travel to the dragon's territory and succeed in the trial before forming the contract.</p>
<p>This would without a doubt be a lengthy process, but once this was done, Sol was sure that his power would skyrocket.</p>
<p>Aside from this, Sol also planned to form a contract with another S rank magical being if it was possible.</p>
<p>Then he would come back home and do the same for Setsuna, Nuwa, and if it was possible, Milia.</p>
<p>The contract was a symbiotic relationship that gave skills to the humans and boosted the growth of the magical beings.</p>
<p>Milia was already a rather powerful Duke. If he could make her even stronger with the contract, then it wouldn't be impossible to reach the level of King in the future.</p>
<p>Honestly, even if not for the trial, Sol would not have formed a contract immediately with the Phoenix. After all, despite its name, the lust-type contract needed love as a fuel.</p>
<p>Sol did not believe in love at first sight. After all, how could you fall in love with someone you knew nothing about?</p>
<p>His harem was also barely balanced, so adding someone with a bad personality would just be a chore and might cause many problems later down the line.</p>
<p>"I am finished."</p>
<p>Standing up, Camelia stretched a little, emphasizing her abundant chest.</p>
<p>While doing so, she was cursing inwardly about how those goddesses liked too much theatrics.</p>
<p>"You seem tired."</p>
<p>Camelia sighed, "Tell me, Sol, do you find it hard to enter your dimension?"</p>
<p>"Huh? Not at all."</p>
<p>"And is it complicated to bring someone in?"</p>
<p>"Neither… Hahaha. I see where you are going. The goddesses do not need prayer."</p>
<p>"Indeed. The goddesses neither need direct worship nor prayer, they represent a concept. For example, as long as there will be people feeling lust, it would be the same as worshipping Luxuria.</p>
<p>In the same way, all realms belong to the goddesses. As such, opening a portal between the Astral and the mortal realm is just a matter of waving their hands."</p>
<p>Sol nodded as he mulled what she was saying,</p>
<p>"Do you mean to say that as long as Lust exists, Luxuria can never die?"</p>
<p>"Sol… Goddesses cannot die."</p>
<p>Camelia's voice held hints of warning and her eyes were begging him to stop.</p>
<p>Clearly, it was one thing to not show respect to the goddesses and it was another thing to discuss how to kill them.</p>
<p>Sol was not surprised at the notion of goddesses being invincible because of the concept they represented.</p>
<p>But this begged some questions.</p>
<p>Goddesses could not die as long as the sin or virtue they represented existed–this was a fact.</p>
<p>Goddesses were the creator of all living beings–at least that was how the myth went.</p>
<p>If so, what concept did the goddesses represent before the existence of life? How could there be Lust, Wrath or chastity without living beings?</p>
<p>People in this world believed that sins and virtues existed because of the goddesses.</p>
<p>But if so, what about his world?</p>
<p>The more Sol learned about this world, the more he understood how little he actually knew.</p>
<p>Secret within secrets.</p>
<p>Lies and truths mixed together.</p>
<p>Would he get all the answers he wished for once he became a demi-god?</p>
<p>He did not know.</p>
<p>What he did know though, was that the veil would be slightly lifted once he officially took the throne of Lustburg.</p>
<p>"Sol, everything is ready. Now, normally a door should have already opened, but since we need fixed coordinates. You need to use your blood and pour it right here."</p>
<p>She pointed at the magical circles in the ground.</p>
<p>"Stand over the circle then let your blood flow. Just a few droplets will suffice."</p>
<p>The magical circle she was pointing to was the same one she had used during the events of Saint Fall.</p>
<p>"You know, why do all those rituals require the use of blood?"</p>
<p>Camelia gave a bitter laugh, "Trust me, you aren't the only one asking this question."</p>
<p>The two of them laughed for a short while before Sol finally walked and stopped once he was in the circle.</p>
<p>The two of them looked at each other, plethora of emotions present in their gazes.</p>
<p>Finally, Sol smiled and sharpened his nails before wounding his arms.</p>
<p>The wound immediately closed, but enough blood fell on the circle.</p>
<p>Once it happened, the circle seemed to come alive as it was lit up with scarlet and golden light.</p>
<p>Looking at the magical circle activated, Camelia looked at him with a worried expression and said, "Be careful."</p>
<p>"I will."</p>
<p>And with those last words, Sol vanished completely.</p>
<p>Now alone, Camelia clasped her hands and kneeled down to pray.</p>
<p>"Please, protect him."</p>
<p>This was perhaps her most ardent and fervent prayer.</p>
<p>She did not know what kind of adventures her beloved would live.</p>
<p>She simply hoped that once it ended, he would come back in one piece and still be the man she fell in love with.</p>
