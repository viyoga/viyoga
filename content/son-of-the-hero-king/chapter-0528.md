<p>"While I understand that all land in this continent belongs to Your Highness, I believe I have the right to my own intimacy."</p>
<p>Arachne, who was working on a painting, spoke as she felt Sol enter or rather appear in her room.</p>
<p>She didn't know much about his power but she knew that at his level, he could have been here and observing her for hours.</p>
<p>"Heh. Don't you find this hilarious when it comes from you?"</p>
<p>Arachne gave a bland smile as she stopped working on her painting and turned to face Sol.</p>
<p>Even now, watching him, she continued to be surprised about how much one could change in just a few weeks or months depending on how long he spent in the Astral realm.</p>
<p>She also remembered her conversation with Pandora and how she ended up being devoured helplessly by the prince.</p>
<p>While she complained about some coercion, they both knew that no matter how powerful Sol was, it was impossible for him to truly coerce a King level.</p>
<p>As for his accusations,</p>
<p>"My skill is one of the security systems of Lustburg and it proved to be more than useful during the civil war."</p>
<p>Arachne was Duke class warrior who could share her sight with her creations.</p>
<p>Thanks to how renowned she was and her skills, basically all nobles in the Capital bought her pieces and this meant she could monitor absolutely any nobles she wished to.</p>
<p>The only limit was her own mental capacity as projecting her mind simultaneously to many art pieces was impossible.</p>
<p>"Indeed it's thanks to you that we learned of Gerald Trahison."</p>
<p>Arachne observed Sol's expression with scrutiny before nodding to herself.</p>
<p>"It seems like you managed to walk out of the shadows of betrayal?"</p>
<p>"Did you expect me to crumble and cry at the mention of how Gerald betrayed me?"</p>
<p>"You nearly did the first time. Though while you did not cry, you certainly crumbled a bit."</p>
<p>"Heh. That I cannot deny."</p>
<p>Learning that Gerald was a traitor had been painful and something Sol had tried to deny.</p>
<p>It was only his feeling of caution that made him decide to continue doubting and while Gerald became a triple agent at the end, this did not change the reality of betrayal.</p>
<p>Even so, "I grew up."</p>
<p>Pain was part of the process. Be it physical or emotional.</p>
<p>The pain he received from the betrayal did not erase the happiness he received from the years they spent together.</p>
<p>He did not let one pitfall stop him from advancing and simply shrugged the pain away.</p>
<p>Sol managed to separate those two and continue his life happily.</p>
<p>He did not let one pitfall stop him from advancing and simply shrugged the pain away.</p>
<p>"It's indeed admirable."</p>
<p>Arachne clapped and while her tone was quite antagonistic, she was not lying about her feeling.</p>
<p>Anyone who could stand up stronger after being backstabbed by someone they trusted was worthy of respect.</p>
<p>It wasn't all.</p>
<p>Throughout the many small meetings they had, Arachne had been able to glean more about the man named Sol Luxuria, and where she had once only seen a spoiled prince who grew up in a golden cage, she was now able to see a respectable king who would lead his Kingdom to new heights.</p>
<p>"Well, as interesting as it is to discuss with you, I would like to know why the prince decided to grace my modest house with your presence."</p>
<p>Now that the time of niceties ended, Arachne wanted to know what brought Sol here.</p>
<p>The two of them were in no way close enough for there to be such a visit.</p>
<p>Politically, the fact that out of the three Dukes she was the most estranged from the king should have been very dangerous.</p>
<p>But she didn't care. Either way, she was just waiting for her niece to grow old enough and strong enough to take the title.</p>
<p>It would be their problem then.</p>
<p>At the very least, she knew that Sol would not harm her family and she simply had to do her duty.</p>
<p>This situation allowed her to rest easily and simply focus on her passion.</p>
<p>Art and creating new masterpieces.</p>
<p>"I came up with a proposal which I believe will make you very happy."</p>
<p>Arachne rose an eyebrow at this. The confidence in his voice was making her curious.</p>
<p>Not that she had ever seen him talk without confidence ever since he came back from the Astral realm.</p>
<p>"I am a very picky woman."</p>
<p>"Trust me."</p>
<p>"Is that so." She muttered and nodded, "Very well, what do you need my help with?"</p>
<p>"I need your talent to bring help in creating a world-changing era."</p>
<p>Since Theresa had brought something as nonsensical as Mecha into existence, Sol decided to start going all out.</p>
<p>With the help of Kali and her knowledge, he wanted to bring forth all sorts of technology from his world.</p>
<p>'Didn't they all say that angels are super advanced?'</p>
<p>Sol didn't know how powerful Angels were exactly but he did know that a big difference between two armies would lead to a massacre.</p>
<p>'It's time to continue the tradition I guess.'</p>
<p>Each of his ancestors brought big or small changes to this world through their knowledge from another world.</p>
<p>Some changes may be small, but they were there and were one of the reasons why Lustburg looked to be such a mismatch between middle age and modern age as well as a mix between numerous traditions.</p>
<p>But what about Sol?</p>
<p>Until now he had simply been content to let things as is.</p>
<p>There was no need to create a gun when people could move at the speed of lightning or Sonic speed at minimum, right?</p>
<p>Wrong!</p>
<p>He had thought about it wrong.</p>
<p>No matter how powerful a Duke was, they could not survive a strike from a nuclear explosion.</p>
<p>A bullet may not kill a Duke but it sure as hell could kill many humans.</p>
<p>Then what if all the weapons from Earth were enhanced with runes, magic, and alchemy?</p>
<p>How powerful would a nuclear bomb buff with Mana and so on explode?</p>
<p>He didn't know.</p>
<p>The unknown was scary.</p>
<p>But — it was also worth exploring.</p>
