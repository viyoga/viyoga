<p>The fight in the eternal Battlefield lasted only an instant but it would forever live in the mind of those who witnessed this incredible feat.</p>
<p>Even now, records of what happened were being sent everywhere. After all, the fight between two countries was always of interest for the rest of the world. Even more so when it came to the birth of a new legend.</p>

<p>Like a demon, Lilin made blood flow everywhere, giving the impression that rivers of blood were flowing.</p>

<p>“Jialong and Camel lost.”</p>

<p>“Oh? The geezer lost? How many fought against them? Did a King move?”</p>

<p>“There was only one person.”</p>

<p>“Seriously?”</p>

<p>Perhaps a Duke who already knew their true name and was simply waiting for ascension or a better name. Such a thing was not rare, in fact, the two strongest of their group, the Bull King and the Lion King were in the same situation. They could have jumped to the level of King long ago but they knew that if they did, this would be all. Their chance of becoming a Demigod would be extremely low.</p>
<p>This was not what they wanted and there was no way they would settle for something average.</p>
<p>“Is there still a Duke who is strong enough to win against us in the mortal realm outside of the witches?”</p>
<p>“Who is the one who beat them?”</p>
<p>This was not arrogance on their part. They had truly wreaked havoc all over this world from high to low and even middle. They fought against both devils and angels, and even the elves were not ignored.</p>
<p>Sometimes they won, sometimes they lost but in the end, they always won more than they lost until no one dared to even challenge them.</p>
<p>They were the uncontested strongest in Wratharis and could have all had their clans if they wished.</p>
<p>“Hum… The one who took them down. Well, it’s slightly ironic when you think of this but it’s Lilin Luxuria, daughter of Lilith Luxuria. They call her — The Sword Demon.”</p>
<p>They understood why they mentioned the irony and they all looked at Sun Wukong who was quietly sipping his monkey wine.</p>
<p>The reason both Camel and Jialong went to the war was to make Lilith move faster and appear on the battlefield. But it seemed like the job had been done in a way they did not expect.</p>
<p>“Should we move? Our reputation will be severely affected after this. Will it affect you negatively?”</p>
<p>Sun Wukong who had been mostly silent up to now shook his head and smiled,</p>
<p>“We have never cared about reputation until now. Even if I do need Faith now there is no need to worry about the so-called reputation. This is nothing but an illusory cloud.”</p>
<p>He stood up and continued,” As for Jialong and Camel, don’t worry about them. They will not die. You should all know what is the reality of this war.”</p>
<p>Lion and Bull grimaced, they of course knew that this whole war was a sham. Rather than a sham, it was mostly just a play with many actors and Wratharis King playing the role of the clown.</p>

I can feel that at this rate, the world will change, and not necessarily for the better.
