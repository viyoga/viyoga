<p>This was quite a surprise for Wukong. His body was impervious to water, fire, and metal, his defense was perhaps one of the highest in the world.</p>
<p>“Your sword intent…”</p>

<p>Always acting carefully, always paying attention to the slightest details, always cursing her limitations.</p>

<p>Now though…Now things are different.</p>

<p>Even less so when it came to speed, strength and resilience. His own body was equal to dragon kings after all.</p>
<p>There is no doubt no. There is no way she is a human.</p>
<p>But if not a human then what? He did hear rumors about Lilith being partially succubus but even such a thing should not have been enough. Demons of now in the mortal realm were nothing but fallen Divine Beasts after all.</p>
<p>Well, it doesn’t matter.</p>
<p>Wukong stopped flying and gazed at Lilith in the distance. His clones were dwindling and the number he could create was limited. If things continued like this, he might really lose after wasting all his energy.</p>

<p>“I believe it’s time to stop running away.”</p>

<p>For Wukong, his heart was bursting with happiness because he was finally able to have the kind of fight he so dearly wished for, while Lilith was delighted by her ability to use all the techniques she had long stopped using.</p>
<p>Even as they fought, they kept growing in power and technique. Proving that they were indeed singularities. Beings who could ignore Fate to a certain extent or use it for their benefits.</p>
