<p>“I mean. Technically, it’s your food. So I am just giving back to the original owner.”</p>

<p>Still, good food would always be good food and he could wager there was no being in existence who did not like eating tasty food.</p>
<p>“You act like you haven’t eaten anything in days.” She spoke, resting her chin on the palm of her hand as she observed Sol.</p>
