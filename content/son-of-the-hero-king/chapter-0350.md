<p>Sol had discovered early on about this fact— the law of Fate seemed to follow a system of narratives. Those suspicions of his were confirmed when he had been able to peer into Fate itself through his Divine weapon.</p>
<p>This was how he had been able to twist the threads of Fate around him and Nihil to establish a new fate under the Rule of Three.</p>
<p>&lt;&lt;Deus Ex Machina&gt;&gt; could make the impossible possible at its peak form, but Sol had only been able to make an event with ‘an extremely low possibility’ become a ‘certainty’ through his ability.</p>
<p>This was perhaps because his Zone was not assisted by any Kingly name or because the power of a demigod was still lacking in some aspects to reach the Ultimate form of his Zone.</p>
<p>Either way, Sol was sure that the moment he set foot in Southern Pride, he would be pulled into a new narrative.</p>
<p>In fact, he might have already been pulled in one already.</p>
<p>How it ended would depend on him.</p>
<p>When his thought reached this point, Sol couldn’t help but think of the green-haired witch, Persephone, the Witch of Life.</p>
<p>For that woman, life was nothing but a never ending story.</p>
<p>It seemed that she had been right all along. Her view of life had been the closest to the truth of Fate and the core secrets of this world.</p>
<p>‘Well, this will be fun.’</p>
<p>After he was done dealing with the pressing matters of Lustburg, that needed his attention, he would visit a country full of half-naked beautiful elves who would worship him.</p>
<p>This might be pretty fun, all things considered.</p>
<p>So what if he had to deal with a King-ranked criminal who might attack him as well as a King ranked elf queen that could have transcended if she didn’t fight his lovers?</p>
<p>Worst case scenario he would just flee.</p>
<p>No… He wouldn’t flee. He would just make a tactical retreat.</p>
<p>Those were two very different concepts.</p>
<p>This was the greatest advantage of being a Dimensional Mage and Sol had no problem fleei…retreating in the face of danger.</p>
<p>“Well, this will definitely be fun.”</p>
<p>Tiamat laughed, glee covering her majestic face, “In fact, I hope those bastards appear. We currently cannot find them but if they dare to appear in Southern Pride, I can have Kiyohime or Fafnir descend and help you just in case.”</p>
<p>Since the dawn of the new era, the rules binding divine beasts to their realms were pretty strict.</p>
<p>Divine Beasts at the demigod level could not normally descend into the mortal realm. If they did, they had to have their full strength sealed through a rather painful process.</p>
<p>Descending in such a situation was basically begging to get assassinated. The only demigods she knew who dared to descend despite this rule were Asmodeus and that Nine-Tailed Fox under the virtue of Patience, Tamamo no Mae.[1]</p>
<p>It was crazy how those two crazy bastards did this in order to have children.</p>
<p>Asmodeus at least had a relationship with Echidna. Another demigod.</p>
<p>But that crazy fox went and fell in love with a pure mortal. Giving birth to the one who would later become the Supreme Daughter of Patientia in Wratharis — Inari Kiku Patientia.</p>
<p>For Divine Beasts below the demigod level, they had permission to descend but could not leave the confines of their territory in the mortal realm.</p>
<p>Dragons could only move in Southern Pride.</p>
<p>Phoenixes could only move inside Lustburg.</p>
<p>So on and so forth…</p>
<p>This was the rule.</p>
<p>To change this rule, it was necessary to ask the permission of the Beast of Sin ruling the area they wanted to move in.</p>
<p>If divine beasts below the demigod level wanted to enter Southern Pride, they needed the permission of Tiamat.</p>
<p>If they wanted to enter Lustburg, they needed the permission of Asmodeus. [3]</p>
<p>The only exception to this rule were third generation divine beasts like Sol who could move as they wished.</p>
<p>Tiamat's hand tightened on her armchair at the mere thought of Asmodeus.</p>
<p>While she was asleep, Kiyohime and Fafnir had wanted to descend and take Sol to Southern Pride. So that he could be raised by dragons.</p>
<p>But Asmodeus had refused them passage.</p>
<p>In hindsight, this was the best decision possible. She didn’t know how Sol would have turned if he had been raised by dragons from the start in a country that would basically worship him.</p>
<p>There was also the fact that Asmodeus was most likely just following the orders of Luxuria.</p>
<p>Still, this didn’t abate her wish to at least punch him once in the face.</p>
<p>“Also, you need to understand that outside of those under a contract like Isis, divine beasts from the Astral realm can never participate in a war in the mortal world.”</p>
<p>This was an absolute rule with no exception and in fact, was a rule that applied to all beings from the Astral realm.</p>
<p>This was pretty normal.</p>
<p>After all, in the mortal realm, reaching the Duke ranks was an exploit while King ranks were basically unheard of outside of the Blessed.</p>
<p>If citizens from the Astral realm could participate in the war below, then things would get complicated very fast.</p>
<p>“I understand. Either way, I did not plan to ask for help. Even Isis cannot participate in the war. Or if she does, she can’t do so as a Necromancer.”</p>
<p>Despite how backward the mortal world seemed, there were many conventions put in place to avoid needless massacre.</p>
<p>Like how King rank could not directly participate in the war or attack ground soldiers or how Necromancers were not acceptable on the battlefield.</p>
<p>Sol found those conventions to be bullshit but he also understood the necessity. Without those rules, what stopped a King rank like Lilith from entering the city and massacring people?</p>
<p>Without those rules what would stop Angels or Dwarves to use their weapons of large-scale destruction?</p>
<p>There were also the Demons whose country was filled with necromancers just waiting for someone to break the rule first.</p>
<p>Sol did not wish for Lustburg to become the enemy of all the other countries.</p>
<p>He did not yet have the power to face them all.</p>
<p>Of course, once he did…</p>
<p>Well, that would be another matter.</p>
<p>After all, conquering the world seemed like a pain.</p>
<p>Simply imagining the amount of paperwork that would fall on his lap made him shiver in fright.</p>
<p>Standing behind Sol, Nefertiti hated how she felt slightly happy at the thought that Isis would not be able to be much of an assistance in the war.</p>
<p>She realized once again how ugly jealousy was. And how ugly her inner self had become.</p>
<p>“Well, I guess I said everything I needed to. Come visit me alone at a later date. Now though, I think you have your own matters to attend to.”</p>
<p>Nefertiti took a sharp breath. For an instant, she had felt Tiamat's gaze on her. She couldn’t help but feel her heart constrict at the thought of what would happen next with Sol.</p>
<p>“Very well. This was indeed a delightful and insightful discussion.”</p>
<p>Sol gave a bitter smile as he felt the heartbeat of Nefertiti accelerate. He had been keeping track of her condition all this while and he realized that he really needed to act.</p>
<p>“Let’s go.”</p>
<p>Isis and Nefertiti both bowed to Tiamat once again before following Sol out.</p>
<p>But, just as he reached the door, he felt Isis pulling at his shirt with a frantic expression.</p>
<p>‘What is…Ah.’</p>
<p>“Sorry. Before I go. You must already know but I think Nephthys won't be able to come to greet you soon.”</p>
<p>Tiamat waved her hand dismissively with a smirk,</p>
<p>“I honestly do not care. The price Gabriel will pay for their entry is more than enough to satisfy me and not care for such courtesy.”</p>
<p>“Huh? What did she have to pay?”</p>
<p>“Well…Let’s say it’s a little secret for now. Though perhaps I might inform you of it later. Now go. I wish to rest.”</p>
<p>With a wave of her hand, Sol, Isis, and Nefertiti found themselves back on 8th Heaven, on Kiyohime’s island. More precisely, in Sol’s room.</p>
<p>Sol was incredibly puzzled about what kind of secret this transaction might be. But now he had more pressing matters to attend to.</p>
<p>“Nefertiti, I think we need to talk.”</p>
<p>Nefertiti never heard more frightening words in her life.</p>
