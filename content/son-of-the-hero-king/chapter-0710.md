<p>As the full moon hung in the sky and a slight chill spread in the forest, Sol took shelter on the roots of a large tree. He sat on the roots, taking in his surroundings.</p>
<p>Surrounding him, he observed the peacefully standing animals, grooming themselves.</p>
<p>It was quite a strange sight. Nocturnal animals, daytime ones, or predators and prey stood together without conflicts. Such a sight could also be denoted as nothing short of a miracle.</p>
<p>An amused smile lingered on Sol’s face. He wondered if he should start singing now to complete this fairy-tale-esque scene. Sadly, he was no princess.</p>
<p>The animals were simply attracted to him because of his power and the way he had been treating them during his stay.</p>
<p>Since Sol caused the death of so many animals while fighting Kiyohime, he had been feeling relatively guilty.</p>
<p>There was no way to bring back the ones he had already killed but surely he could do something for the survivors, right? At least that’s how Sol reasoned to lessen some of his guilt.</p>
<p>And just like that, he had now become some sort of guardian deity of this place. Spreading his aura and helping them grow. He was sure that in a few years or decades, some of them might even awaken spirituality and evolve from simple animals to magical beasts.</p>
<p>“I have to say, I am feeling quite impatient to see the results of my work on those animals. What about you?” He spoke loudly but there was no answer. Even after waiting for some time, there was only silence.</p>
<p>Sighing, he swept his hair behind, and continued,</p>
<p>“I do not like bragging but my time is quite precious if you must know? This is the last time I will be calling you. After this, I will stand and be on my way.”</p>
<p>His voice was gruff but he wanted to be honest and transparent here. Truthfully, he was also feeling quite a bit irritated at the moment.</p>
<p>After all, “Since when did the queen of the elves become so cowardly? At the very least shouldn’t your partner try to do something?” He sounded exasperated. Thanks to his power, he could sense and observe everything transpiring in his domain virtually. He had to admit it, it was starting to become painfully embarrassing at this rate.</p>
<p>Silence persisted for a few moments but soon it was replaced by a flurry of words and sounds overlapping with no end until finally a pale silhouette was pushed from behind a tree by a tanned hand.</p>
<p>Now slightly hesitant and fidgeting was none other than the Queen, Satella Superbia. Of course, as Sol mentioned, she was not the only one present in the region. Her partner, the owner of that tanned hand, had not shown herself yet.</p>
<p>“What about you, Supreme Daughter? Do you plan to stay hidden behind the tree for the rest of the night?”</p>

<p>Even without this, the animals were already ready to bolt at just her appearance. After all, she was a dangerous woman despite her lack of confidence and in this forest, the greatest predators were none other than the elves themselves.</p>
