<p>‘Sigh… Forget it. Today and for the next week, I am just going to have fun and not think about anything troublesome. Nothing more, nothing less.’</p>
<p>He would finally be able to realize one of his dreams that he had ever since his time in the original world he came from. Becoming an adventurer might be a really childish dream at this time, but it was still a dream of his and he wished to realize it with his lovers.</p>
<p>‘Well. I should talk to the witches first;’</p>

<p>“Hathor. How have you been?”</p>
<p>The Water Phoenix, Hathor. Considered to be the greatest healer in the Astral Realm.</p>
<p>What was supposed to only be insurance turned out to be one of the centerpieces of his plan to save Lilith…</p>
<p>Hathor was reading a chart when he came inside the ward. It was weird to see the usually drunk and slovenly woman wearing a lab coat while looking so serious.</p>
<p>“Hmm?”</p>
<p>Unlike Aurora, it was practically impossible for Hathor to feel his presence before he actually appeared before her.</p>
<p>But still, she showed no surprise at his sudden manifestation inside the medical ward. With her experience and what she had witnessed lately, she believed that there were very few things that could actually surprise her nowadays.</p>
<p>“What brought you here? No Kingly duties today?”</p>
<p>Hathor smiled and licked her lips when she sniffed a particular fragrance she liked emanating from Sol. She looked down at the bottle Sol was holding and her smile changed into a grin,</p>
<p>“At least you have some manners. What is this?”</p>
<p>Sol shrugged, “I am not exactly an expert in wine or alcohol but some of my ancestors were. I just went through the wine cellar and took whatever seemed the oldest.”</p>
<p>“Ohhh…”</p>
<p>Hathor was not even listening to him anymore as she gazed at the bottle like a snake hypnotized.</p>
<p>“Take it. Anyways it’s a meager gift for what you did. I just wanted to say thanks.”</p>
<p>“Heh.”</p>
<p>Hathor snapped out of her stupor and snatched the bottle from his hand. She walked up to one of the drawers and took out two exquisite crystalline glasses from inside.</p>
<p>“Persephone gave these two to me. It belonged to one of the human kings before the creation of Lustburg.”</p>
<p>Sol was intrigued by this information. He remembered that all Four witches were actually older than the creation of Lustburg. It was one fact that was easy to forget with how young they looked.</p>
<p>‘Now that I think about it, I only know about Medea’s past.’</p>

<p>Hathor caressed the bottle and seemed to hold some kind of ritual with the way she opened it and filled the two glasses.</p>

<p>“To a successful cooperation. To a new friendship.”</p>
<p>“To a new friendship!”</p>
<p>Hathor laughed before slowly savoring the wine. She let out a low moan when the liquid filled her mouth. Her eyes lit up and she looked once again at the bottle.</p>
<p>“Impressive. This wine was made with an interesting technique. I feel like it’s one not of this world.”</p>

<p>“So… You are really going to leave.”</p>
<p>“Indeed. Unlike Isis, who is contracted to you, for a King-rank divine beast like me, staying in the mortal world is not really a good thing.”</p>
<p>The restrictions this world imposed on Hathor made her feel like she was constantly being shackled. It wasn’t a good feeling in any way.</p>
