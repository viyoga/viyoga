<p>[GORFARD MANSION]</p>

<p>“Sir Leonard! Show more respect to his highness!”</p>

<p>“Haha! I was wondering what gives you the guts to speak to me like this, but it seems like you are just someone else's dog.”</p>
<p>Ares’s face immediately flushed in humiliation.</p>
<p>*Sigh*</p>
<p>This sigh made Leonard look away from Ares but just as he turned toward Sol, he was startled to see the young prince facing him, just a few meters away from him.</p>
<p>His eyes constricted at this display of speed, “You…” “Silence”, his mouth immediately closed at those cold words.</p>
<p>‘Was he always this tall?’</p>
<p>Standing in front of Sol, his breath became hurried and his face pale, it felt like each second suddenly began to crawl and as if Sol was becoming taller in front of his eyes.</p>
<p>But more than anything, what made Leonard’s legs tremble the most was the cold indifference he could see in Sol's eyes – as if he was nothing more than a pitiful worm.</p>
<p>This realization made him so ashamed that his fear vanished and was replaced by fury. He was about to arouse his energy when,</p>
<p>*Slap!*</p>
<p>‘Huh?’</p>
<p>Leonard's mind blanked out for a short while before the burning pain on his cheek registered in his mind.</p>
<p>“You!”</p>
<p>“I said. Shut. Up.”</p>
<p>*Slap!*</p>
<p>This was followed by another slap on the other cheek.</p>
<p>This was even heavier than the previous. So much that his brain felt a little dizzy.</p>
<p>Touching his bruised cheek, Leonard's eyes burned fiercely, but he did not dare to utter a word.</p>
<p>If the first slap could be said to be because of his carelessness, the second one perfectly showed that he wasn’t even able to react to Sol's simple move.</p>
<p>Looking at Leonard cowering in silence, Sol gave a small smile as he looked at him, “See? It wasn’t so hard right? Who is the good dog now? Now, you are going to properly apologize, like the good little dog you are...Understood?”</p>
<p>Those words infuriated Leonard so much that he screamed, “Bastard! Who do you think you are!?”</p>
<p>*Slap!*</p>
<p>His bravado was rewarded by another heavy slap.</p>
<p>“I am the prince and, more than anything, I am stronger than you. Now, forget about apologies, such things are unnecessary. Out of my sight. I do not wish to see your face before the start of the banquet.”</p>
<p>Saying so, he turned around and completely disregarded Leonard.</p>
<p>Facing this back, Leonard's face fluctuated between red and white before he finally lowered his head in shame and left the room.</p>
<p>Sol, once he sat back, looked at the stunned expression of Ares but did not care. Still, he inwardly, he sighed,</p>
<p>‘This wasn’t like me to act like this.’</p>
<p>Currently, Sol was in a weird state where his emotions stood at a boiling point.</p>
<p>The fact that Gerald was not only a traitor but also might have tried to kill him was simply too much to learn in one go.</p>
<p>In such a situation, Leonard's appearance served as the perfect outlet to this frustration.</p>
<p>But this wasn’t all, this world wasn’t one where humble people were respected. If he had let that guy go away without setting things straight, Leonard would have taken him for a pushover.</p>
<p>Of course, this wasn’t all. Sol might have a hard time putting a lid on his wrath and his pride, but he wasn’t totally irrational.</p>
<p>For one, he did not go too far when humiliating Leonard. A few slaps and some bad words weren’t particularly grave in the grand scheme of things, even more so since Leonard gave him the perfect excuse by not paying respect to him.</p>
<p>What’s more, the eradication of the Gorfard family was already more or less decided. So tearing all cordial relationships now wasn’t a problem.</p>
<p>‘Well, all that would be worked out later. Now though, I need to discuss with this guy here.’</p>

<p>Once in front of the door of the private room, he took a deep breath and just as he was about to knock,</p>
<p>“No need to knock. Enter.”</p>
<p>He stopped his hand just a few centimeters away from the door and gritted his teeth before finally opening it.</p>
<p>The moment he entered, he calmly looked at his father who was standing with his back facing him.</p>
<p>Even though his father couldn’t see him, he bowed in respect, “Father, I….”</p>
<p>“Spare me the useless genuflexion and tell me why you came here?”</p>
<p>His father's voice and actions were as cold as always, lowering his head to hide his cold eyes, he began to explain.</p>
<p>Of course, he didn’t tell the whole truth. By his words, it was simply the prince acting arrogantly and not respecting him, once he finished his words he waited patiently for his father words,</p>
<p>“And?”</p>
<p>“Huh?”</p>
<p>Turning around, Loki looked at his son with eyes so cold they could have frozen the body of a fire elemental.</p>
<p>“Let me reiterate my question, you dumb idiot. You went and picked a fight even though I ordered you to play well and after getting humiliated by a kid many years younger than you, not only did you not retaliate, but you came here to complain like a little kid!?”</p>
<p>Loki almost roared at the last sentence. Followed by his cold voice, the room seemed to fall in the coldest temperature while frost and snow swirled in the room.</p>
<p>Looking at his son who couldn’t even raise his head under his outburst, Loki suddenly felt so tired.</p>
<p>Covering his face with his hand, he asked, “Let’s not talk about you giving the prince the initiative in this situation. What did you expect me to do? To tear all relationships with the prince even though it isn’t time yet? To beat him? Kill him?”</p>
<p>“N-No…”</p>

<p>“Father!?”</p>
<p>“Do not contradict me! I gave you a chance to perform well and you wasted it. Now go away. Do not make me repeat myself.”</p>
<p>Seeing the cold back of his father, Leonard understood that nothing he said would change anything.</p>
<p>Standing up, he furiously left the room and closed the door with a bang.</p>
