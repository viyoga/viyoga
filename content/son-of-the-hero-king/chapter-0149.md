<p>Somewhere, in a space filled with a grey fog, a castle floated quietly.</p>
<p>The interior of the castle was rather desolate, but at the same time imposing and magnificent.</p>
<p>Directly under the towering ceiling of the white castle, a golden table appeared with fifteen high-blue chairs on either side in a symmetrical arrangement.</p>
<p>The back of each chair dazzled and shone faintly with golden light, drawing the outlines of weird constellations that differed from reality.</p>
<p>Sitting around the table were fourteen women whose features were shrouded in darkness.</p>
<p>On the table, a large chessboard was placed and a game seemed to be ongoing.</p>
<p>The chessboard in itself didn't look particularly special, but if one took a look at the pieces on it, they would understand that it was anything but ordinary.</p>
<p>On one side, 5 Kings, 7 Queens, and one pawn in full gold stood at different places.</p>
<p>Some of them were cracked, some were dim, while others shone brightly.</p>
<p>On the other side, a red queen, and ten red pawns stood.</p>
<p>No matter how one looked at it, this chessboard made no sense, but the players did not seem to care.</p>
<p>It was hard to say how long those women had been facing each other.</p>
<p>It could have been one day or it could have been thousands of years.</p>
<p>It was then that one of the gold pieces, a pawn, began to shine brightly, before slowly changing into a king piece.</p>
<p>The process still wasn't complete, but it was clear that it wouldn't take long–at least from their point of view.</p>
<p>This change seemed to bring attention to the players.</p>
<p>One of them, sitting on a chair with the word *GLUTTONY* inscribed on the back, murmured,</p>
<p>"You seem happy. So the boy is really your new trump card?"</p>
<p>The reason for her attention wasn't the promotion.</p>
<p>After all, over all those years, it has happened a lot of times.</p>
<p>What interested her though, was that this particular piece shouldn't have managed to begin its transition.</p>
<p>In the multiple branches of the possible futures, the chances of it being destroyed were overwhelmingly higher.</p>
<p>This could only mean one thing,</p>
<p>"Just how many singularities did you prepare for this one piece?"</p>
<p>Singularities, beings who were not constrained by fate and had the potential to break free of their shackles.</p>
<p>Those beings were people they could not see the future of, since they were outside of the rules.</p>
<p>Only their mother, the goddess of order and the goddess of chaos could do so.</p>
<p>Each of their pieces had one or two singularities following them, but the one currently promoting was simply overwhelming.</p>
<p>"Heh. Let's just say that I am different from you girls, I have been investing since the start and soon…"</p>
<p>She trailed there but didn't continue.</p>
<p>"Your plans are good and all but I hope he won't break the heart of my little girl."</p>
<p>Castitas complained on the side.</p>
<p>Even though she had more or less mocked Camelia a few days ago in the mortal world, this didn't mean that she did not care about her.</p>
<p>In her own way, of course.</p>
<p>On the other hand, she did not really care about the boy.</p>
<p>No matter how talented he was, at most he would reach demi-god level and at the end, once he lost his blessing like all the kings before him, he would die because of the storm of probability gathered around him.</p>
<p>All the Blessed were bestowed with incredible luck and talent. But such a thing had a price.</p>
<p>The more they used their luck, the faster they would die once they lost it.</p>
<p>This was why, even in the case of long lived species like Elves, Angels or Demons, there were no old generations of kings or queens alive.</p>
<p>The only way to escape this destiny was to jump out of the board.</p>
<p>Luxuria listened to her sisters but did not care.</p>
<p>Looking at the piece under the process of transformation, a deep feeling of elation filled her heart.</p>
<p>'Soon…'</p>
<p>She had been waiting thousands of years for this very moment.</p>
<p>Her expectations were extremely high and she would accept no failure.</p>
<p>Smiling, she trailed her eyes toward the King piece of Ira.</p>
<p>Her sisters were all so shortsighted.</p>
<p>What they needed now wasn't a strong piece–But a new player, a complete game breaker that would flip the table if necessary.</p>
<p>'Sorry sister, but I will have to use your piece to reach the best result possible.'</p>
<p>After all, for a chess piece to become a player, some sacrifices were necessary.</p>
