<p>As the news of the victory of Setsuna in the main battlefront started to spread, the world seemed to shake under the tremendous weight of the new.</p>
<p>It had to be said. But this was different from what had happened in the Eternal Battlefield.</p>
<p>Setsuna may not have beaten two sages. Nor did she actually manage to completely conquer the bastion of Wratharis.</p>
<p>But what she did was in a way even more impressive.</p>
<p>A ceasefire.</p>
<p>After beating down the one known to be the fastest sage in a record time, she managed to completely impose her authority on both Wratharis and Lustburg.</p>
<p>This ceasefire wasn’t just something as simple as conquering a bastion.</p>
<p>It was a message. A message that was so loud and clear it sounded like Thunder rumbling in a clear sky.</p>
<p>She was Setsuna.</p>
<p>She was not just some fleeing princess. But someone with the right to the throne.</p>
<p>She was not just some political puppet. But someone who could inspire respect with her power, talents, and charisma.</p>
<p>The effects of this victory were far-reaching as some individuals started to fan the flame from behind and made the news spread even faster. So fast in fact that even fishing villages in Wratharis that were in the complete opposite direction of the battlefield managed to get access to recording and very accurate news.</p>
<p>For the simple citizens, what Setsuna did or the ramifications behind her acts did not matter in the slightest.</p>
<p>All they cared about was her power. After all, for many of them, the Seven Sages were bedtime heroes and legends. Something akin to gods already.</p>
<p>Finally, the form she showed at the end. The Giant Wolf of legend. A form only a King-ranked member of the royal family should be able to use.</p>
<p>Didn’t this mean that Setsuna was stronger than the current King?</p>
<p>Also, she was so beautiful and she seemed to be far less crazy than the current King.</p>
<p>Such an impression started to become common as whereas citizens did not care much about Setsuna in the past, news about her started to become more and more common.</p>
<p>To the point that everyone knew her story.</p>
<p>Barbs signed her history. Soldiers chatted about it between them. Even small books were shared among the populace.</p>
<p>Setsuna Ira. A princess born from their beloved and benevolent king who had brough an era of peace thanks to his strength and his skills in diplomacy.</p>
<p>Sadly, she had to leave her country in tears and blood as she was chased down by none other than her own uncle!</p>
<p>No name was given. But people knew who it was obviously.</p>
<p>This kind of news were shared everywhere and obviously, soldiers and guards under the order of Lupus started cracking down those gatherings.</p>
<p>Capturing those spokes. Imprisoning them and even executing some of them.</p>
<p>The goal was to inspire fear. But all it did was to inspire rage.</p>
<p>More fuel was added to the fire as news about the King stifled even the right of free speech by punishing it with jail and death.</p>
<p>It was important to remember that Wratharis was not a Kingdom like the other. At the very least, at first glance it was a Republic. This gave citizens many rights that were not present in other kingdoms but in a situation like this, those rights became akin to a cord that was slowly tightening around Lupus's neck.</p>
<p>Soon. The names of Tyrant and Usurper became more and more commonplace.</p>
<p>Whereas only truly loyalists of the previous king called him like this, more and more common people who refused to even have their most basic rights taken away from them started complaining.</p>
<p>Protests started to happen everywhere and while said protests were met with violence….Another to remember was that — Everyone had mana in this world.</p>
<p>Even more so people whose entire bodies were made for war and battle.</p>
<p>Silent protests transformed soon into riots as public outrage began to rise and riots gave place to looting and even more destruction.</p>
<p>Even the guards who were supposed to follow the orders started slowly rejecting them. Even though they did not dare to openly defy Lupus by refusing, they would act weaker or close one eye when some prisoners managed to escape.</p>
<p>Finally, open protests became common as people shouted for Lupus to take off the crown and accept Setsuna back as the official Crown Princess.</p>
<p>All of those events took only one week.</p>
<p>In just one week. The capital of Wratharis did not even see the fire of war but it seemed more akin to a powder keg ready to explode than anything else.</p>
<p>Clang~!!!</p>
<p>“Bastard!! Incompetent fool!! Rebellious Bastard! All the lot of you! They should all be hanged!”</p>
<p>The rage of Lupus spread through the imperial castle, causing all the servants to cower in fear as they could be killed at the slightest mistake.</p>
<p>This was always how Lupus behaved. Respect earned through Fear and power.</p>
<p>This had worked well for him during the last 10 years. But what he forgot was that the only reason such tactics worked was that.</p>
<p>One. He was the strongest.</p>
<p>Two. No one could contest his right to the throne.</p>
<p>Now though…</p>
<p>The fear he always inspired the servant slowly transformed into rage as well.</p>
<p>Because they knew.</p>
<p>They knew now that they did not have to bear this fear. They knew now that there was someone.</p>
<p>The old servants who once served the King back then but had been kept out of a sadistic sense of pleasure by Lupus talked about the good old days. Many of them even knew Setsuna and had taken care of her.</p>
<p>This knowledge gave them hope. This helplessness gave them even more hope. Making a deep yearning for the princess to come back to bloom in their hearts.</p>
<p>Meanwhile, deep in the sanctuary of Wratharis,</p>
<p>Kiku looked at her best friend with a bitter smile.</p>
<p>“You… You truly are very dangerous.”</p>
<p>Shuten Doji smiled, “I will take this as a compliment.”</p>
<p>There was nothing easier in this world than manipulating public sentiment.</p>
<p>“But in this case, the one you should thank is Lupus himself. He gave me all the ammunition I needed.”</p>
<p>She laughed and looked in the distance, “Now our job is done. We already softened the terrain for him.”</p>

<p>When the news of Setsuna’s victory reached Sol, he had to admit that he was both proud and disappointed in a certain way.</p>
<p>His pride came from the fact that Setsuna had indeed proved her might and talent, shocking the world. Furthermore, her actions, as well as those of Lilin for that matter, managed to give back much honor to Lustburg as a whole.</p>
<p>Many people had been looking at his kingdom as some kind of dying powerhouse because they had not been able to produce truly splendid talents in a decade. This situation was now a thing of the past.</p>
<p>The White Knight, The Holy Daughter, the Sword Demon, and Setsuna.</p>
<p>They all managed to shine on the battlefield and keep the points they could not afford to lose from being breached.</p>
<p>His disappointment came from the fact that neither Sun Wukong nor Lupus took the battlefield.</p>
<p>If one of them did, then things would have completely changed. The absolute advantage would have gone to Lustburg instantly as he would have been able to send all his true force to wipe out everything.</p>

<p>He even planned to add the prize of being able to fight Lilith, since the Monkey King seemed to be so obsessed with it.</p>
<p>Sadly, it was refused. Sun Wukong refused to participate in any scheme and he clearly said that if he did act, he would go for the kill.</p>
<p>As much as Sol believed in the four directions or Isis’s king-rank undead, Sol could not bet on the safety of Setsuna or Lilith when facing a singularity like Sun Wukong.</p>
<p>‘Well. It doesn’t matter.’</p>
<p>The war had already shifted in his advantage even if it didn’t seem so. The people currently dying in the battlefield were criminals, and soldiers under the control of nobles who held rebellious thoughts or seemed to act as if they were some uncrowned king in their land far from Lustburg.</p>
<p>By the time the war ended, most of them would be completely eradicated and replaced by actual meritorious soldiers or witches.</p>
