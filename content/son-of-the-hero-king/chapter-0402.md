<p>Sitting on a chair not far from them, Sol crossed his legs and leaned back as he quietly observed them while wondering how he should use the new information he was receiving.</p>
<p>He was slowly starting to understand why all those who had their own realm suffered from voyeurism. There was just something empowering about being able to observe and listen to all the secrets of someone without that person being able to do anything.</p>
<p>‘When I become a demigod I should try covering Lustburg entirely.’</p>
<p>It would be impossible to do so in theory. Even though Tiamat had covered the entire Dragon Territory with her own Dimension, this was because the territory belonged to her in the first place.</p>
<p>The mortal dimension belonged to no one and even a demigod like Nihil could only cover the caver for a short time.</p>
<p>But after his face-off with Lucifer…Sol had discovered that his power was quite useful and unique.</p>
<p>If all the conditions were present, it wouldn’t be impossible for him to repeat the feat of Anubis—Stealing a part of an entire realm or perhaps, even more.</p>
<p>Of course, all of this was for the future.</p>
<p>Right now he had to deal with more mundane problems. Such as saving the life of his aunt who wanted to die, getting an alliance with a group of witches who hated his Kingdom, and ascertaining how useful the woman named Pandora was.</p>
<p>&lt;&lt;Divine Weapon: Eye of Akasha&gt;&gt;</p>
<p>When he finally opened the Eye of Akasha, from his perspective, everything stopped in its tracks.</p>
<p>The world itself became completely still as if time itself became a meaningless notion. All that remained was a world filled with threads connected to each and every thing in existence. It felt as if a great puppeteer was moving strings to control reality.</p>
<p>The world…was weaved by threads of different colors. This was at least how he perceived and identified the strings of fate that he was granted the opportunity of witnessing thanks to the amalgam of all his powers.</p>
<p>‘How blurry.’</p>
<p>Sol once again regretted his own weakness. Back then when he had the power of a demigod, everything felt so vivid. He had felt all-powerful. One whim of hise could change the destiny of an entire world if he so wished.</p>
<p>But now… he felt like those strings were more like shackles restraining him.</p>
<p>‘Forget it.’</p>
<p>He started by observing Milaris. The strings linked the two of them were fused with a pink thread so large and sturdy that it seemed to equal Skuld or Nefertiti. The thread representing subordination was rather small.</p>
<p>Sol immediately understood that in Arachne's mind, even the relationship between a ruler and subordinate was worthless. Only her love for Mars made her interested in Sol. Creating a feeling of contradiction. Seeing him as a nephew but also a reminder that she would never see her love.</p>
<p>Arachne did not like him. In fact, from what he was seeing, he wondered how it was even possible for her to like anyone at all. Her love for his father was basically blinding him.</p>
<p>Shaking his head, he decided to focus on the important target.</p>
<p>Pandora.</p>
<p>Like Arachne, Pandora had a pink thread linking her to Mars. Though this was much much smaller when compared to Arachne. Her love for Mars seemed to be more in the realm of admiration and crush.</p>
<p>He also observed the thread linking her to Invidia, the goddess of envy. He briefly wondered if he could cut it. But unlike the last time when he had absolute certainty that he could do so, now he was drowned in doubt.</p>
<p>‘So, only by becoming a demigod can I cut those threads.’</p>
<p>It was a good thing to learn. He wasn’t worried about becoming a demigod. For him, it was a certainty as long as he didn’t get killed on the road.</p>
<p>‘Now what should I do?’</p>
<p>He didn’t want to start affecting the Fate of those two women as now. He wasn’t so far gone that he would start manipulating people who had done nothing wrong to him and could become potential allies.</p>
<p>Pandora was a King, so the influence he could have would be limited and even without that, she was a staunch ally of her father in the past and from what she said, the royal family was in an even worse situation than they initially thought.</p>
<p>Currently, though, he needed two things from her.</p>
<p>The first one was of course to have an open or secret alliance with Envilya. One that would make sure they would have help when facing Wratharis.</p>
<p>Currently, Nefertiti should have already landed in Southern Pride. He didn’t know how much she could help. But he would have to visit the elves anyways.</p>
<p>On the map, Wratharis was enclosed on nearly all sides by the three countries. The only escape being the sea.</p>
<p>Wratharis might have Sun Wukong. But if Lilith was healed, he was sure that she would be no weaker and Southern Pride also had Siegfried. The man was strong enough to kill a rampaging crazy dragon at the King rank, one thousand years ago.</p>
<p>‘Does Envilya have a powerhouse outside of Pandora and their Supreme daughter?’</p>
<p>He had never heard of one. But this was an avenue worth exploring.</p>
<p>The second thing he needed from Pandora was… allowing Hathor to explore her body.</p>
<p>Lilith was created by Neptune who used the gene of the Nightmare Queen. Pandora was the closest thing to the base form that was used and having her data should be more useful than the ones obtained from normal succubi.</p>
<p>But would Pandora accept? After all, in the eyes of many, the existence of Lilith was an abomination and even if Pandora had once been friend with Lilith during their adventures, they had not seen each other in nearly twenty years.</p>
<p>Nothing was eternal in this world. Parents could backstab their children and the same went for friends.</p>
<p>Furthermore, if Pandora learned that Neptune had used such a forbidden art to create Lilith, this would be a powerful tool in her hand to threaten them. If the world were to learn about this, it would allow Wratharis to have a righteous cause. Perhaps accusing them of walking in the footstep of Echidna or insulting the goddesses.</p>
<p>If the entire Lustburg royal family was deemed as a heretic, they would lose much of their influence over the populace and the political world at large.</p>
<p>‘As I thought, should I use try to manipulate her?’</p>
<p>Sol’s mind warred between the two.</p>
<p>Trusting Pandora meant giving her a loaded gun and hoping she would not shoot him.</p>
<p>Manipulating her now mean that he was taking one step closer to losing all restraint and justifying all means.</p>
<p>So Sol decided to take the middle path.</p>
<p>Give her his trust once and if he failed. He would simply erase her memory. Even if he had to use a part of the remaining divine power for this.</p>
<p>In short, he was giving her an empty gun, while making her think it was loaded. If she tried to shoot, then all she would hear would be an empty click.</p>
<p>‘How time has changed.’</p>
<p>Sol already missed the festival and the time spent with Isis and Sheherazade. He did not really like how he was becoming colder and warier. But if there was something he had learned from his father's history and even his own.</p>
<p>It was that the word &lt;&lt;Betrayal&gt;&gt; was not created for enemies.</p>
<p>Only those close to you could betray you.</p>
<p>Trust was not a right. It was a very rare and precious commodity.</p>
<p>‘Well then, this is enough. Time to act.’</p>
<p>He was about to close his Eye when something caught his attention.</p>
<p>Hum?</p>
<p>Looking down on himself, he could see a great number of pink-colored threads covering him, connected to his soul like last time. The number was ever-expanding, showing that he was quite the scumbag all things considered.</p>
<p>But this was not what really caught his attention. Last time, he could also see one bright golden thread, clearly representing Luxuria, and two dimmer ones, representing Castitas and Superbia, standing out among them.</p>
<p>It was normal. As a human, he was under both Luxuria and castitas and his ancestry from his mother's side linked him with Superbia.</p>
<p>He could also perceive different feelings from those threads.</p>
<p>Greed, from Luxuria. Worry, from Castitas. Indifference from Superbia.</p>
<p>This was how it should have been even now. But this time, what he was seeing was different.</p>
<p>‘One, two, three…four?’</p>
<p>A fourth golden thread had appeared and the feeling coming from this one was very different.</p>
<p>All he could feel was…Hostility.</p>
<p>Sol’s eyes twitched in frustration at this sudden discovery. He wondered just what the hell had happened and which of the goddesses did he suddenly offend for such a feeling of hostility to be born.</p>
<p>‘Perhaps I should have stayed in the Dragon realm.’</p>
<p>One day.</p>
<p>No</p>
<p>It hasn’t even been one day since he came back.</p>
<p>But already there was so much bullshit happening.</p>
<p>‘I want to sleep.’</p>
<p>Life could really be a pain sometime.</p>
