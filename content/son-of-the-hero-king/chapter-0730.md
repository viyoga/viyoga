<p>After Nidhogg and Siegfried left the premises, Sol emerged out of a swirling portal, right in the location where the father-daughter pair were having a heart-to-heart of sorts not long ago. A contemplative expression was etched on his handsome face, pondering the scenario.</p>
<p>After his discussion with the goddess, Sol made sure to return to Jasmine and Satella. He was planning to spend some more time with the two sexy elves with contrasting aspects to them in their residence.</p>
<p>Sending an avatar of his to observe Nidhogg had just been a coincidence, if anything, but as always, his luck helped him catch onto something that could only be described as impressive, mind-boggling even.</p>
<p>Everyone has been underestimating Siegfried. Myself included.</p>
<p>This was a good reminder that despite his powers, aspects, abilities, and contingencies, he was still far from reaching the true level of omniscience— of truly being aware of everything in existence, at least for the universe he resided in, at least.</p>
<p>Siegfried had been hiding his fangs and sharpening his claws for over a thousand years. Though it was true that he was facing a wall, barring him from the demigod realm, his case was totally different from Satella's.</p>
<p>Truly, what an interesting pair of siblings.</p>
<p>His visit to Southern Pride had been filled with many twists and surprises, however, he did not expect Siegfried of all people to give him the biggest surprise of all.</p>
<p>Yet, now was not the time to ponder deeply about this new variable. There were more pressing matters on his hands.</p>
<p>“How troubling.” He could not help but mutter under his breath. The group or cult Siegfried mentioned was an organization he had heard of for the very first time. Despite having relations with the three mortal demigods of the Mortal Realm, he was still not aware of this group. It was a sobering thought.</p>

<p>“It seems like I will have to hurry up some of my plans. If some secret organization is messing around, I need to show them that I do not intend to play their game.” His expression became cold and unfeeling. The Wings of Freedom were more than enough pain he was willing to take on his plate.</p>

<p>{You seem pretty calm.}</p>

<p>{Humph! How could a coward who has yet to have a kid of his own dare to speak about becoming a Father to me?}</p>
<p>“Ouch. I have to admit. This was a painful comeback.” Sol sighed and entered his dimension. Now was not the time to have children.</p>
<p>He knew that they would only be additional weaknesses for him on the journey ahead. He could not afford to add more targets to protect when his potential enemies kept growing in number and power like this.</p>
<p>Making promises for the future about having children was like one of the biggest Death Flags to ever exist in all of reality, and he did not want to trigger it. His lovers were definitely getting impatient and wished for more. But things would come in time.</p>
