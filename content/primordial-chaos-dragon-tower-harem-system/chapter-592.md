<p>The Great War.</p>
<p>The war that defined the fate of the universe. The one where some of the greatest beings to have ever lived fell. That war was the one that shook the cosmos and made every cosmic being shake in their boots.</p>
<p>That battle showed that even the universe needed help—that cosmic beings are eternal, but they are weak when threatened.</p>
<p>Human lives were laid down so they could keep on living.</p>
<p>At the harrowing moments of the Great War, moments before the Chaos Dragon shattered his soul and used it to call forth the 14 Doom Legion, he first gazed into the cosmos and smiled.</p>
<p>At that moment, the Enchantress, the watcher of the timeline, saw what could have been, and for a moment, two teardrops fell from her eyes—one from the left eye and the other from the right.</p>
<p>As the watcher of the timeline, she saw what could have been and what is, and that broke her heart. For a cosmic being tasked with watching, that sight moved her, so she cried.</p>
<p>And two teardrops manifested her feelings.</p>
<p>One was love.</p>
<p>The other was hate.</p>
<p>The love was because what she saw was beautiful. It was something filled with love, something that looked beautiful, and something that made her wish it came true.</p>
<p>The hate was what was—the one that was happening—and that broke her heart. So she cried, and the tears of hate manifested into a person.</p>
<p>However, these new existences weren’t meant to be but were, and that must be corrected. The universe requires balance, so when the universe resets, they were no more.</p>
<p>However, fate had other plans.</p>
<p>Millions of years later, love and hate were reborn. They grew up and lived like normal humans.</p>
<p>But are they truly just that? Were they meant to be just that? This answer came when hate witnessed love taken away by men’s evil.</p>
<p>That was when resentment was born in hate, and her divinity rose. She lived, hating and resenting everything for millions of years until she met a human.</p>
<p>The cosmic child who wasn’t supposed to exist in the first place now has divinity and love in her life. But was that truly who she was?</p>
<p>The love she found—is it enough to manifest her true self? If that wasn’t the case, then can love fix that? And how much love can fix or balance out hate?</p>
<p>The answer to that question is shrouded in mystery.</p>
<p>"Hmm..." Kent brushed his chin when the true name appeared in his mind. It wasn’t like he expected it and didn’t come like the others. This true name was different.</p>
<p>However, he knew it had to be invoked, so he did. He called forth the name...</p>
<p>"She is hate. She is resentful and shall be known as Mal’Thyrria, the Resentful Eclipse. Heed your name and rise, Mal’Thyrria; your destiny has just begun."</p>
<p>A sudden heaviness fell on Kent, sending him to his knees. Then he fell backwards and passed out...</p>
<p>This made his ladies panic, and they wanted to go to his aid, but Gaia, Dori, and Akira appeared and stopped them.</p>
<p>⟪Relax, ladies, the master is fine. He needs to take a nap⟫, Gaia said, making them halt. Meanwhile, in the air, Vexthra was covered in darkness for a few minutes before her body was revealed.</p>
<p>She looked even more elegant, with a dark crown now atop her head. Her hair remained the same silver with some essence of redness in it.</p>
<p>Basically, she became the goddess of hatred and resentment in name, but aside from that, she now embodies the title and its innate belief.</p>
<p>Her body had been reformed to embody her name, Mal’Thyrria. Her gown was also black, and looking into her eyes, one could feel her divinity oozing.</p>
<p>For a moment, her sisters feared for their lives as they gazed into the eyes of hatred and resentment. But after a few seconds, Vexthra returned to her previous self, making them sigh.</p>
<p>With Kent passed out, they knew contending with her would be disastrous.</p>
<p>"Let’s take him inside first," Vexthra said after getting back down. Her sisters nodded, and they carried Kent away.</p>
<p>However, they didn’t leave his side; they wanted to be there when he woke up. After ten days, when he didn’t wake, they knew he would be out for a while, so they decided to take turns caring for him while they waited.</p>
<p>A month passed in the blink of an eye, but Kent still hadn’t woken up.</p>
<p>Of course, his ladies were worried. In fact, they were worried sick, but the three Lolis kept telling them he was fine. After Elaine spent three days using all her abilities on Kent, she finally came to the conclusion that he was indeed fine.</p>
<p>But even so, after three months, when he still didn’t wake, their worry only grew. Thankfully, four months after he went to sleep, Kent woke up.</p>
<p>However, he immediately went into seclusion since his second enlightenment in the sword path happened right when he woke up.</p>
<p>This time, the ladies knew he was fine, so they relaxed and let him finish his enlightenment.</p>
<p>Weeks passed quickly, but Kent remained in his enlightened state, not coming out. Unfortunately for him, the person he was waiting for in the Bloodvein tribe inside the Dead Region came, and Sylara tried to alert him, but when he didn’t answer, she came to inform his ladies.</p>
<p>Naturally, they knew what had to be done.</p>
<p>However, they also didn’t want to leave their man’s side, so Vexthra made Velyra, Vari, Lani, Neomi, Selene, Ingrid, Alina, and Elaine go handle it.</p>
<p>Vari, Lani, and Velyra are all at the Core Formation stage, and the rest are at the Root Sovereign stage, so she knew they could handle the Core Formation demon that appeared.</p>
<p>According to Sylara, the person is a demon who came from the shadows, and judging by his aura, he had already completed six core evolutions.</p>
<p>This made him several times more powerful than tribe chief Malthar. But the ladies are no pushovers either, especially with Alina going with them.</p>
