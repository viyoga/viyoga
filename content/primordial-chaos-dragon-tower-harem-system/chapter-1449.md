<p>[Dream Continent - Dream City - Planet Brymrr]</p>
<p>There are seven extremely large continents on Planet Brymrr.</p>
<p>One of them is the Dream Continent. It is home to the Elves and is also the most beautiful and powerful continent in the world.</p>
<p>Of course, not only do Elves live there, but they also rule it despite there being other races, some even more powerful than most Elves living there.</p>
<p>All of the planet’s alchemy comes from there, since the Elves, long before this planet became a unique planet, had cemented their status as the Alchemy Central of the entire world.</p>
<p>They have a legendary alchemist who, over the years, has done nothing but ensure the world of Alchemy was never dull.</p>
<p>This alchemy leader is a recently ascended Supreme who, long before she reached that stage, could concoct Pseudo-Supreme Pills.</p>
<p>When she finally reached the supreme stage, she announced to the entire world that she would host a live demonstration in 6 months. At this event, she would attempt her first Supreme Pill Concoction.</p>
<p>This is her way of finally reaching the rank of a Pill Supreme.</p>
<p>Legend has it that a very long time ago, she received enlightenment from the Pill Dao, and in that enlightenment, she was given a pill recipe that, to this day, the universe of Alchemy has been looking for.</p>
<p>Dao Enlightenment Pill</p>
<p>It is a pill said to directly connect one to the Dao they practice when consumed. This means if Kent were to take it, there is a big chance he would be connected to the Sword and Pill Dao.</p>
<p>Of course, one would not come face-to-face with the Dao. However, getting a glimpse of the Dao, even from several billion light-years away, can transform just anyone from mediocrity to legendary status.</p>
<p>It is the pill of a lifetime, and since it was given to her by the Pill Dao, it cannot be stolen nor pressured to use it. She had to concoct it at her own pace and time.</p>
<p>This was why two months ago, just when Pseudo-Pill Supreme Ainna was preparing to make a simple pill, the most unexpected person appeared at her doorstep.</p>
<p>She knew powerful people would come to her in the hope of getting their hands on the first-ever Dao Enlightenment Pill.</p>
<p>However, she was taken aback when this person appeared at her doorstep.</p>
<p>At first, seeing her again made her frown and bitter. However, she became happy a few moments later when this friend of hers told her something unexpected.</p>
<p>It turned out that a very long time ago, the two of them met and made plans to reach the peak together, one as a swordswoman and the other an alchemist.</p>
<p>That promise became a betrayal when one of them ran off with a boy who later broke her heart and left her stuck at the god stage for countless years now.</p>
<p>She hated her for that betrayal, but seeing her friend Inaya back, she had no choice but to forgive her. After all, being mad at the ruler of the entire planet isn’t something she wanted.</p>
<p>So they have been living together since then, sharing stories of things that happened while she was away.</p>
<p>After learning all she needed to, Inaya decided to rest and wait for the event that Ainna announced would be happening in 6 months.</p>
<p>She knew she would be the recipient of the Dao Enlightenment Pill, but to make others happy, she let Ainna entertain them, even allowing her to take their gifts with a smile on her beautiful face.</p>
<p>Of course, she expected her days to be as smooth as ever, especially after she learned someone new had appeared on the Sword Dao tablet.</p>
<p>She knew for a fact that Jest had reincarnated because, in her heart, there is only one man who could impress the Sword Dao, and that person is Jest.</p>
<p>But that was ruined when news suddenly began spreading that the Sword Intent left behind by Lord Genesis several years ago had awakened.</p>
<p>One of the reasons why she came back to the Dream Continent was so she could be close to something that used to belong to Lord Genesis.</p>
<p>It was said that before he died, he managed to glimpse into the realm above the Sword Celestial stage. That small glimpse was enough for him to improve his sword intent to the point he was able to leave a part of it, which, since he was gone, had helped countless swordsmen gain enlightenment.</p>
<p>It was even said that the reason why Planet Brymrr still stood after countless wars plagued the universe was because of that sword intent.</p>
<p>So, for such an item to awaken all of a sudden after sleeping for billions of years and through many generations, she couldn’t help but come to a sudden conclusion.</p>
<p>"He is here."</p>
<p>"Who is here?" Ainna asked, staring at Inaya, whose gaze pierced into the vastness of space, staring at the awakened sword intent.</p>
<p>Before today, it was disguised as a brown sword. However, now it seems to have transformed into a bald child about 13 years old.</p>
<p>"Jest. He is back... and I think he is on this planet."</p>
<p>"You always say that ever since that Kent Madson person’s name appeared on the Sword Dao Tablet. When are you going to stop obsessing over him and just move on?</p>
<p>For heaven’s sake, that bastard abandoned you when you needed him the most, so even if he is back, so what?" Ainna shot Inaya a fierce glance.</p>
<p>"Or have you forgotten it took me 100,000 years to transform you from a mess back into a living person. I don’t have the energy or time to do that again. So you’d better put your shit together and get over him."</p>
<p>Inaya sighed, her expression softening. "As if that is an easy thing to do. He is my heart demon, Ainna. I tried many times to move on, but he always comes back to haunt me.</p>
<p>Or do you think I went into a slumber for billions of years because I love to sleep? I tried many times to move on, but until he returned from the dead and made up for what he had promised me, the only thing I could do was obsess over him."</p>
<p>Ainna smirked, but seeing her friend, who was usually fierce like a tiger, now appear soft and emotional, her hatred died down a bit. But she still made her feelings known.</p>
<p>"If that bastard is indeed back like you said, then he’d better pray I don’t find him first. Because if I do, he is going to wish he had never hurt you."</p>
<p>Inaya smiled. "All the more reason for me to protect him from you. The last thing I want is him ending up dead again before I can get back at him myself."</p>
<p>"Tsk, keep defending him." Ainna paused and gave her next words a critical examination before saying them. "Just so you know, that bitch he ditched you for has become a Celestial millions of years ago, while you are still stuck at the Universal God stage with a heart demon."</p>
<p>Inaya shot her friend a sidelong glance before a sigh escaped her lips. "Don’t worry. I will kill her one day, after I kill that bastard."</p>
<p>"As if I will believe that."</p>
<p>They became silent, thinking about what all this means. Now that the sword intent had awakened, many powerful swordsmen would soon appear, so they had to prepare.</p>
<p>Who knows what kind of battle will soon break out?</p>
<p>Most importantly, who knows why it had awakened after being dormant for so long.</p>
<p>Meanwhile, back in the unknown forest, Kent was only a few thousand miles from reaching the edge of teh forest, where he would begin his final journey through its harrowing depths.</p>
<p>He had no idea where he would end up after crossing the forest, but he knew he had to be careful since, even long before he reached it, he could sense an ominous presence coming from within.</p>
<p>Just when he was a few hundred meters away, he came to a stop and scanned his surroundings. Finding no immediate danger, he landed and quickly made his way to a small clearing right atop a mountain.</p>
<p>He wanted to let the Tower do its thing before he made a decision.</p>
<p>The last thing he wanted was to walk into an unknown trap.</p>
<p>He might be powerful for someone of his rank, but if he were to walk into the den of an Immortal Lord, he would die for sure.</p>
