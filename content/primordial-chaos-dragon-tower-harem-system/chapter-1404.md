<p>[Legion Tower]</p>
<p>Avotha stood in the air, his cold gaze falling on the 21 True Immortals. Every last one of them had been rendered immobile, none even having the strength to move their fingers.</p>
<p>Compared to an Immortal Lord, the power of a demigod is several times monstrous. Especially if the demigod is Avotha.</p>
<p>"Uncle Drew, what should we do with them?"</p>
<p>"Knock them out and throw them into the Tower Prison for now. Once everything settles down, we will find a use for them."</p>
<p>Avotha nodded and, with a flick of his hand, every last one of the 21 True Immortals lost consciousness. The tower pulled them away into the Tower Prison.</p>
<p>The next second, Vexthra and her sisters arrived in the Legion Tower.</p>
<p>She moved toward the second cage and grabbed the lock, yanking it off like it was mere paper. Unity, still possessing Risana’s body, walked out.</p>
<p>Once she was outside, she pulled her consciousness out of Risana’s body, causing the young lady, who was still asleep, to fall. However, Vexthra was there to catch her.</p>
<p>She hugged her tightly, tears rolling down her face. While Risana was still small and young, her face was the same as Vexthra remembered.</p>
<p>They were fraternal twins, so Risana looked nothing like her. But even so, as someone who had spent thousands of years with her, Vexthra could never mistake her appearance.</p>
<p>Right now, the lady in her arms looked just like her sister, and that alone was all she needed to know her sister had indeed reincarnated.</p>
<p>Even if Risana would never recall her life as Vexthra’s twin sister, she would be fine with that.</p>
<p>She resonated with the Love Dao Unity had, and she looked just like her dead sister. That was all she needed, and now that she had found her, nothing was more important than that.</p>
<p>Unity walked toward her, then the three of them vanished. Soon, more of them started leaving, each entering Unity’s love space.</p>
<p>In a span of 10 seconds, the only ones left were Dia, Yuyin, and Miryssa. The rest ran after their big sister to make sure she was okay.</p>
<p>Miryssa turned to Uncle Drew, but he gave her the go-ahead to handle things on her own. She was Kent’s wife, so there was no need to even question her authority.</p>
<p>She turned to Dia and Yuyin. "You can follow them if you want."</p>
<p>They smiled but shook their heads. "Let’s make these poor souls feel at home first."</p>
<p>Miryssa waved her hand, and the rest of the cages opened. "Everyone, welcome to your new home. My name is Miryssa, and these are my sisters and friends."</p>
<p>Already, every last person trapped in the cages was scared. But now, surrounded by people they had never seen before, they became petrified.</p>
<p>They were used to seeing Red Foxes, Storm humans, and the evil dark elves. In a way, they are used to that. So seeing humans, demons, dragons, and all kinds of people now surrounding them, they all became even more scared.</p>
<p>However, just as Kent’s name was used to take the blame from the Red Foxes and Storm humans, the same could be done with these people.</p>
<p>"Before you scare yourselves to death, know the one who saved you is none other than Kent Madson. I know most of you have heard about him."</p>
<p>As expected, many reacted when Kent’s name was mentioned. His fame has spread so far that even children have heard of him.</p>
<p>So hearing a familiar name calms them down a bit.</p>
<p>"Were we really saved by Lord Kent Madson?" a Red Fox asked, and Miryssa nodded.</p>
<p>"He sure did, and that is why I would want you all to know that, for now, your home on planet Offin is not safe for you to return to. But not for long. Very soon, if you want to return, we will gladly send you back.</p>
<p>However, you will be staying here for a couple of months, maybe a few years. If you need anything, feel free to ask, and well, if the time to leave comes and you wish to stay, we would not force you."</p>
<p>Many were a bit hesitant, but compared to where they were before they were rescued, at least they weren’t locked in cages now, so they would make do with that.</p>
<p>Of course, it would take some time to adjust, but Miryssa was sure that once they met the others in the tower cities, they would feel safer.</p>
<p>"In that case, why don’t we head to your new home?" Miryssa waved her hand again, and they appeared a couple of meters from Gaiaville. When their gaze landed on the magnificent city, they all became stunned.</p>
<p>"I will take you all through the cities we have here, and whichever one you wish to stay in, feel free to tell me." Miryssa started leading them through the cities, making sure she used the moment to make them feel safer and happier.</p>
<p>She is already aware that the more people living in the tower city, the stronger Kent becomes. So she is determined to make these poor souls stay.</p>
<p>Meanwhile, inside Unity’s Love Space, the ladies were all sitting around a bed, watching Risana sleep soundly. It was a bit weird to be staring at someone that intently while they slept.</p>
<p>However, none of them cared about their strange behavior. In their minds, they all wanted to be there when Risana woke up.</p>
<p>She would not remember Vexthra, but they would make her feel at home regardless of how she felt. She had been through a traumatic experience, and so the least they could do was ease her into this new chance at life.</p>
<p>"This is weird," Unity suddenly said.</p>
<p>"What is weird?"</p>
<p>"Us. What we are doing is creepy. We will be scaring her if she wakes up to the sight of us staring at her like crazy people. I know I would be scared."</p>
<p>They tried to come up with excuses, but after a few seconds of thought, it became clear that they indeed looked like a bunch of crazy ladies.</p>
<p>When it finally sank in, they decided to move away from the bed but remain inside Unity’s Love Space until she woke up.</p>
<p>The Love space was very large and beautiful, so there were a lot of places for them to do all kinds of things while they waited.</p>
<p>Days passed in the blink of an eye. On the outside, Miryssa had helped everyone settle in. All of the Red Foxes chose to stay in Skyjade City, where the Sky Foxes lived. They felt more at home there.</p>
<p>As for the Storm humans, some chose to stay in Gaiaville, while some went to Nature City, where the Elves lived.</p>
<p>They had heard the Elves were natural enemies of the Dark Elves, so they decided to stay there and perhaps learn something from them.</p>
<p>They were received with open arms, so they settled in quickly.</p>
<p>As for the 21 True Immortals, they all woke up to find they no longer had access to their cultivation bases.</p>
<p>The Tower Prison was a special place Gaia had built to keep those whom her big brother deemed unfit to die just yet, despite their sins.</p>
<p>For now, they would have to cool down and think of what was coming for them.</p>
<p>Uncle Drew was already making plans for them.</p>
<p>They are True Immortals, and as such, there are several ways to use them. Kent hated slaves, so they wouldn’t make them their slaves, but they would have a way to keep them on a leash.</p>
<p>After all, Yi Lai, Elaine, and Veera would have the best use for them.</p>
