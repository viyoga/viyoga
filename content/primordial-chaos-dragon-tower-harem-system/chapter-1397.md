<p>[Planet Offin - Immortal World]</p>
<p>Planet Offin is a low-tier Immortal world. As a low-tier Immortal world, the strongest being that can be found there is merely an Immortal Lord. While formidable in a way, considering they are immortal Lords, people on this planet see them as gods.</p>
<p>Medium-tier Immortal worlds have multiple Immortal Lords, with high-tier Immortal worlds having even demigods as the patriarchs and matriarchs.</p>
<p>As for Supreme-tier Immortal worlds like Planet Gemini, demigods aren’t even the leaders of the families there.</p>
<p>So, a low-tier Immortal world had no standing when all things are put into consideration.</p>
<p>On Planet Offin, there are three ruling families. There is the Tate family, ruled by the Red Fox King. They are a race of red foxes, a rare breed of foxes, just like the sky foxes.</p>
<p>There is the Raden family, ruled by the Storm Immortal. They are a race of Storm Humans, beings born with a natural affinity for water. Their affinity allows them to manipulate water in various forms.</p>
<p>The last is the Adin family, ruled by the Dark King. They are Dark Elves and the most vile race on that planet.</p>
<p>They are also the strongest, considering even the youngest heir is a True Immortal and the scum that had been plaguing the red foxes and Storm Humans for hundreds of years now.</p>
<p>"Young master, the fresh batch of slaves is in," a young dark elf spoke from behind a door. Inside the room, a lean dark elf sat on the bed, three red foxes working on his little prince.</p>
<p>Hearing his subordinate’s words, a smile appeared on his face. He pushed aside the three red foxes and put on his clothes, rushing out of the room with a beaming smile on his face.</p>
<p>"Take me to them."</p>
<p>He and his subordinate took to the sky and vanished from sight. A few minutes later, they arrived in a small clearing where the rest of it was a vast forest.</p>
<p>Lined up and in chains were over 200 red foxes and Storm Humans. Looking closely, most of these people weren’t even over 20, considering they’re mostly in the ranks of Root Saints.</p>
<p>While it can take hundreds of years for someone in a medium world to become a Root Saint, in an Immortal world, 20-year-olds can easily become even Core Formation cultivators.</p>
<p>So this shows just how weak and fragile these people were.</p>
<p>"Wonderful..." A smile appeared on the dark elf’s face.</p>
<p>"Lord Gelen, these are the batch our people managed to capture. There were some challenges on the way here, but we managed to sort it out."</p>
<p>"This means they are not going to be missed, right?" Gelen asked with a wicked grin.</p>
<p>"Even if they are going to be missed, none of those weak, spineless idiots would dare go against our Dark Elf race. They are too scared to make a move."</p>
<p>"That is good, then." Gelen turned to the chained slaves and grinned.</p>
<p>The terrified expressions on their faces said it all; they were scared senseless, and that was probably because of the rumors they had heard about the Dark King’s youngest son.</p>
<p>They all knew they were as good as dead because the moment they appeared before this bastard, there was no way out for them.</p>
<p>He is vile and extremely evil. People’s suffering is like candy to his ear. He cherishes their pain and enjoys their screams. So every last one of them knows there is no way out for them.</p>
<p>"Divide them into groups of ten each. The first hunt will take place tomorrow." His subordinates nodded and started separating the slaves into groups of ten.</p>
<p>What he meant by ’the hunt’ was that the ten in each group would be given a chance at freedom. They would be released to run into the forest, and he would hunt them down.</p>
<p>If they managed to escape, that would be their fortune. If they got caught by his arrows, then that was their bad luck. Regardless, nobody was ever able to escape from him.</p>
<p>After grouping them, they got 24 groups, showing they had captured 240 slaves this time around. They gave each group a number.</p>
<p>Within group 2, there is a loli who, despite the despair around her, seems okay with the situation she has found herself in.</p>
<p>There was no sorrow or pain in her eyes. If anything, she seems okay with death hanging over her head. Her fellow red foxes have their fluffy ears and tails twitching, but she seems content with her life.</p>
<p>This wasn’t because she had given up hope. To her, life is not worth living, since she has been on her own ever since she learned to walk. Life had already been cruel to her from the start, so she is fine with how she has turned out.</p>
<p>At least in two days’ time, her misery will end.</p>
<p>After dividing them, they kept each group in a separate cage and locked it with an enchanted lock. Tomorrow, the first batch of hunting will start.</p>
<p>---</p>
<p>Inside the harem tower, Lani had just finished taking her bath and was getting ready to head back to the legion tower when something vibrated inside her space ring.</p>
<p>Reaching inside, she pulled out a book with a black cover. Her expression darkened when the death note appeared in her hand for the first time since she received it.</p>
<p>Unlike some of her sisters, who received weapons after their true-name awakening, she received a kill book called Death Note.</p>
<p>Whenever a name appears in it, she is expected to kill.</p>
<p>It had been dormant for a while now. But today, it gave her a name.</p>
<p>Opening to the first page, she began reading the mission details.</p>
<p>[Mission Grade: Black]</p>
<p>[Target: Gelen Darkwood (True Immortal)]</p>
<p>[Location: Planet Offin - Dark Continent]</p>
<p>[Mission Details: Gelen Darkwood, the youngest heir of the Dark Elf race, had become a nuisance and a threat to the Red Fox and Storm Human races over the years.</p>
<p>His continuous killing of innocents had gone unnoticed for a long time simply because nobody had the spine to do what is right. However, that ends now when he points his arrows at a Heaven’s daughter.</p>
<p>Your mission is to kill him and rescue the unfortunate souls that he planned on killing next.]</p>
<p>[Duration: Two Days]</p>
<p>[Rewards:]</p>
<p>↠ Memory Key (Past Life)</p>
<p>↠ Black Stone</p>
<p>↠ First Volume of the Dark Records</p>
<p>[Note: You can take two aids with you. But the target must die by your weapon. Failure to kill him with your own hand will result in mission failure.]</p>
<p>Lani closed the book and stored it away. She then put on her clothes and left the harem tower to go see her big sister.</p>
<p>Since it had come to this, she knew letting her big sister know what had happened was the only way she could come up with a good plan.</p>
<p>She expected to receive a much smaller mission, one in which she would be tasked with killing a Life- or maybe even a Death-stage target. However, getting a True Immortal as her target is just too much of a danger.</p>
<p>But with Vexthra’s help, she knew it was possible to get this mission done in two days.</p>
