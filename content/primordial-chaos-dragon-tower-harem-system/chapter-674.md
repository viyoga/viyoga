<p>"Eat my fist...master." Kaizo jumped high into the air and descended with a thunderous boom. Kent crossed his hands on his chest and took the blow, sending him staggering back.</p>
<p>But Kaizo was upon Kent the next second.</p>
<p>His fist was coated in lightning, just like Kent’s, but Kaizo’s was much more potent, and every time he punched him, the force behind his punch kept sending Kent staggering backward.</p>
<p>Kent, of course, was handling the punch like a champ, making it seem like he wasn’t bearing the force behind Kaizo’s punch when, in fact, he was feeling something.</p>
<p>The punches contain both pain and terrifying force. Whenever Kaizo’s punch lands on Kent’s body, he feels some excitement in his bones.</p>
<p>This suggests that something else was at play, leaving him wanting more. After a while, he knew what was making him giddy. It turns out that getting punched isn’t too bad when you’re a body cultivator.</p>
<p>⟦Your body is growing stronger from the energy absorbed⟧</p>
<p>Every few minutes, Kent receives this message, and after each message, he becomes stronger. This caused him to be on the receiving end of Kaizo’s fist.</p>
<p>⟦Your body is growing stronger from the energy absorbed⟧</p>
<p>"Kaizo, at this point, if you dont get serious, I will fall asleep from being tickled by you," Kent said with a grin, when the next message appeared.</p>
<p>"Oh, I am just getting started," Kaizo’s voice boomed as his fist crackled with more energy. His hair even stood on end, showing he is using more lightning energy this time.</p>
<p>There was no skill to the fight; it was pure brute strength. However, when Kent saw the fist coming, he knew it was a dangerous one. However, he didn’t dodge.</p>
<p>Instead, he also changed his energy and punched out.</p>
<p>Boom</p>
<p>Crack</p>
<p>Kent heard the cracking sound come from his right arm, indicating his arm had broken, more like the bones in his right hand had shattered. His hand went numb, and then, like he expected, blood started seeping from his fist.</p>
<p>Kaizo broke his arm, but the smile on Kent’s face doesn’t mimic someone whose arm is broken. In fact, the smile conveys a hint of gratitude.</p>
<p>Naturally, Kent was indeed grateful to Kaizo for helping him break through to the Diamond Body cultivation.</p>
<p>⟦Congratulations, you are now a Diamond Body Cultivator⟧</p>
<p>This means his physical strength has skyrocketed, indicating that he has achieved his goal. He felt like he was close when he started absorbing Kaizo’s punches...so seeing the message now, he became happy.</p>
<p>Kent looked at his broken arm, and a smile appeared on his face. "That was dangerous, Kaizo, but now it is my turn to fight back."</p>
<p>Kent activated his Chaos Dragon bloodline, covering his arm with dragon scales. The broken arm straightened and healed in a couple of seconds. This demonstrates the remarkable power of Kent’s bloodline.</p>
<p>"Here I come," Kent’s speed exploded, and before Kaizo knew it, he was in front of him. He punched forward, landing a powerful punch on Kaizo’s chest.</p>
<p>He was too late to react, as Kent’s speed far surpassed his own. However, even though Kent’s speed hadn’t exceeded his own, he would still have struggled to defend against the punch.</p>
<p>It contains more power than in his human form.</p>
<p>This threw Kaizo flying back. But Kent didn’t allow him to land. He moved swiftly across the floor, appearing behind Kaizo.</p>
<p>He punched up, meeting Kaizo’s back. The punch sent him flying forward again. This time, Kent allowed Kaizo to land on the ground.</p>
<p>Kent studied his fist for a second and then looked at Kaizo, who was radiating a golden energy.</p>
<p>His presence grew stronger, and while he hadn’t activated any skill, the strength emanating from him was enough for Kent to know he was about to unleash his bloodline.</p>
<p>"You might want to start using your bloodline now, master. The next punch contains 5% of my bloodline," Kaizo warned before he moved.</p>
<p>His punch crackled with energy.</p>
<p>It became much denser, and judging by the runes around his arm, he was indeed using his bloodline, just a portion of it at least...</p>
<p>But that was enough to create a sonic boom around his fist when he punched out. Kent, who saw this, knew he had to protect himself, so his body was immediately coated in dragon scales, most specifically, his chest.</p>
<p>Then he also punched out.</p>
<p>However, before their fist could even meet, the shockwave being produced was enough to send someone like Krex flying back.</p>
<p>"How is this even possible?" Krex wondered, sitting far from the training ground. The shockwave coming from the two punches is more than he can bear.</p>
<p>Even someone like Gorzak was exerting all of his strength so that he could stand still.</p>
<p>"Observe, brat...this is how real men fight," Gorzak responded with a thunderous laughter, one that blended with the sonic boom being created from the two punches.</p>
<p>The world seemed to have slowed down somehow as the two fists came close to meeting each other.</p>
<p>Even the other legion watching the battle felt their bodies getting pushed back, long before Kent and Kaizo’s fists met.</p>
<p>When they did, a sonic boom, one that carried enough energy to obliterate the training ground and send the legion flying back, resounded.</p>
<p>BOOM!</p>
<p>Kent and Kaizo were thrown backwards, bearing the pressure from each other’s punches. Kaizo, in particular, was sent flying far from Kent.</p>
<p>Kaizo’s arm is broken, bent at an unnatural angle. Golden blood was coming from a couple of cracks on both his arm and chest, and even his face had a few crack marks.</p>
<p>Most parts of his fist lost the flesh, and, looking closely, one of his bones, which looked golden instead of white, has some cracks on it...</p>
<p>That bone has been tempered using Kaizo’s True Divine Angel bloodline, so that one is much stronger than the rest.</p>
<p>However, it still has cracks in it. This shows just how powerful Kent’s punch was.</p>
<p>Kent, on the other hand, was staring at his fist with a dazed look on his face. There was not even a single wound on it. It was like his fist punched nothing.</p>
<p>His body looked perfectly fine, and aside from the force from Kaizo’s punch that entered his body, he looked fine.</p>
<p>’This can’t be possible, right...this shouldn’t be possible, right?’ Kent wondered, raising his head to look at the wrecked Kaizo, who stood up wearing a smile on his face.</p>
<p>Despite his body being injured, he doesn’t seem concerned about his injuries...</p>
<p>"Master, how much of your bloodline did you use..." Kaizo instead wants to measure his strength against his master. He lost, but he wants to know..</p>
<p>Kent scratched the back of his head with his left hand as a guilty expression appeared on his face. "Uhhhm...I used 1%..."</p>
<p>Kaizo fell back on his ass...shocked written all over his face.</p>
