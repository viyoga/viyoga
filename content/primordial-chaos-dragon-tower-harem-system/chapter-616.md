<p>"So what is the plan, master?" Kaizo asked, now back inside the tower. After Kent received the go-ahead from King Hkum and Queen Amorian, Kent sent his undead army away, but the ladies decided to stay and explore the city much more.</p>
<p>Dori and the two princesses went with them, while Kent and Sword King Shawn retired back to the residence they were given.</p>
<p>Sword King Shawn went back to his cultivation, while Kent entered the tower to plan the battle he and his legion would soon embark on.</p>
<p>"The plan is, first, nobody is allowed to die," Kent smiled. "As for the battle that is about to happen, the odds are against us, but that doesn’t mean we can’t handle it.</p>
<p>First, as you all heard, there are some Sovereigns and Core Formation Hellhounds present. But knowing you all, they are as good as dead. This leaves the 100 Spirit Ascension, 24 Spirit Kings, and the Spirit Emperors.</p>
<p>Aside from that, we also have to rescue the prisoners. This means that while we handle the fighters, some of you will sneak in and take out those guarding the prisoners, then lead them away.</p>
<p>That won’t be easy. However, it is doable. However, while that sounded like a good plan, we won’t be doing that.</p>
<p>We will do the opposite. Instead of rescuing the people during the battle, we will do that first and then rain fire on the hellhounds."</p>
<p>"How do we do that?" Zareth asked. Kent turned and pointed toward Veydris, Irina, and Valtor. "You three will handle the rescue."</p>
<p>Everyone became puzzled. They knew Valtor was nearly invisible to most senses, but Irina is not like him, nor is Veydris, so why then would they join him?</p>
<p>If anything, Nyxira should be the perfect person to join him since she is practically a shadow, like Lani and Vari. However, Kent just smiled.</p>
<p>"The reason for choosing the three of them is simple. While Valtor is perfect to infiltrate the base undetected, he will be discovered the moment he uses this." Kent produced a Tier 10 talisman.</p>
<p>"This is a Tier 10 Teleportation talisman that can teleport everyone within a 40-meter radius. The moment he activates it, the Spirit Emperor there will notice, and while I would love to say Valtor has what it takes to face him, it will be a lost cause on his side.</p>
<p>Which is why Irina and Veydris will come with him. They can use the Void to get to him the moment he activates the talisman and intercept the Spirit Emperor.</p>
<p>This means Irina and Veydris will fight and kill the Spirit Emperor."</p>
<p>Kent looked at Irina, and she smiled confidently. Veydris just stood there, not minding anyone. He may be a Transcendent, but now that his Void comprehension is at the Perfect Stage, he can handle the battle.</p>
<p>Irina, being the strongest in his legions, means she is perfect for this battle.</p>
<p>"Now, we still have 24 Spirit Kings to handle. While I would love to say we can easily do it, the truth is we are outnumbered. However, we have Aetheris, so it is possible we can do it." Kent turned to the Flame Giant.</p>
<p>"I know you want to fight, and you will, but for the first thirty minutes, I want you to use your Flame Domain to trap ten Spirit Kings. Can you do it?"</p>
<p>"Yes. Ten is not a problem," Aetheris answered.</p>
<p>"Very good. This leaves only 14 for us to handle. This responsibility will fall on Veera and Nykali. Veera, you are a necromancer who can summon undead.</p>
<p>However, I don’t want you to summon hundreds of them; I just want you to summon the ten Core Formation undead you have."</p>
<p>Zareth and his undead friends smiled weakly, hearing this. ’Looks like she went easy on us when we fought her.’</p>
<p>She indeed went easy on them when they were clearing the dungeons. But now, she had to be serious and unleash her full power this time.</p>
<p>"You will handle five Spirit Kings. You don’t have to kill all of them if you can’t, but make sure you have them locked down."</p>
<p>Veera nodded.</p>
<p>"Nykali, you have a spectral army, right? Use them to handle another five." The Shade King nodded with a bony smile.</p>
<p>"My wives will handle the four left. Then come the 100 Spirit Ascension Hellhounds. They are a bigger Odd, but they are weaker than the Spirit Kings... so everyone except Skoggr, Gorzak, and Kazarak will handle them."</p>
<p>"But master, what about the Sovereigns and Core Formation?" Sylara asked.</p>
<p>"I will handle them quickly. That is why I want Aetheris to hold back ten Spirit Kings. The moment I am done with the Sovereigns and Core Formation, which shouldn’t take over twenty minutes, I will come for the Spirit Kings."</p>
<p>His legion nodded. They didn’t doubt his words simply because they knew their master wasn’t weak.</p>
<p>"Then what would I, Skoggr, and Kazarak be doing since we don’t have a mission?" Gorzak, the barbarian Orc King, asked.</p>
<p>"Oh, your mission is very important." Kent smiled evilly and explained their mission. By the time he was done, his legion could only pity the Orc alliance for making an indirect enemy out of their master.</p>
<p>After a while, Kent went back to Fedora. He informed Sword King Shawn about the battle. As expected, he planned to join in and see what he could do before entering seclusion, which he estimated would take years.</p>

<p>Far at the core of the universe, Lady Universe, who saw and heard Kent’s plans, smiled.</p>
<p>"He hasn’t even awakened a quarter of his bloodline, but he’s already started causing this much chaos. Makes me wonder what he will do once he becomes a full Dragon."</p>
<p>A smile tugged at the corner of her mouth as she looked at the handsome face now biting into an apple.</p>
<p>Suddenly, Kent said something that made her break into a cold sweat.</p>
<p>"I feel like someone is watching me..."</p>
<p>He looked around him for a few seconds but sighed when he couldn’t find anything. He then closed his eyes and decided to take a nap for a few hours while he waited for his wives to come back.</p>
<p>Lady Universe thought, "I must be careful next time. Who knows what will happen when he discovers me..."</p>
<p>Soon, Kent fell asleep, and she went back to staring at his handsome face. A few seconds into it, the Enchantress’ voice entered her ears, causing her to blush.</p>
<p>"Pervert..."</p>
