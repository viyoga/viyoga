<p>Some things are just meant to be, regardless of how much time has passed.</p>
<p>Risana had no idea she and Vexthra were sisters. However, in less than an hour, she had become comfortable around her.</p>
<p>She was smiling and laughing with her, something she had never done before, since she had never had the chance or even the moment to let herself feel joy and happiness.</p>
<p>After revealing everything about her life, while it is mostly filled with heartbreaking moments, it became clear that she was a cultivation genius. She went from nothing to the Spirit Ascension stage in two years using nothing but good old-fashioned low-level cultivation techniques.</p>
<p>She consumed no treasures, as most do.</p>
<p>She just absorbed spiritual qi from the air.</p>
<p>Basically, she had done what takes many decades, sometimes even centuries, in merely two years. That made Vexthra happy. This showed that her sister still had the talent and a chance to soon rise to the rank of a god.</p>
<p>If she could reach where she was now, then there was no telling what else she could do in the next two decades.</p>
<p>When they arrived at the waterfall, the rest of her sisters surrounded them and started asking questions. They were all happy that Vexthra’s sister was finally back.</p>
<p>So many questions were asked, and while Risana was overwhelmed at first, she soon adapted.</p>
<p>It was through the many questions that they learned Risana had no plans to become a martial cultivator. She didn’t want to fight or kill, so she had no plans on learning any martial arts techniques.</p>
<p>This, in a way, came as a surprise to most of the ladies. The universe is chaotic now, so choosing not to learn how to kill is a bit shocking.</p>
<p>However, Unity and Vexthra understood her completely. Unity could sense that a part of her hated battles. The part of her that was once the Goddess of Love hated bloodshed.</p>
<p>In that life, she was all about making sure people had happy endings. It was the reason why she had to sacrifice her life to make sure the Blood Spear King and his wife had a happy ending.</p>
<p>So she understood where Risana was coming from. She just wanted to make sure others were happy, even though she had never had a happy life.</p>
<p>She was destined to comprehend the Dao of Love and Passion, so she would never have the power to kill.</p>
<p>However, she never had to kill anyone to defeat them.</p>
<p>Vexthra had already told them that she and her sister planned to use their divinity to inspire. So, since she had already made up her mind, there was nothing they could do to change it.</p>
<p>Not that Vexthra would dare change anything.</p>
<p>Her time had passed. She had already formed her divinity, so her sister could have hers too. Maybe if she became the Goddess of Love and Passion one day, what she couldn’t do herself would be fulfilled through Risana.</p>
<p>It took the rest of her sisters a few minutes to come to terms with what Risana wanted. In the end, they saw that they had the power to kill for her, so she would always be protected.</p>
<p>Every last one of them had been a terrifying warrior, so she was in good hands.</p>
<p>"Let’s go out and show Risana around," Unity suggested, and they soon left the Love Space and arrived back in the Legion Tower.</p>
<p>They started the tour by introducing the legion to Risana. They had already told her Kent Madson had a hand in her rescue.</p>
<p>As they all expected, she had heard of him before, and upon hearing that this place belonged to him, she relaxed a bit and even asked if she could meet him.</p>
<p>They knew she would have to wait a bit longer since Kent was currently busy. After introducing everyone, they continued to Gaiaville and soon went on to the other cities.</p>
<p>The tower cities were just the most beautiful sight a person could ask for.</p>
<p>After spending an entire day showing her around, they retired to the Tower Garden, where Gaia and her minions were resting after building for years.</p>
<p>Gaia wanted Kent to reach Elina much faster, so she asked him for some more tower points, not because she wanted to cheat him, but because she wanted to build a space where time is several times faster than even inside the tower.</p>
<p>Inside the Harem Tower, a day is an hour outside. However, in the new space she built, a day there is instead an hour inside the Harem Tower.</p>
<p>Initially, she would need three years of outside time to build the portal. But with this new space, she would need less time to finish.</p>
<p>Gaia wanted to use the time to ensure Kent had safe travel, so, with the accelerated time, she and her friends had been working hard. To them, a couple of years had passed since they started working on the portal.</p>
<p>When they saw a youngster arrive in the garden with Vexthra and the rest of Kent’s wives, they quickly rushed to her side.</p>
<p>Risana was also small like them, so she fit in perfectly.</p>
<p>She was even a bit happier seeing these tiny ladies spending time on their own. Risana first asked Vexthra for permission, which she was granted, before joining the troublemakers.</p>
<p>Although she would at least grow up someday if she ate proper food, now that she was a loli, there was no better time than now to have some fun with girls her age.</p>
<p>Vexthra was happy for her, so she said nothing and let her join them.</p>
<p>Inside the tower, there was no danger, so she could do as she wanted. Vexthra also didn’t want to force her to cultivate. When or if she wanted, she could resume her cultivation.</p>
<p>It wasn’t like she would be going back outside for now. There were many things she could do inside the tower to pass the time.</p>
<p>Of course, since she was now with Gaia, Dori, and Akira, Vexthra knew they wouldn’t let her slack off on her training.</p>
<p>Aala was just a mortal when Kent brought her with the Sky Foxes into the tower. But because she had joined Gaia’s team, they had helped her with rare treasures to cultivate ahead of time.</p>
<p>She was now a Core Formation stage cultivator.</p>
<p>Another example was Li Hua, who had yet again entered seclusion since she had already reached the Spirit King stage and even become a Shadow Saint.</p>
<p>Although Kent was paying for the treasures, as long as one joined Gaia’s team, they would be taken care of for life. So after relaxing in the garden for a while, they left, and Risana followed them.</p>
<p>Vexthra actually was happy Risana left. Having her around someone like Selene was a risk she was not willing to take.</p>
<p>So it was better to let her go with Gaia, who at least would teach her a couple of things that would benefit her in the long run.</p>
<p>With that settled, they went back to the Legion Tower to handle the issue with the 21 True Immortals before climbing to the 9th floor of the Planet Killer Dungeon.</p>
<p>They couldn’t just kill them. That would be a waste, so the next logical choice was to make them work. But to do that, they must become their slaves, something Vexthra and the rest of her sisters are not willing to do since Kent hates slavery.</p>
<p>So they needed a different method. Thankfully, Yi Lai, Veera, and Elaine all had their own ways to get the best out of them, alive or dead.</p>
