<p>It had been months since Kent and Adina parted ways back in the Valley of Torture.</p>
<p>Back then, Kent wanted only one thing, and that was for her to make it safely to Planet Mekyn, where she would get to live in peace, at least for a while, before those chasing her caught up.</p>
<p>Months had passed, and she had been living in peace until three days ago.</p>
<p>Three days ago, they received word that the Flame Humans had colluded with the Incubus of Planet Vornix—the same people chasing after her—and would be attacking them in two days’ time.</p>
<p>Naturally, this was unexpected, considering nobody knew she was there.</p>
<p>However, as it turned out, one of the Elven maids serving in the Elven Gardens was a spy planted by the humans. This has left her on edge, considering she had nowhere else to go.</p>
<p>So she and her two sisters, High Priestess Runa and Lady Enyo, have been working around the clock to prepare for battle.</p>
<p>Now, there is a literal immortal standing before them. Even though she had been suppressed to the Heavenly Ascension Stage, she is still a danger.</p>
<p>But what is worse is that she is also a messenger of a demigod who is related to demons.</p>
<p>Adina had been running from a Succubus Supreme this entire time, so she and the demons don’t see eye to eye. Now, there is a messenger from one of them.</p>
<p>Lady Enyo, sensing Adina’s mood, took her hand and gave it a gentle squeeze.</p>
<p>"Please, let’s go inside and talk." That sounded like a logical solution since Adina seemed to be on edge. They soon entered the mansion and took a seat in a large hall.</p>
<p>Adina remained by Lady Enyo’s side. She is one of the Planet Protectors, so being by her side seems like the safest place.</p>
<p>They still don’t know if this lady is after her or merely a harmless messenger, so they have their guards on high alert. It would be too bad if they were to lower their guard and let this dragon lady do anything to Adina.</p>
<p>"Pardon the tardiness, but if you don’t mind me asking, what is the message you have for me?" High Priestess Runa asked, keeping a polite smile to mask the nervousness in her eyes.</p>
<p>"No need to bother with that. I have been on the move for many months, so I don’t have any problem with a little inconvenience." Lyna smiled before taking out a scroll from her space ring.</p>
<p>"I am here merely to deliver this. Lord Agni would have come personally, but he has some personal issues to take care of, and so he instructed me to deliver it on his behalf." Lyna handed the scroll to High Priestess Runa.</p>
<p>She then added in a calm and cautious tone, "He said Lord Chaos will come for it, so you should keep it safe."</p>
<p>"Who is Lord Chaos?" High Priestess Runa asked.</p>
<p>Lyna, who had already been told how to handle this question, smiled. "Oh, Lord Agni said you people may know him by the name Kent Madson."</p>
<p>"Kent." Hearing a familiar name, all three ladies lightened up.</p>
<p>Kent’s name was like cold water poured on their beating hearts. Although Lyna is not a direct subordinate of Kent, she is a trusted subordinate of a subordinate of Kent who is also a demigod.</p>
<p>There is no way somebody like that will harm them. Kent would never harm Adina, because he had promised to protect her.</p>
<p>So, hearing that the scroll was for him, the three ladies calmed down.</p>
<p>However, Lady Enyo, who quickly traced back all Lyna said, widened her eyes when a thought suddenly surfaced in her mind.</p>
<p>"You called him Chaos and even said Lord Agni is the direct subordinate of the Primordial Chaos Dragon. Are you saying Kent is the legendary Primordial Chaos Dragon?"</p>
<p>Lyna, who was equally stunned when she learned of this from Lord Agni, nodded.</p>
<p>Several million years ago, Lord Agni saved Lyna’s mother from certain death. To pay him back, she vowed to serve him for all eternity.</p>
<p>However, Lord Agni had to go into hiding when he stole the first form of the legendary Primordial-grade technique—Finger of Destruction.</p>
<p>Due to this, the dragon lady he had saved long ago became a god.</p>
<p>This means she cannot serve him like she could if she were still an immortal.</p>
<p>The past year, Lord Agni went on a mission given to him by the Universe. This mission, unfortunately, exposed him, and his enemies started chasing after him, so he had to go on the run again.</p>
<p>However, he cannot be on the run and also deliver the scroll he retrieved from the Underworld to Planet Mekyn.</p>
<p>That would be too dangerous, so he sought out this Dragon Goddess, who assigned her youngest daughter, who is merely a True Immortal.</p>
<p>Lyna wasn’t prepared when she found out the owner of the scroll was the same person every god, demigod, and immortal has been relentlessly looking for.</p>
<p>Now, Lady Enyo, who also has her secrets, found out that one of the prominent figures to have ever lived is actually the same brat she had been dreaming about for months now.</p>
<p>"Lord Agni said this cannot be spread, not when Lord Chaos is still weak and cannot contend against immortals and gods.</p>
<p>So please keep it hush and don’t forget to keep the scroll safe."</p>
<p>The three ladies nodded. However, Lyna noticed a frown appear in their eyes, causing her to ask what was wrong.</p>
<p>"It is just that we are preparing for a war that is coming in two days, so the chances of keeping this scroll safe are very low. However, even if using our lives, we will keep them safe."</p>
<p>Lyna gritted her teeth slightly and then turned toward Adina. "Does this war have anything to do with her?" The ladies felt their backs break into cold sweats at her question.</p>
<p>Seeing this, Lyna smiled. "Don’t get me wrong. I have no plans to kidnap a succubus with an Extreme Yin Body Constitution.</p>
<p>However, I am an immortal, and I know how important people like her can be to immortals, gods, and even Supremes."</p>
<p>She turned and looked at High Priestess Runa and continued, "People like you two are destined to become Celestials. Your bodies are pure, and once you reach the Immortal Stage, your true potential will be unlocked.</p>
<p>So I can understand why your lives will be in danger."</p>
<p>Lyna sighed. "Since it has come to this, I will stay and fight with you. I will also send a message to a few friends I met along the way. I am sure they would love to have a good battle, especially if they knew they were fighting for a demigod."</p>
<p>"Thank you."</p>
<p>"Don’t bother. I am a dragon. As you already know, we dragons love to fight, so this is right up my alley. However, you three should be prepared to flee.</p>
<p>The fact that Lord Agni was instructed to deliver this scroll here is a clear indication that you are important to Lord Chaos. Protecting your lives is the only important mission for me."</p>
<p>The three ladies nodded.</p>
<p>Soon, they began planning for the coming battle. They had already sent word to the Ice Demons asking for assistance, but they were also preparing.</p>
