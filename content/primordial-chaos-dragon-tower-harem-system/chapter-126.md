<p>Kent sat with his mouth wide open, his imagination completely shattered. He would now have to figure out what to imagine next.</p>
<p>If the current Tower was but an infant, how terrifying would it become as a teenager or an adult? Just the thought made him giddy.</p>
<p>"Tell me something—would the Tower be visible for anyone to see?" Kent asked.</p>
<p>[No, Master. The Tower is not something anyone but you can see. However, I can create a replica and place it in each Tower space if you wish.</p>
<p>For now, only you have the means to see me. Nobody, including Celestial Gods, can see me. Also, I’ll just answer since you’re already thinking about it.</p>
<p>Though I mentioned different Towers, they’re more like worlds within me. The golden Tower you saw contains all these worlds.</p>
<p>So, if you enter the Harem Tower, you will see all its features inside a unique world that will grow as I grow.</p>
<p>Also, though I didn’t list it, the spiritual qi density inside the Tower is much denser than in this world. Also, a day inside the tower is an hour outside]</p>
<p>"I see. That makes sense. Honestly, I thought there would be Towers scattered everywhere. That would’ve been a low blow. For now, let’s keep things the way they are," Kent sighed.</p>
<p>[As it should be, Master.]</p>
