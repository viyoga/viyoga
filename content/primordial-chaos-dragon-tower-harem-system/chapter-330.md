<p>⟪This is it, Master; you have officially pissed off a lot of powerful people. However, you have also set the stage to add more powerful bodies to your next set of undead.</p>
<p>This means you should finally man up and use your strength.</p>
<p>Now, I can’t tell you how many will come after the Forest King Body, but I can assure you that if you manage to bring back Transcendent bodies, you will be walking away with some powerful undead.⟫</p>
<p>Akira said to Kent, who was now sitting alone in VIP room 13. With the auction over, all that was left was for him to go pay for the items he had bought, as well as the ones his women had purchased.</p>
<p>Since they are now inside the tower, he will have to handle that for them. The dangers surrounding him are just too much.</p>
<p>So staying inside the auction house at the moment was good.</p>
<p>However, the moment he stepped out of the auction house and into the open, his life would be in danger. Not that he cares about that.</p>
<p>For the first time, Kent was not going to hide inside the tower, as the quest he had received was too good for him to reject.</p>
<p>’Don’t worry, Akira, I have no plans to run. Since they want to die so badly, I will wait for them to come. I have already prepared for such an outcome so they can come</p>
<p>⟪Good luck, Master. You have many angels waiting for you in the tower.⟫</p>
<p>’I won’t die, Akira. Just take care of the items. In total, I should have between five to seven undead waiting to be created. I will be sure to bring the bodies to start the process.’</p>
<p>After saying that, Kent stood up and left to claim his items.</p>
<p>Along the way, he met the gaze of many hungry lions and even felt the murderous intent coming from afar. They all want a piece of him and their claws on his treasure.</p>
<p>He simply shrugged them off and moved toward the room where he would be claiming all the items he had purchased.</p>
<p>"Hello, Mr. Kent. Please, Lady Alina would like to have a word with you," the lady responsible for taking the money and handing over the items said to him as soon as he entered the room.</p>
<p>"Show me the way," Kent said with a smile.</p>
<p>He was led into a different room where he now stood face to face with his woman, who was playing the role of a stranger to avoid raising suspicion.</p>
<p>Kent could tell someone was watching this interaction, so despite the allure of getting closer to her, he held back.</p>
<p>"Mr. Kent, on behalf of the Silver Leaf Auction House, I would like to thank you for making this year’s auction a memorable one," Alina said with a slight smile.</p>
<p>"I don’t know about memorable, considering half of them want me dead, but I will admit I enjoyed this auction more than I had hoped," Kent replied, shaking his head.</p>
<p>"All the same, someone with your wealth wouldn’t be a pushover. So thank you for making us have a successful auction" Alina gave a cheeky smile before turning serious.</p>
<p>"I asked you here because I learned you have an Ambrosia Apple, and I would like to buy some. And before you ask, yes, I am buying it for someone who, for reasons unknown, couldn’t come herself."</p>
<p>"I see. Though I have one, I would like to point out that it’s not cheap, meaning it’s going to cost you," Kent said in a tone Alina understood all too well.</p>
<p>The payment would indeed involve a lot of pleasurable noises and perfect positions. The undead have mercy on who this mysterious buyer is.</p>
<p>"We will pay whatever amount you have in mind, provided we get the apple in the end." Alina smiled politely, but inwardly, she was imagining scenes where her boss was screaming Kent’s name.</p>
<p>"Since I will be dealing with some idiots after this, why don’t I leave it with you then? You can give it to whoever this person is.</p>
<p>Just tell them that if they want to know the price, they should meet me a day before the start of the Pill Master Competition at the Evermore Restaurant for lunch.</p>
<p>I will tell them the price by then. In that case, they can use the apple, considering keeping it exposed for too long will lower its potency."</p>
<p>Kent took out a jade box housing the apple and handed it to Alina.</p>
<p>"Thank you for your generosity, Mr. Kent," Alina said.</p>
<p>"I will be on my way then," he said before walking away, making sure the one watching from the shadows didn’t suspect anything.</p>
<p>He returned, paid the trillions of spirit stones, collected everything, and stored the items inside his tower storage before leaving.</p>
<p>He went straight to Uncle Drew, who was waiting for him and ready to take him away from Vastport City. They wouldn’t be returning to the sect, as he wasn’t planning to go back there just yet.</p>
<p>The next time he appears in the sect, he will feared by many. He will make sure of that.</p>
<p>"Where to, young master?" Uncle Drew asked.</p>
<p>"Take me to Caprath City, then you can go back and handle your business. I will deal with the rest on my own."</p>
<p>"But young master..."</p>
<p>"Don’t worry, Uncle Drew. I’ve been training for a long time, and I need this exercise. More bodies mean more challenges, so the faster you get your business done in the underworld and hell, the faster we can rescue the two ladies."</p>
<p>Uncle Drew was skeptical about leaving his young master. However, Kent wasn’t about to allow him to stay, considering he still hadn’t fully recovered from the night hunting.</p>
<p>He also has to get to the underworld and take the souls to be used for his Hell Demon project. This is the only way he can help him now, and Kent, who knew this, wasn’t about to allow him to delay that.</p>
<p>"I will take you there, master."</p>
<p>Kent nodded, and then they left. They could already sense people following them and others hiding, but Kent was already planning how best to welcome them.</p>
<p>Meanwhile, three figures could now be seen standing in front of 20 Root Great Stage cultivators. They all wore frowns on their faces, and from the look of things, they were ready for a massacre.</p>
<p>"Move out. According to the tracker following that brat, he is headed toward Caprath City. We must catch up to him before the others get to him.</p>
<p>Remember, your job is to kill him. We will handle whatever hidden trump cards he has up his sleeves."</p>
<p>"Yes, Saints," the 20 replied. They were the strike force sent after Kent.</p>
<p>The three Metal Saints, who hadn’t managed to secure anything tangible in this year’s auction, were now prepared to go after Kent with the intent to kill him and take his items.</p>
<p>And it wasn’t just them.</p>
<p>The Bat, who had come after him due to his wealth, had also started making his moves. The next few hours, depending on how Kent handled it, would see Kent trying to survive the onslaught from multiple angles.</p>
