<p>"He is all yours, Lady Adina." Han tossed Monarch Aethros aside and took a seat on Baldy’s large sword.</p>
<p>Adina smiled and extended her arm, manifesting a pink leaf. She brought it closer to Monarch Aethros’s head, and it merged with his head. After a few minutes, pink mist spread across his skin, and then he passed out for good this time.</p>
<p>"Once he wakes up, he will become my first-ever Overlord who will lead my Charmed Ones in battle. I can’t wait for when we go on another massacre."</p>
<p>Adina giggled and, with a wave of her hand, Monarch Aethros vanished, appearing somewhere in the Legion Tower. He would be cared for by the maids she had mesmerized.</p>
<p>While they weren’t hardened killers like the guards and members of the Grim Arcane Legends, they weren’t exactly innocent either. So serving as aides to the Succubus Queen was better than being maids for killers and evil men.</p>
<p>"Now, only Big Sister Pandora and Lady Eliza remain." Baldy looked at the two remaining Battlefield Disks. Everyone remained alert, ready to act in case the opposite of their expectations happened.</p>
<p>However, when the next disk opened and Eliza stepped out, everyone’s eyes widened.</p>
<p>While everyone else walked out either dragging, tossing, or holding their opponent, Eliza appeared seated on the back of a dragon.</p>
<p>Naturally, the dragon was Monarch Drakonis.</p>
<p>Before the battle, he was considered the one to look out for in terms of physical strength. He, after all, was a dragon, and dragons were known for their brute strength.</p>
<p>However, aside from his brute strength, he was also known for his arrogance. All dragons were, and that was also perfectly normal.</p>
<p>The most chilling part, however, was the kind of person Monarch Drakonis was, or more precisely, had been. He was overly arrogant, and because of his position in the Grim Arcane Legends, he considered himself above all others.</p>
<p>Because of the image he had built for himself, the last thing anyone would expect to see him do was allow someone to sit on his back. That was something he would never do, even if his life were on the line.</p>
<p>But he had done just what nobody expected him to do.</p>
<p>He had allowed Eliza to sit on his back, legs crossed.</p>
<p>As he landed, one could tell that even in his true dragon form, he was obedient. And when he transformed back into his humanoid form, everyone could finally see that the proud and arrogant dragon had submitted, and that was just too shocking.</p>
<p>"How?" Vexthra asked, not understanding how the person she was worried about had actually turned out to be fine, perhaps too okay to even wonder if she had indeed fought against a Primordial.</p>
<p>"I have beaten him into submission," Eliza said, a smile spreading across her face.</p>
<p>Her friends could only look at her with eyes that seemed to ask, just who are you?</p>
<p>If only they knew the kind of monster they were worried about. Eliza was the one who trained the Blood Spear King. She made sure he learned how to fight, and when he was ready, she sent him into the Blood Trial.</p>
<p>She was the one who forged him, and so to see the Blood Spear King as a monster on the battlefield was to see the one who forged him as a monster among monsters.</p>
<p>Eliza just smiled and waved everyone off. For her, this wasn’t the first time she had done something mind-blowing. In the past, she had done so much that many had even begun to wonder whether she had a limit.</p>
<p>So this win wasn’t too surprising to her.</p>
<p>"Zaila, take him as my first of many gifts to you. I am sure he will be a great Thrall." Eliza offered the poor bastard to Zaila, who beamed with joy.</p>
<p>Zaila was clearly jealous of Adina when she managed to mesmerize a Primordial. Now, she had also gotten one, and from what the two ladies had shown in this battle, in the next battle, even if they went against three times this number, they would have Primordials to protect them and buy them more than enough time to mesmerize or enthrall more warriors.</p>
<p>"I call dibs on the last one." Yi Lai, the Undead Dragon Necromancer, had no plans to remain on the sidelines for long.</p>
<p>"You don’t even have the qualifications to have a Primordial-level undead dragon, Yi Lai. Why ask for something you cannot use?"</p>
<p>Adina, already having this wild imagination, wondered whether, since Zaila could not enthrall two Primordials at the same time, she might as well get lucky with this one.</p>
<p>Her powers grew as she added more people to her legion of Charmed Ones.</p>
<p>The more she mesmerized her opponents, the stronger she became, so she possessed the power to charm two or more Primordials as long as they were at the point where saying no or resisting wasn’t an option anymore.</p>
<p>"I am already at the peak of the Demigod stage. Soon, I will become a God, so who is to say I can’t have a Primordial-level Undead Dragon under my control by then?"</p>
<p>Yi Lai obviously had no plans to back down from obtaining her first Primordial, even though it was only a possibility right now.</p>
<p>Unfortunately for the two ladies, when the last battlefield disk opened and Pandora stepped out, her opponent was already dead, a black spear oozing with the power of evil embedded in his chest.</p>
<p>It was both chilling and frightening.</p>
<p>However, the ladies understood that when it came to this big sister of theirs, she wasn’t someone they could understand easily. She wielded the power of True Evil, and as such, she could not be compared to warriors of this universe.</p>
<p>Her power was beyond this universe, so ending her opponent while still looking as elegant and flawless as ever was perfectly normal.</p>
<p>In the end, the body was given to Princess Dominique, who was a Primordial, so turning another Primordial into one of her undead warriors was within her reach.</p>
<p>Crimson Dagger and his men stood on the sidelines, watching all this unfold with shocked expressions.</p>
<p>"As expected of the wives of the Faceless Assassin. Every last one of them is a monster," Crimson Dagger muttered, and the men nodded.</p>
<p>After a few more minutes spent looting, Crimson Dagger and the others all retreated back to the Legion Tower.</p>
<p>Once those who were temporarily dead awakened, they would throw a party to welcome Inkala back home and celebrate the complete annihilation of the Grim Arcane Legends.</p>
