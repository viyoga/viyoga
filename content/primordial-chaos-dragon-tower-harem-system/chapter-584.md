<p>"So you are telling us the ancient path to becoming a supreme starts from the very beginning of cultivation, and that Primordial Spirits are even better versions of god bodies," Vexthra asked, having heard what Kent had to say.</p>
<p>"Yes," Kent replied. "Per what the Serpent Queen told me, starting from Tier 1 till you become a Supreme is much better than when you get to the god stage.</p>
<p>And I think she is right. She claimed she was the most intelligent person during the primordial era, so I think she wouldn’t lie to herself."</p>
<p>"She is telling the truth. I truly have the ancient technique she is talking about. After the awakening, it appeared in my mind. However, there is a catch." Zaila’s primordial spirit appeared and said.</p>
<p>"What catch?" Kent asked.</p>
<p>"The technique allows me to start the path right now. However, I will undergo tribulation every time I make a breakthrough. Not that it’s something I should be scared of, but I can’t help but wonder, what happens to my main body after I regress to Tier 1?"</p>
<p>"According to the Serpent Queen, you just have to use this technique to strip your cultivation base to Tier One. However, since you can’t just strip it without knowing where to put it, she recommended you use it to expand your mind space.</p>
<p>This way, not only will it help your main body, but it will also help you hasten your cultivation. And the best part: since you live in a mind space, you can continue to grow regardless of the world you find yourself in.</p>
<p>There will be no limitations on your growth. And we both know the stronger you are, the better for your main body."</p>
<p>"This is too much to take in. I consulted many Supremes, but none ever had even the faintest idea that the path to supremacy can start from the very first stage in cultivation."</p>
<p>Vexthra was really shocked to hear something this bizarre. But she knew Kent wouldn’t lie to her, and even if he did, it wasn’t like he would gain anything from it.</p>
<p>So he wouldn’t lie to them.</p>
<p>This begs the question: What will become of the main body as the Primordial body undergoes the tribulation?</p>
<p>This question alone holds so much that she couldn’t begin to comprehend. At the god stage, whenever a god body passes a tribulation, the main body becomes several times power.</p>
<p>So, what will their main body become while the tribulation starts from Tier 1?</p>
<p>"This means, Vex, you will also undergo the same training path. I will awaken your True Name, and then with the Primordial Chambers, you can awaken your primordial spirits.</p>
<p>Once that is accomplished, you will use the same method to regress your cultivation to the first tier and start again.</p>
<p>Don’t worry. Since your main body won’t be affected, you will still be a True God and even start the other path, the DAO path, with your main body while your primordial body undergoes the Ancient path."</p>
<p>"Would that even be possible?" Vexthra asked. "If what happened to Zaila happens to me, then the chances of me coming out with a supreme Primordial Spirit are high.</p>
<p>Asking to lower it to the first stage seems like a lot of work and probably a waste."</p>
<p>"True. However, from what I know, using your cultivation base to expand your mind space or soul space will not only help your main body, but will also expand your mind or soul, depending on the one you awaken.</p>
<p>This means that even though you will still be a True God, your chances of having the power to fight, perhaps as a God King, God Emperor, or even a God Monarch, are very high.</p>
<p>In my opinion, it won’t be a waste simply because you will be expanding your soul or mind, which benefits you greatly."</p>
<p>"I understand. I guess I will do what you suggest, Kent. You are my husband, so I will trust and listen to you," Vexthra smiled. "Even if you want to wipe out all my cultivation and make me start from the start in both my spirit and main body, I wouldn’t complain."</p>
<p>Her sisters smiled while nodding.</p>
<p>They all trust their man, so they wouldn’t hesitate to do as he says.</p>
<p>This sight made Kent happy.</p>
<p>"In that case, Zaila, you should start your training now." Kent gave her the technique and watched her sit and start to cultivate.</p>
<p>In truth, her primordial spirit was the one doing the cultivating. However, the two wanted to do it together since this was an uncharted path.</p>
<p>Thankfully, the technique isn’t that complex, so they grasped it in under two hours.</p>
<p>"I will start now," Zaila said, forming a hand seal.</p>
<p>The moment she did, inside her mind space, a spell diagram appeared. Her primordial spirit stepped in, and like falling from grace, her cultivation base started to regress.</p>
<p>Meanwhile, the energy coming from her was being absorbed by her mind space, quickly expanding it. First, it expanded to about 60,000 square miles on both sides, then it started to change.</p>
<p>The river changed to a sea simply because the place became enormous, and the water body expanded so large that it couldn’t be called a river anymore.</p>
<p>The sea creatures inside also started to develop, some becoming larger and fiercer; however, none tried to attack each other.</p>
<p>By the time her cultivation base reached Tier 12 from Tier 18, the sea was filled with creatures that had become extinct after the primordial era.</p>
<p>The infamous Sea Dragons were now filling the sea, making it both beautiful and dangerous. But it wasn’t only the Sea Dragons that appeared inside the sea.</p>
<p>The Crystal Shark, one of the deadliest sea creatures during the primordial era, also appeared inside the sea.</p>
<p>When her cultivation base hit Tier One, her mind space became so large and filled with energy that if someone below Core Formation were to stand before this large sea, they would pass out.</p>
<p>On the outside, Kent and his women continued to widen their eyes as they stared at Zaila.</p>
<p>Though her cultivation base was at the peak of the Root Ascension stage, her aura had grown so much that even someone at the peak of the Core Formation stage would be suffocated.</p>
<p>Only one person had ever exuded this kind of aura: the leader of the Hand. That overwhelming pressure, that suffocating presence—Zaila had that now.</p>
<p>Her aura alone is enough to render those at the Core Formation stage useless. Perhaps someone with five evolutions under their belt can handle her, but for now, she had proven something that made Kent want all his wives to have:</p>
<p>They must possess Mind Space or Soul Space at all costs.</p>
