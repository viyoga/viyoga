<p>Inside the forgotten temple, the Enchantress wore a panicked expression, watching the same thing Lady Uninverse was watching...the sight of what Kent was going through.</p>
<p>"This wasn’t how it should have gone. He wasn’t supposed to remember something that was made up. This was all supposed to be an illusion... so why is he remembering what isn’t there?" she muttered.</p>
<p>"It’s straightforward," Lady Uninverse suddenly spoke. "It’s because of that event. It fractured his mind and still lingers after all these years."</p>
<p>"Then at this rate, it’s only a matter of time before he remembers, and when that happens, there will be chaos everywhere," the Enchantress said with a panicked expression.</p>
<p>"What should we do?" she asked.</p>
<p>"We do nothing. Interfering will only make things worse, so we do nothing. At this rate, the only ones that can help him are his wives, so we should leave everything to them."</p>
<p>"What if they can’t?" the Enchantress asked.</p>
<p>"Then we prepare for the worst. But I have no doubt they will, because from what I just witnessed, he loved so much. Meaning they are the only ones that can reach him...</p>
<p>If this love is enough for him to threaten to destroy the universe, then I have no doubt he will be able to overcome that part of himself when the time comes with the help of his ladies.</p>
<p>"I hope you are right this time too... if not, I can only wonder what would happen when he gets angry." The two cosmic ladies sighed and continued to watch the events.</p>
<p>---</p>
<p>The lady stared at Kent in shock for a few minutes, digesting what he said. After a long, hard digestion of Kent’s clear declaration that he would destroy the universe and everything it contains, the lady snapped her fingers.</p>
<p>The space around them shattered, and Kent found himself back inside the boat, only this time, there was a destination in sight.</p>
<p>A painful headache rocked his mind when he woke up, making him close his eyes and grit his teeth for a few minutes before he calmed down.</p>
<p>"It was just an illusion..." Kent’s eyes turned cold; however, a familiar voice entered his mind the next second, making him take a deep breath.</p>
<p>⟪Master, you don’t have to worry. All you saw was just an illusion created by a powerful mind cultivator. I don’t know, but her consciousness still remains, and you are going to her now.</p>
<p>Don’t worry. Your mind is safe, and whatever memory you have absorbed is but an illusion. Don’t worry too much about it⟫, Dori spoke, and Kent nodded.</p>
<p>’Thank you, Dori.’ Though he knew it was an illusion, still, he still couldn’t shake the helplessness he felt when he saw his wife’s lifeless body hanging at the dam gate.</p>
<p>A few minutes later, the boat docked, and Kent jumped down. The space around him twisted the moment his leg touched the shore.</p>
<p>This time, he appeared inside a lush garden where a beautiful lady who looked exactly like Zaila was watering flowers.</p>
<p>Though she was much more mature and beautiful, Kent’s eyes wouldn’t lie to him; it was Zaila, true and accurate.</p>
<p>"Before anything, I have three questions for you," Kent said, not lowering his guard.</p>
<p>Though Dori said this was just a strand of consciousness that belonged to the Mind Cultivator, the fact that she was able to mess with his mind that much was enough to tell him she was dangerous.</p>
<p>So he wasn’t lowering his guard.</p>
<p>"Go ahead..." the lady said.</p>
<p>"First question, are you Zaila’s past self?"</p>
<p>"Second, was any of what you said real? And I mean real in the sense that out of the infinite possibilities, I lost my wives, and the end result was hatred."</p>
<p>"And lastly, would the memories I somehow got influence me?" Kent asked the question in one breath, showing how serious he was.</p>
<p>The lady looked at him long and hard, and then she spoke, "I will answer your questions, but before that, answer one of mine first.</p>
<p>Would you really destroy the entire universe for your wives?"</p>
<p>"Yes." She wasn’t even done, but Kent replied, shocking not only her, but two other women listening in on this.</p>
<p>"Why?" the lady asked.</p>
<p>"You said only one question."</p>
<p>"Show a girl some love, would you? And don’t worry, I will let you in on a secret about the memories before you leave, so it’s not like I will be cheating you here."</p>
<p>Kent took a deep breath, walked toward a table, sat down, and poured himself some tea. He also poured one for the lady.</p>
<p>"Well then, why would you destroy the universe for your women?" she asked after taking a sip.</p>
<p>"I am not an idiot, you know. I am someone who, from when I was very young, knew life requires sacrifice. Now, I can start seeing that I wasn’t kidding by saying that.</p>
<p>I have no idea who or what I am. My true self is still locked behind a bundle of memories that have yet to be retrieved.</p>
<p>However, I do know my life is to protect this universe. I know that is my destiny. However, I wouldn’t ask the universe to protect or look after me while I am at it. If she asks, I will say, ’Look after my wives while I am away.’</p>
<p>So when I return and something I went out to protect fails to protect what is dear to me, then what was my sacrifice for? Did I perhaps choose not to sleep in the arms and spend time with my wives just so I can protect everyone else, all for nothing?</p>
<p>If that is not the case, then I want to return to a happy family. But if I return and everything is as I came to meet it in the illusion, I wouldn’t hesitate to reduce this universe to rubble, knowing those I protected were not worthy of living."</p>
<p>"Many innocents will die, you know," the lady said, but Kent smiled and said,</p>
<p>"A wise man once said, the innocent is just someone who hasn’t committed a crime yet."</p>
<p>Three ladies shuddered, hearing that...</p>
