<p>⟦Congratulations, you are now the guardian of the Grayvine Foxkin.⟧</p>
<p>⟦New Guardian Tools unlocked in the Guardian Tower⟧</p>
<p>These two messages made Kent smile. This was confirmation that he had indeed added a new race to his Guardian Tower.</p>
<p>The first was expected, but the second was something he never expected. This was because, up until today, the only two tools he had in the Guardian Tower were the Shadow Spider he used to spy on and kill Bright Faywood.</p>
<p>The other was the Battle Dolls, which, from what he knew so far, would one day become the 14 lost knights. This made him wonder just what had been added now.</p>
<p>But aside from that, he was just happy he had added the Foxkin to his Guardian Tower because the rewards were immeasurable.</p>
<p>He moved on to some of the messages, which mostly discussed the responsibilities he had to fulfill as the guardian of an entire race. He had seen most of those already when he added Li Hua several months back.</p>
<p>But aside from that, he received some rewards, which included the Immortal Grade Ambrosia apple he gave them and the Tier 17 schematics for the array he gave to Sir Ashfur.</p>
<p>The tower seemed to know what he needed at the moment as a guardian and provided just that. That showed he had indeed become the guardian of an entire race.</p>
<p>However, there was more: the reward he would receive from the Grayvine Foxkin race. He had chosen to receive soul essence, and so far, he had something to brag about.</p>
<p>⟦Soul Essence: 1,270,000,600⟧</p>
<p>The soul essence Kent had in reserve was already enough to take him from Tier 13 to Tier 16 in soul cultivation if he wanted. However, Kent didn’t even think about doing that; he knew storing the soul essence would come in handy soon.</p>
<p>Having billions of soul essence was also good, but knowing why he was storing it, he could only wait to see what he could create.</p>
<p>Kent didn’t want to say it, but he had already started to wonder just what type of Soul Path was waiting for him to traverse.</p>
<p>But if the tower had already begun storing soul essence for him, then he could only wait and see what amazing path awaited him.</p>
<p>"Now, back to the Guardian Tools." Kent turned to the tools. He wanted to see if his next batch of tools would be enough to protect the Foxkin if need be.</p>
<p>He quickly accessed the message, and to his shock, the new additions were just two, but each was a powerful tool that he could use.</p>
<p>The first was a 3-meter-tall metallic being. It was like Veydris, but this one was much bigger, muscular, and wielded two deadly blades.</p>
<p>One of the swords seemed to hover behind him while he held on to another. It was clear that he was a skilled swordsman; the sword behind him seemed to act like a spirit weapon.</p>
<p>This made Kent wonder what this swordsman was made of or what went into making such a fighter. The second has the same design, but it was an archer.</p>
<p>⟦Name: Guardian Swordsman⟧</p>
<p>⟦Grade: Tier 14⟧</p>
<p>⟦Rank: SSS⟧</p>
<p>⟦Description: An impeccable swordsman meant to help the guardian in protecting his flocks.⟧</p>
<p>⟦Note: If this guardian’s tool is used in a Medium world, the rank will regress to Tier 12. The grade and rank are upgradable.⟧</p>
<p>⟦Price: 50,000,000 Tower Points⟧</p>
<p>---</p>
<p>⟦Name: Guardian Archer⟧</p>
<p>⟦Grade: Tier 14⟧</p>
<p>⟦Rank: SSS⟧</p>
<p>⟦Description: An impeccable archer meant to help the guardian in protecting his flocks.⟧</p>
<p>⟦Note: If this guardian’s tool is used in a Medium world, the rank will regress to Tier 12. The grade and rank are upgradable.⟧</p>
<p>⟦Price: 50,000,000 Tower Points⟧</p>
<p>"Interesting. To think I will be getting two powerful tools that are comparable to a Spirit King right off the bat. However, the price is a little too much. But I can handle it." Kent smiled.</p>
<p>He didn’t know if this was something he could really focus on to guard the Grayvine Foxkin, but he knew having five of each would put up a good fight.</p>
<p>The best part was that they were SSS rank, which made them comparable to someone who had broken a limit. And while Kent knew they were less likely to lose to a peak Emperor, he knew they would struggle against a Spirit Lord.</p>
<p>However, there was something here that made them dangerous, and that was the fact that they wouldn’t tire. In combat, unless they were destroyed, they would never get tired.</p>
<p>That was enough to show they were meant to guard those he truly wanted to.</p>
<p>"So far, I only have one of each as a gift from the tower for adding an entire race to the tower. This means I can only station these two for now.</p>
<p>However, considering I have billions in tower points, I can go ahead and buy a few more.</p>
<p>I will be entering seclusion, and judging by the number of things I need to accomplish, I don’t know how long I will be gone for, so it is better to have a backup plan."</p>
<p>Kent nodded. With what he had saved up, he knew the chances of his staying in seclusion for years were likely.</p>
<p>Thankfully, time in the tower is much faster than outside, so he knew only a couple of months would pass on the outside, but that still didn’t change the fact that he needed to ensure they were safe while he was away.</p>
<p>"Tower, I will purchase four of each."</p>
<p>His tower points were deducted, and eight Guardian Tools were deposited into the tower storage. Adding to the rewards he got from the tower, he summoned ten powerful metallic beings, each radiating no aura, but just staring at them showed they were dangerous.</p>
<p>"This will do for now. As for the materials to upgrade them, I will give the list to the king; this is a High World—they will have more than a Medium World could offer."</p>
<p>Kent nodded with satisfaction.</p>
<p>So far, as a guardian, he had already ensured the future of those he was protecting was catered for. The only thing left was for him to get back to his business and watch his Soul Essence rise every damn second.</p>
<p>"This is the best," Kent smiled and vanished back to Fedora.</p>
