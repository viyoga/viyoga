<p>"So, are you going to give me a ship or not?" Kent asked.</p>
<p>After sacrificing 10 days, the tower told him that he couldn’t travel directly to Planet Seamus Prime from Planet Nictus. The only way to get there is through one of the six worlds currently under the control of the Planet King.</p>
<p>However, it would take him 4 days to get to the nearest one and another 3 days to reach Planet Seamus Prime. However, if he has a ship and uses the star coordinates the tower gave him, he will reach Planet Seamus Prime before the forbidden wedding takes place.</p>
<p>Kent only needs a ship, and he will bring hell to Planet Seamus Prime.</p>
<p>He doesn’t need any help, and he most certainly isn’t in the right frame of mind to tolerate childish behavior.</p>
<p>"I can’t give you a ship. However, the King can if you can really bring back her daugh... Commander Agnes," General Brianna said, and Kent, who was in a hurry, nodded.</p>
<p>"Then bring me to the king. The more we waste time, the more danger they are in."</p>
<p>General Brianna nodded and gestured for the other generals to follow.</p>
<p>Kent didn’t know if this was some form of trap to take him down. However, he knew one thing: if the king dares make a fuss, he will kill him and take over Planet Nictus...</p>
<p>If he was able to pop down to hell and return as a King, then becoming a King of a planet is not something he will struggle with...</p>
<p>On their way, General Brianna informed the appropriate persons, so when they reached the palace, every last soldier was at attention, weapons ready.</p>
<p>Kent merely smirked and followed General Brianna inside. Yu Lan and the other two beasts entered the palace, too.</p>
<p>They wanted to fight when the planet king attacked... every last beast did. However, realizing the strength of their opponents, Lady Kami advised them to stand down...</p>
<p>Naturally, the Planet King came for them, so after getting them, he left.</p>
<p>Now, knowing Kent planned to rescue them, the three beast companions of the three ladies who had been kidnapped wanted nothing more than to get them back.</p>
<p>When they entered the hall, the first person they saw was the king seated on his throne, with four bodyguards standing behind him.</p>
<p>On his left was another throne, and the person seated inside was the queen, the king’s main wife.</p>
<p>’An Elf King, huh. I thought only Queens ruled the Elven race.’ This was Kent’s first time meeting an Elven King. However, he showed no reaction.</p>
<p>There were also ten Elven elders in the throne room.</p>
<p>Kent didn’t mind the atmosphere. Perhaps before coming to the Past, he would have felt pressured by their auras. However, now that his bloodline had seen a significant awakening, they didn’t measure up to him in any way.</p>
<p>General Brianna went down on her knees when she was close enough...</p>
<p>Kent remained standing...</p>
<p>"My King, this is Kent, the one Commander Agnes talked about."</p>
<p>"Nice meeting you, Kent," King Yesron said, his appraising eyes scanning Kent.</p>
<p>"Glad to meet you, too." Kent looked at the expression on the King’s face, and then he looked at the other Elven elders and immediately knew what was going on in their minds.</p>
<p>However, he had no time to entertain them.</p>
<p>"Pardon my arrogance, King Yesron, but I need a ship, and General Brianna said you are the only one who can provide me one. So here I am..."</p>
<p>Kent’s tone was rude and plain. However, he made it clear why he was there. He had no time to flatter the king when he already felt negative energy coming from them.</p>
<p>"Rude... A mere human lowlife. How dare you be rude before King Yesron? Do you have a death wish?" One of the elders shouted, his finger pointed at Kent.</p>
<p>General Brianna is a Human, a Flame Elemental Human.</p>
<p>When she told Kent she couldn’t give him a ship, she lied. She could give him a ship, but she knew the Elven elders and the king would make a fuss, especially when the ship was going to a place they didn’t dare mess with.</p>
<p>Of course, there was another reason that Kent noticed when he entered the throne room.</p>
<p>When Kent saw the look in the queen’s eyes, he instantly realized she didn’t care whether Commander Agnes lived or died.</p>
<p>Also, he could tell the King was a henpecked king, someone wrapped around the queen’s finger.</p>
<p>This made him understand the real reason why General Brianna brought him there. Naturally, Kent was already angry, so he walked much closer and spoke in a clear tone, one that told the people gathered he meant business.</p>
<p>"I need a ship. You will give me a ship. If not, this place will become very unbearable to you all."</p>
<p>"Activate formation!" Suddenly, one of the generals who had followed them shouted, and Kent, who was already aware of this formation, smirked.</p>
<p>"What are you doing? He is here to save the Princess, who has been captured," General Brianna shouted.</p>
<p>Kent turned and looked at the expression of loss on General Brianna’s face and chuckled...</p>
<p>"They don’t care about her, General. They only allowed me here to get the Soul Flame Fruit I have."</p>
<p>Kent turned to the queen. "Isn’t that right?"</p>
<p>She frowned but answered, "Hand over the fruits you have in your possession and allow yourself to be branded by a slave collar. That is the only way you get to live."</p>
<p>Kent smiled weakly and then spoke. "I wanted to break your legs, but let you live. But I have changed my mind."</p>
<p>Kent activated the taming bracelet, and at once, 50 powerful beasts appeared.</p>
<p>"Kill them all."</p>
<p>In an instant, screams of pain and anguish filled the palace.</p>
<p>Kent turned to General Brianna. "The ship, please."</p>
<p>She shuddered, then waved her hand, and a small ship appeared. Kent caught it and started moving toward the entrance.</p>
<p>"I am coming with you," General Brianna ran after him. Kent didn’t stop her. With or without her, he would be killing the Planet King.</p>
<p>Yu Lan and the other beasts also followed.</p>
<p>Now that Kent was making a kingdom for her sister, she knew the next best thing was to save her and present a new kingdom, born out of blood... a gift handed to her by her future husband.</p>
<p>In no time, Kent was off-world and started heading toward Planet Seamus Prime.</p>
<p>Meanwhile, back in the palace, the King, along with the Queen, the elders, the guards, and everyone who had pointed their weapons at Kent, were being slaughtered.</p>
<p>With the seven monarchs leading the charge, no one was able to resist.</p>
<p>After three hours, everyone was dead.</p>
<p>"Orvath and Zeraphine, stay behind and make sure the Kingdom is ready for her Queen," Sylvara ordered, and the two monarchs nodded.</p>
<p>The rest turned into mist and appeared inside the taming bracelet.</p>
