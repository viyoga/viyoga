<p>Vexthra coming to the tower wasn’t just to see her sister and spend every day having sex with her husband. She had no plans to waste time.</p>
<p>Her major plan was to turn her sisters into powerhouses.</p>
<p>She is a god, so until Kent enters the god realm or finds himself in a unique world, she won’t be able to help him with anything combat-wise.</p>
<p>She is basically useless in that aspect, so as someone who had given him the quest to kill the God of Alchemy, she knew Kent would need all the help he could get.</p>
<p>This made her invest her strength in her sisters.</p>
<p>Unbeknownst to her, the simple human she gave this mega quest to was actually someone the universe fancied in all ways and forms.</p>
<p>Kent, who was meant to rise through the ranks and one day challenge the God of Alchemy, was actually badass from the start.</p>
<p>And this greatness now started to affect her sisters when Kent started bestowing True Names on them. She basically had her job cut in half for her.</p>
<p>So her coming was to prepare her sisters to reach the stage where, whenever their man was in danger, they would be able to help him.</p>
<p>That was why she never let any of them slack off when she started their training.</p>
<p>She first used the goddess’s powers and resources to move their spirit Roots from the Ruby stage to the Divine stage, which is only two grades away from reaching the Celestial grade—the peak of this universe.</p>
<p>This alone lifted their prowess from ordinary to a level they never thought possible. Even Elaine, who was basically clueless about combat, has now gained the power to protect herself from someone like her late master.</p>
<p>Vexthra didn’t hold back, and since her main plan was to ensure her man never lacked, she made sure the ladies she wanted in his life never lacked either.</p>
<p>After their upgrade, they tempered their bodies using some of the most valuable herbs in the universe. She really came prepared.</p>
<p>After tempering their bodies for nearly three months, she started them on a new regimen requiring them to climb to the 300th step in the Golden Titan Stairway.</p>
<p>Since she could use her goddess powers, she ensured they used the pressure to temper their bodies further. It was a difficult task for them, but she made sure none got stuck, especially those without True Names yet.</p>
<p>The first to reach the 300th step was Lady Vari, who, for the past few months, had fully committed to the training Vexthra designed for her.</p>
<p>Her cultivation was reset to the Peak of the Sovereign stage, but she wasn’t angry. In fact, at her current cultivation stage, she felt more powerful than before.</p>
<p>In the Assassin profession, there are different ranks, just like there are ranks in swordsmanship or spearmanship. Assassins also have ranks.</p>
<p>They start from Rank 1, which is akin to a Master Swordsman. Then there’s Rank 2, which is a Sword Grandmaster.</p>
<p>In the assassin path, they call it the Shadow Grandmaster.</p>
<p>Lady Vari is a Shadow Sage, which makes her Rank 4. Vexthra wants her to become Rank 5—a Shadow King. This would make her one of the best and deadliest in the world when it comes to assassination.</p>
<p>To help her gain great control over the shadows, Vexthra lowered her cultivation base. Now, she would have to study the shadows and rise from there.</p>
<p>Thankfully, Kent ensured her disciple’s safety, so she wasn’t worried about her. This allowed her to throw herself into the training so intensely that, when Vexthra started polluting her mind, she couldn’t resist.</p>
<p>And it wasn’t just her. Elaine was the same, and now she just wants Kent to come back and spread their legs.</p>
<p>She knew the two wouldn’t miraculously become her sisters, so she did her best to ensure they walked the path themselves.</p>
<p>This way, when Kent comes back, they would get curious themselves and perhaps want to get in his pants after hearing all there is to hear about him.</p>
<p>The ladies really put a lot into their training.</p>
<p>The first to reach the 300th step was Lady Vari, who was awarded the Body Transcendence Pill.</p>
<p>Thanks to that, and the fact that she was already at the Silver Body stage after the herbal bath, she stepped into the Gold Body stage, making not only her body refined but also her beauty shoot up by leaps and bounds.</p>
<p>She became so hot that if Kent were to see her in just her pants and tank top—considering Vexthra basically made them wear clothes that don’t really do much to cover their bodies—his dragon rod would probably raise its head.</p>
<p>The lady is beautiful by all standards, but her body form and shape have changed after becoming a gold body cultivator.</p>
<p>Nara was the second to reach the 300th step. She also received the Body Transcendence Pill, which enabled her to break through to the Gold Body stage.</p>
<p>Like Lady Vari, she had also become a damsel to die for. And while her cultivation base is only at the Peak of the Transcendent stage, she has a True Name, so she was basically a beauty even before becoming a Gold Body cultivator.</p>
<p>But after she reached the 300th level, her form developed to the point that even her sisters gulped when looking at her.</p>
<p>Neomi was third, also gaining the same reward. Mara and Lady Lani were the fourth to reach the 300th step, then Camilla followed. Selene and Ingrid reached at the same time.</p>
<p>Elsa and Val also reached the 300th step at the same time, followed by Lilian and Unity. Alina and Elaine came last. However, this all happened within 30 days—something even Vexthra never expected.</p>
<p>Still, she was impressed watching every last one of them reach this level in their cultivation.</p>
<p>"How are you ladies feeling?" Vexthra asked, hovering above them. She is a goddess, so the pressure wasn’t having any effect on her.</p>
<p>"I feel like I can punch a hole into a mountain," Unity answered, flexing her muscles—muscles that would make any man gulp if they saw her shape.</p>
<p>"Good. You’ll all need this energy for when our man returns."</p>
<p>"Big sister..." The ladies—every last one of them—blushed upon hearing her say that. Not that it was the first time she said something like it.</p>
<p>’Our big sister is a shameless goddess.’ That was what Unity had said to her the first time she made such a comment. Naturally, they all laughed, and she even puffed her chest, not denying the claim.</p>
