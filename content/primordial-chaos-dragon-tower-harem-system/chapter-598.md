<p>"Master, is he a new addition to the legion?" Kaizo was the first to approach Kent when he arrived in the Legion Tower. The others weren’t far behind.</p>
<p>"I don’t know, Kaizo, but if he proves himself useful, he might get the chance to join the legion one day," Kent said, making Shax turn and look around him.</p>
<p>Sylara was there, and she wasn’t the only one creating an intimidating atmosphere for Shax. All the legions were standing around, excluding Veydris and Irina, who were training inside the void.</p>
<p>The lineup caused Shax to break into a cold sweat. However, Kent just smiled and waved his legion away.</p>
<p>"I want to have a chat with Shax here, so give us the room." The modded and left, leaving him and Shax, who was visibly shocked.</p>
<p>"I know what you are thinking. What would have happened if I had lied or perhaps tried to fight back? The answer is simple: you would lose badly, of course. That would be too bad for you, but I doubt you care about that more than your life, so let’s have a nice chat, shall we?"</p>
<p>Shax nodded like an obedient child. Kent just smiled and sat down.</p>
<p>"First question: How strong is the Sea Race?" Kent knew Shax wouldn’t know much, but he also knew that for someone privy to details of what happened 25 thousand years ago, he was no simple person.</p>
<p>To him, Shax is rotten, but he is an asset...</p>
<p>"I don’t know their inner strength, but from what I saw when we tried to have a talk with them, I say they are powerful. Back then, the Fire, Ice, Blood, Incubus, Succubus, and the Dark Elves sent two people from each state to go to the Sea Race for a talk...</p>
<p>My king sent me to spy on them, not knowing I was part of them. But from what I saw, just one of the sea race people killed all 12 envoys sent. They all died within minutes, and while I am not certain, I can say the killer was a sea dragon.</p>
<p>However, some rumors say the Sea Race has three guards, and even one of them was the Leviathan that roams the depths of the sea.</p>
<p>But I am not certain. However, if the first guard, the sea dragon, could kill some of the powerful demons from the six states, then you get the picture."</p>
<p>Kent couldn’t deny that if the rumors, as Shax says, were true, then he wasn’t ready to face this race. However, he also knew he had to go there one of these days.</p>
<p>As to how he will do that will depend on his moves in the coming months.</p>
<p>"Since you don’t know much, then why don’t we focus on the rest? Among the Dragons, Beasts, Elves, Demons, and Dwarves, which is the strongest?"</p>
<p>"I can’t say for sure, but the Beasts, Dragons, and Elves can contend when it comes to the strength of their leaders. The Elf Queen is terrifying, and so is the Dragon Emperor.</p>
<p>I’ve never seen Lord Qilin of the Beast in action before, but I heard he defeated the leader of a secret organization called the Hand, even taking away her cultivation base.</p>
<p>She regressed to three cores instead of seven, so he is also powerful. But that is all I can say, taking the leaders into account.</p>
<p>However, when it comes to hidden power, I will give it to the Beasts; they have many experts and a powerful new generation of geniuses."</p>
<p>Shax was much more accepting, considering he was screwed if he made any stupid moves. The location he was in made him aware that the brat before him was like a god looking down at him.</p>
<p>So he was giving more context than required, which is helpful to Kent...</p>
<p>Kent just nodded, knowing he had indeed found a good asset to learn more about the outside world from.</p>
<p>"And when you say hidden power, are you talking in the sense of Ancestors of the race or what?" Kent asked.</p>
<p>"Something like that. But they are not the only ones. Most of their experts are in the ancient battlefield of Parting, so you won’t notice them. But when they are called back, you will know how powerful these races are.</p>
<p>Even we demons have many experts hidden away, waiting for a danger to come before they emerge."</p>
<p>"Intriguing. Looks like the outside world is much more fun than this human continent." Kent smiled, already planning his moves.</p>
<p>"You mentioned Dragon Emperor. How many species of dragons are under him?" Kent asked.</p>
<p>"Three species. There are the Crystal Dragons, the Flame Dragons, and the Earth Dragons. The Crystal Dragons are the strongest, but the other two are no pushovers either."</p>
<p>Kent nodded.</p>
<p>"How about the Elves? I learned the High Elves are no more." Shax nodded to his words, making Kent smile. He had met a High Elf before when he reviewed Jest’s memories.</p>
<p>Although dragons are arrogant, the High Elves are the worst kind of arrogant. They are annoying, too, so it’s good that they are no more.</p>
<p>"The last of them ascended long ago. However, the Half-elves are still around and have their own kingdom. Though they are under the elves, so we consider them one."</p>
<p>"How about the one called the Pill Princess?" Kent asked yet another question, but this time, Shax widened his eyes.</p>
<p>"What, is it forbidden to talk about her?" Kent asked, noticing Shax’s expression...</p>
<p>"No. I am just shocked you know of her. Looks like her influence has also reached this far." A smile appeared on his face.</p>
<p>It was not a perverted one; it was more like a proud smile, which looks bizarre considering he is a demon.</p>
<p>This deepened Kent’s interest in knowing this Pill Princess.</p>
<p>"Well, what about her? Is she like some national treasure that even you demons had to cherish and praise?"</p>
<p>"Something like that. She is the only disciple of the one and only Pill Emperor in the entire world.</p>
<p>Everybody respects her not because she is the princess of the Elven race, but because she is the disciple of a Pill Emperor and also a brilliant Pill Saint who can craft 100% pure pills up to Tier 8.</p>
<p>And in case you don’t know, Tier 8 is higher than the stage a Pill Saint can handle.</p>
<p>She hadn’t upgraded her ranking to become a Pill Sage mainly because she wanted to have seven enlightenments in the Pill Path before becoming a Pill Sage.</p>
<p>She had already completed five.</p>
<p>Recently, I learned she even concocted a Tier 9 pure pill, showing how amazing she is. But aside from her clearly impressive feats in alchemy, she is also a core formation expert and has already completed six core evolutions," Shax said with a smile.</p>
<p>"It looks like she is impressive," Kent smiled. "How do I get close to her and challenge her to a duel?"</p>
<p>"Errh?" Shax’s jaw was left hanging...</p>
