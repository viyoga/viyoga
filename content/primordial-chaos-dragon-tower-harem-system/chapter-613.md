<p>"I will come fetch you when the king and the elders decide on your request," Princess Arien said before turning around to leave.</p>
<p>"I will see you later, Sister Tala," Princess Lyra also said to Dori before running after her sister. The small mansion they were given has four rooms, so since there are only three of them, it was the perfect spot for them.</p>
<p>"I will be inside cultivating," Sword King Shawn said before walking away. Now that Dori had solved his problem, he wanted to start early.</p>
<p>His prowess would skyrocket if he managed to break his limit and reach the Sword Emperor stage.</p>
<p>His sword intent and aura had also reached the peak of the Profound stage, so the moment he reached the Sword Emperor stage, he would break into the Heavenly sword intent and sword aura stage.</p>
<p>This would take his swordsmanship to a different level.</p>
<p>In the field of swordsmanship, the intent and aura can grow as far as the Celestial grade. It starts from Basic, which is most used by sword adepts, to the Master stage.</p>
<p>The Grandmasters and a few saints use the Enhance stage until they reach the Profound stage. Most swordsmen get stuck at this stage, simply because they must at least gain three enlightenments in the sword intent or aura path.</p>
<p>Most call it Sword Energy enlightenment.</p>
<p>Without gaining the three enlightenments, they wouldn’t be able to reach the Heavenly sword energy stage.</p>
<p>However, while meeting this requirement is sufficient, merely reaching the Profound stage is enough to elevate one from Sword Saint to Sword Sage and ultimately to Sword King.</p>
<p>However, to reach the Emperor stage, you must attain all three enlightenments. At the Heavenly stage, Sword Emperors, Monarchs, and Sovereigns use it.</p>
<p>Then they move to the Divine sword energy stage, where Sword Legends and Sword Divines can also use it. After those stages, the rest will depend on whether the swordsman can become an immortal.</p>
<p>To unlock Immortal Sword Aura and intent (Sword Energy), they must become an immortal and at least comprehend their Sword Realm into a Sword Law stage.</p>
<p>It’s all complicated and tedious, but as one starts to form the Sword Realm, they must continue to improve, shape, and sharpen it.</p>
<p>Once they reach the Sword Divine stage, where they will be introduced to the various paths in comprehending the Sword Dao, they will be required to comprehend the Dao and, in doing so, upgrade their realm into a Law realm.</p>
<p>This isn’t just about having a realm; they would now have a law that is controlled and improved upon by them alone. The stronger your Sword Law, the stronger its influence on people.</p>
<p>Most call it a Law concept, and it isn’t just for sword cultivators; it is for everyone, where your conceptual understanding will determine your strength.</p>
<p>Most swordsmen spend thousands of years here, comprehending their Sword Law. But in the grand scheme, Sword Law is basically one’s understanding of the path they are following.</p>
<p>So, until they form a Sword Law and become immortals, becoming one of the Immortal Swords is nearly impossible.</p>
<p>Of course, most swordsmen choose to put their swords down at the Divine stage and instead become immortals since they couldn’t form Sword Law.</p>
<p>The Immortal Sword stage is the best part because the moment they reach the Sword God stage, most consider themselves at the peak.</p>
<p>The Sword Supreme and Sword Celestial stages are ones that not many can boldly say they want to rise to. It’s challenging and demanding, but many have reached the Celestial stage.</p>
<p>But one might ask, is the Celestial stage the pinnacle of the swordsmanship path? This question was asked billions of years ago, and to this day, the answer remains unknown.</p>
<p>Kent didn’t want to think too much about that either, so he just sat there as Sword King Shawn walked away.</p>
<p>Sword King Shawn had already gained three enlightenments in the Profound Sword Energy stage, so he was ready to reach the Heavenly Sword Energy stage. Then gradually, he would reach the Divine stage.</p>
<p>"I was right to bring him to this world, where he can grow much faster. This is even good; one day, I might just need his help."</p>
<p>After relaxing for a while since Dori went back to the tower to do some things Kent instructed her to, Kent decided to add his points to his stats.</p>
<p>He first checked how many attribute points he had.</p>
<p>[Attribute Points: 3,400,000]</p>
<p>"Looks like the Bloodvein tribe is doing better than I expected." Kent smiled, looking at the number of points he had left to add.</p>
<p>He also checked his stats.</p>
<p>⟦ STATS ⟧</p>
<p>Physical Strength: 437,650</p>
<p>Physical Agility: 438,260</p>
<p>Physical Stamina: 437,625</p>
<p>Spiritual Strength: 634,275</p>
<p>Mental Strength: 454,665</p>
<p>"So that is 680,000 for each stat," Kent smiled. "Looks like I will become a monster after breaking this damn limit."</p>
<p>His smile bloomed as he commanded the two to add the points.</p>
<p>Powerful energy surged into his body, making him feel like he could easily punch a hole into the planet. However, just as the energy flowed into his body, it suddenly stopped, and messages appeared.</p>
<p>⟦ You have reached your maximization limit. To continue, you must first break your limit. ⟧</p>
<p>⟦ Limit Breaking Task: Kill Five Spirit Kings. ⟧</p>
<p>"Not that I did not expect something like this, but five Spirit Kings... that is a lot, but not impossible." A smile tugged at the corner of his mouth, making him sigh.</p>
<p>"The only headache now is getting to that pill princess... but even before that, I need to raise my alchemy skills to a whole new level.</p>
<p>If Shax wasn’t exaggerating, then it means my opponent is not a simple person. She is a monster in the alchemy path, and from the look of things, getting to challenge her will be a headache.</p>
<p>She had already gained five enlightenments in the Pill path, and I am yet to concoct a Tier 4 pill.</p>
<p>Ah, what a headache," Kent scratched his head. He may be strong and handsome, but this Pill Princess is proving to be a headache.</p>
<p>She can already concoct Tier 9 pills, and as someone who prides himself in the Pill path, Kent doubts she will lower herself to his level and accept his challenge when he can’t even make Tier 8 or 9 pills.</p>
<p>"I guess when I enter seclusion this time, I won’t be coming out until I am at the stage the entire world will fear me...</p>
<p>This means both my Bloodline, Soul, Sword, and Alchemy must be on a whole different level." Kent clenched his fist.</p>
<p>"Wait for me, Pill Princess, I will flip your world upside down and make you kowtow to me..." His laughter echoed in his room as he lay down and soon drifted to sleep, only to be woken up two days later by Dori.</p>
<p>The kings and their elders had arrived at a decision, so Princess Arien came to pick them up. Soon, Kent, Dori, and Sword King Shawn were back in the large hall.</p>
<p>A new face was sitting beside the king this time... a woman.</p>
