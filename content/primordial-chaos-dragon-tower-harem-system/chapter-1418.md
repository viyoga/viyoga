<p>Kaizo was the first to get in line.</p>
<p>He was bored beyond measure, so hearing Vexthra’s words, he jumped down and rushed to the front. The others quickly gathered around to listen to what Vexthra had to say.</p>
<p>"I have reasons to believe that from the 9th floor upwards, we will start meeting Immortals that can threaten some of us. Of course, considering we have demigods and gods among us, we can somehow ignore this threat.</p>
<p>However, the mission details are always strict on the strength level required for each mission. So, despite having the strength, we will be limited to only a small part of what we can utilise.</p>
<p>This means you all should be careful. The dungeon is a dangerous place, and so, regardless of what you think, when it comes to immortals, anything is possible."</p>
<p>They nodded.</p>
<p>"In that case, let’s go. We have floors to clear and rewards to claim." She opened the portal, and they started entering. Just when Miryssa entered, and Vexthra was also about to enter, Kent appeared in the Legion Tower.</p>
<p>"I will tag along for this one. Afterwards, I will leave to handle other things."</p>
<p>She nodded with a smile, already happy that Kent had decided to join them. They knew he had been busy the past couple of months, so they left him undisturbed.</p>
<p>However, since he had decided to join them, allowing him to wasn’t too bad.</p>
<p>They entered the portal together, and it closed behind them.</p>

<p>[Planet Kenai]</p>
<p>"Hmmm," Kent hummed, his expression calm, though the look of intrigue appeared in his eyes. He appeared in a rather thick forest, one in which he could sense thousands of powerful creatures locking onto his location.</p>
<p>He appeared alone despite entering the portal at the same time as Vexthra.</p>
<p>This meant Vexthra may have also appeared somewhere completely different, which, in a way, meant the others may be in similar situations.</p>
<p>There was no mission description.</p>
<p>However, Kent wasn’t afraid. He had something way better.</p>
<p>He activated the soul map, and at once, a large part of the forest came within his sensing range. His brow raised slightly, seeing the thousands of beasts, some already at the True Immortal stage, moving toward him.</p>
<p>He wanted to see if he would find any of his wives or legion within an 80,000 km radius. However, all he saw was danger.</p>
<p>He also could not reach the tower, his legion, or his wives.</p>
<p>"I guess I have to fight my way out then." Gaap appeared in his firm grip, and then he moved, starting a massacre while he waited for the mission description to show.</p>
<p>Meanwhile, far away in a large desert, Vexthra stood, her expression filled with questions. She entered the portal with Kent, but they seemed to have been separated.</p>
<p>Even worse, she had no idea why her strength was suppressed to the True Immortal stage.</p>
<p>She tried to scan her surroundings but found nothing. Also, the mission description hadn’t shown up yet, so she started walking toward the west of her location.</p>
<p>She tried to contact her sisters using the harem connection, but there was no response. It was as if the connection with her sisters and Kent had been severed.</p>
<p>This was the first time something like this had ever happened, so she was a bit in shock while also contemplating why she was suppressed to such a level when she had already risen beyond the rank of a god.</p>

<p>[Dim Cave - Planet Kenai]</p>
<p>Inside a dim cave, hidden somewhere on Planet Kenai, sat a Goblin whose entire body was coated in dark metallic armor.</p>
<p>His chest was slashed open, blood oozing from it. However, he seems to be healing at an unnatural speed, something that is a bit bizarre.</p>
<p>Around him, the aura of blood filled the air. A large, broad sword rested beside his chair, its blade coated in all kinds of blood.</p>
<p>His breathing was ragged, and from the look in his eyes, one could tell he was both furious and afraid.</p>
<p>He remained hidden in the cave, his attention sharp, almost as if he was on the lookout for something or someone.</p>
<p>400 km away, Unity stood on the surface of a large flat stone, her bow firmly gripped in her arms. Just as Kent and Vexthra did, she also tried to contact her sisters, but none responded.</p>
<p>She immediately realized she was on her own, so she quickly reached out to her Primordial Spirits, making them aware of the situation and how best they could help.</p>
<p>In another part, too, Lilian appeared on a fresh battlefield, where the smell of blood hung in the air. The remains of thousands of beasts and humanoids with beastly body parts littered the ground.</p>
<p>Neomi also appeared on a small island, one where she could see a vast expanse of water spanning several thousand miles.</p>
<p>She scanned the island but found no one, not even her sisters. She tried contacting them, but nothing happened. So she found a good spot, sat down, and began meditating. In her mind, until the mission details appeared, she would make no move.</p>
<p>Thankfully, an hour later, the mission description appeared.</p>
