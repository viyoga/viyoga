<p>The question asked by Pill Celestial Zathoriel Prime left the others in a bind. None had a tangible answer. After all, they know Kent is already in danger.</p>
<p>His name appearing on the Pill Dao Tablet made him an asset anyone would love to get their hands on. If they can’t ally with him, then they will suppress and force him to do their bidding.</p>
<p>They are aware of his existence, and so is the rest of the Universe. So there is just no way he would be safe even if they choose to maintain their distance.</p>
<p>The Soul Temple has always had its eyes out for talent, so there is just no way they won’t make their move on Kent once they find out where he lives.</p>
<p>So if that is the case, they must come up with a way to ensure Kent’s safety while also keeping him out of the spotlight.</p>
<p>They must maintain their distance, but they also cannot allow the Soul Temple to get their hands on him.</p>
<p>"There is only one solution, and that is to ensure he isn’t found by the Soul Temple or any of the dark organizations that have been hiding out from the universe’s eye for a while now.</p>
<p>I know he is meticulous. After all, it has been over a year since he became famous, yet no one has managed to make contact with him. This shows he knows what he is doing.</p>
<p>Of course, from what I have gathered, he seems to be in the High World, so maybe that was why. The High Worlds are highly restricted.</p>
<p>However, once he reaches the Immortal World, there is a good chance his anonymity would become useless there. So if we want to help him, we must put measures in place in the Immortal Worlds to ensure he is safe from danger, at least to the best of our abilities," Pill Celestial Korvane Draxil said, and his words seemed to resonate with everyone.</p>
<p>He made some sense, and after digesting what he said, they all knew he was right. Kent is safe now, so they must prepare for when he becomes an Immortal.</p>
<p>"I believe what Drax said is a good move. If our signature starts spreading to the High Worlds, there is a chance the others will become bolder and make dirty moves.</p>
<p>So we should let him be for now. There is no need to add more oil to the fire right now. He is safe and will remain so as long as he remains in the High World.</p>
<p>If anything, we should reach out to the Sword Council and the Ice Temple and see if we can form some alliance on how best to protect such a treasure.</p>
<p>The future of Alchemy is in his hands, and we both know it. So for once, we should drop our pride and make sure the safety of such a talent comes first."</p>
<p>Pill Celestial Tharos Embergrave also made his mind known.</p>
<p>In the end, they all nodded to his words.</p>
<p>Pill Celestial Zathoriel Prime accepted their inputs. The end always justifies the means. If he wants to protect Kent, he must allow him to be for now. Once he reaches the stage where he can’t move on his own, they can come in and offer their help.</p>
<p>The last thing any of them wanted was to be the reason a talent like Kent found himself in trouble he couldn’t handle.</p>
<p>Pill Celestial Elyndra Myrr and Vaelith Moonshade gave an even better recommendation... They wanted to use the Midnight Shadows to protect Kent.</p>
<p>Pill Celestial Vaelith Moonshade has a connection with one of the Shadow Supremes in the Midnight Shadows. He owes her a favour, so she wants to call in that favour to help Kent.</p>
<p>After all, the Midnight Shadows are everywhere. They are in every High, Immortal, God, Supreme, and Celestial World. So with their help, Kent will be safe.</p>
<p>"Do that, Vaelith. They are discreet, and they most certainly hate the Soul Temple, so they would be of great help." Pill Celestial Zathoriel Prime liked the idea.</p>
<p>"I like Vaelith’s idea also. The farther we stay from him, the better, so using the Midnight Shadows would come in handy," Pill Celestial Arkanis Solmere also added his voice.</p>
<p>In the end, a decision was reached, and so Pill Celestial Vaelith Moonshade immediately headed out to handle the task.</p>
<p>In no time, all of them left except Pill Celestial Zathoriel Prime and Elyndra Myrr.</p>
<p>They are the oldest members of the Pill Council, since both of them reached the rank of Pill Celestial during the Primordial Era. They lived through many eras, witnessed many wars, and through it all, preserved the legacy of Alchemy to this day.</p>
<p>They can be said to be the father and mother of Alchemy, as their contributions to the path span several generations.</p>
<p>"It is just like Kevin’s time all over again," Myrr said, her expression softening.</p>
<p>Many years ago, when Prime returned with Kevin Sage, she was one of the few who objected to the idea of him imparting the knowledge he had carried with him for years to such a brat.</p>
<p>In her opinion, Kevin Sage is spoiled and not serious enough to be entrusted with such a responsibility. Fast forward, and she was among the many who gave their all to make sure Kevin became one of the best.</p>
<p>The brat was so intelligent that they even questioned what goes on in his head sometimes. He had developed thousands of ways to concoct pills that to this day seem to have an impact in the world of Alchemy.</p>
<p>Kent showing he could do what had never been done before brought back past memories.</p>
<p>Prime smiled. "You are right. It is just like that brat’s time all over again." He chuckled and continued, "Back then, he always seemed to jump from one trouble to the next, always picking fights when he could just keep that mouth of his shut.</p>
<p>If only he had kept that mouth of his shut and minded his own business, his end wouldn’t have been that tragic. Maybe he would have by now taken the next step."</p>
<p>Myrr sighed.</p>
<p>"It is all in the past now, Prime. The past is the past. All we can do now is move forward and hope that one day, what Kevin couldn’t accomplish will be accomplished by Kent Madson.</p>
<p>Gaining recognition from the Pill Dao this early only shows that he is destined for great things. So for now, let’s allow the Midnight Shadows to protect him.</p>
<p>One day, we will meet him and see if he can carry the task we failed at."</p>
<p>Prime nodded and looked out the window. "Though we have to make contact with him somehow in the next 100 years. I can sense something terrible coming, something that would take the Immortal World by storm, so we should get to him and protect him from that."</p>
<p>Myrr looked at Prime for a few minutes, then smiled. "I guess you never change, huh. Since when did you resume divination again?"</p>
<p>Prime smiled. "Ten thousand years ago, when I sensed two immortals with a much deeper connection to fate. If I were to get my hands on them, then hopefully one day, the Universe Fate Eye, or should I say Eyes, will be opened again. Their destiny is much bigger than even mine."</p>
<p>Myrr stood up. "I am leaving. An old friend of mine needed help, so I will be away for a few decades. Don’t call for me..."</p>
<p>She flashed a smile and then turned into countless golden leaves. Soon, they drifted away.</p>
<p>Prime was left alone to reflect.</p>
