<p>"Waoooow"</p>
<p>An Elven child exclaimed, gazing at the magnificent city now floating at the centre of a blue river. Out of nowhere, this city was revealed, catching everyone by surprise.</p>
<p>Be it the Humans living in Gaiaville, the Bloodvein tribe living in Blood City, or the Elves living in Nature City, everyone came out to see the latest addition to the cities in the tower.</p>
<p>From the base of the floating city to the towering buildings that touch even the clouds, everything is made of jade.</p>
<p>The city has five sections, each connected by a glass bridge. Gaia named them East, West, North, South, and Central cities.</p>
<p>The east city is made of white jade, west of green jade, North of purple jade, South of golden jade and Center of diamond jade.</p>
<p>Each section of the city has a tall tower that stretches as far as the eye can see.</p>
<p>Although many buildings float off the main island, these towers pierce the clouds, allowing all who can stand at the top to gaze at the beauty of the night sky.</p>
<p>Gaia named them Jade Towers, with the one standing in the Central City being the tallest. These towers are for cultivation, since inside them, an hour outside is a day inside.</p>
<p>Instead of building them cultivation gardens as she did with the other three cities, Gaia chose to try something new by building Jade Towers that, in many years to come, would be known as Cultivation Towers.</p>
<p>The people living inside the tower would keep expanding, and soon, instead of calling it Tower Cities, it would become Tower World, with many continents.</p>
<p>As long as Kent is alive, they will continue to grow and multiply. One day, even from just the Tower cities, gods and immortals will be everywhere.</p>
<p>Kent appeared inside the Mountain Garden where his wives and some of his legions stood, gazing at the vast expanse of Skyjade city.</p>
<p>"Gaia has outdone herself yet again," Zaila commented, looking at the city. Even though the other three towns are equally stunning, considering how unique this one was, everyone seemed mesmerised by it.</p>
<p>Just as others admire the other three cities when they see them, they admire Skyjade City, too. In the end, they will stand at the same spot and admire the next city.</p>
<p>Liora and her aunt Yuyin also stood among her sisters. Yuyin was in disbelief, staring at the city that she and her fellow Sky Foxes would be staying in from now on.</p>
<p>The best part is that it is free, and everything they will need will be provided. Basically, all they need to do now is cultivate and live the peace they have sought many times.</p>
<p>Of course, she had no idea they also would be working to keep Kent’s reserve of soul essence always full. But even if they knew, they wouldn’t mind, considering this place is the most beautiful place she has ever seen.</p>
<p>"When would you be moving in?" Nara asked.</p>
<p>"Right now, I suppose. The city is ready, but first, let me talk to Gaia," Kent said before teleporting away. He appeared inside Skyjade city, where Gaia and her pals were toasting to yet another job well done.</p>
<p>"I hope that is fruit juice. You little ones are too young to be drinking alcohol."</p>
<p>"Big Brother."</p>
<p>As expected, they were on him before he knew it. Still, Li Hua and Nyxira aren’t back from the dungeon since she decided to hone her skills there. So only the three cosmic sisters, Yuna, and Purity were in Skyjade city.</p>
<p>After hugging him, Gaia handed Kent a glass of apple juice....</p>
<p>’As expected, they really have the mindset of children,’ Kent said inwardly before taking a sip and letting the taste work its way deep into his bones.</p>
<p>"Juicy," he muttered. Although he is beyond fruit juices, he couldn’t help but let the taste get to him. Once he had enough, he turned to look up the tower stretching as far as entering the cloud.</p>
<p>"Nice job, Gaia," Kent finally said, making Gaia smile happily.</p>
<p>"Big Brother should rest assured that whatever he needs, Gaia and her sisters can do it," Gaia said, taking another sip of her drink.</p>
<p>"I know you will because you ladies are the best."</p>
<p>Complimenting them in a way makes them super happy, so Kent knew moments like this warranted a good compliment.</p>
<p>"Can they move in now?"</p>
<p>"Yes. The city is ready, so Big Brother can bring the Sky Foxes."</p>
<p>Kent nodded and turned to his ladies, who understood what he meant. Liora led them back to Tianxu, where the Sky Fox were ready to move.</p>
<p>Kent joined them, and using his authority as the owner of the Tower, he opened five large portals for them.</p>
<p>"Through these portals is a city where, from now onwards, you will have all the peace in the universe to grow and live happily with friends and loved ones.</p>
<p>Also, remember, this was possible thanks to your Princess. So maybe cut her some slack next time," Kent said with a chuckle.</p>
<p>Although he isn’t really bothered if his wives are Princesses, Queens, or even Empresses, learning that the Sky Foxes don’t see Liora as their Princess simply because she is more human than a fox made him a little angry.</p>
<p>So he made sure they knew she was the one who made all this happen.</p>
<p>As expected, all the Sky Foxes bowed in Liora’s direction and spoke in unison... "We thank the Princess." They can now see the bigger picture.</p>
<p>"Good. You can enter your new home now." They bowed to Kent and then entered through the five portals. Soon, they started arriving in Skyjade city, with their eyes wide and jaws hanging open from sheer shock and disbelief.</p>
<p>Their new home is just too beautiful. In no time, they all entered, and the portals closed when Kent and his wives also entered.</p>
<p>However, before leaving, Kent used his sword qi to carve an inscription into the city.</p>
<p>All over Planet Tianxu, the sudden disappearance of the Sky Fox race alarmed everyone, just like how the massacre of the beasts and humans who went to suppress them a couple of hours ago took everyone by surprise.</p>
<p>They didn’t know just what had happened.</p>
<p>However, the brave ones who chose to enter the island after the Sky Foxes left saw an inscription left behind by the one responsible.</p>
<p>"Kent Madson Was Here"</p>
<p>When they saw this, word spread quickly, and within a day, thousands of powerhouses came to Tianxu to see what had happened.</p>
<p>All they found were dead bodies of thousands of humans and beasts, sending chills down everyone’s spine.</p>
<p>Although they failed yet again to meet Kent, the scene they came to meet told them all they needed to know. The person they are looking for is a danger that can’t be messed with.</p>
<p>Later, the curious ones will find the teleportation gate underwater and activate it. Thousands will enter, but none will return. After all, the danger waiting for them on Planet Modu is not one ordinary warriors could handle.</p>
<p>Lawless worlds are called lawless simply because of the lawlessness there. And since Kent had already angered the three Lawless Monarchs, they would welcome the new arrivals with deadly consequences.</p>
