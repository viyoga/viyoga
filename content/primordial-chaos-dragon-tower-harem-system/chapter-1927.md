<p>Many eras ago, the classic phrase ’Here I come’ was heard by millions of void beasts just when they thought they had gained the upper hand during the Great War.</p>
<p>Initially, they didn’t take it seriously because it carried no weight.</p>
<p>But then he appeared, built like a mountain, his body covered in golden armor, his fists covered in gauntlets, and golden hair flowing down his back.</p>
<p>He introduced himself as Kaizo, the Undead Divine Titan.</p>
<p>When he appeared, only seven Void Generals remained since the other seven had been killed by the Chaos Dragon and his allies. Three out of the seven surrounded him, hoping to bring him down.</p>
<p>But two hours later, he stood tall, his body still covered in golden armor, but now crimson, dyed in blood. His teammates asked if he was alright.</p>
<p>His answer was, ’This is not my blood.’</p>
<p>The history books described him as a battle maniac. Some described him as the warrior who doesn’t fear death, and a few called him the Overlord of the Battlefield.</p>
<p>Today, as Kaizo descended from the sky, the heavens shook, cheering him on, for the legendary undead general who had single-handedly killed three Void Generals had descended into battle with only one conviction: total annihilation.</p>
<p>As their attacks met again, the Sage was pushed back, shock appearing on his face. Kaizo gave him no time to relax. His fists moved like lightning, one punch after another raining down on the Sage.</p>
<p>Kaizo understood the assignment: kill as many as they could before things turned grim for them. They knew they could not contend against 500,000 plus warriors.</p>
<p>Even after spending two weeks planning and working behind the scenes, they could only do so much. So their only plan was to take the opportunity to kill as many as they could, then blow up the stronghold.</p>
<p>Kaizo and 24 others were tasked by Vexthra to make sure the 24 Sages did not have a moment’s rest. Kaizo was the weakest among the 25 in terms of cultivation base, but Vexthra knew he could handle it, no matter the danger.</p>
<p>Away from him, Athena, now suppressed to the rank of a Celestial, faced off against another Sage. She was a Primordial and was supposed to fight one of the Monarchs.</p>
<p>However, Vexthra made a last-minute change, stating that if a Primordial appeared, she would handle them. Han instead took her place.</p>
<p>Since they were using pocket dimensions for the Primordial battles, Vexthra knew the only way the five Monarchs could come out alive was if they defeated their opponents. But for that to happen, it would take considerable time.</p>
<p>That was more than enough for them to finish their mission and leave.</p>
<p>So she held back one Sage while her attention was focused on her surroundings. The remaining four Ancient Undead, belonging to Princess Dominique, who was facing off against one of the Monarchs, were also holding back the Sages.</p>
<p>Aside from them, Elsa’s five Pseudo-Primordial Soul Sentinels had also held back some, and the rest were being handled by the Eternal Wrath legion.</p>
<p>"Princess Lovina, today you do not have to hold back," Vexthra turned to the poison lady within the harem and said.</p>
<p>In the past, they had held Princess Lovina back from using her poison for an extended period of time since she explained that the more she used it, the more it corrupted her soul until one day, her heart would become totally poisoned.</p>
<p>While that would finally bring out her true power, it would also twist her personality, something they feared for her.</p>
<p>However, things were different today, and so they needed her.</p>
<p>"I understand, Big Sister Vex." She sighed, closing her eyes. When she opened them, they had turned purple, and then her clothes changed, taking on a purple hue.</p>
<p>They clung to her skin, accentuating her curves. Even her hair changed, and at once, the space around her became poisonous.</p>
<p>Her fingertips turn black, a sign that she had tapped into the dark arts.</p>
<p>She waved her staff, and three large portals opened in the air, releasing poison into the air. Her teammates were not affected, but the same could not be said for the members of the Grim Arcane Legends.</p>
<p>She waved her staff again and said, "Come out, my children. Time to spread the poison."</p>
<p>At her command, a buzzing sound soon filled the air, instantly drawing attention.</p>
<p>One of the Sages who specialized in poison immediately sensed the danger and gave out a command. "Shoot her down!"</p>
<p>From 3 km away, an archer aimed her bow and unleashed a deadly arrow. It flew through the chaos with deadly accuracy and arrived before Princess Lovina in an instant.</p>
<p>Before it could strike her down, though, a pale white hand came from one of the gates and caught it.</p>
<p>"Pathetic," a buzzing voice spoke from within the first portal.</p>
<p>The owner of the hand that caught the arrow soon walked out of the portal. It belonged to a lady with pale white skin, her eyes much larger than normal. Strangely, she was clad in a wedding gown, her veil rolled behind her.</p>
<p>"You finally called me out, Mother of Poison."</p>
<p>Princess Lovina sighed, regretting that things had come to this. She could feel her soul and heart getting corrupted by poison.</p>
<p>However, she knew she had to eventually embrace her true self.</p>
<p>"Yes, I called you, Daughter of Bees. I need your help."</p>
<p>"As always, Mother. I missed you so much." She laughed, her tone blending humanoid and bee sounds.</p>
<p>She turned and looked at the battlefield, then laughed again and moved, millions of bees buzzing after her. Soon, screams of pain and anguish filled the battlefield.</p>
<p>"Good Lord, who is that?" Unity asked, looking fearfully at Princess Lovina.</p>
<p>"She is one of my daughters." Princess Lovina answered, then she smiled.</p>
<p>However, Unity felt that the smile on her face was very wrong.</p>
<p>’Big Sister Vex, something is definitely not right with Princess Lovina.’</p>
<p>’I know, Unity, but we can’t stop her now, so keep an eye on her and make sure she doesn’t harm herself,’ Vexthra replied, stealing a glance at Princess Lovina.</p>
<p>Just then, the second portal that appeared behind her trembled, and chilling laughter filled the battlefield. This time, the voice was male and blended human and insect sounds.</p>
<p>"Oh, my sweet mother, how this unruly son of yours has missed you."</p>
<p>Through the gate, a man clad in nothing but a cloth tied around his waist walked out, millions of insects pouring out of the portal after him.</p>
<p>The Son of Insects also appeared.</p>
