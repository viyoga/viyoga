<p>===Servant Tower===</p>
<p>-- The term servants may sound bad to many, but in the master’s case, the term servant is a way for those who want to follow the master to grow with him.</p>
<p>-- The servant tower is where the master can add those he deems worthy to become his servants. They are not slaves because they choose to join willingly.</p>
<p>-- The master cannot betray them. They also cannot betray the master.</p>
<p>-- The master receives 10% of the spiritual qi from all those added to the tower when they cultivate.</p>
<p>-- The master receives +100 soul essence from all those added to the tower when they level up.</p>
<p>-- The master can bestow abilities, skills, techniques, and True Names he deems fit to all those linked to the servant tower.</p>
<p>-- When the trust level of servants reaches 80%, they will unlock a unique talent ability called [Servants Destiny].</p>
<p>&lt;Servant’s Destiny&gt;</p>
<p>-- All stats will receive a 120% Boost.</p>
<p>-- Comprehension speed will increase by 120%</p>
<p>-- The chances of awakening a True Name are very high if they don’t have one already.</p>
<p>[Feature Unlocked]</p>
<p>&lt;Servant space&gt;</p>
<p>-- A unique space where all servants can meet and socialize away from the Tower City or other spaces the master grants them access to.</p>
<p>-- The time inside this space is the same as in the outside world.</p>
<p>---</p>
<p>"Not too bad, I guess. But I don’t plan on getting any more servants," Kent said.</p>
<p>[That won’t be wise, Master. With all the amazing things you will be building, you will need servants to manage them all. The more your tower world grows, the more servants you will need.]</p>
<p>"I see. But I have a question. Are these True Names that common?" Kent had to ask. It seemed all he saw now were True Names. Everything about the tower seems to revolve around True Names.</p>
<p>While he had indeed experimented with it when he awakened Uncle Drew to become his first Night Rider, he never truly understood the nature of a True Name.</p>
<p>Vexthra told him that True Names are a person’s free will, but she also said they are much more than that. However, she provided no further explanation. The tower, on the other hand, seemed to know more.</p>
<p>[First, True Names are the rarest things in the universe. In fact, an entire continent may only have one person with a True Name. That is how rare a True Name can be.</p>
<p>However, for some reason, the tower possesses all these True Names that the Master can bestow upon the people he chooses.</p>
<p>I can’t explain why that is because I don’t know. But I do know that True Names embody a person’s free will, as well as their destiny and untapped potential.</p>
<p>Take Uncle Drew, for example. He was just an ordinary carriage driver, but now, he has become one of the legends to ever grace the history books with their might on the battlefield.</p>
<p>Now, he is a Night Rider who commands the night on battlefields. Also, his potential to wield the name Kael Duskbane has been unlocked.</p>
<p>This also means True Names can change a person’s destiny and unlock the potential tied to that True Name. If you bestow and invoke the True Name of a legendary artisan, for instance, that person will begin unlocking the abilities of the legendary artisan.</p>
<p>But True Names are also a person’s free will, so invoking them is akin to enslaving them. However, instead of being negative, since the Master cannot betray the people he links to the tower, this enslavement is nullified.</p>
<p>In a way, the moment you invoke their True Names, they will begin walking the path of the True Name bestowed upon them.]</p>
<p>"I see. Then how are these True Names determine. The True Name Token I used on Uncle Drew has no names on it, yet I manage to bestow on him a true name." Kent asked.</p>
<p>[Even I don’t know. Somehow, I managed to pick the right True Name without knowing how]</p>
<p>"Weird. But can this be trusted? I mean, can this part of you be trusted to pick True Names?" Kent had to ask.</p>
<p>Since true names are meant to change one’s destiny and unlock one’s untapped potential, he had to know if they could be trusted.</p>
<p>[The master shouldn’t worry about that. The tower is meant to protect so it will never harm the master. It’s incapable of harming the master.]</p>
<p>"Okay. I guess I will trust you, Gaia. But now that we have established this, can you tell me how I can obtain more True Name tokens to use on my ladies?"</p>
<p>[The Master can buy them in the Tower shop. Also, you will start receiving them as quest rewards now that the tower has awakened]</p>
<p>"Alright," Kent turns to the last awakened Tower. This particular awakened when he bestowed the night rider’s true name on Uncle Drew.</p>

<p>===Legion Tower===</p>
<p>-- Long ago, during a time of relentless wars, a lone emperor, abandoned by his allies and trusted advisors, faced a grim fate that threatened to shatter the peaceful lives of his people.</p>
<p>-- His army betrayed him, and his generals defected, allying with rival kingdoms and empires. He was left with nothing—no supporters, escape, or hope. Despair consumed him.</p>
<p>-- Then the fateful day arrived, and his empire was swallowed by darkness. Those he once called friends united to destroy everything he had built. In the end, his own sworn brother drove a sword through his heart.</p>
<p>-- In his final moments, the emperor saw a vision—an army numbering in the millions, all bound to him and him alone. This army could not betray him. They would fight for him, unwavering in their loyalty.</p>
<p>-- Yet, this army was no ordinary force. It was not composed solely of humans. Instead, it was a coalition of Spirit Beasts, Vampires, Demons, the Undead, Riders of the Night, Creatures of Myth, and countless others.</p>
<p>-- It was a sight of unparalleled magnificence. With his dying breath, the emperor gave this vision a name—"Legion," for he knew that one day, this legion would rise and grace the battlefields.</p>
