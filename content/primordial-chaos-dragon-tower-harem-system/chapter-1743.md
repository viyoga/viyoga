<p>The moment the poison spread across the battlefield, Enzi arrived before the crystallised dome and placed her hand on it. The next second, it shattered like glass, freeing Liora.</p>
<p>Immediately, Liora slipped back into the shadows and headed toward the Blood Sand Berserker.</p>
<p>Enzi, however, disappeared from the battlefield for a brief moment before taking a step forward. A few seconds later, all four combatants found themselves within an arena-like space made entirely of glass.</p>
<p>"Welcome to the Glass Domain," Enzi said with a smile, standing atop a platform of thick crystal glass.</p>
<p>The Blood Sand Berserker growled, its body burning as though it had been drenched in blazing oil. Unfortunately for it, the Poison Princess Lovina wasn’t someone who ever missed when it came to using poison in battle.</p>
<p>Princess Lovina had come a long way on her journey as a poison cultivator.</p>
<p>Now, instead of relying solely on internal effects, her poison could inflict even more devastating external injuries, the kind that made her an exceptionally deadly warrior.</p>
<p>Just one attack was all it took to render the Blood Sand Berserker immobile long enough for Enzi to pull it into the very place they wanted it.</p>
<p>Three hours earlier, Enzi had come up with the perfect attack plan...</p>
<p>---</p>
<p>"We can beat it if we manage to bring it into my Glass Domain. Right now, if we fight it in the desert, it could overpower us. So the only way is to lure it into my Glass Domain. Liora will distract it long enough for Princess Lovina to poison it.</p>
<p>If everything goes well, I’ll be able to pull it into the Glass Domain, where killing it will be much simpler."</p>
<p>---</p>
<p>Enzi smiled as she watched the first step of the plan succeed.</p>
<p>"Human, you dare attack me in my own domain?" the Blood Sand Berserker said, its body radiating an overwhelmingly powerful aura.</p>
<p>"Your domain? Are you insane or something?" Enzi asked with a frown. "The last time I checked, you kidnapped every last Sand Demon and have been feeding on them. Aren’t you ashamed of yourself?"</p>
<p>Her words came from a place of genuine anger. Learning that millions had been captured by this beast and were probably nothing more than its food had greatly infuriated her.</p>
<p>"Their lives are inconsequential to the bigger picture, so they should be grateful I found a use for them," the Blood Sand Berserker replied.</p>
<p>At once, a terrifying aura erupted from its body, threatening to devour the three women and shatter the domain.</p>
<p>However, when it realised the domain wasn’t going anywhere, it narrowed its eyes at Enzi. Just as it was about to issue another warning, Enzi made her move.</p>
<p>"Threefold Glass Domain Dimension."</p>
<p>The domain shifted, and a second later, the Blood Sand Berserker found itself in three different places at the same time, facing one of the three ladies in each of those spaces.</p>
<p>Even more horrifying, its Demigod cultivation had been reduced to the Immortal Monarch stage.</p>
<p>In one space, its beast form faced Enzi.</p>
<p>In another, its demi-human form faced Princess Lovina.</p>
<p>And in the third, it had become fully human as it confronted Liora.</p>
<p>"What... what have you done?" the Blood Sand Berserker asked, its eyes bloodshot as it stared at Enzi, who wore a smirk at the corner of her beautiful face.</p>
<p>"What do you think?" she replied. "I separated your existence into three, making sure you have to fight each of us in a different dimension. The only way you survive this is by killing all three of us... or by killing me."</p>
<p>She brushed her silver hair behind her shoulder and extended a hand, summoning a sleek crystal sword.</p>
<p>"But let’s be honest—you never stood a chance, even in your true Demigod form."</p>
<p>She moved.</p>
<p>The sound of shattering glass echoed throughout the domain.</p>
<p>In the blink of an eye, Enzi was already upon the Blood Sand Berserker, her sword unleashing deadly arcs of glass that sliced through the air.</p>
<p>The Blood Sand Berserker had no choice but to fight back with everything it had. Otherwise, it would die before it even realised what had happened.</p>
<p>---</p>
<p>In the second glass dimension, Princess Lovina floated gracefully in the air, dozens of poisonous lotuses hovering behind her as she gazed coldly at the demi-human version of the Blood Sand Berserker.</p>
<p>Enzi’s Glass Domain had indeed become much stronger after she advanced to the Immortal stage.</p>
<p>Now, she could do far more than simply trap people inside it.</p>
<p>She could exploit the different aspects of their existence.</p>
<p>Against a beast, she could separate it into three distinct forms—its true beast form, its demi-human form, and its humanoid form.</p>
<p>However, because every aspect of the target was divided, its cultivation was divided as well.</p>
<p>As a result, the Blood Sand Berserker had been reduced to the Immortal Monarch stage in each dimension, drastically lowering its combat power.</p>
<p>More importantly, its consciousness had also been split threefold, forcing it to perceive and react to three simultaneous battles.</p>
<p>Its focus had been completely divided.</p>
<p>The effect on its combat ability became immediately apparent the moment Princess Lovina launched her attack.</p>
<p>"Such a pity."</p>
<p>Her staff swept through the air.</p>
<p>Dozens of poisonous lotuses immediately rained down upon the unfortunate demi-human form of the Blood Sand Berserker, which was already being assaulted simultaneously in the other two dimensions.</p>
<p>---</p>
<p>Liora glided across the battlefield, her body moving as though she were made of wind itself.</p>
<p>She appeared directly before the humanoid form of the Blood Sand Berserker and struck, her left dagger shooting toward its neck.</p>
<p>The Blood Sand Berserker managed to block the attack and quickly retreated.</p>
<p>However, a pillar of glass suddenly rose from the ground, casting a narrow shadow.</p>
<p>That was all Liora needed.</p>
<p>Before the Blood Sand Berserker could track her again, she had already emerged from the new shadow behind it, launching another deadly slash aimed at its neck.</p>
<p>It twisted its body aside, narrowly avoiding a fatal wound, but Liora’s dagger still carved a deep gash across its shoulder.</p>
<p>Without giving it a moment to recover, she thrust her right dagger toward its chest.</p>
<p>Seeing that it had no chance of dodging, the Blood Sand Berserker made a desperate decision and blocked the attack with its hand.</p>
<p>The dagger pierced straight through its palm, drawing blood.</p>
<p>Liora, an experienced assassin, twisted the blade, aiming to inflict pain.</p>
<p>As expected, a surge of unbearable pain coursed through the Blood Sand Berserker’s body.</p>
<p>However, because its existence had been split into three, the pain was instantly transferred to its other two forms as well.</p>
<p>The sudden agony disrupted the beast-form version long enough for Enzi to carve a deep slash across its body.</p>
<p>That injury, in turn, was also transmitted to the remaining two forms.</p>
<p>However, while the beast and humanoid forms had already suffered considerable injuries, the greatest danger lay in the demi-human form.</p>
<p>Princess Lovina’s poison had already taken hold.</p>
<p>It was steadily spreading throughout its body, continuously slowing its movements.</p>
<p>Because all three forms remained connected, the poison’s effects were shared among them as well.</p>
<p>As a result, every one of its forms became noticeably slower.</p>
<p>This wasn’t good news for the Blood Sand Berserker. The longer the battle stretched on, the more danger it would find itself in.</p>
<p>However, even after enduring simultaneous assaults from three dimensions and forcing itself into a heightened state of awareness, it realised it had truly fallen into a deadly trap.</p>
<p>"ROAR!"</p>
<p>So it unleashed a thunderous roar and activated its last trump card.</p>
<p>Then, all of a sudden, a black berserker aura erupted from its body.</p>
<p>"Lovina, do it," Enzi commanded the instant the beast entered its berserker state.</p>
<p>Inside the second glass dimension, Princess Lovina smiled.</p>
<p>She waved her staff before activating one of her deadliest abilities.</p>
<p>In an instant, the entire dimension turned crimson.</p>
<p>Then, from within the sea of blood-red poison, a heart-wrenching scream erupted from the Blood Sand Berserker.</p>
