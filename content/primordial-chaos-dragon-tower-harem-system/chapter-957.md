<p>While Kent was 370,000 years in the past, back in the future, his wives and legion are finally gearing up to face the 4th floor of the Planet Killer Dungeon.</p>
<p>They have 12 years to clear the entire 37 floors of the dungeon.</p>
<p>They had already cleared the first three and are now ready to face the 4th floor.</p>
<p>Vexthra has woken up, and even Val, who nearly died during the battle against the Forsaken Race, has also recovered and is ready to face the 4th floor of the Planet Killer Dungeon with her sisters</p>
<p>"Are we all ready?" Vexthra asked, and her sisters, along with Kent’s legion, nodded in response.</p>
<p>They were finally done looking for all they could find on the third floor, so they might as well keep moving forward.</p>
<p>"Remember, the 4th floor is an upgrade of the previous floors, so we must be on our A game, and no matter what, none of you should get hurt. This dungeon is for us to clear, so we should do well to handle it for Kent.</p>
<p>Getting hurt will delay our progress"</p>
<p>They couldn’t agree more.</p>
<p>Kent’s return to save them during the Forsaken Race invasion was a wake-up call to them, reminding them that they were still weak and that if Kent were in trouble now, they wouldn’t be able to help him much.</p>
<p>So they want to break their limits and reach a level worthy of their rank as Core Formation experts. After all, Kent is also at the Core Formation stage, yet he was able to kill even the avatar of an Immortal.</p>
<p>One could argue that he was able to kill the avatar because he fought him with 2% of his Immortal cultivation.</p>
<p>However, the truth is that any other Core Formation in Kent’s place would have died from just one attack from the avatar. So this shows Kent’s power, and they want something like that.</p>
<p>"Let’s go."</p>
<p>Vexthra opened the portal to the 4th floor, and soon, they appeared inside. However, as soon as they appeared, they saw smoke coming from miles away.</p>
<p>Vexthra quickly checked the quest message.</p>
<p>⟦Name: Planet Killer Dungeon⟧</p>
<p>⟦Floor Name: Church of Destruction (4th)⟧</p>
<p>⟦Power Level: Core Formation - Spirit Lord*⟧</p>
<p>⟦Description:</p>
<p>↠ The Church of Destruction is a ragtag group of demon worshippers. They pray to the great Demon God Balmu, the Demon God of Savagery.</p>
<p>↠ From time to time, they offer a sacrifice to their god, who in turn bestows powers on them. Because of this, the Church of Destruction has, over time, accumulated great power, causing it to slowly get out of control.</p>
<p>↠ They started going out of control to the point that instead of one sacrifice every few months, they now offer sacrifices in the masses, killing as many as they could to please their god.</p>
<p>↠ The king of this world has tried to stop them but failed, causing him to instead protect those he could. Due to this, the smaller towns and villages were left at the mercy of the Church of Destruction.</p>
<p>↠ Your mission is to kill every last member of the Church of Destruction and free the people from years of tyranny.</p>
<p>⟦They have 1200 Core Formation, 400 Spirit Ascension, 300 Spirit Kings, 100 Spirit Emperors, 50 Spirit Monarchs, 10 Spirit Sovereigns, and 3 Spirit Lords. But be prepared for anything⟧</p>
<p>⟦Rewards⟧</p>
<p>↠ 10 Million Tower Points</p>
<p>↠ 100 Billion Soul Essence</p>
<p>↠ Destiny Awakening Token (Unity)</p>
<p>↠ Limit Breaker Key (Everybody)</p>
<p>"Follow me, I will tell you everything on the way," Vexthra began flying toward the location from which the smoke was coming.</p>
<p>Her sisters and the legion followed her.</p>
<p>"So our mission this time is to handle a scum of a church that kills the innocent as a sacrificial offering to their god, who is also a scum. I personally know that bastard and even slapped him once.</p>
<p>However, knowing he had his hand in this mission is all the more reason for us to be cautious and prepare for anything.</p>
<p>He is the kind of god who will stop at nothing to keep growing stronger. This Church of Destruction has for years been offering sacrifices to him, so it is only natural to say we will be facing a true challenge this time."</p>
<p>Vexthra paused, remembering back when she first met this Demon King Balmu. That bastard fell in love with her at first sight.</p>
<p>It ended for him badly, but even so, he was still infatuated with Vexthra, going to extreme ends just to become strong enough to tame the untamable goddess.</p>
<p>In fact, it isn’t just him who is doing this. Thousands of gods want to tame Vexthra, and due to this, they would stop at nothing to grow stronger.</p>
<p>’Perhaps this is a chance to sever one of his energy sources.’ Vexthra sighed.</p>
<p>"They have three Spirit Lords, but for some reason, I believe we should be prepared for a Heavenly Ascension stage warrior.</p>
<p>Thankfully, Veydris has finally reached the Core Formation stage after the upgrade. With him, Irina, Val, and Elyndra working together, we should be able to handle even a Heavenly Ascension stage warrior." They nodded.</p>
<p>"However, before we reveal our trump cards, Vari, Camilla, and Unity, you will face one of the Spirit Lords. Kaizo and Sylara, you will also handle a Spirit Lord, with the last one being handled by Enzi, Liora, Neomi, and Lani." The ladies also nodded.</p>
<p>Vexthra decided to bring Lady Enzi and Liora to challenge the 4th floor with them.</p>
<p>Since Kent had been exposed to some of his secrets, it meant he trusted them. And Vexthra, knowing what was best for her man, had brought them, ensuring they blended in well with the rest of Kent’s wives and legion.</p>
<p>"The rest will focus on the remaining members. If everything goes well, we should be done by the end of the day. But there is a chance some might escape, so Alina, you should prevent that from happening."</p>
<p>After a few minutes of flying, they came to a stop 300 meters from the source of the smoke.</p>
<p>"What the hell?" Unity muttered, looking at a city now in ruins.</p>
<p>"Everyone is dead, and judging by the smell of the smoke, this battle happened not long ago," Selene said, looking at the smoky city.</p>
<p>"This is no battle. This was a massacre," Alina muttered, looking at the charred remains of some people, some even being children.</p>
<p>Vexthra’s eyes turned dark, but Unity was by her side to pull her back. She sighed and thanked Unity before turning to Ingrid.</p>
<p>"Can you see them?" she asked, pointing in a certain direction. "They went that way."</p>
<p>Ingrid activated her Void Eyes and pierced through the void. In no time, she saw what she was looking for.</p>
<p>"They are 4 miles away from a village, 23 miles from here. If we leave now, perhaps we will be able to catch up to them before they completely massacre the village.</p>
<p>There are over 2000 people in the village. At least we can save some of them"</p>
<p>"What if you were alone to go after them? How fast can you get there?"</p>
<p>"I can get there, but they will reach the village faster than I. This means we can’t evacuate them."</p>
<p>Vexthra felt powerless despite being a goddess.</p>
<p>However, before she could ask anything more, Veydris flew into the air, and with one swing of his sword, the void cracked, and Ingrid, who could see through the void, widened her eyes.</p>
<p>"Let’s go. With this, we can stop them before they reach the village." Without wasting time, Ingrid flew into the crack. The rest of her sisters followed, with Veydris being the last to enter.</p>
