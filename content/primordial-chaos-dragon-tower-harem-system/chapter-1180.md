<p>Watching the leader of a Supreme Pill Family screaming his lungs out, everybody gathered there felt their backs break into cold sweats.</p>
<p>The scene is just too unbelievable.</p>
<p>They had on many occasions seen how powerful the head of the Supreme Pyris Pill Family was. He had, after all, demonstrated his power by banning many stores and families from getting pills.</p>
<p>He had even made it so that many people could only buy from his family. Because of this, not only had he become someone feared by many, but his words were supreme in the ten Pill Worlds.</p>
<p>The power he commanded was something surprisingly close to an immortal.</p>
<p>However, such a powerful figure was now burning like a bush, and all anyone could do was step back and sweat from sheer fear.</p>
<p>There is no other emotion around other than sheer fear and terror.</p>
<p>"This is too much... They are all dead."</p>
<p>"Indeed. This is too much."</p>
<p>"Tch, what is too much? These bastards killed my brother, who had just become a Pill Monarch, simply because they wanted the primordial flame that the then Pyris Pill Alliance gave him.</p>
<p>They are wicked, and I, for one, am darn happy these bastards got what they deserve."</p>
<p>"Same here. They blacklisted my pill store and even threatened to kill my family if I didn’t hand over my top customers to them. I wanted them dead, and it seems the universe has answered my prayers."</p>
<p>"Same here."</p>
<p>"Me too... They deserve what they got."</p>
<p>Many people were happy that the era of the Supreme Pyris Pill Family had finally come to an end. The tyranny that had negatively impacted the lives of the masses had finally been eradicated.</p>
<p>Now, it seemed everyone was super happy. Of course, there were those who sympathized with the Supreme Pyris Pill Family; however, they were powerless to do anything—not when Kent had singlehandedly killed them all.</p>
<p>Nobody knew a day like this would ever come; however, now that it had, everyone started to see the dawn of a new beginning for them.</p>
<p>As soon as the man died, Kent formed hand seals, and all their space rings, primordial flames, and techniques they had hidden flew toward him and vanished into thin air.</p>
<p>Kent turned to everyone and started to speak...</p>
<p>"I won’t say much, so you all should listen. Send a message to the other Pill Families and tell them they have a day to put everything they came to meet down and leave.</p>
<p>I do not want to come and meet even a single one of them. Of course, those who wouldn’t listen and even dare take a single pill recipe away, I will spend great time and resources to hunt them down and kill every last one of them."</p>
<p>People took several steps back.</p>
<p>Perhaps, due to how rapidly he had absorbed the blood essence from the people he had killed, his tone and body movements seemed to scream danger.</p>
<p>Hearing his words made them all fear for their lives.</p>
<p>"They have a day to scram, else they can only blame themselves."</p>
<p>After saying that, Kent turned in the direction he and Nefeli went when he wanted to speak with the Grand Pill Tablet.</p>
<p>"NANA..."</p>
<p>Kent called out in a commanding tone.</p>
<p>Suddenly, a powerful explosion rocked the mansion that used to belong to the Pyris Pill Alliance, sending it crumbling. Then, from the ruins, a stone tablet flew toward Kent.</p>
<p>When it got close, everyone’s eyes widened. The tablet stopped before Kent...</p>
<p>"That is the Grand Pill Tablet." Those who had the privilege to see the Grand Pill Tablet recognized it the moment it flew toward Kent.</p>
<p>However, to their shock, the moment the tablet reached Kent, it turned into a white-haired loli. Her beauty instantly captured everyone’s attention.</p>
<p>Nobody had the faintest idea how something everyone regarded as the backbone of the Pyris Pill Alliance turned out to be a human, and from the look of things, she seemed familiar to Kent.</p>
<p>Whispers and murmurs started to spread, but Kent and Nana ignored them.</p>
<p>At the moment, she was a ghost body, so she could not hold hands with Kent.</p>
<p>But she could talk, so that was enough...</p>
<p>"Long time no see, Nana," Kent said, wearing a small smile.</p>
<p>"It is good to see you too, Master." Naturally, Nana was more than happy to finally reunite with her master. After all, she had waited many years just for this moment.</p>
<p>"I guess now that I am here, we can go get the other nine parts to finally make you whole. These people don’t deserve your generosity anymore."</p>
<p>Nana nodded.</p>
<p>Truth be told, she never wanted to divide her body into ten parts. She only did that to remain useful while she waited for her master.</p>
<p>Another reason was that he wanted to keep helping Alchemist across the universe, as her master, the divine Pill Sage, did.</p>
<p>But now that Kent was back, she couldn’t care less about the ten Pill Worlds.</p>
<p>Selene and Neomi moved toward Kent and Nana.</p>
<p>"Another one?" Selene instantly asked, flashing Nana a broad smile.</p>
<p>Kent, who understood what she was hinting at, smiled and turned away. This only gave Selene the chance to tease him some more.</p>
<p>"You must have done some good to the loli goddess to make her gift you all the cute and adorable lolis in the universe."</p>
<p>Kent smirked...</p>
<p>But he couldn’t refute Selene’s shameless words. He indeed had his fair share of lolis.</p>
<p>First was Li Hua, who had now come of age, and to fulfill the promise she made to Kent when he saved her years ago, she began training to become a powerful assassin.</p>
<p>She didn’t even take breaks.</p>
<p>Back then, learning that she had the Atomic Divine Body made him think he would have to spend time with the smallest human he had ever seen; however, Gaia appeared not long after.</p>
<p>Then Akira also appeared, and before long, Dori joined them.</p>
<p>Now, Yuna and Purity had also joined them, and from what they told him, they weren’t leaving anytime soon.</p>
<p>Now, Nana would be joining them, making seven lolis, and there was no telling if she would be the last. In a way, this was more than he could ask for, considering he had already packed seven of them.</p>
<p>So, in a way, Kent was indeed blessed with lolis.</p>
<p>’I should explore the main reason behind this and see if I can figure out why this is happening to me,’ Kent sighed and added, ’Not that I mind, since all of them are amazing in their own way.’</p>
<p>After making up his mind, he decided to handle that later. For now, he had sent a warning and would follow up.</p>
<p>Kent turned back to everyone.</p>
<p>"All members of the Pyris Pill Alliance should meet me here tomorrow morning."</p>
<p>After saying that, they flew into the Pagoda and went straight to the 9th floor. Nana had been living in the Pill World for many years, so she knew every last member of the Pyris Pill Alliance.</p>
<p>She would help him pick a new Alliance Head and, this time around, ensure nothing bad would happen ever again.</p>
<p>The Pyris Pill Alliance was a place where he had made some great gains, so he would most certainly not let it be destroyed by some ambitious families.</p>
