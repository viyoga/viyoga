<p>Getting rid of the five troublemakers was rather easy.</p>
<p>Once they were gone, Kent was left with Uncle Drew, who had been waiting for him to handle his stuff. He had been gone for a while, so they needed to catch up.</p>
<p>"I was getting this close to sending them to the underworld," Uncle Drew said, seeing how Kent had handled the three Lolis and the two immortal troublemakers.</p>
<p>Kent smiled. "They always love to have something to do. In other words, giving them work to do will take their attention from you."</p>
<p>"I suppose it is good to see how to get rid of them from now onwards." Since Uncle Drew isn’t going back to the Underworld anytime soon, learning how to get rid of Gaia and her band of troublemakers was something he seemed happy about.</p>
<p>Once that was cleared, Kent started asking more about Uncle Drew’s time in the underworld.</p>
<p>"How is Nyx?"</p>
<p>"Aside from threatening to send underworld demons after you every single day, she is doing great."</p>
<p>Kent smiled, recalling the time he parted ways with Nyx back when he visited hell. He could tell the soon-to-be Underworld Queen had already fallen for him back then. However, he left knowing they would meet one day again.</p>
<p>Now, she had become a demigod and an Underworld Queen. Her influence is supreme in the ninth level of the Underworld.</p>
<p>"Oh, she said she is close to mastering the first four forms of the Nine-Fold Calamity Whip Art, so if you don’t come to her by the time she is done, don’t blame her if she sends demons to come kidnap you."</p>
<p>"Ah, sounds like something she will do. Remembering those crazy eyes she had on me, maybe that was why I ran away when the time came."</p>
<p>Uncle Drew shook his head, a small smile playing on his lips.</p>
<p>Kent took a deep sigh. "The ladies couldn’t get enough of me, huh?"</p>
<p>The Night Rider captain smirked. He most certainly knew how shameless his young master is, so hearing him say this sounded normal to him.</p>
<p>"Tell me about what happened to you when you went to the underworld a second time."</p>
<p>"A lot happened that I don’t even know where to start." Uncle Drew relaxed his mind and arranged his thoughts before beginning.</p>
<p>"So when I got to the underworld the second time, I asked Queen Nyx to send me to a place called Black Castle. It is a place famous for having a powerful banished soul. The weakest banished soul in the outer section back then was a Spirit Emperor.</p>
<p>So you can guess I was way out of my league for requesting to be sent there."</p>
<p>Kent nodded.</p>
<p>Facing Spirit Emperors at only the Core Formation stage is not something ordinary people can do. He himself had suffered to some extent.</p>
<p>Of course, he quickly breezed through it thanks to his nature, which defies all norms.</p>
<p>"Queen Nyx hesitated for a while, but after convincing her that I would be fine, she sent me there. Thankfully, I was able to convince her to send me.</p>
<p>Naturally, that was a good move on my part simply because reaching the Black Castle had changed everything.</p>
<p>It was a dangerous place for sure; however, it also has powerful and wonderful banished souls. The first few months were taxing and extremely dangerous; however, I managed to survive long enough to form a Night Agent.</p>
<p>Once my first Night Agent was formed and at the Spirit King stage, things became much easier for me. I soon formed 14 Night Agents.</p>
<p>Afterwards, it was all about helping them grow stronger and level up much faster since I was bound to remain at the Core Formation stage till you ascend to a high world."</p>
<p>Uncle Drew paused and let his mind fully encompass the point where everything changed for him.</p>
<p>"On one fateful day, just when we ended a battle that drained us completely, a man who, if I didn’t know better, I would say is your brother, appeared from thin air and saved us."</p>
<p>Kent furrowed his brow, hearing what Uncle Drew said.</p>
<p>Seeing his expression, Uncle Drew smiled. "Trust me, either he is your long-lost redhead twin brother or your father, since he is a copy of you. Of course, he said nothing about his name, but I know for a fact he is a Celestial."</p>
<p>Uncle Drew let the words sink in before adding...</p>
<p>"Maybe he was merely disguising himself; however, I know for a fact he was a Celestial and a friendly one."</p>
<p>"Did he say something that suggested he knew me aside from looking like me?"</p>
<p>"He did. He said saving me and my Night Agents was merely doing you a favour. He even called you a snotnose."</p>
<p>The word struck a chord in Kent’s mind. However, upon reflection, he couldn’t bring himself to accept it.</p>
<p>’It can’t truly be him.’</p>
<p>It seemed he had recognised the person, but he was having a hard time coming to terms with it. In the end, he pushed it to the back of his mind.</p>
<p>"What did he help you with?"</p>
<p>"He awakened an attribute all Night Riders have. It is called [Agent of the Night]. It allows all Night Riders to grow endlessly, regardless of where they find themselves.</p>
<p>It was what allowed me to reach the Death stage, and now I’m moving close to the Immortal stage."</p>
<p>"I see. Sounds like you had a blast."</p>
<p>"That I did. However, I still have some stuff to handle. But before that, I must first become an Immortal."</p>
<p>Kent nodded. He was still shaken about the Celestial, but at least with Uncle Drew now back, he was happy. He would go to Nyx once he was reunited with Elina.</p>
<p>Since he knew Nyx was more than safe, he would seek after Elina first, whose fate remains shrouded in mystery.</p>
<p>"Oh, a friend of yours is now staying with Queen Nyx. I saved her from a bunch of Immortals. I, of course, saved her under Queen Nyx’s orders."</p>
<p>"Which of my friends?"</p>
<p>"She is a Plantkin named Zeraphine, I suppose."</p>
<p>Kent’s eyes widened, his aura leaking slightly... "Who was chasing her? How about the others?"</p>
<p>"She said it was a trusted friend who wanted to kill her for her Immortal Qi. She happens to be a rather powerful and bright lady, you see."</p>
<p>Kent wanted to smile, but his anger began to rise. Having his blood cleansing bath seemed to have shortened his temper. So hearing some of his friends he met 370,000 years ago are in danger made him mad.</p>
<p>"As for the others, she said they are well, except of course the BirdMan."</p>
<p>"Kaelith."</p>
<p>"Yes... that is his name. He had been imprisoned by his family and was being guarded by a demigod. I was powerless to save him, and since Queen Nyx cannot leave the Underworld the way I do, she too cannot go to his rescue yet.</p>
<p>So for now, the one named Kaelith is locked in a rather barbaric prison."</p>
<p>BOOM</p>
<p>Kent’s aura exploded, and Uncle Drew was sent flying.</p>
<p>The mountain garden was completely destroyed by the sudden explosion of his aura,</p>
<p>The people in Gaiaville felt a dreadful feeling press down on them.</p>
<p>Many coughed up blood and passed out, while the strong ones gritted their teeth and continued to endure.</p>
<p>Suddenly, Vexthra appeared in the garden and, with a quick jab, she knocked Kent out cold.</p>
<p>Uncle Drew appeared inside the garden the next second, looking pale... "Well, that went well."</p>
<p>Vexthra glared at him but said nothing. She teleported and appeared in the Harem Tower.</p>
<p>Gaia and her sisters appeared in the destroyed garden, smiles playing on their faces... "Looks like we have to rebuild the garden from scratch."</p>
<p>They were more than happy.</p>
