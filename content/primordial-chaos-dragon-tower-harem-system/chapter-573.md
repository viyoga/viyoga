<p>"Huh, where are Gaia and her sisters?" Mara suddenly asked, noticing the three sisters’ absence.</p>
<p>"Maybe they had had enough and left," Lilian answered, but Kent felt something was weird, so he reached out to Gaia.</p>
<p>’Gaia, is everything alright? You three were here, and the next second, you were gone.’ Kent felt worried for a moment, but when he heard Gaia’s reply, a sigh escaped his mouth.</p>
<p>⟪We are fine, master. We just had enough and left to continue our projects.⟫</p>
<p>’Is Dori okay?’ For some reason, Kent felt like something wasn’t right about what had just happened, and since it had something to do with his memory, he had to ask about Dori.</p>
<p>She is basically his mind after all.</p>
<p>⟪She is fine, master. She is just busy with a project she is working on.⟫ Gaia responded.</p>
<p>"Okay, thank you," Kent smiled.</p>
<p>’Why is Gaia lying to me?’ A sigh escaped his lips, and then he chose to let it slide.</p>
<p>’They never lie to me, so if she did, it must be something she can’t talk about. I have to trust them and hope everything is okay.’</p>
<p>"How are they?" Vexthra asked, knowing Kent could telepathically speak with anyone inside the tower, regardless of who they were.</p>
<p>"They are fine. They just had enough, so they went back to work." In the end, Kent didn’t want to make his ladies worry, so he told them what they wanted to hear.</p>
<p>They went back to waiting for Elaine to wake up. Thankfully, after an hour, she opened her green eyes and then stood up.</p>
<p>She walked closer to Alina and hugged her tightly.</p>
<p>"Thank you, sister Alina," Elaine said, smiling as she buried her face in her chest, showing how happy she was at the moment.</p>
<p>"Don’t worry about it. After everything, I will do the same for your father." Alina brushed her hair and patted her back. "You are now my sister, so get ready to have all the amazing stuff."</p>
<p>The ladies examined Elaine’s body for a few minutes before they stepped back.</p>
<p>"Kent, help me with the curse. Reattach her head and hold her down for a few seconds," Alina said, and Kent walked closer to the beheaded corpse. He did as he was told.</p>
<p>Right when the head landed on the body, it came back to life. However, Kent was a step ahead, so he held her firmly as Alina took out the Celestial Portrait of the Eternal Garden and opened it just large enough to do what she wanted.</p>
<p>An elegant brush appeared in her hands, and then, using it, she drew a few symbols in the air. After she drew the last symbol, she waved the brush, and the symbols flew and entered the cursed body.</p>
<p>"You can let go now," Kent said, letting go and leaving the curse to stay on the ground.</p>
<p>Alina walked closer and crouched down before it. "I will name you Tita. Go inside the garden and continue to grow. When I need you, I will come for you."</p>
<p>The curse surprisingly nodded and flew, entering the Eternal Garden. The portrait shrank and entered Alina’s forehead.</p>
<p>They moved back to Elaine, who was about to explain how she felt after the curse had been removed.</p>
<p>"I have awakened a power that allows me to have control over life and death. However, all those abilities are beyond me.</p>
<p>The current me is just a Life King, so I need to grow some more before I can start using it. However, that doesn’t mean I can’t use my innate body and abilities.</p>
<p>The new me can cultivate herbs to great levels. The current herbs we have in our garden are just Tier 5, but I can now cultivate them to Tier 8 despite being at Tier 7.</p>
<p>And if that is not enough, I can harness the Tier 8 essence in these herbs to cure harrowing injuries and disease.</p>
<p>Also, I can heal anyone below the Core formation completely of their injuries, regardless of how life-threatening it is."</p>
<p>Her sisters nodded, smiles playing on their lips. They were happy for her, knowing she wouldn’t be on the sidelines anymore.</p>
<p>But Elaine was not done.</p>
<p>"As for my combat abilities, I only have one, and it’s this." Elaine spread her right hand, and a tree grew in her palm.</p>
<p>"This is the Razor Blade Immortal Plant." Vexthra immediately recognized the plant.</p>
<p>"Indeed." Elaine smiled. "I can only use 30 leaves at the moment. But though I am just a level 2 Root Transcendent, nobody in that realm can beat me in combat."</p>
<p>"Oh, we have a confident one here," Neomi joked, making her sisters laugh. However, Vexthra just smiled.</p>
<p>"She is telling the truth. The leaves on this tree are called Immortal Razor Piercers. They are very powerful and can, to some extent, ignore all defenses and pierce the soul.</p>
<p>However, in its current form, no Transcendent will be able to defend against it, unless, of course, your defense is as robust as a Root Sovereign."</p>
<p>"Wow, but I thought Life Magicians were more about saving life and not taking it." This time it was Lady Vari who spoke.</p>
<p>Elaine smiled guiltily. "That is indeed true. However, I am not just a Life Magician; I am also a Death Magician. My innate body gives me control over life and death.</p>
<p>So naturally, I must have the means to claim the life of someone instead of just healing." She turned and looked at the overcheerful Kent.</p>
<p>"Also... I can use this method to protect my man." Her voice broke as her cheeks turned beet red. It was her first time, after all, calling Kent her man. Her sisters burst into uncontrollable laughter, watching her flustered look.</p>
<p>After a few minutes, they stopped and had to listen to all she had to say. After that, Kent called Val for her True Name awakening.</p>
<p>The ladies were very happy for Elaine, but most importantly, they were looking forward to when her True Name awakened. They knew that once that happened, her true potential would be unleashed.</p>
<p>Kent put Val’s blood on the True Name Yoken. When he added his blood, sword qi started to fill the Soul Tower.</p>
