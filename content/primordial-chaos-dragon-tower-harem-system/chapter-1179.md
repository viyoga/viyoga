<p>The moment the fist landed on the gate and destroyed it, powerful auras came from all nine levels of the pagoda surrounding Kent in an instant.</p>
<p>He coldly scanned all of them until his gaze landed on one of them. The bastard has an aura of evil around him. Kent immediately knew he had been killing people to make pills.</p>
<p>That was the only way an alchemist could have such a vile aura.</p>
<p>Kent’s cold gaze turned chilling.</p>
<p>He raised his hand and gestured down.</p>
<p>In an instant, the man with his Peak Heavenly Ascension stage cultivation crashed to the floor, his bones breaking under the bloodline pressure Kent was exerting.</p>
<p>With another gesture, the man’s head exploded like a balloon.</p>
<p>"My name is Kent Madson... Yes, that Kent Madson. I am a member of the Pyris Pill Alliance and would love it if you and your family pack out and leave.</p>
<p>I can give you an hour to do that, for which there will be no death. Disobey, and I will slaughter all of you, sparing not even the weakest of you."</p>
<p>Many shuddered, feeling their backs break into cold sweats.</p>
<p>Both the coldness in Kent’s eyes and the tone he spoke in felt so suffocating and dangerous.</p>
<p>"The Pyris Pill Alliance is no more. This is now the Supreme Pyris Pill Family. So it will be in your best interest to pack out and leave."</p>
<p>A Pill Monarch, about Kent’s age but Kent knew he was much older, said, taking a step forward. Three more Pill Monarchs followed him, though Kent could see the nervousness in their eyes.</p>
<p>If nothing concerned them, then the fact that they were facing the Kent Madson everyone had been searching for was enough to make them nervous.</p>
<p>For someone to become the target of everyone was already enough to tell what kind of existence they were.</p>
<p>Kent looked at the Pill Monarch for a few seconds before letting a small smile appear on his face.</p>
<p>"You know, 370,000 years ago, I took the liberty of dismantling the Blue Flame Pill Palace simply because they had colluded with the Evil Pill Sect.</p>
<p>Now, after many years have passed, it seems you losers still haven’t learned your lessons and still think that with the Evil Pill Sect backing you, you can become invincible.</p>
<p>In that case, allow me to offer you one more chance."</p>
<p>Kent turned and looked around him one more time. "Leave everything that belongs to the Pyris Pill Alliance behind, and vacate the premises in the next hour, else there will be hell to pay."</p>
<p>"What if we said no?" the Pill Monarch said again, a small smile playing on his lips.</p>
<p>Kent smiled. "You know, as long as you cannot call an immortal here, then even if there are millions of you, none will escape my wrath today."</p>
<p>The smile on the Pill Monarch’s face froze. However, he recovered and broke the pendant behind him. Immediately, more powerful auras filled the area.</p>
<p>In no time, the entire entrance of the Pill Pagoda became filled with tens of thousands of warriors and alchemists.</p>
<p>Seeing this many people surrounding Kent, the Pill Monarch sneered. "Go down on your knees and beg for your life. A mere Spirit Sovereign dares disrespect an entire Supreme Pill Family... the number one Pill Family in all Ten Pill Worlds."</p>
<p>He smirked and raised his hand, summoning a primordial flame.</p>
<p>"Wow, that is a primordial flame." The people who were trying to get into the Pill Pagoda before Kent arrived seemed mesmerized by the primordial flame the Pill Monarch had summoned.</p>
<p>Kent, however, seemed even more mad seeing his hard work being used by a bastard. The primordial flame was left by him.</p>
<p>The seven flame monarchs brought back hundreds of primordial flames. Before he left, he gave 40 to the Pyris Pill Alliance.</p>
<p>Now, it seems these losers have taken everything. Kent’s patience immediately ran out...</p>
<p>"So, in other words, you losers won’t be leaving as I suggested, right?"</p>
<p>"You’re damn right."</p>
<p>Kent smiled and turned toward Selene and Liora. "Make sure none of them run away."</p>
<p>The two ladies nodded.</p>
<p>"Since you have made your decision, then wiping you out is the only way," Kent summoned Gaap, who was already radiating an intense Blood Sword aura.</p>
<p>"Allow me to rip your lives then."</p>
<p>Kent moved, and heads started flying into the air. He was not holding back, so aside from a few who could see him move in a blur, almost 90% only seemed to notice him after their heads shot into the air.</p>
<p>In no time, the scent of blood filled everywhere.</p>
<p>With every head falling to the ground, golden blood essence moved and entered Kent’s forehead. Since they brought themselves, he could only slaughter them.</p>
<p>The Pill Monarch, who had spoken and even summoned the reinforcement from the Evil Sect, stood in shock, his fragile legs shaking like a twig.</p>
<p>He wanted to run, but his willpower happened to be rather weak, so he lost the ability to even move. He could only watch as the thousands of people who made his family the strongest in all Ten Pill Worlds were being slaughtered one by one.</p>
<p>"STOP!"</p>
<p>Suddenly, a Pill Legend followed by three Pill Sovereigns appeared from the 9th floor and flew toward where the massacre was happening.</p>
<p>Kent didn’t even glance at them. He had given them the chance, and they didn’t take it, so now they could only blame themselves.</p>
<p>"I SAID STOP!" the Pill Legend shouted again.</p>
<p>"He cannot hear you," Selene suddenly said, standing not far from the Pill Legend.</p>
<p>Liora also smiled, and in a mocking tone, she said, "When he is in combat, words like ’Stop’, ’I surrender’, or ’Please spare me’ cannot be heard by him. He is deaf to those words."</p>
<p>"What she said is indeed true. So if you want his attention, you can take up arms and try to kill him. We will not stop you."</p>
<p>"But we will stop you from running, so choose. Go after our man and try to kill him, or run, and we will kill you."</p>
<p>The Pill Legend and the three Pill Sovereigns swallowed nervously, taking a few steps back.</p>
<p>They turned and looked at Kent, who was ripping lives while absorbing the blood essence of his kills, and all they could do was take their chances with the ladies.</p>
<p>So they made a run for it. However, before they could even move ten meters, all three Pill Sovereigns lost their heads to Liora’s daggers.</p>
<p>As for the Pill Legend, Selene pointed her sword at him and spoke in a somewhat regretful tone, "Killing a Pill Legend is indeed a loss. However, having a scum Pill Legend running the show is also a shit show, so you can only blame your life choices."</p>
<p>With that, a pillar that almost all pill stores depended on was beheaded.</p>
<p>"Don’t worry about it. He isn’t the only Pill Legend in this world. The then Pyris Pill Alliance had two Pill Legends. I am sure they will be arriving here anytime soon."</p>
<p>Liora calmed her sister down and then focused back on Kent, who had already killed 90% of the people and was now wrapping up things.</p>
<p>After a few minutes, Kent was done. He appeared before the Pill Monarch and flashed him a mocking smile.</p>
<p>"I’ve already given you a chance to surrender the pagoda back to me, but you thought with numbers and support from some no-names, you stood a chance."</p>
<p>Kent shook his head, then he gave him a cold line of words, "You can die now."</p>
<p>Kent tapped him on the forehead, and his entire body was engulfed in green flames.</p>
