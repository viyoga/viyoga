<p>Every last one of Kent’s wives was shaken to the core as they watched Dia display her abilities for the first time after breaking free from the unknown beings.</p>
<p>They had held supreme authority over her for a very long time.</p>
<p>Finally, she had broken free, and she could use the power that Paragon had helped her obtain, even against a Pseudo-Primordial.</p>
<p>Fiannarel is an Eternal, but Kent made her lower her soul defenses to that of a Pseudo-Primordial, allowing Dia, who had her own Dao, to plant a heart demon inside her soul. This ability isn’t something to be taken lightly.</p>
<p>To be able to infect a Pseudo-Primordial that easily surely had to be considered a very dangerous thing.</p>
<p>Luckily, Dia had a good hold on her abilities, so after making sure Fiannarel experienced just a few minutes of what it feels like to have a heart demon, she removed it.</p>
<p>Fiannarel gasped for breath, dropping to one knee when the heart demon was taken away.</p>
<p>Kent’s wives rushed to her side, making sure she was alright, while some of them stared back at Kent, expecting him to explain some things.</p>
<p>What they had just seen defied common sense, and so they needed an explanation.</p>
<p>"Long story short, Dia had awakened a Dao, and that Dao allows her to harness heart demons on a whim. Now, before you say a Pseudo-Primordial’s soul shouldn’t be easily suppressed by her, you are indeed right; however, you are also wrong since Dia isn’t the same as before.</p>
<p>It is true that a Pseudo-Primordial’s soul is strong, but when affected by a Dao, the soul now has to fight against complex Dao concepts, which those of you who have interacted with Daos before can attest are incredibly powerful."</p>
<p>They nodded, some of them even coming to the realization that they may have been looking at Dia the wrong way...</p>
<p>"Now, consider a situation where someone has their own Dao, meaning they are free to dictate their Dao concepts and make the rules. If such a Dao is used to suppress a soul, do you think the opponent would have an easy time defending against it?</p>
<p>And Dia is not an ordinary person when it comes to harnessing the power of Heart Demons. She had been a Heart Demon for 13 eras. If anything, her accumulated experience is enough for her to even defeat a Primordial.</p>
<p>So rest assured, Dia isn’t the same person you all know. She has become someone whose cultivation base is merely a number used to describe her. Her true power is far more dreadful than that."</p>
<p>They nodded, each finally coming to terms with what they had seen.</p>
<p>It would take some getting used to and a better understanding of how Dao worked before they could finally conclude that Dia may have become the most dangerous among them.</p>
<p>Kent could only smile and watch as his sister returned to her feet.</p>
<p>Though she was still shaken by the heart demon she had faced a few minutes ago, she was an Eternal, so she wasn’t too beaten down. In fact, she seemed remarkably well to have gone through something this dangerous and be back on her feet in the next few seconds.</p>
<p>Kent also knew his wives would spend the next couple of days talking and getting used to Dia’s abilities before they would have time for him.</p>
<p>Vexthra always tries to understand her sisters’ abilities so they can fully integrate them into their combat styles, allowing them to work in synchrony during battles.</p>
<p>So he left them for now. Once they were done, he and Dia would spend some time together to celebrate her victory against the unknown beings.</p>
<p>In the meantime, he left the Harem Tower and returned to the Mountain Garden to meet with Lady Khronisia, who had already been waiting for him for months.</p>
<p>The last time she saw him was when they were on their way to the Blacksmith Association, and they were attacked by the Blood Ancestor.</p>
<p>She had been living inside the tower, and since time runs much faster there, months had already passed.</p>
<p>Of course, years had passed since then, but she spent most of her time in the Tower Cities, where time runs the same as in the outside world.</p>
<p>Those months had only accumulated because she spent most of her time cultivating inside either the cultivation havens within the Tower Cities, where the tower’s time difference applied, or inside the Legion Tower.</p>
<p>Today, however, Tower had informed her to come here since Kent planned to take her back to the outside world and continue their journey.</p>
<p>She needed his help to forge a weapon, and Kent needed her help to finally summon back his unruly hammer, which he had missed a lot...</p>
<p>"It appears a lot of time has passed since we last saw each other," Kent said, smiling at Lady Khronisia, who stood inside the Mountain Garden playing with some birds.</p>
<p>"It appears so," she turned and faced him. "But I had a great time here, which was all thanks to your quick thinking and decisive action back then."</p>
<p>They had indeed come close to death when the Blood Ancestor attacked all of a sudden, so Kent’s saving her was indeed appreciated.</p>
<p>"No need to thank me so much. I nearly got you killed, so accept my apologies, and as I had already promised, I will help you forge one of the deadliest spears the Immortal Realm has ever seen."</p>
<p>Lady Khronisia nodded, allowing a small smile to form on her face.</p>
<p>They left the tower a few minutes later, and after another week of travel, they finally arrived on Planet Flame Haven. It is a planet literally on fire, every part of it lit by red flames.</p>
<p>However, there was no visible heat to worry about.</p>
<p>Kent already knew where the heat was being channeled, but he was still fascinated by the unique nature of this world.</p>
<p>He had recovered his memories as the Nine Underworld Flame Blacksmith, and so he knew he had a private world made specifically for forging weapons.</p>
<p>He had unique flame pools and lava seas in that world, but nothing beat an entire planet on fire.</p>
<p>They arrived at the Blacksmith Association and immediately went to work.</p>
