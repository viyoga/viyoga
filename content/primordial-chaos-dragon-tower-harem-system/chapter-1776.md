<p>The moment Jyoti Lata stopped before Kent, she extended her finger toward his forehead and spoke, "Open your mind and soul to whatever I will be transmitting. Do not resist, and most importantly, do not access it until I am done transmitting it."</p>
<p>Kent nodded and opened his soul. "I am ready."</p>
<p>She nodded, then said something brief about the technique she was about to transmit.</p>
<p>"The Cultivation Art left for the Red Bone Chaos Tribe is called the Red Bone Chaos Devouring Art. As its name suggests, it has to do with devouring and refining anything that contains Chaos Essence.</p>
<p>Depending on how much you’ve mastered it, you can devour several billion years’ worth of energy in mere seconds. Meaning, in battle, if your comprehension is deep enough, you can devour, refine, and replenish your strength and energy without much of a struggle.</p>
<p>Basically, anything that contains Chaos Essence can be devoured, all the way to Laws and Daos. Even Origin Essence and Eternal Laws cannot be excluded."</p>
<p>"Sounds like it is a one-of-a-kind technique, perfect for someone like me who always has powerful enemies," he laughed and said. "In that case, I am more than eager to see what this Red Bone Chaos Devouring Art can do."</p>
<p>"Very well." She pressed her finger against Kent’s forehead, then, after taking a deep breath, began to transmit the technique.</p>
<p>The moment she began, messages started to appear before Kent...</p>
<p>&lt;You are receiving a Limitless Grade Cultivation Technique called the Red Bone Chaos Devouring Art&gt;</p>
<p>&lt;Progress: 2%&gt;</p>
<p>’Limitless, huh?’ A smile appeared on his face.</p>
<p>The progress continued to climb, perhaps much faster than Jyoti Lata had expected, considering the shocked expression on her beautiful face.</p>
<p>However, it soon turned into a smile. ’This kid is a monster among monsters.’</p>
<p>The other Eternals who sensed what was happening also came to the same conclusion. Normally, it takes the average person between 100 and 1,000 years to receive any of the cultivation arts.</p>
<p>However, from the look of things, Kent would soon finish receiving the technique in less than 10 minutes.</p>
<p>"As expected of my baby brother, he sure is full of surprises," Fiannarel smiled beautifully, proud to have someone like Kent as her baby brother.</p>
<p>Within 4 minutes, progress reached 95%, and in no time, the final message appeared.</p>
<p>&lt;You have successfully received the Red Bone Chaos Devouring Art&gt;</p>
<p>"Well, that was quick," Kent said, a smug expression appearing on his face.</p>
<p>Jyoti Lata looked at him with a stunned expression for a whole minute before shaking her head. "Unbelievable. At this rate, you can even receive all techniques before learning them."</p>
<p>Kent smiled and was about to say, ’I bet I can learn it in less than an hour,’ when the Tower spoke...</p>
<p>&lt;The master cannot learn the technique yet. For reasons I cannot explain yet, the master must first awaken the third Blood Gate, form the third Chaos Heart, and cultivate the third Demon Body. Trust me, this will benefit you more than you think.&gt;</p>
<p>’I trust you, Tower.’</p>
<p>He lifted his head and looked at Jyoti Lata. "In any case, I don’t plan on learning any of the techniques yet since I still have to form my third Chaos Heart and the third Demon Body."</p>
<p>"Very well, then I will go next," Borislav, the Tribe Leader of the Void Vein Chaos Tribe, said and walked forward while Jyoti Lata walked back and sat down.</p>
<p>"A little something you should know about the Void Vein Chaos Scripture is that it allows you to harness the absolute power of the Void by connecting to Void Veins or creating your own.</p>
<p>To master it, you must form or connect to at least two Void Veins. Once that is accomplished, the rest will be up to you and how much you can harness the true power of the Void Chaos."</p>
<p>He reached forward and started to transmit the technique.</p>
<p>In all of the Chaos Chasm, his tribe is an expert in stealth and manipulation. They can manipulate the Void to the point that if they don’t want you to catch them, you cannot.</p>
<p>Their power is that great, and now Kent is receiving it.</p>
<p>About the same time it took Jyoti Lata, the transmission ended, and the third Tribe Leader approached Kent while Borislav walked back to his seat, shaking his head with a smile on his face.</p>
<p>Tribe Leader Veylan Seren smiled and said, "The Obsidian Chaos Dragon Physique is the best body tempering, forging, and refining technique you would ever encounter. In fact, I am glad you are a dragon and are also cultivating the Nine Demon Body Tempering Art."</p>
<p>He laughed, causing the other three leaders to curse him deeply, knowing that when it comes to physical strength, the Obsidian Scale Chaos Tribe stands at the top.</p>
<p>While the Red Bone Chaos Tribe is also known for body tempering, their power comes from devouring, whereas the Obsidian Scale Chaos Tribe specializes in true body tempering.</p>
<p>So, to Kent, his laughter made Veylan Seren appear carefree. To the rest of the Eternals, he was mocking them.</p>
<p>"You are going to forge your body into a living weapon, each claw becoming a weapon that can cut through Laws, Daos, and everything it touches.</p>
<p>In fact, now that you have received the Red Bone Chaos Devouring Art, cultivating the Obsidian Chaos Dragon Physique will be much easier. After all, what better way to temper the body than with devoured energy?"</p>
<p>"Lucky me, I guess," Kent laughed, and Veylan Seren laughed with him.</p>
<p>Afterward, he spent 5 minutes transferring the technique to him...</p>
<p>"Remember, kid, if you manage to free us, I will personally spend a million years forging you into a weapon so lethal that even Universe Lords would cower before you."</p>
<p>Veylan Seren said, his aura spreading through the air.</p>
<p>Kent nodded and replied, "I doubt it would take a million years, but I will take you up on that offer."</p>
<p>"Hahahaha, I like your sense of humor, kid. Good, this will make everything simple then," he laughed one last time before walking back to his seat.</p>
<p>"You know, when Fiannarel said you are someone who can see and attack both the past and the future, I had my doubts. However, now, I think you might just be the first person in the history of this world who can master the Essence of Existence."</p>
<p>Tribe Leader Zdzislav stated, causing the other Tribe Leaders to furrow their brows.</p>
<p>"Surely, you must be exaggerating here, Zdzislav," Tumenk, the leader of the Celestial Abyss Chaos Tribe, questioned.</p>
<p>Zdzislav, the leader of the Astral Eye Chaos Tribe, smiled and shook his head.</p>
<p>"I don’t know how he managed it, but rooted deep within his subconscious is the Root of Existence. If he can awaken it and let it bloom into an All-Seeing Chaos Existence Tree, then perhaps he would accomplish what the best of us couldn’t."</p>
<p>"This..."</p>
<p>The Tribe Leaders, and even Fiannarel, who knew just how big a deal the Essence of Existence was, looked at Kent in shock.</p>
<p>Sensing their gazes, Kent flipped his hair to the side and puffed his chest out. "Never underestimate the power of a Chaos Dragon."</p>
<p>The ladies in the hall rolled their eyes while Veylan Seren exploded into resounding laughter.</p>
