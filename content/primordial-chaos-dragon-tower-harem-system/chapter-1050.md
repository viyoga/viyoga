<p>"I can’t believe those fuckers actually have a Soul Extinguishing pill," Nefeli said, her expression dark...</p>
<p>"I only found out when I spied on their leader. It turned out they wanted to steal the soul spirit that was auctioned at the Brown Maple Auction House from the vice sect leader of the Heavens Eye sect.</p>
<p>I wanted to kill him then, but I wasn’t strong enough. Now, I can kill him before he knows how it happened."</p>
<p>"To think they actually have such a thing... This is just too shocking. I can’t believe they are actually colluding with the Evil Sect.</p>
<p>I will make sure they are discredited and branded as traitors, forever shunned from the Pill Worlds."</p>
<p>Kent laughed, watching her expression. "Then be ready to disband the other Pill Alliance, because I know for sure the Zephyros Pill Alliance also has the Soul Extinguishing pill, and I wouldn’t put it past the other Pill Worlds."</p>
<p>"This is just too unbelievable."</p>
<p>Kent shook his head, looking at the angry expression that appeared on Nefeli’s face.</p>
<p>She may have cultivated the persona of an Alchemist simply to stay hidden from her enemies.</p>
<p>However, over the years, the love for alchemy has taken root in her heart, and, without even knowing it, she has become an alchemist and a swordsman.</p>
<p>So, hearing that a place she assumed to be safe was actually being influenced by outside and vile forces made her extremely mad.</p>
<p>"Don’t worry about them, dear. Even if I can’t get rid of them before leaving, I will leave a few capable friends behind to help you handle them."</p>
<p>Nefeli nodded...</p>
<p>Kent kissed her, and then he left the room. Right when he walked out, he met Yuna, who was pacing back and forth.</p>
<p>"Yuna..." Kent called out, and Yuna ran toward him and took his right hand into hers.</p>
<p>"Big brother, that flame seed you gave me is mean," Yuna said, causing Kent to laugh.</p>
<p>He gave a mission to the seven monarchs before going to hell. Within those 30 days, they chased, fought, and collected as many Primordial flames as they could get their hands on.</p>
<p>In the end, the god inside the Pill Ascension Realm added some more, allowing Kent to not only have over a hundred primordial flame seeds with him, but also the majority of these flame seeds actually gain intelligence, allowing them to speak.</p>
<p>The one he gave to Yuna is the Heart Purity Primordial Flame.</p>
<p>Naturally, she was meant to be a bitch to someone like Yuna, who, despite being of age to know basic stuff like how to dress and talk like a lady, was not.</p>
<p>"She is not mean, Yuna; she is just teaching you how to be a proper lady."</p>
<p>"No, big brother, she is very mean. She called me a cockroach and a bug."</p>
<p>Kent chuckled. "Bring her out."</p>
<p>Yuna did, and when the blue flame seed appeared, Kent smiled and spoke, "Yuna was bullied and enslaved her entire life, so she doesn’t know the basic stuff a refined lady should know.</p>
<p>However, I believe if you bond with her and get to know her, you two can grow and help each other. You would even be surprised how happy you will become if you finally get to know her."</p>
<p>The flame seed didn’t say anything for a minute before she spoke. "Fine. But she must listen to me, and she shouldn’t reject my directions."</p>
<p>Kent turned and looked at Yuna. "You should listen to her and be sure to pick a thing or two from her."</p>
<p>"Okay. But if she calls me a bug again, I will drop her in dirty water."</p>
<p>"You wouldn’t." The Primordial Flame said...</p>
<p>"I would."</p>
<p>"You wouldn’t dare."</p>
<p>"Try me."</p>
<p>Kent could only smile, watching as Yuna and her new best friend bickered and walked away.</p>
<p>"They are going to have so much fun together."</p>
<p>With a smile on his face, Kent teleported away, appearing 80,000 km away. His destination is the Blue Flame Pill Palace.</p>
<p>His aim in going was to kill the main brains behind this foul organization. Of course, the immortal armor was still one of his objectives.</p>
<p>However, he wanted to make an example out of the Blue Flame Pill Palace. They dared to collude with people who used vile ways to make pills.</p>
<p>As an alchemist, Kent isn’t going to allow that.</p>
<p>When he appeared 80,000 km away, he saw a tall building in the distance. His soul map immediately covered it.</p>
<p>On the last floor, he saw some Pill Emperors concocting pills. Kent checked and saw that these people were quite experienced.</p>
<p>However, he wasn’t after them. His main targets were in a building 4 km away from this tall building.</p>
<p>Inside, he saw Vice Leader Vince and the old man.</p>
<p>"Looks like they managed to reverse the effect of the bone-corroding poison," Kent said, a mocking smile painting his face.</p>
<p>He then examined them for a moment before a smirk formed on his lips when he noticed something else.</p>
<p>"Though he paid a terrible price for that. Now he can’t walk again," Kent smiled.</p>
<p>He wanted the old bastard to die painfully; however, he seemed to have underestimated their resourcefulness.</p>
<p>"This doesn’t change anything. Since they are now in the same room, I will be sure to leave them screaming and in pain before finally dying this time around."</p>
<p>Kent decided to hear what they were saying first, then, at the right time, he would crash their party...</p>
<p>"Sect leader, I think we should move to the offensive and attack the Heavens Eye sect," Vince said, his expression dark.</p>
<p>"I am considering that too," the old man said, and Vince nodded, a small, wicked smile creeping at the corner of his mouth.</p>
<p>When he came back and met the Sect Master in this state, his blood boiled with the desire to kill all those involved.</p>
<p>However, aside from the mysterious person named Chaos, whom they were aware of, the other targets could be the Heaven’s Eye sect.</p>
<p>Naturally, they wanted to attack and kill every last member of that sect. As alchemists, killing them would be very simple. All they needed was a large dose of poison, and they would get the job done.</p>
<p>"I even have a Formation Expert ready to help with the formation protecting the sect."</p>
<p>"Oh, exciting. When do we plan on doing that?" Suddenly, another voice reached their ears, causing Vince to lunge at Kent.</p>
<p>"Kneel."</p>
<p>Kent gestured with his left hand, and the poor bastard crashed to his knees.</p>
<p>"Oh, please. I don’t want to be considered vile, so just sit down. I don’t want to force a cripple to kneel when I am about to torture and kill you in the most miserable way."</p>
<p>Kent smiled and walked toward a bowl of jellybean fruit, picked one, and threw it into his mouth.</p>
<p>Both the old man and Vince were left frozen, their bodies locked in place. Not even their eyelids were moving. It was like they had lost control over their bodies.</p>
<p>"Good fruit," Kent said.</p>
<p>He picked a few more, put them in his mouth, and savored the taste. When he had had enough, he turned and looked at the two hostages.</p>
<p>"Pardon my rudeness, you two. You can call me Chaos... and I believe this meeting is long overdue... don’t you think?"</p>
<p>A smile that caused the two to break into a showering sweat appeared on Kent’s face. They, at that moment, knew they had finally met the devil, and he looked handsome and very mischievous.</p>
