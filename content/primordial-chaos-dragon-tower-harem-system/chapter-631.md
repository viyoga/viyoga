<p>Kent emerged from the memory to a message that made him smile.</p>
<p>⟦Congratulations on mastering all 1,600,000 sword moves of the Genesis Path⟧</p>
<p>"So that is what all this was about. I knew reviewing this memory would be very helpful." Kent’s smile widened as he felt his body grow sharper, almost like he had become a weapon itself.</p>
<p>"I bet Jest never expected that one day he would need his own hard work to rise swiftly through the starry skies." If Jest were present, he would probably smack Kent silly for riding nonchalantly on his hard work.</p>
<p>But that is what he signed up for when he reincarnated, so he can blame himself for bringing it on.</p>
<p>"But to think I wasn’t the sole swordsman to rise through the ranks, it seems I underestimated the brilliance of many.</p>
<p>Just that they all couldn’t get to the peak, simply because the path they walked was limited, unlike mine. In other words, I should be called the father of the Primordial Sword Path, not the father of swordsmen.</p>
<p>But then again, I understand. I did not only pick the path that led to the peak, I also showed everyone there are different ways out there, and that following someone’s path won’t necessarily make you the greatest."</p>
<p>Kent sighed.</p>
<p>"But how the fuck is Jest going to get to an immortal world when he searched years but couldn’t find a path?" This question made Kent take out the second key and press it against his forehead.</p>
<p>However, instead of entering yet another memory, a diary appeared in his hand.</p>
<p>The title on it read, "Jest Diary 1/9."</p>
<p>"I see. So all the immersive memories were so that I could inherit the 1.6 memories," Kent concluded. However, there was something about the diary that made him feel like it was more than just a book.</p>
<p>Nevertheless, Kent flipped it open and started to read.</p>
<p>[So I found a way to get into the immortal worlds. However, I need to travel back to the Sword Valley and enter the no man’s land.</p>
<p>Of course, this sounds like a bad idea, but the only way to get to the immortal world is to break the last sword move left by the genius swordsmen of the primordial era.</p>
<p>I might die; however, knowing my people are safe and those responsible for slave trading are dealt with, I feel like dying is not too bad.</p>
<p>I know I’m a lunatic, but I have a feeling my reincarnations will be even more eccentric than I. So what can I say? It is genetics, I guess..."_"]</p>
<p>"This bastard," Kent smiled, feeling amused by something he had written himself—well, a more lunatic version of himself who lived billions of years ago.</p>
<p>"But I guess he has a point. The slaves were the priority; whatever crazy thing we did after that is just us being stupid.</p>
<p>Though I dare ask, doesn’t he have a girlfriend? Then again, I saw his life, and even after 250 years, he is still single and a virgin. Can’t wait to hear when he will get laid."</p>
<p>Kent burst out laughing like a maniac. It wasn’t every day a person gets amused by their past self’s sex life. But then again, it is Kent, the Shameless Dragon.</p>
<p>He turned back to the diary.</p>
<p>[At long last, I arrived at the Crossing. However, the no man’s land is no easy place to traverse. I am a Sword Emperor and a Spirit King, but the beasts here are just too much for even a Spirit Emperor to handle.</p>
<p>The last beast I killed was a Spirit Lord who had mastered the Flame to the Perfect stage. I nearly died, and while I managed to kill it in the end, to my future self, if you are reading this, never fight a Mountain Flame Goat if you don’t have control over any flame art.</p>
<p>But I survived, and this time, I am a step away from seeing immortals. Although I must say, I wonder what they look like, what they wear, and how they talk.</p>
<p>I’m not particularly skilled in fashion, of course. Aunty Emri made sure I took the best clothes she had at her store, but what if I look like a beggar in the immortal worlds?</p>
<p>Aaah, this is a headache... However, I will probably have to adapt more quickly before I become a laughingstock to some young immortals.</p>
<p>Lord knows I would love to shut them up with my sword.</p>
<p>Ultimately, violence is the means. Okay, bye for now, I want to see if my life is a hardy or a softy.]</p>
<p>"True Jest, your dressing sucks. No wonder no lady showed any interest in you. Though Aunty Emri indeed made you look more like a bachelor, I wonder how you will adapt in an immortal world," Kent laughed.</p>
<p>"That goat really handed your ass to—"</p>
<p>"Wait, how am I remembering something I am reading as if I were there and saw everything?" Kent immediately recognized something was happening.</p>
<p>It was as if, as he was reading, he appeared to be there, witnessing everything, just as he had when he was immersed in Jest memories. It felt as though he was reliving the memories.</p>
<p>"Dori, what is happening?" Kent had to call a lawyer.</p>
<p>⟪Why doesn’t the master finish reading the diary first? The answer will come when you finish everything. Me telling you will just spoil the fun⟫</p>
<p>Kent sighed and then returned to the diary.</p>
<p>[Well, that is just great. So it turns out that to break the sword move and create the spatial channel, I must first master the sword techniques used in the attack.</p>
<p>I studied it, and when I gained access to the essence of the sword technique, the owner of the sword’s move appeared in the form of divine consciousness.</p>
<p>He said his name was Immortal Monarch Liu Yang, the sect leader of the Transcendent Sword sect, and the sword move he used is the second form of the Transcendent Heavenly Sword Technique.</p>
<p>He gave me the whole technique, and I must master the first two stages. So, I guess it’s back to the No Man’s Land, then. I need to fight stronger monsters now, and I doubt I will come out alive this time.</p>
<p>...Wish me luck.]</p>
