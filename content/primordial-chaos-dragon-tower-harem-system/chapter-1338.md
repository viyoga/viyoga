<p>’Another one, huh?’</p>
<p>Kent smiled, seeing he had obtained yet again another Soul Warrior Candidate.</p>
<p>This means, aside from Calix and the one he was in the process of creating, he had obtained a third candidate who, from the look of things, would become a Core Immortal after the creation process.</p>
<p>’I wonder if he will maintain all four Immortal Qi after becoming a Soul Warrior,’ Kent said inwardly, his mind already looking forward to seeing what would become of his three Soul Warriors.</p>
<p>Vexthra already told him that when entering the Star Gateway, he cannot take anyone with him or summon anyone once he is inside.</p>
<p>This means he will not be able to talk to his wives and legion.</p>
<p>However, what if he doesn’t have to summon anyone—what if the summon is instead a technique he can use in a way it cannot be considered summoning?</p>
<p>In a way, the Soul Warriors are merely a technique linked to his Soul Universe, so since he won’t be barred from using his technique while inside the Star Gateway, it means he can summon his Soul Warriors to aid him while there.</p>
<p>The heavens know he will need them for what is coming.</p>
<p>Unlike the Void Temple that was compromised by his Void Legion, the Star Gateway isn’t going to be that simple. Kent had no idea how hard it would be, but Vexthra told him it would be extremely dangerous.</p>
<p>So for now, he was preparing for that. It was also why he planned on using the flame source he found on the first floor to create his second flame core and form more flame veins.</p>
<p>However, before that, he had to kill the other three beasts required to start the creation process of the portal that would take him much closer to the edge of the galaxy.</p>
<p>’I should handle one thing at a time then.’</p>
<p>Kent turned to Axiom, Kailan, and Kol... "Give me a few minutes to handle something important, then we can leave."</p>
<p>He entered the Soul Universe and quickly made his way to the location where the creation process would take place. The second Soul Warrior, currently in the process of being created, would take 30 days.</p>
<p>This time around, it seemed the process would take even longer. After he initiated the creation process, he saw it would take 42 days.</p>
<p>Naturally, that was a long time. That is a lot of time to wait, but while it should have taken him years to clear the void temple as expected, he knew 42 days were just around the corner.</p>
<p>Iinitial estimation was 5 years tops. But it had been less than 5 months since he entered the void forest, and now, he is leaving.</p>
<p>He soon left the Soul Universe and started preparing to leave the temple. A few minutes later, he was standing outside the red pagoda.</p>
<p>Once outside, he waved his hand, and five runes shaped like keys flew and merged with the pagoda. A few seconds later, the pagoda shrank and vanished into the Soul Universe.</p>
<p>Kent soon started making his way out of the Void Forest.</p>
