<p>[Inside Kent’s Soul Sea - Blood Platform]</p>
<p>By the outside world’s time, Kent had been in seclusion for three and a half weeks. However, considering he entered the Void Temple while inside the tower, he had actually been in seclusion for months.</p>
<p>In the end, spending that much time wasn’t wasted.</p>
<p>Inside his soul sea, he stood calm, his gaze fixed on the large red core now hovering inside the second blood gate.</p>
<p>The first core was formed from a green flame source, so it came out green. However, the second came from a red flame source. Regardless, seeing that he had formed his second flame core successfully made him happy.</p>
<p>"Finally," he muttered.</p>
<p>All over his soul sea, the power of an absolute flame could be felt. This meant that aside from his Ice element, his Flame element had now also reached the absolute stage.</p>
<p>But that isn’t all.</p>
<p>Before entering seclusion, he had only 120,000 flame veins. However, now that he has formed his second flame core, more flame veins are being created.</p>
<p>So as he stood on the platform, looking up at the red flame core, he could feel his strength growing. That, and the fact that he could sense his flame was now at the absolute stage, gave him some sense of satisfaction.</p>
<p>According to what Agni told him, once he manages to form his second flame core, he would be able to use the [Finger of Destruction] even more.</p>
<p>Of course, Kent knew the dangers involved with using such a technique, but he could not deny its power.</p>
<p>Of course, he created his own to compensate for moments he can’t use the [Finger of Destruction].</p>
<p>However, if push came to shove, he knew he could count on that technique and his flame cores to harness its true power.</p>
<p>Until today, he could only use about 10% of its power. However, with his second flame core formed and more flame veins forming, he was sure to push past 50%.</p>
<p>That would surely give him the power to overwhelm even stronger opponents.</p>
<p>Aside from that, there was also his speed, which he could enhance using the flame veins. So far, he could tell that against an Immortal King, their speeds were matched.</p>
<p>Of course, many factors could be considered when determining how fast he can move. In most cases, if the right requirements are met, even a Core Immortal could match his speed.</p>
<p>There are several beings out there who specialize in agility.</p>
<p>An example is the Flame Roc Race that Kaelith is from. They could be considered one of the fastest races in the universe. A person from such a race would be able to easily overpower him in speed, even if the gap between them wasn’t much.</p>
<p>So adding more flame veins would boost his speed and give him an extra edge. And adding his Jade Void Wings, there was a good chance that if he were to face Noctyrr again, he would be able to outrun him.</p>
<p>Kent left his soul sea but didn’t leave the Void Temple. While his core was already formed, he still had the flame veins slowly accumulating, so he remained inside to allow them to form.</p>
<p>Five days later, the 340,000th flame vein was created, and the process stopped again. This meant he had to form a third flame core before he could proceed.</p>
<p>While that was a bummer, the fact that he now had 340,000 flame veins under his command took away the sadness of not being able to form more.</p>
<p>He went from 120,000 flame veins to 340,000. That was more than he could ask for, so he took the win, knowing that one day, he would be able to go even higher.</p>
<p>"This is a win for me. Asking for more would just be me being selfish," he smiled slightly and stood up. He examined his body, then, after confirming he was fine, exited the Void Temple and quickly returned to his soul universe.</p>
<p>Considering how the tower can be restricted easily, he knew he had to keep important things like the void temple inside his soul universe, where, regardless of any restrictions, he could easily access it.</p>
<p>Afterward, he entered the Harem Tower to freshen up. When he arrived there, he saw his wives weren’t back, so he asked the tower to update him on what he had missed.</p>
<p>[While the master was away, the legion and the master’s wives remained on the 9th floor of the Planet Killer Dungeon, where they consumed the rewards from the mission.</p>
<p>Afterward, continued waiting for Lady Unity to finish her transformation. She had yet to come out of the cocoon, so they were waiting for her.</p>
<p>Also, there is someone inside the tower city claiming to be your descendant.]</p>
<p>"Wait, what?" Kent nearly tripped and fell when he heard the last sentence.</p>
<p>"Repeat that again, tower."</p>
<p>[Her name is Queen Eirdis. Sir Axiom, Kailan, and Kol found her when they were roaming Planet Kanei. She has a young daughter, Xandira, who is only 18 months old.</p>
<p>According to her, she is your descendant, born of someone you once gave your blood essence to. I checked her bloodline, and while it is not the same as yours, it indeed has Chaos Essence, meaning she is telling the truth.]</p>
<p>"Oh, that is unexpected but a bit expected." Kent sighed, then a smile appeared on his face.</p>
<p>During the Primordial Era, while roaming in the Blood System, Chaos met a young dragon who had also lost everything at the hands of the Forsaken Race.</p>
<p>It was the first time Chaos connected with someone after he lost Elina to the same vile Forsaken Race. They traveled for a few years since this young dragon also wanted to grow stronger and get revenge.</p>
<p>His dedication touched Chaos. However, while he would have loved to have him by his side, this young dragon was just too weak. He would have died before meeting the first Forsaken being.</p>
<p>However, despite knowing all this, he still wanted to train and become stronger. Chaos tried many ways to persuade him to go on and live a life away from revenge and bloodshed, but this young dragon remained steadfast.</p>
<p>In the end, Chaos decided to bestow his blood upon him and leave him to his fate. The last time they met was that fateful evening when he gave a drop of his blood to the young dragon—something he had never done before.</p>
<p>He had never heard from him again, nor did he bother to even look for him. So hearing that someone was claiming to be his descendant made him remember his longtime friend.</p>
<p>"Did she say anything about where the original wielder of the bloodline is?"</p>
<p>[Yes. According to her, there are rumors flying around that he vanished billions of years ago after offending someone from the Universe Council.]</p>
<p>"Hmmm. Then again," Kent clicked his tongue, a bit annoyed at hearing about the Universe Council.</p>
<p>After learning he was the one who killed the Night Riders and the role the Universe Council played in that, his dark heart had already registered them as enemies.</p>
<p>Hearing their name again only added to the hatred he had for them.</p>
<p>"Let me freshen up and then meet with this person. Who knows, maybe the family I never knew I needed has finally started finding their way back to me, even if destiny is being her usual bitchy self."</p>
<p>Somewhere far away, Lady Destiny smirked, then unwrapped a new lollipop and started sucking on it as she had her mud bath.</p>
