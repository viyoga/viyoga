<p>Kent appeared inside the Legion Tower and immediately entered the Necrosmith Forge since he wanted to start the first creation of his undead legion.</p>
<p>The moment he appeared, he moved toward a panel and pressed his palm on it. It lit up, and a voice spoke. Of course, the voice was similar to Akira in a way.</p>
<p>Naturally, the Creator Necromancy class was created by Akira, so it was only fitting for her voice to control the forge. It was a one-of-a-kind class.</p>
<p>[Welcome to the Necrosmith Forge. Do you want to create an undead?]</p>
<p>"Yes," Kent replied. He wasn’t surprised, simply because he had Gaia and Akira speaking in his head every single day.</p>
<p>[Do you want automatic creations or custom creations?]</p>
<p>"Custom," Kent answered.</p>
<p>The Necro Forge allows him to create undead by giving them names, which include their gender and even their nicknames if he desires, along with personalities and a job class of his choosing.</p>
<p>Of course, if the materials he adds have unique properties, the Forge will tweak them to accommodate all properties.</p>
<p>[Choose the name of your creation.]</p>
<p>"Kaizo. I want his name to be Kaizo, and a nickname that matches his personality, so do well to match that," Kent said.</p>
<p>Kent wasn’t about to use the first creation as a trial run. No, this was something he wanted to see succeed, and if he was lucky enough, he would be walking away with a powerful subordinate.</p>
<p>[Give the personalities of your creation.]</p>
<p>"This is tough, but I want my creation to have the following personalities: Cold but Honorable, Protective, Relentless in Battle, Loyal to His Cause, Fierce and Dominant, and Unyielding."</p>
<p>Kent gave the order, and the voice spoke again.</p>
<p>[Give a job class you want your creation to possess. Note that in the end, the class can change, but it will have the same root properties.]</p>
<p>"Brute Fighter," Kent said, and the cauldron, which had been still a moment ago, stirred and started humming.</p>
<p>[Add your creation materials.]</p>
<p>The cauldron-like item moved closer and opened for him to put the items in. Kent felt like this was just too childish, but then again, it was created by a child.</p>
<p>He knew Gaia and Akira were millions of years old, but he treated them like children, for in his eyes, they were children.</p>
<p>He looked to his left, and the bodies of all he had killed could be seen frozen and arranged for him to use. He contemplated whether he was to raise the items with his hands.</p>
<p>But before he could think, one body moved through the air and entered the cauldron.</p>
<p>’So that is how it works. It uses my thoughts,’</p>
<p>Kent said to himself before using his thoughts to move three Transcendent-level bodies, ten Great Sages. Then he extended his hand, and the skull appeared.</p>
<p>It was large, and the lightning crackling around it showed it was no ordinary item. He tossed it inside and then flicked his hand, dropping a hundred billion spirit stones into the forge like it was nothing.</p>
<p>’I have to multiply my wealth before tomorrow’s auction,’ he said to himself and then tossed the broken spear he bought from the auction into the cauldron.</p>
<p>He added some other stuff.</p>
<p>"That will be all," he said.</p>
<p>[Creation of the first undead has started. Estimated time of completion: 18 hours.]</p>
<p>"Ha. Looks like I’ll have to wait for a while," Kent sighed, then left and went back to the tower shop directly.</p>
<p>He called for Akira, who appeared instantly.</p>
<p>"Akira, tell me the Necrosmith shop uses the tower time when I use it inside the tower," Kent asked, but Akira smiled and scratched her head.</p>
<p>"Just great. Now, I have to wait 18 hours in outside world time before I get to meet my first undead," Kent sighed again.</p>
<p>[On the bright side, you’ll be having so much fun at the auctions tomorrow, so the time will pass quickly. Plus, you might just be surprised by what you’ll get, so don’t think too much about it.]</p>
<p>Akira said, trying to comfort her master.</p>
<p>"I’ll punish you later for making such a mistake," Kent said with a smile.</p>
<p>[Don’t punish me, please. I promise to fix it when you reach the stage to upgrade your class.] Akira pleaded, but Kent just gave her a teasing smile.</p>
<p>He then thought of a compromise, and his smile bloomed when something came to mind.</p>
<p>"Then how about you pick a skill from each of my ladies and make them crazily powerful?" Kent suggested.</p>
<p>[Would that take care of the punishment you had in mind?] she asked. Though she knew Kent was just playing with her, she liked to role-play.</p>
<p>In fact, before coming to this world, she was a popular anime voice and stage actor in a certain world—one she is currently praying they never visit in this life or the next.</p>
<p>Loli Queen Akiriana.</p>
<p>That was the name she used in that world, and they would come back to hunt her later.</p>
<p>"That would indeed handle the punishment," Kent assured her. "But while you’re here, what can you tell me about your third sister?"</p>
<p>[Dori? Well, she’s a weirdo if you ask me. While Gaia can see and go anywhere in the cosmos, Dori is all about thinking and coming up with insane theories.</p>
<p>She’s always on about this and that. She never truly makes sense when doing something. But she’s brilliant, and having her will be a good addition to your strengths.</p>
<p>However, if people who talk a lot bore you, then be sure to lock her up somewhere when you bring her," Akira said nonchalantly]</p>
<p>"It looks like you miss her," Kent said.</p>
<p>[Yeah. I miss that brat; she’s just too careless sometimes,] Akira sighed. She may act like it, but she cares about her sisters just as much as they care about her.</p>
<p>"Don’t worry, I’ll save her soon enough," Kent said, pulling up the interface for the store.</p>
<p>Gaia wasn’t around simply because she said she needed to focus all her attention on creating the paths Kent gave her.</p>
<p>He selected the Spirit Stone Roller Multiplier Bingo card and immediately purchased two. He then retrieved a manual from the storage and handed it to Akira.</p>
<p>"Give this to Gaia; it’s another path she must work on."</p>
<p>[She’s going to be ecstatic. Then I’ll be on my way.]</p>
<p>Akira left and appeared at the tower’s core, where Gaia and different versions of herself were working actively on the projects Kent had given her.</p>
<p>"You have another project, Gaia." She said, making the Loli, who had a pencil tucked behind her ear, like an architecture hard at work, smile cheerfully as she received the Path of Souls and Spirits.</p>
<p>It was the path the universe had gifted Elsa.</p>
<p>Akira smiled, and then she started weaving.</p>
