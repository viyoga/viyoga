<p>"I can’t wait to meet her," Unity said, standing beside Kent, clinging onto his hand.</p>
<p>Her sisters were also around. The time for the tower to sync with Vexthra and create a secure connection that would allow Kent to summon Vexthra to the tower was almost here, and thus, they were waiting.</p>
<p>This connection will allow Vexthra to enter the tower but move through the gaps in the laws without any backlash.</p>
<p>Lady Lani and Nara were also around, with the only one absent being Lady Vari, who was minding her own business by cultivating in peace.</p>
<p>Hearing her disciple was safe put her at peace, so she was cultivating while Kent and his ladies waited for the goddess to land in the tower.</p>
<p>"I wonder what she looks like," Lilian said, already looking forward to finding out.</p>
<p>Kent could only smile at their childish behavior. They all wanted to meet the goddess, but it seemed he underestimated their desire.</p>
<p>It was like chicks waiting for their mother on the other side of the river.</p>
<p>If only he knew, currently, Vexthra was also pacing back and forth, waiting for the six hours to pass. Just like how her sisters wanted to meet her, she was also eager to meet them.</p>
<p>She had wanted this moment for a long time, and as such, this was her chance to meet them. Though she will be spending the next couple of weeks, perhaps years with them, she wanted to meet them asap.</p>
<p>"I wonder what they look like," she muttered, already hungry to meet them.</p>
<p>She walked back to her perfectly laid bed and sat down, anxiously tapping her foot on the ground as she waited for the time to come.</p>
<p>A few minutes later, she felt a connection form between her and something profound. This brought a smile to her face. She knew the Tower had finally latched onto her.</p>
<p>Inside the tower, a message appeared in Kent’s vision, bringing a smile to his face.</p>
<p>⟪Tower Message: A connection has been formed between you and Goddess Vexthra. You can now summon her into the tower.</p>
<p>She will be allowed a thirty-day free stay. After that, 100,000 tower points will be deducted for every day she stays in the tower.</p>
<p>Failure to pay means she will be teleported back to the god realm, and it will cost 10,000,000 to summon her back.⟫</p>
<p>The message was like a joke before Kent’s eyes.</p>
<p>Tower points were something he wouldn’t lack again, considering that at the moment, he had already racked up 45 million tower points thanks to Zareth, the rest of the undead, and Veydris, who were currently farming in the twelve SS-rank dungeons he gave them.</p>
<p>Every kill rewarded him with tower points, so he was now rich in that regard. And he would only grow richer as they started the Calamity Quest in the world of Dusk.</p>
<p>In a few days’ time, he would be swimming in Tower Points.</p>
<p>Kent looked at his ladies and smiled.</p>
<p>"Everyone, welcome your big sister," he said, snapping his fingers. A golden portal opened the moment he snapped his finger.</p>
<p>The ladies immediately focused their attention on the opened portal, expecting to see their sister already.</p>
<p>Thankfully, Vexthra was also eager to meet them, so she didn’t make things hard for them by keeping them in suspense.</p>
<p>Through the portal, the most gorgeous woman any of them had ever set their sights on walked through.</p>
<p>It wasn’t a secret that Kent was packing some of the world’s most beautiful fairies inside his tower. However, watching the silver-red head walk through the portal, they knew she was on another level of beauty.</p>
<p>Vexthra was clad in a red gown that clung to her skin, accentuating her assets.</p>
<p>She truly picked the best dress to impress her sisters, for her hips were well framed, and looking at her cat-like steps, it was safe to assume she was really making them see the difference.</p>
<p>Kent already expected something like this, so he wasn’t surprised when he saw her move through the portal.</p>
<p>With the divine goddess aura around her, it was safe to say she was indeed making a difference, charming her sisters with her smile.</p>
<p>She walked through the portal and stopped when she was a few steps from Kent, who was smiling at the goddess who had finally made her way into the tower.</p>
<p>"Hello, everyone," Vexthra said, smiling at the ladies. The ladies were charmed, each standing there with widen eyes.</p>
<p>Li Hua, who was standing on Kent’s left, tugged on his sleeve, causing Kent to turn toward her.</p>
<p>"Is she a goddess?" she asked in a hushed tone, making both Kent and Vexthra smile.</p>
<p>"Yes, I am a goddess, and no need to whisper. You can call me Big Sister Vexthra," Vexthra replied, causing Lilian to smile. Of course, Kent, who knew what this smile meant, knew what was coming next.</p>
<p>While the ladies were charmed by Vexthra’s presence, Li Hua was an exception. Vexthra wasn’t playing with her, so she kept her focus and mind as her own.</p>
<p>The divine aura around her charmed them, making every last one of them stare at her. Vexthra was up to her antics, and Kent could only smile.</p>
<p>Li Hua was also safe from her divine influence. But this was her chance to have a new, beautiful, and rich big sister despite having dozens of them.</p>
<p>She moved closer and started examining Vexthra like she was a patient in Li Hua’s health center. After a while, she smiled and walked back to Kent, jumping into his arms and bringing her mouth closer to his ear.</p>
<p>"Do you think she likes banana cakes?" she asked Kent this time, doing her best to lower her tone even further.</p>
<p>Kent, who knew the brat in his arms meant nothing good by asking this question, chose to entertain her. He was already made aware of Li Hua’s love for money.</p>
<p>She always liked to have money in her life, for she and her grandma were always on the run, and money was the most valuable item for them.</p>
<p>Seeing a beautiful lady, she knew what had to be done.</p>
<p>Vexthra just laughed, knowing her character, for Kent had told her everything when he visited her the first time. But watching her in action was much better, so she was smiling her head off.</p>
<p>Kent was also the same.</p>
<p>"Vexthra, don’t you think it’s time you stopped messing with your sister?" Kent said, looking at how charmed the ladies had become. They were powerless under her divine aura.</p>
<p>Even her smile alone was more than enough to charm them, but Vexthra was using her divine aura to amplify the effect of her charm. So there was no escaping or recovering from that.</p>
<p>"Unity, you can stop faking it. I know you are not charmed," Vexthra, instead of releasing her sister, said to Unity, who was faking being charmed.</p>
<p>However, when exposed, she smiled and jumped into Vexthra’s arms, planting her face in her chest.</p>
<p>"Looks like I was right. She wouldn’t be affected by divine energy," Vexthra said, hugging her tightly.</p>
<p>"This is great, it looks like I wasn’t the only goddess in the tower; there are two of us," Kent, who was watching the two beauties hug, raised his brow, hearing what Vexthra said.</p>
