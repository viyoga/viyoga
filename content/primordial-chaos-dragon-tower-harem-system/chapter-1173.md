<p>"Let’s say he is indeed a Faceless Agent, that he had somehow joined a secret organization of assassins that once followed the Faceless Assassin and still does.</p>
<p>What then does it mean for him? I know assassins like to keep to themselves. If Liora hadn’t fought multiple times, I wouldn’t have known she was an assassin. So what does this mean for Kent?</p>
<p>Is his life in danger? Does this mean he will be leaving, or is this all one big misunderstanding?"</p>

<p>That would be dangerous, and well, that scares him.</p>
<p>Her question is valid, too. What if the symbol on Kent’s forehead was one giant misunderstanding?</p>

<p>"That is the aura of a seven-star assassin... He has jumped straight to Shadow King."</p>

<p>Suddenly, the symbol appeared again, but this time, it was different. This changed the expressions on the ladies’ faces.</p>
<p>First, it was a broken dagger; however, now, it seems to have changed. Instead of a broken dagger, it is now two daggers crossed to form an ’X’.</p>

<p>Kent could only look at his goddess with a quiet smile.</p>

<p>She actually got to do it, but instead of killing him, she helped him calm down. The bloodlust he felt nearly drove Kent to lose his mind.</p>
<p>If she hadn’t put him to sleep, he would have become a monster for a limited time.</p>

<p>"Don’t worry about it, Kent. We just want you happy and safe, so seeing you are safe now, we are happy," Nara said, brushing her hands across Kent’s hairless chest.</p>
<p>Since her boobs are acting as a pillow for the Chaos Dragon, she has his entire chest to herself.</p>

<p>Kent took a deep breath, his mind going back to the moment he snapped. "It was due to a side effect from one of the steps in my cultivation. However, now that it is out of the way, it will never happen again, at least for the time being."</p>
<p>"But it will happen again, right?"</p>
<p>"Probably. But I will work toward preventing it."</p>
<p>The ladies nodded, but they clearly are worried about him. However, for now, since he said he is fine and that it wouldn’t be for a while before he loses his mind again, they will believe him.</p>

<p>However, once he started forming the blood tattoos, the bloodlust side effect would become an ability he could control.</p>
<p>Whenever his bloodlust is active, not only would he become extremely powerful, but he would even become several times more dangerous.</p>
<p>Just that his mind would shut down, and he wouldn’t be able to differentiate allies from enemies.</p>
<p>So for now, he would hold back and hope that when his next bath happens, the bloodlust wouldn’t activate when he is with people he wouldn’t dare hurt.</p>
