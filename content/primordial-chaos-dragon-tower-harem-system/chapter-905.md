<p>The Forsaken Race isn’t exactly ugly.</p>
<p>If anything, the thousands charging toward Kaizo and his team are rather good-looking. Most have white skin with red horns, just like the faces that have been appearing in the mirror whenever Amix calls the one she refers to as her mother.</p>
<p>They are not pale like the normal vampires. They just have white skin with red horns and imposing heights. Among the thousands who charged forward, 30% are at the Core Formation stage, with the rest ranging between Spirit Ascension and Spirit King.</p>
<p>"How are we going to survive this?" an inner disciple said as he turned and ran away.</p>
<p>If only they knew running wouldn’t take them anywhere, they would have waited for their death—or at least stayed to see what Alina was capable of.</p>
<p>Alina was the only person who charged forward, surprising everyone who had gathered the courage to stand their ground and fight the forsaken race.</p>
<p>Vexthra, who was watching Kaizo, wondered what he was planning by sending only Alina forward. She wanted to see if giving the helm to Kaizo was the right thing or the wrong call.</p>
<p>Alina moved and stopped when she was far enough away from her team. With a flick of her hand, a disc-shaped item appeared.</p>
<p>The items have runic markings on them, with each showing a deadly combination of arrays ready to be unleashed.</p>
<p>"This will be enough to cause some chaos."</p>
<p>A smile appeared on her face as she channeled her energy into it.</p>
<p>The moment her energy entered the disc, a formation emerged from within and rose into the air. Alina didn’t immediately activate it like she did with the previous ones.</p>
<p>She took out a small rune brush and drew runes quickly in the air before sending them flying into the larger formation.</p>
<p>All this happened within seconds, but by the time the Forsaken Race warriors came within 2 km of her, Alina had already completed her formation.</p>
<p>"Eternal Darkness."</p>
<p>The formation expanded, and in an instant, the 20 km radius around the Forsaken Race warriors was plunged into perpetual darkness.</p>
<p>This kind of darkness was absolute.</p>
<p>"Nice one, Alina." Kaizo then turned to Vari, Lani, and the other assassins in the legion. "I really need her head."</p>
<p>Vexthra raised her brow, impressed by the brilliance of Kaizo’s move.</p>
<p>’He is smart. Very smart to come up with such a move.’ She knew that in this very moment, inside the formation, every last Forsaken warrior was blinded both in sight and in senses.</p>
<p>Even their ears can’t hear anything.</p>
<p>Naturally, this gave way for the assassins in the legion to do their unholy work.</p>
<p>"Hahahaha!" Lani’s laughter filled the air as she moved through the shadows, wielding her dagger. Vari was the same, but instead of using her white haired form, which is regarded as her holy or justice side, she was clad in black, with even her pupils dark as she moved.</p>
<p>This shows she was coming for their lives, and she doesn’t need any justification to claim them.</p>
<p>Inside the darkness, Lani appeared behind a Spirit King who, despite being in such close proximity, only managed to sense a calm presence, but before he could identify where it came from, his head came rolling.</p>
<p>Lani wasted no time and moved to the next, her laughter echoing through the darkness. Soon, heads began rolling.</p>
<p>Vari was no different. Wherever she passed, heads fell.</p>
<p>Nyxira, one of the deadliest assassins in Elsa’s Soul Sentinels, was also within the darkness, where she could clearly see her targets.</p>
<p>However, unlike Vari and Lani, she wasn’t going for the kill. Instead, she was merely placing marks on enemies before moving away.</p>
<p>She had a deadly ability that required her to mark her targets in the same way Nyss also marks hers.</p>
<p>Within the span of five minutes, thousands lay dead, with many still struggling to escape the darkness.</p>
<p>Far in the distance, the lady who had given the command to charge frowned. "That is a Tier 14 formation... just where did that formation expert come from?"</p>
<p>Amix, who was standing beside the lady, looked in the direction of Alina and frowned. She didn’t know where she came from, and that alone was something nobody wanted.</p>
<p>Knowing nothing about your enemy is always bad.</p>
<p>"I don’t know where she came from, but I have a way to kill her." Amix retrieved a vial from her space ring and handed it to the lady.</p>
<p>"This is the Heart-Exploding Poison. We just have to get close to her, and she will die screaming." The lady took the poison and looked toward Alina.</p>
<p>"The main strike force will be coming out in the next ten minutes. The moment they get here, all of this farce will be over. However, if we don’t do anything before these ten minutes are up, every last warrior will die."</p>
<p>The lady looked at the darkness before her, and her anger boiled even more.</p>
<p>"But then again, if we let them all die, then the chances of using the bodies for whatever we have planned for the vampires will be even more deadly."</p>
<p>Amix, who wasn’t expecting the lady to abandon her men in battle, frowned slightly; however, before she could say anything, the lady pushed her, sending her flying back several meters.</p>
<p>Right where Amix had stood, a dark figure appeared like smoke and stabbed her dagger at the lady, who used her superior cultivation base to evade by leaping back.</p>
<p>However, when she landed, she felt her leg sink, and she was swallowed by darkness.</p>
<p>Far in the distance, Elyndra, Kent’s fifth undead and also the strongest in the legion, formed a seal right as the lady emerged from another path of darkness.</p>
<p>"Space Lock."</p>
<p>In an instant, the 10 km radius around them was locked down.</p>
<p>"Where am I?" the lady asked, looking around. Her eyes landed on a beauty with silvery-white hair, and her anger burst out. She charged forward, stabbing a sword that appeared in her hand at Elyndra.</p>
<p>However, the undead beauty merely smiled and waved her hand. "Spatial Descent: Infinity Fall."</p>
<p>The stabbing strike came to a halt, and then the lady felt her body start falling. "What is this?"</p>
<p>She panicked, unsure why she was falling, yet she wasn’t hitting the ground. Even worse, she tried to use the void, but she felt her surroundings locked, preventing her from opening it.</p>
<p>"I need her head!" Kaizo, who still hadn’t received the head, shouted.</p>
<p>However, no one minded him, for they knew assassinating a Peak Spirit Emperor wasn’t something they could do...</p>
<p>Well, assassinating a Peak Spirit Emperor wasn’t something that could be achieved easily. However, among Kent’s undead, there was one who had the means to kill the Peak Spirit Emperor’s lady.</p>
<p>Naturally, she was the first to move, even before Kaizo gave the command.</p>
<p>So when the third command came from Kaizo, Velmora, who was hidden inside the Forsaken lady’s shadow, emerged, and with one swift swing of her deadly scythe, the Spirit Emperor lady, someone said to be above them all, lost her head, shocking everyone.</p>
<p>Lady Liora, who was standing beside Lady Enzi, raised her brow. "Shadow Displacement," she muttered, unsure how to feel at the moment.</p>
