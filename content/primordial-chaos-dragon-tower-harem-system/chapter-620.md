<p>"Astral Blade Fall"</p>
<p>Selene activated a skill, summoning dozens of radiant swords to fall on the hellhound like meteors. She and her sisters were coordinating their attacks well, something Vexthra, who was standing far away, seemed happy about.</p>
<p>She analyzed their skills and battle styles and then formed the teams accordingly.</p>
<p>As Selene’s summoned swords rained down, Elsa moved close to the hellhound, unleashing three sword arcs that sliced through the air, arriving at the same time the summoned swords did.</p>
<p>The hellhound, being a spirit king, was able to raise a shield around himself. The swords and arcs landed on it, creating a small crack.</p>
<p>"Now," Zaila said, and Selene moved. Her sword vibrated, unleashing Celestial energy from it. She stabbed her sword forward, landing on the shield created by the hellhound.</p>
<p>However, it couldn’t go through.</p>
<p>The shield was much more powerful than they anticipated. However, that wasn’t something they hadn’t seen coming. In fact, that was what they were aiming for.</p>
<p>The moment her sword landed on the shield, Zaila made her move.</p>
<p>"Mind Shock."</p>
<p>She unleashed a mental attack that struck the bastard hard in the head. This caused the hellhound to lose control of the shield. And when he did, Vari was there with her white hair, waiting for him.</p>
<p>"Bye, stranger."</p>
<p>Just like that, the spirit king who could bring any middle world to its knees lost his head. Vari looked at the headless body vanish into the Necro Forge, a smile playing on her lips.</p>
<p>"Good work, ladies... Now, let’s go handle the Spirit Ascension first." They trusted their sisters to know they would be able to handle their opponents, so they didn’t even consider helping them.</p>
<p>Thankfully, Vari’s team had Zaila, who was just too powerful for her own good. Despite her primordial spirit now being at Tier 1, her mind space was so vast that her Tier 12 mind attacks managed even to affect a Spirit King.</p>
<p>That shows just how important it is to have a bigger mind space or a soul sea, or other spaces yet to be discovered. The bigger the spaces, the more you can utilize your skills.</p>
<p>The moment the first spirit king was taken care of, it became an open market for more to fall prey to. The second team to handle their spirit king was Alina’s team.</p>
<p>From the start of their battle, Alina stood back, drawing in the air. Only Camilla, Mara, and Elaine were fighting.</p>
<p>But even so, they weren’t struggling much, considering they had all become monsters in their own right. They managed to hold the Spirit King back long enough for Alina to complete her formation.</p>
<p>Thankfully, seven minutes later, she was done, and that was when the battle also came to an end. A large sword formed in the air, and the formation locked the Spirit King’s body down, causing the sword to pierce through him.</p>
<p>His body was pinned to the ground before it vanished into the forge.</p>
<p>"Now, I can easily draw that formation in under four minutes." The ladies smiled, hearing Alina’s words. She possessed a skill that enabled her to draw formations and arrays she had previously drawn, much faster.</p>
<p>"Zaila and the others are done and killing the Spirit Ascensions. Let’s go there," Elaine led everyone toward the Spirit Ascension, who didn’t know what was about to befall them.</p>
<p>Not that they had a chance to begin with. The sword Alina drew was just Tier 10.</p>
<p>However, it held the power of the Eternal Grid, an Ancient power that only she could control. This made all her formations stronger and much more deadly.</p>
<p>Unity also managed to drive an arrow through the head of the Spirit King after the one she sent through his head. Her accuracy was impeccable.</p>
<p>She made sure her sisters distracted the bastard for just a second, but that was enough to give them the upper hand against their opponent, who died hating Unity.</p>
<p>As for the last Harem team, they were taking more time simply because their Spirit King was Level 9, just a step away from becoming a Spirit Emperor.</p>
<p>So they were having a hard time.</p>
<p>However, a team containing Ingrid, Neomi, Nara, and Val isn’t one to worry about. They are the selected few in the Dragon Harem who have a unique set of abilities.</p>
<p>Take Ingrid, for example. She is a Voidling, someone who has the Void as her primary source of energy. This means she is fast and deadly. She isn’t the average Sovereign.</p>
<p>Then there is Neomi, who is now fighting in her Shara form. Basically, she was reeking with death qi, and that alone makes her terrifying.</p>
<p>Then there is Nara, a half-Wraith, half-human. She is also a night rider who has the power to harness the flames of hell. Essentially, she is all about killing, which makes her a dangerous individual.</p>
<p>Then there is the Moon Goddess, who can become a literal god at any time and bring the last Hellhound to their knees.</p>
<p>However, ten minutes later, they ended their battle too and joined their sisters. Kent, who had seen these outcomes, knew he had to get on task and handle the five Spirit Kings.</p>
<p>He knew his wives were powerful, but never expected them to be this monstrous.</p>
<p>"Ladies, be a flower, and handle the rest of the Core formations. I need to go handle my business now." They nodded, and Kent moved.</p>
<p>"Alright, Aetheris, release one Spirit King." Aetheris nodded, and one chain on the Spirit King he held in the chained prison broke, freeing him.</p>
<p>The moment he was free, he moved and landed a punch on one of the chains binding his teammates. However, the next second, a powerful beam shot from the chair, drawing a hole through his chest.</p>
<p>He died.</p>
<p>"Dumbass. Anything you throw at the domain bounces back to you, ten times stronger," Aetheris said, his voice booming.</p>
<p>The nine Spirit Kings turned pale, glaring at Aetheris and Kent. Aetheris just smirked and released another one. This time, the released Spirit King knew better, so he didn’t attack the chains.</p>
<p>"Die, invader." Instead, he shifted his attention to Kent, who stood there looking at him. His fist came close to landing on Kent’s body.</p>
<p>However, that didn’t happen; instead, his body froze mid-attack, eyes wide as he slowly fell back and died.</p>
<p>"Damn." Kent, who killed him, was even taken aback.</p>
<p>Inside the chest of the Spirit King was a thick, five-meter dark shard. The Doom Shard he never truly paid attention to had proven to be much deadlier than he thought.</p>
<p>"Well then, Aetheris, release two this time." A smile appeared on Kent’s face as he waited for his next two targets.</p>
