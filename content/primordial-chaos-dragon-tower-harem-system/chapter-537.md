<p>"Zaila will be coming out today," Camilla said, sitting in the love garden with her sisters as they spent the afternoon together.</p>
<p>"Indeed. Too bad Kent won’t be here to welcome her. However, we are here, so we should make her feel welcomed," Vexthra answered, and the ladies nodded.</p>
<p>"Then how should we do that?" Mara asked.</p>
<p>"Very simple—we throw her a party here inside the harem tower. This way, she won’t freak out when she finds out this is a no-clothing space," Vexthra answered, and her sisters could only smile.</p>
<p>"Not that she is new to this. Kent already told me she and he have made progress in their relationship and even promised to rock her world when she comes out.</p>
<p>At this rate, she’ll be getting the good stuff before Lani and Nara." Vexthra didn’t want to tease the two friends, but to make the environment more lively, she did—and as expected, she made them blush.</p>
<p>"Then we should start early. I don’t want her party to be any less entertaining."</p>
<p>"Gaia, dear, why don’t you come here?" Vexthra called out, and the Cosmic loli appeared wearing a suit like a butler. The ladies always cracked up when they saw her in such clothing.</p>
<p>"You called me, my lady." Naturally, Gaia was the biggest and perhaps the most shameless of them all. She asked the question with a straight face and demeanour befitting a butler, making the ladies crack up.</p>
<p>"We want to eat into our man’s account," Vexthra said, and Gaia smiled.</p>
<p>"What belongs to Master belongs to you all. You can buy anything you want, and I’ll put it on his tab," Gaia said, knowing moments like this only come once. She would get her share anyway.</p>
<p>"Nice one, Gaia. I keep liking you every single minute." Vexthra smiled and then told her the plan. "We want a big cake made with both strawberry and vanilla. It should have the writing: ’Welcome Home Zaila, We Love You.’</p>
<p>Aside from that, we need champagne and some of the top-shelf stuff, if you know what I’m talking about." Gaia nodded.</p>
<p>"I got you, my lady."</p>
<p>"Thank you, Gaia." The cosmic loli left to prepare what was required of her. Meanwhile, the ladies freshened up, and in simple clothes, they went back to the body tower, where the Nightmare Chamber was located.</p>
<p>On the door was a countdown, showing that she would be coming out in 10 minutes.</p>
<p>Mara held Camilla’s hand as they waited for her disciple, whom she thought she had lost 20-plus years ago. Now, she is just waiting for her to return healthy, like she should be.</p>
<p>Zaila had never recovered from her trauma of being kidnapped and used as a blood bank for over twenty years by the late Sect Master of the Divine Palace sect.</p>
<p>If Kent hadn’t intervened when he did, she would have formed a heart demon long ago, which would have been hard on her.</p>
<p>As the minutes counted down, the ladies grew nervous. They knew the Nightmare Chamber was the cure, so if that didn’t work, then there was no other way but for Dori to wipe her memory—something that would deprive her of her time with Nara.</p>
<p>"She is coming out." The moment the timer reached zero, the door to the Nightmare Chamber opened, and Zaila stepped out.</p>
<p>However, the moment she did, a painful scream escaped her mouth as she dropped to the ground and held her head.</p>
<p>"Zaila!" Camilla was the first to reach her, holding her in her arms as tears flowed from her eyes. Her sisters, except Vexthra, surrounded her.</p>
<p>The goddess stood back, looking at something that made her brow raise. It was like she knew something was up, and truthfully, she was right...</p>
<p>"Everyone, step back." Vexthra waved her hand, and her sisters were pulled away from Zaila, flung three miles back, leaving Zaila alone on the ground.</p>
<p>"Big sister, what is go—" Selene wasn’t even done speaking when a powerful energy burst out of Zaila’s body, causing her scream to grow louder.</p>
<p>Vexthra vanished and reappeared beside her, placing a hand on her shoulder—not to ease her pain, but to ensure she didn’t pass out.</p>
<p>Dori appeared in the air above them.</p>
<p>"Goddess, don’t let her go. She is on the last step, so make sure she remains conscious," Dori said before she began drawing symbols in the air.</p>
<p>Far from them, Gaia and Akira also appeared, watching this with smiles on their faces.</p>
<p>"I guess the plan worked," Gaia said.</p>
<p>"Indeed. That sneaky Dori really knows how to stir up trouble. But this is good for her. Becoming a mind cultivator would make her someone her sisters can rely on when the time comes," Akira answered.</p>
<p>Dori wrote thousands of symbols and made them move into Zaila’s body. The moment they did, the air came to a complete stop. Then, despite being miles away, the ladies felt their senses pulled into a different realm.</p>
<p>The scene inside the tower changed, bringing a new scene of a river, very large, housing thousands of beautiful, colorful fish.</p>
<p>There were sea unicorns, seahorses, octopuses, whales, sharks—thousands of them. They came in different shapes and colors, making the river elegant and nurturing.</p>
<p>"Where are we?" Lilian asked, looking around.</p>
<p>"Welcome to my realm, sisters. I have been waiting for you." Suddenly, a voice they all recognized spoke, causing them to turn to their left.</p>
<p>When they did, they saw Zaila walking toward them. She was wearing only white pants and a bra, and her thin white nightwear flowed behind her like a cape.</p>
<p>She looked beautiful, more than she did on the outside. Her hair was hazy red, flowing down her back. Her body was also well-developed, growing in all the right places.</p>
<p>"Is it just me, or has Zaila become even more beautiful?" Camilla asked, looking at her disciple, who now appeared confident, radiant, and genuinely happy.</p>
<p>She looked nothing like her broken self before entering the Nightmare Chamber.</p>
<p>"That is because that is not Zaila—at least not the real her," Vexthra replied, and the ladies turned to her.</p>
<p>"If that is not her, then who is she?" Alina asked. To answer this, Vexthra looked at Zaila, and the two smiled.</p>
<p>"She is not the real Zaila, because this is a Primordial Spirit—a true Primordial Spirit."</p>
