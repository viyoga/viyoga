<p>[Pyris Pill Alliance - A Few Hours After The Duel]</p>
<p>"Say that again," Pill Monarch Nefeli said, looking at the man standing not far from him with a calm expression.</p>
<p>"I was in shock, same as you, Pill Monarch. However, the Kent kid won the duel by concocting a low-grade Grey Pill Ring Tier 9 pill. The shocking part is that he was even able to do Triple Extraction with three major ingredients.</p>
<p>Just where did you get such a monster from?" Pill Emperor Hakan asked with a small smile.</p>
<p>Among the few Pill Emperors in the Pyris Pill Alliance, he was the only one who could behave freely around Pill Monarch Nefeli.</p>
<p>They were in the same sect, and she once saved him, so he respected her greatly.</p>
<p>"And you said those three old monsters let him go after he gave the pill to his opponent?" Pill Monarch Nefeli asked.</p>
<p>"Yes. However, I believe they only let him go simply because he invoked the ancient pill rules. I believe they had ulterior motives, but hearing him say the rules caused them to take a step back.</p>
<p>However, now that his name has reached every pill world, it is only a matter of time before people start challenging him to duels."</p>
<p>"I am afraid he will have a hard time in the Pill Ascension realm. Everyone will want to challenge him this time to a combat duel, and knowing he is only at the Core Formation stage, with the nature of that realm, he will struggle," Pill Monarch Nefeli sighed.</p>
<p>However, Pill Emperor Hakan merely smiled. "For some reason, I believe he will swim there like a fish," he said, even laughing.</p>
<p>Pill Monarch Nefeli studied him for a moment before asking, "You sound certain. Is there something I am missing?"</p>
<p>"Well, the fact that Kent was able to notice my presence before I approached him and how he stood calm when the aura of a Spirit Lord slammed into his body, I believe, unless his opponent is at the Heavenly Ascension stage, he wouldn’t have any hardships at all.</p>
<p>If anything, we should pity those who would come after him.</p>
<p>In any case, he said I should tell you he is going to the Heaven’s Eye Sect on Planet Nictus, but he will be back before the Pill Ascension competition."</p>
<p>Pill Monarch Nefeli nodded, and Pill Emperor Hakan left.</p>
<p>"What kind of troublemaker have I entangled myself with?" She couldn’t help letting a small smile appear on her face. She stood up and walked toward the door; however, before walking out, a thought occurred to her, causing her to halt in her steps.</p>
<p>"Don’t tell me he had something to do with the death of several disciples from the Blue Flame Pill Palace.</p>
<p>After all, the one they were looking for is the sect master of the Heaven’s Eye Sect, and the fact that Kent is going there with a strange lady meets all the requirements."</p>
<p>She shook her head and decided not to focus on that. It is Kent’s problem, so he can handle it himself.</p>
<p>---</p>
<p>[Planet Aerithyne - Transfer Channel to Planet Nictus]</p>
<p>Kent held Lady Azela’s hand as they climbed into the large ship that would be taking them to Planet Nictus.</p>
<p>This time, instead of receiving VIP treatment, they were merely led to the 4th floor, where they had their own room, but not as luxurious as the one they had stayed in when they were coming to Planet Aerithyne.</p>
<p>Kent knew he was being followed, so he didn’t use the badge that Pill Monarch Nefeli had given him. He did not want to draw attention to her.</p>
<p>He merely used his new Pill Saint Badge to buy a ticket for himself and Lady Azela.</p>
<p>A few minutes later, they entered their room and settled down.</p>
<p>A couple of hours later, the channel opened, and the ship entered, starting its journey to Planet Nictus. It would take them 7 days to get there.</p>
<p>Kent looked at Lady Azela, who held a relieved expression on her face. She was finally on her way back to her world, where her mother was worried sick about her.</p>
<p>’What a big old softy.’ Kent had never met a grown-up who still relied on their mother as much as Lady Azela does.</p>
<p>’I can’t wait to see the reason why she is that dependent on her mother.’ Kent closed his eyes and pulled his mind back into his memories of Chaos.</p>
<p>This time, instead of reviewing the moment when Elina died, he chose to focus on the happy moments they shared.</p>
<p>As if knowing she would die, she made sure she and Chaos had all the best moments she could think of, and that alone was now helping Kent clear his chaotic mind.</p>
<p>He didn’t come back to the past to escape his trauma. He came to build his strength; after all, that was the only way he could gain access to some things he wanted.</p>
<p>Now, he had become a Pill Saint, and judging by the requirements to become a Pill Sage, Kent knew he would soon become a Pill Sage.</p>
<p>After spending three enjoyable days reviewing the best moments from his past life spent with Elina, Kent chose to proceed with the next set of records from the Primordial Pill Records.</p>
<p>It took him a day to review all.</p>
<p>’Looks like I have received a pill formula for Soul Limit Breaker Pill. However, to do that, I will need the Soul Strengthening Pill, for which I now have the complete formula.</p>
<p>However, the main ingredients are something I can’t easily get even with the tower’s help.’ Kent sighed.</p>
<p>’I saw the Void Soul Flower at the store where I bought the Cauldron and the Flame Spirit. However, just one is 300 million spirit stones</p>
<p>I don’t need this pill because I have Limit Breaking Points, but my wives do, so I must man up and buy as many Void Soul Flowers as I can get my hands on before going back.</p>
<p>If I can obtain all four main ingredients for the Soul Strengthening Pill and the Soul Limit Breaker pill before I return to the future, that would be nice.</p>
<p>I mean, who knows how expensive this ingredient would become 370,000 years from now?’</p>
<p>After checking everything, Kent focused on the next requirement to become a Pill Sage.</p>
<p>⟪Requirements to Become a Pill Sage⟫</p>
<p>↠ Rescue the one named Yuna and deliver her to Pill Monarch Nefeli</p>
<p>↠ Concoct a Tier 9 with a Medium-grade Grey Pill Ring.</p>
<p>’Looks like they don’t want to make things difficult for me this time around.’ Kent smiled. ’In that case, I will be sure to become a Pill Sage soon so I can aim for the Pill King stage.’</p>
<p>The days went by quickly.</p>
<p>Seven days later, Kent and Lady Azela arrived on Planet Nictus. On the outside, it was a green planet with a thin ring around it.</p>
<p>However, on the surface, every city was built using jade. It was as if the entire world were one giant jade that people carved into large cities.</p>
<p>Kent used the Soul Map. He discovered that this planet was significantly smaller than Planet Aerithyne. He was able to see the entirety of the city they arrived in, as well as the next city.</p>
<p>"This is Jin City. We have to take the teleportation channel to Yin City. That is where my sect is located," Lady Azela led Kent to the nearby teleportation channel, and soon, they arrived in Yin City.</p>
<p>It took them a couple of minutes to appear at the front entrance of the sect. However, before they could enter, a caravan blocked their path.</p>
