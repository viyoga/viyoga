<p>After spending time telling his wives all about the amazing people he met when he traveled to the past, Kent decided to spend some more weeks having sex with them, since the first few weeks after he ascended would be him entering seclusion to raise his strength and handle other issues.</p>
<p>Kent had a lot to do, but thanks to the tower, he was able to accomplish most of them while in his bed, surrounded by his wives.</p>
<p>In the end, however, he had to handle everything else that the tower couldn’t.</p>
<p>"You seven will go to Elarion; it is a kingdom of half-elves. You will stay there and use the treasures I have given you to grow stronger.</p>
<p>Afterward, the King and Queenie will open the Ascension Tower for you to ascend to a Supreme High World called Nictus.</p>
<p>Once you get there, visit the palace and tell whoever is there that Lord Chaos sent you. They will allow you to stay there as an honored guest.</p>
<p>After you break through to the Spirit Ascension stage, visit an auction house called Brown Maple. When you arrive, be sure to show them this badge; they will follow your command.</p>
<p>That will be your chance to get what you wanted. Personally, I would recommend finding a suitable sect to join. Knowing the kind of auction house they are, they will surely have some connections with power sects."</p>
<p>The seven disciples from the Celestial Gate Sect nodded in agreement.</p>
<p>Kent waited for them as promised, even though he could have ascended and come back later.</p>
<p>However, planning to end all things in the medium world before leaving, he waited for them, and now that they are here, he made arrangements for them.</p>
<p>"Thank you, Kent," Antlia, the captain of the team, said, bowing slightly.</p>
<p>"No need for that. You have long reached the stage to ascend, so take this as the final push to send you into glory," Kent said with a small smile before turning to Elder Lance.</p>
<p>"I was told you, too, will be ascending soon?" Kent asked, and Elder Lance nodded.</p>
<p>"Since you have provided ways to ascend to dozens of worlds, I think it is time to ascend. After all, I have already become much stronger now... all thanks to you."</p>
<p>Kent nodded, knowing it was indeed the fact. All the elders in the sect have become so much stronger now. In fact, they are all ready for the Supreme High Worlds.</p>
<p>It was all thanks to Kent’s generosity.</p>
<p>"Just don’t forget to take your care package from King Thalirion and Queen Jascia," Kent said in a calm tone. He had already made plans for all of them and would continue providing for them even after he was gone.</p>
<p>After all, they are all working toward making him a powerhouse one of these days. Their influence will also spread his power, so things will become much more vast in the future.</p>
<p>"Thank you, Kent."</p>
<p>The handsome dragon nodded.</p>
<p>He had already informed everyone that he was leaving—Nyss, Princess Elowen (Half-Elf Princess), and Princess Freya (Dwarven Princess), the seeded candidates, and Kent’s friends he had made in Elarion are all coming with him.</p>
<p>He planned to personally send them to a sect that would benefit them. He wanted them to grow independently, so separating them was his goal.</p>
<p>The sect had gathered to bid him farewell and good luck.</p>
<p>"Just take it easy, guys. In 12 years, win the Interplanetary Sect Competition again and then ascend to the next level. Trust me, there is so much fun in the high worlds."</p>
<p>The disciples nodded. To them, Kent is their idol, so his words are law. They will strive to work hard and chase after him.</p>
<p>"Also, follow all arrangements the elders will make for you when ascending. This way, you will have it much easier."</p>
<p>They nodded again. Kent then looked toward King Thalirion and Queen Jascia and smiled. "Don’t worry, I will take care of your daughter. She will be happy and well, so when you ascend, try to hold on till we meet."</p>
<p>They smiled and nodded to his words.</p>
<p>Suddenly, the sky began to darken. Kent waved at everyone and teleported away, appearing 12 miles away.</p>
<p>The tribulation was here, and he looked more relaxed than anything. This sight baffled everyone; however, Kent didn’t seem to care.</p>
<p>Soon, however, a bolt of lightning formed in the clouds, causing many to step back. They were far away, but they felt the danger.</p>
<p>RUMBLE</p>
<p>The bolt fell, and Kent, who was at the receiving end, raised his hand, and the bolt landed on his palm, sending a tickling sensation through his body.</p>
<p>This sight caused many to take a deep breath.</p>
<p>However, a few seconds later, the second bolt fell, this one much stronger and thicker than the first one. However, instead of raising another defense, he merely raised his hand and defended against the bolt.</p>
<p>"Damn."</p>
<p>The sight caused many to take a deep breath once again.</p>
<p>Finally, the last phase of the tribulation came. This time, the lightning was much deeper and darker. However, Kent merely raised his hand, and the lightning landed on his palm.</p>
<p>The moment he defended against the third lightning bolt, a portal opened in the air, and then a powerful sucking force locked on Kent.</p>
<p>Kent waved at everyone, and they also waved back.</p>
<p>Soon, Kent’s body was lifted off the ground and entered through the portal...</p>
<p>---</p>
<p>[Planet Mekyn]</p>
<p>On a grim battlefield where thousands of Elves, Incubi, Ice Demons, Dark Elves, and Flame Humans were locked in a deadly battle, one can sense the aura of death emanating everywhere.</p>
<p>From a quick observation, one could see the Incubi, Dark Elves, Ice Demons, and Flame Humans ganging up on the Elves.</p>
<p>However, despite the many of them ganging up on the smallest race on Planet Mekyn, they weren’t having it easy at all.</p>
<p>Still, no matter how strong they were, they were locked in with warriors from four powerful races. This meant they were not going to have it easy—and indeed, they were not having it easy at all.</p>
<p>Hundreds of Elves were dead, and more kept dying by the second.</p>
<p>"Runa, take Adina to the teleportation gate and prepare to send her away. Be sure to destroy it when she is gone," Lady Enyo, the Elven Planet Protector, said in a grim tone.</p>
<p>She had made arrangements to send Adina away to a random place and hoped she would end up in a good place. That was their last resort, considering the people were after her.</p>
<p>"Please, don’t send me away," Adina said, playing her zither, sending thousands of haunting sounds, runes, and wraiths at the attackers.</p>
<p>"You have to leave, Adina. We will buy you enough time," High Priestess Runa said, cutting through the torso of a Dark Elf, only to be pushed back by an Ice Demon.</p>
<p>Lady Enyo saw a dagger arrive before the High Priestess’s neck, and so she teleported before her and defended, only for an arrow to land on her shoulder, sending her flying hundreds of meters away.</p>
<p>High Priestess Runa and Lyna, the dragon lady who was making things hard for everyone, tried to move to her rescue. However, they were blocked, which left her exposed and at risk.</p>
<p>However, she was an experienced warrior, so she managed to regain her composure. The next second, though, three Heavenly Ascension Stage warriors appeared one meter from her—two stabbing their swords at her while the third locked an arrow aimed at her chest.</p>
<p>However, just when the arrow and the swords could land on her body, a beam of light filled with lightning descended and swallowed both Lady Enyo and the three Heavenly Ascension Stage warriors.</p>
<p>A second later, all three of them were thrown out, their bodies littered with burn marks.</p>
<p>Soon, the light beam dispersed, revealing a new person..</p>
<p>Lady Enyo, who seemed to have been poisoned, looked at the face of the new arrival, and her eyes widened.</p>
<p>"KENT!"</p>
<p>"MOM!"</p>
