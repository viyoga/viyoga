<p>"This one looks almost the same as Vaelros," Kent said, staring at the image of the fourth undead, who also had tattoos on his body and two horns on his forehead.</p>
<p>⟪He indeed looks like Vaelros, but they are two completely different beings. This one has traits of dragons and serpents, so he will possess some draconic and serpentine characteristics.⟫ Akira explained.</p>
<p>"So he is another brute," Kent said with a smile.</p>
<p>⟪No, Master. Initially, I wanted him to be a brute, but we already have two, so this one will be a Warlock—an Eldritch Warlock, one of the ancient warlocks who dominated the universe.</p>
<p>However, this one will be unique simply because you have a Mind Weaver on your team.⟫, Akira said, causing Dori to puff her small chest out.</p>
<p>"Tell me something I should look forward to," Kent said.</p>
<p>He intended to create a Witch Doctor using the Poison Sac he obtained during the auction, so hearing that the next undead would be a Warlock, he wanted to know more.</p>
<p>⟪This undead will have a thing for curses, spells, and runes. Basically, he wouldn’t use any weapons, but fighting him will be very frustrating.</p>
<p>His spells will be random, his curses will be lethal, and his runes will be binding. I can already see him bringing an entire battlefield to its knees, brainwashing them to do his bidding.</p>
<p>And before you frown, I know you hate slavery, so I gave him a personality that will make him despise slavery, meaning he wouldn’t enslave even his foes.</p>
<p>He will just brainwash them and turn them into his puppets—if that makes you comfortable.⟫</p>
<p>Kent just chuckled.</p>
<p>Though he hated slavery, he wasn’t foolish enough to think that people shouldn’t be put on a leash. If killing them was not an option, then brainwashing would do.</p>
<p>"Then what resources are we using this time?" He was bathing in valuable resources now, so he didn’t care how much he spent.</p>
<p>⟪One trillion spirit stones, a large chunk of the Forest King—and this is because soon, you can kill your own Forest King, so don’t be stingy. Aside from that, the Poison Sac will be used here.</p>
<p>Of course, I have extracted the vials you will be giving to the Pill Saint. A part of the Domain Crystal will also be used.</p>
<p>Then, there will also be 24 Root Great Sages, 12 Root Transcendents, 4 Root Ascensions, and one Root Sovereign.⟫</p>
<p>"Then I will be getting a Sovereign this time." Kent smiled, already seeing it happening. Although creating undead costs a lot, he knew this many resources would at least give him a Sovereign.</p>
<p>⟪Indeed. That means you will get your first True Undead.⟫ Dori answered.</p>
<p>"My first True Undead? What about Kaizo and the rest?" He raised an eyebrow.</p>
<p>⟪Don’t get me wrong, Master. Kaizo and the others are undead, but they have yet to awaken their bloodlines, which means they haven’t awakened their True Names.</p>
<p>Undead go through a different evolutionary path. This means that the moment they awaken their bloodlines and True Names, they will truly start walking the path of the undead.</p>
<p>You may not be aware of this, but undead are beings that can grow endlessly since killing them is almost impossible.</p>
<p>However, because of the bloodline and True Name restrictions placed on them, they can’t reach the Immortal stage. They cannot surpass the Life stage, for they are dead.</p>
<p>Dead beings can’t comprehend the Law of Life before becoming immortals.</p>
<p>The highest they can attain is the Empyrean stage.⟫</p>
<p>"But with them having a True Name, that can be solved, right?"</p>
<p>⟪Indeed. This is why you are the perfect master of the undead. With the Tower, all your undead will have their own True Names and bloodlines.⟫</p>
<p>"Aren’t I fortunate?" Kent smiled. He pressed the button, and soon, all the items were added, commencing the creation of his fourth undead.</p>
<p>"Damn, an entire week, huh." Kent shook his head.</p>
<p>"So, the moment Kaizo and the rest reach the Sovereign stage, they will also awaken their True Names, right?" Kent asked.</p>
<p>⟪Yes, Master.⟫</p>
<p>"Great. Why don’t we go celebrate?"</p>
<p>The ladies jumped into the air, cheering in excitement. He opened a portal and stepped out. The three Lolis followed him out</p>

<p>Turning, they saw Kaizo getting up, blood seeping from the corner of his mouth.</p>
<p>"Again!" he shouted and charged at Vaelros, who was bathed in lightning and blood energy, wielding two colossal chains.</p>
<p>"He never learns." Kent shook his head, knowing the brute would never be able to beat his fellow brute, yet he still refused to back down.</p>
<p>⟪He grows stronger from physical beatings too, so it’s more like he’s cultivating.⟫</p>
<p>Kent just shook his head and teleported away. They appeared inside the Mountain Garden.</p>
<p>However, before the ladies could get comfortable, Kent grabbed their small legs and lifted them into the air. A second later, they were hanging upside down by a rope.</p>
<p>Kent smiled, produced three markers, and walked toward them.</p>
<p>⟪Master, what are you doing?⟫ Dori asked in a panicked tone.</p>
<p>"Doing what you did to me, obviously."</p>
<p>When he was inside the enlightenment, they painted his face, which caused his wives to make fun of him. So he is paying them back.</p>
<p>⟪It was Gaia’s idea!⟫ Akira immediately pointed fingers.</p>
<p>⟪You bitch! Wasn’t it our collective idea to decorate Master’s face?!⟫ Gaia glared at her sister.</p>
<p>⟪You are the oldest among the three of us, so you should take responsibility, Sister.⟫ Dori said, making Gaia clench her fists.</p>
<p>⟪We are the same age, you two traitorous bitches!⟫</p>
<p>Gaia would probably find a way to get back at them, and Kent, seeing that, knew exactly what to do.</p>
<p>"They are right, Gaia. You should take responsibility."</p>
<p>The moment Akira and Dori heard that and saw the upside-down smile on their master’s face, they knew they were doomed.</p>
<p>Kent laughed like a maniac as he decorated Gaia’s face. The moment he was done, he ran away, afraid the Cosmic Weaver would trap him somewhere in the cosmos.</p>
<p>Up at the core of the universe, the beautiful lady laughed, clutching her stomach as she watched Kent decorate Gaia’s face.</p>
<p>Her eyes gleamed as she spoke, "I told you he would punish you."</p>
<p>⟪I want revenge. Please, Mother, allow me to lock him away somewhere for a month.⟫</p>
<p>"No. You know he is too precious to me, so I can’t let you do that. If he finds out I gave you the go-ahead, he will punish me too." The woman smiled.</p>
<p>⟪Then allow me to punish these two brats.⟫</p>
<p>She thought for a while and chuckled. "Just don’t make them cry, okay? They are your sisters."</p>
<p>Thus, Queen Gaia was born, and her two servants could only fulfill their duties with painted faces for an entire year.</p>
