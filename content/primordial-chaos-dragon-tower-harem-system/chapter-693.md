<p>"Unity, what is the name of the arrow you just used?" Kaizo asked, looking at the young lady who stole his kill.</p>
<p>Kaizo knew it was his time to shine, yet it ended with Unity sending her arrow through the eye of the Peak Spirit Ascension spider.</p>
<p>Now, all he could ask for was the name of the arrow that managed to reach the Floor Boss without it even noticing.</p>
<p>Unity smiled and opened her palm, causing an arrow to appear. It looked like it had been formed from a rainbow, considering it encompassed every color in existence.</p>
<p>"It is called Blossom Feisty Unity Arrow," Unity said with an embarrassed expression. The sisters just chuckled. It wasn’t hard to tell that the naming wasn’t her idea, but rather that of her Primordial Spirits—most specifically, Feisty’s.</p>
<p>After all, Blossom, her second primordial spirit, created it...</p>
<p>Alina took the arrow and studied it for a few seconds before nodding. "This is a work of art. It doesn’t contain any runes, but it is already enough to handle something like the spider."</p>
<p>She looked at Unity, a smile forming on her lips. "How many of these can you produce?"</p>
<p>"As many as you want, provided I have enough energy." Alina nodded cheerfully.</p>
<p>"I have an Augmentation Array that can turn these arrows into Spirit Lord-worthy killing arrows. If you want, I can start with the first 100."</p>
<p>Unity nodded and created 100 Blossom Feisty Unity Arrows. Since Alina needed something to be busy with, she might as well earn something from it.</p>
<p>Alina smiled and directly sent everything to her grid space. Kaizo knew this wouldn’t be the last time Unity stole his last kill, so he started planning his revenge.</p>
<p>The rivalry between Unity and Kaizo had just begun.</p>
<p>Kent suspected something like that, but he knew it would be a harmless rivalry, so he said nothing. Instead, he turned to the system to examine the rewards and their usage.</p>
<p>After looking around for a while, Kent took out a vial containing an elixir, unclung it, and gulped it down. The moment he did, he felt his bones growing stronger.</p>
<p>"Good stuff." A smile appeared on his face. He took out another and showed it to his legion and wives.</p>
<p>"This is the Eternal Steel Bone Tempering Elixir... drinking it will have some effect on your bones. Take it as a bonus for clearing the first floor." Kent unclung the elixir and poured it into Vexthra’s mouth.</p>
<p>Though it would probably have little to no effect on her, the rewards inside his tower storage included her, so she might as well drink it.</p>
<p>"You can check your space rings for yours." They all checked and took them out, drinking everything immediately. When they did, the feeling of strength coursed through their bodies, making them smile.</p>
<p>Afterwards, Kent gave the Serpent Breath Poison recipe to Princess Lovina, who was more than happy to receive something aside from the elixir.</p>
<p>The next thing Kent brought out was the Map of the Planet Killer Dungeon. It is a device that, the moment he channeled his energy into it, displayed a detailed map of the four floating islands.</p>
<p>"This map will guide you in exploring the island. It features detailed landmarks that make it easy to find something interesting inside the dungeon. I don’t have much time to waste on it, but since we have 12 years to clear this dungeon, you guys can spend some days exploring it.</p>
<p>The map of the second floor will be added after you clear it. So, for now, you can explore this floor before heading to the second floor."</p>
<p>They nodded. Twelve years may sound like a long time, but it will be over soon, so they knew they had to get things going fast before they ran out of time.</p>
<p>"One last thing... Neomi, Selene, Kazarak, and Zharkaz, look into your space rings." The four did as they were told. Neomi pulled out a sleek red key, and the rest pulled out small golden orbs.</p>
<p>"Selene, Kazarak, and Zharkaz, crush your orbs." These are the drops received after clearing the first floor. The tower automatically sent everything into the tower space, and then, using a few tower points, Gaia directed the orbs to their intended recipients.</p>
<p>Selene, Kazarak, and Zharkaz did as they were told, and each received a golden key.</p>
<p>"What you have in your hands are keys that will unlock your memories." Kent looked at Kazarak and Zharkaz. "You two will receive fragments of your memories. It may be a sad or a happy memory, so prepare before using it."</p>
<p>Kent then turned to Selene and Neomi. "You two must be wondering what type of memories you will be unlocking after all your memories are intact.</p>
<p>The answer is simple: every last one of you, regardless of your background, was once someone great. Most of you died defending your family, some died defending their worlds, and some died defending the universe.</p>
<p>However, while you were meant to be dead and remain dead, some idiot did something brilliant years ago, and due to that, you now have a second chance at life.</p>
<p>I don’t know what these memories will do, but whatever you see, treat them as yours, for they are indeed yours—memories of the life you lived billions of years ago."</p>
<p>By the time Kent was done, everyone around him remained quiet... the sheer shock they felt was enough to show they were indeed gobsmacked.</p>
<p>"No need to look shocked... You are bound to remember one of these days, so getting it this early isn’t too bad."</p>
<p>Kent made sure everyone calmed down before he continued with the rest of what he had to say. He would be leaving soon, so he wanted to make sure everyone had what they needed.</p>
<p>The four with the memory keys also decided to hold on to them for now until they all received a key. This way, they could all use them at the same time.</p>
<p>Kent was okay with that, so he let them have their way. After he was done with everything, they decided to start exploring the four floating islands.</p>
<p>"You have to leave, Kent. We have everything under control, so don’t worry about us..." Vexthra said, resting in Kent’s arms.</p>
<p>"Though we will miss you, the mission is also important, so go now while you still have time."</p>
<p>Kent nodded. After kissing all his ladies, he left the dungeon and went back to the tower.</p>
