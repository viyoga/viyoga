<p>"I say we should just eat those fox girls we picked up three days ago," a tiger beast said to another tiger standing atop a wall, acting as the guards of the bandit hideout.</p>
<p>"The leader said they will be sold to the merchants tomorrow, that the Orc King wills it," the second tiger beast said. They are both in their humanoid forms, so they can wield weapons.</p>
<p>They are wielding two large Sabers.</p>
<p>"If it’s an order from the Orc King, then there is no debating that. After all, we work for him, and I heard he is planning on invading another world soon. He had figured out a way to teleport his forces there."</p>
<p>"We will get to have a good battle then. It’s been a long time since I had a good battle with anyone."</p>
<p>The two tigers kept talking while maintaining awareness of their surroundings.</p>
<p>Meanwhile, two kilometres away from them, Kent and his legion stopped and looked at the tall wall guarded by Root Transcendent guards.</p>
<p>"I can see eight of these tigers standing on all four sides of the wall," Sylara said, her red eyes shining.</p>
<p>"That is an easy fix then," Kent said, looking at Nyxira. "Can you handle them?"</p>
<p>"I can," the Soul Sentinel answered without hesitation.</p>
<p>"Then we will wait for your signal." Nyxira nodded and moved with the shadows. Soon, she was nearing the wall, where the two tigers remained oblivious.</p>
<p>"I say we wait this one out, and if nothing comes within the hour, we can just take a nap," the tiger beast said and was about to add something when a dagger appeared at the throat of his friend.</p>
<p>Before he could shout, his friend was dead, and his throat was also slit.</p>
<p>Nyxira materialized from his shadow and glided toward the other side of the wall, keeping her body wrapped in shadow so nobody could sense her presence.</p>
<p>But even if anybody could sense her, it would be the three orcs who were now drinking in a certain room, unaware of the danger approaching them.</p>
<p>It took less than a minute, but all eight guards were killed, and Nyxira gave the signal.</p>
<p>"You know the plan. The rest can be killed on sight, but the three orcs are to be subdued first. We will kill them after we are done with the mission."</p>
<p>They nodded.</p>
<p>Uncle Drew retrieved his mask and put it on giving him a terrifying presence. Strangely, his strength skyrocketed the moment he wore the mask.</p>
<p>He then snapped his fingers, and eight figures, all clad in dark clothes with hoods and masks on their faces, appeared.</p>
<p>Two are Root Ascension, and six are Root Transcendent.</p>
<p>"See you all soon." Both Uncle Drew and his eight hell demons vanished, and the next second, an explosion rocked the fortress of the Crimson Vipers.</p>
<p>"Time to smash things, y’all!" Kaizo was the second to leave, and just as he arrived, a level 3 Sovereign orc appeared with a frown on his ugly face.</p>
<p>Kaizo wasted no time and punched at him, sending him flying. Kent shook his head, watching his brute land that punch before, he also blinked away.</p>
<p>By the time he arrived, Sylara had also isolated another orc, and Veydris was facing the third.</p>
<p>Saden and the sentinels, along with the four battle dolls, also went into action, and just like that, the fortress of the Crimson Vipers was under siege.</p>
<p>"Tempest Fist!" Kaizo roared, and like the brute he was, his fist crackled with lightning as he descended on the orc, who had taken up a rather large spear, his body exuding a potent yellow energy.</p>
<p>"Kaizo, don’t let him stab you with that spear; it is poisonous," Kent cautioned his brute undead, who nodded and distanced himself just fast enough for Kent to unleash his Rapid Sword Slash.</p>
<p>While the attack was effective in pushing the orc back, it did little to no damage. But that was to be expected, considering they were fighting a sovereign.</p>
<p>Of course, Kent wasn’t disappointed; he just never expected his attack to have such little effect.</p>
<p>However, the moment that attack pushed the orc back, Kaizo got an opening, and he didn’t miss.</p>
<p>"Piercing Fist!" Lightning coated his arm and solidified into pointed gloves.</p>
<p>"Eat that, sucker!" His fist went through, and like being pierced by a spear, he dug a hole into the chest of the orc, who screamed in pain after landing outside the fortress, clearing a wide area of the pink forest.</p>
<p>Kent raised his sword and channelled his energy into it. Then, taking a powerful swing, a wide arc of energy shot out.</p>
<p>The orc, seeing the attack, swung his spear to meet it, but before he could completely defend himself, he noticed that Kent had vanished from the air.</p>
<p>The next second, a painful cut landed on his exposed knee, drawing yet another cry of pain.</p>
<p>"Just who are you people? Don’t you know we are under the authority of Lord Balma? You can’t kill us!"</p>
<p>The orc, who wasn’t even allowed to attack by Kent and Kaizo, spoke, only to feel Kaizo’s fist slam into his face again.</p>
<p>"Don’t worry, bro, we don’t really care about your boss. So keep quiet and suffer," Kaizo said with a smile. Even Kent couldn’t hold back his laughter.</p>
<p>This caused the orc to roar as his body burst into energy, now radiating a deeper shade of yellow, showing he had activated an enhancement skill.</p>
<p>The two became alert; their opponent was now much more dangerous.</p>
<p>Meanwhile, Sylara, who was facing the second orc, seemed to be having a field day with the pitiful bastard.</p>
<p>Her claws were radiating potent red abyssal energy as she cut and stabbed the pitiful orc who was unfortunate to have faced such a monster.</p>
<p>On the body of the orc, one could see deep cuts, showing she wasn’t holding back her attacks. Of course, she wasn’t. The being before her was a Sovereign who had reached level 3.</p>
<p>Playing around would just be stupid.</p>
<p>Nearby, Veydris had already severed one arm of the orc. Blood poured from the orc’s body as he staggered, weaponless.</p>
<p>The battle hadn’t even begun in full circle, yet Veydris, Abyss Knight had already ended his battle by severing the weapon-wielding arm of the orc.</p>
<p>Veydris was done with his mission. The arm that once held the weapon was gone.</p>
<p>To prove it, Veydris swung his void blade unleashing a wide sword arc.</p>
<p>The sword’s arc vanished the moment it left the sword.</p>
<p>Before the orc could sense it, a powerful slash struck his chest, sending him flying. He crashed to the ground, unconscious.</p>
