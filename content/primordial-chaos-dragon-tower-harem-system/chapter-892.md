<p>If there is one thing every dedicated Body cultivator chases after and, to some degree, lusts after, then it is Nirvana Body.</p>
<p>A body forged from the flames of Nirvana is one that no Body cultivator would reject. Legends even say that bodies forged from the flames of Nirvana can live for hundreds of thousands of years, even if the spirit is dead.</p>
<p>Naturally, this isn’t much if one puts the natural lifespan from cultivation into consideration.</p>
<p>Hundreds of thousands of years, to some cultivators, isn’t much, for there are immortals who have been alive for a million years.</p>
<p>However, this unique ability of the Nirvana Body is one that only true cultivators understand its value.</p>
<p>Those who truly chase after the Daos (Laws) know that the laws are more visible in the underworld. Naturally, to get to the underworld, one must die.</p>
<p>The underworld is the realm of the dead.</p>
<p>However, just like there are loopholes in computer programs, getting to the underworld also has loopholes, and one of them is using soul bodies.</p>
<p>But there is a downside to this method.</p>
<p>Once there, your body outside the underworld will be vulnerable, and if not returned within a couple of thousand years, it might become worthless. The moment that happens, the spirit will be forever trapped in the underworld.</p>
<p>The body in the world of the living is the only tether the soul has, so the moment it decays, that tether will be severed and the soul will be trapped in the underworld for good.</p>
<p>However, there is a fix, and that is where the Nirvana Body comes in.</p>
<p>This body can remain protected for many years, allowing one to chase and comprehend the Dao in the underworld without fear of their body giving up on them.</p>
<p>Many go on to become gods without attaining the Nirvana Body. However, a true cultivator knows that to get the best out of cultivation, one must attain Nirvana.</p>
<p>Kent’s Mortal Body was burnt away, and his Nirvana Body was slowly forming.</p>
<p>Once complete, not only would he become several times stronger, but he would also be recognized by the Flames of Nirvana.</p>
<p>This means if he wishes to take the Body Refining Path before focusing on attaining Immortal body, burning his body twelve times to attain the Everlasting Nirvana Body, is now within his grasp.</p>
<p>Though the success rate of that path is very low.</p>
<p>---</p>
<p>[Everlasting Nirvana Council – Somewhere in the Dip (A Ruin from the Primordial Era)]</p>
<p>During the Primordial Era, the Dip used to be one of the nine places in the mortal realm where the flames of Nirvana could be found.</p>
<p>Back then, many used those flames to temper their bodies, extending their lifespans. It was one of the many places that always had cultivators, no matter the time of day.</p>
<p>When the Primordial was destroyed, the eight other places with the flames of Nirvana were lost. Only the Dip was found many millions of years ago.</p>
<p>When it was discovered that the flame it was housing was a Nirvana Flame, the predators of the Body Cultivation Path and a few other tyrants formed a council and chose to monopolize it, preventing many from using it.</p>
<p>Even the Supreme Celestial is part of this council, and for billions of years now, this group of cultivators has benefited from this Nirvana flame.</p>
<p>However, at the moment, the fourteen original members of the council were alarmed to see the Nirvana Pool drying up.</p>
<p>It was as if their treasure was dying before their eyes, and despite their strength and authority, none was able to do anything to stop it.</p>
<p>"How is this even possible?" a Celestial with red eyes and four black horns asked, looking at the pool that was quickly drying up.</p>
<p>The shock was too great that, aside from the Celestial who spoke, the rest merely stood there, staring at the place where the blue-colored flame of Nirvana used to burn.</p>
<p>After who knows how long, all fourteen members of the Nirvana Council unleashed their cultivations, each with a pained and angry look on their faces.</p>
<p>With their cultivations, they knew the pool hadn’t dried up naturally. They knew it was done by someone, and that pissed them off.</p>
<p>They wanted to get their hands on the person who dared steal what belonged to them.</p>
<p>If only they knew the owner of the flames of Nirvana had merely taken back what he gave to the universe many years ago.</p>
<p>In eight different places, eight different colored flames dried up just like the one the Nirvana Council had been using to cultivate for many years.</p>
<p>---</p>
<p>Back inside the tower, the golden-red triangular-shaped crystal shuddered, and out of nowhere, nine different colored flames started entering it.</p>
<p>If the Nirvana Council were around now and watching this display, they would be vomiting blood.</p>
<p>The idea that something they had depended on for many years was being absorbed by a mere Core Formation Stage kid— they would even destroy the entire world he was in and go ahead to kill everybody who ever saw Kent as compensation for their loss.</p>
<p>However, as it stands, all nine parts of the Nirvana Flames in the universe were being devoured by Kent’s Source Heart.</p>
<p>After what felt like forever, the last strand of Nirvana Flame entered Kent’s Source Heart.</p>
<p>After that, a golden transparent light formed into a cocoon around the Source Heart and soon solidified into a golden cocoon pulsing with energy.</p>
<p>It took forty-five good days, but on this day, the cocoon cracked and a handsome young man was revealed. Kent’s body appeared, surrounded by a wave of primal aura.</p>
<p>He maintained the same appearance. However, his features had sharpened, making him appear both handsome and, well, dangerous.</p>
<p>The aura around him was several times stronger than before he attained Nirvana.</p>
<p>This meant that, aside from his Primordial Chaos Aura, which made him a monster unlike any other, he had now become several times more monstrous with a Nirvana Body.</p>
<p>When he opened his eyes, nine different colors flashed across his pupils. Clothes covered his body, and Kent stood up.</p>
<p>He flexed his body for a few seconds, then a smile appeared on his face. "Looks like I should go to the Valley of Torture again."</p>
<p>He could feel his body was now many times stronger than before.</p>
<p>"Tower, what has changed about me?" Kent asked.</p>
<p>⟦Aside from the master becoming even more handsome, his strength is now three times what it was before he attained the Nirvana Body.</p>
<p>Aside from that, the master has also attained Super Flame Resistance and Super Flame Comprehension.</p>
<p>Also, the Master’s Chaos Soul Flame has reached Tier 9 and now possesses some powers of the nine elemental Nirvana Flames.</p>
<p>The master has also formed 1,000 Flame Veins.</p>
<p>The last thing is the Nirvana Flame Armor that has already started merging with the master’s body. It is estimated to be complete within 200 days (Outside Time).⟧</p>
<p>Kent laughed happily and looked at the 8,002 steps still left for him to climb. After a few moments, he smiled and flew away from the stairway.</p>
<p>"I have many things to handle, so I will come back later. Next time, I will climb to the top of the stairway to attain an Immortal Body. That is now the target I am looking forward to."</p>
<p>⟦The master must know that attaining an Immortal Body is only possible if the master can handle Immortal Aura, so perhaps the master should chase after the twelve stages of Nirvana and attain the Everlasting Nirvana Body.⟧</p>
<p>"Then I will do that. But first, I must learn the Primal Body Tempering Art."</p>
<p>Kent appeared inside the sword tower and immediately went into a lotus posture. Soon, his consciousness was pulled back into the Realm of Blood and Chaos, appearing before the colossal gate.</p>
