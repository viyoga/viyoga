<p>Uncle Drew quickly recaps to Kent on what transpired when he went to hell.</p>
<p>He started with his duel in which he soloed five true immortals. Of course, he led with that and even emphasised the one vs five part. After all, it is not every day a Death Stage Cultivator gets to fight and defeat five True Immortals.</p>
<p>Then he focused next on when he discovered that the Overlord of hell is also Night Rider. This discovery took Kent aback. He had met Laziel before when he was challenging the Hell Road.</p>
<p>Of course, back then, he felt nothing for him, unlike when he saw Lene and Vargus just now.</p>
<p>However, while that was the case, he most certainly didn’t expect a god to be one of his legion of Night Riders. This thing made him wonder just how many of his legions from past lives are now gods and even Supremes or Celestials.</p>
<p>’I mean, the Eternal Wrath Legion, all of them are immortals. So there is no telling just how many of them would be gods by now.’</p>
<p>With that in mind, Kent started to look forward to when he would meet the various legions he commanded in the past.</p>
<p>Uncle Drew added the rest of the details about his trip to hell. In a couple of minutes, he was done.</p>
<p>"Sounds like it was one hell of a trip. I mean, meeting your teammates sure felt nice, right?"</p>
<p>Uncle Drew smiled and nodded... "It was the best feeling. However, since I couldn’t bring all of them, I had to take only Lene and Vargus with me. They will help rescue your friend and handle other things you need help with."</p>
<p>Sounds like a plan to Kent. Kaelith, from what Uncle Drew said, had suffered enough. It was time to rescue him and punish those involved.</p>
<p>Just thinking about it made his blood boil.</p>
<p>So he let his emotions settle before focusing on the crucial points of Uncle Drew’s report.</p>
<p>"The Dark City, you said it still exists, huh?"</p>
<p>"Yes. Although it was destroyed after the Dark War, its ruins remain. However, aside from Night Riders, nobody else could enter. It takes one to hold absolute control over darkness to enter.</p>
<p>Laziel is the only one who can enter with relative ease and bring back the 12 Night Riders staying there. They thought the veil was thinning and feared the lock holding the Dark Creatures would break, releasing them.</p>
<p>However, the Dark Creatures are long dead. They were wiped out by Rakshasa many years ago."</p>
<p>Kent raised his brow.</p>
<p>He didn’t even have to think twice to believe all Uncle Drew said. He knew that if Uncle Drew said Neomi had wiped out the dark creatures, it was the truth.</p>
<p>Rakshasa was a legendary warrior. Before she wielded the spear, she was known as Shara, the mother of a killer. So her legend cannot be questioned.</p>
<p>Learning this, Kent became even more proud of his wife... ’I will reward her later.’</p>
<p>"It is good to learn that everything is settled. You guys did an amazing job, even if I have no memory of it. Once they are back, we will find something interesting for them to focus on."</p>
<p>Kent turns to Avotha next and said: "I promised you we would get revenge for your mother, and I still stand on that decision. However, for now, you must focus on other things.</p>
<p>We don’t know the extent of the Abyss Dragon’s power, so launching an attack now would be bad.</p>
<p>We also don’t know who should be handled. So for now, we can only bide our time and strike when we have all the details.</p>
<p>This means you should focus more on becoming stronger, and once I become an immortal, we can handle them. However, for now, our immediate mission is to wipe out those bastards who dare imprison my buddy."</p>
<p>Vargus’ eyes flared into flames, immediately raising the temperature in the garden... "Can’t wait to smash some brains to bits."</p>
<p>Of course, his enthusiasm may have caused the millions of people living in the tower cities to shudder.</p>
<p>"Hey, big guy, you are now living with mortals, so know when to release your demigod aura," Kent said, causing Vargus to smile and apologise... "Sorry."</p>
<p>"It is fine. Just take it easy when inside the tower."</p>
<p>He nods with a small smile. He, of course, is on the same wavelength as Kaizo, so battles are his favourite hobby. Hearing they would soon wipe out an entire family of immortals made him giddy.</p>
<p>"So what now?" Lene asked.</p>
<p>"Nothing much. I will be going to the homeworld of one of my wives. From there, I will move to a different planet to check a few things out." Kent paused for a moment and then added... "It turned out I was once known as the Faceless Assassin, so I am going back home to see if I can recover more of my memories."</p>
<p>They just let the information sink in. The idea that the handsome dragon standing before them was once a legendary assassin made them all look at him in a different light.</p>
<p>Of course, he is now nowhere close to his past selves. However, with how fast he was growing, it wouldn’t be long before the gods started running into hiding.</p>
<p>"How much do you know about your life when you were our master?" Lene suddenly asked.</p>
<p>Kent shook his head, "I know nothing of that life. And well, since there is Karma, you also cannot tell me. Until I start to remember myself, none of you can reveal or tell me anything."</p>
<p>They, of course, knew that, so they wouldn’t mess up their lives just because they couldn’t keep their mouths shut.</p>
<p>"Though I want to know. Was I cool and handsome back then?" Kent couldn’t help but ask. He had already met Chaos and Jaqen (Faceless) and knew he was one handsome bastard in both lives.</p>
<p>Of course, although he hadn’t met Jet in person yet, he saw his memories and knew that bastard was also good-looking.</p>
<p>So he was eager to know whether his other life was eye candy too.</p>
<p>Lene looked at Uncle Drew, who turned and looked at Vargus. The brute then turned to Lene. Seeing that she had no choice, she answered.</p>
<p>"Well, you were extremely handsome. However, about being cool, it depends on how you define cool."</p>
<p>Kent looked at her for a few seconds before he said:</p>
<p>"You know, like, did I have a lot of friends, women and admirers?"</p>
<p>Uncle Drew and Vargus turned away to hide their laughter, while Lene is left with a complicated expression on her face... She honestly didn’t know how to tell Kent he was the most hateful, dislikable and worst person ever to walk the face of the universe.</p>
<p>Lene had a few internal struggles, but in the end, she couldn’t bring herself to say what had to be said.</p>
<p>Instead, she asked a question, "How is the Veil Lady? I learnt she is called Nara now."</p>
<p>Of course, Kent knew something wasn’t right, but being asked about one of his wives, he quickly answered.</p>
<p>"She is fine. She is currently in seclusion, attending to something. Once she is done, she will come see you. However, Eryndor and Saden are in the Legion tower. Uncle Drew will take you there later."</p>
<p>In the end, Kent didn’t know what he was like in the past. But that was for the best. He would have to find out for himself.</p>
<p>Later, Uncle Drew gave him the items he’d asked him to get when he went to hell, along with Nyx’s gift.</p>
<p>Once everything was handled, they left for the legion tower while Kent went back to the Harem tower to hold the True Name awakening ritual for his new wives.</p>
