<p>The first person Kent saw was a redhead beauty, whose eyes contained so much killing intent he nearly called her Nefeli. After all, that kind of killing intent, he only felt it from Pill Monarch Nefeli.</p>
<p>However, instead of confronting her, he snapped the sword that came at her, and a sword shot from his Soul Universe, drilling a hole through the swordsman’s head.</p>
<p>"Endless Sword Arc Domain."</p>
<p>He then activated one of Jest’s skills, pushing away the dozens of warriors surrounding the redhead (Queenie). It was only then that Kent’s full body appeared.</p>
<p>His hand reverted to human form.</p>
<p>The battle inside the dome came to a halt when Kent stepped out.</p>
<p>The redhead’s two teammates appeared beside Queenie, each facing Kent with their backs, making sure nobody sneaked up on them.</p>
<p>"Who are you?" Queenie asked, looking at Kent with a suspicious gaze.</p>
<p>Kent smiled and flicked his hair. "You can call me Chaos... a friend of the Paragon... well, more like his tutor, considering he mostly comes to me for tutoring. But don’t tell him that."</p>
<p>Kent turned toward Klaus and waved...</p>
<p>Klaus had no time to wave back; however, he sent a message to Queenie, which made the Asura lady narrow her eyes at Kent.</p>
<p>"No need to be alarmed. I am just here to aid my bro, so point me toward a battlefield, and I will see to it that all enemies are dead."</p>
<p>Queenie didn’t hesitate and pointed toward the new red crack that appeared in the air. "Go help them handle it, and please, if you dare backstab anyone, trust me; death will be the last thing you will have to worry about."</p>
<p>"Ooohhh, a feisty one I see. No wonder my bro was always in a hurry to leave whenever he came to visit." Kent giggled, and then he turned toward the three masked figures.</p>
<p>Queenie had already killed one of them.</p>
<p>"Cool mask... too bad you three are on the bad guards’ side."</p>
<p>Kent opened the soul map and teleported behind the three. Then, with a quick swing of his hand, their heads flew into the air.</p>
<p>His gaze then met King Archon. However, something told him not to kill the King. Kent then turned and counted 420 Life-stage warriors.</p>
<p>He then turns his focus to Queenie and the two ladies.</p>
<p>"Let’s level the playing field, then." A talisman appeared in Kent’s hands. He channeled his energy into it, and a formation appeared in the air.</p>
<p>In an instant, all 430 life-stage warriors felt their cultivation base drop by a realm.</p>
<p>"That will do it." Kent smiled and winked at Queenie. "Don’t thank me; it is just that I am that awesome. However, you have 30 minutes before they return to their full strength."</p>
<p>With that, Kent vanished from the dome and appeared on a small mountain just a few km away from the new battlefield where Uncle Ziggy and the others had just clashed with the new arrivals.</p>
<p>Kent first chatted with the tower...</p>
<p>’So in this universe, my current cultivation base is at the Chaos stage. However, some cultivation realms seem to be missing in this universe.</p>
<p>So the Domain stage in this universe is comparable to Spirit Sovereign in mine, and the Life stage is Spirit Lord.</p>
<p>This means the Death stage will be Heavenly Ascension.’</p>
<p>⟦That is right, master. The cultivation stages in this world are weird and, in a way, very dangerous.⟧</p>
<p>"No kidding, tower." Kent turned and watched where Klaus was fighting, and he could feel his blood boil.</p>
<p>’If I am not mistaken, that lady will be comparable to an Empyrean stage warrior in our universe.’</p>
<p>⟦Yes. In this world, she is at the Monarch stage.⟧</p>
<p>’And what cultivation rank is the Paragon?’</p>
<p>⟦Same as you, master... He is at the Chaos stage.⟧</p>
<p>’I see. In that case, why don’t I clear the battlefield fast?’</p>
<p>⟦The master should really do that. I can sense great danger coming from this portal. It is like somebody really wants this world gone, and they will stop at nothing to achieve it... or until he is dead.⟧</p>
<p>"Good thing I am here." Kent sighed, watching how Klaus parried Loff’s attack and then, with a powerful kick, sent her flying.</p>
<p>"He is worried."</p>
<p>⟦Yes. However, I will do my very best to ensure you are safe.⟧</p>
<p>Kent nodded, but he couldn’t help sensing something strange... "Tower, are you sacking me so you can do something on your own?"</p>
<p>This was the first time Kent sensed such a feeling from the tower. It was like she was hiding something...</p>
<p>"Spill it."</p>
<p>⟦Master is perceptive as ever. I am indeed hiding something... I sense another intelligent life coming from that man, and I want to see if we can work together to ensure the world is saved in the end.⟧</p>
<p>"So you want to make a friend, huh. Good for you, tower..."</p>
<p>Gaap appeared in Kent’s hand, and a mask covered his face.</p>
<p>"Good luck, tower." Kent moved again and appeared on the battlefield. His sword met that of a Life-stage warrior—a human with a pair of horns on his forehead.</p>
<p>Kent kicked with his right leg and pivoted with his left hand, grabbing the Life-stage warrior by the neck. This combo left the warrior disoriented.</p>
<p>This allowed Kent to use the Soul Torture on him.</p>
<p>"A Nethran, huh." Kent snapped his neck and threw him away. Then he looked toward the portal, watching as Death-stage warriors started to appear.</p>
<p>"Heavenly Ascensions in this universe are no joke. However, maybe because these people are a wandering race facing countless dangers in their travels, they are also much more dangerous than others."</p>
<p>Kent didn’t want to ponder on it much, so he teleported and appeared a meter from the Death-stage warriors.</p>
<p>"I don’t know the dynamics of this universe, but from what I read from your pal, you guys aren’t supposed to be here... at least not right now."</p>
<p>"Kill him!"</p>
<p>A Death-stage warrior with a larger horn gave the command, and Kent was surrounded.</p>
<p>"Not a race of talkers, huh..." Kent raised his sword, and in an instant, an intense Profound Blood Sword aura surrounded it.</p>
<p>The advancing Death-stage warriors came to a halt when they sensed the danger.</p>
<p>"Too late. Gaap, let’s reap blood."</p>
<p>[Gladly.]</p>
<p>Kent’s move activated 60,000 flame veins and 2,000 blood runes. In an instant, a terrifying pressure descended on everyone.</p>
<p>"Flame Sea!"</p>
<p>The 2 km radius instantly turned into a sea of flame, causing the Nethrans to feel their energy drain. Kent didn’t bat an eye and began his slaughter.</p>
<p>This was a good time for him to farm blood essence for his second blood core gate. So, having nearly an entire battlefield to himself, he started to reap blood.</p>
<p>As Kent was busy reaping blood, he heard someone issue a command.</p>
<p>"Uncles, Anna, why don’t you leave this place to me? You can go handle the Neburians," Sofia ordered, and Klaus’s uncles nodded.</p>
<p>The moment they pulled back, Sofia turned into a dragon and roared to life.</p>
<p>"That’s more like it," Kent smiled, knowing what Sofia was trying to tell him.</p>
<p>He paused and formed a hand seal. In an instant, the entire battlefield turned into a sea of flame.</p>
<p>"Handle the Death-stage warriors; I will take care of the rest," Sofia said, and Kent nodded. The two dragons started to slaughter, leaving charred bodies in their wake.</p>
