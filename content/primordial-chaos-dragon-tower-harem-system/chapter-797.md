<p>[Inside the Tower - 120 days seclusion; Day 1]</p>
<p>After handling everything, Kent settled down in the sword tower to attend to his primary objectives for entering this short seclusion.</p>
<p>The first thing he wanted to learn was how to create Flame Veins and Flame Cores. Of course, he already has a manual for both processes.</p>
<p>"Agni said it will take time to start forming the veins, that I will need powerful flame essence to be able to manage it.</p>
<p>So I guess I’d better learn the method now, and the rest will be handled after I get to the sect." Kent took out the scroll for creating flame veins and unfurled it.</p>
<p>"Wow, that is one complex diagram," Kent smiled, looking at the intricate design. The diagram is the structure that governs how the body functions, how energy is channeled, and how flame veins can be created.</p>
<p>Essentially, he was about to alter the structure of his body, adding more organs, which, in this case, would significantly enhance his power in all aspects.</p>
<p>It’s like genetic modification, only this time, instead of modifying his body using DNA from other beings, he would be creating new organs—108,000 of them, to be exact—and he would have to do all that while inside the hottest flame region he could find.</p>
<p>"According to Agni, it took him 300 years to fully learn this complex method. However, since I am a genius, this shouldn’t take too long."</p>
<p>Kent smiled and then made the unfurled scroll hover in the air before him.</p>
<p>He channeled his energy into it, and before he could even blink, his consciousness was pulled into the technique.</p>
<p>He appeared within a realm of flames, where everything was made of fire. The stones, plants, rivers, and even the very ground he stood on were made from lava.</p>
<p>However, the sky was not made of flame; instead, it was composed of millions of miniature diagrams formed from runes.</p>
<p>There were just millions of them, and looking past them, Kent could see a large door hidden in the clouds.</p>
<p>"That’s a lot of diagrams. No wonder it took a genius like Agni 300 years to learn this technique," Kent scratched the back of his head.</p>
<p>"Per his teaching, I need to learn and understand every diagram. Every success creates a step that will lead me to the gate of Flame Evolution hidden in the cloud.</p>
<p>The moment I open that door, I can begin walking the path of Flame Vein Creation."</p>
<p>Kent sighed and then sat down. He had a lot of work to do and little time to complete it. He closed his eyes and formed a seal.</p>
<p>The moment the seal formed, a diagram moved and slowly entered his forehead. Kent felt a small understanding of the flame enter his mind.</p>
<p>He quickly began channeling more diagrams into his mind. Soon, he was being bombarded by thousands of diagrams every minute.</p>
<p>A day quickly went by, but the number of diagrams left to learn still numbered in the millions; however, the speed at which Kent was learning them was much faster than one would expect.</p>
<p>After two days, the number started to show signs of reduction. By this time, Kent had learned over a million diagrams.</p>
<p>Three days later, it became apparent that he was learning more and more diagrams as the number of runes started to dwindle.</p>
<p>The process was complex, and although he was making progress, as the days passed, his speed began to slow down.</p>
<p>Of course, sometimes it hastened, and at other times it slowed down.</p>
<p>However, as more and more diagrams entered his mind, a stairway began to form toward the door in the clouds.</p>
<p>---</p>
<p>[Day 25 into mastering the Flame Vein Creation Art]</p>
<p>Kent’s body on the outside started showing signs of change. At first, his soul flame surged and burned his shirt. However, as the days passed, changes began to appear on his body.</p>
<p>On the 25th day, as he was diligently studying the manual, a flame tattoo appeared on Kent’s chest, pulsating with energy.</p>
<p>At first, it was just a tattoo, but as time passed, the tattoo began to spread all over his skin, almost as if it had a life of its own. On the 30th day, it moved and stopped when it reached his forehead.</p>
<p>That was when the true progress Kent never expected started to happen.</p>
<p>Inside the technique space, the diagrams began entering his mind much faster the moment the tattoo stopped at his forehead.</p>
<p>By the 50th day, only a few diagrams remained. By the time the 50th day came to a close, the last diagram moved and entered his forehead, and the final step leading to the door formed, stopping in front of the door.</p>
<p>Kent opened his eyes and stood up.</p>
<p>"That took longer than I anticipated, but I am not there yet." He stepped on the first step, and soon, he was on his way toward the door.</p>
<p>If Agni or anyone who had ever learned this technique heard Kent saying it took him more time than it should, they would probably pull on his ear and spank him for his shameless and rude comment.</p>
<p>A genius like Agni had to spend 300 years learning it, and Kent was already complaining after spending just 50 days.</p>
<p>The audacity of his words said it all.</p>
<p>Soon, he was in front of the door, and with a gentle push, the door opened, and his eyes opened on the inside.</p>
<p>Messages quickly appeared before his eyes.</p>
<p>⟦Congratulations on successfully learning the Flame Vein Creation Manual⟧</p>
<p>⟦Your Primordial Infinity Dragon Art has activated⟧</p>
<p>Kent felt a rush of energy wash through his body, then a new message appeared.</p>
<p>⟦Your Primordial Infinity Dragon Art is merging with the Flame Vein Creation Manual⟧</p>
<p>⟦Merging was successful⟧</p>
<p>⟦Congratulations, your cultivation technique grade has been upgraded from Celestial ↠ Primordial⟧</p>
<p>Kent raised his brow, reading the message.</p>
<p>"Looks like I did something good again." He smiled and then checked the last message, bringing an even bigger smile to his face.</p>
<p>⟦The Flame Vein Creation Manual has merged with your cultivation technique. Whenever you are close to a flame source, the flame vein creation process will automatically start⟧</p>
<p>"Everything is automated," Kent smiled, feeling like he had just won the lottery.</p>
<p>The process that requires one to meticulously stay in a flame-rich region, using hundreds, perhaps thousands, of years to form flame veins, can now be achieved by merely walking through flame-rich regions.</p>
<p>"At this rate, I will just pack my bag and go stay in the Flame Valley for a while. I am sure by the time I leave there, I will form a couple of flame veins."</p>
<p>Kent channeled his energy through his body to see if there were any changes.</p>
<p>He indeed sensed something had changed about his body. Now, he felt that his body lacked considerable strength.</p>
<p>At first, he could feel that his body was perfectly adjusted. But now, he could tell he was missing some things. These things, of course, were the flame veins he had yet to form.</p>
<p>"The sect better have a flame-rich training area, else I will be pissed."</p>
<p>⟦Congratulations to the master for mastering the Flame Vein Creation Manual. The next on your schedule is the Flame Core Creation Manual⟧.</p>
<p>"Thanks, Tower, but when did you create a schedule for me?" Kent asked with a smirk.</p>
<p>⟦As the master grows, so do I, and at this moment in my growth, I saw it best to create a schedule for the master, emphasizing the essential tasks so the master wouldn’t forget⟧</p>
<p>"That is thoughtful of you, Tower. Then I guess I will start learning the second manual now."</p>
