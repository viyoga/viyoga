<p>"So you ladies want to spend a week here, huh?" Kent asked, sitting with his women in Dori’s house. They had just told him they would spend a week there before returning to the tower.</p>
<p>Of course, using outside time, a week was not that long, but compared to tower time, a week was equivalent to many months inside the tower.</p>
<p>That was a lot of time wasted. However, it wasn’t as if they were wasting it for nothing.</p>
<p>They were waiting for Princess Lovina to complete her tempering, then they would enter the Primordial Chamber and initiate the awakening of the Primordial Spirit.</p>
<p>It would take close to 30 years for that to end, but he had a feeling they would come out powerful. Spending a week outside before entering was already enough for him to understand their request.</p>
<p>In the end, he let them stay.</p>
<p>After spending hours with them, he went to see the king one last time.</p>
<p>"These are all Spirit King-grade battle dolls. I will set them at the borders, so you can pull back some of your guards and assign the trustworthy ones to patrol with these dolls," Kent said, introducing the Guardian Swordsmen and Archers to the king, who was very shocked but happy to have such amazing battle dolls.</p>
<p>Kent took out a scroll and handed it to the king. "This is how to upgrade them and the materials required. If you manage to get even five out of the ten items, you can upgrade them to Tier 15, which is the Spirit Emperor stage.</p>
<p>However, even without such extreme upgrades, they are still amazing, so don’t hesitate to use them whenever there is trouble while I am away."</p>
<p>"Thank you, Kent. You really saved me and my people," King Hkum said with a grateful expression. Kent nodded and stood up.</p>
<p>"Oh, before I forget, you can give some of the Ambrosia apples to your allies. I don’t know how much you trust them, but if that trust is enough, then go ahead and give them some." With that, Kent vanished.</p>
<p>The king and his elders exchanged a glance and a sigh.</p>
<p>"Yet again, our supreme advisor has made sure we got the better end of the deal. This is just too great. With this, not only will we soon have the power to counter the Orc alliance, but we can start moving around the world again and even travel outside," King Hkum said.</p>
<p>The elders nodded.</p>
<p>"This is all thanks to Kent. He has indeed brought a change to us," Elder Jorin said. The other elders smiled, hearing words they would forever speak about to the generations yet to come.</p>
<p>"The queen and the children have already entered seclusion; you five should do the same. I have already broken a limit, so I don’t need to enter seclusion urgently.</p>
<p>I will help Ashfur handle the array before doing so. By then, the queen will be out, and she can hold down the fort while I also enter seclusion."</p>
<p>The elders couldn’t argue that. They should truly enter seclusion because when the time comes, they will be the ones facing the most danger.</p>
<p>"Also, about our allies, I have already decided to give them two each for now. Although we are allies, I still have my reservations, so for now, I will provide them with two each. When we see progress in the alliance, we can add more.</p>
<p>Also, General Ragnar has selected 25 trusted soldiers, each a Spirit King, to receive the fruit, so I think we will have more in reserve for now."</p>
<p>The elders nodded and then fine-tuned some details before retiring to seclusion. When the king was left alone, he sighed and then leaned back in his throne.</p>
<p>"Kent Madson, just what kind of existence are you?" His question remained unanswered as he vanished the next second and appeared inside a room where his wife was cultivating the Ambrosia apple.</p>
<p>He sealed the room, then went ahead and did the same for his children before leaving. As he said, with the elders and his wife in seclusion, he would ensure that no harm came to anyone.</p>
<p>Kent also went back to his wives and chose to spend another day with them.</p>
<p>At this point, the Foxkin already knew there were humans in their land, so when Kent and his wives went to a restaurant to eat, they didn’t face any injustice.</p>
<p>Kent spent the day shopping with his wives, eating, and visiting the best places in Emberwood. They even went outside into the Yellow Maple Forest and had some fun there before returning to the city.</p>
<p>After spending the night with them, he returned to the tower. Sword King Shawn had already entered seclusion and was busy at work, so Kent didn’t bother with him before he left.</p>
<p>He already knew he would be gone for months, perhaps a year, so he just left.</p>
<p>Kent quickly appeared in the Tower City to inform Elaine’s parents, who are considered leaders of the humans in the tower, and Tribe Chief Malthar of the Bloodvein tribe that he would be gone for some years.</p>
<p>Since time ran differently inside the tower, they knew the years he was referring to would be equivalent to months on the outside.</p>
<p>"Don’t worry, Kent, we already have everything we need," Lady Rain said after Kent informed them of his plan for the next couple of years.</p>
<p>"Alright. However, if you need anything, Gaia and her sisters are there to assist you. Just let them know." They nodded, and Kent walked away.</p>
<p>After he left, he went straight to the Legion Tower.</p>
<p>"Master, are we going on another mission?" As expected, Kaizo was the first to notice his presence and immediately asked what he wanted to know.</p>
<p>Kent just smiled, regretting that he had made the brute unyielding and always hungry for battle. "You should get yourself a woman, Kaizo. Why is it always about battle to you?"</p>
<p>The undead and the Dungeon bosses laughed. Kaizo was the same, too. Kent looked at them and shook his head.</p>
<p>"I will be entering seclusion soon, but before that, why don’t I upgrade you all?"</p>
