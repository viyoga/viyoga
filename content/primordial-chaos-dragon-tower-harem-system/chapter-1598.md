<p>Yggzrael spent some time telling Kent all he knew about the Abyss Layer.</p>
<p>From what he said, it appeared that in the Abyss layer, there are four overlords, each terrifying opponents.</p>
<p>Yggzrael couldn’t put into words the true extent of their strength. However, from what he said, it appeared these four overlords were stronger than Celestials.</p>
<p>Before coming to the Tormented Chaos Abyss, Princess Pandora told him that her main body was one of the overlords of the Abyss Layer and that she had 25% authority over the Tormented Chaos Abyss.</p>
<p>Since that was the case, it might mean the other three overlords were on the same level as her.</p>
<p>Kent came to this conclusion because, in his opinion, if she were stronger than they, she would have suppressed them a long time ago. However, since she hadn’t, it showed those three overlords were very terrifying.</p>
<p>As someone who knew just how terrifying the Queen of Evil truly was, Kent realized that the other three overlords weren’t beings he could afford to antagonize.</p>
<p>Even when he was at his peak as the Ruthless Bandit, he knew he wouldn’t be able to last an hour against 25% of the Queen of Evil’s power.</p>
<p>Thanks to this reveal, he finally understood why Yggzrael was scared of him.</p>
<p>Apparently, aside from the overlords, there were even Immortal Lord-level beasts in the Abyss Layer. However, these beasts were under the authority of the overlords, and as such, the power they held was not something low-level beasts like them could contend with.</p>
<p>Also, the beasts there, regardless of their cultivation, each had the aura of a Monarch. Because of this, they didn’t dare mess around with beings from that layer.</p>
<p>"So you thought I was from the Abyss Layer," Kent chuckled, a bit helpless at what Yggzrael assumed of him.</p>
<p>"Young Master holds the presence of a Monarch... in fact, Young Master’s presence is even more commanding compared to a Monarch I had once met. I dare not antagonize this young master."</p>
<p>Kent smiled and nodded. "Worry not, I am not from the Abyss Layer. However, I plan on visiting there someday."</p>
<p>Yggzrael only looked at Kent with a bewildered expression.</p>
<p>"Enough about me. How about you, Yggzrael? Do you want to become my contracted beast? If you accept, I will grant you powers far beyond your understanding and even ensure you leave this place and have the chance to explore the universe out there."</p>
<p>If it were before Kent unleashed his [Chaos Monarch’s Sovereignty], maybe Yggzrael would have become mad and attacked Kent out of anger for saying he wanted to make him his contracted beast.</p>
<p>However, in the wild, the strong rule.</p>
<p>Yggzrael had recognized Kent’s strength, so he had no reason to reject the offer he had been given. If it meant saving his neck, taking a master wasn’t too bad.</p>
<p>Even so, he sat there for a minute to analyze a few things.</p>
<p>Before he could respond, Kent smiled and said:</p>
<p>"If you want, I am willing to take you on in a duel. If you can withstand three attacks from me, I will elevate your bloodline to Celestial Grade and even grant you a unique cultivation method that would one day allow you to ascend to the rank of a Monarch."</p>
<p>"I accept."</p>
<p>"Good. Then let’s fight first before signing the contract."</p>
<p>Kent smiled happily.</p>
<p>He came to test his abilities, so having the chance to fight Yggzrael felt like the best thing that had happened to him since he became a True Immortal.</p>
<p>In no time, they were ready to fight.</p>
<p>"Be sure to give it your all, Yggzrael. I don’t know the true extent of my abilities just yet."</p>
<p>Yggzrael nodded and prepared for Kent to attack.</p>
<p>Seeing he hadn’t raised any defense yet, Kent smiled and raised his right hand.</p>
<p>Tapping into the knowledge behind the [Finger of Destruction], he activated it, but instead of fire, he used ice.</p>
<p>BOOM</p>
<p>The cloud exploded, and a large finger appeared, a terrifying pressure descending on Yggzrael. Initially, he was prepared to fight, but first wanted to see what power Kent had.</p>
<p>Now, he saw it, and the expression on his face changed.</p>
<p>Abandoning his human form, he transformed back into a large Tree Dragon, his body covered in crystal ice.</p>
<p>"Frozen Forest Domain,"</p>
<p>Yggzrael tapped into one of his strongest defensive abilities, and at once, a large fortress of ice made from both roots and ice formed, creating a large defensive structure strong enough to block a full-on attack from even a God Ascension stage cultivator.</p>
<p>"That won’t be enough, buddy," Kent grinned, his left eye emitting a black aura. Inside his soul sea, his Evil Core spun, releasing waves of Evil Qi.</p>
<p>Up in the air, dark runes started appearing on the finger that was halfway through the cloud. The moment the first rune appeared, the pressure doubled, and a crack appeared in the [Frozen Forest Domain.]</p>
<p>But it didn’t end there.</p>
<p>As more runes started to appear, the pressure kept increasing.</p>
<p>"What in the hell?" Yggzrael felt his brain go numb as the pressure falling down on him continued to increase.</p>
<p>He tapped into his power and summoned more frozen structures. Aside from that, he activated two more defensive abilities, [Ice Dragon Shield] and [Palm of Carnage].</p>
<p>The moment the two skills appeared, the pressure bearing down on him lessened.</p>
<p>Kent noticed it, so he did the next best thing. He tapped into his Chaos Heart and unleashed 1% of his Chaos Qi.</p>
<p>BOOM BOOM BOOM</p>
<p>The [Frozen Forest Domain], [Ice Dragon Shield], and [Palm of Carnage] shattered the moment this Chaos Essence was added.</p>
<p>Pfft</p>
<p>Yggzrael transformed back into his humanoid form and coughed up a mouthful of blood, his pale expression whiter than before.</p>
<p>Seeing this, Kent smiled and snapped his finger, causing the [Finger of Destruction] to disperse into a mist of ice, Evil Qi, and Chaos Qi.</p>
<p>"What was that?" Yggzrael asked, the look in his eyes telling Kent that if before he was scared of him, now he was terrified.</p>
<p>Kent laughed. "Nothing to be worried about. I just used 25% of my Ice Qi, 5% Evil Qi, and 1% Chaos Qi to unleash that attack. I never expected it would be that powerful."</p>
<p>"Powerful... that attack was terrifying. While I am not that good at defense, I doubt even someone with decent defense would survive such an attack." Yggzrael wiped the blood off the corner of his mouth.</p>
<p>"Just who are you really?"</p>
<p>Kent laughed and stretched his arms. "Nobody special."</p>
<p>Yggzrael could only shake his head and will himself to stop shaking.</p>
<p>What both he and Kent didn’t know was that just now, when Kent activated the [Finger of Destruction], the purple cocoon inside Kent’s soul sea stirred and released a strand of incomplete Dragon Intent.</p>
<p>That incomplete strand transformed the attack beyond what Kent expected. However, because he had no idea what had happened, both of them remained oblivious.</p>
<p>Already, the [Dragon Intent] Seed had reached 78% completion. However, without knowing it, it had already started aiding Kent.</p>
<p>Had it not been for it, Yggzrael would have been able to defend against the [Finger of Destruction]. However, now, things were different, and Kent, who had just defeated a peak-level demigod, could only let his pride and ego inflate.</p>
<p>After making sure Yggzrael had calmed down, Kent retrieved one of the Grim Contracts and activated it. Two runes appeared from it, one merging with Yggzrael while the other merged with Kent.</p>
<p>In no time, the contract was signed.</p>
