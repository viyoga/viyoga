<p>"That was close," Kent, who narrowly evaded the arrow, looked toward his right. Up in the air was a cloaked figure aiming another arrow at him.</p>
<p>The cloaked figure is another Transcendent trying to kill him, just like everyone. So Kent didn’t even bother appraising him.</p>
<p>He quickly moved away but was stopped by the Metal Saint Adolf, who slashed his sword, sending a powerful sword arc toward him.</p>
<p>Kent countered with his own sword arc, but before he could get back into action, another arrow arrived in front of him.</p>
<p>He blinked away, but the next second, Metal Saint Andrew’s spear was upon him. It was a perfectly coordinated strike from the three, but Kent knew it was just a coincidence.</p>
<p>However, he cannot underestimate them.</p>
<p>’Damn, fighting Root Transcendents is no joke,’ Kent thought inwardly, jumping into the air to evade a powerful swing from the spearman, who was determined to kill the brat that had slain one of them.</p>
<p>"Die, brat!" Metal Saint Andrew shouted.</p>
<p>His spear multiplied into dozens as he stabbed forward, sending all of them toward Kent, who swung his sword, creating hundreds of sword arcs that decimated the spears and rained down on the spearman.</p>
<p>"Ouch!" Kent was sent flying when an arrow landed on his shoulder. He rolled on the ground for a few seconds, considering the force behind the arrow was just too much for his strength level.</p>
<p>The archer, moving through the air, flashed forward with a short sword, ready to end the battle. Kent, who was on the ground, smirked, watching him close in.</p>
<p>However, just as he was a meter away, a pointed dark shard shot up from the ground, piercing his chest and killing him instantly.</p>
<p>Kent used the [Doom Shard] skill he received when he added Alina to the harem and it worked like he expected.</p>
<p>It is a perfect sneak attack skill.</p>
<p>It was one powerful skill that allowed him to conjure shards made from black diamonds. The durability alone said it all.</p>
<p>It went through his chest like it was nothing.</p>
<p>"Punk," Kent spat blood on the ground, feeling the pain from the arrow moving through his shoulder.</p>
<p>⟪You’ve been poisoned, master⟫ Akira’s voice entered his ears as he moved to the side, evading the spear that had appeared in front of his face.</p>
<p>’I know, Akira. I just need a minute to recover,’ Kent said, already jumping between attacks. His bloodline was already working on handling the poison.</p>
<p>He clashed with the metal saints again, who started to take him much more seriously now.</p>
<p>"Skybreaker Fist!"</p>
<p>Kaizo’s voice boomed as his body rose into the air, bathed in lightning. His fist was clenched, and all around it, a metal coating crackled with lightning.</p>
<p>He ascended until he was 20 meters above the ground. Then he descended, and the next second, his powerful fist slammed into the ground, throwing everyone into the air.</p>
<p>The Metal Dolls, who were in the air when this fist landed, moved, and the next second, dozens of Transcendents were killed.</p>
<p>"Sorry, Master. I didn’t know that attack would be that dangerous," Kaizo said, making Kent smirk.</p>
<p>Kent’s sword swung forward, and 400 sword arcs rained down on Metal Saint Adolf, who tried to defend against all of them but simply couldn’t.</p>
<p>The only way to defend against that was to cover their bodies with something the sword arcs could not cut.</p>
<p>The defence Adolf raised was destroyed, and his left arm was severed from his body. The moment that happened, Kent moved in for the kill, but Metal Saint Andrew spoke before his sword could pierce his chest.</p>
<p>"Brat, don’t kill him. If you do, you will never know peace ever again," his words were desperate yet arrogant.</p>
<p>"Okay," Kent said, and then stabbed Adolf through the heart, ending him.</p>
<p>"A wise man once said, ’I hate peace; I want problems, always,’" he smiled and then looked at Andrew. "Now, prepare to die, as that is the only way out for you."</p>
<p>The Metal Saint held a grim look on his face.</p>
<p>"Then we can die together," he said, taking a dark pill from his space ring and swallowing it. The moment he did, his eyes turned dark, and he coughed up blood.</p>
<p>"Blood Possession Pill... What an idiot," Kent muttered, but he wasn’t smiling.</p>
<p>"Kaizo, end the battle in the next five minutes," he shouted, and the undead brute nodded.</p>
<p>He cracked his knuckles, and the rest was devastation for the pitiful Transcendents who were utterly useless in front of the brute whose skin was just too thick against their weapons.</p>
<p>"Kaizo, use your domain skill," Kent ordered, sensing something was amiss. He gave the command to protect his life and that of his undead.</p>
<p>"Lightning Domain," Kaizo obeyed and activated the skill, immediately creating a domain around him and the eight pitiful Transcendents now pleading for their lives.</p>
<p>The Dolls were also handling the last of the six Transcendents they had targeted.</p>
<p>On the other hand, Kent was being bombarded by attacks only a Root Ascendant could unleash.</p>
<p>The pill Metal Saint Andrew took raises one’s strength to the next stage for a limited time. Five minutes in most cases.</p>
<p>This meant that he had entered the Root Ascendant stage for the next five minutes. After five minutes, he would self-destruct, and the results would be devastating.</p>
<p>Of course, Kent took all of this into perspective, so he was managing the attacks until Kaizo and the Dolls were done.</p>
<p>"Die, brat!" The possessed Metal Saint shouted.</p>
<p>A dark beam shot from his spear, but Kent cut it in half and countered with his own attack, sending 400 sword arcs at him.</p>
<p>However, they were all neutralized by a dark cocoon created by Metal Saint Andrew, who was coughing blood with every passing second.</p>
<p>"60 seconds more," Kent muttered as he evaded an attack from the now-dying Metal Saint, who was filled with regret at that moment.</p>
<p>He regretted ever coming against such a brat. He regretted not walking away when he was met with the first obstacle.</p>
<p>He regretted not having the chance to stab his sword through Kent’s chest. He regretted many things.</p>
<p>Now, it was ending, and he could only wallow in regret as the seconds ticked down.</p>
<p>Kaizo held the last Transcendent by the neck as his lightning burned through him, separating the head from the body.</p>
<p>The battle dolls had also finished off the six they were dealing with, each bathed in blood like their master and Kaizo, their teammate, who looked like a disaster in humanoid form.</p>
<p>"20 seconds," Kent counted as he kept evading the attacks. The Metal Saint had injured him in a few places, but Kent was healing much faster than he was losing blood.</p>
<p>In fact, whenever he was cut, his healing instantly sealed his wounds, making them appear as if they had never been inflicted. Of course, deeper wounds were different.</p>
<p>"5 seconds," Kent counted down as he blocked a spear thrust aimed at his chest. His leg moved, landing on Metal Saint Andrew’s face and sending him flying.</p>
<p>"4 seconds."</p>
<p>"3 seconds."</p>
<p>When a second remained, He snapped his fingers, and the Metal Saint vanished. The next second, he was on the outside.</p>
<p>Then, a painful scream escaped his mouth.</p>
<p>At the same time, his body expanded, and then—*boom*—he exploded, decimating the two-mile radius.</p>
