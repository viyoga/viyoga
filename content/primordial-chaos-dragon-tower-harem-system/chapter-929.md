<p>"Oh, this is bad," Kent said, walking from the side of the road and finding a place to sit. He needed to calm down first before making any more moves.</p>
<p>His mind was moving at an unprecedented speed.</p>
<p>When he asked Gaia to fix the Pill World, she told him that some things would have to be removed if she were to sync the time of the tower with that of the Pill World.</p>
<p>Naturally, one of those things was Kent’s ability to take anything from the tower storage. At first, he didn’t put much emphasis on that and planned to work around it before entering the Pill World again.</p>
<p>However, because of what happened when he tried to break his limits, and the fact that he wasn’t in the right mindset after discovering what happened to Elina during the primordial era, he entered the Pill World without any planning.</p>
<p>Now, not only is he broke, but he also cannot figure out how to make money.</p>
<p>"If I were to concoct pills and sell them, I would need ingredients and a cauldron, which I don’t have because I have no money on me.</p>
<p>Then again, if I were to write a few techniques and sell them, I would make more money. The only thing is, I don’t have the necessary scroll and ink to do that either.</p>
<p>Let’s not forget that selling one of my refined swords is nearly impossible. Lastly, I can’t leave the Pill World until the 300 days are up."</p>
<p>Kent scratched the back of his head and started to think again. Ten minutes later, he was still no closer to finding a sound solution.</p>
<p>"Maybe I should just go back and ask big sister for some money," he thought. The moment this idea entered his mind, Kent shook it off and then stood up.</p>
<p>"I know she will give me as much as I want, but I don’t feel like that will be appropriate. Of course, I could use the fact that I have given her heaven-defying pill recipes to take a couple of million spirit stones.</p>
<p>However, that would just be too shameless of me," Kent started walking again while his mind worked on coming up with a solution.</p>
<p>⟦The master is forgetting he has something else inside his Soul Universe⟧</p>
<p>Kent nearly paused when he heard the tower’s voice enter his head. However, because of what had happened earlier when he stood in the road, this time he managed to control himself and kept walking.</p>
<p>’Tower, how can you speak? I thought Gaia said you cannot talk to me,’ Kent asked with a slightly relieved tone.</p>
<p>Aside from losing access to the tower storage, he also could not speak with the tower, or at least that was what Gaia said.</p>
<p>⟦I am merely borrowing the days the master has to speak. Every minute spent talking to you is a day off your time limit here⟧</p>
<p>Kent nodded in his head. He knew Gaia was always considerate. She wouldn’t create something without adding a backdoor.</p>
<p>He knew how important the tower was to Kent.</p>
<p>However, hearing that his days were being eaten away by the tower was already a sign that he shouldn’t depend on it too much.</p>
<p>’It is good to hear your voice, tower. However, what else can I use to earn some money aside from the refined swords?’</p>
<p>⟦The master has the Soul Flag inside the Soul Universe. I calculated you might make this mistake after the issue with the Limit Breaker and your memories, so I placed the Soul Flag in the Soul Universe.</p>
<p>All you have to do is find a Soul Temple or auction house and auction off any of the soul spirits inside the flag.</p>
<p>You have five Heavenly Ascension stage soul spirits, four hundred and five Spirit Lords, and thousands of others. You can auction any and use the money to make more money.</p>
<p>But the master should be careful, a preserved soul spirit is a hot commodity many will kill for. Additionally, those inside the Soul Flag have been nurtured in the Soul Universe, making them unique.⟧</p>
<p>Kent’s eyes lit up when he heard what the tower had to say. He never knew the tower would come through for him.</p>
<p>Now that it had, Kent couldn’t be any happier.</p>
<p>’Thank you, tower.’</p>
<p>⟦You are welcome, master. I will be unavailable to speak with you for the next sixty days, so please be cautious. And remember, you are in a Supreme High World⟧</p>
<p>Kent smiled happily as he moved through the busy street. He had his soul map spread out as he searched for the nearest auction house.</p>
<p>He only needed to sell a Spirit King Soul Spirit, and then he would know what to do next. Naturally, Kent didn’t even know soul spirits were valuable.</p>
<p>He had only stored them as ingredients for refining swords. But now that he had discovered they could be used for much more, he began to wonder what else they might be useful for.</p>
<p>"I guess I will find out more after I get some spirit stones," Kent muttered as he spotted a grand auction house, which made him recall his first visit to an auction.</p>
<p>It was when he went to the Silver Leaf Auction House with Unity. Back then, he merely wanted to sell some pills and make a few spirit stones. However, he ended up meeting the woman with the most magnificent butt he had ever seen.</p>
<p>Thinking about it now, he realised that if he hadn’t gone to sell those Tier 0 and Tier 1 pills, he wouldn’t have met Alina.</p>
<p>"Auction houses are the best," Kent smiled and began moving toward the location.</p>
<p>"I’d better be careful then. The place I’m heading to has four Heavenly Ascension stage auras. If I didn’t know better, I would say today is a mega auction."</p>
<p>As he drew closer to the auction house, Kent started to wonder if his guess was correct. A few minutes later, after his soul map covered the entire building, he froze in his steps again.</p>
<p>Thankfully, this time there was no crowd on the road, so nobody hurled insults at him. "This is crazy. Just how common are Heavenly Ascension stage cultivators to the point that Ten can be found in one place?"</p>
<p>Kent looked toward the large tower standing three kilometres away from him.</p>
<p>"Their auras are anything but weak. If I didn’t know better, I would even assume they are beyond the stage of Heavenly Ascension."</p>
<p>For the first time, Kent realised that even if he used his bloodline power and Flame Veins, he wouldn’t be able to handle five of them the way he did with the Forsaken Elders.</p>
<p>A smile appeared on his face. "If a Pill World has this many powerful experts, then how dangerous would a Sword World or Dragon world be?"</p>
<p>"I must become stronger"</p>
<p>He started walking again. "I have already spent ten days here. Before I leave, I must form my Blood Core Gate and add at least nine thousand more Flame Veins. If possible, I must even form a Flame Core.</p>
<p>My ladies and the Legion would have already challenged the fourth floor, or are about to do so. I calculated that they will be able to reach the sixth floor before they can’t go any further.</p>
<p>By then, I should be ready to ascend to a High World. Although I have the Pill World to come and train there, a lot can change in three hundred and seventy thousand years, so I must ascend."</p>
<p>With that resolve, Kent started moving toward the Brown Maple Auction House. A few minutes later, he approached the tall tower.</p>
<p>"Stop right there, human..." A dark elf guard shouted, releasing his Spirit Sovereign cultivation toward Kent.</p>
