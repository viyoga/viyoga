<p>Kent had absolutely no idea when the five dragons appeared.</p>
<p>However, now that he was watching them battle the Nirvana stage warriors, he couldn’t help but feel happy.</p>
<p>He could see the danger was already at its peak, and with Paragon now facing six people in what appeared to be a strange kind of tribulation, the more help they got, the better.</p>
<p>Kent could also tell that more of the warriors were missing, suggesting they were either dead or taken off the board and sent somewhere.</p>
<p>This made him wonder just what kind of ability could allow someone to take an entire unit of warriors off a battlefield.</p>
<p>Then he recalled the Primordial Space his wives have, and he got the answer.</p>
<p>’The necromancer is nowhere to be found. Looks like he has taken some loads off,’ Kent spotted the first change.</p>
<p>Then it struck him...</p>
<p>’I have the soul map, yet I failed to notice these changes. Does it mean my focus was rooted in these Nirvana stage warriors to the point I didn’t even hear or see these changes?’</p>
<p>Clearly, Kent didn’t like this discovery.</p>
<p>He had seen how Paragon created a clone to go save someone and even controlled swords with his mind to aid his friends while still locked in a fierce and deadly battle.</p>
<p>Obviously, that made Kent see a weakness—his mental strength.</p>
<p>His mind was weak, and he could see that now.</p>
<p>’I can’t have this. The moment I ascend, the chances of my adventuring into a unique or immortal world would be very high. If I can’t spot a danger from miles away, then death will become my next neighbour for sure.’</p>
<p>The idea of having the means yet failing to use them made Kent very scared for his own life.</p>
<p>However, he also knew he couldn’t handle whatever this weakness was for now. His mission was to kill the remaining two Nirvana stage warriors and stop the ones setting up the transporter.</p>
<p>"No pressure then."</p>
<p>Kent muttered, swerving with practical ease as he evaded a sword beam unleashed by one of the Nirvana stage warriors.</p>
<p>However, the next second, he was forced to jump back just in time to evade a powerful slash at his wing. The sword scratched the surface of his wing, and the noise produced made Kent, in his dragon form, grin.</p>
<p>It sounded like metal scratching metal. Naturally, Kent didn’t even have to think to know he had absolutely nothing to worry about...</p>
<p>His wings were metallic, or at least would be metallic. He had merely used 25% of his bloodline, so he could only be impressed by how sturdy his wings were.</p>
<p>He moved his massive body back, sending a dust storm into the air. Then, using his colossal wings, he unleashed the dust at the two Nirvana stage angel commanders.</p>
<p>The cloud of dust spread violently, swirling around like a raging sandstorm. The two Nirvana stage angel commanders instinctively raised their defence barriers, hiding their bodies behind them.</p>
<p>The storm was violent and deadly.</p>
<p>The idea that Kent would let them escape also occurred to them. However, Kent had yet to unleash his main attack.</p>
<p>’I learned this from a sea dragon during my time in the Damned Hell Sea.’</p>
<p>Kent took a nosedive and landed on the ground, his dragon eyes glowing a deep molten hue as his claws sank into the ground. He could feel the pulsing energy of magma beneath the battlefield.</p>
<p>This made him happy and also very evil as his colossal eyes scanned the two angel commanders.</p>
<p>’So much fire.’</p>
<p>Drawing upon it, he inhaled sharply, and a fiery glow surged into his throat. He was channelling the fire from the ground, allowing him not only to enhance his own flame but also to add a small amount to it.</p>
<p>"Burn!"</p>
<p>A torrent of molten flame burst from his jaws, swallowing the dust storm and turning it into a blazing inferno.</p>
<p>At first, nothing unusual happened, so the two Angel commanders disregarded Kent’s attack. However, soon, it became clear what kind of danger they were facing.</p>
<p>Their barriers crackled under the assault, sparks flying as the heat tore through their defence and swallowed them in a raging storm of fire.</p>
<p>"Soul attack... retreat!"</p>
<p>One of them shouted, running back while pulling his teammate.</p>
<p>However, how could Kent allow him to go just like that...</p>
<p>Before he could escape, Kent’s tail whipped across the ground like a thunderclap, slamming into his side.</p>
<p>The impact shattered bones and sent him spiralling through the flames. The other angel, who was being pulled back, fell back into the inferno storm.</p>
<p>"Next time, save yourself first before thinking about others... punk."</p>
<p>The second angel commander, who had fallen back into the flames, soared upward, his sword radiating golden light.</p>
<p>"Divine Sword: Descent of Judgment!" he roared, and the sky above split open.</p>
<p>A massive golden sword about 400 meters long, formed from condensed divine energy, descended toward Kent’s head like an arrow fired by a giant.</p>
<p>Kent, of course, knew this attack wasn’t something he could brush off easily. After all, he could sense some divine essence hidden within it.</p>
<p>Though small, as someone who had a god in his harem, he didn’t miss that.</p>
<p>’Cheaters...’</p>
<p>He unfurled his wings wide, their metallic edges gleaming like forged blades. Kent planned to take the divine sword head-on.</p>
<p>With a mighty flap, he ascended to meet the attack head-on.</p>
<p>"Break"</p>
<p>His wings slashed upward, tearing through the golden sword with a screeching sound that echoed through the battlefield. The impact pushed him back, but he stabilised his body.</p>
<p>However, from the shattered sword, shards rained down like broken stars.</p>
<p>"Teleport."</p>
<p>Kent’s colossal body teleported just when the other angel commander saw an opening after the divine sword attack.</p>
<p>Kent may not have as many minds as Paragon, but he could anticipate his opponent’s move, and that told him all he had to know.</p>
<p>Kent appeared above the angel who had unleashed the divine sword. Before he could react, Kent’s claws glowed red-hot, molten scales pulsing with destructive heat.</p>
<p>"Crimson Rend!"</p>
<p>Kent saw this moment as the best time to go for his fifth Nirvana warrior kill, and he took it.</p>
<p>He plunged downward, slashing with both claws. The angel, caught off guard, could only raise his sword and brace for impact.</p>
<p>The air cracked, and two massive arcs of red energy carved through the sky, colliding with the angel’s chest.</p>
<p>The explosion that followed drowned out all other sounds.</p>
<p>The second angel commander’s scream was drowned out as well. He realised he had been played, and that pained him deep in his heart.</p>
<p>However, he realised this was war, and their opponent had proven time and time again to be someone who couldn’t be underestimated.</p>
<p>So all he could do was scream his heart out.</p>
<p>When the flames cleared, the angel commander’s armour was gone, his body trembling, wings reduced to ash. He fell from the sky like a broken doll and slammed hard into the ground.</p>
<p>Kent hovered above, his scales glowing faintly from the heat of his own attack. His breathing was steady, his now crimson eyes thanks to the nirvana flame he used, holding a mocking expression as he stared at the last remaining angel commander.</p>
<p>"Who knew killing angels would be this easy?"</p>
<p>The comment struck at the poor angel’s heart, but before he could move, doom shards erupted from the ground and drove through the burnt angel’s head.</p>
<p>"Oops."</p>
