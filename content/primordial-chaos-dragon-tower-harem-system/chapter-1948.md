<p>"My name is Emmet, and I am here at the command of the Blood Spear King to aid you and your teammates."</p>
<p>Emmet said the exact words Kent told her to say once she arrived on the battlefield. As expected, the moment Vexthra heard her mention the Blood Spear King, she smiled.</p>
<p>"So Kent sent you, huh? Good timing then. We are getting swarmed by these bugs," Vexthra gestured at the chaotic battlefield and said, "You can help lighten our load by killing them all."</p>
<p>Emmet nodded, and then she transformed into her dragon form and roared into the sky. Then she moved, leaving streaks of lightning in her wake.</p>
<p>Those lightning bolts soon transformed into thousands of clones, and they all moved in synchrony, arriving at different parts of the battlefield.</p>
<p>In no time, everywhere was covered in lightning dragons, and the massacre Emmet had started soon spread all over the battlefield. Blood filled the battlefield, with thousands dying every few seconds.</p>
<p>Vexthra took her dear time to look at this massacre and just smiled... "Looks like Kent really is a magnet for powerful beings."</p>
<p>With a smile on her face, she cast her gaze wide and looked at the current state of the battle. Seeing the latest development, her smile widened.</p>
<p>"Who would ever have thought the addition of a single warrior could tilt the state of the battle this much?"</p>
<p>Of course, Emmet had changed the state of the battle by proving she was not to be underestimated. However, with the likes of Princess Dominique and Neveross added to the mix, the battle was already decided.</p>
<p>But even so, she had lost close to 500 warriors, the majority of them coming from the Beast, Eternal Wrath, and Soul Sentinels.</p>
<p>Aside from them, some of her sisters had also died... temporarily.</p>
<p>After Elina fell, Val followed, and then Mara, Camilla, and Lilian. Of course, before they died, they had each done amazing work. In fact, they had done even more than they did during the Dark War.</p>
<p>As for the Monarchs, after Princess Dominique and Neveross came out, the last three hadn’t shown up yet, meaning they were still alive.</p>
<p>But this was fine. After all, out of the 880,000 Grim Arcane Legends, 550,000 had already died, and more kept dying by the second.</p>
<p>It didn’t take long for Vexthra to finally breathe a sigh of relief.</p>
<p>The state of the battle had now tilted in their favour for good.</p>
<p>CRACK</p>
<p>Just then, the void cracked, and Lord Marcus came flying out, his body littered with burn marks, deep cuts, and poison buds ready to pop and release poison into the air.</p>
<p>Right after he appeared, Meiko walked out of the void, holding the Nine Dragon Whip. Her eyes were completely red, and the aura of death could be sensed from her very being.</p>
<p>Aside from that, her cultivation had declined from the Pseudo-Primordial stage to the Celestial King stage, and looking at her now, it was clear she wasn’t done, which also meant her cultivation would only continue to decline.</p>
<p>Lord Marcus seemed to be at death’s door... no, he was indeed at death’s door. As she walked toward him, one could tell by the look on his face that he was terrified of Meiko.</p>
<p>That alone is more shocking than his injuries.</p>
<p>For a primordial to be afraid of someone who had only recently reached the Pseudo-Primordial stage, that is just shocking.</p>
<p>"S-Stay back," he said, dragging his ass on the ground as he moved back, hoping to create some distance between himself and Meiko. However, after moving a few steps, his back struck something.</p>
<p>Turning his head, his wounded body trembling, his gaze landed on Ruby, who had a cold smile on her face. "Where do you think you are going?"</p>
<p>Lord Marcus’ body trembled, but before he could move, Meiko closed the gap between them. Her gaze remained locked on him, and just as she appeared before him, her Nine Dragon Whip moved, and all nine razor-sharp talons stabbed into his chest.</p>
<p>The Nine Dragon Whip is just a single whip; however, with nine dragon souls inside its core, Meiko can easily transform it into nine deadly whips, each holding a single dragon soul.</p>
<p>Lord Marcus screamed as his entire body was suddenly engulfed in flames.</p>
<p>However, within that fire, there was ice that seemed to bite at his heart, lightning that kept sending jolts into his soul, and darkness that had already invaded his mind and wormed its way deep into his subconscious.</p>
<p>All the other elements found something bad to do to him, each causing him to scream. However, Meiko wasn’t satisfied. Her body surged with energy, and reaching forward, she grabbed Lord Marcus’s left arm and tore it off.</p>
<p>Then she grabbed his right arm and tore it off, too. Afterwards, she did the same to his legs, and when she was done, she summoned a dagger and stabbed him in his belly.</p>
<p>Just then, Eliza appeared behind her and grabbed her arms. "That’s enough, Meiko. He is already done. No need to dirty yourself anymore with his blood."</p>
<p>Meiko tried to wiggle her way out of Eliza’s grip. However, Neveross appeared and knocked her out... "She has been taken over by her bloodlust. If we let her continue, there is a chance she will never recover and will be forever lost to that bloodlust."</p>
<p>The ladies nodded at Neveross’s words, and Vexthra looked at Unity, who had also appeared on the scene. "Take her away and make sure she is tucked in. You can stay with her until we return."</p>
<p>Unity understood how important her mission was, so she teleported away, appearing somewhere in the Harem Tower. She immediately stripped off Meiko’s armor and sent her to the bathroom.</p>
<p>Meanwhile, on the battlefield, the ladies gathered around Lord Marcus, who, at that moment, finally understood that he was fucked. Unfortunately for him, his pain hadn’t started yet...</p>
<p>The ladies had more plans for him, and so, to keep him in suspense for now, they let Neveross extract his soul and seal it inside his soul cauldron. Once everything was settled, they would deal with him severely.</p>
<p>In no time, the battle slowly started to draw to an end.</p>
<p>Lord Marcus had already been dealt with by Meiko, who was so pissed that she had to burn her cultivation base just to match his power and even surpass him.</p>
<p>However, just before the last of the Grim Arcane Legends could be dealt with, the void cracked again, and Sect Master Zhai walked out holding Lord Weyori’s head.</p>
