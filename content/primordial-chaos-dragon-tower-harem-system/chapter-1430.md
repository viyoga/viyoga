<p>While Kenna was happily hugging him, Kent dove into her profile.</p>
<p>Just like Calix, her race changed from Void Beast to Soul Demon. However, unlike how Calix only became a Soul Demon, there was a slight change to Kenna’s race.</p>
<p>It turned to [Soul Void Demon].</p>
<p>This gave her both the qualities of a Void Beast and a Soul Demon.</p>
<p>She also came with a Reaper class. However, while she can operate on the soul level, her greatest strength will show itself when void battles are put into focus.</p>
<p>It leans more toward void manipulation on the soul level.</p>
<p>Job Class: [Void Reaper] ↠ Description: With untamed control over the void, the soul warrior can harness the overwhelming power of the void to her advantage. The deeper her understanding of the void, the stronger her control.</p>
<p>The description did justice to most of what Kent needed to know. However, with how much he knew when it came to the Void, there were many avenues to consider.</p>
<p>From the little he learned from Axiom, there are variations of the void. There are the physical, astral, and mental versions of the void.</p>
<p>The physical aspect involved using the void as a medium to move much faster in the physical plane. That happened to be Axiom’s field, so he gave a good explanation, which helped Kent understand quite a lot.</p>
<p>Thanks to that knowledge, he knew Kenna’s version was not the physical aspect of the void.</p>
<p>This means she is on the Astral Void path.</p>
<p>And if that is the case, then she can be considered the perfect assassin. If she can command Astral Void essence, then no soul will be safe from her reach.</p>
<p>While it is hard to hurt the soul, considering soul attacks are a two-way street. If a soul attack backfires, it returns to the attacker, making them experience what they intended for their opponent.</p>
<p>But once they reach a certain level, one can easily defeat their opponent with soul attacks.</p>
<p>However, over the years, soul items had been repurposed into soul defense artefacts. Most of these items can even take on an attack from a god.</p>
<p>In fact, during the Era of the Soul Sentinel, she had crafted many soul items that could take on even a soul attack from a Supreme.</p>
<p>So if someone like Calix were to meet someone with such an item, he would lose, and there is nothing anyone can do about it—unless they have a Kenna.</p>
<p>Kenna’s void power had to do with the soul, so against someone who has a soul defense item, she can use the void to bypass it, or even if she cannot bypass it and attack directly, she can deal powerful damage to the item.</p>
<p>That makes her the perfect battle partner Calix could ask for. She will open a path for him to attack. So regardless of the opponent she meets, with enough control, she can kill them.</p>
<p>Kent moved ahead to the Affinity section.</p>
<p>Affinities: [Void Affinity] and [Soul Affinity]</p>
<p>The [Void Affinity] had to do with her control over the void, while her [Soul Affinity] tackled the soul. While she had only two affinities, compared to Calix’s three, they were perfect for her.</p>
<p>The [Soul Affinity] will come into play when she manages to bypass the defense of a soul.</p>
<p>As for her Aspects, she came with three powerful ones.</p>
<p>Aspects: [Astral Severance], [Void Infiltrator], and [Reaper’s Silence]</p>
<p>[Astral Severance]: Has the ability to see and sever connections within the soul. As long as she can see these connections, she can sever them.</p>
<p>"Hmm, that is a bit over the top, don’t you think?" Kent said, turning to look at Kenna, who was cuddled in his arms. "Your Astral Severance Aspect is a bit overpowered."</p>
<p>"I know, right. It is so cool." Kenna smiled happily.</p>
<p>"Way cooler than Calix’s," Kent decided to make her even happier, since she seemed to be in the mood already.</p>
<p>"Care to throw more light on it?"</p>
<p>"Sure. With the Astral Severance Aspect, I can see all connections in the soul and sever them completely. This means I can sever connections such as soul bonds, contracts, regeneration threads, artifact bindings, and others.</p>
<p>So if my opponent has a soul weapon, the moment I see that soul thread, cutting their connection to their weapon is as simple as swinging my dagger.</p>
<p>Soul contracts to beasts, slaves, and many others can be severed. Anything with a connection to her soul can be severed easily."</p>
<p>"So if I were to meet a beast tamer, with you around, the moment you find the threads binding his tamed beasts to him and sever them, he would lose all connection to them."</p>
<p>"Yes. Once cut, there will be no way to reattach them again."</p>
<p>"Damn. That is awesome." Kent was clearly impressed.</p>
<p>"What about the other two?"</p>
<p>"The [Void Infiltrator] Aspect allows me to infiltrate the soul through the void, while the [Reaper’s Silence] Aspect makes it so my infiltration is as silent as possible."</p>
<p>"So in other words, you can enter my soul without me knowing."</p>
<p>"Yes. However, I will never enter Master’s soul if he doesn’t want me to," Kenna said with a cute pout.</p>
<p>"Tch, as if you haven’t already tried."</p>
<p>Kenna blushed. "I just wanted to see if my abilities work. Safe to say they work, and I also had the scare of my life when I infiltrated your soul. Also, how did you know I infiltrated your soul?"</p>
<p>"I didn’t at first. However, when you saw the [Underworld Soul River], you became scared and lost focus, which caused your aura to leak. That was how I learned it was you."</p>
<p>"That gate of yours is scary, Master. I would never enter your soul sea again. Also, it wasn’t like I could hurt you. You are my Master, and it seems you have the Seal of the Soul Dao, so unless I reach Tier 30, I can’t break past that seal.</p>
<p>By the way, who gave you that seal? If I can become friends with them, we can collaborate and create powerful soul attacks."</p>
<p>"I will introduce you later. But first, how about you tell me your Innate Abilities?"</p>
<p>She has two passive Innate Abilities and one active Innate Ability. The passive ones are [Void Phase Instinct] and [Astral Marking].</p>
<p>The [Void Phase Instinct] allows her to always be within the Astral Plane and the Void Plane. In that state, physical attacks won’t work on her.</p>
<p>For the [Astral Marking], she can instinctively mark a soul once she senses it. The mark reveals connection points between the soul and the artifact. This allows her to know exactly where to sever.</p>
<p>As for the active Innate Ability, [Void Corrosion], it allows her to use her void energy to corrode soul constructs.</p>
<p>This means that even if she can’t sever a soul thread, just by leaving her mark, she can use her void energy to corrode things like soul shields, soul puppets, defensive soul arrays, binding contracts, divine soul chains, and more.</p>
<p>Basically, she has the means to weaken the soul, making it easy for her to destroy it. It is the best possible combination a person can have.</p>
<p>"You are perfect, Kenna. We are going to have so much fun together."</p>
<p>Kenna nodded. She pulled back and opened her palm, and seven triangular-shaped crystals appeared.</p>
<p>"These are the soul crystals I formed. Since you can’t expand your soul yet, you can use them to expand the soul universe or give them to the people linked to your soul. I can see that the stronger they grow, the stronger you also become."</p>
