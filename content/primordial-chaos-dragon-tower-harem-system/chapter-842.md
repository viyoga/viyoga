<p>"I don’t take kindly to those who attack without a reason. Be sure never to repeat that mistake ever again."</p>
<p>The look on everyone’s face changed.</p>
<p>"Did Kent just threaten the Venom King?"</p>
<p>"Yes, he sure did, and I think he has a death wish."</p>
<p>"He is dead. There is no denying that."</p>
<p>Xhan narrowed his gaze at Kent for a few seconds before a green mist started spreading toward Kent.</p>
<p>"He had activated the poison mist ability... Kent is done for."</p>
<p>"He deserves it. Who does he think he is to threaten a core disciple? Not even the sect elders will do that."</p>
<p>Kent pushed Nyss away, making sure she was away from the poison mist. Then a grin appeared on his face.</p>
<p>"First question. What at all do you wish to achieve from doing this?" Kent asked, then he raised two fingers.</p>
<p>"And what is your plan to begin with? Do you think a little poison can harm me?</p>
<p>Lastly, aren’t you scared I will disgrace you in front of all your shameless fans?"</p>
<p>All the questions were ones Xhan ignored, for he was looking forward to seeing what Kent had under his sleeves.</p>
<p>So far, he had heard four shocking things about Kent, and each one pissed him off.</p>
<p>The first was Kent learning 12 Divine grade techniques. That was like a slap to his face, for had it not been for the sect, he wouldn’t have even had the chance to see what a divine-grade technique book looks like before learning one.</p>
<p>So, Kent learning 12 was something he didn’t take lightly. All five seeded core disciples shared this sentiment.</p>
<p>Then there was the part where Kent challenged the entire outer and inner sect and won, as if it were nothing. He even gained the recognition of Lady Enzi and Old Sword, two of the influential people in the sect.</p>
<p>But if that wasn’t enough, Kent even gained the chance to ask for Lady Enzi’s help twice, and will also experience Old Sword’s sword realm.</p>
<p>Though Xhan isn’t a true swordsman, he uses the sword and knows how the sword realm affects one’s understanding of the sword and combat power.</p>
<p>Having the chance to experience it was just too good for someone who had joined the sect less than 3 months ago.</p>
<p>Lastly, Kent was added to the selection trial for the interplanetary trial. That was what infuriated him to the core, prompting him to use the excuse of visiting his sister to test Kent.</p>
<p>He wants to disgrace him and show everyone Kent is a nobody.</p>
<p>However, the pan flipped on him, for the brat was not fazed at his provocation.</p>
<p>"Looks like you are still in the delusion that you are the top in this sect. That might be true to them, but not me." Kent smiled and took a deep breath, sucking the poison into his lungs.</p>
<p>Everyone left their jaws hanging when they saw that. Even Xhan widened his eyes. While many would try not to breathe in the toxin, Kent did so willingly.</p>
<p>"Your poison is nothing but air in front of me. I know a true Poison Queen, one whose aura can kill you a thousand times over. So if you came here thinking you can do anything with your poison, then please turn around and leave.</p>
<p>However, if you stay and try to do anything stupid, I will be forced to do what you wouldn’t want, which is to show the entire sect their best and brightest is beneath me, a mere level 4 Core Formation Inner disciple."</p>
<p>"Big brother Kent, kick his ass. Maybe that will knock some sense into his head," Nyss said from the side, causing her brother to glare at her, but she remained unfazed.</p>
<p>"Big brother Xhan used to be a prodigy until he joined the sect and defeated all the top geniuses, then he started to think he was undefeated.</p>
<p>However, I am sure with a few poison resistance pills, I will be able to wipe the floor with him."</p>
<p>Kent didn’t expect that, so the mysterious and angry character he put on cracked, causing him to burst out laughing.</p>
<p>"Nyss, dear, while I would love to see you wipe the floor with your brother, know that his mastery over poison is almost at the Super stage, which means not even Tier 9 poison resistance pills can help you.</p>
<p>But even if they did, I can see he had already broken two limits, unlike you, with only one limit broken. Aside from that, his foundation has been reformed three times already, whereas yours has only been reformed once.</p>
<p>So, strength-wise, he will shatter every bone in your body; speed-wise, he also surpasses you, thanks to his ability to move through the poison mist. He is more impressive than you in every aspect.</p>
<p>But don’t worry, when it comes to good looks, you surpass him, and your mouth is also louder than his, so perhaps you might be able to win when it comes to verbal battle."</p>
<p>Kent smiled and winked at Xhan, whose face hadn’t turned pale, but the look on his face was pale.</p>
<p>⟦Master, if the cultivation gig doesn’t work out, you can try your hand at becoming a lawyer. I am sure with just your words alone, you can keep criminals out of jail.⟧</p>
<p>’Whose fault is it?’</p>
<p>⟦Well, I grow stronger and more intelligent as you grow, so perhaps you should give yourself the credit, master.⟧</p>
<p>’I suppose I am impressive.’</p>
<p>After Kent visited the sword Dao and inherited its legacy, his perception had sharpened. While that was thanks to his sword gaze, it had also affected all his abilities that involved his eyes.</p>
<p>Now, his Enchanted eyes can see more than they used to.</p>
<p>At first, he couldn’t see how many limits one had broken or how many times they’d rebuilt their foundation.</p>
<p>But now he does, and that alone was enough for him to know more about his opponent before the battle even began.</p>
<p>"Big brother Kent, you are too cruel, but thanks for complimenting my good looks. I indeed have good genes, unlike this silver-skinned bastard brother of mine."</p>
<p>Kent smirked and focused on Xhan. "What you came for today won’t pan out well for you, so take my advice and leave.</p>
<p>The only reason I’m not attacking you now is that we’ll soon be teammates, and since we both want to win for the sect, let’s not become enemies.</p>
<p>That would be detrimental to our team and could lower our chances of winning. That said, if you still want to fight me, then we can take it to the arena."</p>
<p>Kent moved and appeared beside him and whispered into his ear, "You are close to breaking the next limit. Use your time training instead of coming after me.</p>
<p>Trust me, being my enemy is just stupid. If I were you, I would become friends with me instead."</p>
<p>With that, Kent moved away.</p>
<p>Nyss also moved but stopped beside her brother. "Not so tough now. Take my advice and focus on your training instead of looking for trouble with big brother Kent.</p>
<p>I might not have the means to beat you now, but one day, I will surpass you and wipe the floor with you."</p>
<p>With her chin high and shoulders held high like a kangaroo, she left.</p>
<p>Kent, who heard her words, just smiled, shaking his head.</p>
<p>Inwardly, he sent a message to Princess Lovina...</p>
<p>[My love, guess what, I found someone with a poison affinity. You can perform your experiment on him when you are done with your training.]</p>
<p>Somewhere in Fedora, Princess Lovina smiled before turning a hellhound into a carcass, draining all life from it.</p>
