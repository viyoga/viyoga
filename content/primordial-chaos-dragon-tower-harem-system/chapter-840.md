<p>⟦Congratulations, you have successfully formed three sword foundations. Defeat all 13 levels of the bridge to become a sword Saint⟧.</p>
<p>The first message was already enough for Kent to know he had taken a massive leap in his sword path. Forming the foundation of the three sword techniques is something that may sound simple, but it has a much deeper meaning.</p>
<p>To have the foundation is to be able to build upon that in the years to come.</p>
<p>Kent had formed three foundations, each a piece of art.</p>
<p>It was something he never expected to happen to him so suddenly, but it did, and now, he has not only gained a slot to view the sword realm of Old Sword, but he can now use that knowledge to start building the various forms of his sword technique.</p>
<p>His initial plans before meeting the Sword Dao again were to use the knowledge he would gain from Old Sword’s Sword Realm to build the second foundation.</p>
<p>But things are different now. The knowledge will likely be applied to something else.</p>
<p>Kent focused on his body and examined himself.</p>
<p>"I can sense my senses sharpened, my sword intent, and aura had both reached level 3. This is just too good. With this, facing Sword Sages and Kings wouldn’t be a problem."</p>
<p>"Thank you, Sword Dao." His smile bloomed.</p>
<p>He had a blast with the sword Dao, who Kent is sure would be cursing at him at the moment. Not only did he get the legacy of the True Sword Path, but he even extorted him.</p>
<p>That was just too daring.</p>
<p>However, his gamble paid off, and Kent knew in the long run, he would benefit more if he played his cards right.</p>
<p>"With the complete roadmap of the first form of Sword Dao inside my Sword Mind, I can practise it any day. Once I fully master Edge, I will see you again, Mr. Sword Dao."</p>
<p>Kent stood up and walked toward the stone, which now bore no markings.</p>
<p>But it wasn’t just there that all markings vanished. Out there in the universe, all markings relating to the sword Dao’s legacy were wiped, making Kent the only one to take on this legacy.</p>
<p>If he dies before accomplishing reaching the peak, the fourteen marks will reappear and choose a different person.</p>
<p>"I knew picking this mountain was a fortune and not a complete waste of time at all," Kent smiled and then walked away from the stone.</p>
<p>However, he only took a few steps when Lady Enzi appeared before him like a shattered glass. Following her is Princess Elowen, who, from the look of things, had undergone a makeover.</p>
<p>"Did you do something new to your hair?"</p>
<p>Kent asked, ignoring the glass lady whose gaze was focused on him.</p>
<p>Princess Elowen blushed but didn’t say anything, instead standing obediently behind her master, showing she had taken her side. Kent chuckled and looked at the glass lady.</p>
<p>"Lady Enzi, I didn’t know you had a twin sister. I could have sworn she said she wouldn’t give me any of her attention ever again, now that she has a disciple."</p>
<p>The smile at the end of the sentence made Lady Enzi grit her teeth, but in the end, she didn’t do anything to Kent. She knew he was messing with her.</p>
<p>"Are you alright?" She asked</p>
<p>"I am fine."</p>
<p>"Pack up, you are leaving this place." There was no suggestion in her tone. She spoke these words with authority, one that anyone other than Kent would have obeyed without question.</p>
<p>However, the brat didn’t only smile, he waved his hand, "I will be staying here, Lady Enzi. It is quiet and remote, I want the peace."</p>
<p>"I am not asking." Her voice rose a bit like a big sister speaking to her little brother, "It’s either you leave with me now in a quiet way, or I destroy the mountain and you leave with me. The choice is yours."</p>
<p>"Lead the way, big sister."</p>
<p>Kent smiles, knowing that if he wants to keep coming here to stay away from prying eyes, he cannot let the woman destroy the mountain.</p>
<p>"By the way, where are we going?"</p>
<p>To answer his question, the shattered portal opened, and he was tossed in. The next second, he appeared atop a small floating island with a grand mansion sitting on it.</p>
<p>It looks elegant.</p>
<p>"This is my dwelling. From today on, you will stay here. Since you don’t want to join the other cultivation peaks, you can stay here and still represent your peak if you want."</p>
<p>Kent looked around, and he could tell that staying here wouldn’t be too bad,</p>
<p>"Dont worry, I dont have time to spy on your affairs."</p>
<p>Kent smiled, "I wasn’t complaining."</p>
<p>"You can use this flying treasure to move from here to the sect. Without it, you wouldn’t be able to move through the formations."</p>
<p>Kent received the miniature flying ship and stored it inside his tower storage.</p>
<p>Though he had yet to see around the sect, he planned to do that before focusing on other things.</p>
<p>Lady Enzi left with Princess Elowen, who had started her training on becoming the next glass lady.</p>
<p>Long ago, when Lady Enzi received the Glass Cultivation manual from an unknown person, it came in two forms: the Earth Glass Path and the Cold Flame Path.</p>
<p>Lady Enzi has cultivated the Earth Glass Path, and now, she wants Princess Elowen to cultivate the Cold Flame Path.</p>
<p>It was her way of fulfilling the wishes of the mysterious person who had given her the technique.</p>
<p>It would take some time and a lot of hard work, but eventually, she would be able to make it, and when that day came, she would become an unstoppable force in the universe...both of them.</p>
<p>After they left, Kent chose to look around the floating island for a few hours before using the flying boat to speed off toward the sect where he planned to see what the sect life is all about."</p>
