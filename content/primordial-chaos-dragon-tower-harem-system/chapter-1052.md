<p>"This portal will take you to Planet Nictus after I enter the star coordinates," Daniel said and started entering the unique star coordinates for Planet Nictus.</p>
<p>It may be a non-technological world, but they had long developed superior devices that can do what most modern technologically advanced worlds like Earth can’t.</p>
<p>It is all about studying the stars and knowing how to build arrays and formations.</p>
<p>After he was done, Daniel looked at a few runes that appeared.</p>
<p>"You will have to pay 700 million High-Grade Spirit stones."</p>
<p>Kent didn’t hesitate and took out the card he received from the Brown Maple Auction House. Daniel also brought out a purple jade crystal, and Kent tapped the card against it.</p>
<p>In no time, the spirit stones were transferred, and Kent was ready to leave...</p>
<p>"Thank you, Daniel," Kent said, and Daniel nodded with a smile.</p>
<p>"Good luck, young master."</p>
<p>Kent stepped into the portal and was gone.</p>
<p>He traveled through the void at a speed even he couldn’t track or move, even if he were to use 135,000 flame veins.</p>
<p>However, instead of shattering, a bubble made out of cosmic energy enveloped him, preventing him from feeling the pressure.</p>
<p>Five minutes later, inside a certain room on Planet Nictus, a portal opened and Kent stepped out. Right then, he saw someone standing not far from him...</p>
<p>"Welcome to Planet Nictus..."</p>
<p>"Thank you."</p>
<p>"Are you planning on staying in this city, or do you have another place in mind?" the man asked with a smile.</p>
<p>"I am planning to head to Jin City."</p>
<p>The smile on his face vanished when he heard the mention of Jin City...</p>
<p>"Is anything wrong?" Kent asked, and the man nodded.</p>
<p>"Words reaching us now are that the notorious Planet King attacked Jin City and kidnapped the sect leader of the Heavens Eye Sect, along with her mother and Commander Agnes.</p>
<p>Rumor says he plans to make them his wives in five days...</p>
<p>The army had been deployed there, but by the time they reached there, he was gone. The strange part is that during the attack, three other cities were attacked and, like in Jin City, some ladies were kidnapped.</p>
<p>I will advise you to stay away from Jin City for a few days... at least until after the army pulls back."</p>
<p>"I am afraid I can’t do that. My family is in Jin City, so I can’t stay away."</p>
<p>The man sighed. "I understand. Family is important."</p>
<p>"Indeed," Kent smiled, and the man reciprocated his smile.</p>
<p>"In that case, I will send you to Jin City free of charge."</p>
<p>"Thank you."</p>
<p>They walked out of the room, and soon, the teleportation gate opened and Kent’s body vanished.</p>
<p>The moment he appeared in Jin City, he could sense the dull atmosphere. It felt like a fog of death was hovering over them.</p>
<p>Kent didn’t mind anyone. He teleported away, and soon he was only a few kilometers from the Heavens Eye Sect; however, his soul map already covered the entire sect.</p>
<p>Deep inside the core region, he saw dozens of Heavenly Ascension Stage warriors pointing their weapons at the beasts he had left to protect the sect and his lovers.</p>
<p>A lady whose aura was in no way weaker than the Monarchs he had tamed was questioning them. He could even see Yu Lan standing at the back, her gaze cold.</p>
<p>"Where is General Agnes, the sect master, and her mother?" the lady asked, her expression dark.</p>
<p>None of the beasts even bothered to answer her. This alone only added to the anger she was in. She wanted to find General Agnes and rescue her.</p>
<p>She was her priority... however, as it stands, aside from suspecting the beasts, she had no proof they were involved.</p>
<p>But even if they were, she knew taking them in was going to be very hard. After all, she could sense the aura around them, and she knew, aside from herself and a few others, taking on these beasts would be next to impossible.</p>
<p>This was also the main reason she hadn’t gone ballistic yet. However, seeing that the beasts had no intention of answering her began to wear away her patience.</p>
<p>"I will only ask one more time... if you fail to answer me... I will have no reason but to brand you as criminals and have you executed."</p>
<p>"I am afraid you can’t do that." Kent appeared a few meters from her and began walking toward the beasts. When they saw him, their expressions brightened.</p>
<p>"Who are you?" General Brianna asked, wearing a frown.</p>
<p>"I am Kent. Nice meeting you."</p>
<p>Kent stopped beside the beasts and turned toward General Brianna.</p>
<p>"You are Kent... The Kent?"</p>
<p>"Have we met?" Kent smiled but didn’t go any further than that...</p>
<p>"No. But I believe Commander Agnes met you before."</p>
<p>Kent nodded. "I see. Well then, I am leaving now... I have a Planet King to kill, and time waits for no man."</p>
<p>General Brianna’s expression changed. She wanted to say something; however, recalling what Commander Agnes had told her, she held herself back despite Kent’s plain disrespect.</p>
<p>However, the other Generals besides her aren’t aware of the danger they are staring at...</p>
<p>"Who the hell do you think you are, and what makes you think you can just come and go? Are you looking to die?"</p>
<p>Kent turned and looked at the idiot who was planning to throw his life away. "I don’t plan on dying anytime soon, so no... However, you’d better watch where you point that stick, else you wouldn’t know how you died."</p>
<p>Kent waved his hand, and the General’s body was hurled through the air, crashing into a wall 2 km away.</p>
<p>Kent ignored the shock on everyone’s face and turned to the beasts... "We are going to war. Are you all ready?"</p>
<p>"We are ready, Lord Chaos."</p>
<p>"Very good." Kent turned back to the shocked General Brianna and smiled. "I need a ship."</p>
<p>"Huh?"</p>
<p>"You know... one of those things that flies through the air and can travel between planets."</p>
<p>"I know what a ship is, but why do you need one?"</p>
<p>"I can’t exactly walk to Planet Seamus Prime now, can I?" Kent smirked, thinking this conversation was pointless.</p>
<p>"You aren’t seriously considering going there, are you? The Planet King is marrying his wives in 5 days, meaning all his lackeys will be there. Naturally, that means going there is suicide."</p>
<p>"Even better. They will all be in the same place, which means I can kill all of them once and for all."</p>
<p>Everyone present frowned, watching as Kent treated this so casually. It was as if he didn’t even place the Planet King in his eyes. He wasn’t even affected in any way...</p>
<p>"Look. The longer we wait, the more hold that bastard has on my future wives. So do me a favor and lend me a ship..."</p>
<p>Kent walked closer to her and whispered into her ear...</p>
<p>"Give me a ship and I will return your princess to you safe, sound, and still a maiden. Delay, and it is not only going to be the Planet King who will feel my wrath, but the entirety of this planetary system."</p>
<p>General Brianna’s body shuddered, fear gripping her.</p>
<p>Kent waved his hand, and all the beasts except the ones contracted to the three ladies were left standing.</p>
<p>He turned to Yu Lan and asked a question...</p>
<p>"Is she okay?"</p>
<p>"Yes. Sister Agnes is fine... however, she is afraid. They all are"</p>
<p>"Don’t worry, we will rescue them soon."</p>
<p>Kent turned back to General Brianna... and furrowed his brow.</p>
