<p>"So it has been 47 years since I entered seclusion, and everyone has turned into a freak." Kent laughed, shaking his head.</p>
<p>⟪I already told you the day you awakened the tower that it is something beyond this universe. Anyone affiliated with it is destined to rise and become someone worthy of respect.⟫ Gaia said with a subtle pride oozing...</p>
<p>But she wasn’t wrong. Every single person linked to the tower seems to be growing faster than they would have otherwise.</p>
<p>The humans inside the tower are already reaching the peak of the Core Formation stage, with the weakest being at the Transcendent stage.</p>
<p>On the outside, even a Transcendent is worthy of respect. It demonstrates the tower’s effectiveness and highlights its benefits for Kent.</p>
<p>The stronger they become, the more Points and spiritual qi they can give Kent. He has already reached the Level 7 Sovereign stage.</p>
<p>Although this may seem slow, as someone with such a powerful bloodline and having maximized twice, He needs some time to rise. But even with that, he had reached Level 7.</p>
<p>⟪As for the Bloodvein tribe, I’m at a loss for words. The tribe chief, the three elders, and the army general are all at the peak, and each has perfect affinity for Chaos energy.</p>
<p>The moment you rise to a higher world, the tower will upgrade. Once that happens, the Chaos Pool will become much more vitalized, allowing them to rise higher.⟫</p>
<p>Kent smiled. "This all sounds good."</p>
<p>⟪One last thing. Shax, that demon you let go, sent a message. He said that in five months, a chance to enter the Supreme Immortal Sect will appear, so you should make yourself known before then.</p>
<p>He said you can come to the Half-Elf Kingdom; they are much more welcoming to humans.</p>
<p>You have less than ten years inside the tower, so you might want to hurry.⟫</p>
<p>Kent nodded and then stood up. "I need less than a year to catch up to the Pill Princess." His arrogance was spoken out of confidence. With his soul strength, even a Pill Emperor would bow before him.</p>
<p>"I will be inside the Alchemy Tower. Let me know if you need anything or if something urgent comes up." Kent vanished and appeared directly inside the Pill Room.</p>
<p>He waved his hand, and hundreds of ingredients fell on a large table.</p>
<p>"If not for the requirements, I would have ignored the Tier 5 pills and gone straight to Tier 6. But at last, as an alchemist, I must follow the stages regardless of how easy they are." Kent sighed and then pulled a scroll from the tower space, spreading it open.</p>
<p>"I need to concoct ten Tier 5 pills and one each for Tier 6 and 7. Then gain two enlightenments in the pill path. However, my opponent has completed five enlightenments and she is even a Pill Saint.</p>
<p>This means she is superior to me in every way. However, I know a way to gain enlightenment easily..." Kent smiled.</p>
<p>"Getting the first three won’t be an issue." He picked the pills he would concoct and then decided to get to work.</p>
<p>Of course, the reason he was confident he would gain enlightenment was because the Pill Dao favored him—no, it was because of his second pill flame, ’Purifying Flame,’ and the purifying technique he had inherited from the pill records.</p>
<p>"First off, Foundation Building Pill," Kent picked the classic first. "I will try Tier 4 first."</p>
<p>Twenty minutes later, a smile appeared on his lips.</p>
<p>⟦You have successfully concocted a Foundation Building Pill. Grade: Tier 4, Quality: Perfect. Purity: 100%.⟧</p>
<p>"Easy enough. Then why don’t I try Tier 5?" Since he was rich, he bought the herbs in abundance, knowing he would need them all. Ordinary alchemists wouldn’t have been able to afford such wealth, but Kent is loaded.</p>
<p>Thirty minutes later, he retrieved the pill with a smile playing on his lips.</p>
<p>⟦You have successfully concocted a Foundation Building Pill. Grade: Tier 5, Quality: Perfect. Purity: 100%.⟧</p>
<p>"Looks like I am much more impressive and rather fast and efficient after my soul cultivation reached Tier 13."</p>
<p>He scratched the back of his neck and sighed.</p>
<p>"Now starts the real thing. The records covered up to Tier 7, but with the knowledge I have, I can easily do that. However, Tier 8 will be a headache.</p>
<p>I need to get there quickly because, to gain enlightenment easily, you have to try something beyond your current abilities.</p>
<p>This means using Tier 7 skills to concoct Tier 8 pills is impossible, and as such, I should gain my enlightenment."</p>
<p>He quickly started the Tier 6 pill concoction. Forty-five minutes later, the message appeared, and of course, it was 100% pure. Another message appeared after that.</p>
<p>⟦You have successfully concocted a Foundation Building Pill. Grade: Tier 6, Quality: Perfect. Purity: 100%.⟧</p>
<p>⟦You have met one of the requirements to become a Pill Saint.⟧</p>
<p>"Good. Then Tier 7, then."</p>
<p>An hour later, Kent smiled yet again, seeing the messages.</p>
<p>⟦You have successfully concocted a Foundation Building Pill. Grade: Tier 7, Quality: Perfect. Purity: 100%.⟧</p>
<p>⟦You have met one of the requirements to become a Pill Saint.⟧</p>
<p>These messages served as a reminder that he had already reached a stage in alchemy that he never expected.</p>
<p>Of course, in a way, it can be considered a cheat, but as someone who has the knowledge embedded in his mind, all these are just simple processes for him.</p>
<p>Thanks to his ladies, his mind has now become much more powerful, allowing him to comprehend complex processes.</p>
<p>His mind has become much more robust than one could imagine.</p>
<p>If he possesses the knowledge—specifically the refining techniques for Tier 12 pills—combined with his soul strength, he can easily accomplish it.</p>
<p>The only thing holding him back is the requirement to become a Pill Saint, but his talent far surpasses that of a Pill King. His potential is endless.</p>
<p>"Time to start Tier 8. I have ten years to make my presence known on the other continent. By then, I must reach the level that this Pill Princess will beg me to become her master," Kent burst out laughing.</p>
<p>A few minutes after he had calmed down, he got to work.</p>
