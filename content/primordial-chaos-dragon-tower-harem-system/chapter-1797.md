<p>Since Kent had made up his mind to proceed with assimilating his bloodline, he wasted no time and decided to initiate the process immediately. The faster he gets things done, the sooner he gets to return home to his loves.</p>
<p>After all, he had just discovered he was the Chaos Ascension King.</p>
<p>While he knew nothing about that part of his life, so far, he had seen and heard enough to know he was someone very powerful.</p>
<p>Most importantly, he now had a way to know more about what truly happened leading to the events that kick-started his 13 reincarnations.</p>
<p>The only thing hindering him now was how to open the book containing that information. Everything he needed to know was in the book he had received from Tower. As for how he would open it, Tower only said the book would open on its own.</p>
<p>Kent hated the uncertainty, so he wanted to know everything now and, hopefully, plan ahead for what was to come after everything was over.</p>
<p>’Ahh, this suspense is killing me,’ Kent smirked, then pressed "Yes."</p>
<p>&lt;You have initiated Bloodline Assimilation&gt;</p>
<p>&lt;Blood Calamity Slaughter King Bloodline is merging with the Primordial Chaos Dragon Bloodline&gt;</p>
<p>&lt;You have unlocked a Limitless Grade Bloodline Technique: Blood Sea Annihilation&gt;</p>
<p>He dove into the technique and began to learn it. With the cultivation arts he had learned from the seven tribes, it was about time he learned a worthy martial technique, too.</p>
<p>While the seven techniques include some martial techniques, he needs a Limitless-grade technique meant for martial combat only.</p>
<p>Luckily for him, this technique was terrifying by all standards.</p>
<p>It had no forms or levels like most techniques. Instead, it just required blood, soul, and control.</p>
<p>The blood was the source of the technique. When activated, it would draw out the blood sea inside Kent’s soul sea, causing a vast crimson sea to rise into the air.</p>
<p>Then, with his soul, Kent could imbue any form of energy into it. If he wanted, he could go with Chaos Qi, Abyssal Qi, flame, ice, or just go bananas and imbue all of them, provided his soul could handle it.</p>
<p>Then, if he managed to do that, the rest was control. He could shape the blood sea into just about anything and use it to attack. He could transform it into a large sword, spear, scythe, thousands of swords, millions of arrows... anything at all he could control.</p>
<p>Basically, he could transform it into a domain of blood where, if he mastered any law, he could imbue it, thereby making it his own.</p>
<p>So he could imbue his Genesis Sword Domain, when he awakened it, into the Domain of Blood, causing a much stronger mutation to take place.</p>
<p>"With my classes awakened and now as a Demigod, I can unleash my domains that don’t deal with unique mastery," he smiled, knowing that, aside from his Sword Domain, the rest of the domains he wielded in the past could now be unleashed.</p>
<p>"Merging them with this Blood Sea Annihilation sure would prove catastrophic."</p>
<p>And that was the main reason why anything Kent touched turned into a deadlier version of itself.</p>
<p>He always found a way to bring out the worst version of anything, especially techniques.</p>
<p>What could be considered a tamed and basic form of anything in Kent’s hands became a deadly weapon, one that could claim lives on a much larger scale.</p>
<p>He took a deep breath and began making mental plans for how to use this technique in the upcoming battle. After all, he was going against an opponent who probably had millions of minions under her command.</p>
<p>Be that as it may, he alone could not handle them, and since the Undead Legion and Night Riders were away on a mission when he left, he might only be able to summon the Beast Legion and probably his wives.</p>
<p>For now, he wanted to keep his options open, so he asked Tower to go fetch his sister and the tribe leaders.</p>
<p>Fiannarel was from the Infinity Universe, so suppressing her cultivation base wouldn’t cause too much trouble. However, the others were the outliers.</p>
<p>Tower said he could make them his Blood Warriors—a unique attribute that would activate after completing bloodline assimilation.</p>
<p>By making them his Blood Warriors, he could, at will, lower their cultivation bases to the Celestial Stage so they could help him.</p>
<p>He couldn’t do that for the others, but if what Tower said turned out to be the case, then he could bring the people of the Chaos Chasm back to the tower to live there for now.</p>
<p>Kent could already tell that the universe would soon undergo a trial, and that, depending on whether they won, something would change. When that happened, he could let them roam the universe.</p>
<p>For now, he just wanted them not to get their expectations too high.</p>
<p>His mind was soon occupied by the technique, his growing bloodline, and the plans for what he would do next after leaving this place.</p>
<p>---</p>
<p>Meanwhile, on the outside, after an hour had passed and Kent still wasn’t out, everyone’s mood brightened. This was the first time in their lives that someone had lasted that long inside the Chaos Ascension King Trial.</p>
<p>So while they had no idea what was happening inside the trial, they believed Kent was doing well, and so there was a chance he might just succeed.</p>
<p>To them, their freedom was almost there.</p>
<p>The moment the portal fluttered and someone walked out, everyone became ecstatic. They all expected Kent to walk out with good news. However, when they saw Tower walk out, some frowned.</p>
<p>Tower stopped a few steps away from the portal and, perhaps seeing the expressions on their faces, smiled and chose to first wipe away their worries. "First of all, you all have nothing to worry about. Master is perfectly fine, and he is still inside the Chaos Ascension King Trial."</p>
<p>They sighed, but then some picked up on a few details and asked, "Master... who is your master?"</p>
<p>Tower replied, "My name is Celestial Chaos Tower, personal assistant to the Chaos Ascension King. I am also the Temple Guardian and the guardian of the Chaos Chasm.</p>
<p>The person I referred to as Master is naturally Kent Madson."</p>
<p>"WHAT?"</p>
<p>"Are you saying Kent Madson is the Chaos Ascension King?" Zdzislav asked in a shocked tone.</p>
<p>"I believe that is what I said. Kent Madson is the Chaos Ascension King." Tower looked amused, staring at the expressions on everyone’s faces.</p>
<p>They were clearly in shock, learning that what they had already suspected was actually the truth. Kent was the person they had all been waiting for to free them. The person who created their world and made them who they are now.</p>
<p>They wanted it to be Kent, but now that they had found out it was indeed him, none of them could believe their ears.</p>
<p>Fiannarel smiled happily, hearing what Tower had just said about her baby brother. "So baby brother is indeed the Chaos Ascension King," she muttered, almost emotional.</p>
<p>Tower replied to her with a charming smile, "Yes, Master was always the Chaos Ascension King, and he sent me to come fetch you."</p>
<p>"Really?" Fiannarel’s eyes sparkled.</p>
<p>"Yes. In fact, he wants me to bring the seven tribe chiefs too. He has something to discuss with the seven of you," Tower revealed her reason for coming, and as expected, aside from Fiannarel, who was extremely happy, the rest were in shock.</p>
<p>However, they knew they had to heed the call, considering the one summoning them was the Chaos Ascension King. They had no reason to reject his call, after all.</p>
<p>So they left with her, eager to meet the Chaos Ascension King, who now went by Kent Madson, the Primordial Chaos Dragon.</p>
