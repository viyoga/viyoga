<p>As for the Orbital Dominion, it has to do with how he can use these celestial bodies in combat. It turns out he can use them for both attacking and defending.</p>
<p>This tome taught him how to create shields around his planets and how to orbit and align them in a way that makes incoming attacks easy to defend against.</p>
<p>It is all technical, and even though Kent doesn’t have access to the seven tomes left, he knew there was a buildup to the Orbital Dominion. He knew it involved the laws, so he chose to create something and experiment first.</p>
<p>The Stellar Cataclysm is basically the combat aspect of the entire Soul Universe. Kent now possesses abilities like Solar Flare, which allows him to harness the power of the sun and shoot it like a laser.</p>
<p>Abilities like Nova Burst, where he can temporarily transform a summoned star into a nova, releasing an explosive burst of energy that can knock back and severely damage all enemies within a large radius.</p>
<p>That one is something Kent plans on using soon. It has so much potential to end an entire army within seconds.</p>
<p>The best part: the sun that he creates or the planets that he makes can grow endlessly, provided he has enough Soul Essence to keep fueling them.</p>
<p>Additionally, the outcome of how powerful his celestial bodies can be is determined by the strength of Kent’s soul. So, the higher he rises in soul cultivation, the stronger the Soul Universe becomes.</p>
<p>"Too bad I have to get to Tier 20 in soul cultivation before I can create black holes," Kent shook his head. He expected to be able to do something like that now, but it turns out his soul is still too weak for that.</p>
<p>"Though I could experiment and perhaps create something similar, after all, the tomes said nothing about me following a strict rule. This means I have free authority to create anything I want, so that is what I am going to do, I will create anything I want."</p>
<p>Kent stayed inside the cosmic space for a couple of months, learning the three tomes and the other things he could utilize from the first three tomes.</p>
<p>When he was done, he decided to go back to his body. Years had already passed, so he knew he had to take a rest before getting crazy with other things</p>
<p>"Aaaahhhh!"</p>
<p>Kent shouted and jumped back when he opened his eyes.</p>
<p>"You cheeky little devils, you scared me." Kent’s heartbeat rose and fell as he stared at the three lolis who were smiling at him, each covering their face with a hideous mask.</p>
<p>Their faces were a few inches away from Kent when he woke up, so despite him facing harrowing dangers already, they managed to scare the hell out of him—something anyone would have fallen for.</p>
<p>⟪Told you it would work⟫ Dori said, smiling at her master.</p>
<p>⟪You won this one, but next time, I will pick the prank, and I am sure Master will run off like a scared little child when he wakes up.⟫</p>
<p>Gaia bragged, the three of them smiling at their master, who was just looking at them with a calm yet flustered expression.</p>
<p>’They got me good this time.’ Kent couldn’t deny it. His cosmic lolis truly got him this time. The prank was perfect, unlike the last time he had to walk around with a painted face.</p>
<p>"You three, come here," Kent called out, but they wouldn’t wait to know what was about to happen. They bolted.</p>
<p>But Kent was fast. Since he is the master of the tower, he easily locked the Soul Tower, trapping them inside.</p>
<p>⟪Master, I know you already know this because you are from Earth, but punishing children who are just playing pranks is called child abuse. You better not have any funny ideas.⟫ Gaia said, looking somewhat happy that they got themselves in trouble.</p>
<p>In their minds, they were about to play with their master.</p>
<p>Kent looked at their expressions and knew that punishing them would make them happy. However, that didn’t stop him from punishing them.</p>
<p>In the end, they ran off to the Mountain Garden rubbing their bottoms. Kent smirked and decided to check his Soul Essence, currently in storage.</p>
<p>A smile appeared on his face when he saw the staggering numbers showing the amount of Soul Essence in storage.</p>
<p>⟦Soul Essence: 5,910,700,000⟧</p>
<p>"Almost four billion was added. I suppose having an entire race provide me with Soul Essence isn’t a bad thing at all. But is that even enough?"</p>
<p>"Gaia, dear, can you tell me how long it has been since I started awakening the Soul Path?" Kent asked because if what he was thinking turned out to be the reason, then he would be thrilled.</p>
<p>⟪It has been 21 years, Master, a very long time for a person to be asleep. A lot has happened that even I don’t know where to start from.</p>
<p>However, we managed to retrieve your servant. He is training in the dungeons. The rest, I will tell you when you are done.⟫</p>
<p>Kent nodded and then decided to get back inside the Soul Universe to see what he could do with the 5.9 billion Soul Essence at his disposal.</p>
<p>He soon found himself inside the cosmic space.</p>
<p>"What should I create first in my Universe?" Kent wondered, gazing out at the beautiful surroundings. "I can’t even start to think of creating a planet, considering that inside the tomes, the amount required to create something like that is just too much.</p>
<p>However, I can start something."</p>
<p>Kent pondered for a few more minutes and then decided to proceed with something. "I will just start with an asteroid."</p>
<p>Kent opened his palm and then started channeling his Soul Essence. The process was simple; since he knew what he wanted to make, he could just condense it in his palm and then plant it.</p>
<p>In other words, he would create a seed of the celestial body using a part of the Soul Essence. Then, later, with him having an abundance of Soul Essence, he could simply keep feeding it in.</p>
<p>So he started, and after what felt like a month, a small, perhaps atomic-sized asteroid formed in his palm.</p>
<p>"Finally." Kent smiled and then made the seed of the asteroid float away from his hand. Now, I just have to wait and see what will happen.</p>
<p>When he got back to the tower and checked his Soul Essence in reserve, his jaw dropped wide open.</p>
<p>⟦Soul Essence: 3,970,000,000⟧</p>
<p>"Well, isn’t that just great?" Kent smiled, watching his Soul Essence reserve dwindle by the second.</p>
<p>"Gaia, tell me what happened while I was asleep."</p>
