<p>To his wives, it had only been hours, perhaps a few days, since Kent started the True Name awakening ritual.</p>
<p>However, to Kent, thousands of years had passed when he mistakenly remembered something that wasn’t even there.</p>
<p>At most, he was meant to stay in that illusion for 10 days. However, he ended up staying in it for 102,800 years, even remembering up to the point he was a True God. That was something that could break any mind.</p>
<p>Thankfully, Dori intervened and helped him put that memory aside. Though it is still there, it wouldn’t influence him unless he wanted it to, by which he would have to tap into it.</p>
<p>But due to all this, his mind was under so much stress that he drifted into a very long sleep when he let himself relax for a bit.</p>
<p>When he woke up, he felt his body relax, and all forms of stress on his mind were gone. Naturally, he woke up refreshed, and his clarity was on another level.</p>
<p>"Gaia, how long was I out for?" Kent immediately asked, knowing he had been asleep for a long time. His cultivation base was sitting at the peak of the Root Ascension stage, meaning his ladies had made great progress while he was asleep.</p>
<p>The fact that he moved two realms could not happen within a day. So he knew he was out for a while now.</p>
<p>⟪The master was out for 70 days.⟫ Gaia responded.</p>
<p>"Well, that was unexpected, but I deserve some sleep every now and then," he said, smiling.</p>
<p>⟪The master indeed needed that sleep. However, while you were asleep, a lot of things happened. First, we completed the City 30 days ago. Also, we created a Primordial Chamber for every one of your wives.</p>
<p>Talking about your wives, they are now monsters, and lastly, the Bloodvein tribe finished packing since it has already been three days for them.⟫</p>
<p>"Thanks for the update, Gaia. I will play wrestling with your sisters later." Kent freshened up and left to meet his ladies.</p>
<p>When he was a couple of meters away from his wives, they sensed him, and like the loving wives they were, every last one of them jumped into his arms.</p>
<p>He welcomed them with a kiss, including Elaine, Lady Vari, and Velyra this time. Since he added them to his harem, they’ve become much more rooted in the life they’ve stepped into.</p>
<p>"There is something different about you girls," Kent said with a curious expression. He studied them briefly before his eyes widened when he saw something shocking.</p>
<p>Except for Unity, Alina, and Elaine, who were still at the Root Ascendant level, and Velyra, Vari, and Lani, who were at the Core Formation stage, the rest had all broken into the Root Sovereign stage.</p>
<p>Elsa, Val, and Lilian were at the 2nd level; Neomi, Ingrid, and Selene were at the 3rd level; and Camila, Mara, Zaila, and Nara were at the 5th level.</p>
<p>"How?" Kent couldn’t help asking.</p>
<p>"It’s all thanks to Alina’s Eternal Garden Portrait. It allowed us to gain enlightenment and some other things that only the portrait can give," Vexthra replied.</p>
<p>"Even you," Kent raised his brow.</p>
<p>The Eternal Garden contains more than just flowers and plants. It includes scenes of battles, decisive moments in some heaven-shattering duels, and even enlightened moments of most celestials.</p>
<p>Basically, it’s the treasure anyone would like to cultivate with. But there is more.</p>
<p>Aside from having the power to easily gain enlightenment, if one gains the said enlightenment and performs well, the portrait will reward the person.</p>
<p>Most gain powerful techniques, some a deeper understanding of the path they are following, but in most cases, a leap in their cultivation base is the gift they will receive.</p>
<p>It was one of the main reasons why the Supreme Emperor held it close to his heart. Now, Kent’s wives have it.</p>
<p>Aside from Unity, who gained enlightenment on the path of the bow and later received even more knowledge from a supreme being who lived during the primordial era, who was known as a master of the bow, the rest gained a chance to take a massive leap in their cultivation.</p>
<p>"This is too much to digest now." Kent shook his head. He couldn’t just understand how his luck could be this great.</p>
<p>He checked his undistributed stat points, and what he saw shocked him.</p>
<p>[Attribute Points: 2,990,900]</p>
<p>Aside from that, his soul had also reached Tier 12 and even moved further toward the 13th Tier. As for his mind, he now understood where the clarity came from when he woke up.</p>
<p>"Gaia and her sisters are done building the city, so we should go check it out before we go back to the Dead Region," Kent said, taking his ladies to the Mountain Garden.</p>
<p>The sight that greeted them in the city left their jaws hanging when they appeared inside the Mountain garden.</p>
<p>Far in the distance, the crimson city greeted them.</p>
<p>"Wow, that is magnificent." For even a god to say that, the city was indeed a sight. On one side, there was the white, blue, golden, and green city the humans lived in, and on the other side, there was a majestic red city that, instead of looking terrifying because it was red, was rather beautiful.</p>
<p>The buildings are red and extend high into the clouds. The plants in the garden are also red, with some dark and white ones.</p>
<p>Of course, the sun and moon aren’t red, which, in this case, will make the city much brighter so that the non-Bloodveins will not feel suffocated.</p>
<p>A few things are not red, like some trees and pools.</p>
<p>However, the roads are even made red with white pavements.</p>
<p>Even the sky up there looks red. Most of the buildings are 5 to 7 stories tall, but the one sitting in the middle extends into the clouds, showing that it’s where the tribe’s leader lives.</p>
<p>Kent and his ladies got into a flying ship and moved closer to inspect. There are training grounds and even a portal that leads to the Chaos Pool, where the chaotic energy there can even take them to the Perfect stage.</p>
<p>The air is thick with blood qi, and every pool there is made of blood qi and Chaos qi. Being inside the city is enough to elevate your comprehension of the Chaos energy.</p>
<p>After examining everything, they also explored the outer reaches of the Bloodvein ancestral grounds. That was Gaia’s way of gifting them techniques exclusive only to the Bloodvein tribe.</p>
<p>Of course, most of those techniques are also from the Vampires that they copied from the higher worlds. But in the end, they made the city the best one yet.</p>
<p>The humans had already started walking around there, admiring the new city.</p>
<p>"They are going to love this place," Velyra said, wiping the tears from her eyes. The city is just perfect for her race.</p>
<p>"In that case, welcome to Blood City." Gaia and her sisters appeared, causing fireworks to light up the sky as they pulled off the cloth to reveal the name of the new city:</p>
<p>’Blood City’</p>
