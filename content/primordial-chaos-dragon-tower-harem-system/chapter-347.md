<p>⟪Master, put me down.⟫ Akira giggled as they stepped into the necro forge.</p>
<p>"You’ve grown cheeky," Kent smiled. "Sometimes, I can’t help but wonder if you’re an anime character."</p>
<p>⟪What? Who said I’m an anime character? I am not an anime character!⟫ Akira’s giggle disappeared, and a slight panic could be seen on her face.</p>
<p>"Whatever you say. I just hope that is the case... I really hope for that," Kent smiled and chuckled, making Akira curse the bastard in her heart.</p>
<p>"Show me the undead you’ve created." Akira quickly took out the Undead Schematic Creator. She activated it, and a hologram of a figure appeared.</p>
<p>"This is..." Kent raised an eyebrow.</p>
<p>⟪Don’t look shocked, Master. I put my entire soul into this, and let me tell you, as a soul weaver, my soul is rather large.</p>
<p>Also, a part of the domain crystal, the darkness crystal, the eyes of the forest king, one arm, 5 Transcendent bodies, 25 Great Sage bodies, and 1 trillion spirit stones.</p>
<p>For the ascension bodies, you can use them later.</p>
<p>Those are the materials required for this undead. Please thank me afterwards.⟫</p>
<p>Kent smiled.</p>
<p>"I won’t make many changes, Akira. I trust you, so let’s do it." She smiled and activated the forge. She placed the schematic on it and continued with the rest of the process.</p>
<p>After everything was added, the timer was set for 48 hours, making it 2 days.</p>
<p>"Let’s go back and eat something." They left the tower and went to the tower shop to buy some chocolates from Earth.</p>
<p>"Cosmic weavers are the best," Kent smiled as he bit into a chocolate.</p>
<p>⟪I know, right? Gaia is just a cheat. She can take anything from anywhere and even create anything using cosmic energy. Sometimes I am jealous of her.⟫</p>
<p>"No need to be jealous of her. You are special in your own way," Kent said, making Akira smile.</p>
<p>⟪But Mind Weaver is the best. She is literally the ultimate cheat. If she were to be around, the rescue mission would be as easy as walking in there and walking out.⟫</p>
<p>"We will rescue her in the coming days."</p>
<p>⟪Thank you, Master.⟫</p>
<p>"You can just call me Kent when we are alone," Kent said, but the soul weaver shook her head.</p>
<p>⟪Master is Master, so he can only be called Master.⟫</p>
<p>"Suit yourself. But don’t worry too much about your sister. She will be rescued soon."</p>
<p>They stayed in the tower shop for hours until the tower message appeared.</p>
<p>"The upgrade is finished." Akira and Kent vanished and appeared inside the Legion Tower, where the Battle Doll had now taken on a new look.</p>
<p>"Damn, that is one terrifying knight right there," Kent’s smile bloomed as he looked at the dark and red metal knight holding a large sword.</p>
<p>It wore a helmet with sharp spikes and glowing red slits for eyes, making it appear inhuman.</p>
<p>The armour was sleek but covered in jagged, blade-like edges, giving it an aggressive and warlike look and aura.</p>
<p>Red energy pulsed through the veins of its armour. Its gauntlets were reinforced, with claw-like fingertips that could tear through steel. Nobody would enjoy getting punched by that.</p>
<p>A long, flowing black cape trailed behind it. Kent didn’t know where that came from, but he was pleased.</p>
<p>The ancient writings on the armour even suggested it was something taken from legends and myths.</p>
<p>Perhaps it was one of the unholy knights that guarded the dark city of Sabatan.</p>
<p>But while it looked imposing, the greatsword in its hands was enough to strike fear into anyone’s heart.</p>
<p>It was a massive black greatsword etched with glowing red runes. The sword appeared to be infused with a dark force as crimson energy seeped from its edges.</p>
<p>[Image of veydris]</p>
<p>⟪Congratulations, Master. That is one terrifying figure capable of using Void energy. You should be proud.⟫</p>
<p>"I am proud, Akira. This is one terrifying knight," Kent said, his smile contagious. "Akira, did I ever tell you how much I am fascinated by knights?"</p>
<p>⟪You never told me, but I can already see it on your face. You are like a child who has received his Christmas gift from his long-lost ex-girlfriend,⟫ Akira giggled.</p>
<p>"At least I am not an anime character," Kent fired back, making Akira glare at him.</p>
<p>⟪Brand it with your soul energy to connect it to your soul. Though it doesn’t have a soul, it has the ability to reap souls, which will benefit your soul cultivation.⟫</p>
<p>Kent nodded and placed the soul brand on the Metal Doll, which went to its knee and gave him a knight’s salute right after the branding.</p>
<p>"Oh my, this is the best," Kent said, almost giggling. He took out his sword and, like a lord, placed it on the shoulder of the Metal Doll.</p>
<p>"Rise, Sir Veydris. Your legend has just begun," Kent declared, but the next second, his eyes widened when a message appeared right after naming the Metal Knight.</p>
<p>⟪Congratulations on naming your first Knight. You have received the title Master of the Lost Knights.⟫</p>
<p>[Master of the Lost Knights]</p>
<p>→ During the last war of Sanguine, the 14 guardian Knights of Sabatan fought as the first and last stand against the Creatures of the Night.</p>
<p>→ It was said that during the final phase of the siege, the 14 knights laid down their lives to protect the lost children of the mother.</p>
<p>→ Because of their sacrifice, when the mother received her children, she named them the Guardians of the Lost, for she knew once they were lost, they would be found again.</p>
<p>→ You now have the ability to find and awaken these brave knights, for they must exact their revenge when the end comes knocking.</p>
<p>→ Whenever a Lost Knight is nearby, you will know and awaken them, for that is your duty as the Master of the Lost Knights.</p>
<p>Kent remained frozen as he read and reread the messages in front of him. It just didn’t make sense, as this was something he never expected.</p>
<p>All he wanted was to give a cool name of one of his favorite game characters to the metal knight, but instead, he ended up awakening a lost knight.</p>
<p>⟪Master is very lucky. The Lost Knights of Sabatan, huh? It’s been a long time since I heard of the bravest knights to have ever lived.⟫</p>
