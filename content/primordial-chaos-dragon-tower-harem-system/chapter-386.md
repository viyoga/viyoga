<p>"This is the bone-corroding poison. Veydris will apply it to his sword since he is immune to it. This is the weapon we will be using against the Orc King," Kent said to the two undead before him.</p>
<p>"You two will immediately attack when he arrives. Remember, it’s just one attack, so make it count. The moment your attack happens, Veydris will also attack.</p>
<p>It doesn’t matter whether it works or not; vanish into the tower immediately after your attacks."</p>
<p>They nodded.</p>
<p>"Very well, the moment of truth will arrive soon." After instructing them, he looked at Dori. "I will send you away now. Your sisters are waiting."</p>
<p>"Okay, Master, just be careful. The odds are heavily against you, so be very careful." After nodding to her words, Kent sent her away, and she appeared at the mountain garden where Gaia and Akira were waiting for her.</p>
<p>"Looks like life hasn’t been nice to you, Dori," Gaia said, hugging her like a bear.</p>
<p>"I may have been out of my element for a moment there, which landed me in that mess. But life has been good since the last time we met." Akira was hugging her from behind, too.</p>
<p>"I see you finally got the tower up and running. Master must really be thrilled."</p>
<p>"He doesn’t remember that side of him yet. In fact, he had just recently started remembering his life as the Slave Swordsman," Gaia responded.</p>
<p>"He would probably twist your ear if he heard you call him Slave Swordsman," Akira said, and Dori smiled.</p>
<p>"Then thank you both for helping Master come to get me, though the chance of him learning about me in the coming days was high even if you two hadn’t said anything."</p>
<p>"And what makes you think he would have learned of your existence?" Gaia asked.</p>
<p>"Have you forgotten which of us found him first in his life as Lord Genesis, the Divine Swordsman? It was I, and back then, he knew I was a Mind Weaver. After all, I helped him in his path as the first true swordsman.</p>
<p>So whether you said anything or not, he would have realized, and knowing him, the universe had already made contact, so he would have asked for a way to save me.</p>
<p>Either way, he would have come for me," Dori said, and the two sisters just smirked.</p>
<p>"Sometimes, you are just too smart for your own good. Now, let’s sit back and watch how Master makes his first overpowered enemy."</p>
<p>Gaia waved her hand, and the scene around them changed, showing them the pink forest where Kent was about to execute the three Sovereign-stage orcs.</p>
<p>Kaizo and Sylara were hidden out of sight but nearby, while Veydris was inside the void. He didn’t have much to say, but he knew what he must do.</p>
<p>"I really hope I don’t die from being this reckless," Kent muttered. Then he commanded the doom shards, driving them through the necks of the three, killing them instantly.</p>
<p>The moment they died, he took their bodies and blinked away, appearing a couple of kilometres from the execution site.</p>
<p>Two seconds went by, and nobody appeared.</p>
<p>The seconds started to tick, and soon, ten seconds passed. Kent, who was farther away, began to wonder if the being would come at all. But after 30 seconds and still no one appeared, he could only sigh.</p>
<p>He had expected a quick response, but that didn’t seem to be the case.</p>
<p>This made him change his mind, and he was about to abort the mission when the space cracked open, and from the deep, dark crack, a figure walked out.</p>
<p>It was a dark figure with white marks lining his body, giving him an aura of pure strength and danger. He stood about three meters tall, with arms as thick as an adult’s thigh.</p>
<p>He had two white horns.</p>
<p>The moment he appeared, his gaze fell on the destroyed fortress, causing his blood to surge. However, just as he was about to act, lightning flashed through the clouds, and a large fist emerged from the lightning storm that formed on the horizon.</p>
<p>"Mountain-Breaking Fist!"</p>
<p>Kaizo’s voice filled the space as his fist descended on the figure, who looked up and smirked. He raised his hand and flicked his finger, sending a beam of white energy at the fist.</p>
<p>The moment it connected, the fist was destroyed, but Kaizo just smiled, because he saw a red light flash through the surroundings. Before the Orc King could sense it, Sylara was a meter from him.</p>
<p>She stabbed her claw at the Orc King, who held a calm expression. However, just as he was about to attack, Sylara’s red eyes brightened, and the Orc King closed his eyes.</p>
<p>She didn’t attack with her claw again; instead, she turned into a dust of red energy right when a purple sword arc arrived behind her.</p>
<p>At the same time, the Orc King, who had been mind-attacked by Sylara, opened his eyes just as the sword arc appeared before him.</p>
<p>There was no escaping it, so he crossed his arms over his chest and defended against the sword arc. However, a small cut appeared on his arm, making him scoff and ready to kill the three bugs.</p>
<p>Yet, when he scanned the surroundings, only one person was sensed—a demi-dragon hovering in the air with his horns and wings activated, his skin covered in dragon scales.</p>
<p>The Orc King immediately used his energy to bind him, but just as he took hold of Kent, his energy was cancelled, and the dragon smiled at him.</p>
<p>"You might want to take a look at that arm," Kent said, pointing at the Orc King’s left arm, which was now turning red and had already spread past his shoulder, meaning cutting it off was no longer an option.</p>
<p>The poison has taken effect.</p>
<p>"Who are you, and how dare you poison me? Do you not know who I am?" The Orc King’s energy exploded.</p>
<p>"I’m afraid using your energy only hastens the effect. Relax for now—for the next few years, you won’t be able to do anything. While this poison can’t kill you, it will at least prevent you from killing anybody."</p>
<p>Kent smiled.</p>
<p>"Be sure to relax and wait for me. I will return, and when I do, your tyranny will come to an end." He deactivated the dragon enhancement, showing that his true human appeared, and he smiled now that his work was done.</p>
<p>"Be sure to remember my name... Kent Madson, the Chaos Dragon." He winked at the Orc King and snapped his fingers, vanishing from sight.</p>
<p>He had to know that the figure before him was still a Spirit Lord at the 6th level despite cancelling his energy and poisoning him.</p>
<p>The Orc King screamed, causing his tyrannical energy to fill the entire forest, flattening it. However, he coughed up blood, showing the poison was spreading faster.</p>
<p>"Kent Madson... I will hunt you down." He vanished, and as Kent said, he wouldn’t be going anywhere for the next few years.</p>
<p>At the universe’s core, the lady watching Kent’s life unfold smiled and chuckled. "He never changes, always looking for trouble with beings he can’t measure up to..."</p>
