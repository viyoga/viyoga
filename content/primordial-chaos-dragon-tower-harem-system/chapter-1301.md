<p>The moment the demonic human died, the flag fell, and Kent caught it. However, before he could examine it, a powerful primal energy washed over the forest, causing all beasts to turn in his direction.</p>
<p>The energy came from the void temple, showing that the temple master of annihilation has reached his breaking point and, as such, wants Kent dead.</p>
<p>Every last horror hidden inside the forest stirred, and even those surrounding the temple left to go hunt down the bastard who seemed to enjoy slaughtering them.</p>
<p>"I guess I will handle this later." He stored away the flag, and then he extended his hand, summoning Gaap again.</p>
<p>While inside the flag, he knew using the sword would have limited him. As good as it may sound, facing immortals with his Sword King talent is not enough, even if the sword king in question is the reincarnation of the father of swordsmen.</p>
<p>He may have Heavenly Sword Aura and intent, but that isn’t enough. If he were to have his own sword realm, then at least he would have had a good run.</p>
<p>So abandoning his sword was the right move. But he is not inside the flag now, and as such, Gaap had to come back so they could commit the greatest massacre in their life... at least so far in their journey.</p>
<p>It was a wild series of massacres inside the Damned Hell sea, but this one would be on another level.</p>
<p>"Here we go." The Jade Void wings buzz, and he moves with speed blindingly.</p>
<p>Kent limited the number of flame veins to just 50,000, but if one were to see the speed at which he was moving, they would probably only see a blur and a splash of blood filling the air.</p>
<p>Kent took the tower’s decision, and so he decided not to waste time and instead farm for some blood essence and blood while Gaap went for the resentful souls.</p>
<p>The endgame is always to have more blood essence on hand when he needs it. So he went all out, and the result couldn’t be any more chilling.</p>
<p>In less than ten minutes, he had cleared the entire battlefield, and then he turned to the rest of the forest.</p>
<p>Kent is already aware that the forest will have to be cleared once he is done with the Void Temple. But that would be the work of the warriors on Planet Bayla.</p>
<p>Naturally, since he had no plans to waste precious blood, he chose to do it himself.</p>
<p>Five days quickly passed, and in those five days, Kent had not for one moment slowed down. He was on the move, killing everything he met.</p>
<p>Looking at the bloodthirsty aura coming off his body as he moved, one could tell his killing intent had reached a new level.</p>
<p>Even it could be seen in his eyes. They are crimson, and faint smoke continues to emanate from them as he moves. With every beast he kills, the bloodthirsty aura seems to increase.</p>
<p>By the close of ten days, what could have taken hundreds of thousands of warriors and a lot of casualties was accomplished by Kent. He had completely slaughtered every last beast.</p>
<p>Watching blood dripping off his sword, staring coldly down at a spider demon, Kent let a sigh escape his lips.</p>
<p>He raised his head and let out yet another sigh.</p>
<p>Then he deactivated the Jade Void wings, his horn, and his claws.</p>
<p>He sat in a lotus posture and closed his eyes.</p>
<p>The amount of bloodlust assaulting his mind, if not paid attention to, would affect him in the upcoming battles. So with no danger in sight, he sat down and let the passing of the hours help him calm down.</p>
<p>After three more days, he regained his sanity. However, his killing intent had reached a whole different level of danger.</p>
<p>"Tower, what changed with me?"</p>
<p>[Aside from the fact that the master had farmed millions in blood essence, the master had also levelled up his Heaven Sword Aura and intent to Level 9, and even better, the master’s killing intent had transcended beyond insanity.]</p>
<p>"I suppose I went wild, huh?" Kent smiled, feeling his blood boiling like crazy. The bloodlust is still there, just shimmering beneath the surface, waiting to be unleashed.</p>
<p>"Do I even have to have my Blood Cleansing Bath now?"</p>
<p>[The master must have the Blood Cleansing Bath. However, to be on the safer side, the master must do that inside the Void Temple.]</p>
<p>"I also think that is the best move. Better to be in a place I can cause the most damage than out here. Who knows what would become of me when I come out of the Blood Cleansing Bath?"</p>
<p>The first time he had that bath, Vexthra had to step in and save his skin. If not for her knocking him unconscious, he would have caused chaos inside the tower cities. Who knew how many he could have killed that day?</p>
<p>So, entering the Void Temple before doing that is indeed the best bet. If anything were to go wrong, he would just use the bloodlust to his advantage and trash the unlucky temple master who would stand in his way.</p>
<p>"I must absorb the blood essence now. The earlier I clear the temple, the better I can focus on other things. I still have three beasts to hunt for their fangs, so no time to waste."</p>
<p>[That is indeed true. The master has more than enough to saturate both cores and has surplus blood essence for other tasks.</p>
<p>But aside from that, I managed to extract 19 corrupted blood essence from the battle fought inside the flag. That is enough to refine nine Blood Life Seals and ten Blood Death Seals.]</p>
<p>"That is good news, but I will need about four years to refine and absorb all nineteen. That is just too long for me. So I will focus on only my cores. When I am back inside the tower, I will do that.</p>
<p>Of course, if you can leave a marker here, then I would enter the tower and handle that and return to take care of the Void Temple."</p>
<p>[Unfortunately, with the seal in place and the corruption in the air, I can’t do that. The master would have to depend on the passing of time outside, this time around.]</p>
<p>"Then I will focus on the Blood Essence. I want to be able to run circles around the temple masters. Back during the Primordial Era, Chaos always had the upper hand. That was why they hated him the most.</p>
<p>This time around, I want them to do more than hate me. I want them to fear me, because I will be their worst nightmare."</p>
<p>Kent snapped his finger, and ice burst from the ground, creating a cave-like fortress for him.</p>
<p>"Gaap, stay on the outside just in case we miss a target." Gaap transformed into his demon form and gave Kent a nod. He moved out and sat calmly atop the forest, his eyes closed, resentment emanating from him.</p>
<p>Back inside the forest, Kent waved his hand, and the air turned golden. Millions of blood essence appeared, filling the ice fortress with the aura of blood.</p>
<p>"Here we go." Kent formed a hand seal and began absorbing the blood essence at a speed that would shock anyone walking the blood path.</p>
<p>This time, he was merely saturating his cores, so he went all out, and the results soon began to show within minutes.</p>
