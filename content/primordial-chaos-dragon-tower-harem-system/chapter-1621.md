<p>It is well known that a warrior who can use two or more skills simultaneously can be considered a genius beyond reason. It takes a lot of control to keep one skill active while activating another.</p>
<p>That was what the Crimson Blood Demon General had done.</p>
<p>While the [Blood Rain Thousand Pierces] was actively raining down swords on Kent, a new technique was unleashed, and Kent could sense the danger.</p>
<p>He channelled more blood qi into his shields just as the void tore open, and a broken star appeared, blood raining down from it. It was as if this demon had dipped every technique in a pool of blood.</p>
<p>Kent felt his body shake as the broken star’s power fell toward him, bringing with it a terrible pressure. For a moment, Kent wondered just where this technique came from.</p>
<p>There was something hidden within it that he just couldn’t put his finger on.</p>
<p>However, before the star could even reach him, another skill was activated, and all hell broke loose.</p>
<p>"HellFall Blood Devastation,"</p>
<p>The space around Kent warped, and before he knew how it happened, everything exploded, and his body was reduced to nothing. Two seconds from now, he would die.</p>
<p>"Blood Runes,"</p>
<p>Kent summoned all 250,000 Blood Seals and made them all merge with his body. Then, as if he had gone mad, he unsummoned the [Chaos Scale Shield], allowing nothing between himself and the raining swords, and manifested it as a protective layer of dragon scales, coating his armour.</p>
<p>BOOM BOOM BOOM</p>
<p>He unleashed his blood qi, chaos qi, and flame qi at once, his body surging with so much energy as he moved, weaving his way out of thousands of swords despite the danger of the broken star still hanging over his head.</p>
<p>Kent saw his death, and now that he had moved, he knew what would happen next.</p>
<p>BOOOOOOM</p>
<p>The greatsword in the Crimson Blood Demon General’s hand was stabbed into the ground, and the entire domain exploded. The eruption point was where he had stood a moment ago.</p>
<p>However, the fact that he managed to move didn’t mean he walked away unscathed. The shockwave from the explosion pushed him away, a giant sword stabbing through his thighs as he slammed into a mountain 5 km away.</p>
<p>COUGH</p>
<p>Kent coughed up blood, his expression showing the pain he was in. Even with the [Omniscient Jade Eye], he never saw that attack coming. But at least he didn’t die.</p>
<p>"My turn,"</p>
<p>His wounds healed at an alarming rate, causing him to rise to his feet in no time. Possessing the healing ability of a demon god rendered the injury no insignificant.</p>
<p>"Void Cleave,"</p>
<p>Slash Slash Slash</p>
<p>With 4 km between them, the Void Cleaves came with so much power, forcing the Crimson Blood Demon General to muster a powerful defence to block all three.</p>
<p>However, Kent didn’t wait for him to defend against all of them without a backup plan in motion.</p>
<p>Just as the third Void Cleave attack was blocked, the sky turned purple and gold as Kent slashed down with devastating force.</p>
<p>"Chaos Starfall,"</p>
<p>RUMBLE RUMBLE</p>
<p>The sky shook, and the ground trembled as thousands of Chaos stars imbued with sword aura rained down on the Crimson Blood Demon General.</p>
<p>He was forced to raise more defences. However, Kent wasn’t having any of that.</p>
<p>"You may be more powerful than me in terms of strength and sword energy. However, I was the Father of All Swordsmen, bitch."</p>
<p>"Void Cleave."</p>
<p>One swing, yet five Void Cleaves flashed out, crashing into the Crimson Blood Demon General’s defence as he was hurled back, a Chaos star falling on him.</p>
<p>"I was killing Sword Immortals long before I became one. So do you really think killing you would be any different?" Kent stomped on the ground, and cracks formed.</p>
<p>From within, potent evil qi gushed out, plunging the entire domain into a bone-creeping coldness, one that made the Crimson Blood Demon General suck in a cold breath, his mind wondering just how any of this was possible.</p>
<p>He had unleashed his domain, which blocked Kent’s own sword realm and sword intent. That was enough to render him useless inside his domain.</p>
<p>However, that wasn’t the case at all. It appeared that sword intent or no sword intent, Kent had absolutely nothing to worry about.</p>
<p>What was even more alarming was Kent’s uncanny ability to always slip away just when he thought he had gained an advantage.</p>
<p>"Allow me to enlighten you, demon,"</p>
<p>Kent flew into the air, and when he was high enough, a powerful chaos energy exploded from his body as his sword was raised high.</p>
<p>"Abyss Dragon Rend,"</p>
<p>SLASH</p>
<p>He slashed his sword downward, and all hell broke loose.</p>
<p>A colossal dragon formed from Chaos Qi and Sword Qi appeared and lunged at the Crimson Blood Demon General, who had little to no time to defend since he was still under the devastation of the Chaos Starfalls.</p>
<p>What was even more heartbreaking was the fact that just when the Abyss Dragon Rend was mere meters away, Kent unleashed three more Void Cleaves.</p>
<p>The three Void Cleaves caused the ground to literally tremble and erupt, tearing sand and stone as they closed in on the Crimson Blood Demon General almost instantly.</p>
<p>BOOOM</p>
<p>The explosion rocked the Crimson Blood Demon General, sending him flying back like a rag doll.</p>
<p>"I wasn’t known as the greatest swordsman to ever walk the face of this universe if I were mediocrity at best,"</p>
<p>"Infinite Dragon Echo,"</p>
<p>Thousands of Kents appeared, and before the Crimson Blood Demon General could even land on the ground, thousands of sword arcs slammed into him.</p>
<p>"I may have forgotten the kind of person I was since it has been a very long time. However, now that we are here, allow me to claim your life, you demon."</p>
<p>"Infinity Heavens Art," His sword burned with both Blood Qi and Chaos Qi, causing a pillar of red and gold light to shoot up into the sky when he raised his sword.</p>
<p>BOOM BOOM BOOOM</p>
<p>Kent’s entire form exploded with so much power that the moment the sword was slashed down vertically, the Crimson Blood Demon General knew his advantage had ended here and now.</p>
<p>"Domain Severing,"</p>
<p>The crimson domain that had trapped Kent for the past 5 minutes was cut in half, rays of sun penetrating the dome that had covered them.</p>
<p>[Your sword realm and sword intent have been unsealed.]</p>
<p>"Now allow me to show you what real sword potential looks like," Kent stomped on the ground as he unleashed his sword realm.</p>
<p>"Genesis Sword Realm,"</p>
<p>The crimson domain, slowly coming apart, trembled and shattered when a vast sea, spanning several kilometres, erupted, rising into the air and casting a deep darkness behind Kent.</p>
<p>"You may have been genetically modified, buddy, but you aren’t a true Forbidden Yet. Now, taste the power of greatness."</p>
<p>As the Genesis Sword Realm fell down on the Crimson Blood Demon General, all he saw were billions of swords packed together so closely that they looked like water.</p>
<p>’This is more than anything I have ever seen,’</p>
<p>The Crimson Blood Demon General could only watch as his body was consumed by the devastation of the [Genesis Sword Realm].</p>
<p>He wouldn’t die, but Kent wasn’t looking to kill him that way. His sword realm wasn’t strong enough to kill a Sword Immortal. However, the required effect he had in mind was reached.</p>
<p>’Many didn’t know this, but I actually had some form of sword will when I reached the Sword Emperor stage. It allowed me to fight those far beyond my level. That sword will always works in my favor, leaving my opponents unsure of themselves after I unleash it.’</p>
<p>Kent sighed, but then he froze when a sword came out of nowhere and stabbed into his back. The next second, a message appeared.</p>
<p>[Your soul has been invaded.]</p>
<p>Kent paused for a moment, then he turned slightly and saw a lady clad in green armour move toward him. Instead of evading, Kent allowed the sword to stab him.</p>
<p>’Kenzie, time to see what you are capable of.’</p>
