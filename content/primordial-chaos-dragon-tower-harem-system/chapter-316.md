<p>⟪Congratulations on completing the quest titled ’Scoring a Home Run’⟫</p>
<p>⟪Rewards: Received⟫</p>
<p>→ 2,000,000 Tower Points</p>
<p>→ True Name Token</p>
<p>→ Chrono Watch</p>
<p>"So, I now have two True Name tokens, and Lilian’s birthday is the day after tomorrow. I will be giving her one to awaken her potential and then move on from there.</p>
<p>Now, who should I give the next token to, considering Unity has already rejected it? Elsa already has one. Then who should I give it to?"</p>
<p>[The master can just give it to big sister Selene. She will be entering seclusion after the auction to rebuild her path.</p>
<p>I believe her having it will push her further much faster than if she goes on without a True Name.]</p>
<p>Akira spoke, appearing beside Kent.</p>
<p>"I was thinking the same thing, but what about the others? Though they will also be entering seclusion right when Gaia is done, I doubt I can rescue the two ladies before then."</p>
<p>Kent sighed. He will be receiving five True Name tokens after clearing that quest, so he wanted to wait until then.</p>
<p>But after talking with Mara and seeing how she wanted to let him have his way with her just so he could become strong, he realized that he had to speed up his rescue mission.</p>
<p>Also, he saw the need to turn his ladies into powerhouses.</p>
<p>If she thought of that, then the rest would also be thinking the same thing if they found out he had such a plan that required him to gather powerful warriors.</p>
<p>[The master is forgetting some things.</p>
<p>The quest to kill Bright Faywood is already in the bag. All you have to do is give the command, and he is dead. This will give you another True Name token.</p>
<p>Then, there is your night rider lady, Saden, who went on a mission with the ladies. They will be striking this evening, and by tomorrow morning, you will be swimming in Tower Points.</p>
<p>So, though one True Name token is ten million Tower Points, you can buy another one, if not two. This will set you at least five True Name tokens.</p>
<p>Now, Elsa and Unity are already excluded, leaving Lilian, Ingrid, Neomi, Selene, and Alina. Though the three ladies are not in the harem yet, they can have the five you will be receiving after the mission.</p>
<p>This means since you will be buying two more, you will have one for each other, the ladies currently in the harem.</p>
<p>Now, about the mission. You are forgetting you have an Undead on the way. You also have a Night Rider currently kidnapping Root Great Sages and Transcendents.</p>
<p>Though I don’t know why, I have a feeling that when you find out, you will be impressed. That alone should give you the confidence to know the mission will be happening faster than you think.</p>
<p>Of course, you also have one of the hardest metals in the universe currently in your possession and a battle doll with many skills in his arsenal.</p>
<p>If anything, you should be happy now because if—and this is a big *if*—if you get lucky on the last day of the auction, you will be walking away with more than you think.</p>
<p>In fact, if you plan it well, not even your ladies or the gate masters will be joining this rescue mission. You will have everything in the bag.</p>
<p>And let me tell you this: the reward after the mission is not something you can just throw away like that.]</p>
<p>Akira lectured Kent, showing him she was much more experienced than he was. Kent could only nod and smile from time to time.</p>
<p>"I might have forgotten about my gains for a moment there," Kent laughed.</p>
<p>[Maybe you should stop chasing skirts and pay more attention to your surroundings,] Akira giggled.</p>
<p>"Looks like you’re growing a pair, Akira," Kent said, making her continue to giggle as she walked away.</p>
<p>"Wouldn’t it be good if I don’t involve the ladies in this mission?" Kent smiled. "I guess I’ll make good use of the dungeons after the auction. If they do join, then I want the odds to be heavily in their favor."</p>
<p>Kent knew the ladies would beg him to let them fight. Even Vexthra, who knew of his plans, wanted him to include them.</p>
<p>So, he would plan it well and let them fight with the odds on their side.</p>
<p>"Paying a visit to the forest where the skull was found wouldn’t be bad either." After making a small side plan, he turned back to the quest rewards.</p>
<p>He took the item out, and it was a brown watch with no hour, minute, or second hand, nor was there a time displayed on it.</p>
<p>The only thing visible was a green circle on the screen. Kent quickly appraised it.</p>
<p>⟪Name: Chrono Watch⟫</p>
<p>⟪Grade: Mythic⟫</p>
<p>⟪Cooldown: 60 days⟫</p>
<p>→ When the line is green, and you tap the screen, you will be able to slow down time for thirty seconds. This will only affect those you target.</p>
<p>→ Once the thirty seconds are over, time will return to its normal speed.</p>
<p>→ Also, if you apply it to yourself, you will be able to speed up time for yourself for the next thirty seconds.</p>
<p>⟦Note: You will only be able to speed up time by 2.5x and slow it down by -2.5x. The 30 seconds is counted using the original time.</p>
<p>Also, if the strength of your opponent is far greater than yours, the effect might decrease or not work at all.⟧</p>
<p>"Damn, this is a good item," Kent said, quickly putting it on his wrist.</p>
<p>He didn’t need a genius to tell him that this was something he couldn’t look down on. The watch was a gem he had to take seriously.</p>
<p>Slowing down time for your opponents was like giving yourself a chance to kill them while they were in slow motion. But even if they were stronger than him, he could just speed himself up.</p>
<p>That was just too good. Kent turned to the additional rewards, and a smile appeared on his face.</p>
<p>⟪Additional Rewards:⟫</p>
<p>→ 7th Primordial Sword Path</p>
<p>→ 9th Primordial Sword Path</p>
<p>→ 3rd Primordial Spear Path</p>
<p>"Akira, come here. I’ve got some more projects for Gaia. Tell her I’ll reward her when she’s done with everything." Kent’s smile widened as he leaned back and closed his eyes.</p>
