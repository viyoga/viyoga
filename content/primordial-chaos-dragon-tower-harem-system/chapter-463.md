<p>60 seconds before Protector Vari was attacked...</p>
<p>"Master, you might want to end the battle soon. The weather is changing, which in Eldritch magic means a great calamity is about to befall us," Zareth said, smiling at Kent.</p>
<p>Looking at his first True Undead, he couldn’t help but sigh happily. All his undeads are awesome, but this one is the best.</p>
<p>Kent then looked at Protector Navarro.</p>
<p>"I am going to cut your left arm off now."</p>
<p>Before the Kingdom Protector could react, another version of Kent appeared behind him and swung his sword, taking off his left arm.</p>
<p>"Aaarrggghhh!"</p>
<p>A blend of pain, shame, and anger erupted from Protector Navarro’s mouth. Long before the battle began, he used the split body technique he learned from the sword bridge to split a copy of himself and waited for the right time.</p>
<p>He knew doing it himself would be next to impossible, considering the Kingdom Protector had proven to be a formidable opponent.</p>
<p>However, he lost one arm, bringing both shame and pain to him.</p>
<p>His right hand, wielding the spear, moved to drive it through Kent, but a voice spoke from above before he could move a muscle.</p>
<p>"Coil Void Drago!"</p>
<p>A giant dragon, about 300 meters long, appeared in the air and roared, shaking the entirety of Vastport City. Everybody felt it.</p>
<p>Protector Navarro, who was about to move, felt a great pressure fall on him, bringing him to his knees and cracking the ground beneath it.</p>
<p>"Nice one, Zareth," Kent gave his undead a thumbs-up before moving closer to the now kneeling and pale protector.</p>
<p>"You win, Protector Navarro. I wanted to succeed where Zareth failed, but he seemed to be better than I. So I’ll let you go today.</p>
<p>Of course, the next time you see me, I will be able to kill you with a snap of my fingers. I know you believe I can do that for a mere Great Sage just kicked your ass.</p>
<p>Imagine me becoming a Transcendent or an Ascension Stage expert. We both wouldn’t want that to happen, so take my advice and run.</p>
<p>If you run now, the Hand wouldn’t be able to do much to you. Who knows, we might even become friends the next time we meet."</p>
<p>"Clock is ticking, Master," Zareth said, making Kent raise his thumbs up acknowledging his warning.</p>
<p>"Become a good person, Protector Navarro. You have what it takes to transcend one day." Kent tapped his forehead, and using his soul, he placed a seal on him.</p>
<p>The moment he released the seal, Protector Navarro fainted, and then his body vanished. Kent stood up and looked around him</p>
<p>"Let this be known to all—that I, Kent Madson, your very own Swordsman Slash Alchemist—came, saw, and conquered.</p>
<p>I will return again, and when that happens, you all will know what real horror looks like." The words sent a chill down everyone’s spine.</p>
<p>"Just kidding. I will only make those who come against me or any of my allies feel the pain. Also, don’t look too shocked.</p>
<p>I will only be taking one Kingdom Protector today."</p>
<p>Kent extended his index finger, forcing a golden lightning bolt to shoot from it, piercing Protector Han’s forehead and killing him instantly.</p>
<p>His body vanished from the maw of the serpent Zareth was standing on and reappeared inside the Necro Forge.</p>
<p>In that same moment, far from Kent and Zareth, Protector Vari dodged a sudden arrow that came from her left.</p>
<p>However, her battle with Protector Nathan—the strongest of the four Protectors—had proven to be a dangerous one.</p>
<p>Thankfully, she won.</p>
<p>But her left leg was broken and poisoned, making her lose her balance when the arrow returned, stabbing toward her heart.</p>
<p>Knowing she was on her last leg, she closed her eyes as she waited for her death.</p>
<p>However, just when the arrow was a few inches from her, a small dark hole opened on her chest, swallowing the arrow.</p>
<p>Then, from thin air, Veydris appeared, looking toward the direction the arrow came from. Protector Vari opened her eyes, realizing she was still alive.</p>
<p>Of course, when her eyes landed on the metallic knight, she knew Kent had come to her rescue. Knowing this, she surrendered to her fatigue and fell back.</p>
<p>However, before she could hit the floor, Veydris appeared behind her and caught her. Strangely, his metallic knight armor vanished, leaving only a metallic yet leather-like armor around his body.</p>
<p>His height was also reduced, taking the form of an adult human.</p>
<p>The moment he caught her, they vanished into the tower, appearing in the Harem Tower, where the ladies quickly surrounded her and took her from Veydris, who vanished back to the Realm World.</p>
<p>Lilian quickly used her ice element to cast a few healing spells, healing herself.</p>
<p>The Peak Sovereign, who fired the arrow, became alarmed when his attack was neutralized. That was a call for concern.</p>
<p>"We need to leave now," the Core Formation expert who was with Zagan said, grabbing his hand. He knew the situation had suddenly changed, so they must run.</p>
<p>"Oh, but you first have to hear what my master wants to say." Zareth appeared in the air, with the Coil Void Dragon and the Serpent hovering behind him.</p>
<p>His hands were behind his back, yet the pressure falling down on the two made Zagan start bleeding from his nose.</p>
<p>"Dark Elves. How interesting," Kent said, walking toward them with his hands in his pockets.</p>
<p>"Are you two here to spy on me or the entire continent?" Kent asked, stopping a meter away from them.</p>
<p>This distance was intentional—just enough so he could escape if needed, considering the Core Formation expert with Zagan was far more powerful than Protector Navarro.</p>
<p>The only reason Zareth had managed to trap him was because he was using the power of the Coiled Void Dragon and the Alabaster Poison Serpent.</p>
<p>And yes, the two creatures are real, not made using spiritual or abyssal energy. They also, from the look of things, are at the core formation stage.</p>
<p>Kent didn’t know where they came from, but after the battle, he sure would learn more about them.</p>
<p>So, Kent was keeping his distance just in case. He could tell the general was already gathering energy behind him to attack.</p>
<p>"Don’t be alarmed. I don’t plan on attacking you just yet. You’re part of the Hand, so send my message to them."</p>
<p>The two became visibly shocked when they heard him. They said nothing, as no one except the organisation’s top leaders knew their identities. Hearing Kent identify them was a shock.</p>
<p>"No need to look shocked. I know you are Zagan Ty Derius, and you are General Kaelreth Darksoul."</p>
<p>First, they were alarmed—now, they were afraid of the youngster smiling at them.</p>
<p>If only they knew all Kent had to do was stare at them, and their details would appear before him like a report. The enchanted eye isn’t without its perks.</p>
<p>Kent can easily know anything he wants, provided he is willing to know. So, looking at him with a shocked expression isn’t something he cares too much about.</p>
<p>"I know a whole lot about you two to the extend I knew you cheated on your wife with your big brothers wife" Kent smile.</p>
<p>"You are one nasty fellow, Zagan"</p>
<p>Sometimes, the enchanted eye can reveal more than he wanted to know.</p>
