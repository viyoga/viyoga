<p>As expected, when the Sky Fox learned they would be migrating, they all became thrilled. Finally, their years of torment and torture would come to an end.</p>
<p>They used to live in lavish houses, eat proper food, and enjoy peace of mind when their Priestess was alive. However, after the Sky Priestess died, their life turned upside down.</p>
<p>The humans and Beasts ruled them with an iron fist, depriving them of the peace and tranquillity they once had. Had it not been for Yuyin’s bravery, this partial peace they had for the past 5,000 years wouldn’t have been possible.</p>
<p>Now, however, they are about to move in with their Princess, who left, running for her life from her own father. Of course, in the past, most of them hated the idea of a human being their Princess.</p>
<p>However, when they learned she was the one who killed their oppressors and that the dragon that came with her was the infamous Kent Madson, everyone couldn’t contain their happiness.</p>
<p>They readily started packing their loads to move into their new home.</p>
<p>Meanwhile, Liora and Kent ended their quick kiss when Yuyin returned and took Liora away to meet some of the Sky Fox.</p>
<p>She had been away for a long time after all.</p>
<p>Kent, on the other hand, used the time to enter the tower and chat with Gaia, who had already started overseeing the building of the new city for the Sky Foxes.</p>
<p>Kent knew he had no tower points left to use for the Floating City project. The last time he checked, he owned 1.2 billion in tower points.</p>
<p>Of course, if everything were calculated, what he owned would be less than 500 million tower points. However, after Gaia met Li Hua, she changed and began to love money.</p>
<p>So his debt is now in the billions. But despite his debt, he needs help yet again. The Sky Foxes need a city to stay in, and the only one that can help is Gaia.</p>
<p>When he arrived in the Mountain Garden, the two people he threw there had been locked away in the Tower Prison since Uncle Drew didn’t want the little ones to see the kind of torture Avotha was putting them through.</p>
<p>Gaia and her people are busy at work.</p>
<p>"Big brother, you are here." Seeing Kent appear, Gaia and her minions ran and hugged him. They were happy to see him. In fact, they were always so glad to see him.</p>
<p>Kent also enjoys spending time with them, so he welcomed the hug.</p>
<p>"Yes, I am here. I miss you girls," Kent replied, brushing their little heads. After the hug, Gaia looked at him and smiled.</p>
<p>"I assumed big brother is here to negotiate his debt."</p>
<p>Kent smiled but nodded. This made Gaia grin and manifest an eyeglass. Seeing this, Kent knew he was in trouble.</p>
<p>"Follow me, big brother. The plan is ready, and as usual, it is going to cost you." Kent shook his head, and with a forced smile, he followed her.</p>
<p>Uncle Drew and the demigods could only smile as they watched a legendary figure like Kent get dragged around by a midget whose love for money was insatiable.</p>
<p>When they reached the area where the building plan was, Gaia waved her hand, and a 6-foot scroll opened for Kent to see the plan.</p>
<p>Although he is not an architect, he was able to read the plan. It is a large city located on a floating island. The buildings will be made from jade stones but will also feature modern, even futuristic aesthetics.</p>
<p>Most of the buildings will be floating in the air since the Sky Foxes like to fly around. The ground buildings will also be made to fly into the air if the owner wants to.</p>
<p>In short, the city they had in mind will be magical. However, Kent knew such a design would come at a steep cost, so he was both happy and unhappy at the same time.</p>
<p>Gaia intentionally chose this design so she could swindle him out of money.</p>
<p>Of course, a long time ago, Kent promised her he would let her build the tower into something they both would like. So far, she had done a fantastic job.</p>
<p>But the cost is just too much to handle.</p>
<p>"I assume this is worth a fortune," Kent said, and Gaia smiled, adjusting her glasses. Behind her, the Lolis smiled, making Kent wonder just what kind of debt he would be stepping into now.</p>
<p>"I always strive to give Big Brother the best. So, since this is the time to build the tower city into a magical place, I ought to use the best materials. This, of course, is why the master will need 2 billion tower points for this project.</p>
<p>Of course, since the master is broke, I am willing to give him a loan that expires in 5 months (outside time).</p>
<p>After five months, if the master doesn’t pay 70% of what he owes me, I would be forced to trigger the Gaia Quest, where the master will be forced to work nonstop to pay all he owes me."</p>
<p>Kent furrowed his brow, hearing her threatening words. "Are you threatening me?"</p>
<p>"Of course not. I am merely telling Big Brother the consequences of taking advantage of cosmic beings."</p>
<p>Somewhere at the core of the universe, Lady Universe laughed, feeling amused. Since Gaia is a cosmic being, no harm would come to the universe if she were to make the heart of the universe laugh.</p>
<p>She is also a cosmic being, so no karma is involved.</p>
<p>Kent narrowed his eyes at Gaia, who smiled and extended her small yet cute hands. "Do we have a deal, Big Brother?" She used her other hand to adjust her glasses.</p>
<p>Kent, of course, had no choice but to accept her deal. "You have yourself a deal, Gaia. But if I find out you had scammed me again, then you’d better prepare to be spanked till you cry blood."</p>
<p>Gaia pouted and pulled out her little tongue. She had secured yet another contract to build yet another magical city.</p>
<p>Once she is done, what Kent owes her will amount to 3.2 billion.</p>
<p>Of course, Kent knew the tower points, while useless to him and his wives, cosmic beings have a use for them. So he would work hard to pay his debt.</p>
<p>But getting 3.2 billion tower points now will require a lot of slaughter. ’I’d better get on it fast,’ Kent turned and walked away.</p>
<p>Since the location of the planet Kaelith has not been communicated to Uncle Drew yet, Kent instead went to the Legion Tower to get his legion ready for another expedition into the Planet Killer dungeon.</p>
<p>Every last person inside the tower adds to the tower points whenever they kill, so he knew, for now, they would have to farm tower points for him.</p>
<p>When they saw him enter the Legion Tower, they all became happy.</p>
<p>"Master, are we going on a mission?" Kaizo asked, already jacked and ready for some battle.</p>
<p>"Yes. However, you have to give the ladies 12 more days to be ready. The 5th floor is going to be hard, so you all should prepare well before venturing there."</p>
<p>They nodded, already happy to be back on a mission.</p>
<p>Kent stayed for a few hours checking up on all his legion. Once he was satisfied, he left and went back to Tianxu, where the Sky Foxes were still preparing to migrate.</p>
