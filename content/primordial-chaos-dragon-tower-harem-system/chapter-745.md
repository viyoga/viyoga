<p>Kent was surprised when the eight Half-Elves managed to awaken the soul flame. He expected them to do it within a week, but they did it in three days.</p>
<p>Of course, he knew that the moment they consumed the Spirit Grape Fruit and the Ambrosia Apple, their comprehension speeds increased. But three days were just too much.</p>
<p>However, Kent also learned something new: that to consume the ambrosia apple easily, it is necessary to consume the Spirit Grape Fruit first.</p>
<p>That was why they used only a few hours to finish absorbing the two fruits and awaken their actual soul flames—every last one of them.</p>
<p>However, while they managed to awaken their soul flames, Princess Elowen awakened a rather unique flame known as the Phoenix Ice Flame.</p>
<p>It was an Ice flame, one that burns with absolute coldness.</p>
<p>It was unique and challenging to cultivate.</p>
<p>Consuming the spirits of the flames from Flame Valley would only improve her soul flame by 10%. However, she didn’t need to do much, considering she came out with a Tier 7 soul flame.</p>
<p>This means her soul flame is already a Mystical grade flame, so she doesn’t need to level up now. The only reason she is coming with them is to train and gain experience while refining her skills to a certain level.</p>
<p>Unlike Kent and the other four, who would enter the tower before entering the sect, she will join the sect with an invitation and participate in the exams immediately.</p>
<p>A few kilometers from the valley, Kent stopped the carriage, and they alighted.</p>
<p>"This place always creeps me out when I see it," Norion said, looking at the vast, scorching land waiting for them.</p>
<p>"Same here. The last time I was here, not only did those scums from the Supreme Immortal Sect steal my kill, they even got a flame spirit from it. I nearly died, but they got to walk away with my hard-earned kill."</p>
<p>Elia, the other girl who qualified to enter the Flame Tower, said. She has glowing white hair and serene blue eyes.</p>
<p>Kent listened to their complaints and smiled.</p>
<p>"Don’t worry, if we meet anyone from the sect with an ulterior motive, we will be the ones doing the stealing. That said, I doubt they will be reckless inside the Core Region."</p>
<p>"Are we really going to hunt in the Core Region?" Cyran asked, recalling how they nearly died even in the outer reaches of the Inner Region.</p>
<p>"That is where the big guns are. If we want to enter the Flame Tower and Sect with blazing colors, then we have to take the risk now. Trust me, this is good for all of you."</p>
<p>Kent’s plan may sound noble, but he didn’t come to the Core Region of the Flame Valley just to help the Half-Elves. His main plan was to help them, but also create monsters out of them.</p>
<p>He wanted that so that in the future, even when they part ways, he will continue to benefit from their exploitation.</p>
<p>Kent has unlocked a new feature called [Guardian Hand].</p>
<p>This feature awards him ten slots to add ten people he deems fit from any race he takes under his protection.</p>
<p>All those he adds to the Guardian Hand will be like an extension of his role as a guardian. This means they will become people who will act in his stead when he is away.</p>
<p>But it goes beyond that. These new people he added to his Guardian Hand are like a Wi-Fi connection. Whenever they are in the midst of people, they will be able to siphon their soul essence to him.</p>
<p>In other words, simply being around people allows Kent to temporarily connect his soul thread to them and absorb their soul essence. Of course, it won’t affect those whose soul essence he will be taking.</p>
<p>So, aside from having people to act as guards, he now has people who, one day, if they continue to rise, will be able to gain more from them. Kent knew that to create a universe, he needed to have more than enough soul essence.</p>
<p>This guardian hand is another way to spread his reach on the soul essence.</p>
<p>It was one of those features of the tower he had never known existed but now needed. Ten Guardian Hands from every race he added to his guardian tower meant ten more channels he could use to grow.</p>
<p>And with his infinite amount of resources to make them stronger, Kent knew his growth would be endless if he played his cards right.</p>
<p>"Let’s go. We have a lot to do and limited time to get it done, so we’d better start early." They nodded, and Kent started moving while they followed.</p>
<p>"Errh, Kent, aren’t you going to wear armor?" Arion asked, and Kent smiled.</p>
<p>"I don’t need one."</p>
<p>"Why, are you like a body cultivator or something?" Norion asked in a slightly amused tone, not knowing he was about to learn something new about Kent.</p>
<p>"As a matter of fact, I am a Diamond Body Cultivator," Kent grinned.</p>
<p>All eight half-elves looked at him with calm expressions. A few seconds later, Princess Elowen smiled, "Are you sure you are 21 years old?"</p>
<p>"I had my 21st birthday a few months back."</p>
<p>They could only watch him walk in front with their thoughts hidden in their minds. But every last one of them could only hope that by the time they separated from Kent, they would be able to grow to even half his strength.</p>
<p>After moving through the Outer Region without encountering any flame beasts, they stepped into the Inner Region.</p>
<p>’Alright, Tower, I need the hot spots like we discussed yesterday.’ Kent asked the tower to create a map of the Flame Valley and locate where the beasts with cores and flame spirits were.</p>
<p>They are the dangerous ones, so he wanted to use them to train the half-elves while earning their keep. Thankfully, the Tower was more than capable of doing that.</p>
<p>⟦I managed to locate three sections in the inner section where flame spirits can be harvested. Additionally, I discovered a flame-type herb known as Sunlight Poison Flower.</p>
<p>Consuming it will enhance your poison resistance and strengthen your soul flame as well. Of course, it is guarded by powerful Flame Beasts⟧.</p>
<p>Kent smiled and turned to his team after learning all about the three sections. "We will be exploring the east first. When passing through a week ago, I sensed some powerful auras there. After that, we have three more stops to make before entering the Core Region.</p>
<p>"Aside, Princess Elowen, you all have Tier 4 flames, so this will be a good hunt to strengthen them."</p>
<p>They nodded and started moving eastward.</p>
