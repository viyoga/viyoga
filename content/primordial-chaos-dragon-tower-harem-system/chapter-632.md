<p>[Finally... After five years of audacious death battles, I finally managed to combine ten swords, forming a single powerful sword that can literally cleave through the void and create spatial channels.</p>
<p>I must say, the Transcendent Heavenly Sword Technique is remarkably powerful. I struggle even to begin to fathom how unfathomable it is. I spent every single day thinking about how someone could create something this powerful.</p>
<p>It has seven forms, and even the first two forms helped me defeat beings far stronger than me.</p>
<p>...Oh, I fought another Mountain Flame Goat, a peak Spirit King, but ended it in a single blow using just the first form of the Transcendent Heavenly Sword Technique.</p>
<p>Now that I think about it, the single blow was more like ten three-meter-long swords that were formed using Sword Qi and imbued with the power of sword intent.</p>
<p>Even my heavenly sword aura and intent weren’t up to standard, but it was already enough to accomplish my goal, so this is where I hang the towel and be on my way.</p>
<p>...By the way, ignore what I said years back... If you meet a Mountain Flame Goat, be sure to have your spices ready. Their meat is tender and smells great... hehe.]</p>
<p>Kent chuckled, then closed the diary and stood up. He walked forward and stopped. The next second, he conjured a golden sword, making it appear in the air above him.</p>
<p>Then a second sword appeared, followed by the third. Soon, seven swords hovered above him, but a pale look appeared on his face. A few seconds later, the swords vanished, and Kent took a deep sigh.</p>
<p>"I need to learn Heavenly Sword Energy first; only then can I use all ten swords. But even now, just one sword is already enough to make a difference, and I can conjure seven." A smile appeared on his face.</p>
<p>He walked back and sat down in front of the Sword of Enlightenment. The diary appeared in his hand, and then he continued to read.</p>
<p>The Transcendent Heavenly Sword Technique is rather complex. It requires a great understanding of sword energy.</p>
<p>That was why it took Jest five years to master the first two forms of the technique despite being a Sword Emperor.</p>
<p>But who can blame him? To master the sword technique, Jest needed to advance his understanding of sword energy to some extent.</p>
<p>This would allow him to form ten 3-meter swords. For the second form, he must put all ten swords together to create a 4-meter sword. Then, for the third form, he must form a 4-meter sword.</p>
<p>This means the first form must be mastered to 100 swords, which will give him ten swords at the second form, which will form the one 4-meter-long sword at the third stage.</p>
<p>This alone demonstrates the complexity and incredible power of the technique. But if mastered to the seventh form, then perhaps Kent would learn the full power of the Transcendent Heavenly Sword Technique.</p>
<p>[Ah, it feels good to be back here. Now, all I need to do is use the second move, and I will be on my way to the immortal worlds.</p>
<p>But before I leave, I must first say something... God only knows today will be the last time I will be able to express myself freely.</p>
<p>First, I don’t know why Divine came before Immortal in the sword path, but I intend to find out.</p>
<p>Second, I just want to say that if I ever don’t return to Byrmrr, I would want the heavens to know that this is my origin. So, if I somehow become a legend in the future, remember to preserve my heritage.</p>
<p>You wouldn’t want me coming back to hunt you... hehe, just kidding. But I am serious, so hold Byrmrr dear and we won’t have any issues.</p>
<p>On that note... Immortals, here I come.]</p>
<p>Kent closed the book. However, the moment he did, it disassembled into sparks of light and flew into his mind. The next instant, a powerful, profound sword energy burst forth from his body.</p>
<p>Kent’s consciousness was pulled into the realm of enlightenment. He had just entered his third sword enlightenment.</p>
<p>The first happened after reviewing Jest’s second memory packet, and now, the fourth memory had sent him into an enlightened state. Even as the minutes started to pass, his sword energy seemed to grow.</p>
<p>He is required to have achieved seven enlightenments out of eleven before he can become a Sword Saint. So far, he is on his third, and from what happened, it seems to have something to do with the diary he had read.</p>
<p>A year went by, and Kent remained in his enlightened state. Slowly, the years started passing. Seven years into the Enlightenment, a powerful aura burst out of his body, raising him into the air.</p>
<p>Up in the air, ten golden swords, each three meters long, appeared. Then, slowly, they merged into one four-meter-long sword. It hovered in the air for a few minutes before dispersing.</p>
<p>Kent then opened his eyes, and a smile appeared on his lips. "Looks like all I needed was insight from the real owner of the technique."</p>
<p>During the Enlightenment, he appeared in the Sword Valley, where he met the true Immortal Monarch, Liu Yang, the creator of the Transcendent Heavenly Sword Technique.</p>
<p>He gave him some insight, and then he was required to break the sword move that Jest broke before he could leave the enlightenment.</p>
<p>It took him seven years, but he succeeded as a Sword Grandmaster, while Jest took five years to become a Sword Emperor.</p>
<p>Kent laughed, but he understood that he had only gotten this far thanks to Jest’s determination.</p>
<p>"Looks like the diary is the key to gaining enlightenment. In that case, why not dive into the last memory? This will then leave me with seven more memories of Jest to go through." He was ecstatic to know more, so he took out the last key.</p>
<p>That one, too, turned into a diary. The same title was inscribed on it: "Jest Diary 2/9."</p>
<p>"Here we go again." Reading the diary made Kent very happy, so he couldn’t wait to read more.</p>
