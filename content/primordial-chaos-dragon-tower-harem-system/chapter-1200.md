<p>Laziel and the rest of the Night Riders stood frozen, stunned, and bewildered.</p>
<p>The reveal just now is more than they can handle.</p>
<p>None of them was prepared for what Unle Drew said.</p>
<p>First, they learned that their master, the one person who guided them and led them to many victories while protecting them from the shadows, was actually the Primordial Chaos Dragon - a being of legend.</p>
<p>They had heard and discovered many interesting things about the Chaos Dragon to the point that even some of them aspired to become like him.</p>
<p>That is somewhat true for the dragons in the Night Riders. They see the Chaos Dragon as their true ancestor, and so they worship him.</p>
<p>However, as if that wasn’t enough, they also learned that he was once the greatest swordsman to walk the face of the universe.</p>
<p>Most of them, especially the sword users in the Night Riders team, knew of that legendary sword master.</p>
<p>Lord Genesis. The Domain Sword King. Endless Sword King. Infinity Sword King.</p>
<p>Even Uncle Drew admires Lord Genesis. He was born three eras after the legendary Lord Genesis walked the face of the universe.</p>
<p>As a swordsman, he knows his master’s past incarnation laid the foundation that every swordsman is walking on now. He had studied him, and thanks to him, he became a legendary warrior, creating his own legend.</p>
<p>However, that wasn’t enough either. He was also the Faceless Assassin—the most elusive killer to ever appear in this universe.</p>
<p>They know of him, and they adore his methods, for he was the only assassin nobody saw coming. He perfectly portrayed the true essence of an assassin.</p>
<p>But to hit them with the hardest reveal of all, the one who made it possible for these legendary figures to once grace the universe with their majestic selves was actually the most wanted person in the universe at the moment.</p>
<p>The seven Demigods came from the land of the living and had heard of Kent Madson. Even down in hell, his name had reached many ears.</p>
<p>So this reveal took them by surprise.</p>
<p>As for Laziel, he knew of Kent because he told him his true identity when he conquered the 6th level of hell millions of years ago.</p>
<p>Of course, he had no idea he had spent several days with his master and didn’t even recognize him. Well, it isn’t his fault. It wasn’t like Kent had the same appearance in all the many lives he had lived.</p>
<p>But then again, knowing his master was the current ruler of the 6th level of hell also came as a shock.</p>
<p>"This is wild for sure. I never knew the brat who nearly slaughtered every hell demon here was actually our one and only Master," Laziel said with a happy smile.</p>
<p>"Of course, it now makes sense. His brutality back then mirrors the Ruthless Bandit now that I know his true identity."</p>
<p>"This is crazy. If the universe knew that the Ruthless Bandit was actually all these legendary figures, they would flip and scream their souls out."</p>
<p>Uncle Drew stood on the side watching his teammates get accustomed to the reveal he had just dropped on them. Kent is a legend, and it was only right that they knew their master is no loser.</p>
<p>Since he is weaker than they are this time around, he knew they would bully him for sure. But until then, he planned on planting the seed of respect in them before they met him.</p>
<p>"Captain, have you found some of our brothers and sisters?"</p>
<p>"I did. I found three of them. The Veil Lady is one of them."</p>
<p>"Holy smokes. The Veil Lady is already reborn, huh. I guess she is already a god, right?"</p>
<p>Uncle Drew chuckled, hearing the question. "She is still a mortal like me, but much weaker. Of course, that is only temporary since I will be unlocking her seal soon.</p>
<p>But aside from her, I also found Eagle Eye, who now goes by Saden, and the Hell Summoner, who is now known as Eryndor."</p>
<p>"Please tell me he didn’t join any cult this time around."</p>
<p>Uncle Drew just smiled but said nothing.</p>
<p>"Unbelievable. That bastard always likes to join insidious groups just for fun."</p>
<p>In the past, Eryndor was also a member of a cult. However, when Uncle Drew found him and gave him a purpose, he became one of their strongest fighters.</p>
<p>He can summon abominations straight from the deepest levels of hell and unleash them on battlefields. Sometimes, he alone is needed to end battles.</p>
<p>In the many battles they fought, he played a pivotal role.</p>
<p>So despite his flaws, they knew when the time came, he would rise to the occasion. "I can’t wait to meet him and bully him as he did me in the past."</p>
<p>"Me too."</p>
<p>Laziel also smiled and nodded, "Count me in."</p>
<p>"Well, you will get your chance. The young master wants a family of immortals wiped. They captured and imprisoned his friend and kept torturing him every passing second, so he wanted them dead.</p>
<p>He is currently busy, and since he is not yet an immortal, going against thousands of immortals and even a few Demigods is something he can’t handle now.</p>
<p>So I will need two of you to come with me since I can only take three people, and the master wants me to bring a friend of his living here."</p>
<p>"Who?" Laziel asked, since he is the ruler of all of hell.</p>
<p>"A dragon named Avotha."</p>
<p>"Oh, I know him. We have dueled a few times in the past. He is one scary dude..."</p>
<p>"Well, the master wants me to bring him to the land of the living. But since you are here now, I will take two of you with me."</p>
<p>"As for you, Laziel, you must go to the Dark City and bring the others."</p>
<p>"How about guarding the veil?"</p>
<p>"That won’t be needed. The creatures of the Dark had already been wiped out."</p>
<p>"By who?"</p>
<p>"One of Master’s women."</p>
<p>Yet again, the eight Night Riders were left stunned. First, it was their master; now it is his wives. Just how monstrous can one person be?</p>
<p>But Uncle Drew is right. The last of the Dark Creatures were wiped out by the legendary Rakshasa, who was also known back then as Shara, the Mother of Killers.</p>
<p>It was the last battle she ever fought.</p>
<p>"Take Vargus and Lene with you. The rest will make their way to the land of the living on their own. Who knows, maybe you will even meet them soon, since I learnt the Chaos Rift will be opening soon.</p>
<p>Since they are all Demigods, they will definitely enter. Even I will send an avatar."</p>
<p>"Sounds like a nice arrangement."</p>
<p>"Indeed. Just tell Master to hurry and ascend. He would want to enter the Chaos Rift."</p>
<p>Uncle Drew nodded and then turned to Vargus and Lene.</p>
<p>Lene is a redhead beauty. Much calmer and quieter than Vargus. However, looking at those red eyes, she is someone to be feared.</p>
<p>"Be ready to move. Once I am done with what I came here for, we will leave."</p>
<p>They nodded. A few minutes later, Uncle Drew left and headed toward the city where Bilad and Avotha were waiting for him.</p>
