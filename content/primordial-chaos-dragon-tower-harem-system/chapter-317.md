<p>Uncle Drew had a chat with Kent yesterday after he found out what Kent did to those who attacked him and his ladies.</p>
<p>He then asked to handle the second day if they came again.</p>
<p>Kent just said okay and gave him the go-ahead to summon the battle doll whenever he wanted. He then moved inside the tower and started having fun with his ladies.</p>
<p>Not knowing, on the outside, 16 Root Great Sages and 2 Transcendents came after him. One was a Level Four Transcendent, and the other was Level Two.</p>
<p>They were all from the same camp, and just like what happened the day before, none of them walked out alive.</p>
<p>Well, some didn’t die, but none of them had the authority to leave.</p>
<p>Uncle Drew took care of them using all his strength. Right now, it’s 8 a.m. outside, and inside the master suite where Kent and his ladies were supposed to live, Uncle Drew could be seen meditating.</p>
<p>The metal doll was standing behind him with 10 dead bodies littered on the floor. None of them seemed to mind, but the moment Kent appeared, he waved his hand, and the bodies vanished.</p>
<p>"How did it go, Uncle Drew?" Kent asked, causing Uncle Drew to open his eyes and smile.</p>
<p>"It went better than I had hoped, Young Master. I managed to get two Transcendents and six Great Sages before I ran out of energy. The rest were killed by Titan," Uncle Drew said, causing Kent to raise an eyebrow.</p>
<p>"Where are they, then?" he asked.</p>
<p>He only counted 10 bodies, so where are the rest?</p>
<p>"They are in hell, Young Master," Uncle Drew smiled and stood up.</p>
<p>"Hell?" Kent looked puzzled. "Just what are they doing there?"</p>
<p>"I am turning them into hell demons. I am using the flames of hell and the authority I have as the captain of the Night Riders to turn them into hell demons, something only Night Riders can do."</p>
<p>"Wait a minute, are you saying you will be getting two Transcendents and six Great Sages as subordinates?" Kent asked, almost jumping up in joy.</p>
<p>"Yes, Young Master. All hell demons I create will be loyal to me," Uncle Drew was smiling as he said those words.</p>
<p>"This is good, then," Kent was also happy for him. He meant that if he were to add more battle dolls, he could rescue the two ladies sooner rather than later.</p>
<p>"I will be leaving after I take you back to the academy. I need to ensure the ritual goes perfectly," Uncle Drew said, and Kent nodded.</p>
<p>"If you need anything, don’t hesitate to ask," Kent said, and Uncle Drew smiled.</p>
<p>"You want to join the auction today, huh?" Kent could tell he wanted to do that. They are connected on the soul level, so he could feel it.</p>
<p>"There is something they will be auctioning tomorrow that I need," Uncle Drew said, making his reason for wanting to join the auction known.</p>
<p>"How did you know there was something there for you?" Kent asked. Even Alina doesn’t know what will be auctioned until the very last hour before the auction.</p>
<p>So then, how did he know?</p>
<p>"I sensed it when I dropped you off yesterday, so I checked it out later in the evening, and I know they will auction it today.</p>
<p>And don’t worry, not even the Root Ascension expert there wouldn’t have suspected a thing, even if they were in the room when I entered."</p>
<p>Kent sighed, shaking his head.</p>
<p>"I really should find a way to learn more about you, Night Riders."</p>
<p>Uncle Drew smiled.</p>
<p>If he were to tell Kent that he had to kill himself just so he could use the power of the dead to infiltrate the auction, Kent would probably open his mouth wide and stare at him like he was some kind of demon god.</p>
<p>"Go back to the tower for the meditation instead. We will leave within the hour, so recover as much as you can."</p>
<p>"Okay," Uncle Drew said, then vanished into the Legion Tower and began recovering.</p>
<p>Kent informed Alina about Uncle Drew joining the auction. Since he and his ladies would be occupied, he didn’t want to put Uncle Drew through that, so he would have his own VIP box.</p>
<p>Alina was put in a tight spot, but Kent told her to assign him to the VIP box meant for the Malric. She asked why, but Kent just told her to relax, as news would be reaching her soon.</p>
<p>Akira told him that Saden and her team would strike soon, and based on what she said, along with the sudden jump in his tower points, some people wouldn’t be making it to the auction.</p>
<p>"Time to multiply my wealth," Kent grinned and sat down. He used to have 3.2 trillion spirit stones, but he had used quite a lot and was now left with 2.7 trillion. Still, he could be considered very rich.</p>
<p>But it’s better to have more and worry about how to spend it.</p>
<p>"If things go well and I gain something I can use for another undead, I will pour 1 trillion spirit stones into it. Since I am more than rich, I can only use my wealth to gain power too."</p>
<p>He took out the two bingo cards.</p>
<p>They each could multiply their money by 1x, 2x, 4x, 6x... up to 20x. All he had to do was pump his energy into the card, and the number that appeared would multiply his wealth.</p>
<p>He channelled his energy into the first card, and within seconds, a number appeared, bringing a smile to Kent’s face.</p>
<p>"4 times, huh? Not bad for my luck." His wealth increased by 4, making him 10.8 trillion rich. He retrieved the next card and raised it up.</p>
<p>"I better get something cool so I can show off on the last day," Kent said before channelling his energy into it. The next second, the number appeared, and his smile widened.</p>
<p>"That’s what I’m talking about!" The battle doll was staring at him blankly.</p>
<p>"14 times," Kent burst into resounding laughter, making him get ready.</p>
<p>With 150 trillion-plus in spirit stones, he is perhaps the richest person in the entire world.</p>
