<p>While Kent was deep asleep, working on awakening his Soul Path, his ladies were also becoming monstrous over time.</p>
<p>So far, Zaila had already climbed to the 930th step after breaking the limit on her mind and body. It was a huge leap for her. But she wasn’t the only one making progress.</p>
<p>Elsa had already climbed to the 790th step and had become a Diamond Body cultivator, so her next target was the Sage Body stage. Slowly, she was nearing that huge threshold.</p>
<p>Meanwhile, Ingrid and Neomi were also steadily climbing. Ingrid took a few months to ensure her beast companion, whom she decided to call Mini, was also acquainted with the Golden Titan Stairway.</p>
<p>Since Mini couldn’t take on a human form due to her Void lineage, Ingrid helped her climb to the 600th step, which took just seven months.</p>
<p>Thankfully, Mini adapted and could now handle it on her own, so Ingrid joined her sisters to climb while they waited for the rest of their sisters to emerge.</p>
<p>Next to come was Selene, who awakened a Lunar Space, to the amazement of everyone. It was then that they all knew—aside from the Mind and Soul, there were other spaces out there.</p>
<p>The Lunar Space was one beautiful place, even more beautiful than Zaila’s Mind Space. It had a mesmerizing design featuring many gardens filled with some of the most beautiful creatures in the universe.</p>
<p>There were beautiful birds, unicorns, centaurs, and even dragons. However, every one of these creatures was made from Lunar energy, unlike Zaila’s own, which were real.</p>
<p>Selene speculated that they had a purpose, and even a few could communicate with her verbally. But unlike Zaila, who had a technique to summon her creatures to fight for her, Selene didn’t have that.</p>
<p>But that wasn’t too bad. She had her Tier 17 Primordial Spirit regress to Tier 1, so her Lunar Space had become so vast that she had yet to understand all the new changes occurring within it.</p>
<p>Not that she had to worry about that. The Lunar beings inside her Lunar Space would do all the work. She was amazed by how the world appeared within her Lunar Space.</p>
<p>Perhaps they should call it more like a Primordial World, capable of sustaining life. In short, Selene too had become a monster, which made Kent even more dangerous.</p>
<p>His wives were growing, and it was for the best.</p>
<p>She also joined her sisters on the Golden Titan Stairway. Elsa had already reached the 810th step and, thankfully, had become a Sage Body cultivator.</p>
<p>The treasures they received at every 100 steps climbed were doing wonders. They all wanted to be at least the 4th stage, which in this case was 1,200 steps, before Kent emerged from seclusion.</p>
<p>They wanted to surprise him, so they were working hard, and it was already starting to show. Ingrid also became a Sage Body when she stepped on the 800th step and received her treasure.</p>
<p>A few months after Selene came out of the Primordial Space, Alina walked out with a cheerful smile on her face. Her sisters stopped what they were doing to see what she had gained.</p>
<p>They always stopped climbing the stairs whenever a new sister emerged from their Primordial chamber.</p>
<p>Alina awakened Grid Space. She already possessed an ancient energy that she incorporated into her arrays and formations, known as the Eternal Grid. It turned out to go beyond just energy.</p>
<p>Her Grid Space had everything to do with arrays and formations. There were trees inside her Grid Space that had drawings of ancient arrays and formations on them.</p>
<p>The stones even contained some complex arrays that she could use whenever she wanted.</p>
<p>The Grid Space was, in actuality, one massive formation drawn from the Eternal Grid itself. One of the best parts was that when she awakened the Grid Space, her Primordial Spirit used the time difference in the tower to draw thousands of arrays and formations before regressing from Tier 17 to Tier 1.</p>
<p>Of course, one could say she and her primordial spirit could communicate, and later Alina herself could draw them. However, there was one thing about Alina’s new overpowered abilities.</p>
<p>Anything drawn by her Primordial Spirit could be activated with a snap of her finger. This meant the thousands of arrays her Primordial Spirit created were just a snap away from being activated.</p>
<p>Also, with a snap, she could replicate them without having to draw every last symbol again.</p>
<p>This was why, when she came out of the Primordial Chamber, the first thing she did was see Elaine’s parents, and with a snap of her finger, she broke the curse restricting Elaine’s father’s bloodline.</p>
<p>She even planted an array on Lady Rain’s soul. This was so she could give her a Life Magic constitution when her Primordial Spirit rises back to Tier 15.</p>
<p>Inside her Grid Space were thousands of unique constitutions she could give to anyone she wanted.</p>
<p>The only downside was that, unless she became a Grid Emperor, which would allow her to draw Tier 15 to 17 arrays, she would have to wait for her Primordial Spirit to rise back to Tier 15 before she could give Lady Rain a unique constitution.</p>
<p>The arrays for such things ranged from Tier 15 upwards.</p>
<p>But they were unique and from the Eternal Grid Codex itself, so unless the heavens intervened, Lady Rain would soon have unique abilities she could rely on to cultivate herbs like her husband.</p>
<p>But that could wait.</p>
<p>Lady Rain was just happy that her husband could now rise as a Life Magician, whose dream was to become a powerful healer who would handle the injuries of the people inside the tower.</p>
<p>Alina herself was just too happy. She could now draw Tier 12 arrays.</p>
<p>And soon, she could handle Tier 13. Then, when she surpassed Tier 14, she would become an Array Emperor, which in her case meant she would be called the Grid Emperor.</p>
<p>Then again, that too could wait for now.</p>
<p>Although she did not need a strong body to draw arrays, she also wanted to improve her body cultivation since their big sister Vexthra had told them it would come in handy when becoming a god.</p>
<p>After Alina finished all that, she joined her sisters on the stairs.</p>
