<p>"Such a pity, I never knew Spirit Kings would be this weak," Kent said, shaking his head. He had already endured close to ten minutes of beatings from the Spirit Kings, and now, even their punches weren’t hurting him anymore.</p>
<p>He knew this had something to do with his body tempering, so he hadn’t paid much attention to the pain from the beginning, but now that he had stopped feeling the pain, he knew what needed to be done.</p>
<p>"Time for you two to die." Kent blinked, and the next second, he was 4 km away from the two Spirit Kings. Then the void cracked, and four sword arcs flew out.</p>
<p>These four attacks were from the second form of the [Annihilation Sword] technique, [Void Cleave]. This form of the technique depended heavily on the distance between Kent and his targets.</p>
<p>And since he moved back 4 km, the distance was rather vast, so when the attack emerged from the void, it began gathering power from the 4 km it had to travel.</p>
<p>The Spirit Kings at first didn’t sense anything, but when the attack was 1 km away from them, fear pressed down on them.</p>
<p>This forced them to move and evade the attack. However, to Kent’s shock, something he never expected to see happened.</p>
<p>The Spirit Kings couldn’t move their bodies, which was shocking considering they are three realms above Kent.</p>
<p>However, they couldn’t move, so they were forced to defend themselves. However, when the attack landed on them, the shield they created was severed in half, and the powerful force behind the Void Cleave landed squarely on their bodies.</p>
<p>Boom.</p>
<p>They were hurled through the air, landing hard on the ground. One of them lost both hands since he crossed them as a form of defense. His body was battered and bruised all over, and his chest was cut open.</p>
<p>The other died from having half of his body cut off. He managed to die with one arm intact since he didn’t use both hands to defend himself.</p>
<p>Kent appeared beside the barely alive Spirit King and drove a [Doom Shard] through his head, killing him instantly.</p>
<p>"Just two more to go," Kent smiled and gestured for Aetheris to send two more.</p>
<p>Far away, Queen Amorian wore a shocked expression when she saw the outcome of Kent’s single attack. She wondered just what could be so powerful that Spirit Kings couldn’t even defend against it.</p>
<p>However, looking at their dead bodies, she knew even she would have struggled to defend against it. And she was probably right.</p>
<p>The Void Cleave attack was one that Kent had yet to determine how far it could go. In fact, he had yet to determine if the distance could extend as far as several hundred miles.</p>
<p>So far, the farthest he had gone was 4 km, and the results were devastating, so he wanted to see how far and how strong this sword skill could be.</p>
<p>Aetheris released two more Spirit Kings, leaving only four, with three minutes before the four would be freed. The battle had been going on for 27 minutes already, so he was nearing the 30 minutes he could hold them.</p>
<p>Kent knew what had to be done, so he didn’t let the two Spirit Kings play around. Well, one of them started to run when he was released, causing Kent to smirk.</p>
<p>He didn’t chase after him; instead, he used a soul attack on him, shaking his soul. That split second of pain that went through his body made Kent grin.</p>
<p>"You don’t really think I will let you run, do you?" Kent opened the Void, and hundreds of attacks rained down on the bastard.</p>
<p>His shamelessness was just too much. For such a powerful person to choose to run—that was just too shameful.</p>
<p>So Kent sent him away under the rain of attacks from the Void. The twelve days he spent inside the tower weren’t for play. He had saved thousands of attacks in the Void that he was now using to handle the Spirit Kings.</p>
<p>After the fourth Spirit King died, the second started to beg. However, Kent didn’t listen and rained down attacks on him as well.</p>
<p>The moment the fifth Spirit King fell under his assault, Kent felt his body start to grow stronger. His mind remained the same, but as for his soul, he just couldn’t comprehend how monstrous he was becoming.</p>
<p>The process didn’t happen instantly. It was as if things were changing inside his body, so he let it happen, and slowly, his strength continued to grow.</p>
<p>"Release them all, Aetheris." Kent was on a whole new level of strength, so with only a minute left, he asked for the Hellhounds to be freed.</p>
<p>When they were freed, all four of them attacked him.</p>
<p>However, he stood there smiling at them. Just when they were a meter from him, Kent’s sword appeared, and his Profound Sword Intent and Qi burst out, pushing them back.</p>
<p>The sword came from the sheath, and before the four could understand what was happening, Kent executed a series of attacks that created thin lines of sword arcs that cleaved through their bodies.</p>
<p>Two were able to react quickly, coating their bodies with their energy. However, the other two were not so fortunate and succumbed to the attack.</p>
<p>It was a deadly move he learned from Jest.</p>
<p>He never had profound sword intent back, so he never tried it, just like how he never used the ’Limitless Sword Arc Domain’ skill again until he became a Sword Saint.</p>
<p>Kent looked toward the two who sustained some damage and was about to attack when Skoggr, the Orc Chieftain, spoke to him through telepathy.</p>
<p>’We have arrived at the location, Master.’</p>
<p>Kent nodded and turned to Aetheris. "They are all yours."</p>
<p>The Flame Brute smiled and reduced his size to eight feet. "Shall we dance?" Just like that, the dance of flames started as Aetheris began bombarding the two living Spirit Kings with powerful flame attacks.</p>
<p>Kent looked at his ladies, who were now helping Veera and Nykali handle the Spirit Kings that Kent had assigned to them. He then looked at Veydris and Irina, but they were doing fine, considering Valtor had made sure they had the upper hand.</p>
<p>Sword King Shawn was also doing fine, although he had sustained some injuries, he would live.</p>
<p>Kent smiled and vanished. The next second, he appeared on a mountaintop where Skoggr, the Orc Chieftain, Gorzak, the Barbarian Orc King, and Kazarak, the Savage Warden, were.</p>
<p>Ten kilometers away stood a large camp housing thousands of Lionkin soldiers...</p>
