<p>The Hand, one of the most dangerous secret organisations in the world, had just been disrespected in the most shocking way.</p>
<p>Their enemy delivered a message to them using their own member.</p>
<p>That could not be allowed to stand.</p>
<p>The one who delivered the message knew that, and that was why he was damn afraid of the outcome. Thankfully, he was allowed to leave the island with his life.</p>
<p>But that didn’t mean everything was forgiven just like that.</p>
<p>On the contrary, they were pissed as hell.</p>
<p>They already knew what happened long before General Kaelreth delivered Kent’s message, so now, hearing Kent’s clear declaration of war, they most definitely wouldn’t let it slide.</p>
<p>"What are your orders, my lady?" the monkey-faced masked figure asked.</p>
<p>"He must die," the white-haired lady said in an icy tone.</p>
<p>"I don’t know how he knew we were after the keys. He even knew what it does and what we intend to do with it. This alone is enough to know he is not someone we can underestimate or have running around for long."</p>
<p>"I will go to Vastport City right away and kill him, my lady," the tiger-faced masked figure said, clenching his fist.</p>
<p>Though their expressions couldn’t be seen, considering they were blocking their faces with masks, their body language clearly indicated that they were furious.</p>
<p>All seven of them.</p>
<p>They were the Seven Fangs of the Hand.</p>
<p>All over the world, those who knew of their existence could be counted on the single hand. Nobody knew who they were, what they looked like, or even their names.</p>
<p>The only thing they can be known for is that they were just the culprits whenever something huge happened.</p>
<p>Powerful people died by their hands, and the Hand is what it is now because of them. Rumors even suggest that most kings are on their strings, controlled like puppets.</p>
<p>It wouldn’t be an exaggeration to say they are beings that could easily erase this world overnight.</p>
<p>So, for Kent to get on their timeline, it was safe to say they wouldn’t let this slide regardless of how Kent saw it.</p>
<p>They were not afraid of any king or queen, so Kent wasn’t someone they would be afraid of.</p>
<p>But there was someone they were afraid of, and it was the leader of the Hand. The lower members of the organisation called her the Hand, while the seven of them called her my lady.</p>
<p>Those who could rival her strength were just a few in the world. Of course, as to whether they could kill her, only the stars could tell, for she was one terrifying lady.</p>
<p>She was just a Level 3 Core Formation expert, yet the seven before her could not fathom her strength, regardless of how they measured it.</p>
<p>Her existence was one nobody could fathom.</p>
<p>So, regardless of their thoughts, they first asked for her permission before making any move.</p>
<p>"You personally going against him would be a waste. We have more important things to worry about; going after a kid isn’t one of them.</p>
<p>Also, I am not ready yet, so we can only let him have his way for now. But that doesn’t mean we should make his life easy. It would be best if he understood that offending us comes with a huge price," the lady said.</p>
<p>She looked calm on the surface, but the energy surrounding her wasn’t that calm. It was as if she wanted to rip Kent to shreds.</p>
<p>"We are ready to listen to your orders, my lady," the bat-masked figure said.</p>
<p>"Activate the Shadows. Let them hunt down every last person who ever worked for the Alderfords. They must kill them and hang their bodies upside down at the entrance of every city.</p>
<p>They must brand the bodies to send a clear message to him and everyone. Every last one of them must be killed.</p>
<p>Second, send a message to the Ocean Master. Tell him whenever Kent Madson travels across the Black Sea, they must see him as their worst enemy and should not let him pass.</p>
<p>Also, activate all agents in the Ancient Battlefield of Parting. Let them inform every last stronghold there that Kent Madson is in possession of the bodies of two Core Formation experts and a Domain Crystal.</p>
<p>Also, inform the other continents of his presence and make sure they understand he is a flight risk that cannot be entertained.</p>
<p>Be sure to make them understand, especially the dragons. Also, inform the Oracles. Tell them their enemy is Kent Madson. I am sure they will be happy to take him out for us.</p>
<p>Then feel free to inform the Dark Devil Church, too. They are mere pawns, but they have some deep roots. Tell them the one in possession of the Atomic Divine Body is Kent Madson.</p>
<p>Lastly, inform the Hunter.</p>
<p>Let her know a prey is on the loose. She is free to use any means to kill him and anyone he comes into contact with," she spoke with a straight face, but every word that came out of her mouth said it all.</p>
<p>She was determined to see Kent dead, and thus, she was willing to go that extra length to make Kent’s life miserable.</p>
<p>"My lady, what about the beings in the Dark Forest?" the hound-faced masked figure asked.</p>
<p>"No. Even I cannot measure their strength, so we must let them be for safety. If they sense the threat, they will make their move.</p>
<p>Under no circumstances should any of you interfere with those beings."</p>
<p>"Okay, my lady."</p>
<p>"That will be all. Ensure every last person I mentioned learns of Kent Madson. We can only make him popular since he wants to be popular."</p>
<p>The seven masked figures nodded and then vanished from the garden.</p>
<p>The lady poured herself a cup of tea and took a sip.</p>
<p>"Kent Madson. Though you are a huge variable, I cannot let you interfere in my business just yet. What I am after is more important than you can imagine, so handle the hand I’ve dealt you for now. There is more pain on the way."</p>
<p>She took out a token from her space ring and then channeled her energy into it.</p>
<p>"The variable has appeared. Ensure he dies before the gate opens." She placed the token back into her space ring and then walked away, entering her meditation chamber.</p>
<p>Meanwhile, far in the Demon Continent, a figure opened his eyes, radiating a presence that could easily trump the seven masked figures combined.</p>
<p>"Finally, I get to roam." He vanished with his sinister presence.</p>
<p>Kent, who knew nothing of what was happening currently, had his arms around the hips of a goddess, bouncing her on his dragon rod like there was no tomorrow.</p>
<p>A few hours later, his time to go back to the mortal realm had reached.</p>
