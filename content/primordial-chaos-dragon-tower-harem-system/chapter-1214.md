<p>When the voice called, Kent felt something snap in him.</p>
<p>It was as if that voice had called him countless times before, and every time, he was annoyed by it. However, Kent, as much as he would love to say he recognised that voice, his mind was blank.</p>
<p>The voice was a reflection of itself, all jumbled together to form something new.</p>
<p>He looked up, and from one of the mirrors, Enzi flew out. However, while the previous Enzi was undoubtedly beautiful, this version of her was an angel—literally.</p>
<p>She had three pairs of angel wings made of glass, milky-white gold hair falling down her back, and a body that would cause gods to fall to their doom.</p>
<p>However, while Enzi smiled at him, Kent saw right through it and knew she wasn’t the one in control of her body. The smile looked more twisted than it should be.</p>
<p>That, coupled with the fact that she called him ’bastard’, told him what he needed to know...She isn’t his Enzi.</p>
<p>"Aurelion Vaelros, you look much more dragon than before," the one in control of Enzi’s body said, her voice echoing millions of times, each echo making Kent want to smash his head against the countless mirrors.</p>
<p>The haunting effect of her voice started to get to him.</p>
<p>He narrowed his gaze at the person and asked in a concerned tone, "Possessing the body of my wife is not something I take kindly to. So maybe introduce yourself first before attempting to sound friendly."</p>
<p>The lady turned her head sideways, a squint appearing on her face. "Oh, so I am your wife now, huh. Finally, it seems I have managed to worm my way into your dark and twisted heart."</p>
<p>Kent narrowed his gaze even further, hearing what the lady said. "So you are Enzi’s past self."</p>
<p>"Duh."</p>
<p>Kent didn’t say anything. For some reason, he felt like he had been conspired against by Enzi, or at least her past self.</p>
<p>The way the lady spoke hinted that maybe she had tried very hard to fall in love with him, but he didn’t bulge. ’She did say she had found a way into my dark and twisted heart.’</p>
<p>Kent smirked, ’Maybe I was too good to fall in love with her back then.’</p>
<p>"Oh, please. You were mesmerised by my good looks back then. You only played hard to get simply because of those annoying twins. I wonder where they are now."</p>
<p>Kent didn’t reply to her on that. He had come to learn that ladies are as complex as a star enigma. Ignoring them is the best way to live with them.</p>
<p>She can read his mind, and so if he starts thinking about the unknown twins, she might get mad.</p>
<p>"Clearly, you didn’t travel through time and space to come tell me how I was too good to fall for a glass lady."</p>
<p>"Bastard," the lady narrowed her dangerous eyes at Kent.</p>
<p>She moved, and before Kent could react, she was standing right in front of him, a glass dagger held to his throat.</p>
<p>"Call me Glass Lady one more time and see what I will do to you."</p>
<p>"Chill, Glass Lady... I am your husband."</p>
<p>"You..."</p>
<p>She pouted, but she couldn’t bring herself to cut him. Naturally, Kent had already anticipated that.</p>
<p>"Relax, lady. I know you are Enzi, but at the same time, not her. So why don’t we start with your name and why you called me here?"</p>
<p>She stared dangerously close at Kent before a smirk formed on her lips... "You are just too hateful. I wonder why I fell for you in the first place."</p>
<p>"Maybe because I was too good-looking and awesome."</p>
<p>"Oh, please. The Aurelion Vaelros I knew was a bastard, a playboy and one of the laziest people ever to walk the face of the universe. I wonder how you even got all those beasts to follow you."</p>
<p>Kent chuckled and took the lady’s hand. Then, in a somewhat soothing tone, he said:</p>
<p>"I get it. It is said, and it is heartbreaking, but sometimes you have to let go. You tried to fall in love with me, but you weren’t good enough to move my heart.</p>
<p>However, while I may have broken your glass heart into countless pieces, that isn’t enough reason to paint me black. Don’t let your hatred for me compel you to speak ill of my majestic self.</p>
<p>I know myself, and I know I was an awesome, extremely handsome and hardworking person. You don’t have to look down on me simply because I have yet to recall who I was."</p>
<p>"Oh, please," she smirked and then added, "You were a piece of shit who only knows how to play with the hearts of women and dump them. I admit I was young and stupid for falling for you. However, when I found out who you truly are, I blame myself.</p>
<p>I wanted you dead for a while, you know....I still do."</p>
<p>Then a smile appeared on her face. "Though I have to admit, you were intelligent back then. When I met you, I was on the verge of giving up on the glass path, but you took me in and helped me reach my goal.</p>
<p>Perhaps that was the reason why I never hunted you down like the other ladies you toyed with."</p>
<p>The smile then turned into a grin. "But I sure would love to see what will become of you when they find you. Many would have long become gods, Aurelion Vaelros. They will come for you, and I would very much enjoy seeing you suffer."</p>
<p>Kent smirked. "I now see why I didn’t let you in. You are just too wicked to become my lover." Kent looked at her closely and added, "The only reason why a wife will love seeing their husband suffer is if she is wicked herself."</p>
<p>"I am not wicked, Aurelion Vaelros, but I would enjoy seeing you suffer when they come. Wash your neck, Aurelion Vaelros. They are much closer than you think"</p>
<p>"They won’t because I am no longer the person you knew. I am now the Chaos Dragon, so joke’s on you."</p>
<p>The lady laughed out loud, causing the many glasses to reflect her voice... "Keep telling yourself that. You already said that destiny has a way of playing ball with you.</p>
<p>Perhaps, this time, she will score you seven to nil. And yes, that is the total number of heartbreaks you alone had caused innocent women, you hateful playboy bastard."</p>
<p>She flew back and laughed again... "I am Aelvara Shardheart, your first and only disciple. But I guess I am now your wife, so take care of me because if you don’t... hehe."</p>
<p>Suddenly, a light flickered in Enzi’s eye, and the air cleared.</p>
<p>"Kent," Enzi called out, and Kent instantly knew his glass lady was back.</p>
<p>"I feel violated in my own body," Enzi said. Kent tried to speak, but he forced himself to rethink his words.</p>
<p>In the end, he said the ones that came to mind, "Don’t worry, love. It was just some crazy lady who couldn’t handle her heartbreak, so she came throwing a tantrum."</p>
<p>He opened his arms, and Enzi flew straight in. "Glad you are back," Kent said.</p>
<p>"Me too," Enzi also said. However, an evil grin appeared on her face.</p>
<p>’Oh, so I am now a crazy lady, huh?’</p>
<p>Obviously, the Primordial Chaos Dragon had just recorded his first Yandere, one who had some unresolved anger issues.</p>
