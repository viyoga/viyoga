<p>Boom</p>
<p>A sword and spear collided in the air, creating a powerful shockwave that spread far and wide.</p>
<p>Kent was in the air with his dragon wings spread behind him allowing him to move faster. The moment his bloodline moved from 5% to 8%, he sensed something new awakened within him.</p>
<p>Of course, since it wasn’t something he was planning on using at the moment, he instead focused on awakening the bloodline of his women.</p>
<p>However, things are different now.</p>
<p>The wife he was awakening was now stronger and deadlier than the one he knew before the awakening ritual began.</p>
<p>Kent still hasn’t received her true name nor a way to invoke it, so this means the one he was fighting was like the true name she was about to receive.</p>
<p>This also means before Neomi would receive her true name, Kent must defeat the true name.</p>
<p>But the problem is, the person up in the air is first, faster than him. Second, stronger than him in all regards, and lastly, more experience and deadlier than him.</p>
<p>This means Kent is fighting against an odd; he cannot easily defend himself from getting killed. Talk less about defeating her.</p>
<p>Luckily, he has something he can only thank his ladies for.</p>
<p>’Gaia, kindly divide the points I have between my strength and speed.’ Kent gave the order as he evaded a spear thrust that was aimed at his neck.</p>
<p>The lady in the air only attacks to kill.</p>
<p>⟪That will be 12,000 points for each.⟫</p>
<p>Kent felt his strength and speed soar as he matched Neomi’s speed and strength. Of course, he didn’t know if that was her top speed, but she was fast enough to overwhelm any Root Great Sage.</p>
<p>"Rapid Slash!"</p>
<p>Kent unleashed all 450 sword arcs, aiming to force her back.</p>
<p>However, the next second, Neomi took a circular swing, and a gush of wind as sharp as a razor formed around her, cutting the sword arcs into countless pieces.</p>
<p>’Damn, she neutralized my attack just like that.’ Kent was blown away.</p>
<p>But he didn’t dwell on it, as the next second, hundreds of small portals opened up in the air, and razor-sharp spears began raining down from them.</p>
<p>The spears rained down, and Kent had to unleash his sword intent, filling the air with sword qi. The spears were blocked by the field he created; however, a few passed through it.</p>
<p>He unleashed the True Despair sword strike skill, decimating them all.</p>
<p>When the last spear was destroyed, Kent found himself in a world of wind as a powerful force tossed his body around.</p>
<p>He didn’t know when it happened or realized it could be this intense.</p>
<p>’My eyes are really being opened to things,’ he thought, smiling despite his harrowing situation. The wind was something infused with spear qi.</p>
<p>This made him gain something akin to clarity.</p>
<p>The wind was overwhelming, but thanks to his wings, he stabilised himself just in time to see Neomi raise her spear.</p>
<p>"Rakshasa," she muttered coldly.</p>
<p>In an instant, Kent felt his armour cut into from all sides.</p>
<p>"What in the name of..." Kent quickly noticed the change, but before he could find a counter, a figure rose from behind Neomi, towering 12 meters into the air.</p>
<p>It had six arms, and from the moment it appeared, Kent knew he was no match for her. In fact, from the moment that word—or perhaps a name—was uttered, he sensed it.</p>
<p>That name held more power than he could comprehend at his current level. It was like staring at the face of death.</p>
<p>⟪Master must be careful. That is the manifestation of a spear intent. The attacks from it will be very dangerous.⟫</p>
<p>Just as Gaia spoke those words, Neomi raised her spear again and pointed it at Kent, muttering a word that made Kent narrow his eyes.</p>
<p>He closed his eyes and began channelling his energy into his sword.</p>
<p>"Zero Defense Annihilation."</p>
<p>The manifestation behind Neomi clapped two hands together and then snapped them open, instantly forcing a 6-meter-long spear made of spear qi to shoot toward Kent.</p>
<p>Kent stood there with his eyes closed, his sword bathed in sword qi, and the spear closed like a bullet. However, he just stood there, channelling his energy into his sword.</p>
<p>He remained still for a few minutes, but then he moved just as the spear came within a meter of him.</p>
<p>He unleashed dozens of sword attacks in rapid succession, creating intense and lethal sword lights, cutting the spear into many pieces.</p>
<p>Then he moved further, clashing with Neomi and forcing the manifestation to disappear.</p>
<p>But that wasn’t all. Kent raised his sword and took a vertical swing, unleashing a sword arc at the lady, whose eyes were burning more intensely.</p>
<p>"It’s working, my lord. The hungrier she gets, the better," the four-armed lady said to Kent, who could only smile as he battled the woman who, from the start until now, had only tried to attack and go for the kill.</p>
<p>Kent was making sure he didn’t accidentally kill her while she was doing her best to kill him. It was like that situation where no matter what, you have to support your wife, or else she will run with 50% of your wealth.</p>
<p>"Spear of the Wild!" A faint red spear appeared in the air and shot toward Kent.</p>
<p>"True Despair Sword Strike!" He also unleashed his skill, creating a shockwave filled with mist in the air.</p>
<p>Kent vanished into the mist, and a second later, he unleashed the full form of the [Rapid Slash] skill, forcing Neomi, who was caught off-guard, to crash onto the ground.</p>
<p>"Doom Shard!"</p>
<p>Kent commanded as pointed shards made of a black diamond shot from the ground, stabbing into the legs of the lady, who still held the expression of a warrior in her eyes despite the injuries.</p>
<p>"Lightning Slash!" Kent channelled his lightning energy into the sword and unleashed it, but Neomi simply used her spear to block it.</p>
<p>But that was what he aimed for. Kent appeared in front of the lady, whose body was being plague with lightning.</p>
<p>However, right as Kent appeared before her, a hand grabbed him from behind.</p>
<p>The moment he turned, he came face to face with another copy of Neomi—but this copy had six arms. The first four arms grabbed him while the other two raised spears aimed at his neck.</p>
<p>Kent smirked, for he knew the winner had been determined.</p>
<p>"We’ll fight again later, dear," he said.</p>
<p>The two spears stabbed at him, but just as they were an inch from his neck, his body vanished.</p>
<p>Then, from behind Neomi, a split body appeared. "This is going to hurt."</p>
<p>He landed a powerful kick to the back of her head, knocking her out cold. At the same moment, a true name appeared in his mind, and he began to recite it.</p>
