<p>"You dare lay hands on my wife"</p>
<p>Suddenly, a slaughter aura descended on the battlefield right when the chilling words came from Kent’s mouth.</p>
<p>CRACK</p>
<p>The void cracked again, and Kent’s hand came out much longer this time, holding the leg of the man who attacked Val, while his other hand held Val’s bloodied figure.</p>
<p>Kent ejected a powerful energy into her body, causing Val to cough a mouthful of blood before passing out. Elsa arrived beside her and took her away.</p>
<p>CRACK</p>
<p>By the time the second crack sounded, the void was wide enough for Kent to come out. He walked through, still holding the man’s leg.</p>
<p>All this happened within seconds, but within those few seconds, the majority of the warriors, including even some of the vampires, managed to pass through the crack that was separating the Dead Region and Sonox.</p>
<p>Exactly five seconds after Kent caught the man’s leg, his other hand moved and severed it. However, the man didn’t seem to care, as he was suddenly forty meters away in the next second.</p>
<p>His cut-off leg regenerated, and a cold look appeared in his eyes.</p>
<p>"This person is not simple," he said to himself, looking on Kent with a scrutinising gaze. "However, I am a Root Immortal Avatar. Even if he was at the peak of the Death Stage, he wouldn’t be able to hold a candle against me."</p>
<p>The next second, however, his face contorted in shock. "A mere Avatar dares lay hands on my wife."*</p>
<p>Kent’s eyes turned deep red as faint red smoke rose from them. He extended his hand, and a sword appeared in his firm grip. In an instant, the aura around him turned from just blood to blood filled with sword energy.</p>
<p>"Gaia, do it," Kent commanded, and Gaia appeared a few meters from the Forsaken door and pointed at it.</p>
<p>In that instant, the Forsaken Lord’s expression turned ugly. "No, you don’t." He waved his hand, and two large hands moved and held the door that was closing in place.</p>
<p>Then, with another wave, two hundred Royal Vampires retreating from the battlefield were sucked back and hurled toward the door.</p>
<p>Gaia smirked and was about to wave her hand when Kent’s voice stopped her. "Allow them to pass." In an instant, the two hundred vampires passed through the door and appeared in the Forsaken world.</p>
<p>Naturally, the man should have been happy and even tried to escape, but the expression on his face was anything but happy.</p>
<p>Just now, Gaia could have prevented him from kidnapping the 200 vmapires, but Kent stopped her. This baffled him.</p>
<p>He turned to Kent, ignoring the door. "Why?"</p>
<p>The door closed the next second, but he didn’t panic. He can leave at any moment he wants.</p>
<p>Kent didn’t show any expression that said otherwise, but if one looked closely, they could sense a feeling—one that contained both pain, anger, and extreme hatred toward the Forsaken race.</p>
<p>"I don’t know why, but I have never hated anyone the way I hate you lot. So, I want you to use the vampires to open more doors, because that is the only way I will be able to reach you.</p>
<p>So until this hatred is satiated, I will never stop hunting your kind down. That is my promise."</p>
<p>Kent unleashed an aura that caused all the Forsaken warriors to feel primal fear. Most wanted to run, but the door closed.</p>
<p>And now, looking at Kent’s red eyes, they all felt their soul abandoning them.</p>
<p>"I will start with those of you here."</p>
<p>Gaia giggled and waved her hand, sealing the entire Dead Region. Kent moved, and the next second, the scent of blood filled the air.</p>
<p>"Kill him!" Lord Forsaken shouted, feeling his one-way transfer back to the Immortal World severed.</p>
<p>The five Forsaken Elders and the 102,000 Forsaken warriors on the battlefield charged at Kent, whose eyes were completely red.</p>
<p>"Primal Body Tempering Art..." Kent activated the technique that sent him into the Blood Enlightenment.</p>
<p>In an instant, a blood rune appeared on his forehead, and tiny blood essence from the warriors he was killing started entering it.</p>
<p>"Not enough," Kent shouted as he rose into the air and slashed his sword with both hands, causing a large arc of sword energy mixed with blood to flash out.</p>
<p>Hundreds were cut in half vertically. The blood essence flew and entered the Blood Rune, making him gain some more strength.</p>
<p>"You dare hurt my wife... my friends... Die for me." Kent was currently moving at a speed that even the Spirit Lords were finding difficult to track.</p>
<p>"Still too slow." Kent activated his Sword Clone Bodies, allowing 18 identical copies of him to appear in different parts of the battlefield.</p>
<p>But he didn’t stop there. He went ahead and activated the ’Flame Wheels’, causing his speed to surge past what even the Spirit Lords could track.</p>
<p>"Impossible." Deva, the strongest Forsaken Elder, felt her scalp go numb when she saw Kent flash past her, only for a hundred heads to roll to the ground the next second.</p>
<p>Lord Forsaken, who was trying his best to break the restriction Gaia lifted, turned and looked at the battlefield.</p>
<p>"Impossible."</p>
<p>With someone having the cultivation of an early-stage Root Immortal, he saw through Kent’s movement technique. However, what made him turn pale was the aura surrounding Kent.</p>
<p>It wasn’t that the Spirit Lords were weak. It was the fact that the moment they came within 2 meters of him, an aura that caused them to lose hold of themselves for a few seconds,</p>
<p>This causes them to become disturbed and feel a fear that dampens their will to keep fighting.</p>
<p>He saw it plainly, and that caused him to feel some fear toward Kent.</p>
<p>This only caused him to attack the seal even more. However, no matter how powerful his attacks were, the seal remained in place.</p>
<p>"Just give up and wait for my master to kill you." Gaia appeared 200 meters from Lord Forsaken and smiled at him.</p>
<p>"Little girl, open the seal before I make you."</p>
<p>Gaia giggled, "First of all, aside from my master, you have no power under or beyond the heavens to command me. Second, you are destined to die here today, so prepare. And lastly, you are rather weak for an Avatar of an Immortal."</p>
<p>Lord Forsaken shuddered, releasing a powerful aura that caused the space around him to warp. He lunged to attack Gaia, but he abandoned the attack halfway when he saw a smile appear on Gaia’s face.</p>
<p>He turned around and charged at Kent.</p>
<p>"As strong as my master is, he cannot face you and all those idiots at the same time, so relax for now." Gaia pointed her slender index finger forward, and Lord Forsaken froze mid-flight.</p>
<p>He could only watch as Kent slaughtered his people.</p>
<p>Kent had already killed over 50,000 Forsaken warriors. Two Forsaken Elders had already died, and one had lost an arm.</p>
<p>"Filthy bloods, you dare have the audacity to attack the world I live in... You must have a death wish." Kent’s voice was so cold that when it entered the ears of the Forsaken warriors, they couldn’t help but feel their bodies give up.</p>
<p>One hour later, only two Forsaken Elders remained, both injured and panting on the ground. Kent put away his sword and extended his hand. A golden flame appeared in his palm.</p>
<p>Deva and the other Forsaken Elder instantly turned pale when they saw the flame.</p>
