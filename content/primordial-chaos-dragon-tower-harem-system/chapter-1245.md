<p>At best, it would take Gaia 8 days to build the city. She already has a plan, so the next best thing is to start building.</p>
<p>But since that was just 8 hours on the outside, Kent decided to spend them there. Once they are done migrating, he will head to Planet Bayla, which is much closer to the edge of this galaxy.</p>
<p>Words had already spread throughout the floating island city that the ones who had come to their rescue were Liora, their Princess, who many thought was dead, and her husband, Kent Madson.</p>
<p>Yuyin sold the narrative maybe too well, making sure that all who saw Liora as human and thus never wanted her to be their Princess and future Queen would change their minds.</p>
<p>Nobody would be stupid enough to look down on the wife of the legendary Kent Madson. It just isn’t something anybody would dare do.</p>
<p>So when Kent returned to the island, and Liora wasn’t back with her aunt yet, he decided to take a walk.</p>
<p>He is already famous, so he would not face any resistance or scrutiny.</p>
<p>When he walked out of the hall, he was greeted by a little fox girl who was sneaking a peek into the hall, probably trying to see the person everyone was talking about.</p>
<p>"Oh, who do we have here?" Kent said, nearly scaring the living hell out of the little lady. She tried to run, but she calmed down, knowing she had come there to see him.</p>
<p>Many wanted to come, but they weren’t brave enough.</p>
<p>She turned to Kent and spoke, keeping her focus on him as if trying to commit his appearance to memory.</p>
<p>"My name is Aala."</p>
<p>"Oh, such a beautiful name." The name is indeed beautiful for a Foxkin.</p>
<p>But even if it isn’t beautiful, Kent, of course, had already found a liking for the little fox.</p>
<p>He didn’t know if she was also a loli, but considering she is 13 and so small, it wouldn’t be unexpected if her growth buds failed to give her height.</p>
<p>Perhaps because he has over seven of them inside the tower, Kent found Aala likeable.</p>
<p>"Is big brother called Kent Madson?" Aala asked, a little hesitant.</p>
<p>Kent smiled and crouched down. The way Aala was looking at him, if he didn’t reduce his height, she would probably break her neck.</p>
<p>"Yes. I am Kent Madson. Nice meeting you," Kent extended his hand for a shake. Aala looked at his large hand for a few seconds before extending her tiny one.</p>
<p>"Well, Aala, the one who brought me here has abandoned me, so I need a guide. You don’t mind giving this big brother a tour, would you?"</p>
<p>"Aala has lived here for a long time, so of course, Aala will show big brother around."</p>
<p>"Wonderful," Kent lifted her off the ground, causing a yelp to escape her lips. However, when he set her on his shoulder, Aala giggled, feeling very tall.</p>
<p>"Wow, Aala is taller than big brother."</p>
<p>Kent smiled, feeling amused.</p>
<p>"Of course you are. You are a big girl after all."</p>
<p>"Aala is a big girl," she smiled, feeling happy. This made Kent also feel good.</p>
<p>Maybe because he had spent more time around lolis, whenever he is around one, he feels happy. They are just too innocent, both in the way they sound and the way they look.</p>
<p>"So, where to first?"</p>
<p>"Aala will take big brother to the training ground. Aala always goes there to wait for Mother and Father to return from their missions."</p>
<p>"Okay then. Let’s go."</p>
<p>Kent soon left the entrance of the hall and started walking in the direction Aala pointed.</p>
<p>Soon, they were spotted by the Sky Foxes, who were busy packing their stuff. Many of them seemed surprised to see Kent Madson casually walking among them.</p>
<p>Some even seemed shocked to see Aala seated on his shoulder, her little legs wrapped around his neck and her slender yet tiny hands holding his horns.</p>
<p>In their minds, such a legendary person shouldn’t be this down-to-earth.</p>
<p>However, watching Kent converse with Aala, with a handsome smile on his face, they could only let the moment print on their minds.</p>
<p>Kent didn’t mind their looks. He was happy hearing Aala speak in the third person. In a way, his lolis—almost all of them—speak in the third person. They refer to themselves in the third person.</p>
<p>So Aala sounded just like them.</p>
<p>Soon, they were near the arena, where sounds of metal could be heard coming from inside.</p>
<p>Since the Sky Fox only specialises in illusion, their combat power is relatively weak. As a result, once their illusions are broken, they become defenceless.</p>
<p>So to counter that, Yuyin made it a must to train a few of the Sky Foxes in hand-to-hand combat. At heart and in their job class, they are illusionists.</p>
<p>However, when it comes to preserving their lives, knowing a thing or two about handling themselves with weapons is essential.</p>
<p>They called this group of Sky Foxes "Combat Foxes."</p>
<p>Aala’s parents are Combat Foxes, and, from what she told Kent, they are powerful Combat Foxes who venture off the island to search for food for their people.</p>
<p>She always comes to the training arena to wait for them whenever they go on said missions. So she brought Kent to wait for them while they observed the Sky Foxes train.</p>
<p>According to Aala, her parents said they would return today.</p>
<p>When they got close to the arena, Kent flew into the air and landed on the tall wall surrounding the arena. It was open at the top, so when they landed, he could see everything.</p>
<p>Many also noticed him, and as expected, shocked expressions appeared on their faces.</p>
<p>"Don’t mind us. We are just here to observe and wait for Aala’s parents." They nodded and went back to training. Most of them were using the bow, while the rest were training with the sword.</p>
<p>Initial observation already told Kent that they weren’t really good with any of the weapons they were using.</p>
<p>’It is quite pitiful how limitations on one’s bloodline can cause them to live knowing they can never rise above their weaknesses.’</p>
<p>[That is life, Master. Not everyone gets to have it all.]</p>
<p>Kent sighed in his heart.</p>
<p>"Big Brother, aren’t they awesome? Aala wants to be like them when Aala grows her second tail," Aala said with a happy smile, causing Kent to force out a small smile.</p>
<p>"Of course, they are awesome. And I can’t wait to see you swinging your sword when you join them."</p>
<p>Aala smiled, tightening her hold on Kent’s dragon horns. As a Foxkin, once she grows her second tail, she can start cultivating.</p>
<p>It is the same as humans having to wait until they were 16, sometimes 15, before they could start their cultivation journey.</p>
<p>However, seeing that she was already happy to join the Combat Foxes made Kent smile weakly. They aren’t strong enough to his standards; however, to someone like Aala, they are gods.</p>
<p>Suddenly, a portal opened not far from the training arena, and three people came out.</p>
<p>Turning to the portal, Kent saw Liora and Yuyin carrying a lady with a bloody look toward the training arena. He jumped down from the wall and rushed to them.</p>
<p>"Mama," Aala suddenly exclaimed, jumping down from Kent’s shoulder.</p>
