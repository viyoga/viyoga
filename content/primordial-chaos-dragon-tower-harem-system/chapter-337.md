<p>From the start of the battle between Kent and the evil bat, his objective wasn’t to kill him. Kent was rather after his blood to end the battle entirely and prove to everyone that he was no pushover.</p>
<p>To achieve this, he needed to unleash a power currently in his possession—a red token.</p>
<p>This token was the Mirror Enemy reward he had earned from the quest.</p>
<p>It was no ordinary item; this token had the potential to shake the entire four kingdoms and awaken the hidden dangers currently lurking in the shadows.</p>
<p>Kaizo was in the air, closing in on the bat, while Kent struggled to his feet and retrieved his sword, now coated with a thin layer of the bat’s blood.</p>
<p>He placed the red token on the blood, and it was instantly absorbed into it.</p>
<p>Kent then cut his palm, allowing his own blood to drip onto the token. The moment the token drank his blood, it flew from his grip and shot toward the sky.</p>
<p>It stopped at the same level as the two combatants, and then—**boom!**—a powerful energy burst forth from it, halting their clash.</p>
<p>Kaizo vanished and reappeared beside his master. He supported him with one arm. The battle dolls surrounded them, their swords firmly gripped and ready.</p>
<p>"What, you think you can stop me with a trinket?" the evil bat sneered.</p>
<p>"Who said anything about stopping you with a trinket? You will be stopping yourself with this trinket," Kent replied, smiling through his blood-soaked teeth.</p>
<p>"Kaizo, do you know what is more annoying and heartbreaking than being cuckolded?" Kent asked.</p>
<p>"I don’t know if anything could be more heartbreaking than seeing your wife get fucked in front of you, but you can enlighten me, Master," Kaizo replied with a smile, not taking his eyes off the bat, who now wore a deep frown.</p>
<p>"Remember these wise words, Kaizo. Treasure them, for a wise man once said: the only thing worse than being cuckolded is fighting a replica of yourself."</p>
<p>Kent clapped his hands together. "Mirror Enemy, activate!"</p>
<p>The red token burst into a swirling red portal that instantly unleashed a powerful energy from within.</p>
<p>Then, through it, a figure emerged, shocking everyone who saw it. The figure was the evil bat—an exact copy of him.</p>
<p>From height to expression, everything mirrored the evil bat, who took a step back in disbelief.</p>
<p>"This is your enemy, Evil Bat. You shouldn’t have come after me for I already told you: if you come after me, do your best to kill me. Failure means this. And let me tell you, I have more where this came from."</p>
<p>Kent’s laughter filled the air.</p>
<p>"This is another you. So unless you have a way of killing yourself without knowing it, then for all eternity, you will keep fighting yourself. Everything you could think of, he already knew"</p>
<p>As if to prove his words, the mirror bat raised its hand, and portals appeared in the air. Red bats flew out from within, attacking the evil bat, who quickly conjured his own bats in defence.</p>
<p>"You can only try to kill yourself before he kills you. And oh, if somebody else manages to kill your clone, you will die.</p>
<p>If they help you kill it, you will die. And lastly, even if you run and hide in the deepest, darkest hole, he will find you.</p>
<p>So, happy fighting—because that’s all you’ll be doing for as long as I will it."</p>
<p>"What my master is trying to say is... you’re fucked for life... bro," Kaizo smirked.</p>
<p>"Brat, do you want to invoke the wrath of the Evil Sect? Do you think we are weak?" the evil bat shouted as blood spears rained down on him.</p>
<p>"Stupid, do you really think I went through all this trouble just to be scared now? You really are stupid.</p>
<p>I killed all these people today because I wanted to, and in the coming months, I will kill more because revenge must be exacted.</p>
<p>So please, send your minions after me, and I will greet them. But even if they don’t come, I will come for them. So welcome me well, because next time, I will ensure your sect is razed to the ground."</p>
<p>Kent swung his sword, and 450 sword arcs rained down on the bat, who cursed under his breath.</p>
<p>"Damn, that feels good," Kent said.</p>
<p>A mirror copy of the bat now occupied the evil bat, so Kent was able to injure him, and the feeling was damn good.</p>
<p>"Let me try it too," Kaizo said, his fist clenched and lightning crackling around it.</p>
<p>"Go for it, buddy. This is our chance to pay him back for all the painful injuries we’ve suffered."</p>
<p>Kaizo shot into the air like a bolt of lightning, his fist slamming into the evil bat’s spine and sending him flying toward the mirror bat, who landed a powerful kick to his chest, sending him hurtling back toward Kent.</p>
<p>Kent closed his eyes, focusing.</p>
<p>When he snapped them open, his aura surged into overdrive. He unleashed a devastating sword slash, creating a massive sword arc that tore through the air.</p>
<p>The attack struck the evil bat’s chest, sending him flying backwards. The bat cursed and fled, attempting to escape.</p>
<p>"Run all you want. In the end, you cannot outrun yourself," Kent said coldly.</p>
<p>The mirror bat began chasing him relentlessly. As Kent said, the mirror copy would forever be his shadow, a relentless pursuer determined to kill him.</p>
<p>In every way, the evil bat was doomed. Until he managed to kill his mirror copy, he would never know peace again.</p>
<p>The thought alone made all those watching break into a cold sweat—exactly as Kent had intended.</p>
<p>"I hope you all saw what I’m capable of, and let me tell you, I have more where that came from. So, if you want, come and get some."</p>
<p>Kent smiled evilly.</p>
<p>"I will be taking the body of the Forest King and everything else I bought from the auction. So, for those of you thinking of robbing me, wipe that idea from your mind."</p>
<p>"If you don’t believe me, come against me any day, and I promise you, I will ensure you don’t leave disappointed." Kent’s bones were broken, but he didn’t let the pain show on his face or in his voice.</p>
<p>"What’s next, Master? I can see some cowards hiding in the bushes. Should we go after them?" Kaizo asked, his tone loud enough to be heard even from hundreds of miles away.</p>
<p>"No need, Kaizo. Cowards are only good at hiding. I’ll let them grow a pair before going after them."</p>
<p>Kent smiled, noticing the fight still burning in the injured undead. The unyielding personality he had added during its creation was in full effect.</p>
<p>"You heard my master. All cowards are free to go... for now. When you grow a pair, you can come for some beatings," Kaizo said before picking Kent up like a prince.</p>
<p>He glanced toward a certain mountain, and in a flash, he was gone, shooting toward it.</p>
<p>The Battle Dolls followed close behind.</p>
<p>Like that, Kent planted the seed of fear in the hearts of all those who witnessed his battle with the evil bat.</p>
<p>Now, they would all think twice before daring to come after him.</p>
