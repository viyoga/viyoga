<p>Kent opened his eyes, only for a powerful wave of heavenly sword energy to explode out of him. In an instant, his entire body felt like he had been recreated using nothing but sword energy.</p>
<p>His sword intent rose from Level 6 to Level 7 and continued to increase rapidly. The same thing was also happening to his sword aura.</p>
<p>Soon, he reached level 8 but kept climbing.</p>
<p>Basically, his entire understanding of his sword realm was being shaped by what he had learned from Jest’s memories.</p>
<p>The memory ended when he and Inaya met again.</p>
<p>However, before that, Jest went through a lot. First was his training in the Purple Sword Garden. The time he spent there took his sword intent from Level 5 to Level 9. The same was true for his sword Qi/aura.</p>
<p>After he had reached that stage, he knew he had to form his sword realm and finally become a sword emperor. So he left, and with his popularity that was earned thanks to what he had achieved on the sword bridge, he managed to learn five more sword realms.</p>
<p>Ten years later, he formed his own.</p>
<p>Initially, he wanted to follow the path that Divine Collins had told him about. However, he realised he had more time to later shape his sword realm with any element he wanted, so he formed the sword realm by comprehending every last aspect of the 1.6 million sword moves he had learned.</p>
<p>He had already mastered them, but he sat down and examined each move, making sense of every small thing about it. He went through all 1.6 million sword moves.</p>
<p>Once he was satisfied, he formed the Genesis sword realm, the same sword realm that went on to become the Genesis Sword Domain.</p>
<p>When he formed his sword realm, it was basic.</p>
<p>Unlike many realms that manifest crazy stiff, the only thing that changed when he activated his sword realm was the direction of the wind.</p>
<p>Initially, many laughed at him, some even claiming his journey ended there. However, after tasting what he had accomplished, they all gave him the respect he deserved.</p>
<p>But he went beyond that.</p>
<p>He took the conceptualisation of his sword realm to the next level of crazy. He first comprehended the wind element, which he mastered in just five years.</p>
<p>Once he did that, he merged it with his sword realm. That merge gave him all the boost he needed as a swordsman.</p>
<p>The wind element made it so that he could move at a speed so fast that when his sword realm was activated, even the fastest True Immortal would struggle to catch up to him.</p>
<p>However, Jest wasn’t satisfied, so he learned the lightning element. This gave the Genesis Sword Realm the boost in combat power it needed.</p>
<p>Yet again, he went further, and by the time he neared the Sword Divine stage, he had added ice and fire to it as well.</p>
<p>When he became a Sword Divine, he added the metal element, making it the final element he added to his sword realm.</p>
<p>The day he became a Sword Divine, he challenged the sword bridge again and won the fifth level within ten minutes.</p>
<p>Sword Divine Collins tried his best, but he failed miserably. He tried to challenge the sixth level, but facing a Sword Legend was different. Even so, he lasted two hours before he ran out of energy.</p>
<p>That battle made him aware of his limitations, and so he entered seclusion after he left the sect, and for years, he shaped his understanding of the sword and his elements to the point that if he were to face the sixth-level Sword Legend, he would win after a few minutes.</p>
<p>But because he was on the run, he had to give up that idea and embark on one adventure after another. He had plans to return one day to challenge the 6th level.</p>
<p>In the end, he became a Sword Legend himself. By then, he had become a True Immortal.</p>
<p>Everybody wanted a piece of him because his name appearing on the sword dao tablet meant he</p>
<p>knew something many did not.</p>
<p>Thankfully, after Inaya absorbed the sword god energy, she gained so much that the sword dao gave her some of the recognition, taking a few loads off Jest’s.</p>
<p>This allowed him to move around and further strengthen his understanding of his swordsmanship.</p>
<p>The day they met, he was about to enter seclusion and try to reach the Immortal King stage, since he had already formed over seven Immortal Qis.</p>
<p>Kent had no idea what happened next, but he was sure the reason why he hated Inaya had to do with what she did next. Too bad the memory ended before he could see that.</p>
<p>Ten days later, he opened his eyes, a wave of sharp sword intent oozing out from them. His sword gaze had undergone some changes, and that could be seen from the air around him.</p>
<p>Kent quickly checked the messages that had appeared before him. A few seconds later, he looked away and stared at the pagoda in the distance.</p>
<p>He also had the sword bridge inside the sword tower. However, he knew the swordsmen on each floor would be different from the ones Jest’s faced.</p>
<p>Even so, he had to conquer the first six floors, which meant he had to defeat a sword legend while he had no sword realm.</p>
<p>In the memories he had seen, Jest defeated many sword legends and even a few sword immortals. However, that was possible because of his Genesis Sword Realm.</p>
<p>Kent had yet to meet two more requirements before his sword realm would awaken.</p>
<p>He had yet to conquer the first six levels of the sword bridge in this life.</p>
<p>The other requirement was, of course, conquering the sword legend on the sixth level of the sword bridge. This must be accomplished by Jest, meaning he must review memories of that before his sword realm would awaken.</p>
<p>The tower gave him these requirements, so he was sure Jest would do that. This meant he would have to review more of his memories and know what happened next.</p>
<p>This meant he must go see Inaya, for she was the key to everything. If he wants to awaken his sword realm, he must go back home.</p>
<p>As for the first six levels of the sword bridge inside the tower, Kent stood up and flew toward it. He might not have a sword realm yet, but he had copied thousands of sword realms over the years he spent on the move.</p>
<p>And while he had yet to awaken the Divine sword aura and intent, he had knowledge of how to fight sword legends and even sword immortals.</p>
<p>Even better, he had the many sword skills and techniques that Jest had created over the years, now at his disposal. Basically, he was now loaded with so much knowledge about his sword path than he was weeks ago.</p>
<p>The best part was that he could now shape his sword path and correct the mistakes he had made before. He could make sure that this time, not a single mistake would go unfixed.</p>
<p>He might have reached the ranks of Swordsmanship, but that didn’t mean he hadn’t made any mistakes. He had made a whole lot of mistakes, and now, with the second chance he had, he planned on walking a flawless path.</p>
<p>But before that, he had to first clear the sword bridge to the sixth level.</p>
