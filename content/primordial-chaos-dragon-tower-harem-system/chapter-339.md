<p>It has been 15 days since the battle, but to the ladies inside the Celestial-grade tower, it has been 360 days. The time difference between the tower and the outside was just too vast.</p>
<p>They had been inside the tower for close to a year, and within those 360 days, a lot had happened.</p>
<p>When they appeared inside the tower where Kent’s wounded body was lying unconscious in the herbal bath, they all broke into tears.</p>
<p>The sight before them was too heartbreaking. They couldn’t handle it, so they became emotional. Even Mara, Camilla, and Val weren’t spared.</p>
<p>However, after the emotional interlude, they began caring for him as the days went by. They made sure he wasn’t alone, staying by his side for weeks without leaving.</p>
<p>But three months into the waiting, the ladies—who had finally managed to stop blaming themselves for being weak—came up with a plan. This plan required them to ensure their men woke up stronger and healthier.</p>
<p>So, they started training.</p>
<p>All of them inside the tower began training. They also started tempering their bodies, thanks to Akira providing them with everything they needed.</p>
<p>They also started using the Stairway to strengthen their bodies further, ensuring that the next time Kent saw them, they would be at a level where he would allow them to fight alongside him.</p>
<p>So, for months, they kept training. By the time 10 months were over, Elsa managed to reach the peak of the Root Sage stage after levelling up three times.</p>
<p>This made her stronger and added thousands of stat points to Kent’s reserves, which had been depleted to zero after using everything during the battles.</p>
<p>Unity and Lilain also broke through to the Root Sage stage, even rising to level 3.</p>
<p>Selene and Ingrid climbed to level 5 Root Great Sages, becoming monsters in their own right. This further contributed to Kent’s strength.</p>
<p>However, Neomi, who is the most talented among them all, rose to the peak of the Root Great Sage stage, ready to become a Root Transcendent.</p>
<p>Even Mara and Camilla reached the peak of the Root Transcendent stage and began preparing to become Root Ascension stage experts.</p>
<p>As for Val, she had entered the 4th stage of the Root Great Sage stage. Of course, she chose to spend most of her time with Kent instead of training.</p>
<p>While her sisters were away, she kept Kent company.</p>
<p>They tried very hard, and it paid off.</p>
<p>Alina was stuck with her boss so she was the only one who didn’t get to join this training.</p>
<p>"Are you ladies sure Kent will be impressed when he wakes up?" Neomi asked. "You clearly heard what Alina said—Kent not only killed Transcendents, he even defeated a Sovereign."</p>
<p>"Relax, Kent isn’t one to care about stuff like this. He will be impressed regardless of whether or not we became who we are," Selene said, taking her sister’s hand.</p>
<p>"I just want him to be able to count on us next time."</p>
<p>They all meant that. They wanted to fight beside him.</p>
<p>"Let’s just wait for him till he wakes up. I know he will be happy to see us again," Unity said, already planning to hug him for ten whole minutes before letting go.</p>
<p>"Yeah, let’s wait for him to wake up. We’ve each leveled up multiple times, which means his strength has risen again. I’m sure that will be enough to get him on his feet."</p>
<p>The ladies nodded.</p>
<p>They had already revealed to the three ladies yet to join the harem about the influence they had on their man. They told them that aside from having sex with him, he also grew stronger just from their cultivation.</p>
<p>This made them want to join the harem so they could also contribute, but Selene told them to relax until Kent woke up.</p>
<p>"I offered to give him my body so he could grow stronger much faster, but he rejected me," Mara said, looking at the sleeping body in the bath.</p>
<p>"Yeah... Kent said we are his women, not batteries. So, though having sex with you would make him happy, he wouldn’t do it if the reason behind it was just to help him grow stronger," Selene explained.</p>
<p>"I just wanted to help," Mara said with a sigh.</p>
<p>"You being here is enough. Plus, he didn’t reject you entirely, so just wait for him to wake up. I’m sure he will take the next step with you all then," Selene said, comforting her.</p>
<p>"But even if he doesn’t, you can still squeeze his balls dry using your mouth." The ladies blushed at such a comment.</p>
<p>"We should probably make sure he doesn’t go anywhere for the next few weeks. I don’t know about you ladies, but I’m going seven rounds with him," Selene said.</p>
<p>"Same here. Until I pass out for the seventh time, I won’t let him go," Elsa seconded her words. They all wanted to make sure their man woke up to a moment he would never forget.</p>
<p>The battle had been harrowing.</p>
<p>They couldn’t help then, but that didn’t mean they wouldn’t be able to help now. They had already added thousands of points for him to use later.</p>
<p>Now, they were planning on how best to help him recover fully from the battle. So, as his ladies, they all wanted to use their bodies to achieve that.</p>
<p>Kent would probably be smiling if he were in a position to hear their cheeky comments.</p>
<p>"I second what Mom said, too, let’s suck him dry," Lilian said with a smile. The ladies looked at the mother and daughter weirdly.</p>
<p>"Then why don’t we make it a group thing? We should each help one another to make sure he passes out again—and this time, not from getting beaten up," Unity joked.</p>
<p>"You’ll be in trouble if he hears you saying he was beaten up," Lilian teased.</p>
<p>"Well, he’s passed out, so there’s no way he can hear that. But even if he did, he *was* indeed beaten up. He’ll just have to put on his big boy pants and man up," Unity giggled.</p>
<p>But then she froze when a voice entered her ears.</p>
<p>"Why don’t I show you how I was beaten up..." Kent spoke, grabbing her waist.</p>
