<p>"Fuck, this feels like doing mushrooms all over again," Kent said as he woke up back in his body. He felt drowsy, and euphoria and vertigo overwhelmed his senses when he woke up.</p>
<p>The feeling of having his body return back to his body is just too euphoric in a way. It’s like doing bad drugs.</p>
<p>"But the good thing is I succeeded, and I now know that the two ladies are alive and can be rescued." He sighed. "The question is how I’m going to go about it."</p>
<p>He couldn’t free them in his soul form, so it meant he had to enter there in his physical form. This meant he would have to fight, and the battle wouldn’t be simple.</p>
<p>"But to think a whole sect leader of such a prestigious sect would be this evil," Kent sighed.</p>
<p>The idea of that being true was just something nobody would believe, yet it was right inside the sect. And from the skulls he saw, many had fallen prey to his evil practices.</p>
<p>"I’m going to enjoy killing him," he muttered before looking at the third and final quest.</p>
<p>⟪Sect Quest: Clash With Evil⟫</p>
<p>⟪Second: Dangerous Preparation⟫</p>
<p>⟪Difficulty: Very Hard⟫</p>
<p>→ The Sect Master is the Evil behind it all. He has 310 minions, of which 300 are at the Root Great Sage stages and 10 are Root Transendents.</p>
<p>→ He also has a life-saving item that can allow him to teleport to a faraway place if he wants.</p>
<p>→ However, you mustn’t allow that to happen.</p>
<p>→ You must gather allies and ensure your strike team is ready within the next 30 days.</p>
<p>→ Use everything you have to destroy the sect master before evil spreads through the entire sect and consumes everyone.</p>
<p>→ Failure to kill the sect master has a 97% chance to come back and hunt you.</p>
<p>⟪Rewards: ⟫</p>
<p>→ 20,000,000 Tower Points</p>
<p>→ True Name Token (Five)</p>
<p>→ Memory Key</p>
<p>"Hmm, this is a crazy lineup for my first major mission," Kent said, reading through the quest. The odds are just too much for someone like him not to complain.</p>
<p>"But I am not alone, and like I said, with enough planning, I can do this." The minions under the sect master alone are enough to handle the entire sect.</p>
<p>But that can be managed, considering he already has four people who can handle Root Transcendents.</p>
<p>Though the two Saintesses aren’t at the Root Transcendent stage yet, they are no ordinary beings, and after letting them rebuild their foundations, they will become even stronger.</p>
<p>Then there is the battle doll, which has mastered hundreds of sword skills. Gaia provided with all the sword skills Kill wanted.</p>
<p>Now, all he needed were resources to upgrade it. Luckily, the auction is coming, and he can buy rare and powerful metal ores.</p>
<p>This will allow him to upgrade the battle doll to an even higher stage.</p>
<p>"If I buy five more dolls and manage to upgrade them, they will become Transcendents, and thanks to all the skills I will provide them, they can take on multiple targets.</p>
<p>This will take care of the ten Root Transcendents. Then, even if I don’t have enough points for more, I can count on my ladies to handle the Root Great Sages." Kent sighed.</p>
<p>"I will make sure the dolls handle the ten Root Transcendents. Then the ladies will handle the Root Great Sages, while I and the two gate masters go after the sect master."</p>
<p>The plan looks weak, but he has to use what he has.</p>
<p>⟪The master can use the Dungeon Seeker Card to access the A-rank Dungeons, and using the Dungeon Master Token, you can claim the dungeons.</p>
<p>This will allow you to summon the final floor bosses, which will be a Root Great Sage at the peak.⟫</p>
<p>"I almost forgot about that," Kent said, taking a rectangular card.</p>
<p>It’s a card that randomly selects ten dungeons from all over the universe, and using it, one can be teleported to the first floor and back.</p>
<p>The only exception is that you cannot walk out of the dungeon, so when you enter, you can only leave using the card.</p>
<p>Right now, in his hands, he has one that has selected 10 random A-rank dungeons, of which the last floor boss is a Peak Root Great Sage.</p>
<p>However, the only reason why this is something he needs is because of his Dungeon Master Token.</p>
<p>Those tokens allow him to claim any dungeon he wants, provided he manages to defeat the last floor boss.</p>
<p>Also, in doing so, he will have the authority to summon the last-floor boss. And he has ten dungeons to select from.</p>
<p>If he manages to claim all dungeons within the next 30 days, he will have 10 Peak Root Great Sages under his command.</p>
<p>He only has two Dungeon Master Tokens now, but he can buy more from the Tower, which, in a way, will be paid for using the points he will earn by clearing every floor of the dungeons.</p>
<p>Basically, he now has the means to farm for subordinates and points.</p>
<p>"This is great. With me having the points to buy more battle dolls, clearing these dungeons will be easy enough," Kent smiled before falling back on the bed.</p>
<p>"I have two Root Transcendents, three Root Great Sages—four if Saintess Val is counted—and three Root Sages.</p>
<p>Uncle Drew is also there, and the Battle Doll is there as well. Soon, I will be gaining another key to unlock a part of my memory, meaning I will also grow much more monstrous.</p>
<p>I’d say this quest is already in the bag.</p>
<p>I will rest for now and tell Nara that she should be ready because she will be free in 30 days. She and that poor disciple have suffered enough; they should have some peace soon enough."</p>
<p>Kent closed his eyes and drifted into sleep. Using his soul actively had taken a toll on his body, so he drifted into sleep and, for two days, didn’t wake up.</p>
<p>It was only when Selene came out of the Tower—because in two days’ time, the auction, which would last for three days this time, would commence—that Kent finally stirred.</p>
<p>All his ladies had become Body cultivators, but they came out to spend some time with Kent before the auction.</p>
<p>Kent woke up, and he first went back to the cave and relayed the information to the green-haired beauty, who was more than happy to hear it.</p>
<p>Later, he went to check up on the two gate masters, who hadn’t been able to sleep for the past few days. They wanted to hear back from the brat who was looking for ways to rescue Nara and Zaila.</p>
