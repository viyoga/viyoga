<p>If only Kent knew the class he casually imagined when Akira asked him would one day become this monstrous, perhaps he would have asked for much more than just this.</p>
<p>Akira had told him what she could do when she was first summoned, but never in his wildest dreams did he expect this to be the outcome when he asked for a class where he could create an undead legion.</p>
<p>But now, standing before the panel and creating a new undead, Kent just couldn’t believe his eyes.</p>
<p>⟪This time, the undead will be a male,⟫ Akira said, retrieving an undead schematic from which she had modified an already unique undead created by the universe.</p>
<p>One good thing about the undead schematic is that it only creates one-of-a-kind undead. There isn’t a second version of them out there, for they are unique to Kent and Kent alone.</p>
<p>Akira activated the card, causing a projection of a figure to appear.</p>
<p>"This one looks very menacing just from his image; I wonder what kind of powers he will have," Kent muttered, looking at a towering figure with white and red skin and two large horns on his forehead.</p>
<p>⟪He will also have brute strength like Kaizo, but unlike Kaizo, who uses his fists, this one will use chains as his weapon.</p>
<p>And because he is being created from a shard of the darkness crystal and domain crystal, he will also have an affinity for the abyss, just like Sylara.</p>
<p>However, he would be much different if the modifications I made were perfectly integrated. Basically, you will be getting another powerful figure you can use for your quests.⟫</p>
<p>"That is convenient. Then let’s start the creation process." The forge came to life, and the process began. After making sure all the required items were added, Kent started the process.</p>
<p>"Hmm, this one, too, will take 48 hours. That will be a while if I stay inside the tower," he scratched the back of his head.</p>
<p>"Why don’t we go save your sister first? The two ladies are still not up, so we might as well spend the time doing something productive."</p>
<p>The two ladies with him nodded, and they left the forge and went back to the mountain garden.</p>
<p>⟪How are you going to save her, Master?⟫ Gaia asked. In response, Kent retrieved one of two cards he had in his possession.</p>
<p>"By making a wish to the universe, of course," Kent smiled. He had received two cards that allowed him to make a wish, and the universe would grant it.</p>
<p>He already has two wishes left with the Enchantress, but these ones are different, and right now, he just wants to use one on Dori, the third weaver, who is the mind weaver.</p>
<p>"But before that, why don’t you answer this question?" Kent looked at them.</p>
<p>"Gaia can literally see everything that goes on in the cosmos. You can literally weave the strings of fate if you wish; this means your sister, a weaver, is also overpowered.</p>
<p>So why can’t she escape the prison she was in? Second, why can’t you just pick her up like you do with treasures?"</p>
<p>Gaia had told him what had happened to her sister, and from her description, it was clear that she had been captured.</p>
<p>But she is a mind weaver, someone who can use her thinking abilities to escape, so why didn’t she?</p>
<p>Second, he isn’t stupid. He knew if he trained harder for years, the chances of him defeating either of the two ladies would be basically nonexistent.</p>
<p>He doesn’t need a genie telling him that. Then, if that is the case, why can’t she just blast open the prison she was in and escape?</p>
<p>And lastly, if Gaia can see her and knows where she is, then why can’t she just pick her up like a sack of potatoes?</p>
<p>These questions really need to be asked, and Kent is asking them right now.</p>
<p>⟪The answers to your questions are rather simple. A weaver cannot use their powers against anyone in this universe. I am a cosmic weaver, but I cannot go against anyone.</p>
<p>I can easily kill a god since I can see them without realizing my presence. But that doesn’t mean I can just attack and kill them. The moment I do that, I will be a weaver no more.</p>
<p>But even without that, I just don’t have the power to attack or use my powers against anyone, not even against my sisters.</p>
<p>First, if Dori were to use her powers against her captors, she would cause more harm to the fabric of the world she was in, and perhaps, many would perish without her even knowing.</p>
<p>That is the power of a weaver. We have been created as absolute beings, but we are powerless children if you ask me.</p>
<p>A single punch from me can shatter galaxies, but doing so would be very bad for this universe, for we are created from the core of the universe.</p>
<p>Perhaps, if we are outside the confines of this universe, we can freely use our powers, but while inside, it would mean us destroying this universe. None of us want that.⟫</p>
<p>"I think I get the gist of it. In that case, under no circumstance should the two of you use your powers to come to my aid.</p>
<p>I won’t be happy about saving me while millions or perhaps billions perish," Kent said, and the two ladies nodded.</p>
<p>Truth be told, he wouldn’t want the two small humans to become his weapons for battle.</p>
<p>Gaia is literally creating training spaces for him and his ladies thanks to the tower, and Akira is like his personal skill and class artisan.</p>
<p>Asking them to do battle is just being too selfish.</p>
<p>"I guess I will make the wish now and see where that takes us." He drips his blood on the card and says his wish out loud</p>
<p>"I wish for the third weaver, the mind weaver, to be rescued from wherever she is in the universe and be brought to her sisters."</p>
<p>The card shone immediately, and a message appeared before Kent’s vision, making him scratch the back of his head.</p>
