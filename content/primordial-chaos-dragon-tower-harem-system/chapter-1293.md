<p>"So are you saying the Underworld Soul River was created by someone, and that person was one of my 13 incarnations?"</p>
<p>[Yes and no. Yes, in the sense that the skill was created by your incarnation. However, no, since he wasn’t the one who created the Underworld River.]</p>
<p>"I need more context, Tower."</p>
<p>[There are many forbidden things in the universe. Those things, as far as the mind could go, are only meant to be used by the Daos to restore balance to the universe.</p>
<p>They are never meant to be possessed by ordinary men. Of course, it wasn’t that ordinary humans can’t use it; it was just that nobody is crazy enough to dare take what belonged to the Dao.</p>
<p>The river you glimpsed is the Underworld River. It is the river that takes all dead souls to the underworld.</p>
<p>This river takes the resentment from the souls it carries and sends their free souls to the underworld, making it the most dangerous place in the underworld, just like the Damned Hell River.</p>
<p>It is a place filled with resentment of endless souls. Under no circumstances would one be able to take it, let alone harness its power, much less make it their own.</p>
<p>However, somehow, someone was able to make a part of their own by turning it into their strength. So yes, the skill was created, but the water belongs to the Dao of Death.]</p>
<p>Kent remained seated, his pale expression not brightening up. He still seems scared, and that alone is enough to tell what he saw.</p>
<p>[Relax, master. What you saw is merely a fraction of the true Underworld Sea. But even so, what will be coming out whenever you use the Underworld Soul River will be restricted to how much your soul can take and how many resentful souls you will be devouring once you start using it.</p>
<p>I know you are scared, but you have nothing to worry about. So instead of worrying, think more about what you can do with this new ability.</p>
<p>It drains a lot of soul essence, but trust me, it will be worth every last soul essence spent.]</p>
<p>"I am not even about to start to think about how crazy I might have been to wake up one day and dare venture into the underworld simply to steal something of the Death Dao."</p>
<p>Kent forced a smile, trying to take his mind off what he had seen and focus on the positive. However, no matter how much he thinks, there is just no way for him to forget what he saw.</p>
<p>In the end, he decided to think about his next step.</p>
<p>[There is no doubt the master would be welcomed chaotically when he goes back out there. This is why I have created the best move to handle them and at least help you gain your footing.]</p>
<p>"Let me hear what you have, Tower."</p>
<p>[At the moment, the master has about 150 Tier 21 (Life Stage) soul spirits. This means the master has enough firepower to commit yet another massacre through soul explosion.</p>
<p>This time, the massacre will be through soul attacks. I believe with this move, the master would not lose precious blood again like did with the Meteor shower.</p>
<p>With enough blood, the master can even create another Blood Life or Death Seal. So what do you think?]</p>
<p>"It is perfect."</p>
<p>Kent nodded to what the tower said. He indeed would be surrounded on all sides. However, with the plan the tower had come up with, if he made a move, the chances of him killing more would be high.</p>
<p>He can even go so far as to kill more than with the meteorites.</p>
<p>"Since I am done here, I might as well go see what I have to deal with." Kent stood up and stretched his body, letting his muscles loosen. After he was done, he deactivated the formation and quickly left the cave.</p>
<p>Soon, he was flying back to the surface.</p>
<p>---</p>
<p>Meanwhile, on the surface, a sea of beasts could be seen populating the location Kent jumped into the crater. They had been commanded to come by the temple master of Annihilation.</p>
<p>The first loss he had incurred was just too humiliating for him. He wanted to see Kent cornered and struggling; however, in less than 5 minutes, he had lost over 100,000 beasts.</p>
<p>Though the number Kent had killed wasn’t close to wiping 10% of the beasts inside the forest, it was still a huge blow.</p>
<p>During the Primordial Era, Void Temple arrives, and it tries its best to mutate as many beasts, people, and any spirit beings it can. This is to ensure that when it opens, there will be enough army to wreak havoc and steal precious souls to open the Void Gates (Doom Gates).</p>
<p>So, since that was their main objective, they would hate to lose their manpower. So while Kent may have done what he did simply to survive, to them it was an insult, and as such it must be paid in full.</p>
<p>So he sent out twice the number he sent at first, and this time, there are thousands of Death Stage beasts in the mix.</p>
<p>But they aren’t the only monsters after Kent this time. There are 4 Pseudo-Void Beasts with them.</p>
<p>Far from the crater, standing west of where Kent would arrive, is a colossal wolf that seemed to have been made from condensed void matter, 20 meters tall, radiating a corrupted void aura. Its body appears half-real, half-erased, with jagged Claws and Fangs. Its eyes are hollow, and its maw leaks strands of liquid that melt the ground as they fall.</p>
<p>This hideous creature is the Void Howler Beast, one of the beasts whose fangs Kent needed. It is a Peak Death Stage beast that underwent mutation, becoming a Pseudo-Void Beast.</p>
<p>Its hollow gaze was fixed on the location where Kent had jumped into the crater, eagerly awaiting when he would emerge.</p>
<p>On the east stood another terror, a massive serpentine beast with a body wrapped in abyssal ice that seemed to freeze everything closer to it. Its scales resemble cracked glaciers, glowing faintly with deep-sea frost runes.</p>
<p>The Abyssal Frost Serpent had also been deployed.</p>
<p>But they aren’t the only ones. In the south, the Solar-Pelt Lion of the First Dawn is blazing like a morning sun.</p>
<p>These three beasts Kent needs their fangs for the formation that will take him closer to the edge of the galaxy.</p>
<p>However, standing at the north is the Seven-Horn Nightmare Devastating Shark Beast. It is a colossal shark with a body made from what appears to be metal. Seven massive horns curve backwards from its skull—each etched with ancient runes.</p>
<p>This is the beast Kent initially planned to come to Planet Bayla for. It is his other requirement to become a Sword Emperor.</p>
<p>From what he knows of this beast, only sword-related skills can hurt it.</p>
<p>Now, it had become a Pseudo-Void Beast, and judging by how it was looking in Kent’s direction, it had no holy intention toward him.</p>
<p>This time, he will either fight or flee. However, since he is now in the home of the Void Beasts, there is nowhere to run.</p>
<p>Soon, Kent was only a couple of km away from the surface... "Here we go."</p>
<p>150 Life Stage soul spirits appeared and sped toward the surface. Inside the Void Temple, the master of Annihilation sensed Kent coming, so he got ready to make sure he died today.</p>
