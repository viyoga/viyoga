<p>Agni was more than happy to share with Kent all he knew about flame cultivation. He had been obsessed with the Primordial Chaos dragon for a very long time.</p>
<p>He spent years chasing down leads on the primordial Chaos Dragon. He fought unnecessary battles, be it bidding wars or actual battles, just to secure something, anything related to the primordial Chaos Dragon.</p>
<p>He is the definition of a die-hard fan.</p>
<p>So being asked by the True Primordial Chaos dragon to teach him something was already blowing his mind to bits.</p>
<p>Thanks to his experience walking that path, perhaps no one else was better qualified to teach Kent about flame cultivation.</p>
<p>"When it comes to walking the flame path, there are many aspects that must be taken into consideration. The first of them is Flame Veins.</p>
<p>These veins are pathways through which the essence of flames can be harnessed.</p>
<p>Just like how we channel spiritual qi through our meridians, the flame veins act as meridians for the flame essence.</p>
<p>Of course, it goes beyond that. It has many uses and several advantages.</p>
<p>One being, aside from merely having the means to channel the essence of flame, can also use the flame veins to channel spiritual qi.</p>
<p>This means that forming Flame Veins can be used for both flame-related attacks and non-flame-related attacks."</p>
<p>Kent raised his brow, intrigued and shocked by what he had just heard.</p>
<p>Agni smiled.</p>
<p>This was just a strand of his consciousness, but that was enough to teach Kent what he needed to know about what is required to walk the flame path.</p>
<p>Of course, although the full consciousness out there would do a much better job, knowing the basics wasn’t too much to worry about.</p>
<p>He has a complete understanding of what he was saying, and Kent can feel that.</p>
<p>"I know what I said may sound unbelievable, but the truth of the matter is, being in the medium world will make things like this sound unbelievable.</p>
<p>However, there is so much out there that when you learn about it, you will be left in a state of shock, more than you are now."</p>
<p>Kent was indeed shocked.</p>
<p>The concept of having more pathways to channel energy into attacks was one he was familiar with.</p>
<p>Jest had already experimented with that path.</p>
<p>In his quest to move past the limit of the realm of a sword King, he explored this path, and what he found was enough for Kent to believe it was possible to form more meridians.</p>
<p>Of course, as an alchemist, he knew things like this were possible.</p>
<p>However, what blew Kent’s mind now was the fact that he had just been told that flame veins could be used as pathways to channel more spiritual qi into his attacks.</p>
<p>That was just too much.</p>
<p>Having an additional ten meridians was already enough to make someone monstrous.</p>
<p>However, things aren’t as simple as they sound now. Things are getting much more freaky, and thinking about what would happen once one manages to form 108,000 flame veins is just too much to even think of.</p>
<p>If one were to achieve it, there would be 108,000 different ways to harness spiritual qi. And if that isn’t too much to comprehend its danger, then perhaps it isn’t worth the struggle to even think about such ridiculousness.</p>
<p>"Lord Chaos, you have to know, in the world of cultivation, you will see and hear ridiculous things such as what I have just told you.</p>
<p>However, you must understand that in the grand scheme of things, those who manage to seize opportunities soar above all else.</p>
<p>This is why you should strive to walk down the path of the flame.</p>
<p>Now, about the Flame Cores.</p>
<p>Those are much more difficult to form. But when one manages to form just one, their energy output rises. It involves channeling a large amount of flame essence and compressing it into a concentrated form.</p>
<p>Consider it as a secondary energy source, where, in this case, only flame energy can be stored and harnessed.</p>
<p>This means with a flame core at your disposal, you wouldn’t even have to worry about running out of energy at any moment.</p>
<p>And you have to form seven of them."</p>
<p>Kent smiled weakly, knowing the path set before him, though beneficial, would demand a lot. In essence, he could already see himself getting even busier than he was now.</p>
<p>However, upon reflection, a primordial-grade technique was at stake here. Also, although the technique sounded impressive, he could already see a completely new path opening up for him.</p>
<p>He could see himself channeling devastating infernal energy through thousands of pathways, unleashing world-shattering attacks. That was already more than enough for him to know he had scored a good path.</p>
<p>"This all sounds great, and so far, I can already see a path opening. Also, I seem to have grasped the dangers waiting for me in the high and immortal worlds.</p>
<p>It seems there is more than what I can see now, and that alone is enough for me to know life will soon become chaotic for me.</p>
<p>This is why I have decided to add the flame path to my path now. I will walk the flame path and learn the technique. After all, while it might take time, I will become much stronger in the end."</p>
<p>Agni nodded, very happy that Kent had decided to walk the path of the flame.</p>
<p>"I am happy to hear that, Lord Chaos. Right now, you will have to do things on your own, but soon, when my actual body learns of you, I am sure I will do everything in my power to provide you with resources that will hasten your path."</p>
<p>Kent nodded, already knowing that one of these days, he would meet the true demigod named Agni, who had been eager to meet him, the primordial Chaos Dragon.</p>
<p>"So, what do I do now?" Kent asked, and Agni extended his hand, causing two scrolls to appear.</p>
<p>"These are the methods to create Flame Veins and Flame Cores. They will guide you to start walking the path of the flame. However, you must form at least 20,000 flame veins before attempting to form a core.</p>
<p>Also, you don’t have to worry about your path and comprehension rising. You just need to find the Flame Binding Pill and take it. It will hold your path for five years.</p>
<p>The tier should be at least nine, and you should take it before you learn these two methods. Of course, if you manage to form seven cores and create 108,000 veins within five years, you can take another pill called the Flame Release Pill.</p>
<p>They may be rare in your world, but once you get to a high world, you can easily find some of these pills."</p>
<p>"Thank you, Agni. I will be sure to pay you back when we meet again."</p>
<p>Agni smiled, "Merely meeting the primordial Chaos Dragon is enough for me. Also, the two techniques for creating the flame core and vein will be waiting for you on the outside."</p>
<p>Kent nodded, then started asking more questions about the universe out there waiting for him. Agni, of course, was more than happy to tell him all he wanted to know.</p>
<p>Soon, a day had passed since Kent and the others entered the tower. Outside, everyone was stunned, staring at the display board with expressions frozen in place.</p>
<p>The outcome many expected wasn’t happening at all. Some names that weren’t well-known or popular now populated the top rankings.</p>
<p>It just made no sense to all those looking at the display board.</p>
