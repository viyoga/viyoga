<p>During the Primordial Era, many powerhouses rose to prominence. They dominated the vast universe, making a name for themselves.</p>
<p>One such powerhouse was the Serpent Queen.</p>
<p>She was known for her impeccable control of the mind. Back then, many of these powerhouses were known for their deeds.</p>
<p>Some are known for the paths they carved for themselves.</p>
<p>The First Necromancer.</p>
<p>The First Soulmancer.</p>
<p>The First Alchemist.</p>
<p>These are all paths created by some people. Though most of these paths weren’t taken to the peak due to many limitations, the Serpent Queen was known to have taken mind cultivation to the peak.</p>
<p>She became one of the few who were known as progenitors.</p>
<p>However, during the Great War, she died fighting alongside the one she believed in.</p>
<p>After billions of years, she returned again. Hovering in the air with a giant serpent behind her was Zaila, the reincarnation of the Serpent Queen, the Mother of Minds.</p>
<p>Legends speak of the one who was able to defeat the Chaos Dragon in combat—the first and the only. They speak of her impeccable methods that allowed her to play the mind like it were a drum.</p>
<p>They speak of her display during the Great War when she manipulated allies, turning them against each other.</p>
<p>Her impeccable display saved countless lives; however, after a gruesome battle with three Abominations from an unknown universe, she fell with a smile on her face.</p>
<p>She died, but her essence still burns, and her true name will be awakened.</p>
<p>Kent stared at the serpent and Zaila, who was inside a bubble of water that had now turned red with a smile. Knowing what he knew, he was looking forward to seeing what would become of Zaila after her awakening.</p>
<p>After a few minutes, her true name entered Kent’s mind.</p>
<p>"She was once known as the Mother of Minds. She who controls the mind. The progenitor of manipulations.</p>
<p>Legends speak of the one who once gave Chaos a run for his money. Many called her Lady Chaos, for everywhere she went, chaos followed.</p>
<p>The one who knew everything before it was even said. The Mind Reader. The Mind Eater.</p>
<p>Her titles number in the hundreds, but she goes by only one name.</p>
<p>Rise Nyssalith, Mother of Minds, for your destiny awaits."</p>
<p>Right when Kent invoked her name, the bubble of red water burst open, and Zaila was revealed, looking as beautiful as ever.</p>
<p>Like her primordial spirit, her form had changed, making her look gorgeous. Her red hair cascaded down her back like a waterfall.</p>
<p>She held no weapons nor wore any armor. However, the large serpent in the air suddenly moved, circled the sky, then turned into energy and entered Zaila’s forehead.</p>
<p>At that moment, powerful energy burst out of her body. She extended her hand, and an elegant-looking red spear appeared in it.</p>
<p>A smile appeared on her lips as she admired the spear... After a while, she put away the spear and looked at her sisters.</p>
<p>When her gaze met her sisters’, they felt as if all their secrets were laid bare for her to see. It was terrifying. However, because they were sisters, none of them took offense at that.</p>
<p>However, Kent made sure she was aware. "Zaila dear, you might want to keep those eyes away for a while; you are scaring your sisters."</p>
<p>Zaila smiled and deactivated the ability she had used.</p>
<p>She flew into Kent’s arms and kissed him just like the rest of her sisters did. Kent, of course, enjoyed the kiss. He then looked at the serpent mark on her forehead, and a smile appeared on his lips.</p>
<p>"Let’s sit down first. I have something important to tell you all, especially you, Zaila," Kent said, waving his hand.</p>
<p>They vanished back to the harem tower. They sat down in the love garden, and he decided to share everything with his wives.</p>
<p>After a while, their jaws hung loose, dumbfounded to learn that he had received a visit from Zaila’s past. Of course, Kent said nothing about the illusion he had endured.</p>
<p>However, that was irrelevant since the main point was ensuring Zaila and her sisters understood their existence was much more special.</p>
<p>"So we all have past selves?" Lilian asked, and Kent nodded.</p>
<p>"The true names, though I am not sure, are from your past selves. I think you all did something impeccable in your past that made the universe give you unique names that only belong to you."</p>
<p>"If that was the case, was I truly that ruthless?" Neomi couldn’t help asking.</p>
<p>Her sister also thought the same; after all, they knew her names and what they meant, especially her Shara name, which made her the Mother of Killers.</p>
<p>Neomi turned and looked at Velyra, since she was one of the many who worshipped her.</p>
<p>"The Mother of Killers is known not for her beauty but for her incredible talent when it comes to killing," Velyra said what she knew, which only made Neomi want to bury herself.</p>
<p>"I knew it. Somehow, I am a monster in human skin."</p>
<p>Her sisters shook their heads, watching her have an identity crisis. Thankfully, she had a weapon spirit that knew something, so seeing her master struggling, the six-armed lady he got as a weapon spirit appeared.</p>
<p>"The master shouldn’t worry. It is true you once were Shara; however, you became Rakshasa, someone who led armies to fight great battles, saving billions.</p>
<p>Your legend is beautiful. So, while you had a blood-filled past, know that you survived and became someone the universe recognized." After saying that, the six-armed weapon spirit lady vanished back into a spear.</p>
<p>"You heard her. You once were a powerful general who led armies to defend the weak and innocent. You should be proud."</p>
<p>Neomi nodded and chose not to ponder it much for now.</p>
<p>She knew the truth would eventually come out, but until then, she would use what she had to defend and save those she could.</p>
<p>Her sisters were the same. They all wanted to forget the possibility that they were monsters in their past lives and instead live for the future.</p>
<p>Kent saw all this, and a smile appeared on his face. Knowing they were able to take the discovery quite well, he decided to take it to the next level.</p>
<p>"So, I have a message from your past, Zaila. She wants me to tell you to start the path of becoming a supreme right now, and to do that, she gave me a technique that will set you up immediately."</p>
<p>The ladies could only stare at Kent like he was some deity.</p>
