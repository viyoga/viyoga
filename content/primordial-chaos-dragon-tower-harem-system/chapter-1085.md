<p>Unity, who was much closer to Kaizo, smirked, hearing the frustration in Kaizo’s voice.</p>
<p>"You know, these people are actually fighting to save their world. So while their suffering was a thrill to you, maybe try being human for once and see this battle coming to an end as a good thing.</p>
<p>If you want non-stop battles, then wait. Once we go back, the dungeon is waiting for us to clear."</p>
<p>Kaizo grinned, smashing the head of a Monarch with his fist.</p>
<p>"I understand, Unity. I will not say such things out loud. Next time, I will say it inwardly." Unity just shook her head.</p>
<p>"Don’t mind him. He was dropped on his head when his mama gave birth to him," Unity said to her new bestie, Hanna, who was also an archer.</p>
<p>"I understand. We have one of him in this universe, too. His name is Danny." Hanna smiled and looked in the direction Danny was fighting. "I believe he is that mountain of a man over there."</p>
<p>Unity turned and looked at Danny. "I get it. In any case, know that on the next visit, I hope you and I have a duel to see who is the best."</p>
<p>"I would love that."</p>
<p>Unity fired an arrow, and Hanna also cemented today’s promise with her own arrow. But for now, ending the battle would be the next best thing.</p>
<p>---</p>
<p>Nara and Mara also made friends.</p>
<p>"So you three are Elementals," Nara said, looking toward three identical twins who, after a very chaotic battle, took a few hours of rest before coming back again.</p>
<p>They were identical, but each had different hair and eye colors. These distinctions were determined by the nature and color of their elements.</p>
<p>"Yes. I am Aria, an Ice Elemental," the blue-haired beauty said.</p>
<p>"I am Stella, a Fire Elemental," the silver-white-haired beauty also introduced herself.</p>
<p>"And I am Nova, a Lightning Elemental," the redhead also said.</p>
<p>Nara smiled and said, "I am Nara, half human, half wraith, and this is my baby sister, an angel as you can see."</p>
<p>Mara grinned. "We had the same mother and father... in case you are wondering."</p>
<p>Naturally, the three elemental sisters were wondering the same thing. How could two sisters, born of the same parents, be virtually different in race and appearance?</p>
<p>They were both beauties, but one was basically a demon and the other an angel. These two didn’t swim in the same pond, so being sisters was rather unexpected.</p>
<p>"We clearly didn’t wonder why you two are different yet the same. Trust us, we have some of them in our family too," Nova said with a small smile.</p>
<p>"Believe it or not, but we have a mother who is made of fire just like me, a twin daughter who is basically made of poison, and her twin sister made from blood. So we get you."</p>
<p>The five ladies laughed.</p>
<p>"Being in a large harem opens your eyes to see many weird yet beautiful things." Mara and the ladies nodded.</p>
<p>"So, do you ladies also have a schedule for when you get to be with your man?" Nara asked, and Mara added, "We created one just a few days ago before coming here."</p>
<p>Aria looked at her sisters. "We don’t have such an arrangement yet, but we will implement it soon. Though I have to ask... how did you ladies arrange it?"</p>
<p>"Two or maybe three per night. Of course, we also put measures in place so that one of us can have a night to remember if we do something good that impresses our man.</p>
<p>Of course, we both know Alina will get all the goodies, not because she has a magnificent ass, but her brain is always creating something good... Such a bitch," Nara smirked, and Mara chuckled.</p>
<p>Aria, Stella, and Nova grinned. "Oh, we understand. We have two of them, just that ours are also sisters with big beautiful jugs and butts."</p>
<p>Yet again, the ladies laughed, feeling the bond between them grow stronger.</p>
<p>"I wish we could talk to you three when we go back to our universe," Mara said with a sigh. Her sister tapped her shoulder and sighed too.</p>
<p>"Our man is a Paragon, so don’t worry, he will definitely find a way to open a doorway to your universe one of these days," Stella said, and her sisters nodded.</p>
<p>"He sure would. Just don’t be too surprised when you suddenly see us in your universe," Aria said with a small smile. They spent a few minutes planning what they would do once they met again.</p>
<p>After they had had enough fun, they moved to a different battlefield to help clear the rest of the enemies.</p>
<p>More friendships formed between the wives of the Paragon and Kent, promising to one day bring the two families together.</p>
<p>Hopefully, their next meeting wouldn’t be on a battlefield.</p>
<p>Time passed, and soon, the only battles that were happening were the ones where Nykali and Asmodeus kept torturing the pitiful warriors, and some random ones, mostly between Kent and the Monarchs he was farming blood essence from.</p>
<p>Soon, however, even Kent ended his battles and decided to watch Paragon battle the last three Nirvana Stage warriors.</p>
<p>"He is very strong. I wonder if he can survive a punch from me," Kaizo said, standing beside Kent.</p>
<p>The Chaos Dragon looked at Kaizo with the corner of his eye, a sudden urge to knock him overwhelming his senses.</p>
<p>"I need to check your brain and see if everything is alright in there," Kent said, causing those who heard him to laugh.</p>
<p>They could tell Kaizo was a brute through and through. However, they also saw him fight, which told them he was not a muscle brain. If anything, he was brilliant, but sometimes, the comments he made were just too brain-dead.</p>
<p>Clearly, everyone could tell that a single punch from the Paragon was enough to end their existence. However, despite knowing this, Kaizo still made such a comment.</p>
<p>"You know what, one of these days, I will send you down to hell to go have fun there," Kent said, and Kaizo’s eyes lit up...</p>
<p>"I would love that."</p>
<p>Kent just sighed. The brute was indeed all about battles.</p>
<p>A couple of minutes later, the scene of the battle changed. Paragon conjured some dark chains and bound the three together.</p>
<p>’Damn,’ Kent said inwardly, feeling his blood boil.</p>
<p>Suddenly, he saw a large red eye appear in the air, which, for some reason, froze the three instantly. Then, thousands of weapons appeared in the air, and with a wave, they shot forward, and like poking holes into tofu, the weapons pierced their bodies, ending them for good.</p>
<p>Thousands cheered as the three Nirvana Stage warriors were impaled by hundreds of weapons.</p>
<p>Kent smiled, watching as the tribulation slowly dissipated. When the last cloud left the sky, Kent sighed.</p>
<p>He turned and looked at Vexthra, who gave him a calm nod.</p>
<p>For a moment, there was peace and quiet, then the unexpected happened.</p>
<p>Paragon suddenly pushed his Vampire wife away just as a palm strike came striking at him from the void.</p>
<p>Instinctively, Kent tried to move. However, before he could, he saw the void open over Queenie, and yet another palm strike came.</p>
<p>"I got it." Vexthra moved and appeared between the attack and Queenie.</p>
<p>But then, Paragon had already taken the attack head-on, sending him flying back.</p>
<p>However, just when Vexthra was about to block the blow, Kent saw the space around her twist, and then she vanished.</p>
<p>"Vex-" Before Kent could finish his word, another void opened over Nari, freezing her in place.</p>
<p>"Just what in the hell?" He took his dragon form and appeared between the palm strike and Nari.</p>
<p>BOOM</p>
<p>Kent felt his world spin, but he willed himself to stay conscious. It was only when he stood up that he saw more void openings, and more attacks kept coming.</p>
<p>’Come on, Kent, you got this.’ Kent moved, and soon, his colossal body became a shield for more palm strikes.</p>
<p>Two minutes later, Kent was down to his last leg, thousands were injured, and Paragon was on the verge of death.</p>
<p>’Damn it.’</p>
<p>Kent willed himself to move and take Paragon’s place. He saw another attack coming for his wife, and so he decided to use his body as a shield.</p>
<p>However, Kent, knowing he might survive the attack but not his pal, whom he had never had a real conversation with, just yet, made up his mind to take his place...</p>
<p>However, before he could move, a calm yet furious voice spoke, and everything went still...</p>
<p>"ENOUGH."</p>
