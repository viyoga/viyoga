<p>Kaizo fell from the void, landing with a devastating thunder that instantly blasted away everyone in the impact zone.</p>
<p>Of course, he made sure he dropped in a zone dominated by the enemy soldiers, so all those who died from the shockwaves and those who were merely injured were all the enemy soldiers.</p>
<p>He stood up from the dust, standing 3 meters tall, his body covered in lightning armor.</p>
<p>Kaizo didn’t have to use his bloodline for this small battle; however, he had to, considering he was still in the process of breaking through to the Spirit Ascension stage.</p>
<p>"Die for me faster." With a single dash, hundreds were thrown into the air.</p>
<p>From within the void, all of Kent’s legion and wives started pouring out.</p>
<p>Alina, who saw the nature of the battle, quickly sealed the space, preventing anyone from leaving. Afterwards, she activated her killing formation and started targeting more enemies.</p>
<p>By then, Sylara had already begun her blood harvest.</p>
<p>There were hundreds of thousands of warriors to pick from, so she started, and in a way, she would be reforming her Blood Hunger Seal faster than she had hoped.</p>
<p>Kent wanted to take her to the savage region after they settled down in the high world, so she could form her Blood Hunger Seal. However, it seems she is way ahead of him now.</p>
<p>Selene took to the skies and started cutting through the warriors in the air. Unity, who had become much stronger, joined her while the rest of her sisters handled issues on the ground.</p>
<p>"Those Nethrans and Kathorians were too ugly, so I merely took their bodies from the Paragon Universe. However, now that I have more handsome and beautiful vessels, it looks like I will be having me some handsome incubus undeads soon."</p>
<p>Veera, the true necromancer in Kent’s legion, smiled as she unleashed her undead legion into the battle. She was one of the few who benefited greatly from their time in the Paragon Universe.</p>
<p>Thanks to the limitation lift in the Paragon Universe, she managed to gather undead that were comparable to Empyrean and even Life stage warriors in this universe.</p>
<p>Of course, thanks to the limitation in this universe, they were all now peak Heavenly Ascension stage warriors.</p>
<p>However, that is thousands of Heavenly Ascension stage warriors.</p>
<p>In no time, hundreds of Angels, Neburians, Nethrans, and more filled the entire battlefield. This sight sent everyone into a state of panic.</p>
<p>But she wasn’t the only one who had become a monster. Nykali, the Shade King, had also unleashed his thousands of undead spirits (Wraiths), filling the battlefield with howling and screeching sounds.</p>
<p>Naturally, those sounds were mixed with the true screams of the enemy, who started panicking.</p>
<p>Just Veera and Nykali were already enough to end this battle, considering all their undead legions were Heavenly Ascension stage warriors.</p>
<p>However, Kent wanted the enemy to feel true despair, so somebody like Neomi was on the battlefield killing like the mother of killers she was.</p>
<p>Lani’s laughter also filled the battlefield as she worked to remove more tattoos on her skin.</p>
<p>"Take no hostages. Kill them all."</p>
<p>Kent said as he unleashed three powerful swings, each releasing 450 sword arcs, shredding through the enemy.</p>
<p>His eyes were completely cold, killing intent radiating off them. The fact that he nearly lost his mother again, even if she had no idea how she was still alive, scared him.</p>
<p>So he was angry, and now, he was making them all pay.</p>
<p>"Kill them all," Kaizo echoed, and soon, the slogan "Kill them all" filled the battlefield.</p>
<p>"All Elves should retreat," High Priestess Runa shouted after she, Lyna, and Adina managed to move away from the chaos.</p>
<p>They lost thousands of warriors, but they are no longer needed on the battlefield.</p>
<p>"So that is Kent?" she asked, looking at the purple-haired, handsome young man whose sword was claiming lives on the battlefield.</p>
<p>"Yes," Adina nodded with a small smile as she watched the brat she had been missing for the past month. They had parted ways in the Valley of Torture, yet she couldn’t get him off her mind.</p>
<p>"I guess I now understand why the entire universe is searching for him," Lyna said, and then added, "He is beyond a monster. Just look at him; he is killing beings realms above him like it was nothing."</p>
<p>The two ladies nodded.</p>
<p>Just a minute ago, they were at the brink of death, but now, it seemed they would live to tell the tale of what happened here today.</p>
<p>"Runa, tell your people to stop this senseless massacre. We surrender," the same Ice Demon King who teased Runa now wanted to beg for his life.</p>
<p>His expression was dark.</p>
<p>Runa smirked and answered, "They are not my people, bastard. Can’t you see, or are you telling me those ugly eyes can’t see his ears are shorter than mine?"</p>
<p>Adina tried to laugh, but she managed to hold herself back.</p>
<p>Lyna, meanwhile, burst out in resounding laughter, one that made the expression on the Ice Demon’s face turn even darker.</p>
<p>"Maybe you should look behind you before asking for mercy."</p>
<p>The Ice Demon turned at once, sensing someone had appeared behind him. However, just when he turned, his gaze met the gaze of a one-eyed vampire demon.</p>
<p>"Hi, my name is Sylara." With that, the vampire lady’s fangs sank into his neck and sucked his blood and life out of him in one go.</p>
<p>His body instantly became weak. Sylara made sure to suck in his lifeblood so his stamina was drained, and so was his life.</p>
<p>"He is all yours," Sylara tossed his body toward High Priestess Runa, who unleashed four arrows, pinning the poor coward bastard to a large tree not far from them.</p>
<p>This is the elven garden, so there are trees around.</p>
<p>Kent turned and looked at the High Priestess, then at Adina and Lyna, before turning his gaze toward the ship hiding in the clouds.</p>
<p>A few seconds later, two heads fell from the clouds and landed with splat thuds. They burst open like watermelons before even landing on the ground.</p>
<p>The lady guard who stood behind the Incubus King appeared in the air and gave Kent a nod before vanishing into thin air.</p>
<p>It turned out the assassin sent from the Midnight Shadows had been her all along. Her mission was to kill the bastard, but because he was always surrounded by the three warriors that Kent killed using his blood runes’ pressure, she couldn’t.</p>
<p>Now, she had the chance, and it was all thanks to Kent.</p>
<p>Soon, only a few hundred enemy warriors were left, so Kent retreated and approached Adina, Lyna, and High Priestess Runa.</p>
<p>"Kent." Adina jumped into his arms, not caring whether or not she could control herself around him. Right now, she wanted to be in his arms.</p>
<p>Kent, of course, hugged her, making sure she felt safe and protected.</p>
<p>He looked at High Priestess Runa and Lyna and said, "I am Kent."</p>
<p>The two ladies nodded, already aware of his identity.</p>
<p>"I am High Priestess Runa of the Galen Elven Kingdom..." High Priestess Runa also introduced herself in the most polite way possible.</p>
<p>"I am Lyna Thalindra Drakana, a subordinate of Flame Lord Agni."</p>
<p>Kent raised his brow upon hearing Lyna’s words. However, before he could say anything, Vexthra appeared beside him.</p>
<p>"She will be waking up soon. Maybe take things inside. I will make sure the Elves prepare for migration."</p>
<p>Vexthra looked at Lyna and High Priestess Runa, then she turned her attention to Adina, who was curled up like a rabbit in Kent’s arms, and sent him a telepathic message.</p>
<p>’I approve.’</p>
<p>Kent smiled. "Thanks, Vex. I will reward you later."</p>
<p>High Priestess Runa wanted to ask a question; however, the next second, she found herself standing in a garden in a completely different place.</p>
