<p>Kent opened the system interface and began reading the messages that had appeared. He started with the 13 Layered Life Discs because they came with more messages, and he planned to review all of them.</p>
<p>He had failed to name some of his past life classes, so he wanted to see which ones he had missed and what he might have missed back then, since he had no system to check every small change.</p>
<p>&lt;You have absorbed the legacy of the Chaos Ascension King&gt;</p>
<p>&lt;Your 13 Layered Life Discs and Six Mark Fate Disc have activated and are assimilating with the legacy&gt;</p>
<p>&lt;Your 13 Layered Life Discs have assimilated with the Legacy and are extracting the lives you had lived&gt;</p>
<p>&lt;Primordial Chaos Dragon (Chaos) extracted ↠ You have awakened the Chaos Claw Brawler Class&gt;</p>
<p>&lt;Lord Genesis (Jest) extracted ↠ You have awakened the Genesis Swordsman Class&gt;</p>
<p>&lt;Faceless Assassin (Jaqen) extracted ↠ You have awakened the Faceless Assassin Class&gt;</p>
<p>&lt;Ruthless Bandit (Lex) extracted ↠ You have awakened the Grim Evil Spear Mage Class&gt;</p>
<p>&lt;Pill Celestial Kevin Sage extracted ↠ You have awakened the Nine Ascension Mind Demon Class&gt;</p>
<p>&lt;Divine Beast Tamer (Aurelion) extracted ↠ You have awakened the Divine Beast Tamer Class&gt;</p>
<p>&lt;Bone Lich Undead Emperor (Sanders) extracted ↠ You have awakened the Bone Lich Undead Necromancy Class&gt;</p>
<p>&lt;Nine Underworld Flame Blacksmith (Fjorge) extracted ↠ You have awakened the Nine Underworld Flame Blacksmith Class&gt;</p>
<p>&lt;Omniscience Dao Archer (Kalimndra) extracted ↠ You have awakened the Omniscient Dao Archer Class&gt;</p>
<p>Kent looked at the messages, and for a moment, a smile spread across his face. "I have come a long way."</p>
<p>Looking at the first name, Chaos, to the ninth, Kalimndra, the Omniscience Dao Archer, he knew he had come a very long way.</p>
<p>He still had 4 more lives to recall, but with just this one, he knew he could finally face the dangers he had faced in the past and walk away unscathed.</p>
<p>He had failed several times, but now he believed he would succeed, not because he had become much stronger. He knew he would succeed because he now had a sure path to becoming a True Primordial.</p>
<p>Kent tapped on the Chaos Claw Brawler Class to see what it entailed.</p>
<p>[Chaos Claw Brawler Class]</p>
<p>[Legion: Void Legion (Locked)]</p>
<p>[Rank: Celestial+]</p>
<p>[Description: Claws are meant to cut through flesh, tear through bones, and litter battlefields with chaos and mayhem. The Chaos Claw Brawler Class stands as the deadliest Brawler Class in existence. The more the chaos, the stronger it gets, empowering the wielder to cause even more chaos.]</p>
<p>The description wasn’t the most tasteful, but Kent, who had wielded this class before, knew it was on point. The more the chaos, the deadlier the Chaos Claw Brawler Class became.</p>
<p>In the past, the only times he stopped were when there was nothing left to kill or when he had spent all his energy. Now, he would never run out of energy, even if he wanted to.</p>
<p>So the only thing that would cause him to stop was the absence of targets to kill.</p>
<p>Kent was also pleased to see the name of the legion he had assembled during the Primordial Era. He had yet to gather five of them, so it remained locked.</p>
<p>But there was a good chance things would be different soon.</p>
<p>He quickly scanned the rest of his legions and, to his shock, more memories of the lives he had lived flooded his mind, revealing what he had missed.</p>
<p>Lord Genesis formed the 14 Lost Knights. They were his masterpiece since he was the one who saved them, cared for them, and provided them with resources and a path to walk.</p>
<p>Faceless Assassin turned out to have a special legion known as the Forgotten Faceless Agents. This memory had just returned to him, and it was part of how he died during the shadow era.</p>
<p>It turned out that after winning the Shadow Wars, Faceless returned to the Shadow Realm to continue his exploration. While there, he met a group of 45 assassins, each unique in their own way.</p>
<p>They did not know where they came from or where to go. So Faceless made them his agents, and together they explored the Shadow Realm for years until Faceless received the signal to reincarnate.</p>
<p>He left them there but promised to return for them one day.</p>
<p>Kent had always entertained the idea that Faceless had the largest legion since he created the Midnight Shadows. But now, he had discovered the true shadow legion, and they are waiting for him inside the Shadow Realm.</p>
<p>Ruthless Bandit had the Night Riders. While they were already a team, his arrival had forced them to grow stronger and become better versions of themselves.</p>
<p>Kevin Sage had also formed the Eternal Wrath Legion. It was a team of unnamed heroes from hundreds of races. He gathered them, provided for them and using his extensive knowledge, made them into a legion of deadly warriors.</p>
<p>Divine Beast Tamer formed the Thirteen Divine Wonders, of which Yukiko is a member. They are a team of 13 powerful Overlord Beasts, each with a unique talent.</p>
<p>Of course, they were dangerous and unique.</p>
<p>They were the leaders of the Beast Legion. But unlike Faceless, who saw the Midnight Shadows as only an organisation, the Divine Beast Tamer saw all his beasts as a legion, and the universe recognised them all, making them his largest legion yet.</p>
<p>Nine Underworld Flame Blacksmith, of course, formed the Hell Devastators. They were the champions of the Damnation Arena, the same arena he had dominated.</p>
<p>Bone Lich Undead Emperor, of course, formed the Undead Legion.</p>
<p>As for the Omniscience Dao Archer, he formed the legendary Sky Legion. They were a team of powerful archers formed from rejected Elven warriors. They had failed to join any of the Elven armies, so Kalimndra took them in and trained them.</p>
<p>Memories of every last one of them had returned to him. Now, all that remained was finding them, which he knew would eventually happen.</p>
<p>Kent sighed, then focused on the next set of messages.</p>
<p>&lt;Your Six Mark Fate Disc has assimilated with the Legacy and is extracting the Six Primal Bloodlines&gt;</p>
<p>&lt;Blood Calamity Slaughter King Bloodline extracted&gt;</p>
<p>&lt;Do you want to assimilate with this bloodline and make it yours?&gt;</p>
<p>&lt;Error: You have yet to make contact with the remaining True Primordials. Failed to extract bloodlines&gt;</p>
<p>"I suppose meeting the rest should be on my next itinerary," Kent chuckled. His focus on the message showed that he had successfully extracted the Slaughter King’s bloodline.</p>
<p>He turned and looked at the real Tower.</p>
<p>"What will happen if I initiate the assimilation?"</p>
<p>"The Master will unlock the main Limitless Technique that came with the Blood Calamity Slaughter King Bloodline. The Master will then be required to comprehend it, which can take some time. However, when it is over, the Master’s main bloodline will become stronger."</p>
<p>"That is good. Then tell me, for the mission to kill the Dark Empress, can I take my sister with me, or some of the people from the Chaos Chasm?"</p>
<p>Yet again, Tower nodded. "The Master can take Lady Fiannarel with him. However, she will be suppressed to the Celestial Stage. Aside from that, the Master can also make the seven tribe leaders his Blood Warriors. While they will be suppressed, they can still be of great help."</p>
<p>"Take more than that, and the Heavenly Dao will break, exposing the universe to all kinds of threats."</p>
<p>Kent smiled upon hearing this. "Then kindly inform them of the latest development. If they accept, bring them here."</p>
<p>"As you command, Master."</p>
<p>Tower vanished, and Kent initiated the bloodline assimilation process...</p>
