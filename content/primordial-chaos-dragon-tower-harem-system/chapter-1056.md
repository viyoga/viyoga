<p>Kent truly expected a challenge. To some extent, he even expected the Planet King to be stronger than Hell King Bliz...</p>
<p>However, the one he is fighting now is very weak.</p>
<p>For someone with immortal qi and a Supreme-grade bloodline, he is very weak.</p>
<p>[I believe I know what is happening here.] From within Kent’s soul universe, Gaap spoke, drawing Kent’s attention to him...</p>
<p>’What seems to be the issue?’ Kent asked.</p>
<p>[I believe he has two bloodlines conflicting with each other. Right now, his true strength is nowhere near what it should be. I believe he is even working on merging the two together.</p>
<p>Once he is done, there is no telling how much stronger he will become. However, the current him now is much weaker than even the City Lords back in Hell.]</p>
<p>’Well, that is just disappointing,’ Kent smirked.</p>
<p>Watching what he saw to be a dangerous man now scream in pain on the ground made him wonder just what happened a while back, warranting a raise in the quest danger level.</p>
<p>The quest difficulty was raised when the Planet King had finished absorbing the forsaken bloodline. That moment was unexpected, and so it warranted a rise in the danger level he possessed.</p>
<p>However, his ambition and greed for power got the better of him, so he tried to merge the two. This sparked a backlash that reduced his strength.</p>
<p>However, he was still strong enough to kidnap the three ladies and even decided to attack Planet Nictus and other worlds after his wedding.</p>
<p>However, he never expected someone like Kent to arrive seeking his head.</p>
<p>In a way, Kent came at the right time, which was the wrong time for the Planet King. If he had waited 20 more days, he wouldn’t be having it this easy.</p>
<p>Watching as a bloodbath unfolded on the wedding ground, charred bodies scattered everywhere, Kent could only sigh.</p>
<p>’I really hope back in the future, a worthy opponent would be waiting for me.’</p>
<p>He turned back to the Planet King and took away the fire. He waited for the poor bastard to calm down...</p>
<p>"In your next life, be sure not to associate yourself with the forsaken race... They are not good company, and I, Chaos, will never stop hunting them and their associates down."</p>
<p>Perhaps even feeling pity for the bastard, Kent summoned Gaap and drove it through his heart. He truly wanted to have a good battle; however, he was met with disappointment.</p>
<p>Of course, if he hadn’t met the Paragon again inside the Pill Ascension Realm, perhaps he would have had a good battle. But his trip down to Hell has completely changed everything for him.</p>
<p>In a way, Kent defies the norm and has done something he never would have done had it not been for Paragon.</p>
<p>The moment the Planet King died, the quest message rang along with a notification telling him he had become a Sword Sage.</p>
<p>’The only requirement now is to gain five more sword enlightenments in the sword path, and I will become a Sword King,’ Kent smiled...</p>
<p>"I may be strong, but when it comes to sword mastery, I am still far from being the best in a high world. I need to catch up."</p>
<p>After four good hours of massacre, in which Lady Kami, Azela, General Brianna, and Commander Agnes even joined, the legacy of the Planet King was wiped out.</p>
<p>News immediately spread, telling everyone that a new king is in town and that his name is Chaos. Kent intentionally chose not to use his real name for obvious reasons.</p>
<p>However, the mysterious Chaos has spread everywhere, and in no time, the six planets that were under the control of the Planet King and Planet Nictus had all sworn their allegiance to Kent.</p>
<p>It cost him seven days in the past, but he managed to add seven high worlds to his guardian tower, allowing him to immediately start forming another asteroid.</p>
<p>Kent, now left with only seven days to remain in the past, set out to accomplish many things before his time ran out...</p>

<p>Planet Solan is the home world of the Supreme White Tiger Clan, one of the powerhouses in this star system.</p>

<p>There are rumors that even say the ancestor of the Supreme White Tiger Clan has reached the rank of a god; however, he is suppressing himself until he is ready to take the tribulation.</p>

<p>He is meditating in a quiet atmosphere.</p>

<p>"Somebody who has a dying wish," a Lord Immortal said, his killing intent leaking. If Kent saw this, he would probably run with his tail tucked between his legs.</p>
