<p>After years of evil deeds, the legend of the evil Sect Master was over. However, the pain he left behind wasn’t something that could be cleared in a day.</p>
<p>The trauma, the pain, the memories—these were not things that could be erased easily. But the most important thing, which was the Sect Master himself, had been taken care of, thanks to the vixens doing their best to ensure he was sent away in the most agonizing way possible.</p>
<p>Ingrid, Neomi, Lilian, and Unity were very thorough with their kill, so there was no way such evil would find its way back.</p>
<p>Now, the legacy of the evil Sect Master would forever remain inside the Void.</p>
<p>Kent, who could only sigh after seeing what the ladies had done, turned and faced the large expanse of sea stretching beyond the limit of his gaze.</p>
<p>"So, this is the Dark Sea," he said, already looking forward to when he would cross it and witness its dangers. It was the sea that divided the various continents, and within it were thousands of terrifying sea beasts.</p>
<p>After looking at it for a few more minutes, he sighed and turned to Ingrid. "Kindly take us back, dear."</p>
<p>Ingrid nodded, and the next second, she opened the Void and pulled them all through it. It closed behind them, leaving only the mess caused by the battle as a reminder of what had transpired.</p>
<p>After they left, the Kingdom Protector appeared.</p>
<p>"Just who are those ladies, and where are they from?" The display they had given her was enough to convince her that they were not normal humans.</p>
<p>"Just what is it about that kid and the people that surround him? First, it was that brute, now, these four ladies"</p>
<p>Kent had displayed some remarkable skills that left her stunned. But in the end, it was Unity, Ingrid, Neomi, and Lilian who had caught her interest and left her wondering about so many things.</p>
<p>But since no one was there to answer her questions, she could only sigh and leave. She would keep an eye on Kent, and perhaps, if she was worthy, she would learn something about them one day.</p>
<p>Kent and the ladies reappeared inside the underground cave where Veydris, Sylara, Uncle Drew, and Val were waiting for them.</p>
<p>After handling their task, Saden and the three Soul Sentinels were already back inside the tower.</p>
<p>"What should we do with this blood cauldron?" Uncle Drew asked.</p>
<p>"Let’s dispose of it," Kent replied without hesitation. Right now, the Sect Master was dead, so everything linked to him had to be wiped out completely.</p>
<p>"Then let me." Ingrid stepped forward and opened the void yet again, pulling the Cauldron inside.</p>
<p>"That is very convenient of you, Ingrid," Kent smiled.</p>
<p>"Then, the mission is over. We should all head back inside the Tower. I will go back to the sect and later join you."</p>
<p>They nodded and left. Kent sighed and went back to the Sword Gate, where he immediately entered the Tower.</p>
<p>He appeared inside the Harem Tower and quickly found the ladies at the Yin Pool.</p>
<p>Upon reaching there, he saw Nara and Zaila passed out, with Mara and Camilla beside them, supporting their bodies as they recovered.</p>
<p>"How are they?" Kent asked.</p>
<p>"All physical and internal wounds have been healed. Right now, they can only sleep and wake up when they want," Elsa replied, making Kent nod.</p>
<p>"They deserve that much sleep. After what they went through, I wouldn’t be surprised if they slept for a whole month or two." He took a deep breath.</p>
<p>"You can go back to your training if you want; after all, they will be sleeping for a while."</p>
<p>"We will wait for them to wake up," Selene replied, and the ladies nodded in agreement. Kent nodded.</p>
<p>"Then, in that case, I will be busy with a few things, so let me know when they wake up." They nodded, and he left. He went back to the Sword Tower.</p>
<p>He sat down and pulled out the messages he had received.</p>
<p>The first thing he saw was the quest completion message.</p>
<p>⟪Congratulations on completing the quest ’Clash with Evil.’ Your rewards have been released.⟫</p>
<p>⟪Rewards: Received⟫</p>
<p>→ 20,000,000 Tower Points</p>
<p>→ True Name Token (Five)</p>
<p>→ Memory Key</p>
<p>The plan was to finish this quest before awakening the True Name of his ladies. However, he had to do it after obtaining the True Name tokens through other means.</p>
<p>Now, he has five more True Name tokens. This means he can awaken five more ladies, considering he had already made provisions for Val, Alina, Mara, and Camilla.</p>
<p>Even Vexthra has a True Name token to her name, which he will use the moment he brings her into the Tower the next time he visits her.</p>
<p>He wondered if he could place her inside the Tower during his next visit, but Gaia explained that it had to be a process because she was from a higher world.</p>
<p>This process involved creating a channel she could use to enter the Tower.</p>
<p>That process will begin the moment Kent visits her next time. This also means soon, she will have a specialised true name for herself.</p>
<p>Aside from that, he now has three Memory Keys. This means the chances of him learning more about his past as Jest, the Sword Progenitor, are guaranteed.</p>
<p>Strangely, all three keys have been code-marked with the name (Jest), meaning they can only be used for his memories as Jest.</p>
<p>Not that he was upset or anything. This was enough for him to take his swordsmanship talent to the next level.</p>
<p>He had already seen the effects when he used the first Memory Key, so while he knew this would be a smooth ride, he couldn’t help but wonder what would happen next.</p>
<p>He was undoubtedly excited about this. It felt almost too good to be true, but he knew it was legit.</p>
<p>"This is great," he smiled, then looked at the next messages in the same quest.</p>
<p>Thanks to the additional requirements added, now that he managed to complete the rescue within 30 days, he received additional rewards.</p>
<p>⟪Additional Rewards: Received⟫</p>
<p>→ Celestial Dew of Rebirth x2</p>
<p>→ Memory Key (Chaos Dragon)</p>
<p>→ Undead Schematic Creator x2</p>
<p>→ Undead Schematic x2</p>
<p>→ Dungeon Seeker Card Upgrader</p>
<p>→ Dungeon Master Token x4</p>
<p>→ SS-Ranked Dungeon Seeker Card (One Dungeon)</p>
<p>"Damn..."</p>
