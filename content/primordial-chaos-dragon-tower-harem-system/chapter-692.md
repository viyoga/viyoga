<p>On the first floating island, Nara, Lani, Elaine, Val, Vaelros, Khaazal, Gorzak, Zharkaz, and Yi Lai were clearing there. The team-up wasn’t in any particular order. Kent just wanted them to test their strength and see where they must improve, since the first floor was easy enough.</p>
<p>Their side was also dealing with the same type of spiders as the ones Unity’s team was handling on the middle floating island.</p>
<p>It wasn’t necessary, but Nara decided to ride her Hellhorse in battle today. She sat on the terrifying horse, whose body had become almost metal, perhaps bony, with green hellfire burning through the sockets of its eyes.</p>
<p>The moment Nara sat on the Hellhorse, her eyes turned green like the color of the flames, and her two swords were engulfed in flames. As she moved, green flames trailed her.</p>
<p>The Hellhorse was doing all the movements, so all she was doing was swinging her swords, killing and immobilizing as many as she could.</p>
<p>The number of spiders she was immobilizing was higher than the number she was killing. This was because, just like the predicament Vari found herself in, Lani, being an assassin, faced the same dilemma.</p>
<p>However, since Nara was creating the chances, all she was doing was going for the kill. Of course, she could use her scythe, just like how Vari didn’t use hers.</p>
<p>Seeing this as an easy battle, she was using the daggers, honing her skills for when she would truly find herself at a disadvantage, where even her scythe would be useless.</p>
<p>However, Elaine was doing better than one might think. She wasn’t using her death abilities. Instead, she wielded two swords.</p>
<p>One of the swords was white and the other was black.</p>
<p>One might expect a healer to use mage-like skills to kill, but Elaine was doing the opposite. When she awakened her Primordial spirit, the aspect of her that was once known as the Contractor had awakened.</p>
<p>The Contractor was recognized as one of the greatest killers in the history of the universe. That part of her uses the sword, and while she is not a swordswoman per se, she qualifies to become one.</p>
<p>She was dual-wielding, and judging by the speed at which she was moving and killing, it was clear her understanding of the swords was on another level.</p>
<p>As for Val, she was just whooshing through the forest, emitting potent Lunar energy. It was as if the moon goddess had descended upon the space, and given the number of spiders she had already killed, it was safe to say things were going remarkably well for her.</p>
<p>Vaelros was also killing with his Chains of Torment, and Khaazal, the wyvern, wasn’t falling behind either. He wielded his spear, wind gushing out as he moved across the battlefield.</p>
<p>Gorzak, Zharkaz, and Yi Lai were equally stunning on the battlefield. They didn’t seem to be in any difficult situation at all.</p>
<p>Eventually, they were nearing the completion of their battle. Meanwhile, the battles on Islands 2 and 3 had already come to an end.</p>
<p>Island 2 was handled by Lilian, Vari, Zaila, Princess Lovina, Zareth, Nykali, Veera, and Volthar. Their team was just too robust.</p>
<p>When the battle started, Zaila used ’Mind Shock’ on hundreds of the spiders, and Princess Lovina unleashed her poison.</p>
<p>It was a well-coordinated and straightforward attack that brought the spiders’ demise much faster. The two only saved a few for Vari to use as target practice.</p>
<p>In the meantime, Nykali only collected the souls, and Veera raised the dead spiders to become cannon fodder until they leave the dungeon.</p>
<p>She has different classes of undead, so she deemed these spiders cannon fodder. Her primary targets were the floor bosses. They were the ones she wanted to add to her undead legion.</p>
<p>As for Island 3, Selene, Velyra, Neomi, Mara, Elsa, Sylara, Aetheris, Kazarak, and Ankaara handled it. The battle started slowly at first, but the moment blood started pouring on the battlefield, things became much more mesmerizing.</p>
<p>Velyra, whose control over blood is rather high, started to kill much faster, using the blood of the slain as an extension of her sword.</p>
<p>She was shaping the blood to do more than immobilize. To some degree, she was able to tap into the blood inside the wounded spiders and kill them from within.</p>
<p>Though the spiders don’t have the same blood as some beasts or humans, the liquid within them responded to every command Velyra gave, and, like her allies, the blood was what she depended on for most of the kills.</p>
<p>However, she wasn’t the only one who used the blood to her advantage. Sylara also exploited it to her advantage. At first, she had to drink it to gain energy, but this time, just being around it was enough to give her the same effect.</p>
<p>She merely used the blood to coat her claws, and the rest was history.</p>
<p>The Neomi also lived up to her name as the mother of killers. With so much death happening around her, she became much more deadly. Even after the battle ended, she felt like joining in again.</p>
<p>However, by the time they arrived at the second island, the battle had also ended. Then she went to the first, but that place too had ended the fight.</p>
<p>But when they arrived at the middle island, the battle was ending. She quickly joined in to handle the rest.</p>
<p>*Screeeeeech*</p>
<p>Suddenly, a screeching sound came from deep within the forest. "The Floor boss is coming... Hahaha," Kaizo’s voice boomed, readying himself for a good battle.</p>
<p>However, his dreams were shattered the moment the Floor boss, towering 30 meters tall, appeared, when a soft voice suddenly entered everyone’s ear.</p>
<p>"Blossom Feisty Unity Arrow."</p>
<p>A sleek, rainbow-like arrow streaked through the air, entering one of the ten eyes on the Floor Boss, ending its existence before it could show its true powers.</p>
<p>It didn’t even get to show how monstrous it is.</p>
<p>Unity giggled, putting away her bow as she gazed at Kaizo and her sisters, who clearly wanted to have a go at the spider monstrosity she had just taken down.</p>
<p>Unity may not look like it, but just like how she can be quiet, innocent, and calm like Blossom, she can also be annoying like Feisty.</p>
<p>Kent and Vexthra flew down, landing beside the ladies, who immediately surrounded him.</p>
