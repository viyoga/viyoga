<p>After finishing everything up, Kent and the two cosmic beings teleported into Tower City.</p>
<p>Gaia made sure they appeared atop a mountain several kilometers away from the city, where everything would come into view.</p>
<p>In reality, the mountain they appeared on is part of the city, since it has a garden with several fruits already ripe. The garden was meant for relaxation.</p>
<p>This means he and his ladies can come there if they want the view.</p>
<p>But the view is something that is in abundance, for the city he was staring at wasn’t something that could be found anywhere in the universe.</p>
<p>Gaia is a Cosmic Weaver.</p>
<p>She can see everything inside this universe and, if allowed, outside this universe.</p>
<p>This means she has the power to see every village, town, city, country, world, starfield, galaxy, and the entire universe whenever she wants.</p>
<p>And so, using the Tower City builder that Kent gave her, she looked and picked from the billions of views in the universe and created her own.</p>
<p>The cloud buildings. Yes. There are buildings in the clouds.</p>
<p>The rivers, pools, and everything you would expect from the modern world could be found there. There are the herb gardens, the cultivation havens, the inns, and everything else a cultivation world has to offer.</p>
<p>She blended everything together to bring Kent’s vision to reality. Kent wanted to bring a modern world and a cultivation world together when he first awakened the tower.</p>
<p>He wanted to merge those two into his tower, which only he and his people would enjoy.</p>
<p>Basically, he wanted to blend many cultures into one and make it a reality, and right now, Gaia has started that.</p>
<p>Some cultural aspects of Earth can be seen there. There are others he will learn about later.</p>
<p>The diamond grounds, the glassy environment, the lush green gardens, the tranquility of it all, and the happiness he was feeling as he stared at it all.</p>
<p>"This is beautiful," Kent couldn’t help but say as he looked at the clean, modern, futuristic, yet medieval lush city lying before his eyes.</p>
<p>⟪When it comes to building stuff, I am your gal, Master.⟫ Gaia puffed her chest out, showing she was indeed the gal when it comes to building new things.</p>
<p>"You, indeed, are the best. I can tell you have put a lot into it. However, I have a question," Kent looked at Gaia strangely. He saw something that warranted an explanation.</p>
<p>"Why is there an image of you at that gate that reads, ’Sheriff Gaia’? I don’t recall asking you to create a replica of Texas."</p>
<p>⟪Since you are busy with stuff, I can only step in and act as the law of this city. With me around, there will be law and order here.⟫</p>
<p>"But the city is empty. Aside from us and the ladies, nobody else knows such a majestic place exists."</p>
<p>⟪Yet. For now, these are the only people you trust enough to show this world. But you are forgetting something: this is just the Human continent. There is a whole world out there where you will meet new people.</p>
<p>Some will need your help, in which case you will become their guardian. Knowing you, more ladies will come, and that means more people to fill the city.</p>
<p>You also have the Legion Tower, where you have already started growing your force. Though you planned on building barracks for them, this city will be like their relaxation space.</p>
<p>I can go on and list more, but the more you explore this world and even ascend to higher worlds, the more people you will have in your circle. You don’t have to look at it now; focus on the future.</p>
<p>Meaning, when the time comes, you will need a law enforcer, and who better to do that than the Mayor of the city... this gal.⟫</p>
<p>Gaia’s cute smile made Akira want to pinch her nose, but she knew Gaia would instinctively punch her, and that wouldn’t be good for the tower.</p>
<p>"Fine. Just know that as the mayor and sheriff of this city, you are obligated to ensure its development and growth. Do your best to make sure it keeps running and keeps developing."</p>
<p>⟪I have a generous donor, so don’t worry. I will ensure the city keeps growing for years to come.⟫ She smiled, and Kent just smirked.</p>
<p>He knew his tower points were in trouble, but he didn’t care. Soon, he will be swimming in tower points, so he can only let her do as she pleases.</p>
<p>"Let’s check it out before bringing the ladies." Thus, the dragon and his two assistants started to tour the city, going from one place to the next.</p>
<p>⟪The restaurants will be operated using androids. But since we don’t have any residents yet, they are all empty.⟫</p>
<p>Kent nodded.</p>
<p>Back on Earth, some restaurants had robots acting as waiters, so he knew that could be implemented. Of course, he knew androids are basically humans in machine form, as he had experimented with that topic in one of his movies.</p>
<p>’I had so much fun writing the movie. Oh, how I miss being a director,’ his mind wandered back to when he was a director back on Earth.</p>
<p>Now, he was in a cultivation world, and he wasn’t upset about it. If anything, he was much more excited about having the ability to be with women now.</p>
<p>After reminiscing about the past for a few seconds, they moved on and continued to explore the city for a while.</p>
<p>When they were done, they went back to the mountain garden.</p>
<p>"I love it. Now, work on building the Harem Tower too. You already know what to do, so make it happen." Gaia nodded.</p>
<p>⟪I can weave a few soul threads into the city, which will allow those who will be staying in it to become more attuned with the soul energy of the universe.</p>
<p>This means everyone who stays inside this city for a while will automatically become a soul cultivator.⟫ Akira proposed, and Kent accepted in a heartbeat.</p>
<p>"Feel free to do that. But first, why don’t we go create the third undead, and then we can go save Dori, your sister?"</p>
