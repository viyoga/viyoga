<p>Vari came out of the Primordial Chamber after Camilla, looking nothing like how she entered.</p>
<p>Naturally, she was an assassin, so it was expected of her to walk out clad in black. However, she walked out in white armor with white hair.</p>
<p>Of course, this begs the question: has she fled from the darkness and chosen to become an angel? But, of course, the answer was given in a form of transition that occurs from head to toe, changing into her darker side.</p>
<p>Her hair turned from white to black, and so did her armor.</p>
<p>This was because she awakened the Yin-Yang Space. Naturally, this means she now has both light and darkness affinity. And that was precisely what she was meant to be.</p>
<p>Her Darkness Element was the yin, and the light was the yang, and in all, she remained an assassin.</p>
<p>Ironic when you think about it. Someone who uses the light element as an assassin—it just doesn’t make sense, but in truth, it was that part of her that made her dangerous.</p>
<p>Most assassins, or those with a darkness affinity, tend to be limited when they face someone who uses the light element.</p>
<p>Light and darkness don’t blend, but for Vari, she had gained the power to walk hand in hand with both light and darkness, just like Lilian, who has a dual elemental affinity.</p>
<p>Inside her Yin-Yang Space, there is darkness on one side and light on the other, showing her affinity for both.</p>
<p>And as someone whose elemental mastery has already reached the perfect stage, she has become more than dangerous. Her Tier 17 Primordial Spirit regressed, and Vari joined her sisters on the stairs.</p>
<p>Then came Nara, who awakened the Hell Space. That space is natural to all Night Riders, but each has unique touches that are special to the owner.</p>
<p>For Nara, who is half human, half wraith, there is a special place inside her Hell Space that can be called Wraith Space.</p>
<p>There is a hefty tome with a special technique that once took the Wraith Queen from the ashes to the peak hidden inside the Wraith space.</p>
<p>The Wraith Spirit Art was once a powerful technique known only to the Wraith Queen.</p>
<p>This Wraith Space is small, but what it houses is something that could bring chaos to the entire universe if discovered.</p>
<p>However, inside her Hell Space, nobody can find it unless, of course, she willingly allows them access. Not that they could take it out, considering her Primordial Spirit is one of the most terrifying spirits ever awakened so far.</p>
<p>She was at Tier 18 when she regressed to Tier 1. But that wasn’t the terrifying part. All Night Riders have an affinity for the Hellflames.</p>
<p>However, while Nara herself has perfect control of the Hell Flame, her Primordial Spirit’s affinity is already at the Harmony stage, two stages above Nara. However, that isn’t all; she also possesses the Hell Flame Art.</p>
<p>If all flame arts in existence were ranked, the Hell Flame Art would be in the top three, and she had already mastered the first two stages of this terrifying art, something Nara had yet to start herself.</p>
<p>When her sisters found out about this, they all wondered if this was natural for all Primordial Spirits. It just didn’t make any sense.</p>
<p>Not only are their Primordial Spirits special, but they also seem to be utterly different versions of themselves. In the end, they concluded that all of this had something to do with their True Names.</p>
<p>But if Nara were to keep her Primordial Spirit at Tier 18, then even Empyreans would fear her, mainly because the Hell Flame Art is dangerous.</p>
<p>For now, her danger level has been reduced since her Primordial Spirit is now at Tier 1. However, she is not to be underestimated.</p>
<p>After Nara, Lani also emerged with her classic Joker laughter, oozing both Shadow and Death Qi. She awakened the Shadow Space, and with it came the shadows of the slain.</p>
<p>All beings she had ever killed were there inside her Shadow Space. However, while that looked concerning, it was the Shadow Domain inside her Shadow Space that looked even more concerning.</p>
<p>That part of the space came about when her Primordial Spirit regressed from Tier 17 to Tier 1, expanding the Shadow space. Inside the domain are millions of shadows that, at first glance, you would know are not ordinary.</p>
<p>Immortals, Demigods, and a few gods. Perhaps if she had ever killed a Supreme or Celestial, their shadows would find their way there.</p>
<p>Kills from the past are now inside her Shadow Space. However, they don’t move, and she also can’t make them move. They stand there, their intimidating presence evident.</p>
<p>And since her Primordial Spirit has no idea what to do with them either, she just ignored them and focused her attention on climbing the Stairs.</p>
<p>It was a weird space, but when Elaine appeared, it got even more bizarre. She appeared with Life &amp; Death Space. Inside, life is on one side and death on the other, just like Lilian’s Ice and Fire Space or Vari’s Yin-Yang Space.</p>
<p>On the side of life, there is green life everywhere—potent herbs and nourishing waters. But on the death side, everything there is death.</p>
<p>Nothing that reeks of life can be found there. Even when Elaine picked a green plant from the life side and tried to send it to the death space, the moment she stepped in, the plant withered.</p>
<p>It was an instant withering that even shocked Elaine. This made her wonder what would happen to those she put there. Would they die instantly?</p>
<p>The answer remained a mystery, and since Kent told them not to allow anyone aside from their sisters to enter each other’s spaces, she wouldn’t test that theory.</p>
<p>But she also has the Tree of Life in her life space, which so far has only three green leaves. On the death side, blank tombstones could be found in random places.</p>
<p>She doesn’t know what will come out of those graves, but she is looking forward to that.</p>
<p>After helping her Tier 17 Primordial Spirit regress, she joined her sisters on the Stairs. They are all climbing steadily, making sure they catch up to Zaila.</p>
