<p>Alina aspired to create a formation that worked without her commanding it.</p>
<p>In this sense, when danger arises, the formation would assess its severity and act accordingly. If it needed to defend, it would, and if it needed to attack, it would.</p>
<p>Of course, all of this would be made possible by Alina’s own judgment. She created it, so what she knew went into the formation and how it was meant to work.</p>
<p>Her sisters had helped her, but she knew the person who could help her the most was none other than Kent, who had lived many lives and, in all of them, had fought in legendary battles.</p>
<p>His legendary battles had given him extensive battlefield experience. So, with his help, she knew she would be creating something good.</p>
<p>Finally, after 1 million arrays and several million runes, she was done with version one. And while it was just the first version, there was just no way it could be underestimated.</p>
<p>"This is going to be my life’s work," Alina said, a tear dropping from her eyes.</p>
<p>She came up with this formation after Camilla nearly died during one of their battles in the Planet Killer dungeon a few years back. Since then, she had done everything she could to make sure the Battlefield Matrix became a thing.</p>
<p>Today, she had created it, which, from what she had envisioned, was already enough to tell her she had done something good, something amazing.</p>
<p>Kent smiled and wiped the tear off. "It sure is, and I am proud of you, Alina. You have done what you wanted, and now, we are going to make sure it becomes one of the most powerful trump cards we can ever deploy on battlefields."</p>
<p>She nodded.</p>
<p>The next plan would be to ensure it adapts to an opponent’s attack patterns and to identify the most effective way to kill without expending too much energy.</p>
<p>This way, even though the formation grew stronger the more it killed, it wouldn’t spend more than it was gaining.</p>
<p>Luckily for them, the next process could be carried out without actively engaging opponents in battle, as they had with the first version.</p>
<p>She summoned the Battlefield Matrix formation, allowing it to hover on her palm...</p>
<p>"I have included 200,000 different attack patterns and 350,000 defense patterns. Once we return to the tower, my sisters and the members of the legion will allow me to incorporate their attack patterns, which I believe will boost its power by leaps and bounds."</p>
<p>Alina smiled and put away the formation.</p>
<p>"Once I am also done with my missions, I will allow you to put my attack patterns from all incarnations inside. This way, even when you ladies are far from me in battle, it will be like I am there with you."</p>
<p>"Thank you, Kent," Alina said as she hugged him and buried her face in his chest.</p>
<p>For a moment, they remained like that, cherishing Alina’s success in creating the first form of the legendary Battlefield Matrix.</p>
<p>"I will stand guard while you work on implementing the efficiency matrix. If you need help with anything, just say the word."</p>
<p>Alina soon started working on the second version of the Battlefield Matrix while Kent stood guard.</p>
<p>He soon started diving into the information gathered by his Shadow Fiends.</p>
<p>Lady Wu and Veth had already returned to their businesses, making sure that now that Kent was back, they would do less so they could spend more time with him.</p>
<p>This meant finally entrusting most of the work to their subordinates. Kent, of course, sent his shadow fiends to keep them safe and also to see how they did their business; this way, he would know how best to help them.</p>
<p>Aside from them, he had also sent some of his shadow fiends to look for the rest of his ladies, whom he had met when he traveled to the Pill Worlds in the past.</p>
<p>Specifically, he sent them to look for Autumn, Brianna, Lady Kami and her daughter Azela.</p>
<p>Yuna had already informed him of Nefeli’s absence, so he didn’t bother to look for her since she said she had no idea where Nefeli had gone. But he sent some of his shadow fiends to keep watch for when she returned.</p>
<p>For now, his focus was on the four ladies who had done amazingly for themselves ever since they arrived in the Immortal World.</p>
<p>He discovered that Brianna had formed an all-female mercenary group called the Red Scarf Mercenary Group, and they had already dominated the Southern Region, making a name for themselves.</p>
<p>He was proud of her and promised to make sure she would one day stand out as one of the legendary mercenaries in the universe.</p>
<p>Then came Lady Kami and Azela, who had also made names for themselves in the Supreme Fate Sect, one of the best sects in the Immortal Realm. It had branches in the God Realm all the way to the Celestial Realm, so it had a very strong backing.</p>
<p>They both had made great progress in their cultivation as wielders of Fate. Kent never had his doubts when he met them, and now he had been proven right. They were brilliant.</p>
<p>But the one who surprised him the most was none other than Autumn, whom many people in the Immortal Realm and God Realm knew as the Scarlet Archeress.</p>
<p>Unlike the rest, who had reached the demigod stage, she was only at the Immortal Lord stage, but with nine immortal qi.</p>
<p>That was both shocking and borderline insane.</p>
<p>The only way one could achieve that was if one had the resources to refine the immortal qi; two, they had a special constitution that allowed them to easily refine immortal qi; or three, they killed and extracted them from their victims.</p>
<p>Kent, using the shadow fiend, saw Autumn from afar, but given his understanding of killing intent, he knew that Autumn, the most harmless of all the ladies he had met 300,000 years ago, had earned her immortal qi through life-and-death battles.</p>
<p>That left him in awe and, for some reason, very happy.</p>
<p>She would soon join the likes of Neomi, Eliza, Meiko, Ruby, Dia and all the bloodthirsty women in his harem, for Autumn was not a harmless cat; she was dangerous, very dangerous.</p>
<p>Alina was soon done.</p>
