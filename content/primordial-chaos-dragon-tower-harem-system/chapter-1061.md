<p>Lady Kami moaned, her body shaking like a massage chair as Kent offloaded inside her.</p>
<p>Beside them, Lady Azela continued to watch her mother have the best time of her life with Kent.</p>
<p>Kent had arrived at the Sect a day ago.</p>
<p>As soon as he arrived, he went for what he had come for.</p>
<p>Naturally, the two ladies had been waiting for him because they wanted to become his. Kent made them aware, on their way back after he killed the Planet King, that he would make them his women.</p>
<p>They knew the brat wanted them, and, frankly, they wanted him too. A lot had happened in their lives that, if it hadn’t been for Kent, they wouldn’t have survived this long.</p>
<p>So they went right for the main event.</p>
<p>And after a day, they had over ten rounds each.</p>
<p>Kent let them rest for a few hours before they resumed their lovemaking. Now, as he offloaded his dragon juice into Lady Kami’s body, he couldn’t help feeling very happy.</p>
<p>After he was done, he turned to Lady Azela, who smiled and shook her head.</p>
<p>"You said you have to concoct some pills before leaving. Now you have less than three days to go, so get on that too," Lady Azela said, and Kent smiled.</p>
<p>"I can do that after one more round."</p>
<p>"I can’t deny that."</p>
<p>In the end, he went one more round with Lady Azela. Afterward, they freshened up and went to check on the mission he had given the beasts.</p>
<p>The seven Monarchs and the forty-three other beasts would be the ones to see to it that his human wives and friends would be okay while he was away.</p>
<p>Kent planned to awaken their innate beast bodies before leaving. Once he did that, their combat power would be something out of this world.</p>
<p>He also wanted to give them enough pills to strengthen their souls and some to break more limits on their bodies.</p>
<p>"You all have done well," Kent said, staring at the humanoid beasts standing before him.</p>
<p>While the monarchs were working to make Planet Nictus their new temporary home, the others worked around the clock to secure the Pill ingredients he needed.</p>
<p>Kent then spent the rest of the day concocting Innate Talent awakening pills. He had to make every second count, so he worked hard for the pills...</p>
<p>After he was done with the last pill, he decided to head to Planet Pyris to handle his final preparations.</p>
<p>Kent sent the beasts into the Taming Bracelet and then, after an hour, he, alongside Lady Kami, Lady Azela, General Brianna, and Princess Autumn, used the Planet Teleporter to Planet Pyris, where Pill King Flura, Lady Wu, and Veth were already waiting for them.</p>
<p>When they arrived, he immediately called a meeting to address everyone and inform them of his plans for them...</p>
<p>In the meeting, he introduced everyone.</p>
<p>Another person he invited to this meeting was Suoh, the guy who helped him understand the pill words when he first arrived.</p>
<p>The last beasts he had tamed went to him.</p>
<p>After the introductions, he outlined his plans to everyone.</p>
<p>"So the plan is very simple. Since staying in high worlds is not something beneficial in the long run, I have devised a plan to help you all ascend to an immortal world safe and sound.</p>
<p>However, before you leave, you must ensure you’ve handled everything there is to handle in the high world. Of course, if you don’t want to leave a legacy, then don’t.</p>
<p>But one mission dear to me is to help Pill Monarch Nefeli clean up the pill worlds. Dirth has found its way into the ten Pill Alliances, and I want all evil to be weeded out.</p>
<p>Aside from that, I also want the evil sect to be completely wiped out and, if possible, ensure every last one of them doesn’t enter the cycle of reincarnation."</p>
<p>Kent smiled, seeing the expressions that appeared on their faces.</p>
<p>"Just kidding. However, eradicating them is a must."</p>
<p>"We will do that, Kent. No need to worry," Pill Monarch Nefeli said.</p>
<p>She may have been worried about her enemies in the past, but now, after learning that the Monarchs in the Flame Sea would be staying with her, she finally wanted to resume training.</p>
<p>Wiping out an entire sect of evil was a start.</p>
<p>"Great to hear that. However, the most important thing is the Ascension." Kent brought out tablets made from jade. None of the people gathered there had ever seen them before.</p>
<p>"These are Ascension Tablets. The way they work is simple. Just channel your energy into it, and a portal leading to an Immortal World will open, allowing everyone you want to enter."</p>
<p>The faces of everyone present grew solemn as they heard how the Ascension Tablet worked.</p>
<p>"Pardon my words, but what the heck?" Lady Kami said, her expression filled with shock. Everyone was the same as her.</p>
<p>They had seen how Ascension ceremonies worked. Usually, people pay a fortune just to open an Ascension Gate and Ascend to the immortal world linked to that gate.</p>
<p>Some also invoke the Heavenly Tribulation, which is considered the most dangerous yet cheapest way to ascend.</p>
<p>If they passed the tribulation, they would be sent to a suitable Immortal World. There are other ways; however, the most well-known is the Ascension Gate, or tribulation.</p>
<p>Never had any of them seen or heard of an Ascension Tablet. And what was even more shocking was that with a mere push of one’s spiritual qi, they could open a portal to an Immortal World.</p>
<p>"Don’t be shocked, you all. The Ascension Tablet is one of a kind, something only I have, simply because I am that awesome." Kent smiled, making everyone stare at him like he was some kind of monster.</p>
<p>"The best part is, it can also send you back to the High World you activated it in. So if you want to come back here one day, it can bring you."</p>
<p>Perhaps because they were too overwhelmed, they said nothing.</p>
<p>"Well, as to where these two lead, this one sends you to a star system called Salem, while this sends you to another star system called Beasts Kingdom."</p>
<p>"WHAT?"</p>
<p>This time, it was the beasts—specifically, the Monarchs—who shouted.</p>
<p>"Am I missing something?" Kent asked. He was at a loss here simply because he never expected them to become that shocked.</p>
<p>"Are you sure the Ascension Tablet leads to a star system called Beasts Kingdom?" Ignarok asked.</p>
<p>"Yes... that is what the description said."</p>
<p>"So if we use it, all of us will be appearing in an Immortal World located in the Beasts Kingdom."</p>
<p>"That is how it works."</p>
<p>"Damn. Today is the best day of my life," Ignarok said, and the other Monarchs nodded.</p>
<p>"The Beasts Kingdom is a place where every beast in the universe dreams of living. It has ninety-nine worlds, each of which is several times bigger than the Flame Sea.</p>
<p>According to history, this star system was once home to the Divine Beast Tamer. Legend has it that during his era, every beast went there to seek guidance on how to evolve beyond their bloodline limits.</p>
<p>Many say just living there will raise the potential of any beast. Personally, it is not a rumor. After all, I have met beasts from there, and while I can beat my chest and say I am a dragon, I pale in comparison to them.</p>
<p>After all, they are all descendants of beasts that once followed the Divine Beast Tamer."</p>
