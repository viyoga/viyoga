<p>If there is one thing more dangerous than a terrorist, it is a person who gives threats while smiling. That is the next level of psychological manipulation, the kind that Kent used to send his message.</p>
<p>The smile, the words, and the way they were delivered were just too chilling for most not to hear him. Initially, Kent wanted to patronise everyone, but when he saw Elder Mikko and scanned her, he knew he was still lacking.</p>
<p>He saw that when it came to speed, he was lacking, and so he changed his mind. He needed to focus on improving himself.</p>
<p>The sect is a place filled with the best and the brightest, so in one way or another, he is going to find someone with something unique he can’t measure up to, and if he wants to reach perfection and surpass his past selves, then he must ensure he was the best version of them all.</p>
<p>Vexthra, being a goddess and knowing what it takes to reach that stage, advised him to improve the smallest aspect of himself.</p>
<p>The small mistakes he might choose to ignore, the unique way something he is doing can be done, and the idea that he was better than everyone else... all these must be fixed. After all, it is the only way he can attain perfection.</p>
<p>He must embrace the idea that in the journey to the top, he isn’t the only one walking it, and relying solely on himself isn’t the way to walk it.</p>
<p>To reach perfection, he must take the small chances he can get and build upon them. That and only that is the way to the top.</p>
<p>Of course, not all qualify to make him tick.</p>
<p>So while he had found himself in the right place, he wasn’t about to let just anyone waste his time. He has better things to do, so the warning was warranted, and as expected, everyone became quiet.</p>
<p>"What an arrogant guy," one of the inner disciples who came with Elder Mikko said.</p>
<p>"I kind of like his style. Just look at him, there was nothing like fear in those eyes. If anything, he meant everything he said, and that kinda makes him look cool."</p>
<p>"But challenging the whole outer and Inner sects, does he look down on us like that?"</p>
<p>"If you don’t like it, you can challenge him. Of course, it doesn’t have to be a deathmatch."</p>
<p>Elder Mikko hovered in the air, looking at the disciples bicker among each other for a few minutes. She was waiting to see if anyone would say something against what Kent had said, but even after five minutes, nobody had the spine to do so.</p>
<p>She turned and looked at Kent, ’I must recruit him into the Law Enforcement Hall. Someone with this much effect will bring order to the sect easily.’</p>
<p>She was about to proceed with her duties when, suddenly, a voice spoke, accompanied by the sound of glass shattering.</p>
<p>"One would say those words are very arrogant, don’t you think, disciple Kent?" Reality shattered like glass, and she walked out, looking calmly at Kent.</p>
<p>⟦She is a red flag master. Whatever you do, don’t make her angry; after all, you will shatter like glass before you know what happened⟧</p>
<p>’No need to exaggerate it, Tower. I know danger when I see one.’</p>
<p>⟦I wasn’t exaggerating, master. This lady is dangerous, and while I would love to be on your side and tell you there is a chance for you, there is none.</p>
<p>Of course, you can kill her with the asteroid, but would that be something you would want to waste your precious asteroid on⟧</p>
<p>’I get it, Tower. I will treat her carefully and not scare her.’</p>
<p>Kent smiled in his head as he looked at the beauty that had appeared in the air.</p>
<p>"I don’t see it as arrogance. If anything, it is merely a suggestion, one that shouldn’t be too hard to follow."</p>
<p>"Oh, that is thoughtful of you, disciple Kent." The lady smiled, gently landed on the ground, and walked closer to where Kent stood.</p>
<p>"Why don’t I also make a suggestion. I am willing to give 1,000,000 supreme points to any disciple who can last more than five seconds against Disciple Kent ."</p>
<p>"WHAT"</p>
<p>Every eye widened, and jaws swung open. Nobody, regardless of who they were, was able to hold their composure hearing what Lady Enzi said.</p>
<p>Of course, Kent didn’t know what Supreme Points were, but judging by the looks on everyone’s faces, he knew it was something they all would kill for.</p>
<p>Lady Enzi smiled, brightening the sky and shaking hearts.</p>
<p>"Of course, while I would love to allow everyone to challenge him, unfortunately, I don’t have that much time, so we will be using the Great Titan body to select the first 100 disciples with the highest points to challenge him.</p>
<p>I will personally oversee the selection since, at the moment, I am the acting Supreme Elder."</p>
<p>Once again, many were taken aback, but Lady Enzi wasn’t done with her words.</p>
<p>"To the new disciples just joining, know that the supreme point is the way you can survive in this place. You need to buy cultivation resources and even food.</p>
<p>So, being offered a million points isn’t something you should ignore. That said, not more than 20 of you can handle the selection trial, so you are free not to participate.</p>
<p>But those who will qualify will automatically become Inner disciples." Then the third shock hit them, and they all felt their spirits lifted high already to handle everything thrown at them.</p>
<p>They all want to be part of the 100 disciples to challenge Kent, not to win, but to become Inner disciples.</p>
<p>"Of course, while you all will be getting something, the one being challenged must also earn something." Lady Enzi smiled slyly at Kent.</p>
<p>"Disciple Kent, if you manage to defeat the first 20 disciples, you will qualify to become an Inner disciple. If you defeat 50, you will be eligible to use the Spiritual Core Pool for seven days.</p>
<p>If you defeat 70, you will not only become an Inner disciple and enter the Spiritual Core Pool, but you will also have two opportunities to ask me for help with your cultivation.</p>
<p>And finally, if you manage to defeat all 100, first, like I said, you will become an Inner disciple, have 7 days access to the Spiritual Core Pool, get two chances to ask for my help, and now, you will automatically qualify to participate in the upcoming selection trial for the 20 disciples that will represent the sect in the Interplanetary Sect competition.</p>
<p>Aside from that, I will personally give you 500,000 Supreme Points and a chance to choose which Cultivation peak you want to join when you become an Inner disciple."</p>
<p>⟦What a sneaky woman. She managed to use your own words against you.⟧</p>
<p>’She is indeed sneaky. But she might have just given me the chance to accomplish my dreams faster than I expected.’</p>
<p>⟦That is indeed true. According to the sect’s rules, Inner disciples who have achieved some success can seek private lessons from the elders.</p>
<p>Becoming an Inner disciple will bring you closer to both improving your cultivation and gaining the opportunity to challenge the Pill Princess.</p>
<p>This is a golden opportunity, presented subtly.⟧</p>
<p>Kent smiled.</p>
<p>However, the next second, the space cracked in two different places and two people with terrifying auras appeared.</p>
