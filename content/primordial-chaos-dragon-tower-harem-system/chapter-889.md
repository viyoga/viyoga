<p>Within 24 hours, the news of Kent’s sudden appearance in Manelaus had spread far and wide, reaching thousands of worlds.</p>
<p>Millions stormed planet Manelaus, all with the same goal: to find Kent Madson.</p>
<p>Of course, their true intentions remained in their heads.</p>
<p>However, when they arrived, Kent was already gone, as the reports had stated. Many were furious that they couldn’t meet Kent Madson. However, those who took the time to think discovered that Kent had killed the unkillable, bringing back his head.</p>
<p>They began connecting the dots, and that was when they found out the killing had happened in the Valley of Torture.</p>
<p>Those with the wit decided to investigate, and what they found shocked everyone.</p>
<p>In the span of three days, Kent became a person whose nature rivalled the gods, and many simply couldn’t understand how any of it was possible.</p>
<p>Before killing the wandering demon swordsman, Kent left him many messages. Some were found by the dead demon, and some he missed.</p>
<p>However, thanks to these people’s hunger to find Kent, they managed to uncover most of these messages.</p>
<p>The messages spread far and wide.</p>
<p>That was when everyone came to learn of Kent’s true strength and the kind of monster he is. It all made sense somehow, yet the shock even left immortals scratching their heads.</p>

<p>[Planet Mekyn - Elven Garden - Lady Enyo, High Priestess, and Adina’s POV]</p>
<p>The three heaven-defying beauties sat under a white blossom tree, reading a report that had just reached them.</p>
<p>"Unbelievable. It’s hard to believe he actually managed to kill a level 2 heavenly Ascension stage expert. Just what is this Kent Madson made of?" Lady Enyo said, rereading the report.</p>
<p>Adina smiled, looking at the shocked expressions on the faces of the two ladies. "I already told you. Kent is no simple person. Many expected him to be an immortal or even a god.</p>
<p>But the True Kent Madson was but a mere Core Formation brat, and now, he had justified why his name appeared on the Sword Dao Tablet."</p>
<p>The High Priestess, who still hadn’t recovered from the shock Kent had put her in by giving her a god-grade cloning technique, looked at the smile on Adina’s face, and all she could think of was meeting Kent.</p>
<p>She understood that fighting one realm above your own is normal. Some even go as far as fighting two or even three realms above their own.</p>
<p>However, to go as far as fighting someone seven realms above your own was just defying the norms at this point.</p>
<p>Lady Enyo laughed, unsure of how to handle the issue. "Those monsters in the Supreme High Worlds would be furious by now.</p>
<p>From what I know, the strongest among them can at least fight five realms above their own, but even killing someone five realms above them is unheard of.</p>
<p>"I must meet this Kent Madson and, if possible, become his disciple." Lady Enyo stood up with a smile on her face. She looked at the High Priestess. "Runa, make sure Adina takes her cultivation seriously. I will be in seclusion for at least three months to attempt breaking the next limit."</p>
<p>The High Priestess nodded, and soon, Lady Enyo was gone from the garden.</p>
<p>Adina and the High Priestess stayed for a few minutes before they left to handle their own business.</p>

<p>[Unknown Place - Pill Immortal Nefeli’s POV]</p>
<p>It had already been months since Pill Monarch Nefeli started having these strange memories of Kent popping up in her mind.</p>
<p>At first, she thought she was going mad; however, it all started making sense when she found out Pill Immortal Suoh was also having similar memories.</p>
<p>So when she heard Kent’s name had appeared on the Sword Dao Tablet, making him the 14th to appear on such a heavenly tablet, she set out to track someone.</p>
<p>The journey was one she knew would be dangerous; however, someone with her strength wasn’t going to cower just because of a perilous journey.</p>
<p>She was standing in an unknown space, staring at a message she had received from Yuna, her maid.</p>
<p>"How is this possible? From my memories, when we met, he was also a Core Formation expert, and that was over 300 thousand years ago, so how is he still a Core Formation expert?"</p>
<p>Pill Immortal Nefeli wondered, looking at the message. For a moment, she thought she was going mad, or perhaps dreaming. However, she recovered the next second and decided to discard the thought from her mind for now.</p>
<p>The fact that she has these memories, even if they don’t tally with her current thought processes, holds a much deeper meaning, so one day she will find that meaning.</p>
<p>"It will all make sense one of these days."</p>
<p>With that, she continued her journey, unsure when she would reach her destination.</p>

<p>[Supreme High World - Planet Mibos - Nine Royals Sword Sect]</p>
<p>Planet Mibos is one of the Ten Sword Worlds in the High World. It is said to house some of the greatest swordsmen one can find in a High World.</p>
<p>They produced many legendary swordsmen who went on to dominate the universe. Legend even has it that they had some connection to Lord Genesis, the father of all swordsmen.</p>
<p>However, whether that is true, nobody truly knows.</p>
<p>At the moment, in the top sword sect in the world, many disciples were staring at their communication tablets with strange expressions.</p>
<p>They had just learned of the one person who had shaken their hearts and minds a few weeks ago.</p>
<p>"Somehow, this unknown Kent Madson has found his way back into our lives, and this time he has even set the bar this high," a disciple said, sitting down on the ground, unsure of how to feel.</p>
<p>"The bar is indeed too high. Although he is in a Great High World, the fact that he had killed someone seven realms above him, and even this person was a Sword King, while he was a mere Sword Saint, is more than enough to know he is several times more talented than they paint him to be."</p>
<p>"Do you think the Nine Sword Royals can contend with him?"</p>
<p>The person wasn’t even done speaking when suddenly, the space cracked in nine different places, and nine Sword Emperors stepped out.</p>
<p>The moment everyone saw them, they bowed. "We greet the Nine Sword Royals."</p>
<p>Every ten thousand years, the sect selects nine heaven-defying talents in the sword path and nurtures them to become the next nine to storm the immortal worlds and become the next generation of swordsmen that would shake the universe.</p>
<p>The nine that appeared—six men and three ladies—are the current Nine Sword Royals, and they had come out of a thousand-year seclusion.</p>
<p>Naturally, everyone knew the reason why they came, but none dared say it in fear of losing their heads.</p>
<p>However, Kent’s sudden feat had brought them out, and while they appeared calm on the outside, inwardly, they knew they wouldn’t be able to cultivate effectively until they got their hands on Kent and defeated him.</p>
<p>One of them, a level 8 Heavenly Ascension stage cultivator who is a Sword Emperor with a level 6 Heavenly Sword Aura, stepped forward, cracking the space under his foot as he walked through the air.</p>
<p>"Spread the word. The Nine Sword Royals will be participating in the upcoming Mount Genesis Supreme Sword competition. Those who dare can sign up."</p>
<p>With that, they vanished, and as they intended, words spread everywhere, indirectly sending a challenge to Kent that in 15 years’ time, if he dares, he should sign up for the upcoming Mount Genesis Supreme Sword competition, the greatest sword competition held every 10,000 years.</p>
