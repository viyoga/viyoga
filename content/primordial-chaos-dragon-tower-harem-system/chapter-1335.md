<p>This is one of those moments when nothing makes sense. No matter how hard he tried to make sense of it, he always arrived at the point where it all became a mess.</p>
<p>Kent is already aware that during the primordial era, when the Great War entered a dangerous phase after the universe’s guardians abandoned it and ascended, the chaos dragon used his soul to call into the future, his voice travelling through space and time to summon 13 legions.</p>
<p>From those thirteen, he had already met a member from the Lost Knights Legion, some members from the Undead Legion, Eternal Wrath Legion, Soul Sentinel Legion, and the Night Riders Legion.</p>
<p>He had already met 5, leaving him with 8 more to go.</p>
<p>Now it seems he had met the 6th. However, that is where the confusion starts.</p>
<p>If, when the chaos dragon called into the future, thirteen legions appeared, then doesn’t that mean the Void Legion, which was formed by Chaos, was already there with him?</p>
<p>Shouldn’t that mean only 12 legions should have appeared?</p>
<p>Things don’t add up.</p>
<p>There is just no way he is mistaken or, worse, fallen for the scheme of the three void beasts before him.</p>
<p>’They did not seem like the lying type. However, there is also no connection between us to show that they are indeed my legion. I have a connection to the other legions, so why not them?</p>
<p>Of course, inside the void temple, all connections to the tower have been severed. However, at least since they are in the same place, he should have at least some form of connection to them.</p>
<p>But since that is not an option at the moment, he can only resort to using questions.</p>
<p>"You claim you are a part of the Void Legion, right?"</p>
<p>"Yes."</p>
<p>"Then how come you three aren’t even demigods? If there is one thing I know about Chaos, he hates weaklings. So while you seven are at the moment more powerful than I, I know he wouldn’t have chosen you three as his subordinates.</p>
<p>I know it is harsh to hear, but Chaos would never choose you three because you are weak. I know I wouldn’t, so why don’t you tell me who you are really?"</p>
<p>"That is too harsh, Master Chaos," Axiom said with a little chuckle.</p>
<p>"Yes, Master. It seems you have become harsher than you were during the primordial era," Kailan added, also smiling with an amused expression on his face.</p>
<p>"I don’t know about that. I mean, we both know he is a vengeful person, and all vengeful people are bitter and harsher than you can imagine. So I think he is the same as before, just a bit grumpy."</p>
<p>"Kol is right. I believe he is grumpy too."</p>
<p>Kent stood there, his expression turning from one emotion to another, unsure of how to handle these three. While he was seriously looking for an explanation, they seemed to be having fun.</p>
<p>There is just no way he expected that. But even if he did, he most certainly wouldn’t have expected them to be making fun of him.</p>
<p>He was harsh, that much is true. However, he is in no way grumpy.</p>
<p>"I am not grumpy."</p>
<p>"There is no need to deny anything, Master. From where we stand, we perfectly understand you. I mean, we basically betrayed our race by joining your legion. We killed people who were our friends to protect your interests. So if anyone understood your grumpiness, it would be us."</p>
<p>Axiom and his pals laughed, leaving Kent to stand there, unsure of what to even say at this point. He took a deep sigh, and then he focused his attention back on them.</p>
<p>"If you are indeed part of the Void Legion, then prove it."</p>
<p>"With pleasure," Axiom waved his hand, and a white pebble appeared in his palm. "Everything you need to at least confirm our identity is here."</p>
<p>He threw it at Kent, who caught it and gave it a gentle look before placing it in his mouth. In a way, he knew just what to do with the pebble.</p>
<p>So he swallowed it.</p>
<p>The moment he did, he blacked out. However, in less than two seconds, he recovered. At once, his expression turned calm, and a sense of clarity shone in his gaze.</p>
<p>It appeared he had received some important memories.</p>
<p>He said nothing to Axiom and his pals; instead, he sat down and decided to make sense of what he had just seen.</p>
<p>The pebble contained memories of Chaos when he became a demigod. Kent had yet to reach the level where Chaos started clashing with the Void Beasts in the memories he had recovered before today.</p>
<p>However, in this memory, he was a demigod.</p>
<p>In a way, Kent had no idea how, how Chaos found himself in the Void Universe, the same place the Void Beasts came from.</p>
<p>A few hours after he arrived in the void universe, he accidentally ventured into a place similar to a prison.</p>
<p>There, he met 69 imprisoned void beasts, each a terror beyond measure.</p>
<p>At first, Chaos didn’t understand their language; however, after several days there, he began picking up a few words. This made him decide to learn their language so he could communicate with them.</p>
<p>After about 6 months there, he managed to finally have a meaningful conversation with them. It was then he found out they were criminals, or that was how they were branded.</p>
<p>All 69 of them are powerful warriors. It was through their conversation that he discovered something very peculiar.</p>
<p>"Tell me about the Nine, Twenty, and Forty rule." Kent suddenly asked, making Axiom and the three void beasts smile.</p>
<p>"Finally, we are making some progress," Kol said before Axiom started to answer the question.</p>
<p>"Nine leaders, twenty vices, and forty workers."</p>
<p>"What are you three then? Which category were you back then?"</p>
<p>"I was a leader," Axiom said.</p>
<p>"I was a vice," Kailan also answered.</p>
<p>"And I was a worker," finally, Kol answered, nodding.</p>
<p>Inside the prison, he met nine supreme void beasts, the leaders of the prison. Under them were the twenty vices, who were Universal Gods. Then came the workers, who were all within the ranks of a god.</p>
<p>Later, they swore an allegiance to him with a good promise. They would fight for him if he freed them. Chaos freed them since he needed help completing the mission he came to the void universe for.</p>
<p>Now, even after finally verifying their identities, something else is bothering him. This, of course, is the notion of the 13 legions coming from the future to aid him during the Great War.</p>
<p>’This is the last life for me. All 13 lives had passed, meaning all 13 legions had been formed already. So Chaos is the first, then it means there should have been 12 legions instead of 13 that came from the future.</p>
<p>This also means if the Void Legion was already with Chaos, considering he left the Void Universe with all 69 of them, then where did the 13th legion come from? Even better question, who is the 13th legion that heeded the Fated Call and came through the Time and Space portal?</p>
<p>One last question, if the Void Legion were indeed with Chaos when he made the call, then does that mean there were 14 legions instead of 13?</p>
<p>"What the hell is going on..."</p>
<p>Kent has yet again lost his mind over something he had no idea how to make heads or tails of.</p>
<p>Axiom and his two pals wore smiles.</p>
<p>Meanwhile, at the core of the universe, a different conversation was underway...</p>
