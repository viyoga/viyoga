<p>"Paragon," Kent muttered, looking at the cube floating where the man with the white hair had stood just a few seconds ago. He expected a name, but he only got something akin to a title, or perhaps a symbol.</p>
<p>"Makes me wonder why lately I have been getting weird names," Kent smirked. "First, it was Chaos, then there was Faceless, and now, a Paragon who, from the look of this, is very terrifying."</p>
<p>"My life is funny," Kent laughed, and he touched the cube. He examined it for a few minutes...</p>
<p>"...though he is a nice dude. A little easy on the eyes, but he is a nice guy." He smirked yet again and then decided to ponder what Paragon said.</p>
<p>"First, I will be cultivating something akin to a universe inside my soul. Only that everything inside this universe is a weapon. The planets, the moons, stars, asteroids, galaxies, and so forth.</p>
<p>It is my universe, so I can do as I please. I say, not a bad move... Though I doubt it will be easy." Kent smiled, a happy expression on his face as he examined the cube curiously.</p>
<p>"He also said the humans or beasts I created will all be weapons in one way or another. Not that I planned on doing that, unless, of course, these humans or beasts can make a difference in combat."</p>
<p>The soul path he is about to embark on relies heavily on his soul essence. With enough soul essence, he can create anything.</p>
<p>Of course, he doesn’t know how much will go into creating these celestial bodies, but it will be a universe.</p>
<p>He will determine how many galaxies there will be, how many starfields he has to create, and even how many planets to include within a single starfield.</p>
<p>On those planets, he has to create everything like the moons, mountains, and every other thing. And since everything he makes can be used as a weapon, the possibilities are infinite.</p>
<p>"I don’t know where this path came from, but it is something that will indeed make Celestials go to war over. I’d better utilize it well, and if possible, create me a few planets that I can throw at people," Kent laughed, remembering what Paragon said.</p>
<p>Paragon gave him an idea, and as someone who believes that Chaos is the only way, he would most certainly make sure he throws planets at people.</p>
<p>"The guy is not only dangerous, he is also crazy. I’m looking forward to meeting him again. Perhaps next time I will learn a thing or two about him."</p>
<p>With that, Kent brought it close to his chest. "Moment of truth." He pressed it against his chest, and it dissolved inside, or more like melded into his chest.</p>
<p>However, the moment that happened, Kent blacked out and fell into yet another deep sleep. This time, too, when he woke up, he was in a dark space.</p>
<p>But he didn’t remain in the dark for long. The cube appeared and burst into radiant light, filling the space. The light spread, and soon, it started creating something akin to the cosmos.</p>
<p>No planets or galaxies were created. Instead, a large expanse of space, beautiful as it could be, was brought into being. First, all the colors were created.</p>
<p>Then this color diffused into a mesmerizing display of cosmic space, something that holds some form of meaning.</p>
<p>Kent watched all this slowly come to life with a calm expression. He didn’t know why he was given this by the mysterious entity named Paragon, but he was sure that with what was happening in front of him, he would, in one way or another, become someone worthy of his name.</p>
<p>An entire universe to himself, and judging by what he knew now, everything within it is a weapon he can use. That is more than excellent.</p>
<p>Suddenly, ten massive tomes appeared out of nowhere and hovered before Kent.</p>
<p>He wondered where they came from, but before he could even scan them, they burst into sparks of light and entered Kent’s forehead, causing him to pass out again.</p>
<p>This time, he was out for a while, and during his absence, the cosmos slowly took shape, expanding several light-years away. It was very beautiful, and at the center was Kent’s floating body.</p>
<p>After what appeared like forever, Kent opened his eyes, feeling a slight headache. However, after a few minutes, the headache went away, and a smile appeared on Kent’s face.</p>
<p>"Indeed, everything is a weapon inside this universe. And all I need is just Soul Essence."</p>
<p>The tomes that entered his forehead are the ways he can go about using the Soul Universe. Surprisingly, only the first three tomes gave him something; the seven left were locked inside his mind.</p>
<p>"Celestial Forge, Orbital Dominion, Stellar Cataclysm." Kent was puzzled by the names of the three tomes he had access to.</p>
<p>No, the names seemed fine. It was what they meant that left his jaw hanging loose. In fact, he never expected things to be this dangerous.</p>
<p>The Celestial Forge showed him how to create a vast array of entities, including planets, moons, suns, stars, beasts, asteroids, humans, and anything else it desires in its universe.</p>
<p>Of course, it is easier said than done, but that is a piece of cake to Kent.</p>
<p>But that isn’t all. It turns out that aside from creating these celestial bodies, he can also surround them with something called Cosmic Aura, which, according to the tome, enhances the effect of the celestial body.</p>
<p>So, if he manages to surround the moon with a Cosmic Aura, throwing it at his opponent wouldn’t just be a mere throw, but instead, it would contain the user’s will, and in this case, Kent’s will.</p>
<p>And if his will is strong enough, he can affect the laws of the known universe and, to some extent, take the laws of the known universe and make them his own for a limited amount of time.</p>
<p>In short, if his will is strong enough, aside from the force behind his thoughts, he can influence the gravity on the falling body, making it move several times faster.</p>
<p>Aside from that, he can increase the density, making it very heavy. The possibilities are endless...</p>
<p>"This is crazy. I mean, just where did this path come from?" Kent looked at his hand, and he could see he was clenching his fist in excitement. That shows just how powerful he felt at the moment.</p>
