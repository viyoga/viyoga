<p>The Pink Lady was banished from her tribe. Her parents, siblings, friends, and entire tribe despised her simply for choosing to walk a path no succubus had ever dared to tread.</p>
<p>Because of this, she was left to fend for herself, thereby becoming lonely and forgotten by all she would have loved to talk with.</p>
<p>Instead of the love and support of a family, she was cast out, and for millions of years, she never had the love she needed.</p>
<p>However, in this new life that the universe had given her, she had amazing sisters who not only loved her but also adored and respected her choices.</p>
<p>She also had a man in her life who didn’t dislike her because of the limitations on her body as a woman. If anything, now that she could remember part of her past life, she was more grateful to Kent than anything.</p>
<p>He was caring and loving. He even went out of his way to make her happy simply because he thought she wasn’t happy enough.</p>
<p>The emotion that such a selfless act of love and kindness created led her to embrace Kent in a tight, warm hug. She is grateful. Her past self is eternally grateful.</p>
<p>"I love you very much, Kent. I will always love, cherish, and respect you," Adina said, causing Kent to feel a warmth in his chest.</p>
<p>Although he had heard these words many times, each time they evoked a different pleasant emotion within him.</p>
<p>The sincerity in Adina’s words and the feeling he felt from her body made him feel loved and respected.</p>
<p>So without even realising when or how it started, they were locked in a deep kiss, one filled with all the passion they couldn’t yet show to each other.</p>
<p>Kent held her in his tight embrace.</p>
<p>After breaking the kiss, they stared into each other’s eyes for a couple more seconds before entering a second round of kissing. They want to be in each other’s arms for as long as they can.</p>
<p>Naturally, they spent 20 minutes just kissing and admiring one another.</p>
<p>"I love you, Kent," Adina said, planting her face on his chest.</p>
<p>"I love you too, Adina"</p>
<p>Adina smiled and made a grabbing motion. A key-shaped rune appeared between her thumb and index finger.</p>
<p>"This is a Tier 22 Illusion Seal. With this, illusions cast by an illusionist, provided they are not True Immortals, would not affect you." Adina pressed it against Kent’s forehead.</p>
<p>"This is my first gift for you. As I grow stronger, so would the effect of this rune" She moved forward and planted a kiss on his forehead. "Once I become an immortal, I will give you my second and the best gift you will ever receive from any of your wives."</p>
<p>"Bold words," Kent said with a chuckle...</p>
<p>Adina also smiled, but she said nothing. She knew that once Kent had received it, he himself would attest that it was the best gift he had ever received.</p>
<p>Of course, he got the Tower from Vexthra... so well, he would decide when he received it.</p>
<p>Kent held on to her waist, staring at her as if he had just fallen in love with her all over again.</p>
<p>"I wish we could stay like this forever," Kent said, planting a kiss on Adina’s lips. She blushed and placed her hands on Kent’s shoulder.</p>
<p>"I know. However, Elina is waiting for you to come find her, so you must leave." She smiled, looking amused... "My sisters and I have already decided not to disturb you much on your journey, so don’t get distracted."</p>
<p>Kent nodded and kissed her again.</p>
<p>Afterwards, the mist cleared and Kent’s wives rushed toward them. As expected, Adina became their target. She had, after all, become eye candy among her sisters.</p>
<p>Her pink nature had set her apart from them. However, that was also what made her unique in her own way.</p>
<p>Being a virgin succubus was unheard of. However, she had walked that path before and succeeded in awakening her Pure Body.</p>
<p>Now, she was walking that path again, but instead of being disowned and banished, she now had amazing sisters and a man whose love for her was untainted.</p>
<p>Now, aside from being a succubus who was proficient in illusions, she could also play the zither and sing. In fact, one of her illusion powers came from her zither music. She could easily hypnotise anyone with her music.</p>
<p>Kent had already given her a haunted song, one that she couldn’t yet master. However, that was before she had received her True Name. Now, she could easily harness that music to its fullest potential.</p>
<p>The only thing left now was for them to start conquering the remaining floors of the Planet Killer Dungeon. All of them would live to face odds far superior to those they could handle.</p>
<p>After they were done questioning and learning more about her, the ladies decided to have some fun.</p>

<p>[Planet Gemini - Kaelith’s POV]</p>
<p>Meanwhile, somewhere in the immortal world, inside the Guillotine Prison, where every passing second is torture, Kaelith sat in a lotus posture, his body drained of all energy.</p>
<p>However, despite his ragged and worn-out self, there was a fire of hope buried deep within his eyes. He had not given up hope just yet.</p>
<p>All around him, sharp, pointed blades were aimed at her. Every few seconds, one moves and stabs him at a random part of his body.</p>
<p>Each sword is laced with a chemical that enhances the pain by several percentages. So he wasnt just being stabbed, he was being forced to feel the pain.</p>
<p>However, for someone who had been in prison for thousands of years already, Kaelith had grown numb to the pain, so while he was being stabbed several times every day, he didn’t seem to care too much about that.</p>
<p>He was already used to the pain.</p>
<p>Suddenly, the door to his prison cell opened, and three men walked in. Looking at the feathered brow, one could tell they were of the same race as Kaelith.</p>
<p>They were all from the Inferno Roc Bloodline. However, one had blue hair and a coldness to him, showing that while he was of the same race as Kaelith, his flame was the cold kind.</p>
<p>When they entered, Kaelith lifted his head and looked at them. Then, as if he weren’t bothered by the fact that three people he once called brothers now took pleasure in torturing him, he returned to his unbothered self.</p>
<p>"Look who has finally learned his lesson," one of them said, using his leg to lift Kaelith’s chin, forcing him to stare at them in the air.</p>
<p>"Are you ready to talk and tell us how you managed to awaken your Innate Body and hand over the [Sky Roc Art]?"</p>
<p>Kaelith said nothing, nor did he bother to even look at the Roc man who spoke. This, of course, infuriated him. However, he did nothing and simply pushed Kaelith back, letting his back touch a pointed sword.</p>
<p>The pain shot through his body, but he made no sound. Seeing this, the three Roc men became helpless.</p>
<p>In the past, Kaelith would have reacted to the pain. Those sounds used to give pleasure. However, Kaelith seems unbothered now.</p>
<p>The blue-haired Roc booted him in the face and smirked... "You should be lucky Father wanted you alive until he returns from his seclusion now that he has become a True God.</p>
<p>If it were up to me, you would have long joined that useless mother of yours." He spat on him and walked toward the door.</p>
<p>When he reached the entrance, he turned to the other two... "I am going hunting for that Kent Madson. I learned whoever catches him will be rewarded by the Ice Council. I want the drop of the Cold Essence liquid, so see you later."</p>
<p>He left, and soon, the other two chose to also leave after they, of course, tortured Kaelith.</p>
<p>When they left, Kaelith lifted his head, and for a moment, he let a smile appear on his face.</p>
<p>’It won’t be long now,’ he said inwardly before bending his neck to reel in the pain.</p>
