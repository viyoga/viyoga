<p>[Planet Qilin - Everlasting Pill Mountain - Pill Immortal Flura’s POV]</p>
<p>Pill Immortal Flura had done many amazing things in the past few years, and even in the past few months alone.</p>
<p>She went from concocting a Tier 21 intelligent Pill with two violet pill rings to refining the same pill but with three violet pill rings and a single azure pill ring.</p>
<p>This achievement had pushed her closer to the stage where she could start to concoct Tier 22 pills.</p>
<p>She had been having a good streak the past few years, thanks to the knowledge she had acquired years ago when the Pill Ascension Realm opened.</p>
<p>This was why whenever she achieved something great, the thought of how much Kent had helped her always came to mind.</p>
<p>He had helped him greatly, and for that, she is eternally grateful.</p>
<p>However, she wasn’t prepared for the news that came when she was just about to attempt to concoct the pill of her dreams.</p>
<p>This morning, she woke up with big dreams of finally being ready to concoct the Life Ascendence Pill.</p>
<p>It is a pill said to have the power to extend the life of anyone, even a mortal. It was said to even have the power to bring someone back from the brink of death.</p>
<p>However, before she even began to prepare for the concoction, the news of Kent’s name appearing on the Pill Dao Tablet arrived, and she just couldn’t keep calm.</p>
<p>She experienced different types and stages of shock.</p>
<p>The first shock was how much she had missed Kent. It has been over 300,000 years since they parted ways. While it may look like she is an immortal and can live for millions of years, 300,000 years is such a long time for them to be apart, especially when she has feelings for him.</p>
<p>So hearing Kent’s name again made her happy and shocked at the same time.</p>
<p>The second came in the form of Kent revealing his brilliance in the path of Alchemy, yet again.</p>
<p>When they met back at the Pill World, she saw firsthand what Kent was capable of. He defeated her quite easily, and that was just too shocking and eye-opening for her.</p>
<p>So the past 300,000 years had been her waiting for when Kent would make his debut in the world of Alchemy.</p>
<p>Of course, about a year ago, when he resurfaced, he was a swordsman. That revealed soured her mood and made her a bit disappointed.</p>
<p>She expected Kent to become famous through Alchemy.</p>
<p>Today, the pride she felt when she witnessed Kent’s firm belief as an Alchemist was ignited, and that just made her even happier.</p>
<p>But the main shock came from what Kent had achieved. Many years ago, when she was a Pill Monarch, she tried what Kent had just accomplished, but she failed just like the many who came before her.</p>
<p>She had the zeal, but she lacked the determination to achieve that. Now, Kent had achieved it, and that just left her world spinning for a moment.</p>
<p>Suddenly, the pill she was about to concoct became a secondary thing to her. Kent’s coming into her life again, even if only through his achievement, had left her wondering if she had done enough.</p>
<p>Ursa, the Phoenix that Kent had given her for protection, remained by her side all day, making sure she was alright.</p>
<p>After all, Flura had always admired Kent and had been working very hard for the past thousands of years, mainly to win against him when they met again.</p>
<p>However, if Kent had already gained the recognition of the Pill Dao, then what kind of monster would he become when he finally reached the rank of a Pill Immortal?</p>
<p>"Little sister, you are not actually letting this shake your resolve, are you?" Ursa asked, holding her hand.</p>
<p>Flura sat there for a few seconds, then she shook her head and smiled. "I am fine, Ursa. You have nothing to worry about. It was just that I never expected him to make such a comeback, not when he had been absent from my life for all these years."</p>
<p>"I know that, sister. However, I think we both know how much you admire him. You are a Pill Immortal, but every last second, you work hard simply to impress him when you meet again.</p>
<p>So there is bound to be some form of emotion hidden beneath. I am afraid that if you don’t address that, there is a chance it might affect you negatively."</p>
<p>"You worry too much, Ursa. I was worried he had given up the path of Alchemy when his name appeared on the Sword Dao Tablet.</p>
<p>However, now that he has made his debut, I have to be ready for him. There is no telling what kind of surprise he has for us next, so I have to be ready for him."</p>
<p>She stood up and started walking toward the Pill House. Although her name had not yet appeared on the Pill Dao Tablet, she was determined nonetheless.</p>
<p>Soon, she would look for ways to impress the Pill Dao.</p>
<p>For today, she wants to cement her stance as a Pill Immortal proficient in the art of making perfect Tier 21 pills.</p>
<p>The target is a Tier 21 Life Ascendence Pill with three Violet Pill Rings. If possible, she would add two Azure Pill Rings and one Grey Pill Ring.</p>
<p>If she can accomplish that, it would raise the potency of the Pill from the initial 100% by 53 times. That would, without a doubt, put her name on top of the list of Pill Immortals to have recorded such high potency.</p>
<p>Watching her leave, Ursa smiled and shook her head. "I have already done my best; the next will depend on how much you really want to defeat him."</p>
<p>She let out a deep sigh and muttered, "Of course, if possible, I should meet Lord Kent first and make sure he loses when you two meet next."</p>
<p>Pill Immortal Flura entered the Pill House with great determination and started working on the pill. Such a pill would take over two weeks to make.</p>
<p>It requires 24 major and 12 minor ingredients. She would have to take her time and work diligently to make sure she made no mistakes. After all, it took her 30 years to gather the ingredients. If she were to make a single mistake, there would be no do-overs.</p>
<p>However, the meticulousness she had to put into the concoction process would pay off. After two and a half weeks, she would walk out of the pill room with a perfect Tier 21 Life Ascendence Pill with 3 violet, 2 azure, and 2 grey pill rings.</p>
<p>This achievement would catch the attention of millions, for whenever a Life Ascendence Pill is made, the response is always fierce.</p>
<p>Everyone wants their hands on it, and so for the next couple of months, she would have a whole lot of drama to navigate.</p>
<p>This would trigger the Pill God to make his move even more fiercely now. However, with the secret helper that neither Flura nor Ursa is aware of, she would be protected.</p>
<p>Soon, the one she seeks to defeat will arrive, and whether she has what it takes to win against him will be determined by how much she has grown while they were apart.</p>
<p>---</p>
<p>Meanwhile, after spending three months in enlightenment, Kent finally opened his eyes, a smile playing on his lips.</p>
<p>He had become a Pill Monarch, and on top of that, he had received a special gift from the Pill Dao. All this, combined with the fact that he had finally received the first set of memories of his life as Pill Celestial Kevin Sage, added to his joy.</p>
