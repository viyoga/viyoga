<p>Long after the Angelic Order fell and the Angel race parted, a child was born to the Moonlit Angel race during the alignment of the Celestial Star and the Harmonious Moon.</p>
<p>They called her Seraphina Moonveil, for she was the one born when all hope was lost.</p>
<p>She was born during a rare celestial event—an event that happens once every million years. However, millions of years after the Angelic Order fell, peace was not born again in the remaining Angel race.</p>
<p>Her birth brought hope to her people, and for years, she was nurtured. Her name was used to bring peace to the remaining Angel race.</p>
<p>Her name was used to deter all enemies, and as she grew, her radiance started to manifest. Before she turned 20, she became a symbol that stood at the forefront of the Moonlit Angel race.</p>
<p>Under her authority, the broken and shattered Angel race was made whole again. Under her authority, the Moonlit Order was formed...</p>
<p>Just like that, for thousands of years, using her strength and authority, she brought peace to the Angel race, and all who dared to come against her authority were destroyed under her sword.</p>
<p>However, when the creatures of the Void appeared and civilization was in shambles, she rose to the call. Leading the Moonlit Order, she faced the attack head-on.</p>
<p>Thankfully, she wasn’t the only one fighting. Along with the Order, they managed to purge the chaos, but at a great cost. The battle took too many lives, so when it all ended, she knew something had to be done.</p>
<p>That was when she stepped toward the Void in search of the Lunar Blade.</p>
<p>It was said before the disappearance of the Celestial Angel race, a mysterious sword appeared that was meant to be wielded by the one meant to bring the angel race to peace and prosperity.</p>
<p>Legend has it that she never returned for millions of years, causing her name to fade from all minds and her legacy to slowly corrode.</p>
<p>However, when the Void opened again and the creatures from within came, legend has it that on that fateful day, Selphira Lunaris was born.</p>
<p>...not as the leader of the Moonlit Order, but as Selphira Lunaris, the wielder of the Lunar Eclipse Sword and the dancer whose movements alone are enough to mesmerize those she faces.</p>
<p>It was said that before the disappearance of the Celestial Angel race, the fracture that appeared in the nine Angel races ran deeper than one would have expected.</p>
<p>The fracture went as far as affecting the celestial constellation, one that made only one alignment possible. After that alignment, none would ever grace the Angel race again.</p>
<p>That alignment was what birth Selphira Lunaris.</p>
<p>However, Kent and his women stood and stared at the sky, watching the alignment of the Celestial Star and the Harmonious Moon, which had been dimmed for billions of years already.</p>
<p>Far in the cosmos, every Angel race was on their knees, singing the rebirth of the Lunar Child, the one said to usher the Angel race into a new age.</p>
<p>Those who believed in the Lunar Child were kneeling, prostrating themselves before her name.</p>
<p>Kent looked up at the sky, and a smile appeared on his face.</p>
<p>"When all hope was lost... when races were in shambles, she rose from the moon’s radiance, blessed by both the Celestial Mother and the Lunar Warden.</p>
<p>Her name spread far and wide, and wielding the moon’s power and the authority of the celestial star, she brought order to the Universe. Her name was once Seraphina Moonveil, but she became Selphira Lunaris.</p>
<p>...Once again, her name shall be Selphira Lunaris, the Celestial Sword Dancer."</p>
<p>"Rise and heed thy call, Selphira Lunaris, your destiny awaits."</p>
<p>When Kent invoked her true name, the sky turned even brighter, causing everyone to shield their eyes. The brilliance of the light was just too much to be seen with normal eyesight.</p>
<p>However, the light receded after a few minutes. That was when she appeared, dressed in white with golden embroidery lining the edges.</p>
<p>She had white hair with touches of silver here and there. She had the perfect warrior posture, and just like her sisters, her chest and backside didn’t betray her. In her hand was a longsword glowing with lunar energy.</p>
<p>Purple gems were embedded in the hilt of the sword. Her eyes were purple, shining with otherworldly brilliance.</p>
<p>On her back were three pairs of whitish-silver angelic wings.</p>
<p>"Wow, she is gorgeous," Vexthra said, watching their sister fly toward Kent and then jump into his arms, kissing his lips. The whole sight looked dreamy.</p>
<p>However, she could not be ignored since her nature had now changed from human to angel.</p>
<p>"How do you feel?" Kent asked after breaking the kiss. He always asked the same of all his ladies after their true name was awakened.</p>
<p>"I feel like the best version of myself," Mara giggled, looking at her sister. The two were stark contrasts when it came to race.</p>
<p>Nara was a Duskwraith Kin, meaning she was Half-Wraith, Half-Human, while Mara was an angel from the Moonlit Angel race.</p>
<p>One was of darkness, and the other was made of light, like yin and yang.</p>
<p>"I have also reached the peak of the Core Formation stage. The only thing left is to evolve my core seven times, so be ready to go on for days after all of this is over," Kent smiled, hearing her words.</p>
<p>Mara’s bloodline had already awakened to 25% so that she could wield her powers to some extent. Her form was also the same; she could hide her wings.</p>
<p>Aside from that, her beauty remained the same, striking and mesmerizing.</p>
<p>Zaila, who had brought out her Primordial spirit, stared at this wide-eyed. Her Primordial spirit seemed even more shocked by the changes that happened to Mara.</p>
<p>Her nature had basically changed from human to angel, and now, even if she were to face a Peak Core Formation expert who had evolved their core three to four times, she wouldn’t panic.</p>
<p>"Congratulations on your breakthrough," Kent said, smiling. "You should focus more on understanding your new powers. I have a feeling you will discover something interesting."</p>
<p>He turned to Camilla, who was smiling with Mara.</p>
<p>"Camilla dear, it’s your turn."</p>
<p>She walked forward, and just like with Mara, Kent put her blood and his blood on the token. And just like that, the second awakening of the day started.</p>
