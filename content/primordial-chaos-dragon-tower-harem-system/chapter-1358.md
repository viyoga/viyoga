<p>The mountain garden is the best place in the tower for Gaia and her friends. So seeing them have a tea party there, like they have nothing important to do, is just too normal for them.</p>
<p>It isn’t shocking since Vexthra and her sisters had already seen them do all kinds of shameless things, all in the name of relaxing.</p>
<p>They usually wait there to welcome Kent since he mostly enters the tower through there.</p>
<p>However, when Vexthra, Elaine, Unity, and Dia appeared this time, the usually cheerful Gaia and her squad became quiet, almost afraid.</p>
<p>Vexthra, being insightful, immediately connected a few dots, "Unity, take Dia to Gaiaville and have her bathe and change into a pair of new clothes. Elaine, continue her healing there. I have a few things to discuss with Gaia."</p>
<p>They nodded and left promptly. After they were gone, Gaia, Dori, and Akira took a deep breath, feeling their bodies finally relax.</p>
<p>"Are you three okay?" Vexthra asked, walking toward the three cosmic lolis.</p>
<p>"We are fine, Big Sister. It was just that we never expected you to bring that person here. Is Big Brother aware she is here?"</p>
<p>Vexthra suspected this would happen. Gaia and her sisters had been with Kent since the beginning of time, so they knew him well. This means they also knew all kinds of people Kent had encountered in the many lives he had lived.</p>
<p>So Dia’s past is something they knew, and considering how they reacted, Vexthra knew they were scared of her.</p>
<p>"I know Dia was once Kent’s enemy, but is there a need to be afraid of her?" Vexthra asked, her brow furrowed.</p>
<p>"Yeah, Big Sister is not aware because she didn’t know about her true identity," Akira said, looking in the direction Unity and Elaine took Dia.</p>
<p>"Why don’t you enlighten me, ladies?"</p>
<p>Dori looked at her sisters for a few seconds before she spoke, "I can’t say much, but all Big Sister needs to know is, this lady is dangerous. She was Big Brother’s worst enemy during the primordial and the source of his pain.</p>
<p>You can even say the weapon that can easily kill Big Brother is her. So if possible, make sure Big Brother is aware before you allow the two of them to meet. I know you won’t hide things from him, so better to tell him first.</p>
<p>Whenever they meet, things get ugly."</p>
<p>Vexthra frowned, the last sentence making her feel a bit of panic. "Did you say whenever they meet? How many times did they meet?"</p>
<p>This question, however, caused Gaia and her sisters to look a bit pale. The main reason Dia is a danger to Kent is what happened during the Primordial Era. In fact, they are all scared of what would happen this time around.</p>
<p>However, they cannot say it because of Karma. The karmic payment involved is just too steep for them to dare dump that on Vexthra. It would crush her regardless of her nature. Not even a Celestial can handle the truth.</p>
<p>So they hesitated. However, when they were looking for a way to wiggle their way out of this situation, a voice entered their heads, "Tell her, girls. It is best that she knows and finds a way to prevent the damage that is destined to happen."</p>
<p>Gaia took over and began revealing the danger Dia poses to Kent.</p>
<p>"During the Primordial Era, a young lady called Artemis became obsessed with Big Brother, so much to the point that she started stalking him. She tried many times to get close to him, but back then, Big Brother was still grieving Lady Elina’s death, so he had no time for her.</p>
<p>However, because of her persistence, she was able to get close to him. But it only lasted for a few months before tragedy struck.</p>
<p>While Big Brother was in seclusion, his enemies attacked, and while she could have escaped, because she wanted to make a good impression on Big Brother so that maybe he could accept her, she chose to defend him and instead died.</p>
<p>When Big Brother emerged from seclusion and learned what had happened, it crushed him. He was already grieving Lady Elina’s death, so losing Artemis was, if not a reminder of what had happened, then a tragedy he never expected.</p>
<p>However, he wasn’t the only one struck by Artemis’ death. Her sister, who back then was known as Hera, heard the news and, given what they did to Artemis’ body, became extremely angry and blamed Big Brother for everything.</p>
<p>Long story short. She tapped into her anger, and that was where she started her revenge. Over the years, Big Brother had saved many people. However, to get back at him, Hera attacked those he had saved, using their blood as a calling card.</p>
<p>She killed millions just to get to Big Brother. And when she finally did, she came very close to killing him. However, Big Brother managed to kill her.</p>
<p>But before she died, she cursed him, a curse that had followed him throughout his 13 lives, and perhaps now that she is here, this life too."</p>
<p>"Does this curse have something to do with Heart Demon?" Vexthra asked, her expression turning pale.</p>
<p>"Yes, Big Sister. Whenever they meet, the curse will be triggered and awaken Big Brother’s heart demon. However, aside from merely having a heart demon, the curse also makes it so that the stronger Big Brother becomes, the more pain he feels."</p>
<p>Gaia and her sisters trembled, their small bodies becoming even smaller. "This was why Big Brother never became a Supreme or Celestial in all the lives he had lived. The pain wouldn’t let him take that step, so the highest he ever reached was the universal God stage.</p>
<p>If possible, she must be very far from Big Brother because the moment they met, the curse would be triggered and Big Brother would have to go through the pain he went through in the many lives he had lived."</p>
<p>Vexthra frowned, her expression turning ugly.</p>
<p>All she had just heard pointed to Kent’s downfall. There is nothing good about any of what Gaia and her sisters had just said. They can prevent him from meeting Dia for now, but destiny has a way of being cheeky.</p>
<p>She is a goddess, so she knew just how things like this work. One way or another, Kent and Dia would meet, and whatever this curse is would be triggered.</p>
<p>So there is only one way to solve this: break the curse.</p>
<p>However, despite her many years of living, Vexthra had no idea where to even begin... this pained her heart very much.</p>
<p>To her, Kent’s life is more important than hers. So hearing Kent had lived with this much pain in 13 lives pained her greatly.</p>
<p>"Don’t worry, Big Sister. We have a way to solve this, but it would be very dangerous." Gaia walked toward Vexthra and held her hand.</p>
<p>"We have found a way to break the curse. Do you want to know how..."</p>
<p>"Yes," Vexthra would never say no to this. Gaia began with the story of another lady, someone who played a major part in Kent’s life during the Great War.</p>
<p>---</p>
<p>Meanwhile, Unity and Elaine did as they were told. They brought Dia to Gaiaville, where they helped her clean up and change into new clothes.</p>
<p>Afterwards, Elaine continued her healing, making sure by the time Vexthra returned, Dia would be as good as new.</p>
