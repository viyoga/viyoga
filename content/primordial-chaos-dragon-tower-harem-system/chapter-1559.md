<p>The process of dissolving the Chaos Heart Stone within his soul sea was what Kent feared. It is very painful; in fact, he would have to keep his mind focused on other things if he wanted to survive the agonizing pain.</p>
<p>Keeping his focus on the dissolving process will overwhelm his mind with so much pain and suffering.</p>
<p>Thankfully, the thought of him being responsible for the golden statue was enough to occupy his thoughts for a while.</p>
<p>Tower had already pointed out that, while indeed suspecting Paragon was a valid decision to make, there is the possibility that Kent chose to use him for his own good.</p>
<p>The Master of Purgatory had said a lot of things, but everything seemed to revolve around something bigger, something that had to do with his universe and several others out there.</p>
<p>He had no idea what the threat was just yet, but he knew there were going to be seven of them.</p>
<p>So far, he had met two of them: Paragon and the Master of Purgatory. Also, he had a hint that one of said people was the so-called second sworn brother of the Paragon.</p>
<p>He himself is a part of it, because why not?</p>
<p>So if that is the case, then perhaps there is something he is missing. Perhaps, once he recovers all of his Chaos Memories, he would be able to piece a few things together.</p>
<p>’At this point, saving my universe from the void beasts isn’t my only worry. I have to become strong enough to save other universes,’ he sighed. ’But first, I must get to Elina and ensure she is safe and sound.’</p>
<p>His thoughts wandered, and before long, three days had passed. Within those three days, the Chaos Heart Stone had dissolved considerably.</p>
<p>However, just as the process was about to end, Tower made a call that made Kent widen his eyes.</p>
<p>[It appears the master can handle more than one Chaos Heart Stone, so why don’t we add one more to ensure the Chaos Heart formed would be more robust?]</p>
<p>Kent blinked a few times before responding:</p>
<p>"Why not? If I am going to suffer, then I might as well gain something good from all the suffering."</p>
<p>Tower giggled and sent one more Chaos Heart Stone into Kent’s soul sea, causing the boiling sensation to intensify.</p>
<p>’Chaos spent less than five days forming his Chaos Heart. I wonder how long it will take me.’</p>
<p>***</p>
<p>Ten days later.</p>
<p>Kent released a powerful roar, his soul sea filled with so much chaotic energy. The two Chaos Heart Stones had been dissolved, and now, he had started the formation process.</p>
<p>Inside the First Blood Gate, right under the Blood Core, a purple core was slowly forming.</p>
<p>So far, Kent had formed two Blood Gates, and inside each, there was a Blood Core and a Flame Core. Right now, he was adding a Chaos Core (heart) into the first Blood Gate.</p>
<p>Once that was done, he would be able to use and absorb Chaos Energy.</p>
<p>It was surprisingly simple to form the core. The difficult part was the dissolving process. Once that was out of the way, the rest was as simple as forming the core by compressing everything.</p>
<p>In fact, because his cultivation techniques had become so automated, he wasn’t even doing anything. Tower and his body were doing all the heavy lifting.</p>
<p>’Chaos Energy, huh. I should be strong enough to contend even with Immortal Lords.’ Kent smiled, already looking forward to how much stronger he would become once everything was over.</p>
<p>It took three more days for the core formation process to be completed. However, just when that happened, a wave of powerful Chaos Energy exploded out from his body, spreading all over the planet.</p>
<p>Standing outside, Vexthra and her sisters raised their brows, shock appearing on their faces. They felt the pressure, and that was all they needed to know a monster had been born.</p>
<p>They could see that the air around the cave Kent was in had changed. It was much thicker and a bit suffocating. Even though they had no idea how much stronger he had become, they could tell from his aura that he was not the same Kent they knew.</p>
<p>Naturally, that was indeed true.</p>
<p>Kent had become a monster among monsters.</p>
<p>He had finally formed his Chaos Heart, and so he could finally harness the power of his bloodline much more efficiently.</p>
<p>His bloodline would only show its true power when he starts to use Chaos energy. Now that he had formed the chaos heart, he could use it without worry.</p>
<p>He now had access to Chaos Qi, Blood Qi, and Flame Qi. All these energies, if synced together, would make him a powerhouse that nobody was prepared for just yet.</p>
<p>Inside the cave, Kent opened the system interface and began reading some of the messages displayed there.</p>
<p>[You have formed your first Chaos Heart.]</p>
<p>[Congratulations. You have now unlocked Chaos Energy. You can absorb and harness Chaos Qi to power your attacks and spells.]</p>
<p>[You have awakened a unique skill called Chaos Soul.]</p>
<p>[Chaos Soul]</p>
<p>[Rank: Unique]</p>
<p>[Description: You can augment anything that is connected to your soul with Chaos Energy.]</p>
<p>Kent continued to read, most of the messages focusing on the unique skills he had awakened. Most of them were required if he wanted to use his Chaos Energy easily and without restrictions.</p>
<p>Of course, now that he had become an absolute existence, it was only normal for him to awaken such powerful abilities. Now, he could do more than he would have if he were still the old him.</p>
<p>It took some time to finish reading every last message. However, the smile on his face made it clear he was pleased with his gains.</p>
<p>Tower smiled beautifully, knowing that finally, her master was ready to head to the Star Gateway.</p>
<p>"I have already arranged everything for the master. So from here onwards, the master can easily handle the tasks ahead."</p>
<p>"Thank you, Tower." Kent stood up and walked out of the cave, feeling even more energized now. He had become so powerful that he couldn’t wait long enough to test his strength.</p>
<p>’Feels like I am me again,’ he smiled and looked at the clouds. After everything was said and done, the most logical decision he could ever make was to spend some time with his wives.</p>
<p>Once that was handled, he would be on his way.</p>
<p>So, since that logical decision was reached, he left the planet with his wives and Ainna. The next few months (outside time) would be spent in each other’s arms.</p>
