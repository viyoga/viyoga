<p>100 days—that was how long Kent remained inside the enlightenment, according to the tower’s time. However, the moment he opened his eyes, chaotic energy bathed him, threatening to flatten his surroundings.</p>
<p>His body was lifted into the air, and for an entire day, only chaotic sword qi and intent continued to explode from his body.</p>
<p>Slowly, everything calmed down, and he returned to the ground.</p>
<p>The first thing he did was smile at the three lolis smiling back at him. He had no idea the mischief they’d caused.</p>
<p>⟪Congratulations, Master. It looks like you have taken yet another huge leap in your cultivation of the sword path,⟫ Gaia said, and Kent’s smile bloomed.</p>
<p>He had indeed taken a huge leap, and from the look of things, this leap could not be looked down upon.</p>
<p>He had become very powerful, and he could feel it.</p>
<p>"Thank you, Gaia," Kent said.</p>
<p>Of course, while the three ladies were happy for him, part of the reason their happiness showed on their faces was the look on Kent’s face.</p>
<p>Over the course of the last 100 days, they had done their cordial best to decorate his face using markers, and right now, he looked rather funny by all standards.</p>
<p>But he did not know that, so he started checking his strength while the three giggled and laughed about his predicament in their heads.</p>
<p>After mastering the third skill, Kent went ahead and mastered four more, making it seven in total.</p>
<p>That was perhaps the highest number anyone had ever managed to master in any enlightenment.</p>
<p>The fourth skill he mastered was the [Edge of Eternal Night].</p>
<p>It was a rather unique and terrifying sword skill that plunged the battlefield into perpetual night, preventing the opponent from seeing the user’s next attack.</p>
<p>Naturally, Kent didn’t realize how terrifying this skill was until he mastered it.</p>
<p>That was when he saw that using it could easily allow him to land his next attack on an opponent—provided their senses weren’t strong enough to evade while blinded.</p>
<p>Of course, the skill also restricted one’s senses to a certain degree, but if someone possessed a much stronger perception, then it wouldn’t be much of a hassle.</p>
<p>But that was just the fourth skill.</p>
<p>It didn’t have much destructive power, but it was perfect for creating the groundwork for the more powerful skills he would unleash afterwards.</p>
<p>The fifth skill was another that didn’t necessarily deal a destructive blow. However, its stealthy and dangerous nature would make anyone wish it were an active, destructive technique.</p>
<p>Basically, it was the type of skill that, when used would affect the opponent’s perception.</p>
<p>It distorted their senses, making single attacks appear as double or multiple and altering the perceived direction of each strike.</p>
<p>So if a sword slash is coming at their waist, depending on the person’s mental strength, they might see it coming toward their waist or their head.</p>
<p>That was simply too powerful in all aspects, and right now, Kent had mastered such a skill to a stage where he could affect even those several cultivation levels ahead of him.</p>
<p>Then came the sixth skill—a powerful sword move called [Thousand Blades Reflection].</p>
<p>As its name suggested, when used, the opponent would be surrounded by thousands of reflections of any sword move Kent executed.</p>
<p>So if Kent used it on an opponent and unleashed [Rapid Slash], those arcs would multiply into thousands, creating a much more devastating version of the attack.</p>
<p>Strangely, it worked exactly as he had expected. The main attack and the reflections would have the same output and appearance—they are reflections of the same sword move, after all.</p>
<p>However, the disparity between them could still be taken into consideration.</p>
<p>But that wasn’t all. While the reflections mimicked a true attack, Kent could choose to switch them at will, making any of the reflections the real strike.</p>
<p>However, he noticed something the moment he mastered it. It was the effect it had on him.</p>
<p>This skill consumed an immense amount of mental strength, and at his current level, he could use it at most three times in a row before reaching his limit.</p>
<p>But even once was enough for him. The more he grew, the more powerful he became.</p>
<p>The last skill, however, was one that he would have blamed himself for if he had never found it. It was also the reason he dedicated the last month of his time there to learning the names of most of the skills.</p>
<p>The skill’s name was [Dimensional Reflection Slash].</p>
<p>When used, it bent reality, ensuring that every attack from the enemy never reached its target. Instead, the attack was reflected back at the attacker, forcing them to strike themselves.</p>
<p>But there was a catch.</p>
<p>Every attack reflected would have an increase in strength—twice the original power of the attack. It was like taunting an enemy to strike, only to watch them suffer as their own power was turned against them at twice their strength.</p>
<p>However, this skill was also incredibly taxing on his mind.</p>
<p>Plus, not every attack could be reflected. If the attack exceeded his current capabilities, the skill simply wouldn’t work.</p>
<p>Thankfully, he had awakened Sword’s Gaze, which would allow him to recognize which attacks posed a true threat to his life. Those attacks couldn’t be reflected and had to be defended against instead.</p>
<p>But after mastering the skill to some extent and strengthening his mind, he wouldn’t just be able to handle more of these attacks—he could end battles using his enemy’s own power.</p>
<p>Yet, that wasn’t the only thing that had changed.</p>
<p>"I have reached the peak of the Enhanced Sword Qi and Intent stage," Kent muttered, swirling his energy around him.</p>
<p>"Damn, this memory thing is much more powerful than I thought. I can already see this becoming my greatest strength in the coming years."</p>
<p>He didn’t know how this was possible, but he could already sense many changes—one of them being the skill he had just learned while traversing Sword Valley.</p>
<p>Naturally, this filled Kent with confidence, and he turned his gaze toward the Sword Bridge.</p>
<p>"Level Three, here I come."</p>
