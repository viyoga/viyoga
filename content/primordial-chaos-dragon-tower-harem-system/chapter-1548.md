<p>***</p>
<p>"Well, that took a dark turn real quick," Kent smirked, an evil look appearing in his eyes.</p>
<p>Deep within him, an untamed, arrogant will had awakened, making him feel a sudden disdain for the presence he sensed.</p>
<p>On the outside, Vexthra and her friends felt a sudden will that transcended everything else wash over them. For a moment, they all felt like going down on their knees and prostrate themselves at his feet.</p>
<p>However, as the feeling crept in, it suddenly vanished, and a voice came from the cave where Kent was cultivating.</p>
<p>"Sorry, ladies. I lost control there for a moment."</p>
<p>Vexthra smirked. Miryssa narrowed her eyes. Inaya frowned, and Ainna gritted her teeth, cursing the brat who had nearly made her bow down at his feet.</p>
<p>"This bastard. I will strangle him to death one day."</p>
<p>Vexthra tapped her on the shoulder and whispered into her ear, "I would love to see how that turns out for you."</p>
<p>Ainna recalled what she saw inside the harem tower, causing her cheeks to turn red. Knowing she had no way to win this time, she vanished and started unleashing hell on the beasts, further adding to Kent’s growing strength.</p>
<p>"Ah, the unfortunate struggle. We all went through it," Miryssa smirked and also joined the massacre. Inaya and Vexthra followed.</p>
<p>Kent, who saw and heard them through the [Soul Map], smiled and started to wait for all the Blood Qi in his system to be absorbed before he would start the third phase of his cultivation—Body Tempering using the Dragon Spirit Essence.</p>
<p>***</p>
<p>[Rainbow Dragon Monarch - Reincarnated during the Chaos Era]</p>
<p>Meanwhile, all over the universe, terrifying beings—beasts, humans, demons, angels, spirits—whether from this universe or from other realms beyond its confines—feel their backs break into a cold sweat when a sudden cold wind blows past them.</p>
<p>Sleeping somewhere in her private world, a beauty with rainbow-colored hair, nails, and eyebrows slowly opened her rainbow eyes, a smile forming on her lips.</p>
<p>"So you’re finally back, huh?" She raised her head from her pillow and stretched her arms. "My sweet Chaos, how I missed you."</p>
<p>During the Primordial Era, she was known as the Rainbow Dragon Monarch, a member of the Dragon Council, a cabal of dragons that Chaos formed during the last moments of the Primordial Era.</p>
<p>She was among the powerhouses that, at one point, were enemies of Chaos, but when the time came, they died fighting beside him when people he trusted betrayed and abandoned him.</p>
<p>She stood up and walked to the window overlooking the beautiful rainbow trees, perfectly lined all around her world.</p>
<p>"We will meet soon enough, little Chaos."</p>
<p>***</p>
<p>[Crystal Dragon Monarch - Reincarnated during the Shadow Era]</p>
<p>Somewhere else, also in his private world, the Crystal Dragon Monarch lifted his head and looked up at the sky and smiled. "Ah, I guess my days of peace are over."</p>
<p>One could even see a crazy look appear in his eyes. Far from his mansion, inside a huge vault, a pale spear trembled with excitement, sensing its master’s insatiable will to battle slowly rising.</p>
<p>"I guess that madman is back. Master only feels excitement when he is close."</p>
<p>During the Shadow Era, when the Ruthless Bandit dominated, the Crystal Monarch was also reincarnated along with his weapon, the Crystal Heart Spear.</p>
<p>***</p>
<p>[Darkness Dragon Monarch &amp; Shadow Dragon Monarch - Reincarnated during the Dark Era]</p>
<p>Somewhere else, the third strongest monarch after Chaos and Ignarax (Flame Dragon Monarch) plunged his scythe into the head of an abomination of a beast. Twisting the long handle of his weapon, a pale smile formed on his face.</p>
<p>He turned and looked at a shadow coiling around another abomination and smirked. "My dear shadow, looks like he is back."</p>
<p>"Who?" a feminine voice came from the shadow. Because she was intangible, she did not feel the sudden authority the monarch of darkness felt.</p>
<p>"Who else if not that madman Chaos?"</p>
<p>"Oh, I see. We better hurry back and use the small opening we have to torture him before he becomes a monster yet again."</p>
<p>"You read my mind, sweetheart." A chilling smile escaped his mouth. He jumped back into the chaos and started slaughtering more abominations.</p>
<p>During the Primordial Era, they challenged Chaos to a duel 101 times and lost all. However, now, it seemed they did not want to lose.</p>
<p>***</p>
<p>[Lightning Dragon Monarch - Reincarnated during the Divine Era]</p>
<p>Among the 24 Dragon Monarchs that fought and died for Chaos, he was the first to reincarnate.</p>
<p>He was there when the foreign race invaded, and he answered when Lord Genesis called all able warriors to take up arms and fight for the universe.</p>
<p>That call made by Lord Genesis triggered his memories of his past life, and so he fought and bled yet again. This time, however, he did not die, for the battle ended before he could face an opponent he could not kill.</p>
<p>During the Primordial Era, he was among the few who never clashed with Chaos. He saw Chaos as a superior existence, so instead of hating him and trying to prove himself, he sought his guidance.</p>
<p>They became friends, and for years, they shared knowledge with each other.</p>
<p>Today, seated somewhere in the realm of lightning and thunder, he smiled and muttered...</p>
<p>"Big Brother has returned. Looks like I have to hurry and attain the level of Primordial Lightning before we meet."</p>
<p>***</p>
<p>In several other places, the other monarchs reacted similarly. They all felt happy to sense that the Primordial Chaos Dragon had returned.</p>
<p>But it was not just them. Other beasts, humans, and all kinds of brave warriors that were affected by the Spell of Origin felt his return.</p>
<p>Not all of them were strong enough to reincarnate with their memories, but when his presence passed over them, they felt the urge to take up arms and rally behind him, since they knew that with him leading the charge, nothing could stand against them.</p>
<p>It is rare for someone to have this much effect on people, but Chaos did, and this time, he is bound to lead a much bigger and stronger army into battle.</p>
<p>Of course, when allies sense his presence, the foes are bound to do so also.</p>
