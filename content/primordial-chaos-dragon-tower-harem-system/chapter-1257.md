<p>Although Kent had found other uses for the Soul Universe, so far, he had only awakened three abilities for it.</p>
<p>The first ability allows him to convert Soul Essence into creating deadly weapons.</p>
<p>The Soul Universe grows by absorbing Soul Essence. That essence absorbed can be used to create any form of weapon Kent wants, provided he has a clear picture of what he wants to make.</p>
<p>Of course, there are better uses for the Soul Essence. However, as someone whose life is destined to be full of dangers, the [Soul Creation] ability is better used for making weapons for both offence and defence.</p>
<p>At the moment, he had created seven 40,000km wide asteroids, and over 3000 meteors that he keeps tempering inside his soul for when he will need them.</p>
<p>He had other weapons, but the major ones at the moment are the asteroids and the meteorites. He expected to use it during the just-ended battle. But in the end, his blood runes, flame veins, flame and ice elements were enough.</p>
<p>But should a day come when even his 50,600 blood runes and 120,000 flame veins won’t be enough, then he would unleash his trump cards.</p>
<p>The second ability is the [Soul Map], which allows him to see an 80,000km radius without even trying. This ability came when he needed it during his time in the [Valley of Torture].</p>
<p>Of course, back then, he was merely a Core Formation warrior. However, even after becoming a Heavenly Ascension Stage warrior, the distance hasn’t extended as one would expect.</p>
<p>It is still the same 80,000km radius. However, he can now see more details than before.</p>
<p>Back inside the [Flame Sea], there were many occasions where he failed to see the targets until they were much closer. Even Kaelith succeeded in sneaking up on him.</p>
<p>The tower explained that it was because his cultivation was lower than his opponent’s.</p>
<p>However, after this breakthrough, not even Death Stage warriors can sneak up on him now. He had become so much more powerful.</p>
<p>He is more afraid of Spirit Immortals sneaking up, but even so, he knew he had nothing to fear since, aside from sensing the body, the Soul Map also senses killing intent, shadows, and energy leaks.</p>
<p>This makes it impossible to be snuck up on, even by peak Root Immortals. Once he meets a Spirit Immortal, he will know what to think.</p>
<p>He can also share the Soul Map vision with those he wants to. He can even use the Soul Map to communicate with anyone he wants without their permission.</p>
<p>Aside from that, the Soul Map allows him to see openings in the opponent’s attacks and employ them to kill without having to test many possibilities.</p>
<p>The Soul Map has many other uses, but for now, these are the only uses Kent could harness.</p>
<p>Now, he had awakened a new ability: [Soul Weapons].</p>
<p>Basically, this new ability works the same as the [Soul Creation].</p>
<p>However, instead of creating weapons from Soul Essences, with this new ability, he can easily transform ’Soul Spirits’ into weapons, and then the Soul Essence will be used to temper them.</p>
<p>Basically, this ability simply found a use for the Soul Spirits that Kent had been collecting after finding out he could store them inside his Soul Universe.</p>
<p>Now, he has hundreds of thousands of Soul Spirits that have been getting tempered by the Soul Essence for a while now. He had used some as Haunting Souls back in the Flame Sea.</p>
<p>However, now, he had found a way, actually, to turn those souls into weapons. So, whether he keeps them as Haunting Souls, now, instead of merely using them as soul bombs for the [Soul Explosion] ability that came with the [Soul Map], he can do more.</p>
<p>He can pick ten Heavenly Ascension Stage Soul Spirits and transform four into one legendary soul warrior, transform three into a soul armour to cover the soul warrior, then transform the remaining three into a deadly soul weapon, like a sword or scythe.</p>
<p>If this happens, not only would this soul warrior be a legendary combatant, but in the end, Kent can even explode their body, armour, and weapon, creating an explosion akin to ten Tier 19 souls.</p>
<p>Of course, once a soul weapon is destroyed, it never returns to the Soul Universe again.</p>
<p>However, Kent, who always seeks quality over quantity, knew that if a lot of effort were put into creating powerful soul weapons, then worrying about them getting destroyed would be the last of his worries.</p>
<p>"This is a great ability. It is a way of creating legendary warriors from the souls of those I have killed. I will be giving them purpose, and that purpose would be to fight for a better universe."</p>
<p>Kent made a fist and raised it.</p>
<p>"I can already see a legion of Soul Warriors and Soul Weapons pouring from a dark portal, radiating nothing but the chilling power of the soul."</p>
<p>A smile appeared on his lips, "Oh, what a sight that would be."</p>
<p>A legion of Soul Warriors sure sounded like a good thing. Kent already has the Undead Legion comprising 13 powerful warriors.</p>
<p>Although Kent isn’t done creating his undead, the need for more hasn’t crossed his mind yet. Aside from them, he also has 13 legendary warriors in his Eternal Wrath Legion.</p>
<p>No new dungeon aside from the Planet Killer Dungeon remained to be conquered. Once that is done, it will bring the Eternal Wrath legion to 14 members.</p>
<p>However, Kent knew there would be more. After all, when the Chaos Dragon called, the 13 legions that came had over 50 warriors each, except for the [Lost Knights]</p>
<p>Kent is already aware that there are 69 in the Night Riders Legion. So, aside from the [Lost Knights], who he knew there were only 14 of them, the rest had more left to join.</p>
<p>The battle coming requires all legions to be at their peak.</p>
<p>So for now, he has the following.</p>
<p>13 Undead Legion, 13 Eternal Wrath Legion, 14 Lost Knights (but only one of them had undergone his first evolution: Veydris). He also has 4 Soul Sentinels led by the legendary Aether Warden (Soul Sentinel): Elsa.</p>
<p>There is also the Beast Legion, of which he only has 7 in the Immortal World. He can’t wait to reconnect with them.</p>
<p>Then there are the Night Riders, who he can boldly say are the strongest at the moment. They have demigods and even a god among them.</p>
<p>Now, if he plays his card right, then maybe he might just be adding the next legion. But that would depend on whether, when the Chaos Dragon called, a legion known as the Soul Warriors also came.</p>
<p>And if indeed they heed his call and were among the 13 legions, then the question would be, which of his incarnations brought that legion together?</p>
<p>He knew his legions were ones ruled by his incarnations.</p>
<p>"Bit by bit, I will get there. However, I have to hurry. If the forsaken race had started making moves, then sooner rather than later, they would make their big move, and knowing them, chaos and destruction are what they command."</p>
<p>Kent snapped his finger, forming a recliner from ice that he pressed his back against just when the yellow sun broke through the clouds and illuminated the scorched surface of the planet.</p>
<p>He took a deep breath, then he decided to review the memories of the forsaken seer.</p>
