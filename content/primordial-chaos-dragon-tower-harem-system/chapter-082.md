<p>"Tower, before we get into the rewards, can you tell me more about the towers? I seem to have noticed that some were giving me classes and subclasses. What is the difference here?" Kent asked.</p>
<p>So far, he has unlocked the Harem, Alchemy, Swordsmanship, Soul Cultivation, Guardian, and Servant Towers. However, among these six towers, only three had given him a profession, so he just couldn’t wrap his mind around it.</p>
<p>[The towers are in two forms, Primordial and Mystical. Primordial Towers are designed to give you a class and subclasses once they are unlocked. The Swordsmanship, Alchemy, and Soul Cultivation Towers are examples of this.</p>
<p>Then there are the Mystical Towers, designed to aid the master in managing all the different aspects he wants to handle. For example, if you want to manage your women and ensure they grow along with you, the Harem Tower is designed for that.</p>
<p>Because you haven’t fully awakened the towers, you won’t understand much of what I am saying, but the moment you gain first-hand access to them, you will see how advanced and helpful they can be.</p>
<p>Just like how you have Dual Cultivation, you can exploit other avenues once you awaken it. The tower is more advanced than you think.]</p>
<p>"I see. So, how many Primordial and Mystical Towers are there?" Kent asked. Although he had no plans to awaken many, since focusing on all would be a headache, he still had to ask.</p>
<p>[This question cannot be answered as there are many towers to choose from. But since the master asked, I can say that if you meet the requirements, you will be able to unlock hundreds of towers. After all, you are the Master of the Towers.]</p>
<p>"That makes me happy to hear. It would be great, though, if I could bestow towers on my women and help them grow," Kent sighed.</p>
<p>"Alright, let’s dig in." Kent wasn’t about to focus on things he couldn’t access at the moment. Once he becomes a Tower Master by reaching the level of Root Grandmaster, he will be able to focus on that.</p>

<p>[You have successfully completed a quest. Quest Name: Bounce that Ass]</p>
<p>[Rewards: Diary of the Celestial Alchemist Vol. 1]</p>
<p>"Wait, what? Tower, is this what I think it is?" Kent held a smile on his face. If this was what he was thinking, then he was about to become very happy.</p>
<p>[Yes, Master, this is exactly what you are thinking. The diary is an account of the Celestial Alchemist, the same alchemist you are learning from.]</p>
<p>"Damn, could my day get any better than this?" Kent smiled and retrieved the diary. It was a book like the small diaries back on Earth, just as he had expected. But this one was much more mystical in a way.</p>
<p>Just holding it gave Kent a sense of déjà vu. It felt so real to him in ways he didn’t understand. However, he was more than happy to be holding it.</p>
<p>He touched it against his forehead in an attempt to learn it quickly, but it didn’t disperse.</p>
<p>"Huh?"</p>
<p>[The master cannot use the same old way to learn this. This book is the account of the Celestial Alchemist. The master must read it like a good old book should be read.]</p>
<p>"I see." Kent wasn’t disappointed.</p>
<p>It had been a year since he had read like a normal person, so he could only start now. Of course, this diary was just around 70 pages, so he wouldn’t even need a week to finish it.</p>
<p>"Let me put this on hold for now. I suppose it contains additional knowledge on alchemy, and I cannot speed-read it like I used to."</p>
<p>Back on Earth, after he was killed, the police unearthed hundreds of books from his house, earning him the title God of Readers.</p>
<p>He had read hundreds of books, so after checking his online accounts and the reviews he had left, many authors were in tears.</p>
<p>Many had been searching for the reader named ’Xytor’ for years, never realizing their good old movie director was him. He made sure to correct them where they were wrong and encouraged them not to give up on their books.</p>
<p>It had been an emotional moment for many authors back on Earth.</p>
<p>"Next quest, Tower," Kent said with a smile. For some reason, he couldn’t wait to delve into the diary.</p>
