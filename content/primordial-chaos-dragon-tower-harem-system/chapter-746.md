<p>Boom.</p>
<p>The body of a large scorpion flame beast slammed into the ground after Cyran pierced its sixth eye with an arrow. A large crater was created where the body landed. Though not tired, the Eight-Half Elves sigh.</p>
<p>"That was the last of them," Dorren, a handsome young man and one of the four Half-Elves who would be entering the Flame Tower, said, sheathing his sword.</p>
<p>Kent smiled and jumped down from a tree he had stood on while the Half-Elves fought. All around the five-mile radius, there were seven bodies of flame beasts, the strongest being the Six Wooden Leg Spider.</p>
<p>It had already refined its core six times, and the strength it packed wasn’t something the eight Half-Elves had expected.</p>
<p>Thankfully, they had the Princess to help tilt the odds in their favor. She had already refined her core six times, and on the last—</p>
<p>Even so, she had to press on hard before they took down the spider.</p>
<p>Kent, who had seen the battle from start to finish, knew that if this was the finest of a suppressed race, then this world surely held some potential—one he could exploit for his gain.</p>
<p>"That was a good fight. However, you all need to learn how to fight individually against all odds and as a team," Kent said, collecting the seventh flame spirit they had acquired.</p>
<p>"Arion, Cyran, and Helyra are already a team, so they were able to coordinate well, but the rest of you need to adapt. Of course, I saw the coordination in your last battle, and while it isn’t perfect, I know things will be different soon."</p>
<p>They nodded, and Kent produced seven flames. He checked them all, but he already had aspects of every last flame they had farmed.</p>
<p>The moment the Half-Elves saw the colored flames appear in front of Kent, smiles appeared on their faces, wiping away any form of fatigue they may have felt.</p>
<p>"You’ve all done well, so here is your gain." He gave them each a flame that complemented their soul flames. "The reason for having your own soul flame is the ability to inherit the aspects of the flame spirits you absorb.</p>
<p>"For example, the flame spirit that Madea is about to devour has an aspect of illusion. That means the moment she devours it, the illusion aspect will be inherited by her soul flame, which will allow her to use it with her soul flame, and as an archer, she can utilize the illusion with her arrows."</p>
<p>Hearing Kent’s words made them feel even more powerful, for as he had said, they were going to grow endlessly with their soul flames.</p>
<p>For now, they could only hope that by the time they left the Flame Valley, their soul flames would reach Tier 7.</p>
<p>Kent had already noticed something intriguing — his Chaos Flame.</p>
<p>When the Half-Elves awakened their soul flames, he felt how hungry his Chaos Flame had become. He only felt this hunger when he fed on one powerful flame while passing through the Core Region.</p>
<p>This hunger was like a call, showing how much his soul flame yearned to consume the soul flames of others. Unfortunately, aside from the Half-Elves that he planned to use as an extension of his influence, the others didn’t have natural soul flames.</p>
<p>But the same could not be said for the sect.</p>
<p>Kent knew that, one way or another, he would soon be devouring more powerful soul flames.</p>
<p>"Let’s go," Kent said, and they started moving. Their next destination is waiting for them with powerful flame beasts ready to devour them.</p>
<p>"So, what do you guys know about the sect?" Kent saw this as a nice opportunity to pick their brains on what to expect from the sect.</p>
<p>The eight Half-Elves looked at each other before turning to Princess Elowen, since she was the oldest and most knowledgeable among them.</p>
<p>She sighed and began to speak, "Just like you, I don’t know much, but I happen to have the privilege of being the daughter of a king, so naturally, I know some things.</p>
<p>According to what my dad told me, the Supreme Immortal Sect is the strongest in this world. He mentioned something about the Planetary Alliance, where many medium-sized worlds in the same planetary system come together to form an alliance.</p>
<p>Of course, he doesn’t know how many planets are in this alliance, but he said that more than 50 planets can come together to form an alliance. Any less and it will be called a sub-sector alliance.</p>
<p>And this world is represented by the Supreme Immortal sect."</p>
<p>"You know, for someone who doesn’t know much, you sure know a lot," Kent said, already thankful he had someone like a princess in his company for the time being.</p>
<p>Princess Elowen smiled. "Well, I was desperate to find ways to save my mom, so I asked a lot of questions and found out about some things.</p>
<p>Unfortunately, to learn more, I would have to join the sect. But I know enough to say the Supreme Immortal Sect rules this world. They are the strength, defenders of this world, and they represent this world in both the sub-sector and the Planetary Alliance.</p>
<p>They have the resources to grow and nurture talents. So, in case you think this world is boring, perhaps getting into the sect will show you more."</p>
<p>"I guess I can’t wait to enter the sect and find out for myself."</p>
<p>Kent smiled, knowing things were getting interesting now.</p>
<p>He also understood that the Hand was perhaps a small fry now... his focus should be on the sect.</p>
<p>Of course, knowing the Hand was planning to open the doors and unleash the race of the apocalypse on everyone was something he had to fix.</p>
<p>But hearing there was a much bigger alliance housing many worlds, he knew his horizon had to broaden.</p>
<p>’I have been roaming in the wrong pool. If I play my cards right, my name will soon shake both high and immortal worlds.’</p>
<p>Ever since the Paragon gave him the Soul Universe soul technique, he had started to look for ways to grow stronger much faster. For some reason, he felt this soul universe was something he couldn’t ignore.</p>
<p>He knew that when it comes down to it, his ultimate trump card will be the soul Universe, and making it grow much stronger now should be something he wouldn’t take for granted.</p>
<p>If anything, Kent knew housing thousands of asteroids in his soul would surely create the right amount of chaos needed to handle the most potent threat there is.</p>
<p>’Thousands of medium worlds, huh. It will undoubtedly be a wild journey. One I will remember forever.’</p>
<p>Kent grinned, almost feeling the chaos seeping into his bones. That madness Chaos felt was now a part of him, and he began to feel it.</p>
<p>If the leaders of the sect knew a madman was coming into their sect, perhaps they would do everything they could to ensure he never entered their premises. But then again, the Chaos Dragon cannot be held back.</p>
<p>One way or another, Kent would find his way into the sect, and if they don’t take care, perhaps their fear would be realized.</p>
<p>Soon, Kent and his team reached the second location the Tower had mapped and started their second massacre of the day.</p>
