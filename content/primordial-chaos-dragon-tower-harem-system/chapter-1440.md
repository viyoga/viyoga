<p>Kent and Crimson Dagger spent quite some time together learning more about Planet Brymrr. After he had revealed all he knew, they proceeded to discuss the mission he had given the Midnight Shadows.</p>
<p>He made sure Crimson Dagger understood the importance of such a mission.</p>
<p>From all he knew, every Era has its own danger. The Primordial Era brought its dangers, and so did the Divine Era. This means this Era will also bring its dangers, so the remnants of the past must be handled.</p>
<p>This means the Void Beasts, Spatial Beasts, and now the Dark Creatures must be taken care of before the danger meant for this Era arrives.</p>
<p>If they had to face several enemies at the same time, then his years of preparation would all be for nothing.</p>
<p>So he gave this mission to the Midnight Shadows.</p>
<p>Of course, he planned to add the Night Riders to it, since just as there are remnants of the Dark Creatures, there are also the Shadow Beings who appeared during the Shadow Era.</p>
<p>At one point, the Midnight Shadows would have to resume their battle with the Shadow Creatures. But since Crimson Dagger understood the mission at hand, he promised to reach out to every last Faceless Agent and all Midnight Shadows.</p>
<p>It is all hands on deck, so they have to be ready and available for anything.</p>
<p>"Before I forget, the Pill Council reached out after your name appeared on the Pill Dao Tablet," Crimson Dagger suddenly said.</p>
<p>"Oh, my name appeared on the Pill Dao Tablet?" Kent was a bit stunned.</p>
<p>He hadn’t had the chance to go anywhere after his recent breakthrough in alchemy, so he wasn’t aware he had made such a monumental breakthrough.</p>
<p>"You aren’t aware, huh?" Crimson Dagger sighed. "Your name is trending again in the universe. This time, every alchemist, both good and bad ones, wants their hands on you."</p>
<p>"Is that so?"</p>
<p>"I am afraid that is the case, so be careful out there. The Pill Council came to me to help them protect you. They know you are in a high world, but they had no idea which planet you are on.</p>
<p>So for now, they want me to protect you. But seeing you are more than capable, I believe I have nothing to worry about."</p>
<p>"That is indeed true. I can protect myself. But since they asked for your help, tell them you have everything under control."</p>
<p>Crimson Dagger nodded, and a few minutes later, they walked out of the Midnight Temple. After leaving the area, they headed deeper into the forest. A few minutes later, they arrived before a simple cave.</p>
<p>"Although there is no restriction on me here, which means I can spend as much time as I want here, now that I have a mission, I must head back to the Supreme Realm to get things in motion."</p>
<p>A few minutes later, Crimson Dagger left, and Kent returned to the Midnight Temple. He gave Adin some more instructions before leaving.</p>
<p>After leaving the Midnight Temple, Kent headed back to the Soul Tower and entered the Void Temple right away.</p>
<p>He wanted to form his second Flame Core before heading to Planet Brymrr. So with all resources ready, he entered seclusion.</p>
<p>The first floor of the Void Temple has a flame source that came directly from a Primordial Flame, so he got right on it and started working to form his second Flame Core.</p>
<p>Days quickly passed.</p>
<p>Back inside the 9th floor of the dungeon, everyone was working hard to improve their strength.</p>
<p>The three rewards Vexthra distributed turned out to be something much bigger than anything they had initially thought.</p>
<p>While the plan was to wait for Unity to emerge from the cocoon, they discovered they themselves needed time to train as well.</p>
<p>The Limit Breaker Key allowed them all to break one limit. Of course, it was just a single limit, but after they broke it, they became so powerful that everyone was shocked.</p>
<p>Naturally, that happened thanks to the Innate Talent Awakening Pill they took right after. Some awakened innate abilities, while a few awakened other innate talents.</p>
<p>Kaizo even awakened an innate treasure called Lightning Conduit. It comes in the form of an augmenter, which he can use to enhance his gauntlet and increase the damage output of his punches.</p>
<p>Kaizo wasn’t the only one who awakened a treasure. Others did too, and that joy made them take the next treasure seriously.</p>
<p>This was the first time they received a Bloodline Strengthening Elixir. So far, all of them are locked in a suspended animation state after taking the elixir.</p>
<p>Something mystical is happening to them, and that can be seen from the aura coming out of their bodies. Every last one of them had already awakened their bloodlines, so this elixir is basically strengthening them.</p>
<p>But as to how much effect it would have on them, that will be revealed when the process is over.</p>
<p>Meanwhile, Axiom and his two pals continued their massacre across the entire planet. In just two weeks, they had killed hundreds.</p>
<p>In fact, despite Planet Kenai being a prison for some of the dangerous criminals in the immortal realm, when it comes to matters of right and wrong, Axiom and his pals don’t care.</p>
<p>They had no time to listen to their pleading. To them, the more they pleaded, the more they wanted to kill them.</p>
<p>It got to the point that, despite the rivalry between the criminals on Planet Kenai, they started working together to stop the three dangerous-looking humans who seemed to take pleasure in killing them.</p>
<p>But despite their efforts, the three Void Beasts are just too monstrous. They have the upper hand, considering Axiom is an Immortal Monarch with Nine Immortal Qi.</p>
<p>There is just no way several True Immortals coming together will be able to stop them. Even if he was alone in this unholy campaign, there is just no way they could stop him.</p>
<p>So with Kailan and Kol backing him, nobody on the planet could stop them. So they continued, and for the better part of their mission, they had a good harvest.</p>
<p>But 25 days into their massacre, a powerful pressure fell on them just when they were closing in on a cave. The three of them came to a stop and started to resist the pressure.</p>
<p>Axiom had it easy, but his pals were a bit pressured since the pressure was much heavier than they could brush off. This can only mean the danger coming wasn’t one that could be underestimated.</p>
<p>But instead of finding ways to escape, they stayed, and a few seconds later, a dragon lady walked out from the cave holding a baby.</p>
<p>She has pinkish-silver hair that falls down her back. She is also clad in clean armor made from a very durable material, something a criminal sent to such a gory place shouldn’t have.</p>
<p>The moment their eyes landed on her, they all frowned. For the first time since they began their massacre, they hesitated to attack someone.</p>
<p>This made them wonder just why that was.</p>
<p>The dragon lady is an Immortal Lord, and judging by how effortlessly she manages to bring the three of them to a halt, she is no simple Immortal Lord.</p>
<p>She stopped 40 meters from the three Void Beasts, and for a minute, she said nothing. She only kept her eyes on them as if reading their thoughts.</p>
<p>"My name is Eirdis, Queen of the Flame Jade Dragon Race. This is my daughter, Xandira."</p>
