<p>[Your Quest titled ’Void Temple Destruction’ has been completed successfully.]</p>
<p>[Rewards have been released into your inventory]</p>
<p>[Rewards]</p>
<p>↠ All debt owed to Karma would be cleared</p>
<p>↠ 50 Billion Tower Points</p>
<p>↠ Destiny Dream Key</p>
<p>↠ Five Memory Keys (Chaos, Jest, Faceless) &amp; 2 Memory Keys for the Ruthless Bandit</p>
<p>↠ Absolute Life Coin</p>
<p>↠ Forsaken Records (1st Volume)</p>
<p>The quest was supposed to be very difficult. However, in the end, it turned out to be rather simple and a bit unexpected. But looking at the rewards, he knew he had struck it big.</p>
<p>Aside from his debt to Karma, he had now amassed quite a huge sum of tower points. "50 billion is a large sum of money, considering the amazing things I would be doing with it."</p>
<p>Kent smiles. He had indeed struck it big time this time around. Not only did he now have billions, but with the dungeons now being cleared, very soon, he would be swimming in billions of tower points.</p>
<p>Away from that, there is the Destiny Dream Key.</p>
<p>He retrieved a small golden key and appraised it...</p>
<p>[Destiny Dream Key]</p>
<p>[Description: Enter the dream of anyone you are destined with. Just think about them and press the Destiny Dream Key against your forehead.]</p>
<p>The first name that appeared in Kent’s mind is Elina. She is the only person that came to mind, so at the moment, he wanted nothing more than to see what she was dreaming about.</p>
<p>He had already located her through an item called the Destiny Map. This means he shares a destiny with her, and as such, this key would work for her too.</p>
<p>If he can enter her dream and assure her that he is alive and coming for her, perhaps that would lift her spirit a bit.</p>
<p>She might not remember him, but since he does, he knows the best way to let her know he is coming is to invade her dream and try his best.</p>
<p>The universe wants what is best for him, so he would, without a doubt, use the item.</p>
<p>However, before doing anything, he decided to check the rest of his rewards.</p>
<p>Aside from five memory keys for Jest, Chaos, and Faceless, he now gets to remember a different life, too, making it four out of 13.</p>
<p>He didn’t know why he was given the key, but learning that the Ruthless Bandit once entered the Star Gateway, he knew he had to remember something about him and see what made him a big shot.</p>
<p>The journey through the Star Gateway would be harrowing, so he at least wants to know something about this other part of himself before things get a bit complex.</p>
<p>[Absolute Life Coin]</p>
<p>[Description: Leave a trace of your soul in the Absolute Life Coin, which would preserve your life and grant you an additional chance at life even if your head was severed.]</p>
<p>"Wow, I didn’t see that coming," Kent muttered, looking at the Absolute Life Coin in his palm. "The ability to not die even if I die is unheard of."</p>
<p>He is literally getting a second life. So, if he were to die in this one, the coin would revive him. It is like the Life Ascension Pill, only that instead of swallowing it, he merely had to imbue it with his soul essence, and the rest would take care of itself.</p>
<p>This one is even guaranteed.</p>
<p>"Although I have no plans on dying, taking precautions isn’t bad at least."</p>
<p>He channeled his soul essence into it.</p>
<p>[You now have a second chance at Life]</p>
<p>Seeing the message, a smile appeared on his face. Kent didn’t want to jinx it, but while he had hoped the Void Temple was a difficult challenge, he wanted the Star Gateway to be easy.</p>
<p>Vexthra had already spoiled the danger level he would be facing, so he knew he had to be prepared. Having a second chance at life is good enough.</p>
<p>"What could go wrong?" He smiled and retrieved the last item in his rewards.</p>
<p>"The Forsaken Records," Kent muttered, already aware of what content is contained in the book in his hand. It has a red cover and a few tattered ropes falling from the edges. This gave it an eerie appearance and a bit of scare.</p>
<p>[Forsaken Records]</p>
<p>[Volume: One]</p>
<p>[Description: Know the history and hidden secrets of the forsaken race and what they represent.]</p>
<p>"I thought as much. However, what is there even to be worried about? I mean, they basically made first contact with the Void Beast, as per the records I have read, and let themselves be corrupted by it.</p>
<p>So there is nothing to even read about."</p>
<p>Kent hates the forsaken race so much that learning new things about them isn’t something he wants. To him, he already knows what there is to know about them, and that they all deserve to die.</p>
<p>He threw the book into his inventory and stopped thinking about it.</p>
<p>[The master may want to review the content in the book. There is no harm in learning something new about your enemies. There might even be something new and a way to go about handling them.]</p>
<p>Kent nodded, but he still didn’t take the book to read.</p>
<p>"I will need some time to handle that, Tower. For now, I have other things to worry about."</p>
<p>The more he learned about the forsaken race, the better he would understand them, as Tower said. However, it is not so simple for him. Most of his pain came from them throughout the many lives he had lived.</p>
<p>So his hatred for them is also very deep.</p>
<p>"I first have to use the Destiny Dream Key and use all the memory keys. That is more important to me now."</p>
<p>Entering Elina’s dream is something he never expected to happen to him now. He was supposed to go to her and take her home. However, since the process is a bit slow, the only way to reach her is to use a back door.</p>
<p>Kent retrieves the key and presses it against his forehead. The next second, he fell back and fell into a simple sleep.</p>
<p>---</p>
<p>Somewhere else, Elina had just returned from her shift, her entire body dirty. She quickly moved into a cave and cleaned herself. Then she changed into a new set of tattered clothes before entering her small cave.</p>
<p>She sat down beside a small girl who, despite her small arms and legs, also had a slave collar around her neck.</p>
<p>One might even ask why such a loli would look like a threat that had to be put on a leash. However, when born into slavery, the moment you come of age, you would be subjected to the same treatment as the adults.</p>
<p>She planted a kiss on her forehead, then she entered her own cave. Using her dirty work clothes as a pillow, she lay down on her bed. Her next shift starts in 5 hours, so catching some sleep is something all the slaves never joke with.</p>
<p>She lay on her back, her beautiful brown eyes staring at the roof of the cave. A few seconds later, a tear dropped from her eyes. However, she wiped it and turned to the side.</p>
<p>3 minutes later, she was deep asleep.</p>
<p>To her, those 5 hours of sleep are something she wanted mainly for one thing: to dream about the handsome dragon she always seems chummy with for reasons she had no idea of.</p>
