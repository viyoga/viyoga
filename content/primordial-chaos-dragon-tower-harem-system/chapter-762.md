<p>Kent studied the three new arrivals, and a smirk appeared on his lips, but only for a moment. He didn’t want to become arrogant, just as those he had been waiting for had arrived.</p>
<p>The first to appear wasn’t that old. He is in his late 50s and called Elder Lance from the Alchemy Hall in the outer region of the Supreme Immortal Sect. The two with him were also from the outer region but were disciples, Pill disciples, hence the green robes.</p>
<p>They looked down at the various kingdom leaders with no sign of respect in their eyes. If anything, it was the leaders who looked at them with expressions of reverence.</p>
<p>But Kent saw they were paying attention to one person.</p>
<p>They were looking at Uncle Drake, who, just as he looked at everyone, watched them with no sign of fear or respect.</p>
<p>Of course, this look didn’t sit well with the three arrivals. They expected everyone to look at them with respect, but Uncle Drake was making them look like just anyone.</p>
<p>’This should be good.’</p>
<p>Kent slowly stepped aside as the show of arrogance began.</p>
<p>"Who are you?" the elder asked, looking at Uncle Drake as if he would swallow him. Uncle Drake stared at him for a few seconds before responding.</p>
<p>"Nobody."</p>
<p>Those words weren’t received well. And Kent, who knew what was about to happen, stepped aside further, making sure he was far from the impact zone.</p>
<p>BAM!</p>
<p>A palm strike came from the male disciple, landing where Uncle Drake stood. Dust rose into the air, creating an opaque aura that obscured everyone’s vision. Of course, they relaxed as the aftermath of the attack awaited judgment.</p>
<p>"How dare you disrespect an elder from the Supreme Immortal Sect?" The young male disciple shouted.</p>
<p>All around, everyone’s eyes were locked on the place where Uncle Drake had stood, waiting for the dust to clear.</p>
<p>They all expected that attack because it wasn’t the first time it had happened. A while back, something similar happened to the Dragons, resulting in the deaths of three elders from the Earth Dragon and Flame Dragon races that day.</p>
<p>So the moment they looked at Uncle Drake, everyone knew what was about to happen. They expected it, and it happened.</p>
<p>However, after years of being disrespected by representatives from the Supreme Immortal Sect, the script had flipped.</p>
<p>While everyone was looking at where the attack landed, Kent smirked, knowing Uncle Drake had long since made his move.</p>
<p>"What makes you think I care whether or not you are from that sect..." The voice came from behind the disciple who attacked, and before he could move, a firm grip grabbed hold of his neck.</p>
<p>But that wasn’t all; the moment Uncle Drake grabbed him, a powerful force emanated from his body, forcing the elder and the lady disciple to drop hard to the ground, landing on their knees immediately.</p>
<p>Chills ran down everyone’s spine when they saw this.</p>
<p>Of course, aside from the two who were affected, just the shockwave was enough to force the others back several meters.</p>
<p>Uncle Drake dangled the brat in his grip and looked lazily at the Aelder lance. "I don’t care who you are or where you come from.</p>
<p>You are here for a reason, so am I. So, before you expect me to show you respect, show yourself some respect. Acting like you are above all else is simply foolish.</p>
<p>Countless people in this world are much more impressive than you. I know this because I am better than you and will always remain so.</p>
<p>So you may act all high and mighty, but keep that inside your precious sect, not out here.</p>
<p>I will not be lenient next time."</p>
<p>With that, he let go of the male disciple, who crashed to the ground, passing out immediately. Uncle Drake looked lazily around, and when he locked eyes with Kent, a smile appeared on his lips.</p>
<p>Kent gave him a slight nod.</p>
<p>Then, with a wave of his hand, the void cracked, and he vanished inside. It was only when he left that the pressure vanished.</p>
<p>’Nicely done, Uncle Drake. Now, I want to see what they’ll do after this humiliation.’ Kent was rather happy after Uncle Drake thumped the dignity of the overly arrogant reps from the Supreme Immortal Sect.</p>
<p>All three of them were humans, which looked strange. But in a way, Kent connected the dots, and the answer was quite simple.</p>
<p>Sonox is part of the Celestial Conclave planetary alliance, which comprises 64 worlds. It was only natural for humans from this world to come to this one.</p>
<p>Of course, the only reason they would come here was if the sect was as impressive as they said. There are 64 worlds, and Kent knew there would be 64 powerful sects, each representing a world.</p>
<p>So if Sonox were in the top ten best sects, ultimately, those who ranked below them would seek to come and grow under the banner of Sonox.</p>
<p>Seeing humans was normal, but Kent knew they weren’t from Sonox right away after scanning them, of course.</p>
<p>Watching the elder and the lady disciple stand up after Uncle Drake left was rather satisfying to watch. Inwardly, the world leaders were already planning to thank Uncle Drake when they saw him again.</p>
<p>He restored their pride, and while they knew news of this wouldn’t be heard by anyone from the sect, it was good to watch.</p>
<p>The reps didn’t even have the chance to boost before Uncle Drake shut them up, and looking at them now, Kent knew they wouldn’t be able to do anything ever again. The face they lost could not be replaced, and they knew that.</p>
<p>’They are strong, that one is true. Even as an alchemist, their strength level is very impressive. But Uncle Drake is on another level.</p>
<p>His strength is terrifying. So these three would be traumatized for a while. I hope more of them will be in the sect so I can shut them up too. For now, the plan is to get into the sect by being impressive."</p>
<p>A few minutes after Uncle Drake entered the void, the mountain vibrated and the sea of lava surged, filling the space with potent heat.</p>
<p>The shaking lasted for a few minutes, then it stopped. However, the moment it stopped, a giant tower rose from the ground and ascended into the air, standing about 500 meters tall.</p>
<p>The tower seemed to exude a rather potent flame energy, one that made Kent smile. He could already feel his body craving the heat.</p>
