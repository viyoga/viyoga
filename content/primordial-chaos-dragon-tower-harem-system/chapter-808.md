<p>"If it isn’t enough, I will add more to it," Kent said, watching the Pill Monarch, whose brow was raised, looking at Kent with an expression that on one part displayed shock while on the other side displayed an unreadable look—one that made Kent nervous.</p>
<p>He didn’t know whether his request would be received or might get him kicked out.</p>
<p>It sounded like he wanted to bribe the alliance to buy a slot for Suoh, whom he planned to leave a good impression on. However, he was merely trying to make a name for himself and help Suoh while at it.</p>
<p>"Are you sure you want to give 20 soul-type pill recipes to the alliance?" Kent sighed a little, hearing her question.</p>
<p>"Well, I don’t have a use for them, so I might as well give them away to a good cause, and since it will be helping my friend Suoh, I suppose it will be worth it."</p>
<p>Pill Monarch Nefeli studied Kent for a few seconds before asking a question.</p>
<p>"Do you know the value of a single soul-type pill recipe?"</p>
<p>"Can’t say I’ve had the privilege to explore the world of alchemy, seeing I started making pills just three years ago," Kent laughed, knowing he had only lit a pill flame for a quarter of the time.</p>
<p>"So why don’t you enlighten me?"</p>
<p>"Then listen well. In the world of Alchemy, some recipes are rare, so rare that finding them demands great wealth and connections.</p>
<p>Soul-type recipes are one such rarity.</p>
<p>They were once popular during the Primordial Era.</p>
<p>However, after the Primordial Era fell, they became so rare that even I, a Pill Monarch, only have two soul-type pill recipes, and even one of them was the basic taught to all Pill Emperors and above."</p>
<p>Kent furrowed his brow, hearing that. ’Looks like I have something many will kill for.’</p>
<p>Realizing that, his face went pale when he recognized where he was and who had just discovered one of the many secrets he never knew he had.</p>
<p>In his defense, he never knew how amazing the pill recipes he received from the Primordial Pill Records were.</p>
<p>However, now it seemed he had to be more careful, and that started by figuring out whether his "supposed" big sister was greedy or not.</p>
<p>First of all, Kent knew he could not escape, and he most certainly could not face that lady, for she could kill him before he even thought of running away.</p>
<p>He faced a Spirit Lord before, but he knew King Balma would look like a puppy before Pill Monarch Nefeli.</p>
<p>Kent knew he was in deep now, and his mistake was not learning about all the amazing pill recipes he had.</p>
<p>Pill Monarch Nefeli saw the hesitation appear on Kent’s face and smiled, "Don’t overthink, Kent. I have no intention of stealing from you. If anything, I want to advise you instead."</p>
<p>Kent relaxed a little, but he still looked guarded.</p>
<p>"In the future, never reveal that you have any of these pill recipes. Anything related to Souls, Innate Talent, Meridians, and Cores. They are the most closely guarded secrets that cannot be easily revealed.</p>
<p>That said, since you offered, I will take the recipes, but I will only add one in your name since just one is valuable enough to allow your friend to view the Grand Pill Tablet five times.</p>
<p>However, I don’t intend to keep the rest for myself.</p>
<p>I will add the next recipe 10,000 years from today, and the third will be added 100,000 years from when the second was added.</p>
<p>Of course, should I die before then, it will die with me, for I cannot in good conscience add 20 Soul-type pill recipes to the collection of the Pyris Pill Alliance.</p>
<p>Doing so would be calling doom on myself and the entire Pyris Pill Alliance. I hope you understand my good intentions."</p>
<p>Kent nodded. "I guess you do what is good for the alliance and me. In that case, do you want me to write it down or transfer it to you?"</p>
<p>"Transferring will make it much more secure. It will be easy to place seals on said memories and only unlock them when the time intervals to release them reach."</p>
<p>"Then I will transfer it to you."</p>
<p>Kent walked closer and placed his index finger on Pill Monarch Nefeli’s forehead. Using the same method he had used to transfer skills to his undead and legion, he began transferring the recipes to her.</p>
<p>"This is crazy," she muttered as the recipes flowed into her head. When the last one was added, her eyes widened.</p>
<p>"This—"</p>
<p>Kent smiled. "It is something small for you to build your strength, so when it comes to it, you can better protect yourself. That said, if you can find a way to give a few of those pills to Suoh, I will be grateful."</p>
<p>Pill Monarch Nefeli was left stunned, unsure of what to say or how to behave.</p>
<p>Kent just smiled.</p>
<p>He had added the Innate Talent Awakening Pill. Now that he knew how rare soul-type recipes are, he didn’t want to leave her vulnerable.</p>
<p>With the Innate Talent Awakening Pills, if she had the ingredients, she would be able to rise to the level where nobody can kill her, regardless of their ranks, if they are below Divinity.</p>
<p>This would preserve her life, and since Kent had started thinking he was indeed in the past, he wanted to ensure the future didn’t disappoint him.</p>
<p>"I will be sure to treasure it with my life, Kent," Pill Monarch Nefeli said, and Kent nodded. Suddenly, a voice entered his mind.</p>
<p>⟦The master will wake up in 10 seconds⟧</p>
<p>’Good to hear your voice, Tower. Now, do me a favor by capturing an image of what you see now and framing it for me.’</p>
<p>⟦Already ahead of you, master⟧</p>
<p>Kent smiled. He wanted to say something, anything, but something told him he shouldn’t. So he just took in the beautiful looks of the lady before him, and when the timer hit zero, he woke up in the future.</p>
<p>---</p>
<p>[Planet Kryos (Immortal World). Nebulon Mountain. Grand Pyris Pagoda – Last Floor (Present day.)]</p>
<p>A beautiful green-haired, green-eyed, and green-lipped beauty sat in a majestic throne-like chair reading a scroll. Around her, a calm aura—one that would make even devils fall in love with her—filled the room.</p>
<p>And judging by the density of this aura, it was clear she was an immortal, a King Immortal to be precise. Her beauty is unimaginable.</p>
<p>In all of the 100 immortal worlds in the Vornix Immortal Planetary Alliance, she could be counted among the top three beauties.</p>
<p>She was both impressive, intelligent, and terrifyingly powerful.</p>
<p>Suddenly, her vision turned hazy, and then she fell back in her throne, passing out. Two hours later, she woke up feeling fine; however, a name appeared in her mind that made her wonder where it had come from.</p>
<p>"Kent Madson... Who is that?"</p>
<p>---</p>
<p>[Planet Panama (Immortal World)]</p>
<p>Inside a lush garden filled with pill herbs, a handsome elf was attending to his herbs when suddenly, he felt a little lightheaded. Before he could shake it off, he fell and passed out.</p>
<p>Two hours later, he woke up, also fine. However, just like what happened to Pill Immortal Nefeli, a name appeared in his mind, causing him to wonder just who that name belonged to.</p>
<p>"Kent... who is that?"</p>
