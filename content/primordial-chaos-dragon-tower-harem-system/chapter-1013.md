<p>Many people would have loved to show off and be arrogant in this very moment.</p>
<p>However, after the cold opening Kent gave them, they all straightened up.</p>
<p>It was no exaggeration to say Kent had dampened the mood.</p>
<p>However, he was fighting for Karma, and as someone with extremely good Karma, he knew some people were just better off dead.</p>
<p>Pill Monarch Jackson was a scum. From a quick scan with his Enchanted Eyes, Kent saw a list of crimes he had committed. So his death was warranted.</p>
<p>Now, everyone is just hoping the Grim Reaper they all would have looked down on wouldn’t come for their heads next.</p>
<p>As more people started taking the trial, Kent could see a trend.</p>
<p>"Looks like those warriors groomed to become Alchemists aren’t having a good time." A smirk formed on his lips.</p>
<p>The majority of the warriors that the various Pill factions wanted to use to farm Primordial Flames were failing to hit the Golden Soul Barrier.</p>
<p>It wasn’t that they had weak soul strengths. Some even took Soul Boosting Pills before taking the trial, but they still failed to pass.</p>
<p>"This year’s trial is strange," Pill Monarch Nefeli said. She held a curious look in her eyes as she observed the Soul Pillar.</p>
<p>"It is like the trial is specifically singling out the true Alchemist. So far, all who have qualified are true Alchemists. They have love for Alchemy, unlike the others, who only came because they have the power to fight for Primordial Flames."</p>
<p>"Isn’t that obvious? I mean, the Pill Ascension Realm was meant to be a haven for true Alchemists, right?" Kent scoffs. "Maybe it is finally tired of warriors desecrating it, so it changed its selection method, allowing only True Alchemists to enter.</p>
<p>"After all, wouldn’t it be cheating those who can’t fight but actually have a love for Alchemy?"</p>
<p>Pill Monarch Nefeli nodded. "I guess you are right. However, with this change, the chances of things changing this time around are very high.</p>
<p>I mean, look at even the top-ranking Pill Alliance. After 30 people took the trial, only five qualified."</p>
<p>Kent smiled, watching the painful look in the Pill Legend’s eyes.</p>
<p>"It is their loss for being greedy. Now, they will have to face me in there, and trust me, with me in there with them, without my permission, none of them will be getting anything out."</p>
<p>The Pill Monarch could only smile at how petty Kent is. She can tell he really hated the Pill Legends, and because of the hatred for them, he wants to make sure their factions lose heavily.</p>
<p>"Don’t overestimate yourself, Kent. The Pill Ascension Realm is even bigger than this planet, so unless you can be at every place at once, then maybe you should relax a little."</p>
<p>Kent smiled, but he knew what she said was indeed true. However, his pettiness will ultimately be the downfall of many.</p>
