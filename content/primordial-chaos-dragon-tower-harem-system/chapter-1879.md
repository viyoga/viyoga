<p>[Somewhere in the Supreme Undead Heaven]</p>
<p>Kaizo sat atop the skull of a very terrifying-looking Dragon Skull. From the aura around it alone, it was clear that when it was alive, even Celestials would have run from it.</p>
<p>Its size alone was too large to comprehend how much pressure it would have possessed back when it soared through the skies and scorched the earth.</p>
<p>However, it was dead, and Kaizo seemed to have found a liking for its skull, which he had used as his throne.</p>
<p>Standing across the vast skeleton of this dragon were the rest of his teammates and the Green Bone Oracle, who had led them to the Undead Grave, where the treasures left behind by the Bone Lich Undead Emperor were recovered.</p>
<p>It hadn’t been an easy adventure.</p>
<p>Kaizo and his friends faced all kinds of dangers, each far more dreadful than the last. Luckily for them, they had prepared thoroughly for everything, so despite the danger, they finally managed to complete their mission after months of battles.</p>
<p>"This has been eventful at least," Kaizo muttered, looking at the carnage they had left around them.</p>
<p>"We managed to retrieve the treasures. In my view, that was what mattered. But it is also good that we both managed to gain some treasures that will be used to upgrade when we return," Varyn said, and her friends nodded.</p>
<p>They had managed to gain some treasures that, if used well, would push all of them to at least the Supreme stage. That would surely boost their power, allowing them to have their own footing within the Legion.</p>
<p>"We better leave then, since the longer we stay here, the higher the chances that the Undead Monarch will return from wherever he had gone."</p>
<p>Varkhiel’s words were taken to heart, so after resting for an hour, they left, with Kaizo taking the bones of the dragon with him.</p>
<p>A week later, they returned to the Supreme Undead Heaven and booked a tavern, where they started to wait for the Third Undead Princess, who had promised to leave with them.</p>
<p>The last couple of months spent inside the Undead Grave had been spent preparing so that they would be ready when they came out, since there was a high possibility that the Undead Monarch would return before they did.</p>
<p>Luckily, they had the Third Undead Princess on their side, so she made sure they remained hidden from all who might recognize them.</p>
<p>Three days after they returned to the Supreme Undead Heaven, the Third Undead Princess arrived wearing a disguise.</p>
<p>Inasmuch as she would have loved to be seen outside with Kaizo and his friends, she knew her status as a Princess would bring them too much trouble, so she disguised herself to save them the trouble...</p>
<p>"So you have really decided to leave with them, huh?" the Green Bone Oracle asked, leaning against a beam.</p>
<p>The Third Undead Princess smiled and nodded.</p>
<p>"I need to see him and discuss some important issues with him. While there seems to be peace right now in the Undead Realm, there are far darker forces at work, and if we don’t handle these petty conflicts, one day we will all perish at the hands of these darker forces."</p>
<p>Green Bone Oracle didn’t seem too bothered, though. However, he nodded and turned to Kaizo.</p>
<p>"I cannot come with you this time since I have business. But inform Sanders that I will reach out when I’m done with the matters here."</p>
<p>"Master will hear your message."</p>
<p>"Good. Then here you go, the wine you requested. I have been saving some for millions of years, so be sure to inform Sanders I expect something of equal value when we meet."</p>
<p>Kaizo smiled and nodded. "I am sure Master will be ready and fully prepared when you meet."</p>
<p>Their conversation soon ended, and Kaizo and his team were ready to leave...</p>
<p>"I am pretty sure when the Undead Monarch returns and discovers you are not in the Undead Realm, he will be so pissed."</p>
<p>The Green Bone Oracle joked as he watched Kaizo work to open the portal that would take them back to the Infinity Universe.</p>
<p>"All the more reason for me to return before he does."</p>
<p>A few seconds later, a portal opened, and they were ready to leave.</p>
<p>"See you guys later."</p>
<p>Kaizo and his teammates bid their final farewells, and they were soon gone. The Third Undead Princess followed after they had all entered, and then the portal closed behind her.</p>
<p>"I can’t wait for him to return and throw his tantrums."</p>
<p>The Green Bone Oracle allowed a smile to form on his face for a few seconds before it vanished along with his body.</p>
<p>---</p>
<p>[Abyssal Realm]</p>
<p>Meanwhile, somewhere far away, a man with a head of white hair and an aura that would cause even Primordials to tremble in their shoes set down a cup filled with boiling hell wine.</p>
<p>"HMMMM."</p>
<p>He hummed, his expression turning a bit sour.</p>
<p>"So he had returned, huh?"</p>
<p>The expression on his face didn’t change much, but one could tell he wasn’t happy with what had come to mind. Clearly, the Third Undead Princess leaving the Undead Realm hadn’t escaped him.</p>
<p>In fact, he was someone who had always known that the Third Undead Princess and the Bone Lich Undead Emperor shared a past.</p>
<p>He had even tried to use their connection once to get close to Sanders and kill him. However, the Bone Lich Undead Emperor wasn’t someone he could kill easily, resulting in the failure of that plan.</p>
<p>Ever since then, he had kept a close eye on the Third Undead Princess. Now that she had left the Undead Realm, he knew the only place she would go was the Infinity Universe.</p>
<p>’Very well. Since you have returned, then be sure to prepare for what comes next. Once the issue with the Abyssal Chaos is resolved, I will be sure to focus on you.’</p>
<p>He lifted the cup, drank its contents, and set it back down. Kent wasn’t his priority right now, so he could have his few years of peace. Once he was done, he would turn his wrath back on him and make sure he understood what pain truly felt like.</p>
