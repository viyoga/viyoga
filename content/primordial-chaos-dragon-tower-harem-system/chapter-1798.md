<p>"This place is..." Fiannarel asked, looking around the vast forest, teeming with all kinds of laws, Dao, and everything a cultivator needs.</p>
<p>She could sense the unique nature of this place, and so could the seven tribe leaders.</p>
<p>"How is this even possible?" Zdzislav muttered, a shocked expression spreading across his face. "It is like, just by breathing, my comprehension of the Eternal Dao and laws starts to deepen0-."</p>
<p>"Indeed. Is this the source of power that always seems to improve the talent of anyone who takes the trial?" Jyoti Lata asked, and Tower answered.</p>
<p>"Indeed. Master created this cultivation ground a very long time ago. Using his extensive knowledge when it comes to the laws and Dao, he rewrote the very core of the existence of this place. Now, just by breathing, one’s talent improves."</p>
<p>"That... Just how strong would one have to be to rewrite the laws of existence?" Parand was left dumbfounded. She just couldn’t wrap her mind around the fact that just breathing here was all it took to improve one’s cultivation talent.</p>
<p>Tower smiled, shaking her head, almost as if what the eight Eternals were experiencing was merely an ordinary Tuesday for her.</p>
<p>She had been with the Chaos Ascension King for trillions of years and had witnessed all kinds of mind-blowing things, so their shock wasn’t something new to her. She had been shocked before, and more importantly, she had had her fair share of shocking encounters while following Kent.</p>
<p>So she was no longer even going to pretend to be shocked. To her, whatever Kent did from here onwards wasn’t new. Even if he did something so mind-bending, she would just see it as one of the many mind-blowing things he would keep doing as long as he lived.</p>
<p>"It would probably be good if you all could keep an open mind. After all, when it comes to Master, only seven others can measure up, and they each have their fair share of mind-blowing creations and encounters.</p>
<p>You would have a much better time around him by keeping an open mind than letting every small thing break your mind. Trust me, I have witnessed him do one mind-blowing thing after another, like it was a normal morning."</p>
<p>They nodded, but taking her words to heart and putting them into practice would take some time. To them, this was just too mind-blowing to even consider.</p>
<p>Kent, who had been hard at work for a couple of hours, finally let a smile form on his face as the bloodline assimilation finally ended.</p>
<p>&lt;Bloodline Assimilation Complete.&gt;</p>
<p>&lt;Congratulations, your Primordial Chaos Dragon Bloodline has been upgraded from Celestial+ ↠ Primordial&gt;</p>
<p>&lt;You have awakened a Bloodline Ability: Blood Warrior&gt;</p>
<p>[Blood Warrior]</p>
<p>[Rank: Innate]</p>
<p>[Description: The Life Blood of the worthy can transform the ordinary into the extraordinary, and the extraordinary into an existence beyond comprehension. You are worthy to awaken the long-lost Blood Warrior lineage.]</p>
<p>[Blood Warriors: 0/7]</p>
<p>"Looks like I still have a lot to do before my bloodline can move from 45% to 50%." Kent had expected such a breakthrough, but now it appeared he would have to wait and use blood essence rather than temper it.</p>
<p>"But this is good. At least I can now use more of my bloodline in combat." He let a smile form on his face.</p>
<p>Finally, he could leave this place and put an end to the tyranny of the Dark Empress. "I do hope the people backing her make themselves known so I can put them down for good."</p>
<p>He stood up and walked toward a pavilion where Tower and the eight people he had asked her to bring were seated.</p>
<p>When they saw him approach, they all jumped to their feet, and Fiannarel ran into Kent’s arms. He may be the Chaos Ascension King to the natives of the Chaos Chasm, but to her, he was her baby brother.</p>
<p>Kent smiled, seeing how spoiled she was acting. "Big sister, you do know you are too old to be acting like this..."</p>
<p>She smirked but didn’t let go of him. Kent just shook his head, and after she pulled back, he moved to the pavilion, holding her hand.</p>
<p>"We greet the Chaos Ascension King," the seven tribe leaders said as they went down on their knees and bowed.</p>
<p>"You guys don’t have to do that. I may be the Chaos Ascension King, but now, I am merely a Chaos Dragon with Demigod-level strength," Kent said, taking a seat.</p>
<p>The seven tribe leaders lifted their heads and looked back at him. "Even though you are only a Demigod at the moment, we still see you as the Chaos Ascension King," Parand said, making a determined fist.</p>
<p>To them, the Chaos Ascension King was the law, and they had all grown up learning to respect and admire him. So, Demigod or not, Kent was their ruler, and giving him the respect he deserved was only right.</p>
<p>"Regardless, you all have to sit down first before I can reveal why I summoned you here."</p>
<p>They nodded, bowed, and stood up, each taking their seat. Fiannarel had already seated herself beside Kent, looking very happy. Having Kent back was all she had ever wanted.</p>
<p>"I never expected to be the one you all keep talking about. However, now that I have regained my identity, I would first like to point out that I can indeed free you all. However, there is a catch..."</p>
<p>They all smiled. "Whatever the catch may be, as long as we can gain our freedom, we are willing to pay," Tumenk stated, and the rest gave a supportive nod.</p>
<p>"Tumenk is right. We are willing to pay any price for our freedom," Jyoti Lata said.</p>
<p>Kent just smiled and said,</p>
<p>"There is no price to be paid. The catch is that my universe is still too weak, so allowing you guys in is impossible. It will break the rules and allow dangers far beyond what even you guys can handle to come in.</p>
<p>That said, I have a pocket dimension where you all can stay and interact with the people I have already migrated there. Also, I am not entirely sure, but I believe that when I leave here, the pocket dimension will evolve and merge with this world, allowing me to transport the Chaos Chasm there while keeping your homes intact.</p>
<p>I also have a feeling you guys have a much bigger mission that will reveal itself when I get back to my universe. So there will be more exploration to do, and I do hope it will be enough to satisfy your expectations."</p>
<p>Kent could tell by the looks on their faces that they were more than satisfied. He could even tell that if he asked them to remain here for a few more million years while he worked to make the Infinity Universe ready for them to roam, they wouldn’t complain.</p>
<p>That shows how much trust they have in him.</p>
<p>"We are okay with everything you have planned, Lord Kent," Namarie said with a nod.</p>
<p>Kent smiled and nodded back. "That aside, I have another reason for asking for you seven to be brought here. You see..."</p>
