<p>[Planet Zakani]</p>
<p>Planet Zakani is one of the seven Supreme Worlds owned by the Midnight Shadows. It is one of the most protected Supreme Worlds in the universe, primarily because of who lives on it.</p>
<p>Many years ago, long before the Faceless Assassin witnessed his battle with the Mountain Wardog and later recruited him to become the first Midnight Shadow, he was known as Titus Elorm.</p>
<p>Now, he is known as Crimson Dagger.</p>
<p>He is a Shadow Supreme who is not far from becoming a Shadow Celestial. Five million years ago, he killed 10 Celestials in one night when a Calamity Gate opened all of a sudden and Calamity Beasts poured out.</p>
<p>Since that night, nobody who values their life has dared to mess with him. Not even the Soul Temple dares step on his bad side.</p>
<p>So with him guarding a planet, nobody with enough brain cells will dare break in.</p>
<p>However, Pill Celestial Vaelith Moonshade entered such a planet as if she owned it. In fact, when she arrived, nobody aside from Crimson Dagger noticed her presence.</p>
<p>"You do know it is impolite to break into someone’s home like you own the place," Crimson Dagger spoke, his body materializing from a shadow nearby.</p>
<p>Pill Celestial Vaelith Moonshade smirked and sat on a chair under a tree like she was mundane. In natural senses, she is someone who sits at the peak of the universe, yet here she sat looking normal.</p>
<p>Crimson Dagger shook his head and sat in the opposite chair. He looks human, but with deep-set eyes, slightly long ears, and short, dark hair.</p>
<p>"What do you want, Vaelith? And before you start talking too much, know I am on my way somewhere before you came."</p>
<p>Vaelith smirked. "What’s with the attitude, Titus? Can’t you give a small part of your time to a lady in need?"</p>
<p>"You are a Celestial, Vaelith. You are basically beyond my help and attention, so why don’t you cut to the chase and tell me why you are here?"</p>
<p>"Honestly, when that Faceless Guy recruited you, didn’t he teach you how to show compassion?" Vaelith gave another smirk before she got down to business.</p>
<p>"Have you heard of the name Kent Madson?"</p>
<p>Titus furrowed his brow. "Can’t say I haven’t heard of him before. After all, everyone is after him, the last I checked." He paused and gave Vaelith a suspicious look. "Don’t tell me you also want to look for that kid?"</p>
<p>"As a matter of fact, I want him found. After all, his name had just appeared on the Pill Dao Tablet."</p>
<p>"Again?" Titus was left dumbfounded. The last he heard about Kent was when his name appeared on the Ice Dao Tablet. And that was just a few months ago.</p>
<p>How then did he manage to register his name on yet another Dao Tablet?</p>
<p>"Trust me, I was equally shocked when I saw his name. However, that is beside the point. He has already become famous, so I’m coming here today to cash in the favour you owe me."</p>
<p>Titus nodded. "Go ahead."</p>
<p>"I want you to find Kent Madson and protect him using the Midnight Shadows. He must be protected till he reaches the Immortal World, and under our protection."</p>
<p>"Hmm, that is a tough ask, Vaelith. The kid I last checked had only been seen a few times, but he vanished before anyone could get close to him. It is like he has his own private world he vanishes into."</p>
<p>"Oh come on, Titus, I know what you and the Midnight Shadows are capable of, so don’t for one thing think I would believe anything you say."</p>
<p>Titus smirked. "Believe what you want, Vaelith. I have already told you what I know; the rest will depend on what you choose to believe."</p>
<p>Vaelith looked at Titus for a few seconds before she sighed, "I know he is in the High Worlds, so keep an eye out for him, and when he finds himself in danger, he can’t wiggle his way out, aid him."</p>
<p>"That I can do. Also, if I find out something more, I will let you know."</p>
<p>Vaelith nodded, and then she stood up. The next second, she turned into a star in the sky and was soon gone from Planet Zakani.</p>
<p>After making sure she was gone, Titus sighed, "What a headache."</p>
<p>He walked back inside and made his way underground, where a green portal, protected by a rather complex array, shimmered.</p>
<p>Standing before the portal was none other than Thousand Blade, the same Shadow Immortal who placed the formation on the Void Forest.</p>
<p>When he left Planet Bayla a while back, he came straight here to report on the Faceless Assassin’s return. Titus was supposed to leave with him back to Planet Bayla, but before they could leave, Vaelith arrived.</p>
<p>Now that she is gone, they can finally leave. If indeed Faceless is back, then he must as well want to see him with his own two eyes...</p>
<p>"Let’s depart."</p>
<p>Titus said, and Thousand Blade nodded. He stepped into the portal, and soon he was gone. Titus also stepped inside, and soon he was gone. A few seconds later, the portal closed.</p>
<p>They first have to travel to the God World, then to the Immortal World, before finally arriving on Planet Bayla.</p>
<p>It would take them about a month to get there since the travel between planets is a bit technical. Also, Titus is a Supreme, so moving that far, even if his intentions aren’t bad, would still need to be done slowly in order not to break the planet they are traveling to.</p>
<p>---</p>
<p>Meanwhile, all over the universe, every last Alchemist who had heard what Kent had achieved started scrambling yet again to find him.</p>
<p>It is a known fact that the universe hangs by a thread and that only the best would have a shot at surviving when it all comes crashing down.</p>
<p>Kent had proven to be among the best when his name appeared on the Sword Dao Tablet and the Ice Dao Tablet. Those were acceptable parameters.</p>
<p>Many even said he must be an Ice Human to gain the recognition of the Ice Dao. However, when his name appeared on the Pill Dao Tablet, the narrative changed.</p>
<p>Some say he has an ancient master whose knowledge in Alchemy is unmatched. In a way, they were right; Kent does have an ancient master. It is just that his master happened to be himself from the past.</p>
<p>But even if he has a master’s, the recognition came from his own efforts.</p>
<p>Some also say he had inherited the legacy of an ancient master. Yet again, they are right. Kent indeed has memories from his past life, which, in a way, have given him an edge over many.</p>
<p>But yet again, he gained this recognition through his own effort. He took advantage of what he had and, using his own thinking, made it something that caught the attention of the Pill Dao.</p>
<p>But people don’t know that, so they all want to find Kent and make sure he shares what he knows. Yes, people are that shameless. They behave as if everything should be theirs.</p>
<p>But the only downside was that they had no idea where Kent was. Nobody does, and so all their intentions for now will remain in their heads until they find him.</p>
<p>Of course, they would learn the hard way once they find him and decide to show their true colors.</p>
