<p>Long before the Eternal War of the Void, which decimated many worlds and shattered many stars, there was a city called Drakenshade.</p>
<p>It was a militarized fortress known for producing elite warriors. This city was the hub of what many called the Warrior Ascension Gate.</p>
<p>It was given that name simply because all who walked through its gates emerged as warriors capable of facing the greatest obstacles.</p>
<p>It was the city of warriors.</p>
<p>Her name was Vael.</p>
<p>She was the daughter of a dignified knight, Freya, and an accomplished demon hunter, Erik.</p>
<p>Vael was just like her parents—dedicated and disciplined. Under the guidance of her parents, she trained from a young age. By the time she turned 15, she was crowned one of the best spear maidens in Drakenshade.</p>
<p>Many praised her; many sought to bring her into their families as a future bride for their heirs.</p>
<p>However, despite her talent, she always felt different. She could see things others couldn’t—distortions in space, flickers of darkness in the air, and shadows that seemed to whisper her name.</p>
<p>This strangeness plagued her for years until she turned 20.</p>
<p>One fateful night, during a monster subjugation mission, Vael and her squad were sent to hunt a rare, abyss-born creature—a Void Wraith.</p>
<p>These creatures were considered remnants of a long-dead race, existing only in legend.</p>
<p>However, something awakened within her as soon as Vael gazed upon the Wraith with her Void Eyes.</p>
<p>It was as if everything in that moment made sense when her gaze fell on the creature.</p>
<p>Instead of attacking, the Wraith bowed before her. Then, with a whisper only she could hear, it said:</p>
<p>"The last has returned. The Hollow Star watches."</p>
<p>Before she could react, black tendrils shot out from the wraith and branded her body with Abyssal Marks, a sign of her Voidling heritage awakening.</p>
<p>But this wasn’t an awakening that was welcomed. Everyone rejected her When she started becoming who she was meant to be. Everyone, except her father, tried to kill her.</p>
<p>Those branded by the Mark of the Abyss are deemed tainted, and as such, they must be subjugated before they become uncontrollable.</p>
<p>But with her father sacrificing his life, she managed to escape.</p>
<p>Years later, when the creatures of the Void came, and the humans and other civilizations were helpless, Dar’Vhalar, the Abyssborne, appeared, riding on a Void Hound.</p>
<p>Following her and obeying her commands were the Voidlings, who, while hated by all, were actually the guardians meant to protect the universe from the creatures of the Void.</p>
<p>History has it that for 26 days, the Voidlings fought down to the last man. And when everything was in dire straits, Dar’Vhalar, the Void Reaver, made the ultimate sacrifice, becoming the last Voidling to lay down her life so the universe could live on.</p>
<p>Her name was lost over the years, but her legend still remains. And now, she is awakened again.</p>
<p>Kent looked up in the air, watching as a figure who once shook the battlefields, led the Voidlings in battle, and sacrificed her life alongside her brothers and sisters so life could continue was being reborn.</p>
<p>"Her name was lost, but her legend remains. They called her the Tainted, the Reject, the Abomination.</p>
<p>Yet, when the Hollows called, she answered and became who she was meant to be. All spoke her name, and her legend became an inspiration to all.</p>
<p>The echoes of the Void sang her glory and foretold that she would awaken again. They promised she would return to grace the battlefield once more.</p>
<p>Her name was Dar’Vhalar, and her name would be Dar’Vhalar again. Rise, daughter of a hunter, for your destiny is about to begin.</p>
<p>Rise, Dar’Vhalar, the Abyssborne Huntress."</p>
<p>"Come to the Hollow Star, child of the abyss."</p>
<p>The heavens shook as a brown-skinned lady with dark-shaded eyes, who needed no introduction to know she was a huntress, raised her hands and snapped her fingers.</p>
<p>The void cracked, and from within its maw, a black spear shot from the void, landing in her firm grip. The void closed the next second, leaving only the beauty hovering in the air.</p>
<p>She has short, dark hair. Her body is covered in black leather armour, which does a good job of accentuating her features.</p>
<p>She bears the mark of the void on her forehead, showing the Void has accepted her as its own.</p>
<p>However, beyond her appearance, she exudes the aura of a warrior. The kind of presence only those who have killed thousands, or perhaps millions, could exude.</p>
<p>Standing with the ladies, Kent could tell every last one of them was scared.</p>
<p>And they should be, for the true name their sister has awakened was one that once belonged to a huntress, a real predator who has killed many.</p>
<p>She traversed the void to hunt down abominations, slaughtering countless of them. Such a person will have a presence that makes anyone fear her.</p>
