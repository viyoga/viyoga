<p>[6 Hours Later]</p>
<p>From the moment the Tower brought out the green-colored Dao Stone, Kent’s cultivation had started its ascension to the Root Immortal stage.</p>
<p>By then, his soul sea had expanded to 5 million km in diameter, and aside from the mountains and other natural constructs, red and black clouds began to form, showing that the soul sea itself is evolving.</p>
<p>Even so, it never stopped expanding.</p>
<p>Thirty minutes into the Dao energy absorption process, a wave of terrifying aura burst from his body, spreading all over Skull City and extending to the other cities, sweeping across the entire low level of the Star Realm.</p>
<p>All who felt it trembled, their backs breaking into cold sweats.</p>
<p>The energies contained within that aura left them all trembling like they were bugs. However, things didn’t slow down at all. Even after he stepped into the Root Immortal stage, his aura continued to rise.</p>
<p>In less than ten minutes, he moved from Low-Level Root Immortal to Peak-Level Root Immortal. Most importantly, when he reached the Peak-Level Root Immortal stage, a strand of Immortal Qi formed.</p>
<p>From there, his aura only continues to surge. In no time, he entered the Spirit Immortal stage and quickly rose through the ranks until he reached the Peak-Level Spirit Immortal stage.</p>
<p>By then, his soul sea had reached 35 million km wide.</p>
<p>It must be known that in the vast universe, God Ascension-stage cultivators are meant to have a minimum soul sea width of 30 million km. So, for a mere Spirit Immortal to have 35 million, one can only wonder the kind of monster he is.</p>
<p>If put into logical measurement, he soon transcended the rank of a monster when, by the time his realm reached the Peak-Level Spirit Immortal stage, it had expanded to 50 million km.</p>
<p>When he finally stepped into the rank of a Core Immortal, his soul sea reached 100 million km wide.</p>
<p>Naturally, this put him at the same level as an average True God. But Kent is no ordinary person. He had accumulated so much blood qi that even after becoming a Core Immortal, he still had enough energy to push him further.</p>
<p>By then, five hours had passed.</p>
<p>Soon, he managed to push past the Low Level to the Mid Level Core Immortal. But that was where his blood essence started to run out. Even so, before it ran out, it managed to push him to the High-Level stage.</p>
<p>By then, his soul sea had reached 300 million km wide, and his Primal Blood Platform had reached 50 million km wide. Shockingly enough, the two Blood Gates had grown so tall and vast that, inside them, the cores looked like giant suns.</p>
<p>The blood core hung loose, primal blood qi oozing from it. Within it, a feeling of dread could be felt.</p>
<p>Before stepping into the rank of an Immortal, his strength was a bit tamed. Now, it felt like a dam had been opened, waves of terrifying energy exploding out of his body.</p>
<p>If an Immortal Lord were present now, they would not be able to look Kent in the eye. Everything about him had become so powerful that even at the High-Level Core Immortal stage, he could contend with the best Immortal Lords.</p>
<p>However, while things calmed down a bit, just when the six hours were about to end, a small green core slowly rose from Kent’s chest.</p>
<p>The moment the green energy appeared, a smile appeared on the Tower’s face.</p>
<p>"Master had succeeded,"</p>
<p>Just as she said that, a calm yet powerful healing aura exploded from Kent’s body. In an instant, his energy rose, and his high-level ranking moved to the Peak, and before long, he broke through to the True Immortal stage.</p>
<p>Inside his soul sea, Kent opened his eyes, a wave of life energy pouring out of his body. Just as he did, three tomes and seven red fruits appeared before him.</p>
<p>He ignored them all and gazed at a mountain standing 40 million km away. With a single step, he appeared on top of it and gazed down, his eyes landing on a small green sprout.</p>
<p>"No way." Kent covered his mouth with his hand, shock appearing on his face.</p>
<p>As he hovered in the air, the sprout of a tree rapidly started to grow. In a few seconds, it rose a few inches. Soon, it rose two feet and continued to grow.</p>
<p>Kent stood there, his gaze filled with shock as the tree rose. In less than 30 minutes, it bloomed into a green, lush tree. Kent remained in place, every passing second adding to his shock.</p>
<p>Soon, however, he could no longer contain his shock and let out a deep sigh.</p>
<p>By then, he felt his cultivation had broken through to the High-Level True Immortal stage and finally halted. He didn’t care much about that. Instead, he stood there for several minutes before calling out to Tower, who appeared instantly in the air before him.</p>
<p>"Congratulations, Master... Huh," she paused and stared intently at the tree growing visibly before her eyes.</p>
<p>"Oh, so you also seem to be taken aback, huh?" Kent asked, his mind spinning as he tried to make sense of what was happening before him. A lot had already happened; however, none of it mattered at the moment.</p>
<p>The tree growing before them had taken the spotlight.</p>
<p>"Master, I want you to tell me everything you saw when you touched the green energy. Don’t omit even the smallest detail."</p>
<p>Kent nodded and began narrating everything from his arrival on the green planet to walking nonstop for 600 years, meeting Lady Kenzie, learning about her Dao, how she died, the request and the offer, and finally spending thousands of years comprehending her Dao.</p>
<p>"Hold on a minute, Master, did you just say you had comprehended 5% of the Life Dao?"</p>
<p>Kent smiled and nodded. "It was much more complex than I expected, but I did."</p>
<p>"Unbelievable. This is just too crazy even for someone as monstrous as you, Master."</p>
<p>"Why did you say that?" Kent asked, a smile forming on his face.</p>
<p>"In comprehending a Dao, one can compare it to comprehending the laws. Only that, when it comes to comprehending the Dao, you would have to traverse three levels every time you make progress.</p>
<p>For example, if you attain weak comprehension of the Life Dao, before you can move to the medium stage, you must first attain Tier 1 Weak Comprehension, Tier 2, and Tier 3.</p>
<p>In the grand Dao, comprehending 5% of the Dao is akin to Tier 1 High-Level Comprehension."</p>
<p>"Is that so?" Kent grinned. "I guess I am a monster then."</p>
<p>"Oh, trust me, you are the greatest monster. But let’s not focus on that. What we should be concerned about now is the tree." The Tower pointed at the tree that now stood two meters tall.</p>
<p>"I have no idea what this tree is, Tower. Just that I can sense an overwhelming might of the Life Dao coming from it."</p>
<p>"That is because the tree was born from a Dao Seed. I have no idea how you managed to transport a Dao Seed into your soul sea, Master. But what you have to know is this: soon, this tree will bloom, and there is a chance it will gain sentience.</p>
<p>Once it does, it will try to take over your soul. I would advise the Master to be ready by then to go into combat mode, for I am afraid the greatest battle you will ever fight at this stage is slowly growing inside your body."</p>
<p>Kent sucked in a cold breath.</p>
