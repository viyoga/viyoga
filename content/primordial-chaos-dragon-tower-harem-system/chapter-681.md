<p>He and the ladies alongside his legion could have used that time to enter the dungeon and clear every last floor within months. However, a message was waiting for him when he came out of his second draconic enlightenment.</p>
<p>The message read...</p>
<p>⟦Quest Message: An unknown anomaly has occurred, which triggered the Dusk Dungeon to enter an upgrade process. Time till completion: 12,418 days⟧</p>
<p>That was the message, if Kent recalls correctly. The time was estimated based on the tower time, so Kent knew he still had some time.</p>
<p>He doesn’t know what triggered the anomaly, but he knows there is nothing good about anomalies. He is an anomaly, and so far, he has seen how wicked he can be to himself.</p>
<p>That alone pisses him off.</p>
<p>But now, this dungeon has moved from being SSS to a Unique dungeon where even gods can be waiting for them in there.</p>
<p>After all, it is a unique dungeon, and as such, the limit that is placed on other dungeons has been lifted. Kent didn’t know if this was a blessing in disguise or if he was going to submerge himself, his wives, and his legion in a literal hell.</p>
<p>But one thing is sure: he is fucked either way. A unique dungeon isn’t something to be taken lightly... They are rare, and Kent had just found himself in one...</p>
