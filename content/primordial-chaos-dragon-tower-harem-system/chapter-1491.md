<p>After spending a few hours on Planet Brymrr, Vexthra and Miryssa returned to the 10th floor of the planet’s Killer Dungeon.</p>
<p>They said nothing of what they went out to do, aside from informing them that Lene will not be joining them anymore—at least for the duration of Kent’s mission on Planet Brymrr.</p>
<p>After that, they prepared to leave for the 11th floor. They still have 27 floors to go, and thus far, they have already started meeting True Immortals.</p>
<p>Vexthra knew it was only a matter of time before demigods and gods started popping up.</p>
<p>So she wants them to train much faster and, hopefully, when the danger starts appearing, even with their low cultivation base, they can handle it. Already, most of them had long surpassed the strength Heavenly Ascension Stage cultivators should have.</p>
<p>The limits they are breaking and the innate talents they keep awakening seem to keep pushing them to grow stronger than they should be.</p>
<p>If this streak continues for another five floors, then there will be no telling the kind of monsters they would become.</p>
<p>"Alright, since we are all ready, we should move to the next floor." She opened the portal, and they all walked in.</p>
<p>As usual, Vexthra was the last to enter before the portal closed. When she appeared on the 11th floor, she found herself standing with three others: Kaizo, Sylara, and Indie.</p>
<p>"Where are the others?" Kaizo asked, looking around. The others mirrored his action, except Vexthra, who held a calm expression, though deep within, she wondered why she had been suppressed again.</p>
<p>’A True Immortal again, huh?’ A smile appeared on her face even though she knew death in this form was possible. Although at heart she is still a god, now that she has been suppressed to the rank of a True Immortal, if she is dealt a deadly blow, she would die.</p>
<p>But instead of feeling fear, she felt more alive. The fact that she can contribute like her sisters could make her happy.</p>
<p>"Looks like we have been divided into teams for different missions." The moment she said that, the details of the mission appeared, and it was shared with everyone.</p>
<p>⟦Name: Planet Killer Dungeon⟧</p>
<p>⟦Floor Name: Four Fallen Immortals (11th)⟧</p>
<p>⟦Power Level: True Immortal -- Immortal Lord*⟧</p>
<p>⟦Description:</p>
<p>↠ Long before the climax of the Chaos Wars, a great battle was fought between the heroes of the Celestial Dream Sect and the four Chaos Envoys who later became the four fallen immortals</p>
<p>↠ Before the Chaos Beasts invaded this universe, they sent their envoys, beings born from the primal chaos, wielding absolute control over the true essence of Chaos Qi.</p>
<p>↠ Their mission was to create chaos in this universe, drawing all attention from the real invasion.</p>
<p>↠ The four Chaos envoys found themselves in the Celestial Dream Sect, where for 20 straight hours, a great battle was fought.</p>
<p>↠ In the end, every last being was slaughtered by the four Chaos Envoys. Their power was so great that even when faced with the overwhelming forces of the sect, they emerged victorious.</p>
<p>↠ However, despite winning, their rampage was destined to end at the sect grounds. In the end, the heroes won, but at the cost of everything they had.</p>
<p>↠ The four Chaos envoys were trapped by the Grand 616 Arcane Formation. This formation, powered by the chaos energy flowing through their veins, had held for billions of years, sealing the four Chaos envoys away.</p>
<p>↠ The world lived on since then, and for billions of years, it had a semblance of peace. The sacrifice of the heroes didn’t go to waste in the end.</p>
<p>↠ However, just like every good story, 200 years ago, the unthinkable happened. In pursuit of claiming absolute power, some zealots broke the formation that held the four Chaos Envoys, creating a chain reaction that promises to devour the entire world in a few years if not handled.</p>
<p>↠ Your mission comes in five phases.</p>
<p>1. Kill Draxion – The Undying Commander.</p>
<p>2. Kill Selvara – The Crimson Valkyrie</p>
<p>3. Kill Gorath – The Mountain Breaker</p>
<p>4. Kill Velmora – The Ghost Strategist</p>
<p>5. Kill all 200 Agents of the Soul Temple. For this mission to be considered a success, be sure to prevent any member from sending out any distress signal to the soul temple.</p>
<p>↠ Your team has been divided into five. Each must execute their mission with absolute precision. Failure to get this mission done will result in catastrophic consequences that this world is not prepared for.</p>
<p>Rewards</p>
<p>↠ 120 Billion Tower Points</p>
<p>↠ 1.2 Trillion Soul Essence</p>
<p>↠ Limit Breaker Key (Everybody)</p>
<p>↠ Innate Talent Awakening Pill</p>
<p>↠ Soul Ascension Fruit</p>
<p>↠ Chaos Heart Stone (x4)</p>
<p>Vexthra and her team of three took a deep breath after reading the description of their mission. "Looks like things will be much more perilous than the previous ones," Kaizo said, feeling like this mission would perhaps be the most dangerous one yet.</p>
<p>Of course, he is ever prepared for what is to come. In fact, he is more than happy to get things going.</p>
<p>Indie and Sylara knew the kind of person Kaizo is, so they said nothing to him and instead turned to Vexthra.</p>
<p>Indie asked, "What is the plan, big sister?"</p>
<p>"I tried reaching the others, but it seems there is something blocking the connection. This means we have to finish our mission and hope the others can also handle their own."</p>
<p>They nodded to her words. They also tried to contact their friends, but nothing happened. It was just like what happened on the 9th floor.</p>
<p>"But where do we go from here?" Kaizo asked again. This time, Sylara was the one who answered.</p>
<p>She pointed toward the west. "That way. I can see a large statue standing in front of what appears to be a tall mountain."</p>
<p>Because she had drawn a blood tattoo meant for enhanced perception, she was able to see thousands of miles. Her sight now even surpasses Kent, who could sense and see up to 80,000 km.</p>
<p>With a 500 times boost, she could now see even over 200,000 km wide.</p>
<p>Knowing she has the eye of an eagle, they never doubted her. Instead, they started following her in the direction she pointed.</p>
<p>Half an hour later, they arrived 20,000 km from the statue.</p>
<p>To everyone’s shock, what Sylara initially thought was a statue was actually a person wielding a large broadsword.</p>
<p>He stood 200 meters tall, muscles built to punch like a titan. His skin was made of stone, with most of it appearing metallic. They knew the monster standing before them, though only at the peak of the True Immortal stage, was no simple fiend.</p>
<p>Even Vexthra panicked, because she knew the titan of a man standing and looking directly at them was bad news.</p>
<p>"That is one large bastard," Kaizo, who prided himself on being a brute, knew he was nothing compared to this monster of a man.</p>
<p>"Kaizo, how large can you grow after using your transformation innate ability?" Vexthra asked, already formulating a plan in her mind.</p>
<p>"For now, I can grow to 50 meters. However, if I use my bloodline, I can reach 100 meters for 30 minutes before I run out of energy."</p>
<p>"That is good enough. You will be our vanguard. Sylara and Indie will support from the side."</p>
<p>Kaizo nodded and asked, "What will you be doing?"</p>
<p>Vexthra smiled and flicked her hair back—something she learned from Dia (Unity’s sister).</p>
<p>"While this time I can’t sense most of my Immortal Qi, if you three can buy me 25 minutes, I will refine this brute into a golem. I am sure you will love to have a training buddy."</p>
