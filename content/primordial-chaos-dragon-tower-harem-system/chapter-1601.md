<p>As the tribulation churned, Kent remained calm, his hand moving toward his mouth as he tossed one grape after another into it.</p>
<p>He knew the danger coming was not one he could joke around with. However, considering how disappointing his first battle after becoming a True Immortal had been, a part of him felt like this would be a good one.</p>
<p>The tribulation was invoked because of his Dragon Intent. He had only just awakened it, and even at Tier 1, it had invoked a tribulation.</p>
<p>However, while the allure of finally tasting what Dragon Intent sounded like the best thing, Kent understood that the gains would only manifest when he passed the tribulation.</p>
<p>There is also another gain.</p>
<p>Awakening Dragon Intent while also awakening his third element, the lightning element, sounded like something he would be happy about. Not forgetting he would be able to temper his body even more.</p>
<p>[The Master should be ready]</p>
<p>Kent nodded and tossed the last grape into his mouth before sitting down in a lotus posture. The next second, he rose from the ground and flew 40 meters into the air while still in a lotus posture.</p>
<p>Just then, a dark bolt of lightning formed and transformed into a razor-sharp spear. It locked its piercing tip onto Kent.</p>
<p>Kent felt the danger, but he remained calm, his body relaxed as he waited for the spear to lunge at him.</p>
<p>Per the rules, he had to tank three deadly bolts of lightning using his body. He could not use any external defensive abilities.</p>
<p>So summoning his [Chaos Scale Shield] was not allowed. However, the rules said nothing about him taking on the appearance of a demi-dragon.</p>
<p>If he wanted, he could transform into his dragon form, and everything would take care of itself. However, what was a little challenge if it meant becoming stronger?</p>
<p>"What is he trying to do? Do not tell me he plans on taking on the tribulation with nothing but his body," Morveth asked, his eyes widening.</p>
<p>Yggzrael smirked, but deep within those eyes, one could tell he was also scared for Kent, and he had to play it cool. "As expected of my Young Lord. Not even the Heavenly Tribulation can deter him from raising any defense."</p>
<p>Morveth, Baelgor, and Kryzoth looked at him but said nothing. They knew Yggzrael was merely trying to hype himself up to appear nonchalant.</p>
<p>But while they read into his intention, the mere fact that Kent had already shown no sign of using any defense made them even more stunned.</p>
<p>Looking at him just sitting there, doing nothing, made them wonder if Kent had gone mad.</p>
<p>Kent remained seated, his expression calm, as if he were not in any danger as the tribulation churned.</p>
<p>Gazing up at the spear ready to shoot at him, a smile formed on his face.</p>
<p>Just then, the bolt descended in the form of a spear hurled straight at Kent’s chest. Kent’s body released waves of chaos qi as he reinforced his scales, considering he was now in his demi-dragon form.</p>
<p>He made sure they were only sturdier and more refined.</p>
<p>*BOOM*</p>
<p>When the spear landed on his chest, a powerful explosion of lightning erupted, sending dust flying all over. Kent remained seated in the same place.</p>
<p>However, the 13 rope tendrils of fabric attached to his armor like a cloak billowed in the air.</p>
<p>"Tingles."</p>
<p>Kent smiled, reading the message that had appeared before him.</p>
<p>[Your understanding of Chaos Lightning has improved greatly.]</p>
<p>"That is a start." Turning his head up, Kent gazed at the tribulation cloud forming the second attack.</p>
<p>In no time, a large finger formed, crackling with overwhelming lightning. The feeling of dread could be felt from it.</p>
<p>"They are getting serious." Sensing this, Kent tapped further into his chaos qi and circulated it, allowing its potent nature to shield his scales, making them even more powerful.</p>
<p>Just then, the finger moved, and the ground trembled, sending tremors in all directions. Kent felt his brain vibrate as the terrifying pressure from the Lightning Finger closed in on him.</p>
<p>’Damn.’</p>
<p>Kent sensed a foreign intent just a second before the attack landed on his body. This time, he was pushed back. However, he only flew back a meter before his body stabilized.</p>
<p>At once, he felt his connection to the Dragon Intent slowly forming. Aside from that, he could sense his Chaos Lightning awakening deep within him.</p>
<p>Because of his understanding of Ice and Fire, he sensed it. Now, even without the last tribulation attack, he would be able to gradually awaken it over time.</p>
<p>"Ouch, the second attack was a bit painful." Kent looked up, a smile playing on his lips as he gazed at the third tribulation attack forming.</p>
<p>[Your understanding of Chaos Lightning has improved significantly.]</p>
<p>Without any hesitation, he transformed into his dragon form and rose into the air, his giant metallic wings spread open.</p>
<p>He felt the pain after the second attack landed on him, so he was not about to take the last attack for granted.</p>
<p>Naturally, when a giant claw formed, he knew turning into his dragon form was the best decision he had made.</p>
<p>That was because the moment the claw formed, it attacked, and Kent could only watch as it slammed into his back, sending him crashing to the ground.</p>
<p>His body slammed into the ground, five large wounds inflicted by the five claws appearing on his back.</p>
<p>Kent forced his body to stand, a wave of dizziness seeping deep within him. The lightning from the third attack managed to touch even his soul.</p>
<p>However, as he stood there, now back in his demi-dragon form, he could not help but smile. The wounds on his back had already closed and healed. Of course, he still felt the pain, but that would be gone soon.</p>
<p>[Your understanding of Chaos Lightning has improved tremendously.]</p>
<p>Kent skipped this message and focused on the second one while waves of energy burst out from his body, spreading far and wide.</p>
<p>[You have awakened Dragon Intent. Gold Grade: Tier 1.]</p>
<p>[You can now use Dragon Intent however you want, provided you have Chaos Qi and Blood Qi.]</p>
<p>[Because you have awakened Dragon Intent, an innate ability has been unlocked: Intimidate.]</p>
<p>[Intimidate]</p>
<p>[Rank: Innate]</p>
<p>[Ranking: 4 stars]</p>
<p>[Description: As a Monarch, your mere presence is enough to shake the hearts of all lower existences. Intimidate them to do as you wish. Whenever you use Intimidate, they lose 30% of their total combat power.]</p>
<p>Kent just smiled, already happy to have awakened Dragon Intent. The added ability, since it was innate, could also work as a passive, so he knew even if he had enough power, allowing his enemies to cower before him would be fun, at least.</p>
<p>In the end, he had not awakened the Chaos Lightning. However, he had formed a foundation, so he could easily awaken the lightning later.</p>
<p>Kent, however, was not yet interested in that innate skill. His focus, however, is on something else.</p>
<p>"This power..."</p>
<p>He made a small fist, feeling an overwhelming strength course through his body. "This is crazy."</p>
<p>"I feel like with just a pinch of my finger, I can crush even a god." Call it arrogance, but the kind of power Kent was feeling now was not something to scoff at.</p>
<p>In fact, he could tell just by this power that his physical strength surpassed that of even an Early God. The Dragon Intent had awakened, and with it, several terrifying beings locked their sights on him, some even Kent would shiver and run away from.</p>
<p>He had 66 days to stay out of trouble per the instructions Princess Pandora gave him. However, on the fifth day, he had become the center of attention.</p>
