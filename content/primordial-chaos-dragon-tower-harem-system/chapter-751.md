<p>Lord Leviathan’s words hung in the air, thick with tension, as he stared daggers at King Zephyr. The words he had just spoken were not ones to be taken lightly.</p>
<p>The Innate Talent awakening pill is one that a beast, in particular, would not take for granted.</p>
<p>Every beast, whether aquatic, terrestrial, or Aerial, has one thing in common: they all chase after. That thing is innate talent.</p>
<p>Every True Beast strives to awaken at least one innate talent.</p>
<p>The beasts Kent killed in the Flame Region are beasts, all right, but they do not possess the innate intelligence that allows them to adapt, take on human form, and live within society.</p>
<p>They are basically monsters. However, with innate awareness, even a monster can become sociable.</p>
<p>Yet, it extends beyond innate awareness and intelligence. In fact, when the word, innate is mentioned, even gods would have to listen, since it is one thing that anyone, especially beasts, will benefit from.</p>
<p>Innate Talent comes in different forms.</p>
<p>There are innate talents, including Body, Mind, Spirit, Soul, Abilities, Aspects, and many more. Most even say that innate talent is what governs one’s ability to attain perfection.</p>
<p>It comes in different forms. Of course, there are differences when compared to Bloodline talent.</p>
<p>To someone like Kent, who is a dragon, awakening things like Dragon Aura, Dragon Claw, Dragon Breath, and the like are his bloodline talents.</p>
<p>In the case of a swordsman, Sword Aura, Sword Intent, Sword Body, and the like are his sword talents. However, when it comes to innate talents, they are the qualities that make a person unique.</p>
<p>Awakening an Innate Body is akin to sharpening your already-sharpened sword body. Innate Soul would enhance the soul of anyone who awakens it.</p>
<p>Most people believe that two individuals with the same bloodline can possess the same Innate talents. However, that is just not possible. Innate talents are unique to everyone.</p>
<p>Skills awakened from innate talents are unique and otherworldly.</p>
<p>Even if one is dumb, just awakening their Innate Mind will turn them into a once-in-a-lifetime genius.</p>
<p>It is what defines a cultivator, as it is through innate talent that one can comprehend their domains and later form the laws and build their world of law.</p>
<p>Lord Leviathan has already reached his limit. He had long refined his core, broken two limits, attained Sage Body cultivation, reached Tier 14 in soul cultivation, and reformed his foundation ten times.</p>
<p>The current him qualifies to compete with Spirit Emperors and Spirit Sovereigns. In fact, in the Celestial Conclave planetary alliance, he can be counted among the top ten strongest out of 64 worlds in the coalition.</p>
<p>That is how strong he is.</p>
<p>Essentially, he is an apex predator standing at the peak of this world.</p>
<p>But he knew this was not his limit. He knew with the right opportunity, he could rise even more, reaching great heights.</p>
<p>This was why he searched, spent years looking for ways to grow, and while he met failure many times, he managed to find an answer.</p>
<p>Awakening an Innate talent would allow him to raise his strength to a whole new level.</p>
<p>Of course, someone like him is not even meant to be in a medium world. Someone with his talent should be roaming in the high worlds, growing stronger every single day. So why is he still in a medium world...</p>
<p>The answer is simple: as someone who came from a race of predators, rising to a High World would be a waste when his foundation is still not up to standard.</p>
<p>To him, rising to the peak in the Medium World will be a better choice than running to the High World. Of course, that isn’t the only reason.</p>
<p>He is also waiting for something he had been looking forward to for the past 997 years, something that the 38 planetary alliances in the Kred Star system want to witness and participate in.</p>
<p>Most want to experience that before ascending to a high position.</p>
<p>So, he wants to grow stronger so that he can qualify to participate in this enterprise. This means he must obtain one of these Innate Talent awakening pills.</p>
<p>That is the only way for him to have a chance if he wants to secure a slot to participate.</p>
<p>"Do you know who this mysterious alchemist is?" Lord Leviathan asked, but King Zephyr shook his head.</p>
<p>"They didn’t say who this alchemist is. Only that he is talented, perhaps a Pill King or a Pill Sage; however, they announced there will be an auction three weeks from today. I believe they will sell one there."</p>
<p>"That is not good enough. Send men to the surface world. Let them blend into this auction, and when a chance appears, they must grab this pill no matter the consequences."</p>
<p>The order was absolute. There was no denying his words; after all, he meant everything he said. King Zephyr nodded, not daring to say anything. To him, he would bow to Lord Leviathan first before bowing to a god.</p>
<p>"What about Kent Madson?" Queen Nyssa, the leader of the Giant Octopus race, asked.</p>
<p>Of course, at the moment, she is in her human form, but in combat, a giant purple octopus with razor-sharp tentacles would appear, and that is why she is the second strongest of the seven sea leaders.</p>
<p>"Leave him to our geniuses in the Primal Beast Sect. I am sure soon the interplanetary competition will start, and knowing the Supreme Immortal Sect, they would want their core disciples to represent this world.</p>
<p>This means they will participate in the Selection competition. If this Kent is as good as you all paint him to be, then I am sure they will add him to their team.</p>
<p>He will make a good kill for our geniuses. We don’t have to waste time on him. The priority now is the pill or the recipe. I dont care what method you will be using. As long as I get what I want in the end, I don’t care."</p>
<p>Lord Leviathan stayed for a few seconds before vanishing from the dome. The seven leaders remained there for a few more minutes, planning what to do next. After everything was said and done, they also left.</p>
<p>They knew how vital this pill was to their leader, so they would under no circumstances fail to acquire and deliver it.</p>
<p>They couldn’t afford to fail, so they were each preparing for a major undertaking that would bring about significant changes in the world.</p>
<p>The sea race that had remained hidden for years now would soon be making its appearance to the entire world.</p>
<p>Of course, while this was a big deal to the rest of the world, their intentions for making themselves known weren’t noble at all.</p>
