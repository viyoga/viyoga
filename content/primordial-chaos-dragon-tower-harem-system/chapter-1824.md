<p>The Tower has merged back with her other halves, but she is yet to awaken, so for now, Kent planned to send the seven tribe leaders back to the Chaos Chasm to inform their people of the latest developments.</p>
<p>Tower said she would link or merge the Chaos Chasm with the tower, so it would become like another world inside the tower. It might even become the first world to merge with the tower.</p>
<p>However, since that place is for people who cultivate the Essence of Chaos, only those with a Chaos affinity would be allowed to go there.</p>
<p>Kent planned to offer cultivation techniques for cultivating Chaos Essence, especially to his wives and legion. This way, they can benefit from the overpowered nature of the Chaos Chasm.</p>
<p>But the natives of the Chaos Chasm can enter the tower and leave without having any special constitution or affinity.</p>
<p>Basically, Kent is focused on making sure his people have more powerful training companions. With Supremes, Celestials, and even Primordials available, they won’t have any shortage of sparring partners.</p>
<p>"You guys have to go back now and inform your people of the latest developments. Soon, portals will open in the various cities, and they will have the chance to enter the tower.</p>
<p>While it isn’t the vast universe out there, where there are millions of races, they would still be able to interact with people outside their race, which is more than enough for now."</p>
<p>The seven Blood Warriors, who are actually the seven tribe leaders, nodded. They are happy that, finally, they can leave the Chaos Chasm.</p>
<p>"Like I told the others, we will have more battles, perhaps much deadlier than this one, so you should be ready for whenever I come calling."</p>
<p>Kent knew that until he learned the main reason why he had created the Chaos Chasm and the seven races, he could only use the seven Blood Warriors as his personal weapons for the battles he planned on fighting soon.</p>
<p>The others in the Chaos Chasm can’t join, but Paragon told him they were created for a reason, so he hoped to know why he made them soon.</p>
<p>After instructing and thanking them, he sent them back to the Chaos Chasm. After they left, he took a deep breath before turning to the right.</p>
<p>"I suppose you haven’t returned to try and kill me again," Kent said, looking at an empty space. However, a few minutes after he spoke, Phanesha materialized out of thin air, frowning.</p>
<p>"How did you know I was here?" she asked, taking calm steps toward Kent.</p>
<p>"Sneaking up on me is impossible, even if you are the most powerful person in the universe," Kent said with a scoff, unbothered by the fact that he was all alone now with the woman who could easily snuff him out of existence.</p>
<p>"Strongest, you say?" To Kent’s shock, Phanesha smiled. "You really had no idea the kind of life you had lived, huh? To see me as the strongest in the universe clearly shows your ignorance is on another level. But who can blame you? By the standards set by the universe, I am indeed the strongest since I am a Primordial.</p>
<p>But if you think I am the worst thing out there, then you probably have another thing coming. But that isn’t why I am here."</p>
<p>She waved her hand, and the one-page scroll appeared. "Someone said I should give this to you. It can only be deciphered by you, apparently, so here you go," she said, making the scroll float toward Kent.</p>
<p>He caught it and, without scanning it, stored it in his space ring.</p>
<p>Phanesha said nothing about that but instead added what her mother, the Blood Matriarch, had asked her to tell Kent. "One last thing. I was told to tell you the tide is coming, so whatever you are up to, you’d better hurry before you’re too late.</p>
<p>That is all. See you later, or never." She was about to teleport away when Kent asked,</p>
<p>"How do I get in contact with you? You know about our deal."</p>
<p>She narrowed her eyes at Kent, but while the urge to slap him silly came to mind, she reined in her anger and produced a red token. "You can contact me using this."</p>
<p>She threw it at Kent, and before he caught it, she had long vanished. Kent grabbed the token, but to his shock, the force behind the throw sent him flying backward. He had to summon the Heaven-Splitting Jade Void Wings just to brace his fall.</p>
<p>"Bitch... One day, I am going to leave handprints on your ass," Kent cursed, feeling a painful sensation travel through his arm.</p>
<p>Somewhere in the void, Phanesha smirked. "He does have some strength in him, else he would have passed out by now." Regretting that she had missed her only chance to thrash Kent, she vanished deeper into the void.</p>
<p>---</p>
<p>Meanwhile, somewhere in the Celestial Realm on a planet known to belong to the Jian Great Family, chaos and mayhem covered every surface. Pools of blood mixed with fire and smoke permeate the entire place.</p>
<p>It appeared a terrible battle had occurred there, and from the look of things, every last member of the Jian Great Family had been killed. The only people still alive were children, pregnant women, and a few maids and guards who were not of the Jian bloodline.</p>
<p>It appeared whoever had sent the mission to the Blood Empress knew exactly what they wanted, so they made sure the core of the Jian Family was wiped out.</p>
<p>The Blood Temple agents had done a remarkable job cleaning the universe of such filth.</p>
<p>What Kent wanted to do had already been done by the Blood Temple, and it had been done thoroughly. They made sure the chances of the Jian Great Family ever rising back to their former glory were nonexistent.</p>
<p>In fact, given what had happened and the warning issued, those who had survived by chance would forever hide their identities as members of the Jian bloodline.</p>
<p>Colluding with the Dark Empress was just too insidious, and everyone who had a pair of functioning brain cells wouldn’t tolerate seeing a budding Jian Family ever again.</p>
<p>They had become enemies of the universe, and it all happened because of the mistake of a few. But with them used as scapegoats, it had now become clear to everyone that those who dared harbor beings who once tried to destroy this universe would not be tolerated.</p>
<p>The message had been sent out clearly, and the evidence was the river of blood left behind in the Celestial Realm. It had been made clear to everyone, and since it was the Blood Temple that had carried out the massacre, nobody dared question them.</p>
<p>Their reputation was on another level, so no one dared challenge their actions.</p>
<p>Not even the Universe Council dared question them. If only they knew that their hesitation and fear of confronting the Blood Empress was actually a good thing.</p>
<p>If they had come, then the already angry Phanesha, after enduring the teasing from both Kent and Paragon, would have unloaded her frustration on them. Perhaps some of them would have left her planet with broken bones and wounds that would never heal.</p>
<p>In a way, fear sometimes ruled, and these people had escaped a disaster simply because they were too afraid of the Blood Empress.</p>
