<p>Kent was planning to send the Sect Master into the tower, where he would extend his torture until the ladies had recovered and were ready to handle him personally.</p>
<p>But that plan was put on hold when space cracked, and through the Void, Ingrid, Lilian, Neomi, and Unity appeared.</p>
<p>Val, Veydris, Sylara, and Uncle Drew were left behind to handle whatever might have been hidden down there.</p>
<p>Of course, everything was cleared out.</p>
<p>These four never planned to come after the sect master, but thanks to Selene and Elsa monitoring the state of Nara and Zaila, they contacted them and let them go and, in place of Zaila and Nara, inflict more damage on the Sect Master.</p>
<p>However, instead of using the harem connection to move to him, Ingrid wanted to show off her skill. So, like a god, space cracked, and the four ladies appeared; he welcomed them.</p>
<p>Obviously, Kent knew what they were after, so the moment they appeared, he knew there was no stopping them.</p>
<p>However, what happened next was something he had never seen before.</p>
<p>The Sect Master was hurt and broken from all the beatings he had received from Kent and Kaizo. One eye was blinded, both legs were broken, and his left arm was also broken, while his body was littered with holes.</p>
<p>Yet, the moment they appeared and Neomi gave the order, Lilian raised her staff and cast a tier 7 spell that instantly healed the Sect Master.</p>
<p>His legs were healed, his arm was restored, and even his eye was healed.</p>
<p>However, the pain he was in was multiplied, and just from the moaning sound coming from his mouth, Kent could tell he was in pain—a very agonizing pain.</p>
<p>But that pain wasn’t something Neomi cared about.</p>
<p>In her Rakshasa form, she could go on for five hours. However, that form was for battles, since she is a war child. So, instead of tainting her image as a warrior, she changed from Rakshasa to Shara, the killer.</p>
<p>Her armour shifted to dark red, her spear now exuded red blood energy, and the look in her eyes caused the Sect Master to shudder.</p>
<p>Lilian was in her Ice elemental form, but the moment the healing spell took effect, she changed into her Fire elemental form.</p>
<p>Kent warned her not to take her dragon form or demi-dragon form, as he knew he was being watched.</p>
<p>The tower had informed him Lady Vari, or as one would call her, Protector Vari, was around and watching everything.</p>
<p>As for Ingrid, she was in her hybrid form, where she was both a Voidling and a human. Like Lilian, Kent told her not to take out her Void Hound.</p>
<p>That will be exposing too much.</p>
<p>However, that was enough to handle a scum such as the Sect Master.</p>
<p>Unity just stood on the side, calmly watching her three overpowered sisters handle the Sect Master.</p>
<p>"You have made some people very sad due to your hunger for power. This is why you will suffer before dying. And don’t think about killing yourself. In my presence, death only listens to me," Neomi said, and even Kent felt the chill.</p>
<p>But that was one of her abilities as a child of death. In the presence of Shara, one cannot kill themselves. They won’t have the will to kill themselves, or she is the one meant to do the killing.</p>
<p>Then it began.</p>
<p>She moved and swung her spear, forcing the Sect Master to raise his staff, but before he could defend, a spear shot from out of nowhere and grazed his side, cutting a deep wound.</p>
<p>Ingrid was far from Neomi, but her attack connected, thanks to the Void. She sent the attack through the void.</p>
<p>"AAARRRGGHHH!"</p>
<p>The Sect Master screamed as his left eye was engulfed in flames. Lilian, who was also standing back, pointed her staff at him, and as the red gem in her staff began to glow, the pain became more intense.</p>
<p>Kent sighed, and Kaizo shook his head.</p>
<p>He and Kent had their fair share of torturing the Sect Master, but he never expected these beauties to be this aggressive.</p>
<p>"Master, perhaps we should learn from the one with the red eyes. I can tell all her cuts are shallow, but there’s just something about them that makes my spine tingle," Kaizo said, making Kent nod in response.</p>
<p>"I was thinking the same. All her cuts are in places where even the smallest cut will be agonizing." The Sect Master was probably asking for him and Kaizo back, for Neomi and her sisters were doing far more torturing than he and Kaizo had done.</p>
<p>Within five minutes, the Sect Master was riddled with cuts, and his body was bathed in blood from all the wounds inflicted on him.</p>
<p>Unity was just standing on one side, watching her sisters with a smile.</p>
<p>"You ladies should wrap it up," Kent said, and Neomi turned to look at him.</p>
<p>"Should we kill him now or bring him back for when the ladies wake up?" Selene had already told them Nara and Zaila were resting, so she wanted to find out if they should be the ones to kill the evil man.</p>
<p>"Just finish him. The trauma they went through is not something they can recover from easily, and I feel like telling them to kill this man themselves will bring back some unpleasant memories. So, kill him," Kent sighed.</p>
<p>His first plan was to let the two ladies kill the Sect Master, as Alina did with Turyn, but after thinking it over, he didn’t want them to go through that.</p>
<p>He had asked Nara before how she wanted the Sect Master to die, and she said as long as his remains were burnt to ashes, she would be happy.</p>
<p>His existence should be erased everywhere, and his name should not be spoken again. She wanted that, and he was sure Zaila wanted the same thing.</p>
<p>So, it would be best if Kent handled this instead of allowing them to do it.</p>
<p>"Just do it in your own way," Kent added, and that was all the ladies needed to hear.</p>
<p>"Void Cut," Neomi muttered as she swung her spear. Instantly, another scream filled with pain escaped the Sect Master’s mouth as his left arm was cut off.</p>
<p>"Disintegrate," Lilian followed, and while Ingrid cut off his hand, Lilian’s spell engulfed both his eyes in flames. Like frying an egg, they were fried and disintegrated from his face.</p>
<p>"Swing of a Thousand Cuts," Neomi continued. With a single swing of her spear, a thousand cuts, each cutting deep into his body, landed on the Sect Master, turning his scream into a hoarse cry.</p>
<p>"Haunted Arrow," Unity, who had been calmly watching her sisters, chose to let the Sect Master feel her pain as well. She unleashed an attack, and while Kent knew her to be lovely, her arrow wasn’t.</p>
<p>It went through the Sect Master’s mouth, cutting off his scream. It was, as they say, very haunting.</p>
<p>"Ingrid, finish him."</p>
<p>"With pleasure."</p>
<p>Ingrid’s eyes turned darker, and with a gentle step, the Void cracked again. This time, it cracked underneath the Sect Master, pulling him in.</p>
<p>She then snapped her fingers, and it closed. Just like that, both the body and soul of the Sect Master would forever be trapped inside the Void for all eternity.</p>
