<p>Just as Kent had predicted, on almost every cultivation planet in the universe, there was chaos and mayhem everywhere.</p>
<p>The sudden broadcast by the blood messengers, while some took it as a prank, inadvertently opened the door for most people to do what they thought was right.</p>
<p>After all, there was a chance the universe would be coming to an end, so why not just let it burn to the ground now and be done with it?</p>
<p>Safe to say, in less than 5 minutes, billions had died.</p>
<p>In the grand scheme of things, that number was a small number compared to the total number of living beings in the universe.</p>
<p>Of course, some planets had fewer than half a billion living souls, so, in comparison to the lives lost, it could be treated as alarming.</p>
<p>As many were concerned, the Blood War never happened; after all, they had never heard of it until today.</p>
<p>They had never heard of the Nine Savage Heavens, they had never heard of the Blood Spear King, and they most certainly never knew this universe had won the first war against the Blood Race.</p>
<p>So, since something like that had happened and now seemed to have come back to bite them in the ass, they might as well do with their lives as they pleased.</p>
<p>Of course, there were those who also wished for order, so they were doing their best to handle what had happened, and they most certainly would not want this delicate balance to slip out of their control.</p>
<p>But while the rest of the universe was in chaos, the universe’s powerhouses began meeting to discuss the core issue, which revolved around one person—the Blood Spear King.</p>
<p>Of course, every meeting had its own agenda, but it all revolved around one person, and he was none other than the Blood Spear King.</p>
<p>***</p>
<p>[The Universe Council]</p>
<p>There is a planet in the universe called Prime Eternal Garden. It is perhaps the most beautiful planet in the universe. It has everything, from nature to the laws and even the Dao.</p>
<p>A very long time ago, when the universe picked the first Celestial Supreme, this planet was given to him, and ever since then, it has served as the home of every Celestial Leader.</p>
<p>Over the years, as one Celestial Supreme came and went, the Prime Eternal Garden kept getting developed. It is the turn of the 14th Celestial Supreme, and he has also done his part to ensure Prime Eternal Garden is at its all-time best.</p>
<p>Most of the time, he and a few others live there.</p>
<p>However, today, after the broadcast, he issued an order that hadn’t been issued in millions of years, and in less than 10 minutes, the 13 member states of the Universe Council had gathered in the Prime Eternal Garden to discuss what had happened and how to address it.</p>
<p>Han Tan, the 14th Celestial Supreme, sat in the head chair, looking at the 13 Celestial Elders, each belonging to one of the 13 Prime Races in the universe.</p>
<p>While there are millions, perhaps billions, of races in the universe, there are 13 Prime Races that, when looked at from a certain perspective, are considered the true races of the universe.</p>
<p>It sounded a bit racist, but the Universe Council picked these 13 races to govern the affairs of the universe. Which is why, instead of a Flame Dragon Celestial Elder, there was an Ancient Dragon Celestial Elder.</p>
<p>The Ancient Dragons are said to be the first-ever dragons created, so they represent all dragons.</p>
<p>Also, the High Elf Empress came rather than the Elf Empress or Half-Elf Emperor. In the end, the first 13 races said to have been created first were the ones that made up the Universe Council.</p>
<p>For all 13 of them to meet in the same room, it was clear they meant business, so they all turned and looked at the Celestial Supreme, who took a deep breath and spoke...</p>
<p>"I know I don’t have to say much since we all heard the broadcast and felt the impact."</p>
<p>They all nodded, recalling the chill they felt when the Blood Messenger spoke those words. To mere mortals, those words, while they sounded foreboding, were just that.</p>
<p>It wasn’t as grim as one would think.</p>
<p>However, to Supremes and Celestials, especially Pseudo-Primordials and Primordials, they were the most bone-chilling words they had ever heard, making them understand that the challenge wasn’t a farce.</p>
<p>"How do you all think we should handle this?"</p>
<p>The Dragon Celestial Elder, who goes by the name Mythos, spoke, "Celestial Supreme, from where we sit now, I think we all know who we have to search for. The messengers clearly said the challenge must be signed by the Blood Spear King.</p>
<p>Right now, we all have no idea where he is, let alone whether he is still alive. Since the Blood Era, nobody has seen him again, so we can’t say for sure whether he is alive.</p>
<p>But if that isn’t strange enough, the challenge scroll to sign has also been handed to someone we literally have no idea where to find. Basically, the fate of the universe has been handed to a mad woman, and we have to depend on an old legend to save us.</p>
<p>If I didn’t know better, I would say the Blood Race thinks of us as jokes."</p>
<p>"I can’t believe I will say this, but I agree with the Dragon Elder. The fate of the universe is now in the hands of a mad woman who knows nothing but bloodbaths." The High Elf Empress, Sylvian, said, "The last I checked, she had no self-control, and she most certainly isn’t reliable enough to possess something like that.</p>
<p>"I think we should try our best to find her and snatch it out of her hands before she dooms us all."</p>
<p>Many of the elders nodded, supporting Sylvian’s words, the Celestial Supreme included. They knew a lot about the Blood Harvester, and so they knew she could not be trusted to handle the Blood Challenge Scroll.</p>
<p>They had already received word from the Blue Horn Tigerkin Family and knew it was in her possession.</p>
<p>However, just when some could voice their opinions in support of Mythos and Sylvian’s words, a secondary voice reached everyone’s ears.</p>
<p>"Is that so? Then I can’t wait to see how you losers plan to do that."</p>
