<p>Inside a very huge world, where only the color red could be seen, stood a large platform decorated with red lines and runes. At the center of the large golden platform was a massive core pulsating with blood energy.</p>
<p>All around, there seemed to be water; however, this was no ordinary water. Rather, it was a large soul sea, one that contained Kent’s entire soul energy.</p>
<p>The platform had nine nodes.</p>
<p>Currently, atop this large platform, something strange started to happen to one of the nodes.</p>
<p>Hundreds of thousands of Blood Essences were suspended over this node. Slowly, they started to enter the node.</p>
<p>Hours passed, but they continued to enter.</p>
<p>Meanwhile, on the outside, the Damned Hell Sea continued to surge. However, instead of people running back to the City of Chains, more seemed to have been alarmed by this strange phenomenon, so they all started coming over.</p>
<p>In fact, the viewers (those viewing from the other 12 levels of hell are called viewers) who came to watch a bloodbath became intrigued when this strange phenomenon started to happen.</p>
<p>"The Damned Hell Sea is boiling. This can only mean one of two things," a demon dressed in white, two voluminous women in his arms, said, addressing dozens of other demons.</p>
<p>"First, it might be due to a treasure manifesting at the Core Layer of the Damned Hell Sea, and due to that, the Damned Hell Beasts are at war, each trying to get it.</p>
<p>I honestly can see that happening." The demons around nodded.</p>
<p>"Or second, two Overlords are fighting. Naturally, if the person who challenged the sixth level of hell got to the Core Layer, then this battle will be between them and the Overlord whose domain they dare invade."</p>
<p>"If that is the case, then it means the challenger is no simple person."</p>
<p>"Anyone who dares challenge the Hell Road is no simple person. Of course, in my time, I have witnessed three Hell Road challenges, but they all failed.</p>
<p>However, I was told during the Era of the Divine Beast Tamer, a subordinate of his challenged the second level of Hell Road and emerged victorious.</p>
<p>So if this challenge is anything remotely impressive, then I can see him conquering at least five cities, maybe seven maximum."</p>
<p>"You sound confident," another demon said.</p>
<p>"I am confident," the demon with the ladies in his arms responded.</p>
<p>"What makes you think he can’t conquer all 14 cities?"</p>
<p>"Because nobody can." The demon grinned. "I don’t know if you are aware, but when it comes to the 14 cities, their strength is determined by the location.</p>
<p>These cities are the domains of the various Hell Demons. If one were to challenge them in their territory, the only thing that awaited them was death.</p>
<p>Trust me, I know what I am talking about. These 14 cities are not simple cities. They are the heart and soul of the sixth hell level."</p>
<p>"I guess we can only wait and watch what happens."</p>
<p>"I guess we do."</p>
<p>Suddenly, the scene changed, and the boiling sea was calm. However, a bloodthirsty feeling came from the sea, and at once, everybody standing within a 3,000-mile radius felt their bodies tense.</p>
<p>However, the feeling vanished the next second before a heat so powerful erupted from the sea, pushing even those 5,000 miles back.</p>
<p>Inside Kent’s soul sea, the node that was being fed the Blood Essence slowly started to open. As it opened, the feeling of bloodthirst filled his soul and even erupted from his body.</p>
<p>Then, suddenly, a very large gate made of obsidian iron rose from the node and slowly ascended into the sky. After it rose to about 30,000 km in height and 50,000 km in diameter, it stopped.</p>
<p>From within, a red portal formed, and the remaining thousands of Blood Essences were absorbed.</p>
<p>However, just when that was done, the Green Flame Core moved and slowly hovered at the center of the gate.</p>
<p>Flame runes moved slowly, and they started to merge with the Blood Core gate. It took about five hours, but eventually, they merged, and the process of forming and merging the Flame Core and the Blood Core gate came to a close.</p>
<p>However, the next second, a crack appeared in the Green Flame Core, and a rune, very large in fact, came from the crack.</p>
<p>Then another crack came from the large Blood Core at the center of the platform, sending out another rune.</p>
<p>On the outside, Kent’s eyes snapped open, and his body immediately rose into the air, blasting through the dragon mansion.</p>
<p>He appeared outside the mansion, and at once, his clothes were reduced to ash as potent red, green, and golden flames enveloped him.</p>
<p>If one looked closely, they would recognize the green flame as the flames from the Flame Core, the golden flame as the Chaos Soul Flame, and the red flame as the newly awakened Blood Flame.</p>
<p>They cocooned Kent, a process that lasted for a week.</p>
<p>On this day, as the cocoon cracked, a battle-ready Kent appeared. He was covered in red and golden armor made from a blend of metal and leather that appeared to be a unique material.</p>
<p>The design was sharp and elegant, giving off a powerful, fiery presence.</p>
<p>His head is covered in a helmet that is smooth with pointed edges and a glowing slit for the eyes, shaped like burning light.</p>
<p>The chestplate is layered and angular, with a glowing green core at its center that resembles molten fire.</p>
<p>It came with a gauntlet that ended in a razor-sharp claw, one that would drip with blood when used in combat.</p>
<p>The waist area featured several red, flame-like ribbons flowing downward, giving the armor a dynamic appearance.</p>
<p>Suddenly, wings burst from his back, causing the space around him to sizzle.</p>
<p>The helmet melted, revealing Kent’s focused expression. He turned to his left and looked at Gaap. When he extended his hand, Gaap transformed into his sword form and flew toward him.</p>
<p>The moment he landed in his palm, red, golden, and green flames circled around it, causing Gaap to shudder excitedly.</p>
<p>[Let’s gooooooooo...]</p>
<p>Kent had formed his Blood Core Gate, formed his Flame Core, and fully comprehended the first form of the Primal Bone Tempering Art.</p>
<p>Aside from that, 35,000 more flame veins had formed, bringing the total to 70,000. He could now use 5,000 blood runes, and his bloodline power could be utilized at 20% capacity.</p>
<p>Naturally, Kent’s strength had tripled, and that alone was enough to show where Gaap’s enthusiasm was coming from.</p>
<p>[Are we leaving?] Gaap asked.</p>
<p>"Yes," Kent replied. Then he looked at Avotha, who had moved a great deal. "You are still not done learning the Genesis of Death."</p>
<p>"Yes. However, I am closer."</p>
<p>"Then stay here and train." Kent waved his hand, and seven flame seeds appeared. "Guard these seven for me. They want to cultivate inside the Primordial Flame, and since I can’t leave them alone, you must guard them."</p>
<p>Avotha nodded. "Rest assured, I will guard them with my life."</p>
<p>"Great to hear that." Kent lifted his head and looked up at the abyss. "Time for me to go make a name for myself."</p>
<p>"Good luck," Avotha said and stepped back, taking the seven flame seeds.</p>
<p>Kent nodded toward him, and then he fired up 50,000 flame veins. He placed Gaap on his back, and without even a sword sheath, he remained stuck.</p>
<p>"Here I come," Kent shot up at a speed Avotha couldn’t even track.</p>
<p>Fourteen hours later, the sea started to tremble on the surface. Everybody could sense somebody was coming, so they all became alert.</p>
