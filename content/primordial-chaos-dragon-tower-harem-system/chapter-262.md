<p>It was as if not being taken seriously was not something Kent wanted.</p>
<p>He clearly hated that, considering just watching the Saintess behave like she wasn’t putting in much effort pissed him off, so Kent chose to add some heat to the battle.</p>
<p>He blinked away and held his sword sheath with his left hand while his right hand gripped the hilt.</p>
<p>Suddenly, a light flashed, and 36 deadly sharp strings appeared in the air.</p>
<p>The Saintess, who had been using the flying swords, suddenly felt the danger, so she tapped into her sword qi and executed her attack.</p>
<p>She saw that playing around would land her in hot waters.</p>
<p>The strings clashed, sending shockwaves here and there. The attack wasn’t that strong per standards, but it was enough to take care of a Grandmaster.</p>
<p>This was Kent’s way of saying, you better woman up and face me like a lady...</p>
<p>"Again," Kent unleashed the attack once more, sending 36 strings, this time using both sword qi and intent. However, she defended like the first one.</p>
<p>But the attack became deadlier, shocking the Saintess, who recognized the energy she had just defended against.</p>
<p>"Again," Kent shouted, this time making it 48 strings. The Saintess used all three flying swords and her handheld sword to defend, pushing her back slightly.</p>
<p>"You might want to put in the effort, else you will lose—and lose bitterly too," Kent said.</p>
<p>He blinked away, and the next time he appeared, his sword was out of the sheath, causing the energy around him to surge. The sword sheath vanished into his soul sea.</p>
<p>Lightning danced around the sword and his arms.</p>
<p>The Sword Saintess also unleashed her profound intent and qi when she felt how powerful Kent had become after drawing his sword.</p>
<p>Kent moved, becoming a blur. All that could be seen was a streak of lightning moving around the battleground. The blades clashed, and Kent and the Saintess finally began to exert their strength in close combat.</p>
<p>The clash of steel echoed like thunder, each strike sending ripples of energy that tore through the air.</p>
<p>Kent’s movements were precise, his blade a flash of lightning that seemed to cut through space itself.</p>
<p>The Saintess, though initially caught off guard, matched his ferocity, her sword qi swirling around her like a storm.</p>
<p>Her three flying swords darted around her, weaving a deadly dance as they intercepted Kent’s relentless attacks.</p>
<p>The disciples watching were all taken aback by how ferocious the battle had become. They all expected Kent to lose in under a minute. But the flashing and blade clashes echoing in the arena just don’t feel like he was losing at all.</p>
<p>On the contrary, he matched the saintess and even made it so she couldn’t do anything to him as she had hoped.</p>
<p>He has undistributed points, after all...</p>
<p>Kent was pushed back, causing him to exhale, but the next second, he was upon the Saintess.</p>
<p>He dashed forward, his sword slicing horizontally in a wide arc. The Saintess blocked, but the force of the blow sent her skidding back, her feet carving grooves into the ground.</p>
<p>Kent was running while teleporting at the same time. So, the attacks coming from the flying swords are just attacking his afterimage.</p>
<p>His body flickered, and he reappeared above her, bringing his sword down in a vertical slash. The Saintess raised her blade, the impact resonating like a gong.</p>
<p>The ground beneath her cracked, but she held firm, her expression unreadable.</p>
<p>Suddenly, her three flying swords shot toward Kent from different angles, forcing him to retreat.</p>
<p>He twisted mid-air, deflecting two of the swords with quick, precise strikes, but the third grazed his arm, drawing a thin line of blood.</p>
<p>Kent smirked, glancing at the wound before locking eyes with the Saintess.</p>
<p>Of course, the disciples who saw this all cheered, believing the first cut was the step to trashing him.</p>
<p>"I was careless there," Kent muttered loud enough for the saints to hear.</p>
<p>However, she didn’t respond.</p>
<p>Instead, she raised her hand, and the flying swords returned to her side, orbiting her like loyal guardians.</p>
<p>She held a calm expression on her face, but inwardly, she was shocked that Kent was still standing.</p>
<p>Her sword qi intensified, the air around her shimmering with power.</p>
<p>She stepped forward with calculated and calm steps and swung her blade. A wave of qi erupted from the strike, racing toward Kent with terrifying speed.</p>
<p>Kent didn’t dodge. He met the attack head-on, his sword cutting through the wave and dispersing it into harmless sparks.</p>
<p>But the Saintess was already on the move, instantly closing the distance between them.</p>
<p>Their blades met again, metal clashing and filling the air as they exchanged a flurry of strikes. Each movement was calculated, and each strike aimed to exploit an opening.</p>
<p>Inside the VIP room, Saintess Mara held a calm expression, but inwardly, she was shocked by how Kent was handling the battle. It was like he was matched with the Saintess.</p>
<p>Suddenly, her eyes widened when Kent unleashed an attack after breaking the close combat. From what he had observed, the Saintess was good at the close combat.</p>
<p>So when Kent broke away, she knew he also saw that and decided to change the battle style.</p>
<p>But the close combat helped him add 5000 points to all the right places.</p>
<p>Now, he was much faster than before, but the Saintess was no pushover. So, the strategy changed, and this strategy was what he was going to use against the clone.</p>
<p>"Rapid Slash," Kent unleashed the attack, sending 100 sword arcs flying at the Saintess, who was forced to move back and distance herself from Kent.</p>
<p>"Don’t run, I am just getting started," Kent said with a smirk.</p>
<p>He unleashed another, filling the air with 100 Sword arcs, each speeding toward the saintess, who did a 360 swing, sending a gust of wind flying at Kent.</p>
<p>Then she moved through the chaos that appeared behind Kent. She stabbed forward, but before it could land on him, Kent vanished and appeared a couple of meters away.</p>
<p>"Rapid Slash." This time, 200 sword arcs appeared, causing everyone to widen their eyes. The air was filled with beautiful golden arcs.</p>
<p>But instead of admiring their beauty, they were now aiming to claim the Saintess’ life. Even a blind man could see that.</p>
<p>The golden arcs are, without a doubt, beautiful to the eye, but they are deadly to anyone. One cannot admire them simply because they are not meant to be admired.</p>
<p>She started to see her shortcomings. But she must first break even, considering Kent wasn’t stopping his attacks.</p>
<p>He was using the chances he had to unleash a flurry of attacks on the Saintess, making it so she couldn’t get close.</p>
<p>Suddenly, the energy around the Saintess also changed, causing Kent to move back. The intent around her now was much more powerful, causing him to take a cold breath.</p>
<p>’And so it began...the attacks that will determine who wins.’ Kent knew she was about to make her move, so he was prepared for it.</p>
<p>The Saintess defended against the sword arcs, and she pointed forward. The three swords moved.</p>
