<p>"Everyone is ready, Kent," Velyra said, watching the thousands of her tribe members get ready to be transported into the tower.</p>
<p>Kent nodded, and this time, he formed portals that led directly into the Tower city. He looked at the Bloodvein tribe members and issued the command.</p>
<p>"These portals will take you to your new homes." When he issued the order, the three elders flew toward the portal and entered. This was their way of sending their top warriors to check their new home and ensure its safety before they entered.</p>
<p>Back inside the tower, Lady Rain and the humans were already waiting for their new neighbours, so when the portals opened, smiles appeared on their faces.</p>
<p>The three people entered, brightening their smiles.</p>
<p>"Welcome, neighbours, we are glad to have you." The three elders turned and looked at the smiling humans, and then they saw the red city.</p>
<p>The moment their eyes landed on the city, they looked shocked. However, this didn’t last long, as it would be impolite to leave their neighbours hanging.</p>
<p>"I am Farra, an elder from the Bloodvein tribe. This is Zarka, and the big one is Ureth. We are also glad to be here."</p>
<p>Elaine’s mother and the other humans couldn’t contain their smiles.</p>
<p>"Well, this is us, and we will be glad to meet your entire tribe," Lady Rain said, and the three elders nodded. Elder Ureth flew out. The moment he appeared outside, everyone locked their gaze on him.</p>
<p>"Everyone, let’s go home." With a smile, Elder Ureth flew back into the portal and reappeared in the city.</p>
<p>Hearing what he said, every last one of the Bloodvein tribe members started moving into the portals. Kent, who was at the back with his ladies, smiled, watching this.</p>
<p>This was a new beginning for the Bloodvein tribe. They had a new home where they would be out of harm’s way for the foreseeable future.</p>
<p>As for Kent, this was the start of building a safe haven for those who don’t have anywhere to live. He would make sure they find a home and live in peace with their loved ones.</p>
<p>The migration took some time, but eventually, the last of the Bloodveins entered, leaving only the tribe chief. He turned to Kent and smiled.</p>
<p>"Thank you, Kent. We will forever be grateful." With that, Tribe Chief Malthar flew into the portal, and it closed behind him.</p>
<p>"Well, ladies, time to go," Kent said, and his ladies nodded.</p>
<p>Velyra looked around the space for a few minutes before she nodded. Kent sent them inside, but he stayed. A second after the ladies entered, Sylara appeared.</p>
<p>"Keep an eye out here for the next few days. The moment anyone appears here, inform me." The evil demoness nodded and walked away.</p>
<p>Kent then left back to the Tower.</p>
<p>He wanted to ensure that whoever cast the spell wouldn’t go away easily. This time, he wanted to ensure they paid for their crimes just like how the Dreadclaw and Soulshroud tribes did for theirs.</p>
<p>By the time Kent returned to the tower, the Bloodvein tribe had already settled in, and they were celebrating their new home with the neighbors.</p>
<p>Kent appeared inside the Mountain Garden, so he could overlook everything from a distance. Thankfully, Gaia, Akira, and Dori, along with Kent’s wives, were there to help with everything.</p>
<p>After a while, he left to join them.</p>
<p>They spent the entire day celebrating. Kent and his wives even spent the night inside the city with the others, and the next day they checked up on the Bloodvein Tribe before heading back to the Love Garden.</p>
<p>The Bloodvein tribe truly liked their new home, so Kent didn’t spend much time there, knowing he had things to do first.</p>
<p>He now has the Blood Heart Stone in his possession, so he wanted to get everything done with his ladies, and then he would enter seclusion.</p>
<p>However, to his shock, he broke through a few hours after returning to the garden. He didn’t do anything; he was spending time with his ladies when his cultivation suddenly moved to the Root Sovereign stage.</p>
<p>Upon inspection, he found out the spiritual qi came from the Bloodvein tribe. They were already enjoying the abundance of energy in their new home.</p>
<p>Over half of them were cultivating, while the rest still explored the city.</p>
<p>Naturally, those cultivating were the tribe warriors who were under strict orders from their tribe chief to enter seclusion and raise their cultivation bases to the next level.</p>
<p>They were doing this so they would be ready to help when their benefactor needed them. Kent could only smile, knowing this was his life now.</p>
<p>Aside from being with his ladies, the more people who cultivated inside the tower, the stronger he became. His attribute points had already surpassed 3 million and were still counting.</p>
<p>Talk about levelling up quickly—he had indeed struck gold. This made him wonder what would become of him when he added the next ten thousand and the one after that.</p>
<p>In the end, he could only smile and wait for that day to come.</p>
<p>For now, he needed all the time he could muster to work on himself.</p>
<p>He needed to raise his alchemy to the level of a Pill Saint by concocting Tier 6 and 7 pills. Then he would go into a comatose state for at least a year to activate the Soul Path Vexthra left in his soul.</p>
<p>Once that was done, he would work on his sword path by remembering the three parts of Jest’s memory. He was sure this would push him closer to his goals.</p>
<p>After reviewing his memories, he knew he could form the foundations for the two sword techniques he required to become a Sword Saint.</p>
<p>The last two memories would be handled once he was done with the sword path, pill path, and soul path.</p>
<p>For now, the plan was to awaken the true names of the four remaining ladies he hadn’t yet. He spent another day with his ladies talking about random things.</p>
<p>After that, it was Lady Vari’s turn to awaken her true name. Kent knew Vexthra should be next, even the first, but Vexthra told him her sisters were the priority. She was already a god.</p>
<p>Kent activated the true name token, and the awakening started once more.</p>
