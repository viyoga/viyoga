<p>[Moonfang City - Planet Sonoria]</p>
<p>Moonfang City is one of the four cities on Planet Sonoria. It is inhabited by a race of human variants known as Moon Humans. They share a direct connection with the moon, allowing them to harness its power.</p>
<p>Naturally, they are as good-looking as many other races fortunate enough to be favored by the Moon Goddess.</p>
<p>However, looks and character are two completely different things. This is especially true for the ruler of Moonfang City, Lord Vexor.</p>
<p>He is one of the biggest scumbags on Planet Sonoria.</p>
<p>His empire was built upon the suffering of the poor.</p>
<p>Just as many wealthy men go out of their way to ensure the poor remain poor, Lord Vexor had invested both time and money to make sure the people of Moonfang City understood the need to show appreciation.</p>
<p>There are about 300 million people in Moonfang City, and almost everyone fears him.</p>
<p>His slave trade business is the largest on Planet Sonoria. On top of that, he is affiliated with the Soul Temple, which sends aid whenever he finds himself in trouble.</p>
<p>In return for their assistance, he provides people whose souls and resentment are extracted by the Soul Temple to strengthen themselves.</p>
<p>This had been going on for years, and people are aware of it, but with thousands of immortals and dozens of demigods under his command, nobody dared stand against him.</p>
<p>...until today.</p>
<p>Inside a teleportation station, a ship landed, and three beautiful women stepped out.</p>
<p>Unity, alongside Dia and Lilian, walked out, instantly attracting attention. Unity’s dark hair flowed down her back, moving through the air as though every strand possessed a life of its own.</p>
<p>She was clad in an elegant outfit consisting of trousers and a gown that covered her shoulders. Of course, she merely wanted to appear regal while maintaining a fierce and imposing presence, so she concealed part of her armor beneath the gown, or perhaps it was more accurate to call it a cloak.</p>
<p>Now that she had become an Immortal King, her already stunning appearance had become even more breathtaking.</p>
<p>Already blessed with the Celestial-grade Amoris Astraea Bloodline, born from her nature as Artemis, the Goddess of the Hunt, and Amorin, the Goddess of Love, her beauty continued to grow as she gradually recovered the nature and power of her past lives.</p>
<p>Beside her, Lilian had chosen to go redheaded today by switching to her Flame Dragon side. Her crimson dragon horns, paired with her fiery red hair and scarlet eyes, made her look dangerous and, surprisingly, even more attractive.</p>
<p>As the saying goes, men are drawn to dangerous women. Lilian had become the very embodiment of that appeal.</p>
<p>Just like Unity, she had also reached the Peak Immortal King Stage. And with both her Flame and Ice at the Absolute Rank under her command, who was to say that the legendary Dual Dragon Mage wouldn’t reduce Moonfang City to ashes before noon?</p>
<p>Then came Dia, one of the most mysteriously terrifying women in Kent’s harem. She was Kent’s heart demon in 13 lives, yet she was also her own person.</p>
<p>After making peace with herself, she had begun to grow rapidly. As a result, even though she had yet to awaken her bloodline, she had become one of the few women in the harem to reach the Immortal Monarch Stage.</p>
<p>She stood 6’2" tall, making her two inches taller than her sister, Unity. Even with her bloodline still dormant and no True Name having been bestowed upon her, her beauty was evident to all.</p>
<p>She had silver hair, unlike her sister, but aside from that, her athletic physique and the scythe she wielded made her appear more like a warrior than a beauty to be admired.</p>
<p>As they walked away, many men turned to look at them, some even feeling the urge to approach. However, for reasons they couldn’t understand, whenever such thoughts surfaced, they were forced to erase them from their minds.</p>
<p>If only they knew their pitiful lives had been spared because they chose not to approach.</p>
<p>But it was only a matter of time before those foolish enough to ignore the obvious would decide to court death.</p>
<p>A couple of hours later, Unity and her sisters found themselves in a private room, seated around a table and drinking tea. The first thing they had done after arriving in Moonfang City was gather information about their target, Lord Vexor.</p>
<p>After all, they had come for his head. They intended to make sure he would die soon so they could claim their rewards and move on to the next mission.</p>
<p>Just like every team harem, they were competing against one another.</p>
<p>Unity’s team wasn’t the only one out there. With three women in each team, more than a dozen teams were already operating across different regions, handling the missions Kent had received from the Tower and the Quest Pool.</p>
<p>"What are your findings?" Unity asked, taking a sip of her tea.</p>
<p>"Just as the mission stated, he is heavily protected," Dia said, revealing her findings to her sisters. "During my scouting, I discovered that he has over ten thousand immortal guards, with close to one thousand of them being Immortal Lords.</p>
<p>"Aside from them, he also has about twenty-four demigod guards. However, most of them are stationed outside his mansion, so if we can slip through their defenses, killing him won’t be a problem."</p>
<p>"What Dia discovered is the same as what I found," Lilian revealed. "He is indeed very well protected. However, if we can get close to him, killing him will be as simple as a single dragon breath."</p>
<p>Lilian smiled, already fantasizing about how she would barbecue the poor bastard.</p>
<p>Unity merely shook her head, fully aware that after Kent had awakened Lilian’s True Name, her personality had changed as well.</p>
<p>Because she possessed both ice and fire, her ice form was calm, quiet, and composed. However, when she switched to her flame form, all hell would break loose.</p>
<p>"Unfortunately for you, there will be no breath attacks on the target today," Unity said.</p>
<p>Lilian pouted. However, knowing how intelligent Unity was, she said nothing and instead listened to the plan.</p>
<p>"So here’s the plan..."</p>
<p>A while later, they left the restaurant and headed toward the location where the mission would begin.</p>
<p>Meanwhile, inside a luxurious mansion, a Moon Human man with short dark hair and a muscular build sat upon a throne-like chair, surrounded by half-naked women massaging his body.</p>
<p>A few meters away, a demigod guard stood at attention, delivering his report.</p>
<p>"My Lord, I have discovered the location where the three new arrivals are staying. General Tengis has already made arrangements to send men over this evening.</p>
<p>"By tomorrow, all three of them will be in your bed, my lord."</p>
<p>The ruler of all Moonfang City, Lord Vexor, smiled.</p>
<p>"Good job, Torghud. You can take the day off and return tomorrow evening."</p>
<p>Torghud nodded and turned to leave, his expression brightening slightly. However, just as he opened the main door and was about to step outside, a loud dragon roar echoed across the sky, turning it crimson.</p>
<p>Then, from the clouds, a massive dragon emerged, its wings beating against the air as the ground began to tremble.</p>
<p>DONG! DONG! DONG!</p>
<p>Alarm bells rang throughout the Mansion, and powerful immortals and demigods scrambled to their feet, unsheathing their weapons.</p>
