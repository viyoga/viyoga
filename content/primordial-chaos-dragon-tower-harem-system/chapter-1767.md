<p>&lt;Congratulations, you have saturated your first Chaos Heart.&gt;</p>
<p>Just a few minutes into his cultivation, Kent had completely saturated his first Chaos Heart.</p>
<p>His Evil Core, Flame Cores, and Blood Core were already more than halfway saturated, as he had only depleted a portion of their energy to fuel the Chaos Heart Detonation back in the Infinity Universe.</p>
<p>Now that his Chaos Heart had been fully restored, he turned his attention to saturating the rest of his cores.</p>
<p>Before forming his second Chaos Heart, Ice Core, and Lightning Core, he wanted every one of his existing cores to reach full saturation.</p>
<p>So he began cultivating once more, and the results soon became apparent.</p>
<p>&lt;Congratulations, you have saturated your first Flame Core.&gt;</p>
<p>&lt;Congratulations, you have saturated your second Flame Core.&gt;</p>
<p>&lt;Congratulations, you have saturated your first Blood Core.&gt;</p>
<p>&lt;Congratulations, you have saturated your first Evil Core.&gt;</p>
<p>’Damn... this feeling is incredible,’ he thought to himself as every one of his cores brimmed with overwhelming power.</p>
<p>The sensation of untamed energy coursing through his veins made him eager to discover how much stronger he would become once he forged his remaining cores.</p>
<p>Without hesitation, he decided to begin forming his Ice Core.</p>
<p>Using treasures provided by Primordials truly was a blessing. The moment he began refining the Ice Source, which had been compressed into a tiny bottle, Kent realized he might have underestimated just how precious the resources here were.</p>
<p>The Ice Source alone was sufficient to establish the core’s foundation.</p>
<p>Time quietly passed.</p>
<p>Before long, an entire month had gone by, yet his Ice Core was still taking shape. Kent, however, wasn’t in the slightest bit anxious. He still had fifty years ahead of him.</p>
<p>Time was on his side.</p>
<p>Three months later, a terrifying wave of frost erupted from his body, instantly freezing the surrounding space beneath a layer of crystalline ice.</p>
<p>Kent slowly opened his eyes, a smile spreading across his face as he realized another hurdle had been overcome.</p>
<p>&lt;Congratulations, you have formed your first Ice Core.&gt;</p>
<p>Kent smiled as he examined his body, feeling a majestic surge of ice essence flowing through every inch of his being.</p>
<p>"This feeling..."</p>
<p>He slowly clenched his fist, sensing his physical strength rise yet again.</p>
<p>Inside the First Blood Gate, the Blood Core still occupied the highest position, radiating an overwhelming wave of blood energy.</p>
<p>Beneath it floated the Green Flame Core, now overflowing with boundless flame essence.</p>
<p>The Chaos Heart hovered in the third position, followed closely by the Evil Core.</p>
<p>Now, a pure-white Ice Core rested in the fifth position, emanating an endless chill that seemed capable of freezing all creation.</p>
<p>The energy contained within his First Blood Gate alone was now sufficient to sustain prolonged battles without any fear of exhausting his power.</p>
<p>Thousands more Flame Runes had awakened during the process, while his Blood Runes had also become significantly potent.</p>
<p>Satisfied with his progress, Kent wasted no time.</p>
<p>He immediately began forming his first Lightning Core, believing it would elevate his strength to yet another level.</p>
<p>Four months later, golden-red lightning erupted from Kent’s body, transforming the entire training ground into a domain of crackling lightning.</p>
<p>&lt;Congratulations, you have formed your first Lightning Core.&gt;</p>
<p>Kent smiled in satisfaction.</p>
<p>At last, he had formed every core required for his First Blood Gate. Of course, if he ever developed an affinity for another element or energy in the future, he could always forge additional cores.</p>
<p>But in just six months, he had completely perfected his First Blood Gate.</p>
<p>His attention then shifted to the Second Blood Gate.</p>
<p>To complete it, he needed to forge a second Chaos Heart, Evil Core, Ice Core, and Lightning Core.</p>
<p>Naturally, he chose to begin with the Chaos Heart. There was no better place to cultivate one than a world overflowing with Chaos Essence.</p>
<p>Four months later, the long-awaited notification appeared.</p>
<p>An overwhelming tide of Chaos Essence exploded from Kent’s body. The density of the energy was so terrifying that everyone within the Red Bone Chaos Tribe immediately sensed it.</p>
<p>&lt;Congratulations, you have formed your second Chaos Heart.&gt;</p>
<p>&lt;The rate of Chaos Essence absorption has increased by 3x.&gt;</p>
<p>Kent’s eyes widened.</p>
<p>He could clearly feel the amount of Chaos Essence pouring into his body increase threefold.</p>
<p>"What..."</p>
<p>He was completely bewildered.</p>
<p>Inside the tribe’s central hall, Jyoti Lata and the others had also sensed the disturbance, sending their attention to the purple-haired dragon brat somewhere in their tribe.</p>
<p>Jyoti Lata frowned as she watched the enormous currents of Chaos Essence converging toward Kent’s cultivation ground.</p>
<p>"This child... he’s far more talented than I initially gave him credit for," she muttered.</p>
<p>The others silently nodded.</p>
<p>"Perhaps he truly is related to the Chaos Ascension King," Ragini Disha said, still refusing to let go of the idea that Kent shared some connection with the creator of their race and the absolute ruler of the Chaos Chasm.</p>
<p>"For now, we’ll simply observe," Jyoti Lata replied calmly.</p>
<p>"If he later shows any undeniable signs of being related to the Chaos Ascension King, I’ll inform the leaders of the other tribes. At the end of the day, all of us wish to leave this place. If helping him means helping ourselves, then none of us should hesitate."</p>
<p>The others nodded once more.</p>
<p>What the Red Bone Chaos Tribe didn’t know was that Kent’s existence had already attracted the attention of another Chaos Tribe.</p>
<p>They had already begun tracking him.</p>
<p>...</p>
<p>Kent then began forging his second Evil Core.</p>
<p>A full year passed.</p>
<p>It had now been roughly two years since he entered secluded cultivation.</p>
<p>Suddenly, an overwhelming wave of sinister energy burst out of his body, spreading across the entire Red Bone Chaos Tribe.</p>
<p>The moment the residents sensed it, countless experts rushed into the skies, weapons drawn.</p>
<p>"Stand down, everyone."</p>
<p>Jyoti Lata’s calm voice echoed throughout the city, instantly stopping everyone in their tracks.</p>
<p>Meanwhile, Kent remained completely unaware of the commotion he had caused.</p>
<p>His attention was fixed on the notifications before him as a smile slowly spread across his face.</p>
<p>&lt;Congratulations, you have formed your second Evil Core.&gt;</p>
<p>&lt;You have awakened the second Evil Key.&gt;</p>
<p>&lt;Your Evil Eye has grown stronger.&gt;</p>
<p>The first notification wasn’t surprising.</p>
<p>Neither was the third.</p>
<p>It was the second one that completely caught him off guard.</p>
<p>’A second Evil Key...’</p>
<p>That meant he could awaken a second Grand Shaman.</p>
<p>The first Evil Key had allowed him to awaken Grand Shaman Malak, who was currently commanding his Beasts and the Evil Legion within the Star Realm.</p>
<p>Now...</p>
<p>He had obtained the key to awaken another one.</p>
<p>To Kent, that was simply unbelievable.</p>
<p>*"Tower, use the second key now,"* Kent commanded.</p>
<p>A moment later, the reply appeared.</p>
<p>&lt;The Master cannot awaken the second Grand Shaman within this world.&gt;</p>
<p>&lt;The Master must first return to his own universe.&gt;</p>
<p>Kent sighed.</p>
<p>Although slightly disappointed, his excitement quickly returned.</p>
<p>’If forming the second Evil Core grants me another Evil Key... then what will happen after I form the third Evil Core?’</p>
<p>The thought alone filled him with anticipation.</p>
<p>Without wasting another moment, he began forging his second Ice Core.</p>
<p>It took eight months.</p>
<p>After completing it, he immediately moved on to his second Lightning Core.</p>
<p>That process also lasted another eight months.</p>
<p>By the end of his fourth year of cultivation, Kent had successfully completed every core required for both his First and Second Blood Gates.</p>
<p>He slowly opened his eyes and clenched his fists, feeling the overwhelming strength flowing throughout his body.</p>
<p>"I’ll spend the next five years tempering my body," he muttered.</p>
<p>"After that, I’ll awaken the Third Blood Gate... and then begin raising my cultivation realm."</p>
