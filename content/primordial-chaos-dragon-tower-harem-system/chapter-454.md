<p>Nobody knew where they came from.</p>
<p>They just appeared out of nowhere and started displaying shocking abilities, killing as many as they could, even going so far as to trap all those who wanted to run.</p>
<p>This sight was shocking, and looking at the hidden figures emerging only to be killed before they could plead for their lives, one had to say that Kent came prepared this time.</p>
<p>Kent was only walking toward the city gate while his ladies handled matters inside the city. They didn’t let even a fly come near their man.</p>
<p>Talk about qualities of a good harem. Kent could only smile as he moved away.</p>
<p>Three figures appeared out of nowhere a couple of meters from the gate, lunging at Kent. However, before they reached a meter from him, the wind blew past Kent, causing him to smile.</p>
<p>The three lunging at him stopped mid-attack as their heads fell off their bodies.</p>
<p>"You can’t be careless, dear," Selene said, smiling back at Kent.</p>
<p>"Oh, but I can be careless knowing you are around." Kent smiled and then looked to his side, where Mara also appeared, dancing through the air with her sword.</p>
<p>Camilla also made her appearance on his left, and just looking at how quickly she was moving with her attacks, one could tell she was rather skilled with the spear.</p>
<p>"I don’t want to ask, but where is Val?" Kent asked, not seeing her around.</p>
<p>Selene smiled and pointed in a certain direction.</p>
<p>"She located someone she needed to kill first, so she is fighting him. But don’t worry, Elsa and the soul sentinels are around. She won’t get into any trouble."</p>
<p>Kent just smiled and summoned Saden.</p>
<p>"Join Unity and keep her safe." Saden nodded and moved in Unity’s direction.</p>
<p>Unity wasn’t like her sister, who had True Names. She was just a level 5 Root Great Sage, so he was slightly overprotective of her.</p>
<p>Not that her life would be in any danger; he had already given her the necklace that would save her whenever her life was in danger by teleporting her into the tower.</p>
<p>Kent looked in Val’s direction again and smiled.</p>
<p>"You can go, Kent. We will handle them here and join you soon," Selene said before returning to action.</p>
<p>"Have fun, y’all," Kent smiled before he started moving again.</p>
<p>Outside the city, the Middle Finger Assassins remained hidden, with only their leader visible. Not that it mattered, considering the plans Kent had for them.</p>
<p>A couple of meters away in the other direction, the Evil Sect was also waiting for him.</p>
<p>However, they weren’t the only ones.</p>
<p>Some unknown figures could also be seen not far from them, ready to take action. It appeared most of them didn’t want to give the others a chance to take what they came for.</p>
<p>However, far from the city gates, Pill Saint Barton and six others could be seen standing in wait for Kent too.</p>
<p>These six people were all mysterious, considering their faces were covered and their auras were suppressed to the point that sensing their cultivation base would be a problem.</p>
<p>"He is almost here," Pill Saint Barton said. "Wait until the formation is activated before taking action. The other Saints will also take action, but we mustn’t let his body fall into their hands."</p>
<p>"We won’t allow that, so rest assured, Pill Saint," one of the six figures said.</p>
<p>"Very well. The moment the battle is over, as promised, I will give you the Tier 7 pills and even throw in a Battle Doll."</p>
<p>The six figures nodded, and then their focus turned toward the city gate where Kent had just walked out, smiling.</p>
<p>After he walked through the gate, he stopped and looked toward the direction of the Evil Sect.</p>
<p>"Looks like you idiots came for revenge." He wasn’t even finished when a red energy beam shot from one direction and landed before him, exploding the ground.</p>
<p>Kent blinked away before the explosion.</p>
<p>But the moment the dust cleared, the skinny, bony figure who was the Grand Sect Leader of the Evil Sect appeared in the air, holding his twisted staff.</p>
<p>"Hand over your life." His staff shot a beam toward the sky, activating the formation that had been set up beforehand.</p>
<p>It activated, creating a dome that covered a 34-kilometer radius.</p>
<p>The moment it did, people started appearing inside.</p>
<p>The Assassins appeared, Pill Saint Barton and his men appeared, some random figures also appeared, and even Kent saw the Everlasting Immortal Sword Sect elder who had come with two others the last time to take back Val after she became Kent’s servant..</p>
<p>Many others showed up, showing just how greedy people could be.</p>
<p>Kent just stared at the formation and smirked.</p>
<p>"I don’t know how many are still hiding. Not that I care. I just want to inform you all that since you chose to come against me, why don’t we play a game?"</p>
<p>His voice was full of sarcasm despite the formation meant to suppress his soul. Suddenly, lightning flashed in the sky.</p>
<p>"Here I come!"</p>
<p>Kaizo shouted as a powerful punch landed on the formation, shattering it completely. The Grand Sect Leader coughed up blood, showing just how deeply connected he was to the formation.</p>
<p>It was the formation he had sacrificed thousands of lives to create. The Soul Suppression Formation. It was meant to affect Kent’s soul, but it seemed...</p>
<p>"Nice entrance, Kaizo," Kent said to his first undead, causing the brute to smile.</p>
<p>"Thanks, Master." Kaizo looked at the faces of all those gathered and grinned. "Are these the fish we’ll be playing with?"</p>
<p>"Indeed. They thought they had everything under control." Kent smiled, taking out an item from his space ring.</p>
<p>A couple of people took a step back when they saw the item. Kent shook his head, watching this.</p>
<p>"Just like most of you have been looking forward to today’s battle, I was too. This is why I have created the perfect game for everyone."</p>
<p>He channeled his energy into the item, causing it to fly up. The moment it entered the clouds, a powerful energy burst out from it.</p>
<p>Then, from the sky, small stars started raining down on everyone. It took just a few seconds for the stars to land on everyone, even those hidden.</p>
<p>A few tried to dodge or destroy them, but they couldn’t.</p>
<p>"I prepared well for this moment, for I will be leaving when all of you are dead," Kent said as his sword appeared in his grip.</p>
<p>"This is why you should try to kill me, because until I am dead, nobody can leave this formation." Kent laughed as he floated up.</p>
<p>"Ladies and gentlemen, welcome to No Man’s Land." He snapped his fingers, and the terrain within a ten-mile radius changed into a battleground, pulling everyone in.</p>
<p>At the same time, space cracked, and Verydris, alongside thirteen Metal Battle Dolls, appeared. Kent bought the rest.</p>
<p>Now, he has 14 Metal Battle Dolls.</p>
<p>"Shall we?" Kent smiled as the space cracked behind the Grand Sect Leader, sending out a powerful sword arc—one of the many he had prepared twelve hours ago.</p>
