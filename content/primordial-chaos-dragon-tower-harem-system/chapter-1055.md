<p>The arrow arrived before Kent. However, before it could pierce him, he caught it between his fingers and tossed it to the side.</p>
<p>Following behind were Yu Lan, Esme, Lois, and General Brianna, who looked surprised when Kent caught the arrow.</p>
<p>In fact, everyone was surprised by how he defended against an arrow from a Heavenly Ascension stage cultivator.</p>
<p>The alchemist there, who recognized him, started to panic. They saw what Kent could do, and this made them sense the danger coming.</p>
<p>The six generals, even the injured one, stood up and retrieved their weapons. The kings were the same, but the Planet King still remained on his throne.</p>
<p>The brides walking toward the stage halted, and a few even started crying.</p>
<p>Lady Kami turned toward Kent and lifted her veil, a happy expression painting her face...</p>
<p>"What took you so long?" she asked, very happy that Kent had finally arrived.</p>
<p>However, instead of answering her, Kent’s hand moved and caught another arrow that was fired by one of the generals. This time, it was aimed at Lady Kami, so he retaliated.</p>
<p>The arrow he caught was hurled forward, and before the general could dodge, it went through his head, ending him swiftly.</p>
<p>Kent ignored the rest and took Lady Kami’s hand. His soul energy evaded her body, and the seal on her cultivation was lifted.</p>
<p>"I am here now. However, you have to leave with General Brianna... I will follow once I am done here."</p>
<p>Lady Kami nodded, knowing this battle wasn’t something she could participate in.</p>
<p>Kent looked at the Planet King, who was now standing, his expression focused on him.</p>
<p>"So you are the Planet King," Kent asked, a smirk tugging on his lips. He could see it; the bastard was in shock. The way Kent killed one of his generals was just too shocking.</p>
<p>"Who are you?" the Planet King asked.</p>
<p>"Nobody too important. However, I was sent to come kill you and all these idiots, so you can call me the Planet King Killer."</p>
<p>"I see. In that case, why don’t I see how you do that?" the Planet King sneered and snapped his fingers.</p>
<p>"Lock down the planet." His command was immediate, and before Kent could make any more, the entire planet was sealed.</p>
<p>"I knew one day somebody would come for me, so I prepared. Of course, you can try all you want, but since we both know how this will end, you can start by surrendering." The planet king said with plain arrogance</p>
<p>Kent turned and first looked at the ladies standing behind him... "It will be best if you close your eyes... You know, blood and all."</p>
<p>"We are not kids, Kent," Lady Kami said, feeling very safe around Kent despite being surrounded by hundreds of warriors.</p>
<p>"I see. Then better not puke." Kent turned to the Planet King and chuckled, then rubbed his taming bracelet, summoning the 48 beasts within.</p>
<p>Kent moved and appeared before him, causing his eyes to widen.</p>
<p>However, he managed to step back and swing his fist, aiming to push Kent back. However, before his fist could reach him, Kent moved and appeared before the general who sent the spies to the Heaven’s Eye sect.</p>
<p>He placed his hand on his head, and then, with a gentle push, his head entered his body, leaving a headless body standing.</p>
<p>"Hahahahaha... Bastard, your reckoning has finally arrived," Ignarok, who saw how Kent killed the poor general, laughed at the Planet King, whose eyes narrowed.</p>
<p>However, before he could move to intercept Kent, he took another step and arrived before one of his kings and pushed his head inside his body, too.</p>
<p>Blood flowed out, causing the brides who saw this to start screaming.</p>
<p>"Kill everyone except the maids and brides," Kent gave the order, and the monarchs, alongside the other beasts, started their second massacre.</p>
<p>"Don’t fight it; this wedding is going to be your funeral, so chill and let my people handle your people. Your death will be last."</p>
<p>"Like hell I will be." The Planet King pulled out his sword and lunged forward. However, instead of attacking Kent, he aimed at Lady Kami. He can tell Kent came for her, and the other two he personally went to Planet Nictus for.</p>
<p>"Weakling."</p>
<p>Kent took one step and arrived before him. He grabbed the sword and stomped on his stomach, sending him flying back.</p>
<p>Kent took one more step and appeared overhead, his right leg stabbing down. He stomped hard on his ribcage, shattering a few.</p>
<p>Then, with one powerful kick, he sent him flying.</p>
<p>⟦The seal is currently under your control, master. However, it costs 30 days, meaning you only have two more weeks left before your time in the past comes to an end.⟧</p>
<p>’Thank you, tower.’</p>
<p>Kent smiled, watching as the Planet King retrieved a talisman, attempting to escape.</p>
<p>"That won’t work, idiot. You have sealed the planet, remember."</p>
<p>"Release the seal."</p>
<p>Kent burst out in resounding laughter as he watched the desperation on the Planet King’s face. He was afraid and desperate to run... However, Kent had already seen this coming and had to sacrifice his remaining days to take control of the seal from him.</p>
<p>Now, he was trapped, and despite having a trace of immortal qi, he was seriously outmatched.</p>
<p>Kent was not using his bloodline; however, he was using 2,000 blood runes and 40,000 flame veins. The Planet King was not even close to measuring up to him.</p>
<p>"Just who are you?" the Planet King spat out, a mouthful of blood.</p>
<p>"Like I said, I am the Planet King Killer. So relax and wait for your death." Kent turned and started observing the massacre his beasts were unleashing.</p>
<p>After they each absorbed a high-grade Primordial Flame, all seven monarchs and the 43 beasts had become monstrous. They were just killing, as if it were second nature to them.</p>
<p>Sylvara and Ignarok had already killed two generals and two kings and were now killing the guests who had been invited to the wedding.</p>
<p>"Bugs."</p>
<p>Kent saw from a distance thousands of warriors flying toward them. However, instead of moving to attack them, he waved his hand, and thousands of doom shards, sword arcs, flame palms, and many more rained down on them from the void.</p>
<p>Their screams filled the planet.</p>
<p>"You picked the wrong side, bugs; now I can only kill you all."</p>
<p>"DIE."</p>
<p>Suddenly, a white claw of a tiger slashed at Kent’s neck, aiming to sever his head. However, without even turning around, Kent’s hand moved to his back, caught the claw, grabbed the colossal beast summoned by the Planet King, and slammed him into the ground.</p>
<p>But Kent didn’t stop there; he waved his hand, and the Unbral Flame of Oblivion surrounded the colossal tiger.</p>
<p>"Bro, immortal qi and wannabe Supreme Grade bloodline won’t help you." Kent kicked him away, sending him flying into empty space; his roars shook the hearts of every ally.</p>
<p>"We don’t accept surrender, so kill them all."</p>
<p>Kent walked closer to the Planet King and stomped on his leg.</p>
<p>"I expected so much from you. I was told you have a sliver of immortal qi and a Supreme Grade Bloodline. However, compared to those bastards I killed when I came out of the Damned Hell Sea, you are very weak."</p>
<p>Kent chuckled. "I know it is too shocking to say this, considering you are the infamous Planet King that everyone was scared of... But why are you this weak?"</p>
