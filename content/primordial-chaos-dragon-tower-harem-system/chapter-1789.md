<p>"Well, I suppose his comprehension speed is indeed very monstrous," Parand muttered, looking at the vast Void Storm Domain now covering a radius of 100,000 kilometers after just a day of Kent cultivating it.</p>
<p>The other Tribe Leaders, while they didn’t know much about the Heaven Splitting Chaos Wings Art, could tell that what Kent had achieved was far beyond normal.</p>
<p>The mere fact that he had already reached a radius of 100,000 kilometers was more than enough for many Demigods at his level to spend over a year achieving the same thing he had done in a single day.</p>
<p>But things didn’t end there. Ten days later, his Void Storm Domain had reached 1,000,000 kilometers, and by the end of the first month, it had already expanded to one light-year.</p>
<p>They could only sit back and watch him progress faster than anyone at his level should. Thankfully, while they would have loved nothing more than to watch his domain continue expanding, they knew he had a limit, and that limit came a year later.</p>
<p>But by then, his domain had already reached 120,000 light-years.</p>
<p>Kent opened his eyes and jumped to his feet, tapping into the Void Storm Domain. His Heaven Splitting Jade Void Wings flapped, and he vanished from sight, appearing several billion kilometers away.</p>
<p>"WOW, that isn’t normal, right?" he asked, and was about to move again when a terrifying shockwave slammed into his body.</p>
<p>He had never expected it, but even if he had, the shockwave was so powerful that he lost his balance. However, before he could be swept away, Parand caught him.</p>
<p>Kent found himself standing before her, almost as if she hadn’t moved even a finger.</p>
<p>Naturally, she hadn’t moved her body. She had merely reached forward with her left hand and caught him. However, in reality, that casual movement had traveled several billion kilometers.</p>
<p>Parand smiled, almost as if she had read Kent’s mind, and said,</p>
<p>"Think of your domain as an extension of your body. Right now, it is vast and expansive, but if you can harness its power as your own, then naturally, you can stand here and attack someone several light-years away from you."</p>
<p>Kent nodded, then closed his eyes and took a deep breath. He activated the Void Storm Domain and, just as Parand had said, imagined himself becoming the domain and the domain becoming him.</p>
<p>For a moment, his body trembled as he felt himself begin to expand. In no time, he felt as though he were as vast as 120,000 light-years... if that even made sense.</p>
<p>He could see everything around him, his vision expanding as his body grew. ’This is just mind-bending,’ he said inwardly, but thanks to his vast Soul Sea, he felt no discomfort.</p>
<p>Parand smiled, seeing how easily Kent had grasped the concept of becoming one with his domain.</p>
<p>"Now, reach forward and pick a fruit from the tree 40,000 light-years due east of you."</p>
<p>Kent nodded, then focused on the tree. His hand moved, and the next second, a green fruit lay in his palm.</p>
<p>"Wow, this is too overpowered."</p>
<p>Parand laughed, seeing how happy and shocked Kent was after experiencing only the tip of the iceberg. For now, she allowed him to experience things for himself.</p>
<p>After all, he still had one more technique to learn, so all the tutoring they had planned for him would have to wait.</p>
<p>Kent seemed to understand that as well, so after picking a few fruits for Fiannarel and the Tribe Leaders, he returned to his cultivation.</p>
<p>However, he realized one thing.</p>
<p>’Whatever I am here training for, it better be something worth my time.’</p>
<p>He laughed, then focused on the final technique... the Heavenly Chaos Eye Canon...</p>
<p>The moment he tapped into the Heavenly Chaos Eye Canon technique, he appeared in a completely dark space. Everything about the place reeked of danger, but for reasons Kent had no idea of, he knew he was about to experience the biggest breakthrough of his life.</p>
<p>He sat in a lotus posture and closed his eyes. Well, that was what the technique told him to do, so he did, and a few minutes later, streams of golden runes started appearing in the air.</p>
<p>However, just as they appeared, they began to move and entered his forehead.</p>
<p>On the outside, a vertical eye tattoo appeared on Kent’s forehead. Then, from within it, a diagram made of red metal appeared above his head.</p>
<p>Suddenly, a faint grey eye started to form within the diagram.</p>
<p>Zdzislav smiled and clapped his hands. "What an interesting kid. I guess now we can truly say he is the Chaos Ascension King. After all, he has awakened the Eye of Chaos, the most dreadful eye said to have beheld Existence in its entirety."</p>
<p>The other Tribe Chiefs felt their bodies tremble with joy. Even if they turned out to be wrong, they still wouldn’t mind calling Kent their Young Lord.</p>
<p>After all, he had accomplished what none of them could, not even their ancestors.</p>
<p>A year later, a majestic red eye lined with hints of gold, purple, and silver hovered behind Kent, giving him a presence that commanded respect.</p>

<p>[Infinity Universe - Blood Temple]</p>
<p>A woman with flowing red hair and a voluptuous body, seated in a pool of blood, suddenly snapped her eyes open, blood seeping from the corners of both eyes.</p>
<p>Her expression had become pale, so pale that she fell backward and slammed into the edge of the pool. Luckily, she wasn’t easily intimidated, so she managed to calm her racing heart.</p>
<p>"Eye of Chaos... but how," she muttered, looking dazed.</p>
<p>Just now, she had felt a powerful pressure coming from somewhere far away, perhaps too far away for her Eyes of Blood to see. However, she had felt it, and it had terrified her.</p>
<p>Unfortunately for her, she recognized what kind of eye it was. The Eye of Chaos was one of the Seven Primal Eyes. Aside from it, there were the Eyes of Fate, Purgatory, Destiny, Karma, Life, and Death.</p>
<p>From the records passed down through time, the Eye of Chaos was wielded by an existence that sought to rise above Existence itself.</p>
<p>When she started cultivating the Eye of Blood, she learned that while it was possible to see beyond what was and gaze upon what could be, the path to that was impossible.</p>
<p>The records spoke of the day the Eye of Chaos gazed upon Existence. It was said that everything known and long forgotten trembled as it witnessed Existence seeing itself.</p>
<p>The wielder of the Eye of Chaos had bitten off more than they could chew. In the end, they were forced to destroy their own eyes, blinding themselves to the truth, never to see it again.</p>
<p>"But why... how? How can the Eye of Chaos appear again, and in such dangerous times?"</p>
<p>She rose from the pool of blood, and an elegant red dress wrapped itself around her.</p>
<p>She walked out of her mansion and stood atop one of the tallest mountains in the Celestial Realm.</p>
<p>"Has the Chaos Ascension King returned, or is this just fate playing its games on me?" She looked up, almost as if trying to meet someone’s gaze.</p>
<p>Naturally, far within the Primordial Chaos, hidden deep in the Void, a woman seated in a lotus posture opened her blood-red eyes and gazed back at her.</p>
<p>Seeing the blank expression on her face, the Blood Empress sighed and called out,</p>
<p>"Blood Ancestor, send word to the Celestial Supreme. I want to have a word with him."</p>
