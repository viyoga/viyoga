<p>If there is one world where Kent’s name will be known by all, then it is the world that gave birth to the first person to immortalize their name on the Sword Dao Tablet.</p>
<p>Jest, popularly known as Lord Genesis, was the first person to be recognized by the Sword Dao, and he came from planet Brymrr.</p>
<p>He had left an impact on swordsmen. So after him, every other name that appears on the Sword Dao Tablet is respected.</p>
<p>Kent is the 14th name to appear on the Sword Dao Tablet, and that is something nobody saw coming, especially when the first 13 were all swordsmen widely known before they appeared on the tablet.</p>
<p>Just like every other world, this one too is looking for him, so the fact that he now stood before Fallon and Aysel with a smile on his face said everything there is to know.</p>
<p>"Prove it."</p>
<p>Aysel said, walking toward Kent. She stopped beside her sister and added, "Prove you are Kent Madson, and we will spare you."</p>
<p>Her nature as the sin of gluttony cannot be known by others, else she would be in trouble. However, Kent Madson is a legend, and so they can give him the benefit of the doubt.</p>
<p>Naturally, this was also their own way of saving their skin. Kent had already appeared on the Three Dao Tablets. This means he has a powerful background. So if he is indeed who he said he is, asking for his help would go a long way.</p>
<p>"How do you suppose I should go about proving myself?" he asked, not particularly bothered. If he wants to leave, with the Soul Map opened, he can simply teleport away.</p>
<p>He is certain they wouldn’t chase after him, not when it would create so much chaos and expose them to more eyes.</p>
<p>Aysel looked at her sister, who gave her a slight nod. She nodded back and walked toward Kent. She extended her tiny arm, her palm facing outward.</p>
<p>"Place your palm on mine and say your name." She narrowed her big brown eyes and added, "If you are who you said you are, nothing will happen to you. However, if you lie, your soul will be devoured in less than a second."</p>
<p>Kent smiled a little, a bit taken aback by her ability. While he is not lying about who he is, the mere fact that Aysel has such an ability made him wonder just what goes into being one of the Seven Deadly Sins.</p>
<p>’She is probably a part-cosmic being to possess such a power that even the Tower can’t see through.’</p>
<p>With that thought in mind, he extended his hand and placed it on Aysel’s. He took a deep breath and spoke the magic words.</p>
<p>"My name is Kent Madson. I have appeared on the Sword, Ice, and Pill Dao Tablets. I have no plans on hurting Aysel and Fallon since I consider them friends."</p>
<p>A second passed, but nothing happened to Kent. This caused Aysel and her sister to narrow their eyes at him. To be safe, she remained vigilant for a few more seconds before Aysel pulled back.</p>
<p>She turned and looked at her sister. "Big Brother is telling the truth. Big Brother is indeed Kent Madson that we heard about."</p>

<p>I can also tell you that the two of you are hiding from something, or perhaps someone. So, how about we help each other? You allow me to join your team, and in return, I will make sure you and Aysel never have to worry about anything ever again.</p>
<p>You will have a safe place to sleep, and Aysel will have all the food in the universe to eat."</p>
<p>The two sisters turned and looked at each other again. Aysel even moved her head toward the almost empty bowl of fruit. Recalling how delicious it was, her insatiable hunger started to cloud her judgment.</p>
<p>"Why even help us? You have the talent to become popular on your own. You can just buy a few canvases, a few paints, and a brush, and the rest will be magic.</p>
