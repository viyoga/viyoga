<p>⟦The Flame Core Creation Manual has merged with your cultivation technique. Whenever you are close to a flame source, the flame core creation process will automatically start.</p>
<p>However, the core creation process will begin only after you create your first 25,000 flame veins.⟧</p>
<p>The second manual was mastered in ten days.</p>
<p>It followed the same structure as the Flame Vein Creation Manual, and since Kent had already been through the process, it became relatively simple for him to master.</p>
<p>However, the moment he was done, the message he received was clear and similar to what Agni told him: without the Flame Veins, he wouldn’t be able to form Flame Cores.</p>
<p>"Now, I just need to create the veins, and everything will take care of itself." Kent stood up and headed to the Alchemy Tower to do something he had been hoping to try all this time.</p>
<p>When he awakened the Alchemy Tower, he received some items that, when used the right way, would further aid his understanding of pill making. Inside the harem tower, he had the love garden, the yin pool, and the like.</p>
<p>Inside the Alchemy Tower, there is a Pill World room where, upon entering, he will be transported into a dreamlike state to witness alchemy from a different perspective.</p>
<p>He has a similar feature in his sword tower and has been trying to make good use of it for a while, but hasn’t yet.</p>
<p>Now, he planned on using the Pill World to kill time and perhaps learn something new about alchemy before going to the sect where he would be facing someone so far that he had heard only fantastic things about.</p>
<p>So he entered the Pill World room inside the Alchemy Tower, where a game-like pod was set at the center of the room.</p>
<p>"Tower, wake me up when the time to go to the sect is nearing," Kent gave an order to the tower before sleeping in the pod.</p>
<p>The moment he entered, his vision turned hazy, and then he was pulled into a realm beyond his own.</p>
<p>---</p>
<p>[Planet Pyris – Grand Pill Pagoda: 7th Floor (340,7000 years ago)]</p>
<p>Kent appeared in a large hall filled with people of different races.</p>
<p>The first thing he noticed was the distinct color of the clothes most people were wearing, regardless of their race.</p>
<p>The majority were clad in green robes and cloaks of different designs. Most had emblems on them that indicated the sect to which they belonged. A few also wore distinctive clothes that showed their family’s origin.</p>
<p>Naturally, all these people were the alchemists in the world he had been pulled into.</p>
<p>He examined his surroundings, taking in races such as demons, elves, humans, dragons, elementals, and many more, all found in the same world.</p>
<p>This means the world he was pulled into is a much bigger one, housing more than 20 different races that he could count.</p>
<p>"Brother, you look like you’re lost." Suddenly, a young elf tapped Kent on the shoulder, prompting him to turn around.</p>
<p>When his gaze landed on the young elf clad in a green robe of the Lotus Bloom Pill Sect, his brow furrowed.</p>
<p>’He can see me,’ Kent thought inwardly.</p>
<p>’Was Gaia messing with me by telling me nobody would see me when in the Pill World?’ Kent’s mind was a mix of shock and intrigue. He never expected to be seen. But now that he was seen, a whole different door opened for him.</p>
<p>"Yes, brother..."</p>
<p>"Suoh."</p>
<p>"Yes, brother Suoh, I am indeed lost." Kent smiled. "This happens to be my first time here, so I am a little lost."</p>
<p>"I get it, brother..."</p>
<p>"Kent"</p>
<p>"It happens to the best of us. I was lost when I first came here, and it took me some time to figure things out, but eventually, I did.</p>
<p>However, don’t worry, I will help you understand all you want to know about this place," Suoh said with a rather happy smile.</p>
<p>"Thank you, brother Suoh. I will be rather happy to learn more about this place."</p>
<p>The handsome elf smiled. "But of course. For someone of your status to be allowed here on the 7th floor, you must be from one of the 5 Pill Families.</p>
<p>As such, it is my duty to help you to the best of my abilities. Just don’t forget, my name is Suoh."</p>
<p>Hearing his words, Kent turned and looked at his chest. That was when he saw the Pill Grandmaster badge hanging there.</p>
<p>It was then that everything clicked.</p>
<p>’I guess as a Pill Grandmaster, I had no business being on this floor. But he thinks I am from an influential family, and that was why I was allowed access.</p>
<p>So, he is doing this to curry favor with my supposed family when, in fact, I am not from any influential family. But I can use this to my advantage.’</p>
<p>"I will be sure to remember your name, brother Suoh." Hearing Kent’s words, the handsome elf smiled.</p>
<p>"Welcome to the Grand Pyris Pill Pagoda. This is the 7th floor, where only alchemists from the Pill Sage stage are allowed access. I am a Pill Sage with a silver badge.</p>
<p>I don’t know if you’re aware, but having a silver badge is a significant achievement in the grand scheme of things when considering Pill Sages. Of course, soon, I will get my gold and platinum badge, and then I can upgrade to become a Pill King."</p>
<p>Talk about selling oneself for opportunity—Suoh was indeed doing a terrific job at that. Kent could only smile, knowing none of this would go anywhere.</p>
<p>Suoh thought Kent was from a renewed family, but Kent knew that in fact, he was from nowhere. But he chose to play Suoh’s game.</p>
<p>"I am sure Brother Suoh has the talent to rise to the peak. I really think so," Kent smiled, seeing the pleased expression in Suoh’s eyes.</p>
<p>"By the way, why don’t we start from the basics first, like, is this a medium, high, or immortal world?" Kent asked.</p>
<p>"Pyris is a high world. It is one of the ten Pill Worlds on this side of the galaxy. Of course, with your background, you already know a Pill world is one of the most powerful worlds in many star systems in the high worlds.</p>
<p>Pyris is ranked 7th and is one of the hubs for alchemists. The place we are in right now is the Grand Pyris Pill Pagoda, a 12-floor pagoda where the greatest knowledge one needs on the path of alchemy can be found.</p>
<p>We are currently, as I said earlier, on the 7th floor. And today, there will be a duel between two Pill Kings. I don’t know if you know them, but they are two of the best Pill Kings in the Pyris Pill Alliance.</p>
<p>The fact that you are here should be a blessing for you, as you will witness some impeccable flame control techniques that only a few are privileged to study.</p>
<p>I heard there is a secret chamber in the pagoda where those who are privileged to enter will come into contact with a piece of the Grand Pill Tablet, a relic left by the first Pill Celestial."</p>
<p>Kent’s heart shook when he heard the Grand Pill Tablet. The word sounded familiar to him, and that suddenly ignited his hunger to see this tablet or the piece of it currently hidden inside the Grand Pyris Pill Pagoda.</p>
<p>"Do you happen to know the name of this Pill Celestial?" Kent asked, hungry to know more; however, before Suoh could answer him, a Pill Emperor entered the large hall, causing everyone to quiet down immediately.</p>
