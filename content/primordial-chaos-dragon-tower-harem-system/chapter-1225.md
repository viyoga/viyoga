<p>It wasn’t just Kent; even his ladies were drawn to the answer Elwen gave them.</p>
<p>Hearing that she also has the Extreme Pure Yin Body, they all seemed eager to learn more about it. Even Runa and Adina were anxious to know more about their body constitutions and see what lies ahead for them.</p>
<p>Being prepared early is always better than entering the chaos without a plan. Vexthra managed to get them some info on their body constitution.</p>
<p>However, she wasnt able to get much since the Extreme Pure Yin Body is very rare.</p>
<p>Now they have someone with the same constitution, and, judging by what she has said and revealed, she knows more than they could ask for.</p>
<p>So instead of getting down to business on why Indie has called them, she told Elwen to speak more about the Extreme Pure Yin Body constitution.</p>
<p>"We all want to know, Elwen, so why don’t you tell us what you know?"</p>
<p>Elwen nodded and started to speak...</p>
<p>"Naturally, there can only be one Extreme Pure Yin Body in an era. The only time a second Extreme Pure Yin Body appears in the same era is if the one wielding it dies or contaminates their body before awakening the True Extreme Pure Divine Yin Body."</p>
<p>Everyone turns and stares at Runa and Adina.</p>
<p>The two are in the same era, yet they both have the Extreme Pure Yin Body. It is strange, and coming from someone who has the same body, the ladies were a little taken aback.</p>
<p>As for Kent, he linked it to their past, and so he knew this all had fate’s handwriting over it. Fate likes to play games, and perhaps this is one of her games.</p>
<p>"Of course, as to why they both have the Extreme Pure Yin Body, I can’t explain. However, what I do know is that the Extreme Pure Yin Body is one of the greatest body constitutions in the universe.</p>
<p>It gives us supreme control over the Yin Qi, which, in other words, gives us control over ice. However, those are just added bonuses to what the True Extreme Pure Yin Body can do." Elwen paused for a moment and let what she said sink in.</p>
<p>"At the core, most will say having the Extreme Pure Yin Body makes you pure and unblemished. However, as someone who has had this body constitution my whole life, I know that its true potential appears from the immortal stage through to the god stage.</p>
<p>It will make things a thousand times easier for us.</p>
<p>Forming Immortal Qi becomes as easy as cultivating spiritual Qi. In fact, with such a body, once you become an immortal, the Yin Qi stored in your core automatically forms Immortal Qi for you, allowing your control over ice to reach the next stage.</p>
<p>You will become undefeated at the Root Immortal stage right off the bat.</p>
<p>Second, once you awaken your body constitution and attain ice affinity, you will have control over the Absolute Ice from the start."</p>
<p>"Really?" Runa asked, her expression showing she was happy to hear that... "I learnt Absolute Ice is the peak of ice a cultivator can attain."</p>
<p>Elwen smiled and shook her head, "Maybe to you mortals. However, from what I know and have experienced, that is not even close."</p>
<p>"How so? Are there other forms of ice higher than Absolute Ice?" Adina asked. It is not every day that what one believes turns out to be wrong, so she wanted to know.</p>
<p>"Absolute Ice is the start. The next ice, even colder and more potent, is the Extreme Ice. That is what I have now. However, from research, I found out there is another called Ultimate Ice that comes right after the Extreme Ice.</p>
<p>But aside from that, there is even one that was only rumoured, but I never saw it. It is called Primordial Ice. Naturally, without the Extreme Yin Pure Body constitution, most can only reach the Absolute Ice.</p>
<p>Of course, there are other ice constitutions out there that can go beyond the Absolute Ice stage. However, the Extreme Pure Yin Body is a sure way to reach Ultimate Ice one of these days.</p>
<p>However, if you manage to awaken your constitution without tainting your body, then trust me, once you become an immortal, you two will become a force to reckon with."</p>
<p>"Looks like you ladies are monsters in human form," Unity said with a broad smile. Her sisters were also thrilled to hear that two of them have this much potential.</p>
<p>"I can only wait and see what you ladies turn out after your True Name awakening," Selene said, her expression brightening up.</p>
<p>"What did you just say?" Elwen asked, her brow furrowed, eyes locked on Selene. The latter smiled and turned to Kent, who just shrugged. He was more focused on the chilling potential of his two virgins.</p>
<p>Selene turned to Elwen... "We all have True Names, you see."</p>
<p>"But that shouldn’t be possible," Elwen said, looking even closer at the ladies now... "True Names are something the universe gives to those who have made significant contributions. The last I checked, to earn that, it takes a lot."</p>
<p>"Maybe we have done excellent service to the universe, so she rewarded us." Unity smiled and added, "In any case, we all have True Names, and since we have someone who can invoke True Names as our man, Runa and Adina will soon be getting theirs."</p>
<p>"True. Our husband is not an ordinary person, you see," Indie added, making sure she gave Kent a wink.</p>
<p>The seven silent Claws turn to Kent and narrow their eyes at him once more...</p>
<p>The Chaos Dragon merely smiled, feeling some pride bubbling in his chest.</p>
<p>"I think we know you," Thira, one of the silent Claws, said, walking toward Kent. She got too close, in fact, allowing her to sniff him.</p>
<p>"Although you are not him because you have no Chaos aura, I know you are him." She moved and stood behind Kent.</p>
<p>"Thira, play nice," Indie warned, knowing Thira’s character. She wasn’t worried they would kill him, but she was concerned about what they would put him through.</p>
<p>"You are Lord Chaos, aren’t you?" Thira said, or more like asked.</p>
<p>Kent smiled and responded:</p>
<p>"I don’t know what you are talking about."</p>
<p>"I knew it," Thira’s eyes sparkled with interest. The rest of her sisters also walked, surrounding Kent and shielding him from his wives.</p>
<p>"So you are Lord Chaos, huh. Looks like destiny didn’t play a game on us this time around." Nyssa placed her slender hands on Kent’s shoulder and gave him a smile that didn’t reach her eyes.</p>
<p>Indie quickly teleported to Kent’s side and glared at her guards...</p>
<p>"Relax, Empress. He is much weaker now, so we wouldn’t do anything to him." They all smiled wickedly.</p>
<p>This time, Kent felt a chill run down his spine.</p>
<p>"Knowing him, his strength will soon skyrocket. So we will wait for him to reach the stage where we can have some sparring sessions." They moved away from Kent with smiles that made the Chaos Dragon decide instantly.</p>
<p>’I have to tame these wild ones before they break me,’ Kent smiled quietly.</p>
<p>This time, it was the seven ladies who felt chills down their spines.</p>
<p>Selene, who saw this smile, became happy. She tapped into the connection between herself and Kent and gave him her support.</p>
<p>’Fear not, love. You will have them screaming soon enough.’</p>
<p>Kent smiled and replied, ’I will depend on you, cupcakes.’</p>
