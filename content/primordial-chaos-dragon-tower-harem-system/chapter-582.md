<p>"Would he truly do that?" the Enchantress asked, not wanting to believe what she had just heard.</p>
<p>"I believe he will do just that. You know him, the real him. You know when he loves something, he will do his utmost best to protect it.</p>
<p>He protected me once, and so did you, so don’t think for one moment he wouldn’t do the same for his wives.</p>
<p>The question you should be asking now is whether we are doing enough to protect what he loves.</p>
<p>We have limitations on ourselves because we are cosmic beings. However, are we doing enough to protect what he holds dear?</p>
<p>Can we face him when his expectations are not met? I know I will do everything in my limited power to protect him, and I know you will too. But would that be enough?"</p>
<p>The two ladies went quiet, digesting everything as they listened to Kent speak.</p>
<p>"Don’t think too much about it. I am not doing all this training, so I will one day watch my ladies get slaughtered. I won’t allow it, so don’t take my words to heart.</p>
<p>Just take it as me messing with you," Kent laughed and then sipped his tea.</p>
<p>"Alright, you can go ahead and answer my questions." Kent leaned back and started to wait for the lady to answer.</p>
<p>She was shocked, but she had to answer his questions and tell him the reason she was there.</p>
<p>"I am indeed Zaila’s past, the Serpent Queen. I died during the primordial era, so this is just a strand of my consciousness. Not much is known about me simply because I wiped everything about me before I died.</p>
<p>However, I left this consciousness in my Primordial spirit before dying. It was to allow me to meet the one who dares to call himself my husband and test his mindset on how much he can handle.</p>
<p>I am a lot of work, you know, so I wouldn’t want a man who couldn’t handle me." She and Kent also smiled.</p>
<p>"Oh, trust me, I can handle you alright," he licked his lips, making the lady blush. Divine consciousness or not, the brat would make her blush, especially knowing she is Zaila’s past self.</p>
<p>The Kent now and the Kent from a few minutes ago are completely different. This one is more shameless and easygoing, and the previous one is more wicked, sinister, and evil.</p>
<p>"I hope you can keep up after her awakening," the Serpent Queen said with a smirk.</p>
<p>"Trust me, this Chaos Dragon got you covered," Kent answered, and the lady looked at him calmly but with questioning eyes...</p>
<p>"Chaos Dragon?" she asked.</p>
<p>"Yup. The Chaos Dragon." Kent smiled and winked at her, making the Serpent Queen gasp and close her mouth with those slender arms.</p>
<p>"There is no need to think too much about it. Now, my second question," Kent said with a smile.</p>
<p>In the vast universe, some things or people cannot be spoken about; one of such people was the Chaos dragon.</p>
<p>Vexthra tried, and she nearly died, so Kent knew better now.</p>
<p>"For your second question, the answer is no. What I showed you was just an illusion I created from your happiest memories. The reason behind it was to see how you would handle regret.</p>
<p>And from what happened, let’s just say I underestimated how much you love your wives," the Serpent Queen smiled.</p>
<p>"For the third question, the memories are yours. As to whether they will affect you, I don’t know. But now you know, and what you do with it is entirely up to you."</p>
<p>Kent nodded. "Thanks for answering my questions."</p>
<p>The Serpent Queen nodded and decided to get right down to business. She didn’t have much time to begin with, and now her time had also been cut short due to the turn of events.</p>
<p>"I brought you here because I want to tell you two things. The first being my primordial spirit. I know you met her already, so I will get right to it...</p>
<p>I want you here because of the Ancient Tempering Technique every primordial spirit comes with. This has something to do with my becoming a supreme in the future."</p>
<p>Kent focused his attention on what she was saying.</p>
<p>"I know you already know there are two paths to Supremehood: the Dao path and the Ancient path. The technique I am talking about is for the Ancient Path.</p>
<p>The Ancient path is that after you become a True God, you must create a god body and make it undergo the four divine tribulations.</p>
<p>However, what if I were to tell you this path can start being traversed from the Tier 1 stage?"</p>
<p>"Are you saying you can create a god body from the start?" Kent asked with a raised brow.</p>
<p>"I... Zaila already has a god body. Primordial spirits are the better versions of god bodies." Kent’s brow raised even further.</p>
<p>"God bodies are also called Soul bodies. The gods, out of their pride and arrogance, choose to call it God body. Immortals even call it an immortal body.</p>
<p>And since primordial spirits are the transcended form of Soul bodies, essentially, they are better versions of god bodies."</p>
<p>"Unbelievable," Kent could only smile, hearing something this ridiculous, yet somehow it made sense.</p>
<p>Kent asked, "So you want me to give a technique to your primordial spirit?" The Serpent Queen shook her head.</p>
<p>"I already have the technique or will have it after the awakening. What I want you to do is to make sure I start using it from Tier 1. This is a secret I learned when I became a supreme during the primordial era.</p>
<p>Make sure the new me starts using it from the start. This will make this much easier in the future."</p>
<p>"But she is at Tier 18. I say it’s already too late," Kent said.</p>
<p>Zaila’s primordial spirit is already at Tier 18 in mind cultivation, so asking her to start from Tier 1 is impossible.</p>
<p>"That is the second reason you are here, to give you this." An orb appeared in her palm, and she gave it to Kent. The moment he touched it, it dispersed into sparks and entered his forehead.</p>
<p>"That technique will allow her to either transfer her cultivation to Zaila or use it to expand her mind space. I recommend her mind space. You can also give it to her sisters after they awaken their primordial spirits.</p>
<p>Knowing how smart I was, I have no doubt she would find a way to help her sisters awaken their primordial spirits." The serpent queen smile.</p>
<p>"Very well. I will do that." Kent saw her body start to fade, so he knew he had to hurry with his questions.</p>
<p>After a few exchanges with the Serpent Queen, during which Kent even learned the secret she promised, Kent asked a question that made the Serpent Queen smile.</p>
<p>"Were you and the Chaos Dragon a thing during the primordial era?"</p>
<p>"No, but I wish we were..." Her smile turned into a cute laughter as she faded away.</p>
<p>"I look forward to loving you, Chaos. Don’t disappoint me this time..." She laughed as she faded away completely.</p>
<p>Kent’s vision returned to the Soul Tower.</p>
<p>At the same time, a powerful energy descended into the tower, and an enormous yet elegant-looking red and golden serpent appeared and stopped behind Zaila.</p>
