<p>Eventually, only one hour remained before the tower closed.</p>
<p>But a lot had happened.</p>
<p>First, those destined to fail still couldn’t even comprehend a heavenly grade flame art. No matter how hard they tried, they couldn’t accomplish it.</p>
<p>It may be harsh to say, but spending resources on such people would be a waste, for they lack the natural talent to even try without external help.</p>
<p>While some eventually managed to learn at least one technique, others failed even to scratch the surface.</p>
<p>However, away from there, some managed to reach the second level and even the third level.</p>
<p>The main fun, however, happened on the 4th level.</p>
<p>Some years back, only one person managed to reach that level. This time, 24 managed to get there, and some even went beyond.</p>
<p>Princess Elowen and her friends were told to learn seven techniques each by Kent, for he had helped them reach Tier 12 in soul cultivation before they entered the tower.</p>
<p>So, thanks to their souls enlarging to a rather proportioned degree, they managed to complete their task 36 hours into the 72 hours they would be spending inside the tower.</p>
<p>By the time they each comprehended the seven Mythic grade techniques, the first ten ranks on the display board were occupied by the half-elves.</p>
<p>Kent was in the first spot, with Princess Freya sitting in the second spot, having mastered eight mythic grade techniques.</p>
<p>She is naturally gifted, so comprehending eight mythic-grade techniques was sufficient for someone of her standing.</p>
<p>From the third to the tenth were taken by the half-elves, and the eleventh was taken by Helga, Princess Freya’s friend and maid.</p>
<p>She is the daughter of the queen’s maid and has been friends with the dwarven princess from childhood. They are inseparable in a way, and their talent is almost on par.</p>
<p>Surprisingly, the twelfth spot was taken by Kella, who was so shocked that she didn’t know what to say. She managed to reach the 4th level; however, what she never expected was learning six mythic-grade techniques.</p>
<p>In fact, on the outside, the Elven queen was even more shocked than her. She didn’t know much about Kella, but seeing her name there made her happy to some extent, although not to the extent she had wanted to be.</p>
<p>She expected her geniuses to dominate the charts, but the display board wasn’t on her side at all. Of course, Kella tried, but she couldn’t compete against Kent’s side.</p>
<p>The remaining names were claimed by the seven survivalists who, against all odds, managed to reach the 4th level.</p>
<p>However, when all was done at the 4th level for the eight half-elves, they were ready to head to the 5th level. Kent told them to head there once they finished the 4th level.</p>
<p>So they did, and that was when things began to change. It was unexpected, but it happened anyway.</p>
<p>Princess Freya asked if she could go with the half-elves.</p>
<p>She saw how they arrived at the 4th level and, as someone whose intelligence was now much sharper than before, she recognized that all eight half-eleves weren’t simple at all.</p>
<p>So she gambled, and to her surprise, Princess Elowen accepted her offer, even extending it to her friend, Helga.</p>
<p>However, one person nobody expected to be taken was Kella, who wasn’t even sure why they had taken her.</p>
<p>She merely wanted to try her luck, for at that moment, she didn’t see herself as an elf; she saw herself as a daughter who wanted to make a good life for herself and her father.</p>
<p>She wanted to do what Kent told her to, and so she took a big swing and it caught a fish.</p>
<p>Her request was sincere, and when she was done asking, she received only one answer: "Ok."</p>
<p>Princess Elowen accepted her offer, and for a moment, tears fell from her eyes. However, she recovered, and that was when the eight half-elves began helping the three loads they had taken with them to the 5th level.</p>
<p>As they climbed to the 5th level, Agni allowed Kent to watch. The sight brought a smile to his face.</p>
<p>He never expected the half-elves to be that generous, even going out of their way to take along an elf.</p>
<p>However, after six hours of climbing, they all reached the 5th level.</p>
<p>That was when they discovered Kent’s sleeping body. Initially, they thought something had happened to him, but upon closer inspection, they saw he was just asleep.</p>
<p>This left their jaws hanging by a thread. However, after recovering, they went to inspect the techniques they would be learning.</p>
<p>In the end, all eleven of them found a technique to master, and for hours they were at it.</p>
<p>---</p>
<p>[One Hour Before The Flame Tower Closed]</p>
<p>"Thank you, Agni. I have learned a great deal over the past few hours. I will be sure to apply them all, and perhaps the next time we meet, we can go on adventures together," Kent said, shaking Agni’s hand.</p>
<p>"I am glad I was helpful," Agni replied.</p>
<p>"Before you leave, take this." Agni handed a token to Kent. "It is the core of the Flame Tower. Since I have completed my mission for leaving my consciousness here, the moment the tower closes, I will cease to exist here.</p>
<p>When that happens, the tower will be left masterless. That means it won’t open again. However, I already realize the people of your world gain a lot from it, so I want you to become the master of the Tower."</p>
<p>Kent smiled upon hearing that. He was already a master of a much more powerful tower. But this is also good.</p>
<p>"I will be happy to take over from you."</p>
<p>"Great. Also, in the future, if you have some Flame Arts, you can leave them for future generations to benefit. You can even change the rules, like when the tower awakens and who can enter.</p>
<p>That said, it is time for you to go back.</p>
<p>I know we will meet again one day because I will look for you."</p>
<p>"Take care, then. And be mindful of those chasing after you. When you find yourself in a situation you can’t handle, invoke my name.</p>
<p>I am sure I will find my way to help you. And don’t worry, the universe will not hurt you. So you have my permission to use my name."</p>
<p>Agni went down on his knees and bowed to Kent. "Thank you, Lord Chaos. I am flattered. I will be sure to use your name when I truly need it."</p>
<p>Until then, see you later, Lord Chaos."</p>
<p>Kent felt his vision blur, then he woke up back inside the 5th level.</p>
<p>When he opened his eyes, his gaze landed on eleven people sitting on the ground looking back at him.</p>
<p>But aside from them, four things were hovering before him. One was the small token he would be using to claim ownership of the Flame Tower.</p>
<p>The second and third were the techniques he needed to learn to create Flame Veins and Flame Cores. The last, of course, was a piece of the tome containing the first form of the Finger of Destruction technique.</p>
<p>’I will get into this later,’ Kent waved his hand, and all four items entered his tower storage. He looked at his friends and the three new faces with them.</p>
<p>"So, how many divine techniques were you able to learn?"</p>
<p>Everyone smiled.</p>
<p>"We could only manage one, but that is enough to shape our futures," Princess Elowen answered.</p>
<p>"That isn’t too bad. That said, we should be ready to enter the sect in a week. The future starts there."</p>
<p>An hour later, the tower closed, and everyone was transported out.</p>
