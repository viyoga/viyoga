<p>"Rest assured, master, we will make sure every last one of them is sent to the afterlife," Kaizo said, standing beside the mind-controlled leader of the evil sect.</p>
<p>"I know you will." Kent would have loved to be the one who would take on the mission and kill every last member of the evil sect; however, since he has a more pressing mission at hand, he is letting Kaizo and the legion handle it.</p>
<p>"Be careful, though. There is a god behind the evil sect, and if she catches wind of what is happening, she might just send down her wrath, so be on high alert and always run into the tower when there is danger."</p>
<p>"We will, master."</p>
<p>Kent nodded. He isn’t afraid of his people failing to complete this mission, he knew they will get it done.</p>
<p>What he is more afraid of is them coming on the radar of the evil goddess, the true mastermind behind the evil sect.</p>
<p>Although there will be backlashes for interfering in mundane affairs as a goddess, the backlash also wouldn’t kill them, so most gods will risk it and send down their divine wrath on those who would mess with them.</p>
<p>This is why Kent is skeptical about giving the mission to them.</p>
<p>However, he knew that to get the job done, he must make sure every last member of the evil sect is dead.</p>
<p>Many years ago, after he killed the planet king, who at that time was the leader of the evil sect, Nefeli and the others merely attacked the headquarters of the evil sect.</p>
<p>Of course, they managed to purge the evil sect; however, there were many branches left to sever back then, so the evil goddess tapped into them and brought the evil sect back, much bigger and more evil than before.</p>
<p>So this time, Kent plans to let his people take care of them down to the last leaf. This way, rising back up will be next to impossible.</p>
<p>Of course, the evil sect will always find its way back up, but it wouldn’t be for another thousands of years.</p>
<p>By then, Kent will find a better way to make sure the ten Pill Worlds are safe from them.</p>
<p>Also, his influence will spread far and wide by then, making sure the evil sect thinks a million times before directing their gaze toward just anyone.</p>
<p>"Master, once Goddess Vexthra returns, we will challenge the 5th floor of the Planet Killer Dungeon. I have already informed Uncle Drew before he left for the underworld.</p>
<p>Also, Uncle Drake reached out; he said he is on his way to a planet not far from this place, so we will meet up with him and handle the evil sect before challenging the dungeon."</p>
<p>Kent nodded.</p>
<p>They have only 12 years to clear all 37 floors of the dungeon. So far, they’ve only cleared 4, and from the look of things, the next six floors will be as dangerous as they can be.</p>
<p>However, with Uncle Drew back, the chances of them going even further than 10 floors are rather high. Uncle Drew is at the Death stage, and so are over 6 of his 14 Night Agents.</p>
<p>This means, regardless of the danger they find themselves in, they wouldn’t have to worry much about anything.</p>
<p>Of course, they also run the risk of meeting immortals since the dungeon is akin to a unique world. This means there might even be gods inside.</p>
<p>So taking cautious steps is the most important thing they can do. Of course, Kent would be more than happy to clear the dungeon since once it had been cleared, the floor boss would become his to command.</p>
<p>This means if the final floor boss is a god, he will have a god under his command. This also means he has more to gain than to lose.</p>
<p>"Just make sure to summon me whenever you face a danger you can’t handle."</p>
<p>Kaizo nodded.</p>
<p>Kent had already reached the Spirit Lord stage, so facing even a Death stage cultivator isn’t going to be a problem.</p>
<p>But since he knew anything was possible, he decided to include himself less and focus on his main mission. This way, he legion will grow much stronger than and be able to prove their worth like they wanted to.</p>
<p>After the conversation, Kent joined the legion to attack the first three outposts of the evil sect. Once he made sure his legion could handle the mission, he left it in Kaizo’s capable hands.</p>
<p>His next destination is Planet Tianxu, which is Liora’s homeworld.</p>
<p>It is a planet for humans, Spirit Beasts, and Sky Foxes. Liora, of course, is a human; however, she is also part Sky Fox from her mother’s side.</p>
<p>Kent couldn’t wait long enough to meet his in-laws since, according to Liora, her family on both her father and mother side is still alive, the last time she saw them.</p>
<p>Of course, from the way she spoke, one could tell she hated her father and that side of her family. However, her mother died when she was young, so she had no choice but to live with her father.</p>
<p>Kent is looking forward to meeting them regardless.</p>
<p>Most of his wives are orphans, so meeting his in-laws, in a way, sounds like a wonderful experience to him.</p>
<p>So once he was done putting all ten Pill Worlds under his command by adding them to his Guardian Tower, they left.</p>
<p>He couldn’t add them to his guardian tower when he first came to the Pill Worlds, but now, he will know when they are in danger and do his best to handle it before things escalate.</p>
<p>It will take them five weeks to get to Planet Tianxu, so they have more than enough time to spend together.</p>
<p>Kent plans to use the five weeks to attend to the needs of his wives, especially the new ones he added recently.</p>
<p>So once they entered the ship and left the Pill World, he and Liora entered the tower, where they would have more than enough time to themselves.</p>
<p>Liora also wants to fully become one with Kent, as the rest of his wives have.</p>
<p>Selene, who decided to enjoy the ride, summoned Unity and Alina to ride with her while their man attended to their new sisters.</p>
