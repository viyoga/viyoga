<p>Because Sahir was now a Celestial and also a Supreme Mage, his aura was on an entirely different level. Tapping into space and moving at such a speed was bound to generate a terrifying shockwave.</p>
<p>So the moment he moved and appeared before Kent, the resulting shockwave blasted the dragon backward, sending him flying through space and crashing through several asteroids.</p>
<p>On top of that, Kent’s body was instantly frozen, leaving him unable to stabilize himself.</p>
<p>BOOM! BOOM! BOOM! BOOM!</p>
<p>Sahir, who immediately realized he had made a blunder, facepalmed and moved again, catching Kent before he could slam into a particularly massive asteroid.</p>
<p>COUGH! COUGH!</p>
<p>Kent coughed up blood, his body growing limp.</p>
<p>Kent was perhaps the strongest Immortal in the entire universe, but under the direct force of a Celestial, he was injured so severely that he seemed little different from a mortal.</p>
<p>"Sorry, buddy."</p>
<p>Sahir gently set Kent down on an asteroid and sat behind him, channeling his energy into Kent’s body.</p>
<p>Inside Kent’s soul sea, a very angry dragon roared at the cracked sky. It seems his robust soul was even injuried.</p>
<p>Several years ago, when he had performed the Ice Emperor Ritual and awakened Sahir’s bloodline, something similar had happened.</p>
<p>Back then, he had been frozen like an ice statue for two months.</p>
<p>Had it not been for possessing flame elements at the time, he would have frozen to death.</p>
<p>It was precisely why he had warned Sahir before initiating the ritual this time around.</p>
<p>In the end, however, things seemed still went out of the window and an even painful occurance happened today. Kent swore next time, he wouldn’t bring the bastard back...</p>
<p>Thanks to his monstrous healing abilities, Kent only needed about two days to recover from injuries that could have easily killed an Immortal Lord.</p>
<p>"You crazy bastard, I warned you not to try that again, but clearly, you didn’t listen," Kent said, glaring at Sahir, who held a smile on his face, very happy that his bro was fully healed.</p>
<p>Kent looked at that smile and smirked. He stood up and stretched his body a few times before turning back to Sahir.</p>
<p>"Don’t expect me to hug you, punk. I only hug women."</p>
<p>"Of course I know that," Sahir replied, already aware of this rule. Kevin Sage hated hugging people, especially men, so Sahir wasn’t looking for a hug. However, he would be happy if he got one.</p>
<p>Unfortunately for him, that chance went out the window when he sent Kent flying through asteroids. But he was a big boy, so he didn’t care much.</p>
<p>"So, reincarnation really does exist, huh?" he said, staring at Kent as if he’d seen a stranger, yet this stranger was his best friend and sworn brother.</p>
<p>"It does exist, and the fact that you and I are standing here now is all the proof you need. I reincarnated and was recovering my memories. I only recently recovered my memories of Kevin Sage, and that led me to you."</p>
<p>Sahir furrowed his brow and said in a questioning tone, "You make it sound like Kevin Sage was just one of your many lives."</p>
<p>Kent said nothing, which made Sahir widen his eyes. "Hold on a minute, are you saying what you speculated last time was true?"</p>
<p>Kent smiled and nodded. "Big time. It turns out I was badass in many lives before we met, but I just couldn’t recover my memories back then."</p>
<p>"Unbelievable." A shocked expression appeared on his face. "What kind of lives did you live before we met? And don’t tell me you were the incarnation of the Ruthless Bandit."</p>
<p>Kent grinned. "What if I were? In fact, what if I were the reincarnation of the Primordial Chaos Dragon, the legendary Lord Genesis, the Faceless Assassin, the Divine Beast Tamer, and many more?"</p>
<p>Sahir, who knew Kent well, realized he was subtly revealing the truth.</p>
<p>His eyes widened as shock crossed his face.</p>
<p>In the past, Kevin Sage always speculated that the reason he was better than everyone—and yes, Kevin was that arrogant—was because he believed he had lived previous lives that were influencing him.</p>
<p>Now, it appeared he wasn’t joking at all.</p>
<p>"That explains why you were able to help me tame Little White," Sahir said, a small smile playing on his lips. During the Ice Era, he had an ice dragon mount.</p>
<p>Thanks to this mount, he was able to traverse the starry skies and go places without having to move even a finger.</p>
<p>Of course, what he didn’t tell many people was that the first time he met Little White, he nearly died. That dragon came close to killing him. However, when he asked Kevin for aid, things went rather smoothly.</p>
<p>"Turns out you were the legendary Divine Beast Tamer. No wonder Little White liked you more than even me, his master."</p>
<p>Kent laughed, wondering whether or not that beautiful dragon was still alive.</p>
<p>"How do you feel now?" Kent asked after examining Sahir’s condition with his Omniscient Jade Eyes for a few seconds.</p>
<p>"I think I am stronger than before. Now, I can cast 100 spell circles without having to burn too much of my soul essence. It shows I am getting closer to a breakthrough."</p>
<p>"Good to hear that." Kent walked to the edge of the asteroid and stared down into the darkness below. Then he sighed and turned back to Sahir.</p>
<p>"So, what do you plan on doing next? You have just reincarnated, but you must know that while you were gone, a lot happened, some of it relating to you. What do you plan on doing next?"</p>
<p>Sahir thought for a moment and answered, "I will first return to the Celestial Realm and find out more about what happened to my family after I was gone. I will also use the time to catch up on events from over the years.</p>
<p>I may have been at the top back then, but years have passed, so I wouldn’t be surprised if I am no longer at the peak.</p>
<p>Once that is handled, I will venture into the Ice Abyss and search for Little White’s whereabouts while also training for when you will need my help."</p>
<p>Kent nodded and pressed his finger against Sahir’s forehead. "I have given you permission to access a secret world.</p>
<p>If you need help, come get me. Of course, if it weren’t for the fact that I have things to do, I would have escorted you for a while longer.</p>
<p>However, just like you, there are many others who once walked through thick and thin with me, so I must find their whereabouts and restore their strengths."</p>
<p>"I understand, Sage. I will be sure to stay in touch."</p>
<p>"That is great to hear, Sahir. Say hi to Azirah for me when you reunite with her."</p>
<p>"I will," Sahir said with a smile before extending his hand and summoning his staff. With a simple gesture, the void exploded open.</p>
<p>However, unlike the usual dark maw that opened whenever one wanted to travel through the void, this one manifested as a decorated gateway, its interior filled with icy mist.</p>
<p>"You must become stronger soon, Sage. The last time, we were forced to sacrifice our lives. This time around, I intend to kill every last one of them. So become stronger soon..."</p>
<p>"I will. However, you should also be careful out there. You may be a Supreme Mage, but there are others like you out there, and they would hate to see a rival."</p>
<p>Sahir smirked and stepped into the void gate. "They can try."</p>
<p>The void closed, and he was gone.</p>
<p>After he left, Kent smirked and sat down, taking a pill from his ring and swallowing it.</p>
