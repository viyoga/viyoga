<p>The process took hours but less than a day. The moment the clock struck the 18th hour, Kaizo’s cocoon cracked, and waves of energy poured out.</p>
<p>Golden lightning danced around the cocoon as more cracks started to appear on its surface.</p>
<p>A few minutes later, the cracks widened, and one half of the cocoon fell away, revealing Kaizo’s body. As always, he stood tall and muscular, but now with much longer golden hair and an even more refined physique.</p>
<p>As someone who once wielded the Divine Titan Physique and the Divine Titan Bloodline, Kent knew that Kaizo had awakened both powers, and that was why his body looked even more refined and filled with power.</p>
<p>As more pieces of the cocoon fell away, more of his body was revealed. His body was covered in metallic golden and black armour, with the upper portion sleeveless and the lower half protected by matching armoured trousers.</p>
<p>When the last piece of the cocoon fell, a wave of power that could only be unleashed by a Demigod burst from his body, filling the entire space.</p>
<p>Kaizo had gone from an Immortal King to a Peak Demigod in one go. That was the power of a True Undead.</p>
<p>He had also awakened his Unbreakable Fist Job Class, the same class that had made him a terrifying warrior back in the day.</p>
<p>When he was finally free, he summoned his gauntlets and flexed his muscles, something every brute in his position would have done. After making sure he had examined enough, he looked at Kent and smiled.</p>
<p>"I am finally back, Master."</p>
<p>He had recovered his past-life memories, and so he knew that Kent was the same person as Sanders, the Bone Lich Undead Emperor.</p>
<p>Sanders was Kent’s past incarnation, one who had lived during the Undead Era. He was the creator of the Undead Legion, a force led by the Undead Titan, Kaizo.</p>
<p>Now that he had awakened his true self, there was no telling what he would do next.</p>
<p>"Glad to have you back, Kaizo," Kent said with a smile before turning toward Zareth’s cocoon, which had also started to crack.</p>
<p>"ROARRRRRRR!"</p>
<p>"HISSSSSSS!"</p>
<p>A powerful roar and a dangerous hiss erupted from the cocoon as two beams of light shot into the air, and a wave of terrifying suppression descended upon everyone.</p>
<p>High above, the Alabaster World Serpent and the Obsidian Void Dragon appeared, each colossal and terrifying in their own right.</p>
<p>Kent, who had been the one to create Zareth in the first place, widened his eyes when he sensed the power level of the two beasts.</p>
<p>’True Gods. Then does that mean Zareth is also a True God?’</p>
<p>His thoughts, however, were interrupted when the cocoon fell apart, and Zareth was revealed. He was a Demigod, but he possessed the aura of a True God. Immediately, Kent linked that anomaly to the two beasts.</p>
<p>Honestly, even he didn’t know why these two beast spirits had come with Zareth in the first place.</p>
<p>From what he could remember, when he used the bones of the Alabaster World Serpent and the Obsidian Void Dragon to create him, he had expected a man with affinities for Void and Poison to appear, which he did. However, he had also awakened an affinity for Spirits.</p>
<p>This affinity granted him the ability to summon the two beast spirits and even merge with them, which would raise his power to an entirely different level.</p>
<p>Right now, he had awakened his Dual Coiled Beast Ancestors Bloodline and his Abyssal Warlock Job Class.</p>
<p>Most importantly, all three elements he had awakened were already at the Absolute Rank. He was also a Peak Demigod and had awakened the Spirit Symbiosis skill, an ability that allowed him to merge with the two Beast Spirits.</p>
<p>His skin remained pale, but now his hair had been braided into large locks that resembled living serpents. When he stepped down from the air, his spear appeared beside him, and a smile tugged at the corner of his lips.</p>
<p>"It is good to be back," he muttered, already planning which beast he would devour next in order to strengthen both himself and his two Beast Spirits.</p>
<p>Just when Kent was about to ask Zareth a few questions, Sylara’s cocoon cracked, and waves of blood qi filled the space.</p>
<p>Kent suddenly found himself surrounded by a dense blood mist, and before he could pull himself back, his consciousness was dragged away, appearing before a lady clad in crimson robes, seated upon a terrifying throne forged entirely from bones.</p>
<p>Kent recognised the lady at first glance. After all, it was none other than Sylara in her true form. She possessed a single eye, and with his Omniscient Jade Eyes, Kent could finally tell that the eye can do far more than simply fire destructive beams.</p>
<p>"I suppose you brought me to your soul space because of our agreement, Sylara," Kent asked, and she nodded.</p>
<p>"Indeed, Master. I was created from a single bone. However, the complete skeleton is located somewhere within the Undead Monarch’s personal vault. We must retrieve it so I can become whole."</p>
<p>"We will get it, Sylara. I promise that this time, we will recover your complete skeleton."</p>
<p>"Okay, Master."</p>
<p>She smiled, and the next second, Kent found himself back in his body.</p>
<p>"What a powerful Blood Qi," Velyra muttered, her brow furrowed as she studied the blood mist filling the surroundings.</p>
<p>As someone who also cultivated Blood Qi, she could immediately tell that Sylara, in her true Undead form, was a monster.</p>
<p>Sylara stepped out with silent, graceful steps.</p>
<p>She was clad in a far more refined set of armour this time. She wore a pair of black trousers lined with crimson stripes and a long-sleeved shirt that clung tightly to her body and was zipped all the way to her neck.</p>
<p>A pair of daggers rested at her waist, while the claws extending from her fingers remained as elongated and deadly as ever.</p>
<p>Back in the day, she was known as the Crimson Harbinger, a terrifying assassin whose mere presence was enough to decimate entire battlefields.</p>
<p>Like the others, she had emerged as a Demigod, but only at the High Rank, unlike Kaizo and Zareth, who had reached the Peak Rank.</p>
<p>She had also awakened her Abyssal Reaper Job Class, while her affinities for Blood and Void had both reached the Absolute Rank.</p>
<p>Her Primordial Bloodmoon Wraith Bloodline had also awakened, bringing with it several powerful bloodline abilities.</p>
<p>The moment her awakening concluded, chains began to dangle through the air as a sudden storm filled with crimson lightning engulfed the entire space.</p>
<p>This signalled the awakening of the infamous Vaelros, the Crimson Tyrant.</p>
<p>"Everyone, move back," Kent instructed, and everyone immediately retreated farther away.</p>
<p>While the storm of crimson lightning was unlikely to kill any of them, creating some distance still seemed like the most logical decision.</p>
<p>As they moved back, the rattling sound of chains grew louder and louder.</p>
<p>CLANG! CLANG! CLANG!</p>
<p>The crimson storm overhead became increasingly violent as terrifying waves of energy washed across the area.</p>
<p>BOOOOOM!</p>
<p>Suddenly, an explosion rocked the surroundings.</p>
<p>High in the air, Vaelros’s body hung suspended amidst countless thick crimson chains.</p>
<p>His long red hair flowed freely behind him as waves of terrifying aura poured endlessly from his body.</p>
<p>"Looks like he awakened both his Chain Sovereign Class and his second class, the Abyssal Doombringer," Kaizo muttered.</p>
<p>Kent nodded in agreement.</p>
<p>Vaelros was renowned for his chains, and that explained much about his origin.</p>
<p>He possessed affinities for Chains, Lightning, and Darkness, all three of which had already reached the Absolute Rank.</p>
<p>His Abyss Chain Titan Bloodline allowed him to channel abyssal energy as part of his already versatile arsenal of power sources.</p>
