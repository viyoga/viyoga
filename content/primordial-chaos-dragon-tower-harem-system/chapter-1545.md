<p>Kent only spent a couple of minutes with his wives before leaving with Vexthra and Miryssa to meet the High Priestess.</p>
<p>"You should know that in order to secure the dragon vein, we had to use your name to convince. Even so, they seem to have some trust issues, so you must do well to convince them, especially the High Priestess."</p>
<p>Kent nodded. Convincing women is his field of expertise.</p>
<p>His wives had no worry on that front, so soon, they entered a large hall where the elders and the High Priestess had been waiting for them.</p>
<p>The moment Kent stepped into the hall, eyes turned to him.</p>
<p>He retracted his dragon horns so only his slightly longer ears could prove he was a dragon. And maybe his eyes, since they were a bit primal rather than mundane.</p>
<p>At the head of the hall, a blue-haired beauty sat on a throne, her gaze locked on him.</p>
<p>’A demigod, huh.’</p>
<p>Kent smiled inwardly, feeling that the High Priestess was trying to pry into his secrets. He knew she would never succeed, so he showed no expression to suggest otherwise. Instead, he maintained his stride, and before long, they were standing before everyone.</p>
<p>"Hi everyone, this is Kent Madson."</p>
<p>Kent smiled. "Nice meeting you all, amazing people, and sorry for making you wait."</p>
<p>None of them said anything. Rather, they turned to the High Priestess, who remained calm, her gaze still locked on him. A few minutes later, she sighed and gestured for them to sit.</p>
<p>Kent and his two wives walked and sat down on the chairs reserved for them.</p>
<p>"So you are Kent Madson," the High Priestess asked.</p>
<p>"That is me."</p>
<p>"I see. Considering the kind of fame you have, I expected someone very scary, not a harmless boy."</p>
<p>Kent smiled handsomely. "I get that a lot, High Priestess. When people learn I am Kent Madson, they assume I am some kind of monster who would make even the gods break into a cold sweat.</p>
<p>The truth of the matter is, I am harmless but also extremely dangerous when I have to be. That said, I am sure we are not here to talk about how harmless I am. We are here to talk about whether or not I have what it takes to protect you and your people."</p>
<p>Kent smiled and turned to Vexthra and Miryssa. "Let’s pretend these two beautiful ladies aren’t literal goddesses."</p>
<p>Their eyes widened. Clearly, Vexthra and Miryssa failed to tell them they are goddesses.</p>
<p>Kent didn’t allow them to interrupt him, though. "If you and your people move into the place I have in mind, first of all, you will have nothing like poverty chasing after you. I will provide everything from food to a place to sleep.</p>
<p>Of course, since you are people and people require a sense of purpose, while I will provide cultivation resources, you will also have a way to work and earn money for your own expenditure.</p>
<p>Second, you will have the chance to interact with others. There are humans, Foxkins, Elves, Bloodveins, and a few others. It is still a rising city, so you will see more later.</p>
<p>Third, let’s talk about security. Aside from my dear wives, there are several immortals, demigods, a god soon to be a supreme, and, well, an existence that can even scare away a celestial there to protect you and your people.</p>
<p>Lastly, while I can’t tell you why just yet, know that when it comes to me, there is no telling just what is impossible. So far, I have appeared on three Dao Tablets, and while I hate to brag, the entire Cult of Wisdom and Pill Council wants me to join their ranks. In fact, they have hired the best of the Midnight Shadows to protect me from the shadows.</p>
<p>So ask yourself, even if you all are in danger, which I doubt would ever happen, wouldn’t the reason that I would also be in danger be a more than enough reason for the Pill Council or Cult of Wisdom to send down their aid to protect me and, by extension, protect you all..."</p>
<p>There was no sound for ten minutes.</p>
<p>They all seemed to have a lot to think about. In the end, a sound decision was made. "We accept your explanation and will take you up on your offer."</p>
<p>Kent smiled. "A sound decision, High Priestess. I do believe my fine ladies here explained the dynamics of where you are going to you."</p>
<p>She nodded. "They did, and we accept."</p>
<p>Kent asked no further questions and merely turned to Vexthra. "I believe it is time to send them to their new home."</p>
<p>She smiled and connected with her sisters.</p>
<p>They all flew away, some teleporting to the other cities. The High Priestess had already announced that there was a chance they would be migrating to a new place, so they had all started preparing.</p>
<p>Meeting Kent was a formality, and to be frank, she was impressed by what she heard.</p>
<p>She had already planned to move her people to a different planet since every single day they faced all kinds of danger, be it from mutated beasts or unstable void tears that seemed to appear at random.</p>
<p>The offer to move into the tower was sound, and thanks to Vexthra and Miryssa, they agreed. Now, she merely tapped into some formations and announced to everyone that they would soon be moving out.</p>
<p>With space rings at their disposal, they quickly packed their stuff and were ready.</p>
<p>Vexthra gave a new command, and soon the portals were opened.</p>
<p>Before long, they started to move into the tower, some even jumping through only to land on a clean surface made from the finest jade. Before them was a large city spanning several thousand kilometers.</p>
<p>It was vast and beautiful, leaving every last one of them stunned for a moment when they laid eyes on it. It took some of them several moments to come to terms with the fact that this was the place they would be staying for the rest of their lives.</p>
<p>Meanwhile, back on Planet Rainy, Kent felt his soul universe expanding with every soul that entered the portal. In no time, he felt as if he were about to break through, that his soul would rise far beyond Tier 19.</p>
<p>That didn’t happen. However, he knew his soul had become much stronger.</p>
<p>He was even sure he would be able to surpass an Immortal Lord in terms of soul strength whenever he ascended to the next rank.</p>
<p>It took hours for everyone to enter.</p>
<p>At some point, he was even unsure whether the city could hold all of them. Thankfully, even after all 16,590,000 of them entered, 20% of the houses remained unoccupied.</p>
<p>When the portals were closed, Kent saw a different kind of message appear, putting a smile on his face.</p>
<p>[Master, I will be unavailable for two days to undergo an impromptu upgrade]</p>
<p>Kent was happy, so even when he lost access to the tower, he didn’t mind. Thankfully, he had no reason to go into the tower for now since he had to handle his Chaos Heart creation and dragon body tempering.</p>
<p>Of course, if he needed anything, Gaia was available to help.</p>
<p>For now, with Miryssa, Vexthra, Inaya, and Ainna protecting him, he had nothing to worry about. Vexthra led him to the dragon vein, and she even aided him in extracting all the Dragon Spirit Essence.</p>
<p>Once that was handled, they moved to a place they had already prepared, and after he entered, they sealed it and began guarding him.</p>
