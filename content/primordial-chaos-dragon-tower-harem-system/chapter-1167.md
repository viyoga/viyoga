<p>"KILL THEM"</p>
<p>The order came from a tower standing 70,000 km away.</p>
<p>The moment it did, every last warrior charged forward, aiming for Kent and Uncle Drew’s lives.</p>
<p>Their secret must die with Kent and Uncle Drew, who had casually invaded their planet using a portal meant for their warriors sent to Fedora.</p>
<p>Thousands charged at Uncle Drew and Kent, and thousands more started arriving on the scene.</p>
<p>The last thing they wanted was for their secret to be leaked to the outside. That would be certain death for everyone, including children. They knew the universe hates the forsaken race and those who sympathize with them.</p>
<p>All those affiliated with them are prosecuted and killed without any mercy.</p>
<p>During the Primordial Era, the forsaken race caused so much harm, killing many regardless of their nature or origin. Many innocents died due to their evil ways.</p>
<p>It got to the point that the heavens had to intervene. Thanks to their intervention, the forsaken race was sealed in their worlds and could not leave.</p>
<p>They even made sure that no matter how strong they were, they would remain sealed in their world.</p>
<p>Of course, the rules did apply to all those who would aid them. However, there is a punishment for them.</p>
<p>The heavens would mark them as traitors, and as such, killing them all would be doing the universe a big favor.</p>
<p>All those who kill sympathizers of the forsaken race gain favours from the universe in the form of good karma. So whenever a world is marked as traitors, not even children will be spared.</p>
<p>It is a dark universe after all.</p>
<p>However, there are loopholes to everything. The forsaken race had found them, and through the allies they formed with the normal worlds, they started worming their way back into the universe.</p>
<p>Naturally, Kent was unfriendly toward them.</p>
<p>They took his one true love from him, and for that, he would make them pay. He had already recovered enough of Chaos’s memories and saw how Elina’s death had changed Chaos.</p>
<p>Back then, became so bitter that when he finally came from the Blood System, worlds bled.</p>
<p>He killed so much that it got to the point that all worlds that had dealings with the forsaken race started confessing in an attempt to save their skins.</p>
<p>However, regardless of their pleas, overnight, millions would die.</p>
<p>That was how much Chaos hated the forsaken race, and now, he still seems to hate them.</p>
<p>So, watching thousands of warriors who were affiliated with the forsaken race charge at them, his anger boiled up.</p>
<p>Kent’s bloodline power and blood seals exploded, and at once, all the warriors charging at him came to a halt and dropped to their knees.</p>
<p>Those flying in the air crashed to the ground, raising dust.</p>
<p>"I was prepared to only kill the leader of this planet and his immediate subordinates for bringing chaos into a world under my guardianship.</p>
<p>However, now that I know you people are affiliated with the forsaken race, I am going to kill all warriors regardless of whether you knew or not.</p>
<p>Merely having leaders who went into bed with the forsaken race is a stain on your records, and for that, you all must die."</p>
<p>There was no remorse in Kent’s eyes.</p>
<p>The current him is akin to when Chaos found Elina’s body.</p>
<p>His anger is now controlling his heart. However, despite that, he knew his limit and, like Chaos, he would not kill children, pregnant women, and those who couldn’t even lift a sword.</p>
<p>However, he will kill all warriors. After all, that is a promise he made to himself many years ago and will keep to the day all forsaken race is eradicated completely.</p>
<p>"Once all warriors are dead, this planet will be sealed and turned into a low world where cultivation will be a thing of the past."</p>
<p>Even Uncle Drew seemed shocked by what Kent had just said. Downgrading a planet from High to Low is not possible.</p>
<p>But then again, this is Kent...</p>
<p>Uncle Drew understood that when it comes to Kent, there is no telling what is possible and what is not. He is a mystery nobody will be able to understand.</p>
<p>Soon, screams started echoing everywhere.</p>
<p>Thousands of doom shards kept bursting out of the ground, drawing holes through hearts, heads, chests, and many more. It was a gory sight, yet necessary.</p>
<p>’No mercy for the evil ones, I suppose. They called this on themselves.’</p>
<p>Uncle Drew just stood on the side, eating his grapes as he watched the massacre Kent was committing.</p>
<p>"Please, show some mercy. We had no choice but to accept their offer. If we had rejected them, then they would one day come back to kill us," the man who gave the order from the tower several miles away appeared 30 km from Kent and tried to reason with him.</p>
<p>Kent raised his cold eyes and stared at him.</p>
<p>"If the forsaken race came, they would have killed everyone, including babies. At least I will be sparing babies and non-warriors."</p>
<p>With that response, the massacre continued, and all the poor souls could do was wait for their turn.</p>
<p>In no time, tens of thousands had died, but Kent was not slowing down at all.</p>
<p>He may be cheerful and kind. However, when he is angry, he will do everything in his power to make sure the source of his anger is eradicated.</p>
<p>He will eradicate the forsaken race and their helpers one day. For now, he will start with those who choose to go against the universe’s mandate and side with the forsaken race.</p>
<p>---</p>
<p>Meanwhile, back in Fedora, Kaizo and his team were also committing a massacre. They were keeping to their word and making sure they killed all and took no surrenders.</p>
<p>Kaizo had already broken a few bones in King Balma’s body. And he is working toward breaking a few more.</p>
<p>"Please, stop this madness. We want to surrender," King Balma, who was sent crashing into a mountain, said, struggling to stand.</p>
<p>Kaizo hovered in the air, smirked. "If the tables were to turn and you were in our place, would you have accepted our surrender?"</p>
<p>King Balma couldn’t answer. He knew, deep down, that he would laugh and order his men to butcher everyone. He was the kind of savage who takes pleasure in others’ suffering.</p>
<p>He had killed thousands already to reach where he was now. So he most certainly would have cherished the idea of accepting no surrender.</p>
<p>"That is what I thought," Kaizo sneered and broke his knuckles, creating a bone-breaking sound. "Just because you had the strength doesn’t mean you can be arrogant. Trust me, I learned it the hard way.</p>
<p>But then again, when the odds are in your favor, there is no telling what you can do. The point is, you messed with the wrong crowd, and now your reckoning is upon you.</p>
<p>So keep quiet and suffer. Believe me, you will soon have an eternal sleep to think about your life choices."</p>
<p>Kaizo charged at King Balma, who kept taking out one life-saving treasure after another. Eventually, he ran out of life-saving treasures, and so he could only endure the heavy punches landing on him.</p>
<p>Olive and Ivy were also torturing the 34 Heavenly Ascension Stage warriors. They were still alive, but looking at the pain they were in, death would be a mercy to them at this point.</p>
<p>The rest of the legion and Kent’s wives were also having quality time with the orcs and their allies.</p>
<p>All in all, it was a one-sided massacre that would usher the world of Fedora into a new age of peace, one born out of blood.</p>
