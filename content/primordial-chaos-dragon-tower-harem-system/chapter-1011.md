<p>The entire space became completely quiet...</p>
<p>Everybody, regardless of the faction they were from, was quiet.</p>
<p>Not even a peep came from them.</p>
<p>Their gazes were locked on Kent, who stood holding Pill Monarch Jackson’s severed head by the hair.</p>
<p>Nobody, not even Pill Monarch Nefeli, saw that coming.</p>
<p>In fact, nobody saw what happened. It was after Pill Monarch Jackson’s headless body fell to the ground that they realised Kent had moved and was now holding the severed head and a bloody dagger.</p>
<p>"What have you done?" Suddenly, a Pill Sovereign standing with the 4th Pill Alliance, the Zephyros Pill Alliance, shouted, his hand reaching to grab Kent.</p>
<p>However, with a swing of his dagger, the man’s wrist came off cleanly. His painful cry filled the space... further sending chills down everyone’s spine.</p>
<p>"Your hands are too dirty to touch me."</p>
<p>Kent took a step and was now standing several meters away from the Zephyros Pill Alliance stand. However, even so, eyes were still on him.</p>
<p>"Kent, what did you just do?" Pill Monarch Nefeli asked.</p>
<p>Kent looked at her and sighed, "Stay out of this, Pill Monarch Nefeli. This is between me and the Zephyros Pill Alliance. They dare to kill my sister, thinking they can get away with it. Too bad they picked the wrong target.</p>
<p>So stay out of it and let me deal with them."</p>
<p>Pill Monarch Nefeli didn’t know what Kent was talking about, but she could sense he was serious, so in order not to drag the Pyris Pill Alliance any further into the mess Kent had just created, she took a step back.</p>
<p>Of course, Kent used the Soul Map to tell her to play along... so she had double reasons to hold herself back.</p>
<p>"Kid, do you know what you have just done?" another Pill Sovereign asked, exuding his Heavenly Ascension-stage cultivation.</p>
<p>However, Kent merely smirked, feeling the aura blow past him like wind. "I know what I did. I killed scum. What are you going to do about it?"</p>
<p>Everybody felt their backs break into a cold sweat upon hearing Kent’s words. They could tell he wasn’t affected in any way despite being under the suppression of a Heavenly Ascension-stage cultivator’s aura.</p>
<p>In fact, Kent seemed unfazed, almost as if he wasn’t even feeling anything aside from the wind blowing.</p>
<p>Kent looked at the faces of the six Pill Legends in attendance and spat disdainfully, showing how disgusted he felt at the moment.</p>
<p>"You know, I came here with so much respect for you six; however, seeing your expression when this scum taunted Pill Monarch Nefeli, my respect for you has dropped to a different kind of low."</p>
<p>Gasps spread out, causing many people to shudder, despite not being the ones who spoke. However, Kent wasn’t done.</p>
<p>"Kid, do you have a death wish?" A pill Emperor standing with the Solaryn Pill Alliance lung at Kent. However, he merely raised his dagger and muttered a word.</p>
<p>"Kneel"</p>
<p>Despite having a Heavenly Ascension stage cultivation base, he crashed to his knees, his entire body pressed by an invisible force.</p>
<p>"Weak"</p>
<p>Kent turns back to the Pill Legends.</p>
<p>"So what, you’ve heard a youngster who isn’t even at the Pill Saint stage was able to form a Pill ring, so you felt threatened.</p>
<p>But, to maintain your image, you picked someone who had a past with my Pyris Pill Alliance to taunt and hide your faces behind him, waiting to strike and take me down before I grow up to surpass you."</p>
<p>Kent sneered, "The truth is, I am better and will always be better than all of you, so get used to it. However, today I don’t have time for six more scum. Today, I came because of him and him."</p>
<p>Kent pointed his dagger toward a Pill Emperor whose expression was a mix of fear and anger.</p>
<p>His name is Pill Emperor Jonas, the disciple of the now-dead Pill Monarch Jackson.</p>
<p>"75 years ago, you murdered a harmless girl named Nancy." Pill Monarch Nefeli’s expression changed upon hearing a familiar name.</p>
<p>It wasn’t an act.</p>
<p>Hearing the name touched her. However, she said nothing.</p>
<p>"She was just 37 years old. She was full of life and ambition to one day become a Pill Legend, even to transcend beyond that. However, you murdered her, thinking you would get away with it." Kent laughed, his expression turning dark.</p>
<p>"Or what, you thought she was an orphan, that nobody would miss her? I guess you are wrong... You touched the wrong person, and today you will pay with your life."</p>
<p>It was shocking how many people immediately recalled what happened. It seemed that many knew what had happened, yet they all remained quiet.</p>
<p>After all, a Pill Sage is insignificant in the grand scheme of things, so they brushed it aside.</p>
<p>However, Karma never forgets, and today, it came to collect what was owed him.</p>
<p>"You know, for such a powerful Pill organization, you bunch are sure a big disappointment. The Pill Rules clearly stated that all life, regardless of how insignificant, is important.</p>
<p>However, because she was nothing, because she was a mere Pill Sage, you all saw her as nothing. If it were up to me, I would have killed every last one of you and cleansed the filth that had wormed its way into this grand establishment.</p>
<p>However, as I said, I am here for two people. Their time of reckoning is today, and so they will pay with their blood.</p>
<p>However, I will keep a very close eye on all of you. Just because you are a Pill Legend doesn’t give you the right to do as you please.</p>
<p>Today, I will honour my sister’s memories and spare your useless lives. But there won’t be a next time."</p>
<p>Kent stomps on the ground, and obsidian shards burst out, stabbing Pill Emperor Jonax through his legs, arms, heart, and head.</p>
<p>After killing him, Kent drops the head.</p>
<p>"If any of you want to say something, now will be the best time. After all, you all want some pointers on how to form pill rings, right?"</p>
<p>The smile that Kent is known for returned to his face, causing many to take a few steps back.</p>
<p>The fear they were in now said it all; Kent is a danger that no one wants to mess with. Even his cute smile felt dark</p>
<p>After five minutes, no one steps forward. Kent sighed and looked toward the direction of the Aerithyne Pill Alliance, his gaze meeting that of Pill Sage Flura’s.</p>
<p>"Good to see you again, Pill Sage Flura, and congratulations on your promotion to Pill Sage stage."</p>
<p>"Likewise, Pill Saint Kent."</p>
<p>Kent smiled, "Let’s have fun in the Pill Ascension realm when it opens."</p>
<p>After saying that, I flew back toward the section where the Pyris Pill Alliance was standing. The disciples around him scattered when he landed on the platform.</p>
<p>Kent didn’t mind; he instead walked toward Pill Monarch Nefeli and muttered, "Sorry..."</p>
<p>"You don’t have to apologise, Kent. You did nothing wrong... You merely avenged your sister."</p>
<p>Kent nodded and sat in a lotus posture.</p>
<p>When he closed his eyes, his consciousness appeared at the shore of a blue river where a certain man was sunbathing.</p>
<p>"Nicely done, Chaos Dragon. That will be five years bought until I come seeking what you owe me."</p>
<p>"Thanks for your generosity, Lord Karma."</p>
<p>The man nodded and waved his hand, and Kent was sent away, back into his body.</p>
<p>"That is sweet of you, Karma. I never knew you had a soft spot for him, too."</p>
<p>"Mind your own business, Fate. Some things are not gossip-worthy for you to pry into." The man closed his eyes, but a small smile appeared on his lips.</p>
<p>"Prepare well, Kiddo; carrying the Karma of a goddess is not something that can be paid easily... Your bad days are coming, so be prepared."</p>
