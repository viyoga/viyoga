<p>Hearing Kent’s grim words clearly didn’t sit well with the leaders of the Bloodvein tribe. They clearly didn’t expect to be told they had ten years left to live.</p>
<p>"Clearly, you must be joking, Kent. Though the battle is coming, we don’t know the outcome, yet somehow you say we will be dying?" Velyra asked, not accepting Kent’s words.</p>
<p>"I didn’t know what else to say, but from my investigation, I found out your tribe will be wiped out in ten years—if you remain in this domain, that is."</p>
<p>"But how? And why can’t we live here after ten years?" Tribe Chief Malthar asked.</p>
<p>"That is because your land is poisoned, and, well, your bloodline has also been poisoned. I only found this out when I overheard the Tribe Chief and his elders talking about it.</p>
<p>It seems they’ve been planning this for a while now." Kent didn’t want to tell them his system told him.</p>
<p>An intense killing qi exuded from the Tribe Chief’s body upon hearing that their land and bloodline were poisoned—and that it had been so for a while now.</p>
<p>Also, the people they treated as friends were the perpetrators. These two things are just too much for them to come to terms with</p>
<p>"But why? Why would people we interact with every day do something like this? We share a good relationship with them, both in this region and outside during explorations.</p>
<p>So just why would they do something like that?" Velyra just couldn’t believe what she had heard. She most definitely could not believe her ears.</p>
<p>"Why else would someone do this if not to take your treasures and make you submit? I don’t know what you know about the outside world where I come from, but these things happen every day. Allies often conspire against each other for their own gain.</p>
<p>This is no different. The only question now is what you will do in response."</p>
<p>What Kent found inside the cave was a Tier 13 spell—something that shouldn’t be possible in this world, considering the limitations placed on it.</p>
<p>However, there indeed existed a Tier 13 spell that had seemingly been active for years.</p>
<p>After examining it, he discovered that it affected the Bloodvein bloodline and even the land. According to the tower’s deduction, they had less than a decade to live in their domain.</p>
<p>This, of course, made Kent wonder who had the means to cast such a powerful spell. He thought long and hard, but he just couldn’t come up with a logical explanation—aside from the fact that it coincided with what happened to the Life Magicians years ago.</p>
<p>"I have a question, though. It’s a long stretch, but do any of you know or have met someone outside this world who isn’t human, dragon, elf, dwarf, beastkin, or demon?"</p>
<p>"No, we’re unaware of anyone like that, considering we killed anyone who wasn’t part of the three major and four minor tribes."</p>
<p>"I see. In any case, the spell is a Tier 13 spell that can’t easily be destroyed. And even if we had the means to destroy it, we wouldn’t want to alert the caster."</p>
<p>"Are you implying the caster is outside this region?" Elder Ureth asked.</p>
<p>"I suspect so. There’s a similar situation in my world, and looking at your situation now, I believe the perpetrator is the same and not from this region."</p>
<p>"Then what should we do?" Velyra asked.</p>
<p>Kent smiled. "That will be the good news. I have two options, which I believe are the best for you. First, I will help you win the battle and even help you claim the lands belonging to the two major tribes.</p>
<p>This means you will kill the majority of them, which I don’t mind doing. However, the chances of you alerting the caster are very high, and I don’t know if your tribe will thrive naturally there when you move.</p>
<p>However, it’s my only option that comes for free, so what do you think?"</p>
<p>They didn’t immediately answer. This option really did sound reasonable; however, the chances of them liking it were low.</p>
<p>"You are right. Our tribe is of blood. We have an affinity for blood, and as such, this domain, which contains both Chaos and Blood Qi, is perfect for us.</p>
<p>Moving to a new domain is not something we can do. Of course, killing every last one of them is something we will do if we have the chance.</p>
<p>Since they can’t spare us, there is no need to spare them. We fight, even with evil."</p>
<p>"I say they are True Evil. But knowing the first option is a no-go, I’ll say the next option is also good, though it comes at a price." Kent’s smile made the Chief, elders, and Velyra gulp.</p>
<p>Those eyes were of a predator who wanted nothing more than to sink his fangs into the veins of his prey.</p>
<p>"Say I have the means to create a new domain where your kind could live in peace, free from danger. Basically, you would live the best life possible.</p>
<p>And the best part? The Mother of Killers is also there, training. So, what do you think?" Kent asked with a smile that said many things.</p>
<p>Name-dropping Neomi was just his way of spicing things up. This way, no matter how expensive the price was, they wouldn’t see it as a loss if they could be near their god.</p>
<p>"Are you being serious?" Velyra asked, the Chief and elders sharing the same questioning expression.</p>
<p>"Why would I lie about something like this?" Kent said with a thoughtful expression. "I just want to help. However, this help comes with a price."</p>
<p>"And the price is?" Chief Malthar asked, looking nervous.</p>
<p>"No need to look nervous. I just want the Blood Heart Stone in exchange for offering you a better accommodation. How does that sound?"</p>
<p>The five people in the room locked their murderous eyes on Kent when he mentioned the name of something that was supposed to be a secret.</p>
<p>They didn’t know how he knew that, but they most certainly didn’t want to take him for granted.</p>
<p>"Just who are you?" Velyra asked.</p>
<p>"Kent Madson..." Kent answered with a smile as he stood up. He knew they needed to think long and hard about his offer.</p>
