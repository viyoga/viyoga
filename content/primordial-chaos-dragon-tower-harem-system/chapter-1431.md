<p>Kent received the Soul Crystals from Kenna.</p>
<p>He could sense the strongest and purest form of soul essence condensed in each crystal. This made him understand why he could advance from Tier 16 to Tier 19 when he created Calix.</p>
<p>So far, aside from using them to increase his soul cultivation, he now knew he could use them to expand, decorate, and strengthen his Soul Universe.</p>
<p>But there is more...</p>
<p>"Are those the only uses for it?" he asked.</p>
<p>"No. I used two to create the waterfall and the darkness domain."</p>
<p>"Oh, so you also can do that, huh?"</p>
<p>"Yeah. To use it, you just have to imagine what you want and activate the crystal by channelling your energy into it."</p>
<p>Kent left his brow furrowed a bit, blown away by how easy and ridiculous it was to use the Soul Crystal. He expected it to be technical, since Calix made it sound like he needed special knowledge to use it.</p>
<p>But hearing how easy it was to use it, he was blown away.</p>
<p>Merely imagining the thing he wanted to create and activating the Soul Crystal was as simple as it could get.</p>
<p>Of course, he knew there was more to it, but for now, he only needed to know this much.</p>
<p>If the process was this simple on the surface, then regardless of how deep he went, there wouldn’t be much to consider, since at the end of the day, he could create whatever he wanted by merely thinking about it.</p>
<p>He already had knowledge of conceptualisation. The Death Star Cannon came about from what he was able to imagine.</p>
<p>If he was able to conceptualise something that grand, then, when it came to creating basic stuff like a weapon or artefacts, he could easily handle it.</p>
<p>One good thing about the Soul Crystal is that its use is instant. The moment the idea was formed, the crystal would form it. It was that simple and fast.</p>
<p>Even better, if he were to master conceptualisation now, when he becomes a god, where all battles are fought through conceptualisation of the laws, he could easily handle it.</p>
<p>So with these Soul Crystals and the Soul Universe in general, if he planned well, he could easily get things done.</p>
<p>"This is awesome to know. I guess I will hold on to them for now."</p>
<p>"You do that. I, on the other hand, have plans to form more since I can see you have added more Immortal-grade Soul Spirits to the fold."</p>
<p>Kent nodded, already pleased to hear that. The more soul crystals, the better.</p>
<p>Kenna was a Core Immortal with two Immortal Qi. Although already powerful enough, if she were to become a True Immortal, things would be even better.</p>
<p>"So what are your next plans aside from wanting to form more Soul Crystals?"</p>
<p>"I want to form Immortal Qi. I only have two, which is too embarrassing for a Core Immortal. So I want to form at least three more.</p>
<p>Thankfully, I have all the resources I need in one place, so I will be spending the next couple of months working on that. I am sure Calix and that weird guy would be doing their own thing to grow stronger, so I must hurry."</p>
<p>"Which weird guy?" Kent asked. He knew she was referring to the third soul warrior.</p>
<p>So far, he had created three Soul Warriors.</p>
<p>The first was Calix, whom he formed from the soul of a demon. The second was Kenna, who seemed to like it when she hugged him. And the third was the Temple Master, who was killed on the last level of the Void Temple.</p>
<p>He was the Void Beast whom Axiom and his pals had kidnapped and tortured when they hijacked the Void Temple.</p>
<p>Kent actually felt pity for him, so he let him off easily. In the end, he came back as a Soul Warrior candidate, so he decided to add him to the Soul Legion.</p>
<p>"That guy only does one thing. He sits in a meditative posture facing a weird-looking mural all day. I tried to talk to him, but he ignored me, so I stopped going to him. Very weird guy."</p>
<p>Kent smiled, already eager to meet his third Soul Warrior. He should like a whole mood.</p>
<p>However, with his soul still healing, he decided to get that handled first. After all, he had time on his hands even while inside the Soul Universe.</p>
<p>From what he had seen so far, it was clear to him that if he played his cards well, he would be walking away after his next mission with so much power.</p>
<p>After all, the stronger Soul Spirits he adds to his stockpile, the stronger his Soul Warriors will become. And the stronger they become, the more he benefits.</p>
<p>For now, he would take things one step at a time.</p>
<p>He would be heading to a Unique Planet soon, so he needed all the strength he could get. Once that was handled, he would think of what to do next.</p>
<p>He asked Kenna a couple more times. Once that was handled, he submerged his entire body into the pool to heal much faster.</p>

<p>[Planet Kenai - 9th Floor of the Planet Killer Dungeon]</p>
<p>Meanwhile, somewhere on Planet Kenai, Axiom, Kailan, and Kol stood behind a tall mountain, evil grins on their faces.</p>
<p>They are lying in wait for the Immortal Monarch heading their way.</p>
<p>Axiom is a Void Architect.</p>
<p>His strongest ability is strategy and crafting. When he appeared on the 9th floor alone, he didn’t panic or even bother reaching out to anyone.</p>
<p>He tapped into the void, which, thanks to his Immortal Monarch strength and the fact that he has 9 Immortal Qi, enabled him to see more than anyone.</p>
<p>Initially, something prevented him from sensing the 7 Calamity Beasts. However, his surveillance revealed that there were many powerful beings on the planet.</p>
<p>So, as an architect, he began crafting void mechanisms to withstand whatever dangers would come their way.</p>
<p>A few seconds ago, one was triggered.</p>
<p>Knowing someone was heading toward them, he pulled his friends away and waited for them to walk into his trap.</p>
<p>"Nice catch, Axiom. I knew you would always find a way for us to have some fun," Kol said, spinning a thick arrow between his fingers.</p>
<p>"True. Standing around and doing nothing is boring. It is like being back inside the Void Temple all over again," Kailan also spoke, leaning on the blunt end of his spear.</p>
<p>Axiom smiled and replied, "I know. So now that we have found a prey, I say we make sure it regrets ever thinking it had cornered us."</p>
<p>Kol and Kailan nodded.</p>
<p>Kailan is an Immortal King with 8 Immortal Qi, while Kol is a True Immortal with 7 Immortal Qi. Just Kol alone would be enough to handle the Immortal Monarch coming after Vexthra and her team.</p>
<p>However, because they were bored, they wanted to team up on him and have some fun.</p>
<p>Seated in a lotus posture not far from Unity’s cocoon, Vexthra smirked and chose not to interfere in their business.</p>
<p>Kent had already told her not to worry about Axiom and his pals. They are destructive and hate following orders, so letting them have fun occasionally isn’t too bad.</p>
<p>As for the Immortal Monarch coming toward Vexthra’s team, just when he came out of the void and was able to move forward, Axiom appeared, and his body froze.</p>
<p>"Greetings. The name is Axiom. I will be your friend for the next couple of hours if you don’t mind."</p>
<p>Axiom snapped his fingers, and reality shifted.</p>
<p>The next second, the Immortal Monarch found himself standing inside the void, surrounded in a triangular formation by three evil-looking immortals.</p>
