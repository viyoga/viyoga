<p>Though they didn’t know Kent, seeing him toss two ingredients into the cauldron at the same time caused them to widen their eyes.</p>
<p>They knew alchemy to some degree, so they understood that extracting the medicinal essence from the herbs was the first step in achieving the goal. Of course, they also knew it was absolutely imperative not to make a mistake at that stage.</p>
<p>If Kent were to make a mistake at the beginning, the pill would come out damaged or incomplete. Extracting essence from two different ingredients simultaneously was simply too reckless.</p>
<p>However, they all knew people who could do that. One of them was the Pill Princess, who dominated the pill world before she was invited to the sect by the Pill Empress herself.</p>
<p>She was able to do what Kent was doing—extracting essence from multiple ingredients simultaneously. It was something just too shocking.</p>
<p>So watching Kent do it was like the biggest shock to them. It was telling them the pill princess wasn’t the only genius to walk the pill path.</p>
<p>However, they also understood that some people are born geniuses, so they could only watch as he tossed the second set of ingredients.</p>
<p>’At this rate, the moment I get my Soul Flame to the 7th Tier, I will be able to extract essence from at least four ingredients. Of course, the aim is to be able to toss all ingredients into the cauldron and extract the essence at the same time.</p>
<p>Both my mental and spiritual strength are at a stage where I can do that. The only thing now is to practice more and raise the quality of my soul flame.’</p>
<p>While the king and his family were shocked when he tossed two ingredients into the cauldron and started dual extraction, Kent was thinking of complete extraction—one where all ingredients are thrown into the cauldron at once.</p>
<p>Then again, geniuses just don’t know when to stop, considering their potential is limitless.</p>
<p>After tossing the fifth set of ingredients into the cauldron and extracting the essence, Kent began the second stage, where he burned impurities and condense the pill.</p>
<p>Naturally, most alchemists tend to burn impurities first before condensing the pill. That is the easiest way to go about pill making.</p>
<p>However, Kent’s method is different. The techniques Kent was using had some major changes. The first was the condensing and burning of impurities.</p>
<p>Kent was merely burning off the impurities and ensuring that the medicinal essence he had extracted didn’t burn away too quickly. Of course, his understanding wasn’t yet deep enough, so he couldn’t achieve complete extraction or purification.</p>
<p>He might want to train for a few more years if that’s his goal. But his techniques allowed him to keep the medicinal potency of the pill at a higher level than most.</p>
<p>The moment that was done, he changed techniques to condense the pill fully.</p>
<p>"It is starting, so get ready, my poison queen," Kent said</p>
<p>Princess Lovina nodded and formed a hand seal. Vexthra moved back with Princess Elowen. "It will hurt for a few minutes, Queen Jascia, so endure it, and it will be worth it in the end."</p>
<p>She blinked, signaling that Lovina could go ahead. She had been through a thousand years of pain. A few minutes wouldn’t break her.</p>
<p>"I am ready," Lovina said, and Kent nodded. A few seconds later, the smell of something fragrant started seeping from the cauldron.</p>
<p>Lovina formed another hand seal, causing her eyes to turn purple. She had activated one of her overpowered skills and pressed her hand against the queen’s forehead.</p>
<p>"Don’t resist the process. It will be over before you know it." After saying that, she circulated her energy and started directing it through the queen’s body.</p>
<p>Her energy went through her body for a few minutes before Lovina changed the circulation path and moved her energy toward her core.</p>
<p>There, beside the withered core the queen formed years ago, was a black core. It was much smaller compared to the withered core, but the energy pulsating from it was much more potent than one would expect.</p>
<p>Her energy enveloped it and then enveloped her withered core, too. Then, from the outside, Princess Lovina’s energy flared.</p>
<p>It was at that moment that the queen felt the pain. "Don’t resist, Queen Jascia. I am merging the Poison Core with your withered core. The moment that is done, you will gain more energy than you can dream of."</p>
<p>The king and his daughter held their breath, fists clenched, as they watched the pained expression on the queen’s face. Thankfully, like Lovina said, it lasted for a few minutes before the pain subsided.</p>
<p>At that moment, Kent was also done with the pill, filling the room with its fragrance. "Here you go." After taking out the pill, he handed it over to Lovina, who quickly brought it to the queen.</p>
<p>"Swallow this and let the energy wash through your body."</p>
<p>The queen did as she was told and swallowed the pill. The energy entered her body, filling her with a potent nourishing energy.`</p>
<p>A few seconds later, what Lovina was after was activated. The feeling of great power, one the queen hadn’t felt before, entered her body and started to rise.</p>
<p>Her eyes widened, causing Lovina to smile.</p>
<p>"What you are feeling is a mutated power formed from your Spiritual Core and the incomplete Poison Core you formed.</p>
<p>I had to complete the formation and merge it so that, in the future, instead of consuming poison to grow your Poison Core and absorbing spiritual energy to strengthen your cultivation, you can do both, regardless of what you consume.</p>
<p>So if you consume poison, your cultivation will also be affected. And the same happens to your poison potency when you cultivate.</p>
<p>This also means that while most people find it hard to build upon their foundation after reaching the peak, you will continue to grow your foundation wider should you choose to stay here forever without ascending."</p>
<p>The king, her daughter, and the queen were visibly shaken, having heard something so ridiculous yet so real. It was like Lovina had just broken the limit placed on the world.</p>
<p>While the queen will be at the Core Formation, her foundation will continue to grow, transforming her into a freak in any medium world.</p>
<p>That is just too much, and to some extent, wasted, should she choose to stay in Sonox. Of course, Kent just smiled, knowing what he knew.</p>
<p>’It was like the universe and the tower knew this would happen, so they gave me something to fix that.’ Kent smiled and watched as the queen’s cultivation base started to rise.</p>
