<p>After Saden and Nyxira brought Dori and the two ladies out, the two passed out when they saw Sylara.</p>
<p>A few seconds later, the other two soul sentinels also returned with seven more, and like the first, they lost consciousness when they saw Sylara.</p>
<p>She looked terrifying, and the blood energy around her was enough to scare anyone.</p>
<p>"Poor souls... They were taken from their families, and there was nothing any of them could have done," Dori said, looking at the nine figures on the ground.</p>
<p>"Do you know them?" Kent asked.</p>
<p>"I only know these two," she replied, pointing at the two ladies who had been brought out with her. They were both Foxkin.</p>
<p>"The one with dark hair is the daughter of a leader of the foxkins, and that is her servant."</p>
<p>"Were you taken with them?" Kent asked.</p>
<p>"Yes. I was their advisor of sorts, helping them handle some diplomatic matters. We travelled for 3 years, but on one of our trips, we encountered an unfortunate incident about a year ago.</p>
<p>Unfortunately, we wandered into this path, and that’s why we’re now here."</p>
<p>"1 year is a lot. They could have sold or perhaps kill you long ago, however they didn’t. I guess that’s to be expected from people who seem to be working for someone," Kent nodded.</p>
<p>"I guess it was good we came when we did, else you would have been sold. However, how did you find yourself in this world?" Kent asked.</p>
<p>"That’s a long story," Dori said, scratching the back of her head.</p>
<p>It turned out she had found herself in that world after discovering they were under the suppression of a tyrant.</p>
<p>When she arrived, the Tyrant Orc King Balma held supreme authority over the world, but thanks to her scheming and planning, a resistance started to rise.</p>
<p>The Foxkin leader Hkun led one such resistance.</p>
<p>He had recently stepped into the ranks of a Spirit Lord. Though he hadn’t reached the level of the Orc King, hope arrived when the Catkin also welcomed their first Spirit Lord.</p>
<p>She and the princess had gone there to negotiate an alliance, and if that was successful, their chances of reclaiming their lands would be high.</p>
<p>Thankfully, their alliance became a reality.</p>
<p>Next was the Tigerkin, who also wanted to reclaim their lands.</p>
<p>However, on their way to negotiate the alliance, the Rabbitkin found them first, and after a week of playing hide-and-seek, they ended up here.</p>
<p>Their families had no idea this had befallen them, and thanks to her being a Mind Weaver, though she couldn’t go against these people, she managed to draw suspicion away from the Foxkin.</p>
<p>This meant that the resistance would rise within the next 15 years if the timeline was correctly tracked.</p>
<p>"So you were building a resistance against the tyrant king," Kent said with a smile. "I never expected you to be a rebel. I guess appearances can be deceiving."</p>
<p>Dori’s cheeks turned red at the compliment. "I just wanted to help them, considering the previous world I came from was also one of beastkins."</p>
<p>"So now that you are free, what do you intend to do?" Kent asked. She looked at the two ladies and then the seven with them and sighed.</p>
<p>"They must first be returned to their homes. However, 15 years from now, a great battle will happen, and they will lose.</p>
<p>For the moment the Rabbitkins sense our presence near the territory of the Tigerkin, the Orc King was made aware.</p>
<p>However, since you are here now, I can say if the resistance doesn’t do anything stupid before the 15 years, which I calculated will be the tipping point, then you can turn the scale for them."</p>
<p>Dori smiled. "I hope Master wouldn’t turn down a good fight." She knew Kent from all his incarnations since she had been there from the beginning, so asking this question was from years of experience.</p>
<p>"Don’t worry; I will transcend within the next six to ten years, so by then, I will be in the element that will allow me to handle this Balma dude.</p>
<p>We can only think of a way to set him back a few years." Kent smiled and looked at Veydris.</p>
<p>Dori also looked at the Metal Knight, who had Void Energy around him.</p>
<p>"Does the Master want to use poison on the Spirit Lord?" Dori asked, and Kent nodded.</p>
<p>Securing a poison wouldn’t be hard for him, considering there are dozens in the tower shop and millions in points to purchase.</p>
<p>Applying it is where the problem is. Kent would have to get close, and that is not an option, so the next best thing is Veydris, who could use the Void to attack.</p>
<p>"Considering he has less than 30% control over the Void, he has a 21% chance of hurting the Orc King.</p>
<p>But if this sister and this brother are added to the equation, then he will have a 36% chance of poisoning him," Dori said, and Kent sighed.</p>
<p>"Just 36%. That is too low."</p>
<p>"That is if they are the only ones. However, statistically, they are the only ones who can attack and wouldn’t die on the spot. This is a Spirit Lord we are talking about."</p>
<p>"What if we wait another 40 hours before we execute these three orcs? I know their boss will come the moment they are executed, and by then, I will have another subordinate on the same level as them. Won’t that increase the odds?"</p>
<p>"That will indeed increase the odds, but these three are supposed to meet a supplier in 6 hours. If they don’t, the chances of the Orc King arriving ahead of time are very high.</p>
<p>So, it can only be done within the next 6 hours."</p>
<p>"Hmm," Kent looked at Veydris, Sylara, and Kaizo.</p>
<p>"You heard the calculator lady; there is only a 36% chance of succeeding in this enterprise. So, no matter what happens, you all attack only once using your strongest moves.</p>
<p>The moment you attack, go back to the tower immediately."</p>
<p>The three nodded.</p>
<p>"Then, Uncle Drew, use your mind-reading skills to know their homes and then use the night to send them away." Uncle Drew nodded, and all the unconscious prisoners were taken away.</p>
<p>"Let’s get ready."</p>
