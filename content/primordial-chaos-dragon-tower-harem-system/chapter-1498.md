<p>The moment Kent’s words came out, the entire restaurant became quiet. Every last person eating dropped their chopsticks, their jaws hanging open wide.</p>
<p>"Hahahahahahaha..."</p>
<p>Suddenly, the Immortal Emperor, clad in polished armor, laughed, his expression turning slightly amused and a bit angry.</p>
<p>He stood up and walked away from his table, leaving the three Immortal King beauties behind. They didn’t seem a bit scared since, in their eyes, their young master would crush the bug who dared disrespect him.</p>
<p>Everyone came to the same conclusion. They recognised who Kent dared to mess with, and so they knew how this would end.</p>
<p>"You want me to go down on my knees and apologize?" he spoke, taking a step forward.</p>
<p>Kent set down his chopsticks again and wiped his mouth with a napkin before turning toward the annoying bastard.</p>
<p>"I believe my words weren’t ambiguous."</p>
<p>The Immortal Emperor stopped, unsheathed his sword, and pointed it at Kent. At once, a wave of killing intent started emitting from his body, causing those closer to him to take a series of steps back.</p>
<p>The waitresses around moved back, some running to the back room to call the manager.</p>
<p>"Do you know who I am?"</p>
<p>"I don’t know, and I most certainly don’t care. All I ask is for you to go down on your knees and apologize."</p>
<p>"Just who is this kid? Does he even know who he is messing with?"</p>
<p>"I don’t think he has any idea. Just look at him; this is my first time seeing him in this place."</p>
<p>"I am sure he is not from around here. But even if that was why he didn’t know who he was messing with, the fact that he is looking down on an Immortal Emperor shows he has a death wish."</p>
<p>"I am sure. Though I wonder why the lady he is dining with seems rather calm despite her companion courting death."</p>
<p>Attention turned to Lene, who was still eating her food as if her Master wasn’t facing off against an Immortal Emperor.</p>
<p>"Before I kill you for the insult, know that when you are gone, your lady friend will become my servant for the rest of her useless life. You should have known better than to mess with the son of a Demigod."</p>
<p>The sword pointed at Kent vibrated, and a wave of divine sword aura gushed out, forming into a three-meter-long sword.</p>
<p>"Now die."</p>
<p>He stabbed his sword forward, forcing the manifested sword to move toward Kent, causing the very air around it to tremble.</p>
<p>Everyone inside the restaurant knew Kent was done for. Some let out a sigh while others shook their heads. However, when the sword arrived before Kent, all of them widened their eyes in shock.</p>
<p>Just when the sword reached his chest, Kent raised his hand and caught it between his fingers. It felt so effortless that before anyone could make sense of what had happened, the sword came to a stop.</p>
<p>"Not impressed."</p>
<p>Kent muttered.</p>
<p>Then he moved his other hand and used his index finger to gently tap the blade of the sword. It let out a sharp ringing sound, and it vibrated before shattering into fragments.</p>
<p>Many gasped, and the Immortal Emperor took a few steps back, sweat breaking on his brow.</p>
<p>Kent merely smiled and pointed his finger forward.</p>
<p>"I am only a Sword King, but watch carefully and learn how it is done."</p>
<p>Sword aura moved along Kent’s arm toward his finger. Once it reached there, it formed into a thin sword which started to grow bigger the moment sword intent was applied.</p>
<p>At once, everyone inside the restaurant sensed a powerful sensation emanating from the sword. That sensation only grew as the sword increased in size until it was three meters long, just like the one he was attacked with.</p>
<p>By then, even people outside the restaurant sensed an overwhelming amount of sword energy coming from the restaurant.</p>
<p>"Who is that? I have never felt Heavenly Sword Energy that pure before," a few wondered, some even rushing into the restaurant to witness who was unleashing such power.</p>
<p>The Immortal Emperor, however, felt like dropping to his knees at once.</p>
<p>While he had already achieved divine sword energy, the feeling coming from Kent’s Heavenly Sword Energy was so overpowering that he felt so small before him.</p>
<p>"You may be a Sword Divine, but my understanding of the sword is far superior to yours." A smirk formed on his lips as he added, "When I was fighting Divine swordsmen, your great-grandfather wasn’t even a sperm in his great-great-grandfather’s balls.</p>
<p>What makes you think you can measure up to me. Now have a taste, and be sure to remember this sensation." The moment the words left his mouth, Kent flicked his finger, and the sword he had manifested shot forward at a terrifying speed.</p>
<p>The Immortal Emperor crossed his sword over his chest, hoping to block the attack. However, when the sword struck it, he was sent flying, blasting holes through the walls of the restaurant.</p>
<p>Every jaw drop, beads of sweat breaking on their foreheads.</p>
<p>"You could have gone down on your knees and apologised as I demanded," Kent sighed and sat back down. He reached into his space ring and pulled out a pouch containing spirit stones, then set it down on the table.</p>
<p>"That should cover my bill and handle the repairs."</p>
<p>Lene set her chopsticks down and reached for Kent’s hand. Then, under everyone’s stunned gaze, they vanished into thin air.</p>
<p>By the time the Immortal Emperor recovered and entered the restaurant again, Kent was gone, leaving him with a few cracked ribs and torn armour.</p>
<p>His furious scream filled the restaurant and spilt outward.</p>
<p>Kent and Lene appeared 80,000 km away and began heading to the hotel. He could have ignored the Immortal Emperor; however, Kent wanted to create a scene—small enough but impactful—so that word would start to spread about him.</p>
<p>This was his way of drawing Inaya’s attention.</p>
<p>---</p>
<p>Unbeknownst to him, he had drawn more than Inaya’s attention.</p>
<p>All the way in the Elven Continent, inside an underground space that could easily contain a million people, a young bald elven child who wasn’t a day older than 13 opened his eyes, turned his head sideways, and looked toward the Ice Continent.</p>
<p>This child, while he may appear human and harmless, is actually a sword intent left behind by Lord Genesis during the Divine era.</p>
<p>It was said that after he reached the peak and comprehended sword will, he gained enlightenment that allowed him to glimpse into the next realm beyond the Sword Celestial stage.</p>
<p>This breakthrough gave him a new kind of sword energy that enhanced both his sword intent and aura. A portion of that sword intent was what had recently transformed into a bald child.</p>
<p>After his transformation, he had not moved even an inch. He just sat there all day, his body unmoving. However, today, he moved, and those who were paying close attention even claimed they heard him mutter something.</p>
<p>"Big brother."</p>
<p>Inaya muttered, staring at an Elven Demigod who came to report what had happened to her. "Are you sure that was what he said?"</p>
<p>"Yes, my lady. I have confirmed it from more than a hundred people."</p>
<p>"Thank you. You can leave now." He bowed and left. After he was gone, Inaya turned and looked at her friend, Pill Supreme Ainna.</p>
<p>"Don’t say it."</p>
<p>Inaya smiled and took hold of her friend’s hand. "Cast a divination for me." She reached into her space ring and pulled out a patch of hair. "You can use this."</p>
<p>Ainna sighed, knowing she had no choice but to do as her friend wanted. "You are a lost cause, Inaya."</p>
