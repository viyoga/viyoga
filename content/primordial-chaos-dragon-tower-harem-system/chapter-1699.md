<p>Weeks had passed inside the tower since Kaizo and the undead warriors ventured into the Undead Realm to handle the mission Kent had sent them on.</p>
<p>After they were gone, Uncle Drew also left with his team of Night Riders, comprising Nara, Eryndor, Saden, Vargus, and Lene.</p>
<p>Kent couldn’t open a gate to their destination as he did for the undead warriors, so they would have to travel to the Western Region using teleportation gates and Space Channels.</p>
<p>Of course, they could have allowed just one of them to reach a planet closer to their destination so the rest could teleport to his side. However, to Kent’s surprise, they all wanted to travel together, including Lene and Nara.</p>
<p>They wanted to use the journey as a bonding session since it had been a long time since they had done anything together.</p>
<p>Perhaps with Kent’s other legions coming back to him, Uncle Drew wanted to use the mission to remind his people why they were considered the greatest legion ever to walk the face of the universe.</p>
<p>Kent had no problems with them going on a road trip.</p>
<p>So they left a few days after Kaizo and the undead warriors.</p>
<p>After them, some of Kent’s wives also chose missions they deemed worthy of their expertise.</p>
<p>Unity, Dia, and Lilian formed a team and set out on a mission to Moonfang City on Planet Sonoria. Their mission was to eliminate an Immortal Lord called Vexor.</p>
<p>Kent let the tower project the missions, and they chose that one, so he allowed them to go.</p>
<p>Aside from them, Mara, Indie, and Adina also picked a mission and left a few hours after Unity’s team departed.</p>
<p>Soon, the rest of his wives formed their own teams and ventured out to complete missions and explore the world of immortals.</p>
<p>Some of them would travel to all five regions, but most of the missions were in the Eastern Region.</p>
<p>The tower and the quest pool basically wanted Kent to work and earn rewards, rewards that would aid him in raising his own strength and that of his legions.</p>
<p>So far, he had done a remarkable job awakening the undead and unleashing some of their power. Their awakening meant he, too, had become stronger.</p>
<p>After all, with the Undead Bond skill awakened, he had inherited some of their power.</p>
<p>He might only be a Peak Immortal King, but the current power he could unleash was simply overwhelming.</p>
<p>For now, only his Primordial Chaos Dragon Bloodline had awakened, but he knew it was only a matter of time before he discovered the true reason why he had undergone thirteen reincarnations.</p>
<p>A few weeks after most of his wives had departed the tower and embarked on their missions, Kent stood in the Harem Tower with Pandora, Vexthra, Yukiko, and Umai, staring at the cocoon housing Elina’s body.</p>
<p>It had been over a year inside the tower since Kent gave her the Nine Tribulation Dragon Blood Essence.</p>
<p>Kent wanted Elina to be happy, so since becoming a dragon was something she had always wanted, he gave her the blood essence.</p>
<p>If he had taken it himself, perhaps he would have become even more monstrous. But Elina’s happiness came first. So he did what he had to do, and now he could only smile and admire the cocoon.</p>
<p>Pandora, Vexthra, Yukiko, and Miryssa were the only ladies remaining inside the tower.</p>
<p>Naturally, Pandora and Yukiko were too overpowered to handle any of the missions, while Umai and Vexthra stayed behind to handle affairs in the Tower City.</p>
<p>Kent had no problem with them staying inside the tower. He knew they chose to because they had more pressing concerns. However, a day would come when even they would have no choice but to venture out into the world.</p>
<p>Umai joined Vexthra in handling affairs inside the Tower City simply because she wanted the dragons to be able to blend in with the other races.</p>
<p>Umai had always been gentle, even as a dragon. Back when she followed the Flame Dragon, Uncle Drake, she wasn’t as arrogant as the others, so she easily blended in with anyone, no matter where she went.</p>
<p>Now, she was doing her best to ensure that the dragons Kent helped bring into the tower would live in peace and make friends with humans and other races.</p>
<p>A few hours into their wait, the cocoon cracked, and nine different colours of lightning started to surround it.</p>
<p>Kent smiled when he saw the lightning. During the Primordial Era, when he faced the Nine Tribulation Dragon, he saw firsthand how monstrous she was.</p>
<p>Back then, she only had access to six Tribulation Lightnings.</p>
<p>However, even with just those six, she managed to force Chaos to go all out.</p>
<p>Now, Elina was inheriting that power, and that alone filled him with joy.</p>
<p>A few minutes later, the cocoon exploded, and Elina’s body was lifted into the air. Lightning danced across her body, shifting from one colour to another. As the lightning slowly died down, her true appearance was revealed.</p>
<p>She had long silver hair with faint crimson streaks running through it. She was already a beauty before taking the blood essence. However, after her transformation, she had become even more beautiful.</p>
<p>Her right eye had become crimson, while her left had turned golden. On her forehead, two elegant crimson-gold dragon horns could be seen. Kent knew she would absolutely love her new appearance, especially her horns.</p>
<p>Her skin had become flawless, and a dragon tail covered in crimson scales manifested behind her. Kent knew her tail and wings could be activated and deactivated at any time.</p>
<p>However, knowing Elina, he knew she would love having them activated, at least until the novelty wore off.</p>
<p>Aside from her appearance, she had reached the Peak Immortal Lord Stage, but the aura around her showed she was far stronger than an ordinary Peak Immortal Lord.</p>
<p>She had also awakened four Tribulation Lightnings, making her the wielder of four lightning elements, each at the Absolute Rank.</p>
<p>She had also awakened the Nine Tribulation Dragon Bloodline at the Supreme Stage and even awakened five innate bloodline skills.</p>
<p>The most alarming thing, however, was the mark of a dragon claw on her forehead. The claw itself was nothing unusual. Kent had seen plenty of such marks before. However, the most alarming part was the star inside the claw.</p>
<p>Kent had seen that star mark before.</p>
<p>During his life as the Ruthless Bandit, he once fought a Qilin that claimed to come from a place called Avalon. During their battle, the Ruthless Bandit had the upper hand.</p>
<p>However, when the star mark activated, he was easily overpowered, and the last words the Qilin said before vanishing from sight were...</p>
<p>"Lord Avalon awaits you in Avalon."</p>
