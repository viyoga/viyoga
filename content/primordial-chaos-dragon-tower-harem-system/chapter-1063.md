<p>BOOM</p>
<p>The moment Kent came out of the Pill World, a powerful aura exploded out of him. This aura contained both the flame and blood aura, a clear sign of all that had changed in him during his stay in the past.</p>
<p>Kent sat down and closed his eyes, letting his body adapt to the new changes.</p>
<p>Meanwhile, Akira, Dori, and Gaia appeared in the Alchemy Tower the moment they sensed Kent’s presence.</p>
<p>They woke up a month ago while Kent was still in seclusion.</p>
<p>They missed their master and big brother so much that they couldn’t wait to talk to him again. However, since he was currently busy, they could only wait.</p>
<p>"I wonder what changes have happened to master," Dori said, already craving to merge back into Kent’s mind and read all his memories. She was the mind weaver, so she wanted to know.</p>
<p>"I know his soul strength is already at Tier 16. However, his soul universe has already reached Tier 2. It seems he had formed more bonds with High Worlds in the past," Akira also said.</p>
<p>"How many are we talking?" Gaia asked. She is connected to the tower (System), so she focuses on interacting with the cosmos to make her master’s life easier.</p>
<p>Akira and Dori are the ones more connected to him on a personal level...</p>
<p>"I can’t tell for sure, but there are over 100 High Worlds connected to his soul universe. It seems he added one or maybe six worlds to his soul universe, and these worlds formed bonds with other worlds, creating a chain."</p>
<p>"That is good then. Looks like master didn’t waste his time in the past," Gaia said, her expression containing pride and happiness.</p>
<p>She was the one who built the chamber that allowed him to go to the past, so hearing Kent had gained so much from it made her extremely happy.</p>
<p>"Master has also formed a blood core gate, a flame core, 135,000 flame veins, 5,000 blood runes, and even refined a blood death and life seal. The current master is extremely strong."</p>
<p>Just when the three lolis were getting more into what had changed in Kent, a portal suddenly opened inside the Alchemy Tower, causing them to narrow their eyes.</p>
<p>At the same time, Kent also opened his eyes.</p>
<p>"What is happening?" Kent asked, but the three lolis didn’t hear him. They were more focused on the portal</p>
<p>However, a beep inside his mind gave him the answer to his question.</p>
<p>⟦Your Destiny Key Has Activated. Destination: Earth-616. Duration: 1 Day⟧</p>
<p>[The energy coming from this portal is not of this universe.] Gaia, the cosmic weaver, was able to pinpoint the anomaly from a mere glance.</p>
<p>"That is true. It is because this portal leads to a different universe, and I think I know where."</p>
<p>[Master,] the three lolis jumped on him, sending Kent falling on his back.</p>
<p>"How are you three?"</p>
<p>[We are fine, master. We missed you so much... When we woke up, you were in seclusion, so we missed you very much,] Akira said, planting her face in Kent’s chest as she smiled and breathed a sigh of relief.</p>
<p>"I missed you, too. Now that you are awake, I am extremely happy..."</p>
<p>[We too,] Gaia said.</p>
<p>But then she pointed at the gate. As a cosmic weaver, sensing a different energy from another universe alarmed her.</p>
<p>One day, when Kent is strong enough, she will be in charge of identifying all universes that pose a threat to this one and, if possible, help eradicate them.</p>
<p>That is her main job.</p>
<p>[Big brother, you said you know where that portal leads... Can you tell me where?]</p>
<p>"I have only met him twice, but it seems this portal leads to a universe that has a paragon."</p>
<p>[A paragon, as in someone who doesn’t respect the heavens and their laws?] Gaia said, and Kent nodded.</p>
<p>"He helped me a few times. He was even the one who gave me the Soul Universe. So I have to go and handle whatever is required of me there."</p>
<p>[We understand, master. However, know that aside from the tower, we cannot go with you. You can’t also summon your legion or any weapon the moment you cross over.]</p>
<p>"I’d better place my weapons and everything of importance into my soul universe." The three lolis nodded in agreement with Kent’s words.</p>
<p>Gaia waved her hand, and a talisman appeared...</p>
<p>[Lady Alina told us to give this to you. It is a Tier 14 talisman called Second Chance. According to her, it gives a second chance to whoever uses it. She thinks since you are always taking risks, you should have it.]</p>
<p>Kent received the talisman and smiled... "Where are they?"</p>
<p>[Your wives are currently on the way to Celestial Gate Planet, where the interplanetary Sect competition will be happening. Something about going to support their new sisters, whose disciples will be participating in.</p>
<p>As for the legion, they are still on the 4th level of the Planet Killer Dungeon, exploring the Church of Destruction.]</p>
<p>"I see."</p>
<p>[Yes. And the glass lady left a message as well. She said you should hurry and come... The competition starts in a little over a day, and your name is on the list.</p>
<p>Also, you should know Lady Yi Lai has received a new dragon undead. That lady sent down to the world of undead dragons succeeded in getting a second chance at life.]</p>
<p>"Good thing I will be staying in this new universe for just one day. And tell them I will come back as soon as I return from this new universe."</p>
<p>[Okay, master. Be sure to bring some souvenirs from this new universe. I want to learn more about them, and the fastest and easiest way is to examine some things from there.]</p>
<p>"I will. Also, if it is anything like the Earth I came from, then I might even return with ice cream and pizza."</p>
<p>[Master is the best.]</p>
<p>The three lolis started jumping up and down, showing how happy they were...</p>
<p>Kent ignored them, went to the tower shop, and started buying weapons for this new trip. He didn’t need to be told this trip wasn’t an invitation to a wedding.</p>
<p>He could tell he was about to jump into yet another battle, so he started to prepare.</p>
<p>He might be strong in his universe, but this was a new one, and if there was any indication of what to expect, meeting Paragon twice was all he needed to know.</p>
<p>"You three can go watch this competition in case I am not back early, and it is starting."</p>
<p>[Okay, master.]</p>
<p>Kent walked toward the portal but stopped and opened the tower shop, buying one last item. A second later, a mask appeared in his palm, and then he placed it inside his soul universe as he changed into his battle wear.</p>
<p>A flaming ring, about a meter in diameter, appeared behind him. The ring had nodes for nine flame cores. But at the moment, only one node had a green core.</p>
<p>However, the ring quickly formed into three pairs of flame-feathered wings.</p>
<p>’Tower, initial recording mode. I want everything captured.’</p>
<p>⟦Recording Mode Activated⟧</p>
<p>Kent nodded, then stepped into the portal and vanished the next second.</p>
<p>The portal also vanished.</p>
<p>Meanwhile, on a certain battlefield, a Life Stage warrior timed and perfectly executed a move, one that caught a certain redheaded beauty whose blood aura was enough to suffocate Domain Stage warriors.</p>
<p>Her other two teammates tried to come to her rescue; however, they were blocked, leaving her life in danger.</p>
<p>However, just when the sword could pierce her neck, a dragon-clawed hand burst from the void and grabbed and snapped it in half...</p>
<p>...a voice that spread all over Earth followed.</p>
<p>"Ladies and gentlemen, the Chaos Dragon has arrived."</p>
