<p>The last time Kent came to Fedora was when he came to rescue Dori from the Crimson Vipers, an elite bandit group that worked for the Tyrant Orc King, Balma.</p>
<p>Fedora is a ’High World’ ruled by the Beastkins. There are eight Beast races, to be precise: the Tigerkin, Foxkin, Catkin, Rabbitkin, Lionkin, Batkin, Hellhounds, and the Orcs.</p>
<p>The Orc King Balma is the strongest in the land, and long before Dori arrived, his rule was supreme.</p>
<p>However, thanks to Dori’s schemes and planning, the Foxkin managed to break free from his rule when their leader, Hkum, became a Spirit Lord.</p>
<p>With her superior knowledge, she was able to attract the Tigerkin and perhaps the Catkin to their side, strengthening the rebel alliance.</p>
<p>Although the leaders of the Tigerkin and Catkin are not yet at the Spirit Lord stage, they are strong enough to fight one. So the Rebel Alliance was rising steadily.</p>
<p>However, things became dull after Dori and her two friends, one of whom was the Foxkin’s youngest princess, were captured.</p>
<p>Thankfully, Kent, his undead, and Veydris managed to poison Orc King Balma, rendering him incapable of fighting for the next few years.</p>
<p>Kent didn’t know what had happened since then; however, when Sword King Shawn asked him for help expanding and strengthening his sword realm, Kent knew adding him to the rebel alliance was the right move.</p>
<p>He had no problem bringing his undead to this world, but Kent didn’t want to do it simply because doing so would escalate the timeline that Dori had set. This was the main reason he didn’t want to send them there.</p>
<p>Per Dori’s calculation, a great war will happen within the next 15 years. However, Kent happened, and that timeline is now shaky.</p>
<p>The battle will start when the Orc King manages to force the poison out of his bloodstream.</p>
<p>So Kent knew he had to get ahead of the timeline, and since he couldn’t get his undead and other legions there due to some restriction, he had to go for the next best option.</p>
<p>Sword King Shawn is different. He wants to cultivate in a challenging environment, and since Kent needs someone in that world to report to him, he chose him.</p>
<p>Of course, he considered sending him to the tower to train in the dungeons, but in the end, he decided to send him to Fedora.</p>
<p>This way, he wouldn’t only grow beyond the limit of a medium world but also gain more experience by helping the Rebel Alliance handle the attackers from the Orc Alliance.</p>
<p>If he were to send him to the tower, his growth would stagnate at the peak of the core formation stage, since the rank of the owner determines the realm of those living there.</p>
<p>This means everyone inside the tower will not level up after forming their core and evolving it seven times. Of course, one could say Kent should just go to Fedora so they can keep growing.</p>
<p>However, the tower grows as Kent grows, and according to what Gaia told him, he must either use an Ascension platform to ascend or undergo the heavenly tribulation.</p>
<p>These are the only ways the limit will be broken. So Kent can find himself in an immortal world today, but the people connected to him would not continue to grow, considering they are in an immortal world.</p>
<p>But that is also good. The moment they reach the peak of a medium world, they can continue to grow their minds, souls, and bodies, which, from what he knew, don’t have any limitations inside or outside the tower.</p>
<p>So he didn’t want to limit the Sword King, whose swordsmanship is already at the peak of this world. He needed the danger to grow, and what better way to do that than to send him to a high world.</p>
<p>He is not linked to the tower, so he can continue to grow there. One of the many cheats the world teleporter can do. Not even the laws can stop him.</p>
<p>They appeared atop a mountain somewhere in Fedora. Kent quickly scanned the area to determine their location.</p>
<p>⟦Location: Mountain Hyib, territory of the Rabbitkin⟧</p>
<p>"Looks like we are in enemy territory," Kent said, and Dori nodded. But to Kent’s shock, the world teleporter in his hand beeped, and some names appeared.</p>
<p>⟦World: Fedora⟧</p>
<p>⌊ Tigerkin Territory</p>
<p>⌊ Rabbitkin Territory</p>
<p>⌊ Catkin Territory</p>
<p>⌊ Foxkin Territory</p>
<p>⌊ Hellhound Territory</p>
<p>⌊ Lionkin Territory</p>
<p>⌊ Batkin Territory</p>
<p>⌊ Orc Territory</p>
<p>This means he can use it to teleport to any territory he wants inside Fedora. "How convenient," Kent smiled and turned to Sword King Shawn.</p>
<p>"Welcome to Fedora, Sword King. This is how a high world feels."</p>
<p>"It’s indeed a wonder that I was in a medium world a second ago, and now, I am in a high world. I feel energized," Sword King Shawn said with a smile.</p>
<p>"Yeah, well, you needed a place to expand your Sword Realm, so this is a good enough place for that. There are many beasts here, and the danger is also more harrowing than the medium world we came from, so have fun."</p>
<p>"Thank you... I will benefit more here and perhaps even finally make some progress in my sword path." Sword King Shawn was ecstatic.</p>
<p>He found a primitive sword path while exploring the ancient battlefield of parting. He had been stuck for a while now. Hopefully, he can make progress soon.</p>
<p>"Let’s go before the Rabbits sense our presence," Kent teleported them away the next second. A few seconds after they were gone, a rabbit in his humanoid form appeared, wielding a large sword.</p>
<p>His realm was at the Spirit King stage.</p>
<p>"Maybe I was mistaken, but I could have sworn I sensed three people appear here," the Rabbit man said, looking around. After finding nothing, he left...</p>
<p>Far in the territory of the Foxkin, Kent, Sword King Shawn, and Dori appeared. However, two powerful presences descended on them a second after they appeared, causing them to turn pale.</p>
<p>Kent didn’t use his Rule Breaker skill to cancel the pressure simply because he could feel his bones strengthening immediately after the pressure came.</p>
<p>It was like the pressure was tempering his bones, something he found interesting.</p>
<p>Soon, two Foxkins appeared, each wielding a weapon. They were clad in battle armor and looked like they had their fair share of battles to boast about.</p>
<p>’Spirit Kings,’ Kent recognized their ranks.</p>
<p>"Who are you, and what do you want trespassing in our territory?" one of the Foxkins asked.</p>
<p>"I am Kent. I want to have an audience with your leader," Kent responded, only for the pressure to increase. However, before the two Foxkins could do anything, Dori walked out from behind Kent and glared at them.</p>
<p>"Vexen, Ralen, you two will do well to treat these two with respect."</p>
