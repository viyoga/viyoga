<p>After the public display of intimacy inside the pool, Kent and the ladies started spending time inside the city. He had promised them he would come and spend time with them, so he did.</p>
<p>They had fun and ate a lot of good food.</p>
<p>They did a lot of sucking and licking, which left them craving more. Naturally, the tower is a place where they have more than enough time to do a lot of things.</p>
<p>Since they have gained new bodies, their second yet first time will also be in the most romantic settings.</p>
<p>The three who had yet to get intimate with him were also now prepared, and right after the awakening ritual of the fourth Night Rider, they would have their moment with him.</p>
<p>But every good thing comes to an end, so after ten days, they left the city and returned to the Legion Tower, where Uncle Drew was waiting.</p>
<p>The moment they appeared there, and Nara set her sights on Uncle Drew, a sense of familiarity washed over her, causing her to ask:</p>
<p>"Have we met before?"</p>
<p>"As a matter of fact, yes. We did meet before, 68 years ago to be exact. However, what you are feeling now isn’t because of that; it’s more about what is about to happen to you.</p>
<p>I know you can hear the call, and soon, that call must be answered," Uncle Drew said before turning to look at Kent.</p>
<p>"Young Master, with your permission, I can start the process," Uncle Drew said, and Kent nodded.</p>
<p>"Before we start anything, do you have any questions you would want answers to? I know this is all strange to you, but I think you have some questions you need to ask."</p>
<p>"What would I become?" she asked. She could feel it deep in her bones. The changes that are coming would change her, so she wants to know.</p>
<p>"You will become an existence known as a Night Rider. Most would call you Riders of the Night or the Night Warden. And as a Night Rider, you are neither dead nor alive, since you hang in the middle.</p>
<p>You will become an invincible being in the night and have supreme authority over the flames of hell. You will also have insight into the supernatural, and if you once were, then you will be again," Uncle Drew explained.</p>
<p>"If you once were, then you will be again. Can you explain this part?"</p>
<p>"It sounds exactly as it means. The Night Riders were beings who fought in these universes, but we all died."</p>
<p>Kent raised an eyebrow. "This is new information," he said. He had asked before, but Uncle Drew knew little to nothing about who he was or what any of this meant.</p>
<p>"Yes. You remember that Queen of the Underworld I told you about?" Uncle Drew asked.</p>
<p>"Nyx..." Strangely, Kent had always had this name on his lips since he first heard it.</p>
<p>"Yes. According to her, when we met in the Underworld, I am something of an incarnation of the first Night Rider. And yes, she knew I was a Night Rider before I even told her.</p>
<p>But after spending a few years working with her, I found out a small part of the truth. We Night Riders are indeed incarnations of our past selves.</p>
<p>I tried to understand how we came into being, but she said only some ancient book — one that takes one’s longevity when read — could disclose that.</p>
<p>She also said every Night Rider is still born with their memories locked within them, and it will take some time to start recalling them.</p>
<p>But she helped me unlock a very small portion of mine. This was the reason I slowly went from Root Sage to Transcendent. I discovered a secret from my past as a Night Rider, and the effect was what you saw."</p>
<p>Kent smiled, as he understood where he was coming from. He himself had grown by leaps and bounds after his second memory recovery, so he knew Uncle Drew was telling the truth.</p>
<p>But while he was smiling, he still couldn’t figure out who this Nyx lady was or why she was going out of her way to help his subordinate.</p>
<p>Of course, there wasn’t an answer to his question now, but he was bound to find out in the coming years.</p>
<p>Nara listened to Uncle Drew talk about the Night Riders, and at every turn, something within her seemed to want to awaken.</p>
<p>"Another question. Would becoming a Night Rider separate me from my sister forever?" Her biggest fear now was being away from her sister.</p>
<p>She wanted to spend the rest of her life with her sister and new friends if possible.</p>
<p>"No. Though you will become a Night Rider, you can stay with your sister until we have a mission to embark on. However, the moment there is a mission, there should be no excuse.</p>
<p>So for now, your mission is to protect the young master."</p>
<p>She nodded.</p>
<p>These conditions were excellent in her eyes. She liked the fact that she could stay by her sister’s side and only leave if there was a mission.</p>
<p>"But don’t worry too much. I will be away for nearly two years, so you will have more time to stay with your sister daily, especially if they are spent inside the tower space."</p>
<p>"Okay. This is indeed perfect for me. And don’t worry; I will not neglect my duties," Nara said, making the rest of the ladies smile.</p>
<p>They knew what was about to happen the moment she accepted her destiny. They wanted to see what would become of her after she awakened to her True Name.</p>
<p>They had all witnessed how amazing and dangerous their own awakenings were, but afterward, they became incredible women. Now, they wanted to see what would become of their sister the moment she awakened.</p>
<p>After saying that, Uncle Drew nodded.</p>
<p>"In that case, we can start the process now." Uncle Drew touched her forehead and muttered some words. A few seconds later, the sky above them started turning.</p>
<p>⟪I call dibs on another dragon⟫ Lilian said through the harem connection.</p>
<p>⟪I say a wolf⟫ Ingrid added, and just like that, the rest started speculating. Lilian had come out as a dragon, and Ingrid as a Voidling.</p>
<p>So they were all eager to see what kind of race she would be awakening as. They gave their predictions, leaving only one person.</p>
<p>⟪Sister Selene, what do you think?⟫ Ingrid asked.</p>
<p>⟪Judging by the color of the clouds, I’d say she will be coming out as a wraith⟫.</p>
<p>The sisters shook their heads, hearing such a nonserious answer from her.</p>
