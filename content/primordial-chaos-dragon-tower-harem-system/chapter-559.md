<p>When he appeared inside the Mountain Garden, Kent asked his three assistants, "How is the city building project going?"</p>
<p>They are resting in the garden like he hadn’t given them a mega project to work on.</p>
<p>The garden was reserved for him and his ladies, though sometimes, Elaine’s parents came there to relax. Kent had told Gaia to give them that privilege.</p>
<p>It was a beautiful view that anyone would desire to have.</p>
<p>⟪We are hard at work, Master. I have already been to seven different Bloodvein Tribes and captured their culture. So the end result will be great⟫</p>
<p>Gaia said with a nonchalant expression.</p>
<p>As a Cosmic Weaver, she could be anywhere since she was connected to the cosmos. She was overpowered for sure.</p>
<p>"Glad to hear that. Now, what do you want to talk about?" Kent asked.</p>
<p>⟪It’s about the dungeons⟫ Dori said. Kent sat down and started to listen.</p>
<p>⟪First of all, the initial deduction that the dungeon bosses will slowly start to recover their true realms as they move through the worlds is indeed true. However, it’s not entirely the only way out for them⟫</p>
<p>"How so?"</p>
<p>⟪I believe that, though the Medium World limitation is suppressing them, the dungeons are also doing the same. They have their own limitations as well⟫</p>
<p>"Let me guess, the rank of the dungeon determines the strength level of the beings found within," Kent said, and the three lolis nodded.</p>
<p>⟪Every dungeon, from rank A to the last rank, has limits. The 12 dungeons your team has cleared are all SS-rank dungeons. This means the beings in there have limitations.</p>
<p>Some unique dungeons don’t have this limitation. However, an SS-rank dungeon has its last floor boss at the peak of the Root Sovereign stage.</p>
<p>This means an SSS-rank dungeon would have the last floor boss at the Core Formation stage. However, there are unique situations where the higher the floors, the stronger the beings in them.</p>
<p>This means there are some dungeons where the rank of the last floor boss may actually be found on the lower floors. In such dungeons, fear the last floor bosses⟫, Dori explained, and Kent seemed to grasp the idea.</p>
<p>"This means the SSS dungeon I will be clearing soon would have the same settings." He smiled. "I look forward to that."</p>
<p>"However, if these dungeons have suppression on the last floor bosses, then doesn’t that mean we can remove the limit by destroying the dungeons?</p>
<p>Though that would be a waste, having immortals is more valuable than having such weak training spaces," Kent said. The three lolis shook their heads.</p>
<p>⟪Destroying the dungeons won’t solve the problem. What we can do is upgrade the dungeons to higher ranks. The moment we upgrade them, their cultivation base will rise.⟫</p>
<p>"And how do we do that?" Kent knew a headache was coming. The moment the word ’upgrade’ came out of Gaia’s mouth, he knew something huge would follow.</p>
<p>⟪The dungeons can be upgraded using Tower Points. However, while I would love to say I set the prices on things, the truth is, I don’t. In any case, to upgrade a single dungeon from SS rank to SSS, you need 100 million Tower Points.⟫</p>
<p>"I knew it. Nothing comes cheap these days," Kent sighed. He didn’t have that many Tower Points at the moment.</p>
<p>But even if he did, he had 12 dungeon bosses with untapped potential. If he were to choose, who would he pick first?</p>
<p>⟪The Master shouldn’t worry. The SSS dungeon you have yet to clear has 23 floors. I believe by the time you are done with that, you will have enough points to upgrade every last Dungeon.</p>
<p>In this way, you wouldn’t only be raising their ranks, but you would also be upgrading the training grounds for your legion to train in.⟫</p>
<p>"I guess you’re right, Dori. I really need to think ahead sometimes," Kent said with a smile.</p>
<p>He knew that if he managed to upgrade the dungeons even once, their importance would skyrocket, giving him more than he bargained for.</p>
<p>"I have a question, though. Do you ladies know of the 67th Continental World?" Ankaara had said she was from that world, so Kent wanted to find out which of the three lolis knew.</p>
<p>⟪We do. The 67th Continental World is one of the most famous and powerful immortal worlds out there. It’s also very large. In fact, compared to this world, I would say that 200 of this world wouldn’t be enough to match its vastness.</p>
<p>However, aside from its size, it also has some of the most powerful races. The White Crystal Elemental Race is one of them.⟫</p>
<p>"Then, do you know the Frostveil Royal Family?" Kent wanted to probe for information, but the three ladies smiled.</p>
<p>⟪Master, you remember when I said not everything can be spoken about? This is one of them. I don’t mind talking about the other 66 races there, but this one can’t be discussed.</p>
<p>I can’t say why, but I know you’re open-minded. You’ll understand where I’m coming from.⟫. Gaia clearly wanted to answer Kent’s question, but doing so would put her master at risk.</p>
<p>"I understand, Gaia. I guess I was too angry at what they did to Ankaara’s mother and her that I wanted to know more and perhaps start plotting her revenge."</p>
<p>⟪We understand, Master. But for now, let’s focus on what we can handle and let the immortal world issues stay in the immortal worlds.⟫</p>
<p>Kent nodded and stood up.</p>
<p>"I will be busy for the next few days, so only call when you need something urgently."</p>
<p>⟪Have fun, Master.⟫ The three lolis giggled.</p>
<p>They knew what their master would do for the next few days—not that they expected anything less.</p>
<p>They also went back to their business.</p>
<p>Kent appeared inside the Legion Tower, and because Kaizo was passed out, he decided to spar with Gorzak, the Barbarian King who had already attained Diamond Body in Body Cultivation.</p>
<p>The spar was brutal.</p>
<p>It was pure strength—no active skills or elements were used—and they fought bare-chested, so the punch marks were printed on Kent’s body by the time they were done.</p>
<p>He didn’t pass out, but he was broken in more than one place. With a defeated sigh, he teleported back to the Harem Tower for his ladies to take care of him.</p>
