<p>Kent and everyone who entered the tower were ejected, and the large doors of the tower closed, sealing it for another three years.</p>
<p>Cheers rang out from those who managed to master at least one flame art. Their happiness was palpable.</p>
<p>Of course, they should be happy. What they had accomplished was something that would shape their futures forever.</p>
<p>They were delighted to finally have a chance to join the sect, which would continue to help them grow beyond their wildest expectations.</p>
<p>Essentially, learning even a heavenly grade technique had opened doors for them.</p>
<p>However, while some were celebrating, others were shedding tears because they couldn’t even learn a single technique.</p>
<p>That was their loss, for they now have no chance of even taking the sect entrance exams, let alone entering to grow further.</p>
<p>Their one chance to change their lives was now taken away by their inability to learn even a single technique.</p>
<p>Of course, those who manage to make progress are now smiling, knowing in 7 days, they will be embarking on a new journey, one that will see them grow beyond their imaginations.</p>
<p>Then again, although they managed to secure a slot to enter the sect, not all would make it, for another obstacle lay ahead of them.</p>
<p>There are the sect entrance exams that will determine who gets to enter and those who will be going back empty-handed.</p>
<p>Looking at the display board, their smiles painted their faces. At least, now that they have a chance, they would give it their all to enter the sect.</p>
<p>Kent, of course, was sitting at the top, having mastered 12 Divine-grade techniques. His dominance was as plain as day to everyone.</p>
<p>Mastering 12 Divine-grade techniques is too much for someone like him to manage per the expectations of others. But he did manage it, and the outcome is already too much for anyone to understand.</p>
<p>Of course, he wasn’t the only one.</p>
<p>In the second spot was Princess Freya of the Dwarven race. The cute and short beauty managed to learn eight Mythic-grade techniques and a Divine-grade technique.</p>
<p>That is just too much if her score were to be compared to that of the Pill Princess, who only managed to learn two.</p>
<p>Of course, the fruit Kent gave her helped her learn the techniques, but even without that, she had the means to learn at least three, which already shows that her potential is much bigger than the Pill Princess.</p>
<p>So, Kent already planted the first domino in getting to the pill princess by making Freya even more impressive.</p>
<p>Then came the third spot, occupied by Princess Elowen, who mastered seven Mythic-grade techniques and one Divine-grade technique.</p>
<p>The 4th down to the 10th were all half-elves. The 11th was Princess Freya’s friend, Helga, and on the 12th was Kella, who managed to learn six Mythic-grade techniques and a Divine-grade technique.</p>
<p>It was both her fortune and luck to meet someone like Kent. He had shaped her life in ways that aiming to become one of the pompous royals wouldn’t be able to compare to her now.</p>
<p>The seven survivalists took the remaining spots from the 13th to the 19th.</p>
<p>The outcome predicted by everyone was just not what was showing on the display board.</p>
<p>This had changed, and it was now clear who would earn the rewards from the sect and who would achieve significant success among this year’s newly accepted soon-to-be disciples.</p>
<p>Elder Lance flew into the air and looked at everyone, eyes lingering on Kent much longer before addressing the crowd.</p>
<p>"Congratulations, everyone. You’ve all done a great job entering the Flame Tower and coming out with techniques that will shape your futures.</p>
<p>First of all, you should take pride in your achievements. For the first time since this tower was discovered, not only have 24 people reached the 4th level, but 12 have even made it to the 5th level.</p>
<p>That is something you all, regardless of your ranking, should be proud of.</p>
<p>Second, I know you all are aware of the outcome of your achievements. According to the Supreme Immortal Sect’s rules, those who manage to learn a technique will be granted a chance to take the sect’s entrance exams.</p>
<p>That will happen a week from now, so be ready. You will be transported to the sect a week from today.</p>
<p>Lastly, as promised before the start of the trial, the first, second, and third spots will be awarded by the sect. A week from today, your rewards will be delivered.</p>
<p>However, I have something else to say." Elder Lance looked at everyone and smiled.</p>
<p>The arrogance he felt when he first arrived was now replaced by some form of respect as he looked at the various faces that managed to change his expectations of what was said about the world of Sonox.</p>
<p>He was told people from this world are trash. The sect doesn’t hold them in high esteem.</p>
<p>Unless one manages to demonstrate their brilliance through either the Flame Tower or the ancient battlefield of Parting, or perhaps they are royals, entering the sect is nearly impossible.</p>
<p>But now, he was proven wrong.</p>
<p>This was why he knew the elders at the sect would have a hard time coming to terms with what had been achieved.</p>
<p>"Those of you who managed to learn at least one technique, make sure the next six days are all about gaining a deeper understanding of it.</p>
<p>Go through the forms you’ve already mastered and be sure to relearn them, gain a better understanding, and, if possible, add another form to it.</p>
<p>The entrance exams will be challenging, because the sect only takes in those with potential. You’ve already proven you have some potential, so prepare well."</p>
<p>His words resonated in everyone’s head. However, he wasn’t done. He moved toward the half-elves, specifically Cyran, Arion, and Helyra.</p>
<p>"You three have proven that, against all odds, age is just a number. This is why, even though the sect only accepts those who are 100 years old or younger through the flame tower, you three will be an exception.</p>
<p>You three managed not only to learn seven mythic-grade techniques, but also you even learned a divine-grade technique. That is admirable.</p>
<p>Representing the supreme immortal sect, I am pleased to extend an official invitation to you three. A week from today, you are welcome to join everyone and participate in the sect entrance exams."</p>
<p>"Thank you," the three said, nodding as smiles played on their lips.</p>
<p>"Looks like I won’t be using my slot to invite you three after I become a core disciple in a year," Kent said, just loud enough for Elder Lance to hear.</p>
<p>He raised his brow but said nothing. He moved back, and after waiting for the Flame Tower to dive back into the sea of lava, the void opened, and the three of them vanished.</p>
<p>The moment they left, everyone turned their attention toward Kent and the half-elves. Some held hostile intents while others wore looks of admiration.</p>
<p>A few minutes later, some started leaving. They wanted to get down to the bottom of things, so they left. The elves left first, for their shame at falling behind the half-elves was just too much.</p>
<p>The demons left second, followed by the dragons, then the beasts, and eventually the dwarves. However, before leaving, Princess Freya and her friend Helga came to thank Kent.</p>
<p>Once they were gone, the half-elves also left.</p>
