<p>"Master, sit in a lotus posture and let your soul flame flow throughout your body." Kent nodded and sat down. He closed his eyes and, after a few seconds of calming his nerves, he let his soul flame move through his body.</p>
<p>In an instant, Kent felt the warmth spread through his body like a parasite.</p>
<p>"Good." Nana nodded and moved closer to Kent.</p>
<p>"Master, your current soul flame is only Tier Nine. Though it is more than enough to help you in concocting Tier Nine and even Tier Ten pills, I believe that after this procedure, it will reach Tier Eleven or even Twelve, which will be enough to concoct up to Tier Thirteen or even Fourteen pills.</p>
<p>However, the master must know the danger that comes with having Tier Twelve and higher soul flames, especially in High Worlds."</p>
<p>Kent smiled and opened one eye to look at Nana. "Let me guess, they will try to catch me and devour my flames, right?"</p>
<p>Nana nodded. "In the world of alchemy, soul flames are the most valuable item an alchemist can have. Of course, these flames can be found by slaying flame-type beasts. However, acquiring a suitable flame spirit at higher Tiers, like Tier 12, is next to impossible.</p>
<p>While killing a normal Spirit Lord beast is manageable to some extent, meeting a Spirit Lord Flame beast is a whole different ball game.</p>
<p>So most alchemists prefer to devour the flames of their fellow alchemists."</p>
<p>"I see. In that case, I will be very careful," Kent spoke, but if one looked closely, they would see that an evil smirk appeared on the side of his mouth.</p>
<p>"The master should indeed be careful. I can sense that the master is strong, but this is a supreme high world where the person beside you is a deadly blade, and you will never know.</p>
<p>That said, the master can visit the Flame River and hunt at least a beast that has a Tier Nine soul flame. I will teach you a method and even create a foundation for you to use two flames at once.</p>
<p>This way, in places where the master cannot reveal his soul flame, he can use the secondary."</p>
<p>"You are the best, Nana. One day, when we meet and I can finally get back all ten pieces of that item, I will rebuild you a cute little loli body and have you follow me across the starry skies."</p>
<p>Nana blushed slightly. "I would love that, master. But for now, let’s focus on the flame upgrade and armour refining."</p>
<p>Kent nodded and lost his left eye. However, in his head, he couldn’t help but think of what Nana said.</p>
<p>"Supreme high worlds seem more mysterious than I expected." Kent’s mind moved and started analysing his next move.</p>
<p>"So far, I haven’t yet completed the first form of the Primal Body Tempering Art, so I can’t fully use it. However, thanks to those filthy forsaken warriors, I’ve managed to cultivate 5% of the first form, which has already boosted my strength twofold.</p>
<p>Of course, although I even killed five Heavenly Ascension stage warriors, they weren’t that strong or talented, suggesting they were of the standard of a Legacy High World, not even close to the standards of a Spirit Lord in a Great High World.</p>
<p>However, I also didn’t use my entire strength in killing them. Sjit, I even killed an Avatar with the strength of a Root Immortal. That makes me awesome"</p>
<p>Kent smiled, not realising that he had only faced the Avatar at 4% of his strength, and that he hadn’t used immortal qi; otherwise, Kent would have suffered greatly.</p>
<p>"Now, if I were to use the 7% access I have of my bloodline and the one thousand flame veins altogether, then I believe even Heavenly Ascension stage warriors in a Great High World will be crushed by me.</p>
<p>Not forgetting my Soul Universe.</p>
<p>However, the idea is to dominate even Supreme High Worlds, so with these three hundred days I have here, I will find ways to raise my strength and, if possible, battle a Heavenly Ascension stage warrior."</p>
<p>Kent smiled in his head. "Then again, if the feeling I felt from big sister Nefeli is remotely accurate, then I need to man up and train harder, for whether I choose to believe it or not, I am not invincible yet in a high world.</p>
<p>But I will be before ascending.</p>
<p>After all, my ladies and legion, after the battle with the forsaken, they now want to reach their limits inside the Planet Killer Dungeon before allowing me to ascend.</p>
<p>Confidently, I have this place and even the Sword Words to man up my game. When I ascend, I want the entire high world to know a monster has arrived... I want immortals to quake when they hear my name."</p>
<p>"I will be starting now, master."</p>
<p>Kent nodded, and Nana placed her slender finger on his forehead. A bone-shattering pain rocked Kent’s body when Nana’s finger touched his forehead.</p>
<p>He gritted his teeth as cold sweat broke on his forehead.</p>
<p>The pain spread throughout his body, causing Kent to clench his fist so tightly that blood started pouring from his palm.</p>
<p>"Endure it, master. The pain will pass soon."</p>
<p>Kent wasn’t in the mindset to nod to Nana’s words, as he was in so much pain that even when a day passed, he had no idea.</p>
<p>After three days had passed, the pain started to subside. Kent felt his body begin to grow stronger.</p>
<p>In truth, this strength boost had started from day one, but he was in so much pain that he couldn’t notice. Now that he did, he allowed a smile to appear on his face.</p>
<p>On the fourth day, Kent stopped feeling the pain, and instead, he began to feel a nourishing sensation throughout his body.</p>
<p>"The nourishing feeling the master is experiencing is the armour fusing with your soul flame." Nana’s voice entered Kent’s ears, causing him to nod.</p>
<p>If only he had known that things would have been much less painful if Nana had followed the process she had told him.</p>
<p>If she had first upgraded the soul flame before refining the armour, the pain would have been far less.</p>
<p>However, the unruly Nana knew that such a process would be time-consuming and wasteful, so she chose a method that refined the armour and upgraded the flame simultaneously.</p>
<p>But it didn’t come without its rewards. Aside from the powerful upgrades, Kent’s body had now adapted to pain so excruciating that he would hardly feel much if one day he were to undergo a similar process.</p>
<p>In a way, Nana had helped him, and that alone was more than Kent could be thankful for.</p>
<p>A few hours after the fifth day, two messages appeared, and the sound caused Kent to smile.</p>
<p>⟦Your soul flame has undergone an upgrade after merging with the Nine Nirvana Flames. Your soul flame now has all the properties of the Nine Nirvana Flames⟧</p>
<p>⟦Your Chaos Soul Flame has been upgraded to Tier Twelve⟧</p>
<p>However, a few seconds after this message appeared, another one followed, causing Kent to smile cheerfully.</p>
<p>⟦Congratulations. Your Nirvana Armour has been refined.⟧</p>
<p>⟦Your Nirvana Armour has fused with your Eternal Draconic Jade Body.⟧</p>
<p>⟦Your strength has improved by 300%. Your health has improved by 200%.⟧</p>
<p>Kent felt his body grow stronger.</p>
<p>"This is more like it." Kent lit his golden flame, which flickered with nine distinct colours. This display made him feel like he was on another level.</p>
<p>Nana looked at this with a weak smile. "Congratulations, master. I have created the foundation for you to have a second Pill Flame. That one can be used when you don’t want to draw attention to your soul flame.</p>
<p>Now, how about I give you a few techniques since you seem to be lacking in the techniques department when it comes to pill making?"</p>
