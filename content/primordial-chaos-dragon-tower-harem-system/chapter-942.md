<p>Kent and Lady Azela followed the attendant toward a large ship that hovered two meters above the ground. On the way, Kent pondered what had just happened with Daniel.</p>
<p>He examined the badge inside his space ring for a moment. ’Looks like there is still more to Big Sister Nefeli. The fact that I was just referred to as Young Master either means they saw me as her son or maybe a brother.</p>
<p>I am banking on the latter. But if that is the case, then what kind of background does she have?’</p>
<p>Kent tried to think about this for a moment. However, he wiped the thought from his mind the next second.</p>
<p>’What am I even thinking of? Big Sister is a Pill Monarch. Just that alone is enough to render all these great families useless.’</p>
<p>With that thought registered in his mind, Kent chose not to dwell on it anymore.</p>
<p>’Though being called Young Master does have a nice ring to it. Makes me feel pampered.’ A small smile appeared on his face as they were led to a stand.</p>
<p>Slowly, the stand floated and started lifting them onto the ship.</p>
<p>Lady Azela stood behind Kent, staring daggers at his back. She was now more than ever intrigued to learn more about Kent’s background.</p>
<p>’Once all this is over and I manage to refine a soul puppet, I will investigate his background. Although he is merely a Core Formation cultivator, with how resourceful he is, being on his good side wouldn’t be a bad thing.’</p>
<p>After a few seconds, they landed on the ship, where hundreds of people were already waiting for the channel to open so they could be on their way.</p>
<p>The Transfer Channel was like a teleportation gate. However, instead of sending them instantly to their destination, this one created a channel that linked to another Transfer Channel on Planet Aerithyne.</p>
<p>Once this channel was created, the ship, reinforced with millions of arrays and formations, would fly through it at several times its usual speed.</p>
<p>It was one of the most complex phenomena in the universe that many people struggled to understand.</p>
<p>During the Primordial Era, one had to know the star map to travel between worlds using the void as the natural channel.</p>
<p>But now, one merely needed a star crystal, and they could move through the void without fear of getting lost and appear on a completely different planet.</p>
<p>"Young Master and My Lady, this way please." The attendant led them toward a door. People kept staring at them as they moved. The ship was large, like the ones that ran on the sea.</p>
<p>However, it had some forms of wings made from a material Kent had no idea of, nor any interest in examining.</p>
<p>His focus, however, was on the many combat-ready formations embedded in the ship. ’Looks like travelling through transfer channels isn’t as safe as one might think.’</p>
<p>They were led to a room on the last floor of the ship. It had six floors. However, the architecture and design of the last floor, which was the sixth, were more luxurious, indicating that it was made for VIP travellers.</p>
<p>"Young Master, this is your room. I will come around later to take your order."</p>
<p>Kent smiled. "Don’t worry about that. Just a glass of wine will be enough."</p>
<p>The attendant nodded and, with a gentle bow, left, leaving Kent. He rolled down the window and gazed out, looking over the large expanse of city before his eyes.</p>
<p>"In a few minutes, you will be off-world," Kent said, looking at Lady Azela. "How do you feel about that?"</p>
<p>Lady Azela looked at him but showed no expression that suggested whether she was happy or not. However, she was relieved in her heart...</p>
<p>"I will be happy once I am back in my world and inside my sect."</p>
<p>"I guess you are right. But feel happy knowing you are sitting within the sensing range of your enemy, yet they have no idea."</p>
<p>Lady Azela turned away from Kent and looked toward a building several miles from their location. She showed no reaction, though her eyes said otherwise. Naturally, she was looking at the old man through the Soul Map.</p>
<p>The old man was seated in a lotus posture with his eyes closed, his divine sense spread across a 40,000 km radius.</p>
<p>A 30,000 km sensing range is the maximum for someone who has reached the peak of the Heavenly Ascension stage. However, with the old man breaking sixteen limits on his body, he was able to expand his range to 40,000 km.</p>
<p>"Don’t worry, I will be sure to end him and give his soul spirit to you," Kent said with a lazy expression. Naturally, he planned on cleaning house.</p>
<p>Knowing the Blue Flame Pill Palace was looking to take over the Pyris Pill Alliance, he felt the need to act.</p>
<p>And now that he had a reason, taking out two scums that could threaten his future investment seemed logical.</p>
<p>After a three-hour wait, the ship was ready to depart.</p>
<p>Creating the transfer channel took three minutes. However, the moment it opened, the ship rose into the air and soon zoomed into the transfer channel, beginning its journey through the void.</p>
<p>The moment they left Planet Pyris, Lady Azela let out a sigh.</p>
<p>Kent smiled. ’Saving fairies both in the future and the past does feel nice.’</p>
<p>Seeing Lady Azela close her eyes and slowly enter a meditative state made his smile widen. He knew she had been panicking the entire time, so she needed the quiet to calm her nerves.</p>
<p>Kent also closed his eyes and decided to handle his current mental state. The issue with his memories from his past life as Chaos still weighed heavily on his heart.</p>
<p>He plans to fix his mind before going back to the future.</p>
<p>Soon, he entered a state that sent him back to the moment he discovered Elina’s bloodied body. This time, however, he was merely observing as an outsider.</p>
<p>Time flew by, and Kent remained in that state. He made sure to note down every last detail of that horrible moment that had forever changed his life.</p>
<p>After having had enough, he pulled his mind back to the present and chose to suppress his emotions for now. A time would come for him to unleash his fury.</p>
<p>’Elina, if you’ve reincarnated, I hope you are living a good life. The universe owes that much,’ Kent sighed.</p>
<p>’I will find you even if it means killing my way through the universe. Nothing will stop me from finding you.’</p>
<p>Lady Azela, who was looking at Kent’s face, felt a chill run down her spine when Kent opened his eyes and their gazes met. She withdrew her gaze immediately, unsure if she could hold her composure if she maintained eye contact for long.</p>
<p>Kent didn’t say anything and merely looked out the window.</p>
<p>"We’ve arrived, Young Master." The attendant knocked on the door at that very moment and informed them of their arrival.</p>
<p>"Let’s go." Kent led the way, and soon, they were back on the deck of the ship.</p>
<p>"Welcome to Thyne City, Young Master," the attendant said. "This is the capital city of this planet and the most wonderful place to be."</p>
<p>Kent nodded and immediately activated the Soul Map. Soon, the 80,000 km radius was within his reach.</p>
<p>"I guess we will be on our way." The attendant nodded and led them to the platform that carried them back down. Soon, Kent and Lady Azela made their way toward a tall pagoda standing 75,000 km away.</p>
