<p>[First Opponent - Primordial Abyssal Flame of Despair - Hell Lord: Morveth, the Despair Sovereign]</p>
<p>Right when Kent entered the Primordial Tower, the feeling of despair overwhelmed his senses. His surroundings changed, and then he found himself standing on a vast plain of black ash beneath an eternal twilight sky.</p>
<p>There were broken black pillars littered everywhere.</p>
<p>There were also broken weapons and bones everywhere. Most of these bones appeared to be buried in the ground, with only a few protruding partially through the dirt.</p>
<p>"Creepy," Kent said, looking around him.</p>
<p>However, just when he was about to look for the Hell Flame, his sixth sense kicked in, allowing him to narrowly evade the chain that came for him.</p>
<p>But that wasn’t all.</p>
<p>Just when the chain struck the ground, an explosion with devastating might occurred, pushing Kent back. His body was consumed in a mushroom of despair, dust, and heat.</p>
<p>But before he could relax and examine what was happening, he sensed a powerful chain coming at him again.</p>
<p>’Annoying’</p>
<p>15,000 flame veins kicked in, and this time, he not only dodged but also saw where it was coming from.</p>
<p>So with a wave of his hand, a sword came from his Soul Universe and flashed at deadly speed toward the location from which the chain came.</p>
<p>CLANG</p>
<p>He heard a clang sound, and then the sword flew back. Kent caught it in a firm grip, watching as a figure slowly appeared from thin air.</p>
<p>He was a tall person, with a skeletal frame, violet eyes, and long black hair. His height was approximately 3 meters, indicating that he wasn’t an ordinary person.</p>
<p>This discovery didn’t change Kent’s expression.</p>
<p>However, upon seeing what the person was wearing, he could only smile. It was a robe woven from what appeared to be screaming shadows, a sigh that he was indeed the flame of Despair.</p>
<p>"So they managed to send someone to their death again," Morveth, the human form of the Primordial Abyssal Flame of Despair, said with a chuckle.</p>
<p>"I don’t have much time, Mr., so if you have something to say, just say it before I kill you," Kent said with a sigh.</p>
<p>The pressure coming from Morveth was even higher than the Monarchs he tamed in the Flame Sea. However, even with that discovery, he wasn’t at all feeling despair.</p>
<p>In fact, his expression was still the same, showing he wasn’t being pressured by a mere flame.</p>
<p>Morveth looked at Kent and laughed, "I believe this is what they call arrogance. But I like it, makes killing you fun."</p>
<p>"So are those your last words?"</p>
<p>Morveth waved his hand, and thousands of chains manifested in the air and shot at Kent.</p>
<p>However, with a sigh, his body vanished, and then he appeared behind Morveth, his sword buried in his head.</p>
<p>"H-how?" Morveth asked, his expression bony and dark.</p>
<p>"I have flame veins, bitch." With a twist, Morveth’s body died, and a flame seed appeared in the air. Kent grabbed it and tossed it into his Soul Universe.</p>
<p>On the outside, Gaap gasped, seeing what had just happened.</p>
<p>"That didn’t just happen, right?... Right?"</p>
<p>He wasn’t the only one taken aback. The white-bearded man was the same. He saw Kent move, but then, as if he teleported, the next time he appeared, he was standing behind Morveth.</p>
<p>"I guess he really has what it takes to handle them." He smiled and then waved his hand, and a scroll appeared. The name, "Hell Flame Foundation Mantra," was written on the back.</p>
<p>He held it firm in his grip, deciding if he should give it to Kent or not.</p>
<p>Meanwhile, inside the Primordial Hell Tower, a door appeared leading to the second floor. Kent walked in and appeared in the next Hell Flame domain.</p>
<p>---</p>
<p>[Second Opponent - Primordial Flame of Wrath - Hell Lord: Kaelzor, the Wrath Tyrant]</p>
<p>This time, Kent appeared in an endless battlefield with rivers of lava everywhere.</p>
<p>If he were an ordinary flame user, it would be a no man’s land for him; however, as he stood, gazing at his opponent, all Kent could feel was the intent to kill.</p>
<p>"So you killed Morveth," Kaelzor, the humanoid form of the Primordial Flame of Wrath, said, a smug look on his lips.</p>
<p>He had a hulking body, with crimson skin marred by molten cracks across his arms and chest, and a fiery mane of hair.</p>
<p>"He was rather weak for a Primordial Flame," Kent said, holding a sword in his firm grip.</p>
<p>"I guess you are right, but here is where you die."</p>
<p>Kaelzor crossed his twin war axes, and then he slashed down, manifesting a flaming meteor in the air. The ground shook, and the air grew dense.</p>
<p>Kent narrowed his eyes when he saw the flaming meteor and felt the pressure bore down on him.</p>
<p>However, the next second, a smile appeared on his lips.</p>
<p>"Fake meteor. Why don’t I show you what a real meteor looks like?" He also slashed his sword, and a meteor, about half the size of the one Kaelzor manifested using his flame, appeared.</p>
<p>It fell with devastating force and blasted through the meteor that Kaelzor had manifested. Then still intact, he moved toward Kaelzor.</p>
<p>However, Kaelzor was an experienced warrior by the standard of a flame, so he launched an attack at the meteor.</p>
<p>"What an idiot." Kent, however, allowed him to attack his meteor because, to him, the main attack was in the event that his meteor was destroyed by his opponent.</p>
<p>BOOM</p>
<p>A powerful soul attack was unleashed when Kaelzor’s attack blasted through the meteor. Kent stood with a smile on his face as the soul shockwave blasted past him.</p>
<p>Kaelzor coughed; however, no blood came simply because he had no blood. However, for a crimson-gold flame, he had become rather pale.</p>
<p>He became very weak, so Kent merely walked toward him and held his sword to his neck.</p>
<p>"No hard feelings."</p>
<p>With a simple swing, the head came off. He stored the flame seed in his Soul Universe and entered the door leading to the third floor.</p>
<p>---</p>
<p>[Third Opponent - Primordial Flame of Agony - Hell Lord: Zariel, the Tormentor]</p>
<p>WHIP</p>
<p>Just when Kent appeared on the third floor, a whip came from behind him, sending a wave of power, one that caused Kent’s body to tremble.</p>
<p>Thankfully, he was using 15,000 flame veins, so he jumped into the air to evade.</p>
<p>However, while in the air, a figure appeared overhead, stabbing a spear toward his chest.</p>
<p>’Well played.’</p>
<p>Although he was in trouble, he couldn’t help but acknowledge the smartness deployed by Zariel, the Primordial Flame of Agony.</p>
<p>The attack was well-timed and executed.</p>
<p>’Too bad you met me.’ The soul map opened, and Kent vanished. In his place, a Tier 14 soul spirit appeared, and then BOOM, it exploded.</p>
<p>Zariel was blasted back, half his body gone.</p>
<p>"Sneak attacks don’t work on me, kid. I am the Faceless Assassin. I do the sneak attacks, not the other way round."</p>
<p>His sword went through Zariel’s chest, and then the Primordial Flame of Agony seed appeared. He grabbed it, and then with a mere look, he tossed it into his Soul Universe.</p>
<p>"Looks like as I climb higher, their strength increases," Kent noticed the change after nearly falling to Zariel’s sneak attack. Thankfully, he was prepared for anything, so he was able to survive.</p>
<p>The next door opened, and Kent walked in, appearing in the domain of Corruption. As soon as he stepped in, he began to sense his health depleting rapidly.</p>
<p>---</p>
<p>"This is where your luck ends, kid," Gaap said, a smug smile on his face. The Primordial Flame of Corruption was one of the most dangerous Hell Flames in existence...</p>
<p>So, being exposed to it, it was only a matter of time before his health ran out.</p>
<p>However, the next second, his expression changed from smugness to shock...</p>
