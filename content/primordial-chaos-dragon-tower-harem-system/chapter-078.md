<p>Jacob lifted his head slowly, his face pale and battered from when the Dark Legion Guards picked him up from a brothel where he was planning to spend the rest of his meaningless life after leaving his family.</p>
<p>His eyes held a mix of anger, confusion, and something close to resignation. He didn’t speak at first, only stared at Kent as if trying to measure the sincerity behind his words.</p>
<p>He is angry, for one. But that was all: just anger.</p>
<p>Now, however, he heard something he never thought he would hear from the second most hateful person he had met.</p>
<p>Daisy Ashland is first by the way.</p>
<p>For a moment, there was silence. The weight of the question hung in the air, pressing on his rational mind. He could make a decision now that would change his life.</p>
<p>Finally, Jacob broke the silence.</p>
<p>"And if I refuse?" he asked, his voice hoarse but steady. He wasn’t about to act arrogant despite resolving himself to death days ago.</p>
<p>Yet death doesn’t want him, it seems.</p>
<p>Kent crossed his arms and tilted his head slightly. "Then I’ll release you," he said plainly. "You’ll be free to go wherever you want. I do not need someone unwilling to follow me."</p>
<p>Jacob’s eyes narrowed. "Why not just kill me, then? It would solve all your problems."</p>
<p>Kent gave a faint smirk. "I don’t have a reason to kill you. You’re not a threat to me, Jacob. But you could be an asset if you choose to be."</p>
<p>The words seemed to catch Jacob off guard. He blinked a few times, his shoulders slumping slightly as he processed the offer.</p>
<p>"You’ve already taken everything from me," Jacob said bitterly. "What’s left for me to serve you for?"</p>
<p>Kent’s expression hardened, his voice colder now.</p>
<p>"That’s up to you. You can keep clinging to your grudge and die with nothing, or you can find a new purpose. I’m offering you a chance to rise above what your family made you—a chance to stand for something real."</p>
<p>The prison fell silent again. Jacob’s hands clenched into fists, the chains around his wrists rattling softly. His breathing was uneven, but he held an expression that said he wasn’t stupid to do anything stupid.</p>
<p>"I’ll do it," he said at last.</p>
<p>Kent nodded a faint hint of approval in his eyes. "Good then. You won’t regret your decision."</p>
