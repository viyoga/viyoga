<p>"Up next, we have a Tier 3 Flawless Pill from the Perfect Alchemist."</p>
<p>Alina said, and everyone went quiet, their expression aimed at the small jade box sitting on the mechanized table.</p>
<p>They all heard of the perfect alchemist simply because the pills he brought out in Caprath City were all 100% pure.</p>
<p>These pills are like gold mines, so there was even an auction for them in Caprath City. It was also the reason why Alina managed to secure the position of perfect alchemist this time around.</p>
<p>Not even the owner of the auction house knew of the identity of the perfect alchemist, so to think a new pill had appeared and it was even Tier 3, they all wanted to hear its effect and, perhaps, try to bid.</p>
<p>"The perfect alchemist, he had a new pill. The last time I heard his pills appear was in Caprath city and it was bought by the sword Saintess from the Divine Palace Sect...A new pill appeared again."</p>
<p>The people were hungrily waiting for Alina to tell them the nature of the pill and what it could do.</p>
<p>"Before I tell you the name of the pill, I have a message from the perfect Alchemist," Alina said, scanning the room.</p>
<p>"He said and yes, the perfect alchemist is a he. Actually, he is just a young boy who is not older than 20.</p>
<p>He said he would be making his appearance in the upcoming Pill Master Competition in this same city a month from now.</p>
<p>He will be participating so if you want to know him, do well to be in attendance"</p>
<p>Alina exaggerated a bit, but she got the message out, and everyone took it in like it was the most important information they had ever heard.</p>
<p>"Now to the pill. It’s an Innate Talent Awakening Pill. And as you all know, Innate pills are basically about one’s talent.</p>
<p>Well, this pill is the same. Just like you all know, if this were to be a Tier 9 or 10, which I know only a few had even heard of, then know that this pill does similar work but in a toned-down version.</p>
<p>This pill enhances your talent, which enhances your spirit roots and helps you break through bottlenecks.</p>
<p>Yes, those stuck at the Root Grandmaster stage can become Root Saints, and Root Saints can become Root Sages. lastly, according to the Perfect Alchemist, those stuck at the Root Sage stage can become Root Great Sages after taking this pill.</p>
<p>And the best part, your talent, which will be upgraded to help you break through, will be permanent. So, unlike the realm ascension Pill, this pill affects you permanently.</p>
<p>So like I said, this will help you breakthrough and along with that, you will gain a boost on your Cultivation and Comprehension abilities"</p>
<p>"The bidding is set for 400,000 and there can only be a minimum of 10,000 increment."</p>
<p>"Let’s start bidding now"</p>
<p>"500,000..."</p>
<p>Alina wasn’t even done when someone raised the price by a whopping hundred thousand. But he wasn’t the only one...</p>
<p>"One Million..." Another person said, just like them, the war started with the price rising steadily.</p>
<p>It’s just a Tier 3 Pill, but it’s a 100% pure pill, something nobody will pass up on. In fact, the alchemists around are all chasing after the pill for two things.</p>
<p>They wanted to study to see if they could understand how he managed to remove all the impurities. Alchemists have ways of knowing that.</p>
<p>Though it’s not guaranteed, they can make a breakthrough, and this will help them improve the purity of their own pills.</p>
<p>The second was the pill recipe.</p>
<p>Innate Talent Awakening Pill is one of the rarest pills an alchemist can make. It wouldn’t be an exaggeration to say that no alchemist on this human continent has this recipe.</p>
<p>But they know what the pill is and they know what the real deal can do.</p>
<p>But they have no idea what the recipe is, and so they are now battling to see if they can get the recipe from the pill and perhaps try concocting it.</p>
<p>"5 million," someone said, but he was overtaken the next second.</p>
<p>Inside VIP room 13, Kent was lying comfortably in Selene’s embrace, watching as people bid on the pill.</p>
<p>"When did you make this pill?" Selene asked. Among his ladies, only she, Lilian, Elsa, and Unity truly believed Kent was an alchemist. The others just didn’t believe someone like Kent could be an alchemist.</p>
<p>In their eyes, he was too young to be capable of concocting pills.</p>
<p>But hearing Selene’s question, they all turned toward him.</p>
<p>"A couple of weeks ago. I just wanted to try my hand at a Tier 3 pill before the competition, and well, I succeeded after three tries... yay," Kent said, making the ladies still look at him strangely.</p>
<p>"Fine, here you go," Kent said, taking out four pillboxes containing pills from his space ring and handing them to the ladies.</p>
<p>"These—these are all perfect pills!" Gate Master Camilla almost shouted. Thankfully, the noise cancellation had been enabled, so it blocked her voice from escaping the room.</p>
<p>"Are you shocked, impressed, and charmed?" Kent asked, causing the Gate Master to blush slightly. The look on Ingrid, Neomi, Val, and Mara’s faces said it all; they were all shocked.</p>
<p>"How many talents do you have?" Gate Master Mara asked. The bidding war was still blazing hot.</p>
<p>"I am a man of many talents. Stick around, and you will find out," Kent said. He didn’t want to get into the technicalities now. The moment he entered her pants, she would know everything.</p>
<p>As for the master and her disciple, he had already told them, but they just didn’t believe him.</p>
<p>"How old are you, anyway?" Ingrid asked, already expecting a headache.</p>
<p>"I am 18, dear, but don’t worry—inwardly, though, I feel like I am over 30," Kent laughed, causing the ladies to look at him like they had seen a monster.</p>
<p>The bidding war, however, lasted for an hour until a certain Pill Grandmaster bought it for a whopping 90 million spirit stones.</p>
<p>Though the pill wasn’t on the same level as a legendary item, it was more than qualified to be on the same stage as them.</p>
<p>That was done, and the final item was brought out, shocking everyone.</p>
