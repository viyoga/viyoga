<p>"You... How did you get in?" Zayn asked, watching Kent and the two ladies walk out of the mansion as they started to leave Gate Master Mara’s residence and head toward the Tasty Home Pavilion.</p>
<p>"We clearly saw you leave, so how did you enter?"</p>
<p>Kent smiled and smirked at the five brats who had been spoiled too much by their master, making them think everything was rainbows and sunshine.</p>
<p>"Kids, a wise man once said, ’When one door closes, thousands of windows open.’" His laughter filled the lush passage leading away from the mansion.</p>
<p>"You..."</p>
<p>The ladies beside him held back their laughter, but smiles could be seen on their faces.</p>
<p>The five disciples gritted their teeth but could only watch as Kent and the ladies walked away. They would tell their master to fix the formation because someone had managed to break in without the formation stopping them.</p>
<p>After they left, Kent and the two ladies quickly found themselves inside the Tasty Home Pavilion, where he showed his platinum-grade badge and was immediately led to the top floor of the pavilion.</p>
<p>It took a few minutes for their orders to be brought to them.</p>
<p>The moment the food was set down, Kent started staring at Lady Lani, who was also looking at him.</p>
<p>"This was all your scheming, wasn’t it?" she asked.</p>
<p>"It’s only natural for a man to treat such a beauty like yourself to a good dinner. So, why don’t you dig in and tell me how delicious it is?" Kent’s smile bloomed as he looked at her.</p>
<p>"You..."</p>
<p>Lady Lani just couldn’t understand how a person could be this shameless yet also very likeable.</p>
<p>She knew he planned on looking at her face without the veil, but she also didn’t want to take it off. She glanced at Alina, who was smiling at her.</p>
<p>Had the situation been different, perhaps Alina would have helped her. But now, she could only endure and follow through with it.</p>
<p>"Don’t fight it, Big Sister. He is that shameless, and from the moment you agreed to his dinner invitation, you had already fallen into his trap."</p>
<p>Lady Lani sighed and removed her veil, revealing her enchanting beauty.</p>
<p>"Are you happy now?" she asked, making Kent smile.</p>
<p>"More than you can imagine."</p>
<p>Alina just smiled on the side.</p>
<p>They started eating right afterwards. Since they were on the top floor, the dishes were the best the Pavilion had to offer. So every plate of food was the best of the best...</p>
<p>For an hour, they indulged in some of the finest delicacies.</p>
<p>After they had eaten their fill, they got down to business.</p>
<p>"So, to what do I owe the pleasure of a visit from such a beauty?" Kent asked, sipping on a spirit drink. Lady Lani sighed. Over the hour, she had somehow grown more comfortable being around Kent.</p>
<p>"I was hoping to see if we could form something akin to an alliance. My auction house and other businesses can provide you with what you need, and in return, if you have something of value to sell, you will use our services.</p>
<p>We will provide locations of treasures we know of but couldn’t handle the retrieval. We can even arrange a team to aid you should you need one.</p>
<p>All we ask is to use our services to handle the sale of such items.</p>
<p>It’s a win-win situation for both of us. So, what do you think?" Lady Lani asked.</p>
<p>"Not a bad deal." Kent nodded. "However, do you think your auction house has what it takes to handle the stuff I will be bringing?" he asked.</p>
<p>"Though I don’t like to brag, I am someone who has more than you can think of. In fact, if the Ambrosia Fruit is already enough to break your mind, then wait till you see the rest of my treasures."</p>
<p>Alina rolled her eyes at his clear bragging, despite him saying he didn’t like to brag.</p>
<p>But although he bragged to the lady, she start to look forward to such treasures .</p>
<p>In fact, she was now looking at Kent with puppy eyes. As someone who had seen and appraised loads of treasures, she started to drool over the potential treasures Kent would bring.</p>
<p>"Don’t worry about whether or not we can handle your treasures. Provided you have them, we can sell them even if it takes weeks of bidding."</p>
<p>"Okay then. You can rest assured I will deliver anything I want sold to your auction house," Kent promised her.</p>
<p>"Great," Lady Lani smiled, brightening her beauty even more.</p>
<p>"You should smile more, Lady Lani." Kent, of course, didn’t miss that chance to add his shameless suggestion.</p>
<p>"Then, do you want to join our expedition to the Ancient Battlefield of Parting? We know where the main chunk of the meteor is and how to safely get there.</p>
<p>The only hard part is retrieving it since, per our reports, it’s protected by three Sovereign-level spirit beasts.</p>
<p>However, the team, which will comprise 12 people, already has seven Ascension-stage experts and one Sovereign, leaving only five people left to complete the 12 able team.</p>
<p>If you were to join, your guardian would also be allowed another slot. You also wouldn’t have to complete the Calamity mission.</p>
<p>It was the requirement at first, but from your battles, we can tell you are capable. So we want you on board"</p>
<p>Since she had success asking for the alliance, she grew comfortable so she asked for another. This time, it was something Kent was actually looking forward to.</p>
<p>Getting the ore will help him to upgrade more battle dolls and create more undead he could use to enter even more harrowing places.</p>
<p>So hearing her request, Kent smiled.</p>
<p>"Hmm, that is some offer you have there, Lady Lani." But he pretended to be in a deep thought.</p>
<p>"Oh, please, I know you are thrilled, so why pretend?" Alina, however, had had enough of Kent’s antics, so she shattered all the facade he had around him.</p>
<p>"You demoness, why make it so obvious? Naturally, Lady Lani, I will be thrilled to join the expedition. Don’t worry about giving a slot to that brute; he can get there without the help of the Sovereign. In fact, a few friends will be joining, but they won’t need a slot."</p>
<p>Lady Lani smiled. "If you say so, then I will inform the team."</p>
<p>"Big sister, you know you can be free around him, right? Holding back your happiness is not good for your health," Alina, however, wasn’t done.</p>
<p>She had shattered Kent’s facade, so she had to touch Lady Lani’s composure.</p>
<p>"You..." As expected, Lady Lani covered her face, embarrassed at being discovered. She was indeed very happy for she had succeeded in getting what she came for.</p>
<p>She had already thanked Kent on their way to the Pavilion and now, her two requests were accepted. But Alina just didn’t allow her to leave with her dignified expression.</p>
<p>"Don’t mind her, Lady Lani. I have spoiled her too much," Kent said, holding Alina’s waist and pulling her closer to himself.</p>
<p>"Do you want me to show you around the sect?" Kent asked.</p>
<p>"But you told me you only know two places, so where do you plan on taking her?" Alina giggled and looked at her big sister.</p>
<p>"Kent, dear, if you plan on wooing my big sister, then you should do better than this," Alina’s giggle turned into laughter, leaving Lady Lani blushing like a ripe tomato.</p>
