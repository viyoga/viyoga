<p>The legend of the Everlasting Healer didn’t start out as benevolent as one would expect. When people hear the name Healer or Life Magician, they quickly picture a benevolent entity worthy of admiration.</p>
<p>However, the mother of life magicians, the one they called the Everlasting Healer, didn’t start out as this benevolent entity worth looking up to.</p>
<p>She started out with the name The Contractor.</p>
<p>Legends speak of the greatest killer during the Primordial Era. They talk about the one who can kill anyone and anything, regardless of who they are or where they are.</p>
<p>They called her the Jackal, for she always finds a way to kill.</p>
<p>However, to understand where she came from, we would have to know about the first human kingdom to rise during the Primordial Era and why they were the first to fall.</p>
<p>The legend started when the first human awakened a rare bloodline called the Dark Lotus, which is said to have come from the law of death itself.</p>
<p>This human rose through the ranks and soon stood on equal footing with the dragons, Beastkin, Elves, Dwarfs, and other races, who, until the rise of this human, ruled over the humans and made them their slaves for thousands of years.</p>
<p>No human had ever risen to a level that could challenge the other races. However, this human did, and when he got to the top, he realized he wasn’t the only one.</p>
<p>There was another human who also awakened a similar bloodline as his, but she wasn’t a killer; she was a life giver. She protected the lives of their masters, and for years, that was her life.</p>
<p>But when the White and Dark Lotus come together, they become unstoppable. One is the killer, and the other is the healer. That is when humans rose and said no to their masters.</p>
<p>The first human kingdom rose, and humans were freed. Legend spoke of the legendary battle that set humans on the Primordial maps.</p>
<p>However, thousands of years later, the chilling defeat of the other races didn’t sit well, so they came after the humans. That was when the yin and yang saw the horrors of a loyal slave.</p>
<p>Their own people betrayed them, and in the end, they took their own lives, jumping into the abyss. However, what nobody would have expected was the end product of the union between light and darkness.</p>
<p>Nobody expected the coming of the Golden Lotus, the one that wielded both the power of life and death.</p>
<p>The Contractor was born full of hatred and resentment.</p>
<p>She grew up in the abyss where the creatures of darkness dwell, and through battles and tempering, she became a force that one day rose from the darkness and consumed the entire land.</p>
<p>Legends speak of her deeds.</p>
<p>She slaughtered the entire bloodline of dragons.</p>
<p>She made an entire empire of elves sever their ears, an apparent disrespect to their race.</p>
<p>She poisoned an entire ocean, killing millions of sea creatures, and that was just a few of the things she did. She, at some point, became the true definition of fear.</p>
<p>She became unstoppable...</p>
<p>The legends spoke of the day the 14 defenders, chosen by the universe to defend the 14 doom gates, came after her.</p>
<p>These 14 were said to hold unimaginable power; however, when they came, the Contractor was waiting for them, rendering 13 out of the 14 useless.</p>
<p>Only the Chaos Dragon managed to get to her. However, the legends spoke of when he defeated her and was about to drive his sword through her heart, but stopped mid-strike.</p>
<p>The Chaos Dragon let her go for reasons nobody knew.</p>
<p>That was the day the contractor put down her weapons. No one knows what was said between her and the Chaos Dragon.</p>
<p>But she didn’t go back to the abyss; instead, she turned to her life-giver side and decided to heal instead of destroy.</p>
<p>That was when she went from the Contractor to the Everlasting Healer, a being who was the sole reason the Primordial Chaos Dragon managed to stand tall when everyone was fleeing.</p>
<p>Today, the same being is awakening her innate powers, and nobody knows if she is coming out as the Contractor or the Everlasting Healer.</p>
<p>Either way, she earned her name among the legends who once ruled the heavens and stood tall before harrowing dangers.</p>
<p>Elaine was up in the air, surrounded by a golden lotus. The energy coming from it was both calming and evil. Kent stood below, watching this go on for two days until her true name appeared.</p>
<p>"She who holds the power over life and death. The one who holds dominion over who gets to live and die. She was the Contractor, the greatest killer to have ever lived.</p>
<p>Then she became the Healer, someone who healed the universe and protected it when needed. She was seen and rewarded, and she received a unique name.</p>
<p>Rise, Lianhua Zhenxin, the true heart of the lotus.</p>
<p>Rise, for your destiny awaits."</p>
<p>The golden lotus opened slowly, holding a pale-skinned beauty with golden hair. She wore only a lightweight gown, and her body was covered in golden angel wings.</p>
<p>When she opened her eyes and flapped her wings, a wave of healing energy washed over everyone, making them feel like they could drink poison and still live.</p>
<p>Elaine flapped her wings and slowly descended into Kent’s arms. She kissed him deeply and then tapped his forehead.</p>
<p>The moment she did, a message appeared before his vision.</p>
<p>⟦You have received the Everlasting Healer’s blessing⟧</p>
<p>⟦You have awakened an innate ability: Regenerate⟧</p>
<p>⟦Ability: Regenerate⟧</p>
<p>⟦Type: Innate⟧</p>
<p>⟦Stage: Super⟧</p>
<p>⟦Description: Your healing abilities are now amplified 400%. All injuries can be healed within seconds. Severed body parts can regenerate within minutes.⟧</p>
<p>"Thank you," Kent smiled.</p>
<p>Though his dragon’s health allows him to heal 130% faster, getting a boost of 400% isn’t something to take lightly.</p>
<p>"How do you feel?" Kent asked, but Elaine smiled and hugged him. "I feel fine. The new me is more than I could ask for. So I should be the one thanking you."</p>
<p>She smiled and put away her wings. However, her hair remained golden, and her clothes remained the same.</p>
<p>Not that the clothes mattered, but something connected to the soul has meaningful significance, so Kent was intrigued and planned on examining it later.</p>
<p>Kent’s women rushed over to Elaine, and like her other sisters, they examined every part of her new body. Afterwards, Vexthra’s turn finally came.</p>
