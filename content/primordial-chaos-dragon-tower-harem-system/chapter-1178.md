<p>[Pyris Pill World]</p>
<p>It had been over 370,000 years since Kent had come to this planet. He planned to come back someday, but he never expected it to be this early.</p>
<p>Of course, to him, it is merely a few months since he had last been there. But in reality, hundreds of thousands of years had passed.</p>
<p>Now, he and his two wives had appeared somewhere in Pyris Pill World.</p>
<p>Kent quickly opened the Soul Map to see if he could recognise where they had appeared. It didn’t take long for him to realise which city they were in.</p>
<p>"We are in the capital, Pyris City."</p>
<p>"I think so too. It has been 6,000 years, but I recognise some buildings," Liora said, looking around. She was now dressed in green with her true Pill Monarch Badge hanging on her lapel.</p>
<p>Kent only found out about her true alchemy rank today when they were getting ready to leave.</p>
<p>It turned out she was not only a genius when it came to assassination, but she was also a good alchemist—something she learned just to blend in with everyone.</p>
<p>From Faceless’s memories, Kent learned that one of the requirements he set for the assassins he had recruited was to have two identities that could not be faked.</p>
<p>Thanks to the Faceless Art, he can become anyone he wants at any time. However, his team of assassins had to learn. So instead of them straining themselves to copy others, he made them all learn different crafts.</p>
<p>Some became blacksmiths, artisans, alchemists, and many more.</p>
<p>This way, if they wanted a blacksmith dead, sending another blacksmith would not only allow them to get closer to their target much faster, but even the target wouldn’t know there was an assassin after them.</p>
<p>When he killed the demon lord, he had to pose as an artisan for three years. By the time he got the job done, he had forgotten about himself. Thankfully, one day, perhaps thanks to his subconscious, he came back to his home world.</p>
<p>The moment he got back, he recovered his true self. So, he made it a must to always remember to come back there whenever he forgot who his true self was.</p>
<p>So, seeing Liora as a Pill Monarch didn’t come as a shock to him. If anything, he started to see her as a true assassin who deserved to become a Faceless Agent.</p>
<p>She was also one of his trump cards to get back to the top chairs of the Midnight Shadows.</p>
<p>"Let’s head to the Pyris Pill Pagoda. There is someone I need to talk to."</p>
<p>Neomi wanted to say something, but she held on, knowing Kent would find out soon enough.</p>
<p>They started moving toward the Pill Pagoda, still standing at the center of the city. Kent had been there multiple times in the past, so he was happy to be back again.</p>

<p>"When I left 6,000 years ago, the Supreme Pill Families had already monopolised the pill market in all ten Pill Worlds. They had taken over every Pill Alliance and now call them Pill Families.</p>
