<p>Lyna had been on the move for the past 7 months straight. She went from one planet to another, one teleportation channel after another, always on the move, not even stopping to have a moment of respite.</p>
<p>She had faced many challenges along the way, but because of the importance of the trip she was on, she persevered through it all.</p>
<p>Finally, looking at the blue planet just within arm’s reach, she allowed herself to breathe and smile a little.</p>
<p>"I am finally here."</p>
<p>A few minutes later, a flying ship so large that it was able to transport thousands of people landed on Planet Mekyn, home of the Flame Humans, Ice Demons, and Elves.</p>
<p>The moment the ship landed—well, hovered a meter off the ground—Lyna jumped down and vanished into the crowd. Soon, she approached a teleportation platform.</p>
<p>"Hello, beauty, where do you intend to go today?" an Ice Demon, not too handsome nor too ugly, asked, a line of smoke escaping the corner of his lips as he pulled back a long pipe.</p>
<p>Lyna studied the man as if checking if he was bad news.</p>
<p>She had been on the move for a very long time, so she had come to know all the bad seeds that should be avoided and which ones to be friendly with. This allowed her to see the true character of anyone at first glance.</p>
<p>Thankfully, this person was not bad news.</p>
<p>"I would like to go to the Elven Kingdom today," Lyna said, keeping a polite smile on her face. She had learned to always smile, even when she didn’t want to.</p>
<p>Smiling while talking to strangers in a way makes them like you and actually deters them from having bad intentions, causing them to think twice before acting negatively.</p>
<p>"Elven Kingdom, huh?"</p>
<p>"Yes. I have business there, and the earlier I get there, the better for me."</p>
<p>The man nodded. "It will cost you, though."</p>
<p>"How much then?"</p>
<p>"Just 20,000 high-grade spirit stones."</p>
<p>Lyna narrowed her gaze at the man. Of course, 20,000 to ordinary folk is a lot. However, to someone like Lyna, it is nothing at all.</p>
<p>She was from an Immortal World, and as an immortal herself, she used Spirit Crystals and, on occasion, God Stones. Just one Spirit Crystal is equivalent to 10,000 high-grade Spirit Stones.</p>
<p>So she was rich. However, she learned that to avoid suspicion, making people perceive you as poor would make them less likely to rob you.</p>
<p>As expected, the man smiled guiltily and raised his finger. "Of course, there is a discount going around today, so that will be 15,000. That is already good enough, don’t you think, fairy?"</p>
<p>Lyna looked at him for a few minutes and then gave him the money. "Thanks for your generosity, Mr."</p>
<p>The Ice Demon smiled weakly and activated the teleportation formation.</p>
<p>"Have a safe trip, fairy."</p>
<p>With that, Lyna nodded and stepped into the open channel. Soon, her body vanished into thin air, leaving the man to sigh. Soon, however, a grin formed on his face.</p>
<p>"That is 5,000 profit for me. Less than I had hoped, but good enough." He pocketed the 5,000 spirit stones and only logged the 10,000.</p>
<p>Somewhere in the Elven Kingdom, a teleportation gate lit up and Lyna materialized.</p>
<p>"Welcome to Galen," an Elven maid said, bowing slightly to Lyna. She nodded with a calm expression.</p>
<p>Perhaps because Lyna was a Dragon and also a Heavenly Ascension Stage cultivator, the Elven maid lowered her pride and greeted her.</p>
<p>Of course, if Lyna had shown her true self when she talked to the Ice Demon, that cunning bastard would have quaked in his boots.</p>
<p>However, she merely put on a human appearance and masked her aura, making her appear both weak and less important. This allowed her to move much more freely and avoid many gazes.</p>
<p>Now, however, she had reached where she wanted, so she took on her demi-dragon form, allowing her to have golden hair, a golden and beautiful pair of horns, and, well, golden eyes.</p>
<p>She was from the Supreme Golden Dragon Race, one of the most powerful beasts in the Immortal World. They had several gods and even a supreme among them.</p>
<p>Even her mother is a god.</p>
<p>"I would like to be taken to the High Priestess of this kingdom," Lyna said.</p>
<p>"Of course. Please follow me."</p>
<p>The Elf led Lyna out of the teleportation gate, causing many who saw them to start wondering why there was a dragon among them now.</p>
<p>"I am sure she is here to seek an alliance with our race."</p>
<p>"I guess so. However, why come alone?"</p>
<p>"I wondered the same thing. If she and her kind wanted to seek an alliance with us, why then come alone? Why not bring more people as a sign of goodwill?"</p>
<p>"She even came through a teleportation gate. That doesn’t scream alliance-worthy to me."</p>
<p>"What do you losers expect? She is a dragon, and dragons are known for their arrogance."</p>
<p>More people wondered about various things as they watched the dragon being led toward a different teleportation gate.</p>
<p>On Planet Mekyn, the Elves were the smallest race, yet the strongest. They had more capable warriors who could easily dominate any battlefield, so from time to time, different races came to seek alliances with them.</p>
<p>Today, in their eyes, there was no difference. However, they couldn’t help wondering why Lyna came alone. Many came with dozens of people; however, she came alone.</p>
<p>Of course, they could only speculate because, in the end, she was here, and they had no idea why she came.</p>
<p>Soon, however, Lyna and the maid entered the teleportation gate and arrived in a different part of the city.</p>
<p>Lyna was led to a large mansion made from jade glass. At the entrance, three Spirit Lord Elves stopped them.</p>
<p>"This place is off-limits to anyone. The High Priestess is busy, and she would love not to be disturbed," one of the guards said, a smirk forming on his lips.</p>
<p>The maid turned and looked at Lyna, unsure of what to do. She was but a maid, after all.</p>
<p>Lyna, however, studied the guard for a few seconds before she lost interest.</p>
<p>Her eyes then glowed golden, and in a voice that shook the very ground, throwing the guards back and echoing across a several-mile radius, she spoke.</p>
<p>"My name is Lyna Thalindra Drakana, a messenger of the Flame Lord Agni, who is a direct subordinate of the Primordial Chaos Dragon. He has a message for the High Priestess, and I would very much like to deliver it before it is too late."</p>
<p>By the time her voice receded, the three guards had passed out. The authority she used wasn’t entirely her own. It was a combination of hers and the authority of the Chaos Dragon given to Lord Agni, the demigod Kent met in the Flame Tower back in Sonox.</p>
<p>The three Spirit Lords stood no chance at all.</p>
<p>Suddenly, three powerful figures appeared—two Elves and one Succubus.</p>
<p>The blue-haired Elf spoke, "I am High Priestess Runa. This is Protector Enyo and Lady Adina."</p>
<p>Lyna nodded. "I am Lyna. I have a message from Lord Agni, the Flame Lord, and one of the demigods in the Holy Fire Demon Castle."</p>
<p>The three ladies raised their brows, hearing what Lyna had just said.</p>
<p>"An immortal," Adina suddenly panicked.</p>
