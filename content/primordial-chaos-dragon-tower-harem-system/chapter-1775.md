<p>Kent already knew the gist of what he was required to do before signing the contract with the Red Bone Chaos Tribe. However, hearing his sister say it made him finally understand the severity of everything.</p>
<p>Everyone was counting on him, so he knew he could not let them down. No matter what, he must rise to the occasion as he had done in the past.</p>
<p>"The seven tribe leaders have an offer for you. But before they say anything, I want to ask you something. How confident are you that you can master a technique, perhaps one so powerful that you would still need it even after becoming an Eternal one day?</p>
<p>I know you were a monster back during the Sun Era, but this is a different world altogether. So, how confident are you?"</p>
<p>Kent’s gaze narrowed as he heard his sister’s question. He took a deep breath, then sighed.</p>
<p>"I only needed a few seconds to learn Primordial-grade techniques, so maybe I will need about an hour or a day to master any technique you throw my way."</p>
<p>Fiannarel looked at Kent for a few seconds, then smirked.</p>
<p>"I guess we can go see them now. After all, if everything turns out in our favor in the end, we will have more time to catch up."</p>
<p>"That is true."</p>
<p>Kent took Fiannarel’s hand, and they started walking toward the building where the seven tribe leaders were waiting. If it were up to Fiannarel, they would all bring out their treasures and hand them over to Kent.</p>
<p>She trusted her brother, whom she had seen accomplish one incredible feat after another back in the Infinity Universe.</p>
<p>Now, learning that he was the legendary Lord Genesis, the father of all swordsmen, she believed he could achieve even more.</p>
<p>"Are you nervous?" she asked along the way, but Kent shook his head.</p>
<p>"Not really. I just don’t want to be stuck here for long. As much as I enjoy the life I am living here, the Infinity Universe is in danger, and my presence is required. So no matter what, I have to return home before it is too late."</p>
<p>Fiannarel nodded.</p>
<p>"Don’t worry. If you are anything like you were before, then I trust you will make it out. After all, you claim you had figured out reincarnation, so who is to say this isn’t all part of your big, elaborate plan?"</p>
<p>Kent chuckled.</p>
<p>"Let’s not blow things out of proportion, big sister. For all we know, I came here by accident."</p>
<p>"Somehow, I doubt that," Fiannarel laughed. Soon, they approached the building.</p>
<p>Kent, however, couldn’t help but wonder if all of this was merely one huge coincidence or just another event in his life that he had never known existed, like what happened when he absorbed the Blood Heart Seed he received from the Bloodvein Tribe.</p>
<p>Deep down, he doubted any of this was part of an elaborate plan orchestrated by the Spell of Origin. The thirteen soul fragments he had sent across space and time never made it here.</p>
<p>So naturally, the Spell of Origin clearly had no influence over this. That made him wonder once again if any of this had something to do with the Chaos Ascension King.</p>
<p>’Can’t be a coincidence, can it?’</p>
<p>They stepped into the hall, and at once, he felt seven terrifying gazes land on him. However, instead of feeling threatened, he seemed rather calm, almost unbothered that he was merely a Demigod standing before seven Eternals.</p>
<p>"Wow, tough crowd," he said jokingly, causing the seven tribe leaders to take deep breaths and reassess the brat standing before them.</p>
<p>He was no ordinary person, and they could finally see that. So they all changed their approach and, rather than seeing him as a brat, they chose to regard him as someone worthy of their trust and respect.</p>
<p>Jyoti Lata spoke.</p>
<p>"Kent Madson, these are the leaders of the other tribes in the Chaos Chasm. I invited them here following a discovery I made about the Chaos Ascension King Trial after I saw what you can do.</p>
<p>I don’t know if my deduction is correct, but I believe that to pass the Chaos Ascension King Trial, one must be able to harness the power of all seven tribes.</p>
<p>In other words, to pass the trial, you must learn all seven cultivation arts left behind by the Chaos Ascension King.</p>
<p>The other tribe leaders have agreed to give you their techniques, and so will I. Now, we just need to know if you can handle it.</p>
<p>We seven are all Eternals, yet none of us has mastered more than four levels of our respective cultivation arts, so you can only imagine how complex and demanding they are."</p>
<p>Parand took over.</p>
<p>"Also, to master all the techniques, you need to be exceptionally strong in body, soul, and mind. I have already witnessed you temper your body, and I have to say you are quite talented. However, how strong are your mind and soul?"</p>
<p>To answer their questions, Kent smiled and extended his soul, covering the entire space.</p>
<p>Then, in what could be considered the snap of a finger, the entire area was flooded with soul essence, so much so that all seven tribe leaders jumped to their feet.</p>
<p>Then their eyes widened when Kent spoke directly into their minds.</p>
<p>’Pretty strong, don’t you all think?’</p>
<p>"This..." Namarie tried to speak, but no words came out.</p>
<p>"Just how vast is your soul?" Zdzislav, the tribe chief who liked to flirt, asked.</p>
<p>"As vast as a universe, for lack of better words."</p>
<p>His soul had indeed become vast and boundless before coming here. However, after continuously leveling up and forming more cores, it had become even denser and far more powerful.</p>
<p>If he were still in the Infinity Universe, he would have tried creating more soul warriors. However, he could only wait until he returned.</p>
<p>Fiannarel smiled happily from the side, proud that her little brother had impressed the seven tribe leaders. This meant they could finally make headway.</p>
<p>"I don’t know how complex a cultivation technique that surpasses the Eternal realm can be, but I trust and believe in myself. I can master it in no time.</p>
<p>And before you call me arrogant, know that I have a record of mastering almost any technique in a less than thousandth of the time it would take the next prodigy.</p>
<p>So you can all rest assured that if what Tribe Leader Jyoti Lata said is indeed the case, your freedom will soon be yours."</p>
<p>They could no longer doubt his words, for he had already shocked them. While he could not even tickle their minds or souls, the mere fact that he could tap directly into their mental link and speak to them left them stunned.</p>
<p>They are Eternals, and he isn’t even a god yet.</p>
<p>Kent had proven himself to them, and with that, they were ready to hand over the most valuable treasure of their tribe.</p>
<p>"I will go first since I was the one who started all this," Jyoti Lata said, taking a step forward.</p>
<p>Kent took a deep breath and prepared himself for what was about to happen. If he had mastered all their techniques, perhaps returning home would already be within reach.</p>
<p>’I have only glimpsed the rank above the Sword Celestial stage, but instead of Primordial Essence, I saw Eternal Essence. If these techniques are anything like that, then I am about to experience the greatest moment of my life after thirteen reincarnations,’ he thought with a smile.</p>
<p>’Tower, are we set?’ he asked his assistant, and the response came immediately.</p>
<p>&lt;We are good to go, Master.&gt;</p>
