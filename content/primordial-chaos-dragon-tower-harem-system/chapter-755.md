<p>"What happened to you eight... Just a few days ago, you all appeared like greenhouse flowers, but now, I can’t even begin to fathom how this was even possible." Queen Jascia shook her head, looking at the eight half-elves who had taken Flame Valley.</p>
<p>They smile, feeling proud of their minimal achievements. Kent has already made them aware he has big plans for them, so they knew soon, their Queen would have yet another thing to be shocked about.</p>
<p>She turned to Kent and said, "Thank you."</p>
<p>Kent nodded with a smile, standing beside Uncle Drake.</p>
<p>She didn’t need an explanation to know that the half-elves standing before her were no longer simple. She felt the aura coming from them, and as a warrior, she recognized their strength.</p>
<p>King Thalirion and the Ten Elders were also present since Kent and his team first came to the palace before heading to their various homes.</p>
<p>They also recognise the improvement based on the aura oozing from the Half-Elves. They knew things had finally turned for the better for them.</p>
<p>"They’ve all worked hard, and the reward is as you all can see. Of course, with me around, soon enough, the whole world will understand the advantage of having two sets of blood flowing through your bodies."</p>
<p>The King and the elders couldn’t deny his words. They knew Kent was more than capable of doing that. They now more than ever trusted him to the point that they would do anything he asked them.</p>
<p>Hearing his promise wasn’t one they would doubt. And Kent isn’t a greedy person. So far, the Half-elves are blessing him with millions of soul essence every single day.</p>
<p>This means that while going all out to help him isn’t required, he understood the value in quality, so he would do all he could, and regardless of how much it cost him, he would transform these half-elves into a powerful race.</p>
<p>The stronger they become, the more powerful he also becomes, so being greedy will be depriving himself of an opportunity to build a universe inside his soul.</p>
<p>"By the way, this is Uncle Drake. He is a Flame Dragon, and while there are flame dragons in this world, they cannot be compared to him.</p>
<p>We met at the Valley, and one thing led to another; he will be staying here for at least the next three years. I believe with him here, you wouldn’t have to worry about anything."</p>
<p>The elders were visibly shaken when they heard what Kent said.</p>
<p>They’d been wondering who the terrifying dragon behind Kent was, but now, hearing he was on their side and would be their guardian for the next few years, they couldn’t help but shake a bit.</p>
<p>"We greet Senior Drake. Please take care of us for the next few years you will be staying here," the elders said, bowing slightly.</p>
<p>While they could tell Kent was indeed young, though terrifying, they were also able to determine that Uncle Drake was old and powerful.</p>
<p>So they paid their respect, and Uncle Drake nodded.</p>
<p>"No need to be polite. I will merely be staying here as a means to deter all those with evil intent. As to whether I will even get a chance to do that, considering none of you is any weaker than I am, I guess we will soon find out."</p>
<p>The elders smiled, reading the subtle compliment hidden within Uncle Drake’s words. He acknowledged them and made sure they knew he wasn’t there to steal their thunder.</p>
<p>But of course, when they couldn’t handle it, he would step in. Because while nobody would want to admit it, even Kent himself knew the dragon standing beside him was a terror.</p>
<p>Even Kent doesn’t think he can defeat him.</p>
<p>The drake he met a week and a few days ago was no more. This time, something primal, something straight from the Primordial Era, had awakened, and while Kent could sense it, he knew something was still hidden from him.</p>
<p>He didn’t know if the universe was trying to protect his feelings, but something about Uncle Drake still made his skin crawl. That alone made him wonder just what could make him, a chaos dragon, feel that way.</p>
<p>"You all can retire back to your homes. Tomorrow morning, all eight of you should assemble here. Of course, be prepared for anything," Queen Jascia said, and everyone scattered.</p>
<p>"Uncle Drake, your lodging will be in the palace and has already been prepared. You can head there when you are done."</p>
<p>Uncle Drake nodded and turned to Kent. "If you want a place to get a stronger flame spirit, try entering the Hell Zone in the Ancient Battlefield of Parting. Of course, be prepared for the dangers that await you.</p>
<p>You will die before you know how it happened." After those words, he turned and started following the maids, who, judging by the look on their faces, were already charmed by the dragon man.</p>
<p>After he left, King Thalirion looked at Kent. "Where did you get such a monster from? Not even the Crystal Dragon King has this much presence. In fact, comparing him to this senior is a huge insult to senior Drake."</p>
<p>"Honestly, I don’t know how dangerous he is, but fear not, he is a trusted companion." Everyone accepted that answer and later dispersed. Kent followed the king and the queen back to the meeting hall since they had something else to report to him.</p>
<p>It turned out they would now be meeting with the elves after the Flame Tower opening. Knowing the half-elves are rising, they want to make peace. However, whether their intentions are noble or not, they will soon find out.</p>
<p>Tomorrow, the tower will open, and they will head to Kiryu, the kingdom of the Qilin race, who are the leaders of the beast races. The tower is located there, and that is where all other races will send their best and brightest.</p>
<p>---</p>
<p>[Universe Core. Home of Lady Universe]</p>
<p>The beautiful lady sat in her chair with a smile on her face. She had just watched Kent and Uncle Drake stand side by side, and while it wouldn’t be amusing to someone like the half-elves who saw it, she had a different experience.</p>
<p>She had already seen all of Kent’s past lives, and that was the main reason she was smiling. Of course, it didn’t take long for the Enchantress’ voice to enter her ears.</p>
<p>"So they started as companions this time, huh... I wonder what will happen when they get back their memories of how they met during the primordial era."</p>
<p>Lady Universe laughed, "They will probably try to kill each other, knowing them."</p>
<p>"I most certainly would love to see the Flame King and the Chaos Dragon exchange blows and claws one of these days."</p>
<p>The Enchantress chuckled, "It will be a good one, considering our dear Chaos would most certainly want to fight the Flame King again."</p>
<p>"Well, Chaos already seems to notice there is something icky about the Flame King. He doesn’t know it was his memories, but of course, I may be the Universe’s heart, but I don’t control the flow of the Universe’s fate, so we can only wait."</p>
<p>"We should probably prepare for that battle; after all, I can already feel it brewing in the air."</p>
<p>The two ladies were almost happy to see a battle that once shook the primordial Era. The Chaos dragon and the Flame King met as enemies but fell as brothers.</p>
<p>Now, they are back again, and as to whether the rivalry will continue, only time will tell...</p>
