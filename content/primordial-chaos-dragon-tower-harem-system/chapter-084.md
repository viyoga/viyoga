<p>"For my first wish, I wish for something that can transport me anywhere I want to go in the universe," Kent said, holding his breath.</p>
<p>While on Earth, he had spent many years learning about the nature of wishes.</p>
<p>He had watched countless movies that explored the concept of wishes and genies, and back then, he often fantasized about having the chance to make one himself.</p>
<p>Of course, his wish at the time was simple: to be able to use his dick fully and experience pleasure. However, that was just a fairytale in his mind back then.</p>
<p>Now, things were different. He was no longer in a world where cultivation was impossible, and the chance to make real wishes was finally within his grasp.</p>
<p>His goal was to break the system of wishes.</p>
<p>Many would wish for riches, charm, or revenge, but he wanted something he could use forever. Of course, this wish was intended to help him visit the god realm—specifically, the Realm of Hatred and Resentment.</p>
<p>He wanted to find a way to meet Vexthra, his first wife and the goddess who had transmigrated him to this new world and given him everything he now had.</p>
<p>Although he could speak to her every 100 days for five minutes, he needed a way to see her, even if only for a day or an hour. He had no desire to venture recklessly into the god realm, where he could be obliterated at any moment.</p>
<p>He only wanted to see Vexthra, with whom he hoped to spend time occasionally.</p>

<p>- Soul Essence: 100,000 (Max)</p>

<p>- Once requirements are met, the orb needs 96 hours to activate.</p>
<p>- Once used, the orb goes into a 60-day cooldown.</p>

<p>- You will be forcefully returned after 72 hours.</p>

<p>"All these options are good. Tower, which one should I choose?" Kent asked. All three options were good and could allow him to visit Vexthra, but there were limitations.</p>
<p>[The master can choose any of the three options, but I prefer the third option. The reason is simple: the third option allows the master to bring back any item of choice.</p>
<p>Of course, storing something inside your soul while you haven’t awakened your soul sea is impossible.</p>
<p>But the master has Tower storage, something that is akin to soul storage. This means you can bring back any item of your choice.</p>
<p>Of course, the cost is huge, but the master has the soul bead, which can power the compass five times.</p>
<p>Once I am awakened, we can tweak it to add more time. But comparing the price to the chance for the master to see the Goddess, I would advise the master to pick the third option.]</p>
<p>"Then I choose the third option," Kent said with a smile.</p>
<p>Immediately after he gave the order, a small compass appeared in his hands. As soon as he touched it, he could feel the power brewing within it.</p>
<p>[Message from the Enchantress: You have used one of your three wishes. If you ever want to make a wish, just tap into the connection that has formed between us and say your wish.</p>
