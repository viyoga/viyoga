<p>"Zaila dear, why don’t you wait for me on the bed? I’ll be back in a few hours," Kent said, kissing her in front of her sisters. Zaila, of course, wanted nothing more than to get what she was after.</p>
<p>It had been over a month since she woke up from the Nightmare Chamber, and aside from the need to tell Kent what went down inside, she first wanted to become his woman.</p>
<p>She had held onto this for the thousands of years she miraculously spent inside the Nightmare Chamber. So, hearing her turn was about to come, she immediately ran into the room, not minding her sisters.</p>
<p>Kent then looked at Nara and Lady Lani. "Your turn will come soon, so relax a little. A day at most." They nodded before he focused his attention on Elaine and Lady Vari.</p>
<p>"It looks like you two are blending in," Kent said with a smile, scanning their bodies from top to bottom. This gaze made the two ladies blush.</p>
<p>Kent just laughed. He knew they had fallen long ago, thanks to the shameless goddess currently smiling at him.</p>
<p>Vexthra used the corner of her eye to point in Velyra’s direction before licking her lips as she smiled at him. It was clear she meant nothing good.</p>
<p>"Velyra, I will be away for a few days. Make sure they don’t pollute you. And don’t worry—24 days here is just a day outside, so you have loads of time to spend with your goddess."</p>
<p>Kent waved them goodbye and left. The moment he was gone, Vexthra dropped her clothes and gestured for the ladies to go back to the Yin pool.</p>
<p>Naturally, they pulled the redskin beauty with them.</p>
<p>Kent appeared inside the mountain garden where the three lolis were resting in their mini-bikinis, sipping Mai Tais as they gazed over the city.</p>
<p>Kent couldn’t help but shake his head, watching the display. They were too small, so the setting just seemed funny.</p>
<p>He pictured Li Hua being with them, but unfortunately, the small human had fallen in love with meditation, so she wasn’t around much.</p>
<p>"Looks like you three don’t have anything to do. Why don’t I put you to work?" Kent said, walking toward them. They stood up and smiled at him.</p>
<p>⟪Master, you are back⟫, Gaia said, jumping into his arms. The other two weren’t far behind. Kent playfully fell back, making the three lolis giggle as they played with his hair.</p>
<p>"Since when did you three become jumpy?" Kent asked—not that he minded spending time with his three cosmic assistants.</p>
<p>⟪We just missed you. You don’t know that while it was just a week for you, it’s been months for us. And since we don’t have anything going on, we kinda missed you.</p>
<p>Plus, we already created the design for eight more Undeads, so play with us for a while⟫</p>
<p>Dori spoke like a spoiled child despite being millions of years old.</p>
<p>"Fine, we can play for two hours since I have a beauty waiting for me," Kent said, causing the three lolis to smirk. They knew their master was a spoiled honey bastard who couldn’t stay away from his ladies.</p>
<p>Not that they minded. They were the ones creating all the things for him and his ladies.</p>
<p>Kent, true to his word, spent the next two hours playing with them, which mostly involved wrestling.</p>
<p>Knowing they were basically children and couldn’t use their full strength lest they risk destroying the tower, he made himself weak, allowing them to win all the rounds.</p>
<p>Surprisingly, they were very happy whenever they won a match. Kent just let them be happy for the two-hour duration.</p>
<p>⟪Thanks for spending time with us, Master. I will create a unique skill for you when I have time⟫ Akira said with a smile.</p>
<p>⟪I will allow you to pick one item from the Gaia Store. Know that this is a separate store with only my personal collection⟫, Gaia also said.</p>
<p>Dori just smiled.</p>
<p>⟪I don’t have anything material to give you, but I know a place where you can go to get something valuable. When you have time, I’ll take you there⟫</p>
<p>Kent nodded.</p>
<p>Meanwhile, far at the core of the universe, the lady watching every second of Kent’s life play out smiled and spoke,</p>
<p>"Are you sure you want to give it to him that easily? I could have sworn you said you wouldn’t allow him to have it until he fulfills the promise he made to you billions of years ago."</p>
<p>⟪I guess he’s growing on me. Plus, since I made it for him, he can have it now. It doesn’t change anything⟫</p>
<p>"I hope you’re right. This brat tends to forget things easily," the lady said again.</p>
<p>⟪I am his mind, so I’ll remind him—even if it takes millions of years⟫</p>
<p>"You really—... Ah, forget about it. Do as you please. I’ll remain here, watching everything play out."</p>
<p>⟪Don’t worry, Mother. He will notice you soon enough, and knowing him, he’ll find his way to you⟫. Dori smiled, watching Kent pinch Gaia’s nose.</p>
<p>"I will surely wait for that day to come," she smiled.</p>
<p>⟪Of course, if you’re too hungry to wait, you can always create a clone to hunt him down and get what you want. I know you can easily do that. After all, you are the universe⟫</p>
<p>"Go away, Doriana. I’m done talking to you." The lady blushed at Dori’s cheeky words. However, a wave of determination appeared in her eyes.</p>
<p>"I might just take her idea if he doesn’t find me soon." A smile that would shatter planets and destroy galaxies appeared on her lips.</p>
<p>Far in a world nobody dares step into, inside a temple no one dares enter, a beauty whose hair falls down her back, touching the floor, smiles for some reason.</p>
<p>She is the Ancient Enchantress, a being born on the same day the universe came into existence.</p>
<p>"What a slut," she muttered, causing a new voice to echo through her temple.</p>
<p>"As if you are any different," the enchantress smirked.</p>
