<p>"Here I come."</p>
<p>The classic line, spoken only by Kaizo, the Divine Angel Titan, spread across the battlefield.</p>
<p>From within the portal, Kaizo, clad in armless golden armor, descended, his fist crackling with lightning.</p>
<p>BANG</p>
<p>His fist connected with the jaw of a monarch, the first to appear from the rift. The smug expression on his demon face was wiped away by the fist.</p>
<p>His jaw shattered, and his teeth flew out, scattering across the ground.</p>
<p>But it didn’t end there. Kaizo’s fist, which was covered in potent lightning, had disoriented the poor guy, allowing a certain archeress to also make her move.</p>
<p>"Astra Arrow."</p>
<p>An arrow was fired, which streaked across the horizon, drawing a beautiful line like a beautifully painted grid line by a seasoned painter before going through the demon’s head.</p>
<p>His brain exploded, and blood gushed out, painting the sky.</p>
<p>Up in the air, Unity appeared, holding her bow in her first grip. She is an archer but also a painter, so she started balancing the two, thereby increasing the lethality of her arrows.</p>
<p>Although her two primordial spirits are still sleeping, she is in no way weak.</p>
<p>"Showoff."</p>
<p>Kaizo ignored the annoying Unity, who, aside from her uncanny accuracy with the bow, excels in annoying him.</p>
<p>It started as a rivalry at first, which it still was; however, they learned to work together to achieve their goal.</p>
<p>Kaizo is a brute with so much attack power. So, mostly, whenever he lands a blow, the lightning he imbues in his punch mostly disorients the targets, making them falter for a second.</p>
<p>That is when Unity mostly comes in.</p>
<p>She never misses, so Kaizo trusted her to get the job done.</p>
<p>Now that they are up against thousands of beings far beyond their current capabilities, he knew with Unity keeping an eye out, no single warrior would slip up on him.</p>
<p>Naturally, Kaizo is more than happy to get the battle going even if the odds stacked against him are far beyond his current strength.</p>
<p>Kent watched as his legion descended on everyone.</p>
<p>The Undead Legion led by Kaizo came.</p>
<p>The Eternal Wrath Legion led by Aetheris, the flame brute.</p>
<p>His wives, led by Vexthra. Even Lady Enzi and Liora came through the portal.</p>
<p>But they aren’t the only ones. The Bloodvein Tribe, led by Tribe Chief Malthar, also came to help Kent save this world.</p>
<p>The portal that brought Kaizo and the rest also came for the ladies who had just settled down on Planet Celestial Gate.</p>
<p>Now, all they can do is fight.</p>
<p>From the rift, hundreds of monarchs keep coming.</p>
<p>Of course, Kent knew his legion alone was not enough to hold them, so when he saw his goddess wife appear, he immediately contacted her.</p>
<p>[Vexthra dear, you don’t think now that you are in a different universe, you can use your abilities to clear the battlefield, right?]</p>
<p>Vexthra, who heard Kent’s words in her head, smiled.</p>
<p>’I can’t. The least I can do is face off against other gods, which I can sense are actively scanning you and two others.’</p>
<p>[Let me guess, their targets are the extremely handsome but not quite close to my level white-haired young man over there, and the beautiful redhead spilling blood.]</p>
<p>’Yes. They can’t touch you simply because you are not from this universe, but they can curse you, which I believe they have already.’</p>
<p>[I feel fine, love, so I guess their curse didn’t work on me.]</p>
<p>’True. But they can break the rules and attack you. Also, if the gods attack, I can protect you and the lady, but not the white-haired man over there. For some reason, I feel like helping him directly will be a bad thing.</p>
<p>Honestly, I can’t even bring myself to think maybe they want me to help her. It seems they are looking for more excuses to kill him directly.’</p>
<p>[Then we shouldn’t help him directly then. However, we can help his friends, right? After all, there is no harm in helping the friends of a Paragon, the last time I checked.]</p>
<p>’Yes. Alina is already on it.’</p>
<p>Vexthra looked around and saw that, despite bringing with her over four dozen warriors, the odds were still against them.</p>
<p>However, just when she was about to think of a plan, she spotted seven demons appear on the battlefield. Her eyes narrowed, scanning them.</p>
<p>"Strange, these seven aren’t like the others."</p>
<p>Vexthra scanned a few warriors from Earth and tried to compare their auras. However, she arrived at the same conclusion.</p>
<p>"They reek of death and resentment." As the goddess of hatred and resentment, she was able to discern some form of familiarity from them.</p>
<p>This caused her to narrow her eyes, sensing something suspicious. She traced their aura and saw they came from the white-haired young man currently facing off against six people she knew deep down had no business fighting.</p>
<p>’Kent, do you have a way to talk to the Paragon?’</p>
<p>[I do. What do you want to ask him?]</p>
<p>’Ask him whether these seven demons are from the underworld.’</p>
<p>[Okay.]</p>
<p>Vexthra could sense the aura of a god on them. In fact, this aura seemed to be influencing them somehow. Naturally, she wouldn’t have minded, but she could sense the aura of resentment on them.</p>
<p>That aura shouldn’t come from them, considering they also have the aura of death on them. So she could only come to a sudden conclusion—the seven demons were not happy about whatever or whoever released them.</p>
<p>’They are warriors, and to think somebody is pulling strings to limit them. I hate gods that play games with mortals.’</p>
<p>[My dear goddess, it seems these seven are from the underworld. They came about thanks to the mercy of the underworld god.]</p>
<p>’I see.’</p>
<p>Kent, who stabbed his claw into the chest of a monarch warrior, felt something strange come from Vexthra, making him raise his brow.</p>
<p>[Vexthra dear, you aren’t about to pick a fight with an underworld king, are you?]</p>
<p>’I hate gods who play with mortals.’</p>
<p>Kent grinned. [Go kick his ass, will you?]</p>
<p>’I will be back.’ Vexthra vanished from the battlefield, and Kent, who knew a god was about to have a very nasty day, grinned wildly.</p>
<p>"Oh, how I wish I could see that."</p>
<p>[Don’t worry, big brother, we will record everything.] Just when Kent thought everyone had appeared, the three lolis appeared, clad in battlewear, even though they couldn’t fight.</p>
<p>"You three are angels." The three mischievous lolis vanished from the battlefield too.</p>
<p>Kent could only grin happily. Although they were still losing, the fact that his goddess wife was about to have some action made him very happy.</p>
<p>Vexthra had been frustrated countless times when her sisters fought, and she couldn’t do anything. As a goddess, unless a god came after her man, she couldn’t do anything. So being presented with this chance felt like a once-in-a-lifetime opportunity.</p>
<p>Is he worried his wife will lose? Definitely not.</p>
<p>Is he worried his wife will succeed in killing a god? Kent knew that it was less likely to happen.</p>
<p>It wasn’t that Vexthra was weak in any way. If anything, she could easily kill a god since her law had already reached the threshold to become a supreme.</p>
<p>The only reason she hasn’t taken the supreme tribulation is because she was also walking the ancient path. She wanted to become a dual supreme, someone who walks both the Law Path and the Ancient Path.</p>
<p>However, Vexthra didn’t want to make things hard for her man, so she wouldn’t kill the Underworld King (god).</p>
<p>However, she was going to have a serious conversation with him, making sure he understood that messing with mortals had consequences.</p>
