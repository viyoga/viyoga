<p>"One last thing, if I want to enter the Upside Down Underworld Forest, where do I get an underworld token?"</p>

<p>"You are kidding, right?" Ryen asked, looking at Kent with a stunned expression.</p>
<p>"I am dead serious. I want to get one since my reason for coming here is to enter the Upside Down Underworld Forest. This is why I would like to know where to go to get one."</p>
<p>"Haven’t you heard?"</p>
<p>"Heard what?" Kent stared at Ryan with a calm yet questioning gaze.</p>
<p>He seemed to be missing something, which is perfectly understandable. It has been billions of years already since he ventured into the Upside Down Underworld Forest, so it is normal if some things skipped him.</p>
<p>However, from the way the siblings are looking at him, it seems something big happened that he would soon be made aware of.</p>
<p>Ryan stared at his sister and then sighed... "From what I know, about half a million years ago, the Upside Down Underworld Forest fell."</p>
<p>"Fell? As in the upside down fell to the ground?"</p>
<p>"Yes. It fell, and nobody knew what caused it. However, from what I was told, when it did, something nobody was prepared for stirred within it.</p>
<p>All who went to inspect never returned. Later, rumours spread that the mysterious Midnight Shadows sent immortals to come and check." Ryan paused and calculated his words before continuing.</p>
<p>"They said twenty immortals entered and only one returned, barely hanging on to his life. They said he said only one word before passing out. He never woke up. Also, nobody was able to decipher the meaning behind that word."</p>
<p>"And what word was that?"</p>
<p>Ryan hesitated, but he said it, "Zhaeryn."</p>
<p>Kent’s eyes widened as he came to a sudden stop, his heartbeat rising in an instant.</p>
<p>Ryan and Ryen also stopped and turned to look at Kent. They were shocked to see he had turned pale. Naturally, they seemed to think he understood the word.</p>
<p>However, they didn’t dare ask him.</p>
<p>"Are you all right?" Instead, they decided to check up on him. Hearing Ryen’s concerned voice, Kent pulled himself together.</p>
<p>"I am fine." He let a smile appear on his face, but the two siblings knew he was merely putting up a farce. Kent was not fine one bit.</p>
<p>However, yet again, they didn’t dare ask him anything aside from looking at him. Eventually, Kent recovered and returned to his former self.</p>
<p>"Please tell me they’ve sealed the entrance after the fall," Kent suddenly asked.</p>
<p>"They did. An Immortal Lord from the Midnight Shadows came to seal it personally. From what I’ve heard, only a select few from the Midnight Shadows can enter.</p>
<p>Of course, all of this is what people said, and I heard. I apologise if I didn’t know everything."</p>
<p>"Thank you," Kent said and resumed flying. Ryan and Ryen followed.</p>
<p>Kent remained quiet, but his mind was spinning like a ball rolling down a steep slope. He had wanted to hear something dangerous, but what he had chanced upon was beyond dangerous.</p>
<p>’It can’t be. It can’t be them. It is still too early to be them.’</p>
