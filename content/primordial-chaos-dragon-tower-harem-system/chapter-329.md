<p>"990 Billion"</p>
<p>"1 trillion"</p>
<p>"1.2 Trillion". Sword King Shawn placed his bid.</p>
<p>"1.4 Trillion." The wicked Poison Witch, for the first time since the auction started, also joined.</p>
<p>"1.7 Trillion" Pill Saint Barton, the same pill saint that Kent bought the Venom from placed his bid.</p>

<p>But the bidding grew, and more started adding their voices. It was like some of them had prior notice that such an item would be sold, so they were throwing in their bids like hungry lions.</p>

<p>"Brat, what is your name again...Yes, Kent. Why are you quiet. Are you out of cash after spending it on stupid stuff?" The voice contains malice, and it’s full of mocking.</p>

<p>The moment the voice spoke, no new bid came.</p>
<p>People were trying to see if they recognized that name, but even half a minute late, nobody said anything until Sword King Shawn spoke.</p>
<p>"Evil Bat, what are you doing here?" His question was simple, but an intense hatred could be sensed behind his words.</p>
<p>In fact, aside from his hatred, the sudden mention of the name ’Evil Bat’ made many breaks into a cold sweat.</p>
<p>"Old Shawn, I heard your disciple wandered into my territory a couple of years ago but couldn’t come out in one piece. Although it’s been a couple of years, I would like to offer my condolences."</p>
<p>The voice spoke again, and the laughter that followed made the sword King’s eyes shine with intense bloodlust.</p>
<p>His sword intent and Sword Qi threaten to gush out. But he held himself back because that would be a grave disaster.</p>
<p>"Wait...Evil Bat. Is that the sect master of the Evil sect?" Somebody asked, and like a plague had fallen over the place, people turned pale. The name is like a disease to many.</p>
<p>"Wait, that cannot be him, right? The Evil Sect doesn’t come around these places. The last time they were here was when they attacked the Everlasting Immortal Sword Sect.</p>

<p>"I think this time, the danger that has fallen on the brat is not one he can be arrogant about. The stakes have risen."</p>

<p>"Indeed. Go back to when you came from before things turn bad for you" Pill Saint Barton said.</p>
<p>"Hahaha," He laughed in a rather sick tone, "the last time I checked, this auction is open to everyone and since you cannot threaten me, I have equal right to be here. Unless the auction is going against their own words."</p>
<p>Everyone became quiet. Indeed, the auction is open to everyone, and since he came with an invitation, he was allowed entry.</p>
<p>"And before you all think you have what it took to come against me, you might want to first become Root Soverigns...Hahahaha"</p>
<p>The faces of all the big shots inside the auctioned house turn grim. The Evil Bat is now a Root Sovereign, just a stage away from becoming a Kingdom Protector or a Forest King.</p>
<p>That is just something they cannot go against.</p>
<p>Just like that, although they hated the evil practitioner, they were forced to back down.</p>
<p>Meanwhile, a Quest message appeared before Kent, but he brushed it aside for the moment it was revealed that the voice was from an evil practitioner; something in him snapped.</p>
<p>"Tsk, to think a mere nobody portraying as a Root Soverign can be arrogant in front of me. When have your wings grown to that level Bat?" Kent asked in a voice filled with disdain.</p>
<p>"Gasp"</p>
<p>This was the reaction that echoed in the auction house. Even the evil bat gasped, for he never expected someone like Kent to make such a comment.</p>
<p>"You came look for trouble with me so what, you can fluent your stolen wealth...Hahaha. I don’t blame you. It’s the fault of the seniors in the kingdoms that allowed you to grow to this stage.</p>
<p>But I will make you a promise today. You will die whether you come after me or not. And I will be taking the body of the Lava wolf with me.</p>
<p>So please, do your worst, and please, be sure to try your best for your ending is neigh."</p>
<p>Kent’s words were spoken like a true man. This made some of the seniors bend their heads in shame.</p>
<p>The bat came to intimidate them, and he succeeded, yet a mere brat who hadn’t even reached the Root Great Sage stage yet just shut him up.</p>
<p>That is just bold.</p>

<p>"I know some of you seniors were planning to go after the one who will end up buying the Lava wolf body. I will be buying it, and so I ask the seniors not to come after me, for I have an offer none of you can reject."</p>

<p>"Please Kingdom Protector, I know you don’t deal with minimal things like this, but I want to leave something in your care," Kent spoke, showing he knew the kingdom protector who appeared the second day was still around.</p>
<p>Unlike most, Ingrid was able to see everyone, and she was the one who told him the kingdom protector was around and in VIP room 1</p>
