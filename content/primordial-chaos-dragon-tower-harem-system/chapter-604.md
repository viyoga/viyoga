<p>While his wives were away fighting, Kent was in the mountain garden wrestling with Gaia, Dori, and Akira.</p>
<p>Gaia created a wrestling ring for them, so they truly indulged in the wrestling fantasy inside the mountain garden. No one could blame them. They were just spending time together, and since everyone was happy, the fun continued.</p>
<p>Kent was losing miserably.</p>
<p>⟪I can’t believe you are this weak, master... Makes me wonder where all your bragging was coming from,⟫</p>
<p>Gaia said, holding one of Kent’s legs up while her body leaned on him. She was clad in designer sportswear, showing professionalism.</p>
<p>Akira struck the ground three times, and since Kent couldn’t move, it signaled she was the winner.</p>
<p>"I am strong; it’s just that you three are monsters. That’s why I keep losing." Kent sighed, his expression defeated. This made the three lolis smile, showing satisfaction in defeating their master.</p>
<p>⟪Don’t cry, master. One of these days, you’ll rise to our level, and perhaps by then, you won’t look defeated after we beat you miserably.⟫ Dori and her sisters giggled.</p>
<p>Kent just sat down and smiled.</p>
<p>He could tell his three assistants were genuinely happy defeating him. It was weird because they were very happy, and he didn’t know if they truly were billions of years old.</p>
<p>Whenever they defeated him, the satisfaction, the smile, and the genuinely ecstatic expression said it all; they were thrilled, and that strangely made him very happy.</p>
<p>Though he was playing pretend, their happiness said everything he needed to know. Kent looked at the three sisters and smiled.</p>
<p>"Akira, you’re next," he called out, and Akira came running. She was throwing punches in the air and moving her legs like a professional wrestler.</p>
<p>Gaia came to officiate. Soon, the battle was underway. Akira grabbed Kent’s leg and pulled it forward. Of course, Kent had to fall, so he faked tripping and fell back.</p>
<p>However, before landing on the ground, Akira jumped up and slammed him to the ground using her elbow, which looked very funny given her body size.</p>
<p>Akira stood up and ran to the side of the ring, slamming her body against the spring ropes. The momentum gathered from the slam pushed her even faster to create more force.</p>
<p>Then, when she gathered enough momentum, she moved and slammed her body into Kent, making the shameless Chaos Dragon make hurtful sounds.</p>
<p>Akira smiled and dragged him to the center of the ring, laying him down. She then climbed to the third rope and jumped high into the air, and came down, slamming hard on Kent’s body.</p>
<p>Then she grabbed his leg and leaned on him.</p>
<p>Gaia moved, sliding across the surface of the rubber floor.</p>
<p>Bang! Bang! Bang!</p>
<p>She slammed her tiny palm on the floor three times. Kent didn’t even make a single move as Akira held him down throughout the three strikes.</p>
<p>When she let him go, Kent lazily threw his hand, striking the floor, and lay on his back, gazing at the blue sky with a defeated expression. Akira and her sisters were jumping up and down, celebrating yet another win.</p>
<p>A couple of meters away, Kent’s women were left gobsmacked as they stood frozen, looking at what could be said to be the most shocking thing they had ever seen in their entire lives.</p>
<p>However, while one would think they were shocked watching Kent get defeated, their focus wasn’t on that. Instead, they are fascinated by the game Kent and his three assistants were playing.</p>
<p>"What is this game?" Vari muttered in a dazed tone, looking at the wrestling ring.</p>
<p>⟪Big sister, do you want to play? Master is too weak for experts like us,⟫ Gaia said, looking at Kent’s women.</p>
<p>"What is this game?" she asked, sounding louder this time.</p>
<p>⟪It’s called wrestling. Do you want to try it? We promise we won’t disgrace you like we did with Master,⟫ Dori said, raising the scout’s honor symbol.</p>
<p>By then, Kent lifted himself and sat inside the ring, smiling at his wives.</p>
<p>"It’s a game from my world called wrestling. All you have to do is weaken your opponent and press their back to the ground. If, after three strikes, they can’t move, you win."</p>
<p>"Sounds fun," Unity jumped into the ring, ready to show her strength. However, the moment she landed inside, something happened.</p>
<p>"I can’t feel my cultivation base," Unity said.</p>
<p>"Yeah. The ring locks everything, turning you into a mortal."</p>
<p>Gaia made sure all grounds were covered when she made the ring. It takes one’s cultivation base away, leaving them with only their mortal strength...</p>
<p>[Unity, dear, make sure they win in the end.] Kent used the harem connection to talk to Unity, who smiled and understood the assignment.</p>
<p>Dori climbed into the ring, and just like with Kent, the battle started, causing the ladies who had never seen something like this to become ecstatic. They all wanted to try it.</p>
<p>After a few more minutes, Dori defeated Unity, doing a backflip to celebrate. Lilian jumped into the ring next, facing off against the feisty Gaia, who also defeated her opponent.</p>
<p>She gave Lilian the classic ’you can’t see me’ celebration, causing Kent to smile as he watched all this. Just like that, all his women joined the wrestling, bringing a warm atmosphere to the mountain garden.</p>
<p>This was all he wanted—an environment filled with love and happiness. He was getting some, and the feeling was just too much to describe.</p>
<p>Kent spent a few minutes making sure his ladies understood the back-and-forth before walking toward the shocked princess, who couldn’t decide where to focus her attention.</p>
<p>When she turned left, the city lay before her like a river.</p>
<p>The elegant city of humans featured many towering glassy buildings, futuristic designs, and calm sights that would never tire the eye.</p>
<p>Then, away from such a beautiful city, the red city loomed afar from where she stood, yet she could feel the elegance deep in her bones.</p>
<p>She did not seem to hate it one bit, and seeing the redskin beings moving about, playing with the humans and doing all kinds of things, many thoughts passed through her mind.</p>
<p>But if she looked away and focused on the wrestling match, that alone made her wonder just where in the cosmos she had found herself.</p>
<p>"So, this is the real you, huh... not bad for the Poison Wicked Witch." Kent approached her with a smile.</p>
<p>"By the way, welcome to the tower."</p>
