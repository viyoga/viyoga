<p>Kent’s wives chose to spend a week in Fedora before returning to the tower. When they came back, Kent was deep in seclusion, so they didn’t disturb him.</p>
<p>Instead, they went to the Body Tower and spent five years there, climbing the tower. This was so Princess Lovina could catch up with them.</p>
<p>She already had a strong foundation, so aside from tempering her body with some of the harrowing poisons out there, she was allowed to climb the Golden Titan Stairway just like the rest of them.</p>
<p>It took her three months, but she managed to reach the 30th step, which was the goal. The treasures she found on the 100th, 200th, and 300th steps elevated her to the level of a Silver Body cultivator.</p>
<p>This strengthened her body, and after cultivating for three more months, they all started to climb even further.</p>
<p>Naturally, Unity and Princess Lovina, the two who hadn’t awakened their true names yet, were left in the dust, but that didn’t mean they couldn’t climb.</p>
<p>The gap left behind by their sisters was a couple of dozen steps, but they were slowly catching up.</p>
<p>The goal was to reach the 600th step. It took them five years, but they all reached there, and thanks to the gifts received on the 400th, 500th, and 600th steps, they achieved the Diamond Body in body cultivation.</p>
<p>They were thrilled to the point of wanting to reach the 900th step, but Vexthra took them to their Primordial chambers instead.</p>
<p>Their main target was to awaken their Primordial spirits.</p>
<p>This left only Zaila, who gave them a grin and remained on the stairway. It would take her sisters between 10 and 30 years to awaken their Primordial spirits.</p>
<p>This meant their consciousness would reside in that special place for nearly 30,000 years.</p>
<p>It would be a long time, and it was going to be a very harrowing journey, but they were determined to rise to the occasion and surprise their man when he came out of seclusion.</p>
<p>Their motivation was that they and the Legion would handle the mission on Planet Dusk, while Kent would handle the other things on his shoulders.</p>
<p>Gaia and her sisters might be cheeky sometimes, but when they saw their master had a lot to do and little time to do it all, they went to tell his wives, and this was why they made up their minds to ensure he didn’t carry everything alone.</p>
<p>So they entered the Primordial Chambers with determined smiles.</p>
<p>Years passed, and within those years, Zaila, who had already awakened her Primordial spirit, managed to break into the Sage Body stage in body cultivation after climbing the 800th step.</p>
<p>However, that was where she got stuck. Aside from that, her Primordial spirit, which had started from Tier 1 again, had already reached the 5th Tier.</p>
<p>And that was when the first of her sisters came out of the Primordial Chamber.</p>
<p>It had been seven years since they entered, but Elsa came out with a smile and a cultivation base sitting at the peak of the Core Formation stage.</p>
<p>She had completed all seven core evolutions and awakened a Soul Space. Not that it wasn’t to be expected. Her Primordial Spirit also came out, sitting at the 16th Tier stage in soul cultivation.</p>
<p>Elsa is the Soul Sentinel, so awakening a Soul Space is natural.</p>
<p>However, she ensured that her Primordial spirit underwent the same procedure as Zaila’s, expanding her Soul Space while her primordial spirit regressed to Tier 1.</p>
<p>But this only made her even more powerful. She later joined Zaila on the stairway, who had already reached the 850th step. Elsa spent 7,000 years in the Primordial Chamber.</p>
<p>A year later, Neomi came out dripping red. When she awakened her Primordial spirit and emerged, her sisters shuddered at the sight of her.</p>
<p>She awakened a space called Killing Space. When Elsa and Zaila entered that space, it was like walking into a domain of killers.</p>
<p>Most of the things there had, in one way or another, a property conducive to killing. For example, a certain serene garden inside her Killing Space contained all kinds of poison meant for killing.</p>
<p>It was, without a doubt, very dangerous to look at.</p>
<p>Her Primordial spirit came out sitting at the Tier 17 stage, making her a Spirit Lord. She also dissolved the cultivation base of her primordial spirit, further expanding the Killing Space.</p>
<p>Like Elsa, she emerged at the peak of the Core Formation stage.</p>
<p>A few months after she came out, Ingrid also walked out, radiating the primal energy of the Void. It was a terrifying sight, especially when she walked out with a deep golden panther.</p>
<p>When her True Name was awakened, the tattoo of this Void Creature appeared on her body.</p>
<p>Now, walking out of the Primordial Chamber, the 3-meter-tall golden panther was beside her, and looking into those eyes, it was plain as day that they were the eyes of a predator.</p>
<p>As usual, Ingrid came out sitting at the peak of the Core Formation stage, but so was her beast companion, who could talk and had perfect affinity for the Void.</p>
<p>Ingrid awakened the Void Space, and her Primordial spirit was at the Tier 17 stage. She, of course, came with many gifts, and one of them was the ability to summon the Void Beasts.</p>
<p>However, she had to go back to Tier 1 and start all over again.</p>
<p>Not that it wasn’t for the best.</p>
<p>Because it was for the best, Zaila’s Primordial Spirit was already at Tier 6, but the benefits she was receiving from it were already enough to allow her to stand toe to toe with a Spirit King.</p>
<p>The Primordial spirits were making it possible for the ladies to reach their full potential.</p>
<p>Three weeks after Ingrid came out of the Primordial Chamber, Zaila climbed to the 900th step. However, instead of attaining Nirvana, she broke one limit on her mind and one on her body.</p>
<p>This brought her strength and mental resilience to a whole new level. She became so powerful that she started to climb the Stairs even further.</p>
<p>A year later, Selene emerged from her Primordial Chamber...</p>
