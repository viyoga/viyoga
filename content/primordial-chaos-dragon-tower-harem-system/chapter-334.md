<p>45 minutes ago, when Kent was surrounded, those hiding began sharpening their weapons, waiting for a chance to take what they came for.</p>
<p>Then Kent, along with everyone else, vanished, leaving in their place a cylindrical disk. This shocked them all.</p>
<p>They were hiding to take advantage of when he was down, but instead of that happening, everyone vanished.</p>
<p>Twenty minutes later, they still had not returned. Of course, these hidden figures began emerging with the intent of seizing the disc hovering in the air.</p>
<p>They knew Kent was inside, so the moment they got their hands on it, they would be able to trap him the moment he got out.</p>
<p>However, that didn’t go as planned.</p>
<p>The first person who tried taking the disc was thrown across the air like a ragdoll. There was a forcefield around it, making it impossible to take.</p>
<p>This meant Kent could only be captured and killed the moment he got out of the space he entered with the dozens of transcendent.</p>
<p>That is if he came out alive. If he was dead, the one that would come out would become their target.</p>
<p>And so, many began to gather, with a few Root Ascension stage experts disguised as Transcendents also within grabbing range of the disc, unaware that something good was on its way.</p>
<p>Well, it’s bad for them but good for Kent.</p>
<p>His initial plan was to send out the five Battle Dolls first, and then Kaizo would follow. This would give him a chance to assess the situation and deal with it accordingly.</p>
<p>He was stupid to think only the 4 dozen people he took with him were the only ones coming after him. So, he was making plans for what would happen next.</p>
<p>However, the moment Metal Saint Andrew took the blood possession pill, things changed for him.</p>
<p>And so, Kent waited and kept counting until the seconds reached the ticking point. That was when a figure appeared in the midst of the over a hundred people around the disc and those hiding closer to it.</p>
<p>The moment Metal Saint Andrew appeared, people dashed toward him, but they came to a halt when a painful cry escaped his lips.</p>
<p>That was when they recognised him and also realised something was wrong.</p>
<p>"Everyone, run—" The voice didn’t even finish the shout when a powerful explosion rocked the space, killing as many as it could.</p>
<p>Painful screams and those who didn’t even get to scream were the result of Metal Saint Andrew’s self-destruction.</p>
<p>In a way, he got his wish. He didn’t die alone.</p>
<p>The result said it all. The two-mile radius was decimated, and dozens of Root Great Sages and Transcendents died.</p>
<p>Dozens more were injured, with even a few Ascendants harmed. This was a surprise attack, and they fell for it.</p>
<p>"Kill them all." Suddenly, a familiar voice entered their ears, forcing those who could look to turn toward the direction it came from.</p>
<p>There, standing in the air, was Kent, who pointed his sword forward as five metallic figures moved with the wind.</p>
<p>In the next moments, people began dying, with even two unfortunate Root Ascension stage experts falling prey to Kent’s perfect attack plan.</p>
<p>The metal dolls were perfect for killing, for they didn’t make mistakes when it came to aiming for the lives of those they were facing.</p>
<p>"Hahahahah!"</p>
<p>Suddenly, a sinister laughter echoed through the air, accompanied by a bone chain.</p>
<p>It shot toward Kent with the intent to stab him through the heart, but before it could get within two meters of him, a hand grabbed it and pulled forward, sending the attacker flying toward him.</p>
<p>"Pick someone your own size," Kaizo said, landing a powerful punch on the person’s face and breaking the mask in half.</p>
<p>A bony face was revealed.</p>
<p>"You ugly bro... I’m going to enjoy smashing your face." Kaizo said before he moved, and the clash between him and the Level 3 Root Ascension Elder from the Evil Sect began.</p>
<p>"Die, brat." Kent, of course, knew he was just about to start the real battle, so he was ready when the voice came and a dagger appeared before him.</p>
<p>Instead of dodging, he moved forward and kicked forward instead, sending an invisible figure flying.</p>
<p>"Your camouflage won’t work on me, so fight like real men and die like the idiots you are," Kent said, blinking away.</p>
<p>He appeared behind one of the Evil Sect elders, kicked, and aimed another blow at his neck. However, it was blocked, and a dagger came for his neck instead.</p>
<p>But he blinked away again, and then lightning burst out of his body. His aura also skyrocketed, and he moved, showing that his speed had increased as well.</p>
<p>Kent added 13,000 points out of the 23,000 he had to his speed and the rest to his strength and stamina, so the current him was filled with energy.</p>
<p>He moved, leaving afterimages behind.</p>
<p>The trail of lightning flashed its golden hue as he moved at a speed even the peak Transcendents he was fighting couldn’t seem to track.</p>
<p>Stab...</p>
<p>Suddenly, his sword went through the chest of one of the Evil Sect elders, but the elder turned to smoke and vanished. The next time he appeared, blood was coming from his lips.</p>
<p>"You can’t kill me using physical means, brat." A sinister laughter escaped his lips. "Now die."</p>
<p>He moved and stabbed his dagger at Kent. The attack was shifted, but Kent had become much faster, so he dodged.</p>
<p>Both Elders were assassins, but instead of using the element of surprise against him, they were now trying their best to outpace him.</p>
<p>"I know I can’t kill you like that, but I made you look cocky for a moment there," Kent said, blocking his attack and grabbing hold of his arm.</p>
<p>"Soul Shock." Kent activated his soul skill, and for a moment, the figure blacked out as his soul was attacked suddenly.</p>
<p>"Die." That was all the elder heard before his head was severed from his body. "Always go for the head, for it cannot walk without the body", Kent added coldly.</p>
<p>"What?!"</p>
<p>The other elder, who was closing in, retreated when he saw his fellow elder die through physical means.</p>
<p>It shouldn’t have been possible, considering they always turned to smoke whenever a physical attack landed on them.</p>
<p>They were the perfect assassins and also two of the seven darkness-type people all over the human continent.</p>
<p>So how, then, did he die?</p>
<p>He was shocked beyond his senses.</p>
<p>"Behind you," Kent said, and the elder fell for it. He looked back but saw nothing. When he looked forward again, he was now face to face with Kent.</p>
<p>"Peekaboo."</p>
<p>A soul attack landed on his soul as well, and then his head was severed. Kent took both their bodies into his Necro Forge, knowing they were valuable for what he had planned after the battles.</p>
<p>The moment the second elder died, the one fighting Kaizo was smashed to the ground, creating a crater.</p>
<p>"Skybreaker Fist!" the Brute activated his devastating skill again.</p>
<p>Like a meteor, Kaizo descended from the sky, landing a powerful attack on the elder and ending him in the most painful way possible.</p>
<p>*Rumble.*</p>
<p>Thunder and lightning flashed through the sky.</p>
<p>Kaizo rose into the air and stopped beside his master, who was looking into the distance.</p>
<p>The Battle Dolls also moved, stopping behind Kent and creating a protective shield around him.</p>
<p>All around, the only things left were the charred remains of those who had died in the blast. The rest were killed, and their bodies were taken into the Necro Forge.</p>
<p>"Who’s next?" Kent asked, casting a scene that commanded fear. Everyone watching was terrified of him.</p>
<p>He not only have five Battle dolls, he also have a Peak Root Transcendent who can easily kill a level three Root Ascension stage expert.</p>
<p>That is a lot to handle.</p>
<p>"Very well, brat. You can die now..."</p>
<p>Of course, just as some can’t read the room, some can’t see reason.</p>
<p>Bats filled the sky as a figure whom everyone aside from the Kingdom Protectors feared appeared.</p>
