<p>"Now you must give her a name," Kent said, holding a golden key.</p>
<p>Commander Agnes nodded and focused on the beast lady, who was looking forward to having a name.</p>
<p>"From now onwards, I will call you Yu Lan."</p>
<p>The golden key shone brighter and moved toward Commander Agnes’s forehead. With one swift movement, it entered her forehead, sealing the contract.</p>
<p>Their bodies glowed golden for a couple of seconds, then their eyes widened in surprise.</p>
<p>"A...a Supreme technique." Both Commander Agnes and Yu Lan felt their backs break into a cold sweat, unsure of what to think.</p>
<p>Kent smiled, "Indeed, you both have a Supreme Technique now. The more you trust in each other and grow as two inseparable people, the more benefits you will receive."</p>
<p>"Wow. I never knew there was a method this magical for taming beasts." Commander Agnes said, feeling extremely lucky.</p>
<p>"That is because you are a human with limited knowledge," Sylvara said with a small smirk. "There once lived a Beast Tamer who every beast wanted to form a contract with him.</p>
<p>They said he had so many methods of taming beasts that every beast tamed would become a powerhouse.</p>
<p>In fact, in the immortal world, the legend of the Divine Beast Tamer remains to this day, inspiring many beasts to wish they had lived during his time.</p>
<p>If one day you make it to the immortal worlds, be sure to pay respects to his many statues. They said even his statues hold so much blessing that if you are lucky, you might just receive some.</p>
<p>Many had received his blessing, and now, they are powerhouses."</p>
<p>"Okay, Monarch." Commander Agnes knew better than to take offense at whatever Sylvara said.</p>
<p>Kent just smiled, though his curiosity was piqued by hearing about the Divine Beast Tamer. He certainly wanted to learn more about him, but for now, he had business to attend to.</p>
<p>"Take this, Yu Lan." Commander Agnes handed one of the Soul Flame Fruits to her beast companion. She then turned to Kent.</p>
<p>"Thank you very much, Kent. If it wasn’t because of you, I wouldn’t be here now. So thank you."</p>
<p>"No need to thank me. You were brave, and I like brave people." Kent gave her a look that made her heart skip a beat. "Take Yu Lan and go back. I will stay here for 3 more weeks before leaving.</p>
<p>Afterwards, I will come look for you on Planet Nictus."</p>
<p>"Okay. Just be careful."</p>
<p>"I will be. After all, who dares hurt me when I have a Monarch as my wife?"</p>
<p>Commander Agnes smiled and shook her head.</p>
<p>It would take her some time to digest the fact that Kent and the Monarch were actually lovers and seemed to have even crossed the line already.</p>
<p>"Yu Lan, let’s go." Commander Agnes pressed her forehead, and a golden key glowed, and Yu Lan vanished inside.</p>
<p>She then took out a return Talisman and channeled her energy into it. "Until we meet again, Kent."</p>
<p>"Until next time, Commander Agnes."</p>
<p>She transformed into a streak of light and shot upward, vanishing into the clouds.</p>
<p>"Next stop, domain of the Silver Flame Minotaur Monarch, Orvath."</p>
<p>Kent and Sylvara transformed into a streak of light and shot toward the Cinderhorn Plains, the domain ruled by Orvath.</p>
<p>---</p>
<p>[Moon of Planet Cosima - Commander Agnes]</p>
<p>Right when she vanished, she appeared on the moon, catching many people’s attention. Commander Agnes ignored all their gazes and quickly headed toward the Transfer Channel that would take her back to Planet Nictus.</p>
<p>Her plan now was to get back to Planet Nictus and give the Soul Flame Fruit to her general.</p>
<p>However, as she started making her way toward the Transfer Channel, three Beastkins blocked her path. One was a Tier 19 beast, and the other two were Tier 18.</p>
<p>"Look what we have here, if it isn’t Commander Agnes of the Nictus Useless Army."</p>
<p>From just the tone, Commander Agnes knew the three people were from the Planet King army. Naturally, she knew nobody would be coming to her aid.</p>
<p>Nobody was crazy enough to cross people linked to the Planet King.</p>
<p>"I don’t want to make things hard for you, so why don’t you empty your space ring and follow us to our dwelling? We promise not to molest you and would even let you leave after a week."</p>
<p>One of them, a Goatkin with two pointed goat horns, said, his eyes filled with lust. He was the Tier 19 beast.</p>
<p>"I know, right. With how caring we are, we wouldn’t even make you regret spreading your legs for us," the Lizardkin said, licking his lips.</p>
<p>"I will even heal you afterwards. This way, you wouldn’t feel any pain," the Rabbitkin said, each looking toward Commander Agnes with lustful eyes.</p>
<p>They knew on this side of the universe, as long as you were affiliated with the Planet King, nobody would dare resist you.</p>
<p>So Commander Agnes was the perfect catch.</p>
<p>Unfortunately for them, the current Commander Agnes was not someone they could bully. Perhaps, they would have had better luck if it were her before she met Kent.</p>
<p>Now, she was different.</p>
<p>As she raised her head and looked at the faces of the three Beastkins blocking her, a look of disgust appeared on her face.</p>
<p>"Even if I am hungry to spend a night with a man, I wouldn’t consider a stinky goat."</p>
<p>The faces of the three beasts, especially the Goatkin, twisted into an angered expression when they heard her words.</p>
<p>The Lizardkin lunged at her with bloodshot eyes. However, before he could lay his claws on her, a figure appeared behind Commander Agnes and grabbed the Lizardkin by the neck.</p>
<p>"AaarrggghhH!"</p>
<p>His scream was cut off as his head was burned off his body.</p>
<p>"What!"</p>
<p>The Goatkin reacted, attempting to make a run for it, but Yu Lan was much faster.</p>
<p>She appeared before him and then, with one quick jab, her hand went through his chest, appearing behind him with his heart in her firm grip.</p>
<p>Flame surged in her hand, and then, like burning paper, his heart turned to ash.</p>
<p>The Rabbitkin also tried to make a run for it, but unfortunately for him, Yu Lan had already marked him for death.</p>
<p>Before he could move, an arrow went through his head.</p>
<p>After killing the Goatkin, Yu Lan looked around, her eyes filled with disdain, and she issued a warning, "If you want to die, block our path."</p>
<p>After saying that, she moved and stood beside Commander Agnes, who couldn’t help feeling invincible at the moment.</p>
<p>"Let’s go, sister," Yu Lan said, making sure everyone heard how she addressed Commander Agnes.</p>
<p>The two ladies moved, and soon they booked their ticket, which was free thanks to Commander Agnes’s status.</p>
<p>All over, people could only look at the beautiful lady beside Commander Agnes until they left the moon.</p>
<p>Yu Lan was a Tier 19 beast who had broken 22 limits on her body. This made her extremely dangerous.</p>
<p>The Goatkin stood no chance against her.</p>
<p>Words spread out like fire, reaching Planet Nictus long before they even arrived. Words of a beauty walking with the infamous Commander Agnes, whom everyone thought was dead, spread like wildfire.</p>
<p>Many people became happy, but some felt rage. In all, when she reached home, she was welcomed by 10 Tier 19 powerhouses... not that she needed them, considering she had Yu Lan by her side.</p>
