<p>After resting for a bit, Kent decided to get back to work. He planned to go around and check on everyone for a while, but knowing he could ask for help, he used the tower to check on them and then returned to his training.</p>
<p>"The Lost Realm, a place filled with people lost to history and perhaps time itself," Kent muttered, wondering about many things.</p>
<p>The Lost Realm that Faceless talked about piqued his interest, and as someone who had already heard something about worlds going missing, he wanted to one day explore it and see if he could find something.</p>
<p>"I wonder if the long-lost angel race I read about a few years ago would be there." The angel race that went missing with that mysterious swordsman.</p>
<p>The manual titled ’City of Angels’ was one of the random things he received that now starts to make sense. It discussed the disappearance of the Strongest Angel City Race, possibly the original Angel Race.</p>
<p>It was also in the story that he learned about the mysterious swordsman said to have created the ’Starfall’ sword technique.</p>
<p>"What was his name again... Oh, Bob. The one who was able to defeat a clone of mine... millions of years ago, perhaps billions of years ago. I wonder if I will find him there too."</p>
<p>His expectation and interest were already oozing, wondering about so many things to the point he wanted nothing more than to get there fast enough.</p>
<p>In a strange way, he wanted to be there. Even Faceless told him to get where he wanted to go, he must go there, so it wasn’t like Kent’s feelings were unfounded. He knew something wanted him there, so he was going to go one of these days.</p>
<p>"However, Faceless also said I must rise through my weakness and gather strength. That is the only way I will be able to reach my goal.</p>
<p>Chaos also said the same thing, and while I can tell they are asking too much of me, I know they did what they did, so I can have it easy. This also means I must surpass their achievements and prove to myself I still got it."</p>
<p>Kent took a deep sigh and then decided on the path he would follow next.</p>
<p>"I have already completed the memories, and that alone gave me the strength to roam a High World freely. But while that is good, I am not the only monster out there, so to be self-assured, I must complete my plans.</p>
<p>Next, I need to awaken my Soul Path. That is the only way for me to use my soul cultivation much more efficiently.</p>
<p>I have already seen what Elsa can do, and while she is only at Tier 12 in soul cultivation, if not for my soul immunity, I wouldn’t stand a chance against her."</p>
<p>Kent sighed, his mind drifting back to his wife, who had already made sure his soul was free from harm.</p>
<p>Elsa helped him become someone who could use soul attacks, but the same attacks would never hurt him. Talk about a good wife.</p>
<p>"I’d better get on that for now. Who knows what kind of gift is waiting for me?" Kent smiled and then readied himself. He vanished and appeared inside the Soul Tower.</p>
<p>"Okay, Akira, I am ready." The Soul Weaver appeared along with Gaia and Dori. Since this involves the soul, a Soul Weaver is required.</p>
<p>"So, how is it going to be?" Kent asked, staring at his three assistants.</p>
<p>⟪The skill is buried deep within your soul. As I said, I don’t know where she got it, but I have to say, the process will take some time, and while I love to estimate, you will be looking at a range of 10 to 60 years.</p>
<p>The reason is simple: the stronger your soul, the more tedious it is to awaken this path, so for now, you have to relax and be out for a while.</p>
<p>Your soul is already at the Tier 13 stage.⟫</p>
<p>"So I will be unconscious for that long, huh?" Akira and her sisters nodded. Kent sighed and glared at them.</p>
<p>"I know you three have something planned. I won’t tell you not to do it, but know this: if I wake up and find out you did something to my body, you better prepare your bumbums because I will spank you silly."</p>
<p>The three lolis giggled, hearing what their master was saying.</p>
<p>⟪Don’t worry, master, we don’t have anything planned, so be assured.⟫</p>
<p>"Good," Kent smiled and then readied himself. However, before he could ask Akira to go ahead, a message appeared on his screen, bringing a smile to his face.</p>
<p>⟦Your servant, Jacob Ashland, has a trust level of 80%. You can now invoke his True Name and unleash his true potential.⟧</p>
<p>"Ladies, I have a mission for you three," Kent said, and the three lolis became very happy hearing that.</p>
<p>⟪What kind of mission, Master?⟫ they asked.</p>
<p>"I want you to locate my first servant, Jacob, and bring him to the tower. I can ask the tower to teleport him here, but since you three are bored, why don’t you take a walk outside for a few weeks?</p>
<p>You can even decide not to use your abilities and look for him like a mundane. This way, by the time you get back, I will be nearing the completion of my Soul Path awakening.</p>
<p>What do you think?"</p>
<p>⟪Rest assured, Master, we will get it done⟫. They were so happy that Kent could see the smiles on their faces and how much they wanted to get down to business quickly.</p>
<p>"Then let’s get down to business here first." Akira nodded and laid Kent on his back. Then she pressed her finger on his forehead, and before Kent knew it, his eyes turned hazy and he passed out.</p>
<p>The three lolis looked at his sleeping face, and smiles appeared on their faces.</p>
<p>⟪Such a beautiful face. Drawing all over it wouldn’t be a waste at all⟫, Gaia said, and her sisters nodded.</p>
<p>⟪Let’s get the mission on the road first. I have a feeling we will come back to a different version of Master, one that will shock even us.⟫</p>
<p>Dori and her sisters left the tower immediately and began searching for Jacob Ashland.</p>
<p>Though Kent never looked for him after leaving him with the money to handle his cultivation and a few items to keep him safe, it seemed Jacob had finally gathered some trust in Kent, and now, his true potential would be unleashed.</p>
<p>But until then, Kent was on a journey to awaken his Soul Path.</p>
