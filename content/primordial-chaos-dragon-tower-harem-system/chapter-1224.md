<p>During the Primordial Era, when the Underworld Queen rose to prominence as one of the powerhouses in that era, she was always followed around by seven dangerous ladies, each a force to be reckoned with.</p>
<p>They called them ’The Seven Silent Claws’ back then.</p>
<p>Nobody has ever seen their faces since they like to walk around wearing masks. In every battle fought by the Underworld Queen, the seven of them took the vanguard. Because of them, the Underworld Queen ruled unopposed for millions of years.</p>
<p>However, when she vanished, they were also not heard from again until today.</p>
<p>The Primordial Era was destroyed, but it seems just like most; they also survived.</p>
<p>And now that their queen had awakened again, they answered her call and came. Their arrival immediately calmed the worry in Kent’s heart.</p>
<p>Something else took root. Something he believes is tied to the Chaos.</p>
<p>Looking at them kneeling and bowing before her, Kent wore a complicated expression. For some reason, he feels like the seven ladies on the ground don’t like him very much.</p>
<p>Of course, he quickly linked it to Chaos, since when the Primordial Era is mentioned, he was the one to call. Clearly, it seems to him that Chaos may have done something to them to make them hate him.</p>
<p>"Stand, ladies, we are not in the Primordial Era anymore," Indie said with a smile. Then, in a high, cheerful tone, she welcomed them: "Welcome to the Infinity Era, Cats."</p>
<p>The seven ladies stood up and started looking around with happy expressions... The beautiful environment inside the Harem tower instantly captured their attention.</p>
<p>"Beautiful," one of them, by the name Sorynn, said, gazing at the Love Garden.</p>
<p>Perhaps because all seven of them are happy with the environment, none of them noticed Kent early. Their focus was drawn entirely to the beauty around them.</p>
<p>However, a few seconds later, they saw him, and all seven of them paused on him and narrowed their gaze at him.</p>
<p>Kaelis, the poison expert in the Seven Silent Claws, took off her mask, revealing her beautiful face with a frown... "You look familiar," she said, taking a few steps toward Kent.</p>
<p>Kent smiled despite being stared down by seven Universal Gods. He cleared his throat and replied, "I don’t think so, lady. This is my first time meeting you."</p>
<p>He turned and looked at all seven of them before introducing himself, "I am Kent Madson, a dragon and the husband of your Empress."</p>
<p>Hearing his words, all seven turned toward Indie as if asking if it was true... Indie smiled and nodded... "He is telling the truth. I have given my heart to him, and I don’t regret that decision. So you will treat him as you would treat me."</p>
<p>They nodded and turned back to Kent. The frown is still on their faces, for deep within them they recognise Kent, and that makes them suspect him.</p>
<p>Well, he did lock their queen in the underworld, never to return to the land of the living again. Although he later lifted that ban, they had already developed hatred for him and tried to kill him many times.</p>
<p>It was their queen who told them to stop before they did.</p>
<p>However, if they sense Kent is the same bastard who locked their Empress, he will probably take some beatings.</p>
<p>However, Indie wouldn’t allow that to happen. Kent is more important to her than even her role as the Empress of the Underworld.</p>
<p>Seeing them look suspiciously at Kent, she decided to take their minds away from him.</p>
<p>"Girls, these are my sisters. The adorable one is called Unity. She is everyone’s favourite." Indie smiled, and so did the rest of her sisters.</p>
<p>The Seven Silent Claws also smiled, knowing they would pamper Unity anyday. She just looked too adorable.</p>
<p>All seven of them had taken off their masks, revealing their beautiful smiles.</p>
<p>Kent joined them, but he didn’t draw the attention of the seven goddesses to himself since the more they looked at him, the higher their chance of recognising him.</p>
<p>"The pale one is Elsa; she is a Soul Sentinel, one of the greatest soul cultivators in this era." They nodded toward Elsa. Even though Indie exaggerated a bit, she would indeed rise to her past self one of these days.</p>
<p>The legendary Soul Sentinel would one day ride into battle with her legion of Soul Sentinels.</p>
<p>"The cheerful one is Selene." Indie pauses and picks her words carefully, "I wouldn’t play with her if you don’t want your backs on a soft bed and legs spread open."</p>
<p>Her sisters burst into uncontrollable laughter, while the Seven Silent Claws wore confused expressions. They had no idea why they were laughing.</p>
<p>Indie, of course, didn’t explain anything to them. Selene had already marked her prey, and regardless of the gap between them, she would work her magic on them.</p>
<p>"She is called Enzi, a Crystal Angel and a Glass Elemental Manipulator. Personally, I think you shouldn’t antagonise her since she likes trapping people in glass dimensions." Enzi said hi to the seven guards, ignoring the latter part of what Indie said.</p>
<p>"And that is Liora, a dangerous assassin that none of you should underestimate."</p>
<p>Nyssara, a crimson-haired beauty, turned her head to the side and studied Liora. After a few seconds, she nodded... "She has all the qualities. I will train her from now on. You can call me Nyssa."</p>
<p>Liora nodded and smiled, "I will be glad to learn from you, Senior Nyssa."</p>
<p>"No need to call me that. You are the Empress’s sister, so we naturally serve you all. Training will be a way of making sure the Empress has powerful people around her that she could trust in times of need."</p>
<p>There is no denying the logic in her words. Liora nodded with a smile. Having a Universal God as her teacher is a boon few can boast of.</p>
<p>Indie turned to Arien, but before she could speak, Nyssa spoke, cutting her off...</p>
<p>"We know you," she said, pointing at Arien.</p>
<p>Elwen, another beauty with long ash-brown hair, took over, "Although you are a mortal now, I can never forget the scent of a legend like you." They recognised Arien’s past self as the Nine-Tailed Void Fox.</p>
<p>"Intriguing, who would have thought a legend as you would now be sisters with the Empress?" She paused for a few seconds and revealed something intriguing...</p>
<p>"You were rivals, you know."</p>
<p>Indie turned and looked at Arien, who smiled weakly, already aware of that. She had crossed paths with Indie’s past long before she met Chaos in the Blood Region.</p>
<p>Now, it seems she later met her again, and they even became rivals.</p>
<p>"I don’t know about the past, but Indie and I are best friends and sisters now."</p>
<p>"That is good to me. Having two of my favourite people back is good enough for me."</p>
<p>Indie smiled and turned to Runa and Adina. However, yet again, the seven guards recognised them... well, they recognised the kind of people they are.</p>
<p>"Extreme Pure Yin Body, huh. That is rare, but to think there are two of you here at the same time... very interesting."</p>
<p>This time Kent intervened and asked...</p>
<p>"Please, what do you know about the Extreme Pure Yin Body Constitution?"</p>
<p>"A lot, actually," Elwen answered and then made a ball of pure ice float on her palm... "After all, I am also a bearer of the Extreme Yin Constitution, only that I have already awakened the True Extreme Yin Divine Body."</p>
