<p>Five days later, Kent had concocted over a hundred Soul Strengthening Pills and 57 Limit Breaker Pills.</p>
<p>"Is that the last of them?" Sylvara asked, cleaning the sweat off Kent’s forehead.</p>
<p>"Yes. The ingredients are used up, and since two major ingredients are still missing, I cannot concoct the Innate Talent Awakening Pill.</p>
<p>However, once we get back to the human world, I will purchase them and work on that."</p>
<p>All seven monarchs are around this time. They had worked hard to gather all the ingredients for the Innate Awakening Pill, after some of their subordinates reported that they had discovered other herbs.</p>
<p>However, there were still two ingredients short.</p>
<p>However, there are enough Soul Strengthening Pills and Limit Breaker Pills to help them become much stronger.</p>
<p>Kent waved his hand, and all 50 beasts he had tamed appeared. Kent wanted to brief them before they left, so he called them over.</p>
<p>"First of all, I would like to thank you all for helping with searching for the ingredients. I only wanted ingredients for two pills, but you all outdid yourselves.</p>
<p>Now, I have rewards for you all.</p>
<p>However, please note that we will be leaving this place soon. This means the chances of you all coming back here are virtually nonexistent. But fear not, where we are going is full of life and advantages that I am sure you all will enjoy greatly."</p>
<p>They all seemed happy hearing Kent speak.</p>
<p>"But before you all ask, know that in the coming weeks, we will be taking on a series of adventures. These adventures promise to make you all grow much stronger.</p>
<p>For now, take these pills and return to your living space. Use them to strengthen yourselves."</p>
<p>Kent waved his hand, and each of the 50 beasts received a Limit Breaker Pill and two Soul Strengthening Pills.</p>
<p>"Take the green pills first before the blue ones."</p>
<p>They nodded, and they all vanished into the Taming Bracelet, leaving only Kent and the seven monarchs.</p>
<p>"Use the time to work on your soul first, before breaking the 30th limit, which will be your limit for this world. Once the Planet King is dead, you will prepare to ascend to an Immortal World.</p>
<p>By then, I will have enough ingredients to make more Limit Breaker Pills for you." They nodded and vanished into the bracelet, leaving Kent alone.</p>
<p>"Though it won’t work on me, testing won’t harm me too." He tossed a Soul Strengthening Pill into his mouth, but as expected, when he swallowed it, he only felt a strand of soul energy wash into his body.</p>
<p>"Ahhh, what was I expecting?" Kent laughed and tossed the Limit Breaker Pill, which also did nothing to him.</p>
<p>However, instead of frowning, he only smiled, knowing there were millions of Limit Breaker Points waiting for him to use in the future.</p>
<p>"Since that is the case, then I might as well leave now."</p>
<p>Kent walked out and then retrieved the return talisman.</p>
<p>"It’s been a blast, Flame Sea. Maybe one day I will come back here, but until then, farewell and goodbye."</p>
<p>With that, he turned into a beam of light and shot toward the sky...</p>
<p>---</p>
<p>[Planet Cosima’s Moon]</p>
<p>Thousands of warriors, sellers, and buyers were going about their daily activities as if there was no tomorrow.</p>
<p>The moon is the safest place for those who dare venture into the Flame sea, so they are all protected.</p>
<p>Many were planning to head to the Flame Sea and try their luck, while many were buying loot from the adventurers who had returned.</p>
<p>Of course, none of them was aware that the big shots in the Flame Sea were no more.</p>
<p>Well... even if they knew, that didn’t mean there was a shortage of Tier 19 beasts there.</p>
<p>The Flame Sea was still dangerous, and now with the Monarchs gone, it was bound to turn even more chaotic. New leadership would need to be selected, and this process was likely to be chaotic in the months to come.</p>
<p>Kent, who nobody would have expected to last a second there, suddenly appeared, drawing attention from hungry buyers.</p>
<p>Whenever a person appeared from the Flame Sea, they scrambled around them to buy loot. However, when they saw Kent, many could only smirk, with some narrowing their eyes at him.</p>
<p>"Some people are born lucky. It seems this brat is one of them, having survived such a place," a man said.</p>
<p>"Indeed. Just look at how bony he looks. I am sure he suffered greatly, but his luck saved him."</p>
<p>"Lucky brat. But still, let’s see if we can get some loot from him."</p>
<p>Just when these hungry buyers were making their way toward Kent, a man appeared before Kent and took his hand, dragging him away.</p>
<p>When the buyers saw this, they resigned and walked away.</p>
<p>Inside the very store that Kent bought the talismans from when he was heading to the Flame Sea, he stood, gazing back at the seller.</p>
<p>"So you return," the man said.</p>
<p>Kent grinned.</p>
<p>"Told you I would return, old man."</p>
<p>The man smiled. "I guess you did. Luck seems to be on your side."</p>
<p>Kent shook his head and smiled. "I guess so."</p>
<p>The man could only shake his head and smile. He is happy to see Kent back and well. He never expected him to ever return, not with his weak cultivation.</p>
<p>Kent waved his hand, and Silver Flame Crystals, about 600kg, and five violet lotuses appeared.</p>
<p>"Like I said before leaving, here are your souvenirs." The man was left stunned, looking at these items. He waved his hand, and the shop door slammed shut.</p>
<p>"Kid, where did you get these items?"</p>
<p>Kent smiled. "Let’s just say I was lucky enough to meet a kind man down there. He claimed he was a monarch and that he liked the way I sang, so he invited me to his domain.</p>
<p>It turned out it was his birthday, and he wanted me to perform. After performing, he gave me this." Kent pointed at the Silver Flame Crystals he got from Orvath’s domain.</p>
<p>"Later, a guest at the birthday celebration who saw my talent in music also invited me to her domain. She only wanted me to sing a happy song for her pet butterfly.</p>
<p>After my sterling performance, she gave me these lotus flowers. Now, since you helped me, I can only repay you like I promised." Kent smiled, his expression that of a kid who had returned from somewhere many claimed was no man’s land.</p>
<p>The old man, who went by the name Rudolf, stared at Kent with widened eyes. The story Kent told was of no importance to him; it was the characters in the story that caught his interest.</p>
<p>’He met the Silver Flame Minotaur Monarch and the Violet Flame Lotus Monarch and lived to tell the tale.’</p>
<p>Kent’s image in his mind had been raised to a whole different level. To him, Kent was now someone who had done the impossible.</p>
<p>"Kid, do you know just one of these flowers or even a kilogram of these crystals is enough to cause a great battle to break out on this moon? Are you sure you want to give me all these?"</p>
<p>"I have no use for them, so take them. Of course, if you think it is still too much, then take it as a down payment for when we meet again in the future.</p>
<p>For now, I need to go back home before my sister starts freaking out," Kent said, beginning to walk toward the door.</p>
<p>"Kid, I never caught your name. At least let me know the name of my benefactor."</p>
<p>Kent grinned, a mischievous look appearing on his face. "You can call me Kent... Kent Madson."</p>
<p>After saying that, Kent opened the door and walked away.</p>
<p>"Kent Madson, huh... I will never forget this name."</p>
<p>A few hours later, Kent left the moon and headed back to Planet Nictus. From there, he would take a detour to Planet Drakthys, where he would rescue Yuna before heading back to Planet Pyris.</p>
<p>Pill Monarch Nefeli was waiting for him so they could head to the Pill Ascension Competition.</p>
