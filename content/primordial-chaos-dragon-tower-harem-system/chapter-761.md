<p>After Kent’s vivid and clear display of dominance using just his words, a sense of calm returned to the place, and everyone entered a state of contemplation about what Kent had just said.</p>
<p>There was just something about the way Kent spoke that made them listen. His words reached them, and even those who didn’t want to listen were compelled to hear him.</p>
<p>It was just too bizarre, but the state of Flame Mountain was calm.</p>
<p>Meanwhile, Kent was talking to his goddess.</p>
<p>[I was lost in the moment and forgot to check on the eight new undead. However, don’t worry; you can proceed to the third floor when you’re ready.</p>
<p>The eight undead will be added once I am out of the Flame Tower.]</p>
<p>"Don’t worry about us. Just focus on yourself," Vexthra responded, and Kent smiled inwardly.</p>
<p>[By the way, have you found anything else on the second floor?] Kent asked. The ladies were still on the second floor, exploring everywhere.</p>
<p>Since they gained a lot from the first floor, they were now trying to see if they could gain anything else from the second floor.</p>
<p>"We have already explored 80% of the second floor, and aside from a few ancient texts that only Unity can translate, we have yet to find anything in the lines of a skill, technique, or whatever.</p>
<p>Of course, we have marked everything on the map so later you can explore it and see if you can make heads or tails of everything."</p>
<p>Vexthra was meticulous about the entire process, ensuring that everything was marked for later reference.</p>
<p>Kent most certainly would love to explore every section of the floors, as he knew it had something to do with his past one way or another.</p>
<p>[Don’t worry too much about digging for everything now. A time will come when you will have to relax. You can use that time to conduct further research. For now, handle the most essential and let me know how it goes.</p>
<p>Also, in case you can’t reach me in the next three days, don’t panic.]</p>
<p>"Okay. Talk to you later," Vexthra cut the connection and went back to exploring the second floor.</p>
<p>Meanwhile, Kent turned back to the half-elves. "Looks like we are all entering the tower."</p>
<p>They nodded.</p>
<p>With the dwarves giving them one slot and Lord Qilin giving them two, Arion, Helyra, and Cyran would be entering the Flame Tower with them.</p>
<p>This was beneficial for them. Kent knew they had come a long way thanks to the grueling training they underwent inside the Flame Valley.</p>
<p>It has molded, refined, and built them to handle whatever the Flame Tower throws at them.</p>
<p>Kent had already managed to help them raise their souls to Tier 12, just one step away from reaching his level.</p>
<p>Of course, the resources needed to reach that stage would cost him billions in tower points, so he planned to give them a soul technique like he did with his ladies. They can handle the rest on their own.</p>
<p>In fact, he discovered something that inspired him to invest his energy in them. After adding the seven half-elves to the Guardian Hand, he saw a leap in the amount of soul essence he was receiving from them.</p>
<p>It was now 1.5 times the initial amount, and he knew that after they emerged from the tower, it would increase again.</p>
<p>Kent deduced that the people he added to the Guardian Hand affect the amount of soul essence he could earn from a race he added to the Guardian Tower.</p>
<p>The stronger the ten get, the more soul essence he will receive.</p>
<p>This meant that if he were to go back to Fedora and add ten people to the Guardian Hand, the amount of soul essence he received from them would increase exponentially.</p>
<p>It was a good thing for him because, regardless of what happened, he would be able to increase his strength forever by just ensuring that ten people out of millions never slacked off on their training.</p>
<p>’Imagine all of them becoming gods one day. I will probably be earning so much that building an asteroid could happen within seconds.’</p>
<p>Kent smiled. "Since we are all entering the Flame Tower, I expect each of you to come out with at least two flame arts mastered. Anything less and you will spend ten days in the core region of the Flame Valley."</p>
<p>They laughed happily, knowing full well that the brat would actually do it.</p>
<p>During their time at the Flame Valley, Kent pushed them all to their limits, and only when they were at their breaking points did he intervene.</p>
<p>If he were able to do that, then he would be able to do worse later.</p>
<p>A while later, the shadow demon king and his elders also came and, just like the others, left by giving a slot to the half-elves, meaning the princess would also be able to enter.</p>
<p>The last to come were the dragons, who, although still prideful, understood it was the least they could do. However, the queen politely rejected the offer by asking for the slot in three years.</p>
<p>This was received well by the dragons. After all, if they had indeed given a slot to the half-elves, that meant one of their best and brightest wouldn’t be able to enter the Flame Tower.</p>
<p>Of course, if Uncle Drake had wanted to enter, Queen Jascia would have accepted the slot. However, the Flame King said he already had a flame art, so he passed up the offer.</p>
<p>After a while, everyone started to relax. However, it didn’t take long for the main people everyone was there to meet to appear.</p>
<p>The void cracked open, and a man clad in green robes appeared through the cracks, exuding a rather potent aura. Kent raised his eyebrows when he saw the man.</p>
<p>But he wasn’t the only one who came. Behind him were two young individuals. One was a young man with short blonde hair, handsome by some standards, and the lady was also cute but with wavy blue hair.</p>
<p>They were both clad in green robes just like the man. Of course, their auras weren’t any weaker than the man’s, who was an elder.</p>
<p>They were the representatives from the Supreme Immortal Sect, the main attraction at events like this.</p>
<p>The moment they appeared and hovered in the air, looking down at everyone, Kent immediately knew they didn’t see them as worthy beings, not even in the slightest.</p>
<p>However, when their eyes landed on Uncle Drake, he saw a flicker appear in all three of their eyes.</p>
<p>’Interesting...’</p>
