<p>"What did you just say?" the patriarch of the Supreme Solaryn Pill Family asked, veins popping on his bald head.</p>
<p>"My Lord, a person claiming to be Kent Madson had stormed the Supreme Pyris Pill Family and slaughtered every last one of them. He also sent a message that he had given us an hour to vacate, else..."</p>
<p>The man’s gaze turned cold.</p>
<p>He clearly didn’t like what he was being told.</p>
<p>He, after all, was the head of the second biggest and most powerful pill family in the ten Pill Worlds, so hearing he was being commanded to vacate where he called home clearly didn’t sound or taste well to him.</p>
<p>However, the threat was significant—one he could not afford to ignore.</p>
<p>He knew deep down that he was in the wrong. Seventy thousand years ago, he and nine other Pill Families attacked the Ten Pill Alliances, and overnight, they took over.</p>
<p>It was a shock to many, but they moved quickly, and before long, they had taken over the businesses and channels of the then Nine Pill Alliances.</p>
<p>They killed many people to secure their position, so they knew this threat wasn’t going to be a walk in the park. There was even evidence showing that Kent meant business.</p>
<p>"Send a message to the people. We must handle this brat before he grows a pair."</p>
<p>Obviously, he and his people would not be vacating as Kent had instructed.</p>
<p>The one who gave the report on what had happened to the Supreme Pyris Pill Family nodded and left, unaware that whatever they said had already been conveyed to Kent, and he would be visiting them next.</p>
<p>Nana had one of her parts in that Pill World, so she was reporting everything that was happening there to Kent.</p>
<p>Before Kent came to the Pill World years back, the Solaryn Pill Alliance was the top rank in all Ten Pill Worlds; however, after Kent came and elevated the image of the Pyris Pill Alliance, they dropped to second place.</p>
<p>So naturally, now that the Pill Families had taken over, they were the second strongest Pill Family, and as such, they wouldn’t bend the knee without a fight.</p>
<p>And they weren’t the only ones. The Fourth and Seventh Pill Families also chose to stand their ground. They even started calling their allies and banding together to take care of Kent.</p>
<p>Aside from that, others who were also interested in Kent in all the bad ways had already started making their way to Solaryn, where Kent would have no choice but to come.</p>
<p>Of course, those who wanted to catch his favor would also try their best to please him. In a way, coming to Planet Solaryn was a must; after all, it was also the planet closest to Neomi’s home world.</p>
<p>As for the intelligent ones, they quickly vacated as instructed.</p>
<p>Meanwhile, instead of panicking, Kent was more than relaxed despite the odds mounting against him. He was not scared in any way. After all, he had more than enough warriors under his command to use in the upcoming battle.</p>
<p>He was not worried in the least.</p>
<p>He had already experienced how powerful he was, so he was even looking forward to their next bout. Of course, that didn’t mean he was bloodthirsty to the point he didn’t know when to be civil and when to be chaotic.</p>
<p>In fact, under normal circumstances, considering what the Pill Families had done—the number of alchemists they had killed just to rise to power—Kent shouldn’t have even given them an hour to pack up.</p>
<p>He should have killed every last one of them, for they had hurt many people.</p>
<p>However, Kent, despite his bloodthirsty nature, chose to walk the path Unity wanted for Chaos in this life. Thankfully, he had more than enough emotional support.</p>
<p>His mother was also there to keep him straight, so at the moment, he was a rational human/dragon who chose to give his enemies a chance to keep their lives. Those who disobeyed, he, of course, would kill as compensation for the pain they had caused.</p>
<p>Kent, his wives, and Nana decided to wait for the true members of the Pyris Pill Alliance that were driven out to come so he could put things in place properly this time around.</p>
<p>In the meantime, he decided to find out all that had transpired when he left three hundred seventy thousand years ago.</p>
<p>"When the master left, Lady Nefeli became the leader who oversaw that everything was left in good hands. They destroyed the Evil Pill Sect as you instructed.</p>
<p>After that, they purged all the filth from the various Pill Alliances, making for the first time a peaceful and true Pill World.</p>
<p>I was happy, and everyone was happy.</p>
<p>They even gave the Nine Pill Worlds four Primordial Flames each. Of course, because of the limited number of Primordial Flames, they cultivated them into intelligent flames.</p>
<p>I gave them the technique and recipe to do that. This way, everyone could use them without anyone owing them. It was one of the best moments for the Ten Pill Worlds.</p>
<p>However, after they ascended and the next six generations of alchemists also left, the ambitious Pill Worlds decided to seize everything.</p>
<p>Of course, I intervened somehow and helped some promising alchemists escape. However, many died.</p>
<p>Since then, the Ten Pill Worlds had gone downhill. The Evil Sect had also resurfaced about forty thousand years ago, making things even worse.</p>
<p>So that is what happened after you left."</p>
<p>"I know it’s been hard on you, Nana, but thank you for caring for the Alliances. Now that I am here, I will make sure this never happens again."</p>
<p>Nana nodded her small head.</p>
<p>She had indeed done well, considering she was not even whole. If it hadn’t been for her, many would have died.</p>
<p>However, now that Kent knew what had indeed happened, the idea of sparing any of them seemed to become a mistake in his mind.</p>
<p>However, he had already promised to spare those who would take his generosity, so he would fulfill that promise. However, the Evil Pill Sect would soon be completely eradicated.</p>
<p>After a couple of hours, the important members of the Pyris Alliance arrived, and Kent commenced the meeting.</p>
