<p>The Immortal Realm has only five galaxies, unlike the High World, which has several. There are the Eastern, Western, Northern, Southern, and Central Galaxies.</p>
<p>Each galaxy, however, is so large that many galaxies from the High World could fit inside just one. This was why there are millions of planetary systems and thousands of star systems within a single galaxy.</p>
<p>Sekesua is a star system in the Eastern Region. It is one of many, and within the Sekesua Star System, there are dozens of planetary systems, including Bormase.</p>
<p>Bormase has only seven planets, with Krobo as the largest and Lumenore the wealthiest. Lumenore is home to the Sun Elves, Wood Elves, Moon Elves, Dawn Elves, and Wind Elves.</p>
<p>They said that throughout the Sekesua Star System, Planet Lumenore was home to the most beautiful race. Elves were naturally good-looking. However, a planet packed with five different Elven races truly had a variety of Elves that stood out.</p>
<p>Even in the entire Eastern Galaxy, despite there being millions of planets and races, the Princess of Lumenore was counted among the top ten beauties of the Eastern Galaxy.</p>
<p>Princess Euren, the most mysterious person in all of the Sekesua Star System and beyond.</p>
<p>She wasn’t someone popular like most princes and princesses. People hardly saw her; however, whenever she appeared, her beauty caught everyone’s attention.</p>
<p>Most of the time, she only made appearances when there was war. They said her role as a princess mostly involved going to war. Perhaps because of this unique trait about her, she was someone many never seemed to forget.</p>
<p>However, in reality, the princess was rarely seen, not because she was busy with royal duties. She wasn’t seen often simply because she liked to sleep.</p>
<p>It was said that as one cultivates and grows stronger, sleeping becomes something of the past to them. However, sleep was something Princess Euren never seemed to get enough of.</p>
<p>Over the years, her family thought she was just lazy. After all, while the rest of her siblings worked hard cultivating and learning royal duties, she was hard asleep.</p>
<p>They even named her the Sleeping Beauty.</p>
<p>However, as the years passed, they began to discover something alarming about her. While she spent most of her time sleeping, her cultivation continued to rise. It was as though the more she slept, the stronger she became.</p>
<p>Their greatest shock came when she, among all her sisters, was the first to awaken her bloodline and become a Demigod while asleep.</p>
<p>It was then that her parents and some trusted aides of the royal family realized that she wasn’t someone simple.</p>
<p>But by the time they discovered her true identity, their enemies had also discovered it as well.</p>
<p>From the Supreme Realm, messengers had been sent to take her away since the Judge of the Divine saw her as a threat, or so they were made to believe.</p>
<p>As the Sin of Sloth, one of the Seven Deadly Sins, they considered her someone who posed a threat to the universe.</p>
<p>Of course, what many didn’t know was that the one who truly wanted her was someone who intended to take her sin and bestow it upon themselves. While the sins are deadly, they also have great talent and unique paths.</p>
<p>The universe has many rules, but they apply only to gods, immortals, and mortals. Supremes and Celestials can bend those rules to do whatever they want.</p>
<p>So while a Deadly Sin was indeed a danger to the universe, unless they were provoked, they wouldn’t use their power recklessly. Princess Euren was a very peaceful person.</p>
<p>However, the sneaky Supreme who ordered her capture had no intention of backing down, so he ordered six kingdoms to use any means necessary to retrieve the princess.</p>
<p>Thirty days ago, they attacked; however, what they expected and what happened did not align. In fact, the moment they appeared on Planet Lumenore and surrounded it, something chilling happened.</p>
<p>Out of nowhere, portals started to open, and nightmare creatures began pouring out.</p>
<p>Many panicked, but it was already too late; the Sin of Sloth had made her move, and the six kingdoms lost more than they could afford to lose.</p>
<p>They received firsthand experience of what the Sin of Sloth was capable of. As someone who loved to sleep, she found her path as a cultivator tied to dreams and nightmares.</p>
<p>Just like how Beast Tamers tame beasts, she tamed nightmare creatures. Perhaps if they had noticed her nature earlier, they would have succeeded in capturing her.</p>
<p>But she had lived for thousands of years, taming hundreds of thousands of nightmare creatures. It took her just ten minutes to prove to the six kingdoms, including the biggest among them all, that Planet Lumenore was not a place they could come and go from without consequences.</p>
<p>The Sin of Sloth liked to sleep, but she was not as weak as one would expect.</p>
<p>However, by attacking the six kingdoms, she had inadvertently gone against a Supreme who held immense power and was highly motivated to obtain the Dao of the Sin of Sloth.</p>
<p>So she did not take kindly to her actions, and so she sent an army of Demigods and Immortals, handpicked from among his vassal gods, to bring Planet Lumenore to its knees.</p>
<p>Today, the entire planet was surrounded by hundreds of thousands of Demigods and Immortals whose mission was to use any means necessary to bring in the Sin of Sloth.</p>
<p>Today, Princess Euren woke up, sensing the danger surrounding her planet.</p>
<p>Up in the air, three demonic-looking Demigods sneered when they saw the beautiful princess appear on the front lines, followed by her two brothers, Prince Caelrion and Prince Letharion.</p>
<p>Some of the generals also followed her alongside thousands of Elven archers, mages, bladesmen, and others.</p>
<p>"Are you here to surrender?" one of the demon Demigods asked, a sneer plastered across his face. To many, he was bad news, especially because he had single-handedly brought down entire armies.</p>
<p>They called him the Lone General.</p>
<p>Since he had appeared here, it was clear their mission was not only to bring in the princess, but also to reduce Planet Lumenore to rubble.</p>
<p>Princess Euren scanned the Lone General for a few seconds before smirking.</p>
<p>"Whoever sent you clearly had no intention of leaving my people in peace, whether I surrendered or not. But even if they would let us go in peace, what makes you think I would surrender to a loser like that?</p>
<p>"Or do you people think I am someone who can be messed with? If I wanted to, I could destroy this entire star system within days. However, I like peace and quiet, so I held back my power. But since we are here now, allow me to open your eyes."</p>
<p>She pointed at the three demon generals.</p>
<p>"I warned you all."</p>
<p>A large portal opened, and from within, hundreds of thousands of nightmare creatures started pouring out.</p>
<p>The battle started, and at once, the three demon generals realized they had to be careful, or this would be their death.</p>
