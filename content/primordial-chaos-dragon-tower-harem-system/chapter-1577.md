<p>[Planet Brymrr - Ascension Field Prepared by Inaya for her ascension]</p>
<p>Since the time inside the Star Gateway is much faster than the outside, Kent may have spent 100 days already in there, but on the outside, it had been just 10 days.</p>
<p>Within those ten days, Vexthra made arrangements for her sisters and legion, making sure the dungeon mission continued, especially since Kaizo, who had now reached the Spirit Immortal stage, wouldn’t let her rest.</p>
<p>After the tower was upgraded, the dungeons inside the Legion Tower moved to the new tower. The tower calls it the Dungeon Tower. Aside from the 12 dungeons already in the tower, two more were added.</p>
<p>However, they remained locked, preventing anyone from entering.</p>
<p>Vexthra had already placed them as SS-grade dungeons with at least 24 floors. This means the last floor bosses are Demigods.</p>
<p>Once cleared, Kent would be getting two more demigod subordinates.</p>
<p>However, the other 12 dungeons had upgraded to S grade, making the final floor bosses, such as Veera, Shade King Nykali, and the rest, all rise to the Immortal stage, with Ankaara even reaching the Immortal Lord stage.</p>
<p>Due to this, they all wanted to head back to the Planet Killer Dungeon and finish clearing the floors before Kent returned. So Vexthra gave the command and system permission to Uncle Drew and sent them away, leaving only her, Miryssa, Inaya, Ainna, Umai, and Daliah.</p>
<p>They spent some time inside the tower, making sure Inaya was ready since her Supreme Tribulation is only a few days away.</p>
<p>After enough planning, they returned to Planet Brymrr, where she would face the heavens and prove she deserves to be a Supreme.</p>
<p>Naturally, Supreme ascension and Celestial ascension are regarded by many, since according to the rules set by the Universe Council, the birth of a new Supreme means the universe has gained one more strength.</p>
<p>The heavens recognized this, so whenever a Supreme was about to appear, they would resonate far and wide, making many aware.</p>
<p>Because of this resonance, the enemies also try to use the chance to strike when the one taking the tribulation is occupied.</p>
<p>So Kent left Baldy to protect her just in case. But even he is not enough, not if a high-ranking Celestial from the Soul Temple arrives. So before the tribulation, Vexthra made a gamble and used all of Kent’s Tower points and even borrowed some to purchase an item from Gaia.</p>
<p>She believes it would be enough to give Inaya the chance to successfully break through to the Supreme stage.</p>
<p>When they returned to Planet Brymrr, the first thing Inaya did was enter a vault buried deep in the ocean. Guarding the vault is a universe god-level beast called Green Horn Angel Unicorn.</p>
<p>It was her mount during the divine era and also one of the strongest beast gods in the universe.</p>
<p>Inside the vault is a slick sword, one that had been left there for billions of years. After the Divine Era, she hadn’t touched it.</p>
<p>So one could only imagine the excitement the sword spirit, which had been asleep, felt when her master came back for her.</p>
<p>The moment she retrieved her sword and mount, they moved to the ascension ground she had prepared several years ago.</p>
<p>They got to work immediately.</p>
<p>A few days later, the rainbow sky appeared, signaling the imminent ascension of a Supreme.</p>
<p>"Baldy, you should be on high alert."</p>
<p>As someone who had previously offended many powerhouses, they knew slacking even for a second would cost them. So Vexthra wanted everything to go well.</p>
<p>Baldy nodded and walked back, stopping 200 km away.</p>
<p>"We should also go to our positions," Vexthra suggested, and they dispersed, each going to a location where they believed would give them enough advantage to handle any threat.</p>
<p>"Good luck, Inaya."</p>
<p>In less than five minutes, clouds started forming, and with them, the arrival of some powerhouses.</p>
<p>Demigods, Gods, Supremes, and Celestials started appearing on the planet. While not all of them could make it because of the distance, they all felt it, and so those who were close came.</p>
<p>However, as more powerhouses started to appear, one question lingered on everyone’s mind.</p>
<p>"Who is the lady about to ascend?"</p>
<p>None of them recognized Inaya, as was to be expected, given that she had lived during the Second Era (the Divine Era). Beings from that era are known as Ancient Ancestors, since almost all of them had long become Celestials and the backbone of this universe.</p>
<p>A mere God cannot be from that era, so none even entertained that idea.</p>
<p>Soon, this question began to disturb many, to the point that even the Celestials wanted to command Inaya to reveal her identity.</p>
<p>Safe to say she did, and when those who knew of her existence recognized her, the shock left them all gobsmacked.</p>
<p>Just when the tribulation cloud was almost formed, Inaya opened her eyes, and when she did, ten identical copies of herself burst out from her body and flew into the air, each having a distinct aura and hair color.</p>
<p>The aura of Wrath, Sorrow, Joy, Fear, Love, Hatred, Despair, Hope, Calm, and Obsession surrounded them, each exuding the power of a Sword Supreme.</p>
<p>"Oh my, it is her... The Ten Emotions Sword Supreme, the second God to become a Sword Supreme, and the partner of the legendary Lord Genesis."</p>
<p>The moment her identity was revealed, many gasped in shock.</p>
<p>Up until today, many of them had only heard of her from their ancestors or read about her in ancient records.</p>
<p>Seeing her in person left all of them wondering just what they were about to witness.</p>
<p>A Supreme who had tried to follow the path of the Ten Emotions Sword Supreme but failed looked at her idol with a look of delight.</p>
<p>"I only managed to master four emotions, yet my swordsmanship is on another level. I can’t believe I am about to witness the real deal."</p>
<p>She retrieved a recording device and started recording everything. She wasn’t the only one; some Celestials even began secretly recording.</p>
<p>Even before the tribulation was revealed, word had spread far and wide, reaching both friends and foes. Soon, Planet Brymrr would witness the gathering of the universe’s greatest.</p>
<p>However, the main event soon revealed itself.</p>
<p>*RUMBLE* *RUMBLE*</p>
<p>From the tribulation cloud, seven banners engraved with the heads of seven different beasts appeared and hovered just below the cloud, releasing terrifying Supreme Qi.</p>
<p>*GASP*</p>
<p>Yet again, everyone gasped, including some of the Celestials.</p>
<p>"The Seven Ancient Beast Banners... just how..."</p>
<p>Many recognized the banners, for in the history of Supreme ascension, all who faced it failed to ascend, many dying in the process.</p>
<p>Because it had claimed the lives of many supreme candidates, it was named the Seven Supreme Omen.</p>
<p>Facing it in an Ascension Tribulation is the unluckiest one can be. In fact, everyone present felt a sudden pity for Inaya, for in their hearts, she had already failed.</p>
<p>"Such a pity." A Celestial shook his bald head. "From what I know, nobody had ever passed the Seven Ancient Beast Banners trial. The universe will again lose yet another powerhouse because of them having bad luck."</p>
<p>"You are wrong, Tankhak. Someone passed this trial before," another Celestial by the name Bloj said, his hand passing through his beard.</p>
<p>When he spoke, many who heard him turned and looked at him. Many recognized him as an elder from the Cult of Wisdom, so they could tell he knew something.</p>
<p>"Who?" Celestial Tankhak asked.</p>
<p>"A very long time ago, a madman known by all as the Ruthless Bandit faced the Seven Ancient Beast Banners and passed it with easy, becoming the first Supreme to achieve that."</p>
<p>RUMBLE</p>
