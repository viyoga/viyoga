<p>While Adina was looking forward to meeting a mysterious, perhaps ancient, old lady, the face that greeted him was that of a beauty, not even a year older than the High Priestess.</p>
<p>She had purple hair that fell down her back and a pair of golden eyes. She was, in fact, a beauty who would make many men drool at the sight of her.</p>
<p>The aura around her was also calm and welcoming, almost harmless if one doesn’t know what she is and what she has done with her Bow and Arrow.</p>
<p>They appeared before her like ghosts, and the first question she asked was, of course, about the one called Kent Madson.</p>
<p>"What do you know about Kent Madson?" The question was directed at Adina.</p>
<p>She gulped, feeling the overwhelming aura, though a welcoming one, swirl around the lady.</p>
<p>"I met him on my way to this world. He was the one who saved me and ensured I reached the teleportation mountains in one piece."</p>
<p>The lady sighed in her heart, knowing her window had closed. It had been a month since Adina arrived in Mekyn, so she knew Kent would be long gone by now. If she were to go there, she would be too late.</p>
<p>But even so, she wanted to know more about him.</p>
<p>To the rest of the universe, Kent Madson became popular a few months ago, but to Lady Enyo, Kent Madson had been appearing in her dreams for three good years now.</p>
<p>She started dreaming about him at about the same time Kent arrived in Sonox.</p>
<p>At first, it was just flashes of dreams of this boy whose smile was brighter than stars. Back then, she would occasionally wake up smiling. It even made her want to go to sleep every evening so that she could dream about him.</p>
<p>However, as months passed, the dreams started demanding answers.</p>
<p>It got to the point she had to drop everything and focus on finding everything she could about this mysterious Kent Madson. However, no matter how hard she looked, she found nothing.</p>
<p>It made her pull away from the outside and seclude herself in her small mountain abode.</p>
<p>However, a few months ago, rumors began spreading about a mysterious name that suddenly appeared on the Sword Dao tablet.</p>
<p>The Kent Madson that made her think she was going mad became known to everyone, and just like what happened to her, everyone started looking for him.</p>
<p>The name was known to many, but there was no face to the name. At first, many youngsters tried to use the name to gain the favour of immortals, but they regretted it in mere hours.</p>
<p>And so for weeks now, nobody dares come out claiming to be Kent Madson.</p>
<p>The name Kent Madson was a mystery.</p>
<p>She tried her best, but knowing it was never going to pan out in the end, she entered a state of self-imposed seclusion, telling everyone not to disturb her.</p>
<p>But now, she was more eager to learn more about Kent Madson from someone who actually saw him.</p>
<p>"What was he like?" Lady Enyo asked.</p>
<p>Adina smiled, "Shameless. That is the best way I can describe him. That shameless bastard."</p>
<p>For some strange reason, Lady Enyo smiled, recalling some moment in her dream where this Kent Madson would beat his chest and brag about something he could never achieve, even if he had thirteen lives to chase after it.</p>
<p>She would wake up smiling, sometimes even laughing, but as much as she would love to know what this Kent Madson was to her, she never seemed to figure it out.</p>
<p>"But aside from that, he is reliable and terrifying."</p>
<p>"Terrifying, how so?" Lady Enyo asked.</p>
<p>"For starters, he is just a Core Formation stage cultivator, but he can easily kill Spirit Lords."</p>
<p>"What?" Both Lady Enyo and the High Priestess exclaimed upon hearing Adina’s words.</p>
<p>"He is dangerous. In fact, before I parted ways with him, he was planning on killing a Level 2 Heavenly Ascension stage demon.</p>
<p>I don’t know if you have heard of him, the wandering Demon swordsman."</p>
<p>"Wait a minute," The High Priestess raised her finger, unsure if she should deploy me to the Valley of Torture despite Adina telling her not to.</p>
<p>"No need to look shocked, High Priestess. It has been a month already; I am sure he has killed him by now. Trust me, Kent is no simple person.</p>
<p>He is friendly, fierce, and resourceful. For now, you all wouldn’t take my words seriously, but when you meet him, you will understand."</p>
<p>The High Priestess looked at Lady Enyo, who held a smile on her face. "My lady, this Kent Madson sounds a lot like you."</p>
<p>Lady Enyo smirked, "I wasn’t able to kill Spirit Lords when I was at his level, so don’t measure me to him. This Kent Madson sounds more like those freaks in the Supreme Worlds and Immortal Worlds."</p>
<p>"Well, he said he is from a washout Medium world. So I think measuring him to them is an insult to Kent. If he were born in such worlds, then he would already be killing gods."</p>
<p>"Looks like you and this Kent are rather close," Lady Enyo spoke with a teasing smile, causing Adina to blush. The High Priestess smiled, watching this.</p>
<p>’At least she is smiling. This is a good thing.’</p>
<p>Watching the smile on Lady Enyo’s face made her very happy. The past few months have been tough.</p>
<p>She was obsessed with Kent Madson to the point that every word that came out of her mouth was all about Kent. However, now, she seemed to have a part of her hunger quenched thanks to Adina.</p>
<p>"What do you plan on doing now, my lady?" The High Priestess asked.</p>
<p>"Nothing. Now that I know he is real, it is only a matter of time before we meet."</p>
<p>The High Priestess sighed, knowing this was indeed a good thing. She wouldn’t have to worry about her for a while, at least.</p>
<p>"Take Adina to the Yin Cave and have her cultivate there. I will handle some issues here, then I will come out of seclusion to help you handle the issues you are facing."</p>
<p>The High Priestess nodded and left with Adina.</p>
<p>When they were gone, Lady Enyo smiled weakly.</p>
<p>"Kent Madson, a beautiful name."</p>
<p>With that, she entered a room to freshen up and then decided on what to handle next. Knowing Adina had spent days with Kent meant she would have some excuse to learn more about him.</p>
