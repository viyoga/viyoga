<p>"Haa."</p>
<p>Kent sighed, putting away the flying swords.</p>
<p>"I am still weak."</p>
<p>Despite just killing someone seven realms above him, he still felt weak.</p>
<p>"Tower, I want you to measure my performance against an opponent from a Supreme High World. Then tell me the percentage that I will die fighting them."</p>
<p>[The master in his current form, factoring in the Primal Chaos Core but not his bloodline, would have an 8% chance of killing a Level 2 Heavenly Ascension stage from a Supreme High World.</p>
<p>If the master were to use his bloodline at 3%, he would have a 38% chance of killing the same person. However, if the master were to raise the mastery level of his skills to perfection, then his chances of success, everything included, will rise to 54%.]</p>
<p>Kent smiled slightly, unsure if he had to look down on himself or be proud. Thankfully, the tower knew when to come into play.</p>
<p>[But the master should know that he still hasn’t refined his core yet. According to the information I have, the master must form one of the nine Core gates before the master can be regarded as someone who has refined their core seven times.</p>
<p>That said, if the master had formed his core before this battle, things would have ended much faster and less taxing. Also, there is the part about the master’s sword path.</p>
<p>So I suppose I should say the current form of the master is ready for a Great High world, but can’t dominate it yet.]</p>
<p>"So in the end, I am still not strong enough to dominate even a Great High World."</p>
<p>[Yes. The master in his current form can easily face multiple Spirit Sovereigns and walk out unscathed. However, without sneaking up on the Spirit Lords, you aren’t within your current capacity to dominate a Great High world. However, there is a light to this.]</p>
<p>"I sure would want to know, Tower."</p>
<p>[The master only used 1% of his bloodline powers despite having access to 3%. If the master were to use all 3%, then even Spirit Lords, provided they don’t number in hundreds, can be handled.</p>
<p>Also, if the master were to raise his sword mastery to Sword Sage, things would go more easily for him next time. But for now, the master shouldn’t antagonize any Heavenly Ascension stage expert. They are not simple at all.</p>
<p>The one you killed wasn’t truly a worthy Heavenly Ascension stage cultivator or a proper Sword King, and I think you know it.]</p>
<p>"Yeah," Kent smiled weakly, "Stealing power from the blood of his kills through a forbidden art does have its downsides."</p>
<p>[Indeed. So the master should be careful when around Heavenly Ascension stage experts for now, until perhaps the master has formed his core gate and managed to learn the Primal Body Tempering Art.]</p>
<p>"Good to know. But now that this bastard is dead, I suppose it is time for me to leave," Kent sighed and was about to turn around when the tower spoke again.</p>
<p>[The master must cut his head off and send it to the Olive Sword Guild. It is on Planet Menelaus, where Iver is from. Before the requirement is marked as completed, you must send it to the designated location.</p>
<p>Killing him is but the first part of the requirement.</p>
<p>Thankfully, I have the coordinates to that world so you can go there using the ’World Teleporter.’]</p>
<p>"In that case," Kent’s sword moved swiftly, and the head of the wandering demon swordsman came off, "it’s time for me to hit the road. I will return here soon to continue to hunt."</p>
<p>Kent put away the head and his sword and activated the World Teleporter. Soon, he was gone from the Valley of Torture.</p>
<p>---</p>
<p>[Planet Mekyn – The Elven Garden – Adina and the High Priestess]</p>
<p>It had already been a month since Adina arrived on Planet Mekyn. As a ’Great’ High World, she was surrounded by powerful cultivators.</p>
<p>Even the High Priestess was a Level 3 Heavenly Ascension stage cultivator. Since she also possessed the Extreme Pure Yin Body, Adina felt at peace knowing she was no longer alone in her endeavors.</p>
<p>She learned that, despite the High Priestess having the same body constitution as her, she wasn’t on the run. This was because of the Planet Protector.</p>
<p>According to the priestess, the Planet Protector was a heavenly genius, one who appeared once every million years.</p>
<p>Thanks to her protection, she has been able to remain in her world for thousands of years. Though not the strongest world in their planetary alliance, she was protected by someone very powerful.</p>
<p>Adina wanted to meet this Planet Protector, but the High Priestess told her she was in seclusion. However, she was still aware of her presence. This made Adina feel at home, so from the moment she arrived, she started to live a good life.</p>
<p>Of course, at first, she wanted to open the pouch Kent gave her, but she couldn’t bring herself to open it for fear she would find something she couldn’t handle.</p>
<p>And so weeks passed, and finally, she managed to gather the courage to open the pouch.</p>
<p>What she found inside took her breath away, just as Kent had planned.</p>
<p>There were many heaviness-defying fruits inside the pouch, billions of spirit stones, and, well, thousands of cultivation resources, including some armors and quick getaway treasures.</p>
<p>Then spent half his fortune in Tower Points to make sure she was sent away with enough to keep her alive for the time being.</p>
<p>Adina was indeed shocked to find all these treasures. However, what shocked her the most was the two scrolls inside the pouch.</p>
<p>One was addressed to her and the other to the High Priestess, whom Kent named ’Adina’s guardian.’</p>
<p>Adina contemplated reading it first, but in the end, she went and handed it over to the High Priestess, who quickly opened it and began to read.</p>
<p>"I know you don’t know me, and I also don’t know you. However, know that when it comes to Adina, I am her guardian, so please take care of her.</p>
<p>I planned to come get her one of these days, but before then, you must make sure she takes her cultivation seriously and, should the need call for it, make sure she runs as far away as possible.</p>
<p>I don’t know what martial technique will be suitable for you, so I’ve added a God Grade cloning technique in this scroll instead. Learn it and use it to protect her. I will reward you when we meet in person.</p>
<p>And oh, one last thing... You can call me Kent Madson."</p>
<p>When the High Priestess was done reading the scroll, her jaw was almost on the ground. Adina, who didn’t know what had happened, walked behind her and chose to see what was written in the letter.</p>
<p>When she saw the message, she was equally mesmerized and even blushed thanks to Kent’s shamelessness.</p>
<p>She had yet to read her scroll, but seeing what Kent wrote in the letter addressed to the Priestess, she knew her own would leave her wishing she had never met Kent.</p>
<p>Even so, she looked forward to her own.</p>
<p>"Are you alright, Big Sister?" Adina asked. She and the High Priestess had become sworn sisters, so she addressed her as such.</p>
<p>The High Priestess turned and looked at her for a few seconds before speaking two words.</p>
<p>"Let’s go."</p>
<p>The priestess didn’t say where they were going, but Adina followed her. They flew away from the garden and headed toward a small house sitting atop a mountain where only white plants grew.</p>
<p>The moment they approached the house, Adina felt her body enveloped by a superior energy, one that could seize her body at any moment.</p>
<p>And the worst part, she knew no matter what she did, she wouldn’t be able to free herself.</p>
<p>They landed on soft white grass and walked forward for a few seconds before the High Priestess, who was also the Queen, went down on her knees. Adina followed her promptly.</p>
<p>"Great Planet Guardian, we want to have an audience with you... It is about Kent Madson."</p>
<p>The door opened the next second, and their bodies were dragged inside. It closed promptly...</p>
