<p>Although Shax was not in a favorable territory, he couldn’t help but laugh when he heard Kent’s bold words. It was like hearing the most unbelievable thing ever.</p>
<p>Getting to the Pill Princess and challenging her to a duel was just too arrogant from Shax’s perspective. It was like hearing Kent say he was going to storm the Dragon Empire and dethrone the Dragon Emperor by telling him to stand up and let him have his throne.</p>
<p>The audacity in Kent’s words made Shax unsure whether to be indefinitely amused or angry at the arrogant human before him.</p>
<p>In the end, he just shook his head.</p>
<p>"She is impossible to get to, so forget your ideas about challenging her. Even if you are the best in the human continent when it comes to pill making, you will be a child before her... that would be bullying, and I don’t think she would want that."</p>
<p>Kent was dumbfounded, hearing this.</p>
<p>"Clearly, you are exaggerating. She is the princess of the Elven Empire. I doubt she will be hard to get to." Kent wasn’t sure where Shax’s thoughts came from, but he knew he was exaggerating when he said the Pill Princess couldn’t be approached.</p>
<p>If anything, it should be more like getting to her will be much more difficult, but not impossible. However, saying she is impossible to approach was just too much.</p>
<p>Even he was sure the Supreme Celestial, whom Vexthra said was the ruler of the entire universe, could be approached in some way.</p>
<p>So a normal elven princess shouldn’t be hard to get to...</p>
<p>"I say she is impossible to get to because even her family can’t easily see her, and that is the Elven Queen I am talking about. Getting to her own daughter is not easy."</p>
<p>Kent just continued to stare at Shax as he spoke, making him wonder many things. Thankfully, Shax knew when to let out more information, so he let him speak.</p>
<p>"The reason you can’t get to the Pill Princess is because she is not in the Elven Empire. She is in the most powerful place in the entire world—the Supreme Immortal Sect, the number one sect in the entire world."</p>
<p>"This means you can’t get to her since you are not a disciple there. And don’t even ask me how to become a disciple. You can’t get in, so forget all ideas of getting to the Pill Princess."</p>
<p>"Supreme Immortal Sect—that is a fancy name for a sect in this medium world," Kent smiled. Just hearing the name was enough for him to start planning...</p>
<p>Before he can become a Pill Saint, he must defeat the Pill Princess in a duel. Now, he was just learning that getting to her was impossible simply because she was in the most secure, powerful, and perhaps the only place in the world that was hard to get to...</p>
<p>"There is no place in this world that I, Kent Madson, can’t get to..." Kent bragged, making Shax shake his head. However, a few seconds into his amused expression, his eyes widened.</p>
<p>"You... are Kent Madson." Up until now, he had never heard Kent’s name, so he didn’t even know the person who was messing with him. But now he did, and the revelation shocked him.</p>
<p>"Why, is my name taboo in your race?" Kent couldn’t help asking, watching the shocked expression on Shax’s face.</p>
<p>"No. But you are the most wanted person in the entire world. I don’t know what you did to The Hand, or whether the rumors about you are true, but they spread the word that you are in possession of someone having an Atomic Divine Body.</p>
<p>And in case you are not aware, someone with an atomic divine body is like the greatest treasure anyone would chase after."</p>
<p>"This news made everyone want to get their hands on you, and they even said you have more wealth than a kingdom."</p>
<p>"You shouldn’t step on any of the continents if you don’t want to die, and you should certainly never try to cross the Black Sea. Trust me, you don’t want that."</p>
<p>Kent didn’t know what to say to this, so he just smiled.</p>
<p>’Looks like my warning to The Hand pissed them off so much that they are using all their efforts to ensure I don’t see the light of day.’ His smile bloomed, showing no sign of fear.</p>
<p>’Oh, I most certainly will enjoy destroying that organization.’</p>
<p>"I see. Thank you for telling me, but I will come soon enough and use the Black Sea to get there, so I hope they try to stop me... I most certainly am going to enjoy killing them."</p>
<p>Shax remained quiet, not even wanting to think too much about the events that would happen when Kent showed his face on the other continents. That was something he didn’t want to think about.</p>
<p>"So you are saying you don’t know how to get into this Supreme Immortal Sect, right?" Kent asked, and Shax sighed.</p>
<p>"Only the best of the best in the world can get in. Of course, your human empire has not been part of the selections since the humans declined long ago..</p>
<p>But if you want to get in, then find a way to integrate into the other races and show the world your prowess, and perhaps that will be your way in."</p>
<p>Kent nodded.</p>
<p>He went on to ask more questions. When he was done, he decided not to kill Shax but to make him his informant.</p>
<p>Of course, he didn’t add him to the Legion Tower. He added him to the Servant Tower and made Elaine heal him.</p>
<p>His target was the Blood Demon King, who created the spell that caused the Bloodvein tribe to migrate. Of course, he would destroy the spell soon enough, but he first wants to send a message to the creator.</p>
<p>That would also be Shax’s way of keeping his head on his body. If he does as he was told, he might get more information from this demon alliance and perhaps even gain more insight into how they want to destroy the seal.</p>
<p>After all, Shax said, there are two methods, but the other method is tough to manage. But now, they might try that considering their first method was a no-go...</p>
<p>He wants to know all their plans, and Shax will get all that information.</p>
<p>Shax promised to gather what he could on the shadow demons and the other demon states. Kent needed all this because once he came out of seclusion, he would start working toward his end goals in this world.</p>
<p>One of them was to become a Pill Saint and perhaps a Pill Sage... maybe even a Pill King before ascending to a higher world.</p>
<p>He must prove himself to the world and show how best to do that if he does not dominate everywhere.</p>
<p>He sent him away after ensuring he was prepared for his undercover mission. Kent ensured he had what he needed to gather more information on the demons and even the elves.</p>
<p>He couldn’t do that in the Dragon Empire because they wouldn’t even allow him access to their lands. But he could enter the Elven Empire since he had connections there.</p>
<p>After Shax left, Kent went to see how the Bloodvein tribe was faring; however, on his way, a sudden quest made him turn and move back to the Harem Tower...</p>
