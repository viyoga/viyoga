<p>"I am afraid I can’t do that." Kingdom Protector Nathan said, taking out his sword. Behind him, hundreds of Peak Sovereigns and a few Core formation experts appeared.</p>
<p>There were even three peak core formation experts in the fold. This was a clear sign that the four kingdom protectors weren’t the strongest.</p>
<p>There are hidden gems, and it seems they are all in this together.</p>
<p>"So you all are in this together...even you, Grandpa," Princess Lovina said, looking toward a bald man who seemed to be in his late 60s. He is one of the three Peak Core formation experts.</p>
<p>"Sorry, little girl. If your father had listened to reason and handed over the Dream crystal, none of this would have happened. Thankfully, you are here now, so why don’t you hand over that necklace around your neck?" the old man said with a smile.</p>
<p>Princess Lovina turned and looked at the necklace around her neck, focusing on the crystal inside a small golden circle. It looked almost like a diamond, and the shine coming from it looked dreamy.</p>
<p>"So because of this, you killed every last person of the Deckling family." Princess Lovine bit her lips, her blood boiling as she looked past Kingdom Protector Nathan, locking gaze with her grandpa, who once carried her on his neck and played with her.</p>
<p>Just remembering those fond memories seems to make her blood boil even more.</p>
<p>"Yes. Your father and mother wouldn’t hand that thing over, and since it can’t be forcefully taken, I will advise you to hand it over, girl.</p>
<p>The path of cultivation is a torturous one. If they had listened and handed over the crystal, everything would have ended well for them. Perhaps they would be alive today. So don’t make the same mistake your parent made."</p>
<p>The Dream Crystal was something that was discovered in the ancient battlefield of Parting. It was said to hold the dream of an immortal who once lived in this world.</p>
<p>Inside the dream is the location of his hidden treasures, which he left behind before he ascended.</p>
<p>Princess Lovina’s father found it on one of his explorations in the ancient battlefield of parting, but his cultivation base wasn’t high enough to view the dream of an immortal, so he held on to it.</p>
<p>However, the evil men always want what doesn’t belong to them, so they came after it. When he refused to hand it over, they started to use force. Perhaps they thought killing a few would compel him to hand it over.</p>
<p>But he didn’t, and since they can’t be forcefully taken unless Lovina’s father hands it over, they killed everyone, even killing him in the hope that after he died, the Dream Crystal would pick someone else.</p>
<p>However, when the massacre ended and they checked, the Dream crystal was long gone, making their massacre a pointless one. But now, what they spent hundreds of years looking for was now right in front of them.</p>
<p>Princess Lovina’s eyes filled with tears, knowing she had lost everything because of a trinket. Then, she grabbed the dream crystal, and before anyone could move, she crashed it, causing everyone to turn pale.</p>
<p>"What did you do?" Princess Lovina’s grandpa shouted.</p>
<p>"I got my revnge, you old bastard. No wonder mom always called you an ugly, useless, good-for-nothing, lazy, wasteful person who doesn’t have his priorities straight.</p>
<p>You are a disgrace to all grandpas. Why am I even calling you that...you don’t deserve such a title, you ugly thing calling himself a human"</p>
<p>"Kill her." The old man’s blood was boiling so much that blood started seeping from the core of his eyes.</p>
<p>Protector Nathan stabbed his sword forward, aiming to end Princess Lovina’s life. Of course, she made no attempts to dodge. The revenge she wanted went better than she had hoped.</p>
<p>The hopes of everyone who was after the Dream Crystal were crushed in her palm, which felt good. So she was ready to die. Not that she could survive the force she was up against.</p>
<p>Protector Nathan’s sword, coated in sword qi, was stabbed toward her. However, just when the sword could touch her, a kick came out of nowhere and landed on Protector Nathan’s face, sending him flying.</p>
<p>"Why don’t you pick someone your own size?" Vari said, appearing in front of Princess Lovina. The next second, a chilling laughter entered everyone’s ears.</p>
