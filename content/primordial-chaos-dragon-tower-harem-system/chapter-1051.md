<p>Kent looked at the two men frozen under his pressure and smiled.</p>
<p>"I know what you are wondering. How did we end up in this mess?"</p>
<p>Kent grinned, "You were the leader of the Blue Flame Pill Palace, and you were the Vice Leader... a rather obedient and trustworthy one, considering you could have killed him and taken his place.</p>
<p>However, you didn’t, and now, here we are.</p>
<p>You were supposed to be respected, unbeatable, and scary.</p>
<p>However, here you are suppressed and soon to be tortured, all because you couldn’t take a loss like a man, and instead chose to chase a harmless woman all the way to her world for something she won fair and square."</p>
<p>Kent pushed the Old Man down and sat in his chair, placing his leg on the table. His left hand supported his chin as he looked at them.</p>
<p>"First of all, why don’t you tell me the main reason why you went after the Soul Spirit?"</p>
<p>Kent lit the Umbral Flame of Oblivion, bringing it closer to Vice Leader Vince’s face. The danger within the flame made his skin crawl. Naturally, that told him what he needed to know...</p>
<p>"We wanted it to gain favor with the Planet King," Vince said, a pained expression on his face. The Umbral Flame of Oblivion isn’t something to be taken lightly.</p>
<p>"Now I am intrigued," Kent leaned in the chair and crossed his legs.</p>
<p>"Humor me."</p>
<p>Vince winced, but he had to speak because the pain he felt from the dark flames on Kent’s palm was something his soul was afraid of.</p>
<p>"The Planet King is the leader of the Evil Sect. He helped us rise to the level we are at now, so we respect him and his organization.</p>
<p>However, we want to be able to rise above the Pill Alliance, and to do that, we have to gain the favor of the Planet King.</p>
<p>This means if we were to present a Tier 18 Forsaken Soul Spirit to him, not only would he be happy; he might pull us into his inner circle..."</p>
<p>Vince paused when he suddenly noticed something. He turned his head, which he could actually do now, and looked at the Old Man, who was looking back at him with a frown.</p>
<p>"You idiot... what have you done?" the Old Man shouted, his fury bubbling up.</p>
<p>"Hey, don’t shout at him like that," Kent replied, his expression a mix of anger and amusement...</p>
<p>"Yes, why are you shouting at me like that?"</p>
<p>"This idiot," the Old Man gritted his teeth and said, "he is clearly mind-controlling you. The Vince I knew wouldn’t dare divulge such sensitive information to anyone... definitely not to some no-name."</p>
<p>"That is harsh, Leader," Vince said, a frown appearing on his face. "You would do well not to talk to Master Chaos like that."</p>
<p>Kent smiled, feeling very proud.</p>
<p>When he teleported inside the room, he wanted to use the nirvana flames to torture them. However, he soon realized he was much stronger now.</p>
<p>Despite being weak mentally before, now that he had broken three limits on his mind, if he used his bloodline power to suppress them, he could mind-control them.</p>
<p>He tried it with Vince, and looking at him now, Kent knew he was as good as dead.</p>
<p>"So the Planet King is the leader of the Evil Sect."</p>
<p>"Yes, Master Chaos."</p>
<p>"Very good, Vince. I will ensure your death is very painful."</p>
<p>Vince nodded with a smile. "I deserve to die painfully, Master Kent."</p>
<p>Kent read his memories, and so far, if he had the chance, he would boil hot water and pour it down the bastard’s throat for causing so much pain to innocent families.</p>
<p>He killed and separated so many families that one couldn’t begin to think a person could do such vile things.</p>
<p>"How about the armor you bought from the Brown Maple Auction House?" Kent asked, and Vince retrieved the armor from his space ring.</p>
<p>Kent took it and placed it directly inside his space ring.</p>
<p>He walked to the Old Man and activated the Soul Torture. In no time, he penetrated the old man’s soul defense and locked on his soul core.</p>
<p>This means he will die regardless of whether he is guilty or not. Also, his mind is now Kent’s to do with as he pleases.</p>
<p>Kent retrieved two daggers and placed them on the table, and stood up...</p>
<p>"You each have a dagger. All you have to do is skin each other alive. You are not allowed to scream. Also, Old Man, in 8 minutes, stop breathing and die like the scum you are.</p>
<p>Vince, as for you, after he dies, continue to skin yourself alive until you can’t handle it anymore. Only then are you allowed to die."</p>
<p>"Yes, Master Kent."</p>
<p>Kent nodded and vanished, leaving the two to take action.</p>
<p>Vince grabbed a dagger and cut the old man’s ear off.</p>
<p>In retaliation, the old man cut his nose off. Vince took off his other ear, and what shouldn’t be called upon anyone had just landed on these two simply because they had crossed the wrong person.</p>
<p>But even if they hadn’t crossed Kent, killing thousands of mortals and using them as ingredients for the Soul Extinguishing pill warranted what was happening to them a thousand times over.</p>
<p>Kent left them, but he kept watching them skin each other alive. He left a strand of the Umbral Flame of Oblivion.</p>
<p>In 25 minutes, it would burn the mansion to the ground, bringing an end to the tyranny these two had inflicted over the years.</p>
<p>While Kent was walking away, a voice suddenly entered his mind, causing him to halt in his step immediately...</p>
<p>"Lord Chaos, we are under attack."</p>
<p>One of the beasts he had left on Planet Nictus reported to him.</p>
<p>"By who?" Kent asked. Thanks to the bracelet, he could talk to them over long distances.</p>
<p>"The Planet King. I think he is after Lady Azela, Kami, and Agnes."</p>
<p>Kent tried to harness the teleportation ability of the taming bracelet, but it didn’t work...</p>
<p>’Tower, what is happening?’</p>
<p>[The taming bracelet is only at Tier 1. You can use it to teleport only when it is Tier 4.]</p>
<p>’Damn it.’</p>
<p>"Lord Chaos, he had taken the three of them and is living."</p>
<p>"What about the formation... is it active?"</p>
<p>"Yes, Lord Chaos... But it doesn’t have any effect on him."</p>
<p>"Are they hurt?"</p>
<p>"No, Lord Chaos." The beast reporting to him paused for a moment before adding more context. "I think they surrendered. I just received a transmission from Lady Azela... She said she was sorry that she would miss you."</p>
<p>Kent’s heartbeat slowed down. He knew what she meant...</p>
<p>"Tell her to give me five days."</p>
<p>Kent teleported to the teleportation gate, where the same man who had given him VIP treatment a few months back was still working.</p>
<p>"Good to see you again, young Master," Daniel greeted, and Kent responded with a nod.</p>
<p>"I need to get to Planet Nictus, but I can’t wait seven days to get there. Is there a quick way?" Kent asked, and Daniel smiled.</p>
<p>"There is indeed a way to get you there within minutes... but it will cost you a fortune even if you are you."</p>
<p>"I don’t mind..."</p>
<p>"Lord Chaos, what are your orders?" The beast asked again...</p>
<p>"Sit tight and make sure no harm comes to the disciples of the sect. I will be there in a few minutes."</p>
<p>Daniel led Kent to a room where a white portal that was exuding faint cosmic energy stood.</p>
