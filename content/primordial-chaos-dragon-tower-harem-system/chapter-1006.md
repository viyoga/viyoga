<p>"Kid, you actually returned alive."</p>
<p>Kent yet again stood under the appraising gaze of the man who sold him the ticket when he was leaving for Planet Cosima.</p>
<p>Back then, the man tried to persuade him not to go to such a place. However, Kent was keen on going, so in the end, he let him join the ship without paying a dime.</p>
<p>Now, after a month, Kent again stood before him, only that this time, he had returned from Planet Cosima alive and healthy.</p>
<p>"No need to look shocked, big man, I am not easily killed." Kent laughed. It is perfectly normal to assume someone like him will be food to the weakest beast in the flame sea.</p>
<p>After all, he is very weak, considering he is at the Core Formation stage.</p>
<p>However, Kent is no weakling, and the man can see that now.</p>
<p>"How did you survive there?" the man asked, and just as he had with Old Rudolf back on the Moon, he told the man a story that left him wondering just what kind of existence Kent is.</p>
<p>"You are one lucky bastard, kid."</p>
<p>"True. But I am happy I was able to make it back. I won’t be going back there for a while, at least."</p>
<p>"You really shouldn’t. Use this experience as a lesson to strive for greatness. You have had one hell of an adventure, kid."</p>
<p>Kent nodded and retrieved a space ring from his soul universe and handed it over to the man. "Take it as a token of your generosity and the care you have shown me." Kent handed the ring to him.</p>
<p>"You can call me Kent Madson, by the way."</p>
<p>After saying that, he left.</p>
<p>The man, who went by Charles, looked into the space ring and froze. He looked to call Kent back and ask him some questions, but he was nowhere in sight.</p>
<p>Charles looked in the space ring again, double-checking to see if he was seeing correctly.</p>
<p>"No way, right... He didn’t actually kill this many beasts."</p>
<p>Inside the space rings are the body parts of some of the beasts he had killed. Kent is already aware of how valuable these body parts are to blacksmiths, so he allowed the Monarchs and the rest of his tamed beasts to harvest these body parts.</p>
<p>Thankfully, Sylvara had many space rings from the many humans she had killed in the past.</p>
<p>He packed everything into one and gave it to Charles, who had helped him in the past without even knowing him.</p>
<p>Kent stood 2000 km away, looking at Charles through the Soul Map. "It is your good fortune to meet me, Charles. In the future, let’s meet again."</p>
<p>After saying that, he went to the nearest teleportation platform and quickly made his way back to the Heaven’s Eye Sect.</p>
<p>"Stop right there," a guard at the entrance stopped Kent just as he reached the entrance. He could have used the Soul Map to teleport, but he didn’t.</p>
<p>"Name and ID, please."</p>
<p>Kent smiled and said, "Kindly tell the sect leader Kent is here to visit."</p>
<p>The guard shuddered and quickly changed his expression when he heard Kent’s name...</p>
<p>"I am sorry, Mr. Kent. The sect leader is waiting for your arrival."</p>
<p>"Oh." Kent furrowed his brow. ’It seems she couldn’t wait to see me again.’</p>
<p>Kent smiled, feeling good about himself. He thought Lady Azela might be missing him too much that she couldn’t wait for him to return, so she made sure nobody blocked his path when getting back to the sect.</p>
<p>With a path opened for him, Kent entered the sect and, using the Soul Map, he vanished, appearing in Lady Kami’s residence.</p>
<p>The moment he appeared, the gate leading to the mansion opened, and four people rushed out, ready to battle. However, upon seeing who dared intrude, they calmed down...</p>
<p>"Kent, you are back." Lady Azela shouted, almost running to go hug him, but she held herself back.</p>
<p>Kent saw this and smiled. "I am indeed back. Did you miss me?"</p>
<p>None of them minded him.</p>
<p>"Commander Agnes, fancy seeing you here. You too, Yu Lan."</p>
<p>"Great to see you again, Kent," Commander Agnes said.</p>
<p>"Likewise."</p>
<p>After delivering Kent’s message to the mother and her daughter, she decided to stay and, using Yu Lan’s strength, protect them for as long as Kent was away...</p>
<p>She sent words to her superiors that she would be staying in the Heaven’s Eye Sect for a while.</p>
<p>Although they wondered why, having delivered a cure for their strongest general, they allowed her to do as she pleased.</p>
<p>Of course, her status as a princess also contributed to her getting this princess treatment. So she had been with the sect leader and her mother for the past few days.</p>
<p>They entered the mansion, where Kent was bombarded with many questions. After answering the ones he could, he asked how they were...</p>
<p>It was then that they informed him about the Planet King’s offer, which they knew they couldn’t reject, for the lives of the thousands of disciples in their sect would be in danger if they dared to refuse becoming his brides...</p>
<p>However, Kent merely smirked, hearing the worry in their tone...</p>
<p>"No need to worry about that bastard... I will handle him soon enough," Kent said, making them aware of his plans to kill the bastard...</p>
<p>"For now, go about your normal activities. In 3 months’ time, he will be dead, and you will be free to live your life to the fullest."</p>
<p>Kent waved his hand, and two Golden Keys appeared in the air. He channeled his energy into them, and a dragon and a phoenix appeared in their human form.</p>
<p>This startled the four ladies. Even Yu Lan was startled...</p>
<p>"Bring your hands," Kent said, asking for Lady Kami and her daughter’s hands. They obeyed and stretched their hands.</p>
<p>Kent took their blood and dropped it on the keys. Lady Kami’s blood landed on the key for the dragon, and her daughter got the phoenix...</p>
<p>Soon, the contract was formed, and they each received a supreme-grade technique.</p>
<p>"I will be leaving for a month. While I am away, your new best friends will protect you. Of course, if it is beyond them, these guys will handle it."</p>
<p>Kent waved his hand again, and 43 more beasts in their human form appeared.</p>
<p>On his way to Planet Nictus, he thought long and hard about whether he should send all 50 beasts to the Pill Ascension Realm.</p>
<p>However, after a few internal struggles, he decided against it and instead chose to leave them behind. The seven Monarchs were enough for whatever awaited him there.</p>
<p>Now, he knew where to leave them.</p>
<p>"Who are these people?" Lady Kami asked, feeling like a bug before the terrifying beasts standing behind Kent.</p>
<p>"They are my friends," Kent laughed, before adding, "In any case, they will protect you two until I return. For now, relax and live life like there is no tomorrow."</p>
<p>Kent turned toward Commander Agnes. "Bring the general you talked about here 40 days from now. I want to have a meeting with her."</p>
<p>"Okay."</p>
<p>"Where are you going next?" Lady Azela asked, feeling reluctant to part with Kent.</p>
<p>Seeing this, Kent walked toward her and hugged her, pulling her much closer to him, feeling her boobs pressed against his chest.</p>
<p>"My big sister is waiting for me so we can head to the Pill Ascension competition we talked about. Don’t worry, I have people who will protect me."</p>
<p>He planted a kiss on her forehead, causing her to blush.</p>
<p>Kent then moved toward Lady Kami and kissed her forehead, too.</p>
<p>He repeated the same thing with Commander Agnes.</p>
<p>"You ladies should wait for me. When I return, we will spend some quality time together."</p>
<p>They nodded, and after Kent handled some other things, he left.</p>
<p>His next stop was Planet Drakthys to rescue Yuna, someone who would play a big role in his future.</p>
