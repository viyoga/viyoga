<p>Up in the air, the dragons are spraying flames on each other, their roars of pain causing many to break into visible sweat.</p>
<p>The fact that Kent was able to command two dragons to attack each other is already a clear indication that he is much more monstrous than they gave him credit for.</p>
<p>"Unbelievable... How are we going to win against this?" an elder from the first-ranked Celestial Gate Sect said, her expression dull and filled with defeat...</p>
<p>"Even I cannot do what he is doing, how much more the disciples?"</p>
<p>The concept that a disciple can possess such terrifying powers never crossed their minds. To them, there is always a limit to how much a person can grow.</p>
<p>This is a medium world where everything has a limit. Reaching the Core Formation stage and refining your core is the end. This limit prevents people like Kent from appearing in medium worlds...</p>
<p>However, watching him dominate his opponent tells them what they needed to know...</p>
<p>"This competition is already won by the Supreme Immortal Sect... At this rate, even if they combine all seven sects in the quarterfinals, they wouldn’t be able to defeat him."</p>
<p>"What a monster."</p>
<p>"Just where did they get him from?"</p>
<p>"I was told he came from Sonox. I even heard some reports say this year, the number of talents that came from that washup world is just too staggering."</p>
<p>"Looks like the Supreme Immortal Sect is about to rise."</p>
<p>"That is only natural. I mean, aside from this monster, I saw how the rest of their disciples handled their battles. It was like they had seen danger and lived to tell the tale. Their expressions during the battles said it all."</p>
<p>Although Kent will be ascending, he plans to make the sect a powerhouse in the medium worlds. Kent knew he couldn’t go to every last medium world and become its guardian.</p>
<p>That is impossible.</p>
<p>So, he plans to become the guardian of the sect, Sonox, and the Celestial Ascendence Planetary Alliance. Then, using the sect and Sonox as his base, he will leave them powerful treasures and will keep sending them more, since he can come to any medium world he has been to through his tower.</p>
<p>This is to ensure that, even after thousands of years, powerhouses will continue to rise from his protected worlds.</p>
<p>Kent has both a long-term and short-term plan.</p>
<p>The short-term plan is to make the entire Alliance his Soul Essence farm. By making the alliance head his subordinate, all 54 planets in the alliance will become his.</p>
<p>This number will, of course, expand as more worlds join them. His ultimate plan for this short-term plan was to change the planetary alliance (system) into a star system.</p>
<p>As for his long-term plan, he learned it from Vexthra.</p>
<p>He isn’t aiming to reap the benefits now. His aim is to reach godhood and reap his benefits. The power of faith, as Vexthra calls it, is one way to strengthen one’s divinity and make one a powerhouse.</p>
<p>Kent knew his Soul Universe was the path for him to become the strongest there is, so his divinity would be linked to how many worlds he would become a guardian to.</p>
<p>So far, he is getting close to having so much Soul Essence that when he started throwing asteroids at people, he wouldn’t feel the loss.</p>
<p>So he has ambitions, and, as it happens, dominating the Primal Beast Sect is a way to achieve them. Today’s battle will become something to be told for years to come, and Kent is ensuring that everyone understands this.</p>
<p>"Octopuses cringe me up, so why don’t you go let the Manta Rayfolk over there electrocute you?" Kent used his mind manipulation on yet another two beasts.</p>
<p>Soon, he is only under the assault of three beasts—well, one of them has lost a limb, so only two and a half, and soon, it is about to be only two.</p>
<p>Kent evaded an attack from the seahorse and blocked a demi-shark’s attack. Then, stomping on the ground, the two beasts were blasted away, allowing Kent to move and stop before the crab...</p>
<p>"I don’t eat crabs, so know that this torture is merely a way for me to feel good about myself, and wipe away past humiliations. No hard feelings."</p>
<p>"AAaaarrrrgggghhHHH!"</p>
<p>The crab let out another scream as Kent yanked off yet another limb. But he didn’t stop there; he grabbed yet another one and pulled it off before teleporting away.</p>
<p>The seahorse and the shark were upon him, causing Kent to grin and use his mind manipulation ability again...</p>
<p>"You found this shark on top of your woman... As a loving husband, what will you do to him?" The seahorse, which was in the middle of an attack, halted, then turned sharply and glared at the shark.</p>
<p>The poor guy froze. However, Kent had no intention of making him feel relieved.</p>
<p>He placed his hand on the seahorse’s shoulder, almost like they were the best of friends, and whispered into his ear like a witch... "He didn’t even use a condom... can you imagine how that felt for him... and your woman?"</p>
<p>The Seahorse had no idea what a condom was, but he was triggered nonetheless, resulting in a quick and decisive lunge, one that took the shark by surprise.</p>
<p>The two engaged in fierce combat, one that allowed Kent to grin and move to the crab...</p>
<p>"Now that you have three limbs left, how about I take two and let you have one? They will grow back, but in order not to make me an evil person, I cannot in good conscience take all six legs."</p>
<p>Everyone who heard him paled...</p>
<p>Even taking three legs in Kent’s eyes was mercy. To them, that is the most evil thing a person can ever say to their fellow living being.</p>
<p>"Please, I am begging you, don’t hurt me anymore," the crab said, making Kent grin.</p>
<p>"No, no, no, no... We cannot have that. You losers planned to hurt my sect in ways nobody saw coming, so don’t beg me. Instead, beg me to hurt you."</p>
<p>With tears in the crab’s eyes and a smile that didn’t reach his eyes, he spoke... "Please, I want you to hurt me more. I deserve it."</p>
<p>"That is better."</p>
<p>Soon, two more screams came from the crab, who had no idea that the only reason Kent targeted him was that one of Kent’s bullies back on Earth was nicknamed Crab.</p>
<p>The man had a crab tattoo drawn on his back and claimed he was a "crab king."</p>
<p>Kent suffered so many humiliations and beatings from him to the point that he had to hide from him for a whole year just so he could have some semblance of peace.</p>
<p>So if he can’t have his revenge on him, he might as well channel all that hatred to the Crabfolk.</p>
<p>This crab won’t be the last beast to suffer under Kent’s torture. Every other crab out there will one day feel his wrath simply because of a trauma from his past life on Earth, which had followed him to this world.</p>
<p>An hour later, all seven beasts are severely injured, so Kent decided to free them.</p>
<p>"We-we surrender," the seahorse said, looking at Kent with eyes filled with fear and anger.</p>
<p>"That was satisfying," Kent exhaled, his mood brightening up a bit. Every last spectator turned pale.</p>
<p>’Looks like he is finally recovering from his time in the Paragon Universe,’ Vexthra said, and her sisters nodded with a smile... Lady Enzi included.</p>
