<p>"Soul Monarch, doesn’t ring a bell," Kent said, not really bothered by the fact that an unknown person had given his wife such a grand inheritance.</p>
<p>His focus is mostly on the fact that his wife will soon inherit one of the seven grandest legacies in all the Multiverse.</p>
<p>Of course, while he had no idea who the mysterious Soul Monarch was, Elsa seemed to have some idea.</p>
<p>However, judging by the look on her face, it was clear she didn’t know the person, though the name seemed to have triggered something within her.</p>
<p>Maybe it had something to do with her past life.</p>
<p>But since nobody seemed to notice, she didn’t bother to say anything.</p>
<p>Right now, the focus was on Neomi, who was about to embark on a journey to a place she had never expected she would one day go.</p>
<p>She had no need for that legacy in the past, so she never bothered to go after it.</p>
<p>Now, however, she had a reason to go and inherit what she had won with her own two hands.</p>
<p>If she succeeded in acquiring what she had gone there for, there was a chance she would be ready for what Kent had chosen for her to do.</p>
<p>She was only a demigod now, but if she could climb to the rank of a Supreme or Celestial, then there was no way she wouldn’t be ready to face whoever the Blood Race threw at her.</p>
<p>Already, even as a demigod, she was a threat to the Supremes, so when she climbed to that rank, who was to say what would become of her?</p>
<p>There was no telling what would become of her after she inherited the legacy of the Ancient Primal.</p>
<p>Kent knew she had to leave.</p>
<p>However, since she would be going away for that long, he planned to spend some time with her before she left, so she wouldn’t miss him too much.</p>
<p>But before that, he had to assemble a team that would escort her to Hell and, if possible, closer to her destination.</p>
<p>Luckily, he had the perfect candidates.</p>
<p>If Uncle Drew and the rest of the Night Riders were around, he would have asked them to escort her.</p>
<p>But since they are not here, he had to turn to the next capable people he knew would take her to where she wanted to go.</p>
<p>Kent turned to face Grand Shaman Vorgun, who had been living with him since he decided not to head over to the Star Realm and aid Grand Shaman Malak, who was conquering the various levels with the rest of the Evil Legion and the Beast Legion.</p>
<p>He was also turning some of the Star Beasts into Evil Beasts because, at the end of the day, Kent planned to have a dedicated Evil Army that could harness the power of true evil to do his bidding.</p>
<p>"Big brother, I will trouble you to escort Neomi to Hell and, if possible, closer to her destination. While Hell isn’t as dangerous as before, now that a new Hell Monarch will be selected, I don’t want there to be any accidents."</p>
<p>"Rest assured, Kent, I will make sure she gets there in one piece. That said, I have never been to Hell, so we will need a guide."</p>
<p>Kent nodded and snapped his fingers, summoning Hades, who was asleep in a recliner chair... "Wake up, bastard. Why do you always like to sleep?"</p>
<p>Kent called out, his leg moving to give Hades the push he needed to wake up from his slumber.</p>
<p>Hades, who had been wronged, opened his eyes and glared at Kent. However, after noticing everyone was looking at him, he smiled and stood upright... "Well, I didn’t know we were having a family meeting."</p>
<p>His words were ignored by Kent while the rest smiled, causing a smile to appear on his face as well.</p>
<p>"I have a mission for you." Kent turned and looked at Neomi. "You will be escorting Neomi to Hell and perhaps beyond Hell in a couple of weeks’ time. Your job is to lead her through the paths that are less likely to kill her. As for the dangers, leave them to Big Brother Vorgun."</p>
<p>"Easily done. Let me know when she is ready to leave." And with that, Hades vanished back into his soul sea to sleep again.</p>
<p>Kent just smiled but said nothing.</p>
<p>Hades had been with the Chaos Ascension King for years. Kent had only just awakened this aspect of his abilities, so who was to say just what kind of character he had yet to show? For all he knew, Hades was someone who could sleep in the middle of a fight.</p>
<p>’At least he always comes through when I need him the most.’ With that observation, Kent knew he was at least useful, and now that Neomi needed help, he would be more than useful.</p>
<p>As an incarnation of Death’s spirit, he knew when something could kill. He could sense death and knew when he was staring at it, so he would be more than needed for the kind of journey Neomi was about to embark on.</p>
<p>If the first path leads to death, he will know and make sure they avoid it.</p>
<p>After the issue with Neomi was handled, Kent turned to Kaizo. "I would have to refine the materials you have collected for me before I can use them to upgrade you. However, since we are here now, I might as well ensure you either come out as a Supreme or Celestial.</p>
<p>For that to happen, I have to go somewhere first. It will take only a few months, so when I return, I will work diligently to upgrade you."</p>
<p>Kaizo nodded, already pumped for when he would get to face the Blood Race and prove to them why he was known as the Undying Divine Titan.</p>
<p>Kent already had two more candidates to add.</p>
<p>One was Veydris, the leader of the 14 Lost Knights, who was also planning to upgrade after the materials he needed had been refined enough inside his soul universe.</p>
<p>The next person he would be adding to the team was Uncle Drew, whom he knew would return from Hell before the hundred years were up.</p>
<p>Since he had added the Mother of Killers, then he might as well add Kael Duskbane, the greatest warrior to ever walk the face of this universe.</p>
<p>With him added, he would need five more. Kent didn’t know who else qualified to join what he had planned, but when the time came, he knew at least four capable warriors would be going with him.</p>
<p>As to whether he would select more, it would all depend on what he managed to discover within the next 100 years.</p>
