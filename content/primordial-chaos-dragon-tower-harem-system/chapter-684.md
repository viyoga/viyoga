<p>Crack.</p>
<p>Boom.</p>
<p>"Impossible... I don’t believe you are stronger than me... That is impossible," Tiger King Tyron said, rising from the ground with a broken jaw, bleeding nose, dislocated shoulder, and—well—his face was just broken beyond repair.</p>
<p>Yi Lai stood a few meters away from him, looking perfectly fine. She had no blood on her—the blood on her wasn’t hers—but the blood of her opponent, now coating her fist, showing how powerful she had become.</p>
<p>"I don’t believe that in just under two years you have surpassed my thousands of years of effort... I don’t believe it."</p>
<p>Yi Lai chuckled. "Then become a believer, because this is happening."</p>
<p>She moved, and before King Tyron could sense her movement, she was already on him with her fist planted on his jaw. The force behind the punch pushed him back several meters, but Yi Lai was on him the next second.</p>
<p>The battle hadn’t been going on for long, but she had already destroyed King Tyron’s face and arms without much struggle.</p>
<p>Yi Lai was not stronger than King Tyron’s strength-wise, but when it came to speed, she was three, perhaps four times faster.</p>
<p>So she may not be strong, but she made up for the gap in strength with her speed, and since you can only punch what you can keep up with, King Tyron wasn’t having a good day, to say the least.</p>
<p>Yi Lai was having a field day with him, and that alone was showing in the number of bones she was breaking.</p>
<p>Meanwhile, Veera and her undead swordsmen were almost done with the battle. Nethar, Yi Lai’s undead dragon, was enough to handle the weaker tigers. So Veera’s joining was overkill.</p>
<p>But thanks to her joining, things ended much faster. When the battle came to an end, she turned and looked in Yi Lai’s direction. "Well, she is really taking her time to get her pain out... good for her."</p>
<p>Veera smiled and then turned toward Nether, who was just standing there watching Yi Lai trash the Tiger King.</p>
<p>Boom.</p>
<p>Yi Lai sucker-punched the Tiger King, sending him flying back. His body was broken beyond repair, and judging by how long it took him to stand up, this time, she became a believer and knew he was done for.</p>
<p>However, when he saw that all his ambush of Tigers had been killed, his aura took on a deeper and darker turn, transforming into his 25-meter tall True Beast form.</p>
<p>It had red fur, with some metallic coatings around its legs and head. These partially covered his body, serving both as defense and decoration, showcasing his elegance.</p>
<p>But Yi Lai didn’t care about that. Her mission was to make the bastard suffer before dying, and so far, she had succeeded in the suffering part. Now only the dying part remained...</p>
<p>The moment King Tyron became a tiger, he lunged at Yi Lai, who didn’t even flinch. That was because she knew the Tiger couldn’t keep up with her in the first place. So the moment the tiger came closer to her with his claw, she moved and drove her fist into the tiger’s neck.</p>
<p>The punch was powerful enough to send him flying back. She moved and appeared in front of the Tiger, which towered over her.</p>
<p>However, disregarding the tiger’s imposing height, she formed a bolt of dark lightning and struck it. It exploded upon contact, forcing the Tiger to let out a powerful roar and stagger, falling a few seconds later.</p>
<p>Yi Lai smirked and then summoned her sword. She unleashed two powerful sword arcs, striking the thick, incredibly hard, metallic-coated legs of the tiger, rendering him immobile.</p>
<p>When she got closer, she rose slowly into the air and stared down at the tiger. "Not so tough now, are you?"</p>
<p>The tiger roared furiously, trying to muster the courage to stand... but it just wasn’t possible. However, that didn’t stop him from sending a blast of red energy, which Yi Lai evaded effortlessly.</p>
<p>"All the humiliations I suffered at your hands a hundred years ago... I finally got my revenge. So you should die knowing your bad decisions were the cause of your doom.</p>
<p>I will sleep now, knowing you and your kind are no more..."</p>
<p>She raised her sword, and then, up in the air, a bigger version of her sword manifested. It was taller, thinner, and sharper. However, the moment she gestured downward, the sword moved and drew a hole through King Tyron’s head—ending his damn existence for good.</p>
<p>Yi Lai exhaled and then landed softly on the ground.</p>
<p>"Happy now?" Veera appeared the next second and stood beside Yi Lai.</p>
<p>"I guess I am... the years of hatred have been vented... The only ones left are the Oracles and the Hand. But I will deal with the Oracles now and the Hand later."</p>
<p>Veera just shook her head and smiled...</p>
<p>"In any case, I won’t be taking this beast... He was just too weak to be a worthy undead. Although I doubt I will gain much from the ones we will face soon... But at least they will be humanoids."</p>
<p>Yi Lai nodded.</p>
<p>If she had the means to raise undead, she most certainly wouldn’t have raised the tiger as her undead either. It wouldn’t be because she hated him—well, that is part of it—but she most certainly wouldn’t take such a weakling as her undead soldier.</p>
<p>Veera had the means to make the Tiger King stronger over time, but she already had enough weaklings she was nurturing with far superior items than the body of King Tyron.</p>
<p>"Then why don’t we leave for the main mission... I doubt there is something else here for us," Veera said, and Yi Lai nodded.</p>
<p>"Let’s go." She touched Veera’s shoulder, and the next second, they vanished. Nethar had already disappeared into Yi Lai’s body, so they left and appeared somewhere in Vastport City.</p>
<p>The moment they appeared, Yi Lai took out a device and channeled her energy into it... A few seconds later, a red orb appeared from it and started flying away.</p>
<p>"Let’s go." With that, they started moving after the red orb.</p>
