<p>"Weaklings"</p>
<p>Sylara’s claw dug into the throat of one of the minions of the demon god of destruction, tearing and pulling his oesophagus out.</p>
<p>"Maybe you are not weak. Maybe it is your god that is weak."</p>
<p>Sylara planned on battling the three Heavenly Ascension stage warriors, but a couple of seconds into their clash, they sensed that Veydris was dangerous, so they ignored her, making the Vampire demoness pissed.</p>
<p>So tapping into her anger, the Spirit Lords and those below were now at her mercy. They outnumbered her by hundreds, but she wasn’t in any way worried.</p>
<p>Sylara’s nature allows her to become her strongest in the presence of blood.</p>
<p>As long as there is blood, she will become nearly invincible. So with blood flowing all around her, both her speed and strength had been amplified.</p>
<p>And with the Blood Hunger seal burning, she was practically burning her life force for strength.</p>
<p>Naturally, she was now several times stronger, and looking at how fast she was moving, by the time her 12-minute power-up runs out, she will kill her fair share.</p>
<p>Veydris, however, was blocked, but as a metallic warrior, no one could read his expression and know how he was faring.</p>
<p>However, one thing was certain: Veydris was out of his league against the three Heavenly Ascension stage warriors facing him. However, he had created the situation where killing him would take considerable time.</p>
<p>Naturally, none of the demon god’s followers knew what was happening to Neomi, and it had already been four minutes since the start of the battle.</p>
<p>Of course, their god could not tell them anything until after 10 minutes. So despite him sensing the presence of a god, he was helpless at the moment.</p>
<p>Neomi sat, and Velyra stood guard behind her, waiting for her goddess to finish with what she was doing.</p>
<p>In the past, she was the saintess of their tribe. She was the one who saw to it that prayers and sacrifices were offered to their goddess, the mother of killers.</p>
<p>Even now, she still believed in her and would forever believe in her.</p>
<p>So standing guard for her was an honour.</p>
<p>Exactly five minutes into the awakening, Neomi opened her eyes, and a wave of divine energy exploded out of her body.</p>
<p>"Praise to Shara, the mother of killers."</p>
<p>Velyra said this with a respectful look on her face.</p>
<p>"You know you don’t have to do that, right? You are my sister."</p>
<p>"I know." Velyra smiled, "However, I want to. Even though I don’t know how, you have helped me on many occasions in the past, and as such, my faith in you has deepened to the point I cannot change who I am just because we are now sisters."</p>
<p>Neomi felt touched hearing such deep words from her sister. She could tell Velyra had complete trust in her, and that alone opened a feeling she never knew she could have for a female.</p>
<p>She walked close to Velyra and gently kissed her forehead.</p>
<p>When she pulled back, a tattoo of two daggers crossed appeared on Velyra’s forehead. Neomi then pressed her finger on Velyra’s forehead and muttered a couple of words.</p>
<p>"For your trust and belief in me, and your dedication to walking the road of killers, I hereby bestow my power on you. Take it and use it to protect those you care about."</p>
<p>In an instant, Velyra felt like she could crush stars with a mere snap of her fingers.</p>
<p>However, a second later, the feeling reduced to her now feeling like she could kill a Spirit King with a mere slap.</p>
<p>"I have given you 80% of my power, but because it is too much for you, I sealed it. You will have access to it when you reach the Spirit King stage.</p>
<p>Use it whenever you want, but don’t use more than 1% within 48 hours, else you will explode."</p>
<p>"This-" Velyra was in shock, unsure of how to handle her new power-up. Tears fell down her face.</p>
<p>Neomi wiped away her tears, then, planting another kiss on her forehead, before looking toward the battlefield.</p>
<p>"Why don’t we fight together while I am still the Mother of Killers?"</p>
<p>"Mmm," Velyra nodded like a little girl meeting Santa for the first time.</p>
<p>Neomi pulled out her two daggers, and Velyra pulled out her sword and charged toward the battlefield.</p>
<p>"Nicely done, Neomi. Though the price you will be paying now will be much steeper," Vexthra said, but she seemed happy about how Neomi handled Velyra.</p>
<p>The two ladies soon arrived on the battlefield, changing the scene.</p>
<p>"Veydris, get on with your task quickly," Neomi ordered, and Veydris vanished into the void. Neomi took his place.</p>
<p>"Killing Circle." She threw her dagger, which circled around her and the three Heavenly Ascension warriors. A red circle formed, and with that, she was locked in with them, only coming out either when she died or she killed them all.</p>
<p>Velyra, however, moved and was soon in front of the church.</p>
<p>The two Heavenly Ascension stage warriors standing guard moved to step toward her. However, before they could reach her, she activated a movement technique that utilized the power of blood, something abundant on the battlefield.</p>
<p>"Swap."</p>
<p>Velyra vanished, and Sylara took her place. With that, Velyra has finally stepped into the blood, which rose and formed into armor around her.</p>
<p>She and Sylara swapped places, and with that, she willed the blood on the ground to fight for her.</p>
<p>"Blood Bullets."</p>
<p>Screams of pain filled the space as thousands of bullets began drawing holes in the demon god’s followers.</p>
<p>Five minutes later, Vexthra smiled.</p>
<p>"This battle is as good as done." She looked up at the sky as if staring at someone. "Oh, what wouldn’t I give to see the constipated look on Balmos’ face right now."</p>
<p>Veydris entered the church and, while he was instructed to destroy the main statue, he started destroying them all.</p>
<p>Soon, however, he arrived before the main target.</p>
<p>In that moment, thunder rocked the heavens, but before it could descend, Veydris severed the head of the statue, cutting off the lightning.</p>
<p>Balmos was a second too late.</p>
<p>It was a coincidence, but Veydris sure made sure the strike happened when Balmos had hope of turning things around.</p>
<p>With their support cut off, the battle ended a couple of minutes later, with Kaizo landing the last blow.</p>
<p>He was waiting for the moment when Veydris would sever the god’s connection to the church. So when Vexthra gave them the order to attack, he didn’t hesitate.</p>
<p>---</p>
<p>Somewhere in the god realm, a dark-haired, dark-horned, and rather ugly man slammed his fist on a table, causing an entire mountain to shatter into powder.</p>
<p>"Who dares attack and kill my followers? Do they have a death wish?"</p>
<p>Balmos, the demon god of destruction, screamed, his voice spreading over the planet he was in. He waved his hand; a portrait of Neomi appeared.</p>
<p>"Somebody..."</p>
<p>A second didn’t even pass as a figure appeared before him and knelt down.</p>
<p>"I want you to spread this image. Whoever can identify and point us in the right direction will receive 1 million God Crystals and a God-Grade technique of their choice.</p>
<p>Those who can deliver her head will become direct disciples under me for 20,000 years"</p>
<p>"Yes, my lord." The person left, and Balmos continued to fume.</p>
<p>"If I don’t kill you, then I am not Balmos."</p>

<p>Somewhere in the past, Kent, who was flying toward the domain of the Lotus Empress, narrowed his eyes.</p>
<p>’Hmm, some idiot is tired of living, so he wants to die. Well, I will gladly send you packing if you dare lay a finger on what is mine.’</p>
