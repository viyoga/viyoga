<p>Kent’s main objective for facing Grand Elder Ruolan wasn’t to see if he could confront a sovereign.</p>
<p>No, he had already reached a level that gave him the confidence to face even multiple Peak Sovereigns and walk away smiling.</p>
<p>This confidence came from what happened the day before the battle.</p>
<p>When he left for the Pill Master Competition, his ladies gathered and held a meeting. In this meeting, they each shared their views on how best to support their man.</p>
<p>Naturally, it boiled down to sex.</p>
<p>However, Alina raised a point that made them all aware of something.</p>
<p>They realized that while sex allowed their man to grow, he didn’t depend solely on that. The points he gained whenever they leveled up or broke through were equally valuable.</p>
<p>They concluded that, aside from relying on sex to level up, they would actively cultivate too.</p>
<p>Whenever he is not around or there is no stronger woman around to increase Kent’s cultivation base, they will get down to good old traditional cultivation.</p>
<p>So, thanks to the time difference inside the tower, they got down to business.</p>
<p>Only a few days passed for Kent, but for them, months passed, and these months were spent cultivating.</p>
<p>Naturally, their hard work paid off when Unity, Lilian, Elsa, Alina, Val, Ingrid, Neomi, and Selene leveled up.</p>
<p>Val, Ingrid, Neomi and Selene are already Transcendents, so their level-up gave Kent thousands of points.</p>
<p>This gave Kent many points, which he added to his stats.</p>
<p>However, something happened after he had distributed about 70% of the undistributed stats points.</p>
<p>Gaia told him a couple of weeks ago about something called maximization.</p>
<p>It occurs when his stats can’t handle any more points, requiring him to break that limit and add more. The moment he managed to break that limit, his strength would enter a new phase he couldn’t comprehend.</p>
<p>So when that happened, Kent entered the Body Tower.</p>
<p>It was a choice between cultivating his soul or body.</p>
<p>Naturally, it had been a long time since he entered the Body Tower, considering that after his sword body and body cultivation had merged, he attained a Silver Body.</p>
<p>This meant that to break the limit, he had to become a Gold Body cultivator.</p>
<p>What he never expected was what happened after that.</p>
<p>Thanks to reaching the maximisation level, his strength was on a new plane. This gave him the confidence to climb the stairway to levels he never thought possible.</p>
<p>When he received the Golden Titan Stairway, Gaia told him that it was divided into seven segments, each containing about 300 steps.</p>
<p>She also told him that after he had climbed 300 steps, he would receive a mystical-grade tempering treasure.</p>
<p>But while that was the main goal, for every 100 steps climbed, he would receive a Heaven-grade tempering treasure. There are some other technicalities, but so far, these two are the main ones.</p>
<p>Naturally, Kent didn’t dwell on that too much.</p>
<p>However, when he reached the first 100 steps, he was given a liquid treasure called Bone Tempering Elixir. Kent didn’t pay much attention to it.</p>
<p>But the moment he drank it, the outcome became clear. His bones were tempered, and he could tell something extraordinary had happened when it was done.</p>
<p>He felt like his bones had transcended a limit he wasn’t aware of.</p>
<p>Gaia only giggled when he asked her, so Kent continued to climb and soon reached the 200th step.</p>
<p>If only he knew the Elixir he had just drank was something that started turning his bones into something he would only appreciate when he reached the Immortal Body stage.</p>
<p>When he reached the 200th step, he received the Bone Tempering Elixir again.</p>
<p>Naturally, he didn’t expect anything new, considering the first and second grades were the same. But after drinking it, he realized the Golden Titan Stairway was a gold mine.</p>
<p>His strength kept rising to the point he felt that a punch from Evil Bat, from when he and Kaizo faced him, wouldn’t do any damage anymore.</p>
<p>He also felt his speed enhanced, meaning he had entered a new phase in the path of body cultivation.</p>
<p>This was why he tried so hard to keep going, and after five days inside the tower, he barely made it to the 300th step.</p>
<p>But he did, and when his reward came and he appraised it, he nearly dropped it.</p>
<p>The treasure wasn’t one he saw coming, and he didn’t expect it at this early stage. In his memories as Jest, he once went to an auction where it was auctioned.</p>
<p>It was a great bidding war that went outside the auction house, causing the death of hundreds.</p>
<p>It was called the Body Transcendence Pill, which is of great value to any cultivator, but it’s even more valuable to a body cultivator.</p>
<p>Of course, Kent expected many things but never anticipated getting a Tier 9 perfect pill after climbing the 300th step.</p>
<p>He took it eagerly and continued to absorb it for another five days until his breakthrough came on the 11th day after entering the Body Tower.</p>
<p>He reached the Gold Body stage, breaking his limit and allowing him to allocate the rest of his points.</p>
<p>In fact, after becoming a Gold Body cultivator, his Sword Body also advanced, which enhanced his swordsmanship.</p>
<p>Kent never expected that.</p>
<p>Even Gaia, who created the Tower, didn’t anticipate it. It was a shock to both of them.</p>
<p>Thankfully, the brilliant Dori managed to deduce the reason.</p>
<p>She mainly attributed it to a mutation. Body Cultivation, Soul Cultivation, and Qi Cultivation aren’t typically practiced by a single person.</p>
<p>In fact, it’s rare. But Kent wasn’t the first to tread this path.</p>
<p>However, what made this special was the Sword Body he had integrated.</p>
<p>This created something akin to a channel now open to other aspects, like the Soul Body, the Perfect Qi Body, and others he will get to discover on his path to the peak.</p>
<p>His Sword Body ensured that his breakthrough influenced his other aspects whenever he achieved a breakthrough in Body Cultivation.</p>
<p>Thus, his soul, swordsmanship, and even his Qi were affected when he became a Gold Body cultivator.</p>
<p>Kent didn’t realize just how formidable he had become. He gained so much that he never knew a Peak Sovereign wouldn’t scare him anymore.</p>
<p>Of course, he realised that when he faced the Peak Sovereign. It was then he understood he had surpassed that stage.</p>
<p>He only fought the Sovereign to gauge how powerful he had become, and truthfully, he was astonished.</p>
<p>Now, watching his ladies and the undead battle his enemies, he couldn’t help but wonder what had transpired during those few days in the Body Tower.</p>
<p>"If this continues for another maximization, not even the Forest Kings could stand against me. This makes me want to fight one now."</p>
<p>Kent laughed as he saw Mara sever Pill Saint Barton’s head. It was a clean cut that made him smile through his laughter.</p>
<p>The ladies are each killing their fair share of their foes, making Kent smile while watching them. This harem is rather wield, considering his ladies are bloodthirsty fiends who didn’t even bat an eye killing their fellow humans.</p>
<p>But who could blame them? Between their man and these people, not even a god could save them.</p>
<p>But then another attack struck the formation, making Kent smirk.</p>
