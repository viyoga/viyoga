<p>Grand Shaman Vorgun surely deserved first place, considering the kind of assistance he had offered Kent and the legion during the Dark War.</p>
<p>Not only did he single-handedly create over a thousand Soul Fiends, but he was also able to upgrade each one to the peak of the Celestial stage, something that allowed them to handle more of the pressure, allowing the rest of his teammates to have it easy.</p>
<p>So, having the most points surely didn’t come as a shock to anyone.</p>
<p>The second with the most points in the legion, of course, was Dia again. Her points in the harem ranking were enough to win her a spot in the legion ranking, too.</p>
<p>Then came third, and this time it was Shade King Nykali who took that spot.</p>
<p>Just like Grand Shaman Vorgun, he also possesses the ability to weaponize souls. His ability allows him to turn ordinary souls into soul wraiths, or, as he prefers to call them, Shades.</p>
<p>So he used it well during the Dark War, and aside from merely ranking third, he also managed to upgrade his Shades to a much higher grade.</p>
<p>The fourth spot was taken by Veera, whose undead warriors had proven to be rather resilient. They gave her an edge, and that alone allowed her to rank that high.</p>
<p>Thankfully, Adina came fifth, and the remaining spots were filled by Zaila, Elsa, and the rest.</p>
<p>"You all did great. Personally, I am not sure my points will rank even in the top thirty, so you all should be proud," Kent said to his legion, causing them all to smile and nod.</p>
<p>"Tower will take you to the place to pick the rewards I have prepared. Afterwards, after I am done handling some business, we will plan how to handle the missions ahead of us."</p>
<p>Kent knew better, so he turned and looked at Kaizo. "Of course, if you have nothing to do, use the time to clear the rest of the dungeons. We can’t keep the other members of the Eternal Wrath Legion waiting."</p>
<p>They soon dispersed, and Kent decided to meet with the undead legion to have a much-needed catch-up, considering he had sent them on a mission.</p>
<p>However, before the meeting could start, Kent noticed something peculiar.</p>
<p>He paused before he could say what he was about to say and scanned the faces of the people around him. One face stood out, and this time he noticed it.</p>
<p>For a moment, no words were spoken.</p>
<p>Then Kent smiled and stepped forward. "I honestly don’t understand why you chose to wear a disguise before me, Dominique."</p>
<p>The Third Undead Princess, who perhaps wished to check whether Kent could see through her disguise, smiled and removed it, revealing her pale face, a look that showed she was indeed an undead.</p>
<p>"One would never know until they try." She walked closer to Kent and hugged him. "Nice to see you again, Sanders. Or do you prefer I call you Kent instead?"</p>
<p>"Whichever works for you," Kent hugged her back...</p>
<p>Kaizo and his pals knew the two needed some alone time, so they moved back.</p>
<p>After hugging for a few minutes, Kent pulled back and looked at Dominique. "The last time we saw each other, you tried to kill me."</p>
<p>Dominique blushed, but she nodded. "I did try, didn’t I?"</p>
<p>Kent just smirked, knowing she didn’t truly mean to kill him. If she had meant her attacks, perhaps things would have ended much more chaotically.</p>
<p>Kent took her hand and walked to a bench under a tree somewhere in the Legion Tower. His wives had noticed the presence of another powerhouse, so he could already sense their gazes on them.</p>
<p>However, since he held her hand, none of them tried to approach them, fearing that something might happen to Kent. But then again, who were they kidding to even think someone inside the tower could harm Kent?</p>
<p>"How have you been, Dominique?"</p>
<p>"What can I say? After you left, I went into seclusion to recover my strength and increase my cultivation. I spent millions of years doing that. Afterwards, I spent the rest of the time working around the clock to increase my summons."</p>
<p>She looked around for a few seconds before snapping her fingers, causing five portals to open—four purple portals and one red portal.</p>
<p>Kent furrowed his brow when the portals opened. He knew what was coming, but still, he was stunned when the portals opened.</p>
<p>From the four purple portals, four Pseudo-Primordials, each powerful enough to shake the heart of an army, walked out.</p>
<p>Kent recognized one of them.</p>
<p>"White Lady," he said, smiling at the lady clad in white armor.</p>
<p>She scanned Kent with a hateful gaze before turning to stare at Sylara, who held a smug expression on her face.</p>
<p>"I see she still hasn’t forgotten about what happened back then," Kent said, referring to the White Lady, who still seemed pissed.</p>
<p>"Just give her some time. I am sure she will come around."</p>
<p>Kent just laughed and looked at the three other Pseudo-Primordials. All of them are what the undead call Ancients. They are a special kind of undead, possessing powers far more dreadful than those of regular undead.</p>
<p>They first appeared when Sanders went to the Undead Realm. In fact, Kaizo is the first-ever Ancient, for he was the first-ever undead Kent had created.</p>
<p>Later, he got to know that the Ancients came from a realm unknown even to him. Long story short, he managed to find a way to summon them.</p>
<p>But since he had no use for that method back then, people like the Third Undead Princess surely could benefit from it, so he gave it to her and a few others.</p>
<p>She summoned the White Lady, and back when she sided with her father to attack him, the White Lady fought Sylara. Safe to say she was defeated, and that defeat had followed her ever since then.</p>
<p>Of course, Kent knew how to solve their rivalry, but for now, his focus turned to the red portal.</p>
<p>From within stepped a figure who possessed no aura, yet as she walked out, everyone inside the Legion Tower stopped whatever they were doing and turned in her direction.</p>
<p>"Oh damn... she’s hot." Kent blurted out, absentmindedly</p>
