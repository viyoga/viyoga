<p>BOOM</p>
<p>A wave of terrifying Supreme Qi burst out of Inaya’s body for the 10th time, showing she had already reached the 10th layer. The first layer was reached 5 minutes ago, yet in no time, she had already reached the 10th layer.</p>
<p>"What a monster," Vexthra muttered, a smile playing on her lips.</p>
<p>She had lived a bitter life for most of her existence. All she knew was anger, hatred, and resentment. She had never once smiled.</p>
<p>But that all changed when she met Kent and her sisters. Now, she only wanted one thing, and that was to protect the happiness she had gained. She wanted her sisters to become strong enough to protect their man, no matter the danger.</p>
<p>As long as Kent lives, she will never run out of happiness.</p>
<p>So, watching Inaya ascend from one layer to another, she could only smile inwardly and wish she could go further.</p>
<p>Han and Baldy wore smiles as they watched with vigilant eyes. They could not slack off right now. Just then, an 11th explosion of Supreme Qi came from Inaya, signalling her ascension to the 11th layer.</p>
<p>"Hahahahahaha." Just then, a Celestial lady clad in a flowing white dress with blue patterns embroidered into it appeared in the air and laughed.</p>
<p>"For billions of years, we, the descendants of the Kazuto Great Family, have searched for the Ten Emotion Sword Maiden to pay her back for killing our ancestors and elders. We searched far and wide, but—"</p>
<p>"Excuse me, lady, can you skip to the part where you make your threat and we kill you?" Baldy interrupted her, causing a furious expression to appear on her beautiful face.</p>
<p>"You hairless maggot, do you think you can protect her from us?" Just as she said that, a portal opened ahead, and powerful auras descended from it, filling the place.</p>
<p>"We have been waiting for that bastard who calls himself Lord Genesis or this bitch to appear. Today, she dies by our hands."</p>
<p>"Is that so?" Han spoke calmly and walked forward. "All I see are bugs sent to the slaughter. A single sword move is all I need to wipe you out from the face of this universe."</p>
<p>Just then, Inaya ascended to the 12th layer.</p>
<p>"Why waste time when she is already vulnerable?" a voice came from far away. A second later, the void opened, and a man clad in red-and-blue armless armour stepped out, his hands crossed over his chest.</p>
<p>Han narrowed his eyes at this person, sensing danger. The new arrival reeked of blood and death, a feeling that caused many to take several steps back.</p>
<p>"It would be in your best interest not to block my path since I intend to kill the bitch for what she did to my family. I will not leave until she dies, so step aside and let me kill her."</p>
<p>Han’s eyes narrowed. ’Baldy, protect Big Sister with all you’ve got. It will take a while to kill this person,’ he transmitted to Baldy before turning to face the red-armoured Celestial.</p>
<p>"I am afraid I cannot do that."</p>
<p>"Then die first." The Celestial moved, and in no time, he and Han vanished into the void. A battle of that magnitude cannot be fought on a planet like this.</p>
<p>Just one strike would severely damage the structure of the planet.</p>
<p>Baldy now faced against 10 Celestials, turned and looked at Vexthra. "Don’t worry about me. Just make sure nobody gets close to Big Sister."</p>
<p>"I will." Vexthra had already summoned her bow and was ready to burn her own cultivation to protect her sister.</p>
<p>"Well then, shall we?" Baldy stomped on the ground, and all ten Celestials, including himself, also vanished into the void.</p>
<p>The moment they left, Vexthra sensed people moving toward them, some wearing malicious grins.</p>
<p>"What can a mere God do against a Supreme such as myself?" a man holding a wooden staff said, pointing it at Vexthra.</p>
<p>Five others stood behind him, their presence radiating killing intent.</p>
<p>Vexthra sighed, then she ignited her Laws: Hatred and Resentment.</p>
<p>When the aura of her laws swept across everyone, they all frowned, some instinctively taking steps back.</p>
<p>"Big Sister Inaya had offended all of you eras ago, so I trust it has been several billions of years piling your hatred and resentment toward her." A rare smile appeared on Vexthra’s face as she manifested an arrow.</p>
<p>"Too bad you all are now in the presence of the Goddess of Hatred and Resentment." Just as she said that, a powerful aura of hatred and resentment exploded from her body, forcing many to clutch their heads.</p>
<p>"Give me your hatred." Her arrow flew, and a Supreme closer to her was killed, her arrow and his hatred contributing to his own death.</p>
<p>Vexthra did not even allow anyone to charge before she started firing arrow after arrow at the remaining Supremes. With so much hatred in the air, she felt like a drunkard.</p>
<p>The Six Ancient Primordial Beasts also stood guard, making sure nobody, absolutely nobody, sneaked up on their master, who had already reached the 15th layer.</p>
<p>Just like that, chaos broke out on planet Brymrr, causing the entire planet to tremble and forcing many citizens to take cover, fearing their deaths were already upon them.</p>
<p>While this was happening, Inaya’s Supreme soul body stood on the 16th layer, a beast she had not seen before, charging toward her and radiating intense Supreme Dao energy.</p>
<p>The emotion of hatred ignited, and she also charged forward, meeting the beast in a deadly collision of Supreme Qi empowered by the energy of the Sword and Emotions Dao.</p>
<p>A few seconds later, Inaya found an opening, and before the beast could make a move on her, her sword sliced through its neck, cutting its head off.</p>
<p>The beast transformed into an orb and flew into Inaya’s body. On the outside, she released the 16th aura, showing she had reached the 16th layer.</p>
<p>Her Supreme soul body vanished, then reappeared on the 17th layer. She faced off against another beast. The 18th and 19th were the same. She met beasts that could destroy entire planets in mere seconds.</p>
<p>However, it took her less than a minute to cut them all down.</p>
<p>When the 19th beast was defeated, she sighed and muttered, "I must hurry. My enemies are already on their way, and so I cannot be here for long, else my sisters and friends would be in danger."</p>
<p>She gritted her teeth, and then her body was lifted to the 20th layer. This time, when she appeared, her opponent was a humanoid, an Elf holding a bow.</p>
<p>"Lower life form, do you dare take an arrow from me?"</p>
<p>"Low life form?" Inaya furrowed her brow. "Bitch, we both are Elves, so who are you calling a lower life form?"</p>
<p>The lady with the bow smirked. "A half-elf is no Elf in my eyes."</p>
<p>"Then allow this half-elf to kill you since she is in a hurry." Inaya turned into a blur, and the Elf lady frowned. The next second, her bow moved to her left, meeting a powerful swing from Inaya.</p>
<p>Clang.</p>
<p>Their weapons met, and she grinned. However, her eyes rolled back, showing the white part, when a powerful kick from the sole of Inaya’s boot landed on her face.</p>
<p>The kick rattled her head, and using that, Inaya went for the kill.</p>
<p>"A half-elf just killed you."</p>
<p>Inaya’s sword moved, and the Elf lost her head. She had ascended to the 21st layer.</p>
