<p>[Inside the Yin Pool]</p>
<p>"He still hasn’t added her," Unity said telepathically, looking at the two naked women beside him. Both Lilian and Elsa nodded since they hadn’t felt any connection form between them and Saintess Selene yet.</p>
<p>"Maybe he’s taking his time... She is his Master, after all. We all know he really loves her," Vexthra, who was also in the group chat, commented, lying on her bed in the God Realm.</p>
<p>"So how should we handle this?" Lilian asked. "You know, the moment she joins the harem, how should we handle it?"</p>
<p>"Obviously, Kent would ask us to let Sister Vexthra talk to her first, like he did with us all. So we have to allow her to have her time with our goddess sister. After that, we will have our time with her," Elsa said with a smile.</p>
<p>Just like Lilian and Unity, she also spent some time with Vexthra and received a technique that would allow her to strengthen her cultivation base and foundation.</p>
<p>Obviously, this was something he learned from the many books she read on the Astra Sentinel.</p>
<p>"How about we all join in on the talk this time? This way, we can all know more about each other and how best to help our man," Vexthra proposed, and the ladies accepted.</p>
<p>"Then it is decided. From now on, whenever a new sister joins the harem, we all welcome her at the same time. This will help strengthen our bonds and find ways to help our man.</p>
<p>This is especially true since I can’t help him actively as you ladies can," Vexthra sighed.</p>
<p>"You don’t have to say that, big sister. We are all growing stronger thanks to your selfless help. We cannot repay you, so in return, we will do our best to help Kent, which will allow him to visit you more often," Unity said, bringing a small smile to Vexthra’s face.</p>
<p>Being a goddess has its upsides and downsides.</p>
<p>"Then I will do my best to ensure you ladies grow. But don’t forget our motto..."</p>
<p>""If she is beautiful and a good person, drag her into the harem.""</p>
<p>The four ladies said this with bashful expressions. They had started this practice when Saintess Selene came to them for advice. Essentially, they would be more involved in recruiting their "sisters," since Kent was being selfish.</p>
<p>The more ladies he brought into his harem, the stronger he would grow. Vexthra had told them that Kent was no ordinary person, so the ladies had now formed a recruiting team to seek out beautiful and virtuous women.</p>
<p>Meanwhile, atop the large bed, Saintess Selene had just discovered that her one and only disciple had offended the strongest and most dangerous Alchemist on the human continent.</p>
<p>As if that weren’t enough, he was planning to kill her. Of course, in some strange and foolish way, Selene believed and trusted that Kent would actually be able to accomplish this. However, that was precisely the problem.</p>
<p>If he really went through with it and killed her, he wouldn’t be able to live in peace on this continent. Pill Saints were the backbone of the kingdoms, and killing one was a grave offence. In fact, powerful cultivators would band together to hunt him down.</p>
<p>"Fine. I will only teach her a good lesson. Beyond that, I won’t do anything that would incur the wrath of others," Selene finally decided.</p>
<p>He might be strong and resourceful, but he wasn’t invincible.</p>
<p>"Then why don’t we get down to business? I have to make you my woman officially, you know," Kent said, cupping her cheeks.</p>
<p>She blushed and then made a selfish request that made Kent smile.</p>
<p>"I already told you I have a friend, right?" she asked, and Kent nodded. "In reality, I see her more like my sister—my big sister—since she’s older than me and also stronger than me."</p>
<p>"I see. What about her, then?" Kent asked.</p>
<p>"I want you to make her your woman too. I don’t want her to keep living that scared life, and since you’re someone who grows through sex, having a Level 3 Root Great Sage as your woman can go a long way, right?"</p>
<p>Her face was pink as she made the request. She was clearly embarrassed, considering she hadn’t officially become his woman yet and was already making requests.</p>
<p>"You don’t have to say anything. I was already planning to go after her the moment we get to the academy. It’s always good to have both sisters in your bed."</p>
<p>Kent laughed.</p>
<p>"Now, before I make you my woman, there is something you should know. It’s also something that will change your life, so keep an open mind."</p>
<p>Selene nodded.</p>
<p>"First off, I have four official wives."</p>
<p>This statement made her eyes widen as she glared at him, clearly asking, ’Really?’</p>
<p>"Aside from Unity, Lilian, and Elsa, I have another, but she’s not in this world."</p>
<p>"What do you mean, not in this world?" Selene asked. She was starting to grow sceptical, just like any of his women, when he first told them about Vexthra.</p>
<p>"She’s a goddess, so she’s in the God Realm. I know it sounds far-fetched, but you have to believe me. She’s a goddess named Vexthra and my first wife."</p>
<p>"I’m telling you this because some things will change the moment I add you to the harem. Of course, you can decline, and I will still treat you as my master and wouldn’t hate you."</p>
<p>Saintess Selene didn’t immediately say anything. She pondered the sudden revelation and carefully processed what Kent had just said.</p>
<p>"Do any of your women know about this?" she asked.</p>
<p>"Yes. In fact, the moment I add you to my harem, you’ll be able to talk to her telepathically. But that’s not all. She’ll also be able to do some things for you. And since you are my master, and I trust you, know that these changes will change your life."</p>
<p>Kent looked at her for a few seconds and then said the magic words.</p>
<p>"So tell me, Master, do you want to become my woman and forever join my harem as my woman and only mine?"</p>
<p>"Yes. But I am still your master, and if one day you get a new master, know that I will always be your first master," she pouted while saying that.</p>
<p>Kent just smiled and tapped into the connection with Vexthra. The moment his voice entered her head, she smiled and informed the rest of the ladies.</p>
<p>[My goddess, a new sister is joining the harem. You already know the drill.]</p>
<p>"Tsk... That’s all you know. You should start paying me for my services," Vexthra pouted.</p>
<p>[It’s not my fault you’re a goddess, so stop complaining and do your job,] Kent smiled, making Saintess Selene suspect something was happening behind her back.</p>
<p>She wanted in on that.</p>
<p>"You’d better come visit me soon, or else I’ll break some rules and come there myself," Vexthra smiled, knowing full well she wouldn’t lightly do that unless she wanted the wrath of the universal laws.</p>
<p>[Don’t worry, you’ll be descending soon. For now, just give me two months tops.]</p>
<p>"Okay, my love. Now, hurry up and add my new sister. I can’t wait to turn her into a monster with the sword."</p>
<p>Kent smiled and gave Gaia the go-ahead. Immediately, he felt the connection form between him and his master.</p>
<p>Saintess Selene also sensed that, but then she froze when a voice entered her head.</p>
<p>[Welcome to the harem, Saintess Selene... I am Vexthra, the goddess of hatred and resentment.]</p>
