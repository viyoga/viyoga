<p>"How... How did you get back here?" Sylvara asked, her chest rising and falling as she stared at the cold, looking eyes aimed at her.</p>
<p>Zeroth and the two Kings aimed their weapons at Kent but did not attack.</p>
<p>However, just like their Monarch, they were taken aback by how suddenly Kent appeared before them.</p>
<p>"That is not important," Kent said. "The most important thing should be that you repeat what you just said."</p>
<p>"I didn’t say anything," Sylvara said, causing Kent to narrow his cold gaze at her.</p>
<p>"I heard you say the Planet King has the blood of the forsaken race flowing through him. Tell me I heard wrong."</p>
<p>"How did you hear that? You left, and I checked the 70,000 km radius before speaking, so how did you hear me?" Sylvara was in shock, mainly because Kent had heard her, which shouldn’t have been possible.</p>
<p>She made sure Kent was gone before she spoke. However, the fact that Kent heard her not only shocked her, but it also pained her.</p>
<p>She was hoping Kent would die by facing the Planet King. That would be ideal, knowing his death would make her happy for the loss she had sustained in their clash that lasted only a couple of minutes.</p>
<p>However, that wouldn’t be the case anymore since the brat was standing before her, asking her questions about what she said to only her subordinates.</p>
<p>"Lady, there are many things in this universe that merely staying inside this planet will deprive you of the chance of learning about.</p>
<p>I am one of these things, and now, you will answer my questions."</p>
<p>"You can kill me because I won’t speak."</p>
<p>"Oh, you will," Kent smirked, and then his eyes turned reddish gold.</p>
<p>"I believe you will speak, lady, because I will command you to." Kent smiled, and then he tapped into his bloodline power.</p>
<p>’I hope this works, else I will be so embarrassed.’</p>
<p>"I command you to speak not out of duress, but out of your obedience to your saviour, the Primordial Chaos Dragon."</p>
<p>Sylvara’s body stiffened when Kent’s words entered her ears.</p>
<p>Kent smiled and then deactivated his bloodline power. The moment his eyes turned back to purple, Sylvara dropped to her knees, breaking into a cold sweat.</p>
<p>This sight caught her subordinates off guard, but Kent didn’t mind them. His focus was on what she did next.</p>
<p>Sylvara raised her head and locked gazes with Kent. A few minutes later, she frowned.</p>
<p>"You are still alive," she muttered, causing Kent to look at her weirdly.</p>
<p>"Of course I am alive..." He didn’t understand why she asked that, but Kent brushed it off and asked,</p>
<p>"What, are you wishing for my death?"</p>
<p>"No, but invoking the name of the Primordial Chaos Dragon is a death sentence. You should be dead by now."</p>
<p>"Ha," Kent laughed. "Are you serious?"</p>
<p>"Yes, I am. You should be dead now. Nobody dares invoke the name of the Primordial Chaos Dragon. The rules of the heavens prevent anyone from using the name of the Son of the Universe.</p>
<p>Unless you have the permission of the Primordial Chaos Dragon, using his name is a big NO. You should be dead."</p>
<p>Kent continued to laugh, causing Sylvara to frown. Of course, it was funny, and as someone who knew this was his chance to get what he wanted, Kent first wanted to get the laughter out of the way.</p>
<p>"Young lady, I don’t need anyone’s permission to invoke my own name. Or are you telling me I must first ask the permission of the heavens to use my own name?"</p>
<p>"Impossible. You can’t be him."</p>
<p>"Believe what you can, in the end, I am the Primordial Chaos Dragon, and there is nothing you can do about it.</p>
<p>Of course, if you were to tell me what I needed to know, I might grant you the permission to use my name once."</p>
<p>Kent never knew he would be meeting a fortune like this just when he thought he had hit a roadblock.</p>
<p>Who would have expected the one person proving stubborn would now appear docile because she recognised his greatness... well, she knew of his past self as the Primordial Chaos Dragon.</p>
<p>Of course, Sylvara was proving to be a nuisance, and that alone was more than enough for Kent to finally put her in her place.</p>
<p>"Ember, I give you permission to invoke the name of the Primordial Chaos Dragon. Now, use that power to order Zeroth to go down on his knees."</p>
<p>Ember, who was standing behind Kent, nodded and turned to his former master and King.</p>
<p>"I command you in the name of the Primordial Chaos Dragon to go down on your knees."</p>
<p>The heavens shook, and the entire valley felt a mighty pressure descend on everyone. They all turned pale from the mere danger they each felt in that moment.</p>
<p>Zeroth fell to his knees, and even his hands were touching the ground.</p>
<p>Kent waited for a minute, and then he snapped his finger. "Now, do you believe me?"</p>
<p>Sylvara was pale, shock written all over her face.</p>
<p>She went down on her knees and bowed toward Kent. "I am sorry, my lord. I failed to recognise your greatness and even dared to insult your magnificence. Please punish this rude servant."</p>
<p>The three Kings, Niraya, and all the beasts still alive went down on their knees, their foreheads touching the ground.</p>
<p>If this sight was compared to the scene several minutes ago, one wouldn’t believe their eyes. Things had changed, and this change was one that even shocked Kent.</p>
<p>However, in a twisted kind of way, Kent felt his bloodline urge him to punish Sylvara.</p>
<p>He smiled and walked toward her. "Stand up."</p>
<p>She obeyed and stood up. Kent eyed her from head to toe. When his eyes passed her waist area, he stopped on her heavy hips, which, even from standing before him, showed she had a heavy backside.</p>
<p>Kent saw her ass wiggle during their battle; however, back then, he was Chaos, not Kent, so he paid no attention to it. However, now, in his true self as Kent, his mischievous side appeared.</p>
<p>Kent turned to Ravhen. "How many Soul Flame Fruits are mature enough for harvesting?"</p>
<p>Ravhen looked at the Monarch, who looked at him with a cold gaze. It was as if she meant to tell him, answer him before I gut you.</p>
<p>"There are 12, my lord."</p>
<p>"Kindly take Ember with you back to your domain, pluck all 12 safely, and give them to him."</p>
<p>"I will do that, my lord."</p>
<p>"Great." Kent laughed and then turned to Zeroth. "How about you, buddy? Do you have something worth my time?"</p>
<p>"I do, my lord," Zeroth replied. "I have a 50,000-year-old Nether Abyss Fruit."</p>
<p>’Would you look at that. I now have one of the ingredients required to make the Limit Breaker Pill,’ Kent smiled.</p>
<p>"Good, harvest it and wait for me in your domain. I will come for it."</p>
<p>"Okay, my lord." Zeroth had become so obedient that one would ask if he was the same person who wanted to kill Kent for killing his subordinates.</p>
<p>Of course, he was still angry, but he dared not do anything reckless, considering even the Monarch was being respectful...</p>
<p>"What about you, Iskar?"</p>
<p>"I don’t have any fruit treasure; however, I have a drop of the Nether Green Flame Essence."</p>
<p>"Are you sure..." Kent’s smile bloomed.</p>
<p>’Looks like I will be forming my core before leaving this place.’</p>
