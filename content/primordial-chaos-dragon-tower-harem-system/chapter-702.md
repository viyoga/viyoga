<p>"Fuck, this is not how I expected my day to go," Kent muttered, looking around him. Bodies of beasts he had slain lay strewn on the ground.</p>
<p>He had just stepped into the inner section of the Flame Valley, yet the danger he found himself in wasn’t one he expected. Of course, he dealt with it, but the energy expended in the battles was much more than he used for the other flame beast he killed in the outer section.</p>
<p>The last beast he took down was the same as the first beast he killed when he stepped into the Flame Valley. But this one had already reached the Core Formation and had refined its core three times.</p>
<p>Of course, its bloodline remained unawakened, but it was already too dangerous to be underestimated. So Kent took the battle seriously.</p>
<p>Thankfully, he wasn’t some weak, overconfident brat who chose to throw his life away. Kent has the strength to back up his arrogance, so he used it to continue moving forward.</p>
<p>Still, he hadn’t yet found a single beast with flame spirits. But regardless of that setback, he was moving, and slowly he neared the edge of the Core area.</p>
<p>His resistance was still coming along, but not enough to take him to the first stage in Flame Resistance cultivation.</p>
<p>Thankfully, the heat was even more scorching in the inner section, and as he neared the core, he could sense that he was about to enter a different level of hotness.</p>
<p>"Wow, damn..." Kent moved to his right and rolled on the ground, evading a piercing attack. The attack struck the ground, creating a loud explosion that sent a shockwave in every direction.</p>
<p>Before Kent could recover, another attack came from an unknown direction. It just zipped through the air, aiming to pierce a hole through Kent’s forehead.</p>
<p>However, he dodged again and spread his senses. That was when Kent saw who was attacking him...</p>
<p>"A beetle."</p>
<p>It was a large beetle with iridescent fiery wings and a glowing horn. The horns mounted on its head were where the attacks came from, and judging by how fast the attacks were coming, it was clear Kent had met a real challenger this time.</p>
<p>He quickly scanned it...</p>
<p>⟦Name: Fire Seraphim Beetle⟧</p>
<p>⟦Bloodline: None⟧</p>
<p>⟦Realm: Core Formation (6/7)⟧</p>
<p>⟦Flame Spirit: Blaze Wisp⟧</p>
<p>"Interesting... I will be getting my first flame spirit," Kent smiled cheerfully as he prepared for battle.</p>
<p>Kent retrieved his sword, and sword qi swirled around it while the air was thick with sword intent. Kent knew he had found himself in a sticky one.</p>
<p>Right when his sword appeared, a thick shade fell on Kent. Up ahead, a giant beetle with 20-meter-wide wings appeared, lunging at Kent.</p>
<p>Kent quickly distanced himself by blinking away. However, the beetle didn’t abandon the attack. Instead, it landed heavily on the ground, causing it to shake.</p>
<p>Then, from the shock, a powerful explosion erupted from the ground, shooting lava at Kent.</p>
<p>Sword qi surged from Kent’s blade, cutting through the thick, scorching lava as he retaliated with a swift slash aimed at the beetle’s wings.</p>
<p>The wings were large, so it was an obvious target. However, Kent was shocked when his attack was evaded.</p>
<p>The beetle twisted midair, narrowly avoiding the blow, but Kent saw a faint scorch mark trail behind. The beetle screeched—a terrifying sound that echoed through the valley—before charging again with relentless fury.</p>
<p>Kent leapt backward, narrowly escaping a deadly thrust. The ground beneath him cracked and smoldered from the heat of the attack.</p>
<p>Landing on the ground, Kent unleashed a ’Doom Shard’ from the ground, sending it through the wings of the beetle. This attack worked, causing the creature to falter midair.</p>
<p>However, to Kent’s shock, his next attack struck the beetle, but it dispersed into nothingness.</p>
<p>’Illusion.’ Kent wondered, watching his Doom shard move through the air before it vanished.</p>
<p>Of course, it wasn’t a real illusion per se, but the beetle was able to use visual distortions to deceive Kent...</p>
<p>The attack struck an illusion while the main beetle was already upon Kent. He used ’Blink’ to distance himself, buying a second to ponder what had just happened.</p>
<p>"I have enough mental strength to see through illusion... so why couldn’t I see through the beetle’s illusion?"</p>
<p>⟦That was because the Fire Seraphim Beetle has the Blaze Wisp flame. It possesses properties that allow it to distort space, creating an illusion of fire energy in the air. You failed to see it because of the high concentration of fire energy in the air.⟧</p>
<p>"That makes sense," Kent muttered and then blinked away again. A large quantity of flame energy will indeed cause such an illusion to be missed by Kent. He was basically in the Beatles’ domain after all...</p>
<p>Kent readied his attacks and went into battle again.</p>
<p>This time, he unleashed the ’Rapid Slash’ skill, sending 450 sword arcs into the beetle. It managed to evade a few, but some landed on its injured wings, causing it to falter yet again.</p>
<p>This gave Kent the best opportunity to attack, and he seized it. His sword flashed forward just one time, but dozens of sword lights flashed out, landing on the other wing of the beetle.</p>
<p>The moment those attacks connected, the beetle lost control of its flight and landed heavily on the ground. Thankfully, when it comes to killing things on the ground, Kent has the best skill.</p>
<p>Dozens of doom shards emerged from the ground, piercing the giant body of the beetle. That was enough to kill it.</p>
<p>The doom shard skill had already become much stronger, so it would have killed the beetle even if Kent used just one.</p>
<p>The moment the beetle died, the flame spirit emerged from the horn protruding from its head.</p>
<p>Kent moved toward it in an attempt to retrieve it. But as he got closer, his senses started to falter. "So that is how it is," Kent became intrigued. He quickly approached and retrieved it.</p>
<p>⟦Name: Blaze Wisp⟧</p>
<p>⟦Grade: Heaven⟧</p>
<p>⟦Beast Flame Chart: 6075⟧</p>
<p>⟦Effect: Grants enhanced fire resistance and the ability to create fire illusions.⟧</p>
<p>"Tower, what is this beast Flame Chart?" Kent asked, noticing the new addition when he appraised the spirit flame.</p>
<p>⟦The Beast Flame Chart is a universal ranking set to determine the most powerful beast flames in existence. The one you have just obtained is ranked 6075... Very weak.⟧</p>
<p>"You don’t have to tell me, Tower, I know it is weak," Kent smiled and then chose to devour the Blaze Wisp spirit flame...</p>
