<p>The moment Jyoti Lata heard what Borislav said, she knew it was time to implement her perfectly elaborate plan.</p>
<p>Deep within, she trusted and believed Kent would be able to free them from the Chaos Chasm.</p>
<p>She reached this conclusion because, aside from Kent, nobody from her world and those from the Infinity Universe who had come to the Chaos Chasm possessed more than two cores.</p>
<p>More importantly, no one else possessed two Chaos Hearts. It was her first time seeing such an anomaly, and because of that, she trusted and believed Kent would be able to help them.</p>
<p>The second reason, and perhaps the most important one, was that she had come to this conclusion because of the deductions she had made over the years while experiencing the Chaos Ascension King Trial.</p>
<p>In the last four trials, she had seen that, aside from the first level, none of the participants had advanced to the second level.</p>
<p>Of course, once someone failed the trial, all memories of what had transpired inside it would be wiped from their mind. So she had no idea what they had seen and experienced.</p>
<p>However, she was wise enough to draw some conclusions. Her most serious conclusion was that the trial had seven trials, each representing one of the tribes.</p>
<p>This meant the only person who could pass the trial would be someone who could wield the cultivation art of all seven tribes.</p>
<p>The Chaos Ascension King left a powerful cultivation art for each tribe, and so she believed that to pass his trial, one person should be able to wield all seven cultivation arts.</p>
<p>Kent had proven himself so far, making her see him as worthy of such a chance. Most importantly, Kent could perhaps be the only one capable of wielding both the cultivation art of her tribe and that of the other tribes.</p>
<p>Since that was the case and because every being in the Chaos Chasm desired their freedom, she baited the other tribe leaders into coming to her tribe to witness Kent in action.</p>
<p>While the energy produced by Kent’s cultivation was potent, it was not strong enough for beings millions of light-years away to sense it. So she amplified it, making sure it reached them just enough to pique their interest.</p>
<p>Borislav, the leader of the Void Vein Chaos Tribe, was the first to arrive.</p>
<p>A few hours after he arrived, the second tribe leader came. He was from the Obsidian Scale Chaos Tribe, the third-strongest tribe in the Chaos Chasm.</p>
<p>"It appears there is something good happening here if Borislav of all people came before me," said Veylan Seren, the tribe leader of the Obsidian Scale Chaos Tribe.</p>
<p>Just like the beings of the Red Bone Chaos Tribe, he was also a towering man, standing about ten feet tall, with jet-black skin covered in obsidian dragon scales.</p>
<p>Massive horns protruded from his forehead, and four glowing red eyes lined his face perfectly.</p>
<p>He appeared to be in his late fifties but possessed a fit, muscular physique.</p>
<p>Just like Jyoti Lata and Borislav, he was also a True Eternal.</p>
<p>The only reason his tribe was the third strongest was that, aside from him, there was another True Eternal and a Real Eternal, bringing the total number of Eternals in the Obsidian Scale Chaos Tribe to three.</p>
<p>This surpassed both the Red Bone Chaos Tribe and the Void Vein Chaos Tribe, which only had two each.</p>
<p>"I never expected old Veylan Seren to come this fast. The last I checked, I was told you broke your back by biting off more than you could chew," Borislav said with a laugh, causing Veylan Seren to smirk.</p>
<p>"Unlike you, who enjoys the comfort of your throne, I actually go out there fighting the terrifying creaures of the Void from swallowing our world."</p>
<p>Borislav sneered and responded, "Nobody sent you, old man."</p>
<p>Veylan Seren simply brushed him off and turned to Jyoti Lata.</p>
<p>"Looks like you have found yourself a gem, Jyoti Lata."</p>
<p>"More like he found me, but I’ll take the compliment," she laughed, looking at Kent as he took another consumable and swallowed it.</p>
<p>"Are you training him to challenge the Chaos Ascension King Trial?" Veylan Seren asked.</p>
<p>Jyoti Lata nodded.</p>
<p>"That’s right. He is from the Infinity Universe, just like that lady in your tribe who came millions of years ago. But unlike her, this kid has the constitution to wield all seven Arts left behind by the Chaos Ascension King."</p>
<p>"Oh, so that was why you called us here," Veylan Seren said, a smile appearing on his face. "It appears you want to bet everything on him, even going as far as giving him the infamous Nine Demon Body Tempering Art."</p>
<p>"I am not easily impressed, Veylan Seren, so naturally, he seems to have earned a place in my mind. For now, you can watch, and if you are satisfied with what you see, you can contribute to his growth. Also, it would be good if you could summon that lady in your tribe."</p>
<p>"You think she would recognize him?"</p>
<p>"I don’t know, but she did mention a Chaos Dragon before, and this kid is a dragon who possesses Chaos Energy, so it is worth checking."</p>
<p>"Very well."</p>
<p>Veylan Seren sent a message to his tribe before turning his attention back to Kent, who was now cultivating the second Demon Body.</p>
<p>A day later, the third tribe leader arrived.</p>
<p>"Hmm, I guess I came to the right place," said Zdzislav, the tribe leader of the Astral Eye Chaos Tribe, as he walked out of a void gate with an elegant posture.</p>
<p>He stood about seven feet tall, with indigo skin, the kind that made him stand out even among the other tribe leaders.</p>
<p>He had six glowing eyes, while a ring of floating cosmic eye tattoos hovered behind his back. His hair resembled flowing starlight. Undoubtedly, he was exceptionally handsome.</p>
<p>He also has a True Eternal Cultivation base.</p>
<p>"If it isn’t the man with the unpronounceable name," Borislav joked.</p>
<p>Zdzislav immediately became annoyed by the bastard who seemed to find humor in everything.</p>
<p>"Stay out of my business, Borislav."</p>
<p>He walked toward Jyoti Lata, on whom he clearly had a crush.</p>
<p>"Jyoti Lata, dear, I hope you’ve been well," he said, sounding unusually friendly and flirtatious.</p>
<p>"Tch, what a hopeless bastard," Borislav muttered with a smirk before turning his attention back to Kent, who had no idea he had already begun attracting some of the most terrifying cultivators in the Chaos Chasm.</p>
<p>Jyoti Lata ignored Zdzislav. She knew he would not mind acting shameless if it meant getting even the slightest smile from her.</p>
<p>She had learned to ignore him a billion years ago.</p>
<p>Luckily, only a few minutes after Zdzislav arrived, the fourth tribe leader also appeared.</p>
<p>This time, it was a woman, and she arrived with an overwhelming presence that clearly showed she had long since broken through to the Eternal King Realm.</p>
<p>She was Parand, the tribe leader of the Storm Wing Chaos Tribe.</p>
<p>Like Zdzislav, she possessed an average height, standing at 6’7", with blue-grey skin that gave her an alien yet strangely human appearance. Long jet-black hair flowed gracefully down her back.</p>
<p>Four enormous feathered Chaos Wings spread majestically from her back. If Kent were to see those wings, his eyes would undoubtedly widen in surprise. Although he possessed only three pairs, his Jade Void Wings looked almost identical to Parand’s.</p>
<p>Lightning tattoos traced elegant patterns across her skin, while crown-like horns extended from her forehead.</p>
<p>"Zdzislav, sometimes you should just take the hint and know when someone says no. Or do you want me to smack the word ’NO’ into your skull?" Parand said, flashing a beautiful smile.</p>
