<p>BOOOOOOM!</p>
<p>The hammer descended with terrifying force, every last energy being cultivated by Kent surging out from its rugged metallic head. As it moved, the surrounding space shattered, and the void grew unstable.</p>
<p>The pressure from it was also on another level, so much so that the very ground began to tremble, with mountains collapsing in the process.</p>
<p>Emmet, who came into the battle looking down on Kent, was finally forced to defend herself.</p>
<p>Her dragon energy surged, forming a massive lightning barrier around her body. Still, she didnt put much into her defense since she didnt think an attack from a demigod could overpower her.</p>
<p>However, the moment the hammer struck, the barrier shattered like glass.</p>
<p>BANG!</p>
<p>The hammer struck her body, and Emmet was sent crashing into the ground, creating a crater that stretched several kilometers wide.</p>
<p>The entire region trembled.</p>
<p>"Damn, that was satisfying," Kent muttered, blood trickling from the corner of his mouth. Although the future he saw using the Omniscent Dao Eyes didn’t happen to him, he could still feel the imaginary pain.</p>
<p>Seeing that he had managed to send her opponent crashing into the dirt, he was overjoyed.</p>
<p>However, his expression quickly changed.</p>
<p>He could sense it.</p>
<p>Emmet wasn’t injured.</p>
<p>In fact, the terrifying aura rising from the crater told him that his attack had only managed to annoy her. She is pissed now, and Kent knows she is coming for his head.</p>
<p>"You little bastard!"</p>
<p>BOOOOM!</p>
<p>A pillar of silver lightning erupted from the crater, and Emmet emerged, her long hair fluttering wildly as dragon scales began appearing on her cheeks and arms.</p>
<p>Her previously arrogant expression had disappeared.</p>
<p>In its place was a cold, murderous gaze.</p>
<p>"Second Hammer"</p>
<p>Kent didnt allow her to have the upper hand.</p>
<p>He knew he had to hold her back, and so he unleashed the second form of the Sixty-Six Hammer Art.</p>
<p>This time, two large hammers descended, and the pressure doubled that of the first hammer. Emmet was forced to defend again, but this time she knew better and spent more energy creating the defensive barrier.</p>
<p>"Third Hammer," Kent’s voice boomed across the space, and Emmet frowned.</p>
<p>She wasn’t even done defending the second hammer, and yet Kent was channeling a third attack, which would be triple the power of the second hammer.</p>
<p>The moment the three hammers descended, a look of caution appeared in Emmet’s eyes. Her body shifted, and she immediately transformed into her demi-dragon form.</p>
<p>She punches forward, sending a beam of compressed lightning. The moment the two attacks met, they canceled each other out.</p>
<p>But Kent didn’t hold himself back at all.</p>
<p>All seven energies surge into his hammer, and then he unleashes the third hammer again. However, this time, he coated the hammer with Blood Runes.</p>
<p>He only added 5% of his blood runes, but when the three hammers descended, Thousands of them coated their surfaces, causing the hammers to tremble as they moved.</p>
<p>ROARRRR</p>
<p>Emmet unleashed a dragon roar, her authority spreading throughout the space around her. Kent narrows his eyes and also unleashes his own dragon roar.</p>
<p>ROARRRRRRR</p>
<p>The pressure that was supposed to restrict him vanished into thin air.</p>
<p>"Again,"</p>
<p>He unleashed the third hammer, and this time he added 10% Blood Runes, 10 Flame Runes, and 5 Blood Death Seals.</p>
<p>RUMBLE RUMBLE</p>
<p>The sky turned completely dark, and the hammers that were supposed to descend at the same time now descended one after the other.</p>
<p>BANG</p>
<p>BANG</p>
<p>BANG</p>
<p>They descended with thunderous roars, causing the very fabric of reality to distort and warp. The pressure was just too much that even Emmet had to tap into the power of the Dao.</p>
<p>&lt;Dao energy detected&gt;</p>
<p>Tower alerted Kent the moment Emmet started using her Dao. Thankfully, Kent had prepared a counter for her.</p>
<p>"Let’s go for a swim."</p>
<p>Kent pressed the space between his brows, and then his eyes lit up, red, smoky energy coming out of them. The moment that happened, a large gate slowly rose from the ground and towered in the air.</p>
<p>The gate was colossal and eerie.</p>
<p>The moment it was fully out, resentful energy filled the place, and even before the gate’s doors opened, one could hear the howls, roars, and screams of the resentful souls hidden behind the Underworld Soul Gate.</p>
<p>Kent pointed his left index finger forward and commanded:</p>
<p>"Underworld Soul River... Open!"</p>
<p>The large doors of the Underworld Soul Gate parted, and a wave of water surged out. Thousands of resentful souls screamed as the water swallowed Emmet.</p>
<p>"Third Hammer... Again!"</p>
<p>Kent rose into the air one more time and unleashed the third hammer of the Sixty-Six Hammer Art. This time, Emmet frowned so much that her eyes flashed crimson, and Kent sensed an unfathomable pressure coming from them.</p>
<p>’Go ahead, use your Primordial Qi, and let’s see if you can live to tell the tale.’</p>
<p>Kent said inwardly, practically begging for Emmet to use her Primordial Qi against him. Since he was not a Primordial, the moment she used it against him, the heavens would react and punish her.</p>
<p>Naturally, there was even a chance her Dao Heart would be injured. If that happened, killing her would be within his reach.</p>
<p>He had already unleashed the Underworld Soul River, which he could now sustain for 2 minutes. With his Soul Universe sustaining his soul, he could use it more frequently. In fact, if he wanted to push his soul, then he could even keep it active for 5 minutes.</p>
<p>That was a long time for him to devastate her soul to the point where, the moment the heavens stepped in, winning would be much easier than he could ever dream of.</p>
<p>But Emmet also knew that, and so she didn’t dare tap into her Primordial Qi.</p>
<p>Instead, she tapped into her Celestial Qi, which at the moment was far denser than most Celestial Qi. She unleashed a deadly beam, this time blasting the hammer before it could even get closer to her.</p>
<p>She turned her gaze to Kent and moved, aiming to kill him once and for all.</p>
<p>"No way I would allow that."</p>
<p>Kent teleported away and appeared in the air, right below the clouds. Then his right index finger struck down, and the heavens trembled.</p>
<p>"Finger of Destruction..."</p>
