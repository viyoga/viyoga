<p>Who is Metal Saint Rocky, one might ask?</p>
<p>He is someone sitting at the peak of the Root Transcendent stage. He is a Saint-level blacksmith and holds considerable power in the Kyrrith Kingdom.</p>
<p>He has allies and friends in high places. It wouldn’t be an exaggeration to say that he is someone who cannot be offended, even by a king. His influence is very high and, well, supreme.</p>
<p>So why, then, did a mere Root Sage tell him to go hide, warning him of the consequences if he were to attack him?</p>
<p>Scratch that. Did Kent just say he was attacked yesterday but killed all of them?</p>
<p>"Clearly, he was joking, right?" someone asked, but the look on the faces of everyone listening to the interesting turn of events said it all.</p>
<p>The brat indeed said something like that, and right now, he had threatened someone he accused of sending assassins after him and his ladies.</p>
<p>That is just too wild.</p>
<p>"Are you threatening me? Do you know who I am?" Metal Saint Rocky asked through clenched teeth. It doesn’t take a genius to know he was very angry at the moment.</p>
<p>"I don’t know who you are, nor do I care. And of course, I am threatening you," Kent replied, raising an eyebrow.</p>
<p>"Why else would I say all those things if not to threaten you? I know you were part of those who tried to kill me and my ladies yesterday. If I were to display the bodies, some of those unfortunate mice would be your minions.</p>
<p>So yes, I am threatening you. And while you will boast and say you have the power to destroy me, know that I also have the power to end your existence within a minute.</p>
<p>If you don’t believe me, try attacking my soul again like you three did yesterday. Just indulge me and try. I will show you that in this life, there are some people you cannot piss off."</p>
<p>Elsa was already smiling and praying in her heart for the blacksmith to attack Kent. This will be her chance to show off her abilities.</p>
<p>Kent was getting angry, causing his aura to start to leak. But he held himself back from unleashing the aura of hatred and resentment Vexthra gave him.</p>
<p>"I thought so. All you know is how to make big words, but when it’s time for action, you chicken out," he sneered.</p>
<p>"Let this be a wake-up call to all those who sent 25 Great Sages and 7 Transcendents after me and my ladies yesterday. They are all dead, so stop looking for them.</p>
<p>Now, if you are still not convinced, then try again, and please, this time, come yourself. Having scum like you in this life is already an insult to some of us.</p>
<p>So, if you want, try again and see if you will succeed. You don’t know me, but I know you well enough to say none of you who came after me yesterday can succeed in killing me.</p>
<p>So let this be the first and last. If it happens again, don’t say I never warned you," Kent spoke in a cold tone that, for some reason, sent a chill down everyone’s spine.</p>
<p>The audacity of his words said it all: the brat is no easy cookie to crack.</p>
<p>⟪Proud of you, master. You’ve spoken like a true Dragon.⟫</p>
<p>’You can give me gifts instead,’ Kent nearly laughed. That would have destroyed the atmosphere he had created.</p>
<p>Though Gaia was just teasing him, he indeed had spoken with great authority.</p>
<p>"Ahem," Alina, who was standing and hearing all these threats being thrown in the air, cleared her throat.</p>
<p>"Let it be known that this is still the auction house, and as said yesterday, if you don’t have the money to win a bid, don’t resort to violence.</p>
<p>You two have broken that rule, and as such, you will be suspended from bidding on the last item tomorrow." Alina spoke like a true woman in authority.</p>
<p>This caused everyone to sigh in their hearts.</p>
<p>Banning Metal Saint Rocky was okay for them. But Kent is someone destined to make the last day of the auction memorable, so banning him didn’t sit well with them.</p>
<p>They wanted to protest, but they also didn’t want to anger Alina, who held a strict look on her face. She has all the power at the moment.</p>
<p>"My lady, this is their first offense, so why don’t you tone it down for them? Take it as me owing the Silver Leaf Auction House a favor," suddenly, a voice spoke from one of the VIP rooms.</p>
<p>"I hope that a favor from a kingdom protector can pardon them." The voice faded away, leaving everyone wide-eyed.</p>
<p>The person was gone, and the words she left behind were too much for even Alina to handle.</p>
<p>Suddenly, a beautiful lady wearing a veil appeared behind Alina and held her by the shoulder.</p>
<p>"Auction Master," Alina said, bowing slightly.</p>
<p>"No need for that, Alina. You have done a good job, even better than what I would have been able to do. So why don’t you let me take it from here?" the lady said, making Alina smile and step back.</p>
<p>The lady looked toward Kent’s VIP room and then at Metal Saint Rocky’s room.</p>
<p>"Under normal circumstances, you two would be banned from all auction houses operated by the Silver Leaf. But since a dignified person, a Kingdom Protector, has asked to pardon you, I will let this slide.</p>
<p>However, ensure this is the last time this happens while inside my auction house." The lady spoke, and the authority she exuded commanded obedience.</p>
<p>"Thank you for your benevolence, my lady," Metal Saint Rocky said, bowing slightly.</p>
<p>"My mom said when a beautiful angel speaks, you can only take it to heart and never let it slip away. So since you spoke, I can only take it to heart," Kent also said, but his words weren’t exactly what the lady in the veil was expecting.</p>
<p>However, she maintained her composure and continued.</p>
<p>"Since that is settled, I would like to announce that tomorrow we will be auctioning 75 items, of which only 5 will be Heaven-grade.</p>
<p>60 will be Legendary-grade items, two Mystical items, and seven will be like the skull that was just auctioned off.</p>
<p>But as for our last item to conclude this year’s auction, I don’t even know what it is. It was a last-minute addition from an anonymous seller, so we can only wait and see."</p>
<p>"Those who won bids today, come for them after we close. And those who didn’t, you can visit our Green Gate Bazaar later—some things will catch your attention.</p>
<p>See you all tomorrow."</p>
