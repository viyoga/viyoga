<p>If only the powerhouses gathered on planet Brymrr knew they were in the presence of legendary beasts, perhaps all of them, regardless of their cultivation, would be on their knees, bowing to their majestic selves.</p>
<p>Celestial Bjok and a few who had heard of some of these beasts were all left with their jaws hanging loose as they gazed at the line-up now facing Inaya.</p>
<p>Nobody knew why all five remaining beasts appeared when the rule stated that a new beast would come once every 24 hours.</p>
<p>All five of them appearing at the same time wasn’t part of the rules.</p>
<p>However, as they appeared and assumed their humanoid forms, one could tell something significant was about to happen.</p>
<p>If the rules were followed, then the beast to come after Tharos was the Violet Wing Storm Roc, one of the fastest beasts in the sky. In the Great Records, they said that during the Great War, her speed rivaled Celestial-level Void Beasts.</p>
<p>Her self-created skill, called [Sky Splitter Dive], is rumored to hold so much power that, at her descent, stars, moons, and even planets can split open, shattering asteroids as if they were made of limestone.</p>
<p>Now, as she stood with purple hair falling down her back, five pairs of metallic angel-type wings, and a refined athletic body, one could only admire her beauty, forgetting that the most attractive thing about her is her speed.</p>
<p>Her personality is cold, embodying the essence of both danger and distance.</p>
<p>Shockingly, as she looked at Inaya, a smile appeared on her beautiful face. Her human name is Zephyra.</p>
<p>Beside her stood perhaps one of the most handsome men in the universe.</p>
<p>During the Primordial Era, he ruled over an island of lions called the Divine Island. He was respected and revered by many, especially by the beasts he ruled.</p>
<p>They called him the Golden Mane Celestial Lion, and among humans, he was known as Lios. Because he stayed away from battles and chose to live a life of peace, many regarded him as weak.</p>
<p>For years, he was ridiculed and called all kinds of names by his rivals until some became bold and poked the hornet’s nest.</p>
<p>A team of 25 god beasts tried to hunt on his island and ended up killing one of his people.</p>
<p>By the time the Primordial Chaos Dragon reached there, the heads of all 25 gods were hanged on spikes, acting as scarecrows for all who dared come close to his domain.</p>
<p>Since then, nobody has dared to call him weak or look down on him. Today, Lios stood with his hands clasped behind his back, a calm look on his face as he regarded Inaya.</p>
<p>His golden hair and perfect cheekbones made him the center of attention for many women around.</p>
<p>Beside him stood another dangerous beast, the Crimson Shell Flame Tortoise, or as humans called him, Moltros.</p>
<p>The Crimson Shell Flame Tortoise embodied the essence of endurance and defense. His defense rivaled that of even the Primordial Chaos Dragon, and he was also one of the few beasts to become Monarchs during the Primordial Era.</p>
<p>Legend has it that whatever strikes that lands on his ultra-hard, flame-rich shell, it bounces back with ten times the strength. Even if he chose not to fight and decided to lie there, no attacks below the True Supreme stage can penetrate his defense.</p>
<p>Moltros had now transformed into a muscular, shirtless man with reddish, metallic skin, the kind that shows the defensive monster he is.</p>
<p>Standing beside him are the legendary White Horn Moon Kirin and Shadow Talon Chaos Qilin.</p>
<p>Many called them twins, for as far as one could remember, they had been together, despite one being a unicorn and the other a Qilin.</p>
<p>Nareth was the unicorn who wielded control over Ice, Light, and Time. The records describe her as a support-type warrior, for they say she can speed up time to see the next few seconds, paving the way for Xarion, known for his control over Chaos and Destruction, to do his thing.</p>
<p>They were the dynamic duo, the kind of beasts you want on your side, not against you.</p>
<p>Nareth has long hair and a beautiful body, while Xarion has bluish-purple hair and a handsome appearance.</p>
<p>Among all seven beasts, Xarion was the strongest of them since he was already at the Peak-Level True Supreme stage. But even with that power, as he took a step forward, a smile could be seen on his face.</p>
<p>"The first time I saw him, he was locked in battle with a Celestial-stage Forbidden King. From the start to the end of the battle, he dominated the Forbidden King until he killed him," Xarion smiled...</p>
<p>"As someone who wields the power of Chaos, I saw him as my mentor, and so I followed him around until the day he noticed me and, to my shock, approached me.</p>
<p>That encounter changed my life for good and also awakened a sense of respect that I, Xarion, would honor one day." He turned and looked at his friend, or more like a twin, Nareth, before adding.</p>
<p>"I am saying all this simply because my sister Nareth has the power to tell when someone is lying. If what you had said now was a lie, I would have killed you in a heartbeat, for I would not allow anyone to tarnish the image of Lord Chaos." He grinned, causing Inaya to feel her legs grow weak.</p>
<p>"Luckily for you, what you said wasn’t a lie, meaning you had indeed met Lord Chaos and even became his disciple."</p>
<p>Nareth stepped in. "The disciple part can be debated later. However, I have reasons to believe you share a close relationship with Lord Chaos, so all five of us decided to make things short for you."</p>
<p>Lios’s smile caused his already handsome appearance to glow. "Since you are Lord Chaos’s disciple, we have no reason to judge your worth. You qualify to become a Supreme, so accept our defeat."</p>
<p>All five of them bowed, and with that, the tribulation came to an end. However, despite the tribulation cloud receding, no one present said a single word.</p>
<p>What they came there to witness didn’t happen; rather, something even more shocking happened.</p>
<p>First, it was made known that Lord Chaos is back and that he shared a close bond with the Ten Emotions Sword Supreme.</p>
<p>Second, seven legendary beasts from the Primordial Era became contracted beasts of the one they were meant to challenge. Not once in the history of tribulations had something this wondrous ever happened.</p>
<p>But as everyone gazed at the six beasts standing before Inaya, the tribulation cloud and the humanoid silhouette now gone, it became clear that she had indeed become the master of this legendary figure.</p>
<p>"Hahahahaha..." Suddenly, a loud laughter came from the clouds, followed by a voice only a few recognized. "What a spectacle. To think I would witness something this unexpected."</p>
<p>A man with flowing black hair appeared from the clouds, seated inside a wooden-like throne. Behind him, the concept of a garden was visible, spreading and commanding absolute respect.</p>
<p>When many set eyes on this Celestial, their expressions changed. Most of the Supremes and Celestials bowed their heads.</p>
<p>"We greet the Garden Master."</p>
<p>Seated in his chair, a smile appeared on his face, and he nodded at how well he was received. He turned and looked at Inaya, then at the six beasts, and was about to speak when the sky suddenly turned red.</p>
<p>"Oh, looks like I made it here on time..." A different voice was heard, and everyone tensed.</p>
