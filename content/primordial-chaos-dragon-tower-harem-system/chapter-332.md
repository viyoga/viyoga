<p>Kaizo, Kent’s first undead, is a monstrosity of a man. The saying "I am built differently" is taken from him. He is a terrifying sight to behold. A being built differently.</p>
<p>Kaizo is a towering warrior with a muscular physique, his body sculpted like steel. Just looking at him is enough to tell that swords and pointy items won’t have much effect on him.</p>
<p>He has long black hair that looks as though it is standing on its own in groups. Some strands fall behind him, but just looking at his hair shows his strong affinity for the lightning element.</p>
<p>He wears a dark, sleeveless martial gi with a thick, tattered belt tied around his waist. His wrists are wrapped with glowing blue bands, crackling with electricity. His lower attire consists of flowing dark pants.</p>
<p>The moment Kent sent everyone into the battle space he planned to use during the rescue mission, he summoned his first undead, Kaizo.</p>
<p>The attackers, now feeling the pressure emanating from him, broke into a cold sweat. The battle space is just like the outside world, with the laws and everything thing working the same as the outside world.</p>
<p>The only difference was that Kent could send out and pull in people.</p>
<p>He appeared beside his undead and looked down at the people who were still lost about where they were.</p>
<p>"Let’s end this quickly. There are still targets waiting for us on the outside," Kent said, and Kaizo nodded.</p>
<p>He cracked his knuckles and turned his neck sideways, creating a cracking sound. That alone broke the confidence of many.</p>
<p>Kaizo smiled down at the 40 figures Kent managed to pull inside the battle space. The smile didn’t reach his eyes.</p>
<p>This alone made most of them break into more sweat.</p>
<p>"First Kill", he muttered.</p>
<p>He then pointed at a Root Transcendent holding a spear. At once, a lightning bolt shot from his fingers, drilling a hole through the man’s forehead.</p>
<p>"It worked, huh? Then why don’t we start with a clap?" Kaizo moved and appeared in the middle of a group of eight Root Transcendents.</p>
<p>"Thunderclap!" He clapped his hands together, and lightning burst out, knocking four away and killing four on the spot.</p>
<p>"This will be fun," Kaizo laughed, and then he jumped toward a Root Transcendent who was holding a sword. His fist crackled with lightning as it descended on the poor bastard.</p>
<p>"On second thought, take your time, Kaizo," Kent smiled behind his mask and waved his hand. At once, five powerful Battle Dolls, each at the peak of the Root Great Sage stage, appeared and jumped into action.</p>
<p>"You three are mine," he said, turning toward the Three Metal Saints, and then he vanished.</p>
<p>The next second, his sword left its sheath and aimed at Metal Saint Andrew, who was caught off guard by the sudden disappearance and reappearance of the brat.</p>
<p>Kent’s sword came close to severing his neck, but he had to dodge to the side when an attack came from Metal Saint Rocky, who was wearing a gauntlet.</p>
<p>Kent moved back a few meters before stabilizing himself. The Rocky was named after his build and fighting style.</p>
<p>He is a brute, and the shockwave from his punch said it all. The bastard is powerful. He was pushed back and tried to stabilise himself.</p>
<p>However, he isn’t even done when Metal Saint Adolf appears behind him, aiming his sword with the intent of stabbing him in the back.</p>
<p>However, Kent blinked away, leaving nothing behind.</p>
<p>Adolf’s sword struck the air.</p>
<p>Kent appeared behind one of the Root Great Sages still present, and before this unfortunate soul could realize what had happened, his head came rolling down.</p>
<p>Kent blinked away again, and when he was within range of another Root Great Sage, he attacked, and that head, too, was on the ground the next second.</p>
<p>The three Root Transcendents who witnessed this frowned.</p>
<p>This was a huge disrespect to them. Not only did a junior disregard their presence and kill two people right under their noses, but he also did it in a way they couldn’t comprehend.</p>
<p>Kent was teleporting between attacks, and from their perspective, he could do it continuously for who knows how long.</p>
<p>Kent went ahead and dispatched the last of the Root Great Sages, whose bodies vanished the moment they fell.</p>
<p>When he was done, he turned toward the three Metal Saints and swung his sword at Metal Saint Rocky.</p>
<p>"72 Sovereign Weaving Strike!"</p>
<p>The 72 chains with pointed tips aimed at Rocky. They moved swiftly through the air, but when they were a few meters from him, they split up, catching Andrew and Adolf off-guard.</p>
<p>Naturally, they all raised their defences.</p>
<p>Rocky crossed his arms in an X shape, creating a forcefield around him. Andrew stabbed his spear into the ground, also creating a defence. As for Adolf, he took a large swing, sending a sword arc to counter the chains.</p>
<p>They each mounted their defences, but that was exactly what Kent was after.</p>
<p>"Rapid Slash!"</p>
<p>His sword flashed forward, and 300 sword arcs appeared in the air.</p>
<p>"What?!" Rocky shouted, trying to mount another defence, but he was a step too late. All 300 sword arcs rained down on him alone, sending him flying.</p>
<p>Kent blinked away and appeared kilometres away, where, by his calculation, the Metal Saint would land. However, instead of activating another attack, Kent took out an item and threw it at him.</p>
<p>It was a fist-sized ball. He threw it at the brute, and when it landed on his body, it exploded.</p>
<p>"Aaarrrggghhh!"</p>
<p>A painful cry escaped the Metal Saint’s lips as his body slammed into the ground with a heavy thud.</p>
<p>All over his body, metal spikes in the form of nails could be seen embedded, causing blood to pump from every wound.</p>
<p>He rolled on the ground, screaming as the nails, imbued with a poison designed to amplify pain, began to invade his body.</p>
<p>The agony would only continue to increase as long as he remained alive. So he kept screaming his heart out on the ground, bringing a smile to Kent’s face.</p>
<p>"You will suffer before dying," Kent said coldly.</p>
<p>Kent didn’t even turn to face him. Instead, he dodged to the side, evading a spear thrust that could have killed him.</p>
<p>"No, you won’t," Kent muttered, realizing Metal Saint Andrew had intended to save his partner on the ground.</p>
<p>In an instant, Kent blinked and appeared beside the screaming body of the Root Transcendent, who was in that situation mainly because he had been caught off-guard by Kent’s careful planning.</p>
<p>The three are Transcendents, so killing them without tricks should be something Kent would entertain when dozens of Transcendents surround him.</p>
<p>"Kaizo!" Kent called out.</p>
<p>At that moment, a powerful kick landed on the body of the Metal Saint, sending him flying toward the menacing Kaizo, who was effortlessly wasting away the lives of those attacking him.</p>
<p>Kaizo, who needed no verbal command, clapped his hands, and thunder rumbled inside the battle space.</p>
<p>Then, as if commanding a bolt of lightning, a massive bolt struck Metal Saint Rocky’s chest, drilling a hole straight through him.</p>
<p>"One down, two more to go," Kent grinned behind his mask. He had killed one and was ready to kill the two left.</p>
<p>However, before he could move to attack them, his body froze as an arrow appeared in front of him, speeding toward his chest.</p>
