<p>The Ascension stage Metal Spider truly lives up to its name.</p>
<p>Unlike the metal spiders at the Core Formation stage, which have 12-meter-long legs, this one’s legs stretch an imposing 20 meters, and its body is colossal, with thick, armored plates gleaming with an eerie metallic sheen that seems almost impervious to ordinary weapons.</p>
<p>Just looking at it conveys the message that it isn’t an easy target to cut.</p>
<p>Its segmented exoskeleton pulses faintly with toxic, mist-like energy, veins of glowing blue light tracing intricate patterns across its surface.</p>
<p>It appears to have been decorated with ancient runes, when, in fact, the patterns are merely there to make it even more imposing.</p>
<p>Camilla trails her eyes across its limbs, long and slender yet bristling with sharp barbs, ending in pincers capable of crushing metals.</p>
<p>"That is one hideous yet terrifying spider," Camilla smirked, watching the spider emerge from the thickness of the forest.</p>
<p>It has multiple eyes glinting like shards of obsidian. As it moves, the ground trembles beneath its weight, sending vibrations through the floating island’s surface.</p>
<p>Thick, steam-like mist coils off its body, hinting at the toxic venom it can unleash. Every step it takes sends a low metallic clang echoing, a dreadful warning to all who dare stand against it.</p>
<p>"The rules of combat aren’t fair sometimes... A dwarf like me has no business killing such a monster, but in the end, I must because it is necessary..." The spear in her hand started to give off a powerful spear aura.</p>
<p>"The current you can only handle 5% of my power... We can push past that when it comes down to it, but for now, only 5% can be accessed without you passing out," the Spear of Dawn spoke.</p>
<p>Camilla smiled, "I just need 2%."</p>
<p>One of the three souls hidden inside the spear has awakened, making the spear a terrifying killing tool. However, Camilla is still too weak to handle more than 5% of her true power.</p>
<p>But then again, Camilla doesn’t really need too much of a boost since she has more than enough means to fight a spirit Ascension stage spider.</p>
<p>So asking for 2% is even overkill. When the spider fully emerged from the forest, Camilla flew into the air, her hair blowing back in the gentle wind.</p>
<p>She was clad in lightweight whitish-silver armor. The Spear of Dawn was pulsing with energy so potent that when she pointed it at the spider, it felt its body grow heavier.</p>
<p>Its movement slowed down and eventually came to a halt.</p>
<p>"Starfall Lance."</p>
<p>The moment she muttered, a giant spear, about 15 meters long, appeared in the air. It was made of pure star energy, infused with spear qi.</p>
<p>The spider was locked down when the spear appeared, to the shock of Kent and Vexthra, who were watching from afar. Camilla stabbed her spear forward with one hand, and just like that, the giant spear in the air shot out, impaling the spider to the ground.</p>
<p>It couldn’t even raise its 20-meter-long metallic legs to defend itself. The spears went through its body, pinning it to the ground.</p>
<p>Camilla shook her head and flew away, joining the thick of the battle... It was rather unfortunate that the spider couldn’t withstand the 2% willpower of her spear.</p>
