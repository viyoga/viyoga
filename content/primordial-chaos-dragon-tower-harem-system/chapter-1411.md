<p>It took Kent an hour and 15 minutes to extract the essence.</p>
<p>That was a bit longer than he expected.</p>
<p>However, since he wouldn’t be burning impurities, considering he had achieved perfect extraction, he went ahead and, within 10 minutes, condensed the pill.</p>
<p>That was much faster than he anticipated.</p>
<p>Once these steps were out of the way, he moved ahead to the main event, which had to do with refining the Azure Pill Ring.</p>
<p>For that, he already had the [Nine-step pill ring refinement] technique. It is the best in the game, considering it was invented by the legendary Kevin Sage, so he tapped into that and started working his way up.</p>
<p>Basically, he would have to divide the essence for the Pill ring into 9 parts, refine each separately, and then merge them to form the Azure Pill Ring.</p>
<p>Technically, it was impossible. So, with what he had achieved in the past weeks alone, Kent knew he was on the right track. H wasted no time and started the refinement.</p>
<p>What he was attempting to do had never been done before, so he was giving himself all the time in the world to succeed.</p>
<p>The process started on the right track, and as each level was completed, the next was taken over until, after an hour and a half, all nine essences had been refined.</p>
<p>"Moment of truth."</p>
<p>Kent let a small smile appear on his lips as he slowly merged the nine essences and started refining the Azure Pill Ring.</p>
<p>What a few who were merely curious had attempted and failed was now being attempted by Kent Madson. As to whether he would succeed where others failed, it would depend on how badly he wanted to become a Pill Monarch.</p>
<p>If he were to fail right now, he would have to attempt it again using a Tier 12 pill. However, even though he had confidence in himself, simply being able to accomplish the perfect extraction of a Tier 11 herb as a Pill Emperor was all the motivation he needed.</p>
<p>He had already done what only a few could do while at the Pill Emperor stage. So he was ahead of many, and that alone was translating into what he had accomplished in these past few hours of pill-making, which was finally nearing its end.</p>
<p>A pill with an Azure Pill Ring would become 7 times more potent. So if Kent managed to make this pill, instead of its initial 100% potency, it would become 700% potent, since it was a perfect pill.</p>
<p>That was just too much to even consider.</p>
<p>But Kent was considering it, and after 30 minutes of refinement, he applied the last method needed to bring out the pill ring.</p>
<p>Kent held his breath like his lastborn was about to jump off a building. He knew the moment had arrived, and so when he made his move, the outcome became clear.</p>
<p>A faint, sweet, and wonderful smell escaped the cauldron, filling the alchemy tower in an instant. Kent didn’t dare breathe; he continued to refine the last part of the essence for five minutes.</p>
<p>With every minute that passed, the smell of the pill kept intensifying until it stopped when a white smoke escaped the cauldron.</p>
<p>The moment the smoke appeared, Kent sighed, since he knew white smoke meant a Grey Pill Ring. It was a bit heartbreaking, but he kept his composure.</p>
<p>However, a second later, blue smoke also escaped the openings in the cauldron. Kent’s heart skipped a beat for a moment, then a broad smile that came from the heart appeared on his face.</p>
<p>"YES..."</p>
<p>His mood brightened immediately because, at that moment, the process ended, and when the cauldron opened, the pill that came out was not what he expected.</p>
<p>The plan was to concoct a pill with an Azure Pill Ring. But what he was looking at now was a perfect Tier 11 Bone Tempering Pill with not one but two pill rings. The first, of course, was the Grey Pill Ring, which was much deeper compared to the previous ones he had refined.</p>
<p>The second was the Azure Pill Ring, which was also much deeper, all things considered. Basically, he was aiming for 7 times potency, but he achieved 10 times potency instead.</p>
<p>Kent reached for the pill; however, before his hand could touch it, his world darkened, and he passed out. His consciousness had entered enlightenment.</p>
<p>The Pill Dao saw his effort, and so he was being rewarded.</p>
<p>Messages flooded his vision.</p>
<p>---</p>
<p>[Pill Council - Pill Dao Tablet Update]</p>
<p>Just like how there is the Sword Council, the Ice Temple, or even the Celestial Council, there is also the Pill Council, where all decisions concerning the world of pill-making are made.</p>
<p>All alchemists who had ever stepped into it are legends or were legends.</p>
<p>This council is said to have existed since the dawn of time and has been working tirelessly to ensure the world of alchemists is secured and elevated to a level where pill-making would become the centre of everyone’s life.</p>
<p>Already, alchemy had secured its place as one of the professions nobody can do away with.</p>
<p>The Pill Council had brought out many recipes, some that had even raised the bar so high that the Pill Daos had recognised them.</p>
<p>Just like the Sword Dao Tablet, where those who have made a significant contribution in the path of swordsmanship will be recognised, the Pill Dao Tablet is also the same.</p>
<p>However, it is a bit special.</p>
<p>On the Sword Dao Tablet, only the swordsman’s name and rank are shown. However, with the Pill Dao Tablet, the name, rank, and achievement are displayed for all to see.</p>
<p>Whenever a Pill Dao Tablet records a name, it is broadcast to every last known branch of the Pill Council, be it in the Medium, High, Immortal, God, Supreme, or Celestial World.</p>
<p>So everyone became shocked when, 14 million years after the last name appeared, just now, the Pill Dao Tablet recorded another name, and this name, everyone had heard many times in the past couple of months alone.</p>
<p>In fact, he is the most famous person in the entire universe, and he has just become even more popular.</p>
<p>The Pill Dao Tablet only records the names of those who had done something incredible in the path of pill-making, so Kent, who was known for his name appearing on the Sword Dao Tablet and even the Ice Dao Tablet, was the last person expected to make an appearance here.</p>
<p>The reason is simple. Swordsmanship is a demanding profession. It takes every last ounce of one’s time to walk such a path.</p>
<p>So his name appearing on the sword dao tablet shows that he is one of the hard-working swordsmen in the universe. Similarly, he could also add an elemental affinity to his swordsmanship, which every swordsman does. So again, gaining recognition there was also possible.</p>
<p>However, taking on yet another demanding profession, such as alchemy, and gaining recognition from the Pill Dao is unheard of. It is impossible to walk such paths at the same time.</p>
<p>Now, he alone had gained the recognition of three Daos, and that alone was just something nobody saw coming.</p>
<p>He is like the outlier, the anomaly that nobody looks forward to, but when they least expect it, he swoops in and takes everyone by storm.</p>
<p>His name had appeared on the Pill Dao Tablet, and so yet again, he had become the most famous person in the entire universe. His name had started causing chaos yet again.</p>
