<p>"Fall Back!"</p>
<p>Sect Leader Deimos shouted in a commanding tone, causing thousands of warriors to move back.</p>
<p>He sensed the six vampires coming through the crack, and so he commanded his people to fall back. Of course, even without him commanding them to fall back, they themselves could sense the dangers coming through the crack.</p>
<p>However, just when everyone was retreating, Zareth moved forward.</p>
<p>"Big sister, shouldn’t we go help them?" Unity asked. Vexthra shook her head.</p>
<p>"Per the details I have here, the six coming are the Six Blood Royals. They aren’t the strongest of the vampire race, so let’s allow Kaizo and Zareth to handle them.</p>
<p>The main danger will soon appear, and I need you all in full strength for it. Kent isn’t coming anytime soon, so we have to at least be able to save this world."</p>
<p>Unity nodded and focused her eyes on the crack in the air.</p>
<p>From within, the six people appeared with a powerful aura that immediately forced those who couldn’t move back much faster to go down on their knees. Some were coughing up blood, with a few passing out.</p>
<p>Thankfully, many were able to move back following Sect Leader Deimos’ command.</p>
<p>Zareth looked coldly at the new arrivals and smiled. "It will be best if you six focus on me instead of looking at her. Trust me, even if there are millions of you, getting to her will be next to impossible."</p>
<p>Instead of replying to him, one of the Six Blood Royals charged forward with a spear aimed at Zareth’s chest.</p>
<p>Zareth shook his head as a smile appeared on his face. "What an idiot."</p>
<p>He tapped his forehead with his index and middle finger and muttered coldly, "Petrifying Gaze."</p>
<p>A third eye opened on his forehead, resembling the eye of a serpent. Then, from within, a thin white light shot out and landed on the approaching Blood Prince’s body, turning him into a stone statue instantly.</p>
<p>His approach came to a sudden halt, and then lightning struck him.</p>
<p>"Explode for me."</p>
<p>Just when the fourth Blood Prince turned to stone, Kaizo’s fist went through his chest, turning him into chunks of rock.</p>
<p>The faces of the five remaining Blood Royals turned ashen as one of them was reduced to blocks of stone.</p>
<p>Kaizo floated beside Zareth and grinned at the five Blood Royals.</p>
<p>"It will be best if you all come at the same time. Don’t blame me for bullying you if you don’t heed by warning."</p>
<p>"Kill him."</p>
<p>The remaining five charged forward, their target now being Kaizo, who smiled and raised his hand, summoning a ring of golden lightning around them.</p>
<p>Zareth smirked and vanished from the ring of lightning. As for the five Blood Royals, they came to a halt when the ring appeared.</p>
<p>"No need to stop. The lightning ring only lowers your strength by 30%." With a grin on his face, Kaizo charged forward.</p>
<p>The five Blood Royals recovered from their shocked state and charged too. However, before they could get close to Kaizo, he vanished.</p>
<p>"Where did he go?" the First Blood Princess asked, looking around.</p>
<p>The others were doing the same. However, a few seconds later, Kaizo appeared through a strand of lightning and slammed his fist into the First Blood Princess’s face before vanishing again.</p>
<p>Meanwhile, the Blood Princess was hurled through the air, and just when she was about to touch the lightning ring, a powerful bolt of lightning, much thicker and deadlier, shot from the ring, drilling a hole through her forehead.</p>
<p>"I forgot to tell you, getting close to the ring is a death wish."</p>
<p>Kaizo appeared behind the Third Blood Prince, grabbed hold of both his hands, and hurled him forward.</p>
<p>The First Blood Prince reacted quickly, jumping forward to grab the Third Blood Prince. However, just when he was about to grab his leg, Kaizo appeared in the air and, using his knee, he slammed it hard into the First Blood Prince’s chin, causing a few teeth to fly out.</p>
<p>"Hope I didn’t get the fangs."</p>
<p>The First Blood Prince felt his world spin for a few seconds, but he recovered when a painful cry came from one side of the ring.</p>
<p>When he turned and looked, he saw the Third Blood Prince’s body letting out smoke as he fell to his death. The ring barbecued him to death.</p>
<p>In that very moment, the First Blood Prince and the remaining Blood Royals felt they had fallen into the trap of a monster who wasn’t even taking them seriously.</p>
<p>Naturally, Kaizo wasn’t taking them seriously, for regardless of how powerful they were, Kaizo had faced those several times stronger than them.</p>
<p>So they were mere bugs before him.</p>
<p>On the battlefield, the warriors had retreated far back, keeping the vampires on one side of the battlefield.</p>
<p>Naturally, everything had come to a halt, with everyone looking at Kaizo and the Blood Royals.</p>
<p>All vampires under the command of the six blood royals were on the battlefield, but none were attacking.</p>
<p>Far in the distance, Vexthra raised her brow when she sensed something coming from within the crack. She used the connection to send a message to Princess Lovina and Alina.</p>
<p>"End the battle."</p>
<p>They nodded.</p>
<p>Princess Lovina moved forward and raised her staff. "Time to end this." Her staff shone purple as her poison energy moved through it.</p>
<p>"Poison World."</p>
<p>A thick purple mist came from her staff and rose up, forming into a portal. Then, from the portal, more purple mist appeared and began forming into more portals.</p>
<p>Princess Lovina waved her staff and muttered some strange words. However, when she finished, balls of black mist came from the thousands of portals that appeared in the air.</p>
<p>"Grand Battle Formation—Activate."</p>
<p>At the moment the balls appear, the busty Alina also made her move.</p>
<p>Up in the air, a blue formation housing hundreds of smaller formations appeared and swallowed the black mists that Princess Lovina had summoned.</p>
<p>"First Formation: Battleground Movement."</p>
<p>Just when the vampires were looking up, a formation appeared on the ground, and black mist enveloped them, causing many to start coughing.</p>
<p>"What is this?" one vampire shouted, feeling her body giving up on her.</p>
<p>"Retreat," she called for a retreat. However, before any of them could move, Alina activated the second and third formations.</p>
<p>"Space Lock."</p>
<p>"Earth Movement."</p>
<p>From all sides, the ground started moving.</p>
<p>Those who attempted to fly felt something restricting them, forcing them to stay on the ground. However, those who were moving or managing to move felt the ground give up on them.</p>
<p>It was as if, for every step they took, three more were added to the distance between them and the crack. And while all this was happening, the mist was spreading among them.</p>
<p>After enveloping 80% of them, Princess Lovina smiled and waved her staff.</p>
<p>"Poison Reversal."</p>
<p>All those within the poison mist came to a standstill, and then, as if they had eaten bad food, they all started vomiting blood.</p>
<p>"What in the hell?"</p>
<p>Lady Enzi muttered, looking at the vampires vomiting blood, with some dropping dead as their bodies began drying up.</p>
<p>"Just what kind of monsters are these people?" She looked from Princess Lovina to Kaizo and back, and all she could see were monsters that she couldn’t measure up to.</p>
<p>Within minutes, 80% of the vampire army was turned to blood, and all of the blood was being channeled by Sylara, who was still hovering in the air.</p>
<p>The warriors were left staring at all this with shocked expressions.</p>
<p>By the time the poison managed to reach 90% of the vampires, the rest vanished back into the crack.</p>
<p>Vexthra looked at Unity. "Time for you to shine."</p>
<p>"Finally."</p>
<p>Unity vanished and appeared beside Alina, causing the warriors on the ground to turn toward her.</p>
<p>However, Unity’s focus was on the crack. She took out an arrow that, the moment she brought it out, everyone, regardless of strength level, felt the danger within.</p>
<p>Vexthra shook her head, looking at the arrow in Unity’s hand. "Mercy on those vampires."</p>
<p>Unity placed the arrow in her bow and pulled back, aiming at the crack.</p>
