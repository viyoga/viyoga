<p>The moment the disciples of the Celestial Gate Sect saw Kent, their expressions changed. Obviously, their enthusiasm had taken a hit.</p>
<p>They witnessed how brutal and powerful Kent was during the quarter-final battle against the Primal Beasts sect. Due to this, they all came to the conclusion that they would lose against him.</p>
<p>One can say that their tendency to give up easily shows weakness. However, there is no weakness involved when a mortal runs from a god.</p>
<p>To them, Kent’s strength is akin to a god.</p>
<p>He is overpowered, so they are afraid of him.</p>
<p>Kent just smiled and shook his head. The opponents had already given up before the battle even began.</p>
<p>Soon, Kent, Vanna, Nyss, Tom, and Ellis entered the arena to face the Celestial Gate Sect.</p>
<p>"Looks like you guys don’t feel well... Are you not feeling well?" Nyss asked, flashing a mocking smile. The disciples from the Celestial Immortal Sect grit their teeth, feeling helpless against the Supreme Immortal Sect.</p>
<p>’Just where did they get this monster from?’ That was the thought on their minds as they watched Kent slowly walk into the arena.</p>
<p>With Kent around, the Supreme Immortal Sect called for a team battle right off the bat. They wanted to get things done much faster.</p>
<p>"Nyss, stop teasing them. They are just not used to being the second-ranked sect," Kent replied, also flashing a mocking smile. "They will get used to it next time."</p>
<p>"I understand, big brother. In any case, I don’t blame them for being weak."</p>
<p>Of course, despite Kent’s strength, he never failed to enjoy the moment. Thankfully, the shameless Nyss was with him.</p>
<p>"Blaming them is useless. They have tried and reached second; that is good enough."</p>
<p>"True," Nyss looked at the disciples of the Celestial Gate Sect, "unlike some beasts who didn’t know their place, at least you golden-faced, handsome, and beautiful ones know when to concede. That shows guts and character growth."</p>
<p>"Who said we are conceding? In your dreams. You think we are conceding just because you have a cheat?" the captain of the Celestial Gate Sect said, her expression dark despite having a golden face.</p>
<p>Nyss, who heard her unexpected words, smirked. Kent also smiled, hearing them refer to him as a cheat.</p>
<p>"First of all, big brother is not a cheat. He is a person just like you and me. The only difference is that he is much more impressive, handsome, and brave than you.</p>
<p>Also, no need to put on a brave face. We both know I alone am enough to handle you seven," Nyss finished her sentence with a wink, causing everyone’s mouth to twitch.</p>
<p>The shamelessness was just too much.</p>
<p>Not that most of her words were wrong; she definitely had the right to defend her big brother. It was just that she had done so in the most exaggerated way possible.</p>
<p>Claiming she could defeat all seven of them without Kent’s help was already too much. However, her words seemed to have done the magic.</p>
<p>The seven disciples started to doubt themselves. They were being underestimated and looked down on by someone they knew, deep within their hearts, they could defeat. However, because she had a strong backer, they were now her prey.</p>
<p>’Damnit, why did such a monster come from that backwater sect? This was supposed to be my big win before I ascend... Now, lasting even a minute is not possible against him.</p>
<p>And this bitch, how I wish I could wipe that smirk off her face.’ Antlia, the captain of the Celestial Gate Sect, said inwardly.</p>
<p>"Elder, please start the match so we can get this over with." In the end, if she were forced to listen to Nyss much longer, she would explode, so she could only ask to start the battle that she knew deep down she would lose.</p>
<p>"I know we all know the rules. But one last warning, ’No killing.’"</p>
<p>After saying that, the elder stepped into the air to let the disciples have the field to fight in.</p>
<p>Kent stood at the back, allowing Vanna, Tom, Ellis, and Nyss to take the lead. They would handle the captain and three others, while he would take care of the rest.</p>
<p>"BEGIN!"</p>
<p>"Golden Dragon Strike!"</p>
<p>The moment the elder started the battle, the Celestial Gate Sect disciples decided to go all out by using one of their signature skills, the Golden Dragon Strike.</p>
<p>A large golden dragon made from spiritual qi suddenly appeared in the air and roared, causing the very air to shake and quake.</p>
<p>"Oh..."</p>
<p>Kent became intrigued when he saw this. The technique clearly had the potential to do some damage to the enemy. However, that wasn’t what intrigued Kent.</p>
<p>Antlia, the captain, was the one who activated the skill; however, the six remaining teammates standing behind her formed a hand seal, and all their spiritual energy was channeled into the dragon, making it appear much denser and extremely robust.</p>
<p>’So they can transfer their energy to one person, huh... Looks like this sect is decent. They have more potential than the Supreme Immortal Sect.’</p>
<p>Kent smiled and moved forward, creating a distance between himself and his team. "Stay back."</p>
<p>They nodded and hid behind him, knowing full well this attack was beyond them.</p>
<p>’Making them a direct competition to the Supreme Immortal Sect will force them to work hard.’ Kent smiled and extended his hand, forming a flame ball.</p>
<p>"This technique of yours is decent; however, mine is the real deal."</p>
<p>The flame ball in his hand formed into a small dragon, which slowly rose into the air and began to expand. In three seconds, just when the golden dragon was ready, his own green dragon made from flame was ready.</p>
<p>"Now, let’s see whose dragon is the best."</p>
<p>Kent gestured forward...</p>
<p>ROAR</p>
<p>The two dragons roared at the same time, causing both heat and spiritual energy to distort in the air. The heat spread to everyone, causing the disciples from both sides to pale in fear. The heat was just too much.</p>
<p>Kent waved his hand quickly, forming a shield over them.</p>
<p>He even protected the Celestial Gate disciples from the heat, showing he had no interest in hurting them.</p>
<p>Up in the air, the two dragons met, and the ending was decided instantly. Kent’s dragon easily tore through the golden dragon. After doing that, it rose into the air and gave one powerful roar before turning its gaze on the Celestial Gate disciples.</p>
<p>"Well... Should we continue?"</p>
<p>All seven disciples, despite being protected by the dome that Kent had created, felt their backs break out in sweat. The green dragon looked very terrifying.</p>
<p>Being stared at by the dragon was like being stared in the eye by death.</p>
<p>"We surrender," Antlia said, saving herself and her team the trauma. They were not ready for such a battle, not with their current drained bodies. One could see the thankful look in their eyes when she surrendered.</p>
<p>Kent smiled and gestured forward. The flame dragon moved toward him, and by the time it reached him, it had turned into a miniature dragon that landed on his palm and vanished.</p>
<p>The kind of control and flame essence that went into manifesting a flame dragon and controlling it flawlessly was something many could not do.</p>
<p>But thanks to Chaos, Kent could now do it. In fact, he could do more than that now.</p>
