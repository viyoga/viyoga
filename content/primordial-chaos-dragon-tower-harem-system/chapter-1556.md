<p>"Okay, Master, let’s get started," Tower said, pointing her finger at Kent’s forehead. Judging by how tiny she was compared to Kent’s colossal dragon body, even a mosquito was bigger.</p>
<p>But her size doesn’t matter in this regard. The moment she took action, Kent felt his body begin to change. He felt it—he was about to transcend so many stages of weakness, finally stepping into the rank of a True Chaos Dragon.</p>
<p>When Chaos had his first tribulation, he went from fighting Immortal Kings to Immortal Monarchs when he was merely a True Immortal. But when he formed the Chaos Heart, even demigods were within his capabilities, especially in moments when the chaos of the battle had reached the point where nobody was safe.</p>
<p>Tower remained in that state for ten minutes before a delighted expression suddenly appeared on her face.</p>
<p>"This is unreal," she muttered, fascination etched all over her face.</p>
<p>"What is it?" Kent asked.</p>
<p>"Master, this statue—it is beyond anything I have ever seen or imagined. It is like... I can’t even put it into words."</p>
<p>"Ah, that monster literally gave me an entire universe that resides in my soul. What makes you think he isn’t capable of doing more than that?"</p>
<p>"I suppose the Master is right. But this is beyond the soul universe, Master." Tower’s eyes sparkled. "This is like the masterpiece manual that has been designed to override everything, allowing you to unlock and awaken every last bit of your potential."</p>
<p>"Master, if before you were using 60% of your potential, then from now onward, this statue will push you to be at your absolute peak. You will even hate yourself if you aren’t performing at your best."</p>
<p>"My good luck, I suppose. That said, I really need all the help I can get, so we might as well use this now and think about how we can repay him later."</p>
<p>Tower nodded. "I suppose the Master is right. In that case, let’s get down to business."</p>
<p>She formed a hand seal, and deep inside Kent’s soul sea, the statue trembled and released waves of unknown energy.</p>
<p>"The Master will lose consciousness for the duration of the process. Rest assured, you are in good hands."</p>
<p>"Okay. I need some sleep after the torture I was put through by that damn—"</p>
<p>Tower knocked him out before he could finish his sentence. "Master talks too much."</p>
<p>She used her hand to cover her mouth and laugh. Afterward, she turned and looked at Vexthra and her sisters. Her gaze scanned them for a moment before landing on Ainna.</p>
<p>"Big Sister Ainna, please, I need your help."</p>
<p>"Huh?" Ainna turned and looked behind her, perhaps assuming Tower had made a mistake and was actually speaking to someone behind her. When she saw nobody there, she frowned.</p>
<p>"Me? What do you want from me?"</p>
<p>Tower smiled. "You are going to help me rebuild a body for my Master. Well, I need your supreme essence, but since it would be impolite to take it, I suppose you have to personally help me."</p>
<p>Ainna narrowed her eyes and was about to protest when Inaya grabbed her hand and pulled her forward, sending her flying toward Tower. "Ainna, just stop being petty and help my husband. I will owe you one."</p>
<p>"You—"</p>
<p>In the end, she sighed and glared at Tower just when she stopped a meter from her.</p>
<p>"What do you need me to do?"</p>
<p>"Very simple, Big Sister. When the Master sheds his scales, I will use the dragon spirit essence to further temper his body. You must use the supreme essence to do the same thing I will be doing."</p>
<p>"Hasn’t he already tempered his body?" she asked, a bit taken aback by Tower’s request.</p>
<p>"Yes, Master indeed has tempered his body. But it was only 60% done. Now, we have to complete the remaining 40% and make sure that when new scales are formed, he will become a mortal with a body worthy of a Supreme."</p>
<p>Ainna looked at Tower intently for a few minutes and then chuckled. "Tch. You and your Master will owe me a great deal. You had better pay up when I come knocking."</p>
<p>Tower smiled and nodded. "Rest assured, Big Sister, we will pay up with interest."</p>
<p>"I hope so."</p>
<p>Standing on the ground, Inaya smiled and turned to her sister. "Forgive her. She is just too petty."</p>
<p>"Oh, we are not mad." Vexthra smiled. "I, for one, would love to see just how hard she would fall when Kent gets his hands on her."</p>
<p>"Indeed." Miryssa nodded, a smirk forming at the corner of her lips.</p>
<p>Inaya just laughed.</p>
<p>"I wonder if that would ever happen. Ainna is the type of person who doesn’t know what she wants."</p>
<p>Vexthra placed her hand on Inaya’s shoulder and chuckled. "Leave everything to Selene. She will make sure she gets what she wants without her even knowing it."</p>
<p>They laughed.</p>
<p>Meanwhile, Ainna and Tower got to work.</p>
<p>A golden light surrounded Kent’s body for an hour before; suddenly, his scales started to fall off. "Big Sister, you can start when I give the command."</p>
<p>Ainna nodded and prepared.</p>
<p>Within ten minutes, every last scale came off Kent’s body.</p>
<p>The dragon spirit essence appeared, and Tower started using it to temper Kent’s body. Inside his soul sea, the golden statue emitted a brilliant light and began releasing golden runes, which flew in all directions.</p>
<p>"Big Sister, you can start now."</p>
<p>Ainna channeled her supreme essence and started tempering Kent’s colossal body. While she would have loved to torture him, she recognized this was an important moment for Kent and her friend Inaya, so she swallowed her anger and chose to do better work first.</p>
<p>After all, no matter how monstrous Kent becomes, he would be nowhere near the rank of a Supreme. So there would be plenty of time to get petty.</p>
<p>The process seemed to be going smoothly.</p>
<p>Once they were done, Kent would naturally form new scales, so for now, making his body strong enough was the priority. The golden statue was also doing its job—unlocking limits Kent hadn’t yet broken and awakening potentials he had yet to.</p>
<p>The process was affecting all his nature, be that of his dragon, sword, assassin, and other professions.</p>
<p>When he awakened, he wouldn’t be the same Kent anymore. His physique, mind, and soul would change, bringing out the best in him.</p>
<p>"I can’t wait to see the kind of monster he will become," Miryssa muttered, and her sister nodded.</p>
<p>Vexthra opened her mouth to say something, but she never got the chance. The next second, all three of them turned their gaze to the left, their vision piercing thousands of kilometers to look at three people who had just appeared through a secret teleportation gate.</p>
<p>A smile formed on their faces. "They are here."</p>
<p>"How should we handle this?" Inaya asked, summoning her sword as her evil Sword Dao ignited.</p>
<p>"Let’s capture all three of them and send them to the Tower Prisons for now."</p>
<p>Inaya and Miryssa nodded and vanished. Vexthra remained to stand guard.</p>
<p>---</p>
<p>Meanwhile, while everything was happening, Kent appeared in the middle of a desert, the scorching hot sun burning his scalp. "Ah, my bad luck. I only wanted to take a nap. Is that too much of an ask?"</p>
<p>He looked around for a few seconds before a frown appeared on his face.</p>
<p>He asked in a lazy tone, "Why am I here?"</p>
<p>"To see me, of course," a voice came from behind him. Turning around, his gaze landed on the last person he wanted to see.</p>
<p>"Ruthless Bandit..."</p>
