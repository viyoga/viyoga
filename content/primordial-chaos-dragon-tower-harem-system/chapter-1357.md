<p>It took some time to calm Dia down.</p>
<p>The moment she killed Pilio, it felt like a huge load had been taken off her. This brought back all the pent-up emotions she had buried deep in her heart over the years.</p>
<p>Now that she had avenged the entire elven race and her family, all forms of authority held by the Dark Elves had broken, freeing her to live a life of freedom.</p>
<p>Of course, there are still some dark elves in the other cities. However, they aren’t a threat, not when the head of their leader would be hanged at the entrance of the city soon.</p>
<p>So basically, she had nothing else to fear.</p>
<p>"Are you alright?" Selene asked, giving her a hand so she could stand up.</p>
<p>Dia nodded and turned to look at the headless body of the Dark Elf King she had killed. A few seconds later, a smile appeared on her face.</p>
<p>Yes, she had avenged her parents, loved ones, and every last Elf who fell to the sword of this wicked dark elf. They had caused so much damage, but now, their era of tyranny is over.</p>
<p>"Bastard." She stomped on the headless body.</p>
<p>"Well, that is just overdoing it, Dia. He is already dead," Selene said, maintaining her usual giggle.</p>
<p>"Sorry. Even though he is dead, I still find him hateful and extremely ugly."</p>
<p>"He does look ugly by all standards. However, he is dead now, so why don’t we go back? I am sure your people would love to see you alive and well..."</p>
<p>"You are right." Dia nodded, then she turned and looked at Uncle Drew and the others, "But first, can you please help me free the other Elves still prisoners and slaves to the Dark Elves."</p>
<p>For a group of ten to be able to bring an entire city to its knees, Dia knew they were no simple people, so she had better take advantage of their generosity.</p>
<p>Of course, she also, in a strange way, knows Selene and the others would do anything she asks. It may be a gut feeling, but she knew they would help, no matter what she asked of them, as long as it was not below their moral code.</p>
<p>"About that," Selene turned to Vari, who merely gave her a nod.</p>
<p>Selene nodded and reached out to Vexthra. A second later, the whole gang teleported to the city, startling Dia.</p>
<p>However, what shook her even more was the blonde who jumped into her arms and started crying all over her chest. Unity’s speed was just too fast for her to track, so before she knew it, she was crying on her chest.</p>
<p>Dia is taller than Unity, so her face was between her boobs, soaking her wet. Despite the bloodstains and the dirt on her clothes, Unity didn’t seem to care.</p>
<p>Dia was startled.</p>
<p>However, before she could even think about wiggling her way out of Unity’s arms, a feeling of love and happiness suddenly assaulted her, making her stop pushing away and start patting Unity on the back instead.</p>
<p>She didn’t understand where those feelings came from, but she didn’t hate them.</p>
<p>After about 10 minutes, Unity finally calmed down. Still, with tears rolling down her face, she raised her head and looked at Dia’s face.</p>
<p>"I-I missed you so much, sister."</p>
<p>Dia tried to raise her brow and perhaps say she had no sister left, that both her sisters and parents were brutally murdered. However, those words vanished from her mind, and the only thing she could think about or feel was how happy she was at the moment.</p>
<p>Unity already had memories of her past life as Artemis, so she knew the face staring at her was her sister, Hera. She knew deep within her that she was her.</p>
<p>Dia, on the other hand, had no idea who Unity was. However, deep within her, she knew this lady was important to her. But even if Unity is nothing to her, the fact that she soaked her clothes with her tears made her know she was going through some things, so it is better to consider her feelings for now.</p>
<p>This was even why she let a smile appear on her face: "You are so beautiful," she said, making Unity smile as well.</p>
<p>"You are also beautiful. Just a little bit dirty..."</p>
<p>"Yeah. I was captured and tortured for three days, so I never had time to bathe and change clothes." Dia’s smile brightened, unaware that just by talking with Unity, her personality from her past life was slowly resurfacing.</p>
<p>Unity took her hand and turned toward her sister, "You have already met Selene and Vari."</p>
<p>Dia nodded...</p>
<p>"The rest are all my sisters and friends. This is my big sister Vexthra. Aside from my husband, she is the next most important person in my life."</p>
<p>"Really, Unity..." The rest of her sisters protested, clearly feeling that Unity is a bit biased. Vexthra, on the other hand, was very happy to hear what Unity said. She may be a goddess, but when with her sisters, she is rather down to earth.</p>
<p>Unity merely smiled and spent the next couple of minutes introducing everyone. Her family is big and lively, so she made sure Dia understood the kind of people she would be spending time with from now on.</p>
<p>"Thank you all for coming to my aid when I desperately needed it."</p>
<p>"Don’t worry about it, Dia," Vexthra said, stepping forward. She took her other hand and spoke in a reassuring tone, "Now that we are here, we will do everything in our power to make sure you and your people are forever free from the tyranny of all who seek to destroy."</p>
<p>Dia nodded.</p>
<p>Vexthra turned toward Uncle Drew, "The mission is now complete, so the restriction has been lifted. As long as you don’t use your demigod powers, you are free to kill as many as you want.</p>
<p>Go to this marked place and make sure every last dark elf is slaughtered. Save the Elves and bring them to this city for migration into the tower."</p>
<p>"Migration?" Dia asked with a raised brow.</p>
<p>"Yes, sis, this place is ruined, so we are relocating you and everyone to a much more beautiful place. Of course, we will first take you there, and if you like it, then we can proceed."</p>
<p>Dia looked around for a few minutes before she nodded. She could see the state of the city, and, as people who prefer nature and beauty, this place is neither hygienic nor suitable for them to live in.</p>
<p>"Alright, get to work."</p>
<p>Uncle Drew took charge from Kaizo and started dividing the teams into 12, since they had to attack 12 places. A minute later, all 12 teams had been formed.</p>
<p>"See you later, Dia," Selene said before jumping onto one of Yi Lai’s undead dragons, and they left. All twelve teams used mounts.</p>
<p>Soon, only Unity, Elaine, Vexthra, and Dia remained. Elaine was healing her injuries while Unity merely kept holding her.</p>
<p>As for Vexthra, she knew she had to commission Gaia to expand Nature City to accommodate the new Elves they would be bringing into the Tower.</p>
<p>A few minutes later, the four of them vanished and appeared in the Mountain Garden, where Gaia and her band of troublemakers were having a tea party.</p>
