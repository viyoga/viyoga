<p>The battle that happened at the Hound Barracks and the Lionkin stronghold has generated a rather sizable income in the form of Tower points.</p>
<p>Currently, Kent is swimming in Tower points.</p>
<p>So, his first order of business was to upgrade his 12 Dungeon Bosses. Each one of the dungeons requires 100 million Tower points to upgrade from SS to SSS.</p>
<p>This means that the moment the dungeons are upgraded, more levels will be added, and the strength of the last floor bosses will also increase.</p>
<p>They will be at the peak of the Core Formation stage, something he needed for his mission to save Planet Dusk. He wanted them ready for battle any day.</p>
<p>"The plan at first was for me to come out of seclusion before upgrading you all, but knowing I will be gone for a while, I will do that now.</p>
<p>This means the dungeons will be upgraded, and as such, you all will also get a boost in your strength. So, your first task after the upgrades is to use the dungeons to train, sharpen your skills, and wait for me to emerge from seclusion.</p>
<p>The moment I finish my tasks, I will take you to a new place where we will encounter some of the strongest beings you can face in a medium world.</p>
<p>Of course, we have already faced armies from a high world, so you all would think armies from a medium world are beneath you.</p>
<p>But I beg to differ. The dungeon we will face is one of the most dangerous places you can enter in a medium world, so be prepared."</p>
<p>They all nodded.</p>
<p>Kent wasn’t exaggerating.</p>
<p>The quest to save Planet Dusk is something he knew would be harrowing, so he aspires to raise the strength of his legion first. He had a feeling the danger waiting for them was not going to be easy.</p>
<p>"The moment I start the upgrade, you all will go to sleep for a while, so you can enter your dungeons now." The Dungeon Bosses nodded.</p>
<p>Kent already knew of Valtor’s condition and knew that after the upgrade, he would wake up, stronger than ever. Once the Dungeon Bosses left, Kent turned to the Undead.</p>
<p>"Akira will see to your upgrades. Yours too will take some time, so when you are done, use the dungeons to train. And Zareth, you can use the chance to unlock more of your inheritance.</p>
<p>I saw what you can do even against Spirit Ascension stage experts. I have a feeling that the mission we will embark on after I am out of seclusion will require you to be at your strongest, so you should all prepare well.</p>
<p>Gaia will add eight more friends while I am away, so make them feel comfortable." The Undead Legions nodded.</p>
<p>Kent turned to the cocoon housing the Hunter, who was now undergoing her evolution to become a Dragon Necromancer, someone who can summon only undead dragons.</p>
<p>Kent had a feeling she would wake up with a surprise, so he asked his Undead Legion to pay attention to her when she is out and make her feel welcome while he is away.</p>
<p>After he was done with them, he went to see his three assistants.</p>
<p>⟪We will miss you, master.⟫ Gaia said, clinging to Kent’s leg, not wanting him to leave. Akira was doing the same to his other leg, while Dori held his clothes.</p>
<p>"Relax, you three, it is not like I am going away for good. I will be inside the Sword Tower, the Soul Tower, or the Alchemy Tower. You can come and watch me if you want," Kent said with a laugh.</p>
<p>⟪That is not the same. We would miss playing wrestling with you,⟫ Dori said. He knew they enjoyed wrestling with him, but hearing them say it made him sigh inwardly.</p>
<p>"Then how about this? When I am out of seclusion, we can wrestle for an entire week and even go ice skating too... if you can build one."</p>
<p>⟪Really?⟫ They all asked.</p>
<p>"Yes. I’ll play with you all for as long as you’d like. So, what do you say?" Kent asked, and the three lolis smiled cheerfully.</p>
<p>⟪It is a deal, master. We will wait for you to emerge from seclusion. And don’t worry, we will handle the undead creations while you are away.⟫</p>
<p>Kent nodded at their words.</p>
<p>They had eight more undead to create using the undead schematic. As for resources, he had more than enough to make them very powerful, so Kent didn’t doubt they would indeed get the job done well.</p>
<p>"If you are bored, you can go to Fedora and play with the little fox princess. I am sure she will be happy to meet Lady Tala’s friends," Kent said with a laugh before vanishing from the mountain garden.</p>
<p>He knew her sisters would torment her to tell them all about Lady Tala, so he left before she could set her murderous sight on him.</p>
<p>The first thing Kent did when he entered the Sword Tower was to sacrifice 1.2 billion Tower points to upgrade the dungeons.</p>
<p>⟦Congratulations on upgrading your dungeons from SS ↠ SSS⟧</p>
<p>⟦Upgrade will take 120 days⟧</p>
<p>⟦The floors of the dungeons will increase from 10 ↠ 17⟧</p>
<p>"Looks like this will keep them busy for the next couple of months while I am away." Kent nodded with satisfaction and then checked his remaining balance.</p>
<p>[Attribute Points: 1,090,900]</p>
<p>[Tower Points: 970,400,000]</p>
<p>[Skill Upgrade Points: 911,100]</p>
<p>⟦Soul Essence: 1,270,900,600⟧</p>
<p>A smile appeared on his lips. "It has already been a couple of hours, but it seems the Foxkins are much harder working than I thought."</p>
<p>Watching the Soul Essence grow was enough to make him happy. It turned out that he hadn’t wasted his wealth for nothing.</p>
<p>After ensuring everything was in order, he turned his attention to his tasks.</p>
<p>"First, I will review Jest’s memories, all three of them, and then move on to the Chaos Dragons’ memories, and then the Faceless Assassin.</p>
<p>Afterward, I will handle the Soul Path before ending with Alchemy. By then, my wives will be out of seclusion, and the legion will become stronger.</p>
<p>They can even start handling the dungeon in Dusk while I find my way into the Supreme Immortal Sect and challenge the Pill Princess.</p>
<p>I only hope she lives up to the hype, for I won’t be coming out a wannabe Alchemist. It is time for the Perfect Alchemist to rise and dominate the entire universe."</p>
<p>Kent sighed and retrieved the first memory key...</p>
