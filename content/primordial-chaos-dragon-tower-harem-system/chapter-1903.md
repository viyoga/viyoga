<p>Sometime later, after making sure he spent some more time with his wives, Kent was ready to head over to the Flame Paradise with the mission of retrieving his Blood Spear, the weapon he wielded during the Blood Era.</p>
<p>Of course, he didn’t plan on going alone this time.</p>
<p>He would be going with Alina, whom he planned to help complete her Battlefield Matrix, the next-generation formation he planned to build into something this universe had never seen before.</p>
<p>The more Kent heard about her plans, the more intrigued he became. He would be travelling with her this time, and hopefully, by the time they returned, she would be able to complete the first phase.</p>
<p>Before leaving, however, he made sure to put measures in place for Kaizo to start his training.</p>
<p>It was just 100 years.</p>
<p>However, even if the time difference inside the tower made it several hundred years for him, that was still not enough time to train. Then again, it was Kaizo; even if he had a year to prepare, he would be ready in 6 months’ time.</p>
<p>"I know the kind of warrior you are, Kaizo, so I won’t bother telling you how serious the mission we have in 100 years’ time is. But to make sure you are aware, train well because no matter what, the duel will be a next-level kind of danger, so do your best to break your limits before I return."</p>
<p>"I will do that and some more before you return, Master."</p>
<p>Kent smiled and nodded. If it were Kaizo, he’d be okay knowing he wouldn’t slack off. Luckily for him, there were several dungeons he could train in.</p>
<p>He wouldn’t lack, not even for a moment.</p>
<p>After he finished the other things, he was ready to leave with Alina.</p>
<p>"Good luck, and take care of Alina. If something happens to her, you will answer to me." Fiannarel warned before she teleported away.</p>
<p>Although she was still shy, Fiannarel came to bid Kent farewell as he was ready to leave with Alina.</p>
<p>"Rest well knowing I will not allow anything to happen to her." Kent smiled, and then, after a few more hugs and kisses with his wives, he left...</p>
<p>The Flame Paradise was located somewhere in the Supreme Realm. Luckily for Kent, he could travel to all levels of worlds in the universe without any restrictions.</p>
<p>This meant going to the Supreme Realm was as easy as eating candy for him.</p>
<p>And since he had no restrictions, he could take as many people as he wanted...</p>
<p>So, after leaving the tower, he tapped into the connection he shared with his Shadow Fiends and, using it, opened a portal to Planet Nuhan, one of the most powerful Supreme Planets in the universe.</p>
<p>It was home to mainly warriors, the deadly and unhinged kind.</p>
<p>The gateway to the Flame Paradise is located there, so the kind of people who frequent it are warriors with nothing to lose. Every single day, thousands enter that dreadful place, and every single day, thousands die there, yet more will come the next day.</p>
<p>The rewards they earn from adventuring there are just too good to the point that they mostly disregard their own safety.</p>
<p>Kent knew that entering the Flame Paradise would be hard, especially when he and Alina were just demigods. The minimum requirement to enter is Supreme.</p>
<p>So, knowing that, he asked someone else to come with him.</p>
<p>The moment he stepped out of the portal, he sensed the presence of a Supreme approaching him... "Sorry for calling you out of your seclusion, Uncle Drake."</p>
<p>The flame dragon, who once challenged the Primordial Chaos Dragon, hoping to suppress him and make him his subordinate, smiled and replied, "No need to be apologetic, Chaos. I myself want to seclude myself in the Flame Paradise if I want to be ready for when the Blood Race comes."</p>
<p>"Oh." Kent furrowed his brow. "Are you planning to participate in the deathmatch?"</p>
<p>"Not really. But I know enough to realise that this challenge is merely a trap. The main event will take place behind the scenes while the challenge is happening, so I am preparing for that."</p>
<p>Kent smiled and nodded. "As they say, great minds think alike."</p>
<p>The first thought that came to mind when he heard the challenge was that it was a trap. There was no way the Blood Race would, after their defeat during the Blood Era, return only to propose something that simple.</p>
<p>So, hearing he wasn’t the only one who saw through their antics made him happy. "Then we can travel together for now. When we get there, we can go our separate ways."</p>
<p>Uncle Drake was okay with that, so they soon left for the city where one of the gateways was located.</p>
<p>"Good to see you again, Fairy Alina. I hope you have been well." Uncle Drake turned to Alina and said, a smile tugging at the corner of his lips.</p>
<p>He was more familiar with Kent’s wives because he knew them during their time in Sonox, the medium world Kent was transported to after he died on Earth.</p>
<p>"I have been great, Uncle Drake. What about yourself?"</p>
<p>"Better than I had hoped, and soon, I am sure I will be even better." He laughed, and Alina laughed with him...</p>
<p>Kent just smirked, knowing Uncle Drake would return a monster from the Flame Paradise, but he would surely walk along the thin line between life and death several times.</p>
<p>His training regimen was always the harshest and most dangerous among the Dragon Order.</p>
<p>Perhaps that was the reason why he was the second strongest in the Dragon Order after the Chaos Dragon.</p>
<p>"Have you already started selecting the 9 others you will be going with for the Blood Challenge?" Along the way, Uncle Drake asked...</p>
<p>"Yes. I have already selected Kaizo and Neomi."</p>
<p>"Oh, the Mother of Killers herself." Uncle Drake smiled and added, "Looks like you are taking the Blood Challenge very seriously."</p>
<p>"Since I have planned to crush them to a pulp, then I might as well pull out all my aces in that challenge. Kaizo is a menace, and so is Neomi. Naturally, when the time comes, they will understand the kind of monsters they will be facing."</p>
<p>Kent grinned and added, "I myself will be teaching them a good lesson 100 years from now."</p>
