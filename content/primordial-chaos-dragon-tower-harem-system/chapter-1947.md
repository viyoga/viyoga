<p>Emmet’s body trembled, and her aura changed, now filled with something far more dreadful.</p>
<p>Kent’s smile froze, then his Omniscient Dao Eyes activated, but instead of seeing five seconds into the future, he saw just a second and a half, and it was of his death.</p>
<p>Just then, a tail attack arrived before him, and before he could even make a read of the tail attack, a second passed, leaving only half a second remaining.</p>
<p>"Fuck!"</p>
<p>Kent tapped into his Chaos Scale Shields and was about to coat his entire body with thick draconic armor when a hand reached from behind and patted him on the shoulder.</p>
<p>Kent’s already frozen body grew cold, not because of the coldness of the hand that touched it, but more like the killing intent in that touch froze his blood.</p>
<p>However, just when he expected his end to come at that moment, the person moved past him.</p>
<p>Kent only saw his long, crimson-colored, disheveled hair. However, that didn’t change his frozen state. It was only when the person turned and smiled back at him that his body lightened up.</p>
<p>"Oh, dear Heavens, you really are back, Master."</p>
<p>Kent’s brows knitted, then realization dawned on him... "Y-You are Sanguine?"</p>
<p>"In the flesh."</p>
<p>Kent’s eyes widened.</p>
<p>Just now, he saw his own death, yet the second and a half had long passed, and he remained alive. What did that mean?</p>
<p>In short, it meant the person now smiling at him was so powerful that his mere presence could influence life and death.</p>
<p>His presence could determine who lives and who dies, and there could never be a second opinion on that. Whatever he said was final.</p>
<p>To have such influence only meant one thing: the person’s understanding of the Dao of Life and Death was so deep that his mere presence could bend life and death to his will.</p>
<p>Kent wanted to say something, but Sanguine raised his finger to stop him for a moment... "Just a sec, Master."</p>
<p>He turned slowly and locked eyes with Emmet, whose body was frozen in place, her expression pale as ice... "Revenge is a bitch, isn’t she?" An evil smile formed on Sanguine’s face.</p>
<p>Sanguine’s hand reached forward, and Emmet was pulled forward, her head entering Sanguine’s arm. Her body trembled, but she was powerless to free herself from Sanguine’s grip.</p>
<p>"You clearly never expected this day to come, did you?" Sanguine’s evil smile grew wider. "For one and a half era, you had me chained down in your basement, never even dreaming of a day when my majestic self would be free. Now that I am free, what do you suppose I should do with you?"</p>
<p>Emmet wanted to say something, but she couldn’t even open her lips to say the words that were caught in her throat.</p>
<p>"Don’t kill her." Suddenly, Kent’s words reached Sanguine, causing him to turn sharply and look at Kent.</p>
<p>"Huh, why, Master?"</p>
<p>"She will be more useful alive than dead."</p>
<p>"But she had tortured me for years, Master." Sanguine frowned deeply, but Kent didn’t change his mind.</p>
<p>"I will compensate you for that. However, keeping her alive, as I said, will prove more useful than leaving her dead."</p>
<p>Sanguine looked at Emmet for a few seconds before he sighed and released his grip on her head. "Thank my master with all your soul; after all, he is the only person who can make me change my mind, and now he has used that power to free you."</p>
<p>Kent sighed, knowing he was a bit selfish for this one.</p>
<p>If he had suffered at the hands of someone for millions of years, the first thing he would do after he was freed would be to kill the person.</p>
<p>Denying Sanguine that satisfaction was already bad enough.</p>
<p>However, as he said, she would prove more helpful alive than dead.</p>
<p>"Relax, Sanguine, between her and the Blood Race, I am sure you would enjoy killing the latter more."</p>
<p>Sanguine took a deep breath, then a smile spread across his face. "I heard they are back and even dare to challenge the master."</p>
<p>"They sure did." Kent also let a smile appear on his face, both happy that Sanguine was back and that he still saw him as his master, despite being even more powerful than Mjolnir.</p>
<p>Sanguine looked at Emmet and said:</p>
<p>"Now that I look at things, you are indeed a small fry." He looked at Kent and asked, "What should I do with her?"</p>
<p>In response, Kent extended both hands and manifested two different red seals.</p>
<p>"The left one is the Blood Thrall Seal. Those I put this on will lose all free will and are destined to die whenever I wish for it. In normal times, I don’t even tell them they will die from this seal. I just put it in them.</p>
<p>However, this one is the Blood Warrior Seal. It grants you the power of blood and transforms you into a warrior capable of walking the blood path with me. But it must be taken willingly, or else it will backfire, and you will die violently."</p>
<p>Kent grinned, an aura of evil emanating from his body.</p>
<p>"Tell me, Emmet, which one do you want?"</p>
<p>Five minutes later, she chose the Blood Warrior Seal, and Kent placed it on her. However, he didn’t activate it for her to undergo her transformation and become a Blood Warrior.</p>
<p>"For your first mission, before your transformation, go aid my wives and legion in annihilating the Grim Arcane Legends."</p>
<p>Emmet found herself moving through a thick void, and then she appeared inside a majestic place where 14 arenas hung in the air.</p>
<p>Before she could examine this place further, the space around her twisted again, and she found herself on a chaotic battlefield.</p>
<p>’Prove to me you are capable of being my Blood Warrior.’ Kent’s voice echoed in her head.</p>
<p>Just then, 3 km from her location, Vexthra was pushed back by a Celestial, and from behind her, another Celestial lunged forward, his claws poised for a deadly blow.</p>
<p>Emmet immediately felt like saving this lady from dying was what she must do. So she moved, and before the claws could land on Vexthra, a spear formed from lightning stabbed into the head of the Celestial who was trying to land the sneak attack.</p>
<p>Her other hand caught Vexthra before she could slam into the boulder.</p>
<p>"Huh, who are you?" Vexthra asked, her eyes widening.</p>
