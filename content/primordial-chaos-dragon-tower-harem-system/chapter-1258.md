<p>It took Kent just 3 hours to go through all of the forsaken seers’ and the other forsaken immortals’ memories.</p>
<p>Since she was merely a clone, the memories she had were only from when she was made into a clone. However, that was enough for him to know the real Atarah.</p>
<p>"As expected from a Forsaken Seer, she really is crafty." The little he had gotten about her true self, Kent saw she had already anticipated her death, and as such, she wiped most of the vital information from her mind.</p>
<p>She wiped the location of the forsaken world she is from. She wiped what she looked like or even what she sounded like.</p>
<p>In fact, the only reason Kent is even sure she exists is the fact that he had killed her clone. Her planning was rather detailed and airtight, making it impossible for Kent to figure out where she was from.</p>
<p>Even the other forsaken immortal is basically useless. Aside from the forsaken art Kent found, everything in his mind was messed with by the Forsaken Seer.</p>
<p>However, there was one thing Kent found that was worth it. His suffering wasn’t in vain after finding that.</p>
<p>"She may not have thought it was necessary, however, to someone like me, this is the best." Kent smiled, taking out the destiny map.</p>
<p>He activated it and panned in his galaxy. Then, as if looking for a needle in a haystack, he started examining the map. After five minutes, he sighs and flicks his own forehead.</p>
<p>"Dumb me. I have a sentient tower, and here I was searching for something extremely hidden," he laughed, and then he asked the tower for help.</p>
<p>"Tower, help me locate Lanster Barbarian Star System."</p>
<p>The map rotated and stopped on a small part of the map.</p>
<p>"Enlarge and activate a 3D model of the entire star system."</p>
<p>The map moved again, but this time it expanded and transformed into a star system with just 4 planetary systems.</p>
<p>In each planetary system, there are only ten planets. Naturally, that isn’t enough to make a planetary system. A planetary system requires at least 50 planets, and a star system requires close to 15 planetary systems.</p>
<p>However, looking at the enormous sizes of the planets in each planetary system, one can only see why only four planetary systems managed to form a star system.</p>
<p>"Tower, let’s do some calculations here, shall we?"</p>
<p>[I am up for it, master]</p>
<p>Kent nodded and asked, "Take my current location to be Planet Bayla. Now, plot a course from there to the edge of the galaxy and tell me how long it would take to travel there—factor in teleportation gates, teleportation channels, and other methods that can hasten the travel.</p>
<p>When done, also take the Lanster Barbarian Star System, specifically the Bone Dragon Planetary System, into account.</p>
<p>Examine how long that would take if I were to travel there instead of going the previous routes we have mapped."</p>
<p>Kent had seen something in Atarah’s memories that, if he could harness its power, he would be saving several years of travel in his quest to cross over to the other galaxy.</p>
<p>Getting to Elina is a must; however, he must also make sure he doesn’t waste time travelling by any old route.</p>
<p>If he can find a way to reach where he wanted to go faster by passing through even dangerous routes, as someone who is already undisputed in the High Worlds, Kent is up for it.</p>
<p>He wants to get to her fast and hopefully make sure she is okay before focusing on other things.</p>
<p>The tower soon finished her calculations.</p>
<p>[After factoring in all that the master had said, I calculated that to move from Planet Bayla to the edge of the galaxy would take about 25 to 30 years.</p>
<p>However, from Planet Bayla to the Bone Dragon Planetary System would only take 12 years, and from there to the edge of the galaxy would take no more than 6 years.]</p>
<p>A beaming smile appeared on Kent’s face, hearing what the tower had said. The reason for this was because of something else he had seen in Atarah’s memories.</p>
<p>It turned out the dragons she brought were a small clan from a planet called Yaohe. This planet is in the Bone Dragon Planetary System, which is one of the four planetary systems in the Lanster Barbarian Star System.</p>
<p>They were being suppressed there by a different Flame Dragon Race, which is the primary ruling race on the planet Yaohe.</p>
<p>So when she offered them the deal, they accepted, and she took them away through a teleportation portal in an underground cave somewhere on Planet Yaohe.</p>
<p>If Kent could build the same teleportation portal on his side, then with the coordinates in his mind, he would be on Planet Yaohe in less than 6 weeks instead of 12 years.</p>
<p>"Tower, examine the Six Fangs Teleportation Portal Formation and tell me how long it would take you or Gaia to build it."</p>
<p>[The master will need the fangs of six different beasts. Once that is found, Cosmic Gaia will need about 5 years (outside time) to build it. Of course, it is going to cost billions of tower points, but that can be arranged later.]</p>
<p>The smile on Kent’s face didn’t falter. If anything, he seems even more eager to get right to things. Of course, the sneaky Forsaken Seer wiped the name of the six beasts from her mind as a precaution.</p>
<p>However, the tower is there for moments like this.</p>
<p>"Tell me the names of the six beasts."</p>
<p>[The master must hunt and kill the Six Beasts and harvest their fangs. Their names are as follows: Void Howler Beast, Abyssal Frost Serpent, Solar-Pelt Lion of the First Dawn, Chrono-Jaguar of the Lost Hour, Thunderbone Basilisk, Netherlight Devourer.</p>
<p>Luckily, three of the beasts can be found on Planet Bayla. In fact, the beasts that must be killed as one of the requirements to become a Pill Emperor can be found there, too.</p>
<p>So, your next stop, Planet Bayla, you will make much more progress there. Both the Void Howler Beast, Abyssal Frost Serpent, and Solar-Pelt Lion of the First Dawn live in a perilous forest where your strength will be tested, and you will come close to death’s door many times.]</p>
<p>Kent smiled, a memory of his life as the faceless assassin resurfacing. "The Upside Down Underworld Forest."</p>
<p>[Indeed. Just like Planet Modu, that forest is akin to a lawless domain, where the limits of a High World don’t apply.</p>
<p>But compared to Planet Modu, I will advise the master to be prepared for anything.]</p>
<p>"You don’t have to spell it out to me, Tower. I have lived on Planet Bayla before and have been to the Upside Down Underworld Forest. So I know the kind of danger I will be walking into."</p>
<p>Kent stood up and stretched... "But I am ready to face anything if it makes getting to Elina faster."</p>
<p>There is nothing else to look for since the four foxkins are dead. They had already been used as vessels to summon four forsaken beings.</p>
<p>"I will get revenge for them later." Kent teleported away, appearing in the Soul Tower to see what he could do using his newest ability.</p>
<p>The Soul Weapon ability.</p>
