<p>[Congratulations, your blood has evolved to the next stage: Golden-red Overlord Blood]</p>
<p>Kent remained seated, his eyes widened as he stared at the message before him. The idea that he was now an Overlord just seemed to strike him as a prank.</p>
<p>He was a beast, and so he understood that reaching this stage required a lot. Overlords were beings whose presence commanded absolute respect. No matter how weak they might be, their aura alone could compel just about anyone to bow before them.</p>
<p>Kent had even read that Overlords became more powerful as more beasts submitted to them. So, to be an Overlord was to be someone whose authority was absolute and strong enough to lead.</p>
<p>In his past life, Chaos had only reached the Monarch stage, but even then, he was respected by the people he led into many battles.</p>
<p>Now, he was an Overlord, and just as his blood had evolved to that of an Overlord, his bloodline started to awaken beyond the stage it was before coming to this world.</p>
<p>Back when he formed his first Chaos Heart in the Infinity Universe, his bloodline had risen to 35% awakened. Now, it had started to rise.</p>
<p>In only a few minutes, it rose by 5%.</p>
<p>But with that 5% increase, his presence became even more lethal to comprehend.</p>
<p>Suddenly, Kent felt some people appear behind him. He first closed the panel, stood up, then turned, hoping to see Jyoti Lata and the rest of the leaders of the Red Bone Chaos Tribe.</p>
<p>However, when his gaze landed on the seven tribe leaders, he felt his body tremble. Before panic could set in, his aura flared, and the fear vanished.</p>
<p>&lt;You are immune to all forms of intimidation from Eternals and below&gt;</p>
<p>The message brought a smile to his face. However, before he could address the seven leaders, Fiannarel appeared, and the moment their gazes met, Kent froze.</p>
<p>For five full minutes, he stood motionless, his gaze locked intently on Fiannarel. But while he was frozen, Fiannarel was also rooted to the spot, tears rolling down her face.</p>
<p>Then, suddenly, they both moved, tears streaming down their faces. In what could be considered the most emotional reunion ever, Fiannarel jumped into Kent’s arms.</p>
<p>Perhaps if Kent had chosen his human form, he would have been the one to jump into Fiannarel’s arms, since they are almost at the same height.</p>
<p>"Big sister," Kent said, tears rolling down his face.</p>
<p>Seeing this, the seven tribe leaders left the scene to give the siblings a chance to catch up.</p>
<p>Just now, the moment Kent and Fiannarel gazed at each other, his mind was flooded with memories of the life he had lived during the Sun Era, the era that witnessed one of the greatest wars and the rise of the greatest warrior ever to wield the bow.</p>
<p>They called him the Omniscience Dao Archer, an Elven Prince, and the beloved son of the wisest Elven Empress in the history of the universe.</p>
<p>He was the youngest of four siblings and the one who led the battle against the Sun Demons, completely annihilating them down to the last warrior.</p>
<p>The Omniscience Dao Archer was someone with the sight to see both the past and the future and shoot his arrow either way. In other words, he could kill his opponents by firing his arrows into the past, present, or future.</p>
<p>His existence made no sense. In fact, his overpowered nature completely contracted his image in many minds. It was common knowledge that, while growing up, aside from jumping from one woman to another, he was basically useless...</p>
<p>Ir turned out that was the image he showed everyone...</p>
<p>Everyone except one person, his big sister, Fiannarel.</p>
<p>She was already a general in the Elven Army and highly respected.</p>
<p>One day, a rumor began spreading that she had a second identity, called Scarlet Fingers.</p>
<p>During the Sun Era, Elven merchants who traded with other races began encountering bandits while transporting goods from one planet to another.</p>
<p>When this started, many merchants hired guards to protect them, but every time, the bandits got the better of them.</p>
<p>That was until someone mysterious started helping them. Initially, no one suspected General Fiannarel, who preferred to be called General rather than Princess.</p>
<p>However, as more people experienced the devastating power of this mysterious archer, they began to wonder whether it was General Fiannarel, known for her impeccable bow skills.</p>
<p>Soon, many began spreading the word that General Fiannarel was the Scarlet Fingers.</p>
<p>However, when she heard this and realized she was not the one, she began searching for the person behind it. Soon, she realized she was making no progress, so she decided to disguise herself.</p>
<p>She disguised herself as a merchant, and that day, she finally came face to face with the Scarlet Fingers, who, to her shock, was her useless brother, using her name and skills to get out of trouble.</p>
<p>She was so shocked that she nearly collapsed. But when she recovered and questioned him, she found out her brother desired peace, but he also hated seeing their people die at the hands of bandits.</p>
<p>So he created a secret identity to help.</p>
<p>When she questioned how he could wield the bow like a pro yet she had never seen him train, considering he spends most of his time chasing ladies or sleeping, his answer left her jaw hanging.</p>
<p>"I train in my dreams, big sister. Whenever I sleep, I find myself on battlefields where I have to fight, or else I wake up feeling like I’ve been hammered into submission. So I train in my sleep."</p>
<p>She knew her brother desired peace, so she made him her Shadow General. This way, he could serve in the army while continuing to live his carefree life.</p>
<p>However, when the Sun Demons came and death spread everywhere, the Omniscience Dao Archer appeared and changed everything.</p>
<p>The entire universe witnessed what he was capable of, and Fiannarel finally saw the kind of person her little brother truly was.</p>
<p>Seeing him carry the entire universe on his back, she made a vow to ensure he succeeds and has a happy ending. That was why, during the war, when the danger reached a certain point, she decided to go all out with no intention of failing.</p>
<p>In the end, instead of death, she survived and found herself transported to a strange world.</p>
<p>For the first few million years, she blamed herself, feeling like a deserter. After all, she had promised her brother that she would stand by his side until the very end. If she had died, she would not have blamed herself, for she had given it her all.</p>
<p>However, she survived, and what was even worse was that she could not return to her world, no matter how hard she tried. That had eaten away at her sanity for years.</p>
<p>However, over time, she came to accept her new life.</p>
<p>Never in her wildest dreams did she think the brother she had thought about every single day for the past 13 billion years would now be in her arms.</p>
<p>"Is it really you, Kalimndra?"</p>
<p>She asked, referring to Kent by the name he was known by during the Sun Era.</p>
<p>"It is me, big sister. I missed you so much," Kent replied, causing the hug to become even tighter and incredibly soul-soothing.</p>
<p>The siblings who had once stood on the same battlefield, making plans for what they would do after the war ended, only to be separated before they could fulfill any of them, had now reunited in an emotional reunion—one that was never meant to happen in this life or the next.</p>
