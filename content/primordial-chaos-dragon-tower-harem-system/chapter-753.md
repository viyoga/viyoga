<p>After the dangerous battle against the Crab Flame Beast, the team rested for only two hours before moving to the next region to handle the flame beasts there.</p>
<p>Of course, before leaving, Kent shared the flame spirits among the seven of them, keeping only two for himself.</p>
<p>It was no guarantee that absorbing the two flames would finally give him the boost to reach the 8th Tier in flame quality.</p>
<p>However, he managed to acquire more powerful flame aspects, making his soul flame even stronger to the point that reaching Tier 8 wasn’t his concern.</p>
<p>What Kent was after was to master a flame art and see what his soul flame would be capable of.</p>
<p>Of course, he had already expected that the moment he obtained a flame art, his combat power would skyrocket.</p>
<p>But he didn’t know just how much progress he would make. So, Kent decided that for now, he would wait to see the changes manifest themselves, and when that happened, he would know what to do next.</p>
<p>Next, Kent had officially made the Half-Elves, except Princess Elowen, his subordinates by adding them to the Guardian Hand. The moment he did, he felt like his domain of influence had spread yet again.</p>
<p>Aside from that, he also felt their loyalty levels, and while he never expected it, all seven were already above 80%. That was more than enough to show they were adapting to his teaching, and soon, they would fully adapt to him.</p>
<p>Once that happened, Kent knew they would become much more integrated into his plans for them.</p>
<p>After adding them, they progressed forward, aiming to finish the battles much faster and spend some time absorbing the cores they’d collected.</p>
<p>The battles in the 7th, 8th, and 9th regions were not that harrowing.</p>
<p>But they weren’t easy either.</p>
<p>Thankfully, it ended much faster than he had hoped. But right when the 9th battle ended, something started to happen inside the Tower, causing Kent to pause and send everyone into the tower for the first time.</p>
<p>They appeared inside the Legion Tower, causing them all to widen their eyes at the change in scenery. However, they couldn’t take in the sight to their heart’s content, as the moment they appeared, a powerful aura explosion emanated from one side of the vast space.</p>
<p>They turned to the direction the aura came from, only to see a colossal dragon landing in the air, looking at them.</p>
<p>Uncle Drake had evolved from being a Flame Drake to a True Flame Dragon.</p>
<p>His Dragon body has taken on a red hue with a few dark patches here and there. Of course, being a flame dragon, the red was much more dominant on his colossal body.</p>
<p>The scales shimmered with an otherworldly glow, one that made him appear both majestic and warrior-like.</p>
<p>He had a rather large set of wings spread wide, casting a large shadow on the ground. The claws on his four legs looked terrifyingly sharp. They ended in dark, metallic claws, one that looked almost as if it could claw through reality itself.</p>
<p>And looking at his massive body and lazily scaled tail, it wouldn’t take a genius to know a dragon of this caliber had already reached the peak and needed a bigger field to play in.</p>
<p>Kent scanned him while paying attention to the crazy red eyes and the two sets of twisted dark dragon horns mounted on his head.</p>
<p>⟦Name: Uncle Drake⟧</p>
<p>⟦Age: 15,008⟧</p>
<p>⟦Bloodline: Flame Kings Lineage (20%)⟧</p>
<p>⟦Race: Primordial Flame Dragon⟧</p>
<p>⟦Realm: Core Formation (7/7)⟧</p>
<p>⟦Soul Cultivation: Tier 13⟧</p>
<p>⟦Body Cultivation: Diamond⟧</p>
<p>⟦Flame Resistance: Super⟧</p>
<p>⟦Flame Comprehension: High⟧</p>
<p>’Well, that is a nice display for someone who had just become a dragon,’ Kent smiled, watching the large dragon stare back at him.</p>
<p>"Congratulations on becoming a dragon... Looks like your years of dreaming have finally become a reality. Although I must say, you are much more impressive than I expected."</p>
<p>Uncle Drake didn’t immediately speak. Instead, he transformed into his human form, now assuming a much more refined and handsome appearance.</p>
<p>He maintained his red hair, but now he had a rather cute dark horn framing his handsome face perfectly. Although he still appeared to be in his late 40s.</p>
<p>His height had decreased, now standing at 7 feet, but with a well-toned, muscular look. Even the three sets of red dragon scales on his forehead made him look even more eye-catching than he had in his demi-drake form.</p>
<p>His wings could be summoned and put away at will, so unless he needed them, he didn’t have to have them on his back.</p>
<p>He moved and stopped a few meters away from Kent, then smiled. "It was all thanks to you. I would have never made this progress anytime soon."</p>
<p>"Then I guess it was indeed destined for us to meet. So, congratulations to you for becoming a powerful version of who you wanted to be.</p>
<p>Now, I can boldly say the moment you appear in the outside world, everyone will feel what it feels like to be called a powerhouse."</p>
<p>Kent took a few items and handed them to Uncle Drake. "Use the time here to strengthen your foundation... We still have some work to do on the outside.</p>
<p>Once we get back to the Half-Elves’ land, I will introduce you to their king and queen as the kingdom protector. After that, we will see what happens next."</p>
<p>Uncle Drake nodded. He had already planned to become useful, having finally achieved his dreams. Next, if he continued to strengthen himself, he would be allowed to spread his wings.</p>
<p>For now, using his intimidating presence to deter those who meant harm to the Half-Elves wasn’t something to be angry about.</p>
<p>Kent turned to the Half-Elves and could tell they wanted to stay in the tower for a bit. However, he knew they had a mission to handle.</p>
<p>"We are leaving. When we come out of the Flame Tower, I will allow you to explore this domain. For now, we have work to do."</p>
<p>Though they didn’t want to leave, they had to nod reluctantly, and with a wave of his hand, they were sent back to the outside world.</p>
<p>"We only have three more regions to clear, and we will be done. The rest of the days will be spent on something else, so let’s get back to work."</p>
<p>And so they did. They went back to hunting flame beasts for the next few days. On the 8th day, they cleared all 12 regions and started to absorb the cores.</p>
<p>Meanwhile, geniuses from every race were already preparing to enter the Flame Tower, located in the Beasts Empire...</p>
