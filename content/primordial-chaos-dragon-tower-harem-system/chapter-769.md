<p>Kent wasn’t offended by being called a loser.</p>
<p>If anything, he was amused looking at the struggling Elf who he knew deep within his heart wouldn’t make it to the 4th level.</p>
<p>The path leading there is much more torturous.</p>
<p>If Kent were to guess, she would lose determination on the 60th step, because that is where he could see his friends.</p>
<p>"Have you ever heard of blind determination?" Kent asked, sitting on the step, legs crossed and left hand supporting his chin.</p>
<p>"Blind determination is when you want to prove yourself to someone as a form of pleasing them. You fight so hard just so you could show everyone you are worthy to be among the privileged.</p>
<p>You, Kella, aren’t meant to be among such people.</p>
<p>You are not privileged like the overly pampered elves you are chasing after. They had a silver spoon stuck in their mouth from the day they were born.</p>
<p>You had to fight for it. You worked hard to build your strength and managed to get into the lineup because your people took ten slots from the half-elves.</p>
<p>However, you are here now, and while you think learning a Mythic grade technique will make you a royalty like them, know that at the core, you are just Kella, daughter of an Artisan who happened to awaken her soul flame, and is naturally gifted to wield the flame.</p>
<p>Don’t chase after them. Don’t walk with blind determination.</p>
<p>Instead of thinking of pleasing the Elf Queen, think of pleasing yourself.</p>
<p>You are not inferior to anyone, not when you possess talent like yours. So instead of looking to become one of those greenhouse flowers, surpass them instead.</p>
<p>For if you continue to chase after them, you will falter, and the result will be devastating for you." Kent stood up and looked ahead, seeing the backs of Cyran and Helyra.</p>
<p>’Looks like they took the Pill and would soon awaken an innate talent.’ He smiled and turned back to Kella.</p>
<p>"If you truly want to get to the 4th level, then start climbing for yourself and not the Elf Queen. That said, I like talented people, so I will give you this.</p>
<p>You have 2 days and 17 hours-ish before the tower closes. This means you still have time. However, if you continue down this path, you will be wasting time.</p>
<p>But if you manage to reach the 4th level, take this fruit. It will help you in the mastery of at least two Mythic grade techniques.</p>
<p>I can help you easily climb, but I won’t. So use your determination as your strength, and the reward will be yours. Remember, fighting to please someone is not the way.</p>
<p>Of course, if you can change your determination, the fight to please your father. That is much better than aiming to become a royalty."</p>
<p>With that, Kent walked away, leaving the Lotus Plum mind Fruit with Kella.</p>
<p>The Elven girl looked at the fruit for three whole minutes before storing it away. She had a lot of thinking to do and a short time to do it.</p>
<p>She had no idea how Kent managed to read her thoughts and know what she was chasing after. She was indeed trying to be recognised by the Elven Queen, as she wanted to live a life of affluence.</p>
<p>Perhaps she wanted to appear regal like her mates. So she worked for it. However, what nobody, not even the Elf Queen, knew was that, unlike most of the Elves who had inherited their soul flames from Flame Beasts, Kella awakened her own manually.</p>
<p>He father made sure she covered that up, since he knew if it were recognized, she would be taken from him and made into a doll that the Elves would use to boost their power.</p>
<p>They will use her name to boast, and that is not something he wanted for his daughter.</p>
<p>So she remained low-key and hoped one day, when the elven queen recognized her, she would show this amazing side of herself.</p>
<p>"He even knows my secret. Just who is this kid?"</p>
<p>She sat down and started to come to terms with herself.</p>
<p>Meanwhile, Kent had already reached Cyran and Helyra, who are seated in the lotus posture, absorbing the essence of the pill they’ve taken.</p>
<p>Kent didn’t want to disturb them, so after dropping a pouch before them, he moved ahead.</p>
<p>On the 64th step, Arion, Doreen, and Elia were also there. He left the care package with them and moved ahead. On the 67th step, he caught up to others: one Dwarf, one Flame demon, two Flame Dragons, and an Elf.</p>
<p>Kent moved past them, knowing full well they would be able to make it to the 4th floor.</p>
<p>On the way, he passed by another Elf, then he caught up to the last three members of his group on the 85th step. He left the care package with them and moved ahead.</p>
<p>On the 90th step, he caught up to another Elf, then on the 92nd step, he caught up to a flame Dragon, and on the 97th step, he finally caught up to Freya Stoneforge, the genius among the flame users in the current generation.</p>
<p>"More grease to your elbow, little one," Kent said with a smile as he tossed a pouch containing two lotus plums.</p>
<p>"You are smart, so you will know what to do once you eat one." With that, Kent moved ahead and soon appeared on the 4th level, where 36 tomes were held aloft, each a Mythic-grade technique.</p>
<p>Kent walked through and examined the names of the techniques. He knew he shouldn’t underestimate the grades of techniques, and he was right; the techniques are no simple ones.</p>
<p>"I mean, these are mythic grade techniques. I would be an idiot if I didn’t take them." Kent grinned, an evil expression plastering his face.</p>
<p>He paid the tower 12 million tower points, and like with the first three levels, it copied the techniques.</p>
<p>Kent was satisfied with his gain because he knew this was a good harvest. One Mythic-grade technique will cost several hundred million spirit stones, so paying 12 million tower points for 36 of them is already more than sufficient.</p>
<p>"Now, I need to get to the 5th level where nobody has ever been."</p>
<p>He looked toward the fifth level and smiled.</p>
<p>"Entering the sect with Divine grade techniques, huh. I wonder what the others will think when I come rolling in with divine grade techniques."</p>
<p>With a blissful smile, Kent started ascending the stairway leading to the 5th level.</p>
