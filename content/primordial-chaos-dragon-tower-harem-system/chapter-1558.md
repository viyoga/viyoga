<p>Before the scale shedding, Kent had dark-gold scales with a few red stripes. However, as the scales formed, a much richer golden-red color started to take form.</p>
<p>This time, the scales were clean, perfectly textured, and extremely beautiful.</p>
<p>If before he was a bit rough like a crocodile, then he had now become more dragon-like.</p>
<p>However, the beauty and color are only minor aspects of the new scales forming.</p>
<p>The main focus had to do with the nature of the scales. At first glance, the only thing that would come to mind is whether the scales are metallic or bone-like.</p>
<p>The texture itself seems bone-like; however, closer inspection reveals a metallic feel and look. Kent had always had metallic scales; however, this time, he seemed to have formed a much better version.</p>
<p>He had become stronger, and his scales seemed to have contributed immensely.</p>
<p>They look bone-like but feel metallic and, most importantly, are super durable. It took about three hours for his scales to form. When it was done, he flew into the air and moved about for a few minutes before descending. Before landing, he transformed into his humanoid form.</p>
<p>His wives rushed to his side, hugging him all over.</p>
<p>Although his appearance hadn’t changed much this time, he seemed even more handsome than before. His height rose to 6’5", just an inch, but a visible one.</p>
<p>Most of the changes happened internally. His strength is on another level. Even without checking the system interface, he could tell he had become much stronger, and that alone was enough.</p>
<p>"Thank you all for protecting me while I was out. Though I still have one last step to take, I can’t wait long enough to thank you properly,"</p>
<p>Vexthra, Miryssa, and Inaya blushed.</p>
<p>Kent turned and looked at Ainna, who seemed to have become tired after spending the past seven days using her Supreme Qi to temper Kent’s body.</p>
<p>He wanted to thank her; however, right when their gazes met, she scoffed and turned away.</p>
<p>Kent just laughed and turned back to his wives.</p>
<p>"I hope there were no troubles while I was out,"</p>
<p>The three ladies smiled. "There were a few, but we handled them. We will tell you everything when you are done."</p>
<p>He nodded.</p>
<p>If they were ordinary people, he would have worried. Being worried about three gods is just him being silly and a bit spoiled.</p>
<p>"Then I will get right on the next step,"</p>
<p>He kissed them and walked back to the cave. A lot had to be done, but for now, he knew that when the Chaos Heart was formed, he would be ready to head out to Elina’s Galaxy.</p>
<p>He would handle the rest as he goes.</p>
<p>Tower appeared beside him. "Congratulations, Master. It appears you have become much stronger than even I anticipated."</p>
<p>"I suppose I have you and the ladies to thank for that. However, how much stronger have I become?" he wanted to know in terms of percentages, and Tower was quick to pull up the information.</p>
<p>[Congratulations on shedding and forming your first Chaos Scale.]</p>
<p>[Your attributes have been boosted. Strength: 80,000%. Health: 40,000%. Mental: 30,000%. Soul: 20,000%]</p>
<p>Kent blinked a few times, then a smile formed on his face. "I guess things could have been worse,"</p>
<p>"I skipped some stuff for now since you really don’t need them. However, here is a recap of a few important ones." She raised one finger to count...</p>
<p>"First, aside from the boost in attributes, your bloodline has become much stronger. In fact, it has even become perfect. Now, aside from the two innate abilities you had unlocked, because of the boost, you are eligible for four more.</p>
<p>"More importantly, the [Blood Calamity Fist] and [Chaos Scale Shield] have undergone some upgrades. I wouldn’t bore you with the details, but know that the [Blood Calamity Fist] is now 20,000% more powerful. The [Chaos Scale Shield] is 40,000% stronger.</p>
<p>"Second, you can now harness all 250,000 blood runes you have awakened. It also means you are ready to start forming Blood Tattoos. That is the next stage of the [Bone Tempering Art] master.</p>
<p>"Third, and this is very important, you can now summon a unique armor called [Chaos Blood Armor]. It was awakened when you formed the scale. I don’t know how much deeper it went yet, but when wearing it, all attacks that land on you will lose 40% of their power. Aside from that, you will gain twice your strength while wearing it.</p>
<p>"That is 160,000% boost.</p>
<p>"Lastly, you have awakened two new skills. The first is [Dragon Tail Whip] and the second is [Dragon Gaze]. It seems you had inherited those from the statue."</p>
<p>Tower smiled and waved her hand, summoning the golden statue.</p>
<p>"Master, before you digest all I have just said, there is something you must first see." Tower painted at the bottom of the golden statue.</p>
<p>Thanks to Kent’s newly enhanced eyesight, he saw the initials at a glance. "This is...?"</p>
<p>"I know, right? When I first saw it, I was a bit taken aback, but then it struck me. If one wants to cheat destiny and go against the limits of the universe, who can they trust to ensure they succeed?"</p>
<p>"Someone with no ties to the heavenly laws..." Kent responded absentmindedly.</p>
<p>This made Tower smile. "A paragon indeed. When I saw your initials on the statue, the thought that came to mind was why the paragon would use your initials to mark something they had done. It didn’t make sense.</p>
<p>Then I stopped and thought, and that was when I saw it. This statue was your handiwork, Master. Somehow, you managed to unlock the path to becoming an Absolute, and so you manipulated events just to ensure that today, you would have it."</p>
<p>Kent’s hands shook for a moment, a bit taken aback by what he had just heard. However, while it sounded ridiculous, the fact that his initials now marked the golden statue told him all he needed to know.</p>
<p>"I was the cause of all this. I set the events in motion, making sure that in my first tribulation, I would face the Absolute Dragon and find a way to win. But why go through all this trouble just to get back something that is originally mine?"</p>
<p>Tower smiled. "Cause and effect, Master. What you inherited can be considered something forbidden. I am sure the paragon came to possess it because he was the only one who could hold onto it without the heavens making a fuss.</p>
<p>And since you can’t have it back as a gift because the heavens wouldn’t have allowed it, you designed everything with Paragon’s help so that you would have to fight for it and win it in what could be considered a fair trial.</p>
<p>In other words, you took advantage of the heavens to return something they forbade back to you now as an accepted treasure."</p>
<p>Kent’s mind expanded and contracted, trying to make sense of everything for a few seconds before he sighed and grabbed one [Chaos Heart Stone].</p>
<p>"All these theories are making my mind heat up. I will think about it when I am done forming the Chaos Heart."</p>
<p>Tower smiled and put away the statue.</p>
<p>"I guess the Spell of Origin did more than reincarnate people," she muttered, then snapped her fingers, summoning a formation.</p>
<p>"The Master should swallow the Chaos Heart Stone."</p>
<p>Kent nodded and tossed the stone into his mouth and swallowed it. The moment he did, his eyes turned red.</p>
<p>"The Master cannot sleep this time around."</p>
<p>Heat surged within him, his eyes becoming even redder. Inside his soul sea, the blood sea started to surge. A few seconds in, it began to boil, vapor rising into the air.</p>
<p>Deep under the blood sea, the Chaos Heart Stone slowly started to dissolve.</p>
<p>"The Master must hold on while the Chaos Heart Stone dissolves."</p>
