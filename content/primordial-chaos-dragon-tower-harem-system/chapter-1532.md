<p>Axiom tried to protest, but in the end, he reluctantly handed over the Void Prison to Kent.</p>
<p>"No need to cry, Axiom. I will only take a few of them, provided they meet the requirements I have in mind," Kent wasn’t planning on depriving his subordinates of fine delicacies.</p>
<p>Axiom and his fellow Void Beasts feed on blood essence, the main blood and soul of their victims. It was one of the monstrous innate abilities of Void Beasts that made them terrifying beings.</p>
<p>Killing one person or beast gives them three sources of essence to absorb. Those who even devour the body gain four. So, based on the number of immortals Axiom had absorbed, there is a big chance he would be reaching the Immortal Lord stage after devouring them.</p>
<p>If Kent were to deprive him of that, it would be the most evil thing he would ever do to any of his subordinates.</p>
<p>Thankfully, Kent only wanted those who had the mark of Soul Warrior candidates. For reasons he can’t yet explain, he can now see those who, after their death, will become Soul Warrior candidates.</p>
<p>Maybe it was because his soul had become more robust after recalling all memories of Jest.</p>
<p>The demigods he killed had the mark. That was why he stole Vargus’ kills. Now, however, he wants to see if he can find more of them inside the Void Prison.</p>
<p>After that was settled, Vargus proceeded to tell him the details of their mission.</p>
<p>"So all this destruction was merely to save some princess,"</p>
<p>"Yes," Vargus nodded. "It was a side mission we received after clearing the 11th floor of the dungeon."</p>
<p>"I see." Kent turned and looked at Avotha, who held Warlord Biana by the neck.</p>
<p>"Vargus, you said the mission said killing the warlord is optional, right?"</p>
<p>The brute Shadow Rider nodded.</p>
<p>"In that case," Kent called out to Avotha, "Buddy, place a death seal on her instead. Such a fine warrior shouldn’t be wasted through senseless death,"</p>
<p>Avotha nodded and stretched his left hand, pressing his thumb against her forehead. On his chest, a tattoo of a triangular eye glowed, then moved along his arm and merged with her forehead.</p>
<p>Avotha then formed a hand seal, causing his third eye to release waves of death Qi. The energy flowed into the triangular eye tattoo, causing Warlord Biana to spasm before losing consciousness.</p>
<p>The moment the other immortals saw that their leader had been defeated, they scrambled and ran. Soon, every last one of them vanished, running as fast as their legs could carry them.</p>
<p>Up in the air, the two Immortal Lords that Fero took on came crashing to the ground. Fero let out a powerful roar and took a nosedive, stabbing his claws into their chests.</p>
<p>After wriggling for a few moments, they couldn’t hold on for long and passed out. It would take more than a stab to the chest to kill an Immortal Lord, especially if the attack came from an Immortal King.</p>
<p>But Fero never planned to kill them in the first place.</p>
<p>To him, expanding his master’s Undead Dragon Legion is of importance. So he will kidnap them, and after treating their injuries, they will be sent into the Dragon Ascension Realm to take the trial just like he did.</p>
<p>After checking if they had really passed out, he tossed them directly into the tower prison. The tower will handle their injuries.</p>
<p>He then transformed into his demi-dragon form, revealing a large wound on his chest. He gave it a brief look and turned away, his gaze landing on Kent.</p>
<p>They had met once, but it was brief. This time, however, they would have the chance to talk.</p>
<p>But before that, Kent turned and looked at Axiom. "Fetch the prisoner you are here for,"</p>
<p>He nodded and vanished back into the void. In the next few seconds, he appeared somewhere in the Silent Graves camp. He landed a kick on the door and walked in.</p>
<p>Just when he was about to take a turn, a dagger flew past his face, barely missing his nose.</p>
<p>"Wow, wow, slow down there, Rabbit. I am here to save Luciana." He raised his hand in surrender and stepped out, turning to his left. There, he saw five ladies dressed in maid clothing, with slave collars around their necks.</p>
<p>Three of them were demonkin, judging by the horns on their foreheads. The one who threw the dagger had dark horns, while the other two had green and red horns. The fourth was an elf whose green hair and eyes suggested she was a Green Elf. The last, however, was a Rabbitkin.</p>
<p>"Gotcha. You must be Luciana. My name is Axiom, right hand of the legendary Primordial Chaos Dragon. An undisputed void architect whose void traps have claimed the lives of powerful supremes and Celestials. The undisputed warrior who claimed the life of the crown prince of House Draham."</p>
<p>Axiom paused when he noticed the five ladies were giving him a creepy look. "What, do I have something on my face?"</p>
<p>They didn’t reply to him. Seeing that, he smirked and chose not to continue his introduction. In his mind, their mundane minds lacked the capacity to comprehend the sheer weight of his achievements.</p>
<p>"Anyways, my friends and I were sent here to rescue you, so let’s get moving."</p>
<p>He turned to leave, but Luciana didn’t move to follow him. He stopped and looked at her. "Why aren’t you coming?"</p>
<p>"How do I know you are who you say you are?"</p>
<p>Axiom frowned deeply. "I don’t have time to explain myself to any of you. All I can say is, I was sent here to fetch you. So come with me willingly, or I will drag you like a sac full of dead bodies."</p>
<p>Luciana frowned, and the demonkin who threw the dagger moved to shield her. Seeing this, Axiom smirked and was about to pull Luciana into the void when Kailan appeared.</p>
<p>"Come on, Axiom. Must everything be solved with violence?"</p>
<p>"If not violence, then what else?" He smirked and regarded the princess again before stepping back for Kailan. He knew Kent sent Kailan, knowing the kind of unhinged bastard he really is.</p>
<p>’Master sure knows me.’ Being recognized as an unhinged bastard made him feel proud of himself.</p>
<p>Seeing the smile on his face, Kailan shook his head and retrieved a contract from his space ring, showing its seal to the five ladies. The moment Luciana saw the seal, she rushed forward to have a closer look.</p>
<p>"This...!"</p>
<p>"It is the seal of the high priestess of the Green-Skin Rabbitkin race. My name is Kailan, a member of Kent Madson’s legion. He sent us to come get you,"</p>
<p>"Kent Madson," the red-horned demon jumped forward and asked before Luciana could.</p>
<p>"Indeed. That Kent Madson. He is a few blocks away. Your master has been captured, so you are free to go. If you want to meet him too, feel free to follow us,"</p>
<p>Kailan turned and looked at Princess Luciana. "Are you ready to leave now?"</p>
<p>She nodded and then turned to the four other maids behind her. "But they are coming with us too."</p>
<p>"The more, the merrier." Soon, Kailan led the five ladies out and headed toward Kent’s direction. After they were gone, Axiom smirked.</p>
<p>"What a headache. I could have just kidnapped them and made everything short," he smirked a second time and then headed deeper into the mansion.</p>
<p>Since the owner of this camp was dead, naturally, her treasure vault needed a new owner. Axiom, who is known for stealing from even Celestials, knew he had to do justice to the treasures hidden within.</p>
