<p>One of the trump cards Kent brought with him is a unique talisman that temporarily boosts his bloodline by 5% for 24 hours.</p>
<p>Kent didn’t need it before when his bloodline hadn’t reached 20% awakened yet. Of course, he also didn’t pay attention to it simply because the talisman is ridiculously expensive.</p>
<p>However, now he does, and if the fact that he is in a completely different universe or the fact that he is fighting beings far stronger than he is isn’t enough, then now, he has to fight dozens of said opponents at the same time, and that is just too much for a person.</p>
<p>So he used it, and at the moment, what could be described as a primal predatory feeling was what everybody started to feel the moment Kent turned into his true dragon form.</p>
<p>His body is about 300 meters long. Not even close to the true beast form of someone like Ignarok or even Sylvara. However, 300 meters is already long enough to put him in the rank of a collosal beasts.</p>
<p>His scales are dark golden, with a few patches of red. His wings are also very wide, even longer than his body. It is about 350 meters wide, and a metallic sheen could be felt from them, making every flap so suffocating and, well, just dangerous.</p>
<p>His dark golden horns aren’t as twisted as a primordial dragon’s should be, but that is to be expected, considering he is only at 25% at the moment.</p>
<p>However, what made him a danger are his tail and legs...</p>
<p>His tail is lined with metallic scales and ends with a pointed metallic spike, the kind that is designed to pierce anything.</p>
<p>As for his legs, they ended in metallic talons, so hard and piercing that anything they touched would be shredded beyond recognition.</p>
<p>As he flaps his wings and circles around the Crimson Guillotine Array, which was still holding the red-winged angel he feel like a god looking down on a bug.</p>
<p>Suddenly, the array exploded, freeing the monarch.</p>
<p>"Dragon breath."</p>
<p>Kent, who was both excited to have finally turned into his dragon, unleashed his dragon breath, and a wave of black flames blasted out, consuming the poor monarch.</p>
<p>"AAarrrgggHHHH!"</p>
<p>His screams drew the attention of many angels; however, when they saw the dragon in the air, none dared move.</p>
<p>When Kent finished breathing the flames and let the monarch catch his breath, everyone’s back turned cold. The poor angel, still on his knees, was barbecued, and while he was dead, his body continued to twitch.</p>
<p>That is how much pain he was in before he died.</p>
<p>It was all thanks to Kent using the Umbral Flame of Oblivion.</p>
<p>’So aside from my soul flame and my core flame, I can also use the Nine Nirvana Flames through dragon breath.’</p>
<p>This discovery made Kent turn and look toward the direction where 12,000 angels appeared, the majority of them at the Death Stage, while over 40 were monarchs and 8 were in the Nirvana Stage.</p>
<p>However, what caught his attention the most was a white-haired angel lady holding a metallic bow, her expression cold.</p>
<p>’She seems to be looking toward the redhead, too. Just why are they keen on killing her?’</p>
<p>⟦Master, the Paragon said can you handle the lady wielding the bow. He said she is a level 8 Nirvana Stage warrior, while the two you killed were just level 2.⟧</p>
<p>Kent looked up at the air and saw the Paragon looking toward him.</p>
<p>Just from staring at him, Kent could tell that whatever this tribulation is about, Paragon has been waiting for it from the start.</p>
<p>Aside from that, he could tell that Paragon had become much stronger, just like how he, too, had become stronger now that he was in his dragon form.</p>
<p>Kent gave him a nod, telling him he shouldn’t worry.</p>
<p>’Tell him not to worry and that he should focus on the tribulation. With me here, these angels are about to have a bad day.’</p>
<p>⟦Good luck, master, and please, don’t do anything reckless.⟧</p>
<p>’You already know me, Tower... I value my life more than anything.’</p>
<p>Kent turned back to the angel lady and growl, smoke coming from the corner of his mouth.</p>
<p>He can tell that even if he takes on the Angel lady, who at the moment is the most dangerous warrior on the enemy side, there are still 40 monarchs and 8 Nirvana stage warriors coming from the blue portal.</p>
<p>And from the green portal where the 419 warriors appeared, 9 of them are Monarchs and 6 Nirvana stage warriors too.</p>
<p>Thankfully, the moment they arrived, all portals closed, showing they were the last to arrive.</p>
<p>However, the question still remains, how are the Earth warriors going to fight such odds when their strongest is now about to face a different challenge.</p>
<p>’So is this how being a paragon feels like...always clashing with odds far beyond your level.’ Kent felt pity toward the paragon, who he could tell was both angry and scared at the moment.</p>
<p>He saw him looking around, watching his people panic and many dying simply because of him. All of this is happening because of him, so at the moment, despite knowing all these warriors signed up for the worst, watching them die made him powerless.</p>
<p>’This is just too cruel. I wish Kaizo and the others were here to take some loads off their neck.’</p>
<p>Kent sighs, knowing that it is impossible. However, he also knew killing the Angel Lady would take off a major player from the board.</p>
<p>He flaps his wings and, in an instant, vanishes, appearing before the angel lady known in the Divine Angel race as Arella.</p>
<p>"Dragon Claw."</p>
<p>Kent swung his claw, unleashing a red dragon claw arcs that tore the void in an instant. Arella saw him move and knew she could block him. However, the speed Kent used was beyond her calculation.</p>
<p>But even so, she didn’t panic.</p>
<p>Her bow turned into a sword, and she struck with a powerful swing, meeting the claw in a rather powerful explosion.</p>
<p>Both of them were pushed back.</p>
<p>Arella turned to three angels and issued a command before turning back to face Kent. "Set up the transporter."</p>
<p>Her beautiful white wings spread, and she instantly dashed toward Kent, aiming for his life. However, Kent wasn’t about to waste his strength on a bug.</p>
<p>"Dragon Tail."</p>
<p>Kent attacked with his dragon tail, but Arella didn’t let it come close to her. She moved back, narrowly evading the deadly spiked tail.</p>
<p>However, it didn’t end there; she even went ahead and unleashed a sword arc before transforming her sword back into a bow.</p>
<p>She fired a random shot that instantly opened a wormhole, dragging both of them in. When they appeared, Kent saw they were several miles away from the battlefield.</p>
<p>’She wants to isolate me from them...Just what is she planning?</p>
<p>Kent dodges another arrow, and then he unleashes a dragon claw. However, just when he was about to unleash another attack, he sensed his surroundings twist.</p>
<p>’Someone or some people are hiding in the void...I need to be careful.’</p>
<p>Kent started fighting Arella with half his focus on the space around him. He can sense the void thanks to his Chaos blood. So while he can’t tell how may are hidden, he knew they are waiting for the best moment to attack.</p>
<p>"Projectile Domain."</p>
<p>She unleashed her domain, and in an instant, Kent sensed 14 points of contact where her arrows could land on him.</p>
<p>He didn’t know how it happened, but through the soul map, he saw that the domain had selected 14 points on his body that an arrow could pierce.</p>
<p>Obviously, Arella didn’t know her domain had been figured out.</p>
