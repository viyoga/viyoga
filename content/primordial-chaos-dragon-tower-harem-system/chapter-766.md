<p>⟦I have analysed all the Flame Arts on the first level. There are 102 of them. Seventy-two are Attack-type Flame Arts, 20 are Defense-type Flame Arts, and ten are Enhance-type Flame Arts.⟧</p>
<p>⟦If the master wants these techniques, replicating them will cost just 1 million tower points.⟧</p>
<p>"Go ahead. I don’t need them, but I am sure I will be able to learn something from them," Kent smiled as he stepped on the 100th step. The moment he did, he felt all forms of heat vanish from his surroundings.</p>
<p>Some people noticed him, but with them busily looking for a technique to learn or those already learning, talking was no luxury they could afford.</p>
<p>The Half-Elves had long passed the first level, so he had no reason to talk to anyone. The only ones he wanted to talk to were the seven he picked from the dozens on the first floor.</p>
<p>The tower took one million tower points and copied the techniques as if they were nothing.</p>
<p>Of course, the tower is overpowered to an extent that surpasses anyone’s understanding. It can do many things if Kent wants.</p>
<p>The only things it can’t do are go against laws that cannot be broken without consequences. If anything, something as simple as this Flame Tower isn’t a match for the overpowered Tower inside Kent’s soul.</p>
<p>The Flame Tower is just an Immortal-grade item. It is powerful, perhaps even powerful if it were in a High World. However, when measured against Kent’s tower, the gap is just too vast.</p>
<p>So he copied the skills and would use them later because he wasn’t just copying them for nothing.</p>
<p>Kent walked toward a Tigerkin and tapped him on the shoulder. "Follow me if you want to have a shot at reaching the third level and mastering a technique."</p>
<p>With that, Kent moved toward a beautiful Qilin lady and said the same thing to her before moving to a Dwarf. Afterwards, he moved to a Flame Demon and a Dark Elf. The last two were an Ice Demon and a Shadow Demon.</p>
<p>It wasn’t the lineup one would pay attention to, but Kent saw something in them and, in his own way, planned to help them. In the future, they will pay him back several times over.</p>
<p>After tapping the seven, Kent moved to the side and let them take their time to come. Two minutes later, they stood before him.</p>
<p>The others who saw this strange behavior could only wonder silently in their heads. They had no idea what Kent was doing, but they knew it was none of their business, so their focus was on their own affairs.</p>
<p>Kent studied their faces and sighed.</p>
<p>"I will make this short so you can get back to your business. Don’t ask me how I know, but I know that you seven are looking for the best enhancement Flame Art that can aid you in climbing further.</p>
<p>First of all, don’t be ashamed of seeking help. Be proud, because you are survivalists, someone who knows how to survive at all costs.</p>
<p>That said, I have a way to help you seven. All I ask is to reach the 3rd floor, which, if you don’t slack, you can make it within one day, or one and a half if your determination isn’t strong enough.</p>
<p>Now, my help isn’t for free. Of course, I am not asking you to become my slaves. I hate slavery, so wipe that out of your head. However, I do want something from you, and that is to see you survive the next 3 years.</p>
<p>If you manage to survive and grow much stronger over the next 3 years, I will tell you what I want in return for helping you today.</p>
<p>And for what I have to offer, I will help you climb and have a shot at comprehending at least two Mystical-grade Flame Arts on the third floor.</p>
<p>This means I will help you now, and you will pay me back three years from today. Are you in?"</p>
<p>It took a moment, but the seven nodded, bringing a smile to Kent’s lips as he took out three fruits: Immortal Grade Ambrosia Apple, 10,000-year-old Spirit Grapefruit, and Lotus Plum Mind Fruit.</p>
<p>He handed them to all seven of them. "Eat the grape first, apple second, and lotus last. Afterward, you can proceed to learn these techniques...</p>
<p>Also, please do not mention the three items I gave you to your leaders. If they ask, tell them I helped you, but you cannot say what the help was.</p>
<p>I know you’re smart, so you’ll figure something out.</p>
<p>If you try anything funny, don’t say I didn’t warn you."</p>
<p>Kent went ahead and told them the names of the techniques he picked for them based on their profiles.</p>
<p>"Until then, see you all later." With that, Kent left and headed to the second level, leaving the seven stunned youngsters behind.</p>
<p>It was only after Kent left the first floor that they breathed. They looked at each other for a moment before Lorin, the lady Qilin, placed the grape in her mouth and ate it.</p>
<p>The others followed her, and soon, all seven of them were in the lotus posture, absorbing the energy they felt entering their bodies.</p>
<p>Meanwhile, on the outside, all names had been displayed on the board. However, some names stood out.</p>
<p>At the top was Freya Stoneforge, the youngest princess of the Dwarves. Her appearance at the top was normal; after all, she is known as the best flame user in the younger generation.</p>
<p>Following her was Helga Forog, best friend of Freya and the second-best flame user in this year’s selection to enter the power.</p>
<p>Prince Roran Fireclaw, the second prince of the Flame Dragons, took the third spot. He is also an impressive young person, someone everyone knows and recognises. He has a remarkable journey ahead of him.</p>
<p>An Elf took the fourth spot, and the fifth was also an Elf. Then, from the 7th onwards, there was a mix of other races. The 26th to the 33rd were all half-elves.</p>
<p>Kent was still below the 50th percentile because he had delayed his climb. But now that he had found the ones he wanted, his journey had only begun.</p>
<p>"It’s been only an hour, but a few have already reached the 2nd Level. It seems this year, we might actually be seeing some impressive talents," Elder Lance said, tracking Kent’s name as he moved slowly through the ranks.</p>
