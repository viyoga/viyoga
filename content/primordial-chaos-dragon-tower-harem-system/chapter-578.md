<p>"Death Note?" Camilla asked.</p>
<p>She and her sisters looked at Lani weirdly, wondering what kind of weird ability she had.</p>
<p>"What does it do?" Selene asked.</p>
<p>Lani looked at Kent and smiled.</p>
<p>The brat who read the Death Note Manga, watched the Death Note anime, and even worked on the live action with a few director friends back on Earth couldn’t help but wonder if it was the same Death Note he knew.</p>
<p>"Don’t look at me, answer their question..." Kent couldn’t help smiling.</p>
<p>...though deep down, he wished and really wished it were the same Death Note.</p>
<p>Wouldn’t that be cool? I mean, writing someone’s name in the Death Note, and it’s a guarantee they will die. This means he could just let Lani write the names of all his enemies and just sit back and watch them all die.</p>
<p>"This is my Death Note. It’s one of a kind. I don’t know where it came from or who made it, but it has blank pages. On any random day, a name and a countdown will appear inside.</p>
<p>Before the countdown reaches zero, I am required to kill the person. Failure to do so will come with some punishment, but if I manage to kill the person, I will be given a random gift.</p>
<p>Now, you must be wondering... what if the name of a god appears in it, or the name isn’t from our world? What then am I going to do?" Lani smiled.</p>
<p>"It’s simple. There are four different colors the names will appear. Each color has a meaning, like strength, location, and many more.</p>
<p>First, if the name appears in blue, it means they are in your world, and in most cases, in my kingdom or perhaps empire, depending on the structure of that world.</p>
<p>Most names in blue are sometimes in the same cultivation stage as me or below me. These names come with less punishment and less reward.</p>
<p>Then there is the green name. That name is still in the same world as me, but a realm or two above me. These killings are marked with great rewards but are also dangerous to execute—if you are an amateur, of course." Lani smiled, saying that.</p>
<p>Her sisters smirked.</p>
<p>It appeared it wasn’t Val alone who wanted to flex her abilities.</p>
<p>However, while they would have loved to say they could take her own, every last one of them, excluding Vexthra, knew the lady before them had become the strongest of them all.</p>
<p>Even Zaila couldn’t help but wonder if she could take her on without her Primordial spirit backing her. Though in the end, they all came to the same conclusion:</p>
<p>’She is our sister; no need to be afraid of her.’</p>
<p>Lani teased her sisters for a few more seconds before continuing...</p>
<p>"The form of names comes in black. Those names are not only outside my world but also belong to powerful people like kings, queens, princes... mostly names of royalty.</p>
<p>They are some of the demented royals who use their power to destroy lives and suppress the poor. Names of higher-ups in kingdoms and empires also come in black.</p>
<p>These missions are high risk since the target or targets can be three or four realms above me. However, there is a catch to this...</p>
<p>Knowing the mission is high risk, the Death Note will aid me in completing it. Basically, I will have to do the killing, but the Death Note will help me with planning.</p>
<p>Though that won’t be needed, considering my abilities are something even Spirit Monarchs should fear. But that is just by the way.</p>
<p>The last color is red, and that is the calamity missions. The names that appear in this color are so dangerous that even those of the same rank as the target cannot handle them.</p>
<p>They can be in different worlds, perhaps hidden realms. These missions don’t have countdowns, so they can take millions of years to complete.</p>
<p>However, it would be best if I completed them fast enough, since piling names will be bad for me. I would be required to finish all the names; otherwise, piling them to some extent will bring some punishment."</p>
<p>"Isn’t this sinister in a way? I mean, you aren’t looking to kill these people, so why punish you for it?" Unity asked, not liking her sister’s new ability one bit.</p>
<p>"You’re forgetting about the gift part," Lilian said, looking at her friends.</p>
<p>"I know about the gift part. But asking one person to go against beings this dangerous isn’t that punishment enough? I mean, why even ask for your help in the first place?"</p>
<p>The ladies seemed to understand where Unity was coming from. However, Lani just smiled and held her hand.</p>
<p>"The red names—I can ask for help from you all. In fact, those who help me complete a red mission, whether directly or indirectly, will receive a gift from the Death Note.</p>
<p>Meaning I won’t be in danger if I have you ladies to protect me." Unity smiled this time, knowing that she would be safe at least with them.</p>
<p>Lani smiled, watching Unity’s smile blossom. The cute archer whose True Name awakening ritual can’t be performed with the True Name Token has become everyone’s favourite.</p>
<p>So watching her smile, she couldn’t help but smile with her.</p>
<p>"You know, I don’t know where this Death Note came from, but I cannot help but wonder if it’s punishment for something I did in my past life," Lani chuckled...</p>
<p>"But it is one powerful treasure I have, and soon, I will know just what it is made of," She added.</p>
<p>"There’s no need to think about that. Whatever you did, I am sure it was noble, and because of that, the Death Note chose you to take care of the scum of society," Vexthra said, making sure her sister got the comfort she needed.</p>
<p>Kent and the ladies nodded at her words. The pain they felt for her during her awakening was a clear sign that her past self had gone through something that brought them pain.</p>
<p>However, now that she had become someone powerful, they could only be happy for her.</p>
<p>"Just wait for now. We will talk about your unique abilities when we are done with everyone." Kent wanted to investigate the matter thoroughly before making his final decision.</p>
<p>He turned toward Zaila and smiled. "It’s your turn, cutie."</p>
<p>Zaila and her Primordial Spirit smiled. The primordial spirit then entered her mind space, and she stepped forward.</p>
<p>She placed her blood on the True Name token, and Kent did the same. However, the moment his blood landed on the token, his vision darkened.</p>
