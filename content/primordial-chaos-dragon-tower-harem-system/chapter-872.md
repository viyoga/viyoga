<p>"So that is the teleportation mountains," Kent said, staring at three tall mountains forming a triangle.</p>
<p>"Yes. The three mountains serve as natural conduits to the universal space channel. It was something created by nature.</p>
<p>Provided you have a channel key that points to where you want to go, you just need to use ten million high-level spirit stones or ten thousand high-level spirit crystals to activate it."</p>
<p>Kent nodded. "In that case, since it was created by nature, it cannot be tampered with, and as such, anyone who uses it cannot be tracked."</p>
<p>Adina nodded.</p>
<p>"I guess nature indeed has its beauties." Kent was rather fascinated by the nature of the teleportation mountains. It was the best way to vanish without a trace.</p>
<p>"In that case, you have to leave now. The next team of mercenaries will probably pick up on our last battle and track us here. The faster you leave this place, the better for all of us."</p>
<p>"Are you tired of me that you couldn’t wait to send me away?" Adina asked with a beautiful pout. Kent chuckled, knowing she was just happy.</p>
<p>However, despite knowing she was just being cute, he couldn’t help teasing her a bit.</p>
<p>"I am afraid if you stay for a day longer, I will be forced to make my move on you, and trust me, I don’t miss."</p>
<p>Iver snorted behind Kent, feeling amused. Kent had long taken the mind control from his mind, but he chose to stay with them before Adina was gone.</p>
<p>Kent even tried sending him away after their last battle a day ago, but he said he wanted to see the mission come to an end.</p>
<p>Of course, the tower was always a step ahead, so it marked him and planted some mechanisms inside his mind for future use and safety. Kent only trusted his legion, his wives, and his dead mom.</p>
<p>Adina glared at Kent and smirked.</p>
<p>"You had your chance days ago, and you blew it," she teased, sticking out her tongue.</p>
<p>Kent laughed, and then he took something from his tower storage and handed it to Adina. "It is a soul-hunting technique called Song of the Fallen. It has a necrotic touch to it, so learning it will perhaps allow you to stay in one place for a while.</p>
<p>I know your main talent is in music and illusions, so perhaps this is the best technique for you. It is a Supreme grade technique, so you probably should pay more attention to it."</p>
<p>Adina’s hands shook, almost dropping the technique scroll in her hand. Hearing the grade of the technique was enough to tell her what she had to know.</p>
<p>Tears fell from her eyes, and while her body kept telling her not to do it, her heart told her otherwise, so she moved and hugged Kent, planting her face in his chest, soaking the poor dragon’s new shirt he bought using 500,000 tower points with her tears.</p>
<p>Of course, as a gentleman, he only patted her on the back. "No need to cry, young lady. It is just a Supreme technique. The next time we meet, I will probably give you a couple of Celestial grade techniques."</p>
<p>Adina stayed in his chest for a few minutes before pulling away with red cheeks.</p>
<p>Kent smiled and then threw a storage pouch to her. "For you not to cry again and soak my shirt, you should open that when you get to your destination."</p>
<p>Adina nodded, still having her red cheeks.</p>
<p>After calming down, she wanted to give Kent something, but she wasn’t sure what to give him. Kent is rich, and she knew that now, so she didn’t know what would be appropriate to give him as a gift.</p>
<p>In the end, she took out her sword and handed it to Kent. "Here you go. I don’t have a supreme grade sword technique, so you can have my sword. I probably won’t be needing it again anyway."</p>
<p>Kent accepted the sword and stored it away.</p>
<p>"In that case, you should be on your way." Adina nodded and flew into the air.</p>
<p>"Till we meet again, Kent," she said, then turned to Iver, "You too."</p>
<p>Both Iver and Kent nodded, watching her fly away. A few seconds later, she was standing at the center of the three mountains. She took out ten thousand high-grade spirit crystals and placed them on the teleportation ground.</p>
<p>The formation activated instantly. Adina looked at Kent again and then, with a smile on her face, she channeled her energy into the Channel Key.</p>
<p>The formation lit up even more, and then, with a beam that separated the clouds, she was transported away.</p>
<p>At that very moment, Kent heard the tower beep, signaling he was done with the quest. He also felt his connection to the Tower space form again.</p>
<p>This brought a smile to his face. He turned to Iver. "Time for you to go too."</p>
<p>Iver nodded. He wouldn’t be using the teleportation mountains because he had a way to get back without the mountain.</p>
<p>He first went down on his knees. "Thank you for sparing my life. In the future, when we meet again, I will be sure to pay back this kindness."</p>
<p>Kent just waved his hand dismissively, telling Iver he shouldn’t worry. He took out a storage pouch and handed it to him.</p>
<p>"Just take good care of yourself. One day I will come to your world, and I will need you to show me around."</p>
<p>Iver nodded, then retrieved a talisman from his space ring. Kent watched him start channeling his energy into it. After a couple of minutes, he was ready to go.</p>
<p>"Till we meet again, Iver."</p>
<p>With a wave, he was also gone.</p>
