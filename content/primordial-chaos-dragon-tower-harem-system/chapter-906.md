<p>Nobody knew when Velmora slipped into the lady’s shadow.</p>
<p>In fact, even Vexthra, although not a goddess at the moment, had a much higher divine sense than everyone present. But even she didn’t know when Velmora made her move.</p>
<p>Perhaps the only one who knew something about what happened was Lady Liora, who managed to figure out part of the process after the head of the Spirit Emperor came rolling.</p>
<p>Naturally, the move Velmora used was one she had prepared ahead of time. She is an Assassin who thrives in secrecy. So she made her move that thinking about it now, they all felt their scalps go numb.</p>
<p>Right when the door was opening, at the exact moment it started sending out powerful auras, Velmora saw an opening appear in the lady’s defense, allowing her to use a skill only Liora recognized.</p>
<p>’Shadow Displacement’</p>
<p>The skill allowed her to swap shadows with the lady.</p>
<p>The lady’s shadow came to her, and hers went to the lady, allowing her to move and appear instantly whenever she wanted.</p>
<p>So she waited, and just when the lady was working out a plan against Alina, Velmora knew her time had come. So she attacked Amix, which was a decoy for the main attack.</p>
<p>What she never expected was Elyndra trapping the lady. Trapping her was like 80% of her work has been lifted off her.</p>
<p>The only thing left was for the lady now trapped in the ’Space Lock’ to focus on her captor, while Velmora, who had sharpened her scythe back when she was training in Fedora, was more than ready to continue her unholy beheading.</p>
<p>It all played out just as she had planned it with an added sauce.</p>
<p>"Just how did she get there?" Unity muttered, looking at Velmora, who picked up the head and threw it at Kaizo.</p>
<p>The unruly Kaizo caught it and stored it in his space ring.</p>
<p>"Something seems to be blocking them from coming, so why don’t we kill them all before the big guns appear?"</p>
<p>Half of the legion moved, and soon, the slaughter that the forsaken race never saw coming descended on them.</p>
<p>The sect elders and the various kings and queens could only stand and watch as Kent’s ladies and legion rapidly slaughtered the forsaken warriors as if their cultivation bases weren’t even a factor.</p>
<p>In no time, they killed every last one, with only Amix taken alive.</p>
<p>"This is just the first phase or more of a show of what is to come. This time, you all should be ready." With that, Vexthra vanished back to the tower for everyone’s safety.</p>
<p>If she were to stay, the chances of her joining the battle would be high, and while having a goddess on their side would be ideal, her mere presence in the battle would bring doom to everyone. So it was best if she wasn’t there at all.</p>
<p>However, with the tower’s overpowered abilities, watching what was happening on the outside in real-time was a mere wave away.</p>
<p>Alina deactivated the formation and immediately started setting up the next one for the next phase of the battle.</p>
<p>---</p>
<p>Meanwhile, on the other side of the large door, thousands of warriors ranging from Spirit Ascension upward stood ready, watching the green portal slowly turn red like the color on the other side.</p>
<p>The moment it turned red, they would move and begin the invasion. If any of them were to go now, they would lose their cultivation base by one stage.</p>
<p>Though just one, none wanted to do that—or more like their leaders didn’t want them to drop by a stage—so they sent ten thousand to clear the way.</p>
<p>The world was vast, and the sky was painted in a gloomy gray hue. Around the planet, if one were to look from the outside, there was a green ring filled with countless restrictions.</p>
<p>These restrictions were placed there to prevent anyone on this planet from leaving.</p>
<p>However, this restriction was proven null when the Forsaken door opened. It was like whatever authority the restriction had was broken by the forsaken door.</p>
<p>Inside a large building, ten people, all at the Heavenly Ascension stage, were seated, watching a projection of green dots turning red at a rapid pace.</p>
<p>Among the ten, one lady sat in a much more extravagant seat, clearly showing she was the leader of the nine. Naturally, her level 5 Heavenly Ascension stage cultivation shows she was the strongest.</p>
<p>Of course, if Amix were present, he would have been happy to finally meet the lady she called her mother.</p>
<p>They called her the Forsaken Queen Deva. She was the sole reason the invasion was possible. Though she couldn’t leave her world, there were many loopholes that allowed her to worm her way into the heads of innocent beings in the vast universe.</p>
<p>One of them was Amix, the leader of the Hand.</p>
<p>"It seems the people of that world are much stronger than we gave them credit for," Deva said, looking at the rapidly changing green dots.</p>
<p>"Does this mean they have what it takes to counter us?" one of them, a level 3 Heavenly Ascension stage Forsaken warrior, asked.</p>
<p>"Don’t be ridiculous, Boris. We are a high world, and they are a medium-low standard world. What makes you think they have the means to face the kind of army we have on the outside?</p>
<p>Even if they were a high world, with the force we have under us, with the force I command, this invasion will be a success. We cannot fail, not when the others are counting on us."</p>
<p>Deva kept staring at the dots until a deep green one suddenly turned red. Her expression shifted instantly.</p>
<p>"They were able to kill a Peak Spirit Emperor, meaning they are not simple. We have to destroy them once and for all."</p>
<p>She looked toward the direction of the portal leading to Sonox and issued an order.</p>
<p>"All Spirit Emperors, charge out when the portal is red. After that, follow the previous setup."</p>
<p>Over twenty thousand Spirit Emperors flew into the air and prepared for the portal to turn pure red.</p>
<p>On the other side, Alina had tens of thousands of runes in the air. They moved and merged into a massive formation on both the ground and in the sky.</p>
<p>"They will be here soon, so be ready. And like I said, if you can’t face them, retreat," Kaizo shouted before moving to the front of the line. Beside him stood Uncle Drake, who had taken his full dragon form and was ready for battle.</p>
<p>Behind them stood Kent’s ladies and his legion. Then there was the Bloodvein tribe led by tribe chief Malthar. And finally, behind the legion were the Supreme Immortal Sect Elders and the disciples, with a few warriors from Sonox who chose to stay.</p>
<p>Among them were Lady Aelith, Lord Qilin, Queen Jascia, King Thalirion, and several others. This was their world, and running away before the enemy even arrived was not something they planned on doing.</p>
<p>On one side, the Vampires were locked inside a formation that Alina had trapped them in. She could not allow them to retreat back to Sonox when the core of the world’s power was facing a much greater threat.</p>
<p>Asha was hovering over them.</p>
<p>"They are almost here." The light in Kaizo’s eyes burned fiercely as he stared at the portal.</p>
<p>"Charge!"</p>
<p>With that, Kaizo moved, and so did Uncle Drake—right at the moment the Forsaken warriors arrived.</p>
