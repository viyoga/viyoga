<p>"What is this place?" Princess Lovina asked, looking puzzled.</p>
<p>"This is a secret world I own. You can think of it as a pocket world," Kent replied. The wrestling match between his three assistants and his women continued joyfully.</p>
<p>Meanwhile, he stood to the side, talking to Princess Lovina. Until a few hours ago, everybody knew her as the Wicked Poison Witch, one of the seven Pill Saints—until Kent killed a couple of them.</p>
<p>Not that they didn’t deserve it. However, the Wicked Poison Witch turns out to be more than just a wicked lady who doesn’t care who she poisons. She turns out to be more than a wicked witch, but a Princess, or was once a princess.</p>
<p>Princess Lovina stood there in a daze.</p>
<p>The discovery that she was in a new world outside Sonox, or perhaps hidden within Sonox—not that it was important, but the fact that she was in a new world—shocked her.</p>
<p>The display—from the human city named Gaiaville to the Blood City, which is inhabited by the Bloodvein tribe, and back to the wrestling ring—was just too much for her medieval cultivation world mindset to comprehend.</p>
<p>Looking at her stunned expression, Kent smiled.</p>
<p>"You will get used to it soon enough... In any case, the spiritual qi here is much denser and purer than the outside, so use it to solidify your cultivation base before your foundation is destroyed."</p>
<p>Princess Lovina nodded, hearing what Kent said.</p>
<p>She anticipated that today would be the end of her life, but it turned out her life was much harder, and this was thanks to Kent, who sent his women to intervene at the crucial moment.</p>
<p>Now, for Kent helping her cure the poison that had plagued her for hundreds of years, she promised to become his servant for ten years, but now she will serve him for 100 years.</p>
<p>"Thank you for saving my life... again," Princess Lovina said with a small smile.</p>
<p>The old, ragged woman known as the wicked Poison Witch was nowhere to be seen, and now news of her being the long-dead princess of Kyrrith had spread everywhere.</p>
<p>It caused a great deal of mayhem, considering many never expected her to have such a background. But in the end, they had to come to terms with it...</p>
<p>Kent smiled. "Don’t worry about it. I just did what I felt was right. But the fact that you thought only you could take down an entire kingdom is just too shocking... What were you thinking?</p>
<p>I know you spent years planning your revenge, but you had to have known that kingdoms aren’t as simple as they appear.</p>
<p>You had to have known you would fail in killing everyone... If that is the case, then perhaps you planned on dying alongside the ones you went to kill?</p>
<p>"I don’t know about you, but when it comes to revenge, I like to finish them off and go back to my bed and sleep, not dying in the end.</p>
<p>Using your life to get revenge is not revenge; it’s just plain stupidity."</p>
<p>Princess Lovina sighed.</p>
<p>In truth, she had known her plan wouldn’t succeed simply because she suspected the danger she was facing wasn’t something she could win. However, she also had other plans.</p>
<p>That last-minute attack that could have claimed her life was also her final move to kill everyone, including the Core Formation experts.</p>
<p>Her core was loaded with poison that she planned to detonate. Had she succeeded, she would have killed all of them, and Vale City would have become uninhabitable for hundreds of years.</p>
<p>So, in a twisted kind of way, she actually planned on dying to unleash her strongest move.</p>
<p>But now, hearing Kent’s words, she couldn’t help but wonder if she was just being stupid, putting her life on the line.</p>
<p>"Ah... don’t worry about it. You are safe now; that is the only important thing." Kent sighed and then called out to Dori.</p>
<p>"Get dressed, Dori, we are going somewhere." Dori nodded and blinked away. A few seconds later, she came back wearing all black.</p>
<p>"Dori, we are not going to a funeral at Coachella, so why are you dressed like an up-and-coming goth queen?" Dori was clad in black trousers, a hoodie, and even some stylish shoes, almost giving Yeezy a run for their money.</p>
<p>⟪We are going out, so I need to dress to impress,⟫ Dori smiled, saying that. Kent just smirked and waved at his women.</p>
<p>"I am going out. When done, show Princess Lovina here around." They waved back. Kent and Dori then vanished and appeared somewhere near Vastport City.</p>
<p>Kent retrieved a carriage, and they left Vastport City, heading toward Eldorath, in the northern part of the human continent.</p>
<p>⟪Where are we going, master?⟫ Dori asked.</p>
<p>"We are going to see a friend of mine. He needs my help, and I think you can help him get it," Kent responded.</p>
<p>After a while, they entered the borders of the Eldorath Kingdom. Just as the Athea Kingdom is known for commerce, buying, and selling, the Eldorath Kingdom is known for producing food, minerals, and clothing.</p>
<p>They are the fashion kingdom and a rather nice place to be if you are fond of fashion. Basically, everyone there is either a fashion icon or a fashion designer. They make the best clothes, down to the finest armor.</p>
<p>However, Kent and Dori weren’t going there for the fashion. He was going to meet with Sword King Shawn, whom he had promised to meet a month ago.</p>
<p>They didn’t enter Eldor City. Instead, they moved higher into the mountains until they came to a stop at a stone platform featuring a large statue of a man holding a sword.</p>
<p>A few minutes after they arrived, Sword King Shawn also appeared.</p>
<p>"Good to see you again, Kent," Sword King Shawn said with a polite smile, shaking Kent’s hand.</p>
<p>"Good to see you too, Sword King Shawn... This is my assistant, Dori," Kent responded, introducing her.</p>
<p>"Oh, congratulations on your breakthrough. It looks like you moved to the third level of the Core Formation stage within a month."</p>
<p>Sword King Shawn nodded. "Thanks for helping me upgrade my spirit root to the immortal grade. It even helped my soul reach the Tier 11 stage."</p>
<p>Kent smiled. "In that case, why don’t we get going?" Kent retrieved the world teleporter and placed his hand on Sword King Shawn’s shoulder.</p>
<p>The next second, they vanished...</p>
