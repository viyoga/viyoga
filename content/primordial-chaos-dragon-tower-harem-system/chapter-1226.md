<p>From what Elwen said, at this point in their life, and thanks to the peace they have, finding the coldest place to cultivate is the next best thing for them.</p>
<p>According to her explanation, when they reach the bottleneck between the Death stage and the Root Immortal stage, they would undergo a tribulation called Black Frost Lightning.</p>
<p>This tribulation will test their resistance to ice. So while they still have the time, the next best thing is to cultivate in an icy location.</p>
<p>No gods and supremes are chasing after them now, so they have all the time in the universe to cultivate it well.</p>
<p>As it turns out, Elwen has a place for them to do just that. After all, they didn’t survive the Primordial Era and live this many eras just lying around doing nothing.</p>
<p>They were celestials back then, but many eras had passed, and to preserve the legacy of the Underworld Empress, they had to sacrifice their cultivation.</p>
<p>Now, they are universal gods, but as beings who had once lived as celestials, reaching that stage again is only a matter of time.</p>
<p>So when Runa and Adina are ready, she will take them there to train. It is for their own good, and Kent had no problem with that. The stronger his women get, the better for him and them.</p>

<p>"We have to take the Empress to the underworld to undergo her initiation ritual. Of course, she isnt required to come with us right now. Whenever she is ready, we will go for her.</p>

<p>"Is something happening there?"</p>
<p>Thira answered, "Yes. As it turns out, after the Empress died, a no-name calling himself the god of the underworld, had somehow gained authority over hell and is now ruling it.</p>
<p>However, now that the Underworld Mansion has arrived, his influence is slipping, so he started to organise a resistance with his fickle dream of retaining his dominion."</p>

<p>Indie smiled, feeling amused. Although she can’t challenge even an immortal at her current level, she knew for a fact that whatever those people are doing won’t amount to anything.</p>
