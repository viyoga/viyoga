<p>Kent had already revealed the true nature of the Planet Killer Dungeon to his wives and legion, so they knew death was permanent inside.</p>
<p>This meant that regardless of which side dies, there would be no respawning. So knowing a village was about to be attacked made them want to kill these lackeys of the demon god even more than ever.</p>
<p>The villagers are innocent in the games of the gods; thus, saving them would give them good karma, something almost all of Kent’s ladies and legion have.</p>
<p>At the moment, the members of the Church of Destruction were 2 miles away from the village when the sky suddenly cracked, causing them to halt.</p>
<p>However, the next second, they all scattered, with some vaporising as Kaizo emerged from the void with his entire body coated in golden lightning.</p>
<p>His fist landed on the ground, sending a burst of golden lightning fueled by his bloodline power. This attack was enough to bring chaos to the battlefield in an instant.</p>
<p>Those closest to the impact zone were vaporised by the lightning.</p>
<p>The others appeared the next second, also emerging with terrifying attacks.</p>
<p>"Retreat, it is an ambush," one of the Spirit Lords shouted, causing hundreds of his team members to start running back in haste.</p>
<p>It was indeed an ambush, and Vexthra wasn’t going to let them run away.</p>
<p>"Alina, prevent them from escaping."</p>
<p>The busty Alina was already on the move. She flew into the air and formed a hand seal, manifesting a formation. Well, she manifested a skeleton of the true formation.</p>
<p>"Don’t allow the Spirit Lords to escape." Vexthra knew Alina needed time, so she gave the order, and her team moved.</p>
<p>"Glass Wall." Lady Enzi picked the target that she, Lady Liora, Neomi, and Lani would be fighting.</p>
<p>The Spirit Lord, who was speeding away, suddenly struck an invisible wall, causing glass to shatter. The impact was that strong. However, before he could resume his escape, Lady Enzi made her true move.</p>
<p>"Mirror Dimension." In an instant, the four of them, including the Spirit Lord, fell into a dimension made of mirrors.</p>
<p>"We have 25 minutes before this place comes down," she warned before moving, creating a glass-breaking sound. The next second, her sword clashed with the Spirit Lord, who was wielding a spear.</p>
<p>"A mere Core Formation nobody dares to think you can trap me." His Spirit Lord aura flared, but the ladies had been under worse, so they shook it off and charged forward.</p>
<p>"Lani, do your thing; we will create an opening for you," Neomi said before she moved forward, maintaining her normal form.</p>
<p>In fact, even Lani was using her normal form. They wanted to become strong, so they chose to fight in their normal forms and only take on their battle forms when they had no other options.</p>
<p>On the outside, Kaizo and Sylara had also clashed with the second Spirit Lord, taking him out of the equation.</p>
<p>The third one was also surrounded by Vari, Camilla, and Unity.</p>
<p>Unity was maintaining her distance simply because she didn’t want to miss any of her shots.</p>
<p>Her Primordial Spirits wouldn’t be able to help her since they were currently asleep from overexerting themselves during the battle against the Forsaken race.</p>
<p>However, even with them absent, she was still causing the Spirit Lords to falter.</p>
<p>Camilla was the main attacker, with Vari making sure the Spirit Lord was always watching his back, while Unity was going for the killing shots.</p>
<p>"Alina, when you are done, retreat and go ensure none of them slip away into the village." Alina nodded, drawing a few runes in the air.</p>
<p>After five minutes, the formation was set, and she moved away.</p>
<p>A few minutes later, she stood over the village, looking over the terrified expressions on their faces. Most parents were clutching their children. They all held weary looks on their faces.</p>
<p>When they saw Alina, the few among them who had some cultivation base launched their attacks at her. However, Alina merely brushed them aside with a wave of her hand.</p>
<p>"Relax, everyone, I am not here to hurt you," Alina said, looking toward the strongest in the village.</p>
<p>"My companions have intercepted the Church of Destruction and are handling them. I just came here to ensure none of them slipped away and entered the village."</p>
<p>She waved her hand, and a formation appeared. It enveloped the entire village. After a few seconds, she sighed, "I have placed a formation over the village.</p>
<p>It will prevent anyone aside from me or my companions from entering. It will protect you until we have taken care of them. None of you should step out, else the formation will not be able to protect you."</p>
<p>With that, Alina teleported and appeared beside Vexthra.</p>
<p>"It is done. None of them slipped into the village, and I made sure none could enter."</p>
<p>"Good. You can join your sisters."</p>
<p>Alina nodded and appeared over the battlefield. "Battle Matrix."</p>
<p>Alina activated her Battle Matrix formation she had started working on after they cleared the third floor. A green formation appeared and expanded, covering half of the battlefield.</p>
<p>Her rune knife appeared in her hand, and she started drawing runes. In no time, she added an attack array to the formation, causing it to start attacking.</p>
<p>Vexthra, who saw this, smiled.</p>
<p>"If she is in a high world, or Immortal worlds, Formation families and sects will be bowing at her feet to join them." Her smile turned into pride. "She really is intelligent."</p>
<p>For a goddess to admire and compliment a mortal, they might have done something even a goddess doesn’t seem to think they themselves could.</p>
<p>Bear in mind, Vexthra is a Rune Master herself. So she understood what Alina did, and that was why she called her a genius.</p>
<p>Many formation experts spend weeks, or even months, to create an attacking array as complex, beautiful, and lethal as the one Alina had created within mere minutes.</p>
<p>And the incredible part is that the attacking formation she added was now learning and adapting to the battlefield, making it something that will continue to grow until it becomes unstoppable one of these days.</p>
<p>"Until she reaches the Immortal world, I will never let her join any sect. With her talent, those bastards will take advantage of her."</p>
<p>While Vexthra was admiring Alina, Kaizo’s voice boomed across the battlefield.</p>
<p>"Die, scum!" Sylara stabbed her claw into the chest of the Spirit Lord they were fighting and threw him, crashing to the ground.</p>
<p>RUMBLE</p>
<p>The cloud turned golden as lightning and thunder surged within.</p>
<p>"Titan Overbearing Fist!"</p>
<p>Kaizo came crashing toward the Spirit Lords, whose gazes turned hazy for a moment. "Eat my fist, scum!"</p>
<p>BOOM</p>
<p>Kaizo’s fist landed on his head, exploding it like a watermelon. The ground where the Spirit Lord stood exploded, sending shards of stone flying everywhere.</p>
<p>"That takes care of it." Kaizo moved into the chaos and resumed slaughtering the members of the Church of Destruction.</p>
<p>Sylara also moved, and the next second started gutting the Spirit Sovereigns and those below. She, in fact, had become a menace after forming the Blood Hunger Seal.</p>
<p>If it were up to her, they would be having bloody battles like this always. After all, with every bloody battle, her hunger grew. And as her hunger for blood increased, so did her strength.</p>
<p>Naturally, unless the Blood Hunger Seal is destroyed, she will keep growing stronger.</p>
<p>Of course, she still had a secret that even Kent didn’t know.</p>
<p>But if her plan worked out in the end, she would reveal everything, which would be something that was promised to send her and those around her on a journey of massacre and bloodbath.</p>
