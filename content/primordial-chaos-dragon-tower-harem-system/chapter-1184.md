<p>The teleportation gate Kent and his wives arrived at isn’t that far from the Pill Pagoda, so the moment they arrived, the soul map gave him a clear picture of what awaited him.</p>
<p>He shared the view with his wives, who only shook their heads as they watched thousands, perhaps millions, stand and wait for their doom.</p>
<p>"Sometimes, I don’t get it," Selene said, her expression showing a lost look. "These people clearly heard and perhaps even saw the massacre that happened in Pyris. That was a clear sign for them to run for their dear lives, yet here they are, waiting for something deep down most of them had no business being here for."</p>
<p>Liora smiled and said, "They are a lost cause. I have already seen people far more stupid and dangerously arrogant simply because they believe they have the upper hand after being on top for a while.</p>
<p>Just because under the Pill Families, they faced no opposition, they let that cloud their judgment, so they think they are the ones in charge here.</p>
<p>Not that they are mistaken, it is normal for them to feel that way. They have held the power for a while now, so they are not afraid of anyone. The only downside is they are coming against someone like Kent, who is not afraid of the odds."</p>
<p>The two ladies turned and looked at Kent, who seemed calm and composed despite there being thousands of Heavenly Ascension Stage warriors waiting for him with all kinds of weapons ready to take him on.</p>
<p>"Let’s go. They aren’t going to kill themselves."</p>
<p>They soon left the teleportation gate and headed for the pagoda, where the thousands of alchemists, their guards, and reinforcements were gathered and waiting for them.</p>
<p>In no time, they were only a few km away. Naturally, many had already sensed him, so they started to get ready. Kent and his wives came to a stop when they were only 2 km out.</p>
<p>At once, thousands of people encircled them.</p>
<p>All of them were ready to kill, so when they saw the person who threatened them, they rushed to create a barricade around him.</p>
<p>Kent remained calm, his expression not showing any panic. Naturally, Liora and Selene were the same. They had no fear in their eyes.</p>
<p>A Pill Legend walked forward, his shoulders held high as he addressed Kent in a somewhat arrogant tone.</p>
<p>"Brat, you still have a chance to die swiftly since we are being merciful today. All you have to do is bow and ask for forgiveness.</p>
<p>That will give you a swift and decisive death." His arrogant words have given the thousands surrounding Kent hope and the energy to attack when they give the order.</p>
<p>Kent looked at the man with a smile.</p>
<p>This sight angered him; however, Kent didn’t give him the chance to continue.</p>
<p>He merely snapped his fingers, and one of the sword realms under his disposal activated.</p>
<p>At once, thousands of broken swords made from sword aura appeared in the air and encircled the Pill Legend.</p>
<p>He immediately turned pale. Sweat painted his face and back.</p>
<p>Thousands took several steps back, the feeling of dread enveloping them. Naturally, since Kent had merged his blood qi with his sword aura, whenever he used it, both the sharpness of a sword and the dreadful feeling of blood filled the air.</p>
<p>This feeling of dread now seemed to have enveloped everyone, making them all break out in cold sweats.</p>
<p>Kent turned to the Pill Legend and smirked before he said, "Why don’t you chill for now? I came here for her."</p>
<p>Kent pointed at a lady wearing a veil standing far back in the crowd.</p>
<p>"You are the current leader of the Evil Pill Sect, right? Don’t ask me how I know, I just do. However, since you are here, it means you came with the intention of ending me, so why don’t you come after me directly and let these idiots go?"</p>
<p>The veil lady maintained her calm expression; however, she was taken aback by how easily Kent found her out. That wasn’t exactly how she wanted things to go, but in a way, Kent had figured her out.</p>
<p>Of course, she chose to play calmly; however, things were not as simple as they seemed, especially when someone like Kent was involved.</p>
<p>"Your name is Luna Ovidia. You came from a planet far from this place. Naturally, you and the main leaders of the Evil Sect all want to take over the entire Pill Worlds and use them to make evil pills that will be sent to your employer, the Evil God, Morissa."</p>
<p>Kent chuckled, "Too bad you met me, since after today, there will be no Evil Sect nor evil pills."</p>
<p>Kent moved, and within 2 seconds, he appeared in 14 places. By the time he returned to his original position, 14 heads lay on the ground.</p>
<p>He used just 500 flame veins, but because of his current cultivation base, his speed was several times faster than one would expect.</p>
<p>The veiled lady broke into a cold sweat and was about to make a run for it when a dagger was pressed against her neck. "Be a dear and don’t make things difficult for yourself." Liora had long made her move, knowing her man will have a use for her.</p>
<p>Kent turned to the thousands gathered.</p>
<p>"Those who want to leave, you have a minute to distance yourself further from this place. After a minute, I will spare no one."</p>
<p>Hearing what Kent said, thousands started to run, yet thousands remained standing. They were, of course, from the Evil Sect and some devoted members of the Pill Families.</p>
<p>They wouldn’t run, not when they were being threatened by a brat and his two wives. Of curse, the evil sect members wouldnt run when their leader is at the mercy of Liora, the Scarlet Angel</p>
<p>Naturally, they regretted that decision since after a minute, Kent started his massacre like he promised.</p>
<p>Strangely, those who stayed couldn’t even move their fingers, so they merely remained standing as Kent killed them rather easily.</p>
<p>By the time the last of them was cut down, Kent broke through to the Spirit Lord Stage and even managed to climb to Level Three.</p>
<p>It was a devastating massacre, one that left those who ran to wipe the idea of ever coming against the Pill Worlds off their minds.</p>
<p>Once things were settled, Kent mind-controlled the Evil Sect leader, who would take his legion to all the hideouts of the Evil Sect, where they would kill all members of the Evil Sect.</p>
