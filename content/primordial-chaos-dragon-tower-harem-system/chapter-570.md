<p>Kent learned from Elaine’s father that they were once a family of powerful Life magicians. Long before the battle of parting, the Wynthrall rose to the peak of this world, producing many immortals.</p>
<p>Back then, one could rise to the ranks of Heavenly Ascension, and after calling down the heavenly tribulation, they could ascend to the Immortal worlds.</p>
<p>There were no High worlds or Medium worlds back then.</p>
<p>However, once one reached the limit, which was at the Heavenly Ascension stage (the peak in a high world now), they would ascend using tribulations or an ascension stone.</p>
<p>The Wynthrall family produced many geniuses who were sent away to enrich the family name in the immortal worlds.</p>
<p>They were bigger and more powerful, even bigger than the most powerful sects. However, that all came down within the blink of an eye.</p>
<p>When the war that separated the races and placed limits on the world descended, they lost everything. Their bloodline was cursed, and from what Kent learned, this curse was a Tier 15 spell that even affected the Elves.</p>
<p>By estimation, the spell came from the Dark Region. However, nobody dares go there simply because nobody wants to know what is hidden there.</p>
<p>Just like how nobody dares enter the Dead Region in the ancient battlefield of parting, nobody wants to enter the Dark Region, too.</p>
<p>However, the same curse that brought down an entire family and perhaps an entire race was now being broken, and the one doing that was the inexperienced Formation Master who had now miraculously gained the means to draw thousands of runes within minutes.</p>
<p>Kent could just smile and stand there watching as thousands of runes joined the ones already in the air.</p>
<p>"I don’t understand something. If the spell is still active, how can you break the curse on her?" Kent asked, thinking about the Tier 15 spell that was used to curse Elaine’s bloodline.</p>
<p>"More importantly, are you telling me you can write Tier 15 formations to break Tier 15 spells?" Vexthra also asked, and her sisters, except Unity, wanted both answers.</p>
<p>"What’s there to question? Sister Alina is awesome." Unity, of course, tried to hype her sister, which this time, her sister didn’t mind.</p>
<p>They wanted to know what Kent and Vexthra had asked about.</p>
<p>"No, I can’t write Tier 15 formations yet, but that doesn’t mean I can’t break the curse. And for the question on whether the curse can be broken without the main spell being destroyed, the answer is simple.</p>
<p>I am merely taking her out of the equation." Alina answered a whole lot of questions.</p>
<p>"How are you going to take her out of the equation?" Val asked.</p>
<p>"Also very simple. The formation I am creating contains two different arrays. One is called the Separation Array. It separates the curse from Elaine. Basically, I will be cloning the curse.</p>
<p>Then, once I do that, the second array, the Isolation Array, will completely isolate the curse from her, leaving her to enjoy her bloodline like the curse was never there.</p>
<p>And with the True Name on the way, I can already see something coming out of this."</p>
<p>"I don’t really understand anything she said. Big sister Vexthra, can you explain?" Val said, wanting to understand all Alina had said.</p>
<p>"It sounded simpler than it really is, but what she is doing is, as she said, very simple. She will separate the curse on Elaine’s bloodline using the same formation/array used for cloning.</p>
<p>Only this time, the clone will be the curse, and the main body will be Elaine with a perfect bloodline," Vexthra explained.</p>
<p>"However, I also have a question... Alina, what or where would you put the clone, which is the curse? I suppose destroying will be impossible unless, of course, I intervene."</p>
<p>Her sisters turned toward the beautiful Alina.</p>
<p>"I will put it inside the Celestial Portrait of the Eternal Garden. Something this powerful should not be destroyed; you never know when you will need it."</p>
<p>"This..."</p>
<p>Vexthra was puzzled.</p>
<p>The Celestial Portrait of the Eternal Garden is something immortals, gods, supremes, and even celestials want to glimpse at least once in their lifetime.</p>
<p>It contains profound teachings, people, events, and history that a single chance to glimpse will benefit anyone.</p>
<p>However, such a profound treasure is now being used as a vault to house a Tier 15 curse.</p>
<p>’This... if the Supreme Celestial hears this, he is going to be pissed. I can already see him going on a rampage.’</p>
<p>’I better make sure she never takes this out in public. If it belongs to her or once belonged to her, then she can create a new one. I am sure nobody will try to kill her for that.’</p>
<p>An hour passed, and Alina was still drawing the rune. Thankfully, she stopped and formed a seal after a few more minutes, bringing all the runes together to form one powerful rune.</p>
<p>"This is Tier 10... You are already a Formation King," Vexthra said, a little shocked.</p>
<p>"It’s all thanks to our man," Alina smiled, causing Kent’s head to grow big. But who could blame him? Being surrounded by dozens of beautiful women, each complimenting him.</p>
<p>Even a Saint would feel some pride in that.</p>
<p>"I am going to put this formation in you. Don’t worry, it is not painful, and will be over before you know it," Alina said, and Elaine nodded.</p>
<p>"Alright, you all can step back except Kent." Her sisters did, and Kent walked closer.</p>
<p>"What do you want me to do?" Kent asked.</p>
<p>"The moment the curse is out, I want you to cut its head off. And don’t ask. I want you instead of big sister Vexthra because your Eternal Body makes you immune to curses."</p>
<p>"I guess that is something new I am just learning about my new mutated body," Kent smiled and got ready.</p>
<p>Alina directed the formation rune and entered Elaine’s forehead.</p>
<p>The moment it did, a powerful energy burst out of her body, and with it was another copy of Elaine. However, this one had deep, dark eyes instead of the green ones on Elaine.</p>
<p>As Alina directed, Kent’s sword came and went like a flash. On the ground, however, was the severed head of the curse that appeared like a person wearing Elaine’s face but with a dark set of eyes.</p>
