<p>To unleash the [Claw Art], Kent is required to master Dragon Intent.</p>
<p>Back when Chaos used this technique, he wasn’t required to have Dragon Intent before he could unleash it. He merely combined Chaos Qi and Blood Qi to power the technique.</p>
<p>Now, he must have Dragon Intent before he can use it. The problem, however, is that he has no idea what Dragon Intent is. He had been a dragon before, but today was his first time hearing that there was something called Dragon Intent.</p>
<p>Turning to Tower, he expected her to have an explanation.</p>
<p>But to his shock, she smiled and pointed at the purple cocoon 20 million km away. With a single step, Kent appeared before it.</p>
<p>His [Heavenly Jade Eye] scanned it, and what he saw made him wonder if he was lucky or just fortunate.</p>
<p>[You have discovered Dragon Intent.]</p>
<p>[Your Dragon Intent is currently in the process of forming. Absorb more Chaos and Blood Energy to speed up the process.]</p>
<p>[Current Progress: 23%]</p>
<p>Kent smiled and nodded. "It isn’t like I need this technique right now anyway. Once I am out of this place, I will make sure it awakens fully by absorbing Chaos and Blood Essence."</p>
<p>Seeing everything worked out in the end, Kent smiled and turned to Tower. "What was it you wanted to show me?"</p>
<p>Tower waved her hand, and seven red fruits appeared before Kent. "This came with the three tomes. I believe they are skills you must learn."</p>
<p>Kent nodded and reached for the first red fruit. He examined it for a few seconds before tossing it into his mouth. The moment it entered his mouth, it dissolved, spreading all over his body.</p>
<p>Kent felt his body become stronger and more energetic. A few seconds later, messages appeared before his eyes.</p>
<p>---</p>
<p>[You have consumed a Life Dao Skill Fruit.]</p>
<p>[You have awakened a new skill: Absolute Healing.]</p>
<p>[Absolute Healing]</p>
<p>[Rank: Celestial]</p>
<p>[Description: As long as your heart and soul are not completely crushed, you can heal from any injuries. The more energy you have, the faster the healing process.]</p>
<p>---</p>
<p>Kent furrowed his brow, a wave of happiness surging through his body. He can already be likened to someone with rather superior healing abilities. However, this new skill has elevated the process to a whole different level.</p>
<p>Reaching forward, he grabbed another fruit and tossed it into his mouth. Like before, the same thing happened, and before long, the messages appeared.</p>
<p>[You have consumed a Life Dao Skill Fruit.]</p>
<p>[You have awakened a new skill: Demon Master Regeneration.]</p>
<p>[Demon Master Regeneration]</p>
<p>[Rank: Celestial]</p>
<p>[Description: You now regenerate your lost energy and injuries 10,000 times faster.]</p>
<p>"Damn..." Kent felt his soul sea surge, and then waves of energy started pouring into his body. All of a sudden, he felt his Blood Core and Chaos Heart growing stronger.</p>
<p>His [Primordial Chaos Eternal Body] and [Primal Chaos Art] made it so that his body can convert all kinds of energy, provided there is enough in the atmosphere.</p>
<p>So while there is no Blood Qi or Chaos Qi in the air, the Spiritual Energy is being converted. Initially, Kent sensed he was growing stronger just by being there.</p>
<p>However, this new ability has sped things up.</p>
<p>While the process is still a bit slower, the mere fact that his Blood Core has already increased by 1% saturation shows it is only a matter of time before the Chaos Heart does as well.</p>
<p>"I know demons have high regeneration abilities; however, I never expected a Demon Master to possess this much regeneration."</p>
<p>Tower smirked, hearing what Kent had said. "The master is overestimating demons. While it is indeed true that True Demons possess high regeneration abilities, a Master Demon can boast only 1,000 times regeneration.</p>
<p>The fact that the master has 10,000 times that shows he is no ordinary person. In fact, the master can be compared to a Demon Saint or even a Demon Sage."</p>
<p>Kent smiled, feeling like he had looked down on himself. But with more fruits left to eat, he reached forward and grabbed a third one.</p>
<p>---</p>
<p>[You have consumed a Life Dao Skill Fruit.]</p>
<p>[You have awakened a new skill: High Regeneration.]</p>
<p>[Because you have already awakened Demon Master Regeneration, a mutation has occurred. Demon Master Regeneration has merged with High Regeneration.]</p>
<p>[A new skill has been formed. You have unlocked a new skill: Demon Saint Regeneration.]</p>
<p>[Demon Saint Regeneration]</p>
<p>[Rank: Celestial]</p>
<p>[Description: You now regenerate your lost energy and injuries 250,000 times faster. You can regenerate lost limbs and organs in seconds.]</p>
<p>Kent looked at the message and smiled. Ignoring the surge of energy pouring into his body, he grabbed the second fruit and tossed it into his mouth.</p>
<p>---</p>
<p>[You have consumed a Life Dao Skill Fruit.]</p>
<p>[You have awakened a new skill: Great Regeneration.]</p>
<p>[Because you have already awakened Demon Saint Regeneration, a mutation has occurred. Demon Saint Regeneration has merged with Great Regeneration.]</p>
<p>[A new skill has been formed. You have unlocked a new skill: Demon King Regeneration.]</p>
<p>[Demon King Regeneration]</p>
<p>[Rank: Celestial]</p>
<p>[Description: You now regenerate your lost energy and injuries 500,000 times faster. You can regenerate lost limbs and organs in mere seconds. As long as you have a body part left, you can regenerate your entire body.]</p>
<p>"This is getting out of hand."</p>
<p>Kent laughed and tossed the fifth fruit into his mouth. Before long, a similar mutation happened, and so he continued to the next, and before long, all seven fruits were consumed.</p>
<p>By then, the wave of energy pouring into Kent’s body had reached a whole new level.</p>
<p>His regeneration ability had also reached a new level that even a Demon Lord would cry tears of anguish if they learned a mere dragon had obtained such regeneration.</p>
<p>---</p>
<p>[Your Demon Lord Regeneration has undergone both a mutation and an upgrade.]</p>
<p>[Your Demon Lord Regeneration has mutated and become Demon God Regeneration.]</p>
<p>[Your Demon God Regeneration ability has been upgraded from Celestial grade to Innate.]</p>
<p>[Demon God Regeneration]</p>
<p>[Rank: Innate]</p>
<p>[Ranking: 4 Stars]</p>
<p>[Description: You can now regenerate your lost energy and injuries 5 million times faster.]</p>
<p>"Hmm, I never knew a day like this would ever come. With this, I can go all out and wouldn’t have to worry about running out of energy," Kent laughed, his expression showing how happy he had become.</p>
<p>The kind of power in his hands now is just beyond what he had expected. Not even Chaos at his demigod stage possessed such power.</p>
<p>"Congratulations, Master. Looks like trading the Life Dao Stone from Gaia was a good trade," Tower giggled, very happy for her master.</p>
<p>While Kent was taken aback to learn that the stone came from Gaia, he had no idea he had already paid for it and was even in debt.</p>
<p>Aside from Vexthra using all of his Tower Points and even borrowing some to buy the [Void Null Shield], Tower, after her upgrade, was able to contact Gaia and inform her of the latest development.</p>
<p>Of course, since she is a cosmic being, giving something as valuable as the Life Dao Stone would break the balance of the universe, so she sold it for a staggering 100 billion Tower Points.</p>
<p>This price offset the karma involved.</p>
<p>Tower bought it, and now that Kent had benefited from it, she had no regrets.</p>
<p>"Time for the master to go back to his mission. A lot had happened while you were locked up in here."</p>
<p>Kent nodded and opened his eyes on the outside, meeting Princess Pandora’s gaze.</p>
