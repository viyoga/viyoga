<p>"Thank you, Kent. Had it not been for you, we wouldn’t have made it even to the 3rd level," Norion said the moment they stepped out of the portal.</p>
<p>Kent smiled and shook his head. "I merely provided the tools; you did all the work, so you should be proud of yourselves."</p>
<p>"Even so, you’ve changed our lives, and for that, we will forever be grateful," Princess Elowen said with a bow, and the seven others followed.</p>
<p>The hall became calm, filled with happiness, as all eight half-elves looked at the human who had made their dreams come true and even added some additional perks to it.</p>
<p>Not only had they reached the 5th level of the flame tower, somewhere nobody had ever gotten, but they also managed to learn a divine-grade technique—something that can take them as far as an Immortal world, and, in their case, will continue to increase in strength as their soul flames grow stronger.</p>
<p>Aside from that, he even helped them awaken their Flame Bodies.</p>
<p>What he had done for them was akin to changing the fate of an entire race; only this time, he had done it by turning eight half-elves into powerhouses.</p>
<p>They were grateful, and if Kent were to be a god, he would have felt their fate in his strengthening. Of course, he could see it for the soul essence he was receiving from the Half-Elves had just increased by 3.</p>
<p>He smiled, feeling like an accomplished con artist.</p>
<p>Of course, this time, he hadn’t scammed anyone, but had worked for it.</p>
<p>"The next few days should be about you eight training like your lives depended on it. The endgame is to rise above all else and surpass everyone who stands in your path.</p>
<p>Use the next few days to get accustomed to your Flame Bodies and focus on three key techniques. If you can’t, focus on two, but prioritize defense and enhancement.</p>
<p>I have a feeling you eight are going to be famous in the sect."</p>
<p>They nodded and walked away to attend to their tasks. Uncle Drake walked into the hall a few seconds later and sat beside Kent.</p>
<p>"They’ve even surpassed expectations. Not only did they manage to learn seven mythic-grade techniques, but they also learned a divine-grade technique.</p>
<p>This time, my race will also soar above all else," King Thalirion said with a cheerful smile. The elders and Queen Jascia wore the same smile.</p>
<p>"They indeed surprise even me. However, this also shows that they have potential that will take them even further. For now, they must join the sect.</p>
<p>That said, I believe they have what it takes to get in, so we shouldn’t be worried. Also, I think with the path they’ve taken, their growth will only continue to soar."</p>
<p>Kent turned to Uncle Drake. "You must help them in the next few days. You are the best person to help them when it comes to using the flame."</p>
<p>"I will do my best." Uncle Drake was already looking forward to this assignment; after all, he could tell something had changed about them.</p>
<p>They looked and felt more confident, and for that, he wanted to see what he could do to bring out more of their potential.</p>
<p>"I will be gone for the next six days, so I might even miss the auction. But don’t worry, Uncle Drake is here for any trouble.</p>
<p>Additionally, be prepared to start receiving visitors soon. I know you can handle them. After all, you already have your own thing going on."</p>
<p>The Half-Elves laughed, and Kent stood up.</p>
<p>Now that he saw a way forward in the path of flame cultivation, he wanted to work on that. But first, he must visit the ladies currently preparing to enter the third floor of the Planet Killer Dungeon.</p>
<p>A few seconds later, he left...</p>

<p>[100 Immortal and 99 Divine World Star System – Divine World Naia – The Devil’s Flame Peak]</p>
<p>Planet Naia is one of the 99 Divine worlds in the 100 Immortal and 99 Divine World Star System. In the realm of immortal worlds, this star system is one of the strongest.</p>
<p>It contains 100 immortal worlds and 99 Divine worlds. Planet Naia is one of the divine worlds that, by understanding, is capable of housing both immortals and gods.</p>
<p>It is much larger, perhaps 10,000 times larger than Sonox. And just like Sonox, it is a mixed-race planet, meaning it has humans, Elves, Orcs, Dragons, and the like.</p>
<p>However, away from all that, it also has some of the most dangerous places in the universe.</p>
<p>One such place is the Devil’s Flame Peak. It is a mountain made of flames and magma. Its terror is unlike any other.</p>
<p>Those below the True Immortal stage dare not set foot even thousands of miles from it. Even Lord Immortals go there at their peril.</p>
<p>Perhaps only demi-gods and gods dare go there. But even gods with no good grasp of the flame dare not get cocky and try to traverse the vast and colossal mountain said to share a boundary with the first Layer of Hell.</p>
<p>However, while many dare not venture there, some with balls large enough have gone. Of course, some never return; however, Agni Sauron, the Flame Lord, has been in seclusion there for the past 24 thousand years.</p>
<p>He went there after he finally managed to hide the first form of the [Finger of Destruction] Technique. Over the past 24,000 years, he has been cultivating his flame resistance and comprehension.</p>
<p>However, just a few minutes ago, someone who had his eyes closed for the past 24 years finally opened them.</p>
<p>That simple gesture caused the Devil’s Flame Peak to shudder, causing a monster stampede that the Flame Orcs bordering the location where he was secluded would have to take care of.</p>
<p>"Hahahahaha..." Agni, on the other hand, didn’t care about the fact that he had unleashed hordes of immortal stage beasts on the orcs.</p>
<p>His laughter rang out for many minutes before calming down.</p>
<p>"The Primordial Chaos Dragon has finally awakened, and the first person to know is me." His smile bloomed. "I will meet you soon, my lord. This humble servant will soon meet you and become a vessel you can command."</p>
<p>His fist slammed his chest three times like a warrior swearing his allegiance.</p>
<p>Suddenly, the space around him cracked, and a scroll fell. Agni caught it with a surprised look and immediately unfurled it.</p>
<p>The moment his eyes landed on the content of the scroll, a shocked look appeared on his face...</p>
<p>⟦You have been recognized by the Last of the True Primordials – The Primordial Chaos Dragon. To show your dedication and commitment to serving him, you are hereby commanded to cross the boundary and retrieve the Hell Flame Scroll and deliver it to the High Priestess of Mekyn (High World).</p>
<p>You have three years to complete it. Failure is not an option.</p>
<p>After the mission is done, your rewards will be delivered with added perks. Good luck.⟧</p>
<p>Agni looked at the scroll disintegrating in his hands, a smile blossoming on his face. He stood up and walked for a few seconds before standing at the entrance of the cave he was in.</p>
<p>"I guess it is time to get chased down to hell." His laughter echoed in the mountains as he vanished into thin air.</p>
