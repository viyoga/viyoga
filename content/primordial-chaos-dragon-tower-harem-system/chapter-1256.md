<p>As always, everything must come to an end, and as such, the battle between Kent and the Forsaken Seers ended like Kent wanted.</p>
<p>Of course, there were some hiccups here and there. However, Kent wanted them all dead, so he ensured the end matched the outcome he envisioned.</p>
<p>They are all dead from both his hand and that of the immortal core explosion.</p>
<p>However, one of the hiccups was the fact that he couldn’t clear his debt as he had envisioned. Per the calculation the tower made, if he were to kill every last warrior, the amount of tower points he would have earned would have been enough to clear his debt.</p>
<p>He did kill all, but the outcome wasn’t as he expected.</p>
<p>Despite all dragons and forsaken beings dying, he is still 1.2 billion tower points in debt. Naturally, that was because of the Immortal Core explosion.</p>
<p>That explosion had killed more dragons than he would have wanted. And since he wasn’t the one who made the kills, the tower points that should have come from them didn’t.</p>
<p>In fact, their death also wasted precious ’Blood Essence’ and ’Soul Spirits’.</p>
<p>To earn a tower point, Blood Essence or Soul Spirit, Kent was supposed to be the one to do the kill. Of course, in the aspect of Soul Spirit and tower points, if his legion and wives kill, he can collect them.</p>
<p>All linked to him adds to the tower point and soul spirits. So if Vexthra ended up killing a god, Kent will earn tower points and the soul spirit from that kill.</p>
<p>However, in an open battle like this, the one who earns the loot is only Kent, so when the core exploded and thousands died, they didn’t count as Kent’s kills.</p>
<p>This deprived him of precious tower points to clear the debt he owes Gaia.</p>
<p>But he had cleared 2.2 billion off his debt, which is more than enough to keep him going for now.</p>
<p>Kent walked to a block and coated it with ice before sitting down. He looks at the horizon and lets out a deep breath.</p>
<p>The battle is over, but he doesn’t seem satisfied.</p>
<p>He had just killed a clone of a True Immortal who he thought would have given him a run for his money. However, in the end, she was such a disappointment.</p>
<p>The battle as a whole was a huge disappointment.</p>
<p>But Kent also knew she would notice him now, and soon, she would come after him in her True Body. Maybe then, he would get to experience the power of a forsaken seer.</p>
<p>But while the battle wasn’t up to the standard of a forsaken battle, Kent had discovered something dangerous that the rest of the universe should be made aware of.</p>
<p>The forsaken race is making moves in the shadows, and if the universe doesn’t do anything, soon, the Forsaken War will happen again, and this time, the universe will find itself in grave danger.</p>
<p>The forsaken race was only sealed by Arien’s past self during the Primordial Era.</p>
<p>She never placed a limit on their growth—she couldn’t. So they had been growing since the Primordial Era and multiplying.</p>
<p>One day, they would come, and Kent, inasmuch as he would love to slaughter them then since his campaign had always been to kill the forsaken race, wasn’t blinded by hatred anymore, so he knew he had to draw the universe’s attention to this.</p>
<p>’I will do my best. If nobody listens, then it will be on them.’</p>
<p>He took another deep breath and tapped into his status page to see the gains he had received after absorbing thousands of Blood Essence into his first blood core...</p>
<p>’Although my first core isn’t saturated yet, I feel so powerful now.’</p>
<p>[You have absorbed enough Blood Essence to level up. Spirit Lord Level 4]</p>
<p>[You have absorbed enough Blood Essence to level up. Spirit Lord Level 5]</p>
<p>[You have absorbed enough Blood Essence to level up. Spirit Lord Level 5]</p>
<p>.</p>
<p>.</p>
<p>.</p>
<p>[You have absorbed enough Blood Essence to level up. Heavenly Ascension Level 4]</p>
<p>Upon reading the messages, a pale smile spread across Kent’s handsome face. He had taken a massive leap toward the Heavenly Ascension stage in just 13 minutes.</p>
<p>The unholy baptism of chaos flame was so violent that within minutes, thousands had died. And since he has the tower and the Soul Universe to handle the Blood Essence absorption, he just ranked up like a madman.</p>
<p>’This is indeed like the fortuitous encounter many talk about. Who knew that in the pursuit of scum who like to prey on the weak would land me in such a Blood Essence farming?’</p>
<p>Kent didn’t even know how to feel about the progress he had made. He at least expected to reach the peak of the Spirit Lord stage; however, in the end, killing four immortals tended to have so many more benefits than he anticipated.</p>
<p>’Hmm, let’s hope I can hold my own against those immortals when we go to save Kaelith. If I can kill just a dozen immortals, I might just saturate the first blood core and even take steps toward saturating the second blood core.</p>
<p>But even if I couldn’t do much, the fact that I will get to see how strong immortals are would be of great help to what Vexthra hinted at.’</p>
<p>’Kent, I have saved her meridians and put her in an induced coma. Unfortunately, her little daughter was so heartbroken that she passed out.</p>
<p>This is why you should kill the person involved slowly and painfully. I hate people who hurt the innocent.’ Elaine’s furious voice entered Kent’s mind, causing a pale smile to appear on Kent’s face.</p>
<p>[The battle is over, my love. However, rest assured, I will find a way for us to get our revenge.]</p>
<p>Elaine nodded, pacing back and forth inside a room where Aala and her mother were soundly asleep.</p>
<p>Since the one involved is dead, the curse can now be broken. Liora was working on that as they spoke.</p>
<p>’When do you plan on returning?’ Elaine asked.</p>
<p>[Gaia is still not done with the city for the Sky Foxes, so I will hang around for a few hours to check a few things. When she is done, I will meet you ladies on Planet Tianxu.]</p>
<p>’Okay. Take care.’</p>
<p>[I will, my love.]</p>

<p>He quickly assessed that.</p>
