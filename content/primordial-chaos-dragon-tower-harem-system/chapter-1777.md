<p>"I never knew a day would come when Zdzislav would praise a man," Veylan Seren said in a teasing tone, causing Zdzislav, known for singing praises to women as a form of flirting, to roll his eyes.</p>
<p>Initially, one would think Borislav was the rowdy one. However, Veylan Seren was actually the one with a more easy-going attitude.</p>
<p>Borislav just loves teasing everyone, regardless of their strength or the seriousness of the situation.</p>
<p>But what Veylan Seren said was indeed true. Zdzislav is known for perfection and not easily impressed.</p>
<p>The Astral Eye Chaos Tribe specializes in cultivating the Heavenly Chaos Eye Canon. It is one of the deadliest cultivation arts in the Chaos Chasm.</p>
<p>They cultivate their eyes to see the unseen and perceive things that most people would find extremely hard, if not impossible, to see, such as the Laws of Chaos and others.</p>
<p>Aside from that, they can also harness Chaos into beams and all kinds of abilities that, from what many had seen and witnessed over the last billions of years, make them one of the most feared races.</p>
<p>The ability to see before reacting or to predict an outcome merely from looking at a person makes them unpredictable and, in most cases, extremely dangerous to underestimate.</p>
<p>Kent had, at one point, cultivated the Omniscience Dao Eye, which had evolved through many stages into what it was before he entered the cycle of reincarnation.</p>
<p>At the moment, he had reached the Omniscient Jade Eye. However, with the technique he was about to receive and his extensive knowledge of cultivating his eyes, there was no telling what kind of monster he would soon become.</p>
<p>"There is hope for eye cultivators after all," Zdzislav said before transmitting the technique to Kent.</p>
<p>"Remember, according to the records left behind by past experts, the Heavenly Chaos Eye Canon has the ability to unlock the 7th dimension, where Existence was said to have originated from.</p>
<p>Nobody had ever reached that stage. In fact, the furthest anyone had ever reached was the 5th dimension, so I can’t say much. However, I believe and trust that one day, you can make it that far. When that day comes, and I still live, I would love to experience it, even if just for a moment."</p>
<p>Kent nodded. "I will do my best not to disappoint your expectations of me, Tribe Leader."</p>
<p>"I know you won’t because I trust you."</p>
<p>After he returned to his seat, Namarie, the leader of the Eternal Root Chaos Tribe, stood up and immediately started to speak...</p>
<p>"My Eternal Root Chaos Tribe is known for possessing healing abilities beyond measure. That is true by several degrees.</p>
<p>We are indeed a tribe of healers. However, we also possess a lethal side, though we lean more toward healing since, in my opinion, that’s better for everyone.</p>
<p>The World Root Chaos Scripture I am about to transfer to you has two parts. We have the Life Part, which concerns healing, and the Death Part, which concerns killing.</p>
<p>You can learn both, but I would advise you to go for the healing first. In my opinion, you need your life intact before you can think about killing someone else."</p>
<p>Kent nodded, then opened his soul and mind to receive the technique.</p>
<p>Afterward, Tumenk of the Celestial Abyss Chaos Tribe smiled, approached Kent, and placed his hand on his shoulder before speaking...</p>
<p>"There are nine stages to every Chaos Heart, and the Primordial Abyss Chaos Heart Art is meant to cultivate all nine stages."</p>
<p>Kent furrowed his brow, intrigued.</p>
<p>"Your current Chaos Heart hasn’t even stepped into the first stage. The moment it does, the current power of your Chaos Heart will multiply by ten. The second stage will multiply it by a hundred, and the third by a thousand."</p>
<p>"That sounds too good to be true," Kent said with a laugh, but Tumenk just smiled and said...</p>
<p>"It does sound too good to be true. However, it is the source of my power, so I wouldn’t lie. You will first cultivate the Abyss and merge it with Chaos, awakening Abyssal Chaos Energy, the pinnacle of Chaos Energy cultivation.</p>
<p>Once that is achieved, you will have to channel Abyssal Energy and Chaos Energy to refine your Chaos Heart until you achieve all nine stages."</p>
<p>"Which stage have you reached currently?" Kent asked.</p>
<p>"The 4th stage, still nowhere near the 5th stage. But even if I get stuck here, I have enough energy to keep me going until I reach the Universe Stage."</p>
<p>"Wow, that sounds incredible."</p>
<p>Tumenk smiled and answered, "That it does. However, it would take time to cultivate even a single stage, so for now, I will transfer it to you and provide more than enough treasures to aid you in reaching at least the first stage."</p>
<p>A few minutes later, he walked back to his seat, and the last tribe leader stood up.</p>
<p>Parand narrowed her eyes at Kent for a few seconds before she spoke, "I can sense a familiar wing treasure on you, kid. Do you happen to have a pair of Chaos Storm Wings?"</p>
<p>She summoned her four pairs of metallic wings, causing Kent to raise his brow. Then he also summoned the Jade Void Wings. The moment the wings appeared, Parand’s eyes widened, and she quickly moved toward them.</p>
<p>She touched both pairs as if to see if they were the ones she remembered.</p>
<p>As expected, they were the ones mentioned in the records.</p>
<p>"I believe these are called Jade Void Wings," she asked, and Kent nodded.</p>
<p>Her eyes sparkled upon getting the confirmation. This made Kent and everyone else wonder why she was crying.</p>
<p>Naturally, she gave them the answer a few minutes later.</p>
<p>"My tribe cultivates the Heaven Splitting Chaos Wings, perhaps the deadliest flight-type technique in the world if your comprehension is deep enough. However, to cultivate the Heaven Splitting Chaos Wings, you need to first awaken a wing."</p>
<p>She gestured toward her wings and continued, "Mine are the Flame Chaos Wings, meant to harness both the flame and chaos. Depending on which wings you awaken, you will have a dedicated cultivation method for them.</p>
<p>Over the years, rumors have circulated that when the Chaos Ascension King bestowed the technique on the first ancestor, he mentioned a wing that harnesses the power of the Void. This wing, if awakened, is destined to become the first ever to reach nine pairs and be unparalleled by all.</p>
<p>We have tried for years to awaken it, but have failed every time. Never in my wildest dreams did I expect it to appear here today, and with someone who doesn’t even have the Heaven Splitting Chaos Wings cultivation art."</p>
<p>She looked Kent in the eye and asked, "How did you awaken the wings?"</p>
<p>"I didn’t awaken them. They were a gift from a very powerful Void cultivator who lived during the Primordial Era. I stumbled upon a trial he left during the Chaos Era of the Infinity Universe, and after passing it, these wings were handed to me.</p>
<p>Since then, they have been mine."</p>
<p>Parand stood stunned for a few minutes before a smile appeared on her face. "I don’t really care where you got the wings from. As long as you can cultivate the Heaven Splitting Chaos Wings, we will all witness the power of the infamous Jade Void Wings."</p>
<p>She transferred the technique to Kent in less than five minutes, then walked back to her seat.</p>
<p>Kent was a bit stunned by all that had just happened, but he brushed the lingering effects aside and focused on the next important details.</p>
