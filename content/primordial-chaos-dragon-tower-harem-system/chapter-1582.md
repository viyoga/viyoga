<p>The arrival of the one Celestial was well within expectations. The Garden Master was one of the few people who personally knew Lord Genesis and, by extension, knew Inaya.</p>
<p>In fact, he was once a disciple of the Whispering Wind Sword Sect when Inaya was the sect master. So his appearance could be taken lightly.</p>
<p>However, the second arrival was what made many frown, some turning pale.</p>
<p>A terrible pressure filled with blood and killing intent descended on everyone, causing even some Celestials to grit their teeth.</p>
<p>"Sangreos, what are you doing on this side of the Universe?" the Garden Master asked, a frown appearing on his otherwise handsome face.</p>
<p>"Hahahahahaha, Matthew, what makes you think I can’t come here when clearly something historic is happening? All you think I will miss the rebirth of the Ten Emotions Sword Supreme."</p>
<p>At the mention of Inaya’s title, a crimson river tore from the clouds and descended on the planet, causing it to tremble. Had it not been for the Garden Master anticipating this and already taking measures, millions would have died.</p>
<p>From the clouds, a man with black hair streaked with deep crimson descended. He has pale skin with veins that glow visibly beneath it.</p>
<p>His eyes are made of liquid rubies, dripping faint blood tears. As he descended, many instinctively took steps back, some moving to hide behind the Garden Master.</p>
<p>When the 100th Elder of the Soul Temple appears, nobody needs to be told to run. So they ran, and now, as they hid behind the Garden Master, the killing intent filling the place seemed to have receded a little.</p>
<p>Sangreos, or as many called him, the Crimson Sovereign of Eternal Veins, turned and looked at Inaya with a twisted smile.</p>
<p>Xarion stepped forward, shielding Inaya from the danger. His expression narrowed, but he said nothing to the Celestial, whom, deep down, everyone present knew was the strongest currently present.</p>
<p>Even the Garden Master had to acknowledge the strength of one of the 1000 elders of the Soul Temple. He ranked 200th, and he is a Celestial King. One can only wonder how strong the master of the Soul Temple would be.</p>
<p>"Isn’t it funny how we all seem to have ended up at the same place?" Celestial Sangreos said, his twisted smile widening. Looking past Xarion, his gaze landed on Inaya.</p>
<p>"The last time we met, you and that monster killed my brother and best friend, leaving me to die in that godforsaken den. Now, look at me, living the best time of my life, and you are about to die before you have the chance to even form your Supreme Platform."</p>
<p>The six supreme beasts standing before Inaya unleashed their aura, every last one of them ready to fight to the death. As long as Inaya is alive, even if they die, they can be brought back.</p>
<p>"Stay behind me, my Lady," Moltros said, his defense already activated. Being the one with the strongest defense, his duty would be to protect Inaya with his life.</p>
<p>"How funny. It appears the Heavens Faction has been so peaceful that now mere Supremes think they can contend against a Celestial."</p>
<p>Celestial Sangreos laughed and turned to the Garden Master. "Haven’t you told them what happened the last time we fought?"</p>
<p>BOOOM</p>
<p>The Garden Master stood up, and his aura exploded, pushing back the red aura that had appeared in the air. "Becareful, Sangreos, you are far from home, so tread carefully, else it won’t end well for you."</p>
<p>"Very confident of you, Matthew. However, we both know killing me is impossible, not when there is blood running through everyone’s veins here."</p>
<p>"What if you don’t have blood but ice?" Before the Garden Master could reply, a lady spoke, and the temperature suddenly dropped.</p>
<p>A large bluish star appeared and enlarged, taking up a vast space. Then, from within, a beauty with blue hair and an hourglass shape walked out, her expression cold like ice.</p>
<p>In her hand is a staff made of a mystical material found only in the Celestial realm.</p>
<p>"What if there is no blood running through my body but ice?"</p>
<p>"I never expected the Azure Star Sovereign to also make an appearance here," Sangreos remarked, his expression shifting from delight to caution.</p>
<p>While before the Garden Master, he seemed relaxed and even arrogant, the person who had appeared was someone he could not mess around with.</p>
<p>The cold look on the Azure Star Sovereign’s face broke as a pale smile appeared, illuminating her beauty. "A bit bold of you to come all the way here without backup, don’t you think?"</p>
<p>Sangreos didn’t respond; instead, he looked at Inaya and smirked. "Knowing you are back fills me with joy. Now, I will do everything in my power to ensure you don’t live to see another day."</p>
<p>"Hahahahaha, the exact same words I wanted to say," another voice filled the space, followed by lightning infused with killing intent.</p>
<p>The lightning surge caused many to block their ears and close their eyes. Just looking at it was like staring death in the face.</p>
<p>BOOOM</p>
<p>A bolt tore from the sky and crashed down, raising dust into the air. When the dust settled, a green-haired, three-eyed man holding a metallic fan was revealed, and the air in the space suddenly turned electric.</p>
<p>All three Celestials recognized the new arrival, and so did many others.</p>
<p>Obviously, seeing nobody smile was a clear indication that this person is no good news.</p>
<p>"Rueno, what are you doing here?" the Azure Star Sovereign asked, ice radiating from her form, her hold on her staff tightening.</p>
<p>The reason for this apprehension was that the new arrival is someone she can’t even fight. Rueno attained Celestialhood during the Dark Era and had joined the Universe Council for the better part of the next few eras.</p>
<p>However, after a lady he had taken a fancy to died in a battle against a Primordial Beast that mysteriously appeared on a planet she was living on, he became cold, and over the last billion years, he started walking the dark path, using the pain and suffering of others to temper his killing intent.</p>
<p>Wherever he appears, pain and suffering follow.</p>
<p>Today, his presence clearly meant something bad would soon be going down.</p>
<p>"Relax, Xuane, I am here to kill these primordial beasts. As for the lady, I will leave her to Sangreos." He paused and narrowed his green eyes at the Azure Star Sovereign and added, "Of course, if you try to be a hero, I will have no choice but to permanently disfigure your face."</p>
<p>SLAP</p>
<p>Before anyone could process the threat Celestial Rueno threw at the Azure Star Sovereign, a slap from a very small hand landed on his face, sending him crashing down.</p>
<p>In his place, a bald kid holding candy straws stood, his expression filled with anger. "You four are very chatty. Right now, my big sister is about to form her Supreme Platform, so if you don’t want trouble, stand on the side and watch or leave."</p>
<p>Baldy’s eyes flashed with cold sharpness. "If not, I would be forced to put you down... permanently."</p>
<p>His words dropped on everyone’s heart like cold steel.</p>
<p>As his words hung in the air, a clapping sound came from an ordinary-looking man seated on a large sword. Everyone, including Baldy, turned to look at the person who had clapped.</p>
<p>"Big Brother..."</p>
<p>Under everyone’s stunned gaze, the brat who dared to slap a Celestial King teleported and dived into the arms of the ordinary-looking man.</p>
<p>On the ground, Celestial Rueno, with a tiny handprint on his cheek, stood up and channeled his energy, killing intent surging from his form.</p>
