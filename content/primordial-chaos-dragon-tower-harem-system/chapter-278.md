<p>When Kent left the Gate Master’s residence, he went straight to his master’s place. Along the way, he met many disciples, each of whom gave him a look of respect.</p>
<p>They were indeed a bit more respectful now, considering the one walking past them had defeated a saintess in a duel.</p>
<p>As soon as he reached the mansion, he pulled up one of the three messages he had received. One was for making the Saintess his servant. Another was for completing the first quest to find the mysterious Nara.</p>
<p>And the last was the new quest about what to do next to find her.</p>
<p>⟪Congratulations on completing the second quest in the ’Rehabilitating the Saintess’ chain quest. Rewards Received ⟫</p>
<p>⟪Rewards: Recieved⟫</p>
<p>→ 1,000,000 Tower points</p>
<p>→ Arcane Array Path</p>
<p>→ Heavenly Red Dragon Rune Blade</p>
<p>"A path for an array master, huh?" Kent picked up the manual with the golden cover.</p>
<p>It’s a path, just like the sword and spear paths. This path will take Alina from the foundation-building stage to the peak if she has the talent and time for it.</p>
<p>Obviously, she is going to be very happy to have the chance to chase her dreams finally. It would make her very happy indeed.</p>
<p>He then took out the next item.</p>
<p>⟪Heavenly Red Dragon Rune Blade⟫</p>
<p>⟪Grade: Mythic⟫</p>
<p>→ A rune knife forged from the remains of a Red Dragon.</p>
<p>→ All runes carved using this knife will have a 40% strength boost and an aura of a red dragon.</p>
<p>"I don’t know anything about arrays and runes, so I will leave everything to Akira and Alina. Since she is a weaver, it’s only normal for the two to become friends."</p>
<p>After putting away the knife, Kent called out to Gaia and commissioned another path, which the cosmic loli accepted with a beaming smile.</p>
<p>In fact, when Gaia appeared, she had a pencil strapped behind her ear, showing she was working on the architecture for the three jobs he had given her.</p>
<p>Kent only smiled and added another to it. The more busy she was, the less time she would spend thinking about her sister. Well, Kent already has plans to rescue her.</p>
<p>After she left, he dug into the next quest.</p>
<p>⟪Congratulations on completing the first quest in the ’Clash With Evil’ sect quest. Rewards Received ⟫</p>
<p>⟪Rewards: Recieved⟫</p>
<p>→ Battle Space Domain x2</p>
<p>→ The Lost Undead Schematic (Creator Necromancer) x2</p>
<p>→ 1,000,000 Tower Points x2</p>
<p>This one was a sect quest. It was a mini-chain quest containing only three quests. He had already completed one, and since it was his first, all rewards were multiplied.</p>
<p>He received 2 million Tower points, and the rest were also received in double.</p>
<p>Now, he didn’t know what the two other rewards were yet, but he certainly knew receiving double wasn’t something he would argue about. He received them because he worked for them.</p>
<p>He retrieved the first item, and a cylindrical-shaped artefact appeared. It had no edges, and runic markings could be seen all around it.</p>
<p>Kent quickly appraised it, and the results were shocking.</p>
<p>⟪Name: Battle Space Domain⟫</p>
<p>⟪Grade: Divine⟫</p>
<p>→ A specialized indestructible domain where you have the freedom to battle anyone you want simply by activating it within a 300-meter range of the person or beast.</p>
<p>→ The space is indestructible, perfect for chaotic battles that could easily destroy landscapes and terrains.</p>
<p>→ You have the authority to send whoever you want inside and also the authority to bring out those you wish.</p>
<p>"Damn, this is a good item," Kent grinned, already picturing how best he would use it.</p>
<p>"A battle so dangerous that I would have to send my opponent inside a pocket dimension just to save the space. I wonder when I will fall into a battle of that magnitude."</p>
<p>Kent sighed, but he knew that battle was just around the corner. He took the second disc, which looked and had the same description and use as the first one.</p>
<p>"I will give this one to the girls. Who knows when they might want to challenge each other and wouldn’t want to do it inside the tower," Kent smiled.</p>
<p>"Wait, does this mean when I use this inside the tower, the time difference will have an effect on it?" Kent asked, and Gaia responded.</p>
<p>"Awesome. Then this will really help them," Kent put away the discs and retrieved the next item.</p>
<p>⟪The Lost Undead Schematic (Creator Necromancer)⟫</p>
<p>→ A schematic of a one-of-a-kind undead that hasn’t been seen before.</p>
<p>→ You also have the authority to change the schematic and add your preferences.</p>
<p>→ When you are done, you can use it to create the undead, and the results will be exactly as you wanted.</p>
<p>"Wait a minute. Gaia, care to throw some insight into this?" Kent said, and Gaia was happy to help.</p>
<p>[Basically, the universe has created a one-of-a-kind undead that, from the look of things, is some kind of alien. This undead will be the first of its kind if and when created.</p>
<p>However, that isn’t all. Per the description, you can make your own changes to it. These changes can include the design, abilities it must have, its aspects, and whatnot.</p>
<p>The strange part is, all you have to do is write them. However, you will be required to pay more to create it. So, the more changes you make, the more materials you need to create it.]</p>
<p>"Well, I’ll be damned," Kent laughed, reading the descriptions again. The schematic in front of him is just too good to be true.</p>
<p>Even without any modifications, he can tell that what he was holding cannot be underestimated. It’s something he would probably need in the coming months.</p>
<p>"I most certainly need to start using my Necromancy class because this is just too good to just sit inside my tower storage," Kent laughed again before putting away the schematic.</p>
<p>He had two, and from what he now knew, he would have to spend some quality time creating the next best undead he could using his Necromancy class.</p>
<p>He calmed his beating heart down and then chose to look at the next quest he would have to embark on. The moment he adds the Saintess as a servant, the next quest for her too will show.</p>
<p>He is basically living the quest life...Lol.</p>
