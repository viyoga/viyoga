<p>The universe is vast and old.</p>
<p>It has lived for billions of years.</p>
<p>Endured billions of wars.</p>
<p>Seen grim massacres.</p>
<p>Tasted the metallic sourness of blood.</p>
<p>And cried bitter tears whenever her beloved fell.</p>
<p>The universe saw legends rise and fall. She saw worlds born and die.</p>
<p>But she also saw beauty, peace, and tranquility.</p>
<p>However, among all this devastation, she saw the birth and rise of one person heralded as the bringer of chaos.</p>
<p>He was born without a known lineage, and nobody can trace his ancestry.</p>
<p>Those who tried to pierce into his fate lost their fate. Those who wanted to read his destiny lost their destiny.</p>
<p>In the end, they called him the last of the True Primordials — beings who were born when the universe formed. They came with the universe and are one with the universe.</p>
<p>And so, they named him the Primordial Chaos Dragon, the first and last of his kind.</p>
<p>His legend is etched at the heart of the universe and echoes in its outer reaches, telling everyone of the one named the Primordial Chaos Dragon.</p>
<p>Such a person is no simple man. His mere name demands respect, and aside from him, nobody else can invoke it.</p>
<p>Lord Agni Sauron, the Flame Lord, has lived for billions of years, seeing what he could and hearing what he could hear.</p>
<p>However, among the many things he heard, the only thing he kept was the name of the one who once existed, whose name has been recognized by the universe.</p>
<p>Whenever he invokes his name with intent, the universe acknowledges him.</p>
<p>That name was invoked today, and the entirety of the Flame Domain, where the Flame Lord pulled into Kent, shook heavily.</p>
<p>The Sea of lava surged, and the floating island shuddered, chunks tearing apart.</p>
<p>The air grew heavy, and as all this happened, Kent stood tall, his form exuding an air of strength, dominance, and someone meant to stand above all.</p>
<p>The one meant to go down on his knees now stood tall, and the demigod was rather humbled.</p>
<p>He dropped to his knees and, for the first time in his life, paid his respect to the one who deserved it.</p>
<p>"This humble servant pays his respect to the Primordial Chaos Dragon, the greatest who stood above all else. The greatest of all dragons. The bringer of Chaos."</p>
<p>Kent stood frozen, then a grin appeared at the corner of his lips. He was frozen not from fear. This time, he was frozen in shock.</p>
<p>He never expected a demigod to fall to his knees and pay him respect. He, a mere mortal, was being respected by a demigod...</p>
<p>That’s just too much.</p>
<p>"Ahem," Kent cleared his throat. "You can stand up."</p>
<p>"Thank you, my lord." The demigod stood up, but not before thanking Kent.</p>
<p>"You can just call me Kent."</p>
<p>"I dare not. If the universe hears me dare disrespect the Primordial Chaos Dragon, according to the legends, I will be struck down and my lineage wiped out...</p>
<p>Though I don’t have a lineage left, I don’t dare disrespect the Primordial Chaos Dragon."</p>
<p>Kent scratched the back of his neck, not knowing what kind of headache he had found himself in. Just when he thought it was cool to brag about his awesome self, he landed himself in a rather unpleasant situation.</p>
<p>Being called by these titles didn’t sound nice in Kent’s ears. To him, being called Kent was enough. However, looking at the demigod who was now looking at him with reverence, he knew he wasn’t going to take no for an answer.</p>
<p>"What is your name?" Kent asked, sounding like the bigger man between the two of them.</p>
<p>"I am Agni Sauron, but to the rest of the universe, I am known as the Flame Lord."</p>
<p>"Then I will call you the Flame Lord."</p>
<p>"No. My lord shouldn’t do that. Referring to me by my title is an insult to you. I am not worthy to be called by the Primordial Chaos Dragon using my title."</p>
<p>Kent smiled, "Then why don’t we compromise? You can call me Kent, and I will call you Agni. If you call me the Primordial Chaos Dragon, I will call you the Flame Lord.</p>
<p>And since I’m too awesome to call you the Flame Lord, then we can all address each other by our first names. Of course, if you don’t want to use Kent, you can call me Chaos. That is my second name."</p>
<p>Agni went into a moment of contemplation. After weighing the demand, he was forced to accept one name, and that was Chaos.</p>
<p>However, as someone who had spent billions of years worshipping the Primordial Chaos Dragon, just calling Kent by his name didn’t sit well with him.</p>
<p>So he compromised.</p>
<p>"I will call you Lord Chaos or Lord Kent. That is the lowest I can go."</p>
<p>Kent just smiled, "I guess whatever makes you happy will do. That said, I think this is the best time to tell me why I am here.</p>
<p>Also, you shouldn’t worry about the universe. She can be uptight sometimes, but trust me, she wouldn’t do anything to you.</p>

<p>"Who expected things to end up this way?" A smile appeared on her lips as she looked at the thread. It was the thread of fate, one belonging to the Chaos Dragon.</p>

<p>Kent’s fate line thickened to the point that the Enchantress had to examine it to see if any more abnormalities had occurred. But from what she had seen, nothing of the sort happened.</p>
