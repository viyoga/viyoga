<p>"This is..." Kent was left dumbfounded, unsure of how to handle what he was seeing.</p>
<p>The young lady, or more accurately, the young girl, looked at Kent with her big eyes, which made her look charming. She turned sideways to see if Kent would recognise her.</p>
<p>However, after five minutes, when Kent showed no sign of recognising her, she pouted. The look on her disappointed face only made her appear much cuter and irresistible.</p>
<p>"Sorry, but I don’t recall ever being your master. But even if I were your master, I currently have no memory of that life since I recently reincarnated."</p>
<p>"I understand, Master. But I was hoping you would at least recognise me. Nana has been lonely for a very long time."</p>
<p>A sad expression appeared on the young girl’s face.</p>
<p>Kent, who saw this, felt his heart break slightly.</p>
<p>"Although I can’t recall the part of my life where you came in, I now have the Primordial Pill Records up to the Pill Grandmaster stage. So if you and I were related, then I am sure very soon, I will become aware of you."</p>
<p>Hearing this, Nana brightened up and smiled.</p>
<p>"The master has the Primordial Pill Records?"</p>
<p>Watching her smile, Kent also smiled and answered, "Yes. I have the records."</p>
<p>"Great. Then this time, the master will have much more success in the pill path than before." Nana circled around Kent, her giggles filling the room.</p>
<p>"Has the master already accomplished Batch Extraction, Double Concoction, Pill Flame Merge, or any of the basic stuff?" Nana looked at Kent with a glint in her eyes.</p>
<p>If one didn’t know better, they would think she was the master and Kent the disciple.</p>
<p>Kent smiled weakly. "I haven’t yet had the chance to go that far in the Pill Path."</p>
<p>Pah!</p>
<p>Somebody smacked the back of Kent’s head, causing him to laugh.</p>
<p>"Let me guess," Nana looked at him closely and said, "You are a swordsman now, so you chose to waste your time with the sword when you should be paying attention to your Alchemy path.</p>
<p>I can’t say I am disappointed in you simply because you are my master, but I know if this continues, you will become a vegetable."</p>
<p>Nana clapped her hands behind her and pondered with an ancient face, almost as if she were thinking of the best punishment to give Kent.</p>
<p>"We can’t have this. You are a Pill Grandmaster. Back then, you could concoct Tier 9 Pills while at the Pill Grandmaster stage. In fact, you were even able to touch 70% purity for Tier 10 pills.</p>
<p>How come you are talentless this time around?"</p>
<p>"Can’t say I am talentless. I have already attained four Pill Enlightenments, awakened my Soul Flame, and can concoct Tier 8 pills. I now have the required Pill Flame grade to concoct Tier 9 pills with some practice.</p>
<p>However, if you help me with what I came here for, I wouldn’t even need to practice before outright concocting Tier 9 pills. So I can’t say I’m wasted or talentless."</p>
<p>Nana looked at Kent for a few minutes before letting out a sigh.</p>
<p>"I guess it’s to be expected. If I remember correctly, you had plans to walk a different path if you were to reincarnate. So while your past was for pills, the future is for other things.</p>
<p>However, since you still have a thing for pill making, I will naturally help you. But first, I have a request that you can’t deny."</p>
<p>Kent raised his brow, hearing Nana’s childish tone. He wanted to tell her he was the master, but after hearing his failures listed to him, he just sighed.</p>
<p>"I am listening."</p>
<p>"I can tell you are at the barrier, almost at the stage of a Pill Saint. So once you reach the Pill Saint Stage, go to the world that these people call Drakthys.</p>
<p>There is someone named Yuna. She is an apprentice to some good-for-nothing Pill King. Kill the Pill King and free this Yuna. Afterwards, bring her here."</p>
<p>"I will do as you say, Master Nana." Kent laughed, "But if you don’t mind me asking, who is this Yuna?"</p>
<p>Nana smiled. "You might not remember yet, but back when you were at your prime, you once met somebody who had a rare body constitution called the Immortal Pill Body.</p>
<p>You helped her when she desperately needed it, and after helping her, she gave you a drop of her blood. That drop was what helped you become a Pill Immortal." Nana paused.</p>
<p>"I know I don’t have to tell you this yet, but I have been tracking that benefactor’s lineage and, at the moment, this Yuna is the only one left that I know of.</p>
<p>I have nine more pieces scattered in nine different worlds. From time to time, I use them to sense several million miles in search of her bloodline, but so far, only this Yuna has been found.</p>
<p>I know this doesn’t align with your objectives for coming here, but if she were to be discovered as someone possessing a Pill Body, her freedom would be taken from her, and she would forever become an ingredient for her captor.</p>
<p>So bring her here and hand her over to the lady who opened this place for you."</p>
<p>"I see. I will do that. However, why give her to Pill Monarch Nefeli?"</p>
<p>Nana just smiled. "Because she has a piece of your inheritance, meaning I have access to her soul. If she were to try anything funny, I would end her existence with a snap of my finger."</p>
<p>A red glow appeared in Nana’s eyes for a moment, making Kent smile weakly. The harmless Nana was more dangerous than one would expect.</p>
<p>"But aside from that, she is a good person. She is also talented, and you seem to have a good relationship with her. So this will be the best time to cement that relationship."</p>
<p>"You sound like an Immortal at the moment. But rest assured, I will refine this Pill King and extract his essence blood before letting him hurt this Yuna any further.</p>
<p>That said, I must become a Pill Saint first since I have other objectives for travelling this far to meet you."</p>
<p>"You want to know how to merge your Soul Flame with the Nine Nirvana Flames in your body," Nana smiled adorably at Kent.</p>
<p>Kent frowned for a moment before nodding.</p>
<p>"How did you know that? Did you read my mind?"</p>
<p>Nana smiled and shook her tiny head. "I don’t have to read your mind to know something like that. I can sense the Nine Nirvana Flames in your body.</p>
<p>Though they are weak since you haven’t begun walking the Twelve Paths of Nirvana, merging them with your Soul Flame would indeed make it powerful. However, the process is complex and painful."</p>
<p>"Pain is a best friend. That said, can you help me?"</p>
<p>"I can help you, Master. I can even do more than help you. I will first help merge the Nirvana Flames with your Soul Flame, then I will use the chaos from the merge to refine your Nirvana Armour.</p>
<p>And lastly, I will give you the Forbidden Flame Merging and Evolution Technique. Since you chose to walk the path of Pill and Slaughter, I can only do my best as your Pill Assistant to ensure you don’t kick the bucket anytime soon."</p>
<p>"Nana, have I ever told you how cute you look?" Kent cupped her cheeks and smiled cheerfully.</p>
