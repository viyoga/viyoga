<p>(A/N: Before you start reading, take note of these differences in cultivation stages. The first is the cultivation stages in the universe Kent traveled to, and its equivalent is after the equal sign.</p>
<p>Domain stage = Spirit Sovereign, Life stage = Spirit Lord, Death stage = Heavenly Ascension, Monarch = Empyrean, Nirvana = Life Law, Core Formation = Death Law)</p>
<p>***</p>
<p>Kent’s sword went through the neck of a Nethran warrior, ending his pitiful life. Then, with a movement so blinding, Kent appeared behind another and killed them too.</p>
<p>The speed he was moving at, only the Death stage warriors could track him.</p>
<p>However, despite seeing him move and being able to track him with their eyes, they just can’t mount a defense fast enough to block his attacks.</p>
<p>It felt like they saw him—they saw his sword move—but when they raised their defenses to block him, it was already too late...</p>
<p>Of course, Kent was only using 60,000 flame veins this time.</p>
<p>The Death stage warriors in this universe are like Heavenly Ascension stage warriors in his universe. However, Kent couldn’t help noticing something.</p>
<p>The Death stage warriors he is clashing with are much stronger than those in his universe. At first, he thought it was because of the two missing cultivation bases that this new universe didn’t have.</p>
<p>However, as he started defending against the attacks from the Life and Death stage warriors, he sensed something else. The spiritual energy in this universe is much denser.</p>
<p>This means that, naturally, all attacks will hold more weight than in his universe, or at least high and medium worlds in his universe.</p>
<p>Kent is already aware of the world dynamics in his universe.</p>
<p>If a Core Formation expert from an immortal world were to arrive in a high world, the chances of a Core Formation warrior in a high world defeating them are very low.</p>
<p>This is due to the difference in spiritual qi in the two worlds. So, feeling their attacks now, he can only tell that this universe has more monsters.</p>
<p>’I have never been to an immortal world in my universe, but based on the difference I am feeling now, I can say this world is comparable to an immortal world in my universe.’</p>
<p>Kent parried an attack, and then his left hand moved, cutting the throat of a Nethran Life Stage warrior with a dagger.</p>
<p>’What a crazy universe... No wonder it has a paragon... the entire universe is a mess. There is no organization to anything; even the cultivation realms are a mess, and on top of all that, breaking the rules is something simple here.’</p>
<p>"Doom Shards"</p>
<p>Obsidian shards burst out from the ground, and thousands of warriors were caught off guard. Some defended, sustaining minor injuries, while some were just too late to react, the shards becoming their death.</p>
<p>The only upside to Kent’s overwhelming strength is that he feels his core drain less thanks to the spiritual energy in this universe.</p>
<p>Kent pivoted on his heels and then, using his left hand, he grabbed a Domain stage warrior by the neck and threw him at a Life stage warrior.</p>
<p>"Annihilation Strike"</p>
<p>Kent swung his sword, sending out a red sword arc that cut through the Nethran Domain stage warrior and a few others. The Life stage warrior lost his left hand, and so he moved back. However, a doom shard burst out of the ground and stabbed him through the head.</p>
<p>’I will be sure to advise Paragon to fix the cultivation realms in his universe once he defeats the heavens. The mess is just too dangerous if one doesn’t take care.</p>
<p>Also, something about this Nethran race smells fishy. It is like they don’t care if their people die or not... This is not a good feeling, especially coming from me.’</p>
<p>Just when Kent ended his thought, he sensed a powerful presence appear on the battlefield.</p>
<p>’An Empyrean in my universe, but a Monarch here,’ Kent looked toward the Dragon and the Phoenix, and he could tell they were doing well.</p>
<p>’I guess I’d better see how long it will take me to handle this one.’ Kent locked his gaze on the new arrival, causing the lady to turn in his direction.</p>
<p>Kent smiled, "Since you came, why don’t you stay?"</p>
<p>With a speed so blinding, he moved and clashed with the lady. Kent didn’t make any adjustments to his stats, so he merely clashed with her.</p>
<p>However, the moment their swords met, he sensed it.</p>
<p>’Stronger, faster, and deadlier.’</p>
<p>Kent immediately increased his flame veins to 100,000 and blood runes to 4,000, while his bloodline was immediately activated to 10%.</p>
<p>They clashed again, but neither was pushed back. However, Kent immediately saw it—the new lady wasn’t taking him seriously.</p>
<p>This, in a way, enraged him. He immediately unleashed two deadly attacks, forcing the lady to see she was about to have a world of pain.</p>
<p>"Soul Universe Art - Meteor Shower"</p>
<p>"Void Fracture"</p>
<p>The sky turned dark, and red meteors, about the size of a car, rained down. At the same time, the void cracked, and sword arcs started raining down.</p>
<p>"Domain." Echo, the dual-bladed swordswoman who came to clear the path for her team, unleashed her domain, instantly changing their surroundings.</p>
<p>Kent immediately found himself in a valley where, on the ground, thousands of swords were stabbed into the earth.</p>
<p>[Activate the Blood Death Seal,] Gaap said the moment they appeared in the domain. Kent obeyed because he, too, could sense the danger he had landed himself in.</p>
<p>The meteor shower and the sword arcs were neutralized by the domain, causing Kent to see something that could have cost him if he had unleashed an asteroid.</p>
<p>’Domains ride on a different law, so if I had used the asteroid, even if I managed to land a blow, the majority of the damage would have been absorbed.</p>
<p>This is an eye-opener.’</p>
<p>Suddenly, the swords in the domain started to fly from the ground. Echo, who appeared and was hovering in the air, pointed her sword at Kent, and the swords in the air lunged at him.</p>
<p>"Flame Wheel."</p>
<p>Still using just 100,000 flame veins, Kent moved, and his speed exploded, allowing him to evade the swords and even close in on Echo. However, the lady saw him move and reacted.</p>
<p>’Looks like Monarchs are no joke.’ Kent smiled and then unleashed his entire bloodline; however, he held back on the flame veins and the blood runes.</p>
<p>Instead, he decided to use something he never knew he needed at the moment.</p>
<p>"Finger of Destruction."</p>
<p>Kent channeled his flame core and pointed forward. In an instant, the domain shuddered, and a flaming finger appeared in the air.</p>
<p>Echo frowned and waved her hand, causing thousands of swords to form into a dragon’s head and move toward the finger. However, just when they were about to meet, Kent smiled and formed a hand seal.</p>
<p>He clenched his fist, causing the finger to explode. The domain cracked and shattered...</p>
<p>"NO!"</p>
<p>Echo coughed blood, but then she saw Kent move behind her and attack. However, if killing a Monarch were that easy, the Paragon wouldn’t have been holding back one for the past seven minutes.</p>
<p>Echo raised her sword and blocked Kent’s attack. However, Kent was no simple warrior either. His left clawed hand moved and landed an attack on her chest, sending her tumbling back...</p>
