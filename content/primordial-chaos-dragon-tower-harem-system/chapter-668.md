<p>Sylveria is the name of the Elven Kingdom. It is a beautiful kingdom, one where life is good. It isn’t too much to say it is one of the most beautiful kingdoms in the world.</p>
<p>The natural settings surrounding their kingdom made the elves even prouder and, well, arrogant. It is even said that just stepping into the borders of the Elven Kingdom can heal all kinds of fatigue.</p>
<p>It is a nice place and very prosperous.</p>
<p>There are three cities inside the Elven Kingdom, and the queen of the elves is located in Moonglade City.</p>
<p>Her palace is situated on a mountain, and judging by how much craftsmanship went into building such a palace, it holds great importance to the elves.</p>
<p>Inside the palace, the Queen of the elves, a silver-haired beauty who can rival immortals, sat on an elegant throne made out of the rarest woods and stones.</p>
<p>Its beauty alone made the queen shine even more.</p>
<p>Away from the throne, five people were seated—three on her left and two on the right.</p>
<p>"My queen, what do you suppose we do? It seems that giving the five slots still hasn’t triggered the half-breeds to bend the knee to us. I think they still haven’t learned their lesson," one of the elven Lords spoke in a tone dripping with arrogance.</p>
<p>"What Lord Arlen said is indeed true, my lady. They even declared an open competition, allowing every last one of their people to compete, and the top five would have a chance to enter the Flame Tower.</p>
<p>They still believe they have a shot at sending yet again one of their kin to the Supreme Immortal Sect," Lord Folwin, one of the most disliked elven lords in the world, spoke, and as one would expect, the four with him nodded.</p>
<p>Queen Elenara just sat there saying nothing. Of course, the only reason she could sit and say nothing was that she also shared their way if thinking.</p>
<p>She wasn’t called the Bitch Queen for no reason. The half-elves hate her, and deep down, she knew she was arrogant and a very sinister person. But who can stop her?</p>
<p>Someone who can go head to head against someone like the Crystal Dragon Emperor or Lord Qilin of the Beastkin... she isn’t an easy target.</p>
<p>"My Queen, I believe you should address this swiftly and show these half-breeds they can never rise to our level... not when we have the Pill Princess on our side," Lord Folwin said, and the other lords nodded.</p>
<p>"Indeed, my Queen... It’s time we show them who’s in charge. They may have some aspect of our blood, but they are half-breeds, nothing compared to us...If anything, they are like the humans who are forgotten by the world."</p>
<p>Queen Elenara studied everyone’s expression, and then a sigh escaped her lips. "Just pick five of our best and send them to participate in the competition.</p>
<p>Since it is an open competition and we cannot reclaim the slot we gave them, the best we can do is win it back fairly. I am sure the few who couldn’t get to be part of the 25 we initially selected will be glad to have this chance."</p>
<p>The elven lords who knew how manipulative their queen was smiled when they heard her words. It was indeed true that they couldn’t take back the slot they had given to the half-Elves... so why not send their best to take it back fairly?</p>
<p>After all, since this is an open competition where anyone could participate, they really shouldn’t even have to think too much about it. It’s a brilliant idea to get what they want without making it look like they are taking back the slots.</p>
<p>A smart move on their part.</p>
<p>"Now that we have a solution, there is another issue, my Queen," Lord Arlen said, looking at Queen Elenara with reverent expression.</p>
<p>"Speak..."</p>
<p>"It is about that human named Kent. Since the Hand gave the notice, our spies in the human continent haven’t seen or heard from him again. It has already been two years, but nobody has spotted him.</p>
<p>However, that’s not all. It turns out the demons faced an issue recently, and it is related to the human named Kent. This shows he is on the Ancient Battlefield of Parting. But we are yet to locate him."</p>
<p>"Also, it turns out we aren’t the only ones looking for him, but we have to be the first to reach him and retrieve the Divine Atomic Body."</p>
<p>The Elven Queen nodded. "We truly have to get to him first before the others do. The fact that the Sea Race is showing interest in him means we cannot allow him to be captured by the others.</p>
<p>Kill him on sight when you see him, but if you can kidnap him too, that will be good." The five lords nodded.</p>
<p>"Remember, we are after the Divine Atomic Body. Under no circumstances should we fail." The lords nodded. Then, after a few back-and-forths, they left, leaving Queen Elenara to herself.</p>
<p>Several minutes after the lords left, a hidden doorway opened, and another beautiful lady with silver hair, identical to Queen Elenara’s, walked out. She looked older than Queen Elenara.</p>
<p>However, while she was probably thousands of years old, she didn’t appear a day older than thirty.</p>
<p>Of course, she was the ancestor of the Elven race, a being who participated in the Ancient Battle of Parting... someone who fought and killed hundreds of High Elves several years ago.</p>
<p>Looking at her posture and calm aura, it was plain that someone like her could easily handle the Dragon King with a flick of her finger.</p>
<p>She was once known as Queen Aelith... Now, she only cultivates and strengthens her foundations.</p>
<p>"Mother, you are out." Queen Elenara stood up and made the throne available for her mother. However, she waved her hand and sat in one of the chairs where the lords had been seated a couple of minutes ago.</p>
<p>"You are the queen now, Elenara, so don’t give away your throne just like that," Lady Aelith said, sitting casually in her chair.</p>
<p>Queen Elenara smiled. "Mother, why are you out... is everything alright?"</p>
<p>"Everything is alright. I just wanted to get some fresh air after spending hundreds of years in seclusion. Tell me, what is going on... have you succeeded in subjugating the half-breeds?"</p>
<p>"No, mother, but they will soon be bending the knee. I give it a few months. However, there is something else you must know.</p>
<p>Recently, the Hand spread news that a human by the name of Kent Madson owns a Divine Atomic Body..."</p>
<p>Lady Aelith raised her brow, then a crooked smile appeared on her lips. "Looks like I came out at the right time..."</p>
