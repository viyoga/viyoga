<p>However, this Undead Ancient is far more beautiful than she is.</p>

<p>Her skin is flawlessly pale white, with not even an atomic defect visible. Every aspect of her complexion is pristine, pretty, and enchanting.</p>
<p>As for her facial features, she has an oval-shaped face, deep blue eyes, and a smile that seemed to bring meaning to just about anything.</p>
<p>Every step she took sent waves of attraction to almost everyone. In fact, aside from Kent, even his wives were mesmerized by her beauty.</p>
<p>The only ones who managed to resist her allure were Pandora, Mjolnir, and Fiannarel. Even Kent was taken by surprise by just how good-looking she was.</p>

<p>Athena stopped beside Dominique and bowed, revealing her cleavage, which, judging by the size of her breasts, made Kent feel his heart tremble.</p>

<p>Athena smiled and answered, "That is true. Undead beings cannot become Primordials. However, that rule only applies when one is in this universe. The moment they step out of this universe, they can sense and comprehend the Origin Dao."</p>
<p>Kent nodded, slightly expecting such an answer.</p>
<p>He knew with certainty that the rules could be bent to suit one’s desires, so hearing it from Athena wasn’t too shocking, as one would have expected.</p>
<p>He was even sure the Undead Monarch had already reached the realm of a Primordial. After all, back when they fought, he was already at the Pseudo-Primordial stage.</p>

<p>The answer isn’t one he is ready to hear. But even if there were such a mysterious place, Kent also knew, and he even possessed a place where his undead could comprehend the Origin Dao...</p>
<p>Chaos Chasm, the world of the Chaos Ascension King.</p>
<p>Since it is his birthright and he has already claimed the legacy that came with it, he can easily use it, and he most certainly has plans to do so...</p>
