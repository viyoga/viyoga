<p>"Kent, you and Sister Nara will be the only ones attending the competition. We want her to spend some time with you, so take only her," Neomi said to Kent, who was sleeping comfortably, letting his ladies have their way with him.</p>
<p>"Are you ladies sure? There is room for everyone, and I know you all want to spend some time with me on the outside," Kent asked, but they shook their heads.</p>
<p>"You will have your hands full with Alina and Nara, so focus on them and the competition. We will be on standby to step in when the fights start."</p>
<p>Neomi answered, already packed and ready for battle. They wouldn’t want their man to fight alone, especially after he told them about the battle they missed when he rescued Dori.</p>
<p>"Plus, you might get lucky with Lady Lani and her Master, so be ready for anything."</p>
<p>Selene, of course, had other plans.</p>
<p>"She is a Kingdom Protector, Selene. At least let Kent grow a little. The chances of him dying from her aura alone are very high. So giving him the idea to chase her is like telling him to poke the hornet’s nest."</p>
<p>Neomi smirked, saying that.</p>
<p>"Please, it’s Kent we are talking about. He is probably putting plans in motion already. Just make room for the first Kingdom Protector." Selene just laughed.</p>
<p>The ladies smirked. But of course, they knew what she said was indeed possible.</p>
<p>Kent had already interacted with the lady and knew she was someone worth watching. Whether he would shoot his shot would depend on whether he had the strength to withstand her beatings.</p>
<p>Selene was just stating facts.</p>
<p>"You ladies are thinking too much. This is a competition I am going to; no need to add so many add-ons to it.</p>
<p>I will just go there, do the competition, and then go pay my goddess a visit in the god realm. Other things can wait."</p>
<p>"Whatever. Just don’t get shameless when you set your eyes on any vixen. We may be accepting, but not all vixens can play with that dick."</p>
<p>The ladies laughed.</p>
<p>"Tsk."</p>
<p>Kent just smirked and spent the night with his ladies, letting each of them get a fill of his dragon juice. Once daybreak came, he was ready to leave with Nara.</p>
<p>"Is the veil really necessary, huh, Sister?" Mara asked, staring at the red-clothed lady who had a red veil on her face.</p>
<p>Her shape in the clothes was already enough to attract attention, so adding a veil was like telling everyone, ’I am a mysterious lady, so look at me’.</p>
<p>"I still don’t want to let anyone know I am alive, so I will go incognito for now." Kent smiled, hearing Nara’s answer.</p>
<p>"Don’t worry too much. Even if they recognize you, they wouldn’t be able to do anything. You are going out with me; these small fries wouldn’t have what it takes to touch even a hair on your head."</p>
<p>They just ignored him and pulled Nara to the side.</p>
<p>"You have to do your best, Sister. You know what he likes, so do that, and you will return smiling," Selene said, and Nara nodded, glancing at the nicely dressed, handsome man she would be going out with.</p>
<p>No lady would hate walking side by side with a handsome bastard like Kent.</p>
<p>After spoiling her mind for a while, they let her leave with Kent, who took her hand. They appeared in Saintess Selene’s residence and quickly made their way out of the sect.</p>
<p>The moment they came out of the sect, Kent retrieved a flying carriage he had bought from the tower shop. When the carriage appeared, all the guards at the entrance started drooling.</p>
<p>It looked majestic.</p>
<p>It’s made of gold and jade, making it gleam as it moves away.</p>
<p>"Isn’t that the disciple of the Saintess who fought with the Evil Sect?" one of the guards asked, watching the carriage fly away.</p>
<p>The other guard thought for a moment and nodded.</p>
<p>"Looks like he changed a little, so I couldn’t recognize him immediately. But that is indeed him. I heard he had angered some very powerful people, and now they’re looking for him."</p>
<p>"Then why is he going out all by himself? Is that lady with him perhaps some big shot?" a guard asked.</p>
<p>"Maybe. But the fact that he is going without an aide means he is confident in his abilities, so who are we to question that?"</p>
<p>"You are right."</p>
<p>They could only sigh and watch the carriage disappear into the horizon, its golden and jade exterior catching the light as it soared gracefully through the sky.</p>
<p>Inside the carriage, Nara looked out the window and smiled.</p>
<p>"It’s been a long time since I saw this," she said, smiling. She used to fly over this place whenever she was going on a mission, so seeing it again was like déjà vu.</p>
<p>"We can visit all the places you want. We have time and a flying carriage, so everything is possible," Kent said, also watching the mountains pass below them.</p>
<p>"Thank you, Kent, but I wouldn’t want to trouble you," Nara said with a smile.</p>
<p>"You will go whether you like it or not, so you better pick the first destination you have in mind," Kent chuckled, making Nara smirk.</p>
<p>"Don’t you know when a lady is being subtle?"</p>
<p>"Why be subtle when you can go wild or go home? Plus, if you’re concerned about my safety, then know that two Peak Root Transcendent are following us, and I’m sure the moment we cross the mountains, they will attack."</p>
<p>"Do you want me to handle it?" Nara asked, already drawing her sword.</p>
<p>"Oh, come on. Though I believe in women’s power, this is not something you should dirty yourself with. I will handle it," Kent smiled and vanished from the carriage.</p>
<p>He appeared in the air 4 km away and stood still.</p>
<p>"I don’t know if you are a friend or foe, but if you don’t come out, I will be forced to take drastic action." Kent wasn’t even done speaking when a light flashed from his side.</p>
<p>He quickly stepped back, evading the attack.</p>
<p>A smirk appeared on his face. He looked to the left and then the right, and his smirk turned to a chuckle.</p>
<p>"Well then, shall we?" The golden sword sheath appeared in his grip, and then he vanished.</p>
<p>The next second, two sword lights flashed, and before the two people hiding could realize what happened, their heads were severed, causing them to fall freely from the air.</p>
<p>Kent appeared where he had vanished from.</p>
<p>"They never learn."</p>
<p>He vanished again and soon was back inside the carriage. Meanwhile, two life crystals shattered somewhere in Roland City, causing a level 6 Sovereign hooded figure to snap his eyes open.</p>
<p>"He is finally out," he muttered coldly.</p>
