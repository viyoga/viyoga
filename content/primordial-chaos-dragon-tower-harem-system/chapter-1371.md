<p>Kent remained in his enlightened state for three full days.</p>
<p>He had already been through this process several times, so it wasn’t something new to him. This enlightened process had helped him awaken more of his bloodline and some Innate Aspects.</p>
<p>However, this time around, when he came out of the enlightenment, instead of his bloodline shooting past the 26%, something completely different happened.</p>
<p>The moment he opened his eyes, a powerful aura exploded out from his body, and his body was lifted into the air, instantly getting cocooned in a mix of red and golden scaly-looking shells.</p>
<p>This had never happened before.</p>
<p>So even Gaia, Akira, and Dori created clones and sent them to guard him. They wanted to see what would happen to him when he woke up.</p>
<p>Thankfully, they didn’t wait for long, since 20 days later, the cocoon cracked and soon came undone. From within, a handsome golden-haired dragon was revealed, causing Gaia and her sisters to turn and look at each other.</p>
<p>They weren’t concerned about the fact that Kent had become more handsome. Kent was already handsome, so adding more handsome to the bag was not something to be shocked by.</p>
<p>Their stunned looks came from the diagram hovering behind him. It seemed to be made of wood or some kind of metal with a golden-brownish color.</p>
<p>The design consisted of three overlapping circles, with an intersection at the center.</p>
<p>The three circles had different colors. One was red, the second was black, and the last was white. Just a simple glance is enough to tell it is something of higher importance.</p>
<p>"The Origin Law Diagram," the three cosmic lolis muttered at the same time, a look of excitement appearing on their tiny faces.</p>
<p>Before Kent could even examine how strong he had become, they grabbed him and dragged him to the side to have a better look.</p>
<p>"What is happening, ladies?" Kent asked, but they ignored him and continued searching and examining the three rings for a couple of minutes before turning to him.</p>
<p>"Congratulations, big brother."</p>
<p>"Thank you. But can we start by telling me what the hell I have awakened, considering you three seem even more pumped than I am."</p>
<p>"Big brother is not smiling now because he has yet to understand what he has awakened. But even if he were to see what the tower had to say, I am not sure Big Brother would understand it.</p>
<p>So we will take our time to explain it, and well, you do well to follow, because what is behind you is something that will one day make you the overlord of this universe. It is the answer to everything," Gaia said, then Dori took over.</p>
<p>"Before we talk about the diagram behind you, big brother should know that when it comes down to what makes a god a god, it all revolves around three things: Divinity, Domain, and Law.</p>
<p>These three things define a god and would one day make one a supreme. Regardless of what path one walked, the moment they reach the stage to become a god, the only thing they would need is these three things.</p>
<p>However, while many say divinity and domain are important, I say they are wrong. I say at the end of the day, the laws are what govern one’s journey to the peak.</p>
<p>We all came to the conclusion that Divinity grows when the god is a faith god. Their domain expands as the number of their worshippers increases. However, when it comes to the laws, it is all about comprehension/understanding."</p>
<p>Akira took over...</p>
<p>"Many years ago, before the universe nearly came to an end at the hands of the Void Beasts, there lived six beings known as True Primordials. They were being created by the universe with the intent of becoming its guardians.</p>
<p>However, when they arrived, they found something rather binding, something that made them come to the conclusion that when it comes to the source of all things, the laws of the universe cannot be done away with.</p>
<p>They found out that the laws of the universe came from somewhere, a place they called the Origin. It is the origin of all laws and their understanding.</p>
<p>They were beings created by the universe, so they were masters of all law.</p>
<p>However, from the day they discovered there is an Origin of all laws, they became obsessed.</p>
<p>So they decided to study it, and what they found made them give up everything to ensure we have this discussion today."</p>
<p>Gaia took over...</p>
<p>"The diagram behind you is the Origin Law Diagram. It is a creation directly from the origin law source, and contained within are the six seals of the Origin Law.</p>
<p>To better protect the universe, the six true primordials gave up everything to create this item, giving you a heads-up on what is coming and what must be done to prevent it.</p>
<p>Basically, this diagram contains six seals of Origin, seals that would one day open and allow you to touch the origin law of all things.</p>
<p>Basically, contained within this diagram is the secret of the universe."</p>
<p>"The origin law, huh..." A smile appeared on Kent’s face. When he met Axiom and his pals on the last floor of the Void Temple, they said Chaos was able to send them back in time because he had touched the origin law.</p>
<p>Kent placed no emphasis on it before. However, now that he had heard what his three cosmic lolis said, he couldn’t help but wonder just what kind of treasure he had awakened this time around.</p>
<p>"Now that I think about it, did I use the origin law to call into space and time to call the 13 legions to my aid during the great war?"</p>
<p>Kent’s mind started to spin. He indeed had just learned something big, and that alone was enough to tell him what he needed to know.</p>
<p>"What about the seventh space at the center?" Kent asked. The three circles interconnecting had formed some intersections, with the middle one formed from all three circles.</p>
<p>"Big brother should know that the true primordials comprehended an aspect of the Origin Law, and so they sealed it inside the ring. Since Big Brother is also a True Primordial, he must comprehend his own origin law and seal it.</p>
<p>No one can use another’s Origin law comprehension. However, with the Origin Law Diagram, Big Brother can use the origin law of the previous true primordials."</p>
<p>"Amazing. I guess I have struck it big now," Kent laughed, feeling very happy.</p>
<p>"Big brother had indeed struck it big this time. The only thing is for Big Brother to become an immortal so he can start comprehending the laws.</p>
<p>The moment big brother gains absolute mastery of any law, he will start seeing the origin law. That would be when the Origin Law Diagram will come into play.</p>
<p>But even now, if the master were to study the energy patterns in the diagram, he would be able to learn something good from it."</p>
<p>Kent nodded and dismissed the diagram.</p>
<p>"Thanks, ladies. I will be sure to play with you all when I am done here."</p>
<p>Gaia and her sisters nodded and vanished. They hid some things because of karmic backlash. However, they trusted their big brother would be able to figure it out on his own.</p>
