<p>What Kent saw when he scanned Kenji was just too horrific. He is wanted by the Dragons, Elves, Beasts, Dwarves, Half-Elves, and even the Demons.</p>
<p>To be wanted by the demons means a lot...</p>
<p>His crimes are just too numerous, and from the first few Kent read, he couldn’t bring himself to think that a person could be this evil.</p>
<p>⟦Name: Kenji Namota⟧</p>
<p>⟦Title: The Blade Whisperer | Tiger Fang⟧</p>
<p>⟦Race: Tigerkin⟧</p>
<p>⟦Age: 6,278⟧</p>
<p>⟦Realm: Core Formation (7/7)⟧</p>
<p>⟦Affiliation: The Hand⟧</p>
<p>↠ The Blade Whisperer, one of the deadliest assassins to ever grace the people of Sonox with his evil means of assassination. All his kills were horrific and disturbing.</p>
<p>↠ The only assassin whom everyone knows but nobody was able to do anything to. His reputation precedes him, and that is why he is the most wanted man in the world.</p>
<p>↠ The Dragon Emperor wanted him for the brutal assassination of his only daughter, who had not even begun cultivating.</p>
<p>↠ He poisoned the sacred waters of the Elven Kingdom, causing countless deaths and a famine that lasted three hundred years.</p>
<p>↠ He slaughtered an entire caravan of beast tamers, sparing no one, including children and the elderly. Some of these children were the maids of the children of nobles, whom he wanted to kill.</p>
<p>↠ He infiltrated the Dwarven stronghold and destroyed their ancient forge, crippling their ability to produce weapons for decades.</p>
<p>↠ He ambushed the Half-Elves’ crowning ceremony and tried to assassinate the new king, but ended up injuring the Queen instead.</p>
<p>↠ He unleashed a cursed plague upon a demon village, turning its inhabitants into mindless monsters. The town had to be purged, which cost the lives of 240,000 people living in the village</p>
<p>↠ He assassinated the brother of the current leader of Beastkins during a sacred ceremony, defiling their most holy ground.</p>
<p>-</p>
<p>-</p>
<p>-</p>
<p>Kenji Namota is not merely a killer; he is a force of destruction whose evil touches every corner of the world. His list of carnage was so extensive that Kent would have to read everything during his free time.</p>
<p>However, for now, while Kenji was the evil everyone wanted dead, Kent had a much different plan for him.</p>
<p>Kent first used ’Soul Torture’, allowing him to take hold of his mind. Then he began his evil killing plan...</p>
<p>"Contact your leader." Kent gave him a command.</p>
<p>Kenji did as he was told. He took out his communication crystal and channeled his energy into it...</p>
<p>The moment the lazy voice of their leader sounded from the crystal, Kent smiled.</p>
<p>"I don’t believe we’ve officially met, lady... I am Kent Madson, and while you can’t see me, whatever you think of me, it is probably true. I am that handsome.</p>
<p>Anyway, I didn’t call to tell you how handsome I am. The reason for the call is to tell you in person that you’ve been a great disappointment.</p>
<p>I expected a result. I expected you to have already opened the gates... but what did I get? A bunch of weaklings who couldn’t even withstand a single punch.</p>
<p>Not that I expected much from an organization that calls itself ’The Hand.’ I mean, what kind of idiot names their organization ’The Hand’? It’s just stupid.</p>
<p>However, I suppose it suits you, so if that makes you sleep at night, then I’ll be happy to call you the ’Hand.’</p>
<p>Before I trail off, know that the next batch you send my way will all die, so heed my advice and invest your time in searching for the keys.</p>
<p>Get all seven and execute your big, bad plan. I will be there waiting to stop whatever walks through those gates. Basically, as long as I am alive, whatever you have planned will never come to fruition.</p>
<p>Anyway, you can go back to work now. I have to kill the one with the tiger mask. It turns out he was a wanted man in not one, but multiple kingdoms.</p>
<p>Talk to you later..."</p>
<p>With that, Kent stopped talking and crushed the communication crystal. His focus turned to Kenji, and then a grin appeared on Kent’s face.</p>
<p>"Killing you with my own hands would be a waste, so why don’t you do it yourself? Since you like killing, please try this on yourself.</p>
<p>You have seven minutes to cut yourself ten thousand times, but you are not allowed to scream or cry. Also, before the seven minutes are over, you cannot die. And please, I will count—so ten thousand cuts, painful but no tears, bloody but no screams. You can start now."</p>
<p>The command was absolute. There was no fighting it.</p>
<p>So, under the horrified gaze of everyone around, Kenji drew his dagger and started cutting himself. He began with his left arm, slicing wounds into it as he gritted his teeth.</p>
<p>Deep within, he wanted to stop. He tried to scream; he wanted to cry. But the command Kent gave him was meant for torture, something Kent wished to use only on the scum of society.</p>
<p>He wanted to make them bleed, feel the pain deep within, and try their best to resist, but in the end, they would succumb, unable to withstand the torture.</p>
<p>So when Kent gave the order, he was prepared to become evil to punish evil.</p>
<p>"Get creative, Kenji. Your handsome face is there; your nice hair is there... I need to see a bloody face, a bloody scalp, and a severed nose.</p>
<p>Become the Joker—cut that mouth of yours open. Get creative, Kenji, or I’ll be forced to do it for you.</p>
<p>Prove to everyone you are the Blade Whisperer, the Tiger Fang... show them how creative you are. Make them believe you have what it takes to terrorize kingdoms."</p>
<p>As Kent spoke more commands, Kenji continued to bleed, feeling his energy drain, but he couldn’t stop—not when he was nowhere close to the ten thousand cuts Kent demanded.</p>
<p>Kenji cut his mouth wide open, causing most of the elves to turn away. They could feel his pain; they could feel that tear in their own mouths, and the sensation was one they weren’t prepared for.</p>
<p>Lady Aelith looked at Kent intensely, her face pale as she imagined herself in Kenji’s place. The thought alone made her shudder.</p>
<p>’This human, I am not crossing him ever again. If possible, I would prefer not to see him again. I will run... no, I will enter seclusion as soon as I return. That is, if he let me leave with my life’</p>
<p>She made her plan. She would rather run into hiding than risk another chance of staring Kent in the eye. The Chaos Dragon scared her shitless.</p>
