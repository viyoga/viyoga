<p>The battle ended much faster than one would have hoped.</p>
<p>However, despite the battle ending, everyone couldn’t help staring at Kent like he was some freak of nature. Of course, he is a freak. Everything about him is freaking monstrous.</p>
<p>Nobody in a medium world is supposed to have that much control over flame manipulation - not even the most powerful.</p>
<p>Creating a dragon from mere flame qi and controlling it that cleanly is unheard of.</p>
<p>In fact, even high worlds wouldn’t have such a freak.</p>
<p>"Even with my understanding of flames, I can only cast spells to manifest beasts. Just how deep is his understanding of flame manipulation?" Lady Liora muttered, looking at Kent’s back.</p>
<p>"This is my first time seeing him do something this bizarre. It is like he has become stronger again," Vexthra responded to Lady Liora, who, despite her nature as an assassin, had made a life for herself as an alchemist in order to blend in.</p>
<p>She had wandered many worlds and met many powerful and brilliant people, but this was her first time meeting someone like Kent.</p>
<p>Of course, she is already aware of Kent’s monstrous nature. She had witnessed him do the impossible; however, what he had just done was not something that could be easily achieved, just by anyone, especially in medium worlds like this.</p>
<p>Flame manipulation at such a molecular level is near impossible.</p>
<p>There are people out there who can do it; however, even they have to put their entire concentration into it before they can manifest such a large dragon.</p>
<p>However, Kent merely opened his palm and manifested a ball of flame. Before they could wonder what he planned on doing, a flame dragon appeared in his palm. Then it enlarged, and there it was.</p>
<p>But the shock didn’t end there. Just when the heat exploded, he waved his hand and opened a dome that protected both his team and his opponents.</p>
<p>That is just too much for anyone to handle.</p>
<p>"He is a monster," Lady Liora said, shaking her head.</p>
<p>"Oh, he is indeed a monster, both on the battlefield and in bed." Selene deliberately moved close to Lady Liora just to say those words into her ears before moving back.</p>
<p>As expected, the poor lady shuddered not from fear, but from how alluring Selene’s voice made the whole thing sound.</p>
<p>’Damnit...’</p>
<p>She, after all, was doing her best to hold herself from falling for the Chaos Dragon. However, now, with such alluring words spoken into her ears, she cannot help but think more about Kent than she should.</p>
<p>There is just something about Selene’s voice that seems to turn on any woman. Kent even suspected she was secretly a succubus in disguise. However, that idea cannot possibly be true.</p>
<p>After all, he was the one who awakened her true name and witnessed her transformation. Selene is a Celestial Human Hybrid. So, a succubus is merely something she behaves like, but not the actual her.</p>
<p>Kent’s wives giggled, seeing the struggle Lady Liora was going through.</p>
<p>’Soon, she will fall like a meteor,’ Vexthra said inwardly and then focused her attention back on Kent...</p>
<p>"It is of no use to look sad and broken just because you lost. The only reason you lost is that you are not strong enough. However, if you keep training, one day, you will be able to do more than what I just did."</p>
<p>He waved his hand, and all the heat still in the air vanished into thin air. "My advice to you all is, ascend fast because, be as it may, there are more opportunities in the high worlds than in the medium worlds."</p>
<p>Kent then added, making sure the disciples from the Celestial Immortal Sect didn’t feel defeated. "Because you managed to make it to the second rank, I will bestow upon you several series of gifts that will help you become much stronger.</p>
<p>Finish up everything here in about a week, and then come to the Supreme Immortal Sect. I will help you break three more limits, strengthen your soul, and awaken an innate talent. I will also help you upgrade your Spirit Root.</p>
<p>Then, when all that is over, I will help you safely ascend to a Supreme High World. The rest will then depend on how much you want to grow."</p>
<p>After saying that, Kent walked away, leaving everyone in shock. All he had just said is not something that can be done easily. It would take people thousands of years to even accomplish one of the many things he had listed.</p>
<p>However, Kent casually said them as if it were no big deal.</p>
<p>Well, to him, it is indeed no biggie; however, to the ordinary folk, it is like hearing him say he will hand them planets as gifts for winning second place.</p>
<p>"You heard him. Big brother is that rich and resourceful, so be grateful you have won his favour. Of course, I played a part, so in the future, be sure to call me Big Sister Nyss."</p>
<p>After Nyss also said her piece, she walked away too. Her words and demeanor made them believe that Kent indeed had what it took to help them, as he had promised.</p>
<p>The disciples from the other sects began subtly pinching their elders, telling them to make a move for them, too.</p>
<p>They are, after all, also planning on ascending. If they can gain Kent’s favour, then they will be able to grow much stronger in the Supreme High World.</p>
<p>Kent, of course, knew what he was doing.</p>
<p>With this bold declaration, if he came through for the seven disciples, his credibility would be cemented in their hearts.</p>
<p>He can do all he said since he has the means. As for the Supreme High Worlds, thanks to Gaia fixing the Pill World Chamber, the planet teleporter was able to record the coordinates of the planets he had been to when he travelled to the past.</p>
<p>All he needed was to build an ascension platform inside the Tower or add the coordinates to the ascension tower already built in Sonox.</p>
<p>Either way, he had been to quite a few Supreme High Worlds, so he would be more than ready to send them on their way...</p>
<p>Soon, he will have more worlds connected to him, further strengthening him.</p>
<p>After the shocking words Kent had spoken had receded, the winners were announced, and the competition came to a close.</p>
<p>Everyone went their own way while Kent went to meet with Lord Munny again.</p>
<p>Since he promised to meet him again when the competition came to an end, he went to meet him and have a talk. Of course, it would be more about Kent stating terms and Lord Munny replying with a nod.</p>
<p>There would be High and Immortal Worlds to take under his wing; however, making medium worlds a part of his strength is also good enough.</p>
<p>With many worlds joining him, he will only keep growing.</p>
<p>Lord Munny was already there when Kent got there. It seemed he was more than eager to become Kent’s subordinate now more than ever.</p>
<p>He had awakened an innate ability and broken his 15th limit. All this, added to the fact that Kent could send one to a Supreme High World, spoke volumes.</p>
<p>So he came to hear what he had to say and see if he would be earning more than he could have ever dreamt of.</p>
