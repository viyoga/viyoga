<p>Kent’s eyes landed on the fourth dungeon boss, who, despite undergoing training inside the Dead Region, knew at a glance that a battle with the flame brute wielding the hammer wouldn’t end well for him.</p>
<p>⟪I know you can sense it, master, and no, you can’t measure up to him, strength-wise. This person is made from the remnant of a dying star, one of the seven fallen bodies. You don’t need to know what these fallen bodies are just yet.</p>
<p>However, the person before you is made of one of the fallen bodies known as the Flameheart. His flame is the essence of a star—someone you can’t handle easily, despite having chaos qi running through you.</p>
<p>However, battling him will be good for you since it will temper your body and make you more flame-resistant.</p>
<p>And before you ask, his comprehension of the flame element is already at the Perfect stage, even for an Immortal King.⟫</p>
<p>’Damn, why are my subordinates more impressive than me?’ Kent said inwardly, hearing Dori speak about the flame brute speak.</p>
<p>His true height is 12 feet; however, he maintained 10 feet during the battle with the hunter, and now, he is 7 feet.</p>
<p>But despite the reduction in height, he is still intimidating.</p>
<p>"I am Aetheris. I also don’t have any recollections yet, but I know I am impressive by all standards."</p>
<p>"That’s the arrogance I’m talking about. Just because he’s stronger than all of us doesn’t mean you have to rub it in our faces, okay?" Kaizo glared at the brute, who smirked.</p>
<p>His body is made of molten lava, but no heat is coming from him. This is because his control of flame is at the Perfect stage, allowing him perfect control over it.</p>
<p>"Don’t worry, Kaizo. He can be arrogant for now. When he meets that lady, I’m sure he’ll shut up." Zareth, who caused Kaizo’s trauma when they faced Aetheris, consoled him.</p>
<p>Kent just laughed, knowing he must get to know what happened inside the dungeon since he wasn’t with them.</p>
<p>Next was the lion-headed beast-man, who, from the looks of things, seemed to prefer being quiet. He stood there silently, not saying anything until Kent looked at him.</p>
<p>"I am Ka’zarak."</p>
<p>That was all he said. However, Kent didn’t mind scanning the rest. It turns out he is much more impressive.</p>
<p>His nature is that of a warden of beasts, just like the third Night Rider Kent had undercover inside the Dark Devil Church.</p>
<p>However, there is a twist to his powers. Instead of summoning the beasts, they summon him instead and engage in combat with him.</p>
<p>If he wins, they become his subordinates. However, he can’t be summoned now; those he is contracted to are also dormant.</p>
<p>However, he can summon five beasts from the dungeon he commands.</p>
<p>"Nice having you on the team, Ka’zarak," the lion-headed man nodded.</p>
<p>"I am Veera, a necromancer," the sixth dungeon boss introduced herself, causing Kent to look around.</p>
<p>"If you’re looking for that lady, she’s inside that cocoon," Kaizo pointed in the direction of a large, dark cocoon. The hunter he had taken as his servant was still undergoing her transformation.</p>
<p>"So I have three necromancers on my side now, huh? I wonder which one of you is more impressive," Kent said, causing Veera to turn and look at Nykali, the bone-headed dungeon boss.</p>
<p>Nykali isn’t exactly a necromancer; however, since he summons spectral beings, he can be considered one. Veera, on the other hand, can be dubbed both a warrior and a necromancer.</p>
<p>Both she and Ka’zarak are True Immortals. However, because of the suppression of the medium world, they have regressed to Peak Sovereigns, though their true realms still await recovery.</p>
<p>The seventh is also an orc. However, he is also a barbarian, making him even stronger than Skoggr. Kent looked at him and knew Kaizo would become his best friend.</p>
<p>"I am Gorzak, the Barbarian King."</p>
<p>⟪The barbarian race—one of the best body cultivators in the universe. No wonder he’s already a Diamond Body at his current cultivation level. You should exchange blows with him, master.</p>
<p>Make it a pure brute battle, where no skills aside from raw muscle strength will be used.⟫ Dori said with a giggle.</p>
<p>’I will do just that,’ Kent smiled.</p>
<p>Then comes the assassin. He is a slender man with the perfect human form, except for his eyes, which are like those of a panther. However, everything else about him is human.</p>
<p>During the fight with the hunter’s abominations, he morphed into a partial panther form, taking his strength to the next level. Now, he is a simple human dressed in black.</p>
<p>"Valtor," he said, simply stating his name, showing how secretive he is as an assassin.</p>
<p>Both he and the Barbarian King, Gorzak, are True Immortals. Kent smiled happily, knowing his legion kept rising with the passing of the days.</p>
<p>"This is your home now. I will, however, find a way to uncover your true backgrounds and help recover your memories. But for now, feel free to live your best lives here.</p>
<p>If you need anything, just say the word, and it will be provided."</p>
<p>All eight of them nodded.</p>
<p>"Master, when can we go on a mission?" Kaizo asked. But Kent grabbed his ear and pulled him closer to whisper.</p>
<p>"I’ll give you a new mission—if you can defeat Gorzak." Kent had noticed Kaizo didn’t like the guy, so he wanted to keep him quiet for a few days.</p>
<p>However, he was shocked when Kaizo lunged at the guy, and just like that, the two brutes engaged in fierce combat.</p>
<p>"Don’t mind them," Kent laughed, watching the display. "I will bring over the last four dungeons."</p>
<p>"Nice one. I can’t wait to meet that hateful lady again. Also, I think she has a crush on Veydris," Sylara said, getting herself ready for battle.</p>
<p>"Wait, Veydris has a girlfriend? This is interesting." Kent turned and looked at his emotionless knight, who had emerged from his metal armor and was now clad in sleek metal and leather armor.</p>
<p>His height had also reduced, and surprisingly, he looked adorable.</p>
<p>"Veydris was the one who defeated her. But I think she let him win. I’m not sure, however, one thing is certain—she’s a psycho," Sylara said, clearly not liking the mysterious lady.</p>
<p>Kent laughed and asked, "If she’s a psycho, what does that make you?"</p>
<p>His laughter rang out as he bound the four dungeons to the tower, making four more portals appear beside the existing eight.</p>
