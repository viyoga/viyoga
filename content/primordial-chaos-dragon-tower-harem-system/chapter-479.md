<p>"So the two beasts behind you are the innate spirits of the Coil Void Dragon and the Alabaster Poison Serpent?" Kent asked, and Zareth nodded.</p>
<p>Kent had invoked Zareth’s true name, and as such, the contracting process that allowed Zareth to inherit the bloodline of the World Serpent was now complete.</p>
<p>This meant he now had the bloodline of two powerful beasts in him. He has already unlocked 20% of the Coiled Ancestor Bloodline, but this was already enough for him to harness it in ways nobody could imagine.</p>
<p>First, this would allow him to summon the spirits of the Coil Void Dragon and the Alabaster Poison Serpent, which were the innate spirits of the original beasts.</p>
<p>This means they are not created from Zareth’s energy...</p>
<p>Strangely, their strength was that of someone at the peak of the Core Formation stage. However, Kent knew they went deeper than that, just by feeling their aura.</p>
<p>Also, Zareth could use Eldritch magic and create curses thanks to the blessing of the World Serpent. As for weapons, the scythe was already enough for him, considering he could also create weapons out of thin air.</p>
<p>Not to mention, the bloodline of the World Serpent granted him an incredibly rare ability: the Power of Word Weaving.</p>
<p>With it, Zareth could bind reality itself through spoken incantations, creating chains, barriers, and even devastating attacks by simply uttering specific phrases tied to ancient laws.</p>
<p>Kent watched silently, noting how the two spirits floated behind Zareth with majestic and oppressive auras.</p>
<p>The Coil Void Dragon’s form shimmered like a shifting void, bending light and space, while the Alabaster Poison Serpent coiled lazily, exuding a venomous mist that corroded even the air.</p>
<p>"You are insanely powerful, Zareth," Kent laughed, knowing his future battles would be one less chaotic thanks to his presence.</p>
<p>He couldn’t help but wonder what would become of Kaizo, Vaelros, and Sylara when they reached the Root Sovereign stage and awakened their own True Names.</p>
<p>Zareth had awakened the Coiled Ancestor Bloodline and now had a True Name. This, in a way, meant he was destined to keep rising without any limitations.</p>
<p>"Tell me, how would you continue to grow? Kaizo needs metallic minerals, and you saw Vaelros and Sylara; they need blood, so what about you?" Kent asked.</p>
<p>"I just need a large number of beast bodies. It doesn’t matter how weak they are; I need their bodies to extract the energy from them.</p>
<p>In fact, if I can get more now, it would be great. I have the blessing of the World Serpent, but I need to do a ritual if I want to awaken even one of his powerful skills.</p>
<p>So if I can get more beast bodies now, I will be very happy," Zareth said.</p>
<p>"How many are we talking here?" Kent asked.</p>
<p>"A lot," Zareth answered.</p>
<p>Kent, of course, thought about it for a few minutes and sighed.</p>
<p>"I guess keeping you five in here is a little suffocating." He brushed his chin and then retrieved three cards.</p>
<p>"These three cards will allow you to access dungeons. I don’t know how many levels are in each dungeon, but I know the last-floor bosses will be at the peak of the Root Sovereign stage.</p>
<p>So, since you need bodies, I guess you should take the four of them with you and clear these twelve dungeons." Kent handed him the first card.</p>
<p>"That one will allow you to access ten SS-rank dungeons, while these two access one each. With all five of you working together, this will be over soon." Kent handed the other two cards over.</p>
<p>He went ahead and retrieved twelve Dungeon Master Tokens.</p>
<p>"Whenever you clear a dungeon, use one of these to claim it. All you have to do is attach it to the dungeon’s core, which is located on the last floor. The rest will be done by me."</p>
<p>Zareth accepted the cards with a smile.</p>
<p>"By the time you are done with the 12 dungeons, I will also be done here, then we can go ahead with the real mission, where the odds will be much steeper.</p>
<p>Zareth nodded, and they went away with the items stored in his unknown storage. Kent stood and watched as he delivered the mission to his teammates, causing Kaizo to punch the air excitedly.</p>
<p>The brute who craves battle was the most excited. Kent knew the next time they met, he would be a little satisfied.</p>
<p>Kent smiled and left.</p>
<p>He appeared in the harem tower next, where he immediately located his ladies inside the Yin Pool, cultivating.</p>
<p>However, he also senses Protector Vari inside the garden, also cultivating. After a few back and forth, he chose to see her first, considering she most definitely has thousands of questions to ask him.</p>
<p>He was soon inside the garden, walking toward the direction of the Kingdom Protector, who came to his aid when he needed it.</p>
<p>Of course, with Zareth around, the chances of Kent getting hurt or even being overwhelmed are nonexistent.</p>
<p>Even before invoking his true name, Zareth could harness some part of the Coiled Ancestor Bloodline, so he was formidable and could have held back two Kingdom Protectors without much difficulty.</p>
<p>But the fact that she went out of her way to even face the strongest of the three who came after Kent shows she held their brother sister relationship in her heart.</p>
<p>She clearly appreciated all the help Kent gave her. So she wanted to pay him back in her own way, and that was holding back one of her fellow Kingdom Protectors.</p>
<p>After walking for a couple of seconds, Kent appeared in the location she was cultivating, but he stepped as their gaze met.</p>
<p>He stared into her eyes and smiled at her before walking nearer to her.</p>
<p>"How are you doing, Big sister?" Kent asked, stopping in front of her and sitting down.</p>
<p>"I am fine, thanks to you," She replied with a soft expression. She woke up about a week ago, considering that only three days passed on the outside, but 72 days passed inside the tower.</p>
<p>"Thanks for helping me deal with those three idiots," Kent said again, studying her expression.</p>
<p>"Tsw," She chuckled, "I have a feeling even without my help, you would have come out unscathed." A rare smile appeared on her face, then replaced by a helpless sigh.</p>
<p>"Looks like the name Kingdom Protector is just a joke. To think a mere Great Sage could sever the arm of a level 3 Core formation expert. Just who or what I you?" Lady Vari asked with a bittersweet smile.</p>
<p>Her confidence is shaken, and Kent could see it. This caused him to smile.</p>
<p>"No need to look down on yourself, big sister. I am just awesome, capable, Handsome, and extremely powerful, that’s all."</p>
<p>Lady Vari could only shake her head.</p>
<p>"Tell me where I am."</p>
