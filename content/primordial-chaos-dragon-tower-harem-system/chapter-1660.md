<p>[Shore of the River of Styx]</p>
<p>The River of Styx spanned billions of kilometers wide, surrounding the entire Underworld and reaching all thirteen levels. Its vastness, coupled with the resentment dwelling within it, made it one of the most dangerous regions in the Underworld.</p>
<p>When a soul died, it would appear on the shore of the River of Styx, where, depending on its nature and affinity, Grim Reapers would transport it to the level best suited to mold it into an Underworld fiend.</p>
<p>Whenever a person appeared there, it meant they had just died.</p>
<p>However, today, a portal opened and three living beings stepped out, landing softly on the shore. Kent, who was the last to emerge, scanned the vast sea stretching before his eyes, and a strange wave of familiarity washed over him.</p>
<p>’I feel like I have stood here before.’</p>
<p>No memories surfaced, but he instinctively knew he had once stood upon this very shore.</p>
<p>Regardless, he let the thought slide and turned to Indie.</p>
<p>"What next?"</p>
<p>Indie smiled and clapped her hands twice.</p>
<p>"Rise, Yama. Time to get back to work."</p>
<p>Kent felt the sand beneath his feet tremble, and then a wave of decay and death filled the surrounding space.</p>
<p>[Your understanding of Evil Qi has improved slightly.]</p>
<p>Kent ignored the message and narrowed his eyes, watching as ripples began spreading across the surface of the water.</p>
<p>"What is happening?" Miryssa asked, her gaze fixed on the same spot Kent was staring at.</p>
<p>Indie smiled and replied, "You will see in a few seconds."</p>
<p>As the ripples grew increasingly chaotic, the death energy in the atmosphere thickened, causing Kent’s eyes to widen slightly. His evil core rapidly absorbed the energy even as he stood still.</p>
<p>More importantly, his understanding of evil began to broaden. It was as though merely standing there was affecting him.</p>
<p>Suddenly, the topmast of a bone-like pirate ship emerged from the dark waters of the River of Styx. A tan-skinned man clad in tattered trousers and an armless vest stood halfway at the bow, a red scarf tied around his neck and a pirate hat resting lazily atop his head.</p>
<p>As the ship emerged, Kent felt a terrible sense of dread wash over him. Instinctively, he sensed danger from both the ship and the man, but he did not panic.</p>
<p>After all, it was Indie who had summoned him.</p>

<p>"It is you."</p>
<p>Kent frowned slightly.</p>
