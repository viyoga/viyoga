<p>Zeraphine was slightly stunned when she heard what Kent said.</p>
<p>"Are you sure we should go there? The last time I checked, she had several Immortals and over a dozen Demigods protecting her thanks to her family’s standing on Planet Fiamha."</p>
<p>Kent smirked, finding Zeraphine’s concern unnecessary.</p>
<p>In his opinion, even if the entire planet came after him, they wouldn’t be able to scratch his back. After all, he could summon dozens of Demigods with a flick of his hand.</p>
<p>But even if he chose not to, he alone could handle them.</p>
<p>His power was more than enough.</p>
<p>"You have nothing to worry about, Zeraphine. We are merely going for her head, so it should be simple enough. Of course, I want you to redeem yourself, and the only way to do that is to show her that even in the face of thousands, you have absolutely nothing to fear."</p>
<p>He reached for her hand and gave it a gentle squeeze.</p>
<p>"The last time I checked, you’ll be going with me. And let me tell you, I am far stronger than I look."</p>
<p>Indeed, he was far stronger.</p>
<p>Just like how the Symbiosis innate skill would allow him to inherit a percentage of the power of his Beast Legion, his Undead Class possessed a similar ability known as [Undead Bond].</p>
<p>This bond allowed him to inherit the power of his undead without affecting them in any way.</p>
<p>Since he had awakened that ability after his undead evolution, his strength had reached an entirely new level. He had inherited part of Kaizo and the other 11 undead currently under his command.</p>
<p>With that much power under his command, getting revenge for Zeraphine would be as simple as walking in and walking out.</p>
<p>"I have some things to take care of, so let’s get your revenge before I move on to them."</p>
<p>Zeraphine nodded after a few minutes of thought.</p>
<p>"In case we find ourselves in a dangerous situation, we should run immediately."</p>
<p>Kent smiled and nodded.</p>
<p>"We will. But I do hope we don’t find ourselves in such a situation."</p>
<p>They soon took flight and sped toward Crayon City, where the family of Zeraphine’s so-called best friend lived.</p>
<p>Kent had now awakened his Undead Class, and with it, he had recovered his memories.</p>
<p>However, instead of feeling happy, he realized his life had just become far more complicated.</p>
<p>Inside his Undead Codex was the Undead Legion he had once commanded.</p>
<p>However, to bring them back, he first had to perform a series of rituals, rituals that could only be carried out in specific locations.</p>
<p>Kaizo and the others were fortunate to have already been revived.</p>
<p>Now, he was left with unlocking some of their abilities and helping them awaken their full potential.</p>
<p>He planned to do that for all twelve undead currently under his command.</p>
<p>After that, he would embark on a journey to awaken the rest while preparing to venture back into the Chaos Abyss, this time with no intention of leaving without his parents.</p>
<p>Of course, to do that, he had to become a Demigod or even a god first.</p>
<p>But Kent had no plans to wait that long.</p>
<p>He had no idea what condition his parents were in after millions of years had passed, so delaying was not an option.</p>
<p>Thankfully, the tower had quests for him to follow, quests that involved reclaiming his undead while building his strength on the side.</p>
<p>The only downside now was his other professions.</p>
<p>He had a series of tasks to complete before becoming a Sword Legend.</p>
<p>He also had to become a Pill Immortal so he could carry out the revenge on the pill god for vexthra.</p>
<p>He also needed to participate in the Trial of Wisdom, as it was the only way to gain access to hidden records and further expand his knowledge of the universe.</p>
<p>Lastly, he had to prepare for the Chaos Rift’s awakening.</p>
<p>Ever since the day he learned about it, he had been plagued by a sense of unease.</p>
<p>Something deep within him kept warning him not to enter.</p>
<p>Yet Kent knew he had no choice.</p>
<p>After all, he had already received a quest related to it.</p>
<p>As they soared toward the city, Kent couldn’t help but sigh inwardly.</p>
<p>’Sorry, Mother, Father. You will have to wait a little longer. I have some things to take care of first, plus I am not strong enough.’</p>
<p>If they were still alive and had met Prince Anvil, they would reunite with him one day.</p>
<p>’For now, I must focus on my alchemy while awakening the rest of my undead.’</p>
<p>Zeraphine had no idea that while they were flying toward the city, hundreds of Shadow Fiends continuously emerged from Kent’s shadow, each one moving in a different direction.</p>
<p>As they headed toward different targets, it wouldn’t be long before more undead joined his legion.</p>
<p>He wanted to stay ahead of things.</p>
<p>By the time the city came into view, thousands of Shadow Fiends had already escaped from his shadow.</p>
<p>Many had successfully slipped into the shadows of travelers who would soon leave the planet for other worlds, bringing them, and by extension, Kent, closer to his intended targets.</p>
<p>For now, only revenge occupied his thoughts.</p>
<p>They landed and quickly made their way toward the city gate, where five guards stood watch.</p>
<p>The moment their gazes landed on Zeraphine, Kent noticed a cold glint flash through their eyes.</p>
<p>All five were Immortal Emperors.</p>
<p>However, since Zeraphine was now a Demigod, they showed no arrogance beyond the obvious hostility in their eyes.</p>
<p>One of them retrieved a communication token and sent a message to whoever was on the other end.</p>
<p>A few minutes later, Kent and Zeraphine stood before the gate, with the five guards blocking their path.</p>
<p>"I wonder why you are blocking us when everyone else paid and was allowed to enter," Kent said, a smile forming on his face.</p>
<p>"You are not welcome in Crayon City," one of the guards said, his gaze locked onto Kent, whom he assumed was the weaker of the two.</p>
<p>After all, Kent was merely an Immortal King, while he himself was an Immortal Emperor.</p>
<p>Going against Zeraphine, a Demigod, seemed like a terrible idea.</p>
<p>Unfortunately for them, they had targeted the wrong person.</p>
<p>Kent laughed upon hearing the guard’s response.</p>
<p>"But today is my first time ever coming here. Are you saying I am too handsome to be allowed entry?"</p>
<p>His words caused the guards to frown.</p>
<p>However, with Zeraphine standing beside Kent, it was practically impossible for them to make a move.</p>
<p>Kent noticed their hesitation and laughed even harder.</p>
<p>"I know you contacted your employer and informed them that Zeraphine is here. That is fine, since I came for the big fish anyway. However, since you dare prevent my magnificent self from entering the city, I hereby sentence the five of you to ten days of eternal torment."</p>
<p>Kent flicked his hand, and black smoke engulfed the five guards.</p>
<p>Their screams soon echoed throughout the city, drawing the attention of thousands.</p>
<p>Kent stepped over the bodies of the guards he had just cast into the Realm of Evil, where they would endure endless torment.</p>
<p>He had come for revenge, and so he decided to wield the power of evil to punish his enemies.</p>
<p>Kent summoned the Undead Throne and sat down.</p>
<p>Then, with a gentle wave of his hand, Zeraphine was pulled onto his lap.</p>
<p>She had no idea how it happened, but she did not resist.</p>
<p>"My name is Kent Madson, and this message is for the Crayon Great Family."</p>
<p>His voice was calm, yet it carried across the entire city.</p>
<p>"Hand over the bitch named Mercy, and I will leave this place without laying a finger on any of you. Resist, and watch me bring an end to your fragile lives."</p>
<p>His voice spread throughout the city, traveling over 200,000 kilometers.</p>
