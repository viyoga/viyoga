<p>[So I killed the last of the three great families today, and it felt nice. At long last, they won’t be able to cause any more damage to anyone.</p>
<p>However, I now find myself in a difficult situation. That old bastard. It turns out that killing the three great families was a bad thing in some way.</p>
<p>I discovered that Lumen is home to a rare type of mineral called Spirit Stones. These spirit stones contain spiritual energy that most cultivators utilize to enhance their cultivation bases.</p>
<p>Not that I need it...</p>
<p>For centuries, the three great families have been fighting over it, hence the chaos that we, the commoners, have had to endure.</p>
<p>However, aside from their battles, they were also the force that prevented others from invading.</p>
<p>It turns out that the only three gateways to Lumen were protected by a powerful formation that, from that old bastard’s mouth, not even immortals could break.</p>
<p>But now that I have killed them, it will only be a matter of time before those outside Lumen find out and invade. When that happens, I will be powerless to stop them.]</p>
<p>"Aah, what a dumbass," Kent smirked, flipping to the next page of the diary. However, when he read the first line, his back broke into a sweat.</p>
<p>"He is crazy, totally crazy."</p>
<p>[Actually, I wanted them to invade. I want them to come so I can start cultivating this damn technique the Leviathan gave me when I visited the Primal Void.</p>
<p>I need the blood, loads of blood, and since they are serving themselves on a silver platter, who am I to reject?</p>
<p>The Primal Bone Tempering Art requires me to slaughter as many idiots as I can to temper my bones. I know it is sinister, but that is the only way I can live up to the contract I made with the Leviathan.</p>
<p>So I have to bide my time and wait for the invaders to come.]</p>
<p>"Crazy," Kent took a deep breath, feeling the craziness seep into his bones. The fact that someone who was only a spirit king was waiting for possible immortals was enough for him to realize how crazy he had been in the past.</p>
<p>"However, the Primal Bone Tempering Art is what I need right now.</p>
<p>It will not only enhance my body and bones, but it will also make me a predator, someone worthy of once being the opponent of the infamous Abyssal Leviathan, the overlord of the wild."</p>
<p>Kent laughed, feeling the craziness stir within him.</p>
<p>---</p>
<p>Inside the Mountain Garden, Dori and her sister were watching their master with smiles on their faces.</p>
<p>⟪I can start to see that craziness surface. Mercy on the souls of those who would anger him. I fear for them,⟫ Gaia said, shaking her small head. Her sisters were the same.</p>
<p>⟪His bloodline is already thirteen percent, yet that craziness has started to surface. Makes me wonder, would this version of him become even crazier?⟫ Akira asked.</p>
<p>⟪Only natural. He has lived many lives after all, and while I won’t say all of them were crazy like the Chaos Dragon era, he was a menace in all, so it is only normal.</p>
<p>The question, however, should be whether he can control the chaos and not exacerbate it, as he did in Lumen.⟫</p>
<p>The three sisters nodded, looked at each other, and sighed in unison.</p>
<p>---</p>
<p>Kent flipped to the next page of the diary.</p>
<p>[So I killed 12 thousand people today. Although I couldn’t reach all of them, the 12,000 are from a world called Myrrath. I don’t know where it is located, but I believe they are demons who can breathe fire.</p>
<p>Makes me sad I can’t do that despite being a dragon. However, I learned something new... Killing is so much fun.]</p>
<p>Kent’s eyes remained on the book, looking at the words written in black, and he just couldn’t fathom how someone could be this crazy.</p>
<p>"At this point, I think we were crazy — I was crazy. But strangely, it felt good slaughtering them. After all, they came to destroy my world, and I wouldn’t let that happen.</p>
<p>All those who cross me will die."</p>
<p>Kent’s eyes turned golden, and the aura of chaos radiated from him. Without even knowing it, his claws were activated as he said those words. However, a few seconds later, a sigh escaped his lips.</p>
<p>"I was a menace. However, while I understand what I did, perhaps I could have been more welcoming and not gone straight to slaughter." He smiled, then continued to read.</p>
<p>[Okay. So the slaughter I committed wasn’t in vain. I managed to comprehend the first form of The Primal Bone Tempering Art.</p>
<p>This made my claws three times stronger, and my Dragon Aura now has that primal kink to it. I think I am becoming a predator, and the feeling is incredible.</p>
<p>However, I also think I may have gone overboard the last time. Perhaps I will be more civil next time and try to make things more peaceful.</p>
<p>However, the people of Lumen are happy with my leadership. Life is good, and while the slaughter is too much, they seem not to care.</p>
<p>Perhaps they are too afraid to say anything, considering on occasion I hear whispers of people saying I am a tyrant. Not that I care.</p>
<p>I don’t need their praise anyway. The only thing I need now is to grow stronger and have my battle with Leviathan.]</p>
<p>Kent flipped to the next page.</p>
<p>[So the civil approach didn’t work, so I had to lay waste to a thousand more. This time, they put up a fight. However, they couldn’t break past me.</p>
<p>Also, I managed to get one of their boats, which can travel through the spatial channels.</p>
<p>I am considering leaving Lumen to give them an in-person warning. However, I am afraid they will come while I am gone, so for now, I am weighing my options.</p>
<p>On one side, I could wait for the strongest of them to come, then after I kill all of them, I can travel to nearby worlds and give them a stern warning.</p>
<p>Or I can risk it and go bananas.]</p>
