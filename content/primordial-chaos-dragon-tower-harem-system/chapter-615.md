<p>One thing to note about the limit on worlds is that it only affects a person’s cultivation. This means that if an immortal descends to a medium world, only their cultivation will be suppressed to the Core Formation stage.</p>
<p>Of course, since they need their cultivation base to release their immortal aura, being suppressed won’t stop them from unleashing a much more powerful presence than a normal Core Formation stage expert could release.</p>
<p>This was why, when Aetheris, the second strongest in the Eternal Wrath Legion, appeared, the aura he released was that of a Spirit King at the peak.</p>
<p>But that wasn’t all; when the king and his people laid their eyes on him, they felt a fear that only comes from being under the aura of someone several times stronger than themselves.</p>
<p>And that was possible because Aetheris is an Immortal King suppressed by the dungeon to the Peak of the Sovereign stage.</p>
<p>"This is Aetheris," Kent said, smiling at the king and his people. A few seconds later, Kent turned to Aetheris, "Buddy, can you take away the heat?"</p>
<p>Aetheris nodded, and just like that, the heat inside the hall vanished, making everyone sigh. Aetheris also lowered his height to 2 meters.</p>
<p>"Hello everyone, I am Aetheris, a Flame Warden with perfect affinity for the Flame element." He may be made of lava, but that didn’t prevent him from smiling.</p>
<p>"So what do you think, Queen Amorian? Does this prove I can handle this mission?" Kent asked.</p>
<p>"He is powerful; however, that doesn’t mean he alone can make a change. But let’s assume there are more like him out there.</p>
<p>Can you truly promise this mission will be a success? The Hellhounds took some children too, and we would want them to come back alive."</p>
<p>If the Foxkins attack and even manage to rescue their people, the hellhounds will hit back hard next time. So this mission will be without their interference.</p>
<p>"Since you will meet them soon anyway, why don’t I bring all of them now?" Kent smiled and started summoning his legion.</p>
<p>Kaizo was the second to come out, looking smug as ever. Then Vaelros followed, bringing his imposing presence like the brute he is. He is the second brute in the undead legion, but instead of using his fist, he uses enchanted chains.</p>
<p>Sylara appeared, bringing her evil presence that made Princess Lyra move and hide behind her mother. Kent laughed when he saw this.</p>
<p>Zareth appeared next, chewing on an apple—something Kent nearly slapped him for. The classic appearance with an apple in hand was something Kent had invented, but now Zareth had stolen it.</p>
<p>’I have to create a new entrance move.’ Kent smirked but didn’t say anything to his strongest Undead, who had the power of the World Serpent running through his veins...</p>
<p>Skoggr appeared, but the moment the Foxkins set their sights on him, they nearly drew their weapons. After all, Skoggr was an orc, and the foxes hated the orcs. However, Kent told them he was not from this world.</p>
<p>Kent had contemplated bringing him, as he was familiar with the history between the Fox and the Orcs. However, after a few considerations, he realized Skoggr’s appearance would be even better than he had hoped.</p>
<p>Imagine an orc attacking the allies of the Orcs; that would indeed cause some chaos. He was looking forward to that.</p>
<p>Khaazal also appeared, wielding his Spear of the Tempest. After him, Nykali, the Shade King, appeared, further terrifying everyone, considering his head is a skull. Of course, he wasn’t offended when everyone became wary of him upon his appearance.</p>
<p>If anything, he was thrilled to incite such a reaction from them. The fear in their eyes made him feel a sense of relief—a rather twisted bastard who thrived on the fear of his opponents.</p>
<p>His backstory would be something else.</p>
<p>Kazarak, the Beast Warden with a lion’s head, also appeared. Yet again, the foxes became wary of him because they hated the Lionkin. However, this was beneficial for them, as Kent’s plan would be much more chaotic for the orc alliance.</p>
<p>Veera, the necromancer, also appeared. Then Gorzak, the second orc, also appeared. He is a barbarian orc, so his presence didn’t cause much reaction.</p>
<p>Valtor, the panther assassin, also appeared, keeping his presence as quiet as he could. As an assassin, he was someone who valued silence and shadows.</p>
<p>Irina, the strongest in Kent’s legion, appeared, dragging Veydris with her. Naturally, the moment she appeared, everyone knew she was indeed the strongest simply because they couldn’t sense any aura from her.</p>
<p>Zharkaz, the dragon with a broken horn, also appeared. For him, that dragon arrogance came with him, and since dragons are naturally regarded as the peak of the beast race, the foxes felt this primal fear when he arrived.</p>
<p>But Kent made sure he didn’t ride on their fear, so he just stood to the side, arms folded. Then Vorthar, the demon lord, appeared.</p>
<p>Lastly, the ice beauty, Ankaara, appeared, bringing her natural charm with her. In fact, when she appeared, all the intimidating aura in the hall vanished, replaced by a calm and loving presence.</p>
<p>This raised her status in the heart of the foxkins higher than that of her friends. Kent observed this lineup, and a smile appeared on his lips.</p>
<p>But he didn’t stop there. The next minute, a red-skinned beauty appeared. Kent wanted his women to join the battle, too, so he called them to join the team.</p>
<p>Velyra was the first to arrive, followed by Vari. Elaine was third, followed by Elsa. Lilian also appeared. The Unity also came.</p>
<p>Lani, Selene, and Ingrid appeared simultaneously. Neomi, Alina, Zaila, Mara, and Camilla also appeared simultaneously.</p>
<p>Nara and her friend Lani also appeared, with Vexthra appearing last. She stood beside Kent since she wouldn’t be fighting, only spectating.</p>
<p>However, looking at the lineup, the king and his people were taken aback.</p>
<p>"What do you think? Can I handle the task now?" Kent asked.</p>
<p>They didn’t even need to answer that. The moment the first six appeared, they knew Kent had what it took to handle the task.</p>
<p>Their cultivation base was indeed lacking, but the army before them was unnatural. They could tell they were much stronger than they looked.</p>
<p>"When will you be attacking the Hellhounds?" Queen Amorian asked.</p>
<p>"Tomorrow. By then, I will finish setting everything up that will leave the Orc Alliance in shambles by the time I am done."</p>
<p>Kent laughed. "When I am done, the Hellhounds and the Lionkin will be the last of your problems."</p>
