<p>"So those are the memberships we offer. The Tasty Home Pavilion is a continent-wide establishment, so no matter where you go on the human continent, you can use it. In fact, having a membership will even give you some benefits in other establishments," said the heavy-chested manager of the restaurant.</p>
<p>Though she knew nothing about Kent, the fact that he walked in with a member holding even just a silver badge was enough to tell her he was not simple.</p>
<p>"Then, can I know the prices for each membership?" Kent asked.</p>
<p>"The silver membership is 100,000 spirit stones. This will allow you access to the first three floors. Additionally, it permits one person to accompany you.</p>
<p>The gold membership is 1,000,000 spirit stones. This grants you access to the first five floors. You can also bring two people with you, each of whom will receive gold membership treatment.</p>
<p>The diamond membership is 10,000,000 spirit stones. With this, you will have access to the first eight floors and can bring five people with you.</p>
<p>Furthermore, this card allows you to stay for as long as you want in our hotels and inns across the continent for free.</p>
<p>You will also gain access to other services we provide and even have the opportunity to request a personal cook.</p>
<p>As for the platinum membership, which is our highest tier, it costs 100 million spirit stones. With this, you can access the top floor of the ninth floor.</p>
<p>You will receive a 70% discount on all food and free services such as hotels, massages, and even security, which can include multiple Root Transcendents at no cost.</p>
<p>There are numerous freebies you will receive at any establishment we own. In fact, with the platinum membership, you can bring up to 30 people with you, and none of them will be required to pay fees.</p>
<p>Additionally, with the platinum membership, any customer you recommend will receive a 30% discount on all memberships."</p>
<p>Kent listened to all the amazing packages, and a smile appeared on his face. The manager saw the smile and smiled back.</p>
<p>She held an expectant gaze, which showed she was really hoping Kent would buy a membership, earning her a huge commission.</p>
<p>If only she knew Kent was about to change her life.</p>
<p>"I will be buying a Platinum Membership. Also, kindly upgrade the membership of the lady I came with to the Platinum grade," Kent said casually.</p>
<p>The manager froze in her seat.</p>
<p>Kent said something, and her mind couldn’t process it immediately.</p>
<p>"Did-did you just say you want a platinum membership?" she asked in a bated breath.</p>
<p>Kent looked at her expression and smiled.</p>
<p>"Yeah. You know what they say, you must impress the beauties by showing off your wealth," Kent smirked, saying that. There was no saying like that... he just didn’t know what to do with his money.</p>
<p>He could buy most of the things he needed with Tower Points, so spirit stones were like something he could use on the side. It was like how cultivators view gold coins these days...it is worthless to him...at least for now.</p>
<p>Well, doing things like this was what he could use spirit stones for. So, as they say, the more you have, the less you know how to spend it.</p>
<p>The manager kept staring at Kent—not because she was admiring his handsomeness, but because she was shocked beyond her wits and couldn’t process how to proceed.</p>
<p>"Take your time," Kent said, leaning back in his chair as he waited for the manager to recover. Naturally, it took her a full minute.</p>
<p>"Right away," she said, pulling a jade token from her space ring and injecting spiritual qi into it. Then she began asking Kent for his details. Whenever Kent answered, the token would glow.</p>
<p>A couple of seconds later, the process was done, and she entered an inner office. When she walked out, she was holding yet another jade token. However, this one was much more elegant and golden in color.</p>
<p>"Here you go, and this is for your lady friend," she said, handing the tokens to Kent, who accepted them and continued to stare at her.</p>
<p>He was looking to see if she would understand why he was staring, but she seemed clueless. Kent smiled and said,</p>
<p>"Next time, compose yourself well before handing over such expensive jade crystals to a stranger without taking the money first."</p>
<p>Kent took two pouches from his space ring and handed them to her. She received them with trembling hands. Kent simply smiled like an overly privileged rich kid and left the office.</p>
<p>The manager put the money away and ran after him, her movements causing her ample chest to bounce up and down. Kent was walking ahead of her, so he couldn’t see such an elegant sight.</p>
<p>"Distinguished customer, because you are our first Platinum customer, you will enjoy free food for the next 30 days. No matter how much you eat or how much you bring with you, it will be free," the manager said, her tone shifting.</p>
<p>She had indeed earned a significant profit thanks to Kent, so she ensured his stay at the Tasty Home Pavilion would be smooth.</p>
<p>"Then I will take you up on the freebie," Kent replied, still walking. A hundred million spirit stones were not something to be underestimated—not in the slightest.</p>
<p>So, for him to have poured out close to 200 million spirit stones was simply too overwhelming, to say the least. Kent had to be the richest person she had encountered since becoming the manager or even since she started working at the Tasty Home Pavilion.</p>
<p>’I need to inform the pavilion mistress of this... This young man is not simple... She would want to know more about him and perhaps extend an invitation to him,’ she thought.</p>
<p>"You’re back," Ingrid said, looking strangely at Kent, who was smiling at her.</p>
<p>"Yeah... Sorry for making you wait so long," Kent said before turning to the manager. "Can you lead us to the ninth floor? I’m very hungry and would love to have a bite."</p>
<p>The manager nodded...</p>
<p>Ingrid, however, was shocked when she heard Kent’s request. "The ninth floor is for platinum members..." She paused and looked at him. "Don’t tell me you have a Platinum membership."</p>
<p>Kent smiled. "What can I say? The heavens wouldn’t forgive me if I don’t give a fairy such as yourself the best treatment." He retrieved the golden jade crystal token.</p>
<p>"You’re also a platinum member now, so good luck enjoying all the benefits." He handed the badge to her, causing Ingrid’s body to stiffen upon contact with it.</p>
<p>The manager watched all this with a complicated expression. It was a simple exchange, but she could swear she sensed no intimate air between them.</p>
<p>’So, did he just buy the membership because they are friends?’ she wondered.</p>
<p>Haha... If only she knew the Chaos Dragon was playing games, she would have looked at him in a completely new light.</p>
<p>Ingrid struggled with the idea that she was now a platinum member. However, she didn’t get to stay in her trance for long, as Kent dragged her away to the ninth floor, where they entered a private room and began to enjoy themselves.</p>
<p>Kent was rather hungry despite being a cultivator who could go months without food. Well, he is from Earth, after all...</p>
<p>On the outside, however, the angry disciples appeared... but all they would encounter was great disappointment.</p>
