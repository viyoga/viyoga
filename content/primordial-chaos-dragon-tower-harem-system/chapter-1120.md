<p>[Planet Blue Haven - Sylvara’s POV]</p>
<p>It had already been over 370,000 years since Kent parted with his pals from the past. To him, it had been only a few days, but to them, thousands of years had passed.</p>
<p>A lot had happened in the lives of his lovers, friends, and companions.</p>
<p>Sylvara, who met Kent with her clone body, had now merged with her main body and eagerly started waiting for the time she would meet Kent again.</p>
<p>360,000 years ago, when she and the others ascended to the immortal world, as Kent had planned, they arrived in the "Beasts Kingdom."</p>
<p>It was as they’d heard — a haven for all beasts. She and the others, of course, faced some opposition from the beasts living there.</p>
<p>However, since they had spent 10,000 years strengthening their foundations before ascending, it took them less than a thousand years to make their stance clear to every beast.</p>
<p>They were not to be messed with, and that message was heard loud and clear.</p>
<p>Sylvara remained in the Beasts Kingdom for 60,000 years and raised her strength to Spirit Immortal before leaving. She managed to sense her main body, so she left to become whole.</p>
<p>At the moment, she had reached the Immortal Monarch stage, a powerhouse in her own right. However, despite reaching such a terrifying stage, she couldn’t help missing Kent every single day.</p>
<p>She had her fair share of troubles. Of course, someone of her standing and beauty was bound to have her share of troubles.</p>
<p>Her mother had passed through the God Tribulation and ascended to godhood, so Sylvara was the one taking care of her clan.</p>
<p>There were demigods in their clan, and even a god — her grandmother. However, Sylvara was now in charge of affairs, being a royal, now that her mother was no longer around.</p>
<p>Of course, once she had consolidated her power, she would return, and Sylvara could reunite with her brothers and sisters.</p>
<p>She missed them terribly; however, the one she missed the most was the purple-haired brat who swept her off her feet.</p>
<p>From the very second she parted with Kent, she had been missing him like hell. However, she had already known it would take a lifetime before they met again.</p>
<p>That had given her a reason to wait. However, as the days passed and more trouble found her, she couldn’t help but sit and only miss the handsome dragon who made her heart skip a beat.</p>
<p>"I will meet you one of these days, Kent," she muttered, looking at the taming bracelet on her wrist.</p>
<p>She had not, even once, taken it off, since she knew doing so would be akin to showing she was tired of wearing it.</p>
<p>She can’t take it off, not even in the slightest.</p>
<p>"My lady, the people from the Great Sumin Immortal Family are here again," said a lady — a Snakekin who needed no introduction — to Sylvara, her expression filled with annoyance.</p>
<p>She clearly hated the people who dared worry her mistress.</p>
<p>"Tell them to go away. I have no interest in marrying that useless bastard. If they try to make a fuss, you have my permission to kill them."</p>
<p>The lady smiled upon hearing the command given to her. "I will do as you say, my lady." She walked away, and Sylvara could only sigh.</p>
<p>"Hurry and come to me, Kent, or else I will lose my mind."</p>
<p>***</p>
<p>[Beast Kingdom - Ignarok’s, Orvath’s, and Balrion’s POV]</p>
<p>Inside a large hall made purely out of gold, three men, each a powerhouse, sat drinking wine made from a rare, immortal fruit.</p>
<p>They looked relaxed, but one could tell Orvath was troubled. He, after all, had been traveling the Beast Kingdom for the past 40,000 years in search of ways to grow stronger.</p>
<p>To rise from one immortal stage to the next can take hundreds of thousands of years. It is also one of the reasons why the strength gap between immortals is as vast as heaven and Earth.</p>
<p>Orvath had just returned from his trip, so they had to bring him up to speed.</p>
<p>"It has been over 370 thousand years since we last saw the master. I know only Vey and the rest of the ladies will say this, but I kind of miss him," Balrion, the Flame Titan Monarch that Kent tamed back in the Flame Sea, said, taking a sip from his drink.</p>
<p>Ignarok, the Tyrant Flame Dragon, and Orvath, the Flame Minotaur Monarch, nodded with silent smiles on their lips.</p>
<p>"It is only normal to miss him. Even I miss him sometimes," Ignarok also admitted his weakness, something he wouldn’t have done in the past.</p>
<p>Now, all seven Flame Monarch Beasts that Kent had tamed had grown much closer. They didn’t feel any threat in telling their weaknesses and secrets to each other.</p>
<p>That was how much they trusted each other.</p>
<p>"Have you heard from the others?" Orvath suddenly asked the question on his mind. Balrion nodded and answered...</p>
<p>"Sylvara is now the matriarch of the Divine Flame Serpent Clan since her mother has ascended into godhood and is now in seclusion. She said she will pay us a visit when her mom is out.</p>
<p>As for Vey, she is currently having issues with her family. We asked if she needed help, but she rejected the offer, citing that she would handle it herself. Of course, we sent three Phoenixes to go help her despite her stubbornness. You already know how she can be.</p>
<p>As for Kaelith and Zeraphine, we don’t know where they are."</p>
<p>Ignarok took over, "The last I heard, Zeraphine went to the Chaos System to help a friend, but that was the last I heard from her. As for Kaelith, we heard nothing from him.</p>
<p>We also cannot reach either of them since the distance is too vast. However, I am sure they are well."</p>
<p>Ignarok smiled, "I mean, who would dare hurt them? They are subordinates of the Primordial Chaos Dragon. The one who dares lay a finger on them is doomed for all eternity."</p>
<p>The three immortal beasts smiled.</p>
<p>Ignarok had already reached the peak of the immortal stage — Immortal Lord — while Balrion and Orvath were both at the Immortal Monarch stage.</p>
<p>"I guess I will go see Sylvara next since she is available compared to Vey," Orvath said, and his two brothers nodded.</p>
<p>"You should. I can tell you have made a lot of progress. Use this time to strengthen yourself before the Chaos Rift opens. We are only 100 years away."</p>
<p>Orvath nodded to Ignarok’s words and soon left.</p>
<p>He had indeed made a lot of progress over the past 40,000 years, so now was the best time to harness his strength and prepare for what was promised to be one of the most dangerous yet rewarding expeditions of their lives.</p>
<p>The Chaos Rift opens to the Chaos Region, allowing immortals to go and explore. Demigods can even enter, but they will be suppressed to the immortal stage.</p>
<p>This Chaos Region can be a world from the Chaos Era or even a large planetary, stellar, or galactic system from the Chaos Era.</p>
<p>This means there will be treasures, and where there is treasure, there is danger.</p>
<p>Everyone was preparing, and Orvath had to prepare as well.</p>
<p>"I will go to Vey’s star system and keep a low profile just in case she needs help." Balrion also made his decision, and Ignarok nodded.</p>
<p>"Even though dragons and phoenixes don’t see eye to eye, if her life is in danger, summon me without any delay. She is stubborn and strong, but she is also one of the master’s lovers, so she cannot die."</p>
<p>"Okay. See you later then," Balrion left, and Ignarok also went back to cultivating.</p>
