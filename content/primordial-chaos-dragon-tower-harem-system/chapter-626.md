<p>’The faceless assassin, huh?’</p>
<p>Kent was both amused and a little taken aback by the audacity of this claim. The faceless assassin was one of his past lives. He had already received a memory key to start retrieving those memories.</p>
<p>But who would have thought he would find a follower of his in this world?</p>
<p>’Talk about a small world... well, more like a small universe. But who would have thought a follower who believes in the Faceless Assassin, one of my past lives, would be sitting across from me?’</p>
<p>Kent looked at Sir Ashfur, who was conversing with King Hkum, and a smile appeared on his lips, but it vanished the next second, as the king and his people seemed to have come to a conclusion.</p>
<p>[Thanks, Vex, see you later.]</p>
<p>Kent said to Vexthra before focusing back on the king and his people. Again, Queen Amorian was the one to speak.</p>
<p>"We only have one question, and an honest answer would be great."</p>
<p>Kent nodded. "Ask away, Queen Amorian."</p>
<p>She also nodded and asked the sole question they all wanted answers to. "Would this guardianship affect my people in any way that would be bad for them?"</p>
<p>Kent smiled and answered, "As far as I am aware, aside from me becoming a guardian, that must ensure everyone is safe. Everyone will be safe from all forms of harm.</p>
<p>So be assured, they will be safe and happy."</p>
<p>Everyone nodded.</p>
<p>So far, they would trust their supreme advisor and take Kent as their guardian. If they ended up in bed with the devil, then they would only pray that this devil is better than the Tyrant King of the Orcs, King Balma.</p>
<p>Kent smiled, noting their accepting expressions.</p>
<p>"In that case, I will only ask once..." They looked at him as if he were some kind of guardian sent from the heavens. "Do you all want me to become your guardian?"</p>
<p>There was a pause. Then, they nodded, "We do."</p>
<p>Kent smiled, and then, as he expected, a message appeared before his view.</p>
<p>⟦Do you want to become the guardian of the Grayvine Foxkin?⟧</p>
<p>"Yes," Kent answered, and then dozens of messages started to appear. He focused on one that appeared in red because he knew it was urgent.</p>
<p>⟦Tower Request: How do you want to receive your gains from the Grayvine Foxkin?⟧</p>
<p>↠ Spiritual Qi</p>
<p>↠ Soul Essence</p>
<p>’I didn’t know why this was now optional, but I think I will go for Soul Essence simply because I have a nagging feeling I will be needing more of that in the years to come.’</p>
<p>When he became a Tier 13 soul cultivator, he received the option to save soul essence, which in this case would be like tower points he could later use to raise his soul cultivation.</p>
<p>He didn’t know why it had become like that, but a nagging feeling kept telling him it had something to do with his yet-to-awaken soul path.</p>
<p>So, if that was the case, he wouldn’t hesitate to store more of it. Now, he would not only receive it from killing, but the Grayvine Foxkin race would become his source of soul essence.</p>
<p>This made him wonder just what would become of him when he awakened his soul cultivation path.</p>
<p>"I don’t know what happened, but I feel like I can depend on you, Brother Kent." Just as Kent was quickly checking through the messages, Princess Lyra said this, causing her family to look at Kent strangely.</p>
<p>They can all feel what she felt.</p>
<p>Kent turned and looked at them, and he smiled. "Anyways, as your official guardian, my first order of business is..."</p>
<p>He looked at them with a smile that held many meanings. However, while their hearts raced, they thought he would say something outrageous, but what he said instead left their jaws hanging.</p>
<p>Kent took out a golden fruit from his space ring. The moment everyone’s eyes landed on the fruit, a sense of attraction dawned on them.</p>
<p>"This is an Immortal-grade Ambrosia apple. When consumed, it will raise the grade of your spirit roots to the Immortal grade.</p>
<p>In other words, if you consume this fruit, you will be able to break both the first and second limits easily." Kent laughed and threw the fruit at Sir Ashfur. "You can examine it if you want."</p>
<p>Hearing he was a follower of his, Kent didn’t want to even think suspiciously of him again. And now that he had become their guardian, he would know if someone was plotting against the Foxkins.</p>
<p>Since he couldn’t sense any ill intent from him, he trusted that he would give sound judgment. As expected, after five minutes, he looked at Kent and smiled, though a shocked expression was on his face.</p>
<p>"This is indeed an Ambrosia fruit, and judging by its color, it is indeed Immortal grade." The king, the elders, Queen Amorian, and her children looked at Kent in shock.</p>
<p>The brat who liked the attention retrieved a pouch and made it float toward the king. "Inside are 100 Immortal-grade Ambrosia apples. Take one each and pick your most trusted men and share it among them.</p>
<p>I don’t really care how you share it, but make sure they are trusted to the point they won’t question anything you ask them to do."</p>
<p>King Hkum nodded, feeling his heartbeat rise and fall. He wasn’t the only one; they were all shocked beyond their understanding.</p>
<p>Never in their wildest dreams did they think they would be getting the means to break their limits this easily. It was just never in their books.</p>
<p>"Also, Sir Ashfur, this is a Tier 17 defense and attack array. It will be challenging and time-consuming to set up, but I trust you will be able to complete it.</p>
<p>The moment this is set up, not even King Balma can break it. And when you use it to attack, the spirit Emperors will run from it. Even Spirit Lords will be wary." Kent again threw a scroll to Sir Ashfur and stood up.</p>
<p>"I will be off now. If anything, talk to Tala; she will attend to your needs." Kent walked away, teleporting into the tower the moment he was outside.</p>
<p>He was so excited to see his gains that he immediately opened the messages. When his eyes landed on the first two messages, his smile bloomed.</p>
<p>⟦Congratulations, you are now the guardian of the Grayvine Foxkin.⟧</p>
<p>⟦New Guardian Tools unlocked in the Guardian Tower⟧</p>
