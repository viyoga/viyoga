<p>Kent came to assist Lady Khronisia, who had already invited top blacksmiths to witness her final forge as an Immortal Blacksmith.</p>
<p>She would soon ascend to become a God Blacksmith. So she wished to leave her mark in the Immortal Realm, allowing her name to live on for years to come.</p>
<p>Because of this, by the time Lady Khronisia was ready to begin, the forging arena she had selected had already become crowded with all kinds of blacksmiths and powerful figures from the Eastern Region of the Immortal Realm.</p>
<p>Many blacksmiths hoped for a day like this to experience forging at its finest level. So when word spread, they came.</p>
<p>Some came to witness what Lady Khronisia had planned to forge, while others came to observe and learn from her.</p>
<p>Of course, the majority came when word spread that Kent Madson was the forge master Lady Khronisia had chosen to assist her.</p>
<p>It was known that Lady Khronisia had been searching for someone who possessed Absolute Named Ice. Many had Absolute Ice, but they didn’t know its true name.</p>
<p>Kent was different. His ice had a true name, and so he was the perfect person she wanted. In the past, when Kent’s name appeared on the Ice Elemental Dao Tablet, many tried to look for him but failed.</p>
<p>They recognized the potential his ice element could bring. So, learning that he would be serving as Lady Khronisia’s assistant left many looking forward to what they could achieve together.</p>
<p>"It has been a while since an event like this was organized. I wonder if this will be the last one for at least a while," some of the spectators started to converse, wondering about many things.</p>
<p>Most had been in the world of blacksmithing for years, so they knew just how events like this usually played out.</p>
<p>"There is no need to get emotional about this. If Lady Khronisia manages to leave her mark as she intends, then I am sure many will follow in her footsteps."</p>
<p>"That would be great. We allowed the world of alchemy to overshadow us for too long. So this is the best time to rise and reclaim the fame we once had during the Flame Era."</p>
<p>Upon hearing the declaration, many straightened up and waited for the event to start.</p>
<p>An hour later, Lady Khronisia walked onto the stage, with Kent following behind her. As they arrived, the noise died down, everyone focusing on what was about to be said before the main event began.</p>
<p>"Thank you all for coming on such short notice," Lady Khronisia said, looking across the gathered crowd.</p>
<p>"I wouldn’t want to waste anyone’s time by saying all kinds of things. I believe my forge today will do more of the talking. However, if what I have in mind today comes to pass, then for the first time in millions of years, the Immortal Realm will finally witness the birth of a Soul-type Immortal Grade Spear, forged using Sacred Flame and True Ice."</p>
<p>Everyone gasped upon hearing what Lady Khronisia had said.</p>
<p>Normally, the flames every blacksmith used were either Soul Flames produced by their own souls or Primordial Flames absorbed after being tamed.</p>
<p>Sacred Flames were a myth to most since they had never seen one.</p>
<p>They knew they existed, but they had never seen one before. To learn that she would be using one today surely came as a shock.</p>
<p>However, everyone soon turned to look at Kent, and suddenly it all made sense.</p>
<p>Still, some questioned her.</p>
<p>"I am not one to be jealous of your gains, Lady Khronisia, but isn’t using Sacred Flame a bit too reckless for such an event? After all, you have never used some before, so what makes you think you can handle it?"</p>
<p>The question was valid, and Lady Khronisia would have worried about it had it not been for what had happened between her and Kent a couple of days ago.</p>
<p>After they arrived at the Blacksmith Association, Kent taught her a flame-control technique and temporarily granted her control over his Nine Nirvana Flames. Since she would be using them, she might as well learn how to control them.</p>
<p>Of course, that alone wasn’t enough, since it takes time and a lot of soul cultivation to harness Sacred Flames to a level where forging with them would be within her reach.</p>
<p>Then again, she had stayed inside the tower for months, allowing Gaia and her minions to prepare her for this very moment.</p>
<p>Her soul was now robust, perhaps already at the level of a Supreme Blacksmith, so she was able to learn the technique and harness the power of the Nine Nirvana Flames in merely two days of practice.</p>
<p>She was smart, and that alone was all there was to know about her.</p>
<p>So, hearing the question, she smiled and answered, "I am sure I can handle it. I wouldn’t be here if I couldn’t handle a Sacred Flame."</p>
<p>Many sighed, some taking her decision as a reckless move driven by a hunger for greatness. But then again, she sounded confident, so they could only wait to see what she had planned.</p>
<p>Because of the revelation that she would be using Sacred Flames, many seemed not to have picked up on the soul-type aspect of the weapon she intended to forge.</p>
<p>Regardless, she was ready to start...</p>
<p>"Today, Kent Madson will be assisting me as my forge master. He has already accepted my proposal, so allow him the respect every forge master deserves."</p>
<p>They all clapped their hands, something they did for all forge masters. They would never be recognized as having helped forge the weapon, since their fame ended with its forging.</p>
<p>Regardless, they were respected by many for their role in history.</p>
<p>Not many blacksmiths needed forge masters, though...</p>
<p>"Since all has been said, then allow me to begin the process." She extended her hand and lit a green flame, instantly covering the space around her.</p>
<p>Turning toward Kent, she nodded.</p>
<p>Kent smiled and snapped his fingers, summoning a sleek golden anvil that caused many to gasp. Kent and Lady Khronisia didn’t seem bothered, as Kent retrieved the materials and the forging process soon began...</p>
