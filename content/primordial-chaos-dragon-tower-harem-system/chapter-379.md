<p>Among all the things Kent was expecting, the quest he had received wasn’t one of them. He never expected this, and after reading the lengthy descriptions, he just couldn’t believe his eyes.</p>
<p>⟪You have received a Universe Quest⟫</p>
<p>⟪Quest Name: Shadow Guardian of Planet Dusk⟫</p>
<p>⟪Quest Type: World Quest⟫</p>
<p>⟪Quest Difficulty: Calamity⟫</p>
<p>→ Planet Dusk was once a bustling world filled with billions of Duskborn—humans with darkened, shadowy skin and glowing violet or crimson eyes.</p>
<p>→ There were millions of powerful cultivators. The world was thriving, and everyone was happy.</p>
<p>→ Though they only had one Planet Protector, the threat they faced wasn’t something that required protection.</p>
<p>→ They were the most peaceful you could ask for.</p>
<p>→ But that all came crashing down 15,000 years ago when the Obsidian Dark Demon Ant Queen Elxn of Planet Kuynx invaded this peaceful world, and everything changed for the worse.</p>
<p>→ Billions couldn’t make it, for the magnitude of the battle was just too devastating.</p>
<p>→ The battle lasted for 200 days, and when they finally managed to kill the Ant Queen, 90% of their warriors were wiped out, and 60% of the race was decimated.</p>
<p>→ But they won, as it should be. However, before the queen died, she left a package for them.</p>
<p>→ The Ant colony was left behind, and it was meant to mature 7500 years ago, of which the infamous Ant King Ketu and his thousands of Obsidian Dark Demon Warrior Ants were meant to finish what she started.</p>
<p>→ However, the universe believed in fair chances, so it turned the colony into a Dungeon to buy the world of Dusk a chance and set restrictions on it that prevented it from causing any harm to the Duskborn.</p>
<p>→ They must first grow and build their strength back to the stage where they can face such a hidden threat.</p>
<p>→ But every good thing comes to an end.</p>
<p>→ Two years ago, Ant King Ketu broke through to the Core Formation stage; thus, it has risen to the stage where the restrictions of the Ant Dungeon could no longer hold it.</p>
<p>→ In two years, the restrictions will be broken, and the strongest being the World of Dusk has ever seen will be unleashed, with hundreds of Root Sovereigns, thousands of Root Ascensions, and tens of thousands of Root Transcendents raining down on them.</p>
<p>→ This cannot be allowed to happen, for the Duskborn are one with the universe, and as such, they must be protected.</p>
<p>→ This means you must become a guardian again and prevent a calamity from befalling them.</p>
<p>→ The universe has picked you to become her weapon against these terrors.</p>
<p>→ You have two years to handle this threat and ensure the world of Dusk, who is unaware of such a threat hanging over them, never knows they narrowly escaped a great calamity.</p>
<p>→ Below are the details of the dungeon and the reward for completing the quest.</p>
<p>⟪Rank: SSS⟫</p>
<p>⟪Levels: 32⟫</p>
<p>⟪Power Levels: Root Transcendents - Core Formation⟫</p>
<p>⟪You can enter the dungeon at will. Just will it, and you will be teleported inside.⟫</p>
<p>→ There will be rewards for clearing every level, but below are the overall rewards after clearing the entire dungeon. The hidden rewards mean they will be revealed after the quest is over.</p>
<p>⟪Rewards:⟫</p>
<p>→ 1 Billion Tower Points</p>
<p>→ True Name Tokens (20)</p>
<p>→ Five Destiny Memory Keys (Unity)</p>
<p>→ Ten Memory Keys (4 diff. Incarnations)</p>
<p>→ Ascension Platform</p>
<p>→ Hidden Rewards (Ten)</p>
<p>Kent was left stunned, for this was just too much for someone like him, despite knowing this was meant to happen when he became the son of the universe and was granted the Universe Quest Pool.</p>
<p>But now that he had received this quest, he could tell shit had just gotten real.</p>
<p>"An SSS rank quest, huh... That is a huge one," he sighed.</p>
<p>⟪I wouldn’t say the master was unfortunate, for this is a big opportunity for you. The odds are indeed against you, but these will prepare you for the dangers that will come.</p>
<p>Facing hundreds of Sovereigns will be a good way for you to measure your strength against odds that just can’t be ignored.</p>
<p>Also, you will be getting some good Sovereign bodies for your undead. I can already see hundreds of undead warriors moving at your command.</p>
<p>But if that isn’t all, after defeating this Ant King, you will not only have a Core Formation stage warrior on speed dial, but you will also have a powerful dungeon future legions will be able to train in.</p>
<p>For now, plan well and face this with determination. The rewards are just too good to be ignored.⟫</p>
<p>Gaia said, and Kent could only nod. Like she said, this was a huge boon for him, for this is where shit gets real. If he wants to prepare for the apocalypse, he must face the dangers of this quest.</p>
<p>⟪Plus, you already have material to create 6 more undead and 12 dungeons you can use to train with your ladies before this quest begins.</p>
<p>2 years is over 40 years inside the tower, so you have enough time to prepare. Just make good use of your tower, for this is a big game for you, and you must play it.⟫</p>
<p>Akira also added her voice, and Kent could only smile.</p>
<p>"You two are the best. I guess we must rescue Dori next to reunite you three sisters."</p>
<p>⟪Of course, we are the best. And don’t worry about that slacker. Not even gods can kill her⟫</p>
<p>Kent smiled.</p>
<p>He knew if this quest was as it was described, then this was his chance to prove to the universe that he was qualified to be the guardian once again.</p>
<p>"Let’s go explore the city," Kent suggested, and the two lolis nodded. The quest was a good one, but the mechanisms behind it weren’t something simple.</p>
<p>The beautiful lady sitting at the universe’s core smiled when she saw the ready expression on Kent’s face.</p>
<p>"Some destinies are meant to be remembered and reawakened." The image of Unity appeared: "She once laid down her life for her people, and now a world that also lost its protector has triggered her destiny."</p>
<p>The smile on her face bloomed.</p>
<p>"She was once known as a warrior with the bow, for she was the best archeress in the universe. She was known as Love, the protector who laid down her life for her people.</p>
<p>Now she must awaken her destinies and become who she once was and who she was meant to be."</p>
<p>She snapped her finger, and far away into the cosmos, inside a dungeon, a key appeared on the first floor, which entered the body of a level 3 Root Transcendent Obsidian Dark Demon Warrior Ant.</p>
<p>"Your aim will determine your destiny, sister... I look forward to when you awaken."</p>
