<p>During the primordial era, long before the eternal battle against the unknown, there was a unique dragon named Vaeltharion. She was a unique type of dragon due to two distinct aspects of her bloodline.</p>
<p>A very unique bloodline.</p>
<p>She was a dragon with both Absolute Ice and Everlasting Flame. They called her the Twin-Breath Tyrant, for her flame and ice were like the bane of existence, changing everything for everyone.</p>
<p>Her command over flame and ice made many people afraid of her. So, they did the only thing cowards do and schemed against her.</p>
<p>244 days.</p>
<p>That was how long it took to kill her. The Battle of Ice and Fire lasted for 244 days, during which the Twin-Breath Tyrant laid waste to civilizations and decimated countless armies and powerhouses.</p>
<p>However, it was all for nothing, for in the end, she couldn’t move ahead and become a dragon god, like the infamous Chaos Dragon.</p>
<p>So, in her last moments, she cried to the universe, and she listened.</p>
<p>"I may be dying, but my legacy will never die. I will rise again, and my fury will burn the landscapes of my foes, bathe civilizations in flames, and freeze the happiness of those who oppose me.</p>
<p>"I shall rise again, and my name will be known by Celestials, for the name that will carry the legacy of the Twin-Breath Tyrant will awaken one day."</p>
<p>Kent and the ladies gazed at the two dragons circling a dragon core in the air, and they could both feel the fury emanating from them.</p>
<p>One was a blue dragon made of ice, and the second was a red dragon made of fire. The energy coming from the half-blue and half-red core was something Kent could tell was the dragon’s energy.</p>
<p>"What is going on? Is Lilian going to be okay?" Elsa asked with a worried expression.</p>
<p>Though she and Lilian had become sisters, she still saw her as her daughter. Watching as her body was reduced to a core just after she added her blood to the True Name Token made her deeply concerned.</p>
<p>"She will be fine. You went through the same thing and came out just fine, so relax and just watch" Kent smiled and continued to watch.</p>
<p>An hour later, the dragons merged with the blue-red core. Kent, who was staring at the core, felt the True Name and the method of awakening appear in her mind, forcing a smile to her lips.</p>
<p>"The Fury of the Everlasting Flames and the Stillness of the Eternal Ice. A union born out of chaos and understanding. The rise of the Frostfire.</p>
<p>The awakening of a menace. The one who possesses the flame’s fury and the ice’s coldness. She has risen and is ready to take the mantle of the Dual Dragon Mage, the Frostfire Dragon Mage.</p>
<p>She is Aezrith Nivalis Ignis, the Eternal Frostfire Dragon.</p>
<p>Rise from the flames and freeze the heart of all. Rise, Aezrith Nivalis Ignis."</p>
<p>When Kent spoke those words, the place turned icy and scorching hot. The flame and the ice were burning like never before.</p>
<p>In fact, the sensation was something many wouldn’t truly understand, for ice and fire were now in union, and the feeling coming from it said it all. It is not something many would want to come into existence.</p>
<p>From up in the air, a blue-red dragon looked down upon the bugs on the floor and roared.</p>
<p>It circled the air briefly before returning to its original position.</p>
<p>Then it transformed, and a breathtaking beauty appeared. Everyone first noticed the colour of her eyes: one eye was blue, and the other was red.</p>
<p>But they are breathtaking.</p>
<p>She has long blue hair with strands of red in it, and her body has changed. She now has a red and blue dragon horn on her forehead.</p>
<p>Her skin is covered in shades of red and blue scales, making her appear both majestic and powerful.</p>
<p>Her body is an hourglass of seductiveness, and even her chest area has grown, giving her a presence that no man can look away from.</p>
<p>But after appearing like that for a moment, the horns disappeared, and she transformed again. This time, her eyes turned completely blue—both of them.</p>
<p>Then, instead of fire and ice qi emanating from her, only freezing cold ice came from her. Her eyes were blue, and her hair was also blue.</p>
<p>But that, too, lasted only a few seconds before she changed again. This time, her eyes turned red, and her hair also became red. That was her fourth form, and Kent, who was on the ground, smiled.</p>
<p>’Four forms, huh? Just what bloodline has she awakened?’ He scanned her and focused solely on her bloodline. When he saw it, a part of her smiled happily, as if she had met an old friend.</p>
<p>⟪Bloodline: Primordial Frostfire Dragon Lineage (25%)⟫</p>
<p>→ This bloodline descends from a legendary dragon known as Vaeltharion, the Twin-Breath Tyrant, who wielded both Primordial Frost and Eternal Flame—a nearly impossible combination.</p>
<p>→ The one who inherits this bloodline shall be called a Frostfire Dracovian, for she will have command over both ice and fire.</p>
<p>’Just 25%, and she can already have a full-on dragon form. I guess the moment I awaken my bloodline to 10%, I can also rise to a similar stage or close.’</p>
<p>Kent smiled.</p>
<p>Lilian stayed in the air for a few minutes before descending with an air of grace. She had taken on the form of Ice, meaning unlike Elsa, who can only maintain her sentinel form for a few hours a day, she can do so all day.</p>
<p>This also means that in her demi-dragon form, which is the union between fire and ice, she can only maintain it for a short while.</p>
<p>As for her dragon form, Kent knew that though she could take on her full dragon form, she would need to awaken more of her bloodline to maintain that transformation permanently.</p>
<p>She came right into Kent’s waiting arms and locked into a fierce kiss with her. During the kiss, a red and blue dragon tattoo appeared on Kent’s forehead for a moment before it disappeared.</p>
<p>However, in Kent’s vision, a message appeared, shocking him.</p>
<p>He had never heard of the legacy of the Frostfire Dracovian, but now, in his arms, was a beauty who commanded that bloodline.</p>
<p>Strangely, this beauty had just bestowed upon her something gods would kill for.</p>
