<p>The Pill Saints are already seated in a VIP room overlooking the competition that was about to begin.</p>
<p>Under normal circumstances, they only come for the Pill Concoction trials, but today, there is someone there they want to keep their eyes on.</p>
<p>Kent is a target they all want to exploit, so they will take advantage of any opportunity to get familiar with him.</p>
<p>Kent had already shown remarkable prowess and wealth, and as such, he is their target.</p>
<p>But that can wait; the competition has just started.</p>
<p>"The first trial will test your spiritual strength to ensure you meet the minimum requirement," the elder said.</p>
<p>"In the previous competition, you were only required to reach the green mark of the Soul Pillar, but this time around, you will be required to be at the peak of it, which will make you qualified.</p>
<p>This is to ensure that those who will remain aren’t overwhelmed by the rest of the trial. So, if you want to have a shot at qualifying, you are required to have soul strength as high as the Tier 4 stage."</p>
<p>The Soul Pillar has several markings that can be used to determine someone’s soul strength. The first mark is white, the second is blue, the third is yellow, the fourth is green, and the fifth is gold.</p>
<p>Since the green is at the fourth stage, this means it is required of one to have a soul strength of at least 4th Tier in soul cultivation.</p>
<p>Kent, who is at the 10th stage in soul cultivation, looked at the pillar and could only sigh.</p>
<p>⟪Would the master follow the plan and not show everything from the start?⟫ Dori asked. Kent had already told his three assistants his plan.</p>
<p>’Indeed,’ Kent replied.</p>
<p>Though he isn’t a Pill Grandmaster yet, his soul strength is at the 10th Tier, something not even the Pill Saints could boast of.</p>
<p>Most of them are only at the 7th Tier, so if his true soul strength were to be made public, many would bleed. This was why he wouldn’t do that.</p>
<p>But his main reason for not wanting to do that was because of the last requirement to become a Pill Grandmaster.</p>
<p>He must challenge a Pill Master to a duel. Now, this is a competition; it can’t be seen as a one-on-one competition.</p>
<p>However, he can make it a one-on-one competition while showing everyone he is capable. Since he won’t be shining just yet, he will make it so the one who will be shining would want but to challenge him.</p>
<p>His end game is that.</p>
<p>So instead of scaring everyone, he will play the game.</p>
<p>Of course, he knew the game was rigged, which was perfect for him. He will ensure that at the fourth stage of the trial, the competition will be fierce, and as such, one of the geniuses will challenge him.</p>
<p>’Ladies, just keep things moderated so I don’t break its pillar. It only has 8 markings, and I am at the 10th Tier, which will destroy it.’</p>
<p>⟪Then where would the master want to be?⟫</p>
<p>’Keep me below the second ranker.’</p>
<p>⟪Okay, master.⟫</p>
<p>Being the first from the get-go will be hard, so he will handle the geniuses by breaking their momentum while looking for his opponent.</p>
<p>"Without further ado, Michael Gyan, step forward." A young man in his early twenties stepped forward and walked toward the Soul Pillar.</p>
<p>"Just place your hands on it and channel your spiritual energy into it," the elder said, and the young man obeyed. He did as he was told, and as soon as he channeled his energy into the pillar, it lit up.</p>
<p>The white lit up, followed by the blue, yellow, and green. It moved and stopped just when the gold lit up.</p>
<p>"You’ve qualified to go to the next stage." The young man beamed with joy as he walked back smiling.</p>
<p>"Stacy Henny" was the next call. She also managed to stop at the gold rank, showing that she has the soul strength to handle the rest of the trial.</p>
<p>Kent stood on the side holding his tag, showing he was 46th to be called.</p>
<p>But he just stood there looking for his target, which he would use as the requirement to become a Pill Grandmaster.</p>
<p>Slowly, more people started passing until a dude named Lowell couldn’t get past the green light. He stopped in the middle, showing he couldn’t get to the next stage of the contest.</p>
<p>The dude just sighed and walked away, joining the audience.</p>
<p>After the tenth person was tested, Ragnar, one of the seven geniuses, turned and walked toward the pillar since he was the 11th.</p>
<p>"That is Ragnar; he is the disciple of Pill Saint Devin," one person in the audience said, and everyone started talking about him.</p>
<p>"I wonder if he also makes berserker pills like his master," one of them said.</p>
<p>"It’s only natural. Just like how the disciple of the Wicked Poison Witch specializes in poison pills, the rest of the disciples of the other saints wouldn’t be any different."</p>
<p>"If that is the case, then he is fortunate, I guess."</p>
<p>Ragnar stopped at the pillar and channeled his energy into it. The moment he did that, the first three lights lit up and quickly moved past the fourth and on to the fifth, which is a gold light.</p>
<p>It moved and stopped at the peak of the fifth stage, showing that his soul strength was at the peak of the fifth Tier.</p>
<p>The audience went wild, cheering him on.</p>
<p>Meanwhile, Kent just smirked and continued to watch.</p>
<p>A few calls later, the next genius went. Lucas, the disciple of Pill Saint Desmond. He also stopped at the peak, just like Ragnar did.</p>
<p>Naturally, after the fifth genius also showed that they are at the peak of the fifth Tier, everyone could only cheer for them.</p>
<p>However, when the fifth genius left, a barefooted lady whose demeanor and appearance resembled someone more in tune with nature and highly spiritual approached the pillar next.</p>
<p>Kent raised an eyebrow when he sensed her use her soul strength to appraise him first before going.</p>
<p>When she touched the pillar, the first four lights lit up instantly, shocking everyone.</p>
<p>"That is Elaine, the top genius among the seven geniuses," someone said, smiling lustfully as they gazed at the emotionless Elaine channeling her energy into the pillar.</p>
