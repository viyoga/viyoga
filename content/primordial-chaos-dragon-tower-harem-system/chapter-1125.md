<p>[Planet Qilin - Everlasting Pill Mountain - Pill Immortal Flura’s POV]</p>
<p>Tribulation lightning fell from the sky, judging a pale white Tier 21 Pill that had just been formed. Standing below it, forming complex hand seals, is a beauty whose name had reached several star systems and even galaxies.</p>
<p>She is Pill Immortal Flura, one of the most accomplished alchemists in the universe at present.</p>
<p>Thousands of years ago, when she arrived in this world as a Pill Monarch, many looked down on her. After all, she was merely a Pill Monarch in an Immortal World.</p>
<p>Nobody took her seriously; not even the Pill Sect’s factions and alliances accepted her.</p>
<p>However, Pill Immortal Flura didn’t give up.</p>
<p>She slowly built herself up, using every chance she could get to learn something new. Soon, however, her name started to spread.</p>
<p>Eventually, the name Pill Immortal Flura reached many worlds, planetary systems, star systems, and even galaxies.</p>
<p>It was only then that the powerful pill factions and sects that rejected her started to try to recruit her again. However, as someone who has achieved such great success that she even has demi-god customers, she had no reason to join them.</p>
<p>Thus, for thousands of years now, she has been working extremely hard to enhance her understanding of pill making, and hopefully, when she meets Kent again, she will be able to defeat him.</p>
<p>At the Pill Immortal stage, there are three tiers of pills that can be made: Tier 20, 21, and 22.</p>
<p>The pill in the air is a Tier 21 Immortal Pill, and the fact that the heavenly tribulation judging it has five layers of color indicates that the pill will be of high grade.</p>
<p>Ten minutes later, the cloud receded, announcing that her pill met all requirements of the Heavenly Pill Dao. It had two violet pill rings around it, which means the pill’s potency had been enhanced by 12 times.</p>
<p>But since there are two violet rings—one from her effort and the other from the Heavenly Pill Dao—the potency of the pill is now 24 times more potent.</p>
<p>That is how much more valuable the Immortal Pill is.</p>
<p>If there were three violet pill rings, the potency would have been 36 times greater.</p>
<p>The moment the tribulation ended, the pill transformed into a bird and tried to fly away. However, Pill Immortal Flura was ready. She formed a hand seal, and a triangular shield formed around it.</p>
<p>With a simple gesture, she then dragged it back.</p>
<p>"Let me go, human. In case you are not aware, I am an Immortal-grade pill. You will do well—"</p>
<p>A pill bottle appeared in Pill Immortal Flura’s hand, and she placed the talkative pill inside and put it away.</p>
<p>From Tier 15, the Heavenly Pill Dao judges pills and determines which ones meet all requirements of a good pill and which should just be discarded.</p>
<p>At Tier 18, the pill, after judgment, gains intelligence and can even transform into humans or any other form the Heavenly Dao deems them worthy.</p>
<p>The pill Flura had just formed is a Tier 21 Pill with two violet pill rings. However, looking at the expression on her face, one can see she isn’t happy.</p>
<p>Ursa, the phoenix that Kent gave her as her guardian and friend, walked toward her. "Little sis, no need to look dull. You have just formed your first Tier 21 Immortal Pill with two violet pill rings. That is something to be happy about."</p>
<p>"I know, Ursa. It is just that I can’t help but think Kent has long surpassed me," Pill Immortal Flura said, taking a deep sigh.</p>
<p>"He is a monster, Sis. People like him shouldn’t be the targets one should chase after." Ursa smiled, recalling the mischievous smile on Kent’s face when he bid them goodbye 370,000 years ago.</p>
<p>"He is a monster, but you are also a monster. I mean, just look at you. An entire Pill Faction with three Pill Gods is scared of you simply because they are threatened by your talent.</p>
<p>I say that is something to be proud of. Just like how you have a Kent, they also have a Flura."</p>
<p>Pill Immortal Flura smiled, hearing Ursa’s words. "You always know how to cheer me up."</p>
<p>"Lord Kent said I should always keep you happy, so I am just doing that."</p>
<p>"Oh, so if it wasn’t for him, you wouldn’t have made me happy?"</p>
<p>Ursa smiled and answered, "Probably not."</p>
<p>"Tch, what a shameless sister I have." She took Ursa’s hand, and they started walking toward a small yet elegant mansion sitting atop the same mountain they were on.</p>
<p>Ursa is an Immortal Monarch, while Flura is an Immortal Emperor.</p>
<p>Naturally, Ursa is in charge of all battles since she is a martial cultivator and a terrifying blue flame phoenix.</p>
<p>She had been protecting Pill Immortal Flura from the Blaze Pill Valley for thousands of years already, and she would continue doing it for thousands more to come.</p>

<p>[Planet Bakora - Lady Wu and Veth’s POV]</p>
<p>Planet Bakora can be regarded as the capital planet in the Bakora Wealth Star System. It has many planets, of which even Planet Qilin (the planet on which Pill Immortal Flura lives) is a part.</p>
<p>In this star system, the most powerful thing is money, and so many engage in all kinds of businesses and dealings just to make a living.</p>
<p>It is a wealthy planet, and this is evident in the buildings, people, and the natural beauty of the planet itself.</p>
<p>On this planet, there are many businesses, but two of them stood out.</p>
<p>The Brown Maple Auction Empire and the Dream Pill Emporium.</p>
<p>Lady Wu and Lady Veth are two of the most powerful women on this planet and in the Bakora Star System.</p>
<p>Their businesses extend far and wide to the point that even the powerhouses from other star systems try to go into business with them.</p>
<p>At the Dream Pill Emporium, all kinds of pills that you want can be procured for you and sold at a reasonable price. They have connections with numerous alchemists, sects, and alchemical factions.</p>
<p>Lady Veth, the owner, is not only beautiful but also intelligent and dedicated. She has many suitors, but in her head, only one person matters.</p>
<p>Then comes the Brown Maple Auction Empire, whose roots can be traced back to High Worlds and is also one of the biggest and grandest auction empires there is in the star system and beyond.</p>
<p>They have three events that cannot be missed unless, of course, you want to miss out on heaven-defying opportunities.</p>
<p>They hold mega auctions every 1,000 years, 10,000 years, and 100,000 years, where items that have never been seen before are sold.</p>
<p>In 65 years’ time, the third mega auction, which is hosted every 100,000 years, will take place. So Lady Wu and Lady Veth are working around the clock to organize a big auction, even bigger than the one that happened 100,000 years ago, which had even drawn the attention of the gods and demigods.</p>
<p>This time, they want to make it even bigger because they have received so much that needs to be sold.</p>
<p>This auction lasts for 5 whole months, as that is how much needs to be sold. One would say buying a 0.002% stake in this business will make you a wealthy man, no matter what.</p>
<p>In essence, these two ladies are doing way better than Kent had imagined. He merely wanted them to have a good life; he never expected them to become this wealthy and powerful in just 370,000 years.</p>
<p>However, they are, and as the person who made all this happen, Kent will certainly get to enjoy the fruit of his labor.</p>
