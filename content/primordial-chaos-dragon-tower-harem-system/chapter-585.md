<p>"The changes are just mind-busting. Not only has your river become a sea, but there are millions of sea creatures in here." Kent said, holding Zaila’s hands as he and her sisters explored her soul sea.</p>
<p>There was water everywhere, filled with millions of sea creatures, some even Vexthra didn’t recognise. Thankfully, in a strange way, Zaila knew their names and loads of stuff about them.</p>
<p>But aside from the sea, thousands of mountains littered the space, creating something akin to a mountainous island all over her soul sea.</p>
<p>There were some strange plants too, and this time, Kent and his wives didn’t enter with their consciousness; they were there with their true bodies.</p>
<p>Zaila explained that when her mind space expanded, it became something akin to a world that could sustain life regardless of the form they came in.</p>
<p>It is true that they can enter with only their consciousness, but they can also enter with their main body, which makes it a world.</p>
<p>A young world, but a world nonetheless.</p>
<p>Vexthra even explained that once you become an immortal, your core will evolve into a true world that people can live in.</p>
<p>Most immortals even go as far as creating their own living beings after comprehending the law of creation to the Perfect Harmony level.</p>
<p>Though only one in a billion will waste that much time.</p>
<p>But having a true world means you can keep beings in there, and the best part, you will be the god of that space.</p>
<p>This makes Zaila a literal god in the 60,000 square miles of space housing thousands, perhaps millions, of sea creatures. However, there is more.</p>
<p>Thanks to having such a large mind space, her aura has also grown to the extent that she can now exert more of her mind than when it was merely 6,000 square miles.</p>
<p>This means the current her has become ten times more powerful than the previous her, and even back then, her strength was already enough to shut up even the mother of killers.</p>
<p>So one could only imagine how monstrous she had become now..</p>
<p>The only downside now is that her Primordial spirit has regressed to Tier 1, so she can’t help her until she rises again.</p>
<p>"So, do you like, have the power to summon this creature to fight for you?" Ingrid asked, playing with a crystal shark.</p>
<p>If people from the Primordial Era saw this, they would probably run away screaming. A crystal shark was one of the powerful sea beasts that could harness both water and ice.</p>
<p>During the Primordial Era, they were creatures that caused great mayhem everywhere. Crossing the sea or oceans was impossible then.</p>
<p>Without the means to contend against them, passing through the sea would be the last thing one wanted.</p>
<p>However, not only are they friendly and playing with mere mortals, but these mortals even want to know if they can be used as a fighting force.</p>
<p>And the answer is, "Yes. They can help me in battle. However, my current self is too weak to even consider comprehending the technique for controlling them.</p>
<p>I must at least be at the Spirit King stage to comprehend it. Thankfully, I have an intelligent Primordial spirit that can do that once she rises back up. So, yes, one day, I will be able to command every last sea creature here."</p>
<p>"That will be awesome. You alone will have an army that can go against the armies of great kingdoms. I can’t wait to see that." Vexthra and her sisters smiled happily as they jumped on the sea dragons and started riding them across the sea.</p>
<p>The only ones left at the shore were Kent and Zaila’s primordial spirit.</p>
<p>"They look happy," Kent said, and the primordial spirit nodded with a smile.</p>
<p>"By the way, I have a name now. You can call me Y’lyssai; that was my name in my past life," the primordial spirit said, and Kent turned and looked at her strangely.</p>
<p>"You already started having memories of your past self?" he asked, but Y’lyssai shook her head.</p>
<p>"Not yet. I only remember my name somehow. However, I am sure the moment I start comprehending the ancient path technique, the memories will come," Y’lyssai answered, making Kent smile.</p>
<p>"I guess that will be cool. Knowing about your past will have great effects on you, trust me. I know what I’m talking about." Kent placed his head on her lap and looked up at her.</p>
<p>"The name is a mouthful, so why don’t I shorten it and make it Lyssa?"</p>
<p>"Whatever makes you happy, I guess. In any case, I will be in seclusion for the next couple of years, perhaps even the next ten years, and try to bring my cultivation back to Tier 18.</p>
<p>This is so I can help my main body get the most out of our inheritance. So don’t miss me too much."</p>
<p>Kent just chuckled. He still didn’t fully understand Primordial spirits, but looking at Lyssa, he could tell she was very intelligent, which showed how profound they were.</p>
<p>"I look forward to what you will do in the future," Kent said. A few seconds later, he yawned and then dozed off, entering a deep sleep.</p>
<p>A few hours later, his wives came back.</p>
<p>"He is asleep, huh?" They looked at his sleeping face and smiled.</p>
<p>"He’s not telling us everything that went on when she visited my past, but I think he needs this sleep, so let’s let him," Lyssa said, and the ladies nodded.</p>
<p>Soon, they were back inside the Harem tower, where Kent was allowed to rest.</p>
<p>Meanwhile, the ladies went back to the garden, and Alina retrieved the Celestial Portrait of the Eternal Garden for them to view.</p>
<p>This time, even Vexthra joined them. Then, a few hours into the viewing, Elaine gained enlightenment. Her sisters soon followed with Vexthra, the goddess being the last.</p>
<p>She had dreamed of having the chance to view this portrait for millions of years now, but the Supreme Celestial only allows his close friends access.</p>
<p>Now, she can view it millions of times if she wants. After all, it now belongs to her sister. So she won—in the end, all the years she spent hating the supreme Celestial paid off.</p>
<p>They all gained enlightenment, bringing them closer to breaking their limits and raising their strengths.</p>
