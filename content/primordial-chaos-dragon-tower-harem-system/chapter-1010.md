<p>’Is it just me, or does it appear like there are more warriors than Alchemists?’ Kent used the Soul Map to send a telepathic message to Pill Monarch Nefeli.</p>
<p>She turned and looked at Kent, who gave her a smile.</p>
<p>’Every faction wants Primordial flames, so they groomed warriors into Alchemists and sent them to the Pill Ascension Realm specifically to fight for Primordial flames.</p>
<p>Be sure to avoid such battles and focus on techniques and herbs. Trust me, I have been there before and can testify that what makes that place extra dangerous is your fellow Alchemists.’</p>
<p>Pill Monarch Nefeli smiled to herself, knowing she suffered because she chose not to unleash her dark past. If she had exposed her true self that early back then, she would have been discovered by now.</p>
<p>’I see,’ Kent said, a smirk forming on his lips, ’They should avoid me then. If not, then only a handful will be returning with their heads attached to their bodies.’</p>
<p>Pill Monarch Nefeli shook her head but said nothing.</p>
<p>While Kent was a mere Core Formation cultivator, she had killed enough to recognize a killer when she saw one.</p>
<p>She can tell Kent is a killer...a Bloodthirsty predator.</p>
<p>But even if that didn’t confirm her suspicions that Kent was dangerous, the fact that he was able to make someone like her, who back home was feared by millions, shudder by merely locking a gaze with him was enough to tell her what she had to know.</p>
<p>’Ahh, maybe I should warn the disciples eyeing him to back off before they throw their lives away.’ She sighed and then focused on leading everyone to the Pill Platform, a sacred place in Solaryn.</p>
<p>After an hour, they came to a clearing where hundreds of people could be seen heading toward a tall platform.</p>
<p>From afar, using the Soul Map, Kent saw that the platform was approximately 700 meters in diameter, forming a circular shape.</p>
<p>It had ten sections reserved for the ten Pill Worlds. Kent scanned the ten sections and saw that most of the people present were Pill Emperors, with a few Pill Monarchs also in attendance.</p>
<p>However, the section reserved for the top Pill Alliance, specifically the Solaryn Pill Alliance, was packed with Pill Saints, Sages, Kings, and even five Pill Monarchs.</p>
<p>As he continued to look, he saw more people arriving, each taking their assigned stand within their respective factions.</p>
<p>Naturally, it would take them another hour to get there, but with his Soul Map, he was observing everything.</p>
<p>’Oh, it is going to be a bloodbath for sure. Just look at them, they can’t wait to gut one another.’</p>
<p>From a mere glance, Kent could tell what was running through their minds.</p>
<p>Their expressions said it all. They hated each other, and so they wanted to kill one another as early as possible, so they could feel good about themselves.</p>
<p>---</p>
<p>One hour later, they arrived and took their place in the section of the platform reserved for the Pyris Pill Alliance.</p>
<p>Kent finally used his own eyes to observe the settings of the platform.</p>
<p>’Looks like the gate opens at the center, and those nodes are probably reserved for pill duels.’ Kent identified a few more things.</p>
<p>’This place serves many purposes than merely housing the gate to the pill realm.’</p>
<p>In no time, the last person came.</p>
<p>Looking around, Kent spotted some high-profile Alchemists from the top three sects.</p>
<p>’Pill Legends, the pinnacle of Pill Making in a High World and beings capable of making pills that invoke both the judgment and blessing of the Pill Dao.’</p>
<p>Kent’s Alchemist heart compelled him to give these people the respect they deserved. To be able to make a pill worthy of the recognition of the Pill Dao was no easy feat.</p>
<p>To him, true Alchemy began at the point where pills concocted invoke heavenly tribulations. Those pills are True Pills recognised by the Heavenly Pill Dao.</p>
<p>So, looking at the three Pill Legends, standing with the Solaryn Pill Alliance, two with the Lunareth Pill Alliance, and one with the Thaloryn Pill Alliance, he could only give them the respect they deserved.</p>
<p>As for the Pill Sovereigns, even one was standing with the Pyris Pill Alliance alongside the seven Pill Monarchs.</p>
<p>Suddenly, a Pill Monarch from the 4th-ranked Pill Alliance looked in the direction of the Pyris Pill Alliance, specifically at Pill Monarch Nefeli, and smiled...</p>
<p>"Glad to see you are still as beautiful as ever, Pill Monarch Nefeli. Hope you’ve been well," Pill Monarch Jackson asked, his smile anything but good intentions.</p>
<p>However, Pill Monarch Nefeli merely glanced at him and scoffed, "I don’t have time for your nonsense, Jackson. If you have something to say, man up and say it."</p>
<p>The expression on Pill Monarch Jackson’s face faltered, but he recovered it to save face... though his tone when he spoke again contrasted with the cheerful one he opened with.</p>
<p>"Still cold as ice, I see. Makes me wonder how cold you will be if you were an Ice Elemental Human." He smiled, and a few others with nefarious intentions smiled with him...</p>
<p>"Words going around are that you have taken in a disciple who, at the Grandmaster stage, could concoct Tier 9 pills with a grey pill ring.</p>
<p>Care to introduce this talent to us and perhaps see if he can give us some pointers?"</p>
<p>The expression on Pill Monarch Nefeli’s face changed.</p>
<p>When Pill Monarch Jackson saw this, he smiled, knowing he had touched a nerve.</p>
<p>Of course he did.</p>
<p>After all, 75 years ago, Pill Monarch Nefeli, then a Pill Emperor, found a young, promising Alchemist, someone she recognized as a true genius in the art of pill making.</p>
<p>She was brilliant and had so many years ahead of her. However, when the Pill Ascension Realm opened and she entered, only her space ring came out.</p>
<p>This space ring was in the hands of a disciple of Pill Monarch Jackson.</p>
<p>Naturally, that said everything she needed to know.</p>
<p>It touched her heart, and for a moment, she nearly committed a massacre.</p>
<p>However, recalling all she had to sacrifice before finally finding some semblance of peace and normalcy, she held back.</p>
<p>However, up until today, she had never forgotten about Nancy, the young girl she had picked up from the street and planned to make her disciple when she reached the Pill King stage.</p>
<p>Of course, she planned to kill this bastard, his disciple, and everyone in their inner circle before leaving. But for now, she held back.</p>
<p>However, hearing him mention Kent brought back these suppressed memories.</p>
<p>Kent, who was standing beside Pill Monarch Nefeli, felt a rather dense killing intent that even he couldn’t exude come off her body.</p>
<p>However, she suppressed it the next second.</p>
<p>This made Kent realize she was angry and very emotional, so naturally, he had to do something.</p>
<p>He looked at Pill Monarch Jackson, and for a moment, he saw a killing intent flash in his eyes. However, what surprised him was the look in the eyes of the Pill Legends.</p>
<p>’They are behind this... all of them.’ Kent felt like vomiting when he saw their hidden expressions.</p>
<p>’To think I gave them the respect they deserve just a moment ago.’ His anger bubbled.</p>
<p>’Relax, big sister, I will handle it.’ He said to her, using the soul map.</p>
<p>Before she could stop him, Kent stepped forward and, using his left hand, pointed at Pill Monarch Jackson.</p>
<p>"You know, I have been around for only 21 years, but this is the first time seeing someone who doesn’t know they won’t be celebrating their next birthday."</p>
<p>Kent’s body vanished, and when he appeared, Pill Monarch Jackson’s head was off his body...</p>
