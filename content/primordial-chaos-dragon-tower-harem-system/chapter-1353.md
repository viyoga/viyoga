<p>[3 Days Ago - Planet Gladiator]</p>
<p>"Let go of me, you bastards." A young lady wiggled her arm, attempting to free herself from the grip of three burly men, too large and strong to call them dark elves.</p>
<p>But they are, in fact, Dark Elves.</p>
<p>As for the lady, she is slender and a bit weak, a unique trait of an Elf. However, she is in no way weaker than a Death Stage warrior. That is because she has so much influence and threat to many in her world.</p>
<p>She lives in a world of constant battle and bloodshed. From the day she was born – 1300 years ago – she had never known peace, not even for a moment.</p>
<p>If not running from one blade to another, then it is chasing after a threat to eliminate it. Her life had always been about battle.</p>
<p>From what she knew, long before she was born, Planet Gladiator was a peaceful world, ruled by a benevolent Elven Queen. However, when the Dark Elves arrived, everything turned upside down, plunging the peaceful world into chaos and constant battle.</p>
<p>The entire planet, with billions of people, was taken over. However, just like in any dictatorship, there are rebels. She was born into the family of rebels.</p>
<p>So from the start, she had already become an enemy of the Dark Elves. She had helped many escape their tyranny and achieve some form of normalcy.</p>
<p>However, a couple of days ago, she was betrayed by one of her lieutenants, leading to an ambush. She killed quite a lot of Dark Elves, but with no reinforcement coming, she was finally subdued.</p>
<p>"There is nowhere else to run, Rebel Dia. Your days of defiance have come to an end." One of the Dark Elves said before knocking her unconscious.</p>
<p>The three Dark Elves took a deep breath. Looking around, the bodies of dozens of dead Dark Elves could be seen.</p>
<p>It is clear they lost many men before finally subduing her.</p>
<p>"What a headache. I am going to enjoy torturing her after Lord Pilio is done with her."</p>
<p>"Same here. She has caused us to lose too many men." After making sure everything was in order, they left the scene and headed back to the city.</p>
