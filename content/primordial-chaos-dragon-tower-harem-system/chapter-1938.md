<p>The first person to die on Vexthra’s team was Silvernok, the Thorned Emperor. He is a member of the Forbidden Beast Legion, one of the dangerous ones who, despite being only at the Demigod stage, has killed dozens of Gods and even two Supremes.</p>
<p>His offensive power is on another level.</p>
<p>However, his defense was weak, and that was why he died.</p>
<p>He was shot down by an archer who is a Celestial. Silvernok tried all he could to defend against the arrow, but the power behind it was too much. So before he knew it, he was already on the ground.</p>
<p>However, before life could slip out of his lungs, the necklace around his neck activated, and he was teleported away, appearing inside a cocoon in the Legion Tower.</p>
<p>[Your teammate Silvernok is dead]</p>
<p>A message appeared before Vexthra, who had returned to the battlefield. Her eyes narrowed, but before she could make a sound, she sensed a killing intent lock onto her.</p>
<p>Then she felt her life threatened.</p>
<p>Turning toward the direction of the killing intent, she saw a bow aimed directly at her. The Celestial who killed Silvernok had turned her arrows toward Vexthra.</p>
<p>Before Vexthra could do anything, an arrow flew toward her at a deadly speed. She had no time to raise any defense, and considering a Celestial had fired the arrow, she felt her life slipping away even before the arrow arrived before her.</p>
<p>But just when the arrow was 30 meters away, a golden lightning bolt appeared before Vexthra and stood between her and the arrow.</p>
<p>BOOOM!</p>
<p>A powerful shockwave erupted, and Vexthra was pushed back. When she stabilised herself, she saw Kaizo standing tall, his right hand gripping the neck of the Pseudo-Primordial he was tasked to kill.</p>
<p>Now sticking out of the back of the dead Pseudo-Primordial was the arrow meant for Vexthra.</p>
<p>Kaizo tossed the dead Pseudo-Primordial and turned to look at Vexthra. "Let me take care of her."</p>
<p>Vexthra nodded, and Kaizo vanished from sight. The next moment, he appeared behind the Celestial who fired the arrow, his fist slamming into her back.</p>
<p>Her body was hurled through the air, slamming into a building 2 km away. Kaizo was right before her before she could even rise back up, his fist slamming into her face.</p>
<p>The impact broke her nose and mouth, causing blood to pour out like a fountain.</p>
<p>Kaizo didn’t allow her the chance to recover at all. His fist kept moving, every last one slamming into his target until, 10 seconds later, his last punch exploded her head open, blood spraying everywhere.</p>
<p>With his fist covered in blood, Kaizo moved to the next target, and soon, his presence could be felt all over the battlefield. Whenever he moved, golden lightning followed, unleashing chaos and mayhem on everyone and everywhere.</p>
<p>Two minutes after Silvernok died, a second casualty appeared, and soon a third appeared.</p>
<p>Vexthra noticed that as they drew closer to the 45-minute mark she had set, the enemy also seemed to gain the upper hand.</p>
<p>Still, with close to 200,000 already dead on the enemy side, she knew if they pushed forward just a little, they would be able to overcome the growing number of threats left for them to deal with.</p>
<p>Five minutes before the 45 minutes were up, and after ten casualties later, one of the battlefield cubes glowed, and Neveross appeared, holding the neck of Monarch Solran, who, against all odds, had died, but there was no blood or injury on his skin.</p>
<p>He still looks handsome and radiant, but he is dead. His soul had been wounded so much that he couldn’t keep himself alive anymore.</p>
<p>The moment the warriors of the Grim Arcane Legends saw this, tens of thousands lost their balance and fell, dying in the process since Vexthra and her team had no plans to spare even a single one of them.</p>
<p>"That’s right, peasants, one of your monarchs is dead, leaving you behind for far worst. So ask yourselves, if he fell to a Pseudo-Primordial like me, what makes you think you guys stand a chance at all?"</p>
<p>Neveross turned toward Veera and tossed the body of the now-dead Monarch Solran toward her.</p>
<p>Veera smiled and stored away the body, which, if she played her cards right, would awaken to become a powerful Pseudo-Primordial undead.</p>
<p>"Now, allow me to harvest your souls." The soul cauldron appeared, and thousands of souls poured out. Soon, screams and anguish followed.</p>
<p>Neveross held a smile on his face as the souls he had harvested, refined, and branded started killing for him.</p>
<p>Yukiko, who had been relieved of her duties inside the hair domain, appeared beside Neveross. "You sure have become much stronger than I remember."</p>
<p>Neveross turned to her and smiled. "I ranked 5th during the Chaos Era. This era, I want to rank 3rd or maybe 2nd."</p>
<p>"Why not first?" Yukiko asked, but Neveross just smirked.</p>
<p>"The only person who can stand against tha 13-headed bastard is the master himself. Aside from him, I don’t see anyone, not even that fiend who nearly became the 14th Divine wonder, could compete against him."</p>
<p>Yukiko nodded, for she knew what Neveross said was indeed true. All 13 Divine Wonders have their own fields of expertise. So they are all special in their own way.</p>
<p>However, the strongest of them, the Thirteen-Headed Calamity Dragon, or as they mostly call him, the Scourge of Chaos, is a terror none could compare to.</p>
<p>"But who knows, if I keep collecting and refining souls, maybe one day I will surpass him. That said, I can feel you have suppressed your power. Is there any reason for that?" Neveross asked, but Yukiko just smirked.</p>
<p>"Master placed three seals on me before he died. He didn’t tell me why, but I know it is for my own good, so we can only wait for him to come and remove them."</p>
<p>Neveross had nothing to say about that. In the past, the Divine Beast Tamer placed seals on the Thirteen-Headed Calamity Dragon just to mask his power a bit in order not to draw too much attention.</p>
<p>If he was restricting Yukiko’s powers, then maybe he had his reasons.</p>
<p>"Talk to you later, Neveross. We have a job to do."</p>
<p>In no time, the 45 minutes Vexthra had designated had passed, but they were not yet ready to leave. By now, 15 casualties had appeared on her side, and the 16th was one of her sisters.</p>
