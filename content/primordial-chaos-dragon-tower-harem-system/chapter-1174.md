<p>Obviously, being asked out of the blue whether they wanted to become Kent’s wives was unexpected and shocking.</p>
<p>That is rather true for the cat.</p>

<p>Princess Arien already told her father and mother, and they approved, too. They know Kent more.</p>
<p>However, for Indie, it was through Arien that she met Kent’s wives. She had spent some time with them and got to like their way of life.</p>

<p>She fell in love with that lifestyle and wanted to be a part of it.</p>
<p>The only problem, however, was that she had never met Kent before. She knew nothing about him. Yet, that didn’t stop her from falling in love with him.</p>
<p>Now, she had finally met him, and while it was brief, she was captivated by his good looks and strength.</p>

<p>Now, she saw the one in charge of it all, and while his potential is nowhere near Vexthra’s, she was able to see the real Kent, and that turned her on.</p>
<p>Clearly, she fell in love with Kent’s strength, something she never knew was possible. Now, she had been offered the chance to become his forever.</p>
<p>However, it felt too sudden.</p>
<p>She turned to her friend Arien, and from the look on her face, she too felt it was sudden. Suddenly, they turned to the one who knew Kent better...</p>
<p>Lady Liora turned and looked at them too before shifting her gaze to the glass lady... Enzi.</p>

<p>Selene smiled and moved closer to the three ladies. She placed her hand on Indie and Arien’s shoulders.</p>
<p>Every last one of her sisters expected her to say something shameless. However, when she started to speak, they all turned quiet...</p>
<p>"I never told anyone this before. However, when I was 11 years old, I witnessed a man assault my big sister to death."</p>
<p>Selene turned and looked at Neomi, who seemed lost.</p>
<p>She then chose to speak to her, so while holding Indie and Arien, she started to reveal her dark past...</p>
<p>"My father was one shameless bastard who couldn’t stick to one wife. He used them and then dumped them. My mom was one of his many victims.</p>
<p>He started out as a loving father and supportive husband; however, once he had gotten what he wanted from you, he would dump you and throw you aside.</p>
<p>My mother gave birth to two of us. My sister was 4 years older than me. She was my best friend and pillar of support.</p>
<p>Back then, when my father offended some powerful cultivators, he chose to abandon us and run, saving his useless life.</p>
<p>One night, out of nowhere, the cultivators broke into our home and killed everyone. They spared no one. The men were instantly killed, including little boys, and the ladies were assaulted to death.</p>

<p>Tears fell from her eyes, but she wasn’t the only one; her sisters were also crying.</p>
<p>"I’ve always believed in love and happiness, but that one gory night changed everything. My sister saved me, but at the cost of her life.</p>

<p>I would be lying if I said we became friends and eventually sisters because she was a nice person. I became her friend due to our shared bitterness, and it was that bitterness that kept us going.</p>
<p>We killed all those who caused us pain... I hunted down my own father and killed him, hoping that would change anything, but the trauma seemed to have rooted its way into my heart by then. I was a lost cause, as most would say."</p>
<p>Selene let a painful smile appear on her face.</p>
<p>"You know, my big sister and I used to fantasize about our future back then. We both wanted to become wives of wealthy young heirs and, using our husbands’ money, open an organization where we would use any means necessary to punish unfaithful husbands."</p>
<p>Selene chuckled, and her sisters also smiled, wiping their tears.</p>
<p>"Thinking about it now, Atina should have stopped me from coming up with that silly idea."</p>

<p>"The point of all this was... I had given up on love... Shit, I hated seeing a man and a woman happy together. That was how bitter I was, and for a while, I lived it.</p>
<p>However, everything changed when I met Kent, and through his shamelessness and perhaps a moment of hesitation, I fell for him.</p>
<p>Because of him, I have known what love is. Because of these amazing ladies, I have regained my happiness again.</p>
<p>So becoming his wives is probably the best decision you will ever make."</p>
<p>An evil smile then crossed Selene’s face, prompting her sisters to welcome the real Selene back...</p>
