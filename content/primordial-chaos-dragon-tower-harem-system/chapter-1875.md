<p>"So, where have you been, and what have you been up to while I was gone?" Kent asked, walking to a nearby cliff.</p>
<p>Mjolnir followed him and stood beside him, his arms crossed over his chest.</p>
<p>"When you gave me your soul, allowing me to awaken, I became the temporary owner of your personal world. So I returned here after the war, for a few thousand years, to cultivate and better understand my powers and nature.</p>
<p>Afterward, I started travelling the universe, learning the way of life wherever I found myself. Luckily for me, after travelling for a while, I managed to become a Primordial. I never expected it, but I knew one day I would become one.</p>
<p>So when I did, I ventured into the Universe Ocean to explore some more and gather resources for when you returned. I returned a hundred thousand years ago, and ever since then, I have been looking for signs of your return.</p>
<p>It took some time, but I did when you released a bit of the Nine Underworld Flame Blacksmith Flame Qi a couple of months back. Now, here I am and, well, glad to see you again."</p>
<p>Kent nodded and smiled.</p>
<p>"Looks like you hadn’t lived a boring life at all." Venturing into Primordial Chaos sure showed he had seen his fair share of things and experienced various events.</p>
<p>He is a Primordial, but with a much stronger foundation. On his own, he is a menace, and in Kent’s hands, who knows what kind of carnage they would cause?</p>
<p>Maybe Kent would even have an easy time navigating the Primordial Chaos one day.</p>
<p>"So what are your plans now?" Kent asked again.</p>
<p>Considering Mjolnir is a Primordial and also a living weapon, his strongest living weapon thus far, telling him to become his go-to weapon would be akin to looking down on him.</p>
<p>He can be used against Celestials, but he would have to suppress his own power to the Celestial stage since using Primordial Qi against Celestials would invoke the wrath of the heavens.</p>
<p>Basically, using him would violate so many rules...</p>
<p>Maybe some of those rules would not invoke the wrath of the heavens, but they would certainly go against Kent’s own pride as a cultivator and a warrior.</p>
<p>Regardless, Mjolnir is a weapon, and he must be wielded as such...</p>
<p>But as to how she wished to be handled, Kent wanted to find out from him instead.</p>
<p>Mjolnir turned to look at Kent and said, "I know the kind of person you are, so I know you will never wield me in a battle against Celestials and below. This is why I will not expect you to ask me to follow you now.</p>
<p>However, I also cannot just leave and wander on my own, so why don’t we go back to how things used to be, when you would forge weapons that defied common sense, and I will ride there in your grip, shaping them?" He smiled slyly.</p>
<p>"I can sense the aura of the Nine Dragon Whip on you, which means you have already reunited with them. The last time we forged them, we used primitive materials.</p>
<p>Luckily for you, I brought back thousands of minerals from my time in Primordial Chaos so we can work on turning the Nine Dragon Whip into a living weapon.</p>
<p>At least, that will be a challenge, right? You have never forged a living weapon outright, so take this as your first trial. Hopefully, you will either excel or fail.</p>
<p>Regardless, I can be your forging hammer for now until you are ready to venture into battles that require a more dreadful weapon."</p>
<p>"That works for me. I indeed have so many weapons to forge, and in case you are not aware, there are dangers everywhere, so I might be needing you as my war hammer sooner than you think."</p>
<p>Mjolnir nodded, feeling relieved.</p>
<p>He may have tried to play stubborn, but he really missed Kent. Not that he would admit it. But as the simple bone, crudely shaped into a hammer by a seven-year-old, showed, their bond runs deep.</p>
<p>Kent turned slightly and looked at Mjolnir, who was staring ahead, his expression calm.</p>
<p>"In your travels, did you happen to go to Hell?"</p>
<p>"I did. It was where I went first right after I left this world."</p>
<p>"Then what happened to the Hell Devastators after I died?" Kent clearly had no idea that one of the Hell Devastators was currently inside the Tower...</p>
<p>Since he hadn’t been to the Legion Tower since he woke up, considering most of the time he had spent with Safira was in the Tower City, he had yet to meet Ruby.</p>
<p>"Out of the ninety-six Hell Devastators, thirty-six perished during the Flame War. The remaining sixty, half of them returned to Hell and had been exploring the Hell Calamity Zones.</p>
<p>The other thirty, from what I could find out, had also ventured into all kinds of calamity zones this universe had to offer. Some even ventured into the Abyssal Chaos and the Forsaken Seas.</p>
<p>Honestly, they seem to have a thing for dangerous zones, so I wouldn’t be surprised if the next time you see them, they are monsters or perhaps dead."</p>
<p>Kent smirked and looked down the cliff, staring deep into the scorching sea of lava.</p>
<p>He laughed. "I picked them as my legion for a reason, Mjolnir. Every last one of them is crazy in their own way. So rest assured, even if they were to meet the most dreadful enemy, I wouldn’t be too worried about their safety, because they will survive somehow."</p>
<p>Kent sounded confident, which showed he indeed had that much confidence in the Hell Devastators. But then he turned around and spread his arms.</p>
<p>"Enough about the Hell Devastators. How about we go see our old friend we had chained down there? I am sure she will be happy to see us again... or not."</p>
<p>He fell backward, plunging headfirst into the lava below.</p>
<p>Mjolnir watched Kent falling into the lava, and a sigh escaped his lips.</p>
<p>"Sometimes I forget he has been alot more crazy than anyone I’ve ever met since he was seven years old."</p>
<p>He chuckled, then jumped down as well, following closely behind Kent.</p>
<p>After a few seconds of falling, they plunged into the lava pool and vanished. The ripples they left behind lasted only a moment before calm returned to the surface.</p>
<p>Meanwhile, in the scorching depths of the lava pool, a woman of flawless beauty and long gray hair slowly opened her eyes. Chains rattled as she turned her head to look up.</p>
