<p>In no time, the Sky Foxes settled in their new home.</p>
<p>The Humans, Bloodvein, and Elves went over to welcome them as they did for each other. The humans were the first, and Kent’s wives welcomed them. Then they welcome the Blood veins, and together, they welcome the Elves.</p>
<p>Now, it was the turn of the Sky Foxes.</p>
<p>Of course, they seemed startled when they saw them, especially the Humans. However, learning that their Princess made this happen, they knew they had nothing to worry about.</p>
<p>In the end, not all Humans are the same.</p>
<p>Truthfully, after just a day together, they started to bond with them.</p>
<p>Kent seemed pleased with this development.</p>
<p>Later, he left his women there, knowing they would love to stay and make sure everything was handled well.</p>
<p>In the end, they would only be adding to his debt. However, whatever makes them happy is fine with him. He is about to embark on a dangerous mission, and so would they.</p>
<p>They will be challenging the 5th floor of the planet-killer dungeon, so more tower points to come.</p>
<p>Vexthra told them she would return early, so in three days they would venture into the dungeon yet again.</p>
<p>This means, regardless of how much they spend, they soon will be paying for it, and hopefully, this time, Kent will never go into debt ever again.</p>
<p>But before that, he had something else to handle.</p>
<p>"Tower, have you looked into the Supreme Sky Fox Inheritance inside Liora’s soul?"</p>
<p>After learning that Liora’s mother died from an inheritance from a powerful Sky Fox beast and that she was killed after giving the inheritance to Liora, Kent told the Tower to look into it.</p>
<p>Liora no longer needs such an inheritance, since she now has a True Name. So if he can extract it and give it to her people, that would be for the best.</p>
<p>For now, he had nothing he could do to help the Sky Foxes become powerful like the other races. He is limited in that knowledge.</p>
<p>However, if this inheritance is good, then maybe once one of them inherits it, they can unlock the secret of their bloodline and help the others.</p>
<p>[I did, Master, and it is as you anticipated. It is indeed a Supreme Grade inheritance that goes above and beyond to unleash the hidden powers of the Sky Fox race.]</p>
<p>"Interesting. Do you have any details on who this inheritance came from... like a name or title?"</p>
<p>[As a matter of fact, I do. From what I found on her, she is known as Rael, the Sky Fox Sovereign. The only account on her was that she lived during the Chaos Era and was among the ten legendary Primordial Beasts who followed the Divine Beast Tamer in that era</p>
<p>From her account and the others’, they were the sole reason the Chaos War ended in favour of the universe.</p>
<p>However, I know nothing of her powers and traits. But I do know she was a legendary being, and contained in this inheritance is all the Sky Fox race needs to evolve and unlock their hidden powers.]</p>
<p>"So their inheritance isn’t for just one person. It was meant to be used by all the Sky Fox."</p>
<p>[Yes, Master. Once extracted, Cosmic Gaia can build an ancestral site for them to inherit the knowledge and hopefully, one day, legends could rise from their ranks.]</p>
<p>"I see. That does make some sense. However, Gaia is probably going to have a field day with this." Kent smiled, feeling yet another headache coming.</p>
<p>Gaia is going to charge him a fortune for this.</p>
<p>Of course, thinking about the Six Fangs Teleportation Portal Formation she would also have to work on, Kent knew he would have to work extra hard if he wanted to never go into debt again.</p>
<p>But then again, cutting the time it will take him to go from his current position to the Lanster Barbarian Star System to just 5 years and a few weeks is worth any amount.</p>
<p>Also, if he gets to make the Sky Fox stronger, then one day, not only would he be getting Soul Essence from the many worlds he is connected to, but even gods and immortals will be his source of strength in both Soul Essence and divine faith energy.</p>
<p>"I need to talk to Gaia yet again," Kent said, already aware of the joy this will bring to Gaia.</p>
<p>Building the formation will take 5 years (outside time). That would be 120 years inside the tower. So it is going to cost a lot, and Kent can already see that.</p>
<p>But instead of spending 25 years moving to the edge of this galaxy, it will only take him approximately 10 years and loads of tower points.</p>
<p>That is better than spending 15 years more on the road.</p>
<p>Kent teleported to the Mountain Garden, where he knew Gaia and her sisters would be spending time with Uncle Drew and the demigods.</p>
<p>They jumped on him the moment he appeared. It had only been a day since they last saw him, but that didn’t stop them from welcoming him enthusiastically.</p>
<p>After spending a few minutes hugging their tiny bodies and making sure they are fine, he pulled Gaia to the side for some serious talk.</p>
<p>"I know you are probably going to act shamelessly and overcharge me, but take a look at these two things and tell me how much it would cost you and how long it would take to build them."</p>
<p>Kent smirked, feeling annoyed. However, in the entire universe, there is no better builder than Gaia, so he gets what he gets.</p>
<p>Gaia smiled and started examining the blueprint for the Six Fangs Teleportation Portal Formation and the Supreme Sky Fox Inheritance. After a couple of minutes, she was done, a smile brightening her face.</p>
<p>"It is going to cost you, Big Brother."</p>
<p>"Just tell me and stop throwing dust in my eyes."</p>
<p>"As you wish." She started with the inheritance.</p>
<p>"Building an inheritance shrine for a Supreme Inheritance is not an easy thing. A lot of weaving will go into it, so I will need my sisters’ help, which means it will cost you even more.</p>
<p>However, with their help, we would need just 100 days to complete it, with a budget of 3.5 billion tower points. This covers our overtime and health insurance, not forgetting accident fees."</p>
<p>Kent could only stare at the shameless, tiny bandit hovering in the air before him. The need to grab and squeeze her even crossed his mind, but he just sighed.</p>
<p>"What about the Six Fangs Teleportation Portal Formation?"</p>
<p>Gaia smiled... "Well, as long as the master brings the fangs of the six beasts, with as few as 30 billion tower points and dedication from my team and me, we can have it working in as little as 80 years.</p>
<p>The initial estimation should have been 120 years. But with enough motivation, 80 years is enough."</p>
<p>That would be 1.7 years taken off. However, that would also mean he must farm more tower points.</p>
<p>"Any discount?" Kent asked. However, Gaia shook her head, but then she smiled... "If the master promises to do wrestling with us every ten years, then we can take 5 billion tower points off it."</p>
<p>"Deal."</p>
<p>Gaia jumped into his arms with a giggle.</p>
<p>Kent just let her have this moment.</p>
<p>One day, he is going to get his revenge...</p>
