<p>It takes one with both confidence and absolute strength, and some form of craziness, to willingly walk into a trap, knowing the outcome can’t be determined based on assumption.</p>
<p>A preplanned trap always has layers of reinforcement to ensure it captures the one they want.</p>
<p>Walking into the teleportation gate, Kent knew there was danger waiting for him on the other side.</p>
<p>However, inasmuch as he would like to turn and leave, learning that the one involved is a forsaken seer, his anger had clouded his judgment.</p>
<p>The endgame is always to uproot the forsaken race from the face of the universe.</p>
<p>To many, the forsaken race had been sealed away. However, to Kent, he knew one day, they would come, and many would die as before.</p>
<p>So to prevent that, he wouldn’t let any opportunity to kill, even if a dozen of them passed him by. He is too committed to let such a chance slip away.</p>
<p>Hatred and heartbreak have a way of making even the best of society extremely bitter.</p>
<p>In the end, Kent walked through the teleportation gate only to appear at the centre of a killing formation created by thousands of dragons.</p>
<p>"Hmmm"</p>
<p>Kent made a low hum, slowly lifting his eye to see what danger he had stepped into.</p>
<p>His gaze met that of a pale-looking lady with white horns and a thin ring of Immortal Qi moving around her. She has a slender physique, with little to see on her chest and back.</p>
<p>However, the look in her eyes – piercing and scrutinising – told Kent that he had indeed stepped into a wild one.</p>
<p>’A Root Immortal,’ Kent made no facial expression. However, deep down, his heart was beating wildly. ’Oh, this is going to be fun.’</p>
<p>[How is this going to be fun, master? From what I see, we have a peak Root Immortal, who is a forsaken being. 4 high-level Root Immortals, also forsaken beings.</p>
<p>200 Death stage, 1200 Life stage, 20,000 Empyreans, and over 400,000 Heavenly Ascension stage cultivators with nothing but murder in their eyes.</p>
<p>I may not be a person, but there is no way this is going to be fun at all.]</p>
<p>Kent smiled and shook his head, ’Maybe to them. However, from where I stand, I see billions of Tower Points, Blood Essence, and Soul Spirits begging me to harvest them.</p>
<p>If that is not going to be fun, then I don’t know what would.’</p>
<p>[You are crazy.]</p>
<p>’No, I am not. My mom had me tested.’</p>
<p>Kent chuckled.</p>
<p>There are only 24 forsaken beings. Five of them are Immortals, and the rest are dragons with forsaken blood in them.</p>
<p>This allowed them to bypass the limit of high worlds, allowing most of them to reach the Death stage. Kent had already encountered someone before with a similar arrangement.</p>
<p>However, unlike him, who failed to complete the merger of the two bloodlines, the dragons seem to have achieved that, lifting the planet restriction off the cultivation.</p>
<p>However, Kent knew, deep down, that things aren’t as simple as they appear. Even so, he let the moment become funny to him.</p>
<p>He didn’t expect dragons to go into bed with the forsaken race... not when their ancestor, the Chaos Dragon, had killed billions of them.</p>
<p>"Are you having your last smile before a Forsaken King takes over your body?" Suddenly, the pale-looking lady whom Kent knew to be the forsaken seer spoke, a wave of Immortal Qi mixed with forsaken qi pouring from her body.</p>
<p>Kent stopped smiling and focused his attention on her. "And you are—"</p>
<p>The pale lady turned ashen, her expression switching between anger and embarrassment.</p>
<p>"Insolent." Suddenly, a death stage forsaken being lunged at Kent, a spear aimed for his chest, waves of forsaken qi emitting from it.</p>
<p>"Tch," Kent chuckled and raised his right index finger. Waves of ice condensed at the tip, causing the space around him to turn cold. Then, with a sudden command, he shot a thin beam of ice.</p>
<p>"Begone."</p>
<p>The ice beam moved faster than the wind and drew a thin hole through his forehead. No blood came out, just the sudden and instant snuffing of life that the forsaken man used to hold dear to his chest.</p>
<p>His body stopped midair and fell, slamming to the ground with a loud thud.</p>
<p>’Although I hate you bastards, you sure have potent blood essence.’ Kent chuckled, feeling the potent energy wash into his [Blood Core.]</p>
<p>He raised his head and looked at the forsaken seer. "Well, care to tell me who you are and why you placed a curse on the mother of my little friend? Also, while you’re at it, care to tell me where the four you took are?"</p>
<p>The ashen expression on her face turned cold. Then she lifted her staff and, with a cold tone, spoke... "I, Atarah, would make sure that before your body is taken away, you would suffer."</p>
<p>"Looks like we share the same thought. However, while killing you all 24 will give me pleasure, the rest of you... as a fellow dragon, I am very disappointed in you.</p>
<p>Where is your pride as a dragon? Bending over to let the forsaken race fuck you is the last thing I ever expected from you lot."</p>
<p>The thousands of dragons roared, releasing powerful dragon authority, all aimed at Kent.</p>
<p>"Oh, please, stop tickling me."</p>
<p>Kent extended his hands, and they turned into razor-sharp claws ending in piercing talons.</p>
<p>"KILL HIM BUT KEEP HIS BODY INTACT!" Atarah commanded, and Kent felt the weight of attacks come from all directions.</p>
<p>The first barrage of attacks slammed toward him like a collapsing star. He, a lone dragon, stood under a devastating barrage of attacks, all coming from beings that could flatten any High World.</p>
<p>’Tower, say roughly, how much in Tower Points would I be earning after this slaughter?’ Instead of fear, Kent chose to calculate his gains.</p>
<p>He is facing five Immortals and hundreds of Death-stage cultivators, after all, not to mention the thousands of others.</p>
<p>[The master can rest assured that he will be paying off his debt if he lives to tell the tale.]</p>
<p>’Ah, lucky me.’</p>
<p>Waves of deadly dragon breath, Immortal Qi blasts, and chaotic flames blasted toward him, tearing the air like it was made of paper.</p>
<p>The world trembled for miles, space vibrating violently under the crushing force directed at him.</p>
<p>Kent didn’t move.</p>
<p>Instead, he simply narrowed his eyes at the approaching danger... and the temperature around him plummeted.</p>
<p>A dome of pale-blue frost erupted from beneath his feet, expanding outward like a blooming flower. It expanded large enough before coming to a halt.</p>
<p>Every attack that entered the frost domain slowed, thickened, and then froze mid-flight.</p>
<p>Dragon flames turned to ice sculptures, Immortal Qi beams shattered like they were mere brittle glass, and physical strikes stopped completely as though the world itself paused.</p>
<p>"I love ice." Kent grinned.</p>
<p>With a flick of his wrist, the frozen barrage exploded into powdery dust.</p>
<p>ROAR</p>
<p>A chaotic roar escaped his mouth, causing the hundreds of thousands of dragons to feel their bodies tense.</p>
<p>"Let the killing begin." Kent locked his eyes on two of the Root Immortals.</p>
<p>His claws glimmered coldly, then, tapping into the soul map’s scanning range, he vanished and reappeared behind one of them.</p>
