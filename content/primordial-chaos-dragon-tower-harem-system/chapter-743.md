<p>"I will take the one wielding the axe; you can have the other," Kaizo said to Unity, who smiled back at him as the two soldiers appeared.</p>
<p>They were both clad in dark armor, but one wielded a spear and the other an axe. Naturally, Kaizo was after the one wielding the axe because he knew they were both brutes.</p>
<p>"Fine," Unity said, knowing that if she were to become unruly this time, Kaizo would actually lose his mind. So she took the one wielding the spear and started firing arrows at him.</p>
<p>Kaizo charged at the one wielding the axe, unleashing a powerful punch that created a sonic boom in his wake, shaking the very ground before landing on the axe of the brute general.</p>
<p>He crossed his arms over his chest just as Kaizo’s fist landed. It created a powerful impact, one that sent the general skidding back.</p>
<p>However, instead of flying away, he came to a halt after moving back three meters. Kaizo grinned.</p>
<p>"Looks like this one is much more powerful than the Spirit Ascension beasts on the first floor."</p>
<p>Unity had the same thought when she saw how the spear general was evading and blocking her arrows. Every shot was blocked or evaded.</p>
<p>However, instead of feeling afraid or angry, Unity felt some excitement, just like Kaizo, who also felt this would be a good warm-up.</p>
<p>So they started getting fiercer.</p>
<p>Kaizo began moving much faster, punching with greater force. In response, the general launched an even more aggressive attack and blockade. However, from a distance, Vexthra saw that it was only a matter of time before the two generals met their end.</p>
<p>She saw Kaizo wasn’t even trying.</p>
<p>His strength was robust, and his foundation was firm, so facing someone who hadn’t even had a chance to land an attack on him yet said it all.</p>
<p>Of course, it wasn’t like Kent hadn’t made sure of that.</p>
<p>He ensured that the materials used to upgrade his undead were of the highest value. This way, after their upgrade, not only would their foundations be untouched, but when it came down to sheer strength, they would be unlike anything else.</p>
<p>Kaizo, Vaelros, Zareth, and Sylara were all impressive in their own right.</p>
<p>So Kaizo wouldn’t even face any threat if dozens of these generals attacked him. He slowly kept pushing the general back.</p>
<p>Unity was the same.</p>
<p>Meanwhile, with people like Neomi, Velyra, and the rest handling the main soldiers, the battle was almost over.</p>
<p>Unity toyed with the general for a couple of minutes before taking out one of the arrows Alina had modified with her arrays. The arrows were already deadly, but with Alina’s modification, they became something else.</p>
<p>She activated no skill or bow enhancement. However, when the arrow entered her bow and she pulled on the bowstring, she felt something that caused her to look back at Alina, who was grinning at her.</p>
<p>Unity wondered what Alina was doing when she added these arrays to the arrows.</p>
<p>However, instead of dwelling on that, she released the bowstring, causing the arrow to flash out of her bow, appearing behind the head of the general.</p>
<p>The arrow went through his head before he even realized it.</p>
<p>It was instantaneous.</p>
<p>At the same time, Kaizo also had had enough. He channeled lightning into his palm and used a movement skill that let him get closer to the general before landing a palm strike on his chest.</p>
<p>The lightning Kaizo gathered on his palm exploded, rupturing the organs in the general’s body.</p>
<p>It would have been agonizing if Kaizo hadn’t done it right, but his [Divine Brute Palm Strike] skill is meant to kill instantly.</p>
<p>Just one clean attack and all organs will be destroyed. So Kaizo used it and the general couldn’t block it...</p>
<p>Right when the second general appeared, the floor boss came, looking imposing.</p>
<p>He held a club in his hands, stood almost three meters tall, and was clad in an even darker shade of metallic armor.</p>
<p>He had a red eye patch, dark hair, and the aura emanating from him was peak Ascendant, but stronger than the two Kaizo and Unity had killed.</p>
<p>"Let’s go," Velyra said, and Princess Lovina nodded. They moved, and soon they were closer to the red-patch general.</p>
<p>Princess Lovina formed an orb in her palm and threw it at the red-patch general, who swung his club, shattering the orb instantly.</p>
<p>It was one quick swing that shattered the orb, but it was his fault for choosing to smash it.</p>
<p>The moment he did, he felt it—a fiery burn, one that couldn’t be ignored—enter his body, burning deep within.</p>
<p>His skin remained unchanged; there was no external burn whatsoever. However, on the inside, the burn was too much, causing the general to drop his club and start screaming.</p>
<p>"Did I go too far?" Princess Lovina asked Velyra, who shook her head, smiling. Anything that is poisonous is considered excessive.</p>
<p>Velyra wanted to start the torture early, so she moved, executing a series of sword swings that immediately landed on the general, drawing blood.</p>
<p>Velyra moved back and looked at Lovina. "Handle the rest. And make sure he suffers like big sister said."</p>
<p>Lovina nodded and extended her palm.</p>
<p>Thousands of orbs appeared and then transformed into green wasps. They moved through the cut marks Velyra had created and entered the general’s body, starting to eat him from the inside.</p>
<p>His painful cries were so loud that Veera, the dreadful necromancer, who was planning on taking this floor boss as her undead, changed her mind.</p>
<p>"He will be too traumatized even in death, so he is worthless."</p>
<p>And she wasn’t wrong; what was happening to the Red Eye Patch General wasn’t simple. If anything, it was just too evil.</p>
<p>Velyra created the cut marks, drawing blood, so she could use her blood manipulation skills to keep healing the general, while Lovina used the poison wasps to torment him from the inside.</p>
<p>If it were only Princess Lovina, the general would have died within a few seconds, but thanks to Velyra, he had been screaming for ten minutes already.</p>
<p>After a while, Vexthra told them to end it.</p>
<p>Velyra deactivated her skill, and Lovina snapped her fingers, turning the general into a husk. His body deflated, meaning the wasps had consumed every last organ in his body.</p>
<p>It was a horrifying sight, but the ladies and the legion didn’t seem to care.</p>
<p>Vexthra received the Quest completed notice, making her smile. The quest ended faster than she had expected.</p>
<p>They quickly began searching for new clues while Vexthra reported to Kent, who was riding a carriage with the eight half-elves, en route to the Flame Valley.</p>
