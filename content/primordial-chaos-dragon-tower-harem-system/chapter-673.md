<p>"Master, do you want to exchange a few punches with me?" Kaizo asked with a grin. The fact that he had become powerful was a key reason why he wanted to meet Kent in a punch-out.</p>
<p>This was his way of showing his master that he had become stronger and that he was now ready to go on missions. Of course, he had always wanted to be on missions.</p>
<p>Kent looked at Kaizo for a few minutes and then scanned him...</p>
<p>⟦Name: Kaizo⟧</p>
<p>⟦Bloodline: True Divine Angel (25%)⟧</p>
<p>⟦Realm: Core Formation (Peak)⟧</p>
<p>⟦True Realm: Spirit King⟧</p>
<p>⟦Body Cultivation: Diamond⟧</p>
<p>⟦Rank: SSS+⟧</p>
<p>⟦Job Class: Divine Thunderborn Berserker⟧</p>
<p>’So his true realm after the upgrade was actually at the spirit King stage, but has been suppressed. I guess using high-quality bodies to upgrade him wasn’t so bad. This will give him a head start when I ascend to a higher world.’</p>
<p>Kent smiled and then stood up. "I'm sure, Kaizo, you will be a good sport."</p>
<p>Kaizo grinned and then moved, appearing on one of the many training grounds in the legion tower. Kent also moved and stood opposite him.</p>
<p>The distance between them was not that wide, showing that the clash would be instantaneous. Of course, it would be a spectacle, considering they wouldn’t be using their weapons, only their fists.</p>
<p>...Well, Kaizo’s fist was his weapon... But Kent wouldn’t be using his sword or dagger, considering he had fallen in love with daggers after recalling Faceless Memories.</p>
<p>He stood and locked gazes with the grinning Kaizo, who already had golden lightning dancing across his body.</p>
<p>Around the training ground, the legion moved to watch what was about to happen. Nathan, now known as Krex, was among them.</p>
<p>Over the past years, he had spent every single second trying to come to terms with his role as Kent’s servant. He struggled to come to terms with that.</p>
<p>At the end of every pondering, he only arrives at a singular answer, which is to engage in a battle with Kent, who would have to prove himself worthy to be called his master.</p>
<p>So now, he wanted to see what Kent was capable of against Kaizo, the third strongest among them. Kent had yet to add him to a legion simply because he didn’t know which one of them to add him to.</p>
<p>The undead are basically undead beings, so it wasn’t like he could join them. As for the Night Riders, he also didn’t qualify to join them.</p>
<p>The Eternal Wrath has its unique origins, which are from the last floor of every dungeon. The Soul Sentinels also have their lineage, so he could only stand on the sidelines and, perhaps soon enough, his legion name would be made known.</p>
<p>But for now, he just wanted to see Kent’s prowess.</p>
<p>"Who do you think will win?" Ankaara, the ice elemental, asked.</p>
<p>"Of course, Kaizo will win. He has basically become a menace since his upgrade, and now that he has gained a true name, I can’t wait to see how he trash Master—"</p>
<p>Gorzak, who was the first to fall after Kaizo’s upgrade, said with a booming smile. The others smiled, watching this. The Barbarian King just seemed too attached to Kaizo after his defeat.</p>
<p>"I say Master has this in the bag," Aetheris said, looking at Kent’s small figure standing against Kaizo’s bigger frame.</p>
<p>"I think Master will win too," Skoggr said, looking curiously at Kent. In fact, this was their first time seeing Kent about to clash with someone after he single-handedly killed seven Spirit Kings...</p>
<p>Aside Aetheris, the rest didn’t see him do that...so they are actually looking forward to this fight...</p>
<p>"Are you ready?" Kent asked.</p>
<p>"Born ready," Kaizo responded... "Created ready, I mean."</p>
<p>With that, they both moved, and their fists met in a resounding clash. Lightning burst, filling the place. Kaizo uses lightning, and so does his master, Kent, so it was a thunderous boom that shook the training ground.</p>
<p>Kaizo’s golden lightning is powerful. However, Kent’s doesn’t pale in comparison..</p>
<p>Strangely, after he went through Chaos’ memories, his lightning was slowly changing from golden to red.</p>
<p>Kaizo struck yet again, his fist blazing with thunder as he launched a lightning-infused jab. Kent barely dodged the strike, which whipped past his cheek and tore a small crater into the training ground. The ground trembled beneath their feet.</p>
<p>"That was close," Kent smiled, watching the ground crack open wide. The force behind the punch was enough to snuff a Sovereign off their mortal coil.</p>
<p>But Kent just smiled...</p>
<p>Kent countered immediately, closing the gap with a flurry of punches. Each blow was precise, his fists moving with a speed that blurred them into streaks of motion.</p>
<p>Kaizo blocked and parried, his own fists radiating thunderous energy that cracked the air. He was an expert in hand-to-hand combat after all...so this was his field.</p>
<p>However, Kent was once known as Chaos, one of the strongest brutes to have ever lived. He was also proficient in hand-to-hand combat using his fist. Countering Kaizo’s punch was merely child’s play for him.</p>
<p>The clash of fists sent shockwaves rippling through the legion tower. Every strike resonated like a hammer on steel. Dust and small stones exploded outward from where their fists collided.</p>
<p>However, whenever the ground was obliterated, it promptly healed itself. Also, while the punches could accidentally kill them, the tower was a place you couldn’t just die, even if you wanted to.</p>
<p>So the two were not fearing for their lives as they threw punches at each other.</p>
<p>Kaizo’s lightning fists surged with power, glowing brighter with every strike. He unleashed a series of brutal hammer fists, each blow meant to break Kent’s arms.</p>
<p>But Kent’s body moved with perfect balance, absorbing the impacts, his own fists striking back with blistering speed and unyielding force.</p>
<p>Kent’s right hook landed squarely on Kaizo’s jaw, sending a ripple through his body. Yet Kaizo barely staggered, his grin widening as golden lightning flared wildly over his skin.</p>
<p>Kaizo spun low and launched a sweeping blow, his fist cutting through the air like a bolt of thunder. Kent ducked, the strike smashing into the training ground, shattering the stone beneath him.</p>
<p>It rebuilt the next second.</p>
<p>With a roar, Kent exploded upward, delivering a devastating uppercut charged with his shifting lightning aura—the blow connected with Kaizo’s chest, sending him flying backward with a thunderous crash.</p>
<p>Kaizo slammed into a pillar but pushed himself up instantly, lightning crackling violently around him. His eyes burned with fierce determination.</p>
<p>"Not bad," Kaizo said, licking a trickle of blood from his lips. "But I’m just getting started."</p>
<p>Kent smiled calmly, muscles tensing for the next exchange. Around them, the legion held their breath, witnessing the battle of two lightning warriors whose fists could tear mountains.</p>
<p>One was muscular, and the other looked like he was competing for the next Miss Universe.</p>
<p>The pure fist fight had only just begun.</p>
