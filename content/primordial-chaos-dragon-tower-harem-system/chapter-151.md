<p>"But just because the master now knows he may be a reincarnation from the past doesn’t mean the master can tell anyone. That is not allowed—at least, not yet," Akira said, making Kent nod.</p>
<p>He knew some things were meant to be hidden.</p>
<p>"Gaia, use 500,000 Tower points to build a place you two can spend time with Li Hua. By the way, do you two eat, or are you robots?" Kent asked.</p>
<p>Akira and Gaia smiled at his childish question.</p>
<p>"We are cosmic beings, master, not robots. And yes, I eat. I don’t know about Gaia, though. She uses universal energy as her food, so she might just be one of those weirdos," Akira said, jumping behind Kent when she felt Gaia’s glare on her.</p>
<p>"Tsk. I eat, master. In fact, I’ve been meaning to try some banana cakes, but since I wasn’t supposed to reveal myself until this bitch appeared, I didn’t get the chance," Gaia replied.</p>
<p>"Good, then. Use the points I gave you to build a house for the three of you. Of course, if it’s not enough, then do it since you’ll be living in it," Kent said with a grin.</p>
<p>The only thing on his mind now was how the fuck things had taken such a sudden turn.</p>
<p>But then again, he had already seen freaky when he awakened a Night Rider and an Aetherwarden. Having Weavers wasn’t something to freak out about.</p>
<p>"Also, the master cannot tell anyone about our existence. For now, I am just a Tower assistant, and that idiot is a Soul Sentinel. Only the master can know about our true existence since we told him."</p>
<p>Kent nodded at Gaia’s words. The last thing he wanted was danger following him because he chose to become a loudmouth.</p>
<p>"Off you go, then. I will come to the Harem Tower later." Gaia and Akira entered the tower, leaving Kent to recollect his thoughts.</p>
<p>A lot had just happened, but he wasn’t about to let it overwhelm him. For now, he knew Gaia was more than just a tower, so he could only use this advantage.</p>
<p>"It doesn’t matter. I must first check the trinkets Vexthra gave me." Kent retrieved the space ring she had given him.</p>
<p>"This one is much better than mine," Kent said, taking his own off. He then placed the one Vexthra had given him onto his finger. He looked inside, and, well, there were dozens of items.</p>
<p>"She really went all out for mere trinkets," Kent chuckled before pouring everything onto the bed. There were 21 items in total.</p>
<p>Four swords, a dagger, a bow, a staff, eight pieces of armour, and six necklaces. Kent quickly used [Enchanted Eyes] to appraise the items, but what he saw left his jaw hanging loose.</p>
<p>"That is a lot of soul items you have there, master. Do you want me to weave some upgrades into them? I’ll just take 21 soul crystals," Akira said, appearing suddenly in the room.</p>
<p>"Akira, shouldn’t you be planning your new home with Gaia? What are you doing here?" Kent asked though he wasn’t dismissing her presence or offer.</p>
<p>"This is just a strand of my consciousness. All weavers can do that. I can even help you with that if your soul becomes strong enough," Akira replied as she began turning the soul items on the bed.</p>
<p>"The one who created these things might really love you very much. It’s full of divine energy," Akira said, making Kent smile.</p>
<p>"She is indeed my first woman and the one who brought me to this world. Of course, I know you knew that already, considering you are a weaver," Kent just sighed.</p>
<p>Gaia and Akira seemed to shift his perspective on things now.</p>
<p>"You are not wrong. But I can’t know everything, master. Unless you give me permission, I cannot do as I please," Akira replied, still meticulously checking the items.</p>
<p>"However, I know enough to know this bow is for your wife, called Unity. Don’t ask me how I know. The name of the bow is [Unity’s Divine Bow], with a skill called [That’s My Sister]."</p>
<p>"Your first wife certainly has a sense of humour. She is also a good Artisan since she was able to create such a perfect, soul item." Akira seemed impressed.</p>
<p>Kent just smiled and checked the swords. Three didn’t have names, but one already had a name, and well, it’s her humour that counted here.</p>
<p>[My Man]</p>
<p>That was the name Vexthra gave the sword, and for a moment, Kent couldn’t stop his mouth from twitching. The goddess seems to have a lot of free time on her hands.</p>
<p>But they were all good items. The three other swords were yet to be named. Also, the dagger was for Kent, for obvious reasons.</p>
<p>But he didn’t reject it.</p>
<p>As for the armour, they were the same, but from what he saw in the appraisal, they were made to adapt to the user’s preferences. This meant it would become a cloak if he wanted it to appear like a cloak.</p>
<p>However, the buffs in the armours were just too good to reject. Kent even wondered why something like this would be allowed in the mortal realm.</p>
<p>But who was he to question something good?</p>
<p>As for the necklaces, they were life-saving items that could be charged using soul energy. They all have the same effects in them. There are three effective abilities in them.</p>
<p>The first one is called [Cosmic Shield]. It creates a cosmic shield around the user, protecting them from any sudden attacks. Of course, the user can activate it with a thought—at will.</p>
<p>It is also automatic in case the user isn’t aware of a sudden attack.</p>
<p>The second is [Cosmic Blast]. This one creates a cosmic blast from the energy stored in the necklace. The blast can kill opponents three realms above the owner, making it, in a way, a perfect killing weapon.</p>
<p>However, this depends on how much energy the necklace has stored before the blast.</p>
<p>Akira even mentioned that it would take some time to accumulate enough energy, but with the runes inscribed in the necklace, the blast would be several times more powerful.</p>
<p>Kent could only thank his lover the next time they met.</p>
<p>The last effect is [Teleportation]. The user must create an anchor somewhere safe, and they can teleport back to it in times of emergency.</p>
<p>This, of course, is useful but not necessarily essential in his eyes.</p>
<p>He could escape any battle with the tower, or so he thought. According to Akira, the moment he engaged in combat, the chances of Gaia taking him away were very slim.</p>
<p>Doing so would affect him in some way. So, the necklace was the safest way for him and his women. This, of course, nearly made him open a portal to the god realm to have a moment with his goddess.</p>
<p>All 21 items were just too overpowered for someone in this middle-grade world. But who was he to complain? He tucked everything away and promised Akira he would let her know when he got the Soul Stones.</p>
<p>Of course, without any further weaving, the items were already powerful.</p>
<p>A few minutes later, he called his three women over, and then he called his master, too. It was time to send them to the tower.</p>
<p>Of course, he trusted his master enough to take her, too. But if she tried something funny, he would just obtain his first resource for his first Undead General...lol.</p>
