<p>Billions of years in the future, the legends would speak of the day a purple light lit up on planet Brymrr.</p>
<p>They would say that, on that day, 64 Celestials, 176 Supremes, 420 Gods, and 2,001 Demigods fell, none of them knowing how they died.</p>
<p>The legends would speak of the day bodies, cut into several pieces, rained down from the sky, clouds, and the void, as though it were raining body parts.</p>
<p>The Sword World would speak of the voice that heralded the downfall of an entire great family.</p>
<p>"You all just died."</p>
<p>The Kazuto Great Family had been searching for Inaya for billions of years to settle a debt they believed she owed them. After all, during the Divine Era, their founder, the one who formed the Kazuto Great Family, tried to make Inaya one of his wives.</p>
<p>When she refused him, he sent a Sword God after her.</p>
<p>For years, Inaya ran, hid, and did all kinds of humiliating things to stay alive until she met Jest, who saved her and later helped her kill the Sword God.</p>
<p>After the Sword God was killed, they proceeded to seek out Lord Kazuto, and, as one would expect, they killed him.</p>
<p>Kazuto’s descendants, whom Jest and Inaya spared, grew up and later decided to seek revenge. Unfortunately for them, by the time they came looking, Inaya had vanished from the face of the universe, and Jest was long dead.</p>
<p>However, they never stopped looking. To them, Inaya and Jest were a stain on their reputation, so their death would finally elevate their statues to a new level.</p>
<p>So when they learned she had resurfaced and was ascending to the Supreme Stage, they mobilised their entire force to come and kill her.</p>
<p>Many of them were stopped and transported into the void by Baldy and Han. However, a few managed to enter the planet’s surface.</p>
<p>Unfortunately for them, Vexthra and her team were no simple lineup. Vexthra alone had killed over 60 Supreme and 300 gods, a feat no god in the entire universe can beat their chest and say they can accomplish.</p>
<p>Even so, despite Vexthra and the others holding their ground, they were all reduced to pitiful states. Thankfully, just when it was all coming to an end, Inaya finally passed the 30th Layer, and she returned to her body.</p>
<p>Meeting Jest was an unexpected welcome, one that gave her more than she asked for.</p>
<p>Watching as bodies rained down, severed into several pieces, one could only wonder how she was able to do that.</p>
<p>The answer was rather simple.</p>
<p>After she received the golden orb and her world darkened, she appeared at a crossroads, where she had to make a decision on how to use the Eternal Sword Intent.</p>
<p>It was an intense debate. However, in the end, she chose her sword as the recipient.</p>
<p>Jest never used his seven strands on himself, so why should she?</p>
<p>So she gave it to her sword, and that was when the first-ever sword with an Eternal Weapon Spirit was born. Just one swing was all it took to kill that many cultivators.</p>
<p>While the energy used was quite steep, it was worth it.</p>
<p>Inaya rushed to Ainna’s side and, with a wave of her hand, she vanished into the tower. Vexthra and the others followed.</p>
<p>They weren’t at the point where they would die. However, Vexthra and Ainna would be out for a very long time. Even so, Inaya forgot about revenge because her sister’s life was her priority.</p>
<p>She sent everyone, including her six Primordial Beasts, into the tower, and she soon left without even acknowledging anyone.</p>
<p>A few seconds after she left, Han and Baldy appeared from the void, their bodies littered with sword injuries.</p>
<p>Baldy looked around, seeing the carnage around him.</p>
<p>"Let it be known that we will come for revenge. All who lifted their weapons against my big sister and family today will face the same fate. I will personally make it my mission to ensure their entire bloodline is wiped from the face of the universe."</p>
<p>After issuing his warning, Baldy vanished into the tower.</p>
<p>Han smiled and said, "I will talk to him and maybe calm him down a bit. However, when my big brother returns and makes a decision, then I, Han, the Grim Sword, will aid in the unholy massacre."</p>
<p>He also vanished, leaving the entire planet in stunned silence.</p>
<p>---</p>
<p>Back inside the Tormented Layer, Kent suddenly paused just as he was about to sever the head of a Forbidden Beast. He froze in his tracks, allowing the spider beast to attack.</p>
<p>However, Kharvok stepped in and defended against the attack aimed at Kent’s chest.</p>
<p>"Something bad has happened, Tower."</p>
<p>His body trembled, and his face went pale as he watched his hands shake.</p>
<p>"Tower, what has happened? I feel like something bad has happened."</p>
<p>"Nothing bad has happened, Master. I can’t sense any danger either."</p>
<p>"Really? I feel like something bad has happened. Can you check up on the girls and see how they are doing?"</p>
<p>"I did, and they are fine, Master."</p>
<p>"Hmm... Okay. I guess learning that Gods are coming after me seems to make me anxious."</p>
<p>Kent forced out a smile and returned to the battle.</p>
<p>---</p>
<p>Inside the Harem Tower, Inaya stood inside a room where five healing pods lay on the floor. Inside them were Vexthra, Lene, Ainna, Umai, and Daliah.</p>
<p>After the tower was upgraded, this room appeared in almost all the towers. Gaia and her goons worked on improving them. They are the best healing stations in the tower.</p>
<p>The moment they returned to the tower, Inaya made sure all five of her sisters were placed inside the healing pods, which Gaia said would speed up their healing.</p>
<p>Hovering beside Inaya was Tower in her humanoid form.</p>
<p>"Did he notice?" Inaya asked, and Tower nodded.</p>
<p>"I hate lying to Master. He would be angry when he finds out I lied to him, Big Sister."</p>
<p>"Don’t worry, Tower. You said it yourself, he is in a dangerous place right now, so we shouldn’t make him restless by revealing what happened to the ladies. When he returns, I will explain everything and make sure he doesn’t become mad at you."</p>
<p>"Okay, Big Sister. I will keep an eye on them so you can enter seclusion to solidify your Supreme Platform. You cannot risk it, not when you have reached the 30th layer."</p>
<p>Inaya nodded, and after spending a few minutes there, she left. As much as she would love to go on a rampage right now, she knew her sisters came first. So until the last of them recovered, she would swallow her anger for now.</p>
<p>Meanwhile, out there in the universe, word of what had happened on planet Brymrr started to spread, reaching many people. In no time, news travelled as far as the Universe Council and even the Soul Temple, shaking them all to the core.</p>
<p>The Ten Emotions Sword Supreme had returned, and instead of welcoming her with open arms, the universe came against her.</p>
<p>Those who knew the history of the Ten Emotions Sword Supreme and Lord Genesis started to panic.</p>
<p>They were a deadly duo in the past. They weren’t known as the Blood Sword Couple for no reason.</p>
<p>Shockingly enough, even some old sword monsters who had entered seclusion for millions of years started to come out of seclusion when news of what had happened reached them.</p>
<p>To them, the deaths of the thousands meant nothing. What they were interested in was the one who executed the attack.</p>
<p>They wanted to meet her and discuss the sword.</p>
<p>Somewhere far away in a brothel filled with Gods, Supremes and even Celestials, a pink-haired incubus played with the bodies of beautiful women as though they were his trinkets.</p>
<p>Judging by the looks on the ladies’ faces, they seemed to be enjoying themselves.</p>
<p>Suddenly, a seductive lady walked in holding a plate of fruits. "My Lord, have you heard? Apparently, somewhere out there, a huge battle broke out between a very powerful group of people and a team of only a dozen or so warriors. However, in the end, the entire family was slaughtered.</p>
<p>They said a bald kid was part of the team that won. You once said you like bald kids, so I figured you want to know."</p>
<p>"Oh really?"</p>
<p>A handsome smile appeared on the pink-haired man’s face.</p>
<p>"Ladies, it seems it is time for me to return. Time for a family reunion."</p>
