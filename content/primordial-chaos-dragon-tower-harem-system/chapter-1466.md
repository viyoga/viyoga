<p>"You know, when Queen Randel called, saying someone would be coming with her referral token, I expected you to be some terrifying beast. Who would have thought you were just a human mortal?"</p>
<p>Lord Bimk said, her eyes narrowing a bit as she walked into the VIP room and sat in the chair opposite Kent.</p>
<p>"Though now that I have met you, mind telling me what business a human mortal like you had inside that place? Even better, how did you survive meeting her?"</p>
<p>Kent let a smile appear on his face. To be honest, he himself couldn’t make sense of what Lord Bimk had asked, despite actually meeting and surviving the Unknown Forest.</p>
<p>Considering his status and rank, there is just no way he should have survived that place. So, going as far as getting the referral of such a terrifying beast shocked her.</p>
<p>"What can I say? I happen to have a good impression on people," he laughed in the end, and Lord Bimk also couldn’t help but chuckle.</p>
<p>There is no way Kent would tell her all he had to do just to secure a safe passage from the Unknown Forest. If he were to get into it, then he would be forced to reveal his true identity.</p>
<p>There is just no way anyone would believe he had killed a Peak Immortal Lord and a pseudo-demigod. So changing the topic was the best move. Thankfully, Lord Bimk had little time, so she got right to the point.</p>
<p>"I owe Queen Randel a favour, so since she said you can cash in her favour, you have 10 minutes to ask me anything you need, and I will do my best to help you."</p>
<p>’A favour, huh?’ Kent felt touched that Queen Randel was using a favour she could have used for something better to aid him instead. ’Then again, I have given her more than she asked for, so there is that.’</p>
<p>The treasures he left her are worth more than her favour.</p>
<p>"First thing I want to know is the name, location, and cultivation rank of the current ruler of this planet."</p>
<p>Lord Bimk blinked a few times when she heard the question Kent asked. For a moment, she just couldn’t help but wonder if Kent was messing with her. However, when she saw the look of seriousness on his face, she narrowed her eyes.</p>
<p>"Wait, you actually want to ask that? Aren’t you a bit ahead of yourself, asking such big questions?"</p>
<p>"I know. However, I really want to know, so if possible, kindly answer my question." There is no beating around the bush here. After all, there is just no way he would come all this way if he didn’t want to know that.</p>
<p>"A very weird question, but I will answer." She sighed and started to answer, "From what I know, the ruler of this universe is the legendary Ten Emotions Sword Supreme.</p>
<p>Of course, this knowledge came from billions of years ago, just when the planet became a Unique World, so there is a chance it is outdated.</p>
<p>After all, it had been billions of years already, and considering the many Supremes that had appeared, anything is possible.</p>
<p>There are some even claiming that a Celestial lives here, so there is a strong chance she has long been dethroned. As to whether that is indeed the case, you would have to find out yourself.</p>
<p>However, unless there is another ruler, if you ask anyone, they would say she is the only known ruler. As to where you can find her, you can go to the two strongest continents in the world, the Elven Continent or the Dwarven Continent.</p>
<p>Most of the Supremes and Gods reside on those continents."</p>
<p>"I see," Kent nodded, feeling a bit happy now that he had made some progress. "It is going to be a road trip then."</p>
<p>Lord Bimk turned her head sideways and looked at Kent with both a stunned and amused expression.</p>
<p>"First of all, there is nothing like a road trip here. Second, what are you even happy about? From where I stand, with your current standing, moving from one city to another would be a headache, much less continental travel."</p>
<p>Kent blinked a few times before asking, "Care to elaborate more on that?"</p>
<p>"There is nothing more to say. From what I know, long before the planet ascended and became a Unique World, travelling from one continent to another was simple.</p>
<p>You just had to use teleportation gates, and if the distance is vast, using Teleportation Channels would do. However, now continental travel requires either using the Continental Teleportation Channel or crossing the Domain Sea.</p>
<p>The Continental Teleportation Channel is the safest but also the hardest to use since only the most powerful people can afford the tickets, or even better, only they have the qualifications to book a ticket.</p>
<p>The Domain Sea is very dangerous, but also the cheapest and easiest to get to. However, looking at your frail body, taking that route is suicide."</p>
<p>Kent sat there frozen for a few seconds, unsure of what to even say. Here, he had hoped to find out more about Inaya, only to learn that she could be in two places, but with no easy way to reach her.</p>
<p>He never expected this journey to be full of perils and obstacles. The least he expected was maybe finding it hard to know more about Inaya; he never looked forward to what Lord Bimk had just said.</p>
<p>And as if to make matters even worse, Kent immediately knew his chances of securing a Continental Teleportation Channel ticket were next to none.</p>
<p>This means he must take the Domain Sea.</p>
<p>But from Lord Bimk’s tone, it appears the Domain Sea isn’t a good place to traverse.</p>
<p>"Domain Sea, what can you tell me about it?"</p>
<p>"Think pirates, Sea Demons, the Underwater Cult, and all kinds of horrors you can imagine coming out to get you." She smiled, seeing the dark expression appear on Kent’s brow.</p>
<p>"Not to discourage you, depending on how deep your pockets are, you can secure a ticket to a ship where the guards would be strong enough to protect your dear life. But I wouldn’t count on it.</p>
<p>When the danger is chilling enough, all those guards will save their skins, so it would be best if you enjoy Snow City and leave when you have had enough."</p>
<p>"That is not something I can do. I am here on a mission, and so I must cross to the continents you mentioned.</p>
<p>If by chance I manage to get a Continental Teleportation Channel ticket, then fine, but now that I know there is a way to at least have some semblance of safe travel through the Domain Sea, I have to keep pushing forward."</p>
<p>There are many reasons why considering his current path sounds like a bad idea. However, he had to go, and so he would at least try his luck first.</p>
<p>"Whatever you think is best, kid. All I know is, staying here is the best decision you will ever make. But if you insist, then I will recommend trying your luck with the Seven Flag Sea Shanti.</p>
<p>They will head to the Dwarven Continent in three months’ time. Although they will take the Domain Sea, they are known for possessing some of the finest guards.</p>
<p>Their last voyage was a success, so choosing them is the best advice I can give you. Of course, if you want to try the Continental Teleportation Channel, the Falcon Crew is known to have much softer requirements.</p>
<p>Regardless, the Dwarven Continent is the closest to us, so going there first is the safest option."</p>
