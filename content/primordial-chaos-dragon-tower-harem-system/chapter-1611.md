<p>Kent understood one thing.</p>
<p>He knew that the current him wasn’t strong enough to awaken these people and command them to fight for him. He may have become their master, but he cannot use them just yet.</p>
<p>Even before he could face them, they had to send the shadow of their shadow of another shadow all the way to the 100th shadow simply to not overwhelm him with their power.</p>
<p>That kind of power isn’t one he can unleash just yet.</p>
<p>But that is fine.</p>
<p>He already has more than enough legions under his command. These beings wouldn’t even have anything to do to aid him, even if he could release them now.</p>
<p>However, there is one thing he can do: utilise their knowledge for personal growth.</p>
<p>He recognised that all nine of them are ancient beings from a different Universe known as the forgotten Universe. Aside from that, from the way they spoke, it seems they had been conscious for a while now, meaning they know quite a lot.</p>
<p>So why not let them guide him in matters he doesn’t know? This way, he can grow much faster and release them. While fighting for him would be nice, Kent also wants his legion to live good lives.</p>
<p>Away from fighting for him, he wants them to be able to build a life for themselves.</p>
<p>So as he faced them, the need to know more burned in his heart.</p>
<p>The dark-haired man on the first statue smiled upon hearing Kent introduce himself. "So you are him, huh?" his eyes sparkled.</p>
<p>"We have witnessed your life in full, Chaos Dragon, and I have to say, of all the talents we have seen in our limitless lives, you are within the top 13, perhaps ranked even at the top." The man suddenly paused, and his eyes narrowed.</p>
<p>"Did you just say you are the Genesis Swordsman, the Divine Beast Tamer, and the King of Evil?"</p>
<p>Kent smiled and nodded. "They were my incarnations."</p>
<p>"Wow. That is unexpected," the nine undead generals turned and looked at each other.</p>
<p>The man on the third statue, a rather calm man from the look in his eyes, asked:</p>
<p>"If you are the Genesis Swordsman, the Divine Beast Tamer, and the King of Evil, then you would happen to also be the smartest man to ever walk the face of this universe, Pill Celestial Kevin Sage."</p>
<p>Kent smiled and nodded.</p>
<p>"What about the Nine Underworld Flames Blacksmith?"</p>
<p>"Yet to remember that life... at least if it were me. But what I do know is, including the life of the Primordial Chaos Dragon, I have lived 13 lives in total."</p>
<p>Kent could see the shock and excitement appear in the eyes of the Nine Undead Generals. Clearly, they had witnessed all his lives, and perhaps that was why he ranked among the top 13 greatest of all time.</p>
<p>Kent had already seen his life as Lord Genesis and the Ruthless Bandit in full, and so he knew he was indeed the greatest in that era. So he wouldn’t be that shocked if he was all 13 they had witnessed.</p>
<p>"This is interesting. To think our first ever master would be the person that made us stop and stay in this universe," the lady seated on the 6th statue said, recalling when the undead pagoda they were in drifted soundlessly for years until they arrived in this universe.</p>
<p>"Tell me, Kent Madson, what do you want to know? You must remember that we are beings of great power. However, even with that power, we cannot violate the rules of this universe.</p>
<p>Of course, as you grow stronger, those rules can be bent somehow to reveal some secrets that you would find very interesting. So ask; if we find your questions within reason, we will answer them all."</p>
<p>Kent nodded and asked the first question that came to mind, "What cultivation base are you seniors currently at? I can tell that you are way past the Celestial stage and perhaps even above the Pseudo-Primordial stage. So are you at the Primordial Stage, and if yes, how did you get there?"</p>
<p>If it were before Kent recovered his memories to the peak in two lives, he wouldn’t have asked this question. However, things are different now; he knows way more than ordinary folk.</p>
<p>The seventh lady smirked. "Your question can be answered partially. First of all, know that the Celestial stage is not the peak, and neither is the Pseudo-Primordial. In fact, I don’t even think the 4th Tier is the peak."</p>
<p>Kent said nothing and waited for more context, but none came. He then realised that was all the lady had planned to say.</p>
<p>’4th Tier, huh?’</p>
<p>He saw somewhere before that the 1st Tier is the Mortal Stage. That starts from Root Awakening all the way to Heavenly Ascension. The 2nd Tier starts from Empyrean to Immortal Lord. The 3rd Tier starts from a demigod all the way to a Celestial.</p>
<p>’So the 4th Tier is probably the Primordial Stage, and perhaps there are higher tiers after that. But if that is the case, then does that mean there are Primordials in this universe?’</p>
<p>"Don’t stress yourself, Kent Madson. In due time, everything will be revealed. Now, ask questions that will benefit you, and hurry—your time here is slipping."</p>
<p>"Okay." Kent thought for a moment and asked a question that would indeed benefit him.</p>
<p>"You mentioned I have not yet reached 20% of my potential. Since that is the case, can you tell me how I can push that a little bit higher?"</p>
<p>"A good question," the man on the 5th statue nodded.</p>
<p>"First of all, I would advise you to temper your body. I can tell you have attained the Nirvana Body. That is okay for someone your age and cultivation.</p>
<p>However, if you want to move ahead, then walk the Nine Nirvana Tempering Path and temper your body in the essence of true Nirvana Flames. We have located all nine Nirvana Flames and will give you the locations when you ascend to the Immortal Realm.</p>
<p>Second, cultivate your Beast Taming abilities and unlock an innate skill called Symbiosis. This skill allows you to inherit a percentage of the contracted beast’s power.</p>
<p>Unlike how you can inherit their soul strength, Symbiosis will instead allow you to inherit their strength and abilities, enabling you to wield immense power without having to do much.</p>
<p>Of course, it also depends on how strong your body and meridians are. This means you must exceed the Nirvana Body and step into the Immortal, God, Supreme, and Celestial Body. We have a technique that can aid you, but you are not strong enough for it just yet.</p>
<p>Find one and use it to reach the God Body, and then we will give you that technique.</p>
<p>Third, it would be advisable to pay attention to your many lives. There is more to gain there, especially the experience you have acquired in all those lives. Utilise them well and never stop chasing after more strength.</p>
<p>I don’t know why you were made our master, but I do know that if we are here, then danger is coming. So challenge yourself, and whenever you see an opportunity, grab it.</p>
<p>Lastly, pay attention to your legion’s growth. I have a feeling that when you ascend, something good will happen to you."</p>
<p>Kent nodded, taking everything the undead general said to heart.</p>
<p>"Another question," Kent said, turning toward the first undead general. "Aside from True Sword Energy and Absolute Sword Energy, are there others?"</p>
<p>The question was directed to him simply because he was the only sword wielder among the nine undead generals.</p>
<p>He smiled and nodded. "Indeed. There are others."</p>
