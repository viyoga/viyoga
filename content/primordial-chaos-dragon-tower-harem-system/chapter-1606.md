<p>Kent made a fist, feeling an overwhelming force move through his arm. For a moment, he let himself be lost in the power coursing through his veins.</p>
<p>’This power, is there anyone below the god grade that can take a punch from me?’</p>
<p>[Of course, there are plenty. However, if we factor in five things about you, then I don’t even know what would become of you.]</p>
<p>Tower responded, causing Kent to furrow his brow.</p>
<p>Yeti is down, its energy severely drained after using the glacier attack.</p>
<p>While he isn’t at the point where he cannot continue the battle, for some reason, when Kent destroyed his glacier, he was struck with a terrible backlash that knocked him down.</p>
<p>Seeing he wouldn’t be rising back up anytime soon, Kent decided to engage Tower for now.</p>
<p>"What are those five things, Tower?"</p>
<p>[First, you are only a True Immortal. That means your strength is still being limited by your cultivation base. What do you think would happen if you reach the Demigod stage?</p>
<p>Second, the idea is to merge several energies together using your fist as the medium. Now, you only have access to Chaos Qi, Evil Qi, Blood Qi, and Flame Qi.</p>
<p>While you managed to use Dark Qi and Ice Qi, I think we both know they did little to nothing in the grand scheme of the skill. However, what happens when you awaken those two and other elements later?</p>
<p>Third, there is your Primal Blood Platform. Right now, you have two blood gates, which means you can form two cores of each element or energy. What happens if you awaken all nine Blood Gates? Nine cores for each element.</p>
<p>Fourth, the blood seals currently come from two blood cores. But as I said, you are destined to awaken nine blood gates, which means you will form nine blood cores. What happens when you can harness the power of over a million blood seals?</p>
<p>Fifth, from what I have observed, your Blood Life and Blood Death Seals also seem to have contributed greatly. At the moment, you have only 25 of each. But we both know it is only a matter of time before you form more.</p>
<p>In fact, there are even your Chaos Scales, Dragon Intent, Sword Intent, and several other energies you can channel or have an affinity for.</p>
<p>If I am not mistaken, the [Universe Breaking Fist] has the capability to evolve to the Supreme and maybe even the Celestial stage.</p>
<p>If that is not enough reason to consider the current monstrous nature of your skill, then I don’t know what is. I dont want to blow my own horn, but as a true primordial, what happens when you manage to make a Primordial grade technique?]</p>
<p>Kent only held a smile on his face as he finally assessed the kind of power he currently possesses. The kind of power he alone commands is enough to put him at the top of every ranking.</p>
<p>He may have appeared on three Dao tablets and even seemed to have drawn the attention of powerful people. However, that exposure is nothing compared to the kind of power he now holds.</p>
<p>"If people learn of my current capabilities, they are going to claw their eyes out,"</p>
<p>Kent threw his head back and unleashed a heartwarming yet thunderous laugh, the kind that made his four demigod beast companions wonder what had gotten into their master.</p>
<p>Even Yeti, who was supposed to be in a half-conscious state, seemed to have snapped back to life.</p>
<p>"Damn, this time around, I will show the Void Beasts the kind of monster they have offended,"</p>
<p>Somewhere at the heart of the universe, a smile formed on Lady Universe’s face for a brief moment.</p>
<p>Kent lowered his gaze and looked at Yeti, who looked back at him.</p>
<p>Just then, a quest popped up before his vision again.</p>
<p>"Looks like after your upgrade, more quests started flooding in, Tower," Kent muttered before accepting the mission.</p>
<p>***</p>
<p>[You have received a side quest: First Forbidden Beast Companion.]</p>
<p>[Quest Level: SS]</p>
<p>– Objective: [Make Yeti your first Forbidden beast companion]</p>
<p>– Reward: [The Secret Manual, Ten Divine Eye Secret Art &amp; The Undead Pagoda]</p>
<p>---</p>
<p>Kent smiled, reading the quest. It seemed simple enough, yet the rewards made it seem as though it could not be cleared easily.</p>
<p>Looking down, he saw Yeti had already started gathering energy. It appeared it hadn’t had enough of the beating Kent had given it.</p>
<p>Kent pondered what to do. If they fought again, he would simply use the [Claw Art], which he could not guarantee would end well for Yeti.</p>
<p>So he wanted a calmer way to ensure that he and Yeti reached a good agreement, not a forced one.</p>
<p>He could tell that the irrational being he expected might be the Yeti before him now. From the moment they clashed, Kent sensed the primal feeling all monsters shared.</p>
<p>Yeti doesn’t have the calm and humane feeling he sensed from Yggzrael and the others.</p>
<p>There was more to learn, but that could wait.</p>
<p>Suddenly, an idea popped into his mind. "Tower, use one of these orbs that appeared under the Bloodline Skill Tree. You said they are related to Beast Taming right? Use one please."</p>
<p>A few seconds later, a message flared before Kent’s vision.</p>
<p>---</p>
<p>[You have awakened a new Divine Beast Tamer Aspect: Tamer]</p>
<p>[Tamer]</p>
<p>[Rank: Innate]</p>
<p>[Rating: 4 Star]</p>
<p>[Description: Beasts and monsters that share destiny with you find your presence comforting. You are likely to sense some kinship between you and the beast or monster.]</p>
<p>---</p>
<p>On the ground, Yeti suddenly felt a connection with Kent, one that made it pause and stop channeling its energy.</p>
<p>Kent felt the same thing. He and Yeti shared a destiny.</p>
<p>"Looks like we have to take a step back and have a nice conversation," Yeti growled, a wave of ice energy oozing out of its body as it looked menacingly at Kent.</p>
<p>"Fighting me would not end well for you. I have already defeated you twice, so it would be in your best interest to at least take a step back. You can already sense that I mean you no harm.</p>
<p>If you want me to prove myself, I will. However, know this, when it comes to life or death, killing you would be very simple."</p>
<p>Kent thought for a moment and then decided to prove himself.</p>
<p>"If you don’t believe me, why don’t you look up?" As he said that, he pointed upward, and from the clouds, ten meteors bathed in flames broke through the thick black sky, the ground trembling as they descended.</p>
<p>Yeti growled again, its body trembling. The force from the ten meteors pressed down on it. Kent made sure all ten meteors locked onto it directly, meaning all ten would strike it no matter where it moved.</p>
<p>It was one of the abilities he had awakened after his soul universe expanded upon becoming an Immortal.</p>
<p>Thankfully, another ability called [Soul Recall] had awakened, so just when the pressure became more severe on Yeti, Kent snapped his finger, and the ten meteors vanished into thin air.</p>
<p>Yeti dropped to one knee, its eyes locked on Kent. Now, a sense of fear was evident in them.</p>
<p>Kent smiled and said, "Like I said, it would be good if we can have a nice conversation like two adults who share a common goal."</p>
<p>"And that is?" Yeti spoke, waves of ice mist spreading from its body, freezing the already frozen ground.</p>
<p>"For starters, we can address the fact that you have yet to awaken your innate body."</p>
<p>"I am a Forbidden. Innate bodies aren’t something my kind can awaken."</p>
<p>"Naturally, not. However, with the right pill, you can become a monster unlike any you ever dreamed of."</p>
