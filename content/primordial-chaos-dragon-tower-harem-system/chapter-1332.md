<p>Alina, Lilian, and Velyra also walked toward the colossal body of the beast. It took them an hour and 4 minutes, but they finally managed to bring it down.</p>
<p>"What are we going to do with it?" Lilian asked. Any warrior would love to have their hands on such a colossal body. It is perfect for crafting and much more.</p>
<p>"Nothing. We already have the core, so the next thing we will be doing is to get out of here," Alina responded, turning toward the direction of the Fire King.</p>
<p>After a few moments of contemplation, she flew toward him... "You can have the body of the beasts; we don’t need it."</p>
<p>While having the body of an Empyrean will surely come in handy, Alina knew it was too disfigured to contribute much to anything.</p>
<p>The one who would have benefited the most is Veera. She is a Necromancer after all.</p>
<p>However, even she has several stronger dead bodies than this beast. Their time in the Paragon Universe gave them so much that this beast body is nothing in their eyes.</p>
<p>"Are you sure?" the Fire King asked, and Alina nodded.</p>
<p>"The core is enough. You can also share the rewards with those who made a move on the lion when it broke free. Of course, if it is huge enough, use it to take care of the families of those who lost someone during the rampage."</p>
<p>The Fire King cupped his fist and bowed, showing his gratitude to Alina and her team for coming through for him.</p>
<p>"Don’t worry about it." Alina smiled and started flying back. However, before she could get far, the Fire King asked...</p>
<p>"At least, can we know the names of our benefactors?"</p>
<p>Alina stopped and answered, making sure her words were heard by quite a lot of people... "Our names are not important. Just know that Kent Madson sent us."</p>
<p>With that, she teleported away, appearing beside her team. Then they both teleported back to Vexthra and the rest of the team.</p>
<p>Everyone who heard what Alina said became stunned. The Kent Madson everyone keeps hearing about turns out to have even a monstrous team on his side. That is just something many didn’t see coming.</p>
<p>Words quickly started spreading, announcing to everyone the appearance of five powerful warriors who claimed to be warriors under the infamous Kent Madson.</p>
<p>Some who were smart enough to record most of the battle, and where Alina revealed their identity, started selling the videos, making millions in high-grade spirit stones.</p>
<p>---</p>
<p>Meanwhile, just when Alina and her team arrived beside Vexthra and the rest of the team, a message indicating a successful mission completion appeared, making everyone happy.</p>
<p>"Congratulations, Alina. You have successfully led a team of your own in clearing a dungeon floor. That is wonderful."</p>
<p>Alina smiled, feeling happy to be praised by her big sister. While Vexthra is indeed her sister, the fact that she is a goddess yet praising her made her happy.</p>
<p>Unity, of course, wasn’t happy to hear Alina being praised. However, she said nothing and only continued to pout. While she is the sweetest of them all, they know that if they keep falling for her schemes, she will continue to be a spoiled brat.</p>
<p>So Vexthra and her sisters decided to make sure she never got her way again, at least while Kent is away.</p>
<p>"So where do we search this time around?" Elsa asked, seeing the mission on the 6th floor finally come to an end.</p>
<p>"I have already scanned the entire planet, but it seems there is nothing to look for here. The place where the beasts were summoned is completely destroyed, so I believe we have nothing to look for here."</p>
<p>"Then are we going to the seventh floor?"</p>
<p>"No. We each have two Limit Breaker Keys, an Innate Talent Awakening Pill, and an Innate Talent Booster Pill. We have to go back to the tower and use them before heading to the 7th floor.</p>
<p>Very soon, we will start facing even immortals, so we must be prepared. Breaking two more limits and awakening an Innate Talent is going to go a long way for sure."</p>
<p>"Okay, big sister."</p>
<p>Vexthra opened the portal to the legion tower, and they all entered. Soon, they were back inside the tower, where time is on their side. Vexthra quickly shared the rewards, then she and her sisters teleported away, appearing in the Harem Tower.</p>
<p>"Let’s get to work." They nodded and vanished, appearing in their various treehouses. The only ones left were Miryssa, Yuyin, and Vexthra.</p>
<p>Vexthra took them to the Yin Pool, where they stripped naked and entered. Since Kent was not around, Miryssa and Yuyin didn’t mind getting naked.</p>
<p>"Use the Limit Breaker Keys first, then focus on the Innate Talent Awakening Pill and Booster Pill."</p>
<p>Vexthra said before pressing the key against her forehead. This isn’t her first time doing this. She had done it four times already, so it was nothing new to her.</p>
<p>Miryssa watched as Vexthra’s soul energy started to stir. This sent her into a wave of shock.</p>
<p>She knew breaking a limit while at the god stage was near impossible. While breaking a limit benefits them greatly, the resources required to even dare attempt such a risky endeavour are mind-blowing.</p>
<p>It is why many go above and beyond to break more limits while mortal and at the immortal stage. The moment they become gods, that luxury becomes something so distant.</p>
<p>’I can’t believe I am about to break two more limits after billions of years of no progress.’ She became emotional just thinking about this prospect.</p>
<p>In the end, however, she pressed the key against her forehead, and her soul energy began to stir, indicating she was about to break a limit on her soul.</p>
<p>As for Yuyin, the moment she pressed the key against her forehead, she felt her mind clear, then, as if she had undergone enlightenment, her comprehension speed and ability surged.</p>
<p>’Amazing, after so many years of trying, I have finally broken the first mind limit.’ She smiled, tears rolling down her face.</p>
<p>She had only broken 6 limits on her body, despite being a Heavenly Ascension-stage cultivator. Unlike most, she and her people didn’t have the luxury to cultivate in peace, so the least she was able to do was break six—two on her soul, body, and cultivation.</p>
<p>Now, she had broken her 7th, and soon the 8th was broken on her mind again, making her see more than she had been able to perceive before.</p>
<p>The same was for Miryssa, who managed to break her 65th and 66th limits. Vexthra had finally broken her 69th and 70th limits, making her mental and soul strength surge.</p>
<p>’Looks like my Law Domain has already reached the Supreme stage, but I can make it better,’ Vexthra said inwardly with a smile.</p>
<p>Just one limit more broken in her mind, and she had already seen this deep into her law comprehension.</p>
<p>Perhaps the Stardrop Noir she drank while at the god realm may have also played a part. But this breakthrough would have taken her thousands of years.</p>
<p>’I guess I will thank Kent properly when he is back.’</p>
<p>After saying that, she swallowed the Innate Talent Awakening Pill.</p>
