<p>The words Spirit Stones Multiplier Bingo Card were neatly inscribed on its surface in elegant, golden letters. Just looking at them exudes luxury and money.</p>
<p>Kent turned the card over in his hand, inspecting it closely. Unity leaned in, curious as ever. "What’s that?" she asked.</p>
<p>"A bingo card, apparently," Kent replied, holding it up so she could see.</p>
<p>He wasn’t worried about Unity knowing some of his secrets. Vexthra had lovingly warned her about some of the dangers and rewards of being with Kent. Essentially, she cautioned her against entertaining any funny ideas.</p>
<p>But that was just Vexthra being overprotective. Kent had no fear of the women he placed the seal on betraying him. While it wasn’t a slave seal, it prevented them from uttering a word of betrayal without catastrophic consequences.</p>
<p>Even without the seal, Vexthra had already ensured that her technique included an additional safeguard. If Unity dared to betray Kent, and the Harem seal somehow failed, her Anti-Betrayal seal would activate.</p>
<p>Essentially, the goddess wanted to ensure that no harm could come from those Kent trusted to be part of him on his journey to the top. She was determined to help him live a good life surrounded by trustworthy lovers.</p>
<p>Unity was now one of them. She belonged to Kent—body, heart, and soul. She wouldn’t betray him, even under threat. Then again, with her sister being a goddess, anyone daring to threaten her would quickly learn the consequences.</p>
<p>"But it’s not just any bingo card. Let me check the details."</p>
<p>***</p>
<p>[Spirit Stones Multiplier Bingo Card]</p>
<p>[Grade: Legendary]</p>
<p>Effects:]</p>
<p>- Grants the user a chance to multiply Spirit Stones through a game of bingo.</p>
<p>- Each square represents a task or challenge. Completing a row, column, or diagonal activates the multiplier.</p>
<p>- Multiplier varies depending on the number of lines completed, ranging from 2x to 20x.</p>
<p>- Unused after 30 days, the card self-destructs.</p>
<p>***</p>
<p>Kent raised an eyebrow as he read the description. "Interesting... This could turn out to be extremely useful—or a complete waste, depending on the challenges. But still, 2 times is better than nothing. No lost here"</p>
<p>Unity chuckled softly. "I guess Big Sister Vexthra didn’t say all those things for nothing. You really are a mystery. Makes me wonder what I would’ve done if I’d chickened out when you wanted me to become your woman.</p>
<p>Thank you."</p>
<p>Her gaze reflected genuine emotion, her feelings clear and heartfelt. Unity was indeed very happy at that moment. Her decision to take such a bold step, despite knowing the societal downsides, had proven to be a great one.</p>
<p>"Hey, listen to me. You are now my woman, so I don’t want to see you acting inferior to anyone. You deserve all the love and care now, so be proud instead.</p>
<p>"Plus, I currently don’t have any achievements, so basically, I’m a nobody. But by the time people start to recognize me, you’ll also become a powerhouse. And I doubt that even if people knew your background, they’d dare to mock you.</p>
<p>"But even if they do, it’ll just give me a reason to cut them down," Kent said with a cute smile. Yet Unity knew he meant every single word.</p>
<p>"Then don’t slack off. Make me happy so I can make you happy too," she grinned.</p>
<p>"Oh, I see..." Kent smiled and returned his attention to examining the card. Unity continued to stare at him, lost in thought. Suddenly, a voice entered her head.</p>
<p>&lt; You’re happy now, aren’t you? &gt; Vexthra’s voice spoke, causing Unity’s eyes to widen for a moment.</p>
<p>&lt; Big sister, how are you contacting me? I thought we had already exhausted our five minutes, &gt; Unity asked. The five-minute telepathic connection between her and Vexthra had been used up, so how was she contacting her now?</p>
<p>&lt; I am a goddess, little sis. Although, for some reason, I couldn’t connect with Kent, when I tried it on you, it worked. Now, we can talk all day long. &gt;</p>
<p>&lt; This is good. I guess Kent’s going to love to hear this, &gt; Unity smiled.</p>
<p>&lt; Yeah, but for now, don’t tell him. Let this be our little secret until I figure out a way to include him, &gt; Vexthra said, making Unity nod slightly.</p>
<p>Although she didn’t know how Vexthra managed to contact her, Unity knew she had to trust her, even though keeping such a secret from Kent didn’t sit well with her.</p>
<p>&lt; Don’t worry, Unity. I just need a few days. If it doesn’t work out, you can tell him. Maybe he can take advantage of it. He’s already found a loophole, so I bet he could come up with something too. &gt; Unity smiled, this time genuinely.</p>
<p>At least she wouldn’t have to hold the secret for long.</p>
<p>"You can tell Vexthra I said hi." While Unity was doing her best to hide her expressions so Kent wouldn’t see them, his voice suddenly entered her head, making Unity pale slightly.</p>
<p>&lt; Shit. &gt; Unity transmitted to Vexthra.</p>
<p>&lt; He found out, didn’t he? &gt; Vexthra asked, an amused expression playing on her face as she sat in a hot tub in the god realm.</p>
<p>&lt; Yes... I guess I wasn’t sneaky enough, &gt; Unity said, casting Kent a guilty but cute smile.</p>
<p>"You don’t actually think I wouldn’t notice something like this, do you?" Kent said with a teasing smile.</p>
<p>Just like Vexthra, he had tried using his connection with Unity to tap into her connection with Vexthra to gain some extra minutes, but something was preventing him.</p>
<p>Of course, the Tower had said it was the laws governing the world. This meant that, at his level, he wouldn’t be able to penetrate those laws.</p>
<p>However, he knew Vexthra could, and the only reason she was able to do so was because of the tower tattoo mark under their bellies. Unlike Kent’s women, he didn’t have the tattoo, which was understandable considering he was the one giving them out.</p>
<p>But what he didn’t know was that the moment he added two women to the harem, their souls were linked. Since they both possessed the same essence—the yin essence—so Vexthra used this bond to weave through the laws, giving her direct contact with Unity.</p>
<p>"You are not mad we tried to hide something this important from you?" Unity asked.</p>
<p>"Why would I be? This is a good thing. You can now talk to your sister any day. This also means if I need something, I can just ask you to tell her. It’s a win for me, and since she is a goddess, you can benefit greatly by consulting her on your cultivation.</p>
<p>"So from the way I see it, this would be less work for me teaching you and more time for other things," Kent said with a perverted smile.</p>
<p>Unity leaned in and hugged him. "From me and Big Sister," she muttered before turning away and beginning to converse with Vexthra.</p>
<p>Kent smiled and then decided to focus on the auction. At that moment, another item was sold for 400 spirit stones. Then the next item was brought in.</p>
<p>It was an old sword sheath with no beauty to it, but the moment Kent saw it, thanks to his Appraiser’s Eye skill, he recognized its worth.</p>
<p>He immediately made up his mind to buy this sword sheath no matter what.</p>
