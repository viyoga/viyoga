<p>What could have been a battle that would push Kent to his limits soon turned into a training session for him. He didn’t even feel the pressure that he should have felt or as one would expect, since he had employed the overwhelming power of the [Eye of Chaos].</p>
<p>Every movement was either in or out of the hundreds of eyes in the air.</p>
<p>The tiger, in Kent’s opinion, was very powerful, so powerful that if he were to face it before his training in the Chaos Chasm, he would have died from the first two attacks.</p>
<p>However, things were different now. His speed, strength, and even defense were at an all-time high. The sheer level of power coursing through his body was enough to give him an edge against even a Primordial.</p>
<p>But Kent knew he was in no way close to killing a Primordial. But if he wanted to, he could also kill one. He had the means to kill one, and it was his legendary hammer, Mjolnir.</p>
<p>So for now, he could only train his abilities and, more importantly, make sure whenever he faced a terrifying opponent like this, he would use the opportunity to train a part of his strength until he was ready to become a god, which he believed would push him even closer to killing a Primordial.</p>
<p>Against this tiger, however, he had figured out the method to kill it, and right now, he had already chipped away 20% of its power.</p>
<p>"Things would have been so much easier if killing intent worked against you," Kent said with a smirk before landing a kick on the neck of the tiger. Its head flung back, and its body slammed into the ground, creating a crater.</p>
<p>It was back up again, but before it could gain the upper hand again, Kent vanished into one of the eyes and appeared through another.</p>
<p>He pointed his sword forward, and a wave of Sword Aura shot out, transforming into a large sword before landing on the side of the tiger.</p>
<p>"Too bad, really, too bad," he sighed and vanished into another eye.</p>
<p>Since his strongest power now was his killing intent, he knew if it worked against this tiger, then perhaps he would have already ended its miserable life.</p>
<p>[Master, I think I know who sent this tiger.]</p>
<p>A few minutes into the battle, Tower spoke, causing Kent to grin.</p>
<p>"Do tell, Tower, who dared send such a machine after me?"</p>
<p>[It is the fatty you killed after the auction, or at least another body of his living in the Celestial Realm. After you killed his Immortal Body, he might have felt the pain, and so he sent this monstrosity after you.]</p>
<p>"I knew it." Kent smiled, evading a claw attack and landing his own attack on the side of the tiger, sending it flying back several meters.</p>
<p>"That bastard sure has unlimited wealth. To have such a treasure, he must really be extremely wealthy."</p>
<p>[Probably so. The price of this tiger is several times higher than regular Celestial-grade treasures. Master might really have pissed him off.]</p>
<p>Kent laughed.</p>
<p>If he also had multiple bodies and one was killed, he would be furious, so he perfectly understood the feeling the fatty had gone through.</p>
<p>But who was to be blamed? If he hadn’t turned Meiko into his slave, Kent wouldn’t have been that extreme. At best, he would have tortured him and let him go.</p>
<p>But he crossed the line, and so he made sure he paid with his life.</p>
<p>"I figure since a body of his is in the Celestial Realm, then he must also have one in the God and Supreme Realms," Kent stated, a grin forming at the corner of his lips.</p>
<p>[I don’t know why Master wants to know, but yes. I have tracked his aura to both realms.]</p>
<p>The grin turned into an evil smile. "Great. After we are done with the missions I have, we will pay his God body a visit and play with him. I would love to see what kind of monstrosity he will send after me next."</p>
<p>Tower knew that if she said anything against Kent’s plan, her master would go ahead and do it anyway, so she could only support him and add it as another mission to his already long list of missions.</p>
<p>A few minutes later, Kent stopped, looked at the severely damaged tiger, and smiled. "Killing you will be a waste of resources. I better secure you first, then study how you were refined. Who knows, I might be able to repair you and make you even more powerful."</p>
<p>Kent formed a hand seal, and a portal opened beside the tiger. A large hand reached out from within the portal and grabbed the tiger, pulling it inside.</p>
<p>Kent smiled, knowing that with his understanding of blacksmithing, he would be able to forge the tiger into something worth fearing even by Primordials one day.</p>
<p>He closed his eyes and appeared inside his soul sea, where Mjolnir had already tied down the tiger and was seated on it with a relaxed expression.</p>
<p>"Don’t accidentally kill it, Mjolnir."</p>
<p>"I won’t," he replied and then asked, "Are we there yet? I can already feel the pressure of two powerful beings. One is even stronger than I am and very twisted based on his aura, while the other is very hostile."</p>
<p>Kent furrowed his brow, and then he smiled. "The twisted one surely is who we are here for. As for the hostile one, they will be your opponent, so be ready."</p>
<p>Mjolnir nodded. "Then are you going to fight as a dragon, swordsman, mage, or blacksmith?"</p>
<p>Kent thought for a moment and smiled. "For old times’ sake, I will fight as a blacksmith." He looked at Mjolnir, who had a smile on his face, and smirked. "Are you happy now?"</p>
<p>Mjolnir didn’t answer, but Kent knew he was happy.</p>
<p>Kent himself was also happy. At least today, he would share the same battlefield with his old-time friend.</p>
<p>Among all the weapons he had wielded in his past, Mjolnir had been the closest to him, like a family.</p>
<p>He had been with him since he was 7. So while he might be a bit popular now due to his past incarnations, if they could share the same battlefield again, he knew his legendary hammer would love that.</p>
