<p>"I already told you who I am. You just chose not to listen. In any case, don’t blame me for being cruel, but I think we all know cruelty went out the window when you helped cast a Tier 13 spell that is slowly killing your own neighbors."</p>
<p>Kent’s gaze turned cold.</p>
<p>"I know humans can be cruel, but I never expected a small group such as yourselves to be this evil." He then turned and looked at two elders from the Dreadclaw clan.</p>
<p>"You two can drop dead too." With a snap of his fingers, the two elders could only clench their chests before dropping to their deaths.</p>
<p>"It’s only fair I share the death among the tribe." Kent turned to one elder from the Soulshroud tribe and their army general, the only two not wearing a Tier 11 soul protection artifact.</p>
<p>"Death is calling you two." Their tribe chief tried to defend them, but Kent had long planted the soul bomb, so it was useless.</p>
<p>He didn’t stay back when he told Velyra to go join her father for nothing. Those few seconds were spent planting soul bombs.</p>
<p>"Alliance, charge and kill them all!" Tribe Chief Rokar, who had lost a son and two elders, became furious and ordered his tribe warriors to charge forward.</p>
<p>However, before General Xant of the Bloodvein tribe could give an order to his soldiers, Kent raised his hand and snapped his fingers.</p>
<p>His eyes shone, and like the coming of the apocalypse, the void shattered and thousands of sword arcs rained down, decimating the ranks of the army that charged forward.</p>
<p>"No need to be in a rush. I have more where that came from." The alliance army came to a sudden stop, panic and fear setting in.</p>
<p>"You’d better take out your secret weapon that was meant to guarantee your victory, or else the next wave of attacks will reduce your ranks to half," Kent said, looking smugly at the two tribe chiefs.</p>
<p>He really expected them to do that. However, to his shock, they didn’t.</p>
<p>"In that case, don’t blame me."</p>
<p>Kent snapped his fingers again, and this time, the attacks that came were multiplied using the [Thousand Blade Refraction] skill.</p>
<p>This caused his hundred attacks to multiply into thousands. When the attack was released, Kent felt a large chunk of his energy drain.</p>
<p>The two tribe chiefs tried to defend, but they could only do so much. By the time all the attacks landed, close to fifteen thousand soldiers had died.</p>
<p>"This is..." Even the Bloodvein tribe was shaken, watching the devastation unfold before their eyes. They never expected a single person to be this destructive.</p>
<p>If only they knew this was Kent being merciful.</p>
<p>He could have killed all of them, considering the three days he spent weren’t wasted. He had been planning well, and all the attacks he stacked were imbued with Chaos Qi.</p>
<p>This meant even the Peak Sovereigns were but little flowers in front of his attacks. They had little resistance against it, and knowing Kent, he had made sure there were no mistakes.</p>
<p>"Tribe Chief Malthar, I’ll leave that brute to you. Be careful, though—he has a powerful soul weapon." Kent blinked and appeared a few meters from Tribe Chief Calthar.</p>
<p>"Why don’t you show me what you’re hiding?" Kent kicked forward, landing a powerful punch on the tribe chief that sent him flying.</p>
<p>At the same time, Tribe Chief Malthar also attacked, clashing with Tribe Chief Rokar, who was seething with rage.</p>
<p>"Bloodvein army, slaughter them all!" General Xant commanded, raising his weapon. "For the Mother of Killers!"</p>
<p>"For the Mother of Killers!"</p>
<p>They charged forward and clashed with the army from the alliance, whose ranks had been decimated and whose formations were completely broken.</p>
<p>Taking advantage of that, they stormed into the weakened ranks and began slaughtering them.</p>
<p>Elder Ureth clashed with an elder from the Soulshroud tribe who was a weapon refiner. The enemy took out ten weapons and started controlling them to attack.</p>
<p>However, thanks to Kent’s opening, the brute elder was on fire as he charged at the weapons, sending them flying. He knew his job was to kill that elder, and he focused entirely on doing just that.</p>
<p>Elder Ferra also clashed with another weapon refiner who used daggers instead. However, upon closer inspection, those daggers had been refined with thousands of souls, making them more dangerous than even a Peak Sovereign.</p>
<p>Elder Zarka, on the other hand, was clashing with Elder Vak from the Dreadclaw tribe. He was the last remaining elder, considering Kent had already killed the other two.</p>
<p>Meanwhile, Velyra was locked in combat with the prince of the Soulshroud tribe. He was also a Core Formation expert who had completed three evolutions.</p>
<p>Their battle prowess was matched, and considering his weapon was a mystical-grade soul sword, he was packing severe attacks.</p>
<p>However, Velyra wasn’t worried. She had more control over Chaos Qi than the prince, so she knew she could handle him.</p>
<p>Tribe Chief Rokar had already transformed into his full beast form, taking the shape of a 12-meter-tall panther with silver fur.</p>
<p>All members of the royal bloodline of the Dreadclaw clan are silver panthers, so he had taken that form. And thanks to him wielding his full strength, Tribe Chief Malthar found himself facing a mountain.</p>
<p>However, he wasn’t the leader of the Bloodvein tribe for nothing. His combat power was something that could not be ignored. He was essentially an overpowered warrior who wielded both blood and chaos energy.</p>
<p>His close combat abilities are impeccable.</p>
<p>Kent had always wondered why Malthar had three eyes instead of two like everyone else. Today, the answer has become clear.</p>
<p>The third eye allowed him to see a few seconds into the future. So, despite the overwhelming strength of his opponent, he always seemed to be one step ahead of Tribe Chief Rokar.</p>
<p>Though he couldn’t use it continuously, using it occasionally was proving to be a tremendous advantage.</p>
<p>General Xant, on the other hand, was leading his army to decimate the ranks of the alliance forces, causing some even to begin fleeing.</p>
<p>He used just a single attack to kill the General from the Dreadclaw army. It was a mistake on that general’s part for freezing when he should have been decisive.</p>
<p>This gave General Xant the chance to charge toward even those fleeing.</p>
<p>Knowing that the cause of their land’s decay was this very alliance, the Bloodvein warriors weren’t letting them escape so easily.</p>
<p>With their leaders being held back, it was only a matter of time before the alliance army was completely leaderless.</p>
