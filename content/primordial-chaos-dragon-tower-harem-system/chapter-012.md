<p>Inside a makeshift apartment, a man could be seen sitting behind a flameless cauldron, looking rather happy for obvious reasons. He appeared to have achieved something extraordinary, but if one looked closely, one could tell he was dead.</p>
<p>Yes... the dude is dead. This man is called James Hogan; he is an alchemist in Caprath City, one of the major cities in the Althea Kingdom. In Caprath City, James is known as the shameless alchemist.</p>
<p>He is brilliant but incredibly shameless. No matter the situation he finds himself in, he always has a way out. He had been like that for many years until, finally, his mischief caught up with him.</p>
<p>In a few days, he would be selected among a few alchemists to go and see if they could cure the sick daughter of a merchant.</p>
<p>James, who might have gotten a hint of this, was eager to get what he wanted. The merchant would promise that whoever was able to cure his daughter would get to marry her. Though nobody knew, James—sneaky as ever—managed to get wind of it.</p>
<p>And because of this, he started preparing for the event. However, due to his haste and eagerness to please, he overexerted himself and died before he could finish concocting the sex-enhancement pill he had planned to use.</p>
<p>One could say he died planning to make someone’s daughter beg her for more. He planned to go down on her hard.</p>
<p>He looked happy when he died, meaning he had succeeded. Inside the cauldron, one could see the pill sitting peacefully in there.</p>
<p>Suddenly, the space cracked inside the room, and a young man with whitish-purple hair fell out. Kent landed on the floor just a few meters from James Hogan. He passed out.</p>
<p>It took a few hours, but gradually, he opened his eyes, taking in the new environment he had been thrown into. After adjusting his vision for a few moments, he sighed and then stood up.</p>
<p>His gaze immediately locked onto James, and then, like a flood, he sensed a load of memories pour into his mind.</p>
<p>"James Hogan, huh... So that was the backstory she had prepared for me," Kent said, shaking his head. He received the memories of James, which meant he now knew a lot about the city and kingdom he had been transported to.</p>
<p>"I guess she did plan everything..." Kent sighed as he walked closer to James. However, before he could approach, messages started ringing in his mind.</p>
<p>"Here we go," he sighed again, sitting down beside the dead James, who held a smile on his face even in his last moments.</p>
<p>Kent’s attention immediately focused on the messages that had appeared, neatly arranged by the Tower.</p>
<p>[Congratulations on completing your first quest by making the Goddess of Hate and Resentment smile.]</p>
<p>[Rewards received. You can take out the rewards by accessing your Tower storage. Current items in storage: Primordial Alchemy Records, Primordial Genesis Sword Art, Infinity Cultivation Art.]</p>
<p>[You have already received the Primordial Chaos Dragon Legacy and Unlocked Primordial Chaos Dragon Harem System.]</p>
<p>[Congratulations on completing your second quest. You have received 10,000 Tower Points. These points can be used to purchase items from the Tower Store.]</p>
<p>[The store will be unlocked when you break through to the Root Grandmaster stage.]</p>
<p>"Hmm, I guess the things I wrote in my movies aren’t far-fetched," Kent muttered, then returned to the messages. He had completed three quests, after all.</p>
<p>[Congratulations on completing your third quest. You have received 50,000 Tower Points and two unique SSS-Rank Skills.]</p>
<p>"SSS-Rank skills—that’s wild. I wonder what they are," Kent thought, wanting to check them immediately, but the next set of messages caught his attention. He set the skills aside for now and focused on the other messages.</p>
<p>[Congratulations on adding the first member to your Harem. You have unlocked some features and benefits.]</p>
<p>[Features]</p>
<p>- Telepathy: You can telepathically communicate with the members of your harem. This feature also extends to your harem members; they can communicate with you and among themselves.</p>
<p>- Bestow: You can bestow skills and techniques to your harem members. At your current level, you can bestow only one skill. As you level up, you will be able to add more.</p>
<p>- Take: Besides the random skill that is obtained after adding a member to your harem, you can also access their skill page and take two more skills. This number will increase as you continue to level up.</p>
<p>[Note: More features will be added as you rise in ranks.]</p>
<p>[Benefits]</p>
<p>- Your soul grows stronger as you add more members to your harem. This benefit arises from the link formed between you and your partners.</p>
<p>- You earn part of the spiritual qi absorbed by your harem members as they cultivate.</p>
<p>"Well, that’s great, I guess," Kent smiled. "Does this mean my soul strength has become stronger now that Vexthra is in my harem?"</p>
<p>"Scratch that, telepathy—does it mean I can talk to her now? How does it work, Tower?" Kent asked, eager to see if he could reach his sugar goddess.</p>
<p>[The Master can indeed contact Goddess Vexthra, but due to the realm difference, the connection can hold for only 5 minutes every 100 days. However, as the Master ascends to higher realms, this connection will last much longer.]</p>
<p>"That’s awesome. Can I contact her now? If so, how?" Kent asked again.</p>
<p>[Yes... You must first focus on the connection formed between you. Just concentrate, and you will sense it.]</p>
<p>Kent closed his eyes and focused. Almost instantly, he tapped into the connection, making his smile bloom.</p>
<p>Somewhere in the Realm of Hatred and Resentment.</p>
<p>Vexthra, who was sitting on her bed reading a novel, suddenly felt a sudden jolt coming from below her belly. She looked down, only to see a tower tattoo appear there. It was a puzzling sight, but before she could wonder what was happening, a voice entered her mind, making her eyes widen.</p>
<p>&lt;My sugar goddess, can you hear me?&gt;</p>
<p>"Kent..." Vexthra called out.</p>
<p>&lt;The one and only. Told you it wouldn’t be long before hear from me,&gt; Kent bragged.</p>
<p>"How is this possible? You are in the Mortal Realm, and I am in the God Realm. This shouldn’t be possible. How are you contacting me?" Vexthra asked.</p>
<p>&lt;I have a tower,&gt; Kent replied.</p>
<p>&lt;By the way, we can only talk for five minutes every 100 days. So I just wanted to check whether it works, and it does, so for the next 100 days, we can talk for five minutes. We’ve already spent 40 seconds, so I’ll be hanging up now.</p>
<p>And hey, thanks for sending me to a dead alchemist, very creative&gt; Kent added smiling.</p>
<p>"You needed a backstory, so I gave you one. The unknown disciple of a dead alchemist is the perfect backstory," Vexthra said with a smile.</p>
<p>&lt;I’ll talk to you later, Vex. For now, let me settle down and get my affairs in order. These five minutes can’t be wasted... Bye.&gt;</p>
<p>Kent cut the connection, leaving a smile on Vexthra’s face. Kent had left her not long ago, but she was already starting to miss him.</p>
<p>One might think that for someone who had lived for millions of years, missing someone would be the last thing on her mind. But, well, she was a different person now, thanks to Kent bringing life back into her lifeless eyes.</p>
<p>Back in the makeshift apartment in Sonox, Kent returned to the messages. Most were about what to do next to unlock the towers. When he was done with them, he accessed the SSS-ranked skills.</p>
