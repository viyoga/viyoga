<p>Kent didn’t know how long had passed. However, when he opened his eyes again, he saw himself in the harem tower, lying comfortably on the love bed.</p>
<p>Immediately, he knew what was about to happen, so he teleported and appeared inside the sword tower just in time for a powerful sword energy to explode out of his body.</p>
<p>In that instant, he sensed the very air around him had become razor-sharp, almost as if the air had turned into countless tiny swords.</p>
<p>It felt rather overwhelming and a bit chilling, considering the energy in the air is merely Heavenly sword energy.</p>
<p>He is still one step away from unlocking Divine sword energy. So, for heavenly sword energy to be this powerful, one can only wonder just what kind of monstrous talent Kent has.</p>
<p>As the minutes passed, the feeling grew sharper and sharper until it ended three days later. Once that ended, Kent sighed, and then he started what he had been waiting for from the very moment he started recalling Jest memories.</p>
<p>He commenced the formation and awakening of his sword realm. The moment he began, a raging sea emerged, filling the sword tower.</p>
<p>If one looks from a higher perspective, they would see water raging like a storm. However, in reality, it was countless swords fused so closely together that they appeared like a sea.</p>
<p>It was the sword realm that once made Jest the most dangerous swordsman to ever live. Unlike many swordsmen whose conceptual sword-realm frameworks manifested as a static realm, his was forged purely from his sword energy.</p>
<p>This means that what appears to be water is, in reality, his sword energy. Every last sword was his sword energy manifested into a realm.</p>
<p>This was why, as he grew stronger, his sword realm continued to expand, be reshaped, and augmented over and over again.</p>
<p>Also, since his sword realm came into being through his sword energy, the stronger he becomes, the more dangerous it becomes.</p>
<p>Basically, as he learned new things, he could continue to transform his sword realm.</p>
<p>There was no limit to what he could do with it.</p>
<p>So watching as his sword realm came into being yet again made him smile.</p>
<p>The sea continued to surge for a couple of hours before it subsided.</p>
<p>It was then that Kent breathed a sigh of relief.</p>
<p>With his sword realm awakened successfully, he could finally beat his chest and say he was ready for whatever would come against him.</p>
<p>He may not be a sword Celestial yet, but he has that knowledge, so he is as dangerous as they come.</p>
<p>"Tower, tell me we all made it out alive." The last thing he remembered was telling a certain bald kid to kill the Celestial who attacked him.</p>
<p>Since he was with Lene, Fallon, and Vika, he had to ask.</p>
<p>Thankfully, Tower’s response put his mind at ease. He had also just recovered all of Jest’s memories, and what he saw finally told him what he needed to know.</p>
<p>His hatred for Inaya was completely washed away. In his memories, he saw the impossible decision he had to make and the pain that decision had caused.</p>
<p>However, now that he had recalled his memories, learning that Inaya was still alive made him very happy. That was the outcome he wanted, and seeing it come to pass made him feel at peace.</p>
<p>However, with his memories recalled in full, he finally discovered just how harrowing his heart demon really was.</p>
<p>The crazy redhead was Dia’s incarnation. Believing Chaos was the cause of Artemis’ death led her to hate him until her hatred for him turned into his curse.</p>
<p>During the primordial era, she promised to follow him for all eternity. She indeed meant that, and after seeing what she was capable of, Kent believed that in this life, he wouldn’t let her have the upper hand.</p>
<p>Already, he managed to locate Dia early, so if Inaya could help him get Safira back from Amon’s Guillotine Cave, the decision he had to make in the past wouldn’t be repeated in this life, too.</p>
<p>"Tower, how long was I out for?"</p>
<p>[The master was out for two months outside of time.]</p>
<p>"That is roughly three years of tower time." Kent sighed and pulled up the system interface to read the messages he had received.</p>
<p>[Congratulations, you are now a Sword Emperor.]</p>
<p>[Congratulations, you have successfully awakened your sword realm.]</p>
<p>[Because of how deep your understanding of sword energy is, you have awakened True Heavenly Sword Energy.]</p>
<p>[You have unlocked a new sword skill: Heaven Sword Shower.]</p>
<p>[Heaven Sword Shower]</p>
<p>[Rank: Supreme]</p>
<p>[Description: You can form countless swords using sword aura and imbue them with sword intent. Every sword imbued with sword intent will have a 10,000% increase in damage output.]</p>
<p>Kent grinned wildly, "This is better than any of the sword techniques I have used in the past."</p>
<p>Already, his sword realm allows him to transform sword energy into real swords. Now, he will not only be able to form the swords, but each will also have a 10,000% increase in damage output.</p>
<p>That, added to the fact that he can exert his will in his sword realm, made it so much more powerful.</p>
<p>"I will have so much fun with this skill," he said as he moved ahead with scrolling through the messages he had received.</p>
<p>Most of them had to do with achieving what many swordsmen dreamt of.</p>
<p>True Sword Energy.</p>
<p>Because he was only at the Sword Emperor stage, he only had access to Heavenly Sword Energy. However, with him ascending to True Heavenly Sword Energy, there was just no telling what he could do.</p>
<p>He had not reached the stage where, with just his sword energy alone, he could do more damage without having to activate his other techniques.</p>
<p>Kent proceeded to review the requirements he needed to meet before he could ascend to the sword divine stage.</p>
<p>[Requirements to become a sword divine]</p>
<p>↠ Attain Absolute Heavenly Sword Energy</p>
<p>↠ Kill the Crimson Blood Demon General</p>
<p>Kent narrowed his eyes at the two requirements.</p>
<p>The first was something he had heard about but had never achieved. Back when he attained True Sword Energy, he learned that there is another realm beyond that called Absolute. However, neither he nor anybody he had ever met managed to achieve that.</p>
<p>In fact, he never believed there to be something like that until he glimpsed the realm above the sword celestial stage. That was when he believed it.</p>
<p>But even so, he could not attain that until his last breath. Now, before he could attain the sword divine stage, he must first touch that realm.</p>
<p>While he loves a good challenge, being asked to do something he did not know how to start was a bit more than he could handle.</p>
<p>As for the second challenge, he would have to meet the person he had to kill before he could decide whether or not he had what it takes to kill them.</p>
<p>"This is merely a challenge. I will handle it with ease, and when the time comes, I will handle this demon general like he were a bug," Kent laughed and stood up.</p>
<p>He knew nothing came easy, but for now, with his memories of Jest fully recovered, he not only had the knowledge but also the experience to back it up.</p>
<p>He had seen thousands of battles that Jest had fought and experienced every crashing moment of them. So it was safe to say he was now on the same level of experience as the greatest swordsman to ever live.</p>
<p>The only limiting factor was the fact that he had a much weaker body. His body was akin to a True Immortal. While that was good enough, he could not perform some maneuvers that the old Jest could.</p>
<p>"Ah, what am I even worried about? At the end of the day, it is all about how much better I am and how much weaker my opponent is." He sighed one more time and decided to go see his wives.</p>
<p>But first, he returned to the harem tower to freshen up.</p>
