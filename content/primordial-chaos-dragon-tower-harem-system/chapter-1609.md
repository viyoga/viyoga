<p>Reading the reward for the quest again, Kent could still not believe his eyes.</p>
<p>Clearly, he must be seeing it wrong.</p>
<p>One of the overpowered changes that occurred within his soul sea after he broke through to the Immortal stage was the 24 coffins said to hold the bodies of the 24 Grand Shamans of the Unknown Universe.</p>
<p>According to what Kent already knew, thanks to recovering the Ruthless Bandit’s memories, when the Unknown Universe formed, it brought forth 24 Grand Shamans, beings of unknown measure.</p>
<p>The Queen of Evil knew little about them. But according to her, these beings had already reached the rank of Ancient Evil, two ranks above her.</p>
<p>Kent is only at the first rank, which is the True Evil rank. However, after this rank, there is the Great Evil, Cursed Evil, Unholy Evil, Tyrant Evil, Supreme Evil, and finally Ancient Evil.</p>
<p>---</p>
<p>"These 24 Elders were the ones who carved the foundation of my race and protected us for billions of years before that ambitious Evil King colluded with the creatures of the beyond, sealed them, and banished them.</p>
<p>If one day you come across just a fragment of their legacy, thank your lucky stars. I cannot even begin to tell you what I, a Tyrant Evil, am capable of, much less an Ancient Evil."</p>
<p>---</p>
<p>Those were the words the Queen of Evil said to the Ruthless Bandit when he asked about the Grand Shamans. Now, Kent not only has the coffins of all 24 of them, but in a couple of weeks, he might just end up awakening one of them.</p>
<p>’I don’t even know how to feel right now,’ Kent said inwardly, a part of him feeling very impatient.</p>
<p>He had just read that the hierarchy he created before leaving the Star Realm had shattered. Initially, he believed the Evil King’s influence still remained in all Three Heavens.</p>
<p>However, it became clear to him when he discovered that only the Skull Race, now an evil race, still had him in their hearts. The rest in all seven gateways had abandoned him. Even in the first heavens, only one race still held him in their heart.</p>
<p>This meant the second and third heavens had long since abandoned him and his rule.</p>
<p>A part of him wanted to let them be. However, after he rationalized and started thinking straight, he knew that leaving them to do as they pleased would be bad for the outside universe.</p>
<p>The star beasts aren’t dead. Even now, the Infinity Universe is being invaded from time to time as the seals that bind them in the star gateway weaken. If that is allowed to continue, in a few years’ time, the Infinity Universe would be invaded again, and the entire universe would plunge into chaos.</p>
<p>He doesn’t want that to happen, not when the Dark Queen, perhaps now an Empress, and her fiends, among other horrors, remain at large.</p>
<p>So his soul warriors are making sure all-star beasts in the Lower Heaven are dealt with and converted into beings he would have absolute control over.</p>
<p>The only problem was that once they conquered the Lower Heaven, moving to the High Heaven and the Supreme Heaven would be difficult.</p>
<p>He doesn’t have the power to face gods, Supremes, and Celestials at the moment. There is a chance he may even encounter Pseudo-Primordials.</p>
<p>He and his subordinates cannot handle those kinds of odds.</p>
<p>However, everything changes if he were to awaken a Grand Shaman and put him or her in charge of conquering the Star Realm and other banished realms like the Tormented Chaos Abyss.</p>
<p>’The possibilities are endless.’</p>
<p>Kent made a fist and smiled. He looked at the quest again.</p>
<p>’24 forbidden beasts, huh. I already have Kharvok, meaning there are 23 more to go. As for the others, I would surely be happy to commit a massacre. After all, I need blood Essence.’ Kent formed some plans in his head, and a roadmap was drawn.</p>
<p>’Tower, purchase Bloodline Tempering Pills and Foundation Strengthening Pills for four people.’ Kent planned on making his four Grim-contracted beasts strong enough to carry out a mission for him.</p>
<p>Plans had changed, and so he must adapt.</p>
<p>[This might be the best time to tell the master that he owes me billions in Tower Points.]</p>
<p>’What? When did I use my 97 billion Tower Points?’ Kent’s eyes widened. Before leaving for the mission, he checked his Tower Points, and he had close to a hundred billion.</p>
<p>So far, he had used less than a million. So where did the rest go?</p>
<p>[Goddess Vexthra and the other legion members made some purchases that involved borrowing from me. Since what they required was important, I allowed them to owe me. Now, I believe the master owes me 112 billion Tower Points.</p>
<p>However, since the master needs these pills, I will add them to his tab, and when the master is out of here, we will make arrangements on how to pay me back.]</p>
<p>Kent could only remain seated there, his expression a bit dark. He wasn’t angry, just worried that something major might have happened, and his wives had no choice but to spend that much on a treasure.</p>
<p>’I need to hurry,’</p>
<p>Taking a deep breath, he told Tower to purchase the pills. Afterward, he handed them over to the four demigods with whom he had signed the Grim Contract.</p>
<p>"Eat these pills. When you’re done, I have missions for you."</p>
<p>They nodded and started to cultivate the pills.</p>
<p>Since it had come down to this, Kent knew he had to assign one of the quests to these Grim-contracted beasts. Since he must handle both the forbidden and abominations, he knew he had to assign the rest of the demigods to his other contracted beasts.</p>
<p>At the end of the day, it was either they submit or they die. Kent didn’t want them to die, but he also trusted and believed that even if they died, they would still be his.</p>
<p>His Necromancy class had been dormant for a while now.</p>
<p>It wasn’t because he couldn’t create more undead; it was just that he had no reason to do so. The Planet Killer Dungeon wasn’t difficult enough to pose a threat to his legion, so he lets them be.</p>
<p>Now, however, Kent sensed something bad was about to happen, and so he was being pushed to amass an army.</p>
<p>Before coming to the Star Realm, he believed he was here to fight his way to Elina. While that was true, he now realized there was a much bigger threat he had to handle first.</p>
<p>’I don’t know what is coming, but I pray that when it arrives, I will have the strength and army to handle it.’</p>
<p>Kent turned toward Kharvok and saw he was already halfway through his innate body awakening. Once he is done, his strength would double.</p>
<p>Kent planned to go with him to hunt down the other forbidden and make them his contracted beasts. Once that is handled, he will proceed to the Central Region and deal with the demon there.</p>
<p>Becoming a Sword Divine requires him to attain Absolute Heavenly Sword Energy and kill the Crimson Blood Demon General. Attaining the Absolute Heavenly Sword Energy is possible and quite easy, really.</p>
<p>He had witnessed someone use it before during his life as the Ruthless Bandit. So he would be learning it before meeting that demon.</p>
<p>Once he defeats it and becomes a Sword Divine, the four abominations would be next. Since they are a terror, per what Yggzrael said, then he must prepare well to meet them.</p>
<p>Kent moved away from his beast, currently cultivating, and retrieved a small black pagoda. It was very small; however, Kent knew it had to be awakened first before it would function.</p>
<p>’Tower...’</p>
<p>[The master is required to add a drop of his blood essence to awaken the Undead Pagoda.]</p>
