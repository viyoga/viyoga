<p>"Sounds Serious," Kent said, hearing what Ignarok said about the Beasts Kingdom.</p>
<p>"Very serious. The Beasts Kingdom is not a place just any beast can go to. I learned that the weakest beasts there are all divine beasts with so much potential.</p>
<p>And as beasts straight from beasts that once followed the Divine Beast Tamer, you can only imagine the kind of monsters they are." Ignarok is happy, and the other six monarchs, including Sylvara, are also happy.</p>
<p>This made Kent sigh with happiness as well. He knew that, with their positive attitude today, they would like their new home—the Beasts Kingdom—and thrive there.</p>
<p>"How about Salem? Does anyone have any idea what that star system is like?"</p>
<p>Zeraphine nodded, "I only heard of it once at an auction. But apparently, it is a star system dominated by Humans, Elves, and Dwarves.</p>
<p>It is one of the strongest star systems, housing powerful immortal worlds. That is all I know."</p>
<p>"Thank you, Zeraphine." Kent turns and looks at Nefeli...</p>
<p>"It is just a star system. There are many of them out there, so once you reach Salem, you can seek directions to a more suitable world. I know you would prefer to live in a world where the Pyris Pill Alliance is... so find your own way when you get there."</p>
<p>She nodded.</p>
<p>"All of you are the same. Once you get there and you feel like exploring other galaxies, just go for it. However, be sure to keep in touch with each other to know how life is treating each other."</p>
<p>They nodded.</p>
<p>Kent then retrieved a scroll and handed it over to Pill Monarch Nefeli... "There are recipes for the Limit Breaker Pill and Soul Strengthening Pill.</p>
<p>I won’t have time to make enough pills for everyone, so you, Yuna, and Flura will have to work on concocting them.</p>
<p>Also, since Lady Wu is in the auction business, she can get you the ingredients much faster and at a cheaper price."</p>
<p>They nodded again.</p>
<p>After Kent spent more time telling everyone his plans, the meeting came to an end, and Kent immediately went back to concocting more pills.</p>
<p>He had already given all the flame users a High-Grade Primordial Flame Seed and also gave Sylvara the Taming Bracelet. He can’t take them to the future, so he can only leave the one thing that will keep them safe in the hands of his trusted wife.</p>
<p>Hours passed in the blink of an eye.</p>
<p>Kent worked on so many pills that when he finally stopped, everyone received one Soul-Strengthening Pill and three Limit Breaker Pills.</p>
<p>In addition to that, he had also given one Innate Talent Awakening Pill each to everyone, allowing the beasts to finally awaken their Innate Beast Bodies and the humans to awaken innate talents, too.</p>
<p>This brought him great joy.</p>
<p>Knowing his subordinates are now more powerful than before made him feel free.</p>
<p>They will thrive, and one day, he will get to meet them again.</p>
<p>Aside from that, he and Pill King Flura had a heart-to-heart, and, just like Nefeli, she wanted to wait until they met again.</p>
<p>But it wasn’t just her; even Zeraphine, the Lotus Flame Monarch, and Vey, the Phoenix Monarch, all showed their desire to become his woman. However, they want to wait until they meet again in the future.</p>
<p>Kent promised them he would surely spend quality time with all of them when they meet in the future.</p>
<p>He is already looking forward to meeting them again.</p>
<p>He spent some time drawing a map for the store he promised Veth.</p>
<p>Surprisingly, Lady Wu decided to take on the cost of building it and even promised to run advertisements during the annual auction, which is 14 days away.</p>
<p>Once everything was set and Kent had only a few minutes to leave, he called everyone to one last meeting.</p>
<p>"Remember, if in the future you were to hear of me, prioritize your safety first before anyone discovers you are all related to me. Trust me, a lot of danger surrounds me, and I wouldn’t want any of you to be dragged into it."</p>
<p>They nodded.</p>
<p>Kent knew his name had already spread in the future. He was already aware that immortals and even gods were after his trail, so he didn’t want his future investment to be wasted, not when he knew he would be fine regardless of who and what came after him.</p>
<p>He wanted them to be safe, and now that he had their confirmation, he knew he could leave in peace.</p>
<p>⟦The master will return in five minutes⟧</p>
<p>Kent spent the next few minutes discussing some random things with them. When his time had finally come, he smiled as his body turned into sparks of light and vanished.</p>
<p>When he was gone, everyone remained in the room for a few minutes. After they managed to calm their hearts down, Nefeli spoke...</p>
<p>"We first have to build a solid foundation in this star system, and to do that, we need to make Veth the biggest pill seller in the whole of the Ten Pill Worlds...</p>
<p>Kent has left us with so many pill recipes that even the Pill Worlds haven’t seen before. If we can make her become that powerful, then we can easily eradicate all those in higher positions who dare pollute this star system.</p>
<p>Also, those of us who still haven’t broken the 30th limit must work harder to accomplish that before ascending.</p>
<p>I plan for all of us to be ready to ascend in 10,000 years’ time. I know being an immortal sounds good, but we must be ready before ascending.</p>
<p>Lastly, we must also make Planet Nictus very powerful, and to achieve that, we must make Princess Autumn a powerful Queen, and if possible, an Empress."</p>
<p>Everyone nodded to her words.</p>
<p>Even Ignarok, the strongest of them, decided to listen to Nefeli’s arrangement.</p>
<p>Kent had left her in charge since she was much more experienced.</p>
<p>After they finished planning, they started work.</p>
<p>The first five years after Kent left, the Dream Pill Store was established, and only Legendary Pills were sold there.</p>
<p>Ten years after he was gone, the Dream Pill Store had opened more branches in all Pill Worlds.</p>
<p>Also, Planet Nictus became a powerful world after the other worlds, once under the control of the Planet King, decided to swear their allegiance to Queen Autumn, who three years later became Empress Autumn.</p>
<p>A hundred years later, Lady Wu bought the Brown Maple Auction House.</p>
<p>A thousand years after she bought the auction house, she finally acquired all the shares from all the shareholders, making her the sole owner.</p>
<p>Five thousand years later, Planet Nictus now had over 50 worlds under it, making Empress Autumn the strongest person in the star system.</p>
<p>Within those five thousand years, the evil sect had been eradicated thanks to the chilling efforts of Ignarok and Nefeli.</p>
<p>Also, within that same five thousand years, Nefeli became a Pill Legend, and even Flura reached the Pill Sovereign stage.</p>
<p>They later spent another 5,000 years cementing their foundation before they ascended.</p>
<p>Every last beast went to the Beasts Kingdom, while the Humans and Elves went to Salem. However, every ten thousand years, Empress Autumn returns to Planet Nictus to ensure her legacy still stands.</p>
<p>Of course, she knew nobody would dare touch her empire; however, as an excuse, she did this simply because she believed one day, Kent would come back, and she wanted to be around to welcome him.</p>
