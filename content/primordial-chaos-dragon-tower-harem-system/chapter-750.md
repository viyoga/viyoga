<p>The Ancient Sea Race has only ever welcomed one race, and that was the humans. Of course, not all humans were welcomed; however, they once opened their doors to the Wynthralls, the then-life magicians of the human race.</p>
<p>It was safe to say they only ever saw the Wynthralls, as the only worthy race they considered equals.</p>
<p>However, after the battle of parting, they separated from the rest of the world and never interacted with the outside world ever again</p>
<p>But while they never came out, they surely knew everything that was going on outside.</p>
<p>Naturally, this was possible because of the hand.</p>
<p>Years back, when the hand was established, they came into an agreement with the Sea race. They will provide them with information in exchange for a space to set up their headquarters on the Black Sea.</p>
<p>This was received well by the Sea Race and since then, they have had a cordial relationship with the hand. However, they never even bother to come out and see things for themselves.</p>
<p>They saw the races in the outside world as worthless.</p>
<p>However, things have changed now. This change came when the hand informed them of a name and a reason to be wary of that name.</p>
<p>Kent Madson.</p>
<p>They didn’t think much of him at first, but they do now, for they had just received two reasons to... even more reasons, in fact.</p>
<p>A few days ago, Thalorin, the outer sea guardian, came with some news and a gift. When Kent was passing through to the half-elves’ kingdom, the dragon that blocked him and tried to kill him did as Kent told him to.</p>
<p>So he reported to his leaders, and while he was scolded, he wasn’t killed like he had expected. Of course, his punishment wouldn’t free him from his duties anytime soon. But even so, he was happy he wasn’t killed.</p>
<p>When he relayed the message to his leaders, a meeting that hadn’t taken place in thousands of years was soon to happen.</p>
<p>This meeting would bring together the leaders of the seven sea kingdoms to discuss the recent events. Of course, while it may sound ominous, the meeting was actually only to see if they could gain something from getting to know Kent Madson.</p>
<p>However, a few hours before the meeting, they received different news, causing the meeting to be rescheduled for the next day.</p>
<p>This time, instead of just the seven leaders, the leader of all seven kingdoms, Ocean Master Levaithan, joined them for the news they received was one they never expected.</p>
<p>---</p>
<p>[Arc Dome - Ancient Leviathan City]</p>
<p>Arc Dome is a city approximately 45 kilometers in diameter. It is the home of the Ocean Master, the only survivor of the ancient Ocean Leviathans in the world of Sonox.</p>
<p>In the ancient Sea Race, he is the strongest and oversees the protection of all seven kingdoms. Of course, after the battle of parting, he hadn’t really done anything aside from rebuilding and firming his foundation for the past 25 thousand years.</p>
<p>He participated in the Battle of Parting, and while many living in the world had also participated in that war, Lord Leviathan was a Peak core formation expert at the time.</p>
<p>He led the Sea Race in the battle, and to this day, his legend still echoes in the deeper reaches of the world. He fought beside the Humans, and when the war was over, he retreated to the Arc Dome, only coming out to explore once every five centuries.</p>
<p>But today, he came out and was about to meet to discuss the future of the Sea Race, for while Kent Madson is a threat, the news they received on the Half-Elves told them something different.</p>
<p>Inside a large hall, built entirely from glass and jade, eight people were seated. Seated at the main chair is a man clad in dark robes. He is sitting in a sleek throne made using the finest wood and polished using star dust.</p>
<p>He appeared to be in his late 40s, and judging by his posture, he had some muscle tone. He is no weakling.</p>
<p>Lord Levaithan has no aura around him. It was as if he were the weakest person one would ever meet. If one doesn’t know him, they would think he isn’t even a cultivator, for his aura has been refined to the point he could control it at will.</p>
<p>He has silver hair, handsome facial features, and intimidating red eyes.</p>
<p>But he wasn’t the only one with no aura in the hall. Sitting on both right and left, away from his throne, are the seven leaders of the sea race.</p>
<p>After Lord Leviathan, they are the next strongest of the sea race.</p>
<p>Lord Levaithan sat there calmly for a few minutes before he started to speak.</p>
<p>"We all know why we are here, so let’s get this over with." The seven leaders nodded,</p>
<p>"My lord, we received words from our spies in the outside world. It appeared the surface world is about to grow stronger if we dont step in.</p>
<p>According to the reports, they are now in possession of several treasures that we have no idea where they originated from.</p>
<p>I dont know if these reports are exaggerated, but judging by how it was sent, I have all the reasons to believe the surface world would grow too powerful soon."</p>
<p>"You said nothing concrete, King Zephyr. What had they gotten that made you think they could surpass us? Bear in mind, aside from the Supreme Immortal Sect, we are the only race with a connection to other worlds.</p>
<p>When it comes to resources, we have more than enough, so why would they threaten our standing as the strongest in the world?"</p>
<p>Lord Zephyr paled a little when the ocean master questioned him. Lord Zephyr is the king of the Manta Rays.</p>
<p>They are known for their combat strength, for when it comes to those with a natural affinity for lightning, they are the best in the world.</p>
<p>In fact, during the battle of parting, the Manta Rays led the charge and fought bravely like the warriors they were.</p>
<p>King Zephyr himself can be considered the third strongest in the sea race. But even he paled when he was questioned directly by the Ocean Master.</p>
<p>Just a few words from him were enough to cause someone who had refined all seven cores 12 years ago to turn pale.</p>
<p>King Zaphyr quickly made his report vivid and concise.</p>
<p>"The report shows they have treasures like an immortal grade Ambrosia apple, 10,000-year-old Spirit Grape Fruit, Soul Cleansing Fruits, and more.</p>
<p>Naturally, all this can be purchased at the alliance headquarters; however, what called for this meeting was the discovery that they have a mysterious alchemist who can concoct a Tier 8 Innate Talent awakening Pill..."</p>
<p>"What did you just say?"</p>
<p>Lord Levaithan, who had been resting on his throne, suddenly lifted his back and questioned in a tone that made all seven Sea Leaders pale immediately.</p>
