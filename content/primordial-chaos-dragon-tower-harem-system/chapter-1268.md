<p>Kent circles in the air for a few minutes, getting used to the Jade Void Wings. They are like a new extension of his body, so he wants to feel it naturally first before unleashing their true power.</p>
<p>After getting a feel of it, he landed slowly on the ground, a beaming smile painting his face.</p>
<p>"This is amazing. I can choose which wing to use," he muttered.</p>
<p>Before fusing with the wings, he was concerned about something in both good and bad ways.</p>
<p>He knew fusing with the wings meant making them a part of him. He was okay with that. After all, Vexthra and the tower told him that the moment he was done fusing with the wings, his speed would skyrocket.</p>
<p>That was a good thing considering the kind of missions he has ahead of him. However, Kent couldn’t help wondering about the implications.</p>
<p>Vexthra said she had read in some records that relate to the [Jade Void Wings.] From what she said, the Jade Void Wing is a treasure even celestials will kill for.</p>
<p>It is a universal treasure that all would desire to have.</p>
<p>So if fusing with it makes it his wings, wouldn’t that become a problem when someone very dangerous were to recognise it?</p>
<p>Kent is not stupid. He knew even with Vexthra’s help, he wasn’t invincible. Vexthra already told him that even she could be in danger when the right warrior came around.</p>
<p>So displaying too much to attract more attention when he already has many eyes on him isn’t something he can take in easily.</p>
<p>In short, the three pairs of wings that he had fused with are Universal Treasures that celestials would love to rip from his back with their bare hands and make them their own.</p>
<p>Thankfully and luckily, now that he had fused with the wings, he saw that his worry was unwarranted.</p>
<p>"There are three natures of my wings now." Kent spread the Jade Void Wings and let a wave of vibration buzz out. This form is the Jade Void Wing True form.</p>
<p>It is dark, with a silver metallic sheen. Void runes littered its surface, glowing with power.</p>
<p>However, when he willed it to disappear, it did, and then his own dragon wings appeared. In this form, he maintains his true dragon wings.</p>
<p>In that form, he is already faster. And if he were to add the [Flame Veins] and harness the power of his [Flame Wheels], his speed would be several times faster.</p>
<p>However, Kent wasn’t aiming for that. He smiled and activated the third nature of his wings.</p>
<p>This time, when the void wing appeared, it pressed and merged with the dragon wings instantly, causing the void to tremble.</p>
<p>In no time, three pairs of dark wings lined with complex golden runes and engravings appeared, causing a grin to appear on Kent’s face.</p>
<p>"Now, this is what I call wings," he muttered, feeling an untapped power coursing through his wings. The feeling is just too electric to ignore.</p>
<p>Kent could tell that if his speed in both the [Jade Void Wings] and his own [dragon wings] could match that of a Spirit Immortal, then when they merged, not even a peak Spirit Immortal could see him coming.</p>
<p>Of course, he isn’t saying that to look down on immortals. Kent is already aware that some cultivate just speed and flight as their combat style. Such people could give him a run for his money.</p>
<p>However, the speed in him now gives him the right to place himself in the ranks of a Spirit Immortal in terms of speed.</p>
<p>"And the best part, no god, supreme or celestial will recognise the [Jade Void Wings] in this form." That is all he could ask for now. After all, he can tell he is still lacking six more pairs of the Jade Void Wings.</p>
<p>But that is a problem for another day.</p>
<p>Kent smiled and willed the wings to disappear.</p>
<p>Of course, when he unlocks the first form of the wings, after he is done mastering the first form of the [Void Drive] technique, using the wings in their dark silver form would have so much versatility.</p>
<p>However, when he needs to harness his true speed, merging them would be the best move.</p>
<p>That, in a way, is what Kent wanted, and he would be super proud of himself when he faced someone faster than himself, yet with these wings, he would manage to defeat them or escape from them.</p>
<p>"I need to face some danger and see if I can live up to the true nature of the [Jade Void Wings] like the hype it carries."</p>
<p>After making sure he had had enough of the wings, he decided to think of what he planned on doing next.</p>
<p>He is going to the planet Bayla to learn more about his life as the Faceless Assassin and to venture into the Upside Down Underworld Forest.</p>
<p>Naturally, that is now his main objective.</p>
<p>It turned out, aside from just the three beasts he must kill, there is one more he must kill as a requirement to become a Pill Emperor.</p>
<p>"Advancing my pill knowledge is a must. As I draw closer to the Immortal World now, I am sure the mission Vexthra gave me will start to set itself up.</p>
<p>So, I need to prepare. Killing a god isn’t going to be easy, but I must be ready for the day I will get my hands on him."</p>
<p>Kent let his mind calm down a bit, allowing him to think of what he must do first when he gets to Planet Bayla.</p>
<p>"I just need to acquire an underworld pass at the Warrior Tower or the Pill Tower, then I can enter the upside-down underworld forest." Kent smiled and muttered, "It has been billions of years, but I doubt those fuckers will be any less corrupt."</p>
<p>Kent shook his head and wished to be proven wrong. However, if those bastards who think they are better than everyone dare to mess with him, he would probably test a few things on them.</p>
