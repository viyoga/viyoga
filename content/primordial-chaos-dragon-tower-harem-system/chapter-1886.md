<p>The reunion and meeting with Dominique ended with Kent promising to help her kind against the corruption worming its way into the core of the Undead realm.</p>
<p>Of course, he planned to do it in his own way and on his own terms.</p>
<p>He may have entertained the idea of teaming up with the undead monarch, but that is just him wanting to make sure that annoying bastard wouldnt cross him when he travels to the undead realm.</p>
<p>First, since Kent had already planned to go to the Abyssal Chaos for his parents, who had been trapped there since the undead era, he is destined to either come face to face with the new Abyssal Chaos King or atleast a part of his power.</p>
<p>He had no plans to start a massacre there while he hadn’t yet gotten his family out.</p>
<p>Should incase he manage to get them out of the abyssal chaos realm, then he would find a way to either stop the abyssal chaos king or atleast delay him a bit so he can prepare how best to handle it.</p>
<p>Already, Kent has some missions left to handle.</p>
<p>One of those missions had to do with the Flame Demon race. The two black dungeons inside the tower point to the Flame Demon realm and the Ice Demon realm.</p>
<p>The flame demon realm holds the flame demons that attacked the universe during the flame era. They couldn’t defeat them all, so just like he had done in many other eras, the Nine Underworld Flame Blacksmith sealed them away.</p>
<p>The seals are getting weak, and one day, they are destined to return. However, Kent had no plans to welcome them back into this universe, not when a major threat like the void beasts is on their way, whether he likes it or not.</p>
<p>Aside from them, there are also the issues with the Ice demons who attacked the Ice era. Kevin Sage was also forced to seal them, so he had to handle that.</p>
<p>Aiding the Undead Race is merely a courtesy he wishes to render to a friend.</p>
<p>Of course, the second reason why he planned to save them was because of his own gains.</p>
<p>Inasmuch as he would love to raise his shoulders, inflate his ego and say he had nothing to lose, the truth of the matter is he had a alot to lose if the undead realms fell or became corrupted.</p>
<p>His undead legion needs materials to grow, and since he cannot venture into the primordial chaos right now, that is the main hub for him to gather resources.</p>
<p>So he cannot afford to stand by and watch it burn to the ground.</p>
<p>This means, despite having thousands of things to do, he had to factor in some of these secondary yet dreadful missions.</p>
<p>Luckily for him, his legion grows stronger with every battle, so he is prepared to embrace them all. But still, he wishes to handle the issue with the flame demons first before the abyssal chaos and the undead realm.</p>
<p>But even before any of that, he wishes for his swordsmanship and alchemy to rise to atleast the Immortal stage. Kent’s overall power surpasses even Celestials, but he still lacks when it comes to his swordsmanship and alchemy.</p>
<p>He wishes to handle that, and, as it happens, in the months ahead of him, he will be handling some of it.</p>
<p>But even before that, he still has some minor missions, one being spending time with his wives, awakening their true names, upgrading the undead legion, creating five more undead and even refining the materials he planned to use to upgrade the 14 Lost Knights.</p>
<p>’So many things to do but little time to handle all of them,’</p>
<p>Despite having so much time inside the tower, he still seems to have no time at all.</p>
<p>The funny part is, he still has to handle some missions outside the tower, missions that will bring him face-to-face with some friends and allies from his past lives.</p>
<p>In fact, one of them is destined to be executed in the coming days unless Kent steps in to save them...</p>
<p>’First things first, let me handle the most important things first...’ After making sure Dominique and her ancient undead warriors settled in, Kent went to Dia, who had ranked first in kills during the Dark War and started spending time with her.</p>
<p>He had also caught up with his undead legion on how their mission in the undead realm went. Learning what had transpired made Kent sigh and wish he had gone with them.</p>
<p>If he had known the undead monarch wasn’t around, he surely would have gone with them. But then again, he had on his own made so much progress, so missing out on some fun wasn’t too bad.</p>
<p>At the end of the day, Kaizo and his pals managed to bring back the bones he would be using to create the other undead generals.</p>
<p>The plan is to create them, upgrade them using Chaos Materials so they will gain Chaos affinity, send them to the Chaos Chasm to train and prepare for their trip to the Abyssal Chaos.</p>
<p>This time, they are going to dominate, unlike the first time when only one of them went with him. Even back then, Kent had no way of harnessing the abyssal chaos qi there.</p>
<p>This time around, he planned to not only have an easy time but also use the place to temper himself and his undead legion.</p>
<p>He has 12 of them at the moment, and he will soon add 5 more, bringing the total to 17. When the time comes, he would add more, and hopefully, by the time the Undead Realm falls into danger, his undead would be at the stage where they can make a difference in the grand scheme of things.</p>
<p>Kent even received the materials they’d gathered and had begun tempering them within his soul Universe for when he was ready to upgrade them.</p>
<p>Back then, when they all reached the God stage, they awakened mind-bending powers. This time, too, Kent planned to ensure that each of them rose to the Peak of Godhood, or even to early Supremehood.</p>
<p>This way, they can fight even Celestials and not feel much pressure.</p>
<p>But that all can wait. After all, he had some beauties to entertain, and one of them wishes to spend three god months with him.</p>
