<p>"Gaia, Dori, and Akira, you have outdone yourselves this time," Kent said, holding Gaia and Dori in his arms. Akira clung to his neck like a koala bear.</p>
<p>The three giggled, watching the city with their master. Velyra and her sisters were on the ground exploring the city, building to building.</p>
<p>⟪Don’t worry too much, master. We can turn this place into a Celestial world with enough tower points. We have already surpassed the Medium world, so it’s only a matter of time and tower points.⟫</p>
<p>Kent smiled, hearing that. But he couldn’t deny her claims, though. The city indeed is akin to a high world. This means with enough tower points, they can do wonders.</p>
<p>After a while, the ladies returned with excited looks.</p>
<p>"This place is perfect," Velyra smiled.</p>
<p>"In that case, why don’t we go bring your people?" Kent said, and his wives nodded. However, before they could leave, Elaine’s mom and a few ladies in the city flew toward them.</p>
<p>"Kent, we would like to know when our new neighbours will be coming so we can prepare," Lady Rain asked.</p>
<p>"They will be here within the next ten days," Kent responded, and Lady Rain, alongside the ladies, smiled.</p>
<p>"That is enough time to prepare a welcome meal."</p>
<p>Kent and his ladies smiled, hearing that.</p>
<p>"You go ahead and do that, Lady Rain. If you need anything, tell Gaia," Kent said, and the overly cheerful mother-in-law smiled.</p>
<p>"See you all in ten days." Kent waved his hand. The next second, he and his ladies appeared in the air inside the Bloodvein tribe, drawing everyone’s attention.</p>
<p>Some even drew their weapons, but they lowered them when they saw Velyra and Kent.</p>
<p>"It’s the priestess... and our saviour," they exclaimed, looking toward Kent with revered expressions.</p>
<p>"Who are the others...?"</p>
<p>"They look like our saviour... I say they are his friends."</p>
<p>"Dumb head, they clearly are his wives... Can’t you see how they are all around him?"</p>
<p>Kent and his wife’s appearance started to cause a bit of a stir. Thankfully, the tribe chief and the elders were quick to come out.</p>
<p>The moment they appeared, the commotion died down.</p>
<p>"Kent, you are back," Tribe Chief Malthar said with a relieved sigh. Seeing his daughter in one piece made him very happy.</p>
<p>"Let’s go inside first," Tribe Chief Malthar said before turning to his people, "Hurry up with your preparations, we will be migrating in a few hours’ time"</p>
<p>They nodded and went back to their business.</p>
<p>Soon, Kent and his ladies were inside the throne room. Tribe Chief Malthar looked around, and at every turn, his expression changed.</p>
<p>Looking at the aura of the ladies around, Kent told him that if his people were to clash with them, the battle would be over in under an hour.</p>
<p>Kent saw this and smiled.</p>
<p>"Let me introduce them. They are my wives, every last one of them..." He went ahead and made sure every last detail of his wives was captured in his introduction.</p>
<p>When he was done, he turned to Velyra and smiled, "And as it appears, I couldn’t hold back my feelings, so your priestess is now one of my wives."</p>
<p>Velyra covered her face with both her palms, clearly embarrassed by how Kent shamelessly made the introduction speeches. It was like he rehearsed before coming.</p>
<p>’All these amazing women are his wives. Just who is this kid?’ the elders couldn’t help wondering. However, after a few minutes, they returned to normal.</p>
<p>"It looks like our Velyra is all grown now," Elder Farra said, smiling. Elder Ureth and Zarka couldn’t hold back their smiles either.</p>
<p>"Your mother would be proud of you if she were to see you today," Tribe Chief Malthar said, making Velyra tear up. Thankfully, her sisters were there for her.</p>
<p>Kent also knew her mom had died when she was young, so he sighed and just let her sisters handle it. After a few minutes, she recovered and decided to report what she saw to her father and elders.</p>
<p>"The place he is taking us is amazing, Father. It has everything we need and some things we don’t even think we need. The place is so rich in Chaos Qi that reaching the Perfect stage in this life wouldn’t be impossible."</p>
<p>Velyra went on for nearly an hour, ensuring they understood everything by the time she was done.</p>
<p>After hearing everything Velyra said, the tribe chief and the elders were taken aback by how much the place looked.</p>
<p>"Get the people ready, Father; this is a new beginning for us... a new place where we can call home." The elders and Tribe Chief Malthar nodded.</p>
<p>Hearing it from Velyra and Neomi, who they immediately recognised as the Mother of Killers, they knew the place indeed would be best for them.</p>
<p>After a few more questions, Velyra took her sisters away to show them around, leaving only Kent inside with the elders and the tribe chief.</p>
<p>"Looks like you really came to save us. I am grateful." The tribe chief took out a jade box and handed it to Kent, who received it with a smile.</p>
<p>"You not only saved us from our enemies but also from a great disaster. This alone is enough for us to hand over our most prized possession.</p>
<p>All we ask is that in the future, whenever a Bloodvein tribe member is in trouble, you help us help them."</p>
<p>Kent nodded, "Don’t worry, Tribe Chief, I will not stop until I ensure everyone with your bloodline, regardless of where they are, is protected. That is my vow to you, and may the Blood Heart Stone bear witness."</p>
<p>The elders and Tribe Chief Malthar nodded and then excused themselves. Kent stored away the stone and went out.</p>
<p>When he was out of the Bloodvein tribe, he transformed into his demi-dragon form and started flying toward the Dreadclaw tribe.</p>
<p>It took him just an hour to get there. He didn’t want to do it at first, but knowing the friendship between Larkas, Lonki, and Karvas, he wanted to ensure their protection.</p>
<p>He sneakily entered the tribe and left them what he had come to give them. If fate wills, they will meet again.</p>
<p>After that, he came back to the Bloodvein tribe, and hours later, they were ready to start moving.</p>
