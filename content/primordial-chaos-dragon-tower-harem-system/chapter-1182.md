<p>Kent was surprised that on such short notice, Two Pill Legends, three Pill sovereigns, and seven Pill Monarchs that used to be members of the Pyris Pill Alliance would heed his call and come running this quickly.</p>
<p>However, they came, and it was thanks to their love and passion for Alchemy.</p>
<p>Three hundred seventy thousand years ago, Nefeli told him that the Pill Alliances were established to ensure every living soul could buy a pill at affordable prices. Their mission was to use Alchemy to improve the quality of life in the universe.</p>
<p>So when these people were called, they came knowing their vows to make Alchemy first over everything was their strongest suit now.</p>
<p>"Thank you all for coming." Since thousands reported, he chose to meet the top rankers.</p>
<p>"We are glad to be called back here. None of us had dreamed a day like this would ever come," one of the Pill Legends, an Elven lady called Terra, said, her expression calm and collected.</p>
<p>Of course, the happiness in her voice could not be missed. She was darn happy, and everyone could see and also feel it.</p>
<p>I mean, who wouldn’t be happy to see their enemies and oppressors crash beneath the feet of someone claiming to be Kent Madson?</p>
<p>There was no telling whether he was indeed the Kent Madson they had all been looking for, but they were happy he had finally liberated them from their oppressors.</p>
<p>That was just too much for any of them to keep their emotions bottled up.</p>
<p>"Well, we are here now, and the Pill Family is no more. That is more than enough to be happy about. However, we have to ensure the Pill Alliances never die, and that begins with this alliance."</p>
<p>They nodded to Kent’s words and listened to what he had to say.</p>
<p>"Years ago, when Pill Monarch Nefeli added me to the Pyris Pill Alliance, her dream was to create a powerful and quality Pill World where, when it comes to Alchemy, nobody should lack, no matter what they want.</p>
<p>She had done that, and from what I learned, the last six generations had followed in her footsteps. That is enough reason to know it can be done again.</p>
<p>So this time, I plan to personally set up the power dynamics here. I will personally select the leaders and ensure the dream envisioned by your predecessors lives on.</p>
<p>Of course, this time around, I will make sure the Pill Families never happen again. I will make sure, before they even entertain the idea, every last one of them loses their heads."</p>
<p>The Alchemists gathered in the Ninth Level of Hell shuddered upon hearing Kent’s words. They were already afraid of him. However, because this was a safe meeting he called, they were not worried he would snap and kill them.</p>
<p>Even so, they were scared of him, and that was enough reason to tell them how serious this meeting and his words were.</p>
<p>"I have already done my background research on every last one of you, so I will select the next Pill Leader and then set up the structure and power dynamics from there."</p>
<p>Kent went ahead and selected the next Pill Leader for the Pyris Pill Alliance and then started from there. He chose those who would manage the alliance’s various affairs.</p>
<p>This time, instead of giving them pill recipes, he gave them techniques to use. These techniques, of course, came from Nana, who knew everything about Alchemy.</p>
<p>He also implemented some safety measures, most of which were private, making it so that no matter what, the enemy would never see him coming.</p>
<p>Of course, he knew the human mind is susceptible to manipulation and greed, so there is no telling what any of these people would do.</p>
<p>They could one day choose to collude with another Pill Family to cause mayhem here and there, like their predecessors did, causing the deaths of tens of thousands.</p>
<p>However, he had already put measures in place to prevent that.</p>
<p>"Remember, making any attempt to betray this alliance will, if not immediately, then painfully eat at your life and slowly worm its way into your heart, which I promise will be excruciating and painful to the point you will cry, but tears will not come because you have already cried every living tear out of your body.</p>
<p>However, when you think that is all, you will be mistaken, because my plan is to kill you in the most painful way possible.</p>
<p>So remember that before you choose to betray this humble alliance. Trust me, this time around, even if I am millions of miles away, I will know what is happening here, and as such, I will come."</p>
<p>Kent smiled and shook his head. "Of course, you are free not to believe me now, but fear not. The day will come, and I will make sure when it does, there will be no second time to reconsider anything."</p>
<p>They nodded like obedient children.</p>
<p>Kent nodded, but inwardly, he couldn’t believe he would be saying such cringeworthy words ever in this life. He knew that threatening these people could be done quietly and privately; however, he had no plans to implement any security measures just yet.</p>
<p>He may have made a pit stop in this place simply because he had to, but he had no plans on staying here for long.</p>
<p>If asked what was going on, he would say saving Elina was several times more important than saving the Pill Worlds.</p>
<p>However, since he was here, he would make sure to set things straight before leaving. The fact that he even arrived here when he did was already fated.</p>
<p>After he added a few details to his plans and the power structure of the newly reestablished Pyris Pill Alliance, the meeting ended.</p>
<p>Within a few hours, the alliance resumed operations. Many who were late to the meeting were brought up to speed, and, well, they had no choice but to accept and hope that this new alliance would thrive into a good and prosperous one.</p>
<p>The last thing he did was give them the Primordial Flames. This time, however, no one person would have it, just like Nana did for the other alliances back in the day.</p>
<p>The Pill Flames would have intelligence as they should, so that whenever any of the top-ranking members of the alliance wanted to make high-quality pills, they could use them.</p>
<p>It was all sound and amazing—one that, if managed well, would not only last the Pill Alliances a lifetime but would uplift the image of the Pill Worlds yet again.</p>
<p>After that was done, Kent left Planet Pyris and headed to Solaryn, where thousands, perhaps millions, were waiting for him.</p>
