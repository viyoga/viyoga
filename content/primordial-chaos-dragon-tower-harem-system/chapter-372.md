<p>Kent would love to know more about this mysterious Nyx, but that could wait, as the mission was important. She helped his subordinate, meaning he must thank her someday.</p>
<p>"We will talk about this Queen of the Underworld later. For now, let’s discuss the mission," Kent sighed.</p>
<p>"This mission is already in the bag. I have created another undead who is at the 8th level of the Ascension stage. She alone is enough, but since the ladies want to join the fight, she will just babysit them.</p>
<p>However, now that you are here, I’m not sure where to place you. Whether you join the mission or not, we will be able to rescue the two ladies, so why don’t you join the ladies and help them on the side, too?</p>
<p>I will be moving with Kaizo, Saden, and the three Sentinels. This means we will take on the Ascension-stage evil practitioner hiding down there."</p>
<p>Kent just didn’t know if he should laugh or cry. When he received the quest to save the two ladies, the odds he found out were on another level.</p>
<p>However, now that he was ready, he just couldn’t help but wonder just what his presence there would do, considering he has someone like Kaizo, Veydris, Sylara, Neomi, Selene, Ingrid, Lilian and Elsa on the team.</p>
<p>They are just too overpowered now, and he just could think of how ridiculous it seems to pit them all against the sect master.</p>
<p>Even Uncle Drew felt how ridiculous it looked.</p>
<p>"I understand, young master, but I will let one of my Hell Demons go with you. He won’t join the mission; he will just ensure you are not harmed.</p>
<p>Please don’t reject this. I know you suffered during your battle with the Evil Sect, so let me leave him with you. He is a shadow, so he will just hide in yours," Uncle Drew insisted.</p>
<p>Kent could only nod.</p>
<p>If his Night Rider wanted to do that, then it was fine. He naturally didn’t need it, as he wouldn’t even be fighting if everything fell within his calculations.</p>
<p>"Then be ready." After finalizing everything, Kent went to relax. He would be the one to enter the underground cave physically.</p>
<p>But the moment he did, the sect master would notice his presence, which meant he had to act fast.</p>
<p>He relaxed and waited until only one hour remained. That was when he brought everyone to the Legion Tower.</p>
<p>"I will bring you all out when I appear inside the cave. Sylara, you should release your presence the moment you come out. This will scare the bastard from approaching the two ladies.</p>
<p>This means he will run. But don’t worry about him running. Just release your presence.</p>
<p>However, while he might run, his minions won’t, so you ladies know what to do. The two ladies are the priority, so get to them first and take them inside the tower.</p>
<p>You can clear the underground cave and go back inside the tower when everything is done. I hope we all understand what to do."</p>
<p>From his ladies to his undead, everyone nodded.</p>
<p>"Very well. I will bring you out in the next hour." They moved away and began to wait.</p>
<p>Mara and Camilla got closer to him and hugged him. "Be careful, Kent. Your safety is also very important to us."</p>
<p>"Don’t worry, I have measures in place to ensure my safety." After kissing them, Kent went back to the sect, and with his casual clothes on, he left the Sword Gate and headed to the Tasty Home Pavilion for a quick bite.</p>
<p>Leaving there, he moved toward the mansion of the sect master in casual steps. All those who saw him didn’t suspect he was going on an important mission that would save the sect in many ways.</p>
<p>After 55 minutes, he arrived 2 kilometres away from the sect. That was when he stopped and looked toward the mansion in the distance.</p>
<p>"Your time is up, Sect Master. All the years spent doing all kinds of evil to people comes to an end today."</p>
<p>He moved again and was soon 1 kilometre from the mansion. He stopped once more and used the [Rule Breaker] skill, cancelling all effects of formations on him.</p>
<p>He wanted to teleport inside, so he negated all formations that would prevent his teleportation. When the skill was applied, he blinked and appeared under the blood cauldron hanging by thick chains.</p>
<p>It didn’t even take a second, but the sect master sensed his presence, and like a god, he activated his minions.</p>
<p>But Kentt was also a step ahead.</p>
<p>Sylara, the Harbinger, was brought out. Immediately, as she emerged, she unleashed her level 8 Root Ascension stage aura, which filled the space with its tormenting pressure.</p>
<p>The cavern shuddered, and all the openings that were made into fake walls collapsed as monstrous beings began running out.</p>
<p>The reaction time was quick, but Kent was a step ahead. He teleported again and appeared beside the cages held by the chains.</p>
<p>The two ladies who felt a shift In the air when Sylara unleashed her presence were upstanding.</p>
<p>"Ladies, your Prince Charming is here," Kent smiled and brought out his ladies, Uncle Drew and Veydris.</p>
<p>"He is running, so I’ve got to go." He winked at Nara, who held a shocked expression on her face when her eyes fell on her sister, Mara.</p>
<p>It’s been like a dream, but now, it was a reality right infront of her.</p>
<p>The fake walls opened, and monstrous beings emerged. Clad in her Rakshasa form, Neomi sprang into action immediately, followed by her sisters.</p>
<p>"Big sister Mara, you and Camilla should free the ladies," Selene said, and then she took her battle form.</p>
<p>The slaughter began.</p>
<p>But it would end sooner than they expected.</p>
<p>Kent appeared inside a large space, and that was when he came face to face with the sect master, who was activating an artefact.</p>
<p>"You can’t run, Sect Master. Just give up and accept your death," Kent said as he brought out Saden, Kaizo, and the three Soul Sentinels.</p>
<p>They leapt into action and began slaughtering the minions surrounding them.</p>
<p>In fact, Kent hadn’t expected the mission to go this smoothly, especially considering the look of panic on the sect master’s face.</p>
<p>What he didn’t know, however, was that Sylara had done more than release her presence. She had used a skill that consumed all the Root Transcendent stage minions under the sect master’s control.</p>
<p>This was why he was more than scared when Kent appeared.</p>
<p>"DIE!" The sect master threw an item at Kent, but Kaizo blasted it with his lightning before it could reach him, filling the air with poison.</p>
<p>At the same time, the sect master was bathed in a green light and vanished with mocking laughter.</p>
<p>"I will kill you all" Those were his last words before he vanished.</p>
<p>In his mind, he had escaped. However, he failed to notice he came after someone who had a celestial tower at his disposal and a vengeance to exact.</p>
<p>Kent was meant to save the ladies but couldn’t allow the sect master to escape. His mission was to ensure he died...</p>
<p>Yet, when he did, a smile appeared on Kent’s lips.</p>
<p>"Saden, ensure everything is taken care of here. When you’re done, join the others and go back to the tower. We’ll be back."</p>
<p>An item appeared in his palm, which, if looked at closely, resembled the one the sect master had used.</p>
<p>"You can run, but you can’t hide." His smile widened, and then, like a ghost, he and Kaizo vanished.</p>
<p>The next second, they appeared atop a mountain with a vast sea on one side and a dense forest on the other.</p>
<p>The sect master, who had managed to escape, now found himself face-to-face with Kent once again.</p>
<p>"Well then, Kaizo, do you want to go first?"</p>
