<p>Kent laughed and stared at the seething Angel Lady who wanted nothing more than to end him with a single sword strike.</p>
<p>"I only have one question," Kent said, raising a finger. "Why would the heavenly court mess with the awakening between two worlds?"</p>
<p>To buy time, Kent activated a skill he received when he added Princess Lovina to his harem.</p>
<p>It wouldn’t be able to penetrate Atria’s mind and cause her to say things she wouldn’t want to say, but it was strong enough to at least make her talk.</p>
<p>"I don’t talk to mongrels," Atria said, which caused Kent to narrow his gaze at her. For some reason, he hated being called a mongrel. However, he kept his cool and instead chose to handle it like a man.</p>
<p>"Mongrel, you say. I guess this universe is different from mine, yet the same. Though I pity you... The mission you are on is a failure... You cannot kill the one you are after because I wouldn’t allow you."</p>
<p>Kent turned and looked at the redheaded beauty he had saved the moment he arrived in the universe.</p>
<p>During the past two hours that they had fought, Kent caught Atria observing the redhead every chance she got. Every time she looked at her, a wave of killing intent flowed from her, making him wonder just what she was after.</p>
<p>However, after she had tried to sneakily kill her, Kent finally realized she wasn’t only after her; she was on a strict mission to kill that redhead.</p>
<p>Kent noticed the changes that were happening in the redhead. When he first arrived, if he and she had fought, he would have killed her in under thirty minutes.</p>
<p>However, as time passed and the battle only grew chaotic, he started to sense that if they were to clash now, an hour wouldn’t be enough.</p>
<p>He realized that the redhead was like Sylvara; the longer the fight, the stronger she became. Naturally, the main factor in this was the blood on the battlefield...</p>
<p>"You are scared of her... No, you are not scared... You are scared of what she will become if she is left unchecked for a while. That is the case."</p>
<p>The look on Atria’s face changed, but before she could make a move, Kent spoke again, this time saying something that struck a nerve.</p>
<p>"Do you love your family... You know, your sister, currently on Planet Nimbus, your mother, who is a governor in the Intergalactic Angel War Council... Your brother, who left home and started chasing after a lady like a simp, or your little boyfriend who told you not to take this mission...yet you did."</p>
<p>A grin appeared on Kent’s face. "I believe his name is Micah."</p>
<p>Atria faltered, and her heartbeat nearly came to a stop when Kent spoke again. However, she turned pale when Kent added more words...</p>
<p>"I am not a firm believer in hurting the innocent. However, after appearing in this world and watching what you people are capable of, I don’t mind aiding my bro in killing your family members.</p>
<p>"I believe my beautiful Alina calls it the price you pay for your stupidity. I have already sent the name of your world, family, their description, and even where your vacation home is to my bro, the paragon.</p>
<p>"I may not be able to personally do it, but I think my bro would be more than happy to kill the family of someone working for the Heavenly Court." Kent formed a hand seal, causing a formation to open in the air.</p>
<p>"Why... why would you want innocent people to die?" Atria said, her expression dark.</p>
<p>Kent had been trying to probe her status page for the past hour, but aside from her name and age, nothing else showed...</p>
<p>He tried all he could, but nothing happened.</p>
<p>However, that all changed when Kent started using the "Mind Mess" skill on her. He began to see more of her status.</p>
<p>Soon, everything became clear to him, and that was when he saw the danger he was in, but also a way to buy himself some time.</p>
<p>Of course, what Kent saw made him lose hope in humanity for a moment...</p>
<p>"You talk about innocence when deep within you, you knew all these people were innocent. Even the Paragon is innocent. However, you don’t care about that.</p>
<p>All you care about is pleasing the heavens. You don’t care what happens to the innocent as long as you score some brownie points with the heavens.</p>
<p>So why should I care? Also, my strength comes when there is chaos, so making sure your families follow you in death is the best recipe for you to get mad, so I can do this."</p>
<p>"Crimson Guillotine Array — Open."</p>
<p>The moment Kent said that, the array opened, and over a hundred thousand swords filled the air. Kent formed a few more seals, and then the swords lit with dark flames.</p>
<p>"Attack."</p>
<p>Atria, who was both angry because Kent discovered her secret and scared for the life of her family, decided to let go and kill the bastard.</p>
<p>However, Kent wasn’t about to make things easy for her.</p>
<p>"Guillotine Lotus Formation!"</p>
<p>Ten thousand swords moved to create a lotus flower-like formation around Atria, causing her movement to come to a stop. Then the swords attacked, and all she could do was retaliate.</p>
<p>However, fighting against swords from hell and the Umbral Flame of Oblivion was no easy thing.</p>
<p>Swords kept coming, and despite her blocking and even destroying a few more, the changes were just too little since there were thousands more raining down on her.</p>
<p>"Guillotine Dragon Formation!"</p>
<p>While the lotus formation was working its magic, Kent activated another formation, forming a colossal dragon made of swords that moved toward her and swiped its tail, causing a thousand swords to strike with eerie precision and great speed.</p>
<p>Atria wrapped her wings around herself, and the surface of the wings turned metallic just as the swords arrived.</p>
<p>BOOM!</p>
<p>A powerful explosion rocked the battlefield, creating dust that covered both Kent’s and Atria’s vision. Naturally, they each made a move to catch the other off guard.</p>
<p>However, who was Kent if not someone who wanted just this moment to happen...</p>
<p>’Go, Gaap, you have five minutes.’</p>
<p>Kent’s sword moved and entered the Crimson Guillotine Array. The main killing move is yet to be created.</p>
<p>If Kent had a shot at killing Atria, then Gaap must succeed in his mission.</p>
<p>⟦Master, I have heard back from Icon. She said her master becomes strong when using any of his bloodlines.</p>
<p>The one he is using now is called the Primordial Vampire Monarch Bloodline. It allows him great control in close combat, warfare, and blood manipulation.⟧</p>
<p>’So he is not channeling his bloodline power like I am doing?’</p>
<p>⟦No, he is merely riding on the boost.⟧</p>
<p>’What a monster. Even without holding true control over his bloodline, he is holding back two powerhouses and has even managed to injure one.</p>
<p>That is another level of monster.’</p>
<p>Kent smiled, watching the Paragon send the Angel man crashing to the ground. Then, at the same moment, he saw him manifest a clone that slipped away and was heading in a certain direction.</p>
<p>Kent focused on the clone, which moved toward the direction where a certain woman clad in futuristic armor was locked in fierce combat with a Life Stage warrior.</p>
<p>Naturally, the woman was being trashed. However, it felt like she wanted to be in that position long enough for the clone to arrive.</p>
<p>Just when the Life Stage warrior pushed the woman to a corner and was about to end her, the clone split into two clones and grabbed him.</p>
<p>In the microsecond granted by the new clone, Kent saw the woman point forward, and a beam made from water drew a fist-sized hole through the Life Stage warrior’s head.</p>
<p>Then the clones pointed at the woman, sending her into the pagoda, which stood not far from where she fought.</p>
<p>Kent turned his focus back to the Paragon, who was still locked in fierce combat...</p>
