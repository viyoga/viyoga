<p>Kent ignored all the murderous gazes directed at him and walked toward the direction his teammates stood. When he got close, Nyss took his hands and placed them against her cheek, checking his temperament.</p>
<p>"Big brother, how are you. Lady Enzi said you had to rest for a few hours because you weren’t feeling well."</p>
<p>Kent turns and looks at Lady Enzi before answering, "I am fine, Nyss, I’m better than fine, even."</p>
<p>Nyss nodded. She was worried sick when she heard Kent was unwell. Though she knew there was no need to worry, she couldn’t help but worry for her big brother, who, in reality, was several years older than him.</p>
<p>It is all crazy and cute at the same time.</p>
<p>After Nyss pulled back her hand, Kent looked at the disciples from the Primal Beast sect and smiled. This sight infuriates them even more.</p>
<p>They’ve been made aware of Kent’s true identity as the one who defeated the Ancient Sea Race. Of course, they are aware he wasn’t the only one, that the entire Supreme Immortal sect was involved.</p>
<p>However, knowing the one who made the decision to tie the fate of the Sea Race to that of Sonox, forever making them warriors who will fight for the planet, they all wanted nothing more than to kill the brat.</p>
<p>However, looking in his mocking smile now, they all could tell he wasn’t afraid of them...not even in the slightest.</p>
<p>However, they are beasts; they pride themselves on being the best, so they are ready to teach him a lesson. Even though there is a no-killing rule, breaking his bones to cripple him will come in handy soon.</p>
<p>"What a bunch of losers. They have been trying to pick a fight with us since we arrived on this planet." Nyss smirked, "If I could, I would slit their throats and feed their own blood to them."</p>
<p>Nyss spoke at just the right volume for the disciples of the primal Beast sect to hear her.</p>
<p>Kent smiled, "I know, right. I wanted to slaughter them all, but the rules won’t allow me. However, there is no rule preventing us from hunting them after the competition."</p>
<p>This time, Kent activated the Blood Death Seal, allowing his killing intent to travel with his words. When they felt it, every last beast trembled...</p>
<p>"That will be amazing, big brother. I will be sure to bring my tools for a proper massacre when we are done here." Nyss smiles, truly believing Kent will take her on a killing spree.</p>
<p>Of course, Kent wouldn’t mind. However, he is just not going to bother with them. They are far too beneath him.</p>
<p>"Stop messing around, you two. This is a friendly competition," Lady Enzi said, appearing beside Kent and Nyss.</p>
<p>"No need to worry, Lady Enzi. We were just messing around, "Kent said with a handsome smile.</p>
<p>"Or not. We would never know, I guess," Nyss grinned, which made the situation even worse. However, she said nothing, only smiling and shaking her head.</p>
<p>A few minutes later, the Elder to handle the match between the Supreme Immortal Sect and the Primal Beast sect arrived.</p>
<p>"Welcome once again, everyone. As we all know, this is the first quarterfinals match, so we should all pay attention to the rules.</p>
<p>As before, there are individual battles in which each sect sends one disciple to fight. Whoever loses will send the next participant. Of course, the winner can also choose to replace their disciple.</p>
<p>However, whenever a sect is down 4-0, that is an automatic loss. But, before they are down four to none, they can choose to switch to team battle, where each sect will pick seven disciples to represent them.</p>
<p>As usual, there is a no-killing policy, so be warned."</p>
<p>Every nodded...</p>
<p>"Also, know that this match is being watched by the alliance head and a few officials, so give it your best."</p>
<p>Kent smiles, knowing this is a perfect time to show everyone there is a new Overlord in town. The Alliance head is his target. If he can make him his subordinate, then automatically, the entire Alliance will be his to rule.</p>
<p>In Kent’s case, he was only after the soul essence.</p>
<p>So he would use the Primal Beast Sect to get what he wanted. This also meant the Primal Beast sect was about to have a very bad day...</p>
<p>"Each sect should send its first participants."</p>
<p>Kent smiled and decided to move into the arena.</p>
<p>"Kick their ass, big brother." The overly enthusiastic Nyss said, giving the Primal Beast sect a thumbs down.</p>
<p>"You know it," Kent smiled and walked into the arena. On the other side, the Primal Beast sect elder sent their first disciple, a Tigerkin, to fight Kent first.</p>
<p>The moment he stepped into the arena, he locked his killing-intent-filled eyes on Kent...</p>
<p>"You are a dead bug, human," he said, making sure Kent understood he meant business.</p>
<p>"Oh, I am so scared right now," Kent laughed, causing the Tigerkin to bubble with anger.</p>
<p>The elder, overlooking the battle, sighed and then made a reminder, "Remember, there is a no-killing rule, so better not aim your attacks to kill."</p>
<p>"Don’t worry, elder, he wouldn’t be able to get close before even landing a blow," Kent said with a smile, but the Tigerkin said nothing and only continued to look at Kent with murderous eyes.</p>
<p>After a few seconds, the elder decided to start the match.</p>
<p>"Begin."</p>
<p>"Haaaaaa." The Tigerkin lunged at Kent with a battle cry the moment the match began. He wanted his pound of flesh, so he charged forward.</p>
<p>Kent remained at ease watching the poor guy run at him in slow motion. If he wanted, he could move, remove his pants, and be back where he stood without anyone noticing when he moved.</p>
<p>Only Vexthra would have seen him move.</p>
<p>However, Kent was not that petty, so he stood there, waiting for the poor tiger to get close.</p>
<p>"DIE." The moment he reached him, he unleashed a powerful Rend attack with his clawed hand, aiming to kill Kent in an instant. However, to his shock, Kent raised his hand and caught it.</p>
<p>Kent grinned, then he made his move, "Here comes the pain."</p>
<p>BANG.</p>
<p>Using the Tigerkin’s own momentum, Kent lifted him into the air and slammed him on the ground, breaking a few bones. However, he didn’t stop there.</p>
<p>Lifting him again, Kent started slamming him more on the ground.</p>
<p>BANG BANG BANG BANG BANG.</p>
<p>Gasps, shock, and weariness washed over everyone as they watched Kent body-slam the Tigerkin over a dozen times before releasing him.</p>
<p>By then, the poor guy had lost consciousness, a couple of bones broken in his body.</p>
<p>"How boring. We haven’t even gotten to the best part yet." Kent sighed and added, "I was going to break your bones, then when done, I would use my own strength to dislocate and rearrange some bones, making sure when fixing it for you, the pain would be even more unbearable for you.</p>
<p>However, I am no sadist, so I will let you slide this time. However, there won’t be a next time."</p>
<p>Kent kicked him off the stage and then turned to the Primal Beast sect elders...</p>
<p>"We move to team battle." One of the elders immediately said, seeing no way they could survive such brutality. If they continued individual battles, news of them losing 4-0 to their rivals would spread like wildfire.</p>
<p>Kent turned to Lady Enzi and smiled...</p>
<p>"We accept, but only one disciple will be participating if you don’t mind," Lady Enzi replied, causing the faces of everyone from the Primal Beast sect to turn grim with anger.</p>
<p>"This is going to be fun," Kent said with a grin.</p>
