<p>The void opened in the clouds, and Kaizo dropped down, landing on the ground with a loud thud. Dust rose into the air, and when it cleared, Kaizo was revealed wearing a grin on his face.</p>
<p>The fact that 5,000 of their men had been assassinated at once had yet to be resolved; however, when they saw Kaizo appear, they forgot about it.</p>
<p>"Really, a demigod?" Monarch Drakonis said with a smirk.</p>
<p>But his brow furrowed when Kaizo raised his hand and snapped his fingers.</p>
<p>"Legion Call."</p>
<p>Void gates exploded open, and from within, the legion marched out, each wearing a serious expression. Dia led the harem out, all of them ready and pumped for the battle ahead.</p>
<p>In the absence of Kaizo, Sylara led the undead legion.</p>
<p>Elsa led the soul sentinels, who immediately caused those who knew history to widen their eyes when they saw them.</p>
<p>Ant King Ketu led the Eternal Wrath legion.</p>
<p>Ixeron led the Forbidden Beast legion, and Xelthar led the Abomination Beast legion.</p>
<p>Veydris and the rest of the Lost Knights weren’t present, and neither were Uncle Drew and the Night Riders. But with Kaizo and the rest arriving, the people who initially looked down on Baldy and Kaizo became alert.</p>
<p>They sensed some powerful auras, and so they all felt the threat.</p>
<p>When the last of them appeared, the portals closed, and Baldy exploded into resounding laughter. "Told you losers I have big brothers and sisters."</p>
<p>Monarch Solran and the four other monarchs became even more alert; the frowns on their faces widened and deepened.</p>
<p>Vexthra walked forward and coldly said, "We are here for Inkala, and we will not leave until she is in our care. You can try to stop us, but rest assured, 70% of you will be dying today, and there is nothing you can do to stop that."</p>
<p>"Activate the formation."</p>
<p>Pandora commanded, and behind the line, three Celestial-grade Dark Fiends who had been enthralled by Zaila during the Dark War formed hand seals.</p>
<p>Outside the main gate and along the walls of the stronghold, runes lit up, and a formation went live.</p>
<p>Nothing flashy happened, but Vexthra smiled and said, "Now, it is just you losers and us. No help is coming."</p>
<p>As if to confirm that indeed no help was coming, some people tried to use their communication jades and tokens, but they didn’t work.</p>
<p>"I will ask again, hand over Inkala, and we will call it a day," Vexthra said, knowing deep down that even if they handed her over, they would go on killing until they had no choice but to retreat.</p>
<p>But as expected, the monarchs had no intention of losing face, not when they had guests around...</p>
<p>"In your dreams," Monarch Solran said, taking a step forward.</p>
<p>Vexthra smiled and sighed. "In that case, you give us no choice but to play dirty."</p>
<p>She turned to Fiannarel. "Big sister, do it."</p>
<p>Fiannarel winked and moved, taking strides so fast that nobody managed to track her, not even Sect Master Zhai, who was known for his fast movement techniques.</p>
<p>But while they couldn’t track her, all five monarchs had a terrible feeling. As expected, before they could blink, the carriage carrying the ten maidens who were meant to become Monarch Solran’s concubines was taken away.</p>
<p>Fiannarel appeared 2 km away, and then, with a wave, she vanished back into the tower, her part done.</p>
<p>She was an Eternal, so even a slight mistake could unleash calamity on this universe. Her power was too great for this universe, and so she was used only to rescue Inkala.</p>
<p>The rest, Vexthra and her sisters, alongside the legion, would handle it.</p>
<p>"BITCH! DIE!"</p>
<p>Monarch Solran’s eyes turn crimson.</p>
<p>He moved and appeared before Vexthra, his claws aiming to dig out her throat. However, before his claws could reach her, a hand appeared and caught his.</p>
<p>"Hi, my name is Neveross. Pleasure meeting you."</p>
<p>A cylindrical-shaped item appeared in Neveross’s other hand, and then the two of them vanished inside.</p>
<p>One down, four more to go.</p>
<p>Vexthra looked at Monarch Voron and smirked. "Time for you to go too."</p>
<p>Pandora narrowed her eyes at her opponent, then, with a smirk, vanished, and Monarch Voron, who didn’t know how, also vanished. In their place, another cylindrical-shaped object remained, shining with a dull light.</p>
<p>Eliza also smiled, and soon, she was gone, Monarch Drakonis vanishing with her.</p>
<p>Monarch Nexus saw that if he didn’t make his move, Vexthra and her teammates would isolate them. So he did. He tried to reach Baldy; however, Princess Dominique reached him first, and they both vanished.</p>
<p>In the end, only Monarch Aethros remained.</p>
<p>"Who are you people?" he asked, his expression calm.</p>
<p>"The Primordial Chaos Dragon sent us, punk," Baldy replied, stealing the moment from Kaizo, who wanted to brag a little.</p>
<p>"Are you mental? The Primordial Chaos Dragon is dead," Monarch Aethros said, his eyes narrowing.</p>
<p>"Not anymore, buddy. Now, if you don’t have much to say, can we fight?" Instead of Athena taking on the last Primordial, Han, Baldy’s big brother, smiled and winked at Baldy before vanishing alongside his opponent.</p>
<p>"Kick his ass big brother," Baldy said.</p>
<p>In the end, only the Sages remained, and all of them were in shock.</p>
<p>However, three Primordials still remained, each a formidable foe. Lord Marcus and Lord Weyori were allies of the Grim Arcane Legends, so they most certainly would not stand by and watch the stronghold brought down to its knees.</p>
<p>Aside from that, more warriors started to appear on the scene, both members of the Grim Arcane Legends and allies invited to the birthday.</p>
<p>They didn’t just want to sit on the sidelines and watch their allies being killed. Plus, given the numerical difference, they were confident that with close to 80 Pseudo-Primordials on their side, Vexthra and her people were as good as dead.</p>
<p>Unfortunately for them, what happened next dampened their spirits.</p>
<p>Ruby walked forward, every step leaving blood lotuses in her wake. When she was finally in front of her teammates, she smirked and spoke, directing her message straight at Sect Master Zhai.</p>
<p>"Hey, bastard, are you going to sit there and watch Master’s wives get beaten to death, or get up and kill some bastards for us?"</p>
<p>Sect Master Zhai smiled weakly and lifted his head. "No wonder I hate you the most, Ruby."</p>
<p>"Get in line, punk."</p>
<p>The weather suddenly started to change.</p>
