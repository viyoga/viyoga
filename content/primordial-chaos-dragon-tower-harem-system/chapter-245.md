<p>When Uncle Drew left to check on Eryndor’s progress, Kent decided to rest for a couple of hours. When he woke up, he immediately accessed his rewards from the Third Quest.</p>
<p>It was the chained quest he received when he met the Spear Saintess. It was also the chain quest that kept prompting him on where he should go next and what to do.</p>
<p>Now, he was halfway there.</p>
<p>The third quest was a success. The Spear Saintess had her enlightenment; now, she was just a step away from becoming a Spear Sage.</p>
<p>Of course, that plan would be put on hold for now. The quest reward is just too enticing to be put aside.</p>
<p>Kent had received some rewards that would break her streak and halt her progress—for the better. Once she is done, everything about her will change, making her a powerhouse that will one day rise to the peak of power.</p>
<p>He checked the rewards.</p>
<p>⟪Congratulations on completing the third quest In the ’Become Friends With Spear Saintess Neomi’ Chain quest. Rewards received ⟫</p>
<p>⟪ Rewards: Received ⟫</p>
<p>→ 500,000 Tower Points</p>
<p>→ +10% to Friendship Meter</p>
<p>→ One Time Tower Store Pass</p>
<p>⟪ Additional Rewards: ⟫</p>
<p>→ The 4th Primordial Path of the Spear (Neomi)</p>
<p>→ The Secret Manual of the Voidlings (Ingrid)</p>
<p>"Nice one," Kent smiled, reading through the rewards.</p>
<p>He had already sent four subordinates to the Tower Store to buy what they would need to handle the slave traders. But it seemed he wasn’t in any way broke.</p>
<p>The Tower points from the first, second, and third quests were enough to keep him going. In fact, he was now rich in Tower points.</p>
<p>But until Saden and the three Soul Sentinels left the Tower Store, he wouldn’t be happy just yet.</p>
<p>"Looks like she trusts me even more. I guess whatever went on in the Enlightenment made her put all her hopes and dreams on me... Just as I wanted it.</p>
<p>Lol."</p>
<p>⟦Friendship Meter: 85/100% ⟧</p>
<p>He checked the friendship meter, and to his shock, instead of 70%, it was now 85%. This means that somehow, she had added 15% to it, which wasn’t something he had expected, but it was here now.</p>
<p>"I should probably go see her soon. Who knows? I might get lucky and have a master and her disciple at the same time."</p>
<p>He may have been a slave in his past life, but he is living the life now, and that’s all that matters.</p>
<p>"The additional rewards are also no joke. If this is the way, then I shall walk it."</p>
<p>Right now, all he knew about the Void Eyes was that they once belonged to a race known as the Voidlings. But from how Vexthra described it, they seemed to be more than that, and now Kent has a way to find out.</p>
<p>However, Ingrid’s name was ingrained into the reward, meaning it was for her alone. But the moment she becomes his woman, he will be able to see into it.</p>
<p>"Looks like the way forward is the right one... I must make them smile and moan from pleasure."</p>
<p>After forming all kinds of shameless plans in his head, he started to ponder the best way to find out who the mysterious Nara was—</p>
<p>The quest to find the next Night Rider requires him to find whoever this mysterious Nara is. This means he must do some good old-fashioned detective work.</p>
<p>"I will check the sect records first, and then I will just ask the Spear Saintess to see if she knows something, that’s if I don’t find anything in the sect records.</p>
<p>But first, I must research; who knows what I might find," Kent made his plan.</p>
<p>"Tsk..I am even forgetting Ingrid is a nerd. I will just ask her and see what she knows."</p>
<p>He stood up and was about to freshen up when a fourth quest suddenly appeared.</p>
<p>________________</p>
<p>⟪ Fourth Chain Quest: Become Friends With Spear Saintess Neomi ⟫</p>
<p>⟪ Quest Difficulty: Depends ⟫</p>
<p>-&gt; You manage to earn the Spear Saintess trust...</p>
<p>-&gt; The only thing left is to thrust and ensure she understands that something else is worth dying for aside from the spear.</p>
<p>-&gt; Of course, it is only right to dominate. So why don’t you make it a master and disciple moment? It could be fun.</p>
<p>-&gt; You are the Primordial Chaos Dragon...so show your might.</p>
<p>⟪Rewards: ⟫</p>
<p>→ 1,000,000 Tower Points</p>
<p>→ Dungeon Master Token</p>
<p>→ Universe Wish Token</p>
<p>⟪Additional Rewards: ⟫</p>
<p>→ Voidling Blood Essense</p>
<p>→ Path of The Void</p>
<p>Kent stared at the messages with a smile playing on his lips.</p>
<p>"The universe sure knows how to spoil a guy," he muttered shamelessly. The proposal presented was something he wouldn’t turn down.</p>
<p>It was like being offered rewards for breathing. No matter the outcome of the quests he had received up to this point, he wasn’t about to back down.</p>
<p>Since it all worked out in the end, he could only smile and take it as it should be. Now, all that was required of him was to handle both the master and her disciple at the same time.</p>
<p>He had already handled Unity and Lilian, which was when he was still weak. Now, he was more than ready to go the extra mile.</p>
<p>Kent was ecstatic, so he freshened up and left the Sword Gate. Right now, his objectives were to get stronger, find out who Nara was, and handle both the master and her disciple.</p>
<p>Since Unity and the rest of his ladies were now handling Alina’s mental state, he could only focus on handling things to the best of his abilities.</p>
<p>He arrived at the Spear Gate, but before he could enter, he was stopped by a group of disciples led by Randy. The expression on his face said it all: he was very angry.</p>
<p>Well, he was beaten black and blue by him just a couple of days ago, so in a way, he is beyond pissed. He is a noble heir who prided himself on being strong, but after the humiliations he had been through recently...</p>
<p>Killing Kent is something he would enjoy very much. If possible, he wants to challenge him again, but something tells him he will lose again.</p>
<p>So he can only do what arrogant heirs do best...being arrogant.</p>
<p>"The Spear Gate is off-limits to Sword Gate disciples," Maxwell said, looking smug. He is one of Randy’s minions.</p>
<p>"I know, but do you have the strength to stop me?" Kent asked, looking coldly at him while a mocking smile played on his lips.</p>
<p>"I’m not going to say it again; the Spear Gate is off-limits to Sword Gate disciples. Turn around and leave before we make you."</p>
<p>Maxwell said, pulling a spear from his space ring. The disciples behind him did the same, with only Randy and a few others standing still, not attempting to take out their weapons.</p>
<p>"I don’t have time for your nonsense today, so until we meet next time," Kent said, waving at Randy. The next second, he was 4 km away, leaving the disciples to wonder what had just happened.</p>
<p>Obviously, Kent, who had been inside the Spear Gate before, knew where to go. So he used the [Blink] skill and blinked away, leaving them stunned, and for a moment, they could only grit their teeth.</p>
