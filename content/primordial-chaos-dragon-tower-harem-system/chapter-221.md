<p>"Master, do you see yourself as inferior?" Kent asked, now back at the Sword Gate with his master.</p>
<p>When he had learned her identity after she came to the Alderford Mansion to make him her disciple, he had only seen her as the most badass lady aside from his first wife, Goddess Vexthra.</p>
<p>She was the Sword Saintess of the Athea Kingdom, the most powerful person whom even an entire noble family wouldn’t dare go against. She was a force the kingdom could rely on, and so on.</p>
<p>But beneath it all, she was feeling inferior and insecure.</p>
<p>And yes, the worst part was that Kent had missed it despite having such an overpowered set of eyes. That was just too bad—and very bad on his part.</p>
<p>So now, he would listen and pay more attention before he left for his own training and other pursuits.</p>
<p>"I’m sorry you had to find out like this, but the truth is, my talent in cultivation is very weak. Basically, I don’t have much stamina, strength, or any of that since, despite rising to this stage in the sword path and cultivation level.</p>
<p>My cultivation was achieved mostly using pills and other treasures.</p>
<p>I had a copper-grade spirit root, which was upgraded to silver-grade when I became your woman, thanks to the technique given to me by sister Vexthra...</p>

<p>A few teardrops fell from her eyes.</p>
<p>In her entire life, all she ever wanted was the power to be the one protecting her sister. Saintess Neomi was like the one who raised her.</p>
<p>She had nearly sacrificed her chance to enter such a prestigious sect—all for her. Though it was something the Spear Saintess would have done again if given the chance, Saintess Selene wanted to repay her.</p>

<p>But at the moment, she was very emotional, especially after being saved again by her big sister.</p>

<p>Kent took out a Golden Ambrosia Apple. "Do you know what this is?" he asked, and the Saintess nodded with a shocked expression.</p>
<p>"Then you know that this will raise your Spirit Root grade to the Diamond Stage. After that, I will give you another one that will take you to the Ruby Stage without having to wait 100 years.</p>
<p>"This will even take your Spirit Root one grade above your sister. But don’t worry about her, I will give her one too. For now, I just want to focus on my woman."</p>

<p>She was very emotional at the moment, and Kent could only keep wiping her tears.</p>
<p>"Thank you, Kent... Thank you very much," she said, hugging him. Kent could only smile and be happy for her.</p>
<p>"Just focus on upgrading your Spirit Root. When you are done, I have another gift for you. In fact, you are going to love that gift more than this," Kent said with a smile.</p>
