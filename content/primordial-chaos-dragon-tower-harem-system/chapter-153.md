<p>Kent stood with his arms behind him as he gazed at four otherworldly beauties and Li Hua, who wore shocked expressions on their faces.</p>
<p>He had brought them to the tower, specifically to a garden where a treehouse could be seen. He had waited for two hours after Gaia and Akira went to build their home in the tower before bringing the five ladies inside.</p>
<p>The two Weavers seemed to have built a treehouse in the small garden. The place was very charming, and from its appearance, it was clear they had used the rarest kind of materials.</p>
<p>Kent regretted giving them the 500k Tower Points. He had expected a large house, but the two lolis seemed far more childish yet devious.</p>
<p>"Everyone, welcome to my personal domain," Kent’s words snapped them back to their senses, his lips curving into a faint, confident smile.</p>
<p>"Kent, what is this place?" Saintess Selene asked, her brows furrowed in confusion.</p>
<p>"This is my personal domain, which I, and only I, have control over. Nobody else—not even gods—can access it," Kent said, snapping his fingers with a sly smirk.</p>
<p>Suddenly, the two lolis appeared, startling everyone.</p>
<p>Gaia returned to her Fairy Pirate form, her expression as mischievous as ever, while Akira stood in her young, adorable form, her backpack still secured on her back.</p>
<p>"This is Gaia and Akira. They are my assistants," Kent explained, his gaze shifting to Gaia with a subtle nod.</p>
<p>"Well, she is more like my assistant for this place. As for Akira, I have a major position planned for her. But for now, just see them as my assistants."</p>
<p>Kent kept his expression neutral, carefully avoiding revealing the truth: they were Weavers, two of the three ever to grace this universe.</p>
<p>Although he knew the ladies in front of him had no idea who the Weavers were, Kent preferred it that way. It was for their own good—knowing too much could bring them unnecessary harm.</p>
<p>It also made things easier for Kent; he wouldn’t have to worry about his ladies accidentally revealing anything they shouldn’t.</p>
<p>"Hi, everyone! I’m Akira," Akira said cheerfully, extending her small hand for a handshake. Her innocent smile was infectious, yet there was a spark of mischief in her eyes.</p>
<p>Saintess Selene hesitated briefly, her gaze shifting between Akira’s hand and her face, before accepting the handshake with a slight nod.</p>
<p>"Hmm, what a powerful sword body... Too bad you haven’t even awakened or reached the first form yet," Akira remarked, tilting her head and flashing a knowing grin.</p>
<p>Saintess Selene’s eyes widened in shock, her lips parting as if to speak but no words came out.</p>
<p>"She has a knack for reading the innate nature of people," Kent interjected with a guilty smile, scratching the back of his head. He sighed inwardly, already envisioning the trouble Akira was bound to cause.</p>
<p>He approached Unity first, then Lilian, and finally stopped in front of Elsa.</p>
<p>"Can I see them? Your soul sentinels," she asked, her tone calm but carrying an air of certainty, as though she already knew everything about her.</p>
<p>Elsa glanced at Kent, searching for his reaction. The subtle anticipation on his face made her smile weakly, a faint blush colouring her cheeks. Kent seemed just as curious as Akira was about her soul sentinels.</p>
<p>His first summon was a troublemaker, and now he couldn’t help but wonder what kind of beings she had under her command. Elsa currently had three soul sentinels.</p>
<p>Elsa waved her hand, and three beings appeared - all Peak Root Sage experts.</p>
<p>______________</p>
<p>One is a dark, robbed figure wielding daggers. Well, she has daggers sheathed on her waist and legs and some pointy knuckle weapons on her arms.</p>
<p>She wore a mask covering her mouth and nose and an eyeliner covering her eyes in black paint. She has next to no presence. It was like she didn’t have any aura or life running through her.</p>
<p>Kent appraised her and found out she was the SSS-class summon. A peak saint with powerful abilities. Strangely, she can upgrade her class as she climbs the cultivation chart.</p>

<p>"This is Nyxira. She is an assassin, and a very powerful one. The dude there is Kaelion; he is a swordsman, and the last dude is also a swordsman, called Kael," Elsa introduced them.</p>
<p>The three gave a small bow but didn’t speak.</p>
<p>Kent nodded with a small smile.</p>
<p>’It seems this team is much more powerful than I expected.’ He suddenly felt the urge to increase his own summon, but he knew better—not to go for quantity but quality.</p>
<p>"Everyone, welcome to the family. I am Kent." Kent introduced himself and was about to do the same for Gaia, but then he saw Akira looking closely at the three soul sentinels.</p>
<p>"Master, I think you should let these three spend some time with us," Akira said, looking back at Kent with her big, cute eyes.</p>
<p>Kent wanted to dismiss her request, but Elsa told him it was okay through telepathy. She wouldn’t need them anyway... at least for now. Also, staying in the tower was the best for them.</p>
<p>"Okay, but don’t cause trouble for them," Kent said, but then he froze for a second when he saw Li Hua staring daggers at the assassin lady.</p>
<p>In return, Nyxira also looked back at the small human. It seemed the two were having a stare-down.</p>
<p>"Big brother, I want to play with this big sister." Li Hua turned back to Kent, holding a banana cake she had retrieved from her pouch.</p>
<p>The small human was trying to bribe him. Of course, it was of no use since Kent had already made up his mind to leave her there.</p>
<p>He had no plans to take her to the Harem Tower. That was no place for children.</p>
<p>"Then I will trouble you three to take care of these small ones. They can be a handful, but bear with them," Kent said.</p>
<p>The three Sentinels, who had already received word from the mistress, nodded and then sheathed and put away their weapons. A few seconds later, they were now clad in normal clothes.</p>
<p>That was when Kent saw the elf’s ear on Nyxira and immediately decided to get an elf or two in the future.</p>
<p>"Alright, ladies, why don’t we go to where I wanted to bring you in the first place?" Kent smiled.</p>
<p>"Let’s go to the World of Pleasures."</p>
