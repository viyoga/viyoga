<p>As for Veera, the necromancer in the team, she was a member of a necromancy cult known in the universe as the Odyssey.</p>
<p>She didn’t recall her rank, whether she was high-ranking or just a normal member, but she knew the Odyssey was a group she had wanted to join from the day she became a necromancer.</p>
<p>She also recalled entering a fragment of the great battlefield—the battlefield of the Great War, the one that happened during the Primordial Era.</p>
<p>She remembered meeting her first human undead there, since, to her shock, she couldn’t raise any dead spirits there. It was like some laws suppressed her from doing that.</p>
<p>However, the spirit she met became her first trump card, which she used to gain access to even more battlefields.</p>
<p>Her necromancy is the grave-robbing kind. However, she also has a class ability that allows her to merge multiple undead to form more powerful ones.</p>
<p>This skill works on both humans and beasts, and now that her memories have started to surface, the skill is available to her to use.</p>
<p>As for how she found herself on the last floor of the dungeon, she had a vague idea.</p>
<p>It was a friend who joined another organization that was at odds with the Odyssey. It felt like a knife plunged through her heart when she recalled that memory.</p>
<p>But since she didn’t know for sure, she could only speculate that perhaps things weren’t as simple as they appeared.</p>
<p>As for Gorzak, the Barbarian King, he outright knew how he ended up on the last floor of the dungeon—it was because his wife had conspired with the enemy. During his tribulation to become an Immortal King, he was attacked.</p>
<p>Those tribulations are dangerous, so immortals tend to seek the most secure place they can find. Most pass out after their tribulation.</p>
<p>It takes a great deal to overcome such tribulation. Most become weakened, and in Gorzak’s case, he was attacked in his weakened state.</p>
<p>The last thing he remembered was falling into the abyss...</p>
<p>It wasn’t hard to see Gorzak’s desire for revenge, and Kent promised him that. It was also one of the reasons Kent made up his mind to start showing his true strength soon.</p>
<p>The only reason he wouldn’t ascend now was because of the Hand, but that doesn’t mean he would have to remain hidden...</p>
<p>From what he knew, the moment the first Apocalypse Key was found, this world’s fate had been sealed. Of course, the apocalypse will descend, and for some reason, the universe wants him there.</p>
<p>Not that he would run off when he could stay and see what the apocalypse looks like.</p>
<p>In his defense, he could just kill every last member of the Hand, but he was told that one way or another, the gate of the apocalypse would be opened.</p>
<p>So he wants to handle that first before ascending. His reason is simple: he wants to ensure the world in which his journey began is not destroyed in his absence.</p>
<p>Of course, this also concerns Vexthra. He doesn’t want his wife’s world to be destroyed. Vexthra came from Sonox, so he wants to save it before he leaves.</p>
<p>Kent wanted to get that done first. But the moment that was done and he ascended, he would seek Gorzak’s world and help him get revenge.</p>
<p>For now, though, the Barbarian King could wait. Things were much more complex than they looked.</p>
<p>Valtor, the Panther Assassin, only remembered his time in the Veil of Eternal Damnation. This assassin organization had trained some of the most lethal assassins in the universe’s history.</p>
<p>It was even rumored that the infamous Faceless Assassin was once a member, and as someone who admired the Faceless Assassin, Valtor joined—and that was all he recalled.</p>
<p>However, that was enough for him, as he finally understood why he had become an assassin.</p>
<p>It was to follow in the Faceless Assassin’s footsteps. Kent was rather happy to hear him say that. It turns out that the aloof man he met in his memories had more fans than he let on.</p>
<p>First, it was Sir Ashfur, and now, it is Valtor.</p>
<p>However, Kent had not yet revealed his identity as the Faceless Assassin. He would do that some other time. So for now, they could remain in the dark. He would tell them one of these days... Telling them will be good though..</p>
<p>Especially for someone like Valtor, whom he knew would become much more lively now. His reason for becoming an assassin had been revealed, so he would now become more active...</p>
<p>Irina, the strongest of them all, just recalled killing her entire family because they chose to submit to the same king who killed her brother and took her sister hostage just because she refused to marry him.</p>
<p>Kent expected her to be seething with anger when she spoke, but to his shock, she merely smiled.</p>
<p>When he asked why, Irina casually smiled and answered, "I don’t leave loose ends. Since I took care of my family, I’m certain the other was also taken care of."</p>
<p>When Kent asked what if she failed and that was why she found herself on the last floor of the dungeon she came from, she smiled again and answered, "I don’t fail when it comes to killing... the fact that I am here is probably me being tired of killing and choosing to retire."</p>
<p>If her brothers and sisters were wary of her before, then now they were scared of her. The nonchalant way she spoke said it all; she was confident in her words, and that alone made them recognize why she was the strongest and the most dangerous among them.</p>
<p>Zharkaz, the dragon general, grew more arrogant after remembering some battles he had won. Naturally, this caused his combat strength to rise.</p>
<p>However, aside from that, his bloodline had also awakened, and he could now assume a more refined version of his humanoid form.</p>
<p>They all could do that now. This allowed them to develop a more refined human appearance. Their True Names awakening made them more relatable...</p>
<p>However, Vorthar was still a demon lord and an angry one at that.</p>
<p>It turned out that after years of waging war for his demon king, that bastard had actually been in bed with his wives, and as such, they were the cause of his death, which happened at the hands of ten thousand demons.</p>
<p>Death by a thousand cuts turned out to be much more dubious when ten thousand demons were doing it. His anger was just too deep for Kent to feel.</p>
<p>So he knew waging war on some demons was now on his calendar. If possible, he would also stage a death by a thousand cuts for the said demon king... if he was still alive.</p>
<p>Ankaara already had her memories, so aside from becoming more beautiful and dangerous, the rest of the Eternal Wrath Legion was now having their memories come back to them.</p>
<p>At the moment, they were in fragments...</p>
<p>But one thing was certain—they all wanted revenge, and it would happen...</p>
