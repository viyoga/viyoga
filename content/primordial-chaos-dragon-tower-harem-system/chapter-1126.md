<p>[Planet Gangis - Autumn’s POV]</p>
<p>Planet Gangis is one of the major warrior planets in the Immortal Worlds. It is a world of warriors, and Princess Autumn, who became Empress Autumn before ascending with her sisters and friends, chose to live there.</p>
<p>She had been living there for thousands of years already.</p>
<p>Over the years, she had done so much and proved her status as a warrior, earning the title Scarlet Archeress.</p>
<p>Among the dozens of people Kent had met and befriended in his time at the Pill World 370,000 years ago, she was the second to reach the Immortal Lord stage with 9 Immortal Qi.</p>
<p>She is a terrifying Archeress, someone who has even caught the interest of the gods and demigods.</p>
<p>Rumour even has it that during an expedition into an Ancient Ruin 12,000 years ago, she severely injured a demigod while just at the Immortal Monarch stage.</p>
<p>Due to this, she had entered seclusion and started preparing for when the demigod would come for revenge.</p>
<p>Nobody had seen her for the past 12,000 years. However, she is still alive, and for that, everyone wanted to wait for when she comes out.</p>
<p>Meanwhile, inside an Immortal Cave somewhere on the outskirts of Planet Gangis, Eris, a green-haired dragon lady, stood beside Autumn, attending to an injury on her shoulder.</p>
<p>Obviously, the battle between the demigod happened, but she didn’t come out unscathed like many had believed.</p>
<p>She was injured, and that was evident on her shoulder now.</p>
<p>"How is the pain now?" Eris asked, putting medicine on the small but painful cut on her shoulder.</p>
<p>"The divine energy is almost gone, so the pain has lessened."</p>
<p>Eris nodded. "If you had relaxed and healed first before taking the tribulation to become Immortal Lord, the pain would have long subsided."</p>
<p>"I know, Eris. However, if I had become Immortal Lord before meeting that bastard, his death would have been swift." Autumn smiled, recalling how close she came to killing the demigod.</p>
<p>"I know you are an overachiever, Autumn, but Lord Kent said your health and safety are important, so maybe next time don’t go fighting demigods." Eris didn’t want to say it, but she is darn proud of Autumn.</p>
<p>As a dragon, this level of arrogance in Autumn is to her liking.</p>
<p>"Just don’t go looking for trouble for now. The Chaos Region will open in the Star System that Big Sister Sylvara lives in. She sent word that we should come.</p>
<p>Naturally, that bastard will also come. He wouldn’t miss the chance to kill you, so I have sent word to Big Sister Brianna. She will come with Kenina."</p>
<p>"I see. How is she, by the way?"</p>
<p>Eris smiled. "She is just like you. The last I heard, she and her mercenary group had taken on an entire cult of zealots and eradicated them, even killing their Immortal Lord leader in a one-on-one battle."</p>
<p>Autumn smiled, missing her sister, best friend, and guardian.</p>

<p>[Planet Panara - Brianna’s POV]</p>
<p>Planet Panara is also a warrior planet, perhaps even more competitive than Planet Gangis. However, they are both in the same Star System but different planetary systems.</p>
<p>General Brianna, the reason why Kent got to meet Princess Autumn back in the Flame Sea, is now a prominent figure in this world.</p>
<p>Planet Panara is vast and largely inhabited by demons. However, a lone human managed to build a power there that cannot be questioned, not even by the Demon Kings.</p>
<p>When they ascended thousands of years ago, General Brianna wanted to pursue her own dreams of forming a mercenary team and dominating the world through blood and iron.</p>
<p>She had achieved her dreams, forming the Red Scarf Mercenary Group, an all-women mercenary team featuring some of the most beautiful yet lethal women a man could ever meet.</p>
<p>Over the years, they had undertaken hundreds of missions and completed every single one of them.</p>
<p>Their recent victory had to do with them taking on a church of demons whose evil ways had claimed the lives of many.</p>
<p>This mission was one that many mercenary teams avoided, yet the Red Scarf Mercenary Team took it on, and they came back victorious.</p>
<p>"Big Sister, Eris has sent word. She said Big Sister Sylvara had sent an invitation to visit her star system within the next 100 years.</p>
<p>A rift that leads to a Chaos Region, said to be from the Chaos Era, will be opening, and she has secured slots for us."</p>
<p>Kenina, a Phoenix lady, said as she combed Brianna’s blonde hair after washing it. Being a mercenary doesn’t mean she must have disheveled hair.</p>
<p>"Oh, I see." General Brianna smiled. "How is the Empress, by the way?"</p>
<p>"After her battle with the demigod, she has been in seclusion, attending to her injuries. However, she will be ready by the time the Chaos Rift opens."</p>
<p>"Good then. We must finish up here fast enough and go see her. It has been thousands of years since we last met. I miss her."</p>
<p>Kenina nodded with a smile..."Me too"</p>
<p>Meeting the Empress, as her sisters call her, will indeed be fun.</p>

<p>[Planet Kyros - Yuna’s POV]</p>
<p>After Nefeli left, Yuna became lonely. She is a troublemaker; however, she only causes trouble when her big sister is around.</p>
<p>Now that she is gone, she dares not cause any trouble with her Root Immortal strength.</p>
<p>"Why the gloomy look?" A pale-skinned human woman asked, sitting by her after appearing from thin air.</p>
<p>This lady is Purity, Yuna’s Primordial Purity Soul Flame. After spending thousands of years together, the Primordial Flame Spirit Kent had given her had finally managed to form her humanoid form.</p>
<p>Seeing her sister and master sad and lonely, she decided to console her.</p>
<p>Yuna turned and looked at Purity before placing her head on her shoulder. "I just miss Big Brother Kent. He said he would be gone for only a while, but it has already been thousands of years. A while shouldn’t be that long, right?"</p>
<p>Purity sighed, knowing Yuna was right. A while shouldn’t be that long. However, she also knew that to cultivators, the passage of years is normal.</p>
<p>But then again, she couldn’t help wondering if Kent is even alive out there. For all they know, he is already dead.</p>
<p>Of course, another thought popped into her mind, which she couldn’t help but form a bad plan from.</p>
<p>"Maybe he is not in the Immortal Worlds. Maybe he is still in the High World."</p>
<p>Yuna lifted her body and looked at Purity. "Are you sure?"</p>
<p>Purity smiled and gave Yuna’s hand a gentle squeeze. "Not one hundred percent sure, but there is no harm in checking out that theory."</p>
<p>"Are you saying we should go to a High World to look for Big Brother Kent?"</p>
<p>"I mean, that is the only way to see if he is still alive."</p>
<p>Yuna chewed her lower lip for a few seconds before nodding. "I like this plan. However, we will be suppressed from the Immortal stage to Tier 19 (Heavenly Ascension Stage), so we should prepare before leaving."</p>
<p>Purity smirked. "I am a Spirit Flame, Yuna. I will not be suppressed. I will remain a Tier 22 (Death Stage) Flame, so there is nothing to worry about."</p>
<p>Yuna seemed to have recognized something, so she smiled. "You are a bad influence, Purity. However, if we will indeed be meeting Big Brother, it is worth the risk."</p>
<p>Obviously, the two troublemakers are about to cause so much trouble in the High Worlds they will appear in.</p>
