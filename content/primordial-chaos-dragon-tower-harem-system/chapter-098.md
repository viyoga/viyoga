<p>When Kent teleported, he appeared inside his room, about 40 meters from Saintess Selene. Now that his teleport skill had been upgraded to [Blink], he could teleport anywhere he had seen or imagined.</p>
<p>This meant that if his divine sense detected a place within a 5000-meter radius, he could teleport there. It was a significant upgrade from the previous one.</p>
<p>When he appeared in the room, he sat on his bed and sighed deeply.</p>
<p>The events that had just happened were a lot to take in. In fact, Kent never expected things to escalate that wildly, that quickly. But things happened, and in a way, he had just revealed his plans to his master, who was left flustered.</p>
<p>While that was a bold move on his part, he also started to realize he was not that strong and could be killed at any moment.</p>
<p>The fact that his master’s aura managed to bring him to his knees was enough to tell him he wasn’t invincible. Without a doubt, he was very weak, and at that moment, he understood that. He knew he was weak—very weak, in fact.</p>
<p>"This is bad. I need to get stronger, much stronger," Kent said, re-evaluating his stance.</p>
<p>"Then, on the other hand, if I manage to get inside my master’s pants, I’ll soon become a powerhouse through sex. Won’t that be awesome?" Kent laughed.</p>
<p>[No need to look smug, master. This is just the start for you. Your master will need much more convincing to get to that point.]</p>
<p>The tower dropped its point of view. However, Kent wasn’t about to let the shameless tower rain on his parade.</p>
<p>But he knew he had to put in the work. He had to ensure things between them got steamy, and for that to happen, he needed to get stronger and do amazing things.</p>
<p>These were the kinds of achievements that would raise his status to the point where his master wouldn’t be able to deny him access. But who cared about that? After all, they would always be indoors and together, so he wouldn’t have to worry about it.</p>
<p>"I need to get my affairs in order and go with Master to the sect soon. There’s a lot to be done, and there, I’ll be able to get much closer to her." After setting his affairs in order, he lay down on the bed.</p>
<p>"Hey, Tower, shouldn’t you reward me for being brave and awesome?" Kent asked with a small smile.</p>
<p>[Don’t overestimate yourself, Master. But while your shamelessness is shocking, I must admit that you were awesome, so I will reward you.]</p>
<p>An item entered the Tower storage, and Kent retrieved it.</p>
<p>"Random Item Bingo Card," Kent read aloud, inspecting the name on the card and appraising it.</p>
<p>[Random Item Bingo Card]</p>
<p>-- By channelling your energy into this card, you will receive a random item that can be gold or trash if your luck is high. It all depends on your luck.</p>
<p>"What a cheat item. This means I can get nothing of value or something incredibly valuable," Kent muttered, then decided to test his luck.</p>
<p>He channelled his energy into the card, which caught fire briefly before an item appeared in his palm.</p>
<p>He quickly appraised it.</p>
<p>[Wealth Dice (One-Time Use Item)]</p>
<p>-- A one-time-use dice with six different faces.</p>
<p>-- Roll the dice by channelling your energy into it. Whatever face appears, your wealth (Spirit Stones) will be multiplied by that number.</p>
<p>"Looks like this one’s gold. At least I won’t lose anything if I get a one, so I can only hope to roll higher numbers and multiply my wealth," Kent sighed. Then, with a flick, he rolled the dice.</p>
<p>The next second, the winning face appeared—it was a four.</p>
<p>[You have rolled a four. Do you want your wealth to be multiplied now or later?]</p>
<p>"Let’s do it now, buddy," Kent said with a small smile. While he didn’t mind the idea of unlimited wealth, he knew he’d need much more with more ladies and people joining his tower.</p>
<p>Because of this, he planned to become rich, and using his riches, he could destroy whoever dared to threaten him.</p>
<p>Right now, Li Hua was safe from the Ashland Noble Family, but he knew that wouldn’t last forever. So, he resolved to use his wealth to grow and ensure those close to him also became stronger so he wouldn’t have to worry about their safety all the time.</p>
<p>[Current Wealth: 2.5 Trillion. Wealth has been multiplied by 4.]</p>
<p>[Current Wealth: 10 Trillion. Good luck.]</p>
<p>"That’s more like it. With this, I won’t need to worry about money for the next few years," Kent sighed. He then reviewed the tasks he needed to complete before using the compass to travel to the God Realm.</p>
<p>At the moment, his sword intent, sword qi, and sword body make him someone at the peak of the Sword Master stage. However, he was still lacking in one aspect.</p>
<p>According to the tower’s requirement, he was to practice the 10,002 Sword Plays, strengthening his sword intent and qi.</p>
<p>He would have to practice all 10,002 before being recognized as a Sword Master by the tower. His current progress looks like this:</p>
<p>[Sword Play Progress: 4,000/10,002]</p>
<p>He still had a long way to go and just two weeks to accomplish that before leaving with his master for the sect.</p>
<p>The other plan was to concoct the remaining 60 Tier 2 pills to complete the second out of four requirements for becoming a Pill Grandmaster.</p>
<p>The third requirement could be handled during the Master Alchemist competition in three months. As for the last one, which involved concocting a Tier 3 pill, now that he was rich, he could easily buy a recipe and the necessary ingredients.</p>
<p>Then, aside from that, there was the compass to enter the god realm. He had the spirit stone to pay and the soul bead to charge it for now.</p>
<p>He was only holding back because of what happened during the battle at the Makosa Prison—how he had killed a Root Sage without knowing how.</p>
<p>He wanted to figure that out first before getting into anything else. He also held himself back from doing anything drastic that would endanger him and those close to him.</p>
<p>So, while he would love nothing more than to storm the Ashland Family and start a slaughter, he knew that was a bad idea.</p>
<p>For the next three days, he will focus on alchemy while waiting for the tower to render the recording of what truly happened during the battle at the Makosa Prison.</p>
<p>He moved to his workstation, retrieved the cauldron, and started concocting pills. His first one came out 98% pure, but after two more trials, he pushed it to 100%.</p>
<p>Meanwhile, after Lilian and Unity returned Li Hua to Saintess Selene, they left to go talk to her mother, who had been caught peeking at them during their intimate session with Kent.</p>
<p>Lilian knew if she didn’t do anything, her mom wouldn’t do anything and continue to hold herself back.</p>
<p>If only she had known that, had she held herself back for a long time, things would have gone haywire quickly.</p>
