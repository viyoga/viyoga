<p>The Abyss Dragon, one of the Overlords of the Damned Hell Sea, was brought to its knees with a mere flick of Kent’s hand.</p>
<p>Kent, who is now using 17% of his 20% awakened bloodline, has completely suppressed the terrifying dragon, giving it not even a chance to move.</p>
<p>⟪Your flame resistance has improved tremendously⟫</p>
<p>⟪Your comprehension of the flame has improved tremendously⟫</p>
<p>⟪Your Soul Flame grows stronger⟫</p>
<p>Three messages appeared before Kent’s vision, making him super happy.</p>
<p>He looked at the dragon, whose entire body was radiating death energy, and the smile on his face grew...</p>
<p>[I have underestimated you. You are not a monster... You are the Overlord of Monsters.]</p>
<p>Gaap, the ever-ready hypeman, hyped Kent, causing the Primordial Chaos Dragon to raise his shoulders. The feeling of pride, strength, and ruthlessness overwhelmed his senses.</p>
<p>"What is your name?" Kent asked, looking down at the pitiful Abyss Dragon, a race of dragons said to originate from the Origin Law of Death.</p>
<p>The Abyss Dragon lineage can be traced back to the Primordial Era. They are said to be creatures with the power to manipulate the energy of death.</p>
<p>They are not undead, but they also cannot be classified as living beasts.</p>
<p>Kent had heard of them. After all, the Divine Angelic Titan Race, the same race Kaizo came from, were once allies in their bid to end the reign of the Abyssal Leviathans.</p>
<p>Back when Chaos met that Abyssal Leviathan, he learned something about the Abyss Dragons, so meeting one in person is not just an honor but a chance to grow even stronger.</p>
<p>The Abyss Dragon looked at Kent for a few minutes, deciding whether or not to talk. After all, in actuality, he was an Immortal King, someone one stage stronger than even Gaap.</p>
<p>If this were normal times, he would have obliterated Kent with that tail attack. However, because there were Hell Laws binding him, the small bug now looking down on him had suppressed him.</p>
<p>In the end, he had to give in to Kent’s bloodline power.</p>
<p>"I am Avotha Draconok Siliconik."</p>
<p>[You wouldn’t happen to be from the Siliconik Abyss Family, would you?] Gaap transformed into his demon form and asked.</p>
<p>Seeing Avotha look at him strangely, Gaap smiled and looked at Kent...</p>
<p>[We can’t kill him,] Gaap said.</p>
<p>"Why?" Kent asked. With a few Blood Essences, he could refine the bastard into another Blood Death Seal, which would raise his killing intent up by several levels.</p>
<p>So he planned on killing him. However, hearing Gaap’s words, Kent became puzzled.</p>
<p>[He is from the Siliconik Abyss Dragon Family, a rather powerful Dragon Family in the Universe. They have a whole galaxy to their name. If I have to guess, this bastard is a direct descendant of the main family.]</p>
<p>Gaap pointed at the neck of the Abyss Dragon, whose body was already 700 meters long.</p>
<p>[Do you see that golden reverse scale? It is a sign that he has already undergone three dragon tribulations and has shed three scales already.]</p>
<p>Kent looked at Gaap with a lost expression. "Bruh, I am a dragon, but I don’t know the first thing about dragons. Better enlighten me."</p>
<p>Gaap burst out into resounding laughter, causing Kent to bend his head and blush in embarrassment.</p>
<p>He had yet to really get into learning more about himself from Chaos’s POV. So for now, aside from summoning his wings for flight, claws to kill, and scales for protection, he was next to clueless about dragons.</p>
<p>[I don’t blame you. After all, you are still not even 30 years old and have only been a dragon for what... three years.]</p>
<p>"Give or take."</p>
<p>[I understand. It is not your fault.]</p>
<p>Avotha, who heard this, looked more closely at Kent, and for a moment, he felt like exploding his core. However, he couldn’t. Kent’s suppression also prevented him from harming himself.</p>
<p>However, learning that the person who suppressed him isn’t even 30 years old, he felt both angry and humiliated.</p>
<p>He had been around for over 120,000 years already, yet a mere bug who isn’t even considered an infant simply because he is too young had brought him to his knees.</p>
<p>Gaap read Avotha’s expression and smirked. [Don’t sweat it, buddy. Some people are just built differently.]</p>
<p>Gaap turned back to Kent to explain things to him.</p>
<p>[The reason why the Beast Race is considered the strongest race in the Universe is because of their dangerous yet rewarding evolution path.</p>
<p>When a beast reaches the Immortal level, they undergoes something called Beast Tribulation. The dragons call it Dragon Tribulation.</p>
<p>It is a procedure where, if one emerges victorious, their scales will be tempered and strengthened. Some say every dragon tribulation strengthens the body, soul, and bloodline, giving not less than a 4,000% boost to the dragon.</p>
<p>The next tribulation multiplies the previous by three.</p>
<p>Whenever they undergo this tribulation, their reverse scales change color. The first tribulation gives Bronze, the second gives Silver, and the third gives Gold.</p>
<p>This bastard has a gold reverse scale, meaning he has undergone three tribulations and survived.]</p>
<p>Gaap sighed and smiled before continuing.</p>
<p>[To be honest with you, if you had entered the Hell Road Gate from an Immortal World, you would have died from that one’s attack.</p>
<p>The reason you survived is that the laws prevent the use of his strength after the three tribulations.</p>
<p>However, that isn’t why you shouldn’t kill him.</p>
<p>The main reason is who his father might be.</p>
<p>I don’t know what he is doing here in Hell, but I know even the Hell Monarch—and yes, aside from the 14 Hell Kings, there is a boss called the Hell Monarch—even he wouldn’t dare kill an offspring of the Silconik Abyss Dragon Family.]</p>
<p>"Sounds serious," Kent said, a smile on his face.</p>
<p>[Very serious. However, we also cannot let him off so easily.]</p>
<p>"I know."</p>
<p>Kent thought for a moment.</p>
<p>’If I refine him, I will become much stronger; however, that will make me an enemy of whoever might be backing him.</p>
<p>However, if I let him go, he will also become my enemy in the future. I cannot allow that to happen, not when I have many people linked to me.’</p>
<p>He gazed at the dragon, which was also looking at him as if daring him to kill it and see what happens.</p>
<p>However, Kent smiled as a wicked idea came to mind...</p>
<p>"I would have loved to refine you into a precious Death Seal. However, now knowing about your background, I can’t kill you.</p>
<p>However, I need you as my ride as I dominate the Hell Road. And second, I also want you to become my ally in the future.</p>
<p>I wouldn’t force you, but know that if you accept my offer, I will give you a Supreme Technique precious to all those who cultivate death energy," Kent smiled.</p>
<p>"I believe you’ve heard of the technique called Genesis of Death."</p>
<p>Avotha’s expression changed when Kent said that. The name rang a bell louder than anyone would expect. After all, he came to Hell, traversed the dangerous depths of the Damned Hell Sea, chasing after clues for that technique.</p>
<p>So this half-dragon couldn’t possibly have it... right?</p>
<p>"You are lying," Avotha said, narrowing his eyes at Kent.</p>
<p>"Bitch, please... The Abyssal Leviathans were my friends... the creators of the Genesis of Death technique."</p>
