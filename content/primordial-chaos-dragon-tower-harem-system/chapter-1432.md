<p>During the Primordial Era, when Chaos crazily ventured into the Void Realm, he met and rescued 69 Void Beasts who were considered criminals of their race.</p>
<p>They aided him in his conquest of the Void Realm, and when he was leaving, they followed him.</p>
<p>Later, when the Void Beasts invaded this universe, they stood and fought for him till every last one of them died. They believed in him, and so they gave their all, and thanks to them, this universe still stood.</p>
<p>Several years later, Axiom, Kailan, and Kol, three of the 69, found their way back to their master.</p>
<p>However, by coming to this universe, they felt restricted. They aren’t having as much fun as they did back in their universe. So they always started looking for ways to get entertained.</p>
<p>On the other hand, Keir was once a brave warrior who led many warriors into harrowing battles. He survived many wars and earned a good name for himself. His reputation was unprecedented.</p>
<p>However, behind that perfect persona was a psychopath who loved to prey on the weak and vulnerable ladies.</p>
<p>He had killed and done all kinds of disgusting things to them to the point that when he was finally caught, many lost their belief in what is considered righteous.</p>
<p>The great warrior they all believed in turned out to be a scum of society.</p>
<p>He was rotten to the core, and so he was sent to Planet Kenai, which, to the rest of the universe, is called the Pitt. It is a place where only suffering and pain can be found.</p>
<p>However, while he may be evil, he was also a good warrior, so he adapted, and for thousands of years, he had gathered strength through slaughter and all kinds of despicable means.</p>
<p>He never expected that just when he found a new pool of prey to take advantage of, he would find himself surrounded by three people that, from a mere glance, he knew were even more evil than he was.</p>
<p>Axiom, Kailan, and Kol are Void Beasts, so of course, they are as evil as they come. They had done all kinds of despicable things before they met Chaos. It was through their deeds that all 69 of them were captured and imprisoned.</p>
<p>To be imprisoned in the void realm might mean that, at heart, they are even more evil than the Void Beasts that attacked the universe during the Primordial Era.</p>
<p>Keir knew he was fucked beyond comprehension.</p>
<p>"Looks like he is scared," Kol said, his bow held firm in his grip.</p>
<p>Axiom created a battleground inside the void, so while they found themselves in there, their surroundings were a burnt forest, one where the smell of danger could be sensed.</p>
<p>"Of course, he is scared. I can tell he is a warrior like us, so there is just no way he couldn’t tell we are a danger to his life," Axiom grinned, his expression filled with satisfaction.</p>
<p>Toying with his prey is something he had mastered a very long time ago. As a Void Architect, he can create all kinds of mechanisms that, once a prey falls into them, their fate is sealed.</p>
<p>Keir had fallen into his trap, and so he wanted to take his dear time and enjoy torturing him.</p>
<p>"I suggest we use the Black Beard method. It is the perfect torture for him," Kailan said, already eager to try it. "It is evil, painful, and stimulating. I would very much have the time of my life if we use that."</p>
<p>Axiom turned toward Kol as if to ask for his opinion. "I have no problem with that. As long as he screams, I would be happy."</p>
<p>"Then it is decided, we will use the Black Beard method."</p>
<p>"Great. Now, let’s talk weapons. I suggest we use Void Daggers laced with Pain Stimulant Poison. This way, every cut will increase the pain severalfold."</p>
<p>Kol shook his head. "Daggers are good, but I will use Void Needles."</p>
<p>Axiom nodded. "That can be arranged, but I am with Kailan. We will use Void Daggers while you use the needles."</p>
<p>"I am okay with that arrangement also."</p>
<p>"Then fine. I will use needles, and you guys can use daggers," Kol grinned, turning to face Keir, who was frozen in place, his expression pale. Looking at him tremble, his void heart trembled with delight.</p>
<p>"Let’s talk segments. Since I will be using needles, I think I should take the head while you two share from his neck downwards. You can have him by dividing him vertically into two."</p>
<p>"That is perfect. I will take the left half, and Kailan will take the right."</p>
<p>Once everything had been planned to the last detail, Axiom turned to Keir. "It is your bad luck that you met us in this life. We are the kind of people who lack basic emotions, so what we find funny may be painful to you.</p>
<p>Regardless, when all things are considered, you aren’t a good person either. So I will advise you not to hold a grudge in your next life. Who knows, maybe we are the reality check you needed before you die."</p>
<p>Keir trembled even more. His legs gave out, and he dropped to his knees. He was about to open his mouth to plead, but Axiom moved his finger up and down, and his lips were stitched tight.</p>
<p>"I hate it when they plead. It makes it seem like I am doing something bad."</p>
<p>"Indeed. From where I stand, we are merely doing what many cannot or wouldn’t dare do."</p>
<p>"You should thank us. Not many can boast and say they died at the hands of void beasts after the primordial era."</p>
<p>"Yes. This is a once-in-a-lifetime opportunity, so cherish it."</p>
<p>Tears rolled down Keir’s face. He had done all kinds of evil things, but he had never met people who discussed evil as if it were a good thing.</p>
<p>Axiom and his pals had surpassed his expectations, and that scared him. And with his lips sealed, he had no choice but to let his tears speak for him.</p>
<p>However, seeing the tears fall down his face, the three Void Beasts felt their hearts swell with happiness.</p>
<p>"He is ready, boys. Let’s scar his soul so that in his next life, he will walk the path of righteousness. As the master used to say, when they do evil, punish them with evil."</p>
<p>Axiom waved his hand, and ropes with hooks latched onto Keir’s hands and feet and lifted him into the air. His hands and legs were pierced, making him hang loose in the air.</p>
<p>"Let’s start from 2 km and increase it by 1km every three attacks."</p>
<p>They moved back 2 km, and each retrieved their weapons. Axiom and Kailan drew daggers, while Kol retrieved needles.</p>
<p>Kailan was first to attack. He threw the dagger, which zipped through the air with deadly precision and passed by Keir’s neck, leaving a thin cut.</p>
<p>Keir moaned his pain, more tears falling from his eyes. It was a simple cut, but the pain that came with it was just too much for him to handle.</p>
<p>Axiom attacked next, leaving another cut on his neck. The same reaction came from Keir, who was being stimulated by a special void-rich poison laced on the daggers.</p>
<p>"My turn," Kol grinned and flicked his finger, sending out a small needle directly at Keir’s eye. It stabbed through and came out from the back of his head.</p>
<p>He screamed, which came out as a moan. This made the three of them grin. What followed next were several throws, each leaving a cut or tiny holes.</p>
<p>Six hours later, after millions of throws and cuts, Keir couldn’t take it anymore and died. It was only then that the three Void Beasts stopped.</p>
<p>"That was awesome. We should find more."</p>
<p>Kol’s advice was accepted, and so for the duration of the time Unity would be spending in the cocoon, the three Void Beasts would roam the entire planet, killing one criminal after another.</p>
