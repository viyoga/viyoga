<p>"How are you feeling, Lil?" Elsa asked, holding her daughter’s hand.</p>
<p>"I am fine, Mom. More than fine. Look, I am now a dragon." She smiled, causing her sisters to smile as well. The beauty they knew had now become something else.</p>
<p>She had become stronger, because they could sense her energy, and she had become even more beautiful.</p>
<p>The body she had before had changed completely. The new her is a force and beauty that can shake the hearts of men.</p>
<p>"So, can you turn into a dragon anytime you want?" Mara asked with a curious expression.</p>
<p>"No, at least not yet. I am two races in one body. So, in a way, you can call me a hybrid."</p>
<p>"Care to explain?" she asked again.</p>
<p>"A part of my bloodline allows me to take on a dragon form, which is derived from ice and fire. But another part allows me to be this—this is like a human form.</p>
<p>So, in a way, I am a blend of the two. However, when I turn into my demi-dragon form, you could say I have balanced the two out, like how I can now balance both flame and ice."</p>
<p>"So, you’re saying your human side is more dominant?"</p>
<p>"I wouldn’t say dominant. It’s more a matter of convenience. Taking these two forms doesn’t require me to use my bloodline actively.</p>
<p>I can shift between ice and fire willingly. I can also turn into my hybrid form for eight hours every day if I want. But for my full dragon form, I can only maintain it for three hours a day.</p>
<p>But it’s enough because, in any of my forms, I can use my bloodline abilities."</p>
<p>The ladies nodded with smiles on their faces. They were not blind; they could tell she had indeed become different yet the same, and the aura emanating from her was one they all wanted to feel.</p>
<p>The fire was powerful, yet the ice was also freezing, as it should be. Lilian was now an Arch Mage, meaning she could cast up to Tier 7 spell circles.</p>
<p>And while that alone was fearsome, she had now become even more monstrous because of the bloodline abilities she had awakened.</p>
<p>Kent didn’t want to spend all the time going through them all, but after reading just a few, he could already see his woman rising to the peak with ice and fire.</p>
<p>As for what this means for the future, he will learn it when they meets the dragons.</p>
<p>After all, the legend of the Twin-Breath Tyrant was one known to many dragon races to this day. She was hated by many. After all, she was deemed an abomination of a dragon.</p>
<p>But that doesn’t mean the Frostfire Dragon, hated during the primordial era, didn’t have sympathizers.</p>
<p>She did, and right now, somewhere in the vast universe, millions are celebrating, for the Ignis star has appeared in the sky, meaning the legacy of the long-lost Frostfire Dragon has risen.</p>
<p>This time, she is not alone.</p>
<p>This time, she has a Sentinel as a sister and a Chaos Dragon as a husband. Her legacy is bound to take the universe by ice and fire.</p>
<p>"Congratulations on becoming you, Lil—or should I call you Ignis, or perhaps Nivalis? Of course, I could also call you Aezrith," Kent asked with a smile.</p>
<p>"Call me whatever name you deem fit. You have already invoked my true name, meaning you have supreme authority over me. That is already enough for me to know I will forever be bound to you.</p>
<p>As for my name, you can decide."</p>
<p>He smiled as she rubbed the dragon pressing against her legs.</p>
<p>"Then from now onwards, I will use all four names to refer to you. Lilian is your name, but Ignis, Aezrith, and Nivalis are also your names."</p>
<p>Lilian nodded, and her sisters smiled with her.</p>
<p>Kent then looked toward Selene, who knew she was next. Unity had a different arrangement that Kent hadn’t uncovered yet.</p>
<p>She needed a destiny token to awaken her true name. So Kent could only stare at her lovingly.</p>
<p>But as he looked at her, he could tell she was happy for her sister, who had become a beauty and a powerhouse.</p>
<p>The smile was genuine, and for a moment, Kent felt emotions he had never felt before just from looking at her.</p>
<p>It was like looking at someone who had done something selfless out of love. That smile gave him a sense of déjà vu for reasons he couldn’t yet understand.</p>
<p>’Just who are you, Unity, and why am I feeling this way?’ Kent thought to himself. He looked at her for a few more seconds before focusing back on Selene.</p>
<p>He retrieved the True Name Token, dropped Selene’s blood on it, added his own, and then activated the token, starting the second awakening of the day.</p>
<p>Selene was raised into the air, just like Lilian.</p>
<p>***</p>
<p>Meanwhile, far away in what one would call the core of the universe, a breathtaking beauty who seemed to be watching something amusing smiled.</p>
<p>The image of Kent looking at Unity appeared in front of her.</p>
<p>"So, you still don’t remember her, huh? I guess after going through many reincarnations, your memories will take a while to surface.</p>
<p>But she cannot wait for that long. She has already done enough for you and everyone, my dear Chaos. You promised her in two different lives, and now, that promise may be fulfilled.</p>
<p>The Archer girl who laid down her life so you could move on needs to wake up again. The stable girl who chose to become the sacrifice so her world and the one she loved could live on must be remembered."</p>
<p>She snapped her fingers, and an orb appeared. Her smile bloomed.</p>
<p>"Her name was Artemis, the one who was born to wield the bow. Her name was Love, for her true essence is love in its purest form.</p>
<p>You must fulfil your purpose, Chaos. She has slept for a very long time." The orb left her hand and travelled through the void until it stopped before the Quest Pool, where it fell in.</p>
<p>Kent, who didn’t know his life was about to go on a rollercoaster because of two promises he made years ago, could only stare at the awakening of yet another legendary figure happening in the air.</p>
<p>The lady at the core of the universe continued to watch with a smile on her face. This time, she was staring at Unity, who was smiling at the happenings in the air.</p>
<p>"I await your awakening, sworn sister. You still have a lot to teach me about love."</p>
