<p>The poison sac is being kept in a glass, which was reinforced using formations to prevent any leakages. Such a deadly poison cannot be allowed to leak out like that.</p>
<p>Kent, who heard what Alina said, raised his head and looked at the poison sac. He immediately scanned it using his enchanted eyes.</p>
<p>⟪Name: Bone Melting Poison Sac⟫</p>
<p>⟪Danger Level: Very Dangerous⟫</p>
<p>→ This is a poison sac from a Root Sovereign-Stage Bone Melting Serpent Beast.</p>
<p>→ Just like its name, its venom is very lethal. A single drop can kill anything at the Root Sovereign stage and below.</p>
<p>→ When used with other ingredients, it can create poison pills that can even melt the bones of a Core Formation stage expert.</p>
<p>[The master shouldn’t let this slide. This one is enough to create another subordinate who can specialize in poisons. I can already see a dangerous witch doctor in the making.]</p>
<p>Akira spoke, and Kent smiled.</p>
<p>’This means I will soon be having two undead subordinates.’ His smile widened as he slipped his hand through the opening in Mara’s clothes and gently kneaded her butt.</p>
<p>’I will be saving the two ladies in the coming days, then.’ Kent spoke again, and like a bell, the universe responded.</p>
<p>It was like the universe was waiting for him to speak from the heart. So, the moment he did, they made modifications to his quest.</p>
<p>⟪Quest Updated: The quest title ’Clash with Evil’ has been updated. The universe has sensed your determination.</p>
<p>If you manage to complete the quest within 30 days, there will be additional rewards, which will remain hidden for now.⟫</p>
<p>’More rewards mean more determination to get the job done. I guess I will be taking this venom now,’ Kent said to himself as he observed the current bid.</p>
<p>It had already reached 400 million and was still rising.</p>
<p>Though the venom wasn’t a legendary-grade item per se, its potency led them to brand it as a legendary item, and now it was being sold as one.</p>
<p>However, while nobody knew that, they understood that anything auctioned on the last day of the auction would sell quite well.</p>
<p>"410 million," a man said from one of the VIP boxes.</p>
<p>"450 million," Kent joined the bid, bringing a smile to the faces of everyone who had been expecting him to jump in.</p>
<p>"500 million," the voice spoke again, causing the crowd to buzz with anticipation as the bid entered the stage they had all been waiting for.</p>
<p>"600 million," Kent said.</p>
<p>"700 million."</p>
<p>"900 million," Kent raised it again. He was kind of enjoying the bid but also trying to understand the character of the person he was competing against for the venom.</p>
<p>From the look of things, the person was also wealthy, which made him wonder who that person was. He had already made enemies with three powerful figures; adding more to it would have been a very bad move on his side.</p>
<p>"2 billion," the person jumped a billion and went straight to two.</p>
<p>"5 billion," Kent raised the price by 3 billion. This was a legendary-grade item, but it wasn’t the kind of item that should cause such momentum.</p>
<p>"10 billion," the person raised the price again, bringing a smile to Kent’s face.</p>
<p>’Looks like this person really is after the venom. He must be an alchemist. Too bad, I have a lot riding on this. I have two beauties to rescue and an evil to eradicate.’</p>
<p>"50 billion," Kent raised it again, and this time, the voice didn’t respond immediately. It went quiet for a couple of seconds before a sigh came from the VIP room.</p>
<p>"I will concede this time. Though I really need this venom, the auction is still too young to spend all my money." There was no anger in his voice. He even laughed as he said those words.</p>
<p>This made Kent place him on a level worthy of his respect, unlike the three blacksmiths who immediately started threatening him.</p>
<p>"The senior shouldn’t worry; I will give you ten vials after the auction. If I didn’t also need these poison sac, I would have let you have it," Kent said.</p>
<p>"Hahaha, thank you, youngster. Just a vial would be enough. Ten is just spoiling me," the man spoke, causing everyone to smile at his shameless words.</p>
<p>Ten is never enough, but having one is already sufficient for someone like him.</p>
<p>"Then I will look forward to meeting you, senior," Kent said.</p>
<p>"Same here, and you can just call me Pill Saint Barton," the man said, and everyone’s mouths opened wide.</p>
<p>"Pill Saint Barton? Isn’t he the Pill Saint of the Endorath Kingdom who created the formula for the Bone Strengthening Pill?" someone asked.</p>
<p>"He is, but he said it was just a variation of the real recipe that he discovered many years ago."</p>
<p>"I know, but to be able to complete the recipe—I mean, he is a genius. I wonder why he allowed that brat to have this poison sac."</p>
<p>"He really doesn’t dabble in poison. If it were the Wicked Poison Witch, she wouldn’t have let this brat have it.</p>
<p>But this is also good; at least it shows the brat isn’t a spoiled brat. Him giving ten vials is already more than enough for someone spending 50 billion to buy it."</p>
<p>"True, the spoiled brat is actually a reasonable brat."</p>
<p>Kent scoffed and chose to introduce himself to the man. "I will do well to remember Senior’s name. But you can just call me Kent. I am actually a Pill Master."</p>
<p>"Then we can have more to talk about during our exchange," Pill Saint Barton said, laughing, showing his carefree lifestyle.</p>
<p>"Hey Barton, stop trying to steal the brat from me. I called dibs on him already," suddenly, a voice spoke from VIP room 30.</p>
<p>"No need to be jealous, Sword King Shawn; I won’t take much of his time. You can have him all to yourself when I’m done with him," Pill Saint Barton said, laughing.</p>
<p>"You better," the man, who was identified as the Sword King and had invited Kent for a talk a day ago, said with a chuckle.</p>
<p>Everyone who heard what had just happened exchanged looks, but none of them could man up and say anything.</p>
<p>The fact that two people sitting at the top of power just negotiated who gets to meet Kent first—the same Kent everyone wants to kill—left them bewildered.</p>
<p>Just what is happening?</p>
<p>"Since there are no other bids, the Poison Sac is sold to the young man in VIP room 13 for 50 billion spirit stones,"</p>
<p>Alina said, snapping everyone back to reality.</p>
