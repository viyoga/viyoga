<p>Before meeting the Flame King, Chaos had already heard a lot of stories about him. They claimed he was someone on the same level as him.</p>
<p>They claimed he had single-handedly held back a beast tide that had ten Demigod-level beasts when he was merely an Immortal Lord. They said many things about him, each one sounding more unbelievable than the other.</p>
<p>Eventually, the stories started to include some other people, whom Chaos later came to know as the 24 Dragon Guards.</p>
<p>Before meeting the 69 Void Beasts and recruiting them to become his guides while inside the void realm, who later became his team, Chaos actually saw the 24 Dragon Guards as the most disciplined and terrifying team he had ever met.</p>
<p>The day he and the Flame King met, they fought a great battle. In the end, Chaos defeated him, barely, but he was soon ambushed by the 24 Dragon Guards. He knew he couldn’t defeat them, so he made a run for it.</p>
<p>He escaped and when he was out of danger, he let out a breath and was about to slow down and make his next move when his shadow moved, and before he could react, his ear came off.</p>
<p>It was that day he met Daliah, one of the most dangerous assassins to ever live during the Primordial Era. She cut his ear off, and that humiliation drove Chaos to seek revenge, ultimately resulting in a great battle between him and the Flame King.</p>
<p>He never got that revenge since teh flame King never gave him the chance.</p>
<p>Today, the woman who made him feel the most humiliated during the primordial era appeared before him again, bringing with her all the past pain he had suppressed.</p>
<p>Their eyes met, and for a brief moment, the pain he experienced when his ear came off flowed into his mind. This caused him to sigh and then smirk.</p>
<p>"Nice meeting you again, Daliah."</p>
<p>She stood there for a few seconds, then bowed. "Greetings to you too, Lord Chaos."</p>
<p>Inaya walked to Daliah’s side and pulled her into her arms. Seeing this, Kent chuckled and turned to Uncle Drake. "Let’s take this to the tower."</p>
<p>He nodded, and before long, everyone vanished, including the dead bodies of every last person they had killed. The tied-up demigods and immortal lords were also taken away.</p>
<p>The people who witnessed the battle were all left wondering so many things.</p>
<p>Eventually, they dispersed as well, and the news began to spread widely.</p>
<p>The name Chaos soon started to spread, causing many to wonder if there was a second Kent Madson on the rise. The sheer details in the spread, along with the exaggeration, painted Chaos as some kind of prodigy who appears once every era.</p>
<p>Eventually, word reached an immortal world called Solan. It is the home planet of the Supreme White Tiger Clan.</p>
<p>Many years ago, when Kent was in the Pill World, he accepted a quest to kill a Planet King. Back then, he didn’t know who this Planet King was.</p>
<p>Later, however, he found out he was someone who had absorbed the blood essence of the Forsaken Race, hoping to use unnatural ways to break through the limit of the High World.</p>
<p>Kent succeeded in killing him. However, what he didn’t know was that the Planet King he killed was actually a young prince of the Supreme White Tiger Clan named Kaine. By killing him, he became the enemy of the entire clan.</p>
<p>They knew the one who killed their prince was someone named Chaos. They had placed a bounty on him, and for years now, they had been searching for him.</p>
<p>When word reached them that a kid named Chaos had appeared on a planet not that far from their star system, they deployed five Demigods to bring him in.</p>
<p>Unfortunately for them, Kent was long gone by the time they arrived.</p>
<p>It pained their hearts. However, after making inquiries for a while, they managed to get a drawing of Kent. Along the way, after asking many questions, they also found something very interesting.</p>
<p>It turned out that when Inaya introduced herself to Daliah, she accidentally revealed her true identity. Hearing that she had been with Chaos the whole time, they immediately returned to their home world to report.</p>
<p>"The Ten Emotions Sword Supreme, huh," a man with an overflowing aura—one that not even a Demigod would possess despite him only being at the Demigod stage—muttered, his expression calm.</p>
<p>His mind spun as he tried to recall something buried deep in his memory. After all, he had lived for over a million years, so there was just no telling how much knowledge he had accumulated.</p>
<p>His name is Cyrus, the current patriarch of the Supreme White Tiger Clan in the Immortal World. He became a god 200,000 years ago, but had decided to remain in the Immortal Realm for now while he handled the issue with his son’s murderer.</p>
<p>"Aaha... I remember now. The Ten Emotions Sword Supreme was a legendary swordswoman during the Divine Era. According to the records, she was the Dao Companion of the legendary Lord Genesis.</p>
<p>After the Divine War, nobody knew where she had vanished to. Some say she fell, others say she abandoned this universe and ascended to a much higher plane.</p>
<p>Later, however, it was discovered that she descended to a High World called Brymrr, which was the homeworld of Lord Genesis. She became their ruler, and for years, she had been there.</p>
<p>If she indeed was seen with that bastard, then maybe they are related."</p>
<p>"Then should we send people to Planet Brymrr and see if they can capture or kill that bastard?"</p>
<p>Lord Cyrus looked at his elder with disdain. "Do you even hear yourself? You want us to send our best to Planet Brymrr and hope we get a chance to kill someone who may be under the protection of that monster.</p>
<p>Do you even know how you sound right now? While many only heard stories about Lord Genesis, do not forget that he had a partner who was equally lethal. She was someone who walked side by side with a legend.</p>
<p>Do you seriously think such a person is someone we want to mess with? Even if we send a Supreme to that place, the chances of them returning in one piece are low. We can’t afford to antagonize someone like that right now.</p>
<p>For now, let’s keep an eye on that planet and report whatever you observe. If we manage to get a chance one day, we will take it.</p>
<p>For now, we can only observe and hope the ancestors in the Supreme and Celestial Worlds will have pity on us and send someone to come and aid us."</p>
<p>The elders nodded, all of them sighing in a defeated manner. They had searched for a long time, only to retreat when they finally managed to locate the brat they were looking for.</p>
<p>After the elders left the hall, Lord Cyrus sighed again. "Damnit. Just when I got close, this bastard had to have someone this powerful backing him."</p>
<p>He let out a third sigh, stood up, and walked away. When he was gone, a moth on the wall grew wings and flew away. After a while, it landed in a vast desert and transformed into a young man with butterfly wings and antennae.</p>
<p>He is a Demigod with a wisp of divine qi in his blood.</p>
<p>"I need to report to my Lady." He again took flight and vanished into the clouds.</p>
