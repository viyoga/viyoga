<p>"Ainna, why are you blushing?"</p>
<p>Inaya asked, a bit stunned, looking at her friend, who hid her red cheeks away from her. She had known her for billions of years, and all this time, she had not seen her blush, not even once.</p>
<p>Ainna is the type of lady who has no affection for anyone aside from her craft.</p>
<p>During the Era of the legendary Pill Celestial Kevin Sage, she was just starting out as an alchemist. Back then, her inspiration was Kevin Sage, a man known as the wisest man in the universe.</p>
<p>She was so obsessed with him that she followed in his footsteps, embracing the craft with an open mindset and the confidence to try something new.</p>
<p>One time, she actually met Kevin Sage. But while many blushed and asked for his autograph, she faced him with several questions, her focus fixed on what she had come for.</p>
<p>Inaya herself saw the will her friend carried and knew that nothing romantic would ever stand in the way of her achieving her goal.</p>
<p>Her blushing today was the last thing she expected.</p>
<p>"What did you see?" Seeing that she was not answering her first question, she asked a different one. She had already said she had found Jest, so she wanted to know what she saw that made her blush that profusely.</p>
<p>Ainna said nothing for a few minutes, replaying the sight she saw over and over again. She willed herself to stop thinking about it, but no matter how hard she tried, the sight of Lene sucking Kent’s dick kept replaying in her mind.</p>
<p>After 15 minutes, she managed to push it to the back of her mind.</p>
<p>She then turned and looked at her friend.</p>
<p>"I found him, and as you said, he is indeed in this world. In fact, you may have long guessed it. That scumbag is indeed Kent Madson. He has reincarnated, I suspect, since he is now a Heavenly Ascension stage cultivator and a dragon."</p>
<p>Inaya said nothing for a few minutes, then a smile appeared on her face.</p>
<p>"Where is he currently?" she asked. Ainna, who felt like she had jumped the gun, considering Inaya was now focused on Kent more than the fact that a few minutes ago she was blushing, was quick to answer,</p>
<p>"He is currently living in a hotel in Snow City." She formed a hand seal, and a mark appeared. "I suspect he would be in disguise, so I left my mark on him. This will help you locate him."</p>
<p>"Huh, aren’t you coming with me?" Inaya asked, seeing that her friend, who a few days ago had bragged about how she would torture Jest when she got her hands on him, had given away the one thing she could use to find him.</p>
<p>That is not like her. Naturally, this action made her mind go back to her first question. This was not like Ainna, and Inaya could see that.</p>
<p>"I-I am afraid that if I get close to him, I might just end up killing him. So you can go alone. I will sit this one out." She flashed a fake smile and added, "Plus, I have to prepare for my Pill conference. Concocting a Supreme Pill is not as simple as swinging a sword,"</p>
<p>Inaya locked her gaze on her for a few more seconds before she grabbed her hand. "Nope. You are coming with me. Using alchemy as an excuse was your mistake, Ainna.</p>
<p>I have lived a very long life, so I know that aside from Pill Celestial Kevin Sage, those monsters at the Pill Council, and the 13 Disciples, there is no one below the Pill Celestial stage who can measure up to you.</p>
<p>You have long prepared for this Pill Conference, so don’t bullshit me. You are coming, and there is nothing you can do to avoid that."</p>
<p>Ainna smiled. "Thanks for the compliment, but don’t say those words in public, or else you might lose your head to a cauldron smash. While your lazy bum was asleep, many legendary alchemists appeared.</p>
<p>That said, I really don’t want to come. I might end up actually killing him."</p>
<p>Inaya stood up and retrieved a pill from her space ring. Ainna smiled when she saw the pill. It was the appearance-altering pill she invented when she reached the Pill God stage.</p>
<p>"You said it yourself. Jest is now Kent Madson. The last time I checked, his name appeared on the Pill Dao Tablet, something you have been struggling to achieve for over a billion years already.</p>
<p>Since that is the case, why don’t you get close and see what kind of person he is now? I know I want to see what that scumbag turns out to be in this life.</p>
<p>I have waited a very long time for this reunion." She tossed the pill into her mouth, and at once, her appearance changed, turning from a beautiful elf into a red-haired Flame Human.</p>
<p>Her Peak God stage aura also slowly changed, taking on the form of an Immortal Lord. At once, she went from a drop-dead red-haired elf to a drop-dead red-haired Flame Human.</p>
<p>Aside from the hair, she looked nothing like the Inaya Jest knew.</p>
<p>"Your turn," Inaya said, extending one more appearance-altering pill to her friend. Ainna sighed and knew that no matter what, she would have to listen to her friend and take the pill.</p>
<p>Inaya would drag her if she had to, so going on her own accord, even if she was pressured, sounded like a good plan.</p>
<p>"Don’t get heartbroken if I go overboard and he ends up dead,"</p>
<p>"You can try, but you won’t succeed."</p>
<p>Ainna scoffed and swallowed the pill. At once, she turned into a beautiful, purple-haired Ice Human. Considering her hair color was purple, her ice aura also came out purple.</p>
<p>This appearance-altering pill does more than alter one’s appearance. It transforms them into different people, granting them the abilities they desired before swallowing the pill.</p>
<p>It was one of her many creations that made her famous in the world of alchemy.</p>
<p>"Let’s go then." She activated a formation around her mansion. Inaya smiled and grabbed her hand. They walked out, and after they were free of the formation, she pointed her finger forward, causing Supreme Sword Aura to form into a tiny sword on her finger.</p>
<p>After it fully formed, she pinched the space in front of them, and it cracked. Soon, the crack spread, and before long, it became undone.</p>
<p>A reddish portal opened, releasing a powerful spatial energy.</p>
<p>"Unbelievable, you actually mastered space law to this extent."</p>
<p>Inaya smiled. "That is what happens when you are stuck at the God stage and run out of hobbies."</p>
<p>Ainna just chuckled and allowed herself to be pulled into the portal. Instead of using the void to travel, which, considering the distance between them and Snow City, would take them at least a week, Inaya chose to use space law.</p>
<p>Thirty-four hours later, they arrived in Snow City, appearing 3000 km away from the city walls. They flew the rest of the way, and a few hours later, they found themselves in the city.</p>
<p>Ainna took Inaya to the hotel she saw in her divination. Since she could still feel that Kent was there, it was only logical that they stay close to him.</p>
