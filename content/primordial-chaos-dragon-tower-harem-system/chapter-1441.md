<p>For a moment, Axiom, Kailan, and Kol became stunned, unsure of what to say.</p>
<p>All they could do was stare at the dragon lady holding the baby for a full minute before Kol asked,</p>
<p>"Why are you telling us your names? Is this your way of begging us not to kill you?"</p>
<p>"No. I know you came to kill me. After all, I was sent here, so naturally, I can be considered a criminal. But before I die, I want to make sure you know my name and plead that you spare my daughter’s life."</p>
<p>Kol said nothing, and neither did his friends, Kailan and Axiom. The frowns on their faces, however, continued to deepen. The longer they stared at Queen Eirdis, the more they felt the need to hold back from hurting either her or her daughter.</p>
<p>This feeling left them wondering just why that was. It doesn’t make any sense to them.</p>
<p>"Why should we spare your daughter? For all we know, she is also a criminal," Kailan asked, but even he didn’t like the taste of the words he spoke, and neither did his friends.</p>
<p>They had followed Chaos for years and learned his ways. One rule he had always followed: never to hurt children or the innocent. So asking why they shouldn’t hurt a baby was the lowest he could go.</p>
<p>Queen Eirdis looked at the three Void Beasts for a few minutes before she said something that left them stunned.</p>
<p>"I am a descendant of the Chaos Dragon, and so is my daughter."</p>
<p>Axiom looked at his friends, stunned. Name-dropping the Chaos Dragon was right on the nose, so they knew they had to proceed with caution.</p>
<p>After a few minutes, he waved his hand, and the pressure that had been suppressing them vanished. This caused Queen Eirdis to take a step back.</p>
<p>"Relax, lady, we won’t hurt you, but you have to come with us."</p>
<p>"Where to?"</p>
<p>"To meet the people who can confirm if you are telling the truth or not." Axiom pointed forward, and the void opened. Before entering, he turned to Queen Eirdis and warned her, "A word of advice. If you are lying, then you had better come clean now before it is too late."</p>
<p>He gave her a minute to recount what she said, but she stood by her words. Axiom sighed, then walked into the crack in the void. Kol followed, then Kailan went after him.</p>
<p>Queen Eirdis stood there for a few minutes before walking into the crack as well. After she entered, it closed behind her.</p>
<p>The next time it opened, it was 100 meters from where Vexthra and her team were training. A few of them were done with their training, so the moment the void opened, they turned toward it.</p>
<p>Seeing Axiom walk out, they sighed. However, when Queen Eirdis walked out, their attention was shifted to her.</p>
<p>"Who is that?" Kaizo was quick to ask.</p>
<p>"Everyone, this is Queen Eirdis and her daughter, Xandira." Axiom paused for a moment. "She said she is a descendant of the Chaos Dragon."</p>
<p>They all widened their eyes, mirroring the reaction Axiom and his pals went through.</p>
<p>"Did I just hear that right?" Elaine asked, standing up from her lotus posture. The rest of her sisters, who had finished their training, followed her lead and also stood up.</p>
<p>"Are you really a descendant of the Chaos Dragon?" she asked, walking toward the Queen and her daughter, who was sound asleep in her arms.</p>
<p>"Yes."</p>
<p>"How is that possible?" Suddenly, Vexthra’s voice rang out in everyone’s ears. It appeared she had also completed her training.</p>
<p>"The Primordial Chaos Dragon had no descendants because he never married. So how can he have descendants?"</p>
<p>The question instantly made everyone become suspicious of the lady. Subconsciously, they all started preparing to attack if she turned out to be a fake.</p>
<p>But what she said next shocked them.</p>
<p>"He indeed never had a family, but his bloodline did. During the Primordial Era, he blessed a young dragon by giving him a drop of his blood essence.</p>
<p>Maybe he knew what he was doing, or maybe he didn’t know his blood would have such an effect. However, this young dragon, after taking his blood, transformed, becoming a version of the Chaos Dragon.</p>
<p>Over the years, his power grew, and for eras, he passed down his bloodline to a single member of his family.</p>
<p>It had finally reached my turn, but two years ago, some members of my family, alongside some members from a very powerful dragon family, colluded to capture me and..."</p>
<p>Queen Eirdis paused and looked at her baby.</p>
<p>"From the first member of my family to ever get the Chaos Dragon bloodline to date, none have gone beyond the God grade. However, my daughter was born with a Supreme-grade Chaos bloodline."</p>
<p>"So they wanted to take it from her and give it to someone else?" Vexthra asked, and Queen Eirdis nodded.</p>
<p>"You have to believe me. I wouldn’t lie about such a matter."</p>
<p>Nobody said anything for a few more minutes before Mara asked, "How then are you in this godforsaken place? The last I learned, only criminals are sent here, so why are you here, and with a baby at that?"</p>
<p>"This is the only place I could think of to keep myself alive long enough to ensure my daughter grows up and awakens her bloodline fully."</p>
<p>"Smart move..." Vexthra nodded, but then a question popped into her mind. "Where is the one who received the blood in the first place? I am sure they would be happy to learn someone down his bloodline had awakened a Supreme-grade bloodline."</p>
<p>Queen Eirdis sighed before answering, "I don’t know. But there are rumours going around in the family that he vanished billions of years ago after having a falling out with the Universe Council."</p>
<p>"The Universe Council again, huh? They really are bold," Velyra muttered, her expression calm, though a look of craziness could be seen behind her red pupils.</p>
<p>"Looks like it."</p>
<p>After learning what the Universe Council did to Kent in the past, they are all angry and would probably do everything in their power to pay them back in every painful way.</p>
<p>They had caused Kent pain in the past, and so there is nothing that can stop them from having their revenge. Now that they’ve heard what Queen Eirdis said, they have more motivation to bring their A game in the future.</p>
<p>They remained standing there, unsure of how to proceed.</p>
<p>Although Queen Eirdis seemed to be telling the truth, the mere fact that she was in such a horrible place, meant only for criminals, made them hesitate to believe her.</p>
<p>However, seeing the baby in her arms, they knew they had to make a decision. After some quick thinking, Vexthra decided to take them to the Tower City, where Avotha and Vargus would keep an eye on her.</p>
<p>The last thing they wanted was for her to cause trouble. Once Kent is done forming his second Flame Core, he will find out if she is indeed telling the truth or not.</p>
<p>Of course, while there is not much she can do considering the Tower is always active, making sure she is aware she is being watched for now will put everyone at peace.</p>
<p>Thankfully, a couple of days later, Kent successfully formed his second Flame Core.</p>
