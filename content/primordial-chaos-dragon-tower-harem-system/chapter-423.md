<p>In the god realm, Vexthra’s domain...</p>
<p>Vexthra had just finished the latest Chapter of her favorite novel, so she threw her device aside and glared at it.</p>
<p>"Damn cliffhangers," she muttered before leaving her bed to prepare a fruit salad. Naturally, she had all the items she needed, so she made herself a mouth-watering salad and went back inside.</p>
<p>She dug in, and a few minutes into her eating, an idea popped into her mind.</p>
<p>"This bastard. When he comes here, I’ll make sure he won’t be able to walk for a year." A wicked smile appeared on her face.</p>
<p>And since she was a being of hatred and resentment, her wicked smile was rather sinister.</p>
<p>"I wonder what he’s doing now." The wicked smile turned into a curious one, so she reached out to Unity, who was akin to her baby sister among all her sisters.</p>
<p>Unity was the first mortal she talked to aside from Kent after she became a god, so she held a special place in her heart.</p>
<p>Also, knowing she couldn’t gain her true name using normal means, she understood that being surrounded by overly powerful and beautiful ladies every day could be overwhelming; she was deeply connected to her alone.</p>
<p>She asked what Kent was doing, and Unity narrated everything to her.</p>
<p>"This bastard. Won’t he go to that Pill Master Competition?" Vexthra asked.</p>
<p>"He still has time, big sister. Our man is someone who has a lot of time on his hands," Unity smiled.</p>
<p>"That’s true. We’re lucky to have him, then," Vexthra smiled, remembering when Kent first made a move on her.</p>
<p>She knew nothing about love back then, so she was just going with the flow. Of course, she tried to resist, but she couldn’t, for she wasn’t able to muster that resistance against him.</p>
<p>But the moment she accepted him, she discovered something called happiness, despite her Law being that of Hate and Resentment.</p>
<p>"They’re done with the second round, so he’ll be reaching out soon," Unity reported, and Vexthra smiled. She hurriedly ate her plate of fruit salad and waited.</p>
<p>It didn’t take long for her smile to bloom.</p>
<p>[Hello there, my heartbeat. I hope you and Unity had a nice chat,] Kent said with a smile, making Vexthra blush.</p>
<p>"You saw her, huh?" Vexthra asked.</p>
<p>[Indeed. Unity is just too innocent and sweet, so she wasn’t good at sneaking around.] When he saw Unity spying on him, he just laughed, for she was rather cute doing the sneaking.</p>
<p>There is something about that just makes her too cute and innocent.</p>
<p>"Don’t punish her, okay? Doing so will mean her big sisters will have to step in, and since we wouldn’t want them beating you up, just reward her instead," Vexthra smiled.</p>
<p>"Don’t worry, Unity is a doll. I wouldn’t want to punish her. But her reward will leave her panting for breath," Kent laughed, causing Val to blush.</p>
<p>"Hurry and add my sister. I want to hear her voice," Vexthra demanded. Kent smiled before telling Gaia to add her.</p>
<p>The moment she was added to the harem, the goddess reached out. Val could only enter the emotional rollercoaster that all her sisters went through.</p>
<p>Kent took that time to review his gains for this time; he actually felt two new changes. One is foreign, so he went to check immediately.</p>
<p>⟪Congratulations, Master. You have added the 11th woman to your harem: Valerie (Val).⟫</p>
<p>⟪You have received an item from the universe: Realm Comprehension Tablet⟫</p>
<p>⟪You have received +110,000 Soul Essence and +6,800 Stat Points.⟫</p>
<p>"My Soul cultivation has finally reached the Tier 10 stage, making me someone akin to a Root Ascension cultivator. This is great," Kent smiled, but his focus was on the second message.</p>
<p>"I have received an item instead of a skill, and this time straight from the universe."</p>
<p>⟪Congratulations, Master, you have received something grand this time,⟫ Gaia spoke, making Kent immediately summon the item and appraise it.</p>
<p>It was a small golden tablet.</p>
<p>⟪Name: Realm Comprehension Tablet⟫</p>
<p>⟪Grade: Supreme⟫</p>
<p>→ A Supreme tablet imbued with the power of the Dao.</p>
<p>→ When comprehending your realms in front of it, the chances of you gaining clarity and attaining enlightenment are 100% guaranteed.</p>
<p>[Note: This only helps you to comprehend whatever you can imagine and understand. The Dao is profound, and making mistakes cannot be reversed.</p>
<p>So comprehend what you can imagine. It can only keep growing endlessly as you grow in your understanding of the Dao.]</p>
<p>Kent was left sitting with his mouth wide open as he stared at the messages.</p>
<p>⟪Are you blown away, Master?⟫ Gaia asked, causing Kent to change his expression.</p>
<p>"Are you kidding me? This is a treasure among treasures. It’s one-of-a-kind, only below the Tower." Kent wanted to burst into resounding laughter, but he managed to hold himself back.</p>
<p>⟪It’s good the Master recognizes greatness. Kindly drop your blood on it, and the Tower will handle the rest. Once again, congratulations, Master.⟫</p>
<p>Kent quickly dropped his blood on the stone, and it vanished from sight.</p>
<p>⟪It will take some time, but once it is done, the Dao Tower will awaken, where you and everyone you want can enter to comprehend the Dao, thus forming your realms.</p>
<p>If any of you form your realm at this level, your future prowess will be limitless. A mage with a Realm, I can’t wait to see that ag-⟫</p>
<p>Gaia nearly overspoke.</p>
<p>Thankfully, she held herself back. But Kent knew there was something there. However, since she stopped, it meant it would be a mistake or perhaps dangerous for him to dig deeper.</p>
<p>He would let it slide for now, but he would get answers later. Just now, she was about to reveal something she shouldn’t have.</p>
<p>⟪Talk to you later, Master.⟫</p>
<p>Kent sighed.</p>
<p>Looking at Val, he knew she deserved all the rewards he would be giving her.</p>
<p>Thus, when she was done with Vexthra, an intense marathon began, and for hours, the Saintess was reduced to a moaning saintess who just couldn’t get enough of her man.</p>
<p>By daybreak, she passed out smiling.</p>
<p>That was when Kent stopped and smiled. For a man to cause his woman to pass out from pleasure meant he had accomplished his role as a man.</p>
<p>He lay beside her, and later, when they woke up, they went one more round before going to meet the rest of the ladies.</p>
<p>Kent was about to leave for the Pill Master competition.</p>
