<p>⟪Looks like the universe is spoiling you, Master,⟫ Akira said with a giggle as she tinkered with the World Teleporter.</p>
<p>"It’s only natural to spoil such an amazing person like myself. Have you ever heard of a Great Sage facing a Spirit Lord before?" Kent asked with a smile.</p>
<p>⟪I guess you’re right, but this is just too much for someone at your level. Do you have any idea what many have to go through and what amount they have to spend just to do interplanetary teleportation?⟫ Gaia asked.</p>
<p>"They are not me, so their loss."</p>
<p>⟪This is indeed a good item. It will help us check up on the state of affairs in the world of Fedora and perhaps create some hindrance for the Orc King.</p>
<p>This will help us buy more time for the resistance. ⟫ Dori analyzed the importance of the teleporter and already saw the potential it held.</p>
<p>"That is exactly what I was thinking. With this, we can go there and perhaps make him vomit blood. For now, he will be in a comatose state for at least five years. By then, I will be close to reaching the peak of this world.</p>
<p>I’m sure by then, I can face him and not run to my mommy."</p>
<p>After playing with the teleporter for a while, Kent moved to the Legion Tower, where Veydris was in hibernation mode, and Kaizo, just like his master, was basking in their success.</p>
<p>As for Sylara, she was drinking blood from a Tigerkin she had taken from the battle.</p>
<p>Uncle Drew was also around. He was the last to teleport back into the tower after dropping off all the prisoners.</p>
<p>Luckily, their families were still around, so they would find them sleeping a few meters from their homes the next day.</p>
<p>"Kaizo, you should probably rest for the next few weeks. I’m afraid after handling things on the outside, the next few years will be spent moving from one battle to the next."</p>
<p>"Bring it on, Master. I am ready for anything," Kaizo said confidently. Perhaps after getting beaten in the coming dungeon battles, he would learn to cool off a little.</p>
<p>After talking with Uncle Drew for a while, Kent moved back to the Sword Tower to use one of the three Memory Keys to unlock more memories of his life as Jest.</p>
<p>He planned to unlock one and assimilate its effects before moving on to the next. But he intended to use all three before the Pill Master competition, which was coming up in seven days.</p>
<p>However, the moment he arrived inside the Sword Tower, Selene called him—the ladies had awakened thanks to the Celestial Dew of Rebirth he had given them.</p>
<p>He quickly went over, and the moment he arrived inside the garden, he saw the ladies seated around a picnic basket, eating fruit.</p>
<p>Nara and Zaila were surrounded on all sides, smiling and talking to each other.</p>
<p>It was a nice sight to behold, and Kent, who saw how happy they were now, could only curse the late sect master for making them suffer all these years.</p>
<p>"Ladies," Kent said, walking toward them. Thankfully, his ladies had chosen to put aside their shamelessness and wore clothes so they were all decent and well-dressed.</p>
<p>Of course, they wouldn’t wear such clothes outside, but inside, they were free to wear revealing outfits. Nara and Zaila were no different in that regard.</p>
<p>Their shapes and assets are nearly showing for the Dragon Lord to appraise. However, Kent wasn’t being shameless in that department today.</p>
<p>"Kent!" Val was quick to jump into his arms, welcoming him as if they had been apart for years.</p>
<p>In truth, the mission in Fedora had taken just 17 hours. But that was 17 days for them, so it was indeed a long time for all of them.</p>
<p>Kent hugged her and made sure she was comfortable before looking at Nara with a smile.</p>
<p>"So, this is you. I didn’t get a good look at you the last time, and since the previous meetings were with a bat, I have to say, you’re not bad-looking at all," Nara said, now full of energy.</p>
<p>"It’s only natural for someone like me to be handsome," Kent replied, making Nara laugh.</p>
<p>"Handsome, capable, and shameless," Nara said, turning to look at her sister. "I approve."</p>
<p>Mara blushed upon hearing her sister’s words. However, a warmth spread across her chest as she listened. Nara was her idol.</p>
<p>She was the one who had protected her when they were in danger. Nara had used her body as a shield against attacks from all the dangers they had faced together. Now, hearing her approve of her relationship, Mara felt overwhelmingly happy.</p>
<p>"I guess now that I have your approval," Kent said, turning to look at Mara, "you won’t be getting any sleep."</p>
<p>Mara had to hide her face behind her sister, blushing so profusely that if her disciples were to see her, they would probably draw their weapons and charge at her.</p>
<p>The Mara they knew was not the same as the one blushing profusely now.</p>
<p>"I want to take you all somewhere, but since this is a picnic, let’s finish the basket first," Kent said, sitting down in the waiting arms of his ladies. The picnic resumed.</p>
<p>"Zaila, huh? A beautiful name," Kent said, making the spear genius, who was supposed to have died twenty years ago, smile.</p>
<p>"Thank you for saving us," Zaila said.</p>
<p>"Don’t worry about it. It was the right thing to do," Kent replied, causing his ladies to roll their eyes.</p>
<p>"You ladies don’t have to be like that. We all know Camilla is a beauty, and having such a beauty by my side is a blessing. Making sure she becomes happy is what I wanted, and now, look at her—she’s over the moon."</p>
<p>Camilla, just like Mara, was also blushing. She had already confessed to Zaila about her decision to take Kent as her man, and just like Nara, Zaila had approved.</p>
<p>Naturally, Zaila had tried to encourage her master to get into the market decades ago, but Camilla had never listened. So, hearing that she now had a man in her life, Zaila couldn’t have asked for a better welcome-home gift.</p>
<p>They ate the fruits, joked, laughed, and reminisced about many things. When they were done, Kent teleported them to the tower city, where they were thrust into the most elegant city they had ever seen.</p>
<p>The moment they appeared, a loli in a cute little suit approached them. "Welcome to Gaiaville, the dream city."</p>
