<p>It took Kent five minutes to reach the second level.</p>
<p>The steps leading to the 2nd level were much more challenging to climb, considering the air there was comparable to the core region of the Flame Valley.</p>
<p>Kent had already been there, so it was easy for him to gauge that.</p>
<p>However, with his ’Flame Resistance,’ ’Diamond Body Cultivation,’ ’Eternal Draconic Jade Body,’ and finally, his ’Chaos Soul Flame’ at work, he walked upward like he was taking a stroll across the countryside.</p>
<p>On the way, he passed by a few dozen geniuses doing everything they could to climb to the second floor.</p>
<p>He saw a few who he knew wouldn’t be able to make it and would have to go back to the first floor. He passed by a few who could make it, but whose chances of learning a Flame Art would be slim, and then those who knew what they were doing and would gain something from it.</p>
<p>None of these people moved Kent’s heart, so he walked by them as if they were nothing. When he saw those he knew would benefit him, he knew.</p>
<p>So, without glancing at anyone, he arrived on the second level, where he saw quite a few already seated, learning the techniques they had chosen.</p>
<p>’Legendary grade is not bad, and like the Elder said, as one’s mastery of their Flame increases, so would the output of the techniques regardless of their grade.</p>
<p>It’s just like sword aura and intent. The stronger the energy, the more powerful the skill used becomes.’ Kent noted that down and chose to move on.</p>
<p>He can get a legendary grade technique for as low as 300,000 tower points, so they were useless to him now. However, he paid the tower 5 million tower points to copy all 70 techniques.</p>
<p>They will have their use later. For now, he could only move on.</p>
<p>Kent left the second level and started moving toward the 3rd level.</p>
<p>After climbing three steps, three messages appeared before his eyes.</p>
<p>⟦You have absorbed a large sum of Flame Essence.⟧</p>
<p>⟦Your Flame Resistance grows stronger.⟧</p>
<p>⟦Your Chaos Soul Flame grows stronger.⟧</p>
<p>"Looks like the real deal starts from here." Kent smiled, and then, without even doing anything, the tower activated his cultivation technique.</p>
<p>⟦Your Primordial Infinity Dragon Art has activated. Your cultivation speed is now 40,000 times faster.⟧</p>
<p>Kent smiled, reading the new message. He had even forgotten about his cultivation technique, as he had to dual-cultivate with his wives to grow stringer instead of cultivating like everyone else.</p>
<p>However, now that it had activated, Kent saw how impressive it was. It turned out he was much more amazing than he let on, and that was evident when the technique activated.</p>
<p>All the heat on the steps vanished.</p>
<p>To Kent, the steps leading to the 3rd level were just normal steps, since all that was meant to block him was absorbed, or more accurately, devoured, sharing everything equally among his Body Cultivation, Soul Flame, Flame Resistance, and his True Cultivation, which began to soar.</p>
<p>"It was 4,000 times when I got this world. But now that I have awakened 16% of my bloodline, the speed has increased."</p>
<p>Kent kept moving, knowing full well that this was all thanks to the tower, which, after all, he had created in his past life.</p>
<p>"I guess I am just that impressive," he laughed, moving past the first person also climbing to the third floor.</p>
<p>Kent moved forward for a while and then stopped and turned back to the Elf he had just passed.</p>
<p>’With the tower’s protection, I can kill this dude and get away with it, but that would be a waste. Looking at his determination, he has some potential in him.’</p>
<p>He looked at him for a few seconds and then took a spirit grapefruit from his space ring and threw it to him.</p>
<p>"When you reach your limit and can’t go any further, eat that. Of course, don’t try climbing to the 4th floor since you don’t have the means."</p>
<p>With that, Kent left, leaving the elf to contemplate whether or not to eat. After a few internal struggles, he stored the fruit inside his space ring and kept climbing.</p>
<p>He would hold on to that for now until his limit wouldn’t allow him to climb any further.</p>
<p>⟦Why did you help him master⟧? Just when he was a few steps away from the Elf, the tower asked. Kent just smiled.</p>
<p>’He looks like an actor I worked with back on Earth. Although he was human, if we put a prosthetic ear on him, the two would be practically twins.’</p>
<p>If the tower were human, she would have probably laughed.</p>
<p>Kent was messing with the tower, but a part of what he said was genuine. Though his main reason for helping the Elves was so that if one day, the Elves and the Half-Elves found peace, he would make him a Guardian Hand.</p>
<p>Until then, that was all the help he could render, and he did the same for all those he passed by on his way to the 3rd floor.</p>
<p>They will thank him later, and if in three years they manage to meet his expectations, he will take them to a much bigger pool where they can grow endlessly.</p>
<p>When Kent arrived on the 3rd level, he saw a few others deep in their learning processes.</p>
<p>These ones were the geniuses the Supreme Immortal sect was truly looking for. They were the geniuses who could further strengthen the sect.</p>
<p>After ensuring his friends weren’t there and copying all the techniques, he began moving to the 4th level. His strength continued to rise as he climbed.</p>
<p>Meanwhile, after three hours, the seven youngsters Kent had helped on the first level each completed absorbing the fruits he had given them, and they began the techniques he had told them to master.</p>
<p>They felt stronger, smarter, and, more importantly, determined.</p>
<p>However, nothing prepared them for what happened when they entered the technique space to learn their techniques.</p>
<p>The first person was able to master all five forms of the technique in just 10 minutes. When Lorin, the beautiful Qilin lady, opened her eyes, she felt as though she could fly through the steps leading to the second level.</p>
<p>And she wasn’t the only one; the others felt the same way when they opened their eyes in less than 15 minutes.</p>
<p>On the outside, the display board showed the results of what was happening inside the tower for all to see, and for the first time, everyone swallowed nervously.</p>
