<p>A lot happened while Kent was out working on his soul path all those years. First was the awakening of his ladies.</p>
<p>Before he entered the deep sleep, only Elsa, Neomi, Ingrid, Selene, Alina, Camilla, Vari, Lani, Nara, Elaine, and Lilian had awakened their Primordial spirits.</p>
<p>However, a few years after Elaine emerged from her Primordial Chamber, awakening the Life and Death Space, Mara also awakened her Primordial spirit and emerged from her Chamber with the most elegant space among her sisters who had already awakened their Primordial spirits.</p>
<p>"Angel Space."</p>
<p>That was the name of the Primordial World, and just as its name suggests, it is beautiful. Naturally, Mara’s true name originated from the Moonlit Angel race, so this means she is a Moonlit Angel.</p>
<p>Her Angel Space embodies all the best aspects of her, from the cosmic river to moon streams, gardens, floating islands, and many more.</p>
<p>Back when the true Angel race was still in this known universe, there were some beautiful butterflies called Angel Butterflies. She has most of those inside her Angel Space.</p>
<p>They don’t have much use now, but she can fly on their backs both inside her Angel Space and on the outside. That demonstrates their usefulness.</p>
<p>She joined her sisters on the stairs.</p>
<p>A month later, Val also came out with the same Space as Selene. The two rivals actually awakened the same space.</p>
<p>Of course, every Primordial Space/world is unique, so despite awakening the Lunar Space, Val’s own is different. For starters, her space is suspended in the clouds and has two half moons instead of the full moon in Selene’s own.</p>
<p>It was a sight to behold. Also, she awakened something similar to Nara’s Hell Flame Art. Her own is the Lunar Flame Art, meaning she had awakened the infamous Lunar Flame.</p>
<p>This made her someone to be feared. However, her awakening was overshadowed when Velyra came out with a rather powerful space.</p>
<p>"Blood Space."</p>
<p>Velyra awakened Blood Space, one that conveys its true meaning. Neomi’s Killing Space is dangerous, but Velyra’s Blood Space is terrifying.</p>
<p>Aside from the Blood Sea inside and the fact that the majority of the things are red, she awakened a Primordial Spirit who came out sitting at the Tier 18 stage with a perfect Harmony affinity for blood.</p>
<p>That was something none of her sisters saw coming, but she walked out with blood Qi oozing from her body, showing the red-skinned beauty was no longer just a follower of the Mother of Killers.</p>
<p>She had become a killer herself, and the arsenal at her disposal was just too much for someone to wield. She also awakened an Art of a kind.</p>
<p>"Blood Berserker Art."</p>
<p>Even after her Primordial Spirit regressed, she remains a formidable warrior who will wreak havoc on the battlefield.</p>
<p>After her, Princess Lovina also walked out. She awakened a rather sinister space that perfectly matches her wicked Poison Witch personality.</p>
<p>"Poison Space."</p>
<p>Her Primordial Space is rather dangerous. Everything inside the space is poisonous or can be turned into poison. In fact, she has one large cauldron inside her Poison Space that can be used to cook poison.</p>
<p>And since her True Name hasn’t been awakened yet, she wouldn’t be able to start following the Ancient Path like her sisters.</p>
<p>So her Tier 17 Primordial Spirit was hard at work, creating some of the most dangerous poisons there are.</p>
<p>In her defense, she believed that with the right poison, everything can be settled. It was a twisted way of thinking, yet being one of them now, the ladies just laughed.</p>
<p>However, they couldn’t deny the fact that Princess Lovina’s poison affinity was now on a different level. If she had awakened her Primordial Spirit before going for revenge, the battle would have ended in under ten minutes.</p>
<p>After she awakened her Primordial Spirit, three years passed before the next sister walked out. This time it was Vexthra, the goddess herself.</p>
<p>She walked out smiling because, finally, she knew her dream to fight beside her man had come true. She awakened a space that matches her divinity.</p>
<p>"Hateful &amp; Resentful Space."</p>
<p>One interesting thing that happened when she awakened her space was that the other path, which is the Dao Path, was mastered by her, or more importantly, her Primordial Spirit mastered it.</p>
<p>She came out sitting on the 7th stage of Goodhood, the ’God Lord’ stage, showing that she had comprehended 1,000 Daos (Laws).</p>
<p>This means that at any moment, if she wanted, she could break through and become a Supreme. However, she had to take it slow and first handle the Ancient Path.</p>
<p>This meant she had to regress her Primordial Spirit to Tier 1.</p>
<p>This, of course, was what Vexthra was after. Though fighting with a Primordial Spirit is dangerous, she is a god; she can easily handle a bunch of mortals, even if she is at a lower Tier.</p>
<p>So, she regressed her Primordial Spirit to Tier 1, and that expanded her Primordial Space to several million miles in diameter.</p>
<p>It became so large that Vexthra felt her True God cultivation base strengthen even further. And the best part was that her Law of Hatred and Resentment continued to improve as well.</p>
<p>It was safe to say the Vexthra who came down from the god realm wouldn’t be returning the same anymore. She had become very powerful and happy.</p>
<p>After she was done, she decided to supervise her sisters as they climbed the stairs. Then, three years after Vexthra came out, Unity finally walked out of her Primordial Chamber, oozing the aura of love.</p>
<p>Cherry blossoms, like an angel of love, literally surrounded her. The air around her was subtle and filled with positive energy.</p>
<p>Her sisters stopped all they were doing because the moment Unity walked out, they all felt the need to surround her and feel the love oozing from her. It was like they were bewitched.</p>
<p>Even Vexthra was swept by the aura coming from her sister. As someone who harbored hatred, this was like an awakening of the love buried deep within her soul.</p>
<p>"Love Space."</p>
<p>That was the name of the space Unity awakened. A beautiful space filled with love. Everything there was made with love and for love.</p>
<p>However, while that was awesome, the most shocking part was her Primordial Spirits.</p>
<p>Yes, Unity awakened two Primordial Spirits.</p>
