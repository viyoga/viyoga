<p>Gaia will soon be done with the city, but before that, Kent wanted to test out how efficient his newest ability is.</p>
<p>He had just awakened it, and since he has quite a lot of Soul Spirits, the next step was to see if he could create soul weapons from them.</p>
<p>Right now, he doesn’t know the skill’s limit or whether there is one. For all he knew, with enough Soul Spirits, he could create a god-level soul weapon.</p>
<p>And if he goes through the route of turning Soul Spirits into Soul Warriors, then he might be walking around with literal gods soon.</p>
<p>That would be cool too. The ability to summon gods from deep within his soul would be one hell of a flex. Of course, he knew things aren’t always rainbows and sunshine, so he could only hope.</p>
<p>After he arrived in the Soul Tower, Kent quickly entered the Soul Universe, knowing that inside the Soul Tower, he could easily slip in and out of the most dangerous place known to man—the Soul Universe.</p>
<p>Once outside, he can’t easily do that since he risks others slipping in uninvited.</p>
<p>As usual, when he appeared inside the Soul Universe, a smile appeared on his face.</p>
<p>Looking at seven colossal asteroids and 3000 meteors just waiting to be summoned made him super happy.</p>
<p>But aside from them, there are also thousands of spears and head-sized explosives Kent made purposely for attacking the soul.</p>
<p>The asteroids, meteors, and spears are meant to attack both the body and the soul. However, the soul bombs are for only the soul.</p>
<p>They were his latest creations, so seeing hundreds of thousands of them made him grin. The worlds he had added to his guardian tower sure are feeding him with soul essence.</p>
<p>"I’ve worked really hard for all this. One day, I will show just how deadly I am in combat." Kent clenched his fist. All his preparations had been for when he would need them.</p>
<p>Now, he would be venturing into the one place that is making even his skin crawl.</p>
<p>Back when he entered the Upside Down Underworld Forest, it was to train and prepare for a mission. He wanted to take on the appearance of a powerful beast that lives there.</p>
<p>However, in the end, he abandoned the idea since getting through the millions of beasts there was a headache.</p>
<p>He failed that mission, but he left with valuable knowledge that couldn’t have come at a better time. Although he couldn’t get what he wanted back then, that knowledge now gave Kent an idea of what to expect.</p>
<p>So coming here to try his hand at the [Soul Weapon] skill wasn’t just to test the use of this new ability, but also to see if he could create something he could use when there.</p>
<p>While his legion is free at the moment, Kent doesn’t plan on sending them into the maw of danger simply because he has them. They have their missions, and so he would use his own way to get this mission done.</p>
<p>He teleported and appeared in a different section of the Soul Universe, where thousands of Soul Spirits sat in lotus posture, absorbing the abundance of Soul Essence in the air.</p>
<p>The beasts that can’t sit, hover in the air, while most slept on the ground, absorbing the Soul Essence.</p>
<p>When Kent arrived, they all opened their eyes and turned to him. One could see the respect in their eyes as they gazed upon him.</p>
<p>The organisation is strange, as one would expect. The Soul Spirits are in groups of several hundreds or tens of thousands.</p>
<p>Most of the groups comprise people he had killed in a particular battle.</p>
<p>He could see the dragons he had just killed, the angels and aliens he had slaughtered during his adventurous time in the Paragon Universe, and the many hell beasts and demons he had killed when he went to hell.</p>
<p>Roughly, there are close to 500,000 Soul Spirits inside the Soul Universe, and Kent would have to pick one and create a Soul Warrior out of them.</p>
<p>Kent expanded his vision, allowing him to see thousands of Soul Spirits. Those with the strongest spirits look entirely different. They are much more solid and oozing with power.</p>
<p>Naturally, those who had been inside the Soul Universe the longest have deeper soul bodies. Looking over, he saw thousands of such soul spirits.</p>
<p>"Strange, it is as if I have done this before." Like déjà vu, Kent felt a sense of familiarity as he scrutinised the Soul Spirits.</p>
<p>That feeling that made one think they had done something before, but for as long as they could remember, they had never done such a thing, was now making Kent blink a couple of times.</p>
<p>"I am just overthinking. I have never had the Soul Universe technique until this life. So maybe I am just overthinking."</p>
<p>Even so, the feeling lingered. He knew he was merely making himself feel good, but there was no telling whether he had done so, maybe using different means.</p>
<p>As he continued to look through the Soul Spirits, whenever he found a suitable candidate that he planned to turn into a Soul Warrior, something in him seemed to reject them.</p>
<p>However, if his mindset was to turn them into soul weapons, then there are thousands he could pick from.</p>
<p>"Really strange," Kent smiled inwardly and continued to look.</p>
<p>Soon, he had seen about 98% of the Soul Spirits, but none caught his attention. He didn’t know why, but even the Soul Spirits of the forsaken immortals he had killed didn’t seem right to be transformed into a soul warrior.</p>
<p>"Maybe I can only go for a soul weapon for now, and hopefully, one day I will find the one that I can turn into a soul—"</p>
<p>Kent stopped and narrowed his eyes at a hooded figure sitting somewhere at the back of the thousands of Soul Spirits.</p>
<p>Kent instantly recognised the figure as one of the Hell Demons he killed back in the Damned Hell Sea.</p>
<p>Looking at his soul quality, he clearly is stronger than a Heavenly Ascension Stage warrior; however, due to the suppression of the hell road, he was Tier 19 when Kent killed him.</p>
<p>Now, sitting with two daggers in his firm grip, the feeling that keeps rejecting the others flipped, and now, it seems to like this one. Looking closely, Kent saw a golden core deep inside his chest.</p>
<p>This golden core is radiating a subtle power that feels both too familiar and too distant to Kent.</p>
<p>"Come."</p>
<p>Kent pointed at him, and the hooded figure teleported to him. Just when he appeared before Kent, a message appeared before his vision...</p>
<p>⟦Do you want to transform this Soul Spirit into a Soul Warrior⟧</p>
<p>"Really strange." Kent smiled, but he quickly selected yes because that was why he had come to the Soul Universe in the first place.</p>
<p>⟦Soul Warrior Transformation Process Started. Duration: 7 Days⟧</p>
<p>Under Kent’s shocked gaze, the hooded figure was cocooned, and then, from the thousands of Soul Spirits around, they began turning into golden orbs and merging with the cocoon.</p>
<p>In less than a minute, over a thousand Soul Spirits were absorbed.</p>
<p>"I expect greatness in seven days." Kent spent some more time looking for a second Soul Spirit with a golden core, but failed.</p>
<p>In the end, he left the Soul Universe. Three days later, Gaia is done with the city, meaning the Sky Foxes can move in.</p>
