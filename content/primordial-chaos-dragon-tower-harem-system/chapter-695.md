<p>A day had passed since Kent left the human continent for the Elf Kingdom.</p>
<p>At the moment, he was moving very fast, and while he knew there was danger lurking within the sea, he didn’t really care much about that.</p>
<p>The speed he was going was fast enough to get to his destination without much trouble. So he just sat in a meditative posture and continued to feel the serene environment around him.</p>
<p>A few hours later, Vexthra reached out to him...</p>
<p>[Vexthra, my love, are you missing me already?] Kent asked the moment he heard Vexthra’s voice in his head.</p>
<p>Vexthra smirked, standing at the edge of the middle floating island, inside the dungeon. "Nobody misses you. So just focus on your mission."</p>
<p>[Ouch... that hurts, Vex... very hurtful.] Kent smiled and asked why she contacted him.</p>
<p>"We managed to explore every last detail on the map, but we only found something small. One was a spirit grape tree that had some powerful, soul-nourishing grape fruits, over 10,000 years old.</p>
<p>I transplanted three inside the Love Garden and five in the Mountain Garden with the help of the tower, and left the rest inside the dungeon. Also, I left a couple of hundred grapes in the Tower Storage."</p>
<p>[That is a good one then.] Kent smiled.</p>
<p>"Yes. However, the second thing we discovered is related to Unity. It turns out Blossom was once the goddess of Love. We don’t know much, but it was much more apparent considering how Unity reacted to this information."</p>
<p>[How did she react to it?]</p>
<p>"Well, first, the language the texts were written in was one only she recognized, despite not knowing she could read or speak that language."</p>
<p>Additionally, after translating the writings, which were akin to a diary of the Goddess of Love, Unity felt as though she was there, experiencing everything firsthand.</p>
<p>It was weird, but after reading everything, it became something akin to a...</p>
<p>[Memory]</p>
<p>"Yes. It was just a few bits of memory, but it turns out that just that can make her gain more than she expected. Her Primordial spirit, whom we named Blossom, gained additional gifts she could use to help strengthen Unity.</p>
<p>It was just too strange how a memory could have made such a change in a person." Vexthra was both happy and shocked when Unity gained a bit of her memory.</p>
<p>She knew this was the turning point for her sister, so she was happy for her but also shocked that the memory had been able to cause such a significant change.</p>
<p>Kent, on the other hand, was just smiling as the flying boat sped across the Black Sea. Though its name was exaggerated at the outer sections, as he moved further from the shores, the sea indeed got darker by the passing hours.</p>
<p>So its name, the Black Sea, was indeed warranted.</p>
<p>[Just make sure that as more memories are discovered, she doesn’t hastily divulge everything to her sisters. It may not seem like much, but revealing too much about one’s past can be dangerous.]</p>
<p>"I made her aware..." Vexthra responded, and Kent nodded.</p>
<p>[Then keep looking for more clues for her. The moment I get a way to awaken her true name, I won’t hesitate to do it.</p>
<p>Also, the eight new undead will be ready in two weeks, so for now, keep doing what you deem necessary and don’t rush to go through the floors.]</p>
<p>"We won’t rush. Now that we know Unity has more to gain in the dungeons, we will be taking our time to see how much we can help her."</p>
<p>[Great... then I will talk to you later... I have incoming.]</p>
<p>"Okay... have fun."</p>
<p>Kent narrowed his gaze at the swarm of monsters swimming after him and toward him.</p>
<p>"Looks like the sea monsters don’t want me to pass." He wasn’t even done when a large shark with metallic teeth lunged at him.</p>
<p>Kent’s sword appeared, and with a quick swing, the shark was cut in half, sending it crashing back into the water, instantly changing its color to red.</p>
<p>But it wasn’t the only monster that attacked. Kent immediately found himself surrounded from all sides, forcing him to slow down the flying boat.</p>
<p>"A good day to farm tower points and Soul Essence." With a smile on his face, he started killing the sea monsters. They weren’t that strong. Most were at the Root Transcendent stage, so he was basically bullying them.</p>
<p>However, coming in large numbers was both to Kent’s advantage and disadvantage. His advantage is that he will get to farm more Tower Points and Soul essence.</p>
<p>But bad for him because he allowed them to get closer. If he is not quick and powerful enough, he will be overwhelmed.</p>
<p>And as the minutes passed and stronger ones started appearing, Kent had to abandon his boat, sending it into the tower space, and chose to fly on his own.</p>
<p>This made it much more convenient to kill, and he was doing just that. His level 2 Sword Qi and Intent were active, so even the monsters that managed to slip through his attack couldn’t get within a meter of him without being shredded to meat paste.</p>
<p>After attaining the four sword enlightenments, Kent’s Profound Sword Qi and Intent had become much more potent. He was using just the sword qi with the intent to kill dozens of monsters, as if it were the most normal thing to do.</p>
<p>Within ten minutes, Kent had killed thousands of sea monsters, and more continued to come. Not that he was complaining, considering every single kill was giving him something he needed.</p>
<p>The tower points and the soul essence were flooding in per kill, so he was having a rather great field trip even if he had to abandon his ship.</p>
<p>One hour later, the Black Sea within a 10-mile radius had become the Red Sea, and that was when Kent knew the true danger was coming.</p>
<p>Of course, he felt it, far from him. He felt something or somebody locked their murderous gaze on him. It didn’t take long for him to see who wanted him dead by sending tens of thousands of monsters to their death.</p>
<p>"A sea dragon," Kent smirked, watching the dragon move at a terrifying speed toward him.</p>
<p>"Maybe I should get a sea dragon to help me cross the sea next time." Kent smiled and then locked gazes with the colossal dragon, who quickly appeared, splashing water on Kent.</p>
