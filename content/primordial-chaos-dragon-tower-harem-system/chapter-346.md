<p>"We will, buddy. The bat will regret coming against us."</p>

<p>"How is your training coming along?" Kent asked.</p>
<p>"I have mastered all my skills and can now use them more actively without causing too much damage to my allies."</p>

<p>"I see. But don’t worry too much. The next opponent we’ll be going after is already as good as dead." Kent and Kaizo smiled. They would be lying if they said the battle with the bat wasn’t fun.</p>

<p>"But how does this work? Do you, like, have memories of your past, or are you having some memories that show who you were? Or are you a blank slate?"</p>

<p>This means he might have been a warrior from somewhere.</p>
