<p>In what should have been a happy reunion between a father and his daughter, it had now turned into a slaughter unlike any Planet Tianxu had ever witnessed.</p>
<p>The massacre that should have been dealt to the Sky Fox was instead being subjected to their oppressors.</p>
<p>Liora is like a shadow.</p>
<p>Wherever she moved, heads and blood flew into the air.</p>
<p>She promised a massacre of every last one, so with Kent’s assistance, using 1000 blood runes to suppress the Beasts and Humans, she was basically moving from one warrior to the next, cutting their heads off.</p>
<p>She wasnt even putting in much effort.</p>
<p>If only Yun Wusheng hadn’t got greedy over something that he could never have, all of this would have been prevented.</p>
<p>The Supreme Sky Fox inheritance is something rumoured to hold the power to make a Sky Fox Supreme. They said it contains the pure blood of a Supreme Sky Fox that once ruled a vast part of the Universe.</p>
<p>That legendary Fox, from what is known, was one of the 12 Calamity Beasts that followed the Divine Beast Tamer.</p>
<p>Naturally, a legacy from him was something that, when ordinary people get their hands on it, their lives will never be the same.</p>
<p>However, it was only a rumour until apparently it wasn’t.</p>
<p>It turned out that the late Fox Priestess had found it in the ancestral ruins of the Sky Fox race and given it to Liora instead of Yun Wusheng. That had triggered him into killing Liora’s mother and, in a way, nearly ended up killing Liora too.</p>
<p>Now, the decisions he had made have, in a way, come back to haunt him.</p>
<p>Liora came back for blood, and now that she had awakened her True Name and bloodline, she had no use for said legacy... just revenge that she had dreamt of many times over.</p>
<p>Kent stood on the side, a faint smile on his face as he watched Liora vent her frustration. She had long wanted to get her revenge after all.</p>
<p>Although she said nothing to him, she already had plans with her sisters to come back one day to take her revenge. Now, it seems only she is enough.</p>
<p>"What a pity. What could have been a father-daughter bonding session had now turned into a slaughter. Makes me wonder just what kind of scum you are."</p>
<p>Kent appeared beside the frozen Yun Wusheng, who was watching the slaughter happening around him, but he was powerless to do anything.</p>
<p>After his session with his wives, Kent decided to resume his mission. But first, he brought Liora back to visit her mother’s side of the family.</p>
<p>Her mom was a Sky Fox. Her father, of course, is the scum who was colluding with the Beast to suppress the Sky Foxes.</p>
<p>Kent only wanted to bring her back, and then he would be on his way after meeting the Sky Fox. However, just when they arrived, they noticed the Sky Fox race was in danger.</p>
<p>Naturally, with him around, this worked in Liora’s favour. So, without even holding back, she decided to vent her anger on all who stood against the only family she had. The only family linked to her mother.</p>
<p>There are only about 100,006 Sky Foxes on Planet Tianxu, so the idea that they would be facing odds about 20 times their number is just too barbaric. Their number would have reduced even further.</p>
<p>Thankfully, Kent and Liora arrived when they did.</p>
<p>"You can never honestly tell just what goes on in the mind of idiots. You killed her mother and tried to kill her. Now you want to oppress her family.</p>
<p>I dont know if you are just stupid or your brain cells are just completely useless. Among all the women you could have suppressed, you picked the one I love.</p>
<p>I mean, scums like you shouldn’t even be allowed around women. You don’t qualify to even smile at them.</p>
<p>Now, when she is done wiping out your warriors, I will make sure your name is forever wiped from every mind and forgotten.</p>
<p>However, instead of a painless death, I am going to take my time to kill you, making sure every passing second feels more painful than the last.</p>
<p>Of course, you will grow numb to the pain at one point. However, as long as I live and breathe, I will always find a way to make you scream."</p>
<p>Kent walked in front of him and looked him in the eye... "That is my promise to you, father-in-law."</p>
<p>The smile that followed made Yun Wusheng see Liora as an angel. The smile on Kent’s face is too evil for him not to break into severe panic.</p>
<p>However, no matter how much he panicked, he knew his life from here on would be painful. The dragon man before him is just too scary.</p>
<p>In less than an hour, every last soul was dead. Their headless bodies littered the ground. Like she promised, not even a single warrior was spared.</p>
<p>Their blood had been used to appease the weapon of the Universe. Liora was once the weapon the universe used to handle scums that sought to destroy everything.</p>
<p>She had killed abominations that can’t even be mentioned out loud. She alone had done the universe so much service that when she died, they became sad, so they gave her a second chance at life.</p>
<p>Now that she is back, she has done what she has always wanted to do. She came back to protect her people.</p>
<p>Although she isn’t a Sky Fox, despite her mother being one, at heart, they are her people, and so protecting them was a way of showing her late mother that she would never let anything happen to them.</p>
<p>Now, she had done just that.</p>
<p>"What should we do with this scum?" Kent asked, pushing Yun Wusheng forward just as Liora returned to his side... He then added, "Killing him will be mercy. I suggest we torture him first."</p>
<p>Liora looked at her father for a few seconds before she nodded, "When Velyra comes out from seclusion, we will get on that."</p>
<p>Kent nodded with a smile. He knew his redskin beauty was perfect for what he had in mind. With Velyra’s control over blood, unless she wanted to, Yun Wusheng would never die.</p>
<p>She can torture and skin him alive, but as long as he had blood in his body, she would be able to keep him alive for as long as possible.</p>
<p>Basically, Yun Wusheng is fucked for life.</p>
<p>Kent seeks only one thing: to make his wives happy.</p>
<p>He didn’t want to see them sad in any way. So whenever he finds out someone had done something to any of them in the past that still haunts them, then, as a good husband, getting back at them is only appropriate.</p>
<p>Yun Wusheng had killed his mother-in-law and tried to kill his wife. As such, he helped her get her revenge. However, the man in question would have to be subjected to a different kind of torture.</p>
<p>This time, he wants her happy, and if torturing her old man will do that, then he is also delighted. In the end, it is her happiness he seeks.</p>
