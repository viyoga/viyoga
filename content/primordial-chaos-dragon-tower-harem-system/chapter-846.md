<p>⟦Congratulations, you are now a Sword Saint⟧</p>
<p>⟦Your sword intent and sword Qi have been upgraded to Level 4⟧</p>
<p>⟦Your understanding of the sword will now be boosted by 3 times the normal speed⟧</p>
<p>"As expected, the rewards are worth the struggle." Kent smiled.</p>
<p>He had been through a lot, and finally, he managed to rise to the level he is at. Of course, the journey was rewarding, and he knew that, too. But finally becoming a Sword Saint felt good.</p>
<p>Though his understanding is almost on the level of a Sword King, he knew that when it comes to the path he is walking, which is the genesis path, there is nothing like limited understanding.</p>
<p>He is always learning, and that alone is a clear indication of the path he has set for himself. He walked it before, and now, he plans to improve on it even more.</p>
<p>"I didn’t awaken any new aspect, but with this breakthrough, my already awakened aspects have improved, and that alone is good enough.</p>
<p>My sword gaze has improved tremendously, and with my sword body already merged with 16 split bodies, my combat power will become much more powerful.</p>
<p>The only thing left now is to train my mind so I can use the Clone Bodies more actively."</p>
<p>Kent turns to the requirement he must fulfill before becoming a Sword Sage.</p>
<p>⟦Requirements to become a Sword Sage⟧</p>
<p>↠ Gain all 11 enlightenments and attain Heavenly Sword Intent/Aura.</p>
<p>↠ Defeat Sword King Maklin in a sword duel.</p>
<p>↠ Kill the Wandering Demon Swordsman and present his head to the Olive Sword Guild.</p>
<p>↠ Absorb the Jade Token and use the energy to refine your sword gaze.</p>
<p>↠ Comprehend the Grand Sword Aura Tablet.</p>
<p>Kent looked at the requirements and sighed.</p>
<p>He was already on the track of gaining all 11 enlightenments. Of course, according to the tower, seeing and talking to the sword Dao is worth more than any enlightenment.</p>
<p>But Kent knew that was plain exaggeration.</p>
<p>If he wanted to make it big, then he must gain all 11 enlightenments. He had already attained four, and thanks to the opportunities that will be opening for him soon, he might gain some more.</p>
<p>However, the process demands more from him, and Kent isn’t sure if he can handle all that while also managing other things.</p>
<p>He still needs to pay attention to other things like his Alchemy, Dragon side, and many more. So, focusing on the sword alone, this demand made her panic a little.</p>
<p>"Then again, I have the sword World, so things aren’t too complex yet. As for the second requirement, I am sure I will ask around when I get back to the sect."</p>
<p>Kent didn’t want to jinx it, but he hoped his second requirement could be found in the sect. The Pill Princess was almost in his reach, so he wanted this one to go smoothly, too.</p>
<p>However, Kent also knew he could only hope for now.</p>
<p>"As for the third through to the fifth, I will have to ask Old Sword about it. Something tells me none of the last three requirements will be a walk in the park."</p>
<p>Kent stood up and stretched his arms.</p>
<p>"The next phase of my path will be a wild one, and I can only hope the outcome will be worth it. I must at least become a Sword Sage before ascending."</p>
<p>Kent walked away from the sword bridge and appeared in the body tower.</p>
<p>He ignored the bridge and went straight to the tempering room, where he immediately took out a couple of herbs and prepared a herb bath.</p>
<p>"It will take 2 days, that is 48 hours inside here, to finish tempering my body. That will be the perfect time for me to return to the sect and try the Perfect Chamber."</p>
<p>Kent climbed in and closed his eyes as the tempering process began.</p>
