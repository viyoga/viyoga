<p>The next day arrived, and Ross was surrounded by his girls, each as stunning as the last.</p>

<p>Since this was just a local game, there was no need for the team to take the bus.</p>

<p>Instead, Ross drove to the city basketball arena with his entourage, their arrival causing quite a stir.</p>

<p>The arena was packed, teeming with students from both Sunset Hills University and Pendle Norton University.</p>

<p>Parking was a challenge, but they managed to find a spot in the crowded lot.</p>

<p>The best part of the event?</p>

<p>Admission was free, which meant an even larger crowd had gathered to cheer for their respective teams.</p>

<p>Dennis and Daryl were there too, sharing a few playful jabs with Ross before the game started.</p>

<p>Their banter was lighthearted, the kind of camaraderie Ross had grown to appreciate.</p>

<p>But while the game hadn’t begun yet, it was clear that most of the attention wasn’t on the players warming up—it was on Ross’s girls.</p>

<p>The men in the crowd couldn’t keep their eyes off them, and the whispers started almost immediately.</p>

<p>&quot;Damn, bro, look at those bitches!&quot; one guy muttered, nudging his friend.</p>

<p>&quot;I’d kill to have just one of them babes,&quot; another said, his voice thick with envy.</p>

<p>&quot;And he’s got seven?!&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;He added more to his list! Deym!&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;Every single one is drop-dead gorgeous.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;Ross Oakley is one lucky bastard!&quot;</p>

<p>The envious stares and murmurs only intensified as Ross’s harem stood confidently by his side, completely unfazed by the attention.</p>

<p>The boys in the crowd looked on with pure jealousy, their admiration for Ross’s skills on the court now mixed with a grudging respect—and outright envy—for his off-court success.</p>

<p>Beep!</p>

<p>The whistle blew, signaling the start of the game. The opposing team had the first possession, and the deafening roar of the crowd filled the arena.</p>

<p>The rivalry between Sunset Hills University and Pendle Norton University wasn’t just a game—it was war, fueled by generations of grudges and fierce competition.</p>

<p>&quot;Kill them!&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;Don’t let them score!&quot; Discover hidden tales at empire</p>

<p>&quot;Wreck them, Ross!&quot;</p>

<p>The energy in the stands was electric, but the intensity on the court was even greater.</p>

<p>Trash talk flowed as freely as the basketball.</p>

<p>&quot;So, you’re the so-called ’wonder boy,’ huh? NBA at 18?&quot; the Pendle Norton point guard sneered, dribbling lazily as he crossed half-court.</p>

<p>&quot;Let me tell you something—you just got lucky. I’m going to prove that game was a fluke and show the world you’re nothing but a fraud.&quot;</p>

<p>Ross smirked, his voice calm yet laced with mockery. &quot;Less talk, more action. Honestly, I don’t even consider you clowns worthy opponents. I belong on a much bigger stage. This place?&quot; He gestured around the court dismissively. &quot;This is just a circus to me—and guess what? You and your boys are the main attraction.&quot;</p>

<p>The point guard’s face twisted in anger. &quot;You arrogant bastard,&quot; he spat, before charging forward. He attempted a flashy crossover, aiming to blow past Ross, but the plan went disastrously wrong.</p>

<p>Ross read the move perfectly, reaching out to tap the ball free. He hustled for the loose ball and snatched it away with ease. Without wasting a second, he sprinted toward the opposing side of the court.</p>

<p>Stopping just behind the three-point line, Ross could have gone for an easy layup, but that wasn’t his style.</p>

<p>Confidence coursing through him, he rose for a jumper instead.</p>

<p>Before the ball even reached the hoop, Ross turned away, certain of the result.</p>

<p>Whoosh!</p>

<p>The ball sliced cleanly through the net, not touching the rim. The crowd erupted in a deafening cheer, their reaction confirming what Ross already knew.</p>

<p>He glanced back at the point guard, his smile widening. Leaning in just enough for his opponent to hear, Ross spoke softly, his words dripping with confidence.</p>

<p>&quot;Only three-pointers for me today. Get ready to witness the most unforgettable game of your life.&quot;</p>

<p>The point guard’s jaw clenched as Ross jogged back to his side of the court, his presence commanding attention from everyone in the arena.</p>

<p>The tone for the game had been set—and it was clear who would dominate.</p>

<p>Beep!</p>

<p>Twenty minutes later, the whistle blew, signaling the end of the first half.</p>

<p>The scoreboard displayed a staggering 169 to 23. Even someone completely unfamiliar with basketball could tell the outcome was already set in stone.</p>

<p>No comeback could change this. It would take nothing short of a miracle to close such an insurmountable gap.</p>

<p>The fans and students of Pendle Norton sat in stunned silence, their earlier enthusiasm snuffed out.</p>

<p>Hope was a distant memory, replaced by resignation.</p>

<p>Not that it mattered to Ross, our OP evil MC, who was already scheming how to make the most of his 15-minute halftime break.</p>
