<p>The air was filled with muffled sobs and words of comfort.</p>

<p>For the first time in days, Karen felt warmth again—the kind that came not from safety, but from love.</p>

<p>But what Karen didn’t know... was that someone else had followed her into the bunker.</p>

<p>Someone she hadn’t expected to see again so soon. Or ever.</p>

<p>&quot;Karen.&quot;</p>

<p>Her name was spoken in a low, familiar voice. She turned around sharply, her heart skipping a beat.</p>

<p>Standing just a few steps away was a tall, handsome man—his clothes dusty, his expression weary, but his smile genuine.</p>

<p>&quot;Marco...&quot;</p>

<p>Karen gasped, her emotions overwhelming her. She rushed to him and threw her arms around his neck, holding him tightly as if afraid he might vanish if she let go.</p>

<p>&quot;You’re safe... I can’t believe it—you’re really safe!&quot;</p>

<p>Marco chuckled softly, brushing a strand of hair from her face.</p>

<p>&quot;Of course I am. You didn’t think I’d let the world end without finding you first, did you?&quot;</p>

<p>For a brief, fleeting moment, Karen smiled.</p>

<p>It was like the old days again—the warmth of his touch, the steady comfort of his voice.</p>

<p>But then, her smile slowly faded. Reality crept back in like a shadow.</p>

<p>Ross.</p>

<p>The deal she had made. The promise she had given in desperation.</p>

<p>Karen’s chest tightened. She couldn’t meet Marco’s eyes anymore.</p>

<p>Guilt swirled inside her like a storm, twisting and clawing at her heart.</p>

<p>She didn’t know how to explain any of it—what she had agreed to, or what it meant.</p>

<p>Karen took a shaky breath, forcing a smile she didn’t feel. &quot;I’m just... so glad you’re here,&quot; she whispered.</p>

<p>Marco smiled warmly, unaware of the shadow looming over them both.</p>

<p>&quot;Nothing’s ever going to separate us again,&quot; he said confidently.</p>

<p>Karen’s stomach twisted at his words. If only he knew.</p>

<p>Because somewhere above them—far beyond the safety of the bunker—Ross was watching everything unfold through a live feed, a faint smile curling on his lips.</p>

<p>And Karen knew, in her heart, that Marco was about to face the shock of his life.</p>

<p>And there was nothing she could do to stop it.</p>

<p>***</p>

<p>Another hour passed before the final phase of the evacuation concluded.</p>

<p>The sound of armored boots thundered through the underground corridors as dozens of tall men in full combat suits entered the bunker.</p>

<p>Each one carried advanced weaponry—sleek rifles, reinforced suits, and sidearms polished to perfection.</p>

<p>Their visors reflected the dim red lights of the entry bay, and the rhythmic clank of metal echoed like a heartbeat across the vast chamber.</p>

<p>Then came the final moment.</p>

<p>With a grinding roar, the colossal gates of the bunker slid shut.</p>

<p>Layer after layer of steel interlocked until even sound seemed to vanish.</p>

<p>The faint tremor that followed made it feel as though the earth itself had sealed them off from the dying world above.</p>

<p>For a while, there was only silence.</p>

<p>Then Ross’s voice filled the air—calm, smooth, and utterly in control.</p>

<p>&quot;Attention. Requesting everyone to gather in the main hall.&quot;</p>

<p>The announcement reverberated through the halls. Families stirred from their quarters.</p>

<p>The soldiers formed lines.</p>

<p>The newly arrived civilians moved uncertainly, whispering to one another as they followed the illuminated signs toward the grand central hall.</p>

<p>It took five minutes before everyone assembled—hundreds of anxious faces staring up at the raised dais.</p>

<p>The hall itself was immense, reinforced by titanium beams, the air faintly humming with electricity.</p>

<p>It felt like a cathedral built for survival.</p>

<p>Then, the sound of approaching footsteps drew everyone’s attention.</p>

<p>Ross appeared.</p>

<p>He descended from an upper corridor with Althea at his side, her crimson hair glowing under the white lights like liquid fire.</p>

<p>Her beauty alone was enough to command silence, but beside Ross—tall, composed, and radiating quiet authority—they looked like a pair out of myth, the king and queen of this underground realm.</p>

<p>Ross stopped before the crowd, resting his hands on the steel railing of the platform.</p>

<p>For a moment, he said nothing.</p>

<p>His piercing gaze swept the hall as though measuring every soul before him.</p>

<p>When he finally spoke, his voice carried with it both warmth and unshakable power.</p>

<p>&quot;Today,&quot; he began slowly, &quot;marks one of the darkest days in human history.&quot;</p>

<p>A heavy pause followed. Even the children stopped whispering.</p>

<p>&quot;I did not know the exact hour it would come... but I have always been ready to plan against everything. For years, I have prepared—quietly, patiently—for the collapse of the world above if it would happen. And while I could not save everyone, I ensured that those closest to me—those who matter—would be with me in these hard times.&quot;</p>

<p>The crowd exchanged glances. Some looked grateful. Others uneasy.</p>

<p>Ross continued.</p>

<p>&quot;As of now, there are five hundred and sixty-nine of us here. Among you are my wives, my children, my in-laws, my friends, and my soldiers. Every one of you was chosen for a reason. Though the surface has fallen into chaos, down here, we begin anew. We survive. We build. And we rise again.&quot;</p>

<p>He raised a glass that shimmered gold under the lights.</p>

<p>&quot;Welcome... to Aegis. Our refuge. Our fortress. Our new home.&quot;</p>

<p>A wave of applause followed. Some clapped eagerly.</p>

<p>Others hesitated, unsure what kind of future they had just stepped into.</p>

<p>Still, food was brought out—real food, not rations—and the smell of roasted meat and fresh bread filled the air.</p>

<p>For a moment, people almost forgot the screams echoing in the cities above.</p>

<p>Ross’s soldiers distributed sleek black phones to everyone—devices engraved with a strange sigil. &quot;These will function within Aegis even if all communication dies outside,&quot; Ross explained. &quot;Consider them a lifeline.&quot;</p>

<p>By the time midnight arrived, the once-bustling hall had quieted.</p>

<p>Families retreated to their assigned quarters. Guards took positions in every hallway.</p>

<p>The hum of generators became the lullaby of a world reborn underground.</p>

<p>Karen lay awake on her bed, unable to sleep.</p>

<p>Her mind replayed the chaos of the last two days—the screams, the blood, the rescue, Ross’s voice.</p>

<p>Everything felt unreal. Her parents and sisters were safe in their room.</p>

<p>Then—</p>

<p>Buzz.</p>

<p>Her phone vibrated softly on the nightstand.</p>

<p>Karen frowned and reached for it.</p>

<p>There was only one message on the glowing screen.</p>

<p>Come outside. My men will help you find me.—R</p>
