<p>&quot;We move tonight,&quot; one of them said, his voice low but firm, cutting through the tense silence like a blade.</p>

<p>&quot;Isn’t that too soon, boss?&quot; another asked cautiously, shifting uneasily. &quot;You said we’d wait. You said we’d be careful.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;Forget waiting!&quot; the leader snapped, his teeth clenched, eyes flashing with anger. &quot;When I say we move, we move. No discussion. Understood?&quot;</p>

<p>A tense silence followed, but the men didn’t argue.</p>

<p>They knew better than to challenge him when he was like this.</p>

<p>But there was more driving his fury than impatience or recklessness.</p>

<p>Deep inside, the leader’s anger was intertwined with a personal obsession—a silent, gnawing frustration he could barely admit even to himself.</p>

<p>Every time he thought of Sabrina, his control slipped.</p>

<p>She had been a constant image in his mind for weeks: her laugh, her smile, the way she moved.</p>

<p>He had wanted her for himself, and the idea that she might already be close to someone else—someone like Ross—was unbearable.</p>

<p>He could not imagine it. He could not accept it.</p>

<p>His teeth ground together as he imagined her smiling with Ross, laughing at his jokes, walking freely without fear.</p>

<p>The thought twisted something dark and possessive inside him, driving him to act faster, strike harder, and leave nothing to chance.</p>

<p>The men around him, unaware of the full reason behind his fury, only sensed the intensity radiating from their boss.</p>

<p>There was a dangerous edge in his voice, a hunger in his gaze, a storm brewing that they did not fully understand.</p>

<p>Still, they felt it—they felt the energy building, the inevitability of what was about to happen.</p>

<p>Preparations began immediately.</p>

<p>Supplies were gathered, weapons checked, escape routes considered.</p>

<p>They contracted their people outside and contingencies were discussed, but through it all, the leader’s mind remained elsewhere, fixated on a single image: Sabrina.</p>

<p>&quot;Okay, you’re the boss. How do we do this?&quot; one of the men asked nervously, shifting from foot to foot.</p>

<p>The dim light of the evening cast long shadows across the ground, making the small group look even more sinister.</p>

<p>&quot;First, we need to...&quot; the boss began, his voice low but commanding, as he outlined the plan for the night.</p>

<p>Every detail was precise—who would go where, when to act, and how to ensure no one got caught.</p>

<p>The men nodded along, barely hiding their excitement.</p>

<p>Some muttered under their breath about how reckless it sounded, but no one dared question him.</p>

<p>By the time the sun had fully set, they were ready.</p>

<p>More than a dozen of them appeared in the compound, each carrying crates of beer, bottles of hard liquor, and snacks they had scavenged from outside.</p>

<p>The weight of their supplies made them grunt, but they walked with exaggerated bravado, trying to make it look like a celebration rather than a raid.</p>

<p>&quot;Look, it’s Wilson and his men!&quot; someone whispered among the onlookers.</p>

<p>&quot;They brought so much! Wow!&quot; another added.</p>

<p>&quot;Did they go out and get all that loot? Nice!&quot;</p>

<p>People watching from a distance were amused.</p>

<p>Dinner had already been served, and now, with drinks arriving in such abundance, it felt like the perfect timing.</p>

<p>Some even clapped or cheered quietly, thinking it was just a spontaneous party.</p>

<p>At the center of the gathered men, Wilson laughed loudly, slapping a crate of beers with pride.</p>

<p>&quot;Ross! Look what I found!&quot; He gestured to his men as they stacked the drinks carefully in the middle.</p>

<p>Ross turned his head casually, a faint smile on his lips but eyes sharp as a blade.</p>

<p>&quot;Did you go out earlier, Wilson? Not bad. Do you really plan to drink all of that?&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;Yes, Ross! But it won’t be complete without our big boss here. Please, join us!&quot; Wilson’s grin widened.</p>

<p>&quot;Ross!&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;Ross!&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;Ross!&quot;</p>

<p>The men around Wilson began chanting in unison, clapping and stomping in a display of forced enthusiasm.</p>

<p>They tried to create an atmosphere of camaraderie, attempting to apply peer pressure to coax Ross into joining them.</p>

<p>From their perspective, it was a harmless ritual, a way to assert their dominance and inclusion.</p>

<p>But Ross wasn’t fooled.</p>

<p>He could see through their smiles, their eagerness, and the flicker of tension in their eyes.</p>

<p>Every movement, every cheer, every word they shouted revealed the same truth: they were overconfident and careless.</p>

<p>They had no idea the danger they had invited into their midst.</p>

<p>Ross’s grin widened further, stretching into something more chilling.</p>

<p>These men—loud, arrogant, and oblivious—were dead men walking. He welcomed their foolishness.</p>

<p>If tonight was the night they wanted to test their luck, he would gladly oblige.</p>

<p>&quot;Fine,&quot; he said finally, stepping forward.</p>

<p>He took one of the bottles, cracked it open with a deliberate twist, and took a long swig.</p>

<p>The sound of the cap popping echoed lightly, drawing the attention of the group, who erupted in cheers, thinking he was joining the party wholeheartedly.</p>

<p>Ross let them believe it. Let them revel in their sense of victory.</p>

<p>Every cheer, every chant, every careless laugh added to the tension building quietly in his mind.</p>

<p>Each one of them was walking willingly toward their own end, and Ross’s patience, like a coiled spring, tightened.</p>

<p>As he took another drink, the light cast long shadows across the gathering, elongating the shapes of the men around Wilson, making them appear larger, more confident than they were.</p>

<p>They had no idea how quickly that illusion would shatter.</p>

<p>Food and drinks kept arriving, carried in by Wilson’s men with loud laughter and exaggerated effort.</p>

<p>They had scouted and collected supplies from outside, bringing meat, preserved goods, and bottles of alcohol, carefully stacking them in the center of the supermarket.</p>

<p>They could have simply stolen food from Ross—they had considered it—but they had already witnessed how meticulous and strict he was with every supply.</p>

<p>Anyone caught trying to take something would face consequences, and no one dared test him directly.</p>

<p>Instead, they opted to use their own resources, cooking over small makeshift stoves they had brought along, and preparing what they pretended would be a celebratory meal for all.</p>
