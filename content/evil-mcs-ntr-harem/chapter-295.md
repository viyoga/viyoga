<p>At exactly 7 p.m., after dinner, Christian finally summoned his courage.</p>

<p>The room buzzed with casual conversation as everyone drank and exchanged lighthearted small talk, but to him, it felt as if the air had grown heavier.</p>

<p>His heart pounded against his ribcage, threatening to leap into his throat.</p>

<p>&quot;Hahhh…&quot; He exhaled a deep breath he hadn’t realized he was holding, trying to calm the storm of nerves within him.</p>

<p>A quick glance in the mirror reassured him—his suit was crisp, his hair perfectly styled, and his face carried the determination of a man ready to take a leap.</p>

<p>With his resolve strengthened, Christian stepped out of the washroom and scanned the room for Hazel.</p>

<p>The dining area was lively, filled with chatter and laughter, but his gaze immediately locked onto her.</p>

<p>At the table sat three redheaded women, all stunning in their own ways.</p>

<p>Trinity, despite being a mother of two grown daughters, looked barely in her mid-twenties, her beauty timeless.</p>

<p>Althea, the famous actress, radiated a star-like glow.</p>

<p>But for Christian, Hazel stood apart, her quiet elegance surpassing even her glamorous sister and her loving mother.</p>

<p>Christian straightened his posture and began walking toward her with purpose.</p>

<p>The room gradually quieted as everyone noticed his serious demeanor and the nervous energy that clung to him like a second skin.</p>

<p>By the time he stopped in front of Hazel, the room was completely still. All eyes were on him.</p>

<p>Hazel looked up, her expression shifting from curiosity to surprise as Christian knelt down on one knee before her.</p>

<p>&quot;I’m so lucky to have you as my girlfriend, Hazel,&quot; Christian began, his voice trembling slightly but full of emotion.</p>

<p>&quot;But I want more than just luck—I want forever. I love you so much, and I can’t imagine my life without you. Will you marry me?&quot;</p>

<p>He reached into his pocket and opened a small velvet box, revealing a stunning diamond ring that sparkled brilliantly under the warm lights.</p>

<p>The room erupted in gasps, and a few murmurs of admiration rippled through the crowd.</p>

<p>Christian was no ordinary man; as a successful engineer in the construction industry, he had the means to afford such a lavish ring without hesitation.</p>

<p>But more than the material gesture, his sincerity and love for Hazel were palpable, and the onlookers couldn’t help but feel moved by his proposal.</p>

<p>All except Hazel.</p>

<p>She sat frozen in her chair, her face ashen and her eyes wide, as though she’d seen a ghost.</p>

<p>Her mind raced, guilt and shame clawing at her as she glanced around the room.</p>

<p>Her gaze briefly flickered to Ross, who stood off to the side, watching the scene with his usual calm, unreadable expression.</p>

<p>Her chest tightened as she looked back at Christian.</p>

<p>This man, who had given her nothing but love and devotion, didn’t know the truth.</p>

<p>He didn’t know she was no longer the pure, untouchable woman he thought her to be.</p>

<p>How could she marry him now?</p>

<p>How could she lie to him, knowing she’d already crossed a line she could never take back?</p>

<p>Tears welled up in her eyes, and her voice cracked as she spoke.</p>

<p>&quot;I’m sorry, Christian… I’m not ready.&quot;</p>

<p>The room fell silent again, the weight of her words settling over everyone like a heavy cloud.</p>

<p>Christian’s expression shifted from hope to confusion, then to heartbreak as the meaning of her response sank in.</p>

<p>Hazel couldn’t bear to see the pain in his eyes. She pushed her chair back and fled the room, tears streaming down her cheeks.</p>

<p>She didn’t stop until she was alone in her bedroom, where she collapsed onto the bed, sobbing.</p>

<p>She knew what she had to do. Tomorrow, she would end things with Christian for good.</p>

<p>It was the only way to free him from the weight of her lies—and to face the consequences of her own choices.</p>

<p>&quot;Who’s up for some hard drinks?&quot; Robert asked, breaking the heavy silence that hung in the air.</p>

<p>The tension was palpable, and everyone felt a pang of pity for Christian.</p>

<p>&quot;Count me in, Robert!&quot; Ross chimed in, eager to lighten the mood.</p>

<p>&quot;Let’s not stop until one of us drops unconscious.&quot;</p>

<p>As the two men moved to the bar, Trinity leaned over to Christian, whispering something softly in his ear.</p>

<p>Whatever she said seemed to hit him like a hammer.</p>

<p>Christian nodded mutely, his shoulders slumping as he shuffled over to the sofa and sank into it like a man who had just lost his way.</p>

<p>No one could blame him. After years of being with Hazel, he hadn’t expected her to reject his proposal so suddenly—or so painfully.</p>

<p>A few minutes passed in awkward quiet before Christian stood up abruptly, his movements unsteady but determined.</p>

<p>He strode over to join Ross, Robert, and Liam, who were already a few drinks in, gearing up for what Robert jokingly called a &quot;drinking marathon.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;Why are you guys drinking without me? That’s unfair!&quot; Christian declared, trying to sound lighthearted.</p>

<p>But his voice cracked slightly, betraying the storm of emotions bubbling beneath the surface.</p>

<p>No one said anything, but as the drinks started flowing, it became painfully clear just how hurt Christian was.</p>

<p>He downed one shot after another, not bothering to pace himself.</p>

<p>&quot;Hey, take it easy,&quot; Trinity urged, her voice laced with concern as she placed a gentle hand on his arm.</p>

<p>Christian shook his head, his eyes glistening with unshed tears.</p>

<p>&quot;I’m fine,&quot; he muttered, pouring himself another drink.</p>

<p>Everyone exchanged uneasy glances but pretended not to notice the tears that began slipping down</p>

<p>Christian’s cheeks as he threw back another glass.</p>

<p>A broken heart wasn’t something anyone could fix with words, and they all knew it.</p>

<p>&quot;After all that drama, I think it’s time we make some noise of our own.&quot;</p>
