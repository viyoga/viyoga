<p>Bang!</p>

<p>This time, what descended was not a beast nor a storm of fire, but a colossal fortress suspended in the sky.</p>

<p>It was vast beyond comprehension, a floating monolith forged from something darker than night itself.</p>

<p>Its surface was pure black, smooth and impenetrable, swallowing the light around it as though even radiance feared to touch it.</p>

<p>From it emanated a terrifying spiritual pressure.</p>

<p>The moment it appeared, the atmosphere of the entire planet shifted. The wind stilled.</p>

<p>The clouds scattered.</p>

<p>A suffocating weight pressed down upon every living being as if an invisible hand had placed the world beneath a mountain.</p>

<p>People dropped to their knees.</p>

<p>Some screamed. Others could not even muster the strength to make a sound.</p>

<p>The temperature seemed to plummet.</p>

<p>A deep, dreary cold seeped into bones and marrow alike. Even the bravest among humanity felt their hearts shrink in fear.</p>

<p>And yet, before anyone could adjust to this new horror, the sky split again.</p>

<p>Another presence emerged.</p>

<p>Then a third.</p>

<p>Boom!</p>

<p>An enormous eye opened high above the atmosphere, so massive that it dwarfed the tallest skyscrapers ever built.</p>

<p>Its iris shimmered with shifting colors unknown to mankind, swirling like galaxies collapsing inward.</p>

<p>Its pupil was an abyss, bottomless and merciless.</p>

<p>It did not blink.</p>

<p>It simply stared.</p>

<p>The fortress, the eye, and the third looming golden flying boat were positioned far apart across the sky, yet something defied all logic.</p>

<p>Distance meant nothing. Perspective meant nothing.</p>

<p>Every surviving human on Earth could see them clearly, as though they hovered directly overhead.</p>

<p>Even those hiding in underground bunkers.</p>

<p>Even those buried deep within caves miles beneath the surface.</p>

<p>The vision pierced through stone and steel alike, forcing the heavens into the sight of every living being.</p>

<p>No one could escape witnessing it.</p>

<p>The world trembled.</p>

<p>&quot;What are those things?&quot; someone shouted from a crumbling rooftop.</p>

<p>&quot;Are we saved?&quot; another cried, clinging desperately to hope.</p>

<p>&quot;I don’t think so,&quot; a man whispered hoarsely. &quot;Those don’t look like saviors. They look like something far worse. Like predators who just noticed us.&quot;</p>

<p>Panic spread like wildfire.</p>

<p>Cities already reduced to rubble fell silent.</p>

<p>Survivors who had endured waves of catastrophe now felt the last remnants of their courage unravel.</p>

<p>Parents clutched their children. Soldiers lowered their weapons.</p>

<p>Leaders found themselves with no orders left to give.</p>

<p>Some prayed.</p>

<p>Some cursed.</p>

<p>Some laughed hysterically at the absurdity of it all.</p>

<p>High above, the giant black fortress remained motionless, imposing and eternal.</p>

<p>The enormous eye continued its silent examination.</p>

<p>The flying boat pulsed faintly with a golden energy that made the atmosphere ripple around it.</p>

<p>&quot;A living World Heart... I can’t believe I’m seeing one in my lifetime.&quot;</p>

<p>The beautiful woman’s voice was soft, almost reverent, yet it carried clearly across the trembling heavens.</p>

<p>In her luminous eyes flickered the shadows of countless eras rising and falling—civilizations born, empires reduced to dust.</p>

<p>Though her face was youthful and flawless, the ancient weight of time rested within her gaze.</p>

<p>She stood upon a resplendent golden flying boat that blazed like a second sun.</p>

<p>Divine light cascaded from its hull, illuminating half the sky in radiant splendor.</p>

<p>&quot;Indeed,&quot; another voice responded, calm and cold. &quot;And this World Heart will belong to us.&quot;</p>

<p>A tall figure stepped forward from within a dark temple suspended among storm clouds.</p>

<p>The structure loomed ominously in the sky, wreathed in black lightning.</p>

<p>His robes fluttered in the violent winds, his expression indifferent, as if the fate of the world beneath his feet meant nothing more than a passing breeze.</p>

<p>&quot;You two are dreaming.&quot;</p>

<p>The third voice was hoarse and ancient.</p>

<p>A small, hunched old man appeared between them, leaning upon a long, weathered staff.</p>

<p>Though his frame was frail, the space around him warped subtly, as if unable to bear his presence.</p>

<p>His cloudy eyes gleamed with terrifying sharpness.</p>

<p>&quot;You’ll have to die first before you can claim this World Heart.&quot;</p>

<p>A tense silence fell.</p>

<p>Then...</p>

<p>&quot;I suppose we shall find out soon enough.&quot;</p>

<p>A burst of laughter shattered the stillness.</p>

<p>&quot;Hahaha!&quot;</p>

<p>In the next instant, the heavens erupted.</p>

<p>Explosions tore across the sky like blooming stars of destruction.</p>

<p>Golden light collided with black thunder; ancient runes ignited and shattered; space itself cracked like fragile glass.</p>

<p>The shockwaves rippled outward, shaking mountains into rubble and turning seas into towering walls of fury.</p>

<p>The three supreme beings clashed, each strike capable of overturning continents.</p>

<p>But they were not alone.</p>

<p>From the golden flying boat and the dark temple, countless figures poured forth like locusts.</p>

<p>Warships descended.</p>

<p>Armored cultivators streaked through the air.</p>

<p>Beasts of war roared as they plunged toward the trembling earth below.</p>

<p>&quot;Find the World Heart!&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;Kill anyone who dares to interfere!&quot;</p>

<p>The command echoed across the world like a divine decree.</p>

<p>Battles ignited everywhere. Cities were reduced to dust in moments. Forests burned.</p>

<p>Oceans split apart.</p>

<p>The very crust of the planet cracked under the unimaginable pressure of their war.</p>

<p>The scale of destruction defied comprehension.</p>

<p>The earth groaned.</p>

<p>The sky screamed.</p>

<p>And somewhere deep within the world’s core, the living Heart pulsed—once... twice... as if sensing that its fate was about to be decided by blood and fire.</p>

<p>***</p>

<p>While the heavens shattered and the three supreme powers clashed in cataclysmic fury, not everyone chose to stand beneath that apocalyptic light.</p>

<p>Deep beneath the earth—far below the trembling mountains and collapsing cities—someone had already hidden himself the moment those terrifying auras descended upon the world.</p>

<p>Cyrus Thorn.</p>

<p>The instant the three overlords revealed themselves, he had burrowed underground without hesitation, sealing his presence, suppressing his aura, and concealing even the rhythm of his heartbeat.</p>

<p>Below, in the suffocating darkness of stone and soil, Cyrus remained perfectly still.</p>

<p>Then—</p>

<p>&quot;Huh?&quot;</p>

<p>Cyrus narrowed his eyes.</p>

<p>Even through layers of compacted earth and shattered ley lines, he heard it.</p>

<p>A voice.</p>

<p>It did not echo through the air. It did not tremble through the ground.</p>

<p>It spoke directly into his mind.</p>

<p>Cold.</p>

<p>Commanding.</p>

<p>Ancient.</p>

<p>&quot;It’s time to make yourself useful, slave.&quot;</p>

<p>Cyrus’ pupils shrank.</p>

<p>That voice.</p>

<p>He hadn’t heard it in a long time.</p>
