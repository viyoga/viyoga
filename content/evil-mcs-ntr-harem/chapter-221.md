<p>&quot;You didn’t just bring back one, but two Japanese girlfriends. I don’t even know what to say anymore,&quot; Jade said, glancing at Ren and Imari, who were engrossed in their own lively conversation in Japanese.</p>

<p>The two girls were so full of energy and promise, their youth practically glowing.</p>

<p>It wasn’t just their personalities that caught attention either. Both Ren and Imari had striking physical features, ones that were hard to ignore.</p>

<p>Their beauty drew stares, and their enviable figures—blessed in all the right places—made them impossible not to notice.</p>

<p>Imari fit right in with the rest of Ross’s harem. Like Ren and the others, she was curvaceous and undeniably attractive, exuding the kind of confidence that made heads turn.</p>

<p>Ross, sensing Jade’s exasperation, raised his hands in mock surrender.</p>

<p>&quot;I’m innocent, Jade. I swear. I didn’t plan any of this—it just happened.&quot;</p>

<p>Jade narrowed her eyes, her tone laced with sarcasm.</p>

<p>&quot;Oh, it just happened, huh? You’re so helpless, Ross.&quot;</p>

<p>Though Jade now loved Ross just as much as the other girls did, she had retained her sharpness and honesty.</p>

<p>She was frank, often unfiltered, and Ross adored that about her.</p>

<p>He couldn’t stand the idea of surrounding himself with women who were meek or mindless—it would bore him to death.</p>

<p>Jade’s fiery personality added a spark that kept things interesting.</p>

<p>&quot;You know you love me,&quot; Ross said with a grin, leaning back casually as Jade rolled her eyes.</p>

<p>&quot;That’s beside the point,&quot; she replied, crossing her arms but unable to hide the faint smile tugging at her lips.</p>

<p>&quot;And just to be clear, Imari Ito is not my girlfriend,&quot; Ross said, his tone exasperated.</p>

<p>&quot;…yet,&quot; Jade shot back, raising an eyebrow. &quot;I know the type of girls you like, Ross. Imari fits that bill perfectly—from her looks to her attitude, right down to the tips of her nipples.&quot; She folded her arms and let out a long sigh.</p>

<p>Sometimes, being with Ross made Jade feel like a nagging, jealous housewife.</p>

<p>The sheer number of beautiful women around him could drive anyone mad.</p>

<p>Yet, deep down, she knew her frustrations stemmed from just how much she loved him.</p>

<p>As chaotic and infuriating as he could be, there was nowhere else she would rather be than by his side, even if it meant sharing him.</p>

<p>That thought softened her irritation, and a faint smile crossed her lips.</p>

<p>She decided to let the matter drop, leaning back in her seat as the plane carried them home.</p>

<p>The hum of conversation and laughter from the others filled the space, and for a moment, peace settled over her.</p>

<p>***</p>

<p>Meanwhile, in another part of the country Ross and his group had just left, an entirely different scene was unfolding.</p>

<p>&quot;This is all your fault!&quot; A stunningly beautiful woman shouted, her voice breaking as tears streamed down her face.</p>

<p>Her delicate features, usually composed and graceful, were now distorted by anguish.</p>

<p>&quot;If you hadn’t forced our daughter into marrying your friend’s son, she wouldn’t have run away from home!&quot;</p>

<p>Her husband, an imposing man with a sharp jawline and cold eyes, slammed his fist onto the table.</p>

<p>&quot;This was for her own good!&quot; he snapped.</p>

<p>&quot;Do you have any idea the kind of life she could’ve had? She would’ve been a queen—or better! That boy’s family has connections beyond anything you can imagine. Ren could’ve lived in luxury, far above anything we’ve ever dreamed of. But no, she had to be stubborn!&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;And now she’s gone,&quot; the woman sobbed, clutching a tissue in her trembling hands.</p>

<p>&quot;Our daughter is gone, and it’s all because of you. If you can’t find her, I will never forgive you.&quot;</p>

<p>Her voice cracked, and for a moment, the man’s stern expression faltered. He clenched his fists and looked away, refusing to admit his own guilt.</p>

<p>Ren’s mother, however, didn’t need him to acknowledge it—she already knew she bore part of the blame.</p>

<p>She had allowed herself to be swept along by his plans, agreeing to the arranged marriage despite the unease that gnawed at her.</p>

<p>She had convinced herself it was for the best, that it was the right thing for Ren.</p>

<p>But now, with her daughter missing, regret consumed her.</p>

<p>She could still picture Ren’s face on the night that they had last spoken, the way her eyes had silently begged for understanding.</p>

<p>She had failed her daughter, and that failure haunted her.</p>

<p>Taking a shaky breath, she straightened her spine.</p>

<p>&quot;I’m going to fix this,&quot; she whispered, more to herself than to her husband.</p>

<p>&quot;I don’t care what it takes. I’ll bring her back.&quot;</p>

<p>Her husband frowned.</p>

<p>&quot;And what exactly do you think you’re going to do?&quot;</p>

<p>She ignored him. There was no point arguing anymore. Instead, she picked up her phone and started making calls.</p>

<p>Friends, distant relatives, old acquaintances—she reached out to everyone who might have a lead, calling in favors and begging for information.</p>

<p>She even went so far as to bribe people, sparing no expense to track Ren down.</p>

<p>By the end of the night, she had exhausted every avenue she could think of.</p>

<p>But as she sat alone in her study, staring at the scattered papers and unanswered messages, her determination burned brighter.</p>

<p>I will find you, Ren, she thought fiercely. And when I do, I’ll make this right.</p>

<p>Somewhere, deep in her heart, she hoped her daughter could forgive her.</p>

<p>But first, she had to earn the chance to ask.</p>

<p>***</p>

<p>A few hours into the flight, a scene unfolded that left Imari utterly speechless.</p>

<p>&quot;How are you supposed to fit that thing inside you, Ren?&quot; Imari whispered, her wide eyes fixed on the unfolding erotic spectacle on the large bed in the middle of the private plane.</p>

<p>Ren, equally mesmerized but far less scandalized, whispered back, &quot;I don’t know, Imari. But, to be completely honest, I’m excited for when it does happen between me and Ross.&quot;</p>

<p>Imari whipped her head toward her best friend, her jaw practically hitting the floor. &quot;You’ve been corrupted!&quot;</p>
