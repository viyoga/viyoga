<p>The man’s gaze was sharp and proud as he approached Ross’s home.</p>

<p>The guards stationed outside immediately became alert.</p>

<p>&quot;State your business.&quot;</p>

<p>The scar-faced man showed no fear despite being surrounded.</p>

<p>Instead, he stepped forward and raised his voice.</p>

<p>&quot;Ross Oakley!&quot;</p>

<p>His powerful voice echoed throughout the area.</p>

<p>&quot;The United Earth Alliance has officially been established!&quot;</p>

<p>The surrounding people were instantly shocked.</p>

<p>The United Earth Alliance?</p>

<p>Many had heard rumors that the major survivor settlements across the city were attempting to unite against the growing threats facing the survivors of humanity.</p>

<p>Apparently, those rumors were true.</p>

<p>The messenger continued proudly.</p>

<p>&quot;After discussions between the strongest factions in this city, a new leader has been selected.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;A grand meeting will be held tonight.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;Representatives from every major force have been invited.&quot;</p>

<p>His eyes swept across the surroundings before finally settling on the residence.</p>

<p>&quot;You and your people are among those invited.&quot;</p>

<p>The messenger’s tone carried a hint of authority, as if he were delivering a command rather than an invitation.</p>

<p>Behind him, the group of rough-looking evolvers stood silently with crossed arms.</p>

<p>Powerful auras leaked from their bodies.</p>

<p>Some were clearly attempting to intimidate the city’s residents.</p>

<p>However, the effect was minimal.</p>

<p>Many of Ross’s subordinates simply looked at them as if they were fools.</p>

<p>After spending time around Ross, their standards had risen dramatically.</p>

<p>No matter how powerful these visitors appeared, they seemed insignificant compared to the terrifying existence who ruled this city.</p>

<p>The messenger frowned slightly.</p>

<p>He had expected shock.</p>

<p>Perhaps even respect.</p>

<p>Instead, he was met with calm and indifferent gazes.</p>

<p>The atmosphere became somewhat awkward.</p>

<p>Inside the residence, news of the visitors quickly reached Ross.</p>

<p>Upon hearing the report, a faint smile appeared on his face.</p>

<p>&quot;The United Earth Alliance?&quot;</p>

<p>Ross leaned back comfortably.</p>

<p>&quot;Interesting.&quot;</p>

<p>He had expected the major human factions to eventually unite.</p>

<p>Mutants evolved daily.</p>

<p>Monsters grew stronger.</p>

<p>What interested Ross more was the identity of this so-called new leader.</p>

<p>Someone capable of gathering Earth’s strongest factions under a single banner was certainly not an ordinary individual.</p>

<p>Ross slowly stood up.</p>

<p>His eyes carried a trace of amusement.</p>

<p>&quot;It seems things are about to become much more interesting.&quot;</p>

<p>Ross merely smiled after hearing the invitation. He had no interest in attending such a meeting personally.</p>

<p>Instead, he summoned Brandon.</p>

<p>&quot;Go in my stead.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;Yes, Boss.&quot;</p>

<p>Brandon immediately nodded.</p>

<p>Taking a few trusted men with him, he departed for the meeting held by the newly formed United Earth Alliance.</p>

<p>Several hours later, he returned.</p>

<p>Ross was relaxing comfortably when Brandon entered and reported everything that had happened.</p>

<p>&quot;The alliance wants to organize a large-scale expedition,&quot; Brandon explained.</p>

<p>&quot;They believe the areas beyond the cities contain resources, opportunities, and answers regarding the apocalypse. They want every major faction to contribute manpower.&quot;</p>

<p>Ross listened quietly before a faint smile appeared on his face.</p>

<p>&quot;So that’s what this is about.&quot;</p>

<p>Brandon nodded.</p>

<p>&quot;They’ve gathered over a thousand evolvers and soldiers. Their goal is to explore the outskirts beyond the established safe zones.&quot;</p>

<p>Ross shook his head.</p>

<p>&quot;They want men to explore the outskirts of the city?&quot;</p>

<p>A look of amusement flashed across his face.</p>

<p>&quot;Are they tired of living?&quot;</p>

<p>Unlike the alliance leaders, Ross knew exactly what lurked beyond humanity’s current territory.</p>

<p>He had long discovered that the city they were in was merely a small space of relative safety surrounded by an ocean of unimaginable danger.</p>

<p>Still, he wasn’t interested in stopping them.</p>

<p>Sometimes people only learned through experience.</p>

<p>&quot;Send a few men with them. Lead them.&quot;</p>

<p>Brandon understood immediately.</p>

<p>&quot;Yes, Boss.&quot;</p>

<p>A day later, the expedition officially began.</p>

<p>More than a thousand armed men gathered outside the city.</p>

<p>Soldiers.</p>

<p>Survivors.</p>

<p>Evolvers.</p>

<p>Representatives from countless factions.</p>

<p>Their confidence was high.</p>

<p>After all, this was one of the largest military operations humanity had organized since they arrived in this alien world.</p>

<p>Many believed they were about to uncover new opportunities.</p>

<p>Others dreamed of finding treasures or gaining more strength.</p>

<p>Few understood what truly awaited them.</p>

<p>The massive force advanced cautiously through the wastelands.</p>

<p>At first, everything appeared normal. They were in a huge forest.</p>

<p>Hours passed without incident.</p>

<p>This only increased everyone’s confidence.</p>

<p>&quot;Maybe the danger was exaggerated.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;Yeah. Everyone keeps talking about how terrifying the outside world is.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;I don’t see anything dangerous.&quot;</p>

<p>Many began laughing and chatting.</p>

<p>Some even mocked those who had warned them.</p>

<p>Then they crossed the invisible boundary.</p>

<p>The moment they moved beyond the limit that experienced survivors refused to cross, everything changed.</p>

<p>An oppressive feeling suddenly descended upon the area.</p>

<p>The air itself seemed heavier.</p>

<p>Several evolvers instinctively felt danger.</p>

<p>Before anyone could react...</p>

<p>ROAR!</p>

<p>A deafening roar exploded across this alien forest.</p>

<p>The sound shook the earth.</p>

<p>Everyone froze.</p>

<p>&quot;What was that?!&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;A mutant?&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;No... something much stronger!&quot;</p>

<p>Their expressions changed instantly.</p>

<p>Then they saw it.</p>

<p>A gigantic beast emerged from the ruins.</p>

<p>It resembled a lion, but only vaguely.</p>

<p>Its body was several times larger than an elephant.</p>

<p>Black scales covered its muscles.</p>

<p>Rows of razor-sharp teeth protruded from its mouth.</p>

<p>Its crimson eyes radiated pure killing intent.</p>

<p>The beast stared at the expedition force as if looking at prey.</p>

<p>Many people’s faces turned pale.</p>

<p>&quot;What kind of monster is that?!&quot;</p>

<p>The next moment, the creature moved.</p>

<p>Boom!</p>

<p>The ground exploded beneath its feet.</p>

<p>Its speed was unbelievable.</p>

<p>It transformed into a black blur.</p>

<p>&quot;Watch out!&quot;</p>

<p>Too late.</p>

<p>Puchi!</p>

<p>A soldier disappeared into the monster’s jaws before he could even scream.</p>

<p>Crunch!</p>

<p>Blood sprayed everywhere.</p>

<p>The man’s body was crushed between the beast’s teeth as though he were made of paper.</p>

<p>The horrifying sight instantly shattered the army’s confidence.</p>

<p>&quot;It ate him!&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;Oh my God!&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;Open fire!&quot;</p>

<p>Rat-tat-tat-tat!</p>

<p>Thousands of bullets erupted from rifles, machine guns, and mounted weapons.</p>

<p>A storm of metal descended upon the monster.</p>

<p>The sound of gunfire filled the battlefield.</p>

<p>Yet what happened next made everyone’s blood run cold.</p>

<p>Clang! Clang! Clang!</p>

<p>The bullets bounced harmlessly off the beast’s scales.</p>

<p>Some failed to penetrate at all.</p>

<p>Others only left tiny marks before falling to the ground.</p>
