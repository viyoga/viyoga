<p>&quot;Right this way, Miss Johnson,&quot; one of Ross’s bodyguards said, stepping forward with a polite nod.</p>

<p>The plan was to escort her back to her home for the night.</p>

<p>Laura glanced past him at the three massive figures standing a few paces away—Ross’s personal security detail.</p>

<p>They were like immovable statues, their sharp eyes scanning the surroundings even now.</p>

<p>&quot;What about your boss? Are you just going to let him wander off like that?&quot; she asked, her voice edged with concern as she tilted her head toward Ross’s retreating figure.</p>

<p>&quot;The big boss is fine, Miss Johnson,&quot; another bodyguard replied without missing a beat. &quot;It would take a lot more than the little drink he’s had tonight to bring him down.&quot;</p>

<p>Laura frowned slightly. &quot;A little drink? He’s had enough to make me dizzy just watching.&quot;</p>

<p>The guard chuckled. &quot;Trust me—this isn’t much for him.&quot;</p>

<p>For a moment, Laura hesitated.</p>

<p>Logic told her to let the professionals handle it, to get into the waiting car and go home.</p>

<p>But there was something about the sight of Ross—his usually sharp, confident stride dulled to an almost sluggish pace—that made her uneasy.</p>

<p>&quot;...,&quot; she murmured under her breath, shaking her head. &quot;Forget it.&quot;</p>

<p>Before the bodyguards could say more, Laura stepped away from them and quickened her pace, heading straight after Ross.</p>

<p>She told herself it was just to make sure he got to his room without incident.</p>

<p>But a small, inexplicable part of her simply wanted to see for herself that he was alright.</p>

<p>***</p>

<p>Thud!</p>

<p>Ross’s body collapsed onto the bed of his hotel room with a heavy thump.</p>

<p>The mattress sagged slightly under his weight, and moments later, a soft, almost rhythmic snoring filled the quiet space.</p>

<p>Laura stepped back, gasping for breath.</p>

<p>Leading Ross into the room had been far more exhausting than she anticipated.</p>

<p>One arm was slung over her shoulders, holding his weight as best she could, and her muscles burned from the effort.</p>

<p>Sweat had soaked through her blouse, sticking to her skin, and her hair clung to her damp forehead.</p>

<p>She took a moment to steady herself, her chest rising and falling rapidly.</p>

<p>Glancing at Ross, she noticed his own skin glistening with perspiration, a flush across his cheeks from the combination of alcohol and exertion.</p>

<p>His normally sharp, composed features were softened, relaxed in sleep—but she couldn’t help the flicker of worry that crossed her mind.</p>

<p>&quot;Mr. Oakley,&quot; she said gently, kneeling slightly to adjust his position.</p>

<p>&quot;You need to change your clothes before you sleep. Otherwise, you’re going to get sick.&quot; She shook him lightly, hoping to rouse him.</p>

<p>&quot;Zzzzz...&quot;</p>

<p>Only the soft sound of his snoring answered her.</p>

<p>Laura sighed and wiped her palms on her skirt.</p>

<p>She shifted slightly to make sure he was lying comfortably on his back, careful not to disturb him too much.</p>

<p>The blanket was partially bunched under him, so she tugged it up to cover his chest, her fingers brushing lightly against his warm skin.</p>

<p>The heat radiating off him was almost dizzying, a mix of exertion, alcohol, and natural body warmth.</p>

<p>She stood there for a moment, her hands lingering at her sides.</p>

<p>Part of her wanted to just leave him to rest, but another part of her—a quieter, more insistent part—couldn’t shake the concern gnawing at her.</p>

<p>She hated that she was this worried about a man she barely knew, but the sight of him so vulnerable and unguarded pulled at her instincts.</p>

<p>Finally, she took a step back, exhaling slowly. &quot;At least I got him here safely,&quot; she murmured to herself. &quot;That’s enough for tonight.&quot;</p>

<p>Even so, she couldn’t tear her gaze away immediately, her mind restless with questions she didn’t dare voice aloud: Why did he drink so much?</p>

<p>How did someone like him handle it normally?</p>

<p>And, more unsettling, why did seeing him like this stir something in her she wasn’t ready to understand?</p>

<p>A few moments passed, and Laura let out a long, weary sigh.</p>

<p>She knew she couldn’t just leave Ross in this state, even if part of her wished she could retreat and pretend none of this had happened.</p>

<p>&quot;What have I gotten myself into? I should have just gone home,&quot; she muttered, shaking her head.</p>

<p>Her hands moved instinctively, adjusting his jacket and loosening his tie, trying to make him as comfortable as possible without waking him.</p>

<p>Every motion felt heavier than it should, her muscles aching from the effort of helping him into the room earlier.</p>

<p>Ross lay sprawled across the bed, completely unguarded, the faint rise and fall of his chest the only sign that he was alive.</p>

<p>There was something disarming about him like this—normally so composed, so commanding, yet now utterly unaware of his surroundings.</p>

<p>Laura’s pulse quickened at the thought.</p>

<p>She could feel a warmth creeping up her neck and across her cheeks, an odd mixture of anxiety, concern, and... something she couldn’t quite name.</p>

<p>The room itself seemed to close in around her.</p>

<p>The soft hum of the air conditioning, the faint scent of the wine he’d drunk, even the shadows cast by the low lamp on the nightstand—it all combined into a strangely intense, almost electric atmosphere.</p>

<p>Her senses felt heightened, every detail sharper than it should be.</p>

<p>Laura shook her head again, trying to steady herself.</p>

<p>Focus, she told herself. He’s just a man who needs rest. That’s all.</p>

<p>Her hands moved to straighten the sheets, smoothing out the wrinkles where he had collapsed.</p>

<p>She couldn’t help but notice how vulnerable he looked, and it tugged at something protective inside her.</p>

<p>For a moment, she paused, standing over him and studying his face.</p>

<p>Even in sleep, he radiated confidence and command, the kind of presence that drew people in naturally.</p>

<p>She found herself both frustrated and fascinated by it, unsure whether she was admiring him, worrying about him, or simply caught up in the intensity of the night.</p>

<p>Laura finally took a deep breath and moved back slightly, giving him space while still keeping a watchful eye.</p>

<p>She told herself again that she should leave, that she had already done enough.</p>

<p>And yet, she lingered, torn between responsibility and a growing, inexplicable curiosity about the man who had so effortlessly unsettled her thoughts.</p>
