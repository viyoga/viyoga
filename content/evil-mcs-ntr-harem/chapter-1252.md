<p>The invading forces spread out across the entire Earth, their spiritual senses sweeping over every inch of land like an invisible tide.</p>

<p>Mountains were scanned in an instant, oceans churned under unseen pressure, and even the deepest, most hidden corners of the world were not spared from their probing awareness.</p>

<p>They searched ruthlessly.</p>

<p>Nothing escaped their detection... cities, ruins, underground caverns, even the faintest fluctuations of spiritual energy were dragged into the light.</p>

<p>To them, this world was nothing more than a newly discovered resource, ripe for the taking. Yet... despite their confidence, none of them dared to remain in one place for too long.</p>

<p>A subtle unease lingered in their hearts. It was as if the very air carried a warning. As if this world... had something very wrong inside it.</p>

<p>Then, without warning...</p>

<p>&quot;Who dares invade a realm already marked by me?!&quot; The voice descended like heavenly judgment. It did not merely echo... it crushed.</p>

<p>The sky fractured with sound, the clouds scattered violently, and the very laws of the world seemed to tremble under the weight of that single roar.</p>

<p>Countless cultivators felt their chests tighten, their blood stagnating as an overwhelming pressure descended upon them. Some weaker ones nearly fell from the sky on the spot.</p>

<p>Then a figure appeared.</p>

<p>High above the heavens, beyond the clouds, a massive silhouette slowly took form.</p>

<p>At first, it was nothing more than a distortion in space, but within moments, it solidified into a towering presence that eclipsed everything beneath it.</p>

<p>He stood like a god overlooking mortals.</p>

<p>His long white hair flowed endlessly behind him, each strand seeming to carry a life of its own.</p>

<p>His crimson robes billowed like flames, radiating a terrifying aura that dyed the sky in faint shades of red.</p>

<p>His face was incomparably handsome, yet there was no warmth in it... only cold authority, the kind that came from absolute power.</p>

<p>His eyes opened. And in that instant, it felt as though the entire world had been locked in place. No one dared to move. No one dared to breathe.</p>

<p>It was as if a single thought from him could decide life or death for everything beneath the heavens. Then the whispers began.</p>

<p>&quot;This... this can’t be...&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;That aura... it’s him...&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;The Emperor of the Ghost Wood Forest!&quot; Shock turned to terror in an instant. Some cultivators trembled uncontrollably, their faces drained of all color as recognition struck them.</p>

<p>Others instinctively began retreating, their bodies moving before their minds could even process what was happening.</p>

<p>&quot;He’s captured the World Heart!&quot;</p>

<p>That single sentence shattered whatever courage remained.</p>

<p>The World Heart—the core of the planet, the very source of its existence. Whoever controlled it... controlled everything.</p>

<p>&quot;Everyone, escape now!&quot;</p>

<p>Panic erupted.</p>

<p>There was no longer any thought of exploration, no thought of profit or opportunity. Survival became the only thing that mattered.</p>

<p>Streams of light burst across the sky as cultivators activated their escape techniques one after another. Space tore open, revealing cracks and gateways as they fled in all directions.</p>

<p>Some burned their essence to move faster, others discarded treasures just to gain a moment’s advantage. No one dared to stay.</p>

<p>Because they all knew that facing the Emperor of the Ghost Wood Forest, especially one who held the World Heart, was no different from courting death.</p>

<p>Even the boldest among them did not hesitate. Within seconds, the once-crowded skies became a chaotic storm of fleeing figures. Within tens of seconds, that storm thinned.</p>

<p>Within a minute...</p>

<p>There was nothing left. The heavens fell silent.</p>

<p>The pressure that once blanketed the world slowly receded, like a tide pulling back into the abyss.</p>

<p>The crimson hue faded from the sky, and the clouds gradually returned to their natural state. High above, the colossal figure remained for a brief moment longer.</p>

<p>His gaze swept across the now-empty world, indifferent... as if everything that had just occurred was nothing more than a trivial interruption.</p>

<p>Then, without a word, his figure began to fade.</p>

<p>His massive form dissolved into nothingness, leaving behind no trace... no fluctuation, no residue, as though he had never appeared at all. But the damage had already been done.</p>

<p>Silence blanketed the Earth once more. Yet it was no longer the same silence as before. It was heavier. Oppressive. Filled with an unspoken truth...This world... now had an owner.</p>

<p>And anyone who dared to challenge that fact would face consequences far beyond their imagination.</p>

<p>What could be seen not long after was something far more shocking and difficult to comprehend even for seasoned cultivators... Earth itself... vanished.</p>

<p>From the vast, endless expanse of the Milky Way, the once-visible planet simply disappeared without warning.</p>

<p>There was no catastrophic explosion, no collapsing spatial storm, no remnants of broken astral matter. It was as if a hand had reached into the heavens and casually plucked it out of existence.</p>

<p>One moment it was there. The next, it was gone. Leaving behind only a hollow void in its place. It was not destruction. It was removal. Someone had taken the entire world.</p>

<p>Scooped it up as though it were no more significant than a stone on the roadside... and carried it away without the slightest hesitation or permission.</p>

<p>The cultivators who had only just managed to escape the Earth’s atmosphere and retreat to a safe distance were the first to witness this horrifying scene.</p>

<p>At first, they thought their senses were mistaken. Some even tried to re-scan the region repeatedly, refusing to believe what they were seeing.</p>

<p>But reality remained unchanged. The starfield where Earth had once existed was empty. Utterly empty. The realization struck them like a hammer to the soul.</p>

<p>Their worst fears were no longer speculation. They were truth. A cold silence fell among the survivors before panic erupted.</p>

<p>&quot;Quickly! Send word back to the clan immediately!&quot; one cultivator shouted, his voice trembling despite his attempts to remain composed.</p>

<p>&quot;The Emperor of the Ghost Wood Forest... he possesses a World Heart now!&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;Repeat it clearly! Do not misreport even a single detail!&quot;</p>
