<p>Miku’s trembling hands reached out, hesitating for a moment before gently brushing against his arm.</p>

<p>The warmth of his skin was enough to make her cry harder.</p>

<p>&quot;Ace...&quot; she whispered, voice breaking as tears began to fall freely.</p>

<p>She wanted to call his name again, to wake him, to tell him how much she missed him.</p>

<p>But the words wouldn’t come.</p>

<p>They were trapped in her throat, tangled with everything she had done to make this moment possible.</p>

<p>Ross.</p>

<p>The memory struck her like lightning—the heat of his body, the sound of his voice, the way he had taken her until she couldn’t even remember her own name.</p>

<p>She had screamed for him, begged for him, cried from both pain and pleasure.</p>

<p>And now, here she was, kneeling beside her husband, the echo of another man still lingering in her mind and body.</p>

<p>Guilt surged through her chest, sharp and cold. Her tears turned bitter.</p>

<p>How could she face Ace after what she had done? After what she had allowed?</p>

<p>Her hand clenched over her heart, but she forced herself to breathe.</p>

<p>She couldn’t afford to fall apart now—not when Ace needed her, not when she had finally gotten him back.</p>

<p>&quot;I’m sorry...&quot; she whispered hoarsely, her voice trembling. &quot;I’m so sorry, Ace...&quot;</p>

<p>The apology felt empty, swallowed by the quiet room.</p>

<p>But she wasn’t sure who it was truly for—her husband, or herself.</p>

<p>She wanted to hate Ross. She wanted to curse him, to scream at him for forcing her into that corner.</p>

<p>But deep down, beneath the anger and the guilt, was something she didn’t want to admit—relief.</p>

<p>Because if she hadn’t done what he wanted... Ace wouldn’t be here now.</p>

<p>And that truth terrified her.</p>

<p>Miku wiped her tears roughly, her expression hardening. She had made her choice.</p>

<p>It didn’t matter how it happened or what it cost her. Ross had kept his word, and Ace was alive.</p>

<p>For that, she would bear the guilt in silence.</p>

<p>She looked down at Ace’s sleeping face once more, her hand brushing the hair from his forehead.</p>

<p>&quot;If I had to do it again,&quot; she murmured, her voice trembling but firm, &quot;I wouldn’t hesitate.&quot;</p>

<p>The world outside groaned and burned, but inside that small room, Miku clung to her fragile miracle—her heart torn between love, shame, and a gratitude she could never voice aloud.</p>

<p>***</p>

<p>&quot;Hmmm...&quot;</p>

<p>Ace groaned softly as consciousness returned to him.</p>

<p>His vision blurred for a moment, then slowly cleared, revealing the faint golden glow of morning light seeping through the curtains.</p>

<p>The first thing he saw was his wife sitting at his bedside, her shoulders shaking, her face buried in her hands.</p>

<p>&quot;Miku...&quot; his voice came out weak, barely more than a whisper.</p>

<p>Her head shot up instantly.</p>

<p>For a split second, disbelief froze her features, and then tears flooded her eyes.</p>

<p>&quot;Ace!&quot; she cried out, her voice cracking as she lunged forward to wrap her arms around him.</p>

<p>He winced slightly at the sudden embrace, but his heart ached more than his body.</p>

<p>He could feel the tremor in her hands, the desperation in her grip, and he realized just how broken she must’ve been while he was gone.</p>

<p>&quot;I’m here,&quot; he murmured, his voice rough. &quot;I don’t know how... but I’m here now.&quot;</p>

<p>Miku sobbed against his chest, her tears soaking through his shirt.</p>

<p>&quot;I thought I lost you. I thought you were gone forever...&quot;</p>

<p>Ace’s hands gently brushed her hair, still soft and fragrant, though she looked thinner, more tired than he remembered.</p>

<p>The last thing he recalled was staring weakly out the window of their home.</p>

<p>His foot had throbbed unbearably, his vision darkened—and then, nothing. Silence. Cold.</p>

<p>Now, to open his eyes again and see her there—it felt like waking from a nightmare he couldn’t quite escape.</p>

<p>They stayed like that for a long while, clinging to each other as if the world around them might vanish if they let go.</p>

<p>Words were few, tears plenty.</p>

<p>Sometimes Miku spoke between sobs, sometimes Ace simply nodded, unable to process how he had come back, or what it cost.</p>

<p>After nearly an hour, Ace was finally strong enough to move.</p>

<p>He sat in a wheelchair that had been provided for him—by Ross, as Miku quietly mentioned.</p>

<p>The name stirred something unfamiliar in him. Gratitude, perhaps. Or unease.</p>

<p>When Ross finally stepped into view, Ace’s breath caught in his throat.</p>

<p>The man was striking—tall, broad-shouldered, and radiating an effortless confidence that filled the room.</p>

<p>His face, handsome to the point of intimidation, looked as though it had been carved to perfection.</p>

<p>For a moment, Ace could only stare, frozen in quiet astonishment.</p>

<p>He had always thought himself good-looking.</p>

<p>He’d taken pride in that—his appearance, his charm, the way Miku used to gaze at him with admiration.</p>

<p>But beside Ross, he suddenly felt small, dull, like a shadow standing next to the sun.</p>

<p>&quot;Ross,&quot; Miku said softly, stepping to the side. &quot;This is my husband.&quot;</p>

<p>Ross nodded politely, his expression calm but unreadable.</p>

<p>&quot;Good to finally meet you, Ace. Awake. You’ve been through a lot.&quot;</p>

<p>Ace swallowed hard, his fingers tightening on the armrest of the wheelchair.</p>

<p>The man’s voice was deep, confident, commanding. Even his presence seemed to carry weight.</p>

<p>&quot;Miku told me everything,&quot; Ace said, forcing a smile, though his throat felt tight.</p>

<p>&quot;It was you who saved me. Thank you, Ross. We owe you more than we can ever repay.&quot;</p>

<p>Ross gave a faint smile—gentle, but distant.</p>

<p>&quot;No debt,&quot; he said simply. &quot;I only did what anyone should have.&quot;</p>

<p>But Ace could feel it—the unspoken tension, the invisible imbalance between them.</p>

<p>He didn’t know why, but something about Ross made his heart tighten.</p>

<p>Gratitude mixed with discomfort, admiration tainted with fear.</p>

<p>Miku stood between them, her hands clasped tightly together.</p>

<p>Her heart pounded as she looked from one man to the other, her expression calm but her eyes betraying guilt.</p>

<p>Ace didn’t notice it, not yet.</p>

<p>He only knew that his wife had cried for him, that she was safe, and that he was alive because of Ross.</p>

<p>But as Ross’s gaze lingered for a moment too long on Miku, something deep inside Ace stirred—a quiet unease, a whisper of insecurity that he couldn’t name.</p>

<p>Still, he smiled, his voice steady.</p>

<p>&quot;Thank you again, Ross. Truly. I won’t forget this.&quot;</p>

<p>Ross nodded once more, his faint smirk never fading.</p>

<p>&quot;You’re welcome, Ace. I’m just glad she didn’t have to face it all alone.&quot;</p>

<p>Miku froze for half a heartbeat, her breath catching. Ace didn’t notice—but Ross did.</p>

<p>The three of them gathered around the table for a hearty meal.</p>

<p>Plates were filled with grilled meat, fresh fruits, and warm bread — a feast so rich that Ace couldn’t help but stare in disbelief.</p>

<p>He hadn’t seen this much food in months. Not since the world had turned upside down.</p>

<p>&quot;This is incredible,&quot; Ace said, his eyes darting between the roasted chicken and the vibrant salad bowl.</p>

<p>&quot;Where did you even get all this? It’s like you’ve got your own Walmart hidden somewhere.&quot;</p>

<p>Ross chuckled, leaning back casually in his chair. &quot;Something like that.&quot;</p>

<p>Ace looked at him curiously, waiting for an answer, and Ross finally smirked.</p>

<p>&quot;I’ve always been a paranoid man,&quot; he said.</p>

<p>&quot;Figured there was at least a one percent chance the world would end in my lifetime. So I stocked up—food, fuel, water, the works. You could say I prepared for the apocalypse before it ever started. I’m what people used to call a ’prepper.’&quot;</p>

<p>Ace blinked, impressed. &quot;That’s... smart. Really smart. Guess it paid off, huh?&quot;</p>

<p>Ross gave a humble shrug, though there was quiet pride in his eyes. &quot;I like to think so.&quot;</p>

<p>The conversation carried on pleasantly after that.</p>

<p>They talked, laughed, and shared stories as if the weight of the world had momentarily lifted.</p>

<p>For Ace, it felt like breathing fresh air for the first time in years.</p>

<p>For Miku, it was harder — she smiled, but every time her eyes met Ross’s, her heart gave a guilty tremor.</p>

<p>When the meal was done, Ross opened a bottle of wine to celebrate Ace’s recovery.</p>

<p>The three raised their glasses in a quiet toast.</p>

<p>&quot;To survival,&quot; Ross said, his deep voice carrying a certain finality.</p>

<p>&quot;To survival,&quot; Ace echoed, grinning widely before downing his drink.</p>

<p>The wine flowed freely after that. Ace, unaccustomed to drinking so much anymore, was soon laughing too loudly, slurring his words as the alcohol took hold.</p>

<p>Eventually, he staggered to the bed, collapsed face-first onto the mattress, and began to snore almost immediately.</p>

<p>The room fell quiet.</p>

<p>Ross watched him for a moment, a faint smirk tugging at the corner of his lips.</p>

<p>Then his eyes drifted toward Miku, who stood uncertainly near the table, her fingers fidgeting with the edge of her sleeve.</p>

<p>&quot;Come to my room later,&quot; Ross murmured, his voice low enough that only she could hear.</p>

<p>Miku froze. Her breath caught in her throat.</p>

<p>Ross didn’t repeat himself — he didn’t need to.</p>

<p>He simply turned away, leaving her standing there in silence as Ace snored peacefully behind her, unaware of everything that was about to unfold.</p>
