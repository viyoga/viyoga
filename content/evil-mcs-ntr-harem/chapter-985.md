<p>The weekend arrived, and one of Chicago’s grandest mansions stirred with life.</p>

<p>The vast estate, usually serene and stately, was now a hive of activity.</p>

<p>Chefs and caterers hurried through the marble halls, waiters set crystal glasses upon long tables, and decorators adjusted flower arrangements worth more than an average man’s yearly salary.</p>

<p>The air smelled of roasted delicacies and luxury perfume—a clear sign that tonight was a celebration fit for the elite.</p>

<p>By early evening, sleek limousines and luxury sedans began to roll into the circular driveway one after another.</p>

<p>The sound of expensive engines echoed through the courtyard as the city’s wealthiest and most powerful figures stepped out, their laughter mingling with the music drifting from the ballroom inside.</p>

<p>Judging by the endless parade of designer gowns, tailored suits, and cars worth fortunes, it was obvious that only Chicago’s upper echelon had been invited to this event.</p>

<p>Then, a new sound stole everyone’s attention—a deep, throaty roar that didn’t belong to any ordinary car. Heads turned.</p>

<p>Conversations halted.</p>

<p>Cameras and curious eyes shifted toward the mansion’s entrance as a striking red sports car glided up the driveway like a predator among prey.</p>

<p>Its body gleamed under the golden lights, every curve and reflection commanding admiration.</p>

<p>&quot;Whew! Look at that beauty,&quot; one of the younger servers whistled, unable to contain his awe.</p>

<p>&quot;Damn,&quot; his partner muttered. &quot;I’d sell my left ball just to own that ride.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;Ha! I’d go bald and broke just to take it for a spin,&quot; another added, laughing under his breath.</p>

<p>&quot;I wonder who the owner is,&quot; someone else said, craning their neck for a better look.</p>

<p>Their question was answered moments later when the driver’s door opened.</p>

<p>Out stepped a tall man in a perfectly tailored black suit that seemed made just for him.</p>

<p>His movements were calm, deliberate—radiating confidence without even trying.</p>

<p>The mansion lights caught his features just right: sharp jawline, piercing eyes, the kind of face that could silence a crowd without a word.</p>

<p>Although not particularly handsome but with how his face was always on social media and such, this man was without a doubt one of the most popular faces in the world today.</p>

<p>Ross Oakley.</p>

<p>He adjusted his cufflinks casually, completely unfazed by the murmurs spreading around him.</p>

<p>Then, to the astonishment of onlookers, he walked around the car and opened the passenger door himself.</p>

<p>And out stepped Nina.</p>

<p>She was radiant under the chandelier lights, dressed elegantly, her arm slipping naturally through his.</p>

<p>Together, they looked like a power couple straight out of a fantasy—untouchable, magnetic, and impossible to ignore.</p>

<p>&quot;Wait a second...&quot; one of the servers whispered, eyes wide. &quot;That’s Ross Oakley!&quot;</p>

<p>The name rippled through the small crowd near the entrance.</p>

<p>Phones subtly came out for pictures, whispers grew louder, and even some of the guests stopped to stare.</p>

<p>Ross, however, seemed oblivious—or perhaps he simply didn’t care.</p>

<p>His calm smile never wavered as he guided Nina up the steps and into the grand hall, the crowd parting instinctively to make way for him.</p>

<p>Every step he took carried quiet dominance, the kind of presence that didn’t need an introduction.</p>

<p>By the time they disappeared through the mansion doors, the murmurs had only grown louder, filling the air with curiosity, admiration, and just a hint of envy.</p>

<p>***</p>

<p>Nina arrived at the mansion, her heels clicking softly against the polished marble floor as she scanned the crowd for her parents.</p>

<p>The evening was in full swing—laughter, music, and the low hum of conversation filled the grand hall, blending with the soft glow of chandeliers and the scent of fresh flowers.</p>

<p>Her eyes quickly landed on her mother, the birthday celebrant, radiant in an elegant gown that complemented her natural poise.</p>

<p>With Ross at her side, Nina approached confidently. &quot;Mom, this is Ross, my boyfriend,&quot; she said brightly. &quot;Ross, this is my mom, Jennifer.&quot;</p>

<p>Ross inclined his head in greeting, his eyes briefly meeting Jennifer’s with a mixture of charm and calculation.</p>

<p>&quot;A pleasure to meet you, Jennifer. I brought a small gift for your special day.&quot; From his suit pocket, he produced a delicately wrapped box and handed it to her.</p>

<p>Jennifer accepted the gift with a courteous smile. &quot;How thoughtful of you. Thank you, Ross.&quot; Her lips curved politely, but Ross caught the subtle flicker in her eyes—a guarded look, a trace of appraisal and disapproval hidden beneath the surface civility.</p>

<p>It was barely perceptible, yet he knew instinctively that this was someone who did not easily open herself to strangers.</p>

<p>&quot;The pleasure is mine, Ross,&quot; Jennifer said after a moment, her tone polite but measured.</p>

<p>&quot;I’ve heard your name mentioned in the news more times than I can count, but I didn’t realize you were dating my daughter. Last I heard, Nina was seeing that quarterback guy.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;He’s old news, Mom. We broke up,&quot; Nina replied, a small smile of defiance on her lips, as though closing a Chapter she had no desire to revisit.</p>

<p>Jennifer chuckled softly, a sound that was warm yet carefully restrained.</p>

<p>Her eyes flicked to Ross again, lingering just long enough to gauge him.</p>

<p>Ross returned her gaze, unflinching, a faint, knowing smile playing at his lips.</p>

<p>He could sense her scrutiny, the subtle judgment behind her composed exterior, and it intrigued him.</p>

<p>Most people either fawned over him or shied away—but Jennifer seemed different.</p>

<p>He read her mind and he was not surprised to know that Jennifer did not like him for his daughter at all.</p>

<p>The conversation drifted into safe territory—small talk about the party, the guests, and the city—but Ross’s attention remained subtly divided.</p>

<p>He noticed the slight lines around Jennifer’s eyes when she laughed, the graceful tilt of her neck, the confident way she carried herself.</p>

<p>She was elegant, intelligent, and undeniably captivating, a woman who radiated a quiet authority without effort.</p>

<p>As the night progressed, more guests approached, eager to meet Ross.</p>

<p>Businessmen, investors, and other prominent figures made their way through the crowd, shaking his hand, exchanging pleasantries, and seeking the charisma that his reputation promised.</p>
