<p>After that day, Alistair and his siblings were granted the freedom to venture outside whenever they wished, on the sole condition that Brandon accompanied them.</p>

<p>At first, the rule was enforced strictly.</p>

<p>During their earliest outings, their mothers followed closely behind, never letting the children stray too far from their sight, weapons always drawn and senses stretched taut.</p>

<p>Those first trips were tense.</p>

<p>Every distant groan, every shadow between ruined buildings, sent their hearts racing.</p>

<p>The undead roamed in numbers, and the world beyond their safe zone was unforgiving.</p>

<p>More than once, their mothers had to intervene, cutting down zombies before they could overwhelm the group.</p>

<p>But that phase did not last long.</p>

<p>With each excursion, Alistair and the others grew noticeably stronger.</p>

<p>Repeated battles tempered their bodies and sharpened their instincts.</p>

<p>They learned how to move together, how to cover each other’s blind spots, and how to strike decisively without hesitation.</p>

<p>The heart stones they gathered after every successful hunt accumulated rapidly, each one fueling further growth.</p>

<p>Their abilities began to evolve at an alarming pace.</p>

<p>What once required careful planning and constant supervision soon became routine.</p>

<p>Hordes that had previously forced them to retreat were now cut down methodically.</p>

<p>Zombies fell in droves, their bodies littering the streets as the siblings pressed forward without fear.</p>

<p>Gradually, their mothers stopped following on every trip.</p>

<p>At first, they only stayed behind occasionally. Then, more often than not.</p>

<p>Eventually, they no longer accompanied the group at all, content to watch them leave from a distance, their expressions a mixture of pride and lingering concern.</p>

<p>Even then, one thing never changed.</p>

<p>Brandon was always with them.</p>

<p>Everyone knew how strong Brandon was.</p>

<p>It was not something he boasted about, nor something he ever demonstrated seriously—but those who had sparred with him understood all too well.</p>

<p>His movements were calm, controlled, and terrifyingly efficient.</p>

<p>No matter how hard they tried, no one could truly push him.</p>

<p>Among Ross’ wives, only Althea could manage to stand her ground against him for more than a brief exchange.</p>

<p>Even so, there was no certainty that she was actually forcing him to exert himself.</p>

<p>Their spars often looked casual, almost playful, devoid of killing intent or desperate struggle.</p>

<p>That uncertainty unsettled everyone.</p>

<p>They could not tell where Brandon’s limits truly lay—or if he even had any.</p>

<p>As for Ross, his reputation grew steadily with each passing day.</p>

<p>Everyone acknowledged his strength, and many openly admired him.</p>

<p>Most believed his power stemmed from the countless heart stones he possessed, assuming that sheer resources were the reason he advanced so quickly.</p>

<p>It was a comforting explanation.</p>

<p>Unfortunately, it was also wrong.</p>

<p>None of them knew the truth.</p>

<p>None of them realized that Ross’s strength had already surpassed all known limits—that he was no longer merely strong, but peerless.</p>

<p>In fact, long before anyone noticed, Ross had already become the strongest man in the entire multiverse, an existence standing far above the ruins of this broken world.</p>

<p>And yet, he remained silent, allowing everyone to misunderstand.</p>

<p>For now.</p>

<p>Week by week, Alistair and his siblings continued their expeditions beyond the safety of Aegis, venturing deeper into the devastated city with every passing raid.</p>

<p>At first, the survivors they encountered were few—lonely figures hiding in basements, collapsed buildings, and sealed apartments, clinging to life through nothing but fear and stubborn will.</p>

<p>Most of those early rescues were barely alive.</p>

<p>As the siblings grew stronger, their confidence and efficiency increased alongside their power.</p>

<p>What once required careful scouting and cautious movement gradually transformed into methodical operations.</p>

<p>Routes were mapped, danger zones cleared, and entire districts slowly reclaimed from the undead.</p>

<p>With greater strength came greater reach.</p>

<p>Within just three months of relentless zombie raiding, the number of rescued survivors skyrocketed.</p>

<p>The siblings saved more than fifteen hundred people, pulling them back from the edge of extinction.</p>

<p>By then, the population of Aegis had swelled to over two thousand, transforming the settlement from a refuge into a growing community.</p>

<p>For those who were saved, the experience was nothing short of overwhelming.</p>

<p>Many broke down the moment they crossed the gates.</p>

<p>Hardened expressions shattered, and bloody tears streamed down their faces as they realized they were no longer running, no longer hiding, no longer waiting to die.</p>

<p>Some fell to their knees, trembling as if the ground itself were the only thing keeping them upright.</p>

<p>They had survived hell.</p>

<p>Most of the rescued were painfully weak.</p>

<p>They possessed no combat abilities, no awakened powers, and no means to fight back.</p>

<p>Killing zombies had never been an option for them.</p>

<p>Instead, they had endured by hiding in silence, rationing scraps of food, and praying each night that the undead would not find them.</p>

<p>Hunger had carved deep lines into their bodies.</p>

<p>Many were emaciated, their ribs clearly visible beneath thin skin.</p>

<p>Some were carried into Aegis unconscious, teetering on the brink of death.</p>

<p>Infection, exhaustion, and despair had hollowed them out long before they were rescued.</p>

<p>But Aegis was different.</p>

<p>Inside its walls, survivors were met not with judgment, but with care.</p>

<p>Those who had awakened healing abilities worked tirelessly, moving from one injured person to the next.</p>

<p>Wounds that would have once been fatal slowly closed. Fevers broke.</p>

<p>Starving bodies were stabilized and nourished back to health.</p>

<p>Food was shared.</p>

<p>Shelter was provided.</p>

<p>Order was restored.</p>

<p>For the first time since the apocalypse began, people slept without clutching weapons or listening for groans in the dark.</p>

<p>Hope—fragile but real—began to take root.</p>

<p>Aegis was no longer just a place to hide.</p>

<p>It was becoming a symbol of survival, a beacon in a dead world, and the quiet foundation of something that might one day resemble a future again.</p>

<p>Still, as the anniversary of the zombie apocalypse arrived, the world seemed to hold its breath.</p>

<p>Cities lay in ruins, the streets still haunted by the echoes of the fallen, and yet life—fragile and stubborn—had clawed its way back.</p>

<p>It was on this day that someone, who had spent all the intervening time searching for a way to escape or fix his predicament, finally stirred.</p>
