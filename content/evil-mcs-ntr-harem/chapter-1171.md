<p>Cyrus Thorn opened his eyes, slow and deliberate.</p>

<p>They gleamed with a sharp intelligence, cold and calculating, reflecting a mind that had never stopped planning.</p>

<p>&quot;So... it begins,&quot; he muttered under his breath, a faint smile tugging at the corners of his lips.</p>

<p>The world was going to change again—but not for the better.</p>

<p>The fragile balance that had barely formed over the past year was already beginning to crack.</p>

<p>More bloodshed awaited.</p>

<p>More pain, more suffering, more screams swallowed by ruined streets and empty skies.</p>

<p>Entire settlements would rise only to fall, and countless lives would be extinguished before they ever understood why.</p>

<p>Cyrus Thorn felt nothing.</p>

<p>The fate of the world did not concern him.</p>

<p>The living, the dead, the desperate struggles of survivors—none of it mattered.</p>

<p>Compared to the chain wrapped around his very existence, the apocalypse itself was little more than background noise.</p>

<p>A full year had passed.</p>

<p>Three hundred and sixty-five days of confinement, humiliation, and relentless frustration—and yet he still had no solution.</p>

<p>No method. No path forward.</p>

<p>The enslavement Ross had imposed upon him remained flawless, absolute, and infuriatingly elegant.</p>

<p>It was not merely control over his body, but over his will, his instincts, and even the direction of his thoughts.</p>

<p>Every attempt to resist was anticipated.</p>

<p>Every loophole was sealed.</p>

<p>Every act of defiance was rendered meaningless.</p>

<p>He had tried everything.</p>

<p>He had pushed his powers to their limits, only to feel the invisible restraint tighten further.</p>

<p>He had experimented with self-inflicted damage, hoping pain might disrupt the control.</p>

<p>He had invoked forbidden techniques, twisted laws, and shattered his own mind again and again—only for it to reassemble under Ross’s authority, unchanged and unbroken.</p>

<p>Failure followed failure.</p>

<p>Yet surrender never crossed his mind.</p>

<p>Cyrus’s hatred was cold, patient, and enduring. It did not burn wildly—it waited.</p>

<p>As long as he still breathed, there remained a possibility, no matter how distant, of escape. He believed this with absolute certainty.</p>

<p>Slowly, he closed his eyes once more.</p>

<p>In the darkness of his mind, countless theories unfolded.</p>

<p>Hypotheses were tested, discarded, refined, and tested again.</p>

<p>He dissected his own bondage layer by layer, probing for flaws too subtle for even Ross to notice.</p>

<p>If the chains were perfect, then Cyrus would simply have to become something that perfection could not account for.</p>

<p>He would adapt.</p>

<p>He would endure.</p>

<p>He would experiment without end.</p>

<p>Whether it took years, decades, or the rest of eternity did not matter.</p>

<p>Cyrus Thorn would find a way out of this dire predicament—</p>

<p>or he would turn his very existence into a weapon sharp enough to wound even the one who enslaved him.</p>

<p>***</p>

<p>&quot;Alright. Remember what I told you,&quot; the man whispered, his voice barely more than a breath carried through the stale air.</p>

<p>&quot;Absolute silence. Absolute stealth. No sudden movements. No mistakes.&quot;</p>

<p>He raised two fingers in a practiced signal.</p>

<p>The small group of survivors nodded, each one tense but composed.</p>

<p>They had done this many times before.</p>

<p>Over the past year, scavenging missions like this had become routine—dangerous, yes, but familiar.</p>

<p>They had survived through discipline, patience, and abilities perfectly suited for moving unseen.</p>

<p>They trusted the method.</p>

<p>They trusted each other.</p>

<p>One of them quietly activated an invisibility spell.</p>

<p>A subtle distortion rippled outward, and the group vanished from sight, their figures dissolving into the surrounding environment.</p>

<p>Even their heat signatures and faint spiritual fluctuations were suppressed, allowing them to blend seamlessly into the ruined city.</p>

<p>They began to move.</p>

<p>Every step was deliberate. Every breath carefully controlled.</p>

<p>The streets were littered with abandoned vehicles and broken concrete, but none of them made a sound.</p>

<p>Zombies wandered nearby—groaning, dragging their feet—but never once turned their heads.</p>

<p>The group slipped past them like ghosts.</p>

<p>Their target soon came into view: a massive warehouse squatting at the edge of the district, its metal walls corroded and its loading bay doors half-crushed by time.</p>

<p>From the outside, it looked forgotten. From their earlier scouting, they knew it was anything but empty.</p>

<p>Inside were supplies.</p>

<p>Food. Medicine. Tools.</p>

<p>Enough to keep their people alive for weeks.</p>

<p>They circled the structure, scanning carefully. No unusual movement.</p>

<p>No concentrated undead presence. Everything appeared exactly as it should.</p>

<p>Too perfect.</p>

<p>They approached the entrance, preparing to slip inside and seal the door behind them—</p>

<p>When every single one of them froze.</p>

<p>A wave of dread washed over the group simultaneously. Skin prickled. Hair stood on end.</p>

<p>Their instincts screamed danger, loud and overwhelming.</p>

<p>Then a voice cut through the air.</p>

<p>&quot;So this is where you’re hiding.&quot;</p>

<p>It was thin and emotionless, spoken with casual certainty—as if the speaker had been watching them the entire time.</p>

<p>The invisibility spell shattered like fragile glass.</p>

<p>Before anyone could react, something moved.</p>

<p>The leader barely had time to turn before he was struck.</p>

<p>His body lifted off the ground, slammed violently into the warehouse wall, and crumpled with a sickening crack.</p>

<p>Blood sprayed across rusted metal.</p>

<p>Screams erupted.</p>

<p>&quot;AHHHHH—!&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;No—! NOOOO—!&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;RUN! RUN—!&quot;</p>

<p>Panic obliterated their discipline.</p>

<p>The carefully maintained formation collapsed instantly as shadows lunged from impossible angles.</p>

<p>One survivor was dragged screaming into the darkness, his cries cut short by tearing flesh.</p>

<p>Another tried to activate an escape ability, only to be ripped in half mid-cast.</p>

<p>There was no enemy they could clearly see.</p>

<p>Only movement.</p>

<p>Only death.</p>

<p>They ran blindly, crashing into shelves and crates, slipping on blood-slick floors. Teeth sank into necks.</p>

<p>Bones shattered beneath crushing force. One by one, they were pulled down, devoured, and silenced.</p>

<p>Within minutes, it was over.</p>

<p>The warehouse returned to stillness.</p>

<p>Blood pooled across the concrete floor. Supplies lay scattered, untouched.</p>

<p>Not a single survivor remained—only gnawed bones and fading echoes of terror.</p>

<p>And somewhere in the shadows, something unseen lingered...</p>

<p>patiently waiting for the next ones who thought they were safe.</p>

<p>All around the world, the same nightmare was unfolding.</p>

<p>In shattered cities, hidden bunkers, underground shelters, and fortified settlements, chaos erupted almost simultaneously—as if some unseen signal had been given.</p>
