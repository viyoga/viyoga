<p>Miss Su quietly released her spiritual sense and swept it over Jiang Chen.</p>

<p>Nothing.</p>

<p>No spiritual energy.</p>

<p>No cultivation fluctuations.</p>

<p>Not even the faintest trace of a hidden technique.</p>

<p>As far as her senses could determine, Jiang Chen was nothing more than an ordinary mortal.</p>

<p>That made the situation even stranger.</p>

<p>If he isn’t using cultivation, then how is he doing this?</p>

<p>Miss Su’s curiosity deepened.</p>

<p>She had encountered many experts and many unusual gambling techniques throughout her years running the Golden Fortune, but she had never encountered someone like Jiang Chen.</p>

<p>There was clearly a secret.</p>

<p>And she intended to uncover it.</p>

<p>She allowed a charming smile to return to her face.</p>

<p>&quot;Let’s take another short break, Young Master Jiang Chen.&quot;</p>

<p>She leaned slightly closer.</p>

<p>&quot;I have some excellent wine in my private room upstairs. Would you like to join me for a drink?&quot;</p>

<p>She spoke confidently.</p>

<p>Miss Su had enough experience with men to know that very few would reject such an invitation from a woman in her position.</p>

<p>Jiang Chen’s eyes immediately brightened.</p>

<p>&quot;Really?&quot;</p>

<p>He stood up without hesitation.</p>

<p>&quot;Good! I’d love that.&quot;</p>

<p>He gave her an eager smile.</p>

<p>&quot;Lead the way, Miss Su!&quot;</p>

<p>Miss Su’s smile deepened.</p>

<p>She turned around and began walking toward the stairs, secretly wondering just how many secrets this strange young man was hiding.</p>

<p>Behind her, Jiang Chen followed happily.</p>

<p>But beneath his excited expression, his eyes were calm.</p>

<p>So she’s finally getting serious.</p>

<p>Interesting.</p>

<p>They entered Miss Su’s private chambers, and Jiang Chen immediately understood why she had invited him upstairs.</p>

<p>The room was elegant and comfortable, far more luxurious than the gambling floor below.</p>

<p>Soft chairs were arranged around a small table, while several shelves were filled with neatly organized books.</p>

<p>Along one wall stood rows of fine liquor, each bottle seemingly more expensive than the last.</p>

<p>Ross looked around appreciatively.</p>

<p>&quot;Not bad.&quot;</p>

<p>He had no intention of making himself uncomfortable, so he immediately chose one of the soft chairs and sat down.</p>

<p>&quot;It’s actually quite relaxing up here.&quot;</p>

<p>Miss Su remained standing.</p>

<p>At first, her expression was still pleasant and welcoming.</p>

<p>But after Jiang Chen settled into his chair, her expression gradually changed.</p>

<p>The warmth disappeared.</p>

<p>Her beautiful eyes became sharp and calculating.</p>

<p>&quot;How did you do it?&quot;</p>

<p>The sudden question didn’t surprise Jiang Chen at all.</p>

<p>He leaned back comfortably.</p>

<p>&quot;You mean how I cheated?&quot;</p>

<p>Miss Su’s eyes narrowed.</p>

<p>&quot;Yes.&quot;</p>

<p>Ross smiled.</p>

<p>&quot;It’s simple.&quot;</p>

<p>He casually glanced at the liquor shelves before looking back at her.</p>

<p>&quot;Or don’t tell me that only the house is allowed to cheat while the players aren’t?&quot;</p>

<p>The amused expression on his face made Miss Su’s brows furrow.</p>

<p>That wasn’t the reaction she had expected.</p>

<p>Most gamblers who were caught cheating would become nervous, frightened, or immediately attempt to deny everything.</p>

<p>Jiang Chen was different.</p>

<p>He was sitting comfortably in her private chambers as though he were visiting a friend’s home.</p>

<p>There wasn’t even the slightest trace of fear in his expression.</p>

<p>More importantly, he had openly admitted that he had cheated.</p>

<p>Miss Su studied him carefully.</p>

<p>Where does his confidence come from?</p>

<p>She had already investigated Jiang Chen’s cultivation.</p>

<p>There was none.</p>

<p>She had also swept him with her spiritual sense and found no hidden expert accompanying him.</p>

<p>Yet despite that, he had walked into her private room without hesitation.</p>

<p>It was almost as though he wasn’t sitting inside the private chambers of the owner of one of the most powerful gambling houses in the region.</p>

<p>It was as though he were the one in control.</p>

<p>Miss Su’s frown deepened.</p>

<p>&quot;Young Master Jiang Chen,&quot; she said slowly, &quot;you seem very confident.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;I am.&quot;</p>

<p>The answer came immediately.</p>

<p>Miss Su’s eyes flashed.</p>

<p>&quot;Even after admitting that you cheated?&quot;</p>

<p>Jiang Chen shrugged.</p>

<p>&quot;Why wouldn’t I be?&quot;</p>

<p>He smiled again.</p>

<p>&quot;Besides, if your gambling house can use tricks to make customers lose their money, shouldn’t customers be allowed to have a few tricks of their own?&quot;</p>

<p>Miss Su stared at him for several breaths.</p>

<p>She couldn’t decide whether Jiang Chen was incredibly bold or simply didn’t understand the danger he was in.</p>

<p>But one thing was becoming increasingly certain.</p>

<p>This young man was hiding something.</p>

<p>And now she was more determined than ever to discover exactly what it was.</p>

<p>Still, Miss Su wasn’t frightened.</p>

<p>She had managed the Golden Fortune for many years and had encountered all kinds of difficult customers.</p>

<p>Jiang Chen’s confidence might have been unusual, but she wasn’t about to be intimidated by it.</p>

<p>&quot;Return the money you won,&quot; she said coldly. &quot;Once you do that, you can leave. From this moment onward, you are also permanently banned from entering the Golden Fortune.&quot;</p>

<p>She gestured toward the door.</p>

<p>&quot;You may go.&quot;</p>

<p>Jiang Chen stared at her for a moment before breaking into an amused smile.</p>

<p>&quot;I don’t think so.&quot;</p>

<p>His tone remained completely relaxed.</p>

<p>&quot;That money is mine. I won it fair and square or, at the very least, no more unfairly than the house operates.&quot;</p>

<p>Miss Su’s expression darkened.</p>

<p>Jiang Chen leaned back in his chair.</p>

<p>&quot;But I have another offer.&quot;</p>

<p>Miss Su narrowed her eyes.</p>

<p>&quot;What offer?&quot;</p>

<p>Jiang Chen smiled mischievously.</p>

<p>&quot;Kneel and acknowledge your new master.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;What are y...&quot;</p>

<p>Before Miss Su could finish her sentence, something completely unexpected happened.</p>

<p>Her body suddenly moved on its own.</p>

<p>&quot;No!&quot;</p>

<p>Her expression changed instantly.</p>

<p>She tried to stop herself, but her legs refused to obey her commands.</p>

<p>Step by step, she was forced forward.</p>

<p>Within a few breaths, she found herself kneeling before Jiang Chen.</p>

<p>Miss Su’s beautiful face became filled with shock and disbelief.</p>

<p>&quot;How...?&quot;</p>

<p>Jiang Chen looked down at her and laughed.</p>

<p>&quot;See? That wasn’t so difficult, was it?&quot;</p>

<p>He couldn’t help but feel pleased with the sight.</p>

<p>For someone who had been treated with disdain only moments earlier, seeing the proud owner of the Golden Fortune suddenly kneeling before him was certainly satisfying.</p>

<p>Miss Su gritted her teeth and desperately tried to regain control of her body.</p>

<p>Ross, meanwhile, remained completely calm.</p>

<p>He had no intention of immediately taking things any further.</p>

<p>In fact, he had already made up his mind about something.</p>

<p>Not her.</p>

<p>He glanced at Miss Su thoughtfully.</p>

<p>She was undeniably beautiful, but Ross wanted his first woman in this cultivation world to be someone who had never been involved with anyone before.</p>

<p>Miss Su had already experienced far more of the world than he wanted for his first relationship, and she even had a daughter.</p>

<p>Maybe someday.</p>

<p>Ross smiled faintly.</p>

<p>But not now.</p>
