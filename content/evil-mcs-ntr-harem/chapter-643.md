<p>With the full backing of an entire nation, Ross implemented his plans with unwavering precision.</p>

<p>Nothing stood in his way. Infrastructure projects were launched overnight, welfare programs expanded rapidly, and entire regions transformed under his command.</p>

<p>His influence reached every corner of the country, touching lives in ways people had only dreamed of.</p>

<p>He pushed his agenda even further, not with brute force, but with benevolence—earning admiration, awe, and near-worship.</p>

<p>In the eyes of the world, Ross Oakley was nothing short of a miracle.</p>

<p>&quot;Ross Oakley is unreal! He helps people every single day, and on such a massive scale—it&#x27;s changing lives!&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;I know, right? Every day it&#x27;s something new. He&#x27;s always donating, building, giving back. I even heard he&#x27;s planning to construct a city just for the homeless—free housing, free education, everything!&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;Wow! Ross must&#x27;ve been a saint in his past life. He&#x27;s such a good man. I wish every billionaire would follow his lead.&quot;</p>

<p>News anchors, social media influencers, politicians, and even rival nations praised him. Documentaries were made about his generosity.</p>

<p>Schools began including chapters about his life. He wasn&#x27;t just a public figure anymore—he was a global symbol of hope, a modern-day messiah.</p>

<p>Yet beneath the surface of all the admiration and goodwill, Ross remained an enigma.</p>

<p>He didn&#x27;t care for fame, nor did he chase after glory.</p>

<p>He never invested in futuristic inventions or theoretical tech; he focused instead on practical, instant solutions—food, shelter, medical aid.</p>

<p>It wasn&#x27;t innovation he sought. It was influence. He didn&#x27;t want to impress. He wanted to control.</p>

<p>This was exactly what he desired.</p>

<p>In the silence of his private estate, far from the noise of the masses and their praises, Ross stood by a tall window that overlooked the city he&#x27;d rebuilt.</p>

<p>The sky was a sheet of ink above him, the stars veiled by thick clouds. He gazed at the darkness with eyes that saw far beyond the night.</p>

<p>&quot;The fish has taken the bait,&quot; he murmured, voice low and calculated. A slow, knowing smile crept across his lips—sharp, cold, and far from kind.</p>

<p>Everything was going according to plan.</p>

<p>People saw a savior. But Ross? He saw pawns. Tools. Pieces on a grand board, moving exactly where he wanted them to go.</p>

<p>His generosity was real, yes—but so was his ambition. The help he gave was just the first step.</p>

<p>&quot;Soon,&quot; he whispered again, the smile deepening as the wind outside picked up. &quot;Very soon.&quot;</p>

<p>***</p>

<p>December of 2033 arrived, and with it came another grand milestone in Ross Oakley&#x27;s ever-evolving legend—he married Brenda and Colleen, bringing his number of wives to a staggering twenty-nine.</p>

<p>The world, already enraptured by his every move, exploded with excitement. News channels across continents paused their regular programming to cover the opulent ceremony.</p>

<p>Social media platforms were flooded with images of Ross and his brides, all dressed in custom-made attire that combined elegance, tradition, and futuristic fashion.</p>

<p>The wedding was not just a personal celebration—it was a global phenomenon.</p>

<p>Held in the capital city Ross had personally revitalized, the event saw leaders, celebrities, scientists, and royals in attendance.</p>

<p>Entire nations declared a public holiday in honor of the occasion.</p>

<p>Drones captured aerial footage of the colossal parade that followed the ceremony, and holographic displays projected the couple&#x27;s vows in multiple languages.</p>

<p>For many, this wasn&#x27;t merely a wedding—it was history unfolding in real time.</p>

<p>But beyond the spectacle, Ross&#x27;s mind was always several steps ahead.</p>

<p>Brenda and Colleen, having come from a time long before the present, were strangers to this world.</p>

<p>Yet Ross had prepared for this. He gave them new identities, meticulously crafted down to the smallest detail.</p>

<p>Their legal records, background stories, and even their social profiles were seamlessly integrated into the databases of 2033&#x27;s modern society.</p>

<p>He&#x27;d spared no effort to ensure they could adapt quickly and comfortably.</p>

<p>Private tutors, myriad learning devices, and curated cultural immersion programs were made available to them.</p>

<p>Their wardrobes were stocked with clothing designed not just for style, but to subtly guide them through the norms and expectations of their new reality.</p>

<p>Ross knew there would be an adjustment period—but he also knew one unshakable truth: he was there to make the transition effortless.</p>

<p>And when Ross Oakley wanted something, the world bent to accommodate it.</p>

<p>&quot;There&#x27;s nothing in this world that can&#x27;t be solved,&quot; he had told them, his voice calm and sure as always. &quot;You&#x27;ll never walk alone. I&#x27;ve made this world ours.&quot;</p>

<p>By January of 2034, the celebrations had barely quieted, yet Ross was already back to work.</p>

<p>He began the year not with rest, but with a renewed wave of global humanitarian campaigns.</p>

<p>Massive aid convoys, powered by new technology, were dispatched to regions suffering from climate disasters.</p>

<p>He rolled out universal health programs in underdeveloped countries, eradicating diseases that had plagued them for decades.</p>

<p>Entire communities were lifted out of poverty almost overnight as he established clean energy grids, educational institutions, and housing initiatives.</p>

<p>He wasn&#x27;t just giving people money—he was giving them the infrastructure to thrive.</p>

<p>Every corner of the globe bore his fingerprints.</p>

<p>People spoke of Ross not just as a man, but as a myth come to life.</p>

<p>Children idolized him, scholars studied him, and world leaders deferred to him.</p>

<p>But in the quiet moments, in the private sanctuaries of his sprawling estate, Ross never lost sight of what all this was truly for.</p>

<p>Every act of kindness, every gift to humanity, was a brick in the foundation of the empire he was building.</p>

<p>Not just an empire of land or power—but of devotion, dependency, and legacy.</p>

<p>And at the heart of it all stood Ross Oakley, the man who had reshaped the world in his image.</p>

<p>The months rolled by, and as the world settled into the rhythm of the new year, one event began to dominate headlines—the NBA playoffs.</p>

<p>Stadiums buzzed with anticipation, fans around the world marked their calendars, and social media exploded with predictions and debates.</p>

<p>But amidst the chatter, one name stood above all others.</p>

<p>Ross Oakley.</p>
