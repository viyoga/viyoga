<p>Ross knew this.</p>

<p>He had seen the threads of the technique unravel before his eyes, thanks to his intimate knowledge of Cyrus Thorn, the man behind its power.</p>

<p>Every effect, every mutation, every twisted evolution of the zombie hordes had been calculated long ago—and he understood it all.</p>

<p>The devastation that others only began to recognize as it unfolded was no surprise to him.</p>

<p>Yet Ross felt nothing for it.</p>

<p>The plight of the world, the suffering of its people, the death toll climbing by the millions—none of it mattered.</p>

<p>He and his loved ones were safe. They would endure. Survival was guaranteed.</p>

<p>The collapse of civilizations, the agony of those left behind, the corruption of the World Heart—these were not his concern.</p>

<p>What mattered to Ross was how he would spend this interlude, this rare pause between waves of chaos.</p>

<p>A lull where danger had momentarily receded, where the world was forced to catch its breath.</p>

<p>For someone like him, moments like these were precious, rare, and deserving of attention.</p>

<p>And Ross had his ways of finding amusement.</p>

<p>He moved through the world with a predator’s calm, enjoying the vulnerability of others, observing them from a distance, seeing how they reacted to fear and uncertainty.</p>

<p>The helplessness of survivors, their panic, their confusion—it fascinated him.</p>

<p>He took advantage of it, shaping events to entertain himself, manipulating outcomes subtly while remaining untouchable.</p>

<p>Among his preferred diversions was hunting, not out of necessity, but out of pure sport.</p>

<p>He relished challenges, and there was no greater thrill for him than pursuing the beautiful, the elusive, and the unwary.</p>

<p>The chase was intoxicating, a distraction from the monotony of waiting for the next wave of destruction.</p>

<p>And in this brief lull, Ross indulged fully, meticulously planning his games, selecting his targets with precision, and ensuring that every pursuit satisfied his exacting standards.</p>

<p>To the rest of the world, he might have appeared detached or even coldhearted—but beneath that calm exterior burned a mind that thrived on control, on strategy, and on the satisfaction of knowing that he alone moved freely while chaos reigned all around.</p>

<p>The apocalypse raged outside, the zombie hordes advanced, and the World Heart trembled under the weight of despair—and yet, Ross walked through it all, untouched, calculating, and entirely unconcerned.</p>

<p>&quot;So, let’s see who I’ll be playing with today,&quot; Ross muttered.</p>

<p>His voice was calm, almost idle, as though he were deciding how to spend a lazy afternoon rather than peering into the fate of the world.</p>

<p>His divine sense unfurled completely.</p>

<p>It spread outward in every direction, unhindered by distance or obstruction, washing over continents, oceans, and ruined cities alike.</p>

<p>Mountains might as well have been dust, and walls no more than paper.</p>

<p>Every living being was laid bare before him—heartbeats echoing faintly, thoughts flickering like dying embers, fear and hope intertwined in fragile threads.</p>

<p>He observed them all.</p>

<p>Survivor camps huddled behind crumbling defenses.</p>

<p>Lone wanderers scavenged through the remains of civilization, clinging to desperation. Some prayed.</p>

<p>Some cursed. Some had already resigned themselves to death.</p>

<p>Ross perceived it all with detached clarity, his mind filtering through countless existences in an instant.</p>

<p>Most were uninteresting.</p>

<p>Too broken. Too dull. Too predictable.</p>

<p>He passed over them without pause, dismissing entire regions as effortlessly as one might skim past words on a page.</p>

<p>Opportunities were abundant, yet very few were worth his attention.</p>

<p>Then—</p>

<p>His divine sense halted.</p>

<p>Among the countless flickering lives, one presence stood out.</p>

<p>Not because of overwhelming strength, but because of contrast. Resolve buried beneath fear.</p>

<p>A will that had not yet been crushed. Potential still untouched by despair.</p>

<p>It aligned perfectly with the unspoken standards Ross carried within him.</p>

<p>A faint smile slowly formed on his face.</p>

<p>&quot;There you are,&quot; he murmured, his tone soft but unmistakably possessive. &quot;You’ll do.&quot;</p>

<p>The world around him trembled ever so slightly—not from force, but from authority.</p>

<p>Space bent to his will, folding inward without sound or resistance.</p>

<p>There was no flash of light, no ripple of energy for others to sense.</p>

<p>One moment Ross stood where he was, and the next, he simply wasn’t there anymore.</p>

<p>Reality closed behind him as if he had never existed.</p>

<p>Elsewhere, far beyond mortal perception, Ross emerged seamlessly at his destination.</p>

<p>He stood close—far closer than any normal being should have been—yet remained unseen.</p>

<p>His presence was perfectly concealed, his aura erased, his existence reduced to nothing more than a shadow that even fate itself struggled to detect.</p>

<p>He observed his chosen target in silence.</p>

<p>Every breath. Every subtle movement. Every emotion flickering beneath the surface.</p>

<p>Ross did not rush. He never did.</p>

<p>After all, anticipation was part of the game—and he had all the time in the world.</p>

<p>Washington, D.C.</p>

<p>&quot;I think that’s our target,&quot; the woman said, her voice low but firm, stating the obvious.</p>

<p>She was strikingly beautiful, with short crimson hair that barely brushed her chin.</p>

<p>Her combat armor, fitted tightly yet designed for mobility, covered her from neck to toe—but even through the layers of reinforced plating, her tall, athletic frame was impossible to ignore.</p>

<p>She carried herself with the calm assurance of someone who had faced unimaginable horrors and survived.</p>

<p>Her sharp eyes scanned the ruined streets below and the sky above, assessing every detail.</p>

<p>&quot;Stay alert,&quot; the man beside her replied, his voice steady but edged with caution.</p>

<p>Tall, handsome, and clad in identical combat gear, he exuded quiet confidence.</p>

<p>Yet the tension in his shoulders betrayed the weight of the situation.</p>

<p>&quot;We’ve all seen what these things are capable of. Don’t underestimate her.&quot;</p>

<p>They both looked upward.</p>

<p>Hovering far above the skyline was a baby girl—a vision both horrifying and impossible.</p>

<p>Her small, delicate body floated effortlessly, motionless in the air as though gravity did not exist.</p>

<p>She looked pristine, untouched by the devastation around her, yet her expression was anything but innocent.</p>

<p>Wide eyes gleamed with maniacal glee, and a twisted smile played across her lips.</p>

<p>The sheer unnaturalness of her presence made the two hunters tense instantly.</p>
