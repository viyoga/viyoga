<p>The insult hung in the air like poison.</p>

<p>One of the men flinched, finger twitching on the trigger. Another shifted uncomfortably.</p>

<p>They&#x27;d faced threats before—arrogant men, desperate men, even men with nothing to lose. But Ross Oakley wasn&#x27;t like any of them.</p>

<p>He was calm. Too calm.</p>

<p>It wasn&#x27;t just confidence—it was something colder. Like he already knew how this would end, and none of them mattered.</p>

<p>Inside the control room, Thomas watched the live feed in silence. He could feel the tension through the screen. And the worst part?</p>

<p>Ross hadn&#x27;t even drawn a weapon…</p>

<p>And still, he was the most dangerous man on the field.</p>

<p>A few tense breaths passed in absolute silence. The kind of silence that presses against your ears, heavy and stifling.</p>

<p>On the monitors, Ross Oakley stood still, hands calmly at his sides, eyes unwavering.</p>

<p>Thomas stared at the screen, calculating. Weighing the risks. This wasn&#x27;t some idiot off the street.</p>

<p>It was Ross—Ross Oakley, the man whose name alone made experienced mercenaries sweat.</p>

<p>Finally, Thomas made the call.</p>

<p>&quot;Cuff him and bring him in.&quot;</p>

<p>His voice was steady, but beneath it ran a current of unease.</p>

<p>The men stationed outside heard the command through their earpieces and didn&#x27;t hesitate.</p>

<p>They moved in with practiced precision, surrounding Ross even tighter.</p>

<p>One of them pulled out a pair of thick black cuffs—custom restraints forged for high-risk targets.</p>

<p>Heavy, unforgiving metal that clamped down like a vice.</p>

<p>&quot;Oh?&quot; Ross said, cocking an eyebrow as the cuffs clicked shut behind his back.</p>

<p>&quot;Brand-new bracelets? Fancy. I didn&#x27;t expect gifts today. How thoughtful. I&#x27;ll give some gifts later on.&quot;</p>

<p>He chuckled softly, not a trace of fear in his voice. The cold steel bit into his wrists, pinning his arms behind him, but he walked forward like it was just another day.</p>

<p>The guards led him through the halls of the compound, rifles at his back, but it was clear—Ross wasn&#x27;t intimidated. If anything, he was enjoying the walk.</p>

<p>Thomas watched from the center of the hall as Ross was brought in. The place was designed for intimidation—gray stone walls, flickering lights, a wide, echoing space meant to disorient and unnerve.</p>

<p>Six masked guards stood in place with rifles at the ready.</p>

<p>And then there was Thomas himself—handsome, confident, still wearing that well-groomed arrogance that made people hate him before he even spoke.</p>

<p>Ross stepped into the center of the room, hands bound, and lifted his gaze to meet Thomas&#x27;s.</p>

<p>&quot;You gonna offer me a drink or just keep pretending this place doesn&#x27;t smell like cheap metal and fear?&quot; Ross asked casually.</p>

<p>Thomas didn&#x27;t take the bait. He stared at him for a moment, searching for a weakness, a crack in the armor. He found none.</p>

<p>&quot;What&#x27;s your plan?&quot; Thomas finally asked. His voice was cool, but his fingers twitched slightly at his side. &quot;You came here alone. No backup. No escape route. That&#x27;s not like you, Ross.&quot;</p>

<p>Ross&#x27;s smirk deepened.</p>

<p>&quot;You really think I&#x27;d walk into a place like this without an ace up my sleeve?&quot;</p>

<p>Thomas&#x27;s eyes narrowed. He knew Ross too well. This man didn&#x27;t play fair, and he didn&#x27;t make empty threats. There was a reason he was here, and there was a reason he was so calm.</p>

<p>Thomas had read his profile from top to bottom.</p>

<p>From the streets of Parkland City to the private military contracts overseas, Ross&#x27;s record was spotless—and terrifying.</p>

<p>Precision kills. Disappearing acts. Negotiations that ended with blood on the walls.</p>

<p>Although there was no evidence that he did it himself, everyone was convinced that he was the mastermind behind these mysterious killings.</p>

<p>Suicidal? No. Calculated? Always.</p>

<p>&quot;You&#x27;ve got someone here,&quot; Ross said smoothly, &quot;someone who doesn&#x27;t belong to you.&quot;</p>

<p>Thomas&#x27;s face remained unreadable, but Ross could feel the shift in the air.</p>

<p>&quot;I&#x27;m here to take her back. And,&quot; he added, voice dropping just a notch lower, &quot;to pay you back for your crimes. Don&#x27;t worry—I came prepared.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;You think this is a rescue mission?&quot; Thomas asked, voice dry. &quot;That girl isn&#x27;t yours anymore. She&#x27;s mine now. Mine. She belongs to this house now.&quot;</p>

<p>Ross chuckled. &quot;That&#x27;s cute. Really. But you should&#x27;ve done your homework. Jade&#x27;s not just a girl. She&#x27;s my wife. And I&#x27;m not the kind of man who lets people touch what&#x27;s his.&quot;</p>

<p>Thomas&#x27;s gaze flickered, just briefly, to the side. That told Ross everything.</p>

<p>He pressed on. &quot;I tolerated your bullshit. Even let you pretend for a moment that you were worth her time. That maybe, maybe, she might fall for your charm. But you? You couldn&#x27;t even measure up to the man she forgot on her worst day. Your charm?&quot; He scoffed.</p>

<p>&quot;Trash. Compared to my big cock, you&#x27;re just a joke with expensive cologne.&quot;</p>

<p>The insult cut deeper than any bullet. A couple of the guards stiffened, glancing at each other behind their masks.</p>

<p>They weren&#x27;t used to seeing their boss get talked down to—especially not by a cuffed man.</p>

<p>Thomas&#x27;s expression darkened, his lips tightening at the corners.</p>

<p>But Ross wasn&#x27;t finished.</p>

<p>&quot;You had your fun, Thomas. You tried to play king. But the game&#x27;s over now.&quot;</p>

<p>He stepped forward just slightly, metal cuffs clinking behind him.</p>

<p>&quot;You should&#x27;ve known better than to lay hands on what&#x27;s mine.&quot;</p>

<p>For a moment, no one moved.</p>

<p>The tension in the room was electric—guns raised, fingers twitching, and in the center of it all, Ross Oakley smiling like the devil himself.</p>

<p>Ding!</p>

<p>The soft metallic chime echoed faintly in the tense silence.</p>

<p>A breath later, Ross moved.</p>

<p>The cuffs binding his wrists fell away with a sharp clack, as if they&#x27;d never truly held him in the first place. For a split second, no one moved—frozen by disbelief.</p>

<p>Then everything happened at once.</p>

<p>Ross turned to the man closest to him. In a single, fluid motion, his fist rocketed upward in a brutal uppercut.</p>

<p>Crack!</p>

<p>The sound was sickening—bones snapping, flesh tearing. The man&#x27;s head whipped back violently, and then—boom.</p>

<p>Blood sprayed in a wide arc as Ross&#x27;s bare fist punched straight through the man&#x27;s jaw and shattered his skull from beneath the mask.</p>
