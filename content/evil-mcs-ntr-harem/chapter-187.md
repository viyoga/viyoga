<p>The world around him had blurred, reduced to a singular focus: tearing Ross away from Althea and ending this nightmare.</p>

<p>&quot;I&#x27;m going to kill the two of you!&quot; he bellowed, his voice echoing through the room.</p>

<p>For years, he had been the envy of everyone—rich, famous, and with the most beautiful girlfriend by his side.</p>

<p>His life had been perfect. Had been. Until now.</p>

<p>Reaching the bed, Robin lunged at Althea, his hand outstretched to grab her arm.</p>

<p>But that was all he managed to do—reach.</p>

<p>&quot;Pak!</p>

<p>The sound of Althea&#x27;s slap cracked like a thunderclap.</p>

<p>Robin&#x27;s vision blurred as his head snapped to the side, and he stumbled backward, tripping over the edge of the bed and crashing to the floor.</p>

<p>Dazed, he clutched his stinging cheek, the unmistakable imprint of her hand already blossoming red against his skin.</p>

<p>When he looked up, his disbelief was palpable.</p>

<p>His mouth opened and closed like a fish out of water as he stared at Althea, who remained poised on the bed, her eyes blazing with resolve.</p>

<p>&quot;I told you, Robin,&quot; she said, her voice cold and unyielding.</p>

<p>&quot;I may still have some feelings for you, but they&#x27;re nothing compared to what I feel for Ross. My love for him is immeasurable—something you could never hope to understand. So don&#x27;t interfere with our first night together.&quot;</p>

<p>Her lips curved into a wicked smile, her tone softening into something dangerously sweet.</p>

<p>&quot;Of course, you&#x27;re free to watch. Maybe even play with yourselves. Not that it&#x27;ll matter after tonight.&quot;</p>

<p>Her last words were a whisper, so quiet that only Ross could hear them.</p>

<p>He smirked, his dark eyes gleaming with satisfaction.</p>

<p>Althea knew him too well—better than anyone ever had—and every word from her lips was a testament to her devotion.</p>

<p>Robin staggered to his feet, his fists trembling with frustration and humiliation.</p>

<p>He opened his mouth to retort, but no words came out.</p>

<p>The cameramen, who had been filming everything, exchanged uneasy glances but didn&#x27;t dare stop recording.</p>

<p>The scene was too magnetic, too surreal to interrupt.</p>

<p>One of their hands was also busy playing with their man tools. That never stopped at all.</p>

<p>Althea didn&#x27;t spare Robin another glance.</p>

<p>She turned back to Ross, her hands sliding up his chest as she pressed her lips to his once more.</p>

<p>For her, Robin was already a relic of the past—forgotten, irrelevant, and powerless to change what had already been set in motion.</p>

<p>Robin for his part stood frozen, his breath caught in his throat.</p>

<p>For several moments, he couldn&#x27;t move, couldn&#x27;t think.</p>

<p>His cheek still burned from Althea&#x27;s slap, but the pain barely registered.</p>

<p>It was nothing compared to the turmoil raging inside him.</p>

<p>He replayed the strike in his mind, each moment a cruel reminder of how drastically things had changed.</p>

<p>Althea, the woman he had cherished for six long years, had not only turned her back on him but had physically lashed out.</p>

<p>The woman he thought he knew—the sweet, caring, virtuous woman—felt like a stranger now.</p>

<p>He thought of all the time he had spent with her, nurturing their love, putting her happiness above all else.</p>

<p>He had always believed they were destined to be together, that their bond was unshakable.</p>

<p>But now, staring at her as she tangled herself around Ross without shame or hesitation, that belief shattered into a thousand irreparable pieces.</p>

<p>&quot;Just give up, man,&quot; one of the cameramen quipped, breaking the suffocating silence.</p>

<p>His tone was casual, almost amused, as he adjusted the angle of his camera to better capture the scene.</p>

<p>&quot;Althea&#x27;s found herself a new daddy. And hey, you&#x27;re famous and handsome—you&#x27;ll bounce back. Beautiful girls are everywhere.&quot;</p>

<p>Robin&#x27;s fists clenched at his sides, his knuckles whitening as he fought back the urge to lash out.</p>

<p>He wanted to scream, to punch something, to do anything to release the storm of emotions tearing him apart.</p>

<p>But he couldn&#x27;t. He just couldn&#x27;t.</p>

<p>&quot;And watch where you&#x27;re running next time, Robin!&quot; the other cameraman chimed in, shooting him a scowl.</p>

<p>&quot;You nearly ruined the greatest sex scandal of all time!&quot;</p>

<p>Robin recognized the man as the one he had tripped over earlier in his blind rage.</p>

<p>The cameraman was already back to filming, his attention entirely on the bed, where Althea and Ross were completely absorbed in each other.</p>

<p>Robin&#x27;s vision blurred as tears welled up in his eyes.</p>

<p>He blinked rapidly, trying to keep them at bay, but they spilled over, hot and unchecked.</p>

<p>He hated himself for crying, hated the weakness it showed.</p>

<p>But no matter how much he tried, he couldn&#x27;t stop.</p>

<p>&quot;Pathetic,&quot; he muttered under his breath, his voice barely audible.</p>

<p>He stood there, unmoving, as the scene played out before him like a grotesque theater of betrayal.</p>

<p>Althea&#x27;s laughter rang out, soft and melodic, but it felt like a dagger to his chest.</p>

<p>She pressed herself closer to Ross, her movements fluid and uninhibited, every action a stark contrast to the modest, reserved woman Robin had known.</p>

<p>The love they had shared felt like a distant memory, a cruel illusion that had been ripped away to reveal the truth: she had never truly been his.</p>

<p>She belonged to Ross now, heart, body, and soul.</p>

<p>Robin&#x27;s tears continued to fall as the realization sank in. He was no longer part of her world.</p>

<p>She had made her choice, and it wasn&#x27;t him.</p>

<p>For the first time in his life, he felt truly powerless, like a spectator in his own tragedy.</p>

<p>The cameramen continued their work, occasionally muttering to each other about angles and lighting, their indifference only deepening Robin&#x27;s humiliation.</p>

<p>He wanted to yell at them, to demand they stop, but what was the point?</p>

<p>Nothing he said or did would change the outcome.</p>

<p>Defeated, Robin wiped at his eyes with the back of his hand, but the tears wouldn&#x27;t stop.</p>

<p>In the end, Robin just stood there, watching helplessly as the scene unfolded before him.</p>

<p>He felt utterly impotent, powerless to do anything to change the situation.</p>
