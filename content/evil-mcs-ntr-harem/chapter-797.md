<p>Ross gave a crooked grin behind the mask.</p>

<p>&quot;They tried,&quot; he said, shrugging.</p>

<p>&quot;I’ve had my share of flings. Fast and fun. No strings attached. But nothing ever lasted. I never wanted it to. Until now.&quot;</p>

<p>She narrowed her eyes playfully. &quot;Let me guess—something about tonight feels different?&quot;</p>

<p>He leaned in slightly, elbows on his knees, voice low and deliberate. &quot;Doesn’t it?&quot;</p>

<p>Cara laughed again, this time tilting her head back, amused but not charmed—not yet, anyway.</p>

<p>&quot;Nice try. But I’m already immune to guys like you. Especially the ones who sound like they swallow poetry books for breakfast.&quot;</p>

<p>Ross smiled, unbothered. &quot;Then maybe I’m not like the others.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;Or maybe I’ve just heard every line in the book,&quot; she countered, lifting a brow. &quot;And yours just has better delivery.&quot;</p>

<p>Ross looked at her for a moment longer, something unreadable in his eyes.</p>

<p>Then he leaned back again, letting the silence settle between them.</p>

<p>The music from the pool thudded in the background, distant and inconsequential now.</p>

<p>He let out a slow breath.</p>

<p>&quot;I wasn’t lying about the accident,&quot; he said quietly, tone more grounded.</p>

<p>&quot;It changed things for me. The way people looked at me... the way I looked at myself. I started wearing this mask to hide it at first. But somewhere along the way, it became easier to keep it on than take it off.&quot;</p>

<p>Cara’s smile faded slightly. She looked at him differently now—not with pity, but with a quiet kind of respect.</p>

<p>&quot;I’m sorry,&quot; she said.</p>

<p>&quot;Don’t be,&quot; Ross replied with a shrug. &quot;It’s just skin. And honestly? I think it forced me to grow up. I learned to stop relying on what people saw and start focusing on what I could make them feel.&quot;</p>

<p>She held his gaze for a long moment, then gave a small nod. &quot;That’s a pretty good philosophy.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;I try,&quot; Ross said, a faint smirk returning. &quot;Besides, it makes the mystery more fun, doesn’t it? Makes you wonder what I’m hiding under here.&quot;</p>

<p>Cara chuckled. &quot;Oh, I already know what’s under there.&quot;</p>

<p>Ross raised a brow. &quot;Do you?&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;Yup. A guy who’s too clever for his own good and just a little too used to getting what he wants.&quot;</p>

<p>He laughed at that, genuinely. &quot;You’re not wrong.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;I rarely am,&quot; she said with a wink.</p>

<p>There was a beat of silence, and then Cara looked away, her voice quieter. &quot;Still... I think it’s brave.&quot;</p>

<p>Ross tilted his head. &quot;What is?&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;Showing up like this. Not pretending to be perfect. Being honest, even when you’re clearly still guarded.&quot;</p>

<p>For once, Ross didn’t have a witty comeback. He just nodded, almost imperceptibly.</p>

<p>A light breeze swept across the deck, rustling the leaves of nearby potted palms and lifting strands of Cara’s hair, which she tucked behind her ear almost absentmindedly.</p>

<p>In the background, someone from the crew shouted about resetting lights, but neither of them reacted and just let the film continue.</p>

<p>Ross has explicitly said that he would be the one to cut a scene and no other had that ability.</p>

<p>It was just the two of them again—alone in a crowded space.</p>

<p>&quot;Maybe I’m just saying that as a defense mechanism,&quot; Cara said softly, glancing at him from the corner of her eye, &quot;but truly? I’m still figuring everything out. Again.&quot;</p>

<p>Ross turned to face her fully, his voice barely more than a murmur. &quot;Then I’ll wait.&quot;</p>

<p>She smiled at that, but this one wasn’t playful or teasing. It was gentle. Real.</p>

<p>&quot;Cut!&quot;</p>

<p>Ross gave the signal again, and the crew instantly shifted gears.</p>

<p>They changed sets with seamless coordination, moving from the patio to the pool, then to the game room and beyond.</p>

<p>It was a scene of eventual chemistry: Cara and Ross swimming beneath the sun, splashing each other like kids; sitting across from each other at a small table, locked in an intense game of chess, their gazes flirting more than the moves on the board; walking side by side under string lights with bare feet brushing against cool tiles, their casual laughter echoing into the night.</p>

<p>Through each take, the subtle progression of emotion was undeniable.</p>

<p>The camera captured it all—the way Cara’s eyes lingered just a little longer on Ross, the slight tremble in her voice when she laughed, the way her body instinctively leaned toward him when they talked.</p>

<p>Her character, once distant and guarded, was changing. She was falling for him.</p>

<p>And maybe, just maybe, the line between acting and reality was starting to blur.</p>

<p>Eventually, the crew moved inside.</p>

<p>The bedroom set had been prepared in advance: dim lights, soft jazz playing low in the background, candles flickering around the room, and a slight breeze from the air conditioner brushing against the curtains.</p>

<p>It wasn’t explicit—just a moment of vulnerability and intimacy. A kiss.</p>

<p>Nothing more.</p>

<p>Cara stood on her mark. So did Ross.</p>

<p>They were close—almost too close. His presence was magnetic.</p>

<p>The mask only made him more mysterious, more alluring.</p>

<p>She could feel the tension building in her chest as they stared at each other silently, waiting for the director’s cue.</p>

<p>She knew what the script called for. She’d read it a dozen times. She’d agreed to it.</p>

<p>It was just a kiss.</p>

<p>But before anyone could speak, a voice rang out across the set.</p>

<p>&quot;Stop! I don’t like this at all!&quot;</p>

<p>Everyone froze.</p>

<p>Corey stepped into the scene, fury radiating off him like a storm.</p>

<p>His face was flushed, and his eyes were wide with disbelief and rage.</p>

<p>His clenched fists shook at his sides.</p>

<p>&quot;Why the hell are you forcing Cara to make a kissing scene with you, D?! You absolute maniac! You’re disgusting!&quot;</p>

<p>His voice cracked as he yelled. A few of the crew looked away awkwardly.</p>

<p>Others simply watched, stunned.</p>

<p>Cara’s eyes widened, but she didn’t move. Her breath caught in her throat.</p>

<p>Ross didn’t flinch. He stood calm, steady, and still—like he had expected this.</p>

<p>His voice, when it came, was flat and composed.</p>

<p>&quot;It’s just a kiss, Corey,&quot; Ross said. &quot;This is a scripted scene. Everyone here knew what it was going to be.&quot;</p>
