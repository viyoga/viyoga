<p>The man gritted his teeth, forcing himself upright despite the pounding in his skull and the burning pain in his eyes.</p>

<p>He clenched his fists, channeling every ounce of strength and skill he had to resist the psychic onslaught.</p>

<p>His aura flared violently, a ripple of power that distorted the air around him, but even this barely seemed to affect the child.</p>

<p>Bella’s eyes narrowed. She doubled the speed and intensity of her attack.</p>

<p>More spheres erupted from her hands, weaving intricate patterns, striking from multiple angles, attempting to overwhelm her target.</p>

<p>Each strike carried the force of a small bomb, detonating with deafening booms that shook the crumbling cityscape.</p>

<p>The ascended child tilted slightly, dodging some spheres with impossible reflexes, her body twisting and bending in ways that no human should have been able to replicate.</p>

<p>Red energy flared around her small form as she retaliated, sending out pulses that forced Bella to duck and roll to avoid being flung across the street by sheer force.</p>

<p>Smoke, debris, and the roar of unleashed energy filled the air.</p>

<p>The battlefield had become a storm of chaos, light, and death.</p>

<p>Every movement of the child radiated malice and power.</p>

<p>She was not merely fighting—they were struggling against a force that had transcended humanity itself.</p>

<p>Bella felt her heart pound, not from fear, but from the adrenaline of facing something so overwhelmingly dangerous.</p>

<p>The man, still staggering from the psychic backlash, grit his teeth, trying to regain focus, knowing that one wrong move could mean instant death.</p>

<p>Above them, the child hovered, twisting and spinning, her laughter echoing like a cruel melody through the broken city.</p>

<p>“Hihihi! I’ve got you now!” the zombie girl cackled, her high-pitched voice carrying a chilling mix of childish delight and pure malice.</p>

<p>Before Bella could react, the child vanished from sight, leaving only a ripple in the air, as if reality itself had been bent around her.</p>

<p>In the next instant, her tiny hands shot forward—aiming straight at the center of Bella’s chest.</p>

<p>Puchi!</p>

<p>It appeared that the girl’s hands had pierced through Bella’s heart, an impossible strike that would have felled any normal human instantly.</p>

<p>Poof!</p>

<p>Yet the image flickered and vanished, leaving only Bella’s composed form, unharmed.</p>

<p>It had been a projection, a shadow of speed and malice meant to test reflexes and sow panic.</p>

<p>Bella’s eyes narrowed, her body tensed, and her mind raced—she had expected no less.</p>

<p>The child tilted her head and scanned the surroundings, her sharp gaze catching a faint, glowing trace of movement: the path Bella had taken as she dodged the phantom strike.</p>

<p>But Bella was not alone.</p>

<p>Cradled firmly in her arms, the man struggled to maintain balance, his aura flaring as he tried to keep pace with the unnatural speed of the chase.</p>

<p>“You came… but chose to run?” the child taunted, spinning lightly in the air with impossible agility.</p>

<p>“Do you think I’d let food escape from my mouth so easily? Run, run, run! I’m coming for you two! Hihihi!”</p>

<p>And with that, she leapt into motion, bounding forward with eerie grace.</p>

<p>She skipped and hopped through the air like a playful predator, twisting mid-flight with unnatural flexibility.</p>

<p>Every movement carried a sickening combination of childlike innocence and terrifying precision.</p>

<p>Yet her speed was terrifyingly real—matching Bella’s and sometimes surpassing it, despite her tiny frame.</p>

<p>Skyscrapers loomed like jagged teeth above them, and shattered streets sprawled below, littered with debris, abandoned vehicles, and the remnants of a world already torn apart.</p>

<p>The chase became a whirlwind of motion—red energy traces following Bella’s movements, the man flanking her, and the child darting unpredictably, her laughter echoing off the ruined walls.</p>

<p>Bella’s mind worked in overdrive.</p>

<p>She calculated every angle, every possible escape route, every debris-laden obstacle.</p>

<p>Her body moved in perfect sync with her thoughts, dodging shards of concrete, vaulting over wrecked cars, and spinning midair to keep the man safe.</p>

<p>Each step, each leap, each flick of her hands sent dozens of energy spheres spinning forward, creating a protective barrier as much as an offensive weapon.</p>

<p>The child laughed again, high-pitched and cruel, clearly amused by their evasions.</p>

<p>Yet she was relentless.</p>

<p>Each skip and hop wasn’t mere play—it was a predator learning, adjusting, testing their reflexes.</p>

<p>Every movement was a demonstration of superiority, a reminder that she was no ordinary foe.</p>

<p>“Faster!” Bella urged herself silently, feeling the child’s oppressive aura pressing in.</p>

<p>The air around them shimmered from the collision of their energies.</p>

<p>Each leap created gusts strong enough to topple light debris, and every energy sphere streaked through the air leaving a glowing trail that lit up the ruins in crimson flashes.</p>

<p>The man in her arms groaned under the effort, struggling to regain his footing while enduring the lingering psychic backlash from earlier.</p>

<p>Yet he clung to her, knowing their survival depended on coordination and timing.</p>

<p>Every misstep could be fatal.</p>

<p>Above them, the child twisted midair, her small form moving impossibly fast, her eyes gleaming with a terrifying combination of playfulness and hunger.</p>

<p>It was no longer just a chase.</p>

<p>It was a contest of reflexes, skill, and raw supernatural power, unfolding across the jagged ruins of Washington D.C.</p>

<p>Debris rained around them, dust and smoke thickened the air, and the shattered city became a labyrinth of danger and opportunity.</p>

<p>Bella and the man had to weave through it all, predicting the child’s impossible movements while maintaining their own momentum.</p>

<p>Bella gritted her teeth, her muscles screaming in protest as she barreled through the wreckage-strewn streets.</p>

<p>Her arms ached under Sebastian’s weight, every step heavier than the last, but she refused to slow.</p>

<p>Dust stung her eyes and lips, choking the air, and shards of debris littered every inch of the ruined city.</p>

<p>Broken vehicles, shattered signs, and splintered beams threatened to trip her with each frantic stride.</p>

<p>Yet even in the chaos, her focus was absolute.</p>

<p>“Hang on, Sebastian,” she whispered, tightening her arms around him.</p>

<p>Her voice trembled only slightly, betraying the storm of fear and determination roiling inside her.</p>
