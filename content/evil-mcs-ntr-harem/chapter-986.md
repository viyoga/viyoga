<p>Ross obliged each one, charming without arrogance, polite without condescension.</p>

<p>He engaged them in conversation with practiced ease, a natural talent that drew people in, yet all the while, his eyes continuously returned to the birthday celebrant.</p>

<p>Jennifer, despite herself, seemed aware of his quiet attention.</p>

<p>She maintained her composure, smiling at conversations around her and laughing politely, yet Ross could sense a subtle shift in her posture whenever his gaze found hers.</p>

<p>There was a spark of awareness there—an unspoken acknowledgment of the tension, the curiosity, and perhaps even the intrigue that had silently begun to thread itself between them.</p>

<p>Nina, seemingly oblivious to the silent game unfolding, chatted happily with friends, leaving Ross and Jennifer in their own unspoken contest of glances and measured words.</p>

<p>He noted every small detail: the way her fingers lingered on the edge of her glass, the slight arch of her brow, the way her voice carried warmth but with a hint of steel beneath it.</p>

<p>She was not easily impressed, nor was she naïve, and Ross found that challenge irresistibly appealing.</p>

<p>And though the rest of the party continued in its usual rhythm—laughter, music, polite exchanges—Ross felt a subtle thrill in the air, a quiet tension that hinted at possibilities beyond the formalities.</p>

<p>Behind Jennifer’s polished exterior, he could sense layers waiting to be explored, mysteries that teased and beckoned him.</p>

<p>By the time the evening reached its peak, Ross had mingled with most of the guests, offering smiles, handshakes, and brief conversations.</p>

<p>Yet his focus never wavered.</p>

<p>The birthday celebrant, poised and elegant, remained the center of his attention, a magnetic presence that drew his eyes again and again.</p>

<p>In the end, the birthday celebration had been a resounding success.</p>

<p>The grand hall gradually emptied as guests bid their farewells one by one, offering polite thanks, compliments, and handshakes.</p>

<p>The music softened, the chatter faded, and the lingering scent of flowers and fine cuisine filled the quieted space.</p>

<p>Ross remained at Nina’s side, a silent anchor amid the aftermath of the evening.</p>

<p>They would be staying at the mansion tonight, and the thought of uninterrupted time together brought a subtle smirk to his lips.</p>

<p>&quot;I hear you’ve been investing in mining recently, Ross,&quot; Nina’s father began, his tone curious yet deliberate, as if weighing each word.</p>

<p>&quot;How much did you put into capital in that area?&quot; His eyes gleamed with the hunger of a businessman always on the lookout for opportunity, eager to test Ross’s acumen.</p>

<p>Ross answered calmly and confidently, detailing his investments, strategies, and future plans.</p>

<p>Nina’s father nodded approvingly, occasionally asking for clarification, while Ross replied with ease, seamlessly commanding the conversation without arrogance.</p>

<p>For a while, it seemed as if the two men were locked in a private world of business talk, until a familiar voice gently cut through their discussion.</p>

<p>&quot;You should sleep, dear. And don’t forget to take your medication beforehand,&quot; Jennifer said softly, though her tone carried an unmistakable note of authority and concern.</p>

<p>&quot;Sure. I’ll see you in the morning, Ross—if you’re still here by then,&quot; Nina’s father added, offering a small, tired smile as he limped away.</p>

<p>Every movement betrayed that he was far from fully healthy, each step a careful effort, reminding everyone of the fragility beneath his otherwise commanding presence.</p>

<p>With the room finally quiet, only Jennifer and Ross remained.</p>

<p>The bustle of the party had faded into a hushed intimacy.</p>

<p>Nina had already gone ahead to freshen up, leaving the space between them charged with an unspoken tension.</p>

<p>The soft glow of the chandeliers reflected off polished surfaces, casting warm shadows that seemed to linger on Jennifer’s graceful form as she moved.</p>

<p>&quot;Can you come with me for a minute, Ross?&quot; she asked, her voice even, measured, but carrying a subtle undertone that hinted at more than just a simple request.</p>

<p>&quot;Of course. After you, Jennifer,&quot; Ross replied smoothly, his eyes following her every movement.</p>

<p>There was an almost imperceptible tension in the air, a silent acknowledgment of curiosity and the promise of something unspoken.</p>

<p>As they walked together down a quieter hallway, Ross felt the night shift in its own way.</p>

<p>The grandeur of the mansion faded behind them, replaced by a more intimate, almost private atmosphere.</p>

<p>His thoughts were alert, anticipatory, noting the soft sway of her dress, the calm but deliberate cadence of her steps, and the way her presence seemed to command attention without trying.</p>

<p>Jennifer paused before opening a door, glancing back at him with a faint smile that didn’t quite reach her eyes.</p>

<p>&quot;I wanted to speak with you alone,&quot; she said, her voice lower, more deliberate, carrying an undercurrent that piqued his interest.</p>

<p>Ross stepped closer, matching her measured pace. There was an energy here—charged, subtle, and dangerous in its allure.</p>

<p>Tonight would be different, he realized, the air between them heavy with expectation.</p>

<p>And as he followed her into the room, he felt a thrill of anticipation surge through him.</p>

<p>Not for the party, not for the celebration, but for this—an encounter that promised intrigue, challenge, and something far more compelling than simple conversation.</p>

<p>They walked into one of the mansion’s private rooms, secluded from the rest of the household.</p>

<p>The heavy doors closed behind them with a soft but decisive thud, cutting off the distant sounds of laughter and clinking glasses.</p>

<p>Jennifer turned to face Ross, her expression unyielding, and for a moment, the air between them felt thick with unspoken words.</p>

<p>&quot;I don’t like you for my daughter, Ross,&quot; she said, her voice calm but edged with steel.</p>

<p>&quot;And I most certainly don’t want you to marry her, if that’s what you were thinking.&quot; She held his gaze without flinching, letting the weight of her conviction sink in.</p>

<p>&quot;Nina would only end up miserable by your side. I know your type—so many wives, so many distractions. Eventually, she wouldn’t even matter to you. She’d be alone in her own marriage, and I refuse to let that happen. That’s why I want you to end your relationship with her.&quot;</p>
