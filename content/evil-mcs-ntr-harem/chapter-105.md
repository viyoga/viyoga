<p>Julian and April, of course, had no choice but to comply. The weight of the situation pressed heavily on them, and as they were transferred to another car, they exchanged nervous glances.</p>

<p>Another man from the Demon Mask group took the wheel of the new vehicle, and the other car followed closely behind them, its headlights cutting through the dark.</p>

<p>The journey was eerily silent, the only sound being the hum of the engine and the occasional squeak of tires on the road.</p>

<p>But Julian was the most nervous of them all. His mind raced, his heart pounding in his chest as he tried to make sense of what was happening.</p>

<p>The quiet made everything feel worse, as if the stillness in the car was a warning of something terrible to come.</p>

<p>&quot;Where are you taking us?&quot; Julian asked, his voice shaky but desperate for answers. He turned toward the man in the front seat, hoping for any sign of mercy or explanation.</p>

<p>The man glanced at him through the rearview mirror but said nothing. Julian’s mind scrambled, trying to think of something that would change their fate. He clutched at any semblance of hope he could muster.</p>

<p>&quot;My family is rich! Just call this number, and I’m sure they’ll give you anything you want! Money! Anything!&quot; Julian’s voice cracked as he desperately offered everything he could think of.</p>

<p>&quot;Please, just let us go. You don’t need to do this. I’ll pay. We can work something out!&quot;</p>

<p>His words fell flat, echoing into the silent car. The man didn’t even look back. The indifference was chilling. Julian’s heart sank deeper.</p>

<p>He could feel the cold grip of fear tightening around his chest, and his gut churned with a terrible certainty.</p>

<p>He wasn’t a fool. He had a great intuition, an uncanny sense of danger, and everything in him told him this was far worse than anything he could have imagined.</p>

<p>His mind tried to rationalize, to convince himself it wasn’t what he feared, but deep down, he knew he was right. He didn’t like the direction this was going.</p>

<p>His thoughts raced, but his mouth was dry, unable to form the words that might change their fate.</p>

<p>He tried again, his voice more forceful this time, hoping to break through the wall of silence that surrounded them. &quot;Please, I’m begging you, we didn’t do anything. Just let us go.&quot;</p>

<p>With a sinking feeling, Julian finally fell silent, the weight of the situation pressing down on him like a suffocating force. His eyes drifted to April, her face pale with fear, her hands trembling in her lap.</p>

<p>She met his gaze, her eyes wide with terror. He reached over, taking her hand in his, squeezing it tightly.</p>

<p>For a moment, neither of them spoke. The only sound in the car was the faint hum of the tires against the asphalt. Julian wasn’t sure if he was trying to reassure April or himself.</p>

<p>The silence between them felt suffocating, but he held on to her hand as if it was the only thing keeping him grounded.</p>

<p>In the end, all Julian could do was hold on, not knowing what was coming, not knowing if he could protect them from whatever fate awaited them in the dark unknown.</p>

<p>One hour later, the oppressive silence of the night was broken by the unsettling sounds of a farm coming into view.</p>

<p>The distant clucking of chickens, the low grunts of pigs, and the occasional bleat of sheep echoed through the air.</p>

<p>To Julian and April, the sounds were more like an omen, deepening the unease that had been growing inside them.</p>

<p>They had no idea where they were, and the further they traveled from the main roads, the more isolated this place seemed.</p>

<p>It felt as though they were being led to the edge of the earth, where only darkness awaited. If anyone knew about this place, it might as well have been a secret from the world itself.</p>

<p>This was, of course, none other than James Sullivan’s hideout and farm, a place they had never heard whispered about. Not even in rumors.</p>

<p>A place that seemed too far removed from civilization, as if it was designed to disappear from view, to hide in plain sight.</p>

<p>The car’s engine hummed as they slowed to a stop, and the masked men moved swiftly, their movements sharp and calculated.</p>

<p>Julian’s heart pounded in his chest, his mind racing with the fear of what awaited them.</p>

<p>&quot;Come out. My boss is waiting inside,&quot; Brandon said, his voice hard, with no room for argument. There was an edge to his tone, one that made it clear he was not someone who enjoyed talking.</p>

<p>His words were simple and cold, and they carried an authority that made it clear this wasn’t the first time he’d given such an order.</p>

<p>Reluctantly, Julian and April were forced out of the car, the cold night air hitting them with a shock as they were escorted by gunpoint.</p>

<p>Brandon led the way, his every step purposeful, while the rest of the masked men followed silently behind them.</p>

<p>There was no attempt to explain, no attempt to comfort or reassure them—only the chilling sounds of their footsteps in the night and the muffled whispers of the men around them.</p>

<p>The walk seemed to stretch on endlessly, but in truth, it was barely two minutes before they arrived at a massive building. The lavishness of the room they entered stunned them both.</p>

<p>The walls were decorated with rich, dark wood, and the heavy furniture looked expensive, as if someone had spared no expense in making this place both intimidating and comfortable.</p>

<p>A California king-sized bed sat at one side, its sheets impeccably smooth, almost as if it had never been used. The contrast between the homely farm outside and the wealth inside was jarring.</p>
