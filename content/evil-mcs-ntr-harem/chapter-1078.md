<p>The three sisters sat in silence for a long moment — the kind of silence that said more than words ever could.</p>

<p>Finally, Chelsea stood up.</p>

<p>&quot;Just promise me one thing,&quot; she said. &quot;Don’t let this destroy you. Or Marco. Or us.&quot;</p>

<p>Karen nodded slowly. &quot;I promise.&quot;</p>

<p>But as her sisters left the room and the door closed behind them, Karen sat on her bed and stared into the wall.</p>

<p>The weight she thought she’d shed came creeping back, heavier than before.</p>

<p>***</p>

<p>Day after day, Karen’s sisters couldn’t stop hovering around her.</p>

<p>Every time she walked into the room, they exchanged knowing glances, barely containing their curiosity.</p>

<p>It was obvious they wanted to know everything about her and Ross. Chelsea and Lea, in particular, were relentless.</p>

<p>&quot;So tell us, Karen,&quot; Chelsea said one morning, leaning across the table with a mischievous grin.</p>

<p>&quot;What exactly happens between you two every night? You’ve been glowing lately.&quot;</p>

<p>Karen’s cheeks flushed instantly.</p>

<p>&quot;There’s nothing to tell,&quot; she said, trying to sound casual, though the faint smile tugging at her lips gave her away.</p>

<p>&quot;Oh, please!&quot; Lea giggled, tossing her hair back. &quot;You’re practically floating when you walk! Don’t even try to hide it.&quot;</p>

<p>Chelsea gasped suddenly, covering her mouth an hour later after they won in digging out the details from Karen.</p>

<p>&quot;Wait—you said he lasted seven hours? Seven? That can’t be real! Is that even possible—or safe and healthy?&quot;</p>

<p>Lea’s eyes widened dramatically. &quot;And you said he’s how big? Fifteen inches? Karen, seriously, how are you even walking straight?&quot;</p>

<p>Karen’s face turned bright red as her sisters burst out laughing.</p>

<p>She tried to swat at them, but the more embarrassed she became, the more they teased her.</p>

<p>&quot;Okay, okay! That’s enough!&quot; she finally said, though she couldn’t help but laugh too.</p>

<p>But of course, that wasn’t enough for her sisters.</p>

<p>They kept firing off questions—about his touch, his stamina, how he made her feel.</p>

<p>They giggled and whispered like schoolgirls sharing secrets, completely absorbed in their own amusement.</p>

<p>It was different back when Marco was in the picture.</p>

<p>Her sisters never bothered to ask about him because they knew nothing serious had ever happened between the two.</p>

<p>Marco had not fucked their sister Karen.</p>

<p>But Ross—Ross was a different story altogether. He had taken all of Karen’s bases and more.</p>

<p>The home runs that he did were countless and explosive indeed.</p>

<p>By the end of the evening, Karen was exhausted from all the teasing, but her sisters were delighted.</p>

<p>They had gotten every juicy detail they wanted—or at least, enough to keep them gossiping for days.</p>

<p>As Karen finally retreated to her room, she could still hear them giggling in the living room, whispering Ross’s name with playful disbelief.</p>

<p>And despite her embarrassment, she couldn’t help smiling to herself.</p>

<p>And with such a big secret, it was only a matter of time before a few close people in Karen’s life found out.</p>

<p>No secret ever stayed buried for long—especially not something as shocking as this.</p>

<p>&quot;Is it true? You and Ross?&quot; a beautiful woman asked one morning, her voice soft but laced with disbelief.</p>

<p>Karen looked up from the cup of coffee she was stirring absentmindedly.</p>

<p>Her heart skipped for a beat. &quot;Where did you hear that, Eliza?&quot; she asked, trying to sound calm, though her hand trembled slightly as she set the spoon down.</p>

<p>Eliza didn’t respond right away.</p>

<p>She crossed her arms and studied Karen closely, her sharp eyes reading every flicker of emotion on her face.</p>

<p>&quot;Yes,&quot; Karen finally said after a long silence. Her voice came out quieter than she intended. &quot;It’s true. And it’s not... it’s not what you think.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;Not what I think?&quot; Eliza raised a brow. &quot;Karen, you already have a fiancée and now this?&quot;</p>

<p>Karen sighed and leaned back against her chair, her gaze drifting toward the window.</p>

<p>&quot;It’s not like I’m planning to return to singing anytime soon. There’s nothing to return to. The zombies had taken over everything. The whole world has gone crazy.&quot; She paused, then gave a faint, humorless smile.</p>

<p>Eliza’s expression softened for a moment, but concern quickly took over again.</p>

<p>&quot;I understand, Karen. I really do. But Ross? He’s not exactly the kind of man I imagined you ending up with.&quot;</p>

<p>Karen’s lips tightened. &quot;I know. I can’t explain it. It just happened,&quot; she replied quietly.</p>

<p>Karen could of course not say that she got bored with Marco and experimented with Ross which led her to spreading her legs for him in the end.</p>

<p>Eliza sighed, lowering her voice.</p>

<p>&quot;Marco was okay, but Ross...&quot; She hesitated, searching for the right words.</p>

<p>&quot;He’s... unpredictable. Dangerous, even. You’re walking into something you might not be able to walk away from, Karen. I’m worried for you.&quot;</p>

<p>Karen’s fingers tightened around her cup, and for a moment, she didn’t answer.</p>

<p>The warmth from the drink seeped into her hands, grounding her in the midst of her swirling thoughts.</p>

<p>&quot;Maybe,&quot; she murmured finally. &quot;But Ross makes me feel alive again. After everything I’ve been through... maybe that’s all that matters.&quot;</p>

<p>Eliza looked at her friend for a long time, seeing both strength and fragility in her expression.</p>

<p>&quot;Just promise me you’ll be careful,&quot; she said softly.</p>

<p>Karen met her gaze and nodded slowly. &quot;I will. I promise.&quot;</p>

<p>But deep inside, even she wasn’t sure if that was a promise she could keep.</p>

<p>***</p>

<p>Last but not least, Karen’s mother had also heard the rumors—but unlike others, she chose not to confront her daughter about them.</p>

<p>She trusted Karen’s judgment and believed that, in the end, her daughter had the right to make her own choices.</p>

<p>It was her life, after all.</p>

<p>So, she pretended not to know anything.</p>

<p>She went about her days as usual—or at least as normal as life could be in this strange new world they now lived in.</p>

<p>The days had a quiet, uneasy rhythm, filled with routines that were meant to keep everyone sane.</p>

<p>One afternoon, she decided to visit the library.</p>
