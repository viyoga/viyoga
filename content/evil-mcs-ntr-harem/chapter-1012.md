<p>&quot;What do you want?&quot; Karen asked, her voice trembling with both anger and fear.</p>

<p>&quot;Oh, you know what I want, Karen,&quot; Ross replied smoothly, his tone maddeningly calm, confident, and cruelly patient.</p>

<p>&quot;You’ve always known. You even craved it. But tonight, I’d like to make it official.&quot;</p>

<p>His voice dropped lower, almost intimate. &quot;You’ll be mine. And in exchange... I’ll help you.&quot;</p>

<p>Karen froze. The words sent a chill through her body.</p>

<p>She could hear faint growls echoing outside the hotel corridor, the pounding of heavy footsteps, the world collapsing around her — and yet, Ross’s voice cut through it all like a whisper from the devil himself.</p>

<p>&quot;You’re insane,&quot; she muttered, but her voice cracked halfway through.</p>

<p>&quot;Maybe,&quot; Ross said, a soft chuckle escaping his lips. &quot;But I’m also the only man in this city who can save you. And you know it.&quot;</p>

<p>He let the silence stretch, savoring the moment.</p>

<p>Karen could almost imagine his smirk — that quiet, knowing smile that had once made her blush and now made her stomach twist in dread.</p>

<p>&quot;Make your decision fast, Karen,&quot; Ross continued, his tone suddenly sharper.</p>

<p>&quot;Because I’m not sure how long I can hold them off. You might not survive if you take too long to think.&quot;</p>

<p>Her breath hitched.</p>

<p>Hold them off? Did that mean he knew where she was? Was he watching her even now?</p>

<p>&quot;Ross...&quot; she whispered, panic rising in her throat. &quot;What do you mean?&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;You’ll understand soon,&quot; Ross said, almost gently. &quot;Now — your answer?&quot;</p>

<p>Karen closed her eyes, tears streaming down her face. Every instinct screamed not to trust him.</p>

<p>Ross was dangerous — powerful in ways she couldn’t yet understand.</p>

<p>But outside the barricaded door, the dead roamed. She could hear them — dragging their feet, growling, slamming against other rooms.</p>

<p>And her family... her family might still be out there.</p>

<p>Finally, she spoke, her voice small and broken. &quot;Okay,&quot; she said. &quot;But I have conditions.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;Oh?&quot; Ross said, amused. &quot;Let’s hear them.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;I want my parents safe,&quot; Karen said, her voice trembling. &quot;My sisters too. You’ll protect them, Ross. All of them. That’s my only condition.&quot;</p>

<p>A long pause followed, and then Ross laughed softly. &quot;Fine, Karen. Still selfless to the end.&quot; He exhaled, and his voice turned to silk.</p>

<p>&quot;Very well. You have my word.&quot;</p>

<p>Before Karen could reply, the line went dead.</p>

<p>She lowered her phone slowly, heart pounding in her ears. Eliza stood beside her, pale and trembling. &quot;What did he say?&quot;</p>

<p>Karen didn’t answer. She couldn’t. She didn’t even know what she’d just agreed to.</p>

<p>Then, five minutes later — the world shook.</p>

<p>A deep rumbling noise echoed from outside, followed by the whir of rotor blades.</p>

<p>Eliza rushed to the window and pulled the curtain aside just enough to peek. Her jaw dropped. &quot;Karen... there are helicopters out there!&quot;</p>

<p>The sound grew louder, closer.</p>

<p>From the streets below, bright lights cut through the darkness, sweeping across the hotel’s shattered windows.</p>

<p>Engines roared. Tires screeched.</p>

<p>Then came the pounding on their door — three sharp knocks, followed by a cold, authoritative voice.</p>

<p>&quot;Miss Karen! We’re here under direct orders from Mr. Ross! Stand clear from the door!&quot;</p>

<p>Before either woman could react, the barricade shook, and with a thunderous crash, the door was blown open.</p>

<p>Smoke and dust filled the air as a team of heavily armed men in black combat suits poured into the room.</p>

<p>Their movements were fast, disciplined — almost inhumanly so.</p>

<p>&quot;Clear! Room secure!&quot; one barked, sweeping his rifle across the corners.</p>

<p>Karen shielded her face from the flying debris, coughing as she looked up at the soldiers.</p>

<p>Each one wore night-vision goggles, bulletproof vests, and automatic rifles that gleamed under the emergency lights.</p>

<p>One of them stepped forward, his expression unreadable beneath the visor.</p>

<p>&quot;Miss Karen?&quot; he asked, his voice filtered through the helmet’s comm. &quot;We’ve been sent to extract you and your companion. You’re safe now.&quot;</p>

<p>Eliza let out a shaky breath and broke down crying. &quot;Oh thank God... thank you! Thank you so much!&quot;</p>

<p>Two soldiers gently lifted her to her feet, while another offered Karen a hand.</p>

<p>She took it numbly, still too stunned to speak.</p>

<p>The soldiers led them out of the room, their boots echoing down the dimly lit corridor.</p>

<p>Bodies littered the floor — both zombies and humans.</p>

<p>The air was heavy with smoke, blood, and gunpowder.</p>

<p>As they reached the stairwell, Karen glanced over her shoulder at the shattered hotel room — her last piece of normal life, gone.</p>

<p>She didn’t know what waited for her outside.</p>

<p>But one thing was certain.</p>

<p>Ross had kept his promise.</p>

<p>And now, she belonged to him.</p>

<p>***</p>

<p>They traveled by helicopter, the wind whipping through the open chopper as the city disappeared beneath them.</p>

<p>Below, the landscape gradually changed—buildings gave way to barren fields, and the familiar signs of civilization vanished.</p>

<p>The world became silent, empty, and strangely still, as if this place had been erased from maps long ago.</p>

<p>After a tense flight, the helicopter descended onto a secluded clearing.</p>

<p>The terrain was rugged, almost desolate, and at first glance, it seemed abandoned.</p>

<p>But Karen knew better. This was the location Ross had chosen for his secret bunker—their sanctuary, their prison, and the center of his control.</p>

<p>The doors of the helicopter opened, and the cold wind rushed in, carrying with it a faint metallic scent.</p>

<p>Karen stepped out carefully, her eyes scanning the area, nerves taut.</p>

<p>The bunker loomed ahead—part underground, part fortified structure built into the landscape, almost blending into the barren earth around it.</p>

<p>An hour later, Karen’s heart leapt when she saw her family arrive at the bunker as well.</p>

<p>&quot;Mom! Dad! Chelsea! Lea!&quot;</p>

<p>Karen’s voice cracked as she stumbled forward, tears welling in her eyes.</p>

<p>She saw her family—alive, safe, and waiting for her inside the underground bunker.</p>

<p>In that instant, the fear and exhaustion that had been gnawing at her finally broke, and she ran straight into their arms.</p>

<p>They embraced tightly, crying, trembling, unable to speak for several long seconds.</p>
