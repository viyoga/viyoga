<p>&quot;Shhhh... don’t make a sound.&quot;</p>

<p>The woman in the sleek business skirt — Karen’s manager, Eliza — pressed her finger to her lips.</p>

<p>Her lipstick had smudged, her once-perfect hair was loose and messy, but her eyes were sharp with fear.</p>

<p>Karen’s heart thudded violently against her chest.</p>

<p>The room around them was dim, the curtains drawn tight.</p>

<p>Only the flickering light from the chaos outside gave the two women a fragile illusion of safety.</p>

<p>They had been trapped in the hotel room for over an hour now.</p>

<p>What was supposed to be a calm meeting about their upcoming shows had turned into a nightmare neither could comprehend.</p>

<p>Karen had come here hoping to talk — to focus on work, to get her mind off Ross and the confusing feelings he had stirred inside her — but now, none of that mattered.</p>

<p>Outside, the world was ending.</p>

<p>The chaos had started so suddenly it didn’t even feel real.</p>

<p>One moment, they were sipping coffee and talking about song arrangements; the next, the lobby had erupted into screams.</p>

<p>Guests ran for their lives as staff and strangers alike turned into rabid, bloodthirsty monsters.</p>

<p>All it took was one bite, one drop of infected blood — and they changed.</p>

<p>Their eyes glazed over, their bodies twitched, and then they lunged for the nearest person like wild animals.</p>

<p>Karen had seen this a thousand times before — in movies, in television shows.</p>

<p>But seeing it unfold in front of her eyes was different. Horrifyingly different.</p>

<p>&quot;I need to call my mom and dad,&quot; Karen whispered, her voice trembling. &quot;My sisters too—I need to make sure they’re okay.&quot;</p>

<p>Her shaking fingers hovered over her phone screen, tears welling up in her eyes.</p>

<p>She could barely breathe as the realization struck her — she might never see them again.</p>

<p>Eliza reached out quickly and snatched the phone from her hands.</p>

<p>&quot;No! If you make a sound, they’ll hear us.&quot; Her whisper was harsh but desperate.</p>

<p>&quot;Do you hear that? They’re right outside!&quot;</p>

<p>Karen froze. And then she heard it — a dragging noise, like heavy feet scraping across carpet.</p>

<p>A low, guttural moan that seemed to echo from somewhere just down the hall.</p>

<p>&quot;God...&quot; Karen whimpered, her lips trembling. &quot;Eliza, they’re getting closer.&quot;</p>

<p>The older woman moved silently toward the door and pressed her ear against it.</p>

<p>The sound grew louder — groaning, scratching, the wet squelch of something being dragged.</p>

<p>The smell of smoke and blood hung thick in the air, seeping in through the vents.</p>

<p>&quot;We’re not going out there,&quot; Eliza said firmly. &quot;We wait until it’s quiet.&quot;</p>

<p>Karen sank to the floor, her hands shaking uncontrollably.</p>

<p>&quot;Quiet? You think it’s ever going to be quiet again?&quot; Her voice cracked, tears spilling freely now.</p>

<p>&quot;I should’ve stayed home... maybe my family—maybe they—&quot;</p>

<p>Eliza crouched beside her, her voice softening. &quot;Listen to me, Karen. You can’t think like that. You’re strong, okay? You’ve always been strong.&quot; She placed a trembling hand on Karen’s shoulder. &quot;We’ll get out of this. I promise.&quot;</p>

<p>For a moment, their eyes met — fear against determination — and it was enough to calm Karen’s sobbing, even if just barely.</p>

<p>Then came another sound.</p>

<p>Thud.</p>

<p>The door shuddered. Both women froze.</p>

<p>Thud. Thud. Thud.</p>

<p>Something — or someone — was pounding on it from the other side.</p>

<p>Eliza grabbed the nearest object she could find — a metal lamp — and motioned for Karen to stay back.</p>

<p>The pounding grew louder, harder, until the doorframe began to crack.</p>

<p>&quot;Stay behind me!&quot; Eliza hissed.</p>

<p>Karen pressed herself against the wall, heart hammering so hard it hurt.</p>

<p>She could see the shadow under the door — a pair of feet shuffling, dragging.</p>

<p>A low, animal growl filled the room.</p>

<p>Then, suddenly—</p>

<p>Bang!</p>

<p>The door shuddered violently, nearly bursting open as splinters of wood flew through the air.</p>

<p>Karen screamed, stumbling backward, her heart slamming against her ribs.</p>

<p>The pounding outside grew louder — something heavy was slamming against the door again and again, snarling and scraping like an animal possessed.</p>

<p>Eliza’s eyes widened in terror before a flash of instinct took over.</p>

<p>She knew that the metal lamp she held would be ineffective against that thing.</p>

<p>&quot;Move the cabinet! Block the door—quick, help me!&quot; she hissed.</p>

<p>They both lunged into motion.</p>

<p>Adrenaline surged through their veins as they grabbed anything they could — chairs, the nightstand, even the small dresser near the bed.</p>

<p>Their hands slipped on the polished wood, but they didn’t stop.</p>

<p>The humanoid creature outside roared, slamming one last time before the barricade finally held.</p>

<p>The impact rattled the cabinet, but the door didn’t give.</p>

<p>The sound of snarling lingered for a few tense seconds... then faded.</p>

<p>Heavy footsteps shuffled away, dragging slowly down the hall until silence returned.</p>

<p>Eliza fell against the wall, panting, her chest heaving with exhaustion.</p>

<p>&quot;Oh my God... it’s gone,&quot; she whispered, voice trembling.</p>

<p>Karen nodded shakily, tears spilling from her eyes. &quot;We... we actually survived that.&quot;</p>

<p>Without thinking, the two clung to each other in desperate relief, their bodies trembling.</p>

<p>The room still smelled of fear — sweat, dust, and faint traces of blood from the hallway.</p>

<p>It was only then, in the quiet aftermath, that Karen’s mind began to clear.</p>

<p>And like a spark in the dark, she suddenly remembered something — Ross.</p>

<p>Ross had known.</p>

<p>He’d said something earlier that didn’t make sense at the time — about being safe, about preparing for what was coming.</p>

<p>The memory hit her like ice water.</p>

<p>&quot;Eliza,&quot; Karen said breathlessly, pulling away. &quot;Get me Ross’s number. Now.&quot;</p>

<p>Eliza blinked at her, confused. &quot;What? Karen, what are you—&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;Just give it to me!&quot; Karen snapped, fumbling for her phone. &quot;Please.&quot;</p>

<p>Something in Karen’s tone made Eliza comply without another word.</p>

<p>She searched through her contacts with shaking fingers and began dictating the number.</p>

<p>Karen entered it quickly and pressed the call button.</p>

<p>The line rang once.</p>

<p>Twice.</p>

<p>Then it connected.</p>

<p>&quot;Ross...&quot; Karen gasped, barely able to form the words.</p>

<p>On the other end, his voice came smooth, calm — almost too calm for the chaos that surrounded them.</p>

<p>&quot;Well, hello Karen,&quot; he said, his tone laced with faint amusement. &quot;I knew you’d call.&quot;</p>

<p>Karen’s lips parted, but no sound came out.</p>

<p>&quot;So,&quot; Ross continued, his voice dropping to a soft, knowing murmur, &quot;let’s speak our terms.&quot;</p>
