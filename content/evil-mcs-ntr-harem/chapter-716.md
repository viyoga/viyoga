<p>Ross entered the House of Fortune, the heavy doors closing behind him with a quiet thud.</p>

<p>The atmosphere inside buzzed with a strange mix of anticipation and curiosity.</p>

<p>All eyes turned toward him—some intrigued, others cautious—as he stepped onto the glossy marble floor beneath the glittering chandeliers.</p>

<p>Seventeen contestants had already arrived.</p>

<p>He paused, scanning the room in silence.</p>

<p>Nine men. Eight women.</p>

<p>One by one, his eyes evaluated them.</p>

<p>The men were loud, brimming with ego and testosterone, each trying to assert dominance in their own way—jokes, laughter, casual bragging.</p>

<p>Some flexed subtly while chatting; others tried to command attention with stories that reeked of exaggeration.</p>

<p>Then came the women.</p>

<p>Three were, frankly, unfortunate in appearance—either due to features that didn’t sit right or a lack of presence altogether.</p>

<p>One had a face so average it was almost impressive in its forgettability.</p>

<p>But the final five—ah, they were different.</p>

<p>They stood out without even trying.</p>

<p>Beauty came easy to them, ranging in age from 18 to 25.</p>

<p>One had a fiery look in her eyes and curves that turned heads wherever she walked.</p>

<p>Another had a quiet elegance, like a porcelain doll brought to life.</p>

<p>A third wore confidence like a second skin, her laugh echoing like a chime.</p>

<p>Ross took in each detail—from the way they carried themselves to the subtle glances they threw at the cameras.</p>

<p>These were women used to attention.</p>

<p>And soon, they’d have his.</p>

<p>A hidden smile formed behind his sleek black mask.</p>

<p>He had plans for these five—but that would come later.</p>

<p>For now, it was time to blend in. Time to play the part.</p>

<p>&quot;Hey, man. How’s it going?&quot; a voice cut through the chatter, friendly and open.</p>

<p>Ross turned to face the speaker.</p>

<p>&quot;I’m Corey,&quot; the guy continued, extending a hand.</p>

<p>&quot;What’s your name? And, uh, what’s with the mask? You some kind of ninja or something?&quot;</p>

<p>Corey looked like the textbook definition of a popular guy.</p>

<p>Tall, clean-cut, and clearly aware of his good looks.</p>

<p>His smile was easy, his confidence effortless. His muscles were toned and well-shaped, hinting at dedication in the gym, but compared to Ross—whose physique was not just built but carved from years of rigorous discipline—he came across as soft around the edges.</p>

<p>Ross accepted the handshake with a firm grip. &quot;Big D,&quot; he said evenly, offering nothing more.</p>

<p>Corey chuckled. &quot;Big D, huh? That supposed to stand for something?&quot;</p>

<p>Ross said nothing. His silence spoke volumes.</p>

<p>Corey raised a brow, half-amused and half-unsettled. &quot;Alright then... mystery man. Welcome to the circus.&quot;</p>

<p>As Corey drifted back toward a cluster of other contestants, Ross took a slow walk around the room.</p>

<p>He noted alliances already forming, rivalries already brewing beneath the surface. People were choosing sides without even realizing it.</p>

<p>That was fine.</p>

<p>Let them talk, laugh, flirt, or fight.</p>

<p>He wasn’t here to play their game.</p>

<p>He was here to rewrite the rules.</p>

<p>***</p>

<p>The day wore on, and Ross remained as distant as ever.</p>

<p>He sat quietly in a corner of the lounge, arms crossed, his masked face unreadable.</p>

<p>He didn’t attempt to join any group conversations.</p>

<p>He didn’t laugh at jokes, offer introductions, or even make eye contact unless necessary.</p>

<p>He was simply... there.</p>

<p>A looming presence that neither approached nor retreated. Just watching. Waiting.</p>

<p>Naturally, the others took notice.</p>

<p>&quot;Look at him,&quot; one of the girls whispered, not even bothering to lower her voice that much. &quot;He hasn’t spoken to a single person all day. I bet he’s the first one to get evicted.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;Same,&quot; another chimed in, rolling her eyes. &quot;It’s like he doesn’t even care. I mean, come on—he’s wearing a mask. Who does that? He looks more like a paid actor or some weird stuntman than a real contestant.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;I don’t know what the producers are thinking,&quot; someone else added with a scoff. &quot;Maybe it’s some twist? Like, ’Guess who the silent weirdo really is.’ Whatever it is, they gave us a clown, not a housemate.&quot;</p>

<p>And yet, despite all their mocking, the group couldn’t seem to stop talking about him.</p>

<p>Ross—Big D—had become the most discussed figure in the house, all without uttering more than two words.</p>

<p>&quot;Big D... what kind of name is that anyway?&quot; one of the girls said with a giggle. &quot;If it means what I think it does, I wouldn’t be surprised. Just look at his build. That man is thick and tall. Like, damn.&quot;</p>

<p>The other girls laughed, pretending to brush it off, but a few of them had already stolen second glances.</p>

<p>He was a mystery wrapped in muscle, and whether they admitted it or not, curiosity was slowly turning into obsession.</p>

<p>Ross, sitting in silence, heard everything. Every whisper. Every insult. Every joke at his expense.</p>

<p>And he smiled behind the mask.</p>

<p>***</p>

<p>Lunch hour arrived, and the atmosphere in the house shifted into one of forced cheerfulness.</p>

<p>With cameras rolling from every corner, the contestants sprang into action, each eager to show off their best side.</p>

<p>Smiles were wide, voices overly friendly, and laughter came just a little too easily.</p>

<p>They chopped vegetables, stirred pots, set the table—everyone pitching in to look like a harmonious, cooperative group.</p>

<p>Everyone... except Ross.</p>

<p>He hadn’t moved an inch from his spot.</p>

<p>While the others bustled about the kitchen, trading banter and pretending to bond, Ross sat alone like a silent sentinel.</p>

<p>Arms folded, legs stretched out, mask hiding every flicker of expression.</p>

<p>He didn’t lift a finger. Didn’t offer help. Didn’t even glance in their direction.</p>

<p>Some shot him annoyed glances. Others ignored him completely.</p>

<p>&quot;He really thinks he’s above this?&quot; one muttered under their breath while dicing onions.</p>

<p>&quot;Let him rot in the corner,&quot; another whispered. &quot;He won’t last.&quot;</p>

<p>Eventually, lunch was ready. The aroma of grilled meats, stir-fried vegetables, and steaming rice filled the house.</p>

<p>The contestants gathered around the long dining table, plates in hand, still smiling for the ever-present cameras.</p>

<p>Just as someone reached for a serving spoon, a sharp, clear chime echoed through the house.</p>

<p>Ding!</p>

<p>Everyone froze. Heads turned toward the overhead speakers, anticipation flickering across their faces.</p>

<p>Even Ross shifted slightly in his seat, one eyebrow lifting behind the mask.</p>

<p>Something was about to happen.</p>
