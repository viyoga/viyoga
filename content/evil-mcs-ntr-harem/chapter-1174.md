<p>&quot;I know,&quot; the woman said quietly, her jaw tightening as a shiver ran down her spine. &quot;Let’s move. That face... it’s... it creeps me out.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;On your signal,&quot; the man replied, his eyes narrowing.</p>

<p>Around them, energy crackled faintly, subtle but ready, as though both of them were holding back the storm that simmered just beneath the surface.</p>

<p>The woman inhaled deeply.</p>

<p>Her fingers brushed against the hilt of a small blade at her side, feeling the pulse of power that flowed through her armor and into her very body.</p>

<p>Then, in one smooth, lightning-fast motion:</p>

<p>Bang!</p>

<p>The ground beneath her feet seemed to vanish as she moved.</p>

<p>Her form blurred, vanishing from the man’s side in an instant.</p>

<p>In the blink of an eye, she reappeared far to the side, landing with a barely audible whisper on a fractured rooftop.</p>

<p>Almost immediately, dozens of palm-sized, crimson energy spheres materialized around her.</p>

<p>They spun in perfect synchrony, each one glowing with raw destructive power, humming like furious bees, their light reflecting off shattered glass and ruined concrete.</p>

<p>She raised her hands, directing them with precise gestures, her every movement a dance of deadly intent.</p>

<p>The spheres shot forward with terrifying speed, streaking through the air like a barrage of scarlet comets aimed at the hovering child above.</p>

<p>The wind from their motion shredded the air, whipping dust, debris, and papers from the ruined streets below into chaotic spirals.</p>

<p>The man watched, calculating, ready to follow, his own aura rising.</p>

<p>He could feel the air distort as her attack cut through it, the immense energy warping the space around her.</p>

<p>Every instinct screamed danger, yet every movement she made was flawless, precise, and lethal.</p>

<p>The child above tilted her head, as though curious, almost amused, at the incoming attack.</p>

<p>Her small form shimmered faintly with a strange aura, one that radiated both innocence and lethal intent.</p>

<p>The contrast was disorienting—how could such a young body project such palpable menace?</p>

<p>The hunters knew this fight would not be simple.</p>

<p>The city around them had already been reduced to ruin, but now the sky itself was the battlefield.</p>

<p>Every discarded building, every cracked street, and every shattered vehicle was a potential obstacle, a weapon, or a trap.</p>

<p>The woman’s energy spheres closed in.</p>

<p>The child’s smile widened, just slightly, and a faint red shimmer began to radiate outward from her tiny hands.</p>

<p>The air thickened. Time seemed to stretch.</p>

<p>Boom!</p>

<p>The explosions tore through the air before the energy spheres could reach the child.</p>

<p>The concussive force rattled nearby buildings, sending shattered glass and chunks of concrete raining down into the streets.</p>

<p>Dust and debris swirled in thick, choking clouds, momentarily obscuring the battlefield.</p>

<p>&quot;New food! I’m hungry!&quot; the child shouted, her voice unnaturally loud, echoing in every direction like a distorted chime of madness.</p>

<p>It was a sound both innocent and horrifying, a child’s tone twisted by a malevolent intelligence far beyond her years.</p>

<p>Even seasoned hunters felt a chill crawl down their spines.</p>

<p>The child remained completely unscathed by the onslaught of energy spheres, though the woman and her companion had anticipated this.</p>

<p>After all, their mission was not to kill, but to capture.</p>

<p>Every movement, every strike, every explosion had been carefully measured—not to destroy, but to manipulate and control.</p>

<p>Even the woman’s initial attack had been deliberate in its restraint.</p>

<p>The barrage of crimson spheres, the blinding explosions, the precise maneuvers—they had all been designed to herd the target, to test it, and to draw it into a trap.</p>

<p>The goal was not devastation, but control; not death, but leverage.</p>

<p>Bang!</p>

<p>Before anyone could react, the child shot downward.</p>

<p>Her body moved impossibly fast, descending like a streak of red and white light.</p>

<p>She arrived within meters of Bella in an instant.</p>

<p>Then, almost mockingly, she froze midair, her tiny form hovering silently, almost taunting her opponents with the impossible control of her movement.</p>

<p>&quot;Oh? Someone’s out here too? Hihihi,&quot; the child giggled, her laugh light and playful, yet it carried an undercurrent of pure malice.</p>

<p>&quot;More food, more fun,&quot; she added, twisting her head in a motion so unnatural that it defied all human anatomy.</p>

<p>Her eyes locked onto the man who had attempted to stop her, turning fully backward to stare directly at him while her small body remained suspended in midair.</p>

<p>Bella’s heart skipped a beat, though her face remained composed.</p>

<p>This was no ordinary child and no ordinary zombie.</p>

<p>This child was far worse—an ascended variant of the zombies, evolved beyond reason.</p>

<p>Every instinct screamed danger.</p>

<p>&quot;You... want to hold me, boy? Dream on!&quot; the child spat, her voice dripping with cruelty far older than her innocent appearance suggested.</p>

<p>The man staggered backward, clutching his skull as a blinding, stabbing pain ripped through him.</p>

<p>His knees buckled, and he spat blood onto the cracked pavement.</p>

<p>His eyes streamed crimson, and a wave of nausea washed over him.</p>

<p>It felt as though his very skull might shatter under the psychic assault of the child’s gaze.</p>

<p>&quot;Bella! RUN!&quot; he shouted, desperation making his voice crack.</p>

<p>Bella’s lips pressed into a thin line. Panic did not touch her.</p>

<p>Years of training, countless battles, and unflinching discipline kicked in.</p>

<p>Her mind became a razor-sharp focus.</p>

<p>She assessed the child’s position, her careless stance, the aura of her power, and every possible angle of attack.</p>

<p>Whoosh!</p>

<p>Countless red energy spheres erupted around Bella, each the size of a palm, spinning and glowing with deadly intensity.</p>

<p>She gestured sharply, directing them with precision.</p>

<p>They flew like a swarm of meteors toward the hovering child.</p>

<p>Boom!</p>

<p>The spheres collided with the ascended child in a storm of light and explosions.</p>

<p>The shockwave tore through nearby streets, sending debris tumbling into craters in the asphalt.</p>

<p>Dust, smoke, and energy flares filled the air, making the world seem almost unreal.</p>

<p>Yet the child remained suspended, completely unscathed, tilting her head with a hint of amusement.</p>

<p>Her laughter, light and musical, cut through the chaos:</p>

<p>&quot;You think it’s that easy? Hihihi...&quot;</p>
