<p>&quot;Don&#x27;t speak her name!&quot; Kevin growled, his voice a low, dangerous rumble.</p>

<p>&quot;Do that again, and I&#x27;ll smash your teeth down your throat!&quot; He stepped closer to Ross, his fists clenched and his eyes blazing with fury.</p>

<p>At six foot one, Kevin had the advantage of height, but Ross didn&#x27;t seem fazed. Though shorter, Ross&#x27;s muscular frame exuded raw power and confidence, contrasting Kevin&#x27;s lean, athletic build.</p>

<p>Ross smirked, utterly unfazed by Kevin&#x27;s threat.</p>

<p>&quot;I don&#x27;t just speak nonsense,&quot; he said coolly.</p>

<p>&quot;I can bring Ayla back to you—provided you win, of course.&quot; He flicked his fingers, his tone laced with a sinister certainty.</p>

<p>&quot;What are you talking about? Are you crazy or some—&quot; Kevin&#x27;s words died on his lips as the room was suddenly bathed in a brilliant light.</p>

<p>A circular portal, glowing with a shimmering, ethereal brilliance, materialized in the air. Kevin&#x27;s breath caught in his throat as a stunningly beautiful young woman stepped through.</p>

<p>She was clad in nothing but a simple blue hospital gown, her bare feet soundless against the wooden floor.</p>

<p>Her face was a picture of confusion, her wide, startled eyes scanning her surroundings. But when her gaze landed on Kevin, recognition lit up her features.</p>

<p>Without a moment&#x27;s hesitation, she ran to him, throwing herself into his arms and clutching him tightly.</p>

<p>&quot;Kevin!&quot; she cried, her voice breaking as tears streamed down her face.</p>

<p>&quot;Ayla!&quot; Kevin&#x27;s voice was choked with emotion as he embraced her. He held her as though afraid she might disappear again.</p>

<p>&quot;I missed you! I thought I&#x27;d lost you forever!&quot;</p>

<p>Ayla sobbed against his chest, her hands clutching at his shirt.</p>

<p>&quot;The last thing I remember…&quot; she whispered, her voice trembling, &quot;was driving, and then… something hit my car from behind. After that… nothing. I woke up in this bright light, and then suddenly I was here.&quot;</p>

<p>Kevin pulled back just enough to look into her tear-streaked face. Without thinking, he leaned in and kissed her, pouring every ounce of his relief, longing, and love into the gesture.</p>

<p>Ayla melted into the kiss, her arms wrapping around his neck. When they finally broke apart, both of them were flushed, their faces glowing with a mix of desire and overwhelming emotion.</p>

<p>&quot;What happened to me?&quot; Ayla asked, her voice soft, almost fragile. She seemed oblivious to everyone else in the room or the surreal surroundings she had emerged into. Her focus was entirely on Kevin.</p>

<p>&quot;You were in an accident,&quot; Kevin explained, his voice gentle yet heavy with the weight of the memory.</p>

<p>&quot;A logging truck&#x27;s brakes failed, and it crashed into your car. It was a miracle you survived, Ayla. But… you were declared brain-dead. For almost a year, the doctors urged your parents to pull the plug, but they refused. They believed in you. They never gave up hope.&quot;</p>

<p>Ayla&#x27;s lips parted, her eyes glistening with fresh tears as she absorbed his words. But before she could respond, another voice cut through the emotional reunion, cold and mocking.</p>

<p>&quot;But the same can&#x27;t be said about you, right, Kevin?&quot; Ross interjected, his smirk returning with cruel satisfaction.</p>

<p>&quot;You gave up on your lovely girlfriend, Ayla. It didn&#x27;t take you long to start hitting on my sexy girlfriend, Jessica. And now, here we are.&quot;</p>

<p>The venom in Ross&#x27;s tone snapped Ayla out of her emotional haze. For the first time, she noticed the other man in the room.</p>

<p>Her gaze turned to Ross, and confusion gave way to something sharper—apprehension, perhaps even anger—as the weight of his words sank in.</p>

<p>&quot;I don&#x27;t know what your game is, Ross, or how you did what you did—making Ayla appear here,&quot; Kevin said, his voice trembling with a mix of anger and disbelief. He stepped forward, his fists clenched at his sides.</p>

<p>&quot;But let me make one thing clear. If you mess with Ayla, I won&#x27;t hesitate to kill you if I have to!&quot;</p>

<p>Ross leaned back in his chair, a smug grin spreading across his face.</p>

<p>&quot;Good words,&quot; he said mockingly, his tone dripping with amusement.</p>

<p>&quot;But words are just that—words. Actions, Kevin, are what truly matter.&quot; He snapped his fingers, and with a shimmer of light, a table appeared between them.</p>

<p>On its surface sat an ornate chessboard with intricately carved pieces, their details gleaming in the cabin&#x27;s warm light. Ross stood and took a seat on one side of the table, gesturing toward the other chair.</p>

<p>Jessica, standing nearby, sighed heavily and moved to stand behind him, her expression unreadable.</p>

<p>&quot;What if I say no?&quot; Kevin asked, his gaze darting between Ross, the chessboard, and the door. It was clear he was weighing his options, his mind racing to find a way out of this surreal situation.</p>

<p>Ross&#x27;s grin widened, his eyes glinting with malicious delight.</p>

<p>&quot;Simple,&quot; he said, his tone cold.</p>

<p>&quot;I&#x27;ll take your girlfriend right in front of your eyes. And there&#x27;s nothing you can do about it.&quot;</p>

<p>Before Kevin could respond, Ross raised a hand.</p>

<p>Ding!</p>

<p>Ayla vanished in an instant.</p>

<p>Kevin&#x27;s heart leapt into his throat.</p>

<p>&quot;Where did you take her?! Bring her back!&quot; he shouted, his voice raw with panic.</p>

<p>Ross chuckled softly, as though enjoying Kevin&#x27;s torment.</p>

<p>Ding!</p>
