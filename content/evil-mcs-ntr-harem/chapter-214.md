<p>&quot;Wow! This place is amazing!&quot; one of the girls exclaimed, her voice filled with awe as they gazed down at Tokyo’s glittering skyline from the sky.</p>

<p>The sprawling city stretched endlessly below them, with its iconic skyscrapers and neon lights painting a vivid masterpiece that seemed almost otherworldly.</p>

<p>The sight was so breathtaking that even the chatter in the group quieted, replaced by a collective sense of wonder.</p>

<p>When they landed, the group disembarked from their private aircraft and climbed into their sleek, bus-sized RV.</p>

<p>It was a luxurious vehicle, equipped with everything from plush leather seating to a minibar.</p>

<p>The only person left behind to manage the RV was Freddie, Ross’s ever-reliable puppet servant.</p>

<p>Enjoy new adventures from freewebnovel</p>

<p>As April’s bodyguard and driver, Freddie had become a familiar presence in the group, second only to Ross himself.</p>

<p>He was a quiet but steady figure, always ensuring things ran smoothly, and he had grown used to the whims and eccentricities of Ross’s entourage.</p>

<p>Ross, however, had more than just Freddie at his disposal.</p>

<p>Since Althea entered his life, his security arrangements had reached a new level.</p>

<p>Puppet bodyguards—silent, deadly, and ever-loyal—were a constant shadow for Althea, ensuring that she won’t be bothered while going out in public. Three hundred bodyguards no less.</p>

<p>Yet, in Tokyo, Ross doubted he would need to employ such drastic measures.</p>

<p>This wasn’t the kind of place where Althea’s fanbase usually lurked in the legion, and Althea, always mindful of their visibility, had taken steps to keep a low profile.</p>

<p>She wore oversized sunglasses and a chic wide-brimmed hat, her usual beauty somewhat muted but no less striking.</p>

<p>&quot;So, where to first?&quot; Ross asked, turning to the women as Freddie guided the RV through the bustling Tokyo streets.</p>

<p>With his morning tea in hand, Ross leaned back into his seat, perfectly at ease.</p>

<p>This trip wasn’t about him—it was for his women, and he was content to let them enjoy every moment.</p>

<p>&quot;Food. I’m starving,&quot; Sophia declared without hesitation.</p>

<p>Her voice carried a playful edge, and her confident demeanor was hard to ignore.</p>

<p>Spoiled rotten by Ross, Sophia had grown accustomed to having her every desire fulfilled.</p>

<p>While Ross treated all his women with indulgence, Sophia took pride in being his first woman.</p>

<p>It gave her a sense of superiority, often leading her to act like the queen ant of their little colony—though her self-assured attitude could occasionally rub the others the wrong way.</p>

<p>&quot;Food it is then,&quot; Ross replied with a nod, taking another sip of his coffee.</p>

<p>Forty minutes later, they pulled up in front of the renowned Aragawa restaurant, a name synonymous with culinary perfection.</p>

<p>Inside, the group was treated like royalty.</p>

<p>The staff, awestruck by the stunning women and Ross’s commanding presence, went above and beyond to ensure their experience was unforgettable.</p>

<p>The food lived up to its reputation—succulent Wagyu beef, perfectly paired sides, and rich desserts that melted in their mouths.</p>

<p>Laughter and conversation filled the air as the group relaxed, their bond growing even stronger over the shared meal.</p>

<p>Afterward, their adventure truly began. Ross had planned an itinerary that showcased Tokyo’s most iconic attractions.</p>

<p>They started with the serene beauty of Sensoji Temple, where they marveled at the ancient architecture and soaked in the peaceful atmosphere.</p>

<p>From there, they moved to the Meiji Jingu Shrine, walking through its lush, sacred forest and taking in the contrast between old traditions and Tokyo’s modernity.</p>

<p>At Hamarikyu Gardens, they strolled among beautifully manicured landscapes, the city’s skyscrapers providing a striking backdrop.</p>

<p>Ross, always mindful of time, had arranged a helicopter to whisk them between locations, ensuring they could cover as much ground as possible.</p>

<p>From above, the city looked even more magical, and the women took countless photos to remember the experience.</p>

<p>By evening, exhaustion had begun to set in.</p>

<p>The day had been packed, and even Ross could feel the weariness creeping in.</p>

<p>Still, when someone suggested a night out clubbing, no one objected.</p>

<p>The idea of ending their day with music, dancing, and drinks was too tempting to pass up.</p>

<p>Despite their tiredness, the group rallied.</p>

<p>Dressed to the nines, they made their way to one of Tokyo’s hottest clubs, ready to immerse themselves in the city’s vibrant nightlife.</p>

<p>The energy was electric, and as they stepped inside, all eyes turned to them.</p>

<p>It was the perfect finale to a day that would undoubtedly become one of their most cherished memories.</p>

<p>At the club…</p>

<p>&quot;Damn, look at those girls!&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;Every single one of them is gorgeous!&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;I can’t even decide who’s the most stunning!&quot;</p>

<p>The men in the club—locals and tourists alike—were rendered speechless as Ross walked in, flanked by his harem.</p>

<p>It wasn’t every day that you saw a group like this.</p>

<p>Each woman was a head-turner in her own right, and together, they formed a breathtaking lineup that seemed almost unreal.</p>

<p>What made the scene even more striking was their height.</p>

<p>Most of Ross’s women stood close to six feet tall or over, and with their high heels, they towered over nearly everyone in the room.</p>

<p>Their sleek dresses, perfect makeup, and confident strides only amplified their magnetic allure.</p>

<p>Heads turned, conversations stopped, and all eyes followed them as they glided across the club like queens entering their court.</p>

<p>&quot;Hey, ladies! Want to have some fun? I’m c—&quot; One man, braver than most, stepped forward, flashing what he probably thought was a winning smile.</p>

<p>He positioned himself in front of the group, trying to catch their attention.</p>

<p>He wasn’t prepared for the reply.</p>

<p>&quot;Sorry, we’re all already married,&quot; Jade said smoothly, her lips curling into a knowing smile.</p>

<p>Her voice carried a playful, teasing edge that somehow made the rejection sting even more.</p>

<p>The rest of the women giggled softly, amused by the man’s boldness and his obvious defeat.</p>

<p>The group walked past him without a second glance, leaving behind a faint trail of their perfume—an intoxicating blend of floral and musk that lingered in the air like a siren’s call.</p>

<p>The man stood frozen, unable to do anything but watch as they disappeared into the crowd, their figures swaying with confidence.</p>

<p>&quot;Man…&quot; he muttered under his breath, his throat dry.</p>

<p>His friend tapped him on the shoulder, snapping him out of his daze.</p>

<p>&quot;Forget it, dude. They’re way out of our league,&quot; the friend said, shaking his head.</p>

<p>&quot;Yeah, no kidding,&quot; the man replied with a sigh.</p>

<p>He glanced back at the bar, but the image of Ross’s group had already burned itself into his mind.</p>

<p>&quot;Out of our league? They’re from another planet entirely.&quot; The man said, his voice heavy with disappointment.</p>

<p>Meanwhile, Ross and his women claimed a VIP booth overlooking the dance floor.</p>

<p>Ross leaned back in his seat, his presence effortlessly commanding respect and envy from everyone who saw him.</p>

<p>His women settled in around him, laughing and chatting, their radiant smiles enough to light up the dimly lit club.</p>

<p>The rest of the night carried on, but for those who had seen Ross and his harem, it became the highlight of their evening—if not their entire trip.</p>

<p>An hour past midnight, Ross heard three soft, hesitant knocks at the door.</p>

<p>The sound was almost drowned out by the raucous laughter and off-key singing of his women, who were still doing karaoke at this hour.</p>

<p>But Ross heard it clearly—he had been expecting it all along.</p>

<p>&quot;My Japanese girlfriend has finally arrived,&quot; he murmured to himself, a knowing smile tugging at his lips.</p>

<p>Rising from his seat, he walked to the door, choosing to open it personally.</p>
