<p>Sandra’s expression twitched slightly, clearly displeased that her attempt to deter him had failed.</p>

<p>Diane, on the other hand, beamed.</p>

<p>&quot;Great! We’ll leave Saturday morning. It’s gonna be fun—you’ll get to see the animals, the crops, and oh! We even have a new batch of ducklings. They’re adorable.&quot;</p>

<p>Ross nodded, amused by her enthusiasm.</p>

<p>&quot;Sounds like a good time.&quot;</p>

<p>Amy smiled, resting her chin on her hand as she watched the interaction.</p>

<p>It was rare for Ross to agree to anything so casually, but she liked the idea of him joining them.</p>

<p>A part of her was curious to see how he’d handle life outside his usual luxury.</p>

<p>Her mother, who had been quietly observing, finally spoke up.</p>

<p>&quot;It’ll be nice having you join us, Ross. Just be prepared—farm life isn’t all relaxation. There’s real work involved.&quot;</p>

<p>Ross smirked. &quot;I don’t mind getting my hands dirty.&quot;</p>

<p>Sandra huffed under her breath, stabbing her fork into her food with a little more force than necessary.</p>

<p>Amy couldn’t help but grin. This trip was going to be very interesting.</p>

<p>***</p>

<p>The week flew by, and before they knew it, Saturday morning had arrived—the day of the much-anticipated trip to the family farm.</p>

<p>Ross pulled up to the house in his sleek, midnight-blue sports car, its engine purring softly as he stepped out, dressed impeccably in casual yet stylish attire.</p>

<p>Steven, Amy’s father, eyed the low-slung vehicle with a raised eyebrow.</p>

<p>&quot;Your car won’t handle the road to the farm, Ross. It’s a bumpy ride up there,&quot; he remarked, his voice carrying a note of concern.</p>

<p>Ross smiled, unfazed.</p>

<p>&quot;It’s fine, Steven. I actually grew up on a farm myself,&quot; he replied, rolling up his sleeves. Without hesitation, he joined the family in packing their belongings.</p>

<p>The family had gathered a substantial amount of supplies for the farm—fresh groceries, tools for minor repairs, and personal luggage.</p>

<p>They loaded everything into Steven’s large, rugged truck, its bed groaning under the weight of the cargo.</p>

<p>The morning sun cast a warm glow over the scene as they worked together, laughter and chatter filling the air.</p>

<p>An hour later, with the truck packed to the brim, they were ready to depart.</p>

<p>Steven took the driver’s seat, his wife settled beside him in the passenger seat, Amy and Diane occupied the middle row, while Sandra and Ross squeezed into the back.</p>

<p>The rear seats were cramped, surrounded by bags and supplies, leaving little room to maneuver.</p>

<p>Only their heads were visible above the pile, creating a comical sight.</p>

<p>Ross, however, seemed perfectly at ease.</p>

<p>The confined space and rustic setting didn’t bother him in the slightest; in fact, it brought back fond memories of his own upbringing.</p>

<p>Sandra, on the other hand, was less than pleased.</p>

<p>She mumbled and complained under her breath, her discomfort evident as she shifted in her seat, trying to find a more comfortable position amidst the clutter.</p>

<p>As the truck rumbled to life and pulled away from the city, the scenery began to change.</p>

<p>The tall buildings and bustling streets gave way to open fields and dense forests.</p>

<p>The road narrowed and became rougher, with potholes and uneven terrain causing the truck to jostle and bounce.</p>

<p>The air grew fresher, carrying the earthy scent of nature, and the sounds of urban life were replaced by the chirping of birds and the rustling of leaves.</p>

<p>Despite the bumpy ride, the family remained in high spirits.</p>

<p>They sang along to songs on the radio, shared stories, and teased each other good-naturedly.</p>

<p>Ross joined in, his deep laughter blending with theirs, further endearing him to Amy’s family.</p>

<p>Sandra, however, remained aloof, her discomfort turning into irritation as the journey progressed.</p>

<p>She shot occasional glares at Ross, who seemed oblivious to her displeasure.</p>

<p>Amy noticed her sister’s sour mood and gave her a reassuring smile, hoping to ease her discomfort.</p>

<p>The journey from Parkland City to Stanlow Town was a long seven-hour drive.</p>

<p>The car was packed with luggage, snacks, and the occasional conversation, though much of the trip was spent in comfortable silence.</p>

<p>Along the way, they stopped for lunch, grabbed snacks, and took restroom breaks at gas stations.</p>

<p>By the time they reached the halfway point, the sun was already high up in the sky, casting a bright hot radiance over the landscape.</p>

<p>The rhythmic hum of the car and the gentle rocking motion had lulled most of the passengers to sleep.</p>

<p>Sandra’s sisters, Amy and Diane, were curled up in their seats, breathing softly.</p>

<p>Their mother had also dozed off, her head resting against the window.</p>

<p>Only their father, Steven, remained fully awake, his hands steady on the wheel as he navigated the open road.</p>

<p>Suddenly, Sandra winced, a sharp pain shooting through her leg.</p>

<p>&quot;Ouch!&quot; she gasped, instinctively gripping her thigh.</p>

<p>Ross, seated beside her, turned his head. &quot;What’s wrong?&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;My leg—it’s cramping,&quot; she muttered, trying to stretch it out but failing to relieve the tension.</p>

<p>Ross didn’t hesitate. &quot;Come here. I’ll give you a massage.&quot;</p>

<p>Sandra blinked at him in disbelief. &quot;What?&quot;</p>

<p>Without waiting for an answer, Ross moved quickly, gathering the supplies piled beside Sandra and shifting them to the center.</p>

<p>Then, he patted his lap expectantly.</p>

<p>&quot;You’re insane,&quot; Sandra whispered harshly, her face heating up.</p>

<p>&quot;You don’t actually expect me to sit on your lap, do you?&quot;</p>

<p>Ross raised an eyebrow.</p>

<p>&quot;Suit yourself.&quot; He leaned back and closed his eyes, making no effort to fix the now-displaced supplies.</p>

<p>As a result, his bare arms and legs pressed lightly against Sandra’s, the warmth of his skin making her hyper-aware of their proximity.</p>

<p>Sandra inhaled sharply, trying to ignore it, but the throbbing pain in her leg wasn’t going away.</p>

<p>She shifted uncomfortably, biting her lip.</p>

<p>&quot;Ugh…&quot; She clenched her fists, debating whether her pride was worth the discomfort.</p>

<p>After another few seconds, she exhaled in defeat.</p>

<p>&quot;Fine,&quot; she grumbled. &quot;But if you try anything, I swear I’ll kill you.&quot;</p>

<p>Ross smirked but said nothing as Sandra hesitantly moved onto his lap, her movements stiff and awkward.</p>
