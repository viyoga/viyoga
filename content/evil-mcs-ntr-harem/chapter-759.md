<p>Tianna was ravished by Ross for hours, lost in wave after wave of relentless passion.</p>

<p>As usual, he only stopped when the first rays of sunlight began to creep through the windows.</p>

<p>Their bodies were tangled together, slick with sweat, flushed and breathless.</p>

<p>The air still held the scent of sex and skin, heavy and warm.</p>

<p>Heaven, already spent from her epic lovemaking with Ross earlier, lay curled up beside them, equally exhausted.</p>

<p>The three of them collapsed into a peaceful slumber, their bodies wrapped in satisfied stillness.</p>

<p>If pleasure had a sound, it was the silence that followed—the calm after a storm of moans, gasps, and whispered names.</p>

<p>But elsewhere in the house, the mood was vastly different.</p>

<p>Not everyone slept well that night.</p>

<p>In the quiet of the communal bathroom, Hugh stood frozen in front of the mirror, staring into his own eyes as though searching for something—answers, maybe, or just some version of himself he still recognized.</p>

<p>He didn’t know how long he’d been there. Time had blurred into one long ache.</p>

<p>Trevor walked in, pausing when he saw Hugh unmoving.</p>

<p>&quot;You okay?&quot; he asked, voice low with concern.</p>

<p>Hugh blinked slowly, almost forgetting where he was. &quot;Yeah,&quot; he muttered. &quot;Just thinking.&quot;</p>

<p>Trevor approached cautiously. &quot;It’s rough in here sometimes, man. The silence, the cameras, no phones, no distractions. It messes with your head.&quot; He placed a hand on Hugh’s shoulder.</p>

<p>&quot;That’s why we do morning workouts. Keeps the blood flowing, helps clear the fog. Come join us later, alright?&quot;</p>

<p>Hugh hesitated, lips pressed into a thin line, before he finally nodded. &quot;Okay.&quot;</p>

<p>But even as he agreed, the hollow feeling in his chest remained.</p>

<p>He took another deep breath, dragging his gaze back to the mirror. He looked tired.</p>

<p>Not just physically, but soul-deep.</p>

<p>Like a man who had lost something important and hadn’t yet figured out how to mourn it.</p>

<p>And he had lost something.</p>

<p>He could still hear Tianna’s voice echoing in his mind—those soft, lewd moans that had filled the house last night.</p>

<p>He hadn’t meant to hear them. No one did. But they were impossible to ignore.</p>

<p>That voice, once his favorite sound in the world, now haunted him.</p>

<p>He’d known it the moment she cried out Ross’s name. She wasn’t his anymore.</p>

<p>She had been claimed.</p>

<p>Not just in the physical sense—though that was clear too—but emotionally.</p>

<p>She had given herself to another man. Completely. Freely.</p>

<p>And Hugh had no place left in that world.</p>

<p>It was like being locked out of a house he used to live in, forced to watch through the windows as someone else enjoyed what was once his.</p>

<p>He hated himself for still caring.</p>

<p>Their breakup was not messy at all. They didn’t even had one.</p>

<p>No cold words, misunderstandings, and missed chances.</p>

<p>It was just Hugh knew that she could never love Tianna again.</p>

<p>Not after last night and they were as good as exes at this time.</p>

<p>But some part of him had always held on to the idea that maybe—just maybe—they could fix it.</p>

<p>Now that possibility was dead, buried under the weight of Tianna’s moans and the sound of another man making her feel what he never could.</p>

<p>Hugh swallowed hard.</p>

<p>The world was full of beautiful women. He knew that. But beauty wasn’t what he missed.</p>

<p>It was the connection. The conversations.</p>

<p>The quiet smiles Tianna used to give when she thought no one was looking.</p>

<p>The way she used to lean on his shoulder during long bus rides.</p>

<p>Still, Hugh reminded himself why he was here. One million dollars.</p>

<p>That kind of money didn’t just change lives—it rewrote them.</p>

<p>It was the key to freedom, to rebuilding, to starting fresh.</p>

<p>Tianna was a loss, yes, but not the end of the world.</p>

<p>He just needed to stay focused.</p>

<p>Still staring at his own reflection, Hugh gave himself a silent promise: After this show is over, I’ll find someone better. Someone who stays.</p>

<p>He stepped away from the mirror, ready—at least for now—to move forward.</p>

<p>Though Hugh had finally decided to let go, it was a completely different story for Tianna.</p>

<p>For her, last night hadn’t just been an experience—it had been a revelation.</p>

<p>A door had opened, leading her into a world she never knew existed.</p>

<p>A world of pleasure, of surrender, of breathtaking intimacy.</p>

<p>Everything felt different now. Everything felt alive.</p>

<p>Sex had been a mystery to her, something spoken about in hushed tones or glamorized in stories.</p>

<p>But no amount of fantasy could have prepared her for the real thing—especially with Ross.</p>

<p>His touch was addictive, his voice intoxicating, his presence magnetic.</p>

<p>From the moment he entered her, Tianna had been swept into a storm of ecstasy that felt like it would never end.</p>

<p>She came again and again, more times than she could count, her cries echoing through the night like a song she hadn’t known she could sing.</p>

<p>And the most surprising part?</p>

<p>She wasn’t tired.</p>

<p>She didn’t wake up sore or aching, as she’d been warned to expect.</p>

<p>On the contrary, she felt amazing.</p>

<p>Her entire body felt light, as though she had shed something heavy she didn’t even know she’d been carrying.</p>

<p>Her limbs were relaxed, her skin tingled with warmth, and her mind was clearer than it had been in years.</p>

<p>She felt refreshed.</p>

<p>Reborn.</p>

<p>There was a calm, vibrant energy flowing through her veins, pulsing in time with her heartbeat.</p>

<p>She could almost feel it humming just beneath the surface of her skin, like a quiet magic.</p>

<p>Maybe it wasn’t all in her head. Maybe something has changed.</p>

<p>Ross had come inside her. Not just once, but again and again, filling her to the brim, marking her in a way that felt far more than physical.</p>

<p>Something unnatural. Something divine. She couldn’t explain it and she couldn’t know either.</p>

<p>She just felt that something had changed.</p>

<p>What she did not know was that Ross had given her more than pleasure—he had given her power.</p>

<p>A gift. A boon.</p>
