<p>Ten minutes later, Kirito found himself standing on an enormous baseball field.</p>

<p>He could hardly believe such a grand setup was hidden in the backyard of Ross&#x27;s mansion, yet here he was, standing on the pitcher&#x27;s mound.</p>

<p>In an unexpected twist, he picked this challenge above everything else he could have done tonight.</p>

<p>Opposite him stood Brandon, poised to deliver a powerful swing at the ball Kirito was about to throw.</p>

<p>&quot;Let me go over the rules again,&quot; Ross began, his authoritative voice cutting through the quiet evening.</p>

<p>&quot;Each of you will throw 100 pitches. Every time the striker hits the ball, even if it doesn&#x27;t result in a home run, it counts as one point. However, bunts don&#x27;t count. Whoever has the most points at the end of the match wins.&quot;</p>

<p>Ross sat comfortably on the sidelines, flanked by Mari and Reina, both of whom seemed equally not interested in the unfolding game.</p>

<p>&quot;You can start whenever you&#x27;re ready,&quot; Ross announced, leaning back as if he were settling in for an entertaining spectacle.</p>

<p>Kirito took a deep breath and focused, gripping the baseball tightly.</p>

<p>He turned it over in his hand, feeling its weight, before blowing on it for good luck.</p>

<p>Baseball was more than just a sport to him; it was a deep part of his past. As Japan&#x27;s most popular sport, baseball had shaped much of his younger years.</p>

<p>Kirito wasn&#x27;t just any baseball player—he was a prodigy.</p>

<p>His exceptional skills as both a pitcher and a batter had earned him an invitation to join Major League Baseball during his college years.</p>

<p>However, practicality had won out over passion. Kirito chose to focus on founding his businesses, believing that path would yield greater financial rewards.</p>

<p>His gamble had paid off spectacularly—by the age of 25, he had become a billionaire.</p>

<p>Even so, Kirito&#x27;s love for baseball never waned.</p>

<p>He remained devoted to the sport, practicing and playing religiously every Saturday.</p>

<p>This lifelong connection to the game gave him confidence in his abilities, and now, standing on the field, he felt that same competitive fire reignite.</p>

<p>As he prepared to throw the first pitch, Kirito couldn&#x27;t help but smile.</p>

<p>This wasn&#x27;t just a friendly challenge anymore—it was a chance to prove that he hadn&#x27;t lost his edge.</p>

<p>More than anything, this felt like another chance at life.</p>

<p>Kirito doubted Ross would let him walk away tonight after all the bloodshed his men had caused.</p>

<p>A witness to such a crime was a liability, and Kirito knew it. His only hope was that Ross would honor his word when it was all over.</p>

<p>Still, before throwing the first pitch, there was something he needed to address—something that had been gnawing at him since he arrived.</p>

<p>&quot;You don&#x27;t need to blame your lovely wife for anything. I forced myself on her. She&#x27;s still the innocent, faithful woman you know—just caught in... unfortunate circumstances.&quot;</p>

<p>Ross spoke in perfect Japanese, his words cutting through the tense atmosphere like a blade.</p>
