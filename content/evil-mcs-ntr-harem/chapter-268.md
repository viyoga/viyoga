<p>The room fell silent except for the faint sound of Derek’s labored breathing. His confession hung in the air like a curse, heavy and suffocating.</p>

<p>Iris’s lips quivered, her eyes wide with disbelief.</p>

<p>&quot;Derek…&quot; she whispered, her voice barely audible. The weight of his words crushed her.</p>

<p>She looked at her son, this broken, arrogant shell of a man, and realized the horrifying truth.</p>

<p>She had raised him to believe the world owed him everything.</p>

<p>She had turned a blind eye to his faults, excusing his behavior, spoiling him at every turn.</p>

<p>And now, here he was, a monster of her own making.</p>

<p>Tears slid silently down her cheeks as the reality of her failure as a mother settled into her heart.</p>

<p>She opened her mouth to say something, anything, but no words came.</p>

<p>Ross straightened, his cold eyes flickering to Iris.</p>

<p>&quot;Now you see,&quot; he said, his voice cutting through the silence.</p>

<p>&quot;This is what your ’good boy’ has done. Six lives destroyed because he thought he was entitled to them. And you, Iris… you enabled him.&quot;</p>

<p>Iris could only stare at Ross, the weight of his words crushing her further.</p>

<p>This nightmare wasn’t just about Derek’s sins—it was about her complicity in creating them.</p>

<p>&quot;So, what are you going to do about it?&quot; Ross asked, his voice calm but laden with menace.</p>

<p>&quot;Your ’good boy’ wanted to steal my girl,&quot; he continued, his eyes narrowing.</p>

<p>&quot;And he almost succeeded.&quot; He paused to make his lie work better, his gaze drifting over Derek’s bloodied, broken form before returning to Iris.</p>

<p>&quot;I should just kill him right here and now and feed him to the fishes.&quot;</p>

<p>Ross leaned closer, his cold smile cutting like a knife.</p>

<p>&quot;What do you think, Iris Davies?&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;No! That’s not right!&quot; Iris shouted, her voice trembling.</p>

<p>Her eyes brimmed with fresh tears, which she wiped away angrily, more out of frustration at herself than fear of Ross.</p>

<p>&quot;If you really have evidence against my son, then turn him over to the police! Let justice take its course. This vigilante justice of yours is cruel… it’s inhumane!&quot;</p>

<p>Ross raised an eyebrow, the smirk never leaving his lips.</p>

<p>&quot;Justice?&quot; he echoed mockingly.</p>

<p>&quot;That word sounds nice, doesn’t it? But justice takes time, Iris. It’s slow. It’s blind. And it’s messy.&quot;</p>

<p>His smile darkened as he turned to glance at Brandon, who stood silent and imposing like a statue.</p>

<p>&quot;I don’t have the patience for that kind of ugly mess. So, I’m back to square one—back to my original intent.&quot;</p>

<p>He chuckled softly, the sound chilling in its sincerity.</p>

<p>&quot;Hehehe.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;Wait!&quot; Iris blurted out, her voice desperate.</p>

<p>She took a step forward, her hands trembling as they clutched at her bare chest to shield herself.</p>

<p>The vulnerability in her stance was stark, but her resolve remained firm.</p>

<p>Ross stopped mid-turn, his interest piqued. He turned back to face her, his eyes glinting with amusement.</p>

<p>&quot;Why?&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;You brought me here,&quot; Iris said, her voice steadying despite her obvious distress.</p>

<p>&quot;I don’t know how you did it—how you took me away from my home and brought me to this… place. But you must have a reason. What do you want from me?&quot;</p>

<p>Her tone was sharp, almost accusatory, but there was a flicker of fear behind her anger.</p>

<p>She was a smart woman—too smart to not understand the implications.</p>

<p>And yet, she forced the thoughts aside, unwilling to let herself fully believe them.</p>

<p>Ross chuckled again, this time more openly.</p>

<p>&quot;You’re a smart woman, Iris,&quot; he said, tilting his head as if to appraise her.</p>

<p>&quot;You’ve got an intelligent head on those lovely shoulders. And you know exactly what men want from you.&quot;</p>

<p>He stepped closer, his smirk widening.</p>

<p>&quot;I’m not so different from the rest of them.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;You’re a maniac!&quot; Iris spat, her voice rising in pitch as her frustration and fear collided.</p>

<p>&quot;Evil shouldn’t be repaid with more evil! It’s madness!&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;In a perfect world, you might be right,&quot; Ross said, shaking his head slowly.</p>

<p>&quot;But you, of all people, should know that this world is far from perfect. And I’m not here to play by its broken rules.&quot;</p>

<p>His words hung heavy in the air, the gravity of the situation crashing down on Iris like a tidal wave.</p>

<p>She gritted her teeth, her mind racing to find a way out, but the realization that she was utterly trapped made her chest tighten with dread.</p>

<p>Still, a mother was a mother to the very end.</p>

<p>&quot;Take my son to the hospital, and I’ll agree to whatever you want,&quot; Iris said finally, her voice breaking as she made the ultimate sacrifice.</p>

<p>Ross tilted his head, his expression unreadable.</p>

<p>&quot;No,&quot; he replied coldly. &quot;He stays here. This will be part of his forceful rehabilitation.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;You’re a monster,&quot; Iris whispered, her eyes filled with both anger and despair.</p>

<p>Ross let out a low chuckle, his dark amusement chilling.</p>

<p>&quot;I know I am,&quot; he said with a shrug.</p>
