<p>The Eastmount students roared louder than ever, their chants echoing through the gym like a thunderstorm.</p>

<p>The sheer volume of their voices drowned out Sophia and her friends, reducing their cheers to faint whispers in the cacophony.</p>

<p>But Sophia and the rest refused to back down.</p>

<p>They cupped their hands around their mouths and screamed as loud as they could, determined to make their voices heard.</p>

<p>Their unwavering dedication to Ross was impossible to ignore, even in the overwhelming noise of the opposing crowd.</p>

<p>The taunter stood directly in front of Ross, guarding him closely as their team took first possession of the ball.</p>

<p>Ross, however, didn&#x27;t bite. He simply smiled, his expression calm and unbothered, before replying coolly, &quot;Let me see you laugh after the game, then.&quot;</p>

<p>Without waiting for a response, Ross moved. His footwork was precise, a blur of motion that left the trash-talking defender stumbling to keep up.</p>

<p>In one smooth action, Ross bypassed him completely, cutting through the court with ease.</p>

<p>As he reached the three-point line, Ross didn&#x27;t hesitate. He planted his feet, rose into a perfect jumper, and released the ball with a practiced flick of his wrist.</p>

<p>What happened next was pure showmanship.</p>

<p>Before the ball even reached its apex, Ross turned away, not bothering to watch it land. His back was to the hoop when the sound rang out:</p>

<p>&quot;Whoosh!&quot;</p>

<p>The ball sailed cleanly through the center of the net, a flawless three-pointer that sent the crowd into a frenzy.</p>

<p>The gym erupted, but the loudest voices by far belonged to Ross&#x27;s girls.</p>

<p>&quot;Go, Ross!&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;You&#x27;re amazing, Ross!&quot;</p>

<p>Their cheers rang out above the rest, their excitement palpable as they practically jumped out of their seats in celebration.</p>

<p>Ross smirked, barely acknowledging the chaos he had just caused. The scoreboard flashed: 3–0—Ross had scored in the first ten seconds of the game, and he had done it with effortless style.</p>

<p>The taunting player, now red-faced and flustered, could only glare at Ross as the crowd continued to roar.</p>

<p>The smirk Ross threw over his shoulder as he jogged back to his position was enough to make the defender grit his teeth in frustration.</p>

<p>&quot;Keep laughing, buddy,&quot; Ross called casually. &quot;It&#x27;s going to be a long game for you.&quot;</p>

<p>By the time the first half ended, Ross had delivered a performance so unbelievable it left the entire crowd in stunned silence.</p>

<p>He had scored 105 points single-handedly, while the opposing team had only managed to scrape together 6 points.</p>

<p>Every shot Ross took was from beyond the three-point line, and every single one was flawless. A perfect game.</p>

<p>&quot;You don&#x27;t need to play the second half, Ross,&quot; the coach said firmly, though there was a hint of awe in his voice.</p>

<p>Scoring 105 points in just 20 minutes was already beyond anyone&#x27;s comprehension. Piling on more points would have been excessive, even cruel.</p>

<p>&quot;We&#x27;ve got a massive lead. Let the rest of the team finish this one off.&quot;</p>

<p>Ross shrugged, unbothered. &quot;Alright, if that&#x27;s what you want.&quot; He nodded at the team. &quot;I trust you guys to seal the win.&quot;</p>

<p>While Ross was perfectly capable of shooting from his own basket and landing it in the opposing hoop, he knew better than to push his limits too far in public.</p>

<p>Displaying that level of skill would raise questions, and Ross wasn&#x27;t ready to reveal his true capabilities to the world.</p>

<p>After handing over the game to his teammates, Ross stripped off his jersey and joined his girls on the sidelines.</p>

<p>The rest of the match was a blur to him—he spent the second half relaxing with his group of stunners, enjoying their attention and basking in the glory of the inevitable victory.</p>

<p>By the time the final buzzer sounded, the scoreboard read 156–99. The opposing team had managed to close the gap slightly, but it was far from enough.</p>

<p>Ross&#x27;s team walked off the court with their heads held high, the taste of victory sweet in their mouths.</p>

<p>The excitement from the game rippled through the school for days, with Ross&#x27;s name on everyone&#x27;s lips.</p>

<p>The man nodded, his expression deadly serious. &quot;Anything.&quot;</p>

<p>Ross&#x27;s lips curled into a grin. He couldn&#x27;t help but laugh at the absurdity—and the opportunity—of it all.</p>

<p>&quot;Well, then. Tell your boss you&#x27;ve got a deal,&quot; he said, extending his hand.</p>

<p>His rise to stardom was only beginning, and he was more than ready to take the world by storm.</p>
