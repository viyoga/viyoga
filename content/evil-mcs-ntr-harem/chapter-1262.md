<p>The creature didn’t even slow down.</p>

<p>It was as if they were attacking a moving fortress.</p>

<p>&quot;Impossible!&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;Our weapons aren’t working!&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;Retreat!&quot;</p>

<p>Fear spread through the ranks.</p>

<p>For the first time, they realized why the older survivors had repeatedly warned them never to travel too far beyond the city limits.</p>

<p>The cities were not humanity’s prison.</p>

<p>They were humanity’s shelter.</p>

<p>Beyond them existed a world ruled by monsters.</p>

<p>And the beast standing before them was only the first creature they had encountered.</p>

<p>Far away inside the city, Ross watched through a remote viewing ability and chuckled.</p>

<p>&quot;They’ve finally learned.&quot;</p>

<p>His gaze drifted toward the distant wilderness.</p>

<p>What the expedition was facing now was merely the beginning.</p>

<p>Compared to the true horrors hidden beyond humanity’s borders, that lion-like beast was little more than a welcome party.</p>

<p>An hour later, the expedition finally returned.</p>

<p>Or rather, what remained of it.</p>

<p>Out of the more than one thousand men who had confidently ventured beyond the safe zone near the city, fewer than fifty managed to make it back alive.</p>

<p>The sight shocked everyone.</p>

<p>Many of the survivors collapsed the moment they reached the city gates.</p>

<p>Some gasped desperately for air.</p>

<p>Others trembled uncontrollably.</p>

<p>Several broke down in tears.</p>

<p>A few even fell to their knees and kissed the ground, grateful simply to be alive.</p>

<p>The confidence and excitement they had possessed when leaving the city were completely gone.</p>

<p>In their place remained only fear.</p>

<p>Pure fear.</p>

<p>The expressions on their faces looked as though they had just escaped from hell itself.</p>

<p>Word spread throughout the city at astonishing speed.</p>

<p>People gathered around the survivors, desperately asking questions.</p>

<p>&quot;What happened?&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;Where’s my husband?&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;Where’s my brother?&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;Where are the others?&quot;</p>

<p>The survivors struggled to answer.</p>

<p>Many of them were still trying to process the nightmare they had experienced.</p>

<p>&quot;The monsters...&quot;</p>

<p>One man spoke with a pale face.</p>

<p>&quot;There were monsters everywhere.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;They came from the forests, the mountains, even underground.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;We couldn’t stop them.&quot;</p>

<p>Another survivor started shaking as he recalled the scene.</p>

<p>&quot;Thousands died.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;They were slaughtered.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;It wasn’t a battle.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;It was a massacre.&quot;</p>

<p>The crowd grew increasingly alarmed.</p>

<p>Soon, grief began turning into anger.</p>

<p>Whenever a tragedy occurred, people naturally searched for someone to blame.</p>

<p>And before long, rumors began spreading.</p>

<p>&quot;Something strange happened out there.&quot;</p>

<p>A survivor suddenly spoke up.</p>

<p>The surrounding crowd immediately listened.</p>

<p>&quot;What do you mean?&quot;</p>

<p>The man pointed toward Ross’s territory.</p>

<p>&quot;We were all being attacked.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;The monsters were killing everyone.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;But Ross Oakley’s men just stood there.&quot;</p>

<p>The crowd erupted instantly.</p>

<p>&quot;What?&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;They didn’t help?&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;Is that true?&quot;</p>

<p>Another survivor immediately shook his head.</p>

<p>&quot;No, that’s not entirely true.&quot;</p>

<p>Everyone turned toward him.</p>

<p>&quot;I saw one of those lion beasts attack Ross’s men.&quot;</p>

<p>The survivor swallowed nervously.</p>

<p>&quot;The creature charged straight at them.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;And then?&quot;</p>

<p>The man’s eyes widened as he remembered what he had witnessed.</p>

<p>&quot;One of Ross’s subordinates casually slapped it.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;Slapped it?&quot;</p>

<p>The crowd stared at him in disbelief.</p>

<p>The survivor nodded vigorously.</p>

<p>&quot;Yes.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;The beast was sent flying hundreds of meters away.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;It crashed through several trees.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;And after that...&quot;</p>

<p>His voice became quieter.</p>

<p>&quot;It never attacked them again.&quot;</p>

<p>The crowd fell silent.</p>

<p>Many found the story unbelievable.</p>

<p>That giant monster had wiped out hundreds of elite evolvers.</p>

<p>Yet one of Ross’s men had casually swatted it away?</p>

<p>It sounded absurd.</p>

<p>But multiple survivors quickly confirmed the story.</p>

<p>&quot;I saw it too.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;So did I.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;Those monsters completely ignored Ross’s people afterward.&quot;</p>

<p>The atmosphere became increasingly tense.</p>

<p>Some people looked shocked.</p>

<p>Others looked suspicious.</p>

<p>Then someone finally voiced what many were beginning to think.</p>

<p>&quot;If they were that strong...&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;Why didn’t they help everyone else?&quot;</p>

<p>The question hung heavily in the air.</p>

<p>No one answered.</p>

<p>&quot;They clearly had the power to kill those beasts!&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;My son died out there!&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;My husband died too!&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;My entire squad was wiped out!&quot;</p>

<p>The grief-stricken families became emotional.</p>

<p>Reason slowly gave way to anger.</p>

<p>&quot;They could have saved people!&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;They watched us die!&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;They are traitors to humanity!&quot;</p>

<p>The accusations spread rapidly through the crowd.</p>

<p>More and more people joined in.</p>

<p>Many had lost friends.</p>

<p>Others had lost family.</p>

<p>Some had lost everything.</p>

<p>They needed somewhere to direct their pain.</p>

<p>And Ross’s group had become a convenient target.</p>

<p>Not everyone agreed, however.</p>

<p>A few survivors immediately objected.</p>

<p>&quot;That’s ridiculous!&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;They never agreed to protect us!&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;The expedition wasn’t even their idea!&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;Besides, they warned everyone that the outside world was dangerous.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;Nobody listened.&quot;</p>

<p>Arguments erupted across the city.</p>

<p>One side blamed Ross’s people for not intervening.</p>

<p>The other side argued that every participant had willingly joined the expedition and accepted the risks.</p>

<p>The city soon became divided.</p>

<p>Meanwhile, inside his residence, Ross calmly listened to the reports.</p>

<p>His expression remained unchanged.</p>

<p>The accusations, the rumors, the anger of the crowd...</p>

<p>None of it mattered to him.</p>

<p>Humans were predictable.</p>

<p>When things went well, they praised heroes.</p>

<p>When things went badly, they searched for villains.</p>

<p>Ross understood this better than anyone.</p>

<p>He simply smiled and continued drinking his tea.</p>

<p>The people who blamed him failed to understand a simple truth.</p>

<p>His men had not gone there to babysit the United Earth Alliance.</p>

<p>They had gone there to observe.</p>

<p>The alliance had chosen to ignore the warnings.</p>

<p>The alliance had chosen to venture beyond the safe zone.</p>

<p>And the alliance had paid the price for its arrogance.</p>

<p>Ross took another sip of tea and gazed toward the distant wilderness beyond the city walls.</p>

<p>The monsters that slaughtered nearly a thousand men were merely creatures living near the edge of humanity’s territory.</p>

<p>Compared to the true horrors hidden deeper within the world, they were insignificant.</p>

<p>If humanity became this divided after facing only the outermost dangers, Ross wondered how they would react when they discovered what was truly waiting for them in the darkness.</p>

<p>Ross’s camp was then targeted by the masses. It was then that Ross had to act also.</p>

<p>He walked to the front and looked sad.</p>

<p>&quot;This is a tragedy and no one wanted this to happen.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;There’s no point in shoving blame when it’s time for us all to be united.&quot; Ross said.</p>

<p>&quot;Lies!&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;Traitor!&quot;</p>
