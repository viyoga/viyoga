<p>The smell hit Heaven like a drug the second the door opened.</p>

<p>Something meaty, slow-cooked, with spices that danced in the air like warm hands caressing her skin.</p>

<p>It wrapped around her, pulled her in like a siren’s call, and this time—this time—there was no turning back.</p>

<p>She stood just outside the dining area, gripping the doorway as if it could anchor her to what little pride she had left.</p>

<p>Her heart pounded in her chest.</p>

<p>Her throat was dry. Her jaw was clenched so tightly she thought her teeth might crack.</p>

<p>People were laughing, eating, savoring each bite.</p>

<p>And Ross?</p>

<p>He was standing near the table, casually spooning something golden and fragrant onto a plate.</p>

<p>His back was to her, but she could feel it—he knew she was there.</p>

<p>Her vision tunneled.</p>

<p>Something inside her snapped.</p>

<p>She walked in with stiff, determined steps. The room quieted just slightly, sensing the tension.</p>

<p>She didn’t care who was watching. She didn’t care what anyone thought.</p>

<p>She crossed the distance in seconds and stopped directly in front of him.</p>

<p>Ross finally turned, eyes calm, lips tugged into that maddening smirk.</p>

<p>&quot;Heaven,&quot; he greeted smoothly, like she hadn’t just looked like she was going to explode. &quot;To what do I owe the pleasure?&quot;</p>

<p>She stared at the plate in his hand, and for a moment, the only thing she could do was breathe. Or try to.</p>

<p>&quot;Give me a plate,&quot; she said, her voice low, husky, cracking under the strain of her restraint. &quot;Now.&quot;</p>

<p>Ross didn’t answer immediately.</p>

<p>He tilted his head slightly, studying her like a painter might study a nearly finished work—examining the cracks, the flaws, the emotion bleeding through the surface.</p>

<p>&quot;Are you sure? You know that there’s a big price for a plate.&quot; he asked, his tone teasing. &quot;You were so determined to resist me before.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;Don’t push me,&quot; she whispered, eyes locked onto his like a warning—or maybe a plea.</p>

<p>The smirk widened just a touch.</p>

<p>&quot;As you wish but... if you take this plate and eat it, you should know that you need to be my girlfriend after.&quot; he said.</p>

<p>And with a calm, deliberate motion, Ross placed a full plate in front of her—steaming, fragrant, utterly divine.</p>

<p>&quot;I don’t care.&quot; Heaven replied.</p>

<p>Her hands trembled as she took it, and though she didn’t say a word, everyone in the room knew:</p>

<p>He’d won something.</p>

<p>And Heaven, in that moment, didn’t care.</p>

<p>She devoured the plate of food in a flash, barely giving herself time to breathe between bites.</p>

<p>Each mouthful sent waves of ecstasy through her senses, overwhelming her with flavor so rich, so layered, that she was left trembling by the time she set her fork down.</p>

<p>Tears shimmered in her eyes—not of sadness, but of sheer, stunned delight.</p>

<p>&quot;Oh my god...&quot; she breathed, wiping at her cheeks with the napkin.</p>

<p>&quot;This... this is insane. It’s so good! How the hell are you making food like this?&quot;</p>

<p>Ross chuckled softly as he leaned against the kitchen counter, arms crossed, watching her with amusement.</p>

<p>&quot;It’s a trade secret. You’ll have to marry me if you want to know.&quot; he said with a smirk.</p>

<p>&quot;You wish,&quot; Heaven said, shaking her head, eyes still wide.</p>

<p>Ross grinned. &quot;But I do love to wish and I always get what I wish for.&quot;</p>

<p>She blinked at him, reeling at his arrogant words.</p>

<p>&quot;As for me cooking, I learned from a master,&quot; he said at last, voice laced with a hint of nostalgia. This was of course a big lie.</p>

<p>&quot;An eccentric old man who lived alone on top of a mountain far, far away. He taught me everything he knew. But he’s gone now... and with him, the recipes he guarded. As far as I know, I’m the only person left who can make these dishes.&quot;</p>

<p>Heaven stared at him, partly moved by the story, but also completely baffled.</p>

<p>&quot;You mean to tell me a guy living on some random mountain trained you to be a god in the kitchen?&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;Well,&quot; Ross said, grinning wider now, &quot;not just any mountain. And not just any ingredients.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;What’s that supposed to mean?&quot; she asked, narrowing her eyes.</p>

<p>Ross said nothing.</p>

<p>He turned and began gathering the plates, walking toward the sink as though the conversation were finished.</p>

<p>But in truth, Heaven’s instincts weren’t wrong.</p>

<p>He had indeed used the ingredients in the house—rice, vegetables, spices—but he had also mixed in something else.</p>

<p>Something beyond this world.</p>

<p>Chunks of meat from spirit beasts that roamed the divine plains of a xianxia realm.</p>

<p>Herbs cultivated under the light of twin moons.</p>

<p>A splash of broth distilled from immortal fruits.</p>

<p>Every bite she had taken was infused with spiritual essence that could fortify a cultivator’s soul and reshape their body from within.</p>

<p>And now, though Heaven didn’t feel it yet, changes had already begun.</p>

<p>Her cells were regenerating at a faster rate. Her immune system had evolved, now impervious to common illnesses.</p>

<p>Her bones were denser, her skin subtly tougher—still soft to the touch but far more resistant to injury.</p>

<p>Even her senses had started to sharpen. A headache that had lingered in the background all day had vanished completely.</p>

<p>All of this, from a single meal.</p>

<p>Ross dried his hands on a towel and looked over his shoulder at her.</p>

<p>&quot;You’ll feel it soon enough,&quot; he said cryptically.</p>

<p>&quot;What?&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;Nothing,&quot; he replied with a wink. &quot;Just get ready. Next time, I’m making dessert.&quot;</p>

<p>Ross leaned back in his chair, patting his stomach with satisfaction.</p>

<p>&quot;That hit the spot,&quot; he said, glancing at Heaven with a teasing smile. &quot;All done. Now... since I cooked, you get to wash the dishes.&quot;</p>

<p>Heaven blinked, then frowned in mock annoyance. &quot;Seriously?&quot; she pouted.</p>

<p>&quot;House rules,&quot; Ross replied with a grin, leaning back like a smug king surveying his domain.</p>

<p>With a dramatic sigh, she stood up and began gathering the plates.</p>

<p>&quot;Fine, fine. You better make dessert next time, though,&quot; she muttered, half-joking, half-hoping.</p>

<p>She carried the dishes into the kitchen and walked up to the sink—only to be immediately ambushed by several housemates who were loitering nearby.</p>

<p>&quot;What are you doing, Heaven?&quot; one of them asked, clearly baffled.</p>
