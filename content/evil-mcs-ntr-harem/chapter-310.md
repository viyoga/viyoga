<p>Hours later, Ross and his stunning entourage were still at it, playing a few more rounds in the casino.</p>

<p>Luck finally tilted slightly in his favor, and he managed to win back $200 million.</p>

<p>However, he was still $300 million short of recovering all his losses.</p>

<p>&quot;I guess it’s time to call it a night,&quot; Ross said, standing up and stretching casually.</p>

<p>He glanced around, noticing that the girls were no longer focused on the games.</p>

<p>Instead, their eyes were fixed on him, brimming with a hunger he recognized all too well.</p>

<p>Their gazes weren’t subtle. They practically radiated desire, as if ready to devour him the moment they had him alone.</p>

<p>Ross couldn’t help but chuckle to himself. He had been away from them for two nights, and their impatience was understandable.</p>

<p>After all, they were used to his nightly attentions, and the &quot;break&quot; had only intensified their cravings.</p>

<p>Not that Ross could blame them. With the kind of chemistry they shared, it was hard to keep their hands off each other.</p>

<p>Their nights together were filled with unrelenting passion, interrupted only by the occasional bathroom break or when nature’s monthly visit forced a pause.</p>

<p>&quot;Yes, let’s!&quot; one of the girls chirped eagerly, snapping him from his thoughts.</p>

<p>The others quickly followed suit, gathering their bags and standing up with barely contained excitement.</p>

<p>But their energy wavered as they noticed Ross suddenly stop in his tracks, his attention fixed on something—or someone—ahead of them.</p>

<p>The girls exchanged puzzled looks as they followed his gaze.</p>

<p>Standing there, just as expectantly as before, was the same sharply dressed man who had earlier invited Ross to a high-stakes poker game.</p>

<p>His polished demeanor hadn’t wavered, though his anticipation now bordered on desperation.</p>

<p>The way he stood, almost like a loyal dog eager to please its master, made it clear that he was banking on Ross’s participation to secure a hefty bonus—or perhaps something more.</p>

<p>&quot;What the hell. Why not?&quot; Ross said, a wide grin spreading across his face.</p>

<p>He had a knack for sensing when something interesting was about to happen, and the poker table at the other end of this invitation practically promised excitement.</p>

<p>Turning to his companions, he added with a mischievous glint in his eye, &quot;Let’s make one more stop before heading back, ladies. I’ve got a poker game to play. Hehehe.&quot;</p>

<p>Without waiting for a reply, Ross strode toward the handsome man, his confidence radiating with every step.</p>

<p>The girls sighed collectively, though their exasperation was tinged with amusement.</p>

<p>&quot;Of course,&quot; one muttered under her breath, but they followed him without hesitation.</p>

<p>They were used to Ross’s whims and knew that wherever he went, the night was bound to get even more interesting.</p>

<p>They were led to a private room in the casino, a space lavishly furnished with polished wood, velvet chairs, and golden accents.</p>

<p>Waiting for them inside were several men, each exuding an air of quiet authority and a group of goons with big muscles and intimidating presence.</p>

<p>At the center of it all sat the enigmatic Mr. Adams, who finally revealed himself in the flesh.</p>

<p>The moment Ross and his women entered, the atmosphere shifted.</p>

<p>The room, spacious moments ago, suddenly felt stifling as if the sheer presence of Ross’s entourage had compressed the air.</p>

<p>His women—gorgeous, confident, and exuding a natural allure—instantly captured every man’s attention.</p>

<p>Eyes roamed unabashedly over their curvaceous figures, and not a few men found themselves visibly affected.</p>

<p>The tension in the room thickened as some even began sniffing the air like dogs in heat, overwhelmed by the intoxicating scent of the women’s natural beauty.</p>

<p>Ross’s companions were undeniably the epitome of perfection, women so exquisite that their mere presence made the ordinary seem utterly mundane.</p>

<p>Standing to greet them, Mr. Adams extended his hand toward Ross.</p>

<p>&quot;Ross Oakley. I’m glad to finally meet you. I’m Don Adams,&quot; he said with a practiced smile.</p>

<p>Don Adams was a fit, middle-aged man with a neatly groomed mustache that complemented his distinguished appearance.</p>

<p>He radiated the confidence of a seasoned businessman, but the subtle tension in his shoulders and the well-armed security stationed around the room hinted at a man who valued his safety above all else.</p>

<p>Ross shook his hand firmly, his own expression unreadable.</p>

<p>The two exchanged polite yet calculated pleasantries, each sizing the other up.</p>

<p>Beneath the surface of their words lay an unspoken assessment—both men trying to gauge the character and motivations of the other.</p>

<p>When the introductions were complete, Gwen, one of Ross’s sharpest companions, leaned in close to whisper.</p>

<p>&quot;I don’t like the look of this, Ross,&quot; she murmured, her voice low and tinged with unease.</p>

<p>Her detective instincts were practically screaming at her to get out of here fast.</p>

<p>Ross gave her a reassuring smile, replying in a whisper, &quot;No matter. Even if the heavens fall tonight, I’ll shoulder it all for you and the rest.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;Hmph!&quot; Gwen responded with a soft huff, her hand instinctively brushing against her hip—only to find it empty.</p>

<p>She felt vulnerable without the familiar weight of her handgun, a sensation she disliked intensely.</p>

<p>Ross stepped forward, casually addressing the room.</p>

<p>&quot;So, I hear the game is poker. I’ll admit, I’ve only ever watched videos online and haven’t played a single hand myself. But that’s fine—learning is half the fun. Now, let’s cut to the chase. How much is the wager? I don’t mind losing a few more hundreds of millions tonight.&quot;</p>

<p>He flashed a charming smile, his tone lighthearted. Ross was never one to judge a book by its cover, and he didn’t dismiss the possibility of making an ally.</p>

<p>But his open-minded approach was quickly dashed by Mr. Adams’s next words.</p>

<p>&quot;Money, to men like us, means nothing, Mr. Oakley,&quot; Mr. Adams said, leaning back with an air of casual arrogance.</p>

<p>His lips curled into a sly smile before he licked them suggestively, his eyes roaming over Ross’s women with barely concealed lust.</p>

<p>&quot;I want to bet on something more meaningful.&quot;</p>

<p>The temperature in the room seemed to drop. Ross’s eyes narrowed slightly, the polite veneer on his face hardening.</p>

<p>His women exchanged tense glances, their earlier unease now sharpening into silent resolve.</p>

<p>Ross didn’t reply immediately. Instead, he let the silence stretch, his piercing gaze locking onto Mr. Adams.</p>

<p>When he finally spoke, his voice was calm yet carried a weight that made the entire room pause.</p>

<p>&quot;Interesting,&quot; Ross said, his smirk returning but colder this time.</p>

<p>&quot;Why don’t you elaborate on exactly what you mean by ’more meaningful’? I’d hate to misunderstand.&quot;</p>
