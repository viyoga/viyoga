<p>Lois watched from the sidelines as her family buzzed with excitement.</p>

<p>Relatives gossiped and whispered about the celebrity bride and groom, while planners coordinated schedules and décor with meticulous precision.</p>

<p>Yet amid all the pomp and extravagance, Lois felt a lingering sense of unease.</p>

<p>She could see how happy Laura was, and that happiness made it all the harder for Lois to argue or intervene.</p>

<p>Days melted into weeks as rehearsal dinners, fittings, and tastings consumed every waking moment.</p>

<p>Lois found herself navigating a strange mixture of admiration and dread: admiration for the flawless execution of the preparations, dread for the impending reality of seeing her sister wed to a man who, despite appearances, had such a commanding hold over her family and life.</p>

<p>Ultimately, Lois had no choice but to accept the inevitable.</p>

<p>She straightened her shoulders, squared her jaw, and decided to focus on what she could control: supporting her sister in the ways that mattered, quietly observing Ross, and remaining vigilant for any sign that might indicate a problem.</p>

<p>The bitter truth was undeniable, but as the wedding day loomed closer, Lois knew she had to navigate the coming storm with both grace and caution.</p>

<p>Little did Lois know, she couldn’t guard her family all the time—especially not her mother.</p>

<p>&quot;You’re here, Ross. Everybody’s gone to see the reception hall for the wedding. They all left,&quot; Kate said, a hint of curiosity in her voice as she glanced around the empty living room.</p>

<p>&quot;Ah, that’s fine, Kate. I’ll just wait for them here,&quot; Ross replied smoothly, his voice calm and measured.</p>

<p>Yet even as he spoke, his eyes discreetly roamed over his fiancée’s mother.</p>

<p>Kate had aged gracefully; her figure was elegant, her posture perfect, and her youthful beauty could easily allow her to pass as Laura’s sister.</p>

<p>There was a magnetic allure in the way she carried herself, a mature charm that subtly drew attention without effort.</p>

<p>Ross felt the familiar pull of desire, but he masked it expertly, keeping his gaze focused on her eyes and his expression one of a polite, attentive fiancé.</p>

<p>&quot;So... how’s everything going, Ross? You’ve been so busy with the upcoming wedding. How about your business and career?&quot; Kate began, speaking casually but with a trace of amusement in her tone.</p>

<p>She had always been perceptive, and Ross knew that, yet he remained composed, nodding and responding with the perfect measure of charm and consideration.</p>

<p>He took a slow step closer, subtly closing the distance without appearing intrusive. &quot;Everything is going well, Kate. There’s a lot to organize, but Laura and I are handling it. I want everything to be perfect for her,&quot; Ross said, his smile warm and reassuring.</p>

<p>Despite the polite tone, a silent game was unfolding in his mind.</p>

<p>He observed her posture, her expressions, the slight tilt of her head, all the little details that made her human—and irresistibly attractive.</p>

<p>Kate laughed softly, a sound that filled the quiet room.</p>

<p>&quot;You always were meticulous, Ross. I can see why Laura fell for you. I just hope she remembers to take care of herself in all this chaos.&quot; She glanced around, then at Ross again, unaware of the sharp awareness in his gaze.</p>

<p>Ross inclined his head, listening intently. &quot;Of course, Kate. I always make sure everyone I care about is safe and happy. That includes you,&quot; he said smoothly, each word chosen carefully, the charm in his tone undeniable.</p>

<p>He kept his composure, but internally he acknowledged the challenge ahead: a woman like Kate wasn’t easily swayed, yet her presence here—alone with him—offered possibilities he intended to explore in subtle ways.</p>

<p>Kate’s eyes lingered on him for a moment, thoughtful. &quot;Well, I suppose it’s nice to have a quiet moment for once, away from all the chaos. It gives us time to... relax for a bit.&quot; Her tone was innocent, but there was a tension beneath it that Ross sensed immediately.</p>

<p>He responded with an easy smile. &quot;I agree. It’s rare to find these moments nowadays. Let’s make the most of it.&quot;</p>

<p>The air between them held a subtle electricity, a quiet game of glances and words, each measured and deliberate.</p>

<p>Though Ross maintained the appearance of a devoted fiancé, his mind was already calculating, anticipating, and savoring the delicate dance that he had been anticipating for some time.</p>

<p>Thirty minutes into their conversation, Kate’s posture shifted.</p>

<p>She tilted her head slightly and began rubbing the back of her neck with a weary sigh.</p>

<p>&quot;What’s wrong?&quot; Ross asked, his voice laced with genuine concern as his sharp eyes caught the small gesture.</p>

<p>&quot;It’s nothing, really. Just one of my headaches. I get them sometimes,&quot; Kate replied softly, still kneading the tension in her muscles, her fingers pressing into the tight spots that refused to relax.</p>

<p>Ross leaned forward, his expression thoughtful for a moment before a playful grin tugged at his lips.</p>

<p>&quot;I see... Well, sounds like it’s my time to shine. I give massages so good they’ll make you forget what pain even feels like. Headaches? Gone. Stress? Obliterated. Come here.&quot; He patted the wide, cushioned sofa beside him with exaggerated confidence.</p>

<p>Kate chuckled, shaking her head. &quot;Oh no, Ross, that’s not necessary. It’ll pass soon—I’ll just take a pill and let it fade on its own.&quot;</p>

<p>Ross raised an eyebrow, his grin never faltering. &quot;Nope. I insist. Pills are overrated. Trust me on this—my hands are magic.&quot; He stood up before she could protest again, closing the short distance between them with calm assurance.</p>

<p>&quot;Ross—&quot; Kate began, but her words trailed off as he gently took her hand, his touch warm and firm without being overbearing.</p>

<p>&quot;Relax. Just let me help,&quot; he said in a low, soothing tone that left little room for argument.</p>

<p>A minute later, Kate was settled on the plush sofa, leaning slightly forward while Ross stood behind her.</p>

<p>His hands hovered briefly over her shoulders before making contact.</p>

<p>The first touch was gentle—testing, almost hesitant—then his thumbs pressed into the tense muscles at the base of her neck, working in slow, deliberate circles.</p>
