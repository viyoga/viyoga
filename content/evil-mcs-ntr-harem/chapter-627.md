<p>Thomas&#x27;s head pounded. His vision blurred slightly at the edges.</p>

<p>The helplessness was suffocating, the kind of helplessness that stripped a man bare, left him vulnerable in the worst way possible.</p>

<p>&quot;You won&#x27;t choose?&quot; Ross said, feigning disappointment.</p>

<p>&quot;Such a shame. I was hoping you&#x27;d show a little backbone.&quot;</p>

<p>He chuckled under his breath, then turned back toward the women with a casual arrogance, as if he were simply selecting a dessert after dinner.</p>

<p>&quot;Very well,&quot; he said with a smirk. &quot;I&#x27;ll choose for you.&quot;</p>

<p>His eyes locked onto Brenda.</p>

<p>&quot;Come here, Brenda,&quot; Ross commanded, his voice low but filled with an iron authority that brooked no refusal.</p>

<p>Brenda flinched as if struck.</p>

<p>Tears welled up in her eyes as her body began to move against her will, one trembling step at a time toward the large bed at the center of the room.</p>

<p>Her hands clenched into fists at her sides, her knuckles white. She was fighting it — Thomas could see that — but Ross&#x27;s control was absolute.</p>

<p>Thomas watched, every fiber of his being screaming in agony, as his mother was dragged into this nightmare.</p>

<p>&quot;No!&quot; he roared, his voice cracking. &quot;Leave her alone! Leave them both alone!&quot;</p>

<p>Ross didn&#x27;t even glance at him.</p>

<p>He just walked toward the bed, humming a soft, tuneless melody to himself, as if he were preparing for something routine, something inevitable.</p>

<p>And Thomas — strong, capable, determined Thomas — could only watch, bound by invisible chains, as the horror unfolded right before his eyes.</p>

<p>&quot;What&#x27;s happening here, Thomas?&quot; Brenda asked, her voice quivering as her body moved against her will, step by reluctant step toward the bed.</p>

<p>Panic flickered in her eyes, and her hands twitched helplessly at her sides, trying in vain to resist whatever unseen force dragged her along.</p>

<p>Thomas stood frozen, pinned to the wall, every muscle in his body straining uselessly against the invisible chains that held him.</p>

<p>He opened his mouth, but no words came out.</p>

<p>How could he explain?</p>

<p>How could he possibly tell her that she — his mother — and Colleen had been dead for years?</p>

<p>That he had mourned them, grieved for them, buried them in his heart — and yet here they were, standing before him, brought back by means he couldn&#x27;t even begin to understand?</p>

<p>He didn&#x27;t know if they were truly alive, or if they were just cruel puppets twisted into the likeness of the people he loved.</p>

<p>And worse still, he had no way to save them.</p>

<p>The weight of his silence hung heavy in the air, and Brenda, seeing no answer on her son&#x27;s stricken face, turned away with a deepening sense of dread.</p>

<p>When she finally reached the bed, the unseen force that had controlled her abruptly let her go.</p>

<p>Brenda stumbled, catching herself awkwardly on the edge of the mattress, her breathing ragged.</p>

<p>She glanced back at Thomas one last time, silently pleading for an explanation, a solution — anything — but seeing nothing but helplessness and agony in his eyes.</p>

<p>Slowly, she straightened and turned toward Ross.</p>

<p>For a moment, she simply stared at him, as if hoping he would reveal himself to be some terrible dream, something her mind could shake off.</p>

<p>But no — the reality of the man before her was undeniable.</p>

<p>She let her eyes roam, taking in the details she had been too panicked to notice before.</p>

<p>Ross was massive. Even the plain clothes he wore — a simple shirt stretched taut over his broad chest and thick arms — could not hide the raw, powerful musculature beneath.</p>

<p>Every inch of him spoke of strength and dominance, a man built like a weapon honed for violence.</p>

<p>And yet it wasn&#x27;t just his body that terrified her.</p>

<p>It was his presence.</p>

<p>He carried himself with an ease that spoke of utter, unshakable confidence — the kind of confidence that could not be faked or taught.</p>

<p>It was the confidence of a man who knew he could take whatever he wanted, whenever he wanted, without fear of consequence.</p>

<p>He stood there like a lion surveying his domain, knowing that no one could challenge him.</p>

<p>Brenda shuddered involuntarily.</p>

<p>She had met men like this before — men who wore their confidence like a second skin.</p>

<p>Some were merely arrogant, drunk on their own ego. Others... others were something far worse.</p>

<p>Predators hiding behind charming smiles and easy laughter.</p>

<p>And Ross, with his cold gaze and cruel smirk, belonged to the latter category.</p>

<p>There was no kindness behind his eyes. No restraint.</p>

<p>Only a terrifying, predatory hunger.</p>

<p>Brenda&#x27;s instincts screamed at her to run, to flee from this monster — but her legs refused to move. Fear rooted her to the spot, leaving her exposed and vulnerable.</p>

<p>Thomas watched helplessly, every second stretching into an eternity.</p>

<p>Guilt gnawed at him like a ravenous beast.</p>

<p>This is my fault, he thought bitterly. I led Ross here. I disturbed whatever twisted nightmare he was living in. And now they&#x27;re paying the price.</p>

<p>His hands curled into fists, nails digging into his palms until blood welled up, but he barely noticed the pain.</p>

<p>He had faced death before. He had faced loss, betrayal, heartbreak.</p>

<p>But nothing — nothing — compared to the agony of seeing the people he loved most about to be destroyed, and knowing he could do nothing to stop it.</p>

<p>Ross took a slow, deliberate reach closer to Brenda, savoring her terror.</p>

<p>He didn&#x27;t speak. He didn&#x27;t need to.</p>

<p>The cruel amusement in his eyes said it all — this was a game to him, and he was enjoying every second.</p>

<p>Brenda&#x27;s breathing quickened, her hands trembling as she backed away slightly, almost bumping down the floor behind her.</p>

<p>Her knees nearly gave out, but she caught herself at the last moment, standing upright with what little strength she had left.</p>

<p>&quot;Please...&quot; she whispered, her voice barely audible. &quot;Please don&#x27;t...&quot;</p>

<p>Ross only smiled wider.</p>

<p>Thomas bit down hard on his lip to keep from screaming. His vision blurred with rage and helplessness.</p>

<p>There has to be a way, he thought desperately. There has to be something I can do.</p>

<p>But there was nothing.</p>

<p>Nothing but the horror unfolding in front of his eyes.</p>
