<p>It was the first time in weeks Miku felt the simple comfort of being clean.</p>

<p>And yet, even as her body rested, her mind didn’t.</p>

<p>She dreamt of Ace—his hand reaching for hers through fire and smoke, his voice calling her name from somewhere she couldn’t reach.</p>

<p>When morning came, she woke with a start, drenched in sweat.</p>

<p>The light that filtered through the curtains was pale and weak, but it was enough to remind her that the nightmare outside was still real.</p>

<p>She stepped to the window, her heart sinking at the sight.</p>

<p>The street below was crawling with zombies—limping, swaying figures that wandered aimlessly through the ruins of what had once been a quiet suburban neighborhood.</p>

<p>The air outside was still and heavy, filled with the faint scent of decay that the wind carried from somewhere far away.</p>

<p>Her chest tightened.</p>

<p>Ace was still out there somewhere. Maybe trapped in their house. Maybe fighting to survive. Maybe waiting for her.</p>

<p>She couldn’t stay here forever.</p>

<p>Wiping the sweat from her brow, Miku straightened her back, her reflection faintly visible in the dusty glass.</p>

<p>There was fear in her eyes—but beneath it, there was something stronger. Determination.</p>

<p>She wouldn’t give up on Ace. Not now. Not ever.</p>

<p>After a quick breath, she left the room and walked downstairs to the basement.</p>

<p>The house was quiet except for the faint murmur of the TV in the background.</p>

<p>Ross was already awake, seated at the kitchen counter with a mug of coffee in hand.</p>

<p>His dark hair was still damp, as if he had just showered.</p>

<p>The light cast sharp shadows across his face, making his expression unreadable.</p>

<p>Miku hesitated at the doorway, gathering her courage once more. Then, she stepped forward.</p>

<p>&quot;Ross,&quot; she said softly.</p>

<p>He looked up at her with a questioning glance but didn’t speak.</p>

<p>&quot;I know you said no last night,&quot; she continued, her voice steadier now.</p>

<p>&quot;But I can’t just sit here while my husband is out there. I’m not asking you to risk your life for him—not unless you choose to. But please... at least tell me what I need to do. I’m willing to do anything for you. Just please save my husband.&quot;</p>

<p>Ross studied her for a long moment, the faint steam from his coffee curling upward between them.</p>

<p>Something flickered in his eyes—respect, maybe. Or curiosity.</p>

<p>Then he leaned back slightly, setting the mug down on the counter.</p>

<p>&quot;You’re serious,&quot; he said at last.</p>

<p>Miku nodded. &quot;I am.&quot;</p>

<p>Ross was silent for a few seconds longer. Then, finally, he sighed.</p>

<p>&quot;Alright, Miku,&quot; Ross said, a faint smirk tugging at the corner of his lips.</p>

<p>&quot;If you’re that determined to convince me to go back out there and save your husband, then you’re going to have to do a lot more convincing.&quot;</p>

<p>Miku froze. Her chest constricted as a cold wave of fear and helplessness washed over her.</p>

<p>For a brief moment, she felt the walls of the house close in, the dim light of the room pressing down like a weight.</p>

<p>Her mind raced—Ace was out there, in their home or somewhere, and she was trapped here, unable to reach him.</p>

<p>She had thought Ross was different, a protector in a world that offered none.</p>

<p>&quot;I thought... I thought you were different from those men, Ross,&quot; she spat, her voice trembling with anger and disbelief.</p>

<p>&quot;I thought you were better. Upright, good... someone who actually cared. But you’re... you’re just the same trash as them!&quot;</p>

<p>Her words struck the room with the weight of her disappointment.</p>

<p>Every syllable carried her heartbreak, every glance reflected the hope she had once placed in him, now shattered.</p>

<p>Her hands clenched into fists, nails digging into her palms, as if to anchor herself against the swirling storm of her emotions.</p>

<p>Ross didn’t flinch. He leaned back in his chair, arms crossed, his gaze finally leaving the flickering TV.</p>

<p>His expression remained calm—cold, almost detached.</p>

<p>Then he shrugged, a simple motion that somehow carried both dismissal and indifference.</p>

<p>&quot;You might be right,&quot; he said slowly, his tone eerily even.</p>

<p>&quot;And honestly? I don’t care. My door’s open—you can leave anytime. I’m not forcing you to do anything. It’s you who’s been bothering me.&quot;</p>

<p>He turned back to the television, flipping the channel with a practiced motion.</p>

<p>The screen flickered, showing burning cities, abandoned cars, and the endless hordes of zombies wandering aimlessly through streets once filled with life.</p>

<p>The world outside was chaos, destruction, and death.</p>

<p>The stark contrast between the calm in the room and the devastation on the screen made Miku feel even smaller, even more powerless.</p>

<p>Miku’s hands fell to her sides.</p>

<p>Her chest heaved, heavy with frustration, despair, and a growing, gnawing realization: she had no one to rely on in this world except herself—and perhaps Ross, whether she wanted to admit it or not.</p>

<p>The coldness in his voice stung, but beneath it, she could sense a strange kind of clarity.</p>

<p>He wasn’t lying; he wouldn’t put himself at risk for someone he didn’t know.</p>

<p>That harsh truth settled over her like ice, yet somehow, it also gave her resolve.</p>

<p>Minutes passed in silence.</p>

<p>The only sounds were the soft hum of the television and the distant groans of the undead outside.</p>

<p>Miku’s eyes darted to the screen, to the flickering images of chaos, then back to Ross. She bit her lip, feeling a tear slip down her cheek, and then another.</p>

<p>Her pride battled her desperation, but ultimately, survival and hope won over embarrassment.</p>

<p>&quot;...Okay,&quot; she whispered at last, her voice barely audible.</p>

<p>Her shoulders slumped slightly, but her gaze remained steady, fixed on Ross.</p>

<p>She took a small, hesitant step forward, the floor creaking beneath her weight.</p>

<p>Each movement felt like a battle—her fear and her determination pulling in opposite directions.</p>

<p>Ross glanced at her, just once, before returning his attention to the screen.</p>

<p>His expression was unreadable, but there was a flicker—almost imperceptible—of something behind his eyes.</p>

<p>Interest? Curiosity? Respect?</p>

<p>Miku couldn’t tell.</p>

<p>She swallowed hard and moved closer, closing the distance between them.</p>

<p>The air in the room felt heavier now, thick with tension.</p>

<p>Her heartbeat drummed in her ears, mingling with the faint sound of the television.</p>

<p>She felt exposed, vulnerable, yet resolute.</p>

<p>&quot;Ross...&quot; she began again, her voice stronger this time, tinged with determination.</p>

<p>&quot;Tell me what you want me to do.&quot;</p>
