<p>&quot;Yes,&quot; Bella replied without hesitation.</p>

<p>Ross, on the other hand, merely nodded, looking far too relaxed for what lay ahead.</p>

<p>&quot;Just lead the way and I’ll be there,&quot; Ross answered with an easy smile, as if they were about to take a casual walk rather than sprint through a death zone.</p>

<p>Their figures burst forward in a blur, flashing through the dense forest at terrifying speed.</p>

<p>They dodged roots, leaped ravines, and skimmed over uneven ground without ever breaking stride.</p>

<p>The wind screamed past them, tugging at clothes and hair as the forest itself seemed to recoil from their passage.</p>

<p>Ahead of them sprawled a massive horde of zombies, hundreds—perhaps thousands—of decaying bodies shuffling aimlessly under the harsh daylight.</p>

<p>Low groans filled the air, and the stench of rot drifted toward them in waves.</p>

<p>Without exchanging words, the trio veered off course.</p>

<p>Speed alone wasn’t enough here—care and precision mattered just as much.</p>

<p>Part of him hoped—unreasonably—that Ross would finally show signs of strain, lagging behind or at least focusing seriously on keeping up.</p>

<p>Ross was practically lounging in midair, his body reclined as if resting on an invisible bed.</p>

<p>To make it even worse, he was calmly eating an apple, taking slow, deliberate bites, his expression relaxed and almost bored.</p>

<p>While Sebastian and Bella stayed sharp and alert, muscles tense and senses stretched to their limits, Ross looked like a king enjoying a leisurely ride.</p>

<p>That man... Sebastian thought grimly. He wasn’t just strong. He was showing off.</p>

<p>From the outside, it looked abandoned—just another forgotten structure in a dead world—but those who knew the signs could tell it was alive.</p>

<p>They were let in without the slightest hassle.</p>

<p>&quot;Red and Shadow are back!&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;No way... they actually made it back!&quot;</p>

<p>Voices overlapped, disbelief turning into laughter and cheers as people rushed out from rooms, hallways, and lookout posts.</p>

<p>Others ran forward without hesitation.</p>

<p>A few laughed loudly, unable to contain their relief. Others wiped at their eyes, tears streaming down weathered faces they no longer bothered to hide.</p>

<p>More than a week apart might not have meant much before the apocalypse, but now it felt like forever.</p>

<p>&quot;I’m glad you two are back!&quot; a big, broad-built man said as he stepped forward.</p>

<p>He didn’t care who saw him cry.</p>

<p>A full year into the apocalypse, they had all been pushed to their limits.</p>

<p>Every mission thinned their ranks a little more. Every loss made the base quieter, heavier, more haunted.</p>

<p>For a brief time, the base felt warmer, louder, and alive again. Laughter echoed through the corridors, and shoulders that had been weighed down by grief straightened just a little.</p>

<p>A few minutes later, the crowd finally noticed that Bella and Sebastian hadn’t returned alone.</p>

<p>&quot;He’s—&quot; Sebastian began, then paused, as if searching for the right words. &quot;He’s a friend. He’s the one who rescued us,&quot; he finished firmly.</p>

<p>Murmurs rippled through the group as curious eyes turned toward him, sizing him up from head to toe.</p>

<p>Bella shrugged lightly. &quot;That’s just the way he is.&quot;</p>

<p>Before long, Ross found himself being pulled into the group, receiving rough but friendly slaps on the back and earnest handshakes.</p>

<p>Ross might have been a stranger, but in that moment, he was already being treated like one of their own.</p>
