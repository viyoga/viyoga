<p>Unlike the day before—when his sudden appearance had caught her off guard—today, she greeted him with a calm, confident smile.</p>

<p>It wasn&#x27;t forced. It wasn&#x27;t defensive. It was the kind of smile that told a man like Thomas everything he needed to know: she had found her footing.</p>

<p>He noticed it instantly.</p>

<p>&quot;Hmm… this is unexpected,&quot; Thomas thought, narrowing his eyes slightly as he observed her.</p>

<p>He had always prided himself on being a good reader of people—expressions, body language, hidden motives.</p>

<p>And right now, Jade was giving off all the signs of a woman who couldn&#x27;t be easily swayed, much less manipulated.</p>

<p>Her back was straight, her gaze steady. There was no trace of hesitation in the way she carried herself.</p>

<p>In fact, she exuded the kind of certainty that only came from being thoroughly, utterly satisfied—in life, in love, in self.</p>

<p>Thomas almost frowned.</p>

<p>This was bad.</p>

<p>Very bad.</p>

<p>He had plans. Carefully crafted, patiently built plans involving her—her curiosity, her vulnerability, her attraction.</p>

<p>Yesterday, he&#x27;d felt her walls shake. He&#x27;d seen the flicker of something in her eyes—intrigue, maybe even temptation.</p>

<p>But today?</p>

<p>Today, she looked immune.</p>

<p>Someone marked her, he realized, a flicker of jealousy sparking in his chest.</p>

<p>And deep down, even before she spoke, Thomas knew who it was.</p>

<p>Ross.</p>

<p>&quot;A man who can satisfy more than twenty women at the same time is either an absolute monster or a genuine legend. Which one are you, Ross Oakley?&quot; Thomas mused inwardly, the corner of his mouth twitching in amusement.</p>

<p>The headlines, the rumors, the whispers — all of it swirled around the name Ross like smoke around fire.</p>

<p>But Thomas didn&#x27;t linger on such thoughts for long; they were distractions, and he had more pressing matters to attend to.</p>

<p>He turned his attention to the woman walking toward him.</p>

<p>Jade moved with the grace of a queen and the confidence of a CEO, her heels clicking purposefully against the marble floor.</p>

<p>Dressed in a sleek, tailored outfit that screamed power and elegance, she carried herself like a woman who knew exactly what she wanted — and exactly how to get it.</p>

<p>&quot;You look absolutely stunning today, Jade. Is this all for me? I must say, I&#x27;m quite flattered,&quot; Thomas said, flashing a charming smile as he rose from his seat.</p>

<p>His voice was smooth, calculated — a seasoned player testing the waters with practiced ease.</p>

<p>Jade gave a polite smile, one that didn&#x27;t quite reach her eyes.</p>

<p>&quot;Thank you, Thomas, but I&#x27;m not here to flirt,&quot; she replied, her tone firm yet not unkind.</p>

<p>&quot;Let&#x27;s go over the details of our contract. My lawyers will be here shortly. Are yours joining us?&quot;</p>

<p>Thomas waved a hand casually as he took his seat again. &quot;I don&#x27;t need a lawyer. I am a lawyer.&quot;</p>

<p>He wasn&#x27;t bluffing either. With a law degree from Harvard and a track record of ruthless litigation victories, Thomas&#x27;s legal acumen was as sharp as his reputation for violence.</p>

<p>His knowledge of contracts, clauses, and corporate loopholes was as deadly as the weapons he was rumored to carry beneath his designer suit.</p>

<p>Jade arched an eyebrow but didn&#x27;t push further.</p>

<p>&quot;Good for you,&quot; she said coolly. &quot;Now, if you&#x27;ll turn to page seven, I&#x27;d like to clarify the terms surrounding equity distribution.&quot;</p>

<p>The conversation shifted gears into full-on business mode. Legal jargon, financial terms, and strategic projections filled the room.</p>

<p>Both parties moved through the negotiations with sharp minds and sharper tongues, neither willing to yield an inch without reason.</p>

<p>Thomas was impressed. Jade wasn&#x27;t just beautiful — she was intelligent, prepared, and knew her company inside and out.</p>

<p>About thirty minutes later, Jade&#x27;s lawyers entered the room — two men and a woman in dark suits, carrying leather briefcases and exuding the stiff aura of corporate precision.</p>

<p>They exchanged greetings, reviewed the paperwork Thomas had already annotated, and cross-checked every number, clause, and signature.</p>

<p>Eventually, everything was in order.</p>

<p>Hands were shaken, contracts were signed, and the deal was done: a one-billion-dollar investment from Thomas into Jade&#x27;s rapidly growing clothing company — a brand that had already made waves across the US and was poised to expand globally.</p>

<p>As the meeting concluded and the others began to pack up, Thomas leaned slightly toward Jade, a glint of admiration in his eyes.</p>

<p>&quot;You know,&quot; he said, &quot;I&#x27;ve met a lot of businesswomen, but very few could keep up with me the way you just did.&quot;</p>

<p>Jade smiled, finally letting a spark of genuine warmth flicker across her face. &quot;That&#x27;s because most of them weren&#x27;t me.&quot;</p>

<p>Thomas chuckled, standing as she did. &quot;I&#x27;m starting to see that.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;Since everything&#x27;s signed and sealed, I&#x27;d say that calls for a celebration,&quot; Thomas said, his voice like silk draped over steel.</p>

<p>&quot;A few drinks with a new partner—seems fitting, don&#x27;t you think?&quot;</p>

<p>He leaned back slightly in his chair, watching her with that signature confidence that came from years of getting what he wanted — in the courtroom, on the streets, and behind closed doors.</p>

<p>Thomas wasn&#x27;t just inviting her for drinks.</p>

<p>He was probing, testing her again, trying to see if the embers from yesterday&#x27;s tension still burned beneath her polished exterior.</p>

<p>Jade didn&#x27;t respond right away. Her eyes lingered on the signed documents before her, then slowly rose to meet his.</p>

<p>She didn&#x27;t flinch, didn&#x27;t blush, didn&#x27;t avert her gaze. But she was thinking — weighing her options with the precision of a chess master mid-match.</p>

<p>Thomas could practically hear the gears turning in her head, calculating the risk versus reward.</p>

<p>She was guarded, careful. But he&#x27;d seen the flicker — that half-second delay in her breath, the slight shift in her posture.</p>

<p>Subtle, but there. She wasn&#x27;t immune to him. Not completely.</p>

<p>Not yet.</p>

<p>Though the pull wasn&#x27;t as intense as it had been yesterday, it hadn&#x27;t disappeared either.</p>

<p>That much Thomas knew.</p>

<p>It was the same look he&#x27;d seen a hundred times before in women trying to convince themselves they were above his game.</p>

<p>He hadn&#x27;t met one who truly was.</p>

<p>Maybe someday — but not today.</p>
