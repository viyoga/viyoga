<p>Cyrus Thorn tried again.</p>

<p>He extended his divine sense into it, attempting to form a connection, to establish even the faintest resonance.</p>

<p>Still nothing. It was like trying to command the sun by touching its surface. Useless.</p>

<p>Cyrus slowly lowered his hand, his expression darkening. The anger did not explode. It condensed.</p>

<p>He had lived long enough to know that blind rage solved nothing.</p>

<p>His eyes narrowed slightly as his thoughts shifted away from the World Heart and toward himself.</p>

<p>If he could not control it, then the issue was not the artifact... it was the chain binding him.</p>

<p>A faint pressure began to rise from his body—not the overwhelming aura of the Bone Emperor that the outside world feared, but something deeper.</p>

<p>Something more focused. Like a blade being slowly drawn from its sheath. He exhaled once, long and steady.</p>

<p>&quot;I won’t remain like this,&quot; he said. Not a vow shouted to the heavens. Not a declaration meant for others.</p>

<p>Just a simple truth spoken to himself.</p>

<p>Because Cyrus Thorn had not survived until now by accepting being a puppet.</p>

<p>He would persevere down to his last breath.</p>

<p>***</p>

<p>Meanwhile, the survivors of Earth were dealing with a completely different kind of nightmare.</p>

<p>&quot;Look... what is that beast?!&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;Where the hell are we?!&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;Are those dinosaurs flying in the sky?!&quot;</p>

<p>Panic spread through the crowd like wildfire.</p>

<p>Over a million humans had survived Earth’s destruction, a number that once seemed miraculous until one remembered that Earth had once held billions.</p>

<p>What remained now felt less like a civilization and more like scattered remnants of a broken world.</p>

<p>And they were no longer on Earth.</p>

<p>They stood within an unfamiliar, boundless magical forest, a place where the trees towered so high they seemed to pierce the clouds.</p>

<p>The air itself felt different... thicker, alive with strange energy.</p>

<p>Every breath carried an unknown pressure, as though the world itself was watching them.</p>

<p>Monstrous roars echoed in the distance. Massive shadows moved between the ancient trunks.</p>

<p>Some creatures had wings like dragons, others had limbs that bent in unnatural directions, and a few even seemed to float without touching the ground at all.</p>

<p>People huddled together in tight groups, fear tightening their voices.</p>

<p>&quot;This... this can’t be real...&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;We were just on Earth yesterday!&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;Is this some kind of mass hallucination?&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;Where are the zombies?&quot;</p>

<p>But the confusion soon gave way to something sharper... dread.</p>

<p>Because this was no illusion. This was real.</p>

<p>&quot;Look! There’s a man flying in the sky... he’s riding a sword!&quot;</p>

<p>That single shout shattered the fragile calm that had begun to form.</p>

<p>Every head snapped upward at once.</p>

<p>At first, many refused to believe it. It sounded absurd, even in this impossible world.</p>

<p>But then they saw it.</p>

<p>A streak of light tore across the sky, slicing through the clouds with incredible speed.</p>

<p>It curved gracefully, circling once before locking onto their position.</p>

<p>The light slowed.</p>

<p>And descended.</p>

<p>BOOM!</p>

<p>A burst of wind erupted as the flying sword halted abruptly above the crowd, hovering in place as if gravity itself no longer applied to it.</p>

<p>Dust and leaves spiraled outward from the impact, forcing people to shield their faces.</p>

<p>When the wind finally settled, silence fell like a heavy curtain.</p>

<p>Standing atop the suspended blade was an old man.</p>

<p>He wore simple, dark robes that fluttered slightly in the lingering air currents.</p>

<p>His posture was upright, almost rigid, as though even time itself could not bend his spine.</p>

<p>His long white hair was tied back neatly, and his expression was carved from stone... cold, stern, and deeply serious.</p>

<p>His eyes swept over the crowd. Not with curiosity. Not with sympathy.</p>

<p>But with the detached scrutiny of someone assessing something far beneath him.</p>

<p>The pressure that radiated from him alone made several people instinctively step back.</p>

<p>A few even stumbled, unable to endure the suffocating aura pressing down on their shoulders.</p>

<p>He did not speak immediately.</p>

<p>Instead, he looked at them as if confirming something... counting, judging, measuring.</p>

<p>Only after a long moment did he finally open his mouth.</p>

<p>And when he did, his voice was calm... but carried an authority that made even the air feel heavier.</p>

<p>&quot;A manga tay ea mow ta none!&quot;</p>

<p>The old man spoke in a strange, unfamiliar tongue.</p>

<p>Yet, to everyone’s shock, the words were perfectly understood in their minds as if they had always known the language.</p>

<p>It was clearly some form of magic... an unseen force that translated his speech directly into meaning within their consciousness.</p>

<p>The survivors fell silent immediately. No one dared to interrupt.</p>

<p>Even those who had been shouting moments ago now stood rigid, eyes fixed on the old man with fear and confusion.</p>

<p>He slowly lowered his gaze over the crowd as if inspecting livestock.</p>

<p>&quot;You are here in the Ghost Wood Forest,&quot; he said coldly. &quot;Follow me, and I will escort you to your new home.&quot;</p>

<p>His tone was flat, almost indifferent, but beneath it was something far more unsettling... disdain.</p>

<p>He looked at the people of Earth as though they were no more significant than insects crawling at his feet.</p>

<p>In truth, he could not understand why his master had ordered such a task.</p>

<p>Why would someone of such overwhelming power bother with these fragile, useless mortals?</p>

<p>In his eyes, they were weak beyond measure, their very presence almost laughable.</p>

<p>Still, he did not question it.</p>

<p>Obedience to his master was absolute.</p>

<p>Without waiting for a response, the old man turned and stepped onto his flying sword again.</p>

<p>It hovered just above the ground, waiting.</p>

<p>The crowd hesitated for a moment... fear, confusion, and uncertainty clashing within them.</p>

<p>But with no better choice, they slowly began to follow.</p>

<p>For nearly thirty minutes, they were led through the endless expanse of the Ghost Wood Forest.</p>

<p>The deeper they went, the more surreal the world became.</p>

<p>Trees twisted in unnatural shapes, their bark etched with faint glowing patterns.</p>

<p>Strange beasts occasionally watched them from the shadows before disappearing without a sound.</p>
