<p>The group piled into the truck, the excitement palpable as they drove through the countryside.</p>

<p>The lake wasn’t far, but the ride was filled with chatter and laughter.</p>

<p>When they finally arrived, the crystal-clear water sparkled under the afternoon sun, the surrounding trees casting cool patches of shade along the shoreline.</p>

<p>The moment they stepped out of the truck, everyone sprang into action—laying out picnic blankets, setting up a small grill, and dragging coolers down toward the water’s edge.</p>

<p>Ross, however, was momentarily distracted.</p>

<p>As the girls shed their outer clothes, revealing their swimsuits underneath, he let out a low whistle of appreciation.</p>

<p>Sandra’s bikini hugged her curves perfectly, the fabric clinging to her body in a way that left little to the imagination.</p>

<p>Amy wasn’t far behind, her one-piece suit accentuating her long legs and toned figure.</p>

<p>Even the other girls looked stunning, their sun-kissed skin glowing under the bright sky.</p>

<p>But his eyes were plastered mostly on Diane and Adele’s body whom he has not gotten the chance to explore yet.</p>

<p>&quot;Damn,&quot; Ross murmured, a slow smirk forming on his lips.</p>

<p>&quot;You sure know how to make a guy appreciate summer.&quot;</p>

<p>Sandra turned slightly, placing a hand on her hip.</p>

<p>&quot;Like what you see?&quot; she teased, her eyes sparkling with mischief.</p>

<p>Ross chuckled. &quot;More than you know.&quot;</p>

<p>Amy rolled her eyes but couldn’t hide her amused smile. &quot;You’re such a dog.&quot;</p>

<p>Ross merely grinned. &quot;And you love it.&quot;</p>

<p>With that, the group made their way down to the water, eager to escape the heat.</p>

<p>Some immediately dove in, while others waded in slowly, letting the cool water wash over their sun-warmed skin.</p>

<p>The sounds of splashing and playful shrieks filled the air as the afternoon fun officially began.</p>

<p>Ross stretched his arms above his head, letting out a contented sigh.</p>

<p>The day was just getting started, and he had a feeling it was going to be very interesting.</p>

<p>They hadn’t even finished lunch when Ross’s gut feeling proved right.</p>

<p>Screech!</p>

<p>A white van came to a sudden stop near their parked vehicle, its tires skidding over the dirt and kicking up a cloud of dust.</p>

<p>The once-lively picnic atmosphere was shattered in an instant.</p>

<p>Birds took flight from the surrounding trees, startled by the abrupt arrival, and a tense silence fell over the group.</p>

<p>Before anyone could react, the van’s side door slid open with a metallic clang, and ten large, rough-looking men spilled out.</p>

<p>Their appearances alone set off every alarm bell—greasy hair, tattooed arms, ragged clothes, and leering expressions.</p>

<p>They moved with the confidence of seasoned criminals, men who had done this before.</p>

<p>Ross’s jaw relaxed in amusement.</p>

<p>Trouble. And a great opportunity for him.</p>

<p>&quot;Well, well, well… what do we have here, boys?&quot; The leader stepped forward, a grin stretching across his pockmarked face.</p>

<p>He was the ugliest of them all, with a scar running down his left cheek and a crooked nose that had clearly been broken more than once.</p>

<p>He lifted a pistol into the air and fired a single shot toward the sky.</p>

<p>Bang!</p>

<p>The crack of gunfire echoed across the lake, the sound ricocheting off the water and trees.</p>

<p>Gasps of fear erupted from the group.</p>

<p>Adele instinctively pulled her daughters closer, her face draining of color.</p>

<p>Ross remained still. His fingers curled slightly into his side, but his face showed no panic.</p>

<p>One of the goons, a thick-necked man with a missing tooth, let out a low whistle as his eyes roamed over Adele and her daughters.</p>

<p>&quot;Pussy, boss! Four nice pussies!&quot; he sneered, licking his lips.</p>

<p>Ross’s gaze darkened.</p>

<p>&quot;Pussy indeed! Hahaha!&quot; The boss let out a bark of laughter, slapping his thigh.</p>

<p>&quot;I didn’t think we’d see such fine women during our little vacation.&quot; His tone dripped with amusement, but there was an underlying cruelty in his voice, the kind that sent a chill down the spine.</p>

<p>These weren’t just random thugs.</p>

<p>They were part of a notorious kidnap-for-ransom gang, well-known in Chicago’s underworld.</p>

<p>Over the past year, they had carried out a string of high-profile abductions, demanding millions from terrified families.</p>

<p>But after the authorities finally caught wind of their operations, they had gone into hiding, retreating to the countryside to escape law enforcement.</p>

<p>And now, by sheer coincidence, they had stumbled upon fresh prey—three beautiful women in an isolated location, far from the safety of civilization.</p>

<p>Sandra’s breath hitched as she clutched onto Amy’s wrist. Amy, usually so composed, swallowed hard.</p>

<p>Adele, though visibly shaken, squared her shoulders in a futile attempt to mask her fear.</p>

<p>The goons spread out, forming a loose semicircle around them.</p>

<p>Their body language was relaxed—too relaxed.</p>

<p>They weren’t expecting a fight. In their minds, this was already over.</p>

<p>Ross slowly placed his drink down on the picnic blanket. His movements were measured, deliberate.</p>

<p>He leaned back slightly, as if still at ease, but his sharp eyes scanned every detail.</p>

<p>The way the leader held his gun—sloppy, overly confident.</p>

<p>The number of men—ten, all within range.</p>

<p>The distance between him and the nearest attacker—about three steps.</p>

<p>His mind worked quickly, assessing, planning.</p>

<p>They had no idea who they were dealing with.</p>

<p>Not yet.</p>

<p>But they were about to find out.</p>

<p>Still he could not help but formulate a plan of his own. His smile widened as he liked what he saw.</p>

<p>There was so much to innovate here and ultimately serve as an advantage to him in the end.</p>

<p>&quot;Perfect.&quot; Ross thought inside as he finalized his scheme.</p>

<p>The air was thick with tension, the kind that made every breath feel like a struggle.</p>

<p>The leader of the armed men stood at the center of the chaos, his eyes wild and his voice booming like thunder.</p>

<p>His grip on the pistol was steady, but the madness in his gaze betrayed his desperation.</p>

<p>&quot;Nobody move, or the next bullet goes straight through your heads!&quot; he roared, his voice cutting through the silence like a blade.</p>

<p>&quot;You think I’m playing? It’s gonna get real messy, real quick, if you don’t start cooperating!&quot;</p>
