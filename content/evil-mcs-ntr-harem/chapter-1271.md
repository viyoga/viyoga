<p>&quot;Brandon,&quot; Ross called calmly.</p>

<p>&quot;Master.&quot;</p>

<p>Brandon emerged from the shadows without making a sound.</p>

<p>One moment, the corner behind Ross was empty.</p>

<p>The next, the giant man was standing there with his hands clasped behind his back.</p>

<p>Since they were alone, Brandon used the proper form of address.</p>

<p>In public, he always called Ross &quot;Boss&quot; to avoid unnecessary attention.</p>

<p>Behind closed doors, however, there was only one title he would ever use.</p>

<p>Master.</p>

<p>Ross smiled faintly.</p>

<p>&quot;Kindly welcome our guests. Make sure you show them our hospitality.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;As you wish, Master.&quot;</p>

<p>Brandon bowed his head.</p>

<p>Without another word, his body faded into the darkness.</p>

<p>The room became silent once more.</p>

<p>Ross leisurely poured himself another cup of tea.</p>

<p>Far away, the three men continued their discussion about what they found in Falling Leaf City.</p>

<p>Crack.</p>

<p>A twig snapped. The sound was tiny. Yet all three men instantly spun around.</p>

<p>Years of life-and-death experience had sharpened their instincts. Their eyes narrowed.</p>

<p>A giant figure stood several meters away.</p>

<p>The moonlight illuminated Brandon’s broad frame, making him appear less like a man and more like an ancient beast.</p>

<p>&quot;Who are you?&quot; one of the cultivators demanded.</p>

<p>No answer came. Brandon simply stared at them. The silence made their skin crawl.</p>

<p>&quot;Speak!&quot;</p>

<p>The strongest among them took a step forward. The moment he did... Brandon vanished.</p>

<p>The man’s pupils contracted. Too fast. His eyes couldn’t track the movement.</p>

<p>His mind couldn’t track the movement.</p>

<p>By the time he realized what had happened, Brandon was already standing behind him.</p>

<p>A sharp pain erupted in his chest. The man slowly lowered his head.</p>

<p>His eyes widened. A large hole had appeared where his heart should have been.</p>

<p>Several feet away, Brandon stood holding the still-beating organ in his hand.</p>

<p>The man opened his mouth. No words came out. Only blood. His knees buckled.</p>

<p>Moments later, his body crashed into the ground.</p>

<p>Dead.</p>

<p>The remaining two cultivators felt cold sweat pouring down their backs.</p>

<p>&quot;Attack together!&quot;</p>

<p>Both men erupted with spiritual energy. One drew a shining sword.</p>

<p>The other unleashed dozens of flaming talismans. The night sky lit up with brilliant colors.</p>

<p>The sword cultivator struck first.</p>

<p>His blade flashed toward Brandon’s neck with enough force to split a mountain.</p>

<p>Brandon didn’t dodge. He simply raised one hand.</p>

<p>Clang!</p>

<p>The sword stopped. The cultivator’s eyes nearly bulged from their sockets.</p>

<p>Brandon had caught the blade between two fingers.</p>

<p>The supposedly indestructible spiritual weapon trembled violently.</p>

<p>Then Brandon squeezed.</p>

<p>Crack!</p>

<p>The sword shattered into countless fragments.</p>

<p>&quot;What?!&quot;</p>

<p>Before the man could retreat, Brandon’s hand shot forward and seized him by the face.</p>

<p>The cultivator screamed.</p>

<p>Brandon lifted him into the air as though he weighed nothing.</p>

<p>The man desperately punched, kicked, and unleashed techniques.</p>

<p>Nothing worked.</p>

<p>Brandon’s grip never loosened.</p>

<p>&quot;Impossible! Impossible!&quot;</p>

<p>The man’s voice trembled with terror.</p>

<p>For the first time in years, he felt true fear. Brandon looked into his eyes. Then he tightened his grip.</p>

<p>The cultivator’s scream abruptly ended.</p>

<p>His body went limp.</p>

<p>Brandon casually tossed the corpse aside like discarded trash.</p>

<p>The final man witnessed everything.</p>

<p>His courage shattered.</p>

<p>&quot;Monster!&quot;</p>

<p>He turned and fled.</p>

<p>Every ounce of spiritual energy within his body exploded forth as he desperately tried to escape.</p>

<p>His figure became a streak of light racing across the horizon. Unfortunately for him...</p>

<p>Brandon was faster. A dark blur flashed across the night.</p>

<p>The fleeing cultivator suddenly sensed a presence beside him. His heart nearly stopped.</p>

<p>Brandon was running next to him. Keeping pace effortlessly.</p>

<p>The giant man turned his head and looked at him. The man nearly lost control of his flight technique.</p>

<p>&quot;No... no... please...&quot;</p>

<p>For the first time in dozens of years, he begged.</p>

<p>Brandon remained silent. His hand shot forward. The cultivator felt the world spin. The stars rotated.</p>

<p>The moon flipped upside down. Only then did he realize he was falling.</p>

<p>His vision dimmed.</p>

<p>His final sight was Brandon standing above him like an immovable mountain.</p>

<p>Then darkness consumed everything. Three corpses lay scattered across the ground.</p>

<p>Not a single one had managed to escape.</p>

<p>A few moments later, Brandon vanished into the shadows.</p>

<p>When he returned to Ross, there wasn’t a single drop of blood on his clothing.</p>

<p>&quot;Master,&quot; Brandon said calmly.</p>

<p>Ross took a sip of tea.</p>

<p>&quot;Our guests enjoyed the hospitality?&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;Yes, Master.&quot;</p>

<p>Ross smiled.</p>

<p>&quot;Excellent.&quot;</p>

<p>Ross paused for a brief moment, letting his words settle into the silence of the room. Then he spoke.</p>

<p>&quot;Let the people know we’ll only stay here for a month. After that... we’ll have a castle to conquer.&quot;</p>

<p>There was no hesitation in his tone. No doubt.</p>

<p>Only quiet certainty, as if he were stating something already decided by fate itself.</p>

<p>&quot;Understood, Master.&quot;</p>

<p>Brandon’s voice was flat, disciplined.</p>

<p>Without another word, his presence blurred at the edges.</p>

<p>The shadows around him seemed to swallow him whole, and in the next instant, he was gone and vanished without a trace, as though he had never existed in the first place.</p>

<p>Silence returned to the room.</p>

<p>Ross remained seated, fingers lightly resting on the arm of his chair.</p>

<p>The world outside continued on, unaware that something had just shifted in its balance.</p>

<p>Slowly, he closed his eyes.</p>

<p>His divine sense expanded outward. The world unfolded in his mind like a living map.</p>

<p>And then, he saw it. A kingdom. Ross’s lips curved slightly. He pushed his senses deeper.</p>

<p>Power structures revealed themselves like peeling layers of an onion. So many cracks.</p>

<p>So many weaknesses waiting to be pressed. Ross exhaled slowly, as if savoring the discovery.</p>

<p>&quot;This place...&quot; he murmured under his breath. &quot;It’s already halfway broken.&quot;</p>

<p>A faint glint appeared in his eyes.</p>

<p>Not excitement in the childish sense but the calm satisfaction of a strategist seeing a board perfectly arranged for his hand.</p>

<p>He leaned forward slightly and focused more on the women that he might conquer.</p>

<p>He was not at all disappointed in what he found.</p>

<p>Ross opened his eyes again, the faintest smile lingering on his face.</p>

<p>&quot;I’m going to enjoy this so much.&quot;</p>
