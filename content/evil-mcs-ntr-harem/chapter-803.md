<p>Cara took a deep breath. &quot;I... I think I’ve found someone I like. I didn’t mean for it to happen. I really didn’t.&quot;</p>

<p>Corey’s face darkened, but he didn’t speak yet.</p>

<p>&quot;It’s Big D, isn’t it?&quot; he said after a moment, his voice bitter.</p>

<p>She didn’t speak, but her silence confirmed it. Her hands trembled slightly in her lap.</p>

<p>&quot;Unbelievable.&quot; Corey scoffed and stood up. &quot;It’s Big D. Of course it is. It’s always him.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;I didn’t plan this,&quot; she said quickly, standing too. &quot;I just... it happened. The more time I spent with him... the more I realized—&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;You realized I was just a placeholder?&quot; Corey snapped, his voice rising.</p>

<p>&quot;No!&quot; she said quickly, stepping forward. &quot;That’s not it. I liked you. I really did. But with D, it’s different. I don’t even understand it fully. I just know that if I don’t follow what I’m feeling, I’ll regret it.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;Regret,&quot; Corey muttered, half-laughing in disbelief. &quot;You know what I regret? Thinking you were different.&quot;</p>

<p>He shook his head and walked away, his footsteps loud and angry on the stone path.</p>

<p>She watched him disappear into the house, her own heart breaking under the weight of her decision.</p>

<p>A tear slipped down her cheek. She wiped it away quickly.</p>

<p>Inside the house, things were different now. People could sense the shift.</p>

<p>Even Heaven gave Cara a curious look the next morning during breakfast.</p>

<p>Tianna nudged her, whispering something about drama, but Cara wasn’t in the mood to laugh.</p>

<p>Later that day, Ross found her by the pool. She was alone, legs dipped in the water, deep in thought.</p>

<p>&quot;Rough night?&quot; he asked casually.</p>

<p>She looked up, surprised he approached her so soon. &quot;Yeah... you could say that.&quot;</p>

<p>He sat beside her, silent for a while before speaking again. &quot;I heard.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;Of course you did,&quot; she said quietly.</p>

<p>He turned toward her, his voice softer than usual. &quot;So what now?&quot;</p>

<p>Cara glanced at him. &quot;I don’t know. I told Corey the truth. That was the hardest part. I’m not proud of hurting him.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;You didn’t lie,&quot; Ross replied. &quot;You followed your heart. In this place, that’s rare.&quot;</p>

<p>She gave him a small, sad smile. &quot;You make it sound noble.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;It is,&quot; he said simply. &quot;Now the only thing left is to figure out what we are.&quot;</p>

<p>Cara didn’t answer right away.</p>

<p>She stared at Ross, her heartbeat loud in her ears, her thoughts racing with every possible consequence of the words she was about to say.</p>

<p>The lights in the house were dimmed for the evening, casting warm shadows across his face, but to her, he seemed to shine brighter than anyone else.</p>

<p>&quot;You know I like you, D,&quot; she said at last, her voice trembling slightly—not from fear, but from the weight of what she was finally admitting out loud.</p>

<p>Ross looked at her, a slow smile forming on his lips.</p>

<p>&quot;That’s what I heard,&quot; he said casually, cocking his head slightly. &quot;And honestly, it’s not unusual. Every girl likes me. No one’s ever managed to resist my charm.&quot;</p>

<p>Cara gave a small laugh—half-annoyed, half-endearing.</p>

<p>That was the Ross everyone knew: smug, confident, unshakable.</p>

<p>But she needed something more than bravado now.</p>

<p>&quot;And then what?&quot; she asked, taking a step closer. &quot;If I come to your side... if I give in to this feeling, what then? That makes five women, D. Five beautiful, intelligent, capable women all drawn to you. What are your plans for us? Is this just a phase, or do you actually know what you’re doing?&quot;</p>

<p>Her voice was firm now, steady. She wasn’t looking for promises wrapped in silver words.</p>

<p>She was asking for the truth, whatever that looked like.</p>

<p>Ross’s smile faded into something more thoughtful.</p>

<p>He ran his thumb along his jawline as he regarded her, eyes narrowing not in judgment but in reflection.</p>

<p>&quot;You want the truth?&quot; he asked quietly. &quot;Then here it is.&quot;</p>

<p>He stepped closer to her, so close she could feel the heat radiating from his body.</p>

<p>&quot;I didn’t plan this. I never came into this house expecting to care about anyone—especially not five women who could each destroy me in their own ways.&quot;</p>

<p>Cara looked at him, eyes wide. He continued.</p>

<p>&quot;But here I am. And I do care. About each of you. You’re not just a number to me, Cara. None of you are. I don’t see you as trophies or background characters. I see strength, passion, individuality. And yes... I want to keep all of you close. I want to protect you. I want to love you—each of you in your own way. And if that makes me a fool, then fine. I’ll be the happiest fool alive.&quot;</p>

<p>He took her hand gently, fingers brushing hers. &quot;This isn’t some fantasy to me. I want to build something real—even if the world doesn’t understand it. But I can’t do it alone. I need you with me. Will you trust me, Cara?&quot;</p>

<p>Cara felt something bloom in her chest—hope, fear, excitement, desire—all tangled together. She didn’t answer immediately.</p>

<p>Her mind was a whirlwind, but her heart... her heart already knew.</p>

<p>She finally gave him a slow nod. &quot;Then I guess I’m yours,&quot; she whispered, her voice barely audible. &quot;But don’t you dare break my heart, D.&quot;</p>

<p>Ross leaned in and pressed a soft kiss to her forehead. &quot;Never,&quot; he whispered back. &quot;You’re too precious for that.&quot;</p>

<p>They stood there in silence for a moment, the house quiet around them, the air humming with a tension that was no longer uncertain—but filled with possibility.</p>

<p>From a distance, someone might have seen just two contestants whispering in the corner.</p>

<p>But for Cara, this was the moment everything changed.</p>

<p>And she didn’t regret a thing.</p>

<p>Later that night, Cara found herself alone with Ross on his bed.</p>

<p>The other four girls had retreated to the women’s room, leaving the two of them in quiet isolation.</p>

<p>The air was thick with anticipation, and Cara’s heart raced as she realized—tonight was the night she would fully give herself to him.</p>

<p>Tonight, Ross would make her his woman.</p>
