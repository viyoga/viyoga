<p>Maya&#x27;s jaw tightened. She said nothing, but her glare spoke volumes.</p>

<p>The gang leader chuckled, a deep, guttural sound that sent a ripple of unease through the room.</p>

<p>&quot;It&#x27;s payback time, doll. And I&#x27;ve got a little job for you.&quot; He paused, savoring the moment. &quot;You&#x27;re going to call that boyfriend of yours, and you&#x27;re going to tell him to drag his ass down here. Oh, and make sure he brings all his little girlfriends with him. I hear he&#x27;s got quite the collection.&quot; His grin widened, his gold tooth flashing again. &quot;If they&#x27;re half as pretty as you, I&#x27;m sure we&#x27;ll find... uses for them.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;Go to hell, Nico Cross,&quot; Maya spat, her voice sharp and defiant.</p>

<p>She knew this was the leader of the Black Cross Gang, a man deeply entrenched in the city&#x27;s underworld, with his hands in countless shady dealings.</p>

<p>Nico&#x27;s grin didn&#x27;t falter. If anything, her resistance seemed to amuse him. He spread his thick arms, gesturing to the room around him.</p>

<p>&quot;Hell, huh? Sweetheart, this is my heaven. And pretty soon, it&#x27;s going to be yours, too.&quot;</p>

<p>The gang behind him snickered, their laughter low and mocking, but Nico silenced them with a single raised hand.</p>

<p>He stepped closer to Maya, his heavy boots thudding against the floor with deliberate slowness.</p>

<p>&quot;You&#x27;ve got some fire, doll. I like that. It&#x27;s going to make breaking you all the more satisfying.&quot;</p>

<p>Maya refused to flinch, though every instinct screamed at her to step back.</p>

<p>Unfortunately, the chair held her firmly in place, leaving her no room to escape.</p>

<p>Her nails dug into her palms as she clenched her fists, drawing on every ounce of her willpower to keep her composure.</p>

<p>Nico leaned in, his face inches from hers. His breath reeked of tobacco and cheap liquor, and his small, beady eyes gleamed with sadistic pleasure.</p>

<p>&quot;You know, I think I&#x27;ll start with that smart mouth of yours,&quot; he murmured. &quot;Pretty soon, you&#x27;ll be begging to use it for something else entirely.&quot;</p>

<p>Maya couldn&#x27;t hold back her rage any longer. &quot;Fuck you,&quot; she snapped, her voice cold and unwavering.</p>

<p>Nico threw his head back and laughed, a loud, grating sound that echoed through the room.</p>

<p>The gang behind him joined in, their cruel laughter filling the space like a sinister chorus.</p>

<p>&quot;You&#x27;ve got spirit,&quot; Nico said, his laughter subsiding into a smirk. &quot;I&#x27;ll give you that. But let&#x27;s see how long it lasts when the fun really begins.&quot;</p>

<p>He took another step closer, his massive hand reaching out as if to grab her, but he stopped short, letting the moment hang in the air.</p>

<p>He wanted to see her squirm, to watch her fight against the fear threatening to consume her.</p>

<p>He reached out, tilting her chin up with his thick fingers, forcing her to meet his gaze.</p>

<p>His beady eyes roamed over her face, taking in every detail with an unsettling intensity.</p>

<p>Her short hair framed her features in a way that only seemed to enhance her youthful appearance, making her look more vulnerable than she felt. To him, she was perfect.</p>

<p>&quot;With a face and body like yours,&quot; Nico mused, his lips curling into a twisted smirk, &quot;I&#x27;m almost hesitating to send you down to this whore den.&quot;</p>

<p>He waved his hand toward the far side of the room, where several women cowered in their beds.</p>

<p>They trembled visibly, their wide eyes darting between Nico and Maya as if reliving their own nightmares.</p>

<p>It was clear they had been awake, silently bearing witness to the unfolding drama.</p>

<p>Many of them were victims—kidnapped, coerced, or blackmailed into a life of forced prostitution.</p>

<p>The hopelessness in their expressions was a chilling reminder of what awaited Maya if she failed to escape.</p>

<p>Nico&#x27;s smirk widened as his gaze returned to her.</p>

<p>&quot;But then again,&quot; he continued, his tone laced with mockery, &quot;I think I&#x27;ll be the first in line to sample that sweet little body of yours.&quot;</p>

<p>He threw his head back and let out a loud, cruel laugh, the sound echoing through the room.</p>

<p>The other gang members chuckled along, their leering faces adding to the oppressive atmosphere.</p>

<p>Maya&#x27;s stomach churned, but she forced herself to remain defiant, her glare never wavering.</p>

<p>She couldn&#x27;t let him see the fear she felt clawing at her insides.</p>

<p>Maya&#x27;s eyes darted to the gang, searching for any sign of hesitation or weakness among them, but there was none.</p>

<p>These men were killers, hardened by years of violence and crime. Whatever humanity they might have once had was long gone.</p>

<p>Nico turned back to her, his smirk widening.</p>

<p>&quot;Here&#x27;s how this is going to work, doll. You call your boy, or I start breaking things. Maybe those pretty little fingers of yours. Or maybe I start with someone else you may care about. Some of these broken sluts for example.&quot;</p>

<p>Maya&#x27;s mind raced, her heart pounding like a drum.</p>

<p>She knew what Nico was capable of, knew the kind of horrors he and his gang could unleash.</p>

<p>But she also knew she couldn&#x27;t give him what he wanted.</p>

<p>Nico leaned in closer, his voice dropping to a whisper. &quot;The clock&#x27;s ticking, doll. Don&#x27;t make me lose my patience.&quot;</p>

<p>Still, as Maya thought of Ross—someone just as dangerous, if not more mysterious than Nico—her nerves began to steady.</p>

<p>The memory of Ross&#x27;s unpredictable nature and obvious connections gave her a sliver of confidence.</p>

<p>The memory of those big men wearing demon masks still haunted her dreams from time to time.</p>

<p>Maya straightened slightly, masking her fear with a forced calmness.</p>

<p>&quot;My phone&#x27;s in my left pocket,&quot; she said, her voice steady but edged with warning. &quot;But don&#x27;t try anything funny. If you do, I won&#x27;t call my boyfriend, no matter how much you try to force me.&quot;</p>

<p>Nico studied her for a moment, a flicker of amusement in his dark eyes.</p>

<p>Then, with a smirk, he nodded toward the big man who had dragged her here.</p>

<p>&quot;Do it, Plum,&quot; Nico said, his tone casual yet commanding. &quot;And remember—no unnecessary touching. You and the boys will all get your turn once I&#x27;m done with this bitch.&quot;</p>

<p>Plum grinned, his thick fingers flexing eagerly. &quot;You&#x27;re the best, boss,&quot; he said with a chuckle before stepping forward to follow orders.</p>

<p>Maya kept her glare locked on Nico, refusing to give him the satisfaction of seeing her flinch as Plum reached into her pocket and retrieved the phone.</p>

<p>He held it up triumphantly, as though it were some kind of trophy, before tossing it to Nico.</p>

<p>Meanwhile, somewhere in the city, in the same dingy hotel where Ross had taken Mary, a phone began to buzz.</p>

<p>The sound cut through the chaotic room, filled with the lewd, wet noises of bodies colliding and muffled cries of extreme pleasure.</p>
