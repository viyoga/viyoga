<p>“It’s time.”</p>

<p>The words were simple, but they carried weight.</p>

<p>The kind of weight only shared understanding and years of quiet patience could give.</p>

<p>“I suppose it is,” one of the women replied, her voice soft yet steady.</p>

<p>“Ross has more than proven himself. Not just as a husband, but as a father too.”</p>

<p>“Agreed,” another nodded. “Alistair, his eldest, is already ten. And Ross treats him and all the other children the same—no favoritism, no distance. Just love. Steady, unshakable love.”</p>

<p>“He’s a good man,” the third said, almost in a whisper, as if voicing that truth aloud might make it more real, more sacred.</p>

<p>They were seated in a sunlit garden room, the windows thrown open to let in the crisp morning breeze.</p>

<p>Cups of tea sat forgotten on the table between them, steam curling lazily upward.</p>

<p>This wasn’t just a casual gathering.</p>

<p>It was a meeting of hearts, a moment of unity forged in silence and understanding.</p>

<p>Ross wasn’t with them—he didn’t know about this meeting, and that was the point.</p>

<p>This was for them. For the women who shared his life, his bed, his children.</p>

<p>For the women who had waited.</p>

<p>They had each walked their own path to get here.</p>

<p>Some had come into Ross’s life early, others later. Some through chance, others by fate.</p>

<p>But all of them had stayed—willingly, fiercely, because they saw the man beneath the legend.</p>

<p>The man who worked tirelessly to protect them, to provide for them, to love them in ways that defied explanation.</p>

<p>And now, they wanted to give something back.</p>

<p>“We’ve waited long enough,” one wife said, folding her hands on her lap.</p>

<p>“Ross has been so busy building for us, for the family. He’s given and given without ever asking for anything in return.”</p>

<p>Another wife nodded, her eyes warm. “It’s time we remind him that we see him. That we love him. Not just for what he does, but for who he is.”</p>

<p>The others murmured their agreement, a circle of women united not by competition, but by devotion—to him, and strangely, to each other.</p>

<p>There was no jealousy here, no rivalry. Only understanding.</p>

<p>And so the planning began.</p>

<p>It was to be a surprise. Something Ross wouldn’t see coming.</p>

<p>While he was occupied—likely in some high-level meeting or buried under the weight of paperwork—they would prepare everything.</p>

<p>The location, the timing, the details.</p>

<p>Every element had to be perfect. Not extravagant, but personal. Intimate.</p>

<p>A celebration not of power or wealth, but of family. Of love.</p>

<p>They spoke in hushed tones, excitement bubbling beneath their careful words.</p>

<p>Ideas were exchanged, laughter slipped in between memories, and slowly, a plan began to take shape.</p>

<p>It wasn’t just about throwing a party or giving him a gift—it was about creating a moment.</p>

<p>One that would speak to the heart of the man they all adored.</p>

<p>And all the while, they believed they were being discreet.</p>

<p>They believed Ross was completely unaware.</p>

<p>But what they didn’t know—what none of them realized—was that Ross had already sensed something.</p>

<p>He noticed the lingering glances, the hushed conversations that stopped when he entered the room.</p>

<p>The way they all seemed just a little more coordinated lately, a little more united in their secrecy.</p>

<p>He wasn’t a man easy to fool. And while he hadn’t pried or pressed, he’d been watching.</p>

<p>Quietly. Amused.</p>

<p>Let them think they’re surprising me, he thought with a rare smile.</p>

<p>Let them have their moment. I’ll pretend not to know. For them.</p>

<p>Because for all the power Ross Oakley held—for all the influence, the riches, the fear his name inspired in others—this was the part of his life that mattered most.</p>

<p>His family. His women. His children.</p>

<p>And as they worked together, plotting what they thought was a secret, Ross went about his day with a quiet warmth in his chest—knowing that, whatever they had planned, it was born from love.</p>

<p>And love, he would never take for granted.</p>

<p>***</p>

<p>One night, Ross returned home later than usual.</p>

<p>The sky outside was a canvas of deep velvet, dotted with stars, and the quiet hum of the city beyond his estate reminded him just how far he’d come.</p>

<p>He loosened his tie as he stepped out of his sleek black car, his body aching for the comfort of home, the warmth of familiarity.</p>

<p>What he didn’t expect—what he could never have prepared for—was the sight waiting just beyond the front door.</p>

<p>As he entered the foyer, the soft scent of jasmine and something sweeter—something unmistakably primal—hit him first.</p>

<p>He paused, brow furrowed, catching the edge of a sultry melody drifting from the living room.</p>

<p>The lights were dimmed, casting the space in a golden, ambient glow. And then he saw them.</p>

<p>All of his wives—every single one—stood together in the living room, a vision of beauty and seduction.</p>

<p>They were clad only in delicate lingerie: silk, lace, and whispers of fabric that barely covered their curves.</p>

<p>Each woman wore something different, tailored to her own sense of allure—Jasmine in crimson, Sophia in pale blue, Natalie in black with garters that clung like shadows.</p>

<p>Their eyes sparkled with mischief, desire, and love.</p>

<p>Ross stopped mid-step, stunned. “Well, well,” he drawled, lips curving into a slow grin. “Either I’ve died and gone to heaven… or you’ve all decided to make a man’s dreams come true.”</p>

<p>“We’ve been waiting for you,” Jasmine purred, sauntering forward on bare feet. Her fingers trailed down his chest, over the buttons of his shirt.</p>

<p>Ross let out a low laugh, already unfastening his cuffs. “I smell something in the air… something sweet. Excitement. Lust.”</p>

<p>He leaned in close, his voice a playful whisper. “I’d say someone—or several someones—are hungry tonight.”</p>

<p>Sophia giggled softly. “Maybe we are. Maybe we’ve had a meeting.”</p>

<p>Ross raised an eyebrow as he began removing his jacket. “A meeting?”</p>

<p>Natalie stepped forward then, lips painted a sultry red, her confidence magnetic. “A meeting about you.”</p>

<p>Ross’s grin widened. His shirt hit the floor. “Do tell.”</p>
