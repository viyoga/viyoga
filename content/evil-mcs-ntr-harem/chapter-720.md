<p>The night ended not with laughter or conversation, but with the sound of collective stomachs grumbling in quiet frustration.</p>

<p>Though everyone had eaten earlier, their thoughts were haunted by the tantalizing aroma of Ross’s mysterious meal.</p>

<p>No one had dared ask him for even a taste—not after the way they had treated him during dinner.</p>

<p>Too proud. Too afraid.</p>

<p>And so, one by one, the housemates retreated to their rooms, defeated not by a challenge, but by a scent.</p>

<p>They lay in their beds, eyes staring at the ceiling, minds spinning with guesses and imaginations.</p>

<p>Was it steak? Braised pork? Some kind of curry?</p>

<p>Whatever it was, it smelled like heaven.</p>

<p>Some even dreamed about it. One girl murmured in her sleep, &quot;Just one bite...&quot; Another turned over and sighed deeply, her stomach growling in sync.</p>

<p>It was ridiculous—and yet none of them could deny it. Ross had gotten under their skin... and into their heads.</p>

<p>When morning came, a few housemates woke up hungry, despite having eaten the night before.</p>

<p>It was like their bodies had been waiting for his food.</p>

<p>Meanwhile, the weekly challenge continued without incident.</p>

<p>Though tensions still lingered, the group managed to stay organized.</p>

<p>They had agreed on a rotation system—one person would stand inside the circle for an hour while another kept watch, talking with them and making sure they didn’t drift off or accidentally step out.</p>

<p>The buddy system worked surprisingly well.</p>

<p>It created small moments of camaraderie in the dark, and gave people a chance to bond over shared determination.</p>

<p>No one wanted to be the one who failed the task—not on the very first week.</p>

<p>&quot;It’s not that hard,&quot; one boy yawned as he watched the clock tick down. &quot;An hour’s nothing if someone’s there to talk with you.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;Yeah, but wait until it’s 3 AM and you’re cold and tired,&quot; the other replied, sipping tea.</p>

<p>Still, despite the discomfort, everyone pulled their weight.</p>

<p>The house boss—or whoever the mysterious voice belonged to—seemed to be easing them into the competition.</p>

<p>The first weekly task wasn’t cruel. Just a test of consistency, cooperation, and willpower.</p>

<p>And as the house quietly pushed through the night, one thought kept returning to them—lingering like the scent that had invaded their dreams:</p>

<p>What else is Ross hiding?</p>

<p>***</p>

<p>Morning arrived with golden light streaming into the house, casting long shadows across the patio.</p>

<p>As part of their routine, the boys gathered in the backyard to begin their daily workouts—stretching, lifting, doing push-ups and light sparring to get the blood flowing.</p>

<p>Some of the girls joined in as well, eager to stay active or simply to enjoy the morning energy.</p>

<p>It wasn’t long before attention shifted—not to the exercises themselves, but to the people doing them.</p>

<p>Shirtless torsos, tight sportswear, and perfectly toned bodies quickly became the center of focus.</p>

<p>Among them, a few stood out more than the others—Corey with his clean-cut face and chiseled abs, and several of the five standout beauties, whose slim waists, radiant skin, and flirty energy had the boys subtly competing for their attention.</p>

<p>Flirtatious banter started to bubble beneath the surface.</p>

<p>&quot;So, uh... you work out every morning too?&quot; one guy asked hopefully, flashing a smile at the girl beside him.</p>

<p>&quot;I do now,&quot; she said with a wink, giggling as she picked up her pace.</p>

<p>It was all textbook reality TV.</p>

<p>The soft beginnings of showmances—the hook that reeled viewers in.</p>

<p>Flirtation, budding romances, and the inevitable jealousy and conflict to come... all of it was exactly what the audience loved.</p>

<p>It was a careful dance of attraction, rivalry, and strategy.</p>

<p>Then Ross stepped out.</p>

<p>Wearing nothing but black workout shorts and a sleeveless shirt, he strode onto the patio like he didn’t even notice the others.</p>

<p>The entire yard fell silent for a moment.</p>

<p>&quot;Damn...&quot;</p>

<p>Someone muttered it under their breath, but everyone heard it.</p>

<p>Ross’s body was a sculpted marvel—massive and defined, with muscles that looked carved from stone.</p>

<p>His shoulders were broad, arms thick with muscle and veins, and his core was tight, powerful, and practically glowing under the morning sun.</p>

<p>Every movement he made flexed another group of muscles. He looked like a warrior from an ancient myth, more carved statue than man.</p>

<p>One of the girls, who had been casually stretching, suddenly forgot what she was doing. &quot;Is that... real?&quot;</p>

<p>Another whispered, &quot;He’s built like a tank. Look at those arms—holy hell.&quot;</p>

<p>Even some of the guys paused mid-rep, their confidence wobbling.</p>

<p>Corey, who had been used to being the top male eye candy, glanced over at Ross with a clenched jaw.</p>

<p>&quot;Show-off,&quot; he muttered, but even he couldn’t deny the sheer physical presence Ross commanded.</p>

<p>The women, however, were less restrained in their reaction.</p>

<p>Their eyes roamed, breaths catching involuntarily.</p>

<p>They tried not to stare—but their bodies betrayed them.</p>

<p>A flush of heat climbed up necks. Lips parted. Throats dried.</p>

<p>It wasn’t just the physique—it was the energy Ross gave off. Quiet, focused, self-assured.</p>

<p>He didn’t preen. He didn’t flex or pose like the others.</p>

<p>He simply existed, and that alone shifted the air.</p>

<p>Unreal.</p>

<p>That was the word that stuck in everyone’s mind.</p>

<p>He began his own warm-up with slow, precise movements—stretching, shadowboxing, and doing push-ups at an unhurried pace.</p>

<p>Every muscle moved like it was forged for one purpose: dominance.</p>

<p>And once again, without saying a single word, Ross had captured the attention of the entire house.</p>

<p>Breakfast came, and once again, Ross was excluded.</p>

<p>Just like the night before, the rest of the housemates divided the food amongst themselves, deliberately leaving him out of it.</p>

<p>There were no invitations, no gestures of kindness—only sidelong glances and quiet snickers as they made sure every plate was accounted for, leaving Ross with nothing.</p>

<p>But if they thought that would bother him, they were mistaken.</p>

<p>Ross didn’t say a word.</p>

<p>He simply turned on his heel, walked into the kitchen, and began cooking.</p>

<p>It was like watching a master at work. Within minutes, the air was filled with the rich, mouthwatering scent of sizzling garlic, butter, and herbs.</p>

<p>Then came something sweet—fruit, maybe cinnamon—and then something savory and smoky.</p>

<p>The entire house was pulled into that aroma against their will.</p>

<p>Again.</p>

<p>As the others sat down outside with their scrambled eggs and toast, their heads slowly turned back toward the source of that divine smell.</p>

<p>&quot;Seriously, what the hell is he making this time?&quot; one girl murmured, already regretting the dry toast in her mouth.</p>

<p>No one had the courage to ask. Not yet. They were too proud. Or too ashamed.</p>

<p>Meanwhile, Ross plated his food and walked calmly to a small table by the edge of the garden patio.</p>

<p>He sat down, his massive frame relaxed, a smirk playing at his lips as he set his breakfast in front of him.</p>

<p>It looked like something off a gourmet magazine cover—colorful, neatly plated, steaming and fresh.</p>

<p>He picked up his fork, ready to take the first bite—</p>

<p>When suddenly, the chair opposite him scraped back.</p>

<p>Someone sat down.</p>

<p>Ross blinked once, surprised. Then a smile curled at the corner of his mouth as he looked up.</p>

<p>There she was.</p>
