<p>His eyes flickered briefly to Julian, but they quickly returned to April, lingering there for just a moment too long. The intent in his gaze was clear—there was no mistaking what he wanted.</p>

<p>&quot;I&#x27;ll give you 10 minutes to start. And if you fail to do so, I&#x27;ll begin with what I have planned for you, and I promise you, it won&#x27;t be pretty. No mercy. Not a single ounce.&quot;</p>

<p>The words hung in the air, sharp and heavy, as though they had the weight of a thousand threats behind them.</p>

<p>Julian&#x27;s heart pounded in his chest, but he refused to show weakness, refusing to give Ross the satisfaction of seeing his fear.</p>

<p>He clenched his fists, but the unease still crawled through him like a creeping sickness.</p>

<p>&quot;Fuck you! Just get this over with and take us back!&quot; Julian spat, his voice trembling with restrained fury. His chest heaved, each breath feeling like it was being sucked out of him.</p>

<p>&quot;We don&#x27;t like playing games here. You should know that my family is rich and influential. They&#x27;ll be looking for us. They&#x27;ll comb the entire city. And when they do, you won&#x27;t be able to hide from them. You&#x27;ll regret every second of this.&quot;</p>

<p>Ross simply raised an eyebrow at Julian&#x27;s outburst. His smile remained, but it was a faint curve of amusement, as though he found the threat laughable.</p>

<p>He didn&#x27;t even bother to look up from his glass as he took another sip of wine, clearly uninterested in what Julian had said.</p>

<p>&quot;Suit yourself, then,&quot; he replied, his voice cool and indifferent. He made no further comment and returned to his relaxed position on the bed, as if Julian&#x27;s words had not affected him in the slightest.</p>

<p>The tension in the room only deepened. The masked men remained silent, standing like shadows in the corners, their eyes hidden behind their masks, giving nothing away.</p>

<p>No one moved, no one spoke, and the minutes crawled by like an endless torture. The food, still steaming and untouched, seemed to mock them.</p>

<p>Every second that passed felt like it was pulling them deeper into this nightmare. Time itself seemed to slow, as if the room had frozen around them.</p>

<p>Ten minutes dragged on, each one feeling longer than the last. Julian&#x27;s stomach twisted with hunger, but his pride refused to allow him to touch the food.</p>

<p>April&#x27;s hand tightened around his, but she didn&#x27;t speak. They both knew the situation was escalating, but neither of them knew what they could do to stop it.</p>

<p>They were powerless, trapped in a room with a man who had no regard for them, a man who had everything in his control.</p>

<p>Finally, the silence was broken.</p>

<p>&quot;Time&#x27;s up,&quot; Ross said, his voice soft, almost regretful, as he set his wine glass down on the nightstand. He stood from the bed with deliberate slowness, his movements fluid and confident.</p>

<p>There was no more pretense, no more small talk. His eyes locked onto April, and the smile that played on his lips was dark and knowing.</p>

<p>&quot;Come here, April. You should know why I invited you here tonight.&quot;</p>

<p>Julian&#x27;s breath caught in his throat, his heart pounding in his chest. The weight of Ross&#x27;s words hung in the air like a death sentence.</p>

<p>There was no mistaking what he wanted. And it wasn&#x27;t just the food that was being served to them.</p>

<p>Ross&#x27;s intentions were clear now—there was no more subtlety, no more games. He was going to take what he wanted, and they had no choice but to comply.</p>

<p>April didn&#x27;t move at first, her body frozen in place. But she couldn&#x27;t hide the fear in her eyes, the uncertainty, the realization that they were completely out of their depth.</p>

<p>Julian&#x27;s instincts screamed at him to do something, anything to stop what was about to happen, but his hands were tied, his options exhausted.</p>

<p>&quot;Come on, April,&quot; Ross said again, his voice growing more insistent, and there was no hiding the menace beneath his calm tone.</p>

<p>He took a step toward her, and in that moment, Julian realized just how far gone they were. His heart sank.</p>

<p>This was the moment they had been dreading—the moment everything changed, when their worlds were torn apart by a man who had nothing to lose.</p>

<p>In the silence that followed, the only sound was the slow, deliberate shuffle of Ross&#x27;s footsteps as he approached, his intentions unmistakable.</p>

<p>Ross stopped several steps away from his captives, his eyes fixed on them as they trembled, but tried to maintain the façade of bravery.</p>

<p>Julian&#x27;s jaw clenched, and April&#x27;s chest rose and fell with shallow breaths.</p>

<p>They both exchanged a quick, nervous glance, their eyes darting to the corner of the room where Brandon stood, massive and still as a statue.</p>

<p>The automatic rifle clutched tightly in Brandon&#x27;s hands reminded them of the brutal reality they faced. Every step they took could be their last if they made the wrong move.</p>

<p>A single word from Ross or a twitch of Brandon&#x27;s finger could end it all.</p>

<p>&quot;What exactly do you want from me?&quot; April&#x27;s voice wavered slightly, though she already knew the answer. The air between them was thick with fear and anticipation, but she had to ask.</p>

<p>Maybe, just maybe, hearing it from his mouth would make it feel less real, less inevitable.</p>

<p>Ross&#x27;s lips curled into a predatory grin, his cold eyes gleaming with malice. &quot;Don&#x27;t ask questions you already know the answer to, April. You&#x27;ve never been a dumb, slow girl. Not even once in your life.&quot;</p>

<p>He took a slow step forward, his eyes never leaving hers, his grin widening as he closed the distance between them. The air between them grew denser, as though charged with electricity.</p>

<p>Julian&#x27;s chest tightened, his fists clenched, but he didn&#x27;t move. He didn&#x27;t dare.</p>

<p>Ross&#x27;s gaze never wavered as he moved closer. April could feel his breath on her skin as he reached up, his fingers brushing against her face with an almost sickening tenderness.</p>
