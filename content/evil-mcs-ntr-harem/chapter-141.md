<p>Thud!</p>

<p>The sudden metallic clang shattered the silence, reverberating through the dimly lit basement.</p>

<p>Maya flinched, her pulse spiking as the noise triggered a wave of raw fear deep in her chest.</p>

<p>&quot;Pick them up,&quot; the burly gangster barked, his voice rough and grating.</p>

<p>A pair of handcuffs landed at her feet with a dull clatter.</p>

<p>He loomed over her, his shadow stretching ominously across the cold, concrete floor.</p>

<p>&quot;Put your new bracelets on. I&#x27;m not taking any chances with a strong woman like you. I&#x27;ve seen what you can do.&quot;</p>

<p>He paused, a cruel smirk curling his lips.</p>

<p>&quot;Besides, we wouldn&#x27;t want to lose such a valuable investment, now would we? A beauty like you—with that tight little body? You&#x27;ll be one of our top earners in no time. Hell, the men will be lining up for you.&quot;</p>

<p>Maya&#x27;s stomach churned at his words.</p>

<p>Her skin crawled under his predatory gaze, but she forced her expression to remain neutral, masking the storm of revulsion and fear boiling inside her.</p>

<p>Slowly, she bent down to retrieve the cuffs, her fingers trembling despite her best efforts.</p>

<p>Her eyes flicked upward, watching the man holding her at gunpoint.</p>

<p>He wasn&#x27;t the sharpest tool in the shed, but he wasn&#x27;t careless, either. He kept a safe distance, ensuring she had no chance to disarm him.</p>

<p>The cold steel of the cuffs bit into her palms as she straightened, her mind racing.</p>

<p>Every instinct screamed at her to fight, to run, but the odds were stacked against her. For now, she had no choice but to play along.</p>

<p>Click.</p>

<p>The handcuffs snapped shut around her wrists, locking her movements in place. She flexed her fingers, testing the restraints, but there was no give.</p>

<p>The big man watched her like a hawk, his eyes gleaming with satisfaction.</p>

<p>&quot;Good girl,&quot; he muttered, stepping back and grabbing something from a nearby wall.</p>

<p>With a practiced flick of his wrist, he unfolded it—a wheelchair.</p>

<p>&quot;Sit,&quot; he ordered, gesturing with the barrel of his gun.</p>

<p>Maya hesitated for a split second, but the sharp edge of his tone left no room for defiance.</p>

<p>She lowered herself into the chair, her muscles tense and coiled like a spring.</p>

<p>The man moved quickly, his rough hands securing her to the wheelchair with a series of thick ropes.</p>

<p>He tied her tightly, his motions efficient and methodical. It was clear this wasn&#x27;t his first time restraining someone.</p>

<p>Once he was satisfied with the bindings, he grabbed the wheelchair&#x27;s handles and pushed her into an adjacent room.</p>

<p>The air grew heavier with every step, thick with a nauseating stench that made her stomach twist.</p>

<p>The room was dark, lit only by a single flickering bulb that cast eerie shadows on the walls.</p>

<p>He parked the wheelchair in the center and stepped back, lighting a cigarette as he leaned against the wall.</p>

<p>&quot;Now, all we need to do is wait for the boss,&quot; he said, exhaling a plume of smoke. His tone was casual, as if this was just another day at the office.</p>

<p>Maya&#x27;s eyes darted around the room, her heart pounding in her chest.</p>

<p>It took a moment for her vision to adjust, but when it did, the sight before her made her blood run cold.</p>

<p>The room was filled with beds—rows of them, crammed together in the dim space.</p>

<p>Figures lay sprawled across the mattresses, their bodies eerily still.</p>

<p>Women.</p>

<p>At least twenty, maybe thirty of them. Their slender forms were barely covered, and their shallow breathing was the only sign that they were alive.</p>

<p>The stench of the room was oppressive, a sickly blend of sweat, sex, and stale drugs that clung to every surface.</p>

<p>The faint sound of muffled sobs reached her ears, mingling with the occasional groan or shuffle.</p>

<p>Maya&#x27;s stomach churned, bile rising in her throat. This wasn&#x27;t just a prison—it was a nightmare.</p>

<p>A place where humanity was stripped away, leaving nothing but despair.</p>

<p>She tugged at her bindings, her heart pounding harder. She had to get out of here.</p>

<p>She had to do something. But as the ropes dug into her skin, she realized just how dire her situation was.</p>

<p>The gangster took another drag from his cigarette, his eyes half-lidded as he watched her.</p>

<p>&quot;Don&#x27;t bother struggling,&quot; he said, his voice laced with amusement. &quot;You&#x27;re not going anywhere. Not until the boss gets here. Then we&#x27;ll see what kind of future we&#x27;ve got planned for you.&quot;</p>

<p>Maya clenched her fists, her nails biting into her palms. The fight inside her hadn&#x27;t dimmed—if anything, it burned brighter. She wouldn&#x27;t let them break her. Not here. Not ever.</p>

<p>Three hours later, they arrived, their heavy footsteps echoing down the empty floor before they even came into view with the door opening thereafter.</p>

<p>Maya tensed as the group emerged from the shadows, led by a hulking figure who filled the narrow doorway like a mountain.</p>

<p>The leader was an imposing man, easily six foot five, his bulky frame a combination of fat and raw strength.</p>

<p>His broad shoulders strained against his leather jacket, and his arms—thick as tree trunks—hung at his sides with a relaxed confidence that only added to his menace.</p>

<p>Though his body carried no visible muscles, the sheer power radiating from him was enough to send a chill down Maya&#x27;s spine.</p>

<p>His pale, ruddy face split into a cruel grin, revealing a gold tooth that gleamed under the flickering light of the single bulb overhead that appeared next.</p>

<p>Behind him came a swarm of gang members, more than a dozen, each one just as menacing in his own way.</p>

<p>Their faces were a grotesque gallery of scars and tattoos, their eyes cold and lifeless, like wolves circling prey.</p>

<p>The air around them seemed to grow heavier, laced with the acrid stench of sweat, leather, and stale cigarette smoke.</p>

<p>Maya&#x27;s heart pounded as the group spread out, their leader stepping forward with a swagger that spoke of years of unchecked power.</p>

<p>He stopped a few feet in front of her, tilting his head to one side as he studied her like a predator sizing up its meal.</p>

<p>&quot;Maya Pierce,&quot; he said, his gravelly voice thick with mockery. &quot;I&#x27;ve been waiting for this moment.&quot; His grin widened, cruel and predatory.</p>

<p>&quot;I remember your wonder boy. Didn&#x27;t he rough up some of my boys a few weeks back? Thought he could play hero, huh?&quot;</p>
