<p>Marissa’s hands trembled on her lap.</p>

<p>She took another deep, shaky breath, trying to calm the storm inside her chest.</p>

<p>She thought of her daughters, of the trust they had placed in her over the years, of the love that should have been pure and untouchable.</p>

<p>And now that love felt fragile, cracked, and vulnerable—like a delicate vase dropped to the floor.</p>

<p>&quot;I... I’m so sorry, Lea,&quot; she whispered again, the words nearly lost in the silence of the room.</p>

<p>&quot;I don’t want to lose you, or your trust. I can’t promise I’ll never make mistakes again... but I can promise I’ll never stop loving you. Please... try to understand. Please try to forgive me, even if it’s just a little.&quot;</p>

<p>The room fell silent, the air heavy with unsaid words, with shock, hurt, and an overwhelming tide of emotion neither of them could yet untangle.</p>

<p>And Marissa stayed there, sitting in her chair, trembling, ashamed, and hoping against hope that her daughter’s love for her could somehow survive the truth.</p>

<p>A bitter, mocking laugh escaped Lea’s lips, a sound so sharp it seemed to cut through the opulent stillness of the kitchen.</p>

<p>She leaned against the marble countertop, her knuckles white as she gripped the edge, her body a taut line of furious tension.</p>

<p>&quot;What is it, Mom? What is the grand, cosmic secret I’m missing?&quot; she began, her voice dripping with a sarcasm that barely masked her hurt.</p>

<p>&quot;What does Ross have that Dad doesn’t? Is it just... that?&quot; She let the question hang, poisonous and heavy in the air.</p>

<p>&quot;Is it really that life-altering, that earth-shattering, to have Ross’s giant cock buried deep inside you? Is that the magic key that makes you forget your husband, your family, your own daughters?&quot;</p>

<p>Marissa flinched as if struck, her hand flying to her throat. &quot;Lea, stop it this instant! You have no idea what you’re saying!&quot;</p>

<p>But Lea was a dam that had broken, and the torrent of her anger would not be contained.</p>

<p>She pushed off the counter, her eyes blazing. &quot;Maybe Chelsea and I should just try it for ourselves. Maybe that’s the only way we’ll ever understand! It seems to be the family trend, doesn’t it? A whole dynasty of women, all tripping over themselves to fall for the same man. First Karen, then you... who’s next? Are we just checking off a list?&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;How dare you!&quot; Marissa scolded, her own voice trembling now, not just with anger but with a profound, gut-wrenching fear.</p>

<p>Her mind raced, painting a horrifying tableau: her husband’s kind, trusting face crumbling as he discovered not just his wife’s betrayal, but a conspiracy of silence and desire that included his entire family.</p>

<p>He wouldn’t just be devastated; he would be utterly annihilated, his entire life’s foundation exposed as a lie.</p>

<p>The image was so potent she felt a wave of nausea.</p>

<p>This wasn’t just an affair; it was the potential destruction of their entire world, and she was its willing architect.</p>

<p>A long, suffocating silence descended upon them.</p>

<p>The cheerful ticking of the digital clock in the room became a torturous metronome marking the death of their previous normalcy.</p>

<p>When Lea finally spoke again, all the sharp-edged anger had bled out of her voice, leaving behind the raw, vulnerable plea of a frightened daughter.</p>

<p>&quot;Is there no other choice, Mom?&quot; she whispered, her back still turned. &quot;Can’t you... can’t you just leave him? For good? For us? For Dad?&quot;</p>

<p>The reply was instantaneous, a reflex born of something far deeper than mere want. &quot;I can’t, Lea. I can’t.&quot;</p>

<p>Marissa’s answer was so swift and absolute it stole the air from the room.</p>

<p>There was no pause for consideration, no pretense of a moral struggle.</p>

<p>The truth, ugly and undeniable, was out.</p>

<p>She wasn’t trapped by Ross; she was enslaved by her own need.</p>

<p>The memory of his possession was a fire in her blood, a primal craving that had rewired her very soul.</p>

<p>The thought of returning to the bland, comfortable intimacy of her marriage was not a return to safety, but a sentence to a life of gray, aching deprivation.</p>

<p>She was addicted, and the drug was Ross—not just the physical thrill, but the intoxicating sense of being truly, wildly desired in a way she had forgotten was possible.</p>

<p>She had chosen her addiction, and in that single, breathless reply, she had chosen it over her family.</p>

<p>&quot;I understand, Mom. You may leave now. I’ll need some time to think about this... whether I tell Dad or not. Please lock the door after you go.&quot; Lea’s voice was calm, measured, but there was a firmness in it that Marissa couldn’t ignore.</p>

<p>It carried the weight of someone setting boundaries—someone who had just glimpsed a side of their parent they hadn’t expected.</p>

<p>Marissa stood frozen for a moment, searching for words, but none came.</p>

<p>How could she explain herself when even she knew she had crossed a line?</p>

<p>Her mind raced with excuses that felt hollow the moment she thought them.</p>

<p>She opened her mouth, closed it again, and finally realized there was nothing to say that could make things right this time.</p>

<p>Lea’s eyes held hers steadily, a quiet challenge in the way she waited, patient yet unwavering.</p>

<p>Marissa felt a pang of guilt, sharper than she had anticipated, tightening her chest.</p>

<p>She wanted to reach out, to say something—anything—but the words dissolved in the weight of her own shame.</p>

<p>Without another word, she nodded, a small, defeated motion, and turned toward the door.</p>

<p>She hesitated for a fraction of a second, glancing back at her daughter.</p>

<p>Lea was already sitting upright, composed yet distant, like a wall she couldn’t breach.</p>

<p>Marissa exhaled slowly and stepped out of the room, the sound of her footsteps muted against the floor.</p>

<p>When the door clicked softly behind her, she realized how final it sounded.</p>

<p>She lingered in the hallway for a moment, her hands trembling slightly, before moving on—leaving Lea to her thoughts and the heavy silence that now filled the room.</p>
