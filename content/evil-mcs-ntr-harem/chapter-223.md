<p>&quot;Do your parents know that you’re here, Imari? We don’t want them to be worried about you,&quot; Althea asked, her calm voice cutting through the comfortable hum of conversation about thirty minutes into the meal.</p>

<p>Imari paused, setting down her fork with a thoughtful smile.</p>

<p>&quot;Yes, they know. In fact, I’m considering transferring to the college here.&quot;</p>

<p>Her words hung in the air for a moment, drawing curious glances from the others.</p>

<p>It was clear her parents trusted her completely—a rare freedom for someone raised in wealth.</p>

<p>They had always believed that mistakes were a part of life, an essential path to learning and growth.</p>

<p>Imari cherished them for that. She understood how fortunate she was to have parents who trusted her judgment, even if it led her down unexpected roads.</p>

<p>&quot;That’s a bold move,&quot; Althea replied, her lips quirking into an approving smile. &quot;Not everyone has that kind of independence.&quot;</p>

<p>Imari’s eyes brightened at the subtle praise. &quot;I’ve been wanting to make my own choices for a while now,&quot; she admitted. &quot;Coming here feels… right.&quot;</p>

<p>The rest of the night passed in pleasant conversation, filled with warmth and laughter.</p>

<p>By the time they finished eating, the table was nearly cleared of food. The girls showered Ross with compliments, their bellies full and their moods light.</p>

<p>&quot;You’re amazing, Ross! You’ve set the bar too high for anyone else to cook for us again,&quot; Ren teased, leaning back in her chair.</p>

<p>&quot;I’ll expect seconds tomorrow,&quot; Imari added with a grin.</p>

<p>Ross chuckled, clearly pleased with the praise. &quot;Anytime, ladies. Just say the word.&quot;</p>

<p>With that, they retired for the night, heading upstairs to their respective rooms.</p>

<p>***</p>

<p>The house fell silent under the weight of midnight.</p>

<p>Moonlight streamed faintly through the curtains as Ren and Imari lay side by side in their shared room.</p>

<p>Neither of them was asleep; their hearts still raced from the thrill of their unexpected adventure and the promise of more to come.</p>

<p>Then, a soft knock broke the stillness.</p>

<p>Knock. Knock.</p>

<p>Ren froze, her breath catching in her throat. Beside her, Imari stiffened. The sound was gentle but unmistakable.</p>

<p>They turned their heads toward the door almost in unison.</p>

<p>&quot;…,&quot; Imari and Ren exchanged a glance, the unspoken words hanging between them.</p>

<p>They didn’t need to ask who it was. They already knew.</p>

<p>And they knew what he wanted.</p>

<p>The quiet stretched, heavy with anticipation, as the soft knocks came again.</p>

<p>Knock. Knock.</p>

<p>This time, Ren reached for Imari’s hand, squeezing it lightly.</p>

<p>&quot;What should we do?&quot; she whispered, her voice barely audible.</p>

<p>Imari swallowed hard, her cheeks tinged pink even in the dim light.</p>

<p>&quot;I… I don’t know,&quot; she replied, her voice equally uncertain.</p>

<p>Outside the door, the hallway remained silent, save for the faint rustle of someone shifting their weight.</p>

<p>Whoever stood there wasn’t leaving anytime soon.</p>

<p>The two girls stared at each other, their hearts pounding in unison.</p>

<p>A minute passed in tense silence, the weight of the situation pressing down on the two girls.</p>

<p>Finally, Ren broke.</p>

<p>&quot;Is that you, Ross?&quot; she called softly, though she already knew the answer. Her voice was steady, but her words carried an excuse.</p>

<p>&quot;We can’t… I have my period.&quot;</p>

<p>The pause on the other side of the door was brief, and when Ross spoke, his voice was calm, unyielding.</p>

<p>&quot;I checked the plane’s garbage and this house too, Ren. I know you don’t have any monthly visitors yet.&quot; His tone grew sharper, edged with authority.</p>

<p>&quot;I brought you here easily, and I can send you back to your parents just as easily. You have thirty seconds to decide.&quot;</p>

<p>The ultimatum hung heavy in the air, each second ticking by as Ren and Imari exchanged anxious glances.</p>

<p>Two dozen breaths later, the quiet click of the door unlocking broke the stillness.</p>

<p>The door creaked open, and Ross stepped inside. His sharp gaze scanned the room.</p>

<p>Ren stood near the bed, her delicate figure wrapped in a pair of modest white pajamas, the soft fabric emphasizing her chaste beauty.</p>

<p>Beside her, Imari lay partially hidden beneath the thick blanket, her dark eyes peering out, tense yet defiant.</p>

<p>Both girls, who were at the same height—5’3&quot;—were strikingly beautiful in their own right.</p>

<p>Ross’s gaze lingered on Ren, the faint scent of her skin reaching him even from across the room.</p>

<p>Desire flickered in his chest, rising with every heartbeat. He had waited long enough to claim his prize, and tonight, he would have her.</p>

<p>&quot;Imari,&quot; Ross said, his voice cool and commanding, &quot;you can go to the room next door. It’s empty. Ren and I have a business deal to finish.&quot;</p>

<p>Imari didn’t move. She tightened her grip on the blanket and lifted her chin stubbornly.</p>

<p>&quot;Nope. I’m staying,&quot; she said firmly, her voice steady despite the pounding of her heart.</p>

<p>&quot;I know what you want from Ren, and I’ll be here to make sure you don’t bully her too much. You need to be gentle—it’s her first time.&quot;</p>

<p>Ross arched an eyebrow, surprised by her boldness. He had anticipated hesitation, maybe even useless begging, but Imari’s resolve amused him.</p>

<p>It also told him she understood the reality of the situation.</p>

<p>Ross was strong—Imari had seen that back on the plane.</p>

<p>He could carry two women at once as if they weighed nothing, completely in control even when making love without pause.</p>

<p>The memory had clearly stayed with her.</p>

<p>For a brief moment, Ross studied Imari. Though he wanted them both, he had no intention of taking Imari tonight.</p>

<p>She was a challenge for another time—one he would savor when the moment was right.</p>

<p>&quot;Suit yourself,&quot; he said with a shrug, his tone laced with indifference. Then, without another glance at Imari, he turned his full attention to Ren.</p>

<p>The room seemed to shrink as Ross closed the distance between them.</p>

<p>Ren stood frozen, her chest rising and falling with quick, shallow breaths.</p>

<p>Up close, the faint blush on her cheeks and the nervous flicker in her eyes were all the more evident.</p>

<p>She looked delicate, almost fragile—yet irresistibly tempting.</p>

<p>Ross reached out, his fingers brushing her chin gently, tilting her face up to meet his gaze.</p>

<p>&quot;It’s time to fulfill our deal,&quot; he murmured, his voice low and firm.</p>

<p>Ren swallowed hard, her eyes darting toward Imari for a fleeting moment.</p>

<p>Imari didn’t speak but held her ground, watching intently as if silently reminding Ross of her presence.</p>

<p>Ross’s lips curled into a faint smile. Let her watch. It wouldn’t change the outcome.</p>

<p>He leaned closer, his warm breath fanning against Ren’s skin as his fingers trailed down her arm, igniting a shiver in their wake.</p>

<p>This was his prize, and tonight, he would finally claim what was his.</p>
