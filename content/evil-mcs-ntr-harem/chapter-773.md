<p>Steve then vanished without a trace.</p>

<p>Of course, the whole world would never know the dark truth behind Steve’s disappearance.</p>

<p>After the savage ordeal inflicted by Brandon and his men, Steve was quietly whisked away to a place unlike any other—a place where time seemed to stand still, and suffering stretched on without end.</p>

<p>It was a hidden prison, a realm of endless torment where pain became timeless.</p>

<p>This was the very same place where Thomas had been sent before him, and now Steve was destined to join him, trapped in a cycle of eternal anguish and punishment.</p>

<p>No headlines would report it. No whispers in the media. Just silence.</p>

<p>A secret erased from the world’s memory.</p>

<p>Meanwhile, back in the house, Ross reveled in his victory.</p>

<p>His enemies had been crushed, their power broken and scattered like dust in the wind.</p>

<p>A satisfied, almost cruel smile played across his lips as he watched the pieces fall perfectly into place.</p>

<p>Even more thrilling was the sight of Lily—once hesitant, now fully embracing her place within his circle.</p>

<p>The way she moved with confidence, the way her loyalty was sealed—it was all part of his grand design.</p>

<p>The house of fortune was his domain, and those who chose to stand with him would rise. Those who opposed him would fall.</p>

<p>Tonight was just the beginning.</p>

<p>The night promised excitement, celebration, and new alliances forged in the glow of conquest.</p>

<p>For Ross and his growing faction, this was more than a victory—it was the dawn of a new era.</p>

<p>And none of them would forget it.</p>

<p>&quot;Heaven, Tianna—go back to your beds in the girls’ room for now,&quot; Ross said, his tone calm but firm. &quot;Lily is a virgin, so I’m going to take my time with her.&quot;</p>

<p>Heaven smiled knowingly, the kind of smile that held both mischief and trust.</p>

<p>&quot;Fine. I can wait one night without any action in bed,&quot; she said with a playful glint in her eyes.</p>

<p>Tianna laughed softly, nudging Heaven as she added, &quot;Me too. Just take care of Lily, okay? Don’t break her.&quot;</p>

<p>The two women exchanged teasing looks before turning and heading back toward their room, their footsteps fading into the quiet of the house.</p>

<p>Ross watched them go, then shifted his attention fully to Lily, who remained by the pool, her expression steady but tinged with nervous anticipation.</p>

<p>He stepped closer, the warm night air carrying the faint scent of water and jasmine.</p>

<p>Ross’s gaze fixed on Lily’s eyes, searching for any sign of hesitation.</p>

<p>&quot;You’ve got one last chance to say no, Lily,&quot; he said, his voice low and serious. &quot;After this, there’ll be no turning back.&quot;</p>

<p>Lily met his gaze without flinching. &quot;I’ve made up my mind, D. I’m not going to change it.&quot;</p>

<p>Ross allowed a slow smile to spread across his face, a mixture of pride and something deeper—respect for her resolve.</p>

<p>It was unusual for a virgin to be so composed, so willing. Most would tremble or try to escape, but Lily held her ground.</p>

<p>Curiosity sparked within him, and he reached out with his divine sense, a subtle energy probe that revealed more than just the surface.</p>

<p>What he found inside Lily was extraordinary.</p>

<p>A rare gift, something powerful and unique that explained her courage and calmness.</p>

<p>It was this gift that made her special, that made her ready to step into this new Chapter without fear.</p>

<p>Ross’s smile deepened. &quot;As you wish,&quot; he said softly.</p>

<p>He extended his hand, and Lily took it, her fingers trembling slightly before curling around his firmly.</p>

<p>Together, they left the poolside behind and walked through the quiet halls toward the boys’ room.</p>

<p>The atmosphere between them was charged—not just with anticipation but with the unspoken promise of something profound.</p>

<p>Ross could feel it, and he knew Lily could too.</p>

<p>Tonight was more than just a first for her. It was the beginning of something that would change them both.</p>

<p>When Ross and Lily stepped into the room, all eyes immediately locked onto them.</p>

<p>The energy shifted; a mix of excitement, envy, and curiosity rippled through the group.</p>

<p>The boys had grown used to Ross’s effortless ability to claim the most beautiful women in the house—and seeing him lead Lily inside only fueled their admiration and rivalry.</p>

<p>&quot;Wow, Lily! I can’t wait to hear how loud you’re gonna moan later!&quot; one of the boys called out with a wide grin, his tone teasing but genuine.</p>

<p>&quot;Yeah! Big D’s adding another to his collection,&quot; another chimed in, nudging his friend with a smirk. &quot;He’s on a roll!&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;D, save some for us! Don’t take them all!&quot; a third shouted, laughter bubbling up around the room.</p>

<p>They spoke with a mix of jealousy and respect, knowing well that Ross was untouchable in this game.</p>

<p>Ross simply smiled at their antics, unfazed and confident.</p>

<p>He enjoyed their energy, the playful competitiveness—it was all part of the house’s rhythm.</p>

<p>&quot;Close the lights and enjoy the music, boys,&quot; Ross said smoothly, his voice low and commanding.</p>

<p>There was no need to raise his tone; they knew the drill.</p>

<p>One by one, the boys flicked off the lights, plunging the room into a dim, sultry glow that danced against the walls.</p>

<p>Ross reached for a large blanket and pulled it over himself and Lily, pulling her close beneath its warm cover.</p>

<p>The room fell silent except for the soft hum of music beginning to play—slow, rhythmic, and charged with promise.</p>

<p>He could feel Lily’s heartbeat quicken beneath his hand, her breath catching in the stillness.</p>

<p>Tonight was a milestone, not just for her but for them both. The air thickened with anticipation.</p>

<p>The show was about to begin.</p>

<p>&quot;I thought it’d be much hotter and darker in here,&quot; Lily murmured softly, her voice barely above a whisper.</p>

<p>She lay on her back, feeling the warmth of Ross’s body pressed gently against hers beneath the blanket.</p>

<p>The room was dimly lit, but somehow, she could still see him clearly—his eyes, the slight curve of his lips, the steady rhythm of his breath.</p>
