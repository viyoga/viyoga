<p>That hit harder than Cara expected.</p>

<p>For a long second, neither of them said anything. The lights from the set buzzed faintly.</p>

<p>The crew, sensing something private unfolding, respectfully gave them distance, pretending to be busy but still listening.</p>

<p>Cara slowly let out a breath.</p>

<p>&quot;Hahhh...&quot; Her chest rose and fell as she tried to ground herself.</p>

<p>There was no denying it—something inside her hurt.</p>

<p>A quiet, biting sting that she couldn’t name.</p>

<p>Why does this feel personal? It’s just a scene. Just a dumb weekly task in a tv show.</p>

<p>Just a kiss... right?</p>

<p>Still, she couldn’t deny the flutter of disappointment that Ross hadn’t chosen her for something more... human.</p>

<p>She wasn’t sure whether she wanted to be seen as talented or desired.</p>

<p>She only knew she hated feeling disposable.</p>

<p>And yet, despite everything, she still respected the way Ross handled it.</p>

<p>Cold as it was, it was honest.</p>

<p>She looked at him again—really looked at him.</p>

<p>He wasn’t mocking her. He wasn’t manipulating her.</p>

<p>He had simply laid the cards on the table.</p>

<p>No pretense. No lies.</p>

<p>Maybe that’s why it hurt.</p>

<p>Cara straightened her shoulders and lifted her chin.</p>

<p>She gave herself a second to collect the storm inside, then looked at the director and gave a nod.</p>

<p>&quot;Okay. Let’s do this.&quot;</p>

<p>The words came out clean and clear.</p>

<p>Ross gave her a look—steady and unreadable—and nodded in return.</p>

<p>They both turned to their marks.</p>

<p>The lights adjusted. The director gave instructions softly over the headset.</p>

<p>A soft track began playing, one meant to underscore intimacy and quiet tension.</p>

<p>Corey stood frozen on the sidelines, arms crossed tightly over his chest, jaw clenched so hard it trembled.</p>

<p>The director raised his hand.</p>

<p>&quot;Quiet on set. Roll cameras.&quot;</p>

<p>And just like that, the moment began.</p>

<p>Cara stood in front of Ross under the low light of the bedroom scene, trying to push aside the ache that lingered in her chest.</p>

<p>She didn’t know if her trembling hands were from nerves or something deeper.</p>

<p>But she did know this—</p>

<p>She wasn’t acting anymore.</p>

<p>And that made the scene far more real than it was ever meant to be.</p>

<p>&quot;Are you sure about this, Cara?&quot; Ross asked, his voice low, laced with concern.</p>

<p>It wasn’t part of the script—this line was real, unscripted.</p>

<p>His eyes, half-hidden behind the sleek black mask, searched hers with something far softer than the confident, cocky energy he usually gave off.</p>

<p>Cara stood there, heart thudding in her chest.</p>

<p>The lights weren’t too bright, but everything around her suddenly felt exposed.</p>

<p>Her lips parted slightly as she tried to remember the lines—simple, playful, teasing.</p>

<p>That’s all this was supposed to be.</p>

<p>She drew in a slow breath. Then another.</p>

<p>And finally, the words came back.</p>

<p>A sly smile curved her lips, a flicker of mischief briefly covering the storm brewing behind her eyes.</p>

<p>&quot;Why?&quot; she said, her tone light. &quot;Is the famous playboy getting nervous in front of a pretty little woman like me?&quot;</p>

<p>Ross smirked. But there was a flicker in his gaze—a brief spark of something deeper.</p>

<p>&quot;Of course not,&quot; he murmured. &quot;I’m just letting you know one thing...&quot;</p>

<p>He stepped forward—close enough that she could feel the warmth of his body, the subtle scent of his cologne, a clean blend of citrus and smoke.</p>

<p>&quot;Once I start... I don’t remember the word stop anymore.&quot;</p>

<p>The moment froze. Then—</p>

<p>Ross leaned in and kissed her.</p>

<p>Softly at first.</p>

<p>His lips pressed against hers with surprising gentleness, careful and controlled—as if testing the waters, making sure she had the space to pull away if she wanted to.</p>

<p>But Cara didn’t move.</p>

<p>For the first second, she was stunned.</p>

<p>This was it—her first kiss—and it felt nothing like what she had imagined. No fireworks.</p>

<p>No slow-motion cinematic haze. Just pressure. Warmth. Tension.</p>

<p>And then—</p>

<p>Her eyes fluttered closed, and something shifted.</p>

<p>The nerves dissolved, replaced by an unfamiliar flutter low in her belly.</p>

<p>Her fingers twitched at her sides. Her breath hitched in her throat.</p>

<p>It was as if her entire body suddenly remembered something she never knew to begin with.</p>

<p>&quot;Hmmmm...&quot;</p>

<p>A soft sound escaped her lips before she could stop it.</p>

<p>Ross didn’t pull away. In fact, he deepened the kiss, just slightly. Not too much.</p>

<p>Not aggressive. But with enough certainty to tell her that he wasn’t acting anymore either.</p>

<p>His hand came up slowly, gently cupping her cheek, thumb brushing just beneath her eye.</p>

<p>She didn’t expect the jolt that came from that single touch.</p>

<p>It spread through her chest like a spark catching dry leaves.</p>

<p>What is this feeling?</p>

<p>The kiss grew warmer, slower, deeper—not in intensity, but in meaning. It wasn’t about lust.</p>

<p>It wasn’t even about the scene anymore.</p>

<p>It was about two people, standing under hot lights in front of a camera, falling into something that shouldn’t be happening but was.</p>

<p>Cara’s thoughts began to unravel.</p>

<p>She forgot the script.</p>

<p>She forgot the cameras.</p>

<p>She forgot Corey—Corey, who was probably still watching from the shadows with fists clenched and heart shattered.</p>

<p>Her mind screamed at her to pull back, to remember her place, to treat this as just another scene. But her body betrayed her.</p>

<p>She responded.</p>

<p>Her hands moved—hesitant at first, then instinctively reaching up to Ross’s chest, fingers curling slightly into his shirt as if searching for balance, or maybe reassurance.</p>

<p>His hand moved to the small of her back, resting there protectively, pulling her just an inch closer.</p>

<p>She wasn’t acting.</p>

<p>Not anymore.</p>

<p>This kiss... it wasn’t planned. Not like this.</p>

<p>And somewhere deep inside her—past the fear, the confusion, the tight ache building in her chest—Cara felt something bloom.</p>

<p>Something dangerous. Something real.</p>

<p>But it ended as quickly as it began.</p>

<p>Ross pulled away, not abruptly, but with care. As if he didn’t want to, but had to.</p>

<p>His eyes remained locked on hers. He was breathing slightly harder than before.</p>

<p>So was she.</p>

<p>Silence settled in.</p>

<p>Everyone on set was frozen. The camera kept rolling. No one dared to speak.</p>

<p>Not the director. Not the crew.</p>

<p>Not even Corey, whose fists were trembling at his sides off-stage, eyes wide in disbelief.</p>
