<p>Ashley woke up feeling… amazing.</p>

<p>It was as if she had just experienced the most restful sleep of her entire life—no nightmares, no stress, no guilt weighing on her chest.</p>

<p>Just peace. Warmth.</p>

<p>Her body still tingled faintly, as if the memories of the previous night refused to leave her skin.</p>

<p>Her eyes fluttered open, and the first thing she saw was him—Ross.</p>

<p>Or rather, Ross wearing Cyril&#x27;s face. But she didn&#x27;t know that of course.</p>

<p>He was seated at the edge of the bed, placing a steaming plate of food on the bedside table.</p>

<p>Eggs, toast, meat, a bit of fruit—nothing fancy, but the kind of meal made with care.</p>

<p>The smell alone made her stomach growl in betrayal.</p>

<p>It was only then, as she shifted slightly under the covers, that Ashley realized she was back in her own bed.</p>

<p>She blinked, disoriented.</p>

<p>&quot;Where&#x27;s Cyril?&quot; she asked softly. Her voice was hoarse from sleep and a little uncertainty.</p>

<p>Regardless of everything that had happened—everything he had done—Cyril was still her husband.</p>

<p>Ross didn&#x27;t look at her immediately. Instead, he adjusted the tray and straightened a napkin before responding.</p>

<p>&quot;I don&#x27;t know,&quot; he said casually. &quot;He&#x27;s gone. Probably holed up in some hotel somewhere in the city. He don&#x27;t have the face to talk to you now, Ashley.&quot;</p>

<p>Ashley fell silent.</p>

<p>The silence stretched between them like a taut string, one neither dared pluck too hard. She didn&#x27;t know where to begin, what to say.</p>

<p>Her emotions were too much—twisting around inside her, conflicting and confusing.</p>

<p>Part of her was furious. Furious at being played. Furious at being fooled, seduced, manipulated.</p>

<p>And yet…</p>

<p>Another part—the quiet part, the one buried deep—felt something else.</p>

<p>Something warmer.</p>

<p>If she was honest, the time she spent with Ross… it had been one of the most precious, freeing, happy moments she&#x27;d ever had. It made her feel alive. Wanted. Seen.</p>

<p>She turned to say something, anything—but Ross beat her to it.</p>

<p>&quot;Eat first. Talk later,&quot; he said gently, his voice low but firm. &quot;Remember—you&#x27;re not just eating for yourself anymore. You already have our baby inside you.&quot;</p>

<p>Her breath caught at that.</p>

<p>Ross didn&#x27;t wait for her to respond. He picked up the tray and scooped a spoonful of scrambled eggs, holding it out for her like she was delicate, like she might break.</p>

<p>Ashley stared at him, wide-eyed, torn between exasperation and something dangerously close to affection.</p>

<p>&quot;I&#x27;m not a baby,&quot; she muttered, trying to sound annoyed as she pushed his hand away. &quot;I can feed myself.&quot;</p>

<p>Ross only chuckled under his breath and let her take the tray.</p>

<p>She sat up slowly, adjusting the blanket around her chest. Even as she started to eat in silence, her thoughts refused to quiet down.</p>

<p>This—he—wasn&#x27;t supposed to make her feel this way. He had been part of the trick, part of the game that made her question everything.</p>

<p>And yet his gestures…</p>

<p>The tenderness in his voice, the way he looked at her like she was the most important person in the world—it felt real. Too real.</p>

<p>Ashley hated herself a little for enjoying it. For finding comfort in his presence.</p>

<p>For feeling the subtle swell of joy at knowing she wasn&#x27;t alone in this—not really.</p>

<p>She chewed quietly, her eyes drifting toward Ross, who now leaned against the wall, watching her—not with smugness, not with arrogance, but with a strange softness that made her heart ache.</p>

<p>And though no words were spoken, a thousand unspoken questions hovered in the air between them.</p>

<p>&quot;Perfect. That&#x27;s a good beautiful woman right there,&quot; Ross praised with a smirk as Ashley leaned back in the bed, having polished off every last bite of the meal.</p>

<p>His eyes sparkled with satisfaction, watching her like an artist admiring his finished masterpiece.</p>

<p>He hadn&#x27;t expected anything less—after all, he was the one who made it.</p>

<p>And Ross didn&#x27;t cook like an ordinary man.</p>

<p>His cooking skills had reached a level where even immortals, with their refined palates and access to heavenly delicacies, would kneel just for a single taste.</p>

<p>It wasn&#x27;t arrogance—it was truth backed by experience. His dishes didn&#x27;t just satisfy hunger; they enchanted the senses, stirred the heart, and, most importantly, softened even the most stubborn of women.</p>

<p>Ross knew that conquering a woman&#x27;s heart wasn&#x27;t always about wealth or words.</p>

<p>Sometimes, it was about flavor—about making her feel something with every bite.</p>

<p>His kitchen prowess had been one of his most powerful tools, and it had never failed him. Tonight, once again, it had done its magic.</p>

<p>&quot;Mmm…&quot; Ashley licked the corner of her lips slowly, still trying to process what she&#x27;d just eaten.</p>

<p>Her eyes remained slightly dazed, as though she&#x27;d been momentarily transported to another world.</p>

<p>&quot;What… was that?&quot;</p>

<p>She blinked, placing a hand over her stomach. It was almost as if the food hadn&#x27;t just filled her—it had awakened something in her.</p>

<p>&quot;I don&#x27;t even know what I just ate,&quot; she whispered. &quot;But it was… it was insane. Like, everything I&#x27;ve ever had before this was just cheap garbage compared to what you just made. I swear, my taste buds were crying.&quot;</p>

<p>Ross chuckled as he wiped down the counter, amused by her stunned reaction. &quot;Told you I could cook. You didn&#x27;t believe me.&quot;</p>

<p>Ashley narrowed her eyes slightly, still skeptical. &quot;Okay, but seriously—did you put something in it? A love potion, maybe?&quot; Her tone was teasing, but there was a flicker of suspicion in her gaze.</p>

<p>&quot;A love potion?&quot; Ross laughed, a deep, rich sound that filled the room.</p>

<p>He turned to her, his expression shifting into something far more confident—cocky, even.</p>

<p>&quot;Do I really look like the kind of guy who needs to resort to such low-class tricks?&quot; He tossed the cloth aside and walked over to her, leaning in close. &quot;And besides…&quot;</p>

<p>He gave her a slow, almost smug grin.</p>

<p>&quot;Why would I need a love potion when I&#x27;ve already put a baby inside you, Ashley?&quot;</p>

<p>Her breath caught. Just like that, the playful atmosphere shifted.</p>

<p>Ashley&#x27;s eyes widened, and her hand unconsciously moved to her flat belly, resting there as if trying to confirm the truth in his words.</p>

<p>She didn&#x27;t speak. Couldn&#x27;t. The weight of his sentence hung heavy in the air.</p>

<p>A baby.</p>

<p>She was quiet for a moment, the realization sinking in deeper with each passing second. The warmth of the food in her stomach now felt different—more personal, more symbolic.</p>

<p>It wasn&#x27;t just a meal anymore. It was a beginning.</p>

<p>Ross watched her with quiet satisfaction. He didn&#x27;t gloat further. He didn&#x27;t need to. Her silence was enough.</p>

<p>Ashley finally looked up at him, eyes soft with emotion and a thousand thoughts behind them.</p>

<p>&quot;…You&#x27;re impossible,&quot; she whispered, her voice barely audible.</p>

<p>Ross simply smiled. &quot;And you&#x27;re glowing already, honey.&quot;</p>

<p>A brief silence settled between them, thick with unspoken questions, before Ashley finally cut to the heart of it.</p>

<p>&quot;Who are you really?&quot; she asked, her voice low but steady.</p>

<p>Ross didn&#x27;t miss a beat. &quot;I&#x27;m a secret operative for the government. Spy work, black ops, the kind of stuff that never makes it to the news,&quot; he said coolly.</p>

<p>&quot;Don&#x27;t ask who &#x27;they&#x27; are—I wouldn&#x27;t tell you even if you did. That&#x27;s why no one knows your husband Cyril has a brother. A twin, at that. My records? Wiped clean.&quot;</p>

<p>It was an easy, convenient cover—one that gave him room to maneuver and just enough mystery to be believable.</p>

<p>Ashley stared at him, trying to process the weight of it. It sounded like something out of a movie, but something about the calm in his voice made her believe it—at least a little.</p>

<p>&quot;What… what do you want to do now?&quot; she asked quietly. &quot;Are you leaving? Me and our baby?&quot;</p>

<p>She&#x27;d seen movies about secret agents. Mysterious men living double lives, vanishing for months, risking everything.</p>

<p>She didn&#x27;t know how accurate those were—but the thought of being left behind, raising a child alone, filled her with anxiety.</p>

<p>&quot;Simple,&quot; Ross said, flashing a half-smile. &quot;I&#x27;ll take care of you and our baby, of course. But with the nature of my job, I might disappear for long stretches. It&#x27;s not ideal—but I&#x27;ll always come back.&quot;</p>

<p>To him, it was a manageable situation. A little side story that could even add some thrill to his life.</p>

<p>Ashley took a long, slow breath. Her thoughts spun in a whirlwind—too many changes, too fast. Her heart was conflicted. Ross was offering a future, but it was one laced with uncertainty.</p>

<p>&quot;Let me think first,&quot; she said softly. Her voice trembled just enough to reveal how overwhelmed she felt.</p>

<p>Ross gave a small nod, respectful of her space. He knew better than to push.</p>

<p>After a long pause, Ashley finally looked up. Her eyes were tired but resolute.</p>

<p>&quot;I want to talk to my husband first,&quot; she said.</p>

<p>&quot;Of course,&quot; Ross replied gently. &quot;I&#x27;ll even help you set it up.&quot;</p>

<p>Ashley&#x27;s lips curved into a faint smile, the first in a while. &quot;Thank you, Lyric.&quot;</p>

<p>She hoped, deep down, that somehow she could return to a peaceful life—even if peace now felt more complicated than ever before.</p>
