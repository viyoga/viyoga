<p>A day later, the flying boat finally descended from the skies and landed in a remote forest far from the Ghost Wood Forest.</p>

<p>The massive vessel settled gently onto a clearing, causing nearby trees to sway from the pressure generated by its powerful formations.</p>

<p>Birds and magical beasts scattered in every direction, frightened by the arrival of such a grand artifact.</p>

<p>&quot;Everyone, we’re here,&quot; Ross said.</p>

<p>One after another, the passengers disembarked from the flying boat. More than two thousand people stepped onto solid ground and stretched their bodies after spending a day traveling through the skies.</p>

<p>Once everyone had safely gotten off, the enormous flying boat began to glow. Golden runes lit up across its surface before the vessel rapidly shrank. A moment later, it disappeared completely.</p>

<p>The children immediately noticed.</p>

<p>&quot;What? Where’s the flying thingy, Mom?&quot; one of Ross’s little children cried. &quot;I want to ride it more! Waaa!&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;Me too!&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;Bring it back!&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;Bring it back now! Waaaa!&quot; Several children began crying at once. The adults exchanged helpless smiles.</p>

<p>Many of the children had spent the entire journey running around the flying boat, treating it as a giant playground. To them, the trip had ended far too quickly. Ross simply smiled as he watched the scene.</p>

<p>Kids would always be kids. No matter what world they lived in, children remained children. They laughed easily. They cried easily. They became attached to the strangest things.</p>

<p>The flying boat was merely a transportation artifact, but in their eyes it was the greatest toy in existence.</p>

<p>&quot;They still have a lot of growing up to do,&quot; Ross thought.</p>

<p>The mothers quickly comforted their children while promising that they would ride another flying boat in the future. Eventually the crying subsided. A minute later, Ross turned toward the rest of the group.</p>

<p>More than two thousand people stood behind him. All of them were former citizens from Earth. There were guards, servants, family members, followers, and trusted subordinates.</p>

<p>All of them were waiting for his next command. Ross nodded.</p>

<p>&quot;Let’s go. We need to walk for about an hour before we arrive at our new home.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;Yes, Ross!&quot; The group immediately began moving. Fortunately, this world was vastly different from Earth. The air itself contained spiritual energy, nourishing all living things.</p>

<p>The trees were taller, the rivers were wider, and even ordinary animals were much stronger than their earthly counterparts. As they traveled through the forest, they encountered numerous magical beasts.</p>

<p>Some resembled wolves with crimson fur. Others looked like giant snakes covered in scales as hard as steel. There were even strange birds with wingspans reaching several meters.</p>

<p>However, none of them posed a threat. Brandon and his men quickly demonstrated why Ross trusted them. Whenever a magical beast appeared, it was swiftly eliminated.</p>

<p>Some were killed with guns. Others were frightened away by overwhelming one punch given by Brandon that easily killed one large wild beast in the form of a bear.</p>

<p>Thud.</p>

<p>The massive corpse crashed onto the ground. The surrounding children stared with wide eyes.</p>

<p>&quot;So cool!&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;Uncle Brandon is amazing!&quot;</p>

<p>Brandon merely smiled. He had a soft spot to Ross’s children. To him, defeating such creatures required almost no effort.</p>

<p>As a result, what should have been a dangerous journey through a wilderness filled with magical beasts felt more like a leisurely walk through a park.</p>

<p>The group continued onward without suffering a single casualty. Time slowly passed. More than an hour later, the dense forest finally began to thin. The trees became less crowded.</p>

<p>Signs of civilization started appearing. A dirt road emerged. Then came wooden fences. Then farms. Finally, the group reached their destination. A small town appeared before them.</p>

<p>Smoke rose from chimneys. Merchants could be seen walking through the streets. Children played near wooden houses. Farmers worked nearby fields.</p>

<p>Compared to the mighty cities of cultivators, the town was modest and unremarkable. Yet it was peaceful. Ross stopped and observed the settlement quietly.</p>

<p>Many townspeople also noticed the massive group approaching. More than two thousand people entering a small town was impossible to ignore.</p>

<p>Curious gazes immediately landed on Ross and his followers. Some townspeople looked nervous. Others looked surprised. A few simply wondered who these newcomers were. Ross smiled faintly.</p>

<p>The group entered the town without incident. The moment they arrived, Servant Lin immediately got busy.</p>

<p>As someone who had served under Bone Emperor Cyrus Thorn, handling mundane matters like purchasing land and arranging accommodations was a simple task.</p>

<p>With enough spirit stones and influence, many problems ceased to be problems.</p>

<p>Within a few hours, Servant Lin had already secured a large piece of land on the outskirts of town along with numerous buildings that could temporarily house Ross’s followers.</p>

<p>&quot;Master, everything has been arranged,&quot; Servant Lin reported respectfully. Ross nodded in satisfaction.</p>

<p>&quot;Good work.&quot;</p>

<p>Servant Lin nearly sighed in relief. Earning Ross’s approval always felt like surviving a great calamity. Soon, Ross’s group began moving into their new residences.</p>

<p>However, their arrival had already attracted the attention of the entire town. More than two thousand strangers appearing out of nowhere was impossible to ignore.</p>

<p>The local residents gathered in small groups and whispered among themselves while secretly observing the newcomers.</p>

<p>&quot;Who are these people?&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;I’ve never seen them before.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;There are so many of them.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;And why are they all speaking such a strange language?&quot; The townspeople looked confused.</p>

<p>The language spoken by Ross and the people from Earth was completely different from the language of this cultivation world.</p>

<p>To the locals, the words spoken by Ross’s followers sounded bizarre and incomprehensible. It was as though they were hearing an alien tongue.</p>

<p>&quot;I can’t understand a single word they’re saying.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;Neither can I.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;Could they be foreigners from some distant kingdom?&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;Shhh! Lower your voice!&quot; One middle-aged man hurriedly warned his companions.</p>

<p>&quot;Don’t you see the cultivator leading them? He’s strong!&quot; The man pointed at Servant Lin. The others immediately became quiet.</p>

<p>Although most of the townspeople were ordinary mortals, they could still sense that Ross and some of his companions were not ordinary people. Cultivators possessed a unique presence.</p>

<p>The day ended and Ross and his group had their first night in this town.</p>
