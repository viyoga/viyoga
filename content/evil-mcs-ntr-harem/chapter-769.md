<p>Heaven.</p>

<p>She sat still as stone on the edge of the couch, her body frozen, her breath caught painfully in her chest.</p>

<p>Her hands gripped the fabric of her shorts tightly, knuckles going white.</p>

<p>No...</p>

<p>Her heartbeat thundered in her ears.</p>

<p>Her mind screamed at her to breathe, to blink, to do something, but all she could do was stare.</p>

<p>Because she knew that face.</p>

<p>Intimately.</p>

<p>Personally.</p>

<p>Painfully.</p>

<p>It wasn’t just some actor or celebrity from a magazine.</p>

<p>It was him.</p>

<p>The man who had once completed her world.</p>

<p>And now, he was standing in the house—this house—as the new guest.</p>

<p>&quot;Heaven?&quot; Tianna beside her asked, nudging her lightly. &quot;You okay? You look like you’ve seen a ghost.&quot;</p>

<p>But she couldn’t answer.</p>

<p>Her throat was tight, her voice stuck somewhere behind the panic rising in her chest.</p>

<p>It had been not long ago that she saw him last.</p>

<p>But all it took was one look at him—one glance into those cold, familiar eyes—for every good meaningful memory to come rushing back.</p>

<p>His voice.</p>

<p>His face.</p>

<p>His stature.</p>

<p>She barely heard the others reacting around her.</p>

<p>&quot;Is he a model?&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;Do you think he’s here to shake things up?&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;Maybe it’s another twist! Like a love triangle or something.&quot;</p>

<p>Only one person knew the real twist.</p>

<p>And she was sitting there, drowning in it.</p>

<p>Heaven’s chest rose and fell as she tried to steady herself. Her thoughts spiraled.</p>

<p>The man—Steve—walked confidently into the living room, his gaze locked onto Heaven. His lips parted, clearly about to speak to her.</p>

<p>But before he could say a single word, a tall, imposing figure stepped directly into his path.</p>

<p>Ross.</p>

<p>The mood in the room shifted instantly. Gone was the casual curiosity from earlier—now it was thick with tension.</p>

<p>&quot;Back up, boy,&quot; Ross said coolly, his voice low and authoritative. &quot;She’s mine already.&quot;</p>

<p>Steve’s eyes narrowed. His jaw clenched.</p>

<p>&quot;Fuck you.&quot; The words were sharp, almost a growl. His fists curled tightly at his sides, knuckles pale. He looked ready to swing—but he didn’t.</p>

<p>Not because he didn’t want to.</p>

<p>But because he remembered.</p>

<p>He remembered the House Boss’s warning. One punch, and he’d be thrown out. Game over.</p>

<p>So he held himself back. Barely.</p>

<p>Ross, on the other hand, looked completely unbothered. He smirked, eyes gleaming with amusement—and something colder beneath.</p>

<p>&quot;I don’t need any of that attitude from you,&quot; Ross said, stepping a little closer. &quot;Heaven already does that to me... and more.&quot;</p>

<p>He leaned in just enough to make it sting.</p>

<p>&quot;Sad for you, boy. I got her cherry. And now?&quot; Ross’s grin widened. &quot;You won’t ever touch her again.&quot;</p>

<p>Gasps echoed from the onlookers, some already whispering. The whole room had gone still, sensing a storm brewing right in front of them.</p>

<p>Steve looked like he might explode.</p>

<p>But then—</p>

<p>&quot;D...&quot; a voice cut through the tension like a blade.</p>

<p>Both men turned.</p>

<p>It was Heaven.</p>

<p>She stood a few feet away, eyes locked on Ross, her expression conflicted. She took a slow breath before speaking.</p>

<p>&quot;Hmmm?&quot; Ross asked, his tone casual—but his eyes were still locked on Steve, watching for the slightest twitch.</p>

<p>&quot;Can I... can I speak with Steve for a minute?&quot; Heaven asked softly. &quot;Alone, if that’s possible.&quot;</p>

<p>Whispers rippled through the room again.</p>

<p>Ross turned his head slowly, finally looking at her.</p>

<p>He studied her expression—calm, hesitant, a little guilty. She wasn’t angry, and she wasn’t afraid. But there was something in her voice. Something that told him she needed to clear something up.</p>

<p>Ross tilted his head. &quot;You sure about that?&quot;</p>

<p>Heaven nodded. &quot;I just need a moment. To explain. I owe him that much.&quot;</p>

<p>Ross was silent for a few beats. Then, finally, he replied with a smirk.</p>

<p>&quot;I trust you, Heaven,&quot; Ross said calmly, his eyes never leaving Steve. &quot;But I don’t trust him. So yeah—go ahead and talk, explain, whatever. But I’ll be there.&quot;</p>

<p>His voice was cool, resolute.</p>

<p>&quot;Either that... or don’t bother. Your choice.&quot;</p>

<p>The tension thickened.</p>

<p>Heaven was silent for a moment, caught between two worlds—past and present.</p>

<p>Then, without a word, she stepped toward Ross and gently wrapped her arms around his.</p>

<p>Her soft curves pressed against his muscular arm, her cheek brushing his shoulder.</p>

<p>It was a small gesture—but a loud message. She had made her choice, at least for now.</p>

<p>Steve’s eyes darkened. His jaw tightened as his gaze lingered on the intimate display.</p>

<p>The sight of Heaven, the woman he had come all this way for, clinging to another man—especially Ross—was a bitter pill to swallow.</p>

<p>Ross smirked, clearly enjoying the moment.</p>

<p>&quot;Guess Heaven really is mine, boy,&quot; he said, voice mocking.</p>

<p>Steve didn’t respond.</p>

<p>He didn’t flinch.</p>

<p>But his fists curled even tighter, knuckles white, the tendons on his neck taut with restrained fury.</p>

<p>Several minutes later, the group made their way to the pool area—an open, breezy space lit by glowing orbs and soft ambient light.</p>

<p>The tension hadn’t faded; if anything, it simmered even more now, like heat rising off the water.</p>

<p>Ross lounged in one of the deck chairs with Heaven nestled beside him, her fingers laced with his.</p>

<p>Tianna was seated on the other side, legs crossed, watching the drama unfold with mild curiosity and amusement.</p>

<p>Steve stood across from them, arms crossed, his gaze locked on Heaven but flickering toward Ross every few seconds like he was just waiting for an excuse.</p>

<p>No one spoke for a long moment. The silence said more than words ever could.</p>

<p>But just as things were settling into that uneasy quiet... the soft click of the sliding door opened again.</p>

<p>Footsteps.</p>

<p>Everyone turned.</p>

<p>And then—</p>

<p>Another person stepped into the pool area.</p>

<p>Someone none of them had expected.</p>

<p>Heaven and Tianna exchanged a quick, knowing glance. No words were spoken, but the understanding between them was immediate and unspoken.</p>

<p>They both sensed it—felt it deep in the pit of their stomachs.</p>

<p>This person’s unexpected arrival, the timing, the way she looked at Ross with such quiet intensity... it was more than just coincidence.</p>

<p>The air seemed to thicken, charged with a new kind of electricity.</p>

<p>Every small movement, every breath, held weight.</p>

<p>It was as if the calm before a storm had settled over the pool area, pressing down on everyone’s shoulders.</p>
