<p>Ross didn’t blame them—power always came with a price, and none of them knew what that price would be.</p>

<p>&quot;I won’t force anyone,&quot; he said after a long pause, his tone softening.</p>

<p>&quot;This is your choice. Whether you take one or not, you’re still my family. You’ll still have a place here, in this bunker, under my protection.&quot;</p>

<p>He looked around the hall, meeting every gaze—one by one.</p>

<p>&quot;But remember this: the world outside isn’t waiting for us to decide. It’s changing every day. If we don’t grow stronger, we’ll be swept aside. These stones... they’re our chance to change that.&quot;</p>

<p>Silence followed once more, but it wasn’t the same kind as before.</p>

<p>Now, it was filled with tension, with the quiet thrum of courage being born in hesitant hearts.</p>

<p>Ross watched them closely.</p>

<p>He knew this moment would decide everything—who was brave enough to step forward, and who would choose the safety of the familiar.</p>

<p>Either way, the future of his people would begin here, in this hall, beneath the glow of the heart stones that pulsed like living hearts in his hand.</p>

<p>&quot;I’ll go first, husband,&quot; Althea said, her voice calm but resolute.</p>

<p>The crowd fell silent the moment she spoke.</p>

<p>Even among the hundreds gathered, no one dared to interrupt her.</p>

<p>Ross turned to her with a faint smile tugging at his lips.</p>

<p>He descended from the platform, the faint ripple of his power making the air hum.</p>

<p>In his hand was a heart stone—a bloody crystal so pure that its light seemed to pulse in rhythm with his heartbeat.</p>

<p>He handed it to her gently, his fingers brushing hers for a brief moment.</p>

<p>Not that she needed it, of course.</p>

<p>Nobody but Ross knew the truth: Althea was the strongest being on the planet, second only to Ross himself.</p>

<p>Her strength was already beyond comprehension, her deeds hidden and waited for the perfect timing to show themselves.</p>

<p>Today would be the day that everything would be revealed.</p>

<p>But this demonstration wasn’t for her.</p>

<p>It was for everyone else—to inspire, to unite, and to show what the heart stones could do.</p>

<p>The strongest woman alive was willing to take the first step.</p>

<p>Althea smiled faintly, understanding the unspoken reason. &quot;Then let’s give them a good show,&quot; she said softly.</p>

<p>Without hesitation, she lifted the heart stone to her lips and swallowed it whole.</p>

<p>The reaction was immediate.</p>

<p>A deep hum vibrated through the air, resonating in the bones of everyone nearby.</p>

<p>The faint glow of the stone spread through her body, veins lighting up like threads of molten gold beneath her skin.</p>

<p>Her breathing grew shallow, her expression tightening as if she were struggling to contain something immense within her.</p>

<p>The audience leaned forward, some clutching their robes, others holding their breath.</p>

<p>Even though the heart stone’s power was immense, to Althea it was almost nothing—a drop of water falling into an endless ocean of her already boundless energy.</p>

<p>Still, she played her part perfectly.</p>

<p>Her body trembled slightly, sweat glistening on her skin as she released a soft, strained gasp.</p>

<p>&quot;I... I feel strange,&quot; she said, her voice deliberately wavering.</p>

<p>Then, she raised her hands.</p>

<p>For a heartbeat, nothing happened.</p>

<p>Then...</p>

<p>&quot;Whooosh!&quot; Flames erupted from her palms. The crowd cried out in awe.</p>

<p>The fire was wild at first, licking and snapping at the air like a feral beast.</p>

<p>The heat distorted the space around her, turning the air into waves of gold and crimson.</p>

<p>Yet, under Althea’s control, the chaos slowly gave way to grace.</p>

<p>The fire bent to her will, swirling in perfect harmony around her figure.</p>

<p>Her long hair fluttered in the heat, her violet eyes reflecting the light of her flames.</p>

<p>Gasps rippled through the crowd.</p>

<p>&quot;She’s controlling fire!&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;Incredible! So that’s the power of the heart stone!&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;Her aura... it’s overwhelming!&quot;</p>

<p>The ground beneath her feet had begun to blacken and melt, yet she remained untouched, poised and calm amid her inferno.</p>

<p>She clenched her fists, and the flames condensed into a single orb that hovered above her palm—then vanished in an instant.</p>

<p>Silence.</p>

<p>Then applause.</p>

<p>Shouts of admiration erupted, and the tension that had gripped the crowd gave way to exhilaration.</p>

<p>Althea gave a graceful nod toward her husband and stepped aside, her expression soft but proud.</p>

<p>Ross met her gaze, a faint smirk playing on his lips. He didn’t need to say a word—she had played her role perfectly.</p>

<p>&quot;I’m next, Ross!&quot; a young man suddenly shouted, unable to contain his excitement.</p>

<p>His voice broke the lingering stillness as dozens of others immediately followed suit.</p>

<p>&quot;Me too!&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;Let me try!&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;I want to know what kind of power I’ll get!&quot;</p>

<p>They surged forward, faces bright with anticipation.</p>

<p>Some of them were trembling, others grinning like children before a festival.</p>

<p>The thought that they could awaken power like Althea’s filled them with wild hope.</p>

<p>Ross’s eyes swept over the crowd. The corner of his lips curved upward in quiet satisfaction.</p>

<p>The demonstration had gone exactly as planned.</p>

<p>High above, the ceiling shimmered faintly with the lingering heat of Althea’s flames.</p>

<p>The scent of ozone and smoke hung in the air, mixing with the collective excitement of hundreds of hearts beating in unison.</p>

<p>The age of ordinary men was over.</p>

<p>And the era of the heart stones had just begun.</p>

<p>***</p>

<p>Two hours later, the great hall of Ross’s stronghold had transformed into a place of dazzling chaos and wonder.</p>

<p>Nearly every adult had now taken a heart stone, and the air itself shimmered with power.</p>

<p>Beams of light streaked through the ceiling as ordinary people tested their newfound abilities.</p>

<p>Laughter, shouts, and bursts of energy filled every corner of the immense chamber.</p>

<p>Even Ross’s undead guards—stoic and expressionless as ever—had stepped forward to receive theirs.</p>

<p>Brandon too had followed suit, though both he and the guards knew it was merely for show.</p>

<p>Their loyalty and strength were already absolute, gifts that Ross had bestowed long ago.</p>

<p>Still, the symbolism mattered and everyone had to act in a play.</p>
