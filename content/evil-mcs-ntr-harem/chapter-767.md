<p>This was then followed by Heaven screaming Ross’s name so passionately it silenced the hallway.</p>

<p>What truly amazed everyone, though, wasn’t the intensity.</p>

<p>It was the fact that no one saw anything.</p>

<p>Not even a glimpse.</p>

<p>The covers on Ross’s bed never moved aside. Nothing peeked out.</p>

<p>Just a giant, oddly still blanket—and all the chaos happening beneath it.</p>

<p>Now, as the group gathered around the kitchen island sipping coffee and nibbling toast, the discussion naturally turned toward Ross’s &quot;midnight show.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;I swear, that guy’s using a magic blanket,&quot; Jason said, shaking his head as he poured cereal into his bowl.</p>

<p>&quot;Like, how the hell do you do that for hours and not even a foot pops out?&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;Forget the blanket,&quot; one said, already laughing,</p>

<p>&quot;How’s he not dead? Two women. Loud as hell. And didn’t it sound like they both finished multiple times?&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;Bro’s got infinite stamina,&quot; another housemate joked. &quot;I need whatever supplements he’s on.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;Maybe he is the supplement,&quot; someone added with a wink, sending the group into another wave of laughter.</p>

<p>They kept going, piling on theories:</p>

<p>&quot;He’s got a cloning jutsu. There’s no way one guy’s doing both at once.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;Maybe he’s an alien. That would explain a lot.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;Or a sex demon. A handsome one.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;He’s not even trying to hide it either. Like, ’Yeah, I bang Heaven and Tianna. What of it?’ I’d be cocky too, damn.&quot;</p>

<p>Lily sat nearby, pretending to focus on her breakfast, but her heart wasn’t in it.</p>

<p>She forced a small smile when others laughed, but inside, she was drowning in a swirl of emotions.</p>

<p>Ross.</p>

<p>That name felt different now.</p>

<p>The man behind the magic blanket... the same man who had kissed her last night like she was the only girl in the world.</p>

<p>The same man who had touched her like he already knew exactly how to unlock her.</p>

<p>And yet, he had stopped.</p>

<p>Refused to go further.</p>

<p>Refused, despite being the very same man everyone here believed to be insatiable and selfish.</p>

<p>That contradiction played over and over in her mind.</p>

<p>She stabbed at a piece of melon on her plate.</p>

<p>Of all people, it had to be him, she thought, cheeks heating again as flashes of the kiss came back.</p>

<p>His hand on her waist. His lips.</p>

<p>The things he said before leaving her breathless in the kitchen.</p>

<p>He was everything she told herself she didn’t want—arrogant, promiscuous, unpredictable.</p>

<p>And yet... last night, in that moment, he had shown more restraint and control than most guys she’d dated in the real world.</p>

<p>&quot;Lily, you good?&quot; someone asked, pulling her out of her thoughts.</p>

<p>&quot;Huh? Yeah,&quot; she replied quickly, flashing a smile that didn’t quite reach her eyes. &quot;Just tired. That hangover’s no joke.&quot;</p>

<p>She excused herself soon after, heading to the bathroom with her coffee in hand. But inside, her mind was running circles around itself.</p>

<p>Everyone was making jokes about Ross like he was a walking meme, a legend, a player beyond reason.</p>

<p>But only she knew that last night, he could’ve had her.</p>

<p>And didn’t.</p>

<p>And that made him even more dangerous than any of them realized.</p>

<p>***</p>

<p>Lunch came and went, followed by a lazy afternoon.</p>

<p>Then dinner passed, and still, Lily managed to avoid him—barely.</p>

<p>She kept herself busy, helping in the kitchen, playing card games with some of the others, and laughing a little louder than usual to mask her nerves.</p>

<p>But deep down, she knew it was only a matter of time.</p>

<p>And then, it happened.</p>

<p>Ross stood up from the table where he’d been playing a popular board game with Heaven and Tianna.</p>

<p>With a casual stretch and a smirk on his face, he turned to the two women beside him.</p>

<p>&quot;Excuse me for a bit, ladies. I just need to talk to someone real quick,&quot; he said smoothly.</p>

<p>Heaven raised an eyebrow, amused.</p>

<p>Tianna just smirked knowingly, as if she’d already figured out what was about to happen.</p>

<p>Ross stepped away from the table and started walking—slowly, deliberately—through the living room.</p>

<p>His eyes were locked on one person. And the moment he moved, the rest of the house noticed.</p>

<p>&quot;Uh-oh. Look,&quot; someone whispered. &quot;Big D’s on the move.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;Who’s he going after now?&quot; another girl asked, eyes wide with anticipation.</p>

<p>&quot;Is it me?&quot; someone else said, half-joking and half-hopeful.</p>

<p>The girls in the living room began whispering among themselves, their playful chatter laced with nervous excitement.</p>

<p>Ross’s reputation wasn’t just legend—it was dangerous.</p>

<p>His charm was as smooth as silk, and his prowess in bed had become mythological in the house.</p>

<p>The fact that he walked with such confidence only made him more alluring.</p>

<p>Every step he took had heads turning. All eyes followed him.</p>

<p>Ross didn’t speed up. He didn’t speak. He didn’t even glance sideways.</p>

<p>He just walked—like a lion approaching its prey.</p>

<p>And then he stopped.</p>

<p>Right in front of Lily.</p>

<p>A loud gasp cut through the room, followed by someone shrieking, &quot;Oh my God! He’s gunning for Lily!&quot;</p>

<p>The room went wild.</p>

<p>Mouths dropped. Some girls clutched their imaginary pearls.</p>

<p>Others started whispering even faster, glancing back and forth between Ross and Lily like they were about to witness a royal proposal—or a public execution.</p>

<p>Lily froze.</p>

<p>She’d tried to keep her head down all day, acting normal, pretending last night never happened.</p>

<p>But now, with Ross standing right in front of her, she couldn’t pretend anymore.</p>

<p>Her heart slammed against her chest as the heat rushed to her face.</p>

<p>Ross leaned in slightly, his voice low and private despite the attention.</p>

<p>&quot;We need to talk.&quot;</p>

<p>His eyes held hers, steady and unreadable. No teasing. No smirk.</p>

<p>Just that same quiet confidence that had left her breathless last night.</p>

<p>Lily opened her mouth to respond, but the words caught in her throat.</p>

<p>She could feel every stare in the room, every breath held in anticipation, waiting to see what would happen next.</p>

<p>&quot;There’s nothing to talk about. Please... just leave.&quot;</p>

<p>Lily’s voice was soft but firm.</p>
