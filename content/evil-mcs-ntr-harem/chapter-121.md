<p>&quot;Stand up.&quot;</p>

<p>The cold, biting command yanked Julian out of his daze.</p>

<p>His body ached, and his mind felt like it was teetering on the edge of collapse.</p>

<p>He&#x27;d spent the night on the hard floor, sleepless and haunted by the memories of April—the woman he once believed loved him—surrendering herself completely to Ross.</p>

<p>Every vivid detail replayed mercilessly in his head.</p>

<p>April&#x27;s moans, her shameless pleas, the way she begged Ross to take her harder, faster—it was a nightmare he couldn&#x27;t escape.</p>

<p>She hadn&#x27;t just given in; she&#x27;d craved it. That realization cut deeper than any physical wound.</p>

<p>&quot;I said, stand up!&quot;</p>

<p>Bang!</p>

<p>A sharp kick landed squarely on Julian&#x27;s side, jolting him back to reality. Brandon loomed over him, his face twisted with disdain.</p>

<p>The impact sent a shockwave of pain through Julian&#x27;s body, leaving him gasping for air. It felt like his back might snap in two from the sheer force.</p>

<p>&quot;Stop—please!&quot; Julian croaked, curling into himself as he shielded his head with trembling hands. &quot;I&#x27;ll get up. Just… give me a second.&quot;</p>

<p>Brandon sneered but didn&#x27;t strike again.</p>

<p>Groaning, Julian forced himself to his feet, every movement a reminder of his bruised and battered state.</p>

<p>His legs wobbled beneath him, but he stood, head bowed like a man awaiting judgment.</p>

<p>It was only then, as he glanced around, that he realized something was off. This wasn&#x27;t the same room he&#x27;d been in last night.</p>

<p>The dim lighting, the unfamiliar furniture—it was all different. Panic bubbled up in his chest as his thoughts immediately went to April.</p>

<p>Where is she? What happened to her?</p>

<p>The questions burned on the tip of his tongue, but he didn&#x27;t dare voice them.</p>

<p>Brandon&#x27;s eyes, cold and unyielding, locked onto his, daring him to speak. Before Julian could gather the courage, Brandon broke the silence.</p>

<p>&quot;Walk.&quot;</p>

<p>The single word carried an unspoken threat, one Julian wasn&#x27;t brave enough to test. Gritting his teeth, he obeyed.</p>

<p>Each step was agony, but he forced himself forward, following Brandon as they moved through unfamiliar hallways.</p>

<p>The oppressive silence stretched between them, broken only by the sound of their footsteps.</p>

<p>The air was heavy, suffocating, and Julian&#x27;s mind raced with possibilities.</p>

<p>Were they taking him somewhere to finish him off? To humiliate him further? And what about April? Was she still with Ross? Was she even alive?</p>

<p>The questions gnawed at him, but the pain in his body and the looming presence of Brandon kept him silent.</p>

<p>After what felt like an eternity, they emerged into the cold morning air.</p>

<p>The city lay ahead in the distance, a stark reminder of the life Julian had once known—a life that now felt impossibly far away.</p>

<p>They took Julian&#x27;s car. Brandon drove, his face concealed behind the same menacing demon mask, while Julian sat in silence in the passenger seat.</p>

<p>The tension was suffocating, the hum of the engine the only sound between them.</p>

<p>Julian kept his gaze fixed on the road ahead, too afraid to look at Brandon or ask any questions.</p>

<p>An hour later, the car rolled to a stop in front of the university.</p>

<p>Brandon stepped out of the driver&#x27;s seat, moving with deliberate ease, as if he had all the time in the world.</p>

<p>Without a word, he reached into his pocket and tossed something into Julian&#x27;s lap.</p>

<p>Julian flinched, then looked down to see his phone.</p>

<p>His stomach churned when he unlocked it and saw what Brandon had loaded onto the screen—videos and photos from the previous night.</p>

<p>The images were horrifyingly clear: April writhing under Ross, her cries of ecstasy filling the room, while Julian was in the corner, caught in the ultimate humiliation.</p>

<p>There was even a clip of him—his shame laid bare—pleasuring himself as he watched.</p>

<p>His blood ran cold.</p>

<p>&quot;Speak a word about last night,&quot; Brandon said, his voice low and sharp, &quot;and I&#x27;ll release everything. Everyone will see you jacking off to your girlfriend getting fucked. You&#x27;re nothing but a pathetic little worm.&quot;</p>

<p>Julian&#x27;s head snapped up, his eyes wide with panic, but Brandon wasn&#x27;t finished. He stepped closer, his shadow looming over the car.</p>

<p>&quot;And after that?&quot; Brandon added, his voice even darker now, &quot;I&#x27;ll come to your house and personally cut your family&#x27;s heads off in front of you. Every. Single. One.&quot;</p>

<p>It was the longest string of words Brandon had spoken so far, and each one landed like a punch to Julian&#x27;s gut.</p>

<p>His mouth went dry, and his heart pounded in his chest as Brandon turned and walked away without waiting for a response.</p>

<p>Julian sat frozen, his grip on the phone tightening. He wanted to scream, to cry, to fight back somehow—but he knew he couldn&#x27;t. Not against them. Not after this.</p>

<p>He glanced out the window, watching Brandon&#x27;s retreating figure disappear into the crowd of students. For the first time in his life, Julian felt truly powerless.</p>

<p>Julian didn&#x27;t see his girlfriend, April, that day. He had expected her parents to grow concerned and contact him, but no call ever came.</p>

<p>It only deepened his unease, making him suspect that Ross had provided some convincing alibi to keep them in the dark.</p>

<p>As for his own family, Julian kept things simple. He told them he&#x27;d gone to a party the previous night and had gotten drunk.</p>

<p>His apology for worrying them seemed to suffice, and they didn&#x27;t press him further.</p>

<p>But the weight of uncertainty gnawed at him. Alone in his room, he whispered, &quot;Where is she?&quot; The question hung in the air, unanswered.</p>

<p>A terrible thought crept into his mind—what if April was dead? Yet, he clung to hope, praying desperately for her safety.</p>

<p>His prayers were answered the next day.</p>

<p>When he saw April again, relief and dread collided within him.</p>

<p>She was alive, still as beautiful as ever, her smile and composure giving nothing away to the world.</p>

<p>But Julian knew better. He alone understood the horrors she had endured at Ross&#x27;s hands.</p>

<p>Her outward perfection was a mask, hiding the scars he couldn&#x27;t erase.</p>

<p>&quot;Julian!&quot; April cried the moment she saw him, her voice trembling with raw emotion.</p>

<p>She threw herself into his arms, clutching him desperately as sobs wracked her body.</p>

<p>Julian held her tightly, his heart breaking at the sound of her tears.</p>

<p>&quot;It&#x27;s okay,&quot; he murmured, his voice soft and reassuring. &quot;It&#x27;s not your fault. None of this is your fault. Nobody wanted this to happen.&quot;</p>

<p>He gently stroked her hair, trying to offer her the comfort she so desperately needed. Julian forced himself to be strong, for her sake and his own.</p>

<p>He wanted to believe they could heal together, that they could rebuild what had been shattered.</p>

<p>But alas, fate has a bad twist in store for Julian.</p>

<p>Ross had already marked April as his possession, and resisting him was like trying to grasp the sun with bare hands—impossible and destined to burn.</p>

<p>No one could take what belonged to Ross, let alone steal it away.</p>

<p>April was trapped, whether she realized it or not, and Julian could do nothing but hold her close, clinging to the fragile hope that somehow, they could defy the odds.</p>

<p>***</p>

<p>That night...</p>

<p>&quot;Do you really have to go?&quot; Julian asked softly once April&#x27;s classes were over.</p>

<p>His voice was tinged with desperation, his eyes searching hers for any sign of hope.</p>

<p>April hesitated, guilt flashing across her face. &quot;I have to,&quot; she said with a heavy sigh, her shoulders slumping. &quot;I can&#x27;t refuse him.&quot;</p>

<p>Her voice wavered as she spoke, the weight of her words dragging her down.</p>

<p>Ross had her in his grasp, wielding the damning sex videos like a weapon. She hated the power he held over her, but there was no escaping it.</p>

<p>Ding!</p>

<p>The sound of a notification cut through the tense silence. April glanced at her phone and froze.</p>

<p>Her eyes widened in panic, and without another word, she turned and bolted, leaving Julian standing there in stunned silence.</p>

<p>He watched her retreating figure, the ache in his chest growing unbearable.</p>

<p>She hadn&#x27;t even offered an excuse, and yet, Julian understood. The message was from Ross.</p>

<p>The realization hit him like a knife to the heart.</p>

<p>He stood there, rooted to the spot, letting the pain seep into every corner of his being.</p>

<p>&quot;Fuck you, Ross!&quot; Julian screamed, his voice trembling with the sting of bitter defeat.</p>

<p>His chest heaved, and it felt as though his heart might explode from the sheer force of his rage.</p>
