<p>Ross paused for a brief moment, turning his head just enough to give her a quick, amused glance. His lips curved into a small, teasing smirk.</p>

<p>&quot;You make me blush, Maya. I hope you enjoy the show, then.&quot; His words were light, but the underlying coldness in his tone made it clear he wasn&#x27;t joking.</p>

<p>With that, Ross raised his hands, and a hammer materialized in his grip.</p>

<p>It gleamed under the dim lighting, its weight evident as he held it casually, almost as though it were nothing more than an everyday tool.</p>

<p>Maya had a fleeting thought—How did he get that hammer?—but the memory of his previous cryptic answers stopped her from voicing the question.</p>

<p>Still, she trembled in place when she remembered something.</p>

<p>Maya could not help but shiver at the memory of this very same hammer.</p>

<p>The sound of it, the brutal impact, the screams—it all came rushing back to her in a tidal wave.</p>

<p>She felt her stomach churn, a wave of nausea rising within her.</p>

<p>For a moment, it felt like the world around her spun, and she almost puked right then and there.</p>

<p>Her throat tightened, and the bitter taste of acid bile rose up, threatening to escape.</p>

<p>But she managed to hold it back, her body trembling as she fought to regain control.</p>

<p>She swallowed hard, the taste lingering in the back of her throat, but she forced herself to breathe deeply, trying to push the memories—and the sickness—away.</p>

<p>Maya stayed silent instead.</p>

<p>&quot;No! No, please!&quot; one of the gangsters shouted, his face contorting with panic as he scrambled to free himself from the ropes that held him.</p>

<p>&quot;Stay back!&quot; another yelled, his voice cracking with desperation.</p>

<p>&quot;Not me! Go to Plum or the boss first!&quot; A third gangster, his hands trembling, looked frantically at Nico, who was sitting, frozen, his eyes wide with fear.</p>

<p>The bravado was gone—replaced with raw, primal fear.</p>

<p>Ross&#x27;s smirk deepened as he surveyed them, his eyes locking onto the one who had spoken last.</p>

<p>&quot;Oh, don&#x27;t worry,&quot; he said with chilling calmness, his voice dripping with mocking amusement. &quot;I&#x27;ve got time for all of you.&quot;</p>

<p>And then, the hammer fell.</p>

<p>The first blow was swift and brutal, and the sharp crack of it echoed in the room, followed by a strangled scream.</p>

<p>&quot;AGGGGHHHHHHHH!&quot;</p>

<p>The sound of it was almost unbearable, but Ross didn&#x27;t flinch. He didn&#x27;t even pause as the man writhed beneath his grasp, his face contorted in agony.</p>

<p>Instead, Ross continued, each swing measured and deliberate, each hit a reminder that mercy was not something he offered to those who crossed him.</p>

<p>Maya stood rooted to the spot, her gaze locked on Ross and the gangsters.</p>

<p>She felt a strange mix of horror and awe—horror for the violence unfolding before her eyes, and awe at the sheer efficiency and coldness with which Ross handled it.</p>

<p>The gangsters, who had once been so confident, now begged for mercy. The ones who had mocked him, who had threatened Maya, now screamed in terror, pleading for their lives.</p>

<p>But Ross paid no heed to their pleas. He continued to swing the hammer, each impact accompanied by another scream, another echo of pain.</p>

<p>The air in the room grew thick with blood, sweat, and fear, the stench of it heavy in the small space.</p>

<p>The gangsters&#x27; cries filled the room, each one more desperate than the last, but Ross&#x27;s expression never wavered.</p>

<p>Maya could barely tear her eyes away, her heart pounding in her chest.</p>

<p>Part of her wanted to turn away, to block out the gruesome scene in front of her, but another part—one she refused to acknowledge—couldn&#x27;t help but be mesmerized.</p>

<p>There was something undeniable about Ross&#x27;s control, about the way he moved with such precision and purpose.</p>

<p>Each swing of the hammer was like an artist&#x27;s brushstroke, each hit a deliberate step in a process only Ross understood.</p>

<p>The gangsters, now bloodied and broken, begged for their lives, but their cries fell on deaf ears.</p>

<p>&quot;No, please! Not me!&quot; one screamed, his face a mask of terror. &quot;I&#x27;ll do anything! Just let me go!&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;Stay back, I said!&quot; another pleaded, his voice frantic. &quot;Go to Plum first, or Nico! Please! They&#x27;re the ones at fault here! We&#x27;re just following orders!&quot;</p>

<p>Maya stood there, unable to look away, as Ross carried out his grim work. He was like a force of nature—unstoppable, ruthless, and unrelenting.</p>

<p>The hours seemed to stretch on, each minute more agonizing than the last.</p>

<p>The sounds of the gangsters&#x27; tortured screams and the sickening thuds of the hammer filled the room, mixing with the low hum of the lights above.</p>

<p>Finally, when the last of the gangsters had been silenced, their bodies lying in a broken heap on the floor, Ross lowered the hammer with a sigh of satisfaction.</p>

<p>He wiped his brow, as if the task had been a mere inconvenience, and looked at the carnage he had left behind.</p>

<p>&quot;All done,&quot; he said softly, his tone casual, almost like he had just finished a routine task.</p>

<p>For eight hours, Ross worked, each strike a deliberate blow meant not just to break their bodies, but to prolong the agony these bastards would endure.</p>

<p>Every scream, every pleading cry, was music to his ears, but he wasn&#x27;t satisfied with just their screams.</p>

<p>He wanted them to feel it—feel every second of their torment, every ounce of suffering. He didn&#x27;t rush.</p>

<p>There was no need to.</p>

<p>The hours stretched on as he carefully broke bones, shattered spirits, and pushed them beyond the point of sanity.</p>

<p>Maya was still frozen, her body trembling as she took in the aftermath.</p>

<p>The room was eerily quiet now, save for the soft drip of blood falling onto the floor, the gangsters motionless in their painful defeat.</p>

<p>She had ever seen anything like it once. Even after everything she had witnessed, the brutality, the coldness, was staggering.</p>

<p>Ross turned to her, his expression unreadable for a moment, before it softened.</p>

<p>He stepped closer, reaching out to gently cup her cheek, his touch tender despite the violence he had just unleashed.</p>

<p>What was truly unbelievable was that, despite the hours of brutal torture, not a single drop of blood touched Ross&#x27;s body or clothes.</p>

<p>&quot;It&#x27;s done,&quot; he whispered, his voice low and soothing.</p>

<p>Maya nodded slowly, her emotions a tangled mess.</p>

<p>She could still hear the echoes of the screams in her mind, but in Ross&#x27;s arms, there was a strange sense of peace, a feeling that nothing could harm her while he was there.</p>

<p>&quot;Let&#x27;s go,&quot; Ross said, his voice steady. He offered her his hand, and without a word, Maya took it, allowing herself to be led away from the bloodied room.</p>

<p>They walked through the door, leaving the remains of the gangsters behind, their bodies silent witnesses to Ross&#x27;s wrath.</p>
