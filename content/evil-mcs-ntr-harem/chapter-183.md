<p>&quot;Althea! I’m coming!&quot; Robin shouted, his voice echoing down the hallway as he sprinted to the elevator. Sweat beaded on his brow, his heart pounding harder with each step.</p>

<p>He jabbed the button for their floor and paced impatiently as the elevator doors slid shut.</p>

<p>This time, he wasn’t alone. He had enlisted the help of three hotel staff members and secured the master key to their royal suite. Panic clawed at his chest.</p>

<p>Ross Oakley wasn’t just any man—he was a notorious womanizer, known for his audacity and charm. Robin’s thoughts spiraled into worst-case scenarios, each more infuriating than the last.</p>

<p>What if Ross was forcing himself on Althea? What if she was trapped, unable to call for help? The mental images made his blood boil.</p>

<p>&quot;Come on, come on! Can’t this thing go faster?&quot; he muttered, hitting the &quot;close door&quot; button repeatedly as though it might shave seconds off his journey.</p>

<p>The hotel staff exchanged glances but remained silent, aware of the tension in the air.</p>

<p>Finally, the elevator chimed, and the doors opened. Robin shot out like a bullet, rushing down the corridor.</p>

<p>His footsteps thundered against the carpeted floor as he reached their suite, fumbling with the key. He threw the door open, braced for a fight—and froze.</p>

<p>There was Althea, sitting on the plush sofa in the living area, her legs crossed gracefully. She held a steaming cup of coffee, the ceramic mug delicately cradled in her hands.</p>

<p>She looked up, her expression calm, her lips curling into a faint smile.</p>

<p>&quot;You’re back, Robin. And you brought the cavalry with you,&quot; she said, a hint of amusement in her voice.</p>

<p>Robin blinked, his adrenaline-fueled rage sputtering out like a candle in the wind.</p>

<p>&quot;The door was locked, and I thought that guy might’ve done something horrible to you. Are you okay, babe? Where’s Ross?&quot; His eyes darted around the suite, searching for any sign of the man who had made him panic so deeply.</p>

<p>The three hotel staff members hesitated at the doorway, shifting awkwardly. They were trained to be discreet, but the tension in the room was palpable.</p>

<p>Being in the presence of two global superstars made it all the more nerve-wracking.</p>

<p>&quot;He left,&quot; Althea replied with a shrug, her tone light as though she hadn’t noticed the whirlwind of panic Robin had endured. She gestured toward the doorway.</p>

<p>&quot;Now, would you kindly show your new friends to the door? We don’t need their services right now. Sorry for the false alarm, gentlemen.&quot;</p>

<p>She turned her radiant smile to the staff, who visibly relaxed at her kind demeanor. It wasn’t every day that someone as famous as Althea Quinn addressed them so graciously.</p>

<p>Each of the men blushed, their professionalism briefly faltering in the face of her charm.</p>

<p>&quot;There’s no problem at all, ma’am,&quot; one of them replied, bowing slightly. &quot;We’re here to serve our guests. Please don’t hesitate to call us if you need anything.&quot;</p>

<p>With that, they made their exit, leaving Robin and Althea alone. The door clicked shut, and silence settled over the room.</p>

<p>Robin ran a hand through his disheveled hair, his shoulders sagging with relief.</p>

<p>&quot;I was so worried, Althea. You don’t know how crazy I felt thinking he might’ve...&quot; He trailed off, unable to finish the sentence, his fists clenching at the memory of his panic.</p>

<p>Althea set her coffee cup down on the table with a quiet clink and stood up. She walked toward him, her expression softening. Reaching out, she touched his arm, her fingers light against his skin.</p>

<p>&quot;Robin,&quot; she said gently, &quot;There’s something we need to talk about.&quot; Her voice, though calm, carried a weight that made Robin’s stomach tighten. He looked down at her, his concern shifting to apprehension.</p>

<p>&quot;I don’t like the sound of this. Why do I feel like you’re breaking up with me, Althea?&quot; Robin frowned, leaning forward on the couch.</p>

<p>The air felt heavier than usual, the weight of unspoken words settling over the room.</p>

<p>Althea hesitated, avoiding his eyes. &quot;It’s… complicated,&quot; she admitted, her voice barely above a whisper. &quot;It’s a long story.&quot;</p>

<p>Robin’s jaw clenched, his patience thinning. &quot;Then start talking. I deserve an explanation.&quot;</p>

<p>She took a deep breath, her hands trembling slightly as she began to recount her tale.</p>

<p>Her words were halting at first, fragmented, but as she spoke, the pieces fell together, painting a picture that sounded too fantastical to be real.</p>

<p>An hour later, Robin sat in stunned silence, his expression a mixture of disbelief, confusion, and anger. He blinked several times, trying to process everything she’d just said.</p>

<p>&quot;So, let me get this straight,&quot; he said, his voice rising with each word. &quot;You’re telling me that you’ve suddenly remembered a past life, that in that life, Ross was your husband, and now you’re leaving me to go back to him? Althea, that’s not just unbelievable—it’s insane! We live in a modern world with science and technology. There’s no place for this outdated, hocus-pocus crap! Magic? Soul arts? They’re not real. They only exist in books and movies!&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;I know how it sounds, Robin,&quot; Althea said, her voice calm despite the storm brewing in his. &quot;But it’s true. I didn’t ask for this, and I didn’t want to hurt you. But I can’t ignore what I’ve remembered. I can’t ignore what I feel.&quot;</p>

<p>Robin shot to his feet, pacing the room as frustration poured out of him. &quot;Do you hear yourself? Do you realize how insane this all sounds? This is the kind of thing people get locked up for saying out loud!&quot;</p>

<p>She didn’t respond immediately, letting his words echo in the room. When she finally spoke, her voice was steady. &quot;I know it’s hard to believe. I wish it wasn’t true, Robin. But it is—for me, for Ross. I didn’t choose this. It’s just… the way things are.&quot;</p>

<p>Robin stopped pacing, turning to face her with a pained expression. &quot;So you’re going back to him,&quot; he said, his voice barely audible.</p>
