<p>The people were enraged, their angry voices echoing throughout the safe zone.</p>

<p>Some cursed Ross openly, while others pointed fingers at him as though he were the source of every misfortune that had befallen them.</p>

<p>The atmosphere was tense, and hatred could be seen in countless pairs of eyes.</p>

<p>Ross simply shook his head.</p>

<p>If their insults had been directed at him alone, he would not have cared in the slightest.</p>

<p>He had endured far worse throughout his life.</p>

<p>However, what bothered him was the fact that many of them were dragging his children into the matter.</p>

<p>Some of his sons and daughters were still very young, especially those who had been born during the zombie apocalypse.</p>

<p>They were innocent and had done nothing wrong, yet they were being judged simply because they were related to him.</p>

<p>Ross glanced at his family for a brief moment.</p>

<p>His expression remained calm, but a faint coldness flashed through his eyes.</p>

<p>He could tolerate attacks against himself, but he had little patience for anyone who targeted the people he cared about.</p>

<p>&quot;If you hate me that much, then we’ll go.&quot;</p>

<p>Ross shrugged casually as though the entire situation meant nothing to him.</p>

<p>His calm reaction only made some of the crowd even angrier.</p>

<p>They had expected him to defend himself or argue back, but Ross did neither.</p>

<p>He stood there with a relaxed posture, completely unaffected by their accusations.</p>

<p>The truth was that he wasn’t angry at all.</p>

<p>In fact, everything was unfolding exactly as he had expected.</p>

<p>This outcome had always been one of the possibilities he had considered.</p>

<p>Human nature was difficult to change. When people were afraid, they searched for someone to blame.</p>

<p>When tragedy struck, they looked for a target to vent their frustrations on.</p>

<p>Ross understood this better than anyone.</p>

<p>To him, there was little value in remaining here any longer.</p>

<p>The zombie apocalypse had once been exciting. It had been filled with danger, challenges, and opportunities.</p>

<p>But after so many years, the thrill had long since disappeared.</p>

<p>There were few enemies left who could threaten him, and there was almost nothing left in this world that could surprise him.</p>

<p>More importantly, he had already achieved everything he wanted here.</p>

<p>He possessed unimaginable wealth and power.</p>

<p>He had built a massive faction and gathered countless followers.</p>

<p>Beautiful women surrounded him wherever he went, and his name was known across the entire world.</p>

<p>Yet despite all of that, Ross found himself feeling bored.</p>

<p>A mortal world could only offer so much.</p>

<p>His gaze drifted toward the distant sky, as though he could see beyond the heavens themselves.</p>

<p>Now that he had entered a xianxia world, a far grander stage awaited him.</p>

<p>This world was filled with immortal cultivators capable of moving mountains and splitting seas.</p>

<p>Ancient sects ruled vast territories. Divine beasts roamed forbidden lands.</p>

<p>Hidden realms contained treasures that could shake the heavens.</p>

<p>Most importantly, there were fairies, immortal maidens, saintesses, empresses, and goddesses whose beauty surpassed anything found in the mortal realm.</p>

<p>A smile slowly appeared on Ross’s face.</p>

<p>The women he had met so far were undoubtedly beautiful, but they were still mortals.</p>

<p>Their lifespans were limited, and their horizons were small compared to the extraordinary beings that existed in the cultivation world.</p>

<p>The thought of meeting those legendary women stirred excitement within him.</p>

<p>He imagined proud saintesses who looked down upon all men, cold immortal fairies who had never known love, and peerless empresses who ruled over countless cultivators.</p>

<p>The challenge alone was enough to ignite his interest.</p>

<p>For the first time in a long while, Ross felt genuinely excited.</p>

<p>His adventure was far from over.</p>

<p>In fact, it felt as though it was only just beginning.</p>

<p>The zombie apocalypse had merely been the first Chapter of his story.</p>

<p>The cultivation world would be the next.</p>

<p>And Ross intended to leave his mark upon it just as he had done everywhere else he went.</p>

<p>Less than an hour later, Ross and his people walked out of the city for good.</p>

<p>Originally, their group had numbered more than three thousand people.</p>

<p>Over the past few days, many survivors had joined them after hearing stories of Ross’s strength and leadership.</p>

<p>However, after the devastating deaths that had occurred earlier, fear spread rapidly through the crowd.</p>

<p>Hundreds chose to stay behind, unwilling to risk their lives any further.</p>

<p>As a result, only around twenty-five hundred people remained.</p>

<p>Ross stood at the front of the group with a calm expression.</p>

<p>He did not try to persuade those who left to stay. Everyone was free to make their own choice.</p>

<p>Those who wished to follow him could come. Those who wished to remain behind could do so as well.</p>

<p>The long line of people stretched across the road as they prepared to leave.</p>

<p>Families carried their belongings while warriors kept watch over the surroundings.</p>

<p>Some looked nervous while others appeared determined.</p>

<p>&quot;Will we be okay, Mom?&quot; a little child asked one of Ross’s women while tightly holding her hand.</p>

<p>The beautiful woman smiled gently and patted the child’s head.</p>

<p>&quot;We will be,&quot; she replied confidently. &quot;Trust in your father. He has never let us down. Not once.&quot;</p>

<p>The child nodded after hearing those words. The fear in his eyes faded slightly.</p>

<p>Many people nearby overheard the conversation and found themselves agreeing with her.</p>

<p>Throughout the years, Ross had overcome countless impossible situations.</p>

<p>Whenever disaster struck, he was always the one standing at the forefront.</p>

<p>Again and again, he had achieved miracles that nobody thought possible.</p>

<p>Their faith in him had become unshakable.</p>

<p>Soon, the massive group began moving forward.</p>

<p>Step by step, they marched away from the city.</p>

<p>The giant walls behind them gradually became smaller and smaller until they were little more than distant silhouettes on the horizon.</p>

<p>Before long, Ross led everyone toward the vast forests that stretched endlessly across the land.</p>

<p>Towering ancient trees blocked out much of the sunlight.</p>

<p>The forest appeared mysterious and dangerous, like a world untouched by civilization.</p>

<p>Without hesitation, Ross walked straight into it.</p>

<p>His followers quickly followed behind.</p>

<p>After a while, the last traces of the group disappeared among the dense trees and thick vegetation.</p>

<p>It was as though they had been swallowed whole by the wilderness.</p>

<p>From atop the city walls, many people watched their departure.</p>

<p>&quot;They’re doomed.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;They won’t survive out there.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;The forest is filled with monsters and unknown dangers.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;Leaving the city is suicide.&quot;</p>

<p>The earth’s survivors shook their heads and whispered among themselves.</p>

<p>In their eyes, Ross and his followers were marching toward their deaths.</p>

<p>They believed they would never see them again.</p>

<p>What they did not know was that they were witnessing the beginning of something far greater.</p>

<p>This was not the end of Ross Oakley’s story.</p>

<p>It was merely the start of a new Chapter.</p>

<p>Beyond those forests lay a vast cultivation world filled with powerful sects, ancient clans, hidden realms, and countless opportunities.</p>

<p>Adventures beyond imagination awaited him.</p>

<p>The people in the city thought Ross was walking toward destruction.</p>

<p>In reality, he was walking toward destiny.</p>

<p>And before long, the entire xianxia world would come to know his name.</p>
