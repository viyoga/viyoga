<p>Capítulo 1114: Chapter 1114 Agility</p>

<p>A ripple passed through the group—fear, relief, uncertainty all mixed together.</p>

<p>The five women leaned in closer to Giana, their faces pale from exhaustion and the ever-present sense of danger that clung to them like a second skin.</p>

<p>Giana’s jaw tightened.</p>

<p>“Three people… inside a house this deep in the dead zone? That’s not normal.”</p>

<p>“Maybe they’re like us,” whispered Delia, the youngest of the five.</p>

<p>Her hands trembled slightly as she clutched her pistol, knuckles white.</p>

<p>“Or maybe they’re worse,” muttered Suri darkly. “Humans are more dangerous than zombies nowadays.”</p>

<p>No one disagreed.</p>

<p>Giana surveyed their surroundings with cautious eyes. The house looked sturdy—too sturdy.</p>

<p>The windows weren’t boarded with scrap wood but reinforced with metal panels.</p>

<p>The front yard, though dark, had a strange orderliness to it.</p>

<p>Even the cracked stone path leading to the doorway felt… maintained.</p>

<p>People lived here. Thrived, even.</p>

<p>And that was terrifying in its own way.</p>

<p>“At this point,” Giana said softly, “we either take a chance… or die out there tonight.”</p>

<p>A cold gust swept through them, carrying the faint groans of distant walkers.</p>

<p>That alone made the choice.</p>

<p>She lifted her hand and knocked gently on the door—two soft taps, barely audible.</p>

<p>The others held their breath, every muscle tensed as they waited. They expected silence.</p>

<p>They expected to stand there in the dark for long, agonizing minutes.</p>

<p>Maybe knock again.</p>

<p>Maybe risk being heard.</p>

<p>Maybe give up.</p>

<p>But none of them expected the door to open almost immediately—three breaths, no more, no less.</p>

<p>The women froze.</p>

<p>Their instincts exploded into motion.</p>

<p>Guns snapped up.</p>

<p>Fingers curled around triggers.</p>

<p>Feet shifted into defensive stances.</p>

<p>Hearts pounded so hard each woman wondered if someone else could hear it.</p>

<p>But none of them were prepared for the figure standing in the doorway.</p>

<p>The lantern light from inside washed over him, outlining the shape of a man who looked nothing like a starving survivor or a desperate wanderer.</p>

<p>He stood tall, relaxed—too relaxed.</p>

<p>As though he had known they were coming long before they ever decided to knock.</p>

<p>His eyes were calm.</p>

<p>His posture unthreatened.</p>

<p>His presence overwhelming.</p>

<p>Every woman felt their breath hitch for a different reason—shock, fear, awe, something primal they didn’t want to acknowledge.</p>

<p>He didn’t seem afraid of their guns. He didn’t seem bothered by their trembling stances.</p>

<p>He simply stood there.</p>

<p>Watching them with unreadable eyes.</p>

<p>For a moment that stretched long and thin, no one dared speak.</p>

<p>The night around them went silent, even the distant groaning of zombies fading into a muted hum.</p>

<p>Giana swallowed hard.</p>

<p>She had seen monsters. She had seen men who tried to be monsters.</p>

<p>But this man…</p>

<p>This man felt like something entirely different.</p>

<p>And none of them knew if that was salvation—</p>

<p>—or the beginning of a new nightmare.</p>

<p>“You all seem stressed out. Please, come in.” Ross said with a gentle smile—the kind that somehow cut through the tension in the air yet made every woman’s pulse spike for an entirely different reason.</p>

<p>He stepped aside, hand extended in a polite, almost regal gesture.</p>

<p>The five women hesitated at the threshold, clutching their guns, unsure if stepping inside was salvation or a trap.</p>

<p>But then…</p>

<p>They truly saw Ross.</p>

<p>Under the warm interior lights, his face was revealed in full—sharp, perfectly sculpted features, eyes calm and steady, a presence that radiated unshakeable confidence.</p>

<p>The kind of beauty that didn’t belong in a world like this.</p>

<p>He’s so handsome…</p>

<p>The identical thought struck all five of them like a spell.</p>

<p>Their breath caught. Their stances faltered. It wasn’t lust, not yet—it was sheer disbelief, awe, shock.</p>

<p>The kind of face that made someone forget every danger outside.</p>

<p>Against their own judgment, their feet began moving forward on their own.</p>

<p>They entered the house silently, one after another, drawn in like moths to a steady, impossibly warm flame.</p>

<p>Ross led them deeper, down reinforced steps into the lower levels of Aegis.</p>

<p>The basement opened not into a cramped shelter, but into a wide, well-lit living space with polished floors, comfortable furniture, and a smell none of them had experienced in months—fresh food.</p>

<p>Two people sat waiting: Miku, sharp-eyed and composed, and Ace, protective and wary.</p>

<p>“There’s food on the table. Help yourselves,” Ross gestured, voice calm and welcoming.</p>

<p>The five women froze again—not from fear, but from the shock of seeing something unthinkable.</p>

<p>Actual plates. Actual dishes. Steam rising from warm meals. Clean water. Fruit. Even soup.</p>

<p>Miku’s eyes darted between Ross and the group, a quiet disbelief tightening her expression.</p>

<p>He really told me five… and five arrived.</p>

<p>Earlier that day, Ross had casually said, “Prepare food for five visitors.” She hadn’t questioned it.</p>

<p>She had learned better than to doubt Ross’s instincts.</p>

<p>But even then… seeing it play out so precisely still made her heart skip.</p>

<p>How does he do it every time…?</p>

<p>But then again, this was Ross. The impossible was normal when he was involved.</p>

<p>The women didn’t approach the table.</p>

<p>Hunger gnawed at them, but instinct told them to wait—to show respect, to follow whatever custom this sanctuary followed.</p>

<p>Giana stepped forward first, posture straightening despite her exhaustion, her tone formal.</p>

<p>“Thank you for taking us in. My name is Giana.” She motioned to her companions. “This is Libby, Valery, Joy, and Avery.”</p>

<p>The others gave tired nods, still keeping one eye on Ross—unable to help it, really.</p>

<p>His presence naturally drew their attention like gravity.</p>

<p>Ross introduced Miku and Ace with casual ease.</p>

<p>Ace gave them a polite nod, though he kept glancing at Libby’s wound like a man prepared to act the moment she collapsed.</p>

<p>Only after the names were exchanged did Giana’s controlled composure crack. Her expression darkened with guilt and fear.</p>

<p>“There’s something you all need to know,” she began, swallowing hard. “And before I say it, I want to apologize. Truly.”</p>

<p>Ross raised an eyebrow, listening without interrupting.</p>

<p>Giana continued, voice low.</p>

<p>“I’m afraid we may have led trouble straight to your door.”</p>

<p>A cold heaviness settled over the room. Valery’s fingers twitched on her gun.</p>

<p>Avery stared down at the floor. Joy closed her eyes, perhaps sensing the shifting tension in the air.</p>

<p>Giana took a deep breath. “There are people after us. Very dangerous people. The type who take what they want and kill whoever gets in their way.”</p>

<p>Her gaze shifted to the blonde woman leaning heavily against the wall—Libby.</p>

<p>Libby’s face was pale, sweat-dampened.</p>

<p>A thick wrap of cloth covered her abdomen.</p>

<p>Blood, dark and dried but still fresh in places, seeped through the fabric.</p>

<p>“She was shot,” Giana said softly.</p>
