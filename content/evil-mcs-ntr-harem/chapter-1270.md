<p>Servant Lin was confused, but no matter how puzzled he felt, he would never do something as foolish as questioning his master, Ross Oakley.</p>

<p>The more he learned about the young man, the more terrified he became.</p>

<p>Every day seemed to reveal another shocking secret, another impossible feat that shattered his understanding of the world.</p>

<p>At first, he had thought Ross was merely an exceptionally talented cultivator.</p>

<p>Later, he realized that description was laughably inadequate.</p>

<p>Ross was a monster that defied all common sense.</p>

<p>As those thoughts flashed through his mind, Servant Lin looked around at the crowd gathered in Falling Leaf Town.</p>

<p>What he saw surprised him.</p>

<p>The soldiers were dead. The tyrannical king’s forces had been completely wiped out.</p>

<p>Logically, the townspeople should have been celebrating.</p>

<p>They should have been cheering, crying tears of joy, and thanking the heavens for their newfound freedom.</p>

<p>Yet not a single smile could be seen.</p>

<p>Every face was pale.</p>

<p>Every pair of eyes was filled with fear.</p>

<p>No one dared to speak.</p>

<p>&quot;...&quot;</p>

<p>The silence was deafening.</p>

<p>It hung heavily over the entire town like a dark cloud.</p>

<p>Suddenly, a middle-aged man stepped forward. His face was twisted with panic and despair.</p>

<p>&quot;What did you do?!&quot; he shouted.</p>

<p>His voice broke the silence.</p>

<p>&quot;You’ve sent us to our graves!&quot;</p>

<p>The crowd immediately erupted into chaos.</p>

<p>&quot;It’s over!&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;The king will never forgive this!&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;We’re all dead!&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;He’ll slaughter every man, woman, and child in this town!&quot;</p>

<p>Fear spread faster than wildfire.</p>

<p>Many people did not even wait to hear anything else.</p>

<p>They turned and sprinted toward their homes as fast as they could.</p>

<p>Some tripped over themselves in their panic.</p>

<p>Others dragged their family members along while screaming for them to hurry.</p>

<p>Within minutes, the peaceful streets of Falling Leaf Town became a scene of complete disorder.</p>

<p>Doors slammed open.</p>

<p>People rushed inside their homes and began stuffing valuables into bags.</p>

<p>Gold, silver, food, clothing, family heirlooms... anything they could carry was taken.</p>

<p>Mothers grabbed their children.</p>

<p>Husbands pulled their wives along.</p>

<p>The elderly were supported by younger relatives as entire families fled toward the town gates.</p>

<p>To an outsider, it would look as though a natural disaster was about to strike.</p>

<p>The reason was simple.</p>

<p>Everyone knew the king’s temperament.</p>

<p>The soldiers Brandon had killed were not ordinary men. They were the king’s personal forces.</p>

<p>If the king discovered what had happened, he would never let the matter rest.</p>

<p>In their minds, the outcome was already decided.</p>

<p>The king would return with a massive army.</p>

<p>He would burn Falling Leaf Town to the ground.</p>

<p>He would execute everyone connected to the incident.</p>

<p>Whether they were innocent or guilty would not matter.</p>

<p>By sunset, nearly every resident was preparing to abandon the town they had lived in for their entire lives.</p>

<p>Meanwhile, someone merely scratched his head in confusion.</p>

<p>&quot;Why are they running away?&quot; he asked.</p>

<p>Servant Lin could only smile bitterly.</p>

<p>To Brandon and Ross Oakley, the king was nothing more than an insignificant ant.</p>

<p>To the people of Falling Leaf Town, however, he was a terrifying ruler whose wrath could destroy their lives with a single command.</p>

<p>An hour later, Falling Leaf Town felt like a ghost town.</p>

<p>A massive exodus had taken place.</p>

<p>The streets that were once filled with merchants, travelers, and townsfolk were now eerily quiet. Doors and windows were shut tight, and countless homes had been abandoned in haste.</p>

<p>Of the several thousand residents who had once lived there, only a few hundred remained.</p>

<p>Most of those who stayed behind were elderly men and women who were simply too old and frail to travel.</p>

<p>Others saw no point in fleeing.</p>

<p>They knew King Ji Canghai’s ruthless reputation all too well and fully believed that he would hunt down every last resident of Falling Leaf Town regardless of where they ran.</p>

<p>To them, escape was merely delaying the inevitable.</p>

<p>Including Ross Oakley’s group of over two thousand five hundred people, along with the remaining townsfolk, there were only around twenty-seven hundred people left in the entire town.</p>

<p>The atmosphere was heavy.</p>

<p>Everywhere Ross and his companions went, they could feel gazes following them.</p>

<p>Those gazes were filled with fear.</p>

<p>They were also filled with resentment.</p>

<p>In the eyes of the remaining townsfolk, Ross Oakley and his people were the reason disaster had befallen Falling Leaf Town.</p>

<p>Had they never arrived, the king’s soldiers would still be alive, and the town would not be facing extinction.</p>

<p>Yet despite their anger, no one dared voice their complaints openly.</p>

<p>The memory of Brandon effortlessly slaughtering the king’s men was still fresh in their minds.</p>

<p>***</p>

<p>Several days passed.</p>

<p>Then the people everyone feared finally arrived.</p>

<p>Three figures appeared outside Falling Leaf Town.</p>

<p>They wore dark robes embroidered with the royal insignia.</p>

<p>Their expressions were stern, and powerful auras radiated from their bodies.</p>

<p>It was immediately obvious that they were not ordinary cultivators.</p>

<p>The town stood wide open, allowing them to enter without obstruction.</p>

<p>As soon as the elderly residents saw the newcomers, their faces turned pale.</p>

<p>Some trembled uncontrollably.</p>

<p>Others lowered their heads, not daring to meet their eyes.</p>

<p>The three men calmly walked through the nearly deserted streets.</p>

<p>The empty city around them only deepened their frowns.</p>

<p>Eventually, one of them stopped before an old man sitting beside a rundown house.</p>

<p>The old man’s body visibly shook.</p>

<p>The leader stared at him coldly.</p>

<p>&quot;Where are the king’s men?&quot; he asked.</p>

<p>The old man’s lips trembled.</p>

<p>For a moment, he seemed unable to speak.</p>

<p>&quot;They’re... they’re dead...&quot; he finally stammered.</p>

<p>The three investigators narrowed their eyes.</p>

<p>&quot;Dead?&quot; the leader repeated.</p>

<p>&quot;Y-yes.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;Who killed them?&quot;</p>

<p>The old man swallowed hard.</p>

<p>&quot;It was a newcomer named Ross Oakley. Well... not exactly him. One of his followers did it. I don’t know the man’s name.&quot;</p>

<p>The moment those words left his mouth, a chilling silence descended upon the street.</p>

<p>The expressions of the three investigators immediately darkened.</p>

<p>A terrifying pressure slowly began to spread from their bodies.</p>

<p>&quot;Someone dared to kill the king’s soldiers?&quot; one of them muttered.</p>

<p>The old man nearly collapsed from fear.</p>

<p>Meanwhile, far away in a courtyard, Ross Oakley slowly opened his eyes.</p>

<p>A faint smile appeared on his face.</p>

<p>&quot;It seems the king has finally sent someone to investigate.&quot;</p>
