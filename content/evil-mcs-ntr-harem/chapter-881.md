<p>Laura remembered every moment Ross had treated her like a queen, every instance of devotion and care.</p>

<p>She knew he hadn’t changed, and she realized with a shiver that she could never love anyone else in his place.</p>

<p>Without a word, Laura leapt forward, pressing herself against Ross and claiming his lips in a hungry, demanding kiss.</p>

<p>The world around them seemed to dissolve—the past, the future, all outside concerns vanished.</p>

<p>Passion ignited, and soon the room was alive with the intoxicating symphony of desire.</p>

<p>The soft sounds of skin against skin mingled with gasps, moans, and whispered names, each breath and touch driving them further into ecstasy.</p>

<p>Lois leaned into him as well, their movements intertwining, creating a rhythm that balanced the three of them.</p>

<p>Laura’s hands roamed over Ross’s body, exploring, claiming, asserting her own hunger, while Ross guided them both, his control absolute yet gentle, reading their every reaction, every moan, every shiver of pleasure.</p>

<p>Time lost all meaning.</p>

<p>Every movement, every kiss, every gasp pulled them deeper into a state of mutual surrender.</p>

<p>The boundaries between them blurred, replaced by the raw, unrelenting intensity of lust and desire.</p>

<p>The hotel room became their private world, a place where emotions and physical need intertwined, leaving no room for doubt or hesitation.</p>

<p>Hours passed, the night stretching on, filled with waves of ecstasy that neither woman had expected.</p>

<p>Ross continued to move between them, his hands and lips ensuring both Lois and Laura were completely consumed by pleasure.</p>

<p>Their cries, their writhing, their shared need echoed through the room, a testament to the power he wielded—not just over their bodies, but over their hearts.</p>

<p>When at last the fervor subsided, all three of them lay entwined, drenched in sweat, hearts pounding in unison.</p>

<p>The room was quiet except for their ragged breaths and the occasional soft moan escaping their lips.</p>

<p>Ross pulled them close, one on either side, his arms holding them securely, a gentle reminder that no matter how wild the night had been, he remained their anchor.</p>

<p>Both women looked up at him, eyes heavy with exhaustion, lust, and love.</p>

<p>There was no jealousy now, only trust—absolute and unshakable.</p>

<p>They had given themselves to him completely, and he had given them the same.</p>

<p>In that moment, the three of them understood something unspoken: they were bound together, not just by desire, but by the trust, love, and devotion Ross demanded—and that they willingly offered.</p>

<p>***</p>

<p>After a night of unrestrained passion, the three finally surrendered to sleep, tangled together in a warmth that carried into the dawn.</p>

<p>When morning came, the scent of frying eggs and sizzling bacon drifted through the room.</p>

<p>Ross had risen early, humming softly to himself as he cooked, leaving the twins to wake in each other’s company.</p>

<p>Laura stretched lazily under the sheets before rolling over to glance at her sister.</p>

<p>Lois was already awake, staring quietly at the ceiling, as though lost in thought.</p>

<p>For a long moment, neither spoke.</p>

<p>The silence between them felt heavier than it should have, weighed down by the reality of what they had stepped into.</p>

<p>Finally, Laura broke it. Her voice was tentative, almost fragile. &quot;What about Billy?&quot;</p>

<p>Lois winced slightly, as if she had been waiting for that question all along.</p>

<p>She turned her head to meet her sister’s gaze, her expression conflicted.</p>

<p>&quot;I don’t know,&quot; she admitted after a pause. &quot;I broke up with him... and now I’m with Ross. Just like you.&quot;</p>

<p>The words hung in the air, blunt yet honest.</p>

<p>Laura studied her sister, seeing the blush creeping across her cheeks, the way her lips trembled as if afraid to say more.</p>

<p>Lois let out a small laugh—soft, embarrassed.</p>

<p>&quot;It’s strange. I keep telling myself I shouldn’t feel okay with this, but... I am. As long as he doesn’t hurt me—and doesn’t hurt you—I think that we found the man for us. And...&quot; She trailed off, cheeks burning brighter.</p>

<p>&quot;It’s hard to not love someone who’s made me feel things I didn’t even know were possible.&quot;</p>

<p>Laura swallowed, her throat dry. She understood exactly what Lois meant; the memory of last night alone was enough to make her heart race.</p>

<p>But guilt pressed against that warmth, tugging at her like an anchor.</p>

<p>&quot;How are we going to tell Mom about this?&quot; she asked at last, her voice tight with unease.</p>

<p>The question lingered in the quiet between them, heavier than either of them wanted to face.</p>

<p>Lois shifted uncomfortably, pulling the blanket closer as if it could shield her from the reality of it.</p>

<p>She gave no immediate answer—because she didn’t have one.</p>

<p>What could they possibly say?</p>

<p>That they had both fallen for the same man, that they shared him, that they were... happy?</p>

<p>Laura let out a long sigh, staring toward the kitchen where Ross’s voice carried faintly as he hummed, oblivious to their turmoil.</p>

<p>&quot;She’ll never understand,&quot; Laura whispered, more to herself than to Lois. &quot;She’ll think we’ve lost our minds.&quot;</p>

<p>Lois reached out then, her fingers brushing against her sister’s hand, a small gesture of reassurance.</p>

<p>&quot;Maybe,&quot; she said softly. &quot;But whatever happens, at least we’ll face it together.&quot;</p>

<p>Laura squeezed her hand, her lips pressing into a thin, uncertain smile.</p>

<p>She wanted to believe that. She needed to believe it.</p>

<p>Because the truth was, their lives had already changed—and there was only more fun to be expected from here on out.</p>

<p>***</p>

<p>A week later, after days of nervous glances and hushed conversations, Laura and Lois finally decided they could not put it off any longer.</p>

<p>Their mother deserved to know the truth.</p>

<p>The three of them sat together in the living room.</p>

<p>Kate poured tea for her daughters, her movements graceful as always, unaware of the weight pressing down on them.</p>

<p>She noticed how the twins kept exchanging looks, as if silently urging the other to begin.</p>

<p>Finally, Laura set her cup down, her hands trembling slightly. &quot;Mom... there’s something we need to tell you.&quot;</p>

<p>Kate raised an eyebrow, curious. &quot;Oh? You both look so serious.&quot;</p>

<p>Laura took a deep breath, her heart pounding. &quot;It’s about Ross.&quot;</p>

<p>At that name, Kate’s expression softened with familiarity. &quot;Ross? Your fiancé?&quot; She looked at Laura and smiled faintly. &quot;What about him?&quot;</p>

<p>Laura hesitated, then forced herself to speak the truth. &quot;He’s... not just mine anymore. Lois and I... we’re both engaged to him.&quot;</p>

<p>The words struck like a thunderclap in the quiet room.</p>

<p>Kate froze, the teacup in her hand trembling just slightly before she set it down on the table.</p>

<p>&quot;Oh?&quot; Her voice was calm, but her eyes widened with disbelief.</p>

<p>She looked from one daughter to the other, as if trying to make sense of what she had just heard.</p>

<p>At first, her instincts screamed at her to object, to ask what madness this was.</p>

<p>Laura had been engaged to Ross first—so how could Lois step into something like this?</p>

<p>But as the initial shock began to settle, Kate’s thoughts drifted to Ross himself.</p>

<p>She remembered his calm, steady nature, the way he spoke with respect, the way he always seemed to think of others before himself.</p>

<p>He was strong yet gentle, a man who carried responsibility on his shoulders without complaint.</p>

<p>A man who knew how to care for a woman—perhaps even two more.</p>

<p>Her heart warred with her mind. On the surface, it was unthinkable, scandalous even.</p>

<p>Yet deep inside, she could not deny that she understood how her daughters had fallen for him.</p>

<p>Wasn’t it natural for women to be drawn to such a man?</p>

<p>Kate let out a slow breath, pressing her lips together.</p>

<p>She wanted to scold them, to demand explanations, but instead she found herself smiling faintly.</p>

<p>&quot;I see... So that’s the truth.&quot;</p>

<p>Laura and Lois both tensed, bracing themselves for her anger.</p>

<p>But instead, Kate reached out and clasped their hands.</p>

<p>Her touch was warm, steady, reassuring.</p>

<p>&quot;Listen to me, both of you,&quot; she said softly. &quot;I have lived long enough to know that happiness is rare, and when you find it, you must hold onto it. If Ross is the one who makes you happy—both of you—then who am I to stand in your way? I believe he is a good man. As long as he treats you with love and respect, then I will accept this.&quot;</p>

<p>Tears welled in Lois’s eyes. Laura’s lips trembled as relief flooded through her chest.</p>

<p>Without another word, they leaned into their mother’s embrace.</p>

<p>Kate wrapped her arms around them both, pulling them close, kissing their foreheads tenderly as if they were little girls again.</p>

<p>&quot;My sweet daughters,&quot; Kate whispered. &quot;No matter what paths you take, you will always be mine.&quot;</p>

<p>For a long while, they stayed like that, clinging to each other.</p>

<p>To anyone who might have seen them then, the three of them could have easily been mistaken for sisters.</p>

<p>Kate’s youthful beauty, her radiant skin and graceful figure, made her seem closer to their age than their mother.</p>

<p>In that moment, it was as though three sisters sat bound together by love, facing the world as one.</p>
