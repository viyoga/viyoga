<p>The moment that name left his mouth, complete silence filled the room.</p>

<p>Several women stared at him with wide eyes.</p>

<p>Others looked completely stunned.</p>

<p>A few even wondered whether they had heard him correctly.</p>

<p>Ross Oakley?</p>

<p>That Ross Oakley?</p>

<p>The man whose name had once dominated news headlines across the world?</p>

<p>The lottery winner.</p>

<p>The basketball phenomenon.</p>

<p>The internet celebrity.</p>

<p>The famous actor and director.</p>

<p>The man whose life story had become known to hundreds of millions of people.</p>

<p>Everyone had heard of Ross Oakley at some point.</p>

<p>Even if they had never followed his story closely, they still recognized the name.</p>

<p>The problem was that the man sitting before them looked nothing like the Ross Oakley they remembered.</p>

<p>In the old videos and photographs, Ross had possessed an ordinary appearance.</p>

<p>He wasn’t ugly.</p>

<p>He wasn’t exceptionally handsome either.</p>

<p>He looked like a normal young man.</p>

<p>The current Ross, however, was something else entirely.</p>

<p>The difference was so great that nobody would have connected the two without being told.</p>

<p>His appearance seemed almost unreal.</p>

<p>The women found themselves staring at him repeatedly during the conversation.</p>

<p>Every angle of his face appeared flawless.</p>

<p>His skin seemed perfect.</p>

<p>His eyes possessed a mysterious charm that drew attention effortlessly.</p>

<p>His jawline looked as though it had been sculpted by a master artist.</p>

<p>Even simple actions like smiling or speaking carried a natural elegance that made him appear extraordinary.</p>

<p>Several women secretly wondered whether such beauty should even be possible.</p>

<p>The longer they looked at him, the more unbelievable it became.</p>

<p>One woman eventually laughed helplessly.</p>

<p>&quot;If you hadn’t told me yourself, I would never have believed it.&quot;</p>

<p>The others quickly nodded.</p>

<p>They shared the same thoughts.</p>

<p>The Ross Oakley from before and the Ross Oakley sitting before them now seemed like two completely different people.</p>

<p>If Ross were to walk through a crowd, thousands of people would notice him instantly.</p>

<p>Not because of his fame.</p>

<p>Not because of his power.</p>

<p>Simply because his appearance demanded attention.</p>

<p>In fact, calling him handsome almost felt insufficient.</p>

<p>The word itself seemed too weak.</p>

<p>Handsome described movie stars.</p>

<p>Handsome described celebrities.</p>

<p>Handsome described models.</p>

<p>Ross had already surpassed those categories entirely.</p>

<p>He looked less like an ordinary human being and more like a masterpiece that should only exist in myths and legends.</p>

<p>If Ross claimed to be the second most handsome man in existence, none of the women would dare argue.</p>

<p>The reason was simple.</p>

<p>They genuinely could not imagine anyone more attractive than him.</p>

<p>Such a person would have to surpass perfection itself.</p>

<p>And perfection, by definition, could not be improved.</p>

<p>Even among a million survivors gathered in one place, Ross stood out like a blazing sun among countless stars.</p>

<p>No matter where he went, attention naturally followed him.</p>

<p>Whether people loved him, feared him, envied him, or admired him, they could not ignore him.</p>

<p>Ross Oakley had always been extraordinary.</p>

<p>Now, after everything that had happened, he had become something even greater.</p>

<p>A man who seemed destined to stand above the rest of humanity.</p>

<p>It did not take long for the crowd to notice Ross Oakley’s arrival.</p>

<p>The moment he stepped into view, heads began turning one after another. Conversations died down as people stared in disbelief.</p>

<p>&quot;Wait... is that really Ross Oakley?!&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;No way! It can’t be!&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;It is him! I’d recognize that face anywhere!&quot;</p>

<p>The crowd quickly became excited.</p>

<p>&quot;I can’t believe it. He looks like a superstar!&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;A superstar? Are you blind? He was already a superstar before the apocalypse. Now he looks like some kind of heavenly prince descended to the mortal world!&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;Seriously, there isn’t anyone who can compare to him anymore.&quot;</p>

<p>People whispered among themselves while stealing glances at Ross.</p>

<p>His appearance had always been exceptional, but after gaining power and evolving multiple times, his presence had become overwhelming.</p>

<p>Every movement seemed effortless and confident. Simply standing there made him the center of attention.</p>

<p>However, not everyone was focused on Ross alone.</p>

<p>Many eyes soon shifted toward the women accompanying him.</p>

<p>&quot;Heh, who cares about superstars anymore?&quot; one man scoffed. &quot;This isn’t the old world. Fame doesn’t matter now. Power is what matters.&quot;</p>

<p>His gaze wandered toward Ross’s wives.</p>

<p>The women walking beside him were stunning.</p>

<p>Each possessed beauty capable of turning heads wherever they went.</p>

<p>Their confidence, elegance, and powerful auras only made them even more captivating.</p>

<p>Many men in the crowd found themselves unable to look away.</p>

<p>&quot;You talk big,&quot; another man replied. &quot;But do you really think that’s something you should be staring at?&quot;</p>

<p>The first man frowned.</p>

<p>&quot;What do you mean?&quot;</p>

<p>The second man pointed toward the soldiers surrounding Ross.</p>

<p>&quot;Look around. Every one of those men is armed to the teeth.&quot;</p>

<p>Several people immediately looked.</p>

<p>Ross’s followers stood in disciplined formations around him.</p>

<p>They carried advanced firearms and wore high-quality armor that radiated strength and authority.</p>

<p>Their expressions remained cold and alert as they scanned the surroundings.</p>

<p>One glance was enough to make many people nervous.</p>

<p>&quot;And those are only the guards you can see,&quot; the second man continued. &quot;Who knows how many powerful ability users are hidden among them? If someone causes trouble, they’ll probably be dead before they can even react.&quot;</p>

<p>The first man swallowed.</p>

<p>&quot;Good point.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;Exactly. Ross Oakley isn’t some celebrity protected by bodyguards anymore. He’s one of the most powerful people on the planet.&quot;</p>

<p>The surrounding crowd nodded in agreement.</p>

<p>Many of them had heard the stories.</p>

<p>Ross defeating powerful enemies.</p>

<p>Ross establishing his own faction.</p>

<p>Ross leading survivors through impossible situations.</p>

<p>Ross obtaining treasures and abilities that ordinary people could only dream about.</p>

<p>His reputation had spread across countless settlements and survivor communities.</p>

<p>To many people, he had already become a living legend.</p>

<p>&quot;Look at the people following him,&quot; someone else remarked.</p>

<p>Thousands of survivors accompanied Ross’s group.</p>

<p>Their equipment was superior.</p>

<p>Their morale was high.</p>

<p>Most importantly, they looked safe.</p>

<p>In a world filled with monsters, zombies, and danger, that alone inspired envy.</p>

<p>&quot;I wish I could join his faction.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;You’re not the only one.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;I heard his territory back on Earth was one of the safest places left.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;It probably is. Just look at them.&quot;</p>

<p>The discussions continued endlessly as Ross walked through the area.</p>

<p>Meanwhile, Ross himself paid little attention to the stares and whispers.</p>

<p>He had long since grown accustomed to being the center of attention.</p>

<p>The people around him could only watch in awe as he continued forward, surrounded by his wives, children, trusted followers, and loyal soldiers.</p>

<p>The scene looked less like a group of survivors and more like the procession of a king returning from conquest.</p>
