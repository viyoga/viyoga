<p>Eventually, the news reached Kristine.</p>

<p>She hadn&#x27;t been actively looking for it—just scrolling casually through her feed when a familiar face caught her attention.</p>

<p>At first, she thought it was some kind of mistake, a lookalike. But the longer she stared, the colder the pit in her stomach grew.</p>

<p>There, in a dimly lit photo with tousled sheets and bare skin, was Sasha. Her best friend.</p>

<p>On top of Ross—the great Ross Oakley—the same man who was infamous for being untouchable, always in the center of some scandal or another.</p>

<p>He lay there smirking, hand resting on Sasha&#x27;s thigh as she kissed his chest like it was the most natural thing in the world.</p>

<p>And it didn&#x27;t stop there.</p>

<p>Another photo. Then another. Then an entire thread, each more provocative than the last.</p>

<p>Sasha wearing nothing but Ross&#x27;s shirt. Sasha with her leg draped over him.</p>

<p>Sasha&#x27;s flushed face, lips parted, her body pressed against his.</p>

<p>Kristine&#x27;s jaw dropped. Her heart pounded in her chest as disbelief flooded through her.</p>

<p>&quot;This… this can&#x27;t be true,&quot; she whispered to herself.</p>

<p>She scrolled down to the comments.</p>

<p>&quot;Is that the new girl?&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;Sasha&#x27;s got guts, I&#x27;ll give her that.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;Ross is definitely not sleeping alone tonight.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;She looks like she&#x27;s been ruined in the best way.&quot;</p>

<p>Kristine winced. Her fingers trembled as she tapped on Sasha&#x27;s profile—filled now with sultry captions and bold selfies. It was like looking at a stranger.</p>

<p>This wasn&#x27;t the Sasha she knew. The sweet, soft-spoken girl who used to panic over double texting a crush.</p>

<p>The one who blushed when someone mentioned thongs or dared her to wear a low-cut top.</p>

<p>What the hell happened?</p>

<p>Her thumb hesitated for a second before she hit Call.</p>

<p>She needed to hear Sasha&#x27;s voice. She needed to believe there was a reasonable explanation, that this was just a misunderstanding—or a stunt, or a joke.</p>

<p>Something. Anything.</p>

<p>The phone rang.</p>

<p>Once.</p>

<p>Twice.</p>

<p>Then Sasha picked up.</p>

<p>But the moment Kristine heard the background, her breath caught in her throat.</p>

<p>&quot;Ahhhhh…&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;Ohhhhh…&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;Ughhhhh…&quot;</p>

<p>Pak. Pak. Pak.</p>

<p>The unmistakable rhythm of sex—the sounds of bodies colliding, of someone being thoroughly and unapologetically ravished.</p>

<p>Sasha&#x27;s voice echoed through the receiver, a mix of gasps and laughter, totally unbothered.</p>

<p>&quot;What&#x27;s—ahhhh—up, girlfriend?&quot; Sasha finally said, her voice breathless but chipper.</p>

<p>She followed it with a sharp moan, and then a giggle, like this was the most casual call in the world.</p>

<p>Kristine&#x27;s face went scarlet. &quot;Are you… are you doing what I think you&#x27;re doing, Sasha?!&quot;</p>

<p>Another moan.</p>

<p>Then Sasha&#x27;s voice, smug and teasing: &quot;Mmmh… and what&#x27;s that?&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;I—just—call me back when you&#x27;re done!&quot; Kristine blurted, scandalized. &quot;I can&#x27;t talk to you like this!&quot;</p>

<p>She hung up, nearly throwing her phone across the bed as she sat there in complete shock.</p>

<p>She didn&#x27;t move for a full minute.</p>

<p>Her heart was still racing, her mind spinning with disbelief.</p>

<p>The Sasha she knew would never answer a call in the middle of sex. Sasha was a virgin!</p>

<p>The Sasha she knew would never post half-nude pictures with a man known around the world for his hedonism and power.</p>

<p>And yet… she had. And clearly, she didn&#x27;t care what anyone thought.</p>

<p>Kristine ran a hand through her hair, trying to make sense of it all.</p>

<p>It wasn&#x27;t just that Sasha was with someone. It was who she was with. Ross.</p>

<p>A man with a reputation sharp enough to cut. A billionaire playboy, a ruthless businessman, and now apparently, Sasha&#x27;s lover.</p>

<p>She remembered all the times they used to stay up late talking about love and anything else under the sun.</p>

<p>Never did Kristine imagine her bestfriend for Ross of all people.</p>

<p>As far as she knew, they had only met once and that was when Kristine was there also.</p>

<p>And now here she was—moaning into the phone while being wrecked by the most infamous man on the internet.</p>

<p>Kristine didn&#x27;t know whether to be horrified… or be shocked.</p>

<p>A strange mix of emotions bubbled inside her—disbelief, confusion, maybe even utter surprise.</p>

<p>What had changed? What had Ross done to her? Or was this always inside Sasha, waiting to be unleashed?</p>

<p>Kristine glanced at her phone again, still buzzing with notifications about Sasha&#x27;s latest posts.</p>

<p>She clicked on one of the photos. It was Sasha lying across Ross&#x27;s lap, wearing nothing but a sheet and a lazy smile.</p>

<p>Ross&#x27;s hand was between her thighs, and his smirk looked like a promise of sin.</p>

<p>This can&#x27;t be real, Kristine thought again.</p>

<p>But it was.</p>

<p>And for the first time… she wasn&#x27;t sure if she wanted to pull Sasha away from that life—or dive in after her.</p>

<p>An hour later, Kristine finally received a call back from her best friend.</p>

<p>&quot;Sasha!&quot; she blurted out the moment she answered. &quot;What the hell is going on with you?&quot;</p>

<p>Her voice trembled between frustration and disbelief, but Sasha sounded calm—almost too calm, like she was floating in her own little world, untouched by reality.</p>

<p>&quot;I don&#x27;t know why you&#x27;re so worked up,&quot; Sasha said with a dreamy sigh. &quot;I&#x27;m just a woman in love, Kris. And Ross? He said he&#x27;s going to marry me soon.&quot;</p>

<p>Kristine was momentarily speechless.</p>

<p>&quot;Marry you?&quot; she echoed.</p>

<p>&quot;Yes,&quot; Sasha replied, like it was the most natural thing in the world. &quot;He told me I&#x27;m different. Special.&quot;</p>

<p>Kristine&#x27;s mouth opened, but no words came out. Deep down, she believed Sasha—after all, Ross had done this before.</p>

<p>Lavish attention, whispered promises, extravagant gifts. He collected women like trophies, and many of them proudly wore his name like a brand.</p>

<p>It was no secret that Ross had a harem of wives and lovers across cities and countries.</p>

<p>And apparently, Sasha was just the latest addition.</p>

<p>It should&#x27;ve been disturbing. But Sasha didn&#x27;t sound like a victim. She sounded… blissful. Almost intoxicated.</p>

<p>Still, Kristine tried to convince herself it was just a phase, something that would pass once the initial thrill wore off.</p>

<p>She was wrong.</p>

<p>Because over the next few weeks, things escalated fast.</p>

<p>One photo broke the internet.</p>
