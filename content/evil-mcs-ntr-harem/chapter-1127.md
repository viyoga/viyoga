<p>Men and women huddled together, shielding trembling children with their bodies.</p>

<p>Some clutched each other so tightly their knuckles turned white.</p>

<p>A grandmother fell to her knees, praying under her breath.</p>

<p>Two small siblings sobbed loudly, faces buried in their mother’s torn jacket.</p>

<p>Bruno’s smirk widened.</p>

<p>&quot;See that? You move one more inch and they all die! Every last one of them!&quot;</p>

<p>Ross didn’t even slow.</p>

<p>&quot;Do it.&quot;</p>

<p>His voice was soft, almost casual.</p>

<p>Then he smiled—calm, relaxed, unbothered.</p>

<p>&quot;I don’t care.&quot;</p>

<p>The entire room froze. Even the air felt like it stopped moving.</p>

<p>Bruno blinked, momentarily stunned.</p>

<p>&quot;Wh–what? You crazy bastard, I said I’ll kill them!&quot;</p>

<p>Ross walked closer.</p>

<p>&quot;Do it.&quot;</p>

<p>Bruno’s face contorted in rage. He wasn’t used to being ignored. Not here. Not in his kingdom.</p>

<p>&quot;You asked for it!&quot;</p>

<p>He swung his arm down violently.</p>

<p>&quot;DO IT!&quot;</p>

<p>The world exploded.</p>

<p>BANG!</p>

<p>BANG-BANG!</p>

<p>BANG-BANG-BANG-BANG!</p>

<p>A thunderous barrage of automatic gunfire erupted all at once.</p>

<p>Bright flashes tore through the dim room like lightning.</p>

<p>The recoil of weapons echoed like machine-fed war drums.</p>

<p>Bullets rained toward Ross in a dense, deadly wall—dozens per second.</p>

<p>Sparks flew as metal stormed through the air.</p>

<p>Powder smoke billowed outward, filling the entire space with choking haze.</p>

<p>Survivors screamed and curled into tight balls on the floor.</p>

<p>Children shrieked and covered their ears.</p>

<p>Even Bruno’s own men flinched at the violence of their own gunfire.</p>

<p>But Ross...</p>

<p>...kept walking.</p>

<p>The bullets reached him—and stopped.</p>

<p>Just inches from his face, the entire volley froze in mid-air, vibrating violently like angry insects trapped in glass.</p>

<p>The metal groaned, softening as heat surged through each round.</p>

<p>Their casings melted first, dripping like wax.</p>

<p>Then the cores liquefied into glowing fragments, falling to the ground with sizzling splatters.</p>

<p>One of the men stopped shooting, eyes wide with terror.</p>

<p>&quot;H–he’s not human...&quot; he whispered.</p>

<p>More bullets fell.</p>

<p>More steam rose.</p>

<p>The firing slowed as panic took over.</p>

<p>Ross finally lifted a hand—and flicked an imaginary speck of dust off his coat.</p>

<p>Bruno took an involuntary step back, sweat pouring down his face despite the cold air.</p>

<p>&quot;W–What... what the hell are you?!&quot; he choked out, stumbling as he tried to distance himself from Ross’s approaching figure.</p>

<p>Ross didn’t answer.</p>

<p>He simply smiled.</p>

<p>The kind of smile that told everyone in the room that their worst nightmare had just arrived—and the massacre had only begun.</p>

<p>&quot;Me? I’m a person of justice—the one who makes things right in this cursed world,&quot; Ross said, his voice flat but echoing in the store like a death sentence.</p>

<p>As he raised his hand, shelves rattled.</p>

<p>A hammer shot off a rack and tore through the air, slamming perfectly into his grip.</p>

<p>Metal glinted under the flickering lights.</p>

<p>In his hand, it wasn’t a tool. It was an executioner’s weapon.</p>

<p>Bang!</p>

<p>Ross launched himself forward, the tiles beneath his feet shattering from the explosive force.</p>

<p>The nearest man didn’t even get a chance to scream.</p>

<p>Puchi!</p>

<p>The hammer came down like a comet. Skull, bone, and brain matter burst in a red spray.</p>

<p>The body collapsed instantly, legs twitching as Ross was already gone—already behind the next man.</p>

<p>He moved like a shadow cutting through a field of wheat.</p>

<p>Every swing was a life ended, every step a corpse left behind.</p>

<p>Limbs snapped, ribs caved in, spines broke with sickening cracks.</p>

<p>Men screamed, begged, tried to flee—but Ross was everywhere, untouchable, inevitable.</p>

<p>Blood misted the air with every strike, painting the supermarket aisles crimson.</p>

<p>&quot;What the fuck! Kill him! Kill him now!&quot; Bruno’s voice cracked, high-pitched with panic.</p>

<p>He stumbled backwards, waving his arms as his men opened fire.</p>

<p>RAT-TAT-TAT-TAT!</p>

<p>Bullets tore through the air but froze an inch from Ross, hanging suspended like time itself bowed to him.</p>

<p>Dozens of abilities followed—fireballs exploding, wind blades slicing toward him, crackling lightning bolts, even shards of ice meant to impale him.</p>

<p>None of it worked.</p>

<p>The attacks hit his skin and fizzled out harmlessly, more like raindrops than weapons.</p>

<p>The bullets clattered to the floor at his feet.</p>

<p>The store grew colder as every man present realized the horrible truth:</p>

<p>They couldn’t hurt him. Not even a little.</p>

<p>Ross didn’t even bother looking at their attacks.</p>

<p>He simply walked through them, hammer dragging against the floor—scraping, ringing, promising death with every step.</p>

<p>Then he moved again.</p>

<p>BANG!</p>

<p>CRACK!</p>

<p>Puchi!</p>

<p>Thud!</p>

<p>Crunch!</p>

<p>Screams turned to gurgles. Pleas turned to silence.</p>

<p>A man tried to crawl away, leaving a smear of blood behind him—Ross stepped on his back, crushed his spine, and moved on without a glance.</p>

<p>Another tried to use his ability to fly up to safety, but Ross leaped, grabbed his ankle, and slammed him down like a sack of meat.</p>

<p>One minute felt like an eternity of carnage.</p>

<p>When the violence finally stopped, the supermarket looked like a slaughterhouse.</p>

<p>Bodies lay scattered, mangled beyond recognition.</p>

<p>Blood pooled between the aisles, dripping from shelves and painting the floor crimson.</p>

<p>Ross stood alone in the center, not a drop of blood touching him—every drop had curved around him, afraid to stain him.</p>

<p>Only Bruno remained.</p>

<p>Trembling. White as ash. Cornered against the shelf of canned goods.</p>

<p>His knees shook so hard he could barely stay standing.</p>

<p>The reek of blood filled the room, and Ross’s shadow fell over him like a death omen.</p>

<p>He had been left alive for one reason:</p>

<p>He was next.</p>

<p>&quot;Fuck you!&quot; Bruno screamed, voice cracking as he turned to flee.</p>

<p>His heavy boots clanged on the blood-splattered floor as his skin began to shift—darkening, hardening, turning into gleaming sheets of steel.</p>

<p>Metal crept across his arms and shoulders like living armor, covering every inch of him until he stood like a steel giant.</p>

<p>This was his pride.</p>

<p>This was why he led.</p>

<p>This was why no one dared defy him.</p>

<p>Guns couldn’t penetrate him.</p>

<p>Knives bent against him.</p>

<p>Even abilities from awakened survivors bounced off harmlessly.</p>

<p>But all of that meant nothing against Ross.</p>

<p>Bruno spun around with a roar and threw a monstrous punch toward Ross’s face—a blow that could smash through concrete.</p>

<p>Ding!</p>
