<p>&quot;It all makes sense now,&quot; Wesley muttered through gritted teeth, more to himself than to anyone else.</p>

<p>&quot;No wonder you&#x27;ve been hard to reach lately, Sasha. I&#x27;m out here thinking everything&#x27;s fine—meanwhile, you&#x27;ve been letting this asshole take you out behind my back?&quot;</p>

<p>Sasha opened her mouth, but no words came. Her heart was pounding.</p>

<p>She hadn&#x27;t meant to hurt anyone. She hadn&#x27;t even thought things through.</p>

<p>It had all started off so simple—just a few dates. A distraction. A curiosity.</p>

<p>But Ross had a way of making her feel seen in a way Wesley never had. It had spiraled before she could stop it.</p>

<p>&quot;You&#x27;re embarrassing yourself,&quot; Ross said coolly, breaking the silence.</p>

<p>He wasn&#x27;t mocking, but his words cut deep. &quot;Put your ego back in your pocket before someone gets hurt.&quot;</p>

<p>That was the last straw.</p>

<p>&quot;Fuck you, Ross!&quot; Wesley shouted, his voice cracking under the weight of rage and betrayal.</p>

<p>And then, without warning, he reached into his jacket and yanked out a pistol.</p>

<p>Gasps echoed around the park as a few bystanders, who had started to take notice of the escalating argument, scattered for cover.</p>

<p>Birds flapped away from a nearby tree as the tension exploded into chaos.</p>

<p>Sasha screamed.</p>

<p>Ross didn&#x27;t flinch. His gaze locked on Wesley, cold and focused.</p>

<p>He had seen this kind of recklessness before—guys who let their emotions rule their actions, who brought weapons to solve problems they didn&#x27;t understand.</p>

<p>But this wasn&#x27;t the time for bravado. One wrong move, and someone could die.</p>

<p>&quot;Don&#x27;t be stupid,&quot; Ross said, his voice sharp now, commanding. &quot;You pull that trigger, and your life is over. Not just because of what&#x27;ll happen to me—but because of what&#x27;ll happen to you.&quot;</p>

<p>Wesley&#x27;s hand trembled slightly. He hadn&#x27;t expected Ross to stay calm.</p>

<p>He wanted him to panic, to back off, to show fear.</p>

<p>But Ross wasn&#x27;t giving him that satisfaction.</p>

<p>And Sasha—her voice finally returned—stepped forward, her face pale. &quot;Wesley, please… put it down. This isn&#x27;t you. Just—just stop.&quot;</p>

<p>For a moment, everything hung in the balance.</p>

<p>And then…</p>

<p>&quot;Fuck you! Go to hell first!&quot; Wesley shouted, his voice cracking with rage and desperation.</p>

<p>He wasn&#x27;t thinking anymore. Reason had long since left him.</p>

<p>In that moment, he did what far too many foolish, jealous boyfriends had done before him—he got stupid.</p>

<p>Bang.</p>

<p>Bang.</p>

<p>Bang.</p>

<p>The shots rang out in rapid succession, deafening and chaotic.</p>

<p>People nearby screamed and scattered, ducking for cover.</p>

<p>Birds took to the sky in a flurry of wings. The air itself seemed to split.</p>

<p>But Ross didn&#x27;t flinch.</p>

<p>Each bullet missed him by mere inches, whizzing past with deadly intent.</p>

<p>His eyes narrowed, not in fear—but in cold, focused resolve.</p>

<p>In the very next second, Ross dashed forward like a bullet of his own.</p>

<p>In a blur of motion, he closed the distance and was instantly upon Wesley.</p>

<p>His foot came up hard and fast—a brutal kick straight to the groin.</p>

<p>Wesley cried out, his body folding in pain as he collapsed to the ground.</p>

<p>But Ross wasn&#x27;t finished.</p>

<p>He dropped low, straddled him, and unleashed a storm of punishing blows.</p>

<p>His fists rained down on Wesley&#x27;s face, knuckles cracking against bone with merciless precision.</p>

<p>Each punch was calculated, vicious, and full of a personal edge.</p>

<p>Ross had always taken special satisfaction in wrecking a pretty face like this—and Wesley&#x27;s was no exception.</p>

<p>Blood splattered across the pavement as the handsome features that once inspired envy were reduced to a swollen, broken mess.</p>

<p>&quot;S-Stop…!&quot; Wesley choked through broken lips.</p>

<p>But Ross didn&#x27;t hear him—not through the red haze.</p>

<p>It was only when Sasha finally snapped out of her shock and rushed over that the assault came to an end.</p>

<p>&quot;Ross, stop! You&#x27;re going to kill him!&quot; she cried, throwing her arms around him from behind.</p>

<p>Her touch was soft, trembling, but firm. Ross froze, her voice cutting through his fury.</p>

<p>He allowed himself to be pulled back, his chest heaving as he glared down at the broken man beneath him.</p>

<p>A tense silence followed, broken only by Sasha&#x27;s ragged breathing and Wesley&#x27;s groans.</p>

<p>Then came the sound of sirens in the distance—approaching fast.</p>

<p>Within minutes, flashing red and blue lights painted the scene.</p>

<p>Police officers stormed in, weapons drawn, shouting commands as they secured the area and disarmed Wesley.</p>

<p>Medics rushed to his side.</p>

<p>Ross stood tall, blood staining his knuckles, calm as ever.</p>

<p>Sasha looked up at him, eyes wide with conflicting emotions—fear, relief, awe. Ross didn&#x27;t say a word. He didn&#x27;t need to.</p>

<p>He&#x27;d made his statement loud and clear.</p>

<p>Ross was briefly questioned by the police, but they let him go soon after.</p>

<p>Multiple eyewitnesses backed up his story, and the nearby security cameras had captured the entire incident.</p>

<p>It was a clear case of self-defense.</p>

<p>Wesley, on the other hand, wasn&#x27;t so lucky. He was rushed to the hospital, escorted by officers who kept a close eye on him.</p>

<p>With the stunt he had pulled, jail time was inevitable. Ross would make sure of it.</p>

<p>No amount of money or connections was going to save Wesley from the consequences—not this time.</p>

<p>As the chaos settled, Ross turned to Sasha. &quot;Come on. I&#x27;ll take you home.&quot;</p>

<p>They resumed their walk in silence, the tension from earlier still lingering in the air.</p>

<p>Sasha&#x27;s apartment was close by, just a few blocks away.</p>

<p>The night had grown colder, quieter, as if the city itself was catching its breath.</p>

<p>When they finally reached her building, Ross turned to leave.</p>

<p>But just as he stepped back, Sasha reached out and caught his sleeve. Her voice was soft, barely above a whisper.</p>

<p>&quot;Don&#x27;t go. I don&#x27;t want to be alone tonight.&quot;</p>

<p>It was the kind of request Ross had been expecting—perhaps even waiting for.</p>

<p>After what she&#x27;d just been through, it was only natural.</p>

<p>A near-death experience could shake even the strongest heart, and tonight, she didn&#x27;t want to face the darkness by herself.</p>

<p>Ross looked at her for a long moment, then gave a small nod.</p>

<p>&quot;Alright,&quot; he said gently. &quot;I&#x27;ll stay.&quot;</p>
