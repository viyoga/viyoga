<p>Ross already knew the film would be a massive success, just like his first one.</p>

<p>The buzz surrounding it was undeniable, and with his name attached, it was practically destined to dominate the box office.</p>

<p>That certainty allowed him to put in only the bare minimum effort in the promotional circuit, attending interviews and press events with the same casual confidence that had made him a global sensation.</p>

<p>His real focus, however, was on something far greater—his long-term goals.</p>

<p>Brick by brick, he was shaping the reality he had envisioned for himself, turning his wildest ambitions into tangible achievements.</p>

<p>Wealth, power, influence—he was accumulating it all at a terrifying pace.</p>

<p>And at his side was Nicole, the woman who had unexpectedly become a significant part of his life.</p>

<p>One evening, after another long day, they sat together in his penthouse suite overlooking the city skyline.</p>

<p>The view was breathtaking, but Nicole&#x27;s mind was elsewhere. She had been quiet, lost in thought, and Ross could sense it.</p>

<p>Finally, she turned to him, her fingers nervously twisting the hem of her dress.</p>

<p>&quot;Isn&#x27;t this too soon, Ross?&quot; she asked, her voice uncertain.</p>

<p>Ross raised a brow. &quot;What is?&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;This… marriage.&quot; She exhaled softly.</p>

<p>&quot;I mean, I expected us to get married eventually, but I didn&#x27;t think it would happen this fast.&quot;</p>

<p>Ross leaned back, studying her with an unreadable expression.</p>

<p>Nicole pressed on, her uncertainty growing.</p>

<p>&quot;We only met in the first quarter of the year, and now you&#x27;re already planning our wedding for December. It just feels… like everything is moving too fast.&quot;</p>

<p>Ross scoffed, shaking his head.</p>

<p>&quot;It&#x27;s not.&quot; His voice was firm, confident.</p>

<p>&quot;We&#x27;ve already done everything married couples do—and more. A wedding is just a piece of paper, a formality. It&#x27;s a one-day event. Nothing more.&quot;</p>

<p>Nicole hesitated. She wanted to argue, to tell him that marriage meant more to her than just a legal formality.</p>

<p>But the way Ross spoke made it clear that, to him, this was simply another step in his unstoppable march forward.</p>

<p>Still, she forced a smile and nodded. &quot;Okay.&quot;</p>

<p>But deep down, a heavy feeling settled in her chest.</p>

<p>There was something she hadn&#x27;t told him—something she wasn&#x27;t ready to admit just yet.</p>

<p>And as Ross continued talking about their future, she wondered how long she could keep it to herself.</p>

<p>***</p>

<p>One month later, the event finally came to pass—a tense, formal encounter set against the backdrop of Nicole&#x27;s family estate, a grand villa exuding old-world charm and authority.</p>

<p>Ross found himself standing in the expansive, meticulously decorated foyer, his presence as composed and defiant as ever.</p>

<p>The polished marble floors and richly paneled walls bore silent witness to the gathering storm of emotions that was about to unfold.</p>

<p>Before Ross could settle into the comfortable armchair provided for guests, Nicole&#x27;s father swept into the room with a purpose.</p>

<p>His face, set in a hardened scowl, conveyed disdain and an unwavering certainty in his disapproval. In a voice that brooked no argument, he began, &quot;Go back, young man. We don&#x27;t accept your relationship with our daughter.&quot; His words were crisp and uncompromising, slicing through the room&#x27;s heavy silence like a knife.</p>

<p>&quot;And if you&#x27;re here to ask for our blessings,&quot; he continued with a dismissive shake of his head, &quot;then you can&#x27;t expect to receive them. We believe you are not a man of honor.&quot; His tone was laden with scorn, and it was clear that he viewed Ross as a disruptive force—a man who had led his daughter astray.</p>

<p>&quot;My daughter has been miserably misguided in accepting you into her life. Ian was a good man—a man you can never live up to,&quot; he declared, his words echoing off the ornate walls.</p>

<p>Ross, far from shrinking under the barrage of insults, maintained a steady, measured smile.</p>

<p>His eyes sparkled with a mixture of amusement and calculated resolve.</p>

<p>In that moment, he was not merely defending his honor; he was asserting his indomitable nature.</p>

<p>The challenge had been thrown down, and he welcomed it without a trace of hesitation.</p>

<p>As the tension escalated, Ross&#x27;s gaze drifted casually to the elegant woman standing nearby—Nicole&#x27;s mother, a striking figure whose beauty and poise contrasted sharply with her husband&#x27;s bristling hostility.</p>

<p>Her calm, almost amused expression hinted at an unspoken alliance, one that added an unexpected twist to the confrontation.</p>

<p>Ross mused internally, a wry smile playing at the corners of his lips.</p>

<p>&quot;This is going to be interesting,&quot; he thought, savoring the subtle defiance in her eyes as they met his.</p>

<p>For a long, suspended moment, the room held its breath.</p>

<p>Every detail—from the faint rustle of silk dresses to the barely perceptible tapping of shoes on the marble floor—amplified the gravity of the exchange.</p>

<p>Nicole&#x27;s father, seemingly intent on laying down his ultimatum, saw only his disapproval reflected in every polished surface of the room.</p>

<p>He was convinced that his stern words would shatter Ross&#x27;s resolve, that his vision of honor would prevail.</p>

<p>But Ross was not one to be cowed by bluster or old-fashioned notions of propriety. With a measured tilt of his head and a cool, unyielding tone, he finally broke the silence.</p>

<p>&quot;I appreciate your candor,&quot; he said, his voice calm yet edged with a hint of defiance. &quot;However, my intentions with your daughter are clear, and my track record speaks for itself. I do not seek your approval—I seek to build a life together, a future that neither of us can fully predict but both of us are willing to fight for.&quot;</p>

<p>The room seemed to vibrate with the weight of his words. His statement was a quiet yet potent declaration of independence, a refusal to be defined by someone else&#x27;s narrow standards.</p>

<p>&quot;I&#x27;m sorry for wasting your time,&quot; Ross said and added coolly before turning on his heel and walking away without another word. His departure was swift, his posture rigid with restrained emotion.</p>

<p>&quot;Ross!&quot; Nicole called after him, her voice laced with urgency. But before following, she turned to look at her parents, disappointment flickering in her eyes.</p>

<p>Once again, they had let her down. Once again, they had tried to control her life.</p>

<p>This time, however, she didn&#x27;t waste her breath arguing with them. There was nothing left to say.</p>

<p>Without another glance, she turned and ran after Ross, her footsteps echoing in the grand hall as she chose to walk away—just like him.</p>
