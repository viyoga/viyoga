<p>Bella stood a short distance away, motionless.</p>

<p>She was face to face with Ross.</p>

<p>Ross was crouched near a flat stone, his movements unhurried as he sharpened his knives.</p>

<p>The steady sound of scraping metal against whetstone cut through the silence, rhythmic and unsettling.</p>

<p>Tiny sparks flickered now and then, briefly illuminating the lower edge of his cloak.</p>

<p>Sebastian slowed his steps.</p>

<p>Even now—especially now—Ross hadn’t removed his cloak.</p>

<p>The hood hung low over his face, concealing his features entirely. It was strange.</p>

<p>The night wasn’t cold. If anything, it was thick and suffocating, yet Ross remained wrapped in layers as if the world itself couldn’t be allowed to touch him.</p>

<p>Bella stood directly in front of him, far too close.</p>

<p>She didn’t speak. Neither did Ross.</p>

<p>Sebastian felt a knot tighten in his stomach.</p>

<p>The scene felt wrong, like he had walked into the middle of something he wasn’t meant to see.</p>

<p>Bella’s posture was stiff, her shoulders slightly raised, as though she were torn between stepping closer and fleeing altogether.</p>

<p>Ross continued sharpening his blades, utterly calm, as if Bella’s presence meant nothing—or worse, as if he had expected her to come.</p>

<p>He’s really weird, Sebastian thought, a chill crawling up his spine.</p>

<p>The forest around them was silent. No insects.</p>

<p>No wind. Just the soft scrape of steel and the growing sense that something unseen was unfolding right in front of him—and that whatever Bella had sensed... it wasn’t gone yet.</p>

<p>&quot;What’s wrong?&quot; Ross looked up from his knives, his movements slowing as his sharp eyes settled on Bella.</p>

<p>His expression shifted into something carefully neutral, almost curious, as if he were trying to read an unfamiliar situation.</p>

<p>Bella didn’t answer him.</p>

<p>In fact, she didn’t seem aware of anything at all.</p>

<p>Her body moved before her thoughts could catch up. One step forward. Then another.</p>

<p>Each movement felt wrong, heavy, as if she were being dragged by invisible strings. Inside her mind, chaos erupted.</p>

<p>What am I doing?! Stop—stop right now!</p>

<p>She screamed at herself, panic clawing at her thoughts, but her mind no longer had control.</p>

<p>Her arms lifted on their own, trembling only for a brief moment before wrapping themselves tightly around Ross’s waist.</p>

<p>The contact sent a jolt through her, cold and shocking, yet her body held on as if it were the most natural thing in the world.</p>

<p>Sebastian froze for half a heartbeat.</p>

<p>Then he saw clearly.</p>

<p>Bella—his Bella—was hugging another man.</p>

<p>The world seemed to tilt.</p>

<p>&quot;Let go of her!&quot; Sebastian roared, rage exploding out of him unchecked.</p>

<p>His voice echoed through the forest, raw and furious, carrying with it disbelief, betrayal, and fear all tangled together.</p>

<p>Energy surged violently inside him as his ability responded to his emotions.</p>

<p>The familiar pressure built rapidly, power coiling and ready to be unleashed.</p>

<p>He took a step forward, eyes locked onto Ross, fully prepared to attack.</p>

<p>But Ross moved first.</p>

<p>He lifted both arms slowly, deliberately, palms facing outward in surrender.</p>

<p>His knives lay forgotten beside him.</p>

<p>&quot;Hey—wait!&quot; Ross barked, irritation flashing across his face. &quot;Calm the hell down, Sebastian! I’m not doing anything!&quot;</p>

<p>He glanced down at Bella clinging to him, then back at Sebastian, clearly annoyed despite the tension.</p>

<p>&quot;For heaven’s sake, it’s your wife who’s taking advantage of me!&quot;</p>

<p>The words hit Sebastian like cold water.</p>

<p>Absurd. Insulting. But... not entirely wrong.</p>

<p>Sebastian’s breathing was heavy, his chest rising and falling as he fought the instinct to strike.</p>

<p>His eyes darted over Ross—his raised hands, his rigid posture, the way he carefully avoided touching Bella back.</p>

<p>Slowly, painfully, Sebastian realized Ross wasn’t resisting because he wasn’t the one in control.</p>

<p>With a sharp exhale, Sebastian forced the power back down.</p>

<p>The pressure eased, though the anger didn’t.</p>

<p>&quot;Bella,&quot; he said urgently, stepping closer, his voice dropping into something far more fragile. &quot;Bella, wake up.&quot;</p>

<p>He reached out instinctively, then stopped himself. His hand hovered in the air, trembling.</p>

<p>He remembered the last time he’d grabbed her too roughly—the fear in her eyes, the way she had flinched.</p>

<p>He couldn’t do that again.</p>

<p>&quot;Bella,&quot; he called again, louder now. &quot;Look at me. It’s me.&quot;</p>

<p>No response.</p>

<p>Her eyes were distant, unfocused, staring at nothing.</p>

<p>Her grip on Ross remained firm, almost desperate, as if she were afraid he might vanish if she let go.</p>

<p>Her body was tense, unmoving, caught in some unseen force that ignored Sebastian’s voice entirely.</p>

<p>&quot;Bella!&quot; he shouted, panic bleeding into his tone. &quot;Please—wake up!&quot;</p>

<p>Once. Twice. Again.</p>

<p>Still nothing.</p>

<p>The forest around them felt unnaturally quiet, the crackle of the fire suddenly too loud, too sharp.</p>

<p>Ross shifted uncomfortably but didn’t dare move her himself, clearly aware that one wrong motion could trigger Sebastian again.</p>

<p>&quot;She’s not hearing you,&quot; Ross muttered grimly. &quot;Something’s wrong.&quot;</p>

<p>Sebastian clenched his fists, helplessness gnawing at him as he watched his wife cling to another man, lost somewhere he couldn’t reach.</p>

<p>Whatever had taken hold of Bella wasn’t letting her go—and for the first time since they’d met Ross, Sebastian felt something colder than anger settle in his chest.</p>

<p>Fear.</p>

<p>In the end, Sebastian had to swallow his pride.</p>

<p>There was no other option.</p>

<p>&quot;She won’t let go,&quot; he said tightly, avoiding Ross’s eyes. &quot;Just... carry her to the bed.&quot;</p>

<p>For a brief moment, Ross looked surprised.</p>

<p>Then he glanced down at Bella, who was clinging to him with unconscious determination, her fingers gripping his armor as if he were the only solid thing in a shifting world. With a small shrug, Ross nodded.</p>

<p>&quot;Fine,&quot; he said. &quot;But don’t blame me if this gets weirder.&quot;</p>

<p>He moved carefully, slow and deliberate, as if any sudden motion might trigger another reaction.</p>

<p>Bella didn’t resist as he laid her down on the makeshift bed of grass and folded clothes.</p>

<p>Her body relaxed the moment her back touched it, and for a heartbeat, Sebastian allowed himself to hope it was finally over.</p>

<p>Then Bella stirred.</p>

<p>With surprising speed, she rolled and sprang forward, moving purely on instinct.</p>
