<p>A week passed, and Ross’s life seemed to return to normal. However, one noticeable change was Imari’s choice of attire.</p>

<p>She had begun dressing in increasingly provocative outfits compared to when she first joined the group.</p>

<p>Now, whenever Ross was around, she barely wore anything at all. It was understandable, given the circumstances—she was the only remaining virgin in the group and couldn’t help but feel left out.</p>

<p>Unfortunately for her, Ross approached the situation with calculated patience. Just as he had done with Mary in the past, he intended to let the tension build.</p>

<p>He would wait, letting Imari’s desires simmer until she could no longer contain herself. When that moment came, she would be the one to approach him, surrendering willingly and on her own terms.</p>

<p>As for Reina and Mari, they remained at the house, taking on the roles of its caretakers. Ross maintained a subtle balance with them, keeping their intimate moments separate from the rest of his harem.</p>

<p>This separation wasn’t just about discretion—it was also a calculated move. Ross didn’t want to shock Ren by revealing the depth of his growing closeness to her mother.</p>

<p>Ren would eventually find out, of course, but Ross was determined to control the timing of that revelation. For now, he kept things quiet, allowing events to unfold at his own pace.</p>

<p>Ross had something important to address, and as the group gathered that evening, he prepared himself for the reactions his announcement would bring.</p>

<p>The atmosphere was lighthearted at first, but Ross’s serious tone quickly drew their attention.</p>

<p>&quot;I’m going to L.A.,&quot; he said, breaking the casual chatter. His gaze swept across the room before he added, &quot;And Althea is coming with me.&quot;</p>

<p>The room went still for a moment as the group processed his words. Then, one of the girls spoke up, curiosity laced with concern.</p>

<p>&quot;Why are you two going to L.A., Ross?&quot;</p>

<p>Ross took a deep breath. This wasn’t news he could sugarcoat, and there was no point in hiding it. Sooner or later, they’d all see the changes in Althea, and secrecy would only lead to misunderstandings.</p>

<p>&quot;She’s pregnant,&quot; he said, his voice steady but laced with emotion.</p>

<p>&quot;WHAT?!&quot; The reaction was immediate and overwhelming. Gasps echoed through the room as several of the women covered their mouths, their expressions a mix of shock and disbelief.</p>

<p>&quot;I know this comes as a surprise,&quot; Ross continued, raising his hands slightly to calm the room.</p>

<p>&quot;But yes, Althea is eight weeks pregnant. I’ve decided I don’t want to wait any longer to make things official. It would only get harder for her as time goes on, so I plan to marry her soon.&quot;</p>

<p>Another wave of shock rippled through the group.</p>

<p>&quot;You’re getting married?!&quot; one of them exclaimed, her voice tinged with sadness.</p>

<p>Ross nodded, meeting their wide-eyed stares with a calm and determined gaze. He understood their reaction—this was unexpected, and emotions were running high.</p>

<p>&quot;I know this is a lot to take in,&quot; he said, his tone gentle. &quot;But there’s more. I’m not going to marry her alone. I want to marry all of you… if you’ll have me.&quot;</p>

<p>The room fell silent as his words sank in. For a moment, it seemed as if time had stopped. Then, one by one, their expressions shifted from shock to joy.</p>

<p>The air filled with exclamations of agreement as they rushed toward him.</p>

<p>&quot;ROSS! YES!&quot;</p>

<p>Tears streamed down their faces as they embraced him, the overwhelming emotion of the moment bringing them closer than ever.</p>

<p>It wasn’t just a proposal—it was a promise to share their lives together, to be a family in every sense of the word.</p>

<p>What followed was a whirlwind of planning. The women, their initial shock replaced with excitement, dove into discussions about the wedding.</p>

<p>Ross suggested holding it in three weeks to minimize stress on Althea and her pregnancy. Everyone agreed it was the perfect timeframe, not just for Althea but also for the group as a whole.</p>

<p>It would be a shared celebration, a testament to their unique bond.</p>

<p>That night was unlike any other. The joy of the engagement, the depth of their love, and the promise of their future together transformed the evening into something extraordinary.</p>

<p>Their bedroom became a sanctuary of passion and connection, filled with whispered vows, gentle caresses, and unrestrained bliss.</p>

<p>It wasn’t just about physical intimacy—it was a celebration of the life they were building together, a union that defied convention but felt utterly right to everyone involved.</p>

<p>As Ross lay surrounded by the women he loved, he couldn’t help but feel a profound sense of fulfillment.</p>

<p>This wasn’t just his family—it was his future, and he was determined to protect and cherish it with everything he had.</p>
