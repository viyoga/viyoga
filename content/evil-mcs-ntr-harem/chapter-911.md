<p>&quot;Mind if I sit here? Looks like you’ve got the best seat in the place.&quot;</p>

<p>She blinked. Her heart hammered in her chest.</p>

<p>A dozen answers fought to come out at once, but all she managed was a mute nod.</p>

<p>How could she ever say no to a larger than life man like Ross Oakley.</p>

<p>Ross chuckled under his breath and slid into the seat opposite her, setting his tray down.</p>

<p>He took a sip of his Coke as if nothing about this moment was unusual.</p>

<p>For him, maybe it wasn’t. For her, it was surreal—dreamlike, almost absurd.</p>

<p>She sat frozen for a moment, burger half-eaten in her hands, unsure if she should say something or just let the silence stretch.</p>

<p>The sound of her own heartbeat filled her ears, drowning out the chatter and clatter of the restaurant.</p>

<p>Ross glanced at her, an amused smile tugging at his lips.</p>

<p>&quot;Relax. I don’t bite. Not unless it’s a burger.&quot; He held one up, grinning, before taking a bite.</p>

<p>That broke the tension just enough for her to laugh—softly, nervously.</p>

<p>She lowered her gaze, cheeks warm, and tried to remind herself that he was just a man sitting across from her.</p>

<p>And yet, every part of her knew this moment wasn’t ordinary. This was Ross Oakley.</p>

<p>And this was the very first moment their paths crossed.</p>

<p>She would look back on it for years to come as the beginning of everything.</p>

<p>Ross and the young woman ate in silence for a minute, the noise of the restaurant filling the air—the sizzle of the fryers, the chatter of other students, the clink of trays being set down.</p>

<p>For Carrie, every sound felt muted, distant, as if the entire world had dimmed the moment Ross Oakley sat across from her.</p>

<p>Then, true to his nature, Ross broke the silence with ease.</p>

<p>He leaned forward slightly, confidence radiating off him in a way that was neither forced nor arrogant—it was simply who he was.</p>

<p>&quot;You were right earlier,&quot; he said with that signature half-smile, the one she’d seen countless times on television and in magazines.</p>

<p>&quot;I’m Ross. Pleasure to meet you...&quot;</p>

<p>He extended his hand across the table, pausing for her to respond.</p>

<p>&quot;Carrie,&quot; she replied, her voice quieter than she intended. She placed her hand in his.</p>

<p>The moment their skin touched, she felt it—strength.</p>

<p>His hand was large, warm, and steady, the grip firm yet effortless, like he could crush hers without meaning to, yet chose restraint.</p>

<p>It wasn’t just a handshake; it was the kind of touch that carried power, dominance, and certainty.</p>

<p>For a heartbeat, she forgot to breathe.</p>

<p>Carrie forced herself to look up into his face, and the sight only deepened her awe.</p>

<p>He was already past forty—she knew that from headlines and biographies—but sitting here now, it was impossible to believe.</p>

<p>He looked like a man in his mid-twenties, carved by youth and vigor, as if he lived permanently in his prime.</p>

<p>His skin was unlined, his eyes sharp, his presence magnetic.</p>

<p>And it wasn’t just his appearance.</p>

<p>Behind that face was the legend: Ross Oakley, the man with dozens of wives—each one a beauty that could grace the stage of any Miss Universe contest.</p>

<p>He was a billionaire, an NBA champion, a movie star, a man whose life had outgrown ordinary definitions.</p>

<p>Ross Oakley wasn’t just a man anymore. He was a force of nature, larger than life itself.</p>

<p>Sitting across from him, Carrie felt the air thicken, heavy with his aura.</p>

<p>She couldn’t describe it any other way—just being near him made her feel smaller, fragile, as though she had stumbled into the orbit of someone far beyond her reach.</p>

<p>And then he looked at her.</p>

<p>Not a passing glance, not a casual flicker of his eyes—he looked at her, steady and unblinking, as if she was the only person in the room.</p>

<p>Carrie’s pulse spiked.</p>

<p>Under the weight of his gaze, she felt stripped bare, every defense peeled away.</p>

<p>It was like he could see through her skin, into her heart, into every hidden longing she had never spoken aloud.</p>

<p>She shifted in her seat, suddenly aware of how tightly she was clutching her soda cup.</p>

<p>A warmth crept up her neck and into her cheeks.</p>

<p>Ross’s lips curved again, not unkindly, but with a faint trace of amusement—as though he already knew what she was feeling, as though he’d seen this reaction a thousand times in a thousand different faces.</p>

<p>&quot;Carrie,&quot; he repeated softly, rolling her name on his tongue. &quot;Pretty name.&quot;</p>

<p>The simple compliment made her chest tighten.</p>

<p>She wanted to say something back, anything at all, but her throat felt dry, her thoughts a tangled mess.</p>

<p>For all the boys who had chased her in the past, none had ever made her feel like this.</p>

<p>She had always been in control, aloof, distant. But here and now, under Ross Oakley’s gaze, she felt completely defenseless.</p>

<p>It was the first time in her life she realized how dangerous attraction could be.</p>

<p>&quot;Thanks,&quot; Carrie replied softly, her voice barely above a whisper.</p>

<p>Her shyness showed in the way she lowered her eyes and fidgeted with the straw of her drink.</p>

<p>There was nothing in the world that could make her feel brave in front of Ross Oakley—a man who had achieved everything she could only dream of.</p>

<p>He had money, fame, power, and the kind of legendary reputation that made people treat him like royalty wherever he went.</p>

<p>She, on the other hand, was just a struggling college student, still completely reliant on her parents’ support.</p>

<p>She’d thought about taking on part-time jobs like many of her classmates, but her parents had insisted she focus on her studies alone.</p>

<p>Compared to the empire Ross Oakley commanded, her life felt so small, so ordinary, almost laughable.</p>

<p>And yet, here he was. Sitting across from her. Speaking to her.</p>

<p>Carrie could hardly believe how the night had unfolded, how fate had decided to push her into the orbit of someone so far above her world.</p>
