<p>While Ross remained seated comfortably in his chair, Miss Su was experiencing an entirely different situation.</p>

<p>She was still kneeling before him, her beautiful face filled with disbelief and frustration as she desperately tried to regain control of her body.</p>

<p>She tried moving her arms.</p>

<p>Nothing.</p>

<p>She tried standing.</p>

<p>Nothing.</p>

<p>She even circulated the spiritual energy within her dantian and attempted to forcefully break whatever mysterious restriction had been placed upon her.</p>

<p>Still nothing.</p>

<p>Her body simply refused to obey her.</p>

<p>It was as though every muscle, every nerve, and even her spiritual energy had been disconnected from her will.</p>

<p>She could think freely, she could feel everything happening around her, and she could clearly understand what was going on, but her body would only move according to Ross’s commands.</p>

<p>Just how strong is he?</p>

<p>Miss Su’s heart trembled.</p>

<p>How is he doing this?</p>

<p>She had cultivated for many years and had already reached the Foundation Establishment Realm.</p>

<p>Within Golden Fortune, she was considered a powerful cultivator, and even many of the guests who visited the gambling house had to show her respect.</p>

<p>Yet before Ross, all of that strength seemed completely meaningless.</p>

<p>She couldn’t even resist a single command.</p>

<p>Could he be in the Core Formation Realm?</p>

<p>That was the lowest possibility she could think of.</p>

<p>Perhaps he was even stronger.</p>

<p>The thought made Miss Su’s expression turn solemn.</p>

<p>If Ross was truly a cultivator several realms above her, then it explained why she couldn’t sense anything from him earlier.</p>

<p>His cultivation was simply too far beyond her own ability to perceive.</p>

<p>But there was another problem.</p>

<p>Ross had been sitting there so casually the entire time.</p>

<p>He wasn’t even paying attention to her.</p>

<p>It was as though controlling a Foundation Establishment cultivator was no more difficult than moving a finger.</p>

<p>Miss Su clenched her teeth inwardly.</p>

<p>Who exactly is this Jiang Chen?</p>

<p>She had originally thought that he was merely an arrogant young master who had somehow cheated the gambling house.</p>

<p>Now she realized that she had been completely wrong.</p>

<p>This man was far more dangerous than she had imagined.</p>

<p>And yet Ross himself seemed completely unconcerned about her thoughts.</p>

<p>He leaned comfortably against the back of his chair and looked around the luxurious chamber.</p>

<p>The confrontation from earlier appeared to have already been forgotten.</p>

<p>After a moment, he rubbed his throat.</p>

<p>&quot;I’m thirsty.&quot;</p>

<p>Miss Su’s eyes widened.</p>

<p>Ross casually pointed toward the collection of wine bottles displayed along the wall.</p>

<p>&quot;Bring me your finest wine.&quot;</p>

<p>The instant the command was given, Miss Su’s body moved.</p>

<p>She wanted to resist.</p>

<p>She wanted to remain where she was and demand that Ross release her.</p>

<p>But her legs moved anyway.</p>

<p>She slowly stood and walked toward the wine collection.</p>

<p>No!</p>

<p>Miss Su screamed inwardly.</p>

<p>Her mind desperately fought against the invisible control, but her body simply ignored her wishes.</p>

<p>She reached the shelves, selected one of the most expensive wines in the room, and returned to Ross.</p>

<p>She placed the bottle and a cup on the table with trembling hands.</p>

<p>Ross glanced at the bottle and nodded approvingly.</p>

<p>&quot;Good. You have expensive tastes.&quot;</p>

<p>Miss Su’s face twitched.</p>

<p>Of course I have expensive tastes! That’s one of the finest wines in Golden Fortune!</p>

<p>She wanted to shout those words, but no sound came from her mouth.</p>

<p>Ross picked up the bottle, poured himself a cup, and took a leisurely sip.</p>

<p>His expression immediately brightened.</p>

<p>&quot;Not bad.&quot;</p>

<p>Miss Su could only stare at him helplessly.</p>

<p>The situation was becoming more absurd by the second.</p>

<p>She had invited him upstairs intending to interrogate him, perhaps even force him to reveal how he had cheated the gambling house.</p>

<p>Instead, she was now standing obediently beside him, serving him wine in her own private chambers.</p>

<p>And the worst part was that she couldn’t do anything about it.</p>

<p>Ross took another sip and smiled.</p>

<p>&quot;This place really does know how to entertain its guests.&quot;</p>

<p>Miss Su’s heart sank.</p>

<p>She suddenly had a very bad feeling that the night was going to be much longer than she had originally expected.</p>

<p>From that day onward, Ross practically made the Golden Fortune Gambling House his second home.</p>

<p>He could often be seen on the second floor, casually sitting in Miss Su’s private chambers as though the place belonged to him.</p>

<p>Sometimes he would stroll to the railing and look down at the bustling gambling hall below, watching the gamblers come and go.</p>

<p>Strangely enough, he no longer participated in the games.</p>

<p>After winning an absurd amount of money, Ross seemed to have completely lost interest in gambling.</p>

<p>Instead, he behaved more and more like the actual owner of the establishment.</p>

<p>He would occasionally give an instruction, ask for something to eat or drink, or simply sit there enjoying himself while Miss Su remained nearby.</p>

<p>Naturally, this strange development did not escape anyone’s attention.</p>

<p>The patrons and workers of Golden Fortune began whispering among themselves.</p>

<p>&quot;This can’t be happening!&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;Is Miss Su actually together with that Jiang Chen?&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;It’s hard to believe! Jiang Chen might be handsome, but everyone knows he’s a trash who can’t cultivate. How could Miss Su possibly choose someone like him?&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;Perhaps he used some kind of trick on her!&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;Nooooo!&quot;</p>

<p>One young man clutched his chest dramatically.</p>

<p>&quot;The goddess I’ve admired for years is actually with another man?! My heart can’t take this!&quot;</p>

<p>Another patron sighed.</p>

<p>&quot;Forget about your heart. I think the entire Golden Fortune is going crazy.&quot;</p>

<p>Everywhere Ross went, people secretly watched him.</p>

<p>Some were jealous.</p>

<p>Some were confused.</p>

<p>Some were heartbroken.</p>

<p>And quite a few were simply curious about what had happened between him and Miss Su.</p>

<p>After all, Miss Su was no ordinary woman.</p>

<p>She was the manager of Golden Fortune and a respected Foundation Establishment cultivator.</p>

<p>Countless young cultivators had tried to win her favor over the years, yet she had never shown much interest in any of them.</p>

<p>Now, seemingly overnight, she had become inseparable from Jiang Chen.</p>

<p>To the outsiders, it was an unbelievable development.</p>

<p>But the workers of Golden Fortune were even more confused because they knew something was definitely wrong.</p>

<p>Miss Su’s behavior wasn’t normal.</p>

<p>She had always been decisive, proud, and incredibly difficult to approach. Yet whenever Ross was around, she seemed strangely obedient.</p>

<p>Among everyone watching the situation, Xue Lengyan was perhaps the most suspicious.</p>

<p>She had served Miss Su for a long time and knew her personality better than most people.</p>

<p>Something wasn’t right.</p>

<p>She quietly observed Ross for a while before her expression became serious.</p>

<p>I need to tell the young miss about this.</p>

<p>Xue Lengyan didn’t dare confront Ross directly. Whatever had happened between him and Miss Su was clearly beyond her ability to understand.</p>

<p>She hurried back to a private room, took out some paper and a writing brush, and quickly began composing a letter.</p>

<p>Her hand moved rapidly across the page as she recorded everything that had happened.</p>

<p>She finished the letter, folded it carefully, and sealed it.</p>

<p>&quot;This matter is too strange...&quot;</p>

<p>Xue Lengyan murmured.</p>

<p>&quot;I hope the young miss can figure out what’s going on.&quot;</p>

<p>With that thought, she immediately arranged for the letter to be delivered.</p>
