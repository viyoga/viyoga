<p>&quot;I feel that my f...&quot; Ross began, but the surge of energy caught him mid-sentence.</p>

<p>He groaned, clutching his face with both hands and bending forward slightly, groaning in a deliberately exaggerated display as if the stone were burning him alive.</p>

<p>The crowd collectively held its breath.</p>

<p>Some gasped, while others leaned in closer, unsure whether Ross was in grave danger or not.</p>

<p>For one full minute, Ross remained in the act—staggering slightly, teeth clenched, aura flaring wildly, his entire body shaking as though it were struggling to contain the stone’s overwhelming power.</p>

<p>The room was utterly silent; even the sounds of elemental powers in the hall seemed to fade in comparison.</p>

<p>Every eye was fixed on him, every mind straining to comprehend what they were witnessing.</p>

<p>Then, gradually, he straightened.</p>

<p>Slowly, deliberately, he removed his hands from his face and lifted his head.</p>

<p>The aura around him pulsed with quiet authority, the chaotic sparks condensing into a faint shimmer that circled his body like a halo.</p>

<p>The change was undeniable—subtle yet unmistakable.</p>

<p>His eyes shone brighter, sharper.</p>

<p>His posture exuded confidence and power in a way that made even the most seasoned men take a step back instinctively.</p>

<p>&quot;Ross... your face is...&quot; someone whispered, their voice barely audible, carrying disbelief and awe.</p>

<p>A ripple of murmurs ran through the crowd, quickly growing into a wave of astonished exclamations.</p>

<p>&quot;It’s... different!&quot;</p>

<p>People craned their necks, leaning forward, whispering to their neighbors.</p>

<p>Even those who had just gained incredible abilities themselves paused, unable to tear their eyes away from him.</p>

<p>There was something almost otherworldly about his presence now, as if the simple act of consuming the heart stone had elevated him to a new plane entirely.</p>

<p>Ross smiled faintly, the curve of his lips subtle but unmistakable.</p>

<p>His calm, controlled gaze swept over the crowd.</p>

<p>The previous chaos of excitement and experimentation seemed to hush under the weight of his presence.</p>

<p>Even the most reckless and inexperienced men instinctively slowed their movements, as if some invisible force urged restraint.</p>

<p>For a long, tense moment, silence reigned.</p>

<p>Then, as realization began to settle in, awe rippled through the hall like wildfire.</p>

<p>Everyone in the great hall was stunned, but one group—women of all ages, extremely beautiful and ordinary alike—reacted with an intensity that far outstripped the rest.</p>

<p>Their collective gasp filled the space, reverberating off the walls like the echo of a sudden storm.</p>

<p>&quot;He’s... he’s so handsome!&quot; one whispered, her voice quivering with disbelief.</p>

<p>&quot;Oh my god...&quot; another breathed, covering her mouth instinctively, as if even speaking aloud might shatter the spell of what they were seeing.</p>

<p>Ross had undergone a transformation that left even the most vivid imaginations pale.</p>

<p>Where once his features had been perfectly ordinary—pleasant, yes, but nothing to inspire awe—they now radiated a breathtaking, almost supernatural allure.</p>

<p>Every line of his face seemed sculpted with precision, every contour sharp yet harmonious.</p>

<p>His jawline could have been carved from marble, his lips perfectly balanced, his eyes glinting with a depth of intelligence, dominance, and charm that made onlookers shiver.</p>

<p>It was more than mere attractiveness. It was magnetism incarnate.</p>

<p>His presence alone demanded attention.</p>

<p>Even the most beautiful men in the world, taken together, would pale when set beside him.</p>

<p>He was no longer just a man—he was a standard by which all other men would be measured, and all would fall short.</p>

<p>The women in the hall flushed deeply, their faces warming with a combination of admiration, awe, and something closer to desire.</p>

<p>Some instinctively clutched their dress to their chests, others covered their mouths, unable to form coherent words.</p>

<p>Their breaths caught.</p>

<p>Hearts pounded violently.</p>

<p>Some even felt a strange, dizzying pull in their chests, as if his presence alone had stirred something deep within them.</p>

<p>Even Ross’s wives, standing nearby, were not immune.</p>

<p>Their eyes followed him with a mixture of pride, longing, and anticipation.</p>

<p>Subtle smiles curved their lips as their minds wandered, imagining the private moments that might unfold tonight.</p>

<p>Every nerve seemed alive, every heartbeat synchronized with the silent rhythm of Ross’s aura.</p>

<p>It wasn’t only his physical beauty that drew them in—it was the combination of power, confidence, and undeniable presence.</p>

<p>Every movement he made, every tilt of his head, seemed to command the space around him.</p>

<p>Even the air seemed to hum with recognition of his superiority.</p>

<p>The simplest gestures—straightening his posture, lifting his gaze—sent ripples through the crowd.</p>

<p>The sheer perfection of his face, the intensity of his eyes, the power that radiated from him—it was overwhelming.</p>

<p>Some women leaned forward, eyes wide, unable to look away.</p>

<p>Others whispered to one another, words lost in breathless gasps, trying and failing to articulate what they were witnessing.</p>

<p>Ross was no longer merely handsome.</p>

<p>He had become an ideal, a man so extraordinary that the notion of comparison seemed almost meaningless.</p>

<p>Ross held up his hand and called for a mirror.</p>

<p>One of his undead guards, silent and precise as ever, stepped forward and placed a polished, full-length mirror before him.</p>

<p>As Ross gazed at his reflection, he allowed himself a rare moment of appraisal.</p>

<p>What he saw confirmed everything the hall had already whispered.</p>

<p>His features were sharp and perfectly proportioned; his jawline strong and defined, his eyes gleaming with an almost magnetic intensity.</p>

<p>Every angle of his face seemed sculpted to perfection, his aura of power and confidence radiating outward even through the glass.</p>

<p>If he were to call himself the second most handsome man in the world, he knew without a doubt that no one would dare claim the first.</p>

<p>Not a single living man could hope to surpass him now.</p>

<p>Ross tilted his head slightly, studying his reflection, and a small, self-assured smile curved across his lips.</p>

<p>&quot;Not bad,&quot; he murmured to himself, the sound low and confident.</p>

<p>The effect on the crowd was immediate.</p>

<p>A soft chorus of audible moans and gasps of desire echoed through the women nearby, each reacting instinctively to the irresistible presence and striking beauty of the man before them.</p>

<p>Some covered their mouths in embarrassment, others couldn’t tear their eyes away, and a few simply swayed slightly, as if drawn forward by a magnetic force.</p>

<p>Ross’s smile widened just slightly, catching the reactions without breaking his calm composure.</p>

<p>He allowed himself the satisfaction, the quiet thrill of knowing exactly the effect he now had—not just in power, but in sheer, undeniable allure.</p>

<p>The mirror reflected more than just his face; it reflected the dominance, the confidence, and the charm that now made him untouchable in every sense.</p>

<p>And all around him, desire, admiration, and awe mingled in the air, filling the hall with an intoxicating energy that seemed to orbit him alone.</p>
