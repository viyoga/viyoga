<p>&quot;Semester break! Yes! I want to go on vacation! I’m so tired of school! I hope the school burns to the ground!&quot;</p>

<p>Jasmine shouted, her voice echoing through the halls as the final exams finally came to an end.</p>

<p>Many students overheard her outburst, but none disagreed.</p>

<p>Instead, they enthusiastically echoed her final sentence, their voices ringing out in unison.</p>

<p>This unexpected chorus brought a smile to Jasmine’s face, her frustration momentarily replaced with shared camaraderie.</p>

<p>Her frustration had been building for weeks. Late nights, endless cups of coffee, and the pressure to perform had left her drained.</p>

<p>But now, with the finish line finally behind her, she felt a wave of relief wash over her.</p>

<p>She had poured countless sleepless nights into studying, and though she was mentally and physically exhausted, she felt confident that she had passed all her courses.</p>

<p>Sophia, walking beside her, smiled at her friend’s outburst.</p>

<p>&quot;A vacation sounds amazing. So, where do you want to go, Jas?&quot; she asked casually.</p>

<p>The two had been inseparable for years, their friendship growing stronger with every challenge life threw at them.</p>

<p>Since becoming part of Ross’s world, their bond had only deepened.</p>

<p>They were more like sisters now, sharing secrets, dreams, and even mischief.</p>

<p>&quot;Anywhere but here!&quot; Jasmine said without hesitation, her face lighting up with a mischievous grin.</p>

<p>&quot;Maybe Las Vegas? Let’s gamble away all of Ross’s money.&quot; She laughed, the very idea sparking excitement.</p>

<p>The thought of Ross’s seemingly endless wealth made her bold.</p>

<p>She didn’t feel even a shred of guilt about the suggestion—if anything, the absurdity of it made her want to do it even more.</p>

<p>Sophia chuckled, her own excitement building.</p>

<p>&quot;Hmm… sounds like a solid plan. I could use some of that crazy Vegas energy right now,&quot; she replied, her eyes twinkling as she imagined the possibilities.</p>

<p>As the day wound down, the two couldn’t shake their excitement.</p>

<p>That evening, they decided to pitch the idea to Ross.</p>

<p>Jasmine and Sophia, always a dynamic duo, carefully plotted their approach, knowing that convincing him wouldn’t be hard—Ross loved indulging them.</p>

<p>When they finally brought it up, the group eagerly joined the discussion.</p>

<p>Destinations flew across the table—London, with its rich history and charm; Rome, a city of ancient wonders and romance; Dubai, an ultra-modern paradise of luxury; New York, the city that never sleeps; and, of course, Las Vegas, the playground of excess.</p>

<p>&quot;I think I want to go to Tokyo, Japan,&quot; Ross said, nodding confidently.</p>

<p>A wide grin lit up his face, and the gleam in his eyes was unmistakable.</p>

<p>&quot;I smell something fishy about that, Ross,&quot; Maya interjected, narrowing her eyes as she crossed her arms.</p>

<p>&quot;Are you planning to pick up a Japanese girlfriend and bring her back home with us?&quot; Her voice was sharp, tinged with suspicion.</p>

<p>Maya’s instincts as a private investigator were rarely wrong, and they were firing on all cylinders now.</p>

<p>&quot;Yup, Big Ross definitely has some naughty plan in mind,&quot; Natalie chimed in, her tone teasing as she leaned back and smirked.</p>

<p>She loved using her favorite nickname for him, especially when it added fuel to the fire.</p>

<p>&quot;One hundred percent—a new girl. This time, a Japanese goddess,&quot; April said, joining in with a knowing look.</p>

<p>It was almost like the women had developed a sixth sense for Ross’s antics.</p>

<p>Ross leaned back in his chair, grinning even wider. &quot;That wasn’t my plan at all, but now that you’ve brought it up… Hehehe…&quot; His chuckle was unmistakably mischievous, sending groans and eye rolls rippling through the room.</p>

<p>&quot;There’s no helping you, Ross,&quot; Jade said with a sigh, her displeasure evident as she frowned.</p>

<p>&quot;Aren’t we enough for you already?&quot; She gestured around the room.</p>

<p>Including Gwen, they were nine women strong, and she genuinely believed their unconventional family was big enough.</p>

<p>Jade didn’t mind sharing Ross—most of the time—but the idea of adding more women into the mix made her uneasy.</p>

<p>&quot;You’re more than enough for me, Jade,&quot; Ross replied warmly, his expression softening. He stood and walked over to her, wrapping her in a gentle hug before pressing a soft kiss to her lips.</p>

<p>&quot;I love all of you the same. Even if I had a million women, nothing would change. I’d still love you all as much as I do now. That’s a promise.&quot;</p>

<p>His words, filled with sincerity, seemed to calm Jade’s worries.</p>

<p>The tension in the room eased, and the other women watched silently, appreciating his effort to reassure them.</p>

<p>Ross knew better than anyone that his harem wasn’t just a collection of beautiful women—they were individuals with their own insecurities, emotions, and desires.</p>

<p>Their setup was far from perfect, but he was always ready to show his love and commitment when it mattered most.</p>

<p>&quot;Bah… You really have the sweetest mouth, Ross,&quot; Mary said, breaking the silence with a teasing grin.</p>

<p>&quot;No man can compare to you. Honestly, they’d die of shame at your shamelessness!&quot;</p>

<p>Her quip brought a burst of laughter from the group, the mood lifting instantly.</p>

<p>&quot;So… Japan it is, then,&quot; Althea said, her excitement bubbling to the surface.</p>

<p>&quot;I’d love to see the country again—it’s such a beautiful place. Nice choice, my love. Waku waku suru!&quot; Her enthusiasm was infectious, and the others quickly joined in.</p>

<p>&quot;Yes, please!&quot; Jasmine chimed, clapping her hands together.</p>

<p>&quot;Itadakimasu!&quot; Natalie added with a grin, throwing in the phrase playfully.</p>

<p>&quot;Baka!&quot; April teased, laughing as she said it.</p>

<p>&quot;Ohayoo gozaimas!&quot; Maya joined in, trying to mimic a formal tone but breaking into giggles halfway through.</p>

<p>&quot;Sayonara!&quot; Mary finished, waving dramatically like they were about to board a plane that second.</p>

<p>..</p>

<p>…</p>

<p>The room erupted in playful Japanese phrases and laughter as the group’s excitement for their upcoming trip grew.</p>

<p>While Ross might have started the conversation with a mischievous grin, he couldn’t help but feel a surge of warmth seeing his women so happy and united.</p>

<p>For him, that was the best part of these shared adventures—bringing them all closer, one wild trip at a time.</p>

<p>What they didn’t realize was that someone was about to throw a wrench into their carefully laid plans for the night.</p>
