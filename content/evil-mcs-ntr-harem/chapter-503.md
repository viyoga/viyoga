<p>Steven and Ross remained frozen, their eyes locked on the gun pointed at them.</p>

<p>Steven’s mind raced, searching for a way out, but the leader’s erratic movements made it clear that any wrong move could be their last.</p>

<p>Ross clenched his fists, his jaw tightening as he acted like he wanted to lunge forward.</p>

<p>But the odds were against them and he also did not want to expose his powers just yet.</p>

<p>The leader’s gaze shifted between Steven and Ross, his impatience growing by the second.</p>

<p>&quot;Where’s the key to your car? Throw it over here, now!&quot; he demanded, his voice sharp and unyielding.</p>

<p>Steven hesitated, his eyes flicking toward Ross.</p>

<p>They had no weapons, no backup, and no time to think.</p>

<p>The leader noticed the hesitation and snapped.</p>

<p>He swung his gun toward Adele, the barrel now aimed squarely at her head.</p>

<p>&quot;You think I’m joking?&quot; he sneered, his voice dripping with malice.</p>

<p>&quot;The keys, or she dies! And after her pretty head explodes like a ripe melon, you’ll watch me and my boys have our way with her!&quot;</p>

<p>Adele let out a choked sob, her hands trembling as she raised them in surrender.</p>

<p>Her eyes darted toward Steven, pleading silently for him to do something—anything.</p>

<p>Steven’s heart pounded in his chest, his mind screaming at him to act.</p>

<p>But what could he do?</p>

<p>The leader’s finger hovered over the trigger, and the other two men stood ready, their guns trained on Steven and Ross.</p>

<p>&quot;Stop! Don’t hurt her!&quot; Steven blurted out, his voice cracking under the weight of his fear.</p>

<p>He fumbled in his pocket, his hands shaking as he pulled out the car keys.</p>

<p>&quot;Here—take the keys! Just don’t hurt anyone, please!&quot;</p>

<p>He tossed the keys toward the leader, who caught them with a smirk.</p>

<p>But the gun didn’t waver from Adele’s head.</p>

<p>&quot;That’s a start,&quot; the leader said, his tone mocking.</p>

<p>&quot;Now you—sit down.&quot; The leader pointed at Steven, who was still standing, his body tense with fear.</p>

<p>Steven’s heart pounded as he glanced at his family, their faces pale with terror. He swallowed hard.</p>

<p>&quot;What’s this about? Just take our money and go. Please, just don’t hurt us,&quot; he tried to reason, his voice shaking.</p>

<p>The leader’s eyes darkened.</p>

<p>&quot;Did I ask you a question?&quot; he snapped.</p>

<p>&quot;One more word out of turn, and I’ll blow this woman’s head off! You hear me?&quot; He waved his gun toward Steven’s wife, who whimpered, clutching her daughters tightly.</p>

<p>Steven stiffened, his breath catching in his throat.</p>

<p>&quot;Okay, okay. Just don’t hurt my family,&quot; he pleaded, raising his hands in surrender before slowly lowering himself into a chair.</p>

<p>Without hesitation, the bad ugly men closed in, ropes in hand.</p>

<p>Their movements were swift and precise, looping the cords around Steven’s wrists and torso, securing him tightly to the folding chair.</p>

<p>The rough fibers bit into his skin as they pulled the knots taut, ensuring there was no chance of escape.</p>

<p>Ross, sitting beside him, received the same treatment.</p>

<p>The way they were tied—firm, unyielding—left no doubt that these men had done this before.</p>

<p>Many times.</p>

<p>The leader paced before them, his expression unreadable.</p>

<p>Then, with a smirk, he clapped his hands together.</p>

<p>&quot;Now,&quot; he said, his tone almost casual, &quot;let’s play a game.&quot;</p>

<p>Steven exchanged a wary glance with Ross.</p>

<p>&quot;What… what kind of game?&quot; he asked hesitantly.</p>

<p>The leader chuckled, tapping the side of his gun.</p>

<p>&quot;A simple one. You tell me your story. Who you are, what you do, who else might come looking for you. And don’t even think about lying.&quot; He gestured to one of his men, who stepped forward, cracking his knuckles.</p>

<p>&quot;We have ways of finding out the truth.&quot;</p>

<p>Steven’s stomach twisted. He knew they had no choice.</p>

<p>The questions started—sharp, relentless. Where did he work? How much money did he have?</p>

<p>Who else lived in the farm house? Who would notice if they went missing?</p>

<p>Minute by minute, the intruders extracted every detail—about Steven, his family, their routine.</p>

<p>And, of course, they didn’t forget to question the women also.</p>

<p>The leader leaned back, satisfied.</p>

<p>&quot;Well, well. That wasn’t so hard, was it?&quot;</p>

<p>Steven’s throat was dry.</p>

<p>His mind raced, trying to think of a way out, but with the cold steel of a gun looming over them, he knew one thing for certain.</p>

<p>This nightmare was just beginning.</p>

<p>The ugly leader sat back, satisfied with what he had learned.</p>

<p>He had always enjoyed the power that came with his position, the way fear twisted on his victims’ faces.</p>

<p>But today, he was especially eager.</p>

<p>There were four beautiful women under his control, and he intended to savor every moment before passing them along to his men.</p>

<p>He licked his lips, already imagining their screams—</p>

<p>Then his world shattered.</p>

<p>Poof!</p>

<p>His consciousness vanished in an instant, his brain liquefying like a candle melting under an open flame.</p>

<p>Yet, strangely, there was no pain. No sudden collapse.</p>

<p>No indication that anything had happened at all.</p>

<p>His body remained seated, his eyes still sharp, his expression unchanged.</p>

<p>Nobody noticed that the man who had been in control mere seconds ago was gone.</p>

<p>What remained was a perfect replica—a puppet bound to Ross’s will. The leader’s mind was intact.</p>

<p>He retained every thought, every memory, every ounce of his cruel and cunning nature.</p>

<p>But none of it mattered now. His desires, his ambitions, his will—everything belonged to Ross.</p>

<p>For a moment, his expression flickered, a split-second pause as new orders settled into place.</p>

<p>The gears of his mind turned, now fueled by a force beyond his understanding.</p>

<p>His lips parted as if to speak, then pressed together.</p>

<p>A second later, his features twisted, as if struck by sudden realization.</p>

<p>He turned to Ross, his lips curling into a wide, ugly grin.</p>

<p>&quot;Hey… I know you,&quot; he said, his voice laced with something new—something unnatural.</p>

<p>&quot;You’re Ross Oakley! The guy who’s been hoarding beautiful women left and right!&quot; the leader laughed, his voice dripping with amusement.</p>

<p>A dead puppet was really the perfect actor of all.</p>
