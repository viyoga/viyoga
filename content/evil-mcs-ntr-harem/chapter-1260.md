<p>When they finally emerged and reunited with the people waiting outside, Ross’s wives and women quickly noticed something strange.</p>

<p>The world around them appeared exactly as they had left it.</p>

<p>The sun was still in nearly the same position. The surroundings were unchanged.</p>

<p>Even the people outside looked as though they had barely moved since Ross had brought them away.</p>

<p>At first, they were confused.</p>

<p>Then realization slowly dawned on them.</p>

<p>&quot;Wait...&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;Something isn’t right.&quot;</p>

<p>One of the women looked around before speaking in disbelief.</p>

<p>&quot;How much time has passed?&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;Only one night,&quot; someone answered.</p>

<p>The response instantly stunned them.</p>

<p>&quot;What?&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;Only one night?&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;That’s impossible!&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;We spent an entire month with Ross!&quot;</p>

<p>The women exchanged shocked glances.</p>

<p>For a moment, they even wondered if they had heard incorrectly.</p>

<p>A month.</p>

<p>They had clearly spent a whole month with Ross.</p>

<p>During that time, Ross had taken them over and over again.</p>

<p>He made them come rivers of love juices.</p>

<p>Yet according to everyone outside, less than a single day had passed.</p>

<p>The realization left them speechless.</p>

<p>&quot;What kind of ability is this?&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;To think Ross can control time itself...&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;This is unbelievable.&quot;</p>

<p>Their beautiful eyes widened with amazement.</p>

<p>As powerful ability users themselves, they understood better than most just how terrifying such an ability truly was.</p>

<p>A month of training in the time it took others to experience a single night.</p>

<p>A month of preparation before a battle.</p>

<p>A month of recovery after an injury.</p>

<p>A month to learn new techniques, comprehend laws, or refine treasures.</p>

<p>The more they thought about it, the more absurd it seemed.</p>

<p>Such an ability could completely overturn the balance of power in any place.</p>

<p>Even ancient gods would go mad with greed if they learned of it.</p>

<p>The women immediately lowered their voices.</p>

<p>They gathered closer together, speaking only in whispers.</p>

<p>&quot;This secret cannot be revealed.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;Absolutely not.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;If our enemies discover this, they’ll stop at nothing to obtain it.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;They would mobilize entire armies.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;They might even target us to force Ross to reveal the secret.&quot;</p>

<p>Everyone nodded in agreement.</p>

<p>Although they knew Ross was powerful, there was no reason to create unnecessary trouble.</p>

<p>In a universe filled with ambition, greed, and endless conflict, secrets like this were worth more than any treasure.</p>

<p>The women silently made a vow.</p>

<p>No matter what happened, they would protect this secret.</p>

<p>Ross observed their reactions and couldn’t help but chuckle.</p>

<p>To him, their concern was understandable.</p>

<p>But it was also unnecessary.</p>

<p>The women were protecting what they believed to be one of his greatest trump cards.</p>

<p>In reality, it was merely a small convenience.</p>

<p>Ross had long surpassed the level where such things mattered.</p>

<p>If his enemies somehow learned the truth, what then?</p>

<p>Would they challenge him?</p>

<p>Would they try to seize his treasures?</p>

<p>Would they attempt to threaten the people he cared about?</p>

<p>Ross almost laughed at the thought.</p>

<p>Those people would soon discover the difference between imagination and reality.</p>

<p>After all, Ross was no longer simply a powerful cultivator.</p>

<p>He was practically the owner of the multiverse itself.</p>

<p>Countless worlds existed beneath his authority.</p>

<p>Endless dimensions were connected to his domain.</p>

<p>Entire realities could be created or destroyed with a single thought.</p>

<p>The flow of time.</p>

<p>The laws of space.</p>

<p>The foundations of existence itself.</p>

<p>All of these were things Ross could influence.</p>

<p>Compared to the powers he truly possessed, allowing a month to pass inside a realm while only one night passed outside was hardly worth mentioning.</p>

<p>It was like a wealthy king being praised for owning a single gold coin.</p>

<p>The gap was simply too large.</p>

<p>As Ross looked toward the sky, countless stars reflected in his eyes.</p>

<p>The women followed his gaze.</p>

<p>Although they could not fully comprehend the extent of his power, they could sense the boundless confidence surrounding him.</p>

<p>It was the confidence of a man who stood above all things.</p>

<p>A man who had conquered every obstacle before him.</p>

<p>A man who feared nothing.</p>

<p>Ross smiled softly.</p>

<p>No matter how chaotic the future became, no matter how many enemies appeared, none of it truly concerned him.</p>

<p>If danger came, he would destroy it.</p>

<p>If enemies appeared, he would crush them.</p>

<p>If entire universes stood against him, he would simply reshape reality itself.</p>

<p>That was the level he had reached.</p>

<p>The women stared at him, their hearts filled with admiration.</p>

<p>Some looked at him with love.</p>

<p>Others with devotion.</p>

<p>A few with outright worship.</p>

<p>Because the longer they remained by his side, the more they realized that Ross was unlike any being they had ever encountered.</p>

<p>He was not merely extraordinary.</p>

<p>He was a miracle walking among mortals.</p>

<p>And despite possessing power beyond imagination, he still treated them with affection, protected them from harm, and shared his life with them.</p>

<p>That was why they loved him so deeply.</p>

<p>As they stood together beneath the open sky, surrounded by friends and loved ones, a warm feeling spread through their hearts.</p>

<p>The future remained uncertain.</p>

<p>The universe remained chaotic.</p>

<p>Countless challenges undoubtedly awaited them.</p>

<p>Yet none of that mattered.</p>

<p>Because Ross was with them.</p>

<p>And as long as Ross stood beside them, they felt as though nothing in existence could ever truly threaten their happiness.</p>

<p>***</p>

<p>A few days quickly passed, and for the first time in a long while, there was relative peace within the nameless city.</p>

<p>The survivors continued rebuilding their lives while evolvers trained diligently, preparing themselves for whatever dangers the future might bring.</p>

<p>For many people, these peaceful days felt like a blessing.</p>

<p>Unfortunately, peace never lasted forever in the apocalypse.</p>

<p>One afternoon, an unexpected group arrived at Ross’s residence.</p>

<p>Their arrival immediately attracted attention.</p>

<p>More than a dozen men marched through the streets with confident expressions.</p>

<p>They wore combat gear stained by countless battles, and powerful energy fluctuations radiated from their bodies.</p>

<p>At first glance, anyone could tell they were not ordinary evolvers.</p>

<p>These were experts.</p>

<p>Veterans who had survived countless life-and-death struggles.</p>

<p>Leading the group was a middle-aged man with a scar running across his face.</p>
