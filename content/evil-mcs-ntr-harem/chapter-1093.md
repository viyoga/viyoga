<p>Capítulo 1093: Chapter 1093 Minimalist</p>

<p>“We both know… we don’t have any experience in something like this. I mean, how are we supposed to—”</p>

<p>Chelsea cut her off with a quiet, steadying hand on Lea’s shoulder.</p>

<p>“Shh,” she said softly, her eyes locking onto her sister’s.</p>

<p>“Trust me.” Her voice was calm, almost unnervingly so, but beneath it there was a spark of determination.</p>

<p>“Just follow my lead. That’s all. Karen already told us what Ross likes. We know enough to handle this.”</p>

<p>Lea’s stomach twisted. Her mind raced with doubts and fears—what if they failed?</p>

<p>What if they made a mistake?</p>

<p>The thought of letting Karen and Mom down made her feel like her chest was being squeezed.</p>

<p>She swallowed hard and nodded, though her hands shook slightly.</p>

<p>Chelsea, meanwhile, felt a thrill rising inside her. A plan was forming, precise and cunning.</p>

<p>Every detail she could remember from Karen’s words and her own observations was slotting into place.</p>

<p>She imagined how Ross might react, how to control the situation without giving themselves away.</p>

<p>There was a certain rush in knowing they had a chance to outsmart him—just a slim, precarious chance, but enough.</p>

<p>“Okay,” Lea murmured finally, forcing herself to take a deep breath. She glanced at Chelsea, searching for reassurance. “But… what if something goes wrong?”</p>

<p>Chelsea’s lips curved into a faint, confident smile. “Nothing’s going to go wrong,” she whispered. “Not if we stay calm, and just… do exactly what I say. We’re not rushing into this blindly. We have the advantage—we know what he wants. He thinks he’s in control, but right now, we are in control.”</p>

<p>Lea bit her lip, still nervous, but the tight knot of fear in her chest loosened slightly.</p>

<p>She let herself draw courage from her sister’s confidence, from the careful way Chelsea was planning every step.</p>

<p>She could almost feel Chelsea’s mind working, like a chess player several moves ahead.</p>

<p>Chelsea leaned in closer, her breath warm against Lea’s ear. “Focus on me, and nothing else. Watch my cues, follow my lead. That’s all we have to do.”</p>

<p>Lea nodded again, this time more firmly. Her heart still raced, but she could feel a small spark of determination growing.</p>

<p>They could do this. They had to do this. For Karen. For Mom. For themselves.</p>

<p>Chelsea’s eyes flicked toward Ross, lying there like a predator waiting for a sign, and the thrill of the challenge sent a shiver down her spine.</p>

<p>They were about to walk a dangerous line—but for once, she felt ready.</p>

<p>“Let’s do this,” Chelsea whispered, almost under her breath, a quiet promise to herself and to Lea. “Together.”</p>

<p>Lea’s hand brushed against hers, and for a brief, fleeting moment, they shared a silent pact—two sisters standing on the edge of a gamble, hearts pounding, minds sharp, ready to take control of a situation no one else thought they could.</p>

<p>The room seemed to shrink around them, the quiet almost deafening, as they readied themselves.</p>

<p>Every second stretched, heavy with anticipation.</p>

<p>And then, slowly, deliberately, Chelsea gave the smallest nod to Lea. It was time.</p>

<p>“Now… we want you to undress,” Chelsea said, her voice quiet but firm, carrying a weight that brooked no argument.</p>

<p>Her eyes never left Ross, and the air between them seemed to crackle with tension.</p>

<p>Ross’s lips curved into a slow, lazy smile.</p>

<p>“Can’t do it,” he said, his tone teasing, almost amused. “You told me not to move. And now this? Maybe you should plan your instructions a little better.” He leaned back on the bed, arms crossed loosely, utterly still, his confidence radiating in the quiet room.</p>

<p>Chelsea let out a long, exasperated sigh, the sound thick with frustration.</p>

<p>“Arghhh…” she muttered under her breath, then stepped closer to the bed, her movements deliberate. “Fine. I’ll do it myself.”</p>

<p>Lea hesitated, her feet frozen for a moment before she slowly followed, the nerves making her steps cautious.</p>

<p>Her hands fiddled at the hem of her shirt, twisting it nervously as she walked.</p>

<p>She glanced at Chelsea, searching for reassurance. The older sister’s eyes met hers, sharp and unyielding, and she felt a flicker of courage ignite in her chest.</p>

<p>Both sisters were dressed simply: jeans and fitted shirts, their usual refuge attire.</p>

<p>Practical, modest, safe. There were men around; this wasn’t a place to flaunt themselves.</p>

<p>Lust was the last thing on their minds—they had other priorities.</p>

<p>Yet, as they approached, they both felt the undeniable truth: their bodies were gifts in ways they had long struggled with.</p>

<p>Curves, subtle lines, grace in motion… these were the very traits that had made Ross target Karen and Marissa.</p>

<p>Beauty, they realized, could be a weapon and a curse all at once.</p>

<p>Chelsea’s hands moved with a mixture of hesitation and determination.</p>

<p>She reached for Ross’s belt, her fingers brushing against the cold metal buckle.</p>

<p>She fumbled briefly, and her heart thudded against her ribs, before she managed to undo it.</p>

<p>The pants followed, sliding down slowly as Ross remained motionless, watching.</p>

<p>Lea’s breath hitched.</p>

<p>She could feel the heat rising in her chest, a mixture of fear and anticipation curling in her stomach.</p>

<p>Chelsea’s fingers trembled slightly as she reached for the boxers, sliding them down with careful precision.</p>

<p>When they were finally removed, both sisters froze. Their eyes widened in simultaneous shock.</p>

<p>The reality of Ross’s form before them was far more imposing than either had expected.</p>

<p>Lea’s hands twitched at her sides, gripping the edge of the bed for support.</p>

<p>Chelsea’s lips parted, a barely audible gasp escaping her.</p>

<p>The room seemed impossibly still, every second stretched taut.</p>

<p>Even the faint rustle of fabric or the whisper of a breath sounded loud in the quiet space.</p>

<p>They exchanged a brief glance, silent communication passing between them. Disbelief.</p>

<p>Surprise. A quiet acknowledgment of the danger and the power in front of them.</p>

<p>Yet, beneath it all, there was a thread of resolve. They had chosen this.</p>

<p>They had decided to confront this. And somehow… they could do it.</p>
