<p>&quot;I’m sorry, Steve. I really am... but I don’t think I can go back.&quot;</p>

<p>Her words hung heavy in the air.</p>

<p>&quot;I’m with D now,&quot; she continued, glancing briefly at Ross, whose hand still rested calmly on her thigh.</p>

<p>&quot;And I’m happy with him. I didn’t expect this. I didn’t plan for it. But it happened, and I won’t lie to myself just to make someone else feel better.&quot;</p>

<p>She hesitated, her voice gentler now. &quot;Please... don’t stay here and torture yourself. Go back. Walk away before you hurt yourself even more.&quot;</p>

<p>For a moment, Steve didn’t move. He just stood there, frozen, his face unreadable.</p>

<p>Then, slowly, he took a long, deep breath.</p>

<p>His chest rose and fell as he swallowed whatever storm was building inside him.</p>

<p>He gave a single nod.</p>

<p>He didn’t say a word.</p>

<p>No curses. No threats. No final plea.</p>

<p>He simply turned around and walked out of the room—his footsteps echoing softly behind him, each one heavier than the last.</p>

<p>And just like that... he was gone.</p>

<p>The room remained silent for a few seconds longer, as if even the walls were processing what had just happened.</p>

<p>Then Ross let out a low, satisfied hum.</p>

<p>&quot;Well,&quot; he said with a grin, &quot;that went smoother than I expected.&quot;</p>

<p>***</p>

<p>Steve walked out of the house like a ghost. Cameras and reporters swarmed him, bombarding him with questions the moment he stepped outside.</p>

<p>&quot;Steve! What happened in there?&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;Did Heaven really choose Big D?&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;Are you leaving the show for good?&quot;</p>

<p>He barely registered their voices but he answered them as if nothing wrong was happening.</p>

<p>After that, he just kept moving forward, eyes glazed, like he was walking in a trance.</p>

<p>A few shallow, rehearsed responses left his mouth, but he couldn’t remember what he said or even who he said it to.</p>

<p>Everything felt distant—like someone else was speaking through him.</p>

<p>He got into his car and drove.</p>

<p>The city lights passed in a blur. Red. Green. Yellow.</p>

<p>He didn’t remember stopping at any lights. Didn’t remember turning onto his street.</p>

<p>All he knew was one moment he was on the road, and the next... he was home.</p>

<p>He stood in his front hallway for a long time, staring at nothing.</p>

<p>Then the weight of it all came crashing down on him like a tidal wave.</p>

<p>He clenched his fists, shaking his head slowly, trying to make sense of the fog in his mind.</p>

<p>&quot;Fuck,&quot; he hissed. &quot;What the hell just happened to me?&quot;</p>

<p>The last thing he remembered clearly was standing in front of Heaven, pouring his heart out—fake or not.</p>

<p>He remembered the words, her rejection, the way she looked at Ross, the way Ross touched her like she belonged to him.</p>

<p>He remembered rage—pure, unfiltered rage—boiling in his veins.</p>

<p>He was supposed to lose it. He wanted to lose it.</p>

<p>If Heaven had chosen Ross, then he had sworn to himself he’d stay.</p>

<p>He’d stay and make their lives miserable.</p>

<p>He’d be the ghost in the walls, the unwelcome shadow in their bedroom.</p>

<p>He would crash their private moments especially when they were fucking, taunt them, expose their weaknesses.</p>

<p>He would never let them have peace.</p>

<p>That had been his plan.</p>

<p>He was so sure of it.</p>

<p>So how the hell was he here now?</p>

<p>&quot;I... I didn’t leave,&quot; Steve whispered to himself. &quot;I wasn’t going to leave...&quot;</p>

<p>He stumbled into the living room, collapsing onto the couch as panic began to rise in his chest.</p>

<p>&quot;Did they make me leave? No... nobody touched me. I walked out on my own.&quot; His voice was shaking now.</p>

<p>&quot;But why? I didn’t want to. I didn’t want to!&quot;</p>

<p>He stood up again, pacing, like a caged animal.</p>

<p>His mind raced for answers, for anything that could explain what had happened.</p>

<p>Did Ross do something to him? Did someone hypnotize him? Drug him?</p>

<p>Was this some kind of sick joke orchestrated by the producers?</p>

<p>&quot;No,&quot; he growled. &quot;That doesn’t make sense. I was in control. I had to be.&quot;</p>

<p>But the longer he tried to convince himself, the more uncertain he became.</p>

<p>His thoughts spiraled—memories warping, twisting.</p>

<p>He couldn’t even recall walking to his car. Had he blacked out?</p>

<p>Had something else taken over?</p>

<p>He looked at his own reflection in the dark TV screen across the room. His face looked pale.</p>

<p>Hollow. Almost unfamiliar.</p>

<p>&quot;This isn’t me,&quot; he whispered again. &quot;I don’t just... give up.&quot;</p>

<p>A long silence filled the room.</p>

<p>And then, slowly, a terrifying thought crept into his mind, one he hadn’t dared to consider until now:</p>

<p>What if someone—or something—forced him to leave?</p>

<p>What if Ross had more than charm?</p>

<p>What if Ross wasn’t just some lucky guy with good looks and a smug attitude?</p>

<p>What if there was something darker about him—something that could control people?</p>

<p>Steve sat back down, his entire body trembling now. He’d gone in with a plan.</p>

<p>A cruel, deliberate, perfect plan. And instead, he’d walked out like a sheep.</p>

<p>Against his will.</p>

<p>And now, alone in his house, the silence was suffocating.</p>

<p>The more he thought about it, the more the sense of unease grew inside him like a sickness.</p>

<p>He wasn’t sure what scared him more—that he had lost control...</p>

<p>Or that someone else had taken it from him.</p>

<p>Steve sat on the edge of his bed, still dazed and confused.</p>

<p>His mind was a chaotic storm of broken thoughts and unanswered questions.</p>

<p>He tried to piece everything together, but nothing made sense.</p>

<p>It was like his memory had gaps—big ones—and the more he tried to think, the more his head throbbed.</p>

<p>He didn’t even notice the hulking figure that had silently appeared behind him.</p>

<p>By the time he sensed something was wrong, it was already too late.</p>

<p>&quot;PAK!&quot;</p>

<p>A heavy slap landed on the back of his head—loud, sharp, and powerful.</p>

<p>His vision went white, and then everything faded to black.</p>

<p>***</p>

<p>When Steve woke up, his head was pounding and his limbs refused to move.</p>
