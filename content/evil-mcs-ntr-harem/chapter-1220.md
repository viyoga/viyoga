<p>Ross could already imagine it: Jenny’s warm, soft body under his, her gasps and moans mingling with the sounds of the night.</p>

<p>The thought made a slow, confident smile creep across his face.</p>

<p>But for now, he would play it cool, charm her, make her feel intrigued and safe.</p>

<p>The chase was as much a part of the thrill as the victory itself.</p>

<p>Tonight wasn’t over, and he didn’t intend to let this moment slip away.</p>

<p>Ross talked with Jenny for hours, their conversation flowing effortlessly despite the chaos and noise of the crowded safe zone.</p>

<p>They laughed at small jokes, shared cautious smiles, and even debated a few points, though the underlying tension between them was undeniable.</p>

<p>By the time Ross finally stood and left, the clock was nearing midnight, and Jenny was left alone with her thoughts.</p>

<p>Almost immediately, her friends swarmed her, buzzing around like excited bees.</p>

<p>&quot;Jenny! That’s Ross! He’s a freaking powerhouse!&quot; one of them gushed, practically bouncing in excitement.</p>

<p>&quot;You should have gone to his room! Can you believe it?!&quot; another exclaimed, grabbing her shoulder.</p>

<p>&quot;What were you even thinking, Jenny?! Don’t let an opportunity like this slip through your fingers!&quot; a third cried, eyes wide, voice trembling with excitement and disbelief.</p>

<p>Jenny could barely keep up with the barrage of advice.</p>

<p>She wanted to laugh at the absurdity of it, yet at the same time, she felt a pang of exasperation.</p>

<p>They acted like she had just lost the chance of her life—but this wasn’t some ordinary world.</p>

<p>This was a world overrun by zombies, unpredictable and merciless.</p>

<p>Every day was a gamble, and survival was never guaranteed.</p>

<p>For Jenny, love had been a luxury she couldn’t afford... not after losing her husband just months ago.</p>

<p>Her friends didn’t know, of course.</p>

<p>They didn’t know the depths of her loss, the nights she spent awake haunted by memories, the fear that letting herself care again might be a betrayal to the man she had loved and lost.</p>

<p>To them, she was just a single woman faced with a rare, powerful, and undeniably attractive man.</p>

<p>Yet Ross, unconcerned by the rejection or the noise around her, returned the next day.</p>

<p>And the day after that.</p>

<p>Each time, he sought her out, sat beside her, and spoke with the same warmth, ease, and subtle charm that had drawn her in on the first night.</p>

<p>Slowly, he began to uncover the walls she had built around herself—not with force, but with quiet persistence, patience, and understanding.</p>

<p>He noticed the hesitation in her eyes whenever someone mentioned love.</p>

<p>He saw the flinch in her voice when he joked about relationships.</p>

<p>And he understood that she wasn’t rejecting him out of disinterest—she was protecting a fragile heart that had already been broken once.</p>

<p>For Jenny, it was a confusing mixture of fear and longing.</p>

<p>The world around her was harsh and unforgiving, where death lurked in every shadow, and yet Ross made her feel something almost impossible to articulate.</p>

<p>He made her feel safe, even when he was bold and direct. He made her laugh, made her forget, if only for a moment, the constant threat of the apocalypse.</p>

<p>And, most dangerously, he made her heart ache in a way she hadn’t thought possible again.</p>

<p>Days turned into a week, and the tension built, each encounter peeling away another layer of her defenses.</p>

<p>Every smile, every touch, every conversation drew her closer, but still, she hesitated.</p>

<p>Love was a risk she wasn’t sure she could take—not in this world, not with her history of loss.</p>

<p>Yet Ross never pushed too hard.</p>

<p>He respected her boundaries, even as he tested them gently, guiding her toward desire without forcing it.</p>

<p>He understood that the chase was part of the thrill, that the anticipation could be as intoxicating as the act itself.</p>

<p>Finally, after seven long days of quiet persistence, laughter, and fleeting glances charged with meaning, Jenny felt the walls she had built around her heart begin to crumble.</p>

<p>One evening, after another long conversation filled with laughter and quiet confessions, she looked up at him, eyes bright with vulnerability and courage.</p>

<p>&quot;Yes... Ross,&quot; she whispered, the words trembling but firm.</p>

<p>And just like that, she allowed herself to take the step she had feared for so long.</p>

<p>She followed him to his room, heart racing, mind alive with the thrill of possibility.</p>

<p>In a world where life could end at any moment, she was taking a risk—but this time, it was a risk she chose for herself.</p>

<p>For the first time in months, maybe years, Jenny felt something she hadn’t felt since before the apocalypse: hope, desire, and the tentative stirrings of love.</p>

<p>Ross had broken through her walls, not with force, but with persistence, charm, and the rare ability to make her feel alive again.</p>

<p>Of course, Jenny had no intention of just throwing herself at Ross. That was not part of her plan at all.</p>

<p>She had come simply out of curiosity, wanting to see what his room looked like—and what she found surprised her.</p>

<p>&quot;It’s... so big!&quot; she gasped as she stepped inside, her eyes widening.</p>

<p>The space was far larger than she had expected. A huge TV dominated one wall, a fully equipped kitchen gleamed in the corner, and to her amazement, there were even two bathrooms.</p>

<p>Jenny had never imagined that such a spacious and well-furnished place could exist behind the unassuming door she had just passed through.</p>

<p>Ross, of course, had adjusted the place with his reality-bending powers, something only he knew.</p>

<p>To anyone else, it would have seemed ordinary, but nobody else could feel the subtle hints of power and precision woven into every detail.</p>

<p>&quot;It’s okay, come on. I prepared something,&quot; Ross said casually, a small, confident smile playing on his lips.</p>

<p>He led her toward a table that was laid out beautifully, filled with a variety of dishes that made her heart skip a beat.</p>

<p>Every single one was her favorite food, arranged as if he had spent hours thinking about what would make her happy.</p>
