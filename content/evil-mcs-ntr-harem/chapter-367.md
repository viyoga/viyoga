<p>Three days later, Rose found herself standing amidst the lively crowd gathered at Ross&#x27;s grand estate. The air buzzed with excitement, the atmosphere festive and full of cheer.</p>

<p>December 28 marked Ross&#x27;s birthday, and he had chosen to celebrate it in the most extraordinary way—by giving away millions of dollars to people he knew and even those he didn&#x27;t.</p>

<p>Ross had explained his reasoning to her the night before: &quot;What&#x27;s the point of having so much if I can&#x27;t share it? Birthdays are meant to spread joy, and I have enough to make a difference in so many lives.&quot;</p>

<p>It was a side of Ross that Rose hadn&#x27;t fully seen before, and it left her both intrigued and deeply moved.</p>

<p>&quot;NEXT!&quot; boomed a voice from the crowd. It was Ross&#x27;s neighbor, a burly man who seemed to take great pride in managing the line.</p>

<p>&quot;Geneve Nestor! Friend of Ross from high school, 1 million dollars!&quot; he announced.</p>

<p>The crowd erupted into cheers as Geneve stepped forward, eyes wide with disbelief as she accepted her check.</p>

<p>&quot;NEXT!&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;Enrico Hill! Schoolmate of Ross from first grade, 500,000 dollars!&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;Edgar Buena! Stranger, 100,000 dollars!&quot;</p>

<p>The announcements continued, one name after another, as Ross&#x27;s generosity touched countless lives.</p>

<p>Some recipients were old friends, while others were complete strangers. Ross made no distinction; his goal was simply to spread as much happiness as possible.</p>

<p>Rose watched it all with a sense of wonder. The sheer scale of the event was overwhelming, with hundreds of people gathered in an orderly queue that stretched out of sight.</p>

<p>Ross had spared no expense, hiring a team of bodyguards to ensure everything ran smoothly. Any attempts at theft or disruption were swiftly and silently dealt with.</p>

<p>Ross&#x27;s rules were clear: no one took what was his without facing the consequences.</p>

<p>As the day progressed, Rose couldn&#x27;t help but feel a deep respect for Ross. His generosity wasn&#x27;t just about money—it was about creating moments of joy and hope in the lives of others.</p>

<p>It was a birthday celebration unlike any other, and it made her realize just how unique Ross truly was.</p>

<p>By evening, the crowd had thinned, though the event showed no signs of slowing down.</p>

<p>Rose found herself seated with a group of Ross&#x27;s wives and other women who had gathered to celebrate his special day.</p>

<p>&quot;So, will you come with us back to Parkland City after this?&quot; one of the women asked, her tone light but curious.</p>

<p>Rose hesitated, glancing around the group. She had met most of them during one of Ross&#x27;s infamous parties—a night of wild debauchery that had left her blushing for days.</p>

<p>They all radiated confidence, their flawless bodies and poised demeanors a testament to the unique world they shared with Ross.</p>

<p>&quot;I don&#x27;t think so,&quot; Rose replied after a moment.</p>

<p>&quot;My family is here, and my work keeps me busy, so I&#x27;ll probably stay.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;Oh, really?&quot; another woman teased, raising an eyebrow with a sly smile.</p>

<p>&quot;I didn&#x27;t think Ross would be so trusting as to leave you here all alone.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;Nope,&quot; a third chimed in with a knowing laugh.</p>

<p>&quot;Ross will definitely assign a bodyguard to watch over her. You know how protective he is.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;Agreed,&quot; said another, shaking her head with a grin.</p>

<p>&quot;We all know Ross can be a bit… possessive at times.&quot;</p>

<p>Their laughter was infectious, and Rose couldn&#x27;t help but join in. She had already noticed Ross&#x27;s fiercely protective nature. If he suspected someone might try to hurt or betray him, he wouldn&#x27;t hesitate to act.</p>

<p>Still, the thought of having a bodyguard shadowing her every move was a little daunting.</p>

<p>&quot;We&#x27;ll see,&quot; she said with a small smile, trying to brush off her nervousness.</p>

<p>As the conversation continued, Rose reflected on how much her life had changed in such a short time.</p>

<p>Her breakup with Edward, which once seemed so significant, now felt like a minor hiccup compared to the whirlwind world she had entered with Ross.</p>

<p>Looking around at the joy and celebration surrounding her, Rose realized something else: despite her initial reservations, she felt at home here.</p>

<p>For the first time in years, she was part of something bigger—a world where passion, generosity, and loyalty intertwined in ways she had never imagined.</p>

<p>As the festivities continued late into the night, Ross approached her, his expression warm but tired.</p>

<p>&quot;Enjoying yourself?&quot; he asked, his voice soft but filled with genuine curiosity.</p>

<p>Rose smiled, meeting his gaze.</p>

<p>&quot;More than I thought I would. You&#x27;ve given so much today, Ross. It&#x27;s inspiring.&quot;</p>

<p>He shrugged, a playful grin tugging at his lips.</p>

<p>&quot;It&#x27;s my birthday. What better way to celebrate than by sharing what I have?&quot;</p>

<p>And with that, the night carried on—a celebration of life, love, and the boundless generosity of a man who had everything yet chose to give so much.</p>

<p>One week later, Ross and his companions returned to Parkland City. The bustling metropolis welcomed them with open arms as Ross prepared to begin his second year in college.</p>

<p>Despite his extraordinary nature, he found solace in these mundane pursuits, deciding to embrace his time in the mortal world one step at a time.</p>

<p>For now, he was content to enjoy the life he had built and the people around him.</p>

<p>Meanwhile, back in Mirfield Town, Rose sat by her bedroom window, staring at the dark, starless sky above. A deep sigh escaped her lips, her thoughts lingering on Ross.</p>

<p>&quot;I miss him already,&quot; she whispered, her voice tinged with longing.</p>

<p>&quot;Who are you missing?&quot;</p>

<p>The unexpected voice startled her, sending a shiver down her spine. She spun around, her heart racing—and froze in disbelief.</p>

<p>&quot;Ross?&quot; she gasped, her eyes widening.</p>

<p>&quot;How are you here? I saw you leave earlier!&quot;</p>

<p>Ross smirked, stepping closer as she threw her arms around him in a tight embrace.</p>

<p>&quot;Oh? Did you forget my nickname already?&quot; he teased, his tone playful as his hands slid down to cup her soft, curvy backside.</p>

<p>Rose couldn&#x27;t help but smile at his familiar touch.</p>

<p>&quot;Miracle Maker,&quot; she murmured, shaking her head. For now, she didn&#x27;t care about the impossibility of his presence or how he managed to be in two places at once. Those questions could wait.</p>

<p>All that mattered was the heat that ignited between them as Ross pulled her closer, his lips brushing against hers.</p>

<p>His presence was intoxicating, making her forget everything except the way he made her feel.</p>

<p>Before long, the quiet of the night was replaced by the sound of their shared passion, their sweet moans echoing softly through the room.</p>

<p>For Rose, it was a moment of pure bliss, a reminder that Ross had a way of always being exactly where he was needed—whether it defied logic or not.</p>
