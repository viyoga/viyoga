<p>The zombie apocalypse had raged for two full years, an unrelenting storm of death and chaos that left the world barely recognizable.</p>

<p>Cities lay in ruins, their skyscrapers crumbling like brittle bones, streets littered with the abandoned remnants of humanity’s former life.</p>

<p>From the eight billion people who had once walked the Earth, only a few thousand remained, scattered across desolate lands, clinging to life in pockets of ruin.</p>

<p>Although the Evolved humans were present, their arrival did little to change the outcome.</p>

<p>They had always been the elite, the next stage of humanity’s desperate struggle against the undead, possessing abilities and strength far beyond ordinary survivors.</p>

<p>Yet even their impressive powers were no match for what they faced now.</p>

<p>The dead walkers had grown stronger, faster, and more relentless than anything the Evolved had encountered before.</p>

<p>Each step they took seemed to carry a horrifying weight, and their movements were coordinated with a terrifying precision.</p>

<p>In an instant, what should have been a manageable battle became a massacre.</p>

<p>The Evolved fought valiantly at first, unleashing their abilities with calculated precision and devastating force.</p>

<p>But the sheer numbers and newfound power of the dead walkers were overwhelming.</p>

<p>One by one, the Evolved fell, consumed by the unending tide of relentless, gnawing horrors.</p>

<p>What had been their advantage—their speed, strength, and evolved skills—was now irrelevant.</p>

<p>The undead had surpassed them, turning their once-feared adversaries into little more than helpless prey.</p>

<p>Within moments, the battlefield all over the world was littered with the fallen.</p>

<p>The Evolved, those who had once been humanity’s hope, became nothing more than zombie food, their screams swallowed by the unending moans of the dead.</p>

<p>Fear, despair, and hunger had hollowed them out, stripping away their hope until it seemed impossible for them to recover.</p>

<p>Every day was a fight, not just against the ravenous undead, but against the creeping hopelessness that gnawed at their minds.</p>

<p>Even the base that Ross had established, with Base Commander June at the helm, had not been immune to disaster.</p>

<p>Over the course of the ongoing chaos, it had been breached multiple times, each assault tearing through defenses and leaving destruction in its wake.</p>

<p>What had once been a thriving population of over 300,000 people had been reduced to a mere few hundred survivors, scattered and traumatized.</p>

<p>The base, once a symbol of security and strength, now stood as a shadow of its former self, its walls scarred and its halls silent except for the groans of the wounded.</p>

<p>Ross, however, paid little attention to the sheer scale of death around him.</p>

<p>His concern extended only to those who belonged to his personal harem, such as Bella and June, ensuring their safety above all else.</p>

<p>Aegis’s stronghold remained unbreached, as did his supermarket base, keeping all of his loved ones safe and sound.</p>

<p>Everyone else—soldiers, civilians, staff—was, in his eyes, nothing more than expendable flesh, a tool to be used and discarded at will.</p>

<p>Their lives and deaths held no real weight for him; yet he reveled in the illusion of concern, playing the role of a caring leader for anyone who might be watching.</p>

<p>Then, exactly two years after the zombie apocalypse began, the unimaginable occurred.</p>

<p>The sun, once a steadfast beacon in the sky, began to dim.</p>

<p>First it dulled to a pale yellow, then a sickly orange, until it finally disappeared entirely.</p>

<p>The world was plunged into total darkness, an oppressive, suffocating black that swallowed everything.</p>

<p>Shadows merged into shadows, and even the faintest glimmer of light was gone.</p>

<p>The wails of humanity rose in chorus, sharp and agonizing, echoing across empty cities and barren wastelands.</p>

<p>The sound was a lamentation of a species at the brink, a mournful reminder of the fragility of life.</p>

<p>&quot;It has finally arrived,&quot; Cyrus Thorn murmured, his eyes scanning the blackened sky.</p>

<p>He remained in a lotus position, his body still and composed, yet his mind simmered with anticipation.</p>

<p>Despite his earlier failure to remove the bindings that shackled his soul, he felt no fear.</p>

<p>None of that mattered now.</p>

<p>The world itself had reached a breaking point, a moment of complete vulnerability.</p>

<p>Cyrus’s gaze lingered on the horizon, where darkness stretched endlessly.</p>

<p>The air was thick with tension, and the silence between the cries of the few survivors seemed almost alive.</p>

<p>Everything had been reduced to its most basic elements: survival, fear, and power.</p>

<p>The apocalypse had stripped the world bare.</p>

<p>Two years of suffering, death, and decay had left it hollowed, weak, and desperate.</p>

<p>The undead roamed, yes, but even they were little more than instruments of chaos compared to what was coming.</p>

<p>The stage was set. The time had come.</p>

<p>A heavy, almost suffocating silence hung over the land, stretching out for what felt like an eternity.</p>

<p>The few remaining humans huddled in fear, their eyes scanning the darkness, their hearts pounding with dread.</p>

<p>And then, from the east, the stillness was shattered by a deafening, ear-splitting sound.</p>

<p>Bang!</p>

<p>The ground trembled under the force of the impact, sending dust and debris swirling into the air.</p>

<p>From the horizon emerged a colossal flying boat, a vessel so immense that it seemed to consume the skyline.</p>

<p>Its length stretched at least three thousand feet, and its breadth rivaled entire city blocks, making even the largest structures around it look insignificant.</p>

<p>Every surface of the boat gleamed with a golden radiance, a dazzling light that cut through the shadows and illuminated the devastation below.</p>

<p>For a few heartbeats, that brilliant glow was the only source of illumination, casting long, sharp shadows and making every detail of the surrounding ruins visible.</p>

<p>The sight alone was enough to leave onlookers paralyzed with awe.</p>

<p>Its sheer scale spoke of unimaginable power and advanced technology, a craft designed not merely to travel, but to dominate.</p>

<p>Even the undead, restless and relentless as they were, seemed to hesitate at the presence of such an overwhelming force.</p>

<p>For a brief moment, the world felt suspended, caught between fear and wonder.</p>

<p>Every eye turned toward the glowing behemoth, every heart filled with questions and dread.</p>

<p>And then, as if the spectacle itself were not enough, a new presence emerged on the horizon, more ominous, more threatening than anything seen before.</p>

<p>Boom!</p>

<p>A deafening sound tore through the air, and another massive behemoth appeared, approaching from the opposite direction.</p>
