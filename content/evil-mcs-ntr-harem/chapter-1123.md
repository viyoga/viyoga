<p>For a moment, the room froze. Forks hovered mid-air. Plates rattled softly against the table.</p>

<p>The women stared at him, their eyes wide in shock.</p>

<p>&quot;Wait... what?&quot; one of them whispered, uncertain if she had heard him correctly.</p>

<p>&quot;Did... did he just say he’s going to save them?&quot; another asked, her voice trembling.</p>

<p>Ross simply nodded, his expression unreadable.</p>

<p>There was no hesitation, no doubt in his voice, just quiet certainty.</p>

<p>The women’s hearts skipped a beat—they had expected safety, maybe guidance, but not this.</p>

<p>Not a direct plan to march into danger, to rescue the people they had thought were beyond saving.</p>

<p>Confusion, disbelief, and a flicker of hope mingled in their minds, leaving them silent, staring at Ross as he leaned back in his chair, calm, composed, and ready for what would come next.</p>

<p>In Ross’s case, there was going to be absolutely no downside to what he was about to do.</p>

<p>Not a single negative consequence would reach him.</p>

<p>His real identity as Ross Oakley—Parkland City’s infamous one-man superstar—was safely hidden behind a flawless disguise.</p>

<p>With the heart stone buff, he had gained epic features into the face of the most impossibly handsome man alive, a face so breathtaking that even the most beautiful women found themselves speechless when they looked at him.</p>

<p>There was no risk of being recognized. No trail. No proof.</p>

<p>And beyond all that, Ross didn’t need to stand in the spotlight to make things happen.</p>

<p>If he wished, he could command armies from behind the scenes, guiding events from the shadows like a puppeteer.</p>

<p>He could raze a city and people would still argue over whether he had ever even been there.</p>

<p>Compared to that, sneaking out to have a little fun without the women in Aegis knowing was effortless.</p>

<p>Aegis itself was comfortable—luxurious even—with enough soft beds, warm meals, and affectionate company to make any man retire early.</p>

<p>But the truth was simple: the &quot;selection&quot; inside Aegis had grown small.</p>

<p>Most of the women living within its walls were already his wives, loyal, fierce, and utterly devoted to him.</p>

<p>Their affection warmed him, but it also meant that whenever he wanted to feel the thrill of something new, he had to step outside his fortress.</p>

<p>The group of women he had just rescued—or rather, stumbled upon by chance—stared at him with wide eyes as he spoke.</p>

<p>They were all beautiful in their own ways, some delicate, some fiery, some bearing the signs of hardship from the world outside.</p>

<p>Their faces were a mix of awe, disbelief, and a fragile kind of hope that had been nearly extinguished before Ross appeared in their lives.</p>

<p>To Ross, their stunned reactions were amusing.</p>

<p>He smiled, the expression slow and confident, the kind that made hearts skip without permission.</p>

<p>&quot;You heard me right,&quot; Ross said, his voice deep and steady.</p>

<p>&quot;I can’t stand aside and do nothing when I see people suffering. Not when I have the strength to change it.&quot;</p>

<p>A hush fell over the room. Even the sound of the wind outside seemed to pause.</p>

<p>&quot;In one hour,&quot; Ross continued, &quot;we’re heading out. I’m going to liberate your friends from those toxic maggots who’ve been tormenting them.&quot;</p>

<p>The statement hit them like a bolt of lightning.</p>

<p>Gasps. Soft whispers.</p>

<p>Hands covering trembling lips. Some of the women even looked as if their knees were about to give out.</p>

<p>Their friends—taken, abused, trapped under the rule of scum who used cruelty as currency.</p>

<p>They had prayed for a miracle, but none expected their miracle to arrive in the form of a man so handsome it felt unreal.</p>

<p>Not a single one of them objected. Why would they?</p>

<p>Their hearts were burning with a desire for vengeance, for justice, for the chance to reclaim pieces of themselves they feared were already lost.</p>

<p>And if anyone could make the impossible possible, it was Ross—this mysterious, overwhelmingly powerful stranger who had appeared like fate itself.</p>

<p>But even as excitement surged, a silent question hung in the minds of every woman present:</p>

<p>How?</p>

<p>How was one man—no matter how strong, how handsome, or how confident—supposed to take on an entire camp of dangerous scum?</p>

<p>How could he hope to free their friends without losing his life?</p>

<p>Ross felt all those questions hanging in the air. He could practically taste their uncertainty.</p>

<p>And yet, the smile on his face only widened.</p>

<p>Because Ross Oakley already had the answer.</p>

<p>And when he acted... the world itself trembled.</p>

<p>More than that, Ross knew that he was going to have his fun.</p>

<p>***</p>

<p>An hour later, preparations were complete.</p>

<p>The sun hung low, casting long orange beams across the cracked pavement as everyone gathered around the large van Ross had chosen.</p>

<p>It was an old, heavy-duty model—military-grade in its prime—its metal frame reinforced and its interior surprisingly spacious.</p>

<p>Ten people could fit inside comfortably, and today it carried five anxious women who had lived through more horror than most could imagine.</p>

<p>They sat clustered together, stealing glances toward the front where Ross sat behind the wheel.</p>

<p>His posture was relaxed, one arm draped over the driver’s seat, fingers drumming rhythmically.</p>

<p>He looked like a man about to take a simple evening drive, not someone preparing to storm a dangerous camp overflowing with criminals and monsters.</p>

<p>But outside the vehicle, tension simmered.</p>

<p>Miku and Ace stood beside the sliding door, their faces inches apart, locked in a heated argument that had been building for the past ten minutes.</p>

<p>&quot;I really don’t think we should go, Miku. This is dangerous,&quot; Ace whispered harshly, fear twisting her words.</p>

<p>She kept glancing over her shoulder, as if expecting a zombie to emerge from behind a rusted car at any moment.</p>

<p>Miku crossed her arms, her stance stiff and unyielding.</p>

<p>&quot;I want to go, Ace. This is important. You heard what those women said—what those monsters did. I can’t stay behind knowing that.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;You’re not thinking clearly!&quot; Ace shot back, her voice cracking.</p>

<p>&quot;What can you even do out there? We don’t have powers like Ross. We’re just regular people with no abilities whatsoever!&quot;</p>
