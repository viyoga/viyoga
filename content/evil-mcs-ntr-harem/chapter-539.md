<p>The days passed, but Brandon never returned.</p>

<p>The massive sum of cash remained untouched in a room adjacent to his office, sitting there as if waiting for someone to claim it.</p>

<p>Strangely, the room had no lock—not even the simplest of security measures.</p>

<p>Only Brandon had the keys, and no one else seemed to have access.</p>

<p>More baffling was the fact that no one questioned the money&#x27;s presence, nor did anyone inquire about its whereabouts.</p>

<p>It was as if it had been completely forgotten.</p>

<p>Ted, however, couldn&#x27;t shake the unease growing inside him.</p>

<p>He knew that the superstore was under constant surveillance, with cameras installed in nearly every corner of the building.</p>

<p>There should have been footage of everything—who entered, who left, and most importantly, what happened to the money.</p>

<p>Yet, something about the situation felt off.</p>

<p>Deciding to trust his instincts, he made his way to the security room once more, hoping to find answers.</p>

<p>What he discovered, however, left him stunned.</p>

<p>&quot;Where did they go?&quot; Ted muttered under his breath, his pulse quickening.</p>

<p>His eyes scanned the screens, but no matter how many times he checked, the result was the same—there were no security cameras in Brandon&#x27;s office or the side room where the hundreds of millions of dollars had been stored.</p>

<p>That wasn&#x27;t normal. The security system covered every other part of the superstore, including storage areas far less significant than this one.</p>

<p>So why had these particular locations been left unmonitored?</p>

<p>His mind raced as he tried to piece together the reasoning behind such a glaring security gap.</p>

<p>Then it hit him.</p>

<p>&quot;Of course,&quot; he thought, his hands clenching into fists.</p>

<p>&quot;Ross wanted to hide the money. He made sure there was no trace of its existence whatsoever.&quot;</p>

<p>The realization sent a chill down Ted&#x27;s spine. This wasn&#x27;t just negligence—this was intentional.</p>

<p>Someone had gone through great lengths to ensure that no one, not even the security team, would ever find out what really happened in that room.</p>

<p>&quot;Millions of dollars in dirty money…&quot; Ted muttered, staring at the stacks of cash before him.</p>

<p>His heart pounded, and his hands trembled slightly.</p>

<p>The sheer amount of money sitting in that unguarded room was mind-boggling.</p>

<p>It would be so easy—just one quick grab, and his life would change forever.</p>

<p>But he clenched his fists, shaking his head.</p>

<p>&quot;No, that&#x27;s not me,&quot; he told himself firmly. He refused to be that kind of person.</p>

<p>He had always prided himself on being honest, hardworking, and faithful to his principles.</p>

<p>With renewed determination, he turned away from the tempting sight and forced himself to focus on his work for the rest of the day.</p>

<p>He threw himself into his tasks, trying to drown out the thoughts clawing at his mind.</p>

<p>Yet, no matter how hard he worked, no matter how many reports he completed or meetings he attended, the image of that money never fully left him.</p>

<p>It lingered in the back of his mind like a whisper, nudging him, tempting him.</p>

<p>That night, when he went home, the unease followed him.</p>

<p>He climbed into bed beside his wife, Grace, feeling more exhausted than ever.</p>

<p>She was beautiful, kind, and patient—always supporting him no matter how stressful his days were.</p>

<p>But tonight, as he lay beside her, he felt a heavy weight pressing down on him.</p>

<p>&quot;I&#x27;m sorry, love,&quot; he whispered, staring at the ceiling.</p>

<p>&quot;I don&#x27;t know what&#x27;s wrong with me. Maybe I should see a doctor tomorrow.&quot;</p>

<p>A week passed, yet the problem remained.</p>

<p>No matter how much he tried, he couldn&#x27;t bring himself to be intimate with Grace.</p>

<p>He avoided her touch, dodged her concerned questions, and buried himself in work to distract himself.</p>

<p>But deep down, he knew the real issue. It wasn&#x27;t exhaustion. It wasn&#x27;t stress.</p>

<p>It was the money and something more insidious.</p>

<p>It had taken root in his mind like an obsession.</p>

<p>&quot;Is this because of stress at work? Am I pushing myself too hard?&quot; he asked himself, grasping for any excuse other than the truth.</p>

<p>Before he even realized what he was doing, he found himself standing in front of that room again.</p>

<p>His pulse quickened as he turned the handle and stepped inside.</p>

<p>The money was still there.</p>

<p>Stacks upon stacks of crisp hundred-dollar bills, untouched, unclaimed.</p>

<p>A fortune beyond anything he had ever seen in his life.</p>

<p>He let out a shaky breath. Part of him had expected it to be gone, taken by someone else who had the same thoughts running through their mind.</p>

<p>But no—it was still there, waiting.</p>

<p>His conscience screamed at him to walk away. To quit the company.</p>

<p>To call the authorities and expose Ross Oakley&#x27;s massive money-laundering operation.</p>

<p>But another voice—quieter, yet far more persuasive—whispered something else.</p>

<p>&quot;Before I do that… should I at least take some? Just a little? Nobody will know, right?&quot;</p>

<p>His hands twitched at his sides.</p>

<p>He could take a small amount, just enough to make a difference in his life.</p>

<p>Just enough to pay off some debts, secure his family&#x27;s future, maybe even give Grace the life she deserved.</p>

<p>It wasn&#x27;t like he would take all of it. It wasn&#x27;t like anyone would even notice a few stacks missing.</p>

<p>Before he could talk himself out of it, he reached forward, grabbing a thick bundle of bills.</p>

<p>It was heavy in his hands, the weight of temptation pressing down on him.</p>

<p>By his estimate, the stack alone was worth a million dollars.</p>

<p>His breath hitched as he shoved it into his bag.</p>

<p>A cold sweat ran down his back as he left the room, his heart hammering in his chest.</p>

<p>Every step felt like it could be his last. He expected alarms to blare, security guards to grab him, Ross himself to storm in and demand what he was doing.</p>

<p>But nothing happened.</p>

<p>Nobody stopped him. Nobody even looked at him twice.</p>

<p>That night, he went home, every sound making him flinch.</p>

<p>The rustling of the trees outside, the distant hum of cars, even the soft ticking of the clock—it all felt deafening.</p>

<p>He expected the police to come knocking at his door at any second, expected the weight of his crime to come crashing down on him.</p>

<p>But the night passed peacefully.</p>

<p>Grace slept soundly beside him, completely unaware of the stolen fortune hidden just a few feet away.</p>
