<p>Ross knew, with absolute certainty, that women would be dying to meet him, to speak with him, or even just to stand near him.</p>

<p>And he wasn’t wrong.</p>

<p>Whispers followed him wherever he went, hushed conversations pausing as he passed.</p>

<p>Eyes lingered, some bold and admiring, others shy and secretive, but all undeniably drawn to him.</p>

<p>Subtle smiles, playful glances, and barely concealed admiration accompanied his every step.</p>

<p>Even seasoned warriors and high-ranking officials couldn’t help but steal glances, sensing the aura of power and confidence radiating from him.</p>

<p>Ross moved through the base like a predator in a forest of prey, aware of the attention but unfazed, savoring it instead.</p>

<p>Every step, every casual tilt of his head, seemed to draw more eyes toward him.</p>

<p>He could feel the magnetic pull of desire and curiosity, a thrill that was almost intoxicating.</p>

<p>For him, the base wasn’t just a place filled with allies or enemies—it was a playground, a place where his reputation and skill gave him absolute control over the tides of attention.</p>

<p>It didn’t take long for Ross to draw the attention of the rowdiest, loudest bunch in the base.</p>

<p>Their reputation preceded them—men who lived for chaos, laughter, and the occasional brawl, yet tonight, they had found a new source of excitement.</p>

<p>&quot;Hey there, Mr. Teleport Guy! Come over here, Ross! Our hero! Let’s drink!&quot; one of them shouted, his voice booming across the hall.</p>

<p>The others quickly followed suit, their laughter and cheers bouncing off the walls.</p>

<p>The moment one of them called, the rest joined in like a wave, hollering, clapping him on the shoulder, and shoving bottles toward him.</p>

<p>Drinks and food arrived in a steady stream.</p>

<p>Despite the scarcity of supplies in this zombie-infested world, no one hesitated to make sure Ross had more than enough.</p>

<p>Plates were piled high with whatever they could spare, bottles of liquor passed hand to hand, and the rowdy group insisted that he eat and drink as much as he could.</p>

<p>Many of them went without or took just scraps, but they didn’t care.</p>

<p>This night was for Ross, their savior, their legend.</p>

<p>Ross allowed himself to indulge.</p>

<p>He ate greedily, drank freely, and laughed along with them, knowing this rare moment of celebration might be fleeting.</p>

<p>In a world constantly under threat, nights like this were luxuries few could afford.</p>

<p>Yet here he was, surrounded by gratitude and awe, a living reminder that some things were still worth celebrating.</p>

<p>&quot;We got one more bottle, Ross! Drink!&quot; one of the men shouted, waving it over his head like a trophy.</p>

<p>The rest of the group erupted in cheers and applause.</p>

<p>Faces shone with gratitude, admiration, and relief.</p>

<p>Survivors who had lived under constant fear now looked at him like he was a beacon, a man who had single-handedly changed their night from despair to hope.</p>

<p>Ross caught the bottle with ease, a slow grin spreading across his face.</p>

<p>He raised it in acknowledgment, letting the noise and chaos wash over him.</p>

<p>Every shout, every cheer, every slap on the back reminded him of what he had accomplished—and what was at stake in this brutal world.</p>

<p>He knew his power and reputation made him untouchable tonight, yet he also understood that this night, like so many others, would pass.</p>

<p>Still, for now, he would enjoy it.</p>

<p>He would let himself be the center of celebration, drink in hand, laughter echoing in the hall, and the warmth of admiration pressing in from every direction.</p>

<p>Nights like this were rare, fleeting, and dangerous—but that made them all the more worth living.</p>

<p>Hours passed, and by the time evening had fully settled over the base, the impromptu celebration had finally begun to wind down.</p>

<p>Laughter still lingered in pockets, but the noise had dulled to occasional chatter and the clinking of glasses as the last few survivors nursed their drinks.</p>

<p>Ross leaned back for a moment, letting his eyes scan the crowd. Faces shone with gratitude, awe, and relief. It was a rare sight in a world so full of death and danger, and he allowed himself a small, satisfied smile.</p>

<p>&quot;Alright, guys, I had a great time. Thanks for the party!&quot; Ross announced, his voice carrying easily over the remaining murmurs. &quot;Now, I need to go find some more fun. Appreciate everything!&quot;</p>

<p>With that, he waved casually and began moving through the dense corridors of the base.</p>

<p>The place was already packed to an almost unbearable degree.</p>

<p>Over three hundred thousand people—soldiers, civilians, and traders—crammed into a base designed to comfortably hold a hundred thousand.</p>

<p>Every alley, stairway, and hall was alive with movement: the shuffle of feet, the chatter of voices, the occasional shout from someone bumping into another.</p>

<p>Supplies and food were stretched thin, but no one seemed to mind tonight—the night belonged to Ross, and everyone had their eyes on the man who had saved them all.</p>

<p>To most, the crowd would have been suffocating. To Ross, it was invigorating.</p>

<p>Tight spaces meant opportunity. Close quarters meant observation.</p>

<p>The press of humanity around him heightened his senses, allowed him to feel the subtle movements of everyone nearby, and increased the thrill of his hunt.</p>

<p>Every glance, every whispered conversation, every nervous shuffle fed into his awareness, and he smiled at the simple joy of it.</p>

<p>He lifted his focus, letting his divine sense sweep effortlessly across the horde of people.</p>

<p>A sea of energy, life, and subtle intentions flowed beneath the surface.</p>

<p>Some were soldiers, disciplined and alert. Some were civilians, nervous and restless.</p>

<p>And somewhere in the midst of it all, Ross could detect the faint, delicate signature of a woman who caught his attention—not flashy, not obvious, but a presence that set her apart.</p>

<p>The hunt had begun without anyone realizing it. Ross’s gaze lingered on her, reading her movements, noting her reactions to the crowded space around her.</p>

<p>She moved with grace even amidst the throng, small adjustments in posture and pace betraying confidence and awareness.</p>
