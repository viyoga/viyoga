<p>There were only two players left at the poker table, each with one million in chips.</p>

<p>The stakes were higher than money—it was a battle of egos, with everything on the line.</p>

<p>The dealer shuffled the deck and dealt the flop: two 9s and a 10 of hearts.</p>

<p>Both players glanced at their cards.</p>

<p>&quot;All in,&quot; Mr. Adams declared without hesitation, pushing his entire stack forward with a triumphant smirk.</p>

<p>His hand was strong—two 9s and a deuce, giving him four of a kind. Victory, to him, was inevitable.</p>

<p>Ross glanced down at his hand: a 3 and a 4 offsuit. The worst possible combination.</p>

<p>For a moment, he remained silent, his expression unreadable.</p>

<p>The dealer, meanwhile, was struggling to keep a straight face.</p>

<p>He’d been in this situation countless times before and knew the game was rigged.</p>

<p>Every hand he dealt was carefully orchestrated to ensure Mr. Adams always won.</p>

<p>No matter the opponent or the stakes, the outcome was predetermined.</p>

<p>That was why, when Ross finally spoke, his words sent a ripple of shock and mocking through the room.</p>

<p>&quot;Call,&quot; Ross said calmly, his voice unwavering as he pushed his chips forward.</p>

<p>The dealer hesitated for a fraction of a second before dealing the turn card.</p>

<p>It was the Queen of Hearts. The tension in the room thickened as everyone’s eyes darted to the table.</p>

<p>Before the river could be revealed, one of Mr. Adams’s men approached and whispered something in his ear.</p>

<p>His grin widened, his confidence surging.</p>

<p>&quot;It seems like this is my lucky night, Mr. Oakley,&quot; he chuckled, clearly savoring the moment.</p>

<p>&quot;…&quot; Ross did not utter a reply and was in deep thought at this time.</p>

<p>Adams leaned toward his man and murmured a command.</p>

<p>The man nodded and left, returning less than a minute later with an extraordinary sight—a stunningly beautiful girl clad in a black skirt dress.</p>

<p>Her appearance immediately captured everyone’s attention. The dress clung tightly to her figure, revealing more than she seemed comfortable with.</p>

<p>Her trembling hands repeatedly tugged at the hem of her skirt, trying to cover herself.</p>

<p>It was obvious she wasn’t used to wearing such revealing clothing, and the situation was clearly overwhelming for her.</p>

<p>A chorus of whistles erupted from the group of men watching, their lecherous gazes fixed on her.</p>

<p>Her face turned crimson, and her wide eyes glistened with unshed tears.</p>

<p>It seemed as though she might break down at any moment, but she bit her lip hard, forcing herself to stay composed.</p>

<p>Taking a deep, shaky breath, she stepped forward tentatively, her every movement betraying her fear.</p>

<p>She stopped in front of Mr. Adams, her eyes filled with terror. She looked at him as though she were staring into the face of a wild beast, and in many ways, she was.</p>

<p>Adams leaned back in his chair, his grin stretching even wider.</p>

<p>&quot;So you finally made up your mind, huh?&quot; he sneered, his eyes running up and down her trembling form.</p>

<p>Before the girl or anyone could respond, Ross’s voice cut through the room like a blade.</p>

<p>&quot;I also want her to be part of our deal,&quot; he said, his calm, authoritative tone shattering the lewd stares of the men around him.</p>

<p>The girl’s wide, fearful eyes darted to Ross, a flicker of hope lighting in them despite her overwhelming anxiety.</p>

<p>The room fell silent, all attention now on the man who had just rewritten the stakes of the game.</p>

<p>&quot;A bit late, don’t you think, Mr. Player?&quot; a rude remark came from the back, drawing the room’s attention.</p>

<p>One of Mr. Adams’s men leaned casually against the wall, a smirk plastered across his face.</p>

<p>His voice was loud and mocking, his tone dripping with contempt.</p>

<p>The other goons erupted into laughter, clearly enjoying the spectacle.</p>

<p>They were thrilled to witness the culmination of the night, confident in their boss’s inevitable victory.</p>

<p>For them, this was just another game, another conquest.</p>

<p>They all knew Mr. Adams would be the first in line to claim his prize.</p>

<p>But as a &quot;generous&quot; leader, he always allowed his men to share in the spoils—once he’d had his fill, of course.</p>

<p>Over the years, they had lost count of the women who’d been caught in their sadistic twisted games.</p>

<p>Yet tonight’s target seemed to surpass them all, making this their most exciting catch to date.</p>

<p>The laughter grew louder as the goons exchanged glances, looking at Ross as though he were the biggest fool they’d ever encountered.</p>

<p>&quot;…&quot; Mr. Adams raised a hand, silencing them immediately. The room fell quiet, the air suddenly heavy with tension.</p>

<p>&quot;My apologies for my men’s behavior,&quot; Mr. Adams said with a faux smile, his tone dripping with condescension.</p>

<p>&quot;Uneducated monkeys, the lot of them. It’s hard to train animals to act like humans.&quot;</p>

<p>The men around him chuckled nervously, unsure if his words were meant as a reprimand or a jest.</p>

<p>Mr. Adams prided himself on conducting business with an air of civility, even as he thrived on corruption and depravity at the same time.</p>

<p>The bad stuff all remained in the dark.</p>

<p>&quot;I’m used to animals, Mr. Adams,&quot; Ross replied calmly, his voice cutting through the tension like a blade.</p>

<p>&quot;Should I euthanize them for you? Help clear up some of the useless space in your inventory?&quot; His words were polite, almost friendly, yet they carried an undercurrent of menace.</p>

<p>The room stilled. Mr. Adams’s men bristled, their earlier amusement replaced with unease.</p>

<p>Mr. Adams, however, merely shook his head, maintaining his composed demeanor.</p>

<p>&quot;No need,&quot; he said smoothly, as though Ross’s suggestion hadn’t fazed him in the slightest.</p>

<p>&quot;They serve their purpose—as always.&quot;</p>

<p>Ross gave a faint nod, his expression unchanging. His gaze shifted briefly to the girl, Jessica, who stood trembling by Mr. Adams’s side.</p>

<p>&quot;As for Jessica,&quot; Mr. Adams continued, flashing Ross a broad smile, &quot;yes, you can have her—provided, of course, that you win.&quot; His confidence was palpable, his tone almost taunting.</p>

<p>He leaned back in his chair, fully convinced of his victory.</p>

<p>&quot;Of course,&quot; Ross replied, matching Mr. Adams’s smile with one of his own. It was calm, collected, and sharp enough to cut glass.</p>

<p>&quot;Let’s see the river card, then.&quot;</p>

<p>The room held its breath, all eyes locked on the table.</p>
