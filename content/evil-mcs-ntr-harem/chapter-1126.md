<p>&quot;These bastards didn’t protect survivors,&quot; Ross muttered. &quot;They hunted them. They raped women. They abused their own people. Anyone weaker, anyone desperate... they used them.&quot;</p>

<p>His lip curled in disgust.</p>

<p>&quot;Men like that have no right to breathe.&quot;</p>

<p>Giana and the rest exchanged uneasy glances. Their hearts were a mess of relief, fear, and confusion.</p>

<p>They were grateful he had cleared the danger... but terrified at how easy he made murder look.</p>

<p>Ross took another step, and his tone grew quieter—more honest, more chilling.</p>

<p>He kicked one of the corpses aside with casual disdain.</p>

<p>&quot;And men like these? Monsters... real monsters? I’ll never pity them.&quot;</p>

<p>The group followed behind him slowly, cautiously, almost afraid to walk too close.</p>

<p>They saw his back—broad, calm, unyielding—and realized something undeniable:</p>

<p>If the apocalypse had birthed demons...</p>

<p>Ross was one of them.</p>

<p>But he was their demon.</p>

<p>And in a world this hellish, that might be the only kind of guardian anyone could hope for.</p>

<p>Ross kept walking, his pace unhurried, his expression unreadable.</p>

<p>Every guilty man who dared stand in his way met the same fate—a sudden, horrifying collapse as if something colossal had stomped them flat.</p>

<p>Bones snapped like brittle twigs, organs burst like overripe fruit, and blood sprayed across the floor in wide arcs.</p>

<p>The once-clean supermarket tiles were now painted in red.</p>

<p>Ace, Miku, and the rest followed several steps behind him, their legs trembling.</p>

<p>None of them had ever seen something like this—not even during the worst days of the apocalypse.</p>

<p>Ross wasn’t fighting. He wasn’t even exerting effort. He simply walked... and men died.</p>

<p>Ace whispered under her breath, though her voice shook violently.</p>

<p>&quot;W-What... is he...?&quot;</p>

<p>Giana hugged herself tightly.</p>

<p>&quot;The Ross we know... he wouldn’t...&quot;</p>

<p>But her words faltered, drowned by the wet crunch of another body collapsing ahead of them.</p>

<p>Their kind, polite, almost gentle Ross—the one who smiled and reassured them, who drove the car, who talked to them like they mattered—felt like a memory fading into smoke.</p>

<p>The Ross walking ahead of them now felt like a cold executioner, an unstoppable force wearing a human face.</p>

<p>The deeper they entered the superstore, the more survivors they encountered.</p>

<p>Some men held pipes, knives, or stolen firearms.</p>

<p>Many were shaking, realizing the intruder had already carved a bloody path through their comrades.</p>

<p>&quot;No... no no no—please!&quot;</p>

<p>A man crashed to his knees the moment he spotted Ross approaching.</p>

<p>His hands shot up in surrender, tears streaming down his face.</p>

<p>Ross stopped.</p>

<p>The air thickened.</p>

<p>Ace held her breath.</p>

<p>But Ross walked past him without inflicting harm.</p>

<p>The man collapsed forward in relief, sobbing uncontrollably.</p>

<p>And then it happened again—a terrified young man in torn clothing pressed his body against the wall, trembling so hard he could barely speak.</p>

<p>Ross’s eyes passed over him like one might glance at a piece of furniture. He was spared.</p>

<p>Giana watched, heart pounding, confusion twisting in her chest.</p>

<p>Why was Ross letting these ones live?</p>

<p>She couldn’t ask—not while Ross was drenched in the quiet, suffocating aura of a killer.</p>

<p>Not while bodies were still collapsing like crushed tin cans around them.</p>

<p>Instead, she simply followed, her boots stepping over cracked skulls and shattered bones, her eyes fixed on Ross’s broad back.</p>

<p>As they walked through the aisles, Ross’s judgment became clearer.</p>

<p>—The terrified, powerless men: spared.</p>

<p>—The ones with guilt thick in their eyes: crushed instantly.</p>

<p>—The ones who had laughed while women cried: obliterated.</p>

<p>—The ones who had enjoyed violence: wiped out before they realized they’d been targeted.</p>

<p>Ross didn’t need explanations.</p>

<p>He didn’t need confessions.</p>

<p>He could see their filth the moment his eyes landed on them.</p>

<p>&quot;More of them...&quot; Miku whispered as they crossed into the hardware section.</p>

<p>Groups of survivors huddled there, clutching whatever they could find.</p>

<p>Some had been forced into servitude. Others were clearly victims.</p>

<p>When Ross approached, they backed away instinctively, fearing the same fate as the corpses outside.</p>

<p>But Ross walked past them.</p>

<p>Not one died.</p>

<p>The spared men stared at him in disbelief, some collapsing in relief, others breaking down in tears after days—maybe weeks—of terror.</p>

<p>One of them whispered shakily:</p>

<p>&quot;He... he knows... who the real monsters are...&quot;</p>

<p>Finally, after passing through multiple aisles, crossing over what felt like a battlefield of blood and broken bodies, they reached the back of the superstore.</p>

<p>A set of heavy employee-only doors hung crooked on their hinges, forced open earlier by some panicked survivor.</p>

<p>Behind them was a massive storage area.</p>

<p>Dimly lit.</p>

<p>Smelling of sweat, fear, and something rancid.</p>

<p>Dozens of people huddled there, packed together like cattle. Some were crying silently.</p>

<p>Others kept their eyes down, too terrified to move. This was the heart of the supermarket’s torment.</p>

<p>And at the center of it all, lounging on a heap of blankets and stolen supplies like a tyrant on a trash throne...</p>

<p>...was Bruno.</p>

<p>He lifted his head when Ross stepped inside, his confident smirk freezing halfway.</p>

<p>Around him stood his loyal dogs—goons who had carried out his orders, abused the weak, and ruled this place with iron fists and twisted smiles.</p>

<p>They tightened their grips on their makeshift weapons, but their eyes were wide, filled with panic.</p>

<p>Ross didn’t stop walking.</p>

<p>His boots echoed through the storage area like tolling bells announcing death’s arrival.</p>

<p>Every guilty man flinched.</p>

<p>Every innocent person swallowed and prayed.</p>

<p>Giana, Ace, Miku, and the others stood at the entrance, watching in stunned silence as Ross finally came face to face with Bruno and the monsters who had turned this supermarket into a kingdom of pain.</p>

<p>The true confrontation was about to begin.</p>

<p>&quot;Make a move and I’ll have my men kill these people!&quot; Bruno roared, his voice booming through the storage hall.</p>

<p>The big, bald brute stepped forward, his boots crunching on debris and spent shell casings.</p>

<p>A wicked grin stretched across his scarred face as he jabbed a thick finger toward the terrified crowd.</p>

<p>Behind him, over three dozen armed men spread out in a rough semi-circle, guns raised and abilities flaring unevenly across their bodies.</p>

<p>Their fingers tightened on the triggers. Their eyes darted between Ross and the helpless survivors.</p>
