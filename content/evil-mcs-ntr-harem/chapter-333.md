<p>The two talked for three hours, though it felt like only minutes.</p>

<p>Their connection was undeniable, and if this were a different world—free of complications and shadows—they might have already been fast on their way to becoming lovers.</p>

<p>Unfortunately, this wasn&#x27;t such a world. Ross Oakley did not take kindly to anyone approaching those he considered his possessions, and Jessica knew it all too well.</p>

<p>&quot;I hate to bring this up, but… can I have your number, Jessica? I&#x27;d love to stay in touch and see you again.&quot; Kevin scratched the back of his head awkwardly, his expression earnest and slightly hopeful.</p>

<p>It was obvious he liked her—a lot. Jessica&#x27;s exquisite beauty had drawn him in from the start, but as they talked, it was her quiet strength and thoughtful nature that captivated him.</p>

<p>Not only was she stunning, with a figure that turned heads wherever she went, but she also had an air of vulnerability that made him want to protect her.</p>

<p>Kevin could see the envious glances from other men around them, and it filled him with a sense of pride.</p>

<p>To an outsider, it might even seem like Jessica was his girlfriend, sitting there beside him with a soft smile and a twinkle in her eyes.</p>

<p>But Kevin&#x27;s happy confidence evaporated the moment Jessica responded.</p>

<p>&quot;I&#x27;m sorry, but I can&#x27;t. In fact, I already have a boyfriend,&quot; she said honestly, though her tone carried an unmistakable weight.</p>

<p>Kevin&#x27;s brow furrowed, the warmth in his expression fading as he processed her words.</p>

<p>&quot;That&#x27;s hard to believe,&quot; he said after a moment. &quot;If I were your boyfriend, I&#x27;d never leave you alone like this. Don&#x27;t lie to me, Jessica. I know you&#x27;re not telling me the whole truth.&quot;</p>

<p>Jessica&#x27;s shoulders tensed, and she turned her gaze back to the endless ocean, avoiding his piercing eyes.</p>

<p>Kevin continued, his voice calm but insistent.</p>

<p>&quot;You said earlier that you were having problems with money, and then, conveniently, a &#x27;rich family member&#x27; came out of nowhere to help. That same family member paid for this entire trip, right? Jessica, I&#x27;m a psychology major—I can spot a lie when I see one.&quot;</p>

<p>She flinched at his words. He wasn&#x27;t wrong; she had struggled to maintain eye contact when explaining her situation earlier.</p>

<p>Kevin had noticed her hesitation, her nervous glances, and the subtle tells that betrayed her discomfort.</p>

<p>&quot;I don&#x27;t know what&#x27;s really going on with you,&quot; Kevin said softly, leaning forward slightly, &quot;but you don&#x27;t have to lie to me. I want to help, Jessica. I—&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;Stop.&quot; Jessica&#x27;s voice was firm but shaky as she cut him off. She turned back to him, her eyes filled with a mixture of regret and fear.</p>

<p>&quot;You can&#x27;t help me. No one can. Please, just… let this go.&quot;</p>

<p>Kevin opened his mouth to protest, but the look in her eyes silenced him. There was something deeper, something darker, behind her words, and he could tell she wasn&#x27;t lying now.</p>

<p>Jessica stood abruptly, clutching her purse tightly.</p>

<p>&quot;Thank you for the conversation, Kevin. It really helped,&quot; she said, offering a weak but genuine smile. &quot;Take care.&quot;</p>

<p>Before Kevin could respond, she turned and walked away, leaving him alone with his thoughts—and a sinking feeling that something wasn&#x27;t right.</p>

<p>Unbeknownst to him, Jessica&#x27;s every move had been under watchful eyes.</p>

<p>Somewhere in the shadows, Ross Oakley was already aware of Kevin&#x27;s interest, and the consequences of this encounter were about to unfold.</p>

<p>***</p>

<p>Jessica sighed as someone cut through her thoughts as she was already walking back to her room.</p>

<p>&quot;Where were you, Jessica? Ross has been asking about you.&quot;</p>

<p>She had barely taken a step onto the ship&#x27;s deck before April found her, her expression equal parts curious and concerned.</p>

<p>Jessica liked April; the girl was sharp, and Jessica valued intelligence above all else. Their conversations had always been engaging, a rare solace amidst the chaos of her life.</p>

<p>&quot;I just went out to get some fresh air,&quot; Jessica lied smoothly, though her mind churned. The truth was far less palatable.</p>

<p>Their shared room reeked of sex, the scent an unavoidable reminder of her unwanted role as a spectator to the endless intimacies around her.</p>

<p>Unlike Reina and Mari, who had some measure of freedom, Jessica&#x27;s fate was sealed. Ross had made it clear she was to become one of &quot;his girls.&quot;</p>

<p>The way his gaze lingered on her, sticky with intent, was something she&#x27;d endured for years. It was a look she recognized all too well—the look of a man who wanted her.</p>

<p>April&#x27;s brow furrowed.</p>

<p>&quot;Did you tell Ross you were stepping out for air?&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;No. Why? It&#x27;s no big deal, right? He&#x27;s busy enough with you and the rest of his wives.&quot; Jessica&#x27;s nonchalance was a shield, though it failed to ease April&#x27;s worry.</p>

<p>&quot;Jessica! Come with me.&quot; April grabbed her wrist, pulling her toward a secluded part of the cruise ship. They slipped into an empty room, April closing the door firmly behind them.</p>

<p>&quot;What I&#x27;m about to tell you doesn&#x27;t leave this room. Ross is dangerous. Never make him angry.&quot; She shivered, as though reliving a distant nightmare.</p>

<p>Jessica&#x27;s lips curled into a bitter smile.</p>

<p>&quot;You think I don&#x27;t know that? Why do you think I&#x27;m here?&quot;</p>

<p>April&#x27;s eyes widened, then narrowed in understanding.</p>

<p>&quot;You&#x27;ve only seen Ross&#x27;s cruelty from the sidelines, haven&#x27;t you? To others, and not to those you care about. Never directly to you or your family. That&#x27;s why you&#x27;re still defiant. Let me warn you—if you keep pushing him, he will find a way to break you. And when he does, you&#x27;ll have to live with the consequences.&quot;</p>

<p>Jessica said nothing, her silence heavy with unspoken fears. April&#x27;s trembling voice softened to a whisper.</p>

<p>&quot;I just hope you&#x27;re ready for what&#x27;s coming.&quot;</p>

<p>They returned to the main cabin, the ship&#x27;s largest, where a raucous party was in full swing. Laughter and music filled the air, accompanied by the clinking of glasses.</p>

<p>&quot;Where were you two?&quot; one of the girls called out, handing them drinks.</p>

<p>April quickly concocted a story, her words blending seamlessly with the party&#x27;s din. Jessica, meanwhile, retreated to a corner, nursing her drink.</p>

<p>The bitter taste of expensive wine burned her throat, but it dulled her senses, a small mercy in the chaos of how her life turned out to be.</p>

<p>A voice behind her shattered her fragile reprieve. &quot;I heard you made a friend.&quot;</p>

<p>Jessica froze. The hairs on her neck stood on end as Ross&#x27;s voice, smooth and menacing, coiled around her like a hunter closing in on its prey.</p>
