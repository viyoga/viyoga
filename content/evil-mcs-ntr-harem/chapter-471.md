<p>Monday came, and Amy finally saw William again.</p>

<p>&quot;I’ve missed you, Amy!&quot; William pulled her into a tight hug, his usual warm smile on full display.</p>

<p>Amy’s eyes widened slightly as she took in his face. His teeth—perfect and white—had been completely restored. It was as if nothing had ever happened.</p>

<p>&quot;I missed you too,&quot; she murmured, pulling back to get a better look at him.</p>

<p>&quot;How are you? I saw the video… you were beaten up and tied up, William. Ross did that to you. We should report him to the police.&quot;</p>

<p>Her voice trembled slightly with frustration. She had spent the past week trapped in Ross’s grasp, pretending everything was normal, forced to entertain his whims.</p>

<p>But deep down, she despised him. No amount of forced dinner dates or polite smiles could change that.</p>

<p>She knew exactly what Ross wanted from her. He had said it himself during their first meeting—he only wanted her body. Nothing else.</p>

<p>William’s expression faltered for just a second before he quickly masked it with a chuckle.</p>

<p>&quot;Ross? What are you talking about, Amy?&quot; He waved a dismissive hand.</p>

<p>&quot;That whole thing? It was nothing. Just a prank. My old friends from high school set it up… you know how they are.&quot;</p>

<p>Amy’s stomach twisted.</p>

<p>She knew he was lying.</p>

<p>His voice was too forced, his smile just a little too tight. The William she knew wasn’t a good liar.</p>

<p>But why? Why was he protecting Ross?</p>

<p>Amy grabbed his hand, squeezing it tightly. &quot;William, you don’t have to be scared. If we go to the police—&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;I said it was nothing, Amy.&quot; William’s voice dropped, his grip on her hand tightening just enough to make her pause.</p>

<p>&quot;Let it go.&quot;</p>

<p>Amy stared at him, searching his face for answers. There was fear in his eyes, buried deep beneath his forced nonchalance.</p>

<p>Ross had gotten to him.</p>

<p>And just like that, Amy realized the truth.</p>

<p>She and William weren’t just dealing with some arrogant rich boy.</p>

<p>They were dealing with a monster.</p>

<p>As the day came to an end, William and Amy planned to have dinner together, looking forward to spending some quiet time alone.</p>

<p>They had just reached the parking lot, ready to leave, when an unwelcome voice interrupted them.</p>

<p>&quot;Funny seeing you here, Amy,&quot; Ross said smoothly, a smirk playing on his lips. He leaned casually against his car, exuding confidence.</p>

<p>&quot;I was just about to grab dinner. Care to join me?&quot; His eyes flickered toward William, clearly enjoying the provocation.</p>

<p>Amy tensed beside William, her grip on his arm tightening. William, already annoyed, squared his shoulders.</p>

<p>&quot;She’s my girlfriend. Stay away from her,&quot; he said firmly, his voice edged with anger.</p>

<p>Ross let out a chuckle, shaking his head.</p>

<p>&quot;Oh, William,&quot; he sighed mockingly.</p>

<p>&quot;Looks like you’re asking for another beating. Walk away now and save yourself the trouble.&quot;</p>

<p>William clenched his fists, his pride refusing to back down.</p>

<p>&quot;You’re delusional. Get the fuck out of here,&quot; he shot back, stepping slightly in front of Amy as if shielding her from Ross’s presence.</p>

<p>Ross studied him for a moment, amusement dancing in his eyes.</p>

<p>&quot;Still haven’t learned your lesson, huh?&quot; he mused, shaking his head.</p>

<p>For a moment, the tension was thick, the air crackling with unspoken threats. Amy glanced nervously between the two men, her heart pounding.</p>

<p>Then, just as suddenly as he had appeared, Ross let out a low chuckle and turned away.</p>

<p>&quot;Fine,&quot; he said, walking back to his car.</p>

<p>&quot;Enjoy your night while it lasts.&quot;</p>

<p>He slid into the driver’s seat, revved the engine, and drove off, but the unease he left behind lingered like a shadow.</p>

<p>William and Amy enjoyed their dinner together, laughing and talking as they basked in each other’s company.</p>

<p>For a while, it felt like nothing else in the world mattered—just the two of them, lost in the moment.</p>

<p>When the evening came to an end, William drove Amy home, watching with a fond smile as she stepped inside and waved goodbye.</p>

<p>Satisfied, he turned his car around and made his way home, completely unaware of the nightmare awaiting him.</p>

<p>As he reached his front door, a strange sense of unease settled over him. The house was dark and quiet, but something felt… off.</p>

<p>He shook off the feeling, blaming it on exhaustion, and pushed the door open.</p>

<p>The moment he stepped inside, his heart nearly stopped.</p>

<p>Someone was already there, lounging comfortably on his couch, as if he owned the place. A tall, muscular figure dressed in all black, his presence exuding danger.</p>

<p>&quot;Howdy, boy,&quot; the man drawled, a slow, wicked grin stretching across his face.</p>

<p>&quot;Let’s have some fun, shall we?&quot;</p>

<p>William’s blood ran cold. Brandon.</p>

<p>Panic surged through him, and without thinking, he turned to flee.</p>

<p>&quot;No—!&quot;</p>

<p>He didn’t make it two steps before something heavy slammed into the back of his head. A blinding pain exploded through his skull, and the world tilted violently.</p>

<p>He barely registered the floor rushing up to meet him before darkness swallowed him whole.</p>

<p>When William regained consciousness, his body screamed in pain.</p>

<p>His wrists were bound, his head pounded, and his vision swam as he tried to make sense of his surroundings.</p>

<p>The dimly lit room smelled of sweat and blood, the air thick with the metallic tang of his own suffering.</p>

<p>Then came the first blow.</p>

<p>A sickening crunch echoed through the room as a fist connected with his jaw. White-hot agony shot through his face, and a choked cry escaped his lips.</p>

<p>Then another.</p>

<p>And another.</p>

<p>It went on for what felt like hours. Brandon didn’t hold back. His punches were precise, practiced, and devastating.</p>

<p>Every time William tried to shield himself, another strike would land, each more brutal than the last.</p>

<p>His ribs ached. His face was swollen beyond recognition.</p>

<p>Blood trickled down his chin, staining his already tattered clothes.</p>

<p>But the worst part? The unbearable pain in his mouth.</p>

<p>Something was missing.</p>

<p>A groggy realization hit him—his teeth.</p>

<p>All of them were gone!</p>
