<p>Ted imagined their passionate nights together, their connection so strong, but his body remained stubbornly unresponsive. Nothing was working.</p>

<p>In an attempt to shake off the frustration, he started focusing on other images—other fantasies.</p>

<p>He pictured another woman, someone else entirely, hoping the distraction might help him regain control, but even that failed.</p>

<p>He pushed harder, thinking of his sister-in-law, someone who always seemed to stir him in the past, but even that didn&#x27;t help.</p>

<p>He spent several more minutes trying, but each time, his efforts fell short.</p>

<p>The harder he tried, the more he felt his confidence slipping. His body refused to cooperate.</p>

<p>&quot;Why... why is this happening?&quot; Ted muttered under his breath, unable to hide the panic rising within him.</p>

<p>His heart pounded in his chest, and a sense of dread washed over him.</p>

<p>Grace, sitting by his side, reached out gently, touching his shoulder with concern.</p>

<p>&quot;Ted, it&#x27;s okay. We don&#x27;t need to rush. Maybe you&#x27;re just stressed. Let&#x27;s try again tomorrow.&quot;</p>

<p>But Ted couldn&#x27;t shake the feeling of unease.</p>

<p>Something was wrong, and he couldn&#x27;t fix it, no matter how hard he tried.</p>

<p>His mind raced through every possibility, and yet none made sense.</p>

<p>This wasn&#x27;t a simple case of nerves or exhaustion; this was something else entirely.</p>

<p>&quot;What&#x27;s happening to me?&quot; Ted finally asked, his voice a mix of frustration and fear.</p>

<p>Ted tried for a long time, but no matter what he did, his little brother remained stubbornly unresponsive.</p>

<p>A heavy silence hung between them.</p>

<p>Grace, lying beside him, reached out to touch his arm, offering a reassuring smile.</p>

<p>&quot;It&#x27;s okay, love. It happens.&quot;</p>

<p>He exhaled sharply, forcing a chuckle.</p>

<p>&quot;Yeah… maybe I&#x27;m just tired. Let&#x27;s try again tomorrow as you said, okay?&quot;</p>

<p>She nodded, brushing her fingers through his hair, but Ted could see the slight disappointment in her eyes. It made his stomach twist.</p>

<p>He quickly pulled his pajamas back on, kissed her on the cheek, and turned onto his side, feigning sleep.</p>

<p>But he couldn&#x27;t. His mind wouldn&#x27;t let him.</p>

<p>He had never experienced this before.</p>

<p>Never.</p>

<p>No matter how exhausted he was, no matter the circumstances, he had always been able to perform.</p>

<p>He had always taken pride in making sure Grace was satisfied, and now—this?</p>

<p>He felt like a part of him had failed, like something inside had broken.</p>

<p>He closed his eyes, trying to shake the thoughts away, but they refused to leave him alone.</p>

<p>It wasn&#x27;t until sometime past midnight that he finally drifted into a restless sleep.</p>

<p>The morning came too soon, and when he woke, his body felt like lead.</p>

<p>He stared at the ceiling for a long moment before dragging himself out of bed, the weight of last night still pressing on his chest.</p>

<p>&quot;I&#x27;ll do better tonight,&quot; he muttered to himself, trying to push past his doubts.</p>

<p>He refused to believe this was anything more than a one-time thing.</p>

<p>With that thought, he forced himself into his routine—showering, dressing, and heading to work as usual.</p>

<p>But the moment he stepped into his office, he realized that today was going to be anything but usual.</p>

<p>A sharp knock interrupted his thoughts, followed by the heavy thud of boots against the floor.</p>

<p>&quot;Hey, Ted, got extra space in your office?&quot;</p>

<p>Ted looked up to see a large man filling the doorway.</p>

<p>Brandon—one of Ross&#x27;s most trusted men. His presence always meant something serious was going on.</p>

<p>Ted straightened in his chair. &quot;I have an empty room. Why?&quot;</p>

<p>Brandon stepped inside, arms crossed. &quot;I need to move some stuff. Could use an extra set of hands.&quot;</p>

<p>Ted nodded, already standing. &quot;Of course.&quot;</p>

<p>Brandon was someone Ted had grown familiar with over the years.</p>

<p>They saw each other regularly in meetings, and if there was one thing Ted knew about him, it was that he was always hands-on, always precise.</p>

<p>If Brandon was handling something personally, then it was important.</p>

<p>Ted suppressed a sigh. Looks like today was going to be long.</p>

<p>They stepped outside, and Ted came to an abrupt halt. His breath hitched, his stomach tightening at the sight before him.</p>

<p>Just outside his office stood a dozen heavily armed men, their rifles slung over their shoulders, their movements efficient and purposeful.</p>

<p>But what truly made his heart pound was what they were handling.</p>

<p>Stacks upon stacks of cash.</p>

<p>Not just a few bundles—mountains of money.</p>

<p>Towering piles of neatly wrapped bills, packed into crates and duffel bags, being hauled into the building with alarming efficiency.</p>

<p>Ted&#x27;s mouth went dry as he tried to process the sheer amount. It wasn&#x27;t a few million.</p>

<p>By his rough estimate, this was hundreds of millions, possibly more.</p>

<p>&quot;Boss? Where do we take this?&quot; one of the armed men asked, shifting his grip on a heavy duffel bag.</p>

<p>Brandon barely glanced at him before turning his gaze to Ted.</p>

<p>That look sent a clear message: You know what to do.</p>

<p>Ted swallowed hard, then nodded stiffly.</p>

<p>&quot;Here,&quot; he said, his voice coming out more automatic than intentional.</p>

<p>His body moved as if on autopilot, his hands reaching out to help even as his brain screamed for answers.</p>

<p>What was this money for? Where did it come from? And why was he involved?</p>

<p>His pulse thundered in his ears as he lifted a duffel bag, feeling the surprising weight of the cash inside.</p>

<p>The bills were crisp, bound tightly in thick stacks. Every fiber of his being told him this wasn&#x27;t normal—this wasn&#x27;t just company business.</p>

<p>Yet he worked.</p>

<p>What else could he do?</p>

<p>The men moved with practiced ease, as if this were just another routine job.</p>

<p>Ted, on the other hand, felt like he was walking through a dream—one that teetered on the edge of a nightmare.</p>

<p>They worked for hours, the weight of the money—both literal and metaphorical—pressing down on him.</p>

<p>By the time they finished, the sun hung high in the sky, and the once-empty room was now filled to the brim with cash.</p>

<p>Ted stepped back, wiping the sweat from his brow as he surveyed the stacks surrounding him.</p>

<p>A whole room of money.</p>

<p>And then, like a slow-moving storm cloud, a thought settled heavily in his mind.</p>

<p>Is Ross Oakley doing something illegal?</p>
