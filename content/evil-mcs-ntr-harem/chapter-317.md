<p>The explanation brought a few giggles, but Ross knew it was only half true.</p>

<p>His parents had likely heard the rumors about his unconventional lifestyle, but they wouldn’t fully believe it until he introduced the girls to them in person.</p>

<p>Still, he wanted to ease into that conversation gradually.</p>

<p>Her quip drew laughter from the group, lightening the mood.</p>

<p>The next day, Ross stepped off the plane at Northpass City Airport, the salty breeze from the nearby sea greeting him. He chuckled to himself, his lips curling into a smile.</p>

<p>&quot;The last time I traveled to Parkland City, I took a boat and spent an entire night crossing the water,&quot; Ross muttered, recalling the journey with a mix of nostalgia and amusement.</p>

<p>&quot;Now I can get here in an hour by plane. But the journey’s not over yet.&quot;</p>

<p>Normally, the next leg of his trip would involve a bus ride over 50 kilometers of winding roads, taking at least an hour.</p>

<p>But this time, Ross had arranged for a helicopter, which would whisk him home in just 20 minutes.</p>

<p>&quot;Money really does buy convenience,&quot; Ross mused as he boarded the sleek helicopter. &quot;It’s like saving time is the ultimate luxury.&quot;</p>

<p>He could have teleported directly to his hometown, but where was the fun in that?</p>

<p>Skipping every step of the journey felt like using cheat codes in a game—efficient, sure, but it took away the thrill of the experience.</p>

<p>As the helicopter lifted off, Ross leaned back, watching the familiar landscape unfold beneath him. The final stretch of his journey home had begun, and he couldn’t help but feel a tinge of excitement.</p>

<p>Twenty-five minutes later, Ross finally arrived in his hometown, Mirfield. Nestled among sprawling rice fields, this small farming town of about 15,000 people had always been a haven of tranquility.</p>

<p>Unlike the bustling, fast-paced cities Ross had recently become accustomed to, Mirfield moved at its own leisurely rhythm, where everyone knew each other, and time seemed to stretch endlessly.</p>

<p>Time stood nearly still here, and change was almost imperceptible.</p>

<p>As the van pulled up in front of his family home, familiar sights and sounds greeted him—the chatter of children playing in the distance, the faint aroma of fresh-cooked rice wafting from a neighbor’s kitchen, and the hum of crickets signaling the approach of night.</p>

<p>It didn’t take long for word to spread that Ross was back in town.</p>

<p>His parents greeted him warmly, their smiles a testament to their pride and joy. Though Ross had only been away for less than a year, the reunion felt like a homecoming long overdue.</p>

<p>True to their expectations, Ross hadn’t come empty-handed. He distributed sacks of rice to the neighbors who showed up, ensuring no one left empty-handed.</p>

<p>For those who didn’t receive rice, he handed out cash instead, instructing them to buy what they needed.</p>

<p>The astonished looks on their faces confirmed what Ross suspected—his parents had been discreet about the 10 million usd he’d given them. It was a wise move.</p>

<p>In a small town like Mirfield, flaunting wealth could quickly attract trouble. His relatives, however, were another story. They knew about his winnings, and many had been shamelessly messaging him for gifts.</p>

<p>Ross didn’t mind; he was generous with his fortune, knowing it cost him little to share. He could practically make moneys out of thin air.</p>

<p>As for security, Ross had long ensured his family and the entire community were well-protected. Unbeknownst to the townsfolk, he had stationed protective puppets throughout Mirfield.</p>

<p>These sentinels would neutralize any threat before it could reach his loved ones, ensuring peace of mind for everyone.</p>

<p>The afternoon passed in joyous celebration. Over a hearty lunch, Ross and his parents caught up on everything—from changes in the town to family gossip.</p>

<p>His parents shared stories of local events, and Ross listened with genuine interest, feeling a sense of grounding he hadn’t realized he missed. For now, he kept quiet about his upcoming wedding.</p>

<p>He had five days to ease them into the idea, and he wanted to savor every moment of this visit before broaching such life-changing news.</p>

<p>As evening descended and the household quieted, Ross finally broached a question that had been lingering in his mind.</p>

<p>&quot;How is Mrs. Lawrence, by the way?&quot; he asked, feigning nonchalance.</p>

<p>His parents exchanged knowing looks before his mother teased him with a playful smile. &quot;If we didn’t know any better, your father and I would think you came back just to check on your childhood crush.&quot;</p>
