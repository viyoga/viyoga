<p>Emma left the house at precisely 5:30, giving the new nanny final instructions before stepping out.</p>

<p>Her daughter, Erica, was in capable hands, and the cameras installed throughout the house gave her some semblance of comfort.</p>

<p>Still, leaving Erica wasn’t easy, but Emma reminded herself why she was doing this. It was for her daughter, for the miracle that had saved her life.</p>

<p>At exactly 6:00, Emma arrived at the Autumn Palms Hotel.</p>

<p>She had reserved a private dining room and ordered an assortment of dishes for her guest.</p>

<p>The table was lavishly set, but Emma barely noticed. Her heart pounded with apprehension, a knot of dread tightening in her chest.</p>

<p>She didn’t want to be here, but she had no choice. Erica meant everything to her, and she would do anything—anything—to protect her.</p>

<p>By the time the clock struck 7:00, Ross entered the hotel.</p>

<p>He strolled through the lobby, just as he had during their first meeting, but this time, there was no pretense.</p>

<p>His mask of politeness was gone, replaced with a confident, almost predatory smirk.</p>

<p>When he reached the private room, he slid into the seat opposite Emma without hesitation, his presence commanding the space.</p>

<p>&quot;I trust my healing skills were… appreciated,&quot; Ross said, his tone smug as he immediately began digging into the food.</p>

<p>Emma, sitting stiffly across from him, hadn’t touched her plate. Her hands were clasped tightly in her lap as she forced herself to meet his gaze.</p>

<p>&quot;What do you want in return, Ross?&quot; she asked bluntly.</p>

<p>Ross raised an eyebrow, a piece of steak halfway to his mouth.</p>

<p>&quot;What I’ve always wanted, Emma. You.&quot; His words were casual, as if he were commenting on the weather, and he resumed eating without waiting for her reaction.</p>

<p>Emma’s stomach churned, but she pushed the discomfort down. She refused to let him see her break.</p>

<p>&quot;Tell me about yourself,&quot; Ross continued, his tone light but probing.</p>

<p>&quot;Entertain me while I eat.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;What’s there to tell?&quot; Emma said with a weary sigh. &quot;I’ve already told you everything. My life is boring. Long story short.&quot;</p>

<p>Ross chuckled, clearly amused.</p>

<p>&quot;Boring, huh? Well, where’s the father? That’s an interesting place to start.&quot;</p>

<p>Emma stiffened, her fingers tightening around her fork.</p>

<p>&quot;Dead,&quot; she replied curtly.</p>

<p>&quot;Drowned in soup,&quot; she added with biting sarcasm, making it clear she had no interest in the topic.</p>

<p>Ross smirked, undeterred.</p>

<p>&quot;Ah, so he ran off after getting you pregnant. Classic tale. Beautiful girl, single mom, struggling to make it work. How old are you, anyway? Twenty?&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;Twenty-four,&quot; Emma corrected sharply, the clatter of her silverware against her plate betraying her irritation.</p>

<p>&quot;Close enough,&quot; Ross said, his grin widening. He leaned back in his chair, watching her intently.</p>

<p>&quot;I wonder if...&quot;</p>

<p>The conversation dragged on, with Ross teasing Emma at every turn.</p>

<p>His questions were invasive, his comments laced with mockery, but Emma held her ground, refusing to let him see how much his words cut.</p>

<p>As the evening wore on, Ross seemed to enjoy himself more and more, while Emma’s patience wore thin.</p>

<p>She could feel the weight of his gaze, the unspoken threat lingering in every word, and yet she stayed.</p>

<p>For Erica, she would endure this. She had no choice.</p>

<p>&quot;What can I say? You’re a strong, beautiful woman, Emma. And that’s exactly why I chose you,&quot; Ross said with a sly smile as he stood up.</p>

<p>He placed a thick stack of bills on the table as a tip, then extended his hand to her.</p>

<p>They had been eating and talking for two hours, a time Ross seemed to savor entirely.</p>

<p>Emma, however, felt every second drag like an eternity.</p>

<p>Her thoughts kept drifting back to Erica—her sweet baby waiting at home, the one she wanted to hold, teach, and cherish every moment with.</p>

<p>&quot;Time to claim my reward,&quot; Ross said with a grin, his tone leaving no room for doubt about his intentions.</p>

<p>Emma remained silent, her expression blank as she reached for his hand.</p>

<p>She led him through the elevator and up several floors to the suite she had booked.</p>

<p>The click of the lock behind them echoed in the room as she turned to face him.</p>

<p>&quot;Let me make one thing clear, Ross,&quot; she began firmly, her gaze unwavering.</p>

<p>&quot;I know you like to keep your women close, to show them off to the world like trophies. But I can’t do that. I have a child to think about. This has to stay between us. Promise me.&quot;</p>

<p>Ross raised an eyebrow, clearly amused.</p>

<p>&quot;Why complicate things, Emma? You could just stay with me. I’ll take care of you and your daughter. Hell, I’d even marry you so she has a father figure growing up.&quot; His voice was steady, almost sincere, as if he genuinely believed his offer was the perfect solution.</p>

<p>Emma let out a dry laugh, shaking her head.</p>

<p>&quot;Hold on. Let’s not get ahead of ourselves,&quot; she said, her tone sharp.</p>

<p>&quot;I’ll let you fuck me—anytime you want, anywhere you want. But my daughter is off-limits. I don’t want her growing up in some dysfunctional mess, watching a man with a hundred women parade through her life. That’s my boundary, Ross. Take it or leave it.&quot;</p>

<p>Ross’s grin widened, a spark of mischief dancing in his eyes.</p>

<p>&quot;Alright, be it as you wish. But I have all the time in the world to change your mind.&quot;</p>

<p>Before Emma could respond, Ross stepped forward, his intent clear.</p>

<p>Emma braced herself, suppressing every thought that didn’t involve protecting her daughter. Whatever this arrangement entailed, she would endure it—for Erica’s sake.</p>

<p>&quot;Hmmmmm...&quot; A muffled sound escaped her as, for only the second time in her life, her soft, sweet lips were captured by a man whose desire for her was unmistakable.</p>

<p>His touch was firm yet deliberate, as though he were savoring every moment, every taste.</p>

<p>Her breath hitched as the warmth of his lips melded with hers, a stark contrast to the cool air of the room.</p>

<p>There was an undeniable intensity in the way he kissed her—a mixture of longing, possession, and a hunger that seemed insatiable.</p>

<p>It wasn’t just a kiss; it was a claim, a silent declaration of how much he wanted her.</p>
