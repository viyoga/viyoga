<p>That night, Thomas didn&#x27;t settle and couldn&#x27;t sleep until very late, as he continued his study of Ross&#x27;s women.</p>

<p>He made notes—times of day they were alone, places they visited without Ross, names of friends who could be turned or watched. Patterns. Vulnerabilities. Entry points.</p>

<p>Charm one. Distract another. Befriend the quiet one. Seduce the boldest.</p>

<p>It was all strategy. All manipulation.</p>

<p>But as his fingers moved over the keyboard, as he compiled a plan so intricate it could pass as poetry, there was a brief pause in Thomas&#x27;s heart. A flicker.</p>

<p>A memory. A time when he wasn&#x27;t a weapon—just a man who wanted to love and be loved.</p>

<p>That time was long gone.</p>

<p>He shut the thought down and opened the secure communication channel on his device.</p>

<p>MESSAGE TO HQ – ENCRYPTED</p>

<p>Target research complete. Entry strategy underway. Proceeding with soft contact phase.</p>

<p>—Casanova</p>

<p>He closed the laptop with a quiet click and let the silence of the room wrap around him once more.</p>

<p>Tomorrow, he would begin. And one of Ross Oakley&#x27;s precious women would be the first to meet him.</p>

<p>But which one?</p>

<p>He smiled to himself.</p>

<p>&quot;Let&#x27;s see who&#x27;s the easiest to fall,&quot; he whispered.</p>

<p>***</p>

<p>One week later, Thomas was finally ready to set his plan into motion.</p>

<p>After days of careful observation and strategic planning, he&#x27;d studied Ross&#x27;s women in depth.</p>

<p>He didn&#x27;t go near the wives who already had children—there were four of them now: Althea, Mari, Stephany, and Penny.</p>

<p>All four had given birth within the past few years, and Thomas knew Ross was more possessive of them than ever and vice versa.</p>

<p>They were, in his eyes, marked and untouchable—for now. Thomas wasn&#x27;t reckless.</p>

<p>He was calculating.</p>

<p>He understood that the key to dismantling a man like Ross wasn&#x27;t brute force, but precision.</p>

<p>So instead, Thomas turned his attention to someone different. Someone who had a past.</p>

<p>A woman with a history of infidelity, one who had once strayed in a moment of weakness—and Ross had snatched her up early, long before he became the larger-than-life figure he was now.</p>

<p>She wasn&#x27;t just a pretty face—she had power, status, and perhaps... lingering temptation.</p>

<p>Dressed in a sharp, charcoal-gray business suit that hugged his massive frame, Thomas looked every bit the part of a corporate alpha.</p>

<p>His suit was tailored to perfection, the lines crisp, the fabric expensive.</p>

<p>His tie was blood-red—a subtle message, a silent threat.</p>

<p>He adjusted the cufflinks on his sleeves as he strode confidently into the grand lobby of the skyscraper, his polished leather shoes tapping rhythmically against the marble floor.</p>

<p>The building itself screamed wealth and power.</p>

<p>Sleek glass walls, minimalist decor, and a quiet hum of professionalism hung in the air like perfume.</p>

<p>Without a word, Thomas approached the elevator and pressed the button.</p>

<p>He didn&#x27;t need to ask for directions. He already knew exactly where he was going.</p>

<p>As the elevator rose higher and higher, the numbers ticking up on the digital display, Thomas took a moment to smooth down his lapels and glance at his reflection in the mirror-like surface of the elevator doors.</p>

<p>Piercing blue eyes stared back at him—cold, calculating, and dangerously charismatic.</p>

<p>His jawline was sharp, his features symmetrical, almost too perfect.</p>

<p>At six foot five, Thomas was a towering figure of raw, masculine power.</p>

<p>Even in a business suit, his muscles were impossible to miss.</p>

<p>He was a man built like a soldier, but dressed like a CEO. Intimidating. Seductive. Deadly.</p>

<p>Ding.</p>

<p>The elevator doors slid open with a soft chime, revealing a tastefully decorated hallway lined with frosted glass offices and elegant lighting.</p>

<p>Thomas stepped out, his heavy footsteps muffled by the plush carpeting beneath him.</p>

<p>Heads turned as he walked past—mostly women—each one caught off guard by his commanding presence.</p>

<p>Conversations slowed, eyes widened, and for a split second, the whole floor seemed to pause.</p>

<p>He arrived at his destination—a large double-door office with a silver plaque that read Ivory Fashion and Accessories. Before he could even say anything, one of the secretaries stood up abruptly and approached him showing competence and training.</p>

<p>&quot;Mr. Jones?&quot; she asked, slightly breathless. &quot;Mrs. Oakley is expecting you. Please follow me.&quot;</p>

<p>She tried to maintain a professional tone, but her eyes betrayed her.</p>

<p>They flicked up and down his frame, lingering far too long on his broad chest, his powerful arms, the confident set of his shoulders.</p>

<p>She tried to hide the way her cheeks flushed, the way her breathing subtly quickened.</p>

<p>But Thomas noticed. He always noticed.</p>

<p>&quot;Thank you,&quot; he said smoothly, his voice deep and velvety, like silk wrapped in thunder.</p>

<p>The woman nearly stumbled on her heels.</p>

<p>It wasn&#x27;t just his looks—it was the way he carried himself, the way his voice wrapped around you like a lover&#x27;s touch.</p>

<p>Every word he spoke felt like it was meant for you and you alone. It was overwhelming, disarming.</p>

<p>Dangerous.</p>

<p>As she led him through the office, past a row of glass-walled conference rooms and elegantly dressed staff, more eyes followed.</p>

<p>Murmurs spread behind cupped hands. Who was this man? What did he want with Mrs. Oakley?</p>

<p>Thomas didn&#x27;t care. Let them stare. Let them wonder.</p>

<p>He had come here for a reason.</p>

<p>And behind the next door… was the first piece of Ross Oakley&#x27;s empire he planned to destroy.</p>

<p>Thomas stepped into the office, and the doors behind him shut with a quiet, almost reverent click.</p>

<p>The sound echoed faintly in the quiet room, sealing him inside a space that exuded quiet power and grace.</p>

<p>At first glance, the room was elegant—tastefully furnished with dark woods and warm earth tones, the walls lined with framed degrees and minimalist art.</p>

<p>A large window behind the desk flooded the space with golden afternoon light, casting soft shadows across the floor.</p>

<p>But Thomas wasn&#x27;t paying attention to any of that.</p>

<p>What hit him first wasn&#x27;t the sight of the woman sitting at the desk, though she was undeniably beautiful, her head bowed as she focused on a stack of papers.</p>

<p>No—what struck him was the scent.</p>
