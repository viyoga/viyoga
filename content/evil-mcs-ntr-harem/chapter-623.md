<p>Thomas wasn&#x27;t a fool.</p>

<p>He&#x27;d seen enough death to know when it was creeping up behind him, whispering cold promises in his ear.</p>

<p>He turned and ran without hesitation, instincts overriding pride, training overriding fear. Survival first—questions later.</p>

<p>But he didn&#x27;t get far.</p>

<p>He had barely taken his third step when his body stopped. Just… stopped. It wasn&#x27;t fatigue.</p>

<p>It wasn&#x27;t injury. It was as though invisible chains had wrapped around his limbs mid-motion and pulled tight.</p>

<p>He tried again—his mind screamed, muscles flexed—but it was like trying to push a mountain with bare hands.</p>

<p>Immovable. Absolute. Panic fluttered in his chest, but he crushed it down, forcing himself to stay composed. Struggling was useless now.</p>

<p>So he did the only thing he could—he turned his eyes, slowly, carefully, back to the monster behind him.</p>

<p>And that was what Ross had become. No longer a man.</p>

<p>No longer someone Thomas could rationalize with or even hate properly. He wasn&#x27;t dealing with a rival. He wasn&#x27;t dealing with a human being anymore.</p>

<p>He was facing something far beyond that. Something that had shed its humanity and put on its skin like a costume.</p>

<p>&quot;What are you?&quot; Thomas asked, his voice steady and cold despite the dread crawling through him. He wouldn&#x27;t give Ross the satisfaction of hearing fear. No, not yet. Maybe not ever.</p>

<p>Ross&#x27;s grin stretched wider, a twisted parody of amusement.</p>

<p>He stepped closer, hands behind his back like a gentleman about to deliver a lecture—though the glint in his eyes promised anything but kindness.</p>

<p>&quot;I&#x27;m Ross,&quot; he said with a mock shrug, as if that explained everything. &quot;As you&#x27;ve known. For a very long time.&quot;</p>

<p>Then his tone shifted—sharper, laced with venom.</p>

<p>&quot;And you… you&#x27;re Thomas Trump. The Casanova. The man who tried to crown me with a green hat. Bold of you.&quot;</p>

<p>Thomas&#x27;s jaw clenched.</p>

<p>Ross had uncovered his true identity with nothing more than a glance, a feat that spoke volumes about the terrifying reach of his intelligence network.</p>

<p>It wasn&#x27;t something to be taken lightly. After all, only the highest-ranking officials in the U.S. government—those buried deep within the most clandestine circles—were even aware of his existence.</p>

<p>No one else. Not the military. Not the public. Just the topmost echelons of power.</p>

<p>Ross took another step forward, now well within arm&#x27;s reach, though Thomas remained paralyzed, floating like a lifeless doll. Ross circled him slowly, studying him with unsettling interest.</p>

<p>&quot;Look at you,&quot; Ross muttered, voice almost reverent. &quot;Handsome. Charming. That effortless smirk. I get it now. I finally get it.&quot;</p>

<p>He paused in front of Thomas again, gaze intense.</p>

<p>&quot;The daughters of Eve… they couldn&#x27;t help themselves. You had them all wrapped around your finger. The pretty boy with the devil&#x27;s smile.&quot;</p>

<p>Ross leaned in close, his breath brushing Thomas&#x27;s ear.</p>

<p>&quot;You were born lucky. Silver spoon. Natural talent. Women fell into your bed like it was gravity. And yet… you just had to touch what was mine.&quot;</p>

<p>Thomas remained silent, breathing slow, measured. He couldn&#x27;t afford to show weakness.</p>

<p>Ross pulled back slightly, eyes gleaming.</p>

<p>&quot;Ohhh… I&#x27;m going to enjoy this.&quot;</p>

<p>He reached out and gently traced a finger down Thomas&#x27;s cheek, mockingly tender.</p>

<p>&quot;I&#x27;m going to make this pretty face cry rivers of blood. I&#x27;ll carve regret into every inch of it. And when it&#x27;s all over, when you&#x27;re begging for it to stop…&quot; He paused, licking his lips. &quot;Then I&#x27;ll really start.&quot;</p>

<p>With a wave of his hand, Ross commanded the air itself.</p>

<p>Thomas, still frozen, was lifted from the ground, hovering like a marionette, helpless as his body began to drift forward, following Ross like a lifeless puppet.</p>

<p>There was no resistance left. No control.</p>

<p>The laws of physics had no meaning here. Whatever Ross was—whatever he&#x27;d become—he had transcended the realm of ordinary monsters.</p>

<p>And Thomas?</p>

<p>He was nothing more than a canvas for Ross&#x27;s masterpiece of vengeance.</p>

<p>The hallway stretched before them like a tunnel of silence.</p>

<p>Each step Ross took echoed softly, measured and deliberate, the sound swallowed by the thick air around them.</p>

<p>He moved like he belonged here—not as a guest, but as a sovereign reclaiming territory.</p>

<p>His hands were tucked casually into his coat pockets, his posture relaxed, composed, and entirely unconcerned.</p>

<p>Trailing behind him was Thomas.</p>

<p>Frozen mid-stride, his limbs unmoving, his body suspended a few feet off the ground as though the laws of gravity had been rewritten just for him.</p>

<p>His eyes darted wildly, the only part of him that could move. He was helpless—reduced to a floating puppet—and the humiliation of it twisted his insides like a knife.</p>

<p>They passed door after door, flickering overhead lights casting long shadows across the tiled floor.</p>

<p>The entire facility, once a hive of armed personnel and locked-down protocols, now felt more like a tomb.</p>

<p>And then came the surveillance room.</p>

<p>It should&#x27;ve been filled with techies, voices calling out alerts, the whirring of cameras rotating across monitors. Instead, there was only emptiness.</p>

<p>The swivel chairs sat still. The walls, soundproofed for tactical coordination, now echoed faint static from abandoned headsets.</p>

<p>Thomas&#x27;s gaze darted toward the empty stations, horror growing in his chest.</p>

<p>They were gone—all of them. No blood. No sounds. No struggle. Just… gone.</p>

<p>No one would ever know how Brandon had had his feast here.</p>

<p>The surveillance team—the first line of defense—were now tucked safely inside his boundless, gluttonous belly.</p>

<p>Their final screams had been muffled by walls and flesh alike. No evidence at all that they existed.</p>

<p>A chill ran through Thomas.</p>

<p>&quot;Release me, Ross!&quot; he barked, desperation sharpening his voice.</p>

<p>&quot;You&#x27;re not walking away from this! Everything is recorded! My bosses will have seen it by now—they&#x27;ll be here any minute!&quot;</p>

<p>Ross didn&#x27;t even look back.</p>

<p>&quot;….&quot;</p>

<p>Nothing. Silence. Not even a glance of acknowledgment.</p>

<p>Thomas clenched his jaw. &quot;You&#x27;re digging your own grave. I swear to God—people are coming. They&#x27;re armed. They&#x27;ll put you down like the freak you are.&quot;</p>

<p>Still no response. It was like talking to a wall.</p>
