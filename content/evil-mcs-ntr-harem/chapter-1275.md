<p>The door of the lead carriage slowly opened. A middle-aged man stepped out.</p>

<p>His expression was calm, yet his eyes were sharp and imposing.</p>

<p>Years of commanding armies had forged an invisible pressure around him.</p>

<p>This was General Lei Jun. The most powerful military figure within thousands of miles.</p>

<p>A younger man exited the carriage shortly afterward.</p>

<p>His features resembled the general’s somewhat, indicating a close relationship.</p>

<p>Father and son.</p>

<p>Both carried themselves with the bearing of seasoned warriors.</p>

<p>Their backs were straight as arrows. Their steps were steady and firm.</p>

<p>To the spectators, it almost looked as though they were advancing onto a battlefield rather than entering a private residence.</p>

<p>Every movement attracted attention. Every step caused countless hearts to tense.</p>

<p>The two men eventually arrived before the castle gates.</p>

<p>Standing guard at the entrance was a young man whom General Lei Jun had never seen before.</p>

<p>The guard wore simple clothing, but his posture was disciplined.</p>

<p>General Lei Jun stopped before him. The younger man beside him remained silent.</p>

<p>The surrounding Golden Armor Legion also halted immediately.</p>

<p>Ten thousand soldiers became motionless at once. The scene was incredibly imposing.</p>

<p>General Lei Jun looked toward the castle entrance before speaking.</p>

<p>&quot;Please inform Master Ross Oakley that I am here to speak with him.&quot;</p>

<p>His tone was polite. Far more polite than many people had expected. The guard immediately straightened.</p>

<p>&quot;I’ll do that, General! Please wait a moment!&quot;</p>

<p>The young man sounded nervous.</p>

<p>After all, the person standing before him was the famous General Lei Jun.</p>

<p>Without wasting another second, he turned around and practically ran into the castle to deliver the message.</p>

<p>Meanwhile, General Lei Jun calmly waited outside. His expression remained composed.</p>

<p>However, countless hidden spies nearly lost their minds from excitement.</p>

<p>The meeting they had all been waiting for was finally about to happen.</p>

<p>On one side stood General Lei Jun and his ten thousand elite soldiers.</p>

<p>On the other stood the mysterious Ross Oakley, who had erased the Ji Clan overnight.</p>

<p>Three minutes later, the guard returned.</p>

<p>However, the expression on his face was somewhat awkward. General Lei Jun immediately noticed it.</p>

<p>The guard hesitated briefly before speaking.</p>

<p>&quot;General... a wife of Master Ross Oakley informed me that Master Ross will only receive you after three hours. He is currently preoccupied with other matters.&quot;</p>

<p>The words caused the younger man’s eyebrows to twitch.</p>

<p>Three hours?</p>

<p>Making a general wait for three hours was an insult in many places.</p>

<p>The guard himself was already preparing for the general to become angry.</p>

<p>After all, Lei Jun commanded armies numbering in the hundreds of thousands.</p>

<p>His authority within the region was immense.</p>

<p>Countless clan leaders would personally rush out to greet him the moment he arrived.</p>

<p>Yet to his surprise, none of that happened. General Lei Jun merely nodded.</p>

<p>&quot;I see.&quot;</p>

<p>His expression remained completely calm.</p>

<p>&quot;That’s fine. We will wait.&quot;</p>

<p>The guard blinked in surprise.</p>

<p>Truthfully, the general had expected something far worse.</p>

<p>A mysterious expert capable of wiping out the Ji Clan overnight was not someone who would care much about military authority or worldly status.</p>

<p>Compared to being outright refused, waiting three hours was already a favorable outcome.</p>

<p>&quot;Please follow me, General.&quot;</p>

<p>The guard respectfully gestured toward the castle interior.</p>

<p>General Lei Jun and the young man followed him without complaint.</p>

<p>Soon, they arrived at a spacious reception hall. The room was lavishly decorated yet surprisingly elegant.</p>

<p>Rare paintings adorned the walls. Ancient spiritual plants emitted refreshing fragrances.</p>

<p>The furniture alone was worth a fortune. The two men were invited to sit.</p>

<p>Servants quickly arrived with various delicacies. Fine wine. Rare spiritual fruits.</p>

<p>Exquisite dishes prepared by master chefs. Everything was presented with impeccable hospitality.</p>

<p>Yet neither General Lei Jun nor his son touched any of it. They simply sat quietly and waited.</p>

<p>The younger man occasionally glanced toward his father.</p>

<p>&quot;Father, is this really necessary?&quot;</p>

<p>He transmitted through divine sense. General Lei Jun calmly took a sip of tea before replying.</p>

<p>&quot;If a man can erase the Ji Clan overnight, then waiting three hours is hardly a difficult task.&quot;</p>

<p>The younger man immediately fell silent.</p>

<p>The truth was that even now, nobody knew what had happened to the Ji Clan.</p>

<p>There were no corpses. The entire clan had simply vanished from existence.</p>

<p>That mystery alone was enough to make any intelligent person cautious. Thus, the two continued waiting.</p>

<p>One hour passed.</p>

<p>Then two.</p>

<p>Then three.</p>

<p>Throughout the entire period, General Lei Jun displayed extraordinary patience.</p>

<p>Not once did he complain. Not once did he show dissatisfaction.</p>

<p>Finally, exactly three hours later, the familiar guard returned.</p>

<p>He bowed respectfully.</p>

<p>&quot;Master Ross Oakley will see you now, General. Please follow me.&quot;</p>

<p>General Lei Jun immediately stood.</p>

<p>&quot;So the time has come.&quot;</p>

<p>The guard nodded before leading the pair deeper into the castle.</p>

<p>The further they walked, the quieter the surroundings became.</p>

<p>After about a minute of walking, they finally arrived at a large courtyard.</p>

<p>There, seated comfortably beneath a pavilion, was a young man.</p>

<p>Ross Oakley.</p>

<p>The moment General Lei Jun and his son laid eyes upon him, both men froze.</p>

<p>Shock flashed across their faces. Not because Ross appeared terrifying.</p>

<p>Not because he radiated overwhelming pressure. Quite the opposite. Ross looked completely ordinary.</p>

<p>In their senses, he appeared no different from a mortal who had never cultivated a single day in his life.</p>

<p>There was no spiritual pressure. No cultivation fluctuations. No indication of strength whatsoever.</p>

<p>Nothing.</p>

<p>General Lei Jun’s eyes narrowed slightly. His son was equally stunned. This was impossible.</p>

<p>Both men possessed considerable cultivation. Yet neither could perceive the slightest hint of Ross’s true strength.</p>

<p>There were only two possibilities.</p>

<p>Either Ross was genuinely a powerless mortal.</p>

<p>Or his cultivation had reached a level so far beyond theirs that they could not even begin to comprehend it.</p>

<p>Neither man was foolish enough to believe the first possibility.</p>

<p>After all, the events of the previous night still echoed throughout Hidden River Fortress.</p>

<p>The Ji Clan had been obliterated.</p>

<p>An entire great clan had disappeared without a trace. And Ross Oakley was responsible.</p>

<p>That fact alone was enough to dismiss any notion that he was an ordinary mortal.</p>

<p>General Lei Jun immediately became even more cautious.</p>

<p>The more harmless Ross appeared, the more dangerous he seemed.</p>

<p>Because only truly terrifying experts could stand before others while appearing completely ordinary.</p>

<p>As the father and son approached, neither dared underestimate the young man sitting before them.</p>

<p>Not even for a moment.</p>
