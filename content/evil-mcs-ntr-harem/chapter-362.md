<p>&quot;Do you want something, Dr. Rose? Do you even know what time it is?&quot; Ross asked, glancing at the unknown number on his phone.</p>

<p>&quot;It&#x27;s Christmas Eve. I know. But I need you—I need you now,&quot; Dr. Rose said, her voice tinged with panic.</p>

<p>&quot;For what? I don&#x27;t think we&#x27;re close enough for you to be asking favors, Dr. Rose. I&#x27;m hanging up, so say your piece quickly,&quot; Ross replied coldly.</p>

<p>&quot;Wait! I&#x27;ll owe you a big favor. Just help me this once!&quot; Dr. Rose&#x27;s panic was palpable now.</p>

<p>&quot;A big favor? Unless it involves you sleeping with me, I&#x27;m out,&quot; Ross said, his tone dripping with indifference. He waited for three breaths, then hung up.</p>

<p>Ring. Ring. Ring.</p>

<p>Five breaths later, just as Ross had expected, the phone rang again.</p>

<p>Ross picked up the call again but didn&#x27;t utter a single word. He leaned back in his chair, letting the silence stretch as he waited for Dr. Rose to speak first. It didn&#x27;t take long.</p>

<p>&quot;One night. I&#x27;ll agree to one night with you,&quot; Dr. Rose finally said, her voice tight with anger. Yet beneath the edge of her words, the unmistakable tremor of desperation betrayed her true emotions.</p>

<p>Ross smirked, though his tone remained cold and calculating.</p>

<p>&quot;One night? That&#x27;s all your father&#x27;s life is worth to you? One single night?&quot; He let the question hang in the air for a moment before continuing.</p>

<p>Dr. Rose inhaled sharply, the weight of his demand rendering her momentarily speechless.</p>

<p>The line went quiet, but Ross waited, his confidence unshaken. He could practically hear her internal struggle.</p>

<p>&quot;How... How do you even know about my father?&quot; she finally asked, her voice low and cautious.</p>

<p>&quot;I didn&#x27;t tell anyone. I only got your number from Alvin.&quot;</p>

<p>Ross chuckled softly, the sound carrying an almost predatory quality.</p>

<p>&quot;Let&#x27;s just say I know more than most. About you. About your father. About everything that matters.&quot;</p>

<p>She swallowed hard, gripping the phone tightly. His words, his tone—it was like dealing with a force of nature.</p>

<p>An unfathomable mystery.</p>

<p>She wanted to demand answers, to figure out how he could possibly know about her father&#x27;s sudden heart attack when even the hospital staff had barely been informed.</p>

<p>But deep down, she knew she wouldn&#x27;t get anything from him. Ross wasn&#x27;t the type to explain himself.</p>

<p>Dr. Rose&#x27;s mind raced. She hated the position she was in, hated that she needed his help. Yet, no one else could do what Ross could.</p>

<p>The thought of losing her father terrified her, and if giving in to Ross was the price, then so be it.</p>

<p>&quot;Fine,&quot; she said at last, her voice trembling but resolute.</p>

<p>&quot;Come to the public hospital. Right now.&quot;</p>

<p>Ross&#x27;s smirk deepened, though she couldn&#x27;t see it.</p>

<p>&quot;That&#x27;s more like it,&quot; he said before the call ended abruptly.</p>

<p>Dr. Rose stared at the phone in her hand, her heart pounding. She hated him, hated herself, hated the whole situation.</p>

<p>But there was no time for regret. She had made her choice, and now, all she could do was hope it wouldn&#x27;t destroy her completely.</p>

<p>&quot;Jackpot.&quot; Ross grinned, then left the place without a backward glance. He left a clone behind to keep the party going with the rest of his harem.</p>

<p>Of course, Ross wouldn&#x27;t trust just any clone to handle things where it truly mattered.</p>

<p>None of his clones were allowed to make love to his wives—that was a privilege reserved solely for his true self.</p>

<p>&quot;Better make this quick,&quot; Ross muttered as he teleported to the hospital. Space and time bent to his will, the concepts themselves nothing more than tools at his disposal.</p>

<p>In the blink of an eye, he arrived, already dressed in attire suited for the occasion. He strode through the hospital corridors and stopped at the door of a private room, knocking firmly.</p>

<p>&quot;Come in,&quot; a woman&#x27;s voice called from inside.</p>

<p>Ross pushed the door open and stepped in, his gaze sweeping across the room.</p>

<p>There were three occupants: Dr. Rose Yates, a middle-aged woman who was clearly her mother, and an older man lying unconscious in the hospital bed—Rose&#x27;s father.</p>

<p>&quot;Hello, Mrs. Yates. I&#x27;m Ross, your daughter&#x27;s boyfriend,&quot; Ross said with a charming smile as he approached the older woman, extending his hand for a shake.</p>

<p>Mrs. Yates raised an eyebrow in mild surprise but took his hand gracefully.</p>

<p>Ross&#x27;s eyes briefly widened as he felt the unexpected strength in her handshake.</p>

<p>&quot;Oh? That&#x27;s a surprise. I thought Edward was…&quot; Mrs. Yates trailed off, her tone slightly pointed as she released Ross&#x27;s hand.</p>

<p>&quot;He&#x27;s old news, Mrs. Yates,&quot; Ross said smoothly. &quot;I&#x27;m just here to offer my support to the family. I have no doubt Mr. Yates will recover fully and be back on his feet before the New Year.&quot;</p>

<p>Ross walked over to Dr. Rose, planting a light kiss on her cheek, then turned his attention to the man in the bed.</p>

<p>Without hesitation, he reached out and placed a hand briefly on the patient&#x27;s arm.</p>

<p>The touch was fleeting, but Dr. Rose noticed it, her heart screaming silently for a miracle.</p>

<p>She prayed her father would wake up, healthy and whole, just as Ross had promised. But the room remained quiet, her father still motionless.</p>

<p>&quot;So, Ross,&quot; Mrs. Yates began, breaking the silence. &quot;What can you tell me about yourself? You seem a little young for my d—&quot;</p>

<p>Mrs. Yates began small talk.</p>

<p>They chatted for thirty minutes, Ross navigating the conversation with his usual charm.</p>

<p>Mrs. Yates was polite but guarded, though Ross managed to win a few genuine smiles from her as the minutes passed.</p>

<p>Then, suddenly—</p>

<p>&quot;Hmmm…&quot;</p>

<p>Dr. Rose&#x27;s eyes widened, her hands flying to her mouth as her father stirred, his eyelids fluttering open.</p>

<p>&quot;Dad!&quot; she gasped, rushing to his side, tears spilling freely down her cheeks.</p>

<p>Mrs. Yates clasped her hands together, her elegant composure giving way to relief and gratitude as she whispered a heartfelt, &quot;Thank God.&quot;</p>

<p>Ross watched the scene with a knowing smile, his arms crossed.</p>

<p>&quot;Told you,&quot; he said softly, his voice barely audible over the cries of joy in the room.</p>
