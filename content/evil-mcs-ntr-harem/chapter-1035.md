<p>Ashley gasped as she read the message, her heart pounding so hard she feared it might burst from her chest.</p>

<p>Without a second thought, she grabbed a jacket to throw over her long robe, though it barely did enough to cover her trembling body.</p>

<p>She rushed out of her room, her bare feet barely making a sound on the floor as adrenaline propelled her forward.</p>

<p>A man was waiting for her in the corridor, standing silently as if he had known she would come.</p>

<p>Without a word, he led her through the winding hallways, guiding her with quiet precision toward Ross’s room.</p>

<p>Ashley’s breath came in ragged gasps, half-run, half-walk, each step making her robe shift and cling in ways that left little to the imagination.</p>

<p>Her chest heaved noticeably, her heart racing as much from urgency as from desire, and she couldn’t help the way her body betrayed her anxiety.</p>

<p>Finally, they arrived. The door opened, and Ashley stepped inside, eyes wide, trying to steady herself.</p>

<p>Ross was there, waiting, calm, and impossibly composed.</p>

<p>The sight of him made her heart skip again—partly from longing, partly from fear of what was to come.</p>

<p>She felt tears prick her eyes, hope and fear warring within her.</p>

<p>&quot;Ross... is it true? Did you... really have news about my husband, Lyric?&quot; Her voice wavered, heavy with the weight of disbelief and desperate hope.</p>

<p>Ross let out a slow, dramatic breath, as though savoring the moment before speaking.</p>

<p>His gaze softened, yet carried a weight that made Ashley’s pulse hammer in her chest.</p>

<p>He could have acted on the undeniable opportunity in the room, could have taken her right there, letting desire rule over reason. But he didn’t.</p>

<p>He loved her too much as Lyric to hurt her like that. Definitely not in any way that would shatter her.</p>

<p>Instead, he chose patience, a deliberate approach that would leave its mark more subtly, equally painful, yet ultimately more gentle compared to its alternative.</p>

<p>&quot;Sit,&quot; he finally said, his voice gentle but commanding, pulling her gaze from his eyes to the firmness of his posture, the quiet strength in his presence.</p>

<p>&quot;We need to talk... but you need to be ready for what I’m about to tell you.&quot;</p>

<p>Ashley nodded.</p>

<p>&quot;Tell me, Ross... where is my husband?&quot; Ashley asked, her voice trembling yet laced with determination.</p>

<p>Each word came out sharp, defiant, as if forcing herself to face the impossible.</p>

<p>&quot;Please... sit down first, Ashley,&quot; Ross said gently, his tone carrying a calm authority that made her hesitate only for a moment.</p>

<p>She complied and sank into the nearest chair, her fingers clutching the armrest like a lifeline.</p>

<p>Her heart was racing, pounding so hard she could feel it in her throat.</p>

<p>The air felt thick, heavy with dread.</p>

<p>Ross picked up a remote control and pressed a button.</p>

<p>The massive flat-screen on the wall flickered to life. At first, the image was blurred, grainy, but then it slowly sharpened, focusing on a man.</p>

<p>Dust and grime coated his face, and a streak of dried blood marred the corner of his lips.</p>

<p>Even through the filth, even through the obvious suffering, Ashley’s breath caught—Lyric was still undeniably handsome.</p>

<p>There was a quiet strength in his gaze, a stubborn resilience that shone through his pain.</p>

<p>&quot;Ashley... I’m sorry,&quot; Lyric’s voice was hoarse, raw, yet full of emotion. &quot;I... I can’t come home. I tried... but I was a fool. Trying to be a hero, trying to save everyone... I was unlucky.&quot;</p>

<p>He lifted his arm into view.</p>

<p>Deep bite marks raked across it, flesh torn and missing, blackened at the edges as it began to rot.</p>

<p>The wounds had stopped bleeding, but the damage was horrifying, a grotesque reminder of his ordeal.</p>

<p>Ashley’s stomach churned at the sight, a mixture of fear, revulsion, and heartbreak knotting inside her.</p>

<p>&quot;I... I know my boss will send you this message. I couldn’t leave without saying goodbye...&quot; His voice faltered, breaking into a whisper that made Ashley lean forward instinctively.</p>

<p>&quot;I led a good and meaningful life. The happiest moments... were the ones I spent with you. I have no regrets... only that I couldn’t spend more time with you and Daniel.&quot;</p>

<p>Lyric’s eyes, though weary, shone with memories.</p>

<p>&quot;I remember when... the first time I held you in my arms. The first time we laughed together without a care in the world. Every small moment we shared—it gave my life meaning. I love you, Ashley... always have, always will. I wish... I could have stayed with you, seen Daniel get married someday, walked by your side...&quot;</p>

<p>His voice wavered, cracking as he recounted memories, laughter and tears mixing in the cadence of his words.</p>

<p>He spoke of quiet evenings, of fleeting touches, of unspoken promises.</p>

<p>Time and again, he professed his love, each repetition heavier than the last, until his throat tightened, his voice failing him.</p>

<p>Then came a sound that made Ashley shiver uncontrollably—a low, deep growl.</p>

<p>It was primal, inhuman, like a beast that had gone a million years without sustenance.</p>

<p>The phone couldn’t capture the full depth of it, but the vibrations reached her chest, rattling her very soul.</p>

<p>The growl didn’t just speak of hunger—it spoke of something ancient, relentless, and terrifying that was hunting him... or something he was now part of.</p>

<p>Ashley felt tears slide down her cheeks, burning her skin, as her mind reeled.</p>

<p>The man she loved, the father of her child, had endured horrors she could barely imagine.</p>

<p>He had fought, suffered, and survived—just barely—but now he was out of reach, trapped in a nightmare she couldn’t even begin to penetrate.</p>

<p>Ross watched silently from the side, his face unreadable, but there was a tension in his posture, a controlled patience that spoke of plans Ashley could not yet understand.</p>

<p>He didn’t interrupt, didn’t try to console her. He knew she needed to feel the weight of the truth, to confront it fully, before anything could be done.</p>

<p>As for Ashley, it felt like her heart got broken into pieces.</p>
