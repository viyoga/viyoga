<p>Ross rose from his chair and looked out to the ceiling and let his divine sense roam onto the ruined cityscape.</p>

<p>The morning light cut through the clouds, spilling across the desolate streets and shattered buildings.</p>

<p>To most, it would’ve been a depressing sight—evidence of civilization’s downfall.</p>

<p>But to Ross, it looked like opportunity.</p>

<p>A grin spread slowly across his face.</p>

<p>Even from this faraway distance, he could already sense it—movement in the streets, signs of small survivor groups gathering supplies, fighting over scraps.</p>

<p>To him, they were not obstacles, but possibilities. Some could be allies, others enemies, and maybe a few... entertainment.</p>

<p>The world beyond his bunker was untamed and dangerous, but that only made it more appealing.</p>

<p>He thrived in chaos.</p>

<p>It was where he could truly stretch his power, test his limits, and remind the world who he was.</p>

<p>Ashley’s situation had been a pleasant distraction, something personal and grounding amid the madness.</p>

<p>But now that she was firmly under his wing, Ross’s focus turned once again to the wider stage.</p>

<p>The apocalypse had stripped away rules, governments, and order—and in its place, men like him could rise without restraint.</p>

<p>He adjusted his jacket, his eyes narrowing slightly as a distant explosion echoed through the streets.</p>

<p>&quot;Looks like things are finally getting interesting,&quot; he muttered with a smirk.</p>

<p>Behind him, the bunker was quiet—safe, secure, and full of people who trusted him.</p>

<p>But Ross Oakley had never been content with just survival. He wanted more—adventure, dominance, excitement.</p>

<p>And outside that door, the world was waiting for him to take it.</p>

<p>***</p>

<p>&quot;I need to go find us some food, honey. Otherwise, we won’t last another month,&quot; the woman said firmly, brushing a streak of dirt from her face.</p>

<p>Her jeans were torn and caked in mud, and the white T-shirt she wore was grimy and sweat-stained from days of survival.</p>

<p>Every movement she made radiated determination, even in the face of the chaos outside.</p>

<p>The man slumped in a chair, his body battered and weak.</p>

<p>One leg was wrapped in makeshift bandages, his torn clothing barely shielding him from the chill.</p>

<p>&quot;You can’t go,&quot; he said, his voice strained and shaky. &quot;It’s too dangerous out there, Miku.&quot;</p>

<p>Her hands went to her hips, and she turned to face him fully.</p>

<p>&quot;I don’t have a choice,&quot; she said, her tone steady. &quot;If we don’t find food soon, we won’t survive another week, let alone a month. I can handle it.&quot;</p>

<p>He shook his head violently, fear etching every line on his face.</p>

<p>&quot;The streets are crawling with them, Miku! You saw what happened to the Johnsons yesterday—they didn’t even stand a chance. I can’t let you go!&quot;</p>

<p>Miku stepped closer to him, her expression softening, but her resolve never wavered.</p>

<p>&quot;I know it’s dangerous. I know what’s out there. But staying here, waiting for food to appear... that won’t save us. We have to act, or we die anyway.&quot;</p>

<p>The man swallowed hard, his chest heaving.</p>

<p>He wanted to argue, to beg, to somehow stop her—but he knew deep down that she was right.</p>

<p>In this world, hesitation was death. Every day was a gamble, and they couldn’t afford to let fear rule them.</p>

<p>&quot;I’ll be careful,&quot; Miku said, voice calm but firm.</p>

<p>&quot;I won’t take unnecessary risks. I’ll watch every corner, every shadow. And I’ll be back before dark.&quot; She adjusted the straps of her worn backpack, checking to make sure the small knife at her belt was secure.</p>

<p>It wasn’t much, but it was all she had to defend herself.</p>

<p>The man reached out, gripping her arm for a brief moment, his eyes filled with desperation.</p>

<p>&quot;Just... promise me you’ll come back.&quot;</p>

<p>Miku squeezed his hand, giving him a reassuring smile. &quot;I promise. I always do.&quot;</p>

<p>With one last glance around the room, she stepped toward the door.</p>

<p>Outside, the world was silent, almost unnervingly so.</p>

<p>The streets were littered with abandoned cars, shattered windows, and the occasional distant groan of the infected.</p>

<p>Every shadow seemed alive, every corner hiding a new threat.</p>

<p>Yet, despite the danger, Miku walked with purpose, her every step deliberate.</p>

<p>Behind her, the man watched from the cracked doorway, his heart pounding.</p>

<p>He knew she was strong, resourceful, and brave—but in this world, even the strongest could fall.</p>

<p>He could only hope her skills, instincts, and sheer determination would be enough.</p>

<p>As Miku disappeared into the ruined streets, the man leaned back against the wall, closing his eyes for a moment.</p>

<p>Survival was no longer just about strength—it was about cunning, timing, and the willingness to take risks.</p>

<p>And Miku... she had all of that.</p>

<p>Miku didn’t head for the obvious places like supermarkets or corner stores.</p>

<p>Everyone who tried that was already dead—or worse. She’d seen it with her own eyes: the moment someone broke through those glass doors, dozens of zombies would come rushing in from every direction, drawn by the sound and scent.</p>

<p>No, she wasn’t going to make the same mistake.</p>

<p>Instead, she chose the quieter route—her neighborhood.</p>

<p>The gated community had once been peaceful, filled with laughter, barking dogs, and weekend barbecues.</p>

<p>Now, it was a silent graveyard.</p>

<p>Cars sat abandoned in driveways, doors left hanging open, and the smell of decay floated faintly in the air.</p>

<p>Her husband’s voice echoed in her head: &quot;Don’t go, Miku. It’s too dangerous.&quot;</p>

<p>But what choice did she have? He couldn’t move.</p>

<p>His leg was infected, and their supplies had run out two days ago.</p>

<p>If she didn’t bring something back soon, they’d both starve.</p>

<p>She adjusted the strap of her small backpack and crouched low behind a fallen gate.</p>

<p>&quot;Brrrr...&quot;</p>

<p>The sound came from somewhere nearby—a low, gurgling moan that made her heart freeze.</p>

<p>She peeked carefully around the corner and saw them: a dozen zombies wandering aimlessly along the street.</p>

<p>Their movements were slow and uneven, like broken puppets.</p>

<p>Their eyes were blank, their skin gray and peeling.</p>

<p>One of them dragged a half-torn bag of groceries, spilling rotten fruit across the pavement.</p>
