<p>Capítulo 1115: Chapter 1115 Camera</p>

<p>Ace inhaled sharply. Miku stiffened.</p>

<p>One of the newcomers—Avery—pressed back tears but held them in, determined not to appear weak.</p>

<p>Giana finally looked at Ross again, guilt and desperation mixing in her eyes.</p>

<p>“I’m truly sorry, Ross. We were out of options. We didn’t know where else to go. We didn’t mean to bring danger to your home.”</p>

<p>Silence settled—thick, suffocating, everyone waiting for Ross’s reaction.</p>

<p>Would he kick them out?</p>

<p>Would he scold them?</p>

<p>Would he refuse to involve himself?</p>

<p>Would he see them as burdens… liabilities?</p>

<p>But Ross simply stood there, unreadable, observing them with those calm, piercing eyes—</p>

<p>—and for the first time since they arrived, all five women understood one thing very clearly:</p>

<p>They had entered the home of someone far more dangerous than anything that chased them.</p>

<p>“It doesn’t matter,” Ross said, waving away their panic as though it were nothing more than dust in the air.</p>

<p>His presence alone steadied the room.</p>

<p>“Since you’re already here, you might as well stay. As for those who are after you…” His eyes hardened, a quiet storm gathering behind them.</p>

<p>“I’ll take care of them when they come.”</p>

<p>A ripple of relief mixed with fear passed among the women.</p>

<p>“But right now,” Ross continued, his tone softening again, “the most important thing is treating Libby.”</p>

<p>His gaze settled on the blonde girl.</p>

<p>Libby flinched slightly under the attention—not because she feared him, but because she felt exposed.</p>

<p>She was young, barely eighteen, her beauty still in that delicate stage between innocence and maturity.</p>

<p>Even with sweat on her brow and pain distorting her features, she was stunning.</p>

<p>Her golden hair glued to her cheeks, her breathing shallow, her hands pressing on her wounded abdomen.</p>

<p>Ross smiled at her—calm, almost tender.</p>

<p>“Lie down on the sofa, Libby. I’ll treat your wounds.”</p>

<p>Libby swallowed, fear and hope mixing in her eyes. She glanced toward her friends.</p>

<p>Giana gave her a reassuring nod, her lips tight with worry. The others stepped aside, making space.</p>

<p>Libby took a shaky breath and limped toward the sofa, each step slow and painful.</p>

<p>Her knees trembled as she lowered herself down, hugging the edge of the cushions as though afraid she might sink through them.</p>

<p>A soft groan escaped her lips.</p>

<p>Ross moved behind her, pulling a wooden chair and setting it beside the sofa.</p>

<p>He sat down smoothly, like someone who had done this a thousand times.</p>

<p>The room grew quiet—so quiet that the faint drip of water somewhere in the basement echoed like a drum.</p>

<p>He placed both hands gently over Libby’s wound. His palms were warm, almost soothing.</p>

<p>Libby’s breath hitched, but she did not pull away.</p>

<p>Ross closed his eyes.</p>

<p>“No matter what happens, Libby,” he murmured, voice steady and low, “don’t move. Understand?”</p>

<p>“Y-Yes…” she whispered.</p>

<p>Her hands clenched the sides of the sofa.</p>

<p>Ross inhaled deeply—once, twice—and then began.</p>

<p>The first change came subtly: a soft hum, almost like the vibration of metal struck gently.</p>

<p>Then light began to gather around his hands.</p>

<p>Faint at first, barely visible… then brighter, a soft green glow blooming like fireflies waking at dusk.</p>

<p>A collective breath sucked in.</p>

<p>“H-How is he doing that?” someone whispered.</p>

<p>“What is that light…?”</p>

<p>“Is that his ability from the heart stone?”</p>

<p>Ross’s jaw tightened. Sweat beaded across his forehead. His breathing grew harsher, strained.</p>

<p>The green glow intensified, swirling like mist around his fingers before sinking directly into Libby’s flesh.</p>

<p>Libby gasped, arching slightly—but Ross pressed her shoulder down with one hand.</p>

<p>“Don’t move,” he repeated, voice rough.</p>

<p>She bit her lip until it nearly bled.</p>

<p>A minute passed. Then two. The air itself felt heavier, as though drawn toward Ross and Libby.</p>

<p>The women exchanged worried glances. Even the bravest among them looked pale.</p>

<p>Then, suddenly—</p>

<p>Ross’s eyes snapped open.</p>

<p>His body jerked forward.</p>

<p>A violent choke sounded from his throat before he spat out a mouthful of dark, wet blood onto the stone floor.</p>

<p>“ROSS!!”</p>

<p>Their screams overlapped, panic exploding through the room.</p>

<p>Libby froze, trembling hysterically.</p>

<p>Giana rushed forward but stopped when Ross lifted a shaking hand, silently commanding her to stay back.</p>

<p>His hands returned to Libby’s wound, still glowing—though dimmer now, flickering like a candle in the wind.</p>

<p>More sweat dripped down his face, sliding down his temples, soaking into his shirt.</p>

<p>His breath grew ragged, each inhale a struggle.</p>

<p>“Why doesn’t he stop?!” one of the girls cried.</p>

<p>“He’s pushing himself too much!”</p>

<p>“Someoen do something!”</p>

<p>Miku stood on the side, hands clenched so tightly her knuckles turned white.</p>

<p>She knew Ross was acting. She knew he was in no real danger.</p>

<p>But the sight of him coughing blood, trembling, forcing himself to continue—it pierced something inside her.</p>

<p>The performance was too flawless, too convincing. For a moment her heart forgot reality.</p>

<p>“He… he’ll be fine,” she said softly, though her voice shook.</p>

<p>Ross didn’t look fine.</p>

<p>His face was pale, his lips slightly parted as if he was barely holding back another cough of blood.</p>

<p>His fingers trembled over Libby’s skin.</p>

<p>And still—he didn’t stop.</p>

<p>He held on as though Libby’s life depended on it.</p>

<p>The women watched with wide eyes, caught between awe and terror, their breaths held, their hearts pounding in their chests.</p>

<p>Ross leaned closer, sweat dripping from his chin onto the sofa.</p>

<p>The glow pulsed again—stronger this time—and a faint, sizzling sound echoed through the room as the light seeped deeper into Libby’s wound.</p>

<p>Libby whimpered.</p>

<p>“You’re doing great,” Ross whispered weakly. “Just… hold on.”</p>

<p>Another pulse of green.</p>

<p>Another wave of strain washing over him.</p>

<p>Another moment where his body looked ready to collapse.</p>

<p>And yet, he continued—never breaking, never faltering.</p>

<p>Even if every second made him look more and more like he was paying a terrible price for saving her.</p>

<p>Five agonizing minutes crawled by.</p>

<p>Ross’s breathing had grown shallow, each exhale sounding more strained than the last.</p>

<p>The green glow around his hands dimmed until it flickered weakly—like a candle about to die.</p>

<p>Then—</p>

<p>“Hak!”</p>
