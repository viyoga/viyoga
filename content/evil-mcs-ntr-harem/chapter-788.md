<p>Chosen to give herself wholly, without hesitation. And she would do it again.</p>

<p>With her heart beating faster, Joan took a step forward, then another.</p>

<p>Her smile grew, warm and unshaken.</p>

<p>&quot;D!&quot; she called out, her voice full of sunshine and certainty.</p>

<p>Ross looked up from his conversation and spotted her instantly.</p>

<p>A smile pulled at the corner of his lips—casual, proud, possessive.</p>

<p>He didn’t need to say anything. His eyes said it all.</p>

<p>Joan was welcome here.</p>

<p>One of the women at his side glanced over her shoulder at Joan and raised a glass slightly in greeting.</p>

<p>Another gave a soft nod, almost like quiet acceptance.</p>

<p>There was no hostility, no territorial tension—just a quiet acknowledgment of what Joan now was: one of his.</p>

<p>Joan’s smile widened, her steps lighter as she made her way toward them.</p>

<p>Whatever anyone else in the house thought—whatever rumors they wanted to spin—none of it mattered anymore.</p>

<p>She had stepped into the fire and emerged changed.</p>

<p>And now, she was exactly where she wanted to be.</p>

<p>&quot;Hey, girls,&quot; Joan said softly as she stepped up to the table where Ross’s women lounged, their laughter still lingering in the air like music.</p>

<p>They all turned to look at her.</p>

<p>For a moment, there was silence—curious, slightly guarded—but then Heaven gave her a nod, Tianna patted the seat next to her, and Lily offered a faint but knowing smile.</p>

<p>Joan exhaled in relief. She sat down, feeling both nervous and strangely excited.</p>

<p>It was the first time they were truly speaking to her—not as competition, not as strangers, but as part of the same world. Part of Ross’s world.</p>

<p>As they ate and chatted, the conversation eventually shifted to last night.</p>

<p>Joan didn’t shy away.</p>

<p>In fact, she shared openly—every thought, every feeling, and what it had meant to give herself completely to Ross.</p>

<p>&quot;I’ve always known I was different,&quot; Joan began, her voice soft but steady.</p>

<p>&quot;I never felt like I wanted to lead or be the one in control. I wanted to serve... to belong to someone. To be owned.&quot;</p>

<p>The other girls listened closely.</p>

<p>&quot;I’m a submissive,&quot; she said clearly. &quot;Now, my body and life exist for D’s pleasure. Whatever he commands, I’ll obey. No hesitation.&quot;</p>

<p>There was a beat of silence.</p>

<p>&quot;So... you’re a sub?&quot; Heaven blinked, trying to process it. &quot;I thought that kind of thing only existed in books and movies.&quot;</p>

<p>Joan laughed gently. &quot;Most people think that. But it’s real. For some of us, it’s not a fantasy—it’s who we are.&quot;</p>

<p>She looked at Ross, who was watching her now with interest—and something deeper, something possessive in his gaze.</p>

<p>&quot;I’m his. Completely.&quot;</p>

<p>Ross stood, walked over without a word, and cupped Joan’s face in his hands.</p>

<p>His lips crashed onto hers with raw intensity. It wasn’t a gentle kiss—it was claiming.</p>

<p>And Joan surrendered to it, melting under his touch as the others watched with a mixture of admiration and heat in their eyes.</p>

<p>When he pulled away, he smirked. &quot;That’s a good slave,&quot; he said, voice low and rich.</p>

<p>Joan’s face glowed with pride, her cheeks flushed, and her breath unsteady.</p>

<p>Tianna looked at her for a moment, then turned to Ross. &quot;I guess I’m your slave too, D. You already own my heart. My body’s just catching up.&quot;</p>

<p>Ross raised an eyebrow, clearly amused—and pleased.</p>

<p>Lily leaned in next, her voice soft.</p>

<p>&quot;Me too. I never said it before, but... I’ve never felt safer or more wanted than I do with you. If being yours means giving everything... I’m ready.&quot;</p>

<p>Heaven hesitated for a beat, then nodded as well. &quot;I used to think I had to be strong all the time. But maybe strength is also about knowing who you want to surrender to. You already know how far I’ll go for you, D.&quot;</p>

<p>Joan looked around at the women—once so distant, now united.</p>

<p>Each of them different, each bound by their own reasons... but all of them choosing Ross.</p>

<p>Ross stood tall at the head of the table, arms crossed, a satisfied glint in his eyes.</p>

<p>His gaze moved from one woman to the next, resting on each with a kind of hungry affection, as if mentally reminding himself that each of them was his—willingly, fully.</p>

<p>&quot;My girls,&quot; he said, his voice like velvet laced with fire. &quot;Loyal. Beautiful. Mine.&quot;</p>

<p>They smiled at him—some with bashful blushes, some with fiery pride, but all with unmistakable belonging.</p>

<p>And Joan? She knew in that moment she wasn’t just accepted—she was cherished.</p>

<p>No longer alone. No longer uncertain.</p>

<p>Now, she was part of something bigger. Something raw. Something beautiful.</p>

<p>And she wouldn’t trade it for anything in the world.</p>

<p>The hours slipped by, and soon the tension in the house became palpable.</p>

<p>It was finally time for the first eviction night of the season—a night that would change the dynamic of the game forever.</p>

<p>For the first time, one of the three nominated contestants would be forced to pack up and leave, their journey cut short before it could truly begin.</p>

<p>All the housemates dressed in their best, the usual casual wear replaced with elegant dresses, crisp shirts, and styled hair.</p>

<p>The main hall was lit dramatically, cameras stationed at every angle to capture every glance, every reaction, every flicker of emotion.</p>

<p>It was the kind of night that could make or break a contestant’s image.</p>

<p>Among the nominated were Ross, Lily, and Jason.</p>

<p>Lily, draped in a silky emerald dress that shimmered under the lights, sat at the edge of the couch, her hands nervously clasped together.</p>

<p>Despite her natural beauty and charm, she couldn’t hide her unease.</p>

<p>Her eyes darted toward the others, searching for reassurance she couldn’t find.</p>

<p>Jason stood near the back of the room, pacing slightly, pretending to stretch but clearly battling nerves.</p>

<p>His usual bravado had faded into silence, his jaw tight and his brow furrowed.</p>

<p>For someone who had walked into the house with big plans, this early threat of elimination felt like a personal failure.</p>
