<p>Ross had seen countless beautiful and accomplished women through his divine sense—actresses, business moguls, singers, dancers.</p>

<p>Yet, despite their allure, there was always something lacking.</p>

<p>He could almost see the stains on their perfection, the marks left by past lovers or sordid scandals.</p>

<p>&quot;Tsk, tsk,&quot; he muttered, leaning back in his chair with an air of disdain.</p>

<p>&quot;It&#x27;s so hard to find a pure girl these days. They&#x27;re picked and devoured so young.&quot; His tone dripped with derision, but his fingers tapped in the rhythm of a popular song in his room, his divine sense working subtly in the background.</p>

<p>A minute later, he stopped.</p>

<p>There she was. An exquisite red-haired beauty. The moment her image filled his eyes, Ross sat forward, his interest piqued.</p>

<p>&quot;She&#x27;ll do,&quot; he murmured, a sly smile curling his lips. &quot;One of the most sought-after actresses in the world, and with good reason. That face, that body, and she&#x27;s incredibly talented to top it off.&quot; His eyes flicked across her profile, scanning every detail with predatory precision.</p>

<p>&quot;But… how should I get her?&quot; His voice was thoughtful, almost casual, but the intensity in his gaze betrayed his darker intentions.</p>

<p>This girl lived on the other side of the world, far beyond the reach of a simple meeting or chance encounter.</p>

<p>Worse, Ross&#x27;s reputation wasn&#x27;t exactly conducive to charming women through traditional means.</p>

<p>His name was synonymous with scandal, his exploits with women public knowledge.</p>

<p>His facial assets also did little to help him. His face was the kind that, if you saw it once, you&#x27;d likely forget it the next.</p>

<p>It was the facial equivalent of a generic brand: bland, inoffensive, and utterly forgettable. No one would ever mistake him for a heartthrob or a model.</p>

<p>&quot;Blackmail again?&quot; he mused aloud, tapping his fingers on the desk. &quot;Or maybe force?&quot;</p>

<p>He let the thought linger as his divine sense roamed, diving deep into her life.</p>

<p>He examined everything—her daily routine, her connections, and most importantly, her family.</p>

<p>What he found left him speechless.</p>

<p>&quot;Unbelievable,&quot; he muttered, shaking his head. &quot;This family&#x27;s like a pack of saints. Did they save the world in their past lives?&quot;</p>

<p>Her parents were successful business owners, her sister was a thriving CEO, and her brother was a pro gamer dominating the esports scene.</p>

<p>There wasn&#x27;t a single blemish to exploit. No hidden scandals, no dirty secrets, nothing he could use to manipulate her into submission.</p>

<p>&quot;No skeletons in the closet,&quot; he murmured, frustration creeping into his voice.</p>

<p>&quot;What are they, a fairy-tale family?&quot; He leaned back, staring at the ceiling, his mind racing.</p>

<p>&quot;So… force it is?&quot; The words felt hollow, unexciting. He had taken women by force before—it was efficient but lacked the thrill of ingenuity.</p>

<p>He wanted something more this time. Something fresh, novel.</p>

<p>For five long minutes, Ross sat in silence, his mind turning over possibilities.</p>

<p>Then, like a lightning bolt, inspiration struck.</p>

<p>His lips stretched into a wicked grin, his eyes gleaming with dark anticipation.</p>

<p>&quot;I found something better. This will be fun. Hehehe,&quot; he said, his voice filled with sinister glee.</p>

<p>The plan forming in his mind was unlike anything he&#x27;d tried before.</p>

<p>It was bold, intricate, and promised a thrill he hadn&#x27;t felt in a long time.</p>

<p>On the other side of the world, a lovely 20-year-old woman was having a vivid dream.</p>

<p>It started blurry, as dreams often do, but gradually the images sharpened, becoming almost painfully real.</p>

<p>She saw the face of a man, heard his voice, and then, as if by magic, lived an entire lifetime in a single night—a hundred years condensed into fleeting moments.</p>

<p>She experienced their shared joys, their children, the countless adventures and tender moments they had together.</p>

<p>When it was over, she woke up with tears streaming down her cheeks. But these weren&#x27;t tears of sorrow.</p>

<p>&quot;Hahhh…&quot; she exhaled, her chest heaving as she wiped her face.</p>

<p>The emotions from the dream lingered, a bittersweet mixture of joy and longing.</p>

<p>She had felt it so vividly—she and that man had lived a beautiful, fulfilling life together.</p>

<p>Fearful that the memory would slip away like most dreams, she immediately tried to recall every detail.</p>

<p>To her astonishment, she could remember everything with startling clarity.</p>

<p>Every word, every emotion, every moment—it was all there.</p>

<p>&quot;What was that?&quot; she whispered, her voice trembling.</p>

<p>For the next week, the dreams returned, night after night.</p>

<p>Each time, the experiences were as vivid as the first.</p>

<p>By the end of the third week, she was no longer convinced these were dreams at all.</p>

<p>&quot;This isn&#x27;t just my imagination,&quot; she muttered to herself, pacing her bedroom in the middle of the night.</p>

<p>&quot;I think… I think these are memories. Memories of my past life.&quot;</p>

<p>She rubbed her temples, trying to make sense of the impossible.</p>

<p>It was like a floodgate had opened, granting her access to a life she didn&#x27;t even know she had lived.</p>

<p>She could recall it all, even minute details, like what she had done at the age of five in that life.</p>

<p>&quot;I have my complete memory of a past life? This is nuts!&quot; she said, letting out a shaky laugh.</p>

<p>The woman hadn&#x27;t told anyone about her experiences, not even her boyfriend.</p>

<p>How could she?</p>

<p>Telling someone she remembered a past life would make her sound insane.</p>

<p>But her carefully balanced world came crashing down one afternoon while she was scrolling her social media account on her phone.</p>

<p>She got to watch a basketball highlight reel.</p>

<p>The broadcast replayed from a match that had taken place three weeks ago.</p>

<p>Her attention, however, was glued to a young man who had scored a staggering 169 points in the first half.</p>

<p>Her breath caught.</p>

<p>&quot;He is…&quot; she whispered, her heart pounding.</p>

<p>The shock was too much to contain, and tears spilled down her cheeks, flowing uncontrollably.</p>

<p>Yet even in her emotional state, the tears only enhanced her beauty, her face glowing with a mixture of awe and heartbreak.</p>

<p>She didn&#x27;t need confirmation. She knew. The man she saw on the screen wasn&#x27;t just any player.</p>

<p>He was him. The man from her dreams—the one she had loved, lived a lifetime with, and lost.</p>

<p>Fate had brought him back into her world.</p>

<p>The woman immediately rushed to book the earliest available flight, her hands trembling with urgency.</p>

<p>Her heart raced as she made call after call—nearly a dozen in total—coordinating plans and tying up loose ends.</p>

<p>With each conversation, her determination grew.</p>

<p>Once everything was set, she stepped out of her home, a newfound energy coursing through her.</p>

<p>Her every step was light and purposeful, as though the weight of her discovery had lifted her into a world filled with hope and possibility.</p>
