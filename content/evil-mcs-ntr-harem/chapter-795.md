<p>And now Ross—arrogant, smug, insufferably calm Ross—was walking in at the eleventh hour to steal the spotlight.</p>

<p>But there was no way around it. Corey glanced around the room, reading the faces.</p>

<p>They were already shifting toward Ross, already moving.</p>

<p>Defeated, he swallowed his pride.</p>

<p>&quot;...Fine,&quot; he said quietly. &quot;Okay.&quot;</p>

<p>Ross gave a single, satisfied nod. He didn’t gloat. He didn’t need to.</p>

<p>&quot;Good,&quot; he said, stepping forward. &quot;If we had more time, I would’ve gone with horror. Psychological, surreal, maybe a twist ending. Something that grabs the audience by the throat. But with only a little time left, romance is our best bet. Simple, emotional, intimate.&quot;</p>

<p>He let that hang in the air before continuing, his voice smooth and deliberate.</p>

<p>&quot;We’re changing the cast. I’ll take the male lead. The female lead will be Cara.&quot;</p>

<p>That was the spark that lit the fuse.</p>

<p>Corey sat upright, staring at him. &quot;Wait—what?&quot;</p>

<p>Ross turned toward him, unbothered. &quot;You heard me.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;No. I don’t agree to that.&quot;</p>

<p>Ross finally gave him his full attention, eyes cool and steady.</p>

<p>&quot;Why not? You and Cara barely have anything real between you. The two of you? You’re just a love team. A gimmick for the cameras. There’s no depth.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;That’s not true.&quot;</p>

<p>Ross raised an eyebrow. &quot;Isn’t it? She refused to do a kissing scene with you during the last shoot. I was there. Everyone was.&quot;</p>

<p>The room shifted again—tense, awkward, full of people pretending not to watch but listening to every word.</p>

<p>Corey looked away for a moment, his jaw clenched.</p>

<p>It was true.</p>

<p>Cara had said she wasn’t ready. That it was too soon. She hadn’t even tried to fake it.</p>

<p>She’d pulled away during the take, apologized, and claimed she needed time.</p>

<p>At the time, Corey had brushed it off, told the others it was just nerves. But it stung.</p>

<p>And now, Ross was digging that knife deeper.</p>

<p>&quot;You two are attracted to each other. Sure. Who wouldn’t be?&quot; Ross said, voice like velvet over steel. &quot;Cara’s gorgeous. You’ve got a decent face. That’s not love. That’s a spark. And sparks burn out fast.&quot;</p>

<p>He took a step closer, his tone low, but clear enough for the entire room to hear.</p>

<p>&quot;What I’m about to film—it needs something stronger than sparks. It needs fire. Control. Commitment. And I don’t have time for anyone who’s afraid to go all in.&quot;</p>

<p>He turned his back on Corey, dismissing him without another glance.</p>

<p>&quot;I’ve already got a script in mind. A story of two people trapped in a lie and will have fast progression. It’ll be intimate. Beautiful. All I need is the right partner.&quot;</p>

<p>He looked to Cara.</p>

<p>She didn’t speak. She didn’t hesitate.</p>

<p>She stood up and walked toward him, silent and composed. Ross said something to her.</p>

<p>Instructions about the movie.</p>

<p>Corey’s heart sank.</p>

<p>She hadn’t even looked back.</p>

<p>Ross gave her a nod and began issuing quiet instructions to his women, who were already moving to prepare costumes, makeup, lights, and sets.</p>

<p>The living room transformed quickly. Furniture was shifted, props were gathered.</p>

<p>A laptop was opened for last-minute script notes. They weren’t wasting a second.</p>

<p>The rest of the contestants stood awkwardly on the sidelines, waiting for instructions, still processing how quickly everything had changed.</p>

<p>Corey remained seated, frozen in place. His pride burned. His chest ached.</p>

<p>He wanted to call her back, to say something that would make her change her mind.</p>

<p>But deep down, he knew—it wasn’t just about Ross stealing the role.</p>

<p>It was that Cara hadn’t resisted.</p>

<p>Without a word.</p>

<p>And Ross?</p>

<p>He hadn’t even looked triumphant.</p>

<p>He had expected it.</p>

<p>Because to Ross, this was never a question of if.</p>

<p>Only when.</p>

<p>Ross took command without hesitation.</p>

<p>The moment everyone agreed to follow him, the air in the house shifted.</p>

<p>Gone was the aimless anxiety and half-hearted effort. In its place came a steady, pulsing energy—like something important was finally taking form.</p>

<p>He moved fast, wasting no time. Chairs were rearranged, curtains drawn, soft lighting set just right to create mood and shadows.</p>

<p>The others followed his directions with quiet urgency. No one questioned him now.</p>

<p>Something in the way he spoke made it clear—Ross wasn’t just playing around.</p>

<p>He knew what he was doing.</p>

<p>He chose a minimalistic approach—no elaborate sets, no unnecessary fluff.</p>

<p>There was no time for it. Everything had to be focused, precise, and emotionally charged.</p>

<p>He scrapped the old plot entirely.</p>

<p>Gone was the cliché story about lovers reuniting at a class reunion.</p>

<p>In its place was something deeper. Grittier. More human.</p>

<p>A story about a fake relationship built on appearances, cracking under the weight of real emotions neither side had planned for.</p>

<p>It wasn’t just about love—it was about confusion, betrayal, pride, and the raw vulnerability of people caught in something they couldn’t control.</p>

<p>Ross outlined the new scenes like a true director. Every moment mattered.</p>

<p>Every line had a purpose.</p>

<p>He handed out tasks and assigned roles with clarity.</p>

<p>&quot;You hold the boom mic. You handle lighting. You... background extras, but stay silent.&quot;</p>

<p>Then he grabbed the main camera himself—refusing to trust anyone else with the most important part.</p>

<p>&quot;From now on, listen and move on my cue,&quot; Ross said.</p>

<p>The room nodded.</p>

<p>And then...</p>

<p>&quot;Action!&quot; Ross barked.</p>

<p>Silence fell instantly.</p>

<p>The camera zoomed in.</p>

<p>Cara sat at the edge of the bed, her hands trembling as they clutched the hem of her dress.</p>

<p>Her shoulders quivered.</p>

<p>She stared at the floor for a few seconds, her chest rising and falling as though she were preparing herself for something.</p>

<p>Then, she snapped.</p>

<p>&quot;Damn you!&quot; she screamed, voice hoarse with anguish.</p>

<p>She wasn’t just reciting lines.</p>

<p>She was feeling them.</p>

<p>Tears streamed down her cheeks, and her words came out broken, like they had been trapped inside her for years.</p>

<p>The entire house froze behind the scenes. Nobody moved, nobody blinked.</p>

<p>Even Seth—their original director—stood completely still, watching in disbelief as Cara delivered a performance far beyond anything they’d seen from her before.</p>
