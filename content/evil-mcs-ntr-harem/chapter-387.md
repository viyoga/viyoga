<p>Beep!</p>

<p>&quot;The Knights stomped the Arrows with a staggering 50-point difference! Final score: 143 to 93!&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;Wow! Ross Oakley is living up to the hype!&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;He scored 115 points in his first NBA game! What the f—&quot; Fans and commentators alike buzzed with excitement.</p>

<p>The arena was still electric from the game, and everyone was struggling to process what they had just witnessed.</p>

<p>Ross Oakley, the teenage sensation, had delivered a performance for the ages.</p>

<p>He had been nothing short of perfection, sinking every shot with surgical precision. Not a single miss. It was as though the laws of basketball didn’t apply to him.</p>

<p>Watching him play felt surreal, a masterful display of skill that left spectators questioning if what they had seen was even real.</p>

<p>As the post-game festivities unfolded, Ross found himself in the spotlight during his first-ever NBA post-game interview.</p>

<p>The press room was packed, cameras flashing, and journalists clamoring for answers.</p>

<p>&quot;Congratulations on the win, Ross!&quot; one sports journalist began.</p>

<p>&quot;Everyone’s still in shock over your performance. You were incredible to watch. I just have to ask—are you ever going to miss a shot?&quot;</p>

<p>Ross chuckled, his confidence evident but not overbearing.</p>

<p>&quot;Thank you. Of course, I can miss. No one is infallible. I doubt I’ll keep a perfect scoring record once the Finals are done,&quot; he replied, his tone calm and measured.</p>

<p>&quot;The Finals?&quot; another journalist interjected, eyebrows raised.</p>

<p>&quot;Isn’t it a little arrogant to be thinking that far ahead, Ross? Your team is only in the Play-In Tournament right now. It’s not even guaranteed that you’ll make it to the playoffs.&quot;</p>

<p>Ross leaned back slightly, his gaze steady and unshaken.</p>

<p>&quot;I don’t think it’s arrogance,&quot; he said evenly.</p>

<p>&quot;I’m just confident in what I can do. If I’m healthy, I’ll dominate anyone on the court. No one can guard me. Even if they put five men on me, I can score wherever and whenever I want. That’s not arrogance—it’s just a fact.&quot;</p>

<p>His words hung in the air, bold and unapologetic. While some in the room admired his self-assuredness, others exchanged skeptical glances.</p>

<p>Doubts lingered—after all, this was only his first NBA game.</p>

<p>Still, the buzz around Ross Oakley continued to grow.</p>

<p>Whether or not people believed his words, one thing was clear: the basketball world was witnessing the rise of something extraordinary.</p>

<p>And while many doubted him now, Ross seemed more than ready to silence every critic one game at a time.</p>

<p>***</p>

<p>Several games later, the Parkland Knights emerged victorious, dominating their way into the playoffs.</p>

<p>Ross Oakley’s incredible performance had become the talk of the basketball world.</p>

<p>His flawless streak—having yet to miss a single shot during the Play-In Tournament—elevated his already soaring popularity to new heights.</p>

<p>Fans, commentators, and analysts couldn’t stop speculating about whether Ross’s perfection would hold through the playoffs.</p>

<p>After the final game of the Play-In, the Knights’ locker room was a scene of celebration and relief.</p>

<p>But as Ross exited the court, surrounded by his wives and team staff, an extraordinarily beautiful journalist approached him with a radiant smile.</p>

<p>She carried herself with the poise of someone used to getting what she wanted.</p>

<p>&quot;Ross, can I have an exclusive interview with you? I promise I’ll make it worth your while,&quot; she said, her voice smooth and alluring.</p>

<p>The way she leaned slightly forward and maintained unwavering eye contact made her intentions clear—this wasn’t just about journalism.</p>

<p>Ross barely glanced at her before replying, &quot;Sorry, but I’m kinda busy right now.&quot; His tone was calm but dismissive, and without another word or a second look, he walked away, his focus already elsewhere.</p>

<p>The journalist stood frozen for a moment, stunned that she had been so easily brushed off.</p>

<p>Ross’s wives, who were following closely behind, noticed the exchange.</p>

<p>One of them—Natalie, known for her sharp tongue and keen insight—paused and turned back to the journalist.</p>

<p>&quot;Are you a virgin?&quot; Natalie asked bluntly, her tone neutral but her question sharp enough to slice through the journalist’s confidence.</p>

<p>The journalist blinked, unsure if she had heard correctly.</p>

<p>&quot;What kind of question is that?&quot; she asked, trying to keep her composure.</p>

<p>But Natalie’s unflinching gaze made her rethink dismissing the question as a joke.</p>

<p>Reluctantly, she replied, &quot;Of course not. There aren’t any virgins past the age of 20 in this day and age.&quot;</p>

<p>Natalie gave a slight nod, then asked, &quot;Are you married?&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;No,&quot; the journalist replied, her confusion growing.</p>

<p>&quot;Are you faithful to your boyfriend?&quot; Natalie’s third question was delivered with calm precision, but it carried an air of judgment that made the journalist hesitate.</p>

<p>She faltered for a moment before shrugging.</p>

<p>&quot;I don’t know. I don’t think so,&quot; she admitted, her voice uncertain.</p>

<p>Natalie gave a faint, knowing smile.</p>

<p>&quot;I see now why Ross doesn’t even glance your way,&quot; she said.</p>

<p>&quot;You lack the things that interest him. Have a good day.&quot; Without another word, she turned on her heel and rejoined the group of wives following Ross.</p>

<p>The journalist stood there, dumbfounded, as she watched them walk away.</p>

<p>She replayed the conversation in her mind, trying to make sense of what had just happened.</p>

<p>Ross’s wives, meanwhile, continued walking with calm smiles on their faces.</p>

<p>Among themselves, they had long discussed and understood what Ross valued in a woman.</p>

<p>Physical beauty alone wasn’t enough to hold his attention; he sought integrity, loyalty, and strength of character—qualities that set his wives apart from the crowd.</p>

<p>Above all, he sought excitement and the thrill of the chase.</p>

<p>To them, it was painfully obvious why the journalist hadn’t even warranted a second look.</p>

<p>&quot;She really thought her looks would get her somewhere,&quot; Natalie murmured quietly to the others as they caught up with Ross.</p>

<p>&quot;She’s not the first and won’t be the last,&quot; another wife replied with a chuckle.</p>

<p>Ross, overhearing the conversation, glanced over his shoulder with a small smirk.</p>

<p>&quot;What’s so funny back there?&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;Nothing, darling,&quot; Natalie replied with a teasing smile.</p>

<p>Ross shook his head, amused, and continued walking. His wives exchanged knowing looks.</p>

<p>They were confident in their bond with Ross, knowing that no matter how many people tried to catch his eye, none could match what they shared with him.</p>

<p>Meanwhile, the journalist remained behind, still rooted in place, trying to understand why she had failed to even scratch the surface of Ross Oakley’s attention.</p>
