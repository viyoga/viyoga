<p>When the man tried to crawl, the undead stomped on his thigh, shattering bone through flesh.</p>

<p>His howl echoed through the stone chamber.</p>

<p>&quot;PLEASE! PLEASE STOP!&quot; someone begged, dropping to his knees, hands raised.</p>

<p>The undead warrior’s response was a calm, mechanical punch to the face—one that turned the man’s features into a ruin of blood and broken bone.</p>

<p>The air filled with screams—high, panicked, agonized.</p>

<p>The scent of blood, spilled organs, and burnt flesh thickened until breathing became difficult.</p>

<p>Wilson’s men weren’t fighting anymore.</p>

<p>They were just trying to survive.</p>

<p>Trying to crawl away.</p>

<p>Trying to hide.</p>

<p>Trying to escape the nightmare.</p>

<p>But there was no escape.</p>

<p>Every punch carried the weight of a hammer.</p>

<p>Every blow ensured a slow, agonizing death rather than a merciful one.</p>

<p>And through the chaos—through the screams, the cracking bones, the begging, the dying—Ross sat comfortably on his throne.</p>

<p>One leg crossed over the other.</p>

<p>Chin resting lazily in his hand.</p>

<p>A small, satisfied smile playing at his lips.</p>

<p>To him, this wasn’t a battle.</p>

<p>It was entertainment.</p>

<p>A spectacle.</p>

<p>A well-deserved punishment unfolding exactly the way he wanted.</p>

<p>And Wilson’s army—once so confident, once so proud—was being torn apart one agonizing scream at a time.</p>

<p>&quot;This is bad... this is really, really bad...&quot; Wilson thought, his heart pounding like a war drum.</p>

<p>His legs trembled despite himself.</p>

<p>Not from exhaustion—but from the suffocating pressure of despair crushing him.</p>

<p>He glanced at Ross’s army again.</p>

<p>Those demon-masked monsters tearing through his people like beasts.</p>

<p>Unaffected by bullets.</p>

<p>Unaffected by abilities.</p>

<p>Unaffected by anything human.</p>

<p>Then his eyes lifted to Ross—the man he had sworn he killed once—now sitting casually on a throne, one leg resting lazily over the other, watching the massacre as if he were enjoying a private theater performance.</p>

<p>Ross didn’t even flinch.</p>

<p>Didn’t even acknowledge Wilson.</p>

<p>Not as an opponent.</p>

<p>Not even as a threat.</p>

<p>It made Wilson’s blood boil.</p>

<p>If he died here, fine—but he would damn well try to take Ross with him.</p>

<p>He clenched his teeth.</p>

<p>He made his decision.</p>

<p>&quot;AAAAAAAAHHHHHHHHH!&quot;</p>

<p>Wilson roared with everything he had and lunged forward.</p>

<p>The knives orbiting around him spun faster, becoming a swirling, deadly storm of steel.</p>

<p>They slashed and carved through the undead soldiers trying to bar his path.</p>

<p>For the first time, he forced a gap into the battlefield, pushing himself deeper into the chaos.</p>

<p>The air rang with the sharp whistling of blades.</p>

<p>Undead bodies were cut apart—arms sliced off, torsos split open.</p>

<p>Wilson was unstoppable for a single glorious moment.</p>

<p>He could see Ross’s throne clearer and clearer.</p>

<p>He was getting close.</p>

<p>Too close.</p>

<p>&quot;Just a little more... just a little more...!&quot;</p>

<p>Then—</p>

<p>A shadow fell over him.</p>

<p>A massive, towering shadow.</p>

<p>Brandon.</p>

<p>The commander of the undead stepped directly into Wilson’s path.</p>

<p>His crimson-striped demon mask stared down at him with a cold, inhuman stillness.</p>

<p>Even standing still, he radiated an overwhelming sense of doom.</p>

<p>Wilson skidded to a stop, eyes widening.</p>

<p>&quot;You dream far too high, boy, if you think you can approach my master,&quot; Brandon thundered. His voice rolled through the chamber like a landslide—deep, resonant, and final.</p>

<p>Then Brandon moved.</p>

<p>One moment he was standing there.</p>

<p>The next—he was everywhere at once.</p>

<p>&quot;W–what?!&quot; Wilson gasped.</p>

<p>Brandon’s speed was monstrous. His movement blurred, his figure vanishing like a phantom.</p>

<p>Wilson tried to track him—his eyes darting desperately, knives spinning in every direction to guard himself—</p>

<p>But he couldn’t see him.</p>

<p>Couldn’t feel him.</p>

<p>Couldn’t predict anything.</p>

<p>A cold presence appeared behind him.</p>

<p>Wilson’s blood ran cold.</p>

<p>A massive hand closed around his throat.</p>

<p>&quot;Guh—! H...HAAAK!&quot;</p>

<p>He was lifted off the ground instantly, feet kicking helplessly in the air.</p>

<p>Brandon’s grip tightened, crushing his windpipe inch by inch.</p>

<p>Wilson clawed at the hand, fingers slipping uselessly on the iron-like grip.</p>

<p>Spots danced in his vision as oxygen fled his lungs.</p>

<p>He could feel his life slipping away.</p>

<p>Then—</p>

<p>BOOM.</p>

<p>Brandon’s fist struck Wilson’s back with such overwhelming force that his entire body spasmed violently.</p>

<p>A horrific sound echoed through the chamber:</p>

<p>CRRRRRAAAAAACK.</p>

<p>Wilson’s spine shattered.</p>

<p>Not cracked—shattered.</p>

<p>Broken into pieces like glass under a hammer.</p>

<p>It felt like lightning exploding inside his bones.</p>

<p>A white-hot wave of agony tore through him so violently that his consciousness nearly broke.</p>

<p>He couldn’t scream.</p>

<p>He couldn’t breathe.</p>

<p>He couldn’t even move.</p>

<p>Brandon released him—and Wilson’s limp body flew through the air like a broken ragdoll, slamming into the stone floor with a sickening, wet thud.</p>

<p>&quot;Ghh... ah... ahh...&quot; Wilson tried to speak, but only blood bubbled past his lips.</p>

<p>He lay there twitching, his limbs twisted at unnatural angles.</p>

<p>Paralyzed.</p>

<p>Crushed.</p>

<p>Barely clinging to life.</p>

<p>Almost dead...</p>

<p>But painfully, cruelly...</p>

<p>Not dead enough.</p>

<p>Brandon slowly stepped toward him, each footfall echoing with purpose—like death coming for its final victim.</p>

<p>What followed was not simply death.</p>

<p>It was a methodical, merciless, meticulously orchestrated torture—the kind that only beings without empathy, without hesitation, and without the constraints of mortality could deliver.</p>

<p>Ross did not need to give another order.</p>

<p>His undead soldiers understood exactly what their master wanted.</p>

<p>They moved through the hall like a swarm of predators savoring their prey, spreading out among the survivors of Wilson’s men—those still alive, still screaming, still clinging desperately to the hope that something, anything, might save them.</p>

<p>That hope died quickly.</p>

<p>The air soon filled with a symphony of agony.</p>

<p>&quot;AAAAAHHHHHHHHHH!&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;NO—PLEASE, STOP! STOP!&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;MY ARM—MY ARM!! AAAAAHHHHH!&quot;</p>

<p>The screams came from every direction—high-pitched, deep, broken, raw, and feral.</p>

<p>They blended together until they became a single, horrifying chorus that seemed to shake the walls themselves.</p>

<p>The sound traveled up to the ceiling, echoing back down like the wails of tormented ghosts.</p>

<p>It was not the sound of men simply dying.</p>

<p>It was the sound of men being unmade.</p>

<p>Undead fists crushed bones with deliberate slowness.</p>

<p>Hands twisted limbs until joints snapped and tendons tore.</p>

<p>Some men were dragged across the floor, leaving long streaks of blood, only to be lifted by the skull and slammed repeatedly until their faces were unrecognizable.</p>
