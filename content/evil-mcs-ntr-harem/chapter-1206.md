<p>When Ross opened the door, he was taken aback.</p>

<p>Expecting perhaps an older, grizzled man hardened by years of command, Ross instead found himself staring at a woman who exuded both authority and striking beauty.</p>

<p>Her posture was impeccable, her gaze sharp and commanding, yet there was a subtle grace in the way she carried herself.</p>

<p>The desk in front of her was immaculate, and her presence alone seemed to fill the room, leaving no doubt that she was in complete control.</p>

<p>Ross stepped inside, momentarily caught off guard by the unexpected elegance and power radiating from the base commander.</p>

<p>&quot;You must be Ross. Please, have a seat,&quot; the base commander said, gesturing calmly toward the chair across from her desk.</p>

<p>&quot;...&quot; Ross nodded and sat down without a word.</p>

<p>His movements were relaxed, almost casual, but his eyes briefly lingered on her face.</p>

<p>The woman before him was striking—clean, sharp features, well-maintained despite the harshness of the apocalypse, and eyes that carried both intelligence and exhaustion.</p>

<p>She looked like someone who had seen far too much death and still kept standing.</p>

<p>She noticed his stare immediately and offered a small, knowing smile. June Williams was long accustomed to such reactions.</p>

<p>In a world where most people barely survived day to day, someone who carried both authority and beauty naturally drew attention.</p>

<p>Men stared. Women whispered. She had learned to ignore all of it years ago.</p>

<p>&quot;I’m June Williams,&quot; she said calmly. &quot;The commander in charge of this base.&quot; Her tone was firm but not unfriendly.</p>

<p>&quot;Thank you for saving Bella and Sebastian. They are two of my best soldiers—reliable, capable, and loyal.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;I was just in the right place at the right time,&quot; Ross replied after a brief pause. His voice was steady, almost modest.</p>

<p>&quot;I did what anyone would have done.&quot;</p>

<p>June studied him closely, her gaze sharp and evaluating, as though trying to peel away layers she couldn’t quite see through.</p>

<p>After a moment, she let out a quiet sigh and leaned back in her chair.</p>

<p>&quot;I doubt that,&quot; she said. &quot;Most people these days would’ve run. Others would’ve used them as bait. Good men are rare now... especially with how the zombies are growing stronger by the day.&quot;</p>

<p>Her words hung in the air, heavy and bitter.</p>

<p>Silence followed—long, uncomfortable, and filled with memories neither of them voiced aloud.</p>

<p>The room seemed to grow colder as the weight of the world pressed down on them. Lost soldiers.</p>

<p>Fallen bases. Families wiped out overnight.</p>

<p>Every survivor carried ghosts, and this place was no exception.</p>

<p>June’s eyes briefly lost focus, as if she were staring at something far away—perhaps faces she could no longer remember clearly, or names carved into a growing list of the dead.</p>

<p>She had buried too many people under her command.</p>

<p>Ross lowered his head slightly, his expression somber.</p>

<p>He looked every bit the man weighed down by the tragedy of the world.</p>

<p>A quiet sadness settled over his features, his shoulders subtly slumping as if burdened by grief.</p>

<p>In truth, the act came easily to him.</p>

<p>Before the world ended, Ross had been an actor—one of the best.</p>

<p>He had cried on cue, screamed in terror for the camera, and worn despair like a second skin in front of millions.</p>

<p>Compared to that, playing the role of a grieving survivor was effortless.</p>

<p>Yet, mixed within the performance was something real. This world truly was broken.</p>

<p>And even someone like him couldn’t deny the sheer scale of loss.</p>

<p>June finally straightened, her eyes regaining focus as she looked at him again—this time with a trace of respect.</p>

<p>&quot;Regardless of your reasons,&quot; she said quietly, &quot;you saved lives today. That matters more than anything else.&quot;</p>

<p>Ross met her gaze and gave a small nod, neither confirming nor denying anything.</p>

<p>In a world where truth was a luxury, appearances were often enough.</p>

<p>A full minute passed before June finally cleared her throat, breaking the silence.</p>

<p>The faint hum of the lights overhead seemed louder in the stillness.</p>

<p>When she spoke again, the softness from earlier was gone, replaced by the composed authority of a commander who had survived too many crises to waste time.</p>

<p>&quot;Let’s move on to more important matters.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;I’ve already been briefed on what happened between you and Bella,&quot; June said, folding her hands together on the desk. Her voice was calm, deliberate. &quot;I’m not questioning your honesty, but given the nature of the situation, we need to be absolutely certain. We have to identify the root cause of Bella’s... peculiar condition.&quot;</p>

<p>Her eyes narrowed slightly as she continued, watching every shift in Ross’s posture.</p>

<p>&quot;That means running a full set of tests. Bloodwork, scans, and a few controlled observations. You’ll need to cooperate fully. Are you willing to do that, Ross?&quot;</p>

<p>Ross didn’t hesitate. He met her gaze head-on, his expression open and untroubled.</p>

<p>&quot;Of course,&quot; he said simply. &quot;I have nothing to hide.&quot;</p>

<p>June studied him for a moment longer, as if searching for a crack in his composure. Finding none, she gave a small nod.</p>

<p>&quot;Good,&quot; she said. &quot;That makes things easier.&quot;</p>

<p>What followed was nearly an hour of discussion—but it was far from casual.</p>

<p>June’s questions came one after another, layered and subtle.</p>

<p>She asked about his life before the outbreak, his movements since civilization collapsed, the battles he had fought, the people he had lost.</p>

<p>Some questions were repeated later, rephrased just enough to test consistency.</p>

<p>Others seemed harmless on the surface, yet were clearly designed to provoke emotional reactions.</p>

<p>All the while, June watched him closely.</p>

<p>Ross answered with ease.</p>

<p>His tone never wavered, his breathing remained steady, and his expressions shifted naturally when they were meant to.</p>

<p>He revealed just enough to appear sincere while keeping the deeper truths carefully buried.</p>

<p>To June, it felt like she was gradually assembling a clear and logical picture of the man before her.</p>

<p>In reality, she was only seeing what Ross wanted her to see.</p>

<p>By the time the questioning ended, June leaned back in her chair, visibly more relaxed than before.</p>
