<p>For a long moment, Jane said nothing. Her lips parted slightly, but no words came out.</p>

<p>Her mind was spinning, trying to reconcile the man standing before her with the one on the news.</p>

<p>She had always known Joshua to be ambitious, calculating when needed—but corrupt? Capable of this kind of betrayal?</p>

<p>The silence stretched on, and finally, she blinked back the tears threatening to fall.</p>

<p>&quot;It doesn’t matter,&quot; she said quietly. &quot;Josh... we’ll get through this. I promise. We’ll survive this.&quot;</p>

<p>She reached for his hand, her grip firm, even as her voice shook.</p>

<p>She tried to smile—to show strength, loyalty, love—but it faltered.</p>

<p>Her lips trembled, and the smile twisted into something pained and uncertain.</p>

<p>He held her hand in both of his, lowering his head.</p>

<p>&quot;You shouldn’t have to stand by me in this.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;But I will,&quot; she replied. &quot;Because you’re my husband. Because I still believe in the man I married—even if you made a terrible mistake.&quot;</p>

<p>And though her heart was breaking, Jane knew that this was just the beginning of a long, public, and painful fall from grace.</p>

<p>But for now, she stayed beside him, not because she had all the answers, but because she was the only one left who hadn’t turned away.</p>

<p>The following days brought nothing but misfortune for the senator.</p>

<p>&quot;Senator Imprisoned Without Bail.&quot;</p>

<p>The headline flashed across every news channel, plastered on papers in bold, unforgiving print.</p>

<p>Jane stared at it in disbelief, her heart sinking as the reality settled in—her husband, once a respected and influential man, was now a prisoner awaiting trial.</p>

<p>His fall from grace had been swift and brutal.</p>

<p>There would be no bail, no comfort, and seemingly, no justice.</p>

<p>It was a nightmare Jane couldn’t wake up from. In a sudden twist of fate, her world shattered.</p>

<p>The man she had built a life with—the man who had stood beside her at countless charity galas, political dinners, and family celebrations—was now behind bars.</p>

<p>The first days were unbearable, filled with confusion, phone calls, and silence.</p>

<p>No explanations, no reassurances. Just chaos.</p>

<p>But if it was hard for him, it was excruciating for her.</p>

<p>Jane’s perfect, carefully curated life had turned upside down in the blink of an eye.</p>

<p>One moment, she was the wife of a powerful senator; the next, she was a social pariah.</p>

<p>Friends she once dined with no longer returned her calls.</p>

<p>Whispers followed her wherever she went—if she dared go out at all.</p>

<p>She found herself screaming into pillows at night, cursing everything: the justice system, the reporters, the betrayal she felt but couldn’t name.</p>

<p>How could everything go so wrong so quickly?</p>

<p>She couldn’t understand it, couldn’t accept it. It was as if the world had turned against her, and no one was willing to help.</p>

<p>Their mansion—once the pride of their success—had been sealed off, declared a crime scene and impounded as potential evidence. Jane wasn’t even allowed to retrieve her belongings.</p>

<p>She was forced to check into a hotel, clutching a suitcase she barely had time to pack.</p>

<p>Each night, she stared at the ceiling of that cold, unfamiliar room as bills piled up on the nightstand, reminders of a life unraveling.</p>

<p>She knew this couldn’t go on.</p>

<p>The banks had frozen all their assets—hers included—leaving her with no access to their wealth.</p>

<p>Everything they had worked for was now beyond her reach.</p>

<p>Credit cards were declined.</p>

<p>Bank accounts were locked.</p>

<p>She could barely cover the hotel bill, let alone hire the kind of legal team her husband desperately needed.</p>

<p>Desperation clawed at her chest. She called everyone she could think of—old friends, political allies, even distant family members.</p>

<p>She begged, pleaded, tried to strike deals, but all she received were vague reassurances and hollow promises.</p>

<p>&quot;I’ll see what I can do,&quot; they said. &quot;Give it time.&quot; But time was the one thing she didn’t have.</p>

<p>No one dared take her side. No one wanted to be associated with a sinking ship.</p>

<p>In the span of a few days, Jane had gone from a woman of power and privilege to someone utterly alone, abandoned by a world that once applauded her every move.</p>

<p>And still, she clung to hope—bitter, fragile hope—that something, anything, would change.</p>

<p>This torment went on for nearly a month—day after day of silence, dread, and dwindling hope—until one morning, Jane’s phone buzzed with a call from an unknown number.</p>

<p>She picked it up immediately, heart pounding. Maybe it was a lawyer with good news.</p>

<p>Maybe someone had finally come through for her.</p>

<p>But the moment she heard the voice on the other end, her blood ran cold.</p>

<p>&quot;A slap for a life. How do you like how things turned out for you, Jane? I hope you’re enjoying it.&quot;</p>

<p>The voice was mocking, calm, and unmistakably male. Jane froze in place, her fingers trembling around the phone.</p>

<p>&quot;You?!&quot; she gasped, a wave of recognition crashing over her. The voice was familiar—too familiar. And it didn’t take her long to piece it together.</p>

<p>&quot;Did you have anything to do with what happened to my husband?&quot; Her voice rose, laced with fury and disbelief.</p>

<p>&quot;Of course I did,&quot; the man replied smoothly. &quot;That one little slap? It was expensive. I don’t forget people who offend me, Jane. I make them pay. And your husband—well, he paid for your mistakes very well.&quot;</p>

<p>Jane gritted her teeth, rage bubbling to the surface.</p>

<p>&quot;Fuck you, Ross! You think you can just get away with this?! I’ll report you to the police! You’re finished, you sick, arrogant—&quot;</p>

<p>Her voice cracked as hysteria took over.</p>

<p>She screamed every curse she could think of, pacing back and forth in the hotel room, hair disheveled, eyes wild.</p>

<p>But Ross didn’t interrupt her.</p>

<p>He simply listened in silence, as if enjoying every second of her breakdown.</p>

<p>Finally, after several minutes of her ranting, he spoke again—calm, collected, and cruel.</p>

<p>&quot;So... do you want to see your husband go free?&quot;</p>

<p>His words hung in the air like a dagger.</p>

<p>Jane went silent. Her breath caught in her throat.</p>

<p>&quot;What... what are you saying?&quot; she whispered, suddenly unsure of everything.</p>

<p>The game had just changed—and Ross was holding all the cards.</p>
