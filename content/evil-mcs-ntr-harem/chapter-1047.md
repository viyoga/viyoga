<p>They entered the house in silence, moving like shadows slipping through the broken doorway.</p>

<p>The faint creak of the wooden floorboards beneath their boots was swallowed by the moaning of distant zombies outside.</p>

<p>Dust drifted lazily through the stale air, illuminated by the faint shafts of light coming from cracked windows.</p>

<p>It didn’t take them long to find her.</p>

<p>Miku was crouched near an overturned table, methodically placing cans of food into her worn black backpack.</p>

<p>Her motions were calm, careful—each can stacked neatly as though she was building her own fragile sense of peace.</p>

<p>Her lips curved into a soft, fleeting smile, the kind that hadn’t graced her face in days.</p>

<p>For a brief moment, she looked almost happy, lost in the quiet rhythm of survival.</p>

<p>Then she looked up—and froze.</p>

<p>Five men stood in the doorway, their faces half-hidden by grime, their clothes torn and bloodstained.</p>

<p>But what froze Miku wasn’t their appearance—it was their expressions.</p>

<p>Their eyes weren’t the eyes of desperate survivors.</p>

<p>They gleamed with a sick hunger, the kind that made her stomach twist.</p>

<p>The smile vanished from her face as if it had never existed.</p>

<p>&quot;...&quot;</p>

<p>No words came out. She knew there was no reasoning with men like these.</p>

<p>Without thinking, she turned and bolted toward the back door.</p>

<p>Her heart pounded in her ears. The strap of her bag dug into her shoulder as she ran.</p>

<p>Behind her, she heard the muffled laughter of the men, slow and mocking.</p>

<p>&quot;She’s running,&quot; one of them drawled.</p>

<p>&quot;Let her. It’s more fun that way.&quot;</p>

<p>Miku clenched her teeth. She had seen this before—on the news before the world fell apart.</p>

<p>People showing their true nature when there were no laws, no police, no one left to stop them.</p>

<p>She had seen the videos, the women who screamed for help that never came.</p>

<p>And worse—she had seen it in real life.</p>

<p>During her scavenging trips, she had stumbled upon scenes she could never forget: blood on the floor, torn clothes, and the hollow eyes of those who didn’t escape in time.</p>

<p>Not me. Not today.</p>

<p>&quot;Shit!&quot; she hissed under her breath as she reached the front exit.</p>

<p>Her hope shattered instantly—the doorway was crawling with zombies, their rotting arms reaching, jaws snapping as they fought to get inside.</p>

<p>She staggered back, her pulse spiking with panic. She couldn’t go that way.</p>

<p>If those men weren’t here, she could’ve hidden and waited for the zombies to move on.</p>

<p>But now she was trapped between the dead outside—and the monsters inside.</p>

<p>Her eyes darted around wildly until she spotted another door at the end of the hall.</p>

<p>She sprinted toward it and slipped inside, slamming the door shut before locking it with trembling hands.</p>

<p>The small room was dark except for the faint light filtering through a cracked window.</p>

<p>Miku pressed her back against the door, her breath ragged, chest rising and falling as she tried to silence the noise of her own fear.</p>

<p>Her palms were slick with sweat, and she could hear the blood rushing in her ears.</p>

<p>For a long moment, there was only silence. Even the groans of the zombies outside seemed distant now.</p>

<p>She dared to hope that maybe—just maybe—they had lost track of her.</p>

<p>Then came the sound.</p>

<p>Tap...</p>

<p>tap...</p>

<p>tap...</p>

<p>The faint footsteps approached, slow and deliberate. She could hear their muffled laughter, their voices low and cruel.</p>

<p>&quot;Where’d the little mouse go?&quot; one of them whispered.</p>

<p>&quot;She ran in here somewhere,&quot; another answered, his tone almost gleeful. &quot;I can smell her fear.&quot;</p>

<p>The doorknob jiggled. Once. Twice.</p>

<p>Then came the voice.</p>

<p>A soft, mocking whisper seeped through the crack beneath the door, thick with twisted amusement.</p>

<p>&quot;Come out, come out, now, miss...&quot; The man’s voice was sing-song, but his words dripped with menace. &quot;There’s nowhere left to run. Just spread your legs for us and we’ll all be happy.&quot;</p>

<p>Miku clamped a hand over her mouth to keep from gasping.</p>

<p>Every instinct in her body screamed at her to move—but she didn’t dare.</p>

<p>She could hear them breathing just beyond the door.</p>

<p>The air was thick with dread.</p>

<p>And as the doorknob began to turn ever so slowly, Miku realized something with chilling clarity.</p>

<p>The zombies outside were not the real monsters anymore.</p>

<p>Miku pressed her back against the cold wall, her knees trembling as she tried to steady her breath.</p>

<p>Every sound outside the door made her flinch—the shuffle of boots, the low, guttural chuckles of the men, the faint creak of floorboards beneath their weight.</p>

<p>Her hands clutched the small fruit knife she had found earlier, its dull blade glinting faintly in the dim light that leaked through the cracks.</p>

<p>She prayed silently, lips moving without sound. She didn’t pray for strength.</p>

<p>She didn’t even pray for escape. She prayed simply that the end, when it came, would be quick.</p>

<p>The silence stretched on unbearably, broken only by the faint moans of zombies outside the ruined house.</p>

<p>The men waited. She could feel it—like wolves circling prey, savoring the fear before the kill.</p>

<p>Thirty long minutes passed.</p>

<p>Then, a faint sound—a soft metallic click.</p>

<p>Miku froze. Her entire body went rigid. The lock trembled once.</p>

<p>Then again. Something scraped against the wood—metal grinding against metal.</p>

<p>Her heart raced, her hands slick with sweat. She pressed her palm to her mouth to stifle a whimper.</p>

<p>Suddenly, the door jolted violently. The second time, it cracked. The third, it gave way entirely.</p>

<p>BANG!</p>

<p>The old hinges snapped, and the door crashed inward. Dust and splinters filled the air.</p>

<p>Five silhouettes stood in the doorway, their figures outlined against the pale light from the hallway.</p>

<p>Their faces were twisted with satisfaction and hunger—human, yet monstrous in every way that mattered.</p>

<p>&quot;Well, well...&quot; one of them drawled, licking his cracked lips.</p>

<p>&quot;Look what we’ve found. A new bitch for us to play. Beautiful, meaty, and fresh.&quot;</p>

<p>It was Gage, the leader of the group. His voice dripped with cruel delight.</p>

<p>The others grinned and laughed behind him, their eyes devouring Miku as if she were already theirs.</p>
