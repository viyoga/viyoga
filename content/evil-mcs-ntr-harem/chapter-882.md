<p>The days that followed were busy for the Johnson household.</p>

<p>With the wedding date reset, the family had to begin preparations all over again—but this time the matter was larger, grander, and even more delicate.</p>

<p>Ross was no longer marrying just one of the Johnson girls, but both sisters.</p>

<p>The talk of the town had quickly shifted toward envy, gossip, and even disbelief, for very few men could boast of such a fortune.</p>

<p>Naturally, Ross’s visits to the Johnson home became more frequent as the days passed.</p>

<p>The more he appeared, the more his presence seemed to brighten the house.</p>

<p>Servants straightened their backs whenever he entered, Laura and Lois lit up like blossoms in spring, and even Kate herself... found her heart unsettled.</p>

<p>At first, she dismissed it as admiration, the natural fondness one would feel for a man destined to be part of her family.</p>

<p>But the more she saw him, the more complicated her feelings grew.</p>

<p>There was an ease in his smile, a confidence in his words, and a warmth in his gaze that unsettled her composure.</p>

<p>She was old enough to be wiser, but whenever Ross visited, she found herself unconsciously smoothing her dress, checking her reflection, and waiting for his voice.</p>

<p>This afternoon was no different. Ross had come earlier than expected, and Kate found herself lingering in the sitting room with him while her daughters busied themselves elsewhere.</p>

<p>The silence between them stretched, heavy with an unspoken awareness. At last, Kate drew in a small breath and broke it.</p>

<p>&quot;Ross... are you free right now?&quot; she asked, her voice softer than she intended.</p>

<p>Ross turned his attention fully to her, that familiar, effortless charm flickering in his eyes. &quot;Of course, Kate. I’m always free for you. What’s the matter? Is your back giving you trouble again?&quot;</p>

<p>Her lips parted slightly, startled that he had read her so easily.</p>

<p>He had always noticed such things—too easily, perhaps.</p>

<p>His eyes drifted downward for just an instant, catching on her full curves before returning to her face.</p>

<p>Kate caught the flicker, and her heart skipped despite herself.</p>

<p>It was no wonder he teased her about her back pains; her figure had always been generous, though now it felt like a curse that drew attention she ought not want.</p>

<p>Blushing faintly, she nodded. &quot;Yes... it’s been aching all day. Could you... maybe help me with it again?&quot;</p>

<p>Ross’s grin widened, teasing but kind. &quot;Of course. With my magic hands, no back pain will trouble you again—at least not for a few days.&quot;</p>

<p>His confidence made her flustered, though she tried to mask it with a faint smile.</p>

<p>She opened her mouth to respond, but no words came out.</p>

<p>Before she could gather herself, Ross had already stepped closer, one hand brushing lightly against her arm as if guiding her.</p>

<p>The touch was casual, almost innocent, yet it sent an inexplicable shiver through her.</p>

<p>Kate hesitated, her thoughts racing.</p>

<p>This was her daughters’ fiancé—no, soon-to-be husband.</p>

<p>She should not feel this way.</p>

<p>And yet, the warmth of his presence made her reluctance dissolve.</p>

<p>Without another word, Ross placed a steadying hand at her lower back and gently urged her toward the staircase.</p>

<p>The sound of their footsteps on the polished wood echoed faintly in the quiet house.</p>

<p>Servants had long since busied themselves elsewhere, and the daughters were in the courtyard, laughing together—leaving the halls strangely private.</p>

<p>Step by step, Kate followed, her breath unsteady. She told herself it was only a massage, nothing more.</p>

<p>Still, when Ross opened the door to a secluded room upstairs and motioned her inside, her pulse quickened.</p>

<p>The door closed softly behind them, shutting out the rest of the world.</p>

<p>Ross began the massage with an ease that spoke of experience.</p>

<p>His hands moved with firm confidence, kneading away the tension in Kate’s shoulders, smoothing down her spine, pressing just enough to make her release soft sighs of relief.</p>

<p>The warm oil carried a faint herbal fragrance, seeping into her skin and loosening the tightness that had plagued her for days.</p>

<p>Kate had told herself to remain composed, to focus only on the relief, but Ross’s touch was far too skilled.</p>

<p>Every press of his fingers seemed to draw away not just her pain, but her thoughts as well.</p>

<p>Her body relaxed, her mind blurred, and slowly the world around her drifted into silence.</p>

<p>Within moments, her eyelids grew too heavy to hold open. She surrendered, slipping into a deep, peaceful sleep.</p>

<p>When she woke some hours later, she felt as though she had been reborn.</p>

<p>Her limbs were light, her back no longer ached, and for the first time in a long while, she felt truly rested.</p>

<p>But then awareness struck her, sharp and sudden.</p>

<p>Her heart leapt as she realized her state—she was still bare from the waist up, only a thin sheet loosely covering her.</p>

<p>Instinctively, her hand clutched at the cloth, pulling it against her chest as she darted a look around the room.</p>

<p>&quot;Ohhh...&quot; Kate breathed, the sound escaping her lips in a startled gasp.</p>

<p>Ross was there.</p>

<p>He wasn’t looming over her, nor staring at her. Instead, he was fast asleep in a chair beside the bed.</p>

<p>His large frame seemed almost comical against the delicate wooden seat, his arms folded loosely across his chest, his head tilted slightly to one side.</p>

<p>His breathing was steady, calm—completely at ease.</p>

<p>Kate’s lips parted, caught between relief and something far more dangerous.</p>

<p>She knew she should immediately redress herself, wake him, and pretend none of this had happened.</p>

<p>And yet... for a moment, her gaze lingered.</p>

<p>She studied the breadth of his shoulders, the strength in his arms even at rest, the way the chair seemed far too small to contain him.</p>

<p>He looked younger like this, stripped of his usual charm and confidence, but no less commanding.</p>

<p>The sight tugged at something deep within her chest.</p>

<p>Shaking her head quickly, she turned away, ashamed of herself.</p>

<p>She reached for her bra, her fingers fumbling as she fastened it with urgency.</p>

<p>Then she pulled her blouse back on, smoothing the wrinkles from the fabric as if each careful motion could erase what she felt in her heart.</p>

<p>At last, she stood. Her steps were soft, deliberate, as she moved toward him.</p>

<p>Ross was still sleeping soundly, his chest rising and falling in rhythm.</p>

<p>Kate hesitated, her hand hovering just above his arm.</p>

<p>She told herself she was only going to wake him gently, thank him, and send him on his way.</p>

<p>That was all. Nothing more.</p>

<p>Her fingers inched closer, trembling ever so slightly.</p>

<p>And then—just as she was about to touch him—something happened that she had not expected.</p>
