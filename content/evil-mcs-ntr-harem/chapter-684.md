<p>Colton tried to fight back.</p>

<p>He hired lawyers, made public statements, and even clung to the hope that the truth would eventually come to light.</p>

<p>But as the months dragged on, reality set in with a crushing weight.</p>

<p>The justice system moved at a glacial pace—especially when the ones pulling the strings had wealth, power, and influence on their side.</p>

<p>It was like screaming into a void. No one listened, and no one cared.</p>

<p>Each court hearing felt like a performance, a show where the outcome had already been decided.</p>

<p>Witnesses disappeared. Evidence was dismissed.</p>

<p>The media painted him as the villain, and his pleas fell on deaf ears.</p>

<p>He wasn’t just losing a battle—he had already lost the war.</p>

<p>And the one standing over him, triumphant and untouchable, was Ross.</p>

<p>In the silence of his prison cell, Colton often asked himself: Why?</p>

<p>Why would Ross go this far? What did he do to deserve this level of vengeance?</p>

<p>The answer came back to him like a slap to the face—that game.</p>

<p>It had been the NBA finals. Emotions were high. The crowd was roaring.</p>

<p>And in the heat of the moment, Colton had thrown a taunt at Ross that had many people in the arena laughing.</p>

<p>He had mocked him about rumors—ugly, personal rumors—about his wives cheating on him.</p>

<p>Ross had kept a straight face at the time, even smiled.</p>

<p>But Colton remembered the cold fire in his eyes. And now, it made sense.</p>

<p>This wasn’t just revenge. This was calculated retribution.</p>

<p>And the worst part? Ross hadn’t just taken his freedom—he had taken everything.</p>

<p>His reputation, his legacy, his dignity. And now, he had Colton’s woman too.</p>

<p>The same man he had once humiliated was now the one doing the humiliating.</p>

<p>The irony was so bitter, it made Colton sick.</p>

<p>“I’m such a fool,” he whispered one night, his voice hoarse and broken. “I should’ve listened to my teammates… They warned me not to cross him.”</p>

<p>Back then, he’d laughed it off. He thought Ross was all bark and no bite.</p>

<p>He thought his fame and talent would protect him. But now he knew better.</p>

<p>There were some lines you don’t cross, some enemies you don’t provoke.</p>

<p>Ross wasn’t just powerful—he was patient. And he played the long game.</p>

<p>Colton sat on the edge of his cot, his face buried in his hands, tears slipping through his fingers.</p>

<p>He wished he could turn back time, wished he could take it all back. But regrets had no cure.</p>

<p>There was no pill that could rewind the clock or undo what had been done.</p>

<p>The cold bars of his cell reminded him every day that this was his new reality.</p>

<p>No more courtside seats. No more roaring crowds.</p>

<p>No more luxury, no more freedom.</p>

<p>Just concrete walls, steel doors, and endless time to think.</p>

<p>He would be locked up for years—maybe decades—and the world would move on without him.</p>

<p>Ross would move on without him.</p>

<p>And the worst punishment of all was knowing that this fate, this cage, this living hell… was all his own doing.</p>

<p>***</p>

<p>The year went by in the blink of an eye.</p>

<p>Time, it seemed, was always in a rush when life was good.</p>

<p>And for Ross, life was not just good—it was extraordinary.</p>

<p>When December arrived once more, Ross fulfilled his promise.</p>

<p>He married not only Sasha but also Kristine, sealing his love for both women in a ceremony that was nothing short of legendary.</p>

<p>The wedding was held in a private estate overlooking the sea, with winter winds carrying the scent of salt and pine through the air.</p>

<p>It wasn’t just a celebration—it was a statement.</p>

<p>A declaration that Ross Oakley lived by his own rules and bent the world to his will.</p>

<p>Kristine, despite the growing curve of her belly, looked breathtaking.</p>

<p>Her maternity glow only added to her elegance, and her eyes sparkled with quiet joy.</p>

<p>She stood tall and proud beside Sasha, who radiated confidence in a sleek ivory gown that shimmered under the soft lights.</p>

<p>The two women, so different yet perfectly matched, exchanged vows with Ross together, their hands joined as if to say: We are in this as one.</p>

<p>The honeymoon that followed was intimate and magical. Just the three of them, away from the noise and expectations of the world.</p>

<p>They traveled to a secluded island resort, where the days were filled with laughter, warm sun, and stolen kisses.</p>

<p>Nights were tender and passionate, wrapped in silk sheets and whispered promises.</p>

<p>They shared stories, dreams, and quiet moments where nothing needed to be said at all.</p>

<p>It was their paradise—temporary, yet unforgettable.</p>

<p>But they all knew it wouldn’t last.</p>

<p>Ross wasn’t a man meant to be kept in one place for long.</p>

<p>And neither Sasha nor Kristine tried to chain him down.</p>

<p>They understood what it meant to love a man like Ross—a man who walked in power, who carried the weight of ambition, and who drew others to him like gravity.</p>

<p>After the honeymoon, they would have to share him.</p>

<p>There were others in his life—other women, other responsibilities, other destinies intertwined with his own.</p>

<p>And that was okay. Because what they had was real. Their bond was strong.</p>

<p>And they had no doubts about their place in his heart.</p>

<p>Then the calendar flipped.</p>

<p>The year 2035 arrived.</p>

<p>And with it, everything began to shift.</p>

<p>It was the year Ross’s name became more than just a headline or a trending topic—it became legend.</p>

<p>Deals were struck behind closed doors, and enemies once hidden in the shadows began to show their faces.</p>

<p>Powerful people began to fear him, and those who once doubted him now scrambled to earn his favor.</p>

<p>He expanded his empire—businesses, properties, even influence in political circles.</p>

<p>Whispers of his connections spread through boardrooms and back alleys alike.</p>

<p>Some called him a genius. Others called him dangerous.</p>

<p>But all agreed on one thing: Ross Oakley was not someone to be underestimated.</p>

<p>What was the most surprising for him this year was that the majority of his wives wanted something from him all at the same time.</p>
