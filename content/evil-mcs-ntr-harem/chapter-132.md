<p>Ross hummed a soft tune, his voice barely audible yet somehow captivating, as he strolled toward his next class.</p>

<p>His relaxed demeanor, combined with his average looks but commanding aura, drew attention like a magnet.</p>

<p>Students around him couldn&#x27;t help but steal glances, some bold enough to follow him with curious whispers.</p>

<p>Despite the attention, Ross remained composed, his focus drifting elsewhere. As always, the crowd eventually thinned, leaving only Dennis and Daryl, his two closest companions, walking alongside him.</p>

<p>Dennis nudged Ross with his elbow, a sly grin spreading across his round face.</p>

<p>&quot;So... I smell something fishy in the air, Ross,&quot; he said, his voice dropping conspiratorially. &quot;Word on the street is you and your new girlfriend snuck out earlier. Spill it, man. The juicier, the better.&quot; His pudgy cheeks puffed up as he grinned, his anticipation palpable.</p>

<p>Ross smirked but didn&#x27;t immediately respond, which only made Dennis more impatient.</p>

<p>&quot;Yes, please,&quot; Daryl chimed in, his tone light but equally curious. &quot;Share some soup with us, Ross. You&#x27;ve already eaten all the meat!&quot; He chuckled at his own joke, patting his stomach.</p>

<p>Unlike before, when he was lanky and underweight, Daryl&#x27;s improved finances had clearly done wonders for his health.</p>

<p>He looked fuller, healthier, and more confident—a transformation that Ross silently took pride in. Seeing his friends thrive alongside him was something he genuinely appreciated.</p>

<p>Ross finally let out a soft chuckle and shook his head. &quot;Nothing happened,&quot; he said, his tone calm but firm. &quot;April and I just talked.&quot;</p>

<p>Dennis groaned in frustration. &quot;C&#x27;mon, man! Don&#x27;t give me that boring answer. You don&#x27;t just sneak off with someone like April for a talk!&quot;</p>

<p>Ross simply smiled and slid into his chair as they entered the classroom. He was unfazed by Dennis&#x27;s prodding, his thoughts momentarily drifting to the earlier moment with April.</p>

<p>Her confession echoed in his mind—I love you. The way her voice trembled, her cheeks flushed, and her eyes sparkled with sincerity had been unforgettable.</p>

<p>Ross understood all too well why women were drawn to him. It wasn&#x27;t just his money or charisma; it was the aura of strength and capability he exuded.</p>

<p>Women were hardwired to seek security and stability, and Ross provided both effortlessly. It was a loophole in their psychology, one he exploited without hesitation.</p>

<p>Yet, Ross wasn&#x27;t the type to toy with someone&#x27;s feelings only to discard them. No, that wasn&#x27;t his style. April had secured her place in his life, just as others had before her—and others would after.</p>

<p>He saw her as a permanent part of his future, a piece of the grand puzzle he was assembling.</p>

<p>His lips curved into a faint smirk as his thoughts grew darker. April didn&#x27;t know it yet, but she was already his, body and soul. Ross&#x27;s plans for her went beyond mere affection.</p>

<p>He would cherish her, yes, but he would also claim her in ways she could never imagine.</p>

<p>He envisioned her as an integral part of his growing harem—a collection of extraordinary women who would stand by him, devoted and bound to him forever.</p>

<p>Dennis&#x27;s voice snapped him out of his reverie. &quot;You&#x27;re zoning out, man! What&#x27;s on your mind?&quot;</p>

<p>Ross shrugged, the smirk never leaving his face. &quot;Nothing much. Just thinking about how things are falling into place.&quot;</p>

<p>Daryl raised an eyebrow. &quot;You mean with April?&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;Maybe,&quot; Ross said vaguely, leaning back in his chair. He enjoyed keeping them guessing.</p>

<p>The bell rang, signaling the start of class, but Ross&#x27;s thoughts remained elsewhere. His future stretched out before him, a life of power, pleasure, and control.</p>

<p>He wasn&#x27;t just living a dream—he was shaping it, bending the world to his will one step at a time.</p>

<p>* * *</p>

<p>The weekdays of school quickly fell back into their usual rhythm. Soon enough, the weekend rolled around again, and April found herself hatching another plan.</p>

<p>Weekends were now her only opportunity to visit her parents, ever since Ross had claimed her as his girl.</p>

<p>To explain her prolonged absences from coming home, she&#x27;d lied to her parents, saying she had moved into the school dorms to focus on her studies as graduation loomed closer and the coursework became more demanding.</p>

<p>In reality, it was all a fabrication to cover her new lifestyle of sex and even more sex with Ross.</p>

<p>&quot;Mom! Dad! I&#x27;m home!&quot; April called out cheerfully as she stepped through the front door, her arms full of grocery bags and gift parcels.</p>

<p>Following behind her was Freddie, a hulking man Ross had assigned as her bodyguard. Freddie carried the bulk of the load with ease, his towering frame drawing attention.</p>

<p>At first, April had mistaken Freddie for the man Ross had once called Brandon, but since she couldn&#x27;t be certain, she decided to let the thought slide.</p>

<p>&quot;April, come! I&#x27;ve prepared your favorite dishes,&quot; a warm voice called out. A strikingly beautiful woman, clearly an older version of April, emerged from the kitchen.</p>

<p>Behind her came April&#x27;s father, a middle-aged man whose handsome features had aged gracefully.</p>

<p>They greeted April with hugs, but their smiles soon turned curious as their eyes landed on Freddie, who was unloading the bags in the living room.</p>

<p>The calloused hands of April&#x27;s parents, a testament to years of hard labor, momentarily paused. They were long-time employees of a large grocery store in the city, working tirelessly to make ends meet.</p>

<p>Even the modest house they lived in was rented, with payments that grew steeper year after year.</p>

<p>&quot;Who&#x27;s this?&quot; April&#x27;s mother asked, her gaze shifting between Freddie and the numerous items he was unpacking. &quot;And what&#x27;s all this?&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;Ah, this is Freddie, Mom,&quot; April said with a casual smile. &quot;He&#x27;s the bodyguard my boyfriend assigned to me.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;Oh?&quot; her mother replied, raising an eyebrow but smiling warmly. &quot;Julian really is such a thoughtful young man to provide you with a bodyguard. The city&#x27;s crime rate has been getting worse lately.&quot;</p>

<p>April hesitated for a moment before dropping the bombshell. &quot;I... broke up with Julian, Mom. I have a new boyfriend now.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;What?!&quot; her parents exclaimed in unison, their shock evident.</p>

<p>They had met Julian on several occasions and were quite fond of him. He was a charming, handsome young man who had always seemed polite and dependable.</p>

<p>&quot;It&#x27;s a long story,&quot; April said with a soft laugh, steering her parents to the sofa.</p>

<p>She began weaving another carefully constructed lie, explaining how she and Julian had grown apart due to irreconcilable differences.</p>

<p>Then, she launched into a glowing tale about Ross and the whirlwind romance that had brought them together.</p>

<p>As the story unfolded, April casually dropped the biggest surprise yet. &quot;Oh, and tomorrow, I&#x27;m taking you both to look at houses. We&#x27;ll pick one to buy together!&quot;</p>

<p>Her parents froze in disbelief. &quot;What?!&quot; her mother gasped, her hands flying to her mouth.</p>

<p>&quot;You&#x27;re buying us a house?&quot; her father repeated, his voice thick with emotion.</p>

<p>&quot;Yes!&quot; April beamed. &quot;It&#x27;s long overdue, and it&#x27;s all thanks to Ross. It&#x27;s his money. He wants to make sure you both live comfortably.&quot; April&#x27;s words sparked an intense debate between her parents about whether to accept this gesture as a genuine gift or view it as a potential bribe.</p>

<p>The discussion grew heated, with her father leaning toward gratitude and her mother hesitating out of pride. In the end, however, April&#x27;s persistence won them over.</p>

<p>Rejecting such a rare opportunity, especially in today&#x27;s world, felt like foolishness. To turn away luck and a precious boon was simply not an option.</p>
