<p>&quot;You&#x27;re confident, I&#x27;ll give you that,&quot; she said, trying to regain some of her composure.</p>

<p>Her voice had taken on a sharper edge, but the flurry of emotions inside her was harder to control.</p>

<p>&quot;But I&#x27;m not here to play your little games, Ross. So, let&#x27;s just say we&#x27;re both wasting each other&#x27;s time.&quot;</p>

<p>Ross&#x27;s eyes never left her, though his smile became more enigmatic, more knowing.</p>

<p>&quot;Time?&quot; he repeated softly, as if tasting the word on his tongue.</p>

<p>&quot;Sasha, the only time being wasted here is yours. And we both know it.&quot;</p>

<p>His words lingered in the air, like an open challenge that hung between them, unspoken but impossible to ignore.</p>

<p>Sasha&#x27;s heart skipped a beat. She could feel the adrenaline coursing through her veins now.</p>

<p>This wasn&#x27;t just about control or power—it was about something more primal, something that tugged at the edges of her mind and body.</p>

<p>She had to stop herself from reacting to his presence, from letting him win.</p>

<p>But it was hard. Damn hard.</p>

<p>She took a slow breath, steadying herself, before giving him a sharp, almost mocking smile.</p>

<p>&quot;You&#x27;re delusional if you think I&#x27;m going to just fall into your hands.&quot;</p>

<p>Ross leaned forward then, his posture predatory, his eyes burning with that dangerous intensity.</p>

<p>&quot;Maybe,&quot; he said, his voice quiet but thick with meaning. &quot;But if I were you, I wouldn&#x27;t be so sure.&quot;</p>

<p>The words hung in the air, thick with promise, and for a split second, Sasha wondered if she had just made the biggest mistake of her life.</p>

<p>They talked for a while longer before Ross suggested a change of scenery.</p>

<p>Sasha didn&#x27;t object. A part of her was curious to see what a night out with Ross Oakley could really look like.</p>

<p>He called for his driver, but Sasha insisted on using her own car, her subtle way of keeping the upper hand—or so she told herself.</p>

<p>Soon, they arrived at one of the city&#x27;s most exclusive nightclubs.</p>

<p>The neon-lit sign outside was discreet, but everyone who was anyone knew what kind of crowd it attracted.</p>

<p>The line outside stretched around the block, but the moment Ross stepped out of his car, the bouncers straightened and waved him in without hesitation.</p>

<p>Sasha followed, heels clicking against the marble floor, her confidence matching his stride.</p>

<p>Inside, the club throbbed with life—pulsing lights, heavy bass, bodies moving in rhythm.</p>

<p>But the energy shifted the moment Ross and Sasha entered. Whispers spread like wildfire.</p>

<p>All eyes turned to them, and a strange hush fell over the crowd. Ross Oakley had arrived—with a new woman by his side.</p>

<p>People gawked, not just because of who Ross was, but because of how striking Sasha looked.</p>

<p>The contrast of her elegance against the backdrop of flashing lights made her seem untouchable—dangerous, even.</p>

<p>And the fact that she was with him? That elevated her to near-mythical status in an instant.</p>

<p>Ross was used to this kind of attention. Sasha, not so much—but she carried herself with poise, as if she&#x27;d belonged to this world all along.</p>

<p>Despite the packed club, space seemed to open up around them as they made their way to the dance floor.</p>

<p>People instinctively stepped aside, creating a circle—a private stage just for them. It wasn&#x27;t out of fear. It was reverence.</p>

<p>Ross didn&#x27;t command attention. He earned it, simply by being.</p>

<p>They began to dance. Slowly at first, the music swelling around them.</p>

<p>Sasha moved with ease, fluid and graceful, meeting Ross&#x27;s rhythm step for step.</p>

<p>His hands never wandered too far, but there was a constant tension—an invisible pull that tethered them together. A game of proximity, and anticipation.</p>

<p>&quot;How does it feel,&quot; Sasha asked, her voice rising just above the music, &quot;to be so respected? So adored by everyone? They all know you, and they&#x27;re all watching.&quot;</p>

<p>Ross smiled as he moved in sync with her. &quot;It&#x27;s never bothered me,&quot; he said. &quot;But I think you&#x27;re underestimating how much attention you command. A woman like you doesn&#x27;t walk into a room unnoticed. You&#x27;re the kind men regret letting go—and the kind women pretend not to envy.&quot;</p>

<p>Sasha gave a small laugh, but there was a flush rising in her cheeks. &quot;You&#x27;re smooth. I&#x27;ll give you that.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;And yet,&quot; Ross said, leaning a little closer, &quot;you&#x27;re still here.&quot;</p>

<p>She didn&#x27;t respond. Instead, she let her body answer—closing the space between them just enough to let the friction speak for her.</p>

<p>The music shifted, the beat deeper, more sensual, and their dancing followed suit. Her hands slid up his chest, resting just beneath his collarbone.</p>

<p>His breath hitched, barely noticeable, but Sasha caught it.</p>

<p>Their eyes locked again, that same intensity from the restaurant now magnified under the club lights.</p>

<p>This wasn&#x27;t innocent. It hadn&#x27;t been from the start.</p>

<p>There was too much heat between them, too many unspoken things lingering in the air.</p>

<p>And now, with the world watching but no one daring to interrupt, they hovered on the edge of something electric.</p>

<p>Ross leaned in, his breath warm against her cheek, his lips brushing just near enough to feel.</p>

<p>&quot;You&#x27;re not stopping me,&quot; he said, voice low, velvety.</p>

<p>&quot;Should I?&quot; she whispered.</p>

<p>That was all it took.</p>

<p>He kissed her.</p>

<p>At first, it was soft, deliberate—testing the waters. But Sasha didn&#x27;t hesitate.</p>

<p>Her hands moved up, around his neck, and she kissed him back hungrily, lips parting to welcome him in.</p>

<p>There was nothing tentative now. No holding back. It was passion ignited, a week of tension erupting in seconds.</p>

<p>The crowd around them faded into nothing.</p>

<p>The music, the lights, the voices—it all vanished.</p>

<p>There was only the warmth of his hands on her waist, the pressure of his mouth on hers, and the shocking realization that she hadn&#x27;t kissed like this in a long, long time.</p>

<p>Ross broke the kiss first, barely pulling back. &quot;Your lips taste good,&quot; he said softly, forehead resting against hers.</p>

<p>&quot;So are yours,&quot; Sasha breathed, her eyes searching his.</p>

<p>&quot;Hmmmm.&quot;</p>

<p>Their lips met again, slower this time—deeper. It wasn&#x27;t a public display anymore.</p>

<p>It was something more private, more telling. Something Sasha didn&#x27;t want to admit she wanted.</p>

<p>But she did. Every part of her did.</p>
