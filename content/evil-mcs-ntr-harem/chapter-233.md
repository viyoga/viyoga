<p>One week later, nearly two dozen hired ex-soldiers, clad in dark tactical gear and wearing masks, sat in tense silence.</p>

<p>Armed only with pistols, they had been tasked with infiltrating what was described as a relatively easy target.</p>

<p>The men joked among themselves, questioning why such a simple mission required so many of them.</p>

<p>&quot;Overkill, if you ask me,&quot; one muttered with a smirk, adjusting his mask.</p>

<p>&quot;Yeah, but we&#x27;re not paid to ask questions,&quot; another replied, already envisioning the payout. Laughter rippled through the group, lightening the mood as they waited for the signal to move.</p>

<p>Minutes ticked by. Impatience began to creep in.</p>

<p>&quot;What&#x27;s the holdup? Why&#x27;s it taking so long?&quot; one man grumbled, tapping his pistol against his thigh.</p>

<p>&quot;I don&#x27;t know, mate. Don&#x27;t ask me,&quot; another snapped, irritation in his voice.</p>

<p>&quot;Shut your traps!&quot; their leader barked, silencing the chatter. &quot;We&#x27;re a go in a few minutes, so get ready.&quot;</p>

<p>But their masters—those orchestrating the mission—were caught in a moment of hesitation.</p>

<p>Reina Hirose stood apart from the operation, her brow furrowed in deep thought. In front of her was her loyal assistant and bodyguard, watched with a mix of concern and impatience.</p>

<p>&quot;What&#x27;s wrong, boss? Why are you hesitating?&quot; Mari asked quietly, her voice laced with curiosity.</p>

<p>Reina sighed, her eyes distant.</p>

<p>&quot;I don&#x27;t know, Mari. Something doesn&#x27;t feel right. My judgment is clouded by my desire to bring Ren back home. I can&#x27;t tell if I&#x27;m making the right choice.&quot; Her words, spoken in Japanese, carried the weight of a mother&#x27;s conflicted heart.</p>

<p>Mari tilted her head, her tone firm.</p>

<p>&quot;So what, boss? Ren&#x27;s your daughter. If I had a daughter, I&#x27;d do whatever it took to get her back—even kill the president of any nation if I had to.&quot; Her loyalty and conviction were unwavering, a reflection of the lengths Reina herself might go to for her child.</p>

<p>Reina&#x27;s lips pressed into a thin line as she stared at the ground, lost in thought. The seconds stretched unbearably long before she let out a heavy sigh.</p>

<p>&quot;Hah…&quot; she exhaled, and with a steely resolve hardening her features, she made her decision.</p>

<p>&quot;Do it.&quot;</p>

<p>The command was relayed swiftly to the team on standby.</p>

<p>&quot;It&#x27;s on, boys!&quot; the leader of the group called out. &quot;Let&#x27;s make it clean and fast. Check your gear one last time, and let&#x27;s move!&quot;</p>

<p>The team sprang into action, their earlier joking replaced by cold efficiency. Weapons were readied, silencers attached, and the plan reviewed one final time.</p>

<p>The night had just begun, and they were ready to execute.</p>

<p>A few minutes later, the group of infiltrators breached the grounds of Ross&#x27;s mansion, moving like shadows under the cover of night.</p>

<p>Everything seemed to be going according to plan until a figure emerged from the side of the mansion.</p>

<p>Clad in black and wearing a demon mask, the man walked slowly, almost leisurely, toward the main entrance of Ross&#x27;s home.</p>

<p>The eerie stillness of his movements unsettled the infiltrators, forcing them to halt.</p>

<p>The team exchanged silent signals, their decision unanimous: the man in the demon mask had to be eliminated. One of the infiltrators crawled forward, his pistol steady as he aimed for the demon mask&#x27;s head.</p>

<p>Poot!</p>

<p>The suppressed shot rang out, and the bullet struck the demon mask square in the face. But instead of dropping to the ground, the masked figure remained standing, entirely unfazed.</p>

<p>&quot;What the hell?&quot; someone whispered, disbelief rippling through the group.</p>

<p>The masked man tilted his head slightly, as if amused. Then, his voice broke the silence, low and guttural, carrying an unnatural resonance.</p>

<p>&quot;Maggots. Become food in my belly.&quot;</p>

<p>The realization dawned too late. This was no ordinary man.</p>

<p>It was Brandon.</p>

<p>With a faint whoosh, Brandon vanished from where he stood, reappearing instantly behind the man who had taken the shot.</p>

<p>Before anyone could react, the soldier&#x27;s head was severed cleanly from his body, the sound of flesh and bone parting sharp in the night.</p>

<p>Cranch!</p>

<p>Cranch!</p>

<p>Cranch!</p>

<p>The grotesque sound of chewing filled the air as Brandon devoured his victim. The team froze in horror, the reality of their mistake crashing down on them.</p>

<p>&quot;AHHHHHHHH!&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;DEMON!&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;Get away!&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;RUN!&quot;</p>

<p>Poot!</p>

<p>Poot!</p>

<p>Poot!</p>

<p>Poot!</p>

<p>Poot!</p>

<p>Panic broke their ranks as they scattered, firing wildly at the figure that was now a nightmare incarnate.</p>

<p>But it was futile.</p>

<p>Brandon moved with impossible speed, darting between them like a predator among prey.</p>

<p>One by one, they fell, their screams cut short as they were reduced to lifeless husks. His attacks were not just lethal—they were cruelly efficient, each motion precise and devastating.</p>

<p>By the time it was over, the infiltrators were gone, their bodies reduced to grotesque puppet food. Brandon stood amidst the carnage, his form as menacing as ever.</p>

<p>The demon mask glinted in the moonlight as he turned toward the mansion, his voice echoing in the stillness of the night.</p>

<p>&quot;Pathetic. Send more next time. But wait… there&#x27;s two more,&quot; Brandon whispered, his voice carried away by the cold evening wind as he vanished into the shadows.</p>

<p>Back in the car, Reina sat in silence, her instincts prickling.</p>

<p>&quot;Something&#x27;s wrong,&quot; she muttered, her unease deepening.</p>

<p>Just minutes had passed since the team launched their operation, but the ominous feeling in her gut refused to subside. She opened her mouth to say more, but—</p>

<p>BANG!</p>

<p>The sharp crack of glass breaking shattered the silence. The driver&#x27;s side window exploded inward, shards flying everywhere, and in the next instant, Mari was yanked out by something—or someone.</p>

<p>&quot;…&quot; Reina froze for a moment, her mind racing. But panic didn&#x27;t take hold. Years of calculated decisions and high-stakes negotiations had honed her composure.</p>

<p>She quickly reached for the handgun concealed in her bag, crouched low beside the car door, and made herself as small as possible to avoid being an easy target.</p>

<p>Her weapon was trained on the left side of the car, where Mari had been taken.</p>

<p>The air outside was eerily quiet. Reina&#x27;s breath was shallow, her senses on high alert. She didn&#x27;t know how or what had taken Mari, but stepping out of the car would be suicide.</p>

<p>And yet, even with her caution, she wasn&#x27;t prepared for what came next.</p>

<p>Shiiing!</p>

<p>A metallic screech tore through the night as a hole appeared in the car&#x27;s roof. Reina&#x27;s eyes widened in shock as the entire ceiling was ripped away, flung aside with terrifying ease.</p>

<p>She looked up—and there he was.</p>

<p>Brandon.</p>

<p>Standing above her, bathed in moonlight, he was a vision of primal horror. His demon mask glinted menacingly, his figure radiating an aura of unstoppable malice.</p>

<p>Without hesitation, Reina raised her gun and fired.</p>

<p>Poot! Poot! Poot!</p>

<p>The shots rang out in quick succession, her entire magazine emptied into him. But it was futile. The bullets seemed to vanish against him, leaving not even a scratch.</p>

<p>Brandon tilted his head, almost as if amused.</p>

<p>&quot;Those toys don&#x27;t hurt me. Sorry,&quot; he said with an eerie calmness.</p>

<p>In one fluid motion, he leaped down into the car. Reina, despite her resolve, felt her mind falter as she tried to process what she was seeing.</p>

<p>Before she could react further, Brandon&#x27;s hand shot out, gripping her neck with ease.</p>

<p>To him, she was weightless, a mere doll in his grasp. Reina struggled, clawing at his arm, but his grip was unyielding, as though her resistance was nothing more than an annoyance.</p>

<p>The world began to blur. Her vision dimmed, her strength fading. In her final moments of consciousness, she saw his mask, the glowing eyes behind it burning into her soul.</p>

<p>And then, there was nothing.</p>

<p>&quot;You&#x27;re lucky my master wants you,&quot; Brandon said coldly, his voice carrying an unsettling calmness. A whole minute later and he effortlessly hoisted the two unconscious women over his shoulders.</p>
