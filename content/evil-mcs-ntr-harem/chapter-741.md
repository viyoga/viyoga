<p>Without warning, Ross leaned down, his lips brushing close to Tianna’s ear.</p>

<p>Whatever he whispered was so quiet, so deliberate, that not even the house’s high-tech listening devices could pick it up.</p>

<p>The microphones placed all around—designed to capture every sound, every secret—caught nothing.</p>

<p>Just the movement of his lips.</p>

<p>Just the sudden change in Tianna’s eyes.</p>

<p>For a heartbeat, she froze—her expression blank, unreadable.</p>

<p>Then everything shifted.</p>

<p>What happened next left the entire house in shock.</p>

<p>&quot;I’ll do it!&quot; Tianna blurted suddenly. Her voice trembled, too high, too forced.</p>

<p>&quot;Come on, let’s go! I’ll take a bath!&quot;</p>

<p>And just like that, she grabbed Ross’s hand—not gently, not calmly, but with urgent, shaky fingers—and practically dragged him toward the washroom, her face pale, her breathing quick.</p>

<p>The contestants watched in stunned silence.</p>

<p>&quot;What the hell just happened?&quot; someone muttered, breaking the silence.</p>

<p>But no one had an answer.</p>

<p>Whatever Ross had said... whatever he’d done... it had flipped Tianna’s reaction in an instant.</p>

<p>Too fast.</p>

<p>Too strange.</p>

<p>People exchanged glances. Some raised eyebrows. Some frowned in confusion.</p>

<p>It wasn’t just a change of heart—it was like fear had taken hold of her.</p>

<p>And while Ross followed with that same composed smile on his face, eyes forward and calm, Tianna looked like she was being pulled toward something she couldn’t escape.</p>

<p>Everyone was thinking the same thing, but no one dared say it out loud:</p>

<p>What did Ross whisper to her?</p>

<p>And more importantly—</p>

<p>Why did it work?</p>

<p>Inside the washroom, the moment the door shut behind them, Tianna’s demeanor changed completely.</p>

<p>The calm, sweet girl from earlier—the one who blushed easily and spoke in whispers—was gone.</p>

<p>In her place stood someone fierce. Her eyes burned with anger, her jaw clenched tight.</p>

<p>This wasn’t the shy Tianna everyone knew.</p>

<p>This was a version of her that would fight tooth, nail, and bone to protect what mattered.</p>

<p>Her voice trembled with restrained fury as she began, &quot;How did you kn—&quot;</p>

<p>But she didn’t finish.</p>

<p>Ross raised a hand and pressed his fingers gently against her lips, silencing her.</p>

<p>Then, with a glance upward, he pointed at the camera mounted in the corner of the ceiling.</p>

<p>Without a word, he peeled off his shirt and stretched it over the lens, blocking its view.</p>

<p>It was a temporary fix—just enough to cover the camera’s &quot;eyes.&quot; But that wasn’t all.</p>

<p>With a subtle movement of his fingers, Ross activated one of his myriad hidden techniques.</p>

<p>The air shimmered faintly for just a moment, almost like heat waves rising from the floor.</p>

<p>The sound in this small space—their words, their breaths—was now isolated, sealed from the listening devices embedded in the walls.</p>

<p>Only Ross and Tianna would hear what came next.</p>

<p>&quot;Go on,&quot; Ross said calmly, eyes meeting hers with quiet intensity. &quot;You were saying?&quot;</p>

<p>Tianna stared at him, almost in disbelief.</p>

<p>Then she yanked slightly on the chain that connected their wrists, as if testing him.</p>

<p>The metal links rattled softly between them.</p>

<p>&quot;How did you know?&quot; she asked again, voice low but sharp. &quot;No one’s supposed to know.&quot;</p>

<p>Ross didn’t answer right away. He just looked at her with that maddening, unreadable smile.</p>

<p>&quot;Not many people are good at hiding secrets,&quot; he replied finally.</p>

<p>&quot;Especially when they’re trying too hard to appear invisible.&quot;</p>

<p>Tianna narrowed her eyes. &quot;You’ve been watching me.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;Since day one.&quot;</p>

<p>She exhaled sharply, frustrated, caught between anger and anxiety.</p>

<p>&quot;It’s not easy being cuffed to someone like you,&quot; she muttered, shaking the chain. &quot;You’re too quiet. Too smart. Too... observant.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;And you’re too careful,&quot; Ross said, stepping closer. &quot;Which is exactly why I noticed.&quot;</p>

<p>Tianna turned away for a second, hands tightening into fists.</p>

<p>She was cornered. Exposed. And she hated it.</p>

<p>But more than that—she feared what Ross might do with what he knew.</p>

<p>&quot;Don’t worry,&quot; he said softly, reading her thoughts like an open book. &quot;I’m not here to ruin you.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;Then what do you want?&quot;</p>

<p>Ross’s smile deepened, his eyes never leaving hers.</p>

<p>&quot;I want to have you for myself.&quot;</p>

<p>Tianna recoiled slightly, her expression twisting into one of pure disgust. Her voice came sharp and cold.</p>

<p>&quot;Keep dreaming.&quot;</p>

<p>Her tone was laced with defiance, her jaw set. But Ross only chuckled, as if he found her resistance amusing—expected, even.</p>

<p>&quot;Fine,&quot; he said, shrugging lightly. &quot;Say no all you want. But I don’t have anything to lose in this game. You, though? You’ve got far too much on the line.&quot;</p>

<p>His smile didn’t fade, but the warmth behind it vanished.</p>

<p>He stepped closer, lowering his voice, his words now a blade she couldn’t dodge.</p>

<p>&quot;You and Hugh. Real-life boyfriend and girlfriend. You think no one’s noticed how careful you are? How you don’t talk too much. How you avoid looking at each other too long. But I see it. I saw it the moment he stood up for you out there.&quot;</p>

<p>Tianna’s breath caught. She took an instinctive step back, her cuffed wrist pulling Ross with her.</p>

<p>But he didn’t let her retreat far.</p>

<p>&quot;You’ve both kept it a secret to stay in the game. You thought if no one knew, you could keep playing separately. Double your chances. Maybe even win it for yourselves.&quot; He paused, then added, with a mocking tilt of his head, &quot;Smart strategy.&quot;</p>

<p>Tianna didn’t speak. She was too still. Too quiet. Her eyes dropped to the tiled floor.</p>

<p>But Ross wasn’t done.</p>

<p>&quot;One word from me,&quot; he whispered, &quot;and it’s over. I tell the house, the producers, the audience—hell, even a careless slip to the camera crew—and both of you are done. Disqualified for failing your own secret ultimate challenge with the house boss.&quot; The words echoed around the washroom, bouncing off the cold tiles and pressing against her chest like a weight.</p>

<p>Ross leaned in closer, his voice softer now—but far more dangerous.</p>

<p>&quot;And when that happens, that one million dollars you’re dreaming of? The one that could pay for your mom’s cancer treatment?&quot;</p>

<p>He looked her in the eye. &quot;It disappears. Just like that.&quot; He snapped his fingers.</p>
