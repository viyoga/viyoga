<p>&quot;Fuck you, Ross! I don’t know what you did or how you did it—but I killed you once, and I’ll kill you again!&quot; Wilson roared, spit flying from his lips as fury twisted every line of his face.</p>

<p>He pointed his blade at Ross with a trembling hand, not from fear, but from the overwhelming rage burning inside him.</p>

<p>Behind him, his men surged forward, forming a semi-circle around their leader.</p>

<p>A sea of bodies—one hundred twenty-one armed fighters, each wearing a smug, confident expression.</p>

<p>Their boots thudded loudly against the marble floor as they approached, the sound echoing like a war drum.</p>

<p>They weren’t just confident.</p>

<p>They were certain.</p>

<p>One man—even Ross—couldn’t stop them.</p>

<p>Ross, seated lazily on his throne, simply watched them approach.</p>

<p>His fingers tapped rhythmically on the armrest, his expression more amused than concerned.</p>

<p>&quot;Is that so?&quot; he murmured, tilting his head as if seeing something humorous in Wilson’s bluster.</p>

<p>Then his grin sharpened.</p>

<p>Without rising from his throne, Ross lifted one hand and made a slow, deliberate gesture in the air, as if pulling invisible strings.</p>

<p>The temperature in the vast chamber dropped.</p>

<p>A chilling wind swept through the hall, suddenly snuffing out several torches.</p>

<p>Shadows stretched unnaturally long.</p>

<p>The ground trembled—softly at first, then violently enough that some of Wilson’s men staggered.</p>

<p>&quot;What the hell...?&quot;</p>

<p>A chorus of footsteps echoed... dozens, then hundreds... synchronized, relentless.</p>

<p>From every dark corner of the hall—from behind pillars, above rafters, and even through cracks in the stone—figures began emerging.</p>

<p>One mask appeared. Then another. Then ten. Then fifty.</p>

<p>A torrent of masked warriors flooded into view, each wearing an expressionless demon mask, each moving with silent coordination.</p>

<p>Their armor was dark, matte, and cold, their weapons forged with eerie, sinister designs.</p>

<p>They surrounded Ross in a perfect circle, forming a wall of death.</p>

<p>One hundred of them... then even more.</p>

<p>At their forefront stood Brandon, towering and imposing, his demon mask marked with jagged crimson streaks that signified his command.</p>

<p>He rested his massive cleaver-like blade on his shoulder, his posture calm, patient... hungry.</p>

<p>Wilson’s men froze.</p>

<p>Their bravado faltered.</p>

<p>&quot;This... this can’t be real,&quot; one of them whispered, stepping back.</p>

<p>Ross finally shifted, leaning comfortably into his throne as though he had settled into a private theater.</p>

<p>&quot;Well,&quot; he said with a soft chuckle, &quot;now our numbers are equal.&quot;</p>

<p>His eyes gleamed dangerously.</p>

<p>&quot;Boys,&quot; he called out, his voice echoing like a king addressing his army, &quot;make sure they don’t die too quickly.&quot;</p>

<p>He paused.</p>

<p>Then added with a cruel, satisfied smile:</p>

<p>&quot;Make them suffer.&quot;</p>

<p>Brandon lowered his blade.</p>

<p>Every masked warrior leaned forward in unison.</p>

<p>And then—</p>

<p>The horde surged.</p>

<p>A thunderous battle cry exploded through the hall as Ross’s undead minions rushed forward with terrifying speed.</p>

<p>Wilson’s men barely had time to lift their weapons before the first clash rang out—metal against metal, bone against flesh, screams mixing with the thunder of combat.</p>

<p>Ross rested his chin on his palm, watching the chaos unfold before him like a private performance crafted for his amusement.</p>

<p>Blood splattered.</p>

<p>Men screamed.</p>

<p>Bodies flew across the chamber as Brandon carved through the front lines with monstrous strength.</p>

<p>And still Ross watched, calm and entertained, as if he were merely enjoying the opening act of a much longer show.</p>

<p>&quot;No—NOOOOOO! GET AWAY FROM ME!&quot; one of Wilson’s men shrieked, stumbling backward, his voice cracking under the sheer weight of panic.</p>

<p>His scream was swallowed by dozens more as the demon-masked warriors closed in like a tightening noose.</p>

<p>The gunfire started first—wild, frantic, uncontrolled.</p>

<p>Muzzle flashes lit up the hall as bullets tore through the air, slamming into the undead soldiers.</p>

<p>Dozens of rounds punched into their arms, chests, and skulls.</p>

<p>Holes ripped through their bodies; chunks of flesh and bone were blasted out.</p>

<p>But the undead didn’t even flinch.</p>

<p>It was as if their bodies were made of fog and iron at the same time.</p>

<p>Bullets entered—but nothing stopped.</p>

<p>They kept walking, jaws clenched beneath their masks, eyes burning with cold malice.</p>

<p>Abilities followed next.</p>

<p>Fireballs exploded across the chamber, bathing undead warriors in flames.</p>

<p>Electric arcs danced between their bodies.</p>

<p>Wind blades sliced through their torsos.</p>

<p>Ice spikes impaled legs and chests.</p>

<p>A barrage of magic thundered forward so violently that the entire hall shook.</p>

<p>And still... they advanced.</p>

<p>Unhurried.</p>

<p>Unstoppable.</p>

<p>Untouched in spirit even as their bodies tore apart.</p>

<p>Brandon took a blast of fire to the face—an inferno that would have melted steel—and swatted it aside with one hand before continuing forward, his heavy footsteps echoing like a death knell.</p>

<p>&quot;WHY AREN’T THEY FALLING?!&quot; another man screamed, voice breaking.</p>

<p>&quot;THEY’RE MONSTERS! MONSTERS!&quot; someone else cried as he emptied his magazine until the gun clicked dry.</p>

<p>But their terror only deepened when the undead finally reached them.</p>

<p>Ross’s soldiers needed no blades. No spears. No guns.</p>

<p>Only their hands.</p>

<p>And those hands were far, far more dangerous.</p>

<p>The first impact was sickening.</p>

<p>A fist smashed into a man’s sternum, caving it inward like brittle wood.</p>

<p>He dropped, choking on blood, eyes bulging from the instant, catastrophic pain.</p>

<p>&quot;AHHHHHHHH! F-FUCK! PLEASE—PLEASE!&quot; the man gurgled before another blow shattered his jaw and sent teeth spraying across the floor.</p>

<p>Nearby, another fighter swung desperately at an undead warrior—only for his fist to be caught mid-air.</p>

<p>The undead tightened its grip.</p>

<p>Crunch.</p>

<p>The man’s wrist snapped backward at an impossible angle.</p>

<p>He screamed, only for a knee to drive into his ribs, snapping them one by one.</p>

<p>He collapsed in a sobbing heap.</p>

<p>But they weren’t allowed to die quickly.</p>

<p>Brandon reached a fleeing man, grabbed him by the back of his neck, and slammed him into the ground so hard the marble cracked.</p>

<p>The victim writhed, only for Brandon to slowly, deliberately grind his boot into the man’s spine.</p>

<p>Pop.</p>

<p>A scream so raw it didn’t even sound human tore through the hall.</p>

<p>Another undead soldier grabbed a man by the ankle and dragged him across the floor as he clawed and kicked, leaving bloody streaks behind.</p>
