<p>Ross would make sure Ella never forgot the night he took her.</p>

<p>He’d etch himself into her body and memory so deeply that even Mario, despite everything, would understand why it had to be this way.</p>

<p>Yes, Ross thought, standing up and grabbing his keys. He wasn’t just accepting the offer.</p>

<p>He was going to make it unforgettable.</p>

<p>“Ross, you’re here,” Mario said as he opened the front door.</p>

<p>He looked uneasy—his strong, handsome face shadowed by something between guilt and apprehension.</p>

<p>Ross could tell the man hadn’t slept well. There were faint bags under his eyes, and his smile, though polite, didn’t quite reach them.</p>

<p>Still, he stepped aside to let Ross in.</p>

<p>And then she appeared.</p>

<p>Ella.</p>

<p>Ross’s breath caught for a moment, despite himself.</p>

<p>She stepped into view from deeper in the hallway, and for a second, everything around her seemed to slow.</p>

<p>She was every bit as stunning as he remembered—no, more.</p>

<p>The light from the hallway framed her figure perfectly, casting a soft glow that made her skin seem to shimmer.</p>

<p>Her cardigan, pale pink and loosely draped, hung open to reveal a silky camisole in a soft mauve hue that hugged her delicate frame.</p>

<p>The lacy trim along the neckline subtly hinted at what lay beneath without being overtly provocative.</p>

<p>It was tastefully sexy—feminine and elegant.</p>

<p>Her jeans were designer, dark blue, and high-waisted, perfectly tailored to fit her figure.</p>

<p>They hugged her hips and thighs in just the right way, emphasizing her natural curves and long legs.</p>

<p>Even the simple silver earrings she wore caught the light with every small movement, completing a look that was quietly breathtaking.</p>

<p>But it wasn’t just her beauty that held Ross’s attention—it was her expression.</p>

<p>There was a subtle war playing out on her face: the uncertainty in her eyes, the tension in her jaw, the way she clutched one arm across her stomach as if to shield herself.</p>

<p>Her cheeks were lightly flushed, and her eyes, though they flicked to meet Ross’s briefly, dropped away almost instantly.</p>

<p>She was clearly nervous, and not just in the bashful, flirty way women sometimes were.</p>

<p>No—this was something deeper.</p>

<p>Ella was standing on the edge of a choice that both terrified and excited her.</p>

<p>The idea of being touched—claimed—by someone other than her husband was foreign and jarring.</p>

<p>Her entire body seemed aware of the implications of what was about to happen, and Ross could feel the fragile tension in the air.</p>

<p>“Hi… Ross,” she said quietly, her voice barely above a whisper.</p>

<p>It was soft, smooth, and laced with hesitation.</p>

<p>She tucked a strand of hair behind her ear, eyes still not quite able to meet his fully.</p>

<p>Ross gave her a warm smile—reassuring, not pushy. He understood.</p>

<p>This wasn’t a one-night fling for her.</p>

<p>It was a deliberate step into the unknown, taken not out of lust, but perhaps out of loyalty, sacrifice, or even a desperate hope to fix something broken.</p>

<p>“Hi, Ella,” Ross replied calmly, his voice low and steady. “It’s good to see you again.”</p>

<p>There was a silence after that—heavy but not uncomfortable.</p>

<p>Mario lingered by the door, shifting awkwardly.</p>

<p>“I’ll, uh… give you two a minute,” he muttered, turning and disappearing toward the kitchen. He didn’t look back.</p>

<p>Ross slowly stepped further inside, his eyes never leaving Ella.</p>

<p>He took his time, not approaching her directly, but not retreating either.</p>

<p>She stood still, her hands gently clasped now, her chest rising and falling a little quicker than normal.</p>

<p>“You look… beautiful,” Ross said honestly.</p>

<p>Ella blushed deeper, almost visibly trying to suppress a reaction.</p>

<p>“Thank you,” she said, eyes still downcast.</p>

<p>Another pause.</p>

<p>“You don’t have to rush anything,” Ross added. “We go at your pace. This only happens if you want it to.”</p>

<p>That made her finally lift her gaze.</p>

<p>Her eyes met his—and for the first time, there was something new behind them.</p>

<p>Still nervous, yes. But also… a flicker of curiosity. Maybe even desire.</p>

<p>“I… I know,” she said softly. “It’s just strange. I never imagined something like this would ever happen.”</p>

<p>Ross nodded. “Neither did I. But… here we are.”</p>

<p>She looked at him for another long moment, her lips parting slightly as if she were about to say something else—but instead, she simply nodded.</p>

<p>“I’ll get us some water,” she said, almost as an excuse to break the intensity of the moment.</p>

<p>She turned and walked toward the kitchen, her hips swaying slightly, the soft scent of her perfume lingering in the air she left behind.</p>

<p>Ross stood alone in the hallway, exhaling slowly.</p>

<p>This was going to be delicate—but also unforgettable. He had no doubt.</p>

<p>And neither, he suspected, would Ella.</p>

<p>The three of them sat through dinner like actors in a quiet, uncomfortable play.</p>

<p>Every bite, every word, every glance across the table felt rehearsed and hollow.</p>

<p>The food was good—Mario had made sure of that—but none of them were truly tasting it.</p>

<p>Their minds were elsewhere.</p>

<p>They tried to talk about normal things: a new movie, a sports score, even traffic.</p>

<p>But nothing could erase the awareness of what was waiting at the end of the meal.</p>

<p>The small talk was just noise, a flimsy cover for the elephant in the room.</p>

<p>Mario kept glancing at Ella when he thought Ross wasn’t looking.</p>

<p>Ella avoided both their eyes, chewing slowly, her fingers resting on the stem of her wineglass.</p>

<p>Ross said little, only speaking when prompted. He was respectful, composed, but there was something simmering behind his calm expression—something neither of them could miss.</p>

<p>By the time dessert was offered, no one had the appetite for it.</p>

<p>They sat in a few more minutes of awkward stillness before Mario finally stood up and cleared his throat.</p>

<p>“Well,” he began, his voice rough and lower than usual, “I guess… I’ll leave you two to it.”</p>

<p>There it was. The curtain pulled back. The truth acknowledged.</p>

<p>Ella looked up at him, startled by how suddenly the moment had arrived.</p>
