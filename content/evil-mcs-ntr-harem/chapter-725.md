<p>Morning came, and as usual, the house gathered for breakfast.</p>

<p>Everyone had already eaten by the time Ross entered the kitchen.</p>

<p>He moved with calm precision, preparing his own meal with the same mastery as always.</p>

<p>The aroma of sizzling spices and savory broth soon filled the air, enough to make anyone’s mouth water.</p>

<p>He had just plated his breakfast and was about to sit down when Heaven appeared in the doorway.</p>

<p>Her eyes widened at the sight of the food—steaming, vibrant, and impossibly appetizing.</p>

<p>She swallowed hard, unable to hide her longing.</p>

<p>&quot;Where’s mine?&quot; she asked, trying to sound casual but already stepping closer.</p>

<p>Ross looked up at her with a slow, playful smile and pushed a plate forward.</p>

<p>&quot;Right here,&quot; he said. &quot;But... there’s a price.&quot;</p>

<p>He tilted his head slightly, eyes gleaming with mischief.</p>

<p>&quot;You’re my girlfriend now, remember? So, you owe me a kiss.&quot;</p>

<p>Heaven froze. Her lips parted, but no words came out.</p>

<p>She glanced at the food again—perfectly cooked meat, golden rice, sauce that shimmered with heat and spice—and her stomach growled in betrayal.</p>

<p>The cravings were too strong.</p>

<p>The taste of his cooking was something her body had begun to depend on, something her mind couldn’t resist.</p>

<p>&quot;...Fine,&quot; she murmured at last.</p>

<p>She stepped toward him, hesitant at first, then leaned in.</p>

<p>Ross lifted the edge of his mask just enough to reveal his lips.</p>

<p>The kiss she gave was quick, soft, barely more than a brush—but it was enough.</p>

<p>The mask didn’t slide back into place.</p>

<p>Ross chuckled, the sound low and full of mischief, his lips still exposed, eyes gleaming with playful arrogance—and something else, something more primal.</p>

<p>He clearly wasn’t done with her.</p>

<p>&quot;See? Not so bad,&quot; he said casually, as if that light peck had barely scratched the surface of what he truly wanted.</p>

<p>Then his tone dropped, deeper and more intimate. &quot;But... is that what you call a kiss where you’re from? Let me show you how we kiss where I come from.&quot;</p>

<p>Before Heaven could react, Ross reached for her. In one swift motion, he pulled her toward him, catching her completely off guard.</p>

<p>She let out a soft gasp as her balance tipped and she landed squarely in his lap.</p>

<p>Her hands instinctively went to his chest, trying to push off, but she didn’t get the chance.</p>

<p>&quot;W-Wait—D—&quot; she began, flustered.</p>

<p>But he was already leaning in.</p>

<p>His mouth found hers, and this time the kiss was different—hard, consuming, possessive.</p>

<p>&quot;Mmmph—!&quot; she protested into his lips, eyes widening as his arm circled around her waist and held her in place.</p>

<p>Her body stiffened, instinct screaming for space, for control—but then the warmth began to rise.</p>

<p>The kiss was overwhelming.</p>

<p>He wasn’t just pressing his lips against hers—he was claiming her.</p>

<p>His tongue swept across hers without hesitation, his movements confident and skilled.</p>

<p>Her thoughts began to unravel, torn between resistance and desire.</p>

<p>Her heart raced, thundered in her ears, and her hands, once tense against his chest, began to curl slowly into the fabric of his shirt.</p>

<p>She told herself she should push him away.</p>

<p>But she didn’t.</p>

<p>Her body was betraying her.</p>

<p>It wasn’t just the kiss—it was the way he held her, the way his breath mingled with hers, the heat of his skin, the dominance in the way he moved.</p>

<p>Something primal awakened inside her, and it made her pulse thrum wildly.</p>

<p>A soft moan escaped her lips before she could stop it.</p>

<p>Ross deepened the kiss in response, as if rewarding her for giving in.</p>

<p>His hand slid down her back, fingers grazing the curve of her waist before boldly moving to her front.</p>

<p>Heaven gasped when his palm cupped her breast through her top—firm, possessive, unapologetic.</p>

<p>His thumb pressed in, slowly, then more firmly, squashing the soft mound through the thin fabric.</p>

<p>She arched slightly into his touch before she realized what she was doing.</p>

<p>Heat surged through her. Her entire body felt like it was on fire—her skin tingled, her legs trembled.</p>

<p>She clung to him now, her breath coming in shallow, trembling bursts between kisses.</p>

<p>The smell of him—warm, spicy, familiar—filled her senses and made her feel dizzy.</p>

<p>By the time Ross finally pulled back, both of them were breathing heavily.</p>

<p>His hand slipped away, but the heat it left behind lingered like a brand.</p>

<p>Heaven sat frozen in his lap, dazed and breathless, lips swollen from the kiss, her eyes struggling to focus.</p>

<p>Her cheeks were flushed, her hair slightly tousled, and her chest rose and fell with every shaky breath.</p>

<p>Ross looked at her with a lopsided grin, still calm, still composed, as if he hadn’t just turned her world upside down.</p>

<p>&quot;Now that’s a kiss,&quot; he said smoothly.</p>

<p>Heaven didn’t respond. She couldn’t.</p>

<p>She looked away, trying to hide her flushed face, but her heart was pounding too loudly for her to think straight.</p>

<p>She felt like she’d just crossed a line—and there was no going back now.</p>

<p>The scent of the untouched food still lingered in the air, but she couldn’t even look at it.</p>

<p>Her appetite had shifted to something far more dangerous.</p>

<p>And Ross... Ross was watching her like he already knew.</p>
