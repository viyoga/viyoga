<p>Still, Nicole had no choice but to suck it up—this had been her dream since childhood.</p>

<p>She had always wanted to be a great actress, to see her name in the credits of blockbuster films, to walk red carpets and hear the roar of applause from adoring fans.</p>

<p>Now that she finally had a leading role, she was determined to give it her all, no matter how challenging or emotionally exhausting it became.</p>

<p>The only problem? Ross Oakley.</p>

<p>From the moment they started filming, he had been an enigma—one moment the picture of professionalism, the next, a walking temptation.</p>

<p>He carried himself with effortless confidence, his every movement calculated yet natural. He knew how to work a camera, how to control a scene, and, more dangerously, how to control people.</p>

<p>&quot;Breakup scene! Take one!&quot;</p>

<p>The camera rolled, and Nicole took a deep breath, willing herself into character. She was supposed to be devastated, heartbroken, pushing away the man she once loved. But Ross made it difficult.</p>

<p>His eyes, dark and intense, locked onto hers, and his voice, deep and dripping with emotion, sent an involuntary shiver down her spine.</p>

<p>His touch was featherlight yet deliberate, his fingertips grazing her arm as he pleaded for her to stay.</p>

<p>Nicole&#x27;s breath hitched—not because of the script, but because of the very real, undeniable pull between them.</p>

<p>Ross knew.</p>

<p>He could see it in the way her pupils dilated, in the way her chest rose and fell just a little too quickly. He could smell it, that intoxicating scent of her body reacting to his presence.</p>

<p>It was subtle, but he had spent enough time around women to know when one was losing herself in him. And Nicole was losing herself, whether she realized it or not.</p>

<p>It was all part of his plan.</p>

<p>Sooner or later, she would be in his bed, moaning his name, clinging to him in a way no script could ever dictate.</p>

<p>The thought alone sent a surge of satisfaction through him, and though he kept his expression controlled, a smirk tugged at the corner of his lips.</p>

<p>The days bled into weeks, then months. With each passing scene, the shift in Nicole became more evident—not just to Ross, but to everyone around them.</p>

<p>At first, it was subtle. She lingered after takes, laughing a little too much at his jokes, finding excuses to be near him even when the cameras weren&#x27;t rolling.</p>

<p>Then, it became more obvious—small gestures that didn&#x27;t go unnoticed.</p>

<p>She brought him coffee without being asked, fussed over his appearance before scenes, and looked at him with an admiration that bordered on worship.</p>

<p>&quot;Tsk, tsk. Another goddess about to fall for Ross Oakley,&quot; one of the crew members muttered under their breath, watching from the sidelines.</p>

<p>&quot;Yeah, I don&#x27;t get it,&quot; another chimed in.</p>

<p>&quot;She was all about professionalism at first, and now? She&#x27;s practically fawning over him.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;I don&#x27;t see the appeal,&quot; someone scoffed. &quot;What do women even see in him?&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;He&#x27;s rich, tall, ridiculously charismatic,&quot; came the inevitable response. &quot;And a damn good actor. Oh, and did I mention he&#x27;s rich? Like, billionaire rich?&quot;</p>

<p>They turned their heads just in time to see Nicole handing Ross a bottle of water and a protein bar during a break. He accepted it with a lazy smile, murmuring something in that low, husky voice of his.</p>

<p>Whatever he said made her blush, a soft giggle slipping from her lips.</p>

<p>If her eyes could take shape, they would&#x27;ve been glowing hearts by now.</p>

<p>Ross barely had to lift a finger.</p>

<p>She was already his.</p>

<p>And the best part? She didn&#x27;t even realize it yet.</p>

<p>One month later, the final scene of the movie was finally filmed. It was a grand celebration, a wedding finale that required over a hundred extras to fill the background.</p>

<p>Laughter, music, and the joyful hum of the crowd created an almost magical atmosphere. It felt real.</p>

<p>&quot;You may now kiss the bride,&quot; the officiant, dressed in ceremonial robes, declared.</p>

<p>Ross and Nicole turned to each other, their roles slipping into place effortlessly. He cupped her face gently, his eyes searching hers for just a moment before their lips met.</p>

<p>The kiss was tender yet passionate, carrying the weight of every emotion their characters had endured throughout the story.</p>

<p>The audience within the scene erupted into cheers and adoring sighs, their applause echoing through the grand set.</p>

<p>But Nicole barely heard them.</p>

<p>Because in that moment, she wasn&#x27;t sure where the acting ended and reality began.</p>

<p>Ross&#x27;s lips were warm, familiar, and intoxicating. The way he held her, the way he deepened the kiss just slightly before pulling away, left her breathless.</p>

<p>She stared at him, her heart hammering in her chest, and for the briefest moment, she swore she saw something in his eyes—something unspoken.</p>

<p>&quot;Cut! That&#x27;s a wrap! Great job, everyone!&quot;</p>

<p>The director&#x27;s voice shattered the spell, and the set instantly erupted into celebration.</p>

<p>Cheers and applause filled the space as the cast and crew embraced one another, relieved and exhilarated to have completed such a demanding project.</p>

<p>Laughter rang out as drinks were passed around, and the once-fictional wedding quickly transformed into a genuine party.</p>

<p>Nicole tried to join in, tried to smile as everyone clinked their glasses and toasted to their success, but she couldn&#x27;t shake the feeling in her chest.</p>

<p>She had never felt this way before.</p>

<p>And it terrified her.</p>

<p>As the party swirled around her, she found herself drawn to Ross. He stood in the midst of a small group, effortlessly charming everyone with his usual confident smirk.</p>

<p>But when his gaze met hers across the room, something in his expression changed—just for a second.</p>

<p>She hesitated, then walked toward him.</p>

<p>Before she could even say a word, the tears welled up, unbidden and unstoppable.</p>

<p>Nicole felt so much emotions right now and mixed ones that she did not know how to handle them all at the same time.</p>

<p>&quot;Whoa, what&#x27;s wrong?&quot; Ross asked, his brow furrowing in confusion as she stood there, tears streaming down her face.</p>
