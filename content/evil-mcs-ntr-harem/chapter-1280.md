<p>&quot;This is not good,&quot; Su Hanyue muttered inwardly as she watched the situation unfold below.</p>

<p>The Golden Fortune could certainly afford to lose money occasionally.</p>

<p>That was simply part of running a gambling house.</p>

<p>But there was a huge difference between suffering an ordinary loss and allowing one customer to walk away with an absurd fortune.</p>

<p>Jiang Chen’s winnings were growing far too quickly.</p>

<p>What concerned Su Hanyue even more was the fact that the dealer hadn’t managed to turn the game around yet.</p>

<p>Why hasn’t he used the usual methods?</p>

<p>The Golden Fortune had plenty of ways to influence a simple dice game.</p>

<p>They could manipulate the dice, replace them with specially prepared ones, or subtly control the outcome through spiritual energy.</p>

<p>Yet somehow, none of those methods seemed to be working against Jiang Chen.</p>

<p>Another minute passed.</p>

<p>Then the mountain of gold coins in front of Jiang Chen doubled once again.</p>

<p>Twenty thousand gold coins.</p>

<p>The sight finally made Su Hanyue’s expression change.</p>

<p>&quot;That is enough.&quot;</p>

<p>She immediately turned toward Xue Lengyan.</p>

<p>&quot;Get the dealer to stop the game.&quot;</p>

<p>Xue Lengyan nodded.</p>

<p>&quot;Understood.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;Tell Jiang Chen that the game needs a short break. Give him the best food and drinks available. Treat him as an honored guest.&quot;</p>

<p>Su Hanyue’s eyes narrowed as she looked down at Jiang Chen.</p>

<p>&quot;And when the game resumes, I’ll personally be the dealer.&quot;</p>

<p>Xue Lengyan was slightly surprised.</p>

<p>&quot;You’re going to deal personally, Miss Su?&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;Yes.&quot;</p>

<p>Su Hanyue had managed the Golden Fortune for years and had encountered countless gamblers.</p>

<p>But someone who could continuously win against the house like Jiang Chen was extremely rare.</p>

<p>She wanted to see for herself whether his incredible winning streak was truly luck.</p>

<p>&quot;I’ll take care of it immediately.&quot;</p>

<p>Xue Lengyan hurried downstairs.</p>

<p>A few minutes later, she reached the gambling table and approached the dealer.</p>

<p>&quot;That’s enough for now,&quot; she said. &quot;The game is taking a short break.&quot;</p>

<p>The surrounding gamblers immediately started whispering.</p>

<p>&quot;Why are they stopping now?&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;Isn’t Young Master Jiang Chen on a winning streak?&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;Perhaps the Golden Fortune needs time to prepare.&quot;</p>

<p>Jiang Chen merely smiled.</p>

<p>&quot;A break? Fine.&quot;</p>

<p>He didn’t seem bothered in the slightest.</p>

<p>While servants began bringing out food and drinks for him, Xue Lengyan quietly pulled the dealer aside.</p>

<p>&quot;What is happening out there?&quot;</p>

<p>The dealer looked extremely nervous.</p>

<p>&quot;I... I don’t know, Miss Su.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;Then explain why Jiang Chen keeps winning.&quot;</p>

<p>The dealer swallowed.</p>

<p>&quot;I tried everything.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;Everything?&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;I attempted to tamper with the dice, but every time I tried, the result somehow still matched Jiang Chen’s prediction.&quot;</p>

<p>Su Hanyue’s eyes narrowed.</p>

<p>The dealer continued nervously.</p>

<p>&quot;I even replaced the dice ten times.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;Ten times?&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;Yes.&quot;</p>

<p>He looked genuinely bewildered.</p>

<p>&quot;Different dice. Different cups. Different methods. I even personally checked them afterward.&quot;</p>

<p>His expression became increasingly frustrated.</p>

<p>&quot;But somehow, no matter what I do, the dice always seem to land exactly the way Jiang Chen predicts.&quot;</p>

<p>Su Hanyue fell silent.</p>

<p>For the first time, a serious expression appeared on her beautiful face.</p>

<p>Ten sets of dice.</p>

<p>Different methods.</p>

<p>And Jiang Chen still won every time.</p>

<p>That was no longer something she could simply dismiss as ordinary luck.</p>

<p>&quot;Interesting...&quot; she murmured.</p>

<p>Her gaze shifted toward Jiang Chen, who was currently eating and drinking comfortably while surrounded by his mountain of winnings.</p>

<p>&quot;Then I’ll see for myself.&quot;</p>

<p>The corner of Su Hanyue’s lips lifted slightly.</p>

<p>&quot;When the game resumes, I’ll personally deal the dice.&quot;</p>

<p>She wanted to know exactly what kind of secret this young man was hiding.</p>

<p>Ten minutes later, Miss Su finally descended the stairs.</p>

<p>The moment she appeared, the atmosphere on the gambling floor noticeably changed.</p>

<p>Nearly every man’s attention was drawn toward her.</p>

<p>Her mature beauty and graceful presence made her stand out even among the many beautiful women working at the Golden Fortune.</p>

<p>She walked down the stairs with calm confidence, completely accustomed to receiving such attention.</p>

<p>&quot;If I could have a woman like Miss Su in my life, I could die happy,&quot; one gambler muttered.</p>

<p>He had practically voiced the secret wish of every man watching her.</p>

<p>Miss Su ignored the surrounding gazes and walked directly toward Jiang Chen’s table.</p>

<p>&quot;Hello, Young Master Jiang Chen,&quot; she said with a charming smile. &quot;I’m Su Hanyue. I’ll personally be your dealer from this point onward.&quot;</p>

<p>Jiang Chen looked at her before giving a relaxed smile.</p>

<p>&quot;Fine by me. I have a feeling I can’t lose today. Lady Luck seems to be on my side.&quot;</p>

<p>He gestured toward the dice.</p>

<p>&quot;So, what are we waiting for? Let’s begin.&quot;</p>

<p>Miss Su smiled.</p>

<p>&quot;Of course.&quot;</p>

<p>The game resumed.</p>

<p>The dealer placed the dice beneath the cup, shook them several times, and finally stopped.</p>

<p>&quot;Your choice, Young Master Jiang Chen?&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;Odd.&quot;</p>

<p>The cup was lifted.</p>

<p>The result matched his prediction.</p>

<p>The crowd immediately erupted into whispers.</p>

<p>Another round.</p>

<p>&quot;Even.&quot;</p>

<p>Again, Jiang Chen won.</p>

<p>Another round.</p>

<p>&quot;Odd.&quot;</p>

<p>Another victory.</p>

<p>Miss Su’s smile gradually became more restrained.</p>

<p>She had personally prepared the dice.</p>

<p>They were specially modified gambling dice, designed to respond to subtle spiritual energy manipulation.</p>

<p>She had even secretly activated several mechanisms intended to interfere with Jiang Chen’s predictions.</p>

<p>Yet the dice stubbornly refused to cooperate.</p>

<p>No matter what she did, the final result somehow matched whatever Jiang Chen announced.</p>

<p>The young man simply kept winning.</p>

<p>His pile of gold coins grew larger and larger.</p>

<p>Soon...</p>

<p>50,000 gold coins.</p>

<p>Then...</p>

<p>80,000.</p>

<p>And finally...</p>

<p>100,000 gold coins!</p>

<p>The entire gambling floor fell into an uproar.</p>

<p>&quot;One hundred thousand!&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;He made that much in such a short time?&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;Is this really luck?&quot;</p>

<p>Miss Su’s expression became increasingly serious.</p>

<p>She couldn’t understand it.</p>

<p>Her methods should have worked.</p>

<p>The dice should have followed her manipulation.</p>

<p>Yet somehow, every attempt ended in failure.</p>

<p>Finally, she raised her hand.</p>

<p>&quot;Let’s stop here for now.&quot;</p>

<p>The game came to an end.</p>

<p>Jiang Chen leaned back comfortably, looking completely satisfied.</p>

<p>Miss Su studied him carefully.</p>

<p>He’s cheating.</p>

<p>She was almost certain of it now.</p>

<p>But how?</p>
