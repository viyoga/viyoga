<p>Ross held them close after each encounter, never rushing, never pulling away.</p>

<p>His arms became a sanctuary, his voice a balm as he whispered how much he loved them, how beautiful they were, how lucky he felt every single day.</p>

<p>They clung to those words, wrapping themselves in them like the soft blankets they draped over their bodies in between each shared moment.</p>

<p>And his wives loved him back with equal intensity.</p>

<p>They praised his strength, his tenderness, his unwavering care—not just as a lover, but as a man, a father, a partner.</p>

<p>They touched him not just to spark pleasure, but to remind him that he was theirs, body and soul.</p>

<p>By the time the week ended, they lay together in the master bedroom, limbs entwined, skin flushed, the scent of their love still lingering in the air like incense.</p>

<p>The windows were open, the wind soft, the world quiet. A moment of stillness finally arrived.</p>

<p>No one spoke for a long while. There was no need.</p>

<p>Their bodies told the story—of joy, of unity, of love that went deeper than words.</p>

<p>A love reaffirmed not just in passion, but in presence.</p>

<p>And Ross, in the center of it all, smiled.</p>

<p>He knew this week would stay with them forever.</p>

<p>***</p>

<p>One year came and went, and in the blink of an eye, it was already 2036.</p>

<p>Time seemed to move faster when life was full—and for Ross, the past twelve months had been nothing short of extraordinary.</p>

<p>It was the year his already large family grew even larger.</p>

<p>With the arrival of thirty more newborns, he now had thirty-six children, each one a precious piece of his heart.</p>

<p>To most people, the idea of raising thirty-six children sounded impossible—absurd, even.</p>

<p>But to Ross, it was a blessing beyond measure.</p>

<p>Every morning, he woke to the sound of his home alive with energy: the soft wails of babies echoing down the long marble corridors, the rustle of little bodies being tended to, the lullabies playing gently in nurseries.</p>

<p>Some nights were sleepless, others chaotic, but Ross embraced it all with open arms.</p>

<p>His mansion, a vast estate nestled in a private countryside, had been transformed into something like a palace for children.</p>

<p>Every wing had its own nursery suite, each one equipped with state-of-the-art technology, toys, cribs, and play areas.</p>

<p>Colorful mobiles dangled from the ceilings, and plush rugs lined the floors for crawling infants and unsteady toddlers.</p>

<p>The air smelled faintly of baby lotion and warm milk, a scent Ross now associated with comfort.</p>

<p>Despite the scale, nothing ever felt impersonal.</p>

<p>Ross was deeply involved in his children’s lives.</p>

<p>He held them, bathed them, sang to them—even if sometimes he mixed up a name or two in his exhaustion.</p>

<p>He knew which baby liked to be rocked and which one hated baths.</p>

<p>He memorized feeding schedules, teething milestones, and the feel of each tiny hand curled around his finger.</p>

<p>He knew, of course, he couldn’t do it alone. And he didn’t try to.</p>

<p>Ross was a planner—a provider.</p>

<p>He believed in building a life not just with love, but with intention and support.</p>

<p>For every child, he hired three pairs of professional nannies, rotating teams that worked in shifts to ensure round-the-clock care.</p>

<p>These weren’t just any caregivers—they were handpicked, trained in early childhood development, CPR-certified, multilingual, and deeply nurturing.</p>

<p>Each nanny was part of a larger system that ran like a well-oiled machine.</p>

<p>Even with the nannies and house staff, Ross never let himself become distant.</p>

<p>He didn’t want to be the kind of father who only visited his kids between meetings or stayed behind a locked office door.</p>

<p>Every day, no matter how busy his schedule, he made time to check on his children—holding them close, reading to them, or just sitting in a nursery chair as they napped on his chest.</p>

<p>He believed that no amount of help could replace presence.</p>

<p>The grandparents, too, played a role.</p>

<p>His wives’ parents often came to visit and stayed for weeks at a time.</p>

<p>They adored the babies and helped where they could, offering guidance and sharing stories from their own parenting days.</p>

<p>But Ross never allowed them to become burdened by responsibility.</p>

<p>He appreciated their help but made it clear they were there to enjoy, not to labor.</p>

<p>He also never placed too much on his wives’ shoulders.</p>

<p>Though many of them wanted to be hands-on mothers—and were—Ross made sure they had choices.</p>

<p>They were not bound to sleepless nights unless they chose to be.</p>

<p>They had access to time for themselves, for each other, and for pursuing their interests and passions.</p>

<p>Whether they wanted to stay by the children’s side all day or simply visit when they had energy to spare, Ross supported them either way.</p>

<p>And in the rare quiet moments, when the babies were asleep and the house dimmed for the night, Ross would walk the halls with a sense of wonder.</p>

<p>He would peek into the nurseries, watch over the soft rise and fall of tiny chests, and feel the overwhelming warmth of love and legacy.</p>

<p>He knew this time would pass quickly.</p>

<p>Soon, their cries would turn into words, those soft footsteps into sprints, and eventually, one by one, they would begin their journeys into the world.</p>

<p>But for now, in this fleeting season, he had them all under one roof. And it was everything.</p>

<p>He didn’t just feel like a father.</p>

<p>He felt like the heart of a living dream—one he never wanted to wake from.</p>

<p>Ross’s physical needs didn’t go neglected either.</p>

<p>While his wives were still recovering from childbirth—some adjusting to motherhood, others taking the time to rest and heal—Ross found comfort and passion in the arms of several other women.</p>

<p>There were seven, to be exact. Some were known to his wives, part of open understandings and quiet agreements.</p>

<p>Others remained secret, their relationships hidden in the shadows, wrapped in layers of flirtation, discretion, and stolen moments.</p>

<p>He didn’t push for full transparency. Not yet. The mystery, the thrill of secrecy, added a kind of fire that made every encounter feel electric.</p>

<p>The unspoken tension, the careful navigation of boundaries, and the risk of discovery only heightened the excitement between Ross and these other women.</p>

<p>For now, they were enough.</p>

<p>Each of them brought something different—adventure, sweetness, challenge, novelty—and together, they fulfilled the part of Ross’s life that still craved connection and pleasure during this unique chapter of fatherhood.</p>

<p>He wasn’t reckless, though.</p>

<p>He was careful, respectful, and ensured that nothing disrupted the harmony of his home.</p>

<p>It wasn’t forever—just for now, while the women he loved most were healing, and while he managed the balance between responsibility and desire.</p>

<p>And in the quiet spaces between it all, Ross reminded himself: every chapter had its purpose, and this one was no exception.</p>
