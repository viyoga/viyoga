<p>&quot;If you&#x27;d let me, I&#x27;d have you screaming my name for hours—so long and so hard you&#x27;d be begging me to stop. Is that something you&#x27;d want, Sasha?&quot;</p>

<p>His words were bold, provocative, and shamelessly direct—yet delivered with that signature confidence that made it hard to tell whether he was joking, seducing, or challenging her.</p>

<p>When Sasha heard those words, a shiver prickled across her skin, raising goosebumps along her arms.</p>

<p>She blinked once, then again, as if trying to shake off whatever spell had momentarily come over her.</p>

<p>Ross Oakley wasn&#x27;t even her type. Not in the slightest.</p>

<p>His features, while striking to some, didn&#x27;t quite fit her usual tastes.</p>

<p>He wasn&#x27;t traditionally handsome—not in the chiseled, magazine-cover way she tended to go for. And yet…</p>

<p>She would be lying to herself if she claimed the man had no pull. Because Ross Oakley didn&#x27;t need classic good looks.</p>

<p>He had something far more dangerous—raw, undeniable charisma.</p>

<p>It clung to him like a second skin, dripping from every confident smirk and every effortless word that rolled off his tongue.</p>

<p>And it irritated her—deeply—that even after she and Kristine had cornered him with their sharpest tongues, throwing barbed insults meant to rattle him, he hadn&#x27;t so much as flinched.</p>

<p>He simply sat there, legs casually crossed, his expression cool and amused, as if they were nothing more than mild entertainment in an otherwise boring evening.</p>

<p>Sasha hated men who were too sure of themselves. The arrogance, the swagger, the way they acted like the world belonged to them—it all rubbed her the wrong way.</p>

<p>And yet, she found herself glancing at Ross again. Watching the way he held himself.</p>

<p>How he never looked away from her, not even for a second. The confidence wasn&#x27;t forced. It was real. And that made it worse.</p>

<p>She would be a hypocrite to say she felt nothing.</p>

<p>Men like Ross—dangerous, dominant, charismatic to the core—they were hard to ignore.</p>

<p>He had presence, the kind that filled the room and demanded attention without him needing to say a word.</p>

<p>Sasha wasn&#x27;t blind. She recognized power when she saw it, and Ross wore it like a tailored suit.</p>

<p>It was just unfortunate, she mused bitterly, that he already had a harem of wives. Women fawned over him.</p>

<p>Threw themselves at him. And the worst part? He didn&#x27;t even chase them.</p>

<p>They came willingly, eager to orbit around his gravity. Sasha had no interest in becoming just another name on that list.</p>

<p>But that didn&#x27;t mean she couldn&#x27;t have a little fun.</p>

<p>She leaned back, folding one leg over the other, her lips curling into a playful smirk.</p>

<p>&quot;I&#x27;m a hard woman to please, Ross,&quot; she purred, her tone low and flirtatious. &quot;You might not be able to afford my fetishes.&quot;</p>

<p>Ross&#x27;s smile didn&#x27;t waver. If anything, it widened. His eyes—dark, intense, and unwavering—traveled slowly over her face, pausing just a moment too long on her lips.</p>

<p>&quot;I&#x27;m a man of money and riches,&quot; he said smoothly, his voice rich like velvet. &quot;Say what you want. No price is too high for me.&quot;</p>

<p>The air between them grew heavier, charged with something unspoken.</p>

<p>Sasha&#x27;s pulse quickened, but she kept her expression cool, unwilling to let him know he was getting to her.</p>

<p>Still, there was no denying it—he was dangerous. Not in the way most men were, but in the way only men like Ross Oakley could be.</p>

<p>The kind who could make you want to step into the fire just to see how long you could stand the heat.</p>

<p>And the worst part? Sasha was starting to wonder if she was already burning.</p>

<p>&quot;Perfect. I might just have room for another boyfriend on my list,&quot; Sasha said with a teasing lilt, her lips curling into a devilish smile.</p>

<p>Ross chuckled, clearly entertained, and leaned back in his seat like a man who was exactly where he wanted to be.</p>

<p>With a wave of his hand, he ordered another round—something fancy, aged, and expensive—and as the drinks arrived, so did more laughter, more veiled flirtation, and surprisingly thoughtful conversation.</p>

<p>None of them had expected the night to unfold the way it did. Kristine had been prepared for a brief, awkward dinner—at best, a confrontation that ended in them leaving early.</p>

<p>Sasha had walked in ready to tear Ross apart with her words.</p>

<p>But Ross... he had other plans. He didn&#x27;t just hold his own against them—he took control of the whole table, like a seasoned performer stepping onto a familiar stage.</p>

<p>The three of them talked for hours. Ross moved effortlessly from playful to philosophical, from teasing to sincere.</p>

<p>He knew exactly when to make them laugh and when to drop just enough vulnerability to keep them intrigued. The man had a gift, and he used it well.</p>

<p>By the end of the night, even Kristine—usually the more reserved and skeptical of the two—had started to warm up to him. Not that she&#x27;d ever admit it aloud.</p>

<p>When Ross finally stood up, adjusting the cuffs of his designer jacket, he gave them both a charming smile.</p>

<p>&quot;Ladies,&quot; he said smoothly, bowing his head just slightly, &quot;thank you for a most... stimulating evening.&quot;</p>

<p>Then he was gone, leaving behind the scent of expensive cologne and a silence that stretched between the two women like a question neither of them wanted to ask first.</p>

<p>Kristine blinked. &quot;What the hell just happened?&quot;</p>

<p>Sasha leaned back in her chair, swirling the last of her wine. &quot;We got played,&quot; she said, not sounding the least bit mad about it.</p>

<p>Kristine turned sharply to her. &quot;What were you thinking?!&quot;</p>

<p>Sasha tilted her head, pretending to think. &quot;Hmm… probably that I could handle him.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;Handle him? Sasha, that man is dangerous,&quot; Kristine said, her voice a mix of disbelief and warning. &quot;You were flirting with him all night!&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;And you weren&#x27;t?&quot; Sasha shot back, raising an eyebrow.</p>

<p>Kristine&#x27;s cheeks turned pink. &quot;I—what? No! I&#x27;m married!&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;Exactly,&quot; Sasha said with a sly smile. &quot;Which is why I stepped in. He was eyeing you first, you know. I just did you a favor.&quot;</p>
