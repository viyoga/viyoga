<p>The chains inside Cyrus seemed to pulse with anticipation as Cyrus, forced by Ross’s will, slowly rose.</p>

<p>His body moved with a grace that belied the turmoil of his mind, hands tracing precise sigils in the air.</p>

<p>Mystical energy erupted, glowing lines and runes spiraling outward, intertwining with the very fabric of the world.</p>

<p>The ground beneath him quaked as spiritual power surged through the land, rivers of energy flowing like molten silver into the sky.</p>

<p>Cyrus Thorn chanted, his voice booming and commanding, yet every note carried the invisible weight of Ross’s control.</p>

<p>The spells he cast were magnificent and terrifying, capable of reshaping the environment, altering the flow of energy, and bending the very essence of life.</p>

<p>The World Heart—a crystal of unimaginable purity and power—was something both delicate and indestructible, depending on how one perceived it.</p>

<p>To the wise, it was the soul of a world, the core that sustained the flow of time, energy, and life itself.</p>

<p>To the greedy, it was a treasure beyond value, a source of boundless power that could elevate even a mere mortal into a god.</p>

<p>A being of unimaginable power!</p>

<p>Finding one was a feat so rare that entire civilizations had risen and fallen without ever glimpsing such a marvel.</p>

<p>Even across the vast ocean of stars, through the infinite scale of the multiverse, only a handful had ever been discovered.</p>

<p>And each of those had brought both miracles and calamities to the worlds that housed them.</p>

<p>The power sealed within a World Heart was beyond imagination.</p>

<p>It was the very rhythm of creation—the pulse that breathed vitality into mountains, oceans, skies, and the living beings that called them home.</p>

<p>But such power was never meant to be owned.</p>

<p>Those foolish enough to attempt to seize it by force often found themselves erased from existence, their souls devoured by the very force they sought to control.</p>

<p>Cyrus knew this truth.</p>

<p>He had studied the ancient records that spoke of how World Hearts had reacted to greed and ambition.</p>

<p>Some had shattered in fury, detonating in divine explosions that consumed countless universes, their essence scattering back into the void to reform elsewhere in time.</p>

<p>Others had gone silent, sealing themselves away from all who dared approach.</p>

<p>And so, when Cyrus discovered that this Earth hid a World Heart beneath its surface, he did not act rashly.</p>

<p>His instincts screamed caution.</p>

<p>The energy he felt radiating from the planet’s core was alive—watchful, even intelligent.</p>

<p>It had chosen to give birth to humanity, to breathe awareness into clay and grant them will. That was when the idea came to him:</p>

<p>If I cannot take the Heart, I will taint its pulse.</p>

<p>He would corrupt its creation—its children.</p>

<p>By infecting the hearts of men with greed, lust, fear, chaos and hatred, he would twist the harmony that the World Heart maintained.</p>

<p>Once its children turned against one another, its energy would falter, and its purity would rot from within.</p>

<p>A corrupted World Heart could no longer defend itself.</p>

<p>It would become vulnerable, pliable, easy to control.</p>

<p>Cyrus stood at the center of a desolate field, ancient runes glowing faintly beneath his feet.</p>

<p>Black smoke coiled around him, thick and suffocating, carrying whispers from realms unseen.</p>

<p>His body trembled, veins glowing faintly red as the corruption ritual reached its peak.</p>

<p>He raised both hands to the sky.</p>

<p>&quot;By the blood of the fallen stars... by the will of the forgotten gods... awaken!&quot;</p>

<p>The world responded.</p>

<p>Bang!</p>

<p>A shockwave tore through the air, shaking mountains and rending clouds apart.</p>

<p>Lightning flashed without thunder, rivers reversed their flow, and even time seemed to hesitate for a heartbeat.</p>

<p>The land screamed beneath the strain of power unleashed.</p>

<p>When it was over, Cyrus collapsed to the ground, gasping for air.</p>

<p>His chest heaved violently; sweat and blood mixed on his face.</p>

<p>The technique had taken everything from him—his spirit, his vitality, his strength.</p>

<p>He lay there trembling, staring up at the fractured sky.</p>

<p>And then, a voice cut through the fading echoes of his struggle. Smooth, cold, commanding.</p>

<p>&quot;Well done, slave.&quot;</p>

<p>Cyrus’s eyes widened. He knew that voice.</p>

<p>A figure stepped out from the darkness—a tall man with a faint smile that seemed to hold both mockery and amusement.</p>

<p>His presence bent reality itself; the ground wilted beneath his feet, and the air turned heavy with dread.</p>

<p>&quot;I will summon you when I have need of you,&quot; Ross said, his tone casual, almost kind, yet every word carried the weight of an unbreakable command.</p>

<p>Before Cyrus could speak, Ross vanished, disappearing as suddenly and mysteriously as he had appeared, leaving behind only a faint echo of his laughter—and the lingering dread that something far greater had just awakened in this world.</p>

<p>&quot;What a stroke of bad luck,&quot; Cyrus muttered, his voice trembling with a bitter laugh that soon erupted into full-blown hysteria.</p>

<p>The sound echoed through the broken landscape like the laughter of a mad god.</p>

<p>His chest heaved, his body still scorched from the power he had unleashed, yet it wasn’t pain that haunted him—it was humiliation.</p>

<p>He had never imagined that he, Cyrus the notorious Bone Emperor, a cultivator who had walked through dimensions and defied entire sects, could be enslaved so easily.</p>

<p>No battle, no resistance—just a word, a look, and his entire will was bound like a puppet’s string.</p>

<p>His laughter died down to a low, trembling murmur. His eyes darkened as he stared at the void sky above him.</p>

<p>Who... no, what... is that man?</p>

<p>The memory of that man’s face alone made his soul shudder.</p>

<p>The man’s voice carried a resonance that felt older than the stars—an authority that bent existence itself.</p>

<p>No mortal, no god, no ancient creature of chaos or immortal emperor should have been able to wield such effortless power.</p>

<p>Yet Ross had appeared before him, smiled like a bored monarch, and enslaved him as though it was nothing.</p>

<p>Cyrus clenched his fists, nails biting into his palms until blood ran down his fingers.</p>

<p>&quot;I will not remain anyone’s servant,&quot; he growled. &quot;Not now, not ever.&quot;</p>
