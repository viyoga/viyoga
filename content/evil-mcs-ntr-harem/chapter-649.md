<p>Sasha and Kristine exchanged a look that spoke volumes.</p>

<p>He clearly didn&#x27;t get it.</p>

<p>Their perfect evening—unfiltered, uninterrupted, unbothered—was now being encroached upon by a stranger who thought charm alone could make up for his lack of boundaries.</p>

<p>Sasha turned back to him, folding her hands in front of her. &quot;You said your date stood you up?&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;Yeah. Crazy, right? She texted last minute. Said she had an emergency. I was already dressed and out the door.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;Hmm,&quot; Sasha said, nodding. &quot;Sounds rough.&quot;</p>

<p>The tone of her voice didn&#x27;t match her words. She of course recognized who this man was. Only someone who was living under a rock wouldn&#x27;t.</p>

<p>Kristine tried again, more directly this time. &quot;Look, we&#x27;re sorry about your night, but we came here to spend time together. Just the two of us.&quot; She frowned. She&#x27;d seen this man defeating her husband on the court 4 times in a row. Kristine definitely has no love for this person.</p>

<p>The man looked between them, seemingly confused that his presence wasn&#x27;t being welcomed with open arms. &quot;No offense, but wouldn&#x27;t you rather have a little company? I mean, you ladies look like you could use some fun.&quot;</p>

<p>Sasha&#x27;s smile disappeared. &quot;We were having fun. Until you sat down.&quot;</p>

<p>The bluntness caught him off guard, and for a moment, his charm faltered but he wasn&#x27;t disheartened at all.</p>

<p>Kristine, ever the gentler of the two, softened the blow. &quot;We&#x27;re really just here for each other tonight. I hope your evening turns out okay, though.&quot;</p>

<p>There was a beat of silence, tension lingering like a thread in the air.</p>

<p>Finally, the man shrugged, muttering something under his breath as he still sat down comfortably like he belonged in the table with the two lovely ladies. &quot;Didn&#x27;t realize girls&#x27; night meant no guys allowed.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;It does when you invite yourself,&quot; Sasha said, just loud enough.</p>

<p>&quot;I see,&quot; Ross said smoothly, his tone unbothered. &quot;But that doesn&#x27;t faze me in the slightest. I just want to get to know you two beautiful ladies a little better. I&#x27;m Ross, by the way.&quot;</p>

<p>He finally introduced himself with a relaxed smile, though it was unnecessary—everyone knew who he was.</p>

<p>Kristine raised an eyebrow, her expression quickly shifting from neutral to ice-cold.</p>

<p>&quot;We know who you are,&quot; she said sharply. &quot;We just didn&#x27;t realize how shameless you are, Mr. Oakley.&quot;</p>

<p>Her voice carried a mix of scorn and restrained fury. Sasha subtly tensed beside her, sensing the tension rise like a storm front. But Kristine wasn&#x27;t finished.</p>

<p>&quot;I guess we shouldn&#x27;t be surprised,&quot; she continued, arms crossed tightly over her chest. &quot;A man with more than two dozen wives at home, and yet here you are—still prowling, still looking for your next thrill. You disgust me.&quot;</p>

<p>The venom in her words was palpable. She wasn&#x27;t just speaking for herself; she was speaking for someone else—her husband, perhaps. There was a personal sting to it, as if this encounter had become an outlet for long-suppressed resentment.</p>

<p>Ross, however, didn&#x27;t flinch. He didn&#x27;t look offended, insulted, or even remotely annoyed. Instead, he offered a calm, almost amused smile, and turned his eyes to Sasha as though Kristine&#x27;s words had bounced right off him.</p>

<p>&quot;Your friend&#x27;s something else,&quot; he said lightly, chuckling. &quot;Got a tongue like a blade on her.&quot;</p>

<p>Sasha met his gaze, unblinking, her eyes narrowing ever so slightly. She wasn&#x27;t impressed by the charm—or the deflection.</p>

<p>&quot;What are you really doing here?&quot; she asked coldly. &quot;You already have a harem of wives. Are you so empty inside that you chase every beautiful woman you see just to fill the void?&quot;</p>

<p>Her voice wasn&#x27;t raised, but it cut deep. Ross&#x27;s reputation had preceded him: a man who didn&#x27;t just flirt or play around, but collected women like treasures—yet he didn&#x27;t abandon them.</p>

<p>He married them. Provided for them. Took responsibility. That fact alone made Sasha hesitate in judging him outright. Was he a monster… or just a man with a strange idea of love?</p>

<p>Ross exhaled, then shrugged casually, his smile never wavering.</p>

<p>&quot;Ah, that&#x27;s an easy one,&quot; he said, as if the answer was obvious. &quot;I like your friend here.&quot;</p>

<p>Kristine scoffed. Sasha&#x27;s jaw tightened.</p>

<p>&quot;She&#x27;s married,&quot; Sasha said firmly.</p>

<p>&quot;So am I,&quot; Ross replied smoothly. &quot;That should make it easier for us to understand each other, don&#x27;t you think?&quot;</p>

<p>He flashed a smile at Sasha, then at Kristine—unbothered, unashamed, and entirely comfortable in his own skin. To him, marriage didn&#x27;t seem like a boundary.</p>

<p>If anything, it was simply part of the game he was already winning.</p>

<p>Sasha didn&#x27;t respond right away. She stared at him, trying to read beyond the charm and arrogance.</p>

<p>Was he joking? Testing them? Or was this truly how he lived—fearless, unapologetic, and utterly immune to judgment?</p>

<p>Kristine, on the other hand, had already heard enough. Her hands clenched at her sides, her face full of fury and disbelief.</p>

<p>&quot;You think everything&#x27;s a joke, don&#x27;t you?&quot; she hissed. &quot;Women aren&#x27;t prizes for you to collect, Ross Oakley. No matter how rich, famous, or charming you think you are.&quot;</p>

<p>Ross tilted his head thoughtfully, as if considering her words.</p>

<p>&quot;No, not prizes,&quot; he said at last. &quot;But some women… they shine so bright, it&#x27;d be a crime not to try and reach out. And if they let me—if—I don&#x27;t take that lightly and I&#x27;d take care of them with all that I and the whole world could offer.&quot;</p>

<p>There was a strange sincerity in his voice, even if his logic was warped.</p>

<p>Sasha let out a slow breath, not sure whether to be intrigued or horrified.</p>

<p>Still, since Sasha was single, it wasn&#x27;t entirely wrong to step in and help her married friend out of this uncomfortable situation.</p>

<p>At the very least, she could shift the focus and give Kristine a moment to breathe.</p>

<p>&quot;How about me?&quot; Sasha asked, her tone playful but edged with curiosity. &quot;You said you wanted to get to know both of us. Does that mean you&#x27;re interested in me too?&quot;</p>

<p>Ross&#x27;s eyes lit up with amusement, his smile deepening.</p>

<p>&quot;Of course,&quot; he said smoothly. Then, leaning in slightly, voice lowering into something huskier, he added, &quot;If you&#x27;d let me, I&#x27;d have you screaming my name for hours—so long and so hard you&#x27;d be begging me to stop. Is that something you&#x27;d want, Sasha?&quot;</p>
