<p>Two hours later, Brandon finally returned, bringing with him an astonishing haul.</p>

<p>Behind him, his men carried a full dozen beasts a lot like the American bison on their backs as if the massive beasts weighed nothing at all.</p>

<p>The sight immediately drew the attention of the survivors.</p>

<p>&quot;Just how strong are these people?&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;That doesn’t surprise me at all.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;Ross Oakley lived an extravagant and incredibly successful life back on Earth. It looks like he’s no different in this alien world.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;Look at those bison! A single one should take several people to move, yet they’re carrying them like sacks of grain!&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;I want to join his group too!&quot;</p>

<p>The crowd buzzed with excitement and envy as they watched Brandon’s team casually unload their catch.</p>

<p>The display of strength was enough to make many people reconsider their future plans.</p>

<p>In this unfamiliar world filled with uncertainty and danger, strength meant survival.</p>

<p>And Ross Oakley possessed plenty of it.</p>

<p>More and more people began looking toward Ross and his camp.</p>

<p>The women, in particular, could not help but steal glances at him.</p>

<p>Some admired his handsome appearance, while others saw him as a source of safety and stability in a world that had suddenly become chaotic and unpredictable.</p>

<p>After all, Ross was not only powerful, but also wealthy, influential, and surrounded by loyal followers.</p>

<p>Wherever he went, success seemed to follow.</p>

<p>Many women quietly made up their minds.</p>

<p>They wanted to get closer to him.</p>

<p>Some hoped to earn his favor through loyalty and hard work.</p>

<p>Others planned to use their beauty and charm.</p>

<p>A few were even willing to become his women if that was what it took to secure a place by his side.</p>

<p>Meanwhile, Ross remained completely unconcerned with the thoughts of the crowd.</p>

<p>He simply nodded in satisfaction at Brandon’s successful hunt.</p>

<p>&quot;Good work,&quot; Ross said.</p>

<p>Brandon grinned proudly.</p>

<p>&quot;It was nothing, Boss.&quot; Brandon replied in his usual brief words.</p>

<p>The surrounding survivors nearly choked when they heard those words.</p>

<p>A dozen massive bison had been hunted and carried back in only two hours, and this man was calling it &quot;no effort.&quot;</p>

<p>The gap between them felt larger than ever.</p>

<p>At that moment, many people understood a simple truth.</p>

<p>In this strange new world, following Ross Oakley might be the greatest opportunity they would ever receive.</p>

<p>Brandon and his men quickly set to work preparing the freshly hunted game.</p>

<p>Skilled hands moved with practiced efficiency as they skinned, cleaned, and roasted the massive bison over makeshift fires.</p>

<p>Within a short time, the air in the ruined city began to change.</p>

<p>A rich, savory aroma of roasted and grilled meat drifted outward, spreading through the broken streets like a signal of life returning to a dead world.</p>

<p>In this new reality, food was not just sustenance... it was power, comfort, and security.</p>

<p>Of course, Ross was not the only capable group in the city.</p>

<p>There were others scattered across the field, some strong, some organized, and some still struggling to adapt.</p>

<p>Game outside the city had not completely disappeared either. In fact, it was plenty.</p>

<p>But even so, none of them could match what was coming from Ross’s camp.</p>

<p>The smell alone set it apart.</p>

<p>It was richer, fuller, more inviting like a feast that belonged to a world that had not yet fallen.</p>

<p>It did not take long for people to notice.</p>

<p>At first, it was only a few individuals who had nothing left to lose. A lone man with torn clothes.</p>

<p>A woman carrying a child. A group of three who had been surviving on scraps for days.</p>

<p>They approached hesitantly, drawn by instinct more than courage.</p>

<p>But as the scent continued to spread, hesitation turned into desperation.</p>

<p>More and more survivors began to gather.</p>

<p>From small clusters at the edge of the ruins, the crowd slowly grew. Dozens turned into hundreds.</p>

<p>People who had once hidden in fear now stepped into the open, pulled forward by the promise of safety and food.</p>

<p>&quot;Ross, please take us!&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;Let us join your group, please!&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;We can work! We won’t be a burden!&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;Ross! I’ll do anything just give me a chance!&quot;</p>

<p>Their voices overlapped, trembling with urgency.</p>

<p>Some shouted until their throats cracked, while others simply stood with tear-filled eyes, afraid that speaking too loudly might cost them their only opportunity.</p>

<p>A few moments later, the crowd shifted.</p>

<p>Ross appeared.</p>

<p>And instantly, everything went quiet.</p>

<p>It was not that he released any pressure or aura. There was no visible force, no overwhelming energy.</p>

<p>And yet, the effect was undeniable.</p>

<p>His presence alone was enough.</p>

<p>Calm. Steady. Controlled.</p>

<p>He stood like a man who had already decided the outcome of everything around him, someone who did not need to prove his authority because it was simply understood.</p>

<p>Even the most desperate survivors found themselves lowering their voices without realizing it.</p>

<p>Ross glanced over the gathered crowd, his expression unreadable but not unkind.</p>

<p>&quot;Hello, everyone,&quot; he said at last, his tone even and calm.</p>

<p>A few people flinched at the sudden attention, as if being seen by him was something significant.</p>

<p>&quot;There’s no problem with you joining my camp,&quot; he continued. &quot;But I don’t take in lazy people.&quot;</p>

<p>A quiet murmur passed through the crowd.</p>

<p>Ross did not raise his voice, but every word carried weight.</p>

<p>&quot;You are free to stay,&quot; he said, &quot;but you must prove your worth.&quot;</p>

<p>He paused, letting the silence settle deeper.</p>

<p>&quot;You don’t need to be strong. Strength is not required here. Even the simplest tasks matter like cleaning, gathering supplies, helping build a fire, cooking, maintaining order. Anything that contributes.&quot;</p>

<p>His gaze slowly swept across them all.</p>

<p>&quot;I don’t care what position you take. I only care that you do your part.&quot;</p>

<p>Another brief pause.</p>

<p>Then he concluded firmly, &quot;Welcome to my home.&quot;</p>

<p>For a heartbeat, no one moved.</p>

<p>It was as if they could not process what they had just heard.</p>

<p>Then the tension broke.</p>

<p>A woman near the front suddenly collapsed to her knees, covering her face as she cried.</p>

<p>Another followed. Then another. Soon, the sound of quiet sobbing spread through the crowd like a wave.</p>

<p>Men who had been trying to appear strong wiped their faces roughly, unable to hide their relief.</p>

<p>Parents pulled their children closer, as if afraid the moment might disappear if they let go.</p>

<p>&quot;Thank you, Ross... thank you!&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;We won’t waste this chance!&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;I swear I’ll work hard!&quot;</p>

<p>The voices returned, but this time they were not desperate cries of begging. They were filled with gratitude... fragile, but real.</p>

<p>In a world that had stripped everything away from them, even a simple promise of safety felt like salvation.</p>
