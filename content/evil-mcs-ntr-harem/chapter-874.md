<p>Muscles bulged where there had been none, and her delicate features sharpened into a strong, masculine visage.</p>

<p>When the transformation completed, the woman was no more—standing in her place was a massive man, exuding power and menace.</p>

<p>It was Brandon, Ross’s most trusted puppet, hidden perfectly under the illusion of innocence.</p>

<p>The men froze, staring in disbelief.</p>

<p>&quot;What... what are you?&quot; one stammered, stepping backward as if the walls themselves might swallow him whole.</p>

<p>&quot;How... how is this possible?&quot; another whispered, eyes wide, shaking his head in denial.</p>

<p>&quot;This... this isn’t real,&quot; a third muttered, his voice trembling. But no denial could undo what they had just seen.</p>

<p>Brandon’s piercing gaze swept over them, each eye burning with lethal intent.</p>

<p>His lips curled into a faint, cruel smile, the kind that promised retribution and suffering.</p>

<p>Then, without warning, the first scream erupted—a guttural, agonized sound that pierced through the wooden walls.</p>

<p>The men instinctively recoiled as screams of pain, fear, and rage merged into a horrifying symphony.</p>

<p>The echoes bounced off the walls, making the small house feel like a chamber of nightmares.</p>

<p>They tried to move, to escape, but the room seemed to close in around them, their panic building with every second.</p>

<p>It was like a music coming from hell itself.</p>

<p>Silence followed the initial chaos, but it was a heavy, suffocating silence, broken only by the ragged breathing of the terrified men.</p>

<p>Their eyes darted around, trying to find any hint of what was real or what could still save them—but all that remained was the chilling presence of Brandon, a man who was no longer who he appeared to be, and a fear they would never forget.</p>

<p>&quot;Get away from us, you monster!&quot; someone shouted in sheer panic as he bolted for the door.</p>

<p>He clawed at the handle desperately, yanking it with all his strength—but it didn’t budge.</p>

<p>It was as if the door had fused with the frame, sealing their fate inside this nightmare.</p>

<p>&quot;Don’t be impatient,&quot; Brandon said calmly, his deep voice dripping with mockery.</p>

<p>&quot;We have all the time in the world.&quot; A slow, sinister smile spread across his face as heavy footsteps echoed behind him.</p>

<p>From the shadows, several towering figures emerged, their faces hidden behind grotesque demon masks.</p>

<p>One by one, they stepped into the dim light until eleven of them stood in a perfect line—the same number as Billy and his friends.</p>

<p>&quot;Shall we begin?&quot; Brandon asked softly, his words carrying the weight of a death sentence.</p>

<p>His eyes locked on Billy, like a predator singling out its prey, and he began to stalk forward with deliberate, measured steps.</p>

<p>Billy’s breath caught in his throat.</p>

<p>He turned and ran, heart pounding, but there was nowhere to go—the windows were barred, the exits sealed.</p>

<p>Behind him, chaos erupted as the masked giants descended on his friends.</p>

<p>The air filled with screams as the men attacked with brutal precision.</p>

<p>Clothes were ripped away in seconds, shredded like paper under the overwhelming force of their hands.</p>

<p>Some of Billy’s friends fought back, swinging fists in wild desperation, but it was useless—their punches landed with dull thuds, as if striking solid stone.</p>

<p>The masked figures didn’t flinch, their inhuman strength turning the struggle into a grotesque game.</p>

<p>Billy stumbled into a corner, chest heaving, his mind screaming for an escape that didn’t exist.</p>

<p>The sound of tearing fabric, broken bones, and anguished cries clawed at his ears, drowning him in terror.</p>

<p>And through it all, Brandon kept walking toward him—slow, relentless, inevitable.</p>

<p>&quot;My master said to pay special attention to you,&quot; Brandon said slowly, his voice as deep and heavy as a tombstone being lowered into place. &quot;Said I should... entertain you for a long time.&quot;</p>

<p>The words slithered into Billy’s ears, and his stomach dropped. His trembling gaze flicked around the room—what he saw made his blood freeze.</p>

<p>His friends were already in hell.</p>

<p>Screams tore through the air as the masked giants restrained them with ease, ripping away what remained of their dignity.</p>

<p>Clothes were shredded like tissue, fists flew in desperate resistance, but it was like punching slabs of steel.</p>

<p>Every thud of a fist against those bodies made it clear: these things weren’t human.</p>

<p>Billy wanted to look away, but he couldn’t.</p>

<p>The sound of tearing fabric mixed with hoarse cries of pain burned into his mind like a brand.</p>

<p>His legs turned to lead, every instinct screaming at him to run—even though he knew there was nowhere to go.</p>

<p>His blood turned cold especially when the big men fucked the asses of his friends.</p>

<p>&quot;Who... who is your master?!&quot; Billy rasped, his voice breaking as he backed into the far corner, eyes wide with terror. &quot;Why are you doing this?!&quot;</p>

<p>Brandon’s grin stretched slowly, like a predator baring its teeth.</p>

<p>His footsteps echoed softly against the wooden floor, calm and deliberate, each step hammering Billy’s heart harder than the last.</p>

<p>&quot;It doesn’t matter who he is,&quot; Brandon said at last, tilting his head slightly, his voice almost tender—like a lullaby from hell.</p>

<p>&quot;All you need to think about... is me. And the time we’re about to share.&quot;</p>

<p>Billy’s chest tightened. His breath came in short, shallow gasps, sweat dripping down his forehead as his back pressed against the wall.</p>

<p>His mind clawed for options, but there were none. Every exit was sealed.</p>

<p>The windows, the door—everything was locked tight, as if the house itself had turned into a prison.</p>

<p>I can’t let him touch me... I can’t...</p>

<p>&quot;Stay back!&quot; Billy shouted, though his voice cracked, betraying the raw fear in his soul.</p>

<p>He snatched up a broken chair leg from the floor and held it like a weapon, hands shaking violently.</p>

<p>Brandon stopped and stared at the makeshift weapon, then laughed—a deep, cold sound that filled the room like smoke.</p>

<p>&quot;Good,&quot; Brandon murmured, his grin widening. &quot;Fight me. Struggle. It makes it more... fun.&quot;</p>

<p>Billy’s heart hammered so loudly it drowned out the chaos around him.</p>

<p>His friends were still screaming, begging, crying—but their voices sounded far away now, muffled by the blood rushing in his ears.</p>
