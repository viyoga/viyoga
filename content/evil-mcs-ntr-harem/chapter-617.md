<p>Now, Jade merely looked at Thomas like he was a mere joke.</p>

<p>Yes, she was still attracted to him but she mastered the will to fight her urges and this was more than enough to keep Thomas at bay.</p>

<p>Thomas wanted to be direct in his seduction and kiss Jade without asking but Brandon was ever present now beside Jade.</p>

<p>Thomas did not have any chance at all.</p>

<p>One night, Thomas stood in his penthouse apartment, surrounded by opulence—glass, steel, leather furniture, and floor-to-ceiling windows that looked out over the glittering lights of the city.</p>

<p>But none of it comforted him. He stood shirtless by the window, sipping whiskey from a cut-glass tumbler, staring down at the world like a god plagued by mortal doubts.</p>

<p>&quot;Should I keep going... or pick someone else?&quot; he muttered aloud, voice low and gravelly.</p>

<p>He weighed the pros and cons. He could easily find another woman—maybe not one as beautiful as Jade, but certainly one more pliable, more willing. He didn&#x27;t need her.</p>

<p>But that was the problem.</p>

<p>He wanted her now.</p>

<p>Not just for her beauty or the mission. Not even for the thrill of conquest.</p>

<p>But because she had refused him. She had made him feel powerless.</p>

<p>And that... that was something Thomas could not tolerate.</p>

<p>His pride burned hotter than his lust or job.</p>

<p>Logic told him to walk away. His ego demanded that he break her.</p>

<p>He stood there a while longer, staring into the city&#x27;s endless sprawl, until at last, he reached for his phone.</p>

<p>He scrolled past the names of lawyers, assistants, colleagues—until he landed on one that belonged to someone different.</p>

<p>A man who didn&#x27;t work in boardrooms or offices. Someone who operated in shadows, who handled problems that couldn&#x27;t be solved with charm or conversation.</p>

<p>Thomas didn&#x27;t hesitate. He pressed &quot;Call.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;Yo,&quot; came the gruff voice on the other end. &quot;Been a while. You finally need something?&quot;</p>

<p>Thomas sipped his whiskey, voice calm, measured. &quot;Yeah. I&#x27;ve got something I need done. Quietly. No heat. You know how I work.&quot;</p>

<p>The man chuckled. &quot;Of course. Who&#x27;s the target?&quot;</p>

<p>Thomas didn&#x27;t answer right away. He walked across the room, sat down, and leaned back on the couch, staring up at the ceiling.</p>

<p>The plan wasn&#x27;t fully formed yet. He didn&#x27;t want Jade hurt—at least not physically. No, this needed finesse.</p>

<p>She needed to be cornered, emotionally vulnerable. She needed to feel like Ross had truly abandoned her, that there was no one left to turn to.</p>

<p>That he—Thomas—was the only one who cared enough to pick her back up.</p>

<p>&quot;Jade Oakley,&quot; Thomas said at last, his voice low and deliberate.</p>

<p>&quot;Let&#x27;s meet tomorrow. I don&#x27;t want to do this over the phone. Same place. 7 p.m. Don&#x27;t be late.&quot;</p>

<p>A hesitant voice came through the line, quiet at first. &quot;Jade Oakley? Are you talking about one of Ross Oakley&#x27;s wives?&quot;</p>

<p>Thomas narrowed his eyes. &quot;Yeah. Why?&quot;</p>

<p>There was a pause—longer than expected. Then the voice returned, lowered now, almost a whisper.</p>

<p>&quot;You serious? You&#x27;re messing with his woman?&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;Yeah, I said her name. What&#x27;s the problem?&quot; Thomas said, frowning.</p>

<p>The man on the other end exhaled slowly, like he was trying to steady himself.</p>

<p>&quot;Ross Oakley is bad news, Thomas. I mean real bad. He&#x27;s not just some rich guy with power. He&#x27;s... something else. People who cross him don&#x27;t just lose. They vanish. No noise. No trace. Gone.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;I&#x27;ve heard the stories,&quot; Thomas said. &quot;Doesn&#x27;t matter to me. We can plan and even catch him red handed. I know you and I can do it easily.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;I don&#x27;t know, Thosms. I&#x27;ve seen it happen, man. You mess with Ross, and it&#x27;s not just your life on the line. It&#x27;s everyone around you. Friends. Family. Anyone who&#x27;s tied to you.&quot;</p>

<p>Thomas smirked, though there was no humor in it. &quot;Do you really think that&#x27;s enough to put fear in the heart of King Rocky D?&quot;</p>

<p>He half-joked, but there was a weight to his words. The kind that came from a man who&#x27;d stared down worse and walked away. Usually.</p>

<p>&quot;I know you, Thomas,&quot; the voice said with quiet urgency. &quot;Which is why I&#x27;m going to say this, once. Stay away from Ross Oakley. Drop whatever this plan is. Walk away while you still can.&quot;</p>

<p>Thomas&#x27;s smile faded. There was something in the man&#x27;s tone—something that felt final.</p>

<p>&quot;And about the job?&quot; the voice added. &quot;You couldn&#x27;t pay me enough to take it.&quot;</p>

<p>Click.</p>

<p>The line went dead.</p>

<p>Thomas stood there for a moment, staring at the phone in his hand. The words echoed in his mind, but they didn&#x27;t scare him.</p>

<p>No, they excited him.</p>

<p>Because if Ross Oakley really was as dangerous as they said…</p>

<p>Then beating him?</p>

<p>Would be the greatest victory of all.</p>

<p>Thomas would not only get his wife but also put him behind bars or even retire him permanently.</p>

<p>Thomas tinkered with his phone again.</p>

<p>He didn&#x27;t stop there, of course. One refusal wasn&#x27;t enough to deter him.</p>

<p>If anything, it only sharpened his resolve. He had contingencies in place—always had.</p>

<p>So, without missing a beat, he contacted his second option. A different kind of man. Less cautious.</p>

<p>More desperate. The kind of person who didn&#x27;t ask questions as long as the price was right.</p>

<p>Thomas laid out the plan again, adjusting the details to fit his new hire&#x27;s temperament.</p>

<p>He spoke with conviction, with certainty, as if the universe itself would bend to his will.</p>

<p>Failure had never been a word he entertained. Not when it came to Jade. Not when it came to Ross.</p>

<p>He was going to see this through. No matter what.</p>

<p>He would see the plan to fruition, yes. Every piece would fall into place just as he envisioned.</p>

<p>But what Thomas didn&#x27;t know—what he couldn&#x27;t know—was that the wheels were already turning against him.</p>

<p>And in trying to bring down Ross Oakley, he&#x27;d unknowingly stepped into something far larger than his petty need for another job done.</p>

<p>But it would also be the final mistake in his long, chaotic life.</p>

<p>Because some men you don&#x27;t cross.</p>

<p>And some names… you don&#x27;t speak lightly.</p>
