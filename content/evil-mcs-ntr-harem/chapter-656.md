<p>Sasha&#x27;s heart skipped. His reply came so quickly, so smoothly, it was as if he had been waiting. Like he knew she&#x27;d eventually crack.</p>

<p>They began messaging. Light at first. Casual. But then came the call, and after that, another meeting.</p>

<p>Then another. Slowly, almost imperceptibly, they fell into a pattern. It wasn&#x27;t a relationship. It wasn&#x27;t anything official.</p>

<p>But there was something magnetic between them—an unspoken pull.</p>

<p>He never reached out first. It was always her who initiated.</p>

<p>But Ross never hesitated to respond. When she called, he came.</p>

<p>When she asked, he said yes. And every time they met, it felt more dangerous than the last.</p>

<p>He never tried to sleep with her again—not yet—but his touches lingered.</p>

<p>His voice was low and rich when he spoke to her, as if every word was laced with hidden meaning.</p>

<p>And his eyes—God, his eyes—made her feel naked every time they landed on her.</p>

<p>She told herself she had the upper hand. That she was in control.</p>

<p>But it was a lie.</p>

<p>Because she couldn&#x27;t stop thinking about him. About his kiss. About the way his hands fit around her waist like they were made for it.</p>

<p>And more than anything, she couldn&#x27;t stop thinking about what it would be like to let him have her.</p>

<p>Sasha had never given herself to anyone. Not for lack of offers.</p>

<p>Not for lack of opportunity. But because, deep down, she&#x27;d always wanted her first time to mean something.</p>

<p>She wanted it to be more than just heat and urgency.</p>

<p>She wanted it to be with someone who saw her—not just her body, but everything she was.</p>

<p>Ross saw her. Or at least, he looked at her like he did. And that scared her.</p>

<p>Because maybe… just maybe… he could be the one to make her lose herself completely.</p>

<p>Sasha&#x27;s fingers danced over her phone screen as she sent Ross a message.</p>

<p>It was casual at first—just a lighthearted chat, a shared meme, a flirty comment—but it wasn&#x27;t long before they agreed to meet again.</p>

<p>A few hours later, the two sat across from each other at a cozy little diner, the conversation flowing more easily this time.</p>

<p>After dinner, they headed to the movies, sitting close but never quite touching, each silently aware of the growing tension between them.</p>

<p>The weekend after, Ross didn&#x27;t reach out. He wasn&#x27;t playing games—he simply didn&#x27;t feel the need to chase.</p>

<p>Not to his surprise, Sasha called him instead. Her voice was soft but direct as she asked if he wanted to hang out again.</p>

<p>He said yes without hesitation. And just like that, another date happened. Then another. And another.</p>

<p>Over the course of the next few months, the pattern continued. He never called her.</p>

<p>It was always her who reached out first.</p>

<p>They explored new restaurants, strolled through parks, and lingered in coffee shops until closing.</p>

<p>People might&#x27;ve assumed they were a couple, but neither of them had ever defined it.</p>

<p>Despite the flirtation, Ross never pushed for more.</p>

<p>He didn&#x27;t initiate any physical intimacy—not a kiss, not even holding hands.</p>

<p>He was warm and charming, always engaged in conversation, but never crossed that invisible line. Ross wasn&#x27;t in any hurry.</p>

<p>With a household full of beautiful wives who could satisfy his every carnal desire, he didn&#x27;t need Sasha for that.</p>

<p>But Sasha… Sasha was different.</p>

<p>She was still a virgin, and whether she admitted it to herself or not, her body ached for something she didn&#x27;t fully understand.</p>

<p>The sexual tension between them hung heavy in the air during every date, every lingering glance, every moment when their hands brushed just a little too long.</p>

<p>She laughed easily around Ross, felt safe and seen with him, but part of her was beginning to crave more—something deeper, more intimate.</p>

<p>Something she had never experienced before.</p>

<p>Ross, for all his self-control, wasn&#x27;t blind to it.</p>

<p>He could feel it in the way she looked at him, the way her voice softened when they were alone, how she sometimes paused just a beat too long before saying goodbye.</p>

<p>Still, he kept his distance. Whether it was out of respect, caution, or simply patience, he didn&#x27;t act on the desire simmering between them.</p>

<p>And then, one day, something unexpected happened.</p>

<p>They had just finished another peaceful afternoon at the local park.</p>

<p>The sun was dipping low, casting a golden glow over the world, when a voice rang out—sharp, angry, and unmistakably directed at them.</p>

<p>&quot;Sasha? What the fuck are you doing with that guy?!&quot;</p>

<p>Ross turned calmly toward the source of the voice.</p>

<p>A tall, handsome man stormed up to them, his jaw clenched and fists balled at his sides. He looked furious.</p>

<p>Sasha froze, her eyes wide in shock. Her mouth opened slightly, but no words came out.</p>

<p>There was no mistaking the man&#x27;s identity—this was Sasha&#x27;s official boyfriend.</p>

<p>And suddenly, the easy rhythm they had fallen into shattered in an instant.</p>

<p>&quot;I&#x27;m her date,&quot; Ross said, his tone calm and composed, as if the situation didn&#x27;t faze him in the slightest.</p>

<p>His eyes met Wesley&#x27;s with unwavering confidence, his hands casually tucked into his coat pockets.</p>

<p>It wasn&#x27;t bravado—it was certainty.</p>

<p>Wesley took a step forward, his face darkening with rage.</p>

<p>&quot;The fuck you are,&quot; he spat, his voice low and dangerous.</p>

<p>The tension in the air thickened instantly.</p>

<p>Sasha stood frozen between the two men, caught like a deer in headlights.</p>

<p>Her eyes darted from Ross to Wesley, panic rising in her chest.</p>

<p>This wasn&#x27;t how she&#x27;d imagined things coming to light—not like this, not so violently, not so soon.</p>

<p>Wesley was no stranger to jealousy, but this was different.</p>

<p>He wasn&#x27;t just angry—he was humiliated.</p>

<p>The realization hit him like a freight train: while he had believed himself to be her boyfriend, while he&#x27;d been trusting, waiting, thinking she was just busy… another man had been quietly slipping into her life. Into her heart.</p>

<p>And not just any man—Ross Oakley. The guy everyone talked about. The one with money, charm, status.</p>

<p>The one who always seemed untouchable.</p>

<p>The same Ross who had gone viral more than once, who had women fawning over him online and off.</p>

<p>Of course Wesley recognized him. Who wouldn&#x27;t?</p>
