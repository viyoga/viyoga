<p>It had been thirty minutes since the meeting with Thomas ended, yet Jade remained seated behind her desk, motionless, her fingers gently tapping against the armrest of her chair.</p>

<p>The office was quiet now, but it still felt like he was there—like his presence had sunk into the very walls.</p>

<p>He had walked in like he owned the room, like he knew the effect he had on people.</p>

<p>And truth be told, he probably did.</p>

<p>That air of self-assurance, the sharp suit that clung to him just right, and the voice—low, smooth, dangerously calm—still echoed in her ears.</p>

<p>Jade had dealt with many powerful men in her life. Politicians, tycoons, even a few underground figures.</p>

<p>But Thomas... he was different. Too composed. Too poised. Too intoxicating.</p>

<p>She leaned back in her chair with a sigh and glanced at the untouched glass of water on her desk.</p>

<p>Her reflection stared back at her in the glass surface—composed on the outside, but not nearly as calm within.</p>

<p>&quot;What a guy,&quot; she murmured, almost involuntarily.</p>

<p>The words escaped her lips before she realized she&#x27;d spoken them aloud.</p>

<p>She shook her head, forcing herself to refocus, but it was no use. The thought of him lingered like a scent—subtle yet persistent, impossible to ignore.</p>

<p>The rest of the day passed in a blur.</p>

<p>Her meetings felt dull, her assistants had to repeat things twice, and her usually sharp mind wandered more than once.</p>

<p>It wasn&#x27;t until the sun began to dip below the horizon that she finally allowed herself to leave the office, still haunted by the memory of that brief but electrifying encounter.</p>

<p>As her car pulled into the long driveway of the estate, she rolled down the window slightly.</p>

<p>The breeze carried with it the smell of roasted meat and grilled vegetables—savory, rich, comforting.</p>

<p>It tugged at her senses and brought a rare, tired smile to her lips. Home.</p>

<p>She stepped out of the sleek black vehicle, the gravel crunching softly beneath her heels.</p>

<p>The sprawling estate was alive with sound—laughter, splashing water, and the faint thump of music playing in the background.</p>

<p>Here, things were never quiet for long.</p>

<p>With Ross and his multitude of wives, chaos was something of a norm. Extravagance, too.</p>

<p>The household was less like a family and more like a lively court, each member playing their role in the grand drama of domestic life.</p>

<p>But despite the complexity, there was warmth here. Joy. Love. Even if it was unconventional.</p>

<p>Jade followed her senses, the trail of sound and scent leading her through the wide garden path, past flowering hedges and trimmed lawns, until she reached the swimming pool.</p>

<p>And there it was—life in full bloom.</p>

<p>The water sparkled beneath the glow of outdoor lanterns, and the air was filled with the clinking of glasses, the giggles of women, and the laughter of children.</p>

<p>Beautiful women lounged in bikinis around the pool, some dipping their toes in the water, others reclining on chairs with colorful drinks in hand.</p>

<p>Their skin glowed under the ambient light, their smiles relaxed and radiant.</p>

<p>And at the center of it all was Ross.</p>

<p>Of course.</p>

<p>He sat comfortably on a lounge chair, shirtless, golden skin glistening slightly from the heat, surrounded by a dozen adoring gazes.</p>

<p>His aura was magnetic—an effortless blend of charm, confidence, and raw masculinity.</p>

<p>Even after all these years, he remained the gravitational force that pulled people in.</p>

<p>Once upon a time, the women might&#x27;ve shed even their bikinis for an impromptu pool party with him.</p>

<p>But times had changed.</p>

<p>With children now running around the estate, there was a new sense of modesty—at least in public view.</p>

<p>No one wanted to corrupt young minds or start difficult conversations too early.</p>

<p>&quot;Mommy Jade!&quot; four excited voices rang out, cutting through the noise like bells.</p>

<p>She turned, just in time to see her four eldest children racing toward her—barefoot, wet from the pool, towels flapping behind them like capes.</p>

<p>Their eyes lit up with joy, arms wide as they closed the distance.</p>

<p>She dropped to her knees just in time to catch them in a tangle of limbs, giggles, and kisses.</p>

<p>&quot;There&#x27;s my babies,&quot; she whispered, wrapping them close.</p>

<p>Their tiny hands touched her face, her hair, pulling her back into the present—back to where she truly belonged.</p>

<p>Jade spent precious minutes just talking and spending happy time with Ross&#x27; 4 children Alistair, Anya, Adelyn, and Alexander.</p>

<p>They were naturally curious and so inquired about how Jade&#x27;s day went and of course wanted so gifts from her also.</p>

<p>Jade loved to gift clothes to the children that was meant and created only for them.</p>

<p>The scene was chaotic, loud, and slightly overwhelming—but it was hers.</p>

<p>Whatever spell Thomas had cast over her earlier in the day, it began to unravel in the face of this noisy, messy, beautiful life.</p>

<p>These were her roots, her people, her purpose.</p>

<p>She glanced over at Ross, who caught her gaze and gave her a knowing smile, raising a glass in her direction.</p>

<p>Jade smiled back, finally letting herself breathe.</p>

<p>With the sun dipping lower, casting a warm golden hue over the estate, Jade let the sound of laughter and water fade into the background.</p>

<p>Her eyes were focused solely on Ross—calm, relaxed, completely at ease amidst the chaos.</p>

<p>But as her heels clicked against the stone pathway, her steps slowed, turning from casual to deliberately seductive.</p>

<p>She reached for the first button of her blazer.</p>

<p>One by one, she unfastened them, her eyes never leaving Ross&#x27;s.</p>

<p>The cool evening breeze brushed against her skin as she slipped the blazer off her shoulders, letting it fall in a neat puddle at her feet.</p>

<p>Her white blouse was next—smooth silk that clung to her curves—soon peeled away and discarded without hesitation.</p>

<p>Then her skirt, unzipped with a slow drag, slid down her hips and pooled gracefully around her ankles.</p>

<p>In just a few breaths, Jade stood in nothing but black lace: a bra that barely contained her, and matching panties that left little to the imagination.</p>
