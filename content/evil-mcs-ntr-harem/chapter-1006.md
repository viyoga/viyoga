<p>Even now, very few people in the world knew the full extent of Ross’s power — his godlike abilities, his mastery of energy, and the sheer control he had over both the modern and the mystical.</p>

<p>To the public, he was a billionaire, a genius, perhaps even a legend.</p>

<p>But in truth, he was something far greater.</p>

<p>Unfortunately, only some of his wives ever knew about this.</p>

<p>As night fell, Ross stood alone on the balcony of a high-rise overlooking the city.</p>

<p>The lights below shimmered like a sea of stars, but his gaze was fixed far beyond them — toward a distant place, unseen by mortal eyes.</p>

<p>A slow smile curved his lips.</p>

<p>&quot;So it’s finally time,&quot; he murmured. His eyes gleamed faintly, filled with both confidence and amusement.</p>

<p>&quot;Time to meet my new slave.&quot;</p>

<p>And with that, the air around him rippled.</p>

<p>His form blurred, then vanished completely — leaving behind nothing but the soft hum of energy and the whisper of wind where he once stood.</p>

<p>***</p>

<p>&quot;Interesting,&quot; a tall, striking man murmured, his long white hair cascading over crimson robes that seemed to drink in the light around him.</p>

<p>Every movement he made carried a grace that belied the sheer aura of menace he radiated.</p>

<p>&quot;This world... it truly is a hidden gold mine.&quot;</p>

<p>His sharp eyes scanned the horizon, glittering with ambition and greed.</p>

<p>&quot;Even just this world-heart alien alone could grant me limitless benefits,&quot; he whispered, his voice low and intoxicating, almost hypnotic in its intensity. &quot;With it, the path to true immortality wouldn’t even be a dream — it would be within my grasp.&quot;</p>

<p>He let out a low chuckle, almost to himself, his fingers brushing absentmindedly over the folds of his robe.</p>

<p>&quot;But that’s not all... the faith energy here is strong. The devotion of the people... it could serve me well. I wonder if—&quot;</p>

<p>The words died in his throat as a sudden, chilling presence brushed against the edges of his awareness.</p>

<p>His body tensed, the atmosphere around him thickening with warning.</p>

<p>He spun around in a flash, his robes trailing like a crimson river, ready to unleash destruction on whatever dared approach from behind.</p>

<p>What he saw, however, was unexpected.</p>

<p>Standing there, calm and seemingly unremarkable at first glance, was a tall, broad-shouldered man.</p>

<p>His face was ordinary, almost forgettable — and yet, the air around him carried a quiet, oppressive weight.</p>

<p>He radiated a power that was subtle but undeniable, a force that made even Cyrus Thorn hesitate for the first time.</p>

<p>&quot;Yes,&quot; the man said, his lips curling into a confident, almost teasing smile.</p>

<p>&quot;Someone lured you here. That someone... was me. I’m glad you accepted the invitation, Cyrus Thorn.&quot;</p>

<p>At that instant, the air around Cyrus Thorn ignited with raw, malevolent energy.</p>

<p>His aura erupted like a violent storm, shaking the very ground beneath them.</p>

<p>Black chains, countless in number, exploded from his body, writhing and twisting like living shadows.</p>

<p>They moved with terrifying speed and precision, each chain humming with devastating power.</p>

<p>The sheer force was enough to tear the air apart, and the surrounding terrain groaned under the weight of the energy.</p>

<p>Yet as the black chains surged toward Ross, something incredible happened.</p>

<p>The chains, imbued with destructive intent, slowed as they approached him.</p>

<p>They trembled, hesitated, and one by one, sank into the earth, as if bowing to a superior authority.</p>

<p>It was as though a silent command had been issued, an unspoken acknowledgment that the power before them far surpassed anything Cyrus Thorn could wield.</p>

<p>Ross remained motionless, his calm aura unshaken.</p>

<p>The faint curve of his lips betrayed neither fear nor concern, only amusement as he watched the mighty chains that had erupted from Cyrus Thorn’s body kneel in submission.</p>

<p>Cyrus Thorn’s eyes widened in disbelief, his shock transforming quickly into rage.</p>

<p>His lips twisted into a snarl as he realized the impossible: he had underestimated the man standing before him.</p>

<p>The chains that were supposed to annihilate his enemy had faltered, recognizing an authority far beyond his own.</p>

<p>&quot;You... how...?&quot; Cyrus Thorn’s voice trembled, a mixture of fury and disbelief that barely concealed the fear creeping into his heart.</p>

<p>He had always believed himself to be strong, a force of chaos that only a few could challenge.</p>

<p>Yet here he stood, powerless against a man who had revealed only a fraction of his true might.</p>

<p>The world around them seemed to hold its breath.</p>

<p>Trees swayed without wind, dust hovered in midair, and the very energy of the land pulsed in anticipation.</p>

<p>The stage had been set for a confrontation that would shake not just the mountains, but the unseen currents of power flowing through the world itself.</p>

<p>Ross’s eyes gleamed faintly, a spark of amusement in their depths.</p>

<p>&quot;Clever... ambitious... but ultimately, naive,&quot; he said softly. &quot;You thought you could control this world, but it’s already mine in ways you cannot comprehend.&quot;</p>

<p>The air crackled, charged with a tension so thick it felt almost tangible.</p>

<p>&quot;What do you want?&quot; Cyrus Thorn spat, his voice cutting through the air like a whip.</p>

<p>Millions of years of cruelty and ambition had hardened him, forged him into a being feared across realms.</p>

<p>He had lived countless lifetimes under innumerable names, but Cyrus Thorn was the one that carried the weight of his true legacy — a name remembered only by the echoes of history, and one he believed no living soul could recall.</p>

<p>Now, as he faced Ross, a cold realization crept into his heart.</p>

<p>The attack he had unleashed — a strike meant to annihilate his opponent in an instant — had been deflected with terrifying ease.</p>

<p>Cyrus Thorn’s eyes narrowed, a dark glint of defiance burning within.</p>

<p>Death had never terrified him.</p>

<p>Chaos had been his companion, and the world itself had bent beneath his will countless times.</p>

<p>He had known this confrontation might come — the possibility of a rival who could challenge him — and for millions of years, he had prepared.</p>

<p>His mind was steel, his body honed, his soul tempered for battles beyond imagination.</p>

<p>&quot;I want you to serve me, Bone Emperor of the Ghostwood Forest,&quot; Ross said, his voice calm and unwavering, carrying a weight that seemed to press down on the very air around them.</p>
