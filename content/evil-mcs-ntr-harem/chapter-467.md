<p>&quot;Look! Is that who I think it is?&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;Yes! That’s the billionaire Ross Oakley!&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;The goat of basketball!&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;Why is he at our university?&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;Beats me, but I guess we’ll find out soon enough.&quot;</p>

<p>Whispers spread like wildfire as students turned their heads, eyes widening in recognition.</p>

<p>Ross Oakley, the billionaire, the internet sensation, the man whose every move dominated headlines—was walking through their campus as if he belonged there.</p>

<p>No entourage, no bodyguards. Just him. Yet even alone, he commanded attention.</p>

<p>Dressed in nothing more than a fitted shirt and well-worn jeans, Ross still managed to outshine everyone around him.</p>

<p>It wasn’t the clothes that made him stand out but the way he wore them—the effortless confidence, the undeniable presence.</p>

<p>Every step he took was measured, deliberate, exuding an aura of control that sent an unspoken message: he was a man to be reckoned with.</p>

<p>He moved like a lion among sheep, and everyone could feel it. Tall, broad-shouldered, and built like a warrior, Ross possessed a physique that radiated raw power.</p>

<p>The sheer force of his presence made those nearby instinctively shrink back, parting to give him space without even realizing they were doing it.</p>

<p>It wasn’t just his looks or his wealth—it was something deeper, something primal. A man like Ross didn’t need to announce his dominance; it was felt, like the weight of an approaching storm.</p>

<p>Some students stared in awe, others with envy. A few brave ones considered approaching him, only to falter under his piercing gaze.</p>

<p>He wasn’t just rich, wasn’t just famous—he was something more. Something superior. And whether they liked it or not, they all felt it.</p>

<p>Ross didn’t have to walk far. He cut through the parking lot, his confident strides drawing attention even before he reached the school lobby.</p>

<p>Whispers followed in his wake, but he ignored them, his focus locked onto one person.</p>

<p>There she was—his target—seated near the entrance, waiting for someone.</p>

<p>Unfortunately for her, that someone had run into an unexpected delay, giving Ross the perfect opportunity for an introduction. He had no intention of wasting it.</p>

<p>Stopping directly in front of her, he didn’t bother with pleasantries.</p>

<p>Instead, he spoke with the same directness that had made him a billionaire before most people even figured out what to do with their lives.</p>

<p>&quot;I like you,&quot; Ross said.</p>

<p>The young woman turned her head, caught off guard by both the words and the man who had spoken them.</p>

<p>For a split second, surprise flickered across her face, but she quickly masked it with composure.</p>

<p>She was absolutely stunning—eighteen years old, with long, straight, light-brown hair that cascaded past her shoulders in soft, natural waves.</p>

<p>Her skin was smooth and light-toned, glowing under the school’s artificial lighting.</p>

<p>Delicate yet striking, her features held a youthful charm that could steal the breath of any man who laid eyes on her.</p>

<p>Her outfit was effortlessly stylish. A cropped, light pinkish-peach t-shirt clung to her frame, the phrase YOLO printed in a bold, stylized purple font across the front.</p>

<p>The relaxed fit gave her an air of casual confidence, exposing just enough of her toned midriff to be enticing without trying.</p>

<p>Paired with it were high-waisted, light-wash denim shorts, frayed at the hem in a trendy style.</p>

<p>They accentuated her long, slender legs, which she stretched out with the ease of someone who knew exactly how good she looked.</p>

<p>There was no denying it—she was eye candy, the kind of girl who could make heads turn without even trying.</p>

<p>And right now, every pair of eyes in the lobby was glued to them, eager to see what would happen next.</p>

<p>For a moment, she simply studied him. Ross Oakley. Billionaire. Internet sensation. The man whose name dominated headlines.</p>

<p>Of course, she recognized him. Everyone did. His face was plastered everywhere—news articles, social media, magazine covers. Only someone living in total isolation wouldn’t know who he was.</p>

<p>But instead of fawning over him like most girls would, she merely offered a polite, fleeting smile.</p>

<p>&quot;Thank you,&quot; she said lightly. Then, as if he were nothing more than a passing stranger, she turned her gaze straight ahead, feigning indifference.</p>

<p>Ross arched a brow. Oh? So she was going to play it that way.</p>

<p>He could see it—the way her fingers curled slightly on her lap, the way her shoulders tensed just enough to betray that she wasn’t as unaffected as she wanted to appear.</p>

<p>She was aware of him, more than aware, and yet she was pretending otherwise.</p>

<p>Amused, Ross smirked.</p>

<p>He wasn’t the type to be ignored.</p>

<p>&quot;If you think ignoring me will make me go away, you’re making a big mistake. It only makes me want you more,&quot; Ross said smoothly as he slid onto the bench beside her, completely at ease.</p>

<p>The young woman didn’t react at first. No sharp intake of breath, no widening of her eyes—just an almost imperceptible tightening of her jaw.</p>

<p>She had likely dealt with men like him before—arrogant, confident, used to getting whatever they wanted. But Ross Oakley wasn’t just any man.</p>

<p>With a sigh of feigned indifference, she reached for her handbag.</p>

<p>&quot;Not if I do this. Watch me,&quot; she said lightly, rising to her feet in one graceful motion.</p>

<p>It was a simple act—getting up, walking away—but there was an elegance in the way she moved, a quiet defiance in her posture.</p>

<p>She had no intention of entertaining Ross any longer. She wasn’t flustered, wasn’t nervous. If anything, she seemed utterly unimpressed.</p>

<p>But she had barely straightened up when Ross spoke again, his voice calm yet carrying a weight that made the air between them feel heavier.</p>

<p>&quot;If you do that, you might not hear from or see your boyfriend again. So think very, very carefully.&quot;</p>

<p>He didn’t raise his voice, didn’t change his tone. And yet, his words landed like a thunderclap.</p>

<p>The effect was immediate.</p>

<p>Her delicate fingers, still curled around the strap of her handbag, froze in place. Her posture stiffened just enough to betray her momentary shock.</p>

<p>Then, slowly, she turned her head toward him, her expression shifting—shock, then anger, a flicker of uncertainty, before she finally forced herself to regain control.</p>
