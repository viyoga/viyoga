<p>In the center of the room, seated at a large table, was a man. He was nursing a drink, swirling it lazily in his glass.</p>

<p>The glass caught the light from a nearby lamp, casting a brief, shimmering reflection across the surface. Julian’s breath caught in his throat when he recognized the man sitting there.</p>

<p>The familiar face that had been on every screen, in every paper—Ross Oakley, the man who had become a household name.</p>

<p>But something about the way he was sitting there, so casually, his posture relaxed yet commanding, sent a shiver down Julian’s spine.</p>

<p>This was not the recent local celebrity they had seen on television. This was someone far more dangerous, far more unpredictable.</p>

<p>April’s grip tightened on Julian’s hand, but he didn’t flinch. They had both seen him on the news too many times and in the school grounds also to mistake him for anyone else.</p>

<p>&quot;You!&quot; they both exclaimed, almost in unison, their voices echoing in the large room.</p>

<p>The recognition in their eyes was unmistakable, and for a brief moment, a flicker of something like amusement passed through Ross Oakley’s gaze. But it was gone as quickly as it had appeared.</p>

<p>&quot;Ross Oakley!&quot; Julian added, his voice filled with a mixture of disbelief and fury. The man who had made headlines for all the wrong reasons, whose face had become synonymous with wealth and scandal.</p>

<p>Now, he was sitting in front of them like it was all a game.</p>

<p>Ross looked up from his drink slowly, his lips curling into a small, almost amused smile. His eyes, however, remained cold—calculating.</p>

<p>He didn’t seem surprised to see them there, nor did he look particularly interested in their protests.</p>

<p>&quot;Release us at once, or you’re going to regret this stunt!&quot; Julian said, trying to summon all the courage he had left.</p>

<p>His voice wasn’t as steady as he would have liked, but he fought to sound firm, to project strength even though his heart was racing.</p>

<p>He was used to being in control, used to situations where he could handle things, but this—this felt like it was spiraling out of his grasp.</p>

<p>Still, he refused to show weakness. He wouldn’t let April see how afraid he truly was.</p>

<p>April’s eyes flickered with uncertainty, but her grip on his hand tightened, as if she were trying to give him the strength he needed. She knew this wasn’t just about them anymore.</p>

<p>They had been thrust into something far bigger than they could understand, but she refused to believe they were completely powerless.</p>

<p>&quot;You think threatening me will make a difference?&quot; Ross finally spoke, his voice smooth, almost too calm. He didn’t even bother to stand up from the table.</p>

<p>There was something unnervingly casual about his demeanor, as if he had seen far worse than two desperate captives making empty threats. &quot;You’re not in a position to make demands.&quot;</p>

<p>Julian swallowed hard, but he didn’t back down.</p>

<p>He wanted to believe that somehow, some way, they could get out of this, but the feeling in his gut—the one that had never steered him wrong—was telling him that this was only the beginning of something much darker. Read latest stories on empire</p>

<p>April stayed close to him, her nerves just as frayed as his, but her resolve was clear. Neither of them had the luxury of turning back now. They were in this together, for better or worse.</p>

<p>As Ross Oakley leaned back in his chair, the faint sound of ice clinking against the rim of his glass echoed through the stillness of the room.</p>

<p>The atmosphere, thick with tension, seemed to grow even colder with every passing second.</p>

<p>The quiet was oppressive, heavy with the weight of something far more sinister than the mere presence of the masked men surrounding them.</p>

<p>Julian couldn’t shake the feeling that their lives—once so ordinary, filled with all the potential of young love and futures unmarked by violence—were now slipping through their fingers, about to be changed forever.</p>

<p>His gut twisted with dread, the ominous sense that the worst was yet to come gnawing at him.</p>

<p>&quot;Sit. I don’t want to make my guests go hungry,&quot; Ross said, his voice cutting through the silence, as smooth and casual as if he were simply inviting them to dinner.</p>

<p>He stood up from the table with a fluid motion, the sharp lines of his tailored suit moving with him, before strolling toward the bed in the corner of the room.</p>

<p>The bed was large—almost unnecessarily so—and its luxurious linens seemed out of place in such a grim setting.</p>

<p>Ross carried with him a bottle of wine, uncorking it with a practiced ease as he sat down on the edge of the bed, his back relaxed but his presence still undeniably commanding.</p>

<p>He poured himself a drink, taking a slow sip, as though savoring the flavor of the wine as if it were the only thing that mattered in the room.</p>

<p>Meanwhile, the masked men moved quickly to set up the table where Ross had been sitting.</p>

<p>They carried large platters laden with an assortment of food—golden roasted meats, bright vegetables, steaming bowls of rice and pasta, fruits sliced with meticulous care, and delicate desserts arranged like works of art.</p>

<p>The rich scent of the food filled the room, and despite the terrifying circumstances, Julian’s stomach growled in protest.</p>

<p>It was a stark contrast to the dark tension that gripped them all, a bizarre reminder of the normal life they once had.</p>

<p>The men set up two more chairs, placing them beside the table, arranging the food so that it was all perfectly in place—hot and steaming, ready for consumption. But neither Julian nor April moved.</p>

<p>They stood like statues, frozen by the pressure of the situation, unable to comprehend the full gravity of what was happening to them.</p>

<p>The food sat untouched, the table an invitation to something they could not accept.</p>

<p>&quot;I said sit and eat,&quot; Ross repeated, his voice still calm, but now edged with a hint of impatience.</p>
