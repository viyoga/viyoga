<p>The plan—meticulously designed and executed with top-tier operatives—had ended in disaster, just like the countless attempts before it.</p>

<p>Once again, they had underestimated Ross. Once again, they had sent men to their deaths.</p>

<p>Frustration filled the air like smoke.</p>

<p>Faces were pale, eyes wide with disbelief, as if still trying to comprehend how an abduction mission had turned into a massacre straight out of a horror film.</p>

<p>Back to the drawing board.</p>

<p>They had no choice. Their strategy had failed—again. Just as it had the last dozen times.</p>

<p>And every failure only made Ross seem more untouchable. More terrifying.</p>

<p>More inhuman.</p>

<p>As for Ross, the chaos surrounding his enemies was little more than background noise.</p>

<p>He couldn’t have cared less about their failures or frustrations.</p>

<p>In his eyes, they were gnats buzzing around the titan he had become—insignificant, foolish, and destined to be swatted away if they ever truly became annoying.</p>

<p>If Ross wanted to, he could have crushed their entire nation underfoot.</p>

<p>In less than a day, he could destabilize their economy, bring their military to its knees, and turn their so-called leaders into desperate puppets dancing on his strings.</p>

<p>After all, he had done it before—most famously with the United States.</p>

<p>No one had believed it possible until it was too late. Now, the once-mighty superpower functioned with Ross’s quiet approval.</p>

<p>The president smiled for the cameras, but only after receiving whispered instructions through secure lines that all led back to Ross.</p>

<p>But domination no longer thrilled him.</p>

<p>The game had become boring.</p>

<p>There was no challenge in ruling nations anymore.</p>

<p>Politics, military coups, subversive propaganda—he had mastered them all.</p>

<p>At this point, world conquest felt like a child’s game, something better left to overambitious warlords or comic book villains.</p>

<p>Ross had moved on.</p>

<p>He was playing on a different board now. A bigger one.</p>

<p>His vision extended beyond borders, beyond economies, beyond even humanity’s understanding of what power could look like.</p>

<p>While the world obsessed over governments and elections, Ross was engineering legacies, rewriting culture, shaping the next evolution of society itself.</p>

<p>So he let the fools plan and plot. Let them waste time and resources trying to touch him.</p>

<p>Let them keep dreaming. He had more important things to do.</p>

<p>Like enjoy his life.</p>

<p>At that very moment, Ross was reclining in a leather chair inside one of his penthouse atriums—a breathtaking, high-ceilinged space wrapped in glass and overlooking the glittering skyline of the city.</p>

<p>Sunlight streamed in from above, casting golden rays over the marble floors and the quiet ripple of a koi pond built directly into the living room.</p>

<p>In one hand, he held a tablet, lazily scrolling through the daily flood of emails his assistants filtered and forwarded for his personal review.</p>

<p>The subject lines ranged from the flattering to the absurd.</p>

<p>&quot;Starring Role as Antagonist in Mega Budget Film&quot;</p>

<p>Ross scoffed.</p>

<p>&quot;Do a movie with me as the villain? Thanks, but no thanks,&quot; he muttered, tapping delete.</p>

<p>&quot;Exclusive Interview Offer – Highest Bid Ever!&quot;</p>

<p>Delete.</p>

<p>&quot;Invitation to Judge on WorldStar Talent Series&quot;</p>

<p>He rolled his eyes. Delete.</p>

<p>Fame. Publicity. Applause. He had no use for any of it.</p>

<p>The world already knew his name, and he didn’t need to parade himself in front of the cameras to feel powerful.</p>

<p>Attention was a tool—useful when needed, but often just noise.</p>

<p>And then he saw it.</p>

<p>A subject line that made him pause.</p>

<p>&quot;Biggest Reality tv show – Confidential Invitation&quot;</p>

<p>His finger hovered. He tapped it open.</p>

<p>What he read made his brow rise slightly—an expression of genuine interest, rare for someone who had seen and done almost everything.</p>

<p>The message was written in veiled language, hinting at something far more complex than a party or a business proposal.</p>

<p>It wasn’t about money or media. It was... deeper. Possibly entertaining. Definitely intriguing.</p>

<p>Ross leaned forward, reading every line again, slower this time.</p>

<p>&quot;Hmmmm... this could be interesting,&quot; he murmured, lips curling into a small, almost mischievous smile.</p>

<p>He didn’t call anyone. He didn’t delegate it to a handler.</p>

<p>He began typing a reply himself—short, precise, and unmistakably Ross.</p>

<p>&quot;I’ll be there. Prepare accordingly.&quot;</p>

<p>He hit send.</p>

<p>Then he leaned back, staring out over the city with a spark in his eyes. The kind of spark that meant something big was coming.</p>

<p>It wasn’t about ruling the world anymore. It was about reshaping it.</p>

<p>And this time, Ross wasn’t just a player in the game.</p>

<p>He was about to redefine the rules.</p>

<p>***</p>

<p>&quot;What?! Say that again!&quot;</p>

<p>A middle-aged man jumped to his feet, his eyes wide with excitement.</p>

<p>His voice boomed across the sleek, modern office lined with LED screens and marketing posters.</p>

<p>&quot;Ross Oakley accepted our offer!&quot; the young woman standing across from him beamed. She was beautiful, well-dressed, and barely able to contain her joy. &quot;He’s going to be part of our show. He said yes!&quot;</p>

<p>The room fell into a stunned silence before the man erupted again.</p>

<p>&quot;Wow! That’s... that’s unbelievable! But wait—did he ask for some insane appearance fee? You know we have a strict budget. If he demanded something outrageous, we might—&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;No,&quot; the woman interrupted quickly, waving her hand. &quot;He didn’t ask for anything. In fact... he said he’d do the show for free.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;For free?!&quot; The man blinked, stunned. &quot;Ross Oakley? That Ross Oakley? Doing something for free?&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;Yes,&quot; the woman nodded, though her voice grew slightly more cautious. &quot;But...&quot;</p>

<p>The man narrowed his eyes. &quot;But what?&quot; he asked sharply, clearly annoyed by the hesitation.</p>

<p>&quot;He had one condition,&quot; she said.</p>

<p>The man raised an eyebrow. &quot;Aha. There it is.&quot; He crossed his arms, already bracing for something absurd. &quot;Alright. Let’s hear it. What’s the catch?&quot;</p>

<p>She hesitated, then added, &quot;He wants full creative control... over his segment.&quot;</p>

<p>The room fell silent again.</p>

<p>The man stared at her, then suddenly burst into laughter.</p>

<p>&quot;That’s it?! That’s nothing! Of course he can have it! He can wear a flaming crown and speak in riddles for all I care—if Ross Oakley is on this show, we’re going to dominate the ratings!&quot;</p>
