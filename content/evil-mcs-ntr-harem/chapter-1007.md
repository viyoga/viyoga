<p>Cyrus Thorn’s laughter erupted suddenly, loud and harsh, echoing across the mountains like thunder rolling over jagged cliffs.</p>

<p>&quot;Hahaha! All my life, I have been free! I have done as I pleased! I bow to no man, no sect, no heaven — not even death itself could ever subdue me!&quot;</p>

<p>Ross’s lips curved into a faint, dangerous smile.</p>

<p>&quot;I expected nothing less,&quot; he said quietly.</p>

<p>&quot;But freedom... freedom is a fragile thing. There are many ways to enslave a being, Cyrus Thorn. Some far subtler, far more permanent, than mere death.&quot;</p>

<p>Cyrus Thorn’s laughter faltered slightly, a flicker of unease passing through his expression.</p>

<p>Ross’s calm confidence, the quiet power radiating from him, was unsettling in a way that Cyrus had never encountered before.</p>

<p>This was not a mortal man, nor even a cultivator of ordinary legend — this was something far beyond, a force that could bend the very rules of life and death without breaking a sweat.</p>

<p>Before Cyrus could react, the world around him seemed to twist.</p>

<p>Energy surged and coiled like a living thing, and suddenly, the tables turned.</p>

<p>The torturer — the man who had brought fear and death to countless souls — became the tortured.</p>

<p>Cyrus Thorn screamed, a sound that shredded the air and reverberated through the mountains, echoing like a chorus of agony.</p>

<p>&quot;Ahhhhhhhhhh!&quot; His voice carried centuries of pride, cruelty, and rage, now laid bare in pure, unfiltered pain.</p>

<p>The chains that had once obeyed him without question trembled, snapped, and twisted back upon him as if recognizing a far superior force.</p>

<p>The centuries of invincibility he had relied upon, the countless victories, the destruction he had wrought, all seemed to vanish in an instant.</p>

<p>Every memory of domination, every life he had crushed beneath his feet, returned to haunt him as his body and soul were subjected to powers beyond comprehension.</p>

<p>Ross stood silently, the faintest trace of amusement in his eyes.</p>

<p>He watched as Cyrus Thorn writhed, his roars filling the mountains, his aura bending under the invisible chains of Ross’s will.</p>

<p>The world itself seemed to hold its breath, sensing the shift in the balance of power.</p>

<p>A being who had considered himself untouchable, an emperor even, was now kneeling before another — the hunter humbled, the tyrant undone.</p>

<p>Time seemed to stretch, the screams echoing endlessly, until every fiber of Cyrus Thorn’s being trembled with the realization that he was no longer in control.</p>

<p>Ross had demonstrated that power was not just strength or immortality — it was mastery over will, over fate, over the essence of life itself.</p>

<p>The mountains around them quaked under the shockwaves of energy, and even the wind seemed to carry the whispers of the fallen tyrant’s legacy.</p>

<p>Cyrus Thorn, the feared Bone Emperor of the Ghostwood Forest, was learning for the first time what it meant to be truly powerless — and in that moment, Ross’s dominance was absolute.</p>

<p>As the echoes of Cyrus’s agony faded into the night, Ross’s smile widened ever so slightly.</p>

<p>An hour passed, though to Cyrus Thorn it felt like a trillion years at least.</p>

<p>Time had no meaning for him now, only the relentless, suffocating weight of control pressing down upon his body and spirit.</p>

<p>Every bone in his body had been stripped from him, taken and replaced with something alien, something that belonged to another order of power entirely.</p>

<p>He could not move of his own volition; instead, some force beyond his comprehension guided him.</p>

<p>At first, every movement was awkward, clumsy — the elegant grace he had relied upon for millennia absent from his limbs.</p>

<p>Each gesture felt alien, like a puppet being manipulated by invisible strings.</p>

<p>Yet, as seconds stretched into minutes, his motions gradually recovered their natural rhythm.</p>

<p>He moved again with the deadly precision and elegance that had once made him feared across continents.</p>

<p>Ross’s voice broke the silence, calm, chilling, and absolute.</p>

<p>&quot;The art of weaving the bones of your enemies and allies alike, of both humans and monsters, to create an absolute killing treasure... Your Fate-Severing Chains truly live up to their name. But now that its owner’s bones have become part of its links, I wonder... how strong can it really be? Perhaps only you can tell me, slave.&quot;</p>

<p>Cyrus Thorn’s eyes narrowed, but before he could respond, Ross tossed the artifact toward him.</p>

<p>It was a black sphere, roughly the size of a human fist yet impossibly dense, bristling with millions of chains that seemed to writhe as if alive.</p>

<p>When it struck him, the sphere did not merely hit — it fused with his chest, sinking into his flesh and anchoring itself deep inside.</p>

<p>The chains seemed to coil into his body, burrowing into bone, sinew, and spirit until they became inseparable from him.</p>

<p>Thud.</p>

<p>The impact forced Cyrus to his knees.</p>

<p>He struggled to rise, to reclaim the freedom he had always known, but his body would not obey.</p>

<p>Every muscle, every joint, even his will seemed enslaved by a force far greater than his own.</p>

<p>He could feel the chains moving within him, slithering like serpents of pure power, binding him to a will that was not his own.</p>

<p>&quot;Why... why are you doing this to me?!&quot; he roared, his voice a mixture of anger, disbelief, and humiliation.</p>

<p>For countless centuries, he had bowed to no man, no ruler, no god — and now here he was, forced to kneel.</p>

<p>Rage and shame twisted through every fiber of his being, but the more he struggled, the tighter the invisible grip of Ross’s control seemed to become.</p>

<p>Ross stepped closer, his presence calm, yet oppressive.</p>

<p>Every step he took seemed to push down upon the world itself.</p>

<p>&quot;As I told you, Cyrus,&quot; he said softly, almost conversationally, &quot;there are many ways to achieve what we desire. Since you are stubborn by nature, I see no reason to wait for your consent. Your body will obey, even if your mind and mouth resist.&quot;</p>

<p>Cyrus’s chest heaved, every breath a battle between the fire of his rage and the unyielding chains controlling him.</p>

<p>Ross’s gaze softened slightly, a faint, almost predatory smile forming.</p>

<p>&quot;Now,&quot; he continued, &quot;proceed with the plans you had before I arrived. Every ritual, every technique, every step you had prepared in order to capture this world’s heart... perform them exactly as you intended.&quot;</p>
