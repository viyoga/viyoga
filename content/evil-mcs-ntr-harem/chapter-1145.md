<p>The screen flickered to life, illuminating Sabrina’s tired, conflicted expression.</p>

<p>She hesitated—part of her didn’t want to open the photos. She knew it would hurt.</p>

<p>But another part of her felt she had to.</p>

<p>She tapped the gallery.</p>

<p>And then he appeared.</p>

<p>Her boyfriend. Smiling under warm sunlight.</p>

<p>Wrapped around her in a tight embrace at the beach. Making a goofy face during dinner.</p>

<p>Holding her hand as if letting go was never an option. Each picture hit her like a punch to the gut.</p>

<p>Her throat tightened.</p>

<p>She swiped to the next one.</p>

<p>And the next.</p>

<p>And the next.</p>

<p>The more she looked, the heavier her heart became.</p>

<p>&quot;I’m sorry...&quot; she whispered, though no one could hear. The words cracked, barely forming.</p>

<p>She felt sick with guilt—guilt that her thoughts had been drifting toward Ross.</p>

<p>Guilt that her body reacted to him.</p>

<p>Guilt that whenever Ross smiled at her or looked at her with concern, something inside her fluttered.</p>

<p>It wasn’t fair. It wasn’t right.</p>

<p>She lowered her gaze, shame prickling her skin. Am I cheating?</p>

<p>She hadn’t done anything—but the feelings were there.</p>

<p>They were growing stronger every day, and she was terrified of what that meant.</p>

<p>Her eyes blurred with tears. One slipped down her cheek, hitting the phone screen.</p>

<p>Then another.</p>

<p>Then she remembered the cold, crushing truth she had been avoiding.</p>

<p>Her boyfriend...</p>

<p>He was probably dead.</p>

<p>Or worse—he might be one of those zombies outside, shambling endlessly with empty eyes, no recognition, no warmth, no memories of the life they shared.</p>

<p>The idea hollowed her out completely.</p>

<p>The man who once held her with love now possibly wandered as a corpse, driven only by hunger and instinct.</p>

<p>&quot;Why...&quot; Sabrina’s voice cracked into a sob. She covered her face with her palm as more tears fell.</p>

<p>&quot;Why did everything have to end like this...?&quot;</p>

<p>She slid down to the floor, the phone pressed against her forehead as she cried—softly at first, then with trembling intensity.</p>

<p>The grief she had kept dammed up for days came flooding out: fear, heartbreak, confusion, loneliness, guilt. All of it mixed until she could barely breathe.</p>

<p>Minutes passed. Maybe longer.</p>

<p>Eventually, her sobs softened. The storm of emotion subsided just enough for her to lift her head.</p>

<p>She looked at herself in the mirror across the room.</p>

<p>Her eyes were swollen and red, her lips trembling slightly.</p>

<p>But beneath the sorrow, something else shimmered faintly. A spark.</p>

<p>She pushed herself up slowly and stepped toward the mirror.</p>

<p>She gripped the sink with both hands, leaning closer.</p>

<p>&quot;You can’t keep living like this,&quot; she murmured to her reflection. Her voice was hoarse, but firmer now. &quot;You can’t cling to what’s gone.&quot;</p>

<p>It hurt to admit it. It tore her apart. But she needed to say the words.</p>

<p>&quot;He’s gone... and I have to survive.&quot;</p>

<p>She closed her eyes, inhaled deeply, and wiped her tears with deliberate movements.</p>

<p>Her entire demeanor shifted—her spine straightened, her shoulders rolled back, and her expression hardened with resolve.</p>

<p>This world was cruel now. This world demanded strength. And she had to find hers again.</p>

<p>Whatever the future held—uncertainty, danger, even Ross—she would face it head-on.</p>

<p>She pocketed her phone and unlocked the door.</p>

<p>When she stepped out, she wasn’t the same trembling girl who had entered.</p>

<p>Her steps were steadier. Her breathing calmer.</p>

<p>The pain was still there, lingering like a bruise that would take time to fade, but beneath it was a newfound determination.</p>

<p>Sabrina walked forward, ready to face her fate... and ready, perhaps, to slowly let go of her past.</p>

<p>She wanted to see one person so desperately that her whole morning felt like a blur.</p>

<p>Every step she took, every corner she turned, her eyes searched for him—Ross.</p>

<p>And she didn’t have to look long.</p>

<p>As always, he was exactly where anyone could expect to find him: at the center of a small whirlwind of people and responsibilities.</p>

<p>He stood surrounded by workers, survivors, and curious onlookers, answering questions, giving orders, fixing problems, and somehow still managing to look calm and composed.</p>

<p>Ross always carried himself with a kind of natural authority—one that made people flock to him without even realizing it.</p>

<p>Sabrina stayed at a distance at first, her fingers twisting nervously around each other.</p>

<p>She waited five minutes. Then ten. Then twenty.</p>

<p>Nearly half an hour passed before he finally started wrapping up the last of his tasks.</p>

<p>Most people would have gotten tired of waiting, maybe even annoyed.</p>

<p>But not Sabrina.</p>

<p>She didn’t mind at all.</p>

<p>Just watching him—his focused expression, the strength in his movements, the quiet confidence in the way he spoke—made her heart flutter in a way she couldn’t fully hide.</p>

<p>She felt warm. Safe. Drawn.</p>

<p>And, embarrassingly, happy.</p>

<p>When Ross finally noticed her, his face softened.</p>

<p>He dismissed the last worker with a nod, and moments later, the two of them found themselves having lunch together.</p>

<p>They sat across from each other, sharing simple food yet laughing as if it were a royal feast.</p>

<p>Every time Ross chuckled, Sabrina’s heartbeat jumped.</p>

<p>Every time she said something silly and he smiled at her—really smiled—her chest tightened.</p>

<p>Around them, people whispered.</p>

<p>&quot;I swear there’s something between them.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;They look so close.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;They’re totally becoming a couple, aren’t they?&quot;</p>

<p>Sabrina tried to ignore it, but she could feel her cheeks heating.</p>

<p>Ross didn’t seem to care at all, continuing the conversation like the entire world wasn’t watching them.</p>

<p>And though the rumors weren’t true—not yet—Sabrina knew she was getting dangerously close to wanting them to be.</p>

<p>She couldn’t keep pretending she wasn’t drawn to him, not after everything.</p>

<p>Lunch ended too quickly. Too soon.</p>

<p>The moment Ross stood up, Sabrina felt a strange determination settle inside her chest.</p>

<p>She had made a decision last night—one she thought she’d stick with—but now, after seeing him, hearing him laugh, watching him breathe...</p>

<p>She couldn’t hold it back anymore.</p>

<p>She took a deep breath, her hands trembling just slightly.</p>

<p>When Ross turned to look at her, she forced herself to speak before courage slipped through her fingers.</p>
