<p>An hour later, Ross’s group had made camp for the night.</p>

<p>The darkness of the forest surrounded them from every direction, yet unlike earlier in the day, there was a strange sense of peace in the air.</p>

<p>No beasts came to disturb them.</p>

<p>The creatures that roamed this land had already learned a painful lesson.</p>

<p>Countless powerful monsters had died at the hands of Ross and his men.</p>

<p>Although the beasts possessed no true intelligence, their instincts were sharp.</p>

<p>Every fiber of their being warned them that approaching this camp would mean certain death.</p>

<p>As a result, the predators that usually ruled the forest remained hidden in the darkness, watching from afar but never daring to come closer.</p>

<p>The camp itself was lively.</p>

<p>Dozens of fires had been lit throughout the area, illuminating the night and driving away the cold.</p>

<p>Men and women worked together preparing food while children ran around laughing and playing games.</p>

<p>The horrors of the day seemed distant for the moment.</p>

<p>Large pots sat above the flames as delicious food cooked inside.</p>

<p>The smell spread throughout the camp and caused many stomachs to rumble.</p>

<p>&quot;We’re eating well tonight.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;After everything that happened today, we deserve it.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;I thought I was going to die out there.&quot;</p>

<p>Many people nodded in agreement.</p>

<p>The expedition earlier had been terrifying.</p>

<p>Thousands had originally left the safe zone full of confidence and hope.</p>

<p>By the time they returned, only a fraction remained. Many had lost friends, relatives, and companions.</p>

<p>Yet despite the tragedy, the survivors had gained something priceless.</p>

<p>Hope.</p>

<p>Ross Oakley had once again proven that he was different from everyone else.</p>

<p>Whenever disaster struck, he somehow found a way to overcome it.</p>

<p>His existence alone gave people the courage to keep moving forward.</p>

<p>Soon dinner began. Everyone lined up with bowls and plates in hand.</p>

<p>There was steaming rice, fresh vegetables, thick soup, and large portions of roasted meat.</p>

<p>Nobody needed to worry about going hungry tonight. The people ate happily.</p>

<p>Some sat around campfires sharing stories. Others laughed and joked with friends.</p>

<p>Families gathered together and enjoyed a rare moment of peace.</p>

<p>Even the children who had spent most of the day frightened and crying were now smiling.</p>

<p>Ross sat among his wives and children while watching the scene.</p>

<p>His women occasionally glanced at him with admiration.</p>

<p>No matter how many miracles Ross performed, they never seemed to get used to it.</p>

<p>To them, he was their protector.</p>

<p>Their leader.</p>

<p>Their pillar of support.</p>

<p>One of his wives leaned against his shoulder.</p>

<p>&quot;Everyone looks much happier now,&quot; she said softly.</p>

<p>Ross nodded.</p>

<p>&quot;They’ve earned a little peace.&quot;</p>

<p>His gaze swept across the camp.</p>

<p>More than two thousand people were depending on him.</p>

<p>That responsibility would have crushed an ordinary man.</p>

<p>Ross, however, appeared completely relaxed. If anything, he looked bored.</p>

<p>The challenges of this world simply weren’t enough to threaten him.</p>

<p>Time passed. The fires crackled. The stars shined brightly overhead.</p>

<p>A cool breeze drifted through the forest. Eventually, dinner came to an end.</p>

<p>Many people stretched their bodies after eating.</p>

<p>Others began laying out blankets and preparing sleeping arrangements.</p>

<p>The younger children were already rubbing their eyes sleepily.</p>

<p>&quot;Mom, I’m tired.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;Can we sleep now?&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;Carry me.&quot;</p>

<p>Parents smiled and picked up their children. Seeing this, one of Ross’s wives approached him.</p>

<p>She was carrying one of their younger children in her arms.</p>

<p>The little one was already half asleep.</p>

<p>&quot;Do you want me to tuck our babies in for sleep, Ross?&quot; she asked.</p>

<p>Ross shook his head.</p>

<p>&quot;No need for that.&quot;</p>

<p>His answer surprised her.</p>

<p>She blinked in confusion.</p>

<p>&quot;No need?&quot;</p>

<p>Ross slowly stood up from his seat. His eyes turned toward the sky above.</p>

<p>The countless stars reflected within his pupils. A faint smile appeared on his face.</p>

<p>&quot;Tonight,&quot; Ross said calmly, &quot;we won’t be sleeping in this cold forest.&quot;</p>

<p>The people nearby immediately heard his words. Many of them looked over.</p>

<p>Some appeared confused. Others were curious. A few even thought they had misheard him.</p>

<p>&quot;If we’re not sleeping here, where are we sleeping?&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;Don’t tell me we’re marching again.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;It’s already night.&quot;</p>

<p>Questions quickly spread through the camp. Ross ignored them all.</p>

<p>Instead, he continued staring into the heavens as though waiting for something.</p>

<p>Minutes passed.</p>

<p>The atmosphere gradually became strange. People began following his gaze.</p>

<p>&quot;What exactly is he looking at?&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;I don’t know.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;Maybe another monster is coming.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;If a monster was coming, Ross wouldn’t be smiling.&quot;</p>

<p>The crowd whispered among themselves. Even Ross’s wives became curious.</p>

<p>They knew that expression.</p>

<p>Whenever Ross wore that smile, something unbelievable was usually about to happen.</p>

<p>Ten minutes passed.</p>

<p>Then twenty.</p>

<p>Suddenly...</p>

<p>A distant light appeared in the sky.</p>

<p>At first it looked like a star.</p>

<p>Then it grew larger.</p>

<p>And larger.</p>

<p>And larger.</p>

<p>The entire camp fell silent.</p>

<p>Thousands of eyes stared upward.</p>

<p>&quot;What is that?&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;Do you see it too?&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;It’s moving!&quot;</p>

<p>The object rapidly descended from the heavens.</p>

<p>The closer it came, the more stunned everyone became.</p>

<p>Many people stood up.</p>

<p>Others dropped their bowls.</p>

<p>Some even rubbed their eyes, thinking they were dreaming.</p>

<p>The massive object continued descending toward them.</p>

<p>Ross merely folded his arms and smiled.</p>

<p>&quot;Right on time,&quot; he said.</p>

<p>The people looked between Ross and the mysterious object in the sky.</p>

<p>Their hearts began pounding.</p>

<p>Whatever was arriving tonight, it was clearly something far beyond their imagination.</p>

<p>It was a flying boat.</p>

<p>The enormous vessel descended from the heavens, casting a massive shadow over the camp below.</p>

<p>Its size was so great that it blocked out a portion of the moonlight.</p>

<p>Ancient-looking runes glowed along its wooden hull while spiritual energy radiated from every inch of its structure.</p>

<p>The boat slowly came to a stop above the camp, hovering silently in midair.</p>

<p>Everyone stared upward in shock.</p>

<p>&quot;What is that?&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;A flying ship?&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;Am I dreaming?&quot;</p>

<p>Many people rubbed their eyes, unable to believe what they were seeing. Then suddenly...</p>

<p>Bang!</p>

<p>A figure shot out from the flying boat and landed heavily before Ross.</p>

<p>The impact created a small crater in the ground. The man immediately dropped to his knees.</p>

<p>His entire body trembled as he lowered his head deeply.</p>

<p>No one could miss the fear and reverence in his expression.</p>

<p>&quot;Your servant is here, Master!&quot; the man shouted.</p>

<p>His voice echoed throughout the silent campsite.</p>

<p>&quot;Please command me! I am willing to walk through fire and cross mountains for you! If Master has enemies, I shall fight them without hesitation! If Master orders me to die, I shall not even frown!&quot;</p>

<p>The man kept his head lowered, not daring to look directly at Ross. Sweat covered his forehead.</p>
