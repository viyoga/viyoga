<p>&quot;You&#x27;re a brave man, talking trash about my future slave,&quot; Ross sneered, his voice dripping with disdain.</p>

<p>He lifted the hammer, its weight steady in his grip as he eyed the Don with cold malice.</p>

<p>&quot;Guess I won&#x27;t feel a shred of guilt sending you to hell in the most painful way imaginable.&quot;</p>

<p>The Don&#x27;s face twisted in panic, a flicker of desperation replacing his former arrogance.</p>

<p>&quot;No, wait! I can give you anything—money, power, whatever you want. Just… stop! Don&#x27;t do it!&quot;</p>

<p>But Ross wasn&#x27;t interested in bargaining. The Don attempted a feeble parry, he wanted to raise his arms in a last-ditch effort to defend himself.</p>

<p>But the rope on his body held true. It was a wasted effort. Ross brought the hammer down with brutal precision.</p>

<p>&quot;Ahhhhh!&quot; The Don&#x27;s scream filled the room as the hammer struck his knee, the sickening crunch of bone echoing in the silence.</p>

<p>He looked at his broken leg, his face contorted in agony. Hatred flashed in his eyes, his voice a guttural snarl.</p>

<p>&quot;You… you son of a bitch! I&#x27;ll kill you! I&#x27;ll destroy your whole family, you hear me? Damn you to hell!&quot;</p>

<p>Ross tilted his head, his expression unchanging, almost bored.</p>

<p>&quot;Oh, you&#x27;re still talking?&quot; He raised the hammer again, his grip steady, his gaze icy. &quot;Let&#x27;s fix that.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;Bang!&quot; The hammer crashed down on the Don&#x27;s other knee with even more force, shattering the bone beneath.</p>

<p>The Don choked on his own saliva, the sheer, unrelenting pain stealing his breath.</p>

<p>Tears streamed down his face as he writhed on the floor, his body convulsing under the assault of agony coursing through his legs.</p>

<p>Gasping for air, the Don managed a shuddering whisper. &quot;You… you can&#x27;t do this. I&#x27;ll make you pay… I&#x27;ll…&quot;</p>

<p>Ross crouched beside him, a smirk tugging at the corner of his mouth. &quot;Keep making threats if it makes you feel better, but we both know you&#x27;re not in a position to back them up.&quot;</p>

<p>The Don&#x27;s resolve finally cracked as he looked up at Ross, fear pooling in his eyes.</p>

<p>But Ross only watched, a twisted satisfaction gleaming in his gaze, as if each scream and broken bone were just the beginning of the punishment he had in store.</p>

<p>&quot;And your cries of pain… they&#x27;re music to my ears. I want to hear you cry some more. Cry for me, bitch!&quot; Ross&#x27;s grin widened as he spoke, his voice laced with sadistic delight.</p>

<p>Without hesitation, he raised the hammer again and brought it crashing down.</p>

<p>&quot;Bang!&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;Bang!&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;Bang!&quot; The sound of each strike echoed, the blows coming down with relentless force.</p>

<p>Blood splattered across the floor as the Don&#x27;s legs were reduced to nothing but shattered bones and mangled flesh.</p>

<p>His legs, once sturdy, were now twisted, bloody messes that barely resembled human limbs.</p>

<p>Even the sturdy chair to which he was tied couldn&#x27;t withstand the onslaught, splintering beneath the force of Ross&#x27;s fury.</p>

<p>But Ross didn&#x27;t stop. He leaned in close, watching the fear in the Don&#x27;s eyes as he struggled, bound and helpless.</p>

<p>Each scream only fueled Ross&#x27;s determination. It was as if every ounce of hatred, every dark corner of his soul, poured into each swing.</p>

<p>The room reeked of blood and fear, a grim theater for Ross&#x27;s cruelty.</p>

<p>&quot;Please... please... stop!&quot; The Don&#x27;s voice was hoarse, each plea weaker than the last, but he clung desperately to life, his survival instinct strong despite the overwhelming agony.</p>

<p>Somehow, he managed to stay conscious, forced to endure the torture without the mercy of passing out.</p>

<p>Ross continued his assault, moving from the Don&#x27;s legs to his shoulders, arms, and chest, each blow methodically calculated to cause maximum suffering.</p>

<p>The hammer smashed down on the Don&#x27;s ribcage, shattering bones that punctured the skin, blood seeping through his torn clothes.</p>

<p>His arms hung at unnatural angles, fractured and useless, his body a grotesque collage of agony.</p>

<p>The minutes dragged into an hour, the room filled with the wet, sickening sound of bone breaking and flesh tearing.</p>

<p>Ross was merciless, leaving no part of the Don&#x27;s body untouched—except his head, which remained perfectly intact.</p>

<p>The Don&#x27;s face twisted in pain, his breaths shallow, each one a struggle as his body deteriorated into a pulpy, unrecognizable wreck.</p>

<p>In the background, the once-defiant Maya was trembling, her face pale. She had started the day with bold words and fierce resolve, but now, faced with the raw brutality unfolding before her, she couldn&#x27;t contain her disgust.</p>

<p>&quot;Arrrrggghhhh…&quot; She doubled over, her stomach heaving as she retched, the grotesque scene overwhelming her.</p>
