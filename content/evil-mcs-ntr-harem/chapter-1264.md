<p>Ross and his people arrived deep within the thick forest.</p>

<p>By then, night had already fallen, and darkness blanketed the land.</p>

<p>The only sources of light were the moon above and the occasional torches carried by some of the survivors.</p>

<p>The forest was eerily quiet.</p>

<p>Strange noises occasionally echoed from the darkness, making the newcomers nervous.</p>

<p>Every rustling leaf and snapping twig caused them to tense up.</p>

<p>Yet despite the dangerous atmosphere, Ross’s group continued walking as if they didn’t have a care in the world.</p>

<p>Some chatted casually.</p>

<p>Others laughed among themselves.</p>

<p>A few children even played while their parents walked beside them.</p>

<p>It was a scene that completely baffled the people who had joined Ross only a few days ago.</p>

<p>&quot;It’s so strange,&quot; one newcomer whispered.</p>

<p>&quot;I know,&quot; another replied. &quot;They’re all so strange.&quot;</p>

<p>The two looked around at the thousands of people moving through the forest.</p>

<p>Nobody appeared particularly worried.</p>

<p>Nobody seemed afraid of the darkness.</p>

<p>Nobody seemed concerned that monsters could attack at any moment.</p>

<p>It was as though they were taking a leisurely stroll through a public park rather than traveling through a deadly forest during the apocalypse.</p>

<p>&quot;It feels like they have absolute faith in Ross,&quot; a woman said quietly. &quot;Look at them. They don’t even seem scared.&quot;</p>

<p>The others nodded.</p>

<p>She was right.</p>

<p>There was an almost blind trust among Ross’s followers.</p>

<p>Whenever his name was mentioned, their eyes would light up with confidence.</p>

<p>Whenever danger appeared, they would instinctively look toward him.</p>

<p>To them, Ross Oakley seemed less like a leader and more like an unshakable pillar supporting their entire world.</p>

<p>&quot;Well, it’s Ross fucking Oakley we’re talking about,&quot; a man said with a snort.</p>

<p>The others turned toward him.</p>

<p>The man lowered his voice.</p>

<p>&quot;Even back on Earth, he always seemed larger than life.&quot;</p>

<p>A few people nodded in agreement.</p>

<p>Who hadn’t heard of Ross Oakley?</p>

<p>The man who had become a global sensation.</p>

<p>The lottery winner who became unimaginably rich overnight.</p>

<p>The basketball monster who shattered records.</p>

<p>The man whose every action made headlines.</p>

<p>The man surrounded by beautiful women and countless fans.</p>

<p>Back then, Ross had already felt like someone operating on a completely different level from ordinary people.</p>

<p>&quot;But look at him now,&quot; the man continued.</p>

<p>&quot;Before, he was ugly as hell. Now he’s ridiculously handsome. It’s like he became an entirely different person overnight.&quot;</p>

<p>Several women nearby secretly glanced toward Ross.</p>

<p>Even from a distance, he stood out.</p>

<p>His posture was relaxed.</p>

<p>His movements were confident.</p>

<p>Combined with his handsome appearance and powerful aura, he naturally attracted attention wherever he went.</p>

<p>The man continued speaking.</p>

<p>&quot;And let’s be honest. There’s no way he only got better looks.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;What do you mean?&quot; someone asked.</p>

<p>The man pointed toward Ross.</p>

<p>&quot;I bet he’s gained all sorts of powerful abilities too. Otherwise, no one in their right mind would follow him into the middle of nowhere like this.&quot;</p>

<p>The group fell silent.</p>

<p>That theory made perfect sense.</p>

<p>After all, this was no longer Earth.</p>

<p>People were awakening strange powers every day.</p>

<p>Monsters roamed the world.</p>

<p>The impossible had become reality.</p>

<p>Why couldn’t Ross possess incredible abilities as well?</p>

<p>The man then pointed toward another section of the marching group.</p>

<p>&quot;Look over there.&quot;</p>

<p>Everyone followed his gaze.</p>

<p>Brandon and his men were marching alongside the main force.</p>

<p>Unlike the average survivors, they looked like a professional military unit.</p>

<p>Every one of them was heavily armed.</p>

<p>Sophisticated armor covered their bodies.</p>

<p>Protective helmets concealed their faces.</p>

<p>Advanced weapons hung from their shoulders.</p>

<p>The disciplined formation they maintained made them appear intimidating even while walking.</p>

<p>One newcomer swallowed nervously.</p>

<p>&quot;Those guys don’t look weak.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;Not at all.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;They look like they could wipe out an entire army.&quot;</p>

<p>The newcomers exchanged uneasy glances.</p>

<p>The more they observed Ross’s people, the more mysterious they seemed.</p>

<p>The women were beautiful.</p>

<p>The men were well-equipped.</p>

<p>The children looked healthy.</p>

<p>Nobody appeared hungry.</p>

<p>Nobody appeared desperate.</p>

<p>In a world where most survivors struggled just to stay alive, Ross’s group looked absurdly prosperous.</p>

<p>Almost unreal.</p>

<p>One man lowered his voice.</p>

<p>&quot;Do you think we made the right choice joining them?&quot;</p>

<p>A moment of silence followed.</p>

<p>Then another survivor nodded firmly.</p>

<p>&quot;I think joining them might end up being the best decision we’ve made since the apocalypse started.&quot;</p>

<p>As they spoke, their eyes drifted toward Ross’s figure at the front of the group.</p>

<p>Walking beneath the moonlight, the man appeared calm and completely at ease.</p>

<p>As if no danger in the world could possibly threaten him.</p>

<p>And for some reason, simply looking at his back made them feel safer.</p>

<p>A few minutes later, the atmosphere in the forest suddenly changed.</p>

<p>Growls echoed from every direction.</p>

<p>Rustling leaves filled the night.</p>

<p>Then dozens of massive beasts emerged from the darkness and surrounded Ross’s group.</p>

<p>&quot;ROAR!&quot;</p>

<p>Their deafening roars shook the forest.</p>

<p>Some resembled giant wolves.</p>

<p>Others looked like oversized bears covered in black scales.</p>

<p>One particularly terrifying creature resembled an alligator over twenty feet long, with teeth as large as daggers.</p>

<p>The newcomers immediately turned pale.</p>

<p>&quot;Monsters!&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;We’re surrounded!&quot;</p>

<p>Many of them instinctively reached for their weapons.</p>

<p>However, Ross’s veteran followers barely reacted.</p>

<p>Some even yawned.</p>

<p>Others continued chatting as if nothing unusual was happening.</p>

<p>It was almost as though they had seen this countless times before.</p>

<p>The beasts charged.</p>

<p>The ground trembled beneath their feet.</p>

<p>The giant alligator-like beast led the assault, opening its enormous jaws as it lunged toward the group.</p>

<p>Unfortunately for the monsters, Brandon and his men were already moving.</p>

<p>For the second time that day, they were about to teach the local wildlife a painful lesson.</p>

<p>Not a single gun was drawn.</p>

<p>There was simply no need.</p>

<p>Brandon stepped forward and met the charging alligator head-on.</p>

<p>The monster’s eyes flashed with bloodlust.</p>

<p>Brandon’s expression remained calm.</p>

<p>Then he threw a punch.</p>

<p>PAK!</p>

<p>The sound was surprisingly soft.</p>

<p>Yet the effect was anything but.</p>

<p>The gigantic beast instantly exploded into a cloud of blood and flesh, as though it had been struck by a meteor rather than a human fist.</p>

<p>The surrounding monsters froze.</p>

<p>The newcomers froze.</p>

<p>Even some of Ross’s newer followers stood there with their mouths hanging open.</p>

<p>&quot;What the hell?&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;Did he just...&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;One punch?&quot;</p>

<p>Meanwhile, similar scenes unfolded all around the perimeter.</p>

<p>One of Brandon’s men casually slapped a charging wolf-like beast.</p>

<p>The creature was sent flying hundreds of feet into the distance.</p>

<p>Another soldier kicked a giant bear monster and launched it through several trees.</p>

<p>A third casually flicked a finger, sending another beast tumbling across the forest floor.</p>

<p>The battle ended almost as soon as it began.</p>

<p>The monsters had arrived full of confidence.</p>

<p>Now they knew fear.</p>

<p>The surviving beasts let out frightened cries before turning and fleeing into the darkness.</p>

<p>Within moments, the forest became quiet again.</p>

<p>The newcomers stared at Brandon and his men as though they were looking at monsters disguised as humans.</p>

<p>&quot;What kind of people are these?&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;No wonder they’re not afraid.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;Those beasts never stood a chance.&quot;</p>

<p>High above the forest, an old man stood silently upon a flying sword.</p>

<p>His robes fluttered in the wind as he observed everything from afar.</p>

<p>His eyes narrowed.</p>

<p>&quot;These people are strong,&quot; he muttered in a strange alien tongue.</p>

<p>&quot;It seems this is why my master ordered me to serve them, particularly a man named Ross Oakley.&quot;</p>

<p>The old man was one of the subordinates sent by Cyrus Thorn.</p>

<p>Initially, he had looked down upon the assignment.</p>

<p>Now he was beginning to understand why it had been given to him.</p>

<p>Still, his pride remained.</p>

<p>He was an immortal cultivator.</p>

<p>These mortals, no matter how powerful, were beneath him in his eyes.</p>

<p>With a thought, his flying sword descended from the heavens.</p>

<p>The newcomers immediately noticed him.</p>

<p>&quot;A flying sword!&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;It’s him!&quot;</p>

<p>Fearful whispers spread through the crowd.</p>

<p>The old man hovered high above Ross’s group, looking down at everyone from the sky.</p>

<p>Many people raised their heads.</p>

<p>Ross glanced upward.</p>

<p>His expression remained calm.</p>

<p>&quot;I don’t want to keep looking up at an ugly old man all night.&quot;</p>

<p>The crowd instantly became silent.</p>

<p>Ross continued.</p>

<p>&quot;Come down and let’s talk.&quot;</p>

<p>His tone was casual.</p>

<p>Yet those closest to him immediately sensed something dangerous beneath the words.</p>

<p>His wives exchanged glances.</p>

<p>They knew that tone.</p>

<p>Ross wasn’t happy.</p>

<p>The old man’s face darkened.</p>

<p>For thousands of years, nobody had dared speak to him like that.</p>

<p>His eyes filled with fury.</p>

<p>&quot;You ignorant imbecile!&quot;</p>

<p>His voice thundered through the forest.</p>

<p>&quot;Even maggots possess more worth than your useless self. If not for my master instructing me to...&quot;</p>

<p>Unfortunately, he never finished the sentence.</p>

<p>A blur suddenly appeared beside him.</p>

<p>It was Brandon.</p>

<p>Nobody saw how he got there.</p>

<p>Nobody saw him jump.</p>

<p>One moment he was on the ground.</p>

<p>The next he was standing in the sky.</p>

<p>PAK!</p>

<p>A casual slap landed across the old man’s face.</p>

<p>The old man’s eyes widened.</p>

<p>His body shot downward like a meteor.</p>

<p>BOOM!</p>

<p>A massive crater appeared in the forest below.</p>

<p>Dust exploded into the air.</p>

<p>The crowd stared in stunned silence.</p>

<p>A few moments later, the dust settled.</p>

<p>Nothing remained. No movement. No voice. No flying sword. No old man.</p>

<p>Unknown to everyone present, the slap had completely annihilated him.</p>

<p>His body had been reduced to a mist of blood.</p>

<p>His soul, spirit, and consciousness had all been erased instantly.</p>

<p>There would be no escape.</p>

<p>No resurrection.</p>

<p>No rebirth.</p>

<p>No second chance.</p>

<p>Only complete oblivion.</p>

<p>Ross glanced toward the crater.</p>

<p>Then he shrugged.</p>

<p>&quot;I guess we need another guide.&quot;</p>

<p>The crowd didn’t know whether to laugh or cry.</p>

<p>An immortal-looking expert had just appeared dramatically from the heavens.</p>

<p>Less than ten seconds later, he was gone.</p>

<p>Ross waved his hand.</p>

<p>&quot;Let’s keep moving.&quot;</p>

<p>The group immediately resumed marching through the forest.</p>

<p>As for Ross, he didn’t spare the matter another thought.</p>

<p>His children were still present.</p>

<p>He disliked exposing them to scenes of death and violence whenever possible.</p>

<p>The night continued peacefully as thousands of people followed behind him, while the newcomers silently wondered what kind of terrifying existence they had chosen to follow.</p>
