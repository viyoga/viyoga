<p>Thomas of course was not one to let his emotions show and so he continued what he was here to do in the first place.</p>

<p>&quot;I have to admit,&quot; Thomas said, touching the table between them as if showing the gentle caress of a lover, &quot;I expected you to come alone. It&#x27;s not every day a billionaire investor is greeted by a bodyguard twice the size of a doorframe.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;I never come unprepared,&quot; Jade said coolly. &quot;Especially when I know who I&#x27;m dealing with.&quot;</p>

<p>Thomas smiled slowly, savoring her words. &quot;You think I&#x27;m dangerous?&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;I think you&#x27;re unpredictable. There&#x27;s a difference.&quot;</p>

<p>He leaned forward slightly. &quot;Unpredictability keeps things interesting.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;It also gets people killed,&quot; Jade replied, her tone as calm as ever.</p>

<p>Thomas laughed again, genuinely this time. &quot;Touché.&quot;</p>

<p>The hostess returned with their drinks, placing them gently on the table before bowing and retreating. Thomas took a sip of his whiskey, eyes still locked on Jade.</p>

<p>&quot;You know,&quot; he said, &quot;I don&#x27;t usually get turned down twice in one day.&quot;</p>

<p>Jade met his gaze without flinching. &quot;Then you&#x27;ve clearly been dealing with the wrong women.&quot;</p>

<p>Thomas grinned, raising his glass in a mock toast. &quot;Or maybe just the wrong kind of challenge.&quot;</p>

<p>Jade clinked her glass against his, lips curling into a faint smirk. &quot;Be careful, Thomas. Some challenges bite back.&quot;</p>

<p>From the corner, Brandon remained silent, watching everything with the cold, unblinking eyes of a trained wolf.</p>

<p>Thomas leaned back in his seat, the dim lighting of the lounge casting shadows across his face. He swirled the amber liquid in his glass, eyes flicking toward Jade like a predator studying its prey.</p>

<p>He hadn&#x27;t come here just to chat—he wanted more. To him, Brandon&#x27;s watchful gaze from across the room was just background noise. A minor obstacle.</p>

<p>Because if he could win Jade over, if he could get her to surrender herself to him, then Brandon&#x27;s existence would become irrelevant.</p>

<p>After all, a man who holds the woman holds the power—and the dog, in the end, obeys the one with the leash.</p>

<p>But seduction was never simple, especially with someone like Jade who was clearly on guard against his advances.</p>

<p>&quot;I like you, Jade,&quot; Thomas said, flashing a slow, confident smile.</p>

<p>&quot;I&#x27;ll take that as a compliment,&quot; she replied smoothly, her tone polite but distant.</p>

<p>She didn&#x27;t even look at him directly, her eyes instead fixed on her drink. There was no blush, no flustered reaction, not even a smirk. Just silence and disinterest.</p>

<p>Most women would have at least smiled by now. That only made her more alluring—and more infuriating.</p>

<p>Undeterred, Thomas kept talking. He complimented her beauty, her poise, her mysterious nature. He joked, leaned in a little closer, let his voice drop to a husky undertone.</p>

<p>But for every flirtatious jab he delivered, Jade offered nothing in return. She answered when necessary, always with elegance, but never with warmth. Her words were clipped, her tone flat.</p>

<p>It was as though she were participating in a ritual she found no joy in—like she was obligated to humor him, but had no intention of giving him anything more than that.</p>

<p>Thirty minutes passed. Thirty minutes of careful maneuvering, subtle touches, and strategic compliments. And still, she remained as cold and composed as ever. Her eyes never softened.</p>

<p>Her lips never curled into a genuine smile. It was like trying to seduce a statue.</p>

<p>Thomas&#x27;s patience began to fray. He wasn&#x27;t used to this kind of resistance. And so, he shifted tactics.</p>

<p>Enough games.</p>

<p>&quot;I heard you&#x27;ve been married before,&quot; he said abruptly, cutting through the polite façade.</p>

<p>The statement wasn&#x27;t casual. It was sharp, deliberate—meant to pierce.</p>

<p>Jade stilled.</p>

<p>Her hand paused midway to her glass. Then, slowly, she looked at him. The temperature in her gaze dropped several degrees. It wasn&#x27;t surprise he saw in her eyes—it was warning.</p>

<p>She clearly didn&#x27;t like where this was going. And yet, after a brief pause, she gave a calm nod.</p>

<p>&quot;Yes,&quot; she said simply, then tipped her glass and drained it in one go.</p>

<p>The gesture was subtle but telling. Her fingers were just a little too tight around the glass. Her throat moved a little too tensely as she swallowed. She might&#x27;ve worn her mask well, but the tension in her posture betrayed her.</p>

<p>&quot;What happened?&quot; Thomas asked, voice softer now, almost feigning concern.</p>

<p>&quot;Nothing,&quot; she said. &quot;We fell out of love and moved on.&quot;</p>

<p>Thomas raised an eyebrow. &quot;Really? I heard something else.&quot;</p>

<p>Jade&#x27;s fingers froze around the rim of her empty glass.</p>

<p>&quot;I heard Ross came into your life—rather forcefully,&quot; he added, smiling as he leaned in. &quot;Some say things between you and your husband didn&#x27;t exactly end... cleanly.&quot;</p>

<p>He didn&#x27;t spell it out. He didn&#x27;t need to. The implication hung heavy in the air, thick with suggestion. Rumors were a powerful thing—especially when the truth was murky.</p>

<p>And the rumor about Jade and Ross was one that always whispered of betrayal, of affairs and secrets that had never been fully buried.</p>

<p>For the first time, Jade looked at him with something more than polite disinterest. Her eyes flared—not with anger, but with the heat of a memory she clearly didn&#x27;t want to revisit. Still, she didn&#x27;t lash out.</p>

<p>She didn&#x27;t defend herself.</p>

<p>Instead, she set her glass down slowly and folded her hands neatly in her lap, like a lady preparing for war.</p>

<p>&quot;Be careful, Thomas,&quot; she said, voice calm but cold enough to chill his spine. &quot;Curiosity can be dangerous. Especially when it comes from someone who wanted to hear gossip from questionable sources.&quot;</p>

<p>Thomas chuckled under his breath, intrigued. He didn&#x27;t get what he wanted—not yet. But for the first time that night, he&#x27;d struck a chord. And that was something.</p>

<p>He smiled, his interest in her only deepening.</p>

<p>&quot;Then why don&#x27;t you enlighten me, Jade?&quot; Thomas said, his voice low and laced with mock curiosity. He leaned in, elbows on the table, eyes gleaming with something far darker than flirtation.</p>

<p>&quot;Was it the thrill of sneaking around behind your husband&#x27;s back? The rush of being touched by another man while still wearing someone else&#x27;s ring? Tell me—was that what made it exciting for you?&quot;</p>
