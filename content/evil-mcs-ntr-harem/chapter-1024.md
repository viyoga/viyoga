<p>The screams, the blood, the endless, relentless horde—they gnawed at her mind, a dark shadow creeping into every corner of her thoughts.</p>

<p>She felt a cold weight settle in her stomach, a stark reminder that nothing would ever feel safe again.</p>

<p>For the first time since the outbreak, Karen understood the full scale of the disaster.</p>

<p>They might be safe for now, but out there, the world had ended.</p>

<p>And survival would demand more than just hiding in a bunker—it would demand courage, cunning, and a strength she wasn’t sure she possessed.</p>

<p>Karen kept scrolling through her feed, her thumb moving lazily across the screen as more and more horrific videos flashed by—screams, burning cars, people running for their lives.</p>

<p>Her chest felt tight. The world outside really was collapsing.</p>

<p>Then, suddenly, she froze. Her eyes widened slightly as she spotted something that didn’t fit the chaos she had seen so far.</p>

<p>&quot;Wait... look at this. Have you ever seen anything like it?&quot; Karen said, her voice sharp with disbelief as she called out to her sisters.</p>

<p>Chelsea and Lea exchanged quick glances before hurrying over.</p>

<p>Karen held up the phone so they could see the video more clearly.</p>

<p>The footage was shaky, taken from someone’s smartphone, but what it captured was unbelievable.</p>

<p>In the middle of a wide street, surrounded by hundreds of zombies, stood a lone man.</p>

<p>He wasn’t armed—not with a gun or even a bat—but with his fists.</p>

<p>His movements were fierce and precise.</p>

<p>Every punch and kick landed with bone-crushing force, sending the undead sprawling backward in heaps.</p>

<p>The crowd of zombies tried to swarm him, but he moved with inhuman speed, dodging, striking, and weaving between them like a predator among prey.</p>

<p>&quot;Oh my God...&quot; Chelsea whispered, her eyes glued to the screen. &quot;He’s fighting them... barehanded?&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;Look at how strong he is,&quot; Lea said, stunned. &quot;He’s taking them down like they’re nothing. But... he’s bleeding. Look—on his arms.&quot;</p>

<p>The camera zoomed in shakily, and they saw deep gashes and bite marks across the man’s forearms.</p>

<p>He should have fallen, should have been dead or turned already, yet he kept moving, faster and fiercer with every passing second.</p>

<p>His face was smeared with blood, his clothes torn to shreds, but there was something terrifyingly resolute in his eyes.</p>

<p>&quot;Who is he?&quot; Chelsea breathed. &quot;No one should be able to do that.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;I don’t know,&quot; Karen murmured. &quot;He’s like a one-man army...&quot; Her voice trailed off as she continued to watch, transfixed.</p>

<p>The video continued for several long minutes.</p>

<p>Zombies lunged from every direction, and though the man was clearly exhausted, he didn’t back down.</p>

<p>At one point, he grabbed a zombie by the neck and slammed it straight into the pavement with a force that cracked the concrete.</p>

<p>Blood splattered the camera lens, and the person filming gasped in shock.</p>

<p>Lea placed a hand over her mouth.</p>

<p>&quot;He’s going to die out there.&quot;</p>

<p>But he didn’t. Instead, he suddenly broke through the horde, sprinting down the ruined street.</p>

<p>The person behind the camera followed him briefly, capturing his silhouette as he vanished between the burning cars and broken streetlights.</p>

<p>Then the video cut off.</p>

<p>For a moment, none of the sisters spoke.</p>

<p>The silence was thick, heavy with disbelief and a strange, lingering awe.</p>

<p>Karen finally broke it, her tone steady but thoughtful. &quot;We should tell Ross about this.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;Yeah,&quot; Chelsea said softly. &quot;If anyone would understand what’s going on... it’s him.&quot;</p>

<p>Lea nodded, still staring at the frozen frame of the man’s retreating figure. &quot;Maybe he’ll know who that is.&quot;</p>

<p>Karen looked down at the phone again. Her fingers hovered over the replay button, but she hesitated.</p>

<p>&quot;Let’s go find Ross,&quot; she said quietly. &quot;He needs to see this.&quot; She finally decided to be proactive.</p>

<p>As the three of them turned and walked toward the corridor that led deeper into the bunker, the echoes of laughter and chatter around them faded.</p>

<p>Outside, the world was falling apart—but deep down, a new, unsettling thought stirred in Karen’s mind.</p>

<p>If that man could fight like that... if he could survive all that... then maybe he wasn’t just someone.</p>

<p>Maybe he was something else entirely.</p>

<p>***</p>

<p>A few hours later, Ross finally appeared once more.</p>

<p>The chatter in the large underground hall immediately began to fade.</p>

<p>Even before he spoke, everyone could feel the change in the air — the quiet authority, the weight he carried.</p>

<p>&quot;Daddy! Daddy! Daddy!&quot; a child’s voice called out joyfully from near the front.</p>

<p>Ross turned his head slightly, and his stern features softened.</p>

<p>&quot;I’ll be right there, sweetheart,&quot; he said gently, raising a hand. &quot;Just give me a minute, okay?&quot;</p>

<p>The little girl smiled and nodded, clutching her mother’s leg as she looked up at him with admiration.</p>

<p>Ross then stepped forward, his footsteps echoing faintly across the hall.</p>

<p>The lights flickered slightly above, their hum filling the silence that had settled over the room.</p>

<p>He stopped near the front, his gaze sweeping over the crowd — men, women, and children — all waiting for him to speak.</p>

<p>&quot;I know,&quot; he began slowly, his deep voice carrying easily through the hall, &quot;that most of you have already seen what’s happening out there. You’ve watched the chaos. The news, the social media posts... the things crawling through the streets.&quot;</p>

<p>A ripple of unease spread through the crowd. No one needed reminding.</p>

<p>The horrors outside were impossible to forget.</p>

<p>Ross continued, &quot;It seems that not only have the dead begun to walk, but something else — something unexpected — has come with them.&quot;</p>

<p>He raised his hand and snapped his fingers.</p>

<p>A moment later, one of the screens on the far wall flickered to life.</p>

<p>Static filled it for a second before resolving into a shaky video feed.</p>

<p>&quot;This was recorded earlier today,&quot; Ross said quietly.</p>

<p>The image stabilized, showing a man sitting in front of a camera, his face streaked with grime and dried blood.</p>

<p>The background behind him was the ruins of what looked like a small town — burned buildings, shattered glass, and a sky filled with smoke.</p>
