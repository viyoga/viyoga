<p>When evening came, the screening of the house contestants’ film commenced.</p>

<p>The main hall had been transformed into a private theater, complete with velvet curtains, surround sound, and mood lighting.</p>

<p>The contestants filed in, still half-tired from the night shoot, but buzzing with nervous excitement.</p>

<p>As the lights dimmed and the screen flickered to life, the room fell into complete silence.</p>

<p>For the next hour and a half, no one said a word.</p>

<p>The film was a masterpiece in motion—surprisingly emotional, visually striking, and layered with tension, drama, and intimacy.</p>

<p>The chemistry between the characters, especially between Ross and Cara, sizzled on screen.</p>

<p>There were laughs, gasps, and moments that left people holding their breath.</p>

<p>And when the screen finally faded to black and the credits began to roll, the silence lingered—charged, heavy.</p>

<p>Everyone was waiting for the verdict.</p>

<p>***</p>

<p>Then came the slow clap inside a private room in the house.</p>

<p>One man stood at the front. A tall, imposing figure in a black turtleneck and tailored blazer.</p>

<p>His greying hair and piercing eyes gave him an air of authority—and with good reason.</p>

<p>He was none other than the director that the house boss invited, a world-renowned director whose name alone could turn a script into gold.</p>

<p>He had created films that had shattered box office records and earned critical acclaim across the globe.</p>

<p>&quot;I’ve never seen anything like this,&quot; the director said, his voice echoing through the hall.</p>

<p>&quot;This piece isn’t just good—it’s ready. If you asked me, I’d say this could go into limited release tomorrow and still outperform half of what’s in cinemas right now.&quot;</p>

<p>The director turned, facing the house boss with a mixture of awe and suspicion. &quot;Who edited this film?&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;Big D,&quot; the house boss replied with a proud smirk.</p>

<p>The director’s eyes narrowed. &quot;Big D? That’s his name?&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;That’s what he goes by in the house.&quot;</p>

<p>The director folded his arms. &quot;Someone with that kind of talent... and charm... and presence on screen? This man is not an amateur. Not by a long shot. So who is he—really?&quot;</p>

<p>The house boss exhaled slowly, hesitating. &quot;Believe me, I want to tell you. I really do. But I’m bound by contract. All I can say is... Big D isn’t just some contestant. He’s a giant outside this house. A man with reach and power.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;A ghost in the industry, huh?&quot; Elijah said softly, almost to himself. &quot;That explains it. That raw confidence. The vision. The way he handled both sides of the camera... this wasn’t a first-timer.&quot;</p>

<p>He turned back toward the screen, deep in thought.</p>

<p>&quot;Well, when the day comes that he’s ready to take off that mask... tell him I want a meeting. One-on-one. No cameras. No filters. Just the two of us talking film.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;I’ll pass that along,&quot; the house boss said, grinning.</p>

<p>The house boss then announced that the house contestants had successfully completed the weekly quest.</p>

<p>The buzz in the room turned electric.</p>

<p>Contestants whispered to one another, some congratulating Ross in hushed tones, others casting envious glances his way.</p>

<p>Cara sat still in her seat, heart thundering in her chest.</p>

<p>Her cheeks were flushed, not just from the praise, but from memories of what they’d filmed.</p>

<p>Corey was stone-faced, staring straight ahead. He hadn’t clapped. He hadn’t moved.</p>

<p>But the tightness in his jaw revealed everything.</p>

<p>After a few moments, someone stood up, clapping enthusiastically.</p>

<p>&quot;Well done, everyone! We made something special!&quot;</p>

<p>The energy broke into cheers and laughter. Everyone began to rise, stretch, and chatter.</p>

<p>Ross, who had remained silent through the praise, finally stood up and walked over to Cara.</p>

<p>She looked up at him, and for a moment, it was hard to tell whether she was looking at him as her co-star... or something more.</p>

<p>&quot;You did amazing,&quot; Ross said quietly.</p>

<p>&quot;You carried the whole film,&quot; Cara replied, though her eyes lingered on his lips for a second too long.</p>

<p>He smiled. &quot;Maybe. But I couldn’t have done it without you.&quot;</p>

<p>From a few seats away, Corey’s fists clenched.</p>

<p>The house contestants received their weekly budget as always, accompanied by the announcement of the newest nominations.</p>

<p>Unsurprisingly, Ross and Lily were among the three housemates on the chopping block again.</p>

<p>Whispers circled the house. Some said Ross was too dominant, others thought he was playing the game too well.</p>

<p>But when eviction night arrived one week later, he survived yet again—saved by the public for the third week in a row.</p>

<p>Instead, a quieter girl, one who had barely made a splash in the house, was sent home.</p>

<p>The following day, a new weekly task was rolled out—a physically demanding and emotionally testing endurance challenge that required teams to work closely together over several days.</p>

<p>The pressure was on, but amid the hustle and strategy, Cara stood out—not for her performance, but for her lack of focus.</p>

<p>She smiled, she laughed, and she did her part—but anyone paying attention could see she was distant.</p>

<p>Her mind was somewhere else. Or rather... with someone else.</p>

<p>Ross.</p>

<p>She found herself watching him when she thought no one would notice.</p>

<p>The way he talked with the others. The way he led.</p>

<p>The way he teased and challenged people, never apologizing for his confidence.</p>

<p>At first, she’d thought he was just another cocky player in the house.</p>

<p>But now... now she realized he was more than that.</p>

<p>To her, he had become someone she couldn’t stop thinking about—especially after what happened during their movie shoot.</p>

<p>That night, after the lights dimmed and the house was meant to rest, Cara quietly approached Corey.</p>

<p>He was sitting by himself in the backyard, legs stretched out, staring blankly at the stars.</p>

<p>She hesitated, clutching the sleeves of her sweater, her steps slow and hesitant.</p>

<p>&quot;Corey,&quot; she said gently.</p>

<p>He turned his head. His eyes were tired. &quot;Yeah?&quot;</p>

<p>She sat beside him, silence stretching between them for a few long moments.</p>

<p>The cool breeze rustled the leaves of the small tree nearby.</p>

<p>&quot;I need to be honest with you,&quot; she finally said, voice barely above a whisper.</p>

<p>&quot;I don’t want to lie or lead you on anymore.&quot;</p>

<p>He looked at her sharply. &quot;What do you mean?&quot;</p>
