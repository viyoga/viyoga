<p>From that day on, Jennifer realized that Ross’s abilities went far beyond his obvious appetite for beautiful women.</p>

<p>He was a man capable of unimaginable violence, and she was one of the very few in the world who had witnessed it firsthand.</p>

<p>The truth of him—both terrifying and magnetic—settled into her mind like a shadow she could neither escape nor fully understand.</p>

<p>She didn’t know whether knowing this was a blessing or a curse, only that her life had irrevocably changed.</p>

<p>There was no use crying over something she could not change.</p>

<p>The world would not bend to her wishes, and Ross was not a man to be bargained with.</p>

<p>So, slowly, Jennifer adapted to her new life, finding a rhythm within the chaos.</p>

<p>Surprisingly, it wasn’t as hard as she had feared.</p>

<p>Ross made sure she was constantly reminded of her place—and of his power.</p>

<p>Every day, without fail, he claimed her, leaving her body aching, trembling, and yet hungering for more.</p>

<p>The pleasure was relentless, overwhelming, and it forced her to surrender in ways she hadn’t thought possible.</p>

<p>She saw, too, that this was true for all his women.</p>

<p>Each seemed bound to him not just by desire, but by the inescapable gravity of his presence.</p>

<p>The pattern of his dominance became a kind of order in her life, a world defined by extremes of pain and pleasure, fear and ecstasy.</p>

<p>Nights blurred into days, her mind and body growing attuned to the rhythm he set.</p>

<p>Even in moments of quiet, when she tried to reflect on what her life had become, she could not escape the truth: Ross was danger incarnate, yet intoxicating beyond measure.</p>

<p>She had glimpsed the full extent of his violence, and she had felt the full force of his lust—but in the same breath, she had also felt alive in ways that terrified her.</p>

<p>It was exhausting, consuming, and addictive.</p>

<p>Slowly, inevitably, she found herself surrendering not just her body, but her thoughts, her desires, even the smallest fragments of resistance.</p>

<p>In his world, she realized, there was no room for hesitation.</p>

<p>And deep down, she found she didn’t want there to be.</p>

<p>***</p>

<p>The year 2046 drew to a close with Ross marrying both Carrie and Nina, cementing the unusual but undeniably powerful connections he had forged.</p>

<p>Alexa and Jennifer chose to keep their own relationships with him a secret, a decision Ross accepted without hesitation or resentment.</p>

<p>He respected their choice, understanding that not everyone was willing to live so openly within the orbit of his life.</p>

<p>It caused him no inconvenience at all—after all, with his ability to teleport anywhere in the world with a mere blink, distance and secrecy were trivial matters for him.</p>

<p>Ross often reflected on the path that had brought him here.</p>

<p>He had faced challenges, witnessed chaos, and confronted dangers that would have crushed ordinary men.</p>

<p>Yet, here he stood, surrounded by people who desired him, feared him, and—most importantly—trusted him.</p>

<p>He had carved out a life that combined absolute freedom with absolute control, a balance few could ever comprehend.</p>

<p>To him, the unconventional nature of his relationships was not a problem, but a reflection of his unique position in the world.</p>

<p>He had the power, the skill, and the means to shape his life as he wished.</p>

<p>Happiness, he realized, was not something to be sought in the ordinary or mundane—it was in having the freedom to do as he pleased, the ability to protect those he cared for, and the knowledge that he had achieved more than most could even imagine.</p>

<p>And so, as the year ended, Ross allowed himself a rare moment of quiet satisfaction.</p>

<p>He was content with what he had, with what he had achieved, and with the life he had built—a life that many would envy, yet few could truly understand.</p>

<p>He was happy, and for him, that was all that truly mattered.</p>

<p>The world might shift, people might change, but his happiness, rooted in his power, his relationships, and his own unyielding will, was unshakable.</p>

<p>***</p>

<p>Ross spent his days surrounded by his family, enjoying vacations, quiet afternoons, and the rare moments of peace that came with a life few could ever imagine.</p>

<p>Yet, even in this calm, Ross’s mind was never idle, always scanning, always noticing.</p>

<p>One afternoon, as he flipped through television channels with a lazy ease, a voice sliced through the background noise and grabbed his full attention.</p>

<p>&quot;...you broke my heart, and look at me now, I’m free from you, cheating, lying pig...&quot;</p>

<p>The song was fast-paced, energetic, and undeniably catchy, the lyrics carrying a mix of defiance and heartbreak that somehow resonated with him.</p>

<p>He found himself leaning closer to the screen, his sharp eyes studying the performer.</p>

<p>She was young, probably in her twenties, yet there was an unmistakable magnetism about her—confidence in her stance, fire in her eyes, and a presence that demanded attention.</p>

<p>Despite her youth, her fame was already global; she moved with the kind of poise and assurance that made it clear she was no ordinary woman.</p>

<p>Ross’s lips curved into a slow, knowing smile. Something stirred within him—a spark of interest that quickly ignited into certainty.</p>

<p>He always recognized potential, power, and allure when he saw it, and she radiated all three effortlessly.</p>

<p>The way she commanded the screen, the way her voice carried both strength and vulnerability, it intrigued him, drew him in, and left him wanting more.</p>

<p>His eyes scanned the ticker at the bottom of the screen, and when he read the name &quot;Karen Wall,&quot; it was as though a decision had already been made in his mind.</p>

<p>This young, talented, fiery woman was going to be his next wife.</p>

<p>There was no hesitation, no question—just a quiet certainty that this was how things were meant to unfold.</p>

<p>Ross leaned back, still smiling, letting the thought settle.</p>

<p>His interest was not fleeting; it was deliberate, precise, and irresistible.</p>

<p>He could already imagine the paths, the encounters, the subtle ways he would draw her closer, making sure she became part of the life he orchestrated so effortlessly.</p>

<p>Karen Wall was not just a name on a screen—she was the next Chapter, and Ross intended to write it according to his will.</p>
