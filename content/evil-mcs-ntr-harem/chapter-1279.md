<p>&quot;Young Master Jiang Chen! Welcome back to the Golden Fortune! Are you feeling lucky today?&quot; a beautiful hostess greeted him with a bright and professional smile.</p>

<p>She was young, elegant, and clearly well-trained in dealing with wealthy customers.</p>

<p>Her name was Xue Lengyan, and she had quickly become one of the more popular hostesses in the Golden Fortune.</p>

<p>Jiang Chen looked at her and smiled.</p>

<p>&quot;I certainly am, Yan’er. Especially now that I’ve seen you, I have a feeling I’m going to win big today!&quot;</p>

<p>Xue Lengyan covered her mouth and giggled softly.</p>

<p>&quot;Young Master Jiang Chen certainly knows how to make a woman happy. I wish you the best of luck today. Please follow me, and I’ll take you to an available table.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;Lead the way.&quot;</p>

<p>A minute later, Ross was comfortably seated at one of the gambling tables.</p>

<p>The Golden Fortune was already bustling with activity.</p>

<p>Cultivators, merchants, young masters, wealthy families, and even a few mysterious experts could be seen gambling throughout the enormous establishment.</p>

<p>Spiritual stones and gold coins constantly changed hands.</p>

<p>Some people laughed joyfully after winning.</p>

<p>Others cursed their terrible luck after losing.</p>

<p>Jiang Chen, however, wasn’t concerned.</p>

<p>He had come here for a reason. He came here to win. It was an extremely simple dice game.</p>

<p>The dealer placed several dice beneath a small cup, shook them skillfully, and then placed the cup on the table.</p>

<p>Jiang Chen nodded.</p>

<p>&quot;Let’s start already!&quot;</p>

<p>The dealer smiled professionally.</p>

<p>&quot;Please place your wager, Young Master Jiang Chen.&quot;</p>

<p>Jiang Chen casually reached into his soul space and took out a single gold coin.</p>

<p>&quot;Odds.&quot;</p>

<p>He placed the gold coin on the table.</p>

<p>The dealer gave a respectful nod.</p>

<p>&quot;Very well.&quot;</p>

<p>He lifted the cup.</p>

<p>The dice rolled slightly before finally coming to a stop.</p>

<p>A three.</p>

<p>&quot;Odds!&quot;</p>

<p>The dealer smiled.</p>

<p>&quot;Congratulations, Young Master Jiang Chen!&quot;</p>

<p>He immediately pushed another gold coin toward Jiang Chen.</p>

<p>Jiang Chen picked it up with a satisfied smile.</p>

<p>&quot;So I won.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;Indeed.&quot;</p>

<p>The dealer’s expression remained perfectly professional.</p>

<p>The people nearby barely paid attention.</p>

<p>After all, one gold coin was nothing to most of the wealthy cultivators in the Golden Fortune.</p>

<p>But behind the scenes, everything had already been carefully calculated.</p>

<p>The first few games were designed to be easy.</p>

<p>Let the customer win.</p>

<p>Let him taste victory.</p>

<p>Let him believe that his luck was extraordinary.</p>

<p>Then, once confidence and greed took over, the house would gradually increase the stakes.</p>

<p>Eventually, the gambler would lose far more than he had ever won.</p>

<p>This was one of the oldest tricks in the gambling business.</p>

<p>It didn’t matter whether the gambling house was located on Earth or in a cultivation world.</p>

<p>The principles were exactly the same.</p>

<p>Give them a little sweetness first.</p>

<p>Make them believe fortune favors them.</p>

<p>Then take everything.</p>

<p>And the Golden Fortune had used this method countless times.</p>

<p>Unfortunately for them, Jiang Chen wasn’t an ordinary gambler.</p>

<p>As he looked at the gold coin in his hand, a mysterious smile appeared on his face.</p>

<p>&quot;Let’s play again.&quot;</p>

<p>And the game continued.</p>

<p>What was supposed to be a casual gambling session quickly turned into something that nobody in the Golden Fortune could have anticipated.</p>

<p>One game.</p>

<p>Then another.</p>

<p>Then another.</p>

<p>Every time the dealer shook the dice, Jiang Chen seemed to know exactly what the result would be.</p>

<p>&quot;Odd.&quot;</p>

<p>He won.</p>

<p>&quot;Even.&quot;</p>

<p>He won again.</p>

<p>&quot;Odd.&quot;</p>

<p>Another victory.</p>

<p>At first, the surrounding gamblers merely glanced at him.</p>

<p>After half an hour, they started paying attention.</p>

<p>After an hour, the entire gambling floor was practically watching him.</p>

<p>Upstairs, someone hurried through the corridor before stopping in front of a private room and knocking frantically on the door.</p>

<p>Knock! Knock! Knock!</p>

<p>&quot;Miss Su! Miss Su!&quot; Xue Lengyan’s anxious voice came from outside. &quot;Something big is happening downstairs!&quot;</p>

<p>A moment later, the door opened.</p>

<p>An exceptionally beautiful woman stepped out.</p>

<p>If Xue Lengyan possessed the youthful charm of a fresh flower, this woman carried the mature elegance of someone who had already experienced the complexities of the world.</p>

<p>Her every movement was graceful and composed, and her confident demeanor made it immediately clear that she was someone accustomed to being in control.</p>

<p>Her name was Su Hanyue.</p>

<p>She frowned slightly.</p>

<p>&quot;What happened, Lengyan?&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;Please come quickly, Miss Su! You need to see this for yourself!&quot;</p>

<p>Xue Lengyan was still visibly flustered.</p>

<p>Miss Su studied her for a moment before nodding.</p>

<p>&quot;Very well.&quot;</p>

<p>She followed Xue Lengyan down the corridor and stopped at the railing overlooking the main gambling floor.</p>

<p>She didn’t even need more than a glance.</p>

<p>The reason for the commotion was immediately obvious.</p>

<p>The entire area surrounding one particular table was packed with spectators.</p>

<p>At the center of it all sat Jiang Chen.</p>

<p>&quot;Again!&quot;</p>

<p>Ross laughed happily as he casually tossed several gold coins between his fingers.</p>

<p>In front of him was an increasingly ridiculous pile of winnings.</p>

<p>Gold coins were stacked into neat towers across the table, while even more had been piled beside his chair.</p>

<p>Someone nearby quietly counted the approximate amount.</p>

<p>&quot;That’s... more than ten thousand gold coins.&quot;</p>

<p>Another gambler stared in disbelief.</p>

<p>&quot;In only one hour?&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;That’s impossible.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;It can’t be luck.&quot;</p>

<p>The whispers continued spreading.</p>

<p>Ross, however, seemed completely unconcerned by everyone’s attention.</p>

<p>He simply leaned back comfortably and looked toward the dealer.</p>

<p>&quot;Come on. Next round.&quot;</p>

<p>The dealer’s forehead was already covered in sweat.</p>

<p>His professional smile had become increasingly stiff.</p>

<p>Every victory had been devastating for the gambling house.</p>

<p>And what made the situation even worse was that Ross didn’t appear to be slowing down.</p>

<p>From Miss Su’s position above, the mountain of gold coins immediately caught her attention.</p>

<p>Her eyes narrowed.</p>

<p>Ten thousand gold coins was already an enormous sum.</p>

<p>And according to the information she had received, Ross had apparently lost a considerable amount of money at the Golden Fortune in the past.</p>

<p>Yet today, he had returned and was winning at a completely astonishing rate.</p>

<p>In barely an hour, his winnings had already exceeded twice the amount the original Jiang Chen had previously lost at this gambling house.</p>

<p>Miss Su’s expression gradually became serious.</p>
