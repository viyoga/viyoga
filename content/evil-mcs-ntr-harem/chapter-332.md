<p>Jessica Smith could hardly believe how drastically her life had changed in just a few short weeks.</p>

<p>One moment, she had been fearing for her life—and her father&#x27;s—and now, here she was, freely enjoying a cruise with happy honeymooners.</p>

<p>The future no longer worried her; after paying off all her father&#x27;s debts, she found herself with millions to spare.</p>

<p>Life should have been good, but instead, she found herself tangled in the web of a mysterious man.</p>

<p>Ross Oakley had saved her, yes, but he had also claimed her as his own.</p>

<p>Everything still felt like a dream.</p>

<p>To clear her mind, she tried to investigate the whereabouts of Mr. Adams, just to ensure it wasn&#x27;t all some wild fantasy.</p>

<p>Yet, no one seemed to know anything. There were reports about Adams and his entire crew going missing, but that was it—no follow-ups, no further details.</p>

<p>It was clear someone was working hard to bury the truth, and she could think of only one person powerful enough to make it happen.</p>

<p>&quot;Ross Oakley. Just who are you?&quot; Jessica muttered under her breath, the question lingering in her mind.</p>

<p>She would have continued to mull over her thoughts, but someone suddenly interrupted her musings.</p>

<p>&quot;Hello there, I saw the sad look on your face and couldn&#x27;t help but come over. Are you okay?&quot;</p>

<p>Jessica turned around, her eyes slowly moving away from the endless expanse of the ocean to meet the gaze of an extremely handsome man—young, too.</p>

<p>He appeared to be around her age, but what struck her most wasn&#x27;t just his striking good looks.</p>

<p>It was his kindness, evident in the genuine concern on his face.</p>

<p>She could easily tell he wasn&#x27;t just making small talk; there was sincerity in his words.</p>

<p>His eyes locked with hers, and he didn&#x27;t let them wander aimlessly over her body as so many others often did.</p>

<p>His directness and honesty caught her off guard, and for a fleeting moment, she found herself appreciating him for the simple act of showing genuine care.</p>

<p>But that only made her feel emptier inside, as it reminded her of everything she lacked.</p>

<p>Jessica had never been in love, thinking she would eventually find it once she had established herself—once she&#x27;d finished her studies and secured a stable job so that she could provide for her father.</p>

<p>She had always told herself that love could wait. But now, her father didn&#x27;t need to work a single day more in his life.</p>

<p>He was well taken care of, and yet, here she was, alone with a wealth of money and no one to share it with.</p>

<p>The world around her felt like it should be perfect, yet she couldn&#x27;t escape the gnawing emptiness in her chest.</p>

<p>She was caught in a whirlwind of conflicting emotions. Despite all she had accomplished, something felt wrong.</p>

<p>&quot;It&#x27;s none of your business. Please go away,&quot; Jessica replied, her voice harsher than she meant it to be.</p>

<p>She quickly regretted the tone, but it was too late. The words had already left her lips.</p>

<p>And ultimately, she thought that this was also for this kind stranger&#x27;s wellbeing.</p>

<p>She had seen what Ross was capable of when provoked, and despite the young man&#x27;s kind eyes and gentle manner, she didn&#x27;t want to bring any unwanted trouble to him.</p>

<p>The thought of making this handsome stranger the target of Ross&#x27;s wrath sent a shiver down her spine.</p>

<p>The young man didn&#x27;t move at first, and Jessica couldn&#x27;t help but feel a twinge of guilt for snapping at him.</p>

<p>But, he simply offered a small, understanding smile, as if he knew exactly what was going on in her mind.</p>

<p>Without a word, he turned and walked away, leaving her to the silence of the vast ocean and the weight of her own thoughts.</p>

<p>Jessica thought that would be the end of it, but just five minutes later, the same guy returned, carrying a large bucket of popcorn and two sodas.</p>

<p>He walked over with a casual smile, clearly not taking her earlier rejection to heart.</p>

<p>&quot;I love listening to sad stories while eating popcorn,&quot; he said with an easygoing grin.</p>

<p>&quot;I&#x27;ll tell you mine first, and I&#x27;d love to hear yours afterward.&quot; He placed the snacks down between them and sat on the edge of the bench, his posture relaxed but still respectful of her space.</p>

<p>Jessica wasn&#x27;t sure why, but something about his friendly attitude made her smile for the first time in a long while.</p>

<p>It was a genuine smile, one that felt like a small weight had been lifted from her shoulders.</p>

<p>&quot;God, you&#x27;re beautiful,&quot; he added, his tone sincere.</p>

<p>&quot;I&#x27;m Kevin,&quot; he introduced himself, his eyes locking with hers in a way that made her feel noticed—not in a way that made her uncomfortable, but in a way that felt warm and kind.</p>

<p>He raised his hand, offering her a handshake.</p>

<p>For a moment, Jessica hesitated.</p>

<p>She had been wrapped in her own thoughts for so long that she had almost forgotten how nice it was to have a simple, friendly interaction.</p>

<p>Her heart, weary from all the confusion and tension of the past few weeks, told her that maybe it wouldn&#x27;t hurt to have a new friend on this trip.</p>

<p>She needed a distraction from her overwhelming feelings, even if only for a brief moment.</p>

<p>&quot;Jessica,&quot; she replied, reaching out to shake his hand, the simple gesture feeling more grounding than she expected.</p>

<p>Kevin&#x27;s eyes lit up as he started to share his story.</p>

<p>It was a lighthearted tale about his childhood, filled with humorous anecdotes and self-deprecating remarks.</p>

<p>As Kevin continued to talk, Jessica found herself listening intently, almost forgetting where they were.</p>

<p>The conversation flowed easily, with laughter here and there, and for a few precious moments, she felt like things might be okay.</p>

<p>It was such a relief to have a normal conversation after everything she had been through.</p>

<p>Little did either of them know, their conversation hadn&#x27;t gone unnoticed.</p>

<p>From a distance, someone was watching them closely, taking note of every word and action.</p>

<p>The observer&#x27;s eyes lingered on Jessica, sensing the subtle shift in her mood.</p>
