<p>&quot;In fact,&quot; Ross continued, a chuckle escaping his lips, &quot;why should we leave him in the dark? Let’s help him figure it out. I think it’ll be fun to see his reaction.&quot;</p>

<p>His laugh was low and menacing, filled with anticipation for whatever chaos he was about to unleash.</p>

<p>Reina frowned, unease creeping into her voice.</p>

<p>&quot;What are you planning, Ross?&quot; she asked cautiously, her eyes narrowing as she watched his expression shift into one of mischief.</p>

<p>&quot;You’ll see,&quot; he replied cryptically, leaning back further as if to enjoy the suspense.</p>

<p>&quot;But first, you both need to meet my girls again. It’s about time you got properly acquainted.&quot;</p>

<p>That evening, Reina and Mari arrived at Ross’s sprawling estate. The massive mansion loomed ahead, its grandeur casting an imposing shadow in the fading light of dusk.</p>

<p>As they entered, they were greeted by a group of women—Ross’s harem.</p>

<p>The women regarded Reina and Mari with a mix of curiosity and suspicion, their expressions guarded as they listened to the newcomers’ explanation.</p>

<p>Reina, her voice steady despite the knot of nerves in her stomach, spoke carefully.</p>

<p>&quot;We’ll be staying here for a little while,&quot; she began, glancing briefly at Mari for support.</p>

<p>&quot;It’s temporary—just until I can convince my daughter Ren to… come back home with us.&quot;</p>

<p>Her words were met with nods of polite acknowledgment from most of the women, though a few exchanged skeptical glances.</p>

<p>Only Althea remained silent, her sharp eyes studying Reina and Mari intently. She knew that Ross was keeping these lovely women as his pets and slaves for the moment.</p>

<p>The glint in his eyes when he introduced the two women told her everything she needed to know.</p>

<p>This wasn’t about reconciliation or temporary arrangements. This was another one of Ross’s games, and Althea knew better than to interfere.</p>

<p>Later that night, as the household settled into an uneasy calm, Ross retreated to his private study.</p>

<p>The room was dimly lit, the only illumination coming from the soft glow of his computer screen and the faint moonlight streaming through the window.</p>

<p>Sitting back in his leather chair, Ross picked up his phone and dialed a number. He leaned back, a satisfied smirk playing on his lips as he waited for the call to connect.</p>

<p>Kirito Hirose was an ambitious man. His success was not solely due to his drive but also to his understanding of hard work and the importance of connections.</p>

<p>Highly intelligent and well above his peers, Kirito had risen to prominence through relentless effort.</p>

<p>His name was synonymous with success in the business world, and his presence commanded respect in every room he entered.</p>

<p>Kirito’s life revolved around his work. Every decision, every action, was calculated to further his goals. He had only one child, a fact that suited him well given his limited time.</p>

<p>To Kirito, time with his daughter was secondary to his ambitions. He provided her with everything she could ever need materially but rarely spared more than a few moments to engage with her personally.</p>

<p>The pursuit of his dreams left little room for sentimentality.</p>

<p>There was always more to achieve, a never-ending pursuit that propelled him to the ranks of Tokyo’s elite.</p>

<p>Respected by many, he basked in the spotlight, believing he had earned it through sheer determination. Publicly, he was seen as a man of principle, someone who inspired others to strive for greatness.</p>

<p>Privately, however, he often wrestled with an unshakable sense of emptiness—a feeling he dismissed as the price of success.</p>

<p>Yet, life is rarely a fantasy of unbroken success. No one escapes trials, regardless of their shape or form.</p>

<p>And today, Kirito’s test arrived with a resounding crash. The perfectly constructed life he had built was about to face an unanticipated challenge, one he could not ignore or delegate.</p>

<p>Ring</p>

<p>Ring</p>

<p>Ring</p>

<p>The shrill sound of the phone cut through the quiet hum of his office.</p>

<p>Kirito picked up the receiver, slightly surprised at who was calling.</p>

<p>Days had passed in a blur of meetings and negotiations, and he had failed to notice someone’s absence in his life. He answered the phone and said,</p>

<p>&quot;Yes, Reina? Did you get Ren? Are you on your way back to Tokyo now?&quot; Kirito said in Japanese.</p>

<p>His voice was calm, almost mechanical, as his eyes remained on the documents scattered across his desk.</p>

<p>His multitasking skills were as sharp as ever, a habit honed through years of juggling countless responsibilities.</p>

<p>But the next words he heard on the line froze him in place, forcing his full attention on the call.</p>

<p>&quot;Kirito… something’s happened,&quot; Reina’s voice trembled, shattering the routine calm of his world.</p>

<p>There was a fragility in her tone that immediately put him on edge, as if she was struggling to hold herself together.</p>

<p>&quot;Ren… I found her but… ahhhhhh…&quot;</p>

<p>The sound that followed wasn’t just a gasp—it was a moan, unmistakably intimate and achingly familiar.</p>

<p>Kirito froze. His mind registered it, but his heart refused to believe. He knew that sound too well, a sound he’d heard in the throes of their passion, a sound meant only for him. But this time, it wasn’t.</p>

<p>&quot;No,&quot; he whispered under his breath, as if denying it could make it untrue.</p>

<p>The pen in his hand slipped, leaving a dark, unsightly blot of ink on the pristine document he’d been reviewing. It didn’t matter.</p>

<p>The world around him felt as though it had stopped, the weight of her voice suffocating every rational thought.</p>

<p>&quot;Reina?&quot; he called, his voice shaky at first before it sharpened with urgency. &quot;Where are you? What’s happening to you right now?!&quot;</p>

<p>Silence.</p>

<p>He could hear her labored breathing on the other end, could picture her struggling to form the words.</p>

<p>&quot;Ohhh… it’s… nothing…&quot; she finally murmured, her words fragmented and breathless. &quot;I’m just… occupied right now… there’s…&quot;</p>
