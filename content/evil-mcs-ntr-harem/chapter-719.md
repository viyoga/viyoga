<p>One of the boys, emboldened by the group’s success, walked toward Ross with a smug grin on his face.</p>

<p>It was Franco—young, fit, always loud, always pushing his luck.</p>

<p>He lifted the empty rice cooker in one hand and the soup pot in the other, shaking both.</p>

<p>&quot;Hey, Big D,&quot; Franco called with a mock-sweet tone.</p>

<p>&quot;Time for dinner, my guy! Oh wait... it’s all gone! Guess you’ll just have to eat in your dreams tonight.&quot;</p>

<p>He tilted the pot theatrically. Not a drop of soup was left. &quot;Damn, even the steam’s gone. We really cleaned up.&quot;</p>

<p>A few people laughed. Nervous, unsure, but laughter nonetheless.</p>

<p>&quot;Don’t waste your breath, Franco,&quot; Trevor said from behind him, arms crossed, his tone dismissive. &quot;Big Dumb ain’t worth the calories. Let the dog starve.&quot;</p>

<p>But as soon as the words left his mouth, Ross moved.</p>

<p>He didn’t say anything.</p>

<p>He didn’t glare.</p>

<p>He just stood up.</p>

<p>The tension in the room snapped taut.</p>

<p>Franco froze. Trevor fell quiet. Everyone stopped eating, talking, or pretending not to care.</p>

<p>All eyes shifted to Ross, who rose slowly from his oversized seat with the calm, deliberate movements of someone who was in control—and knew it.</p>

<p>He stretched slightly, the fabric of his black shirt tightening around his chest and shoulders.</p>

<p>Then he tilted his head, as if mildly amused by the entire spectacle.</p>

<p>No words.</p>

<p>Just that cold, unreadable stare behind the mask.</p>

<p>Franco swallowed hard and lowered the pots.</p>

<p>Trevor took an unconscious step back.</p>

<p>The rest of the housemates watched in dead silence, hearts pounding.</p>

<p>Ross looked at the empty table, the spotless pots, and the crumbs on their plates.</p>

<p>Then, slowly, he turned and walked away—calm, quiet, without a single word.</p>

<p>And yet somehow, that said more than any outburst ever could.</p>

<p>After he disappeared into the hallway, no one dared speak.</p>

<p>The room was filled with tension and one single, lingering thought:</p>

<p>What the hell is he going to do next?</p>

<p>They didn’t have to wonder long.</p>

<p>While the rest of the housemates ate their dinner outside by the swimming pool, basking in their quiet victory over Ross, something shifted.</p>

<p>A faint aroma drifted through the open patio doors—something warm, rich, and utterly unexpected.</p>

<p>At first, no one paid it much attention. But within minutes, the scent grew stronger. It was fragrant, layered, and mouthwatering.</p>

<p>Someone wrinkled their nose and looked around. &quot;What the hell is that smell?&quot;</p>

<p>Another contestant glanced toward the house. &quot;Wait... is that... food?&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;The fuck?&quot; a guy muttered, sniffing the air like a hound. &quot;Is Big Dumb actually cooking in there?&quot;</p>

<p>That got everyone’s attention.</p>

<p>A few exchanged skeptical looks before curiosity got the better of them.</p>

<p>One by one, they rose from their seats and cautiously made their way back into the house.</p>

<p>What they found stopped them in their tracks.</p>

<p>The kitchen was alive.</p>

<p>Ross stood at the center of it all, sleeves rolled up, moving with smooth, precise motions.</p>

<p>Knives flashed in the overhead light as he chopped vegetables at lightning speed.</p>

<p>A sizzling pan sang on the stove while steam curled from a pot beside it, releasing bursts of complex, intoxicating aromas.</p>

<p>He didn’t speak. He didn’t even look up.</p>

<p>But his hands were a blur of efficiency, working with the kind of confidence that only came from years of experience.</p>

<p>&quot;Holy shit,&quot; someone whispered. &quot;Is he, like... a professional chef or something?&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;He’s moving so fast. Look at the way he handles those knives!&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;He’s—he’s not even trying, and it’s like watching one of those cooking competitions on TV.&quot;</p>

<p>More housemates crowded into the kitchen doorway, wide-eyed and silent.</p>

<p>Even the ones who had mocked him earlier stood there, stunned.</p>

<p>Ross wasn’t just cooking—he was commanding the kitchen like it belonged to him.</p>

<p>He moved with rhythm, grace, and a quiet intensity that was almost hypnotic.</p>

<p>Every motion was purposeful. Every ingredient was handled with care.</p>

<p>It was less like preparing a meal and more like... performing.</p>

<p>He was dancing between counters, spinning from stove to sink and back again.</p>

<p>Oil hissed, spices hit hot pans with a burst of fragrance, and the entire house seemed to melt into that sound, that smell, that presence.</p>

<p>No music played.</p>

<p>And yet it felt like there was a beat—his beat—and he was in perfect sync with it.</p>

<p>The crowd didn’t speak. They didn’t dare. They just watched, mesmerized.</p>

<p>&quot;I can’t believe it. He’s... actually amazing in the kitchen.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;I feel like I just watched a live episode of MasterChef.&quot;</p>

<p>The woman who had insulted him earlier looked down at her half-eaten plate of cold rice and over-salted beef and muttered,</p>

<p>&quot;Shit... we messed with the wrong guy.&quot;</p>

<p>But no one dared go after Ross now.</p>

<p>He had made his move—quiet, sharp, and unforgettable.</p>

<p>And suddenly, the house felt very different.</p>

<p>Ten minutes later, Ross was done.</p>

<p>He stepped back from the stove, wiped his hands on a towel, and calmly plated his food without sparing a single glance at the group still standing near the kitchen entrance.</p>

<p>Whatever he had cooked, it was impossible to tell just by looking—but the smell said everything.</p>

<p>Savory. Rich. Mouthwatering.</p>

<p>It filled the entire house like a spell.</p>

<p>And then it happened.</p>

<p>&quot;Grrrgg...&quot;</p>

<p>One stomach growled. Then another. Then another.</p>

<p>Like a chain reaction, a chorus of hunger echoed through the kitchen and into the hallway beyond.</p>

<p>Heads turned, eyes widened, and everyone exchanged awkward glances.</p>

<p>They had just eaten.</p>

<p>Rice, soup, leftovers—the works.</p>

<p>But none of it compared to this.</p>

<p>The aroma of Ross’s meal clung to their senses, teased their tongues, and awakened a whole new wave of hunger they hadn’t expected.</p>

<p>Saliva pooled in mouths that had no reason to still be hungry. But the body doesn’t lie.</p>

<p>One of the boys muttered under his breath, &quot;Why does it smell better than everything we cooked put together?&quot;</p>

<p>Another girl clutched her stomach and whispered, &quot;I swear I was full ten minutes ago... but now I’m starving.&quot;</p>
