<p>Nina blinked, her lips parting slightly in shock. She had, of course, met Carrie before—Ross had never kept his circle small, and introductions were inevitable.</p>

<p>But she immediately understood what he meant this time.</p>

<p>This wasn’t about a polite introduction.</p>

<p>This was Ross revealing something bigger, something intimate, something that would change the dynamic between them completely.</p>

<p>Carrie wasn’t just another woman in Ross’s life. She was also his woman.</p>

<p>A swirl of emotions hit Nina all at once. Surprise. Unease. Curiosity.</p>

<p>And somewhere in the middle of it, a burning streak of jealousy that clawed at her chest.</p>

<p>She had just given herself to Ross fully, body and heart, and already she had to face the truth of sharing him.</p>

<p>Nina knew that she would get used to this truth fast especially that Ross has dozens of wives lining up for his time and attention.</p>

<p>Ross, of course, didn’t flinch.</p>

<p>He said it like a statement of fact, as though the decision was already made and she had only to accept it.</p>

<p>That was who he was—dominant, unapologetic, unshaken.</p>

<p>And against her better judgment, Nina couldn’t help but feel herself drawn deeper because of it.</p>

<p>But then another thought stabbed through her haze of feelings.</p>

<p>Zack.</p>

<p>She was still, on paper, Zack’s girlfriend.</p>

<p>She had let that boy hold onto her title, even while she had given everything else to Ross.</p>

<p>It felt dirty now, hypocritical, and more than that, dangerous. If Zack ever found out, things could spiral fast.</p>

<p>&quot;I need to end the relationship with Zack as soon as possible,&quot; Nina thought grimly, her hands curling into fists at her sides.</p>

<p>The choice had already been made in her heart—Ross had claimed her completely.</p>

<p>She couldn’t let herself lose him over some flimsy attachment to Zack. Not now. Not ever.</p>

<p>She glanced at Ross then, his tall frame dominating the room, his presence filling the air until it was impossible to think of anyone else.</p>

<p>Nina knew, without a doubt, she would take that leap.</p>

<p>Whatever Carrie’s presence meant, whatever changes were coming, she wasn’t going to back down.</p>

<p>She would fight, she would adapt, and she would make sure she remained Ross’s woman—no matter what it cost.</p>

<p>***</p>

<p>Nina, of course, didn’t have to wait long to act.</p>

<p>The heaviness in her chest had been growing for days, a constant reminder of the choice she had already made in her heart.</p>

<p>Tuesday morning, she picked up her phone and called Zack.</p>

<p>She kept her voice calm, almost detached, though inside she felt a whirlwind of emotions—guilt, fear, and a lingering sense of exhilaration from the memories that haunted her.</p>

<p>They agreed to meet at a quiet, private restaurant, far from prying eyes.</p>

<p>She refused to be cruel enough to break up with him through a text.</p>

<p>She wasn’t the type to humiliate someone she had once cared for, and this was a delicate moment that deserved some level of dignity.</p>

<p>Nor could she do it at school, where gossip spread like wildfire and judgmental eyes would dissect every word, every glance.</p>

<p>Zack deserved more than whispers and rumors; he deserved at least this much.</p>

<p>When she arrived, Zack was already waiting. He stood as soon as he saw her, his posture casual, yet attentive.</p>

<p>His dark hair was neatly combed, his sharp features accentuated by the morning sunlight that filtered through the restaurant windows.</p>

<p>He looked effortlessly handsome, the kind of man who could make anyone’s heart skip a beat.</p>

<p>To anyone else, he would have been perfection. To Nina... he was just Zack.</p>

<p>Ordinary.</p>

<p>&quot;What’s up, babe?&quot; Zack greeted, his grin warm and genuine.</p>

<p>&quot;You didn’t show up to school yesterday, and now today you’re skipping classes just to meet me? Not that I’m complaining. I missed you.&quot; His tone was playful, teasing, as if he were trying to lighten the air with charm and familiarity.</p>

<p>Nina slid into the booth across from him, and for a brief second, she caught herself almost smiling.</p>

<p>Almost.</p>

<p>But the memory of Ross—his strength, his presence, the way he had consumed her both physically and emotionally—pushed everything else away.</p>

<p>That spark, the one that had once made Zack irresistible to her, had been completely extinguished.</p>

<p>She studied him carefully, really looked at him for the first time in weeks.</p>

<p>He was perfect in so many ways, kind and attentive, loyal and steady.</p>

<p>But he could never compete with the chaos, the heat, the raw power of what she had experienced with Ross.</p>

<p>She could feel the contrast in her veins like fire—Zack was safe, predictable; Ross was dangerous, overwhelming, and utterly addictive.</p>

<p>Her chest tightened as a wave of guilt swept over her.</p>

<p>She had made mistakes, yes, but she couldn’t undo what had already happened.</p>

<p>She had tasted desire, and it had shown her a side of herself she couldn’t ignore.</p>

<p>She was not the same Nina who had once been content with Zack’s love and care.</p>

<p>This is harder than I originally imagined, she thought, fingers nervously fidgeting on her lap.</p>

<p>But I can’t live a lie anymore. I can’t keep pretending that what I feel is the same as what I used to feel.</p>

<p>Her eyes lingered on Zack’s, searching for some spark of recognition, some understanding of the storm that had overtaken her.</p>

<p>All she saw was love and trust, and the realization cut her like a blade.</p>

<p>She felt her throat tighten, and yet she knew she had to speak, had to end it before it dragged on any longer.</p>

<p>&quot;I... Zack,&quot; she began, her voice soft, hesitant at first. She swallowed hard, steadying herself. &quot;I need to tell you something... something important.&quot;</p>

<p>Zack leaned forward slightly, his brow furrowing, a small frown forming.</p>

<p>&quot;What is it? You’re scaring me, Nina...&quot; His voice carried genuine concern, and her heart ached at how wrong she was about to make things.</p>

<p>&quot;I... I can’t... I can’t be with you anymore,&quot; she admitted, her words trembling but firm.</p>
