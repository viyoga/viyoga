<p>One of the five beautiful women in the house. Not just pretty—elegant. Confident.</p>

<p>The kind whose presence could silence a room.</p>

<p>She met his gaze with calm poise, unbothered by the curious stares now forming around them.</p>

<p>&quot;Morning,&quot; she said softly, folding her arms on the table.</p>

<p>Ross studied her for a moment, then leaned back slightly in his seat, smile deepening. &quot;Didn’t expect company.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;I didn’t expect to be hungry again,&quot; she replied, glancing meaningfully at his plate.</p>

<p>A pause. Then Ross chuckled—a low, quiet sound that rumbled from his chest.</p>

<p>He cut a piece from his dish and slid the plate slightly forward.</p>

<p>&quot;Curious?&quot; he asked.</p>

<p>She tilted her head. &quot;Very.&quot;</p>

<p>He handed her the fork.</p>

<p>All around the patio, the rest of the housemates watched in silence, unsure what was happening—but knowing one thing for sure:</p>

<p>The walls around Ross were starting to crack.</p>

<p>And someone had just stepped through.</p>

<p>The woman picked up the fork with delicate fingers and speared a piece of meat from the plate.</p>

<p>The aroma alone was enough to make her pause—it was rich and layered, subtly spiced, and unlike anything she’d ever smelled before.</p>

<p>Just as she raised it to her mouth, Ross’s calm, confident voice stopped her.</p>

<p>&quot;A word of warning,&quot; he said, his smile lazy but teasing.</p>

<p>&quot;Once you taste my cooking, everything else will taste like trash.&quot;</p>

<p>She glanced at him, brows lifting in amusement.</p>

<p>&quot;Is that so?&quot; she asked, her voice light but laced with curiosity. &quot;Let me be the judge of that.&quot;</p>

<p>There was a brief silence between them as she placed the piece of meat in her mouth. She chewed once—and then her eyes widened.</p>

<p>Boom.</p>

<p>A detonation of flavor erupted on her tongue. The taste was transcendent, a perfect harmony of sweet, savory, smoky, and spiced—each note distinct, yet seamlessly blended.</p>

<p>The sensation didn’t stop there.</p>

<p>As she swallowed, a ripple of warmth coursed through her body. Her breath caught.</p>

<p>It wasn’t just food—it was an experience.</p>

<p>Her back arched slightly in her seat as the sensation flowed from her mouth down her throat, then radiated outward like a slow-burning fire.</p>

<p>Her skin tingled, her heartbeat quickened, and every nerve in her body seemed to wake up.</p>

<p>A soft, involuntary moan escaped her lips, much to her own surprise.</p>

<p>She closed her eyes for a moment, overwhelmed by the pleasure.</p>

<p>&quot;What... what is this?&quot; she breathed, her voice husky.</p>

<p>Ross chuckled softly, clearly pleased. &quot;Just a little something I whipped up.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;It’s... it’s unreal,&quot; she whispered, staring at the food now as if it were some sacred relic. &quot;I’ve never tasted anything like it. I feel like—I don’t even know how to describe it—it’s like I just had the best feeling of my life, and all I did was chew.&quot;</p>

<p>Ross leaned back slightly, enjoying her reaction. &quot;Told you. You won’t look at ordinary food the same way again.&quot;</p>

<p>She opened her eyes, still slightly dazed, and gave him a look that was a mix of admiration and disbelief.</p>

<p>&quot;You should be arrested for making something this dangerous.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;I’ve been told that before,&quot; he said with a wink.</p>

<p>Still reeling from the taste, she slowly reached for another piece of meat—this time with both excitement and a touch of fear. If one bite had done that, what would the second do?</p>

<p>&quot;I want more,&quot; the woman said, her voice soft but firm as her eyes remained glued to Ross’s plate.</p>

<p>There was still plenty of food left—perfectly grilled meat, golden vegetables glistening with oil, and an aroma that could stir the dead.</p>

<p>She didn’t bother hiding her craving. It wasn’t just hunger now. It was curiosity. Desire.</p>

<p>A need to chase that euphoric sensation she’d just tasted.</p>

<p>She lifted her gaze slowly, meeting Ross’s eyes with expectant calm.</p>

<p>Ross chuckled, resting his chin on his hand as he looked at her.</p>

<p>She was stunning—sharp eyes, full lips, proud posture.</p>

<p>But right now, she looked a little undone.</p>

<p>That single bite had shaken her composure. And he knew it.</p>

<p>&quot;The first bite was a freebie,&quot; he said, flashing a mischievous smile. &quot;From here on out, you’ll have to pay.&quot;</p>

<p>Her expression flickered, amusement flashing behind narrowed eyes. &quot;And what kind of payment are we talking about?&quot;</p>

<p>She asked like she didn’t already know. But there was tension now—excitement, and maybe a little bit of dread. Her tongue darted out to wet her lips.</p>

<p>Ross leaned back, taking his time. &quot;A kiss,&quot; he said casually, &quot;for the next bite. And the one after that... I get to fondle your boobs. Bare.&quot;</p>

<p>For a moment, she just stared at him. There was no shock in her expression—no offense, no dramatic gasp.</p>

<p>Just... silence. Her lips parted slightly, and a slow breath escaped.</p>

<p>And then she laughed, a short and dry sound, shaking her head as if disappointed, but not surprised.</p>

<p>&quot;They were right about you,&quot; she said coolly. &quot;You’re exactly the kind of scum they warned me about. One bite in, and you start acting like a sleazy bastard.&quot;</p>

<p>She pushed her chair back, stood gracefully, and turned on her heel.</p>

<p>Her steps were measured and smooth, her chin held high, her posture regal.</p>

<p>She didn’t look back—not even once—as she walked away from the table and into the hallway.</p>

<p>Ross watched her go, the grin never leaving his face. He wasn’t bothered.</p>

<p>Not in the slightest.</p>

<p>Because he saw what she tried to hide. The hesitation. The way her breath hitched.</p>

<p>The fact that, for one split second, her eyes had dropped—not in disgust, but in consideration.</p>

<p>The way her fingers had brushed against the table’s edge longer than they should have.</p>

<p>And, of course, the fact that she hadn’t actually rejected the food.</p>

<p>She’d rejected him—at least for now.</p>

<p>But he knew.</p>

<p>The taste still lingered on her tongue. The sensation still clung to her skin.</p>

<p>That first bite had set something in motion.</p>

<p>And no matter how she tried to resist, she would be back. Sooner than later.</p>

<p>Ross leaned forward again, picked up his fork, and took another bite of his own food.</p>

<p>&quot;Mmm,&quot; he murmured, savoring it. &quot;No one walks away from perfection forever.&quot;</p>

<p>He didn’t need to chase. The fish had already taken the bait.</p>

<p>And she was hooked.</p>
