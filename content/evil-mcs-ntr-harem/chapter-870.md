<p>Lois froze.</p>

<p>Slowly, she turned her head—her blood going cold as Ross stepped inside, his tall frame filling the doorway, his gaze dark and unreadable.</p>

<p>A cruel smile curved his lips as he spoke softly, &quot;Good morning, Lois. I trust you slept well?&quot;</p>

<p>Her phone slipped from her hands, hitting the floor with a dull thud.</p>

<p>Ross’s lips curved into a slow, dangerous smile as he stepped closer, his polished shoes echoing softly against the wooden floor.</p>

<p>Every movement carried authority, a confidence that radiated like heat and made Lois feel smaller and smaller with each step he took toward her.</p>

<p>He bent down without breaking eye contact, picked up the phone from where it had fallen, and turned it over in his hand.</p>

<p>For a moment, he simply looked at the screen, then at her, his dark eyes gleaming with amusement.</p>

<p>&quot;Don’t let me interrupt, Lois,&quot; he said, voice silky and commanding. &quot;Go on—finish what you started. Set up that meeting with your soon-to-be ex-boyfriend.&quot;</p>

<p>His words sank like poison into her veins. He held the phone out to her, but Lois hesitated, her fingers trembling before she took it from him.</p>

<p>His skin brushed hers, warm and steady, a stark contrast to the icy dread coursing through her body.</p>

<p>She stared at the bright screen for a long moment, her mind screaming at her to find a way out.</p>

<p>But there was no escape.</p>

<p>Ross’s presence was suffocating—like a predator patiently watching its prey.</p>

<p>She could feel his gaze on her even as she typed, each word like a blade carving deep into her chest:</p>

<p>&quot;Billy, can we meet later today? I need to tell you something important.&quot;</p>

<p>Her breath caught when she hit send alongside a few details of the meeting.</p>

<p>The message was gone, and with it, a piece of her heart.</p>

<p>The phone slipped from her weak fingers and landed softly on the bed.</p>

<p>Slowly, she raised her eyes to Ross.</p>

<p>He was already smiling, that same infuriating, confident smile that said everything without words.</p>

<p>&quot;See?&quot; His voice was low, almost a whisper, yet it filled the room like a command. &quot;That wasn’t so hard, was it?&quot;</p>

<p>Lois didn’t answer. She couldn’t. Her throat was tight with emotions she didn’t dare let out—not in front of him.</p>

<p>Ross chuckled, a deep, husky sound that made her shiver.</p>

<p>He stepped closer, closing the gap between them until she could feel his heat, his scent, the raw dominance that rolled off him like a tide.</p>

<p>Gently, but with undeniable authority, he slid a finger under her chin and tilted her face upward.</p>

<p>Their eyes met—hers wide and trembling, his dark and hungry.</p>

<p>&quot;You’re beautiful when you’re obedient,&quot; he murmured, his lips curling into a smirk that sent a chill down her spine.</p>

<p>His gaze swept over her body slowly, deliberately, lingering on the thin dress clinging to her curves.</p>

<p>His eyes darkened, filled with a hunger that made Lois’s breath hitch.</p>

<p>&quot;Now that you’re up,&quot; he continued, his voice a low rumble that thrummed through her bones, &quot;and looking absolutely irresistible... let’s get you something to eat.&quot; His thumb brushed lightly against her lower lip, and the subtle, teasing touch made her heart pound violently in her chest.</p>

<p>Then his tone shifted—still soft, but darker, laced with promise and danger.</p>

<p>&quot;...And after that, we’ll continue where we left off last night.&quot;</p>

<p>Lois swallowed hard, her knees threatening to give way.</p>

<p>Her mind screamed no, but her body betrayed her with a shiver she couldn’t suppress.</p>

<p>Ross noticed. His smirk deepened.</p>

<p>&quot;Good girl,&quot; he said, letting his hand slide down her cheek before pulling away slowly, making sure she felt the loss of his touch. &quot;Come on.&quot;</p>

<p>He turned and walked toward the door without looking back, expecting her to follow.</p>

<p>Lois stood frozen for a few seconds, staring at his broad back, fighting the whirlpool of emotions inside her—fear, anger, guilt... and something else she hated herself for feeling.</p>

<p>Finally, with trembling legs, she obeyed.</p>

<p>The hallway outside was silent, eerily so, and Lois found herself glancing around as they descended a grand staircase into a spacious dining area.</p>

<p>Everything about this house screamed wealth and power—the kind you didn’t cross, the kind you didn’t escape from.</p>

<p>Ross gestured for her to sit at the long dining table.</p>

<p>&quot;Breakfast will be served in a minute,&quot; he said casually, as if this was the most normal morning in the world.</p>

<p>He sat across from her, leaning back in his chair with the relaxed confidence of a man who owned everything in his sight—including her.</p>

<p>Lois sat stiffly, her hands folded tightly in her lap.</p>

<p>Her phone buzzed softly against her thigh—Billy. Her heart lurched painfully.</p>

<p>She didn’t dare look, not with Ross watching her like a hawk.</p>

<p>He noticed anyway. His smirk returned, sharp and knowing.</p>

<p>&quot;That’ll be him, won’t it?&quot; he said, his tone dripping with amusement. &quot;Eager little Billy. Poor guy.&quot;</p>

<p>Her stomach knotted, shame and anger burning in her chest.</p>

<p>She opened her mouth to speak—maybe to defend Billy, maybe to beg—but Ross raised a hand, cutting her off before she could utter a word.</p>

<p>&quot;Don’t,&quot; he said softly, but there was steel beneath the silk.</p>

<p>&quot;Billy doesn’t matter anymore. Not in this world. In my world, there’s only me... and you.&quot;</p>

<p>His words hung heavy in the air, final and absolute.</p>

<p>Lois stared down at her lap, her vision swimming as silent tears slid down her cheeks.</p>

<p>Then Ross leaned forward slightly, his voice dropping to that dark, intimate tone that made her pulse spike against her will.</p>

<p>&quot;Finish your breakfast, Lois. You’ll need your strength... because when we’re done eating...&quot; His eyes dragged over her like a slow caress, &quot;...I’m going to remind you who you belong to.&quot;</p>

<p>Lois’s breath caught in her throat. For the first time since waking up, she realized the truth that made her heart pound harder than ever.</p>

<p>And true to his word, Ross didn’t stop at promises—he claimed her again the moment breakfast was over.</p>
