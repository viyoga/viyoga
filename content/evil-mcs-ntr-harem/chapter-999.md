<p>&quot;Nice show, Karen. Well done.&quot; A portly manager said, giving a hearty high five to the 5’8&quot; woman in heels.</p>

<p>She stood tall and confident, yet graceful, her presence impossible to ignore.</p>

<p>From the top of her naturally black hair to the tips of her perfectly polished shoes, Karen radiated a kind of effortless magnetism.</p>

<p>Her curves were accentuated just enough to turn heads without seeming deliberate, but it was her voice that made her truly unforgettable.</p>

<p>That voice—rich, sultry, and full of emotion—had already won her several hit songs and a legion of admirers.</p>

<p>Countless men had tried to capture her attention, but only one had truly succeeded.</p>

<p>Karen had a boyfriend, of course, and their relationship had become one of the few things in her life she guarded fiercely.</p>

<p>&quot;Thanks,&quot; Karen murmured, her tone a mix of exhaustion and satisfaction.</p>

<p>Beads of fresh sweat clung to her skin, glistening under the dressing room lights.</p>

<p>Tonight’s performance on the famous TV show had demanded everything she had, and she had delivered without holding back.</p>

<p>Every note, every gesture, every glance she had given on stage was saturated with energy and passion.</p>

<p>Perhaps it was this very intensity, this commitment to perfection, that had helped her rise to the top of an industry where so many faded before their time.</p>

<p>She stepped into her dressing room, expecting the quiet sanctuary that followed every performance.</p>

<p>The familiar scent of perfume and the faint hum of the dressing room fan greeted her, promising a brief reprieve.</p>

<p>She sighed and began loosening her shoes, the heels feeling especially heavy after hours of performing.</p>

<p>She reached for a towel to wipe the sweat from her forehead and arms, and as she moved toward her wardrobe to change, she froze.</p>

<p>There was someone else in the room.</p>

<p>Karen’s heart skipped a beat, a mix of surprise and caution rushing through her.</p>

<p>She had expected silence, privacy—a moment to collect herself—but instead, there was an uninvited presence.</p>

<p>A shadow in the corner, a figure leaning casually against the wall, watching her with an intensity that made her pulse quicken.</p>

<p>Her mind raced, trying to identify who it could be. A colleague? A fan who had slipped past security?</p>

<p>Or... someone else entirely?</p>

<p>Karen’s breath hitched slightly, and she straightened, her hands brushing the sweat from her arms, trying to appear calm.</p>

<p>Yet beneath the surface, curiosity and a twinge of apprehension stirred.</p>

<p>&quot;Hello... who are you?&quot; Karen’s voice trembled, a mixture of fear and disbelief threading through every word.</p>

<p>She instinctively stepped back, pressing her hand against the edge of the door, ready to bolt at the slightest movement.</p>

<p>Her pulse raced as adrenaline surged through her veins—this was supposed to be her private sanctuary, a brief haven after hours of performing, yet now it felt invaded.</p>

<p>&quot;I’m a new fan of yours,&quot; came the deep, calm voice of a man.</p>

<p>The man stepped into the light, and Karen froze. Recognition struck her instantly—how could it not?</p>

<p>Ross Oakley.</p>

<p>One of the most famous men on the planet, a man whose name had been whispered in admiration and envy alike for years.</p>

<p>His presence alone commanded attention, and now he stood mere feet away, his gaze fixed on her as if he could see right through her.</p>

<p>&quot;You’re... Ross Oakley,&quot; Karen managed to say, her words caught between awe and alarm.</p>

<p>&quot;I... I’m glad you’re a fan, but... could you please—just leave my room? I’m tired, and I’d like some privacy.&quot; She tried to keep her voice steady, but there was an edge of irritation under the exhaustion.</p>

<p>The fatigue she felt was real—hours of performing, giving everything on stage—but her annoyance had other roots.</p>

<p>Her manager.</p>

<p>Of course he had to be involved; it would be foolish not to assume that he had let Ross in.</p>

<p>He knew her schedule, her routines, and no doubt thought this would be a harmless favor or a little boost to her celebrity image.</p>

<p>But Karen knew better. She knew what Ross’s reputation entailed.</p>

<p>He was a man who didn’t just notice beautiful women—he claimed them, effortlessly and without apology.</p>

<p>And Karen was not just beautiful. She was extraordinary.</p>

<p>Her natural black hair framed a face that could turn heads, her body was sculpted yet feminine, and her voice—her voice—had the power to enrapture anyone who heard it.</p>

<p>Any man, even the most disciplined, could be swayed by her combination of beauty, talent, and sheer presence.</p>

<p>And Ross Oakley, with his infamous charm and irresistible charisma, was the kind of man who could not resist temptation.</p>

<p>Karen felt a jolt of unease.</p>

<p>The thought of him here, uninvited and yet so confidently present, made her stomach tighten.</p>

<p>She shifted slightly, subtly backing toward her dressing table, as if to create a boundary between them.</p>

<p>Yet despite the fear, a flicker of curiosity crept in—a dangerous, intoxicating sense of intrigue.</p>

<p>What did he want? Why her?</p>

<p>And how much of this visit was orchestrated, and how much was him simply drawn here by his own desire?</p>

<p>Her mind raced, trying to calculate the safest course of action.</p>

<p>She could ask him to leave again, firmly this time, even risk offending one of the world’s most powerful celebrities.</p>

<p>Or she could stand her ground, using her composure, her allure, and her sharp wit to navigate the situation.</p>

<p>Karen thought, not for the first time, that it might be a good idea to find a new manager after tonight.</p>

<p>Her current one seemed to have a knack for creating &quot;opportunities&quot; that were anything but convenient.</p>

<p>Letting Ross Oakley slip into her dressing room, unannounced, was a prime example.</p>

<p>She had faced demanding schedules, intrusive reporters, and overzealous fans—but none of them had the effortless audacity that Ross carried.</p>

<p>Still, even with that in mind, she hadn’t been prepared for how direct—and unsettling—he could be.</p>

<p>&quot;It’s fine,&quot; Ross said, his voice calm, confident, and layered with a charm that seemed impossible to resist.</p>

<p>&quot;It wouldn’t be nearly as fun if you were easy to get in the first place. Have a great night, Karen. I really enjoyed watching you perform earlier.&quot;</p>
