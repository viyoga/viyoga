<p>An hour later, dinner had ended. Or more accurately, Ross had finished eating—and he had eaten like a man who had been denied for too long.</p>

<p>He didn’t just eat the meal; he consumed it with intensity, savoring every bite as though it were his last.</p>

<p>There was something carnal in the way he chewed, the way he held his fork like it was an extension of his will.</p>

<p>Jane, who had been too tense to eat at first, found herself unconsciously nibbling from her plate.</p>

<p>Watching him—how present he was in everything he did—made it impossible not to respond in some way.</p>

<p>It was unsettling.</p>

<p>Ross ate like he lived: unapologetically, confidently, and always in control.</p>

<p>And as she observed him across the flickering candlelight, Jane’s carefully constructed judgments started to shift.</p>

<p>He wasn’t attractive in the conventional sense—no sharp jawline, no charming smile that could melt a woman’s defenses.</p>

<p>But he had presence. An undeniable, almost suffocating kind of presence that filled the room without him needing to say a word.</p>

<p>His suit clung to broad shoulders and a thick chest.</p>

<p>He moved with the solid grace of someone who knew how to throw a punch and win.</p>

<p>His voice, his mannerisms, his posture—it all told a story of a man who’d taken things in life by force, not by favor.</p>

<p>He’s dangerous, she thought. And men like him always get what they want.</p>

<p>When the final course had been cleared away and the wine was nearly gone, Ross set down his glass and looked at her with full attention.</p>

<p>There was no more small talk. No more pretending.</p>

<p>&quot;I’m full now,&quot; he said, voice smooth and deep, tinged with something darker. &quot;And it’s time to digest all the food I’ve eaten.&quot;</p>

<p>He stood up, slow and deliberate, like a king rising from his throne.</p>

<p>Then he walked around the table to her side and extended his hand—not gently, not forcefully, but with the unspoken expectation that she would take it.</p>

<p>&quot;Come, Jane,&quot; he said. &quot;It’s time to make you mine.&quot;</p>

<p>His words settled over her like a net.</p>

<p>Jane hesitated.</p>

<p>Her heart beat faster.</p>

<p>Every logical thought in her mind screamed at her to run, to slap him and storm out.</p>

<p>But her body... her body was caught in the strange stillness of inevitability.</p>

<p>She lifted her eyes to meet his, and in that moment, she saw no cruelty—just certainty.</p>

<p>Ross wasn’t begging. He wasn’t pleading or bargaining.</p>

<p>He knew what was about to happen. He had known the moment she answered his call.</p>

<p>And maybe, deep down, she had too.</p>

<p>Slowly, she placed her hand in his.</p>

<p>He helped her up from the chair, his grip warm and solid.</p>

<p>The touch sent a strange shiver through her, one that had nothing to do with cold.</p>

<p>Without a word, he turned and led her through the suite.</p>

<p>The hallway to the bedroom was wide and dimly lit, lined with thick carpeting that muffled their footsteps.</p>

<p>The only sounds were the low hum of the city outside and the quiet rhythm of their breathing.</p>

<p>With every step, Jane felt the weight of what she was walking toward.</p>

<p>She wasn’t just about to sleep with a man she despised.</p>

<p>She was crossing a threshold that would change everything—her pride, her loyalty, her self-worth.</p>

<p>This wasn’t about pleasure. This was survival dressed in silk.</p>

<p>At the end of the hallway stood a dark wooden door, slightly ajar. Ross stopped just before it, turning to glance at her over his shoulder.</p>

<p>&quot;Inside this room,&quot; he said softly, &quot;miracles can happen.&quot;</p>

<p>She said nothing, but her fingers tightened slightly around his.</p>

<p>The door creaked open with a faint push, revealing a bedroom bathed in warm golden light.</p>

<p>The air was heavy with the scent of cologne and something else—something deeper, more intimate.</p>

<p>The king-sized bed dominated the center of the room, covered in rich, dark sheets.</p>

<p>A small fireplace flickered to life on the far wall, casting shadows that danced across the floor.</p>

<p>Ross stepped inside first and turned to face her.</p>

<p>She stood in the doorway, caught between two worlds: the one where she still clung to dignity... and the one where she would surrender it.</p>

<p>He held out his hand again.</p>

<p>&quot;Jane,&quot; he said, his voice lower now, softer. &quot;Come.&quot;</p>

<p>She stared at him for several long seconds, her body trembling—not from fear, but from the gravity of it all.</p>

<p>Then she stepped forward.</p>

<p>And the door shut behind her with a quiet click that felt louder than thunder.</p>

<p>&quot;I just want to make one thing clear,&quot; Jane began, her voice trembling beneath a thin layer of determination. &quot;After I do this, you should—&quot;</p>

<p>But her words were swallowed whole.</p>

<p>Ross moved with sudden purpose, closing the space between them in an instant.</p>

<p>His lips crashed onto hers, silencing everything—her doubts, her warning, her pride. His kiss wasn’t gentle.</p>

<p>It was a claim, a statement, a firestorm that swept through her senses like a violent wind.</p>

<p>&quot;Mm-mmph...!&quot; Jane’s muffled protest barely escaped her mouth as she struggled against him, her hands pressing firmly into his chest.</p>

<p>But Ross didn’t back off.</p>

<p>His large arms wrapped around her tightly, his strength undeniable.</p>

<p>She could feel the muscles under his suit flex as he lifted her clean off the ground without effort.</p>

<p>&quot;No—Ross—wait...&quot; she gasped when she could catch a breath, but her voice was weak against the intensity of his momentum.</p>

<p>He didn’t give her the space to finish.</p>

<p>She was already in his arms, cradled like a delicate thing—a possession—and before she could protest again, he laid her down on the bed.</p>

<p>The mattress dipped beneath her weight, and Ross hovered over her like a shadow she couldn’t escape.</p>

<p>His mouth found hers again before she could speak, before she could even think.</p>

<p>The kiss this time was slower, but deeper. Like he had time to savor it now.</p>

<p>Like he was tasting victory.</p>

<p>Jane struggled beneath him, her legs shifting, her hands attempting to push his chest away.</p>

<p>But he was immovable—like stone.</p>
