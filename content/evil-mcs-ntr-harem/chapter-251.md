<p>The room seemed to freeze as the men stared at the weapons, their collective breath hitching.</p>

<p>Brandon didn&#x27;t aim at any one person in particular; instead, he pointed them generally in the direction of the group, his movements as casual as if he were brushing lint off his coat.</p>

<p>And then, without warning, he fired.</p>

<p>&quot;Bang!&quot;</p>

<p>The deafening roar of the first shot shattered the tense silence. The recoil barely shifted Brandon&#x27;s steady hands, a testament to his immense strength.</p>

<p>One of the men in the line staggered back, clutching his shoulder as blood began to seep through his fingers.</p>

<p>&quot;Shit! He&#x27;s serious!&quot; someone yelled, panic seeping into their voice.</p>

<p>Brandon didn&#x27;t wait for a counterattack. He fired again, his expression unchanging beneath the demon mask.</p>

<p>&quot;Bang! Bang!&quot;</p>

<p>The bullets tore through the air, ricocheting off walls and furniture, each shot sowing chaos and confusion.</p>

<p>One man dove for cover behind a nearby couch, his weapon clattering to the floor in his haste. Another screamed as a bullet grazed his leg, sending him sprawling to the ground.</p>

<p>Despite being outnumbered, Brandon didn&#x27;t flinch. If anything, he seemed to relish the mayhem, moving with a precision that bordered on mechanical.</p>

<p>His towering figure remained rooted in place, a pillar of unshakable confidence amidst the chaos.</p>

<p>The men scrambled to respond, their own guns raised as they shouted orders and curses.</p>

<p>But their shots were hasty, fired in desperation rather than strategy. Brandon was too fast, too deliberate. Each movement he made was calculated, each shot fired with lethal intent.</p>

<p>&quot;You thought numbers would save you?&quot; Brandon growled, his voice cutting through the noise like a blade. &quot;You don&#x27;t even know what real power looks like.&quot;</p>

<p>His words only added to the rising panic among Kirito&#x27;s men, the realization dawning on them that this was no ordinary confrontation.</p>

<p>Boom!</p>

<p>Boom!</p>

<p>Boom!</p>

<p>The explosions from Brandon&#x27;s Magnums were deafening, each shot echoing like a thunderclap in the enclosed room.</p>

<p>The muzzle flashes briefly illuminated the carnage as Brandon executed his work with chilling precision.</p>

<p>His aim was terrifyingly accurate—headshots, every time.</p>

<p>Poof!</p>

<p>One man&#x27;s head burst apart, the grotesque splatter painting the wall behind him in a crimson spray. His body collapsed instantly, the impact shaking the floor.</p>

<p>Another man attempted to retaliate, raising his weapon with trembling hands, but he was too slow.</p>

<p>Bang!</p>

<p>A bullet pierced his skull, cutting his scream short. His eyes rolled back as he fell like a marionette with its strings abruptly severed.</p>

<p>Brandon moved through the chaos with an eerie calm, his sheer size and deliberate movements giving him the presence of an unstoppable juggernaut.</p>

<p>Bullets struck him—one hit his shoulder, another grazed his side—but he barely flinched. His blood seeped through his shirt, but it only served to make him look more monstrous.</p>

<p>The remaining men fired wildly in desperation, their bullets ricocheting off the walls and furniture, but Brandon pressed forward, relentless.</p>

<p>&quot;You&#x27;re wasting your time,&quot; he growled, his voice low and resonant, cutting through the cacophony of gunfire.</p>

<p>In less than a minute, the ten men were dead.</p>

<p>The room fell silent, save for the faint hiss of smoke curling from the barrels of Brandon&#x27;s Magnums. The bodies of Kirito&#x27;s men lay strewn across the floor in grotesque poses, their heads obliterated.</p>

<p>Blood pooled around them, soaking into the expensive carpet.</p>

<p>Brandon straightened, his hulking frame casting a shadow over the carnage. His demon mask, though damaged, still clung to his face, adding an air of nightmarish terror to his already imposing presence.</p>

<p>He turned his attention to the five men near Kirito, his piercing gaze freezing them in place.</p>

<p>Kirito&#x27;s voice cut through their panic like a whip.</p>

<p>&quot;He&#x27;s wearing a bulletproof vest! Aim for the head!&quot;</p>

<p>One of the men, a seasoned shooter, reacted immediately. His gun was up in a fraction of a second, and he fired a single, precise shot.</p>

<p>Bang!</p>

<p>The bullet struck true, hitting Brandon&#x27;s mask square in the forehead. The impact left a jagged hole in the grotesque visage, revealing part of Brandon&#x27;s pale, unflinching face beneath.</p>

<p>The shooter hesitated, disbelief flashing across his features.</p>

<p>Brandon didn&#x27;t even stagger. He simply tilted his head, his movements unnervingly slow, and stared at the man who had dared to shoot him.</p>

<p>&quot;You&#x27;re good,&quot; Brandon said, his voice as cold and steady as ever. &quot;But not good enough.&quot;</p>

<p>With a measured motion, he raised one of his Magnums.</p>

<p>&quot;Nooooo!&quot;</p>

<p>The shooter dove to the side, hoping to evade, but Brandon&#x27;s finger had already pulled the trigger.</p>

<p>Bang!</p>

<p>The bullet struck mid-dive, tearing through the man&#x27;s skull. His lifeless body continued its momentum, tumbling into a roll that ended behind a sofa.</p>

<p>The grotesque irony of the perfect defensive maneuver performed by a dead man was not lost on anyone.</p>

<p>Thud!</p>

<p>The remaining four men stared in horror, their weapons trembling in their hands.</p>

<p>Brandon&#x27;s voice broke the silence, low and guttural. &quot;Anyone else?&quot;</p>

<p>The men dropped their guns in unison, their survival instincts overriding their loyalty to Kirito.</p>

<p>&quot;We… we surrender!&quot; one of them stammered, raising his hands high.</p>

<p>&quot;Please, don&#x27;t kill us!&quot; another begged, his voice cracking.</p>

<p>Brandon&#x27;s head tilted slightly as he regarded them, the faintest hint of amusement in his demeanor.</p>

<p>Then, slowly, he turned to Ross, who sat comfortably in his chair, watching the scene unfold as if it were a theatrical performance.</p>

<p>Ross met Brandon&#x27;s gaze and gave a slight shake of his head.</p>

<p>Brandon&#x27;s lips curled into a faint smirk beneath the remnants of his mask. &quot;Understood.&quot;</p>

<p>Bang!</p>

<p>Bang!</p>

<p>Bang!</p>

<p>Bang!</p>

<p>Four more shots rang out in quick succession. Each one was precise, leaving no room for error. The men dropped one by one, their bodies crumpling to the floor, lifeless.</p>

<p>Now, only Kirito remained.</p>

<p>He stood amidst the carnage, his face pale but resolute. His men—all fifteen of them—lay dead around him, their bodies marking the catastrophic failure of his mission.</p>

<p>Brandon slowly turned his attention to Kirito, his Magnums still smoking.</p>

<p>&quot;Looks like it&#x27;s just you now.&quot;</p>

<p>Kirito clenched his fists, his eyes burning with a mix of fury and desperation.</p>

<p>&quot;You think this is over?&quot; he spat, his voice trembling.</p>

<p>&quot;You&#x27;ll regret this.&quot;</p>

<p>Ross chuckled softly from his seat, leaning back as if enjoying the show. &quot;Oh, Kirito. I think the only one regretting anything tonight… is you.&quot;</p>
