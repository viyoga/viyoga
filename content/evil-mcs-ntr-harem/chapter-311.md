<p>&quot;It’s simple. We don’t just gamble with money—we gamble with lives. How about it?&quot; Don Adams’s voice carried a venomous edge as he leaned back in his chair, a lewd smirk on his face.</p>

<p>&quot;I fancy your women, and I’d love to add them to my collection,&quot; he added, his tone dripping with arrogance.</p>

<p>Unbeknownst to Mr. Adams, those words were the equivalent of signing his own death certificate.</p>

<p>His ignorance of the storm brewing in Ross’s calm demeanor would be his undoing.</p>

<p>His eyes swept over the group of women flanking Ross, pausing particularly on Althea.</p>

<p>She wasn’t just beautiful; she was a global icon—a star adored by millions. The idea of claiming her for himself made Don’s grin widen.</p>

<p>But he didn’t stop there. His gaze moved hungrily to the others, appreciating each woman with an unsettling intensity.</p>

<p>Don didn’t discriminate; he appreciated beauty in all its forms.</p>

<p>And to him, Ross was a connoisseur, a man with an unparalleled ability to select the finest treasures.</p>

<p>&quot;You’ve got excellent taste, Ross,&quot; he chuckled, his words laced with both admiration and mockery.</p>

<p>Ross remained unbothered, his smile steady but unreadable.</p>

<p>&quot;It’s simple enough indeed,&quot; he replied, his voice calm yet carrying an undertone that made Maya tense.</p>

<p>&quot;What if I agree to this gamble? You get all of my women if you win. But if I win… what will you give me in return?&quot;</p>

<p>The room fell silent for a moment. Some of the women gasped audibly, their shock evident.</p>

<p>Ross’s words were like a slap to their faces. How could he even consider such a preposterous wager?</p>

<p>Their eyes darted to him, searching for answers in his calm expression.</p>

<p>But Maya… Maya knew better. She had seen that smile before.</p>

<p>It was the same smile Ross wore when he had crushed those who dared to challenge him, the same smile he had when someone had sealed their own fate.</p>

<p>Maya remembered the hotel basement massacre and she could not help but shiver.</p>

<p>She glanced around the room, noting the lewd grins and lascivious stares of the men surrounding her.</p>

<p>Their expressions told her everything she needed to know about their intentions.</p>

<p>She felt no sympathy for them. Not one bit.</p>

<p>If these men died tonight—and she had no doubt they would—she wouldn’t shed a tear.</p>

<p>They were vile, dangerous, and consumed by their own greed. Yet, a small part of her felt conflicted.</p>

<p>When had she become so desensitized to death?</p>

<p>Was she happy to see these men meet their end, or was it the cruelty within her that scared her most?</p>

<p>Maya was not alone in this sentiment since Gwen also recalled the details of that massacre.</p>

<p>She has no doubt at all in her mind that Ross was the one who did it.</p>

<p>More so that Ross admitted to the crime himself.</p>

<p>As Don leaned forward, his overconfidence evident, Gwen’s stomach churned.</p>

<p>She could almost feel the weight of the decisions Ross was making.</p>

<p>She didn’t know what Ross had in mind, but one thing was clear: tonight would not end without bloodshed.</p>

<p>&quot;Speak your terms. I’ll accept them all,&quot; Mr. Adams said with a confident smile, reclining in his chair as though victory was already assured.</p>

<p>&quot;Anything?&quot; Ross raised a brow, his tone sharp yet casual, as if testing the man’s resolve.</p>

<p>&quot;Anything,&quot; Mr. Adams confirmed with a smug nod, unaware of the storm he was inviting.</p>

<p>&quot;Okay,&quot; Ross said, a small, almost imperceptible smile tugging at the corners of his lips.</p>

<p>He then turned to the women at his side, his voice taking on a softer, reassuring tone.</p>

<p>&quot;Ladies, could you go with Freddie for an hour? Have a drink, relax, and let me finish this quickly. I promise I’ll join you as soon as it’s over.&quot; His words carried a veneer of charm, but beneath it was a calculated intent.</p>

<p>Some of his women didn’t fully understand who he was—or what he was capable of—and there was no reason for them to see that side of him just yet.</p>

<p>&quot;Nope, we’re staying!&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;I’m not leaving without you, Ross!&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;I don’t trust these people!&quot;</p>

<p>The women protested at first, their loyalty and concern evident.</p>

<p>The room buzzed with tension, but Ross was a man who knew how to handle his own.</p>

<p>With a mix of charm, authority, and reassurance, he managed to persuade most of them.</p>

<p>Less than five minutes later, Freddie escorted the majority of the women out of the room, leaving it significantly quieter.</p>

<p>But not everyone left. Maya, Gwen, and Althea remained.</p>

<p>These three knew the truth about Ross—the man behind the charming smile and the lavish lifestyle.</p>

<p>They stayed not out of fear but because they understood his capabilities.</p>

<p>They knew there was nothing in this room that could intimidate Ross Oakley.</p>

<p>Ross turned back to Mr. Adams, his expression shifting subtly.</p>

<p>The smile remained, but there was something cold behind it now.</p>

<p>A monster’s calm.</p>

<p>&quot;Well then,&quot; Ross began, his tone steady, almost conversational.</p>

<p>&quot;If I win, I want just one thing.&quot; He leaned forward slightly, his gaze locking with Mr. Adams.</p>

<p>&quot;I want your life.&quot;</p>

<p>The room froze. For a moment, the air was thick with shock.</p>

<p>Then, as if on cue, several of Adams’s men reached for their guns, their movements tense and jittery.</p>

<p>&quot;Gentlemen,&quot; Ross said coolly, raising a hand as if he were addressing unruly children.</p>

<p>&quot;There’s no need to be impulsive. No need for violence—or guns.&quot; He leaned back, completely at ease despite the loaded weapons aimed in his direction.</p>

<p>&quot;After all, Mr. Adams did say he’d accept anything.&quot;</p>

<p>Mr. Adams hesitated, but only briefly. He waved a hand, motioning for his men to lower their weapons.</p>

<p>&quot;No need to overreact,&quot; he said, forcing a laugh that sounded more nervous than he intended.</p>

<p>&quot;Since this is Mr. Oakley’s wish, then so be it. Let’s play.&quot;</p>

<p>She was breathtaking, and the thought of having her as part of his collection made his excitement nearly unbearable.</p>
