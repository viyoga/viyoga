<p>&quot;This isn&#x27;t something to joke about, Ross,&quot; Emma said sharply, her tone cutting through the low hum of chatter in the place.</p>

<p>She turned away and sat back down, her hands trembling slightly as she rested them on the table.</p>

<p>Her voice was quiet but laced with anger, barely above a whisper.</p>

<p>Even so, she cast a quick glance around, her eyes darting nervously from one table to the next.</p>

<p>To her relief, no one seemed to notice, their attention still focused on their own conversations.</p>

<p>&quot;I&#x27;m not joking,&quot; Ross said, leaning back in his chair with a smug grin.</p>

<p>His voice was calm, almost lazy, as if he were discussing something as trivial as the weather.</p>

<p>&quot;I can heal her. Right here, right now.&quot; He paused, letting his words sink in, his piercing gaze locking onto Emma&#x27;s.</p>

<p>Then, he tilted his head slightly, his expression shifting into something darker, something more calculating.</p>

<p>&quot;But the real question is, what are you willing to give in exchange for this gift of healing?&quot;</p>

<p>Emma stiffened, her hands clenching into fists on the table. She met his gaze, her eyes burning with a mix of desperation and determination.</p>

<p>&quot;Anything,&quot; she said, her voice a low but firm whisper.</p>

<p>&quot;As long as my baby is fine, I&#x27;ll agree to anything you want.&quot;</p>

<p>Ross&#x27;s grin widened, his satisfaction unmistakable. He leaned forward slightly, his eyes never leaving hers.</p>

<p>&quot;Good,&quot; he said softly.</p>

<p>&quot;Then we have a deal.&quot;</p>

<p>Before Emma could respond, her phone buzzed loudly on the table, breaking the tense silence between them.</p>

<p>She snatched it up, her heart pounding as she answered the call.</p>

<p>As she listened, her face lit up with a mix of disbelief and overwhelming relief.</p>

<p>Without another word, she shot to her feet, practically stumbling in her haste to leave.</p>

<p>Ross watched her go, his amusement growing with every hurried step she took.</p>

<p>She was so caught up in her excitement that she didn&#x27;t even look back, let alone remember the terms of their deal.</p>

<p>With a shrug, Ross reached into his pocket and pulled out a wad of cash, tossing a hefty thousand-dollar tip onto the table.</p>

<p>As he stood and adjusted his jacket, he let out a low chuckle, his lips curling into a satisfied smirk.</p>

<p>&quot;Emma Lewis,&quot; he muttered under his breath, heading for the door.</p>

<p>&quot;What a fine, fine woman you are.&quot;</p>

<p>He pushed the door open and stepped outside, whistling a jaunty tune as he walked away, the thrill of the encounter lingering in his mind like a sweet, intoxicating aftertaste.</p>

<p>&quot;Miss Lewis, it&#x27;s a miracle! I&#x27;ve never seen anything like this. Erica is healthy now—healthier than any child I&#x27;ve ever examined,&quot; the doctor exclaimed, his voice brimming with disbelief and awe.</p>

<p>Emma stared at her daughter, who now sat upright on the hospital bed, giggling and playing with a Barbie doll.</p>

<p>The transformation was staggering. Just yesterday, Erica had been frail, barely clinging to life, her body ravaged by Tay-Sachs disease.</p>

<p>Now, she looked like any lively five-year-old, her cheeks rosy and her laughter filling the room.</p>

<p>Emma&#x27;s heart swelled with joy, but an undercurrent of doubt gnawed at her. She clenched her fists and forced herself to speak.</p>

<p>The doctor hesitated for a moment before nodding.</p>

<p>&quot;We do, but there&#x27;s something strange about it. There was a one-second blackout in the recording. When the feed resumed, Erica&#x27;s condition had changed drastically. You can see it for yourself.&quot;</p>

<p>He pulled out his phone and tapped a few buttons, handing the screen to Emma.</p>

<p>She watched intently as the footage played. Erica&#x27;s tiny, motionless form lay on the hospital bed, her breathing shallow.</p>

<p>Then, the screen went dark for the briefest moment—barely a single breath.</p>

<p>When the feed returned, Erica was sitting up, smiling, and reaching out for her toys.</p>

<p>Emma&#x27;s fingers tightened around the phone as she struggled to process what she was seeing.</p>

<p>It was her daughter—there was no doubt about that—but the sudden, inexplicable transformation left her reeling.</p>

<p>Was it truly a miracle? Or was this Ross meddling with powers beyond her understanding?</p>

<p>&quot;Thank you, Doctor,&quot; Emma said quietly, handing the phone back to him. Her mind raced, trying to make sense of the impossible.</p>

<p>The doctor hesitated, his expression turning serious.</p>

<p>&quot;Miss Lewis, what happened to Erica is unprecedented. If you&#x27;re willing, we could study her case further. Her blood or genes might hold the key to helping other children suffering from Tay-Sachs disease. This could save lives.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;No,&quot; Emma replied, her voice sharper than she intended. She stood abruptly, brushing past him to reach her daughter.</p>

<p>&quot;I don&#x27;t want to disclose anything. You can leave now, Doctor. My daughter and I will be checking out this afternoon.&quot;</p>

<p>The doctor opened his mouth to protest but stopped at the resolute look in Emma&#x27;s eyes. With a reluctant nod, he left the room.</p>

<p>Emma turned back to Erica, scooping her into her arms. Her daughter giggled, wrapping her tiny arms around Emma&#x27;s neck.</p>

<p>&quot;Momma,&quot; Erica said clearly, mimicking the voice of her Barbie doll.</p>

<p>Emma&#x27;s throat tightened, and she kissed her daughter&#x27;s soft, chubby cheeks.</p>

<p>&quot;Let&#x27;s go home,&quot; she whispered.</p>

<p>That afternoon, Emma discharged Erica from the hospital. She spent the next week at home, cherishing every moment with her daughter.</p>

<p>The laughter, the hugs, and the endless questions from the curious five-year-old filled her days.</p>

<p>Yet, in the quiet moments, her thoughts kept returning to Ross.</p>

<p>On the weekend, after putting Erica to bed, Emma finally picked up her phone and dialed his number.</p>

<p>&quot;I&#x27;ll see you at the Autumn Palms Hotel at 7 tonight,&quot; she said tersely, her voice steady despite the turmoil inside her.</p>

<p>Before Ross could respond, she hung up, clutching the phone tightly in her hand.</p>

<p>She exhaled slowly, her gaze shifting to the sleeping form of her daughter.</p>

<p>Whatever Ross wanted, she would face it.</p>

<p>Her baby was healthy now, and for that, she would pay whatever price was necessary.</p>
