<p>Capítulo 1116: Chapter 1116 Sweater</p>

<p>Ross’s entire body convulsed.</p>

<p>He lurched forward and spat out a second mouthful of thick, dark blood.</p>

<p>It splattered across the floor with a wet slap, mixing with the first pool and spreading outward in a crimson smear.</p>

<p>Gasps and cries erupted from the women.</p>

<p>“ROSS!!”</p>

<p>“Stop, you’re going to kill yourself!”</p>

<p>But before anyone could grab him, Ross’s eyes rolled slightly, and he collapsed.</p>

<p>His knees buckled, and his body swayed like a falling tree.</p>

<p>This time he didn’t even try to catch himself.</p>

<p>He dropped.</p>

<p>Several pairs of hands lunged forward and caught him just before he hit the ground.</p>

<p>Giana held his left arm, Miku caught his shoulder, and even Libby—still trembling from the healing—reached forward with wide, grateful eyes.</p>

<p>Ross lay limp in their arms, head tilted back, breaths shallow.</p>

<p>His face was almost paper-white, glowing faintly under the dim basement lighting.</p>

<p>A fresh trail of blood trickled from the corner of his mouth.</p>

<p>Yet, somehow, he managed a smile.</p>

<p>A soft, exhausted, heroic smile.</p>

<p>“I-I’m… alright…”</p>

<p>His voice cracked at the end, making the girls panic even more.</p>

<p>Before anyone could speak, Libby pushed herself up from the sofa, her breath catching.</p>

<p>She touched her stomach frantically… then slowly lifted her shirt just enough to reveal smooth, flawless skin.</p>

<p>Her eyes widened.</p>

<p>“The wound… it’s gone!” she shouted, disbelief and relief mixing in her voice.</p>

<p>“Completely gone! I-I feel… I feel healthier than ever!”</p>

<p>The other girls stared in shock before their expressions melted into pure awe.</p>

<p>Then Libby’s eyes filled with tears.</p>

<p>“Ross… thank you. Thank you so, so much!” she cried.</p>

<p>She threw herself into the group hug around him, nearly choking him with gratitude.</p>

<p>The others followed, encircling Ross in a tight ring of warmth, softness, and emotion.</p>

<p>Their whispers of “thank you,” “you saved her life,” and “you’re incredible,” echoed around him.</p>

<p>Ross lifted his head weakly.</p>

<p>“I’m… happy to help…” he whispered.</p>

<p>He made a shaky attempt to stand, pushing off the floor.</p>

<p>His legs trembled dramatically—far more than necessary—and after rising only a few inches, he let his body sway dangerously to the side.</p>

<p>And collapse again.</p>

<p>This time the women caught him instantly.</p>

<p>Except they didn’t just catch him—they smothered him.</p>

<p>Ross fell face-first into a soft, warm cushion of breasts.</p>

<p>A lot of breasts.</p>

<p>Arms wrapped around him from every direction. Two pairs hugged his chest.</p>

<p>Another pressed his cheek into a very generous cleavage.</p>

<p>The world around him became nothing but soft warmth, feminine scent, and the overwhelming sensation of being completely enveloped.</p>

<p>From the outside, it looked like they were desperately trying to support a dying hero.</p>

<p>From Ross’s perspective…</p>

<p>He had ascended to heaven.</p>

<p>“Ahhhhh…” he thought blissfully, nearly purring internally.</p>

<p>“This… this is paradise. Nothing beats being surrounded by huge, soft, perfect boobs…”</p>

<p>Miku pressed closer, worried he might faint. “Ross, stay with us! Don’t pass out!”</p>

<p>Another girl hugged him tighter from behind. “He’s so cold—keep him warm!”</p>

<p>Libby held onto his arm with both of her hands, tears still streaming.</p>

<p>“You saved my life… I don’t know how to thank you…”</p>

<p>Ross’s mind was overjoyed.</p>

<p>Please thank me. All of you. Thoroughly.</p>

<p>But outwardly, he kept his expression half-conscious, eyes half-lidded, lips trembling just slightly.</p>

<p>He continued his act with the dedication of a lifetime Oscar winner.</p>

<p>Every wobble, every shallow breath, every weak smile—perfectly timed.</p>

<p>And the girls responded exactly as he hoped:</p>

<p>Holding him tighter.</p>

<p>Pressing closer.</p>

<p>Surrounding him from every angle like he was the most precious thing in the world.</p>

<p>He let out a soft exhale, both dramatic and satisfied.</p>

<p>Time to milk this just a little more, he thought gleefully as soft chests pressed all around him, cushioning him like divine pillows.</p>

<p>And Ross continued his performance without the slightest crack in character.</p>

<p>Ten full minutes passed before movement stirred from the warmth of the “boobie mountain” Ross had been buried in.</p>

<p>The girls had kept him pinned between their arms and chests, refusing to let him collapse further.</p>

<p>Their worry hung in the air so thickly that even breathing sounded tense.</p>

<p>Then Ross exhaled slowly and lifted his head.</p>

<p>The women instantly straightened, eyes widening, their arms still around him like a net ready to catch him again.</p>

<p>Ross pushed gently against the softness surrounding him and rose from the sea of concerned boobies.</p>

<p>As he stood, several of them instinctively reached out, afraid he might faint again.</p>

<p>Ross smiled at them—weak, pale, but undeniably charming.</p>

<p>“I’m okay now,” he assured, brushing his messy hair back with a shaky hand.</p>

<p>His voice still carried the exhaustion of someone who had pushed his life force too far.</p>

<p>“Really… I’m fine. Thank you for worrying.”</p>

<p>The girls all sighed in relief, though their eyes still scanned him for any sign of pain.</p>

<p>Ross stretched lightly.</p>

<p>“Now,” he continued, “why don’t all of you eat? The soup’s already gotten cold. I’ll heat it up.”</p>

<p>Their expressions tightened again—concerned, protective, doubtful.</p>

<p>His face was still pale. Too pale.</p>

<p>His steps still slightly unsteady.</p>

<p>But Ross walked toward the big iron pot anyway, keeping his posture heroic yet fragile.</p>

<p>Before he could touch the lid—</p>

<p>“I’ll do that, Ross.”</p>

<p>Ace cut in firmly, stepping forward like a soldier ready to take orders.</p>

<p>“You should sit down and rest. Let me handle it.”</p>

<p>Ross blinked once, as if considering it, then gave a reluctant nod—perfectly playing the role of the weakened savior.</p>

<p>Ace rekindled the embers beneath the pot, stirred the soup, and reheated it while the delicious aroma slowly spread through the room.</p>

<p>The five newcomers watched hungrily, their stomachs growling audibly.</p>

<p>A few minutes later, steaming bowls were handed out.</p>

<p>The instant the soup touched their lips—</p>

<p>“WHAT?!”</p>

<p>“This is incredible!”</p>

<p>“This is the best meal I’ve ever had in my entire life!”</p>

<p>“It melts… it literally melts in my mouth!”</p>

<p>The newcomers were wide-eyed, overwhelmed, devouring spoonful after spoonful like starving beasts.</p>

<p>Their reactions were so dramatic that Ace and Miku exchanged a knowing grin.</p>
