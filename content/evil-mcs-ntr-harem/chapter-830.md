<p>Whoever had picked up didn’t speak, perhaps wondering who was calling from an unfamiliar number.</p>

<p>Ross didn’t let the pause last long.</p>

<p>His voice was calm, deliberate, yet edged with enough steel to make even the bystanders shift uncomfortably.</p>

<p>&quot;Don Carlo, this is Ross Oakley,&quot; he began, every syllable rolling off his tongue like the opening move of a chess master.</p>

<p>&quot;I have your son here, blocking my way and making trouble. I want a final fix to this problem. If I’m not satisfied with the ending, then I’ll have to visit your house in person. I know we haven’t had the pleasure of meeting, so I’m looking forward to your failure.&quot;</p>

<p>The street seemed to shrink around them as the words hung in the cold night air.</p>

<p>Even Kenny’s cocky grin faltered just a little, though he tried to cover it up with a scoff.</p>

<p>The men standing behind him exchanged nervous glances, as if suddenly aware they were standing in the wrong place at the wrong time.</p>

<p>A beat of silence followed before a deep, weathered voice spoke from the other side of the line.</p>

<p>&quot;Understood. Sorry for this inconvenience. I’ll fix it.&quot;</p>

<p>The call ended without another word, and the atmosphere shifted immediately.</p>

<p>Not five minutes later, the distant growl of engines filled the street.</p>

<p>Several black luxury sedans rounded the corner in a tight formation, their headlights slicing through the darkness.</p>

<p>Doors opened before the cars even came to a full stop, and men in dark suits moved with silent, precise coordination.</p>

<p>They didn’t ask questions. They didn’t hesitate.</p>

<p>Kenny was seized by the arm and nearly lifted off his feet as they dragged him toward one of the cars.</p>

<p>His goons were dealt with just as swiftly, their protests ignored.</p>

<p>Tires squealed against asphalt as the convoy prepared to leave.</p>

<p>&quot;Hey, fuckheads! Be careful! Do you know who I am?!&quot; Kenny shouted, his voice cracking between outrage and panic.</p>

<p>But no one acknowledged him—not even his own men.</p>

<p>His words were swallowed by the roar of burning rubber as the cars vanished into the night, leaving nothing but the faint stench of exhaust in the air.</p>

<p>Ross pocketed his phone and adjusted his jacket like nothing had happened, his expression unreadable.</p>

<p>To him, it was just another minor inconvenience handled.</p>

<p>To everyone else watching, it was a reminder—Ross Oakley didn’t just win fights.</p>

<p>He ended them without lifting a finger.</p>

<p>Ross looked at Ella with a slow, confident smile—one that made her heart race and her body ache with anticipation.</p>

<p>&quot;Come, my dear Ella. Let’s continue with some more fucking... at your home.&quot;</p>

<p>He took her hand gently, yet with undeniable authority, guiding her down the quiet street toward his car.</p>

<p>The night was far from over.</p>

<p>They would retreat to the privacy of Ella’s luxurious home, a place where passion could ignite without restraint, away from prying eyes.</p>

<p>In that sanctuary, Mario—the weak, broken husband—would have no choice but to watch, powerless and defeated.</p>

<p>His silent tears of jealousy and regret would be the only soundtrack to their intimate conquest.</p>

<p>Ross’s grin deepened as he slid into the driver’s seat.</p>

<p>He was eager for the pleasures still to come, ready to claim every inch of the beautiful woman beside him.</p>

<p>But while Ross savored the night, far away in a dark, cold chamber, Kenny’s world was unraveling in a very different way.</p>

<p>Naked, battered, and trembling, Kenny knelt before his father—a towering figure consumed by wrath.</p>

<p>&quot;Father, please... stop!&quot; Kenny’s voice cracked with desperation, mingled with shame and pain.</p>

<p>But his pleas fell on deaf ears.</p>

<p>The elder man’s eyes blazed with fury, his grip tight on the leather whip that cracked through the stale air with savage precision.</p>

<p>Pak!</p>

<p>Pak!</p>

<p>Pak!</p>

<p>Each strike ripped mercilessly into Kenny’s skin, leaving deep welts that bled dark, sticky blood.</p>

<p>His handsome face was streaked with tears—tears born from pain, humiliation, and a crushing sense of failure.</p>

<p>The room was small, the shadows long and cold, illuminated only by the flickering light of a lone candle.</p>

<p>The harsh sounds of the whip echoed, mingling with Kenny’s ragged sobs, creating a symphony of torment.</p>

<p>His father’s voice was low, cold, and filled with icy disdain.</p>

<p>&quot;You disgrace the family, Kenny. Weakness like yours has no place here. Not only that, you’re even so stupid! I can’t believe that you’re my son at all!&quot;</p>

<p>Kenny’s body shook with each lash, but his spirit cracked the most.</p>

<p>The weight of his father’s wrath crushed him far deeper than the whip ever could.</p>

<p>The torture continued, each lash and strike echoing through the cold, dimly lit chamber until Kenny’s hoarse screams dwindled into pitiful whimpers.</p>

<p>The air was thick with the stench of blood and sweat.</p>

<p>Only Kenny’s screams decorated the room.</p>

<p>Eventually, even that fell silent.</p>

<p>His voice had broken, his throat too raw to cry out, leaving only the faint, ragged sound of his breathing—weak, uneven, and fading.</p>

<p>The men exchanged grim looks but did not stop; their orders were clear.</p>

<p>By the time the last echo died, the oppressive quiet that followed felt heavier than the screams had ever been.</p>

<p>Kenny’s body was a ruin—bruises swelling like dark blooms across his skin, deep welts from the whip cutting into flesh, and blood trickling down in thin, pitiful streams.</p>

<p>Yet somehow, his mind clung to consciousness, his eyes glassy but still defiant in the dim light of the room.</p>

<p>His lips trembled as he spoke, the words barely a whisper.</p>

<p>&quot;Why...?&quot;</p>

<p>For a long moment, Don Carlo didn’t answer.</p>

<p>The old man simply stood there, looming like a shadow carved from stone, his hands clasped behind his back as he paced slowly before his broken son.</p>

<p>His polished shoes clicked against the marble floor with cold precision.</p>

<p>Finally, he stopped and fixed Kenny with a glare so sharp it seemed to slice through the air.</p>

<p>&quot;I told you,&quot; the Don’s voice was low, each word bitten off with controlled fury.</p>
