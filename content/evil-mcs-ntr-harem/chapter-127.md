<p>&quot;I have a surprise for you. Open your bank account and see.&quot;</p>

<p>Frowning, she tilted her head, puzzled by the cryptic message.</p>

<p>Still, she followed his instructions, navigating to her bank&#x27;s app with a mix of curiosity and skepticism.</p>

<p>But when her account balance loaded, her breath hitched. Her hand flew to her mouth as her eyes widened in sheer disbelief.</p>

<p>There it was—a fresh deposit of $100 million.</p>

<p>Her heart pounded in her chest, the absurdity of the number making her lightheaded.</p>

<p>She blinked, shook her head, and stared again, hoping she was hallucinating.</p>

<p>But the figure didn&#x27;t change. One hundred million dollars. No more, no less.</p>

<p>April&#x27;s mind raced. The amount was staggering, almost incomprehensible.</p>

<p>It was more money than she had ever imagined in her wildest dreams.</p>

<p>For a moment, she just sat there, frozen, her phone trembling in her hands as the enormity of what Ross had done sank in.</p>

<p>Finally, her fingers moved, typing out a reply with trembling urgency.</p>

<p>&quot;I can&#x27;t accept this.&quot;</p>

<p>The response came almost immediately, as if Ross had been waiting for her message.</p>

<p>&quot;Of course you can. If you won&#x27;t, then throw it away. I don&#x27;t take back what I&#x27;ve already given.&quot;</p>

<p>Her breath hitched again as she read his reply. Her emotions surged, and tears began to well up in her eyes.</p>

<p>A soft sob escaped her lips, and she quickly wiped it away, glancing around to make sure no one was watching.</p>

<p>How could she possibly comprehend this?</p>

<p>Just months ago, she had seen Ross as an arrogant playboy, a self-indulgent monster who thrived on flaunting his wealth and charm.</p>

<p>And to think, just two weeks ago, he had been a monster who had taken everything from her.</p>

<p>But now… now, he was something else entirely.</p>

<p>A benefactor?</p>

<p>A savior?</p>

<p>No word seemed fitting enough to describe what he had just done for her.</p>

<p>With this money, April realized, she could transform lives.</p>

<p>Her parents, who had struggled for years to make ends meet, could finally have a home of their own—no more renting, no more uncertainty.</p>

<p>She could create scholarships for underprivileged students, giving them a chance to escape the cycle of poverty and chase their dreams.</p>

<p>She could make a real difference, not just as the president of the Supreme Student Council, but as someone who had the power to change lives for the better.</p>

<p>To leave this money untouched in her account would be a waste, a betrayal of her principles.</p>

<p>She took a deep, steadying breath, wiping away her tears as she composed her next message.</p>

<p>&quot;Come see me at lunch break. I also have a surprise for you.&quot;</p>

<p>Hitting send, she leaned back in her chair, her mind already racing with plans.</p>

<p>Whatever it took, she would find a way to repay Ross&#x27;s kindness—not just in words, but in actions.</p>

<p>For the first time in her life, she felt the weight of immense opportunity and responsibility resting on her shoulders.</p>

<p>As the minutes ticked by, her resolve hardened.</p>

<p>This wasn&#x27;t just a gift—it was a turning point.</p>

<p>And she would make sure that every cent of Ross&#x27;s generosity counted, not just for herself, but for everyone who needed a second chance at life.</p>

<p>Lunch couldn&#x27;t come fast enough.</p>

<p>A few hours passed, and lunchtime arrived.</p>

<p>&quot;April, someone&#x27;s looking for you,&quot; one of her friends said, nudging her toward the door.</p>

<p>Curious, she turned her gaze—and there he was.</p>

<p>Ross Oakley.</p>

<p>A man April could no longer define in simple terms of black and white.</p>

<p>He stood at the doorway, clad in his usual white shirt and black jeans.</p>

<p>The clothes fit snugly, highlighting the subtle yet noticeable transformation in his physique.</p>

<p>In just two weeks, he had put on muscle, shedding the thin, almost frail appearance he had sported months ago.</p>

<p>While his face might still be considered average by conventional standards, there was something magnetic about him now.</p>

<p>It wasn&#x27;t his features that drew attention—it was the unshakable confidence in his eyes, the commanding stance that spoke of someone who knew exactly what he wanted and how to get it.</p>

<p>This was no longer the Ross of the past.</p>

<p>This was a strong, willful man, radiating an aura of determination that made him impossible to ignore.</p>

<p>April thanked her friend with a polite nod and immediately stood up to meet Ross by the door.</p>

<p>&quot;A surprise?&quot; Ross asked, his lips curling into a mischievous smile, the sparkle in his eyes making her heart race.</p>

<p>&quot;Yes. Come on, lover boy,&quot; April teased, her voice soft and inviting, a playful grin lighting up her face.</p>

<p>She had quickly learned how to handle Ross&#x27;s unpredictable personality.</p>

<p>Despite their brief time together, she could already see through much of his exterior.</p>

<p>Yes, he was undeniably lewd, maybe even obsessed with sex, and there was no doubt he was ruthless in his pursuit of what he wanted.</p>

<p>But beneath it all, he had an unspoken code—he was a man of passion, not violence.</p>

<p>He never hurt his women, never raised a hand to them.</p>

<p>Instead, he overwhelmed them with pleasure, lifting them to euphoric heights most could only dream of.</p>

<p>Ross wasn&#x27;t perfect, far from it, but April found herself appreciating the nuances of his personality.</p>

<p>He was strong and capable, exuding an aura of confidence that made him difficult to resist.</p>

<p>In truth, he wasn&#x27;t just a man to be tolerated—he was a man who was dangerously easy to love.</p>

<p>As they walked across campus, Ross casually slung his arm over April&#x27;s shoulders, his confidence unmistakable.</p>

<p>His touch was possessive but not forceful, a subtle declaration that she was his.</p>

<p>April didn&#x27;t pull away.</p>

<p>She had grown used to their closeness, even relished it in a way she wouldn&#x27;t openly admit.</p>

<p>Their casual display of affection didn&#x27;t go unnoticed. Whispers erupted around them like wildfire.</p>

<p>Groups of students paused mid-conversation to turn and stare, their eyes following Ross and April&#x27;s every step.</p>

<p>Ross, ever the troublemaker, couldn&#x27;t resist being naughty.</p>

<p>With a sly grin, Ross cupped April&#x27;s ample bosom, giving her a firm squeeze that was as intimate as it was bold, completely unfazed by the shocked students who stared in disbelief at his actions.</p>
