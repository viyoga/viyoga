<p>Ross tilted his head slightly, studying her more closely.</p>

<p>The corners of her eyes were damp, the faint glimmer of tears threatening to spill, yet she refused to give herself fully over to despair.</p>

<p>There was a fire in her, quiet but undeniable—a persistence that intrigued him.</p>

<p>&quot;I guess it’s time to make her mine... again,&quot; he muttered under his breath, his voice low and laced with amusement.</p>

<p>There was a certain thrill in it—the anticipation of conquest, not merely of the body, but of the mind and heart.</p>

<p>He enjoyed the game, the tension, the subtle dance of power and persuasion.</p>

<p>&quot;It’s funny how it’s going to be like I’m basically stealing a woman from my own self.&quot; Ross muttered because Lyric was also him in disguise.</p>

<p>For a long moment, he simply observed, letting the thought settle.</p>

<p>The world outside could burn, collapse, or drown in chaos—it mattered little.</p>

<p>Here, in this moment, the only thing that mattered was the woman in his eyes and the question of how he would claim her attention, once again, in a way only he could.</p>

<p>A slow, almost imperceptible smile curved his lips.</p>

<p>***</p>

<p>&quot;Are you okay, Mom? Has Dad finally contacted us?&quot; Daniel asked, his voice trembling slightly.</p>

<p>The handsome seventeen-year-old tried to mask his worry, but it was clear in the tight line of his jaw and the way his hands fidgeted nervously.</p>

<p>There was nothing he could do to reach his father, and the helplessness weighed heavily on him, a constant reminder of the danger that had overtaken the world.</p>

<p>Ashley forced a smile, though it was brittle and fleeting.</p>

<p>&quot;Not yet,&quot; she said softly, trying to keep her voice steady. &quot;But I know he will. Your father is okay. I can feel it in my heart, Daniel.&quot;</p>

<p>Even as she spoke the words, a single tear slipped down her cheek.</p>

<p>She quickly brushed it away, unwilling to let her son see just how fragile she felt.</p>

<p>When the chaos had first erupted—the streets overrun by the undead, the dead rising from the ground—her world had fallen apart in a matter of hours.</p>

<p>She had feared for Daniel’s life and her husband Lyric’s survival, but Ross’s men had arrived just in time.</p>

<p>Claiming that Lyric was a friend of Ross, they had rescued her and her son, whisking them away to a safer location.</p>

<p>It was a miracle, and one she had been silently grateful for every day since.</p>

<p>Yet now, more than a week had passed since the outbreak, and still, no word had come from her husband.</p>

<p>Each day felt heavier than the last, filled with uncertainty and fear.</p>

<p>The world outside their temporary sanctuary had become a nightmare: buildings burned, streets emptied, and the groans of the undead echoed faintly even in places that had once seemed safe.</p>

<p>Hope was a fragile thing in these desperate times, easily crushed by the weight of reality.</p>

<p>Daniel noticed the faint tremor in his mother’s hands and the tightness around her eyes.</p>

<p>&quot;Mom... I know you’re worried,&quot; he said gently, squeezing her hand. &quot;But we’re okay. We’re alive. That has to mean something, right?&quot;</p>

<p>Ashley took a deep breath, trying to calm herself.</p>

<p>She looked at her son, his dark eyes shining with determination that mirrored the man he was becoming, and felt a swell of both pride and fear.</p>

<p>&quot;Yes, Daniel. That means a lot,&quot; she whispered.</p>

<p>&quot;Being alive... it’s a chance. A chance to keep hoping, to keep waiting for him. We just have to hold on a little longer.&quot;</p>

<p>The room they were in was quiet, the air thick with tension and faint traces of fear.</p>

<p>Outside, the distant moans of zombies reminded them that the world beyond these walls was anything but safe.</p>

<p>The once familiar streets and neighborhoods had become hunting grounds, filled with danger at every turn.</p>

<p>Supplies were scarce, and each day required careful planning and cautious movement to survive.</p>

<p>Daniel leaned against her shoulder, seeking comfort in her warmth. &quot;Do you think Dad’s thinking of us too?&quot; he asked softly.</p>

<p>Ashley pressed her lips to the top of his head, letting herself feel the weight of her son’s trust.</p>

<p>&quot;Of course he is, Daniel. I know it. He’s fighting just like we are. He wouldn’t give up on us—not ever.&quot;</p>

<p>A long silence followed, broken only by the occasional distant sound from the streets outside.</p>

<p>They both knew it could be hours before they heard anything new.</p>

<p>But even in the quiet, Ashley held onto hope. She had to.</p>

<p>For her son, for herself, and for the man she loved.</p>

<p>No matter how dark the world became, she would not let despair take them.</p>

<p>She would remain strong, a steady anchor for Daniel, until the day Lyric returned—or until they found a way to fight back against the nightmare that had consumed everything.</p>

<p>***</p>

<p>Night fell, and still there was no word. Ashley sat by the side of the bed, staring into nothingness.</p>

<p>She missed him.</p>

<p>Though she had only seen Lyric on weekends because of the nature of his work, those precious days together had always been filled with laughter and warmth.</p>

<p>He had been a devoted husband, thoughtful and kind, the kind of man who could make her smile even on the hardest days.</p>

<p>No matter how long he’d been away, he always came back with that same gentle grin and the promise that everything would be all right.</p>

<p>Now, that promise felt so far away.</p>

<p>Ashley brushed away a tear and lay down on the small bed, the soft light from her phone screen casting pale reflections across her face.</p>

<p>She had checked her messages countless times throughout the day, hoping for something—anything—from him.</p>

<p>But there was only silence.</p>

<p>Just as she began to drift toward uneasy sleep, her phone suddenly beeped.</p>

<p>The familiar chime startled her heart into a frantic rhythm.</p>

<p>She snatched it from the nightstand, her breath catching in her throat.</p>

<p>A message.</p>

<p>Her eyes widened as she opened it in haste that she almost dropped her phone.</p>
