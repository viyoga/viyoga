<p>A few days later, a beautiful black-haired woman sat quietly while reading a letter in her hands.</p>

<p>She wore the simple and modest robes of a cultivator, the standard attire of a disciple of a major sect.</p>

<p>Yet even the loose clothing could not completely conceal her naturally graceful figure.</p>

<p>Her long black hair flowed over her shoulders, framing a delicate and beautiful face that could easily attract attention wherever she went.</p>

<p>Her name was Su Hanying.</p>

<p>She was only eighteen years old, yet she had already become an inner disciple of the Heavenly Sword Sect.</p>

<p>Her talent in cultivation was exceptional, and among the younger disciples of her generation, she was already considered someone with a promising future.</p>

<p>At this moment, however, there was no excitement on her beautiful face.</p>

<p>Instead, her brows were slightly furrowed as she carefully read the contents of the letter again.</p>

<p>&quot;What happened, Hanying?&quot;</p>

<p>A young handsome man asked.</p>

<p>His name was irrelevant to the matter at hand, but one thing was obvious from the way he looked at Su Hanying.</p>

<p>He liked her.</p>

<p>Very much.</p>

<p>Whenever she spoke, his attention naturally followed her.</p>

<p>Whenever she frowned, he became concerned.</p>

<p>Even now, he was trying to figure out what could have caused her expression to become so serious.</p>

<p>Su Hanying folded the letter slowly.</p>

<p>&quot;I have to go home.&quot;</p>

<p>Her voice was quiet, but there was a hint of reluctance in it.</p>

<p>&quot;Home?&quot;</p>

<p>The young man was surprised.</p>

<p>&quot;Did something happen?&quot;</p>

<p>Su Hanying nodded slightly.</p>

<p>&quot;It’s nothing. I just miss home.&quot; She lied.</p>

<p>She looked down at the letter again, her beautiful eyes filled with uncertainty.</p>

<p>She had already been away from home for quite some time because of her cultivation in the Heavenly Sword Sect.</p>

<p>She started when she was just 15 years old.</p>

<p>Normally, she wouldn’t have paid much attention to an ordinary letters.</p>

<p>But this letter was different.</p>

<p>The tone was unusually urgent.</p>

<p>Something was clearly happening back home and it was about her mom.</p>

<p>The young man immediately became concerned.</p>

<p>&quot;Do you need someone to accompany you?&quot;</p>

<p>Su Hanying looked at him for a moment before shaking her head.</p>

<p>&quot;No. I should be fine.&quot;</p>

<p>She stood up and carefully put the letter away.</p>

<p>&quot;I’ll report to my elders first. After that, I’ll leave.&quot;</p>

<p>The young man nodded, but a stubborn glint appeared in his eyes.</p>

<p>He had no intention of simply watching Su Hanying leave alone.</p>

<p>In fact, her sudden trip home had immediately made him think that this might be the perfect opportunity to get closer to her.</p>

<p>If fate was giving him an opportunity, he naturally wasn’t going to waste it.</p>

<p>&quot;I’ve actually been thinking about going to Radiant Sky City myself,&quot; he said casually, deliberately making his tone sound as natural as possible. &quot;I’ve heard quite a few good things about the city lately. Since we’re heading toward the same destination, why don’t we travel together, Hanying?&quot;</p>

<p>He smiled confidently.</p>

<p>&quot;It’ll be safer that way, and we can keep each other company during the journey.&quot;</p>

<p>His words sounded reasonable enough, but there was obviously another thought hidden behind them.</p>

<p>If there is a chance, there are plenty of things that can happen.</p>

<p>The journey to Radiant Sky City wasn’t particularly short.</p>

<p>Traveling together meant that they would have plenty of time to talk, cultivate, and perhaps even develop a deeper relationship.</p>

<p>Xiao Fengchen was quite confident in himself.</p>

<p>He came from a wealthy and influential clan, was an inner disciple of the Heavenly Sword Sect, and possessed respectable cultivation talent.</p>

<p>He also had a handsome appearance and a decent reputation among the younger generation.</p>

<p>From his perspective, he was already an excellent candidate for a Dao companion.</p>

<p>Any ordinary female cultivator would probably be delighted if he expressed interest in her.</p>

<p>Unfortunately, Su Hanying wasn’t an ordinary woman.</p>

<p>She paused and looked at him thoughtfully.</p>

<p>The young man’s name was Xiao Fengchen.</p>

<p>She knew quite a bit about him.</p>

<p>He was talented, came from a powerful clan, and had already established himself as one of the more promising inner disciples of their generation.</p>

<p>There was nothing particularly wrong with him.</p>

<p>In fact, from an objective perspective, he was an excellent match.</p>

<p>But feelings weren’t something that could simply be calculated.</p>

<p>Despite spending quite some time together in the sect, Su Hanying had never felt anything particularly special toward him.</p>

<p>She appreciated his talent and respected his background, but that was all.</p>

<p>There was no excitement in her heart whenever she saw him.</p>

<p>No nervousness.</p>

<p>No longing.</p>

<p>No inexplicable desire to stay by his side.</p>

<p>Su Hanying wanted her future Dao companion to be someone who could truly make her heart move.</p>

<p>Perhaps a powerful core disciple.</p>

<p>Perhaps someone even more extraordinary.</p>

<p>She had no intention of settling simply because someone was interested in her.</p>

<p>After all, she was still young.</p>

<p>There was no need to rush into something as important as a lifelong relationship.</p>

<p>She was still waiting for the day when she would meet someone she could genuinely fall in love with.</p>

<p>After thinking about it for several breaths, however, Su Hanying finally nodded.</p>

<p>&quot;Okay.&quot;</p>

<p>She looked at Xiao Fengchen calmly.</p>

<p>&quot;Traveling together will indeed be safer.&quot;</p>

<p>A bright smile immediately appeared on Xiao Fengchen’s handsome face.</p>

<p>&quot;Then it’s settled!&quot;</p>

<p>He secretly clenched his fist in excitement.</p>

<p>He knew that this was only the beginning, but at least he had successfully taken the first step.</p>

<p>Su Hanying, meanwhile, didn’t seem to notice his excitement.</p>

<p>Her thoughts had already returned to the mysterious letter in her possession.</p>

<p>She had a faint feeling that something unusual was waiting for her in Radiant Sky City.</p>

<p>Neither of them knew that this journey would soon become far more interesting than either had imagined.</p>

<p>***</p>

<p>The two traveled toward Radiant Sky City in a large carriage accompanied by a sizable group of Xiao Clan servants, guards, and attendants.</p>

<p>Since Xiao Fengchen came from a wealthy clan, there was naturally no shortage of people willing to accompany him wherever he went.</p>
