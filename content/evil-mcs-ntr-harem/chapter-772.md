<p>Steve’s eyes fluttered open to a dimly lit room—his room—but something felt wrong.</p>

<p>He tried to sit up and realized, with rising panic, that he couldn’t.</p>

<p>Thick ropes bound his wrists and ankles to the bedposts, digging into his skin.</p>

<p>&quot;What the hell...?&quot; he rasped, twisting against the restraints.</p>

<p>Then he saw it.</p>

<p>Standing at the foot of the bed was a massive figure—broad-shouldered, built like a tank—and wearing a grotesque demon mask with horns and a permanent, carved grin.</p>

<p>The figure just stood there, breathing slowly, watching him in silence.</p>

<p>Steve’s blood ran cold.</p>

<p>&quot;Release me! Who the fuck are you?!&quot; he shouted, his voice raw with panic.</p>

<p>And then, from somewhere behind the mask, a voice rang out—deep, familiar, and laced with cruel amusement.</p>

<p>&quot;Well, well... finally awake, are we?&quot;</p>

<p>Steve’s eyes widened.</p>

<p>That voice... he knew it.</p>

<p>He knew it.</p>

<p>But it couldn’t be.</p>

<p>&quot;No... No way...&quot; he whispered, straining against the ropes even harder now, heart pounding in his chest.</p>

<p>The masked man took a slow step forward, tilting his head like a predator toying with its prey.</p>

<p>&quot;Oh, I told you,&quot; the voice continued, chuckling. &quot;You don’t get to walk away that easily, Steve. Not after what you planned to do to Heaven.&quot;</p>

<p>Steve’s breath caught in his throat.</p>

<p>It was him.</p>

<p>It was Ross.</p>

<p>&quot;Big D! Fuck you! Go to hell!&quot; Steve shouted, thrashing against the ropes as rage boiled inside him the moment he heard Ross’s voice.</p>

<p>He jerked his head around, eyes scanning the room, searching for him—but Ross was nowhere to be seen.</p>

<p>That’s when it hit him.</p>

<p>The voice wasn’t coming from someone in the room—it was coming from a speaker, hidden somewhere nearby.</p>

<p>Of course. Cameras. A sound system.</p>

<p>Ross was watching. Listening. Toying with him from a distance.</p>

<p>Steve’s eyes darted to the corner of the room where the hulking man in the demon mask stood silently, unmoving, arms folded over his chest like a statue.</p>

<p>He was a mountain of muscle, a living shadow—and he hadn’t said a word.</p>

<p>&quot;You and your damn masks!&quot; Steve barked, laughing hysterically.</p>

<p>&quot;Bunch of fucking jokers playing dress-up! Let me go, you freaks, or I swear to God I’ll have every one of you behind bars by morning!&quot;</p>

<p>He spat on the floor and snarled. &quot;Don’t you know who I am? Don’t you know who my father is?!&quot;</p>

<p>There was a pause.</p>

<p>Then Ross’s voice crackled back through the speaker, colder than before.</p>

<p>&quot;Oh, we know exactly who you are. And we don’t give a damn.&quot;</p>

<p>Steve froze.</p>

<p>Ross continued, tone cruel and mocking.</p>

<p>&quot;You like fucking, don’t you, Steve? Cry like a baby when you don’t get what you want. Threaten people. Plot revenge. You even wanted to make Heaven pay... for choosing someone better.&quot;</p>

<p>The voice paused, then dropped into something darker.</p>

<p>&quot;So I figured... why not give you a taste of your own medicine?&quot;</p>

<p>The door creaked open behind the masked man.</p>

<p>Heavy footsteps echoed into the room.</p>

<p>One by one, towering figures stepped inside—each wearing identical demon masks, each built like a soldier or a bouncer pulled straight out of a nightmare.</p>

<p>Two. Four. Eight. A dozen. And still more came.</p>

<p>Steve’s breath hitched.</p>

<p>&quot;No... no, no, no—what the hell is this?!&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;There are more than two dozen of them now,&quot; Ross said calmly.</p>

<p>&quot;And more on the way. You wanted someone to break, didn’t you? Someone to cry and beg? Congratulations.&quot;</p>

<p>Steve shook his head violently, yanking at the ropes with wild desperation.</p>

<p>&quot;Stop this! STOP! You sick bastard!&quot;</p>

<p>But the masked men didn’t speak.</p>

<p>They just closed in.</p>

<p>And Ross’s voice, still distant, still amused, echoed one final time:</p>

<p>&quot;Enjoy your lesson, Steve.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;No! Stop! AHHHHH!&quot;</p>

<p>Steve’s agonized screams tore through the stillness of the room, raw and desperate.</p>

<p>His voice cracked under the weight of relentless pain, echoing off the cold walls and mixing with the heavy breathing of the masked men who surrounded him.</p>

<p>Bound tightly to the bed, he was utterly helpless—his body a captive to the merciless hands of those towering figures.</p>

<p>Each one moved with mechanical precision, their grip unyielding as they took turns asserting their dominance.</p>

<p>Steve was no longer a man with threats and fury; he had become nothing more than a broken plaything, tossed between shadowed giants.</p>

<p>His protests and curses turned into ragged gasps as every part of him was invaded—his pride shattered alongside his physical defenses.</p>

<p>What had once been a man filled with arrogance and control was now reduced to trembling sobs and choked cries.</p>

<p>The humiliation burned deeper than any wound, stripping him bare not just physically, but spiritually.</p>

<p>Hours dragged on like an endless nightmare. There was no mercy, no respite.</p>

<p>The masked men came and went like dark phantoms, their heavy footsteps fading only to return again and again.</p>

<p>Steve lost track of time, lost track of himself.</p>

<p>All that remained was a fading whisper of resistance buried beneath the waves of pain and degradation.</p>

<p>Between agonizing moments, his mind reeled with disbelief and horror.</p>

<p>How had he fallen so far? The man who once ruled with threats and cold calculation was now at the mercy of the very cruelty he’d planned for others.</p>

<p>The room was thick with tension and shadows, the air heavy with silence broken only by Steve’s broken cries.</p>

<p>Outside, the world went on oblivious. Inside, a slow unraveling had begun—a reckoning forged in pain and humiliation.</p>

<p>And even as exhaustion threatened to drag him into unconsciousness, he knew the torment was far from over.</p>

<p>There was more to come. The parade of masked figures showed no sign of ending.</p>

<p>The nightmare was just beginning.</p>

<p>This arena of doom stretched on without mercy for a full month—day after endless day of torment and degradation.</p>

<p>Each hour chipped away at Steve’s sanity, each moment eroding the fragile walls he had built around his mind.</p>

<p>The relentless abuse broke him down piece by piece until there was nothing left of the proud, angry man who had walked into that room.</p>

<p>His thoughts grew scattered and hollow; memories blurred and faded into a distant haze.</p>

<p>The sharp edge of his fury dulled into numbness. Slowly, he ceased to be himself.</p>

<p>By the time the month ended, Steve was no longer a man—he was a husk, an empty shell haunted by shadows.</p>

<p>His spirit, once fiery and fierce, had been reduced to a twisted, ugly reflection of what he used to be, barely recognizable even to himself.</p>
