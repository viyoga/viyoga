<p>Then knives—shimmering, razor-sharp, and dozens of them—materialized out of thin air, hovering behind him like the wings of a metallic demon.</p>

<p>Sabrina gasped.</p>

<p>&quot;W–Wil—&quot;</p>

<p>Wilson flicked his wrist.</p>

<p>The first wave of blades shot forward with terrifying speed.</p>

<p>They struck Ross’s shoulders with brutal force.</p>

<p>Thud! Thud! Thud!</p>

<p>Ross was ripped out of Sabrina’s arms as he was thrown backward, his body twisting violently from the impact.</p>

<p>Sabrina stumbled forward with a scream, her hands reaching out in a helpless reflex.</p>

<p>More knives appeared.</p>

<p>This time, hundreds.</p>

<p>They filled the air in a swirling vortex of death before Wilson clenched his fist and sent them flying.</p>

<p>The next instant was a nightmare.</p>

<p>The blades plunged into Ross’s chest, stomach, back—everywhere.</p>

<p>Puchi</p>

<p>Puchi</p>

<p>Puchi</p>

<p>They sliced, pierced, and tore through him, turning his body into a human pincushion.</p>

<p>Blood sprayed in all directions, warm droplets splashing onto Sabrina’s face and dress.</p>

<p>She fell to her knees, paralyzed by shock and horror.</p>

<p>Ross crashed to the ground, his body twitching once... twice... before going completely still.</p>

<p>Knives protruded from him at every angle, his body nearly unrecognizable beneath the forest of steel.</p>

<p>A puddle of blood rapidly spread out from under him, staining the floor.</p>

<p>Screams erupted all around as the drunken crowd finally realized what was happening.</p>

<p>People stumbled away, tripping over chairs, crawling across the floor, anything to escape.</p>

<p>But through the chaos, Wilson stood proudly, chest heaving, eyes locked on Ross’s unmoving figure.</p>

<p>&quot;Easier than killing a dog,&quot; he sneered, stepping forward with a chilling grin. &quot;Ross fuck you so much... tonight you die like the trash you are.&quot;</p>

<p>Sabrina sobbed, reaching toward Ross’s blood-soaked hand, her entire body shaking.</p>

<p>And Wilson’s men—sober, eager, and trembling with bloodlust—began to surround her.</p>

<p>&quot;Hahaha! You’re mine now, bitch!&quot; Wilson lunged forward, fingers stretched wide, his face twisted with triumph as he reached for Sabrina.</p>

<p>But before his hand could touch even a strand of her hair—</p>

<p>the world blinked.</p>

<p>Sabrina vanished.</p>

<p>The hall vanished.</p>

<p>The drunk people vanished.</p>

<p>The screams, the music, the smell of alcohol—</p>

<p>everything dissolved into nothingness.</p>

<p>Wilson stumbled forward violently, nearly falling on his face.</p>

<p>His men yelled, some drawing weapons out of instinct, others gripping their heads in confusion as the air around them warped and twisted.</p>

<p>For a moment, there was only silence.</p>

<p>Then a soft hum began to vibrate through the darkness—low, deep, like the heartbeat of some enormous creature lurking just out of sight.</p>

<p>Light flared.</p>

<p>A pale blue radiance expanded across the floor, revealing a vast chamber that stretched further than their eyes could see.</p>

<p>The ceiling arched high above them, disappearing into shadows.</p>

<p>Massive pillars of glossy black stone lined the circular dome, each one carved with intricate patterns that pulsed faintly with light.</p>

<p>The floor beneath them was polished obsidian, so smooth it mirrored their terrified faces perfectly.</p>

<p>&quot;Where... where the hell are we?&quot; one of Wilson’s men whispered, voice cracking.</p>

<p>&quot;Boss...?&quot; another muttered, stepping closer to Wilson. &quot;What’s going on?&quot;</p>

<p>Wilson didn’t answer. He was scanning the chamber with wild, uneven breaths, panic clawing up his spine.</p>

<p>Sweat beaded on his forehead as the realization hit him—</p>

<p>This wasn’t anything human-made.</p>

<p>This was something else. Something beyond their understanding.</p>

<p>Then every man froze.</p>

<p>Because at the far end of the dome, illuminated by a single shaft of light like a spotlight from the heavens, stood an enormous throne.</p>

<p>It was carved from dark stone and bone, with jagged edges rising like the wings of a demon.</p>

<p>The platform supporting it had multiple steps, each glowing faintly with sigils etched in fiery red.</p>

<p>And on that throne...</p>

<p>Sat a man.</p>

<p>Casual. Relaxed. Almost bored.</p>

<p>But unmistakably alive.</p>

<p>Unmistakably Ross.</p>

<p>Wilson’s jaw dropped, his face draining of color.</p>

<p>&quot;R... Ross?! W–What—That’s impossible! I killed you! I fucking killed you!&quot;</p>

<p>Ross didn’t even flinch.</p>

<p>Instead, he slowly lifted his hands and—</p>

<p>Clap.</p>

<p>Clap.</p>

<p>Clap.</p>

<p>The sound echoed with unnatural volume, bouncing off the great dome and crashing back into them like rolling thunder.</p>

<p>Each clap seemed to slap Wilson across the face, mocking, humiliating, taunting.</p>

<p>Ross leaned forward, elbows resting lightly on his knees.</p>

<p>&quot;You know... watching your face right now is extremely satisfying,&quot; he said calmly, a cold smile touching his lips.</p>

<p>&quot;The confusion. The fear. The stupidity.&quot;</p>

<p>He chuckled.</p>

<p>&quot;You didn’t expect this, did you? Honestly, I can’t blame you. You and your little band of half-brained thugs never did have very high IQ.&quot;</p>

<p>Wilson stumbled backward, shaking.</p>

<p>&quot;This isn’t real! This isn’t real! You were dead! I—&quot;</p>

<p>Ross raised a finger.</p>

<p>Instant silence.</p>

<p>&quot;You think a handful of knives can kill me?&quot; Ross asked, tilting his head. &quot;Me? Wilson, Wilson... you really are a clown.&quot;</p>

<p>His smile widened, revealing a hint of something dark beneath the calm expression.</p>

<p>&quot;And the world needs clowns, you know. Without idiots like you, life would just be too boring.&quot;</p>

<p>Wilson’s men exchanged terrified glances, gripping their weapons tighter.</p>

<p>Ross tapped his finger on the throne’s armrest.</p>

<p>&quot;Oh, right. Your extras.&quot;</p>

<p>He raised his hand and flicked his fingers lazily.</p>

<p>The air trembled.</p>

<p>Thud! Thud! Thud! Thud!</p>

<p>In flashes of light, bodies appeared around the chamber—men materializing one after another until over a hundred reinforcements crowded the space.</p>

<p>Many of them stumbled as they arrived, dizzy and disoriented.</p>

<p>&quot;Boss!&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;What the hell?!&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;Why are we here? I was outside guarding the trucks!&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;I—I was in the bathroom just now!&quot;</p>

<p>The new arrivals looked around wildly, panic spreading like wildfire as they realized none of this made sense.</p>

<p>Wilson swallowed hard, throat tight, eyes darting everywhere.</p>

<p>Ross watched them like a king observing insects from far above.</p>

<p>&quot;Good,&quot; he said softly, resting his chin on his hand again. &quot;Now everyone is here.&quot;</p>

<p>A ripple of cold energy radiated from Ross, sweeping across the dome and raising goosebumps on every man’s skin. His voice deepened, echoing unnaturally.</p>

<p>&quot;No one leaves.</p>

<p>No one escapes.</p>

<p>And no one will die without understanding exactly why.&quot;</p>

<p>He leaned back on his throne.</p>

<p>&quot;Shall we begin?&quot;</p>
