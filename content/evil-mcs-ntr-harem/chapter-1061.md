<p>Miku made sure that Ace was fast asleep before she even dared to move.</p>

<p>The sound of his slow, steady breathing filled the dimly lit room, and for a moment, guilt pressed heavily on her chest.</p>

<p>She sat by the edge of the bed, her fingers trembling slightly as she brushed a stray lock of hair from his face.</p>

<p>He looked peaceful—too peaceful for the storm that she carried within her heart.</p>

<p>She didn’t want to hurt him any more than she already had. That was why she waited.</p>

<p>She waited until the clock struck midnight, until the world outside their home was silent and still, until her conscience could no longer argue with her resolve.</p>

<p>She rose quietly, every step careful not to make the floorboards creak.</p>

<p>Unbeknownst to her, Ace would never discover the truth about what was happening behind his back.</p>

<p>Even if he had heard her voice that night—her cries muffled against Ross’s skin—he would have never believed such a betrayal possible.</p>

<p>His trust was too deep, his love too pure. And that, perhaps, was what hurt Miku the most.</p>

<p>The hallway was dim and cold as she stepped out, her bare feet brushing against the carpet.</p>

<p>Each step she took toward Ross’s room felt like a step further away from who she once was.</p>

<p>When she reached his door, she hesitated for a heartbeat—her hand hovering above the handle, her pulse racing.</p>

<p>Then she opened it.</p>

<p>Ross was inside, sitting by the window with one leg crossed over the other.</p>

<p>The dim light overhead casted him in mysterious glow, outlining his sharp features in silver.</p>

<p>He held a bottle of wine in one hand, a glass in the other. The air smelled faintly of grapes and smoke.</p>

<p>He didn’t even look surprised to see her. He had been expecting her.</p>

<p>She watched him tip the bottle back and take a long drink straight from it, finishing what was left.</p>

<p>The way he handled it—with such effortless control—made it clear he wasn’t even close to drunk.</p>

<p>A full bottle, gone, and yet his gaze remained sharp and unshaken.</p>

<p>Miku realized then that Ross wasn’t a man easily affected by anything—not alcohol, not guilt, not even love.</p>

<p>&quot;Come,&quot; he said softly, setting the glass aside.</p>

<p>His voice was calm, almost lazy, but there was a weight to it that drew her in.</p>

<p>Ross leaned back on the bed, one arm resting behind his head as his eyes followed her every move.</p>

<p>His gaze was steady, intense, but never rushed.</p>

<p>He wanted to see what she would do, how far she would go.</p>

<p>Miku stood frozen for a moment.</p>

<p>The oversized shirt she wore hung loosely over her frame, and her jeans—baggy and plain—were chosen deliberately to hide everything she could.</p>

<p>But she could feel his eyes stripping away those layers with ease, tracing every outline she thought she’d hidden.</p>

<p>Her heart pounded faster the longer he looked at her.</p>

<p>The silence between them was heavy, broken only by the faint ticking of the clock and the hum of her nervous breathing.</p>

<p>She took a hesitant step forward. Then another.</p>

<p>Ross didn’t move, but the faint curve of a knowing smile played on his lips.</p>

<p>Miku swallowed hard. She hated that her body trembled—not from fear, but from anticipation.</p>

<p>She hated that her heart could still race like this for another man when her husband slept only a few steps away.</p>

<p>But still... she walked closer.</p>

<p>She moved to the bed and lay down stiffly, as if her body had forgotten how to bend or respond.</p>

<p>Like a lifeless figure, she stared at the ceiling, forcing herself to remain calm.</p>

<p>If she was going to surrender to him again tonight, she told herself she would not repeat the recklessness of their first encounter.</p>

<p>No wild abandon, no reckless giving in—just control, precision, restraint.</p>

<p>She could manage that, couldn’t she? She hoped so.</p>

<p>Ross didn’t wait long. His voice, low and teasing, cut through the quiet room like a warm knife.</p>

<p>&quot;What’s the matter, Miku?&quot; His eyes glimmered with that dangerous confidence that always made her chest tighten.</p>

<p>&quot;You and I were already too close the other day. Or have you forgotten that so quickly? Doesn’t matter—I suppose I’ll just have to remind you.&quot;</p>

<p>Even as he spoke, he moved toward her, steps silent yet heavy with intent.</p>

<p>There was no hesitation, no pause for consent beyond the one unspoken in the room.</p>

<p>And when he leaned down, his lips claimed hers with ease, boldness, and familiarity, leaving no room for argument.</p>

<p>Miku’s body stiffened at first, her mind telling her to resist.</p>

<p>&quot;Stay calm,&quot; she whispered silently to herself. But her body didn’t listen.</p>

<p>His scent—the mixture of cologne, sweat, and that indefinable something that was uniquely Ross—hit her senses in an overwhelming wave.</p>

<p>It made her heart beat faster, made her breath hitch, made her skin prickle with awareness.</p>

<p>The warmth of his chest, broad and strong, pressed lightly against her, and somehow, in that pressure, she felt both exposed and safe at the same time.</p>

<p>She tried to remain passive, tried to let herself be like she had planned: controlled, restrained.</p>

<p>But within minutes, Ross’s hands, his lips, and the heat radiating from his body had already begun to erode her carefully constructed walls.</p>

<p>Each kiss seemed to pull more from her than the last, as if he were reading her body and unraveling it piece by piece.</p>

<p>She could feel her pulse thrumming through her veins, each beat echoing in her ears.</p>

<p>Her mind raced, conflicted between guilt and desire, between fear and longing.</p>

<p>She hated that she responded so quickly, hated the ease with which he made her melt under his touch.</p>

<p>Yet, even as she hated it, part of her craved it.</p>

<p>Every lingering kiss, every gentle pressure of his lips, every brush of his hands against her skin was a reminder of the night they had shared—and of the invisible bond that had formed between them since then.</p>
