<p>That night, Ross and his entourage headed to the casino as planned.</p>

<p>&quot;I won $50,000, Ross!&quot; one of the girls exclaimed, her eyes sparkling with excitement.</p>

<p>&quot;Unlucky. I already lost a million,&quot; another chimed in with a dramatic sigh.</p>

<p>&quot;I’m going to win all my losses back tonight!&quot; a third declared confidently, determination written all over her face.</p>

<p>The girls had wildly different experiences at the tables—some walking away with big wins, others chalking up significant losses—but that was all part of the thrill.</p>

<p>The unpredictability of the game, the highs and lows, was what made it fun.</p>

<p>Ross, however, wasn’t concerned about their fortunes in the slightest.</p>

<p>With Puppet Freddie keeping a watchful eye on them and their unique status in the multiverse, they were untouchable. Quite literally.</p>

<p>Not even death itself could claim their souls without Ross’s permission.</p>

<p>If anything, their immortality only made the night’s escapades all the more carefree.</p>

<p>As the group strolled into the grand casino, heads turned immediately.</p>

<p>The combination of stunning women and their larger-than-life presence was impossible to ignore.</p>

<p>&quot;They’re here again!&quot; someone whispered.</p>

<p>&quot;What a group of lucky girls!&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;Haven’t they lost enough already?&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;Doesn’t matter. They probably have more money than our entire country combined.&quot;</p>

<p>The staff and regular patrons couldn’t help but murmur as Ross’s group passed by, their presence commanding attention like a royal procession.</p>

<p>It wasn’t just their beauty or charisma—although both were undeniable—but the reputation they had built in just two nights.</p>

<p>The group had a penchant for cashing in million-dollar tickets without batting an eye.</p>

<p>Some walked away winners, but more often than not, they lost hundreds of thousands of dollars in a single sitting.</p>

<p>Yet, they returned each night with the same exuberance, as if money were nothing more than paper.</p>

<p>Ross led the way, his confidence as unshakable as ever.</p>

<p>He smirked at the awed and incredulous stares, reveling in the spectacle they created wherever they went.</p>

<p>His harem followed closely, each woman exuding a distinct charm that only added to their mystique.</p>

<p>At the roulette table, one of the girls called out, &quot;Ross, come on! This is my lucky spin!&quot;</p>

<p>He chuckled, stepping closer. &quot;Alright, let’s see if your luck rubs off on me.&quot;</p>

<p>The dealer gave a polite nod as the chips were placed, the sound of spinning wheels and clinking chips filling the air.</p>

<p>Ross casually tossed down a million-dollar bet, his nonchalance drawing gasps from the surrounding onlookers.</p>

<p>&quot;Is he serious?&quot; someone murmured.</p>

<p>&quot;Of course, he is. That guy doesn’t even flinch.&quot;</p>

<p>Whether they won or lost didn’t matter to Ross and his group.</p>

<p>For them, the casino was just another playground in a multiverse where they held all the cards.</p>

<p>The thrill, the attention, and the fun were all they needed to make the night unforgettable.</p>

<p>An hour later, the group came to a halt.</p>

<p>&quot;Let’s eat. All these losses are making me hungry,&quot; Ross complained, though his tone was lighthearted.</p>

<p>Despite losing a staggering $500 million, he didn’t seem the least bit upset.</p>

<p>In fact, he shrugged it off as casually as if it were a minor inconvenience on a Monday morning.</p>

<p>Ross could have easily cheated, using his powers to tip the scales in his favor, but where was the fun in that?</p>

<p>Gambling was only thrilling when the stakes were real.</p>

<p>He knew that the longer you played, the house always had the edge.</p>

<p>Sure, he won big now and then, but losses were inevitable—and he accepted that without a hint of frustration.</p>

<p>The group made their way toward the casino’s dining area, ready to indulge in some fine cuisine to recharge.</p>

<p>&quot;Wow! Did you see that? He didn’t even flinch after losing $500 million!&quot; a guest whispered loudly.</p>

<p>&quot;That’s Ross Oakley for you. I heard he’s already in the NBA,&quot; another replied, leaning closer to the table gossip.</p>

<p>&quot;But he hasn’t even played a single game yet! How does he have so much money? And from the looks of it, he’s the one bankrolling all those women too.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;Why are you asking me? Do I look like his accountant or secretary or something?&quot; a casino staff member retorted, shaking their head.</p>

<p>Ross’s presence was impossible to ignore.</p>

<p>A gambler like him—one who casually tossed around fortunes most could only dream of—was bound to attract attention.</p>

<p>Whether it was admiration, curiosity, or envy, whispers followed him wherever he went.</p>

<p>A &quot;whale&quot; of his caliber was a rare sight, even in a casino filled with high rollers, and his group’s glamorous aura only heightened the intrigue.</p>

<p>After finishing their dinner, Ross leaned back in his chair, satisfied.</p>

<p>The conversation around them was still buzzing, but he paid it no mind, enjoying the company of his entourage.</p>

<p>Just then, a man approached their table.</p>

<p>Handsome, sharply dressed, and exuding authority, he carried himself with the kind of confidence that only came from being in a position of power.</p>

<p>His tailored business suit spoke volumes about his wealth and status.</p>

<p>&quot;Mr. Oakley,&quot; the man began, his voice smooth and polite, &quot;Mr. Adams has extended an invitation for you to join him in a game of high-stakes poker. He’s eager to meet you.&quot;</p>

<p>Ross tilted his head slightly, studying the man with a curious smirk. His women exchanged glances, clearly intrigued by the sudden proposition.</p>

<p>&quot;High-stakes poker, huh?&quot; Ross mused, his tone amused but noncommittal.</p>

<p>&quot;Tell Mr. Adams I’ll think about it. Right now, I’m enjoying my meal.&quot;</p>

<p>The man gave a courteous nod.</p>

<p>&quot;Of course. I’ll let him know. Should you decide to join, the private table is ready whenever you are.&quot;</p>

<p>With that, the man turned and walked away, leaving Ross and his group to deliberate.</p>

<p>One of the girls leaned in closer to Ross, her eyes sparkling with mischief.</p>

<p>&quot;Are we going to take his offer? You know it sounds like fun.&quot;</p>

<p>Ross chuckled, swirling his drink lazily.</p>

<p>&quot;We’ll see. Let’s finish up here first. No rush—after all, the night’s still young.&quot; Ross ate and drank a lot as he indulged himself well on his first ever trip to the casino.</p>
