<p>&quot;How are you doing this?! You monster! This isn&#x27;t them! I refuse to believe it!&quot;</p>

<p>For the first time in what felt like forever, Thomas&#x27;s calm and composed exterior shattered completely.</p>

<p>His voice, usually steady and measured, cracked with raw emotion as he screamed at Ross.</p>

<p>His fists trembled at his sides, nails digging into his palms hard enough to draw blood.</p>

<p>The sight before him — the faces of his loved ones, smiling, calling his name — was too much to bear.</p>

<p>It had to be fake. It had to be.</p>

<p>Even as the familiar faces tugged at his heart, even as the memories threatened to drown him, Thomas clung stubbornly to the last fragile thread of hope.</p>

<p>This is just a trick, he told himself. Some kind of illusion, a projection, a cruel manipulation.</p>

<p>Maybe it was a camera trick. Maybe Ross had found a way to mimic their voices, their mannerisms. It wasn&#x27;t real. It couldn&#x27;t be.</p>

<p>But all of Thomas&#x27;s desperate rationalizing crumbled with Ross&#x27;s next move.</p>

<p>&quot;Brenda,&quot; Ross said, voice light and mocking, as if this were all a game to him.</p>

<p>&quot;Why don&#x27;t you enlighten your son about a few personal details? Something only you would know. Something to make him understand.&quot;</p>

<p>The words had barely left Ross&#x27;s mouth when Brenda&#x27;s lips moved on their own, as if some invisible force puppeteered her.</p>

<p>Her voice, warm and familiar, filled the room — but what she said made the color drain from Thomas&#x27;s face.</p>

<p>&quot;Thomas, when you were fifteen, I caught you in your room while you were busy playing with your c—&quot;</p>

<p>Brenda&#x27;s eyes widened in shock, horror flickering across her features.</p>

<p>It was clear she hadn&#x27;t wanted to say those words, hadn&#x27;t even chosen to say them.</p>

<p>Her mouth moved against her will, forced to drag a buried, shameful memory into the harsh light of day.</p>

<p>It was the truth — a deeply personal, embarrassing truth — one she would never have willingly shared, especially not now, especially not in front of others.</p>

<p>Thomas felt like the floor had fallen out from under him.</p>

<p>&quot;Stop!&quot; he shouted, the word ripping from his throat in pure panic.</p>

<p>His face was beet red, his heart hammering against his ribs.</p>

<p>Of all the memories, Ross had chosen this one — the single most humiliating moment of his teenage years, a memory he had tried so hard to forget.</p>

<p>And now, thanks to Ross&#x27;s cruelty, it was out in the open.</p>

<p>Worse, it was out in front of his girlfriend, Colleen, who stood nearby with wide, confused eyes, clearly trying to process what she had just heard.</p>

<p>Thomas wanted to disappear. To sink into the floor. To scream.</p>

<p>It was real.</p>

<p>The horrifying, gut-wrenching certainty hit him like a hammer: this wasn&#x27;t a projection or an illusion.</p>

<p>These were truly his loved ones, twisted and manipulated against their will by Ross.</p>

<p>And Thomas, helpless, could do nothing but watch.</p>

<p>&quot;Is that enough,&quot; Ross said, voice dripping with mockery, &quot;or would you like me to ask your beautiful girlfriend Colleen to share some of your fond memories together, too?&quot;</p>

<p>Thomas&#x27;s fists clenched so hard his nails bit into his palms.</p>

<p>His chest rose and fell with labored breaths, his body shaking not with fear — but with a cold, simmering rage that begged for release.</p>

<p>He swallowed hard, struggling to keep the boiling fury inside from spilling over.</p>

<p>&quot;What do you want?&quot; he said finally, his voice low and dangerous, trembling with barely suppressed violence.</p>

<p>It wasn&#x27;t just anger anymore; it was hatred, pure and unfiltered, focused solely on Ross.</p>

<p>If his eyes had been weapons, Ross would have been torn to pieces where he stood.</p>

<p>But the invisible force pinning Thomas to the wall was merciless.</p>

<p>He could do nothing but stand there, powerless, like a spectator trapped in his own personal hell.</p>

<p>Ross only smiled, as if amused by Thomas&#x27;s desperate attempts to hold himself together.</p>

<p>&quot;I want nothing, Thomas,&quot; he said, as if the whole thing was beneath him.</p>

<p>His voice was light, almost conversational, which made his words even more sickening.</p>

<p>&quot;I was living my life in peace, surrounded by the people I love, enjoying every little moment. And then you came crashing in — a man desperate to tear everything away from me, to destroy the happiness I built with my own two hands.&quot;</p>

<p>Ross paused, letting the weight of his words sink in. His smile twisted into something dark and feral.</p>

<p>&quot;And now,&quot; he said softly, &quot;I&#x27;m simply here to return the favor.&quot;</p>

<p>He turned his head slowly, deliberately, and let his gaze linger on the two women standing at the edge of the room.</p>

<p>Brenda and Colleen stood frozen, their faces pale, their bodies trembling ever so slightly.</p>

<p>Ross&#x27;s gaze raked over them like a physical touch, making both women instinctively recoil even though he hadn&#x27;t yet moved.</p>

<p>Thomas&#x27;s heart dropped into his stomach.</p>

<p>He knew what Ross was implying even before the bastard spoke the next words.</p>

<p>&quot;So tell me, Thomas,&quot; Ross said, savoring every syllable, &quot;which one should I pick first?&quot;</p>

<p>A slow, deliberate lick of his lips followed, an obscene gesture that made Thomas&#x27;s gut churn with revulsion.</p>

<p>Thomas thrashed against the unseen force holding him, muscles straining, veins bulging in his neck, but it was futile. He couldn&#x27;t move an inch.</p>

<p>His mouth opened in horror, words tumbling out in a desperate plea. He really could not bear to see his loved ones destroyed right before his eyes.</p>

<p>&quot;I&#x27;ll give you anything you want,&quot; he gasped, his voice raw with emotion. &quot;Money, information, my life — whatever you want. Just don&#x27;t touch them. Please... I&#x27;m begging you.&quot;</p>

<p>For a moment, Ross simply stared at him, as if considering the offer. Then he laughed — a hollow, joyless sound that echoed off the walls like a death knell.</p>

<p>&quot;Isn&#x27;t it a little too late for that now, Thomas?&quot; he said mockingly. &quot;The wheels are already in motion. People are dead. Blood has been spilled because of you. There&#x27;s no going back now. There&#x27;s no mercy left to give.&quot;</p>
