<p>&quot;I feel like I’m cursed. Like my body betrayed me. I can’t even give my wife the one thing she truly wants. What kind of man does that make me, Ross?&quot;</p>

<p>And then, just like that, the weight broke him.</p>

<p>Mario began to cry—no longer holding it in, no longer pretending to be strong.</p>

<p>It wasn’t a quiet, noble shedding of tears. It was raw, broken sobbing.</p>

<p>The kind that made most people uncomfortable. It was messy. Ugly.</p>

<p>Painful to witness. But Ross didn’t flinch. He didn’t look away or make a face.</p>

<p>He just stood there, letting his friend fall apart.</p>

<p>&quot;It’s okay, Mario,&quot; Ross said calmly, placing a steady hand on his shoulder.</p>

<p>&quot;This too shall pass. I know it doesn’t feel like it now, but you’ll get through this. You’re not alone.&quot;</p>

<p>He gave Mario a light squeeze and tried to lift the mood with a bit of humor.</p>

<p>&quot;Besides, you’ve got options. You two could adopt a baby. Hell, a dozen babies if you want. You’re rich as fuck. You could build a mansion just for toddlers and live like a king of chaos.&quot;</p>

<p>But Mario didn’t laugh.</p>

<p>He simply shook his head, slowly, and looked up at Ross with tired, pained eyes.</p>

<p>&quot;My wife, Ella... she doesn’t want to adopt,&quot; he said, his voice low and hollow.</p>

<p>&quot;She wants her baby. Our baby. Something that comes from both of us. That’s all she’s ever dreamed of.&quot;</p>

<p>Ross frowned slightly, sensing where this might be going.</p>

<p>Mario took a shaky breath. &quot;And that brings me to you, Ross.&quot;</p>

<p>Ross narrowed his eyes a bit, not yet responding.</p>

<p>&quot;I want you to help me give her what she wants.&quot;</p>

<p>There was a long silence between them.</p>

<p>The words lingered in the air like thunder before the storm.</p>

<p>Ross’s expression didn’t change right away. He was too calm. Too still.</p>

<p>&quot;You’re going to have to be a little more specific, Mario,&quot; Ross finally said, though he already had a pretty good idea.</p>

<p>Mario swallowed hard. &quot;You’re the only one I trust. You’re strong, healthy, smart... and I know how much women want you. Hell, you’ve got thirty-something kids already, right? Fertility isn’t an issue for you.&quot;</p>

<p>Ross’s brows twitched slightly. &quot;Mario...&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;I want you to be the donor. Or—or even more. If that’s what it takes.&quot; His eyes welled up again.</p>

<p>&quot;She doesn’t know I’m asking. I just... I just want to give her this gift. Even if it can’t come from me.&quot;</p>

<p>Ross leaned back slightly, processing the request.</p>

<p>It wasn’t the first time someone had asked something outrageous of him.</p>

<p>But this... this was personal. And he was of course happy to do it.</p>

<p>&quot;Do you love her that much?&quot; Ross asked, his voice quiet.</p>

<p>&quot;With everything I am,&quot; Mario replied. &quot;Even if the child isn’t mine by blood... I’ll raise them as my own. I won’t hesitate. I just want her to be happy. To feel whole.&quot;</p>

<p>Ross stared at him for a long moment. Then he closed his eyes, exhaled slowly, and said, &quot;This isn’t a decision to make lightly. For you. For her. Or for me.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;I know,&quot; Mario said quickly. &quot;And I’ll owe you for the rest of my life.&quot;</p>

<p>Ross looked at him again—this time with something unreadable in his expression.</p>

<p>Not anger. Not judgment. Just... thoughtfulness.</p>

<p>&quot;I’ll consider it,&quot; Ross said quietly.</p>

<p>&quot;But only if you tell her. If she agrees, truly agrees... then we’ll talk.&quot;</p>

<p>Mario nodded slowly. &quot;Fair enough.&quot;</p>

<p>Ross stood up. &quot;Let me know what she says. And Mario?&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;Yeah?&quot;</p>

<p>Ross offered a small, wry smile.</p>

<p>&quot;Next time you cry like that, at least give me a warning. I almost called for security.&quot;</p>

<p>Mario let out a hoarse laugh through the remnants of his tears.</p>

<p>&quot;Deal.&quot;</p>

<p>***</p>

<p>One week later, Ross received another call from Mario.</p>

<p>His voice was quiet, almost strained, but steady enough.</p>

<p>They arranged to meet at Mario’s house again, and before hanging up, Mario confirmed what Ross had been waiting to hear—his wife, Ella, had agreed to the arrangement.</p>

<p>Ross sat back in his seat, letting the weight of those words sink in. It was happening.</p>

<p>The green light had been given.</p>

<p>He remembered Ella from past team championship celebrations.</p>

<p>She’d always stood out from the crowd—elegant, poised, and undeniably beautiful.</p>

<p>She wasn’t the loud, attention-seeking type either.</p>

<p>There was something refined about her, the way she carried herself, the way her eyes always seemed to observe more than she said.</p>

<p>Back then, Ross noticed her, sure, but out of loyalty and respect for Mario, a man who had been nothing but solid on and off the court... had never entertained any inappropriate thoughts.</p>

<p>She was off-limits. Untouchable.</p>

<p>But things were different now.</p>

<p>Mario, broken and desperate, had essentially offered her up. And she had said yes.</p>

<p>Ross wasn’t a man to force anything.</p>

<p>He didn’t chase women who weren’t interested, and he definitely didn’t involve himself with friends’ wives.</p>

<p>But this wasn’t some underhanded affair done in secret.</p>

<p>This was consented, discussed, and agreed upon.</p>

<p>If anything, it was Mario who came to Ross, not the other way around.</p>

<p>And now that all barriers were down, Ross found his anticipation growing by the hour.</p>

<p>He couldn’t stop picturing Ella—how she’d look when she opened the door, what she’d be wearing, how her expression might shift from nervousness to desire.</p>

<p>Would she blush when she looked at him?</p>

<p>Would she hesitate, or would she be bold, already resigned to what was about to happen?</p>

<p>Ross ran a hand through his hair, already imagining the sounds she’d make—the gasp when he touched her, the soft moans as her body gave in to him.</p>

<p>He could almost feel the heat of her skin, the way her lips would part as he claimed her.</p>

<p>She would surrender herself fully, not because she was being forced, but because deep down, a part of her wanted this too.</p>

<p>And Ross? He had no plans to disappoint. If he was going to do this, he’d do it right.</p>
