<p>With a quiet sigh, Nina turned away and left the room, closing the door gently behind her.</p>

<p>Out in the hallway, the silence felt heavier than it should have.</p>

<p>Her heels clicked against the floor as she walked slowly, lost in thought.</p>

<p>Why am I even bothered by this? she asked herself, her mind circling around what she had just seen.</p>

<p>Zack was kind, protective, admired by everyone in school.</p>

<p>He had status, looks, and the perfect athlete’s body.</p>

<p>But the moment she saw what he had been hiding, doubts had begun to creep in.</p>

<p>She hated herself for even thinking that way, but she couldn’t deny it either.</p>

<p>Her thoughts wandered to the future.</p>

<p>If Zack couldn’t satisfy her... if intimacy was always going to be something he avoided, hidden behind excuses, what would that mean for them?</p>

<p>Could she spend years, maybe even the rest of her life, suppressing her own desires?</p>

<p>The idea sent a shiver down her spine.</p>

<p>Nina hugged her arms around herself as she walked through the dimly lit corridor.</p>

<p>She had always thought she knew what she wanted in a man: strength, ambition, loyalty.</p>

<p>Zack had those things.</p>

<p>But now, she realized there was another layer—something primal, physical, a hunger she had never dared to acknowledge until now.</p>

<p>She bit her lip, frustrated.</p>

<p>Am I shallow for thinking like this? she wondered. Or... am I just being honest with myself for the first time?</p>

<p>Little did Nina know, the answers she sought weren’t going to come from long hours of soul-searching.</p>

<p>Fate had its own cruel, intoxicating plans for her.</p>

<p>Sooner than she could imagine, she would meet Ross Oakley—the man whose very presence radiated dominance, whose smile carried danger, and whose hunger matched her own secret, unspoken desires.</p>

<p>And when their paths crossed, Nina’s carefully constructed world would come crashing down in ways she could never have prepared for.</p>

<p>***</p>

<p>The weekend arrived, and with it, the long-awaited football game.</p>

<p>The sun was high, the air buzzing with the kind of nervous energy only rivalry could create.</p>

<p>&quot;Go! Beat them to death!&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;Show no mercy!&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;Crush them to pieces!&quot;</p>

<p>The stands shook with the chants of students, the roars of both colleges competing to out-scream each other.</p>

<p>What was supposed to be a simple friendly practice game had turned into something much more.</p>

<p>With both schools based in the same city, pride and reputation were on the line, and neither side was willing to give an inch.</p>

<p>Every tackle, every sprint, every play carried the weight of hundreds of eager eyes.</p>

<p>Yet, as intense as the match was, a different spectacle suddenly began to steal the spotlight.</p>

<p>&quot;Wait—look over there!&quot; one student gasped, nudging her friend.</p>

<p>Heads turned as the unmistakable sound of a roaring engine cut through the noise of the crowd.</p>

<p>A sleek, polished sports car glided to a stop right beside the stadium, its glossy paint reflecting the sunlight like a jewel on display.</p>

<p>The luxury vehicle alone was enough to draw attention, but the anticipation of who was inside made the crowd lean forward in curiosity.</p>

<p>&quot;Wow! That’s Carrie Morton’s boyfriend finally stepping out of that ride!&quot; a girl shrieked, her voice nearly drowned out by the whispers and murmurs spreading like wildfire.</p>

<p>The car door swung open, and out he came. Tall, broad-shouldered, and effortlessly confident, he looked like he belonged more on a billboard than in a parking lot.</p>

<p>His presence alone seemed to bend the air around him, drawing stares from every corner.</p>

<p>&quot;He’s huge...&quot; someone breathed, eyes wide in disbelief.</p>

<p>Another student smirked, already gossiping. &quot;Yeah, and I bet he’s just as big where it really counts. Carrie really scored herself a jackpot.&quot;</p>

<p>The game on the field momentarily lost its importance as dozens of eyes shifted away from the players to the man outside the car.</p>

<p>Even students from the rival college couldn’t help but whisper among themselves.</p>

<p>For a brief moment, it felt as though the roar of the crowd wasn’t for the football teams at all—but for him.</p>

<p>Carrie, sitting pretty in the stands, couldn’t hide the smug little smile tugging at her lips.</p>

<p>She didn’t need to say a word; the looks of envy around her said everything.</p>

<p>Most of the students from her campus had their eyes glued to Carrie, whispers passing from mouth to mouth, and the same could be said for Zack and Nina.</p>

<p>Nina noticed the way Zack’s focus slipped, his attention no longer on the field but drifting in Carrie’s direction.</p>

<p>His gaze lingered on the tall figure standing beside her.</p>

<p>Nina frowned, curiosity piqued. She followed his line of sight and studied the man as well.</p>

<p>Who was he?</p>

<p>She squinted, trying to make out his features, but the effort was pointless.</p>

<p>A hat shaded his face, and dark glasses obscured his eyes, making his identity impossible to pin down.</p>

<p>He looked like he belonged to another world altogether, someone who carried both wealth and power in the way he moved, but beyond that, he was a mystery.</p>

<p>Under normal circumstances, everyone would have recognized him instantly.</p>

<p>Ross Oakley’s face had been plastered across news outlets, magazines, and broadcasts all over the globe.</p>

<p>He wasn’t just well known—he was a phenomenon, a walking headline, a man whose every move attracted attention.</p>

<p>But right now? Not a single soul in the crowd had the faintest clue.</p>

<p>Ross had taken precautions. With a flicker of subtle sorcery, he had wrapped himself in an illusion so fine-tuned that no human eye could pierce it.</p>

<p>The spell wasn’t dramatic or showy—it was simple, elegant, designed to bend perception just enough so that those who looked his way would see nothing more than an ordinary stranger.</p>

<p>Not in a million years would they ever guess that Carrie’s boyfriend was Ross Oakley.</p>

<p>Truthfully, he didn’t even need the hat or glasses.</p>

<p>The magic alone was more than enough to mask his true self.</p>

<p>Yet Ross insisted on the disguise, not out of necessity, but out of foresight.</p>
