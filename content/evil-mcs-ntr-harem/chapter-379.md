<p>January came, and with it, the start of the winter semester. Ross was now a second-year college student pursuing a business degree.</p>

<p>Originally, he had chosen the course because it was the cheapest available at his school, aligning with his once-tight budget.</p>

<p>However, now that he was unimaginably wealthy, he saw no reason to change it.</p>

<p>The course was manageable, and despite his growing fortune, Ross wasn&#x27;t particularly interested in academics.</p>

<p>For him, it was a box to check rather than a passion to pursue.</p>

<p>One evening, as the frost settled outside, Ross gathered his wives in the spacious living room of his luxurious home.</p>

<p>He had an announcement to make, and they all sensed it was something significant.</p>

<p>Clearing his throat, he addressed the group with a tone that commanded their full attention.</p>

<p>&quot;I want you all to know something,&quot; he began, pausing to gauge their reactions.</p>

<p>&quot;I&#x27;ve decided to marry again.&quot;</p>

<p>The room grew quiet, and then a wave of murmurs rippled through his wives.</p>

<p>Ross raised a hand to quiet them and continued, listing the names of the five women he intended to marry: Mari, Stephany, Jessica, Rose, and Ayla.</p>

<p>Each name seemed to spark its own ripple of surprise or intrigue among the gathered women.</p>

<p>He didn&#x27;t stop there. Ross had also approached Emma, a woman who had been a significant part of his life, to see if she would join his ever-growing harem.</p>

<p>However, Emma had steadfastly declined. Her reasoning was clear: she wanted to raise her daughter, Erica, in a stable and conventional environment.</p>

<p>Having a father figure with multiple wives didn&#x27;t fit into the world she wanted for Erica.</p>

<p>&quot;Ross,&quot; she had told him firmly, &quot;I can&#x27;t let Erica grow up in a home like that. She deserves stability, and I can&#x27;t give her that if I&#x27;m part of your marriage.&quot;</p>

<p>Emma offered him a compromise. In Erica&#x27;s eyes, Ross would remain a close and trusted friend.</p>

<p>Behind closed doors, however, they would continue as lovers. Ross accepted her terms without argument.</p>

<p>He knew he could have pressed the matter further—after all, his wealth and influence gave him considerable power.</p>

<p>But he chose not to.</p>

<p>He valued Emma too much to force her into a situation she wasn&#x27;t ready for.</p>

<p>Besides, their current arrangement suited him just fine. He had the intimacy he wanted, and Emma had her peace of mind.</p>

<p>Hazel, another significant woman in his life, had also been approached. But like Emma, she declined—though for different reasons.</p>

<p>&quot;It&#x27;s too soon,&quot; she explained. &quot;I want to wait a few more years before making things official. I need to think about my parents, too. It would be too much for them to find out that both Althea and I have fallen for the same man.&quot;</p>

<p>Ross respected her decision, even if it wasn&#x27;t what he wanted to hear.</p>

<p>Patience, after all, had its rewards, and he was confident Hazel would eventually join his family on her own terms.</p>

<p>By the end of January, Ross had officially married five new women, bringing the total number of his wives to 17.</p>

<p>There was no elaborate honeymoon—no extravagant trips or grand displays.</p>

<p>Instead, they celebrated privately in Parkland City, surrounded by the opulence Ross&#x27;s wealth afforded them.</p>

<p>The wedding night was a spectacle in itself. In the intimacy of his home, Ross and his wives came together to consummate their union, each moment steeped in passion and excess.</p>

<p>The scene was one of boundless indulgence, a testament to Ross&#x27;s growing power and the life he had built for himself.</p>

<p>As January gave way to February, Ross found himself at the center of a life most could only dream of.</p>

<p>He was wealthy, powerful, and surrounded by beautiful women who adored him.</p>

<p>Yet, even as his harem expanded, he remained keenly aware that not everything in life could be bought or forced—something Emma and Hazel had taught him well.</p>

<p>This February held something special for Ross—something he had carefully planned and anticipated.</p>

<p>It was finally time to take Jessica&#x27;s cherry.</p>

<p>Unlike some of his other wives, Jessica was untouched, and Ross, despite his indulgent lifestyle, wasn&#x27;t heartless enough to let such an intimate moment be witnessed by others.</p>

<p>He wanted this to be special for her, devoid of embarrassment, messy blood, or unnecessary pain in front of an audience.</p>

<p>To make the experience memorable, Ross decided to save her for last.</p>

<p>He planned an exclusive trip for just the two of them, ensuring they would have the privacy and intimacy needed for such a momentous occasion.</p>

<p>After some thought, he chose a beach setting—a tranquil, romantic backdrop where they could escape the demands of their everyday lives and focus solely on each other.</p>

<p>One weekend in February, Ross and Jessica left Parkland City and headed to a secluded beach resort.</p>

<p>The destination was breathtaking—white sands stretched endlessly under a golden sun, and the azure waves sparkled like liquid gemstones.</p>

<p>Their villa, perched just steps from the shore, was designed for romance, with luxurious amenities and sweeping views of the ocean.</p>

<p>As they settled in, Ross made sure Jessica felt at ease. He spent the day with her, indulging her every whim.</p>

<p>They walked along the shoreline, collected seashells, and laughed as the waves lapped at their feet.</p>

<p>By evening, the sky was ablaze with the colors of the setting sun, casting a warm, golden glow over the water.</p>

<p>She had been in a similar situation before—one that hadn&#x27;t ended well.</p>

<p>&quot;Does marriage prohibit you from making new friends? From talking to other men? If so, being married sounds like a rather spooky prospect,&quot; the man quipped, his playful tone laced with humor.</p>
