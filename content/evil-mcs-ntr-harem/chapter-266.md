<p>His expression was one of casual amusement, as if this was all some twisted game to him.</p>

<p>The masked man, who had said nothing this entire time, remained still, his presence as oppressive as the dark shadows surrounding them.</p>

<p>They said nothing. They did nothing. They simply watched her, unmoved by her distress, her pain, and her son’s suffering.</p>

<p>&quot;Call 911! PLEASE!&quot; Iris shouted, her voice sharp with panic as she sprang to her feet.</p>

<p>Her eyes darted from corner to corner, but there was nothing. No phone. No sign of any way to reach the outside world.</p>

<p>&quot;Karl! Help! Help! Help!&quot; she screamed again, her voice desperate and raw as she called for her husband. Her words echoed in the room, but there was no response.</p>

<p>The silence that followed her frantic cries only deepened her sense of isolation. No one came. Not a single sound from the outside, not a footstep from anyone who might answer her pleas.</p>

<p>She looked around the room, every inch of it now unfamiliar, foreign. The bed, the furniture, the decorations—all wrong. It was nothing like the comfort of her own home.</p>

<p>&quot;Where am I?&quot; Iris whispered to herself, her voice shaking with confusion and fear. She stumbled back, her breath coming in shallow gasps.</p>

<p>&quot;Where is this place?&quot;</p>

<p>It hit her then. She wasn’t in her own home. This wasn’t anywhere she recognized. The walls, the air, the smell—everything felt wrong.</p>

<p>She could almost feel the weight of the situation pressing down on her chest. Her mind raced, trying to piece things together, but it was all too overwhelming.</p>

<p>Frozen in place, she stood there for a few breaths, struggling to process what was happening, to make sense of the nightmare unfolding around her. And then, her instincts kicked in. She couldn’t stay here.</p>

<p>She had to get out. She had to find someone, anyone, to help.</p>

<p>Iris’s legs moved before she could even think. She rushed toward the door, her heart pounding in her ears. The cold metal of the door handle seemed like the only thing standing between her and escape.</p>

<p>She was not about to let him suffer because of these monsters.</p>

<p>Brandon’s hand shot out, trying to grab her, but she was too quick. She felt his fingers graze her skin as he missed, but she didn’t slow down. She couldn’t. There was no time to waste.</p>
