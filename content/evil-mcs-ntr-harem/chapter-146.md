<p>&quot;How did you do that?!&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;What just happened?&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;Ughhhh…&quot;</p>

<p>Nico and his men groaned, their voices tangled with shock and confusion.</p>

<p>The room that had once been filled with their mocking laughter was now heavy with fear and disbelief.</p>

<p>Moments ago, they were armed, confident, and in control. Now, they lay bound and helpless, as if the world itself had flipped in an instant.</p>

<p>All 17 of them, seasoned criminals, stared at Ross as if he were a ghost—a specter that defied their understanding.</p>

<p>Ross chuckled softly, his casual demeanor a stark contrast to the chaos around him.</p>

<p>&quot;It&#x27;s a secret,&quot; he said, his words laced with a playful mockery that only added to their frustration.</p>

<p>With an unhurried stride, Ross walked toward Maya, who was still strapped to the wheelchair.</p>

<p>She watched him approach, her heart pounding—not out of fear, but from a mix of relief and awe.</p>

<p>He looked so calm, so in control, as though the room full of enemies had been nothing more than a minor inconvenience.</p>

<p>Ross knelt beside her, his hands working swiftly to undo the ropes binding her to the chair.</p>

<p>The knots seemed to fall apart at his touch, as though even the ropes dared not resist him.</p>

<p>Then, with a light brush of his fingers, he touched the handcuffs on her wrists.</p>

<p>They fell away with a soft click, the metal clattering onto the floor.</p>

<p>Maya didn&#x27;t even notice the impossibility of it. She had no time to.</p>

<p>The moment she was free, she launched herself into Ross&#x27;s arms, her body colliding with his as if she had been holding back a flood of emotions.</p>

<p>&quot;I knew you&#x27;d come for me,&quot; she whispered, her voice trembling with a mixture of gratitude and raw emotion.</p>

<p>Tears streamed down her face, her sobs muffled as she buried her face in his shoulder.</p>

<p>Ross held her effortlessly, his arms steady as if she weighed nothing.</p>

<p>Her legs wrapped tightly around his hips, her grip on him unyielding.</p>

<p>&quot;Of course I came,&quot; he murmured, his voice steady and soothing.</p>

<p>&quot;How could I not come? You&#x27;re the only Maya in my life. Not even a million armies could keep me away from you.&quot;</p>

<p>His words pierced through the fear and uncertainty that had gripped her heart.</p>

<p>Ross tilted his head, pressing a gentle kiss to her tear-streaked cheek.</p>

<p>For a moment, the chaos around them seemed to fade away, leaving only the two of them in the quiet bubble of their embrace.</p>

<p>Maya clung to him tightly, as though letting go would mean losing him.</p>

<p>&quot;I was so scared,&quot; she admitted in a broken whisper. &quot;I thought—&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;You don&#x27;t have to think anymore,&quot; Ross interrupted softly. &quot;I&#x27;m here. I&#x27;ll always be here.&quot;</p>

<p>Enjoy exclusive adventures from empire</p>

<p>They stayed like that for a while, wrapped in each other&#x27;s arms.</p>

<p>Ross whispered soft reassurances, his calm voice a balm to Maya&#x27;s frayed nerves. Slowly, her trembling subsided, her breathing steadying.</p>

<p>When she finally loosened her grip and slid down from his arms, she glanced around the room, her gaze landing on the dozen women sprawled across the beds.</p>

<p>They were motionless, their bodies weak and lifeless. Maya&#x27;s chest tightened at the sight, her mind racing with thoughts of what they must have endured.</p>

<p>&quot;What about them?&quot; she asked, her voice tinged with concern. &quot;They look so weak... I can&#x27;t imagine what they&#x27;ve been through every night.&quot;</p>

<p>Ross followed her gaze, his expression softening.</p>

<p>&quot;Don&#x27;t worry,&quot; he said gently. &quot;They&#x27;re all sleeping right now.&quot;</p>

<p>Maya furrowed her brows, her eyes darting back to the women. She stepped closer to one of the beds, leaning down to check.</p>

<p>Sure enough, they were breathing deeply, some even snoring softly. Relief washed over her, but it was quickly replaced by curiosity.</p>

<p>&quot;How?&quot; she asked, turning back to Ross, her tone both skeptical and intrigued.</p>

<p>Ross gave her a small, almost sheepish smile.</p>

<p>&quot;It&#x27;s a new technology I hacked from overseas,&quot; he said with a shrug. &quot;You don&#x27;t need to worry about it.&quot;</p>

<p>Maya&#x27;s brow arched, her skepticism growing.</p>

<p>&quot;And that same &#x27;stolen technology&#x27; just happens to knock out, tie up, and handcuff 17 armed men in a matter of seconds?&quot; she asked, folding her arms across her chest.</p>

<p>Ross&#x27;s smirk widened, unfazed by her disbelief. &quot;What can I say? It&#x27;s pretty useful tech. But trust me, the less you know, the safer you&#x27;ll be.&quot;</p>

<p>Maya opened her mouth to protest but stopped when Ross&#x27;s expression shifted.</p>

<p>He turned away from her, his gaze locking onto the gangsters still sprawled on the floor.</p>

<p>The playful glint in his eyes vanished, replaced by a cold, calculating intensity.</p>

<p>&quot;What should we do with them?&quot; Ross murmured, his voice low and dangerous.</p>

<p>Maya followed his gaze, her eyes settling on the defeated men. They squirmed under his unrelenting stare, their earlier bravado shattered.</p>

<p>For the first time since Ross entered the room, they truly understood the depth of their mistake.</p>

<p>&quot;They don&#x27;t deserve mercy,&quot; Maya said, surprising herself with the steel in her voice.</p>

<p>She thought of the women on the beds, the horrors these men had inflicted, and felt a surge of anger.</p>

<p>Ross tilted his head slightly, as though considering her words. Then, without a word, he stepped forward, his movements as silent as a predator&#x27;s.</p>

<p>The air in the room grew heavier, a suffocating tension filling the space.</p>

<p>&quot;Let me make one thing clear,&quot; Ross said, his voice slicing through the silence like a blade. &quot;You don&#x27;t get to walk away from this. Not unscathed. And not alive.&quot;</p>

<p>The gangsters recoiled, their fear palpable. For the first time, Nico—once so cocky and self-assured—looked genuinely afraid.</p>

<p>&quot;You can wait outside, Maya,&quot; Ross said calmly, his voice even as he approached the gangsters. He didn&#x27;t even glance back, already focused on the task at hand.</p>

<p>He knew exactly what she would say.</p>

<p>&quot;No,&quot; Maya replied, her voice firm, though she felt a slight tremor in her chest. She straightened in her seat, refusing to look away.</p>

<p>&quot;I&#x27;ll stay. It&#x27;s not like I haven&#x27;t seen you work before.&quot;</p>
