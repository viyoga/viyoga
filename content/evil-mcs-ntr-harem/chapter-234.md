<p>Thud!</p>

<p>The two Japanese women were tossed carefully onto the massive bed.</p>

<p>The room, filled with expensive furniture, spoke of extravagance.</p>

<p>Yet, in the grand scheme of Ross&#x27;s home, this was merely the basement—a testament to the unimaginable scale of his mansion.</p>

<p>A breath later, Brandon vanished as though he&#x27;d never existed.</p>

<p>Hours passed before the women stirred, awakened by the aroma of freshly cooked food.</p>

<p>Mari opened her eyes first, wincing as a pounding headache assaulted her senses.</p>

<p>She rubbed her temples and then the back of her neck, which throbbed painfully—a cruel reminder of what had happened.</p>

<p>The last thing she remembered was a man in a demon mask before everything went black.</p>

<p>Glancing to her side, Mari noticed she wasn&#x27;t alone.</p>

<p>&quot;Boss! Are you okay?&quot; she asked in Japanese, her voice shaky.</p>

<p>Reina groaned, slowly coming to.</p>

<p>The two exchanged fragmented accounts of their experiences.</p>

<p>Reina described the surreal encounter with the masked man, and their shared fear deepened.</p>

<p>His inhuman abilities left them with only one explanation: advanced warfare technology.</p>

<p>This confirmed their growing suspicion—Ross Oakley was far more than a young man collecting beautiful women.</p>

<p>They attempted to escape, but every door and window was sealed tight.</p>

<p>After an hour of futile struggle, they slumped back, exhausted.</p>

<p>&quot;What do we do now, Boss?&quot; Mari asked, her voice laced with steel bravery.</p>

<p>&quot;We eat and conserve our energy,&quot; Reina said with a resigned sigh before digging into the food.</p>

<p>&quot;But what if it&#x27;s poisoned?&quot; Mari hesitated, her hand hovering over a plate.</p>

<p>&quot;I doubt someone like Ross Oakley would kill us like this—at least not yet,&quot; Reina replied between bites. &quot;If we&#x27;re alive, it&#x27;s for a reason.&quot;</p>

<p>Mari&#x27;s eyes widened.</p>

<p>&quot;You mean… he&#x27;s going to—&quot; She stopped, her voice trembling. The thought alone sent a shiver through her.</p>

<p>She had faced the horrors of combat, but this—this was a fear unlike any other.</p>

<p>For a moment, Mari ate cautiously, her appetite dampened by dread.</p>

<p>But Reina&#x27;s determination spurred her on. If survival required strength, she would need every ounce of it.</p>

<p>Minutes later, both women ate in silence, steeling themselves for whatever awaited.</p>

<p>A full day had passed since their captivity began, and the suffocating weight of uncertainty had only grown heavier.</p>

<p>By the time night fell again, the women&#x27;s nerves were frayed, their minds racing with questions and fears.</p>

<p>When the clock struck midnight, the oppressive silence was broken by the sound of the door unlocking.</p>

<p>The heavy click echoed through the room, and the door creaked open slowly.</p>

<p>Ross stepped inside, his confident stride a stark contrast to the dread that hung in the air.</p>

<p>But he was not greeted with silence or submission.</p>

<p>Bang! Bang!</p>

<p>The moment he crossed the threshold, Mari and Reina sprang into action.</p>

<p>Mari, crouched low, swung the leg of a table with all her strength, aiming directly for his groin.</p>

<p>At the same time, Reina lunged from the side, smashing a chair down onto his head with a resounding crack.</p>

<p>Ding! Ding!</p>

<p>Ross didn&#x27;t flinch. He didn&#x27;t move or raise a hand to defend himself.</p>

<p>He simply stood there, letting the makeshift weapons collide with his body.</p>

<p>The blows, meant to incapacitate, seemed to bounce off him like pebbles against steel.</p>

<p>Mari and Reina stumbled back, their weapons slipping from their hands.</p>

<p>Their faces paled, and their eyes widened in disbelief.</p>

<p>&quot;How—?&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;What are you?&quot; they blurted out, their voices trembling as they instinctively retreated, putting distance between themselves and Ross.</p>

<p>Ross tilted his head, a smirk playing on his lips. His calm, almost amused expression only deepened their fear.</p>

<p>&quot;Me?&quot; he said, his tone casual, almost mocking.</p>

<p>&quot;I&#x27;m your new master. That&#x27;s what I am.&quot;</p>

<p>Before they could react, Ross raised his hand and flicked his fingers.</p>

<p>&quot;Ahhhhhhh!&quot;</p>

<p>Both women screamed in unison as an invisible force seemed to pierce their very cores.</p>

<p>They crumpled to the floor, clutching their stomachs as a burning sensation spread through them.</p>

<p>The pain was unbearable, searing into their navels as if they were being branded.</p>

<p>When the agony finally subsided, they looked down, their breathing ragged.</p>

<p>There, just above their belly buttons, was a tattoo—a small but intricate eternity symbol, glowing faintly before fading into their skin.</p>

<p>Mari and Reina exchanged panicked glances, their minds racing.</p>

<p>&quot;What is this?&quot; Mari gasped, her voice trembling.</p>

<p>&quot;It&#x27;s a mark… His mark,&quot; Reina whispered, horror lacing her words.</p>

<p>Their hands hovered over the tattoos, as though trying to erase what had just happened.</p>

<p>But the marks didn&#x27;t budge, a permanent reminder of the nightmare unfolding.</p>

<p>&quot;This isn&#x27;t possible…&quot; Mari muttered, her voice breaking.</p>

<p>&quot;What are you? A devil?&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;Oni! Akuma!&quot; Reina hissed, her voice sharp with fear.</p>

<p>The words tumbled out of their mouths as if the labels alone could explain what they were witnessing.</p>

<p>Ross chuckled, the sound low and sinister.</p>

<p>&quot;Devil? Oni? You think too lowly of me,&quot; he said, taking a step closer.</p>

<p>Before they could scramble away, another unseen force wrapped around their bodies.</p>

<p>They were lifted into the air, their limbs immobilized, and floated helplessly toward the massive bed.</p>

<p>The invisible grip threw them down with enough force to make the mattress groan under the impact.</p>

<p>Panic consumed them as the realization of what might come next dawned.</p>

<p>They weren&#x27;t naive.</p>

<p>The smirk on Ross&#x27;s face, the inhuman strength he had displayed—it all pointed to one horrifying conclusion.</p>

<p>&quot;No… Please, no…&quot; Mari whispered, her voice barely audible.</p>

<p>Reina said nothing, but her clenched fists and trembling form betrayed her terror.</p>

<p>Ross stood at the edge of the bed, his gaze predatory, his every move deliberate.</p>

<p>He was in no rush, savoring their fear.</p>

<p>Just as the tension reached its breaking point, the door behind him burst open with a soft entrance announcing the presence of the newcomer.</p>

<p>&quot;Oh dear, I do apologize for the intrusion, Ross. I must have startled you. I hadn&#x27;t realized you were entertaining guests. Forgive my unexpected arrival. I was quite eager to speak with you, but perhaps this is also the most opportune moment for what I have in mind.&quot; The woman said and then she closed the door behind her.</p>
