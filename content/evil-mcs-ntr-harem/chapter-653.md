<p>Sasha swallowed, hard. He was close enough now that she could smell the subtle spice of his cologne, mixed with something rawer—like heat and danger.</p>

<p>&quot;You came here because you wanted to know what kind of man I am,&quot; he continued. &quot;So let me tell you: I&#x27;m the kind of man who doesn&#x27;t just take what he wants. I make you want to give it.&quot;</p>

<p>Sasha tried to steady her breathing, but the weight of his words wrapped around her, thick and warm like smoke.</p>

<p>&quot;And what exactly do you want, Ross?&quot; she asked, her voice barely above a whisper.</p>

<p>He didn&#x27;t hesitate. &quot;Tonight? You. In every way a woman can be had. Not just the body, Sasha—I want your surrender. I want to see how far that fire of yours can burn before you melt in my hands.&quot;</p>

<p>She gasped softly, involuntarily. Her spine stiffened and her breath caught in her throat.</p>

<p>For a brief moment, she considered throwing her wine in his face. For another, longer moment, she considered asking him what hotel he had in mind.</p>

<p>Instead, she sat there, heart pounding, skin flushed, and eyes locked on his.</p>

<p>Neither of them spoke.</p>

<p>They didn&#x27;t need to.</p>

<p>The tension between them had spoken volumes.</p>

<p>&quot;You&#x27;re delusional,&quot; Sasha said, her voice steady despite the rapid thumping of her heart. She could feel her pulse in her neck, but she refused to show weakness.</p>

<p>&quot;I&#x27;m sorry to let you know that have a boyfriend already, so that&#x27;s not going to happen.&quot;</p>

<p>Ross&#x27;s smile didn&#x27;t waver. If anything, it deepened, like a wolf recognizing a challenge.</p>

<p>&quot;Something tells me you don&#x27;t like your boyfriend very much,&quot; he said casually, his words wrapped in a knowing tone.</p>

<p>&quot;Because if you did, you wouldn&#x27;t have stayed so… pure and untouched until tonight.&quot;</p>

<p>The implication of his words hit her like a cold slap. He didn&#x27;t even flinch.</p>

<p>He simply watched her, his gaze intense, a quiet confidence radiating from him. The audacity of it left her speechless for a moment, but her heart raced even faster.</p>

<p>How could he know something so personal? Something no one had ever guessed before?</p>

<p>&quot;How do you know that?&quot; Sasha asked, almost incredulously.</p>

<p>Her eyebrows furrowed in disbelief.</p>

<p>There was no way he could have dug into her life so thoroughly.</p>

<p>Was it some sort of intuition? Or was he the type to weave the truth into his charm?</p>

<p>Ross leaned back, hands loosely clasped together, as if he was savoring her reaction. &quot;It&#x27;s a secret,&quot; he replied, a smile playing at the corner of his lips.</p>

<p>&quot;But don&#x27;t worry, after you get to be mine, you&#x27;ll be privy to every little secret I may have, Sasha.&quot;</p>

<p>The words &quot;after you get to be mine&quot; hung in the air like an unspoken challenge, one that clawed at her pride and tested her resolve.</p>

<p>His gaze was direct, powerful, and the weight of it made her pulse quicken.</p>

<p>Sasha&#x27;s expression faltered for a split second. She had the urge to stand up, to walk away, to get some space—anything to distance herself from this dangerously intoxicating man.</p>

<p>But she knew he was watching. He would read every movement. He would see it as her surrender, a retreat before the inevitable, and that thought alone made her grit her teeth.</p>

<p>&quot;Cute,&quot; she said with a sharp laugh, her lips curling into a defiant smile. &quot;But not cute enough to get me.&quot;</p>

<p>Ross&#x27;s smile only deepened, but it wasn&#x27;t playful anymore.</p>

<p>There was something sharper in it now, as if the stakes of this little game had just been raised.</p>

<p>&quot;We&#x27;ll see about that,&quot; he said, his voice low and dripping with promise.</p>

<p>Sasha&#x27;s mind was racing.</p>

<p>She thought about how easily Ross could have pieced together her life.</p>

<p>A man like him—rich, powerful, meticulous—could uncover anything with just a few phone calls, a little money, a bit of charm.</p>

<p>He probably knew everything about her, from the day she was born to the smallest, most intimate details of her life.</p>

<p>The idea of him having that kind of power over her made her skin crawl.</p>

<p>She didn&#x27;t like being vulnerable, didn&#x27;t like the idea that anyone could know so much about her—especially someone like Ross.</p>

<p>The impulse to leave was stronger now, almost overwhelming.</p>

<p>She could get up, walk out of the restaurant, and put an end to this.</p>

<p>He was playing a dangerous game, and she wasn&#x27;t sure if she wanted to stay in it.</p>

<p>But then she met his eyes once again, and she knew, deep down, that he wasn&#x27;t just observing her—he was waiting for her to act.</p>

<p>He knew exactly what she was thinking.</p>

<p>And worse, he probably already knew she would hesitate.</p>

<p>He would savor the moment when she gave in to her curiosity, her desire, or her pride.</p>

<p>Walking away would only make it worse for her.</p>

<p>Sasha&#x27;s grip tightened around her glass, but she didn&#x27;t flinch. She forced a smile, though it was harder to keep up the facade.</p>

<p>&quot;Nice try, Ross. But I&#x27;m not the kind of woman who&#x27;s easily controlled.&quot;</p>

<p>His smile didn&#x27;t falter; in fact, it only widened, like a predator whose prey had just revealed a weakness.</p>

<p>&quot;Neither am I,&quot; he whispered, his voice low enough to send a shiver down her spine.</p>

<p>Sasha&#x27;s breath caught, and for a brief moment, she almost lost herself in the tension that wrapped around them like a noose.</p>

<p>She could feel the heat rising between them, tangible and undeniable.</p>

<p>The way his gaze roamed her face, lingering on her lips, her eyes, her neck—it was a power play, and he knew it.</p>

<p>Every move he made, every word he said, was calculated to break her down bit by bit.</p>

<p>And yet, Sasha held her ground. She wasn&#x27;t going to let him have that satisfaction. Not yet.</p>

<p>This was a game, a dangerous one, but she wasn&#x27;t about to let him think she would be the one to fold.</p>

<p>Sasha would not be defeated in a game she chose to play in the first place.</p>
