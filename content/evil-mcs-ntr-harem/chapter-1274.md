<p>Morning arrived, but Hidden River Fortress was anything but peaceful.</p>

<p>The entire fortress had fallen into a state of chaos.</p>

<p>From the largest clans to the smallest street vendors, everyone was talking about only one thing.</p>

<p>Ross Oakley and his mysterious group.</p>

<p>The shocking events of the previous night had spread throughout the fortress like wildfire.</p>

<p>No matter where one went, discussions could be heard. Tea houses were packed. Markets were crowded.</p>

<p>Even cultivators who normally secluded themselves had come out to gather information.</p>

<p>Unfortunately, nobody seemed to know the truth. Rumors multiplied with every passing hour.</p>

<p>&quot;They say the Ji Clan was wiped out last night.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;I heard the same thing.&quot;</p>

<p>A nearby cultivator lowered his voice.</p>

<p>&quot;But where are the bodies? If an entire clan was slaughtered, there should be corpses everywhere.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;Exactly!&quot;</p>

<p>Another person nodded repeatedly.</p>

<p>&quot;We searched around the Ji Clan’s territory this morning. The buildings are gone. The people are gone. Even traces of battle are gone.&quot;</p>

<p>The listeners felt chills run down their spines. One of them swallowed nervously.</p>

<p>&quot;The dead don’t just magically disappear.&quot;</p>

<p>The group immediately fell silent. After a moment, someone forced out a laugh.</p>

<p>&quot;If you’re so curious, why don’t you ask Master Ross Oakley yourself?&quot;</p>

<p>The speaker instantly turned pale.</p>

<p>&quot;Do you think I’m a fool?&quot;</p>

<p>He hurriedly shook his head.</p>

<p>&quot;I still want to live! I love my life very much!&quot;</p>

<p>Everyone nearby immediately agreed.</p>

<p>The mere mention of Ross’s name caused many people to instinctively lower their voices.</p>

<p>Regardless of what had truly happened, one fact was certain. The Ji Clan no longer existed.</p>

<p>And Ross Oakley was the last person anyone wanted to offend.</p>

<p>A different group of cultivators nearby had their own discussion.</p>

<p>&quot;I heard that many of Master Ross’s followers can’t even speak our language.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;Really?&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;Of course. One of my cousins witnessed it personally. They were speaking a completely different language among themselves.&quot;</p>

<p>The crowd became intrigued.</p>

<p>&quot;A different language?&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;Could they be foreigners from another continent?&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;Or perhaps another realm?&quot;</p>

<p>A middle-aged cultivator suddenly whispered a terrifying possibility.</p>

<p>&quot;Could they be barbarians?&quot;</p>

<p>The surrounding people immediately sucked in cold breaths.</p>

<p>Nobody knew whether that guess was true or not.</p>

<p>However, in times of uncertainty, fear often gave birth to even more frightening rumors.</p>

<p>Soon enough, the story spread throughout the fortress. Some claimed Ross had descended from the heavens.</p>

<p>Others insisted he was an ancient monster who had awakened after sleeping for millions of years.</p>

<p>A few even claimed he was the reincarnation of an immortal emperor.</p>

<p>Every retelling became more outrageous than the last. Yet strangely, no one dared dismiss any possibility.</p>

<p>After all, what ordinary person could erase an entire great clan overnight?</p>

<p>What ordinary person could make thousands of cultivators disappear without leaving behind a single corpse?</p>

<p>The unknown was often far more frightening than the truth.</p>

<p>As the rumors continued to spread, panic gradually took hold of Hidden River Fortress.</p>

<p>Many families packed their belongings before sunrise. Merchants hurriedly closed their businesses.</p>

<p>Entire caravans could be seen leaving through the city gates.</p>

<p>Thousands of people abandoned the fortress overnight. Some feared Ross would target another clan next.</p>

<p>Others worried that a war was about to erupt.</p>

<p>Many simply wanted to put as much distance as possible between themselves and the terrifying man whose name now dominated every conversation.</p>

<p>By midday, the roads leading away from Hidden River Fortress were filled with fleeing cultivators and mortals alike.</p>

<p>The once-bustling city had become noticeably quieter. Fear hung over the fortress like a dark cloud.</p>

<p>And at the center of it all was a single name.</p>

<p>Ross Oakley.</p>

<p>A man whom nobody truly understood. Which only made him even more terrifying.</p>

<p>The entire Hidden River Fortress seemed to be holding its breath.</p>

<p>Everyone was waiting for someone to make the first move.</p>

<p>Countless eyes were secretly focused on the enormous castle that Ross Oakley had seized the previous day.</p>

<p>Whether they were clan leaders, merchants, cultivators, or ordinary citizens, all of them wanted to know what would happen next.</p>

<p>The spies were especially busy. Many had occupied rooftops before dawn.</p>

<p>Others hid inside nearby buildings or disguised themselves as street vendors.</p>

<p>Some even used precious observation techniques to monitor the castle from afar.</p>

<p>Not a single one of them dared to relax.</p>

<p>They feared that the moment they looked away, they might miss something important.</p>

<p>The fortress was unusually quiet. Everyone was waiting.</p>

<p>Waiting for a reaction. Waiting for a confrontation.</p>

<p>Waiting for someone brave enough to approach the terrifying newcomer known as Ross Oakley.</p>

<p>When noon finally arrived, the waiting came to an end. A commotion suddenly erupted near the city gates.</p>

<p>&quot;Look!&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;That’s General Lei Jun’s banner!&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;He finally came!&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;General Lei Jun is here for Ross Oakley!&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;This is going to be huge!&quot;</p>

<p>The spies almost shouted in excitement.</p>

<p>After waiting for an entire night and half a day, the main event had finally arrived.</p>

<p>A massive procession slowly advanced through the streets.</p>

<p>At its center was a luxurious carriage adorned with golden decorations and military insignias.</p>

<p>Behind it marched ten thousand soldiers clad in brilliant golden armor.</p>

<p>The sight alone was enough to make spectators gasp. These men were not ordinary soldiers.</p>

<p>They were General Lei Jun’s personal guards. The legendary Golden Armor Legion.</p>

<p>Every member had survived countless battles. Every member had killed enemies upon the battlefield.</p>

<p>Their movements were perfectly synchronized, their footsteps sounding like thunder as they advanced through the city.</p>

<p>An overwhelming bloodthirsty aura radiated from the legion.</p>

<p>It was the aura of men who had walked through mountains of corpses and seas of blood.</p>

<p>Their iron will and thirst for battle were evident to anyone who looked at them.</p>

<p>Many cultivators watching from afar felt their hearts tremble.</p>

<p>Such an elite force was more than enough to destroy most clans within the fortress.</p>

<p>Soon, the procession arrived before Ross Oakley’s castle.</p>

<p>The streets around the area were already packed with hidden observers.</p>

<p>Nobody dared approach too closely, but countless pairs of eyes watched from every possible angle.</p>
