<p>&quot;La... la... la...&quot; Ross sang and whistled cheerfully as he stirred soup and chunks of meat in a large pot.</p>

<p>Steam rose into the air, carrying a rich, comforting smell that should have eased anyone’s nerves.</p>

<p>He bobbed his head up and down to the tune he made up on the spot, completely absorbed in his own little world.</p>

<p>His movements were relaxed, almost carefree, and his good mood radiated outward.</p>

<p>Anyone else would have found it hard not to smile at the sight.</p>

<p>His happiness was simple, genuine, and utterly infectious.</p>

<p>But Sebastian felt none of it.</p>

<p>He sat nearby, his jaw clenched, eyes fixed on Ross’s back.</p>

<p>Envy churned in his chest, hot and bitter, mixing with an anger he could neither vent nor suppress.</p>

<p>Every laugh, every careless note Ross hummed felt like a quiet provocation.</p>

<p>How can he be so happy? Sebastian thought grimly.</p>

<p>&quot;It’s not his fault,&quot; Bella said softly from behind him, her voice cautious, as if she were afraid even her words might ignite him further.</p>

<p>Sebastian let out a slow, harsh breath.</p>

<p>&quot;I know,&quot; he said, his tone sharp despite himself. &quot;And that’s exactly what makes it unbearable. It’s nobody’s fault. Not his. Not yours.&quot; His hands curled into fists. &quot;I should be able to accept that. I really should.&quot;</p>

<p>He laughed bitterly, the sound empty. &quot;But I can’t. No matter how hard I try, the anger just keeps coming back.&quot;</p>

<p>He turned to look at Bella, pain flashing across his face. &quot;Now I can’t even touch you anymore. My own wife.&quot; His words came out like poison, spat from a wound that refused to close.</p>

<p>Bella’s lips parted, but no sound came out.</p>

<p>She looked away, her expression conflicted, her heart heavy with guilt and helplessness.</p>

<p>She knew Sebastian wasn’t truly blaming her—but that didn’t make the weight any easier to bear.</p>

<p>She stood there in silence, her mind racing as she searched desperately for a solution, any path that might ease the tension between them and pull them out of this suffocating situation.</p>

<p>In the end, only one thought remained.</p>

<p>&quot;I think we should go back to the base,&quot; Bella said quietly, breaking the silence at last.</p>

<p>&quot;...&quot; Sebastian stood motionless in the dim light of the cave, the only sound was Ross’ steaming pot.</p>

<p>He couldn’t look at Bella.</p>

<p>Not because he hated her—not exactly—but because every time his eyes flicked toward her, the images came unbidden: her thighs locked around another man’s hips, her head thrown back, mouth open in that raw, animal sound of ecstasy he’d never pulled from her.</p>

<p>He could already hear the base when they walked in tomorrow.</p>

<p>The mess hall would go quiet for half a second when he walked through the door, then the murmurs would start—low at first, then bolder.</p>

<p>&quot;Poor bastard.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;Couldn’t keep her satisfied.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;Heard she was screaming so loud the dumb zombies called it in.&quot; Someone would make a joke about needing a bigger dick to keep a woman like Bella happy.</p>

<p>Someone else would laugh too loud.</p>

<p>And every single one of them would picture it: her legs spread wide on some stranger’s cot, nails raking down a back that wasn’t his, begging for more, harder, deeper—while Sebastian lay alone in their quarters, staring at the ceiling, pretending he didn’t know.</p>

<p>His stomach twisted so violently he tasted bile.</p>

<p>Bella watched the whole collapse play out across his face. She guessed what that look meant.</p>

<p>&quot;You’re really doing this, aren’t you?&quot; she said, voice low and venomous. &quot;You’re standing there picturing them laughing at you. That’s what’s eating you alive right now, isn’t it? Not me. Not what I did. Just... your ego.&quot;</p>

<p>Sebastian’s jaw flexed.</p>

<p>He wanted to shout that it wasn’t true, that he cared about her, about their marriage, about everything—but the lie tasted worse than the bile.</p>

<p>He did care. Somewhere under the shame there was still love, or at least the ghost of it.</p>

<p>But right now the louder voice in his head was screaming that he would never live this down.</p>

<p>Never walk past the mess hall without someone making a cuckold gesture behind his back.</p>

<p>Never look any of them in the eye again without seeing pity or mockery.</p>

<p>&quot;I’m not—&quot; he started, then stopped. His throat felt full of gravel. &quot;They’ll never let it go, Bella. You know how they are. Once it’s out, it’s out. Forever.&quot;</p>

<p>She barked a short, humorless laugh that echoed off the bare concrete walls.</p>

<p>&quot;So that’s the priority? Your reputation? The big tough soldier can’t handle a little gossip?&quot; She took a step closer, boots scuffing grit.</p>

<p>&quot;You think I wanted this? You think I planned to fuck someone else just to hurt you?&quot;</p>

<p>Her words landed like punches. Each one heavier than the last.</p>

<p>Sebastian finally lifted his gaze.</p>

<p>Her eyes were bright with anger and something worse—disappointment.</p>

<p>Not in him, but in what they’d become.</p>

<p>&quot;I’m sorry,&quot; he said quietly. It sounded pathetic even to him.</p>

<p>&quot;It’s done. I fucked another man. Another man fucked me. What we need now is to move on.&quot; She shrugged, the motion sharp. He winced. Hard.</p>

<p>The silence stretched, thick and suffocating.</p>

<p>Sebastian dragged a hand over his face. His palm came away damp.</p>

<p>He didn’t know when he’d started sweating.</p>

<p>He thought about the ring on her finger, the one he’d slipped on her hand under floodlights and gentle music, promising forever.</p>

<p>And he thought about the men who would look at him tomorrow and know he’d failed at the one thing a husband was supposed to do.</p>

<p>He let out a long, ragged breath that felt like it came from somewhere near his spine.</p>

<p>&quot;Okay,&quot; he said at last. The word scraped his throat raw. &quot;Let’s go back.&quot;</p>

<p>Bella studied him for a long second. No triumph in her expression. Only a tired kind of resignation.</p>

<p>Neither of them spoke again, the heavy silence pressing down on them, until Ross’s voice broke it to announce that the food was ready.</p>
