<p>One month later, Ross Oakley’s ambitious plans finally came to fruition.</p>

<p>Oakleys Supermarket had become a global powerhouse, with branches in every major city and even in some of the most remote and dangerous regions of the world.</p>

<p>It wasn’t just a chain of supermarkets anymore—it was a symbol of Ross’s growing influence and power.</p>

<p>But with success came enemies. In the shadows, those who felt threatened by Ross’s meteoric rise began plotting their retaliation.</p>

<p>What they encountered next was a whole nother story.</p>

<p>When dawn broke, the streets told a different story.</p>

<p>The lifeless bodies of the entire gang were discovered floating in the river, their grisly demise serving as a silent warning to anyone else who dared cross Oakleys Supermarket.</p>

<p>This wasn’t an isolated incident. Similar events began unfolding across the globe.</p>

<p>Criminals and outlaws who thought they could bully or extort Ross’s establishments found themselves meeting untimely—and often brutal—ends.</p>

<p>Government intelligence agencies soon took notice of the alarming pattern and launched investigations into the mysterious deaths.</p>

<p>However, no concrete evidence could be traced back to Ross or his organization.</p>

<p>The operations were clean, precise, and left no tangible leads for investigators to follow.</p>

<p>Still, even the dullest minds in law enforcement circles could see the connection: the string of murders was undeniably tied to Oakleys Supermarket.</p>

<p>The retaliation didn’t stop with criminals.</p>

<p>Prominent businessmen, furious at the massive dent Oakleys Supermarket had made in their profits, tried more underhanded methods.</p>

<p>Some attempted to sabotage Ross’s operations, while others went as far as plotting his kidnapping or assassination.</p>

<p>Yet, one by one, they met the same fate.</p>

<p>Their bodies were found dumped in rivers, alleys, or abandoned warehouses, as if to send a clear message to the world: Ross Oakley was untouchable.</p>

<p>The mounting deaths sent ripples of fear across the business world.</p>

<p>Whispers of Ross’s shadowy reach began to spread like wildfire.</p>

<p>Was it his doing? Did he have a secret network of enforcers ensuring his supremacy?</p>

<p>No one could say for sure, but one thing became clear: challenging Ross Oakley came with a steep price.</p>

<p>As the fear around his name grew, so did his reputation.</p>

<p>Across the globe, nations and their intelligence agencies started taking Ross seriously.</p>

<p>Every major power began monitoring his actions, placing him under a metaphorical microscope.</p>

<p>What they saw only deepened the intrigue—Ross Oakley wasn’t just a businessman.</p>

<p>He was a force reshaping the world in ways no one could fully understand.</p>

<p>Ross, however, remained unfazed by the attention.</p>

<p>He allowed the chaos to unfold, knowing full well that the fear and mystery surrounding his name would only serve to strengthen his position.</p>

<p>Every calculated move was part of a much larger plan, one that he intended to see through to the end.</p>

<p>&quot;Difficult,&quot; the superior replied, leaning back in his chair.</p>

<p>&quot;If Ross is truly behind these mass murders, he’s doing the world a favor. The only ones dying are scum who should have been wiped out ten years ago.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;What about justice, sir? Doesn’t he need to answer for his crimes? What he’s doing is nothing more than vigilante justice,&quot; the agent argued.</p>

<p>&quot;Justice takes time, agent. With all the bureaucracy and red tape we have to wade through just to get a case on the priority list, we’d be dead long before seeing real justice done in today’s world,&quot; the superior said, his tone resigned.</p>

<p>&quot;So, we do nothing then?&quot;</p>

<p>The meeting ended there, but whispers of similar discussions echoed across intelligence agencies worldwide.</p>

<p>Ross never turned anyone away, and every day, a steady crowd gathered at this branch of Oakleys Supermarket—a place dedicated to helping those in desperate need.</p>

<p>Ross wasn’t stingy with his assistance either; everyone was given a fair chance to present their case and seek support from his staff.</p>
