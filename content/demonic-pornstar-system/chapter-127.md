<p>- Tessa: Dear Kaiden, I’m Tessa, guild leader of Nova Circuit. A scout of ours saw the might your team displayed today, and I must say, it left quite an impression. I’d like to offer you a proposition. Would you be able to meet in person shortly?</p>

<p>Tessa was a B-tier awakened with an estimated level of 55-70, with the class Arcane Archer. ’Arcane’ was a highly favored subtype of an archetype; it referred to their ability to fight with energy-based attacks, explosions, lasers, and the like.</p>
<p>David also had the Arcane subtype, though he was an Archmage and not an Archer, making his class superior—hence his A-tier assessment.</p>
<p>As for the guild she led, Nova Circuit, it was a low-tier one. Guilds could be categorized into three broad tiers: low, mid, and high.</p>
<p>What separated the tiers was the quality of their members and their ability to clear dungeons effectively. Funding, prestige, and leadership also played pivotal roles in determining a guild’s tier.</p>
<p>Nova Circuit used to be mid-tier, but they fell back to low-tier. Kaiden didn’t know what had happened as the info he found on them didn’t go into specifics, but a lot of their strongest members decided to leave and join another guild. Perhaps internal conflict or mismanagement had caused the decline.</p>
