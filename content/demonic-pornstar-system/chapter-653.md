<p>The day truly began soon afterward.</p>
<p>Kaiden’s group pushed into a zone they hadn’t touched yet, a wide alpine basin carved between two ridgelines where the monster density was supposed to be excellent and the kill-to-point ratio even better. The association’s intel had flagged it overnight as one of the highest-value areas still available at their engagement tier.</p>
<p>What the intel had not flagged was the group already farming it.</p>
<p>"Oh," Luna said flatly, stopping mid-step. "Wonderful."</p>
<p>Ashbound.</p>
<p>The guild occupied the western half of the basin in what could generously be described as a combat formation. Ash stood at the center, an S-tier awakened with the build of someone who spent as much time in the gym as on the battlefield, flanked by his three A-tier companions: Brittany, Stacy, and Trisha.</p>
<p>All sporting heavy makeup. All busty. All wearing combat gear that prioritized visibility over protection, because when your business model was streaming explicit content on every platform simultaneously, you dressed for the camera first and the monsters second.</p>
<p>Their rotation was sloppy. The spacing between their damage dealers was inconsistent, their pulls were messy, and Brittany nearly ate a Ridgehowler’s claw to the face because she was adjusting her chest plate mid-fight.</p>
<p>But they were here first.</p>
<p>Kaiden assessed the situation in about two seconds, then shrugged. "East side’s open. We’ll work that."</p>
<p>They moved without discussion.</p>
<p>Within minutes, the contrast was visible to anyone watching both streams.</p>
