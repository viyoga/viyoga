<p>GLOBAL AWAKENED NEWS: BREAKING</p>
<p>ECLIPSE: KAIDEN GREY REGISTERS NEW GUILD WITH VESPERA ASHBORN AS REGENT</p>
<p>In a development that has sent shockwaves through the guild landscape, Kaiden Grey - now confirmed as son of Vespera Ashborn and heir to the Ashborn lineage - has officially registered a new guild under the name "Eclipse."</p>
<p>The registration was filed in the early hours of the morning through formal Association channels. Listed as Guild Leader is Kaiden Grey himself. Vespera Ashborn, formerly co-leader of New Dawn alongside estranged husband Magnus Morvane, is listed as Guild Regent, a title that grants her full operational authority over guild affairs.</p>
<p>Sources close to the registration process indicate that the guild’s founding roster already includes dozens of former New Dawn personnel who departed the guild overnight following Vespera Ashborn’s exit. These individuals span multiple departments: legal counsel, strategic operations, logistics, combat personnel, and intelligence. The exact number of defectors has not been confirmed, but internal sources describe the departures as "significant enough to compromise several active New Dawn operations."</p>
<p>Eclipse’s formation comes just hours after last night’s explosive livestream, during which Magnus Morvane’s personal and professional conduct was laid bare by Natasha Volkov, revealed to be a former mistress of the guild leader. The stream also featured Kaiden Grey publicly identifying himself as an Ashborn child for the first time.</p>
<p>NOVA CIRCUIT DISSOLVED</p>
<p>In a related development, Nova Circuit, the guild led by B-tier Arcane Archer, Tessa Verain, has officially dissolved. All members are rumored to have been offered the choice to join Eclipse as full members or pursue other avenues, their contracts voided. No reports have surfaced of anyone electing to go the second route. Tessa herself is listed among Eclipse’s founding members.</p>
<p>Nova Circuit had long been known as the struggling, low-tier guild that was first to make a deal with Kaiden’s mercenary group.</p>
<p>RUNEWOVEN RESTRUCTURED AS SUBSIDIARY</p>
<p>Runewoven, the guild under whose banner Kaiden Grey has been competing, will continue to operate under its existing name but has been formally restructured as a subsidiary of Eclipse. Talia Runewoven, Runewoven’s guild leader, will retain command of the guild’s day-to-day affairs, with a renewed focus on equipment forging, weapon crafting, and material procurement.</p>
<p>Sources familiar with the arrangement describe Runewoven’s new role as Eclipse’s "smithing and equipment arm," a designation that preserves the guild’s identity and its founder’s legacy while integrating it into Eclipse’s broader structure.</p>
<p>WHAT THIS MEANS</p>
<p>Kaiden Grey enters the guild landscape as the head of a pre-assembled operation staffed by veteran professionals who defected from one of the three largest guilds in the country. With Vespera Ashborn at the helm as Regent, Eclipse possesses institutional knowledge, financial resources, and combat personnel that most guilds cannot dream of achieving.</p>
<p>For New Dawn, the losses are twofold. The departure of key personnel weakens internal operations at a time when the guild is already under Association investigation. The establishment of a rival guild led by the Ashborn heir and managed by the Ashborn matriarch transforms what was a family dispute into a direct competitive threat.</p>
<p>The Association has not yet commented on how Eclipse’s registration may affect the ongoing competition standings, though analysts expect a formal ruling soon.</p>
