<p>Kaiden stepped forward.</p>
<p>Behind him, five presences aligned without needing instruction. They had done this too many times already. Formation came naturally now, earned through blood, close calls, and immense trust in one another.</p>
<p>To his right, Luna’s storm aura crackled softly, lightning crawling along the edge of a blade that didn’t exist until she willed it into being. To his left, Calypso grinned.</p>
<p>Moonlight flared behind as Aria rolled her shoulders, crescent sigils orbiting her forearms in slow arcs. Bastet made her presence known with a soft purr, while Nyx began twirling her fingers as space magic responded to her call.</p>
<p>The ground ahead split as something massive pushed its way through broken terrain, stone and bone thrown aside as if they were nothing more than loose gravel. Then another. And another. Shapes emerged from the dust.</p>
<p>Huge silhouettes at first, followed by faster, leaner forms slipping through gaps with terrifying speed.</p>
<p>Kaiden narrowed his eyes.</p>
<p>"That’s not a normal horde."</p>
<p>The interface responded instantly, lines of data snapping into place as the Awakened Media Platform eagerly catalogued what it saw.</p>
<p>At the front, towering brutes advanced in a disciplined line, each step heavy enough to crack the dungeon floor.</p>
<p>[Colossid Brutalizer - Level 70]</p>
<p>Their bodies were slabs of layered muscle and natural armor, each one wielding crude but massive weapons grown from bone and mineral. They didn’t roar or charge blindly. They marched, shields raised, spacing perfect.</p>
<p>They looked akin to evolved trolls, monsterized further until they looked truly hideous and brutal.</p>
<p>Flanking them, shadows blurred.</p>
<p>Fast. Too fast.</p>
<p>[Skirmeshade Stalker - Level 68]</p>
<p>These were lean creatures with bladed forearms and elongated skulls that slipped along the sides of the formation, feet barely touching the ground. They moved in coordinated bursts, constantly repositioning, probing for weakness rather than committing too early.</p>
<p>And above...</p>
<p>Kaiden tilted his head as the air darkened.</p>
<p>Wings.</p>
<p>[Skyreaver Harrier - Level 69]</p>
<p>Sleek, manta-like flyers circled overhead, bioluminescent veins pulsing along their wings. They hadn’t dived yet. They waited, banking in wide arcs, clearly tracking signatures below and waiting for the right opportunity.</p>
<p>And, lastly, threading between all of them, like a nervous system made flesh...</p>
<p>[Chorusbound Savant - Level 70]</p>
<p>Tall, robed monstrosities with elongated limbs and glowing throats. Each movement they made sent subtle pulses through the horde. Kaiden felt it immediately... Mana harmonization. Buffs. Signals. Coordination.</p>
<p>They weren’t just monsters.</p>
<p>They were a proper unit.</p>
<p>Kaiden let out a slow breath. "I’ll be damned," he murmured, loud enough for the stream to catch. "Heavy vanguard, mobile flankers, aerial pressure, and battlefield controllers."</p>
<p>His gaze sharpened. "They’re covering each other’s weaknesses."</p>
<p>The horde surged forward as one.</p>
<p>The Colossid Brutalizers slammed their weapons into the ground in unison, sending a shockwave ripping across the battlefield. Skirmeshades exploded outward, splitting left and right, vanishing into debris and shadow. Above, the Skyreavers folded their wings and began their descent, while the Chorusbound raised their arms, mana threads snapping taut between every unit in the formation.</p>
<p>Aria exhaled, excitement bleeding into her voice. "They’re not only intelligent monsters capable of strategy and restraint, but also are made up of monsters that synergize well with one another... We’re dealing with monsters that came from a high-tier dungeon."</p>
<p>"Yes, B-tier at the very least," Nyx agreed.</p>
