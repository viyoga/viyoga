<p>That was where Vaelira rested.</p>
<p>As soon as her arm dropped, she turned and hurried away, footsteps quick and uneven as she put as much distance between herself and him as possible.</p>
<p>Kaiden followed the direction she indicated. The oppressive weight around him deepened with each step, the air itself seeming to brace for what would come next as he closed in on the warded space where Vaelira lay.</p>
<p>Tessa stood directly in front of the reinforced room, posture squared and feet planted as if she had decided this spot would be her line in the sand.</p>
<p>The black-haired guild leader usually carried herself with teasing ease and an ever-present grin, the kind of woman who defused tension by laughing first and thinking later. That expression was gone. Her jaw was tight, shoulders set, eyes sharp with expectation rather than surprise, as though she had known from the moment she heard what Vaelira did that Kaiden would eventually come walking down this corridor.</p>
<p>She took in his state in a single glance, the red distortion in his eyes, the pressure rolling off him hard enough to make the warding glyphs flicker. Her throat bobbed as she swallowed, courage forcing her one step forward despite every instinct screaming at her to move aside. "I’ll speak to Vaelira," Tessa said, voice steady only through effort. "Let me handle this, Kaiden."</p>
<p>Kaiden did not slow.</p>
<p>The surge hit like a physical impact, wrath flooding through his frame and reinforcing muscle, tendon, and bone with brutal clarity. His arm shot out and closed around Tessa’s throat, fingers locking with crushing force as he lifted and drove her back into the wall behind her.</p>
<p>Stone cracked under the force as her feet left the floor, legs kicking uselessly while her hands flew up to his wrist. She clawed at his grip, breath tearing in and out in broken gasps as panic finally reached her eyes.</p>
<p>Clearly, despite knowing Kaiden would be angry, the woman underestimated just how much.</p>
<p>What she didn’t quite realize was that not only did Kaiden see firsthand what a disgusting filth Maximilian was, but that he’d spent a lot of time observing Alexandra regain her joy of life, to smile and live again as was her human right that Maximilian spat on and nearly ruined forever.</p>
<p>To him, Alexandra was a precious member of his family, the best friend of his lovers, and someone he cared for deeply.</p>
<p>"You will speak to her?" Kaiden repeated Tessa’s words with a low, slow whisper full of hatred and spite. "I’ve heard you say that line one too many times."</p>
<p>Tessa’s shock bled into raw fear. This state of Kaiden was far beyond intimidation, far beyond anger; she couldn’t talk him down with humor, authority, or a voice of reason. She tried to answer and managed only a strangled sound before forcing the words through. "I’m- I’m sorry," she choked, fingers slipping against his skin as her strength faltered.</p>
<p>"Sorry?" Kaiden repeated, his grip tightening as the red in his eyes smeared into violent motion. "You are sorry?"</p>
<p>The corridor seemed to constrict around them as his presence intensified, healers further down the hall scrambling away without daring to look back. Kaiden leaned closer until their eyes were a mere inch away. "Make your decision right now, Tessa Verain. If you stand up for Vaelira one more fucking time, I will consider Nova Circuit to have declared war against me."</p>
<p>"What?! But-" Tessa tried to speak, desperation breaking through as her hands shook against his arm.</p>
<p>"I am done with you," Kaiden declared, cutting her off without raising his voice. "I am done with your incompetence to control your members, and I am done with your excuses. You will take your stance right now. Is that understood?"</p>
<p>Her answer caught in her throat along with his hand, eyes wide and burning as the weight of his words settled in.</p>
<p>Tessa’s eyes locked onto his.</p>
<p>At first, there was only fear there, raw and instinctive, the kind born from a body being denied air and options alike. Then, slowly, something else crept in. Understanding. Not relief, never that, but comprehension, cold and sinking, like realizing the ground beneath you has already given way and you’re only now noticing the fall.</p>
<p>She stopped fighting.</p>
<p>Her legs, which had been kicking in frantic bursts, went still. The tension bled out of her frame in uneven waves, arms dropping limp at her sides as if the strength had simply abandoned them. Her fingers no longer clawed at his wrist. They hung there, useless, trembling faintly.</p>
<p>Her resistance vanished altogether.</p>
<p>Defeat settled into her posture.</p>
<p>The weight of leadership, of responsibility, she had carried with bravado and jokes cracked under the pressure bearing down on her. This wasn’t a crisis she could spin. This wasn’t something she could smooth over with authority or charm. Looking into Kaiden’s eyes, she finally understood that he wasn’t posturing, wasn’t threatening for leverage, wasn’t raging blindly.</p>
<p>He was finished.</p>
<p>Finished compromising. Finished tolerating. Finished being managed.</p>
<p>This was the breaking point.</p>
<p>And she was ordered to make her stance.</p>
<p>Her gaze slid sideways, toward the reinforced door only a few meters away. The thick wards, the heavier glyphs, the space where Vaelira lay protected from the consequences of her own words, welcomed her sight. Tessa swallowed hard, throat working uselessly around the hand still crushing it, and when she spoke, it came out as a thin, breath-starved whisper.</p>
<p>"If you kill Vaelira," she rasped, voice barely carrying, "while everyone knows I stood right in front of her door... I’m finished as guild leader."</p>
<p>Her eyes returned to his, glossy and burning. "You’re giving me an impossible choice, Kaiden Grey. Either I make an enemy of you, or my guild crumbles..."</p>
<p>The words hung between them, fragile and heavy.</p>
<p>That was with a venomous hiss, Kaiden asked,</p>
<p>"Who said I will kill her?"</p>
<p>Tessa’s eyes widened in shock, the kind that hits when a narrative you’ve already resigned yourself to fractures without warning. Her breath stuttered, lungs finally dragging in air as the words sank in, her mind scrambling to realign with a reality she hadn’t considered.</p>
<p>Slowly. Minutely.</p>
<p>She nodded.</p>
<p>Her decision has been made.</p>
<p>The guild leader of Nova Circuit has officially sold a member of her guild out. She sealed Vaelira’s fate with a faint nod of her head.</p>
<p>Kaiden released her.</p>
<p>His hand fell away from her throat without ceremony, and Tessa dropped to the ground as if her strings had been cut, boots hitting the floor hard as she collapsed against the wall and slid down it. She pulled her knees to her chest, folding inward as she sucked in the deepest breath of her life, air burning all the way down as her heart hammered violently against her ribs.</p>
<p>Her hands shook as they pressed against the floor. Her vision swam.</p>
<p>She was alive. Shaken. Broken in ways she couldn’t yet name... But alive.</p>
<p>Kaiden didn’t look at her again.</p>
<p>His attention shifted fully to the reinforced door, gaze settling on it with the same calm, dreadful focus he’d carried since leaving the breakfast table behind. Wrath still coiled beneath his skin, dense and contained, not diminished in the slightest, only sharpened, given direction.</p>
<p>The wards hummed in response to his presence.</p>
<p>Kaiden took a step toward the door.</p>
