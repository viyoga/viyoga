<p>Growing stronger through work suited Celia wonderfully, especially if she could do it very far from anything with tusks. She had begun transferring her business interests to come here; perhaps the experience would still be useful.</p>
<p>Surely there was something she could do here.</p>
<p>[Prior experience recognized.]</p>
<p>[Path Expression available: Quartermaster.]</p>
<p>[Develop the storage, preservation, and distribution of supplies. Continued practice will develop specialized abilities.]</p>
<p>"Quartermaster?"</p>
<p>She looked past the notification toward the settlement. People were still carrying food out to the visitors, while others gathered the empty cups. There would be stores to organize, deliveries to receive, and a great many households needing things at once.</p>
<p>She could help with that.</p>
<p>At her acceptance, the title appeared beneath her new race. Celia touched the faint glow at her wrist, then looked anxiously toward Kaiden.</p>
<p>He was approaching their group with Taigi. Bastet and Calypso followed beside him, and Celia’s stomach tightened at the sight of the two women she had insulted outside.</p>
<p>"Mr. Grey?"</p>
<p>He stopped before her.</p>
<p>"The dungeon accepted me."</p>
<p>"I know."</p>
<p>She searched his face, unable to keep the worry out of hers. The bargain had sounded so simple when she agreed to it, but now she was standing inside his world with the people whose mercy she had risked.</p>
<p>Kaiden smiled and nodded to her.</p>
<p>"I had a good feeling about you. Welcome to your new home, Miss Quartermaster."</p>
<p>Celia’s breath left her in a long, unsteady rush. She had been bracing herself for another condition, another reason she might still lose this chance. He had kept his promise. She was forgiven, and she would live under his protection.</p>
<p>The relief was so overwhelming that she had to cover her mouth before she could answer.</p>
<p>"Thank you."</p>
<p>Lucy slipped her hand into Celia’s free one. The girl’s fingers were warm, with the same new mana Celia could feel beneath her own skin.</p>
<p>She held on gratefully as Kaiden turned toward the children.</p>
<p>...</p>
<p>Lucy still had both choices floating in front of her.</p>
<p>Milo was telling Kaiden about the Skyfiend, with Ben interrupting to explain that he had ridden it too. Luna wandered over to listen, her purple hair catching the sunlight as she leaned around Kaiden to inspect the boys.</p>
<p>"So you screamed your heads off, got back in the carriage, and decided you wanted to be fighters?"</p>
<p>"We were excited," Ben insisted.</p>
<p>"Sure you were."</p>
<p>Lucy would normally have helped make fun of them. Instead, she kept looking past Kaiden toward Taigi and the saber at her hip.</p>
<p>Mrs. Weller noticed.</p>
<p>"Don’t even think about it, sweetheart. You’re not made for that life."</p>
<p>Lucy looked up at her.</p>
<p>"You were frightened out of your wits in the carriage," her teacher continued gently. "You don’t have to follow the boys into everything."</p>
<p>"I wasn’t following them."</p>
<p>Her voice came out so softly that Mrs. Weller bent closer to hear.</p>
<p>The words had hurt. Lucy knew how she had behaved in the cavern. She had cried into her teacher’s shoulder while Milo and Ben went flying, and she would still rather sit safely inside the carriage than hang over those horrible spikes.</p>
<p>But Taigi had been frightened too.</p>
<p>Lucy remembered the woman in Kaiden’s broadcast, wearing little more than a loincloth as she knelt before him. Her chief had ordered her to entertain his guest, then made her beg forgiveness when she failed. She had looked so helpless that Lucy had wanted somebody to take her away from him.</p>
<p>Now that same woman stood nearby in bronze armor, with strong arms that had lifted Lucy down from the carriage as though she weighed nothing. People listened when she spoke. The monsters in the recordings had come at her, and she had fought to protect her people.</p>
<p>On the platform, Taigi had told her, "Back on Earth I wasn’t... very brave."</p>
<p>She had learned here, with people who helped her, and she had offered to help Lucy too.</p>
<p>The girl wanted that so badly that her fingers tightened around Celia’s hand. Mrs. Weller could imagine her growing up safely, cared for by people stronger than she was. Lucy wanted to grow into someone who could do the caring when a monster came.</p>
<p>She looked toward Taigi again.</p>
<p>The commander caught her watching and smiled at her, warm and familiar despite all that shining armor.</p>
<p>"I’d like to learn," Lucy told Mrs. Weller.</p>
<p>"Sweetheart..."</p>
<p>"I’m scared. But she was scared too."</p>
<p>Lucy turned to the choice waiting in her vision.</p>
<p>"Combatant."</p>
<p>[Lifestyle Path: Combatant.]</p>
<p>Warmth flowed down her arms as the core began shaping her strength toward the life she had chosen. She looked at her hands, amazed by the mana responding beneath her skin, then up at Taigi.</p>
<p>"Oh, shit. All three of them?"</p>
<p>Luna stared from Lucy to the boys before turning toward Kaiden. He was smiling at the girl, while Milo had stopped in the middle of describing his flight.</p>
<p>"You picked it too?"</p>
<p>Lucy nodded to him.</p>
<p>"You’re going to be a fighter?" Ben asked.</p>
<p>"Yes. Someone has to keep you two dumb boys in check when Miss Weller isn’t available!"</p>
<p>Lucy lifted her chin at the boys.</p>
<p>"I won’t have you embarrassing Mr. Grey!"</p>
<p>"We wouldn’t!" they shouted together.</p>
<p>"Hmph."</p>
<p>She let go of Celia’s hand and approached Taigi, with Mrs. Weller following close behind. The warrior crouched to meet her, bringing the bright markings on her arms level with Lucy’s eyes.</p>
<p>"Will you still teach me?"</p>
<p>Taigi’s smile spread, so openly delighted that Lucy found herself smiling back.</p>
<p>"Of course I will. I meant what I said."</p>
<p>"I’ve never used a weapon. Not even a kitchen knife..."</p>
<p>"Then we’ll start there. I’ll show you how to hold a practice blade, and we’ll see how it feels."</p>
<p>Lucy glanced down at the saber beside Taigi’s knee, then cautiously touched the commander’s forearm.</p>
<p>"I want to be strong like you."</p>
<p>Taigi covered the girl’s hand with hers and flexed the arm beneath Lucy’s palm.</p>
<p>"My lord gave me the chance to grow, but I still had to work for these. Are you prepared to do that too?"</p>
<p>Lucy threw her arms around her neck.</p>
<p>"Yes!"</p>
<p>With a surprised laugh, Taigi caught her, holding the girl close while Mrs. Weller watched them.</p>
<p>Her teacher still looked worried. When Lucy stepped back, though, Mrs. Weller smoothed her hair instead of asking her to change her choice.</p>
<p>"Carefully, please," she said to Taigi. "She means a great deal to me. They all do."</p>
<p>"I’ll take care of them, ma’am."</p>
<p>"Us too?" Ben had come closer with Milo. "We can learn with you?"</p>
<p>"All of you," Taigi assured him as she rose. "Once you’ve settled into your new homes, we can begin."</p>
<p>Milo studied the houses, already imagining coming out of one with a training sword in his hand. Beside him, Ben was trying to stand as straight as the commander.</p>
<p>"We’ll arrange your lessons around it," Mrs. Weller said.</p>
<p>Ben’s shoulders dropped.</p>
<p>"Lessons?"</p>
<p>"Yes, Benjamin."</p>
<p>"But we’re not even human anymore."</p>
<p>"You’ve changed your race, sweetheart. You haven’t finished your education."</p>
<p>Milo and Ben groaned together.</p>
<p>...</p>
<p>Several hours later, Kanut brought another group to the clearing and had to stop at its edge while people shifted their bags to let them through.</p>
<p>Kaiden could barely see the grass between the blankets. Families sat shoulder to shoulder beneath the trees, with children on their parents’ laps and luggage piled against the trunks. Dungeon-Born carrying food called ahead for everyone to draw their feet out of the narrow aisles.</p>
<p>The crowd had spread along the riverbank and into the spaces between the houses. Each family leaving for its new home freed a patch of ground that the next arrivals immediately claimed, while the waiting line stretched back toward the carriage platform.</p>
<p>He had prepared this place to welcome newcomers. Watching another father turn sideways to get his daughter’s bag through the crowd, Kaiden realized how close they were to filling all the reception space they had set aside.</p>
<p>"Over here! We can fit you beside us."</p>
<p>A woman pulled her bags onto her blanket and beckoned to the father. Kaiden recognized the mother whose son had kept asking whether her result matched his. The boy shuffled closer to her to make room, then offered his cup of water to the girl clinging to her father’s coat.</p>
<p>"We came today too," he told her. "It’s really nice here."</p>
<p>She accepted the cup with both hands while her father thanked the woman making space for them.</p>
<p>Kaiden went to greet them, returning the smiles of those who called to him along the crowded aisle. Some were already Dungeon-Born and offered their thanks; others anxiously asked when their group would be assessed.</p>
<p>"My lord."</p>
<p>Taigi reached him as he finished speaking to the family.</p>
<p>"The homes we’ve prepared are almost full, my lord. At this rate, we’ll soon have to put families in tents."</p>
<p>Kaiden looked toward the path leading out of the settlement. His people were carrying blankets that way, and more visitors were following them with their belongings. Beyond the roofs, another carriage glided toward the platform.</p>
<p>"Our people can manage sharing a tent," Taigi continued. "But the newcomers... Some of them may not be used to that. Especially with children."</p>
<p>Her attention returned to the little girl holding the cup. The child was drinking while her father lowered their bags onto the crowded blanket.</p>
<p>Kaiden could see how worried she was. Taigi wanted their new neighbors to love this place, and she was afraid that crowding them into tents would make them regret choosing it.</p>
<p>They had come here for a home. He wanted to give them one they could be happy in.</p>
<p>It was time for Kaiden to embrace his role as the god of this world and work his magic!</p>
