<p>The corpse hit the ground with a final, wet thud.</p>
<p>It was massive, an apex-class beast that looked like something evolution had personally grown angry at. Thick, layered muscle plated its frame like natural armor with jagged horns curling back from a skull built to ram and gore.</p>
<p>Its hide was still steaming where magic and steel had torn it open. Cracked earth spread beneath it in a rough circle, still humming with residual power.</p>
<p>For several seconds, no one spoke.</p>
<p>They just stood there, weapons lowered, breathing hard.</p>
<p>The girls’ chests rose and fell in uneven rhythms, exhaustion finally catching up now that the danger was gone.</p>
<p>Sweat clung to delicate skin, darkening fabric, tracing slow paths down exposed curves and collarbones. Hair stuck to cheeks and necks, strands plastered by heat and effort, and none of them bothered to wipe it away. They were beyond that stage.</p>
<p>This was the quiet after violence, when the body reminded you just how hard it had been pushed.</p>
<p>Kaiden dragged in a slow breath, then another.</p>
<p>He and Calypso looked the worst of the lot. Both were splattered in blood, red and black layered together until it was hard to tell where one ended, and the other began.</p>
<p>Some of it belonged to the beast now cooling at their feet and their previous victims, while some of it belonged to them.</p>
<p>Cuts lined Kaiden’s forearms and shoulders, shallow but numerous, his armor chipped and cracked in places where it had taken hits meant for flesh. Calypso wasn’t any better. Her armor was basically done for, and her crimson skin was marked with evidence of violence, both deep and shallow.</p>
<p>Together, they’d been a menace.</p>
<p>Where Calypso pushed forward with relentless aggression, using her demonic dominance to carve a path for her and her team, Kaiden was more strategic, using his demonic lover’s absolutely mad style to their advantage, making the best of it. Cycling his stances, he flowed beside the woman like a shadow sharpened into a blade, locking enemies down, opening them up, making every mistake fatal.</p>
<p>With Luna’s speedy storm attacks, the duo was elevated further, becoming a truly brutal trio.</p>
<p>Monsters that tried to disengage died screaming. Monsters that tried to overwhelm them died faster. Watching the three of them work in tandem had been like watching a grinder chew through meat.</p>
<p>The stream was going insane.</p>
<p>Messages flooded the overlay in a constant, chaotic cascade, fangirls spamming hearts, fire emojis, and barely coherent praise, congratulating them on the kill, on the pace, on simply still being alive. Mixed right in were the others. The hate watchers. The ones who lingered not out of admiration, but out of spite.</p>
<p>’Overleveled trash.’</p>
<p>’Enjoy it while it lasts, loser.’</p>
<p>’Can’t wait to watch you choke at the bottleneck.’</p>
<p>’Tick tock.’</p>
<p>Some were playful jabs. Others were venomous enough to rot teeth.</p>
<p>Kaiden ignored all of it.</p>
<p>Instead, his gaze drifted across the battlefield, taking in the sight of his beautiful warrioresses. Tired, bloodied, radiant even now. Powerful bodies forged through endless combat, standing tall despite exhaustion, despite pain.</p>
<p>They were spent.</p>
<p>But they were together.</p>
<p>Kaiden smiled, slow and satisfied, then gave them a single nod.</p>
<p>One by one, they returned it.</p>
<p>Then the sound came.</p>
<p>[Ding!]</p>
<p>It rang clear and sharp in his mind, cutting through fatigue like a blade through silk.</p>
<p>Kaiden’s vision flickered as translucent text unfolded before him.</p>
<p>[Level: 49 XP: 490,000 / 490,000]</p>
<p>Full.</p>
<p>Completely, utterly full.</p>
<p>He exhaled, releasing a quiet huff of air. His muscles still burned. His heartbeat still thundered in his ears. Blood dripped from his knuckles to the dirt below.</p>
<p>He had reached it.</p>
<p>The second bottleneck.</p>
<p>To step beyond this point, to break into level 50, there would be no grinding, no farming, no clever routing around the wall. The system had made that very clear the first time.</p>
<p>You didn’t level past a bottleneck.</p>
<p>You pushed through it.</p>
<p>Kaiden’s grin widened as the air around him subtly shifted, pressure building, reality itself seeming to hold its breath.</p>
<p>’Alright,’ he murmured inwardly as he opened the window fully. ’Show me what you’ve got.’</p>
<p>The window finished unfolding.</p>
<p>Kaiden expected it.</p>
<p>Or rather, he expected something familiar.</p>
<p>During the first bottleneck, the world had treated him like every other awakened. The message hadn’t cared about who he really was, only what box he fit into at the time. The universal system, the cold, omnipresent framework that governed levels, classes, and progression for all awakened, had delivered its verdict plainly and without flair.</p>
<p>He remembered it clearly.</p>
<p>[You have reached your First Bottleneck.]</p>
<p>[Paragon of Sin: Evolution Threshold Detected.]</p>
<p>[To advance, prove your Dominance over Sin.]</p>
<p>▸ Trial One – Prove Your Wrath:</p>
<p>Kill 100 enemies of level 25 or higher in solo combat using only Wrath Stance.</p>
<p>(All assists will nullify the kill. Group kills do not count.)</p>
<p>▸ Trial Two – Feed the Glutton:</p>
<p>Consume a total of 1,000 liters of enemy blood, harvested from creatures or awakened enemies of level 25 or above.</p>
<p>(Only blood consumed through combat-triggered Gluttony abilities will count.)</p>
<p>▸ Trial Three – Conquer the Sinner:</p>
<p>Kill a Sin-Wielder and consume their sin.</p>
<p>—</p>
<p>That had been it.</p>
<p>No commentary. No advice. No deviation.</p>
<p>And more importantly, his own system hadn’t spoken at all.</p>
<p>The Demonic Pornstar System had stayed silent, observing, letting the universal framework handle the bottleneck as if it were none of its concern. At the time, Kaiden hadn’t thought much of it. Bottlenecks were a universal law. Even his absurd system hadn’t interfered.</p>
<p>Which was why his brow creased now.</p>
<p>Because none of that appeared.</p>
<p>No trials.</p>
<p>No conditions.</p>
<p>No proclamation of dominance or sin.</p>
<p>Instead, new text began to scroll into existence, line by line.</p>
<p>[The Successor reached the second bottleneck.]</p>
<p>Kaiden’s eyebrow rose.</p>
<p>Successor?</p>
<p>That wasn’t a title the universal system used. It was his own system speaking, somehow overtaking the message that should’ve come from the universal system.</p>
<p>More lines followed, calm and measured, utterly devoid of emotion.</p>
<p>[System Advisory:]</p>
<p>[Before initiating Second Bottleneck Conditions, optimal synchronization is recommended.]</p>
<p>[Ensure all registered companions reach Level 49 – XP: 490,000 / 490,000.]</p>
<p>[Suggested Action: Enter a brief rest cycle and resume progression.]</p>
<p>—</p>
<p>Kaiden stared at the words.</p>
<p>Slowly, carefully, he scanned them again, as if expecting them to change.</p>
<p>They didn’t.</p>
<p>’What the hell is going on...?’ he thought, genuinely thrown for the first time in a while.</p>
<p>’System, you’ve been very chatty with me since you cannibalized my Dungeon Master System, so how about you don’t recede into your previous antisocial shell? Tell me what’s going on, please.’</p>
<p>Nope, that didn’t work. This wasn’t a conversation.</p>
<p>It was a notice.</p>
<p>A recommendation.</p>
<p>Kaiden let out a long, slow sigh through his nose.</p>
<p>Whatever was happening behind the scenes of his systems, universal or otherwise, this clearly wasn’t something he was going to brute-force answers out of right now. The notice lingered for a moment longer, then faded from his vision on its own, as if satisfied that its message had been delivered.</p>
<p>’Alright... You win...’</p>
<p>He lifted his head and looked at the girls, exchanging a nod with them all before saying, "Let’s fill our bellies, shall we? I’m ravenous."</p>
<p>It was also his way of signaling the end of the current combat run. The stream chat exploded again, some congratulating him, others begging for just a bit more, but he ignored it and motioned for them to regroup.</p>
<p>The return to the Runewoven camp was swift and eventless. Renoa was already there when they arrived, and Kaiden caught the way her posture straightened when she saw them. It was not stiff or nervous like before, but animated. The girl was almost bouncing on her heels.</p>
<p>It seemed she was quickly getting more familiar with having to be near them... Well, near him, as Kaiden knew, was what caused her to act anxious. The poor girl seemed to be a rather diehard fangirl.</p>
<p>"You’re back!" she said brightly, eyes sweeping over the group. "And... wow. You really went all out again, didn’t you?"</p>
<p>Her gaze lingered on Kaiden for half a heartbeat longer than necessary, but then she blushed and smiled at him without averting her gaze.</p>
<p>"Food’s ready," she added quickly, gesturing toward a long table already stacked high.</p>
<p>That got immediate reactions.</p>
<p>They didn’t bother with ceremony. Armor was stripped and dropped, and the trolleys were escorted to be cleaned and maintained.</p>
<p>After that came the blissful few minutes the girls had been waiting for since their day started, and the first drop of sweat materialized on their skin.</p>
<p>Hot water washed away blood, sweat, and the lingering stink of battle. Bruises were inspected, cuts treated, and by the time they regrouped again, everyone looked more like lethal adventurers than walking war crimes.</p>
<p>Afterward, they found themselves seated and tearing into the massive dish of monster meat like starved predators. The dish was dense with power, spiced and prepared to maximize absorption, and Kaiden could feel the fatigue in his limbs slowly recede with every bite.</p>
<p>Conversation returned naturally.</p>
<p>Light, unforced. Complaints about close calls. Jabs about reckless charges, aimed mostly exclusively at a certain red-skinned hothead of a woman. Luna reenacted one particularly ugly moment with exaggerated flair, earning groans and laughter in equal measure. Even Kaiden found himself relaxing, the tension in his shoulders easing as the hours passed.</p>
<p>Then, slowly, inevitably, they went back out.</p>
