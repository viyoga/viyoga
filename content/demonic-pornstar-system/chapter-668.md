<p>The ’logistical purposes’ excuse fooled exactly no one.</p>
<p>Then the professional kicked in.</p>
<p>"Medical first," Renoa said, the stammer gone entirely. "Calypso, those lacerations need cleaning and assessment. Everyone’s armor needs immediate inspection before it’s worn again."</p>
<p>She was already walking, expecting them to follow.</p>
<p>"Showers are heated and ready. I had the mess team prepare high-density recovery meals when I saw you heading south." She glanced at her clipboard, made a note, glanced back up. "Is anyone experiencing mana sickness symptoms? Dizziness, nausea, visual distortion?"</p>
<p>Luna raised her hand.</p>
<p>Calypso raised her hand.</p>
<p>Aria raised her hand.</p>
<p>Nyx raised her hand.</p>
<p>Bastet raised her hand with the dignity of a queen admitting to a minor inconvenience.</p>
<p>&lt;Me too!&gt; Alice’s halo pulsed weakly. &lt;Raise two hands because you speak for the both of us!&gt;</p>
<p>"..." Kaiden did as asked.</p>
<p>Renoa’s clipboard lowered. Her professional composure held for exactly one second as the full scope of how badly they’d pushed themselves registered across her face.</p>
<p>Then it cracked.</p>
<p>"All of you?!" She turned on Kaiden, and for a brief, glorious moment, the stammering fangirl vanished entirely. "You pushed a team of level fifties against a level seventy-nine monster until every single member developed mana sickness?! Do you have any idea how dangerous that is?! Mana sickness at this altitude with these density levels-"</p>
<p>She caught herself.</p>
<p>Remembered who she was talking to.</p>
<p>Remembered that millions of people were watching her scold the man whose content she consumed religiously.</p>
<p>The blush that followed was nuclear.</p>
<p>"P-please follow me," she said, voice three octaves higher than it had been a second ago. "This is serious!"</p>
<p>She turned on her heel and walked very quickly toward the medical station, clipboard pressed against her chest like a shield.</p>
<p>Kaiden watched her go with a grin.</p>
<p>...</p>
<p>An hour later, the recovery tent was quiet.</p>
<p>The medical team had done their work efficiently. Lacerations cleaned and sealed. Mana stabilizers administered. Then they left, leaving them alone to recover.</p>
<p>Armor was collected by Renoa for inspection and repair.</p>
<p>The tent itself was spacious by field standards, a reinforced canvas structure with thick bedrolls laid across raised platforms, privacy screens between sections, and a soft amber glow from mana lanterns that kept the interior warm without being bright.</p>
<p>Most of the girls had surrendered to exhaustion within minutes of lying down.</p>
<p>Luna was out cold, sprawled face-down on her bedroll with one arm hanging off the edge and her mouth slightly open. The gamer girl slept the way she gamed: completely committed and without a shred of elegance.</p>
<p>Nyx had curled into a tight ball beside her, knees drawn up, breathing slow and even. Aria lay on her back with her silver hair fanned across the pillow, one hand resting over her heart, serene even in sleep. Calypso had claimed the largest bedroll quoting her horns making her the biggest, and was snoring softly, her tail coiled around her own thigh.</p>
<p>Alice had detached from her Conduit form and was sleeping in her physical body, curled up next to Aria with her face buried against the Moon Valkyrie’s shoulder. She looked innocent again when she slept. Fragile and young and nothing like the weapon that had vaporized chitin an hour ago.</p>
<p>Kaiden lay on his stomach, shirtless, the hard lines of his back exposed. The ichor was gone, scrubbed clean in the showers, and what remained was muscle built through months of combat, scars that told stories the stream audience could only guess at, and the bone-level fatigue of a man who had earned every point of experience the system had given him today.</p>
<p>He was not sleeping.</p>
<p>Bastet’s hands moved across his back in slow, firm strokes.</p>
<p>The Pharaoh sat on top of him in her desert attire that left her tanned shoulders bare and her midriff exposed. Her golden eyes were half-lidded, her expression carrying the satisfaction of a woman performing a task she considered both her right and her privilege.</p>
<p>Her palms pressed into the muscle along his spine, fingers spreading wide, working the tension out of knotted tissue. She found a knot beneath his shoulder blade and leaned into it, grinding the heel of her palm in slow circles until the muscle released.</p>
<p>Kaiden exhaled into the bedroll.</p>
<p>"Harder?" Bastet smiled, knowing already.</p>
<p>"Harder..."</p>
<p>Her hands slid lower, thumbs pressing into the grooves along his spine. A purr rumbled in her chest, low and steady, vibrating through her palms and into his body. It was involuntary, or at least she’d never admitted otherwise. The purring happened when Bastet was content, and Bastet was content when she had Kaiden beneath her, pliant and warm and entirely hers.</p>
<p>She leaned down to kiss his neck.</p>
<p>"You were reckless today," she said against his body. The words vibrated through the kiss.</p>
<p>"Mm."</p>
<p>"The Pharaoh does not approve of recklessness..."</p>
<p>"Pharaoh? I only know my amazing tanned kitten."</p>
<p>"Master... You’re threading dangerous waters..." Her teeth grazed his skin just enough to make him feel it.</p>
<p>Her hands resumed their work, sliding up his back, fingers kneading the muscles along his neck. She shifted her weight, one knee settling against his hip, her body close enough that he could feel the warmth of her stomach against his skin.</p>
<p>The purring deepened.</p>
<p>On the stream, shot from Bastet’s POV, the chat had settled into its own rhythm. The frantic energy of the fight and the Magnus confrontation had given way to something quieter, the comfortable hum of a community enjoying downtime together. Viewers chatted amongst themselves, dissecting the day’s events, sharing clips, arguing about standings, and occasionally noting that the tanned kitten’s massage technique looked like it should be classified as a national treasure.</p>
<p>Kaiden closed his eyes and let Bastet’s hands work.</p>
<p>For the first time all day, the tension left his body entirely.</p>
<p>...</p>
<p>While Kaiden lay in a warm tent with a purring felinid on his back, a very different scene was playing out in the New Dawn guild hall.</p>
<p>Magnus Ashborn sat alone in his command room.</p>
<p>The door was locked. The projection artifacts were dark. The standings display he usually kept running at all hours had been dismissed with a gesture so violent it cracked the table’s surface.</p>
<p>His children had chosen each other. Again. His son had looked him in the eye on a live broadcast and told him, in the politest possible language, that he was nothing to him.</p>
<p>His fist hit the table, shattering it into smithereens.</p>
<p>He was beyond furious.</p>
