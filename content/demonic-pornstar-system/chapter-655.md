<p>The Manifestation of Wrath was not blind rage.</p>
<p>It was authority over destruction itself.</p>
<p>The red in Kaiden’s eyes deepened. The frost beneath his feet spread another inch outward, slow and patient, the cold of a man who had all the time in the world because he had already made his decision.</p>
<p>He was going to kill Ash.</p>
<p>Not here. Not now. Not where the cameras could record it and the Association could intervene and the penalty would cost his team elimination or worse.</p>
<p>Later.</p>
<p>When the streams were off. When there was no one to hear him scream.</p>
<p>Ash had just moved from "obnoxious nuisance" to "dead man walking" in Kaiden’s mind, and the transition had been as quiet as snowfall.</p>
<p>The pressure that had been building in the basin since his eyes changed color consolidated. It stopped spreading outward and compressed inward, tightening around the space between Kaiden and Ashbound’s group like a fist slowly closing.</p>
<p>Ash held his ground. His grin was dead, replaced by a flat, rigid expression that was equal parts defiance and survival instinct. He was an S-tier combatant. He’d fought monsters that could level city blocks. He was not going to flinch in front of the cameras.</p>
<p>He didn’t flinch.</p>
<p>But behind him, his three girls did.</p>
<p>Brittany’s legs gave out first.</p>
<p>The pressure hit her like a physical wall, and her knees buckled.</p>
<p>Stacy staggered sideways, her rehearsed composure shattering as her hand flew to her chest.</p>
<p>Trisha simply took an involuntary step back.</p>
<p>Three A-tier combatants. All of them higher level than Kaiden. All of them veterans of hundreds of monster encounters. All of them reduced to trembling in the span of a single breath by a man who hadn’t moved his hands, hadn’t even spoken a single threat.</p>
<p>And Ash, standing at the center of his collapsed formation with his grin dead and his spine locked, realized something.</p>
<p>The provocation had worked.</p>
<p>Just not the way he’d planned.</p>

<p>The basin fell quiet after Kaiden and co left, finding a better farming spot.</p>
<p>Ash killed his stream with a sharp mental command.</p>
<p>The recording light died, instead projecting the replay of the last five minutes of combat footage.</p>
<p>But for his team, he replayed something else.</p>
<p>Brittany’s knees buckling. Stacy staggering. Trisha stepping back. All three of them crumbling on a live broadcast while millions watched.</p>
<p>"Look at this," Ash said.</p>
<p>His voice was flat. Cold. The showman’s grin was gone. What remained was the guild officer, the Ashbound family’s S-tier investment, the man whose mother had built an empire and expected returns.</p>
<p>"Look at what you did."</p>
<p>Brittany opened her mouth. "Ash, he-"</p>
<p>"I didn’t ask for excuses." He jabbed a finger at the frozen frame of Brittany on one knee. "You buckled. On stream. In front of both audiences. Against a man who is a lower level than all three of you."</p>
<p>He swiped to Stacy’s stagger. "You grabbed your chest like a civilian having a panic attack."</p>
<p>Then Trisha’s retreat. "And you just stepped backward like a frightened little girl."</p>
<p>The three women stood in a line, armor scuffed, eyes down. The confidence they’d worn like costumes ten minutes ago was gone. What remained were three employees receiving a performance review from their boss.</p>
<p>Which was exactly what this was.</p>
<p>"Three A-tiers," Ash continued, pacing now. "Three awakened combatants who are supposed to represent the Ashbound brand. And you folded from aura. Aura! He didn’t swing. He didn’t cast. He looked at you, and you fell apart."</p>
<p>"His pressure was-" Stacy started.</p>
<p>"I felt the same pressure!" Ash snapped, spinning on her. "I’m the one who was standing a foot from his face! I held my ground. I didn’t move. I didn’t flinch. And what did the three of you do behind me?"</p>
<p>Silence.</p>
<p>"You made me look like I brought amateurs to a standoff. You made the guild look weak. You made the family look weak." He stopped pacing. "My mother is going to see this footage. You understand that, right? She reviews every stream. Every clip. Every metric. And she’s going to see her three handpicked A-tiers drop like training dummies because one man got a bit moody."</p>
<p>Brittany’s hands clenched at her sides. Her voice came out quiet. "It won’t happen again."</p>
<p>"No. It won’t." Ash looked at each of them in turn. "Because next time we encounter that freak and his little harem, you will stand your ground or I will replace you. There are many strong women who would kill for your contracts. Don’t forget that."</p>
<p>Stacy nodded once. "Understood."</p>
<p>Trisha swallowed. "Sorry, Ash."</p>
<p>"Don’t apologize to me. Apologize to the brand." He turned away, pulling up the replay again, scrubbing to his own footage. Watching himself hold his ground. Watching himself not flinch.</p>
<p>He replayed it three times and smiled proudly.</p>
<p>Brittany watched him watch himself and felt the last thread of warmth she held for this man, her once steady team captain, go very, very quiet.</p>
<p>’He’s not checking if we’re okay,’ she realized. ’He’s checking if he looked good.’</p>
<p>She said nothing.</p>
<p>"Now," Ash muttered, dismissing the replay with a wave. "We go back out there, we farm, and we-"</p>
<p>"The Ashen Knight."</p>
<p>The voice came from above.</p>
<p>All four of them looked up.</p>
<p>Two figures stood on the ridge overlooking the western zone. A woman with an immaculate braid, armor maintained with military precision, chin raised with the casual authority of someone who had never once doubted her right to stand wherever she pleased. A man beside her, half a step back, posture disciplined, the faintest edge of amusement tucked behind his eyes.</p>
<p>Both wore the insignia of New Dawn.</p>
<p>Mariana Reyes looked down at Ash and his three girls with the expression of someone who had just watched a child throw a tantrum in a grocery store.</p>
<p>Chinedu Obasuyi didn’t bother hiding his grin.</p>
<p>"The Ashen Knight," Mariana repeated, and the words carried a weight that made it very clear she was not using the title with respect. "What a show that was."</p>
<p>Ash wasn’t in the mood for this. "What do you freaks want?! Fuck off!"</p>
<p>The anger he’d been directing at his girls redirected itself instinctively toward the new threat, and it came out raw and unfiltered because Ash’s composure had already been spent on Kaiden, and he had nothing left for these two.</p>
<p>Mariana didn’t react.</p>
<p>Chinedu’s grin widened.</p>
<p>"Calm yourself," Chinedu said. "We’re not here to cause trouble."</p>
<p>"Then why are you here?!"</p>
<p>Mariana and Chinedu exchanged a glance.</p>
<p>Then Chinedu looked back at Ash, and the grin settled into something sharper.</p>
<p>"We have a proposal."</p>
