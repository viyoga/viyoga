<p>"Alright, girls... Let’s do this together."</p>
<p>He didn’t need to say more.</p>
<p>They instantly understood, having been waiting for his go-ahead for a long time now. They each agreed to do this together once they got home, but thanks to Grace ambushing them with her two bodyguards, they got a bit sidetracked.</p>
<p>Without another word, the Valkyries focused inwardly, each of them accessing their systemic interface.</p>

<p>’Taming a Sexy Boss Monster’</p>

<p>342,781,139 views.</p>
<p>For a second, the room went still.</p>
<p>Then...</p>
<p>Aria, nestled against his side, just... froze.</p>
<p>Her glowing silver eyes locked onto the number, lips parted, brain clearly buffering. She went so still Kaiden briefly wondered if she’d glitched.</p>
<p>"...Aria?" he asked, nudging her shoulder.</p>
<p>Nothing.</p>
<p>She just... blinked very slowly.</p>
<p>Meanwhile, behind the couch, Nyx exploded.</p>
<p>"YEEEEEEESSSS!" she screamed, doing an excited hop in place before launching herself into a chaotic victory dance. "We broke three hundred million?! In less than a day?! The BTS twinks only gained 143 million views with their most viral video, and that took them 24 hours! Do you guys understand what that means?! We’re more viral than any of the pre-mana apocalypse superstars!"</p>
<p>She nearly tripped on her own feet as she spun behind the furniture, arms flailing like an overjoyed maniac.</p>
<p>Kaiden laughed with an incredibly big grin adorning his face, his heart beating from excitement so fast he felt it might just jump out of his chest.</p>
<p>And then he turned to see the final reaction of his Valkyries...</p>
<p>Luna didn’t scream or shout.</p>
<p>She just smirked. Wider than ever before.</p>
<p>That smug, knowing, confident smirk that told him she knew this would happen.</p>
<p>Lifting one hand, she held it up at just the right angle.</p>
<p>Kaiden met it with a firm, resounding high five.</p>
<p>The echo of the clap filled the room.</p>
<p>"Victory tastes sweet," Luna declared, eyes glinting with amusement.</p>
<p>And Aria, still in his arms, finally stirred.</p>
<p>She blinked again. Then very softly whispered, "...three hundred and forty-two million... That’s basically the whole United States!"</p>
<p>She tried to stand, perhaps to walk it all off, but her legs instantly gave out, making the Moon Valkyrie slump back into his hold, landing her perky butt on his lap.</p>
