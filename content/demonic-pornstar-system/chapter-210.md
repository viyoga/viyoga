<p>Her expression was robotic. Eyes blank. Smile twitching.</p>
<p>Alice.</p>
<p>The little brocon pretended not to notice.</p>
<p>Instead of facing any possible allegations, she leaned deeper into Kaiden’s chest like a cat trying to vanish into a pillow. She tugged his coat forward with her dainty fingers, attempting to bury her face in it completely.</p>
<p>But this time, Kaiden wasn’t going to play along for even a second.</p>
<p>He sighed and reached down under her chin.</p>
<p>Alice instantly went stiff.</p>
<p>His finger gently pressed up.</p>
<p>But Alice wasn’t moving.</p>
<p>A full-blown power struggle had begun.</p>
<p>In her head, an entire civil war unfolded.</p>
<p>"Do not lift that chin! If you lift it, you’re gonna get scolded!"</p>
<p>"But it’s Kai! I don’t want to go against his wishes!"</p>
<p>"Doesn’t matter! He’s gonna be disappointed in you!"</p>
<p>"But... but he’ll pet my head after, if I apologize properly, maybe!"</p>
<p>"You taught a young, innocent child how to ritualistically murder dolls, you absolute maniac! What headpats are you expecting?!"</p>
<p>On the outside, her neck trembled with the tension of a medieval drawbridge caught between two armies. Her lips quivered. Her shoulders inched up defensively as if she could physically hide from consequence.</p>
<p>Yes, she was stronger than he. A higher-level awakened, faster, sharper, more potent.</p>
<p>But this was Kaiden.</p>
<p>Her brother.</p>
<p>And if he wanted her to lift her chin...</p>
<p>She would.</p>
<p>With agonizing reluctance, she let him guide her head up until her eyes met his. Slowly, painfully, greatly resembling a guilty kitten who got caught on the kitchen counter.</p>
<p>Before he could even start lecturing her, she blurted out her defense case like a child who’d been cooking it up the whole day.</p>
<p>"I only wanted to get closer to... t-to... to the family member... of... of your l-l-l-" her lips contorted in physical agony, "l-l-lov-"</p>
<p>"Just say it," Luna muttered flatly.</p>
<p>"Lo-ver!" Alice gagged the word out like it was acid on her tongue, then immediately turned her head.</p>
<p>The other girls watched this whole exchange with deadpan stares.</p>
<p>Nyx gave a slow thumbs-up. "I mean... A+ for effort."</p>
<p>Aria didn’t know how to react. Her anger had vanished from the sheer lunacy Alice put up by her mere existence. "I... I guess it’s okay this once...?"</p>
<p>Kaiden exhaled through his nose, eyes still focused on Alice.</p>
<p>"I appreciate you wanting to bond with Lux. But teaching her how to stab dolls at her age isn’t ideal. Especially not without her guardian’s permission."</p>
<p>Alice sniffed like a chastised puppy.</p>
<p>"I understand..." she mumbled, eyes watering.</p>
<p>Kaiden gave her hair a gentle pat in forgiveness.</p>
<p>She instantly leaned into it.</p>
<p>Meanwhile, Lux stood beside them, still holding her poor, decapitated plush, now upside down.</p>
<p>"Are we not stabbing anymore?" she asked innocently.</p>
<p>...</p>
<p>They lingered at the Ashborn residence for a little longer, mostly to soothe the riot of emotions Alice kept hurling in every direction when she felt it in the very air itself that Kaiden’s departure was nearing.</p>
<p>There were snacks involved. Some quiet apologies. A few more plush dolls may or may not have been stabbed in secret.</p>
<p>But eventually, time ran out.</p>
<p>Alice clung to Kaiden’s arm like a particularly determined limpet, refusing to let go until she secured a new treaty of sibling interaction.</p>
<p>"One call a day," she sniffled, eyes glimmering with sheer drama. "Minimum. Or I’m not letting go."</p>
<p>"Fine."</p>
<p>"And you’ll visit me whenever I demand-uh, I mean... whenever you can."</p>
<p>"Sure."</p>
<p>She paused. Calculated. Then leaned in with sparkling eyes.</p>
<p>"Also... if I ever wish to visit you, then I may... correct?"</p>
<p>Kaiden gave a soft chuckle. "We’ll talk about it. But yeah, I don’t see why not."</p>
<p>That was enough.</p>
<p>She squealed into his coat, hugged him one final time, then let him go with the solemn dignity of a yandere empress who had graciously allowed her subject of obsession temporary leave.</p>
<p>They stepped out into the outer gardens of the estate, where trimmed hedges, moonlit cobblestones, and marble fountains awaited them.</p>
<p>Waiting awkwardly by the transport platform, looking vaguely like detained suspects at a border checkpoint, stood Rae and Riven.</p>
<p>The Artificer Twins.</p>
<p>Riven looked impassive, fiddling with something on her tablet. Probably planning a new prototype or researching.</p>
<p>Rae, meanwhile, looked like she might combust with unsaid thoughts.</p>
<p>The chaotic, tanned girl bounced in place as she was busy fangirling. "How was the boss lady, Kaiden? I mean, I know you’ve met her before, but still... Damn, you’re so lucky! Also, what was that shadowy outburst? Even from here, we felt it."</p>
<p>Luna was more than happy to answer. "Her children were rude to Kaiden, so basically she suplexed them into the shadow realm, or something along those lines."</p>
<p>Rae paused in her hops and turned toward the Storm Valkyrie. "The fuckin’ mana goddess from hell?! Did she really suplex her own kids to defend Kaiden?!"</p>
<p>Kaiden gave the chaotic pair a look. "... Not exactly a suplex. And there werewas definitely no shadow realms involved. She just gave them a little slap on the wrist."</p>
<p>"I think I’m in love," Rae breathed.</p>
<p>"I wanted to ask a few things as well," Riven chimed in, while clicking a million times a second on her tablet. "But now isn’t the time."</p>
<p>"Damn right it isn’t," Nyx agreed. "Let’s get our butts back to the househome."</p>
<p>Rae also calmed down, knowing it was time to get to work. "Let’s move."</p>
<p>She activated the artifact vehicle, and everyone but her boarded, settling into their seats.</p>
<p>As the engine pulsed to life, the towering Ashborn manor quickly grew smaller behind them.</p>
<p>Alice could be seen from one of the balconies.</p>
<p>She waved. Fiercely. Almost angrily.</p>
<p>Like she was trying to punch the distance with affection.</p>
<p>The journey back to the bunker was uneventful, but anticipation built with every kilometer crossed.</p>
<p>Why?</p>
<p>Because it was time for them to focus on getting the [Fangirl Bottleneck] quest sorted now, which would reward them with 2 Upgrade Points and a strange thing called the Fangirl Management Interface.</p>
<p>And if that wasn’t enough to get them excited, the fact that they were very close to ranking up the system, thus getting brand new abilities, sure was.</p>
<p>It was time to grind.</p>
