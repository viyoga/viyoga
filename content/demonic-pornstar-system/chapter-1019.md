<p>"Lord Grey. Thank goodness." Her English was fluent. "I’m Mizuno, an awakened. I’ve been sheltering these people in the storage cellars. We heard the fighting stop, so I brought them out."</p>
<p>"You did well keeping them alive." He glanced along the exhausted group. "Is there anyone still down there?"</p>
<p>"Everyone is here. I have a van beyond the cordon. If your soldiers could let it through, we can get out of your way."</p>
<p>"You’re not in our way. Let’s have someone look after them first."</p>
<p>Gold shone around Alexandra’s fingers. The elderly woman drew a deep breath and touched her chest in astonishment, exclaiming to Sonoda in Japanese.</p>

<p>"Of course," Alexandra answered. "But I’d like to help everyone before they travel. Some of them can barely sit up."</p>
<p>Sonoda relayed the exchange to the lieutenant. He joined the people on the ramp with his tablet, asking their names and checking them against the district’s missing-person records.</p>
<p>"Fujisawa Toru?" He offered the screen to the man with the wrapped feet.</p>
<p>The photograph showed a delivery driver smiling beneath a company sign. Toru recognized himself, studying the healthy face on the screen before raising his eyes to the officer.</p>
<p>He whispered a question, frightened of the answer.</p>
<p>"His wife reported him missing," Sonoda explained to Kaiden. "He’s asking whether she got out."</p>
<p>The lieutenant checked the entry and answered Toru at once. The man covered his mouth to stifle a sob as Sonoda translated for the family.</p>

<p>"I’ll rest once they’re settled."</p>
<p>"You sound like a certain someone I know," Kaiden said, looking at his protector.</p>
<p>Alexandra gave him a gorgeous smile, then turned back to Toru as the call connected.</p>
<p>"Toru?"</p>
