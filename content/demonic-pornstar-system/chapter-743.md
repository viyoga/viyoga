<p>"With that out of the way... How lucky are we that Natasha appeared on the stream?"</p>
<p>"Yeah!" Nyx nodded along.</p>
<p>"I was really surprised," Kaiden said.</p>
<p>"I organized everything," Vespera announced.</p>
<p>"...Huh?" Six voices gasped at once.</p>
<p>The silence that followed lasted three full seconds, which was approximately two and a half seconds longer than this particular group of women had ever been collectively quiet about anything.</p>
<p>Kaiden looked at his mother.</p>
<p>"What do you mean, you organized everything?"</p>
<p>His voice was dry. Wry, even. The tone of a man who had watched this woman smile with her eyes closed while her husband’s mistress dismantled his reputation in real time, and was now putting the pieces together in the order they should have been obvious.</p>
<p>Vespera shrugged. A small motion against the couch, the same economical movement she gave to most things that didn’t warrant full sentences.</p>
<p>"He tried to kill my children. I decided to ruin him." Casual. Matter-of-fact. "I went after every weakness he had and every weakness he believed he didn’t have."</p>
<p>That was it.</p>
<p>The girls were stunned. Luna was staring at Vespera with open awe, Calypso’s grin was spreading, and the rest of them looked like they’d just watched a woman confess to arson while filing her nails.</p>
<p>Kaiden studied his mother’s face. Calm. Total absence of guilt.</p>
<p>"So you manipulated me," he said.</p>
<p>Vespera turned her head toward him. Red eyes, unbothered. "What do you mean?"</p>
<p>Kaiden held the look. Then the corner of his mouth twitched.</p>
<p>"No manipulation. Just... orchestration." He exhaled through his nose. "Got it."</p>
<p>Vespera’s attention returned to the middle distance, satisfied.</p>
<p>"Mother-in-law." Aria’s voice came from the opposite couch, tentative. "What would you have done if Kai hadn’t noticed the donation?"</p>
<p>"Natasha was under instructions to send donations until someone acknowledged her. If that failed, there were backup options."</p>
<p>"Wow..." Nyx breathed.</p>
<p>"But what about her?" Aria pressed. Her brow was creased. "If Magnus was willing to kill his own son, a mistress who betrayed him is in real danger."</p>
<p>Vespera looked at Aria for a moment before replying.</p>
<p>"She’s in a hidden village of political exiles in the mountains of Russia. I purchased her a mansion and deposited enough into her accounts that she’ll never need to work again."</p>
<p>Aria exhaled in relief. Then Vespera added,</p>
<p>"He was remarkably stingy with her."</p>
<p>The woman’s mouth curved. A harder edge than the rare warmth she’d shown Kaiden tonight. Amused. Cold.</p>
<p>"Despite keeping a weakness that obvious for years, he never even made the woman wealthy. Buying her cooperation was simple. I didn’t even need to threaten her life or family. She seemed happy to cleanse her hands of that man."</p>
<p>The room shifted.</p>
<p>It was quiet, and it had nothing to do with what Vespera said. It was how she said it. The answers to Kaiden and Aria had been responses. Someone asked, she replied, the exchange ended. That was Vespera. She spoke when spoken to and offered nothing beyond what was required.</p>
<p>But nobody had asked about Magnus’s spending habits. Nobody had asked how willing Natasha was. Vespera had finished answering Aria’s question multiple sentences ago, and she was still talking. She had brought up a new detail, offered her own commentary, and added to a conversation she could have exited in silence.</p>
<p>The Shadow Monarch was chatting.</p>
<p>Kaiden felt it first. A warmth in his chest that had nothing to do with the words and everything to do with the woman beside him choosing, for the first time, to speak to the women of her son as though she wanted to, rather than because she had to.</p>
<p>The girls felt it too. Nyx’s lips parted. Aria’s hand found her own chest. Bastet’s ears had gone completely still, which for a felinid was the equivalent of holding her breath.</p>
<p>"Shadow Mommy." Calypso spoke up suddenly. "If I may."</p>
<p>Vespera’s eyebrows rose, which was a first for her. She hadn’t flinched when her son’s women decided to kneel before her for reasons she still didn’t fully understand, or when they swore oaths she hadn’t asked for.</p>
<p>She chose to ignore the remark.</p>
<p>"Everything you did, the cameras, the money, the planning... Why? I’ve heard a lot about you, and I expected you to have attacked Magnus ever since he began threatening Kai’s life. Why go through all of this instead of attacking him?"</p>
<p>It was the most Calypso question imaginable. The Manifestation of Carnage, whose answer to most problems involved an axe and enthusiasm, genuinely could not understand why a woman with the power to end someone had chosen paperwork instead.</p>
<p>Vespera needed no time to collect her thoughts.</p>
<p>"Magnus Morvane is a man whose entire identity is built on what he controls. His guild, his reputation, his name. Everything he is, everything he believes himself to be, is tied to the empire he spent decades building. Kill him and it’s over. A few moments of pain, then nothing."</p>
<p>Her expression darkened, red eyes turning beyond cruel and sadistic.</p>
<p>"But force him to watch. Let him see the public turn on him, call him a cheater, a woman beater, a man who tried to murder his own son, a guild leader who endangered his people by the self-serving decisions he was making. Let him see the empire he built with his own hands crumble to dust."</p>
<p>Her lips crawled upward, forming a beautifully unhinged expression.</p>
<p>"That is how you hurt a man like him. Death was a mercy Morvane did not deserve."</p>
<p>The room was silent.</p>
<p>Luna’s hands clapped together slowly, twice, then faster. Her face was split open with the kind of admiration the gamer gremlin reserved for the most devastating plays she’d ever witnessed. This was endgame strategy from a woman who played the longest game Luna had ever seen, and the competitive girl in her wanted to take notes.</p>
<p>"Kai, your mom is so cool!"</p>
<p>Nyx was nodding vehemently, her gaze bright. Calypso was laughing, the sound of a woman who had just learned a new definition of carnage. Even Bastet’s tail was swaying in what could only be described as admiration, her golden eyes studying Vespera the way one studies a master at work.</p>
<p>’Terrifying,’ Kaiden shuddered. ’Absolutely terrifying.’</p>
<p>...</p>
<p>The kitchen door swung open.</p>
<p>"Big brother! The puffs are done and the cookies are perfect I only burned one tray which wasn’t really my fault because-"</p>
<p>Alice rounded the corner carrying a tray stacked with pastries, cookies, and what appeared to be an experimental tower of cream puffs that defied several laws of structural engineering. Alexandra followed behind her, holding a second tray with cups and a pot of tea, still wiping flour from her cheek.</p>
<p>Evidently, the kitchen was more chaotic than usual, thanks to a certain ball of energy going ballistic with celebratory joy.</p>
<p>Alice stopped.</p>
<p>She looked at the room. At the five women who were either grinning, laughing, or staring at Vespera with open reverence. At Kaiden’s wry expression. At her mother, sitting beside her son with an expression Alice had never seen on her mother’s face.</p>
<p>"What happened?"</p>
<p>"Mother orchestrated the Natasha stream," Kaiden said.</p>
<p>Alice blinked. "The donation? The call? The crying?"</p>
<p>"Yep."</p>
<p>Alice looked at Vespera. Vespera looked back.</p>
<p>"Even the bruises?"</p>
<p>Vespera said nothing, which was its own answer.</p>
<p>"MOM!!!!" Alice stomped on the ground.</p>
<p>She received no response.</p>
<p>Alice huffed and puffed. Alexandra rescued the tray, set both down on the table, and took her usual position behind Kaiden’s couch with her hands clasped in front of her, back straight, chin up. The posture of a woman attending to her duties.</p>
<p>"Alex," Nyx called from the opposite couch. "Don’t do this. Come, sit with us."</p>
<p>"I’m fine. I’ll just-"</p>
<p>"You’ve been working for a long time and it’s the middle of the night right now..."</p>
<p>"I’m not tired and I enjoy standing! It’s-"</p>
<p>"Fine, have it your way~"</p>
<p>Alexandra’s body lifted three inches off the carpet. Her legs dangled. Her hands flew to smooth down her skirt, and her face went from composed to deeply alarmed in the span of a heartbeat.</p>
<p>"Nyx! This is-" She floated over the back of the couch. "This constitutes the use of awakened abilities against a non-awakened civilian! Under Association Code Section-"</p>
<p>Nyx caught her around the waist and settled her into her lap. Alexandra’s back pressed against Nyx’s chest, and the spatial hold dissolved, leaving her sitting in the pink-haired woman’s arms with the legal argument still dying on her lips.</p>
<p>"Section what?" Nyx asked pleasantly.</p>
<p>Alexandra turned to Vespera. The Shadow Monarch was the highest authority in the room, an operative who could enforce any law she chose to, and Alexandra’s look was enormous and pleading.</p>
<p>Vespera was looking at the cream puffs, and the daughter still glaring at her.</p>
<p>Alexandra’s head whipped toward Kaiden. Her last hope. The man who cared about her, who had saved her, who would absolutely not permit-</p>
<p>Alice stopped glaring at her mother just then and stepped into the gap between the couches, holding a tray of delicacies.</p>
<p>"Big brother! I made these for you!"</p>
<p>The sightline vanished. Alexandra’s mouth opened and closed behind Alice’s back, and Nyx’s arms tightened by a fraction, securing her position.</p>
<p>Kaiden watched it happen from the corner of his eye. Alexandra squirming in Nyx’s lap, batting at her arms, face red. Fighting back. Swatting. Flustered and annoyed and very much present.</p>
<p>A girl mad at her friend for picking her up without permission. Nothing more.</p>
<p>His chest loosened.</p>
<p>He looked up at Alice. "For me?"</p>
<p>"For you!"</p>
<p>"You mean for all of us?"</p>
