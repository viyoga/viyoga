<p>"Mmmm..." Kaiden blinked, his vision slowly clearing as the morning sun filtered through the guild building’s reinforced windows. <p>For a long moment, he didn’t move. He just lay there, wondering why his limbs felt like they had been replaced by lead and why his entire body felt so impossibly heavy.</p>
<p>Then, the memories of the night came rushing back like a flood of heat.</p>
<p>’Ah, yeah... the girls did a serious number on me,’ he mused as a lazy, triumphant grin emerged on his lips. <p>However, he couldn’t help but chuckle dryly. "I’m in grave danger."</p>

<p>However, did any of this make Kaiden feel worried?</p>
<p>No, he felt no anxiety whatsoever about his ability to satisfy his women.</p>
<p>He did not care for logic. He did not care if the combination of them had a hundred times more stamina than he did.</p>
<p>He would not rest until they all fell into a blissful sleep, sporting the happiest of expressions on their delicate faces, even if he had to do it through sheer force of will.</p>

<p>He was completely buried in a living, breathing blanket of gorgeous women.</p>

<p>But as he tried to shift his head to get a better look at his surroundings, he noticed a gap in the ranks.</p>
<p>’Wait... where’s Bastet?’</p>
<p>He looked around as much as his gorgeous "burden" would allow, seeing nothing but tangled silk and discarded armor. It was only when he tried to adjust his neck that he realized he wasn’t actually resting on a silk pillow at all.</p>
<p>The "cushion" beneath his head was warm, slightly firm yet incredibly soft all at once. A perfect, otherworldly sensation.</p>

<p>’Yeah,’ he thought, feeling the collective warmth of five heartbeats surrounding him. ’Dominance as a man is one thing... but being treated like this? That’s what winning in life truly is like.’</p>
