<p>Her eyes began swirling maniacally with manic intensity as she listed her sudden, all-consuming needs. "I want to seduce him whole, body and mind. I want to cook for him, to dress how he likes, to understand him on the deepest of levels. To learn his past, his friends, his family... His enemies..." she added the last part with the most ominous of eyes.</p>
<p>Luna smirked at the sight. The brutal, possessive obsession of the demoness was perfectly clear. And she was all for it. The Storm Valkyrie found Kaiden’s newest find one amusing woman.</p>
<p>"You’ve come to the right place then," Luna stated, clearly intrigued by the depth of Calypso’s commitment. "I’ll teach you everything I know." Then the cheeky girl sent Kaiden a glance that told him he was in grave trouble.</p>
<p>The moment of friendly alliance was shattered as Aria stepped forward, cutting the space between Luna and Calypso.</p>
<p>"I am Aria," she declared with her silver eyes locked on the demoness. She put heavy, pointed emphasis on her next words. "The first wife Kaiden interacted with!"</p>
<p>Luna and Nyx shared a dry look. First wife? They both knew the situation was complicated. While Kaiden did meet Aria first, they all accepted the designation of being his lovers at the same time. If anything, Luna was technically the first to physically initiate things with him, giving him a lap dance and sloppy head during their ice-breaker game.</p>
<p>But... on another note, they had to admit that it was Aria who first went all the way with him.</p>
<p>That is, if one did not count Angel, the pornstar Kaiden had to fuck into the bed until she screamed obscenities, which then allowed him to do the deed with all three girls instead of just one of them.</p>
<p>Putting that aside, Aria had also used the term "wife," which was a giant overreach. There was no ring, no ceremony, no wedding vows. They were girlfriend and boyfriend, or as the System designated them, the Paragon of Sin and his Sinsworn Valkyrie.</p>
<p>Calypso didn’t miss the subtext. Aria called her not the first monster girl as Bastet had, but the first woman. She was declaring herself the queen, human or monster.</p>
<p>The demoness’s own eyes narrowed, mirroring the intensity of the Valkyrie’s. She calmly stepped forward, offering her hand with a sharp movement.</p>
<p>"It is a pleasure to meet you, I suppose," Calypso replied, emphasizing the name with a tone that matched Aria’s own.</p>
<p>Their hands clasped, and the two women glared fiercely into each other’s eyes. It was a silent declaration of war disguised as a simple handshake, a tense, potent moment of feminine competition.</p>
<p>Calypso’s possessiveness collided directly with Aria’s self-appointed seniority.</p>
<p>The tension between them was a tangible force, pulling the focus of the entire area.</p>
<p>Kaiden sighed inwardly. His life was indeed one of constant drama.</p>
<p>He stepped forward and put one large hand on Aria’s silver head and the other on Calypso’s black hair. He began gently stroking both.</p>
<p>Both women gasped at the sudden, unexpected touch. The intense focus they had on their rivalry instantly broke, replaced by rapt attention on their shared man.</p>
<p>"I’ve been through this with Nyx and Luna already," Kaiden stated. "It’s okay to have some friendly competition, but I won’t have you two be outright antagonistic toward each other. You are family members now."</p>
<p>Both Aria and Calypso grimaced. They realized their mistake instantly.</p>
<p>They shouldn’t have put on this ugly display before their man. Such a raw scene of female antagonism was meant for female eyes only; showing it to their shared lover was a sign of poor form.</p>
<p>Calypso quickly lost her aggressive stance. She pulled her hand from Aria’s and apologized fervently to Kaiden. "I got carried away, Darling. Forgive me! Demons like aggressiveness, and we’re very territorial, it’s in my blood!"</p>
<p>Aria, meanwhile, immediately leaned in, pressing her face against his chest for a needy hug. "I’m sorry, Kai... Seeing how fervently you made love to this woman made me very jealous. I promise I will behave."</p>
<p>Kaiden felt the genuine acceptance of his desire for harem peace. He knew this might not be the complete end of their rivalry, but he was happy he made them see logic. He liked some sexy kitten fights, especially if they happened on the pillows, but he drew the line at brutal dog fights between his women.</p>
<p>Luna and Nyx exchanged a look that conveyed volumes. The silent exchange said: Yeah, this guy is clueless. He thinks a simple talk will solve a turf war between a Queen of Hell and the self-proclaimed First Wife. They knew the real battle was only just beginning, and they were ready to enjoy the show - from the sidelines, this time.</p>
<p>Kaiden scanned the remaining sister-wives before settling his gaze on the other monster girl.</p>
<p>He sent Bastet a pointed glance. The gorgeous felinid squirmed under his eyes as she got the message; she should not fight Calypso either. "Yes, Master..." she agreed with a little smile. Despite being chastised, she was happy. Even being scolded by Kaiden made her heart flutter.</p>
<p>"Cali, do you think your dungeon is still active?" Kaiden asked after turning his attention back to Calypso.</p>
<p>The words immediately struck the demoness. Her pointed demonic ears and her long tail twitched violently. Her eyes widened in a mix of surprise and confusion. "Cali, Darling? Do you mean me?"</p>
<p>Kaiden smiled. "Yeah, Calypso is a wonderful name, I love it. But it feels a bit of a mouthful. When we’re not in an official setting, how about I call you Cali instead?"</p>
<p>The question dissolved the last of Calypso’s self-control. She let out a high-pitched, feminine squeal of immense joy. "Kyaa!!"</p>
<p>Her body seemed to melt, and she threw her arms around Kaiden’s neck, hugging him fiercely. Being given a pet name was another step in his personal claim, a layer of intimacy she hadn’t dared to hope for so soon.</p>
<p>Aria, who was still hugging Kaiden from the side, had to fight back a mighty feminine scoff from leaving her lips as she was shoved aside by a little motion of Calypso’s behind.</p>
<p>The few remaining humans watching the scene sighed wearily. The fierce Boss Monster they had feared not long ago was now having the immense mood swings of a teenage girl in love.</p>
<p>If a fight had erupted between monsters and humans when Calypso appeared, nearly all of the humans would’ve been butchered cruelly. Such was the might and size of the demon horde.</p>

<p>"I still have my connection to it, Darling!" she declared. "The link remains strong!"</p>
<p>Kaiden nodded, his expression thoughtful. "Good."</p>
<p>He took Calypso’s hand while his eyes scanned his family, his allies, and his new demons... If they could be called his. Their loyalty was to be tested.</p>
<p>The battle was over, the prize claimed, but the work was only beginning.</p>
<p>"It’s time to check what’s going on with a dungeon that just had its boss monster subjugated," Kaiden decreed. Geralt reluctantly nodded.</p>
