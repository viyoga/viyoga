<p>Nyx was the first to break the silence.</p>
<p>Then Luna followed, snorting with barely suppressed laughter before it spilled over.</p>
<p>"You’ve got one hell of a little sister," Nyx managed to mouth the words, wiping a tear from the corner of her eye.</p>

<p>Kaiden rubbed the side of his head, sighing.</p>
<p>Across from them, Aria and Bastet didn’t laugh, but they weren’t exactly stone-faced either. Aria looked... unsure. She tilted her head, brows drawn in concern. Bastet, meanwhile, rested her chin on her fist, golden eyes narrowed in thoughtful amusement.</p>

<p>"It’s not like that. Alice is just..." He paused, searching for the right word. "Clingy. That’s all. She’s gonna grow out of it. For sure."</p>
<p>"She’s gonna grow out of it."</p>
<p>"For sure."</p>
<p>Nyx and Luna echoed in perfect sync, each word dripping with sarcasm.</p>

<p>None of them looked ready to drop the subject.</p>
<p>They weren’t judging him. But they weren’t pretending this was normal either. Kaiden sighed the third time, heavier than ever before.</p>
<p>"Anyways... Let’s shift focus."</p>
<p>The girls laughed together at him running away, but didn’t press the matter any further.</p>

<p>The System window returned.</p>

<p>*Clap!*</p>
<p>Nyx clapped her hands once, sharp and loud.</p>
<p>"Okay! Hear me out," she murmured with excited eyes.</p>
<p>"Instead of taking this side quest as us assuming that the system is saying we’re doing horribly... maybe we should take it as the system saying we’re just not doing enough."</p>
<p>Kaiden raised a brow. "Not enough?"</p>
<p>Nyx nodded. "Exactly! We’ve been dipping our toes into things, trying to be versatile, trying a little bit of everything. But the fangirl metric doesn’t reward ’a little of everything.’ It rewards obsession. We need to lean in, hard. Stop playing it safe and greedy."</p>
<p>Kaiden was a bit skeptical as he asked, "What do you suggest we do, then?"</p>
<p>Nyx leaned back in her chair, tapping a finger against her lips in exaggerated thought.</p>
<p>Silence hung for a beat.</p>
<p>Then her lips curled into a grin. Slow, confident, and utterly devilish.</p>
<p>"We need to rebrand."</p>
<p>"...Rebrand?" Aria asked, confused.</p>
<p>Luna didn’t speak. She just narrowed her eyes, her amusement gone, replaced with focused scrutiny. She knew Nyx well enough to recognize when her gears were turning fast.</p>
<p>Bastet remained silent but watchful. She didn’t know enough about human entertainment strategy to add to the conversation in any meaningful way for now, but she was clearly intrigued. After all, her master’s future growth potential was the topic being discussed before her.</p>
<p>Nyx nodded, "Rebrand, yes."</p>
<p>Her eyes were sharp now, the usual mischief replaced by genuine strategy. "Think back to that night at the sushi place. When Kaiden made us his Valkyries after the porn shoot."</p>
<p>Her gaze swept across the room, catching the gazes of the other two Valkyries.</p>
<p>"He told us his vision. That we were going to storm the world with content so bold, so captivating, so insane that no one would be able to ignore it. That we’d rise to the very top, not just in numbers, not just in influence, but in legacy. He wanted our faces etched into the damn mythos of this post-apocalypse world."</p>
<p>"And that’s perhaps just a tiny bit too ambitious. At least, for now. What we’re seeing now—what this bottleneck quest is telling us—is that there’s a consequence to trying to appeal to everyone at once."</p>
<p>Kaiden furrowed his brow, listening to his girlfriend intently.</p>

Some content for women, some for men, some for the lonely, the horny, the curious, the bloodthirsty. And it’s worked. We dominate in views and income."</p>
<p>She lifted a finger.</p>
<p>"But that third metric? That devotion? It doesn’t care how wide we cast the net. It only cares about depth. About emotional entanglement. About fixation."</p>

<p>"So here’s what I’m proposing. We stop worrying about appealing to everyone on Planet Earth. We accept a drop in views and income. We narrow our focus. We build something targeted. Intimate. The kind of content that drills deep into the soul of a viewer and stays there."</p>
