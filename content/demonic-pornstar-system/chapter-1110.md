<p>Milo waited for the next line so intently that he forgot Ben still had hold of his sleeve.</p>
<p>He had reached the settlement. Taigi had shaken his hand, a Skyfiend had carried him over the cavern, and somewhere beyond these trees were houses he might be allowed to live in.</p>
<p>The dungeon could still send him back.</p>
<p>Beside him, Ben’s grip tightened. Milo wanted to tell him it would be alright, but he was afraid that if he opened his mouth, he would ask Mrs. Weller the same thing.</p>
<p>[Assessment complete.]</p>
<p>[Eligible for Dungeon Integration.]</p>
<p>"I got it."</p>
<p>The words came out breathlessly. Milo read the notification again, desperate to make certain he had understood, then turned to Ben.</p>
<p>"It says I’m eligible!"</p>
<p>"Me too!"</p>
<p>Ben released his sleeve to grab his shoulder instead, grinning so hard that Milo began grinning back. The relief was enormous. All the way here, he had been trying to imagine how he would bear leaving if the dungeon refused him.</p>
<p>Now it wanted him.</p>
<p>"Mrs. Weller?"</p>
<p>Lucy had caught their teacher’s hand. Mrs. Weller was reading her own unseen message, with tears gathering behind her glasses.</p>
<p>"Yes, sweetheart. Me too."</p>
<p>"All of us?"</p>
<p>At the woman’s nod to her, Lucy pressed against her side. Milo put his cup on their blanket before reaching for Mrs. Weller’s free hand, suddenly needing to make certain she was coming with them.</p>
<p>She squeezed his fingers.</p>
<p>Around their tree, families were discovering one another’s results. A mother laughed through her tears as her son kept asking whether she had the same words, while an elderly man pulled his wife into his arms without waiting for her to finish reading aloud.</p>
<p>"Mr. Grey?"</p>
<p>The man beside the neighboring blanket had remained apart from the embraces. When Kaiden approached him, he lowered his eyes.</p>
<p>"It says I’m still bound to Earth. I thought... I wanted to come."</p>
<p>"Did you want to come, or did you merely lack other options?"</p>
<p>"They’re rebuilding our street..." He rubbed a hand over his mouth. "I keep checking the pictures... Maybe... I’m sorry..."</p>
<p>Kaiden smiled.</p>
<p>"I hope you get your home back. We’ll arrange your return."</p>
<p>"You’re not angry?"</p>
<p>"You came to find out whether this could be your home. It can’t. That’s okay."</p>
<p>The man looked up at him, then managed a grateful nod to Kaiden. A Dungeon-Born carrying a tray paused beside his blanket to offer him water while Kaiden turned toward the other visitors.</p>
<p>Milo’s next notification appeared.</p>
<p>[Kaiden Grey invites you to become an inhabitant of his domain.]</p>
<p>[Acceptance will relinquish your bond to Earth and bind your life to this Dungeon Core. Your race will become Dungeon-Born.]</p>
<p>[The domain’s binding covenant is available for review.]</p>
<p>[Do you accept?]</p>
<p>He had known what the words would say. He had argued with Mrs. Weller about them, filled out the application, and waited for this moment with an impatience that made the days at the shelter unbearable.</p>
<p>Now his eyes kept returning to Earth.</p>
<p>His mother had lived there. His father had lived there. Every memory he had of being their son belonged to the world this invitation was asking him to leave.</p>
<p>"Milo?"</p>
<p>Mrs. Weller bent toward him when he failed to answer.</p>
<p>"What is it, sweetheart?"</p>
<p>"My parents..."</p>
<p>He tried to explain, embarrassed by how quickly his throat had tightened. Ben had gone silent beside him.</p>
<p>"I still want to go home sometimes. Even though I know they’re gone. I think about telling Mom things, and then I remember."</p>
<p>Mrs. Weller drew him against her side, keeping hold of Lucy with her other hand.</p>
<p>"Oh, Milo."</p>
<p>"If I say yes..."</p>
<p>He read the waiting question again. He wanted this home desperately, but accepting it felt cruel for a moment, as though he were agreeing that the old one no longer mattered.</p>
<p>"I don’t want to leave them behind."</p>
<p>"Do you remember what Commander Taigi said about her father?"</p>
<p>Milo found Taigi helping a family understand their invitations, crouched beside their blanket with her saber resting against her leg.</p>
<p>"She still misses him."</p>
<p>"She does. She remembers what he taught her too. You can keep everything you love about your parents. Being happy here won’t take any of that away from you."</p>
<p>His father would never hear about the Skyfiend. Milo had been so excited that, for a little while, he had forgotten how badly he wanted to tell him.</p>
<p>He cried then, with his face against Mrs. Weller’s cardigan. She held him while he struggled through it, and Ben stayed close enough that their shoulders touched.</p>
<p>"I’m coming too," his teacher murmured. "We’ll find our way together."</p>
<p>Milo wiped his face before looking up at her. She was frightened; he could see it even through her smile. She had brought them here anyway, and now she was offering to stay.</p>
<p>"Thank you."</p>
<p>She kissed the top of his head.</p>
<p>Across the clearing, Kaiden told everyone to take their time. The invitations would wait while they read the covenant and spoke with their families.</p>
<p>Milo looked toward the timber homes beyond the blankets. Children who lived here had come to watch, peering around the adults with open curiosity. He wanted to meet them. He wanted to wake up here tomorrow and discover what they did all day.</p>
<p>The longing made him turn back to his invitation.</p>
<p>"I accept."</p>
<p>Warmth struck beneath his ribs, making him gasp against Mrs. Weller’s side.</p>
<p>"Milo?"</p>
<p>"It doesn’t hurt. It’s..."</p>
<p>He drew back to look down at himself. Through his shirt, light shone from his chest and spread along his arms, following channels he could feel opening beneath his skin.</p>
<p>Mana.</p>
<p>He had heard people talk about it all his life. Now it was flowing through him, reaching his fingertips when he spread them, answering as he curled his hand into a fist.</p>
<p>"I can feel it!"</p>
<p>Ben stared at the light beneath Milo’s sleeve before turning back to his own invitation.</p>
<p>"I accept!"</p>
<p>His shout joined voices rising across the clearing. Mrs. Weller’s answer came close to Milo’s ear, with Lucy speaking just after her, and the warmth brightened around their joined hands.</p>
<p>Beyond them, Celia Harcourt whispered her consent.</p>
<p>Light ran between the trees as the core formed its bonds. Families held one another through it, their faces shining in the glow from their own bodies. A little boy shrieked with excitement when he discovered that even his toes were glowing, making his mother laugh while she tried to put his shoe back on.</p>
<p>[Dungeon Vein established.]</p>
<p>[Race: Human ? Dungeon-Born (Human-Origin).]</p>
<p>The warmth deepened inside Milo. Beneath his own heartbeat, he could feel the source feeding him mana, vast and alive somewhere beyond the clearing. His new Dungeon Vein connected him to it, and through that bond the core was sustaining the body he had brought into its world.</p>
<p>He belonged here now.</p>
<p>"Mrs. Weller..."</p>
<p>She looked down at him, her glasses shining with the last of the light.</p>
<p>"I know, sweetheart."</p>
<p>Milo hugged her again, laughing this time. Ben crowded against her other side while Lucy clung to her waist, leaving their teacher struggling to get her arms around everyone.</p>
<p>Across the clearing, Taigi watched a newly changed father lift his daughter into the air. The girl spread her glowing fingers above his head, squealing when he kissed her belly through her shirt.</p>
<p>"There are so many of them," the commander murmured.</p>
<p>Her voice caught. Kaiden turned toward her, and she smiled at him with tears in her eyes, too proud and happy to hide them.</p>
<p>"Our people will have quite a lot of neighbors, my lord."</p>
<p>"Yeah. They will."</p>
<p>He looked over the families still holding one another and raised his voice enough to reach them.</p>
<p>"Welcome home, everyone."</p>
<p>"Thank you!"</p>
<p>Milo shouted it with Ben. Other voices joined them, some laughing, some barely managing the words through their tears, and Kaiden grinned as a woman lifted her little girl’s hand to wave at him.</p>
<p>Then another window opened before Milo.</p>
<p>[Select your Lifestyle Path.]</p>
<p>[Combatant: Develop through battle and training.]</p>
<p>[Supporter: Develop through creation, care, and skilled work.]</p>
<p>"Oh!"</p>
<p>Ben had seen it too. He caught Milo’s eye, and they exchanged a grin before Mrs. Weller could finish reading her options.</p>
<p>"Combatant!"</p>
<p>They said it together.</p>
<p>"Boys, perhaps you could at least read-"</p>
<p>Milo’s fingers tingled as the core directed more mana through his limbs. He flexed his arm, fascinated by the new strength gathering in it, while Ben tugged his sleeve up to inspect his own.</p>
<p>"Can you see anything?"</p>
<p>"I think so."</p>
<p>"You had that muscle before," Lucy informed him.</p>
<p>"Yeah, but it’s better now."</p>
<p>Mrs. Weller removed her glasses to wipe them. By the time she put them back on, the boys had begun comparing their arms.</p>
<p>"Supporter," she said firmly.</p>
<p>Her own confirmation arrived, followed by another message.</p>
<p>[Prior experience recognized.]</p>
<p>[Path Expression available: Nurturer.]</p>
<p>[Help those in your care learn, grow, and recover. Continued practice will develop specialized abilities.]</p>
<p>"Oh..."</p>
<p>At the change in her voice, Milo stopped examining his arm.</p>
<p>"What did you get?"</p>
<p>"Nurturer. It says the work I’ve already done..."</p>
<p>She read the message again. For all the meals she had found, the blankets she had tucked around frightened children, she had spent so many nights afraid of how little she could do if danger reached them.</p>
<p>Now the dungeon was offering to help her become better at caring for them. The same hands could learn to ease more pain, help more children grow, and gain strength through the work.</p>
<p>She accepted with a shaky smile.</p>
<p>[Path Expression: Supporter - Nurturer.]</p>
<p>"Will you get magic too?" Lucy asked.</p>
<p>"Perhaps I will, sweetheart. I’ll have to learn what it can do."</p>
<p>...</p>
<p>Celia Harcourt had chosen Supporter before the boys finished admiring themselves.</p>
