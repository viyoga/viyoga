<p>The Kaiju roared. Rawer than the arrogant bellow it had used to recall the Ravager from Scarlet’s cage, this one was torn from the chest of a creature that had watched its board dissolve in a handful of turns and struggled to keep up.</p>
<p>All six eyes locked on Kaiden through the dimensional barrier.</p>

<p>Every champion it had was locked in combat or dead, all except one. C8. The Shade, drifting alone at E2, the last piece the Kaiju could move.</p>
<p>Kaiden could see the decision forming behind those six eyes, slow and agonized, because the creature’s brain had never been built for choices like this.</p>
<p>It had two options.</p>
<p>Send C8 straight down column E to Row 6. The finish line. Four turns of unopposed advance with no defender piece on the board to stop it, and the match was over.</p>
<p>The Kaiju wins while every one of his girls bleeds in an arena they can’t leave.</p>
<p>Or send C8 to reinforce Champion 5 against the three women who engaged it.</p>
<p>Kaiden kept his face still because the Kaiju was watching him.</p>
<p>The Kaiju’s massive body shifted behind its board. Its claws scraped the ground, grinding deep furrows into the surface, and a low rumble built in its throat that was half growl and half confusion.</p>
<p>For a long moment the creature swayed between the two decisions with the visible distress of a predator being forced to think in a way its brain was never designed for.</p>
