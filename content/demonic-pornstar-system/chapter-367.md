<p>Title: Valhalla’s Sinners gave me back my bond with my daughter.</p>
<p>I’ll be honest: I never cared about adult content. It wasn’t my thing, and whenever I heard about Kaiden Grey, I dismissed it as "not for me."</p>
<p>But after everything that’s come out about ChronosX and Maximilian Vice, I decided to do a little research. I wanted to know what this group was really about. And that’s when I stumbled on their variety content.</p>
<p>Cooking streams. Bath conversations. Gym training. Lighthearted moments that felt... real.</p>
<p>Curious, I asked my daughter if she wanted to watch with me. She’s a teenager, and lately I’ve been feeling that slow drift all parents dread; her pulling away, finding her own world, her own people, while I’m left behind. As a single father, I was especially anxious because I didn’t have a partner. She’s all I have.</p>
<p>I thought she’d roll her eyes at me.</p>
<p>But she didn’t. She sat down, we watched together... and for the first time in months, I saw her light up.</p>
<p>We laughed at their colorful banter. We paused to talk about the food they were making, theorizing what the monster meals might best compare to.</p>
<p>Then we compared ourselves to their training. And we decided, right then and there, that we’d follow along with their regimen - adapted to our bodies, of course. We’re no superhumans who can brush off car crashes and punches by professional boxers.</p>
<p>I’m a little out of shape due to my office job, and she’s a bit chubby (her word, not mine. Please don’t hurt Papa, Honey...), but instead of feeling embarrassed, she actually seemed excited. I thought that maybe this could help her confidence increase.</p>
<p>And then she surprised me even more. She picked out Nyx as her idol. She loves her confidence, her style, her spark. She begged me to let her order clothes inspired by Nyx’s training gear. (The family-friendly ones... I already said yes.) We plan to train together.</p>
<p>Furthermore, I threw all of our processed, unhealthy foods into the garbage and replaced them with the type of food I often see Kaiden or Aria eat, as they seem to be the most... dedicated to their diets, I’ll say.</p>
<p>It’s silly, I know. But it feels like we found our thing. A way to stay connected during these years when she’s growing so fast and pulling away.</p>
<p>So to Kaiden, Nyx, Aria, Luna, and Bastet, I’ll say this: thank you. Thank you for creating something that reaches beyond battles and beyond adult content. Thank you for giving me a way to connect with my daughter again.</p>
<p>I don’t know what the future holds, but I know this: we’ll face it together.</p>
<p>A grateful dad</p>
