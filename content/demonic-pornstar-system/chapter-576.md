<p>Kaiden lifted his gaze fully to the colossal entity.</p>
<p>For the first time since his arrival, it moved.</p>
<p>There was no sudden violence to it. No dramatic roar or quake. The titan did not awaken so much as acknowledge. Reality bent in subtle ways as three of its seven arms began to shift, massive limbs gliding with the inevitability of continental drift. The sky above them seemed to draw inward with clouds spiraling tighter as if pulled by a gravity that had only just been switched on.</p>
<p>The arm bearing the red orb moved first.</p>
<p>Wrath burned within it, compressed and absolute.</p>
<p>The second followed, the orange sphere of Gluttony sliding forward.</p>
<p>Then the third arm stirred.</p>
<p>Purple light bled into the darkness as Pride advanced, vast and commanding.</p>
<p>The three arms extended toward one another.</p>
<p>The orbs touched.</p>
<p>For a heartbeat, the realm held its breath.</p>
<p>Then the colors began to bleed together.</p>
<p>Red did not overpower orange. Orange did not smother purple. Instead, they wove threads of fury, hunger, and dominance, braiding them into something deeper. The combined sphere pulsed slowly and had its surface look alive with shifting hues that never quite settled, mesmerizing in their balance. It was not chaotic, not at all. A synthesis rather than a collision.</p>
<p>Kaiden felt the pressure spike.</p>
<p>The combined orb detached from the titan’s hands and began to drift toward him.</p>
<p>It moved slowly. There was no rush in it, no need to chase.</p>
<p>His instincts screamed at him to move. To dodge. To retreat. He knew, with absolute clarity, that he could step aside and let it pass. His body was ready for motion, muscles coiled, mana stirring on reflex...</p>
<p>And then he understood.</p>
<p>This wasn’t an attack meant to be avoided.</p>
<p>This was a question.</p>
<p>Kaiden’s jaw set. His expression hardened with resolve. He inhaled slowly, grounding himself, letting the breath steady his pulse instead of feeding the surge of instinct clawing for control.</p>
<p>"So that’s how it is," he said quietly.</p>
<p>Kaiden stepped forward.</p>
<p>One step. Then another.</p>
<p>Each footfall was measured, his posture straight, shoulders squared as he met the approaching mass head-on. Whatever this trial was meant to strip from him, whatever it intended to test or expose, he would not face it by flinching away.</p>
<p>"I’m ready," he decreed.</p>
<p>The orb reached him.</p>
<p>Light swallowed his vision. However, despite his expectations, the light did not fade. Instead, what had swallowed Kaiden sank into him, heavy and invasive, as if the orb had liquefied and poured straight through his skin. Pressure became weight. Weight became presence. His bones hummed, not with pain per se, but with something settling in, rearranging him from the inside out.</p>
<p>Wrath ignited first.</p>
<p>Heat crawled through his veins, thick and stubborn, a refusal to cool no matter how his body tried to regulate it.</p>
<p>Then came Gluttony.</p>
<p>A pull, deep in his core, not hunger but space. His body felt suddenly... larger on the inside as if every cell had been hollowed out and reinforced at the same time, capable of holding far more than it should.</p>
<p>Pride followed last.</p>
<p>The world aligned around him with terrifying obedience.</p>
<p>And then...</p>
<p>Kaiden staggered.</p>
<p>His breath hitched as he reached inward on reflex, seeking the familiar overlay of menus and sigils.</p>
<p>Nothing answered.</p>
<p>No system window bloomed before his eyes.</p>
<p>No cooldown indicators. No stance descriptions. No system voice to ground him.</p>
<p>"... Are you there, my antisocial system?" he tried.</p>
<p>His voice echoed back at him, thin and unassisted.</p>
<p>"This is really not the time to remain quiet. Please, just once, stop messing with me."</p>
<p>Silence.</p>
<p>Kaiden tried again, sharper this time. "Open status."</p>
<p>Nothing.</p>
<p>The connection was gone, smothered. It was buried beneath something denser and older.</p>
<p>Despite both his connection to the universal system and his personal one being suppressed, he could feel the stances, undeniably so... but there were no abilities attached to them. No spells to call. No structured techniques to lean on.</p>
<p>Just power.</p>
<p>Unshaped. Uninterpreted.</p>
<p>"Is this akin to taking the training wheels off?" Kaiden grumbled, not liking it. He didn’t realize how deeply ingrained he had gotten to the comforts of being an awakened, and even more so, of having the Demonic Pornstar System in his mind - or wherever it was residing in his body, soul, whatever.</p>
<p>Suddenly, he felt naked and vulnerable, more alone than ever since he slipped on a banana peel and awoke with a far different future awaiting him.</p>
<p>But perhaps that was the point.</p>
<p>Kaiden steadied his breathing and lifted his head again, eyes narrowing as he regarded the distant titan. It returned to being motionless. Evidently, it could move, but it still didn’t look like a living being to Kaiden... Putting it into proper words was difficult for him. Truthfully, this titan was both mesmerizing and ominous at once, making him want to get closer and try to understand what it was, but also want to bolt in the other direction and come back once he’s much stronger.</p>
<p>But alas, that was not an option. To become stronger, he had to confront both the challenge and his nerves.</p>
<p>"No instructions...?" Kaiden muttered, flexing his fingers. "Guess we’re improvising."</p>
<p>He reached inward, searching for the feeling he now knew was Wrath.</p>
<p>The shift was instant.</p>
<p>His heartbeat slammed harder. Heat surged up his spine, sharp and violent, and the world snapped into brutal clarity. Every shadow looked like an enemy. Every inch of space felt like something to be conquered or shattered. His muscles coiled with explosive readiness, power screaming to be unleashed.</p>
<p>And just as quickly, he cut it off. He did so because the moment Wrath surfaced, the titan reacted.</p>
<p>From its immense form, a pulse of deep crimson radiated outward. The sky darkened, clouds tearing into jagged streaks, and the ground ahead split open with violent cracks that bled red light. The realm felt angrier, louder, more unstable, as if reality itself had taken offense.</p>
<p>"Fuck me, I can’t believe I switched it off..." Kaiden grimaced. He actually paused because he got a really bad premonition. ’Or... Perhaps I was just scared.’</p>
<p>The thought unsettled him. Being alone, without his girls, without his system, and locked in some weird location with this titanic creature he couldn’t understand was unnerving Kaiden greatly.</p>
<p>However, this allowed him to realize something crucial. He had to enter a stance for the titan to react, probably beginning a challenge of sorts. This meant he could check the other two stances out as well.</p>
<p>Kaiden decided quickly. "Yeah... that’s going on the later list."</p>
<p>Wrath was far too ominous. He felt it was best to tackle the others first.</p>
<p>He took a steadying breath before calling upon Pride.</p>
<p>This time, the change was colder.</p>
<p>His spine straightened fully, shoulders settling into perfect alignment. The world organized itself around him with unnerving compliance. Distance, threat, relevance, everything slotted neatly into invisible hierarchies. He felt tall, not just physically but existentially as well, as though the ground beneath his feet existed to justify his presence upon it.</p>
<p>Again, the titan answered.</p>
<p>A wave of purple authority rolled through the realm. The fractured ground smoothed into vast, geometric planes. The sky sharpened, clouds forming rigid patterns instead of chaos. Even the darkness seemed to behave, holding itself to an unspoken standard.</p>
<p>Kaiden’s jaw tightened.</p>
