<p>She fed Carnage into the downward swing, burying her axe in the softened chest. Beside her blade, Kaiden rammed a wedge shaped from his blood hammer, switching from Envy to Wrath to blast the split open.</p>
<p>Calypso tore through the exposed mechanisms with a delighted, breathless cry. As she wrenched her axe free, the warrior toppled beside Kaiden, whose legs finally gave out beneath him.</p>
<p>"Everybody?"</p>
<p>"Here," Nyx answered breathlessly.</p>
<p>"Fine," Luna managed. "Fucking... perfect."</p>
<p>Once everyone had answered, he released Wrath and drew the blood back into his gauntlet, grinning at the notification in his sight.</p>
<p>[Level: 85 ? 86.]</p>
<p>"Finally."</p>
<p>His grin broadened as he read it again. After all that climbing, all those stubborn shields, and a final opponent determined to beat him flat, he had earned another level.</p>
<p>Beside the wreck, Calypso leaned over her axe with sweat dripping from her chin.</p>
<p>"Did you get it, Darling?"</p>
<p>"Eighty-six."</p>
<p>"Good." She smiled at him, far too pleased to disguise it. "Now this demoness shall take a beauty break..."</p>
<p>Calypso sank onto the broken column, her axe resting against her thigh. Her chest rose and fell beneath her damaged armor, and when she tried to push damp hair away from her face, her first attempt missed.</p>
<p>Luna had already sat down on the dais steps. Sweat had plastered purple strands to her cheeks, but she seemed unwilling to lift a hand to deal with them.</p>
<p>"Whoever invented stairs," she said between breaths, "can choke on a big one."</p>
<p>"I’ll pass along the complaint if we meet them," Nyx murmured.</p>
<p>The space mage lowered herself beside Bastet at the base of a column. For once, she used her own hands to pull a flask from her bag, taking a long drink before offering it to the felinid.</p>
<p>"Here, tanned bestie."</p>
<p>"Thank you."</p>
<p>Bastet accepted it with both hands. Her ears had drooped, and the gold ornaments in her hair hung crookedly above a face flushed from exertion. After drinking, she rested against Nyx, who gladly leaned back into her.</p>
<p>Kaiden wanted to join them. Getting off the floor would have to come first, and the discovery of how much that hurt made him briefly reconsider the arrangement.</p>
<p>"Kai, your side."</p>
<p>Aria crouched beside him, keeping her weight on the hand she had planted on the tiles. Her silver hair was damp against her neck, her smile tired as she offered him the other hand.</p>
<p>"Could you let Lexi look before you try standing?"</p>
<p>Alexandra knelt at his other side with a weary little sigh before he could even respond. The restoration light gathering beneath her palms had lost much of its earlier brightness, but warmth still spread through the cut along his ribs as she worked.</p>
<p>"There," she murmured after the skin had closed. "Please be careful getting up. I can’t do much about the rest until my mana regenerates..."</p>
<p>"You’ve done plenty. Thank you."</p>
<p>Her smile made him squeeze her hand. When she sat back on her heels, Aria shifted closer and let the maid lean against her shoulder while Kaiden caught his breath.</p>
<p>He looked around at his family, scattered across the battered hall with sweat on their faces and dust worked into every seam of their clothes. Yunohana had joined Luna on the steps, her fan lying open across her lap while she tried to undo a knot in one of its cords.</p>
<p>She gave up and let her hands rest.</p>
<p>"We did rather well," she said.</p>
<p>"We did," he agreed, unable to keep the pride out of his voice.</p>
<p>He had enjoyed every hard-won opening, every moment when one of them made another’s attack possible. Seeing Yunohana find her place among them had made him happier still. Now he wanted them home, fed, and comfortable enough to enjoy what they had accomplished.</p>

<p>"Big Brother..."</p>
<p>Alice’s voice chimed faintly through it. He looked up at once, his amusement giving way to concern.</p>
<p>"I’m coming down."</p>
<p>He raised his arms before the halo dissolved. Golden light gathered in front of him, taking his sister’s familiar shape, and he caught her with one arm beneath her knees and the other supporting her back.</p>
<p>Alice’s head came to rest against his chest. Her hair brushed his chin as she curled closer, too exhausted even to arrange herself with her usual insistence.</p>
<p>"Alice..." He adjusted his hold. "Everything okay?"</p>
<p>"Mhm. Just tired."</p>
<p>Her fingers closed loosely around his shirt.</p>
<p>"Can you carry me, Big Brother?"</p>
<p>His arms ached. Looking down at her heavy eyelids, he found that he cared very little about that.</p>
<p>"Of course I can."</p>
<p>"All the way?"</p>
<p>"All the way."</p>
<p>She smiled against him, her eyes closing while he tucked her more securely into his arms. She had spent the day watching his back, lending him her strength, and burning through her own reserves whenever he needed help. He was very happy to let her stop.</p>
<p>Alexandra brushed a damp strand from Alice’s forehead, then looked up at him with a fond smile.</p>
<p>"I’ll get your bag."</p>
