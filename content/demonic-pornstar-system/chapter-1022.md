<p>"Maybe they’ll learn more of what the fuck just happened..." Luna muttered.</p>

<p>Kaiden could smell the blood before he saw the chairs.</p>
<p>They stood along the tiled wall, each fitted with straps across the chest and thighs. Narrow armrests ended in cuffs, with stained tubing coiled in trays beneath them. A drain ran along the floor between the chairs and a row of empty collection containers.</p>
<p>One of the soldiers lifted a lamp. Its light caught ragged fingernail marks on an armrest.</p>
<p>"She brought them down here to feed," Calypso said.</p>

<p>Beyond the chairs, a second door opened onto a room divided by metal bars. Thin mattresses covered the floors of the pens. Water bottles stood beside shallow food bowls, and a bundle of used dressings had been pushed through one set of bars into the passage.</p>
<p>Alexandra stopped beside him.</p>
<p>"She was feeding them just enough to keep doing it."</p>

<p>"The search is continuing, my lady. They’re checking every cellar."</p>
<p>Bastet studied a dark smear running from a chair to the drain. "How many might she have taken elsewhere before we arrived?"</p>
