<p>"[Kinetic Shard]." Her spell intonation resulted in the stones shattering into dozens of sharp fragments. With a push of her two hands, the shards shot toward the group of glowcap creepers in the form of a hailstorm made of miniature projectiles.</p>
<p>The luminescent monsters reacted quickly: their glowing tentacles whipped out with great speed. They deflected most of the shards mid-air, and the ones that hit only grazed their tough, rubbery hides, drawing small traces of bioluminescent fluid but failing to cause significant damage.</p>
<p>Nyx eyed the monsters as she evaluated the results of her actions. "I still need to increase my Magic stat if I want such measly projectiles to cause good damage, it seems. For now, I have to rely on greater objects to deal sufficient damage." <p>This spell allowed the Space Valkyrie to summon a weapon of her choice, and she could also coat it with one of the four basic elements: fire, water, wind, and earth. <p>The blade shot forward, propelled by her [Telekinesis]. Nyx used all her mental strength to make this projectile traverse through the air as swiftly as she could, and this effort of hers was boosted by her heavy investment into the Magic stat as well.</p>
<p>The targeted glowcap creeper tried to react by flailing its tentacles in a desperate attempt to block the incoming attack, but Nyx’s speed was too much for it. The flaming blade struck true, embedding itself deep in the creature’s spongy body.</p>
<p>Flames erupted from its core, consuming the monster from the inside.
