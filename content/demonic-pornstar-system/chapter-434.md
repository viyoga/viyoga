<p>It was a swirling vortex of energy, framed by two crumbling stone pillars, looking dark and unstable after the ordeal. It remained stubbornly active, waiting for its next visitors.</p>
<p>Calypso led the group to the swirling vortex of energy. "This is the gate to my domain." Her voice was now crisp and informative. "I have been defeated, but my home remains active. I can tell that the core of it is still pulsing strong."</p>

<p>This was a true demonic hellscape.</p>
