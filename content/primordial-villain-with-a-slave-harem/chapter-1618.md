<p>Quinlan led the elders out of the tomb.</p>
<p>The young shrine maidens were waiting in the courtyard where he'd left them, clustered in a loose knot near the path, and the copper-haired girl was the first to look up.</p>
<p>Her eyes went enormous.</p>
<p>The golden aura still poured from Quinlan in waves, warm and autumn-hued, spilling out through [Synchra]'s seams and curling around the armored frame.</p>
<p>She dropped to her knees.</p>
<p>The others followed, one after another, until the entire ring of young maidens was on the stone with their foreheads pressed to the flagstones.</p>
<p>"My Lord…"</p>
<p>Her voice shook.</p>
<p>"He was speaking the truth?!"</p>
<p>"I told you to act respectful!"</p>
<p>"But I-"</p>
<p>Quinlan raised a gauntlet.</p>
<p>"Let's not do this dance. You did nothing wrong. In fact, I'm grateful."</p>
<p>The maidens gasped at those words. They were about to throw an onslaught of protests at him, which Quinlan interrupted before they even began.</p>
<p>"My wife Seraphiel spent some time with Luminara." His voice carried the courtyard without effort. "The First Elf taught her to fight, but she also taught her other things."</p>
<p>He raised his hand.</p>
<p>"[Warp Gate]."</p>
<p>A ring of black light opened beside him, and a human maid with blonde hair stepped through with a wooden tray balanced across both palms. She bowed at Quinlan's side with the tray lifted toward him.</p>
<p>On it sat nine tiaras.</p>
<p>Each was carved from a pale wood that caught the sunlight like polished ivory. Fine vines wove through the bands, and living flowers bloomed along each circlet - pale gold at the heart, deepening to cream at the edges, their stems rooted into the wood itself.</p>
<p>The elders inhaled sharply.</p>
<p>The staff-bearer's wrinkled hand rose halfway to her mouth, and her old eyes had gone wide. Another elder pressed both palms to her chest. Even Isveth, who had been getting more composed since her kowtow, had drawn a breath and not released it.</p>
<p>Quinlan looked at the nine youngsters.</p>
<p>"Mind if I give you something?"</p>
<p>Nine pairs of eyes went wide.</p>
<p>Eighteen ears stood straight up.</p>
<p>They formed a line in front of him without a word being spoken. Copper-haired girl first. The others slotted in behind her in a neat row, hands clasped in front of their robes, spines very straight, ears twitching.</p>
<p>Zero hesitation.</p>
<p>Quinlan grinned at their enthusiasm and lifted the first tiara.</p>
<p>He placed it on the copper-haired girl's head, settling it gently into her hair. The flowers brushed her ear, and the ear twitched once, wildly, and a small broken sound left her.</p>
<p>He moved to the next.</p>
<p>Then the next. Then the last. Nine circlets, nine brave young maidens who stood guard over him while he was casting [Overlord's Eyes], each settled into their hair with the same gentle hand.</p>
<p>The copper-haired girl had both hands pressed over her mouth. Tears ran down between her fingers.</p>
<p>A second young maiden lifted her face. The question came out of her before she could stop it.</p>
<p>"Does this mean…"</p>
<p>She trailed off. Then she tried again, smaller.</p>
<p>"Does this mean Lady Luminara is alive...?"</p>
<p>Quinlan looked at her.</p>
<p>He held the silence for a long second.</p>
<p>Then his mouth curved, slow and warm.</p>
<p>"I hope you'll get to meet her one day without having to go to the Eternal Forest first."</p>
<p>The girl's breath caught.</p>
<p>Behind her, the staff-bearer covered her face with both hands and wept quietly. The other elders had gone very still.</p>
<p>"...she walks..."</p>
<p>One of the other youngsters was sobbing openly now, her hands hovering around the tiara's band, palms cupped without touching.</p>
<p>"My lord..." Her voice barely carried. "Please, my lord, I- I cannot- this is-"</p>
<p>Quinlan nipped that one in the bud.</p>
<p>"I'm sure both Lumi and Sera would be joyous to see their creation on your heads. Now, run along. Me and the elders have work to do."</p>
<p>The copper-haired girl's head jerked up.</p>
<p>"My lord- please, we- let us help. Whatever you ask."</p>
<p>"We can carry. We can keep watch. Anything."</p>
<p>Quinlan glanced at Isveth.</p>
<p>The Head Maiden straightened. Her braid had come half-loose during the earlier kowtow, and a strand had stuck to her tear-damp cheek, but she drew herself up with the authority of her office.</p>
<p>"While young, they have been ordained. They are members of this sanctuary, sworn to the service of the First Elf. Their rank is as true as mine."</p>
<p>"So be it."</p>
<p>Quinlan shrugged and raised his hand.</p>
<p>"[Warp Gate]."</p>
<p>A ring of black light opened at the base of Sylvaenor, and Quinlan stepped through with the elders and young maidens spilling out behind him.</p>
<p>The city rose above him in four tiers.</p>
<p>Ancient trees vast enough to swallow a fortress had been coaxed over epochs into dwellings, platforms, walkways. Spiral staircases wound through hollowed trunks. Vine bridges spanned the gaps between branches. Amber lantern-fruits hung in clusters from the leaves, already glowing faintly against the afternoon light.</p>
<p>Quinlan's boots settled on the root-plaza at the base of the oldest tree. Roots the size of riverbeds had spread outward from the heart-tree and flattened into a natural amphitheater, their grain polished smooth by a million pilgrim feet.</p>
<p>A few elves on the lower platforms paused mid-stride and looked down.</p>
<p>One dropped a basket.</p>
<p>Isveth stepped forward.</p>
<p>"Holy Son."</p>
<p>She faced him squarely. Her braid had been retied at some point between the tomb and here, and the authority of her office had returned fully to her shoulders.</p>
<p>"Please allow us to bring the city to you. The magistrates, the matriarchs, the warrior-captains. They should hear the summons from the right mouths. It is below your station to do this yourself."</p>
<p>Quinlan merely nodded and the elders turned as one. The young maidens were already gone.</p>
<p>…</p>
<p>On the second-tier market platform, the yellow-haired maiden, the same one who had draped a pilgrim's robe over Quinlan's pauldrons the first time she had seen him, planted herself in front of Lady Aurelel Vaer'ethas.</p>
<p>Lady Aurelel was in the middle of measuring six yards of pale-blue silk for a client.</p>
<p>"Lady Aurelel, you have to come to the root-plaza right now."</p>
<p>The matriarch glanced up from her measuring rod.</p>
<p>"...what."</p>
<p>"The Holy Son is here. The First Elf's own son. He's sitting and he's going to speak and you have to come."</p>
<p>"Child."</p>
<p>"Please."</p>
<p>"Have you been into the sacramental wine again?"</p>
<p>"Lady Aurelel! No!!"</p>
<p>The yellow-haired maiden grabbed a fistful of silk.</p>
<p>"Come on."</p>
<p>"Release my merchandise."</p>
<p>"No!"</p>
<p>"Child, I swear to the First Elf-"</p>
<p>Lady Aurelel glanced down at the small pale fingers clutching her sleeves. She looked up at the girl's face, at the wet eyes and the slightly-askew tiara and the ears flattened back in distress.</p>
<p>She set the rod aside.</p>
<p>"I will come. But I will also be having a very long conversation with both you and your Head Maiden about-"</p>
<p>"Thank you!"</p>
<p>…</p>
<p>On the magistrates' terrace three tiers above, one of the elders had walked past a startled aide and planted her staff in front of Magistrate Syllen Virethia's chamber door.</p>
<p>The aide rose to intercept her.</p>
<p>"Elder, the magistrate is in session, she cannot-"</p>
<p>"Child."</p>
<p>The elder did not turn her head.</p>
<p>"Do you deny the First Elf?"</p>
