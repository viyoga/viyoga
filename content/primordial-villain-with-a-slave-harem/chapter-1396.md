<p>Quinlan felt it the moment he stepped forward.</p>
<p>Attention settled on him from every direction. Threads of interest brushed against his skin, old and patient presences taking stock. Many of them did not know what he was capable of. To most, he was simply Luminara's and Miri's adopted son, the late arrival, the curiosity.</p>
<p>He rolled his shoulders once and raised both arms.</p>
<p>Mana slid into place, clean and familiar.</p>
<p>Wind answered first.</p>
<p>The air thickened around his forearms, pressure building as he pushed outward. A rush rolled across the clearing, grass bending, loose fabric snapping. The force surged ahead in the form of a broad shove aimed at the incoming giant.</p>
<p>The wind struck Hagnar's massive form and… slipped.</p>
<p>It rippled along his skin, tugged at his hair, fluttered against his clothes. The man drifted through it without slowing, still hurtling toward the village with an expression of rising panic. "You'll need something much stronger…" he murmured, half begging.</p>
<p>"Oh, an elemental mage?" Kiryssa tilted her head, silver eyes narrowing with interest.</p>
<p>Hanae's eyes narrowed. "With those big muscles of his? I thought he'd be a fighter."</p>
<p>Zen shrugged. "It's manly to have big muscles. Mages can work out, too, you know? But…"</p>
<p>The eastern-featured pair's gaze lingered, slower this time. Hanae watched the way Quinlan stood, the set of his legs, the way his weight stayed centered even as he adjusted to the failed push.</p>
<p>"…He's a fighter, all right," she murmured. "A hybrid."</p>
<p>"That's rare for a primordial of such a low level," Zen commented. "I like it, very ambitious."</p>
<p>Miri's hand tightened around her spear shaft. Her jaw set as she tracked the useless wind with sharp eyes.</p>
<p>Lumi stood rigid beside her, fingers curled at her side, magic gathering unconsciously along her knuckles before she forced it back down. Her lips pressed thin.</p>
<p>Both mothers were extremely worried. Until now, Quinlan always took a backseat in the primordial realm, letting them do the fighting while he watched. While this wasn't a fight per se, it was still a major challenge.</p>
<p>And their son had stepped forward, intending to show his worth.</p>
<p>Naturally, seeing the fire in his eyes, both mothers were anxiously rooting for their son to succeed. No mother wanted to see their child fail.</p>
<p>Quinlan exhaled slowly. While he was surprised at the utterly zero effect his heavy push of wind had, he had known the wind would not be enough. It had been a test, nothing more.</p>
<p>"Okay," he muttered.</p>
<p>His stance widened. Mana sank deeper, heavier. The ground beneath his boots answered with a dull tremor.</p>
<p>Earth rose.</p>
<p>Stone tore free in rough slabs, chunks the size of wagons ripping upward in a staggered line. Dust burst outward as Quinlan swung his arms, hurling the mass skyward with blunt force.</p>
<p>The stones slammed into Hagnar's body in a staggered barrage.</p>
<p>They shattered.</p>
<p>Rock split and burst apart on impact, fragments spinning away like broken pottery. The giant grunted as pieces bounced off his chest and shoulders, barely deflected, his trajectory unchanged.</p>
<p>Quinlan watched the debris rain down.</p>
<p>"Is he really a giant? He's too big…"</p>
<p>Now that Hagnar was closer, Quinlan could guess his proportions with reliable accuracy. The man was around 100 meters (328') tall, full of muscles and adorned with heavy armor and, as if that wasn't enough, a giant warhammer hung on his back. Just the hammerhead itself was bigger than the more humble human peasant houses.</p>
<p>No wonder his spells had so little effect. Hagnar's bulk was just too massive.</p>
<p>Thyra answered before anyone else could. "I'm impressed. Your intuition is strong, child. Hagnar is a giant primordial, but that size isn't natural. He unlocked the Titanic Physique during his journeys, which reshaped his body, scaling it far beyond that of normal giants. Strength, mass, resilience, all of it was greatly increased. When it comes to Strength and Vitality, no one can match Hagnar. Save for perhaps Dragnar if he gets his Primordial Dread class's berserker boosts to reach their peak."</p>
<p>Quinlan's brow lifted a fraction.</p>
<p>So that's it.</p>
<p>Understanding clicked into place.</p>
<p>'Just like my Primordial Breeding Physique,' Quinlan mused inwardly.</p>
<p>After unlocking that ability, he had grown as well. His body had changed when it took root, density, vitality, and endurance all climbing steadily. But instead of runaway size, his path had split elsewhere.</p>
<p>Into a different kind of advantage.</p>
<p>[Blessed Seed]</p>
<p>A trait that let him empower those bound to him through intimacy, weaving strength and growth into the act itself. A gift that bolstered those around him rather than being restricted to his personal power strictly, one that turned connection into amplification.</p>
<p>Quinlan let out a quiet breath through his nose.</p>
<p>Yeah. He was very glad he'd gotten his instead.</p>
<p>Hagnar, for all his overwhelming presence, had only one possible partner. His primordial counterpart, who seemed to be only slightly shorter. Anyone else was simply incompatible.</p>
<p>Quinlan shuddered faintly at the thought.</p>
<p>To be locked out from his girls entirely…</p>
<p>That wasn't a blessing.</p>
<p>That was a curse.</p>
<p>However, Quinlan didn't have time to dwell on it.</p>
<p>Hagnar had jumped from far beyond the treeline, but distance meant little at that speed. The giant was still cutting through the sky with frightening velocity. The air pressure shifted. The shadows beneath him stretched and thickened.</p>
<p>Around Quinlan, subtle movements rippled through the gathering.</p>
<p>Feet adjusted. Hands loosened from restraints. Power gathered just beneath the surface.</p>
<p>They were giving him space. He could tell. But one more failure, and they would step in.</p>
<p>This was it.</p>
<p>Quinlan closed his eyes.</p>
<p>He didn't separate it this time. Didn't reach for one element and then another. He drew everything in at once.</p>
<p>Wind wrapped tight around his frame. Earth answered in his bones, weight and stability anchoring his balance. Heat rolled through his muscles, sharpening motion, driving force forward. Water followed, condensing it all, keeping it controlled.</p>
<p>Mana flooded his limbs.</p>
<p>So did memory.</p>
<p>Hours spent breaking his body down and building it back up. Strikes drilled until form held under exhaustion. Impact trained not to look pretty, only to work.</p>
<p>His eyes snapped open.</p>
<p>"[Elemental Stance: Flame]."</p>
<p>His body wrapped in heat as he called upon his elemental stance that boosted his Strength.</p>
