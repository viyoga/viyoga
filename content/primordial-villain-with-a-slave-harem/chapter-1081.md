<p>To Cassandra, she looked like a cruel demoness who reveled in her enemy’s suffering. A vile, sadistic woman.</p>
<p>But the royal guard’s teeth clenched, golden mana still flaring desperately around her body. She refused to kneel, refused to collapse beneath the weight of a curse, no matter how vicious. Her veins screamed, her blood boiled, but her will did not break. The curse may have snared her, but a curse of this magnitude that was inflicted on so little as a minor cut must’ve had limits.</p>
<p>Cassandra’s sharp eyes darted toward Vex, and there she saw it. The Hexblade was rooted to the spot. To anchor such a spell so deeply, she couldn’t move.</p>
<p>That was the opening. That was the flaw. Cassandra burned her mana like a dying star, her light flaring brighter, golden rays clawing against the encroaching rot.</p>
<p>"Girls..." Vex’s playful voice cut through her focus. She giggled, as if the battlefield was nothing but entertainment. Her tongue flicked across her lips. "This is not a picnic."</p>
<p>"On it," Serika grunted. She was already moving.</p>
<p>"Don’t underestimate her, though; she’s likely in the upper level 60s." Vex’s words, which she added afterward, rang on deaf ears.</p>
<p>The tanned woman’s fist blazed with molten fury, a living inferno condensed into one devastating strike. "[Blazing Calamity]!" The blow crashed into Cassandra’s chest like a meteor, flames crawling across her armor. Metal screamed, plates melted, her breastplate warped and crumbled.</p>
<p>Blade was next. "[Skyshatter]!" Her katana flashed like falling starlight, cleaving through Cassandra’s weakened guard. The cut ripped across her back in a perfect, merciless arc, blood spraying hot against the night.</p>
<p>Cassandra’s body buckled. Pain crushed her.</p>
<p>And then Ghost struck. Her gauntlets raked across skin where the flame fist woman melted armor beforehand, claws screeching across flesh.</p>
<p>Scourge’s axe came down with monstrous force, the edge howling for blood. The moonlit constructs followed, their spectral weapons carving into Cassandra’s flesh.</p>
<p>The black-armored warrior’s blade pierced her side, tearing deep.</p>
<p>More attacks came. More wounds marked her body. Her scream was buried beneath the onslaught. The world was reduced to a storm of blood, steel, and ruin.</p>
<p>When the carnage ended, Cassandra lay broken in the dirt. Her golden light flickered out, consumed by the curse, by pain, by overwhelming force.</p>
<p>And then came silence.</p>
<p>A shadow approached.</p>
<p>Devil.</p>
<p>A lion king whose loyal lionesses did the dirty work for him. Now, all he had to do was claim the prize. The women stood aside, watching as their man dragged his black saber behind him, carving a line in the soil.</p>
<p>His eyes seemed to be freezing cold.</p>
<p>He aimed his saber at the fallen woman.</p>
<p>"[Subjugate]."</p>

<p>And, as it was, Cassandra’s level was far beyond his. Level 66, while he was only level 40.</p>
<p>The magic should have broken instantly, flickering out like a candle in the wind. And for a moment, that was exactly what it did.</p>
<p>The hold slipped. Golden fire flared from her core, her oath-born will lashing out to tear his dominion apart. Even unconscious, she seemed to sneer at him, mocking his audacity as the spell unraveled against the impossible gap.</p>
<p>But Quinlan Elysiar was not like others.</p>
<p>He was far more than a mere criminal, far more than Devil.</p>
<p>Where the spell fractured, he pressed forward. Not with technique. Not with numbers. Not with a skill.</p>
<p>With himself. Quinlan might’ve been vastly below her in levels, but one’s level was not the only determinant of personal might, which was what the [Subjugation] spell looked at, not mere numbers.</p>
<p>His soul powers whispered in her ears, the chorus of the dead dragging their nails across her mind. Elemental might churned within his veins. And behind it all, the primordial corruption of the Harbinger, the Villain who would devour gods themselves, pressed down upon her with the weight of inevitability.</p>
<p>Cassandra’s golden fire faltered. It guttered and dimmed beneath the suffocating shadow that loomed over her very being.</p>
<p>Dominion.</p>
<p>"You will kneel."</p>
<p>Her body convulsed, her spirit buckling beneath the weight of him. She screamed, fought with everything she had, but her resistance slowly began showing cracks, then shattered.</p>
<p>[Ding!]</p>
<p>[You have Subjugated Cassandra Valor!]</p>

<p>She was no longer Cassandra, Royal Guard in the service of Queen Morgana.</p>

<p>But before she could launch into a proper tirade, two massive hands descended onto their heads, one for each woman. Ayame stiffened immediately.</p>

It let Quinlan know she didn’t mean one bit what she said about Ayame’s enslavement, just that her mouth filter was, as it so often happened, faulty.</p>
<p>"I don’t hear the apology!" Ayame hissed.</p>
<p>Vex made no move to apologize, only pushing her tongue out at Ayame. The Hexwitch’s tongue no longer sported the black curse mark from before.</p>

Shock, agony, and disbelief collided in her gaze. She should not have been able to awaken. She should have remained broken, unconscious for a long time until her body collected itself.
