<p>The record spanning centuries of difficult commissions belonged to this little man, who was still trying to persuade a wet lock of hair to stay off his forehead.</p>
<p>He offered his hand. Quinlan clasped it carefully, finding a firm grip beneath fingers marked by old burns and tiny cuts.</p>
<p>"A pleasure."</p>
<p>"Mine as well. Hilda, Helda, and Hulda, my lovely companions."</p>
<p>He indicated the women in turn. Hilda rested her free hand on her hip. Helda gave Quinlan a welcoming nod over her shield, while Hulda smiled from her seat, folding the remaining bread into its cloth.</p>
<p>"Hilda," Quinlan repeated, looking at the axe wielder.</p>
<p>"Yes."</p>
<p>His helmet turned toward the shield.</p>
<p>"Helda."</p>
<p>"Correct."</p>
<p>"And Hulda."</p>
<p>The woman with the hammer nodded to him.</p>
<p>"Very good," Bim said approvingly. "Trouble with that, some people have. Quite baffling."</p>
<p>"Utterly," Ayame agreed, her expression solemn.</p>
<p>Quinlan introduced his companions while Bim’s attention traveled along the gathering. The gnome listened carefully to the names, though by the time Quinlan reached the end of his own women, his gaze had begun returning to Black with increasing interest.</p>
<p>Alexios and the other guests received courteous greetings too. Hilda shifted her axe to give them more space beside the equipment, and Hulda rose to join her companions, taking her hammer in one hand.</p>
<p>Quinlan had met the giant primordials. Their titanic proportions, however, did little to prepare him for Bim reaching sideways from his elevated chair to pat Hilda’s calf. His hand barely covered a patch of the muscle beneath her skin.</p>
<p>"Lovely, aren’t they?" Bim asked.</p>
<p>"They certainly make an impression."</p>
<p>"An excellent eye, you have."</p>
<p>Vex slipped her arm around Quinlan’s, smiling up at him. Bim watched the gesture, then glanced toward the other women gathered comfortably around Black.</p>
<p>"A question. A personal question, yes?"</p>
<p>"Shoot."</p>
<p>"So many women. So different, too. Keeping them all happy, how do you manage?"</p>
<p>"My heart is boundless."</p>
<p>The gnome nodded to him with sudden understanding.</p>
<p>"Ahh. A romantic."</p>
<p>"That’s one way to look at it," Ayame supplied.</p>
<p>Bim considered that while Helda bent down to straighten his twisted collar. He leaned forward to give her fingertips room, then caught her little finger in both hands before she could withdraw it.</p>
<p>"The heart, yes. Very important. But the practical matter, I meant."</p>
<p>Quinlan waited.</p>
<p>The gnome brought his chair closer, its metal feet clicking against the quay.</p>
<p>"In bed. Keeping up with all of them, how do you do it?"</p>
<p>Behind him, Hilda closed her eyes.</p>
<p>"Bim."</p>
<p>"A reasonable question!"</p>
<p>"You’ve just met the man."</p>
<p>Hilda turned toward Quinlan with an apologetic grimace.</p>
<p>"I’m sorry, Black. Please excuse him."</p>
<p>"He’s fine," Quinlan chuckled. "I’m enjoying his company."</p>
<p>"And already something in common, we have. A promising beginning."</p>
<p>Kitsara had turned away, her shoulders shaking. Quinlan looked from the gnome to the three mountainous women surrounding his chair.</p>
<p>Their husband stood barely a meter tall when his contraption wasn’t elevating him. Even Hilda’s fingers had needed care when closing around the handle of a weapon that would have been a tree trunk to him.</p>
<p>He could imagine a few practical difficulties in their private lives.</p>
<p>"Would you rather have chosen a nice little gnome woman, then?" Hilda asked her husband, smiling down at him.</p>
<p>"Never!"</p>
<p>Bim’s answer rang across the loading area. A sailor looked up from the crate he was carrying, then continued on his way while the gnome shook his head firmly at his wife before turning back to Quinlan.</p>
<p>"Gnome women, Black. Met them, have you?"</p>
<p>"I haven’t had the pleasure to talk with one at length."</p>
<p>"Pleasure, he calls it. Three meanings in every sentence. All three, somehow, my fault."</p>
<p>"Have you considered listening?" Helda asked.</p>
<p>"Listen, I do! Understand, I struggle!"</p>
<p>Serika’s laughter escaped, joined by a giggle from Sera. Bim gave them a wounded look before returning to his explanation.</p>
<p>"Dwarven women? Magnificent beards. Better than mine, some of them. Compete with my beloved’s beard, I will not."</p>
<p>"You do take yours very seriously," Hilda agreed.</p>
<p>"A man’s dignity!"</p>
<p>"My ladies, though? Understand them, I do! Hungry, she says hungry. Angry, she says angry. Wants me somewhere, picks me up and takes me there. Such clarity!"</p>
<p>Hulda smiled down at him with open affection.</p>
<p>"I love you, Bim."</p>
<p>The gnome beamed up at her.</p>
<p>"See? Plainly spoken. Beautifully spoken."</p>
<p>Hilda leaned on her axe, smiling despite herself.</p>
<p>"He likes big thighs, Black. He can talk about our personalities all he wants, but I know where he’s looking."</p>
<p>"Powerful women," Bim corrected. "A proper arm, a proper thigh. Someone who could carry me home when I’ve had one too many drinks."</p>
<p>Quinlan chuckled beneath his helmet. Beside him, Vex pressed her cheek against his arm, thoroughly enjoying the little man’s defense of his preferences.</p>
<p>"I see a happy family, my friend. You should not worry about needless things."</p>
<p>"Happy, very happy. Exhausted, also. Thus the question: how do you manage?"</p>
<p>Quinlan rested a hand on his saber’s hilt.</p>
<p>"I’d rather not undress in the middle of the harbor, my friend. But let’s just say my saber isn’t the only highly effective weapon I’m carrying on my person."</p>
<p>"You did not just say that!"</p>
<p>Ayame stamped her foot, her blue eyes wide with disbelief.</p>
<p>"We just met these people! Is that really how you want to introduce yourself?!"</p>
<p>"Honesty is a trait I highly value."</p>
<p>Ayame’s deadpan stare at his utterly shameless answer was one Quinlan wouldn’t forget. It was just far too good.</p>
<p>His women burst into laughter.</p>
<p>"Let men be men, Ayame. It’s rare that Hubby meets someone of his gender he likes right off the bat. Didn’t you want him to have more male friends?"</p>
<p>Vex hugged his arm against her chest, looking so pleased with his answer that Ayame turned her glare on her too.</p>
<p>Bim had begun to chuckle as well, but he caught Kitsara glancing down at the front of Black’s armor. The foxkin’s pleased grin sent his attention toward the other women, several of whom were blushing through their laughter.</p>
<p>"...Joking, you aren’t," he muttered to Quinlan.</p>
<p>"No, I’m not."</p>
<p>Ayame stamped her foot again while Bim stared up at him with immense admiration.</p>
<p>"A true man, you are."</p>
<p>Kaelira had gone over to Hulda’s boots, her violet eyes following the mechanisms fitted around their ankles.</p>
<p>"May I ask what those do?" she said.</p>
<p>"Ah. Those." Bim brightened, lowering his chair beside the open panel in Hulda’s boot and pulling his lenses into place.</p>
<p>"A smith, are you?"</p>
<p>"Yes."</p>
<p>"The vents, can you see them? Stored mana becomes thrust. Direction, I can change. Strength of the burst, also."</p>
<p>Hulda turned her foot to give Kaelira a clearer view, waiting until the elf had found a comfortable place next to Bim’s chair before planting it again.</p>
<p>"There you are. Just tell me if you need me to move."</p>
<p>Beneath the armor’s outer plate, Kaelira could see a ring of narrow vents surrounding the heel, each connected to the rune-work inside.</p>
<p>"For jumping?" she asked.</p>
<p>"Among other things. Hilda, darling, your axe? Slowly, please. Visitors, we have."</p>
<p>Hilda found an empty stretch of quay with room to swing toward the water. She planted one foot and began a measured turn, letting them follow the movement of her axe.</p>
<p>*Psshh!*</p>
<p>A burst from her other boot drove her hip around, accelerating the enormous weapon halfway through its swing. The rush of air struck Quinlan’s breastplate while Hilda brought the axe to a controlled stop, its head held out over the water.</p>
<p>"Oh..."</p>
