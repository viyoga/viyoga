<p>Ah. I've made a mistake. How could I not think that she would react like this? It was so obvious.</p>
<p>"Blossom." I say, which stirs Ayame too, who similarly seems to have missed this small detail. Hearing me call the dog-kin's name Ayame turns her head and grimaces upon coming to the same realization I did.</p>

<p>Her expression brightens like the morning sun. "Blossom is a good girl!" She shouts and then energetically leaps right into my arms and begins enthusiastically licking my face all over.</p>

<p>"Blossom, you're incredible," I say, my voice warm and welcoming. I hold her tight, feeling her happiness radiate through her touch. Her exuberance is overwhelming but in the best way possible, and I wouldn't dare dream of stopping her.</p>
