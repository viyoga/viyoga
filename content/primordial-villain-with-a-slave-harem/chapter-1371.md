<p>The wind howled.</p>
<p>Quinlan cut through the clouds at the front of the formation. Air folded around him in clean lines, not turbulence, not drag. The pressure bent outward as if the sky itself had learned how to move aside.</p>
<p>Behind him, the others flew in a tight cluster, held together by controlled currents that never quite touched the ground below. Rivers. Forests. Roads. All of it slid past in a blur of color and shadow before their eyes.</p>
<p>He concentrated and called upon even more.</p>
<p>The wind obeyed.</p>
<p>Ahead, the land soon darkened. Smoke traced thin lines into the sky. Siege towers. Barricades. Cannons. A city wrapped in tension and steel.</p>
<p>Quinlan's mouth curved.</p>
<p>"We've arrived."</p>
<p>He dropped.</p>
<p>The wind followed his intent, carrying the group down in a sharp arc before flaring outward at the last moment. They landed on a hill overlooking the battlefield.</p>
<p>Bows snapped up at once.</p>
<p>Elven archers occupied the hill, spread in disciplined lines between rocks and shallow trenches. The strategic importance of this high ground was recognized by Elvardia, and they'd sent a group to ensure the enemies didn't occupy it.</p>
<p>Because of that, drawn strings and gleaming arrowheads welcomed their arrival, already aligned with throats and chests.</p>
<p>"Halt!" one of them barked. "Identify yourselves!"</p>
<p>He tilted his head slightly. "Didn't they tell you? We've allied ourselves with Elvardia. I'm Quinlan Elysiar, and we already helped you people bring about the end of Greyhaven. We're here to do the same once again."</p>
<p>Despite his words, the archers did not lower their weapons. They looked a mere moment away from shooting, and the only thing that held them back was not Quinlan's reasoning but the cold eyes they were receiving from Sylvaris Vaelorith, an elf noble they recognized. She was not chained, not collared.</p>
<p>Her being here and supporting the scary-looking man made them pause.</p>
<p>"Hm?" Quinlan raised an eyebrow. Behind him, someone staggered.</p>
<p>Lucille swayed with one hand braced against her axe as her boots scraped the ground. Feng doubled over, dry retching, Felicity barely managing to turn away before doing the same. Ria, the blonde assassin girl who became their new friend, moved between them at once. She had one hand on each girl's back while even her own breathing was uneven.</p>
<p>Quinlan turned, frowning.</p>
<p>"…Huh? What's wrong?"</p>
<p>Ayame stepped up beside him.</p>
<p>"Quin," she spoke up with her eyes on the archers and hand on the hilt of her blade. "You're getting really powerful."</p>
<p>He blinked. "Of course I am? I just got a new level."</p>
<p>The oriental beauty shook her head. "I didn't mean it like that. You're becoming powerful not just because of levels."</p>
<p>She glanced back at the sky they had crossed. A faint hint of disbelief was written on her delicate features.</p>
<p>Serika, his tanned redhead who wielded solar powers, stepped up next to them. Just like Ayame, she was displaying hints of surprise. As another user of the flame, she noted her observation.</p>
<p>"Your level of control and depth is frankly getting scary, Quin. The explosion you created back in Greyhavn was more devastating than ever before, but that's only the beginning. As you said, you are gaining levels, so bigger explosions are to be expected. But… They were concentrated and controlled in a way I never saw you manage before. You compressed the flames until the detonation became so powerful that warriors who should've been able to take it with the combination of their high Vitality and defensive armor were overwhelmed as well. You killed a thousand trained soldiers in a single attack…"</p>
<p>She spoke the words with a face that was a mixture of sheer amazement and excitement, but also shock. Serika couldn't believe the mastery Quinlan was starting to display.</p>
<p>Vex nodded as she stepped up. Just like the other two, she wasn't shaken by the wind but by the badassness of her hubby. "The same happened with your wind!" she chirped while hugging his arm close to her chest with both hands. "You were flying so quickly that you outpaced the speed of command! Can you believe that? We crossed dozens of miles faster than the information arrived at the elven ladies."</p>
<p>The gorgeous Hexwitch's cheeks turned rosy as she spoke those words and squeeled like a schoolgirl who was overcome with how her crush stood out among the other boys during the PE exercises as the girls watched from the sidelines.</p>
<p>Understanding settled in Quinlan's mind.</p>
<p>"Oh."</p>
<p>Before he could say more, hurried footsteps sounded from below.</p>
<p>An elf sprinted up the hill, armor half-fastened, breath ragged. Sweat ran down her temples as she waved her arms.</p>
<p>"Pay attention, everyone! Command says a new allied force will arrive within minutes. Do not fire on a man falling from the sky surrounded by dozens of attractive women of mixed races-"</p>
<p>She stopped.</p>
<p>Her eyes locked onto Quinlan and the group behind him.</p>
<p>Her face drained of color.</p>
<p>"You're already here?!"</p>
<p>The wind tugged at Quinlan's coat as he smiled, slow and unapologetic, parting his arms wide. "What can I say? I am eager to help my favorite new allies."</p>
<p>The elf's eyes went wide.</p>
<p>Her mouth opened, then closed again as she straightened, forcing herself back into command posture. Behind him, several of the girls sighed.</p>
<p>Iris shook her head once, lips twitching despite herself. Feng wiped her mouth and muttered something under her breath. Felicity leaned against Ria, who rolled her eyes with practiced familiarity. Aurora pressed two fingers to her temple and exhaled slowly.</p>
<p>&lt;So shameless!&gt; All of them declared at once.</p>
<p>&lt;Trying to help his new allies, he said!&gt; Kitsara laughed.</p>
<p>&lt;As if he had not just removed thousands of slaves from under Elvardia's nose. Slaves that would have filled their ranks, their workshops, their forges.&gt; Lucille giggled.</p>
<p>&lt;Slaves he will use to build his own nation that topples theirs in the future…&gt; Jasmine added with incredible slyness present in her voice. The girl of humble merchant origins wasn't here in the flesh, as her arrangement with Quinlan was that she would oversee his lands while he fought with the rest of his wives at his side. She was his governor. However, she could send her forces at any moment if Quinlan asked for it and opened a portal from his location.</p>
<p>The elf knew none of these details, however.</p>
<p>She recovered quickly, planting her feet and giving him a firm nod. "Your eagerness is appreciated," she said, voice steady again. "I will inform my superiors of your early arrival."</p>
<p>Her gaze flicked to the group behind him. Then to the sky they had come from.</p>
<p>"My orders are to allow you to act at your own discretion." She turned and gestured toward the battlefield below, smoke drifting over siege lines and glowing runes flickering along the city's barrier. "Therefore, I will leave you to it, Lord Villain."</p>
<p>She bowed her head once, clean and respectful, then moved off the hill at a brisk pace.</p>
<p>Ayame didn't wait a single second.</p>
<p>"Lord Villain," she said with an amused chuckle leaving her lips.</p>
<p>Quinlan shrugged. "It certainly needs an acquired taste. But I think there's a nice ring to it."</p>
<p>Serika smiled, hearing the exchange between Quinlan and his cheeky samurai, but her attention was already on the siege. Her eyes narrowed slightly as she watched the barrier ripple under sustained fire. "That shield's about to fail. A few more minutes at most, I'd wager."</p>
<p>She glanced at him. "Should we do the same again?"</p>
<p>Quinlan shook his head. "No. That was a statement of power. 'We are here, and we are not nuisances.'"</p>
<p>He looked back at the field. "Not only was it dangerous, but you ladies don't regenerate mana the way I do. I want you to spend it on killing strong enemies, not cracking walls. We should let our allies show us that they, too, are worthy."</p>
<p>Many elven eyes narrowed at that. What kind of arrogance is needed to have such a train of thought? Their giant, powerful nation needed to prove that it was worthy of his time?</p>
<p>Serika nodded, clearly satisfied with his reasoning. "Then we watch?"</p>
<p>"Sure."</p>
<p>He stamped his foot once.</p>
<p>The ground shifted. Stone rose in clean, rectangular blocks, smoothing itself into thrones as if carved by an invisible hand. One by one, the girls took seats, settling in with varying degrees of comfort and curiosity, eyes fixed on the unfolding siege below.</p>
<p>The elven archers slowly lowered their bows.</p>
<p>Several of them stared.</p>
<p>A man had fallen from the sky. Claimed alliance. Been given permission to do whatever he pleased, despite the strictness with which their armies tended to operate. Then sat down to watch a city fall like it was a stage performance and had the balls to say he would not do anything because Elvardia had to prove itself worthy.</p>
<p>It was ridiculous. Plain ridiculous. Many eyebrows twitched.</p>
<p>Unconcerned with the opinion of others, Quinlan leaned back, resting his arms against the shoulders of the two closest wives who ended up sitting next to him.</p>
<p>The barrier flickered again.</p>
