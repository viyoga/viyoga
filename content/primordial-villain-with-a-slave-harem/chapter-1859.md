<p>She broke then and there.</p>
<p>The tears came all at once, hot and disbelieving, spilling over his thumbs, and the sob that followed had waited so long inside her that it came out small and wrecked, like it had forgotten its own shape on the way up.</p>
<p>Ayame’s hand came up over her own mouth. Her eyes filled, and she stayed exactly where she was, because this was not hers to interrupt.</p>
<p>Quinlan drew Black Fang down, gently, out of the ruin of her pin and into his arms, one hand cradling the back of her head, the other spread warm across her back, and she went, all of her, no resistance left anywhere in her body.</p>
<p>Her face found the hollow of his neck.</p>
<p>And there, hidden from the firelight, hidden from the hall, hidden from every eye that had ever watched her for weakness, Black Fang nodded.</p>
<p>They stayed that way for a while, her tears cooling against his collarbone, his heartbeat arriving against her chest through both their ribs, slow and enormous and certain.</p>
<p>Then...</p>
<p>"Let me make love to you, Black Fang."</p>
<p>"..."</p>
<p>"..."</p>
<p>"..."</p>
<p>Long silence answered his words, followed by an even smaller, weaker second nod.</p>
<p>Quinlan hugged her tight and began to move again.</p>
<p>Slow and deep, nothing of the duel left anywhere in it.</p>
<p>"...Aahn..."</p>
<p>The moan came out of her whole and unfought, given freely into his neck.</p>
<p>The border guard had stood down. The sounds followed one after another now, low and open and entirely hers, and she made no effort to suppress them.</p>
<p>"Can I..." she whispered, and Quinlan didn’t even wait for her to finish her question.</p>
<p>"Yes."</p>
<p>She moved her limbs to hold him. Arms, legs, breath, every inch of her wound around every inch of him, tight and then tighter, clinging like the world would end the moment she let go. Quinlan wrapped her in both arms and gave her wave after patient wave, and the crest that had been robbed all night began to climb one final time with nothing standing in its road.</p>
<p>At the top of it he pressed his mouth to her ear.</p>
<p>"Whatever you need, the answer is yes. It’ll always be yes."</p>
<p>And he stopped holding anything back.</p>
<p>"...Kh." One raw sound escaped him, his arms locking until her ribs creaked, and even going under, the duelist in her logged it: a direct hit, at last.</p>
<p>He finished without restraint, flooding into her womb at full primordial potency, and low on her belly the Beloved mark lit up through sweat and firelight as [Blessed Seed] crashed headlong into [Eternal Hunger].</p>
<p>The world hitched.</p>
<p>In a freezing cell she had taken what he gave her, and it had been enough to keep her breathing, and she had closed the account there, because breathing was all she had ever needed anything to do.</p>
<p>This was a different order of transaction.</p>
<p>Heat erupted through her from the inside, heat where there had only ever been cold, and the hunger that had gnawed at the bottom of her for as long as she had memories rose up to devour what was pouring in, and gorged, and gorged, and then, for the first time in its long ruinous life, stopped.</p>
<p>Sated.</p>
<p>Nothing anywhere in it that still wanted.</p>
<p>The quiet it left behind was so vast and so foreign that her whole body forgot what it was doing.</p>
<p>And what it was doing was standing at the top of the highest crest of the night, with no thief left anywhere on the summit.</p>
<p>She went over.</p>
<p>The finish took her like an avalanche takes a mountainside, total and long.</p>
<p>Her back bowed, her breath left in one unguarded rush, her arms crushed around his neck. The woman who had never lost anything came, and came, and could not stop coming, surge after surge through a body with no hunger left in it to eat the pleasure before it reached her, feeling all of it, undefended.</p>
<p>She did not hold through it. For the first time in her long life she did not even try.</p>
<p>"Ahhnn...! ...Ahn...! ...Aahh...!"</p>
<p>The cry she gave up to the rafters was long and open and broken, and she clung to him through every last wave of it and hid none of it.</p>
<p>If any ancestor was watching the hall, she no longer cared.</p>
<p>A long time later, or what felt like it, Black Fang sat in his arms, trembling in fine, continuous detail, staring at the man her hands were braced against, her eyes gone back to their ordinary lethal violet and her face wearing an expression no living being had ever seen on it.</p>
<p>Confusion.</p>
<p>Total, catastrophic confusion, on a poker face with nothing left to hide behind.</p>
<p>Quinlan said nothing at all, only tucked one loose strand of hair behind her ear, which was somehow the most outrageous thing he had done all night, and let her stare.</p>
<p>By the wall, the biggest grin in the hall had gone wet at the edges, and its owner kept her historic silence.</p>
<p>...</p>
<p>Deep in the night, long after the brazier had banked itself down to embers and the breathing on his chest had gone soft and defenseless, the ruined silk by the wall began to rustle.</p>
<p>Quinlan turned his head. Ayame was padding toward him across the wreckage on her knees, cocoon and all, eyes half-lidded with sleep and absolute purpose.</p>
<p>She reached his free side, lifted his arm by the wrist, deposited herself against his ribs, and pulled the arm back down over her like a blanket she had ordered in advance.</p>
<p>He looked down at her. She looked up at him. Over the woman sleeping between them, two grins met in the dark and understood each other completely. Not one sound passed; the silence held.</p>
<p>A thumbs up rose slowly out of the silk. It was answered with equal ceremony.</p>
<p>And Black Fang, guardian to everyone and guarded by no one for almost her entire life, slept on until morning, watched over by the two grinning idiots who loved her far too much.</p>
<p>Dawn came up gray and slow past the veranda, finding the dawn dojo in a state its ancestors would have needed several generations of counseling to process.</p>
<p>Broken heritage lay where it had fallen. The cracks and stains in the floorboards were not going anywhere.</p>
<p>The light strengthened by degrees, gray giving way to pale gold across the wrecked boards.</p>
<p>Duty waited at the edge of Quinlan’s mind, patient as a debt: the capital, the war of paper, the promise he had made to a king.</p>
<p>It could have him soon enough.</p>
<p>The woman asleep on his chest had waited her whole long life for one safe morning, and he was not going to be the man who cut it short.</p>
<p>It was well into the morning when she finally stirred.</p>
<p>She woke completely and at once, no fog, no swimming up.</p>
<p>One breath she was gone, and the next the violet was open and aimed at him, running its automatic accounting of exits and threats and angles.</p>
<p>The accounting found nothing to report. Only a man, still grinning, and a little sister beaming back on his other shoulder.</p>
<p>The scoff came, as it always did, and for the first time in its long career it carried no teeth at all.</p>
<p>...</p>
<p>With the legendary night in Fujimori lands concluding, it was time to move on.</p>
<p>There was much to do.</p>
