<p>Rosie puffed up proudly at Felicity’s words. The dryad stretched out one of the branches from her tree before gently lowering it to pat the princess’s light purple hair. "Good girl, Felicity."</p>

<p>"Lord Black!" Felicity jumped to her feet. The sheepishness came immediately after, especially when her eyes landed on the fox wrapped around his arm, tails swishing lazily as she looked far too smug for her own good. One glance was enough to confirm it; Feng hadn’t been far off at all.</p>
