<p>Quinlan shook his head slowly as his face formed a grimace. "That sounds boring, doesnt it?"</p>
<p>"Boring?"</p>
<p>"Yes. Its idyllic, romantic, whatever you want to call it, sure... but boring as fuck as well. Serika, be honest with me." He gave her a look that was half-challenging, half-playful. <p>A sharp nod came from Quinlan. "Exactly. But let me be clear. Fighting on the frontline doesnt always mean swinging swords or shedding blood. Jasmine, for example.
