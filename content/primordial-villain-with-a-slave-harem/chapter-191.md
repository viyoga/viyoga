<p>[Requirements: Primordial race, three of the following: Assassin lvl 10 / Thief lvl 10 / Necromancer lvl 10 / Cultist lvl 10 / Slave Master lvl 10 / Bandit lvl 10 / Pirate lvl 10 / Kidnapper lvl 10, Unused Skill Points 5.]</p>
<p>Aaaand points spent.</p>
<p>The moment I dump my points into the new class, a sharp, searing pain shoots through my eyes.</p>
<p>"Shit!" I curse, staggering backward as I clutch my face. "The system didn't mention anything about this again!"</p>
<p>It's like needles are being driven into my skull, the pain blinding and relentless. My vision goes white, then black, flickering between the two. It's all I can do to keep from screaming. Blossom and Ayame are immediately at my side, concern etched on their faces, but I wave them off while gritting my teeth.</p>
<p>"It's… fine," I manage to growl through the agony, though it feels anything but fine. "Another… damned reconfiguration."</p>
<p>The pain stretches on for what feels like forever, each second an eternity of torment. I've been through body changes before, but this one feels different. My eyes burn as if they're being reshaped from the inside out, molded into something new- something darker.</p>
<p>Then, just as suddenly as it began, the pain vanishes.</p>
<p>I blink. Once. Twice. The throbbing fades, replaced by an odd, almost soothing sensation. Slowly, I open my eyes.</p>
<p>Everything is different.</p>
<p>The dim cavern that had been cloaked in shadows now looks like it's bathed in light. Every detail is sharp, every crevice illuminated. The bloodhound's corpse, the jagged stone walls, the faint cracks in the floor- it's all crystal clear, despite the lack of proper light.</p>
<p>That's not all. I can see far into the distance, as if a binocular was permanently attached to my eyes.</p>
<p>"Your eyes, they're… glowing red." Ayame states in a tone that is full of worry. "Do they hurt?"</p>
<p>"Hahaha! This is amazing! I feel like I've been half-blind all this time!" I shout energetically. I can see so much all of a sudden.</p>
<p>Nightvision, farsight, and maybe even more.</p>
<p>I give a wicked grin. "I've been upgraded once again."</p>

<p>I feel it immediately- the change, the surge. Every fiber of my body hums with newfound power. It's like someone turned the world's gravity down a notch, like I'm lighter, quicker, more in control. My muscles feel denser, more responsive, as if they've been forged from something tougher than before.</p>
<p>There's a sharpness to my senses now- an awareness of the slightest movements, the faintest shifts in the air. Even my breathing seems deeper, smoother, as though my lungs are more efficient, drawing in more oxygen than they ever could.</p>
<p>When I clench my fists, the strength behind them surprises me. It's not just raw power- it's refined, focused. My skin seems tougher too, as though it's braced for what comes next, while my reflexes… I can tell I could react to things much more quickly.</p>
<p>My body isn't just stronger- it's better. Faster. More durable. I feel like I'm on a higher plane of existence physically, a step above what I was moments ago. And the best part? It feels right.</p>
<p>Like this is what I was always meant to be.</p>
<p>'This is… perfect.'</p>
<p>If being a villain means having such benefits, then definitely count me in.</p>
<p>Now I have two free Secondary Class slots left, one will definitely be the Wizard, and the other is up for debate. I really want Healer to be available, but I doubt the Goddess would give it to me. It's most likely no longer compatible with my build.</p>
<p>It would be quite the comical scenario, my main class would be Primordial Villain, while the secondary one Healer, which is only given to those that have noble, altruistic hearts.</p>
<p>A charitable evil.</p>
<p>Sadly, that's definitely not my description.</p>
