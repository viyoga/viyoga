<p>In the next moment, my question was answered when a miniature plant erupted from the ground next to Vex.</p>
<p>"Ah… She’s also here. The first disciple…" Mordecai cursed under his breath.</p>
<p>She? This small plant is a woman? Like a dryad?</p>
<p>It began as a small bud that was no larger than a fingertip. Its petals were of a beautiful pink color with veins of white. In just a moment it began to grow rapidly as it stretched and unfolded with a supernatural elegance.</p>
<p>The flower then began expanding with impossible speed with its petals unfurling layer by layer. It grew taller and wider by the milliseconds. The once miniature flower was now taller than even me. It was a massive, blossoming marvel that held the battlefield in awe.</p>
<p>Next, its stem began thickening and twisting while forming the silhouette of a humanoid figure encased within its bloom.</p>
<p>Holy shit… This creature within the plant was emerging from the earth below and traversing up through the roots into the stem, and then to the flower head… This was one fancy way to teleport, that’s for sure.</p>
<p>It might sound like she was vulnerable during the process, but it only took a few moments from start to finish, and the plant didn’t seem to be feeble at all. I bet it could withstand some good hits.</p>

<p>Damn, Black Fang is surrounded by absolute bombshells. Now I’m truly curious to see how the big boss herself looks. <p>However, her robe opened up above her waist, revealing her gorgeous, fair-skinned back that was tattoed just like Raika’s, though it featured other depictions. Her gaze was intense and her posture exuded extreme grace.</p>
