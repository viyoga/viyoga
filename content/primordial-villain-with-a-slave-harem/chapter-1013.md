<p>Steam curled upwards in white tendrils as Seraphiel moved with the elegance of a seasoned artisan.</p>
<p>Her pale hands barely made a sound as she handled the porcelain instruments, her fingers tracing motions so fluid they seemed choreographed.</p>
<p>She poured the heated water in careful spirals over the dried flowers and crushed leaves, letting the mixture steep just long enough for color and aroma to mix and bloom into something more than what they would be if left separated.</p>
<p>Felicity leaned in with eyes wide, full of admiration. "Miss Elf, your technique rivals even the oldest human maids in the palace! This is proper art!"</p>
<p>Seraphiel offered a smug grin to the teen as her aura visibly brightened by the high praise she was receiving.</p>
<p>Her eyes were half-lidded like a cat basking in well-earned praise. "Of course. It’s only natural. Humans learn their skills, but this is in my blood. My ancestors perfected these brews before your ancestors left their nasty caves."</p>
<p>She sniffed lightly, resembling Blossom’s body language in this unique moment. "I can tell what ingredients are needed in what quantity just by scent and mood. I don’t need recipes."</p>
<p>Felicity let out an amazed "wow..." sound, but then she smirked. "So... Miss Elf, was I still being racist when I asked you to help brew tea because you’re an elf?"</p>
<p>"..." Seraphiel said nothing. Instead, she began humming an old elven lullaby as if the question never existed. Or rather, she did it because it existed.</p>
<p>Felicity stared at her dryly for two seconds. Then burst into bubbly giggles that shook her entire upper body. "Hehehe! I love you people! How can you be so much fun to be around?!" she gasped, wiping her eyes. "This is already the best day I’ve had in years, and we’re only just getting started!"</p>
<p>She looked back over her shoulder, where Quinlan was sitting beneath a vine-covered trellis, with his arms spread out, resting on the backs of his women. Namely, Miss Platinum and Miss Caramel.</p>
<p>Felicity’s smile brightened. "I’m really happy you managed to come."</p>
<p>Quinlan gave her a nod. "The honor is ours."</p>
<p>"!!"</p>

<p>Quinlan suppressed a laugh as Ayame let out a quiet snicker, accompanied by many others. Aurora, Kitsara, Lucille, Jasmine... Even Serika and Vex joined in.</p>
<p>They might’ve been older than the rest, but having such simplistic fun was new to them.</p>
