<p>Quinlan stood still in the glowing quiet, eyes narrowed, lips pressed into a thin, unimpressed line.</p>
<p>"How do I grow these damn seeds into proper divine concepts?"</p>
<p>He waited.</p>
<p>No response.</p>
<p>"...Seriously? Nothing?"</p>
<p>He was rather unimpressed. "I know you’re supposed to be some unfeeling administrator, but come on... I killed a god, fulfilling your cheesy little Avatar-Invader prophecy... I even got ambushed by the loot-ehem orbs Venthros left behind and nearly lost my life as a result, but instead of succumbing, I gritted my teeth and fought with all I had and triumphed, forging a soul realm or whatever this thing is, and all I ask is for a hint. A tutorial pop-up, maybe."</p>
<p>Still silence.</p>
<p>He folded his arms. "Fine. I’ll just ask my pair of sexy, plump mommies. Maybe one of them can guide me since you’re unwilling."</p>
<p>A beat passed, and he was still ignored.</p>
<p>"Or that bratty goddess, Lilyanna. It’s about time she does something useful at long last. Perhaps I can withstand her presence now."</p>
<p>Another pause came. Quinlan then grimaced. "No. I shouldn’t rely on others."</p>
<p>His gaze fell inward again, to the pulsing Seeds nested within his core.</p>
<p>"Let’s try to figure this out."</p>
<p>He paced slowly along a ridge overlooking a yet-forming canyon in his Soul Realm, thinking aloud.</p>
<p>"So... I inherited Venthros’ divine seeds. Wait, no, that sounds painfully gay. Let’s rephrase."</p>
<p>He cleared his throat.</p>
<p>"After felling the corrupted god, I absorbed the remnants of Venthros’ divinity. Two seeds. One of Corruption, the other of the Elements. One dormant, one tainted."</p>
<p>He nodded to himself.</p>
<p>"I need to purify them. Nurture them. Only then can they evolve into proper divine concepts I can wield. Whatever wielding a concept means..."</p>
<p>He glanced back at the still sky above.</p>
<p>"And apparently, my soul isn’t strong enough to hold concepts yet. Soul Tier: Proto-Ascendant." He gave a wry smile. "Thanks, Records. That part was at least quite informative. It must mean I need to grow to wield concepts."</p>
<p>He turned serious again, recalling the final part of the message.</p>
<p>"That last line... not eligible for Pantheon classification." He rubbed his chin. "I guess that means I’m not worthy to sit among the gods."</p>
<p>He let that thought linger... and then smirked.</p>
<p>"Well. Not a surprise. I’m not a god yet. I’m a demigod. A primordial, and quite a young and weak one at that. My mommies could spank me any day of the week should they so desire."</p>
<p>His eyes glinted as he instinctively knew something. "And truth be told... I don’t want to be one of them."</p>
<p>His voice lowered into something cold and resolute.</p>
<p>"I’m not here to join the ranks of the gods. I’m here to end their corrupt reign. I’m the God Slayer, after all."</p>
<p>He turned, looking across his soul realm, the foundation of something new.</p>
<p>"Let them have their Pantheons. I’ll carve a path for myself that doesn’t need a golden seat."</p>
<p>Then he frowned, pondering the real question.</p>
<p>"What even is a god, anyway? Is it just having divine concepts? Do I become one automatically when I master them? Or do I need to abandon this Primordial body?"</p>
<p>Too many questions. No answers.</p>
<p>Not yet.</p>
<p>He clenched his fists, letting the silence settle again.</p>
<p>And then...</p>
