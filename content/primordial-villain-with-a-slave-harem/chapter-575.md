<p>The white-haired beauty’s next words were the last thing Ryker and his team wanted to hear considering their situation. "Raika, be a dear and come here for a second…"</p>
<p>Raika paused her shadow boxing motions mid-swing, and with an annoyed scoff, she walked over to us. "What is it? I was about to win ten punches faster than the last time."</p>
<p>"Come here, you five." Vex didn’t reply to her question but instead ordered Ryker’s team to leave the cave and stand before us.</p>
<p>Ryker was the first to straighten his back and do as told. Before that, however, he turned toward his teammates and confidently said, "Don’t worry, I’ve got this."</p>
