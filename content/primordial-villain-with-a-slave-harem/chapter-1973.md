<p>His gaze snapped back to hers, and Black Fang lifted her chin by the smallest amount. She knew exactly what she was asking for. The surprise in his eyes only seemed to irritate her further.</p>
<p>’Even after all this time, I still have no fucking idea what to expect from this woman,’ Quinlan chuckled inwardly. ’Can she be any more random?’</p>
<p>"Does that hurt?" Seraphiel asked, moving her palm.</p>
<p>"Yes. I’m dying."</p>
<p>Quinlan nearly choked.</p>
<p>This woman was not only a walking talking surprise, but also a terrible actor.</p>
<p>Black Fang tightened her grip on his coat, and he swallowed the laugh before it escaped. Her improvised complaint was growing more awkward every time Sera invited her to elaborate.</p>
<p>Yet beneath his amusement, affection filled him with such force that he wanted to pull her into his arms.</p>
<p>She knew what his Subjugation meant. She had seen his enemies obey, seen the authority he could wield through the bond, and she was offering him that power over herself in the middle of a crowded Guild hall.</p>
<p>She wanted to belong in the conversation, and she trusted him enough to ask for this.</p>
<p>He held her gaze until the impatience in her expression eased. For a moment, he let her see his own question plainly: did she truly want to give him that authority?</p>
<p>Black Fang held his eyes and nodded to him.</p>
