<p>The first thing the girls noticed was that the view only worked one way.</p>
<p>They could see Quinlan clearly through the dome, noting every shift of his duel. What they could not do was draw his attention. Shouting did nothing. Gestures went unanswered. When Lucille tried waving both arms over her head, Quinlan did not react at all. Whether it was because of Nyxara's pink magical mist or the basic property of the dome they didn't know, but neither did it really matter.</p>
<p>They saw how he fought this unconventional battle of his with all he had and more. They also saw how Quinlan was trying to adapt, trying multiple strategies. They saw how he was brought to the brink of defeat multiple times, only for Nyxara to never deliver a finishing blow, instead nuzzling up to him and waiting for him to regain his strength and wits.</p>
<p>It was clear she was playing with their lover, and that angered all women present.</p>
<p>However, slowly, the tide began shifting, making it clear Quinlan wasn't going to throw in the towel anytime soon. He was busy making his counter attack right now.</p>
<p>At this point, all the girls understood he was going to be at this for a long time.</p>
<p>Which meant Ayame's earlier words stopped being advice and turned into consensus.</p>
<p>They broke apart without discussion.</p>
<p>Kiryssa had already moved to the edge of the clearing, where the ground dipped, and the light thinned. She did not call for students. She simply drew a thin blade from her sleeve and waited.</p>
<p>Iris reached her first, leading the charge.</p>
<p>Blossom followed with claws sliding free from her gauntlets with a muted scrape. Ria came last, twin daggers held low, wrists loose.</p>
<p>Kiryssa's eyes moved over them once.</p>
<p>"Good. You're already practiced combatants, that I can tell."</p>
<p>None of them answered.</p>
<p>"Against conventional opponents, that is."</p>
<p>Kiryssa stepped forward.</p>
<p>She vanished.</p>
<p>Iris felt it before she saw it. Pressure slammed into her chest, enough to rattle breath and spike sensation. She staggered back two steps, teeth clenched, every nerve screaming detail into her awareness. Pain mapped itself cleanly across her body, sharp and precise, feeding her rather than slowing her thanks to her Child of Reckoning class.</p>
<p>Blossom reacted on instinct. She lunged with her claws sweeping where Kiryssa had been.</p>
<p>Nothing.</p>
<p>Ria was already moving, daggers crossing to intercept the angle Blossom left open. She felt air split near her neck and twisted, only to find nothing in her reach.</p>
<p>Kiryssa reappeared behind them, nonchalant as ever. She lifted her fingers that were glowing with magic. Upon her doing so, three marks appeared on the necks of the trio, one for each.</p>
<p>"If I were to flick my fingers, three heads would hit the ground in the next moment," she decreed.</p>
<p>Then, seeing their shock and confusion, the drow primordial smiled and lowered her fingers. "And that was the slow version."</p>
<p>"No way…" Ria gasped.</p>
<p>"Lady Kiryssa is so badass!!" Blossom decreed with a wagging tail, eliciting a minor chuckle from the woman.</p>
<p>Iris then began grumbling, "The level gap is too big…"</p>
<p>"Young lady, looking for excuses so soon is the mark of a weakling," Kiryssa smiled, then doubled down when she saw the sour expression on Iris's face. "I was only using the speed I had back when I was your level."</p>
<p>"Ugh…! It's been billions of years, are you sure you remember correctly?"</p>
<p>"… Young lady. Watch your words." "Hehe! What a sore loser!" Ria giggled at her friend.</p>
<p>"Iris is a loser!!" Blossom added fuel to the fire.</p>
<p>The raven-haired woman's forehead suddenly sported numerous throbbing veins and her grip on her sword tightened tenfold.</p>
<p>Kiryssa laughed one final time, then jumped into action for the second time.</p>
<p>The following exchange was worse than the last.</p>
<p>Kiryssa did not vanish this time. She moved just fast enough to be seen, which somehow made it harder to track her. Iris took the first hit again, a flat strike to the ribs that folded her inward before she could turn it. Pain tore through her frame in clean layers. She rode it, feet skidding, vision narrowing into sharp lines instead of blur.</p>
<p>When she swung, she hit nothing.</p>
<p>Blossom tried to capitalize on the opening. She cut in low, claws snapping for tendons. Kiryssa stepped inside the arc, shoulder-checking Blossom mid-lunge and twisting her balance sideways. Blossom hit the ground hard and rolled, instantly getting up. But it was too late.</p>
<p>Ria never stopped moving. She circled, blades testing angles that did not exist yet. Kiryssa let her think she had one. The counter came so fast, Ria barely registered the touch before her dagger was slapped out of her hand and embedded hilt-deep in the dirt.</p>
<p>They regrouped without words.</p>
<p>Again and again, Kiryssa broke their rhythm. She slipped between attacks, turned momentum against them, punished hesitation, and punished certainty even harder. When they collapsed, she waited. When they stood, she struck.</p>
<p>After one particularly brutal exchange, she finally stepped back and gestured with two fingers. "Breathe and discuss among yourselves strategies to overcome me. We will continue in ten minutes."</p>
<p>…</p>
<p>Not far away, Thyra had taken Felicity and Feng to an entirely different stretch of ground.</p>
<p>At first, the two teens and their gazes were locked onto the pink dome and the fierce duel about to unfold inside. However, they were quickly forbidden from looking by multiple of Quinlan's wives, and Serika went as far as to ask Thyra if she could train them herself, saying she believed the mother of humanity would be a great teacher.</p>
<p>Thyra offered no rebuttals and took the job, which also meant she grabbed Felicity and Feng by the back of their collars and began dragging them toward a clearing further away from the pink-tinted dome. The two teenagers dug their heels into the dirt and kept their eyes fixed on the pulsating light behind them. They looked like young cats being pulled away from a bird.</p>
<p>"It's not fair!" Feng shouted at Serika while trying to twist out of Thyra's grip. "You let us fight in the wars, and you let us see humans get ripped apart, but suddenly we aren't allowed to watch Quinlan's duel? That's a battlefield in there, and Quinlan is fighting for his soul. We should be studying his defensive maneuvers and his endurance under extreme pressure…"</p>
<p>Felicity nodded and folded her arms over her chest. "I am worried about his mana circulation and his heart rate. Plus, I have a duty to learn some of those moves. Nyxara is an ancient master and it would be a disservice to my future as a woman if I didn't take notes on her technique. It is strictly for academic purposes and my personal development. Deny me now, I dare you."</p>
<p>"Sure. Denied." All women said together, making Felicity gasp strongly, unable to believe her flawless logic was brushed aside so easily.</p>
<p>"Nice try, young ones," Thyra said and kept walking until the dome was obscured by the trees. "Now, instead of letting those two corrupt your minds, let us get to work."</p>
<p>"B-but Miss Thyra, aren't we right?" Feng asked, refusing to give up so easily. "We're fighting life-or-death battles where we see extreme cruelty on full display at all times. I've killed many men and women with my two hands! Felicity is the same. Why is us seeing people have sex so much worse?"</p>
<p>Thyra smiled and placed a warm hand on the head of each girl as they looked at her with pleading puppy eyes. She stopped walking and turned to face them fully while her expression softened into a truly motherly look.</p>
<p>"Your curiosity is understandable, and it is natural for teenagers to wonder about these things. If you want an education on love and relationships, I can provide that for you as well as combat training."</p>
<p>"Then why can't we just watch them?" Felicity asked.</p>
<p>"Because the scene in that dome is not for the minds of youngsters like you. If that becomes your exposure to intimacy before you experience it yourself, your perception of love will be forever warped. Nyxara is a sadistic demon of lust. You, young human women, should not treat her as someone to imitate. Furthermore, Quinlan is not behaving like a man should when being with a woman he loves because he is fighting for his dear life with all he has. Watching them would only corrupt your mind and make you think that making love is a brutal battle when it is one of the most beautiful aspects of life. That is not something I, or anyone else, wants for either of you."</p>
<p>Both girls looked surprised, and their arguments died away as they processed her words. Feng looked up at Thyra with a curious expression. "Were you a 'player' too back in the day, Miss Thyra?"</p>
