<p>Isara felt them before she saw them. Five hostile mana signatures, closing fast from five different directions, each one radiating the same solar fire that had been hammering her since the fight began.</p>
<p>Five Serikas burst from the smoke.</p>
<p>Each one burning, each one closing with fists raised, and Isara couldn't tell which was real because the mana signatures were identical.</p>
<p>Isara's chains snapped outward in a defensive web and caught the first Serika through the torso. The image dissolved. The second took a chain to the face and vanished. The third dodged left, the fourth came from above, the fifth -</p>
<p>Her chains couldn't cover all of them. She prioritized the two most aggressive signatures and bound them both.</p>
<p>Both were illusions.</p>
<p>'The Nine-Tailed Sorceress.'</p>
<p>Isara knew the class. Everyone in intelligence knew the class. An illusionist archetype of the foxkin race that was so rare that the only living documented wielder was Yoruha, a foxkin who preferred sleeping in her den for centuries at a time over engaging with the world. There was almost nothing in the tactical manuals because Yoruha had never given anyone enough data to build a counter-doctrine.</p>
<p>'This girl is three-tailed and she's already replicating mana signatures at this fidelity? Is it because of him? Every woman around that man fights like she has no right to at her level.'</p>
<p>She had no time for this.</p>
<p>Vex's hex hit her lattice from behind and three chains corroded to nothing. Isara spun, reformed, lashed out, and Vex parried with a sword stroke that redirected the chain into the dirt.</p>
<p>The real Serika came through the gap Vex had opened.</p>
<p>The real Kitsara, running close to her fiery friend, took one look at the fire building in Serika's fists, and shifted six meters to the left because she had extremely strong opinions about not being on fire.</p>
<p>Serika launched forward and closed the distance in a single burning stride. Her fist caught Isara in the chest and the fire behind it burned through what remained of the barrier. The general flew backward and hit the basin wall hard enough to crack the stone behind her. She slid down, tried to raise her hands, and a chain flickered into existence for half a second before Vex's sword cut it apart.</p>
<p>"Finishing now!" Kitsara announced cheerfully. "From over here! Way over here!"</p>
<p>Serika and Vex stood over Isara Blackveil.</p>
<p>The general looked up at them with blood running from her nose and burns across her forearms, her chains scattered around her in fragments. Six centuries of service. A woman who had held the Primordial Villain in her binding, who had watched her friend die and rise as a laughing enemy, who had walked into a kill box knowing it was a kill box because she was Isara Blackveil and she did not run.</p>
<p>She tried to form one last chain. Golden light flickered in her palm.</p>
<p>Vex hexed it dead.</p>
<p>"You fought well," Serika said.</p>
<p>She drove her fist through Isara's chest.</p>
<p>Solar fire erupted from the point of impact and consumed her in a blaze of white-gold flame that burned hot enough to melt the stone behind her. When Serika pulled her fist back, there was nothing left but a scorch mark on the basin wall and the fading echo of golden chains dissolving in the air.</p>
<p>Serika stood there for a moment with her arm still extended, fire cooling around her knuckles, burns running from her wrists to her shoulders where the chains had seared her skin. Her throat was bruised purple where the binding had tried to crush it, and her ribs ached from the concussive blast.</p>
<p>Vex sheathed her sword.</p>
<p>Their eyes met.</p>
<p>Vex held up her hand.</p>
<p>Serika slapped it hard enough to make the Hexwitch wince.</p>
<p>"This is supposed to be a celebratory high-five between victorious friends, not a declaration of war!"</p>
<p>"Yeah. I know."</p>
<p>"… Barbarian," Vex muttered while shaking feeling back into her fingers. Kitsara slammed into both of them.</p>
<p>Her arms wrapped around their shoulders and pulled them together in a tight, bouncing hug that nearly took all three of them to the ground. Her tails were going wild, all three of them whipping back and forth hard enough to scatter debris across the basin floor.</p>
<p>"We did it! We just killed a living legend! A Level 70 living legend! That was incredible! You two were incredible! I was incredible!"</p>
<p>"Kitsara, my ribs -" Serika started.</p>
<p>"Ribs heal! Glory is forever!"</p>
<p>Serika endured the hug for a few seconds, then sighed and looked at the foxkin clinging to her shoulder.</p>
<p>"I still can't believe you stripped naked and did... all of that," she muttered. "The women of Thalorind are far too wild."</p>
<p>Kitsara blinked at her. Genuine confusion crossed her face, which was somehow worse than if she'd been smirking.</p>
<p>"Hm? I didn't strip naked. My tail did."</p>
<p>"... What?"</p>
<p>"My tail projected the illusion remotely. I was fully clothed behind that boulder the entire time." Kitsara pointed at herself, still perfectly armored, not a clasp out of place. "Kitsara would never show off her lady parts on a battlefield. What if Quin saw it? He'd scold me for days. 'Kitsara, you are forbidden from showing your naked body to others, you're mine and mine alone, blah blah blah.' He gets so possessive" She shivered with delight. "Which I utterly love, by the way."</p>
<p>Vex stared at her.</p>
<p>"So your only concern," Vex said slowly, "with creating a naked bushy bondage replica of an enemy general in the middle of a battlefield... is whether Hubby would be angry at you?"</p>
<p>"Obviously? What other concern would there be?"</p>
<p>Vex broke.</p>
<p>The laughter came out of her in a burst she couldn't contain. She pressed a hand over her mouth and it didn't help at all, the giggles spilling through her fingers while her shoulders shook.</p>
<p>Serika's mouth twitched. Then curved. Then she was smiling too, broad and helpless, because the foxkin was looking between them with those big, confused eyes, genuinely baffled by what was so funny, and that made it worse.</p>
<p>"You're just as shameless as he is," Serika managed. "You deserve each other."</p>
<p>"I'll take that as a compliment"</p>
<p>Then his voice rolled down from above.</p>
<p>"Truly, my girls are far too amazing."</p>
<p>All three of them looked up. Quinlan descended from the smoke-stained sky, the Soul Reaper already drawn, its edge pulsing with necrotic light. His gaze swept the basin, taking in the scorch marks, the scattered chain fragments, the aftermath of three fights that his women had won without him.</p>
<p>He grinned behind the helmet.</p>
<p>"[Eternal Damnation]."</p>
<p>The saber pulsed and the basin answered. Souls ripped free from the scorched earth, spectral wisps torn from the bodies of the officers his girls had slain, streaming upward in ribbons of pale light and funneling into the blade one after another. Reina's. Drekken's. Isara's. The Soul Reaper drank them in until the weapon hummed with the fruits of a fresh harvest.</p>
<p>Quinlan looked at his women, battered and burned and grinning up at him with blood on their armor and victory in their eyes.</p>
<p>"Let's continue, shall we?"</p>
<p>But just as he said that, a telepathic voice rang in his head.</p>
