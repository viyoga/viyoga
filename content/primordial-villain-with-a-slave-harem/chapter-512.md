<p>Back when I decided to end these two I didn’t quite know when or how I would go about it. They were the daughters of one of the most powerful men on the whole continent, after all. Now, however, my mind was cleared of any unnecessary doubts. There was only one possibility I could take.</p>
<p>&lt;Ayame, gather the Ascendants.&gt;</p>
<p>Now or never.</p>
<p>I turned toward the purple-haired princess who nestled into her new throne over the dozens of minutes she sat in my lap to quite the degree, "Princess Felicity, I have to take my leave. I’m sorry about the abruptness, and I’m grateful beyond what words can convey for your help. I didn’t deserve it, yet you were ready to part from such wealth for my sake."</p>
<p>Felicity looked up at me with saddened eyes, "I’m sorry I couldn’t help more…"</p>

<p>"Sure." I accepted her crystal which was the same sort of item Vex gave me so that I could call her with the communicator artifact. I just had to embed Felicity’s crystal into its surface. "I’ll call you as soon as time permits me to do so."</p>
<p>Now I had established a direct contact line not only to three incredibly powerful women but also a royal princess.</p>
