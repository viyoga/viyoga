<p>He wrenches the bottle from her grip and, with an almost dismissive flick, hurls it at Julia. <p>Julia cries out, both in surprise and pain, staggering back with wine and glass shards coating her skin. I can't help but find a dark humor in the scene- a fitting retribution for the biggest whore of the world.</p>

<p>The man stands and steps over her body. He approaches Julia, who is now shivering like a rabbit in front of a wolf, with her previous attitude nowhere to be seen.</p>
