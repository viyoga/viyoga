<p>Feng studied the elf standing by the doorway.</p>
<p>"Since when are you so kind?"</p>
<p>Amusement brightened Sera’s eyes as she turned toward the girl.</p>
<p>"The Goddess of Purity blessed me for my kind heart, young lady. Whatever could you be implying about my person?"</p>
<p>"The same bitch who kidnapped your husband because, uhh... she doesn’t like him?"</p>
<p>Sera smiled at Feng as the girl continued.</p>
<p>"The one whose priests were about to put those women we rescued from the goblins into debt slavery? That Goddess of Purity is your alibi for being a kind woman?"</p>
<p>The blonde elf’s smile remained. "A hypocritical bitch Lily might be, but she’s just a sheltered girl with a compassionate heart who has yet to find her way."</p>
<p>Alexios paused with his cup halfway to his mouth. For a thousand years, he’d worshipped the woman they were now discussing as a troublesome girl in need of guidance.</p>
<p>His wry gaze moved between them before he returned to his tea.</p>
<p>"Why do I feel like you’re defending her because your claim of purity depends on her reputation?" Feng asked.</p>
<p>The elf’s smile broke into a giggle.</p>
<p>"Spending all that time alone with Quin gave you a sharp tongue that will never leave you, hmm, self-proclaimed jade beauty of Zhenwu?"</p>
<p>She crossed to the bench, gathering her skirt as she settled sideways across Quinlan’s lap. Both arms slipped around his neck, and her golden hair brushed his cheek as she leaned close.</p>
<p>His hand found the bare thigh exposed by her displaced skirt, stroking its smooth curve while he drew her against him. Her perfect skin pressed against his chest, and her blue eyes were bright with mischief meant for him.</p>
<p>"Let us begin the meeting, Quin"</p>
<p>Sera rested her head against his neck with a contented hum. Quinlan took a slow inhale of her hair’s wonderful smell before looking toward the two girls at the table.</p>
<p>"Let us. But first, Feng, Felicity... As Sera said, Nessa is far too much of an airhead to pose a threat."</p>
<p>Feng began nodding to him, accepting his decision.</p>
<p>"...Is what I might’ve said a few months ago," Quinlan continued. "But why take risks I don’t have to?"</p>
<p>Her nod stopped.</p>
<p>He bent to kiss Sera’s neck, lingering against her warm skin until she tipped her head to give him more room. Her fingers slipped into his hair, and he reluctantly lifted his mouth to continue.</p>
<p>"Our maids are as loyal as anyone could wish for, but they aren’t trained for surveillance. I’d prefer if they weren’t troubled by this task."</p>
<p>Releasing Sera’s waist, Quinlan drew [Soul Reaper] with his free hand and held the saber out beside the bench.</p>
<p>"[Awaken]."</p>
<p>Blue flames streamed from the blade onto the floor, taking the shapes of two Fujimori rogues with daggers at their belts. Both soul soldiers knelt before Quinlan.</p>
<p>"Watch our maid, Nessa, whenever she’s inside the house. Keep yourselves hidden, and keep her away from anything she shouldn’t see or hear."</p>
<p>The two waited, their faces turned toward him beneath their hoods.</p>
<p>"If she learns one of our secrets, however minor it may be, inform me immediately and make sure she doesn’t leave the estate. I’ll decide what happens next."</p>
<p>Both rogues nodded to Quinlan. At his gesture, they rose and slipped into the hall to find places from which they could watch for the maid’s return.</p>
<p>Quinlan put his saber away, freeing his arm to draw Sera close again. Lucille brought the visitors’ teapot to the low table beside his bench and filled a cup for him before taking the nearest chair.</p>
<p>Quinlan turned toward Felicity and the blonde elf seated on the other side of her father. Myrasyn had once worn the elven crown; now she governed the Holy Dominion of Elysaria in his name.</p>
<p>"Felicity, Myra. How would you feel about bringing some soldiers over from Iskaris?"</p>
<p>The young queen smiled at him.</p>
<p>"Thank you for asking me, Emperor. But you can talk to Father about the troops. I won’t consider it disrespectful if you address him instead of me."</p>
<p>"A figurehead queen, are we?" Quinlan grinned.</p>
<p>"Figurehead? I have Father on a tight leash."</p>
<p>Alexios exhaled while his daughter folded her hands on the table, looking thoroughly pleased with herself.</p>
<p>"I’m a proper ruler who delegates to competent people."</p>
<p>Quinlan’s smirk widened.</p>
<p>"I learned from the best," Felicity added, looking between him and Myrasyn.</p>
<p>The governor gave Quinlan an accusing look, which he accepted with zero shame. She’d tried to retire, only for him to put her back in charge of her people.</p>
<p>"What would you have the soldiers do?" Alexios asked.</p>
<p>"Level up. I’m the ruler of Iskaris, and the old barrier is gone. Rosie is hiding our homeland for now, but I don’t intend to rely on the rest of the world remaining ignorant forever."</p>
<p>He reached for his tea while the amusement around the table subsided.</p>
<p>"I need stronger armies to defend my lands and help me conquer more. Our people should have the chance to grow before they’re fighting foreign invaders on their own doorstep."</p>
<p>"Do you have a particular enemy in mind?"</p>
<p>"Not yet. For now, I want to form a mercenary company."</p>
<p>Myrasyn set her cup down, her green eyes attentive.</p>
<p>"Adventuring suits us," Quinlan continued. "We get to explore, meet people, and learn how things work out here. But trying to find adventuring jobs for an army would be awkward at best."</p>
<p>"You’d offer their swords to whoever needs soldiers?" Myrasyn asked.</p>
<p>"For the right price, yes. We earn coin and our troops gain levels fighting alongside the locals. They’ll learn what foreign armies can do before we have to face those armies ourselves."</p>
<p>"You know the elves will support you no matter what," Myrasyn said. "You’re more than just our emperor. But bringing us here as a group of peculiar travelers already took quite a performance. Entire armies? The lizzies would be on us immediately."</p>
<p>Her gaze traveled around the room, taking in the people who’d recently passed themselves off as shipwreck survivors.</p>
<p>"We need to prepare properly before bringing anyone else through. Somewhere to put them, a way to explain their arrival... We can hardly keep filling this house."</p>
<p>Quinlan nodded to Myrasyn.</p>
<p>"I know. I’m telling you what I intend so we can start making preparations. We don’t have to act on it tonight."</p>
<p>"Good," she smiled, accepting his words.</p>
<p>"There’s also the matter of what they’ll say once they’re here," Alexios added. "There’s a chance they’ll share camps with foreign soldiers, eat with them, drink with them... Someone will ask where they’re from. Someone else will boast about a battle."</p>
<p>Quinlan lowered his cup.</p>
<p>"Until we’ve established ourselves properly, anyone we bring over will have to be [Subjugated]. They’ll be under orders to keep our secrets."</p>
<p>"And you’ll be able to speak with them wherever they go," Myrasyn observed.</p>
<p>"That too. [Overlord’s Eyes] will let me see through theirs. When our soldiers serve alongside a foreign army, I can study its equipment, formations, and methods with them."</p>
<p>He smiled at the prospect.</p>
<p>"The people paying us today might be our enemies later. Let them fund the armies that will take them down."</p>
<p>Alexios nodded to Quinlan.</p>
<p>"I’ll make the arrangements. Anyone we consider will know that condition before they agree to come."</p>
<p>His gaze moved to Myrasyn, who chuckled at the unspoken question.</p>
<p>"What? Do you think my soldiers will have a problem with it? I’m afraid I’ll need to replace a great many panties the day they learn they’re to be of such important use to the Holy Son."</p>
