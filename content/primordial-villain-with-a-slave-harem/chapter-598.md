<p>Destruction was not the only role fire played; it didn’t hunger mindlessly…</p>
<p>I opened my eyes, staring into the inferno as understanding dawned on me.</p>
<p>Fire was not just a mindless hunger—it was the agent of change.</p>
<p>Yes, it devoured. Yes, it reduced all to ash. But even in destruction, it served a purpose.</p>
<p>This world was doomed, its soul ecosystem ruined by the greedy gods masquerading around as Astral Conquerors when, in reality, they were nothing more than soul raiders.</p>
<p>Drakwyn had no future, reduced to an existence that was no more than a sad husk waiting for its end. Here, fire was fulfilling its most primal function—erasing what no longer served a purpose.</p>
<p>But in another place, in another time, fire had always been more than a force of annihilation.</p>
<p>Civilization itself had been shaped by it.</p>
<p>Fire cleansed. It cauterized wounds, searing flesh shut to prevent infection. It purified water, boiling away sickness. It forged weapons and tools, turning cumbersome ore into tempered steel. It warmed the cold, gave light to the darkness, and allowed humanity to thrive in a world that would have otherwise been hostile.</p>
<p>All that is to say, fire destroyed, yes.</p>
<p>But it also created.</p>
<p>Without it, there would be no growth. No renewal.</p>
<p>Even in nature, forests that burned under the right conditions were reborn richer and more fertile than before.</p>
<p>As such, destruction was not meaningless.</p>
<p>It was necessary.</p>
<p>It was purposeful.</p>
<p>And perhaps that was what I had failed to see.</p>
<p>I let out a slow breath, feeling the heat sear my skin.</p>

<p>Merely observing the flames all around me would not be sufficient.</p>
<p>I had to let it go.</p>
<p>Not just a little.</p>
<p>Not just in a careful, measured release.</p>
<p>I had to unleash it.</p>
<p>I closed my eyes, took a deep breath, and made a decision.</p>
<p>No more suppressing.</p>
<p>No more fighting.</p>
<p>Only understanding.</p>
<p>According to the words of the Soul Records, the contents of the elixir I consumed were no mere primordial fire but a unique version of it—extracted and purified. <p>To that end…</p>
<p>I released my grip.</p>
<p>The moment I did, the fire within me did not seep out measuredly.</p>
<p>It detonated, bursting forth with anger as if asking me how dare I try to contain it like a dumb pet.</p>
<p>My stomach burned. My flesh blackened, my insides scorched as the pure primordial fire surged forth through me, rampaging to its heart’s content.</p>
<p>Before, when I first drank the elixir, I had fought. Even now, my instincts were screaming at me to resist—to suppress, to cage, to force this fire into submission. That was what my mind had told me. That was what my instincts had told me.</p>
<p>This time, I did nothing.</p>
<p>I did not suppress.</p>
<p>I did not fight.</p>
<p>I did not try to control it.</p>
<p>I let it burn.</p>
<p>And burn it did.</p>
<p>Flames roared out of my mouth, my eyes, and my skin, pouring from me in an eruption of untamed power. My body was no longer a vessel but an open gate, a conduit for something beyond mortal comprehension.</p>
<p>I should have died.</p>
<p>I should have been reduced to ash.</p>
<p>And yet…</p>
<p>I remained.</p>
<p>At first, the pain reached unimaginable heights. My body, my soul, my very existence was being rewritten in real-time, torn apart within the inferno.</p>
<p>Then, something changed.</p>
<p>The agony, the unbearable torment that had consumed me without mercy, vanished in an instant. It wasn’t a slow transition, not a gradual lessening of pain—but a sharp, absolute shift.</p>
<p>The fire no longer burned me.</p>
<p>It drifted up, escaping my body in slow, measured motions as if it had finally realized something—there was no longer any opposition, no force trying to restrain or dominate it. It had lashed out, fought against me because I had fought against it. But now?</p>
