<p>[Primordial Trial Conquered.]</p>
<p>[Dominant Claim Confirmed.]</p>
<p>[Nyxara, Primordial Demon of Lust: Essence Bound.]</p>
<p>Heat surged from Quinlan's core, steady at first, then heavier, as if something had latched onto the rhythm of his heartbeat.</p>
<p>[Human and Demon: Unified.]</p>
<p>[Conqueror and Conquered: Bound.]</p>
<p>Nyxara's presence slid deeper into him. For half a second, everything aligned.</p>
<p>Then something pressed back.</p>
<p>His body was intact. His soul was intact. The pressure came from somewhere else entirely.</p>
<p>From volume.</p>
<p>The world sharpened around him as he became aware of the strain running through his flesh.</p>
<p>[Warning.]</p>
<p>[Structural Instability Detected.]</p>
<p>Inside him, something vast pushed outward, testing limits that had not been meant to hold it. Muscle resisted. Bone held. His skin pulled tight, refusing to stretch further.</p>
<p>Nyxara screamed.</p>
<p>"No!"</p>
<p>The incessantly moaning succubus was gone, replaced by a concerned demoness. "Stop it! You're being torn apart! Fight it, now! If you let this continue, you'll break!"</p>
<p>Quinlan didn't do as told.</p>
<p>He didn't brace. He didn't push back. Instead, calmly, he closed his eyes and looked inward.</p>
<p>Elemental mastery threaded through muscle and nerve, woven directly into his body.</p>
<p>Nyxara's abyssal authority somehow combined with his Primordial Breeding Physique.</p>
<p>Then there was the soul domain business as well… Understanding settled in him.</p>
<p>Too much. It was just too much.</p>
<p>"Resisting now would kill me."</p>
<p>"What?" Nyxara snapped. "Did you rattle your brain when you electrocuted me with your manhood? Is that why you bit my neck?!"</p>
<p>"This isn't an attack," Quinlan decreed while ignoring the woman's nonsense as he had learned to do during their battle so that he could maintain focus and regenerate mana. "It's capacity expansion."</p>
<p>The pressure climbed.</p>
<p>It dragged along his skin from the inside, hot and unyielding, as if his body were being stretched around something that refused to shrink. Back when he first awakened the Primordial Breeding Physique, the sensation had been similar.</p>
<p>He remembered the wooden inn's floorboards beneath his back. The way his hands had clawed into it. The sound of his own voice breaking as his body rebuilt itself without mercy. Blossom's concerned form hovered nearby, worried out of her mind for his safety.</p>
<p>That time, he had been overwhelmed.</p>
<p>This time, he stayed upright.</p>
<p>The tearing worsened. Heat crawled under his skin in lines, following the red-black markings already etched into him, tracing them deeper. His breath slowed instead of hitching. His shoulders remained level.</p>
<p>Nyxara shouted something in his mind. He barely registered the words.</p>
<p>He remembered forcing his body past failure during their battle. The way his muscles had screamed while he kept moving anyway. The way his senses had sharpened the closer he came to collapse. He remembered that edge, that narrow space where the body begged to stop and the will answered by pushing harder.</p>
<p>This was the same place.</p>
<p>The pressure peaked, grinding through him with methodical intent. His vision wavered. His jaw tightened once, then relaxed.</p>
<p>Suddenly, Quinlan was forced to realize that he was even enjoying the process in a morbid way…</p>
<p>'There's no way breeding a slutty succubus turned me into an M…' he grumbled, analyzing why he was excited.</p>
<p>'Ah. It should've been obvious.'</p>
<p>He felt the way he did because he knew what this meant.</p>
<p>Every pulse of strain carried information. Every tearing line came with weight, with permanence. His body was not being punished. It was being rewritten to keep up.</p>
<p>A low breath left his chest, steady and controlled.</p>
<p>Heat flooded him again, deeper, denser. The markings along his skin flared and spread with purpose, locking into place along his shoulders and neck as if they had always been meant to be there.</p>
<p>Quinlan remained still.</p>
<p>'Let it tear,' he thought calmly.</p>
<p>'Let it grow.'</p>
<p>[Entity Load Exceeds Vessel Parameters.]</p>
<p>Nyxara's voice wavered. "Little Ruin… Your body isn't failing, it's… lagging behind you."</p>
<p>"That's because it's built for what I was."</p>
<p>The tearing reached a peak. His vision dimmed at the edges, but his stance remained unchanged.</p>
<p>[Current Physique Insufficient.]</p>
<p>[Adaptive Solution Initiated.]</p>
<p>[Requirement Identified: Expanded vessel capable of sustaining bound demonic essence, elemental integration, and sovereign soul structure.]</p>
<p>Heat flooded his body again, but it was different from before. The tearing turned into reconstruction.</p>
<p>His muscles tightened, densifying. His bones sang under the strain, reforged. The red-black markings along his skin burned, spreading with purpose, locking into new patterns across his skin.</p>
<p>[Ding!]</p>
<p>[New Primordial Physique Unlocked.]</p>
<p>[Primordial Breeding Physique → Abyssal Genesis Physique.]</p>
<p>Nyxara gasped.</p>
<p>The pressure vanished.</p>
<p>Quinlan drew a slow breath and opened his eyes.</p>
<p>The world came back into focus with a new clarity. The familiar weight of his body was still there, but it felt… denser. More present.</p>
<p>He lifted his gaze and summoned a wall of water, observing his body.</p>
<p>His eyes had changed.</p>
<p>The fourfold elemental hues he once carried had fused into a single tone, smooth and reflective, a pale platinum sheen that held light without reflecting it back. Warmth rolled across his skin thanks to the markings. The heat was familiar. It carried the same steady virility Nyxara's skin had radiated when she had pressed into him in the bed, heat that invited without demanding.</p>
<p>"Abyssal Genesis Physique…" Quinlan murmured.</p>
<p>His frame was unchanged and the markings didn't cover his whole body; they were concentrated on his chest, back, and shoulders.</p>
<p>There was no sudden bulk, no monstrous distortion.</p>
<p>The difference was mostly inside, evidenced by Quinlan feeling more in tune with himself than ever before. His powers felt more 'his.' More in his control than awarded to him by the omnipresent entity called the Soul Records, or the universe itself, or however the world worked. Quinlan didn't know yet.</p>
<p>What he knew was that it felt as though he made a giant leap in establishing his own essence, separate from the established great forces. Quinlan smiled faintly.</p>
<p>"Mostly internal changes, huh?" he noted.</p>
<p>His eyes traced the patterns again. They looked… good.</p>
<p>"Admit it" Nyxara purred into his thoughts, her voice already sliding back into its usual cadence. "You love it. You think it's badass, epic, and all that nonsense masculinity stuff you men care so much about."</p>
<p>Then… the succubus's voice rose into a much higher pitch, turning into a mighty squeal. "And I agree! I can't wait to be pinned beneath you as-"</p>
<p>Quinlan closed his eyes.</p>
<p>He focused inward once again and found her presence coiled comfortably.</p>
<p>He flicked his fingers.</p>
<p>The voice cut off mid-thought.</p>
<p>"Silence, finally," he exhaled happily.</p>
<p>Quinlan opened his eyes again and rolled his shoulder. He then got an idea and started pulling the markings back with his will alone. The symbols responded, then faded, sinking back beneath his skin until he looked just as he had beforehand.</p>
<p>But when he released them, they instantly reappeared, reclaiming their rightful position.</p>
<p>"So that's how it works," he mused quietly.</p>
<p>The marks were not mere decoration, not mere tattoos. They were something more, something deeper than that.</p>
<p>"This is my new form…" Quinlan murmured, and just as Nyxara had deduced, he was extremely pleased with his current physical state. Quinlan wasn't one to ink his skin with decorative symbols, but when it was like this… When the markings were connected to the very depths of his essence, representing who he became, well… He loved them.</p>
<p>However, if ever came the need or desire to appear fully human again, he could pull the marks back.</p>
<p>'How cool…' he mused inwardly with a small grin.</p>
<p>Then his gaze dropped.</p>
<p>Scar lay a short distance away, unmoving.</p>
