<p>At the Guild the following morning, Quinlan found his party’s name beneath a contract requiring Grade Six adventurers.</p>
<p>He read the qualification again.</p>
<p>*Minimum party grade: Six.*</p>
<p>*Exception: Unbound, by express request of the client.*</p>
<p>"..."</p>
<p>Ayame leaned against his arm to inspect the notice, her blue eyes traveling down to the name at the bottom.</p>
<p>"How thoughtful of Captain Veshara."</p>
<p>Vex stopped beside them.</p>
<p>"That bitch again."</p>
<p>The contract concerned an island fortress used by several pirate companies. From its sheltered anchorage, their ships preyed on the merchant traffic entering and leaving Sarthal. The harbor guard intended to take the stronghold and was hiring adventurers to accompany its troops.</p>
<p>Quinlan remembered the captain offering him a fortress assault while they stood amid the wreckage she’d made of his inn.</p>
<p>Apparently, she still had a place for him.</p>
<p>"Grade Six?"</p>
<p>The whisper came from a woman studying the neighboring board. Her companion leaned over her shoulder, following the direction of her stare.</p>
<p>"He’s Grade Three."</p>
<p>"His name’s on it."</p>
<p>"I can see that!"</p>
<p>Quinlan took the notice down. Beside the stairs, Alexios had been examining another board with Myrasyn, while Morgana waited with Raika and Orianna a few steps away. The old swordsman looked up when Quinlan approached.</p>
<p>"Found something?"</p>
<p>"Perhaps."</p>
<p>He offered Alexios the paper, then watched the man’s attention settle on the description of the island. Myrasyn moved closer to read it with him.</p>
<p>"This is the job Veshara offered us," Quinlan said.</p>
<p>Alexios glanced at him over the notice.</p>
<p>"You’re considering it?"</p>
<p>"Let’s hear what the Guild has to say about it first."</p>
<p>At the counter, the clerk was already watching their approach. Her smile widened when Black laid the notice in front of her, then faltered as she recognized it.</p>
<p>"Good morning, sir."</p>
<p>"Good morning. I appear to be wanted."</p>
<p>Her gaze dropped to the exception.</p>
<p>"The client was quite specific."</p>
<p>She pressed her lips together before explaining.</p>
<p>"She requested your party for the range of magic available to you and your wives. An island assault calls for considerable versatility, and the Guild accepted her request."</p>
<p>"So we’re invited for our utility?" Aurora murmured.</p>
<p>"Yes. But..."</p>
<p>She placed her fingertips by the printed grade, drawing his attention back to it.</p>
<p>"Please understand that this is a very difficult contract. The exception allows you to participate. The assignment itself still warrants Grade Six adventurers."</p>
<p>"What can you tell us about it?"</p>
<p>She drew a folder from beneath the counter and opened it beside the notice. Several reports had been fastened inside, along with a rough chart showing the island and the approaches to its harbor.</p>
<p>"It serves as a gathering place. Ships come there to repair, trade stolen cargo, and recruit. Some crews remain longer than others, but the fortress gives all of them somewhere to retreat when our patrols pursue them."</p>
<p>Alexios studied the chart.</p>
<p>"Several captains, then."</p>
<p>"Yes. With their own crew and whatever they’ve acquired from the vessels they’ve taken. The captain will provide the military briefing at the harbor, should you accept the quest."</p>
<p>She indicated the estimates listed for the known crews.</p>
<p>"Several identified fighters are level one hundred or above. Which of them will be present depends on the ships in port, but you should expect opponents of that strength."</p>
<p>"How far is the island?" Quinlan asked.</p>
<p>"About a day’s sailing in favorable weather. It’s conveniently placed for raiding our shipping."</p>
<p>"Why is the harbor guard hiring adventurers to take it?" Alexios asked.</p>
<p>The clerk glanced toward the stairs before lowering her voice.</p>
<p>"Most of Sarthal’s strength is reserved for the other three great powers. Sarthal is... not on the best terms with any of its neighbors. The undead nation is actively invading our lands. As a result, piracy is assigned to the harbor guard.</p>
<p>"Captain Veshara has been requesting more soldiers, but the pirates are treated as pests. The royal army can’t afford to deal with pests right now. Unfortunately, the people aboard the ships they attack don’t find them quite so insignificant."</p>
<p>Quinlan kept a finger beside Veshara’s name on the notice.</p>
<p>"She introduced herself by attacking us. Now she’s asking for us by name again. Before I take my family anywhere with her, what is the Guild prepared to guarantee?"</p>
<p>Vex’s hand closed around his wrist. Behind her, Black Fang watched the clerk without speaking.</p>
<p>"This is a state-funded operation under the harbor command’s authority, sir. The Guild holds the deposit, and our representative is checking the vessel and preparations against the promised support."</p>
<p>She opened the folder farther, revealing an order bearing an official seal and a receipt attached beneath it.</p>
<p>"The captain acted without authorization at the inn. Afterward, the Guildmaster required her superior to confirm these recruitment conditions in writing. Your assignment must concern the operation. Private tests or investigations of hired adventurers are expressly forbidden."</p>
<p>Vex leaned forward to read the restriction while Quinlan kept his attention on the clerk.</p>
<p>"What happens if she ignores them?" Quinlan asked.</p>
<p>"You may refuse work outside the agreed assignment. The Guild will support that refusal and take any breach directly to her command. The Guildmaster has endorsed the conditions himself."</p>
<p>Quinlan drew the papers closer, checking the named payer and the endorsement before passing them to Ayame.</p>
<p>"She’ll use the expedition to study us."</p>
<p>"She may. Her curiosity gives her no authority over you beyond the agreed work."</p>
<p>"Has anyone else accepted?"</p>
<p>"Big Ambitions. A Grade Six party. They’re at the harbor already."</p>
<p>"Show me their record," Quinlan said.</p>
<p>"I’ll get it for you, sir."</p>
<p>Before she turned away, he tapped the reward entry on the notice.</p>
<p>"And the reward? This just says to ask at reception."</p>
<p>"Twenty thousand crowns on completion for Unbound. Your party would also receive twenty percent of the captured military equipment and unclaimed treasure. You may keep your share as goods or have it sold and receive the proceeds."</p>
<p>Silky’s treasure had brought them twenty-five thousand crowns once they’d counted the usable coin and sold the valuables they wanted to part with. Their subsequent week of contracts had added roughly three thousand more.</p>
<p>The mansion had been offered at twenty thousand crowns. Thanks to Kaelira’s helpful inspection, he’d paid sixteen thousand.</p>
<p>Aurora observed through the link."&gt; &lt;One successful job would buy the house and leave us four thousand crowns,&gt; Aurora observed through the link.</p>
<p>"&gt; &lt;A little different from picking herbs, isn’t it?&gt;</p>
<p>Three crowns for a basket of fieldleaf. Three hundred for the expedition that had led them to Silky’s nest. Celia had risked financial ruin to put up that reward, and now he was considering a single payment worth more than his new mansion.</p>
<p>After the purchase and their other expenses, he had about ten thousand crowns of local money available to spend. The money needed to settle the loan against Marielle’s freedom was already set aside.</p>
<p>Their wealth in Iskaris was separate. He wanted to establish himself here through what he earned here, and this contract could put considerably more equipment into his people’s hands.</p>
<p>"We could keep useful pieces," Aurora said as she approached the counter.</p>
<p>"Yes. There’s also an allowance worth two thousand crowns from the harbor’s approved supply list, awarded on completion. Certain mana conductors and ward components on it are normally reserved for military customers. You may choose what you need up to that value."</p>
<p>Kaelira’s attention sharpened.</p>
<p>"Could we see that list too?"</p>
<p>"I’ll fetch both, sir."</p>
