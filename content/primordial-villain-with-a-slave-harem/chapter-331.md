<p>"Kekeke! You’ll be paid handsomely." Was all the crazy man, Mordecai, stated in response before shouting, letting his unnaturally deep and monstrous voice travel across the land. "Vex ’The Hexblade’, show your face! It’s time I kill you for what you did a century ago."</p>
<p>Suddenly, a high-pitched feminine giggling rippled across us before a shadow leaped down from the sky.</p>
<p>"Hehehe… The rat boy sure is confident when the odds are on his side." Vex said, but none could tell where she was exactly. However, in the next moment, she landed between us and the trio of high-level enemies, then stabbed her blade into the ground and whispered ominously; "[Dark Covenant: Curse of the Revenant]"</p>
<p>Shadows surged out from her blade that was etched into the earth in the form of a pitch-black river that snaked across the ground and crept toward the enemy soldiers with an unsettling speed. The shadows writhed and twisted like extensions of Vex herself, each one seething with dark energy. They closed in on their chosen targets by slipping beneath their feet and merging with their shadows. For a brief moment, the soldiers’ own shadows rebelled, thrashing against Vex’s influence. However, they succumbed one by one with their forms distorting as they were overtaken.</p>
<p>In each soldier’s shadow now stood a replica of Vex- with her kendo uniform, beautiful silver hair, and drawn blade being completely unmistakable. This sight was incredibly creepy, to be honest. It was as if they just lost their shadows, no. Not lost but had it stolen.</p>
<p>Vex stole their shadows and replaced them with her own.</p>
<p>When the shadows reached the three ’bosses’ they clashed against theirs and despite her spell’s relentless assault, Vex’s shadows were overpowered. "Tch!" The woman scoffed with dissatisfaction. It looked like the trio combated her spell, or simply were just too strong to get their shadows stolen since I didn’t see any of them do anything in particular that indicated that they were fighting back against her strange spell.</p>
<p>Then, one of the soldiers who was very clearly terrified by the Veil Walker’s attack panicked and charged at Vex. She watched the man for a brief moment before equipping a sinister smirk on her lips and welcoming his advance with open arms. He swung his axe with clear intentions to decapitate her- but it passed through her neck as if she were nothing but mist.</p>
<p>A real ghost.</p>
