<p>By the time the brothers realized it, it was too late.</p>
<p>Their enemy stood behind them now.</p>
<p>But Daron, who was the eldest and the strongest among the trio, managed to react. His instincts moved faster than thought. In one seamless motion, he twisted his body and drove his spear backward.</p>
<p>The blade punched clean through flesh and bone.</p>
<p>Straight through their enemy’s chest.</p>
<p>There was no armor to stop it.</p>
<p>Only skin, ribs, and the dull crunch of something vital giving way.</p>
<p>The shaft quivered in Daron’s grip as he felt it strike true.</p>
<p>But he didn’t get to savor it.</p>
<p>Because in that very same instant, Quinlan’s hand came alive.</p>
<p>Wind exploded around his fist and forearm, becoming a vortex of cutting force, howling loudly with compressed violence. It wasn’t just wind anymore, but a motion weaponized. A drill of air and magic, spinning fast enough to bend light around it.</p>
<p>And it drove straight toward the back of Veyne’s neck.</p>
<p>At that precise moment...</p>
<p>The immense velocity of Quinlan’s approach finally caught up with reality. Bolstered by his wind magic even further, this burst of kinetic force slammed into Veyne’s face, snapping his head back. He never even saw the attack. The impact launched him straight into the wind drill, screaming toward him.</p>
<p>His enchanted armor held.</p>
<p>For half a second.</p>
<p>Then it didn’t.</p>
<p>The centrifugal force shredded through the plating, grinding it apart layer by layer before tearing into flesh. Blood atomized into mist. Veins and bones twisted into a spiral.</p>
<p>And then, his head came off.</p>
<p>Ripped clean from his shoulders, the head spun into the air in a crimson spiral, eyes wide with shock as life fled his grasp.</p>
<p>It all happened in a blink.</p>
<p>The spray of blood hadn’t even finished falling when Daron’s eyes went wide.</p>
<p>He felt the recoil through his spear. Knew he’d landed the strike. His instincts were honed razor-sharp. Yet in that heartbeat, when his younger brother’s head was torn from his body, when the gore splattered across his boots, shock overcame all.</p>
<p>"... Veyne?"</p>
<p>He barely breathed the name.</p>
<p>Then his teeth ground together so much that they almost broke. Fury followed.</p>
<p>...</p>

<p>It was the spectacle of it. The precision. The sheer, breath-stealing force of the execution.</p>

<p>Soft sobbing.</p>

<p>They only knew of this noble called Black as a strange man who hid his identity but somehow earned the favor of the royal princess, and perhaps even the king’s. But that was all he was before: an intriguing, mysterious man who stoked their curiosity.</p>

<p>The father, a tall man with an impeccable goatee and the air of someone who had suffered long hours of female chatter every single day, turned his head in their direction.</p>
<p>"You two do realize the age gap between them is straight-up unacceptable, right? There’s no way the Queen would permit such a relationship to blossom."</p>

<p>"Yes, dear... Please keep it to yourself," the countess replied dryly. "I was having an important bonding moment with our daughter."</p>
<p>The man leaned back in his chair. Rather than scowl or argue, he smiled. A slow, thoughtful grin emerged on his features that didn’t quite reach his eyes. He clasped his hands in his lap, watching his daughter out of the corner of his eye with the same analytical calm he used in estate negotiations.</p>
<p>Then, he spoke.</p>
<p>"How do you feel about giving it a shot?"</p>
<p>The brunette blinked. "What?"</p>
<p>That was when realization dawned. Her eyes widened, and her cheeks flared bright pink. "Father! Are you seriously suggesting I throw myself at him?"</p>
<p>She wasn’t entirely horrified at the proposal, not at all. Just surprised. Her eyes moved back to the blood-streaked man in the arena.</p>
<p>"Felicity already has a claim..." she mumbled, barely audible.</p>
<p>But it was the mother who scoffed. "Please. He’s not a loaf of bread at the market; there’s no ’claim’ unless something’s official. And as your father pointed out, Felicity is too young. He’s free meat."</p>
<p>Her gaze swept from her daughter to the opposite stands, where Black’s ten wives stood, each beyond beautiful even with masks covering their faces. They were dangerous, exotic, and visibly proud of their place at his side.</p>
<p>"He seems to be a man who enjoys the finer things in life..." she mused. "And you, daughter, could make any man happy."</p>
