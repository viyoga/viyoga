<p>"I know what you’ve fought," Quinlan replied. "But this battle will see us go against enemies at level 100 and above. You simply aren’t ready."</p>
<p>"But-"</p>
<p>"Feng."</p>
<p>She slumped back in her chair, arms folded so tightly that he wondered whether she was trying to squeeze the indignation out of herself.</p>
<p>"You’re both being awful."</p>
<p>"I agree," Felicity declared. "Completely awful."</p>
<p>The two girls glared at their respective tormentors. Alexios endured his daughter’s fury while Quinlan took the chair beside him, smiling at Feng’s enormous pout.</p>
<p>She turned away from him with an offended little huff.</p>
<p>Felicity caught her friend’s hand across the table.</p>
<p>"We should refuse to speak to them."</p>
<p>"For how long?"</p>
<p>"Until they apologize."</p>
<p>Alexios reached for his cup. Quinlan watched him take a leisurely drink while his daughter looked ready to throw her pastry at him.</p>
<p>"Father!"</p>
<p>"That was brief."</p>
<p>Feng snorted, then tried to look solemn when Felicity glared at her.</p>
<p>"I’m still on your side."</p>
<p>"You’d better be."</p>
<p>Quinlan let them mutter together while he turned toward Myrasyn and Morgana. Raika and Orianna would accompany them too; the rest of the travelers could remain at the mansion or take a look around the city while Ria began looking into Sarthal’s underworld.</p>
<p>"If the briefing satisfies us, I’d like all of you to join the expedition," he said. "Before we choose the soldiers for our mercenary idea or craft any equipment for them, let’s see what they’ll actually face."</p>
<p>Alexios lowered his cup.</p>
<p>"Agreed. We can learn from the dragonkin and this other party as well as the pirates."</p>
<p>"And ask sensible questions once we’ve seen them fight," Myrasyn added. "I’d rather discover what our people lack while we still have time to prepare."</p>
<p>"Precisely."</p>
<p>The girls had fallen silent again. Quinlan glanced back toward them and found Feng whispering into Felicity’s ear.</p>
<p>Whatever she’d said earned a slow, delighted smile.</p>
<p>"Fine," the young queen announced. "We’ll stay here."</p>
<p>Alexios regarded her with immediate suspicion.</p>
<p>"Thank you."</p>
<p>"Nessa will keep us entertained," Feng added.</p>
<p>Quinlan looked between them.</p>
<p>"What did Nessa do?"</p>
<p>"Nothing~" Felicity sang.</p>
<p>"She’s just very fun to tease," Feng explained, her earlier indignation giving way to a grin. "We’ll be helping her get used to the family."</p>
<p>Nessa came through the kitchen doorway with a fresh pot of tea. Both girls turned toward her at once, their smiles so bright that the maid stopped beside the table.</p>
<p>"My ladies?"</p>
<p>"We’ll be spending the day with you!" Felicity said.</p>
<p>"Please take care of them while we’re gone," Quinlan sighed.</p>
<p>Nessa’s face lit up.</p>
<p>"Oh! It’d be my honor!"</p>
<p>...</p>
<p>On the way to the harbor, Alexios fell into step beside Quinlan while the women walked ahead of them down the sloping avenue.</p>
<p>"What did you make of the other party’s record?"</p>
<p>"Centuries of completed commissions. Fortified positions, dangerous escorts, work other parties had already failed to finish."</p>
<p>"And their strength?"</p>
<p>"Their last recorded levels ranged from one hundred and ten to one hundred and twenty-five."</p>
<p>Myrasyn glanced back at them. Quinlan understood her interest; on Iskaris, even their oldest veterans had spent their lives beneath the barrier’s limit. Here they were about to meet an entire party well past level one hundred.</p>
<p>"Is that usual for Grade Six?" Alexios asked.</p>
<p>"Experienced, powerful people, yes. Though the grade depends on the work they’ve proved they can complete. A powerful newcomer still starts at One."</p>
<p>Ayame slowed to join them as a laden wagon passed, its driver calling for room along the avenue.</p>
<p>"Parties of that standing are rare," she said. "Those are the adventurers people hope will take a contract when the others won’t. They earn their reputation by coming back from those jobs."</p>
<p>Quinlan nodded to her. The records had given him plenty to think about, particularly when he considered how much of their time abroad had been spent around ordinary adventurers impressed by everything his family did.</p>
<p>He wanted to see what these veterans could teach them.</p>
<p>Ahead, the avenue opened onto the harbor. The guard’s meeting place occupied a broad stretch of quay beside its storehouses, where sailors were loading supplies onto a moored vessel while a dragonkin officer checked the departing crates against a list.</p>
<p>Beyond the loading area stood three women who made the warehouse doors look rather inadequate.</p>
<p>Quinlan slowed.</p>
<p>Each was close to six meters tall. The nearest had reddish-brown hair in a thick braid, bare arms corded with muscle, and an enormous axe resting against her shoulder. Plates of fitted armor covered her chest and hips, leaving the powerful lengths of her thighs visible above elaborate metal boots.</p>
<p>Beside her, a dark-haired giantess was fastening a shield to her forearm. Its curved face could have sheltered several ordinary soldiers. A mace hung at her waist, the head enclosed in overlapping steel bands.</p>
<p>The third sat on a broad block of stone with a hammer across her knees, holding one foot very still.</p>
<p>"Wiggle, please do not."</p>
<p>Quinlan looked down.</p>
<p>A gnome sat beside her boot in a chair mounted on jointed metal legs. Silver hair stuck out around the magnifying lenses strapped over his eyes, and his pointed nose hovered above an open panel in her ankle armor.</p>
<p>"I’m not moving, Bim."</p>
<p>"Excellent. Continue with that, yes."</p>
<p>He tightened a fitting inside the panel. Blue light passed through the exposed runes, and a mechanism behind the giantess’s heel gave a short hiss.</p>
<p>"There. Better, much better."</p>
<p>She flexed her ankle once he withdrew his tools, studying the movement with a pleased smile.</p>
<p>"That’s comfortable."</p>
<p>"Comfortable and powerful. A wonderful combination, hmm?"</p>
<p>"Like you."</p>
<p>The gnome’s nose lifted. Before he could answer, she bent down and planted a kiss across most of his crown.</p>
<p>When she drew back, his silver hair lay plastered against his scalp in damp clumps. A wet strand had fallen over one of his lenses. Bim peeled it away between two fingers, staring at the evidence of her affection.</p>
<p>"Sweet words. Distracting words. A bath, also. Working, I am."</p>
<p>"You’ve finished."</p>
<p>"With the ankle, yes. With everything else..."</p>
<p>Her fingers closed gently around his middle, her thumb spanning his chest. With his boots dangling beneath her hand, she lifted him out of the chair and set him on the broad shelf of her thigh, then reached into a pouch beside her hammer.</p>
<p>"You can eat first."</p>
<p>She broke a morsel from her loaf and offered it between two fingertips. Bim took a bite and accepted the rest in both hands; the piece she’d pinched off was large enough to occupy him for a while.</p>
<p>From his seat on her thigh, he could inspect the ankle repair without leaving the comfort of his wife. His feet swung contentedly over the edge of her leg as he chewed.</p>
<p>Quinlan heard a smothered laugh. Lucille had brought a hand to her lips, while Serika was watching the pair with undisguised delight.</p>
<p>The axe wielder noticed their approach and lowered her weapon, resting its butt on the paving.</p>
<p>"Here for the briefing?"</p>
<p>"We are. Adventurer Black, of Unbound."</p>
<p>"Oh. You’re the exception."</p>
<p>At that, the gnome stopped chewing.</p>
<p>He peered past the giantess’s arm and adjusted his lenses before pushing them up onto his forehead.</p>
<p>"Unbound?"</p>
<p>"Yes."</p>
<p>"Ah! Curious about you, I was."</p>
<p>The giantess brushed a crumb from his beard with one finger. His entire head went sideways with the stroke.</p>
<p>"The beard, my flower. Just the beard."</p>
<p>"Sorry, love."</p>
<p>She steadied his head between two fingertips and removed the crumb with greater care before lowering him back into his chair. Bim turned a handle beside the seat, extending its legs until he could address Quinlan without having to crane his neck.</p>
<p>"Bim. Leader of Big Ambitions."</p>
<p>Quinlan looked from the damp hair to the badge on the gnome’s coat. A six was engraved on it.</p>
<p>"You’re the party joining the assault?"</p>
<p>"Indeed! Looking forward to working together, I am."</p>
