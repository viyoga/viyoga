<p>Vex then returned her gaze to me and shook her head while responding in full. "As you should know, the Consortium is no longer doing its best to remain on the good side of the duke. <p>"I see… Before you people make plans involving my Heralds, run it by me first. I might not be as strong as you, but I have a lot more tricks under my belt. If I wanted to, I could make your lives a living hell."</p>

<p>I had to chuckle wryly while shaking my head. I didn’t appreciate being tested, it’s not easy on my heart to threaten my future lover who could also tear me apart if she took offense. "Great. Now, could you tell us how you know so much about us?"</p>
<p>"Ah, the impatience of youth…"</p>
<p>"You know, you keep insinuating you’re much older than us, but when I make a note of that fact, you act like I’m a rude piece of shit. Can you tell me what’s up with that?"</p>
<p>"No."</p>
<p>She instantly shot my request down before continuing as if nothing happened, "You made a big blunder multiple times, future hubby. <p>"Don’t put it like that…" I said, not liking the way she spoke about herself.</p>
