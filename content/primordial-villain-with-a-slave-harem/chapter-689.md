<p>Ayame sprang to her feet, sword raised in a tight grip. The clicking of high heels echoed once more through the golden void, growing louder, closer.</p>
<p>Then she appeared again.</p>
<p>The mute woman.</p>
<p>She moved with unshaken grace, her golden-embroidered yukata flowing like liquid silk, her face a mask of serene, unreadable beauty. There was no hostility in her expression. No amusement. No cruelty.</p>
<p>Just silence.</p>
<p>Ayame gritted her teeth, planting her stance firmly. Although she didn't quite understand what was going on, she refused to be caught off guard again.</p>
<p>The woman's hand drifted to the hilt of her katana.</p>
<p>Not this time.</p>
<p>Ayame lunged forward, her blade flashing in a precise, lightning-fast arc.</p>
<p>But it was all for naught.</p>
<p>Her sword whiffed through empty air, as if cutting at a ghost.</p>
<p>The woman was already gone.</p>
<p>Ayame's instincts screamed at her. She twisted, spun, slashed—but the woman was nowhere to be seen.</p>
<p>Then, from behind…</p>
<p>She felt a sharp tug at her collar.</p>
<p>Before Ayame could even process what was happening, a powerful force yanked her backward, throwing her hard onto the ground.</p>
<p>She coughed, gasping for air, only to feel the cold press of steel against her throat.</p>
<p>The woman stood above her, her katana's edge gently resting just beneath Ayame's chin.</p>
<p>Ayame's fingers twitched toward her weapon.</p>
<p>A flicker of movement.</p>
<p>Steel flashed, accompanied by the crackling of electricity.</p>
<p>Her head flew off her shoulders.</p>
<p>She gasped awake, body jerking upright… whole again.</p>
<p>The high heels clicked once more.</p>
<p>The woman had already returned, walking toward her with that same, unreadable expression.</p>
<p>Ayame gritted her teeth.</p>
<p>She wouldn't lose again.</p>
<p>She refused.</p>
<p>This time, she wouldn't wait. The best defense is a good offense.</p>
<p>'If I can't react to my enemy's movements, I must be on the attack!'</p>
<p>Ayame launched herself forward, her blade lashing out with great speed. A perfectly executed strike, meant to kill.</p>
<p>It never even touched her opponent.</p>
<p>The woman was gone, leaving an electric trail behind her wake.</p>
<p>Ayame's mind barely had time to register her own failure before an unseen force slammed into her ribs.</p>
<p>Her world spun.</p>
<p>She crashed onto her side but forced herself up, pivoting into another strike.</p>
<p>Her katana shattered.</p>
<p>The woman stood just behind her, her blade's polished edge now painted with the faintest hint of red.</p>
<p>Ayame couldn't even feel where she had been cut.</p>
<p>Her knees buckled.</p>
<p>Her body collapsed.</p>
<p>Her vision blurred, then faded to black.</p>
<p>She awoke again, breathing ragged.</p>
<p>Her sword was whole again.</p>
<p>Her body was whole again.</p>
<p>The void remained unchanged.</p>
<p>And then—</p>
<p>*Click. Click. Click.*</p>
<p>The high heels echoed, slow, deliberate.</p>
<p>Ayame's fingers clenched into fists.</p>
