<p>Scar lowered her head at Quinlan’s command, obediently answering. "We were invited by Kaede Fujimori to her manor in the capital. She extended to us an offer, a quest. We were asked to aid her. She said that if she succeeded, she would be able to break the barrier that keeps the continent of Iskaris sealed away from the rest of the world."</p>
<p>The air instantly tensed.</p>
<p>Quinlan asked this question because he assumed they were acting as mercenaries for hire. If he offered them more money than Kaede had, then maybe they would give up on their mission. Well, perhaps not him personally, because he doubted Lilith would accept any money he offered.</p>
<p>But this seemed no longer to be on the table. Kaede offered them something he could not match, for he had no idea how to remove the barrier.</p>
<p>He’d already visited it once, and when he went into the water, he found himself teleported back a few paces. Tunneling underground or flying did not solve the problem.</p>
<p>Iskaris was sealed very tightly.</p>
<p>But what Kaede seemed to offer is not escape through the barrier but outright its destruction...</p>
<p>His primordial brain was instantly rushing through a great number of theories.</p>
<p>"How does she plan to achieve that?" Black Fang asked, no longer able to remain in her focused meditative state. This was far too big a topic for her to stay out of.</p>
<p>Black Fang was not only a strong combatant; she used her free time to read a myriad of obscure tomes, doing anything in her power to obtain knowledge, for the woman knew that knowledge was just as important as personal strength, if not more.</p>
<p>Her alert reaction told Quinlan she had no idea how to break the barrier either.</p>
<p>Scar appeared as if she hadn’t even registered the question asked of her.</p>
<p>It didn’t take long for Quinlan to realize what was going on. His order of his soul soldier was to ’answer the questions of my women.’ Scar did not consider Black Fang his woman.</p>
<p>"Answer her questions as well."</p>
<p>"Yes, Master. We were not told specifics, but to us, even the promise itself was enough to make a leap of faith."</p>
<p>"Hmm... I think I understand," Kitsara stroked her chin, looking quite academic in this moment. "Leaving Iskaris is such an impossible task that no one offers it as payment. It must’ve been too absurd to leave on the table."</p>
<p>Scar nodded at the sharp foxkin. "That is indeed the case. She promised that if we helped her, she would shatter the barrier. To us - especially to Lilith - that was an offer too tempting to refuse."</p>
<p>"... Why is that the case?" Jasmine seemed confused. "You are powerful here in Iskaris, almost no one can endanger your lives, especially if you five work together. Being here should be a paradise for your group. Who knows what’s out there?"</p>
<p>Quinlan sent his merchant girl a tender smile; he found her thoughts rather adorable. It was obvious how different Jasmine thought from them.</p>
<p>After all, while the brunette was confused as to why one would want to give up their favorable position, he himself understood the Scarlet Lilies and Lilith in particular perfectly.</p>
<p>"Aside from a select few, Lilith is stronger than anyone. She finds it hard to have challenging fights and must think it stunts her growth."</p>
<p>One might assume Lilith was a weak person with a lot to improve on due to her lacking performance in their latest clash, but one should consider she was fighting high in the skies, tethered to Void.</p>
<p>She was entirely out of her element back there, especially because she faced Quinlan and his many tricks.</p>
<p>Scar was quick to voice her thoughts. "Master is correct. And more than the aspect of intense battles, she has always been an adventurer at heart. To her, being a bird trapped in a cage was suffocating. She longs to see the rest of Thalorind, to step where no one else from Iskaris has ever set foot. We’ve already traveled the continent many times over, taking quests from both the Beastman Confederation and the Alliance of Elvardia. We felt we’d already seen everything. Thus, we agreed with her vision."</p>
<p>Ayame’s brows furrowed. "So this was never about politics. Kaede merely offered you freedom, and you accepted."</p>
<p>Scar nodded once. "Yes. We had grown tired of mere adventurer work. It became too easy for our team. Routine quests asking for monster extermination. Coin, fame, the usual trappings, they all lost meaning. We struggled not against enemies, but against stagnation. Lilith sought something greater in life than continuing this way until we grew too old and tired. And when Kaede placed that path in front of us, we followed her."</p>
<p>"It matched your own desire as well, didn’t it?" Iris suddenly voiced her thoughts. "If you left Iskaris, you hoped you’d be able to gain more strength and return here with enough power to ensure no more children suffered the same fate as you had."</p>
<p>"Yes. At this rate, it would’ve taken me at least a hundred more years to become a true top-tier combatant, but even if that happened, I would not be able to grow strong enough to force my will upon the king and the nobility. I hoped leaving Iskaris would let me gain strength quickly and to a greater extent."</p>
<p>Iris remained quiet, hearing the woman’s words, mulling them over. But then, she looked at the woman who now sported soul-blue skin and asked, "What about now?"</p>
<p>"My sole mission is to serve my master."</p>
<p>Quinlan’s voice cut into the heavy silence.</p>
<p>"What if I released you from my service?"</p>
<p>Scar froze instantly. For the first time since she had appeared, her composure was shattered. Her eyes widened, her breath hitched, and then she dropped to her knees so fast that many couldn’t keep up with her speed.</p>
<p>"If I wronged you, Master, I beg your forgiveness. Please... Unless I did something irredeemable, don’t abandon me."</p>
<p>The women stared, shocked. Scar had been nothing but unflinching until now, not even blinking, even as she recounted her tragic childhood. Yet the mere prospect of release reduced her to a panicked wreck.</p>
<p>Quinlan, however, only watched her with calm eyes. He had kind of expected this.</p>
<p>It confirmed a suspicion that his Elite Soul summons carried an ingrained, extreme loyalty, twisted into the very core of their being.</p>
<p>To them, being released wasn’t freedom but punishment, a sign they had failed him so utterly they were no longer worthy of serving.</p>
<p>"Rise," Quinlan said with a firm tone. Scar obeyed instantly with her head bowed and her whole frame trembling.</p>
<p>"You are not in trouble. I only wanted to see if you retained more of your old self than memories."</p>
<p>Scar’s shoulders shook once, as though relief physically left her body. She didn’t dare raise her gaze, but the fear in her expression slowly gave way.</p>
<p>Quinlan’s gaze lingered on the woman before him.</p>
<p>"How do you feel about your dream now, Scar? Do you still want to see the abuse of children end?"</p>
<p>Her answer came at once.</p>
<p>"If that is what Master desires."</p>
<p>That reply didn’t satisfy him. His eyes narrowed. He wanted to know if his summons had lost everything that once made them living beings. Was she still herself, or nothing more than a soul-blue husk that wore her memories like a mask?</p>
<p>Black Fang studied Scar’s expression with sharp eyes, similarly interested in finding out the answer to the question.</p>
<p>"How do you think you would feel if your master also fought for the same goal you once held?"</p>
