Injections are, in my opinion, perhaps the worst, but chances are that they have no technology to make those in this world, or at least not available to people like these. Smoking would lead to an almost immediate high with the narcotic filling the lungs and airways, reaching the bloodstream easily and swiftly.</p>
<p>Pills, on the other hand, need more time to be ingested, as they first need to pass through the consumer's digestive system.</p>
<p>Does this mean that pills are better than smoking?</p>
<p>No. While they are slower to deliver the desired results, they tend to last longer, allowing for prolonged intoxication.
