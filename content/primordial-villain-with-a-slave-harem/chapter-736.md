<p>Blossom vehemently shook her head. “Master is the greatest man to have ever lived, including all races. He’s smarter than any elf, stronger than any beastkin, and cooler than even the biggest hero from Mom’s bedtime stories!”</p>
<p>"My little girl sure fell hard Natalie whispered with a helpless laugh, brushing some stray locks out of Blossom’s face as a soft, joyful grin bloomed across her lips. “I never thought I’d see the day… but I’m glad. You look so happy, Blossom.”</p>
<p>"She sure is,” Blossom nodded vehemently before snuggling closer to her again.</p>
<p>But then something in Natalie’s eyes shifted.</p>
<p>While continuing to stroke her long-lost daughter’s blonde hair, she slowly sat upright, her expression becoming more focused. Calculating.</p>
<p>She may have stopped Poppy from calling her younger daughter names… but even Natalie had to admit: Blossom had always been just a little too trusting, a little too much of an airhead. Her heart was big, soft, and wildly prone to charging forward before her brain had time to catch up.</p>
<p>And now… she had sworn loyalty to a human?</p>
<p>One seated on a throne, surrounded by royal warriors, commanding powerful spells like some mystical sovereign king?</p>
<p>Natalie’s warm smile remained.</p>
<p>But inside?</p>
<p>She was ready.</p>
<p>If this so-called “Master” had used her daughter… if he had bent Blossom’s trust for cruel ends, if he had broken her spirit and wrapped her in gilded chains…</p>
<p>Natalie would move heaven and earth itself to tear this wretched man apart, no matter the overwhelming odds stacked against her.</p>
<p>Her eyes narrowed as she observed the man from head to toe. Sensing her thought process, Poppy understood what was going on, and she quickly joined her mother in the inquisitive staring. She was the first to address him.</p>
<p>"What do you love about my little sister?”</p>
