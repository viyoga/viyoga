<p>"... What have you done, girl?"</p>
<p>"Ehehehe~!"</p>
<p>She had no proper answer to give.</p>
<p>"Father is so going to kill me if he ever learns of this..." Felicity couldn’t help but giggle.</p>
<p>"Kill you? Then what about the grown guy who enslaved his daughter?" Feng asked, seemingly having the time of her life. Quinlan saw this smug teen be cheeky as all hell, but the expression on her face right now perhaps triumphed all previous ones.</p>
<p>He had to admit, she was the architect here. Not only because she was the one who told Felicity about the existence of [Subjugation], but also because she was quite likely the first true friend Felicity made.</p>
<p>The duo might deny it all they want, but besides him, neither of them managed to create proper bonds before meeting each other.</p>
<p>And while Quinlan cared for Felicity, the existence of Feng, another teenage girl who was not only happy but also thriving while being his slave, must’ve served as a reassurance to the princess.</p>
<p>Feng was the living example of how good one had it being his slave. Though if the princess were to see the Greenvale twins, her opinion on the matter might change a bit.</p>
<p>Quinlan was a cruel master to some. Vivienne and Amara Greenvale have been sentenced to punishments lasting 10,000 days. The former served as the bed warmer for his slaves, while the latter served as a punching bag.</p>
<p>And even after the construction slaves left for Miri Town to work there, he still ensured the twins would serve their sentences. One might call him spiteful or even petty, but he thought otherwise.</p>
<p>He had sentenced the twins to their punishments because he felt their numerous victims were owed this, if not more. Thus, in Quinlan’s eyes, he was carrying out justice each time he teleported the two whimpering bitches to the arms of his slaves.</p>
<p>Anyhow.</p>
<p>Hearing Feng’s cheeky words, Felicity harrumphed. "Lord Quinlan did nothing wrong! I asked to be his slave. If Father has a problem, I’ll deal with it."</p>
<p>Despite knowing Alexios would subject Quinlan to the worst torture imaginable forever and ever if he learned of this - no matter what Felicity said - he appreciated how dedicated the girl seemed to be toward playing her self-assigned bridge role.</p>
<p>"I’d love to see that," Feng giggled, then turned toward the princess with a new expression. With the smugness suddenly gone, she looked... excited.</p>
<p>Excited to share many things with the girl.</p>
<p>She wasted no time; Feng was on a roll.</p>
<p>Now that the threat of Felicity betraying them was eliminated, the floodgates opened. The oriental teen couldn’t stop herself from talking.</p>
<p>Her hands moved as she spoke, while her voice was quick and lively. "Hear this, bratty princess! Quin isn’t just some powerful man! He’s a Primordial. A unique race, superior to that of humans. He’s an immortal in the sense that he’s ageless! He’ll have this annoyingly handsome mug even a million years from now."</p>
<p>"... What?" Felicity gasped, shocked to her very core.</p>
<p>Feng did not slow down to explain. "He’s got more classes and spells than most families combined! You wouldn’t believe how insane some of his spells are!" she decreed, her eyes sparkling. "And get this: he even invaded my world!"</p>
<p>Felicity blinked a million times within a second. "Invaded... your what?"</p>
<p>"World. Basically, another place like Thalorind!" Feng explained proudly. "It’s called Zhenwu. That’s where we met. Back then, I was not only the sole nation toppling jade beauty he interacted with, but he also relied on me so heavily that my back was nearly hurting from all the carrying I was doing! I was his guide, his mentor, his only trusted ally! The poor guy couldn’t even go to sleep without holding onto me!"</p>
<p>There were a great deal of embellishments there, especially with the last sentence. The girl was dejected and about to freeze under the naked night, which is why Quinlan protected her by letting her sleep in his hold. After that, she was basically glued to him every night.</p>
<p>And not because he asked her to.</p>
<p>But Quinlan did not pause the girl’s eccentric bragging. He was watching with great amusement at how this awesome girl was behaving. Quinlan was incredibly happy to see such a lively face of Feng Jiai, letting him know that she indeed needed someone like Felicity, a girl her age.</p>
<p>"We trained together, fought bandits, listened to the rude grumblings of a wrinkly gramps, climbed mountains, nearly died many times..." Feng continued, but then her lips formed into a pout, dissatisfied.</p>
<p>"Then Serika showed up, and suddenly it was not just the two of us. Suddenly, Quinlan was too busy to spend time with me, instead wanting to bathe together with the woman and enjoy her oily full body massages... Hmph!"</p>
<p>Quinlan raised a brow at the girl’s antics, but once again did not interrupt. He didn’t know she felt like this, especially because it wasn’t quite true. Of course, he greatly enjoyed the sensual baths and massages with the fiery redhead, but he did not neglect Feng.</p>
<p>"And then?" Felicity leaned in, eyes wide.</p>
<p>"Then he faced a god!" Feng decreed, as if describing the weather. She was still pretending to be a bit annoyed with him for her perceived neglect. "And killed the fucker."</p>
<p>The princess shot up from her seat. "God?! Slain a god?!"</p>
<p>Then she looked confused, "Wait, what is a god?"</p>
<p>Feng grinned ear to ear, dropping the mistreated girl act. "Yup! There’s a reason he’s called the God Slayer by that weird thing."</p>
<p>Felicity’s mouth opened and closed like she was trying to form words but couldn’t decide which to start with. Feng took that as an invitation to keep going.</p>
<p>"And, yes, there are lots of gods, you know. The one ruling over this world, Thalorind, is just one of many. She’s the governor here. Her name’s Lilyanna, by the way."</p>
<p>Felicity was so shocked she could barely keep up. In fact, she couldn’t. It seemed she gave up and instead decided to mouth the name. She tilted her head, repeating the name softly. "Lilyanna... what a beautiful name! She must be a very nice person!"</p>
<p>Feng giggled. "Oh, I’m not so sure... She and Quinlan butt heads sometimes. This arrogant guy still hasn’t forgiven her for not giving him the Healer class, and she’s still mad about him stealing her souls."</p>
<p>"... Stealing her what?" Felicity whispered. Her face went pale, and her eyes began to lose focus as if her mind was trying to process a thousand things at once. She swayed where she sat, then collapsed against her bedsheets with a muted thud.</p>
<p>"Hey, princess? Anyone home?" Feng waved her hand in front of the girl’s face.</p>
<p>Felicity jolted upright again, shouting at the top of her lungs, "The Goddess is mad at Lord Quinlan?!"</p>
<p>Feng burst into laughter. "Hahaha! You should’ve seen your face! But yeah, they’ve spoken before. And I think if his mothers didn’t adore him so much, maybe she would’ve done something bad to him."</p>
<p>"His... mothers?" Felicity asked weakly. "He has more than one...?"</p>
<p>"Mhm." Feng leaned forward with a sly smirk. "Luminara and Mearie. You might’ve heard of them. Especially Luminara."</p>
<p>Felicity froze. Her lips trembled. "L-Luminara?! The only Luminara I know is-"</p>
