<p>The blonde assassin with red lips didn't even flinch under their stares. She proudly tossed the newspaper back toward Feng, who caught it. Felicity leaned in beside her, both pretending the lipstick wasn't there as they began reading.</p>
<p>At the very top of the page, the title sprawled across in dramatic ink:</p>
<p>THE NIGHT OF DEATH: VILLAIN'S RECKONING</p>
<p>The Primordial Villain has struck once more. In the span of a single night, over a dozen Greenvale estates were assaulted. Several lords were found dead in their bedrooms or private studies…</p>
<p>Outside, Lyra and Iris had drawn closer without realizing it, running side by side as they tried to listen. But there was nothing to listen to. "Read it aloud…" Iris's voice rose from the right of the carriage.</p>
<p>Feng blinked. She and Felicity had been so absorbed they'd forgotten their audience.</p>
<p>She started over, now reading it for the two armored women. Once done, silence settled.</p>
<p>"A lot has been happening…" Felicity murmured.</p>
<p>"Right?!" Ria burst out. "He's been quiet for two months! I thought - ugh! I can't believe I actually did... I thought maybe he got cold feet or something. But no! He was preparing this! He's back in action!"</p>
<p>She clasped her hands together, practically vibrating.</p>
<p>Meanwhile, a quiet discomfort seeped between the two girls beside each other.</p>
<p>Feng's expression tightened.</p>
<p>Felicity noticed immediately. She reached over and gently pressed her hand against the oriental girl's, giving a small squeeze of reassurance.</p>
<p>The exchange was silent.</p>
<p>But they understood each other perfectly.</p>
<p>Feng didn't just look troubled; she looked ashamed.</p>
<p>She couldn't stop thinking that they should be there with Quinlan, standing beside him when he made these moves. Instead, she had not only decided to leave on her little adventure, but she did so while taking Lyra and Iris with her.</p>
<p>She and Felicity might not have been powerful enough to make a big difference, especially before they went on this trip, but Lyra and Iris? They were core members of the group.</p>
<p>Lyra was an amazing tanker, literally protecting Quinlan and his lovers with her own body. Meanwhile, Iris, as the Child of Reckoning, has evolved to become an AoE pain machine, able to deflect and redirect damage she sustains, among a myriad of other useful things the woman was capable of.</p>
<p>Feng's thoughts spiraled.</p>
<p>'What if he suffers a real loss while we're gone? What if he needed us and we weren't there?'</p>
<p>It made her chest feel tight, thinking she was being greedy, and incredibly ungrateful. This was the man who had changed her entire life for the better, going out of his way to ensure she was as happy as possible. What was she doing in exchange?</p>
<p>The fact that he even gave her one of Rosie's unique flowers so that she could get this beyond amazing class of hers, Tidebreaker, was something she didn't think much about.</p>
<p>She even had the audacity to think it was natural, after all, 'he cares about me.'</p>
<p>But in the months of separation, she realized that she'd been taking him for granted all this time. The more time they spent apart, the more her guilt rose.</p>
<p>Felicity squeezed her hand a little stronger.</p>
<p>Her expression was one of support, telling Feng not to do this to herself, that she had nothing to feel bad about.</p>
<p>But Feng didn't look convinced.</p>
<p>Ria lifted a finger with the air of someone dying to deliver the climax of a story she'd been telling.</p>
<p>"Those are old, by the way," she declared. "Super old, multiple hours old! News came in just as we prepared to leave that didn't even make it into today's papers!"</p>
<p>That elicited four heads to turn toward the woman, secretly wondering how she didn't even stop to ask them questions. But this was exactly what made the woman their most ideal teammate.</p>
<p>Ria was, in every measure, an obnoxiously oblivious woman. Not stupid, never that, but completely uninterested in the oddities happening right beside her. She was traveling with four people who had clear secrets, subtle habits, unspoken rules, and the sort of quiet tension that would have set off alarm bells in anyone with her level of perception.</p>
<p>And yet…</p>
<p>She didn't pry.</p>
<p>Not because she lacked the means. Her detection skills were great even by elite standards, but because she simply didn't want to. Her instincts told her these four were trustworthy. More importantly, she could sense they didn't want their lives dissected.</p>
<p>So Ria made a personal decision: she wouldn't dig.</p>
<p>Part of it was Iris and Lyra. Those two were exactly the kind of professionals she had always wanted to work alongside. Calm under pressure, sharp, reliable, and murderously competent. The kind you could build a true team with. Fighting next to them gave her a sense of belonging she hadn't felt with her previous companions.</p>
<p>And, frankly, the fact that this group was all women only made things smoother. In her last party, simply dealing with the moods, egos, and complications of mixed-gender dynamics made her want to tear her hair out.</p>
<p>But here?</p>
<p>They gelled. They understood each other. They moved like parts of the same machine.</p>
<p>Ria would take that over curiosity any day.</p>
<p>"So!" she said, leaning back with a smug grin. "Wanna hear the real recent stuff?"</p>
<p>Ria cleared her throat, straightened her back, and lifted the nonexistent newspaper as if it hadn't even been printed yet with both hands, preparing to perform a dramatic reading on a theater stage.</p>
<p>When she spoke, her tone shifted, sounding like a professional news anchor.</p>
<p>"Breaking development! King Alexios has declared the Royal Stabilization Mandate effective. The temporary wartime emergency law has been enacted."</p>
<p>Felicity jolted upright so fast her hood slid back an inch.</p>
<p>"Wait! WHAT?!" she squeaked. "That shouldn't be possible! Are we under attack from another nation?! Did Elvardia invade the borders?!"</p>
<p>Feng blinked. "What are you talking about? What law is that?"</p>
<p>Felicity grabbed the edges of her cloak, pulling them tight as she tried to steady herself.</p>
<p>"The Royal Stabilization Mandate… It can only be activated when the ruler believes the nation is entering a state of national emergency. Specifically, when the kingdom is facing a two-frontal war, letting the king basically do whatever he wants not only in the Central Region but the whole country!"</p>
<p>Lyra slowed her run. Iris's helmet turned toward the carriage.</p>
<p>Felicity continued in a rush.</p>
<p>"One front is clearly the Vesper Consortium. They've already given the nobles more trouble than expected. And with Quinlan Elysiar defeating the Fujimori armies, that duchy's military power is already greatly hurt. Now he hit Greenvale next, attacking their nobles outright…"</p>
<p>She exhaled, voice rising.</p>
<p>"So yes, I think the Consortium counts as a threat. But they're NOT big enough to justify the Mandate! There has to be a second force, a big enough disaster to title it as bad if not worse than the Consortium itself!"</p>
<p>"But, wait," Feng tried to make sense of this. "The Vraven Kingdom has been at war with both the Alliance of Elvardia and the Beastman Confederation since long before Alexios ever sat on the throne. Why has he not declared this state of emergency a long time ago?"</p>
<p>"Because the kingdom refuses to treat its neighbours as worthy of the mandate. They are treated as 'we can deal with them.'"</p>
<p>"But they can't deal with the Vesper Consortium?" Feng tilted her head.</p>
<p>"Yes, you're finally getting my point! It makes no sense!"</p>
<p>Ria let out a low whistle.</p>
<p>"You're very familiar with our laws and politics for someone your age…"</p>
<p>Felicity froze.</p>
<p>Her mouth snapped shut.</p>
<p>Way too late.</p>
<p>But Ria didn't question it.</p>
<p>Not even a little.</p>
<p>Instead, her eyes sparkled, cheeks flushing with an excitement that had nothing to do with suspicion.</p>
<p>"A two-frontal disaster, huh," she murmured. "You're right."</p>
<p>Feng raised a brow. "What do you mean?"</p>
<p>Ria lifted the imaginary newspaper again.</p>
<p>Her grin widened.</p>
<p>"King Alexios Valorian has officially declared that the Vraven Kingdom is facing a double-front war."</p>
<p>The group leaned in without meaning to.</p>
<p>"One against the combination of the Vesper Consortium, the Alliance of Elvardia, and the Beastman Confederation. The trio of them is to be treated as enemies who might be working together to bring about the downfall of humanity," Ria said slowly.</p>
<p>"And one…"</p>
<p>She paused.</p>
<p>Held the moment.</p>
<p>Then dropped the final line like a hammer.</p>
<p>"Against the Primordial Villain himself."</p>
<p>"What?!" Even Lyra and Iris couldn't keep it quiet any longer. Ria grinned, finishing her reading with starstruck eyes of a girl whose idol did something beyond shocking.</p>
<p>"'The Primordial Villain is to be classified as a threat equivalent to an entire hostile alliance of nations, a singular force capable of bringing ruin to the Vraven Kingdom on his own,' King Alexios decreed."</p>
