<p>While that was going on, I walked up to Iris. "Why did you do this?"</p>

<p>"I'm confident in at least putting up a good fight, and I had my reasons."</p>

<p>She definitely had a point, I was just worried that she would throw her candidacy away. However, I was not her father nor lover, I wasn't even a close friend of hers, thus I had no right to tell her how to live her life.</p>

He was here to 'restore his honor' for being eliminated from the Phenom Trials a century ago, so being matched up against a true phenom with my illustrious class was the best-case scenario for him. Defeating me would be the best honor restoration he could hope for in this event.</p>
<p>"You already told me your level, are you not going to ask me any questions? It would only be fair to do so." I asked probingly.</p>
<p>"No need." He completely dismissed my offer with a tone full of arrogance. Asking for information would be a sign of weakness, so he didn't go for it.</p>
<p>Probing successful, he is looking down on me.</p>

<p>"Take your positions," Vex instructed, and we did just as so.</p>

<p>"[Stone Sentinel], [Flame Serpent]." I began the match by summoning Harold and Agni. They should provide me with a good frontline.</p>
<p>Kael slammed his ornately decorated mage's staff into the ground as a response to my actions and intoned, "[Ice Age]!" and a deafening crack resonated through the arena as jagged veins of ice shot outward in all directions like an icy eruption.</p>
<p>The stone floor trembled as frost erupted and covered the ground, transforming the once neutral battleground into a frozen wasteland. The temperature plummeted instantly.</p>
<p>Agni hissed violently as the ice surged toward him. His fiery coils blazed brightly for a moment as he was fighting back the cold with all his might, but the frost consumed him like a tidal wave. The once-proud serpent solidified into an icy sculpture.</p>
<p>Harold fared no better. The cold seeped into his massive stone form with his legs getting encased in thick frost within seconds. He exerted a great amount of force in protest, but the ice gripping his limbs held firm. The frost sapped his strength and dulled his movements.</p>
<p>Kael's spell didn't stop just by freezing the floor. He terraformed it as well.</p>
<p>Spires of jagged ice erupted around the arena, forming natural barricades that sliced through visibility and limited movement. Snow started falling in the air, reducing the world around me to a winter storm.</p>
