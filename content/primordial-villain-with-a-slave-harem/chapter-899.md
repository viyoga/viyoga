<p>[Class Earned: Primal Elementalist] – Martial Artist attuned to the Fourfold Avatar Core.</p>
<p>But it wasn’t over.</p>
<p>A windstorm howled within his soul.</p>
<p>His core spun. His blood ignited.</p>
<p>His very essence began to twist.</p>
<p>[DING!]</p>
<p>[CLASS COMPATIBILITY DETECTED.]</p>
<p>[Assimilation Possible.]</p>
<p>Quinlan’s head snapped up, sweat beading down his brow. "What do you mean assimilation...?"</p>
<p>Another ping.</p>
<p>[Eligible Classes:]</p>
<p>Child of Agony [Soul-Tempered Survivor]</p>
<p>Stormblade [Elemental Fencer / Lightning-Affinity Hybrid]</p>
<p>Avatar of the Elements [Balanced Elemental Caster]</p>
<p>Primal Elementalist [Elemental Martial Combatant]</p>

<p>[Fusion Path: UNIQUE CLASS FORMATION]</p>
<p>Warning: This path will assimilate all four classes into one.</p>
<p>The decision is final. Undoing is impossible.</p>
<p>Proceed with Assimilation?</p>
<p>Quinlan’s answer was almost immediate. There was only one class out of those that made him pause, the Child of Agony. It held tremendous sentimental value to him, standing as proof of his shared connection to Iris.</p>
<p>But... Iris had already moved on. She had become more. A Child of Reckoning, now. No longer shackled by agony, but wielding it with purpose.</p>
<p>He let the silence linger, just long enough to honor the weight of what had been. And then, with a breath that was half grief and half resolve, he made his choice.</p>
<p>Why cling to what was when the future demanded more?</p>
<p>He wouldn’t abandon the memory. But he would not be ruled by it either.</p>
<p>The class was precious not because of its power, but because of the person tied to it. And that person was still alive. Still breathing. Probably eating his food, breaking his dishes, and arguing with his women back in his home.</p>
<p>So he would offer it up.</p>
<p>Not as a loss.</p>
<p>But as a tribute.</p>
<p>To protect her. To surpass what they were. To become what he needed to be.</p>
<p>"Yes," he said, voice calm and steady.</p>
<p>"Proceed."</p>
<p>The world cracked around him. His soul realm exploded with light.</p>
<p>The four elemental cores surged together, with fire roaring, water spiraling, earth thundering, and wind shrieking.’</p>
<p>[Assimilation Underway...]</p>
<p>[Stabilizing Primal Matrix...]</p>
<p>[Soul Threads Realigning...]</p>
<p>[New Class Formed.]</p>
<p>✦ UNIQUE CLASS RECEIVED: ELEMENTAL ASCENDANT: HARBRINGER OF AEONS ✦</p>
<p>You are no longer merely a mage.</p>
<p>No longer a martial artist.</p>
<p>No longer a survivor.</p>
<p>You are the elemental storm that walks, the warrior-savant who forges worlds—and sunders them.</p>
<p>Fire is your fury.</p>
<p>Water is your will.</p>
<p>Earth is your resolve.</p>
<p>Wind is your freedom.</p>
<p>You wield the elements not as tools, but as living extensions of your soul, channeled through blade, through spell, through sheer indomitable will.</p>
<p>You are change incarnate.</p>
<p>A living epoch.</p>
<p>The Harbinger of Aeons is not merely a title.</p>
<p>It is a destiny.</p>
<p>A burden.</p>
<p>A promise.</p>
<p>Where you walk, the old world ends...</p>
<p>And the new one begins.</p>
<p>The Harbinger of Eons is the herald of a new age.</p>

<p>✦ Aether-Threaded Body: Passive. Your body is a living conduit of elemental will. You no longer "cast" or "strike"—you express power through pure motion and thought, bypassing traditional forms entirely.</p>

<p>...</p>
<p>Quinlan staggered forward a step out of the sheer shock that assaulted his brain. This new class and all his other upgrades... He’d become incredibly mighty compared to his pre-trial self, even though he only gained a single level.</p>
<p>The light faded from his realm.</p>
<p>The barrage of notifications stilled.</p>
<p>And then, silence.</p>
<p>No more prompts. No more trials where he had to fight for his life.</p>
<p>His body hummed, alive with more than qi, more than mana—essence itself. His veins weren’t just conduits anymore. They were living rivers of will. The air clung to him, not out of reverence, but submission. The elements didn’t wait for his command. They listened for his desire.</p>
<p>He lifted his hand.</p>
<p>Fire danced across his knuckles without effort. Not summoned. Not shaped. Just... there.</p>
<p>A twist of his wrist—whoosh—and water flowed, spiraling in precise arcs, obeying his pulse.</p>
<p>He inhaled.</p>
<p>The wind curled around his spine like a blade at his back, coiling for a strike.</p>
<p>Earth? It was in his stance. In the set of his jaw. In the weight of his presence.</p>
<p>He wasn’t wearing power anymore.</p>
<p>He was power.</p>
<p>A slow, savage smile spread across his face.</p>
<p>"Harbinger of Aeons..." he whispered, tasting the words on his lips.</p>
<p>He let them settle into his bones.</p>
<p>This wasn’t just a name. Not a class. Not even a title.</p>
<p>It was a claim.</p>
<p>A warning.</p>
<p>A promise.</p>
<p>He had fought a god now, broken through death, burned away agony, and risen again and again. He wasn’t a survivor.</p>
<p>He was the storm that ended eras.</p>
<p>The one who decided what came next.</p>
<p>"Yeah," he murmured, voice low and dark with satisfaction. "This’ll do."</p>
<p>He took a step forward, and the ground cracked beneath his heel. Not because he meant to, but because it couldn’t contain him anymore.</p>
<p>There was no fanfare.</p>
<p>No audience.</p>
<p>Just the wind, bowing low, and the fire, purring like a beast reunited with its master.</p>
<p>He turned inward one last time.</p>
<p>The soul realm—the crucible of everything he had endured—lay before him.</p>
<p>The mountains had stopped forming, the rivers now flowed without pause. A field of simple grass was the only other sight. It was a barren landscape for now, but...</p>
<p>Quinlan nodded once. "This is enough, for now..." he murmured.</p>
<p>With a thought, the soul realm dissolved around him in streaks of light.</p>
<p>The real world came rushing back.</p>
