<p>Around him, the rest of us gathered, looming over his prone form like silent executioners. No escape. No chance of reinforcements.</p>
<p>It didn't take long for him to break. Not long at all. In fact, it didn't even take more than three seconds.</p>
<p>The lionkin warrior let out a heavy sigh as his body relaxed in reluctant defeat.</p>
<p>"I choose the easy way…" he muttered with a hoarse voice, thanks to the knowledge that he wasn't long for this world.</p>
<p>Darius crouched, resting his thick forearms on his knees as he gave the lionkin a measured look. "Then start talking. What's going on in Emberfang?"</p>
<p>The captive swallowed hard before replying, "Leohtar Sunfang ordered us to fortify our position… but because of the attacks from the bearkin, tigerkin, wolfkin, and foxkin invasions further north, a lot of our war beasts had to be relocated. We still have some left, but we had to send many away to reinforce the border up north."</p>
<p>Vex followed up with a question of her own. "How many warriors and beasts are left defending the city?"</p>
<p>The lionkin shook his head. "No one knows the details except the higher-ups. Scouts like us aren't given sensitive information since we could be captured and interrogated… but I can tell you that there are still plenty of warriors and beasts left to defend Emberfang."</p>
<p>It made sense. The lionkin weren't so careless as to leave their city defenseless, even with the ongoing war. Just because they were struggling to hold the north border didn't mean they should abandon the other parts of the border. Doing so would set them up for total failure, letting us converge on them from all directions.</p>
<p>It was my turn to ask. "What about a dogkin mother-daughter pair? Slaves. Have you seen any brought into Emberfang in recent months?"</p>
<p>His ears perked at the question before he scoffed with dismissal. "If you're looking for slaves, you're asking the wrong person. Any valuable stock gets sent straight to Lionheart.
