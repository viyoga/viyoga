<p>The barrier flickered again.</p>
<p>Dwarven artillery thundered from the siege lines below. Thick shells slammed into the glowing dome, each impact forcing ripples across its surface like water struck by a hammer. Runes flared, dimmed, then flared again.</p>
<p>Orianna leaned back in her earthen seat, one leg crossed over the other, eyes fixed on the spectacle below.</p>
<p>"You people certainly know how to live," she murmured lightly.</p>
<p>The pink-haired, tatted baddie rested her body after the battle of Greyhaven while watching cannons chew at the barrier as smoke rolled across the plains. For someone like her, sitting comfortably on a hill and observing a siege like a curated event was a whole new experience.</p>
<p>As she was a career criminal, Orianna tended to stay away from conflicts of this scale. And as the Consortium used to work in the shadows until they started their beef with Alastair, she never got to see a spectacle that came even close to what she witnessed on this day.</p>
<p>"I know, right?!" Ria gasped while leaning forward so fast she nearly slipped off her chair.</p>
<p>The blonde assassin's eyes sparkled as she stared at the battlefield, then flicked toward Quinlan. Her ears burned. "This is crazy… So crazy…" she murmured, more so to herself.</p>
<p>Iris barely glanced up, observing with a nonchalant expression. "You get used to it. This guy is just as arrogant as he is resourceful."</p>
<p>Ria snapped her head around. "Hah? Get used to THIS?! Never!" She pointed accusingly at her friend. "And, by the way, I can't believe you had an ally like him all along, and we spent months walking everywhere like peasants. We slept in dirty tents, and I did my business in… Anyhow!! I'm mad at you, Iris Ravenclaw! You're a bad woman. I won't call you my friend for at least ten minutes. Hmph!"</p>
<p>"Thank the Goddess. Finally, some peace and quiet."</p>
<p>Ria's eyes nearly popped out of their sockets. "Hey!!"</p>
<p>"I am not a transportation vessel, by the way," Quinlan said calmly.</p>
<p>Ria instantly yelped. "I-I didn't mean it like that!"</p>
<p>"Is that so?" Quinlan mused playfully.</p>
<p>The assassin shrank when she realized her words might be interpreted in a way she didn't mean them, making the girl twist in her seat. Feng and Felicity exchanged a look, then smirked in unison. The two young ladies were enjoying the misery this hopeless fangirl was putting herself into by interacting with Quinlan, who was clearly messing with her, enjoying the situation.</p>
<p>"I'm sorry," Ria blurted, cheeks red. "I really didn't mean to offend you."</p>
<p>Quinlan stood.</p>
<p>Stone shifted softly beneath his boots as he turned toward her, and a small smile tugged at his lips. "Relax. You don't have to walk around eggshells near me. I was just messing with you."</p>
<p>Ria's pout deepened. So did the crimson color in her face.</p>
<p>Blossom looked up at him with her tail wagging behind her butt. She was enjoying listening to the exchange between the ladies and Quinlan while watching the loud cannon fire beneath. Just as Quinlan predicted, the dogkin girl with far too acute senses got used to the loudness of dwarven cannon fire, letting her function normally. However, she was still uncomfortable around it, so much so that she had to fight back the urge to sabotage the horrible machines. 'Not yet, Blossom. You have to wait until Master decides to betray them or you'll ruin his genius plans!!' she reminded herself.</p>
<p>"Master!! Are you going somewhere?" Blossom asked, already half rising from her chair. Her tail swayed behind her without restraint. "Can Blossom come with?"</p>
<p>"Just a quick trip," Quinlan replied. "I'm missing someone. She should be here with us."</p>
<p>Blossom blinked. Her head tilted.</p>
<p>"So…?"</p>
<p>Quinlan smiled and parted his arms. "Sure. Come, Blossom."</p>
<p>That was all the permission she needed.</p>
<p>She sprang from her seat and straight into his waiting arms, light despite the leather and steel. Quinlan caught her easily. Her body pressed close without hesitation, and she giggled cutely, happily. "Hehe!"</p>
<p>The black leather she favored left little to the imagination. The gear hugged her hips and chest tightly, straps crossing skin more than they covered it. Beastkin favoring loose armor that let them feel more natural and thus effective was a hill Blossom was willing to die on. Quinlan gave up, deciding that instead of fighting her on the topic, he'd just enjoy the heavenly sight of his gorgeous lover in her BDSM-style gear.</p>
<p>Warmth bled through the contact. Her tail whipped back and forth as she buried her face against him, nuzzling without restraint. "Master…" she murmured, satisfied.</p>
<p>Synchra shifted.</p>
<p>The armor along Quinlan's chest flowed like liquid metal, parting just enough to expose skin where Blossom's head rested. A faint glow traced the edge of the transformation before settling.</p>
<p>Blossom noticed at once and began purring while nestling her head even closer.</p>
<p>"Thank you, Miss Synchra! You're amazing!"</p>
<p>A small lick of red flame flickered across the exposed metal in response, brief and precise.</p>
<p>Quinlan exhaled through his nose, amused. He could never get enough of his dog girl.</p>
<p>He looked up. "Liora. If you would."</p>
<p>The priestess stiffened when her name was called. She glanced between him and Blossom, then the realization set in. Her posture straightened. She nodded once and rose from her seat, hands already gathering light as she stepped closer.</p>
<p>Quinlan opened a hand. "[Warp Gate]."</p>
<p>Space folded inward, edges curling like peeled bark. A dark doorway split the air beside him, its surface reflecting nothing of the battlefield. "I'll be back before it falls," he said, already stepping forward.</p>
<p>Behind them, the others stayed seated, eyes returning to the barrier below as another volley of shells hammered into it.</p>
<p>Just before Quinlan crossed the threshold, voices rose.</p>
<p>Ayame leaned back in her chair with her arms folded. Anyone who knew the woman knew that the way her eyes sparkled and how her expression turned smug meant nothing good. "By the way, Iris. Did you also handle your business in the bushes?"</p>
<p>Ria yelped. "Also?! I didn't say that I did!"</p>
