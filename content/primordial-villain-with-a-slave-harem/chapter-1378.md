<p>She blinked. "What do you-"</p>
<p>He didn't let her finish.</p>
<p>Quinlan grabbed all of them with a single burst of force and hurled them forward.</p>
<p>Not gently.</p>
<p>Not one bit.</p>
<p>They became screaming projectiles.</p>
<p>The sky tore past them as the ground rushed up at horrifying speed, wind ripping at cloaks and hair. Raika's eyes went wide for half a second.</p>
<p>Then she laughed.</p>
<p>"That's more like it!" she shouted, slamming her fists together mid-air, joints popping as her body aligned instinctively for impact. Her grin stretched sharply and eagerly. "You're still an arrogant bastard, but we speak the same language!"</p>
<p>Behind her, three other figures twisted in the air with terrifying control.</p>
<p>Despite plummeting at extreme velocity, Vex, Orianna, and Black Fang somehow managed to rotate their bodies enough to keep their eyes locked on Quinlan.</p>
<p>Their expressions were no longer merely offended.</p>
<p>They were lethal.</p>
<p>Quinlan winced.</p>
<p>"Use that hatred to fuel your attacks!" he shouted after them.</p>
<p>The glares intensified.</p>
<p>"Hubby!" Vex shrieked. "No kissing me for a week!"</p>
<p>She crossed her arms beneath her chest in a dramatic pout while still hurtling toward the battlefield like a thrown siege weapon, cheeks puffed.</p>
<p>"A whole week! And I won't change my mind, no matter how you beg or say flowery words! I am a woman who can put her feet down when I am forced to!" she added, just to be sure he understood the gravity of his situation.</p>
<p>"You will fold within minutes, my beloved Hexwitch…" Quinlan chuckled to himself with a satisfied expression, watching as they vanished downward, one grinning demon and three very angry women about to hit the city like divine retribution.</p>
<p>The laughter faded with the departure of the ladies.</p>
<p>The wind thinned. The noise of battle below became distant, muffled by height and focus. Quinlan hovered alone now, the sky empty around him.</p>
<p>His posture changed.</p>
<p>His posture changed.</p>
<p>The looseness bled out of him, inch by inch. Shoulders settled. Spine straightened. Breath slowed until it barely moved his chest. The playful tilt of his mouth vanished, leaving nothing behind it.</p>
<p>"Synchra. Soul Reaper."</p>
<p>The air answered.</p>
<p>Red light bled through the seams of his armor. Plates shifted against one another with a muted grind as internal defensive measures woke. From the pocket ring on his finger, metal screamed.</p>
<p>A dark saber tore free, spinning once before snapping into his grasp. Blue flame crawled along its edge, dense and steady.</p>
<p>Quinlan closed his fingers around the hilt.</p>
<p>The last trace of warmth left his eyes.</p>
<p>Below him, thousands fought and died. To him, it resolved into lines, clusters, vectors. Where undead clogged streets. Where humans retreated in knots. Where pressure points waited to collapse.</p>
<p>He was no longer a man watching a battlefield.</p>
<p>He was a solution moving toward a problem.</p>
<p>And the Primordial Villain began his work.</p>
<p>Quinlan's gaze moved with rapid speed, analyzing what he saw at a terrifying pace. He picked out seams in the chaos. A flank where defenders pushed one step too far forward, focused on the undead press. A junction where three streets converged and command signals bottlenecked. A rooftop cluster sheltering healers with no rear coverage. A reserve force waiting just long enough to be late.</p>
<p>Opportunistic angles.</p>
<p>Backs turned. Lines stretched thin.</p>
<p>His mind worked faster than sight. The primordial brain residing within his skull unfolded the field into layers. Movement vectors. Reaction delays. Panic thresholds. He saw not what was happening, but what would happen a few breaths from now.</p>
<p>Then, he cast.</p>
<p>"[Awaken]."</p>
<p>The words left him quietly.</p>
<p>The Soul Reaper saber answered.</p>
<p>Blue flame along its edge intensified, growing brighter, tighter, drawn inward until it looked less like fire and more like compressed light. The weapon hummed in his grip, eager to release the souls contained within its Eternal Damnation storage.</p>
<p>And then…</p>
<p>Nothing.</p>
<p>No release. No explosion of soul soldiers outward.</p>
<p>The spell was live, but restrained.</p>
<p>Quinlan held it there, forcing the power to wait. The pressure climbed, coiling through his arm, across his shoulders, down his spine. His armor groaned softly under the strain.</p>
<p>He closed his eyes, yet the battlefield remained vivid in his mind.</p>
<p>Every point he had marked burned into his awareness, each one anchored by intent. He did not need to see them anymore. They were already his.</p>
<p>Quinlan swung.</p>
<p>It was a simple motion. One clean arc through empty air.</p>
<p>The flame exploded.</p>
<p>The saber's edge flared white-blue, and from it burst a cascade of light. More than a hundred sparks tore free, each one a tiny star shedding a thin tail of fire as it screamed downward.</p>
<p>From the walls below, defenders looked up.</p>
<p>For a heartbeat, some forgot to breathe. Such was the mesmerizing visage they saw.</p>
<p>The sky was falling apart, raining blue fire. It was beautiful in a distant, terrible way. A starfall aimed directly at the city.</p>
<p>Then the sparks exploded and began expanding rapidly, unfolding into forms. Flame pulled inward, condensing, stretching, hardening. Limbs shaped themselves out of burning light. Armor followed, seamless and cruel. Faces formed last, cold and ready to do what was ordered of them.</p>
<p>Quinlan opened his eyes.</p>
<p>"Kill them all."</p>
<p>The order was given by a deep voice that they all heard. It held zero mercy.</p>
<p>Blue-skinned warriors tore out of the fire.</p>
<p>They rose behind shield lines. They emerged from alley mouths. They solidified atop rooftops and dropped down into the midst of command units. Every place Quinlan had marked now had an Elite Soul standing there.</p>
<p>The humans who had stared upward in awe found themselves trapped.</p>
<p>Undead pressed from the front, relentless and uncaring.</p>
<p>And behind them…</p>
<p>These things.</p>
<p>The blue warriors raised their weapons in unison.</p>
<p>High above, Quinlan steadied himself.</p>
<p>The saber dimmed to a controlled burn at his side.</p>
<p>"[Necromantic Codex]," he called.</p>
<p>A book slid into existence, hovering in the air in front of him. Quinlan opened it with his will alone.</p>
<p>Pages turned on their own, stopping where he desired. Elite Souls: 180</p>
<p>Most of them had been added over the past few days. Quinlan has been incredibly productive in recent times, making use of the chaotic situation that enveloped the Vraven Kingdom, in large part due to his machinations.</p>
<p>Thousands had died by his hand or at his order.</p>
<p>His eyes moved to the top of the list.</p>
<p>Scar. Rank 5.</p>
<p>The only one.</p>
<p>Everything below her sat at Rank 4, or those he didn't manage to upgrade yet, due to just having finished their slaying, were below at Rank 1.</p>
<p>He focused on her entry, and the Codex responded, unfolding details. Her stat growth, while not able to be quantified as easily as it was for living beings, as Scar did not have a status window, she gained nearly equivalent to ten levels of stats. More important than that, her class had returned.</p>
<p>The real thing.</p>
<p>Rank 5 did that, making it the biggest boost in power among all the rank-ups thus far. It did not just strengthen: it restored. Scar was no longer an enhanced weapon. She was herself again, sharpened and elevated.</p>
<p>Quinlan closed that page and turned to another.</p>
<p>The restriction glared back at him, unchanged.</p>
<p>To ascend from Rank 4 to Rank 5, only Lesser Souls of level fifty or above were valid fuel. Anything weaker was rejected outright. Burned without effect.</p>
<p>That was the bottleneck. That was why Scar stood alone.</p>
<p>High-level enemies were rare. High-level enemies he could kill were even rarer.</p>
<p>Until Greyhaven.</p>
