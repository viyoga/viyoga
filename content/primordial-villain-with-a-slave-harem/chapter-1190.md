<p>"The First Elf," Feng finished for her, smug as ever. "Exactly that one."</p>
<p>Felicity whipped her head toward Quinlan at long last. Her expression was truly a magical sight. Quinlan wished he had a camera with him. She looked utterly dumbfounded.</p>
<p>He scratched the back of his neck and gave a small, awkward smile.</p>
<p>Rosie, sitting on top of him, patted his head with joy. "Daddy is amazing!"</p>
<p>Luminara being his doting mother, seemed to be the final straw. Felicity’s eyes rolled back, and she slumped over for the second time that day.</p>
<p>Feng looked at Quinlan with the brightest of expressions, laughing so hard she had to wipe tears from her eyes.</p>
<p>"I wanted to overload her with these facts for such a long time! It was so worth the wait."</p>
<p>"And they call me the Devil?" Quinlan shook his head.</p>
<p>"Daddy is a bad influence on children," Rosie decreed while puffing her cheeks. "Daddy should only spend time with Rosie!"</p>
<p>Quinlan let out a quiet chuckle. He had learned to recognize that tone — the mix of possessiveness and jealousy that made his leafy daughter such a handful. She was heavily reminding him of how Vex acted more and more. Quinlan didn’t know what would happen to him if the two teamed up.</p>
<p>Rosie wrapped her tiny arms around his neck, glaring at Feng and the fainted princess as if they were competition. "Hmph..."</p>
<p>He reached up and gave her head a light pat. "You’re already with me all the time. Isn’t that enough?"</p>
<p>"No!!" she muttered with her chin digging into his shoulder. "It’ll never be enough!"</p>
<p>Before he could reply, Felicity stirred with a small groan. Her eyes blinked open, unfocused at first, then snapped toward Feng. Seeing the smug expression plastered across the girl’s face made her lips twitch.</p>
<p>"You’re having too much fun at my expense!" she scoffed, voice full of irritation.</p>
<p>Feng tilted her head, utterly unbothered. "Bullying such an innocent and expressive girl like you feels like it’s my duty as the older and more mature woman. Think of it as practice for adult life."</p>
<p>Quinlan raised an eyebrow when he saw Feng giving him a nasty side-eye glance as she muttered under her breath, "Someone else once gave me similar lessons not long ago..."</p>
<p>Then, her grin sharpened, mischief returning to full force. She straightened her posture and looked at the princess again.</p>
<p>Felicity instantly picked up on it. Her body tensed as she raised her arms defensively. "You... you better not have more of those shocking stories!"</p>
<p>Feng’s lips stretched into a wolfish grin.</p>
<p>&lt;Oh, but I do... Princess~&gt;</p>
<p>Felicity froze. The words hadn’t come from Feng’s mouth; they had echoed inside her mind.</p>
<p>Her eyes darted around, alarmed. "What?! Where did that- What was that voice?!"</p>
<p>&lt;I’m in your head~&gt; Feng’s voice purred in her thoughts again, followed by a faint giggle.</p>
<p>"WHAT?!" Felicity yelped out loud, clutching her head.</p>
<p>Feng finally broke into laughter. &lt;Relax, relax! It’s one of Quin’s spells. Since you’re his slave now, you can use it too. It’s a mental link connecting all his [Subjugated] people, think something at me instead of saying it, and I’ll hear you.&gt;</p>
<p>Felicity hesitated but gave it a try, closing her eyes for a second. &lt;Can... you hear me?&gt;</p>
<p>&lt;Yup!&gt; Feng replied instantly, cheerful and loud enough to make the princess flinch.</p>
<p>&lt;Don’t yell! Are you trying to give me a headache?!&gt; Felicity thought back, startled by how natural it felt.</p>
<p>Feng chuckled aloud. "See? Easy!"</p>
<p>Then that mischievous gleam returned to her eyes. "Watch this." She focused briefly, reaching through the shared mental channel.</p>
<p>A dull, monotone voice responded, overlapping with another equally lifeless one.</p>
<p>&lt;Yes... might I know who I am speaking with?&gt;</p>
<p>Feng smirked. &lt;Doesn’t matter. Introduce yourselves.&gt;</p>
<p>A long sigh passed through the link, followed by two reluctant replies. They knew better than to argue. Despite Quinlan having many slaves, and them speaking to one right now, both of them understood that they were at the bottom of his slave hierarchy.</p>
<p>The rock bottom.</p>
<p>From the most privileged of women, born into a Duke’s family to...</p>
<p>Being treated less than dumb and brutish construction slaves.</p>
<p>&lt;I’m Vivienne Greenvale.&gt;</p>
<p>&lt;Amara Greenvale.&gt;</p>
<p>Felicity went completely still. Her jaw dropped open just before her mind went blank once again.</p>
<p>Then, through the shared mental link, her thoughts rang out in one panicked scream:</p>
<p>&lt;The Greenvale twins?!!!&gt;</p>
<p>Her body went limp for the third time that evening, collapsing backward like a doll with its strings cut.</p>
<p>Felicity lay still for a few moments, eyes wide, breathing unevenly. Then she sat up abruptly and began fanning herself with trembling hands.</p>
<p>"I... I need a minute... Or water. Or both."</p>
<p>Her skin was pale, and her pupils dilated. She looked like someone who’d just witnessed a ghost.</p>
<p>Feng, still smirking, offered a canteen. "You’re holding up better than I thought, honestly."</p>
<p>Felicity grabbed it and took several desperate gulps before setting it aside. Her gaze flickered, distant. Then, as if curiosity outweighed all else, she hesitated and reached for the link again.</p>
<p>&lt;... Greenvale twins?&gt; she thought cautiously. Her mental tone was shaky. &lt;What is going on?&gt;</p>
<p>A pause came. Then two simultaneous, uneasy replies.</p>
<p>&lt;... It’s really her.&gt;</p>
<p>&lt;He subjugated the princess.&gt;</p>
<p>Their thoughts carried disbelief, fear, and an undercurrent of awe.</p>
<p>&lt;There’s truly... no escaping him,&gt; Amara whispered mentally. &lt;Even royalty bends before his will now.&gt;</p>
<p>&lt;Quiet!&gt; Vivienne hissed. &lt;He can hear us anytime he wishes.&gt;</p>
<p>The thought made both of them fall silent immediately.</p>
<p>Felicity frowned and repeated, firmer this time, &lt;Answer my question. What happened to you two?&gt;</p>
<p>The twins exchanged a mental sigh.</p>
<p>&lt;We were defeated and kidnapped,&gt; Vivienne admitted. &lt;Captured by Lord Quinlan on our way back from the auction where we met, princess.&gt;</p>
<p>&lt;Defeated is... putting it lightly,&gt; Amara added bitterly. &lt;We were torn apart and rebuilt under his command.&gt;</p>
<p>Before Felicity could respond, another voice cut through the link. This one was deep, smooth, and commanding.</p>
<p>&lt;Explain yourselves properly.&gt;</p>
<p>The moment Quinlan’s voice entered their minds, both twins jolted physically.</p>
<p>&lt;Y-Yes, my Lord! I’m sorry, we did not mean to taint your image...&gt; Vivienne stammered.</p>
<p>&lt;After Lord Quinlan learned of our crimes, namely the raping and torturing of numerous innocents, we...&gt; She faltered, with her tone trembling.</p>
<p>&lt;The ones we kidnapped using our power as nobles...&gt; Amara continued for her, voice hollow, &lt;and what we did to them... he judged us for it. And punished us accordingly.&gt;</p>
<p>The connection rippled with tension.</p>
<p>&lt;You two scum did what to MY people?!&gt; Felicity’s voice burst in pure outrage. Her expression shifted from shock to righteous fury.</p>
<p>&lt;...We have no words,&gt; came Vivienne’s reserved thought.</p>
<p>Felicity gritted her teeth so much so that they nearly cracked. &lt;I can’t bear hearing your disgusting voices any longer. Get out of my head!&gt; She cut the link off immediately. Silence returned to her mind.</p>
<p>She sat there for a while, staring at the floor, trying to steady her breathing.</p>
<p>Then, slowly, she looked up at Quinlan. Her tone was calm now, too calm for how she had been ever since Feng’s storytime began. "Lord Quinlan, you did what my family failed to do, despite it being our sworn duty. I’m forever in your debt."</p>
<p>Quinlan’s lips curled into a smile, and he winked at the girl.</p>
<p>"My pleasure."</p>
<p>Felicity met his eyes, realizing with full clarity that beneath that calm exterior stood a man capable of both mercy and monstrous judgment - and that she had chosen, willingly, to stand beside him.</p>
<p>She had zero regrets.</p>
