<p>Boots hammered stone in a loose, breaking rhythm.</p>
<p>No formation held. Shields banged into backs. Someone tripped and went down hard. No one stopped for him. Hands and feet shoved him aside without looking. Bodies collided as the street narrowed and widened again between leaning buildings.</p>
<p>Breath tore out of lungs in sharp bursts. Throats burned. Armor straps cut into the shoulders with every step.</p>
<p>"Move!" someone shouted with a raw voice. "Move, move!"</p>
<p>"Hurry, or we're done for!"</p>
<p>A glance over a shoulder.</p>
<p>The sky above the rooftops held a dark shape that closed the distance without effort. Unlike them, it did not have to weave through obstacles. Rooflines fell away beneath it. Smoke parted around it and the red flames its obsidian armor emitted.</p>
<p>A javelin flew.</p>
<p>It arced high, wobbled, then fell short and clattered uselessly across tiles.</p>
<p>Someone laughed, thin and hysterical. "Haha! When does this dream end?! Someone slap me awake!"</p>
<p>"Mommy…!" Someone else sobbed without slowing.</p>
<p>They ran harder.</p>
<p>The street opened into a small square littered with debris and broken carts. A bottleneck. Too wide to hide. Too open to scatter.</p>
<p>The sound came first.</p>
<p>Air compressed with a deep, crushing pressure that shoved at their backs. Shadows snapped tight beneath their feet.</p>
<p>The figure dropped down on them like a falling meteor, and instantly, it exploded.</p>
<p>Stone shattered on impact. The ground caved inward in a violent ring. Men were lifted off their feet as if yanked by invisible hooks. Armor folded. Bodies collided midair. Limbs bent where they should not.</p>
<p>The force rolled outward through the square, ripping through the cluster in a single breath. Those closest vanished into fragments of plate and flesh. Those farther out slammed into walls hard enough to leave stains where they hit.</p>
<p>Dust filled mouths and eyes.</p>
<p>The square fell silent.</p>
<p>When it cleared, nothing moved. Broken weapons lay half-buried in rubble. Shields rested at angles that made no sense. What had been a unit moments ago was reduced to scattered remains pressed into the ground and walls.</p>
<p>…</p>
<p>Quinlan straightened his spine while standing in the crater of his own creation. "Thanks, Synchra," he muttered, always grateful for the protection of his trusted armor. She was doing incredible work. Synchra granted Quinlan plainly unfair levels of tankiness, which in turn made him able to approach combat from a different angle. Before, most of these combatants would've been strong enough to one-shot kill him if their strike connected. But that was no longer the case.</p>
<p>Synchra's red flames rose in response to the praise she received, joyous.</p>
<p>It felt great to be noticed for the hard work she was putting in.</p>
<p>Quinlan's gaze moved once, left to right, counting shapes that twitched or dragged themselves an inch at a time. His hand lifted.</p>
<p>Compressed water snapped into existence at his fingertips, each shot a tight cylinder that bent the air around it. He flicked his wrist.</p>
<p>*Thud. Thud. Thud.*</p>
<p>The bullets punched through helms and throats with clean, short sounds. One man tried to crawl. The next shot folded him flat against the stones. Another raised an arm. Water took it off at the elbow and kept going.</p>
<p>Quinlan tilted his head to the side, watching the futile resistance of his targets. At the same time, he listened to the symphony of alert notifications, his favorite music these days, ringing in his mind.</p>
<p>[Ding!]</p>
<p>[You've reached Level 48.]</p>
<p>He smiled as the last shots landed and the mass execution was completed.</p>
<p>"Amazing," he murmured.</p>
<p>He glanced at the numbers without slowing, eyes tracking the rise. In a single day, the gain eclipsed entire campaigns. Even the lionkin genocide he conducted in Lionheart with the temporary tenfold boost had not climbed this fast. His growing power level, bolstered defenses, and increased proficiency with his magic had changed what was possible. Now, unlike how it was back in Lionheart, his spells were strong enough to kill high-rank targets. Furthermore, he was attacking whole settlements that were full of them, using the blood, time, and money of his newfound allies to his full advantage. It was Elvardia and the Covenant who spent numerous years preparing for this invasion.</p>
<p>It was Elvardia and the Covenant who spent immense resources to make this invasion possible.</p>
<p>It was Elvardia and the Covenant who were now bleeding in the fields.</p>
<p>But it was Quinlan who was reaping the biggest benefits.</p>
<p>Thanks to the hard work of his allies, level fifty was no longer a distant goal to chase. It hovered close, almost close enough to touch.</p>
<p>Quinlan rose out of the crater, lifting high into the air.</p>
<p>One look was enough. The same pattern.</p>
<p>In Greyhaven, it had been the same. The first crush erased the center. Command collapsed. Cohesion died. What remained were fragments. Small knots of soldiers clinging to alleys, doorways, and courtyards. Scattered prey instead of an army.</p>
<p>His eyes traced movement along rooftops and side streets. No dense clusters. No clean follow-ups.</p>
<p>It was no longer worth Quinlan's time. His more than 200 Elite Souls would do the hard work for him.</p>
<p>"Time for plan B."</p>
<p>He flicked two fingers.</p>
<p>Space peeled open on the roof of a tall building overlooking the square. The tear stabilized into a clean-edged portal, light folding inward around it.</p>
<p>A nun stepped through.</p>
<p>She wore round glasses that caught the glow of the remnants of his explosion, and freckles dusted across her cheeks. Mira took two confident steps forward, hands clasped behind her back as she spoke.</p>
<p>"Well then," she said cheerfully, "looks like it's time for me to shine again, hmm, Mister Villain? The terror of the kingdom needs the help of this humble little nun-"</p>
<p>Her foot met empty air.</p>
<p>She blinked once.</p>
<p>Then gravity remembered her.</p>
<p>"CATCH ME, YOU MOTHERFU-"</p>
<p>She dropped.</p>
<p>Arms windmilled. Legs kicked at nothing. Her body twisted, fighting an argument it could not win. The roof slid past above her. The ground rushed up, filling her vision, stone details snapping into sharp focus far too fast.</p>
<p>Her face was an inch from impact.</p>
<p>Wind wrapped around her torso.</p>
<p>The fall stopped.</p>
<p>Momentum vanished as if cut clean. Mira was then jerked upward and slammed gently into solid arms. Quinlan caught her against his chest, one hand braced at her back, the other under her knees.</p>
<p>She stared at him with her glasses crooked and breath coming fast.</p>
<p>"I hate you!" she whined, gripping the front of his armor. "Stop having fun at my expense!"</p>
<p>"Stop worshipping a hypocrite, and I'll consider it."</p>
<p>Mira's face flushed with righteous outrage.</p>
<p>She twisted in his arms just enough to draw back a fist. "You shameless, heretical menace! You're but a villain speaking ill of the Maker! Do you have any idea how much divine retribution you're lining up for yourself?! I'm shocked she didn't smite you down already!"</p>
<p>Then Mira gasped. "Ah! Wait! Maybe I'm the divine retribution she sent to get you in line! Take this!"</p>
<p>She went for a slap.</p>
<p>*Thunk.*</p>
<p>The sound was dull. Solid.</p>
<p>A sharp jolt ran up her arm.</p>
<p>"Ouchhhh!"</p>
<p>Mira recoiled instantly, clutching her hand to her chest, hopping once in midair despite still being held. Her glasses slid further down her nose.</p>
<p>"Ow ow ow ow! You hurt me… Such a brutal sadist…" she whimpered, blinking at him rapidly with accusatory eyes while massaging her hand.</p>
<p>"Women…" Quinlan shook his head with a loud sigh escaping his lips.</p>
<p>"What?!" "A woman and a blind believer… The most delusional combination possible."</p>
<p>"I'm not delusional but aware! You're the one in the wrong!" Mira cried while massaging her poor hand.</p>
<p>"You know, I can't help but notice that the women of Thalorind, mostly you, like to call me a motherfucker. What gives? I mean, I might've sucked on the boobies of my mothers so strong that they orgasmed a little, but-"</p>
<p>"What's wrong with you?" Mira deadpanned.</p>
<p>Quinlan smirked, dismissing her whole existence. "Nothing, of course. I'm perfectly healthy in both body and mind. So, Miss Delulu with the Fist of Divine Retribution, shall we get started?"</p>
<p>Mira puffed her cheeks with lips pressed into a thin line. She glared at the smooth obsidian curve of his helmet.</p>
<p>"Wipe that smug smirk off your face," she muttered.</p>
<p>"What do you mean? You can't see my face."</p>
<p>"I can tell."</p>
<p>There was no pause. No protest.</p>
<p>"Is that so?"</p>
<p>He did not deny it.</p>
