<p>Ayame smirked at her. "Because you stopped before finishing the sentence." She turned slowly as her horrible grin deepened across her face and her eyes locked onto Iris. It was sharp. Deliberate. Infuratingly so. "So, dearest Iris. Explain the logistics of your waste management skills to me."</p>
<p>Iris's temperature dropped instantly. Her spine straightened. The air around her felt thinner. "I will kill you."</p>
<p>"Oh" Ayame giggled softly, utterly unfazed. "Terrifying"</p>
<p>Ria sputtered, trying to interject, when Iris snapped back, voice flat and venomous. "Shut your trap, you damned midget. Are we certain you're truly Miss Black Fang's and Kaede's sister? I wouldn't be shocked to learn that you're Thorga's long-lost sibling instead. You were probably also dropped on your head as a baby, by the way."</p>
<p>Then, just for good measure, the raven-haired, slightly unhinged woman with a foul mouth - at least when it came to interacting with the world's most annoying woman - spat, "You dumb slut."</p>
<p>The elves stationed along the hill, who were acutely listening in, making use of their station to glean some information about this truly strange group, froze.</p>
<p>A heartbeat passed.</p>
<p>Then several of them had to clamp their hands over their mouths. Shoulders shook. Eyes watered. Everyone knew who Thorga was; she was a known dwarven soldier who had a long and prestigious career in the army of Elvardia.</p>
<p>Ayame's grin vanished as if it never existed.</p>
<p>Her posture changed, subtle but absolute. Her hand drifted toward her blade.</p>
<p>"So you have chosen death."</p>
<p>Steel whispered as the duo stood and weapons began to move.</p>
<p>The sound followed Quinlan as he stepped through the portal.</p>
<p>Space closed behind him, cutting off laughter, curses, and the rising tension on the hill. He didn't look back. If the women took it too far, the others would intervene.</p>
<p>Hopefully.</p>
<p>The siege vanished. The noise fell away.</p>
<p>Only the three of them remained.</p>
<p>Blossom lifted her head, looking at him. "Master," she began with complete sincerity, "Blossom thinks Ayame and Iris are great friends!"</p>
<p>Quinlan's brow lifted. His mouth curved into something wry. "You think so?"</p>
<p>Liora stood a step behind them, hands folded at her waist. Her face stayed calm, almost flat, though her eyes lingered on Blossom for a moment longer than necessary. She said nothing.</p>
<p>"Yes!" Blossom nodded so hard her ears bounced. "Blossom can tell. Beneath all the thorns, there are two roses that love each other very much. Enough to die for each other!"</p>
<p>Quinlan paused.</p>
<p>Then he looked down at her. "Would you look at that. My sexy dogkin babe is becoming a poet. Who would've guessed?"</p>
<p>"Hehe!" Blossom puffed up proudly. "Blossom has been learning hard words with her mother, Natalie! Blossom doesn't want to look like a dumb pet."</p>
<p>Quinlan's hand slid up her back, steady and warm. "Hey. You were never anything like that. You're an amazing girl, even if your vocabulary isn't that of a poet."</p>
<p>Her smile softened.</p>
<p>She leaned in, pressing a gentle kiss to his cheek while her tail slowed, brushing against his leg. "Blossom loves Master," she decreed quietly. "But Blossom will try her best to become a wife who can proudly stand next to him. Now that Master is pursuing ladies like Black Fang… Blossom feels a little small."</p>
<p>Quinlan turned his head and kissed her in return. He rested his forehead against hers. "You have no reason to feel that way. But I can see you've already made up your mind."</p>
<p>His thumb brushed beneath her ear. "So instead of deterring you, I'll cheer you on."</p>
<p>Blossom didn't answer.</p>
<p>She just purred, long and deep, and curled closer in his arms.</p>
<p>"Inadequate," Liora suddenly spoke up.</p>
<p>"Hmm?" Quinlan and Blossom both asked in sync, turning toward the woman. The priestess smiled as she looked at Blossom and explained, "Miss Blossom, you said you felt small after comparing yourself to Lady Black Fang. I think using the word 'inadequate' instead of 'small' makes you seem a lot more like a prim and proper lady."</p>
<p>Blossom's ears twitched.</p>
<p>"Inadequ… what was it again?" she murmured to herself, lips moving as she rolled the word around. Her posture straightened little by little. Her eyes brightened. "Inadequate."</p>
<p>She nodded to herself, tail giving a pleased swish. "Yes!" She turned toward Liora and dipped her head politely. "Thank you for teaching Blossom a hard word."</p>
<p>Then she hesitated, glancing up again. "But please don't call Blossom 'Miss.' Blossom is just Blossom to her friends."</p>
<p>Liora's lips curved gently. "Is that so? You are welcome, Blossom."</p>
<p>The space between them felt easy after that. Quiet in a way that did not ask for silence. Quinlan was truly happy to witness this adorable interaction.</p>
<p>They stopped before a wooden door. Quinlan shifted Blossom in his arms and raised a hand.</p>
<p>Knock. Knock.</p>
<p>"Coming!" a female voice called from inside.</p>
<p>The door opened.</p>
<p>Kaelira stood there in loose pajamas patterned with tiny crossed hammers. The fabric hung comfortably, sleeves pushed up to her elbows. She thought the design looked tough; she loved the attire that was gifted to her by the team she led.</p>
<p>However, everyone else who had seen them agreed they were painfully cute. This was never mentioned aloud because the tomboy elf with a tantalizing six-pack would refuse to wear this piece of fabric if she ever found out, and literally no one wanted that to happen.</p>
<p>Her eyes went wide.</p>
<p>She stared at Quinlan as if he had stepped out of a forge fire.</p>
<p>"My lord, I didn't expect you… Aren't you fighting right now?" she whispered before stopping herself short when she realized the change in Quinlan's eyes. She understood that he wasn't happy with something, and she also understood what it was that displeased him.</p>
<p>Her shoulders drew in. Her hands clenched at her sides. She took a breath, then another, clearly wrestling with the words. "Quinlan," she corrected, the name stiff on her tongue. "My p-partner. Not just in the smithy b-but… life as well."</p>
<p>The faint color along her cheeks deepened as she looked anywhere but his eyes.</p>
<p>Quinlan's eyes, on the other hand, began to dance, once again happy. He positioned Blossom so she only occupied one arm and stepped closer, lifting a hand. His fingers slid into her lush, blue-tinged hair and began stroking it slowly, from crown to shoulder.</p>
<p>"Good girl," he purred.</p>
