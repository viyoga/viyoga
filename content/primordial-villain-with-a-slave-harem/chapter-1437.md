<p>Thyra smiled softly and shook her head. "I was much more interested in becoming a strong fighter than anything else. However, I did have a few families of my own over the years, so I can provide you with all the information you need on how proper relationships should work."</p>
<p>"Okay!" both teens shouted together with big smiles on their faces, then sat down expectantly.</p>
<p>Thyra raised an eyebrow and looked at their happy expressions with a hint of amusement. "Why do you look like I am just going to start telling you stories now? You will train until you are exhausted beyond belief. If you have earned it through your hard work, I might share some tidbits with you while you rest."</p>
<p>"What?!" both girls yelled and pouted at the same time.</p>
<p>Thyra ignored their dramatic sighs. There were no spells or weapons present at this training station, only posture and footwork.</p>
<p>A back-to-the-basics training, setting up perfect foundations for the future.</p>
<p>"You both skipped some steps," Thyra said, circling them. "I can see it clearly."</p>
<p>Felicity crossed her arms. "We can handle ourselves. We even went on a long adventure without Quinlan's protection!"</p>
<p>Feng then gingerly added, "Well, we thought we didn't have his protection… But Felicity is right about one thing! We have very powerful classes, I think we should learn how to use those to the utmost."</p>
<p>"You might be able to survive on an adventure, but not thrive in a life-or-death situation. Isn't that right? If you want to become a woman who can shoulder the weight of being the one who protects instead of being protected, then you better listen."</p>
<p>"Ouch!" Felicity yelped, feeling called out.</p>
<p>"Miss Thyra is so kind, but she can spit some painful truths so nonchalantly too…" Feng grumbled.</p>
<p>…</p>
<p>Lucille found Dragnar.</p>
<p>The Primordial Dread stood near the stone rise with his arms folded, observing Quinlan's duel while shaking his head. Lucille walked up to him.</p>
<p>"Can you teach me how to become the dread of war, Dragnar?" she asked, taking the direct approach instead of what many would in her situation.</p>
<p>Hanae, a short distance away, pouted playfully. After all, Lucille asked to study under her previously. She was changing mentors just like this… What a traitor!</p>
<p>But the Bloodmonger only giggled in response. "Lady Hanae, you were the first to offer, that's why I jumped at your offer. I didn't know we could pick and choose from multiple primordials as teachers... If I did, I might've gone with Lady Thyra instead."</p>
<p>Hanae stared at her for a heartbeat, pretending to be hurt out of nothing more than playfulness, then sighed. "You truly are a berserker at heart, despite your gentle-looking appearance."</p>
<p>Lucille grinned, unapologetic.</p>
<p>Hanae shook her head, then smiled despite herself. She was only playing, after all, enjoying having such small, nonconsequential exchanges with mortals for the first time in millions of years. "It is the right choice. Any berserker should learn from Dragnar."</p>
<p>Dragnar looked down at Lucille. "It won't be easy."</p>
<p>"I wouldn't have it any other way!" Lucille decreed with excitement.</p>
<p>Training under Dragnar was not torture as one might expect. The Primordial Dread didn't turn Lucille into mincemeat and expect her to learn from the experience. Instead, what he gave her was pressure. He forced Lucille to manage her escalation, to recognize the point where hunger overtook judgment. He baited her into overextension, punished it once, then waited to see if she learned.</p>
<p>By the end of the first hour, Lucille was breathing hard and her axe was heavier than it had been before.</p>
<p>She was smiling anyway.</p>
<p>She found one amazing teacher.</p>
<p>It was time to make the most of it.</p>
<p>…</p>
<p>Hanae gathered Ayame and Black Fang with a small wave of her hand and led them away from the others, toward a quieter section of the clearing where the ground was even and clear.</p>
<p>She stopped and turned to face them, eyes moving between the two young women with open interest.</p>
<p>"My genes are truly superb," Hanae decreed proudly after a bit, nodding to herself as if confirming a long-held theory. "I already knew it, but it's good to see they didn't lose effectiveness even after so long."</p>
<p>Ayame blinked once, then giggled. "For an ancient lady, you are surprisingly playful."</p>
<p>Hanae's lips curved upward. "Is it a crime for someone like me to be happy, child? My heart is brimming with joy. I can't believe I finally get to interact with mortals after so long… Today is a day I'll celebrate for many years to come."</p>
<p>"No, it's not a crime," Ayame replied with an even bigger smile forming on her delicate lips. "If anything, I hope I will still have just as much joy as you do when I reach your age."</p>
<p>Black Fang inclined her head. "Many lose themselves to time."</p>
<p>Ayame glanced at her. "Are you talking about Yoruha, or the skellies?"</p>
<p>"All of them have numerous problems."</p>
<p>Ayame opened her mouth, clearly ready with something sharp, something the Venomborne Terror wouldn't like. She understood it too, because Black Fang's eyes flicked toward her. The look carried weight. Ayame laughed and raised her hands in surrender.</p>
<p>"Okay, okay. I'll try to be respectful to my big sis. Damn… It's still so weird to call you that…"</p>
<p>"Big sis?" Hanae chirped, eyes lighting up with newfound excitement, looking an awful lot like a schoolgirl sniffing the newest gossip. "I knew I felt something familiar about you two! Tell me more."</p>
<p>Ayame scratched the back of her head. "Seems my bastard of a father sired Black Fang and then ruined her life to save face. Or something horribly cruel like that. I'll sort it out eventually. After I get stronger."</p>
<p>Black Fang exhaled slowly, then shifted her attention back to Hanae. "Speaking of strength. Lady-"</p>
<p>"Please," Hanae interjected gently. "Call me Hanae."</p>
<p>Black Fang nodded. "Hanae. There is a young woman named Kaede. She is Ayame's younger sister, someone who should not be a noteworthy combatant. Instead, she rose almost overnight and now matches me in strength."</p>
<p>Ayame's expression sharpened when the topic of her younger sister was brought up. The mystery that had been eating away at her mind, giving her multiple sleepless nights as she pondered in bed.</p>
<p>"We suspect that her blade is empowering her," Black Fang continued. "Do you know of such a phenomenon?"</p>
<p>Hanae blinked. "It's one thing for a blade to empower its wielder, but that much? This does not sound like anything from my time. Tell me more."</p>
<p>Ayame nodded. "Sure. It started when…"</p>
<p>The explanation went on for a good few minutes, making the eastern primordial's gaze darken multiple times. When she learned of the horrible betrayal both girls experienced, she looked ready to cause extreme bloodshed among the members of her bloodline.</p>
<p>As for the sword, she didn't know what it could be in particular, saying it was best to ask Björn. But that could come later. Hanae gestured for them to take their stances.</p>
<p>"Enough talking. Let me see how my descendants move."</p>
<p>And just like this, fights and training sessions broke out all around Quinlan's dome. As the man fought with all he had, so did his girls and allies do their utmost to become a better version of themselves.</p>
<p>Quinlan, meanwhile, was making progress with his own fight.</p>
<p>It was time to evolve even further!</p>
