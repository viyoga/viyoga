<p>We all ponder on her statement, but it's my beautiful tantrum thrower who speaks up once again. "Hmm… Wizard is one of the few placeholder classes like Warrior, which means it's supposed to let its holders experiment before forcing them to choose a path. However, all Wizards are supposed to be people who ingested the elixir as young kids.</p>
<p>If we assume that the potion somehow analyzed your personality, ideal fighting style, and future prospects it isn't impossible that it forcibly skipped the experimenting phase and instead chose the one that fit you the most.</p>
<p>It most likely understood that you are a selfless person who doesn't care about fame and instead wishes to elevate those that you care about, or something along these lines, hence you became an enchanter, also known as a supporter or buffer.</p>
<p>When this big oaf whose lap you sit in so comfortably like it's your new favorite chair drinks it, I bet my life that he will get some selfish class that will let him deal tons of damage to his enemies instead of helping his allies so that he can look like the main character of the imaginary novel he writes about himself in his head."</p>
<p>'Interesting theory.' I say inwardly, completely ignoring her jab.</p>
<p>I'm not a selfish person at all.</p>
<p>My eyes shift to the alchemical desk where two delicate looking elixirs are sitting. "Blossom, bring me one of those." I instruct.</p>
<p>It's time, finally. What I've been waiting for since I first played Eld*r Scrolls on my father's horrible office computer when I was a mumbling little snot nosed brat.</p>
<p>"Quinlan, please let's think about it more!" Aurora protests. "I wouldn't be able to live with myself if you got killed because of my elixir!" She declares while shifting in my lap in a way that lets our chests meet as she wraps her legs around my hips and hugs my back with her arms while looking up towards my face, staring into my eyes pleadingly.</p>
<p>I kiss the top of her beautiful, lush platinum hair. She's quickly endearing herself into my heart, so much so that now I'm starting to get anxious regarding her choice. Well, based on her proximity and antics I would say that my chances are rather good.</p>
<p>Ayame replies in my stead. "I mean absolutely no offense when I say this, but if you survived it, do you think this primordial cheater with over 700 HP will really die? I'm not concerned at all, in fact I bet he will be fine after a few hours of slight discomfort."</p>
<p>"Blossom is still worried!" My adorable dog-kin yelps.</p>
<p>I chuckle jovially, "then, my two kindhearted girls, you can nurse me back to health afterwards while my little grumpy can continue her bitching session to her heart's content."</p>
<p>""Okay!"" They shout in unison, visibly excited at the prospect.</p>
<p>"Hmph!" Ayame snorts and refuses to meet my gaze.</p>
<p>Without further ado I snatch the vial out of my worried dog-kin's hands and chug it down in a single gulp.</p>
<p>I grit my teeth, ready to face the onslaught of pain I know is about to assault me. Skin blight, exhaustion, popping veins…</p>
<p>Hmm? Why do I feel nothing at all?</p>
