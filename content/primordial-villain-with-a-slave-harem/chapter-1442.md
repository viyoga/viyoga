<p>Jagged bolts of white and violet lightning began to arc between their sweating bodies.</p>
<p>As the elemental electricity surged through Quinlan, it bridged the gap between his soul and hers, turning the point of their union into a high-voltage conduit. But Nyxara was not a mere fledgling succubus; she was the Primordial Demon of Lust, a creature who had spent eons feasting on the lives of heroes. Even as the platinum brand burned into her womb, her Limitless Lust Physique roared in a final defiance. Instead of collapsing, Nyxara's body bucked with a renewed, frantic vigor. <p>"Is... that... all?!" she gasped, her voice a jagged melody of pain and fanatical pleasure.</p>
<p>She leaned into the lightning. Rather than recoiling from the electrical siege, she opened herself to it, attempting to digest the elemental destruction. <p>She was fighting him with the weapon that denoted her whole life: an endurance born of eternal gluttony. She reached back and gripped his hair, pulling him closer.</p>
<p>"Your fire is just... another meal!❤️"</p>

<p>Drink the blood of a level 70+ entity (Incomplete).</p>

<p>Two could play that game.</p>
<p>He leaned into her touch, and before the demoness could register the shift in his aura, he opened his mouth and sank his teeth deep into the side of her neck.</p>
<p>While the trial took place inside the primordial dimension, where damage was undone within moments, the dome itself worked differently. Inside, taking damage was more than possible, evidenced by all the blood and pain their exchange caused thus far.</p>
<p>This was no different, just taken to the next level.</p>
<p>"Ggh!?"</p>

<p>Liquid starlight, spiced with a heat that threatened to incinerate his throat, perhaps...?</p>
<p>As he gulped down the searing, thick fluid, a notification flashed in the corner of his vision, but he ignored it. He was focused on the sensation of her life-force entering his system, mixing with the elemental fire already roaring in his marrow.</p>

<p>Her internal muscles seized, not in a tactical drain of his manhood, but in a series of violent, uncontrollable spasms that still managed to milk Quinlan with a desperate, mind-shattering intensity. However, the scales had tipped.</p>
<p>She was in a worse state, continuously and perpetually orgasming from the sheer, brutal wrongness of it, her head thrashing against his shoulder as she felt her blood and her lust being claimed simultaneously.</p>
<p>Quinlan felt the drain at his center vanish, replaced by an explosive surge of power. The demon blood acted as a stabilizer for the four elements.</p>
<p>"Accept it…" Quinlan growled against the wound in her neck, his voice muffled by the copper-sweet taste of her. "You belong to me. Resistance is futile, demon!"</p>
<p>He reached into the absolute depths of his soul and sent the Soul-Pulse.</p>
<p>The platinum mark on her womb erupted, together with a rush of lightning that traversed her whole body in a single moment. Jagged, crystalline roots of molten mana dove deep into her core, wrapping around her demonic heart. Nyxara's eyes went wide. She realized, too late, that her gluttony had been her undoing. She couldn't consume him. He was too big. He was too much. He was everything.</p>
<p>"My Ruin…" she whispered.</p>
