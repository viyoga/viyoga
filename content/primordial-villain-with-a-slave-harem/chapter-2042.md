<p>"&gt; &lt;It’s risky, but we’re being offered Grade Six rewards. More, perhaps, if the haul is good. Jobs at that grade and above usually take weeks. Sometimes months. Travel, finding the target, tracking it down... This is one planned assault at a known location, a day’s sailing away.&gt;</p>

<p>Fortified positions appeared among them. The party had experience with the work being proposed, and their report would be worth hearing before he committed his family to it.</p>
<p>Kaelira held the supply list where he could see the conductors she’d marked. Beyond her, Alexios and Myrasyn were discussing the island’s approaches.</p>
<p>Serika added. <p>"Unbound stays under my command. We’ll inspect the ship and agree on our role before committing to the expedition."</p>
<p>"We can record that, sir. If you can’t agree, you may decline before departure without penalty."</p>

<p>"We’ll be at the briefing. Can my friends join us on the expedition?" Quinlan asked, indicating Alexios and the others.</p>

<p>"I’m glad. Both girls looked so much happier when they left here."</p>

<p>"Were you hoping to register as adventurers?"</p>

<p>"Is that a trick question, Miss Clerk?"</p>

<p>"Sir?"</p>
<p>"If they register, they’ll begin at Grade One. This contract calls for Grade Six, with one named exception. They wouldn’t qualify."</p>
<p>Behind the clerk, a woman carrying a stack of folders slowed. At the next station, a pen stopped scratching while its owner glanced toward Alexios.</p>
<p>Quinlan could guess the question forming. Would Black put these strangers into Unbound to bring them under his invitation?</p>

<p>"I don’t ask trick questions, sir."</p>

<p>"Of course."</p>
