<p>It was early evening, but the store was already bustling with people.<br>
Dazzling lighting equipment, a spectacular lineup of cameras, lenses, and various gear packed the interior to the brim.<br>
The two of them wove through the crowd and approached a male employee at the counter.<br>
The clerk, who greeted them with a bright smile, gave a polite bow.<br>
「Welcome. Thank you for visiting BWC.」<br>
Han-pan and Miho lowered their heads slightly in return.<br>
The man looked to be of a certain age, but his physique was impressive.<br>
Though streaks of gray were visible through his short hair, he was a middle-aged man who clearly knew how to groom himself, sporting a stylish beard and boasting muscles as massive as Han-pan’s.<br>
The bowtie fastened to his shirt left a strong impression.<br>
From the aura he exuded, he seemed to be someone in a high position in charge of this place.<br>
Han-pan squared his shoulders and spoke to him.<br>
「I’d like to purchase some filming equipment.」<br>
「Certainly. For what purpose are you looking to buy this gear?」<br>
「Uh... I want to take some photos and try making some videos...」<br>
「I see. Then, if you don't mind me asking, what grade of equipment are you looking for? The price varies greatly depending on the grade.」<br>
Miho, who was standing beside him, spoke up as she saw him hesitate to answer.<br>
「Give us the best you have.」<br>
「And who will be the one using it?」<br>
Miho spoke with a smile playing on her lips.<br>
「My son here will be using it... He’s a very talented child.」<br>
「Hmm, if I may be so bold, I don't usually recommend buying the absolute best right from the start.」<br>
「Oh, is that so?」<br>
「Yes. If it’s alright with you, how about I recommend a basic set that can handle both photos and video simultaneously, and then you can make changes from there?」<br>
「That sounds good.」<br>
「Understood. Then please wait just a moment.」<br>
He spoke the name of an unknown model to another clerk nearby.<br>
As that clerk disappeared, the man turned his gaze back to her and asked.<br>
「What kind of objects will you primarily be shooting?」<br>
Miho answered on behalf of Han-pan, who was hesitating.<br>
「He wants to practice by mainly taking photos of me.」<br>
「Oh, is that so? What a wonderful son... Then again, with such a stunning and beautiful mother, I’m sure anyone would want to take your photo! I often take photos of my daughter as well... Hahaha.」<br>
He let out a boisterous laugh.<br>
Just then, a young clerk brought out several pieces of equipment and displayed them in front of them.<br>
It was a camera, a lens, and some simple lighting gear.<br>
「To shoot video, you generally use about two units. One for handheld shots and another fixed on a tripod... This lighting will ensure the images don't become too high-contrast when filming video.」<br>
「What do you think? Do you like it?」<br>
Miho asked while looking at Han-pan.<br>
After examining the models, Han-pan spoke.<br>
「Yes, but are there any other cameras besides this one? I’d like to see something with slightly higher specs... One flagship, one for general use... I already have one of my own.」<br>
「Oh, is that so? Very well.」<br>
He told the model names to the clerk standing nearby.<br>
「It would be even better if the lighting is something that can make skin look beautiful.」<br>
「Understood.」<br>
The man nodded and stepped away for a moment.<br>
Miho looked at the man and asked.<br>
「You said you take photos of your daughter?」<br>
At her words, he replied with a chuckle.<br>
「Haha, that’s right... As a father, capturing my daughter’s growth is a wonderful thing.」<br>
「What kind of photos do you usually take? Since you’re family, I imagine it might get awkward sometimes.」<br>
He gave a thin smile and said.<br>
「Hmm... is that so? I don't find it awkward at all... Would you like to see?」<br>
「Yes, I’d love to!」<br>
The man pulled out his cellphone, swiped his finger across the screen, and showed it to them.<br>
On the screen was a woman who looked to be in her early twenties, perhaps a year or two younger than Han-pan, with a fairly innocent vibe—it was a very well-taken photo.<br>
What was somewhat baffling, however, was that the outfit had a high level of exposure.<br>
She was wearing a skin-tight, clingy dress.<br>
He showed them other photos as well.<br>
Most of them were photos with a rather high level of exposure.<br>
She was wearing tight clubwear-style outfits or bikini swimsuits.<br>
Han-pan felt a strange sensation at the thought of a father taking such photos of his own daughter.<br>
Han-pan found the woman’s appearance in the photos very much to his liking.<br>
He tilted his head and said.<br>
「She’s a wonderful model... She has such a great appearance that it’s hard to believe she’s your daughter. Hahaha.」<br>
At his words, the man gave a thin smile.<br>
「My daughter has a pretty good figure... Fortunately, she didn't take after me... Hahaha. She wants to get into acting, but I’m trying to stop her.」<br>
「Oh my, why is that?」<br>
He spoke with a worried expression.<br>
「Acting isn't easy, and the competition is fierce... I don't want to see my daughter suffering in a place like that.」<br>
「Hmm, this industry is indeed quite competitive and full of noise, so it’s not easy.」<br>
「Haha, right? I’m also somewhat connected to that world, so I hear a lot of stories, which makes me quite worried... There’s a limit to how much I can stop her, though.」<br>
Miho smiled at his words.<br>
「Oh my, is that so? She seems to have a talent for photos... and she seems to have the spark for it, too...」<br>
Miho rummaged through her handbag, pulled out a business card, and handed it to him.<br>
「If she’s interested, tell her to contact me.」<br>
The man took Miho’s business card and examined it.<br>
「Oh... You’re the CEO of an entertainment agency... I think I’ve seen this name somewhere.」<br>
「Yes, it’s a small agency... Since it’s new, we haven't made a name for ourselves in the industry yet, but we’re working hard.」<br>
「Ha, I see... Thank you... I’ll try talking to my daughter... I think she’ll really like this.」<br>
He fumbled through his pocket and pulled out a small business card case.<br>
Then, he pulled one out and handed it to Miho.<br>
Han-pan looked at his business card.<br>
Corporation, Black White and Color CEO Park Sang-pil<br>
「Ah, so you were the CEO here... I didn't recognize you.」<br>
Miho smiled as she looked at him.<br>
「We are connected to many broadcasting companies... Not to brag, but we have a large scale in this industry... Hahaha. Besides, since this is the era of YouTube, we have good relationships with famous YouTubers, and I’m always grateful that they use our services... If we continue our relationship, I think it will be a good business connection for your business as well, CEO-nim.」<br>
「Hohoho, I suppose so... We should have a meal together sometime.」<br>
「Hahaha, I’m always open to a business meeting!」<br>
As he finished speaking, another clerk arrived carrying the cameras Han-pan had ordered.<br>
He handed the boxes to Han-pan and said.<br>
「Are these the ones you wanted?」<br>
Han-pan gave a broad grin and replied.<br>
「That’s right.」<br>
「Haha, as I thought... Everyone has a camera they’re thinking of... Your expectations seem quite high, but I believe this thing will satisfy you well.」<br>
Then, he moved from his spot, came out from behind the counter, and guided them.<br>
「Follow me... I’ll show you the lighting.」<br>
He walked over and stood in front of a piece of lighting equipment.<br>
「This thing is a bit pricey, but it’s excellent for capturing skin.」<br>
He pointed to a light that looked like it belonged on a movie set, with a considerable size.<br>
Just by looking at it, it seemed like the price would be substantial.<br>
Miho nodded and said.<br>
「Good... We’ll buy it all.」<br>
「You’re quite bold... You truly are a wonderful mother. Hahaha... Then I’ll prepare the set accordingly.」<br>
「Yes, please.」<br>
They returned to the counter, and Miho paid with her card right then and there.<br>
The amount was significant, but she didn't seem to mind at all.<br>
Once the payment was finished, he asked.<br>
「Did you bring a car?」<br>
「Yes, it’s parked out front.」<br>
「Excellent. Our employee will load it into your car for you.」<br>
After saying that, he nodded to the clerk standing nearby.<br>
The clerk nodded as if he understood and began gathering the items.<br>
Miho looked at the man and said.<br>
「Make sure to have your daughter contact me.」<br>
The man gave a thin smile.<br>
「Thank you... I didn't know we would form such a connection.」<br>
He bowed his head slightly in farewell.<br>
Han-pan and Miho returned home with the trunk full of equipment.<br>
As she turned off the car engine, Miho spoke.<br>
「Well, Director... are you satisfied now?」<br>
Han-pan nodded and said.<br>
「It’s great. Mother!」<br>
「Hoho, only calling me 'Mother' at times like this?」<br>
Han-pan gave a thin smile.<br>
「Of course not... Hahaha.」<br>
「I don't know anymore... Since the bridge has already been crossed, you’d better do a good job.」<br>
「Yes, ma'am.」<br>
Saying that, Miho got out of the car, left the underground parking lot, and headed to her room.<br>
Han-pan moved the pile of luggage into the living room, then began unpacking and assembling them one by one.<br>
Han-pan’s mind was racing with various thoughts.<br>
While he was preoccupied with examining each piece of the newly bought equipment and learning how to use them, Miho came down to the living room after changing into comfortable attire.<br>
「Oh, you changed your clothes...」<br>
「Why?」<br>
「I was thinking of doing a few test shots.」<br>
「Oh, really?」<br>
「Noona isn't here... and I thought now would be a good time.」<br>
Miho turned her head and looked outside the window.<br>
The sun was setting, turning the sky dark.<br>
「Hmm... Let’s clean up this trash first...」<br>
She said, looking at the living room which was a mess of wrappers.<br>
「Oh, okay.」<br>
After Han-pan said that, he quickly organized the wrappers and cardboard boxes.<br>
Miho brought the vacuum cleaner and swept the floor to remove the dust.<br>
Once things were somewhat organized, Miho spoke.<br>
「Are you hungry?」<br>
「No, why?」<br>
「If I eat dinner now, I’ll get a belly, so if you’re not that hungry, I was thinking of eating after your test or whatever. What do you think?」<br>
「Sure, sure. I understand.」<br>
「Do you have something in mind? For the test shots...」<br>
「Hmm... I wonder...」<br>
As Han-pan fell into deep thought, Miho spoke with a smile.<br>
「I’ll pick out something to wear and come back out.」<br>
「Oh, okay. I got it.」</p>
