<p>“Our plane will soon be arriving at Incheon International Airport. Thank you for flying with our airline today. We hope everyone reaches their desired destinations safely, and please keep your seatbelts fastened until the next announcement.”</p>

<p>As the in-flight announcement echoed through the cabin, the plane prepared to land at Incheon Airport.<br>
It had been a long flight.<br>
Han-pan had been fallen into a deep, sweet sleep.<br>
The peer sitting next to him nudged him with an arm and spoke.</p>

<p>“Hey, Han-pan, wake up… we’re almost there…”</p>

<p>“Um-nya um-nya… really?”</p>

<p>Han-pan opened his bleary eyes and looked out the window.<br>
He could see the unique silt-colored hue of the Yellow Sea and several islands.<br>
After preparing for the landing for a moment, the plane rapidly descended.<br>
Soon, with a heavy thud, the massive aircraft touched down on the runway.<br>
Han-pan’s body leaned forward briefly as the plane quickly reduced its speed.<br>
Having successfully landed, the plane moved slowly toward its designated gate.<br>
Through the window, Han-pan saw the scenery of Incheon Airport under a cloudy sky.<br>
It had been almost a year.<br>
He slowly shook his stiff legs and arms from the long flight and stretched.<br>
It had been a truly sweet sleep.</p>

<p>“Han-pan, what are you going to do now?”</p>

<p>“Huh?”</p>

<p>“Well… I haven’t really thought about it yet. I’m going to go home and think it over slowly…”</p>

<p>“Is that so? Must be nice… having the leisure to think, hahahaha.”</p>

<p>Seeing him laugh with his teeth bared, Han-pan gave a bitter smile and looked back out the window.<br>
In truth, Han-pan had never seriously considered what he would do after being discharged.<br>
However, what he did know was that he had plenty of time to think, and his military career, which wasn't exactly short, had brought about quite a few changes in him.<br>
Fitting for a military life that was a continuous cycle of grueling labor, his body was more physically solid than ever thanks to consistent weightlifting, and it glowed with an alluring radiance.<br>
His body was brimming with vitality and overflowing with energy.<br>
He felt as though nothing would be impossible for a twenty-two-year-old man.<br>
He thought about returning to university immediately, but since his major didn't suit his interests, he considered delaying his return to try something else.<br>
On the other hand, he also thought about getting a job and working for a while at the company his mother ran or the one his father managed.<br>
As the tail of his thoughts suddenly led to his parents, he let out a sigh.<br>
His parents had divorced just before he enlisted.<br>
Han-pan had found this fact incredibly difficult to accept.<br>
He had decided to join the military out of a desire to get away for a while.<br>
Han-pan’s parents had fought loudly and frequently since he entered high school, for various reasons.<br>
His parents were in such a state of conflict that there was never a day of peace in the household.<br>
When he was finishing middle school, his maternal grandfather passed away.<br>
Han-pan’s mother inherited that business. Having to manage a new line of work, his mother was quite stressed, but she soon devoted her entire being to the business and spent her time being very busy.<br>
And this led to their great discord.<br>
Upon entering high school, Han-pan’s mother, Park Mi-ho, changed significantly on the inside, and his father, Kim Tae-sik, found that point extremely displeasing.<br>
He had witnessed the two of them arguing frequently over business-related reasons.<br>
Han-pan’s father, Tae-sik, had started his business by opening a small cafe as a barista when Han-pan was young, and it became famous after he successfully turned it into a brand.<br>
Now, he was the chairman of a coffee specialty franchise with a significant scale that anyone in the country would recognize, operating nationwide. However, he had retired and made a successful transformation into a well-known politician in his local district.<br>
Han-pan’s maternal grandfather had been an early importer of cars in Korea; he became famous, opened a dealership, and was well-known for importing top-tier luxury cars or rare vehicles.<br>
After his grandfather passed away, his mother inherited the business. Though she had many worries, she accepted her father’s dying wish.<br>
Park Mi-ho’s business acumen was quite excellent.<br>
In fact, Park Mi-ho’s business sense had played a large part in Tae-sik, who was just a barista, becoming the CEO of a franchise coffee company.<br>
Once she inherited the business, she used her own professional intuition to evolve it a step further into a high-end car dealership that dealt only with a small number of VIPs in the country.<br>
Later, she even dipped her toes into the entertainment industry, demonstrating enough skill to own a fairly decent agency.<br>
As Park Mi-ho’s business grew rapidly, Tae-sik, who wanted quiet wifely support for his political life, felt considerable dissatisfaction.<br>
Unlike the household atmosphere that was becoming materially more affluent, Han-pan also suffered a lot of stress from the discord occurring within the home.<br>
However, he and his older sisters sincerely wished for their mother’s success and also respected their father’s transformation into a politician as his fatherly role.<br>
Separate from that, the discord blowing between the two of them greatly influenced the lives of Han-pan and his older sisters.<br>
Nevertheless, they all moved on to good schools.<br>
However, the discord between the two was endless, and eventually, after seeing Han-pan enter university, Tae-sik demanded a divorce from Mi-ho.<br>
His conservative personality hated to acknowledge the existence of Mi-ho, who was trying to escape his control.<br>
That point became the catalyst for them splitting up.<br>
And once they divorced, he and his older sisters decided to live with their mother.<br>
There were several reasons, but the decisive one was that they found out their father had another woman.<br>
Han-pan learned while he was in the military that his father had remarried after the divorce.<br>
At first, it was a shock, but he soon accepted it calmly.<br>
He had certainly been a great man before, a man who was as faithful to his family as possible and who protected the household by sacrificing everything.<br>
Han-pan’s father was a kind and good father.<br>
He did not deny that point.<br>
In fact, he felt incredibly grateful for it.<br>
However, he also felt it was true that everyone has their own reasons for their actions.<br>
While serving in the military, Han-pan often had video calls with his family to let them know he was doing well, but the news of the remarriage his father delivered during a call was certainly shocking.<br>
As he was reflecting on his past and lost in various thoughts, the in-flight announcement was heard, and a message appeared indicating that seatbelts could be unfastened. People began to stand up one after another to retrieve their luggage.<br>
Han-pan also unbuckled his seatbelt, stood up, and gathered his things.<br>
After taking his luggage out of the overhead bin and waiting a moment, people slowly moved toward the exit.<br>
Han-pan moved forward slowly within that ripple of people.<br>
After being seen off by the cabin crew and entering the airport terminal through the exit, he felt the unique damp but familiar warmth of his homeland, which he hadn't felt in a long time, wash over his entire body.<br>
He moved slowly along the passage in a group with his peers.<br>
He felt an unknown sense of excitement.</p>

<p>“Hey… how long has it been…”</p>

<p>When one peer spoke, he replied.</p>

<p>“Seriously… Korea is the best after all… hahahaha.”</p>

<p>He laughed cheerfully and slowly approached the immigration counter.<br>
Han-pan scanned his passport at the automated kiosk.<br>
Soon, the automatic doors opened, and after passing through immigration, he headed to the baggage claim and waited for his suitcase to come out.<br>
After a while, the machine began to spit out the passengers' suitcases one by one.<br>
Han-pan was soon able to find his suitcase.<br>
The peers who had found their luggage one by one gathered and looked at each other's faces for a moment.<br>
Many memories and recollections came to mind.<br>
Looking at the peers who had shared the difficult life in a foreign land, a flood of emotions rushed over him.</p>

<p>“Alright… then let’s part ways here… let’s get in touch soon and have a drink… good work, everyone.”</p>

<p>One peer said, and they nodded and exchanged glances.<br>
Then, they hugged each other tightly, encouraging one another for the future that lay ahead.<br>
After releasing the hug with his peer, Han-pan habitually pulled his suitcase toward the airport waiting room.<br>
As the doors opened, he could see many people waiting with their necks craned to see the family or friends they were expecting.<br>
Some people cheered while holding pickets, and some were even shedding tears.<br>
Thinking that meeting family held that much significance, Han-pan looked around and was able to find a familiar face he knew.<br>
And soon, his lips curled upward.<br>
On one side, he saw his father, Kim Tae-sik, standing there.<br>
His father, Kim Tae-sik, spotted him and wore a bright smile.<br>
Han-pan approached him immediately, but the woman standing next to Tae-sik caught his eye.<br>
She was a stranger, but he thought she was incredibly attractive.<br>
She looked to be in her mid-160s in height, and wearing low heels, she gave off a classic allure that felt very demure.</p>

<p>“Hey… son…”</p>

<p>Kim Tae-sik greeted his son with a bright smile and a proud look.<br>
Approaching him, Han-pan put down his suitcase and slowly gave a hand salute as he spoke.</p>

<p>“Sergeant Kim Han-pan, reporting that I have been ordered to be discharged. Unity!”</p>

<p>Kim Tae-sik hugged him and said,</p>

<p>“You worked hard, my son! I’m proud of you, hahahaha.”</p>

<p>Kim Tae-sik was sincerely happy that his son had safely completed his military service.<br>
After holding him for a moment, Tae-sik released him and pointed toward the woman next to him, saying:</p>
