<p>“Yeah, me too.”</p>

<p>So-jo-ya hugged him.</p>

<p>Han-pan also wrapped his arms around her body.</p>

<p>The two of them stayed like that for a while.</p>

<p>After a moment, Han-pan released her and said,</p>

<p>“Mom will be waiting… let’s go quickly.”</p>

<p>“Okay.”</p>

<p>The two headed to the living room and found Mi-ho, who had already started breakfast.</p>

<p>“When your younger brother wakes you up, you should get up quickly, So-jo-ya.”</p>

<p>So-jo-ya gave a bright smile and said,</p>

<p>“Ah… sorry, sorry…”</p>

<p>And so, they sat across from each other and enjoyed a pleasant Sunday breakfast together.</p>

<p>++</p>

<p>“Han-pan-a, you’re going to be late, hurry up!”</p>

<p>“Oh, wait! I’m coming down now! Sorry, Mom.”</p>

<p>Han-pan put on a pair of clean camel-colored cotton pants and a white shirt, and while fastening his buttons, he ran down to the living room.</p>

<p>Mi-ho let out a sigh as she saw him running down, slung her large handbag over her shoulder, and stood up from the sofa.</p>

<p>Then she hurriedly went down to the underground garage.</p>

<p>Han-pan followed her down to the garage.</p>

<p>Mi-ho got into the car and quickly started the engine.</p>

<p>After following her down with quick steps and getting into the car, Han-pan asked,</p>

<p>“Mom, do I really have to go?”</p>

<p>Mi-ho started the car and left the house as she spoke.</p>

<p>“Don’t just stay at home all the time; you should meet some people… It’s a place with many good people, so it’ll be good for you to get to know them.”</p>

<p>As she said that, she turned on some hymns.</p>

<p>The sudden hymns made Han-pan’s head go blank.</p>

<p>“Mom, but since when did you start going to church like this?”</p>

<p>“…”</p>

<p>When she hesitated for a moment, Han-pan pressed her.</p>

<p>“Why? You don’t want to say?”</p>

<p>“Mmm… it’s been a little over half a year…”</p>

<p>“Really? But you know well that I have no interest in this kind of thing, right? And why aren’t you taking So-jo-ya nuna?”</p>

<p>Mi-ho spoke urgently.</p>

<p>“First of all, So-jo-ya said she had other urgent business today… and church isn’t something you go to as a hobby… Once a week, I reflect on the things I’ve done during the week and pray to live the next week diligently… Mom has received a lot of comfort at the church…”</p>

<p>Han-pan let out a sigh.</p>

<p>As soon as breakfast was over, he had decided to go to the church she attended due to Mi-ho’s persistent persuasion.</p>

<p>He knew she was attending church, but being asked to go with her was a bewildering thing.</p>

<p>Mi-ho wanted to introduce her son to good people and lead him to the church to let him know the love of God.</p>

<p>Han-pan was quite flustered by the sudden suggestion to go to church.</p>

<p>He knew she was going, but he hadn't thought she would encourage him to join.</p>

<p>Seeing his mother, who now looked like a chaste woman with no trace of her appearance from yesterday, he wondered if she might be psychologically unstable.</p>

<p>The fact that the nympho from Saturday night disappeared and set out for church on Sunday morning felt like a contradiction, but since everyone has their own contradictions, he decided not to think too deeply about it.</p>

<p>However, the appointment with Hye-su in the afternoon weighed on his mind.</p>

<p>Thinking that he could postpone it if necessary, he decided to play the role of a good son and follow her for today.</p>

<p>Han-pan had always been skeptical of religion.</p>

<p>However, from the expression on his mother’s face, he realized that attending the Sunday church service was an important matter for her.</p>

<p>He changed his mind and shifted his direction to granting his mother’s wish.</p>

<p>The car arrived at the church.</p>

<p>It was located not very far from their house.</p>

<p>The parking lot already looked nearly full.</p>

<p>Parking agents were running around busily, organizing the incoming cars.</p>

<p>They parked the car in an empty spot according to the guidance of one parking agent.</p>

<p>After finishing the parking, Mi-ho got out of the car and headed toward the church, and Han-pan also got out of the car and followed her.</p>

<p>The church was already bustling with many people, and he thought that this place must be quite a famous church.</p>

<p>Han-pan did not like the church, which boasted a high assembly hall and a flashy exterior of a modern building.</p>

<p>Rather than a sacred feeling, the church boasted a luxurious modern architectural design style.</p>

<p>Not only was the size grand, but the luxury cars lined up in the parking lot showed that this was a church attended by quite a lot of wealthy people.</p>

<p>He shook his head and followed his mother into the Main Hall of the church.</p>

<p>While walking, Mi-ho greeted several people who recognized her in a friendly manner, and Han-pan also slightly bowed his head toward them.</p>

<p>He watched her as she walked a step ahead, and she was dressed in a very modest fashion, unlike her usual self.</p>

<p>The kill heels she usually wore were nowhere to be seen; she was wearing flat shoes, and the cream-colored dress she was wearing was a typical wise mother and good wife style that came down below her knees.</p>

<p>Furthermore, she was wearing the lightest makeup he had seen recently and even had her hair tied neatly, so she looked like a completely different person.</p>

<p>No, in fact, it was the image of the mother he had known before.</p>

<p>Han-pan thought that perhaps the reason she attended church was to not completely forget her past self.</p>

<p>What was clear was that she had attempted a tremendous change while he was away in the army.</p>

<p>And that change was not limited to external things but also involved a great internal transformation.</p>

<p>He conjectured that she might have sublimated the process of accompanying pleasurable ache through religion.</p>

<p>His fingers, which had been digging into her fucking cunt amidst all those people at the party yesterday, still remembered the sensation from that moment.</p>

<p>However, the woman walking in front of him now had an appearance so different that it wouldn't be wrong to say she was a completely different woman.</p>

<p>Seeing her dual nature coexisting, Han-pan thought that women were truly mysterious beings.</p>

<p>Arriving at the Main Hall, they were guided to empty seats on the second floor, and after a while, the service began.</p>

<p>The church boasted a very large scale, with a hall so big that there were seats even on the third floor.</p>

<p>Decorations suitable for a church were arranged here and there, and the scale was large enough that there was a separate place in front of the altar where music could be performed.</p>

<p>It was a sacred space that felt quite religious, but at the same time, it felt like being in a concert hall.</p>

<p>After a while, hymns rang out and the service began.</p>

<p>Soon, the pastor who appeared was a man who looked to be in his late 50s with graying hair; despite his short height, he was quite fat, as if he had been eating well.</p>

<p>However, once the sermon began, his voice was very thick and clear, and at the same time, it had great dignity.</p>

<p>He skillfully captured the hearts of the followers by using both a benevolent voice and a strict voice depending on the atmosphere of the story he was telling.</p>

<p>Han-pan was reminded of his memory of a Muslim temple he had experienced during his time in the army.</p>

<p>He recalled the priest of the Muslim temple who was immersed in religion and offered prayers while frantically spitting out unknown words.</p>

<p>He remembered clicking his tongue as he watched that priest presiding over the ritual with overflowing blood and energy, and the people following him, and a similar scene was being staged in the church today.</p>

<p>Because Han-pan was cynical toward religion, he was not swept away by the atmosphere of the pastor’s sermon, and unlike the rising heat of the worship, a sigh escaped him as he grew even colder.</p>

<p>Compared to him, who was sitting with his legs crossed and his arms folded, Mi-ho, sitting next to him, seemed very much absorbed in the pastor’s words.</p>

<p>The pastor continuously poured out a long-winded sermon to the followers.</p>

<p>“Our brothers and sisters must always be vigilant against the whispers of the devil!”</p>

<p>He shouted, clenching his fist.</p>

<p>“That bastard doesn't know when or where he will appear to harm the hearts of our brothers and sisters! In order to overcome the demonic temptation that disturbs our hearts, we must all become one, unite firmly, and move forward centered around the church!”</p>

<p>He raised his clenched fist upward.</p>

<p>“Dear brothers and sisters, we will overcome and achieve victory today, tomorrow, and in this coming week! Even if the filthy devil tries to corrupt us, we must believe in Him and move forward! We will surely be victorious! Amen!!!”</p>

<p>With his hands facing upward and his head bowed, he spoke as if making a solemn proclamation.</p>

<p>At his intense cry, the followers became one as they passionately shouted "Amen."</p>

<p>At the same time, hymns filled the bowels of the hall, heightening the atmosphere.</p>

<p>Mi-ho also shouted "Amen," clasped her hands together, closed her eyes, and prayed.</p>

<p>Han-pan let out a sigh and looked around.</p>

<p>Everyone alike seemed to be proving their own piety by offering prayers and doing their best in their own way.</p>

<p>After a while, the choir behind the pastor came in and began to sing hymns, and the heat reached its climax, becoming even hotter.</p>

<p>Han-pan thought that the pastor was quite good at stirring up the hearts of the followers.</p>

<p>Seeing him bring up stories of the devil, he even conjectured whether this place was a semi-cult group.</p>

<p>However, compared to his unimpressive appearance, the pastor definitely had a mysterious charisma.</p>

<p>And so, the service came to an end.</p>

<p>Han-pan and Mi-ho stood up from their seats.</p>
