<p>“Your father raised you well. Like father, like son; your manners are excellent.”</p>

<p>“Haha, I’m a bit embarrassed.”</p>

<p>Han-pan sat in his seat as he spoke, and she asked while looking at the menu.</p>

<p>“Can you eat a lot?”</p>

<p>“Yes, I have a good appetite.”</p>

<p>“I see. Then I’ll take care of the ordering.”</p>

<p>Hye-su called for the server and ordered the food. After the order was placed, the two sat in silence for a while. Breaking the silence, Han-pan opened his mouth.</p>

<p>“How is it living with Father? He’s conservative, so it might be difficult in various ways.”</p>

<p>She gave her characteristic awkward smile and then took on a thoughtful expression. Perhaps due to her professional tendency as a doctor, she didn’t have many expressions, making it difficult to read her emotions.</p>

<p>“Hmm, I knew that well... but he’s someone with a certain innocence, so I think I like that about him.”</p>

<p>“Ah, I see... How has Father been lately?”</p>

<p>“He’s quite busy with various schedules. There are many people to meet and many places to attend, so he’s often not at home... I’m also busy with my own work, so there are many times we don’t see each other... My husband worried about you a lot that day, Han-pan-ssi... You should contact your father often... He doesn’t express it, but he thinks about you more than you’d think.”</p>

<p>Han-pan felt a rather strange sensation facing a woman who called his own father ‘husband’ in such a private setting. Han-pan smiled brightly and said.</p>

<p>“Ah, I see... I’ll contact him often.”</p>

<p>“Come visit the house often too! The door is always open. There’s even an empty room with a bed.”</p>

<p>“Understood. If it’s not an intrusion, I’ll visit sometime.”</p>

<p>At those words, she waved her hand grandly and spoke.</p>

<p>“An intrusion? Not at all, we’re family now.”</p>

<p>Han-pan thought for a moment about whether she was his family. She was his biological father’s current wife, so she was certainly family. Observing his complexion, she continued.</p>

<p>“Hmm, but... how about you, Han-pan-ssi? It won’t be easy adapting to society again after being discharged. There must be many things you’re not used to.”</p>

<p>“I’m making an effort to adapt.”</p>

<p>“I see.”</p>

<p>Having opened up, the two shared stories about various light topics around them. Soon, the food arrived one by one, and they talked a lot while sharing the meal. They had more similar hobbies than expected, so the conversation flowed. In particular, since Hye-su liked watching movies, she communicated quite well with him on that subject. She talked about the types of movies she liked, and they matched his preferences well. Knowing his father’s personality—someone who didn’t like going to movie theaters—he guessed she had been looking for someone to talk to about such things.</p>

<p>Thus, the two continued their conversation quite pleasantly while sharing the food that filled the full table. She didn’t have children, but judging by her intentions, she seemed to want them. Han-pan wondered if he might end up with a half-sibling. The meal ended as the conversation continued. Soon, dessert and tea were served. The conversation paused for a moment as they both sipped their tea.</p>

<p>“How was the meal? Was it alright?”</p>

<p>Han-pan patted his belly and said.</p>

<p>“It’s really delicious here. I ate well.”</p>

<p>“I’m glad it suited your taste.”</p>

<p>She smiled brightly. Her previously stiff expression had softened into something comfortable, and he found her strangely alluring.</p>

<p>“Er... if you have time, of course... I’m sure you’re busy with various things after being discharged... but please eat with me and see me often.”</p>

<p>“Ah, of course.”</p>

<p>She took off her glasses and rubbed her eyes for a moment. She seemed a bit tired. Looking at Han-pan, who was observing her complexion, she said.</p>

<p>“Ah, excuse me... I’ve been a bit tired lately because there are so many patients...”</p>

<p>“Yes.”</p>

<p>She put her glasses into the case she took from her bag and continued.</p>

<p>“Actually... it’s nothing else... but as you know... Han-pan-nim’s father... that is, my husband and I don’t have a child... We tried many times, but it wasn’t easy... Uh... it’s awkward to say this... but Han-pan-ssi’s father is no longer in a physical condition to produce a child.”</p>

<p>“!!!”</p>

<p>Han-pan was shocked. He couldn’t believe his father’s body condition was such that he could no longer produce a child.</p>

<p>“We tried hard, but since I didn’t get pregnant, I collected my husband’s sperm and sent it for testing, and it came back as azoospermia...”</p>

<p>She sighed as she spoke. At those words, Han-pan thought of his father. He guessed his father had suffered from various heavy workloads and must have received a lot of stress since becoming a politician. Of course, his age couldn’t be ignored, and unlike Han-pan, he wasn’t someone particularly interested in women. Roughly guessing the situation, Han-pan nodded with an understanding expression.</p>

<p>“That’s also why I asked to meet today.”</p>

<p>Han-pan took on a serious expression and waited for her next words. After hesitating for a moment, she spoke.</p>

<p>“Hmm... so... I was wondering if you could treat me like your biological mother, Han-pan-ssi...”</p>

<p>“Ah... yes...”</p>

<p>Han-pan was flustered by her unexpected proposal. No matter how old he estimated her to be, it was quite uncomfortable to treat a woman who looked to be in her late 30s as a mother. Moreover, he was in a situation where he faced his own living biological mother every day.</p>

<p>“Uh... have you spoken with my two older sisters?”</p>

<p>At those words, Hye-su hesitated again before speaking.</p>

<p>“Ah, yes... actually, I tried to get close in various ways, but it’s not easy... With Seo-ah-ssi, the age difference isn’t that great, and she didn’t want it either, so we agreed on her just calling me eonni. In the case of the other sister, she didn’t exactly welcome me warmly...”</p>

<p>Han-pan understood the feeling when reflecting on their personalities in his head.</p>

<p>“Er... so... I’m asking you as well, Han-pan-ssi...”</p>

<p>An awkward silence flowed for a moment, and Han-pan didn’t know what to say.</p>

<p>“Er... I understand... I’ll try my best for now... but I don’t think it will be easy...”</p>

<p>“Yes, I fully understand.”</p>

<p>“Yes...”</p>

<p>Silence flowed again.</p>

<p>“It might be strange to say this... but this marriage is my first, and since I’m a woman, I want to have a child, but it’s realistically impossible. Whether you accept it or not, in some ways, we are family now...”</p>

<p>Internally, Han-pan felt an unknown pity for her. She seemed to be speaking quite sincerely, and that moved Han-pan’s heart.</p>

<p>“Er... then... though it’s very awkward for now, I’ll treat you like a mother... How should I address you?”</p>

<p>After thinking for a moment, she opened her mouth.</p>

<p>“Could you... just comfortably call me Mom?”</p>

<p>“What?... Mom?”</p>

<p>“Yes... I’d be really grateful if you called me that... It’s something I won’t be able to hear in the future, but I really want to hear it.”</p>

<p>“Won’t people think it’s strange? I haven’t asked your age, so I don’t know for sure, but you look too young to be called my mother...”</p>

<p>“Ah... I’m 38 this year. If I had a child early, it wouldn’t be strange to have a child around your age, Han-pan-ssi...”</p>

<p>He nodded. He had often seen people on TV who had children early due to accidents in high school, so by that logic, it wasn’t strange, but it still felt a bit unsettling.</p>

<p>“Yes... actually, it’s a sudden request, so it’s awkward and flustering... but I’ll try.”</p>

<p>“Thank you... I actually had to gather a lot of courage to say this.”</p>

<p>“Yes, I imagine so...”</p>

<p>The two finished the rest of their tea. Han-pan was not only flustered by her proposal but also at a loss as to how to explain this to his own mom. Thinking they had finished their business, Han-pan hesitated and looked around. Hye-su also guessed that their business was concluded.</p>

<p>“Er, then... shall we head out?”</p>

<p>“Ah, yes, let’s. You’re going home, right?”</p>

<p>“Yes, I don’t have any special plans, so I should go home.”</p>

<p>“It’s not far from here; shall I give you a ride?”</p>

<p>“Isn’t your place around here? You’d have to go back...”</p>

<p>“Ah... well... a Saturday afternoon drive would be nice. It’s not like I have anything special to do even if I go home.”</p>

<p>Thinking there was nothing to lose, Han-pan bowed his head and said.</p>

<p>“Yes, then I’ll ask for the favor.”</p>

<p>“Yes, then on the way, there’s a place nearby that makes good coffee. Let’s get some coffee to go from there, alright?”</p>

<p>“Yes, that sounds good.”</p>

<p>The two stood up from their seats. Han-pan moved quickly to hold her jacket so she could put it on easily, and she smiled brightly as she slipped her arms into the sleeves. After she finished paying at the first-floor counter and stepped outside, a car was already waiting. The two got into the car, and she drove toward the cafe she knew nearby. Han-pan’s mind became incredibly complicated.</p>
