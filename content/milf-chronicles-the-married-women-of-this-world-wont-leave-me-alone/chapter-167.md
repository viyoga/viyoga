<p>“Bbibibibibig! Bbibibibibig! Bbibibibibig!”</p>

<p>The sound of the alarm violently shattered the morning silence, echoing through the room.<br>
Hong Jin-woo, who had been deep in sleep, made a frustrated expression as he turned off the alarm on his cellphone and pushed his body up from the bed.<br>
He sat there dazed for a moment before checking his phone.<br>
It was 7:00 AM.<br>
With a sigh, he put on the glasses lying next to him and stood up, scratching his head.<br>
With a haggard face, he left the room and headed for the restroom.<br>
It had already been three months since he started living in this small studio apartment.<br>
Having moved up from the countryside, life in Seoul still felt incredibly awkward and foreign to him.<br>
Everywhere he went was always bustling with people, and the tall buildings and traffic congestion were suffocating.<br>
It was especially bad in the dilapidated area where he lived.<br>
Having moved to the capital with his family’s support, he was determined to succeed in Seoul.<br>
He had submitted hundreds of job applications and gone on dozens of interviews.<br>
Though he was from a provincial university, it was a national one, so he had some confidence, but his spirit had withered significantly once he actually arrived in Seoul.<br>
However, after a period of struggle and much painful effort, he finally landed a job, and today was his first day.</p>

<p>Entering the restroom, he turned on the water of the showerhead and stared into the mirror.<br>
He looked exactly like a newcomer in his mid-twenties just starting his professional life.<br>
His hair and body were a mess, soaked by the water pouring down from the showerhead, and he was still dazed, not yet fully awake.<br>
Standing there for several minutes feeling the water flow over him, he felt his mind slowly begin to clear.<br>
He let out a sigh while looking at his reflection, then squeezed toothpaste onto his toothbrush to clean his teeth while simultaneously washing his hair.<br>
After a quick shower, he stepped out, dried himself off, and applied his skin care.<br>
He dried his hair with a hairdryer, did some light hair styling, and put on the crisp white button-down shirt he had prepared the night before, followed by black slacks.<br>
Once he put on the striped necktie that was hanging with them, he underwent a transformation into a quintessential salesman.<br>
A light spray of perfume lifted his mood with its pleasant scent.<br>
Feeling better, he looked in the mirror, clenched one hand into a fist, and struck a "fighting" pose.<br>
After encouraging himself like that, he straightened his tie.<br>
Finally, he threw on his jacket, grabbed the bag he wore over his shoulder, and left the house.</p>

<p>After walking for a few minutes, he arrived at the subway station.<br>
The place was already a sea of people commuting to work.<br>
Despite being dressed to the nines, he was shoved and crushed like a flattened rice cake among the crowd as he headed toward his first workplace located in Gangnam.<br>
When he reached the station closest to his office, the train doors opened.<br>
The people inside poured out rapidly.<br>
Hong Jin-woo threaded his way through them and exited the station.<br>
Even though it was morning, the August heat was already melting the earth.<br>
He felt like he would be exhausted from the people and the weather before he even reached the company.<br>
But he had to get used to it.<br>
The city where he was born didn't have this many people, so life in Seoul was still very unfamiliar to him.</p>

<p>After walking for a few more minutes, he arrived at his workplace.<br>
The company building was incredibly impressive.<br>
The building, painted in a matte black, had a powerful presence, and the interior boasted a very sophisticated design.<br>
He was overjoyed to be a part of this place.<br>
He opened the door and stepped inside.<br>
A cool chill welcomed him.<br>
Although work started at 9:00 AM, he had arrived 30 minutes early.<br>
Upon entering, he saw a line of supercars on display.<br>
With his eyes sparkling, he imagined himself driving a car like that one day as he headed toward the office.<br>
As he stood before the elevator, several people appeared beside him.<br>
They were all people heading to the office floors.<br>
Hong Jin-woo thought he had arrived early, but it seemed he wasn't the only one.<br>
Just as he was thinking about how hard everyone worked, the elevator arrived, and he was pulled inside with the crowd.<br>
Just as the doors were about to close, a voice called out from outside.</p>

<p>“Wait a moment!!!”</p>

<p>Hong Jin-woo hurriedly caught the elevator door.</p>

<p>“Thank you.”</p>

<p>The woman who stepped quickly into the elevator had a style that was exceptionally good at first glance.<br>
She was wearing a tight ivory skirt two-piece suit; the look was soft yet sharp, which he found very appealing.<br>
In particular, her sleek black horn-rimmed glasses were very impressive, giving off an intellectual yet unique charisma.<br>
He guessed she was definitely older than him.<br>
She looked to be in her late twenties to early thirties, and he wondered where she worked.<br>
The elevator reached the top floor, and people got off one by one.<br>
After stepping out, he lingered for a moment, waiting for the woman in the black horn-rimmed glasses to go first.<br>
He caught a glimpse of her entering another office located at the very edge.<br>
Her workspace was different from his.</p>

<p>He entered the office enclosed in floor-to-ceiling glass and headed toward the area where the dealers worked.<br>
Then he sat in his assigned seat.<br>
A computer and a laptop were set up there.<br>
Not many people had arrived yet, but as time passed, employees showed up one by one, filling the office.<br>
He was sitting there awkwardly when a man soon appeared and spoke to him.</p>

<p>“Oh, it’s the newcomer…”</p>

<p>Hong Jin-woo turned his head to look at the man.<br>
The man reached out a hand toward him.</p>

<p>“Nice to meet you. I’m Team Leader-nim Park Chan-dae.”</p>

<p>Hong Jin-woo stood up, took his hand, and bowed at the waist in greeting.<br>
He was a pleasant-looking man who appeared to be in his early forties.<br>
Though his face looked tired, he had the air of a perfect example of a clean-cut, middle-aged man.<br>
He looked around and spoke.</p>

<p>“Alright, will the new employees who joined the dealer team please gather round?”</p>

<p>At that, several people stood up.<br>
Hong Jin-woo wasn't the only one who had arrived early.<br>
Indeed, everyone was living their lives to the fullest.</p>

<p>“Alright, everyone, we’ll start by greeting the CEO.”</p>

<p>He assembled the new recruits.<br>
There were about six of them—four men and two women.<br>
They all had good style and looked well-groomed.<br>
Compared to them, he felt somewhat intimidated by their style, which seemed so different from his own provincial background.</p>

<p>“Ahem… Shall we go then?”</p>

<p>He led them out.<br>
They headed toward the room where the woman in the black horn-rimmed glasses had disappeared earlier.<br>
He knocked on the door and went inside, and the newcomers followed him into the room.<br>
Hong Jin-woo stepped inside.<br>
It was a fairly large room with tables divided by partitions in the center and on both sides, where employees were seated.<br>
Seeing the woman sitting in the center, Hong Jin-woo’s eyes lit up.<br>
It was the woman he had seen just moments ago.</p>

<p>“Hello, Team Leader-nim Park.”</p>

<p>“Ah, right, Manager Ha. Is the CEO in?”</p>

<p>“Yes, she’s in her office right now.”</p>

<p>“I’d like to introduce our new team members. Is the CEO busy?”</p>

<p>“One moment, please.”</p>

<p>She picked up the phone and dialed someone.</p>

<p>“CEO-nim, Team Leader-nim Park would like to introduce the new employees. Is that alright?... Yes… Yes… Understood.”</p>

<p>She said that, put down the receiver, and walked forward.<br>
Her slender figure caught Hong Jin-woo’s eye.<br>
He wondered what kind of man would get to date a woman like her.<br>
Manager Ha looked at Team Leader-nim Park and spoke.</p>

<p>“Shall we go?”</p>

<p>“Oh, right.”</p>

<p>Her expression was quite cold.<br>
She was so expressionless it felt as if a chill emanated from her, but that only served to highlight her charisma.<br>
As Manager Ha left the room, Team Leader-nim Park followed, and the new employees followed them out.<br>
She knocked on the door of the CEO’s office right next door and opened it.</p>

<p>“CEO-nim.”</p>

<p>“Oh, right. Tell them to come in.”</p>

<p>A soft voice drifted from inside.<br>
Team Leader-nim Park entered the room, looked back at the newcomers, and gestured for them to follow.<br>
The new employees stepped into the office.<br>
The room was very clean, and the scent of luxurious solid wood hit his nose.<br>
The woman sitting in the center stood up from her seat and smiled brightly at them.<br>
She was an exceptional beauty, far beyond what one would expect from the image of a CEO.<br>
She looked to be in her early forties at most.<br>
No, based on her looks alone, she could be in her thirties, but her atmosphere was incredibly sophisticated and overflowing with elegance.<br>
At the same time, an indefinable, subtle sexiness lingered about her.<br>
Unlike a typical CEO, she was wearing jeans, which were so tight that her leg lines were exceptionally prominent as she walked toward them.<br>
For her top, she wore a luxurious white silk jacket over a simple yet casual T-shirt.<br>
Her unusually large breasts made his eyes wander.<br>
In contrast to her refined aura, those tits felt like they existed in a world of their own.<br>
The next thing that caught his eye were the high heels she was wearing, which were incredibly tall.<br>
The heels were very sharp, and the toes were sleek.<br>
They were bright cream-toned heels with the toe area finished in black, adding to her stylishness.<br>
If he had met her outside, he never would have guessed she was the CEO of a company.<br>
Hong Jin-woo felt lucky to be able to work under such a representative.<br>
Walking toward them with a smile, she made eye contact with each of them and nodded.</p>

<p>"Welcome, everyone. I am Park Mi-ho, the CEO of this company."</p>
