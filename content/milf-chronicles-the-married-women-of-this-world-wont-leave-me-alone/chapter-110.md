<p>After the three women left, Han-pan, who had been sitting in the empty living room, stood up and headed to his room.</p>

<p>He sat at his computer and browsed through various issues on the community boards.</p>

<p>A moment later, a knock sounded, and So-jo entered the room.</p>

<p>She was already dressed in comfortable clothes for the house, wearing a loose T-shirt and very short dolphin pants.</p>

<p>Naturally, Han-pan’s gaze was pinned to her long, bare legs.</p>

<p>Conscious of his stare, she slowly approached him.</p>

<p>Han-pan watched her draw near with a sly look in his eyes and asked,</p>

<p>“Oh, really? Got it...”</p>

<p>Han-pan finished the game he was playing and stood up.</p>

<p>As he left the room, she followed behind him.</p>

<p>Looking at her as she approached, Han-pan asked,</p>

<p>“What were you talking about with eonni?”</p>

<p>“I showed her a bit of my collection.”</p>

<p>“Collection?”</p>

<p>“You know, the kind of things men would like...”</p>

<p>Han-pan was suddenly curious about what kind of things were in her collection.</p>

<p>“Show me, too.”</p>

<p>At his words, she poked her tongue out slightly and said,</p>

<p>“When the opportunity arises... when the mood is right... one by one... it’s only fun if you see them one at a time... hoho.”</p>

<p>“Where are Mom and eonni?”</p>

<p>“They’re in the kitchen preparing dinner.”</p>

<p>“Oh, yeah?”</p>

<p>He immediately pulled her face toward him.</p>

<p>Then, he pressed his lips against hers.</p>

<p>“The two of them are waiting... let's share the pleasure later and eat first...”</p>

<p>“Yeah, alright.”</p>

<p>The two went down to the kitchen and saw Miho and Seo-ah, who had just started their meal.</p>

<p>Han-pan and So-jo took their respective seats, and soon the four of them enjoyed a family meal for the first time in a while.</p>

<p>Various short stories were exchanged, but no one brought up the issue regarding Seo-ah at all.</p>

<p>Not only was Miho’s mood a factor, but Seo-ah herself didn't seem to want to talk about her own problem.</p>

<p>Dinner ended without any particular incident, and they cleaned up after finishing the meal.</p>

<p>Returning to his room, Han-pan turned his game back on and immersed himself in it for a while.</p>

<p>A moment later, another knock was heard.</p>

<p>It was So-jo who opened the door and walked in.</p>

<p>“Why?”</p>

<p>“Eonni and Mom are having a drink. Do you want to join?”</p>

<p>A sly smile was plastered across her lips.</p>

<p>It was clear she was plotting some kind of scheme.</p>

<p>Han-pan turned off the computer and stood up obediently.</p>

<p>Snatching the arm of the girl walking ahead of him, Han-pan asked,</p>

<p>“What am I supposed to do?”</p>

<p>At his words, the smile on So-jo’s lips brightened even further.</p>

<p>“Don't say a word and just do exactly as I tell you... They’re already a bit tipsy... You know how eonni is similar to Mom and gets bold when she’s drunk, right? hoho.”</p>

<p>After giving that brief hint, she turned around and headed to the living room.</p>

<p>In front of Seo-ah and Miho, who were sitting on the living room sofa, were already emptied wine bottles, cheese, and a few types of fruit anju.</p>

<p>Judging by their relaxed postures, they seemed quite drunk already.</p>

<p>Han-pan approached them as if nothing were out of the ordinary.</p>

<p>“Are you staying over, nuna?”</p>

<p>“Huh? Well... So-jo suggested we have a drink... It’s nice to have a drink with the family after such a long time... hoho.”</p>

<p>Seeing her looking much more relaxed than the tension from earlier, Han-pan’s heart also felt at ease.</p>

<p>“My son... sit next to Mom...”</p>

<p>Judging by how flushed her face was, she seemed to have already drunk quite a bit.</p>

<p>“Now, let's clink! To our family’s peace and love!!”</p>

<p>“To us!!”</p>

<p>“Phew, that’s good!”</p>

<p>“By the way, eonni... anyway, you’ve basically understood that issue now, right?”</p>

<p>“Huh?”</p>

<p>So-jo shifted the topic to her personal problem, and as her issue was brought up, she hesitated, her expression troubled as she pondered how to answer.</p>

<p>Perhaps because she was drunk, her expression was quite loose, and Han-pan was curious about what she would say.</p>

<p>“Kim So-jo, don't say it!”</p>
