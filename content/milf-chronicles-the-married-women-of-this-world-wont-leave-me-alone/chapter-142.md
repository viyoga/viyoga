<p>Ju Seong-jin turned his head to look at the people around him and spoke.<br>
“Hey everyone, the CEO-nim of Saemmul Bio is here... come say hello...”<br>
At Ju Seong-jin's words, the industry people who were with the group surrounded her warmly and introduced themselves one by one.<br>
And so, they exchanged greetings.<br>
Soon, the flow of conversation shifted to the brand she had launched and Miho's business.<br>
Naturally, the two women became the center of the conversation.<br>
Feeling bored on the sidelines, Han-pan looked around.<br>
It seemed Hwang Yu-hui-nim's daughter was feeling just as bored.<br>
Noticing her expression, he stealthily approached her.<br>
Then, he whispered into her ear.<br>
“Hey... this adult talk is boring. Shall we go somewhere else?”<br>
Byeon Han-hui flinched, startled not only by how he had crept up beside her unnoticed but also by the way he whispered right against her ear.<br>
However, his suggestion was quite tempting.<br>
First of all, she wanted to be somewhere else.<br>
For someone like her, who found mingling with strangers awkward, a party like this wasn't a very pleasant place, and she had been feeling bored for a while.<br>
Yet, at his sudden proposal, she turned her head with a cold expression and spoke in a somewhat irritated tone.<br>
“Excuse me, since when have we known each other well enough for you to speak so informally?”<br>
He felt a bit awkward at her annoyed tone and said,<br>
“Well, we're the same age, so what's the harm in speaking comfortably?”<br>
She made a pouting face.<br>
“I don't drop honorifics with people I've just met.”<br>
Han-pan found her prickly reaction amusing.<br>
Her haughty air made him think she was a sheltered woman who didn't know the ways of the world.<br>
Undeterred by her cold response, Han-pan said slyly,<br>
“Don't be like that... let's leave the adult talk behind and have a conversation between people our own age.”<br>
As he spoke, he suddenly grabbed her hand and gently pulled her away.<br>
Hwang Yu-hui-nim, who was beside her, immediately noticed the two of them leaving.<br>
However, she just watched her without saying anything.<br>
Byeon Han-hui turned her head to look at her mother, who gave a small nod.<br>
Seeing her mother's expression, Byeon Han-hui followed him as if she had no choice.<br>
Han-pan looked at Miho as he left.<br>
She was so preoccupied with talking about herself to the people that she wasn't paying him any mind.<br>
With a smirk, Han-pan held Byeon Han-hui's hand and moved toward the bar.<br>
Sitting down abruptly on a chair at the bar table, Han-pan looked at her and asked,<br>
“Can you drink well?”<br>
At his question, she crossed her arms and said,<br>
“You know exactly who my mother is, don't you?”<br>
Han-pan gave a broad smile and nodded.<br>
“And yet you're asking me to drink?”<br>
“It seemed like she came here in a different capacity today. Aren't you the same?”<br>
“What?”<br>
“Just have a drink.”<br>
He turned to order a drink from the bartender but turned back when he realized he had to drive later.<br>
He remembered that his role today was clearly to escort the two women safely home, and he had to be faithful to that duty first.<br>
Of course, there was no need to imagine what would happen after they got home, but since a good time was surely waiting, he thought it was important to stay sober and said,<br>
“Ah, right... I can't drink today either...”<br>
Dumbfounded by his words, she frowned.<br>
“What? You brought me here when you can't even drink yourself?”<br>
Han-pan felt that her inability to hide her emotions was a stark contrast to her mother.<br>
“Well, non-alcoholic should be fine, right?”<br>
He turned to the bartender and asked if there were any non-alcoholic drinks; the bartender smiled and poured him a non-alcoholic beer before looking at Byeon Han-hui.<br>
After hesitating for a moment, she nodded and sat down beside him.<br>
As she did, her skirt rode up slightly, revealing her thighs.<br>
Han-pan thought that although she was on the slender side, she had quite a nice pair of legs.<br>
Quickly catching his gaze lingering on her legs, she pulled her skirt down and scowled at him.<br>
“Your attitude toward a lady is a bit vulgar.”<br>
Han-pan let out a dry laugh at her words.<br>
“It's not vulgar, it's a compliment. You've got a great body.”<br>
She was somewhat surprised by his blatant praise of her figure.<br>
It was because she had never met anyone who told her so directly that she had a good body.<br>
Since she was young, Han-hui had grown up receiving a lot of attention from the congregation under her father, who held the position of senior pastor.<br>
She was used to being the center of attention, so it was only natural that she was arrogant.<br>
His direct expression of interest was quite foreign to her.<br>
Especially since he felt so different from the scrawny men at church, the impact of a man with such a fine physique was considerable.<br>
To her, the interest of men was all too natural.<br>
In fact, she boasted an outstanding appearance, having properly inherited the genes of Hwang Yu-hui-nim rather than Byeon Gap-tae.<br>
She resembled Kim Chae-won from the popular girl group Le SseraX, with a cute face and short hair that suited her perfectly.<br>
Usually, when she acted cold, men would get flustered or back off, but he was different, and that piqued her curiosity significantly.<br>
“You're pretty. I like you.”<br>
At his subsequent words, she felt her face flush despite herself.<br>
Looking at her reddened face, Han-pan could tell she was a complete amateur when it came to romance.<br>
He found her incredibly cute.<br>
He held out his phone and said,<br>
“Let's eat together tomorrow.”<br>
She was flustered by his sudden proposal and agonized over what to do.<br>
“I have to go to church tomorrow.”<br>
“Fine, then let's eat after the service!”<br>
“...”<br>
He pushed his phone forward again, urging her once more.<br>
“Put your number in.”<br>
She was conflicted.<br>
Since she had never been on a proper date before, she was torn by this man's forward approach.<br>
Han-pan took her hand and placed it on top of the phone.<br>
“There... just press the numbers...”<br>
Han-hui didn't like how he treated her like a child, but she was impressed by how he took the lead like an adult despite being the same age.<br>
His confident appearance was pleasant to see.<br>
She slowly typed in her number and pressed the call button.<br>
When her phone rang a moment later, she took it out of her clutch bag, checked his number, and pressed the end-call button.<br>
“Kim Han-pan.”<br>
She looked up at him.<br>
“That's my name. Save it.”<br>
“I know.”<br>
She saved the number with his name somewhat irritably.<br>
Han-pan watched her actions with a grin.<br>
She was being quite difficult, but he liked that feisty side of her.<br>
After meeting women who were ripe for sex and skilled in romance, meeting such an innocent girl felt quite fresh.<br>
After saving his number, she drank the non-alcoholic beer in front of her and looked at him.<br>
Her expression seemed to ask, "What's next?"<br>
Han-pan quickly read her expression and spoke.<br>
“What do you like?”<br>
Byeon Han-hui consistently disliked his informal way of speaking when they weren't even close.<br>
“Excuse me, but you've been speaking informally this whole time... it's a bit much...”<br>
At her words, he laughed cheerfully and said,<br>
“You're speaking informally too.”<br>
“That's because you're doing it!”<br>
Han-pan widened his eyes and said,<br>
“So, do you hate it?”<br>
“It's unrefined... you shouldn't speak informally to someone you've just met.”<br>
“I guess the informal speech upset you... then I'll apologize! Should I start using honorifics now?”<br>
She looked at him as if he were absurd and said,<br>
“You're doing everything yourself... you're already using it, so what's the point of honorifics now...”<br>
Han-pan could feel that even though she was speaking sharply, she was definitely interested in him.<br>
“Good, then we're eating after the service tomorrow! It's a promise!”<br>
He held out his pinky, and after looking at it, she sighed and hooked her finger with his.<br>
“Great! Lunch with a stunning woman tomorrow!”<br>
Byeon Han-hui didn't dislike his slyness.<br>
But she didn't exactly like it either.<br>
While she liked his mysterious confidence and boldness, his unrefined attitude didn't sit well with her at all.<br>
“What do you like? Let's go somewhere you enjoy.”<br>
“A man should figure that out on his own!”<br>
She realized her mistake as soon as the words left her mouth.<br>
It was as if she were admitting she viewed him as a man.<br>
Han-pan gave a broad grin and said,<br>
“Ah, right... you're right... fine... I'll take care of everything tomorrow!”<br>
Just then, sensing someone approaching, Han-pan turned his head.<br>
Hwang Yu-hui-nim was walking toward them.<br>
She was wearing a faint smile as she looked at them.<br>
Han-pan's gaze naturally drifted toward the skirt she was wearing.</p>
