<p>Ju Seong-jin turned his head to look at the people around him and spoke.</p>

<p>“Hey everyone, the CEO-nim of Saemmul Bio is here... come say hello...”</p>

<p>At Ju Seong-jin's words, the industry people who were with the group surrounded her warmly and introduced themselves one by one.</p>

<p>And so, they exchanged greetings.</p>

<p>Soon, the flow of conversation shifted to the brand she had launched and Miho's business.</p>

<p>Naturally, the two women became the center of the conversation.</p>

<p>Feeling bored on the sidelines, Han-pan looked around.</p>

<p>It seemed Hwang Yu-hui-nim's daughter was feeling just as bored.</p>

<p>Noticing her expression, he stealthily approached her.</p>

<p>Then, he whispered into her ear.</p>

<p>“Hey... this adult talk is boring. Shall we go somewhere else?”</p>

<p>Byeon Han-hui flinched, startled not only by how he had crept up beside her unnoticed but also by the way he whispered right against her ear.</p>

<p>However, his suggestion was quite tempting.</p>

<p>First of all, she wanted to be somewhere else.</p>

<p>For someone like her, who found mingling with strangers awkward, a party like this wasn't a very pleasant place, and she had been feeling bored for a while.</p>

<p>Yet, at his sudden proposal, she turned her head with a cold expression and spoke in a somewhat irritated tone.</p>

<p>“Excuse me, since when have we known each other well enough for you to speak so informally?”</p>

<p>He felt a bit awkward at her annoyed tone and said,</p>

<p>“Well, we're the same age, so what's the harm in speaking comfortably?”</p>

<p>She made a pouting face.</p>

<p>“I don't drop honorifics with people I've just met.”</p>

<p>Han-pan found her prickly reaction amusing.</p>

<p>Her haughty air made him think she was a sheltered woman who didn't know the ways of the world.</p>

<p>Undeterred by her cold response, Han-pan said slyly,</p>

<p>“Don't be like that... let's leave the adult talk behind and have a conversation between people our own age.”</p>

<p>As he spoke, he suddenly grabbed her hand and gently pulled her away.</p>

<p>Hwang Yu-hui-nim, who was beside her, immediately noticed the two of them leaving.</p>

<p>However, she just watched her without saying anything.</p>

<p>Byeon Han-hui turned her head to look at her mother, who gave a small nod.</p>

<p>Seeing her mother's expression, Byeon Han-hui followed him as if she had no choice.</p>

<p>Han-pan looked at Miho as he left.</p>

<p>She was so preoccupied with talking about herself to the people that she wasn't paying him any mind.</p>

<p>With a smirk, Han-pan held Byeon Han-hui's hand and moved toward the bar.</p>

<p>Sitting down abruptly on a chair at the bar table, Han-pan looked at her and asked,</p>

<p>“Can you drink well?”</p>

<p>At his question, she crossed her arms and said,</p>

<p>“You know exactly who my mother is, don't you?”</p>

<p>Han-pan gave a broad smile and nodded.</p>

<p>“And yet you're asking me to drink?”</p>

<p>“It seemed like she came here in a different capacity today. Aren't you the same?”</p>

<p>“What?”</p>

<p>“Just have a drink.”</p>

<p>He turned to order a drink from the bartender but turned back when he realized he had to drive later.</p>

<p>He remembered that his role today was clearly to escort the two women safely home, and he had to be faithful to that duty first.</p>

<p>Of course, there was no need to imagine what would happen after they got home, but since a good time was surely waiting, he thought it was important to stay sober and said,</p>

<p>“Ah, right... I can't drink today either...”</p>

<p>Dumbfounded by his words, she frowned.</p>

<p>“What? You brought me here when you can't even drink yourself?”</p>

<p>Han-pan felt that her inability to hide her emotions was a stark contrast to her mother.</p>

<p>“Well, non-alcoholic should be fine, right?”</p>

<p>He turned to the bartender and asked if there were any non-alcoholic drinks; the bartender smiled and poured him a non-alcoholic beer before looking at Byeon Han-hui.</p>

<p>After hesitating for a moment, she nodded and sat down beside him.</p>

<p>As she did, her skirt rode up slightly, revealing her thighs.</p>

<p>Han-pan thought that although she was on the slender side, she had quite a nice pair of legs.</p>

<p>Quickly catching his gaze lingering on her legs, she pulled her skirt down and scowled at him.</p>

<p>“Your attitude toward a lady is a bit vulgar.”</p>

<p>Han-pan let out a dry laugh at her words.</p>

<p>“It's not vulgar, it's a compliment. You've got a great body.”</p>

<p>She was somewhat surprised by his blatant praise of her figure.</p>

<p>It was because she had never met anyone who told her so directly that she had a good body.</p>

<p>Since she was young, Han-hui had grown up receiving a lot of attention from the congregation under her father, who held the position of senior pastor.</p>

<p>She was used to being the center of attention, so it was only natural that she was arrogant.</p>

<p>His direct expression of interest was quite foreign to her.</p>

<p>Especially since he felt so different from the scrawny men at church, the impact of a man with such a fine physique was considerable.</p>

<p>To her, the interest of men was all too natural.</p>

<p>In fact, she boasted an outstanding appearance, having properly inherited the genes of Hwang Yu-hui-nim rather than Byeon Gap-tae.</p>

<p>She resembled Kim Chae-won from the popular girl group Le SseraX, with a cute face and short hair that suited her perfectly.</p>

<p>Usually, when she acted cold, men would get flustered or back off, but he was different, and that piqued her curiosity significantly.</p>

<p>“You're pretty. I like you.”</p>

<p>At his subsequent words, she felt her face flush despite herself.</p>

<p>Looking at her reddened face, Han-pan could tell she was a complete amateur when it came to romance.</p>

<p>He found her incredibly cute.</p>

<p>He held out his phone and said,</p>

<p>“Let's eat together tomorrow.”</p>

<p>She was flustered by his sudden proposal and agonized over what to do.</p>

<p>“I have to go to church tomorrow.”</p>

<p>“Fine, then let's eat after the service!”</p>

<p>“...”</p>

<p>He pushed his phone forward again, urging her once more.</p>

<p>“Put your number in.”</p>

<p>She was conflicted.</p>

<p>Since she had never been on a proper date before, she was torn by this man's forward approach.</p>

<p>Han-pan took her hand and placed it on top of the phone.</p>

<p>“There... just press the numbers...”</p>

<p>Han-hui didn't like how he treated her like a child, but she was impressed by how he took the lead like an adult despite being the same age.</p>

<p>His confident appearance was pleasant to see.</p>

<p>She slowly typed in her number and pressed the call button.</p>

<p>When her phone rang a moment later, she took it out of her clutch bag, checked his number, and pressed the end-call button.</p>

<p>“Kim Han-pan.”</p>

<p>She looked up at him.</p>

<p>“That's my name. Save it.”</p>

<p>“I know.”</p>

<p>She saved the number with his name somewhat irritably.</p>

<p>Han-pan watched her actions with a grin.</p>

<p>She was being quite difficult, but he liked that feisty side of her.</p>

<p>After meeting women who were ripe for sex and skilled in romance, meeting such an innocent girl felt quite fresh.</p>

<p>After saving his number, she drank the non-alcoholic beer in front of her and looked at him.</p>

<p>Her expression seemed to ask, "What's next?"</p>

<p>Han-pan quickly read her expression and spoke.</p>

<p>“What do you like?”</p>

<p>Byeon Han-hui consistently disliked his informal way of speaking when they weren't even close.</p>

<p>“Excuse me, but you've been speaking informally this whole time... it's a bit much...”</p>

<p>At her words, he laughed cheerfully and said,</p>

<p>“You're speaking informally too.”</p>

<p>“That's because you're doing it!”</p>

<p>Han-pan widened his eyes and said,</p>

<p>“So, do you hate it?”</p>

<p>“It's unrefined... you shouldn't speak informally to someone you've just met.”</p>

<p>“I guess the informal speech upset you... then I'll apologize! Should I start using honorifics now?”</p>

<p>She looked at him as if he were absurd and said,</p>

<p>“You're doing everything yourself... you're already using it, so what's the point of honorifics now...”</p>

<p>Han-pan could feel that even though she was speaking sharply, she was definitely interested in him.</p>

<p>“Good, then we're eating after the service tomorrow! It's a promise!”</p>

<p>He held out his pinky, and after looking at it, she sighed and hooked her finger with his.</p>

<p>“Great! Lunch with a stunning woman tomorrow!”</p>

<p>Byeon Han-hui didn't dislike his slyness.</p>

<p>But she didn't exactly like it either.</p>

<p>While she liked his mysterious confidence and boldness, his unrefined attitude didn't sit well with her at all.</p>

<p>“What do you like? Let's go somewhere you enjoy.”</p>

<p>“A man should figure that out on his own!”</p>

<p>She realized her mistake as soon as the words left her mouth.</p>

<p>It was as if she were admitting she viewed him as a man.</p>

<p>Han-pan gave a broad grin and said,</p>

<p>“Ah, right... you're right... fine... I'll take care of everything tomorrow!”</p>

<p>Just then, sensing someone approaching, Han-pan turned his head.</p>

<p>Hwang Yu-hui-nim was walking toward them.</p>

<p>She was wearing a faint smile as she looked at them.</p>

<p>Han-pan's gaze naturally drifted toward the skirt she was wearing.</p>
