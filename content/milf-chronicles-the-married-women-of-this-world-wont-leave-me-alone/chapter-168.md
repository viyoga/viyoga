<p>Miho looked over each of the new employees with a calm expression.<br>
In keeping with the company's image, everyone possessed clean appearances and sophisticated attire.<br>
However, one among them was a bit different.<br>
Unlike the other new recruits who had polished looks, he was a man who gave off the impression of someone who had done nothing but study.<br>
Thinking that a rather peculiar person had joined the company, she tilted her head slightly.<br>
Miho was certain there must have been some kind of mistake in the HR department.<br>
She soon flashed a bright smile and spoke.<br>
“Now then, everyone, I hope you learn diligently for our company, produce results, and definitely succeed as dealers. Alright! Everyone, fighting!”<br>
Miho smiled brightly and showed her fist.<br>
Hong Jin-woo thought she was someone with a somewhat eccentric side.<br>
She then requested a handshake from each of the new employees.<br>
“Please take good care of our company.”<br>
“I’m looking forward to it. My expectations are high!”<br>
Greeting them one by one, Miho finally reached out her hand toward Hong Jin-woo, who was standing at the end.<br>
Tense, Hong Jin-woo bowed his head 90 degrees the moment she extended her hand and grabbed it.<br>
“I look forward to working with you.”<br>
“I am the one who looks forward to working with you!!”<br>
He spoke as if he were shouting at the top of his lungs.<br>
Miho’s sensitive ears felt like they were ringing, so she frowned slightly before smiling and saying,<br>
“You’re quite spirited… I should be the one asking for your best efforts.”<br>
After offering a smile, she nodded toward Team Leader Park.<br>
Team Leader Park gave her a respectful bow, and the new employees followed suit, bowing their heads before exiting Miho’s room.</p>

<p>Afterward, Team Leader Park took the new employees around the building, explaining various areas.<br>
Hong Jin-woo found it incredibly fascinating and heart-fluttering to see the supercars he had only seen in videos or photos with his own eyes.<br>
As a car otaku in his own right, his gaze sparkled as he looked at the displayed vehicles.<br>
After the tour ended, they returned to the sales department and were assigned their respective mentors.<br>
The person who became Hong Jin-woo’s mentor was a woman named Kim Ha-na, who appeared to be in her late twenties.<br>
She had a considerably glamorous style; her look was so striking that it seemed one had to be this flamboyant to be a dealer.<br>
Her face, framed by long hair styled in sophisticated curls, was that of a typical beautiful woman, where a calm yet kind smile suited her perfectly.<br>
Her large eyes, sharp nose, and plump ipsul were incredibly attractive.<br>
She overflowed with such sophistication that he could say she was the most beautiful woman he had ever met.<br>
He thought she suited the supercars perfectly.<br>
She was wearing a fairly tight, pale pink skirt that reached her knees and a slightly transparent beige shirt, through which her bra seemed to peek through.<br>
He quickly turned his head, worried that she might catch his gaze lingering on her breasts.<br>
As he lowered his gaze, he saw the intense pink high heels she was wearing.<br>
The pointed toes and the high, very thin heels gave off a precarious, provocative feeling.<br>
She approached Hong Jin-woo with a bright smile, handed him his employee ID, and spoke.<br>
“Let’s do our best. Hong Jin-woo-ssi, I am Assistant Manager Kim Ha-na, and I’ve been put in charge of your education this time. I look forward to working with you.”<br>
She looked at him with a beaming smile and extended her hand.<br>
Hong Jin-woo, wearing an expression as if he were receiving a great honor, grabbed her extended hand with both of his.<br>
“I look forward to working with you even more, Assistant Manager Kim Ha-na-nim.”</p>

<p>“Team Leader Park, can you come to my room for a moment?”<br>
“Yes, I understand.”<br>
Upon receiving the call on his cellphone from Miho, Team Leader Park immediately headed to her room, opened the door, and gave a respectful greeting.<br>
When he knocked and opened the door, Miho, who had been looking at her monitor screen, turned her head to look at him.<br>
“Did you call for me?”<br>
“Yes, Team Leader Park-nim. It’s nothing else, but there’s a rather questionable fellow among the new employees who joined the sales team this time…”<br>
Team Leader Park immediately realized which person she was pointing out.<br>
“Ah, yes… it seems the HR department hired him experimentally this time.”<br>
“Experimentally?”<br>
“Well… they judged that there might be customers who feel burdened because we are too flashy, so they thought having a pure friend like him might provide a somewhat fresh stimulation to the customers… He’s a smart fellow, and from what I know, he left a good impression on the HR team during the interview because he has incredibly vast knowledge about cars.”<br>
“Hmm…”<br>
Miho crossed her arms and sank into thought.<br>
“…”<br>
Team Leader Park worried that the HR department had made a mistake.<br>
Although he had basically provided the idea, it was the HR department that made the choice and picked him.<br>
Just as he was agonizing over what kind of excuse to make, Miho clapped her palms together and smiled brightly.<br>
“Fine, well… new attempts are always good.”<br>
After letting out a sigh of relief inwardly, he spoke.<br>
“Yes.”<br>
“However… it’s true that he’s a friend who needs caution. We can’t have him doing anything rude to our customers, so please mark him well.”<br>
He bowed his head and said,<br>
“I’ve already attached a capable person to him.”<br>
“Yes, since it’s Team Leader Park, I trust there won’t be any problems.”<br>
“Yes, I understand.”<br>
Seeing Miho turn her head back to the monitor, he bowed slightly in greeting and left the room.</p>

<p>Returning to the sales department, Team Leader Park called Assistant Manager Kim Ha-na aside.<br>
“What do you think?”<br>
“Pardon?”<br>
“The newcomer.”<br>
Kim Ha-na rolled her eyeballs and spoke.<br>
“He doesn’t really seem to fit in with us… but… well… he seems kind and naive? If he gets caught by a wicked client, he’ll be torn to shreds, won’t he?”<br>
“I suppose so?”<br>
“Please take good care of him.”<br>
“Eh… well… alright… But what was HR thinking, hiring a kid like that?”<br>
“Ah… actually, this was partly my idea… I thought it might be good to have one otaku-like guy like that… Since there are many young friends these days who have made a lot of money in IT, I think if we educate a friend like him well, we can make good use of him.”<br>
“Hmm, yes, I understand.”<br>
She bowed her head and returned to where Hong Jin-woo was.<br>
As she approached, Hong Jin-woo was buried in the company manual he had just received, peering into it.<br>
Sensing someone appearing beside him, he lifted his head and looked.<br>
“Jin-woo-ssi, shall we go to work then?”<br>
“Ah, y-yes!”<br>
He stood up from his seat, looking tense.<br>
The two stepped out into the showroom.<br>
She informed him about the specs of the cars inside the showroom and the parts he needed to explain when meeting customers.<br>
“For about a week, you’ll have to memorize the specs of all the cars we sell… Customers are often quite curious about the specs.”<br>
At her words, he stared blankly at her for a moment before looking at the car in front of them and speaking.<br>
“Uh… this car is the DB12, the representative model of Aston Martin, which boasts a 111-year history since its founding in 1913. It has a length of 4,775mm, a width of 1,980mm, a height of 1,295mm, a curb weight of 1,788kg, and a zero-to-hundred time of 3.6 seconds.”<br>
Kim Ha-na was momentarily dazed by the story that poured out like a burst dam.<br>
Looking at her, he pushed up his glasses and spoke confidently.<br>
“And the engine is a 4.0L V8 twin-turbo with a displacement of 3,982cc, and it’s an outstanding sports car with a maximum output of 680 horsepower and an 8-speed transmission. What’s noteworthy is that with the transition to the DB12, Aston Martin moved past its previous collaboration with Benz and manufactured it independently, boasting a superior interior.”<br>
As he quickly added the specs to the talk about the car without missing a single syllable, Kim Ha-na looked at him with surprised eyes.<br>
“Uh… that’s right… you know it well.”<br>
“Ah, yes… I like cars quite a lot… so I’ve memorized these things from seeing them often, hahaha.”<br>
“Hmm, that’s good… Then what kind of car is this?”<br>
She pointed to another car displayed next to it.<br>
He spoke with an expression as if it were nothing.<br>
“That is the Mercedes-Benz G-Wagon AMG G 63.”<br>
“Ho, you know it well. What about the specs?”<br>
“3,982cc, 577 horsepower, zero-to-hundred in 4.5 seconds, and it began its first production in 1979, continuing to this day. Its history is rooted in the 1937 Benz G5, which was manufactured as a military vehicle during World War II.”<br>
Watching him continue speaking rapidly, Kim Ha-na’s eyes widened.<br>
She began to think that someone quite extraordinary had come under her wing.<br>
“Hmm… that’s amazing… You really are knowledgeable…”<br>
“Hahaha, they said the same thing during the interview. Thank you.”<br>
“Then do you have the specs of all the cars here memorized?”<br>
He nodded his head.<br>
“Of course. I was so excited when I found out there was a place like this in Seoul… Actually, I received job offers from other places too, but I liked this place more, so I applied. Of course, I’m not good at being a dealer yet, so I ask for your kind guidance.”<br>
He bowed his head to Kim Ha-na.<br>
For a moment, Kim Ha-na thought he was incredibly cute.<br>
His unique, untainted innocence felt like a charming allure to her.</p>
