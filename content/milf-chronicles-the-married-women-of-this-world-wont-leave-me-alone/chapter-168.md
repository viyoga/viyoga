<p>Miho looked over each of the new employees with a calm expression.</p>

<p>In keeping with the company's image, everyone possessed clean appearances and sophisticated attire.</p>

<p>However, one among them was a bit different.</p>

<p>Unlike the other new recruits who had polished looks, he was a man who gave off the impression of someone who had done nothing but study.</p>

<p>Thinking that a rather peculiar person had joined the company, she tilted her head slightly.</p>

<p>Miho was certain there must have been some kind of mistake in the HR department.</p>

<p>She soon flashed a bright smile and spoke.</p>

<p>“Now then, everyone, I hope you learn diligently for our company, produce results, and definitely succeed as dealers. Alright! Everyone, fighting!”</p>

<p>Miho smiled brightly and showed her fist.</p>

<p>Hong Jin-woo thought she was someone with a somewhat eccentric side.</p>

<p>She then requested a handshake from each of the new employees.</p>

<p>“Please take good care of our company.”</p>

<p>“I’m looking forward to it. My expectations are high!”</p>

<p>Greeting them one by one, Miho finally reached out her hand toward Hong Jin-woo, who was standing at the end.</p>

<p>Tense, Hong Jin-woo bowed his head 90 degrees the moment she extended her hand and grabbed it.</p>

<p>“I look forward to working with you.”</p>

<p>“I am the one who looks forward to working with you!!”</p>

<p>He spoke as if he were shouting at the top of his lungs.</p>

<p>Miho’s sensitive ears felt like they were ringing, so she frowned slightly before smiling and saying,</p>

<p>“You’re quite spirited… I should be the one asking for your best efforts.”</p>

<p>After offering a smile, she nodded toward Team Leader Park.</p>

<p>Team Leader Park gave her a respectful bow, and the new employees followed suit, bowing their heads before exiting Miho’s room.</p>

<p>Afterward, Team Leader Park took the new employees around the building, explaining various areas.</p>

<p>Hong Jin-woo found it incredibly fascinating and heart-fluttering to see the supercars he had only seen in videos or photos with his own eyes.</p>

<p>As a car otaku in his own right, his gaze sparkled as he looked at the displayed vehicles.</p>

<p>After the tour ended, they returned to the sales department and were assigned their respective mentors.</p>

<p>The person who became Hong Jin-woo’s mentor was a woman named Kim Ha-na, who appeared to be in her late twenties.</p>

<p>She had a considerably glamorous style; her look was so striking that it seemed one had to be this flamboyant to be a dealer.</p>

<p>Her face, framed by long hair styled in sophisticated curls, was that of a typical beautiful woman, where a calm yet kind smile suited her perfectly.</p>

<p>Her large eyes, sharp nose, and plump ipsul were incredibly attractive.</p>

<p>She overflowed with such sophistication that he could say she was the most beautiful woman he had ever met.</p>

<p>He thought she suited the supercars perfectly.</p>

<p>She was wearing a fairly tight, pale pink skirt that reached her knees and a slightly transparent beige shirt, through which her bra seemed to peek through.</p>

<p>He quickly turned his head, worried that she might catch his gaze lingering on her breasts.</p>

<p>As he lowered his gaze, he saw the intense pink high heels she was wearing.</p>

<p>The pointed toes and the high, very thin heels gave off a precarious, provocative feeling.</p>

<p>She approached Hong Jin-woo with a bright smile, handed him his employee ID, and spoke.</p>

<p>“Let’s do our best. Hong Jin-woo-ssi, I am Assistant Manager Kim Ha-na, and I’ve been put in charge of your education this time. I look forward to working with you.”</p>

<p>She looked at him with a beaming smile and extended her hand.</p>

<p>Hong Jin-woo, wearing an expression as if he were receiving a great honor, grabbed her extended hand with both of his.</p>

<p>“I look forward to working with you even more, Assistant Manager Kim Ha-na-nim.”</p>

<p>“Team Leader Park, can you come to my room for a moment?”</p>

<p>“Yes, I understand.”</p>

<p>Upon receiving the call on his cellphone from Miho, Team Leader Park immediately headed to her room, opened the door, and gave a respectful greeting.</p>

<p>When he knocked and opened the door, Miho, who had been looking at her monitor screen, turned her head to look at him.</p>

<p>“Did you call for me?”</p>

<p>“Yes, Team Leader Park-nim. It’s nothing else, but there’s a rather questionable fellow among the new employees who joined the sales team this time…”</p>

<p>Team Leader Park immediately realized which person she was pointing out.</p>

<p>“Ah, yes… it seems the HR department hired him experimentally this time.”</p>

<p>“Experimentally?”</p>

<p>“Well… they judged that there might be customers who feel burdened because we are too flashy, so they thought having a pure friend like him might provide a somewhat fresh stimulation to the customers… He’s a smart fellow, and from what I know, he left a good impression on the HR team during the interview because he has incredibly vast knowledge about cars.”</p>

<p>“Hmm…”</p>

<p>Miho crossed her arms and sank into thought.</p>

<p>“…”</p>

<p>Team Leader Park worried that the HR department had made a mistake.</p>

<p>Although he had basically provided the idea, it was the HR department that made the choice and picked him.</p>

<p>Just as he was agonizing over what kind of excuse to make, Miho clapped her palms together and smiled brightly.</p>

<p>“Fine, well… new attempts are always good.”</p>

<p>After letting out a sigh of relief inwardly, he spoke.</p>

<p>“Yes.”</p>

<p>“However… it’s true that he’s a friend who needs caution. We can’t have him doing anything rude to our customers, so please mark him well.”</p>

<p>He bowed his head and said,</p>

<p>“I’ve already attached a capable person to him.”</p>

<p>“Yes, since it’s Team Leader Park, I trust there won’t be any problems.”</p>

<p>“Yes, I understand.”</p>

<p>Seeing Miho turn her head back to the monitor, he bowed slightly in greeting and left the room.</p>

<p>Returning to the sales department, Team Leader Park called Assistant Manager Kim Ha-na aside.</p>

<p>“What do you think?”</p>

<p>“Pardon?”</p>

<p>“The newcomer.”</p>

<p>Kim Ha-na rolled her eyeballs and spoke.</p>

<p>“He doesn’t really seem to fit in with us… but… well… he seems kind and naive? If he gets caught by a wicked client, he’ll be torn to shreds, won’t he?”</p>

<p>“I suppose so?”</p>

<p>“Please take good care of him.”</p>

<p>“Eh… well… alright… But what was HR thinking, hiring a kid like that?”</p>

<p>“Ah… actually, this was partly my idea… I thought it might be good to have one otaku-like guy like that… Since there are many young friends these days who have made a lot of money in IT, I think if we educate a friend like him well, we can make good use of him.”</p>

<p>“Hmm, yes, I understand.”</p>

<p>She bowed her head and returned to where Hong Jin-woo was.</p>

<p>As she approached, Hong Jin-woo was buried in the company manual he had just received, peering into it.</p>

<p>Sensing someone appearing beside him, he lifted his head and looked.</p>

<p>“Jin-woo-ssi, shall we go to work then?”</p>

<p>“Ah, y-yes!”</p>

<p>He stood up from his seat, looking tense.</p>

<p>The two stepped out into the showroom.</p>

<p>She informed him about the specs of the cars inside the showroom and the parts he needed to explain when meeting customers.</p>

<p>“For about a week, you’ll have to memorize the specs of all the cars we sell… Customers are often quite curious about the specs.”</p>

<p>At her words, he stared blankly at her for a moment before looking at the car in front of them and speaking.</p>

<p>“Uh… this car is the DB12, the representative model of Aston Martin, which boasts a 111-year history since its founding in 1913. It has a length of 4,775mm, a width of 1,980mm, a height of 1,295mm, a curb weight of 1,788kg, and a zero-to-hundred time of 3.6 seconds.”</p>

<p>Kim Ha-na was momentarily dazed by the story that poured out like a burst dam.</p>

<p>Looking at her, he pushed up his glasses and spoke confidently.</p>

<p>“And the engine is a 4.0L V8 twin-turbo with a displacement of 3,982cc, and it’s an outstanding sports car with a maximum output of 680 horsepower and an 8-speed transmission. What’s noteworthy is that with the transition to the DB12, Aston Martin moved past its previous collaboration with Benz and manufactured it independently, boasting a superior interior.”</p>

<p>As he quickly added the specs to the talk about the car without missing a single syllable, Kim Ha-na looked at him with surprised eyes.</p>

<p>“Uh… that’s right… you know it well.”</p>

<p>“Ah, yes… I like cars quite a lot… so I’ve memorized these things from seeing them often, hahaha.”</p>

<p>“Hmm, that’s good… Then what kind of car is this?”</p>

<p>She pointed to another car displayed next to it.</p>

<p>He spoke with an expression as if it were nothing.</p>

<p>“That is the Mercedes-Benz G-Wagon AMG G 63.”</p>

<p>“Ho, you know it well. What about the specs?”</p>

<p>“3,982cc, 577 horsepower, zero-to-hundred in 4.5 seconds, and it began its first production in 1979, continuing to this day. Its history is rooted in the 1937 Benz G5, which was manufactured as a military vehicle during World War II.”</p>

<p>Watching him continue speaking rapidly, Kim Ha-na’s eyes widened.</p>

<p>She began to think that someone quite extraordinary had come under her wing.</p>

<p>“Hmm… that’s amazing… You really are knowledgeable…”</p>

<p>“Hahaha, they said the same thing during the interview. Thank you.”</p>

<p>“Then do you have the specs of all the cars here memorized?”</p>

<p>He nodded his head.</p>

<p>“Of course. I was so excited when I found out there was a place like this in Seoul… Actually, I received job offers from other places too, but I liked this place more, so I applied. Of course, I’m not good at being a dealer yet, so I ask for your kind guidance.”</p>

<p>He bowed his head to Kim Ha-na.</p>

<p>For a moment, Kim Ha-na thought he was incredibly cute.</p>

<p>His unique, untainted innocence felt like a charming allure to her.</p>
