<p>Surrounded by a group of people, Hwang Yu-hui was conversing with those around her, wearing a smile that made her unique, brilliant expression impossible to read.<br>
Han-pan tilted his head, wondering why such an unexpected person was here.<br>
It felt surreal to see her, a pastor's wife, mingling at a lavish party in such extravagant attire.<br>
Han-pan scanned her slowly.<br>
She wore a violet two-piece suit that exuded her unique sense of luxury.<br>
She was the epitome of a sophisticated, upper-class noble lady.<br>
Her aura was quite different from Mi-ho-nim, who could be a bit of a klutz; Yu-hui had a distinct, impressive elegance in this atmosphere.<br>
Today, she wore black-rimmed glasses, which strongly emphasized her intellectual vibe.<br>
Her hair was pulled up in a neat French bun, and long, sparkling earrings dangled from her exposed ears, shimmering under the lights.<br>
The necklace around her long, slender neck was thin but looked just as expensive as the earrings.<br>
Her makeup was bolder than when he’d seen her at church, but it suited her perfectly.<br>
The added touch of sexiness she didn't show at church felt fresh.<br>
Most notably, her lips, painted in a striking red lipstick, stood out.<br>
No one would ever guess she was a woman married to a pastor.<br>
She wore a long, jacket-style top that slightly revealed her waist, accented by large gold buttons that looked incredibly high-end—definitely a luxury European brand.<br>
Below, she wore a matching long skirt that tightly hugged her waist, accentuating her sleek waistline.<br>
Despite being a plain H-line skirt, the way her voluminous buttocks made the fabric bulge was incredibly sexy.<br>
It was just long enough to cover her knees, but that very concealment stimulated an inexplicable curiosity.<br>
Her style was a sharp contrast to Mi-ho-nim or So-jo.<br>
Though most of her skin was covered, she possessed a mysterious allure that drew people in.<br>
Though her attire could be described as something one might see at church, it was glamorous enough for the venue and blended in perfectly with the crowd.<br>
Finally, his eyes landed on her feet, which were clad in high, pink kill heels adorned with extravagant decorations.<br>
The lovely light-pink high heels, combined with their height, made her presence feel superior.<br>
She was like a swan among crows.</p>

<p>As Han-pan stared intensely at her, Hwang Yu-hui noticed.<br>
She turned her head toward him, and her expression flickered with surprise upon seeing him.<br>
However, she quickly composed herself and gave him a bright smile and a nod.<br>
Han-pan, realizing he might have been staring too hard, scratched his head with a grin and nodded back.<br>
Mi-ho-nim, seeing Han-pan nod, looked in that direction and her eyes widened in surprise when she spotted Hwang Yu-hui.<br>
Yu-hui nodded to Mi-ho-nim, who managed a smile and a deep nod in return.<br>
Han-pan leaned in and whispered in her ear.<br>
「There's someone unexpected here.」<br>
Mi-ho-nim turned and whispered back.<br>
「You're right... what is the Samo-nim doing in a place like this...?」<br>
While they were whispering, Yu-hui excused herself from her group and approached them.<br>
Mi-ho-nim turned toward her warmly.<br>
「Oh my, Samo-nim...」<br>
「Hello, Mi-ho-ssi.」<br>
They bowed slightly in greeting.<br>
「I'm here today as the CEO of Holy Rod Bio-Industry... so please, drop the 'Samo-nim' title.」<br>
Mi-ho-nim smiled and said,<br>
「Oh, is that so? I understand. Then... CEO Hwang!?」<br>
「It feels a bit awkward being called CEO by Sister Mi-ho... I suppose I should call you CEO Park as well? Hoho!! It’s quite fresh seeing you out here after only seeing you at church.」<br>
The two exchanged small talk.<br>
Just then, someone approached Hwang Yu-hui.<br>
She was a great beauty.<br>
「Mom! What are you doing? Let's go home now!」<br>
Han-pan stared at the woman who had just called Hwang Yu-hui "Mom."<br>
She looked to be in her early twenties, likely around his age.<br>
Her short cut, just below the ears, suited her perfectly, and her silver-dyed hair was particularly eye-catching.<br>
He liked her large eyes and the clear gaze within them; her sharp, small nose, sleek jawline, and full lips were very impressive.<br>
She was also wearing high heels, so there was hardly any height difference between her and Hwang Yu-hui.<br>
Compared to Mi-ho-nim, who was wearing 14cm platform heels, she seemed to be in the mid-160s in height, and her slender figure gave off a very pure vibe.<br>
Compared to the glamorous and dignified Yu-hui, she was almost plain.<br>
She wore a luxury dress, but since it wasn't form-fitting, he couldn't discern her figure.<br>
Still, like her mother, she had an air of luxury and the look of a typical rich man's daughter who had been well-educated.<br>
Compared to her brother, Associate Pastor Byeon Aram, she seemed much more like a tomboy.<br>
Yu-hui looked slightly flustered by her daughter's sudden appearance but quickly regained her composure.<br>
「Han-hui-ya, Mom is in the middle of a conversation with this person. You shouldn't speak like that so suddenly!」<br>
She then pointed to Mi-ho-nim.<br>
「And this person is someone important to Mom. Say hello... This is CEO Park Mi-ho, a member of our church.」<br>
She showed some wariness at seeing a stranger, but soon gave a slight smile and bowed to Mi-ho-nim.<br>
「Hello... I apologize for earlier...」<br>
「And this is her son, Brother Kim Han-pan...」<br>
After seeing Han-pan, she nodded again, and Han-pan bowed his head with a smile.<br>
「Hello.」<br>
Yu-hui asked Han-pan,<br>
「Brother Han-pan, how old are you?」<br>
「Ah, I'm 22.」<br>
「Oh my, you're the same age as our Han-hui... You two should be friends.」<br>
Han-pan felt a strange interest in her upon learning they were the same age.<br>
Han-hui turned her head away as if his presence was burdensome.<br>
「It's good to see two people the same age... hoho.」<br>
Mi-ho-nim laughed brightly.<br>
「By the way, CEO Hwang, what brings you to a place like this?」<br>
Yu-hui replied calmly,<br>
「Well, it's for work... Our company is expanding into the cosmetics business, so I have a lot of interest in beauty... It's good for business to get to know various CEOs.」<br>
「Oh, is that so?」<br>
Just then, Ju Seong-jin approached them with a laugh.<br>
「Hahaha, there are so many beauties gathered here. CEO Park, who is this lady?」<br>
Han-pan could see deep interest in Hwang Yu-hui on Ju Seong-jin's face.<br>
「Oh my, CEO Ju... calling us beauties... you're too kind... how embarrassing... hohoho.」<br>
Mi-ho-nim laughed, welcoming his presence.<br>
「Uh... this is Hwang Yu-hui, the CEO of Holy Rod Bio-Industry. And this is her daughter...」<br>
Hwang Yu-hui and Byeon Han-hui greeted Ju Seong-jin politely.<br>
Yu-hui said,<br>
「You're CEO Ju Seong-jin, right? It's a pleasure to meet you. I'm Hwang Yu-hui, the CEO of Holy Rod Bio-Industry, as introduced.」<br>
「Ah, I see... Isn't that the company with the cosmetics brand 'Eline'?」<br>
「Oh, you know it well... It's an honor that the CEO of the industry leader knows the products of a small company like ours.」<br>
He looked at her with surprised eyes.<br>
「Oh! I've already heard a lot about Holy Rod Bio and the Eline product line... You're a new company, but you produce many solid, high-quality products that have been getting a lot of attention in the cosmetics market lately... Oh... this is a pleasure.」<br>
He reached out his hand, and the two shook hands firmly.<br>
Han-pan could instinctively see a glint in Hwang Yu-hui's eyes.<br>
He realized that she was no ordinary pastor's wife.</p>
