<p>Surrounded by a group of people, Hwang Yu-hui was conversing with those around her, wearing a smile that made her unique, brilliant expression impossible to read.</p>

<p>Han-pan tilted his head, wondering why such an unexpected person was here.</p>

<p>It felt surreal to see her, a pastor's wife, mingling at a lavish party in such extravagant attire.</p>

<p>Han-pan scanned her slowly.</p>

<p>She wore a violet two-piece suit that exuded her unique sense of luxury.</p>

<p>She was the epitome of a sophisticated, upper-class noble lady.</p>

<p>Her aura was quite different from Mi-ho-nim, who could be a bit of a klutz; Yu-hui had a distinct, impressive elegance in this atmosphere.</p>

<p>Today, she wore black-rimmed glasses, which strongly emphasized her intellectual vibe.</p>

<p>Her hair was pulled up in a neat French bun, and long, sparkling earrings dangled from her exposed ears, shimmering under the lights.</p>

<p>The necklace around her long, slender neck was thin but looked just as expensive as the earrings.</p>

<p>Her makeup was bolder than when he’d seen her at church, but it suited her perfectly.</p>

<p>The added touch of sexiness she didn't show at church felt fresh.</p>

<p>Most notably, her lips, painted in a striking red lipstick, stood out.</p>

<p>No one would ever guess she was a woman married to a pastor.</p>

<p>She wore a long, jacket-style top that slightly revealed her waist, accented by large gold buttons that looked incredibly high-end—definitely a luxury European brand.</p>

<p>Below, she wore a matching long skirt that tightly hugged her waist, accentuating her sleek waistline.</p>

<p>Despite being a plain H-line skirt, the way her voluminous buttocks made the fabric bulge was incredibly sexy.</p>

<p>It was just long enough to cover her knees, but that very concealment stimulated an inexplicable curiosity.</p>

<p>Her style was a sharp contrast to Mi-ho-nim or So-jo.</p>

<p>Though most of her skin was covered, she possessed a mysterious allure that drew people in.</p>

<p>Though her attire could be described as something one might see at church, it was glamorous enough for the venue and blended in perfectly with the crowd.</p>

<p>Finally, his eyes landed on her feet, which were clad in high, pink kill heels adorned with extravagant decorations.</p>

<p>The lovely light-pink high heels, combined with their height, made her presence feel superior.</p>

<p>She was like a swan among crows.</p>

<p>As Han-pan stared intensely at her, Hwang Yu-hui noticed.</p>

<p>She turned her head toward him, and her expression flickered with surprise upon seeing him.</p>

<p>However, she quickly composed herself and gave him a bright smile and a nod.</p>

<p>Han-pan, realizing he might have been staring too hard, scratched his head with a grin and nodded back.</p>

<p>Mi-ho-nim, seeing Han-pan nod, looked in that direction and her eyes widened in surprise when she spotted Hwang Yu-hui.</p>

<p>Yu-hui nodded to Mi-ho-nim, who managed a smile and a deep nod in return.</p>

<p>Han-pan leaned in and whispered in her ear.</p>

<p>"There's someone unexpected here."</p>

<p>Mi-ho-nim turned and whispered back.</p>

<p>"You're right... what is the Samo-nim doing in a place like this...?"</p>

<p>While they were whispering, Yu-hui excused herself from her group and approached them.</p>

<p>Mi-ho-nim turned toward her warmly.</p>

<p>"Oh my, Samo-nim..."</p>

<p>"Hello, Mi-ho-ssi."</p>

<p>They bowed slightly in greeting.</p>

<p>"I'm here today as the CEO of Holy Rod Bio-Industry... so please, drop the 'Samo-nim' title."</p>

<p>Mi-ho-nim smiled and said,</p>

<p>"Oh, is that so? I understand. Then... CEO Hwang!?"</p>

<p>"It feels a bit awkward being called CEO by Sister Mi-ho... I suppose I should call you CEO Park as well? Hoho!! It’s quite fresh seeing you out here after only seeing you at church."</p>

<p>The two exchanged small talk.</p>

<p>Just then, someone approached Hwang Yu-hui.</p>

<p>She was a great beauty.</p>

<p>"Mom! What are you doing? Let's go home now!"</p>

<p>Han-pan stared at the woman who had just called Hwang Yu-hui "Mom."</p>

<p>She looked to be in her early twenties, likely around his age.</p>

<p>Her short cut, just below the ears, suited her perfectly, and her silver-dyed hair was particularly eye-catching.</p>

<p>He liked her large eyes and the clear gaze within them; her sharp, small nose, sleek jawline, and full lips were very impressive.</p>

<p>She was also wearing high heels, so there was hardly any height difference between her and Hwang Yu-hui.</p>

<p>Compared to Mi-ho-nim, who was wearing 14cm platform heels, she seemed to be in the mid-160s in height, and her slender figure gave off a very pure vibe.</p>

<p>Compared to the glamorous and dignified Yu-hui, she was almost plain.</p>

<p>She wore a luxury dress, but since it wasn't form-fitting, he couldn't discern her figure.</p>

<p>Still, like her mother, she had an air of luxury and the look of a typical rich man's daughter who had been well-educated.</p>

<p>Compared to her brother, Associate Pastor Byeon Aram, she seemed much more like a tomboy.</p>

<p>Yu-hui looked slightly flustered by her daughter's sudden appearance but quickly regained her composure.</p>

<p>"Han-hui-ya, Mom is in the middle of a conversation with this person. You shouldn't speak like that so suddenly!"</p>

<p>She then pointed to Mi-ho-nim.</p>

<p>"And this person is someone important to Mom. Say hello... This is CEO Park Mi-ho, a member of our church."</p>

<p>She showed some wariness at seeing a stranger, but soon gave a slight smile and bowed to Mi-ho-nim.</p>

<p>"Hello... I apologize for earlier..."</p>

<p>"And this is her son, Brother Kim Han-pan..."</p>

<p>After seeing Han-pan, she nodded again, and Han-pan bowed his head with a smile.</p>

<p>"Hello."</p>

<p>Yu-hui asked Han-pan,</p>

<p>"Brother Han-pan, how old are you?"</p>

<p>"Ah, I'm 22."</p>

<p>"Oh my, you're the same age as our Han-hui... You two should be friends."</p>

<p>Han-pan felt a strange interest in her upon learning they were the same age.</p>

<p>Han-hui turned her head away as if his presence was burdensome.</p>

<p>"It's good to see two people the same age... hoho."</p>

<p>Mi-ho-nim laughed brightly.</p>

<p>"By the way, CEO Hwang, what brings you to a place like this?"</p>

<p>Yu-hui replied calmly,</p>

<p>"Well, it's for work... Our company is expanding into the cosmetics business, so I have a lot of interest in beauty... It's good for business to get to know various CEOs."</p>

<p>"Oh, is that so?"</p>

<p>Just then, Ju Seong-jin approached them with a laugh.</p>

<p>"Hahaha, there are so many beauties gathered here. CEO Park, who is this lady?"</p>

<p>Han-pan could see deep interest in Hwang Yu-hui on Ju Seong-jin's face.</p>

<p>"Oh my, CEO Ju... calling us beauties... you're too kind... how embarrassing... hohoho."</p>

<p>Mi-ho-nim laughed, welcoming his presence.</p>

<p>"Uh... this is Hwang Yu-hui, the CEO of Holy Rod Bio-Industry. And this is her daughter..."</p>

<p>Hwang Yu-hui and Byeon Han-hui greeted Ju Seong-jin politely.</p>

<p>Yu-hui said,</p>

<p>"You're CEO Ju Seong-jin, right? It's a pleasure to meet you. I'm Hwang Yu-hui, the CEO of Holy Rod Bio-Industry, as introduced."</p>

<p>"Ah, I see... Isn't that the company with the cosmetics brand 'Eline'?"</p>

<p>"Oh, you know it well... It's an honor that the CEO of the industry leader knows the products of a small company like ours."</p>

<p>He looked at her with surprised eyes.</p>

<p>"Oh! I've already heard a lot about Holy Rod Bio and the Eline product line... You're a new company, but you produce many solid, high-quality products that have been getting a lot of attention in the cosmetics market lately... Oh... this is a pleasure."</p>

<p>He reached out his hand, and the two shook hands firmly.</p>

<p>Han-pan could instinctively see a glint in Hwang Yu-hui's eyes.</p>

<p>He realized that she was no ordinary pastor's wife.</p>
