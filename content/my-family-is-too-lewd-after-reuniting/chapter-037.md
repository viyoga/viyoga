<p>“Yes, Hunter Yoon Seong-hyeon’s identity verification is complete. You may enter through that door.”</p><p>As expected, the headquarters was absolute chaos.</p><p>After finishing the identity verification process, I entered the Hunter Association’s temporary operations control room.</p><p>Inside, it was packed with countless Hunters.</p><p>Some were clutching their heads and muttering to themselves, others stood expressionless, and a few were snickering.</p><p>At a glance, there had to be at least fifty people.</p><p>Just then, a man stepped onto the podium.</p><p>The noisy atmosphere inside instantly fell silent.</p><p>The man on the podium was Han Ji-hyeok, the Hunter Association President and an S-rank Hunter.</p><p>With his white hair and beard, he had the dignified presence of a stout, gentlemanly figure.</p><p>In truth, this was the first time I’d seen him in person since the Hunter Training Academy graduation ceremony.</p><p>Han Ji-hyeok stroked his beard a few times before speaking into the microphone.</p><p>“First, I want to sincerely thank all the Hunters who have gathered here in response to the mobilization order.”</p><p>“That bastard drags us here and now he’s acting like we came on our own…”</p><p>A man next to me grumbled under his breath.</p><p>“I will explain the current situation to you with complete honesty. Team Leader Seo Su-yeon.”</p><p>At Han Ji-hyeok’s nod, a woman stepped onto the podium.</p><p>Soon, a large screen displayed information about the Gate, and the briefing began.</p><p>“I am Operations Team Leader Seo Su-yeon. Thirty-six days ago, an S-rank Gate was discovered. Thirty-eight hours ago, the advance team was deployed. And seven hours ago, we lost contact with them.”</p><p>Seo Su-yeon’s briefing was horrifying.</p><p>The initial reconnaissance team had consisted of one S-rank Hunter, thirty A-rank Hunters, and forty-three B-rank Hunters—all of whom had gone dark.</p><p>South Korea only had seven S-rank Hunters in total, four of whom were affiliated with the Association.</p><p>One was Association President Han Ji-hyeok himself—a figure who represented the entire Korean Hunter Association, a man who was practically the institution itself. If he entered this Gate, it would publicly confirm that the Association was a useless organization that couldn’t function without its president.</p><p>Another was a Hunter who was effectively retired, only listed under the Association due to personal ties with the president.</p><p>A third occasionally visited Korea, cleared a few A-rank Gates, pocketed a fortune, then vanished abroad to squander it all before returning to Korea once the money ran out. I’d heard he wasn’t in the country right now.</p><p>And the last one… the only S-rank Hunter actively working for the Association—‘Choi Seo-jin’—was the leader of the advance team that had gone silent inside the Gate.</p><p>In other words, our second reconnaissance team… had to enter an S-rank Gate without a single S-rank Hunter.</p><p>Sighs and complaints erupted from all directions.</p><p>The second team consisted of seventeen A-rank Hunters, twenty-one B-rank Hunters, and seven C-rank Hunters who had already been confirmed for promotion to B-rank.</p><p>Apparently, those arriving after us would be organized into a third reconnaissance team.</p><p>The only silver lining was that, since this was an improvised team, the objective of this exploration wasn’t to take down the boss but to rescue the advance party.</p><p>If we found the advance party while exploring the Gate, and the situation didn’t allow us to continue fighting, we were to immediately retreat. That was the goal of this mission.</p><p>The worst news I’d heard today was… that Hye-na was on the advance party list.</p><p>‘She’ll be fine…’</p><p>Two hours later, we enter the Gate.</p><p>***</p><p>The mood is dire. Some are praying to God, others are on the phone with their families.</p><p>I tried to look for Na-hyeon noona, but in this chaos, it’d be too hard to find her. I gave up quickly.</p><p>We were issued top-tier gear, the kind only S-rank Hunters usually get.</p><p>“Huuu…”</p><p>My instincts are screaming at me, telling me not to step in there.</p><p>“Everyone, our mission is rescue, not exploration. If you follow my orders, we can all return without casualties.”</p><p>A man stood before the Gate. He was the leader of this exploration team, an S-rank Hunter named Lee Seung-chan.</p><p>A Psychic-type Hunter… in other words, a superhuman.</p><p>‘The advance party leader, Choi Seo-jin, was also a Psychic… and now this leader is too. No, don’t read too much into it.’</p><p>“Then, let’s go in. Follow me one by one.”</p><p>Seung-chan must have noticed the grim atmosphere—he forced a smile and stepped through the Gate.</p><p>It was my turn.</p><p>“Huu. I’ll survive. I have to survive. If it gets dangerous, I’ll run without looking back.”</p><p>With that resolve, I stepped through the Gate.</p><p>***</p><p>After entering the Gate, we moved quickly for about four hours, rested for thirty minutes, then resumed our rapid march for several more hours.</p><p>Everyone was on edge, nerves frayed, maintaining a tense silence without exchanging a word.</p><p>The inside of the Gate was grotesquely eerie. It was filled with metaphysical, Lovecraftian horrors—altars and objects that defied reason.</p><p>On the altars were hearts and organs, drenched in blood. The entire area was made of black stone veined with an unsettling purple hue.</p><p>The corpses of monsters littered the ground, proof that the advance party had made it this far without issue.</p><p>Even the monsters’ corpses were bizarre—dozens of eyes, tentacles, all smeared with that same purple blood.</p><p>But there were no bodies from the advance party.</p><p>‘Then what’s with the red blood and organs on the altars?’</p><p>After hours of moving, we hadn’t encountered a single monster.</p><p>That could only mean one thing: the advance party was either struggling in a brutal fight against the boss… or they’d already been wiped out.</p><p>If they were fighting, we should at least hear the sounds of battle, but there was nothing.</p><p>In the heavy silence, ‘Lee Seung-chan’ came to a stop.</p><p>Before us stood a massive wall of smoke.</p><p>“This is…”</p><p>“Haa…”</p><p>“Shit…”</p><p>“Ah… aah…”</p><p>Not just me—every Hunter facing the smoke let out sighs of despair.</p><p>Some even started laughing, too stunned to do anything else.</p><p>I, too, let out a hollow laugh. The situation was far worse than anything I’d imagined.</p><p>Most likely, the boss was waiting inside that smoke.</p><p>That place was probably a completely isolated space, cutting off all communication. And the fact that no one was here… suggested this entrance was one-way.</p><p>We’d probably have to defeat the boss to get out.</p><p>Worse, it seemed like we were the first to discover this smoke.</p><p>‘The advance team was probably dragged in by force. That’s why they couldn’t send a signal.’</p><p>“If we go in, we’re done. Let’s turn back.”</p><p>“I agree. Going in is suicide.”</p><p>Many Hunters refused to enter.</p><p>Lee Seung-chan looked troubled as he spoke.</p><p>“For now… I’ll send a distress signal. The three C-rank Hunters, go back and report this to the Association. Tell them to send a third rescue team immediately. And… we’re going in.”</p><p>The three C-rank Hunters exhaled in relief, as if they’d been spared.</p><p>It wasn’t a wrong call. They wouldn’t be any help here. The priority was getting word out about this situation.</p><p>Lee Seung-chan sighed and pressed the signal button.</p><p>“You want us to go in and die?”</p><p>“Are you joking? You go in if you’re so eager.”</p><p>“This was a rescue mission, right? You’re seriously going in?”</p><p>Lee Seung-chan scanned the Hunters, then spoke with a serious expression.</p><p>“Yes, because we’re professionals. Do your job. No one knows what’s happening in there. It could be as bad as you imagine, but it’s also possible they’re fighting desperately and need reinforcements right now to secure a victory. If you want to leave, I won’t stop you. But you’ll face revocation of your Hunter license and severe penalties—that’s on you. I’m going in. Anyone who wants to leave, do it now. Every second counts.”</p><p>A few seconds of silence followed.</p><p>All the Hunters here were A or B-rank… meaning they knew the power of S-rank Hunters.</p><p>Awakened above the Awakened—monsters so strong you’d question if they even counted as human. That was an S-rank Hunter.</p><p>It was hard to imagine such a freak losing to a boss. The most likely scenario was that they were holding out inside, waiting for backup to deliver the final blow.</p><p>As the silence dragged on, Lee Seung-chan spoke again.</p><p>“Then I assume no one is turning back… I’ll go in first.”</p><p>As Lee Seung-chan entered, the Hunters followed in silence. Everyone, myself included, was trembling, but we suppressed our fear and stepped into the fog to complete the mission.</p><p>***</p><p>BOOM!</p><p>CRACK!</p><p>“This is Rescue Team Leader Lee Seung-chan!”</p><p>“The rescue team is here!”</p><p>“Next attack incoming. Get ready!”</p><p>After walking into the fog, we were transported to a large chamber surrounded by black stone.</p><p>The altars were all made of gold, and atop the largest one sat an indescribably bizarre monster.</p><p>Over a dozen Hunters were locked in combat with the boss, exchanging blows.</p><p>Every time the boss lashed out with its tentacles, the walls crumbled like dust. The sheer wind pressure made the entire room tremble.</p><p>‘That’s the boss…’</p><p>It was a massive, shapeless black blob, writhing as it attacked the Hunters with dozens of tentacles. In the center was a giant eye, and below it, a cylindrical mouth filled with countless saw-like teeth—and deep inside, a red sphere pulsed.</p><p>And… in front of it, at least thirty Hunters lay fallen.</p><p>Some were already dead. Others groaned in agony from their wounds.</p><p>The worst part was… in the corner of the room, Healer-type Hunters were clustered around a blood-soaked S-rank Hunter, Choi Seo-jin.</p><p>She was barely breathing.</p><p>“What the hell happened…”</p><p>Lee Seung-chan muttered in a dazed voice. A Healer-type Hunter answered,</p><p>“There was an enormous attack… Hunter Choi Seo-jin took the full brunt of it with her own body…”</p><p>“Damn…”</p><p>There was no time to waste. After checking the fallen Hunters, I scanned the chaos for one person.</p><p>“Huh?”</p><p>Among the fiercely battling Hunters, I found the person I was looking for.</p><p>‘Hye-na… she’s alive.’</p><p>She was bleeding from multiple wounds and clearly exhausted, but she still stood tall, sword in hand.</p><p>Just then, Lee Seung-chan spoke up.</p><p>“Everyone! Snap out of it! We’re joining the front lines immediately! Support-type Hunters, move the injured to the Healers! All other Hunters, follow me into battle!”</p><p>The one silver lining was that the boss was also covered in cuts and crushed wounds, gushing purple blood like water.</p><p>“Huff… I’m not dying. I promised I’d go back…”</p><p>With my resolve firm, I drew my two knives and charged toward the front lines.</p>
