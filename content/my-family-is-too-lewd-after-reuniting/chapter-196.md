<p>Countless Hunter Association members had gathered in the sparring arena.</p><p>They were whispering among themselves as they looked at me. There was only one reason for it.</p><p>It wasn’t just because of the life-or-death sparring match between a standing committee member and the head of the Intelligence Bureau. They were also staring at my outfit with grave expressions.</p><p>Ahn Yoon-mun, standing in front of me, was holding two daggers and wearing combat gear, but I had come out empty-handed, still in the suit I’d worn to work.</p><p>“You all seem to think I can’t kill you… Apologize now while you still can.”</p><p>Ahn Yoon-mun twirled his dagger as he spoke.</p><p>“Were you always this talkative?”</p><p>“Ah…”</p><p>Ahn Yoon-mun pressed a hand to his forehead as if he had a headache and let out a deep sigh. The match had already begun, so either of us could attack first without issue.</p><p>A massive crowd of Association members had gathered to witness this unprecedented act of madness. The atmosphere was reminiscent of a mixed martial arts stadium. I doubted anyone here actually believed I would win.</p><p>Se-rin had tried to stop me the entire way here, insisting she take my place, but I’d brushed off her concerns, told her it might get brutal, and ordered her to wait outside. Then I’d entered the sparring room.</p><p>Ahn Yoon-mun took his stance and began radiating killing intent.</p><p>‘He really does plan to kill me…’</p><p>I pulled my hand out of my pocket and fixed my gaze on Ahn Yoon-mun. Just as the match was about to begin, the door to the sparring arena swung open, and two people entered.</p><p>“Stop!”</p><p>Han Ji-hyeok burst in, his voice urgent. Beside him, the Intelligence Bureau Chief was staring at me with a serious expression.</p><p>“What the hell is this?! Both of you, stop right now! That’s an order from the Association President!”</p><p>Han Ji-hyeok was clearly furious as he spoke to Ahn Yoon-mun, but Ahn Yoon-mun simply clasped his hands together and bowed politely in response.</p><p>“President, I think there’s been a misunderstanding. I never threatened or coerced him. The committee member requested this match with no strings attached, and I accepted. It’s the truth.”</p><p>“Yes, that’s right.”</p><p>I backed up Ahn Yoon-mun’s explanation, and Han Ji-hyeok stared at me in disbelief.</p><p>“If this was a voluntary agreement between two Hunters, then we have no place to interfere…”</p><p>The Intelligence Bureau Chief muttered, stroking his chin.</p><p>This was probably the exact scenario the Intelligence Bureau Chief had been hoping for. It was obvious what he was after—legally eliminating a standing committee member through a duel, intimidating the other committee members, and seizing control of the election.</p><p>Han Ji-hyeok glared at the Intelligence Bureau Chief, but the man just feigned ignorance, continuing to rub his chin.</p><p>I said to Han Ji-hyeok,</p><p>“President, this is just a mutual agreement to settle a dispute with the Eye Department Chief through a sparring match, as Hunters do. I was absolutely not coerced or anything like that.”</p><p>“I don’t even know anymore…”</p><p>Han Ji-hyeok rubbed his temples as if he had a headache, then walked up the stairs. The Information Bureau Chief followed behind him, wearing a sinister smile.</p><p>“Eye Department Chief? Are we friends? That was your last chance to survive with the President’s mediation, and you’re acting like you’ve lost your mind and want to die?”</p><p>As the tension thickened once more, An Yoon-moon swung his dagger and spoke.</p><p>I simply clapped and laughed at An Yoon-moon’s taunts.</p><p>“What’s so funny?”</p><p>And when I sneered, An Yoon-moon seemed properly provoked—his lips began to tremble. I stopped clapping and said,</p><p>“You talk way too much.”</p><p>“You fucking bastard!”</p><p>BAM!</p><p>An Yoon-moon charged at me in an instant. Even the metal floor of the sparring arena, which he used as a foothold, dented under his force.</p><p>Swish—</p><p>Then, in a flash, An Yoon-moon flew toward me and swung his left arm. I slightly twisted my body to dodge.</p><p>‘Ah.’</p><p>Maybe I’d let my guard down because his attack looked so slow—my tie was slashed by the tip of his dagger and dangled loosely.</p><p>Thinking his strike had landed, An Yoon-moon grinned wickedly and swung his right arm for a follow-up.</p><p>Thud.</p><p>But his confidently swung right wrist was caught by me. Shocked that his attack was blocked, he stared at me with a startled expression.</p><p>“Shit…”</p><p>And deep in my dantian, a surge of rage boiled up.</p><p>It wasn’t because An Yoon-moon had underestimated me. It was because he’d ruined the tie—the most precious gift from the woman I loved more than anything in this world… my wife.</p><p>“This was a gift…”</p><p>“W-what?”</p><p>An Yoon-moon tried to pull his right arm free, but when it wouldn’t budge, he panicked for a moment at the realization that he was being overpowered. But he quickly regained his composure and swung his left arm, aiming to plunge his dagger into my neck.</p><p>I clenched my left fist and drove it straight into An Yoon-moon’s throat.</p><p>CRUNCH—</p><p>“K-KHEHCK!”</p><p>Even though I’d intentionally held back as much as possible, I could still feel the sensation of his neck bones snapping and the muscles in his throat—vocal cords included—tearing apart.</p><p>CLANG!</p><p>“Kheheh! Kehek!”</p><p>Ahn Yoon-mun dropped the daggers in his hands to the floor, then clutched his own throat with one hand and his mouth with the other as he began to stagger.</p><p>His entire body must have been wracked with agony, but with his vocal cords destroyed, he couldn’t even scream.</p><p>He did his best to cover his mouth, but blood started gushing out between Ahn Yoon-mun’s fingers like a fountain.</p><p>The arena, which had been buzzing with noise before the sparring began, fell into complete silence.</p><p>“The necktie… what are you gonna do about it.”</p><p>Crunch.</p><p>“Gwaaaagh!”</p><p>I delivered a low kick to Ahn Yoon-mun, and his leg bone shattered like a cracker. His leg flopped like jelly, and he dropped to his knees.</p><p>Snap.</p><p>“Guh… agh…”</p><p>Then my fist buried itself into Ahn Yoon-mun’s ribs, and the sound of multiple ribs breaking echoed.</p><p>He rolled on the ground, screaming silently.</p><p>Swoosh.</p><p>I picked up the dagger Ahn Yoon-mun had dropped and approached him.</p><p>Even though this unprecedented madness was unfolding live, no one could stop it.</p><p>This duel wouldn’t end until one of us died or someone admitted defeat—and since Ahn Yoon-mun couldn’t make a sound, he couldn’t declare surrender.</p><p>I crouched down beside Ahn Yoon-mun’s head. He stared at me with a face twisted in terror, blood spewing from his mouth and nose.</p><p>It was the kind of look a dog that had spent its whole life thinking it was a beast of prey would make when it finally met a real predator.</p><p>“Nnggwaaah!”</p><p>Still crouched in front of the writhing Ahn Yoon-mun, I grabbed his arm, pressed the dagger to his wrist, and said,</p><p>“A Hunter’s duty is to protect the people from monsters. You shouldn’t be swinging dangerous weapons like this at other humans…”</p><p>Ahn Yoon-mun seemed to realize what I was about to do next, and his entire body began to tremble violently.</p><p>“Hm? Oh… you pissed yourself.”</p><p>Then, as his crotch grew damp, the urine seeped out and mixed with the pool of blood he had vomited.</p><p>“Every time you look at your severed wrist, remember today. Live the rest of your life with your head on straight.”</p><p>“Gwaaagh!”</p><p>Slice.</p><p>“GWAAAAAAH!”</p><p>Slice.</p><p>“Nngh…”</p><p>By the time I had cut halfway through his wrist, Ahn Yoon-mun passed out. A normal person would have died long ago, but as an A-rank Hunter, he had only lost consciousness—he wasn’t dead yet.</p><p>‘If I leave him like this, he’ll die soon.’</p><p>Slice!</p><p>I cut off An Yoon-mun’s left wrist. Blood spurted out like a fountain in all directions.</p><p>Clatter.</p><p>“Huh?”</p><p>The watch An Yoon-mun had been wearing on his severed wrist fell to the floor.</p><p>I picked it up and fastened it onto his right wrist.</p><p>Crunch. Squish.</p><p>Then I stood up, stepped on his right wrist twice, and crushed it completely—watch and all.</p><p>Normally, since he’d tried to kill me, I should have killed him in return. But I decided to show mercy.</p><p>I was someone who might become a father one day, and it didn’t feel right to commit meaningless murder.</p><p>Crunch.</p><p>After stomping on his right wrist one last time, I raised my hand and shouted,</p><p>“I yield.”</p><p>Looking around, about half the people had already fled the sparring arena. Most of those who remained stared at me in terror, but a few watched with fascinated expressions.</p><p>One thing was certain: the Information Bureau Director, who had looked so composed the whole time, was now frowning.</p><p>As I walked away, leaving An Yoon-mun collapsed on the ground, people rushed over to carry him off.</p><p>“Call the medical team, now!”</p><p>Covered in blood, I climbed the stairs. On the second floor, Han Ji-hyeok was staring at me with a look of total resignation, while the Information Bureau Director took a step back when he saw me.</p><p>I walked straight toward him.</p><p>“What the hell are you—”</p><p>Then I wiped my right hand, caked in An Yoon-mun’s blood, on the Director’s suit before offering him a handshake.</p><p>“Director, if you ever need to see me in the future, please contact me directly. I’ll come to the Information Bureau myself.”</p><p>The Director glared at me with a completely twisted expression, but the earlier composure was gone.</p><p>“Stop.”</p><p>Han Ji-hyeok stepped between us and said,</p><p>“That’s enough from both of you. Seong-hyeon—no, Standing Committee Member, come to my office.”</p><p>“Yes.”</p><p>***</p><p>“Was that a bit too much?”</p><p>Since I couldn’t go to the Association President’s office covered in blood after the match, I headed to the shower room to wash off the gore.</p><p>The Association took my bloodstained suit and provided me with a new one.</p><p>I ran into Se-rin outside the shower room. When she saw me covered in blood, she was stunned.</p><p>It wasn’t so much fear as an expression that said, *I knew he was strong, but I didn’t realize it was to this extent.*</p><p>After finishing my shower and changing into a fresh suit, I headed to the Association President’s office.</p><p>“President, may I come in?”</p><p>“Come in.”</p><p>*Squeak*</p><p>As I entered the office, Nerus, seated on the sofa and sipping tea, spoke up.</p><p>“Seong-hyeon, showing mercy to that insect… I was moved by your compassion.”</p><p>“Huh? Did you see it too, Nerus-nim?”</p><p>“I watched from the shadows so the other humans wouldn’t notice.”</p><p>“Seong-hyeon.”</p><p>“Yes, President.”</p><p>“You’re probably not curious, but… the Chief Physician is still alive.”</p><p>“That’s a relief.”</p><p>“Maybe I should’ve just let you kill him.”</p><p>“Huh?”</p><p>Han Ji-hyeok let out a deep sigh before continuing.</p><p>“According to the doctors, his left hand can’t be reattached. They’ll try surgery on his right hand and left leg, but even if it succeeds, he’ll never move the same way again.”</p><p>“···.”</p><p>“On top of that, his vocal cords are severely damaged—irreparable. One lung is shredded from broken ribs, so they’re just removing it. Even if he miraculously recovers, he’ll spend the rest of his life in a wheelchair. With one hand gone and the other too weak to function, he’ll need an electric one.”</p><p>If he’d been a regular person, he would’ve died instantly when his ribs were crushed. But as an A-rank Hunter, he’d somehow survived. It was fortunate I hadn’t taken his life.</p><p>I couldn’t tell from Han Ji-hyeok’s expression whether he was scolding me or just sharing his honest thoughts.</p><p>“That insect reeked of something foul. Who knows how many innocent lives he ruined for his own pleasure…”</p><p>As Nerus muttered, Han Ji-hyeok approached me and patted my shoulder.</p><p>“You’ve been through a lot… This was my failure to anticipate. Still, if something like this happens again, report to me first…”</p><p>“Yes.”</p><p>After exchanging greetings with Han Ji-hyeok, I left the headquarters and took a car home. Even as I walked out, I could feel every Association member sneaking glances at me.</p><p>“Now I’m really famous… Whatever. I’ll just go with it. I don’t even know anymore.”</p>
