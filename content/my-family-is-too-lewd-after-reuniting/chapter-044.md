<p>“It’s nearby, so just finish the exploration and contact me when you’re out.”</p><p>“Yes.”</p><p>Kang Hye-na arrived in front of the D-rank Gate.</p><p>Since this was an unofficial solo exploration, the Hunter Association employee didn’t even reveal their name.</p><p>They only handed over her usual equipment, three days’ worth of food and water, basic medical supplies, and two pages of A4-sized information about the Gate before disappearing.</p><p>‘Such a purely businesslike attitude…’</p><p>Thanks to Hye-na’s influence, the employee seemed quite seasoned. Normally, someone like this wouldn’t be involved in a D-rank Gate operation.</p><p>‘Hmm… a D-rank Gate with grasslands and forests around it…’</p><p>There weren’t many unusual structures—mostly natural terrain, a typical D-rank Gate.</p><p>Since it was an unofficial exploration, the Gate must have been discovered recently.</p><p>“Well… let’s go in.”</p><p>She stepped into the shimmering blue light.</p><p>“Hmm…”</p><p>Before her stretched a grassland and a dense forest. As if guiding her forward, a rough brick path cut through the landscape.</p><p>“The stench of monsters is already hitting me hard.”</p><p>This exploration was the perfect chance to test the Armband’s capabilities to the fullest.</p><p>She set down her bag and stretched.</p><p>“Let’s try running first.”</p><p>Whoosh—</p><p>Her body felt as light as a feather. She was sprinting across the grassland at a speed that was incomparable to before.</p><p>‘As expected…’</p><p>She had anticipated some physical enhancement, but it wasn’t just her imagination—it was real.</p><p>Even while running at this incredible speed, she could change direction smoothly.</p><p>No, actually, her mind struggled to keep up with her now-overpowered body, making her hesitate mid-motion.</p><p>The most surprising part was that even after more than 30 minutes of sprinting back and forth at breakneck speed, she didn’t feel the slightest fatigue.</p><p>‘Running’s good enough for now…’</p><p>She took off all her armor and stuffed it into her bag.</p><p>It was too cumbersome and got in the way of movement. Instinctively, she realized her body was far tougher and sturdier than any armor.</p><p>“Let’s head into the forest…”</p><p>***</p><p>“Grrrr…”</p><p>About 30 minutes after entering the forest, she encountered her first monster.</p><p>At a glance, it was a boar over six meters long.</p><p>Its black bristles flickered like flames, and it stomped its front hooves as if ready to charge at any moment.</p><p>“What should I do first?”</p><p>She planned to experiment as much as possible without equipping the gauntlet, sticking to just the Armband.</p><p>She wanted to test whether there was a difference in strength between her right hand, which wore the Armband, and her left.</p><p>‘Let’s start with the left hand.’</p><p>“Krrr!”</p><p>The boar monster charges at me. </p><p>“Huh.”</p><p>Of course, even before, I hadn’t found the attacks of D-rank Gate monsters particularly threatening, but now they seem so pathetic it’s laughable.</p><p>It’s like I can see exactly where and how it’s going to attack.</p><p>I clench my fists and take my stance.</p><p>Once the monster enters my range, I plan to swing my left fist with all my strength and smash its head in.</p><p>The beast steps into my range. I brace my legs, twist my hips, and swing.</p><p>KWAAAANG—!!!</p><p>‘?!’</p><p>“Huh? Wha—?”</p><p>The monster dies without so much as a death cry.</p><p>“What… the hell.”</p><p>An unbelievable sight unfolds before my eyes.</p><p>The boar monster’s head is completely pulverized and torn off, flying through the air.</p><p>From the headless body, blood sprays like a fountain.</p><p>‘It’s not cut off…’</p><p>Looking closely at the severed cross-section, the head wasn’t cleanly sliced—it was ripped off by the sheer force of the impact.</p><p>“Whoa…”</p><p>I plop down in front of the monster’s corpse, rest my chin on my hand, and sink into thought.</p><p>‘I *did* think I might be able to kill it in one hit before swinging… but I didn’t expect the head to get *ripped* off. This is way too strong…’</p><p>I stand up, clench my right fist, and punch the headless torso.</p><p>THUD—!</p><p>“Hah…”</p><p>The torso splits clean in half, sending guts and blood splattering onto me.</p><p>“This is ridiculous…”</p><p>The performance is beyond what I imagined. From what I can tell, when the Armband is active, the strength in my right and left hands seems equal.</p><p>Fortunately, I’ve figured out something else after smashing a few trees—if I don’t have the clear intent to attack, my strength stays about the same as before.</p><p>In other words, power control is *very* easy.</p><p>‘Good thing my family’s just regular people… at least I don’t have to worry about accidentally crushing them.’</p><p>I pull a towel from my bag, wet it, and wipe the blood off my face.</p><p>‘Now, what’s next…’</p><p>***</p><p>“KUOOOOH!!!”</p><p>After sprinting and clearing trash mobs for about an hour, a much stronger-looking monster appears before me.</p><p>“Maybe a mid-boss…”</p><p>It’s the same kind of boar monster as the first one, but its body is far more muscular, and it exhales flames with every breath.</p><p>‘This should work.’</p><p>“Equip.”</p><p>CLANK!</p><p>Time to test the gauntlet’s performance for the first time.</p><p>“Hm?”</p><p>The golden lines running along the gauntlet at regular intervals seem to glow brighter than when I first saw them.</p><p>‘Must be my imagination.’</p><p>“KUOOO!!!”</p><p>The monster just stood in place, roaring, with no sign of charging at me.</p><p>Even though it was a low-intelligence, purely beast-type monster that would usually lunge at any opponent, strong or not, it cowered and refused to approach me.</p><p>When I took a few steps forward, it flinched and began to back away.</p><p>‘No way.’</p><p>Every time I stepped closer, the monster roared louder, slamming its massive front paws into the ground with a thud.</p><p>And yet, it didn’t feel threatening at all. That roar wasn’t a battle cry—it was a plea to avoid a fight.</p><p>“No way… You… You’re scared of me?”</p><p>I wasn’t sure if it was afraid of the gauntlet on my right arm or of me personally, but one thing was clear: it was terrified.</p><p>My strengthened body filled me with exhilaration.</p><p>‘No need to hold back.’</p><p>I pushed off with my legs and lunged forward in a single burst, swinging my right fist toward the monster’s snout with all my strength.</p><p>BOOM! RUMBLE!</p><p>A deafening explosion rang out, and the impact nearly stole my breath.</p><p>“Huuu…”</p><p>After swinging my gauntlet-clad fist, I felt a slight drain on my strength.</p><p>“Haa…”</p><p>Once I caught my breath and looked up, an unbelievable sight unfolded before me.</p><p>“….”</p><p>The monster’s body had vanished without a trace. Behind where it had stood was a straight-line crater hundreds of meters long.</p><p>All the trees were flattened, and the ground was gouged out as if a meteor had streaked through in a perfect line.</p><p>“What the hell… is this…?”</p><p>“Deactivate.”</p><p>Click.</p><p>I deactivated the gauntlet, rubbed my eyes, stretched, and opened them again.</p><p>“Huh…”</p><p>I had no idea just how strong I’d become.</p><p>‘High-tier A-rank? Maybe even… S-rank? …I don’t know.’</p><p>Of course, compared to those who had survived countless life-or-death battles, my combat sense and experience were far inferior. But in terms of pure physical ability, I might be on par with them.</p><p>“Haa.”</p><p>Looking at the devastated forest, I let out a sigh.</p><p>‘What do I do now?’</p><p>***</p><p>“Phew. This should be enough, right?”</p><p>I sat in front of the flickering escape gate, chewing on some rations.</p><p>After testing the gauntlet’s performance, I took out a few monsters bare-handed, and before long, I encountered the boss.</p><p>I fought the boss with a dagger, and from the moment we faced each other to the second its head rolled off the blade and hit the ground, less than five seconds had passed.</p><p>Clearing a D-rank gate solo hadn’t even taken half a day.</p><p>It was so boring it made me sigh.</p><p>The problem was the craters left behind from swinging the gauntlet.</p><p>After I left, the recovery team would come in and file a report. They’d definitely mention the craters.</p><p>Not wanting to draw unnecessary suspicion, I killed the boss monster but didn’t leave the gate immediately. Instead, I wandered around the forest with the gauntlet on, destroying random patches of land.</p><p>I wanted it to look like the gate had always been in a ruined state.</p><p>It was lucky that this was an unexamined gate.</p><p>And if I left before sunset, it would look suspicious, so I decided to spend the night here and leave tomorrow morning.</p><p>My plan was to casually mention to the staff tomorrow that the D-rank gate had been surprisingly easy.</p><p>I lit a campfire in front of the exit gate and stared at the bubbling kettle on top, lost in thought.</p><p>‘What do I do now…?’</p><p>Becoming absurdly strong was great, but the fact that I had to hide this power gave me a headache.</p><p>Should I pretend to be moderately stronger, or should I leave the Association? I couldn’t decide.</p><p>There were two realistic options.</p><p>The first was to report to the Association. Of course, I’d omit mentioning the artifact. I could say something like, *Well, there was something in front of me, so I drank it, but I don’t remember anything after that, and my physical abilities seem a little stronger now.* Cases like this weren’t common, but they did happen. Sometimes, rare items that enhanced physical abilities were found in S or A-rank gates.</p><p>Whether they’d buy it or not was uncertain, but it was the most realistic and safe approach.</p><p>The second was to leave the Association and join a private company. With the help of some senior acquaintances, it wouldn’t be difficult. I could hide my strength, clear C-rank gates, and live comfortably on a high salary.</p><p>Since private companies were fundamentally in competition with the Association, even if they noticed something odd about me, they’d likely cover for me.</p><p>But now that I had the ambition to aim higher… going private didn’t appeal to me.</p><p>“Haa…”</p><p>No matter how I thought about it, there was no perfect solution. I sighed at the realization.</p><p>Tomorrow, after leaving the gate, I had to go to headquarters and spar with Hye-na.</p><p>And after that… I had to spend the night with Na-hyeon.</p><p>“So busy. Too busy.”</p><p>I tossed a few more branches into the fire, laid out a blanket, and stretched out.</p><p>If I forced myself to close my eyes, I might be able to sleep. Then again, I felt like I could go days without sleep and still be fine.</p><p>‘Is this really me?’</p><p>My body felt so different from before. Was it just healed and strengthened, or had I become something inhuman the moment I put on the gauntlet? Or had my body been completely replaced? My mind was a mess.</p><p>“Just go to sleep.”</p><p>Eventually, I shook off my worries and closed my eyes.</p>
