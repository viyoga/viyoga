<p>Three weeks had passed since Min Se-rin and I had our sparring match.</p><p>Even Yoon Na-hyeon was coming home on weekends now, probably because things at the Hunter Association had settled down.</p><p>But even then, she didn’t come straight home on weekends. Every Friday, once her work ended, I’d get a text from her.</p><p>-XX Motel, Room XX.</p><p>When I got that message, I’d throw on some clothes and head out.</p><p>Seong Yu-na must have already coordinated schedules with her, because Yu-na never asked where I was going.</p><p>By the time I arrived at the motel on Friday, Na-hyeon noona would be waiting—exhausted from the week’s grueling work, her skin a little dull.</p><p>The first week, we’d eaten dinner before heading to the motel, but starting the next week, we just ordered delivery to the room.</p><p>After we ate together, we’d spend the night in a frenzy of sex, and by morning, Na-hyeon noona’s dull skin would be nowhere in sight—her face glowing, radiant. She’d get dressed, leave me with a “I’ll be back in a few hours~,” and disappear.</p><p>I’d decided to take pride in my role.</p><p>‘Maybe I’m a stud *and* a sex therapist, faithfully performing my duties.’</p><p>Other than that, my daily life was no different. I spent all day lying in bed, scrolling on my phone, agonizing over what car to buy.</p><p>‘I’ve got plenty of money… should I flex with an imported car? Or play it safe and get a Korean one to avoid the hate…’</p><p>I’d be researching domestic cars, then suddenly press my phone to my chest, close my eyes, and mutter under my breath,</p><p>“Who am I? A man with 1.1 billion won in cash.”</p><p>Since I’d bought the house, meals were always on time, I had my own room, and no matter how I thought about it, there was nowhere to spend money.</p><p>The only place I *had* spent money was installing an air conditioner in my room. Of course, I didn’t stop there—I put wall-mounted units in Yu-na’s, Na-hyeon’s, and Ji-hyeon’s rooms too, and replaced the ancient, rattling AC in the living room with a brand-new model.</p><p>Yu-na had refused at first, but I forced the purchase through.</p><p>I never thought I’d be the kind of person to say, “Huh? An AC is only a few million won…”</p><p>Yoon Ji-hyeon seemed to look up to me a little more now. Her attitude had definitely softened since the hospital incident.</p><p>I’d tried to give Yu-na 50 million won for living expenses, but she’d refused so stubbornly that I’d ended up not giving it to her.</p><p>Fifty million won just for living expenses…</p><p>‘I’m really rich now, huh.’</p><p>Ever since I became a Hunter, my dream had been to save up enough to buy a big house in the quieter outskirts of the city.</p><p>‘Part of why I saved money when I lived alone… but now, I think it’d be nice if the whole family could live together in a big house… and with a big house, I wouldn’t have to worry about noise from neighbors or thin walls…’</p><p>With high-paying jobs on the horizon, it wasn’t just a pipe dream anymore.</p><p>I didn’t need to grind through E and D-rank requests in bulk to save money like I used to.</p><p>‘Maybe I should focus on a small, elite team and tackle B-rank Gates… At my level, failure isn’t even a concern. And even if we *did* fail, I’m confident I could get the team out safely… If I had… say, 50 billion won, I could live peacefully in a two-story house…’</p><p>After the sparring match with Min Se-rin, I’d expected the Association to put me to work right away, but surprisingly, three weeks had passed with no word from them.</p><p>Vrrr- vrrr-</p><p>Just then, the phone I’d left on my chest buzzed.</p><p>“…?”</p><p>Thinking it was another investment pitch from the bank, I checked the screen—only to see a familiar name.</p><p>-Kang Hye-na</p><p>“Ah.”</p><p>‘Damn… this girl really can’t let me be.’</p><p>“Hello. What’s up?”</p><p>“What are you doing?”</p><p>“Household chores… Are you spying on me?”</p><p>“Why would I spy on you… You’ve always had an overinflated ego, haven’t you?”</p><p>“Forget that. Why’d you call?”</p><p>“Why else? To give you work.”</p><p>“Ugh… Gate exploration?”</p><p>Just when I thought things might stay peaceful for a while, the Association had to ruin my day.</p><p>“No…”</p><p>Hye-na hesitated before answering.</p><p>“Then?”</p><p>“Crime syndicate raid…”</p><p>“Huh?”</p><p>“It’s a classified mission. The briefing will be at HQ. The organization is made up of Awakened—most of the lower ranks are equivalent to C-rank Hunters, mid-level are B-rank, and the executives are on par with A-rank Hunters. If we don’t take them down now, they’ll scatter back into cells. Now that we’ve located their base, we need to arrest them all at once.”</p><p>“Wait… why me? There are plenty of Hunters trained specifically for combat. What’s the Intelligence Bureau doing?”</p><p>“It’s a joint operation with the Bureau, but they’re stretched thin and don’t have enough personnel to deploy immediately. And when it comes to combat, there’s no Hunter better than you… Especially in a situation like this.”</p><p>“Ugh…”</p><p>This was the first request I’d gotten since then, and of course it was combat-related. It was true that during my trainee days, my scores in combat drills had been the highest among all subjects.</p><p>‘But that was back when I was a trainee…’</p><p>Even when I was clearing E and D-rank Gates, I’d beaten E and D-rank Hunters who ignored my orders or acted threateningly—sometimes breaking their limbs to neutralize them.</p><p>‘Still, capturing Awakened criminals on par with A and B-rank Hunters? That’s a tall order…’</p><p>A sigh escaped me.</p><p>Arresting criminal Awakened was indeed part of the Association’s duties. The real issue was that assigning me to a team tasked with taking down a syndicate full of criminals on par with A and B-rank Hunters meant they were treating me as an A-rank Hunter.</p><p>‘Well… I figured they already knew, but I guess they’re not even trying to hide it anymore. Now they’re openly treating me like an A-rank.’</p><p>At this point, hiding it was meaningless. It’d be better to come clean—</p><p>With a few lies mixed in, of course.</p><p>“Hye-na.”</p><p>“Huh?”</p><p>“That day… I mean, the day I entered the S-rank Gate…”</p><p>“Yeah.”</p><p>“Honestly, even I don’t remember it clearly… When I came to, my whole body was wrecked. Both arms were completely shattered, one side of my face was caved in, my organs were severely damaged—I was coughing up blood just from lying still.”</p><p>“Yeah… Keep going.”</p><p>Hye-na’s voice was brimming with curiosity, as if she had been waiting for me to bring this up.</p><p>“When I finally regained my senses and looked around, the boss was dead, and all the Hunters were either unconscious or… dead. I figured I wouldn’t last much longer either, so I was just looking for a place to die when I noticed something at the spot where the boss had fallen.”</p><p>“What was it?”</p><p>“A black bottle… Just a black bottle, no idea what was inside. My consciousness was fading fast, so I drank it. I held it between my feet, pried the lid open with my mouth, and downed the whole thing.”</p><p>“Hmm… And then your body healed?”</p><p>“In the end, yeah. I passed out after drinking it, but when I woke up, my body was fully recovered, and the bottle had vanished. Then you came and helped me to the escape gate, but my vision went blurry again, and I blacked out. After that… I got stronger. That’s all there is to it.”</p><p>“Mmm. I see. So that’s what happened…”</p><p>Hye-na muttered to herself for a long moment, as if she finally understood.</p><p>“Seong-hyeon.”</p><p>“Yeah?”</p><p>“Thanks for telling me.”</p><p>“Eh, whatever… You seemed like you already knew anyway.”</p><p>“The Association President?”</p><p>“Yeah… He had his suspicions.”</p><p>“Right. Actually, the President thought you must’ve eaten or drunk something in there… If we assume you consumed something of immense value, it explains why the spoils from an S-rank Gate were so underwhelming. But don’t worry—you know this already. Legally and ethically, you did nothing wrong by taking it in a life-or-death situation.”</p><p>“Yeah. I know.”</p><p>For now, that was the perfect answer. It let me keep the Armband’s existence completely hidden.</p><p>‘Fuck, I shouldn’t have taken that damn thing that day…’</p><p>From now on, I’d have to play it smart—either casually expose the Armband like it was just a fashion accessory, or cover my wrist whenever I went to the Association.</p><p>“Anyway, that’s what happened that day… But about the request…”</p><p>“Huh?”</p><p>“Is it okay if I refuse?”</p><p>Truthfully, I was still debating whether to accept or not, but I needed to set boundaries with the Association early on.</p><p>If I did them this favor, who knew how much more they’d try to exploit me later.</p><p>“Yeah… If you refuse, there’s not much we can do. We’re the ones who sprung this on you out of nowhere, and you’re not even a Hunter who specializes in PvP… But we did gather a lot of strong Hunters recently based on some intel we received. It’s a hastily assembled team, but aside from you, they’re all A-rank. Beyond that, it’s classified—I really can’t say more… So, does that mean you’re refusing?”</p><p>“···.”</p><p>I thought it over again, but I still felt like refusing was the right call.</p><p>I couldn’t imagine the Information Bureau’s A-rank Hunters—trained for PvP combat—and other A-ranks like Kang Hye-na losing to a bunch of lowlife criminals.</p><p>The biggest issue was the mission itself.</p><p>‘They say “arrest,” but…’</p><p>In reality, there would definitely be casualties on the scene.</p><p>Even if the higher-ups with critical intel were taken alive, the lower-ranking thugs would likely be shot dead if they resisted violently.</p><p>That was just how the Information Bureau’s Hunters operated, from what I knew.</p><p>‘I really don’t want to kill if I can help it…’</p><p>I might end up swinging my weapon to subdue someone, only for them to die in the process.</p><p>Of course, missions like this came with immunity clauses, so I wouldn’t face punishment, but…</p><p>‘Any sane person would avoid getting involved in something that could lead to killing, right?’</p><p>Just as I’d made up my mind to refuse, Hye-na spoke up.</p><p>“Oh, right. The pay is 480 million won… but you’re probably going to turn it down anyway.”</p><p>Hearing that number—480 million—made my resolve waver. If I just closed my eyes and did a few of these jobs… maybe that dream mansion of mine could become a reality.</p><p>“Wait.”</p><p>“Hm? No, don’t feel pressured. There’s no penalty for refusing.”</p><p>“Chief Kang?”</p><p>“Huh? What’s up all of a sudden?”</p><p>“I’ll do it. Whatever.”</p><p>“Seriously? You’re not just doing it for the money, are you?”</p><p>“As if. But hey, any chance of a bonus…?”</p><p>“Nope…”</p><p>“Tsk.”</p><p>“What?”</p><p>I clicked my tongue at the Association’s stinginess.</p><p>I decided to adjust my mindset a little.</p><p>‘Well… it’s for a good cause. From what I can tell, these guys are probably trafficking drugs made from Gate materials or kidnapping Awakened to sell them…’</p><p>“No, then… when do I need to go to headquarters?”</p><p>“Tomorrow.”</p><p>“What?”</p><p>“We’re gathering tomorrow for a briefing, then we’ll be in a four-day training camp to practice the operation before deployment the day after.”</p><p>“Why the training camp?”</p><p>“How are we supposed to let a Hunter who’s heard classified intel just walk out? So, you’re in?”</p><p>“Yeah. I’ll go tomorrow.”</p><p>“Got it. I’ll hang up.”</p><p>Hye-na seemed oddly pleased that I’d agreed.</p><p>“Ugh…”</p><p>‘Looks like my whole week’s shot. I’ll have to tell Yu-na about this at dinner tonight… and then I need to take her at least three times before bed…’</p>
