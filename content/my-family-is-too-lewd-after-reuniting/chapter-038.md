<p>Crunch.</p><p>“Khegh!”</p><p>A tentacle slammed into a Hunter’s head, leaving behind a short death rattle before the Hunter collapsed.</p><p>The Hunter’s head was completely crushed, and soon a pool of blood formed beneath them.</p><p>‘A B-rank Hunter who came in with me…’</p><p>“Next attack incoming! Everyone, get ready!”</p><p>There was no time to mourn the dead. They could only trust their instincts and keep fighting.</p><p>Kang Hye-na’s eyes met mine, but we didn’t exchange greetings.</p><p>We just gave each other a brief nod.</p><p>The B-rank Hunters were dodging dozens of tentacles while endlessly harassing the boss.</p><p>When they drew the boss’s attention, the A-rank Attackers carefully struck.</p><p>The vanguard was locked in this hellish back-and-forth, every second feeling like an eternity.</p><p>Boom!</p><p>I barely ducked my head to avoid a tentacle. If that had hit me, I’d be lucky to just lose a limb—most likely, it would be instant death.</p><p>There was no room for thought. I could only rely on my survival instincts to dodge the attacks.</p><p>But slowly, surely, the boss was taking damage.</p><p>***</p><p>How much time had passed? An hour? Two? Maybe not even an hour.</p><p>The floor was slick with the guts and blood of bisected Hunters.</p><p>Every Hunter still standing—except for the A-rank Tanks—had removed their armor. They knew that even a single hit would mean death, so they poured all their focus into evasion.</p><p>The situation was desperate.</p><p>The number of standing Hunters was dwindling, and the boss was healing its wounds. Its regeneration was grotesquely fast.</p><p>That monstrous black mass rolled its eyeless head around, making no sound at all.</p><p>Even when its body was carved open, it only swung its tentacles, spraying violet blood, still silent.</p><p>The Attackers were exhausted, and the boss’s healing had begun to outpace the damage they dealt.</p><p>Even the Healer Hunters, who had been treating Choi Seo-jin, had joined the front lines to help fight.</p><p>Many Hunters shouted, demanding to know if Choi Seo-jin was dead, but the Healers only replied, “He’s not dead.”</p><p>“Everyone, stay sharp! Don’t let that thing get a chance to scream!”</p><p>The vanguard Hunters kept repeating that warning. No one knew why, but they all sensed that if the boss let out a sound, something terrible would happen.</p><p>Whoosh—</p><p>Crack!</p><p>“Guhk!”</p><p>‘…!’</p><p>A tentacle struck the Hunter holding a shield in front of me. The shield shattered, and the Hunter’s body folded in half.</p><p>The problem was that the tentacle, with the bisected Hunter still impaled on it, was now swinging toward me.</p><p>I was a step too slow.</p><p>CRACK!</p><p>“Kgh—!”</p><p>Instinctively, I thrust out my left arm to shield my organs, and the impact sent me flying into the wall.</p><p>Luckily, it wasn’t a tentacle that hit me—it was the corpse of a Hunter dangling from one. That was the only reason I was still alive.</p><p>“Shit…!”</p><p>A searing pain shot through my left arm.</p><p>When I came to my senses and looked down, the bone was completely shattered, the limb hanging limp and mangled.</p><p>I tried to flex it, but no strength would answer.</p><p>“Haa…”</p><p>Death loomed before me. Watching from a distance only made the situation look even more hopeless.</p><p>The Hunters had already lost the will to fight. Even if reinforcements arrived, it would take hours—and even then, there was no guarantee they could turn the tide.</p><p>That was when a low voice spoke from behind me.</p><p>“Don’t turn around. Just listen.”</p><p>A raspy, blood-flecked woman’s voice… The only Hunter here besides me was Choi Seo-jin, who’d been lying down when we entered.</p><p>“…”</p><p>“I’ve got my eyes closed right now. How bad is it? If you’re fine, don’t say anything. If it’s the worst, cough once.”</p><p>“Cough!”</p><p>I answered with a cough, and blood sprayed from my mouth.</p><p>My body was in terrible shape too. Even after sacrificing my left arm, my organs must’ve taken damage—blood was welling up in my throat.</p><p>It’s over. The Hunters have completely lost their will to fight. Hye-na still looks like she wants to try something, but there’s no way she can do anything in that exhausted state.</p><p>Just as my hands began to tremble with the terror of death, Choi Seo-jin spoke again.</p><p>“Hey… That thing thinks I’m already dead. Honestly, I might pass out any second now…”</p><p>“…”</p><p>“But… if I squeeze out every last bit of strength, I think I can land one final big shot… You in? Cough if you are.”</p><p>Choi Seo-jin was a Psychic Hunter who manipulated electricity—though “Psychic” was a modest term for what was basically a superpower.</p><p>I had no idea what she was planning, but I didn’t have a choice. Living as a one-armed man was better than dying.</p><p>“Kuh—cough!”</p><p>Truly an S-rank’s meticulous approach. The leader of the monsters was staring in our direction with its chilling single eye, as if scanning past us.</p><p>“Then… run toward that thing… There’s a corpse in a golden armor along the way…”</p><p>I remembered—the Hunter in golden armor, head severed, sprawled on the ground.</p><p>“Next to the corpse… there’s a golden shield… Grab it and charge straight at its eye… Just hold its attention for one second… Its weak point is the red sphere in its mouth… I’ll aim for that.”</p><p>“…”</p><p>A bitter laugh escaped me. Choi Seo-jin’s plan was to send me on a suicide charge as bait.</p><p>If you ignored the part where I was the decoy, it was actually a solid strategy.</p><p>The front lines were already doomed. The battle had devolved into a desperate struggle just to survive.</p><p>It was a plan that could only be proposed to me—someone who had been accidentally struck and accidentally sent flying toward Choi Seo-jin, crashing into the wall.</p><p>“Decide… quickly… I’m about to pass out… There’s a golden shield next to that corpse… It’s the most sturdy piece of equipment here, so it should withstand one hit…”</p><p>I couldn’t tell if Choi Seo-jin’s words were a lie meant to reassure me or the truth.</p><p>Even so, I had no choice. Either Choi Seo-jin’s plan succeeded and I survived by sheer luck, escaping the Gate, or I stood here with my shattered left arm dangling, waiting for death. A simple choice between life and death.</p><p>I pretended to cough up blood, covering my mouth and lowering my head before muttering quietly,</p><p>“Kuhk! …But if it works, tell the Association to list me as the second greatest contributor for taking down the boss… And ask them to use an Elixir to treat my body…”</p><p>Elixir. The Association called it a “Highest-Grade Biocatalyst,” but Hunters just called it an Elixir.</p><p>It was a miraculous potion that could instantly heal most diseases or injuries if consumed.</p><p>It was rarely found in A-rank Gates, usually in the form of an ornately carved silver bottle.</p><p>Naturally, it wasn’t something that could be bought with money. And to take full effect, one person had to drink an entire bottle.</p><p>Choi Seo-jin stayed silent for a few seconds before replying,</p><p>“Y-You’re still trying to make a deal in a situation like this? You’re something else… Fine… Even if the Association refuses, I’ll bet everything I have to get you one…”</p><p>“Then… here I go.”</p><p>There was no time to mentally prepare.</p><p>I sprinted toward the boss with all my strength.</p><p>They say your life flashes before your eyes before you die. Time seemed to slow down.</p><p>“You fucking bastard!”</p><p>As I shouted at the boss, its chilling, bulbous eyes locked onto me.</p><p>I snatched up the golden shield in front of me with my right hand and charged.</p><p>Every Hunter was staring at me.</p><p>Their expressions were like they were watching a person who had lost their mind to the terror of death and was attempting suicide.</p><p>A black tentacle shot toward me.</p><p>In my current condition, I couldn’t dodge. All I could do was raise the shield and brace myself.</p><p>KWADDUK!</p><p>My vision went black for an instant. When I opened my eyes, I was airborne.</p><p>I could see my severed right wrist and the shield, now shattered into pieces, floating in the air beside me.</p><p>‘She said it would hold up to one hit…’</p><p>Since I was still alive, not all of Choi Seo-jin’s words had been lies.</p><p>‘I took two hits from that tentacle and lived… Am I lucky or unlucky?’</p><p>Time slowed as if in slow motion.</p><p>As I spun halfway through the air, I caught sight of Choi Seo-jin. She was holding what looked like a condensed ball of energy in both hands, preparing to hurl it at the boss.</p><p>Strangely, every other Hunter except Choi Seo-jin had collapsed.</p><p>I closed my eyes. I thought of Yu-na, Na-hyeon, and Ji-hyeon. All three were smiling with happy expressions.</p><p>My body could no longer feel pain—there wasn’t even any left to sense.</p><p>‘Ah… I want to go… to the sea again…’</p><p>***</p><p>“Cough…! Cough! Haah… *gasp*…”</p><p>I opened my eyes.</p><p>I could feel hot liquid flowing from both ears.</p><p>All I could hear was a high-pitched ‘beep’—no other sounds reached me.</p><p>Every bone in my body felt crushed as I struggled to push myself up.</p><p>“Cough…!”</p><p>My mouth kept vomiting blood.</p><p>My left eye wouldn’t open. I felt no pain from it. My right eye wasn’t normal either—my vision was blurry, the focus off.</p><p>My left arm dangled limply, as if a child could yank it off with a single tug.</p><p>My right arm had been severed at the wrist. No blood flowed from the wound. Instead, a thick, black, squirming liquid had sealed the stump—whether to block the bleeding or corrupt the flesh, I couldn’t tell.</p><p>I walked on my two still-intact legs.</p><p>With every step, blood gushed from my mouth, as if my life would flicker out in mere minutes.</p><p>Staggering, I made my way to where the boss monster had been.</p><p>The floor was coated in a sticky purple liquid, and scattered around it were shattered fragments of a red sphere.</p><p>Choi Seo-jin must have succeeded in killing the boss.</p><p>All the Hunters were collapsed on the ground. Blood trickled from their ears too.</p><p>Even though the boss was dead, the escape gate still hadn’t opened.</p><p>But… there was something even stranger.</p><p>The black wall behind the boss had collapsed. Beyond it was a chamber that looked like an altar.</p><p>It was as if… that altar was calling to me, whispering, *If you want to live, come here.*</p><p>Even though all I could hear was that grating ‘beep,’ it felt like someone was murmuring directly into my ear.</p><p>Bleeding, I entered the altar room. The atmosphere was eerie and unnatural.</p><p>Countless hearts were embedded in the walls on either side. Freshly extracted, they still dripped blood.</p><p>I approached the altar at the front, where two candles burned.</p><p>Resting on the altar was a gauntlet made of black metal.</p><p>‘No…’</p><p>It wasn’t just a simple gauntlet—it was closer to a full armored glove. A sleek design with gold trim accenting its black metal surface.</p><p>The gauntlet, crafted from the kind of black metal a knight might wear, was placed upside down, as if inviting me to slide my right arm inside.</p>
