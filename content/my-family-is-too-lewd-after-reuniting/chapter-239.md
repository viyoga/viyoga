<p>Hello.</p><p>This is Anannon.</p><p>My first work, *It’s Been a While, and My Family is Too Lewd*, has now come to an end.</p><p>First and foremost, I want to express my deepest gratitude to all the readers who stayed with me until the very last chapter.</p><p>I initially started writing with a lighthearted thought—*Maybe I should try writing something too?*—never expecting it to go this far.</p><p>Despite my lackluster writing skills, so many of you showed it love, and that leaves me with a bittersweet feeling.</p><p>At first, I planned to wrap it up within 70 chapters, but the story ended up dragging on, and for that, I’m truly sorry to my readers.</p><p>I’m so grateful, and as for the delays in the later chapters… I have no excuses, even if I had ten mouths!</p><p>I’ll strive to bring you even better works in the future!</p><p>Also, I plan to release side stories on an irregular schedule.</p><p>I still have more to tell about Seong Yu-yeon, Hye-na, and Echo.</p><p>Once again, thank you so much to all my readers.</p><p>I love you!</p>
