<p>We were descending a long, spiral staircase so dark and damp that I couldn’t even see a hand in front of my face.</p><p>A foul, metallic stench tickled my nose. I could only hope it wasn’t the smell of blood.</p><p>“This seems to be the right way… There might be traps, so stay close behind me.”</p><p>As if he’d known from the start that this was the path we were looking for, Lee Seung-jun took the lead, carefully stepping down the endless staircase one step at a time.</p><p>‘Just how many floors underground is this…?’</p><p>As we descended, I watched him mutter, “So it *is* here,” and couldn’t help but ask.</p><p>“Did you know this was the right way from the beginning?”</p><p>“No. I just went with my gut after interrogating that missing-tooth guy. 80-20 odds? You pick up a feel for these things after doing it for a while.”</p><p>‘*You* were the one who pulled his tooth out…’</p><p>“Then… why did you send the others somewhere else?”</p><p>“Well… hmm.”</p><p>Lee Seung-jun stopped walking, glanced back at me, then continued down the stairs as he spoke.</p><p>“Right before the first fight started, everyone on the team except me was shaking. Probably because of a fundamental aversion to killing. Even after the battle ended, they were still trembling—hands, feet, mentally exhausted. Except for you, Seong-hyeon.”</p><p>“Is that why you chose me?”</p><p>“Yes. There might be even more horrific sights down here. Those three could lose their will to fight if they see them.”</p><p>“You say that like I’ll be fine with it.”</p><p>“Of course. You seemed the most nervous before the fight, but once it started, you cut down the enemies without hesitation. Are your hands shaking now? Do you feel guilty?”</p><p>My mind grew tangled.</p><p>It was true—I’d been overwhelmed with tension when facing the enemies, but once the battle began, my mind had cleared, leaving only a strange sense of relief.</p><p>‘The ones I cut down… I avoided vital spots, but they might still bleed out before the support team arrives…’</p><p>Even as I repeated the thought that I might have killed them, I didn’t feel the weight of sin.</p><p>“Those three are probably descending much slower than us. Their instincts will be paralyzed by fear, and they’ll just hope someone else finishes this mission faster.”</p><p>“…”</p><p>“Sorry. I brought up a heavy topic.”</p><p>“Ah, no, it’s fine.”</p><p>“Then how about a different subject? I heard you and Director Kang Hye-na were close friends from the training academy.”</p><p>“Yes.”</p><p>“Can I give you some advice?”</p><p>‘Advice…?’</p><p>“Sure, what?”</p><p>“Don’t get too close to Director Kang. Keep it light—just jokes between friends.”</p><p>“Huh? What’s this about all of a sudden…”</p><p>‘Are he and Hye-na not on good terms?’</p><p>After that, we walked in silence for about ten seconds before Lee Seung-jun sighed and spoke again.</p><p>“Are you aware of how severe the power struggles are within the Association?”</p><p>“I’ve heard the rumors.”</p><p>“They’re more than just rumors. When the organization was small, the president’s personal charisma was enough to keep things in check. But now that it’s grown so large, that’s no longer the case. The conflicts have reached a point where they can’t be mended.”</p><p>“I see…”</p><p>This was fascinating. Lee Seung-jun was sharing information about the Association’s inner workings—details that only someone deeply connected would know.</p><p>“Of course, there’s Choi Seo-jin, the president’s disciple and a direct S-rank Hunter, but he lacks both popularity and any understanding of internal politics. The president desperately needs a strong successor to assist him. The Intelligence Bureau has already started acting independently, ignoring the Association’s directives.”</p><p>“And how does that relate to not getting close to Director Kang Hye-na?”</p><p>“Yes, I’m giving you this advice because I like you, Seong-hyeon.”</p><p>“I don’t understand at all.”</p><p>“Director Kang Hye-na… she’s passionate, the kind of person who sheds tears for others. I’ll admit, she’s a good person. But she’s completely ignorant about the Association’s internal politics. She has zero sense for resolving things politically. She believes everything can be solved with a sense of justice and honesty… She’s an idiot.”</p><p>“…”</p><p>I was starting to feel a little annoyed. What the hell was Lee Seung-jun even trying to say to me?</p><p>We soon reached the end of the stairs. In front of us was a rusted, ominous iron door.</p><p>Lee Seung-jun took out a card he had confiscated from the unconscious man’s pocket and swiped it through the reader.</p><p>Beep! Clank.</p><p>“This bastard… knew all along and played dumb till the end.”</p><p>He then grabbed the doorknob, turned it, and looked at me as he spoke.</p><p>“A superior who doesn’t understand politics will lead their subordinates to their deaths. They’re utterly incompetent. So, Seong-hyeon, shall we go in?”</p><p>As the door opened, the scene before us was a stark contrast to the dim, eerie staircase. A row of pristine operating rooms, equipped with state-of-the-art medical facilities, stretched out before us.</p><p>The sharp scent of various chemicals stung my nose.</p><p>And what I saw next were doctors inside the operating rooms, carefully packaging organs.</p><p>“Ugh—!”</p><p>I barely managed to swallow the bile rising in my throat.</p><p>“Who the hell are these guys?”</p><p>“What the fuck… how did you get in here?”</p><p>About ten gang members, startled, stood up from their chairs, staring at us in shock.</p><p>“Seong-hyeon, over there!”</p><p>Lee Seung-jun pointed to the far end of the underground operating room.</p><p>There, gang members were urgently dragging children bound in chains.</p><p>‘Fuck… these animal bastards…’</p><p>My hands began to tremble—not from fear, but from rage.</p><p>“Hey, can’t you guys wait another ten minutes before coming in?”</p><p>One of the gang members twirled an axe as he approached us.</p><p>Lee Seung-jun muttered quietly.</p><p>“Seong-hyeon, I’ll open the way. Run through that door and secure the children!”</p><p>Lee Seung-jun finished speaking and dashed forward at incredible speed, slashing the throat of the axe-wielding gang member.</p><p>Swish—</p><p>“Huh? What the fuck…”</p><p>The man with the axe muttered in confusion, then blood spurted from his neck like a fountain, and he collapsed where he stood.</p><p>“Fuck, kill them all!”</p><p>“Buy time until the boss gets out!”</p><p>The gang members charged at us all at once.</p><p>“Seong-hyeon, run!”</p><p>“Yes!”</p><p>I sprinted at full speed down the path Lee Seung-jun had opened.</p><p>The gang member who had been pushing the children out the door panicked as I closed in much faster than he expected. He started kicking and throwing the kids out the door.</p><p>Just as all the children were shoved outside and he was about to shut the door, I hurled a dagger into the gap.</p><p>CLANG!</p><p>“What the—fuck—!”</p><p>The dagger lodged in the doorframe fell out, and as the door tried to close, I reached it, grabbed the handle, and forced it open.</p><p>“Huu…”</p><p>As I opened the door, the gang member took a few steps back and drew a knife from his waist.</p><p>“Fucking… useless brats… I told you to move faster…”</p><p>There were at least twenty children, their arms, legs, and necks bound with iron chains. Their eyes were completely lifeless.</p><p>These were clearly the special chains designed to restrain Awakened.</p><p>And their hollow, empty eyes… confirmed they had been drugged.</p><p>I stepped inside and shut the door behind me.</p><p>THUD—</p><p>“What are you doing? You think you can win one-on-one?”</p><p>“Hey… capturing you alive *was* the plan… but I won’t let you walk away clean.”</p><p>“Forget that. I’ve got to kill you and meet the Chinese buyers. Let’s get this over with.”</p><p>I sheathed my dagger and gently stroked the head of the child beside me.</p><p>“Close your eyes…”</p><p>The children nodded and shut their eyes.</p><p>“Equip.”</p><p>CLANK!</p><p>A gauntlet materialized on my right hand. A surge of rage and power coursed through it—something I’d never felt before.</p><p>“What the fuck are you…?”</p><p>The gang member swung his knife at my neck. I seized his wrist.</p><p>CRUNCH—</p><p>“AAAAAAGH!”</p><p>The man’s right wrist shattered like tofu.</p><p>CLANG!</p><p>He dropped the knife, clutching his ruined wrist as he collapsed to his knees, howling in pain.</p><p>“Get up. I’m not killing you.”</p><p>I grabbed his left shoulder and yanked him up.</p><p>CRACK—</p><p>“AAAAAAGH! YOU CRAZY BASTARD! FUCK YOU!!”</p><p>The organization member hung in the air, his left shoulder completely crushed, his left arm dangling limply.</p><p>When I threw the man to the floor, he dragged his flopping arms and started crawling toward the exit.</p><p>“Fuck… ugh! What the hell is that bastard…! Fucking shit… hngh…! Don’t come! Don’t come, you fuck! Stay the hell away!”</p><p>I followed the crawling man and stomped both his ankles with my fists.</p><p>THUD! THUD!</p><p>“NGHAAAAA—”</p><p>The man foamed at the mouth and passed out.</p><p>His ankles wobbled as if they might snap off at any moment.</p><p>A few syringes fell from the unconscious man’s pockets.</p><p>At a glance, they looked like either the drugs he’d injected into the kids or some kind of narcotics.</p><p>‘No idea what it is, but…’</p><p>I picked up a syringe, grabbed the unconscious man’s face, and injected the liquid into both his eyes.</p><p>‘If he goes blind, great. If not, whatever.’</p><p>“Huuu…”</p><p>I had done something monstrous to a monster. And I felt no guilt. If anything, it left a bad taste in my mouth.</p><p>“Release.”</p><p>CLICK</p><p>Leaving the fallen man behind, I went to the children, knelt on one knee, and ruffled their hair.</p><p>“You can open your eyes now… We should go home…”</p><p>The kids seemed well-nourished—probably because they’d been raised for organ trafficking—but their eyes were already dead, like a corpse’s.</p><p>BANG BANG BANG!</p><p>“Seong-hyeon! Are you in there?!”</p><p>Lee Seung-jun must have finished his work; he was pounding on the door in a hurry.</p><p>When I opened the door, Lee Seung-jun saw the unconscious man and the syringes scattered around his face. He nodded and patted my shoulder.</p><p>“Good work. The support team is entering the building now, so we just need to wait here for them.”</p><p>“Yeah…”</p><p>***</p><p>The operation ended in a resounding success. Baek Gwang-hyeon had fled overseas, but he was already on the red notice list, and most of the key executives had been captured and were being interrogated by the Association.</p><p>The Awakened children who had been targeted for human trafficking were mostly orphans or kids sold by their parents. The Association had decided to take these children under their protection as well.</p><p>Out of the 35 Hunters deployed, there were 8 injured, but no fatalities. The injuries weren’t serious, either.</p><p>“Ugh… when the hell do I get to go home…”</p><p>Just as I was listening to the post-op briefing and about to head home, a text came from Na-hyeon.</p><p>-XX Hotel, Room XX. By 7 PM.</p><p>It was like someone had read my mind. I couldn’t help but laugh.</p><p>Right now, all I wanted was to bury this messy, tangled feeling in a woman’s arms.</p><p>“Alright… let’s go.”</p>
