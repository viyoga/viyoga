<p>The sun had risen when I exited the Gate.</p><p>I tried to casually mention to the assigned Association member that there were signs of collapse all over inside the Gate, but the member remained expressionless and indifferent the whole time.</p><p>‘Just the face of an office worker who wants to clock out early…’</p><p>After finishing the exploration, I headed straight to the central headquarters.</p><p>I tried calling Hye-na, but she didn’t pick up.</p><p>‘She must be busy… even though she’s the one who called me.’</p><p>With no other choice, I made my way to the reception desk.</p><p>“Yes, B-rank Hunter Yoon Seong-hyeon’s identity has been confirmed. How may I assist you?”</p><p>A somewhat fastidious-looking Association member, wearing glasses, pushed them up as they spoke.</p><p>“I’m here to see Director Kang Hye-na.”</p><p>The member gave me a puzzled look, tapped a few keys on the keyboard, then asked with a suspicious expression,</p><p>“Director Kang Hye-na has no scheduled appointments. What business do you have with her?”</p><p>“It’s personal.”</p><p>“Haa… If it’s personal, what kind of—”</p><p>The receptionist was staring at me with a mix of annoyance and suspicion.</p><p>I understood. It was only natural for them to be suspicious—a freelance B-rank Hunter showing up out of the blue to meet Kang Hye-na, the central headquarters’ director and an A-rank Hunter, claiming personal business as if they were close.</p><p>“We… arranged a sparring match.”</p><p>“Huh? Haa… If you want to spar with an A-rank Hunter, you’ll need to fill out this form and wait a few days for the designated sparring Hunter to be assigned.”</p><p>The receptionist sighed in exasperation and slapped a form onto the desk.</p><p>Vrrr—</p><p>Just then, my phone vibrated in my pocket.</p><p>The caller ID displayed the name of the person I had come to see.</p><p>‘Kang Hye-na.’</p><p>“Excuse me for a moment.”</p><p>“Y-Yes…”</p><p>The receptionist seemed flustered and scratched their neck.</p><p>“Hey, why didn’t you answer? I’m at the central headquarters.”</p><p>“Sorry, sorry~. I was in a meeting. So you finished the exploration? Where are you?”</p><p>“Reception.”</p><p>“I’ll come there now.”</p><p>“Alright.”</p><p>After hanging up, I looked back at the receptionist. Their face was a mix of confusion and fear.</p><p>“Ah, sorry. Hye-na—uh, Director Kang said she’s coming here now.”</p><p>“W-What? No… That’s not—W-Wait, Hunter Yoon Seong-hyeon—!”</p><p>Just as I turned to sit in a chair in front of the reception desk, the receptionist called out to me.</p><p>“Yes?”</p><p>Then, the receptionist abruptly stood up, bowed deeply, and said,</p><p>“I’m so sorry!”</p><p>“Huh? Yes? What’s wrong…”</p><p>In an instant, the attention of everyone around me was fixed on me.</p><p>“I sincerely apologize for my inappropriate attitude and conduct!”</p><p>I hurriedly approached the receptionist and helped him stand up straight.</p><p>“No, seriously, why are you doing this…”</p><p>“I didn’t recognize you as someone close to Director Kang and acted rudely in both words and manner. Please…”</p><p>“Ah… I really don’t mind. No, if you’re truly sorry, please don’t do this. Please…”</p><p>‘Fuck, is this some new kind of torture?’</p><p>A dozen or so people around us kept glancing between me and the receptionist.</p><p>The other receptionists sitting nearby were looking at the bowing man with expressions that seemed to say, *Pathetic. Tsk tsk.*</p><p>After finally calming the receptionist down, I fled to the central lobby and sat on a bench next to the water dispenser.</p><p>“Right. I need to tell her.”</p><p>-Come to the chair next to the water dispenser in the central lobby.</p><p>I sent a text to Hye-na, drank a cup of water, and sat in the chair, replaying what had just happened in my head.</p><p>‘Damn… Director Kang really carries some weight.’</p><p>It was true that the receptionist’s attitude had been a little rude, but even if I’d been in his position, I’d have been stunned if someone suddenly showed up claiming they needed to meet Kang Hye-na, one of the current power players in the Association, for personal business—especially for a sparring session.</p><p>“Hey~ Yoon Seong-hyeon. Why are you sitting all the way over here?”</p><p>“It’s a long story…”</p><p>“Hmm. Should we head out now?”</p><p>Hye-na and I took the elevator down to the underground training facility in the Association.</p><p>“So, how was the Gate?”</p><p>“For a D-rank, it was pretty easy…”</p><p>Of course, that was a lie. It was the textbook difficulty for a D-rank Gate.</p><p>“That’s why it only took a day?”</p><p>“Yeah, there weren’t many monsters… The boss was a psychic-type, so it wasn’t a big deal.”</p><p>Honestly, I had no idea what ability the boss even had. I’d cut its head off before it could show me.</p><p>“Oh, right. Since it was an unregistered Gate, there’s no reward. You’ve never been to a Gate before either, so you get it, right?”</p><p>“…”</p><p>If anything, this was better. It was a secret only Hye-na and I knew—one that wouldn’t be recorded anywhere.</p><p>“Why? You got a problem with that?”</p><p>Hye-na glared at me as she spoke.</p><p>“The Association’s always been stingy.”</p><p>“What?”</p><p>“Forget it… You’re serious about this, right? The sparring…”</p><p>“Would I joke about it?”</p><p>“…”</p><p>We arrived at Training Room 12 on the fifth basement level. After changing into sparring gear in the locker room, we stepped into the training area.</p><p>He didn’t forget to put on armbands on both wrists either—to hide the Armband.</p><p>Kang Hye-na was still standing there, dressed in a suit.</p><p>“What the hell… Even if there’s a skill gap, you can’t seriously expect me to fight in that?”</p><p>“Huh? You’re not fighting me, though?”</p><p>“What?”</p><p>Just then, the training hall door opened, and a woman in sparring gear entered.</p><p>She had sharp features, red hair tied in a ponytail.</p><p>‘She looks like a cat.’</p><p>“Director-nim… This is…”</p><p>Despite her sharp appearance, her voice was quiet and calm.</p><p>“Yes! This is B-rank Hunter Yoon Seong-hyeon. And this is A-rank Hunter Min Se-rin! Say hello to each other!”</p><p>“Nice to meet you. I’m Min Se-rin.”</p><p>“Yes, I’m Yoon Seong-hyeon. Pleased to meet you.”</p><p>I shot Hye-na a glare, and she just smiled.</p><p>“I said I’d arrange a sparring match, not that I’d fight you myself. Oh, and by the way, Min Se-rin Hunter got promoted to A-rank five months ago. So, each of you grab a sword, and… I’ll watch from above~.”</p><p>“Hey… wait… hey!”</p><p>Hye-na vanished up the stairs. She was now peering down at us through the glass window above.</p><p>“Shall we pick our weapons?”</p><p>Min Se-rin said.</p><p>“Ugh… Yeah.”</p><p>There were only spears and swords available.</p><p>‘No daggers, huh?’</p><p>With no other choice, I picked up a reinforced steel training sword and walked to the center of the sparring arena.</p><p>My mind was a mess.</p><p>‘Why? Why this? No way…’</p><p>I’d assumed Hye-na just wanted to reminisce by sparring, but that wasn’t it.</p><p>A chill ran down my spine.</p><p>‘Damn it… Yoon Seong-hyeon, you idiot.’</p><p>I’d been too careless. Hye-na—no, the Hunter Association—was definitely suspicious of me.</p><p>If they’d been certain about something, they would’ve called me in for questioning right away.</p><p>‘Good thing I put on the armbands.’</p><p>Sending an A-rank Hunter to fight a B-rank was practically the same as a “rank reassessment.”</p><p>It was a system for Hunters dissatisfied with their current rank. Sure, there were the rare cases where a Hunter was genuinely underrated, but 99% of the time, they got crushed and failed to rank up.</p><p>They knew I’d refuse if they outright suggested a reassessment, so they were doing it this way instead.</p><p>‘Should I throw the match? But she’s an A-rank Hunter… No, if I lose on purpose now that they’re already suspicious, it might make them even more suspicious… Maybe I should indirectly prove I’ve gotten stronger…’</p><p>“…If you’re just going to stand there, I’ll take the first move.”</p><p>“Huh?”</p><p>CLANG-!</p><p>Min Se-rin charged forward in an instant and swung her sword.</p><p>My body reacted reflexively, raising my sword to block.</p><p>When I deflected her A-rank Hunter’s two-handed strike with just one hand, Min Se-rin seemed taken aback. She retracted her sword and stepped back.</p><p>“Ah…”</p><p>At this point, it was meaningless to lie. Blocking an A-rank Hunter’s full-force swing with one hand had already given me away.</p><p>‘Think. Think.’</p><p>Min Se-rin rolled her shoulders a few times, did a couple of light jumps in place, then grinned—a smile that sent chills down my spine.</p><p>“Looks like… I can play a little rougher…”</p><p>***</p><p>CLANG-!</p><p>CLANG-!</p><p>About an hour had passed, and the sparring continued.</p><p>Min Se-rin was unleashing an absurdly relentless barrage of attacks.</p><p>‘She looks around my age… but she’s an A-rank Hunter. Is she some kind of genius?’</p><p>What was even more shocking than Min Se-rin’s tireless, dazzling swordplay was the fact that I—without breaking a sweat—hadn’t let a single one of her attacks land. I was deflecting or blocking them all with ease.</p><p>‘She’s full of openings…’</p><p>Even when I saw an opening, I didn’t counterattack. I stuck to defense.</p><p>I was even blocking attacks I could have dodged, and using both hands on my sword when one would have sufficed.</p><p>Part of it was to hide how much stronger I’d become, but there was a bigger problem.</p><p>At some point, Han Ji-hyeok, the Association President, had appeared next to Hye-na on the second floor, watching us.</p><p>‘Fuck. This is really fucked.’</p><p>After 30 minutes, the smile had vanished from Min Se-rin’s face.</p><p>In its place was something that looked a lot like anger.</p><p>‘Must be my imagination… right?’</p><p>There was only one option left. An hour had passed, and she had implicitly accepted that I’d gotten stronger. If I just took one hit and pretended to lose, this could end.</p><p>Min Se-rin took a deep breath and readied her stance.</p><p>‘Next attack… I’ll take a hit and go down. No, I’ll fake it.’</p><p>Just as Min Se-rin was about to lunge at me—</p><p>“That’s enough~.”</p><p>“What?”</p><p>“Huh? Director?”</p><p>“That’s plenty. Min Se-rin, you’ve worked hard. Thank you for humoring my request. Go rest now.”</p><p>“Huh? Oh… Yes, understood.”</p><p>Min Se-rin shot me one last glare before leaving the training ground.</p><p>“Hye-na… I feel like that girl hates me…”</p><p>Hye-na stared at me before speaking.</p><p>“Seong-hyeon, you’ve… gotten stronger.”</p><p>“···.”</p><p>Since everything was already exposed, I didn’t bother responding.</p><p>“Just what the hell happened that day…”</p><p>Just then, Han Ji-hyeok descended the stairs, hands clasped behind his back.</p><p>“Now, now, Hunter Seong-hyeon must be exhausted… Gang Hye-na, could you step aside for a moment?”</p><p>“Yes, Association President.”</p><p>Hye-na smiled and left the training grounds.</p><p>“O-oh, it’s been a while, Association President…”</p><p>“Hmm… It’s only natural that Hunter Min Se-rin would resent you. Even I, watching from the sidelines, felt a little angry.”</p><p>“Huh? What do you mean, all of a sudden…?”</p><p>“I could tell you were holding back against Hunter Min Se-rin. Did you think the fighters themselves wouldn’t notice?”</p><p>“Huh?”</p><p>‘Fuck…’</p><p>The realization that playing defense the whole time had been the worst possible move sent a chill down my spine.</p><p>“···.”</p><p>No excuses came to mind. Lying in front of this man would be meaningless.</p><p>“You should’ve just fought her ruthlessly instead of holding back. Holding back is what makes it suspicious.”</p><p>Han Ji-hyeok said with a laugh.</p><p>“I enjoyed today’s sparring. The Association will be sending you plenty of requests from now on. Complete a few, and I’ll promote you to A-rank right away, so don’t worry and—”</p><p>“Association President…”</p><p>“Speak.”</p><p>At this point, the Association was the most dangerous place to be.</p><p>After the last S-rank Gate exploration, many A-rank Hunters had either died or left for the private sector, leaving the Association in a precarious position—or so the rumors said.</p><p>Just looking at the way Han Ji-hyeok’s eyes gleamed at the thought of putting me to work sent a shiver down my back.</p><p>It was a shame, but now, fleeing to the private sector was my only option.</p><p>“I appreciate the favor, but I don’t understand why you’d assume I’d naturally accept the requests. I’m not particularly eager for a promotion, either.”</p><p>Han Ji-hyeok burst into laughter at my words, grabbed my shoulder, and patted it a few times as if amused.</p><p>Then he leaned in close and whispered,</p><p>“You’ve got guts. I like that even more. I can’t let a talent like you slip away to the private sector. But here’s the thing, Seong-hyeon… I plan to take your secret to my grave. What about you?”</p><p>“···.”</p><p>With that, Han Ji-hyeok left the training grounds.</p><p>“Huuu.”</p><p>I flopped down onto the training ground floor and stared at the ceiling.</p><p>“Fucking hell… this is so fucked.”</p>
