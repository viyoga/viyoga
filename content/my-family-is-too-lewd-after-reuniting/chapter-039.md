<p>Seeing the gauntlet lying there as if it had been prepared specifically for me—me, with my right wrist severed—made me laugh.</p><p>I remembered the words of the S-rank Hunter who had discovered the artifact.</p><p>‘It was as if this weapon had been waiting for me, as if it knew I would need it.’</p><p>There was no need to hesitate.</p><p>‘Whether I do this or not, I’m dead either way…’</p><p>I stretched out my severed wrist toward the opening of the gauntlet.</p><p>The gauntlet attached itself the moment the cross-section of my wrist touched its entrance, as if it had always been a part of my body.</p><p>I couldn’t hear the sound, but I could almost imagine the metallic *clank* it would have made.</p><p>‘This is absurd…’</p><p>I clenched and unclenched my right hand repeatedly. The gauntlet moved and functioned without any discomfort, just like a real hand.</p><p>I carefully ran my right hand over my left eye. The indentations I had felt earlier were gone.</p><p>“…”</p><p>Holding the gauntlet up in front of me, I saw it looked as if it were covered in scales. The black metal had no sheen, and at regular intervals, straight golden lines ran across its surface.</p><p>The fingertips were so sharp they looked like they could draw blood with the slightest touch.</p><p>As I turned my hand this way and that, something suddenly surged up from deep inside me.</p><p>“Ugh…! Blegh! Ugh!”</p><p>I dropped to my knees. Black blood gushed from my mouth like a waterfall.</p><p>I couldn’t open my eyes, and the blood kept pouring from my throat endlessly.</p><p>“Ugh!”</p><p>‘What… the hell… is this?!’</p><p>When I finally came to, there was a pool of black blood I had vomited onto the floor.</p><p>“What… is… this… *cough*!”</p><p>Once I finished vomiting, a pain I hadn’t felt until now hit me all at once. My entire body was wracked with agony.</p><p>It felt like every nerve in my body was screaming. A pain so intense I couldn’t even scream surged through me.</p><p>“Kehk…! Kk…!”</p><p>My vision went black, and in the end, unable to endure the pain any longer, I collapsed face-first into the pool of my own vomit and passed out.</p><p>***</p><p>“Nnngh… ugh…”</p><p>How long had I been unconscious? When I tried to sit up, the black blood stuck to my face stretched out with a *squelch*.</p><p>Disgusted, I wiped my face with my hand and then looked at it.</p><p>And then… I couldn’t help but gape in shock.</p><p>“W-what… the hell… is this…”</p><p>It wasn’t the grotesque, jelly-like black blood coating my hand that shocked me.</p><p>It was the fact that my left arm—which had been completely crushed—was now perfectly restored.</p><p>I touched my ear. Without even realizing it, my hearing had returned to normal.</p><p>I cautiously opened my left eye. My vision was clear.</p><p>I ran my left hand over the left side of my face. The deep indentations I had felt before were gone—completely smooth.</p><p>All the damage to my organs seemed to have been restored—pressing and prodding various parts of my body, I felt no pain.</p><p>“This is… an artifact…”</p><p>At first, I couldn’t tell if this moment was real or just a dream.</p><p>Only after groping my entire body, coughing, and taking deep breaths did I have to accept that this was reality.</p><p>‘No matter how I look at it, it feels real…’</p><p>“But… this.”</p><p>The biggest problem was my right hand.</p><p>‘Do I have to live with this on for the rest of my life…?’</p><p>I tried using my left hand to pull it off, but it wouldn’t budge.</p><p>Thanks to this, I’d survived and even healed my half-crippled body. Even if I had to wear it forever, I couldn’t complain.</p><p>The problem was the Association. The third exploration team would arrive soon, and if I went out wearing this gauntlet, it’d be like bragging that I’d stolen an artifact.</p><p>“This is ridiculous to even say, but at least it should be possible to take it on and off… How do I… deactivate…”</p><p>Clank!</p><p>“…!”</p><p>The gauntlet disappeared. No—when I looked down, a black armband was wrapped around my wrist. A thin black armband with two delicate gold lines running through its center.</p><p>‘No way…’</p><p>I exhaled deeply, clenched my arm, and shouted,</p><p>“Equip!”</p><p>The armband didn’t so much as twitch.</p><p>“…”</p><p>Pushing past the awkwardness, I tried muttering it more quietly this time.</p><p>“Wear…?”</p><p>Clank!</p><p>In an instant, the armband vanished and the gauntlet equipped itself.</p><p>“Unequip…”</p><p>Clank!</p><p>The gauntlet returned to the armband.</p><p>‘What the hell is this…’</p><p>I cackled to myself like a madman for a few seconds before leaving the room with the altar.</p><p>“Huh?”</p><p>The moment I stepped out of the room, the altar collapsed with a rumble. As if there had never been anything there to begin with… it reverted back to a black wall.</p><p>Vroooom—</p><p>As I stood there, dazed, staring at the wall, a familiar sound came from behind me.</p><p>The escape portal had opened.</p><p>“Ha…”</p><p>It felt like someone was playing a cruel prank on me.</p><p>By sheer coincidence, my right wrist had been severed… by sheer coincidence, I’d found an artifact… and to top it all off, that artifact just so happened to be a gauntlet that fit my wrist perfectly.</p><p>It gave me chills, like everything had been designed to turn out exactly like this from the start.</p><p>“Right… now’s not the time for this… Ah! Sh—”</p><p>Suddenly remembering Hye-na lying unconscious, I turned to carry her toward the escape gate—only for an overwhelming wave of dizziness and sleepiness to crash over me.</p><p>“W-what the… hell… is… this… ugh…”</p><p>Barely fighting off the crushing wave of exhaustion, I crawled toward where Hye-na lay collapsed.</p><p>“A… ugh…”</p><p>I dropped to my knees and tried to lift her, but my body refused to obey.</p><p>“Can’t… Hye… na… ugh…”</p><p>Unable to resist the drowsiness and dizziness any longer, my consciousness began to fade—when a loud voice rang out from behind me.</p><p>“There! Over there! The escape gate is open!”</p><p>“Hurry!”</p><p>With my eyes closed and head bowed, exhaling shallow breaths, someone shook my shoulder.</p><p>“Hunter-nim! Hunter-nim! Are you conscious?!”</p><p>“Snap out of it!”</p><p>“First aid for the survivors, then move them to the escape gate immediately!”</p><p>“Hunter-nim!”</p><p>Squeezing out the last of my strength, I left one final word before collapsing onto Hye-na.</p><p>“So… sleepy…”</p><p>***</p><p>“Ah…”</p><p>When I opened my eyes, I saw a white ceiling and an antique-style chandelier.</p><p>I was lying on a plush bed.</p><p>Glancing down slightly, I saw Yu-na asleep on a chair to my left, her head resting on my stomach with one hand on my side. To my right was Na-hyeon.</p><p>Since I was wearing a hospital gown, this had to be a hospital. And this room—it looked like one of those VIP rooms you only see in dramas.</p><p>‘What kind of hospital room has a living room and a sofa…? This is bigger than my living room…’</p><p>“Huh?”</p><p>On the sofa, Hye-na—also in a hospital gown—was sprawled out with one leg draped over the backrest, snoring loudly.</p><p>‘Why is she here…?’</p><p>Aside from the bandages wrapped around various parts of her body, she looked fine.</p><p>‘Thank goodness.’</p><p>Turning my head, I saw Yoon Ji-hyeon asleep on the guardian’s bed to my right.</p><p>“Ah.”</p><p>As I retraced my memories, I yanked my right hand out from under the blanket in a panic.</p><p>A black Armband was wrapped around my right wrist.</p><p>‘It wasn’t… a dream.’</p><p>Reaching out with my left hand, I gently stroked Yu-na’s head as she slept peacefully.</p><p>“Mmm…”</p><p>Yu-na let out a contented sound, then slowly blinked her sleepy eyes open—only to lock eyes with me.</p><p>She jolted upright and stared at me for a few seconds, mouth agape, as if her soul had left her body.</p><p>‘What do I even say…’</p><p>“Yu-na… I… I’m back.”</p><p>Tears began streaming down her face like a broken dam.</p><p>“Huh? Hey, don’t cry, don’t cry.”</p><p>I sat up and tried wiping her tears with my hand, but they kept pouring.</p><p>“Hng…”</p><p>“Huh?”</p><p>Yu-na hugged me tightly and burst into tears.</p><p>“I’m really fine, so…”</p><p>“Seong-hyeon!”</p><p>“Yeah?”</p><p>Hearing Yu-na’s sobs, Na-hyeon woke up and stared at me with tear-filled eyes.</p><p>“So, don’t cry…”</p><p>Na-hyeon hugged me too and started bawling.</p><p>“…”</p><p>I looked to the side and saw Ji-hyeon had woken up at the noise and was watching me.</p><p>“You… not gonna cry?”</p><p>“Y-You… idiot…”</p><p>Ji-hyeon didn’t sob out loud, but tears streamed from her eyes as she came over and hugged me.</p><p>‘Wait… I just took a nap and woke up. Why…?’</p><p>“Back in the day, you were all ‘middle school syndrome’ and said having family would only weaken your resolve when entering a Gate, so you didn’t need any… Looks like you’re pretty popular now.”</p><p>Standing at the foot of the bed was Hye-na, smiling.</p><p>Her eyes were red-rimmed too.</p><p>“Please, don’t you cry. You’re my friend…”</p><p>Hye-na answered with laughter instead of tears.</p><p>Just as our tearful family reunion was unfolding, someone opened the hospital room door and stepped inside.</p><p>The nurse who entered froze when she saw me, shouted, “Doctor!” and dashed off somewhere.</p><p>Soon after, hurried footsteps echoed from the hallway, and several doctors rushed in.</p><p>My family, who had been hugging me, pulled away at the sight of the doctors.</p><p>The oldest-looking doctor, a man with a distinguished air, approached me and asked,</p><p>“How are you feeling right now, patient?”</p><p>“Huh?”</p><p>I had no idea what he was asking.</p><p>“Uh… I’m just a little hungry…”</p><p>I wasn’t joking. Apart from the hunger, I felt no issues at all with my body.</p><p>In fact, I felt more energetic and refreshed than ever.</p><p>The doctor chuckled as if amused by my answer.</p><p>“Patient… you’ve been in a coma for a week.”</p><p>“What? Haah…”</p><p>Hearing that I’d been unconscious for a week left me stunned.</p><p>Now I understood why my family had burst into tears when I woke up.</p><p>According to the doctor, when everyone had collapsed in the Gate, I was the only one found kneeling, and I was immediately rushed to the hospital—but I never regained consciousness.</p><p>They ran every test possible, but there was nothing wrong with my body.</p><p>I had simply been in a coma.</p><p>“I see…”</p><p>“Still, just to be safe… we’ll keep you hospitalized for another week for observation.”</p><p>“Okay.”</p><p>Just then, the door creaked open, and people in suits entered the room.</p><p>The man at the front was… Hunter Association President Han Ji-hyeok.</p><p>I quickly hid my right hand under the blanket.</p><p>“I’m not that stubborn. Just lie down.”</p><p>When I pretended to get up from the bed, Han Ji-hyeok waved his hand, telling me to stay put.</p><p>“What brings you here…”</p><p>Han Ji-hyeok spoke to Yu-na in a gentlemanly tone, bowing his head slightly.</p><p>“I’d like to have a private word with Hunter Yoon Seong-hyeon. Could you spare us a moment?”</p><p>When I nodded at Yu-na to let her know it was fine, everyone filed out of the hospital room.</p><p>This was the first time I’d seen Han Ji-hyeok this close.</p><p>With his neatly styled white hair and well-groomed beard, he looked like a refined middle-aged gentleman.</p><p>Even at a glance, he radiated an incredible presence.</p><p>No wonder people called him the pinnacle of Hunters.</p><p>“How’s your body?”</p><p>“Yes, I’m fine.”</p><p>Han Ji-hyeok stroked his beard, looking intrigued.</p><p>“Hunter Choi Seo-jin mentioned you the moment she regained consciousness. She requested that you be named the second-highest contributor and that we immediately use a top-tier biocatalyst… that is, an elixir on you.”</p><p>“Huh?”</p><p>‘Shit…’</p><p>That look in his eyes was a mix of suspicion and curiosity. Choi Seo-jin had her eyes closed, but the moment I demanded an elixir, she must have realized my condition was dire.</p><p>But I’d returned with all my limbs intact—no, without a single scratch. Of course he’d be suspicious.</p><p>I desperately racked my brain.</p><p>When the tentacle hit me and severed my wrist, sending me flying, every Hunter except Choi Seo-jin had been down.</p><p>Whatever it was, the boss must have done something then.</p><p>In other words, the only Hunter who saw me severely injured was Choi Seo-jin.</p><p>“But… looking at you now, aside from being unconscious, you seem perfectly fine, so I turned down the request. I hope you understand.”</p><p>“Of course… I asked for it just in case I was hurt, but luckily, I wasn’t. Did Hunter Choi Seo-jin not say anything after being refused?”</p><p>I frantically made excuses, then asked about Choi Seo-jin.</p><p>Han Ji-hyeok smiled as he replied,</p><p>“When I told her you had no injuries…”</p><p>My heart pounded. I swallowed hard to hide my tension.</p><p>“…she asked again if you were *truly* unharmed, then just said, ‘I see,’ and dropped it.”</p><p>“Ah… I see.”</p><p>Whatever the reason, Choi Seo-jin… seemed to have some sense of loyalty.</p><p>“Ah, I shouldn’t be burdening a patient with such heavy talk… What I wanted to say was thank you. We can discuss the rest after you’re discharged. A Hunter Association staff member will come explain the compensation details. For now, get plenty of rest.”</p><p>“Yes, thank you.”</p><p>Han Ji-hyeok gave a warm smile before leaving the room.</p><p>“Phew…”</p><p>‘He didn’t notice, right…?’</p>
