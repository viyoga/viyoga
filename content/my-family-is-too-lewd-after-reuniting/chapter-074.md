<p>“Huu.”</p><p>I arrived at the Central Headquarters’ Administrative Affairs building.</p><p>After asking the Hunter Association employees where office worker Kim Hyo-eun was, I made my way to the front of her office.</p><p>When they asked about the reason for my visit, I coldly replied, “Does a Hunter need a reason to see their assigned manager?” and they stopped asking.</p><p>Without bothering to knock, I pushed open the office door.</p><p>The employees working in the office all turned their gazes toward me.</p><p>Everyone in this office was an Association employee handling manager duties.</p><p>Judging by their anxious expressions, my assumption was right—it seemed a Hunter coming directly to this office was never a good sign.</p><p>Maybe the employee who checked my ID and asked about my business on the first floor had already called ahead to the office.</p><p>Taking a deep breath, I approached the nearest employee’s desk and asked,</p><p>“Excuse me. Where is Manager Kim Hyo-eun?”</p><p>“Y-Yes?”</p><p>Though everyone had quickly averted their eyes back to their monitors or paperwork, it was all for show—I could feel the entire office’s attention fixed on me.</p><p>“Ah, that… um…”</p><p>The unlucky employee who had caught my eye first fumbled, only managing to stammer out filler sounds.</p><p>Squeak—</p><p>Just then, the door to a separate office opened, and a middle-aged man stepped out.</p><p>“Ah, are you Hunter Yoon Seong-hyeon, the Hunter assigned to Manager Kim Hyo-eun?”</p><p>“Yes.”</p><p>The middle-aged man smiled warmly, but for some reason, it left a bad taste in my mouth.</p><p>Without realizing it, I felt a deep-seated disdain and prejudice toward non-Hunter Association employees taking root in my mind.</p><p>“How about we talk in my office?”</p><p>“···.”</p><p>The man opened the door to his office and waited for me to enter.</p><p>I looked around, but everyone was avoiding my gaze, so I couldn’t tell who Kim Hyo-eun was.</p><p>With no other choice, I followed the man’s lead into his office.</p><p>“Please, have a seat. Coffee? Or would you prefer tea?”</p><p>Once I was inside as he’d intended, the man drew the curtains to block the view from outside before asking.</p><p>On his desk, a black nameplate gleamed with the words “Administrative Division 3, Section Chief Im Song-hyeon.”</p><p>“Just the point…”</p><p>“Hm?”</p><p>“We’re both busy… let’s get to the point.”</p><p>At my cold retort, the man coughed a couple times, then walked to the water dispenser, filled a paper cup, placed it in front of me, and sat across from me.</p><p>“Hmm… if you insist. Are you here because of a change in your assigned manager?”</p><p>Im Song-hyeon seemed to already know why I’d come—this wasn’t his first time dealing with such a matter.</p><p>“Yes.”</p><p>“May I… ask the reason?”</p><p>I explained to Im Song-hyeon why I wanted to change my assigned manager.</p><p>Since I had already rehearsed what to say hundreds of times on the subway to the Association, the words came out smoothly without hesitation.</p><p>How Kim Hyo-eun had pushed me into this mission without disclosing any details about its background or risks, all for her own achievements.</p><p>How, if nothing had happened, she would have shamelessly defended herself with something like, “So did a bloody incident actually occur?”—and yet, now that a terrorist attack and casualties had indeed happened, she had clamped her mouth shut.</p><p>And how, when I entered this office, the atmosphere made it clear they already knew why I was here—which meant Kim Hyo-eun was fully aware of her mistakes but was sitting somewhere, holding her breath and hiding instead of taking responsibility.</p><p>Im Song-hyeon listened to me with a serious expression, occasionally stroking his chin and nodding in silent agreement.</p><p>After my long explanation, he closed his eyes, nodded, then stood up and bowed.</p><p>“First, as your superior, I apologize for what Hunter Yoon Seong-hyeon has gone through.”</p><p>His sincere attitude softened me slightly.</p><p>“But.”</p><p>But the words that came out of Im Song-hyeon’s mouth next turned my easing heart right back to hostility.</p><p>“But changing your assigned manager will be difficult. No, impossible.”</p><p>“Why?”</p><p>Min Se-rin had clearly said that Hunters could change their assigned manager whenever they wanted.</p><p>And yet here Im Song-hyeon was, boldly declaring it impossible to my face.</p><p>“Isn’t Kim Hyo-eun your first assigned manager, Hunter Yoon Seong-hyeon? For a Hunter to request a change after completing their first mission with their first manager…”</p><p>Im Song-hyeon shook his head with a look of disbelief, a small smile on his face.</p><p>“Ha.”</p><p>A hollow laugh escaped me.</p><p>I remembered what Min Se-rin had said that morning as she dried her hair before leaving the motel.</p><p>She had shown an excessive amount of hostility, warning me that the Hunter Administration Department would absolutely refuse any request to change managers.</p><p>If a manager change went through, the work would transfer to another team—which was practically the same as losing an A-rank Hunter to another team with their eyes wide open.</p><p>Min Se-rin had predicted they would reject the request with some flimsy excuse like, “It’s unprecedented.”</p><p>And her prediction had hit the mark.</p><p>When the topic of assigned managers came up, Min Se-rin had mentioned that she, being under the direct line of the Association President and Department Chief Kang Hye-na, didn’t have one herself. But she had openly expressed her disdain, calling the Administration Department a cancerous organization within the Association.</p><p>At first, I hadn’t fully understood, but now that I was facing this situation myself, a deep sense of disgust and anger boiled up inside me.</p><p>“···.”</p><p>As I stared silently into the void, Im Song-hyeon stood up as if the matter was settled, patted my shoulder, and said,</p><p>“For now… try completing a few more missions. If any other problems arise, I’ll take full responsibility and make the change for you.”</p><p>Hearing his commanding tone, as if he were the one in the higher position, my hands began to tremble.</p><p>When I decided not to ask Hye-na for help, another method Min Se-rin had recommended came to mind.</p><p>‘Ah… I really didn’t want to do this.’</p><p>In the end, I chose the last method Min Se-rin had advised.</p><p>“These maggot bastards always think they’re on top if you ask them nicely…”</p><p>“Huh? What did you just… say?”</p><p>Im Song-hyeon stared at me in shock, his voice trembling as he asked.</p><p>“Forget it. I’ll be transferring departments, so keep that in mind.”</p><p>At my continued informal speech, Im Song-hyeon let out a disbelieving laugh before replying,</p><p>“To where?”</p><p>“Investigation Division 7 of the Intelligence Bureau.”</p><p>The moment the words “Investigation Division 7 of the Intelligence Bureau” left my mouth, Im Song-hyeon’s expression stiffened rapidly.</p><p>Division 7 was a department within the Intelligence Bureau composed solely of human butchers—one of the top three most feared divisions in the entire Central Headquarters.</p><p>Min Se-rin had reasoned that since I’d performed Intelligence Bureau missions twice, it wouldn’t be strange for them to scout me. And since the administrative department cowards were terrified at the mere mention of the Intelligence Bureau, this would be an effective tactic.</p><p>I recalled Min Se-rin’s words as she swung her large breasts while holding a hairdryer, smiling as she said, ‘It doesn’t matter if it’s a lie.’</p><p>She’d added that from an outsider’s perspective, a Hunter who’d been stagnant for years, only clearing low-rank Gates, suddenly entering an S-rank Gate, being named the second-highest contributor, completing two secret Intelligence Bureau missions, and rapidly promoting to A-rank would seem like nothing less than a “black ops agent who’d been secretly working for the Intelligence Bureau all along.”</p><p>All traces of composure and amusement vanished from Im Song-hyeon’s face.</p><p>His expression filled with terror and dread.</p><p>Three years ago… two high-ranking officials from the Administrative Bureau, who had been in conflict with the Intelligence Bureau, were found hanged in their homes—along with their entire families.</p><p>The Administrative Bureau had fiercely protested, claiming it was the Intelligence Director’s doing, and a full-scale internal investigation was launched within the Association. But in the end, no evidence or suspicious findings were uncovered, and the case was closed.</p><p>At the time, public opinion in the community had been dominated by speculation that the Intelligence Bureau had begun a full-scale show of force. Yet, the case was ultimately closed without a single piece of evidence linking the Intelligence Bureau to the incident.</p><p>As public sentiment turned against the Intelligence Bureau, the Director had resigned to end the matter—but for the Administrative Bureau, the incident had been a complete defeat.</p><p>What’s more, the former Intelligence Director had returned as the Incheon Branch Director less than six months later.</p><p>The current Incheon Branch Director, ‘Park Jeong-hoo,’ had been at the center of that incident as the former Intelligence Director.</p><p>Im Song-hyeon stood there, mouth agape, staring at me in silence.</p><p>‘This is working even better than I imagined.’</p><p>Of course, I had no intention whatsoever of actually joining the Intelligence Bureau. I’d just thrown the words out there.</p><p>I picked up the paper cup, chugged the water, then stood up. Glancing back and forth between the framed photo behind his nameplate—featuring Im Song-hyeon, his wife, and their young son—and the ring on his right ring finger, I muttered,</p><p>“Tch… You have a family too, so why…? Your son is still so young. It’s such a shame…”</p><p>“Huh…?”</p><p>Im Song-hyeon stared at me with a look as if the world had just collapsed.</p><p>From his expression, it seemed he now saw me as ‘a Hunter who had deliberately avoided promotions to take on secret missions like assassinations for the Investigation Bureau, but now just wanted to return to being an ordinary Hunter.’</p><p>“I was just planning to quietly clear gates and live out the rest of my Hunter career normally… but then this happens…”</p><p>With that, I left Im Song-hyeon’s office.</p><p>The Association members who had been whispering among themselves fell silent the moment I stepped out.</p><p>As I walked slowly through them, I soon heard the sound of Im Song-hyeon’s office door swinging open, followed by hurried footsteps.</p><p>“Y-Yoon Seong-hyeon Hunter-nim!”</p><p>I turned my head slightly at the desperate call. Im Song-hyeon was staring at me, his face pale.</p><p>I just looked at him in silence. He then grabbed a file folder from beside him, marched over, and smacked a staff member on the head.</p><p>Thwack—</p><p>The staff member, dazed from the blow, stared blankly at Im Song-hyeon.</p><p>A name tag on his chest read ‘Kim Hyo-eun.’</p><p>“Blinded by performance metrics! How dare you deceive a Hunter and send them into danger!”</p><p>Thwack. Thwack.</p><p>After shouting, Im Song-hyeon struck Kim Hyo-eun’s head twice more with the folder.</p><p>‘Ah… this is a bit…’</p><p>Kim Hyo-eun appeared to be a woman in her mid-30s, perfectly ordinary.</p><p>Tears began to stream down her face.</p><p>“What’s there to cry about?! Apologize right now!”</p><p>At Im Song-hyeon’s shout, Kim Hyo-eun stood up, approached me, and bowed deeply.</p><p>“Hic…! I-I’m so sorry! Truly, I’m so sorry!”</p><p>‘No… this is way too dramatic…’</p><p>The effect had far exceeded my imagination, and now *I* was the one flustered.</p><p>I quickly approached Im Song-hyeon, patted his shoulder, and said,</p><p>“Let’s keep what happened here today a secret. Make sure your team keeps their mouths shut… Then I’ll leave the transfer in your hands.”</p><p>Im Song-hyeon rapidly nodded.</p><p>With that, I left the office.</p><p>‘Fuck… there’s no way the Investigation Bureau finds out about this, right?’</p><p>My lie had only been spoken between Im Song-hyeon and me in private. As long as he kept his mouth shut, today’s events would stay quiet.</p><p>Technically, it wasn’t even a lie. When had I ever said I *was* part of the Information Investigation Bureau?</p><p>I’d only said I was *considering* joining.</p><p>‘He didn’t look like he’d tell anyone…’</p><p>It had played out differently than I expected, but I finished the transfer process and took the subway home.</p><p>“Phew… I want to eat the food Yu-na makes.”</p>
