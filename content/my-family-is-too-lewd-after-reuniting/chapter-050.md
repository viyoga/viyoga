<p>“Seong-hyeon, why are you still B-rank?”</p><p>“Probably… because I only take on low-rank gates without properly hunting.”</p><p>After Min Se-rin and I searched the kitchen, we found two cups of ramen hidden in the corner of a cabinet.</p><p>This training facility provides boxed meals at set times. The food is decent and the portions are generous, but since we’re completely cut off from the outside world, there’s nothing to eat if you get hungry at night.</p><p>Min Se-rin turned out to be far more talkative and cheerful than I expected.</p><p>She must’ve had a hard time because of her sharp appearance.</p><p>“Hmm. You might get promoted after this operation.”</p><p>“Huh? Just for participating in one operation?”</p><p>“Yes. After the last S-rank incident, the number of A-rank Hunters dropped a lot, so the Association is internally selecting B-rank Hunters to promote. And since you were second in merit for the S-rank gate… honestly, none of that even matters. If you just beat up the A-rank re-evaluation examiners, they’ll promote you on the spot.”</p><p>“I see.”</p><p>‘A-rank Hunter…’</p><p>Becoming an A-rank Hunter would unlock countless privileges he hadn’t had access to before. Not only would he get a dedicated Association representative, but even a single commission would net him a base fee of 300 to 400 million won.</p><p>But A-ranks weren’t particularly interested in money to begin with. Or rather, their ambition to climb higher outweighed any material desires.</p><p>‘That’s why they keep risking their lives for this shit…’</p><p>“I’ll clean up the dishes! You can go in first, Seong-hyeon!”</p><p>“Huh? Then… thank you.”</p><p>Min Se-rin stared longingly at the empty ramen containers, as if she were still hungry.</p><p>‘She eats a lot…’</p><p>***</p><p>“Good. If we just follow the plan we’ve practiced for the past four days, there shouldn’t be any issues. Since the operation is tomorrow, get plenty of rest tonight.”</p><p>“Yes.”</p><p>Four days had passed since the training retreat began.</p><p>We had practiced with the team members, but whether this even qualified as an “operation” was debatable.</p><p>A total of 35 Hunters were divided into seven teams of five, each assigned to one of the building’s entrances.</p><p>The reason was simple: according to an insider’s tip and the Association’s investigation, the building had seven entrances in total.</p><p>Each team would take one door and charge in.</p><p>Security was extremely tight, and time was limited, so further reconnaissance was impossible.</p><p>In other words, this so-called “operation” was just a forced entry.</p><p>The key to this operation is to infiltrate quickly before they can destroy the evidence, secure it, and arrest the key figures.</p><p>Our team leader is Lee Seung-jun—the same person who had challenged Kang Hye-na in the conference room.</p><p>Originally from the Intelligence Investigation Bureau, he’s said to be one of the best among the Hunters gathered here when it comes to human combat.</p><p>Along with A-rank Hunters Lee Dae-seong, Choi Myeong-ho, Min Se-rin, and myself, that makes five of us.</p><p>Once we force our way in, the mission won’t be a secret anymore, so the Association will publicly announce the operation nationwide the moment it begins.</p><p>Then, immediately, the police and the Association will dispatch support units to surround the building and capture any fleeing suspects.</p><p>‘We don’t even know how many are inside… or the internal layout… and we’re just supposed to go in? Can this even be called an operation?’</p><p>“Alright, then get some rest today and don’t go doing anything unnecessary.”</p><p>“Yes.”</p><p>Everyone exchanged greetings with forced smiles, but the tension was palpable in their expressions. I felt it too.</p><p>***</p><p>At 4 AM, we’re crouched in the dim forest, staring down at the building.</p><p>The structure was far larger and more ominous in person than it had appeared in photos.</p><p>“We move in three minutes. Stay sharp. The door we’re taking is right there.”</p><p>Choi Seung-jun pointed at the building as he spoke.</p><p>On the way here, the Association had issued us masks. Every Hunter wore the same design to conceal their identities during the mission.</p><p>If our faces were exposed, the criminal organization might retaliate against the Hunters themselves or their families, so the masks were a precaution.</p><p>I drew the knife the Association had provided and ran my fingers over it.</p><p>‘A weapon specialized for human combat…’</p><p>We’d each been given a standard dagger, a harpoon-shaped dagger, and six throwing knives.</p><p>The harpoon-shaped dagger was designed to rip out an enemy’s organs. Once it pierced flesh, the barbed blades would tear through the body.</p><p>‘But the goal is to capture them alive…’</p><p>“One minute… left. Don’t worry about the other teams—just focus on the door and run. And don’t forget the key to this mission: the ‘surprise gift.’”</p><p>Silence filled the air, broken only by the shaky breaths of my teammates. Min Se-rin turned her head, glanced at me, and gave a small nod.</p><p>Lee Seung-jun, true to his Intelligence Bureau background, showed no signs of nerves.</p><p>‘This probably isn’t his first rodeo…’</p><p>It was too dark to see, but I knew 35 Hunters were hidden throughout this forest, each near the entrance they’d been assigned. Somewhere out there, Hye-na was also wearing a mask, waiting in the shadows.</p><p>“Ten seconds… I’ll say it again—ignore the other teams. Our only goal is the door. Got it?”</p><p>The team members nodded and got into position to run.</p><p>“Operation starts now.”</p><p>Seung-jun took the lead and started sprinting at full speed, and we followed right behind him.</p><p>Hunters from other teams, who had been hiding in the darkness, began to reveal themselves.</p><p>Two guards stood watch at each door. Soon, the area grew noisy, and the two gang members guarding our target door locked eyes with us.</p><p>“W-what the hell?!”</p><p>“Fuck! Call HQ, now!”</p><p>Seung-jun swiftly pulled out a knife and hurled it at the guard. He was so practiced that it took less than a second from drawing the dagger to throwing it.</p><p>Thunk.</p><p>As the blade buried itself in one gangster’s throat, the other frantically pulled a walkie-talkie from his pocket.</p><p>Slice.</p><p>“N-now—! Gk—! Urk.”</p><p>Seung-jun moved like the wind, closing the distance and slashing the throat of the man holding the walkie-talkie.</p><p>The gangster, his throat deeply cut, stared in horror as blood sprayed from his neck like a fountain. He clutched the wound, but blood gushed between his fingers. His knees buckled, and he collapsed in silence.</p><p>Seung-jun pulled the throwing dagger from the dead man’s throat, muttering to himself.</p><p>“Rusty from disuse, huh? …Well, shall we head in?”</p><p>With practiced ease, he retrieved a card from the corpse’s pocket and swiped it at the entrance.</p><p>Beep—</p><p>Click.</p><p>The door unlocked.</p><p>Other teams’ Hunters were also eliminating guards and opening their doors.</p><p>‘As expected… Hunters from the Intelligence Bureau. If they mark you as a target, you’ll die without a trace.’</p><p>As we pushed through the building’s door, a pungent, unpleasant chemical odor hit us.</p><p>Soon, loud noises echoed from the staircase, and a group of at least twenty gang members appeared before us.</p><p>Each of them was armed with weapons typical of Hunters—longswords, daggers, axes, hammers.</p><p>“What the fuck… who the hell are you?”</p><p>A burly man with a scar across his face cursed as he stepped forward through the crowd. The others immediately straightened up in respect.</p><p>“This is a damn mess. Who was guarding this door?”</p><p>“Ki-ho and Chang-su, boss.”</p><p>“Useless bastards… Hey! Bring me my weapon.”</p><p>A burly man gestured, and one of his underlings brought over a katana and handed it to him.</p><p>We drew our weapons and took up battle stances.</p><p>Lee Seung-jun turned his head to look at us and said,</p><p>“Looks like… our team drew the short straw.”</p><p>The burly man drew his sword, let out a loud laugh, then ran a hand through his hair and said,</p><p>“One look and… I can tell you’re here on an Association inspection… Fuck, isn’t this a bit much? Does the Association have no sense of decency? If you’re gonna attack, at least do it during the day…”</p><p>“Surrender.”</p><p>Lee Seung-jun cut the burly man off and said,</p><p>“What the fuck, man… Can’t you let a guy finish his— whatever. If we surrender, you’ll spare us?”</p><p>“Drop your weapons and surrender peacefully.”</p><p>“Did you pour glue in your ears or something? You won’t even answer a simple question… Alright, boys, kill ‘em all.”</p><p>“Yes, boss!”</p><p>The underlings drew their weapons and prepared to charge at us.</p><p>Cold sweat trickled down my face under the mask, and my heart began to race.</p><p>“Everyone, stay sharp. In a chaotic battle, one moment of carelessness is the end.”</p><p>“Huuu…”</p><p>Soon, one of the underlings lunged at us, and the rest followed.</p><p>“Kill them all!”</p><p>“Fucking bastards, no fear at all!”</p><p>Whoosh</p><p>An underling swung an axe at me. I twisted my body to dodge, then kicked him in the solar plexus.</p><p>Crunch</p><p>“Gaaahk!”</p><p>I felt a few of his ribs snap under my kick. The axe-wielding man collapsed to the ground, coughing up blood.</p><p>‘Slow… At best, C-rank level… The fact that he’s coughing up blood means a rib fragment probably punctured his lung…’</p><p>“You fucking bastard! Die!”</p><p>Three underlings surrounded me.</p><p>‘Don’t overthink it… Capture or whatever, just survive first.’</p><p>***</p><p>Squelch</p><p>“Aaaah! Fucking shit!”</p><p>An underling’s forearm was severed, and he clutched the bleeding stump, rolling around on the blood-soaked floor and screaming.</p><p>“My arm! My fucking arm! Shit!”</p><p>“Haa…”</p><p>The building’s floor was now a sea of blood, and every step I took made a squelching sound.</p><p>The blood-soaked floor was littered with the lucky ones who had merely passed out, the unlucky ones already dead, and those gasping their final breaths as they bled out.</p><p>“Is everyone okay?”</p><p>“Yes.”</p><p>Min Se-rin scanned the area, checking on her teammates.</p><p>Though their protective suits were torn in places, none of the team members were injured.</p><p>“Then…”</p><p>Our gazes turned to the hulking man from earlier—the one who had been so full of himself—now kneeling, his face a bloody mess.</p><p>Lee Seung-jun had one hand gripping the man’s hair while the other was shoved into his mouth.</p><p>Eight adult teeth lay scattered on the floor.</p><p>Lee Seung-jun was drenched in blood, yet not a single wound marred his body.</p><p>“No… There are two paths leading underground, right? Where the hell are the kids you captured? You really won’t talk?”</p><p>Crunch.</p><p>Lee Seung-jun yanked out another of the man’s teeth and tossed it to the ground.</p><p>“KKKAAAHHH! I—I KKKK—HIBALROMMA! DDEUYEOBEORINDA!”</p><p>“…”</p><p>We could only swallow hard as we watched the scene unfold.</p><p>“I’ll ask one last time… Where are they? Hmm?”</p><p>Crunch.</p><p>The man’s canine tooth was ripped out and thrown to the floor. Blood gushed from his mouth.</p><p>“KKKAAAHHH! I DIDN’T DO ANYTHING! KKK—THEY SAID ONLY HIGH-RANKING EXECUTIVES CAN GO IN THERE! Uuuuugh!”</p><p>“Is that so?”</p><p>CRACK!</p><p>Lee Seung-jun, still gripping the man’s hair, drove his fist into the man’s face. With a sickening crunch, the hulking man’s nose shattered, and he collapsed unconscious.</p><p>Lee Seung-jun turned to us and said,</p><p>“Hmm… I tried persuading him, but it seems he really doesn’t know.”</p><p>‘Persuading? This crazy bastard…’</p><p>“We’ll have to split up and head down separately.”</p><p>“…”</p><p>“Seong-hyeon and I will take the right path, so the rest of you should go left. If you run into danger, do *not* engage—radio us immediately. We’ll come right away. Shall we get moving?”</p><p>‘Why me…?’</p><p>As soon as Lee Seung-jun finished speaking, he started down the stairs.</p><p>After exchanging a quick nod with the rest of the team, I followed Lee Seung-jun down the staircase.</p>
