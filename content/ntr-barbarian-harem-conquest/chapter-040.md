<p>Word about a strange barbarian wielding a crimson axe spread through Sylvandar overnight.</p>

<p>Because of these wild rumors, the arena was much fuller than yesterday.</p>

<p>Elven nobles who normally ignored the early bracket matches now packed the expensive lower tiers.</p>

<p>Excitement crackled in the warm morning air while thousands of spectators waited for the second round to begin.</p>

<p>Lord Caerith stepped into the sand pit to a wave of thunderous applause.</p>

<p>This particular high elf represented everything the crowd adored. His aristocratic features were flawless, and his silver-enchanted armor caught the bright sunlight perfectly.</p>

<p>Turning gracefully toward the royal box, Caerith performed an elegant bow to honor the ruling family.</p>

<p>Kane stood on the opposite side of the fighting grounds with his hands resting casually on his belt.</p>

<p>Refusing to offer any polite bows or waves to the crowd, he simply rolled his shoulders to loosen his muscles.</p>

<p>’Show them a little blood,’ Kane thought, keeping his expression bored.</p>

<p>’Lothburk’s spies need to think I struggle against enchanted gear. I have to sell this performance perfectly to lure that four-armed freak out tonight.’</p>

<p>Ding!</p>

<p>A loud gong echoed across the stadium to start the match.</p>

<p>Caerith activated his armor immediately.</p>

<p>Bright blue magic wrapped around the noble’s legs to grant him a huge speed boost.</p>

<p>Rushing forward faster than a normal eye could track, Caerith launched a technically perfect sword combination.</p>

<p>Six precise strikes targeted Kane’s throat, ribs, and leading knee simultaneously.</p>

<p>Slash.</p>

<p>Dodging five of the strikes proved easy enough.</p>

<p>Kane twisted his torso and sidestepped the flashing silver blade with practiced grace.</p>

<p>But he intentionally slowed his footwork for the final thrust.</p>

<p>The sharp edge caught his left arm, slicing a clean line right across his bicep.</p>

<p>Red blood welled up instantly so the entire stadium could see the injury.</p>

<p>A collective gasp rippled through the audience because they finally saw the arrogant barbarian bleed.</p>

<p>&quot;He’s a barbarian after all...&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;Fuck, my money was on him...&quot;</p>

<p>’Good,’ Kane thought, ignoring the mild sting radiating from his arm.</p>

<p>’Let them see the blood. Let them think his magic is actually troubling me.’</p>

<p>To sell the fake weakness further, he staggered backward two extra steps.</p>

<p>Caerith read the stumble as genuine difficulty and pressed his attack aggressively.</p>

<p>The noble completely abandoned his defensive footwork to chase a quick, glorious victory.</p>

<p>That was the mistake Kane wanted him to make.</p>

<p>Reaching over his shoulder, Kane drew Mjoldr.</p>

<p>The familiar crimson aura erupted from the steel instantly.</p>

<p>Silence fell over the stadium as the crowd remembered that terrifying glow from the previous day.</p>

<p>Caerith hesitated for just a fraction of a second, causing his confident smirk to falter.</p>

<p>Kane stepped inside the noble’s wide guard and swung his weapon in from the left in a controlled arc.</p>

<p>[Hidden Property Activated: Soul Brand]</p>

<p>The red blade slammed hard against the elven breastplate.</p>

<p>Creak.</p>

<p>Caerith’s enchanted armor shattered completely upon impact.</p>

<p>Expensive defensive magic dissolved into sparkling fragments that drifted across the sand like dying fireflies.</p>

<p>Stripped of every advantage his family’s money bought him, the noble froze in his suddenly useless metal suit.</p>

<p>&quot;Ah... wait!&quot;</p>

<p>A swift second swing disarmed Caerith cleanly.</p>

<p>Kane hooked the flat of his axe around the silver sword hilt and ripped it right out of the elf’s grip.</p>

<p>Stopping his blade exactly one inch from Caerith’s pale throat, Kane claimed the victory without shedding any more blood.</p>

<p>Absolute silence gripped the arena.</p>

<p>Lowering his weapon slowly, Kane did something that made every watching noble lean forward in their seats simultaneously.</p>

<p>He turned his back on his defeated opponent and faced the royal box directly.</p>

<p>Searching the highest balcony, he locked eyes with Prince Aelrindor across the entire stadium.</p>

<p>Kane nodded once.</p>

<p>This gesture was not a sign of submission, nor was it a wild barbarian challenge. It was a clear acknowledgment between two people who both knew exactly what was happening behind the scenes.</p>

<p>Prince Aelrindor maintained a completely neutral expression on his handsome face.</p>

<p>However, his right hand gripped his armrest tightly enough that his knuckles whitened visibly. The royal heir understood the silent message perfectly.</p>

<p>’How dare a mere lowlife mock me?’</p>

<p>Chaos cascaded through the stadium a few seconds later.</p>

<p>&quot;WOAH!&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;HE WON AGAIN!&quot;</p>

<p>The elven commentator stammered into his crystal microphone, struggling to describe how a common savage broke a priceless set of royal armor with a single hit.</p>

<p>Three wealthy noble families sitting in the front row immediately started arguing loudly over whether to adjust their tournament bets.</p>

<p>Standing in the competitors’ section, Rutheus watched the display with a wide grin.</p>

<p>The giant veteran easily recognized the deliberate one-arm disadvantage Kane used to bait the noble into a trap.</p>

<p>Up in the general stands, Kessa watched intently.</p>

<p>Her eyes remained fixed on the barbarian warlord, holding an expression of deep respect that had not changed since she lost to him yesterday.</p>

<p>Firlia observed the fighting grounds from a high window inside the registration offices.</p>

<p>She pressed her hands flat against the glass, knowing exactly why Kane was staring down the royal family.</p>

<p>Meanwhile, Grieselda stood quietly near the tunnel exit. Instead of watching the exciting conclusion in the sand pit, she kept her eyes focused solely on the cheering crowd above.</p>

<p>&quot;YOU GOT LUCKY.&quot;</p>

<p>Ignoring the renewed cheers and boos from the audience, Kane walked off the field.</p>

<p>He didn’t wave or acknowledge their sudden praise.</p>

<p>Slipping into the shadows of the fighters’ corridor, he sheathed his weapon.</p>

<p>The cut on his arm still bled slightly, but his high vitality was already knitting the torn flesh back together.</p>

<p>Footsteps echoed softly ahead of him in the dim passage.</p>

<p>Firlia stood waiting near the intersection, holding a small rolled parchment in her hands.</p>

<p>She wore her official green robes again, but her posture was much more relaxed than at their first meeting.</p>

<p>&quot;You played a very dangerous game out there,&quot; Firlia whispered, stepping out of the shadows to meet him.</p>

<p>&quot;Aww..., are you worried about me?&quot; Kane smiled, wiping a stray drop of blood from his bicep.</p>

<p>&quot;Did Lothburk’s men watch the fight closely?&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;They watched from the east gate standing section,&quot; she confirmed, holding out the folded parchment.</p>

<p>&quot;And they looked very pleased when Caerith landed that strike on your arm. They think your reaction speed is too slow to handle fast combinations.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;Perfect,&quot; Kane chuckled, taking the parchment and sliding it into his pouch.</p>

<p>&quot;They bought the performance completely. Now they will report back to their boss and tell him I’m an easy target.&quot;</p>
