<p>Murmurs of utter disgust rippled through the upper balconies, followed instantly by a chorus of nobles calling for a swift execution.</p>

<p>&quot;Sever his head right now!&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;How dare this savage speak of the royal war room!&quot;</p>

<p>Raising a single, trembling hand, Aeloria silenced the roaring court while her blue eyes burned holes into Kane’s relaxed expression.</p>

<p>&quot;If it were my choice, Warlord, your skull would already decorate our border gates,&quot; Aeloria hissed, her voice echoing in the sudden quiet.</p>

<p>&quot;But Her Majesty demands you alive. Guards, take him to the lowest cells.&quot;</p>

<p>’Saved by the Queen again,’ Kane mused, allowing the spearmen to drag him backward by his chains.</p>

<p>’I should really send her flowers once I conquer this place.’</p>

<p>Down in the freezing depths of the prison beneath the roots, time seemed to blur until the iron door of his cell finally creaked open.</p>

<p>Instead of the regular guards, Minister Aeloria stepped into the dim light alone.</p>

<p>Gone were her formal council robes, replaced by something far more sinister and revealing.</p>

<p>Long crimson hair flowed in wild waves down her back, framing a face dominated by sharp blue eyes and a cruel, twisted smile.</p>

<p>She wore little more than woven leather straps and translucent crimson silks that left her almost completely bare, stepping toward him with a braided mana-whip coiled in her manicured hand.</p>

<p>’Trying to distract me or just dressing for the occasion?’</p>

<p>Kane wondered, testing the tension of the chains binding his wrists to the stone wall.</p>

<p>’Either way, red is definitely her color.’</p>

<p>&quot;You think you won today,&quot; Aeloria whispered, locking the iron door behind her.</p>

<p>&quot;I usually do.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;You think it’s a joke?&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;No.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;The court wanted to execute you right there on the floor. They wanted to tear you apart.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;Yet here I am, tied up with a half-naked elf in a basement. I call that an upgrade.&quot;</p>

<p>Cracking her wrist, Aeloria sent the whip lashing across his chest.</p>

<p>Slap.</p>

<p>A stinging line of fire bloomed across his collarbone, tearing through his ragged shirt.</p>

<p>Kane didn’t even flinch, keeping his eyes locked on her face.</p>

<p>&quot;Was that supposed to hurt?&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;I am going to peel that arrogance off you strip by strip, barbarian.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;You can try.&quot;</p>

<p>Another crack echoed through the cell, biting into his shoulder.</p>

<p>’Her swing is wide and fueled purely by anger,’ Kane analyzed, watching her chest heave with every strike.</p>

<p>’She doesn’t want information. She just wants my ego broken.’</p>

<p>&quot;You embarrassed my border forces, humiliated my commanders, and stood in my court like you own the continent,&quot; Aeloria sneered, stepping closer until he could smell a faint perfume of lotus mixed with sweat.</p>

<p>&quot;I merely walked through a door you left open.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;I will make you scream.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;My safe word is Menual.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;Insolent dog!&quot;</p>

<p>Slap.</p>

<p>Aeloria swung the whip again, aiming for his ribs, but Kane simply flexed his abdominal muscles to absorb the blow.</p>

<p>&quot;Is this how you run a ministry?&quot; Kane chuckled.</p>

<p>&quot;Because your interrogation techniques need a lot of work.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;I’m not interrogating you,&quot; she spat, pacing in front of him while trailing the leather cord across the floor.</p>

<p>&quot;I am teaching you your place.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;Do you know why I came down here instead of sending the royal torturer?&quot; Aeloria asked, circling him slowly.</p>

<p>&quot;Because you have a crush on me?&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;Because I wanted to watch the light die in those arrogant eyes myself.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;You are going to be waiting a very long time.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;We captured your commanders,&quot; she lied smoothly, her blue eyes flashing with malice.</p>

<p>&quot;They are being broken right now in the adjacent cells.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;Try again,&quot; Kane smirked.</p>

<p>&quot;My body tracks my party members. I know exactly where they are, and it isn’t here. You couldn’t catch Thora if she tied her bootlaces together.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;You rely too much on your strange skills, Warlord.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;I rely on winning.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;A few months ago, this city threw flowers at your feet,&quot; Aeloria continued, ignoring his taunt.</p>

<p>&quot;They chanted your name during the tournament.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;They threw themselves at me in the taverns too,&quot; Kane smiled, a wicked glint in his eye.</p>

<p>&quot;Some of your nobles have very flexible joints.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;You tainted our culture!&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;I gave them a good time.&quot;</p>

<p>Shrieking with frustration, Aeloria unleashed a flurry of vicious strikes that tore his remaining shirt into bloody ribbons.</p>

<p>Pain flared across his chest and arms, but his system passively cataloged the damage while his Warlord vitality held his posture rigid.</p>

<p>Fuck, it hurts.</p>

<p>&quot;A filthy barbarian like you,&quot; Aeloria panted, pausing to catch her breath while glaring at his unbroken smile.</p>

<p>&quot;Never deserve the champion title... you never deserved to walk our streets or breathe our air.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;And yet I took your tournament trophy, your treasury, and your dignity.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;I am erasing all of that today.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;Is that why you dressed up?&quot; Kane taunted, nodding toward her barely-there attire.</p>

<p>&quot;Hoping I would beg?&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;I want you hollow,&quot; she hissed, stepping right into his personal space.</p>

<p>Catching the torn fabric in the leather cord, she gave a vicious yank, ripping the last of his garments away entirely.</p>

<p>Standing completely naked and chained to the wall, Kane simply rolled his shoulders, completely unaffected by the cold air or her glare.</p>

<p>&quot;Well, if you wanted to see me naked, Aeloria, you really just had to ask politely.&quot;</p>

<p>Stepping back, Aeloria let the leather whip coil loosely by her hip.</p>

<p>Her blue eyes swept over his scarred chest and bruised ribs, searching for any sign of shame.</p>

<p>Finding none, her frantic anger melted into a cold, piercing edge.</p>

<p>&quot;You make jokes while bleeding in a cage,&quot; she whispered, tilting her head.</p>

<p>&quot;But beneath the boasting, you are nothing but a monster acting on base instinct. Tell me, Warlord. How many people have you forced yourself onto?&quot;</p>

<p>The teasing smirk vanished from his lips.</p>

<p>That sudden shift in her tone caught him off guard, digging into a rare, quiet corner of his mind he usually kept locked away.</p>

<p>His expression turned grim.</p>

<p>’Thora,’ Kane thought, the memory flashing unbidden beneath his stoic mask.</p>

<p>’I crossed a line when I first transmigrated into this world.’</p>

<p>The Moon-Silver chains clinked softly against the stone as he shifted his weight, refusing to break eye contact with the minister.</p>

<p>&quot;Two,&quot; Kane answered, his voice stripped of all its previous humor.</p>

<p>Aeloria scoffed, a cruel smile returning to her painted lips.</p>

<p>&quot;Only two? I suppose I should be impressed by your restraint. Your kind doesn’t know love. All you do is take what you want.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;I took what I thought was mine.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;You steal,&quot; she countered, stepping closer again until the warmth of her bare skin almost brushed his chest.</p>

<p>&quot;You ruin things because you are incapable of building them. You are a savage playing at being a king.&quot;</p>
