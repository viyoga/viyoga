<p>Walking out of the underground vault, the air shifted from stale basement chill to the warm breeze of the capital.</p>

<p>Kane led the way, his foot ringing against the spiraling stone stairs, while violet energy still pulsed faintly beneath his skin.</p>

<p>Misha followed silently, her eyes scanning the shifting ambient magic of the city, and Sarah trailed them, adjusting her dress with newfound purpose.</p>

<p>Pushing open the grand courtyard doors, Kane stepped out onto the elevated marble terrace.</p>

<p>Below them stood an ocean of armor.</p>

<p>Thousands of soldiers packed the royal square.</p>

<p>Surrendered Menual royal guards stood shoulder-to-shoulder.</p>

<p>The scale of the assembled forces stretched all the way to the outer gates.</p>

<p>Spears and halberds caught the afternoon sun, creating a blinding sea of steel.</p>

<p>Silence cascaded across the courtyard the moment Kane appeared on the balcony.</p>

<p>Every eye locked onto the gladiator with red hair and a crimson axe strapped to his back.</p>

<p>They felt the shift in the city’s wards.</p>

<p>They knew a new sovereign held the reins.</p>

<p>’I need to give them a purpose to fight.’</p>

<p>Channeling his new territory mechanics, Kane projected his voice.</p>

<p>It rolled over the square like a booming thunderclap, reaching every corner of the capital.</p>

<p>&quot;YOU ALL NOW BELONG TO THE BLOODFANG TRIBE!&quot; Kane roared, his voice leaving no room for debate.</p>

<p>&quot;NOT ELVES! NOT MILFHEIM! ONLY BARBARIANS!&quot;</p>

<p>The proclamation hung in the air for a split second before the reaction hit.</p>

<p>Caught in the infectious, overpowering wave of his Conqueror’s aura, the Menual soldiers yielded.</p>

<p>Thousands of southern guards dropped to one knee, lowering their banners to the pavement. The submission was total.</p>

<p>They belonged to him now.</p>

<p>Sarah watched the display from the edge of the terrace, her eyes wide with a mix of awe and calculation.</p>

<p>&quot;I leave the ledgers to you,&quot; Kane said, tossing a passing glance over his shoulder.</p>

<p>&quot;Secure the treasury. Pay our mercenaries their bonuses, and distribute a month’s wages to the surviving city watch. Buy their loyalty before the sun sets.&quot;</p>

<p>Bowing her head gracefully, Sarah smiled.</p>

<p>&quot;Consider it done. I will ensure every vault is properly audited.&quot;</p>

<p>She turned and swept back into the palace, her hips swaying with authority, eager to drown herself in his newly acquired wealth.</p>

<p>Once the terrace was cleared of attendants, Kane walked away from the cheering square.</p>

<p>He found a quiet alcove overlooking the southern gardens and leaned against a marble pillar.</p>

<p>Misha stood a few paces away, her white hair catching the breeze.</p>

<p>She stared out toward the horizon, her delicate hands folded neatly in front of her gown.</p>

<p>’Usually, my little oracle has a piece of advice ready before I even know I need it.’</p>

<p>&quot;You have been quiet,&quot; Kane noted.</p>

<p>Turning her head slightly, Misha met his gaze.</p>

<p>Her dual-tone voice carried a rare note of caution.</p>

<p>&quot;That core is useless without the tree, husband. You absorbed a parasite, but a parasite needs a host to feed on.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;I know,&quot; Kane replied, a smirk touching his lips.</p>

<p>&quot;I will take the tree.&quot;</p>

<p>Misha fully turned to face him, her golden rings swirling with troubled currents.</p>

<p>&quot;That’s dangerous. You are talking about invading the heart of the Elven territory. Queen Sylva is not some local noble you can bully.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;She needed me to fight her battles,&quot; Kane shrugged, entirely unconcerned.</p>

<p>&quot;I figure she owes me.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;The Elven Queen was only good to you because she benefited from your strength,&quot; Misha warned, stepping closer.</p>

<p>&quot;She used your axes to secure her borders. She will not tolerate this. Taking the World Tree means breaking the foundation of her entire race. She will declare a holy war.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;Well, good thing I do not care,&quot; Kane laughed, the sound deep and resonant.</p>

<p>He pushed off the pillar, closing the distance between them.</p>

<p>&quot;Now I have the Milfheim alliance, haven’t I? Let Sylva send her rangers. I reckon my new southern army and your northern sisters can handle a few pointy-eared archers.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;But still...&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;Leave that,&quot; Kane interrupted.</p>

<p>Reaching out, he let his fingers graze the side of her cheek.</p>

<p>Her skin felt cool and flawless, a stark contrast to the rough, violent world he usually occupied.</p>

<p>’I need to remind her to live in the moment.’</p>

<p>&quot;When are we going to do it?&quot; Kane asked, dropping his voice to a low, intimate rumble.</p>

<p>Misha blinked, her tactical train of thought derailing instantly.</p>

<p>&quot;Do what?&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;Couple things,&quot; Kane teased, letting his gaze travel deliberately down her gown before returning to her eyes.</p>

<p>&quot;I just claimed an empire. Usually, there is a celebration involved.&quot;</p>

<p>Misha didn’t blush.</p>

<p>Instead, she looked up at him with a different expression.</p>

<p>Her golden eyes searched his face, stripping away the warlord to see the man underneath.</p>

<p>&quot;My heart cannot handle the pleasure,&quot; Misha stated matter-of-factly, offering a rare glimpse into her physical limitations.</p>

<p>&quot;The seraphic blessing I carry demands emotional equilibrium. A surge of that magnitude would cause my vessel to fail.&quot;</p>

<p>Kane paused, absorbing the reality of her words.</p>

<p>She wasn’t rejecting him.</p>

<p>The oracle could see the threads of fate, but her physical body remained fragile.</p>

<p>Letting out an understanding laugh, Kane stepped back just enough to give her space.</p>

<p>&quot;I know,&quot; Kane smiled, his eyes softening.</p>

<p>&quot;So I will wait.&quot;</p>

<p>Misha tilted her head, clearly surprised by his easy acceptance.</p>

<p>&quot;If something I do makes you uncomfortable, report it to me directly,&quot; Kane instructed, his tone shifting from teasing to sincere.</p>

<p>&quot;We are married, after all. You are my co-ruler, not a prize I won in a tavern brawl. I set the pace, but you tell me where the boundaries are.&quot;</p>

<p>A tiny, almost imperceptible smile touched Misha’s lips.</p>

<p>&quot;Understood, husband.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;Good.&quot; Kane turned back toward the balcony, letting his gaze sweep over the sprawling, white-domed capital stretching out beneath the setting sun.</p>

<p>Resting his hand on the pommel of his axe, Kane took a deep breath of the evening air.</p>

<p>It was time to establish his new empire, and the rest of the world was going to feel the shift.</p>
