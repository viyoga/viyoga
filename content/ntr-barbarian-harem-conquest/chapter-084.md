<p>Kane parried, letting the Soul Brand activate on the third strike.</p>

<p>[Hidden Property Activated: Soul Brand]</p>

<p>[Target defenses shattered permanently]</p>

<p>Ravan’s defenses shattered.</p>

<p>His iron armor remained physically intact, but the inherent combat confidence built by two decades of winning evaporated instantly.</p>

<p>’Shit...’</p>

<p>His eyes widened in sudden, inexplicable terror as the next swing was half a second too slow.</p>

<p>Ducking under the sluggish blade, Kane stepped inside his guard.</p>

<p>He drove the iron haft of Mjoldr directly into Ravan’s solar plexus with bone-crushing force, collapsing the man’s lungs.</p>

<p>&quot;Gah!&quot;</p>

<p>As the commander folded forward, gasping for air, Kane brought his knee up, smashing Ravan’s nose into a bloody ruin.</p>

<p>Bam.</p>

<p>Spitting out broken teeth, Ravan collapsed into the muck.</p>

<p>&quot;COMMANDER DOWN!&quot; a terrified soldier shrieked.</p>

<p>Organized chaos devolved instantly into routing panic.</p>

<p>Standing triumphantly over the bleeding officer, Kane glared at the eight hundred elite soldiers who just watched their beloved commander get utterly dismantled by a single man who fell off a cliff barely two minutes ago.</p>

<p>Raising Mjoldr high, the crimson aura flooded the narrow gorge.</p>

<p>&quot;Yield!&quot; Kane commanded.</p>

<p>His voice carried clearly through the Conqueror’s Presence passive, hitting that specific, terrifying frequency that forced men to drop their weapons and listen.</p>

<p>Fighting didn’t stop immediately.</p>

<p>It took another four bloody minutes for Seraphine’s soldiers to climb down from the ridge, executing anyone who refused to kneel.</p>

<p>Rutheus and his forty tribal warriors appeared at the far end of the channel, completely cutting off any hope of retreat.</p>

<p>Arriving from the mobile route right on schedule, Kessa sealed the eastern gap, her claws already dripping with blood.</p>

<p>The outcome was truly decided the very second that column foolishly entered the pass.</p>

<p>[Combat Concluded: Central Pass Engagement]</p>

<p>[Enemy force neutralized - 847 engaged, 312 casualties, 535 captured]</p>

<p>[Note: The siege engines never deployed]</p>

<p>[They are still sitting in the column behind the choke point. Intact.]</p>

<p>[New equipment available: 12 Menual siege engines - repurposed for defensive use]</p>

<p>[Experience gained: Level 38 → Level 41]</p>

<p>[Stat increases applied]</p>

<p>[Note: You now own siege engines]</p>

<p>[The queen is going to have questions about that too.]</p>

<p>Flicking a chunk of gore off his axe, Kane started laughing.</p>

<p>The pass settled into a chilling quiet.</p>

<p>It carried that silence that always follows a profound amount of organized brutality.</p>

<p>Five hundred and thirty-five perimeter to ensure no late reinforcements crashed the party.</p>

<p>Wandering through the disarmed column, Kessa casually looted the best rations and shiny trinkets from the terrified captives.</p>

<p>She flashed her fangs whenever a soldier complained.</p>

<p>Taking a seat on a boulder, Kane allowed Thora to examine his battered torso.</p>

<p>&quot;You jumped off a ridge,&quot; Thora states, her tone completely devoid of sympathy.</p>

<p>&quot;It seemed like the fastest route down to the actual fight,&quot; Kane replies defensively.</p>

<p>&quot;Fifteen vertical meters,&quot; she points out, slathering a stinging herbal salve across his bruised skin.</p>

<p>&quot;I used a few rocks to break the fall,&quot; he counters.</p>

<p>&quot;Approximately.&quot;</p>

<p>Snatching the linen binding, she wraps it around his chest and pulls it significantly tighter than medically necessary.</p>

<p>The sharp pressure forces a short gasp from his lips.</p>

<p>&quot;Noted,&quot; Kane wheezes.</p>

<p>’I conjecture I earned that,’ Kane thinks, watching her tie the knot with precision.</p>

<p>’She’s definitely enjoying this punishment.’</p>

<p>Sliding gracefully down the rocky incline, Sira approaches the boulder.</p>

<p>Her eyes are already calculating the updated logistics.</p>

<p>&quot;The rear elements of their column,&quot; Sira begins, skipping any pleasantries.</p>

<p>&quot;Those siege engines stopped well before the choke point when the front lines panicked and compressed. They’re sitting completely intact on the eastern approach road.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;I figured they would be,&quot; Kane says, testing his bound ribs with a shallow breath.</p>

<p>&quot;Twelve full engines,&quot; Sira notes, adjusting her bow sling.</p>

<p>&quot;Based on the specifications you ripped from that scouting commander, you know what they were originally built to accomplish.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;Taking defensive walls perfectly intact,&quot; Kane answers.</p>

<p>&quot;They execute a controlled breach rather than burning everything down to ash.&quot;</p>

<p>Sira tilts her head, offering a rare, sharp smile.</p>

<p>&quot;Engineering works both ways, Chief.&quot;</p>

<p>Kane looks at the archer, waiting for the rest of her thought.</p>

<p>&quot;If a machine is built to breach a wall cleanly,&quot; Sira explains, &quot;it is also designed to establish one. Or reinforce an existing position. Or, hypothetically, make a mountain pass permanently impassable to any large-scale military movement.&quot;</p>

<p>’How is a barbarian this smart?’</p>

<p>Scanning the quiet channel, Kane looks at the towering stone walls.</p>

<p>He pictures the dozen captured machines sitting just a kilometer away.</p>

<p>&quot;How long to repurpose them?&quot; Kane asks.</p>

<p>It isn’t a question; it is a direct demand for a timeline.</p>

<p>&quot;I would need to examine their internal components,&quot; Sira admits.</p>

<p>&quot;But constructed mechanisms follow basic logic. The primary function changes the moment you alter their physical orientation.&quot;</p>

<p>Stepping into their circle, Seraphine joins the conversation.</p>

<p>Her white armor remains surprisingly clean despite the recent slaughter.</p>

<p>&quot;My engineers possess distinct experience with Menual siege equipment,&quot; Seraphine offers smoothly.</p>

<p>&quot;We captured several smaller models three years ago during a border skirmish. My people fully reverse-engineered their firing mechanisms.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;You’ve soldiers who know how to operate them,&quot; Kane notes, impressed by the sudden overlapping of resources.</p>

<p>&quot;Operate, maintain, and completely modify,&quot; Seraphine clarifies proudly.</p>

<p>&quot;How long to turn twelve offensive engines into permanent channel fortifications?&quot; Kane asks, locking eyes with the First Blade.</p>

<p>&quot;Give me forty-eight hours,&quot; Seraphine promises without hesitation.</p>

<p>’Repurposed engines, a World Tree node causing panic, and high ridge firing positions,’ Kane calculates mentally.</p>

<p>’This vulnerable pass just stopped being a strategic liability. It becomes a permanent fortress.’</p>

<p>&quot;Get it done,&quot; Kane orders.</p>

<p>Nodding briskly, Seraphine turns on her heel and marches off to shout orders at her lieutenants.</p>

<p>Approaching from the perimeter, Rutheus carries a small tin cup in his remaining hand.</p>

<p>Handing the water to Kane, the veteran takes a seat on the adjacent rock.</p>

<p>They sit in comfortable silence for a long moment, watching the prisoners flinch every time a Milfheim guard walks past.</p>

<p>&quot;The elven queen,&quot; Rutheus finally rumbles, scratching his scarred chin.</p>

<p>&quot;Her precious war room will demand these captured soldiers get processed through official imperial channels,&quot; Rutheus warns.</p>

<p>&quot;Bureaucrats love their paperwork, and they will want the glory of managing the prisoners.&quot;</p>
