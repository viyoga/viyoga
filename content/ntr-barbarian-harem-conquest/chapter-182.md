<p>His first loss ever since he transmigrated into this game.</p>

<p>Stripped of his armor and wearing Tier III Mana-Sealing Shackles forged from cold Moon-Silver, Kane rode in the center of the formation.</p>

<p>Yet, he didn’t walk like a beaten captive. His head remained high, and his shoulders were set with such relaxed authority that the guards kept a ten-pace distance from his transport cart.</p>

<p>Riding a white warhorse beside the cage, Commander Myla stared at his face, her eyes locking onto the faint blue light pulsing in his pupils.</p>

<p>&quot;Those shackles are designed to sever a mage’s core completely. Why are your eyes still glowing?&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;Because I don’t use magic, Myla,&quot; Kane smiled faintly, leaning his shoulders back against the wooden bars.</p>

<p>&quot;Your cuffs are trying to drain a pond that doesn’t exist. How is your shoulder?&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;Mind your tongue,&quot; she snapped, though her left hand drifted up to rub the bruise where he had slammed her into the silver ingots.</p>

<p>&quot;You are marching to the Silver City. The High Council does not show mercy to warlords who sack our borders.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;Good. Mercy is boring.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;I will enjoy torturing you.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;My pleasure.&quot;</p>

<p>’The mana-shackles are annoying; they are blocking my passive health regeneration, but my core system menu is fully operational,’ Kane thought, watching his internal map record every turn of the forest road.</p>

<p>’I can track the geographical coordinates of every mile we march deeper into their territory. They are drawing me a map of their inner defenses for free.’</p>

<p>Sunlight broke through the ancient forest canopy an hour later, revealing Sylvander, the border City.</p>

<p>Built directly into the trunks of colossal World-Trees, the capital rose in hanging silver bridges, white marble terraces, and cascading mana-waterfalls.</p>

<p>Trotting up to the other side of the transport, the greatsword vanguard gestured proudly toward the towers.</p>

<p>&quot;Look closely, barbarian. This is what thousands of years of unbroken culture and architectural perfection look like.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;I won the tournament and fucked almost every woman here. Of course I know how it looks.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;You and your crass tongue.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;You just have to ask.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;Shut up.&quot;</p>

<p>Kane scanned the suspended timber foundations supporting the lower residential rings. &quot;It looks like a giant tinderbox.</p>

<p>One good siege bombardment at the root systems and three of your noble districts slide into the river.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;You have the manners of a mountain boar.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;I am an engineer and a soldier. You built a castle in the clouds because you forgot men fight on the ground.&quot;</p>

<p>’Firlia was right about one thing: this city is drowning in wealth,’ Kane analyzed, his system calculating the load-bearing capacity of the timber bridges.</p>

<p>’But their military doctrine is arrogant. They have never faced a conventional ground invasion because the forest always protected them. If I can negotiate, or force, a trade treaty here, Menual will dominate the southern continent’s economy within five years.’</p>

<p>Instead of marching him toward the upper administrative spires, the cavalry diverted toward the lower terraces.</p>

<p>Word of the border sack had outrun the caravan, and thousands of elves lined the hanging walkways.</p>

<p>Only a few months ago, these same citizens had showered Kane with flowers when he won the Sylvander grand tournament.</p>

<p>Now, they screamed curses and hurled rotting fruit at his cage, booing the Bloodfang Warlord who had cracked their treasury.</p>

<p>&quot;Fickle crowd,&quot; Kane remarked, dodging a thrown peach without standing up.</p>

<p>&quot;They loved a champion,&quot; Myla said coldly as the cart rumbled into the shadows of the lower ward.</p>

<p>&quot;They despise a conqueror.&quot;</p>

<p>They threw him into a holding cell below the root-line, where the air was cold and bitter. For three days, the Elves tried to break his spirit.</p>

<p>Guards withheld rations, poured freezing river water into his cell at midnight, and forced him to wear a second pair of Moon-Silver cuffs that rubbed his wrists raw.</p>

<p>’They want me exhausted and shivering before I see the Council,’ Kane thought on the third morning, ignoring the ache in his ribs as he did push-ups against the stone floor, the chains clinking with every repetition.</p>

<p>’They don’t understand that a northern winter makes this basement feel like a summer resort.’</p>

<p>When the guards finally dragged him up to the High Court of the Sun-Tree, Kane looked thinner, and bruises marked his ribs, but his stride remained steady.</p>

<p>Hundreds of Elven nobles, scholars, and generals lined the balconies of the living timber hall, whispering as the notorious prisoner was brought before the dais.</p>

<p>Seated at the center of the council table sat Minister Aeloria. High-ranking, blindingly arrogant, and harboring a bitter, personal hatred for the barbarian who had humiliated her border forces, she glared down at him with venomous eyes.</p>

<p>Beside her stood the Grand Chancellor and her remaining royal guards.</p>

<p>Two armored guards behind Kane kicked at the backs of his knees, trying to force him down before the council table.</p>

<p>Kane didn’t flinch. Locking his joints, he tensed his legs and planted his boots into the polished floor.</p>

<p>When a guard swung a hardwood spear-shaft across his calves to break his stance, the wood shattered against Kane’s muscle with a loud crack, leaving the barbarian standing tall before the minister.</p>

<p>Kneeling gracefully at the foot of the dais, Myla bowed her head. &quot;Minister Aeloria. I present the Warlord of Menual.</p>

<p>The customs fortress was breached, but we have secured the enemy commander.&quot;</p>

<p>Aeloria leaned forward, her knuckles whitening against the edge of the council table.</p>

<p>&quot;You sack my customs gates, slaughter my magical barriers, and yet you walk into this court standing upright. Do you not know how to kneel, Barbarian?&quot;</p>

<p>Kane met her gaze directly, the blue light of his interface flaring to life in his pupils.</p>

<p>&quot;I only kneel to bury my dead, Aeloria. And since my commanders are still breathing, I suggest we skip the theater and talk about how much your city is going to pay me for peace.&quot;</p>

<p>Outraged gasps erupted across the balconies, but Aeloria raised a single hand for silence, her face tightening into a pale, furious sneer.</p>

<p>&quot;No queen?&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;You aren’t worth her time.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;You don’t know. We have quite some explicit history in war room.&quot;</p>
