<p>It was already dawn.</p>

<p>Sitting alone at the study table, Kane sifted through Firlia’s administrative documents alongside the intelligence ripped from the scouting camp commander.</p>

<p>Overlapping maps covered the wood.</p>

<p>He finally saw the true board.</p>

<p>This wasn’t the flawed narrative spoon-fed to the royal war room, nor was it the limited data the game originally provided.</p>

<p>Rolling his shoulders to ease a lingering stiffness, Kane brought up his status screen.</p>

<p>Checking his numbers hadn’t been a priority since stepping onto the tournament, but he needed a clear inventory of his current capabilities.</p>

<p>[Name: Kane]</p>

<p>[Stage: Warbrand - Late]</p>

<p>[Level: 38]</p>

<p>[Strength: 228]</p>

<p>[Vitality: 191]</p>

<p>[Agility: 102]</p>

<p>[Dexterity: 84]</p>

<p>[Intelligence: 52]</p>

<p>[Wisdom: 47]</p>

<p>[Charisma: 156]</p>

<p>[Dominance: 71]</p>

<p>[Gold Coins: 798]</p>

<p>[Active Skills: Berserker Rage Lv2 - Bloodlust, Conquest Bond, Harem Bond Lv2, Inspect Lv2, Allocation Lv2]</p>

<p>[Passive Skills: Body Enhancement, Primal Seed, Afterglow, Primal Resonance, Battle Mate - Kessa, Conqueror’s Presence]</p>

<p>[Weapon Bond: Mjoldr - Soul Brand active, Evolution: Stage 2 pending]</p>

<p>[Sovereign’s Brand capacity: 12]</p>

<p>[Title: Empire Breaker]</p>

<p>’Stage two is just sitting there,’ Kane pondered, staring at the weapon text.</p>

<p>That grumpy market dwarf swore the axe was still becoming something else. Beating the royal heir was supposed to trigger the final shift.</p>

<p>Divine bloodline mixing with fallen energy. But the shift paused at the edge of completion. Something else is missing.</p>

<p>Flicking the notification away, a new blue box chimed into existence.</p>

<p>[Tournament Champion Reward - Delayed Collection]</p>

<p>[You may select one item from the following:]</p>

<p>[Option 1: Elven Spellblade — Enchanted weapon, Warbrand tier, deals magical and physical damage simultaneously]</p>

<p>[Option 2: Sovereign’s Codex - Ancient tactical document containing 200 years of Elven Imperial military strategy. Grants permanent +15 to Intelligence and Wisdom upon reading]</p>

<p>[Option 3: World Tree Seed - A cutting from the World Tree. Can be planted to establish a minor node of divine energy at a location of your choosing. The node provides permanent magical enhancement to all bonded members within 500 meters]</p>

<p>[Option 4: Dragonblood Awakening Elixir — Temporarily awakens dormant Dragonblood genetic markers. For users with barbarian bloodline: permanent +30 to Strength and Vitality. One use only]</p>

<p>’Wow.’</p>

<p>’That elixir is practically a cheat code,’ Kane thought, leaning back in his chair and rubbing his jaw.</p>

<p>’Thirty permanent points to my primary combat stats just for drinking a potion. That would solve a multitude of problems if I’ve to fight a general.’</p>

<p>Glancing at the second option, he considered the tactical document.</p>

<p>’A codex filled with two centuries of strategies. That closes the blind spot I’ve been fighting since my game knowledge ran out. It makes me a smarter warlord.’</p>

<p>Yet his eyes kept drifting down to the third choice, refusing to look away.</p>

<p>’A node of divine energy. Placed literally anywhere I want. Permanent buffs to all bonded members within five hundred meters.’</p>

<p>Tapping his fingers on the table, Kane let his mind spin through the possibilities.</p>

<p>Grieselda’s mark was already doing strange things as her seal slowly dissolved under his influence.</p>

<p>Furthermore, he now possessed twelve open Sovereign’s Brand slots waiting to be filled with elite killers.</p>

<p>’This seed doesn’t just make me hit harder,’ Kane deduced, forming a lethal conjecture.</p>

<p>’It turns my entire inner circle into a walking nightmare for whoever we face.’</p>

<p>Looking back at the map, his eyes locked onto the western pass.</p>

<p>That was the exact bottleneck Menual’s siege engines intended to squeeze through in less than a week.</p>

<p>’What happens if I plant a divine energy node right in the middle of a warzone?’</p>

<p>Pressing his finger against the blue interface, he finalized his choice.</p>

<p>[World Tree Seed acquired]</p>

<p>[Note: Plant carefully. What grows from this cannot be unplanted.]</p>

<p>[Note: The queen is going to have questions about this. She’s going to ask where you got it. You received it as a tournament reward from her own empire’s prize pool. That conversation is going to be exceptionally interesting.]</p>

<p>A genuine laugh escaped Kane’s lips.</p>

<p>’I’m stealing her sacred tree fragment using her own tournament prize system. I almost want to see the look on her face when she realizes.’</p>

<p>Caelindra stepped into the doorway, snapping his focus back to the present.</p>

<p>She carried the expression of a woman trying desperately to do her boring bureaucratic job while secretly obsessing over something infinitely more dangerous.</p>

<p>&quot;Firlia received confirmation,&quot; the secretary announced, her voice crisp and clear.</p>

<p>&quot;The Empress of Milfheim responded within twelve hours. She demands a direct meeting.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;Where does she want to do this?&quot; Kane asked, casually crossing his booted feet on the edge of the desk.</p>

<p>&quot;She proposed the Ironveil Pass,&quot; Caelindra read from her meticulously organized parchment.</p>

<p>&quot;Neutral territory sitting right between the two empires. Three days from now.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;Send a bird back immediately,&quot; Kane ordered.</p>

<p>&quot;Tell her I’ll be there in two.&quot;</p>

<p>Pausing, Caelindra carefully noted the sudden schedule change.</p>

<p>&quot;You’re planning to meet the Millenia Empress before the Menual cavalry even reaches the western pass.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;Yes.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;And you theorize you can somehow convince a foreign, hostile ruler to redirect her entire military west based on a single conversation,&quot; she pressed, arching an eyebrow.</p>

<p>&quot;Yes.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;In two days.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;Is my ambitious schedule a problem for you?&quot;</p>

<p>Staring at him for a long moment, she finally snapped her portfolio shut.</p>

<p>&quot;No. I’m coming with you.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;I already knew that,&quot; Kane chuckled, dropping his boots to the floor.</p>

<p>Fighting a small grin, Caelindra turned sharply on her heel and marched back down the hall, her posture slightly less rigid than before.</p>

<p>Rutheus barged into the study barely an hour later.</p>

<p>Carrying a dented cup of wine in his remaining hand.</p>

<p>&quot;You look too bored, old man,&quot; Kane greeted, gesturing to an empty chair.</p>

<p>&quot;Please tell me we are going to ruin someone’s day.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;We’re going to ruin an entire empire’s day,&quot; Kane corrected him, sliding the annotated map toward the Dragonblood warrior.</p>

<p>&quot;I need everything you know about the northern mountain approaches. Specifically the goat paths and hunting trails the royal elven scouts always ignore.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;They are bringing siege equipment, right? Those fancy catapults cannot climb steep grades. I conjecture they have to use the flat valley floors.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;Exactly,&quot; Kane agreed, leaning over the map.</p>

<p>&quot;They will be funneled tight.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;If we take the high ground along these ridges,&quot; Rutheus deduced, his eyes lighting up with tactical glee, &quot;we turn those valleys into a complete slaughterhouse. But the footing is treacherous. Regular soldiers will break their ankles trying to march up there.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;Good thing I don’t employ regular soldiers,&quot; Kane smirked.</p>

<p>Somewhere beyond the mountains, twelve siege engines were already moving toward their graveyard.</p>
