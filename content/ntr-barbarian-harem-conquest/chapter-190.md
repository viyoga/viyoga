<p>&quot;You want to conquer the other three empires before the devils arrive?&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;I want to crush their leadership and absorb their armies. And we start with Milfheim.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;Your wife’s territory.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;My mother-in-law’s territory,&quot; Kane corrected, his eyes glowing with that familiar blue interface light.</p>

<p>&quot;She has been looking for an excuse to invade my borders anyway. We beat her to the punch. You lend me a wing of your pegasus riders, and I will hand you the keys to her fortress in a month.&quot;</p>

<p>Sylvara stared at him for a long time, weighing the sheer audacity of his plan against the terrifying prophecy of her most trusted Seer.</p>

<p>&quot;Take his chains off,&quot; Sylvara finally ordered, not breaking eye contact.</p>

<p>Aeloria gasped.</p>

<p>&quot;Your Majesty!&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;Take them off!&quot;</p>

<p>The guards hesitated for a split second before stepping forward and unlocking the Moon-Silver cuffs. The heavy metal hit the floor with a loud clang.</p>

<p>Kane rubbed his raw wrists, rolling his shoulders as his Warlord vitality surged back to full strength, wiping away the fatigue of the last few days.</p>

<p>&quot;Welcome to the war council, Warlord,&quot; Sylvara said.</p>

<p>&quot;Do not make me regret this.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;You won’t,&quot; Kane smiled, turning toward the door.</p>

<p>&quot;Now, someone get me a decent meal and my armor. We have a lot of work to do.&quot;</p>

<p>Roast boar and spiced wine disappeared down his throat.</p>

<p>Kane ate with the focus of a starving wolf, ignoring the disgusted glances from the royal attendants.</p>

<p>Tossing a clean bone onto a silver platter, he finally wiped his mouth with the back of his hand.</p>

<p>&quot;I need my people.&quot;</p>

<p>Sylvara sat across the table, swirling a glass of wine.</p>

<p>&quot;We will send out a messenger. But you will work here, where I can see you.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;I don’t have objections to the hospitality.&quot; Kane leaned back, picking his teeth with a splinter of wood.</p>

<p>&quot;But it’s hard. I need to move to Milfheim.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;You’re going to fight them already?&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;No.&quot;</p>

<p>He grabbed an apple from a crystal bowl.</p>

<p>&quot;I will talk to Misha. She can convince her mother to stand down.&quot;</p>

<p>Aeloria scoffed loudly from the corner of the room.</p>

<p>&quot;The Queen of Milfheim listening to a mongrel warlord? You are delusional.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;I wasn’t talking to you, Aeloria.&quot; Kane didn’t even look at her.</p>

<p>&quot;Look at the board, Sylvara. I already have Menual. With you and Milfheim on board, we already have three empires. The rest are the Beastmen and the Raviers.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;You will talk with the Beastmen?&quot; Sylvara raised a delicate eyebrow.</p>

<p>&quot;I have Kessa,&quot; Kane shot back, taking a bite of the apple.</p>

<p>&quot;But yes. Once Milfheim is smoothed out, I will talk to the tribes.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;You speak as if Milfheim is already yours.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;I hold the princess.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;A hostage?&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;A wife.&quot;</p>

<p>Kane tossed the apple core onto a silver tray.</p>

<p>&quot;And I love her, so I won’t break her heart by killing her mother. That doesn’t mean I will be submissive when the old bat tries to dictate terms. I’m offering an alliance, not my neck.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;If your little princess fails to convince her,&quot; Sylvara warned, &quot;my pegasus riders will burn her castle to the ground.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;If it comes to that,&quot; Kane smiled, a glint in his eye, &quot;I’ll light the match myself.&quot;</p>

<p>One day passed in a blur of maps and logistics.</p>

<p>Sweat dripped from Kane’s chin as he pushed his body off the floor for the five hundredth time.</p>

<p>His Warlord vitality pulsed through his veins, repairing the lingering aches from the whip.</p>

<p>Muscles coiled and released in perfect rhythm.</p>

<p>’Without those cuffs, my passive regeneration is working overtime,’ Kane analyzed, feeling the familiar rush of stamina returning to his core.</p>

<p>’I need to be at peak condition. You don’t negotiate with Beastmen or Raviers looking like a beaten dog.’</p>

<p>He flipped onto his back, transitioning into rapid core exercises.</p>

<p>His interface blinked, showing his strength stats ticking upward, restoring the points drained during his captivity.</p>

<p>Boots echoed down the grand corridor outside his royal guest quarters. The oak doors flew open, startling the elven guards stationed outside.</p>

<p>&quot;Kane!&quot;</p>

<p>Misha crashed into his chest before he even had a chance to stand up. Her arms wrapped tightly around his neck, burying her face into his shoulder.</p>

<p>&quot;You reckless idiot,&quot; she mumbled against his skin.</p>

<p>&quot;Missed you too, wife,&quot; Kane laughed, wrapping one arm around her waist and spinning her around.</p>

<p>Thora swaggered in next, resting her halberd casually over her shoulder.</p>

<p>She hugged him tight, then scanned the luxurious room, then smirked at his bare, scarred chest.</p>

<p>&quot;Looks like you survived the elven torture chambers. We were ready to burn this tree down to the roots.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;Keep your pyromania in check, Thora,&quot; Firlia sighed, stepping past the tall warrior. The strategist adjusted her glasses, holding a stack of ledgers.</p>

<p>&quot;Though I had drawn up three different siege plans just in case.&quot;</p>

<p>Sira slunk in silently.</p>

<p>Her daggers spun lazily in her hands, her eyes darting toward the open balcony to check for snipers.</p>

<p>Kessa twitched her ears, sniffing the air with a wrinkled nose.</p>

<p>&quot;Smells like flowers and arrogance in here. Can we leave?&quot;</p>

<p>’My squad is finally here,’ Kane thought, a genuine smile breaking through his usual smirk.</p>

<p>’Now we can actually get to work.’</p>

<p>&quot;We are staying for a bit, Kessa,&quot; Kane said, patting the beastman on the head.</p>

<p>&quot;Try not to bite any nobles.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;No promises.&quot;</p>

<p>A sudden rush of wind blew through the balcony doors.</p>

<p>Feathers blacker than midnight fluttered into the room.</p>

<p>Grieseld descended from the sky. Her wings folded neatly behind her back as her boots touched the marble floor.</p>

<p>Her eyes locked onto Kane, offering a formal bow.</p>

<p>&quot;The aerial scout routes are established.&quot;</p>

<p>Down the hall, the blind Seer suddenly gripped her wooden staff. Her unseeing eyes snapped toward Kane’s chambers, sensing the sudden shift in ambient magic.</p>

<p>She stepped into the doorway a moment later, her serene face tight with shock.</p>

<p>&quot;You... you brought a Fallen Angel?&quot;</p>

<p>Sylvara, walking right behind her Seer, froze in her tracks.</p>

<p>The Queen stared at Grieseld’s wings, her jaw slightly parted in rare astonishment.</p>

<p>&quot;Yes.&quot; Kane gently set Misha down and turned to face the stunned Elven royalty.</p>

<p>He clapped his hands together, walking toward the large map table in the center of the room.</p>

<p>&quot;Now that the pleasantries are out of the way. Let’s get to our plan. We have exactly ninety-eight days left.&quot;</p>
