<p>Morning sunlight spilled beautifully across the wooden desk in Menual’s central keep.</p>

<p>Sitting alone in his quiet study, Kane took a slow sip from a hot cup of herbal tea.</p>

<p>Steam curled upward, momentarily blurring the blue system screen hovering right in front of his face.</p>

<p>’Time to spend some gold,’ Kane decided, navigating straight to the [Territory Management] tab.</p>

<p>’We cannot wage a war against arrogant elves with basic iron weapons and wooden gates. That is just asking for a humiliating defeat.’</p>

<p>Swiping through the interface, Kane checked his resource pools.</p>

<p>Numbers flashed in bright gold, indicating a very healthy treasury built from recent conquests and smart trade routes.</p>

<p>Tapping a glowing icon, Kane dumped a large portion of his accumulated wealth directly into the city’s infrastructure.</p>

<p>[Blacksmith Forge: Level Up Initiated. Mana-infused crafting unlocked.]</p>

<p>[Barracks: Expansion Complete. Passive Stamina Buff applied to all recruited infantry.]</p>

<p>’Much better,’ Kane smirked, dismissing the screen with a quick flick of his wrist.</p>

<p>’Nothing says empire building like magically enhanced swords and soldiers who do not get tired after five minutes of jogging. Maybe I should upgrade the medical tents next. Taking casualties is bad for business.’</p>

<p>Opening the map interface, Kane examined the blinking red dots scattered along the southern border.</p>

<p>Elven scout patrols seemed to be increasing over the last few days.</p>

<p>’They know something is changing in this region,’ Kane deduced, rubbing his chin thoughtfully.</p>

<p>’Let them panic. Panic makes commanders make stupid mistakes.’</p>

<p>Footsteps echoed down the stone hallway just before the oak door swung open.</p>

<p>Firlia walked into the study, clutching a tall stack of leather-bound ledgers against her chest.</p>

<p>Her pointed ears twitched nervously as she took a seat opposite his desk.</p>

<p>&quot;Morning, Lord Kane,&quot; Firlia greeted, setting the books down with a soft thud.</p>

<p>&quot;Trade routes are clear today. Merchants are thriving, as usual. But if we want to truly dominate the regional market before marching on my homeland, we need a brand new export. Something nobody else has right now.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;Way ahead of you,&quot; Kane replied, reaching under his desk.</p>

<p>Lifting a bulging sack, Kane dropped it right on top of her ledgers.</p>

<p>Frost instantly began creeping across the leather covers, chilling the air around the desk.</p>

<p>&quot;What is this?&quot; Firlia asked, pulling her hands back quickly.</p>

<p>&quot;Leftovers,&quot; Kane grinned.</p>

<p>&quot;Frost scales and diluted azure blood from our little hunting trip up the mountain. Perhaps we can make some use of it.&quot;</p>

<p>Firlia blinked, carefully peeking inside the freezing bag.</p>

<p>&quot;You want to sell mythical beast parts to passing traders?&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;Not directly,&quot; Kane corrected, leaning forward to rest his elbows on the wood.</p>

<p>&quot;Have the alchemists turn the blood into stamina potions. Use the scales to forge frost-resistant armor for Thora’s front line. We keep the best gear for our own soldiers.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;And the excess?&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;We sell it to neighboring lords at a ridiculous premium,&quot; Kane finished, tapping his temple.</p>

<p>&quot;Let them pay for our upcoming war. If they complain about the price, tell them to go punch a giant snake themselves.&quot;</p>

<p>Firlia laughed aloud, shaking her head as she scooped up the freezing sack.</p>

<p>&quot;You are a terrifying merchant, Kane. I will get the alchemists on it immediately. Maybe they can synthesize the venom too.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;Do that,&quot; Kane agreed, standing up.</p>

<p>&quot;Also, send word to the southern villages. Tell them to double their grain production. We will need surplus rations for the march.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;They will want extra compensation,&quot; Firlia warned, tapping a ledger.</p>

<p>&quot;Pay them double,&quot; Kane said easily. &quot;A well-fed army wins wars. Starving soldiers just turn into mutineers.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;Understood,&quot; Firlia smiled, genuinely appreciating his straightforward leadership style. &quot;I will manage the treasury.&quot;</p>

<p>Finishing his tea, Kane decided to stretch his legs.</p>

<p>Stepping out into the crisp morning air of the training yard, Kane instantly found pure chaos.</p>

<p>Dust kicked up in a wide, frantic circle.</p>

<p>Thora and Griselda were currently tearing up the center of the courtyard.</p>

<p>Griselda was clearly still figuring out her new center of gravity.</p>

<p>Spreading her pitch-black wings, the fallen angel launched herself directly into the sky. Hovering for a split second, Griselda dove down, twin daggers flashing in a rapid barrage of strikes.</p>

<p>Thora simply laughed out loud. Planting her boots firmly, the tall warrior swung her broadsword in a sweeping arc, expertly deflecting the aerial assault. Sparks flew everywhere as steel met steel.</p>

<p>&quot;Is that all you got, bird?&quot; Thora taunted, clearly thrilled to finally have a sparring partner who could keep up with her ridiculous strength.</p>

<p>Griselda didn’t reply with words.</p>

<p>Twisting mid-air, she used a powerful flap of her wings to push herself backward, landing gracefully on a wooden training post before launching another dive.</p>

<p>Swish.</p>

<p>Sitting casually on a nearby fence, Sira watched the destruction while loudly crunching on a green apple.</p>

<p>&quot;Ten gold on the angel!&quot; Sira called out, tossing a coin toward a small group of terrified city guards standing nearby.</p>

<p>&quot;Come on, boys! Make a bet! Thora is getting slow in her old age!&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;I heard that, you little rat!&quot; Thora yelled, nearly missing a block.</p>

<p>Watching from the sidelines, Kane crossed his arms over his chest.</p>

<p>A proud smirk slowly formed on his scarred face.</p>

<p>’They are getting stronger every single day,’ Kane observed, entirely ignoring the damaged training equipment scattered around the courtyard.</p>

<p>’Let them bond through combat. A unified vanguard is unbreakable. Maybe I should join them later.’</p>

<p>&quot;Who is winning?&quot; Kane asked, strolling over to the fence.</p>

<p>Sira tossed her apple core over her shoulder.</p>

<p>&quot;Thora has the ground advantage, but Griselda keeps changing the angle of attack. Perhaps a draw if they don’t break the courtyard first.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;Let them break it,&quot; Kane shrugged, pulling out another apple from a nearby barrel.</p>

<p>&quot;I just upgraded the barracks anyway. They can help rebuild it later today.&quot;</p>

<p>Griselda landed smoothly near the weapon racks, folding her wings tight against her back. Panting slightly, she offered Thora a respectful nod.</p>

<p>&quot;You hit very hard,&quot; Griselda noted, her raspy voice carrying across the yard.</p>

<p>&quot;You move like a mosquito,&quot; Thora shot back, though her wide grin betrayed the insult entirely. &quot;Let’s go again. I almost had you that time.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;Break time,&quot; Kane interrupted, stepping into the ring. &quot;I need my vanguard in one piece.&quot;</p>
