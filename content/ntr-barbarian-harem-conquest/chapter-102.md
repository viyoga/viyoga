<p>Stepping out of the bustling tavern, the sun felt surprisingly warm.</p>

<p>Seraphine still refused to look directly at him, her jaw locked in a stubborn, flustered line.</p>

<p>Before Kane could push his teasing any further, a young paladin sprinted across the cobblestones, gasping for breath as she skidded to a halt in front of them.</p>

<p>&quot;First Blade,&quot; the messenger panted, bowing hastily.</p>

<p>&quot;Border Lord. The Empress demands your presence in the grand hall immediately. High Priestess Elyra has halted the betrothal preparations. She invoked the Rite of Submission.&quot;</p>

<p>Seraphine cursed under her breath, a very unholy sound for a paladin.</p>

<p>&quot;Elyra is a fanatic. She views any outsider male gaining political power as a direct blasphemy against the Goddess.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;I guess religious zealots are the same in every single empire,&quot; Kane sighed, cracking his neck.</p>

<p>&quot;Always trying to ruin perfectly good military alliances because they read a book wrong.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;Take this seriously,&quot; Seraphine warned, marching quickly toward the palace.</p>

<p>&quot;The Rite is an ancient law. It requires a trial by combat. If you lose, they will execute you right on the throne room floor, and the Empress will not be legally allowed to intervene.&quot;</p>

<p>’Well, that certainly raises the stakes for the afternoon,’ Kane thought, feeling a familiar spike of combat adrenaline replace his lingering amusement.</p>

<p>Pushing the grand doors open, the atmosphere inside the vaulted hall felt poisonous.</p>

<p>Hundreds of aristocratic women lined the walls, murmuring.</p>

<p>Sitting rigidly on her platinum throne, the Empress offered Kane a look that clearly said, Fix this mess immediately.</p>

<p>Standing in the center of the carpet was High Priestess Elyra.</p>

<p>Draped in flowing golden robes and with red hair, her face contorted in a sneer of pure self-righteous disgust.</p>

<p>Right beside the priestess stood her chosen champion.</p>

<p>The gladiator towered over everyone in the room, standing easily seven feet tall.</p>

<p>Magically enhanced muscles bulged beneath her leather harness.</p>

<p>Resting on her broad shoulder was a spiked iron maul that looked fully capable of crushing a warhorse in a single swing.</p>

<p>&quot;You dare bring this fragile male into our sacred bloodline?&quot; Priestess Elyra shouted, her voice echoing off the marble pillars.</p>

<p>Pointing a bony finger at Kane, she smiled cruelly.</p>

<p>&quot;If you wish to claim royal blood, prove you are not a fragile male. Face the Goddess’s judgment!&quot;</p>

<p>Kane stopped walking.</p>

<p>He looked at the towering gladiator, then at the priestess, and finally up at the Empress.</p>

<p>&quot;Are you sure you want to do this in front of your Empress?&quot; Kane laughed, his voice calm and steady.</p>

<p>He didn’t feel bored.</p>

<p>That spiked maul was a genuinely lethal threat, and one lucky swing would easily separate his head from his shoulders.</p>

<p>But showing even a fraction of hesitation in a room full of predators was a guaranteed death sentence.</p>

<p>He needed to make a statement that this matriarchy would never forget.</p>

<p>Unbuckling the leather strap across his chest, Kane pulled Mjoldr free.</p>

<p>Instead of igniting the crimson blade, he casually tossed the legendary axe sideways.</p>

<p>Seraphine caught the weapon with a startled gasp, the unexpected weight almost pulling her forward.</p>

<p>&quot;Hold this for me, sweetheart,&quot; Kane winked at the First Blade.</p>

<p>&quot;I don’t want to accidentally get her blood on the handle.&quot;</p>

<p>Seraphine’s silver eyes went wide.</p>

<p>&quot;Kane, what are you doing? Pick up your weapon!&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;Against her?&quot; Kane scoffed, stepping onto the carpet completely empty-handed.</p>

<p>&quot;I conjecture that would be entirely unfair.&quot;</p>

<p>Murmurs of shock rippled through the noblewomen.</p>

<p>Facing an enhanced gladiator without a blade was suicide.</p>

<p>Priestess Elyra’s smile widened into a grin.</p>

<p>&quot;Kill the arrogant bastard,&quot; the priestess commanded.</p>

<p>Roaring, the giant gladiator charged.</p>

<p>She swung the spiked maul in a horizontal arc aimed directly at Kane’s ribs.</p>

<p>Swoosh.</p>

<p>The weapon tore through the air with a terrifying, whistling shriek.</p>

<p>Kane didn’t block.</p>

<p>He simply dropped his center of gravity and pivoted sharply to the left.</p>

<p>The spiked iron head missed his nose by a mere inch.</p>

<p>Wind from the swing ruffled his red hair.</p>

<p>’She has reach and power, but she telegraphs her swings a full second early,’ Kane analyzed instantly, his combat instincts taking completely over.</p>

<p>’She fights like someone who expects her opponents to just stand still and block.’</p>

<p>Stepping smoothly inside her guard as the maul carried her momentum forward, Kane glanced over his shoulder at Seraphine.</p>

<p>&quot;Do your fighters always move like overloaded wagons?&quot; Kane called out conversationally, easily sidestepping a frantic backhand strike from the giant woman.</p>

<p>&quot;Focus on the fight!&quot; Seraphine yelled back, clutching his axe tightly against her chest, her knuckles white.</p>

<p>Growling in frustration, the champion lifted the maul high above her head for a crushing downward strike.</p>

<p>&quot;GAHH!&quot;</p>

<p>It was a fatal mistake.</p>

<p>Lifting the weapon exposed her entire midsection and locked her arms in a predictable trajectory.</p>

<p>Before the maul could even begin its descent, Kane stepped forward.</p>

<p>Driving his palm sharply upward, he struck the gladiator’s elbow joint with pinpoint precision.</p>

<p>Bone snapped with a sickening, audible crack that echoed through the silent hall.</p>

<p>The champion screamed, dropping the spiked maul as her right arm bent at an unnatural angle.</p>

<p>Kane didn’t stop.</p>

<p>Grabbing her uninjured shoulder, he used her own staggering weight against her.</p>

<p>A brutal kick to the back of her knee buckled her leg entirely.</p>

<p>&quot;Agh!&quot;</p>

<p>Sweeping his other foot across her ankle, he sent the seven-foot giant crashing onto the marble floor.</p>

<p>Boom!</p>

<p>The impact shook the nearby pillars.</p>

<p>[Combat Complete: Trial by Combat]</p>

<p>[Status: Unarmed Dominance Established]</p>

<p>Stepping casually over the groaning, defeated champion, Kane didn’t even bother breathing hard.</p>

<p>He hadn’t broken a sweat.</p>

<p>The entire grand hall fell completely silent as aristocratic women stared in open-mouthed shock.</p>

<p>A man had just dismantled a magically enhanced gladiator in less than thirty seconds, using nothing but his bare hands and casual conversation.</p>

<p>Priestess Elyra trembled, taking a terrified step backward as Kane slowly approached her.</p>

<p>&quot;Your champion seems a bit fragile,&quot; Kane noted softly, stopping right in front of the golden-robed fanatic.</p>

<p>He didn’t raise his voice. He didn’t have to.</p>

<p>The quiet menace radiating from his scarred frame filled the entire room.</p>

<p>Looking up at the throne, Kane met the Empress’s gaze.</p>

<p>’They respect power,’ Kane reminded himself.</p>

<p>’Time to rewrite their holy rules.’</p>

<p>Looking back down at the trembling priestess, Kane pointed a single finger at the marble floor directly by his boots.</p>

<p>&quot;Kneel,&quot; Kane ordered.</p>

<p>Elyra hesitated, looking frantically toward the Empress for salvation.</p>

<p>&quot;Wha-?&quot;</p>

<p>The platinum-crowned ruler did nothing.</p>

<p>&quot;I said, kneel,&quot; Kane repeated, letting a fraction of his terrifying, bloodlust aura bleed into the air.</p>
