<p>Greta wore a fitted leather bodice that proudly displayed her busty curves.</p>

<p>Instead of looking panicked or rushed by the sudden migration, her composure remained neat.</p>

<p>’She cleans up well,’ Kane mused, appreciating the view as he walked closer.</p>

<p>’I called for her village to pack up, but organizing them this fast takes real leadership.’</p>

<p>&quot;Kane. We received your summons. My people are packed, fed, and ready for whatever you need.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;Glad you made it quickly,&quot; Kane said.</p>

<p>Stepping up to shake her hand, his eyes lingered on her figure for a split second.</p>

<p>&quot;I figure you had to herd a lot of stubborn farmers to get here.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;Farmers learn to move when winter comes knocking,&quot; Greta laughed, crossing her arms in a way that further accentuated her cleavage.</p>

<p>&quot;Besides, you promised us better land. I intend to collect on that promise.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;Come inside,&quot; Kane invited, gesturing toward the open iron gates.</p>

<p>&quot;We have a lot of ground to cover before nightfall, and I need my commanders in one room.&quot;</p>

<p>Walking into the main hall of the manor, Kane found his entire inner circle waiting. The room buzzed with chaotic energy.</p>

<p>Thora sat on the edge of a long wooden dining table, sharpening her twin axes with a whetstone.</p>

<p>Sira and Firlia leaned against the far wall.</p>

<p>Grieselda stood near the hearth, pouring wine into silver cups, while Misha sat quietly in a high-backed chair, her eyes tracking every movement.</p>

<p>&quot;Look who finally decided to come home,&quot; Thora smirked, tossing the whetstone into the air and catching it flawlessly.</p>

<p>&quot;Did you finish playing politics with the Queen?&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;For now,&quot; Kane replied.</p>

<p>&quot;Greta!&quot; Thora shouted.</p>

<p>&quot;Thora and Sira. Nice to see you again.&quot;</p>

<p>Pulling out a chair for Greta, he leaned casually against the head of the table.</p>

<p>&quot;I told the queen what she wanted to hear. Now we get to do what we actually want.&quot;</p>

<p>Greta offered a polite nod to the assembled women, smoothing her skirt.</p>

<p>&quot;Evening, ladies. I hope we aren’t interrupting.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;We were just placing bets on how long Kane would take to start his next war.&quot;</p>

<p>Kane clapped his hands once, instantly commanding the room’s attention.</p>

<p>’Time to rip the bandage off,’ Kane thought, looking around at his fierce companions.</p>

<p>&quot;Pack your gear,&quot; Kane ordered smoothly.</p>

<p>&quot;We leave for Menual today.&quot;</p>

<p>Silence stretched across the dining hall.</p>

<p>Sira blinked, her bow halfway unstrung.</p>

<p>&quot;Today? Chief, we just got back. Half the men are still washing swamp water out of their boots.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;They can wash their boots in the southern fountains,&quot; Kane countered, a savage grin touching his lips.</p>

<p>&quot;The Menual capital is ours. Sarah is currently tallying the treasury, but an empire needs more than just gold. It requires a spine.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;We are relocating the entire vanguard?&quot; Firlia asked, pushing off the wall with sudden interest.</p>

<p>&quot;What about the Elven borders?&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;Queen thinks we are acting as her southern shield,&quot; Kane explained, picking up a stray apple from a bowl and taking a bite.</p>

<p>&quot;Let her believe that. We are taking all the Bloodfang warriors and a few of your best Elven scouts. We will march right into the empire and mold those soft southern humans into our own people.&quot;</p>

<p>Misha tilted her head, her white hair shifting gently over her shoulder.</p>

<p>&quot;You intend to integrate the mercenary forces directly into their noble houses.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;Exactly,&quot; Kane pointed a finger at his oracle.</p>

<p>&quot;Those aristocrats need a reality check. We mix our veterans with their ranks. We train them. We break their old habits and build an army that knows how to fight.&quot;</p>

<p>Thora hopped off the table, her hazel eyes gleaming with excitement.</p>

<p>&quot;So we get to yell at fancy lords and make them run laps in the rain?&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;As many laps as you want,&quot; Kane laughed.</p>

<p>&quot;I figure they need some northern discipline. Greta, your villagers will take over the farmlands surrounding the capital. You get prime real estate, and I get a food supply line I can actually trust.&quot;</p>

<p>Greta’s eyes widened slightly, her red lips parting in pleasant surprise.</p>

<p>&quot;You are giving us the imperial farmlands? Kane, that is worth a fortune.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;You earned it by answering the call,&quot; Kane said simply, holding her gaze.</p>

<p>’Mercenaries win battles, but farmers and merchants hold the territory together.’</p>

<p>As he finalized the logistics in his head, a bright blue notification flashed across his vision.</p>

<p>[System Notification: Sovereign Migration Initiated]</p>

<p>[Condition Met: Mobilizing a faction across territorial borders.]</p>

<p>[Hidden Milestone Achieved: The Conqueror’s Exodus]</p>

<p>[Reward: Unique Skill Acquired — ’Warlord’s Assimilation’]</p>

<p>[Skill Effect: Allied forces train 300% faster. Loyal citizens gain minor stat boosts based on your current level. Hostile subversion attempts within your territory are automatically highlighted in your interface.]</p>

<p>Kane paused, reading the text twice to make sure his eyes weren’t playing tricks.</p>

<p>’A three hundred percent training boost?’ Kane processed, his blood running hot with tactical possibilities.</p>

<p>’I can turn those soft Menual palace guards into elite shock troops in a matter of weeks. The stat boost makes my farmers hit like soldiers. This System really wants me to build an unstoppable meat grinder.’</p>

<p>&quot;Chief?&quot; Thora asked, noticing his silence.</p>

<p>&quot;You have that scary look on your face again.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;Just doing some math,&quot; Kane rumbled, swiping the notification away.</p>

<p>&quot;Sira, sound the horns. Tell Brak to get the wagons hitched. I want the entire column moving south before the moon hits its peak.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;It will be a nightmare to move thousands of people in a few hours,&quot; Sira warned, though she was already tying her hair back to start the work.</p>

<p>&quot;Nightmares build character,&quot; Kane replied smoothly.</p>

<p>&quot;Lyssel, Firlia, grab your scouts. You are running the vanguard. Make sure the roads are clear.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;Consider it done.&quot;</p>

<p>Misha stood up from her chair, walking over to his side.</p>

<p>&quot;The path south is open, husband. But changing the culture of an entire empire will require more than just shouting.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;I know,&quot; Kane smiled.</p>

<p>&quot;That is why I have you do the smart thinking. I just do the shouting.&quot;</p>

<p>Greta stepped forward, adjusting her leather bodice one more time.</p>

<p>&quot;My people are already packed. Just point us in the right direction.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;Follow the banners,&quot; Kane instructed, walking toward the doors to look out over his assembled forces.</p>

<p>&quot;We are going to build something this continent has never seen before.&quot;</p>
