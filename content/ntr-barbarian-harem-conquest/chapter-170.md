<p>&quot;Bring my ribbon back this instant, you winged nuisance!&quot; Misha called out, her hands glowing with soft wind magic as she tried to corner the fluttering creature.</p>

<p>&quot;Leave the poor angel alone since she obviously has good taste in fashion!&quot; Thora laughed, leaping up onto a sturdy table to block Misha’s levitation path with her wooden spear.</p>

<p>&quot;You have enough ribbons in your wardrobe anyway!&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;That was a gift from my husband, you barbarian!&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;Then ask him to buy you another one with all the tax money he just squeezed out of the merchants!&quot;</p>

<p>Watching his two most capable advisors bicker over a stolen tassel while his accountant casually tallied up daily profits, Kane took another long sip of his tea and let the chaos unfold around him.</p>

<p>&quot;Should I intervene before they destroy the armory?&quot; Kane asked, leaning casually against Firlia’s stack of crates.</p>

<p>&quot;Only if you want to pay for the property damage out of your personal allowance,&quot; Firlia warned without missing a beat, turning a page in her ledger.</p>

<p>&quot;Otherwise, I suggest letting them tire themselves out so we can finalize the budget for the Sylvander campaign in peace.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;You truly have a heart of stone, Firlia.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;A heart of stone keeps the treasury full, Commander. Now stand still so I can use your shoulder to balance this inkpot.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;You are treating your Warlord like a piece of furniture.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;You are the most stable thing in this room right now, so stop moving unless you want blue ink all over your favorite tunic.&quot;</p>

<p>The argument in the armory dissolved into chaos the exact moment Griselda dropped the stolen silver tassel directly onto Thora’s head.</p>

<p>Bam.</p>

<p>Spitting out a mouthful of shiny thread, the warrior snarled playfully and leaped onto a reinforced weapon rack, determined to catch the fluttering troublemaker.</p>

<p>Misha immediately joined the pursuit from the air, lifting herself off the stone floor with a surge of wind magic to block the creature’s high escape routes.</p>

<p>’How is Misha doing this?’ Kane thought.</p>

<p>Within seconds, the two most dangerous women in Menual had turned the active military armory into their own personal playground.</p>

<p>Rather than stepping in to halt the madness, Kane leaned his back against Firlia’s stack of wooden crates, taking a long, relaxed sip from his teacup while watching his elite commanders trip over discarded shields and shout insults at one another.</p>

<p>&quot;Catch her by the wings, Thora!&quot; Misha commanded from mid-air, sweeping her hands upward to send a localized gust of wind toward the ceiling, forcing the squawking creature lower.</p>

<p>&quot;If she eats another silk ribbon, I am turning her into a pair of winter gloves!&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;Stop blowing the dust into my eyes, and I might actually grab her!&quot; Thora yelled back, lunging across a wide table of half-finished mail shirts with surprising agility, sending iron rings clattering across the floor.</p>

<p>&quot;You are just jealous because the little angel prefers my hair over yours!&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;She prefers shiny things, you simpleton! Your armor is practically blinding!&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;It is polished steel, not my fault you dress like a wandering apothecary!&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;I am wearing imported southern silk!&quot;</p>

<p>’They are supposedly elite soldiers preparing to conquer an ancient Elven kingdom,’ Kane mused, hiding a grin behind the rim of his teacup as Thora missed a diving grab by mere inches.</p>

<p>’Yet they are currently being outmaneuvered by a creature smaller than a breadbasket. Truly, my vanguard is terrifying.’</p>

<p>Griselda executed a flawless aerial spin to dodge a stream of Misha’s wind, chirping a taunting melody before diving straight down toward the Warlord.</p>

<p>Tiring of the game, she fluttered past Thora’s outstretched hand and landed squarely on Kane’s shoulder.</p>

<p>She curled her warm body against his neck, letting out a loud, content purring hum as she began preening her feathers against his collar.</p>

<p>Both women halted their pursuit instantly.</p>

<p>&quot;I didn’t do anything,&quot; Kane said.</p>

<p>Thora stood balanced on the edge of a blacksmith’s workbench, her chest heaving as she wiped sweat from her brow.</p>

<p>Misha slowly descended back to the stone floorplates, her silver hair wildly disheveled and her silk sleeves slightly rumpled.</p>

<p>The room fell silent save for the rhythmic scratching of Firlia’s quill in the corner and Griselda’s purring.</p>

<p>Exchanging a quick, silent look across the room, the oracle and the warrior seemed to realize exactly how foolish they looked.</p>

<p>A shared understanding passed between them, their rivalry melting away into a synchronized, predatory smirk directed entirely at the man lounging against the supply crates.</p>

<p>Marching over together, they effectively trapped him against the wood.</p>

<p>&quot;It seems our warlord is harboring a fugitive,&quot; Thora declared, resting her hands firmly on her hips as she stepped up to block his escape to the left.</p>

<p>&quot;I believe that warrants an immediate confiscation of his afternoon schedule.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;For once, I agree with the barbarian,&quot; Misha chimed in, closing the gap on his right and linking her arm securely through his elbow, pressing herself close.</p>

<p>&quot;You let us chase that nuisance across the entire armory while you stood here drinking tea. You owe us both a proper apology.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;I was simply observing tactical coordination,&quot; Kane defended himself smoothly, offering a charming smile as Griselda nibbled gently on the edge of his linen collar.</p>

<p>&quot;And I must say, your teamwork needs a little refinement before we attack Sylvander. Your flanking maneuvers were entirely out of sync.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;Our teamwork is exceptional when we share a common goal,&quot; Misha purred, resting her chin on his shoulder right next to the slumbering angel.</p>

<p>&quot;And right now, our goal is keeping you away from your paperwork until sunset.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;We can practice our formation techniques in the training ring together,&quot; Thora suggested, her grin widening as she reached out to tap the hilt of his sword.</p>

<p>&quot;Or perhaps in the upper gardens where the guards cannot spy on us. You have too much stamina saved up, Commander, and leaving it unused is a waste of resources.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;I surrender unconditionally to both of my commanders,&quot; Kane offered, setting his empty teacup down onto the crate behind him.</p>

<p>&quot;Though I expect gentle treatment from my vanguard and exceptional predictions from my oracle if I am dedicating my entire afternoon to your entertainment.&quot;</p>
