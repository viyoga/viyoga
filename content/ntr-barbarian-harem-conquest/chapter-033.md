<p>Kane stepped into the center of the sparring pit, spinning his twin iron axes with ease.</p>

<p>The chained weapons whistled through the air, drawing curious and judgmental stares from across the busy training yard.</p>

<p>He didn’t mind the audience at all.</p>

<p>Brak and two other Bloodfang warriors circled him closely with their shields raised, trying desperately to find an opening in his guard.</p>

<p>They rushed him at the same time, coordinating their strikes perfectly.</p>

<p>Kane ducked under Brak’s spear thrust and brought his left axe up to parry a clumsy sword strike from the second man.</p>

<p>He moved seamlessly, weaving through their attacks while using the blunt sides of his blades to smack their shoulders and ribs.</p>

<p>Kane wasn’t just beating them down for fun.</p>

<p>He was actively training his own footwork to match the fast-paced, relentless rhythm he would need for the upcoming arena battles.</p>

<p>Rutheus stood near the edge of the pit, resting his giant sword casually on his shoulder. He didn’t join the spar, but he watched happily.</p>

<p>A loud chuckle escaped the barbarian every time Kane swept one of his men off their feet or countered a strike with surprising speed.</p>

<p>’What a find,’ he thought</p>

<p>A few elven duelists nearby sneered at the crude display of wrestling and bruising.</p>

<p>They quickly turned their noses up and got back to their own elegant, dance-like training routines, muttering about savages ruining the sacred grounds.</p>

<p>Kane caught Brak’s spear shaft with the iron hook of his right axe.</p>

<p>He twisted his wrist sharply, disarming the young warrior instantly, then tossed the wooden spear aside and took a step back to give his men a much-needed breather.</p>

<p>’These iron axes are taking a real beating,’ Kane thought, noticing a fresh chip on the edge of his left blade.</p>

<p>’They are good for clearing out low thugs and basic forest monsters, but they will not hold up much longer against elite fighters. If I try to block a high-tier elven spell or a seasoned beastman’s club with these, the iron will just shatter in my hands.’</p>

<p>Brak bent over to catch his breath, wiping a stream of sweat from his forehead.</p>

<p>&quot;Will we participate, too, Chief?&quot; the young warrior asked, looking hopefully at the colorful banners hanging above the main arena.</p>

<p>Kane smiled warmly, tossing his axes back into their holsters.</p>

<p>&quot;You guys have definitely gotten stronger over the last few weeks. Your teamwork is getting better every single day, and I am proud of that. But that doesn’t mean you are strong enough to fight the elite elves or the beastmen yet. You would just get yourselves killed out there, so only I will join the matches for now.&quot;</p>

<p>Brak nodded, understanding the harsh reality of the situation.</p>

<p>He knew his chief was only looking out for their long-term survival.</p>

<p>Kane stepped forward and placed his hands on the chests of the other two panting barbarians, giving them a firm push backward to signal the end of the session.</p>

<p>He turned his head and scanned the crowded yard until he found his archer practicing near the back wall.</p>

<p>&quot;Sira,&quot; Kane called out loudly.</p>

<p>She lowered her new bow immediately and jogged over to him. Her orange hair bounced slightly as she stopped right in front of him, waiting patiently for her orders.</p>

<p>&quot;Every day, go to the outer gates at night,&quot; Kane instructed, keeping his voice low so the surrounding fighters would not overhear their plans.</p>

<p>&quot;Walk far away to the right side of the wall. Our men outside will be sharing information about who got past the checkpoints and entered the city. I need you to tell me everything they see.&quot;</p>

<p>Sira nodded quickly, committing the plan to memory.</p>

<p>&quot;Understood. Should we be wary of anyone specific, Chief?&quot;</p>

<p>Kane crossed his arms and let his mind wander back to the countless hours he spent playing this exact tournament in the game.</p>

<p>Playing as the main protagonist usually meant guaranteed victory because of plot armor, but the reality of this physical world was far less forgiving.</p>

<p>The elven prince was the final boss of this arc, but there was a terrifying hurdle waiting in the semi-finals.</p>

<p>’Lothburk,’ Kane thought, picturing the vicious beastman.</p>

<p>’He is a mutant born with four arms, and he tears regular fighters apart just for fun. He is the most dangerous enemy standing in my way before Prince Aelrindor. I need to watch out for him as he doesn’t play by the arena rules, and he loves to cripple his opponents.’</p>

<p>&quot;Just keep an ear out for a beastman named Lothburk,&quot; Kane finally answered, patting Sira on the shoulder.</p>

<p>&quot;If he is in the city, I want to know where he sleeps and who he talks to. Don’t engage him under any circumstances.&quot;</p>

<p>Before Sira could ask any more questions about the mysterious fighter, a quiet presence approached them from the side.</p>

<p>Grieselda walked toward Kane with her usual blank, doll-like expression. Her eyes stared up at him, completely devoid of any visible emotion.</p>

<p>She stopped a foot away and slowly extended her pale hand toward him, holding it open as if expecting a gift.</p>

<p>Kane looked at her empty palm, genuinely confused by the gesture.</p>

<p>&quot;What?&quot;</p>

<p>She simply moved her hand back and tapped a slender finger directly against the center of her own chest.</p>

<p>Kane frowned, trying to decipher her strange charades.</p>

<p>&quot;Heart?&quot;</p>

<p>[System Notice]</p>

<p>[Companion Grieselda is asking for the Grimfang Bear Corrupted Heart.]</p>

<p>Kane stared at the text floating in front of his face, his eyes widening slightly as the pieces finally clicked together.</p>

<p>He looked back down at the strange, emotionless girl he bought from a sleazy merchant back in the village.</p>

<p>Her steady heartbeat pulsed quietly in his ears, reminding him of the hidden power sleeping inside her fragile frame.</p>

<p>’You can forge weapons?’ Kane thought, a new wave of excitement rushing through his veins.</p>

<p>’I knew she was a rare prize, but I didn’t expect her to solve my equipment problem right before the tournament begins.’</p>
