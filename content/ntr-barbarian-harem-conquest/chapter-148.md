<p>Misha settled comfortably against his chest again.</p>

<p>&quot;I can help channel the mana when the time comes. Celestial runes require a delicate touch to dismantle, even with the proper solvent.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;I appreciate it,&quot; Kane smiled.</p>

<p>&quot;Just keep your distance when the vial opens.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;Saintess,&quot; Misha called out, shifting the topic smoothly.</p>

<p>&quot;Why are there traps so far up? Did the temple not forbid locals from trespassing?&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;The temple fell a long time ago, Oracle,&quot; the Saintess answered, her bright tone fading into something somber.</p>

<p>&quot;Once the deities went silent, commoners stopped fearing the mountain. Poachers climbed up here trying to harvest frost-crystals for southern markets.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;Did they find any?&quot; Kane asked.</p>

<p>&quot;They found the Serpent,&quot; she replied bluntly.</p>

<p>&quot;It ate them. Those traps are just remnants of desperate men trying to catch a god’s familiar with rusty wire.&quot;</p>

<p>’Fools,’ Kane thought, shaking his head.</p>

<p>’You don’t hunt a mythical beast with basic snares. You bring overwhelming damage, or you stay home.’</p>

<p>Griselda caught the Saintess’s attention, hands moving in a mocking, questioning rhythm.</p>

<p>[If your gods went silent, why keep the veils? Seems pointless to worship an empty sky.]</p>

<p>&quot;Faith does not vanish just because the heavens go quiet,&quot; the Saintess defended mildly.</p>

<p>&quot;Besides, the veils keep the frostbite off my face. Practicality over piety.&quot;</p>

<p>Kane chuckled, resting his hand over Misha’s fingers.</p>

<p>&quot;I can respect that. Just make sure you stay behind me when the fog actually rolls in. If that snake spits venom, I want all three of you out of the splash zone. Griselda, you stay entirely on support duty. Do not engage directly.&quot;</p>

<p>Griselda frowned slightly but nodded her agreement.</p>

<p>&quot;You are going to take the hit yourself?&quot; Misha asked, tracing his knuckles gently.</p>

<p>&quot;I have the stats for it,&quot; Kane promised, projecting absolute confidence to keep them grounded.</p>

<p>&quot;You point out where the snake hides, and I will handle the rest. I need its blood fresh to break that seal.&quot;</p>

<p>Reaching the base of Fog Mountain took another grueling hour of navigating slippery, frozen trails.</p>

<p>Ruined stone pillars marked the entrance to the Saintess’s forgotten temple, their intricately carved surfaces cracked and weathered by centuries of relentless blizzards.</p>

<p>Kane swung down from his stallion, immediately grabbing the supply bags to unload their provisions.</p>

<p>Stepping inside the crumbling sanctuary, the temperature somehow dropped even further. A single stone statue sat perfectly untouched in the center of the debris.</p>

<p>It depicted a coiled snake, its stone scales worn smooth by time, yet its eyes glittered brightly with pure, untarnished gold.</p>

<p>’Creepy little shrine,’ Kane observed, setting the bags near a relatively intact wall.</p>

<p>’Those golden eyes look like they are tracking every move we make.’</p>

<p>Misha finished arranging their sleeping packs near a shattered altar, then stopped.</p>

<p>She stared out through the broken roof, gazing up at the jagged, snow-covered peak. Her golden rings stopped spinning completely, hovering frozen around her wrists.</p>

<p>&quot;Kane, I am still afraid,&quot; Misha whispered, wrapping her arms around her own chest.</p>

<p>&quot;Don’t be,&quot; Kane replied, walking across the ruined floorboards.</p>

<p>&quot;We are only here for the blood.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;It is not just a random beast, husband,&quot; she insisted, her dual-tone voice trembling slightly.</p>

<p>&quot;It will kill you before you even know it is there.&quot;</p>

<p>Walking up quietly behind her, Kane wrapped his arms around her waist, pulling her back against his chest.</p>

<p>He let the warmth of his enchanted cloak envelop her.</p>

<p>&quot;I understand your worry,&quot; Kane murmured into her white hair, resting his chin on her head.</p>

<p>&quot;But I am strong too. I am not going down without a fight.&quot;</p>

<p>Misha leaned back into his embrace, sighing deeply while her slender fingers gripped his forearms.</p>

<p>&quot;You are not going down at all.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;Okay,&quot; Kane agreed.</p>

<p>Releasing her slowly, he turned back toward the temple’s grand archway, loosening his shoulders to keep his muscles warm.</p>

<p>&quot;I will see the layout first,&quot; Kane announced, checking the straps on his weapon harness.</p>

<p>&quot;Tomorrow, I climb the mountain.&quot;</p>

<p>Leaving the women to secure the campsite, Kane trudged through the knee-deep snow toward the actual base of the ascent.</p>

<p>Frost crusted his boots with every step. The air grew significantly thinner out here, biting at his exposed cheeks like tiny, invisible needles.</p>

<p>A strange, unnatural mist clung to the slopes above.</p>

<p>This wasn’t just weather acting up. It was a localized domain, a specialized hunting ground designed to confuse and isolate prey.</p>

<p>Faint blue runic veins pulsed beneath the glacial ice, remnants of the old gods trying to contain whatever familiar they had left behind.</p>

<p>Staring up at the apex, Kane shielded his eyes against the glaring white reflection of the dying sun. High above the cloud line, something shifted.</p>

<p>It was barely a ripple in the fog, a subtle displacement of the howling blizzard. A long, serpentine silhouette glided seamlessly through the storm, moving with entirely too much grace for something so vast in size.</p>

<p>’That is not just a monster,’ Kane realized, his combat instincts flaring sharply. ’That is an apex predator perfectly adapted to its environment. If I step into that fog blind, it will tear me apart in seconds.’</p>

<p>Griselda walked up beside him, leaving almost no footprints in the snow thanks to her graceful, practiced stride.</p>

<p>She tapped his arm, pointing her pale finger up at the shifting silhouette before her hands flashed in rapid motion.</p>

<p>[It is awake. I can feel the seal on my chest burning just looking at it. The ambient magic up there is suffocating.]</p>

<p>&quot;Good,&quot; Kane smirked.</p>

<p>&quot;Let it wake up. I want it fighting at full capacity when I take its head.&quot;</p>

<p>The Saintess drifted out of the temple ruins to join them, her silver veils whipping wildly in the wind. She followed their gaze toward the summit.</p>

<p>&quot;It senses the brass bell in your inventory,&quot; the Saintess warned, folding her hands inside her sleeves.</p>

<p>&quot;The relic carries the scent of the old monks. You have effectively rung the dinner bell just by standing at the threshold.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;Saves me the trouble of hunting it down,&quot; Kane noted practically.</p>
