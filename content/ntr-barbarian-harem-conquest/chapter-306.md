<p>The three shallow lines across Kane’s chest welled bright and immediate.</p>

<p>Something in the densified joint ground and popped.</p>

<p>Kane turned just enough that the blow crushed into the packed earth beside his head instead, spraying dirt and chips of rock.</p>

<p>&quot;It will not stay broken.&quot;</p>

<p>Kane rolled, came up on refusal, and spat a mouthful of red mixed with grit.</p>

<p>&quot;Then... I break it again.&quot;</p>

<p>Grathak’s densified forearm caught Kane across the upper chest and lifted him clean off his feet before slamming him down.</p>

<p>BAM!</p>

<p>Black fluid and blood burst.</p>

<p>Even densified flesh had limits.</p>

<p>The floor was slick with mixed blood and black fluid.</p>

<p>Neither had yielded the center.</p>
