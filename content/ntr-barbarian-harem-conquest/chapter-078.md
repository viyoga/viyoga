<p>Down in the valley, the hundred Milfheim soldiers shifted into a loose circle without anyone uttering a single command.</p>

<p>Up on the ridge, Kane’s group held their ground.</p>

<p>A single glance told them to stay put, and they listened.</p>

<p>Dismounting from her horse, Seraphine caught the mountain light on her white armor.</p>

<p>It wasn’t just the divine designation.</p>

<p>Her every motion possessed the specific economy of a woman who had been fighting wars long before his own civilization even figured out written language.</p>

<p>She was also beautiful, possessing the structural elegance of someone who stopped trying to look pretty several decades ago and was left with something far more permanent.</p>

<p>’Not relevant right now,’ Kane reminded himself, rolling his shoulders to loosen the tension.</p>

<p>Without bowing or offering any formal ceremony, she lunged.</p>

<p>Her white sword moved at a speed his high-level system barely tracked.</p>

<p>Stormforged Peak meant she operated on a tier where the gap between them became a physical wall.</p>

<p>Her divine blessing amplified her natural capabilities just like Grieselda’s bond enhanced him during the royal arena fight, except this commander enjoyed that power permanently rather than relying on a one-time event.</p>

<p>Refusing to draw Mjoldr right away, Kane chose to read her first.</p>

<p>Five strikes flashed toward him in the span of a single breath.</p>

<p>Twisting his torso, he avoided four completely.</p>

<p>Her fifth blade caught his left arm, leaving a shallow cut.</p>

<p>’Shit, she’s fast,’ Kane calculated, analyzing her flawless footwork while backing away.</p>

<p>’Her patterns are different too. No heartbeat acceleration. No muscle twitches.’</p>

<p>Her designation apparently suppressed the physiological tells his high perception normally relied on to predict attacks.</p>

<p>’I conjecture that fighting her is like fighting a phantom with a sharp blade.’</p>

<p>Reaching over his shoulder, Kane finally drew Mjoldr.</p>

<p>Crimson energy flared from the axe head, meeting the light of her sword.</p>

<p>The air between them instantly sparked as two completely different flavors of ancient power recognized each other.</p>

<p>Seraphine paused for one second.</p>

<p>’She felt that,’ Kane smirked, gripping the haft tighter.’</p>

<p>Her next sequence of attacks came with brutality.</p>

<p>This was no tournament duel with polite rules and medical staff waiting on the sidelines.</p>

<p>Two people stood in a remote mountain valley fighting at full capacity, simply because one needed to evaluate the other, and honest violence was the only language that worked.</p>

<p>Pain flared across his chest as he took three more hits.</p>

<p>One strike caught his ribs, cracking the same bones he broke in the pit fights.</p>

<p>Another slash dragged across his right forearm, slicing deep enough to compromise his grip on his axe.</p>

<p>Her final thrust grazed his shoulder, compounding existing nerve damage from his previous encounters.</p>

<p>Blood ran steadily down his arm, pooling near his knuckles and making the handle slick.</p>

<p>’She thought going easy on me would make me submit?’ Kane deduced, forcing himself to keep moving despite the burning pain radiating through his torso.</p>

<p>He refused to break.</p>

<p>&quot;System,&quot; Kane muttered under his breath.</p>

<p>Activating his Allocation skill, he dumped all his available stat points straight into Agility for the next sixty seconds.</p>

<p>The gap didn’t disappear entirely, as she still maintained a higher baseline, but the margin narrowed enough for him to stop acting like a punching bag and start throwing real responses.</p>

<p>Dodging the slash, Kane swung his armor and landed a hit on her neck.</p>

<p>’One.’</p>

<p>Then Kane surged forward, driving his knee straight into the side of her helmet before swinging Mjoldr in a brutal downward arc.</p>

<p>’Two.’</p>

<p>Seraphine’s posture changed; her one eye visible from the broken helmet was widened.</p>

<p>Swinging Mjoldr with everything he had, his Soul Brand activated on the third exchange.</p>

<p>Bam</p>

<p>[Hidden Property Activated - Soul Brand]</p>

<p>[Target defenses shattered permanently]</p>

<p>Her armor did not shatter like the elven prince’s defenses.</p>

<p>Instead, it disrupted.</p>

<p>The divine blessing stitched into her gear fought fiercely against the fallen energy radiating from his axe.</p>

<p>Two opposing powers generated a sudden friction neither fighter fully controlled.</p>

<p>Staggering backward, Seraphine lost her footing.</p>

<p>She was thrown off balance by the energetic interference rather than the physical impact itself.</p>

<p>Kane closed the distance instantly.</p>

<p>Hooking her sword arm with the curve of Mjoldr’s blade, he bypassed trying to cut her and just redirected her momentum.</p>

<p>Her white sword flew wide, leaving her center completely exposed.</p>

<p>Stepping inside her guard, Kane drove his elbow squarely into her sternum, putting his entire Warbrand-peak Strength behind the blow.</p>

<p>&quot;Guh!&quot;</p>

<p>Gasping for air, she stumbled back three steps before catching herself.</p>

<p>Catching her in the throat, Kane slammed her body on the ground and threw her to the side.</p>

<p>His right arm throbbed, his fingers barely holding onto the axe handle.</p>

<p>But he remained steady.</p>

<p>Across the grass, Seraphine stood with one disrupted armor blessing and a bruised sternum that would likely hurt every time she took a deep breath for the next week.</p>

<p>Looking at him, her face displayed an expression he had never seen on a seasoned fighter.</p>

<p>&quot;You should be dead right now,&quot; Seraphine pointed out, her breathing slightly uneven.</p>

<p>&quot;I know,&quot; Kane agreed, offering a bloody, unrepentant smile.</p>

<p>&quot;The stage gap between our levels is—&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;Real,&quot; Kane finished for her, wiping a stray drop of blood from his chin.</p>

<p>&quot;I fought you anyway.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;Why?&quot; she demanded, sounding genuinely confused by his suicidal persistence.</p>

<p>&quot;Because you needed to see what I do when I’m outmatched,&quot; Kane explained, leaning his weight slightly onto his axe.</p>

<p>&quot;If I declined your challenge, you would report back to your ruler that I was overly cautious. If I begged for special terms, you would tell her I was just a politician looking for a comfortable exit. I conjecture you needed to see what I do when there is no good option on the board and I decide to take it anyway.&quot;</p>

<p>Seraphine stared at him for a long moment, evaluating the madman standing before her.</p>

<p>Then, surprising every single soldier in her elite formation, she smoothly sheathed her white sword.</p>

<p>&quot;You have bruised my ribs, temporarily disrupted my divine blessing, and made me bleed once,&quot; she recounted smoothly, her voice carrying across the quiet valley.</p>

<p>&quot;At your current level. Fighting against mine.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;Yes,&quot; Kane nodded, refusing to look away.</p>

<p>&quot;The Empress will see you,&quot; she declared.</p>
