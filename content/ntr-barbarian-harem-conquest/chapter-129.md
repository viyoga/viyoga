<p>Griselda sat down on the edge of the couch nervously.</p>

<p>An angel of her stature being treated like a patient felt humiliating, but she trusted the warlord enough to remain perfectly still.</p>

<p>Misha approached silently, her footsteps making no sound against the woven rugs.</p>

<p>&quot;Relax,&quot; the oracle whispered.</p>

<p>Reaching out, her delicate fingers pressed against the center of Griselda’s chest, resting right over the hidden seal beneath the fabric.</p>

<p>Instantly, Misha’s pupils vanished.</p>

<p>Her eyes turned completely white as she tapped deep into her seraphic blessing, searching the invisible magical circuits woven into the angel’s flesh.</p>

<p>&quot;The mark...&quot;</p>

<p>Misha murmured, her dual-tone voice vibrating with an overlapping echo that chilled the room.</p>

<p>Griselda looked at her intently, holding her breath while her hands gripped the couch.</p>

<p>&quot;It was a curse,&quot; Misha diagnosed, pulling her hand back abruptly as her eyes snapped back to their normal golden hue.</p>

<p>&quot;Cursed by who?&quot; Kane demanded.</p>

<p>&quot;The gods themselves,&quot; Misha answered, her expression remaining completely flat.</p>

<p>&quot;Why did they do it?&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;Her memory was erased to hide the reason,&quot; Misha explained, tilting her head as she studied the taller woman.</p>

<p>&quot;Whoever put the seal there made sure she could never remember the sin that caused her fall. The magic is designed to eat her grace slowly while keeping her ignorant.&quot;</p>

<p>’Well, that complicates things,’ Kane thought, his brow furrowing as he processed the new information.</p>

<p>’I already knew she got banished from the higher realms. It’s just that this specific reason doesn’t match anything I remember from the original game plot. Back when I viewed this world through a screen, she was just a lore character handing out late-game buffs. I never knew her actual backstory.’</p>

<p>&quot;What should we do?&quot; Kane asked, prioritizing immediate solutions over solving ancient history.</p>

<p>&quot;Kill the curse,&quot; Misha stated simply, as if suggesting they sweep the floor.</p>

<p>&quot;How?&quot;</p>

<p>Misha stared directly into Kane’s eyes, delivering the answer with chilling certainty.</p>

<p>&quot;You must slay The Mountain Serpent.&quot;</p>

<p>Kane’s eyes widened as the world stopped.</p>

<p>Griselda immediately turned toward him, her hands frantically weaving text into the air.</p>

<p>[You are not strong enough, Kane. Don’t even think about it.]</p>

<p>He didn’t reply right away.</p>

<p>Instead, he just stood there, lost in thought while staring at the floorboards.</p>

<p>’I have been worrying about border disputes with elves and human noble factions when the real fight hasn’t even begun,’ Kane realized, feeling a thrill wash through his veins.</p>

<p>’Bringing down that sea beast during the fleet battle was a solid upgrade, sure, but that monster was an A-rank threat at best. The Mountain Serpent is different. That thing is a god’s familiar.’</p>

<p>It was a legendary beast that slumbered on Fog Mountain.</p>

<p>From what Kane remembered playing the game, the creature measured around fifteen hundred meters long, coiling its body around the entire mountain peak in a way that terrified anyone foolish enough to enter its territory.</p>

<p>&quot;Do not overthink it, husband,&quot; Misha advised, breaking the tense silence.</p>

<p>&quot;What?&quot; Kane blinked, looking back.</p>

<p>&quot;You cannot defeat it,&quot; Misha said bluntly, offering zero sugar-coating.</p>

<p>&quot;I have seen you fight. You are strong, yes. But no matter how godly your axe is or how much punishment your body endures, you are still just a human. Besides, your weapon is only operating at ten percent of its original power. You would be swatting a dragon with a twig.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;I understand the odds,&quot; Kane nodded, unfazed by her honesty.</p>

<p>’Mjoldr cannot even put a scratch on those scales right now. I figure I need to level up fast if I am going to raid Fog Mountain. But I’m still going.’</p>

<p>&quot;Does Milfheim know anyone residing near Fog Mountain?&quot; Kane asked, shifting his strategy toward diplomacy.</p>

<p>&quot;Mother has some political connections in that region,&quot; Misha answered, tracing a finger thoughtfully along the edge of her vanity mirror.</p>

<p>&quot;There is an old temple situated near the base of the peak. She knows the saintess who prays to the local deities regularly. The temple acts as a buffer zone.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;Send a message to your mother,&quot; Kane instructed, pacing a few steps across the room.</p>

<p>&quot;Tell her you need a favor, and ask that saintess to meet me halfway.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;But husband...&quot;</p>

<p>Misha frowned, her rings pulsing with concern as she stepped into his path.</p>

<p>&quot;Did you not hear me? It is suicide to challenge a god’s familiar.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;I’m not going to charge in like a madman, Misha,&quot; Kane chuckled, reaching out to ruffle her white hair.</p>

<p>&quot;I know what that beast is capable of. I just need information first.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;The curse does not require you to kill the beast,&quot; Misha clarified, slapping his hand away playfully to fix her hair.</p>

<p>&quot;It only needs the creature’s blood for Griselda to drink. The divine resonance in the blood will shatter the seal from the inside out.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;Which is equally hard,&quot; Kane pointed out, leaning against the table.</p>

<p>&quot;You cannot just walk up and politely ask a fifteen-hundred-meter snake for a pint of blood. If I prick its skin, it will wake up.&quot;</p>

<p>Griselda formed another frantic sentence in the air, the golden letters shaking with her anxiety.</p>

<p>[We can wait! The seal is painful, but I can endure it longer. Please do not risk your life for my sake. I am already broken.]</p>

<p>&quot;I protect what is mine,&quot; Kane told the angel, his tone shifting into an unwavering command that left no room for argument.</p>

<p>&quot;We are fixing your core, Griselda. You are not broken, you are just benched. Let me handle the play.&quot;</p>

<p>Griselda lowered her gaze, overwhelmed by the protection.</p>

<p>Closing his eyes, Kane pictured the vivid scenes from his past life playing the game.</p>

<p>He remembered the digital rendering of Fog Mountain perfectly.</p>

<p>Snow covered the peaks year-round, creating a hostile environment that drained stamina rapidly.</p>

<p>Through his old headset, a haunting, low-frequency hum had echoed all through the day and night, simulating the giant creature’s breathing.</p>

<p>The beast itself surrounded the mountain, using its white scales to merge seamlessly into the swirling fog, making it nearly impossible to track until its jaws were already snapping shut.</p>

<p>Players usually needed a full raid party of max-level characters just to survive the environmental damage, let alone crack the serpent’s armor.</p>

<p>’This will be the hardest boss fight I have ever triggered,’ Kane anticipated, opening his eyes and looking between the two women.</p>
