<p>He fell into step right beside her before she could object.</p>

<p>&quot;Two people walking in the same direction. There is nothing suspicious about that at all.&quot;</p>

<p>She had no real choice, as standing still in the open was far worse.</p>

<p>They walked in total silence for half a block. Kane let the quiet stretch deliberately.</p>

<p>He learned very early in his real life that silence makes nervous people fill the space with things they never meant to say.</p>

<p>The cobblestones clicked softly beneath their boots, and the night air carried the scent of roasted meat from a distant tavern.</p>

<p>&quot;Why are you following me?&quot; Firlia finally asked, keeping her eyes locked straight ahead.</p>

<p>&quot;I’m not following you because you walked right into my path.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;Don’t play games with me, barbarian.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;Kane.&quot;</p>

<p>She shot a sideways glance at him.</p>

<p>&quot;What?&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;My name is Kane, and you stamped my registration papers yesterday. You already know it.&quot;</p>

<p>Another tense silence stretched between them, and her jaw visibly tightened.</p>

<p>&quot;What do you want?&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;Right now? I want to know where a noble registrar goes sneaking through back gates in the middle of the night.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;That’s none of your business.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;Is it the guard captain?&quot; Kane asked playfully, keeping his tone light and conversational.</p>

<p>&quot;The one with the matching blue crystal necklace? Late-night quickie, huh?&quot;</p>

<p>She stopped walking immediately.</p>

<p>They were tucked inside a narrow side street now, far away from any witnesses.</p>

<p>Lamplight from a distant corner caught the polished silver resting at her throat.</p>

<p>Firlia turned to face him fully for the first time tonight.</p>

<p>Up close, her red eyes looked furious and terrified simultaneously.</p>

<p>&quot;You promised you wouldn’t tell anyone,&quot; she hissed.</p>

<p>&quot;I haven’t said anything to anyone,&quot; Kane replied calmly.</p>

<p>&quot;There isn’t a need for me to.&quot;</p>

<p>She stared at him, and the fury in her expression slowly shifted into something much more complicated.</p>

<p>&quot;What do you actually want from me?&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;Information,&quot; Kane answered simply.</p>

<p>&quot;You know every single fighter in this tournament. You know the hidden brackets, the side deals, and the fixed matches. I want to know which ones Lothburk has been paid to destroy before the semifinals begin.&quot;</p>

<p>Firlia caught her breath slightly because she definitely recognized the name.</p>

<p>&quot;If I told you that, they would kill me.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;Nobody would know you told me,&quot; Kane promised.</p>

<p>&quot;I’m very good at keeping useful people protected.&quot;</p>

<p>She studied his face intently, searching for the hidden angle and trying to figure out what he was really after.</p>

<p>Then something subtle shifted in her expression. She stepped forward, closing the distance between them to almost nothing.</p>

<p>And she kissed him.</p>

<p>It wasn’t hesitant or shy.</p>

<p>She pressed her lips hard against his, gripping the front of his shirt tightly with her left hand.</p>

<p>Kane’s hands instinctively dropped to her wide hips, gripping her plump ass through the robes and pulling her even tighter against him.</p>

<p>He could feel how hard her nipples were as they rubbed against his chest.</p>

<p>But his instincts never slept.</p>

<p>In the middle of that heated kiss, he felt her right hand sliding down toward the dagger hidden under her cloak.</p>

<p>Kane caught her wrist in an iron grip right as her fingers touched the hilt.</p>

<p>They broke apart instantly, lips glistening, both breathing heavily.</p>

<p>Her breathing sounded ragged and panicked in the quiet alley, but his expression had not changed at all.</p>

<p>&quot;That was a very interesting approach,&quot; Kane said quietly, holding her wrist firmly enough so she couldn’t pull free.</p>

<p>&quot;You kiss surprisingly well for someone trying to put a blade in my ribs.&quot;</p>

<p>Firlia widened her eyes in defeat.</p>

<p>The assassination plan failed, and she knew she was trapped.</p>

<p>&quot;You... bastard,&quot; she whispered shakily.</p>

<p>Her trembling fingers released the dagger handle slowly.</p>

<p>&quot;The prince,&quot; Kane stated, keeping his voice dangerously low.</p>

<p>&quot;He sent you to do this.&quot;</p>

<p>It wasn’t a question.</p>

<p>All the color drained rapidly from her pale face.</p>

<p>’There it is,’ Kane thought, watching her terrified reaction carefully.</p>

<p>’She didn’t come up with this desperate plan on her own. She was following strict orders from the top, and is completely terrified of what happens now that she failed.’</p>

<p>&quot;He knows about the necklace,&quot; Kane pressed on, piecing the puzzle together perfectly.</p>

<p>&quot;Prince Aelrindor knows about you and the commoner guard captain, and he’s been using it to control you since long before I arrived in this city.&quot;</p>

<p>Firlia lost her noble composure entirely for just a moment.</p>

<p>She pressed her free hand against her mouth, trying to hold back a sob.</p>

<p>&quot;How long has he blackmailed you?&quot; Kane asked, loosening his grip on her wrist just a fraction to show he was not going to break it.</p>

<p>She refused to answer immediately. Then she whispered, &quot;Three years.&quot;</p>

<p>Kane released her wrist and took a step back.</p>

<p>’She’s not my real enemy,’ Kane thought, looking at the broken woman standing before him.</p>

<p>’I haven’t got time to mess with relationship drama.’</p>

<p>&quot;Here is what happens now,&quot; Kane instructed, his voice dropping to almost a whisper.</p>

<p>&quot;You go meet your captain tonight exactly like you planned. You tell the prince I suspected nothing, and I fought off your attack purely by chance. And tomorrow morning, you start telling me everything about Lothburk’s secret arrangements.&quot;</p>

<p>Firlia stared at him in disbelief.</p>

<p>&quot;And what if I refuse?&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;Then I let the prince keep using you like a cheap toy for another three years,&quot; Kane answered simply.</p>

<p>&quot;Or you work with me, and I will give you a safe way out of this city that doesn’t end with your head rotting on a spike.&quot;</p>

<p>Silence settled over the alley once again while she weighed her limited options.</p>

<p>The distant tournament music drifted through the night air, mocking the life-or-death decision happening in the shadows.</p>

<p>&quot;Why would you help me?&quot; she asked, her voice sounding genuinely confused by his sudden mercy.</p>

<p>Kane smiled slowly, letting his confident charm return.</p>

<p>&quot;Because useful people deserve to be rewarded instead of punished.&quot;</p>

<p>He stepped back again to give her a clear path out of the alley.</p>

<p>&quot;Go now. Your captain is waiting.&quot;</p>

<p>Firlia pulled her cloak tight around her shoulders and walked away briskly without looking back.</p>

<p>Kane watched her retreating figure until she rounded the far corner and vanished into the night.</p>

<p>’The prince is already moving his pieces to eliminate me outside the arena,’ Kane thought, feeling a rush of anticipation.</p>

<p>’Which means he is far more threatened by my presence than he is showing the public. Good. Scared people always make careless mistakes.’</p>

<p>He turned around and headed back toward his rented inn room, his mind already running through the endless possibilities of this brand new information.</p>
