<p>Arena attendants reached the bleeding prince first.</p>

<p>Elite medical personnel swarmed the place, applying a magical tourniquet before the crowd even processed what had just happened.</p>

<p>Through the healing magic, the injured heir refused to make a single sound of pain.</p>

<p>Resting in the blood-stained sand with his eyes closed, he just let the clerics work on his ruined arm.</p>

<p>When they finally lifted him onto a woven stretcher, he opened his eyes once and looked at Kane.</p>

<p>It was just a quiet, exhausted acknowledgement. Then the medics carried him quickly through the northern tunnel.</p>

<p>Thirty seconds of silence followed. It was the longest silence in the tournament’s entire five-hundred-year history.</p>

<p>&quot;The... the Celestial Aurora Tournament... has concluded,&quot; the official commentator finally stammered.</p>

<p>His voice lacked the practised, theatrical delivery he used all month.</p>

<p>He sounded much quieter, speaking as if he were just talking to himself in an empty room.</p>

<p>&quot;Prince Aelrindor has yielded,&quot; the commentator continued, finding a bit of his professional.</p>

<p>&quot;The challenger, Kane of the Bloodfang Clan, has been declared the official winner of the five hundred and twelfth Celestial Aurora.&quot;</p>

<p>Pausing briefly, the announcer swallowed hard.</p>

<p>&quot;I’ve been the voice of this grand tournament for fifty years,&quot; he confessed, his voice breaking slightly.</p>

<p>&quot;I’ve seen legendary upsets. I’ve witnessed extraordinary martial performances. But I have never seen anything like what this arena witnessed today.&quot;</p>

<p>Seventy thousand people finally started making noise, though it was unlike anything heard before.</p>

<p>Descending from the royal box, the queen abandoned all protocol.</p>

<p>Walking down the stone steps herself, she forced her attendants to scramble just to keep pace.</p>

<p>A radius of quiet spread through the crowd as people noticed her movement.</p>

<p>Stopping five feet from him, she met his eyes.</p>

<p>They stared at each other for a long moment before she turned her back on him, facing the sprawling arena to address everyone.</p>

<p>Her voice carried perfectly without any amplification magic.</p>

<p>&quot;The Celestial Aurora exists to find the worthy,&quot; the queen said smoothly.</p>

<p>Allowing a brief pause, she let the words echo.</p>

<p>&quot;Effective immediately, the Bloodfang Clan and all barbarian peoples are officially recognised as eligible participants in all future Celestial Aurora tournaments,&quot; she declared.</p>

<p>&quot;Furthermore, this royal recognition extends to all guild registrations, official trade agreements, and permanent residency applications within Elven Imperial territory.&quot;</p>

<p>A loud ripple tore through the noble sections.</p>

<p>Not all of the noise sounded approving, but the queen ignored their whining.</p>

<p>&quot;The tournament champion is entitled to the traditional rewards of victory,&quot; she continued, turning her head just enough to look at Kane again.</p>

<p>&quot;As is customary, the empire extends the formal offer of permanent residency, full marriage eligibility within high elven society, and a manor property within any district of the empire of his choosing.&quot;</p>

<p>She held his gaze.</p>

<p>&quot;If he chooses to accept.&quot;</p>

<p>’She’s trying to buy me,’ Kane mused, looking at her, then at the arena, and finally at the seventy thousand people hanging on his every word.</p>

<p>&quot;I’ll give my answer in three days.&quot;</p>

<p>Something in the queen’s expression shifted, though it wasn’t surprise.</p>

<p>Offering a single nod, she turned and walked away.</p>

<p>The crowd’s deafening noise instantly filled the vacuum she left behind.</p>

<p>A young elf suddenly appeared right at Kane’s elbow.</p>

<p>Dressed in formal, tailored robes and clutching a leather portfolio, she looked like a high-level secretary.</p>

<p>Being young by elven standards meant nothing in terms of actual years, but her posture screamed rigorous training.</p>

<p>Looking up at him with crisp professional efficiency, she clearly intended to do her assigned job correctly, regardless of her personal opinion regarding barbarians.</p>

<p>&quot;I’ve been designated as your administrative liaison for the residency process,&quot; she stated quickly, her tone polite but entirely devoid of warmth.</p>

<p>&quot;My name is Caelindra. When you are ready to discuss the intricate details of your manor selection, the interior staffing requirements, and the associated royal entitlements, I am entirely available to facilitate the paperwork.&quot;</p>

<p>Kane stared down at the stern little bureaucrat.</p>

<p>&quot;Three days.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;Understood,&quot; Caelindra replied, pulling a long quill out to make a swift, precise note in her portfolio.</p>

<p>&quot;I’ll arrange a formal meeting for then. Shall I coordinate with your existing staff, or will you be managing the transition personally?&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;My people will handle it,&quot; Kane said, taking a step forward.</p>

<p>Instead of leaving, she fell into step two paces behind him as he turned toward the southern tunnel.</p>

<p>Apparently, waiting three days only applied to their meeting, not to her physical presence.</p>

<p>Kane decided not to tell her to go away, finding her stubborn dedication faintly entertaining.</p>

<p>Grieselda stood waiting right at the shadowed tunnel entrance.</p>

<p>Approaching the fallen angel, Kane noticed the intricate mark on her collarbone had shifted.</p>

<p>&quot;The prince told me she found someone,&quot; Kane said quietly, pausing beside her.</p>

<p>She held his gaze for a long moment, her golden eyes shining in the tunnel’s gloom.</p>

<p>[Quest Completed: Win the Elven Tournament]</p>

<p>[Reward: Title - Empire Breaker]</p>

<p>[Effect: All negotiation and intimidation checks within the Elven Empire receive a permanent 40% advantage.]</p>

<p>[Noble-rank elves cannot initiate hostile action against you without the queen’s written authorisation.]</p>

<p>[Bonus Reward: Elven Empire Manor - any district, fully staffed]</p>

<p>[Bonus Reward: 15,000 Gold Coins added to inventory]</p>

<p>[Bonus Reward: Sovereign’s Brand capacity increased - maximum bonded members raised from 5 to 12]</p>

<p>[New Passive Unlocked: Conqueror’s Presence]</p>

<p>[Effect: Your mere presence in a location increases allied morale and decreases enemy combat efficiency by 15%]</p>

<p>[Quest Updated: The Coming Storm]</p>

<p>[Menual Empire mobilisation confirmed - 72 hours until border crossing]</p>

<p>[Note: You have three days.]</p>

<p>Footsteps echoed down the corridor before Kane could even dismiss the text.</p>

<p>Thora crashed into his chest first, wrapping her arms tightly around his neck, while Sira slammed into his side, pressing her slender frame against his ribs.</p>

<p>Kane caught them both effortlessly, pulling their soft bodies flush against his.</p>

<p>His strong arms wrapped around them possessively, holding them close as if they were the only things that mattered in the world.</p>

<p>He tilted Thora’s chin up with reverence and captured her lips in a deep, soul-stirring kiss.</p>

<p>Then he turned to Sira, drawing her in just as tenderly, claiming her mouth with the same fierce heat.</p>

<p>When he finally pulled back, Thora rested her forehead against his jaw.</p>

<p>&quot;What now?&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;I want you to call our barbarians inside Sylvandar,&quot; Kane instructed, while his hands slowly caressed down the curves of their backs.</p>

<p>&quot;And send a message back to Greenhaven. Tell them to bring all of our people here.&quot;</p>

<p>Sira looked up at him, her body still pressed tightly to his.</p>

<p>&quot;Are we going to help them?&quot;</p>

<p>A smirk spread across Kane’s face as he glanced back toward the arena.</p>

<p>&quot;We’ll take over.&quot;</p>
