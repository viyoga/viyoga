<p>Firlia crossed her arms, studying his face with a mix of curiosity and lingering fear.</p>

<p>&quot;You’re planning to walk right into an ambush tonight. Lothburk is not a standard arena fighter, Kane. He fights dirty, and he brings armed thugs to ensure nobody interferes.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;I’m counting on it,&quot; Kane replied, leaning closer to lower his voice.</p>

<p>&quot;I want him to bring his friends. It saves me the trouble of hunting them down individually later. Where did he set up his base?&quot;</p>

<p>Firlia let out a frustrated sigh, clearly thinking he was insane.</p>

<p>&quot;If you die tonight, the prince is going to execute me tomorrow morning for failing my mission.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;Then it’s a very good thing I have no intention of dying,&quot; Kane smirked, reaching out to trace a finger gently along her jawline.</p>

<p>She shivered slightly at his touch but didn’t pull away.</p>

<p>The noblewoman was slowly learning to trust the protection he offered, even if his methods were terrifying.</p>

<p>She was shaking slightly from the lingering adrenaline of her failed assassination attempt, but she still managed to stand her ground against the cool stone wall.</p>

<p>&quot;Lothburk fights tonight,&quot; she said.</p>

<p>&quot;Outside the east wall. There is a fighting pit that the royal guard officially doesn’t know about. The prince told him you would be there.&quot;</p>

<p>Kane studied her face in the dim torchlight.</p>

<p>The panicked desperation from last night was completely gone, replaced by something much harder and far more determined.</p>

<p>She was finally choosing to play the game instead of just being a helpless pawn.</p>

<p>&quot;You came to warn me,&quot; Kane noted softly, letting his gaze wander over her tense posture with a quiet sense of approval.</p>

<p>Looking away to hide her face, she pulled her green robes tighter around her shoulders.</p>

<p>&quot;Consider it the first installment.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;Good,&quot; Kane said simply and walked right past her into the fading sunlight.</p>

<p>Sending a quick message through his new spy network was remarkably easy.</p>

<p>He relayed exactly three words to Brak through Sira’s intelligence loop that evening.</p>

<p>&quot;Bring the axes.&quot;</p>

<p>The underground fighting pit sat hidden near the eastern wall of the city.</p>

<p>Finding it required walking through a winding maze of narrow alleys.</p>

<p>It was a rough stone circle dug directly into the ground behind a legitimate merchant warehouse.</p>

<p>Rules simply didn’t exist down here, nor did official announcers or royal guards patrolling the perimeter.</p>

<p>Torchlight flickered against the brickwork to illuminate whatever desperate fighters the underground circuit brought in on any given night.</p>

<p>Kane arrived alone, leaving his usual entourage behind.</p>

<p>Walking solo meant Lothburk wouldn’t suspect a trap, so the overconfident beastman would feel secure enough to monologue.</p>

<p>But actually being alone was a fool’s gamble.</p>

<p>His backup was already in the perfect position. Brak and six Bloodfang warriors crouched silently in the surrounding alleys long before Kane ever arrived, blending flawlessly into the nighttime shadows.</p>

<p>Sira knelt on a flat rooftop twenty meters back with an arrow already nocked to her silkwood bow.</p>

<p>Lothburk was already waiting down in the pit.</p>

<p>The beastman was enormous, standing a full head taller than Kane and built wider than Rutheus.</p>

<p>Four muscular arms crossed confidently over his broad chest.</p>

<p>A protruding lower jaw covered in natural bone armor made him look like a walking nightmare.</p>

<p>He stared up with flat, predatory yellow eyes that entirely lacked any kind of emotion.</p>

<p>Two of his hired thugs flanked the top entrance of the pit, trying their best to look intimidating.</p>

<p>Kane walked right past them without acknowledging they even existed.</p>

<p>Lothburk looked at the red-haired warlord the same way a starving predator looks at a helpless meal.</p>

<p>&quot;You’re smaller than I expected,&quot; Lothburk growled, and his voice sounded exactly like gravel rolling around in an empty wooden barrel.</p>

<p>&quot;You’re just fat as fuck,&quot; Kane replied, stopping right at the edge of the sunken stone circle.</p>

<p>While the mutant glared at him, Kane quietly scanned the four-armed beastman with his system interface.</p>

<p>[Scan Initiated]</p>

<p>[Target: Lothburk - Stormforged Stage 4]</p>

<p>[Level: 67]</p>

<p>[Ability: Quad Strike - All four arms attack simultaneously.]</p>

<p>[Ability: Bone Cage - Lower arms lock an opponent’s limbs while upper arms deliver killing blows.]</p>

<p>[Warning: Direct physical exchange not recommended at current stage.]</p>

<p>[Recommendation: Use environment and Soul Brand within the first ten seconds or disengage.]</p>

<p>’Okay. This will be fun.’</p>

<p>Kane didn’t draw his weapon right away.</p>

<p>Standing in the center of the underground fighting pit, he kept his hands empty.</p>

<p>He needed the mutant to move aggressively and overextend himself to create a perfect opening for the brand.</p>

<p>The air smelled of sweat and blood, typical for a place where desperate men came to die for coin.</p>

<p>&quot;AHHHHH.&quot;</p>

<p>Lothburk let out a guttural growl and launched his first Quad Strike.</p>

<p>All four muscular arms swung in a terrifying, coordinated pattern that left almost zero visible gaps for a counterattack.</p>

<p>Kane wove his body through the chaotic blur of fists, relying entirely on his base agility to survive.</p>

<p>The sudden wind from the near miss ruffled his red hair, proving just how much power the beastman packed into a single punch.</p>

<p>’One mistake and he turns my skull into powder,’ Kane thought, keeping his eyes locked on the moving limbs.</p>

<p>’Patience. Let him get comfortable.’</p>

<p>The second Quad Strike came much faster than the first.</p>

<p>A glancing blow caught Kane right on the shoulder, sending a painful shockwave down his spine.</p>

<p>’Shit.’</p>

<p>He stumbled sideways across the rough floor, trying to catch his balance.</p>

<p>A normal fighter would have suffered a shattered collarbone from that kind of impact, but his high Vitality stat absorbed the worst of the damage to keep him in the fight.</p>

<p>Lothburk saw the clumsy stumble and immediately read it as critical damage.</p>

<p>The beastman grinned, showing off rows of crooked teeth, and committed fully to his signature Bone Cage technique.</p>

<p>&quot;Is that all?&quot;</p>

<p>Spreading his lower arms wide, he lunged forward to trap Kane’s body in an inescapable grip.</p>

<p>That was the opening Kane was waiting for.</p>

<p>Dropping sharply to one knee, he ducked entirely under the sweeping lower arms.</p>

<p>He reached over his shoulder and finally drew Mjoldr in a practiced motion and drove the crimson blade straight upward, sinking the steel deeply into Lothburk’s exposed lower left arm, then landed a flat hit on his stomach, and pulled back for a second to look him in the eye.</p>

<p>&quot;Is that all?&quot;</p>

<p>The third strike landed in the middle of his head.</p>

<p>Bam.</p>

<p>[Hidden Property Activated: Soul Brand]</p>
