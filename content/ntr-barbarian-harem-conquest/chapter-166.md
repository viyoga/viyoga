<p>Misha grabbed the collar of his dress, pulling him down for another bruising kiss.</p>

<p>Kane let her take the lead this time, entirely content to follow her rhythm.</p>

<p>He kept his movements slow and deliberate, teasing her with agonizingly gentle touches that had her chasing his hands.</p>

<p>Every time she tried to escalate the pace, Kane smoothly redirected her, keeping her hovering right on the edge of unbearable tension.</p>

<p>He whispered filthy, descriptive promises against her skin.</p>

<p>&quot;Once that heart of yours is fully healed,&quot; Kane murmured, pressing an open-mouthed kiss against her collarbone.</p>

<p>&quot;I am going to lock these bedroom doors and keep you trapped in this bed for three days.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;Ah... Kane...&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;No council meetings. No ledgers. No magic. Just me, you, and endless time.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;Mmm... three days... yes...&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;I am going to take my favorite silk sash and tie your wrists directly to that headboard. You will not be able to move a single inch unless I allow it.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;Oh... please...&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;You like the sound of that?&quot; Kane dragged his teeth gently over her pulse point, feeling it race frantically against his lips.</p>

<p>&quot;Being completely helpless under me? Having to beg for every single touch?&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;Ah! Yes... Kane, yes...&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;I am going to start right here,&quot; Kane whispered, his breath fanning across her shivering skin.</p>

<p>&quot;And I am going to use my mouth to map out every single inch of you. Slowly. I will take hours just tasting you.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;Oh, gods... Kane... ah... please...&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;I am going to make you so sensitive that just breathing on you will make you shake. You will forget your own name.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;Mmm... yes...&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;You will only know my name. Because you will be screaming it until your throat is completely raw.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;Make me... ah! Kane, make me...&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;I am going to stretch you out, Misha. I am going to take you so deep and so hard that you will not be able to formulate a single coherent thought.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;Ahhh... please! Now... Kane, I want it now...&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;Not yet,&quot; Kane murmured, soothing her with a gentle stroke along her spine.</p>

<p>&quot;Patience, wife.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;I have no patience left.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;Then we will just have to build some.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;It feels like torture.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;The best kind.&quot;</p>

<p>Kane silenced her next protest with his lips, satisfied with his current strategy.</p>

<p>The rest of the morning slipped away in a blur of hushed whispers, tangled limbs, and breathless negotiations.</p>

<p>Kane kept his promise, refusing to push her past her limits, but ensuring she felt every single second of his undivided attention.</p>

<p>The Elven Queen could wait a few more hours to receive her surrender terms.</p>

<p>Right now, the only surrender Kane cared about was happening perfectly within the walls of his own bedroom.</p>

<p>Moving casually off the edge of the mattress, Kane stretched his arms overhead before reaching down to scoop Misha’s discarded morning gown from the rugs.</p>

<p>Tossing the silken garment onto her lap, he offered a satisfied smirk.</p>

<p>&quot;Time to rebuild an empire, wife.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;Give me five more minutes,&quot; Misha groaned softly, pulling the tangled sheets up to her chin.</p>

<p>&quot;Being tormented by a warlord is exhausting work. My legs feel entirely like jelly.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;I barely even started,&quot; Kane chuckled, pulling his own tunic over his head and adjusting the collar.</p>

<p>&quot;Imagine how you will feel after those three days I promised.&quot;</p>

<p>A blush colored her cheeks as she sat up, slipping the gown over her shoulders.</p>

<p>Misha reached for a silver comb resting on the bedside table, dragging it through her disheveled hair with a frustrated sigh. Snags and knots refused to yield to her efforts.</p>

<p>She looked like she just survived a hurricane, Kane noted fondly, stepping closer to take the comb right out of her hand.</p>

<p>A very beautiful hurricane.</p>

<p>&quot;Turn around,&quot; Kane instructed softly, taking a seat on the edge of the mattress behind her.</p>

<p>Misha shifted willingly, resting her back against his chest.</p>

<p>Kane gathered the long, silver strands into his hands, working the comb through the knots with surprising care.</p>

<p>For a man who spent all this time swinging broadswords and breaking siege lines, his touch remained remarkably gentle.</p>

<p>&quot;You have a hidden talent,&quot; Misha murmured, leaning into his warmth.</p>

<p>&quot;Who taught the Warlord of Menual how to braid hair?&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;Survival instinct,&quot; Kane replied easily, dividing the strands to weave a simple, elegant braid.</p>

<p>&quot;If I pull too hard, you will hex me into a toad. I value my current form.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;A very smart deduction.&quot; Misha tilted her head, allowing him better access.</p>

<p>&quot;You would make a terrible toad anyway. Too arrogant.&quot;</p>

<p>Tying the braid off with a small ribbon, Kane leaned down to press a quick kiss behind her pointed ear.</p>

<p>&quot;Done. You look presentable enough for the public.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;Presentable,&quot; Misha scoffed playfully, standing up and smoothing her gown.</p>

<p>&quot;I look radiant. You are just too stubborn to admit it.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;Radiant,&quot; Kane corrected, offering his arm.</p>

<p>&quot;Shall we go see if our vanguard managed to burn the city down while we were distracted?&quot;</p>

<p>Taking his arm, Misha walked alongside him out of the bedchambers.</p>

<p>Stepping into the bustling corridors, they navigated the stone steps leading back down toward the main courtyard.</p>

<p>Guards bowed respectfully as they passed, though a few definitely shared knowing glances.</p>

<p>Pushing the doors open, the crisp midday air greeted them. Training dummies lay splintered across the training ring.</p>

<p>Sira sat cross-legged on a supply crate, tossing a silver coin into the air and catching it repeatedly.</p>

<p>Thora leaned against the weapon racks, sharpening her broadsword with a whetstone. Firlia stood nearby, verifying a merchant ledger with a passing trader.</p>

<p>&quot;Look who finally decided to join the war effort,&quot; Sira called out, catching her coin and pointing it directly at them.</p>

<p>&quot;We were about to send a search party. Or at least slide some water under the door.&quot;</p>

<p>Thora did not even look up from her blade.</p>

<p>&quot;I told you they were busy conducting strategic maneuvers. Warlord business.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;Very funny,&quot; Kane shot back, descending the final few steps into the yard.</p>

<p>&quot;I hope you all used this time productively. I do not want to pay you for standing around gossiping like village merchants.&quot;</p>
