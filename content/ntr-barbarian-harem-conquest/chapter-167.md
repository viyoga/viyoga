<p>Retreating to the highest tower of the central keep offered a rare and highly appreciated commodity.</p>

<p>Peace.</p>

<p>Kane paused at the sturdy door, glancing back at the two armored guards flanking the stairwell.</p>

<p>&quot;Nobody enters,&quot; Kane instructed, crossing his arms over his chest.</p>

<p>&quot;Not Firlia, not Thora, and especially not Misha. If my women ask, tell them I am deep in meditation.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;Yes, my lord,&quot; the guard on the left replied, though his voice wavered slightly at the thought of denying the oracle entry.</p>

<p>Closing the door with a satisfying click, Kane walked over to the polished desk taking up most of the room.</p>

<p>Scattered ledgers and tax reports covered the surface, yet he ignored them entirely, opting instead to summon his interface with a simple thought.</p>

<p>Translucent blue screens materialized in the quiet air, casting a soft glow across the room.</p>

<p>[Territory: Menual. Status: Flourishing.]</p>

<p>[Current Treasury: 80,450 Gold Coins.]</p>

<p>[Trade Route Efficiency: 65% and rising.]</p>

<p>’I love my wives, but a man needs complete isolation to properly extort neighboring lords,’ Kane mused, tapping the treasury number to review the daily breakdown.</p>

<p>’If Misha were here right now, she would demand a new temple before I even finished counting the silver.’</p>

<p>Navigating through the menus, he selected the infrastructure tab.</p>

<p>Building things manually took months of hauling stone and paying wages, whereas the system offered a much faster, albeit expensive, alternative.</p>

<p>He allocated three thousand gold coins to instantly construct a dedicated Merchant Guildhall near the outer gates, skipping the tedious labor process entirely.</p>

<p>A soft chime confirmed the transaction, locking the funds away.</p>

<p>Before he could even examine the new projected income stats, a hesitant knock echoed through the room.</p>

<p>Kane sighed, swiping the screens away with a flick of his wrist.</p>

<p>&quot;I specifically said no one.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;It is Jarn, Commander,&quot; a muffled, trembling voice called out from the hallway.</p>

<p>&quot;The chief builder? It is urgent.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;Come in, Jarn.&quot;</p>

<p>The wooden door pushed open slowly, revealing a very sweaty, very anxious man clutching a rolled-up blueprint to his chest like a shield.</p>

<p>Jarn bowed awkwardly, his eyes darting around the empty room as if expecting to find an executioner hiding in the corners, or perhaps one of the warlord’s wives preparing a hex.</p>

<p>&quot;Speak,&quot; Kane prompted, leaning back in his chair and lacing his fingers together.</p>

<p>&quot;I am currently organizing the entire regional economy, so please make it fast.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;We have a problem with the new alchemy lab,&quot; Jarn stammered, wiping his forehead with a sleeve while taking a cautious step forward.</p>

<p>&quot;The stone shipments from the southern quarries are delayed by three days. Bandits washed out the main bridge, and the caravans are stranded near the river.&quot;</p>

<p>Kane blinked, entirely unfazed by the logistical nightmare threatening to halt his production lines.</p>

<p>&quot;Is that all?&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;Commander, without that stone, the foundation cannot be set,&quot; Jarn explained, his voice rising in panic as he unrolled the blueprint to point at a series of complex diagrams.</p>

<p>&quot;The alchemists are already complaining about the delays, and Lady Firlia will have my head if the potion quotas drop! She threatened to audit my personal finances!&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;Firlia is just passionate about profit,&quot; Kane replied, pulling up his system interface again.</p>

<p>It remained invisible to the builder, making Kane look like he was simply staring into space while addressing a very real crisis.</p>

<p>&quot;Do not worry about the stone, Jarn. You will find the required materials stacked perfectly behind the barracks by noon.&quot;</p>

<p>Jarn blinked in confusion, his mouth opening and closing a few times before he finally found his words.</p>

<p>&quot;But... Commander, the supply lines are completely blocked. It is physically impossible to move that much weight by noon unless we hire a legion of mages.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;I handled it,&quot; Kane stated simply.</p>

<p>He tapped a button on his interface, paying system coins to instantly teleport refined building materials directly into his territory limits.</p>

<p>&quot;Your only job is to ensure the alchemists have enough boiling cauldrons to brew fifty batches of stamina potions a day. Can you manage that, or do I need to find a new chief architect?&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;Fifty batches?&quot; Jarn swallowed hard, staring at the Warlord with a mix of terror and profound awe.</p>

<p>&quot;Yes, Commander! I will double the work shifts immediately! The cauldrons will be boiling before sunset!&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;Excellent. Close the door on your way out, and tell Firlia to leave your finances alone.&quot;</p>

<p>Watching the architect scurry out of the room like a frightened rabbit, Kane allowed a satisfied smirk to reach his lips.</p>

<p>’Magic is definitely expensive, but it makes dealing with logistics so much easier,’ Kane thought, reviewing the slightly depleted treasury numbers.</p>

<p>’Time is money, and I plan to take all the money this region has to offer.’</p>

<p>With the trade hub sorted and the alchemy lab back on schedule, Kane shifted his attention to the military tabs.</p>

<p>The upcoming invasion of Sylvander required a vanguard equipped with more than just enthusiasm and basic steel.</p>

<p>Bringing up the Forge menu, he selected the Tier III upgrade path. It cost another two thousand gold, yet the investment was entirely necessary for his plans.</p>

<p>[Forge Upgraded to Tier III.]</p>

<p>[Blueprint Unlocked: Frost-Bound Broadswords.]</p>

<p>[Blueprint Unlocked: Siege Ballistae.]</p>

<p>Scanning the details of the new weapons, Kane drafted a quick, fake trade manifest on a spare piece of parchment.</p>

<p>’The neighboring lords are desperate for magical weapons to fight off the local monsters,’ Kane analyzed, tapping his quill against the desk.</p>

<p>’We sell them our Tier I gear at a severe markup, making them think they are buying our finest craftsmanship. Meanwhile, we arm our own hidden troops with these new Tier III broadswords.’</p>

<p>It was a beautifully devious plan.</p>

<p>By the time the surrounding kingdoms realized Menual possessed an overwhelming technological advantage, they would have already funded the very army marching to conquer them.</p>

<p>Kane finalized the blueprints, assigning a team of trusted blacksmiths to begin production on the siege ballistae in absolute secrecy.</p>

<p>The elves possessed powerful illusions and ancient barriers, requiring long-range artillery to safely break their defenses before the infantry ever engaged.</p>

<p>’They think I am just a barbarian warlord managing a mercenary camp,’ Kane laughed softly, dismissing the blue screens and standing up from the desk.</p>

<p>’Let them keep believing that. Right up until a frost-bound ballista bolt shatters their front gates.’</p>
