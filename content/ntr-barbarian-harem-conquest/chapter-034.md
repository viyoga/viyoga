<p>Kane and Grieselda walked alone to their inn near the merchant district.</p>

<p>Thora and Sira wanted to look around the city for a few more hours, while Brak and the other barbarians were hell-bent on training back at the Proving Grounds.</p>

<p>Kane felt happy knowing his warriors took their preparation seriously, but he wasn’t happy watching Grieselda right now.</p>

<p>She stood in the centre of the quiet room and reached out toward his waist.</p>

<p>She tapped the iron blade of his right axe twice with her pale finger.</p>

<p>A bright orange glow emitted from her hand, wrapping around the metal in a sudden flash of heat.</p>

<p>&quot;What?&quot; Kane asked, taking a quick step back.</p>

<p>Before he could finish acting surprised, the solid iron blade completely melted into nothing.</p>

<p>Liquid metal did not drip onto the wooden floor as the weapon simply ceased to exist, leaving only the wooden handle gripped securely in his holster.</p>

<p>&quot;Huh? Did... you just?&quot; Kane stammered, staring at the ruined stump.</p>

<p>She simply extended her other hand, gesturing patiently for his remaining weapon.</p>

<p>&quot;You want this too?&quot; Kane asked, handing her the second axe anyway despite his complete confusion.</p>

<p>She touched the edge of the blade, and the same orange glow emitted from her palm.</p>

<p>The iron softened instantly and melted away into thin air.</p>

<p>He stood in the quiet room holding two useless sticks.</p>

<p>&quot;The fuck are you doing?&quot; Kane asked, tossing the handles onto the bed.</p>

<p>Grieselda did not answer with words.</p>

<p>She raised both of her hands and crossed her wrists to make a clear X in the air.</p>

<p>Then she moved one hand down and pressed it firmly over her own heart.</p>

<p>A blue window popped up in Kane’s line of sight.</p>

<p>[System Translation]</p>

<p>[The weapon was not strong enough to withstand the heart.]</p>

<p>’Oh, you are translating for her now,’ Kane thought.</p>

<p>Actually, it made sense.</p>

<p>The corrupted bear was a level fifty boss, so basic iron weapons would just crumble under that kind of raw energy.</p>

<p>He needed a proper vessel for the crafting process, or he would just waste the rare drop entirely.</p>

<p>&quot;Wait a moment,&quot; Kane said, opening his shop interface.</p>

<p>He navigated to the standard weapons tab to see if anything cheap could hold the upgrade.</p>

<p>The blue screen expanded, displaying a long list of basic gear.</p>

<p>[Bloodfang Market]</p>

<p>[Iron Broadsword - 100 Coins]</p>

<p>[Steel Mace - 150 Coins]</p>

<p>[Hunter’s Recurve - 200 Coins]</p>

<p>[Mercenary Spear - 250 Coins]</p>

<p>[Knight’s Kite Shield - 300 Coins]</p>

<p>[Assassin Daggers - 350 Coins]</p>

<p>’Hmm, this is not enough,’ Kane decided, quickly scrolling past the cheap metal options.</p>

<p>’These will just melt like the last ones, and I can’t afford to throw coins away on garbage.’</p>

<p>&quot;Open Tier Two,&quot; Kane commanded aloud.</p>

<p>A brand new array of weapons formed in the air.</p>

<p>The system marked them all as rare items, and each one cost significantly more gold than the basic tier.</p>

<p>[Silvermoon Blade - 1000 Coins]</p>

<p>[Obsidian Twin Daggers - 1500 Coins]</p>

<p>[Frostburn Spear - 2000 Coins]</p>

<p>[Venom-Laced Whip - 2500 Coins]</p>

<p>[Thunderstrike Hammer - 3000 Coins]</p>

<p>[Blood-Iron Claymore - 3500 Coins]</p>

<p>[Sunlight Piercer Bow - 4000 Coins]</p>

<p>[Dragon-Bone Staff - 4500 Coins]</p>

<p>[Void-Forged Gauntlets - 4800 Coins]</p>

<p>[Mjoldr - 5000 Coins]</p>

<p>Grieselda stepped forward and raised her arm. Her slender finger moved directly toward the screen, stopping on the final message at the bottom of the list.</p>

<p>Kane widened his eyes in shock.</p>

<p>&quot;You can see the screen?&quot;</p>

<p>She nodded slowly, keeping her finger pointed at the expensive weapon.</p>

<p>He wasn’t sure how to feel about this strange development.</p>

<p>Thora and Sira never noticed the floating windows, and nobody else in the world had ever reacted to the system interface.</p>

<p>Grieselda was different, proving she held a much deeper connection to the magical laws governing this reality.</p>

<p>Her finger remained stopped at the most expensive weapon on the list, an axe called Mjoldr that cost five thousand coins.</p>

<p>Kane immediately bought it.</p>

<p>He trusted her bizarre instincts, and he desperately needed a new weapon anyway before the tournament began.</p>

<p>[Transaction Complete]</p>

<p>[5,000 Gold Coins Deducted]</p>

<p>A golden light flooded the small inn room, blinding Kane for a split second.</p>

<p>When the light faded, a magnificent new weapon rested heavily in his grip.</p>

<p>The axe featured a sleek, blackened steel handle wrapped in rich leather. The curved blade pulsed with a faint, icy blue aura that cooled the air around them.</p>

<p>It felt balanced in his hands, radiating an undeniable aura of lethality.</p>

<p>[Name: Mjoldr]</p>

<p>[Level: 100]</p>

<p>[Rank: S]</p>

<p>Grieselda reached out and touched the icy metal.</p>

<p>The blade brightened as her orange energy flared around her hand, but it didn’t melt this time.</p>

<p>She held her empty hand out again, waiting patiently for the final ingredient.</p>

<p>Kane opened his inventory and retrieved the rare loot he earned in the forest. He placed the item carefully into her pale hands.</p>

<p>[Rare Material: Grimfang Corrupted Heart]</p>

<p>[Description: A powerful still-beating core filled with ancient primal energy.]</p>

<p>The red core pulsed warmly between her fingers, dripping a few drops of blood onto the floorboards.</p>

<p>Grieselda gripped the corrupted heart tightly with both hands, and her eyes glowed for a brief second.</p>

<p>She pulled her hands apart, breaking the heart cleanly into two separate, bleeding pieces.</p>

<p>The first piece was placed directly into the flat side of the new axe blade.</p>

<p>Then she turned back to Kane.</p>

<p>She held the second dripping half of the corrupted core out toward his face, offering it to him with a blank stare.</p>

<p>Kane stared at the bleeding muscle resting in her pale palm. It dripped blood onto the floorboards, but her eyes remained expectant.</p>

<p>’This sucks.’</p>

<p>He grabbed the bloody piece, tossed it into his mouth, and swallowed it whole before he could gag.</p>

<p>A savage heat exploded inside his chest and spread through his veins like fire.</p>

<p>&quot;Guh!&quot;</p>

<p>Kane grabbed his shirt and fell hard onto his knees as his own heart began to beat faster against his ribs with terrifying speed.</p>

<p>[Warning: Heart rate uncontrolled]</p>

<p>Grieselda stood quietly over him. She held the newly forged Mjoldr in both hands, letting the red aura radiate from the steel blade.</p>

<p>’Wait, what are you doing?’ Kane tried to ask, but his throat seized entirely, so no sound came out.</p>

<p>Grieselda stepped forward without a single word of hesitation.</p>

<p>She swung the axe down and slammed the blade directly into the centre of his chest.</p>
