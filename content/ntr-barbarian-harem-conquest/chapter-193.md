<p>Silence swallowed the throne room whole.</p>

<p>Every noblewoman stared in open shock, while the Empress gripped the armrests of her sunstone throne so hard her knuckles turned white.</p>

<p>Her eyes darted from the warm, golden glow radiating off Misha straight to the scarred barbarian standing right beside her.</p>

<p>&quot;You...&quot; The Empress finally found her voice, though it lacked its usual commanding bite.</p>

<p>&quot;My daughter. You are alive.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;I am home, Mother,&quot; Misha answered, lifting her chin so her aura washed over the marble steps.</p>

<p>&quot;And I brought my husband.&quot;</p>

<p>Gasps rippled through the gathered court.</p>

<p>Armored Paladins instinctively tightened their grips on their weapons, stepping closer to the royal dais.</p>

<p>’Can’t push the alliance while they are holding halberds to my neck,’ Kane thought, keeping his posture relaxed.</p>

<p>’If we start demanding armies right now, this turns into a brawl. Time to play the polite son-in-law.’</p>

<p>Stepping forward, Kane offered a lazy, almost sarcastic bow.</p>

<p>&quot;Your Majesty. It is a genuine honor to meet you again. I know we have a lot of world-saving politics to discuss, but your daughter just flew halfway across the continent to get here. Her heart rate is up, and she is exhausted. She needs food and a real bed before we talk about treaties.&quot;</p>

<p>The Empress narrowed her eyes, clearly torn between ordering his execution and rushing down to embrace her child.</p>

<p>Looking at Misha’s pale cheeks, maternal instinct won the battle.</p>

<p>&quot;We will hold the formal council tomorrow morning,&quot; the Empress declared, waving a dismissive hand toward her royal guards.</p>

<p>&quot;Prepare the grandest guest suites in the east wing. And fetch the royal healers.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;I don’t need healers,&quot; Misha sighed, leaning slightly against Kane’s arm.</p>

<p>&quot;Just a long nap.&quot;</p>

<p>Male servants practically tripped over themselves trying to obey the Empress’s orders.</p>

<p>Ten minutes later, a very nervous steward led the vanguard down a wide corridor lined with towering statues of winged saints.</p>

<p>The poor man kept flinching every time Thora’s boots echoed too loudly against the floor, clearly expecting to be punished at any moment.</p>

<p>This guy looks like he expects me to eat him for dinner.</p>

<p>Deciding to test the waters, Kane leaned forward slightly.</p>

<p>&quot;Hey, friend. What’s good to eat in this place? We prefer meat over whatever fancy salads your nobles eat.&quot;</p>

<p>The steward squeaked, nearly dropping his silver candelabra.</p>

<p>&quot;Y-yes, my Lord! The kitchens can prepare roasted boar, or perhaps some spiced fowl, whatever you desire. Please do not strike me.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;Relax,&quot; Kane chuckled, patting the man on the shoulder.</p>

<p>The steward went entirely rigid.</p>

<p>&quot;Nobody is getting hit,&quot; Kane promised smoothly.</p>

<p>&quot;Just bring a lot of food. My friends have big appetites.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;Right away!&quot; The steward pushed open a set of gilded double doors and practically sprinted back down the hallway.</p>

<p>Kane walked into the suite, letting out a low whistle.</p>

<p>White silk curtains framed expansive windows, and the main living area featured plush velvet couches arranged around a cold marble hearth.</p>

<p>Sira slipped past him like a shadow.</p>

<p>She immediately checked the balcony, peeking over the railing before dragging her fingers along the window frames.</p>

<p>&quot;Three entrances,&quot; Sira murmured, her eyes scanning the vaulted ceiling. &quot;One blind spot near the wardrobe. Balcony is exposed to the southern towers, so keep the curtains drawn if you don’t want snipers practicing their aim on you.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;Nobody is shooting at us today, Sira,&quot; Misha said gently, untying her travel cloak.</p>

<p>&quot;Just doing my job, Princess.&quot;</p>

<p>Thora let out a loud groan, stretching her arms above her head.</p>

<p>&quot;Finally. My back is killing me from sitting on that flying horse.&quot;</p>

<p>She unbuckled her weapon harness and tossed her giant halberd onto a delicate sun-wood table in the center of the room.</p>

<p>A loud crack echoed through the suite as the table fractured right down the middle, splintering under the weapon’s weight.</p>

<p>Everyone stared at the broken furniture.</p>

<p>&quot;Oops,&quot; Thora grinned unapologetically.</p>

<p>&quot;Guess they don’t build things like they used to.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;Thora, please,&quot; Misha rubbed her temples.</p>

<p>&quot;Try not to destroy the palace before we even sign the treaty.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;I’ll just put it on the floor next time.&quot;</p>

<p>Kane chuckled, steering Misha toward the adjoining bedroom.</p>

<p>&quot;You guys secure the perimeter and see if you can bully the kitchen staff into bringing us some drinks. I need to get my wife settled.&quot;</p>

<p>Closing the bedroom door behind them, Kane blocked out the noise of Thora arguing with Sira over who got the biggest couch.</p>

<p>The bedroom was just as extravagant as the lounge, dominated by a huge canopy bed covered in dense layers of white fur and silk.</p>

<p>Misha sat on the edge of the mattress, letting out a long, shuddering breath.</p>

<p>Her divine aura flickered, dimming as the adrenaline of facing her mother finally wore off. She rubbed her arms, shivering slightly in the crisp air.</p>

<p>Wrapping his arms around her, Kane pulled Misha into his chest.</p>

<p>He focused on his own internal energy, letting the ambient heat from his Warlord vitality radiate outward.</p>

<p>His skin warmed up like a furnace, providing an instant shield against the room’s chill.</p>

<p>Misha sighed in deep contentment, melting into his embrace.</p>

<p>&quot;You run hot. It feels really nice.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;Consider me your personal space heater,&quot; Kane smiled, resting his chin on top of her head.</p>

<p>&quot;You did good out there today. Your mother looked like she swallowed a lemon when you told her we were married.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;She is just shocked,&quot; Misha murmured, closing her eyes.</p>

<p>&quot;She always planned to trade me away to secure a border, but she never expected me to find someone like you who could heal me.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;Someone handsome and ridiculously charming?&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;Someone stubborn and deeply annoying.&quot;</p>

<p>Kane laughed, brushing a stray lock of hair behind her ear.</p>

<p>&quot;I’ll take it. But we both know tomorrow is going to be a bloodbath in that council room. She wants my troops, but she doesn’t want me.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;You have to play by her rules, Kane.&quot; Misha tilted her head up to look at him, her expression turning serious.</p>

<p>&quot;Milfheim operates on pride and divine right. If you insult her in front of her generals, she will lock us in the dungeons, devils or no devils.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;I know how to play politics, Princess.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;Your version of politics usually involves punching someone through a wall.&quot;</p>
