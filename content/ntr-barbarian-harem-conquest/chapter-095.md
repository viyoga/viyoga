<p>Waiting for the queen’s reply is useless.</p>

<p>To stop the fleet, Kane needed something other than elves.</p>

<p>Parchment rustled loudly as Kane rolled up the naval schematics.</p>

<p>Seraphine stepped into his study without bothering to knock. Her white armor caught the morning light, commanding his attention immediately.</p>

<p>&quot;You’ve a terrible habit of ignoring closed doors,&quot; Kane noted, leaning back in his wooden chair.</p>

<p>&quot;And you have a habit of making my Empress wait,&quot; Seraphine replied, tossing a sealed scroll onto his desk.</p>

<p>&quot;She officially ran out of patience last night.&quot;</p>

<p>Picking up the scroll, Kane inspected it.</p>

<p>The white wax bore the Celestial Mandate’s intricate crest.</p>

<p>&quot;I suppose she heard about my recent promotion,&quot; Kane smiled, tapping the seal.</p>

<p>&quot;Border Lord certainly sounds much better than barbarian gladiator.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;She heard you control the western pass,&quot; Seraphine corrected, stepping closer to his desk.</p>

<p>&quot;She also knows Chancellor Morvak is shifting his strategy to the sea. You need ships. We have them.&quot;</p>

<p>’She doesn’t miss a single detail,’ Kane realized, appreciating the First Blade’s razor-sharp intellect.</p>

<p>&quot;I’ll leave tomorrow,&quot; Kane decided, standing up to meet her gaze. &quot;Alone.&quot;</p>

<p>Seraphine blinked, her composed facade cracking for a fraction of a second.</p>

<p>&quot;Alone? You are a newly appointed warlord. You should bring your little tribal honor guard. Bring your elven archers.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;I’m traveling to negotiate a fleet alliance, not start a war,&quot; Kane countered, stepping around the desk until he stood mere inches from her.</p>

<p>&quot;Bringing an army looks like a threat. Coming alone looks like confidence.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;It looks like arrogance,&quot; Seraphine whispered, tilting her chin up to maintain steady eye contact.</p>

<p>&quot;Are you worried about my safety, Seraphine?&quot; Kane teased, letting his gaze drop intentionally to her lips.</p>

<p>&quot;I’m worried you will offend the Celestial Court within five minutes of arriving,&quot; she shot back, though a faint flush crept up her neck.</p>

<p>&quot;Milfheim is a strict matriarchy. Arrogant men usually end up missing their tongues or kneeling in penitence.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;Your Empress has never met a man quite like me,&quot; Kane murmured, reaching out to trace the edge of her silver pauldron.</p>

<p>Her breath hitched slightly, though she stubbornly held her ground.</p>

<p>&quot;She eats arrogant men for breakfast, Border Lord.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;Then I sincerely hope she has a very healthy appetite,&quot; Kane grinned, stepping back before the tension boiled over into something far more complicated.</p>

<p>&quot;Ride out with me tomorrow at dawn. You can protect me from all those terrifying noblewomen.&quot;</p>

<p>After informing Thora to manage, Kane left the place.</p>

<p>Crossing the eastern border felt like stepping onto another planet entirely.</p>

<p>Milfheim territory lacked the wild, untamed beauty of the Elven woods.</p>

<p>Everything here existed in perfect, structured order.</p>

<p>Pristine stone bridges crossed crystal-clear rivers.</p>

<p>Intricate shrines dedicated to the Celestial Goddess marked every single crossroad.</p>

<p>Riding casually beside Seraphine, Kane observed the shifting cultural dynamics.</p>

<p>Women held every position of visible authority.</p>

<p>Female merchants argued passionately over trade routes, while female priests blessed passing travelers.</p>

<p>Men served primarily as laborers, farmers, or silent guards performing highly specific duties.</p>

<p>A patrol of Celestial Mandate paladins intercepted them near a busy trading post.</p>

<p>Five tall women wearing polished silver plates blocked the main road.</p>

<p>Their leader glared at Kane with openly hostile eyes.</p>

<p>&quot;First Blade,&quot; the paladin captain greeted, bowing respectfully to Seraphine before pointing a gauntleted finger right at Kane.</p>

<p>&quot;Why is this armed male riding beside you without a penitence collar?&quot;</p>

<p>’They really don’t like outsiders,’ Kane mused, resting his hand casually on his saddle horn.</p>

<p>&quot;He’s the Elven Border Lord,&quot; Seraphine answered, asserting her superior rank.</p>

<p>&quot;And he is an official guest of the Empress. Stand aside, Captain.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;He carries a weapon,&quot; the captain protested, glaring at the axe strapped to Kane’s broad back.</p>

<p>&quot;If you want to try and take it from him, be my guest,&quot; Seraphine offered with a smile.</p>

<p>&quot;But I will not help you clean up your own blood.&quot;</p>

<p>Scowling fiercely, the paladin captain sharply ordered her patrol to clear the road.</p>

<p>&quot;Friendly girls,&quot; Kane chuckled as they rode past the glaring guards.</p>

<p>&quot;They are sworn to protect the divine purity of our empire,&quot; Seraphine explained, her tone softening just a fraction.</p>

<p>&quot;They see you as chaos personified.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;I’m chaos personified,&quot; Kane agreed happily.</p>

<p>Two days later, the Milfheim capital rose over the horizon.</p>

<p>Towering spires of white marble pierced the clouds.</p>

<p>Intricate stained-glass windows depicted ancient battles won by legendary matriarchs.</p>

<p>The city pulsed with a strict, unyielding energy that felt entirely foreign to Kane’s usual rugged experiences.</p>

<p>[Location Discovered: Milfheim Capital City]</p>

<p>[Cultural Alignment: Strict Matriarchy / Divine Order]</p>

<p>[New Quest Initiated: The Matriarch’s Court]</p>

<p>[Objective: Secure naval alliance without starting a holy war]</p>

<p>Riding through the front gates, Kane noted the distinct lack of typical military fortifications.</p>

<p>They didn’t need defensive walls; the entire population moved with the discipline of a zealous, unified army.</p>

<p>Dismounting in the grand courtyard, stable boys rushed forward with downcast eyes to take their tired horses.</p>

<p>None of the male servants dared to look Seraphine directly in the face.</p>

<p>&quot;Remember where you are,&quot; Seraphine warned, walking beside him toward the towering palace doors.</p>

<p>&quot;Do not draw your weapon. Do not raise your voice. The Empress responds to strength, not barbaric displays.&quot;</p>

<p>’Smashing skulls will not solve my problems today,’ Kane realized, adjusting his silver-embroidered tunic.</p>

<p>’Morvak is building a fleet. I desperately need Milfheim’s ships to sink it. I have to play their political game.’</p>

<p>&quot;Lead the way,&quot; Kane offered, giving her a polite, entirely sarcastic bow.</p>

<p>Rolling her eyes, Seraphine pushed the grand doors open.</p>

<p>Stepping into the vast throne room, Kane immediately felt the crushing weight of a hundred calculating female stares.</p>

<p>Noblewomen draped in silks and silver jewelry lined the pristine hall, watching him like hawks evaluating a particularly bold mouse.</p>

<p>At the far end of the room sat the Empress.</p>

<p>She looked nothing like the playful, seductive Elven Queen.</p>

<p>Wearing a crown of woven silver, the Milfheim ruler possessed an icy, untouchable beauty.</p>

<p>Her rigid posture radiated absolute authority.</p>

<p>’This negotiation is going to be a mess.’</p>
