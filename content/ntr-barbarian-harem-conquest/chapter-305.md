<p>Morning arrived with the rolling sound of horns.</p>

<p>Not the single call of a routine changeover.</p>

<p>Multiple tusks and war-horns from the outer posts and the high walls, the layered signal that the empire itself was being summoned to witness.</p>

<p>Kane opened his eyes to grey light and the distant, growing thunder of gathering banners.</p>

<p>Kessa was already awake, striped tail still, listening.</p>

<p>Griselda stood at the window, wings folded, yellow eyes on the movement below.</p>

<p>Kane rose, dressed in simple trousers and an open shirt that would not survive the first exchange, and left Mjoldr against the wall.</p>

<p>No weapons.</p>

<p>The rule had been spoken in council and accepted by the throne.</p>

<p>He would walk into the upper arena the same way he had walked into the first underground confrontation, empty-handed, and unwilling to stay down.</p>

<p>Outside, the capital was filling.</p>

<p>Beast warriors from every major banner still inside the walls streamed toward the upper arena.</p>

<p>Pure-blood retainers took the high terraces.</p>

<p>Southern and river-aligned clusters held lower sections closer to the exits, already positioned for whatever followed the result.</p>

<p>The horns continued, low and insistent, until the entire fighting floor and every surrounding rail stood packed under the rising sun.</p>

<p>Sunset had been the stated time.</p>

<p>The empire had begun gathering at dawn.</p>

<p>Kane walked down the final corridor shirtless.</p>

<p>Griselda’s last spell still lingered under his skin, a thin, cool layer of reinforced tissue and muted pain that would not last the full fight but might buy him the first critical exchanges.</p>

<p>Muscle shifted cleanly with every step.</p>

<p>The cuts from training had closed enough to stop seeping.</p>

<p>He flexed once as he stepped into the open mouth of the upper arena and felt every watching pair of eyes track the movement.</p>

<p>The place was a true colosseum.</p>

<p>Tiered stone rose on all sides, already packed with thousands.</p>

<p>Pure-blood banners dominated the higher rails.</p>

<p>Southern and river clusters held lower sections nearer the exits.</p>

<p>Warriors, war-chiefs, attendants, and every major voice still inside the capital filled the remaining space until the air itself felt heavy with breath and expectation.</p>

<p>Most of them had come to watch a human get ripped apart.</p>

<p>Kane smiled without humor and walked to the center of the dark-stone floor empty-handed, turning slowly so the entire bowl could see he carried nothing.</p>

<p>A few minutes later, the opposite archway opened.</p>

<p>Grathak entered to a rolling wave of sound.</p>

<p>Hands reached from the lower rails to graze his arms, his sides, the muscle of his shoulders as if contact alone conferred something.</p>

<p>He had always been massive.</p>

<p>This morning the densification looked tighter, denser, the dark fur stretched over muscle that seemed carved from something harder than ordinary flesh.</p>

<p>Pitch-black eyes swept the arena and locked on Kane.</p>

<p>Marisa walked beside him in a simple dark wrap, amber gaze fixed on the barbarian and never once rising to the crowd.</p>

<p>Grathak stopped at the edge of the floor, raised both arms, and let out a single earth-shaking roar that crushed every other noise into silence.</p>

<p>&quot;...&quot;</p>

<p>Then he crouched and launched.</p>

<p>BOOM!</p>

<p>Fifty meters vanished in one leap.</p>

<p>He landed so close the stone cracked under his weight, towering over Kane, breath hot and thick with the scent of refined essence.</p>

<p>&quot;You sure you want to do this, barbarian?&quot; Grathak’s voice carried without effort.</p>

<p>&quot;I can still give you a place in my empire. You are strong. Work below me.&quot;</p>

<p>Kane looked up at the pitch-black eyes and the tightened bulk.</p>

<p>&quot;I don’t work below.&quot;</p>

<p>They moved at the same instant.</p>

<p>&quot;Hmm.&quot;</p>

<p>Swoosh.</p>

<p>Both fists met in the space between them with a shockwave that rippled dust from the nearest tiers and forced the front rows back a step.</p>

<p>BAM!</p>

<p>The impact traveled up Kane’s arm like a struck anvil.</p>

<p>Grathak’s forehead smashed forward immediately after, catching Kane high on the temple and sending him skidding sideways across the stone.</p>

<p>The emperor followed without pause, one massive leg driving a kick that landed square in Kane’s ribs and launched him into the arena wall hard enough to spider-web the masonry.</p>

<p>Pain flared through his body.</p>

<p>The system’s suppressors bit down hard.</p>

<p>Kane hit the ground, rolled, and caught the follow-up stomp by the ankle with both hands.</p>

<p>He twisted and heaved with everything the Controlled Rage could give at ninety percent for a three-second burst.</p>

<p>Grathak’s densified mass left the ground for a fraction of a second, enough for Kane to slam him down onto his back and drop onto the larger chest, fists already hammering.</p>

<p>The first punch split skin over Grathak’s cheekbone.</p>

<p>The second landed on the throat and bounced off densified cartilage.</p>

<p>The third drove toward the damage line under the left eye cluster from the Guardian fight.</p>

<p>Grathak’s roar shook the floor.</p>

<p>One enormous hand clamped around Kane’s ribs and flung him off like a rag.</p>

<p>Kane hit, slid, and came up on pure stubbornness.</p>

<p>Blood ran from his temple and the reopened cut on his forearm.</p>

<p>Grathak rose with terrifying speed for something so large, pitch-black eyes flat with the kind of focus that no longer cared about entertainment.</p>

<p>&quot;You still stand after that.&quot; Grathak rolled one shoulder; the densified muscle flexed like cable.</p>

<p>&quot;Keep surprising me.&quot;</p>

<p>Kane spat red onto the stone and settled his weight.</p>

<p>&quot;I’m not most things.&quot;</p>

<p>They closed again.</p>

<p>Grathak’s reach was absurd.</p>

<p>Kane slipped the first sweeping arm by a finger’s width, drove a short knee toward the inner thigh, and felt the impact land on densified meat that barely gave.</p>

<p>A backhand caught him across the shoulder and spun him.</p>

<p>He used the spin, dropped low, and hammered an elbow into the same left-side rib juncture the system had flagged from the underground exchange.</p>

<p>Something gave with a wet, muffled crack.</p>

<p>Grathak’s breath hitched for the first time.</p>

<p>The emperor answered by simply walking through Kane’s next strike and wrapping both arms around him in a crushing clinch.</p>

<p>&quot;Gah!&quot;</p>

<p>Ribs ground.</p>

<p>Air vanished.</p>

<p>Kane’s vision tunneled.</p>

<p>He head-butted upward into the underside of Grathak’s jaw, once, twice, until the grip loosened enough to drop and twist free.</p>

<p>A clawed hand raked across his chest, opening three shallow lines that immediately welled with blood.</p>
