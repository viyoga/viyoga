<p>&quot;It is a beautiful plan,&quot; Kane admitted, looking away from the table to meet her gaze once more.</p>

<p>&quot;It requires patience, but it guarantees a flawless victory with minimal casualties on our side. The system interface won’t even predict a threat level until we are already inside their walls.&quot;</p>

<p>Seraphina tucked the map safely into her leather pouch, stepping backward toward the shadows lingering near the window.</p>

<p>&quot;I will return to Milfheim to finalize the bypass codes. I expect my payment to be ready when I get back, Kane.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;You will have your gold,&quot; Kane promised, watching her form begin to dissolve seamlessly back into the stealth magic.</p>

<p>&quot;And maybe we can finish that business of ours once we make this ready.&quot;</p>

<p>An echoing laugh lingered in the room long after she vanished, leaving Kane entirely alone with his racing thoughts and a perfect strategy for his next conquest.</p>

<p>Silence settled over the room the moment she vanished into the shadows.</p>

<p>Standing by the window for a few lingering seconds, Kane let the night breeze wash over his face before turning his attention back to the bed.</p>

<p>&quot;Was someone just here?&quot; Misha murmured, rubbing her sleepy eyes as she shifted against the plush pillows.</p>

<p>Walking slowly across the floorboards, Kane offered a warm smile and reached out to pull the blankets up around her shoulders.</p>

<p>&quot;Go back to sleep. It was just a ghost.&quot;</p>

<p>Misha sniffed the air delicately, her nose wrinkling in a very cute display of suspicion.</p>

<p>&quot;A ghost that smells remarkably like nightshade and trees. Seraphina was standing right by the bed, wasn’t she?&quot;</p>

<p>’Nothing gets past an oracle,’ Kane chuckled internally, sliding back under the covers and wrapping his uninjured arm around her waist.</p>

<p>’I should have known she was only pretending to be deeply asleep.’</p>

<p>&quot;She dropped by to deliver some scouting information,&quot; Kane admitted smoothly, resting his chin on the top of Misha’s head.</p>

<p>&quot;Nothing for you to worry about right now.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;I am not worried,&quot; Misha replied, tilting her head up to meet his gaze with a playful glint in her eyes.</p>

<p>&quot;I heard most of the conversation anyway. You two have unfinished business, it seems.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;Are you jealous?&quot; Kane teased, arching a single brow as he gently traced a lazy pattern along her spine.</p>

<p>&quot;Not in the slightest,&quot; Misha laughed softly, shifting closer until her cheek rested directly against his bare chest.</p>

<p>&quot;I already knew she was captivated by you. Anyone with eyes can see the way she looks at you when she thinks nobody else is paying attention.&quot;</p>

<p>’She really enjoys playing this game with me,’ Kane mused, enjoying the relaxed banter after such a long, chaotic day.</p>

<p>’No screaming, no arguments, just pure confidence.’</p>

<p>&quot;Captivated is a strong word,&quot; Kane pointed out, feigning a modest tone.</p>

<p>&quot;She just respects my business practices.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;Please,&quot; Misha scoffed playfully, reaching up to trace the faint scars on his collarbone.</p>

<p>&quot;She acts like a hardened warrior who cares for nothing but gold, yet she risks sneaking into a warlord’s bedroom just to ask if you missed her. The poor woman is hopelessly smitten.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;Can you really blame her?&quot; Kane grinned widely, leaning down to press a soft kiss against her forehead.</p>

<p>&quot;I am a very charming man when I want to be.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;You do possess certain talents,&quot; Misha conceded, her voice dropping into a softer, much more intimate register.</p>

<p>&quot;You know exactly how to ruin a woman’s defenses without even drawing a weapon.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;Oh?&quot; Kane prompted, genuinely curious to hear her elaborate.</p>

<p>&quot;Care to list these dangerous talents of mine?&quot;</p>

<p>Misha shifted her weight, propping herself up on one elbow so she could look directly down into his eyes. Her hair cascaded over her shoulders beautifully.</p>

<p>&quot;Well, for starters,&quot; Misha began, a mischievous smile playing on her lips, &quot;you kiss remarkably well. It is a genuine risk for anyone with a weak heart.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;Is that a medical opinion?&quot; Kane chuckled, moving his hand up to cup the side of her face.</p>

<p>&quot;Because I never received any complaints about my methods before.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;It is a factual observation,&quot; Misha countered smoothly, leaning into his touch while keeping her gaze locked on his.</p>

<p>&quot;You kiss like you are trying to consume a person’s soul, Kane. It leaves a girl completely breathless and entirely unable to formulate a coherent thought for at least an hour afterward.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;If it truly poses such a risk to a weak heart,&quot; Kane whispered, his voice dropping an octave as he pulled her slightly closer, &quot;then perhaps I should stop kissing you altogether. For your own safety, of course.&quot;</p>

<p>Misha gasped in mock horror, lightly slapping his chest.</p>

<p>&quot;Do not even joke about such cruel punishments! I am an oracle, so my heart is perfectly strong enough to handle your affection.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;Are you certain about that?&quot; Kane challenged playfully, brushing his thumb across her lower lip.</p>

<p>&quot;Because I seem to recall you melting into a puddle the last time I backed you against the sanctuary.&quot;</p>

<p>A faint blush crept across Misha’s cheeks, though she refused to break eye contact.</p>

<p>&quot;That was a surprise attack,&quot; Misha defended herself quickly, though a quiet laugh betrayed her composure.</p>

<p>&quot;You caught me off guard while I was trying to alert you.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;A good warlord always strikes when the enemy is distracted.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;I am not your enemy,&quot; Misha whispered, her smile fading into an expression of deep, genuine love.</p>

<p>&quot;And you do not need to use tactics to win me over, Kane. You already conquered me completely.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;I didn’t conquer. I love you, just like those women out there. I won’t make this only about me. If Seraphina bothers you, tell me.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;I know.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;Show me,&quot; Kane murmured, tilting his head up toward her.</p>

<p>Misha didn’t hesitate.</p>

<p>Lowering her head, she captured his lips in a slow, tender kiss that spoke volumes without requiring a single word.</p>

<p>She tasted like mint tea and magic, grounding him instantly after hours of dealing with mythical beasts and complex war strategies.</p>

<p>Sliding his fingers into her silver hair, Kane deepened the kiss naturally, savoring the quiet intimacy of the moment.</p>

<p>He let his worries about the Elven borders and looming battles fade into the background, focusing entirely on the warmth of his wife resting securely in his arms.</p>

<p>Pulling back just far enough to catch her breath, Misha rested her forehead against his, her eyes fluttering open slowly.</p>

<p>&quot;See?&quot; Misha whispered, a triumphant little smile returning to her face.</p>

<p>&quot;My heart is still beating perfectly fine.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;Give it a few more minutes,&quot; Kane countered smoothly, shifting his weight to roll her gently onto her back, careful to keep his injured arm out of the way.</p>

<p>&quot;I was just warming up.&quot;</p>
