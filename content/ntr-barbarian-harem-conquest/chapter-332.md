<p>&quot;Do it. Kane, brace stays on for the night unless the system says otherwise. Full clearance confirmation tomorrow. Everyone else stays on departure prep. <p>The meeting broke the way their meetings usually did.</p>

<p>Misha already assigning the next written tasks, Thora checking the compact axe’s edge as if the forest might walk into the yard before morning, Firlia leaving with the outer-watch note, Kessa muttering about painted fools, Griselda remaining at the glass a moment longer before following.</p>

<p>Dinner was quieter than the morning meal had been.</p>

<p>Conversation stayed practical: boat load order, which elven commanders could keep their mouths shut, whether the residual brace would tolerate sea spray without complaint.</p>

<p>No one reopened the forest report.</p>

<p>&quot;Bath. Then sleep. Clearance check at first light.&quot;</p>

<p>Thora grunted agreement.</p>

<p>The private bath was already hot.</p>
