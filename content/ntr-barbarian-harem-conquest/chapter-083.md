<p>’They are coming,’ Kane smiled in anticipation.</p>

<p>Thora felt the vibration simultaneously through her anklets.</p>

<p>Raising one hand without even glancing his way, she signaled the rest of the ridge.</p>

<p>Everyone stopped breathing.</p>

<p>Creak. Creak.</p>

<p>Marching around the eastern bend, the Menual column finally appeared.</p>

<p>They moved with terrifying discipline.</p>

<p>Maintaining a controlled pace, their flank commanders rode up and down the lines barking sharp orders to keep the dressing tight.</p>

<p>These were not amateur raiders or sloppy border militias.</p>

<p>Menual sent seasoned professionals.</p>

<p>Cavalry took the front, armored infantry stacked up right behind them, and somewhere in the back, twelve wooden siege engines creaked along on their wheeled frames.</p>

<p>Squeezing into the pass, the frontline rode forward.</p>

<p>&quot;System, give me a read on the shiny one,&quot; Kane muttered, squinting at a commander in gold pauldrons.</p>

<p>[Target: Commander Aldric Ravan - Stage: Warbrand: Mid]</p>

<p>[Level: 29]</p>

<p>[Assessment: Experienced cavalry commander, twenty years’ service, three successful border campaigns]</p>

<p>[Weakness: Tactical thinking optimized for open ground. Limited experience in confined terrain engagement.]</p>

<p>’He’s good in an open field,’ Kane concluded, watching the man bark orders.</p>

<p>’But put him in a box, and he dies just like anyone else.’</p>

<p>Pushing deeper into the rocky bottleneck, the front riders suddenly hit the invisible World Tree node.</p>

<p>Everything stopped.</p>

<p>It didn’t happen all at once.</p>

<p>The leading six warhorses refused first, rearing up and screaming in sudden panic.</p>

<p>&quot;Wha-what’s happening?&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;Hold your horses!&quot;</p>

<p>Then the next four slammed into them.</p>

<p>A catastrophic ripple effect cascaded backward through the tightly packed column as beasts refused to step forward and cursing riders spurred them on.</p>

<p>&quot;Move!&quot;</p>

<p>The formation folded into itself, crushing legs and snapping bones as animals panicked.</p>

<p>&quot;Hold the line! Control your mounts!&quot; Commander Ravan bellowed, riding forward to assess the sudden disruption.</p>

<p>Standing high in his stirrups, he scanned the terrified horses, then glared at the steep ridge walls boxing them in.</p>

<p>Looking straight up, Ravan froze.</p>

<p>Standing up on the cliff edge, Kane raised Mjoldr high into the air.</p>

<p>A crimson aura erupted from the axe head.</p>

<p>It was deliberate.</p>

<p>To make them aware of incoming slaughter.</p>

<p>Across the yawning gap, Seraphine’s white sword flared in immediate answer.</p>

<p>&quot;Now!&quot; Sira yelled.</p>

<p>Forty composite bows released at once.</p>

<p>Firing from an elevated angle eliminated the value of any upward-oriented shields.</p>

<p>The sound was horribly specific, the crunching impact of forty simultaneous strikes tearing through iron plates and soft flesh.</p>

<p>&quot;Agh!&quot;</p>

<p>Men screamed. Horses shrieked. Blood sprayed wildly across the canyon walls.</p>

<p>Chaos consumed the channel instantly.</p>

<p>&quot;Flanks!&quot; Ravan’s voice tried to cut through the slaughter.</p>

<p>&quot;Deploy left and right—&quot;</p>

<p>’There’re no flanks, you idiot,’ Kane laughed internally.</p>

<p>Vertical walls trapped them perfectly.</p>

<p>Eight hundred elite cavalrymen squeezed into a contained gorge couldn’t perform the one maneuver cavalry actually existed to perform.</p>

<p>Refusing to wait on the ridge, Kane simply stepped off the edge.</p>

<p>Falling fifteen meters down the cliff face, he used protruding rocks as momentary brake points, tearing his boots up before hitting the valley floor at a dead sprint.</p>

<p>[Berserker Rage Lv2 - Bloodlust activated]</p>

<p>Transformation hit him instantly.</p>

<p>Cracked ribs stopped mattering.</p>

<p>A compromised arm suddenly felt fine.</p>

<p>His entire movement pattern shifted from calculated tactics to savagery.</p>

<p>The Menual soldiers staring at him had never seen a single man charge a hundred-man formation with such terrifying joy.</p>

<p>&quot;Kill that barbarian!&quot;</p>

<p>Swoosh.</p>

<p>Slicing through the nearest rider’s leveled lance, Mjoldr severed the shaft just inches from the iron head.</p>

<p>Continuing the spin, Kane buried the crimson blade directly into the rider’s neck.</p>

<p>Slash.</p>

<p>Hot blood geysered upward, painting Kane’s face as he ripped the weapon free, nearly decapitating the man in the process.</p>

<p>&quot;Get him!&quot; someone screamed, pointing a trembling spear.</p>

<p>Flicking his wrist, Kane hurled his axe at the third rider trying to charge.</p>

<p>He didn’t aim for the man.</p>

<p>Catching the horse in the jaw with the flat of the blade, the crunch of bone echoed loudly.</p>

<p>&quot;Neeee-igh!&quot;</p>

<p>The beast shied wildly sideways, its jaw completely shattered.</p>

<p>Inside a packed column, that lateral movement forced neighboring horses to crash together, tossing riders under trampling hooves.</p>

<p>Bones snapped like dry twigs under the stampede.</p>

<p>Catching Mjoldr on the return flight, Kane grinned.</p>

<p>&quot;Who wants to go next?&quot; Kane roared, driving the blade deep into the stone wall at shoulder height.</p>

<p>Using the connecting chain, he vaulted himself right over a wall of spears, his steel-toed boots caving in two different helmets simultaneously as he passed over.</p>

<p>Brains and bone fragments splattered across the stone.</p>

<p>Landing smoothly amidst the gore, he kept moving.</p>

<p>Up above, Sira coordinated the relentless archery volley.</p>

<p>&quot;Shift your fire! Drop the back lines!&quot;</p>

<p>Seraphine’s sixty warriors worked the opposite side with terrifying, divine-designated precision.</p>

<p>Arrows pinned men to the ground, skewering throats and piercing eyes.</p>

<p>Trying desperately to salvage the situation, Ravan barked fresh orders.</p>

<p>&quot;Infantry! Push forward! Widen the damn lane!&quot;</p>

<p>He correctly realized his cavalry was useless here, hoping armored foot soldiers could carve out some fighting room.</p>

<p>Slaughtering his way through the horses, Kane headed straight for Ravan.</p>

<p>He didn’t move in a straight line, instead carving a zigzagging path of severed limbs and spilling intestines, using every screaming soldier as a stepping stone to his real target.</p>

<p>[System: Conqueror’s Presence active - Allied morale +15%, Enemy efficiency -15%]</p>

<p>[Primal Resonance: Mjoldr attack speed +22% while Berserker Rage active]</p>

<p>[Soul Brand: Available - 1 application remaining before cooldown]</p>

<p>Ravan finally spotted the red-haired demon approaching.</p>

<p>’What is he?’</p>

<p>To the veteran commander’s credit, he did not flee.</p>

<p>Drawing a longsword, he spurred his horse forward to meet the threat head-on.</p>

<p>His horse hit the invisible node line and stopped dead.</p>

<p>&quot;Hah?&quot;</p>

<p>Recovering from the sudden halt in under two seconds, Ravan vaulted expertly from the saddle.</p>

<p>Landing cleanly on both feet, he brought his sword up, ready to strike.</p>

<p>&quot;I will mount your head on my saddle!&quot; Ravan spat, charging forward.</p>

<p>’You’re good,’ Kane acknowledged, blocking a vicious overhead strike.</p>

<p>’But I’m so much better.’</p>

<p>Three lightning-fast exchanges followed.</p>

<p>Ravan possessed technically flawless sword work, and his physical size offered a real challenge.</p>

<p>Sparks flew as steel clashed against crimson energy.</p>

<p>&quot;Die, savage!&quot; Ravan grunted, stepping into a sweeping horizontal cut.</p>
