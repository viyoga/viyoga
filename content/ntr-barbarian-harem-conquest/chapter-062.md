<p>&quot;One thousand and one,&quot; Kane whispered.</p>

<p>&quot;One thousand and two.&quot;</p>

<p>Silence filled the inn room around him.</p>

<p>Thora slept deeply. Sira curled on her side, facing the wall, keeping her bow resting against the bedframe within an arm’s reach.</p>

<p>Kessa slept on a woven mat nearby, one pointed ear twitching occasionally as her beastwoman instincts cataloged every faint sound in the building.</p>

<p>Sitting in a chair by the window, Grieselda remained perfectly still and completely awake, watching him count.</p>

<p>&quot;One thousand and three.&quot;</p>

<p>Finishing at twelve hundred, Kane finally stood up and wiped his face with a rough cloth.</p>

<p>Opening his status screen quietly, he reviewed the numbers.</p>

<p>[Name: Kane]</p>

<p>[Stage: Warbrand — Peak]</p>

<p>[Level: 35]</p>

<p>[Strength: 214]</p>

<p>[Vitality: 179]</p>

<p>[Agility: 94]</p>

<p>[Charisma: 148]</p>

<p>[Dominance: 64]</p>

<p>[Gold Coins: 15,247]</p>

<p>[Weapon Bond: Mjoldr — Soul Brand active]</p>

<p>Rising from her chair without a single sound, Grieselda crossed the room to where Mjoldr leaned against the wall.</p>

<p>She didn’t ask for permission. Grabbing the crimson handle, she caused the blade’s red aura to flare briefly at her touch, as if recognizing an old friend.</p>

<p>Carrying the weapon to the window where the light poured in, she began a silent process that took ten minutes.</p>

<p>Kane watched all of it without speaking a word.</p>

<p>Her pale hands moved over the blade in slow patterns. Instead of touching the steel directly, her palms hovered a half-inch above it.</p>

<p>The intricate mark resting between her breasts pulsed in perfect sync with whatever ritual she performed.</p>

<p>It felt like the subtle difference between a fire that merely warms and a fire that burns.</p>

<p>Finishing her work, she held the axe out to him, handle first.</p>

<p>Taking it carefully, Kane felt the difference immediately.</p>

<p>The weapon felt less like a forged tool and more like a living extension of his own arm.</p>

<p>&quot;What did you do?&quot; Kane asked quietly.</p>

<p>Pressing her palm flat over her own chest, she then reached out and pressed it over his heart.</p>

<p>Finally, she pointed a slender finger at the axe.</p>

<p>’The three of us are the same thing now,’ Kane translated mentally, or at least he understood something very close to that meaning.</p>

<p>Looking down at the blade, he noticed the Soul Brand property was still active.</p>

<p>But resting underneath it was something new.</p>

<p>’I will figure out exactly what it is when I bury it in a royal chest,’ Kane thought, strapping Mjoldr securely to his back.</p>

<p>Stirring awake on the bed, Thora watched him for a moment without sitting up.</p>

<p>&quot;How do you feel?&quot; she asked softly.</p>

<p>&quot;Clear,&quot; Kane replied.</p>

<p>It was the most honest answer he could give.</p>

<p>There was no lingering anxiety and no performed arrogance today.</p>

<p>Sitting up, Thora brushed her sleep-tangled hair out of her face. Her eyes looked soft, but her expression carried that stubborn determination she always got when she decided on a course of action.</p>

<p>&quot;Come here,&quot; she commanded gently.</p>

<p>Sitting on the edge of the mattress, Kane let her move behind him.</p>

<p>She placed her warm hands firmly on his shoulders. It wasn’t a soothing massage; she was just making sure he felt her presence right against his back.</p>

<p>&quot;Whatever happens today,&quot; Thora began.</p>

<p>&quot;I know,&quot; Kane interrupted softly.</p>

<p>&quot;Let me finish,&quot; she scolded with a faint smile.</p>

<p>Waiting patiently, he let her find the words.</p>

<p>&quot;Whatever happens today... I was there from the very beginning,&quot; Thora whispered near his ear.</p>

<p>&quot;The Bloodfang camp, the forest, the worms, Greenhaven, the merchant road, Sylvandar. All of it. Whatever today makes you, I was a part of making it.&quot;</p>

<p>Kane stayed quiet for a long moment, letting her words settle.</p>

<p>&quot;You were first,&quot; Kane said, his voice laced with deep affection.</p>

<p>&quot;I know,&quot; she agreed.</p>

<p>&quot;I just needed to say my part.&quot;</p>

<p>Kissing the back of his neck once, she finally moved away to get dressed for the arena.</p>

<p>&quot;Chief. There are people outside.&quot; Brak called from the outside.</p>

<p>Walking over to the window, Kane looked down at the streets of the Honored Guest District.</p>

<p>An hour ago, the avenues were empty. Now, they were overflowing with bodies. The entire capital city seemingly decided simultaneously to move toward the arena.</p>

<p>Every single race and faction marched together.</p>

<p>Elven citizens who had never watched a barbarian fight walked beside beastpeople who traveled miles just for this specific match. Human mercenaries and traveling traders joined the massive herd.</p>

<p>Spotting a cluster of familiar faces, Kane recognized Dragonblood clan members marching proudly.</p>

<p>Rutheus must have sent word to his people.</p>

<p>This semifinal was originally announced as a mere administrative formality. The prince won his quarterfinal, and Kane won his.</p>

<p>But what was happening in the street told a completely different story.</p>

<p>There were no semi-finals.</p>

<p>The city understood that today was a brutal question the empire had been avoiding for five hundred years, and the bloody answer would be visible to everyone present.</p>

<p>’Unmapped territory,’ Kane thought, watching the endless crowd.</p>

<p>Turning away from the window, he cracked his knuckles.</p>

<p>&quot;Let’s go.&quot;</p>

<p>Seventy thousand people filled the Celestial Aurora arena to maximum capacity.</p>

<p>Noise hit Kane like a physical wall before he even reached the tunnel entrance.</p>

<p>Multiple languages and multiple registers blended into the roar of a crowd that didn’t share a common rooting interest, but shared a deep understanding that history was about to break.</p>

<p>Walking through the competitors’ corridor alone, Kane passed eliminated fighters standing along the stone walls.</p>

<p>Some offered respectful nods as he walked by. Standing near the end of the corridor with his arms crossed, the dwarf from the market watched Kane approach. T</p>

<p>he old smith studied Mjoldr with the intense professional interest of a man desperate to see what the weapon would become.</p>

<p>Stepping out of the tunnel and into the bright sunlight, seventy thousand people screamed simultaneously.</p>

<p>&quot;Bring down the elves!&quot;</p>

<p>Win, barbarian! Break the silver crowns!&quot;</p>

<p>They were cheering for the violent moment about to unfold.</p>

<p>Walking straight to the center of the sand, Kane stopped and looked up at the seats.</p>

<p>Elven nobles sat stiffly in their designated luxury areas, while general admission sections packed everyone else together.</p>

<p>Over in the competitors’ gallery, Rutheus stood tall with his left hand resting on the railing and his bound right arm hanging at his side.</p>

<p>Thora and Sira found seats right behind the giant. Preferring to stand, Kessa kept her eyes scanning the crowd for threats purely out of feral instinct.</p>

<p>Kane could see Lyssel standing in the crowd, her face in the shade of pink.</p>

<p>She bought her father to watch the match.</p>

<p>Good.</p>

<p>Up in the royal box, the queen sat without her usual privacy screen.</p>

<p>She was present and visible in a way she hadn’t been during the entire tournament.</p>

<p>Looking across the vast arena, Kane met her gaze.</p>

<p>She looked back.</p>

<p>Neither of them nodded or offered any polite acknowledgment.</p>

<p>Today, an immortal who has forgotten fear will remember what it is.</p>
