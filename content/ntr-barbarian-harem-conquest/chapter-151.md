<p>Staring up through the biting wind, Kane suddenly realized the shifting shadow near his boots wasn’t a head at all. It was merely a single scale resting on the beast’s snout.</p>

<p>Looming within the freezing mist, the true scale of the Serpent defied logic completely.</p>

<p>Coiling endlessly upward, the creature spanned the entire width of the pass, blocking out the gray sky.</p>

<p>’That is not a boss,’ Kane calculated instantly, relaxing his grip on Mjoldr.</p>

<p>&quot;Fuck.&quot;</p>

<p>’That is a natural disaster with fangs. Swinging for the neck is suicide. I just need a single drop, not a trophy.’</p>

<p>Fools died trying to slay legends for glory. Kane just needed an ingredient. Dropping his ego entirely, he changed tactics.</p>

<p>Instead of ringing the brass relic right away, he collapsed forward, burying his face in the frozen drifts.</p>

<p>Playing dead required absolute stillness. Forcing his lungs to shallow out, he slowed his heartbeat, projecting the perfect image of a defeated, freezing traveler.</p>

<p>’You are a predator,’ Kane mused, listening to the subtle shifting of ice above him.</p>

<p>’You don’t rush a frozen meal. You inspect it. You test the waters. You make entirely sure it won’t bite back before you swallow.’</p>

<p>Curiosity easily overcame the ancient creature.</p>

<p>Slithering downward, the Serpent lowered its gargantuan head to inspect the sudden offering.</p>

<p>Frigid breath washed over Kane’s back, frosting his eyelashes instantly and numbing his exposed cheeks.</p>

<p>Every human instinct screamed at him to run, to swing his axe, to do anything but lie still, but his discipline kept him perfectly frozen in the snow.</p>

<p>’Come closer,’ Kane urged silently, feeling the air pressure change dramatically as the jaws opened right above his spine.</p>

<p>’Just a little closer. Open wide, you overgrown lizard. Think I am just another foolish poacher looking for crystals.’</p>

<p>Waiting until the maw parted enough to swallow him in a single bite, Kane finally snapped his left wrist.</p>

<p>Ding.</p>

<p>Ringing the brass relic triggered an immediate, blinding reaction. A shockwave of golden light pulsed outward, shattering the divine camouflage instantly.</p>

<p>The suffocating fog burned away in a flash, leaving the Serpent’s true, terrifying form completely visible for exactly ten seconds.</p>

<p>Gazing up from the snow, Kane scanned the beast rapidly.</p>

<p>Pale, jagged battle scars ran along its jawline, proving this creature had spent centuries fighting off rival myths and other apex predators.</p>

<p>’Found the weak spot,’ Kane grinned, spotting a cluster of chipped scales near the left hinge of its enormous mouth.</p>

<p>’Somebody already cracked the armor for me a few hundred years ago. I just need to open the scab.’</p>

<p>Skipping the killing blow, Kane activated [Berserker’s Rage]. Raw adrenaline flooded his veins in a hot rush, completely erasing the biting cold.</p>

<p>Vaulting straight toward the icy wall to his right, he used the narrow chokepoint to his advantage.</p>

<p>Planting his boots against the slick surface, he performed a rapid wall-jump, launching himself directly at the creature’s exposed jaw like a launched arrow.</p>

<p>&quot;Pardon the intrusion!&quot; Kane shouted, finding his voice amidst the roaring wind.</p>

<p>Aiming Mjoldr perfectly, he struck the pre-existing scar. Steel bit deep into the compromised flesh. Compared to the beast’s overall size, the strike amounted to little more than a papercut, but it worked flawlessly.</p>

<p>A bright spray of glowing, azure blood erupted from the shallow wound, shimmering like liquid starlight in the gray morning.</p>

<p>Reaching out mid-air, Kane swiped an empty glass vial directly through the glowing mist.</p>

<p>Catching a generous splash of the azure fluid, he jammed the cork down with his thumb before gravity took over and pulled him back toward the ground.</p>

<p>Pain triggered pure, unfiltered rage. Hissing loud enough to splinter the surrounding glaciers, the Serpent recoiled sharply, tossing its head back into the sky.</p>

<p>’Objective secured,’ Kane thought, hitting the snowpack shoulder-first.</p>

<p>Rolling backward to absorb the kinetic energy, he landed smoothly on his feet, holding the glowing vial high.</p>

<p>’Time to leave the restaurant before the chef gets really angry.’</p>

<p>&quot;Got your ticket home, angel,&quot; Kane whispered to himself, feeling the warmth of the vial through his leather gloves.</p>

<p>’Griselda is going to owe me a lot more than just perimeter duty for this one,’ Kane laughed internally, securing the prize inside his tunic.</p>

<p>’And Misha... well, Misha gets to say I told you so when she sees how close this thing actually came to biting my head off.’</p>

<p>Failing to secure its meal and nursing a fresh wound, the beast opened its maw entirely.</p>

<p>A torrent of freezing azure venom poured downward, aiming to flood the entire narrow pass and turn the trespassing warlord into a permanent ice statue.</p>

<p>Pain triggered pure rage. Hissing loud enough to splinter glaciers, the Serpent recoiled, sending a shockwave that blasted Kane mid-air.</p>

<p>’Shit!’</p>

<p>Fingers going completely numb, the vial of azure blood slipped straight from his grip. It vanished entirely into the freezing drifts below.</p>

<p>’All that risk for nothing,’ Kane cursed mentally, hitting the snowpack hard.</p>

<p>’Guess I have to do it again.’</p>

<p>Before Kane could even find his footing, the beast lunged. Twisting his torso, he barely dodged the main bite, but a jagged scale clipped his side.</p>

<p>It tore right through his tunic, leaving a burning, bleeding scratch across his ribs.</p>

<p>&quot;Too slow,&quot; Kane grunted, raising Mjoldr.</p>

<p>But the Serpent didn’t pause. Blurring past his guard, cavernous jaws clamped directly onto his right arm.</p>

<p>Frost-coated fangs pierced his muscle, grinding ruthlessly against the bone.</p>

<p>’Can’t lose the arm. Need that to swing.’</p>

<p>&quot;Let go!&quot; Kane roared, punching the creature’s snout with his free hand.</p>

<p>Instead of biting clean through, the predator whipped its head sideways. Momentum ripped Kane completely off his feet.</p>

<p>Tossed like a broken ragdoll, he sailed through the freezing mist, flying over the icy ridge.’</p>

<p>Whoosh.</p>

<p>Crashing brutally, Kane slammed into solid rock. He tumbled across an uneven floor, finally skidding to a halt inside a forgotten, carved cave hidden in the mountain’s flank.</p>

<p>Coughing up copper-tasting spit, Kane clutched his mangled arm.</p>

<p>Blood pooled quickly on the ancient stone beneath him. His side burned, and his right arm hung practically useless.</p>

<p>’Okay,’ Kane wheezed, staring out the cave opening as twin glowing yellow eyes searched the blizzard for his body.</p>

<p>’That went terribly. I really need to stop improvising.’</p>
