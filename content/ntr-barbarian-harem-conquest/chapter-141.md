<p>Kane dismissed the screens with a quick thought, staring down at his bruised and punctured left arm.</p>

<p>Two overgrown swamp rats managed to do this much damage in under five minutes.</p>

<p>’If I am going to fight the Mountain Serpent next month,’ Kane plotted, looking toward the northern horizon where Fog Mountain loomed in the distance, ’I have a hell of a lot of grinding to do before then.’</p>

<p>&quot;Let’s head back,&quot; Kane announced, whistling for his stallion.</p>

<p>&quot;We have a saintess arriving tonight, and I need a fresh tunic before we start talking divine politics.&quot;</p>

<p>Riding through the gates of the capital felt entirely different.</p>

<p>Southern recruits paused their weapon drills in the courtyard, staring wide-eyed as Kane casually tossed the severed river behemoth head onto the cobblestones.</p>

<p>It landed with a wet thud, rolling to a stop near the barracks.</p>

<p>&quot;Dinner,&quot; Kane called out to a stunned quartermaster.</p>

<p>&quot;Boil it down. Share it with the men.&quot;</p>

<p>Misha shook her head and guided him upstairs before anyone could ask questions.</p>

<p>Inside his private quarters, she pushed him onto a stool. Pulling a glowing green jar from his pouch, she unscrewed the lid.</p>

<p>&quot;You really lack basic self-preservation,&quot; Misha scolded gently, scooping out the system-bought healing salve.</p>

<p>&quot;Worked out fine in the end,&quot; Kane chuckled, wincing as her cool fingers rubbed the paste into his mangled bicep.</p>

<p>Muscle fibers immediately began knitting back together.</p>

<p>A soothing warmth replaced the biting pain of the monster’s teeth.</p>

<p>&quot;I am just saying, husband, using your own limb as bait is a poor strategy,&quot; she murmured, leaning close to blow a breath of cool air over the fresh pink skin.</p>

<p>’She actually cares,’ Kane realized, feeling a strange warmth settle deep in his chest.</p>

<p>’Back in my old life, taking damage just meant resetting the game alone in a quiet room. Now I have a wife blowing on my bruises and worrying about my arms. Not a bad trade at all.’</p>

<p>&quot;I will try to use Thora’s arm next time,&quot; Kane joked, wrapping his good arm around her waist.</p>

<p>Misha smiled, pressing a kiss against his uninjured shoulder.</p>

<p>&quot;Much better. Keep your own limbs attached, please.&quot;</p>

<p>Opening his interface, Kane reviewed his new status while Misha cleaned up the supplies.</p>

<p>[Level 142 Reached]</p>

<p>[Stat Points Available: 15]</p>

<p>’That snake is fast and hits like a falling building. Need to match that.’</p>

<p>Allocating ten points into Raw Strength and five into Agility, a sudden rush of energy zipped through his veins.</p>

<p>His grip felt firmer. His reflexes hummed with new life.</p>

<p>Dusk settled over the capital a few hours later.</p>

<p>Firlia knocked on his door, her pointed ears twitching with irritation.</p>

<p>&quot;They are here,&quot; Firlia announced, her tone completely flat.</p>

<p>&quot;And they are quite annoying.&quot;</p>

<p>Walking down to the grand hall, Kane found out exactly what she meant.</p>

<p>A procession of men clad in pristine white armor stood proudly in the center of the room.</p>

<p>They kept their noses raised, whispering snide comments about the wooden architecture and the beastwomen standing guard by the doors.</p>

<p>In the middle of this pompous circle stood a woman draped in flowing silver veils—the Saintess.</p>

<p>&quot;Barbarian,&quot; a tall paladin sneered, stepping forward to block Kane’s path to the guest of honor.</p>

<p>&quot;You stand in the presence of the Fog Mountain Saintess. Cleanse your mind and prepare your soul.&quot;</p>

<p>Kane stopped a few feet away, raising an eyebrow.</p>

<p>&quot;I am ready whenever she is. Hello, Saintess. Thanks for making the trip.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;Silence!&quot; the lead paladin barked, pulling his sword halfway from its scabbard.</p>

<p>&quot;You do not address her directly! You will drop to your knees, bow your head to the floor, and beg for her divine blessing before any words are exchanged!&quot;</p>

<p>Kane stared at the man for a long moment.</p>

<p>’Is this guy serious?’ Kane wondered, genuinely baffled by the sheer audacity of the demand.</p>

<p>’You walk into a warlord’s house, surrounded by my army, and demand I kiss the floor? These holy knights really live in their own bubble.’</p>

<p>&quot;I don’t really do the kneeling thing,&quot; Kane replied politely, crossing his arms.</p>

<p>&quot;You will kneel, savage, or we will force you down!&quot; The paladin signaled his men.</p>

<p>Four other armored knights stepped forward, hands resting on their sword pommels, trying to look intimidating.</p>

<p>&quot;Alright,&quot; Kane sighed, shaking his head.</p>

<p>Instead of drawing Mjoldr, Kane simply tapped into his newly upgraded stats.</p>

<p>He activated a tiny fraction of his aura, directing the pressure straight forward without taking a single step.</p>

<p>Air twisted around him.</p>

<p>A suffocating, crushing weight slammed down onto the paladins.</p>

<p>Crack.</p>

<p>The lead knight collapsed instantly.</p>

<p>His kneecaps smashed into the wooden floorboards so hard the timber splintered underneath him. His men followed a second later, gasping for breath as they were pinned to the ground by an invisible burden.</p>

<p>Sweat poured down their arrogant faces as they struggled to even lift their heads.</p>

<p>&quot;You were saying?&quot; Kane asked, stepping casually over the lead paladin’s twitching body.</p>

<p>Reaching the center of the circle, he offered a hand to the veiled woman.</p>

<p>A musical laugh echoed from beneath the silver fabric. The Saintess took his hand gracefully, completely ignoring her groveling guards.</p>

<p>&quot;The Northern Empress warned me about your temper,&quot; she said, her voice bright and entirely unbothered.</p>

<p>&quot;She said you were practical and did not suffer fools.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;I try to be,&quot; Kane smiled, gesturing toward a nearby table.</p>

<p>&quot;Have a seat. We can leave them on the floor. It builds character.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;Agreed,&quot; she hummed, taking a chair.</p>

<p>Misha walked in a moment later, nodding respectfully to the guest before taking a seat beside Kane.</p>

<p>She poured them all a cup of warm tea, acting as if there weren’t five armored men wheezing on the floor behind them.</p>

<p>&quot;So, Fog Mountain,&quot; Kane started, taking a sip from his cup.</p>

<p>&quot;I plan on paying your deity’s familiar a visit next month.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;A foolish errand,&quot; the Saintess warned gently.</p>

<p>&quot;But if Misha’s mother sent me, I assume you cannot be talked out of it.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;Not a chance.&quot;</p>
