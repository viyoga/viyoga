<p>Kane leaned one shoulder against the stone wall, arms loosely crossed.</p>

<p>Seraphine stood a pace away, silver hair bound high, the short training leather marked with the same dust that covered every other woman who had finished the first day of ranking.</p>

<p>&quot;Do you know how influential she is?&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;I don’t mind. But Milfheim warriors tend to be tight. Like you.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;We met while we were fighting. I’m a first blade. Duty comes first.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;I know,&quot; Kane said. &quot;That’s why taking you slowly made it worth it.&quot;</p>

<p>The laughter faded, but the faint curve of her mouth remained.</p>

<p>&quot;What about the beastmen?&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;We go next week.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;He won’t be easy.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;I don’t expect him to be.&quot; Kane shifted his weight against the wall.</p>

<p>&quot;Anything you know of him?&quot;</p>

<p>Seraphine was quiet for a moment, organizing what she was willing to give.</p>

<p>In the game, the Beastworld had been a hidden region.</p>

<p>Kane had only ever met the few who wandered out of it or were dragged into the larger war.</p>

<p>The deeper maps, the true power structure, the grounds- those had stayed locked behind walls of fog and restricted quests.</p>

<p>He had never walked those forests in person. He had only read the aftermath reports.</p>

<p>Seraphine spoke carefully.</p>

<p>&quot;Grathak has breeding grounds where he kidnaps elves and humans. He keeps them in the deep forest, under the watch of a mythical creature that acts as guardian. He takes what he wants and breeds what he keeps.&quot;</p>

<p>Kane listened without interruption.</p>

<p>When she finished, he asked the only question that mattered.</p>

<p>&quot;You’ve seen me fighting. Would I lose?&quot;</p>

<p>Seraphine did not answer immediately. She looked at him, really looked, measuring the red-haired man who had walked into an empire of women, ranked their warriors in a single morning, and turned down the rising star of their cavalry without raising his voice.</p>

<p>She knew how he killed Morvak inside his empire.</p>

<p>Her silver eyes moved over the line of his shoulders, the easy way he carried the hammer, the absolute lack of doubt in his posture.</p>

<p>&quot;50/50.&quot;</p>

<p>Kane smiled.</p>

<p>The expression was slow and sharp, the same one he wore when a fight turned interesting.</p>

<p>&quot;Good enough.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;You hear a fifty percent chance of dying and call it good enough. That’s why Thora stays close, and Misha watches you the way she does.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;They know what they signed up for.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;If you take her, do it after the ranking ends and do it on your terms. Not hers. She will try to turn the night into leverage. She already tried today.&quot;</p>

<p>Kane pushed off the wall.</p>

<p>&quot;I know. That is why the ranking still has two days.&quot;</p>
