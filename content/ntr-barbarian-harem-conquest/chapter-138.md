<p>&quot;Both of you. Drink it all right now, and stay back near the horses.&quot;</p>

<p>Nadia caught the glass clumsily, staring at the glowing liquid with blatant suspicion.</p>

<p>&quot;What is it?&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;A defence potion,&quot; Kane answered, already unhooking Mjoldr from his back.</p>

<p>&quot;It will keep your bones from snapping if that thing decides to spit a rock at you. Down the hatch, rebel. I don’t have time to play nurse if you get careless.&quot;</p>

<p>Trusting his wife’s serene nod, Kane watched Misha uncork her vial and drink the silver liquid gracefully.</p>

<p>Seeing the delicate oracle consume it without fear, Nadia finally pulled her stopper and chugged the elixir.</p>

<p>She gagged slightly at the bitter, chalky taste, but her skin immediately took on a faint, metallic sheen before fading back to normal.</p>

<p>&quot;Alright,&quot; Kane murmured, stepping closer to the muddy shoreline while keeping his boots firmly planted.</p>

<p>&quot;Find it for me, Misha.&quot;</p>

<p>Closing her eyes, Misha took a slow breath.</p>

<p>When her eyelids fluttered open, her pupils had vanished completely, leaving her eyes glowing a pure white.</p>

<p>The seraphic blessing was activated, allowing her to see the invisible currents of magic flowing through the environment.</p>

<p>Silence hung over the stagnant lake. Kane waited, his grip tightening around the haft of his crimson axe.</p>

<p>&quot;There,&quot; Misha whispered, her dual-tone voice carrying an eerie resonance across the shore.</p>

<p>She raised a slender finger, pointing toward a clustered patch of rotting cypress trees near the far eastern edge.</p>

<p>&quot;Deep below the roots. A large, concentrated mass of foul mana is resting.&quot;</p>

<p>Focusing his gaze exactly where she pointed, Kane watched the ink-like surface carefully.</p>

<p>For a long moment, nothing happened.</p>

<p>Then, far in the corner of the reservoir, a single, deliberate ripple broke the unnerving stillness.</p>

<p>Kane did not wait for the creature to make a formal introduction.</p>

<p>&quot;Get back,&quot; Kane ordered, never taking his eyes off the unnatural ripples forming near the centre of the reservoir.</p>

<p>&quot;Take cover behind those cypress roots right now. Do not step out until I say so.&quot;</p>

<p>Nadia hesitated for a fraction of a second, completely unaccustomed to running away from her own territory.</p>

<p>&quot;You cannot fight that alone! You do not even know what you are looking for yet!&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;Watch me,&quot; Kane replied smoothly, a smirk playing on his lips.</p>

<p>&quot;Move, Nadia.&quot;</p>

<p>Grabbing the rebel’s sleeve, Misha pulled her firmly toward the tree line, trusting her husband’s command implicitly.</p>

<p>&quot;Do not argue with him when he draws his weapon. Just step back and watch.&quot;</p>

<p>Drawing Mjoldr from his back, Kane rolled his shoulders to loosen the joints.</p>

<p>The familiar weight of the crimson axe grounded his focus, narrowing the entire world down to the muddy shoreline and the hidden target.</p>

<p>[System Skill Activated: Berserker’s Rage]</p>

<p>[Effect: Physical strength, speed, and pain tolerance increased by 100% for three minutes.]</p>

<p>Crimson energy flared around his forearms, pulsing in time with his racing heartbeat. Power flooded his veins, sharpening his senses.</p>

<p>Pulling his arm back, Kane launched the axe with everything he had.</p>

<p>Spinning rapidly, the weapon tore a path straight through the water, parting the surface through pure momentum.</p>

<p>Hitting something submerged, a dull thud echoed across the shore.</p>

<p>Waiting a brief second, Kane simply raised his right hand.</p>

<p>Mjoldr tore out of the depths, flying backwards until the leather grip slapped perfectly into his palm.</p>

<p>Checking the curved blade, Kane noticed the edge remained completely clean.</p>

<p>Not a single drop of blood stained the metal.</p>

<p>’It escaped the strike,’ Kane analysed, tapping the handle against his thigh.</p>

<p>’Something that large should not possess that kind of agility underwater. I need to force it out of its element. If it stays hidden in the depths, this fight will drag on too long.’</p>

<p>&quot;Is it dead?&quot; Nadia called out from the trees, trying to peer through the gloom.</p>

<p>&quot;Not even close,&quot; Kane answered, keeping his voice steady.</p>

<p>&quot;Stay quiet. It is hunting.&quot;</p>

<p>Staring at the still surface, Kane waited for another sign.</p>

<p>The reservoir remained unnervingly calm, hiding the monster lurking below.</p>

<p>&quot;Husband! To the left!&quot; Misha shouted, her glowing white eyes tracking the invisible currents of mana swirling beneath the lake.</p>

<p>&quot;It is circling your flank! Gathering speed!&quot;</p>

<p>Turning his head sharply, Kane spotted a sudden surge in the water level.</p>

<p>A rushing wave crested rapidly, barreling straight toward his position on the shoreline like a submerged battering ram.</p>

<p>Instead of retreating to safer ground, Kane pushed off the muddy bank.</p>

<p>Vaulting directly toward the incoming wave, he raised his crimson axe high above his head and brought it crashing down right onto the swelling surface.</p>

<p>A fierce red shockwave exploded from the point of impact.</p>

<p>Water blasted outward in all directions, momentarily pushing the lake back and exposing the hidden predator underneath.</p>

<p>Standing right in the centre of the displaced crater, the beast glared up at him.</p>

<p>’Ugly bastard,’ Kane remarked internally, taking in the mutated nightmare.</p>

<p>It possessed the bloated, rounded torso of a hippopotamus, yet its snout elongated into jagged, crocodile-like jaws lined with serrated teeth.</p>

<p>Six glowing yellow eyes blinked erratically along the sides of its scaly skull, tracking Kane’s mid-air descent.</p>

<p>[Target Identified: Mutated River Behemoth]</p>

<p>[Threat Level: 100]</p>

<p>Opening jaws wide enough to swallow a grown stallion, the behemoth lunged upward.</p>

<p>Gravity finally reclaimed the displaced lake, sending tons of crashing water right back over the creature and swallowing the beast from sight once more.</p>

<p>Landing cleanly back on the solid bank, Kane swung Mjoldr horizontally, sending a crescent arc of red energy slicing toward the last known coordinates.</p>

<p>Slipping smoothly beneath the returning wave, the creature evaded the slash entirely.</p>

<p>&quot;Where did it go?!&quot; Nadia screamed, watching the water boil near the edge of the reeds.</p>

<p>Rising abruptly just a few feet away, the creature hacked loudly from its throat.</p>

<p>A stream of vile green sludge shot from its jaws, splashing directly across Kane’s face.</p>

<p>Vision vanished instantly.</p>

<p>Burning pain flared across his eyes, forcing him to squeeze them shut.</p>

<p>’Poison? No, just a chemical irritant meant to blind prey before the kill,’ Kane calculated, refusing to let panic cloud his judgment.</p>

<p>’Wiping it right now will only rub it deeper into my eyes. Keep breathing. Rely on sound.’</p>

<p>&quot;Kane! Your eyes!&quot; Misha yelled, her usual serene composure breaking slightly at the sight of him being blinded.</p>
