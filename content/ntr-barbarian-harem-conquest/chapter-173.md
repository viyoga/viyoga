<p>The fortified central training ring of Menual was cleared of common soldiers so the elite commanders could witness their Warlord prepare for the southern campaign.</p>

<p>Griselda hovered gracefully above the wooden perimeter, her violet hair catching the sunrise while her black-and-gold wings beat a steady rhythm in the draft.</p>

<p>Far from a mindless pet, the fallen angel observed the ring with sharp tactical interest, crossing her arms as she preened a stray feather from her wrist.</p>

<p>&quot;You claimed our teamwork needed refinement yesterday in the armory, Kane,&quot; Thora challenged, stretching her shoulders before spinning a weighted training halberd between her palms.</p>

<p>&quot;Let’s see if your reflexes can actually handle a coordinated assault from your real vanguard.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;Usually, people pay me a small fortune to jump out of shadows and kill targets,&quot; Sira added, balancing on the top railing while flipping two blunt training daggers in a blur of motion.</p>

<p>&quot;Yet here you are, asking me to work for free on a peaceful Tuesday morning.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;I am paying you in survival lessons, Sira,&quot; Kane answered, stripping off his linen coat and tossing it toward the bench where Misha sat watching.</p>

<p>&quot;If any of you can land a solid blow on me before the sun clears the eastern guard tower, I will double your personal bonus from the Sylvander plunder.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;You already doubled it for us.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;Then a night with that person. Only one.&quot;</p>

<p>The courtyard went silent.</p>

<p>&quot;You should not make gambling bets you cannot afford, Kane,&quot; Griselda smirked from her vantage point in the sky, her voice echoing clearly over the grounds.</p>

<p>&quot;My aerial dives are much faster than your little human reflexes, and I have had my eye on that sapphire necklace in the treasury for three weeks.&quot;</p>

<p>’Fighting them individually is manageable, but taking on my vanguard leader, my master attacker, and a fallen angel simultaneously is asking for a disaster,’ Kane calculated, rolling his shoulders to test his joints.</p>

<p>’Still, pushing my physical stats to the limit is the only way to see if my Tier III upgrades can handle high-speed Elven combat without failing me.’</p>

<p>Without another word of warning, the three-on-one spar initiated.</p>

<p>Thora charged straight down the center with crushing velocity, driving the blunt halberd forward in a rapid sequence of thrusts that forced Kane to rely on his upgraded footwork just to pivot around the shaft.</p>

<p>The moment Kane sidestepped Thora’s primary lunge, Sira vanished into his peripheral blind spots, utilizing the dust kicked up by the vanguard’s boots to launch a high-speed strike at his exposed flank.</p>

<p>Up above, Griselda folded her black-and-gold wings and dove like a falling spear, using wind-enhanced speed to cut off his only viable escape route to the left.</p>

<p>&quot;Too slow on the flank, chief!&quot; Sira taunted as her wooden dagger grazed the fabric of his sleeve, only for Kane to trap her wrist mid-motion and use her own momentum to block Thora’s incoming halberd shaft.</p>

<p>&quot;Don’t use my shield, you cheat!&quot; Thora barked a laugh, pulling her blow at the very last second to avoid taking Sira’s head off while Kane ducked beneath the wooden pole.</p>

<p>&quot;Watch the sky, Warlord!&quot; Griselda chimed, dropping right toward his shoulders with her booted heel aimed straight for his chest.</p>

<p>’Griselda coordinates with Thora instinctively despite their constant arguing,’ Kane noted, planting his back foot to brace for the impact.</p>

<p>’If I rely purely on system speed, they will trap me in a rhythm within three moves. I need to break their formation by targeting the pivot point.’</p>

<p>Activating a burst of enhanced strength, Kane reached upward and caught Griselda safely by the ankle just before her boot reached his ribs.</p>

<p>Swoosh.</p>

<p>Using her downward momentum, he swung her weight gently around in a sweeping arc that forced Sira to leap backward out of her shadow-step, breaking their coordinated triangle and leaving all three women scrambling across the turf to regain their footing.</p>

<p>&quot;That’s hardly a fair maneuver, Kane!&quot; Griselda squawked, flapping her wings frantically to stabilize herself in mid-air before landing gracefully on the grass.</p>

<p>&quot;You cannot simply grab an angel by the leg and throw her at a barbarian!&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;In battle, you use whatever projectiles are available,&quot; Kane laughed, offering a teasing grin as Thora reset her halberd stance.</p>

<p>&quot;And you happen to be very aerodynamic, Griselda.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;I am going to bite your ears for that comment,&quot; the fallen angel threatened playfully, hovering back up several feet while her violet hair whipped around her shoulders.</p>

<p>Before Thora could initiate another charge down the center, a booming laugh echoed across the training ground, halting the action instantly.</p>

<p>Brak stepped through the courtyard gate carrying two massive iron tower shields strapped to his forearms, accompanied closely by Kessa.</p>

<p>Kessa stepped forward with effortless grace, her vibrant orange hair tied back securely in a warrior’s braid while her tufted ears twitched sharply at the ringing sound of clashing wood.</p>

<p>Flexing her fingers to test her wooden training claws, she locked her amber eyes onto Kane with intense competitive hunger.</p>

<p>&quot;You are leaving the best fighters out of your little morning game, Commander,&quot; Kessa purred, cracking her knuckles as she stepped over the perimeter ropes.</p>

<p>&quot;Thora swings hard, but she fights like a human. If you want to train for the southern wastes, you need someone who hunts like a beast.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;I brought the shields, Commander,&quot; Brak rumbled happily, slamming the bottom edges of the iron walls into the ground with a resounding thud.</p>

<p>&quot;Though if Kessa gets her claws on you, iron is not going to help much.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;The ring is getting crowded,&quot; Griselda observed, floating down to hover right beside Kane’s shoulder while keeping a wary eye on Kessa’s claws.</p>

<p>&quot;Should we start charging admission, Kane? Firlia would probably love the extra revenue.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;Let them come,&quot; Kane smiled, gesturing for Kessa and Brak to join the formation.</p>

<p>&quot;Let us see if five of you can finally earn that bonus before breakfast.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;You are growing arrogant because you caught one angel,&quot; Kessa warned, dropping into a low, predatory crouch while her orange tail swished rhythmically behind her.</p>

<p>&quot;Brak, form a wall on his left so he cannot pivot around Thora’s reach again.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;Understood, Kessa!&quot; Brak cheered, raising his shields high as he lumbered forward to close off the side of the ring.</p>

<p>’Facing two elite humans and an angel was a good workout, but adding a beastwoman and a living iron wall turns this into a proper battlefield,’ Kane thought, drawing his practice sword and tapping the flat of the blade against his palm.</p>

<p>’If my stats can survive five minutes against this specific combination without taking a hit, the Elven Queen’s mercenaries are going to have a very miserable week.’</p>

<p>&quot;Whenever you are ready, Commander,&quot; Thora called out, her halberd leveled directly at his chest while Sira melted back into the blind spots behind Brak’s shields.</p>

<p>&quot;I have been ready since dawn,&quot; Kane replied, beckoning them forward with a flick of his blade as the morning sun finally crested the eastern guard tower.</p>
