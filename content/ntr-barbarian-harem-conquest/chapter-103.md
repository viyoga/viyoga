<p>Elyra’s robes spread across the floor.</p>

<p>She stayed down, her chest rising and falling in erratic spasms as the fanatic pride in her eyes slowly curdled into pure, unadulterated terror.</p>

<p>Kane didn’t let the silence sit.</p>

<p>Smashing a gladiator’s bones was just standard combat; breaking a high priestess in front of an audience of her peers was permanent political warfare.</p>

<p>Raising his right hand, he snapped his fingers.</p>

<p>The sharp clack acted like a physical slap, jolting the stunned noblewomen out of their collective daze.</p>

<p>&quot;You,&quot; Kane murmured, aiming his index finger at a silver carafe and a matching goblet resting on Lady Vanya’s abandoned viewing table.</p>

<p>&quot;Fetch those.&quot;</p>

<p>Elyra blinked, her mouth opening in a dry, soundless protest.</p>

<p>&quot;Fetch them,&quot; Kane repeated, dropping his voice into a low, rumbled register that vibrated right through the floorboards.</p>

<p>&quot;Fill that cup with spiced wine. And bring it to me on your knees. If a single drop hits the stone, we are going to find out if your Goddess can regrow a jaw.&quot;</p>

<p>Gasps echoed off the vaulted ceiling.</p>

<p>Several older matriarchs visibly clutched the silver chains at their collars, entirely scandalized.</p>

<p>In Milfheim, men served on their knees; men kept their eyes down.</p>

<p>Forcing the spiritual leader of the empire into the posture of a common household breed was an execution of their entire social hierarchy.</p>

<p>Elyra looked up at the throne, desperately seeking salvation.</p>

<p>Sitting like a carved statue, the Empress looked right through her.</p>

<p>The fanatic broke.</p>

<p>After all, it was her own decision to fight him.</p>

<p>Crawling across the floorboards, actually crawling, her fine dress catching on the rough stone beneath the gladiator’s wet blood, Elyra dragged herself to the side table.</p>

<p>Her fingers shook so wildly the silver carafe rattled against the rim of the cup, splashing red liquid over her own pristine sleeves.</p>

<p>She shuffled her knees back across the carpet, stopping right at Kane’s boots.</p>

<p>Lifting the goblet with both trembling palms, she kept her forehead aimed strictly at the carpet.</p>

<p>&quot;Your... your wine, my Lord.&quot;</p>

<p>Taking the silver vessel, Kane purposely let his knuckles drag across the top of her bowed head, patting her once like a well-trained hound.</p>

<p>’Much better,’ Kane smiled internally, taking a slow, leisurely sip.</p>

<p>’A little basic obedience does wonders for the local culture.’</p>

<p>Over the rim of the cup, his eyes locked dead onto the Empress.</p>

<p>He didn’t bow.</p>

<p>He simply drank her holy order’s pride and swallowed it whole.</p>

<p>Before Elyra could even scurry backward into the safety of the crowd, a sudden rustle of white linen caught Kane’s periphery.</p>

<p>Princess Misha stepped down from the elevated dais.</p>

<p>She moved without making a sound, her bare feet gliding over the marble like a drifting phantom.</p>

<p>Two armored paladins instinctively reached out to grab her shoulders, but the Empress shot up from her throne, raising a sharp, flat palm to freeze the guards in place.</p>

<p>The matriarch leaned forward, her platinum crown catching the candlelight.</p>

<p>She wanted to watch this.</p>

<p>Misha drifted straight through the open space, entirely ignoring the imaginary boundaries that separated royalty from mercenaries.</p>

<p>She stopped less than six inches from Kane’s chest.</p>

<p>Up close, her hollow, pupil-less eyes looked like two pools of frozen gray water.</p>

<p>She didn’t offer a curtsy or a syllable.</p>

<p>Reaching up with both remarkably frail hands, Misha took Kane’s right palm, the same hand still warm from snapping her champion’s joint, and pressed it flat against her own sternum.</p>

<p>’She’s cold,’ Kane realized, his breath catching slightly at the stark drop in temperature against his skin.</p>

<p>Beneath the thin white fabric of her gown, her ribs felt fragile, vibrating with a frantic rhythm that felt completely wrong.</p>

<p>It wasn’t the steady thump of a human heart; it felt like a cracked crystal trying to shake itself to splinters inside her chest.</p>

<p>’Her shattered core is actively trying to siphon my conqueror aura just to keep itself glued together,’ Kane deduced, his brain instantly mapping the strange sensation.</p>

<p>’She isn’t looking for a political husband today. The poor girl is looking for a literal life support system.’</p>

<p>A sudden pulse of azure light erupted from the point where his calloused palm met her chest.</p>

<p>The brilliant flare washed over the vaulted ceiling, turning the stained-glass windows a deep, electric blue.</p>

<p>Misha’s back arched slightly, her frail fingers digging into the back of his hand to anchor herself against the surge.</p>

<p>[Divine Core Resonance Detected]</p>

<p>[Target: Princess Misha - Status: Fractured Divinity]</p>

<p>[Passive Siphon Initiated: Your Conqueror’s Presence is temporarily stabilizing target’s mana channels.]</p>

<p>[Betrothal Bond: Formally Sealed]</p>

<p>Staring at the floating text right beside Misha’s pale cheek, Kane blinked twice.</p>

<p>’Wait, that’s it?’ Kane thought, genuine annoyance cutting right through the majestic display of ancient magic.</p>

<p>’Formally sealed? I don’t even get to fuck her first to make it official? I just hold her hand like a polite schoolboy, and suddenly I own a cursed royal?’</p>

<p>Releasing a tiny, paper-thin sigh of pure relief, Misha’s rigid shoulders finally dropped.</p>

<p>For the first time since she entered the chamber, the fog in her gray eyes cleared just enough to focus squarely on Kane’s face.</p>

<p>She didn’t smile, but her thumb softly stroked the side of his knuckle, claiming him in her own silent, desperate fashion.</p>

<p>&quot;The Goddess has clearly spoken,&quot; the Empress announced, her voice ringing out with triumphant finality as she descended the first two steps of the dais.</p>

<p>&quot;The divine resonance is absolute. The military alliance between Milfheim and the Western Reaches is sealed.&quot;</p>

<p>Looking down at the sobbing priestess on the floor, then at the silent girl clutching his fingers, Kane slowly lowered his wine cup.</p>

<p>’Well, this is great.’</p>

<p>&quot;Send the galleons to my border coves by midnight,&quot; Kane told the Empress, his tone leaving zero room for negotiation.</p>

<p>&quot;And pack Misha’s things. My household does not like waiting.&quot;</p>

<p>The meeting ended, and we returned to our rooms.</p>

<p>Iron bolts slid home with a scrape.</p>

<p>The oak door of the Milfheim guest suite secured them inside, cutting off the distant murmurs of the scandalized court.</p>

<p>Seraphine stood by the threshold.</p>

<p>Her silver chest plate dropped to the floorboards with a loud clatter; holy paladin composure was completely gone, replaced by ragged breathing and eyes blown wide enough to swallow her irises.</p>

<p>&quot;You humiliated our holy order,&quot; she whispered, her voice cracking.</p>

<p>&quot;You are a fucking demon.&quot;</p>

<p>Leaning casually against the carved pillar of the bed, Kane unbuttoned the top of his tunic.</p>

<p>&quot;I’m your betrothed’s new master,&quot; he corrected smoothly.</p>

<p>&quot;And you have been speaking rudely for ten minutes straight. Should I punish you?&quot;</p>

<p>No words left her mouth.</p>

<p>No sword was drawn.</p>

<p>Instead, Seraphine closed the distance between them like a predator.</p>

<p>Grabbing the shirt, she slammed him backward against the solid wooden bedpost.</p>
