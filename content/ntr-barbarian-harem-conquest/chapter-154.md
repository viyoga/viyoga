<p>Waking up felt like chewing on broken glass.</p>

<p>Every nerve in Kane’s body screamed in protest as he cracked his eyes open.</p>

<p>A warm fire crackled nearby, chasing away the biting wind howling outside the ruined sanctuary walls.</p>

<p>Glancing down at his right arm, Kane noticed tight bandages wrapped securely around his bicep. His ribs carried the same neat stitching.</p>

<p>&quot;You really know how to take a beating, husband,&quot; Misha murmured, pressing a wet cloth against his forehead.</p>

<p>&quot;Occupational hazard.&quot;</p>

<p>Pushing himself up with a groan, he leaned against the shattered altar.</p>

<p>’I feel like a carriage ran over me twice,’ Kane admitted, testing the flexibility of his shoulder.</p>

<p>’But the stitching holds perfectly. Misha learns fast.’</p>

<p>Looking past his wife, Kane spotted Griselda pacing relentlessly near the temple entrance.</p>

<p>Her long violet hair whipped around her pale shoulders as she walked tight circles. Golden eyes constantly darted between Kane’s resting spot and a small stone table.</p>

<p>Sitting right in the center of that table was the glass vial, glowing with azure Serpent blood.</p>

<p>’She waited for me to wake up instead of just taking it,’ Kane realized, a genuine sense of respect settling in his chest.</p>

<p>&quot;Hey, angel,&quot; Kane called out, his voice rough.</p>

<p>&quot;Stop wearing a hole in the floorboards. Get over here.&quot;</p>

<p>Griselda stopped instantly. Her eyes locked onto him. Striding quickly across the ruined sanctuary, she stopped just short of the firelight. Her pale hands moved in a flurry of rapid, anxious gestures.</p>

<p>[You are awake. The blood is secure. Are we doing this now?]</p>

<p>&quot;Right now,&quot; Kane agreed, gesturing toward the vial.</p>

<p>&quot;No point in waiting. Misha, you ready to play nurse again?&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;My rings are prepared,&quot; Misha answered, stepping up beside him.</p>

<p>Her bands began spinning in a steady, serene rhythm.</p>

<p>&quot;Celestial seals require a delicate touch. If we just pour the mythical blood over the runes, the sudden mana rush might stop her heart.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;Then keep her heart beating,&quot; Kane instructed.</p>

<p>Turning his attention back to the fallen angel, Kane offered a reassuring nod.</p>

<p>&quot;Your turn, Griselda. Let’s see that lock.&quot;</p>

<p>Taking a shaky breath, Griselda reached for the buckles of her leather tunic.</p>

<p>Her fingers trembled slightly, betraying centuries of pent-up anticipation. Undoing the straps, she let the leather fabric slide off her pale shoulders, dropping it to her waist.</p>

<p>Long violet hair cascaded down her bare back, contrasting sharply with her pale, flawless skin. She turned to face him fully, offering no modesty.</p>

<p>Right in the center of her chest, resting beautifully between her big breasts, sat the intricate golden seal.</p>

<p>Divine runes pulsed with a stubborn, restrictive light, digging deep into her flesh to keep her true nature chained.</p>

<p>’That looks painful,’ Kane observed, keeping his gaze professional despite the highly distracting view.</p>

<p>’Gods really love branding their mistakes.’</p>

<p>Picking up the glass vial, Kane popped the cork with his thumb.</p>

<p>&quot;This is going to burn.&quot;</p>

<p>Griselda tilted her chin up proudly, her eyes flashing with fierce determination. She tapped her collarbone twice.</p>

<p>[I have endured centuries of silence. A little pain means nothing. Do it.]</p>

<p>Kane didn’t hesitate. Tilting the vial, he poured the glowing azure blood directly over the golden runes resting between her breasts.</p>

<p>The reaction hit instantaneously.</p>

<p>Mythical blood acted like a solvent against the divine magic. Sizzling steam rose immediately from Griselda’s chest, carrying the faint scent of burning sugar and wild magic.</p>

<p>Griselda’s eyes rolled back.</p>

<p>Falling to her bare knees, she threw her head back in a silent, agonizing scream.</p>

<p>Centuries of locked mana suddenly flooded her mortal veins, expanding rapidly and tearing through her restrictive limits.</p>

<p>Her entire body convulsed, muscles seizing as the celestial power fought to break free.</p>

<p>&quot;Now, Misha!&quot;</p>

<p>Stepping forward quickly, Misha placed a hand directly onto Griselda’s trembling shoulder. White rings expanded, wrapping around the fallen angel’s torso to smooth out the violent mana currents.</p>

<p>&quot;I have her,&quot; Misha promised, her dual-tone voice echoing with authority.</p>

<p>&quot;Breathe, Griselda. Let the currents flow. Do not fight the rush.&quot;</p>

<p>’Hang on, angel,’ Kane prayed silently, watching the azure blood literally wash the golden runes away.</p>

<p>The golden seal hissed one final time before dissolving completely, leaving her pale skin entirely unblemished.</p>

<p>A blinding pulse of pure white light erupted directly from Griselda’s spine.</p>

<p>Kane shielded his eyes as the shockwave knocked the campfire embers sideways.</p>

<p>Tearing through the air with a sound like ripping silk, two majestic, pitch-black, feathered wings exploded from her shoulder blades.</p>

<p>Swish.</p>

<p>They spread wide across the ruined temple walls. The wingspan looked breathtaking, radiating raw, untamed power.</p>

<p>Griselda slumped forward, her hands bracing against the cold stone floor.</p>

<p>Her beautiful black wings twitched, folding slightly as she struggled to process the sudden rush of sensory input.</p>

<p>Panting heavily, she took a long, stuttering breath. It was her first true, unhindered breath in centuries.</p>

<p>No magical barrier caught in her throat. No divine gag stopped the air from reaching her vocal cords.</p>

<p>Slowly, she raised her head.</p>

<p>Sweeping her long violet hair out of her face, she looked directly up at Kane.</p>

<p>&quot;Kane,&quot; Griselda whispered.</p>

<p>Her new voice sounded raspy, rough from centuries of disuse, yet incredibly human and melodic.</p>

<p>Dropping the formal title completely signaled a profound shift.</p>

<p>She was no longer just a slave communicating through gestures.</p>

<p>&quot;Thank you,&quot; she rasped, tears finally spilling over her eyes.</p>

<p>Leaning back against the altar, Kane crossed his good arm over his chest.</p>

<p>&quot;Looks good on you, angel,&quot; Kane teased, letting the emotional weight of the moment settle.</p>

<p>&quot;Now put a shirt on before you catch a cold. We still have a long walk home.&quot;</p>

<p>Misha let out a laugh, handing Griselda her discarded leather tunic.</p>

<p>&quot;He ruins every romantic moment,&quot; Misha joked, helping the fallen angel guide her arms through the sleeves.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;I heard that.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;You were meant to hear it, husband,&quot; Misha shot back smoothly.</p>

<p>Griselda let out a beautiful laugh, testing the sound of her own joy. Rustling her black feathers, she adjusted the leather over her chest, finally feeling whole again.</p>

<p>’Mission accomplished,’ Kane smiled to himself, listening to the two women talk quietly near the fire.</p>

<p>’Got the blood, broke the seal, and gained a flying angel. Not a bad day’s work for a barbarian.’</p>

<p>&quot;Whenever you are ready to move, just say the word, Kane,&quot; Griselda spoke again, clearly savoring the feeling of the words rolling off her tongue.</p>

<p>&quot;I can scout the path ahead from the sky now.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;It’s not this path we should be worried about.&quot;</p>

<p>Kane said, his eyes changing.</p>

<p>&quot;It’s time to wage war against the Elven Kingdom.&quot;</p>
