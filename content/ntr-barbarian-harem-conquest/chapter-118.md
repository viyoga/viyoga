<p>Perfumed southern nobles clutched crystal goblets, parting nervously as Kane and Misha strode down the center aisle.</p>

<p>Whispers echoed off the pillars, mocking the warrior’s salt-crusted boots and scarred leather harness.</p>

<p>’The whole hall reeks of wine and panic. It feels like a cage full of frightened birds.’</p>

<p>Nobody dared step into his path.</p>

<p>Kane didn’t draw a weapon or raise his voice.</p>

<p>Catching the eye of a whispering countess, he merely flared his gaze.</p>

<p>A tiny fraction of his Conqueror’s intent bled into the air.</p>

<p>Gasping in terror, the woman dropped her goblet, sending shattered crystal flying across the floor.</p>

<p>Kane smirked, enjoying the shattered illusion of their safety.</p>

<p>Armor clanked ahead.</p>

<p>A high-ranking noble blocked the aisle, flanked by elite guards.</p>

<p>Lord Morvak wore a pristine ceremonial plate, his chest puffed out in a display of arrogant bravado.</p>

<p>Standing slightly behind him was his wife, Sarah.</p>

<p>Wearing an elegant crimson gown that made her bust, her expression remained serene and unreadable.</p>

<p>&quot;You tracked river muck into a civilized hall, barbarian,&quot; Morvak barked, resting a hand on a jeweled hilt.</p>

<p>&quot;You might have survived the mud flats, but you are out of your depth here. Surrender your axe.&quot;</p>

<p>Kane didn’t even look at the man.</p>

<p>His instincts bypassed the loud decoy, locking directly onto Sarah.</p>

<p>Morvak sweated profusely, his jaw tight with forced courage.</p>

<p>Sarah’s breathing remained perfectly even.</p>

<p>Tapping her painted fan against her wrist, she set a silent rhythm.</p>

<p>Her amber eyes held a seductive gleam, radiating the dominant confidence of a woman used to bending kings to her will.</p>

<p>’Morvak is sweating through his fancy collar, but her pulse hasn’t skipped a single beat since I walked through those doors,’ Kane thought, his blood warming at the challenge.</p>

<p>’He’s just a loudmouth. She is the one actually running this board.’</p>

<p>Bypassing the drawn guard entirely, Kane stepped right past Morvak.</p>

<p>He leaned down slightly, invading Sarah’s personal space.</p>

<p>&quot;Tell your puppet to stop barking,&quot; Kane rumbled, flashing her a knowing grin.</p>

<p>&quot;We both know who actually holds the leash in this empire.&quot;</p>

<p>Sarah’s serene mask slipped for a fraction of a second.</p>

<p>Sharp, venomous intelligence sparked in her gaze.</p>

<p>Realizing Kane saw right through their hierarchy, she didn’t shrink back.</p>

<p>Instead, she leaned into his proximity, her lips curving into a sultry, challenging smile.</p>

<p>She let her gaze travel down his chest.</p>

<p>&quot;A gladiator with a brain,&quot; Sarah said.</p>

<p>&quot;How refreshing. But knowing who holds the leash doesn’t save you from the bite. Some dogs need to be put down before they ruin the furniture.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;Lord Kane,&quot; a voice boomed from the front of the hall, interrupting the tense exchange.</p>

<p>Sitting at the high table on a throne, the Menual Emperor forced a condescending smile.</p>

<p>Ignoring the tension in the aisle, the monarch acted like a generous deity offering alms to a beggar.</p>

<p>&quot;We acknowledge your minor victories in the reeds,&quot; the Emperor announced grandly.</p>

<p>&quot;We offer you fifty chests of silver and a safe journey back to your northern rocks. Take the coin and leave.&quot;</p>

<p>Sarah caught Kane’s eye again, her smile widening into a promise of ruin.</p>

<p>Snapping her fan shut with a sharp crack, she issued the silent command.</p>

<p>The Emperor dropped his fake smile, his face twisting into an ugly sneer.</p>

<p>&quot;Or die where you stand.&quot;</p>

<p>Hidden doors along the walls burst open.</p>

<p>Two hundred elite royal guards stepped out, leveling loaded crossbows and drawn halberds directly at Kane’s chest.</p>

<p>Nobles gasped, scrambling backward to avoid the impending crossfire.</p>

<p>The trap was set, precise and lethal.</p>

<p>Crossbow bolts aimed at his heart failed to wipe the savage smirk off Kane’s face.</p>

<p>’So fucking cliche.’</p>

<p>’Time to remind them what a true warrior does in a tight cage.’</p>

<p>Moving faster than human eyes could track, his hand blurred over his shoulder.</p>

<p>Mjoldr sang a deadly tune as the crimson blade cleared his leather harness.</p>

<p>Morvak never even drew his jeweled sword.</p>

<p>A ruthless horizontal arc flashed across the aisle.</p>

<p>Slash.</p>

<p>Lord Morvak’s head detached instantly, tumoring across the polished marble while his pristine armor collapsed in a ruined fountain of red.</p>

<p>Screaming a panicked battle cry, the nearest royal guard captain lunged forward with a raised halberd.</p>

<p>Kane pivoted on his lead foot, swinging the axe in a smooth, unhurried backhand.</p>

<p>Crimson steel bit straight through the guard’s iron helmet.</p>

<p>A second head hit the floorboards with a wet thud.</p>

<p>Silence slammed into the banquet hall.</p>

<p>Two bodies hit the tiles before anyone could process the slaughter.</p>

<p>Before a single archer could squeeze a trigger, Kane closed the distance to Sarah.</p>

<p>Reaching out, his fingers wrapped firmly around the back of her neck, pulling her flush against his scarred chest.</p>

<p>He raised Mjoldr, resting the razor-sharp, blood-stained edge directly against her pale throat.</p>

<p>’Let’s see how dominant you act with a blade pressing against your pulse,’ Kane smirked internally, feeling her heart hammering wildly against his ribs.</p>

<p>&quot;Nobody twitches,&quot; Kane rumbled, his voice echoing over the terrified court.</p>

<p>&quot;Or the mastermind loses her pretty head. Drop the crossbows.&quot;</p>

<p>Elite guards froze in their tracks.</p>

<p>The whole noble house stood in shock.</p>

<p>Weapons clattered to the floor as soldiers panicked, unsure how to save their hidden leader without getting her killed.</p>

<p>Sarah’s serene mask shattered into pieces.</p>

<p>Trembling against his grip, her eyes widened with fear.</p>

<p>She felt the warmth of her husband’s blood radiating from the axe blade resting on her skin.</p>

<p>Her dominant facade fractured, revealing a woman mesmerized and terrified by true power.</p>

<p>Keeping his grip secure on her neck, Kane walked her casually up the steps toward the dais.</p>

<p>Reaching the top, he shoved the terrified Emperor right out of the chair with a swift kick.</p>

<p>Tumbling down the stairs, the monarch landed in a pathetic heap beside his broken guards.</p>

<p>Turning around, Kane sat comfortably on the velvet throne.</p>

<p>He didn’t release Sarah. Instead, he forced her down to her knees right beside his salt-crusted boots, keeping the crimson blade angled dangerously near her cheek.</p>

<p>&quot;Your little trap failed, lady,&quot; Kane rumbled, looking down into her wide eyes.</p>

<p>&quot;You built this empire on schemes, but I only respect results. Your life belongs to me now. Serve me, or I will finish the execution. Pour my wine. We have terms to discuss.&quot;</p>
