<p>Muddy riverbanks tore past in a green blur.</p>

<p>Riding the crest of the leviathan’s artificial tsunami, forty obsidian galleons surged over the ten-foot shallows.</p>

<p>Hulls scraped against submerged logs with deep, vibrating thuds that rattled the deckboards.</p>

<p>Holding the main steering rudder with white-knuckled focus, Kaelia rode the boiling wake until the tributary suddenly opened into the sweeping rearguard marshes of the Menual encampment.</p>

<p>With a deafening crash of displaced water, the wave flattened out.</p>

<p>The unburnable armada skidded right onto the soft banks, grounding securely behind enemy lines.</p>

<p>Spitting a mouthful of river spray over the gunwale, Thora spun her twin axes. Her hazel eyes locked onto the panicked camp two hundred yards away.</p>

<p>&quot;We are in their backyard, Chief!&quot; Thora laughed, pointing a blade toward the staging grounds.</p>

<p>&quot;Look at those oversized pigs!&quot;</p>

<p>Across the marsh, Menual mahouts were screaming, desperately trying to wheel their iron-plated war elephants around to face the impossible flank attack.</p>

<p>The ground shuddered as four hundred beasts roared in confusion.</p>

<p>’Standard infantry blades won’t even scratch the laminated iron plates covering those skulls,’ Kane calculated, his eyes tracking the reinforced bone structure of the lead bull.</p>

<p>’I need enough velocity to punch straight through the armor.’</p>

<p>Bypassing his physical inventory, he pulled directly from his accumulated wealth mid-stride.</p>

<p>[Sovereign’s War Cache: Spent 10,000 Gold Coins]</p>

<p>[Unlocking Class Feature: Titan Sunderer (Passive)]</p>

<p>[Description: Converts 30% of your current total Strength into kinetic shockwaves. Weapon density increases by two tiers during downward swings.]</p>

<p>[Title Synergy: Sovereign of the Black Tides active — Stamina drainage negated in river basins.]</p>

<p>’Ten thousand gold to turn my axe into a siege engine,’ Kane smirked internally, feeling a fresh heat flood his forearms. ’Worth every single coin.’</p>

<p>Vaulting directly off the wooden bowsprit, he hit the marshlands at a full sprint. Mud kicked up behind his boots.</p>

<p>A three-ton bull elephant charged down the bank to intercept him, its tusks reinforced with jagged steel bands.</p>

<p>Kane didn’t step aside.</p>

<p>[Feature Activated: Titan Sunderer]</p>

<p>Planting his lead foot, he brought Mjoldr down in a savage, unhurried overhead arc.</p>

<p>Crimson steel met the reinforced steel tusk-guards.</p>

<p>The ancient blade bit straight through the metal bands, ignoring the iron skull-plating entirely to split the beast’s head wide open.</p>

<p>Concussive pressure echoed across the river valley like a dry thunderclap, flipping the colossal frame sideways into the reeds.</p>

<p>[Critical Strike Triggered: Armor Ignored] [Menual Land Forces Terminated: 1/400 Elephants]</p>

<p>&quot;Ripping them down already?&quot; Seraphine’s voice called out.</p>

<p>The former paladin cleared the ship’s rail a split second later.</p>

<p>Shared Tri-Bond Synergy sparked between them, wrapping their movements in a pulsing crimson aura.</p>

<p>Wearing her fitted black scout leathers, Seraphine slipped right between the stomping legs of a second charging elephant.</p>

<p>Her broadsword sang in a low, vicious horizontal sweep that severed the unprotected tendons behind the animal’s knees.</p>

<p>As the beast buckled forward, Thora vaulted off its collapsing flank.</p>

<p>Her twin axes took the heads of three Menual crossbowmen tumbling out of the wooden deck-howdah.</p>

<p>Landing lightly in the mud, Seraphine wiped a streak of warm blood from her cheek. Her silver eyes glowed with the fierce, addictive rush of their shared rhythm.</p>

<p>&quot;Your new upgrade makes quite a mess, gladiator!&quot; Seraphine yelled over the trumpeting beasts.</p>

<p>&quot;I figure the fields needed plowing anyway!&quot; Kane rumbled back, wrenching Mjoldr free from the shattered iron collar.</p>

<p>Clearing the immediate perimeter left a brief, quiet pocket inside the stampede, but Kane didn’t drop his guard to celebrate.</p>

<p>His boots sank into the red marsh as three more armored bull elephants broke formation, charging abreast to crush their pocket.</p>

<p>Their iron skull-plates gleamed under the midday sun.</p>

<p>’Three of them at once,’ Kane calculated, his eyes tracking the narrow gap between their reinforced knees.</p>

<p>’The mahouts are trying to box us against the hull. If they pin us in the reeds, the crossbowmen on the towers will chew us to pieces.’</p>

<p>&quot;Keep your spacing tight! Don’t let their vanguard reform the line!&quot; Kane roared.</p>

<p>Reaching out, his calloused palm caught Seraphine by the shoulder, physically shoving her five paces to the left or right as a spiked iron flail whipped through the empty air where she had just been standing.</p>

<p>&quot;Take the left flanks!&quot; Kane barked, launching himself forward into the center beast.</p>

<p>[Feature Activated: Titan Sunderer]</p>

<p>He didn’t aim for the top of the skull this time.</p>

<p>Sliding low on his shins beneath the center elephant’s jagged tusks, Kane brought Mjoldr upward in a vicious, rising vertical arc.</p>

<p>The kinetic shockwave bypassed the exterior iron breast-plating entirely, exploding upward through the animal’s soft palate.</p>

<p>Wood and bone shattered above him.</p>

<p>The three-ton behemoth dropped instantly, its collapsing bulk forcing the remaining two elephants to violently swerve and crash their massive shoulders into one another.</p>

<p>&quot;They are bottlenecking themselves!&quot; Thora roared.</p>

<p>Vaulting straight onto the tangled, thrashing beasts, the blonde warrior spun her twin axes in a blinding scissor-strike, burying both blades deep into the chest of the nearest screaming mahout before he could blow his signaling horn.</p>

<p>Panicked trumpeting drowned out the shouts of the Menual vanguard as the surviving war elephants finally lost their nerve.</p>

<p>Three-ton behemoths realized no amount of laminated iron plating could stop kinetic shockwaves from turning their brains into jelly, so self-preservation took over. Ignoring the frantic whipping of their mahouts, the animals pivoted in the reeds and charged backward right into their own advancing infantry columns.</p>

<p>’They spent an imperial treasury breeding these monsters, and now their own investment is stomping their soldiers into paste,’ Kane observed, casually sidestepping a spinning wooden howdah that detached from a fleeing bull.</p>

<p>’Morvak really should have trained them for an ambush.’</p>

<p>&quot;Chief! The vanguard is buckling!&quot; Sira shouted, vaulting off a marsh rock to land beside his shoulder.</p>

<p>&quot;Firlia’s archers have the northern ridge covered, but those elephants are trampling the enemy supply wagons!&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;Let them chew their own rations,&quot; Kane laughed, pointing his dagger toward the retreating officers.</p>

<p>&quot;Focus your volleys on the mahouts trying to steer them back toward the ships! Keep the herd running south!&quot;</p>
