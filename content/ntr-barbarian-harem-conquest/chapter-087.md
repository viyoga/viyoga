<p>Stepping onto the courtyard stone, Kane wore only his linen trousers.</p>

<p>Clutching Mjoldr tightly in his right hand, he felt the familiar pulse of energy humming through the handle.</p>

<p>&quot;Thora, stay inside!&quot; Kane shouted over his shoulder.</p>

<p>She shrieked in sudden surprise from the bedroom but quickly agreed.</p>

<p>Slamming the wooden door shut, she slid the iron latch into place.</p>

<p>’I often forget this game is set on hell difficulty,’ Kane mused, rolling his bare shoulders to loosen the muscles.</p>

<p>’None of these politicians or their hired villains are ever worth sparing. They just keep sending bodies.’</p>

<p>Sira materialized right beside his elbow, an arrow already nocked on her composite bowstring.</p>

<p>&quot;Where are they?&quot; she whispered, her silver eyes scanning the slate rooftops.</p>

<p>&quot;Hiding,&quot; Kane smirked.</p>

<p>&quot;Playing hard to find. Professional assassins always prefer the shadows until the very last second.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;Hmm?&quot; Rutheus grunted, stepping out from the guest quarters.</p>

<p>The old veteran had ridden back late last evening to deliver a progress report from the pass, and his timing proved immaculate.</p>

<p>Drawing his broadsword with his single remaining hand, he marched straight into the center of the open courtyard.</p>

<p>&quot;If you’ve got balls, then face us directly, you cowards!&quot; Rutheus roared, his gravelly voice echoing aggressively off the surrounding manors.</p>

<p>Walking slowly into the center of the space, Kane gripped the leather-wrapped haft of his axe.</p>

<p>He needed to draw them out.</p>

<p>Wielding the weapon in a sweeping, upward arc, he forced the crimson aura to erupt.</p>

<p>A blinding, blood-red light washed over the entire property, emitting a terrifying power that illuminated every hidden corner and rooftop ledge.</p>

<p>&quot;What is that?&quot;</p>

<p>Elven neighbors began peeking out of their elegant windows.</p>

<p>Doors creaked open along the cobblestone street as curious, sleepy nobles stepped outside to see what the commotion was about.</p>

<p>They dropped from the roof tiles like rain. Forty killers dressed entirely in midnight fabric.</p>

<p>[System Notification]</p>

<p>[Hostile Targets Engaged: Morvak’s Shadows]</p>

<p>[Passive: Conqueror’s Presence Active]</p>

<p>&quot;Kill them all,&quot; Kane ordered.</p>

<p>Brak met the first wave head-on.</p>

<p>Bringing his iron-alloy shield up, he caught a descending assassin right in the jaw. Bone shattered instantly.</p>

<p>Teeth flew across the paving stones.</p>

<p>Brak didn’t stop there; driving his boot forward, he snapped the man’s kneecap backward before burying a hatchet deep into the killer’s skull.</p>

<p>&quot;Left flank!&quot; Sira called out.</p>

<p>Her bowstring hummed a lethal tune.</p>

<p>Three arrows took flight in rapid succession.</p>

<p>One pierced a shadow’s throat, sending a spray of bright arterial blood painting the white stone walls.</p>

<p>Another pinned a man’s foot to the roof, leaving him dangling and screaming until Lirael finished the job.</p>

<p>The sellsword moved with terrifying efficiency.</p>

<p>Ducking under a poisoned dagger thrust, Lirael spun on her heel and separated the attacker’s arm from his shoulder.</p>

<p>Blood geysered upward.</p>

<p>Kicking the severed limb out of her way, she drove her blade straight through the next man’s chest, twisting the steel to ensure a fatal wound.</p>

<p>Kessa didn’t bother using forged weapons.</p>

<p>Dropping onto all fours, the beastwoman launched herself at a cluster of three assassins trying to breach the servant’s entrance.</p>

<p>Her claws flashed in the red light.</p>

<p>She ripped the first man’s stomach wide open, spilling steaming intestines onto the pristine steps.</p>

<p>Pouncing on the second, her fangs sank deep into his jugular. He gurgled, thrashing wildly while she bled him dry on the pavement.</p>

<p>’She’s definitely enjoying this,’ Kane smiled, ducking a swinging chain weapon.</p>

<p>Stepping inside his attacker’s guard, Kane swung Mjoldr upward.</p>

<p>The crimson blade caught the assassin right under the chin.</p>

<p>It didn’t just cut; it cleaved cleanly through the jawbone, exiting straight through the top of the skull.</p>

<p>Brain matter and bone fragments splattered across the courtyard, coating Kane’s bare chest in gore.</p>

<p>Rutheus laughed, a sound that genuinely terrified the remaining shadows.</p>

<p>A killer tried to flank the old man, mistakenly thinking the missing arm offered a blind spot.</p>

<p>Pivoting smoothly, Rutheus caught the man’s temple with the flat pommel of his broadsword. The skull caved inward with a sickening crunch, dropping the assassin like a puppet with cut strings.</p>

<p>&quot;Keep them away from the study!&quot; Caelindra yelled from a second-story window.</p>

<p>Holding a loaded crossbow, the secretary looked surprisingly capable.</p>

<p>She fired, catching a climber right between the shoulder blades.</p>

<p>Firlia and Lyssel stood right behind her, tossing down anything loose and heavy to break the climbers’ grips, sending two men tumbling to the hard stones below.</p>

<p>Down by the courtyard fountain, Grieselda remained perfectly calm.</p>

<p>An assassin charged her, raising a short sword to strike.</p>

<p>She simply raised her pale hand.</p>

<p>A localized pulse of energy radiated from her elegant fingers.</p>

<p>The killer’s eyes rolled into the back of his head, and he collapsed instantly, blood leaking steadily from his ears and nose.</p>

<p>Only ten assassins remained standing.</p>

<p>Realizing their quiet infiltration had turned into an absolute butchery, they hesitated, exchanging panicked glances.</p>

<p>Kane refused to let them retreat.</p>

<p>[Berserker Rage Lv2 — Bloodlust Activated]</p>

<p>His muscles expanded, hot adrenaline flooding his veins. Closing the distance in a single leap, Kane threw his axe. It spun sideways, taking off a runner’s legs right at the knees.</p>

<p>&quot;GAHH!&quot;</p>

<p>The man collapsed into a screaming, bloody stump.</p>

<p>Catching Mjoldr on the rebound, Kane drove his bare fist right through another man’s ribcage.</p>

<p>He felt the fragile heart burst against his knuckles.</p>

<p>&quot;No prisoners tonight!&quot; Kane roared, ripping his bloody hand free from the chest cavity.</p>

<p>Tackling a fleeing shadow, Brak punched the man’s face repeatedly until it resembled ruined meat.</p>

<p>Aiming high, Sira put a feathered arrow directly through the eye socket of a shadow trying desperately to scale the perimeter wall.</p>

<p>Lirael and Rutheus boxed the last three into a corner.</p>

<p>The final assassin dropped his poisoned daggers, raising his trembling hands in surrender.</p>

<p>&quot;I yield!&quot; the man begged, sinking to his knees in the spreading pools of blood.</p>

<p>Walking over, Kane’s bare chest heaved.</p>

<p>He was bathed in blood.</p>

<p>’This game does not reward mercy,’ Kane conjectured, looking down at the weeping killer.</p>

<p>’Morvak sent these men to murder my people in their sleep. Sparing him just tells that arrogant Chancellor I am soft.’</p>

<p>&quot;Denied,&quot; Kane stated flatly.</p>

<p>Swinging Mjoldr in a brutal downward arc, he split the man’s torso wide open from collarbone to sternum.</p>

<p>Ribs cracked loudly, and punctured lungs ruptured.</p>

<p>The ruined body slumped over, joining the mounting pile of corpses.</p>

<p>[Assassins Eliminated: 40/40]</p>
