<p>Sunlit market squares bustled with morning trade.</p>

<p>Kane walked beside Seraphine, easily keeping pace with her strides.</p>

<p>Everywhere he looked, the cultural inversion was glaringly apparent.</p>

<p>Women shouted prices from produce stalls, haggled over silks, and forged steel weapons over open fires.</p>

<p>Meanwhile, men scurried past with woven grocery baskets.</p>

<p>They kept their heads bowed, eyes fixed firmly on the cobblestones.</p>

<p>One nervous husband stood on a balcony, carefully folding linens while a female guard below gave him a dismissive nod.</p>

<p>’It takes a very specific kind of conditioning to break half a population like that,’ Kane mused, watching a male servant flinch when Seraphine walked too close.</p>

<p>&quot;Do any of the men here actually hold a sword?&quot; Kane asked, casually hooking his thumbs into his belt.</p>

<p>&quot;Only the ones guarding the border camps,&quot; Seraphine answered.</p>

<p>She kept her eyes scanning the crowds, ever the vigilant paladin.</p>

<p>&quot;Inside the capital, they manage the households. The Goddess dictates that women hold the divine mandate to rule and protect.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;I guess that gets very boring for you,&quot; Kane remarked.</p>

<p>Stepping slightly out of the main walking path, he forced her to slow down.</p>

<p>He leaned close enough to catch her familiar scent, like winter pine.</p>

<p>&quot;Knowing every man you meet is trained to just nod and clean your floors.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;It maintains order,&quot; Seraphine shot back, though she didn’t step away from his proximity.</p>

<p>&quot;Order breeds strength.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;Order breeds complacency,&quot; Kane corrected.</p>

<p>&quot;If your opponents never fight back, you forget how to properly swing a sword.&quot;</p>

<p>Seeking a brief respite from the midday sun, they stepped into a busy local tavern.</p>

<p>Laughter echoed off the wooden beams.</p>

<p>Groups of armored women sat around large tables, drinking ale and boasting about patrol routes.</p>

<p>Finding a secluded booth in the corner, Kane slid into the wooden bench.</p>

<p>Seraphine sat opposite him, sitting ramrod straight.</p>

<p>A timid male server quickly scurried over, setting down two cups of spiced wine before practically bowing his way backward into the kitchen.</p>

<p>’He looked like he expected Seraphine to strike him just for breathing near her,’ Kane noted, picking up his cup.</p>

<p>Taking a slow sip, Kane rested his elbows on the sticky wood.</p>

<p>&quot;So, First Blade. Tell me the truth.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;About what?&quot; she asked suspiciously, wrapping her gauntleted fingers around her cup.</p>

<p>Kane pressed forward, sliding his cup across the table until it almost touched hers.</p>

<p>&quot;Do you really want a husband who just washes your armor and stays quiet?&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;It’s the law of our culture,&quot; she argued defensively.</p>

<p>Despite her rigid words, her eyes dropped involuntarily to his lips for a fraction of a second.</p>

<p>&quot;That’s not an answer,&quot; Kane smiled, his voice low enough that only she could hear it over the tavern noise.</p>

<p>&quot;Laws dictate how you act in public. They do not dictate what you crave when the lights go out.&quot;</p>

<p>Seraphine tightened her grip on her cup.</p>

<p>&quot;You speak treason very casually, Barbarian.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;I guess you want someone who pushes back,&quot; Kane continued, completely ignoring her weak threat.</p>

<p>&quot;Someone who pins you to a wall and makes you forget the Goddess.&quot;</p>

<p>’She’s trembling,’ Kane thought with a smirk, watching a deep flush spread rapidly across her chest and creep up her neck.</p>

<p>’The First Blade of the Empire is desperate for a real challenge.’</p>

<p>Seraphine took a sharp breath, her silver eyes flashing with a sudden, competitive fire.</p>

<p>&quot;I’m not like the other women. You would have to try more than that.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;Is that an invitation?&quot; Kane murmured, reaching across the table to casually run his index finger over her armored knuckles.</p>

<p>&quot;Because I always accept a challenge from a beautiful paladin.&quot;</p>

<p>Pulling her hand back, she offered a tight, flustered glare.</p>

<p>&quot;You are betrothed to the Princess.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;Betrothals are just ink on parchment,&quot; Kane shrugged, leaning back against the wooden bench.</p>

<p>&quot;They secure fleets and armies. They do not dictate who I take to my bed.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;You are insufferable.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;I’m honest,&quot; Kane corrected.</p>

<p>&quot;Which is something you rarely encounter in this city. Everyone here tells you exactly what you want to hear because they fear your blade. I do not fear you, Seraphine. And that is why you cannot stop thinking about me.&quot;</p>

<p>Her jaw clenched tight.</p>

<p>She wanted to argue, to quote scripture or threaten him with imperial punishment. But she couldn’t.</p>

<p>Her pulse raced visibly at her throat, betraying her strict training.</p>

<p>&quot;Drink your wine,&quot; she finally muttered, breaking eye contact to stare firmly at the tavern wall.</p>

<p>Kane chuckled softly, taking a long drink.</p>

<p>The spiced wine tasted excellent, but the victory tasted much sweeter.</p>

<p>[Cultural Analysis Complete: Milfheim Dynamics]</p>

<p>[New Insight Gained: Recognizing societal pressure points grants a tactical advantage in conversation.]</p>

<p>Finishing his drink, Kane watched the tavern patrons.</p>

<p>Two female soldiers were arm-wrestling near the hearth, cheering loudly while their male companions silently refilled their mugs.</p>

<p>&quot;If your Empress expects me to act like these local husbands, she’s going to be severely disappointed,&quot; Kane said casually.</p>

<p>&quot;She does not expect you to wash clothes,&quot; Seraphine replied, her composure slowly returning.</p>

<p>&quot;She expects you to provide a strong bloodline and a secure border.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;And what do you expect from me?&quot; Kane asked, tilting his head.</p>

<p>&quot;I expect you to sink Morvak’s ships,&quot; she answered flatly.</p>

<p>&quot;I will,&quot; Kane promised.</p>

<p>&quot;But after the ships burn, and the war is won, what then? Are you going to go back to standing beside a throne, pretending you do not want a man who can put you on your back?&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;Stop talking like that in public,&quot; she whispered, glancing nervously at the nearby tables.</p>

<p>&quot;Make me,&quot; Kane challenged.</p>

<p>Seraphine leaned across the table, her face mere inches from his.</p>

<p>&quot;If we were not in the middle of my capital, I would draw my sword and teach you some manners.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;If we were not in the middle of your capital,&quot; Kane whispered back, his gaze fixed on her lips, &quot;you would not be reaching for your sword.&quot;</p>

<p>Letting the tension hang in the air for a long moment, Kane finally stood up.</p>

<p>He tossed a few silver coins onto the table to cover the wine.</p>

<p>&quot;Show me the rest of your city,&quot; Kane ordered softly.</p>

<p>&quot;We have a lot of ground to cover before sunset.&quot;</p>

<p>Grabbing her cup, Seraphine downed the rest of her wine in a single, unladylike gulp.</p>

<p>She practically stormed out of the booth, her white cape billowing behind her.</p>
