<p>It took him by surprise, an unyielding surge of paladin strength, backed by a sudden, concentrated flare of golden mana that locked her muscles like stone, asserting her dominance before he could even set his footing.</p>

<p>Her mouth crashed onto his.</p>

<p>It wasn’t a sweet maiden’s kiss.</p>

<p>Seraphine was aggressively claiming him.</p>

<p>Her tongue pushed past his teeth in a demanding sweep, slick and frantic as it tangled with his own, tasting of wine and decades of suffocating repression.</p>

<p>Her teeth caught his lower lip hard enough to draw a sharp bead of blood.</p>

<p>&quot;Aren’t you too rough?&quot;</p>

<p>’I figure she finally lost her mind,’ Kane thought, a jolt of adrenaline hitting his chest.</p>

<p>’A holy knight trying to put a warlord in his place. It’s magnificent.’</p>

<p>&quot;Guh!&quot;</p>

<p>Taking control of the rhythm, he kissed her back with ruthless, bruising authority.</p>

<p>He claimed every single inch of her mouth with the rough pace of a maniac, swallowing her small, broken whimper of total surrender.</p>

<p>Suddenly, her fingers tangled deep into his red hair, gripping the strands hard enough to sting as she yanked his head back.</p>

<p>&quot;That’s... enough,&quot; Seraphine gasped out, her chest heaving wildly against his as she desperately tried to reconstruct her shattered paladin discipline.</p>

<p>Kane looked down at her swollen, wet lips.</p>

<p>A slow smirk spread across his face.</p>

<p>&quot;Okay,&quot; Kane rumbled softly.</p>

<p>Then he smashed his mouth right back onto hers.</p>

<p>It was twice as rough as before.</p>

<p>Seraphine stood there half-naked, her ruined shirt hanging off her shoulders, her skin burning a deep feverish red.</p>

<p>Kane stood inches away, his own tunic hanging completely open over his battle-scarred chest, matching her ragged breaths.</p>

<p>He reached out, lazily brushing a messy silver strand behind her pointed ear.</p>

<p>&quot;Next time,&quot; Kane rumbled low, his eyes locked onto hers, &quot;don’t ask me to stop.&quot;</p>

<p>Morning sunlight caught the sails of forty war galleons.</p>

<p>The pristine Milfheim fleet moved in perfect, synchronized lines parallel to the western cliffs, cutting through the gray ocean water.</p>

<p>Riding his warhorse along the high ridge, Kane looked down at his new floating fortress.</p>

<p>Seraphine rode to his left, her silver armor polished and her silence extremely loud.</p>

<p>To his right rode Commander Kaelia.</p>

<p>The red-haired commander had abandoned her previous matriarchal sneer. Leaning out of her saddle, she pointed a leather-gloved finger toward the rocky coastal reef ahead.</p>

<p>&quot;If we anchor the primary galleons behind that southern crag, the ballista firing arcs will overlap the cove entrance perfectly,&quot; Kaelia suggested, looking at Kane with genuine professional respect.</p>

<p>&quot;What do you think, Kane?&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;Keep the secondary pursuit cutters tucked inside the tree line shadow,&quot; Kane instructed, adjusting his leather reins.</p>

<p>&quot;Morvak’s vanguard will look for open water to turn their crude cargo hulls. I want them stepping right into a dead end.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;Flawless positioning,&quot; Kaelia agreed eagerly, jotting the tactical adjustment onto her parchment ledger.</p>

<p>’These Milfheim commanders learn fast once you establish who actually holds the leash,’ Kane mused with a smirk.</p>

<p>’Give them a leader to follow, and they drop their silly gender rules by lunchtime.’</p>

<p>Horns blared from the stone battlements.</p>

<p>Brak stood at the primary dock, expertly tossing mooring ropes to the Milfheim sailors.</p>

<p>Skipping the standard victory speeches, Kane opened the carriage door.</p>

<p>He offered his bare hand to Misha.</p>

<p>The Princess took his palm instantly, her cold fingers wrapping securely around his calloused wrist as she stepped down onto the gravel.</p>

<p>Leading her up the stone steps of the command manor, Kane pushed open the doors to his private study.</p>

<p>The angelic woman didn’t move.</p>

<p>An interface sparked directly inside Kane’s mind.</p>

<p>[The divine creators shattered her core and abandoned the pieces,] Grieselda’s silent transmission echoed in his head, sterile and godlike.</p>
