<p>Kane wrenched Mjoldr free with raw strength and threw himself sideways as a massive forelimb scythed through the space he had occupied.</p>

<p>Claws longer than fighting knives tore furrows in the earth.</p>

<p>Shenron’s broadsword flashed in from the opposite side, scoring a deep line across the creature’s flank.</p>

<p>The mutant screamed, that layered, metallic roar, and the smaller limbs lashed out like tentacles, forcing Shenron to leap back.</p>

<p>Kessa darted in low, blades flashing, and opened a gash along one rear leg.</p>

<p>Griselda struck from above, dark wings driving her down in a precise dive that raked glowing lines across the cluster of eyes.</p>

<p>Several of the weeping black orbs burst. The mutant reeled, then answered with a sweep of its ridged spine that forced everyone to scatter.</p>

<p>Kane reset his grip on Mjoldr.</p>

<p>The axe head was smeared with the black fluid, and the cut he had made was already trying to close, edges of corrupted flesh knitting with unnatural speed.</p>

<p>This is bad.</p>

<p>The mutant turned its remaining eyes on him again, recognizing the source of the heaviest blow.</p>

<p>It gathered itself for another charge, the smaller limbs coiling, the tar-like fluid dripping from its jaws onto the leaf litter where it smoked and ate small holes in the dirt.</p>

<p>Around the clearing, the beast warriors held their positions, waiting for an opening that would not get them killed in a single swipe.</p>

<p>Shenron moved to Kane’s left, broadsword ready.</p>

<p>Kessa flanked right. Griselda climbed for another dive.</p>

<p>Kane spun Mjoldr once, feeling the weight, and set his feet.</p>

<p>But the beast was too fast.</p>

<p>The mutant’s charge hit the clearing like a landslide.</p>

<p>Bam!</p>

<p>Kane barely finished the word &quot;Fuck&quot; before the massive forelimb scythed past his head hard enough to peel bark from a tree behind him.</p>

<p>He rolled, came up with Mjoldr already moving, and put everything he had into a downward cut aimed at the joint where the patchwork shoulder met the elongated spine.</p>

<p>The axe bit deep, then stopped.</p>

<p>Black fluid welled around the edge.</p>

<p>The wound flexed and began to close even as he planted a boot on the creature’s hide and wrenched the weapon free.</p>

<p>&quot;It heals too fast!&quot; Kessa shouted, darting in from the flank. Her blades flashed, opening two long lines across the mutant’s rear leg.</p>

<p>The cuts sealed almost as quickly as they appeared, leaving only glistening black seams.</p>

<p>Shenron met the creature head-on, broadsword carving an arc that took one of the smaller grasping limbs off at the mid-joint.</p>

<p>The severed piece hit the ground and kept twitching.</p>

<p>Griselda dropped like a spear and raked both hands across the cluster of weeping eyes on the left side of the head.</p>

<p>Several burst in sprays of tar-like fluid.</p>

<p>The mutant reeled, slamming into a trunk hard enough to crack it.</p>

<p>Kane used the opening.</p>

<p>He stepped in and drove Mjoldr toward the same ruined eye cluster, aiming to split the skull.</p>

<p>The axe head sank halfway and locked again, as if the bone and corrupted flesh beneath had turned to layered iron.</p>

<p>The system window exploded across his vision in jagged red text.</p>

<p>[System Alert – Aberrant Mutant Variant Analysis]</p>

<p>[Tissue Regeneration: Extreme]</p>

<p>[Physical Damage Reduction: High]</p>

<p>[Corruption Density: Critical]</p>

<p>[Warning: Prolonged exposure increasing]</p>

<p>[Recommendation: Overwhelming force / Target central nerve clusters]</p>

<p>[Berserker Rage efficiency reduced under current corruption load]</p>

<p>Kane dismissed it with a snarl and ripped Mjoldr free. B</p>

<p>lack fluid sprayed across his chest and arms.</p>

<p>The smell was chemical and wrong, burning in his nose.</p>

<p>&quot;Rage it!&quot; Shenron barked, already back on his feet, blood running from a split lip.</p>

<p>&quot;Whatever you used on me—use it now!&quot;</p>

<p>Kane didn’t argue.</p>

<p>Berserker Rage ignited like oil thrown on fire.</p>

<p>Heat flooded every muscle.</p>

<p>Strength spiked hard enough to compress the ground under his boots.</p>

<p>The world narrowed to angles, openings, and the need to end the thing in front of him before the regeneration outpaced every wound they could make.</p>

<p>He met the mutant’s next charge head-on.</p>

<p>Mjoldr came around in a full-powered horizontal swing that caught the creature across the ribs and actually staggered it.</p>

<p>The hide split wider this time.</p>

<p>Black fluid poured.</p>

<p>Kane didn’t stop.</p>

<p>He stepped in, used the axe like a cleaver, and began hacking with short, brutal, continuous blows, forcing the regeneration to work on multiple deep wounds at once.</p>

<p>Kessa flanked low, hamstringing the rear legs again and again, buying seconds each time the mutant tried to turn.</p>

<p>Shenron locked both hands on the broadsword and drove it through one of the smaller shoulder limbs into the meat beneath, pinning it against a fallen log.</p>

<p>The mutant thrashed, tar spraying from its jaws.</p>

<p>Griselda climbed for height, then dove a second time. Her hands glowed faintly with residual fallen-angel power as she raked the remaining functional eyes. More black orbs burst. The creature’s scream climbed into a register that made the beast warriors on the perimeter flinch and cover their ears.</p>

<p>Kane saw the opening.</p>

<p>He planted his feet, drew Mjoldr all the way back, and put the full force of the Rage into a single rising thrust. The axe head punched through the largest remaining eye cluster, continued through bone and pulsing flesh, and buried itself deep into the elongated spine with a wet, grinding crack.</p>

<p>The mutant convulsed.</p>

<p>Shenron threw his full weight onto the pinned limb, holding it down.</p>

<p>Kessa drove both blades into the base of the skull from the side and twisted.</p>

<p>Griselda landed hard on the thrashing shoulders and forced her hands into the ruined eye sockets, blinding whatever senses still functioned.</p>

<p>The creature’s roar broke into a wet, choking rattle.</p>

<p>It slammed itself sideways into the trees, trying to dislodge them, but the combined weight and the axe lodged in its spine kept it from rising fully.</p>

<p>Black fluid poured from every wound in thick, smoking sheets.</p>

<p>The smaller limbs flailed, grabbed at nothing, and finally went limp.</p>

<p>Kane wrenched Mjoldr sideways inside the wound, then tore it free with a spray of tar and shattered bone.</p>

<p>The mutant collapsed in a twitching heap, the regeneration finally failing as the central nerve clusters gave out.</p>

<p>It kicked once, twice, then stilled.</p>

<p>The only sound left was the slow drip of black fluid into the leaf litter and the harsh breathing of everyone still standing.</p>
