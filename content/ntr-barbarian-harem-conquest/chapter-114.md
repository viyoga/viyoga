<p>Noon brought a chaotic surge of energy to the Sylvandar docks.</p>

<p>Bloodfang warriors hauled crates of salted meat and Elven alchemical charges onto forty obsidian galleons.</p>

<p>Standing on the primary pier, Commander Kaelia unrolled a parchment map across a supply barrel.</p>

<p>She tapped her gloved finger against the painted lines, looking highly skeptical.</p>

<p>&quot;Lord Kane, the Princess is brilliant, but she is not a sailor,&quot; Kaelia warned.</p>

<p>She pointed to the western river route on the map.</p>

<p>&quot;That tributary only offers ten feet of clearance. Our galleons require at least fifteen to avoid scraping the hull. We will get beached before we travel a single mile.&quot;</p>

<p>Kane strapped Mjoldr securely across his back, feeling the familiar weight settle against his muscles.</p>

<p>&quot;We aren’t sailing up the river normally, Commander. We are flooding it.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;Flooding it?&quot; Kaelia blinked, her red eyebrows shooting upward in disbelief.</p>

<p>&quot;Sir, I do not think we have enough mages in the entire Elven kingdom to raise a river level by five feet.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;The Abyssal Trench-Stalker owes me a favor,&quot; Kane explained, his voice carrying unshakeable certainty.</p>

<p>&quot;We tether the leviathan to the flagship again. A beast that size displacing water at top speed will create a localized tidal surge. We ride its wake straight over the shallow mud.&quot;</p>

<p>’I think the physics hold up,’ Kane calculated, picturing the beast’s displacement.</p>

<p>’If the monster swims fast enough, we surf right into the Emperor’s lap. The Menual scouts will never see a tsunami coming up a shallow creek.’</p>

<p>Kaelia stared at him. Her jaw dropped slightly before a wild, reckless smile took over her face.</p>

<p>&quot;You are genuinely insane. I will inform the rowers to strap themselves to the benches.&quot;</p>

<p>Footsteps tapped against the wooden planks.</p>

<p>Kane turned, and his pulse spiked in immediate appreciation.</p>

<p>Seraphine and Thora walked down the pier together.</p>

<p>The former paladin had permanently discarded her silver plate.</p>

<p>Instead, she wore fitted black Bloodfang scout leathers.</p>

<p>Her broadsword rested securely against her hip. The material hugged her toned curves, offering maximum mobility and a lethal, understated aesthetic.</p>

<p>’She looks like a proper warrior now,’ Kane thought, his eyes tracing the tight leather across her thighs.</p>

<p>’Shedding the holy armor did wonders for her posture.’</p>

<p>&quot;You look dangerous,&quot; Kane murmured.</p>

<p>Reaching out, he wrapped his arm around her waist and pulled her against his side.</p>

<p>&quot;Thora helped me adjust the straps,&quot; Seraphine smirked.</p>

<p>She leaned her cheek against his shoulder, radiating a relaxed, deeply satisfied glow from their long night.</p>

<p>&quot;They offer much better leverage for a sword swing. I feel unbound.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;You felt pretty unbound a few hours ago, too,&quot; Thora laughed, stepping into his left side.</p>

<p>She bumped her hip against his, hazel eyes dancing with wicked amusement.</p>

<p>&quot;I think my back still has scratch marks from her holy fingernails. Who knew a priestess could scream that loud?&quot;</p>

<p>Seraphine flushed a beautiful crimson but refused to pull away. Instead, she tilted her chin up, capturing Kane’s mouth in a slow kiss.</p>

<p>Her tongue slid past his lips, tasting of confidence and lingering desire.</p>

<p>Breaking the kiss, Seraphine bit her lower lip.</p>

<p>Her breathing hitched, eyes glazed with raw affection.</p>

<p>&quot;I figure we owe you a rematch once we take the capital.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;Keep that energy,&quot; Kane told her.</p>

<p>He reached his other arm out to catch Thora by the belt, pulling his partner closer.</p>

<p>&quot;The Emperor has four hundred war elephants waiting for us. We are going to need a lot of leverage.&quot;</p>

<p>Thora laughed, tracing a calloused finger down his bare chest.</p>

<p>&quot;Elephants just mean bigger targets, Chief. Let us go collect some ivory. My legs are rested, and I’m ready to break something.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;Let us go ruin an empire,&quot; Kane agreed.</p>

<p>Stepping onto the high command tier of the blackened flagship, Kane looked out toward the southern horizon.</p>

<p>The ocean breeze caught his red hair.</p>

<p>Down in the hold, Misha sat securely beside Grieselda, her eyes tracking the enemy miles away.</p>

<p>His two lethal partners stood right at his flanks, their blades ready.</p>

<p>[Urgent Faction Quest Triggered: The Delta Siege]</p>

<p>[Objective: Breach the Menual River Blockade and dethrone the Emperor.]</p>

<p>[Tactical Override: Naval superiority established]</p>

<p>[Proceed with terrestrial invasion.]</p>

<p>&quot;Are you sure the beast knows where to go?&quot; Seraphine asked, bracing her boots against the deckboards as the ship pitched forward slightly in the surf.</p>

<p>&quot;I gave it the coordinates,&quot; Kane grinned.</p>

<p>&quot;And it knows I will turn it into sushi if it misses.&quot;</p>

<p>Kane stepped to the prow.</p>

<p>He flared his Conqueror’s aura outward, letting his presence punch into the gray waves below.</p>

<p>Water began to bulge and boil.</p>

<p>Moments later, the colossal leviathan breached the surface, its eyeless head rising above the whitecaps.</p>

<p>It snapped its jaws, catching the primary iron towing chains without hesitation.</p>

<p>&quot;Cast off!&quot; Kane roared over the surf.</p>

<p>Forty black galleons lurched forward.</p>

<p>They caught the artificial tidal wave generated by the beast’s sweeping tail.</p>

<p>Riding ten feet above the normal waterline, the fleet surged straight toward the shallow western tributary.</p>

<p>Thora threw her head back and laughed, the sound carrying over the crashing waves.</p>

<p>&quot;I pity the Menual scouts today! Imagine watching a mountain of water sail up a river!&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;They won’t have time to watch,&quot; Kane rumbled, his eyes locked on the horizon.</p>

<p>&quot;They will be too busy drowning.&quot;</p>

<p>Kane stood bare-chested at the helm, taking the axe in his hand.</p>

<p>He watched the riverbanks blur past, carrying ruin right to the Emperor’s back door.</p>

<p>Kane watched the muddy riverbanks tear past them, feeling the wild vibration of the leviathan’s wake beneath his boots.</p>

<p>’The Emperor sitting in his little river palace thinks he owns this continent,’ Kane mused, a cold fire spreading through his chest.</p>

<p>’He believes stolen gold and armored elephants make him untouchable. But kings bleed just like anyone else when you swing the axe hard enough.’</p>

<p>He pulled Thora and Seraphine tighter against his ribs, feeling the steady rhythm of their breathing against his skin.</p>

<p>’Once I shatter the Menual crown, I’m done guarding other people’s lines. I won’t just hold a border anymore. I figure it is time I wage war for my own throne. We are going to build an empire from the mud up, and the rest of this world is going to feel our weight.’</p>
