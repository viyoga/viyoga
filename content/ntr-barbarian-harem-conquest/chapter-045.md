<p>Kane wiped a fresh bead of sweat from his forehead, breathing steadily as he lowered Mjoldr to his side.</p>

<p>The courtyard of the inn was relatively quiet now, save for the grunts of his warriors training nearby.</p>

<p>He spent the last two hours drilling through intense combat forms, pushing his battered ribs to their absolute limit.</p>

<p>The crimson weapon pulsed in perfect rhythm with his heartbeat.</p>

<p>Primal Resonance flowed through his veins, making every swing feel faster and much more precise than the last.</p>

<p>Across the dusty yard, Kessa was still running Brak and the Bloodfang vanguard through a grueling set of evasive maneuvers.</p>

<p>She barked corrections, refusing to let any of them rest until they perfected their defensive stances.</p>

<p>Kane watched them for a long moment, feeling a sense of pride swell in his chest. His team was adapting to this hostile environment rapidly, and Kessa was proving to be an effective instructor.</p>

<p>’Time to go,’ Kane thought, sheathing his axe.</p>

<p>He washed the grime from his skin and put on a clean shirt, preparing for the evening meeting Firlia arranged.</p>

<p>Leaving the inn behind, he navigated the busy avenues of the merchant district.</p>

<p>The sun was fully setting now, painting the silver spires in vibrant shades of orange and purple.</p>

<p>Elven citizens filled the cobblestone streets, drinking sweet wine and laughing loudly as they celebrated the ongoing tournament.</p>

<p>Nobody paid any attention to a lone barbarian keeping entirely to the shadows.</p>

<p>Except for a few women who stole glances at him.</p>

<p>Firlia picked a very secure location for this conversation.</p>

<p>The destination was a rooftop garden sitting on top of a tall commercial building. It was elevated enough that anyone watching from street level could not lip-read, providing perfect privacy in a city crawling with royal spies.</p>

<p>Kane climbed a winding stone staircase and pushed open a wooden hatch, stepping out into the night air.</p>

<p>The seer was already there when he arrived.</p>

<p>She sat cross-legged on the outer stone ledge, her feet dangling casually over a five-story drop as if it were a comfortable chair in a tavern.</p>

<p>She didn’t turn her head when the roof hatch closed, but she clearly knew exactly where he stood.</p>

<p>&quot;You aren’t afraid of heights,&quot; Kane observed, walking slowly toward the edge.</p>

<p>&quot;I can’t see them,&quot; she said simply, her voice carrying a melodic tone.</p>

<p>He sat down on a nearby stone bench directly across from her, then took a moment to study her face under the moonlight.</p>

<p>The blindfold was an expensive cut of silk, deliberately chosen and perfectly tied. It wasn’t a disability covering a wound.</p>

<p>&quot;You have been reporting my movements to the prince,&quot; Kane said.</p>

<p>There was no anger or accusation in his voice, just a calm statement of fact.</p>

<p>&quot;Yes,&quot; she agreed without hesitation.</p>

<p>&quot;And warning me simultaneously.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;Also, yes.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;That’s a dangerous game to play in a city like this,&quot; Kane noted, crossing his arms comfortably.</p>

<p>&quot;Playing both sides usually gets you killed by one of them.&quot;</p>

<p>She tilted her head slightly, catching the evening breeze.</p>

<p>&quot;All games are dangerous when you can see how they end.&quot;</p>

<p>Kane leaned forward, resting his elbows on his knees to give her his full attention.</p>

<p>&quot;Then tell me how this one ends.&quot;</p>

<p>She stayed quiet for a long moment, listening to the distant music drifting up from the lively streets below.</p>

<p>&quot;I have seen seventeen versions of this tournament,&quot; she said softly.</p>

<p>&quot;In sixteen of them, the prince wins. His royal bloodline continues without any interruptions. The queen rules for another five hundred years, and the world stays exactly as it is today.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;And the seventeenth?&quot; Kane asked, feeling his pulse quicken with anticipation.</p>

<p>She turned her face directly toward him, even though her eyes could not see.</p>

<p>&quot;You.&quot;</p>

<p>A blue window came to life right in front of Kane’s face.</p>

<p>[Hidden Quest Unlocked: Thread of Fate][The Seer has made her choice]</p>

<p>[Warning: Seers don’t choose losing sides]</p>

<p>Kane processed this new information carefully.</p>

<p>Reading the system notification gave him a strange sense of validation.</p>

<p>The game was literally rigged for the elven prince to win every single time, making Aelrindor the ultimate protagonist of this world.</p>

<p>Kane was the sole anomaly.</p>

<p>’She is betting her entire life on the one timeline where I tear this place apart,’ Kane thought, studying her calm posture.</p>

<p>&quot;What do you want from me in return?&quot; Kane asked, knowing nothing in this city came for free.</p>

<p>&quot;The queen,&quot; she answered, her tone turning completely serious.</p>

<p>&quot;When you take the empire, and you will take it, I want the queen to live.&quot;</p>

<p>Kane stared at her in surprise.</p>

<p>He expected her to ask for gold, a position of power, or maybe safe passage out of the continent.</p>

<p>&quot;Why?&quot;</p>

<p>The seer’s peaceful expression didn’t change at all.</p>

<p>&quot;Because some threads need to survive what’s coming next. She is one of them.&quot;</p>

<p>Reaching a pale hand into her emerald robes, she pulled out a small object and placed it on the stone ledge right between them.</p>

<p>It was a communication crystal.</p>

<p>&quot;This shows the prince everything I tell it to show him,&quot; she explained, pulling her hand back.</p>

<p>&quot;From tonight onward, I will feed him exactly what you want him to see. He will only know the lies we choose to construct together.&quot;</p>

<p>Kane picked up the crystal and turned it over in his calloused fingers.</p>

<p>&quot;You’re handing me control of his intelligence network.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;I’m handing you the next three days,&quot; she corrected him smoothly.</p>

<p>&quot;What you do with them is your choice.&quot;</p>

<p>She stood up effortlessly on the narrow stone ledge, looking impossibly balanced against the strong wind.</p>

<p>&quot;One more thing,&quot; she said, pausing for effect.</p>

<p>&quot;The queen already knows you are here. She has known since you walked through the border gate.&quot;</p>

<p>Before Kane could process that terrifying piece of information, the seer stepped forward off the ledge.</p>

<p>Kane reached forward instinctively to catch her, jumping out of his seat.</p>

<p>His fingers grasped nothing but empty air.</p>

<p>She hadn’t fallen to the bustling street below, and there was no sound of impact. She simply vanished into the night, leaving the stone ledge empty.</p>

<p>Kane stood alone on the rooftop garden, holding the glowing crystal tightly in his fist.</p>

<p>He looked out over the elven capital, feeling the invisible board shifting under his feet.</p>

<p>[Seer: Miria - Alliance Status: Active]</p>

<p>[Intelligence feed to Prince Aelrindor now under Kane’s control]</p>

<p>[Time remaining before quarterfinals: 18 hours]</p>
