<p>The beast stood nearly the height of Grathak, but thinner, built of overlapping plates of dark bark-like hide and exposed ridges of pale, luminous bone.</p>

<p>Antlers of blackened, twisted wood crowned a head that held too many eyes, some set forward like a predator’s, others scattered along the skull in irregular clusters that glowed a faint, sickly green.</p>

<p>Between the plates of hide, veins of the same green light pulsed in slow, deliberate rhythm. Its limbs ended in long, multi-jointed fingers that looked equally suited to tearing or to writing signs in the air.</p>

<p>When it exhaled, the breath carried the smell of wet earth after lightning and something older that had no name in any mortal language.</p>

<p>The system window exploded across Kane’s vision in harsh red.</p>

<p>[Critical Entity Detected]</p>

<p>[Classification: Lesser Manifestation – Forest Guardian Fragment]</p>

<p>[Threat Level: Extreme]</p>

<p>[Divine-adjacent weight confirmed]</p>

<p>[Warning: Standard physical damage severely reduced]</p>

<p>[Warning: Controlled Berserker Output efficiency unknown against this target]</p>

<p>[Recommendation: Survival priority. Observation required before full commitment.]</p>

<p>Kane dismissed the wall of text with a thought and raised Mjoldr.</p>

<p>The manifestation turned its many eyes toward him.</p>

<p>Swoosh.</p>

<p>It didn’t roar and simply advanced, one long, silent stride that closed more distance than should have been possible, and swung a multi-jointed arm in a horizontal arc.</p>

<p>Kane barely got the axe up in time.</p>

<p>Shit.</p>

<p>Bang!</p>

<p>The impact rang through the haft like a struck temple bell and drove him back three full steps, boots scraping stone.</p>

<p>Pain lanced through the still-healing ribs.</p>

<p>He slid aside from the follow-up strike, felt the wind of the claws pass close enough to tug his shirt, and answered with a lateral cut aimed at the glowing veins between the bark plates.</p>

<p>Mjoldr connected. Bark-hide split. Green light flared. But the wound closed almost as fast as it opened, the luminous veins simply rerouting around the damage like water flowing past a stone.</p>

<p>Kane’s eyes narrowed.</p>

<p>&quot;Of course.&quot;</p>

<p>The manifestation pressed forward without pause, both arms working in a fluid, unnatural rhythm that forced Kane to give ground in a wide circle.</p>

<p>He throttled Controlled Berserker Output upward in careful increments: forty percent, fifty, fifty-five, feeling the system struggle to adapt its efficiency against the divine-adjacent resistance.</p>

<p>Every successful parry still sent shock up his arms. Every glancing blow from those multi-jointed hands left bruises that bloomed too fast and too dark.</p>

<p>On the eastern rail, Kessa’s hands gripped the stone hard enough to crack a fingernail.</p>

<p>Griselda’s wings had opened a fraction, ready, though she knew she could not enter the floor.</p>

<p>Near the high seat, Marisa stood motionless, eyes tracking every exchange, the political mask thinned by something that looked almost like fear.</p>

<p>Kane took a long, shallow cut across the outer thigh when he misjudged a feint.</p>

<p>Blood ran.</p>

<p>He answered by stepping inside the next swing and driving Mjoldr’s spike into one of the clustered eyes on the manifestation’s skull.</p>

<p>Shriek.</p>

<p>The eye burst in a spray of green light and black fluid. The manifestation’s first real sound, a layered, resonant shriek that made half the terraces flinch, echoed across the arena.</p>

<p>The wound didn’t close as quickly as the others.</p>

<p>Kane reset his grip, blood dripping down his leg, Controlled Rage humming at a dangerous edge, and watched the many remaining eyes reorient on him with cold, ancient attention.</p>

<p>The manifestation stopped probing.</p>

<p>All of its many eyes locked on Kane at once, the green light inside the bark-hide veins brightening to a painful intensity.</p>

<p>The air temperature plummeted.</p>

<p>Frost formed in jagged lines across the dark stone around its feet. When it moved again, there was no wasted motion, only a sudden, terrifying acceleration that closed the remaining distance in a single bound.</p>

<p>Kane met it with Controlled Berserker Output already climbing past seventy percent.</p>

<p>The first exchange was pure violence.</p>

<p>A multi-jointed hand scythed toward his head. He dropped under it and dragged Mjoldr upward in a two-handed rising cut that sheared through bark-hide and severed two of the glowing fingers in a spray of black fluid and green sparks.</p>

<p>The manifestation didn’t recoil.</p>

<p>It simply rotated the damaged limb and brought the remaining fingers around in a reverse rake that opened three deep, parallel lines across Kane’s chest from shoulder to lower ribs. Blood poured. The system screamed warnings in harsh red.</p>

<p>[Tissue integrity critical – Anterior torso]</p>

<p>[Controlled Berserker Output: 74%]</p>

<p>[Adaptation threshold approaching]</p>

<p>Kane answered by stepping inside the next swing and slamming the spiked pommel of Mjoldr directly into the cluster of eyes on the left side of the skull.</p>

<p>Two more eyes burst.</p>

<p>The layered shriek that followed made several warriors on the lower terraces drop to one knee.</p>

<p>Kane didn’t give it room to recover.</p>

<p>He throttled the Rage higher, eighty percent, the highest he had ever sustained under Controlled output, and launched a relentless series of chopping blows aimed at the same glowing vein junctions he had noticed earlier.</p>

<p>Bark-hide splintered.</p>

<p>Green light flared and sputtered.</p>

<p>Black fluid ran freely down the manifestation’s torso.</p>

<p>Yet every wound still tried to close, the divine-adjacent flesh knitting with stubborn, unnatural speed.</p>

<p>Kane’s own body was paying a steeper price.</p>

<p>A backhand he only partially blocked sent him skidding across the frost-covered stone; the impact reopened the thigh wound from the Second Measure and added a fresh, deep cut along his left hip.</p>

<p>He rolled, came up on pure stubbornness, and spat blood onto the ice.</p>

<p>&quot;Come on then,&quot; he snarled.</p>

<p>&quot;You’ve looked. Now decide.&quot;</p>

<p>The manifestation tilted its antlered head. The remaining eyes narrowed.</p>

<p>For a moment the green light along its veins pulsed in a new, more complex pace, almost as if it were speaking to something farther away and older.</p>

<p>Then it raised both arms and brought them down together.</p>

<p>Kane had no room to avoid it completely.</p>

<p>He crossed Mjoldr above his head and took the full force of the double strike on the axe’s haft.</p>

<p>The impact drove him to one knee.</p>

<p>Stone cracked under his boots.</p>

<p>Pain exploded through both arms and the already damaged ribs.</p>

<p>Something in his left forearm gave with a sharp, sickening pop.</p>

<p>The system flooded his vision with overlapping trauma alerts.</p>

<p>He roared, partly pain, partly the Rage finally slipping closer to its upper edge, and shoved upward with everything he had left.</p>

<p>The manifestation’s arms were forced back just far enough for Kane to drop, spin, and drive Mjoldr in a full horizontal arc at the junction where the luminous spine met the pelvis-like structure beneath the bark plates.</p>

<p>Slash...</p>

<p>The axe head bit deeper than any previous cut. Green light erupted in a blinding sheet. The manifestation’s entire body seized.</p>

<p>A single, resonant tone rolled across the arena, felt more in the bones than heard.</p>

<p>The manifestation staggered as the wound Kane had opened did not close.</p>

<p>Black fluid poured freely. The green veins around the injury flickered and went dark in a widening pattern.</p>
