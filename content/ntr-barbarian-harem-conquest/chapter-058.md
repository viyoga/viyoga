<p>Stepping into the royal banquet hall on the palace’s second floor felt like walking into a painting that cost more than a dozen human cities combined.</p>

<p>Long tables had exotic dishes that cost more per plate than a border village earned in an entire month of hard labor.</p>

<p>Tournament participants from every invited faction filled the seats alongside high-ranking nobles who were specifically assigned to host them for the evening.</p>

<p>Kane’s group sat near the far end, a deliberate slight that screamed: &quot;You are not welcome here.&quot;</p>

<p>Up at the front, the prince’s empty golden chair sat at the head of the table, flanked on both sides by stoic silver wardens.</p>

<p>The pale elf sitting directly across from Kane introduced himself with an arrogant title that took forty seconds to recite, tilting his chin upward as if smelling something rotten.</p>

<p>&quot;I do hope you know how to use proper utensils, savage. We don’t eat with our hands in civilized company.&quot;</p>

<p>Kane took a slow sip of wine, then smiled.</p>

<p>&quot;I’ve been eating just fine without forks lately. But if you keep talking, I might shove that silver one up your ass so you finally have something inside you.&quot;</p>

<p>The elf’s face turned purple.</p>

<p>He snapped his mouth shut and went quiet, choosing to stare at his roasted pheasant rather than risk another verbal spar with the smiling warlord.</p>

<p>Kane looked at the human, who had been harassing Thora for a while.</p>

<p>&quot;A woman like you deserves better company.&quot;</p>

<p>He didn’t stand up from his chair, he didn’t raise his voice, and he did not reach for the crimson axe strapped to his back.</p>

<p>He just looked at the man, letting his dominant aura press down on the space between them.</p>

<p>The human met his cold eyes for approximately four seconds before the suffocating pressure broke his fragile confidence entirely.</p>

<p>&quot;...Great tournament so far,&quot; the mercenary muttered to no one in particular, suddenly finding the tapestries on the far wall incredibly fascinating as he scrambled to look anywhere else.</p>

<p>Watching this silent exchange from across the table, Kessa allowed a slow, satisfied smirk to curl her lips.</p>

<p>&quot;You didn’t even say a single word to him.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;Didn’t need to,&quot; Kane noted, returning his attention to his meat.</p>

<p>The elven women scattered around the hall presented a completely different situation.</p>

<p>Three noble daughters assigned as social hosts for the tournament participants had been watching Kane like hungry wolves since the moment he sat down.</p>

<p>The elven noblewomen were more subtle.</p>

<p>&quot;Is your goblet empty again, Warrior?&quot; the first noblewoman murmured, leaning across the table for the fourth time in half an hour.</p>

<p>&quot;I’ve plenty, thank you,&quot; Kane replied smoothly.</p>

<p>Before he could even take a proper sip, the second woman leaned in from his left.</p>

<p>Her gaze locked firmly on the crimson handle protruding from his back, though her posture suggested she had no idea what she was actually looking at.</p>

<p>&quot;I simply must know,&quot; she breathed, her voice dropping to a whisper that ignored the banquet hall.</p>

<p>&quot;Is the haft made of true ironwood? It looks so... formidable. Does it require a very firm grip to wield properly?&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;It requires tracking a beast for three days and carving the wood yourself.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;Fascinating. You must show me how well those strong hands grip something much thicker than a weapon sometime.&quot;</p>

<p>The third woman proved to be much bolder than her peers.</p>

<p>Waiting for a scheduled course change, she slipped into the empty chair right beside him and leaned close enough that he could smell her expensive floral perfume.</p>

<p>&quot;You fight like someone who’s been watching the game from the shadows for years... and finally decided to play. What do you want from our beautiful city, Kane?&quot;</p>

<p>Kane turned his head, letting his gaze drag slowly down her body before meeting her eyes.</p>

<p>&quot;Everything,&quot; he said, voice low.</p>

<p>&quot;Every proud elven in this hall, every noble title, and every drop of royal blood I can claim. Starting with yours if you keep looking at me like that.&quot;</p>

<p>A genuine, slightly surprised laugh escaped her lips. &quot;Most people who say that only mean the tournament prize.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;I don’t,&quot; Kane promised, letting his gaze drop briefly to her painted lips.</p>

<p>Pausing for a long moment, she seemed to weigh his confidence against his barbarian status.</p>

<p>&quot;My name is Lyssel.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;I know,&quot; Kane said softly.</p>

<p>&quot;You are the third daughter of House Varenthal, and you have been watching me since the soup course.&quot;</p>

<p>Lyssel’s aristocratic composure held firm, but her eyes sharpened with deep interest rather than flashing with embarrassment.</p>

<p>&quot;You noticed.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;I notice most things,&quot; Kane murmured.</p>

<p>He let the heated eye contact hold for exactly one beat longer than socially necessary before finally turning his attention back to the table.</p>

<p>Sitting two seats away, Thora watched this entire flirtatious exchange unfold.</p>

<p>Her expression remained completely pleasant on the surface, yet her eyes carried an entirely possessive edge.</p>

<p>Eating steadily beside them, Rutheus said nothing.</p>

<p>His left hand had stopped practicing the repetitive cup motion and was now simply gripping the clay surface.</p>

<p>Watching the crowded room the way a seasoned veteran watches a potential battlefield, the giant barbarian quietly cataloged every exit, evaluated the armed guards, and mapped out the shifting social patterns.</p>

<p>Up at the front of the hall, the prince’s golden chair remained empty.</p>

<p>Halfway through the decadent banquet, a royal herald stepped forward and announced that Prince Aelrindor sent his deepest regrets.</p>

<p>’The prince is actively avoiding the banquet,’ Kane thought, filing the announcement away in his mental ledger.</p>

<p>Then the energy in the room shifted.</p>

<p>The queen had finally entered.</p>

<p>She stood near the entrance doors and looked across the crowded room with an expression of total authority.</p>

<p>Her piercing eyes found Kane immediately.</p>

<p>She held his gaze across the sea of nobles for exactly three seconds.</p>

<p>Breaking the connection, she looked away and spoke quietly to a nervous functionary hovering right beside her.</p>

<p>Less than two minutes later, a silent palace attendant appeared right at Kane’s elbow, slipping a small, folded piece of parchment onto the table before vanishing back into the crowd.</p>

<p>Kane picked up the note and opened it under the table.</p>

<p>It contained only one line of elegant script and bore no royal signature.</p>

<p>The old district.</p>

<p>Dawn.</p>

<p>Come alone except for the girl.</p>

<p>An invitation.</p>

<p>Kane read the note again, then slowly folded it and tucked it into his pocket.</p>

<p>[Quest Active: Claim Five Elven Wives 1/5]</p>

<p>His eyes drifted across the hall and locked onto the three elegant elven noblewomen who had been shamelessly flirting with him the entire night: Lyssel, Vaelira, and Serelith.</p>

<p>’This should be fun,’ he thought, already imagining their proud faces pressed against the marble while he fucks them.</p>
