<p>The ape hit the ground hard enough to throw leaf litter in a wide ring and lunged for Thora before its back legs had fully settled.</p>

<p>Thora was already moving.</p>

<p>The compact axe rose in a vicious arc, catching the animal across the reaching forearm and opening a dark line through fur and muscle.</p>

<p>Black-tinged blood flicked across the roots.</p>

<p>The ape screamed, high, wrong, the same register as the gate wave, and swung the injured arm anyway, trying to club her off her feet with pure mass.</p>

<p>&quot;Don’t take the arm!&quot; Kane shouted, closing from the side.</p>

<p>&quot;It doesn’t care about the wound—go for joints!&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;I noticed!&quot;</p>

<p>Thora slid inside the swing, let the bulk pass over her shoulder, and drove the axe into the back of the ape’s near knee.</p>

<p>The blade bit shallower than she wanted.</p>

<p>Thickened tissue and corruption plating turned what should have been a crippling cut into a deep annoyance.</p>

<p>The animal pivoted on the other leg and backhanded her hard enough to send her into a root cluster.</p>

<p>Thora hit, rolled, and came up with her shoulder stained and her grip still firm on the axe.</p>

<p>&quot;I am going to skin this thing for a coat,&quot; she snarled.</p>

<p>The ape climbed a low trunk in two pulls and dropped again, this time at Kane.</p>

<p>Mjoldr met the charge in a hard cross-check.</p>

<p>The impact traveled up Kane’s arms and into his cleared ribs; the system flagged load without protest.</p>

<p>He turned the bulk aside, spiked toward the shoulder joint, and felt the point skid on something denser than bone before finding a seam.</p>

<p>The ape shrieked and raked claws across his forearm guard hard enough to score the metal.</p>

<p>&quot;Yellow eyes, same pattern as the gate!&quot; Kane called.</p>

<p>&quot;It will not break from pain. We break structure!&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;On three!&quot;</p>

<p>Thora came back in from the ape’s blind side.</p>

<p>She feinted high, drew the animal’s head toward her, and cut low across the Achilles line of the uninjured leg.</p>

<p>Slash.</p>

<p>This time the blade found deeper purchase.</p>

<p>The ape’s stance shuddered.</p>

<p>It caught her trailing shoulder with a reaching hand and flung her into Kane’s path hard enough that they both had to adjust or go down in a heap.</p>

<p>Kane caught Thora’s weight with his free arm, shoved her back to her feet, and used the ape’s recovery to drive Mjoldr’s edge into the already-spiked shoulder seam.</p>

<p>Black fluid ran.</p>

<p>The animal’s yellow eyes never dimmed.</p>

<p>It opened its mouth and screamed directly into his face, breath hot and sour with corrupted meat.</p>

<p>&quot;Thora—left root! Draw it!&quot;</p>

<p>She understood without further detail.</p>

<p>Thora sprinted along the raised root line, boots finding purchase on bark that ordinary animals would have avoided, and made herself the louder target: axe striking wood, voice sharp, a deliberate invitation.</p>

<p>The ape took it.</p>

<p>Corruption had not erased the simple hunger for the thing that hurt it.</p>

<p>It bounded after her, injured knee hitching, shoulder trailing dark blood, and slammed both fists down where she had been a fraction of a second earlier.</p>

<p>BAM!</p>

<p>The root cracked.</p>

<p>Dirt sprayed.</p>

<p>Thora spun on the far side of the impact and put the compact axe into the animal’s exposed flank twice in quick succession.</p>

<p>&quot;Come on, then! Is this the best the belt has left after spending a whole people on a gate?&quot;</p>

<p>The ape answered by grabbing a fallen limb thicker than her leg and swinging it like a club.</p>

<p>Thora ducked the first arc.</p>

<p>The second caught the axe head and nearly tore the weapon out of her hands.</p>

<p>She held on, teeth bared, and rode the force into a roll that put her under the animal’s chest.</p>

<p>Kane was already there.</p>

<p>He drove Mjoldr up from a low angle into the junction where throat plating met collarbone, using the ape’s own forward weight against it.</p>

<p>The spike sank deep.</p>

<p>The scream that came out of the animal this time had less rage and more surprise.</p>

<p>Black arterial spray painted the roots.</p>

<p>Kane ripped the axe free and stepped out before a flailing arm could take his head off.</p>

<p>&quot;It’s thinning!&quot; he shouted.</p>

<p>&quot;Keep it turning—don’t let it set!&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;I am not its dance partner!&quot; Thora snapped, but she was already moving, forcing the ape to pivot on the bad knee, cutting whenever the yellow eyes lost her for half a breath.</p>

<p>The compact axe was built for this kind of ugly, close work.</p>

<p>She used it without romance, opening lines that did not need to be fatal to be useful.</p>

<p>Blood loss. Joint damage.</p>

<p>The slow arithmetic of a body that refused to feel pain but still needed structure to stand on.</p>

<p>The ape decided to change the arithmetic.</p>

<p>It stopped chasing Thora and went vertical, up the mini-boss tree in a scramble of black fur and pure power, then launched itself outward in a diving tackle aimed at both of them at once.</p>

<p>&quot;The fuck?&quot;</p>

<p>Kane shoved Thora hard to the right and took the left path himself.</p>

<p>The animal hit the ground between them hard enough to shake the clearing.</p>

<p>Claws found Kane’s boot and nearly hauled him under.</p>

<p>He put the butt of Mjoldr into the side of its skull with everything the cleared body would give him.</p>

<p>The impact rang.</p>

<p>Thora came over the ape’s back like a woman climbing a moving wall and buried the compact axe at the base of the skull where the yellow film could not protect the cord.</p>

<p>The blade stuck.</p>

<p>Thora held on for one violent second longer, then lost her footing as the bulk rolled.</p>

<p>&quot;Thora—!&quot;</p>

<p>The ape’s arm caught her in the midsection with a sound like a hammer hitting meat.</p>

<p>&quot;Agh—!&quot;</p>

<p>Thora folded around the blow and flew.</p>

<p>She hit a root cluster hard, bounced, and landed in a heap of leaf litter and dark blood: some of it the ape’s, some of it possibly hers.</p>

<p>The compact axe remained lodged at the base of the animal’s skull for a moment, then tore free as the body thrashed and clattered across the roots out of her reach.</p>
