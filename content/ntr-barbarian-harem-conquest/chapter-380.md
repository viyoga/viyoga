<p>The hammer pulsed in Kane’s grip.</p>

<p>Not the clean red of Mjoldr.</p>

<p>Weight climbed along the haft as if the head had decided the wolf-thing’s skull wasn’t enough and wanted the ground under it as well.</p>

<p>[Weapon recognised: Engraved Hall-Hammer – Root-Binder’s mark]</p>

<p>[Mass modulation: Active]</p>

<p>[Current multiplier: 1.4×]</p>

<p>[Host compatibility: Partial – Rage stabilises grip]</p>

<p>[Warning: Sustained high mass risks joint stress]</p>

<p>&quot;Good enough,&quot; Kane said.</p>

<p>The second wolf-thing came in low, tentacles reaching for his knees.</p>

<p>He let the hammer’s head drop, multiplier climbing on the swing, and the impact didn’t so much strike as erase the front half of the creature into the stone.</p>

<p>Black fluid and cartilage sprayed.</p>

<p>The third tried to flank.</p>

<p>Kane stepped with it, short arc, mass spiking only at the moment of contact so the recovery didn’t tear his shoulder out of the socket.</p>

<p>[Mass multiplier: 2.1× – impact window]</p>

<p>[Mass multiplier: 1.0× – recovery]</p>

<p>The body came apart.</p>

<p>Tentacles slapped the red water and went still.</p>

<p>Silence, except for the flood and his own breathing.</p>

<p>Kane stood on the ledge with Ragnar’s hammer hanging in one hand and the island’s dead arranged below him like an argument he had already lost once.</p>

<p>Red droplets began to fall, a thinner, intermittent spit from the pitch-red sky, each drop leaving a faint smear on skin and stone that smelled of iron and sweet rot.</p>

<p>He moved toward the voice.</p>

<p>The ridge path was broken, half stair, half landslide.</p>

<p>He climbed with the hammer’s head used as a counterweight.</p>

<p>’Misha...’</p>

<p>The island shook.</p>

<p>A short, hard shove from under the stone, as if something vast had turned over in the primary shaft.</p>

<p>A wave climbed the flood behind him and hit the ledge with force enough to take a lesser man off his feet.</p>

<p>Kane drove the hammer down, willed mass into the head, and pinned himself to the rock.</p>

<p>[Mass multiplier: 3.0× – anchored]</p>

<p>[Joint stress: Elevated]</p>

<p>Water sheeted past his legs and fell away.</p>

<p>He did not.</p>

<p>Mist rose from the wet stone.</p>

<p>’Shit.’</p>

<p>It coiled around his boots, his waist, the hammer, and built a silhouette ahead of him on the path: human height, orange suggestion of hair, the set of shoulders he would have known in any layer.</p>

<p>Sira.</p>

<p>Naked.</p>

<p>Or the shape of naked.</p>

<p>Skin pale in the red light, water beading on breasts and the plane of her stomach, orange hair plastered dark to her neck and shoulders, eyes wide and glassy.</p>

<p>She wasn’t supposed to be here.</p>

<p>He left her in Sylvander.</p>

<p>Are the devils already in the Elven Empire?</p>

<p>&quot;Kane...&quot;</p>

<p>Her voice.</p>

<p>The sniff at the end of it.</p>

<p>She walked the last steps without shame in the nakedness, arms coming up, face pressing into his bare chest the way she had after ranks and after debts collected in locked rooms.</p>

<p>&quot;I’m scared...&quot;</p>

<p>His free hand hovered at her back.</p>

<p>The hammer stayed in the other.</p>

<p>She was cold.</p>

<p>The orange hair under his jaw smelled almost like leaf litter and sweat and the manor soap Firlia kept on lists.</p>

<p>&quot;Why did you leave me alone?&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;I didn’t.&quot;</p>

<p>His voice came out rougher than he wanted.</p>

<p>&quot;I was going to come for you. The island—the flood—I came back as soon as the layer would take me.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;...Where is Thora?&quot;</p>

<p>He froze.</p>

<p>The name in her mouth was a hook.</p>

<p>&quot;I will find her.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;...You will?&quot;</p>

<p>She tipped her face up.</p>

<p>Eyes glassy, too bright, the red sky in them.</p>

<p>&quot;Yes?&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;Yes. Where are the others? Misha. Firlia. Kessa. Griselda—&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;They...&quot;</p>

<p>Sira’s mouth widened.</p>

<p>Not a smile.</p>

<p>The jaw went past any human hinge, skin stretching, teeth lengthening into needles, and she bit into the meat of his shoulder with a sound like wet cloth tearing.</p>

<p>&quot;Fuck—!&quot;</p>

<p>Pain was immediate and honest.</p>

<p>Kane shoved.</p>

<p>The thing that had worn Sira’s shape flew back into the mist, orange hair already sliding off a skull that was not a skull, breasts and hips collapsing into a longer, slicker silhouette as the illusion shed itself. The mist thinned on the next red gust.</p>

<p>What stood on the path was not Sira.</p>

<p>A mermaid’s lower half, scaled, coiled, wrong for stone, joined to a torso that had learned human curves only as bait: naked breasts still approximately the right shape, waist still approximately the right narrowness, and from the open mouth a tongue the length of an arm, thick, barbed, dripping black saliva and the blood from his shoulder.</p>

<p>Eyes without irises.</p>

<p>A demon’s idea of a rear-anchor girl, built to say the names that would make a man lower a hammer.</p>

<p>&quot;Kane... don’t leave me... Kane...&quot;</p>

<p>Kane adjusted his grip.</p>

<p>Blood ran down his chest.</p>

<p>&quot;Cute,&quot; he said. &quot;Next time pick a voice that doesn’t ask for Thora first.&quot;</p>

<p>The tongue lashed.</p>

<p>He stepped outside it and brought the hammer across in a rising arc, mass spiking at the last instant.</p>

<p>[Mass multiplier: 2.6× – impact]</p>

<p>The head caught the mermaid-demon in the side and drove it into the broken ridge wall hard enough to crack scale and the stone behind it.</p>

<p>The thing screamed with Sira’s voice and three others layered under it: Misha’s cadence, Firlia’s, a Giant child’s cry from the lower village, and the tentacles that weren’t tentacles, only the tongue splitting, reached for his face.</p>

<p>Kane didn’t give it the face.</p>

<p>He planted, willed mass down into the hammer, and crushed the torso against the rock until the borrowed voices broke into a single wet silence and the scaled coil stopped trying to find his legs.</p>

<p>[Mass multiplier: 1.0× – recovery]</p>

<p>[Host shoulder: Bleeding – recommend binding]</p>

<p>[Local taint signature: Lesser devil-construct – Extinguished]</p>

<p>He stood over the ruin of the bait and spat blood that might have been his own.</p>

<p>&quot;Sira is in Sylvander,&quot; he told the red mist.</p>

<p>No answer except the flood far below and the island’s occasional shudder, as if the primary shaft were still turning over in its sleep.</p>

<p>’This place had turned into hell.’</p>
