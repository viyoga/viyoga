<p>Grathak’s remaining eye flooded completely black.</p>

<p>The densification that had been knitting his ruined ribs and swelling shut the damaged eye cluster suddenly accelerated beyond anything the refined doses had shown before.</p>

<p>Dark fur split along the shoulders and spine.</p>

<p>Bark-like plates of hardened tissue erupted in overlapping layers.</p>

<p>Antler-like ridges of blackened bone pushed from the skull.</p>

<p>Extra eyes, smaller, glowing the same sickly green Kane had seen on the Guardian’s fragment, opened in irregular clusters along the temples and forehead.</p>

<p>The emperor’s frame swelled another half-meter in height and breadth.</p>

<p>When he exhaled, the air itself carried the thick, wet-earth-and-lightning stench of the deep forest’s old power.</p>

<p>The mutation was incomplete, still bound by the pacts that limited full manifestation, but it was enough.</p>

<p>&quot;Heh...&quot;</p>

<p>Kane barely got his good arm up before the transformed fist landed.</p>

<p>BAM!</p>

<p>The impact was no longer densified muscle.</p>

<p>It was divine-adjacent mass. Kane’s forearm broke cleanly.</p>

<p>He was driven into the stone hard enough to crater it a second time.</p>

<p>A follow-up kick caught him in the side and folded him around it; ribs that had already been compromised gave with wet, multiple cracks.</p>

<p>The crowd noise peaked into bloodlust. Pure-blood terraces screamed approval. Southern sections went rigid.</p>

<p>Grathak, now something larger and wronger, reached down, seized Kane by the ruined shoulder and the throat, and lifted him clear of the floor.</p>

<p>Pitch-black and green-glowing eyes stared into Kane’s fading vision.</p>

<p>&quot;I told you,&quot; the mutated voice layered over itself, resonant and final. &quot;You were a worthy human, Kane.&quot;</p>

<p>He smashed him down multiple times until Kane couldn’t move.</p>

<p>Grathak’s massive foot rose, ready to smash the skull against the stone and end it.</p>

<p>High on the rails, Kessa snarled and tried to vault the barrier.</p>

<p>Griselda’s wings snapped open.</p>

<p>Both were immediately swarmed by pure-blood retainers and palace lackeys who had been waiting for exactly that movement.</p>

<p>&quot;Going somewhere?&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;I’ll take the angel to the breeding grounds.&quot;</p>

<p>Steel and claws pinned them before they could reach the floor.</p>

<p>Marisa stood motionless on the northern rail, amber eyes locked on the broken figure in the emperor’s grip, expression unreadable.</p>

<p>Grathak’s foot started down.</p>

<p>Swish.</p>

<p>Mjoldr left the chamber wall half a city away, tore through corridors and open air in a single screaming line of reinforced steel, and struck Grathak in the side of the mutated skull with the force of a siege weapon.</p>

<p>Bang!</p>

<p>Black fluid and green light sprayed.</p>

<p>The emperor staggered, grip loosening.</p>

<p>Kane hit the stone on his knees, vision tunneling, left arm shattered, ribs grinding, blood everywhere.</p>

<p>The system window detonated in harsh, overlapping red.</p>

<p>[Critical trauma threshold exceeded]</p>

<p>[Multiple compound fractures]</p>

<p>[Internal bleeding: severe]</p>

<p>[Emergency override available]</p>

<p>[Full Barbarian Rage – 100 %]</p>

<p>[Time before Exhaustion: 00:01:00]</p>

<p>[Warning: Host body will not survive prolonged output at this level]</p>

<p>Kane’s hand closed around Mjoldr’s haft as the axe rebounded into his grip.</p>

<p>Red aura erupted from his skin in a violent, uncontrolled corona, with killing output, no throttle, and no safety.</p>

<p>The pain receded behind a wall of violence.</p>

<p>He stood on legs that should not have held him, left arm hanging useless, and looked up at the mutated emperor with eyes that reflected the same red light.</p>

<p>&quot;One minute,&quot; Kane rasped, blood running from his mouth.</p>

<p>&quot;That’s all I need.&quot;</p>

<p>Grathak recovered, mutated face a mask of black blood and green-glowing rage.</p>

<p>&quot;You bring the axe after accepting no weapons—&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;I brought the axe after you stopped being the thing that accepted the rules.&quot;</p>

<p>Kane moved.</p>

<p>One hundred percent Rage erased the mass difference for the duration of the timer.</p>

<p>He closed the distance in a blur the mutated Grathak barely tracked, Mjoldr already swinging in a full horizontal arc aimed at the right arm, the one still fully functional.</p>

<p>The reinforced edge, singing with system resonance and output, sheared through bark-hide, densified muscle, and bone in a single catastrophic cut.</p>

<p>The severed arm hit the stone with a heavy sound.</p>

<p>Black fluid and green light poured from the stump.</p>

<p>Grathak’s layered roar shook dust from the highest tiers.</p>

<p>Kane did not stop.</p>

<p>He pivoted, used the emperor’s own staggered momentum, and brought Mjoldr around in a reverse rising cut that took the left arm at the elbow.</p>

<p>The second limb dropped.</p>

<p>The mutated emperor reeled, green-glowing eyes wide with something that was no longer only fury.</p>

<p>Kane stepped in, planted a foot against Grathak’s chest, and kicked the larger body backward hard enough to send it crashing into the already-cratered wall.</p>

<p>The red aura around Kane flickered.</p>

<p>[00:00:47]</p>

<p>Grathak pushed off the wall with the stumps of his arms, bark-plates shifting, green veins rerouting around the catastrophic damage with visible effort.</p>

<p>The incomplete manifestation was trying to adapt, to seal, to keep the body fighting.</p>

<p>Kane gave it no time.</p>

<p>He crossed the floor in three strides and brought Mjoldr down in a two-handed overhead strike aimed at the junction of neck and shoulder.</p>

<p>Grathak twisted; the axe carved a deep, catastrophic line through bark and bone instead of taking the head cleanly.</p>

<p>More black fluid sprayed.</p>

<p>&quot;You—&quot; Grathak’s layered voice was raw. &quot;You were finished—&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;I was finished when you mutated mid-fight and tried to cave my skull in under a foot.&quot;</p>

<p>Kane’s own voice was a blood-choked rasp under the roaring red aura. &quot;Now you lose the arms. Next you lose the rest.&quot;</p>

<p>He pressed.</p>

<p>The mutated emperor had reach and mass even without hands, using shoulders, antler-ridges, and sheer densified bulk like living weapons.</p>

<p>Kane took a glancing blow from an antler that opened a new line across his already-ruined shoulder and kept cutting.</p>

<p>Mjoldr’s spike punched into the vein clusters along Grathak’s side.</p>

<p>The axe head sheared through another section of bark-plating over the ribs.</p>

<p>Each strike landed with the full, suicidal commitment of a one-minute window that would leave the host body broken beyond any ordinary recovery.</p>

<p>[00:00:31]</p>

<p>The crowd had gone past screaming into a stunned, continuous roar.</p>

<p>Kessa and Griselda still fought against the pure-blood swarm holding them, but their eyes never left the center.</p>

<p>Marisa’s gaze remained fixed on Kane, the political mask gone; only calculation and something softer remained.</p>

<p>&quot;Kill him... Kane.&quot;</p>
