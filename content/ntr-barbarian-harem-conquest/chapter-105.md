<p>Gravel kicked up against the stable posts as Kane swung onto the sturdy roan gelding.</p>

<p>Thora caught her own stirrup a split second later, tossing him his leather reins with a sharp flick of her wrist.</p>

<p>&quot;Ride,&quot; she ordered.</p>

<p>They took the narrow cliffside trail at a full, reckless gallop.</p>

<p>Leaving the suffocating air of the Sylvandar manor behind felt like shedding a suit of lead armor.</p>

<p>For two miles, there was only the sound of pounding hooves and the biting ocean wind tearing through their hair.</p>

<p>Thora kept her eyes fixed on the treacherous slope, but her voice cut clean over the galloping roar.</p>

<p>&quot;The mute girl’s wearing a ring,&quot; she called out, leaning into a sharp bend.</p>

<p>&quot;Kessa says her pulse feels like a dying sparrow. We are running an orphanage now, Chief?&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;The ring bought the cedar, Thora,&quot; Kane rumbled back, keeping his center of balance low over the gelding’s mane.</p>

<p>&quot;That’s the entire transaction. Look at the kid, she’s a cracked teacup. The Empress required a signed receipt for forty warships, and I gave her one. It is ink on parchment. It isn’t real.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;And the white knight?&quot; Thora asked.</p>

<p>Turning her head, she gave him a cool, thoroughly observant side-eye.</p>

<p>Her dark braid whipped over her shoulder.</p>

<p>&quot;The whole entryway smelled of winter pine and sweat when you walked in,&quot; Thora noted, her tone dangerously casual.</p>

<p>&quot;You didn’t put your teeth in the Princess, but you definitely put them in someone.&quot;</p>

<p>’No point in spinning noble lies to the one person who actually knows what my blood smells like,’ Kane realized.</p>

<p>Holding his horse steady alongside hers, he met her gaze without a single flinch of hesitation.</p>

<p>&quot;I lost my grip,&quot; Kane admitted bluntly.</p>

<p>&quot;She threw her holy paladin status around inside the guest suite, and I snapped back to put her on the floorboards. It got messy against the plaster. But my belt stayed buckled. I didn’t take her.&quot;</p>

<p>Thora didn’t look away.</p>

<p>Her fingers stayed loose on her reins, her posture dropping its usual sarcastic armor to genuinely test his footing.</p>

<p>&quot;Why?&quot; she asked softly.</p>

<p>Reaching across the dangerous gap between their racing saddles, Kane caught the back of her neck.</p>

<p>His large palm cupped the familiar, corded muscle warmly, his thumb stroking the sensitive line behind her ear.</p>

<p>He applied just enough pressure to let her feel the steady, unshakeable certainty in his grip.</p>

<p>&quot;Because when the high-borns finish their little tea parties, and the imperial gold dries up, I’m still just a gladiator standing in the mud,&quot; Kane rumbled low, his eyes locking onto hers as the horses thundered forward together.</p>

<p>&quot;And you are still the one holding the other axe. The paladin is a sharp sword, Thora. But you are the arm swinging it. I don’t forget where my real house is.&quot;</p>

<p>A slow, satisfied smirk reclaimed her face.</p>

<p>&quot;Was it good?&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;Not even close when compared to you.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;As long as you keep coming back.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;Always.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;Can you save the princess?&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;We’ll try.&quot;</p>

<p>Bumping her boot hard against his stirrup, Thora let out a genuine exhale.</p>

<p>&quot;Good. Because if you started acting like a polite Milfheim lapdog, I would have to drown you myself. Now pace up. Your new fleet is about to get burned.&quot;</p>

<p>Cresting the high grassy bluff overlooking the fjords, the expanse of the gray ocean opened up below.</p>

<p>Tucked securely inside the deep, forested coves sat the forty pristine Milfheim war galleons.</p>

<p>Their white cedar hulls rocked gently in the incoming surf. Standing on the very edge of the precipice, holding the rolled red ambush flags, was Commander Kaelia.</p>

<p>&quot;Report,&quot; Kane instructed, swinging down from his saddle.</p>

<p>Kaelia didn’t waste time on formal bows.</p>

<p>Handing him her brass spyglass, she pointed straight out toward the gray horizon.</p>

<p>Three miles out, twelve clumsy Menual cargo vessels were slowly turning their broadsides toward the empty stone watchtowers.</p>

<p>Mounted on their clunky wooden decks sat crude iron pitch-catapults, their tension arms cranked back to the maximum.</p>

<p>&quot;The sea breeze is pushing inland today,&quot; Kaelia warned, her red brows knitting together as she looked down at her pristine fleet.</p>

<p>&quot;If Morvak’s vanguard gets off a single volley of Greek pitch before our ships clear the narrow cove mouth, the fire will bottleneck us against the rocks. Pristine cedar burns very fast, Lord Kane.&quot;</p>

<p>’She’s right,’ Kane calculated, lowering the spyglass.</p>

<p>’A bottlenecked pitch fire turns my forty-ship armada into a giant floating bonfire before we even hit open water. I need to cheat the physics.’</p>

<p>&quot;Keep your flags rolled for another minute,&quot; Kane told her.</p>

<p>&quot;We are losing the tide—&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;I said wait,&quot; Kane repeated calmly.</p>

<p>Bypassing the standard skill trees, he opened his mental sub-menu and pulled directly from the ten thousand gold coins Queen Sylva had deposited into his name.</p>

<p>[Bloodfang Market]</p>

<p>[Sovereign’s War Cache: Accessed]</p>

<p>[Category: Naval Fortification]</p>

<p>[Selected Item: Abyssal Resin Lacquer (Fleet-Scale)]</p>

<p>[Cost: 5,000 Gold Coins]</p>

<p>[Description: A dense, sub-oceanic alchemical sap. Instantly coats linked wooden vessels in an obsidian, non-conductive skin.]</p>

<p>[Grants 100% immunity to mundane thermal combustion for 12 Hours.]</p>

<p>’Five thousand gold just to paint some cedar,’ Kane smirked internally. ’Worth every single coin.’</p>

<p>Selecting confirm sent a strange, oily shhhk sound rippling across the quiet coves below.</p>

<p>Kaelia gasped, taking a sudden step back from the cliff’s edge.</p>

<p>Starting at the waterline of the flagship, a wave of glossy, obsidian black bloomed across the white Milfheim cedar.</p>

<p>It spread like creeping ink, racing across the linked mooring ropes and swallowing all forty vessels in a matter of seconds.</p>

<p>The elegant matriarchal galleons transformed instantly into pitch-black, unburnable ocean predators, absorbing the midday light like open voids.</p>

<p>&quot;What in the Goddess’s name did you just do to my wood?&quot; Kaelia breathed, her pale hands trembling against her sword hilt.</p>

<p>Reaching over his shoulder, Kane drew Mjoldr.</p>

<p>&quot;I fixed the math,&quot; Kane smiled, a ruthless spark dancing in his eyes.</p>

<p>&quot;Drop the red flag, Commander. Time to eat.&quot;</p>

<p>Crimson silk fluttered against the gray sky as Kaelia released the pole.</p>

<p>They marched into the sea.</p>
