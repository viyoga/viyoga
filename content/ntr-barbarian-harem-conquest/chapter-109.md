<p>Artillery shells shrieked across the bay, ripping the morning fog to ribbons.</p>

<p>Dozens of explosive rounds rained down from the rotating ironclad turrets.</p>

<p>Because Kaelia had fanned their forty unburnable hulls into a loose, shifting crescent, most of the volley plunged harmlessly into the ocean, sending geysers of boiling white surf high into the air.</p>

<p>One solitary shell found its mark, striking the prow of Thora’s ship dead center.</p>

<p>’Standard cedar would splinter into toothpicks,’ Kane thought, watching the fiery orange bloom completely swallow his first mate’s bow.</p>

<p>Instead of shattering, the glossy black resin drank the thermal output greedily.</p>

<p>Concussive pressure redirected outward in a harmless ripple, leaving the obsidian wood untouched.</p>

<p>Thora strode right out of the lingering smoke, laughing savagely as she leveled her weapon toward the nearest enemy paddle-wheel.</p>

<p>&quot;Is that the best the Chancellor’s navy can do?&quot; she roared over the surf.</p>

<p>&quot;My grandmother hits harder with her soup spoon!&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;Keep your prow angled!&quot; Kane shouted back from his own helm, grinning at her uncompromising lack of fear.</p>

<p>&quot;We aren’t close enough to board them yet!&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;Then tell your rowers to put their backs into it, Chief! I assume they want to earn their keep before lunch!&quot;</p>

<p>Down inside the lamplit cargo hold, Misha’s seraphic network sparked to life.</p>

<p>Her voice broadcasted simultaneously through the linked Milfheim communication crystals mounted beside every helm, layered with an eerie, dual-tone resonance.</p>

<p>&quot;Three ironclads turning starboard at your two o’clock,&quot; the Princess stated, her tone entirely devoid of panic.</p>

<p>&quot;Their boiler pressure is peaking. Target their midship exhaust grates.&quot;</p>

<p>’I reckon Grieselda turned the kid into the most dangerous oracle on this continent,’ Kane mused, thoroughly enjoying the supreme tactical advantage.</p>

<p>Leaning over the railing, he bellowed vector adjustments down to his forward ballista crews.</p>

<p>&quot;Pivot thirty degrees starboard! Load the Sylvandar alchemical charges! Aim dead for the steam grates!&quot;</p>

<p>Brass harpoons punched cleanly through the enemy vents.</p>

<p>Three localized, deafening explosions rocked the shallow shoreline as boilers ruptured from the inside out.</p>

<p>Superheated vapor swallowed the Menual gunners.</p>

<p>[Tactical Hit: Critical Boiler Rupture]</p>

<p>[Enemy Vessels Destroyed: 14/100]</p>

<p>&quot;Good hits!&quot; Kaelia called out from the secondary tier, furiously adjusting her naval charts.</p>

<p>&quot;They are bottlenecking themselves against the ruined Elven piers! We have them trapped!&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;Then we close the jaws entirely,&quot; Kane ordered, drawing his broad-daggers.</p>

<p>&quot;Full ramming speed. Break their rudders.&quot;</p>

<p>Obsidian prows crushed through external steering gears with deafening crunches.</p>

<p>Unburnable cedar pinned the ironclads against the ruined stone docks.</p>

<p>Kane vaulted over the gunwale directly onto the deckboards of a Menual mortar-vessel, leaving Mjoldr strapped securely across his shoulders.</p>

<p>[Skill Activated: Berserker Rage Lv2]</p>

<p>Bypassing the armored mercenaries entirely, he drove his daggers straight into the exterior steam-lines running along the railing.</p>

<p>Pressurized mist sprayed across the deckboards.</p>

<p>Soldiers dropped their halberds instantly, shrieking as the vapor trapped inside their breastplates began cooking them alive.</p>

<p>Thora cleared the railing right beside him.</p>

<p>Her weapon took the heads of two stumbling officers in a single, fluid scissor-motion.</p>

<p>Bumping her blood-spattered hip hard against Kane’s thigh amidst the screaming, she flashed him a feral, breathless grin.</p>

<p>&quot;Bet you never cooked dinner this fast in the fighting pits, Chief!&quot; she yelled, entirely in her element.</p>

<p>&quot;I usually prefer my meat roasted, not steamed!&quot; Kane laughed.</p>

<p>Wrapping his calloused arm completely around her corded waist, he pulled her back flush against his chest as another steam vent blew nearby.</p>

<p>He didn’t care about the combat raging ten paces away.</p>

<p>Reaching down, he tilted her chin up and captured her lips in a highly vocal kiss.</p>

<p>Thora tasted of salt and blood, leaning her solid curves back into his thighs with unapologetic hunger.</p>

<p>She tangled her fingers into his red hair, tugging just hard enough to make a deep rumble vibrate in his throat.</p>

<p>’She fits me like a customized gauntlet,’ Kane thought.’</p>

<p>&quot;You are going to get us killed acting like this during a boarding action,&quot; Thora said against his mouth, her breathing ragged and fast.</p>

<p>&quot;I figure a real warrior can multitask,&quot; Kane murmured, biting her lower lip playfully before letting her spin out of his bicep to parry an incoming spear thrust.</p>

<p>&quot;Keep your side of the deck clear! I want this vessel secured before the smoke lifts!&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;Always commanding,&quot; Thora smirked, burying her left axe into a mercenary’s ribs.</p>

<p>&quot;Just make sure you save some stamina for my bunk tonight!&quot;</p>

<p>Across the churning ocean gap, Seraphine claimed the elevated command bridge of an adjacent ironclad.</p>

<p>Surrounded by falling soot and burning rigging, she finally let go of her strict paladin restraint, unleashing the unchained output of her imperial authority.</p>

<p>A blinding, twenty-foot crescent of pure silver mana detached from her broadsword.</p>

<p>The brilliant arc sliced horizontally through the iron-plated smokestacks, completely cleaving the enemy captain’s wheel and the captain holding it, neatly in half.</p>

<p>’She’s making up for twenty years of polite manners,’ Kane reckoned, watching the severed iron smokestack crash into the surf.</p>

<p>’Genuinely lethal.’</p>

<p>[Enemy Vessels Destroyed: 41/100]</p>

<p>Standing amidst the ruined bridge, Seraphine lowered her glowing blade.</p>

<p>She slowly licked a stray bead of mercenary blood from her upper lip, her chest heaving beneath her leather harness as she looked across the burning wreckage to find Kane’s eyes.</p>

<p>Even separated by twenty paces of open water and screaming soldiers, the aggressive tension between them sparked like an open furnace.</p>

<p>&quot;Enjoying yourself over there, First Blade?&quot; Kane called out conversationally, casually sidestepping a frantic sword thrust and burying his dagger into the attacker’s throat.</p>

<p>&quot;I figure these men needed a proper lesson in matriarchal law!&quot; Seraphine yelled back. Her silver irises swam in a heated haze.</p>

<p>&quot;Are you going to stand around admiring me, or are you going to help me sink the rest of this vanguard?&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;I’m on my way,&quot; Kane promised, his pulse hammering as he looked at his two partners systematically dismantling an empire’s vanguard.</p>

<p>&quot;Try to leave a few alive for me.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;No guarantees!&quot; Thora shouted back, taking another officer off his boots.</p>

<p>&quot;Forward ironclad attempting to ram Commander Kaelia’s port side,&quot; Misha’s dual-tone voice chimed back through the crystal, completely unfazed by the flirtation echoing across the command channels.</p>

<p>&quot;Distance: eighty yards. Strike their primary paddle housing now.&quot;</p>
