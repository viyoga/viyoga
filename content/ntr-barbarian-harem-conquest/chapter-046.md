<p>Kane walked down the staircase from the rooftop garden where Lyria had just vanished into thin air.</p>

<p>His mind was running at full speed because the seer had dropped entirely too much information on him at once.</p>

<p>He needed a moment to process the seventeen different versions of the tournament and the terrifying fact that the elven queen already knew he was here.</p>

<p>Kane took a slow breath to calm his racing thoughts before reaching the ground floor.</p>

<p>Firlia was waiting quietly at the bottom of the stairwell.</p>

<p>She wasn’t supposed to be there as she had only arranged the meeting location and was meant to leave immediately.</p>

<p>But she stayed behind.</p>

<p>&quot;She spoke to you,&quot; Firlia stated.</p>

<p>&quot;She did,&quot; Kane confirmed, stopping on the last step.</p>

<p>&quot;Was it bad?&quot;</p>

<p>Kane looked at her for a long moment.</p>

<p>&quot;It was complicated.&quot;</p>

<p>Firlia nodded slowly.</p>

<p>She understood complicated perfectly well, as she had been living inside a complicated nightmare for the last three years.</p>

<p>&quot;The prince sent another message today,&quot; she said quietly.</p>

<p>She fell into step right beside him as they walked out into the empty merchant district.</p>

<p>The stalls were all closed for the night, leaving the streets illuminated only by floating magic lanterns.</p>

<p>&quot;He wants physical confirmation that you are weakened. Lothburk’s report was not convincing enough for him. He wants someone from inside your trusted circle to verify the injuries.&quot;</p>

<p>Kane stopped walking and turned to look at her.</p>

<p>&quot;And you told him you would get close enough to confirm,&quot; Kane said, putting the pieces together.</p>

<p>&quot;Yes.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;So you need something to report back.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;Yes.&quot;</p>

<p>The silence stretching between them felt very different from their previous encounters.</p>

<p>There was no hidden dagger this time, and no assassination plan waited underneath the quiet conversation.</p>

<p>They were just two people standing in an empty street, choosing what happened next.</p>

<p>&quot;Something convincing,&quot; Kane said, keeping his voice level.</p>

<p>Firlia met his eyes without flinching.</p>

<p>Her noble composure remained completely intact as three years of practice under the prince’s thumb made her very good at keeping her face neutral.</p>

<p>But her slender fingers gripped the leather strap of her satchel just slightly tighter than necessary.</p>

<p>&quot;I know exactly what I’m suggesting,&quot; she said.</p>

<p>&quot;Do you?&quot; Kane asked, stepping closer to her.</p>

<p>He wasn’t being threatening. He just moved close enough that she had to make a conscious choice about whether to step back or hold her ground.</p>

<p>&quot;Because I want to hear you say it aloud.&quot;</p>

<p>Firlia held her ground perfectly.</p>

<p>&quot;I need to come back to him smelling like you,&quot; she said, her voice staying surprisingly steady.</p>

<p>&quot;He will believe I got close enough to assess the damage. He will believe you are distracted and weakened, so he will stop looking for other ways to confirm it.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;And your boyfriend?&quot; Kane asked softly.</p>

<p>Something flickered behind her red eyes.</p>

<p>It was fast and tightly controlled, disappearing before it fully surfaced.</p>

<p>&quot;He doesn’t know I’m here,&quot; she replied.</p>

<p>Kane studied her beautiful face for a long moment, reading the tension hidden just beneath her skin.</p>

<p>&quot;I need you to understand something,&quot; Kane said quietly.</p>

<p>&quot;This is not a simple transaction. When this tournament ends, however it ends, you don’t go back to the prince’s leash. You come out the other side with a real choice about what your life looks like.&quot;</p>

<p>Firlia stared at him.</p>

<p>For the first time since he met her in the registration hall, her composure was not a practiced performance.</p>

<p>It was just a stunned silence while she processed something completely genuine.</p>

<p>&quot;You are offering me a way out,&quot; she whispered.</p>

<p>&quot;I’m telling you the door is already open,&quot; Kane smiled warmly.</p>

<p>&quot;You just have to decide to walk through it.&quot;</p>

<p>She reached up and unfastened the top metal clasp of her traveling cloak.</p>

<p>They found a private room in a quiet inn just two streets away from Kane’s own lodgings.</p>

<p>Firlia had the brass key ready in her pocket, which told him she had planned this scenario before the seer meeting even happened.</p>

<p>The room was small but comfortable.</p>

<p>A single oil lamp provided a warm glow, illuminating a large bed with black cotton sheets and a window overlooking the empty avenue.</p>

<p>Firlia set her satchel on a small table and turned to face him.</p>

<p>Her green hair flowed down.</p>

<p>Kane noticed this for the very first time as she always pinned it up in elegant, tight braids every previous time he had seen her.</p>

<p>Left down, it reached well past her shoulders.</p>

<p>&quot;You’ve been watching me since registration,&quot; Kane said, sitting down casually on the edge of the bed.</p>

<p>&quot;You have been hard to ignore,&quot; Firlia replied.</p>

<p>There was something almost dry and sarcastic in her tone.</p>

<p>It was the very first hint of personality slipping past her noble mask.</p>

<p>&quot;Is that a compliment?&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;It’s an observation,&quot; she said. She paused for a moment, letting a small smile touch her lips.</p>

<p>&quot;It might also be a compliment.&quot;</p>

<p>Kane returned the smile and reached out one hand.</p>

<p>She crossed the small room without any hesitation and took it.</p>

<p>They existed in the same space for a moment without anyone forcing an agenda or performing a role.</p>

<p>Firlia sat right beside him on the edge of the mattress.</p>

<p>&quot;I won’t hold back,&quot; Kane warned her one last time.</p>

<p>Then she leaned forward and kissed him.</p>

<p>Her kiss was controlled and slow.</p>

<p>She kissed him like she did everything else, using complete composure and clear intention.</p>

<p>Her soft hand rested flat against his bare chest, feeling his steady heartbeat rather than gripping his shirt for leverage.</p>

<p>Kane let her lead the interaction for much longer than he normally would.</p>

<p>He read her body language the same way he read Lothburk in the fighting pit.</p>

<p>What he found surprised him.</p>

<p>Underneath all her rigid control, there was something fragile that had not been touched gently in a very long time.</p>

<p>The captain she truly loved existed only in secret shadows.</p>

<p>Even her own genuine feelings had been a staged performance for three long years.</p>

<p>Kane closed the distance.</p>

<p>Firlia’s mouth opened to speak, but the words died when his hands seized the front of her robes and tore them apart.</p>

<p>Buttons scattered like seeds across the floor.</p>

<p>She gasped, but Kane already had her pinned, the heavy wood at her back.</p>

<p>&quot;Aahh...&quot;</p>

<p>Firlia’s head hit the door with a soft thud.</p>

<p>’She’s already wet.’</p>

<p>&quot;You came here to spy,&quot; Kane muttered against her skin, his voice a low rumble in the quiet room.</p>

<p>His fingers curled, searching for that spot inside her.</p>

<p>He found it as he noticed her breath hitched.</p>

<p>&quot;What are you telling the prince?&quot;</p>

<p>She tried to answer, to lie, but his fingers worked her, and her voice broke into a whimper.</p>

<p>&quot;That you’re... weak,&quot; she managed, the words strained.</p>
