<p>The screen was too bright.</p>

<p>Leon squinted against the gold-on-black seal until his eyes watered.</p>

<p>The PC fans spun down into an ordinary hum.</p>

<p>Empty cans at the edge of the desk.</p>

<p>The ghost of pizza.</p>

<p>His hands on the keyboard were pale and soft, no callus where Mjoldr’s haft had lived, no burn from a fragment, no black-gold under the nails.</p>

<p>On the side monitor, the livestream chat was a vertical stampede.</p>

<p>[YourMom: Holy shit!!!!!]</p>

<p>[ILoveDicks: Yo developers suck his dick]</p>

<p>[Booblord: He actually did it]</p>

<p>[MobyHater: Bro has no life outside this game]</p>

<p>[RaidOrDie: 100% ON MAX DIFF I CAN’T]</p>

<p>[PixelQueen: MobyDick69 is built different]</p>

<p>[LootGoblin: say, something king]</p>

<p>[YourMom: SAY SOMETHING]</p>

<p>He didn’t.</p>

<p>He couldn’t.</p>

<p>The cursor blinked at the end of a thank-you that didn’t mention red rain or a hinge walking into the sea.</p>

<p>He had come back to the exact place the whole thing had started: a chair, a headset, a room that smelled of recycled air and neglect.</p>

<p>Months of greatest difficulty.</p>

<p>The final devil.</p>

<p>A completion percentage most of the player base would never see.</p>

<p>Chat wanted a reaction.</p>

<p>Sponsors would want a highlight reel.</p>

<p>His throat held nothing that would fit in a donation alert.</p>

<p>He moved the cursor anyway.</p>

<p>The forest map. The old belt. Spawn-adjacent dirt he could have drawn from memory with his eyes closed. He clicked a save point near the first camp, the one after Lain, before the road east, before any of the courts had names that hurt.</p>

<p>The main character loaded in: Blonde hair, early gear, no Mjoldr-tier weapon yet. The camera followed as the avatar stumbled into a ring of tents and firelight.</p>

<p>Pixels. Name tags floating in clean UI font above heads that the engine had built from a budget of polygons and a writer’s loot of adjectives.</p>

<p>Lain’s tag.</p>

<p>Thora’s tag.</p>

<p>Sira’s.</p>

<p>Others.</p>

<p>Leon’s throat closed.</p>

<p>He could still see them. Not the tags, the faces.</p>

<p>Thora’s scars in real light.</p>

<p>Sira’s orange hair full of leaf litter on a wall walk.</p>

<p>Misha’s single tear on a flooding path.</p>

<p>On the monitor, they were assets with collision boxes.</p>

<p>In his head, they were the weight that had gone out of his arms in a wave the patch notes would never list.</p>

<p>[System Initialising...]</p>

<p>[10%]</p>

<p>[25%]</p>

<p>[75%]</p>

<p>He let out a long breath and waited for the white room, the red blink, the Devil’s Mirror, anything that would admit the extraction had not been clean.</p>

<p>Nothing happened.</p>

<p>The progress bar finished into ordinary quiet.</p>

<p>On the game screen, a modest menu unfolded over the campfire glow:</p>

<p>[New Feature: Save Point]</p>

<p>[Resume +]</p>

<p>[Continue Where You Left Off: Arrival of the Devils]</p>

<p>Three lines.</p>

<p>Leon looked at the options until the words stopped meaning structure and started meaning accusation.</p>

<p>Then he shut the machine down.</p>

<p>The monitors went black.</p>

<p>Chat died mid-emote.</p>

<p>The room’s only light became the streetlamp spill through the blinds.</p>

<p>&quot;They are better off without me.&quot;</p>

<p>He said it to the dark so it would exist outside his skull.</p>

<p>Outside, the city was indecently normal.</p>

<p>He walked until his streamer legs complained, then bought food that had never been a sealed ration or a Giant’s banquet haunch: real bread, real coffee, something green because a corner of his brain still ran Firlia’s lists.</p>

<p>He ate on a low wall and listened to traffic.</p>

<p>No system tag estimating devil arrival in days or twenty minutes.</p>

<p>The devils had kicked him out.</p>

<p>That was the shape of it, if he had to put a shape on it.</p>

<p>100%.</p>

<p>Thank you for playing.</p>

<p>MobyDick69 could go worry about sponsorship decks and upload schedules and the fact that his channel had just spiked harder than any raid night in its history.</p>

<p>He could live a life that fit in a chair and a calendar.</p>

<p>That was what he thought.</p>

<p>For three days he answered emails with his hands only.</p>

<p>He recorded a faceless &quot;thanks for the sub&quot; that didn’t mention Blood Wild by name.</p>

<p>He slept in a bed that didn’t contain Thora or Misha. He told himself the pixels on a hard drive were safer than a man who had used protection as a leash.</p>

<p>At night the dream came anyway.</p>

<p>Not the whole island.</p>

<p>Only the moment in the dark water: Thora’s fingers on his jaw, the words close enough that the surge could not quite steal them.</p>

<p>I love you.</p>

<p>He woke up with his hands closed on empty air and the ordinary ceiling above him, and sat on the edge of the mattress until the shaking passed.</p>

<p>&quot;I don’t deserve them.&quot;</p>

<p>The room didn’t argue.</p>

<p>He didn’t either.</p>

<p>But...</p>

<p>&quot;I’m obliged. Give them a life without devils.&quot;</p>

<p>It was a Kane sentence wearing Leon’s mouth.</p>

<p>Obligation without ownership.</p>

<p>A debt that didn’t get to call itself a harem.</p>

<p>He stood, crossed the apartment, and sat in the chair again.</p>

<p>The PC took a moment to wake. The desktop icon for Blood Wild Chronicles sat where he had left it, innocent as any other time-sink.</p>

<p>He didn’t open the stream overlay.</p>

<p>Chat could riot in silence for one more night.</p>

<p>The game loaded to the same quiet menu.</p>

<p>[New Feature: Save Point]</p>

<p>[Resume +]</p>

<p>[Continue Where You Left Off: Arrival of the Devils]</p>

<p>His cursor hung over Resume + long enough for the monitor’s light to make his eyes water again.</p>

<p>Then he shifted it down.</p>

<p>[Arrival of the Devils]</p>

<p>He clicked.</p>

<p>The screen simply darkened, then opened on a sky that was already the wrong colour, bruise-red at the edges, thunder stacked where no weather system should have been.</p>

<p>[New Game Plus conditions not required.]</p>

<p>[Host signature recognised.]</p>

<p>[Layer re-entry: authorised under obligation flag.]</p>

<p>[Warning: Prior completion does not grant immunity.]</p>

<p>Leon’s hands settled on the keys.</p>

<p>Already aching for a haft that was not there yet.</p>

<p>&quot;I’m not coming back to claim,&quot; he said to the empty room, to the dream, to the women who had only ever been name tags on a corporate server and had somehow said words that were never said to him.</p>

<p>&quot;I won’t make decisions for them again. I would let them speak for themselves. Whether they want me or not, I would still defeat the devils and create a peaceful world for them.&quot;</p>

<p>[Welcome To Blood Wild Chronicles, Player MobyDick69]</p>
