<p>Tucking Misha into the bed, Kane pulled the sheets up to her chin.</p>

<p>He placed a kiss on her forehead, waiting a moment to ensure her breathing leveled out into a peaceful rhythm.</p>

<p>Satisfied she was resting, he slipped quietly out of the door.</p>

<p>’She needs the quiet tonight,’ Kane figured, stepping into the corridor.</p>

<p>’My new bedroom is a bit too crowded for someone with a fragile heart.’</p>

<p>Navigating the winding halls, Kane eventually pushed open the doors to Morvak’s former master suite.</p>

<p>This room belonged to him now.</p>

<p>Thora and Sira lay tangled together in the expansive sheets, sleeping peacefully.</p>

<p>Moonlight spilled across their bare shoulders, highlighting the battle scars they wore like badges of honor.</p>

<p>Kane didn’t wake them immediately.</p>

<p>Taking a seat on the edge of the mattress, he rested his forearms on his knees, just watching them breathe.</p>

<p>’I dragged them from a struggling camp all the way to an imperial palace,’ he reflected, a rare wave of introspection washing over him.</p>

<p>’They fought every battle, bled in every skirmish, and never asked for a single coin in return.’</p>

<p>Thora stirred.</p>

<p>She blinked groggily, her eyes struggling to adjust to the dim moonlight.</p>

<p>Spotting his silhouette, she reached out, dragging her knuckles against his waist to pull him closer.</p>

<p>&quot;What is it, Chief?&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;Thora...,&quot; Kane started, keeping his tone unusually low.</p>

<p>&quot;Hmm?&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;I’m sorry.&quot;</p>

<p>Her face blanked.</p>

<p>Any trace of sleepy fog vanished from her expression instantly.</p>

<p>Beside her, Sira shifted, drawn awake by the sudden tension flooding the room.</p>

<p>The tracker rubbed her eyes, sitting up slowly and clutching the blanket.</p>

<p>Kane looked at both of them, his expression stripped of its usual conqueror’s arrogance.</p>

<p>&quot;I’m sorry.&quot;</p>

<p>Sira blinked, a blush spreading across her cheeks at the unexpected vulnerability.</p>

<p>&quot;What happened, Chief? Did some southern noble fuck you senseless? Why are you apologizing in the middle of the night?&quot;</p>

<p>Kane let out a tired laugh, but his gaze quickly shifted back into something far more serious.</p>

<p>&quot;When I fought Lain at your camp and killed him,&quot; Kane began, staring down at his hands.</p>

<p>&quot;Instead of just taking over the warriors and giving orders, I forced myself on you two.&quot;</p>

<p>He paused, letting the words hang in the air.</p>

<p>&quot;I treated you like spoils of war,&quot; he mumbled.</p>

<p>&quot;It wasn’t right. I didn’t care about your feelings at all. I only focused on my own advantage. I wanted to break Lain’s memory and establish dominance, and I used both of you to do it.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;Kane...&quot;</p>

<p>Thora whispered, sitting up fully.</p>

<p>Sira moved over, wrapping her arm around.</p>

<p>&quot;It’s okay,&quot; Thora assured him.</p>

<p>&quot;It’s not,&quot; Kane countered, his jaw tightening with guilt.</p>

<p>&quot;I don’t even know if the effect of you being so attached to me is a side effect of my aura, or if it is real feelings. Sometimes I wonder if my power manipulated your loyalty. I keep thinking about how I can make you two normal again. Free of whatever influence I forced on you.&quot;</p>

<p>Sira rolled her eyes, though her smile was incredibly fond.</p>

<p>She leaned her head against his shoulder.</p>

<p>&quot;You don’t have to make us normal, Kane. We are content. We are exactly where we want to be.&quot;</p>

<p>Thora traced a faded scar on his chest, her touch gentle but firm.</p>

<p>&quot;You were a feral brute back then. We were surviving a brutal, unforgiving camp. You claimed us, yes, but you also kept us alive. You fed us, fought right beside us in the blood, and brought us to a literal throne. Do not insult us by thinking we are just mindless puppets caught in a spell.&quot;</p>

<p>Kane reached out, placing one warm palm against Thora’s cheek and the other against Sira’s face.</p>

<p>’They are stronger than I give them credit for,’ he realized.</p>

<p>Thora traced a faded scar on his chest, her touch gentle but firm.</p>

<p>&quot;I don’t think my forgiveness magically erases what you did,&quot; she said, her eyes meeting his directly.</p>

<p>&quot;But that was just how our life was before you got there. We were always commodities to men like Lain.&quot;</p>

<p>Sira nodded, resting her chin against Kane’s shoulder.</p>

<p>&quot;You just didn’t abandon us after you won.&quot;</p>

<p>’They understand the reality of the mud we crawled out of,’ Kane realized, feeling a tight knot finally loosen in his chest.</p>

<p>’They survived the worst of it, and they still chose to stay.’</p>

<p>&quot;I won’t do it again,&quot; Kane promised, his voice steady.</p>

<p>&quot;You two are important to me. You are my partners, not just prizes.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;We know,&quot; Sira smiled, turning her head to kiss his palm.</p>

<p>&quot;Now get in bed. You are letting all the cold air under the sheets, and I am freezing.&quot;</p>

<p>Kane kicked off his boots, shedding the last of his leather armor.</p>

<p>He lay back in the center of the mattress.</p>

<p>Sira immediately tucked her head under his chin, resting happily against his chest.</p>

<p>Thora curled up on his other side, draping a protective arm across his stomach.</p>

<p>Staring up at the ceiling, sleep refused to claim him.</p>

<p>[New Quest: Stabilize the Realm]</p>

<p>The text hovered in the corners of his vision, a constant reminder of the burdens he just inherited.</p>

<p>’This is the priority right now,’ Kane plotted, listening to the synchronized breathing of the women beside him.</p>

<p>’I cannot expand our borders if the capital is rotting from the inside. But before I can truly lock this territory down, I have a different problem to solve.’</p>

<p>His thoughts shifted to his wounded angel.</p>

<p>’I need to cure Grieselda,’ he planned, mapping out the next few days in his head.</p>

<p>’Once she is healed, I have to make her tell me everything she knows about those angels. She holds pieces of the puzzle I am missing.’</p>

<p>The looming threat of the Elven borders crept into his mind.</p>

<p>Queen Sylva played a dangerous game, and she possessed resources Kane couldn’t fully quantify yet.</p>

<p>’If that Elven Queen actually has the power to involve gods, I cannot just sit here counting stolen silver,’ Kane deduced, his ambition flaring hot and bright in the quiet room.</p>

<p>’A mortal army is useless against divine interference. I need to level up. I need to be better.’</p>
