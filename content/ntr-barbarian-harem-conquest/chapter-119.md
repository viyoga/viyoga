<p>Kneeling beside his salt-crusted boots, Sarah stared at her husband’s severed head.</p>

<p>The once-dominant seductress realized her intricate political web had just been shattered by violence.</p>

<p>Keeping one large hand locked firmly around the back of her neck, Kane rested the axe blade gently against her cheek.</p>

<p>’These aristocrats think a fancy title protects their necks,’ Kane mused, feeling her rapid pulse flutter against his palm.</p>

<p>’I figure a quick reality check sets the new hierarchy.’</p>

<p>&quot;Your empire’s little game ends here,&quot; Kane ordered softly, tilting her chin up to meet his gaze.</p>

<p>&quot;Pour.&quot;</p>

<p>Her fingers shook wildly.</p>

<p>She filled it with red vintage, spilling a few drops onto the carpet before offering it up.</p>

<p>Kane drank deeply, enjoying the flavor.</p>

<p>Instead of handing the glass back, he tilted his wrist, pouring the remaining wine directly over her head.</p>

<p>Red liquid cascaded through her hair, streaking down her face and soaking into her cleavage.</p>

<p>Gasps echoed from the kneeling aristocrats.</p>

<p>Kane let out a savage laugh that rattled the chandeliers.</p>

<p>A hot pulse of rage began building in his veins.</p>

<p>Slaughtering Morvak and the guard captain barely scratched his itch.</p>

<p>Old battle-lust roared to life, the same familiar fury that earned him his legendary reputation on the northern borders.</p>

<p>Veins pressed against his skin. His muscles swelled with unspent energy.</p>

<p>Yanking Sarah upward by her wine-soaked hair, Kane dragged her right onto his lap.</p>

<p>He shifted his grip, forcing her to sit straddling his thigh while he addressed the trembling court.</p>

<p>&quot;Anyone who moves dies,&quot; Kane projected, his voice carrying lethal certainty.</p>

<p>&quot;Anyone who speaks without permission loses their tongue. This empire now belongs to Kane of the North... and his wife from Milfheim.&quot;</p>

<p>Pointing Mjoldr toward two surviving royal guards shivering near the doorway, Kane issued his next command.</p>

<p>&quot;Take Misha to show the empire.&quot;</p>

<p>The guards scrambled over themselves to obey, sprinting down the corridor to retrieve his seraphic partner.</p>

<p>’Let the whole continent know who really sits on the top,’ Kane reckoned, leaning back into the chair.</p>

<p>Sitting astride his leg, Sarah refused to remain a broken trophy.</p>

<p>Wine dripped from her eyelashes, mixing with the scent of blood, but her survival instincts kicked into overdrive.</p>

<p>Fear wouldn’t save her position.</p>

<p>Submission meant exile or death.</p>

<p>Seduction, however, offered a path back to the top.</p>

<p>’She’s recalibrating,’ Kane noticed, watching her amber eyes sharpen.</p>

<p>’Most widows would weep for a week. She is already plotting her next ascension.’</p>

<p>Tracing a manicured nail down the center of his scarred chest, Sarah leaned her wine-soaked cleavage against his leather harness.</p>

<p>She tipped her head back, offering him a sultry smile.</p>

<p>&quot;A northern king needs someone who understands the southern treasury,&quot; Sarah whispered. Her voice steadied as she ground her hips subtly against his thigh.</p>

<p>&quot;You took the throne by force. But holding an empire requires finesse. I can give you both.&quot;</p>

<p>Kane arched an eyebrow, keeping his grip firm on her waist.</p>

<p>&quot;I figure you change loyalties faster than a mercenary, widow. Your husband is still bleeding on the rug.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;Loyalty belongs to the strongest man in the room,&quot; Sarah countered, biting her lower lip as her fingers tangled into his red hair.</p>

<p>&quot;My late husband was a fool who hid behind iron ships and weak plots. You are a true sovereign. Rule beside me, and I will hand you the keys to every vault in the capital.&quot;</p>

<p>Before Kane could answer, Sarah turned her head toward the cowering nobles.</p>

<p>&quot;Keep your faces glued to the floor!&quot; Sarah commanded, her voice cracking like a whip.</p>

<p>&quot;Anyone who looks up will answer to me!&quot;</p>

<p>Nobles buried their faces into the carpets, terrified by her sudden resurgence.</p>

<p>She still commanded their fear, weaponizing it to prove her worth to her new master.</p>

<p>&quot;Let us negotiate the terms properly,&quot; Sarah purred, tracing his jawline.</p>

<p>&quot;The imperial bedchamber is just down the hall. I will show you precisely what southern hospitality feels like.&quot;</p>

<p>Footsteps echoed near the entrance.</p>

<p>Misha walked into the hall.</p>

<p>Golden rings burned in her eyes as she surveyed the bloody aisle, the headless corpses, and the seductive widow straddling Kane’s lap.</p>

<p>’She always reads the room instantly,’ Kane smirked, catching his oracle’s eye.</p>

<p>&quot;The Emperor’s treasury ledgers are secured in his study, husband,&quot; Misha reported calmly, folding her hands in front of her waist.</p>

<p>&quot;The remaining guards have already surrendered.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;Our new captive offered to give me a personal tour of the royal chambers,&quot; Kane rumbled, running a calloused thumb over Sarah’s wine-stained cheek.</p>

<p>&quot;I figure she wants to prove her usefulness.&quot;</p>

<p>Misha tilted her head, analyzing Sarah with seraphic indifference.</p>

<p>&quot;Her pride is dangerous. Break it thoroughly, Kane. We need a compliant treasurer, not a rival queen.&quot;</p>

<p>Giving him a single, approving nod, Misha turned her attention toward counting the cowering aristocrats.</p>

<p>&quot;You have your permission,&quot; Sarah whispered, her breath hot against his neck.</p>

<p>&quot;Now let me show you why I ran this city.&quot;</p>

<p>Kane stood up, hauling Sarah effortlessly into his arms.</p>

<p>She wrapped her legs around his waist, clinging to his broad shoulders.</p>

<p>&quot;Point the way,&quot; Kane ordered, tossing Mjoldr onto the throne cushion.</p>

<p>&quot;But remember who holds the leash.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;Past the tapestries. The guards know better than to interrupt.&quot;</p>

<p>’She thinks a few sweet words and some skin will put her right back in charge. I will let her try, then I will show her the difference between a political scheme and a conqueror.’</p>

<p>Gold-leafed mirrors lined the walls.</p>

<p>Tossing her onto the center of the bed, Kane unbuckled his sword belt. It hit the floor with a loud thud.</p>

<p>Sarah didn’t scramble backward.</p>

<p>Crawling across the sheets on all fours, she looked up at him with ambitious eyes.</p>

<p>She peeled the wine-soaked crimson gown off her shoulders, letting it pool around her waist.</p>

<p>&quot;You took my husband’s head,&quot; Sarah breathed, arching her back to display her impressive curves.</p>

<p>&quot;I expect you to replace what you broke.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;I think you talk too much for a conquered woman,&quot; Kane replied, pulling off his leather harness.</p>

<p>&quot;You aren’t negotiating terms anymore, Sarah. You are auditioning.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;Then watch me perform,&quot; she challenged, reaching out to pull him down onto the bed.</p>

<p>Kane leaned over her, catching her wrists in one hand and pinning them above her head.</p>

<p>&quot;I set the pace here. Try to scheme your way out of this.&quot;</p>
