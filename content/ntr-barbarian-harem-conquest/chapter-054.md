<p>Morning arrived at the waystation without a single trace of awkwardness.</p>

<p>Everything remained exactly as it was the previous day, while Vaela maintained her professional distance with flawless precision.</p>

<p>Ensuring the other guards noticed nothing unusual proved she was remarkably good at her job.</p>

<p>Kane didn’t acknowledge the passionate, sweat-slicked night they shared against the tree, and she offered no secret smiles either.</p>

<p>This mutual understanding kept their travel arrangements smooth.</p>

<p>While packing his gear near the wagons, Kane paused to consider a strange anomaly in his system interface.</p>

<p>He fucked Firlia’s brains out back in Sylvandar, pinning the noblewoman to the bed until she screamed his name and milked him dry, yet the elven conquest quest never formally triggered.</p>

<p>Vaela, on the other hand, had already been claimed by the system for a one-night stand.</p>

<p>’Why did the system stay so quiet, Firlia?’ Kane mused, swinging Mjoldr onto his back.</p>

<p>’She is a high-ranking noble, while Vaela is an elite royal military. Maybe the system requires a much deeper emotional bond to register a true conquest, or maybe I just need to fuck Firlia senseless again when I see her to test if a second round forces the milestone to pop.’</p>

<p>It was a very appealing experiment, so he fully intended to bend her over a desk again to find out.</p>

<p>Only if her boyfriend allowed it, of course.</p>

<p>The final stretch of travel brought the royal caravan to Elaryndal right before midday.</p>

<p>[System: New Location Detected]</p>

<p>[Location: Elaryndal, Imperial Capital]</p>

<p>[Thornwall perimeter reached]</p>

<p>[Passive scan initiating. All party members are being assessed]</p>

<p>[Target Kane: Unknown divine energy signature detected]</p>

<p>[Target Grieselda: Known signature detected]</p>

<p>[Target Mjoldr: Fallen energy signature detected]</p>

<p>[Note: Three separate alerts sent to the palace in the thirty seconds it takes to cross the Thornwall]</p>

<p>[The queen knows you are here before your horse clears the gate]</p>

<p>Kane heard Elaryndal long before he saw the famous skyline.</p>

<p>A low sound vibrated in the air, but it wasn’t quite standard.</p>

<p>It felt much more like a steady pressure pushing against his eardrums. The Thornwall generated a magical frequency that sat right at the edge of physical perception, creating the distinct sensation of being assessed by an entity that possessed no eyes.</p>

<p>Then the majestic city finally appeared over the horizon.</p>

<p>Kane had seen Sylvandar, but he quickly realized he was completely wrong about understanding elven architecture.</p>

<p>Elaryndal grew upward in a way that defied basic construction logic. Graceful white towers reached so high they disappeared into the passing cloud cover.</p>

<p>Slender bridges connected buildings at dizzying heights that made Sylvandar’s walkways look like quaint garden paths.</p>

<p>The oldest structures sat near the very center, towering far above the rest because five centuries of continuous magical growth made them look less built and more naturally evolved.</p>

<p>The queen’s central palace sat right in the middle of the sprawling metropolis.</p>

<p>It stood taller than everything else in the empire, wrapped in a silver light that definitely didn’t come from the sun.</p>

<p>[System: Elaryndal tactical assessment initiated]</p>

<p>[Population: 340,000]</p>

<p>[Military: Imperial Guard 12,000 active. Silver Wardens 300 elite]</p>

<p>[Strategic vulnerability assessment: Incomplete]</p>

<p>[Warning: Forty percent of city infrastructure has no game data equivalent]</p>

<p>[Note: You are in unmapped territory. Your meta-knowledge ends here.]</p>

<p>That final line landed harder than anything the interface had said in weeks.</p>

<p>’Unmapped territory,’ Kane thought, staring up at the shining silver palace with narrowed eyes.</p>

<p>’The game never actually got this far, as the developers never finished the capital expansion. The tournament wasn’t postponed in that timeline. Everything from this point forward is real, so I am flying blind.’</p>

<p>The Imperial Gate consisted of two giant trees grown together to form a living archway over five centuries.</p>

<p>This wooden structure opened smoothly for the approaching caravan, parting like a giant curtain.</p>

<p>As Kane passed through the invisible Thornwall, the strange pressure in his ears intensified briefly before releasing completely.</p>

<p>Grieselda rode quietly beside him, but the intricate mark on her chest pulsed once with a strong, visible light as she crossed the threshold.</p>

<p>Then the rune faded back to its steady, familiar glow.</p>

<p>Inside the city walls, the environment was scaled perfectly for people who had lived there for centuries and never needed to account for anything outside their own refined standards.</p>

<p>The paved streets were flawlessly maintained without a single cracked stone in sight. That’s it.</p>

<p>Elven citizens stopped their daily routines to stare at Kane’s group as they marched down the wide avenue.</p>

<p>They didn’t display theatrical, aristocratic contempt like the snobby nobles in the border city.</p>

<p>Their reaction was unfiltered bewilderment.</p>

<p>A rugged barbarian carrying a glowing crimson axe, walking right through the Imperial Gate escorted by royal guards, was simply not a sight this sheltered population had ever processed before.</p>

<p>A small elven girl pointed a delicate finger at Mjoldr and said something quickly to her mother.</p>

<p>The elegant woman pulled the child’s hand down immediately, but she couldn’t stop herself from looking at the intimidating weapon too.</p>

<p>Rutheus rode tall beside Kane, scanning the rooftops with veteran paranoia, while Kessa kept her eyes locked forward to ignore the stares that reminded her of her unwanted beastwoman status.</p>

<p>The escort finally led them to the Honored Guest District near the prestigious inner ring.</p>

<p>Their assigned residence was a beautiful four-floor tower featuring a private enclosed courtyard and luxurious furnishings.</p>

<p>It looked extremely comfortable on the surface, offering soft beds and running water.</p>

<p>Walking through the front doors, Kane scanned the elegant living area and spotted the surveillance measures almost instantly.</p>

<p>The quiet rooms were monitored from at least three different magical angles that Kane identified in his first five minutes of occupying the space.</p>

<p>Small scrying crystals sat disguised as decorative glass ornaments on the high shelves.</p>

<p>Thora walked in behind him, her eyes darting around the ceiling.</p>

<p>&quot;The magic in this room is suffocating,&quot; she whispered, stepping close to his side.</p>

<p>&quot;They’re watching us.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;Let them watch. Every single word spoken inside these walls is a performance from this moment onward.&quot;</p>
