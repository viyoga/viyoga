<p>“You want to kill me!” Simon shouted, jumping off the bed.</p>
<p>A terrifying surge of power burst from him as he tried to channel his mana, but the next moment, it vanished.</p>
<p>“This…?” Simon froze.</p>
<p>He tried again but nothing happened.</p>
<p>“What’s going on?” he screamed with panic seeping into his voice.</p>
<p>“Keke,” Ethan chuckled quietly.</p>
<p>“Why do you think I am sitting here wasting time?”</p>
<p>Simon sniffed instinctively, then froze.</p>
<p>A faint, sweet scent lingered in the air.</p>
<p>“Ascorbia Power Gas,” he whispered in horror.</p>
<p>His heart trembled.</p>
<p>It was a potential B-rank herb. When burned, it released a gas that disrupted mana control. Prolonged exposure caused hallucinations and severe drowsiness.</p>
<p>It was often used in high-grade cigars or interrogations.</p>
<p>On any normal day, Simon would have noticed it instantly. But Ethan’s pressure and constant provocation had forced his focus elsewhere.</p>
<p>In such a situation,he failed to notice it.</p>
<p>“Where did you get that?” Simon demanded.</p>
<p>“I found it in the chamber after I killed that guy,” Ethan replied casually.</p>
<p>“I see…” Simon muttered.</p>
<p>Then he laughed.</p>
<p>“Hahahahaha!”</p>
<p>“Idiot… Kekke!”</p>
<p>“Then why aren’t you wearing a gas mask?” Simon snarled. “Don’t you know this affects you too unless you’re a poison master?”</p>
<p>With a roar, Simon charged and punched Ethan in the chest.</p>
<p>Simon was sure of one thing. In pure physical strength, no one could match him.</p>
<p>Better to gamble on brute force than wait helplessly.</p>
<p>BANG!</p>
<p>His fist slammed into Ethan’s chest, but instead of crushing flesh, pain exploded through Simon’s knuckles.</p>
<p>Crack.</p>
<p>“Why is your body so hard?” Simon staggered back, clutching his hand.</p>
<p>He then looked up in disbelief.”I’ve been screaming at the top of my lungs,” Simon shouted, “so why isn’t anyone coming?”</p>
<p>“Because I sealed this place with a sound barrier using magic,” Ethan replied calmly.</p>
<p>“Sound barrier?” Simon froze. “How are you using spells?”</p>
<p>“Aren’t you a Knight or this is an artifact.”</p>
<p>Ethan looked at Simon change behaviour.</p>
<p>’He is talking a lot.Might be the effect of medicine.</p>
<p>“Because I am a mage too.”Ethan smiled.</p>
<p>“Surprised?”</p>
<p>“No..I have seen Magic Swordsman.I am rather curious that you aren’t affected.”</p>
<p>Ethan opened his coat slightly and revealed a badge glowing faintly with holy light.</p>
<p>“The Sun God Insignia, from the Goddess of Light.”</p>
<p>Simon felt a chill crawl up his spine.</p>
<p>“You… how do you have this?”</p>
<p>“That’s not important,” Ethan said, stepping forward.</p>
<p>“What matters is what happens to you now.”</p>
<p>Ethan’s eyes turned icy cold.</p>
<p>The temperature in the room dropped sharply, frost forming along the walls.</p>
<p>“You dared to harm my family even after repeated warnings,” Ethan said, his voice low and steady.</p>
<p>“I was not joking when I sent you that message.”</p>
<p>Simon’s pupils shrank as black shadows seemed to crawl around Ethan’s form.</p>
<p>His mind started to fracture.He saw visions of chains, blades, and endless torment.</p>
<p>“Enough!”Simon pulled out a hidden blade and tried to attack but Ethan grabbed it and twisted his wrist at an unnatural angel.</p>
<p>“Everything I am today exists because of my family,” Ethan continued. “Anyone who dares to reach for them will be crushed.”</p>
<p>“AHHHHHHHHHH!”</p>
<p>A horrific scream tore through the chamber, echoing against the prison walls.</p>
<p>But no one came.</p>
<p>Simon’s mind shattered under fear, shock, and despair as the darkness closed in.And in that sealed chamber, only silence remained.</p>
<p>Ethan stood over Simon, his expression cold and unreadable.</p>
<p>Simon lay on the ground, his body twisted, breath coming in broken gasps.</p>
<p>Every attempt to move sent fresh waves of pain through him.The fear in his eyes was no longer arrogance or hatred.It was pure terror.</p>
<p>Ethan crouched down and picked up a thin book from beside the chair.Hall had handed it to him earlier with a strange look.</p>
<p>The title was simple and unsettling.</p>
<p>[100 Ways to Torture and Break a Person.]</p>
<p>Ethan flipped it open slowly.</p>
<p>“Oh,” Ethan muttered, scanning a page.”So hitting this point sends a lightning shock through the nerves.”</p>
<p>He pressed down lightly with his fingers.</p>
<p>Simon’s body jerked violently as a scream tore out of him.</p>
<p>“AAAAHHHH!”</p>
<p>Ethan tilted his head, watching carefully.</p>
<p>“Hm, it really does paralyze the body for a few moments.”</p>
<p>Simon’s limbs locked up, muscles spasming uncontrollably.His mouth opened, but only broken sounds came out.Tears streamed down his face.</p>
<p>Ethan turned another page.”Oh, and hitting just below that point disrupts breathing.”</p>
<p>He applied pressure again, precise and controlled.Simon choked, coughing violently as panic flooded his eyes.</p>
<p>“STOP… PLEASE…!” he begged hoarsely.</p>
<p>Ethan paused, observing him like a specimen.</p>
<p>“So this causes shock without killing the subject,” he said calmly.</p>
<p>“Interesting.”</p>
<p>Simon’s screams echoed, then faded into weak whimpers.His body trembled nonstop.</p>
<p>Another page was flipped.</p>
<p>“This one breaks mental resistance,” Ethan read softly.</p>
<p>He followed the instruction, tapping a nerve cluster.Simon screamed again, louder than before, mind unraveling.Memories, fear, and pain crushed him all at once.</p>
<p>Ethan straightened up slowly.He looked down at Simon, who was twitching helplessly at his feet.</p>
<p>Blood trickled from the corner of Simon’s mouth as his eyes lost focus.</p>
<p>The monster who once laughed now looked broken and completely shattered.</p>
<p>Ethan exhaled slowly and rubbed his forehead.</p>
<p>“Fuck…” he muttered.</p>
<p>“I really became an inhumane bastard, didn’t I?”</p>
<p>He stared at his own hands, then back at Simon.There was no satisfaction in his eyes, only emptiness.</p>
<p>He closed the book and set it aside.</p>
<p>“Ah, I wanted to experiment more,” Ethan said quietly.”But it seems you are done for.”</p>
<p>Simon could no longer respond, his body barely reacting.Only shallow breaths proved he was still alive.</p>
<p>Ethan turned away, his voice cold as ice.</p>
<p>“This is what happens when you touch my family.”</p>
<p>“I do not leave potential dangers alive,” he said quietly. “So goodbye.”</p>
<p>“I hope you have eyes to see Mount Tai.”</p>
<p>Leaving those words,Ethan’s image blurred and disappeared from the shadows.</p>
