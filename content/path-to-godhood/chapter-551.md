<p>The moment Ethan stepped in, he saw a huge ancient hall, and tall pillars stood on both sides while each of them was carved with detailed runes that faintly glowed under the soft light.</p>

<p>The entire place carried a suffocating aura, and the moment he crossed the threshold, he felt something press down on him heavily, and it was not just pressure but something that directly affected his power.</p>

<p>His body stiffened and his senses dropped.</p>

<p>His strength… vanished.</p>

<p>For a split second, Ethan felt as if he had been thrown back to the time when he was nothing more than an ordinary human without any awakening.</p>

<p>His heart skipped a beat.A faint horror rose in his chest.</p>

<p>He tried to channel his power instinctively, but nothing responded, and it felt like everything inside him had gone silent.</p>

<p>“Don’t worry. This is due to the law. Anyone outside the elf race coming here will face this.”</p>

<p>The calm voice echoed through the hall.</p>

<p>Ethan paused and slowly lifted his head.</p>

<p>At the center of the hall, on a raised platform, a throne stood, and resting on it was a woman, sitting in a relaxed posture while holding a scepter loosely in her hand.</p>

<p>She was an elf.</p>

<p>Her long blonde hair flowed smoothly down her shoulders, and it shone softly under the light while her skin looked flawless and pale. Her figure was mature and well-shaped, and there was a natural elegance in the way she carried herself.</p>

<p>She wore a beautifully crafted dress that was decorated with jewels and fine lace, and the fabric flowed gracefully around her body while highlighting her presence.</p>

<p>At the edge of her waist, a golden coronet was crafted, and two letters were clearly engraved on it.</p>

<p>E.B.</p>

<p>Ethan’s eyes widened slightly.</p>

<p>He recognized it instantly.</p>

<p>That dress.It was the same one he had designed and auctioned.</p>

<p>The one that was sold for fifty million gold coins.</p>

<p>“This…” Ethan muttered under his breath.</p>

<p>The Elf Queen looked at him and smiled faintly.</p>

<p>“How does it look?” she asked.</p>

<p>“I hope my appearance won’t disappoint you.”</p>

<p>Ethan blinked once, and then he quickly steadied himself while lowering his head slightly.</p>

<p>“Of course, how can they be,” he murmured.</p>

<p>He took a step forward and gave a respectful bow.</p>

<p>“Pardon my words, but you look as gorgeous as an angel descending into the world,” he said, and his tone remained polite.</p>

<p>After that, he straightened slightly and greeted her properly.</p>

<p>“Greetings, Queen of Elves, Lyria.”</p>

<p>The queen raised her hand lightly.</p>

<p>“Be at ease,” she said.</p>

<p>“I did not call you here to pressure you. I called you because I was interested in you.”</p>

<p>Ethan remained silent and listened carefully.</p>

<p>“I have been curious about the human who crafted such a godly dress,” she continued. “Also, I have heard that the man who crafted all this is someone personally sculpted by God.”</p>

<p>She tilted her chin slightly and observed Ethan carefully.</p>

<p>Her eyes moved across him slowly.</p>

<p>White hair.</p>

<p>Blue, sharp eyes.</p>

<p>A defined masculine face.</p>

<p>A tall and balanced figure that carried both strength and a refined look and most importantly,he was Divine Knight.</p>

<p>And still so young.A faint flicker of surprise passed through her eyes.This was not ordinary.</p>

<p>This was something rare.</p>

<p>Something people would fight over.</p>

<p>“Now that I have seen you, I can say that the rumors about you do not do justice,” she said calmly.</p>

<p>Ethan did not know what to say at that moment, and he felt both surprised and slightly uneasy, but there was also a hint of satisfaction in being acknowledged like this.</p>

<p>“I did not know that rumors about me even reached here,” he said. “I hope this did not spoil your ears.”</p>

<p>The queen gave a soft smile.</p>

<p>“How can it?” she replied.</p>

<p>“With such a look, how can a woman who loves beauty let go of the chance to admire such an appearance?”</p>

<p>Her tone was calm, but her words were direct.</p>

<p>She leaned slightly back on her throne and looked at him with interest.</p>

<p>“In fact,” she added, “I am even prepared to take you as my Prince Consort if you are willing.”</p>

<p>The words fell simply.</p>

<p>But they carried weight.</p>

<p>And for a moment the entire hall felt even quieter.</p>

<p>The hall remained quiet for a moment after the queen’s words, and the weight of what she had just said did not fade easily.</p>

<p>Ethan stood there, and although his expression remained composed, his mind was not calm, and he took a slow breath before speaking.</p>

<p>“…Your Majesty,” he said, and his tone was respectful but firm, “I am honored by your offer, but I have to refuse.”</p>

<p>There was no hesitation in his voice.</p>

<p>Queen Lyria watched him for a few seconds, and her eyes studied him carefully as if she was measuring his resolve.</p>

<p>“You are refusing without even considering?” she asked, and her tone remained calm.</p>

<p>Ethan shook his head slightly.</p>

<p>“I have responsibilities,” he said. “And there are people waiting for me, so I cannot leave everything behind.”</p>

<p>His answer was simple, and there was no attempt to soften it.</p>

<p>The queen leaned back slightly, and then she let out a faint breath before shaking her head slowly.</p>

<p>“…I see,” she said.</p>

<p>There was no anger in her expression, but there was something else, and it felt like quiet understanding.</p>

<p>Then her gaze sharpened slightly.</p>

<p>“Your days in Arcadia won’t be good once you return,” she said.</p>

<p>Ethan’s brows furrowed immediately as he heard that.</p>

<p>“…What do you mean?” he asked.</p>

<p>Queen Lyria did not answer directly, and instead she gave a faint, mysterious smile while her fingers lightly tapped the armrest of her throne.</p>

<p>“Humans are interesting,” she said slowly. “Especially when power, fear, and blame mix together.”</p>

<p>Ethan’s eyes narrowed slightly, and he felt a faint uneasiness rise again.</p>

<p>“Is this about the war?” he asked.</p>

<p>The queen did not confirm it, but she did not deny it either.</p>

<p>“Just be careful,” she said.</p>

<p>“That is all I will say.”</p>
