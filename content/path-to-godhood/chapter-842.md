<p>Ethan knew that while keeping the other titles might not be possible, preserving Osantra’s noble status was somewhat achievable. At this moment, he truly lamented the complications that came with relationships.</p>

<p>’Next time I marry, I won’t marry into any noble family. It’s such a big hassle.’</p>

<p>Ethan had already made up his mind, silently praying in his heart that he wouldn’t have to deal with such troublesome matters again.</p>

<p>After the meeting, Count Alaric handed the matter over to the Empress. After hearing the details, she gave her decision and stated that Ethan could appoint anyone as long as they had the strength to maintain their title.</p>

<p>That was an absolute decision that couldn’t be overturned.</p>

<p>And with this, House Salvos and House Fenwick were purged from the nobility.</p>

<p>Ethan was truly helpless about the matter. He had tried to preserve their positions, but the reality of Eldorian’s noble system left him with little choice. Thankfully, both families were able to understand the situation and didn’t hold any resentment toward him.</p>

<p>In the end, they decided to turn toward business and become merchants. With the support of the Blanks’ branches, they would be able to establish themselves without relying on their noble titles.</p>

<p>As for Duke Osantra, his title was reduced to Count, while House Lancelot was elevated to a Marquess household. Although the arrangements weren’t exactly what everyone had hoped for, they were accepted as the final decision.</p>

<p>As for the remaining three quotas, Ethan directly refused to use them. He decided to return them to the Empress and let her decide how they should be used.</p>

<p>With the matter of the appointments finally settled, Ethan could shift his attention to other affairs.</p>

<p>Following his promotion, discussions with House Osantra also began in earnest. Both sides started making arrangements, and after several rounds of discussion, a wedding date was finally selected.</p>

<p>The wedding would also serve as a celebration for the Blanks’ elevation to a Duchy, making it an important occasion for the entire household. With preparations beginning, the Blanks were about to enter a new Chapter of their history.</p>

<p>The day of the wedding had finally arrived.</p>

<p>The entire Duchy of Blanks was filled with a festive atmosphere. Decorations adorned the streets, and the flags of House Blanks and House Osantra fluttered above the buildings. Nobles from different regions had arrived to attend the wedding, while merchants and commoners gathered along the streets, hoping to catch a glimpse of the grand occasion.</p>

<p>For the people of Blanks, this wasn’t just a wedding. It was also a celebration of their Lord’s elevation to the position of Duke. The once small County of Blanks had now become a Duchy, and its influence had grown to a level that few could have imagined in the past.</p>

<p>Inside the grand hall, servants and attendants moved around busily as they made the final preparations. The long tables were covered with fine cloth, and all kinds of delicacies were being arranged for the guests. The hall itself was decorated with flowers and golden ornaments, giving it a festive yet elegant appearance.</p>

<p>Meanwhile, Ethan stood inside a room while his attendants helped him get ready.</p>

<p>He wore a formal black outfit embroidered with golden patterns. A crimson cloak rested over his shoulders, and the Blanks family crest was displayed prominently on his chest. Although the clothes were different from his usual attire, they suited his position as the newly appointed Duke.</p>

<p>Ethan glanced at himself in the mirror and adjusted his collar.</p>

<p>’Getting married is really troublesome.’</p>

<p>He let out a small sigh, but the corners of his lips soon lifted.</p>

<p>Despite all the trouble surrounding the wedding, he couldn’t deny that he was looking forward to it. Regalina was someone he had chosen to spend his life with, and today marked the beginning of their life together.</p>

<p>Just as he was about to leave, the door opened, and one of his attendants entered.</p>

<p>"My Lord, everything is ready. The ceremony is about to begin."</p>

<p>Ethan nodded and walked out of the room.</p>

<p>As he made his way toward the wedding hall, he could hear the faint sounds of music and the voices of the guests. Several servants bowed as he passed, while the guards standing along the corridor straightened their backs and saluted him.</p>

<p>When Ethan entered the hall, the surrounding conversations gradually quieted down.</p>

<p>The guests turned their attention toward him. Several nobles stood up and bowed respectfully, while others watched him with curious expressions. For many of them, this was their first time seeing the newly appointed Duke of Blanks in person.</p>

<p>Ethan paid little attention to their gazes and walked toward the front of the hall.</p>

<p>At the far end, beneath a beautifully decorated arch, stood the officiant who would preside over the ceremony. Beside him was a place reserved for the bride.</p>

<p>Ethan took his position and waited.</p>

<p>A few moments later, the music changed.</p>

<p>The large doors at the other end of the hall slowly opened, and the guests turned their attention toward the entrance.</p>

<p>Regalina appeared.</p>

<p>She wore a beautiful white wedding gown that flowed gracefully to the floor. Fine embroidery adorned the fabric, and delicate ornaments rested in her hair. Her appearance immediately drew the attention of everyone present, but she seemed unaffected by the countless gazes directed at her.</p>

<p>For a brief moment, her eyes met Ethan’s.</p>

<p>A faint smile appeared on her face.</p>

<p>Ethan returned the smile as he watched her walk toward him. Although he had attended countless important gatherings and faced many dangerous situations, he found himself feeling a little nervous at that moment.</p>

<p>’Why am I getting nervous now?’</p>

<p>He quietly shook his head and waited for her to approach.</p>

<p>Regalina finally arrived beside him. She glanced at Ethan, and a small smile appeared on her lips.</p>

<p>"You look good today."</p>

<p>Ethan chuckled softly.</p>

<p>"So do you."</p>

<p>Regalina’s cheeks turned slightly red, but she quickly composed herself and stood beside him.</p>

<p>The officiant stepped forward and began the ceremony. His voice echoed through the hall as he spoke about the meaning of marriage, the responsibilities of a husband and wife, and the bond that would connect House Blanks and House Osantra.</p>

<p>The guests listened quietly, and even the nobles who had come with political intentions remained silent.</p>

<p>After the opening words, the officiant turned toward Ethan.</p>

<p>"Lord Ethan Blanks, do you take Lady Regalina Osantra as your wife, to stand beside her through the days ahead and cherish the bond you share?"</p>

<p>Ethan looked at Regalina. She was watching him with a calm expression, though he could notice the slight tension in her fingers.</p>

<p>"I do."</p>
