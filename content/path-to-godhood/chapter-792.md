<p>Ethan had barely reached the fourth pillar when several attacks suddenly came toward him from different directions. He quickly moved aside and avoided the first attack, then another one followed immediately. Ethan twisted his body and narrowly avoided it, but before he could move again, several strange tentacle-like things suddenly shot out from the side.</p>
<p>His eyes widened.</p>
<p>The tentacles moved too quickly, and one of them slammed into his body before he could avoid it.</p>
<p>BANG!</p>
<p>Ethan was struck and thrown to the side. Before he could regain his balance, a hoard of attacks came toward him from every direction. Blades, magic attacks and powerful waves of energy crashed into him one after another.</p>
<p>BOOM! BOOM! BOOM!</p>
<p>The attacks continued to mount on Ethan, and the force sent him flying through the underground chamber. He smashed through the debris and disappeared into the dust.</p>
<p>The four family heads stood together near the damaged area.</p>
<p>Garrick Cravik slowly lowered his hand after launching the attack. His expression remained serious as he looked at the place where Ethan had disappeared.</p>
<p>Then he turned toward the other three.</p>
<p>"You all go out and take care of the ones outside. I will pull him down."</p>
<p>Valen gave him an uneasy look.</p>
<p>"Be careful. He isn’t as simple as he looks."</p>
<p>Garrick tightened his grip around his sword.</p>
<p>"Same for me."</p>
<p>The other three family heads did not argue. They turned around and moved toward the barrier. After reaching it, they passed through the opening that Ethan had created and quickly appeared outside.</p>
<p>Garrick remained behind.He turned his attention back toward the distance.</p>
<p>The dust slowly began to settle.</p>
<p>Pieces of broken stone rolled across the ground as a figure slowly stepped out from the debris.</p>
<p>Ethan brushed the dust from his clothes and looked toward Garrick.</p>
<p>"That hurts..."</p>
<p>He slowly straightened his body and stared at the man standing before him.</p>
<p>For a moment, the two simply looked at each other.</p>
<p>Garrick stepped forward and raised his sword slightly.</p>
<p>"This might be the first time we met face to face... I am Garrick Cravik, the head of Sword Clan Cravik."</p>
<p>Ethan looked at him for a moment before a small smile appeared on his face.</p>
<p>"Ethan Blanks."</p>
<p>He gave him a calm greeting.</p>
<p>Garrick extended his sword and pointed it toward Ethan.</p>
<p>"I heard you are a strong warrior. It had been quite long since I fought, so I hope you don’t put me down."</p>
<p>Ethan’s smile remained.</p>
<p>"Then come on."</p>
<p>He tightened his grip around Solaris.</p>
<p>A surge of energy passed through the greatsword, and the weapon began to change in his hands. The large blade broke apart and reshaped itself, forming a single-edged sharp sword.</p>
<p>Ethan lowered his body slightly and pointed the blade toward Garrick.</p>
<p>His expression became serious.</p>
<p>The next moment, both of them disappeared from their positions.</p>
<p>The moment Ethan and Garrick moved, their swords collided.</p>
<p>CLANG!</p>
<p>The sound of the clash echoed across the sky, followed by a powerful shockwave that spread in every direction. Both of them were pushed back for only a moment, but neither gave the other time to recover. Their bodies moved again almost at the same time, and they rushed toward each other with their swords raised.</p>
<p>CLANG!</p>
<p>Their blades collided again.</p>
<p>Then again.</p>
<p>And again.</p>
<p>Their figures became nothing more than blurs in the sky as they exchanged attacks at an incredible speed. Ethan’s sword swept toward Garrick’s neck, but Garrick tilted his head and blocked the blade with his own sword. At the same time, he stepped forward and thrust his sword toward Ethan’s chest.</p>
<p>Ethan quickly turned his body and allowed the blade to pass beside him. He immediately swung his sword upward, forcing Garrick to raise his weapon and block the attack.</p>
<p>CLANG! 𝒻𝘳ℯℯ𝑤ℯ𝒷𝘯ℴ𝓋ℯ𝘭.𝑐ℴ𝑚</p>
<p>The force of the collision pushed both of them apart for a moment then they rushed toward each other again.</p>
<p>Within the blink of an eye, their swords had already clashed hundreds of times. Every collision sent ripples through the surrounding air, and the sound of metal continued to echo across the sky.</p>
<p>CLANG! CLANG! CLANG!</p>
<p>Their movements became faster with every exchange. Garrick’s sword moved in sharp and direct lines, while Ethan’s movements were more flexible as he changed his position with every attack. Neither of them was willing to give the other even the smallest opening.</p>
<p>Ethan suddenly stepped forward and swung his sword toward Garrick’s shoulder.</p>
<p>Garrick raised his sword and blocked it.</p>
<p>At the same time, he twisted his wrist and immediately countered with a sharp strike toward Ethan’s side.</p>
<p>Ethan pulled his body back, and the blade passed only inches from him. He then stepped forward again and struck Garrick’s chest.</p>
<p>Garrick blocked the attack.</p>
<p>The force sent another shockwave through the sky.</p>
<p>BOOM!</p>
<p>The clouds around them were torn apart, and the air seemed to shake from their repeated clashes.</p>
<p>The two continued exchanging attacks, and within an instant, they had already clashed thousands of times high above the Capital. Their swords moved so quickly that ordinary people could no longer follow them. They could only see flashes of light and hear the continuous sound of their weapons colliding.</p>
<p>Every collision created ripples in the air.</p>
<p>Shockwaves spread in every direction, and the clouds above them were completely scattered. The force reached the city below, shaking buildings and sending debris across the streets. Windows shattered, rooftops trembled and several structures began to crack from the pressure.</p>
<p>Ethan noticed the destruction below.</p>
<p>His eyes moved toward the streets.</p>
<p>People were still running through the Capital, desperately trying to escape the fighting. Some were carrying injured family members, while others were trying to protect their children as they searched for somewhere safe.</p>
<p>Ethan’s expression changed slightly.He immediately restrained his power.</p>
<p>His movements became more careful, and he started controlling the direction of his attacks so the shockwaves would not directly fall upon the people below. He could have increased his strength and forced Garrick back, but doing so would also increase the destruction around them.</p>
<p>Because of that, his attacks became weaker.</p>
<p>Garrick quickly noticed the change.</p>
<p>He had been fighting Ethan for only a short time, but he was experienced enough to understand what had happened.</p>
<p>Ethan was holding back.</p>
