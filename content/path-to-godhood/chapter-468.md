<p>Guests below gasped loudly.</p>
<p>“That’s too dangerous!”</p>
<p>“Those idiots are not holding back!”</p>
<p>“Damn… they really want to ruin the wedding.”</p>
<p>But Ethan remained calm as he knew Duke Lancelot will take care of shockwaves and after effects.</p>
<p>His body moved through the air like a shadow.</p>
<p>WHOOSH.</p>
<p>He dodged the first lightning spear.Then he twisted aside as the wind blade passed inches from his shoulder.</p>
<p>The flaming fist of Hamel reached him the next moment.</p>
<p>BOOM!</p>
<p>Their fists collided in midair.</p>
<p>A massive shockwave exploded outward across the sky.</p>
<p>The clouds above the estate were torn apart by the force.</p>
<p>But Hamel’s eyes suddenly widened.</p>
<p>Because Ethan’s strength pushed him backward instead.</p>
<p>Before he could recover, Ethan grabbed his arm and hurled him downward.</p>
<p>CRASH!</p>
<p>Hamel smashed into the courtyard ground below and created another deep crater.</p>
<p>Meanwhile, Garet and Pentos attacked together again.</p>
<p>Wind blades and lightning spears rained down like a storm.</p>
<p>Ethan raised his hand.</p>
<p>Golden light erupted around his body.</p>
<p>The attacks shattered the moment they struck his barrier.</p>
<p>Then Ethan raised his finger and drew several glowing symbols in the air.</p>
<p>A massive array of light spread outward from his body, and a powerful holy aura filled the sky.</p>
<p>His calm voice echoed across the battlefield.</p>
<p>“Holy Sword Judgement Array.”</p>
<p>Dozens of golden swords suddenly appeared in the air.</p>
<p>Each blade glowed with bright divine light.</p>
<p>They surrounded the battlefield like a massive floating formation.</p>
<p>At that moment the attacks from Garet and Pentos collided directly with the descending golden swords.</p>
<p>BOOOOM!</p>
<p>The sky exploded with light.</p>
<p>Wind blades shattered against the holy swords.Lightning spears burst apart like sparks when they struck the glowing blades.</p>
<p>The collision created a massive shockwave that spread across the sky.The air trembled violently as waves of power rolled outward.</p>
<p>Below the battlefield, the shockwave rushed toward the wedding courtyard.</p>
<p>Seeing the violent energy approaching, Rake suddenly took a step forward.</p>
<p>His aura burst out and formed an invisible pressure.</p>
<p>The shockwave shook violently before breaking apart under his power.</p>
<p>But a small portion still rushed downward.</p>
<p>At that moment Duke Lancelot calmly lifted his hand and waved it once.</p>
<p>The remaining shockwave disappeared instantly as if it had never existed.</p>
<p>High above them, the aftermath slowly cleared.</p>
<p>Garet and Pentos froze for a moment when they saw the glowing array surrounding them.</p>
<p>“That… what is that technique?” Hamel whispered in shock.</p>
<p>But it was already too late.</p>
<p>The swords dropped simultaneously.</p>
<p>WHOOSH! WHOOSH! WHOOSH!</p>
<p>Golden blades crashed down around them and formed a glowing prison.</p>
<p>Every direction was blocked by radiant sword energy.</p>
<p>Garet immediately swung his sword and tried to break through the barrier.</p>
<p>CLANG!</p>
<p>His blade bounced back violently.</p>
<p>Pentos launched another storm of lightning spears while Hamel tried to make his way out.</p>
<p>CRACK! CRACK!</p>
<p>But the attacks shattered the moment they touched the golden wall.</p>
<p>The Holy Sword Judgement Array completely sealed the battlefield.</p>
<p>Ethan slowly descended inside the glowing formation.</p>
<p>His sword pointed calmly toward them.Below, the entire wedding courtyard had fallen silent.</p>
<p>Everyone stared at the sky in shock.</p>
<p>Three powerful nobles had attacked together.</p>
<p>And yet Ethan had completely suppressed them.</p>
<p>Inside the golden prison, Garet clenched his teeth while Pentos’ face turned pale.</p>
<p>“Damn… it’s sucking our energy,” Hamel said while slowly getting up inside the array.</p>
<p>He could clearly feel his power being drained.</p>
<p>Ethan looked at them quietly.</p>
<p>Then he spoke in a calm voice.</p>
<p>“This is still my wedding day.”</p>
<p>“So don’t make me ruin it further.”</p>
<p>As if they were being cut by a thousand knives, the three of them suddenly let out extremely miserable screams.</p>
<p>“AAAAAH!”</p>
<p>Their voices echoed across the sky and the courtyard below.</p>
<p>Their bodies trembled violently inside the glowing prison of swords. Blood burst from their mouths as they struggled to remain standing. The golden light from the Holy Sword Judgement Array continued pressing down on them like an invisible mountain.</p>
<p>Hamel fell to one knee first.</p>
<p>Garet staggered backward and grabbed his chest as another mouthful of blood spilled from his lips.</p>
<p>Pentos tried to raise his hands to form another spell, but the moment he gathered mana, the light around them flared again.</p>
<p>“AAAGH!”</p>
<p>The pressure crushed his attempt immediately.</p>
<p>Blood sprayed from his mouth as well.</p>
<p>None of them could even speak properly anymore.</p>
<p>Their earlier arrogance had completely disappeared.</p>
<p>“Shit! What’s going on?”</p>
<p>One of the nobles below shouted in shock.</p>
<p>Seeing the scene in the sky, the entire crowd was stunned.</p>
<p>What exactly had happened just now?</p>
<p>How did the three young nobles suddenly turn into such a miserable state?</p>
<p>Just moments ago they had attacked with terrifying power.</p>
<p>But now they looked like broken men struggling to even remain conscious.</p>
<p>Everyone in the courtyard was filled with confusion.</p>
<p>Whispers spread quickly among the guests.</p>
<p>“Did you see that?”</p>
<p>“I didn’t even understand what happened!”</p>
<p>“Did he attack them?”</p>
<p>“But I didn’t see him move!”</p>
<p>Even some experienced knights looked stunned while staring at the glowing formation in the sky.</p>
<p>However, as long as someone was not an idiot, they could still guess the answer.</p>
<p>This was Ethan’s doing.</p>
<p>Even if they could not understand the exact method, the result was clear.He had instantly suppressed three Legendary rank fighters.</p>
<p>And he had done it in front of everyone.</p>
<p>Many nobles unconsciously swallowed nervously.</p>
<p>The way they looked at Ethan had completely changed.</p>
<p>Earlier some of them had doubted him.</p>
<p>Now those doubts had vanished.</p>
<p>Fear and respect replaced them.</p>
<p>Meanwhile, the soldiers standing around the courtyard looked at the sky with shining eyes.</p>
<p>“That’s our lord…”</p>
<p>“Did you see that?”</p>
<p>“He crushed them completely.”</p>
<p>One of the older knights shook his head slowly while staring upward.</p>
<p>“That technique…”</p>
<p>“That was not something ordinary Legendary rank fighters can use.”</p>
<p>In the center of the courtyard, Amber stood silently while looking at Ethan.</p>
<p>Her eyes were wide with surprise.</p>
<p>Beside her, Julia and the other wives were also watching the scene in the sky.</p>
<p>Their expressions carried the same mixture of shock and pride.</p>
<p>They had already known Ethan was strong.</p>
<p>But seeing him suppress three powerful nobles so easily still shocked them.</p>
<p>At the same time, pride rose in their hearts.</p>
<p>After all, that man was their husband.</p>
<p>And he stood there like an unshakable mountain.</p>
<p>Amber quietly placed her hand over her chest while watching him descend slowly from the sky.</p>
<p>A faint smile appeared on her lips.</p>
<p>Meanwhile above the courtyard, as the Holy Sword Judgement Array slowly faded,Ethan took a step and roared that created a loud sonic blast.</p>
<p>“Is there anyone else who wants to challenge me?”</p>
<p>“If there is then step forward.”</p>
