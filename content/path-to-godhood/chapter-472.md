<p>Fine, little girl,” he said with clear irritation.</p>

<p>“If you can play a flute that satisfies me, then I am going to teach you.”</p>

<p>Miranda’s face lit up instantly.</p>

<p>“Really?”</p>

<p>The man shrugged.</p>

<p>“Don’t celebrate too early.”</p>

<p>“Most people fail before they even begin.”</p>

<p>Miranda nodded quickly.</p>

<p>“I’ll try.”</p>

<p>The man took out a simple wooden flute and tossed it toward her.</p>

<p>She caught it carefully with both hands.</p>

<p>Several people nearby noticed what was happening and slowly gathered around again. News had already spread around the city about the mysterious man who could calm beasts with music.</p>

<p>Now they were curious.</p>

<p>Miranda lifted the flute slowly.She took a deep breath and placed it near her lips.</p>

<p>At first the sound was rough.</p>

<p>A few uncertain notes drifted into the air.</p>

<p>The melody wavered slightly while Miranda tried to control her breathing and finger movements.</p>

<p>The man leaned casually against a wooden pillar nearby while folding his arms.</p>

<p>He barely paid attention.</p>

<p>He had heard this many times before.</p>

<p>Many people had asked him to teach them.</p>

<p>Almost none had the talent required.</p>

<p>Miranda continued playing.</p>

<p>The melody slowly began to stabilize.</p>

<p>Her fingers moved more smoothly along the holes of the flute. The notes became clearer, and a gentle rhythm started forming.</p>

<p>Several people in the crowd exchanged surprised glances.</p>

<p>“Not bad…”</p>

<p>“She actually knows how to play.”</p>

<p>The man still appeared uninterested.</p>

<p>His eyes remained half closed while he listened casually.But Miranda did not stop.</p>

<p>She continued focusing on the melody.</p>

<p>The music slowly grew softer and more controlled. The tone became warm and steady while it floated gently through the air.</p>

<p>Then something changed.</p>

<p>Around the middle of the melody, the notes suddenly became deeper.</p>

<p>They carried a strange emotional resonance.</p>

<p>The sound was no longer just simple music.</p>

<p>It felt calm.</p>

<p>Peaceful.</p>

<p>Almost like the quiet wind moving through a forest.</p>

<p>The man’s eyes slowly opened.</p>

<p>He looked toward Miranda again.</p>

<p>Resonance.</p>

<p>He could feel it clearly.</p>

<p>It was not just sound anymore.</p>

<p>The notes were beginning to resonate with her inner spirit.</p>

<p>A faint invisible ripple spread around her while she continued playing the flute. The music flowed naturally as if her heart and breath had become one with the instrument.</p>

<p>Her soul strength seemed to pour into the melody.</p>

<p>The high notes carried an unusual clarity while the lower tones echoed with quiet strength. The air around them felt calmer as the sound drifted through the street.</p>

<p>Even a few small birds sitting on a nearby rooftop stopped moving and listened quietly.</p>

<p>The man stared at Miranda in complete surprise.</p>

<p>His eyes slowly widened.</p>

<p>Then a faint glow appeared in his gaze while he watched her.</p>

<p>“I can’t believe this…” he murmured under his breath.</p>

<p>Miranda remained focused and continued playing.The melody grew stronger.</p>

<p>Each note carried a natural flow as if the music itself had found a path through her.</p>

<p>The man straightened slowly from the pillar.</p>

<p>His expression had completely changed.</p>

<p>For many years he had searched for someone capable of understanding this art.</p>

<p>No one had ever come close.</p>

<p>Until now.</p>

<p>“Finally…” he whispered quietly.</p>

<p>“Finally someone who has talent similar to mine.”</p>

<p>He shook his head slowly while still watching Miranda play.</p>

<p>“Thank God…”</p>

<p>A faint smile appeared on his face.</p>

<p>“Now finally someone can inherit my skills.”</p>

<p>He looked at Miranda carefully.</p>

<p>For the first time, he was no longer seeing her as an annoying girl following him rather he was seeing a true successor.</p>

<p>……</p>

<p>Inside the study room of the fortress, Ethan sat behind a large wooden desk while reading through several reports. The afternoon sunlight filtered through the tall windows and fell across the scattered documents.</p>

<p>The room was quiet except for the faint scratching sound of a pen and the occasional turning of paper.</p>

<p>A shadow suddenly appeared near the doorway.One of the members of the Black Thorns arrived.</p>

<p>The man bowed slightly before stepping forward and placing a small sealed report on the desk.</p>

<p>“My lord, this concerns Lady Miranda.”</p>

<p>Ethan paused and looked up.</p>

<p>He picked up the report and quickly scanned the contents.</p>

<p>His brows slowly furrowed.</p>

<p>“Miranda… learning from a flutist?” he muttered.</p>

<p>He leaned back slightly in his chair and rubbed his temple.</p>

<p>“Now what’s up with that girl?”</p>

<p>The report described how Miranda had been seen frequently following a mysterious man in the city streets. Several witnesses mentioned the man playing a flute that could calm beasts and influence animals.</p>

<p>Ethan tapped the desk lightly with his finger.</p>

<p>A faint curiosity appeared in his eyes.</p>

<p>“Black Thorns,” he said calmly.</p>

<p>“Yes, my lord,” the shadow replied.</p>

<p>Ethan placed the report down.</p>

<p>“What abilities does this man have?”</p>

<p>The Black Thorns agent hesitated slightly before answering.</p>

<p>“We are still investigating, my lord. But from what we have observed, he appears capable of influencing beasts through sound.”</p>

<p>Ethan frowned.</p>

<p>“Through sound?”</p>

<p>The man nodded.</p>

<p>“Yes.”</p>

<p>Ethan leaned forward slightly.</p>

<p>“Does that kind of skill actually exist?”</p>

<p>He had seen many strange abilities in his life, but controlling beasts through music was something completely new to him.</p>

<p>His eyes narrowed thoughtfully.</p>

<p>“Conduct a preliminary assessment,” he ordered.</p>

<p>“I want to know who this man is, where he came from, and what exactly he is capable of.”</p>

<p>“Investigate from shadow..Make sure not to bother him.”</p>

<p>“Yes, my lord.”</p>

<p>The shadow bowed again and disappeared quickly.Several hours later another report arrived.</p>

<p>Ethan opened it immediately.</p>

<p>But as he read the contents, his expression slowly became more serious.</p>

<p>“They couldn’t find anything?” he said quietly.</p>

<p>The second Black Thorns agent standing before him lowered his head.</p>

<p>“Yes, my lord.”</p>

<p>“We investigated his movements, questioned people who saw him, and checked the records of travelers entering the city.”</p>

<p>He paused briefly.</p>

<p>“There is no trace of him.”</p>

<p>Ethan’s fingers tapped slowly on the desk.</p>

<p>“No background?”</p>

<p>“No known identity?”</p>

<p>“No past records?”</p>

<p>“None, my lord.”</p>

<p>The room became silent again.</p>

<p>Ethan stared at the report in his hand.</p>

<p>If even the Black Thorns could not discover anything about someone, that meant one of two things.</p>

<p>Either the man had appeared recently.Or he was hiding his identity extremely well.</p>

<p>Both possibilities made the situation somewhat dangerous.</p>

<p>Ethan leaned back in his chair and closed his eyes briefly while thinking.</p>

<p>After a few moments he opened them again.</p>

<p>“Keep watching him,” he said calmly.</p>

<p>“But do not interfere.”</p>

<p>“Yes, my lord.”</p>

<p>Ethan placed the report down and folded his arms.</p>

<p>“If he is harmless, then Miranda learning something from him might not be a bad thing.”</p>

<p>He paused slightly.</p>

<p>“But if something suspicious appears…”</p>

<p>The Black Thorns agent nodded.</p>

<p>“We will inform you immediately.”</p>

<p>Ethan gave a faint nod.</p>

<p>“That’s enough.”</p>

<p>The man bowed and disappeared into the shadows again.</p>

<p>Ethan remained seated in the quiet room.</p>

<p>He looked toward the window where the city of Blanks stretched beyond the fortress walls.</p>

<p>After a moment he spoke quietly to himself.</p>

<p>“Well… if something really goes wrong…”</p>

<p>A faint ripple of space flickered briefly around his hand.</p>

<p>“I can just teleport there.”</p>

<p>Then he leaned back in his chair again and returned to reading the remaining reports.</p>
