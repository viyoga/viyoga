<p>The four crashed onto the ground before rolling awkwardly across the floor, and several of them groaned painfully from the impact.</p>
<p>When they finally raised their heads, their expressions immediately changed.</p>
<p>At the very center of the tent sat Ethan.</p>
<p>He was seated calmly upon a chair while resting one arm against the armrest and quietly observing them.</p>
<p>Several lamps illuminated the interior of the tent while the flickering light cast moving shadows across his face.</p>
<p>His expression was completely indifferent.</p>
<p>Yet that calmness somehow felt far more frightening than open rage.</p>
<p>Around him stood Noel, Randall and several officers of the Order of Eternal Blade while every one of them silently watched the prisoners.</p>
<p>Nobody spoke.The silence itself felt oppressive.</p>
<p>One of the prisoners swallowed hard because his throat had already become completely dry.</p>
<p>Ethan slowly lowered the report in his hand and looked toward the four men.</p>
<p>His gaze remained calm.</p>
<p>It made the prisoners feel even more uncomfortable.</p>
<p>For several long moments Ethan simply observed them without speaking while the silence dragged on endlessly and the pressure inside the tent continued increasing.</p>
<p>The four men became increasingly nervous.</p>
<p>Cold sweat began appearing across their foreheads.Finally, Ethan spoke.</p>
<p>His voice was calm and steady.</p>
<p>"Tell me everything."</p>
<p>The prisoners looked at one another.</p>
<p>Ethan slowly leaned forward while his eyes narrowed slightly.</p>
<p>"Who sent you?"</p>
<p>"Who ordered you to spread those rumors?"</p>
<p>"Who is behind all this?"</p>
<p>The four men remained silent while one of them even lowered his head stubbornly.</p>
<p>Seeing this, Ethan slowly nodded.</p>
<p>"Fine."</p>
<p>He leaned back into his chair again.</p>
<p>His expression did not change in the slightest.</p>
<p>"I was hoping we could save some time."</p>
<p>His voice remained quiet.</p>
<p>"But it seems you people prefer the difficult way."</p>
<p>The four prisoners felt their hearts sink immediately.</p>
<p>In the next instant,their mouths were and they were beaten brutally.</p>
<p>The loud sound of whips filled the entire place.</p>
<p>Ethan’s fingers lightly tapped against the armrest.</p>
<p>Tap.</p>
<p>Tap.</p>
<p>Tap.</p>
<p>Each sound echoed clearly throughout the silent tent and made the already heavy atmosphere feel even more suffocating.</p>
<p>Then Ethan slowly lifted his eyes and looked directly at the four prisoners kneeling before him.</p>
<p>"If you speak now, things might remain simple."</p>
<p>"If you don’t..."</p>
<p>He paused briefly while the silence stretched across the tent.The atmosphere suddenly became colder.</p>
<p>"Then prepare to die."</p>
<p>The four men visibly trembled after hearing those words.</p>
<p>Ethan’s gaze remained fixed upon them.</p>
<p>His face showed no emotion.</p>
<p>"And not just die."</p>
<p>His voice became slightly lower and colder.</p>
<p>"I promise you that if I have to dig the truth out myself, I will make your final days so miserable that even your souls will fear remembering them."</p>
<p>The prisoners immediately felt chills run down their backs.</p>
<p>One of them instinctively tried crawling backward while another’s breathing became uneven.</p>
<p>Sweat continuously rolled down their foreheads.</p>
<p>Even Randall silently glanced toward Ethan because he rarely saw him acting like this.</p>
<p>The calm expression.</p>
<p>The cold voice.</p>
<p>The complete lack of emotion.</p>
<p>It was genuinely intimidating.</p>
<p>Ethan slowly stood up from his chair while the sound of his boots echoed throughout the tent.</p>
<p>Step.</p>
<p>Step.</p>
<p>Step.</p>
<p>The sound was neither loud nor fast, but inside the silent tent it felt unusually heavy.</p>
<p>The four prisoners watched him approach and their hearts almost stopped beating.</p>
<p>Ethan stopped directly in front of them.</p>
<p>Then he slowly crouched down and looked into their eyes.</p>
<p>His gaze calmly swept across each of them.</p>
<p>"You have one chance."</p>
<p>"Only one."</p>
<p>His eyes narrowed slightly.</p>
<p>"So think carefully."</p>
<p>"Either you tell me everything."</p>
<p>"Or I will find out myself."</p>
<p>The prisoners’ faces turned increasingly pale.</p>
<p>One of them had already started shaking uncontrollably while another could not even maintain eye contact anymore.</p>
<p>The pressure inside the tent became suffocating.Several knights standing nearby remained completely silent.</p>
<p>The atmosphere felt like a storm before the eruption.</p>
<p>Without even looking back, he calmly spoke.</p>
<p>"Start talking."</p>
<p>"Before I lose my patience."</p>
<p>"My Lord!"</p>
<p>"Yess!"Ethan asked angrily.</p>
<p>But the pressure inside the tent was crushing them.</p>
<p>Then suddenly...</p>
<p>A cold and unbearable aura erupted from Ethan’s body.</p>
<p>BOOM!</p>
<p>An overwhelming killing intent swept across the entire tent like a violent storm and instantly descended upon the four men.</p>
<p>Their bodies stiffened immediately.</p>
<p>Their minds went blank.</p>
<p>The terrifying pressure felt like countless blades pressing against their necks at the same time.The lamps inside the tent flickered violently.</p>
<p>Even the air itself seemed heavier.</p>
<p>Several officers inside the tent narrowed their eyes slightly.</p>
<p>Even they could feel the terrifying killing intent radiating from Ethan.</p>
<p>The four prisoners were completely horrified.</p>
<p>Their breathing became chaotic.</p>
<p>One of them nearly collapsed onto the ground.</p>
<p>Another started trembling so badly that he could barely remain upright.</p>
<p>The third man’s teeth continuously chattered.</p>
<p>The fourth looked as if he was about to faint.</p>
<p>Ethan slowly sat back down upon his chair.</p>
<p>"What happened?"</p>
<p>"You forget to unclog their mouth."</p>
<p>"...."Ethan’s eyelids twitched as he looked at Randall.</p>
<p>Can’t you tell me that before.</p>
<p>"Release that."</p>
<p>He stared at the prisoners for a few moments before finally speaking.</p>
<p>"Name?"</p>
<p>His voice echoed heavily throughout the tent.</p>
<p>Ethan’s expression darkened slightly.</p>
<p>"Give me a fucking name."</p>
<p>The pressure intensified once more.The prisoners felt as if death itself was staring at them.</p>
<p>At that moment, one of the prisoners was completely unable to endure it any longer.His mental defenses shattered instantly.</p>
<p>"SHATAD!"</p>
<p>The man screamed desperately.</p>
<p>His voice echoed loudly throughout the tent.</p>
<p>"It’s Marquise Shatad!"</p>
<p>"It’s Marquise Shatad who sent us!"</p>
<p>The prisoner panted heavily while tears nearly formed in his eyes.</p>
<p>"He told us to spread the rumors!"</p>
<p>"He told us to ruin your reputation!"</p>
<p>"He told us everything!"</p>
<p>The moment those words left his mouth the entire tent fell silent.</p>
<p>Even the knights exchanged glances.Randall’s eyes narrowed.Noel’s expression immediately darkened.</p>
<p>Ethan looked at them silently.His expression did not change but everyone inside the tent could feel it.</p>
<p>The temperature around him seemed to have dropped several degrees.</p>
