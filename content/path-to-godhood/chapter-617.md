<p>"Is that the only reason?"</p>
<p>"Nah," Duke Lancelot replied immediately while waving his hand dismissively.</p>
<p>"There are far more reasons than that."</p>
<p>A faint sneer appeared on his face.</p>
<p>"It’s just that the late Ascension Realm expert was troublesome."</p>
<p>"It won’t be wrong to say that he is a son of bitch."Lancelot cursed hatefully.</p>
<p>"He spent decades hiding behind the scenes while carrying out all kinds of schemes, political manipulations, secret operations, and shady plans that repeatedly provoked the surrounding powers."The Duke shook his head.</p>
<p>"How can one man simply pull so much hate?" Ethan asked in stupor, his brows knitting together in disbelief as he looked at Duke Lancelot.</p>
<p>"You are saying this because you haven’t met that guy!" Lancelot shook his head with a helpless smile before letting out a quiet sigh. "Think about it, my boy... What was Arcadia before?"</p>
<p>"It was just a small kingdom. These small kingdoms were left by the Empire to survive at their mercy. They were discarded because they didn’t hold any significance."</p>
<p>He paused briefly and looked out the nearby window as if recalling the past before continuing.</p>
<p>"Amidst such kingdoms, Arcadia rose, and it began with the rise of a single man."</p>
<p>"He made enemies everywhere.He offended far too many people.And because he possessed overwhelming strength, nobody could do much about it."</p>
<p>His eyes narrowed slightly as his expression hardened.</p>
<p>"But now?"</p>
<p>"Now he’s dead."</p>
<p>"And all those people he offended finally have a chance to settle old scores."</p>
<p>Ethan quietly clicked his tongue.</p>
<p>As expected.</p>
<p>That old bastard really sounded like an asshole.</p>
<p>After thinking for a brief moment, another question entered Ethan’s mind. He shifted in his seat and looked back at Duke Lancelot.</p>
<p>"How many Ascension Realm experts are there in total?"</p>
<p>Duke Lancelot turned toward him before answering in a calm tone.</p>
<p>"The Human Kingdoms currently possess ten Ascension Realm experts.They have the highest number."</p>
<p>"After them come the Demons with nine."</p>
<p>He slowly raised another finger while counting.</p>
<p>"The Orcs have five."</p>
<p>"The Elves have five."</p>
<p>"The Dwarves have three."</p>
<p>Ethan silently absorbed the information while rubbing his chin.</p>
<p>The numbers were much larger than he had expected.Then another thought suddenly crossed his mind.</p>
<p>"Are there any loose ones among them?"</p>
<p>Duke Lancelot blinked in confusion and frowned slightly.</p>
<p>"Loose what?"</p>
<p>Realizing how that sounded, Ethan immediately coughed awkwardly and scratched the back of his head.</p>
<p>"I mean someone who doesn’t belong to any race, Empire, or major faction."</p>
<p>Understanding immediately appeared on the Duke’s face.</p>
<p>"Ah."</p>
<p>"There should be."</p>
<p>"Although they are extremely rare."</p>
<p>His expression became thoughtful as he folded his arms across his chest.</p>
<p>"Besides, don’t forget the Evil Organizations and the Holy Nation."</p>
<p>"Their strength has always been highly secretive."</p>
<p>"Even now, nobody truly knows how many Ascension Realm experts they possess."</p>
<p>Ethan slowly nodded before suddenly remembering something else. Looking up again, he spoke a single word.</p>
<p>"Church."</p>
<p>Duke Lancelot raised an eyebrow.</p>
<p>"What about it?"</p>
<p>Ethan leaned forward slightly, curiosity visible on his face.</p>
<p>"Now that you mention the Holy Nation, I wonder what the Church’s stance is regarding this war."</p>
<p>"Can’t they intervene?"</p>
<p>The moment those words left Ethan’s mouth, Duke Lancelot froze for a brief second.</p>
<p>Then suddenly...</p>
<p>"Hehehehehe..."</p>
<p>A strange laugh escaped him.</p>
<p>Moments later, it became louder.</p>
<p>"Puahahahaha!"</p>
<p>He laughed so hard that he nearly struck the armrest of his chair while trying to steady himself. His shoulders trembled as he continued laughing.</p>
<p>Ethan simply stared at him with confusion written all over his face.</p>
<p>"What?"</p>
<p>Eventually, Duke Lancelot calmed himself and wiped the corner of one eye before taking a slow breath.</p>
<p>"Sorry."</p>
<p>"I just heard the funniest thing I’ve heard all week."</p>
<p>He shook his head while letting out another quiet chuckle before his expression gradually turned serious again.</p>
<p>"Why would they care?"</p>
<p>"This war."</p>
<p>"This suffering."</p>
<p>"This bloodshed."</p>
<p>"To them, it is nothing more than another harvest season."</p>
<p>Ethan frowned deeply.</p>
<p>Duke Lancelot slowly pointed toward the distant city outside the window.</p>
<p>"The more people suffer, the more desperate they become."</p>
<p>"The more desperate they become, the more they pray."</p>
<p>"The more they pray, the stronger their faith becomes."</p>
<p>A faint smile appeared on his face as he continued.</p>
<p>"Think about it."</p>
<p>"Thousands of frightened people asking for miracles.Thousands of grieving families seeking blessings and thousands of soldiers begging for protection."</p>
<p>He spread his hands as though presenting an obvious fact.</p>
<p>"This is the perfect opportunity for the Church to showcase its influence, healing, blessings, and miracles."</p>
<p>"So tell me."</p>
<p>"Why would they stop this farce when they benefit from it?"</p>
<p>Ethan’s expression slowly darkened as he lowered his gaze.</p>
<p>"Still... aren’t they supposed to help people?"</p>
<p>"They can’t even if they wanted to."</p>
<p>Duke Lancelot immediately shook his head.</p>
<p>"Do you honestly think the Empires would listen?"</p>
<p>His gaze became deeper as he looked directly at Ethan.</p>
<p>"Ethan."</p>
<p>"My child."</p>
<p>"The world is far crueler than you think."</p>
<p>His voice softened slightly, carrying an unusual weight.</p>
<p>"People like to speak about justice."</p>
<p>"They like to speak about honor."</p>
<p>"They like to speak about righteousness."</p>
<p>"They like to speak about protecting the weak."</p>
<p>A tired sigh escaped his lips before he slowly closed his eyes for a moment.</p>
<p>"But once power, resources, ambition, survival, and authority become involved, most of those ideals quickly become secondary."</p>
<p>He rose from his chair and quietly walked toward the window. Warm sunlight fell across his figure while he looked at the military camps outside in silence.</p>
<p>"So when you walk onto the battlefield..."</p>
<p>"Do not expect humanity from everyone you meet."</p>
<p>His expression became unusually serious.</p>
<p>"Because the higher you climb, the less of it you will find."</p>
<p>"And the more powerful a person becomes, the easier it becomes for them to stop seeing people as people and start seeing them as numbers, tools, sacrifices, or stepping stones."</p>
<p>The room fell silent after that. Neither of them spoke for several moments. Only the quiet breeze drifting through the open window disturbed the stillness.</p>
<p>Then Duke Lancelot slowly turned around and faced Ethan once more.</p>
<p>"Remember that."</p>
<p>"Because forgetting it is often the first step toward becoming one of them."</p>
