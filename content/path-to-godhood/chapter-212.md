<p>“Shh… don’t scream,” Julia whispered and placed her finger gently on his lips. Her face was slightly red, and her eyes trembled with shyness and nervousness.</p>

<p>Ethan looked around quickly and then closed the door as Julia staggered in.</p>

<p>A strong pungent smell emerged from her.</p>

<p>’She drank but didn’t use her realm to suppress it.’</p>

<p>He stared at her with mixed confusion and concern.</p>

<p>“Do you need something?” he asked.</p>

<p>“Of course. I’m here for an important reason,” she replied softly.</p>

<p>“And that is?” Ethan asked, curious and cautious.</p>

<p>“Th….” Julia’s lips trembled as she tried to speak, but no words came out properly. Her throat felt dry and tight, and her voice refused to obey her.</p>

<p>All the tricks and tips her mother had taught her felt like they were suddenly flushed out of her mind. It was as if someone had wiped her thoughts clean. Her head felt light, and she even swayed slightly, feeling dizzy while trying to decide what to say.</p>

<p>Her fingers clenched the hem of her clothes tightly.</p>

<p>“I… I want to talk..”</p>

<p>“Talk about what?”</p>

<p>Julia opened her lips to speak but no words came out of it.</p>

<p>Ethan stared at her quietly. He observed her stiff posture, her nervous eyes, and the way her shoulders were tense. He could already figure out something was off.</p>

<p>“You aren’t here to seduce me right?”</p>

<p>Julia froze for a second. She nodded instinctively and then shook her head in panic.</p>

<p>“Ye … I mean no… It’s not that..”</p>

<p>Her voice cracked halfway through, and she felt her face burn with shame.</p>

<p>“Ahhh!”</p>

<p>Unable to handle the pressure anymore, she suddenly covered her face with both hands and dropped to her knees. Her shoulders trembled as she knelt there, too ashamed to even look at Ethan.</p>

<p>Ethan blinked in surprise and then let out a soft laugh. He stepped forward and gently grabbed her hand, helping her up.</p>

<p>“It’s okay.. I understand.”</p>

<p>His voice was calm and warm, without mockery.</p>

<p>“Just tell me what you want.. There is no need to do something that makes you feel uncomfortable!”</p>

<p>Julia slowly raised her chin. Her eyes met Ethan’s face, and she froze again. His pale skin glowed softly under the light. His long white hair fluttered lightly around his shoulders. His sharp yet calm features made her breath hitch.</p>

<p>For some reason, she felt herself getting lost in those vast oceanic blue eyes.</p>

<p>BADUMP! BA-DUMP!</p>

<p>Her heart started hammering violently inside her chest. The sound felt so loud that she wondered if Ethan could hear it. Her breathing became shallow, and her body felt restless.</p>

<p>Her face heated up so fast that even Ethan noticed faint steam rising from her head.</p>

<p>“Julia.. Julia..” Ethan reached out to grab her shoulder, but before his fingers could touch her, her body went limp.</p>

<p>She collapsed forward.</p>

<p>Sniff.. Sniff.</p>

<p>Ethan caught her in time and looked down at her flushed face.</p>

<p>“Ahh.. She is drunk..”</p>

<p>He sighed and rubbed his forehead slowly.</p>

<p>“No wonder she acted so bold!”</p>

<p>“Sigh!”</p>

<p>“Girls are so troublesome!”</p>

<p>….</p>

<p>Next morning.</p>

<p>Julia slowly opened her eyes.</p>

<p>“Ahh… What a comfortable sleep!” she muttered lazily as she stretched. She rolled slightly and then paused. Her brows furrowed as she looked around.</p>

<p>The ceiling looked unfamiliar. The room felt different.</p>

<p>“Why does it look unfamiliar.”</p>

<p>“Because it’s my room!”</p>

<p>Her body stiffened instantly. She froze, then slowly turned her head.</p>

<p>Her eyes widened as she saw a long, tall, lean yet muscular beauty lying there casually. His long white hair spread over the pillow, and his calm blue eyes looked straight at her.</p>

<p>“AHHH!”</p>

<p>Julia screamed and jumped up from the bed in shock. Her heart almost leaped out of her chest. She immediately looked down at herself, checking in panic.</p>

<p>“Did we did it?”</p>

<p>“Nah! You just passed out before we can proceed.”</p>

<p>“Umm..” Julia frowned slightly as she thought for a moment. Then she asked carefully.</p>

<p>“Then.. Did I say something!”</p>

<p>“Yes.. You said you loved me to the end of the world and wanted to marry me!” Ethan answered with a small smile.</p>

<p>“Really?” Julia asked with widened eyes, her face full of disbelief.</p>

<p>“Yeah…”</p>

<p>Her fingers fidgeted nervously as she swallowed.</p>

<p>“Then.. anything else?” she asked softly, her voice slightly shaking.</p>

<p>“And I said yes!”</p>

<p>Julia’s expression brightened instantly. Her eyes sparkled, and happiness burst out of her like fireworks. She jumped onto the bed without hesitation.</p>

<p>“You agreed but we weren’t able to do the deed.”</p>

<p>She leaned closer, her voice turning soft and tempting.</p>

<p>“So why don’t we do it?”</p>

<p>Ethan frowned slightly. His gaze lingered on her beauty, and it would be a lie to say he was not tempted. Her closeness, her warmth, and her boldness made his heart race.</p>

<p>He had also been craving some spicy session himself.</p>

<p>However.</p>

<p>“Did your mother know yes?”</p>

<p>“Yes she agreed?”</p>

<p>Ethan nodded slightly and then asked again.</p>

<p>“About your father?”</p>

<p>“No!”</p>

<p>Ethan almost jumped out of the bed.</p>

<p>“What?” His eyes widened.</p>

<p>“Don’t tell me you acted without your father permission?”</p>

<p>He did not fear the Duke, but he did not want a furious father chasing him down while screaming.</p>

<p>How dare you take away the cabbage I had grown without telling me.</p>

<p>“Sorry, we should wait until your father approval.”</p>

<p>Julia pouted slightly.</p>

<p>“But… Mother will scold me?”</p>

<p>“I don’t care.. You need to bring your father approval..”</p>

<p>If it was anyone else, it would be fine. However, he did not want to get involved in messy family drama.</p>

<p>Julia wanted to act cute and stubborn.</p>

<p>However.</p>

<p>BANG!</p>

<p>Ethan grabbed her gently but firmly and guided her toward the door. He opened it and showed her the way out. Then he closed the door firmly behind her and leaned against it, taking deep heavy breaths.</p>

<p>He looked down and sighed deeply.</p>

<p>“Damn… If she keeps on harassing I might really burst out.”</p>

<p>He rubbed his face with both hands.</p>

<p>“Ahh… Getting blue balled doesn’t feel good.”</p>

<p>He had not slept the entire night because a certain girl kept molesting him in her sleep.</p>

<p>Just which sane fertile man could resist, unless that guy was gay.</p>
