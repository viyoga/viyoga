<p>The two sides continued exchanging attacks in the sky. Fire exploded against wind while lightning flashed between them, and every collision sent a deep rumble through the air. The heat from the flames spread across the battlefield, while sharp currents of wind cut through the smoke and made Ethan's clothes snap around his body.</p>

<p>The green-robed figure kept forming strange hand signs, and every time his fingers moved, another attack appeared around him. Flames gathered above his head, wind twisted around his arms, and lightning crawled across his robes before shooting toward Ethan. Ethan also continued casting his spells, using his staff to control the wind and lightning while moving through the attacks.</p>

<p>BOOM! BOOM! CRAACK!</p>

<p>The battlefield around them had already been covered by thick smoke and dust from their constant attacks. The sky was filled with scattered flames and broken streams of wind, while lightning continued flashing through the smoke. The smell of burning earth filled the air, and the ground below was covered with cracks and small fires.</p>

<p>Ethan raised his staff again.</p>

<p>A strong current of wind gathered around the tip as several sharp blades began forming. The pressure made the air whistle around him, and he was about to strike out another wind blade when something suddenly shot out from the smoke.</p>

<p>The green-robed figure appeared directly in front of him.</p>

<p>His robes were still moving from the force of his previous attack, and his palm was already raised.</p>

<p>A strange handprint suddenly formed in front of his palm.</p>

<p>The next moment, it shot toward Ethan.</p>

<p>BOOM!</p>

<p>The handprint tore through the air with enough force to make the space around it tremble.</p>

<p>Ethan's expression changed, and he immediately twisted his body to the side.</p>

<p>The handprint passed dangerously close to his shoulder.</p>

<p>The pressure alone made his armour tear slightly as the attack continued behind him. It smashed through the thick smoke and blasted apart the clouds in the distance.</p>
