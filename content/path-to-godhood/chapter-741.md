<p>Alaster raised one arm and blocked the attack, but the impact forced his feet deep into the shattered ground.</p>

<p>Before he could counterattack, cold air suddenly gathered around him.</p>

<p>Queen Ophelia pointed her staff forward.</p>

<p>"Freeze!"</p>

<p>CRACK!</p>

<p>Thick ice spread across Alaster’s legs and rapidly climbed toward his waist. Several magic circles appeared around him and released freezing chains that wrapped around his body. The ice joined with Caelus’ golden chains and restricted Alaster from both sides.</p>

<p>"ARGHHHH!"</p>

<p>Alaster roared and struggled violently.</p>

<p>His unstable aura surged again as he tried to break free, but that moment of resistance was enough.</p>

<p>Emperor Caelus released the chains from one hand.</p>

<p>His weapon appeared in his grip.He stepped forward.</p>

<p>SWISH!</p>

<p>His figure disappeared.</p>

<p>Alaster’s eyes widened as Caelus appeared directly beside him.</p>

<p>The blade flashed.</p>

<p>SLASH!</p>

<p>A deep line tore across Alaster’s body.</p>

<p>Blood exploded into the air as Emperor Caelus’ strike slashed through him, and the force behind the blow sent his broken figure crashing across the ruined battlefield.</p>

<p>The moment Emperor Caelus’ sword tore across Alaster’s body, everyone rushed forward at once.</p>

<p>They knew this was their chance.</p>

<p>They could not allow Alaster even a single moment to recover.</p>

<p>BOOM!</p>

<p>Emperor Aldric struck first and sent Alaster flying backward. Before Alaster could control his body, Emperor Ezekiel appeared above him and brought his greatsword down with both hands.</p>

<p>BANG!</p>

<p>The heavy blade smashed against Alaster and sent him crashing straight into the nearby mountain.</p>

<p>BOOOOOOOM!</p>

<p>The entire mountain shook violently.</p>

<p>A huge section of the rocky wall collapsed as Alaster’s body sank deep into it. Cracks spread hundreds of meters around him while enormous rocks broke loose and fell toward the ground.</p>

<p>Alaster coughed out blood and tried to pull himself free, but the others were already there.</p>

<p>SWISH!</p>

<p>SWISH!</p>

<p>SWISH!</p>

<p>Sword attacks struck him one after another.</p>

<p>One tore across his shoulder while another cut deeply into his chest. A third sword pierced through his protective aura and stabbed into his side. Before he could even scream, several magical attacks exploded against his body.</p>

<p>BOOM!</p>

<p>Flames swallowed him.</p>

<p>Ice covered his legs.</p>

<p>Golden light slammed against his chest while Emperor Aldric’s sword aura repeatedly struck the mountain around him. Alaster’s unstable blood-red aura desperately tried to protect his body, but it had already become too chaotic. Every attack caused another part of his defense to collapse.</p>

<p>Emperor Ezekiel stepped through the dust with his greatsword resting against his shoulder.</p>

<p>His eyes were filled with disgust as he stared at the bloody figure buried inside the mountain.</p>

<p>"So much planning only to be beaten."</p>

<p>He tightened his grip around his sword.</p>

<p>"As expected from you... No one is sane from the Arcadia family. You are disgusting."</p>

<p>Alaster slowly raised his head.</p>

<p>Blood covered most of his face while several deep wounds crossed his body. One of his arms hung weakly beside him, and his aura flickered wildly around him.</p>

<p>He looked at the people surrounding him.</p>

<p>There was no path left to escape.</p>

<p>His expression slowly changed.</p>

<p>The hatred disappeared for a moment and was replaced by madness.</p>

<p>"If I’m going to die..."</p>

<p>His bloody lips slowly stretched into a twisted smile.</p>

<p>The Emperors immediately sensed something wrong.</p>

<p>Alaster’s remaining energy suddenly stopped protecting his body.</p>

<p>Instead, every bit of power began rushing toward his core.</p>

<p>His body started swelling while blood-red cracks spread across his skin. The unstable energy inside him compressed rapidly and became more violent with every passing second.</p>

<p>Queen Ophelia’s expression changed.</p>

<p>"Move!"</p>

<p>Alaster laughed madly.</p>

<p>"Since I can’t kill you... I will take you down with me, you dorks!"</p>

<p>BOOOOOOM!</p>

<p>His aura exploded upward.</p>

<p>The mountain behind him started collapsing while the blood-red energy inside his body compressed toward a single point.</p>

<p>He was trying to self-destruct.</p>

<p>Everyone immediately pulled back.</p>

<p>However, one person rushed forward instead.</p>

<p>BOOM!</p>

<p>The ground beneath him exploded.</p>

<p>His figure flashed across the battlefield and appeared directly before Alaster.</p>

<p>Alaster’s eyes widened.</p>

<p>"You..."</p>

<p>Ezekiel had already raised his greatsword.</p>

<p>"DIEEEE!"</p>

<p>He swung.</p>

<p>SWISH!</p>

<p>For a moment, everything seemed to become silent.</p>

<p>A thin line of sword light flashed across Alaster’s neck.</p>

<p>The next instant, the world exploded.</p>

<p>BOOOOOOOOOOM!</p>

<p>A gigantic sword arc erupted from Ezekiel’s blade.</p>

<p>The sword light tore through Alaster and continued forward without stopping. The remaining half of the mountain behind him was split apart as the enormous arc traveled across the entire range. Several distant peaks were cut through while clouds high above the battlefield split open under the terrifying force.</p>

<p>The sky shook.</p>

<p>The earth trembled.</p>

<p>For several seconds, the entire world seemed to be covered by the brilliant sword light.</p>

<p>PFFT!</p>

<p>Alaster’s head flew into the air.His eyes were still wide with madness and disbelief.</p>

<p>The blood-red energy gathering inside his body immediately lost control and dispersed before the self-destruction could be completed.</p>

<p>His head spun several times through the air before falling onto the broken ground.</p>

<p>Thud!</p>

<p>Blood spilled from his mouth.</p>

<p>His headless body remained standing against the ruined mountain for another moment before slowly collapsing.</p>

<p>BOOM!</p>

<p>Silence spread across the battlefield.</p>

<p>Emperor Ezekiel stood before the corpse with his greatsword still extended.</p>

<p>His chest rose and fell heavily.</p>

<p>He stared at Alaster’s body for several seconds before raising his bloody sword high above his head.</p>

<p>"It’s done... AHHHHHHH!"</p>

<p>His war cry reverberated through the space around them.</p>

<p>The powerful roar traveled across the ruined mountains and echoed through the distant battlefield.</p>

<p>All those present paused.</p>

<p>The Emperors lowered their weapons while the surrounding Transcendent Knights looked toward Alaster’s corpse.</p>

<p>After such a long and brutal battle, the man had finally fallen.</p>

<p>Ethan stood some distance away and stared at the headless corpse.</p>

<p>His tense shoulders slowly relaxed.</p>

<p>"Haa...What a tenacious bastard."</p>

<p>He released a long breath.</p>

<p>Finally..That bastard was dead.</p>

<p>"So you are Ethan..."</p>

<p>A quiet voice suddenly came from behind him.</p>

<p>Ethan’s heart almost burst out of his chest.</p>

<p>His entire body stiffened.</p>

<p>’What?’</p>

<p>His instincts screamed.</p>

<p>Ethan immediately spun around.</p>

<p>"Whattttt!"</p>

<p>His eyes shook.</p>

<p>A cloaked man was standing directly behind him.</p>

<p>Ethan had no idea when the man had appeared.</p>

<p>There had been nothing.</p>

<p>No footsteps.</p>

<p>No aura.</p>

<p>No killing intent.</p>

<p>He had not sensed even the slightest movement.</p>

<p>The cloaked man slowly raised one hand.</p>

<p>Energy gathered in his palm.</p>

<p>Ethan’s pupils contracted.</p>

<p>"Wait!"</p>

<p>SWISH!</p>

<p>A beam of condensed power shot from the man’s palm.</p>

<p>It was already too close.</p>

<p>Ethan’s body had barely started moving when the attack reached him.</p>

<p>His face immediately turned pale.</p>

<p>’Fuckkkk! I am done for...’</p>
