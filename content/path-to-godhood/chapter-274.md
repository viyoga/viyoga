<p>“Father, he is lying!”</p>

<p>“Don’t listen to him!”</p>

<p>“What… what did you say?” Mark stood up abruptly and pointed at his son, his face burning with rage.</p>

<p>His entire expression twisted.</p>

<p>“Do you mean to say that I am not a wise King?”</p>

<p>“Do you mean to say that I am not righteous?”</p>

<p>“The monarch who rules the hearts of all?”</p>

<p>“Is that what you are saying?”</p>

<p>Albert hesitated, then nodded weakly before stopping himself.</p>

<p>“Ye… I mean, no. I didn’t mean that.”</p>

<p>“I just mean that he is lying about the situation!”</p>

<p>Mark slammed his foot against the floor and looked around.</p>

<p>“Do you think I am a fool who cannot tell lies from the truth?”</p>

<p>“Tell me,” he roared, “which part of what he said about me is a lie?”</p>

<p>At those words, all the nobles lowered their heads.</p>

<p>For a moment, some of them even prayed for the ground to split open so they could jump inside and disappear.</p>

<p>They could almost feel their ears bleeding.</p>

<p>Every one of them was a master liar, but to speak such words with such a straight face and pull off this act of praise and threat at the same time…</p>

<p>Damn.</p>

<p>This man was a god-level liar.</p>

<p>No wonder people said never trust a pretty face.</p>

<p>Just listen to those words, then look at this man.</p>

<p>Did this King look wise?</p>

<p>Even the word “wise” would feel ashamed to be associated with him.</p>

<p>And at the center of it all, Ethan bowed his head slightly, his hands folded, a faint sinister smile hidden from view.</p>

<p>’You want to defeat me in acting?’</p>

<p>’Me?’</p>

<p>’A professional model and part-time actor?’</p>

<p>’Fuck you all. You are years behind.’</p>

<p>“No, Father, you don’t understand!” Albert almost cried. He glared at Ethan with hatred, as if Ethan had stolen something precious from him.</p>

<p>“That is for me to decide,” Mark snapped. “Or do you think you are old enough to understand everything while I have grown too old to judge?”</p>

<p>“Or are you telling me that I am not worthy to sit on this throne?”</p>

<p>“I didn’t mean that!”</p>

<p>“SHUT UP!”</p>

<p>Mark turned and shouted, his voice echoing through the hall.</p>

<p>“Guards! Someone drag away this stupid son of mine and locked him up.”</p>

<p>“You are under house arrest!”</p>

<p>“No!” Albert screamed as the Star Knights rushed forward.</p>

<p>They grabbed him firmly and began dragging him away, his protests echoing down the corridor as the court fell into an uneasy silence.</p>

<p>Albert desperately looked toward Duke Keeves and Duke Longburn, hoping for support. But both men stood frozen, shock written clearly on their faces.</p>

<p>At a time when the lion was roaring at the height of his vanity, what could a pair of mice possibly do?</p>

<p>Nothing.</p>

<p>They could only lower their heads and let him revel in his self-satisfaction. After all, everyone here knew one thing clearly.</p>

<p>This man’s days were already numbered.</p>

<p>Mark then swept his gaze across the hall and shouted, his voice sharp and commanding.</p>

<p>“Imprison that bastard.”</p>

<p>“Put Simon and the head of the Merchant Association into the dungeon.”</p>

<p>Ethan frowned slightly, but he did not let it show on his face.</p>

<p>“His Majesty is magnanimous as always,” Ethan said calmly. “Thank you for listening to the pleas of the people.”</p>

<p>“You have proven once again that despite your age, you are still sound in mind.”</p>

<p>“Hmmp!” Mark nodded with a satisfied grin and proudly rubbed his mustache.</p>

<p>Ethan was about to ask for permission to leave when Mark suddenly spoke again.</p>

<p>“Now that I see it…”</p>

<p>“You are a fine gentleman. And with such strength, I cannot help but admire you.”</p>

<p>“Thank you for praising this lowly one,” Ethan replied politely.</p>

<p>“What lowly one?” Mark waved his hand. “Also, stop being so humble. The more I look at you, the more I like you.”</p>

<p>“So I decided…”</p>

<p>’Decided what?’ Everyone in the court looked toward the King.</p>

<p>“I will grant you a marriage,” Mark announced loudly. “You are free to choose my daughter.”</p>

<p>The entire hall froze.</p>

<p>Eyes widened everywhere.</p>

<p>Even Ethan was caught off guard.</p>

<p>“Your Majesty,” Ethan began carefully, “for now, I have no need—”</p>

<p>“What?” Mark’s expression darkened instantly.”You are refusing me?”</p>

<p>“You dare to refuse me?”</p>

<p>“Am I not the wise King praised by the people, or are you questioning my intelligence?”</p>

<p>“Or are you saying everything you said earlier was false?”</p>

<p>Ethan felt his tongue knot in his mouth.</p>

<p>He looked at the King, who now wore a smug, cheeky smile, clearly enjoying himself as he stroked his beard.</p>

<p>’I’m trapped,’ Ethan thought. ’This old bastard cornered me.’</p>

<p>“I accept, then,” Ethan said at last.</p>

<p>’Having one more woman won’t be a problem,’ he told himself.</p>

<p>“Good. Good,” Mark laughed. “I will choose a girl for you and send her to your estate.”</p>

<p>“Now, adjourn. Everyone get out.”</p>

<p>“This King needs rest.”</p>

<p>He swept his hand dismissively, as if brushing away dust.</p>

<p>The court was dismissed.</p>

<p>As people began leaving, several nobles approached Ethan, forcing polite smiles despite the anger burning in their chests. Appearances still mattered.</p>

<p>Duke Longburn stepped forward first, his expression stiff.</p>

<p>“Lord Ethan,” he said coolly, “you certainly know how to seize opportunity.”</p>

<p>Ethan returned the smile. “Opportunity favors those who act, Duke Longburn.”</p>

<p>“Hmph,” Longburn snorted. “Let us hope your actions do not bring unnecessary trouble.”</p>

<p>Duke Keeves followed, clasping his hands.</p>

<p>“You are bold,” Keeves said. “Very bold. The court has not seen such confidence in years.”</p>

<p>“I merely spoke the truth,” Ethan replied. “If that unsettles some people, then perhaps they should reflect on themselves.”</p>

<p>Keeves laughed softly, though his eyes were sharp. “May your path remain… smooth.”</p>

<p>After exchanging a few more empty pleasantries, the nobles finally dispersed.</p>

<p>As Ethan turned to leave, he suddenly felt it.</p>

<p>Two sharp, heavy gazes locked onto him.</p>

<p>He instinctively turned toward the throne.</p>

<p>But the King had already left.</p>

<p>’Am I hallucinating?’ Ethan frowned inwardly.</p>

<p>He shook his head slightly and continued walking.</p>

<p>Behind him, in the shadows of the court, an old man with a wrinkled face stood silently. His eyes were deep and cold as he watched Ethan’s retreating figure.</p>

<p>“The young cubs know no fear,” the old man muttered softly.</p>

<p>“What a fine young man. Alas, it seems he is destined to stand against us.”</p>

<p>“With this, he has foiled our plans three times already.”</p>

<p>The old man’s lips curved into a thin smile.</p>

<p>“Do not worry.”</p>

<p>“Anyone who stands in our way will be cut apart, sooner or later.”</p>

<p>The shadows swallowed his figure as the great hall slowly emptied.</p>
