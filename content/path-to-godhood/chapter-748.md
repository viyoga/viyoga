<p>"Did..."</p>
<p>"Did you just say that I am a waste of your time?"</p>
<p>Future Ethan simply looked at him without changing his expression."You are Agros."</p>
<p>"Bullshit..."Agros’ face twisted with rage.</p>
<p>"You who don’t know the immensity of Hell and Heaven..."</p>
<p>"Can you stop your chit chat and finish your transformation?"</p>
<p>Future Ethan folded his arms while floating calmly in the sky.</p>
<p>"I can’t wait all day for this."</p>
<p>A thick vein bulged on Agros’ forehead.</p>
<p>His hands slowly clenched into fists as he stared at the white-haired man with pure anger.</p>
<p>The calm expression on Future Ethan’s face only made his anger worse.</p>
<p>He could not understand it.</p>
<p>How could someone stand before him with such a calm expression?</p>
<p>Behind Future Ethan, the survivors had already gathered together. The Emperors, Queen Ophelia, the remaining knights, and the other experts all stared at him with absolute horror and disbelief.</p>
<p>No one spoke for several moments.</p>
<p>Just what was this man?</p>
<p>Their gazes slowly moved between Future Ethan and Ethan.</p>
<p>The two faces looked almost the same.</p>
<p>The difference was the presence surrounding them.</p>
<p>Ethan’s aura was powerful, but the white-haired man carried something completely different. A divine glow surrounded his body, and his presence felt as though he had been touched by the heavens themselves. Every movement carried a calm power that made the surrounding space feel different.</p>
<p>Everyone’s questions became even stronger.</p>
<p>Ethan noticed their gazes and gave a small smile.</p>
<p>"Yeah..."</p>
<p>"It’s me."</p>
<p>"My future self."</p>
<p>His answer struck everyone like thunder.</p>
<p>The survivors stared at him in complete silence.</p>
<p>Some of them even felt as though their minds had stopped working.</p>
<p>Future self?</p>
<p>The man standing before them was Ethan from the future?</p>
<p>The thought alone was enough to leave everyone completely shaken.</p>
<p>Ethan looked at the faces around him.</p>
<p>Awe, horror, and reverence could be clearly seen in everyone’s eyes, and a satisfied feeling rose in his heart when he saw their reactions.</p>
<p>He did not need to keep the existence of his future self a secret anymore. More than that, he wanted to make this appearance an absolute performance.</p>
<p>Once the war was over, many things would change. Humans could never be fully trusted, and Ethan did not know what these Emperors, who had joined hands with him because of an external threat, would do afterward. Would they keep their word, or would they eventually turn against him? Because of that, he needed to create a show that would make them think a hundred, or even a thousand, times before trying anything against him.</p>
<p>He wanted to avoid a situation like Arcadia. Having a monarch and his entire family trying to take your head was an absolutely terrifying thing.</p>
<p>Ethan wanted these new allies and the hidden Ascension Realm experts behind them to understand one thing clearly. If they ever wanted to take him down, it would not be easy. Now that he had the backing of his future self, making a move against him would become even more difficult. He needed them to be afraid. He needed them to understand that provoking him would come with a price.</p>
<p>Amidst these thoughts, another loud explosion suddenly shook the battlefield.</p>
<p>BOOOOM!</p>
<p>The ground trembled beneath everyone’s feet.</p>
<p>Emperor Caelus immediately turned toward the source of the disturbance and pointed ahead.</p>
<p>"Look..."</p>
<p>"It seems they are ready to fight."</p>
<p>Everyone turned around.</p>
<p>From within the dust and ruined ground, a blood-red figure slowly walked forward. The surrounding air seemed to become heavier with every step he took. His body had completely transformed into something that barely resembled a human anymore.</p>
<p>Agros had become a hideous demonic creature.</p>
<p>Two long, twisted horns rose from his head while his skin had turned dark red and covered with black veins. His eyes were deep and bloodshot, and several smaller eyes had opened along his face and neck. His mouth stretched wider than it should have been, revealing rows of sharp teeth. Long claws extended from his fingers, each one dark and pointed like a blade.</p>
<p>Behind him, a thick demonic tail slowly dragged across the ground, leaving deep marks in the broken earth. His body was covered with strange scales and patches of black flesh, while a bloody aura constantly leaked from him.</p>
<p>The creature looked like something that had crawled out from the deepest part of Hell.</p>
<p>Future Ethan looked at his face for a moment before clicking his tongue."Absolute abhorrence."</p>
<p>His expression remained calm.</p>
<p>If he was not trying to show his strength to the world, he would not even waste another moment here. He had already gone through this kind of situation before, and he knew exactly what needed to be done. His goal was not simply to defeat Agros. He wanted everyone watching to remember this battle and understand that he was not someone they could casually provoke.</p>
<p>Future Ethan slowly smiled at Agros.</p>
<p>He raised one hand and casually beckoned him closer.</p>
<p>"Come here...Daddy’s gonna teach you some manners."</p>
<p>Agros’ face twisted with rage.</p>
<p>"Go to hell!"</p>
<p>His mighty roar exploded across the battlefield.</p>
<p>The sound created powerful waves that spread through the air and shook the surrounding mountains.</p>
<p>Future Ethan simply raised one finger and snapped.</p>
<p>The sonic waves suddenly scattered apart.</p>
<p>The terrifying roar disappeared as if it had never existed.</p>
<p>Agros’ expression changed.</p>
<p>Before he could react, his body suddenly disappeared.</p>
<p>The next moment, he appeared directly behind Future Ethan.</p>
<p>His enormous claws flashed with bloody light as he swung them toward Ethan’s back. The sharp claws tore through the air and were only inches away from reaching his body.</p>
<p>Future Ethan did not even turn around.</p>
<p>The moment the claws came close...</p>
<p>BOOM!</p>
<p>A powerful force suddenly erupted.</p>
<p>Agros’ body was forced backward.</p>
<p>He stared at his hand in disbelief.</p>
<p>A portion of his arm was missing from the wrist.</p>
<p>Blood dripped from the damaged area while the flesh slowly began to move and regenerate.</p>
<p>For the first time, the confidence on Agros’ face disappeared.</p>
<p>Future Ethan slowly turned around and looked at him with the same calm expression.</p>
