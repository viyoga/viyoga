<p>He did not know what had happened to him. As he was attacked, he suddenly felt his Draconic Blood and the Flame Tempering Art reacting deep inside his body.</p>
<p>Instead of danger, he felt something different. It felt like a huge opportunity had appeared in front of him.</p>
<p>As if a breakthrough was calling to him. So he let the attack hit him while he focused completely on the feeling that was rising inside.</p>
<p>Under the bombardment of the intense blasts, his body should have been crushed. Yet instead of being damaged, his body grew stronger and harder. His bones felt like steel, and his muscles tightened with unbelievable strength. The Draconic Blood that had been boiling inside him suddenly erupted like a volcano breaking free.</p>
<p>BOOOOM!</p>
<p>Scales appeared over both of his arms and spread across his chest. They extended upward and formed a draconic mask or helmet around his face. His white hair changed and turned into a glowing crimson color. His body temperature rose by a huge margin. The heat coming from him was enough to burn the air.</p>
<p>Flames began to surge around him, swirling like living creatures. They converged toward him, bending and trembling as if kneeling before a king. His body drank in the flames like air.</p>
<p>And at that moment, he broke through the King Knight realm which further boosted his power.</p>
<p>A terrifying intent exploded from his body, shaking the earth. It hit Kai like a wave and gave the demonic Kai a sudden chill that ran down his spine.</p>
<p>“Enoughhhh!” Kai roared loudly. His voice cracked with anger and fear at the same time.</p>
<p>He launched a massive barrage of attacks. Magic circles formed around him and energy waves flew at Ethan from every direction. The strikes came like storms. The air screamed as the magic cut through it.</p>
<p>But Ethan moved swiftly. His body felt weightless.</p>
<p>He weaved through the chaos, dodging each strike with precision. His movements were sharper now. They were faster and filled with unstoppable power. Every time his feet touched the ground, the earth cracked under the pressure and small shockwaves rippled out.</p>
<p>And each breath he took released a burst of fire that scorched the air.</p>
<p>Then Ethan punched.</p>
<p>He pulled his fist back slowly. The air around his hand twisted and trembled. The space around the fist bent and cracked. A loud, ripping sound echoed through the battlefield as if reality itself was being torn apart.</p>
<p>He swung forward.</p>
<p>The punch tore through the air and ripped it open. The wind exploded outward like a bomb. Heat surged. Flames burned the sky. The force of the punch shook the entire land. The ground split apart beneath him and rocks shattered into dust.</p>
<p>The shockwave blasted forward like the punch of a god.</p>
<p>Kai, who had been retreating, suddenly felt his stomach twist. His eyes widened in horror.</p>
<p>Blood burst out of his mouth violently. His ribs cracked. His body shot backward like a broken arrow. He flew across the battlefield and smashed into the ground, ripping through dirt and stone. He crashed and blasted through a layer of rock and slammed into the earth. A huge crater formed and exploded outward, splitting open like a broken shell.</p>
<p>“ARGHHHHHH!” Kai screamed. His voice was filled with pain and despair. “Am I the Demon or you?”</p>
<p>His scream echoed across the silent battlefield.</p>
<p>“Why the hell is this guy getting stronger than me when it’s me who summoned God..” Kai cried out.</p>
<p>His voice broke apart in shock and horror.</p>
<p>It felt like cheating..</p>
<p>He felt like crying. His eyes filled with frustration. This was too much for him.</p>
<p>Everything he did kept failing. Nothing worked. No matter how much power he used, Ethan crushed it.</p>
<p>He cursed his own fate. He wondered why such a demon was allowed to walk freely in this barren land.</p>
<p>Why was he the one facing him?</p>
<p>And why did no one know about him?</p>
<p>Kai knew he could not keep going like this. Gritting his teeth until they almost cracked, he slammed his hands together and started forming symbols. His fingers twisted and locked into complex shapes. He muttered strange chants under his breath. His voice sounded like prayers mixed with curses.</p>
<p>A dense, reddish aura filled the air. It was full of bloodlust and hatred. The air shook. The red energy turned darker until it became pure black, thick like ink. A terrifying power rose from deep within him. Magic circles spun around him. One after another they appeared and overlapped, glowing brighter and brighter.</p>
<p>Suddenly, with a loud swish, a towering pillar of darkness erupted into the sky. The light around them disappeared. Everything went silent. The world dimmed. The colors faded. Within moments, several kilometers around Kai were covered in pitch-black darkness.</p>
<p>People who were watching from afar fell to their knees. Some clutched their heads and screamed. Others cried and begged. Many prayed with shaking voices, whispering desperate words for mercy and divine protection. They did not want to die. They had never felt fear like this. Cries echoed across the hills and fields. The air trembled.</p>
<p>Kai did not care. He raised his hand. His voice was deep and cold.</p>
<p>He spoke.”Abyssal Descent.”</p>
<p>What followed was horrifying. The ground cracked beneath him. Dark fog spread across the battlefield. The air grew heavy with a sick feeling. The sky twisted like something alive.</p>
<p>Trees wilted and died instantly.</p>
<p>Even soldiers far away backed away with terror in their eyes. The spell looked like something from another world. It felt like a curse that rejected life itself.</p>
<p>Ethan watched silently. He narrowed his eyes. His face became serious. He took a slow breath and reached for the hilt of his sword.</p>
<p>“These cultists really do have a lot of tricks,” he muttered quietly. There was no panic in his voice.</p>
<p>He raised his sword and pointed it at the sky. Tiny sparkles of light appeared around him. At first they were few, like wandering fireflies. A moment later there were hundreds. They floated and rotated, forming a spiral of light.</p>
<p>They grew brighter and expanded outward. The light grew hot and burned.</p>
<p>In the middle of the darkness, the lights brought a warm feeling. It spread slowly. A holy humming sound filled the air. It was soft and clear, like a distant sacred song. A huge figure of flame and light formed behind Ethan. It took the shape of a human made of pure brightness.</p>
<p>With a loud boom, a pillar of radiant light shot into the sky. It clashed against the darkness with fierce strength. The figure behind Ethan extended its hand and held the shape of light. Ethan opened his mouth and a powerful glow burst from his body.</p>
<p>Looking up at Kai’s deadly attack, Ethan spoke in a calm voice.</p>
<p>“Let there be light.”</p>
<p>A blinding slash shot forward.</p>
<p>“Sacred Flame Draconic Slash,” he shouted. He named the technique at the moment of his enlightenment.</p>
<p>The light erupted. A furious dragon’s roar echoed across the land. A massive beam of destruction made of pure light surged forward. It coiled like a dragon and charged straight toward the falling darkness.</p>
<p>Then the two powers met.</p>
<p>BOOOOM!</p>
<p>The explosion that followed was beyond imagination. Light and darkness collided in the sky and twisted and tore against each other. The land shook violently and the clouds split open. Shockwaves rolled across the ground.</p>
<p>Trees flattened and ground shook miles away.</p>
<p>Kai’s eyes widened. He watched pure light tear through the abyssal fog and rush toward him. He tried to blink away and teleport. He tried to escape.</p>
<p>But it was too fast.</p>
<p>The beam of light struck him with full force.</p>
<p>“NOOOOOOOO!” he screamed. His voice shook the air. His body burned and his thoughts shattered. He cursed his fate and screamed again. Why did such a powerful man appear here? Why now?</p>
<p>He shouted at his fate but no answer came.</p>
<p>As the light faded and the sky brightened again.</p>
<p>Not a trace of him was left. His existence vanished, erased by the overwhelming force of Ethan’s Attack.</p>
