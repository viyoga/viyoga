<p>Meanwhile, Ethan led the Order of Eternal Blade directly into the heart of the fighting.</p>

<p>His eyes remained locked onto the enemy Divine Realm experts.</p>

<p>Those were his targets.</p>

<p>Those were the enemies he needed to stop.</p>

<p>"Forward!"</p>

<p>His voice thundered across the battlefield.</p>

<p>The knights behind him immediately responded.</p>

<p>Without hesitation, the Order of Eternal Blade charged forward like a spear piercing directly into the enemy formation.</p>

<p>Several Divine Realm experts from the opposing side quickly noticed Ethan’s advance.</p>

<p>"There he is!"</p>

<p>One of them pointed toward him.</p>

<p>"Stop him!"</p>

<p>Another roared.</p>

<p>Powerful auras erupted from all directions as multiple Divine Realm experts rushed forward simultaneously.</p>

<p>The ground beneath them cracked apart.</p>

<p>The surrounding air distorted.</p>

<p>Then the collision finally happened.</p>

<p>BOOOOOOM!</p>

<p>The impact shook the entire battlefield so violently that even soldiers fighting hundreds of meters away felt the ground tremble beneath their feet, and powerful shockwaves spread outward in all directions while forcing nearby warriors to stumble backward and struggle to maintain their footing.</p>

<p>Sword energy exploded across the area continuously while flashes of magic illuminated the battlefield one after another, and the surrounding gorge seemed to be drowned beneath endless waves of destruction.</p>

<p>Several Divine Realm experts immediately surrounded Ethan from different directions.</p>

<p>They clearly intended to overwhelm him through numbers and prevent him from breaking through their formation.</p>

<p>However, Ethan showed absolutely no signs of concern.His expression remained calm.</p>

<p>His sword moved continuously while his free hand began forming spell patterns at the same time, and golden magic circles started appearing around him one after another while filling the battlefield with bright golden light.</p>

<p>The surrounding area instantly lit up.</p>

<p>The enemy experts were caught completely off guard.</p>

<p>Before they could properly react, multiple spells shot outward from the glowing circles.</p>

<p>BOOOOOOM!</p>

<p>A huge explosion erupted among the enemy soldiers and instantly blasted dozens of men into the air.</p>

<p>BOOOOOOM!</p>

<p>Another spell struck an advancing formation and shattered their ranks.</p>

<p>BOOOOOOM!</p>

<p>A third explosion tore through defensive positions and sent soldiers flying in every direction while screams echoed throughout the battlefield.</p>

<p>The Arcadian troops immediately seized the opportunity and launched an aggressive assault.</p>

<p>Their morale surged.</p>

<p>Their momentum rose.</p>

<p>The enemy Divine Realm experts stared at Ethan in complete disbelief.</p>

<p>"What the fuck?!"</p>

<p>One of them barely managed to block Ethan’s sword before shouting in shock.</p>

<p>Another narrowly dodged a spell that exploded beside him and almost cursed aloud.</p>

<p>"What kind of bullshit is this?!"</p>

<p>"Are you a mage or a warrior?"</p>

<p>Several nearby soldiers also looked equally stunned.</p>

<p>Because what Ethan was doing simply made no sense to them.</p>

<p>He was fighting like a frontline warrior while simultaneously casting spells like a high-level mage, and both abilities were being used at a level powerful enough to threaten Divine Realm experts.</p>

<p>Hearing their disbelief, Ethan actually smiled.</p>

<p>The smile was calm and confident.</p>

<p>"I am both."</p>

<p>The words almost made several enemy experts choke on their own breath.Before they could even respond, another series of spells erupted around Ethan.</p>

<p>Massive pillars of fire descended from the sky.Lightning tore through the battlefield.Explosions erupted one after another.</p>

<p>Several enemy formations immediately collapsed beneath the relentless bombardment.</p>

<p>At the same time, Ethan’s sword struck again and forced another Divine Realm expert backward while blood sprayed into the air.</p>

<p>The battle quickly became one-sided.</p>

<p>Several enemy Divine Realm experts suffered injuries.Others were forced into defensive positions.</p>

<p>Some even attempted to retreat.</p>

<p>Meanwhile, the Arcadian side steadily gained momentum with every passing minute.</p>

<p>Victory seemed closer and closer.The enemy formation was beginning to collapse.</p>

<p>The surviving Divine Realm experts were gradually being beaten back.</p>

<p>For a brief moment, it looked as if the battle might soon be over.</p>

<p>However...</p>

<p>Just as Ethan prepared to press the advantage and completely finish off the remaining resistance, a strange feeling suddenly emerged deep inside his chest.</p>

<p>His entire body stiffened.</p>

<p>Every instinct he possessed screamed at him.</p>

<p>Danger.</p>

<p>Extreme danger.</p>

<p>The feeling was so intense that it immediately forced him to stop moving.</p>

<p>Ethan’s expression changed.</p>

<p>The smile disappeared instantly.His eyes narrowed sharply.</p>

<p>Without wasting even a second, he abruptly turned around.</p>

<p>His gaze swept across the battlefield.</p>

<p>Then he saw it.</p>

<p>Far in the distance.</p>

<p>Beyond the main fighting.</p>

<p>Beyond the collapsing enemy formation.</p>

<p>A separate enemy force was moving rapidly away from the battle.</p>

<p>They were not advancing toward the frontline.</p>

<p>They were not targeting the Arcadian soldiers.</p>

<p>They were not attempting to reinforce their allies.</p>

<p>Instead they were heading straight toward the supply convoy.</p>

<p>Straight toward the supply routes.</p>

<p>For a brief second, Ethan’s mind went completely blank.</p>

<p>Then everything suddenly clicked together.</p>

<p>The ambush.</p>

<p>The Divine Realm experts.</p>

<p>The fierce resistance.</p>

<p>The battle inside the gorge.</p>

<p>None of it had been their true objective.</p>

<p>All of it had been a distraction designed to keep Arcadia’s strongest fighters occupied.</p>

<p>Their real target had always been the supplies.</p>

<p>The realization hit Ethan like a bolt of lightning.</p>

<p>"Holy fuckkk!"</p>

<p>Ethan’s voice exploded across the battlefield.</p>

<p>The sudden shout immediately attracted everyone’s attention.Nearby commanders turned toward him.</p>

<p>Randall’s expression changed instantly.</p>

<p>Noel’s eyes widened.</p>

<p>Several nobles followed Ethan’s gaze.</p>

<p>The moment they saw the enemy force rushing toward the rear supply lines, their faces immediately turned ugly.</p>

<p>"The supplies!"</p>

<p>One commander shouted loudly.</p>

<p>"Those bastards are targeting the supplies!"</p>

<p>Another noble’s face immediately turned pale.</p>

<p>"Move!"</p>

<p>"Protect the convoy!"</p>

<p>"Stop them immediately!"</p>

<p>"Don’t let them get through!"</p>

<p>The atmosphere instantly shifted.</p>

<p>What had looked like a victory moments ago suddenly became a full-blown crisis.</p>

<p>Because everyone present understood one terrifying truth.</p>

<p>Losing soldiers was painful while losing equipment was costly.</p>

<p>But losing the supply routes deep inside enemy territory could completely destroy the entire campaign.</p>

<p>Without food, armies starved and the wounded died.</p>

<p>Without weapons and magical resources, soldiers could no longer fight.</p>

<p>And if the enemy successfully burned the food, weapons, medicine, and magical resources supporting tens of millions of soldiers...</p>

<p>Then the Arcadian army would not need to be defeated by the enemy rather it would slowly collapse on its own.</p>
