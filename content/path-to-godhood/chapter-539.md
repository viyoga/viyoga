<p>The three combined attacks came crashing down together, and their intent was clear as they tried to break Ethan’s spell before it could fully activate, but Ethan did not hesitate even for a moment.</p>
<p>He twisted his wrist sharply, and several magic circles spun faster above and around him, while more circles appeared across the sky, layering one over another as energy surged violently.</p>
<p>“Too late,” he muttered.</p>
<p>The next moment…</p>
<p>BOOOOM!</p>
<p>BANNNG!</p>
<p>The magic circles detonated one after another, and the explosions spread across the battlefield like a chain reaction. Each circle burst with immense force, and the combined blasts created a catastrophic wave that shook the entire region.</p>
<p>Fire roared outward in blazing waves, while lightning cracked through the sky and descended like pillars of destruction. Winds howled violently, slicing through everything in their path, and radiant light surged outward, cutting across the battlefield like sharp blades.</p>
<p>The entire area turned into chaos.</p>
<p>Lightning pillars slammed down relentlessly, striking the Orc army and pinning them to the ground, while explosions erupted around them without pause.</p>
<p>“AAAAHHHH!”</p>
<p>“Fall back!”</p>
<p>“Move—!”</p>
<p>Screams filled the air as Orc soldiers were caught in the storm, and many of them were thrown into the air or crushed under the impact of the attacks. Some were burned alive, while others were struck down by lightning or torn apart by the violent winds.</p>
<p>The battlefield had turned into a storm of destruction.</p>
<p>Dust, fire, and energy filled every corner, and visibility dropped as the explosions continued to shake the land.</p>
<p>It was only then that the Orc Divine Knight fully understood what was happening.</p>
<p>His eyes turned bloodshot with anger.</p>
<p>“You bastard…”</p>
<p>He realized it.Ethan was not just defending.He was targeting everything.</p>
<p>The army.</p>
<p>The formation.</p>
<p>The morale.</p>
<p>He was destroying their ability to continue the fight.</p>
<p>The thunderstorm continued to rage, and the ground cracked open under the pressure, while the Orc army fell into panic and disorder.</p>
<p>Amidst all of this,Ethan moved.</p>
<p>Using the cover of smoke, dust, and debris, he shot away from the battlefield at high speed, his figure disappearing into the chaos.</p>
<p>For a brief moment, it seemed like he had escaped.</p>
<p>But a furious roar followed.</p>
<p>“COME BACK, YOU BASTARD!”</p>
<p>The Orc Divine Knight broke through the storm and locked onto Ethan’s fleeing figure, and without hesitation, he gave chase.</p>
<p>“I am going to fucking kill you!”</p>
<p>“Bastard, die!”</p>
<p>The two figures moved across the land at terrifying speed, and the distance between them stretched for miles as they tore through the air.</p>
<p>Ethan did not stop.</p>
<p>But after a while he suddenly halted.</p>
<p>The Orc Divine Knight closed in rapidly, but just as he prepared to strike.</p>
<p>Ethan turned around abruptly.</p>
<p>“Divine Soul Flame.”</p>
<p>CRUNG!</p>
<p>A heavy and invisible force slammed into the Orc Divine Knight’s head, and the flame burned directly into his soul, causing a sharp and intense pain that made his expression twist.</p>
<p>However he had already prepared.</p>
<p>“I am not an idiot,” the Orc Divine Knight growled.</p>
<p>A faint glow appeared around his body as an artifact activated, and a protective barrier formed instantly, reducing the impact of the attack.</p>
<p>Ethan saw it and did not push further.</p>
<p>Instead, he dispersed the flames immediately.</p>
<p>The Orc Divine Knight steadied himself, but before he could react further,chains appeared.</p>
<p>From the void itself, heavy chains shot out and wrapped around his body, binding him tightly in place.</p>
<p>“What—!”</p>
<p>He was caught off guard.</p>
<p>Several attacks followed instantly, emerging from distorted space and slamming into him from different angles, creating a series of explosions that pushed him back slightly.</p>
<p>“Yo…” a faint voice echoed.</p>
<p>For a brief moment, the Orc Divine Knight was restrained.</p>
<p>The chains tightened around him, and their weight felt immense, as if they carried a crushing force that limited his movement.</p>
<p>But he did not panic.Instead, he focused.</p>
<p>A surge of blood-red energy rose from his body as he channeled his power, and his muscles tensed under the pressure.</p>
<p>“Break!”</p>
<p>He swept his arm outward violently.</p>
<p>BOOOOM!</p>
<p>A powerful blast erupted from him, and the chains shattered under the force, breaking apart into fragments as the surrounding space shook.</p>
<p>The restraint lasted only a moment, but that brief opening was enough for Ethan to act, and he did not hesitate as he raised his hand and formed another spell while keeping his eyes fixed on the Orc Divine Knight.</p>
<p>“Holy Sword Judgement Array.”</p>
<p>SWOOOSHH!</p>
<p>Six radiant swords of light appeared high in the air, and they hovered around the Orc Divine Knight while rotating slowly. Each sword shone with a pure and intense glow, and faint runic patterns moved across their surface as they gathered energy.</p>
<p>Then, without warning, all six swords shot forward at once, and they tore through the air with a deep, roaring sound before striking down around him.</p>
<p>BOOOOM!</p>
<p>The moment they landed, pillars of light surged upward and connected instantly, forming a celestial prison that surrounded the Orc Divine Knight from all sides. The light grew brighter and brighter, and the entire area was illuminated as if a second sun had risen.</p>
<p>The pressure became overwhelming, and even the air seemed to freeze under its weight.</p>
<p>The Orc Divine Knight shut his eyes tightly for a moment because the light was too intense, and then he tried to move, but his body felt locked in place.</p>
<p>“What is this—”</p>
<p>He gritted his teeth and forced his power to rise, and then he raised his greatsword and struck forward with all his strength.</p>
<p>CLANG!</p>
<p>A sharp and heavy sound echoed, and the impact created waves that spread outward, but the pillars of light did not break, and instead they held firm while pushing him back.</p>
<p>“My strength…” his eyes widened in horror as he felt something wrong.</p>
<p>“The fuck is going on?”</p>
<p>“ARGHHHHHH!”</p>
<p>He roared and grabbed one of the pillars with both hands, trying to tear it apart, but the moment his skin touched it, a burning pain spread instantly through his arms.</p>
<p>“AAAHHH!”</p>
