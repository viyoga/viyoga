<p>“What do you mean by proof?” Ethan shouted and turned toward Mark. “The Royal Treasury guards themselves came and took the piles of gold.”</p>
<p>He looked directly at the King.</p>
<p>’What the fuck?’</p>
<p>How can the king be so shameless?</p>
<p>When he gave taxes,he even asked for receipts but the people refused saying that there wasn’t any system of giving receipts as proof</p>
<p>A thick vein bulged on his forehead but he decided to calm down.</p>
<p>“Isn’t that right, Your Majesty? This loyal follower has always catered to your wishes and even paid more taxes so the citizens could benefit.”</p>
<p>Mark’s expression stiffened. He looked strangely flustered and raised his voice.</p>
<p>“How would this old man know?”</p>
<p>“Do you think I keep track of every noble’s payments?”</p>
<p>“How would I know?”</p>
<p>He waved his hand dismissively.</p>
<p>“Go and ask the person you paid to.”</p>
<p>“What?” Ethan’s eyes shook as he blinked in disbelief. “Do I need to find that person myself?”</p>
<p>“Yes,” Mark replied coldly. “Go and bring that bastard here. How could he not report it to me? If it’s true, then I will reward you.”</p>
<p>“Alright…” Ethan said slowly. He was genuinely about to turn and leave.</p>
<p>Just then, a calm voice echoed from outside the hall.</p>
<p>“I think we are discussing the wrong things here.”</p>
<p>Everyone turned toward the entrance.</p>
<p>A blond man walked in with a faint smile on his face.</p>
<p>“Your Highness!”</p>
<p>“The Third Prince!”</p>
<p>Everyone quickly bowed as Albert De Rudius, the Third Prince of Ruthiana, stepped inside. He walked forward and bowed respectfully to his father.</p>
<p>“Greetings, Father. I hope I have not interrupted the court.”</p>
<p>Mark snorted but did not stop him.</p>
<p>Albert then turned his gaze toward Ethan.</p>
<p>“Instead of arguing over these trivial matters,” Albert said calmly, “shouldn’t we ask why Lord Ethan did not take part in the war despite possessing such strength?”</p>
<p>Those words lit up the eyes of many nobles. They immediately turned toward Ethan, waiting for his response.</p>
<p>“It’s because I was weak,” Ethan answered honestly. “When the war began, I was only at the Intermediate Stage, and I had many small children.”</p>
<p>He clenched his jaw.</p>
<p>“Since I rose from being a commoner, I value my life. I was afraid.”</p>
<p>“Oh?” Albert smiled faintly, a sinister glint flashing in his eyes.</p>
<p>“That means you rose from a mere Intermediate Stage to this level in just a few years,” he said. “And from what you said, you feared losing your life back then, but now you act so freely.”</p>
<p>Albert’s voice sharpened.</p>
<p>“So does that mean you no longer place Royal Authority in your eyes?”</p>
<p>“You are strong now,” he continued. “Strong enough to disregard everything. So you think you can defy us.”</p>
<p>Countless sharp gazes stabbed into Ethan. They were filled with mockery, resentment, and hatred. Many of them wished to see him fall on the spot.</p>
<p>Mark’s face darkened, and he shouted.</p>
<p>“Is that true?”</p>
<p>“Are you not taking me seriously anymore?”</p>
<p>Albert watched the scene with quiet amusement. He wanted to see how Ethan would escape.</p>
<p>Or whether he could escape at all.</p>
<p>Ethan slowly shook his head and gave a small bow.</p>
<p>“Of course not, Your Majesty. That was never my intention.”</p>
<p>“Then what is it?” Mark demanded.</p>
<p>“It’s because I believe in you,” Ethan replied calmly.</p>
<p>“Huh?”</p>
<p>“What?”</p>
<p>The entire hall was taken aback. No one expected such a response.</p>
<p>Mark frowned, his expression shifting.</p>
<p>“What do you mean?” he asked.</p>
<p>“As I said, I believe in your benevolence.”</p>
<p>“From my days as a commoner, I always looked at you with reverence. Magnanimous, benevolent, and kind.”</p>
<p>“You are the epitome of righteousness, Your Majesty!”</p>
<p>The moment Ethan said this, a deafening silence spread through the entire hall.</p>
<p>Several nobles shook their heads and stared at Ethan in horror.</p>
<p>’What the fuck are you saying?’</p>
<p>’Do you even believe the words coming out of your own mouth?’</p>
<p>Then, slowly, their gazes shifted toward the King.</p>
<p>Sure enough, a huge smile had spread across Mark’s face, so wide it looked like it might tear his cheeks apart.</p>
<p>“Oh?” Mark said, clearly pleased. “Why did you stop? Carry on.”</p>
<p>“Cough!”</p>
<p>Ethan cleared his throat and continued, his voice steady.</p>
<p>“I mean to say that I believe in you. I know that the moment you hear how your people suffered under the tyranny of the Adventurer Guild, you will act immediately.”</p>
<p>“You might not know this, but when I was capturing those bandits, the people who were imprisoned kept saying something.”</p>
<p>He lifted his head slightly.</p>
<p>“They said, ’Our Majesty will avenge us.’”</p>
<p>“’Fear the wrath of our King!’”</p>
<p>“’For he is a righteous warrior whose might will sweep evil away.’”</p>
<p>“They believed that once the King learned of the Adventurer Guild’s crimes, those evildoers would be destroyed. That is why the Adventurer Guild blocked all messages.”</p>
<p>For a brief moment, Albert’s vision went dark.</p>
<p>’Who is he talking about?’</p>
<p>’What the hell is he even saying?’</p>
<p>Ethan then lowered himself and knelt on one knee before the throne.</p>
<p>“Your Majesty,” he said, his expression fearful and sorrowful, yet filled with expectation.</p>
<p>“As a righteous monarch who rules the hearts of the people, I beg you to act and stop the tyranny of the Adventurer Guild.”</p>
<p>“Because if you don’t…”</p>
<p>Mark leaned forward. “Then?”</p>
<p>“Then the people’s hearts will break,” Ethan replied calmly. “Their faith in you will shatter. All the reverence they hold for you will disappear.”</p>
<p>“Your image as a wise and good King will change into that of a foolish ruler who does not care for his people.”</p>
<p>“This devastating blow will be used by others to incite rebellion and sway the masses.”</p>
<p>“NO!” Albert shouted, unable to endure it any longer.</p>
<p>“Father, he is lying!”</p>
<p>“Don’t listen to him!”</p>
<p>“What… what did you say?” Mark stood up abruptly and pointed at his son, his face burning with rage.</p>
<p>His entire expression twisted.</p>
<p>“Do you mean to say that I am not a wise King?”</p>
<p>“Do you mean to say that I am not righteous?”</p>
