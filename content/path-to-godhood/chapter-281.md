<p>The prison guards yawned as the first bright light of dawn spilled across the stone corridor.Their eyes were heavy, and their steps dragged as they carried a metal food tray between them.</p>

<p>The air was cold and damp, and the silence of the prison pressed on their nerves.One of them stretched his neck and sighed, trying to shake off his sleepiness.</p>

<p>Then the warden’s low voice stopped him cold.</p>

<p>“Be careful,” the warden warned.</p>

<p>“Don’t piss him off.”</p>

<p>“If you anger him, he might beat you to death.”</p>

<p>The guard shivered when he heard that.</p>

<p>GULP!</p>

<p>He swallowed his saliva hard and nodded, gripping the tray tighter.”Yes… yes, sir,” he muttered, forcing his legs to move.</p>

<p>He walked forward and stopped in front of the cell.The iron door creaked as he opened it and stepped inside.</p>

<p>For a moment, everything was silent.</p>

<p>Too silent.It felt like even the air had stopped moving.</p>

<p>“Huh… why is it so calm?” he whispered.</p>

<p>He looked around and saw the man sitting on the chair, his broad back facing him.The figure did not move.The guard forced himself to speak.</p>

<p>“Sir… Here is your breakfast.”</p>

<p>There was no response.</p>

<p>The silence grew heavier, squeezing his chest.His heartbeat sounded loud in his ears.</p>

<p>He waited for a shout, a curse, anything.</p>

<p>Nothing came.Fear crawled up his spine.</p>

<p>He slowly placed the tray on the table, his hands trembling.”Sir?” he called again, his voice weaker now.</p>

<p>His throat went dry.He did not want to stay there any longer.Turning around, he decided to leave quietly.But the moment he took a step back, something fell.</p>

<p>THUD!</p>

<p>“Huuhh!”</p>

<p>He jumped in shock, nearly dropping to the ground.</p>

<p>The burly figure had fallen forward onto the floor.</p>

<p>The sound echoed through the cell.</p>

<p>The guard’s mind went blank.For a moment, he was scared out of his wits.His legs trembled so badly he could barely stand.</p>

<p>The body did not move.</p>

<p>“Wait a minute…” he whispered.</p>

<p>“Why can’t I feel his life’s presence?”</p>

<p>Cold air rushed into his lungs as he sucked in a sharp breath.His instincts screamed at him to run, but his feet refused to move.</p>

<p>Step by step, he inched closer.With shaking hands, he grabbed the body and turned it over.</p>

<p>“AAAAAAAAHHHHHHHH!”</p>

<p>A terrifying scream tore from his throat.</p>

<p>The face staring back at him was twisted beyond human recognition.Bones were bent at impossible angles, skin bruised and broken, eyes wide open and empty.It did not look like a man anymore.It looked like something dragged out of a nightmare.</p>

<p>The guard crawled backward in terror, his hands scraping against the stone floor.</p>

<p>“GHOST!” he screamed.</p>

<p>“GHOST!”</p>

<p>“KYAAA!”</p>

<p>He turned around and ran as fast as he could.</p>

<p>His screams echoed through the prison corridors.Panic spread like fire, and guards rushed out of their posts in confusion.</p>

<p>The entire prison descended into chaos.</p>

<p>After a hurried check, the truth was confirmed.</p>

<p>Simon was dead.Especially the warden, who almost pissed himself when he saw the body.</p>

<p>Fear spread across his face as he realized what kind of trouble was coming for him.</p>

<p>…</p>

<p>The news shook the entire capital.</p>

<p>The death of Simon made everyone shiver.</p>

<p>A King-rank Knight had died in broad daylight under the King’s throne.</p>

<p>It was terrifying.</p>

<p>More than that, people were horrified just thinking about who could have done it.</p>

<p>“What is this bullshit!”King Mark slammed his fist on the armrest of his throne.</p>

<p>“Is there even law and order anymore?”</p>

<p>“Killed directly under my rule?”</p>

<p>“Who did this?”</p>

<p>“Just who!”</p>

<p>At that moment, Duke Longburn turned his gaze toward Ethan.One by one, the nobles followed.</p>

<p>Cough!</p>

<p>Ethan cleared his throat and spoke calmly.”Your Majesty… it can be anyone but me.”</p>

<p>“Why not you?” Mark asked, confused.</p>

<p>“Your Majesty, just think,” Ethan said.</p>

<p>“Who had enmity with that man?”</p>

<p>“It was me, right?”</p>

<p>“Then no matter who killed him, I would be under suspicion.”</p>

<p>“I wouldn’t be an idiot to do that and get myself killed.”</p>

<p>“But what if that’s exactly what you did?” Duke Keeves pressed.</p>

<p>“You used that opportunity to kill him. Isn’t that darkness under the lamp?”</p>

<p>Ethan glared at him sharply.’Just you wait… I will remember you and write your name on my list.’</p>

<p>For a moment, Duke Keeves felt a strange chill.</p>

<p>“Yes… you might have used that disguise,” he insisted.</p>

<p>“Your Majesty, they are pinning their anger on me,” Ethan said.</p>

<p>“Even if I did it, how could I do it so swiftly?”</p>

<p>“I killed without making any noise?”</p>

<p>“Did you forget our battle?”</p>

<p>“It shook the area. How could I subdue him quietly?”</p>

<p>Mark frowned deeply and shouted,”Auster! Where the hell did you die? Show yourself!”</p>

<p>Auster appeared instantly and bowed.</p>

<p>“Yes, Your Majesty.”</p>

<p>“What did you find? Any sign of poison?” Mark asked.</p>

<p>Auster shook his head.”We examined the body. There was no poison.”</p>

<p>“The man was beaten and tortured.”</p>

<p>“We checked thoroughly.”</p>

<p>“Just how?” Duke Keeves asked in shock.</p>

<p>Auster spoke carefully.”My guess is that he was kidnapped at midnight using spatial magic.”</p>

<p>“He was tortured, killed, and then left back here.”</p>

<p>“There may have been a mage who silenced everything.”</p>

<p>As the nobles reached different conclusions, Ethan smiled inwardly.After killing Simon, he had used his insignia to remove all poison traces.</p>

<p>The Goddess of Light’s blessing gave him resistance to disease and poison.Unless it was god-level poison, nothing could affect him.</p>

<p>“Enough!” Mark shouted, unable to tolerate it anymore.</p>

<p>“Auster, Baltar, investigate carefully and submit the results.”</p>

<p>“Yes, Your Majesty.”</p>

<p>“And you!” Mark pointed at Ethan.</p>

<p>Ethan stiffened.”I have already sent a list,” Mark said coldly.I will give you one day.”</p>

<p>“Choose.”</p>

<p>Ethan shrank back and bowed deeply.”I will, Your Majesty.”</p>

<p>“Adjourn,” Mark said, waving his hand.</p>

<p>As everyone walked away, Baltar glared at Ethan coldly.</p>

<p>He was sure Ethan had killed Simon.But he had no proof.</p>

<p>“Stop glaring at him,” Auster whispered.</p>

<p>“Don’t piss him off, or you might be next.”</p>

<p>Baltar froze.</p>

<p>’Enjoy it while you can,’ he thought bitterly.</p>

<p>’I will see who has the last laugh.’</p>

<p>Meanwhile, inside an inn.</p>

<p>“This is the list,” Randall said, handing Ethan a file.</p>

<p>Ethan raised his brows and took it.</p>

<p>“So many?”</p>

<p>“Not many,” Randall replied. “Just five.”</p>

<p>“This contains all the information Black Thorns managed to gather.”</p>

<p>Ethan sucked in a cold breath.”Anyone noteworthy?”</p>

<p>“Not that I know,” Randall said.</p>

<p>“They are all the same.”</p>

<p>“….”</p>

<p>…….</p>
