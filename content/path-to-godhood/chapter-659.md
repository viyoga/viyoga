<p>Slowly, his gaze returned to the first reward.</p>

<p>Ten percent Law of Light Comprehension.</p>

<p>He read the words once.</p>

<p>Then he read them again.</p>

<p>His breathing gradually became heavier and his fingers twitched slightly against the armrest of his chair. A strange numbness spread across his scalp while a chill quietly traveled down his spine.</p>

<p>Among all the treasures he had obtained throughout his life, very few had managed to shock him to this degree.</p>

<p>Because this was no longer a technique.</p>

<p>A true Law.</p>

<p>The very foundation that countless Transcendent Knights, and Ascension experts pursued throughout their entire lives.</p>

<p>For a Transcendent Knight to advance into the Ascension Realm, one needed to comprehend a Law. Many powerful experts became trapped at the peak of the Transcendent Realm because they could not take that final step.</p>

<p>His father-in-law was one of them.</p>

<p>Duke Lancelot,his father in law possessed overwhelming strength and stood among the strongest experts in the Empire, yet even he remained stuck before the gates of Ascension because comprehending a Law was simply too difficult.</p>
