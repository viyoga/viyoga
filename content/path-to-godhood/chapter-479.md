<p>“POUR THE KNIGHT INSIGHT!”</p>
<p>“LET IT BLOOM!”</p>
<p>The next moment Ethan activated the final reward.The thousand years of Knight Insight.</p>
<p>A massive surge of combat knowledge and strength rushed into his mind.</p>
<p>BOOOOM!</p>
<p>The energy exploded through his body like a violent storm.</p>
<p>His aura surged outward.</p>
<p>The chamber trembled while cracks spread across the stone floor beneath him.</p>
<p>Ethan threw his head back and laughed loudly.</p>
<p>“HELL YEAHHHH!”</p>
<p>Inside the secluded stone chamber deep within the fortress of Blanks, the violent storm of power surrounding Ethan continued to intensify while waves of mana surged outward from his body and struck the reinforced walls of the room with dull vibrating echoes.</p>
<p>Ethan remained seated in the center of the chamber, yet the calm posture of his body stood in complete contrast with the terrifying transformation taking place inside him.</p>
<p>The Divine Soul Flame that had ignited earlier inside his soul was no longer a small flicker of light. It had grown into a radiant golden fire burning steadily at the core of his spiritual sea. Instead of harming his soul, the flame continued refining it layer by layer while burning away the faint impurities and weaknesses that had accumulated through years of battle and growth.</p>
<p>His soul gradually became clearer and stronger.</p>
<p>The structure of his spiritual core began to transform as the golden flame circulated around it in slow spirals. Each rotation of the flame compressed the spiritual energy inside him and made it denser and purer.</p>
<p>At the same time, the Draconic Bloodline Potion continued spreading through his body.</p>
<p>His veins pulsed violently while the draconic energy merged with his own blood. Crimson flames and faint void-black sparks occasionally burst from his skin before fading again. The mixture of Flame Dragon bloodline and Void Dragon essence created a powerful resonance inside him.</p>
<p>His bones began strengthening as the draconic energy seeped deep into the marrow. His muscles tightened and restructured themselves while layers of hidden potential awakened within his cells.</p>
<p>Even his heartbeat had changed.</p>
<p>Each pulse pushed a powerful wave of mana through his body, circulating energy faster and more efficiently than before.</p>
<p>Then came the final surge.</p>
<p>The thousand years of Knight Insight that he had released earlier finally began integrating completely with his mind and soul.</p>
<p>Countless battle experiences, combat principles, sword techniques, and instinctive reactions merged with his consciousness. The massive accumulation of knowledge no longer remained scattered fragments.</p>
<p>Instead they fused together and formed a unified foundation of martial understanding.</p>
<p>The moment this process completed, Ethan’s aura suddenly changed.</p>
<p>A powerful pressure burst outward from his body.</p>
<p>BOOOOM!</p>
<p>The mana inside the chamber exploded outward in a circular wave as the boundary of his cultivation shattered.</p>
<p>Ethan’s eyes snapped open.</p>
<p>His pupils glowed faintly while a terrifying aura surged upward from his body like a rising storm.The barrier that had separated the Legendary Knight realm from the next stage finally collapsed.</p>
<p>Ethan had stepped into a completely new level.</p>
<p>Early Mythic Knight.</p>
<p>But the transformation did not stop there.</p>
<p>Normally such a breakthrough required long years of stabilization and cultivation. However Ethan possessed an enormous advantage.</p>
<p>The thousand years of Knight Insight continued fueling his growth.</p>
<p>His newly strengthened soul absorbed the knowledge rapidly while his draconic bloodline provided the raw energy needed to support the advancement.</p>
<p>The Divine Soul Flame burned brighter inside his spiritual sea while refining the incoming power.</p>
<p>His aura continued rising.</p>
<p>The pressure inside the chamber became even heavier while the air itself seemed to distort around him.</p>
<p>Ethan’s body trembled slightly as the next wave of power surged through his veins.</p>
<p>His bones cracked softly while reorganizing themselves to support the stronger energy flow. His muscles expanded slightly while the density of his physical body increased dramatically.</p>
<p>Even his breathing carried a faint draconic resonance.</p>
<p>The transformation reached deeper into his soul.The golden flame wrapped around the core of his spiritual sea and compressed it again.</p>
<p>Another invisible barrier shattered.</p>
<p>BOOOOM!</p>
<p>A second wave of energy exploded outward from Ethan’s body.</p>
<p>The stone floor beneath him cracked in several places while the mana lamps along the walls flickered violently. However, as the shockwaves expanded, the protective runes engraved across the chamber walls suddenly activated.</p>
<p>The runic arrays began glowing one after another.</p>
<p>A translucent barrier formed across the entire chamber and quickly contained the violent energy released from Ethan’s breakthrough. Instead of letting the power scatter uselessly, the runes absorbed the overflowing mana and redirected it toward the storage arrays embedded in the walls.</p>
<p>Crystalline mana stones slowly began forming inside those containers.</p>
<p>This clever design had originally been proposed by Mareti, who believed that powerful breakthroughs should not waste the released energy.</p>
<p>Ethan slowly exhaled as the final surge of power settled within him.</p>
<p>His aura gradually stabilized.</p>
<p>The violent storm of energy surrounding him slowly faded into a steady yet overwhelming presence.</p>
<p>He had not only stepped into the Mythic realm.</p>
<p>He had advanced further.</p>
<p>Middle Mythic Knight.</p>
<p>The change was profound.</p>
<p>At the Mythic level, a knight was already approaching the realm that ordinary people considered divine.</p>
<p>Ethan could now feel the world around him in an entirely different way.</p>
<p>Mana no longer felt like something distant or external. Instead it flowed naturally around him as if responding to his presence.</p>
<p>The smallest fluctuations in the surrounding air became visible to his senses.</p>
<p>He could feel the rhythm of energy moving through the fortress walls and even the faint mana currents flowing beneath the ground.</p>
<p>His perception had expanded greatly.His aura had also changed.</p>
<p>It no longer felt like the simple oppressive pressure of a powerful warrior.</p>
<p>Instead it carried a deeper intent.</p>
<p>A mixture of draconic dominance, refined soul power, and overwhelming knightly authority.</p>
<p>Anyone standing near him would feel an instinctive sense of submission.</p>
<p>Ethan slowly stood up.The simple movement caused a faint ripple of pressure to spread across the chamber.</p>
<p>He looked at his hands quietly while flexing his fingers.</p>
<p>The strength inside his body felt completely different now.</p>
<p>More stable.</p>
<p>More refined.</p>
<p>More terrifying.</p>
<p>A faint smile slowly appeared on his face.</p>
<p>“…So this is the Mythic realm.”</p>
