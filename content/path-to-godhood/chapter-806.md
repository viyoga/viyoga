<p>The moment he spoke, Ethan saw a shock flash across Dorian’s expression.</p>
<p>"Who said that?"</p>
<p>"No one needs to say. I can infer it myself." Ethan replied and looked back at the figure.</p>
<p>If there were cultivators in this world, they would already be revered. But since these three were able to control this person, that meant the person might not be from this world.</p>
<p>In many stories, cultivators fighting caused destruction of the void that led them to fall into different worlds. So a cultivator appearing here wasn’t unusual. But this person was quite strong.</p>
<p>He seemed to be both a body and sword cultivator with various divine abilities.</p>
<p>"So this was your final trump card. No wonder you don’t see us in the eye."</p>
<p>Ethan then looked at the others.</p>
<p>"I will take him out. Meanwhile you all subdue them."</p>
<p>Hearing this, Dorian immediately moved the flute and began playing again.</p>
<p>The strange melody spread across the battlefield. The green-robed figure raised his hands and flashed through weird hand symbols one after another.</p>
<p>Ethan’s eyes narrowed.</p>
<p>He had already seen those hand signs before.</p>
<p>The green-robed figure suddenly pushed both hands forward.</p>
<p>The air above him twisted.</p>
<p>A huge mass of flames began forming in the sky. Fire gathered from all directions and compressed into a blazing sphere before the green-robed figure suddenly thrust his palm forward.</p>
<p>The fire exploded outward.</p>
<p>A huge wave of flames rushed toward Ethan.</p>
<p>"A cultivator spell...Let’s see how strong it is."</p>
<p>Instead of being afraid, Ethan was quite excited.</p>
<p>He pulled out a magical staff to enhance his magic power and immediately raised it toward the incoming attack.</p>
<p>"Wind!"</p>
<p>The air around Ethan suddenly began spinning.</p>
<p>A massive current of wind gathered in front of him and compressed into several rotating blades. The blades grew larger as more magical power poured into them.</p>
<p>Ethan swung his staff forward following which the wind blades shot out.</p>
<p>The first fireball collided with the wind attack.</p>
<p>BOOOOM!</p>
<p>The impact created a huge explosion in the sky.</p>
<p>Flames spread in every direction while the wind blades tore through the burning mass. Ethan pushed more magic into his spell, and the wind began rotating faster.</p>
<p>But the green-robed figure raised one hand.</p>
<p>Another wave of flames rushed forward.</p>
<p>It slammed into the wind attack and pushed it backward.</p>
<p>Ethan’s expression changed slightly.He planted himself in the air and gripped his staff with both hands.</p>
<p>The wind around him became even stronger.</p>
<p>The two attacks pushed against each other in the middle of the battlefield. Fire tried to swallow the wind while the wind tore the flames apart.</p>
<p>The surrounding clouds were blown away.</p>
<p>Then the two attacks finally reached their limit.</p>
<p>BOOOOOOM!</p>
<p>Both powers exploded at the same time and annihilated each other.The shockwave spread across the sky and pushed Ethan’s hair and clothes backward.</p>
<p>Ethan’s eyes became brighter.</p>
<p>The green-robed figure wasn’t done.</p>
<p>He raised both hands again.</p>
<p>This time, the flames didn’t gather in one place.</p>
<p>Several smaller flames appeared around his body.</p>
<p>Then they suddenly changed.</p>
<p>Wind began spinning around them and lightning started forming between them.</p>
<p>In just a few seconds, fire, wind and lightning had gathered together.</p>
<p>The green-robed figure moved his fingers.</p>
<p>All the attacks shot toward Ethan at once.</p>
<p>Ethan’s expression became serious.</p>
<p>"Coming together, huh?"</p>
<p>He immediately raised his staff.</p>
<p>A huge magic circle appeared in front of him.</p>
<p>The first fire attack slammed into it.</p>
<p>BOOM!</p>
<p>The barrier shook.</p>
<p>Before Ethan could react, a sharp wind blade came from the side.He moved his staff and created another wind blade.</p>
<p>CLANG!</p>
<p>The two wind attacks collided and scattered into countless smaller currents.</p>
<p>A bolt of lightning suddenly came from above.</p>
<p>Ethan tilted his body.</p>
<p>The lightning passed beside his shoulder and struck the air behind him.</p>
<p>CRAAACK!</p>
<p>The sky lit up.</p>
<p>Ethan quickly swung his staff upward.</p>
<p>A thick bolt of lightning shot out from the magic circle and collided with another lightning attack.</p>
<p>The two lightning bolts twisted around each other before exploding.</p>
<p>But more attacks were already coming.</p>
<p>Three fireballs rushed toward Ethan from different directions.</p>
<p>He raised his hand and created a wall of wind.</p>
<p>The first fireball hit it and exploded.</p>
<p>The second punched through the flames.</p>
<p>Ethan turned his body and swept his staff sideways.</p>
<p>A curved wind blade cut the fireball apart before it reached him.</p>
<p>The third came from behind.</p>
<p>Ethan sensed it and ducked.</p>
<p>The fireball passed over his head and exploded in the distance.</p>
<p>Ethan immediately countered.</p>
<p>He pointed his staff toward the green-robed figure.</p>
<p>Several wind blades appeared around him.</p>
<p>They shot forward one after another.</p>
<p>The green-robed figure moved his hands.</p>
<p>Walls of fire appeared in front of him.</p>
<p>The wind blades tore through the first wall.</p>
<p>Then the second.</p>
<p>By the time they reached the third wall, their strength had already weakened.</p>
<p>The remaining blades were swallowed by the flames.</p>
<p>Ethan didn’t stop.</p>
<p>He rushed forward while continuing to cast spells.</p>
<p>The green-robed figure also moved toward him.</p>
<p>The distance between them quickly closed.</p>
<p>Fire and wind exploded between the two figures while lightning flashed around them.</p>
<p>Ethan swung his staff and released a large wind blade at close range.</p>
<p>The green-robed figure raised his palm.</p>
<p>A wall of fire appeared.</p>
<p>The wind blade struck it directly.</p>
<p>BOOOOM!</p>
<p>The two powers exploded between them.</p>
<p>Ethan moved through the smoke.</p>
<p>Another fire attack suddenly came toward his face.</p>
<p>He twisted his body and avoided it.</p>
<p>A lightning bolt followed.</p>
<p>Ethan raised his staff and redirected it into the ground.</p>
<p>The ground below exploded.</p>
<p>He immediately sent a wind attack back toward the green-robed figure.</p>
<p>The man moved his hand and a powerful gust of wind appeared in front of him.</p>
<p>The two wind attacks collided.</p>
<p>The air itself seemed to shake.</p>
<p>Neither side gave way.</p>
<p>The green-robed figure suddenly formed another hand sign.</p>
<p>Ethan’s eyes narrowed.</p>
<p>Lightning began gathering around the man’s body.</p>
<p>At the same time, flames gathered around his other hand.</p>
<p>Ethan’s grip tightened around his staff.</p>
<p>He could already tell that the next attack would be stronger.</p>
<p>But all it did was to make him more excited.</p>
<p>He was finally fighting someone who used powers that were completely different from the magic and knight techniques of this world.</p>
