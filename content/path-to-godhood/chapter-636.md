<p>A Saint Realm Knight led the charge through the battlefield like an unstoppable spear piercing through flesh, while leading a group of elite experts directly toward the supply convoy positioned deep behind Arcadia’s main forces, and his objective was so clear that there was no need for words because everyone understood that if the supplies were destroyed, the entire campaign would suffer a devastating blow regardless of how many victories Arcadia achieved on the battlefield.</p>
<p>Every soldier who tried to block their path was swept aside mercilessly.</p>
<p>Some were thrown away by the shockwaves released from the Saint’s attacks.</p>
<p>Others were cut down before they could even properly react.</p>
<p>The group moved with terrifying speed while tearing through formations one after another and leaving chaos in their wake.</p>
<p>Their objective was simple.</p>
<p>Destroy the supplies.</p>
<p>Destroy everything supporting the Arcadian army.</p>
<p>As long as the food reserves, medicine, weapons, and magical resources disappeared, the army would slowly collapse regardless of how strong its soldiers were.</p>
<p>The Saint Realm Knight understood this perfectly.</p>
<p>That was why he completely ignored everything else happening around him and charged toward the rear relentlessly while focusing only on reaching the supply convoy.</p>
<p>The soldiers responsible for protecting the rear desperately attempted to form defensive lines while shouting orders at one another.</p>
<p>"Stop them!"</p>
<p>"Protect the supplies!"</p>
<p>"Don’t let them through!"</p>
<p>Their voices echoed throughout the battlefield while hundreds of soldiers rushed forward to intercept the incoming force.</p>
<p>However, against a Saint Realm powerhouse, ordinary soldiers were little more than obstacles standing in the path of a storm.</p>
<p>The Saint swung his sword once.</p>
<p>BOOOOM!</p>
<p>A huge shockwave erupted outward and instantly blasted dozens of soldiers away while completely destroying the defensive formation they had worked so hard to establish.</p>
<p>The line collapsed immediately.</p>
<p>Meanwhile, the experts of Arcadia finally noticed what was happening.</p>
<p>The moment they realized the enemy’s true objective, their hearts jolted violently.</p>
<p>"Damn it!"</p>
<p>"Stop him!"</p>
<p>Several Arcadian experts immediately abandoned their current opponents and attempted to rush toward the rear to intercept the Saint Realm Knight before he reached the supplies.</p>
<p>However, before they could travel far, the experts of Xylos suddenly intercepted them.</p>
<p>BOOOOM!</p>
<p>Powerful attacks collided in the sky.Explosions erupted continuously.</p>
<p>Sword energy and magic filled the battlefield.</p>
<p>The Xylos experts fought desperately.They were not trying to win.</p>
<p>They were not even trying to survive.</p>
<p>Their objective was simply to delay Arcadia for a few moments.</p>
<p>Even a brief delay would be enough.</p>
<p>The brief struggle immediately created an opening.</p>
<p>And the Saint Realm Knight seized that opportunity without hesitation.His figure shot forward like a bolt of lightning.</p>
<p>The supply convoy was now within reach.</p>
<p>Seeing this, Ethan’s eyes narrowed sharply.His expression darkened immediately.</p>
<p>’Fucking hell!’</p>
<p>His fists clenched tightly.</p>
<p>’If they destroy the grain, everyone is going to be in deep shit.’</p>
<p>’I can’t let that happen!’</p>
<p>Without wasting even a moment, Ethan activated his mana.</p>
<p>BOOOOM!</p>
<p>A powerful aura erupted from his body and spread across the battlefield like a violent storm.</p>
<p>The surrounding air trembled.</p>
<p>Several Divine Knights nearby were forced backward by the sudden pressure while even the enemy experts instinctively stepped away from him.</p>
<p>The Divine Realm expert facing Ethan immediately sensed danger.</p>
<p>However, Ethan had already moved.His aura exploded outward once more.</p>
<p>His sword swept across the battlefield with terrifying force and directly forced the Divine Knight away.</p>
<p>The enemy attempted to resist.</p>
<p>He attempted to block.He attempted to stand his ground.</p>
<p>But Ethan’s attack was simply too fierce.</p>
<p>BANG!</p>
<p>The Divine Knight was knocked aside brutally while blood sprayed from his mouth.</p>
<p>Before anyone could properly react, Ethan’s eyes landed upon a fallen spear lying nearby among the wreckage of the battlefield.</p>
<p>Without hesitation, he grabbed it.His fingers tightened around the shaft.</p>
<p>The spear immediately began vibrating violently from the sheer amount of mana flowing into it.</p>
<p>Ethan slowly bent his body.His muscles tightened.</p>
<p>His legs rooted themselves firmly into the ground.</p>
<p>His torso twisted backward while gathering force throughout every part of his body.</p>
<p>A rippling motion spread from his feet to his waist, from his waist to his shoulders, and from his shoulders directly into his arm.</p>
<p>The surrounding soldiers stared in confusion.Nobody understood what he was doing.</p>
<p>Then Ethan moved.</p>
<p>BOOOOOOM!</p>
<p>The spear left his hand.</p>
<p>The ground beneath Ethan’s feet exploded instantly.</p>
<p>Large cracks spread outward in every direction.</p>
<p>Dust and debris were blasted into the air.</p>
<p>The spear shot forward like a meteor descending from the heavens while carrying enough force to destroy everything standing in its path.</p>
<p>The surrounding air shattered.</p>
<p>The battlefield split apart.</p>
<p>Everything before it was blown away.</p>
<p>Soldiers.</p>
<p>Dust.</p>
<p>Debris.</p>
<p>Broken weapons.</p>
<p>Even the air itself seemed unable to withstand the force behind the attack.</p>
<p>The Saint Realm Knight who was rushing toward the convoy suddenly felt danger.</p>
<p>Not ordinary danger.</p>
<p>Not battlefield danger.</p>
<p>A terrifying danger that made every instinct inside his body scream.His entire body reacted immediately.</p>
<p>Without hesitation, he abruptly turned around.</p>
<p>The moment he did, his eyes widened.</p>
<p>A spear was already upon him.</p>
<p>The terrifying projectile was tearing through everything around it while carrying enough force to shake the battlefield itself.</p>
<p>"What?!"</p>
<p>The Saint’s expression changed dramatically.</p>
<p>Without hesitation, he drew his sword.</p>
<p>Mana erupted from his body.The surrounding air distorted.</p>
<p>He gathered every bit of strength he possessed and slashed forward with everything he had.</p>
<p>CRAAAANG!</p>
<p>A crisp metallic sound echoed throughout the battlefield.</p>
<p>The collision created a massive ripple that spread across the gorge.</p>
<p>The ground shook.</p>
<p>The cliffs trembled.</p>
<p>The surrounding soldiers stared in horror.</p>
<p>Then...</p>
<p>BOOOOOOM!</p>
<p>A deafening explosion erupted.A terrifying shockwave spread outward and instantly sent everyone nearby flying.</p>
<p>Several soldiers crashed into the cliffs.</p>
<p>Others were thrown across the battlefield.</p>
<p>Some were buried beneath rubble.</p>
<p>The Saint gritted his teeth and tried to push the spear away.</p>
<p>For a brief moment, it seemed as though he had actually stopped it.</p>
<p>Then suddenly...</p>
<p>The tip of the spear exploded.</p>
<p>"What?!"</p>
<p>His eyes widened in disbelief.</p>
<p>The unexpected burst of force completely caught him off guard.</p>
<p>The blade of his sword cracked.</p>
<p>CRACK!</p>
<p>The sound echoed clearly.</p>
<p>A moment later...</p>
<p>It broke.</p>
<p>The remaining force hidden within the spear exploded outward violently.</p>
<p>BOOOOOOM!</p>
<p>The Saint Realm Knight was sent flying through the air like a cannonball.</p>
<p>His body shot backward uncontrollably.</p>
<p>BANG!</p>
<p>He slammed directly into the side of the gorge.</p>
<p>The mountain wall shattered instantly.</p>
<p>Rocks exploded everywhere.</p>
<p>His body blasted through one layer of stone after another while creating a long trail of destruction through the cliffside.</p>
<p>The entire gorge shook from the impact.Large chunks of rock collapsed from above.</p>
<p>Dust clouds rose into the sky.</p>
<p>Finally, the Saint crashed deep into the rocky mountain wall.</p>
<p>Broken stones collapsed around him.</p>
<p>Blood sprayed from his mouth.</p>
<p>His armor was cracked.</p>
<p>His sword was shattered.</p>
<p>His body was buried beneath rubble.</p>
<p>And for several long moments...</p>
<p>The entire battlefield fell silent while soldiers from both sides stared at the destruction caused by a single spear thrown across the battlefield.</p>
