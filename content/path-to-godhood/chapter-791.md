<p>Countless thoughts appeared in his mind one after another, but none of them seemed fast enough. Every method required him to understand the structure first, and that would take too much time. Ethan slowly lowered his gaze as his mind continued searching for another answer.</p>
<p>Then suddenly, his eyes changed.</p>
<p>A thought flashed through his mind.</p>
<p>Chaos.</p>
<p>He stood still for a moment. The power of Chaos was different from his other abilities. It did not need to overpower something through pure strength. Chaos existed to break order and turn things into disorder.</p>
<p>And this magic formation was built entirely on randomness and order. Every magic circle had its own place with every connection had its own purpose, and every flow of mana followed a fixed path. The entire barrier worked because everything was connected in the correct order.</p>
<p>Ethan’s eyes slowly narrowed. If he could introduce Chaos into the structure, then perhaps he did not need to understand every single part of it. He only needed to break the order holding it together.</p>
<p>A faint light of understanding appeared in his eyes.</p>
<p>"Chaos..."</p>
<p>He whispered the word while staring at the magic formation. His mind began moving again as he carefully looked at the connections between the magic circles.</p>
<p>If one connection became disordered, the next connection might be affected. If the energy flow lost its proper path, another part of the formation could become unstable. Then the disorder could spread from one section to another and slowly affect the entire structure.</p>
<p>Countless possibilities appeared in Ethan’s mind.</p>
<p>He could disturb the mana flow.</p>
<p>He could corrupt the connection between the circles.</p>
<p>He could make the energy move in the wrong direction.</p>
<p>He could turn the perfect structure into a mess.</p>
<p>The more he thought about it, the clearer the idea became. Ethan slowly raised his hand toward the magic formation, and his expression became focused as he prepared to test his idea.</p>
<p>A strange feeling began to gather around his fingertips.</p>
<p>It was a power that felt different from the energy he had used before. The space around his hand seemed to become slightly unstable as the power of Chaos slowly gathered.</p>
<p>Ethan stared at the magic formation without blinking.</p>
<p>Now he only needed to find out whether Chaos could actually affect this barrier.</p>
<p>Ethan slowly raised his hand toward the barrier and began using the power of Chaos. He first started to manipulate the mana around him and slowly changed its nature, turning the normal flow of mana into Chaos energy. The process was not simple, and Ethan had to carefully control the energy so it would not lose its form before reaching the barrier.</p>
<p>Once enough Chaos energy gathered around his hand, Ethan pressed his palm against the barrier.</p>
<p>At first, nothing happened.</p>
<p>Ethan did not expect much from it. He continued pushing the Chaos energy into the barrier while carefully watching the magic circles. Then, after a few moments, his eyes suddenly widened.</p>
<p>The barrier in front of his hand had started to change.</p>
<p>The stable flow of energy began to lose its order, and the surface of the barrier slowly started to disperse. The lines of mana that had been connected perfectly began to separate from one another as if something was pulling them apart.</p>
<p>"What?"</p>
<p>Ethan stared at the scene with surprise.</p>
<p>It actually worked.</p>
<p>The Chaos energy was slowly breaking the order of the barrier.</p>
<p>A faint smile appeared on Ethan’s face as he increased the amount of Chaos energy flowing from his body. He pressed both hands against the barrier and began forcing the layers apart.</p>
<p>The barrier started to twist and shake.</p>
<p>The energy around his hands became more chaotic as Ethan pushed deeper. The solid layer of the barrier began to separate from the center, and Ethan forced the broken parts toward the sides.</p>
<p>With a powerful push, he tore open a large gap.</p>
<p>Ethan stepped forward.</p>
<p>The moment his body passed through the barrier, he immediately raised Solaris.</p>
<p>The greatsword was covered in powerful energy as Ethan swung it toward the pillar.</p>
<p>BOOOOOOOOM!</p>
<p>The blade slammed into the pillar with thunderous might.</p>
<p>Cracks spread across its surface before the entire structure broke apart. The magic circles connected to it flickered violently, and the pillar collapsed under the overwhelming force.</p>
<p>Ethan looked at the destroyed pillar, and his heart leaped in joy.</p>
<p>"One down...Few more to go."</p>
<p>He did not waste any more time.</p>
<p>Ethan turned toward the next pillar and moved swiftly through the underground chamber. The Chaos energy continued to gather around his hands as he reached the next barrier and repeated the same process.</p>
<p>He manipulated the mana, turned it into Chaos energy and pressed it against the barrier.</p>
<p>The stable energy began to disperse again.</p>
<p>Ethan forced his way through and attacked the second pillar.</p>
<p>BOOM!</p>
<p>The pillar broke apart.</p>
<p>Without stopping, Ethan moved toward another one.</p>
<p>The same process happened again.</p>
<p>The barrier was torn open, and Ethan stepped inside before swinging Solaris with full force.</p>
<p>BOOOOOOM!</p>
<p>The third pillar shattered under his attack.</p>
<p>Ethan looked toward the remaining pillars. His expression became more confident as he prepared to continue.</p>
<p>He quickly moved toward the fourth pillar and raised his hand to use Chaos again.</p>
<p>But before he could do anything, a powerful attack suddenly shot toward him from the side.</p>
<p>He quickly moved aside and avoided the first attack, then another one followed immediately.</p>
<p>Ethan twisted his body and narrowly avoided it, but before he could move again, several strange tentacle-like things suddenly shot out from the side.</p>
<p>Ethan’s expression changed.</p>
<p>He immediately moved his body to the side.</p>
<p>The attack passed dangerously close to him and slammed into the ground behind him.</p>
<p>BOOM!</p>
<p>Ethan landed several steps away and slowly raised his head.</p>
<p>His eyes became sharp as he looked toward the direction from which the attack had come.</p>
