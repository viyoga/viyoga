<p>Shockwaves ripped through the battlefield.</p>

<p>Ethan’s blurred image danced through the air, dodging every attack with terrifying precision. He moved around the Wyvern King, closing in rapidly, his presence pressing closer with each heartbeat.</p>

<p>Before the Wyvern King could understand what was happening, it saw it.</p>

<p>A massive greatsword descended from above like a falling ruler from the heavens, blazing with overwhelming power.</p>

<p>BOOOOOOOOOOOOM!</p>

<p>A roar exploded across heaven and earth, shaking the entire battlefield. The impact shattered the air itself.</p>

<p>RUMBLE! RUMBLE!</p>

<p>The sky trembled as shockwaves rippled outward. The Wyvern King felt a force so violent that its mind went blank. Its massive body was driven straight down toward the ground.</p>

<p>BOOOOOOM!</p>

<p>Its enormous form slammed into the earth, creating a deafening explosion. Dust and debris blasted upward, covering the area in a thick cloud.</p>

<p>Ethan did not stop.</p>

<p>Gripping his greatsword tightly, he accelerated downward, descending like a falling star, aiming to deliver another crushing blow.</p>

<p>“Don’t say I didn’t give you a chance. I asked you to surrender but you refused.”</p>

<p>Ethan slammed down onto the ground with crushing force. The earth cracked beneath his boots as he raised his greatsword high, muscles tensing as he prepared to strike. His eyes were sharp and focused, filled with killing intent. However, just as he moved closer, he noticed something that made his instincts scream.</p>

<p>The Wyvern’s face had curled into a sinister grin.</p>

<p>Its cracked lips slowly spread wider, revealing rows of sharp teeth glowing faintly with destructive energy. Its chest expanded, and mana surged violently inside its throat.</p>

<p>In the next instant, the Wyvern opened its mouth and released its breath.</p>

<p>A beam of pure destruction burst out, tearing through the air with a deafening scream.</p>

<p>“FUCKKKK YOUUU!”</p>

<p>Ethan roared, veins bulging across his arms and neck. He brought his greatsword down with all his might, pouring every ounce of strength, aura, and rage into the strike.</p>

<p>BOOOOOOOOOOM!</p>

<p>The greatsword collided directly with the beam of mass destructiveness. The impact was catastrophic. The beam twisted violently as if struck by an unmovable mountain. It wrapped around the blade, bending and warping, screaming as raw power clashed against raw power.</p>

<p>Fire, lightning, and compressed mana tore at each other relentlessly. The ground beneath them shattered, splitting apart like fragile glass. The air screamed as the two opposing forces tried to overwhelm one another. Neither side gave way.</p>

<p>From afar, in the eyes of the onlookers, all they could see was a blazing beam and a massive greatsword locked together. Light blinded their vision, and the pressure made it hard to even breathe. The clash compressed inward, shrinking into a single point of unbearable density.</p>

<p>The power could not tolerate itself anymore.</p>

<p>It reached its breaking point.</p>

<p>BOOOOOOOOM!</p>

<p>A horrifying explosion erupted, louder than thunder cracking the sky. Shockwaves burst out in all directions, tearing through the battlefield. Trees were uprooted. Rocks shattered into dust. The ground caved in as if struck by the wrath of the heavens.</p>

<p>From the heart of the blast, the Wyvern’s massive body was shot downward. It smashed into the ground and dragged across it violently, carving a deep trench as its scales scraped against stone.</p>

<p>Moments later, Ethan emerged from the explosion.</p>

<p>He appeared above the Wyvern and slammed his greatsword down onto its torso with terrifying force. The blade crashed into its chest, making the creature cough out thick blood. The impact smashed the Wyvern into the ground again, sending debris flying everywhere.</p>

<p>However, before Ethan could follow up, the Wyvern’s tail flicked violently.</p>

<p>Ethan reacted instantly and raised his arm to block.</p>

<p>BOOOOOOM!</p>

<p>The impact sent Ethan flying. His body tore across the land like a projectile, smashing through trees and rocks one after another. He rolled across the ground for miles, leaving destruction in his wake. Finally, he came to a stop and forced himself upright.</p>

<p>He shook his left hand slowly. Dark draconic scales covered his arm, faintly cracked but intact.</p>

<p>“Damn… It’s whip lash is so hard.. If not for scales, it might have cracked.”</p>

<p>His eyes narrowed as he stared ahead. The Wyvern rose slowly from the rubble, its aura heavier than before. For some reason, it felt stronger than when the fight had begun.</p>

<p>It was as if the creature was adapting to the battle and was learning through the battle.</p>

<p>“If it’s not broken then that just meant I haven’t hit it hard enough to break more..”</p>

<p>Rooarrr!</p>

<p>The roar of war shook the land, but Ethan froze when he saw what happened next.</p>

<p>The Wyvern took a step forward.</p>

<p>Then with another step it’s body began to change.</p>

<p>The massive frame trembled as bones shifted and cracked. Its front legs twisted inward, shrinking as joints reformed. Scales peeled away and merged into flesh. Its wings folded and compressed, shrinking rapidly. The long neck shortened, and the monstrous head reshaped itself.</p>

<p>The transformation was slow and unsettling.</p>

<p>The huge body continued to shrink until a tall, lean man stood where the Wyvern had been. Dark hair fell loosely over his shoulders. His purple eyes glowed with demonic light. His face was sharp and cold, carrying an inhuman presence. Two small wings rested behind his back, twitching slightly.</p>

<p>The air around him felt heavy.</p>

<p>“Sorry… It takes time to get used to this after a long hibernation.”</p>

<p>His voice was calm but deep, carrying authority and disdain.</p>

<p>Ethan stared at him without blinking.</p>

<p>“You mean?”</p>

<p>“Yes!”</p>

<p>The man lifted his chin slightly, eyes filled with pride.</p>

<p>“I have gotten weaker due to the centuries of hibernation.”</p>

<p>A faint smirk appeared on his lips.</p>

<p>“In my peak, you won’t even have the chance to stand before me, you inferior species.”</p>

<p>The moment his words ended, his eyes flared brightly. A crushing mental pressure slammed into Ethan like invisible bullets. The force drilled into his head, crawling toward his brain, attempting to crush his consciousness.</p>

<p>Ethan staggered back a step.His teeth grinded as he resisted the sudden mental attack.</p>
