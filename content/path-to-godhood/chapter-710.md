<p>The next few days passed steadily.There was no war nor were there any surprise attacks.</p>

<p>There was not even the slightest sign that either side was preparing to launch another conquest.</p>

<p>Everything looked calm on the surface.</p>

<p>Yet that very calmness made everyone even more uneasy.</p>

<p>A strange atmosphere hung over the battlefield every single day. Soldiers continued their patrols while keeping one hand close to their weapons. Scouts were sent out more frequently than before, and every report was treated with extreme caution. Even the commanders found it difficult to relax because they felt as though something could happen at any moment.</p>

<p>Everyone’s heart beat a little faster than usual.</p>

<p>Anxiety quietly spread through both camps.</p>

<p>Many soldiers could not sleep properly at night. Some would suddenly wake up after hearing the slightest noise, thinking the enemy had begun attacking. Others spent long hours staring toward the distant horizon, waiting for the first signal of war.</p>

<p>They all believed that the fighting would resume within a day or two.</p>

<p>After all, the hatred between both sides had only grown stronger after everything that had happened.</p>

<p>But...</p>

<p>One day passed.</p>

<p>Then another.</p>

<p>Then another.</p>

<p>Neither side made a move.</p>

<p>The Allied Army remained firmly inside its camp while the Arcadia Army also stayed where it was.It was almost as if both sides had silently reached the same understanding.</p>

<p>Neither wanted to be the first to provoke the other.</p>

<p>At the same time, neither side trusted the other even in the slightest.</p>

<p>Both armies remained on full alert while carefully watching every movement made by the opposing camp. Scouts continued gathering information every day, and commanders constantly discussed possible enemy plans. Everyone waited for the other side to make the first move, yet that moment never came.</p>

<p>The battlefield remained locked in a strange and fragile peace.Anyone with experience could tell that this silence would not last forever.</p>

<p>It simply meant that both sides were waiting for the right opportunity.</p>

<p>And so...</p>

<p>The uneasy peace continued for ten full days.</p>

<p>On the morning of the tenth day, the peaceful atmosphere suddenly changed.</p>

<p>BOOM!</p>

<p>A terrifying aura descended upon the Allied Army camp without any warning.</p>

<p>The pressure spread across the sky like a massive invisible mountain before crashing down over the entire camp.</p>

<p>The ground beneath everyone’s feet trembled.</p>

<p>The tents shook violently.</p>

<p>Many soldiers instantly turned pale while weaker cultivators struggled to remain standing. Horses became restless and let out frightened cries as the overwhelming pressure swept through the camp.</p>

<p>Inside a secluded room, Ethan had remained in closed-door cultivation for the past ten days.</p>

<p>His eyes suddenly opened.</p>

<p>A faint frown appeared on his face as he sensed the familiar pressure spreading through the camp.</p>

<p>"They are here."</p>

<p>The moment those words left Ethan’s mouth, another powerful wave of pressure swept across the Allied Army camp.</p>

<p>BOOM!</p>

<p>The air itself seemed to tremble.</p>

<p>The sky above the camp rippled as dozens of powerful figures flew across the heavens at unbelievable speed. Their auras merged together into an overwhelming force that spread in every direction. Strong gusts of wind roared across the camp as their presence shook the ground below. Countless soldiers instinctively raised their heads toward the sky while many weaker cultivators found it difficult to even breathe.</p>

<p>Leading the procession were the experts of the Xylos Empire.</p>

<p>Dozens of powerful figures flew in perfect formation with a middle-aged man at the very front. He was tall and broad-shouldered with long dark blue hair flowing behind him. His sharp golden eyes calmly overlooked the battlefield while an invisible pressure naturally spread from his body. He wore deep blue royal robes embroidered with golden dragons, and every movement carried the dignity of a ruler who had stood at the peak for countless years.</p>

<p>He was the Emperor of the Xylos Empire.</p>

<p>Emperor Caelus Drakhar.</p>

<p>Following closely behind came the experts of the Morvath Empire.</p>

<p>At their front stood another emperor whose presence was completely different. His silver hair reached his shoulders while his deep green eyes remained calm and unreadable. His handsome face showed little emotion, yet everyone could feel the immense authority surrounding him. He wore emerald-colored royal armor instead of robes, giving him the appearance of a warrior king who had personally fought through countless battlefields.</p>

<p>He was the Emperor of the Morvath Empire.</p>

<p>Emperor Aldric Morvath.</p>

<p>Not far behind them flew the entourage of the Valorant Empire.</p>

<p>At their head was a tall man wrapped in black and crimson royal robes.</p>

<p>His sharp blood-red eyes looked down at the camp with complete indifference. A cruel smile rested on his face while his terrifying aura carried a thick killing intent that made many people shiver just by looking at him. Even from a distance, he gave others the feeling of a ruthless tyrant who would not hesitate to slaughter thousands if it served his purpose.</p>

<p>He was the Emperor of the Valorant Empire.</p>

<p>Ezekiel Von Valorant.</p>

<p>Finally...</p>

<p>The last group slowly descended from the sky.</p>

<p>Unlike the overwhelming killing intent of the others, this group carried an elegant yet majestic aura.</p>

<p>At the very front stood a woman whose appearance immediately drew everyone’s attention.</p>

<p>She possessed long, silky silver hair that gently flowed behind her like a waterfall beneath the sunlight.</p>

<p>Her sapphire blue eyes were calm and beautiful, yet they held an unquestionable authority that made it difficult for others to meet her gaze for long. Her flawless face seemed almost unreal while her fair skin appeared to glow beneath the daylight.</p>

<p>She wore an elegant white royal gown embroidered with golden patterns, and a royal cloak rested over her shoulders. A delicate golden crown sat upon her head, completing the graceful appearance of a queen.</p>

<p>She looked gentle.</p>

<p>Yet the unmatched aura surrounding her clearly told everyone that she stood among the strongest people on the continent.</p>

<p>She was the ruler of the Eldoria Kingdom.</p>

<p>Queen Ophelia Eldoria.</p>

<p>As the four rulers and their entourages slowly descended toward the Allied camp, the terrifying pressure covering the battlefield became even heavier.</p>

<p>One after another...</p>

<p>The nobles, commanders, generals, and experts hurried out of their tents.</p>

<p>The moment they recognized who had arrived, their expressions changed dramatically.</p>

<p>Without the slightest hesitation, they immediately knelt on one knee.</p>

<p>"Greetings, Your Majesty!"</p>
