<p>Far away at the Blanks estate, the atmosphere had also turned chaotic after the heavens suddenly darkened, and the terrifying pressure that spread across the world made even ordinary servants collapse to their knees in fear.</p>
<p>Inside the mansion, Sophia rushed out toward the balcony while her face looked pale, and the moment she looked up at the distorted sky, her entire body stiffened.</p>
<p>"What... is that..." she muttered while gripping the railing tightly.</p>
<p>Beside her, Rina’s calm expression had completely disappeared, and even her hands trembled slightly while staring at the blurry giant figures in the heavens.</p>
<p>"That pressure..." she whispered.</p>
<p>"It feels suffocating..."</p>
<p>Lia had already gone pale, and she instinctively hugged the younger children closer while fear filled her eyes.</p>
<p>The violent shockwaves spreading through the skies made windows tremble continuously, and every distant explosion caused the entire mansion to shake faintly.</p>
<p>Sophia clenched her teeth tightly while looking toward the distant battlefield direction.</p>
<p>"The children..." she muttered anxiously.</p>
<p>The moment those words were spoken, the expressions of the others changed immediately.</p>
<p>Diana slowly covered her mouth while unease spread across her face.</p>
<p>"They are still at the frontline..." she whispered.</p>
<p>For the first time since the war began...</p>
<p>A genuine fear settled inside all of their hearts.</p>
<p>....</p>
<p>Ethan and Rathlos stood completely frozen on the balcony while staring toward the distant heavens, and neither of them spoke for a very long moment because their minds were still trying to process what they had just witnessed.</p>
<p>The sky itself still looked distorted, and huge cracks of light flickered faintly across the atmosphere while waves of pressure continued spreading through the world one after another. Even though the battle had already ended, its aftermath still remained, and the air around the capital felt heavy and unstable as if the heavens themselves had been shaken apart.</p>
<p>The capital below had completely descended into chaos.</p>
<p>People screamed across the streets while guards shouted orders from every direction. Some civilians had already fallen to their knees while praying desperately to the gods, and others looked toward the sky with pale expressions while trembling uncontrollably. Mothers tightly hugged their children while merchants hurriedly closed their shops and tried to run back toward their homes.</p>
<p>Even the elite guards stationed around the Imperial Palace no longer looked calm, and many of them had their hands tightly gripping their weapons while cold sweat covered their foreheads.</p>
<p>Fear had spread everywhere.</p>
<p>Ethan slowly swallowed his saliva while staring upward blankly, and his palms had unknowingly become soaked in sweat.</p>
<p>"What... the hell did we just witness..." he muttered hoarsely.</p>
<p>Even his voice sounded dry and stiff.</p>
<p>Beside him, Rathlos looked equally shaken while his eyes remained fixed on the distant sky where the blurry figures had disappeared.</p>
<p>"That..." Rathlos spoke slowly, and even his lips twitched slightly while speaking.</p>
<p>"That wasn’t a battle between humans anymore."</p>
<p>He paused briefly before continuing.</p>
<p>"That was literally like gods fighting above the world."</p>
<p>Ethan remained silent because he knew Rathlos was right.</p>
<p>The destruction they witnessed had completely surpassed common sense.</p>
<p>Mountains had been flattened like sand, and the oceans themselves had erupted violently under the aftermath of their attacks. Even space had seemed unstable while huge cracks spread through the sky during their clash.</p>
<p>And the most terrifying part was...Those blurry figures did not even seem to be using their full strength.</p>
<p>Ethan suddenly remembered the giant sword piercing through the glowing figure’s chest, and his body instinctively shivered slightly.</p>
<p>That attack he could not even comprehend properly.</p>
<p>Forget dodging or resisting.</p>
<p>If even a fragment of that power touched him, he would probably disappear instantly without leaving behind even dust.</p>
<p>Rathlos slowly grabbed the balcony railing tightly while his claws unconsciously dug into the metal, and his expression looked extremely ugly.</p>
<p>"Pentios..." he muttered quietly.</p>
<p>Ethan immediately turned toward him.</p>
<p>"You know that name?" he asked quickly.</p>
<p>Rathlos remained silent for a moment before nodding slowly.</p>
<p>"I heard it long ago," he replied quietly.</p>
<p>"But I never thought such beings actually existed."</p>
<p>His eyes trembled slightly as if recalling something unpleasant.</p>
<p>"That guy with the flute..."</p>
<p>Rathlos paused briefly, and then he looked toward Ethan seriously.</p>
<p>"That person is even more terrifying."</p>
<p>Ethan frowned deeply.</p>
<p>"The flute player?" he asked.</p>
<p>Rathlos nodded slowly.</p>
<p>"The swordsman was terrifying because of strength," he said.</p>
<p>"But the flute player..."</p>
<p>His voice lowered slightly.</p>
<p>"He interfered in the middle of a battle between beings like that and casually decided the outcome."</p>
<p>The moment those words entered Ethan’s ears, his heart sank slightly.</p>
<p>Because Rathlos was right.The golden figure had only paused for one brief second after hearing the flute melody.</p>
<p>Just one second.</p>
<p>And that single moment had decided everything.</p>
<p>Ethan slowly exhaled while trying to calm his restless thoughts, but his mind still felt chaotic.</p>
<p>Too many things had happened too quickly.</p>
<p>First came the strange warnings from Haster.</p>
<p>Then Mareti’s strange hints.</p>
<p>And now this monstrous battle above the world itself.It all felt connected somehow, but Ethan still could not see the full picture.</p>
<p>Below them, loud bells suddenly began ringing across the capital.</p>
<p>DONG! DONG! DONG!</p>
<p>Large groups of soldiers rapidly moved through the streets while imperial experts continuously flew toward the palace, and the atmosphere only grew heavier with every passing second.</p>
<p>Ethan narrowed his eyes slightly while watching the movement below.</p>
<p>"They are preparing for something," he muttered quietly.</p>
<p>Rathlos nodded immediately.</p>
<p>"After something like this, how can they stay calm?" he replied.</p>
<p>Then suddenly, Rathlos’s expression stiffened slightly as if he had remembered something important.</p>
<p>"Wait..." he muttered.</p>
<p>He slowly turned toward Ethan.</p>
<p>"Your children are still on the battlefield."</p>
<p>The moment those words entered Ethan’s ears, his expression changed instantly.</p>
<p>His pupils contracted slightly, and the previous shock vanished from his mind almost immediately while a cold unease spread through his chest.</p>
<p>If even the distant aftermath of that battle could shake the capital this badly...</p>
<p>Then what about the frontline where the battle had actually happened?</p>
<p>Ethan immediately clenched his fists tightly, and his aura unconsciously rose slightly around him.</p>
<p>Rathlos looked at him quickly.</p>
<p>"You are going there?" he asked seriously.</p>
<p>Ethan’s eyes remained fixed toward the distant horizon while his expression turned grim.</p>
<p>"I need to confirm they are safe," he replied immediately.</p>
<p>Because after witnessing that godlike battle...</p>
