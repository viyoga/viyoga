<p>Agros roared so loudly that the surrounding air shook.</p>
<p>"Stop underestimating me!"</p>
<p>Future Ethan’s expression immediately darkened.</p>
<p>"Excuse me?"</p>
<p>He looked at Agros as if he had been wrongly accused.</p>
<p>"I don’t have a habit of underestimating the enemy. I always overestimate them."</p>
<p>He slowly looked Agros up and down.</p>
<p>"But despite that..."</p>
<p>He paused for a moment before shaking his head.</p>
<p>"Fuckkk you!"Agros roared.</p>
<p>BOOM!</p>
<p>His bloody aura exploded around him and sent a powerful shockwave across the battlefield.</p>
<p>Ethan, who had been watching everything from the side, felt his expression fall.</p>
<p>’Isn’t this fucker showing off a lot? I understand the need to aura farm, but at this moment, you are rage baiting too much.’</p>
<p>He quietly sighed inside.</p>
<p>’I should remembering this. Don’t let the enemy talk too much next time.’</p>
<p>’It’s an headache.’</p>
<p>More than that, Ethan was already getting strange looks from the people standing behind him.</p>
<p>He slowly turned around.</p>
<p>The Emperors and the surviving knights were looking at him with complicated expressions. A few of them quickly looked away and coughed as if they had suddenly become interested in the battlefield.</p>
<p>Only Emperor Alderic looked directly at him.</p>
<p>He gave Ethan a bitter smile.</p>
<p>"I didn’t know you had such a personality."</p>
<p>"I don’t."</p>
<p>Ethan immediately cut him off with a cold expression.</p>
<p>Alderic stared at him for a moment.</p>
<p>"I understand."</p>
<p>He nodded without trying to argue.</p>
<p>Ethan’s expression became even more annoyed.</p>
<p>He had not expected the man to accept it so easily.</p>
<p>Meanwhile, in front of them, Agros continued glaring at Future Ethan with his fists tightly clenched. His bloody aura kept rising as his anger reached its limit, while Future Ethan simply stood in the air with his hands folded and waited.</p>
<p>The difference between their attitudes made the situation even more uncomfortable for everyone watching.</p>
<p>Agros looked like he was about to tear the entire world apart.</p>
<p>Future Ethan looked like he was waiting for someone to finish complaining so the real fight could finally begin.</p>
<p>Agros suddenly threw his head back and roared. The bloody aura around his body became even darker as countless drops of blood began rising from the ground. The blood did not fall back down. Instead, it gathered in the sky and quickly covered the entire battlefield like a dark red cloud.</p>
<p>The sky slowly turned crimson.</p>
<p>A strange pressure descended over everyone.</p>
<p>Then the blood cloud began raining.</p>
<p>It was not ordinary rain. Every drop carried a terrifying amount of energy, and wherever it touched the ground, the rocks immediately began corroding. Trees that had somehow survived the battle withered in seconds while deep holes appeared across the earth. Even the remaining miasma was swallowed by the blood rain and became part of the attack.</p>
<p>The blood did not simply fall from above either. Some drops suddenly turned into long spikes before shooting sideways. Others gathered together and formed sharp blades that flew toward Future Ethan. Huge blood snakes crawled out of the ground while twisted arms formed from the red liquid reached toward him.</p>
<p>The entire battlefield seemed to have become a world made of blood.</p>
<p>Agros spread both arms and continued pouring his power into the attack.</p>
<p>The bloody rain became heavier.</p>
<p>The ground cracked.</p>
<p>The sky darkened.</p>
<p>Several strange symbols appeared within the blood cloud, and every symbol released a different kind of dark power. One caused the blood to explode on contact, while another created thin threads that tried to enter the body and drain its life force. Another caused the blood to harden into black-red spears that fell from the sky with enough force to pierce through mountains.</p>
<p>Future Ethan simply stood in the middle of it all.</p>
<p>The attacks reached him from every direction, but none of them managed to touch his body. Every time something came close, a faint distortion appeared around him and the attack simply disappeared.</p>
<p>Agros gritted his teeth.</p>
<p>He suddenly stopped the blood rain and raised both hands.</p>
<p>Dark symbols began forming between his fingers.</p>
<p>One after another, he moved his hands through a complicated series of signs. The bloody energy around him started gathering at an alarming rate, and even Future Ethan’s relaxed expression finally changed.</p>
<p>His eyes moved toward Agros’ hands.</p>
<p>"Heeeeyyyy!"</p>
<p>Future Ethan suddenly called out.</p>
<p>"Use any other move but not that."</p>
<p>His voice carried an unusual amount of seriousness.</p>
<p>Agros froze for a moment.Then his eyes lit up.He finally found something.</p>
<p>For the first time, Future Ethan had actually asked him not to use an attack.</p>
<p>A confident expression appeared on Agros’ face as he continued forming the signs.</p>
<p>But before he could complete the final sign, the space beside him suddenly distorted.</p>
<p>BOOM!</p>
<p>A destructive force slammed into his body.</p>
<p>Agros was sent flying across the sky before he could even understand what had happened.</p>
<p>His body spun through the air.</p>
<p>Before he could regain control, Future Ethan appeared behind him.</p>
<p>His fist struck Agros directly in the back.</p>
<p>BOOM!</p>
<p>Agros dropped from the sky like a stone.</p>
<p>Future Ethan followed him down.Just before Agros hit the ground, a hand grabbed his face.</p>
<p>The next moment...</p>
<p>BOOM!</p>
<p>Future Ethan slammed him into the earth.</p>
<p>The ground beneath Agros collapsed as cracks spread in every direction.</p>
<p>Future Ethan stood over him while holding his face against the broken ground.</p>
<p>"Son of a bitch..."</p>
<p>He slowly tightened his grip.</p>
<p>"I wanted to play with you more, but it seems you are eager to die."</p>
<p>Agros’ voice came out muffled beneath his hand.</p>
<p>"Yes... kill mmmmmeeee if you want, but stop humiliating me."</p>
<p>His voice was filled with anger and pain.</p>
<p>Future Ethan looked down at him with a cold expression.</p>
<p>"Didn’t I say to kill yourself if you want to die?"</p>
<p>Agros’ face twitched violently.</p>
<p>The humiliation had reached a level he could barely endure.</p>
<p>"Finish me!"</p>
<p>Future Ethan released his face and stepped back.</p>
<p>"Just kill yourself."</p>
<p>Agros slowly raised his head from the crater.His bloodshot eyes were filled with rage.</p>
<p>The bloody aura around his body began rising again, and the entire battlefield could feel his anger becoming heavier with every passing moment.</p>
