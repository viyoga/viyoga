<p>The battle between Ethan, Grommash, and Ragnok had just resumed, and the air was already trembling under the pressure of their power, while the ground beneath them continued to crack with every movement they made.</p>
<p>Grommash charged forward again with a furious roar, and his axe tore through the air with crushing force, while Ragnok moved from the side with his blades flashing like shadows, trying to corner Ethan from both directions at once.</p>
<p>Ethan stepped forward instead of retreating, and his sword met Grommash’s axe head-on, while his body twisted slightly to avoid Ragnok’s incoming strike, and the clash erupted once more with a violent shockwave.</p>
<p>BOOOOM!</p>
<p>The force pushed everything around them back, and nearby soldiers were thrown off their feet as dust and debris rose into the air.</p>
<p>But before the next exchange could happen something changed.</p>
<p>A terrifying pressure descended from above, and it felt like the sky itself had been split open.</p>
<p>Ethan’s instincts screamed.</p>
<p>He looked up and saw a sword arc.</p>
<p>It tore through the sky like a falling star, and the sheer power behind it made the world tremble as it descended toward him.</p>
<p>“Shit—!”</p>
<p>BOOOOOOM!</p>
<p>The attack slammed into the ground, and a massive portion of the battlefield was cut apart, as if the land itself had been sliced open. The shockwave spread outward violently, and Ethan barely managed to dodge in time as he threw himself aside.</p>
<p>The ground behind him exploded, and the aftermath left a deep scar across the land.</p>
<p>Ethan’s heart pounded heavily as he steadied himself and looked up.</p>
<p>A figure was descending slowly from the sky.</p>
<p>The moment Ethan saw him clearly,his pupils shrank.</p>
<p>“FUCKKKKK!” he bellowed hoarsely, and his voice carried raw terror.</p>
<p>It was an Orc Divine Knight.</p>
<p>The figure landed heavily on the ground, and the impact alone sent cracks spreading across the battlefield. He stood tall, his massive frame even more overwhelming than Grommash’s, and in his hand was a greatsword that radiated terrifying pressure.</p>
<p>His gaze locked onto Ethan.</p>
<p>“Human” he said, and his voice was deep and filled with killing intent.</p>
<p>“I have finally found you…”</p>
<p>He raised his greatsword slightly.</p>
<p>“Today… I am going to slap you to death.”</p>
<p>Ethan’s entire body tensed, but he did not step back.Instead, he turned his head sharply toward Han and Hale.</p>
<p>“Hale and Han, retreat… I will cover for you,” he said quickly.</p>
<p>Han hesitated for a moment.</p>
<p>“But..”</p>
<p>“Fuck your ‘but’… just do what I say!” Ethan roared, and his voice cut through the chaos.</p>
<p>There was no time to argue.</p>
<p>Han clenched his teeth, and Hale gave a tight nod.</p>
<p>“Don’t die,” Hale said quietly.</p>
<p>Ethan did not respond.</p>
<p>He had already made his decision.If this was how it was going to be then he would go all out.</p>
<p>A violent energy erupted from his body.</p>
<p>“Battle Frenzy.”</p>
<p>BOOOOM!</p>
<p>His blood began to boil, and his aura surged wildly as his power climbed rapidly, pushing past his current limits.</p>
<p>“Draconic Transformation.”</p>
<p>The next moment, changes began to appear across his body.</p>
<p>Dark red scales started forming over his skin, spreading across his arms, chest, and shoulders like a living armor. The scales overlapped each other, creating a natural battle armor that shimmered faintly under the light, and sharp edges formed along his forearms like claws.</p>
<p>His presence changed.It became heavier and more dangerous.</p>
<p>The air around him distorted as heat began to rise.</p>
<p>Then flames erupted.</p>
<p>WHOOSHH!</p>
<p>A storm of fire burst out from his body, and it spread outward like a raging hurricane. The flames twisted and roared, sweeping across the battlefield and forcing both Orcs and demons to retreat instinctively.</p>
<p>The heat alone was unbearable.</p>
<p>Some of the Orc soldiers were caught in the wave and thrown back, while others raised their weapons to shield themselves as the flames burned around them.</p>
<p>Chaos spread instantly.Ethan stood at the center of it.</p>
<p>His eyes glowed faintly, and a crimson aura crackled around him like lightning, mixing with the flames and creating a terrifying presence.</p>
<p>His blood roared and his power surged.</p>
<p>Although he was still at the Mythic rank.The strength he was releasing now had already gone far beyond it.</p>
<p>The Orc Divine Knight narrowed his eyes slightly as he observed Ethan.</p>
<p>“Good,” he said slowly.</p>
<p>“Now this looks interesting.”</p>
<p>The moment Ethan’s transformation was still settling and the draconic scales had just begun to fully cover his body, the Orc Divine Knight did not wait any longer and moved without hesitation.</p>
<p>He raised his greatsword and stepped forward, and the space beneath his feet shattered as he closed the distance in an instant.</p>
<p>“Die!”</p>
<p>The blade descended.</p>
<p>BOOOOOOM!</p>
<p>Ethan did not dodge.</p>
<p>He stepped forward and took the blow head-on.</p>
<p>Their weapons collided, and the impact created a deafening explosion that shook the entire battlefield. The ground beneath them collapsed instantly, and a massive crater formed as shockwaves spread outward, throwing soldiers from both sides into the air.</p>
<p>Dust, fire, and broken earth filled the surroundings.</p>
<p>For a moment, nothing could be seen clearly.</p>
<p>Then,within the chaos, two figures stood locked in place.</p>
<p>Ethan’s sword held against the Orc Divine Knight’s greatsword, and the force between them cracked the air itself. His draconic scales glowed faintly under the pressure, and flames continued to surge around him like a storm.</p>
<p>The Orc Divine Knight grinned.</p>
<p>“So you can take it,” he said.</p>
<p>Ethan did not reply.</p>
<p>Instead, he pushed back.</p>
<p>BOOOOM!</p>
<p>A surge of power erupted from his body, and he forced the Orc Divine Knight a step back, while flames burst outward and burned the surrounding ground.</p>
<p>The two moved again.</p>
<p>CLANG! BOOM! SWISH!</p>
<p>Each clash between them tore apart the battlefield, and every strike carried enough force to destroy mountains. The Orc Divine Knight’s attacks were heavy and overwhelming, while Ethan’s movements were sharp and relentless, filled with both strength and control.</p>
<p>Meanwhile, Grommash and Ragnok rejoined the fight, circling around and looking for openings.</p>
<p>The Orc Divine Knight glanced briefly at the battlefield behind Ethan and spoke in a cold tone.</p>
<p>“Kill the rest.”</p>
