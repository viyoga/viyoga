<p>Ethan finally finished the work load after which he opened the system panel.He had forgotten to take a look on wedding night with Amber.</p>

<p>The next day he had hardly waited when urgent reports poured in about troubles brewing with trades routes blocked by novels.</p>

<p>He was sure it was created by the Ambrose family.</p>

<p>Thankfully it was solved as he offered a few chests to them and dresses to their wives.</p>

<p>[Host’s partner has an aptitude of X rank warrior and is at the Middle Stage of Legendary Knight.]</p>

<p>[Host has gained X rank warrior aptitude.]</p>

<p>The sudden information shocked him.</p>

<p>For a moment Ethan simply stared at the notification screen in silence.</p>

<p>If the situation had been any different, he might have completely forgotten about this ability of his bloodline.</p>

<p>His bloodline had the power to draw out potential from his wives.</p>

<p>But until now, most of the women he married had relatively lower aptitude compared to him. Because of that, the improvement he gained each time had been limited.</p>

<p>This time was different.</p>

<p>Amber possessed much higher potential.</p>

<p>“Oh my god…X rank…”</p>

<p>“Damn…”Ethan couldn’t help cursing in disbelief.</p>

<p>From the stories he heard,Sir Lancelot was a commoner by birth yet he has such a huge potential.</p>

<p>“Truly one in a billion.”</p>

<p>Ethan slowly leaned back in his chair and smiled faintly.</p>

<p>“So it finally happened,” he murmured.</p>

<p>He raised his hand slightly.</p>

<p>“Collect.”</p>

<p>Swoosh!</p>

<p>The moment he gave the command, Ethan felt a powerful surge of energy spread throughout his entire body.</p>

<p>It felt as if invisible chains deep inside him were suddenly snapping apart.</p>

<p>His bones trembled slightly and his muscles tightened while mana began circulating rapidly through his veins. The energy flowed from his chest toward every corner of his body.</p>

<p>The process was not painful.Instead, it felt like a heavy weight had been lifted from his body.</p>

<p>Invisible locks that once restrained his strength slowly broke apart one after another. His foundation felt stronger and more stable.</p>

<p>The energy refined his body layer by layer.His breathing became deeper and calmer.</p>

<p>His senses grew sharper.</p>

<p>Even the flow of mana inside his body became smoother than before.</p>

<p>Ethan quietly sat there and allowed the power to settle within him. His fingers tapped lightly on the armrest of the chair while the last traces of energy slowly faded.</p>

<p>After several minutes the surge finally calmed down.</p>

<p>Ethan opened his eyes and exhaled slowly.</p>

<p>“Not bad,” he said quietly.</p>

<p>“System…Compared to this,the SSS origin bloodline feels inadequate, doesn’t it?”</p>

<p>[Host,you are underestimating it.]</p>

<p>[SSS is the peak power from your world.It feels low because the ceiling of this world is higher compared to that one.]</p>

<p>“Oh!”Ethan nodded with an understanding gaze.</p>

<p>…</p>

<p>Meanwhile another piece of good news soon arrived.</p>

<p>Amber had become pregnant.</p>

<p>When Duke Lancelot heard this news, his first reaction was pure shock.</p>

<p>“What?”</p>

<p>His eyes widened slightly in disbelief.</p>

<p>“I did not imagine there would be a child so soon.”</p>

<p>He rubbed his forehead and paced slowly across the room.</p>

<p>“My own daughter still behaves like a child sometimes.”</p>

<p>“How is she supposed to take care of another child?”</p>

<p>His expression looked both worried and confused.</p>

<p>But after thinking for a moment, he gradually calmed down.The estate was full of experienced women and servants who could help.</p>

<p>Amber would not be alone.</p>

<p>There were many people who would help raise the child.</p>

<p>With that thought, Duke Lancelot sighed deeply.</p>

<p>“Well… I suppose things will work out.”</p>

<p>Meanwhile inside the estate Amber received careful attention every day.</p>

<p>Servants and attendants made sure she rested well. Doctors visited frequently to examine her condition.</p>

<p>Ethan himself often came to see her whenever he had time.</p>

<p>Sometimes he simply sat beside her and talked quietly.Other times he checked the reports of the doctors personally.</p>

<p>As the months passed, Amber’s belly gradually grew larger.</p>

<p>At first she felt a little embarrassed by the sudden changes in her body. She would sometimes hide behind blankets when people visited her room.</p>

<p>But slowly her mindset began to change.</p>

<p>Feeling the small life gradually forming inside her womb changed something deep within her.</p>

<p>Her thoughts became calmer.</p>

<p>Her behavior became more mature.</p>

<p>There was a quiet sense of responsibility in her eyes now.</p>

<p>A faint but unusual aura slowly began surrounding her body.</p>

<p>Ethan noticed the change immediately.</p>

<p>Normally a person’s aura weakened slightly during pregnancy.</p>

<p>But Amber’s aura seemed to grow stronger instead.</p>

<p>It became more refined and more stable.</p>

<p>It almost felt as if her entire being was slowly transforming.</p>

<p>Ethan could not fully understand the reason.</p>

<p>Perhaps it was the emotional transformation of becoming a mother.</p>

<p>Or perhaps something deeper was happening inside her body.Either way, she had clearly changed.</p>

<p>Half a month later the long awaited day finally arrived.</p>

<p>Amber was pushed into the delivery room.</p>

<p>Ethan naturally waited outside the door.</p>

<p>He stood quietly in the corridor with his arms folded.</p>

<p>Several servants moved nervously through the hallway while preparing hot water and clean cloth.</p>

<p>Doctors and midwives hurried in and out of the room.</p>

<p>The atmosphere felt tense.</p>

<p>Ethan remained calm but his eyes never left the door.</p>

<p>Time seemed to pass slowly.</p>

<p>Then twenty minutes later a loud cry suddenly echoed from inside the room.</p>

<p>The cry of a newborn baby.</p>

<p>At the same moment a powerful aura fluctuation burst out from the delivery room.</p>

<p>The entire building trembled slightly.</p>

<p>The air itself seemed to shake.</p>

<p>Even the ground beneath their feet vibrated faintly.</p>

<p>Ethan’s eyes widened.</p>

<p>This was the first time he had experienced something like this.</p>

<p>“Ah!”</p>

<p>“What’s going on?”</p>

<p>“Are we under attack?”</p>

<p>Several servants and guards panicked immediately.</p>

<p>Some guards even drew their weapons.</p>

<p>Ethan quickly stepped forward to stabilize the situation.</p>

<p>But before he could say anything, Mareti spoke calmly beside him.</p>

<p>“Don’t worry.”</p>

<p>“This is just a normal process when a strong individual is born and the world recognizes it.”</p>

<p>Ethan paused and looked at him.</p>

<p>“Normal…?”</p>

<p>“Yes,” Mareti replied calmly.</p>

<p>“Children born with powerful talent sometimes cause this kind of reaction.”</p>

<p>Hearing this explanation, Ethan slowly relaxed.</p>

<p>Then another notification appeared before his eyes.</p>

<p>[Detected the birth of history offspring.]</p>

<p>[The child is born with X rank Knight potential and hidden talent Berserker.]</p>

<p>[Reward: Special Gift *1]</p>

<p>Ethan froze for a moment.</p>

<p>“X rank…”</p>

<p>“Hidden talent…”</p>

<p>He sucked in a cold breath.</p>

<p>Even for someone like him, this was shocking.</p>

<p>But after a few seconds he slowly calmed himself.There would be time later to examine everything carefully.</p>

<p>Right now his attention remained fixed on the door of the delivery room where his child had just been born.</p>

<p>His expression slowly softened.</p>

<p>For the first time that day, a small but genuine smile appeared on his face.</p>
