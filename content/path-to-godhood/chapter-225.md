<p>Randall’s return along with the eighty soldiers allowed Ethan to push his plans further.</p>

<p>Those eighty soldiers were now veterans and had all reached the Intermediate Stage.</p>

<p>With their strength, Ethan decided to craft an elite Knight Squad.</p>

<p>However, a small issue made him freeze.</p>

<p>“Now what should I name this?” He scratched his hair, trying to come up with something that sounded decent.</p>

<p>“Uncle Randall, any suggestions?”</p>

<p>“Uh… What about Super Knights?” Randall suggested.</p>

<p>Ethan stared at him for a second, then pretended he never heard it. He slowly turned toward Oliver.</p>

<p>“You?”</p>

<p>“Supreme Knight!”</p>

<p>“…”</p>

<p>Ethan blinked his eyes in silence, staring at the two of them.</p>

<p>’One spoke Super. Another said Supreme. Why is their naming sense so distorted?’</p>

<p>“My Lord… Have you thought of any?” they asked together, looking at him with bright expectations.</p>

<p>Ethan felt pressure building in his chest. He took a deep breath and said, “How about Black Blank Knights.”</p>

<p>“…”</p>

<p>A deep silence settled between them like a heavy cloud.</p>

<p>It seemed none of them had any real creativity.</p>

<p>Suddenly, a knock on the door broke the awkward atmosphere.</p>

<p>“Come!”</p>

<p>A servant entered and bowed respectfully.</p>

<p>“Sir, you have guests.”</p>

<p>“Who?”</p>

<p>“It is the mercenary group that you hired previously.”</p>

<p>“Huh!” Ethan’s eyes lit up immediately.</p>

<p>He stood up at once. “Treat them well and bring them to the hall.”</p>

<p>…..</p>

<p>“Sir Joel… It’s nice to meet you again.” Ethan reached out and shook Joel’s hand.</p>

<p>Joel looked overwhelmed.”Lord Ethan… Can you stop calling me Sir… I feel unworthy.”</p>

<p>“No need for such humility. You deserve it.” Ethan smiled warmly.</p>

<p>He then greeted the others.”Ryu, Haro, Irina. It’s nice to see you all again.”</p>

<p>They bowed lightly.</p>

<p>“It is nice to meet you again, Lord Blank,” Ryu said politely.</p>

<p>Irina smiled. “We are honored to stand before the Blank family again.”</p>

<p>Haro nodded firmly. “Thank you for inviting us, Lord Blank.”</p>

<p>“So what’s the occasion? Need any help?” Ethan asked respectfully, folding his arms as he leaned back.</p>

<p>Joel looked at the other three. They exchanged quick glances and nodded to each other silently. After a brief eye contact, Joel stepped forward.</p>

<p>“Sir… Is the previous offer still on?”</p>

<p>“Which offer?” Ethan frowned, trying to remember.Then his eyes widened.</p>

<p>“Wait… Don’t tell me?”</p>

<p>“Yes…” Joel answered.</p>

<p>Then all of them stood up slowly and bowed deeply before him.</p>

<p>“We swear loyalty and devotion to the Blank family. We will serve them with all our strength and with all our heart.”</p>

<p>All four lowered their heads.</p>

<p>“You…”Ethan felt his heart jolt with happiness but he first calmed himself.</p>

<p>“Have you thought about this carefully?”</p>

<p>They looked at him for a brief moment and then without any shred of hesitation nodded.</p>

<p>“Yes!”</p>

<p>’Ahhh!What a joyful week!’</p>

<p>After the disaster at the Duchy, good things kept coming one after another.</p>

<p>First Randall returned back after advancing to Advance Knight and now this.</p>

<p>This would give a significant boost to the power of House Blank.</p>

<p>An experienced Advance Knight teamed along with four Intermediate ranks.</p>

<p>Ethan stood up quickly and grabbed all four by their arms, pulling them back up.</p>

<p>“Why did you suddenly change your decision?”</p>

<p>As soon as he asked, they looked at each other again. But this time, Ethan noticed a strange shyness and blush in their eyes.</p>

<p>“My Lord… It is time for us to settle down.”</p>

<p>“Uhh…,and we decided to marry one another,” Haro said suddenly.</p>

<p>“One another?”</p>

<p>“Huh!”</p>

<p>Everyone turned toward Randall who gasped loudly, staring at the four of them.</p>

<p>Ryu raised his fist and lightly punched Haro’s shoulder. “Don’t say weird things.”</p>

<p>Joel let out a small bitter laugh.”Irina and I are going to tie knots alongside Haro and Ryu.”</p>

<p>Ethan blinked, then he broke into a wide smile.</p>

<p>“Damn… That’s another joyous occasion. Congratulations.”</p>

<p>Irina lowered her gaze shyly, her cheeks warm.</p>

<p>Ryu scratched the back of his head and looked away.</p>

<p>Haro let out a nervous laugh.Joel exhaled softly like he had been holding it in for a long time.</p>

<p>Ethan placed a hand on Joel’s shoulder and spoke with gentle warmth.”This is great news. I am truly happy for all of you.”</p>

<p>Randall stepped forward as well, clapping lightly.”Congratulations. You all deserve happiness after everything you went through.”</p>

<p>The temperature of the room warmed with emotion.</p>

<p>Joy, relief and hope blended together.</p>

<p>The mercenaries who once wandered without a</p>

<p>home now finally found a place where they could settle.And the Blank family had gained loyal warriors who would stand by them for life.</p>

<p>Ethan looked at all four of them with pride.</p>

<p>“From today onward, you are part of us. Welcome home.”</p>

<p>The four lowered their heads deeply again.</p>

<p>“Thank you… Lord Blank.”</p>

<p>……..</p>

<p>The joining of Joel did not create a big stir since not many people knew about them, but those who did were shocked.</p>

<p>“Damn.. Another Advanced stage..”</p>

<p>“Now with two Advanced stages we aren’t any worse than a Duchy are we?”</p>

<p>“Less.. we can even flatten a Duchy.”</p>

<p>“Shut up.. You are gonna get us killed, you damn idiot.”</p>

<p>The soldiers whispered among themselves, filled with sudden energy at the unexpected turn of events.</p>

<p>Meanwhile, with another group joining the discussion, the atmosphere slowly settled.</p>

<p>“So you are stuck at naming the squad.” Joel spoke while rubbing his chin. He looked at Irina, waiting for her suggestion.</p>

<p>Ethan explained the whole problem to Joel, who immediately suggested asking Irina. Among them, she was the most skilled in such matters.</p>

<p>As for why Ethan did not ask his wife, it was because Rina was pregnant, so he did not want to trouble her. Lia was a bit of a simpleton, and Diana was still in seclusion. As for Sophia, it was better not to ask her at all, or she might already start planning a rebellion.</p>

<p>Irina paused for a moment and then spoke softly, “Lord Ethan… Generally the names of such special regions are based on the speciality of the troops.”</p>

<p>She looked at him calmly. “What is the speciality of the Legion you want to create?”</p>

<p>The question struck Ethan and left him dazed for a second.</p>

<p>“Specialties… I… I haven’t thought that much.”</p>

<p>He then turned toward Randall.</p>

<p>“What speciality do our troops have?”</p>

<p>“Speciality… Do they even have any speciality.. And if they do then why didn’t I know that?” Randall asked with furrowed brows, making everyone twitch slightly.</p>

<p>Irina looked at both of them and spoke patiently, “Then why don’t we create order.”</p>

<p>“In this we can have various Legions. We will select and put them into Legions based on their character.”</p>

<p>“Hmm.. That’s a good suggestion..”</p>

<p>Everyone nodded at Irina, agreeing with her idea.</p>

<p>“So what do we call it?” Randall asked.</p>

<p>“Name.. How about the Order of Eternal Blade.”</p>

<p>“Order of Eternal Blade?” Ethan muttered, as if tasting the words.</p>

<p>“Yes, Order of Eternal Blade.. Something like the wielder may die but the spirit and will with which he holds the blade is carried and passed through the generations.”</p>

<p>Hearing this, Ethan could not hold himself back and slammed his hands on the table.</p>

<p>“Damn.. That’s a sickass description. I like it…”</p>

<p>“Me too.”</p>

<p>“Yes… It has quite a meaning..”</p>

<p>Everyone applauded Irina, impressed by her thoughtfulness.</p>
