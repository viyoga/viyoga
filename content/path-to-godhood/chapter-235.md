<p>The system voice echoed clearly.</p>

<p>[Life Enhancement Plant.]</p>

<p>This plant grows only in mana rich regions and needs at least thirty years to mature and bear fruit. Each fruit can increase a person’s lifespan by forty years. Even someone on their deathbed could rise and run once they consume it. The fruits can also be processed into a high grade elixir capable of healing deadly diseases and lifting higher level curses.</p>

<p>Ethan inhaled sharply.</p>

<p>“Hisss… Is this really miraculous?”</p>

<p>He reached out slowly, touched one of the buds and lifted the pot carefully in his hand. The plant was ugly, yes, but something inside him told him it was priceless.</p>

<p>His eyes softened with a new sense of wonder.</p>

<p>Something this plain could change a life.Maybe even many.</p>

<p>As for the elixir part,for that he needed an alchemist and that’s too high level one.</p>

<p>Alas,the highest Alchemist is of D rank that worked for Royal.Most of the potions were maneuvered from Empires that’s why the prices were high.</p>

<p>Ethan kept the plant aside,and focused on cultivation.</p>

<p>The next moment a huge influx of knowledge and experience rushed into his mind, creating a blast that echoed from his body.</p>

<p>A thunder-like typhoon seemed to shake him as his body radiated strong fierce waves that shook the entire building.</p>

<p>The 200 years of mage insight poured into his mind.</p>

<p>Complex knowledge, patterns and runic understandings surfaced inside him one after another.</p>

<p>Ethan felt a bit nauseous as the sudden knowledge hit him and shook his mind.</p>

<p>“Get a grip Ethan.”</p>

<p>He bit his lip and tried to contain the vast knowledge.</p>

<p>The sensation of the mage upgrade felt like his mind was stretching and tearing at the same time. His mental world twisted as if trying to rebuild itself. Pressure squeezed his thoughts and old fragments of fear rose like shadows.</p>

<p>His inner demons whispered nonsense in the corners of his consciousness. The knowledge was too much, too fast, and it felt like madness clawed at his mind.</p>

<p>His mental aura skyrocketed and, with a heavy blast, it broke through to Master Mage. But it did not stop. Instead it kept rising with increasing volatility that almost made him lose his mind in chaos.</p>

<p>Strange thoughts and murmurs started echoing inside him. They sounded like broken voices and twisted laughter.</p>

<p>“Ahah. What is going on? What did I do wrong?” Ethan asked, unable to pinpoint the source of the madness and the chaotic thoughts.</p>

<p>Just as he thought he would lose himself completely.</p>

<p>ROARRR!</p>

<p>A draconic roar erupted from the depth of his soul that shook him out of his chaotic state.</p>

<p>The roar overwhelmed the madness and pushed the chaotic thoughts into a corner like frightened insects.</p>

<p>Then a burst of mental waves erupted from Ethan.</p>

<p>BOOOOM!</p>

<p>The mental attack passed through the barrier and stones and hit the soldiers and workers outside, knocking them out instantly.</p>

<p>Within a second every person in a two-hundred-meter radius of Ethan’s training chamber collapsed unconscious.</p>

<p>Ethan lost his senses for a moment. When he finally opened his eyes again, he clicked his tongue.</p>

<p>“Damn… I fucked up…”</p>

<p>And right after that came the loudest scream.</p>

<p>“ETHAAAAN YOU BASTARDDDDSS!”</p>

<p>…..</p>

<p>It was a happy atmosphere across the entire estate.</p>

<p>With another child born, it was naturally time for celebration. Every time a child came into the world, Ethan would give everyone a small bonus along with a food and beer party. Because of that, the gardeners and the rest of the workers were working extra hard to finish their chores so they could join the celebration later.</p>

<p>But just then, a sudden wave of force hit everyone.</p>

<p>For a moment it felt like a heavy hammer struck their heads, knocking them unconscious and taking them down on the spot.</p>

<p>“Aaah…”</p>

<p>“Kyaaa…”</p>

<p>Screams and groans filled the air one after another as people clutched their heads and fell to the ground. Tools dropped, buckets spilled, and bodies collapsed as confusion spread everywhere.</p>

<p>Sophia, who had been overseeing the preparations, was suddenly slammed by the same force. Her body swayed violently and she staggered back. Her vision blurred and her surroundings spun in circles. She dropped to her knees, one hand gripping the ground.</p>

<p>“Now what is this?” she roared angrily and shook her head hard, trying to steady herself.</p>

<p>After a moment her sight cleared, though a sharp headache throbbed through her skull. She forced herself to stand and looked around. The scene left her completely stunned. Many of the workers and servants lay knocked out on the ground. Even several guards were unconscious.</p>

<p>It took her a few seconds to gather herself, and then she noticed a clear pattern. Anyone below the Late Beginner Stage had been flattened by the impact.</p>

<p>Her eyes narrowed as she turned toward the distant training building. That was where the force had come from. And sure enough, everything inside her snapped at once.</p>

<p>“ETHAAAAAANNNNN!” she screamed in anger.</p>

<p>She dashed toward the building, her feet pounding against the ground. When she rushed inside, the sight before her made her anger melt instantly into shock and worry.</p>

<p>Ethan was on the floor, growling in pain, gripping his head with both hands. His face was pale and twisted as if he was fighting something inside himself. His body trembled and he struggled to breathe steadily.</p>

<p>Sophia’s heart dropped.</p>

<p>“Ethan!” she shouted, her voice filled with fear.</p>

<p>She hurried forward, knelt beside him, and reached for him with trembling hands. She slipped an arm around his shoulders, guiding him gently down to rest against her support.</p>

<p>“Ethan, stay with me,” she said softly, her worry overtaking every other emotion. She held him firmly yet carefully, keeping him steady as he fought through the pain that had shaken the entire estate.</p>

<p>She looked toward the collapsed workers outside and then back at Ethan who wasn’t responding.</p>

<p>After staying still for a moment she whispered,”Are you done acting?”</p>

<p>SHIVERR!</p>

<p>Ethan felt goosebumps and started to sweat.</p>

<p>“I see…You won’t wake like this.”saying that Sophia’s hand moved.</p>

<p>The next moment fierce screams erupted.</p>

<p>“Ahhhhhhhhhhh!Not my balllllll!”</p>
