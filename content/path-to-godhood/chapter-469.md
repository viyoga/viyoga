<p>“Haha…of course not.”</p>

<p>“Not at all, not at all, Lord Ethan.”</p>

<p>“Who is in his right mind would cause you such trouble on such an important day.”</p>

<p>“Lord Ethan’s actions just now really opened our eyes.”</p>

<p>Having just witnessed Ethan’s terrifying strength, everyone naturally made sure to give him proper respect. Many nobles quickly stepped forward with polite smiles while praising him openly.</p>

<p>Some raised their wine cups while speaking loudly so others could hear.</p>

<p>“Marquise Ethan’s strength is truly remarkable.”</p>

<p>“Those young men were simply asking for trouble.”</p>

<p>“For someone to challenge a hero of the frontier like that was foolish.”</p>

<p>For a while the atmosphere became lively again. Conversations restarted, servants poured wine, and soldiers laughed loudly while recounting what they had just seen.</p>

<p>Meanwhile, the three young nobles who had attempted to cause trouble had already become background characters.</p>

<p>No one paid attention to them anymore.</p>

<p>Their earlier arrogance had completely vanished. They stood on the side of the courtyard with pale faces and lowered heads while several guards watched them carefully.</p>

<p>Many guests simply ignored them.</p>

<p>If Ethan had not ordered his subordinates to escort them away quietly, some people even believed that the angry soldiers present might have dragged them out themselves.</p>

<p>After the unwelcome guests were taken away, the ceremony continued smoothly.</p>

<p>The priest stepped forward again and opened the ceremonial book while clearing his throat.</p>

<p>He spoke clearly so everyone could hear.</p>

<p>“Do you, Ethan, accept Amber as your wife and vow to stand beside her through prosperity and hardship?”</p>

<p>Ethan looked at Amber calmly.</p>

<p>Then he answered without hesitation.</p>

<p>“I do.”</p>

<p>The priest nodded and turned toward Amber.</p>

<p>“Do you, Amber, accept Ethan as your husband and vow to walk beside him through the trials and victories ahead?”</p>

<p>Amber looked at Ethan again for a brief moment.</p>

<p>Her eyes softened slightly.</p>

<p>Then she nodded gently.</p>

<p>“I do.”</p>

<p>The priest lifted his staff slightly and spoke in a firm voice.</p>

<p>“Then by the blessing of the temple and the authority of the Empire, you are now husband and wife.”</p>

<p>For a brief moment the courtyard remained quiet.</p>

<p>Then the soldiers erupted into loud cheers.</p>

<p>“LONG LIVE MARQUISE ETHAN!”</p>

<p>“LONG LIVE LADY AMBER!”</p>

<p>Mugs were raised high into the air.</p>

<p>Laughter and cheering filled the courtyard while the musicians began playing lively music.</p>

<p>Amber looked slightly embarrassed by the sudden noise while Ethan let out a small laugh beside her.</p>

<p>Not far away, Duke Lancelot watched the scene and quietly sighed in relief.</p>

<p>‘Thank God…’</p>

<p>‘Finally that troublesome kid will be gone.’</p>

<p>‘Kekek… now I don’t have to clean up her mess anymore.’</p>

<p>Under the witness of many imperial nobles, Ethan and Amber officially became husband and wife.</p>

<p>The wedding celebration lasted for the entire day.</p>

<p>Food and wine were served endlessly while soldiers and nobles mingled together. Stories from the battlefield were shared, and the entire estate remained lively until late evening.</p>

<p>Eventually the last guests began leaving.</p>

<p>Carriages rolled away from the estate while servants cleaned the courtyard.</p>

<p>When the celebration finally ended and the estate became quiet again, Ethan walked toward Amber’s chamber with an expectant mood.</p>

<p>He pushed the door open slowly and stepped inside.</p>

<p>The candlelight inside the room glowed softly while the night outside had grown calm.</p>

<p>A few minutes later, the quiet room was filled with soft and lively sounds as the newly married couple began their first night together.</p>

<p>…..</p>

<p>Meanwhile, far away from the estate, the three defeated nobles traveled back along the road with miserable expressions.</p>

<p>Their clothes were still stained with dust and dried blood.</p>

<p>No one spoke for a long time.</p>

<p>Finally Hamel broke the silence.</p>

<p>“Now what are we going to do?” he asked bitterly.</p>

<p>Pentos let out a tired sigh while rubbing his forehead.</p>

<p>“With what face are we going to return?”</p>

<p>Garet clenched his fists while staring ahead through the carriage window.</p>

<p>“We have been made into a joke.”</p>

<p>None of them spoke again after that.</p>

<p>The humiliation of that day hung heavily in the air while the carriage continued moving through the dark road.</p>

<p>The road outside the Duke’s estate had already grown quiet.</p>

<p>The celebration inside the manor was still faintly visible from a distance. Lantern lights flickered along the walls, and distant laughter could still be heard from the remaining soldiers who continued drinking and celebrating.</p>

<p>But far away from that joyful atmosphere, a dark carriage moved slowly along the cold road.</p>

<p>Inside the carriage, the mood was completely different.</p>

<p>Garet, Hamel, and Pentos sat in silence.</p>

<p>The earlier arrogance on their faces had completely disappeared. Their clothes were still dusty from the battle, and faint stains of dried blood could be seen on their sleeves.</p>

<p>None of them looked at each other.</p>

<p>The humiliation from earlier still weighed heavily on their minds.</p>

<p>Finally Hamel slammed his fist lightly against the side of the carriage.</p>

<p>“Damn it…” he muttered.</p>

<p>Garet stared outside the window with a cold expression.</p>

<p>“We underestimated him,” he said quietly.</p>

<p>His voice carried bitterness.</p>

<p>Pentos remained silent while leaning back against the seat. His eyes were half closed as if he was thinking deeply.</p>

<p>After several minutes, the carriage reached a crossroads outside the province.</p>

<p>Pentos suddenly spoke.</p>

<p>“I have something to take care of.”</p>

<p>Garet glanced at him briefly but did not ask questions.</p>

<p>Hamel only snorted.</p>

<p>“Do whatever you want.”</p>

<p>Pentos stepped down from the carriage and walked a short distance away from the road. The cold night wind moved quietly through the trees while the moonlight illuminated the empty path.</p>

<p>He reached a small clearing and stopped.Then he took a crystal from inside his coat.</p>

<p>The crystal glowed faintly as he activated it.</p>

<p>A ripple of light spread across its surface.</p>

<p>Moments later, a shadowy figure appeared inside the crystal projection.</p>

<p>The figure’s face remained hidden in darkness.</p>

<p>A calm voice came from the crystal.</p>

<p>[So how is it?]</p>

<p>Pentos looked slightly irritated.</p>

<p>“He is strong,” he said coldly.</p>

<p>“And he seems quite arrogant.”</p>

<p>He paused briefly.</p>

<p>“We were beaten up as expected.”</p>

<p>For a moment the figure on the other side remained silent.</p>

<p>Then the voice spoke again calmly.</p>

<p>[I expected that much.]</p>

<p>Pentos’ expression immediately turned sour when he heard that.</p>

<p>“What do you mean you expected that much?” he asked with clear annoyance.</p>

<p>His grip on the crystal tightened slightly.The shadowy figure remained calm.</p>

<p>[It seems our preliminary analysis of him still fell short.]</p>

<p>The figure paused briefly before continuing.</p>

<p>[Duke Lancelot might be the one who saved him from the assassination.]</p>

<p>Pentos frowned slightly.</p>

<p>“Hmmm.”</p>

<p>He nodded slowly.</p>

<p>“Duke Lancelot’s support has formed a huge barrier that will protect that bastard.”</p>

<p>The shadowy figure remained silent for a moment.</p>

<p>Then the voice spoke again.</p>

<p>[It will.]</p>

<p>[But not for long.]</p>

<p>Pentos immediately raised his head.</p>

<p>“Not for long?”</p>

<p>“What do you mean?”</p>

<p>His tone carried curiosity and a hint of unease.</p>

<p>For a brief moment the shadowy figure did not answer.</p>

<p>Then the figure leaned slightly forward.</p>

<p>Even through the crystal projection, Pentos could sense the cold amusement behind the words.</p>

<p>The shadowy figure’s lips slowly curved into a twisted smile.</p>

<p>[Only time will tell.]</p>

<p>The crystal flickered once.</p>

<p>Then the projection disappeared completely.Pentos lowered the crystal slowly.</p>

<p>The cold wind moved across the clearing again.</p>

<p>His expression remained serious as he stared into the darkness ahead.</p>

<p>“These old foggies and their way of making things mysterious is really a pain in the ass.”</p>
