<p>For a few seconds after the Direwolf suddenly stopped, the entire square remained frozen.</p>

<p>No one moved.</p>

<p>People stared at the giant beast sitting quietly in front of the frightened child. The boy was still sitting on the ground with wide eyes while trembling slightly.</p>

<p>Then the murmurs slowly began.</p>

<p>“What… what just happened?”</p>

<p>“That wolf was about to bite the child!”</p>

<p>“How did it stop like that?”</p>

<p>Several people wiped sweat from their foreheads while staring at the beast with lingering fear.</p>

<p>Some mothers quickly ran forward and pulled their children back into their arms. Others simply stared at the Direwolf with disbelief.</p>

<p>Meanwhile, the gentle flute melody continued drifting through the air.Everyone slowly turned their heads toward the direction of the sound.</p>

<p>From between the crowd, a man walked forward calmly while playing the flute.</p>

<p>His steps were slow and steady.</p>

<p>The flute remained near his lips while the melody continued flowing softly.</p>

<p>The crowd instinctively parted to give him space.The man walked straight toward the Direwolf.</p>

<p>Even Miranda watched him carefully now.</p>

<p>The man finally stopped in front of the giant beast.</p>

<p>The melody faded.</p>

<p>He lowered the flute slowly.Then he bent down and gently reached toward the Direwolf’s head.</p>

<p>His hand brushed against the thick fur behind the wolf’s ear.</p>

<p>The beast let out a soft sound and wagged its tail slightly like a large dog enjoying attention.</p>

<p>The man scratched its neck gently while smiling faintly.The wolf almost looked childish at that moment.</p>

<p>After a moment the man paused and lifted his head.</p>

<p>His gaze turned toward the tamer standing nearby.</p>

<p>“You should take it out carefully lest it harm others,” he said calmly.</p>

<p>The tamer hurried forward immediately.</p>

<p>He wiped the sweat from his forehead while nodding repeatedly.</p>

<p>“Yes… yes. Thank you.”</p>

<p>“I didn’t expect it to go wild like that all of a sudden.”</p>

<p>The man simply nodded once.</p>

<p>“Just tame it properly.”</p>

<p>The tamer quickly grabbed the wolf’s leash again and began guiding the animal away while the guards rushed forward to restore order in the square.</p>

<p>Several city guards arrived and started calming the crowd.</p>

<p>“Everyone step back.”</p>

<p>“Give them space.”</p>

<p>The small boy was quickly picked up by his parents while they thanked the guards and hurried away.</p>

<p>Meanwhile, the man quietly slipped through the crowd as if nothing had happened.</p>

<p>He began walking away from the square.</p>

<p>But just as he reached the edge of the street, someone stepped in front of him.</p>

<p>Miranda.</p>

<p>She stood there with her arms folded while looking directly at him.The man stopped.</p>

<p>He looked at her calmly for a moment.</p>

<p>Then he raised an eyebrow slightly.</p>

<p>“Pretty girl,” he said casually, “didn’t your parents tell you not to follow a stranger?”</p>

<p>Miranda ignored the comment.</p>

<p>Her eyes were focused on the flute still held in his hand.</p>

<p>“Teach me that,” she said directly.</p>

<p>The man tilted his head slightly.</p>

<p>“Teach what?” he asked while feigning ignorance.</p>

<p>Miranda pointed toward the flute.</p>

<p>“Teach me how you controlled the beast with that flute.”</p>

<p>The man shook his head lightly.</p>

<p>“You are mistaking something.”</p>

<p>“No,” Miranda replied firmly.</p>

<p>“I saw it clearly with my own eyes.”</p>

<p>“Your notes hit the beast and stopped it.”</p>

<p>The man studied her expression for a moment.</p>

<p>She did not look like she was joking.</p>

<p>He sighed softly and turned slightly as if preparing to leave.</p>

<p>“It was just a coincidence,” he said.</p>

<p>But Miranda quickly stepped forward and blocked his path again.</p>

<p>“I know what I saw.”</p>

<p>Her voice was serious now.</p>

<p>The man frowned slightly.</p>

<p>“You are being stubborn.”</p>

<p>Miranda suddenly bowed her head slightly.</p>

<p>The action surprised him.</p>

<p>“Please teach me,” she said quietly.</p>

<p>Her tone was no longer demanding.Instead it sounded sincere.</p>

<p>“I am serious.”</p>

<p>The man looked at her for a few seconds.Miranda raised her head again.</p>

<p>Her expression carried determination.</p>

<p>“I know I might be asking a lot,” she continued.</p>

<p>“But I really want to learn that.”</p>

<p>“I have never seen someone calm a beast like that before.”</p>

<p>The man crossed his arms slowly while studying her face.</p>

<p>“You are asking a stranger to teach you something without even knowing who he is,” he said.</p>

<p>Miranda shrugged slightly.</p>

<p>“You stopped a Direwolf from killing a child.”</p>

<p>“That’s enough for me.”</p>

<p>The man could not help letting out a quiet chuckle.</p>

<p>“You are quite straightforward.”</p>

<p>Miranda did not back down.</p>

<p>“I am serious.”</p>

<p>The man looked down at the flute in his hand for a moment.</p>

<p>Then he looked back at her again.</p>

<p>“You really want to learn that badly?” he asked.</p>

<p>Miranda nodded immediately.</p>

<p>“Yes.”</p>

<p>The man sighed quietly.</p>

<p>“You know,” he said slowly, “learning something like this is not simple.”</p>

<p>Miranda did not hesitate.</p>

<p>“I am not afraid of difficult things.”</p>

<p>The man watched her for another moment.</p>

<p>Then a faint smile appeared on his face.</p>

<p>“Well,” he said calmly, “that depends.”</p>

<p>Miranda blinked.</p>

<p>“Depends on what?”</p>

<p>The man lifted the flute slightly.</p>

<p>“Depends on whether you are actually capable of learning it.”</p>

<p>The man smiled slightly while looking at Miranda.”Not everyone can learn this just because they want to,” he said calmly.</p>

<p>“One needs to have a particular talent.”</p>

<p>He glanced briefly at Miranda’s clear eyes that were staring at him with determination.</p>

<p>For a moment he simply looked away and turned around as if to ignore her.But Miranda did not give up.</p>

<p>From that day onward, she began following him everywhere.</p>

<p>If he walked through the market, she was there behind him. If he stopped near the city gates, she stood nearby watching quietly. Even when he sat beside a quiet street corner playing his flute, Miranda remained not far away while observing him carefully.</p>

<p>“Father said persistent efforts lead to success..As his daughter, how can I give up easily.”</p>

<p>Day after day it continued.</p>

<p>Sometimes she tried speaking to him again. Sometimes she simply stayed silent and watched.</p>

<p>At first the man ignored her completely.</p>

<p>But after several days he finally sighed in frustration.One afternoon he stopped walking and suddenly turned around.</p>

<p>Miranda nearly bumped into him.</p>

<p>“Fine, little girl,” he said with clear irritation.</p>

<p>“If you can play a flute that satisfies me, then I am going to teach you.”</p>

<p>Miranda’s face lit up instantly.</p>

<p>“Really?”</p>
