<p>He swung upward, and a massive arc of light mixed with chaos tore through the incoming attacks, shattering them completely.</p>
<p>The Abyss Sect member tried to bind him with shadow tendrils, but Ethan stomped the ground.</p>
<p>CRACK!</p>
<p>A shockwave burst out, destroying everything around him.</p>
<p>Then his aura surged even higher.</p>
<p>From behind him, the phantom of a dragon slowly emerged, formed from blazing energy. Its massive body coiled in the air, and its eyes burned like molten fire.</p>
<p>ROOOOOAR!</p>
<p>The dragon roared, and the sound alone shook the battlefield.</p>
<p>Ethan lifted his sword.</p>
<p>“Die.”</p>
<p>The dragon opened its mouth.</p>
<p>BOOOOOOOOOOOM!</p>
<p>A catastrophic beam of light shot forward, and it collided directly with the combined attack of the five enemies. For a brief moment, the two forces struggled, and the entire space trembled violently.</p>
<p>Then the sky seemed to be shattering.</p>
<p>The beam crushed everything.</p>
<p>The attacks of the five were completely annihilated, and the remaining force slammed into them, sending all five flying across the battlefield.</p>
<p>BOOM! BOOM! BOOM!</p>
<p>Their bodies crashed into the ground, and blood spilled as they were heavily injured.</p>
<p>Ethan did not stop.</p>
<p>He rushed forward again like a storm.</p>
<p>CLANG! CLANG! CLANG!</p>
<p>His sword moved continuously, and every strike carried terrifying power. Holy Light burned, Chaos energy tore, and every slash ripped through flesh and defenses alike.</p>
<p>The battlefield turned brutal.He was not holding back any ore.</p>
<p>He had three lives given by the puppet.</p>
<p>And one of them was already gone.</p>
<p>That thought burned in his mind.His eyes turned colder.</p>
<p>“…I will burn you all down.”</p>
<p>His attacks became even fiercer, and the five enemies, who had come with confidence, were now being pushed back completely, unable to withstand the storm he had become.</p>
<p>The battlefield had already turned into chaos, and before the remaining enemies could even steady themselves, Ethan moved again while his killing intent surged sharply across the area.</p>
<p>His figure blurred and then vanished, and in the next instant he appeared right in front of one of the demons who was still trying to recover from the earlier clash. The demon’s eyes widened in panic, and it hurriedly raised its arm to defend itself, but it was already too late.</p>
<p>Ethan swung his sword without hesitation.</p>
<p>CLANG!</p>
<p>The impact crushed through the defense, and before the demon could react further, the blade cut down completely.</p>
<p>SWOOSH!</p>
<p>The Solaris Tyrant sliced straight through its body, and the Holy Light mixed with Chaos energy burst out from the wound, tearing the demon apart from the inside. Its body could not even hold together, and it exploded into pieces while blood scattered across the ground.</p>
<p>The sight alone made the remaining enemies freeze.</p>
<p>At the same time, one of the Church of Darkness members tried to intervene, and he raised his hand to release a divine attack, but Ethan turned toward him instantly and struck again.</p>
<p>BOOM!</p>
<p>A massive arc of light erupted from the blade, and it slammed directly into the cloaked figure. The divine barrier around him shattered under the force, and his body was sent flying across the battlefield while he coughed out blood.</p>
<p>That single exchange broke whatever confidence they had left.</p>
<p>Fear spread instantly.</p>
<p>“…Run!” one of the demons shouted, and this time none of them hesitated as they turned and tried to escape.</p>
<p>The battlefield shifted again, and Han and Hale, who had been watching everything unfold, finally snapped out of their shock and reacted at the same time.</p>
<p>“Don’t let them escape!” Han roared as he rushed forward, and Hale followed closely behind him.</p>
<p>They intercepted two of the fleeing enemies, blocking their paths and forcing them back into combat, and the clash resumed on their side with loud impacts echoing again.</p>
<p>However, one of them managed to slip through.</p>
<p>The Abyss Sect member.</p>
<p>He did not even look back, and his body turned into a streak of dark energy as he fled at full speed, trying to escape the battlefield as fast as possible.</p>
<p>Ethan’s gaze locked onto him instantly, and his expression turned colder.</p>
<p>“…You think you can leave?”</p>
<p>He stepped forward slowly, and then his eyes shifted downward.</p>
<p>The spear lay on the ground.</p>
<p>The same spear that had pierced through his clone.</p>
<p>For a brief moment, his grip tightened as he picked it up, and a faint killing intent flickered in his eyes.</p>
<p>Then he poured his power into it.</p>
<p>His arm trembled slightly as draconic scales spread across it, and his fingers twisted into a claw-like form. Crimson patterns lit up along his skin, and a violent surge of fiery mana burst out from his body, distorting the air around him.</p>
<p>The ground beneath his feet cracked as the pressure increased.</p>
<p>“Run,” he said quietly, while pulling his arm back.</p>
<p>“Let’s see how far you go.”</p>
<p>The energy gathered rapidly, and the spear began to tremble under the force being poured into it.</p>
<p>Then—</p>
<p>He threw it.</p>
<p>BOOOOOOM!</p>
<p>The spear shot out like a falling meteor, and the moment it left his hand, the space around it twisted violently as if it could not withstand the speed. A massive shockwave followed behind it, and the sound barrier was shattered instantly.</p>
<p>The sky seemed to split apart as the spear cut through the air.</p>
<p>Everything along its path was destroyed.</p>
<p>The fleeing Abyss Sect member felt it immediately, and his body stiffened as a terrifying sense of danger washed over him.</p>
<p>His eyes widened in horror.</p>
<p>“NOOOOOO…”</p>
<p>He tried to move faster, but it was already too late.The spear caught up in an instant.</p>
<p>BOOOOOOOOOOOM!</p>
<p>A massive explosion erupted, and the impact shook the entire battlefield. The ground collapsed, and the shockwave spread outward in all directions, flattening everything within its range.</p>
<p>Even distant mountains cracked under the force, and the land was torn apart as if struck by divine punishment.</p>
<p>A huge cloud of smoke and fire rose into the sky, covering the area completely.For a moment, nothing could be seen.</p>
