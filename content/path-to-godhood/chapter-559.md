<p>With the Shaman gone, Rathlos no longer had to split his focus, and he turned his full attention toward the remaining Mythic Knights while preparing to finish the fight quickly, but before he could press forward, a sudden force crackled across the mountains and froze the battlefield for a brief moment.</p>
<p>“You dare harm Beastmen?”</p>
<p>A voice echoed across the sky, and it carried heavy pressure that pressed down on everyone present.</p>
<p>“I want to see who dares to kill them.”</p>
<p>BOOOOOOOM!</p>
<p>A strong pillar of light erupted, and a streak flashed across the sky while tearing through the clouds and shaking everything in its path.</p>
<p>The pressure alone was enough to make weaker soldiers stagger, and even the ground trembled under it.</p>
<p>Rathlos reacted instantly, and he raised his sword while channeling his power to block.</p>
<p>CLANG!</p>
<p>A crisp, ringing sound erupted, and the impact sent Rathlos flying backward while his body tore through the air and crashed across the mountainside before slamming into the ground.</p>
<p>Dust and debris rose.</p>
<p>Amber’s eyes narrowed sharply, and she moved forward to assist him, but just as she stepped.</p>
<p>“Stop!”</p>
<p>SWOOSH!</p>
<p>Rathlos’s body shot up from the rubble, and a dense killing intent radiated from him while a deep and chilling aura spread outward, and darkness gathered around his figure again.</p>
<p>He wiped the bluish blood from his lips and let out a low growl.</p>
<p>“It has been long since I had a good fight,” he said.</p>
<p>Across from him, the Beastman Divine Knight stood with a heavy hammer resting on his shoulder, and his eyes narrowed as he looked at Rathlos carefully.</p>
<p>“A Wyvern human…” he muttered.</p>
<p>Then his expression changed slightly as if he recalled something.</p>
<p>“You… what is your relationship with Ragorck?” he asked.</p>
<p>Rathlos did not hesitate.</p>
<p>“That’s my father,” he replied.</p>
<p>The Beastman’s gaze turned colder.</p>
<p>“So you are his son?” he said, and a chilling intent flashed in his eyes.</p>
<p>There was no more talk after that.</p>
<p>BOOOOM!</p>
<p>The clash exploded instantly, and Rathlos stepped forward while his sword cut through the air, and darkness followed his blade as if it were alive, stretching outward and striking with deadly force.</p>
<p>The Beastman raised his hammer and blocked, and the impact created a shockwave that spread across the battlefield while sending snow and debris flying.</p>
<p>The ground cracked beneath them.</p>
<p>The Beastman swung his hammer again, and the attack came down with crushing force while aiming to break Rathlos completely.</p>
<p>Rathlos twisted his body and avoided the direct hit, but the force still brushed past him and slammed into the ground, and the impact created a deep crater.</p>
<p>Without slowing, Rathlos countered, and his sword moved in a sharp arc while releasing a wave of darkness that cut through the air and struck the Beastman’s side.</p>
<p>SHIIIING!</p>
<p>Blood spilled, but the Beastman did not step back, and instead he roared while swinging his hammer sideways.</p>
<p>BOOOOM!</p>
<p>The blow landed, and Rathlos was thrown back again while his body slammed into the ground and rolled across the shattered terrain.</p>
<p>He pushed himself up immediately, and his breathing grew heavier, but his eyes remained sharp.</p>
<p>The Beastman advanced again, and his attacks came one after another while his hammer struck with overwhelming force, and each blow carried enough power to crush bones.</p>
<p>Rathlos blocked some, dodged others, but he still took hits.</p>
<p>His body shook with each impact, and faint cracks could be felt in his bones, but he did not fall.</p>
<p>Instead, he pushed forward again.</p>
<p>Darkness surged around him, and his sword moved faster while leaving trails behind, and each strike carved into the Beastman’s body.</p>
<p>Cuts began to appear.</p>
<p>Blood flowed.</p>
<p>But the Beastman endured, and he continued to press forward while ignoring the pain.</p>
<p>Their clash shook the entire area, and every collision sent waves through the battlefield while nearby soldiers were forced to retreat just to avoid being caught in it.</p>
<p>BOOOOM!</p>
<p>CLANG!</p>
<p>SHIIIING!</p>
<p>The sound of their fight echoed continuously.</p>
<p>Rathlos stepped in again, and this time his body shifted slightly while dark scales appeared across parts of him, and his strength surged for a brief moment.</p>
<p>He closed the distance instantly.</p>
<p>Before the Beastman could react, Rathlos slammed his head forward.</p>
<p>CRACK!</p>
<p>His head collided directly with the Beastman’s face, and the impact shattered the air while a sickening sound echoed.</p>
<p>The Beastman’s nose was crushed, and blood burst out as his head snapped backward.</p>
<p>For a brief moment he staggered.</p>
<p>And the fight only grew more brutal from there.</p>
<p>Rathlos did not give the Beastman any time to recover, and he stepped forward again while forcing his battered body to move, and darkness gathered tightly around his blade as his aura surged for one final push. His breathing was heavy, and each breath came out rough, but his eyes stayed locked on his opponent and did not waver.</p>
<p>“…Let’s end this,” he muttered, and his grip on the sword tightened.</p>
<p>The Beastman roared loudly and swung his hammer again, and the strike came down with brutal force while aiming to crush Rathlos completely, but Rathlos did not retreat this time and instead chose to move forward.</p>
<p>For a brief moment, everything around him seemed to slow down, and a strange stillness appeared as the darkness around his sword condensed sharply and became dense and focused.</p>
<p>“Shadow Severance Art,” he said under his breath.</p>
<p>SWISHHH!</p>
<p>His blade flashed forward, and the strike cut through space itself while slipping past the Beastman’s defense, and before the hammer could even land, a clean cut passed through the Beastman’s arm.</p>
<p>CRACK!</p>
<p>The arm separated instantly, and blood burst outward while the hammer slipped from his grip and crashed heavily onto the ground, and the sound echoed across the broken terrain.</p>
<p>The Beastman’s eyes widened for a split second as shock flashed across his face, but Rathlos did not stop there.</p>
<p>BOOOOM!</p>
<p>He followed with another strike, and the force slammed the Beastman down into the ground while creating a deep crater and sending debris and snow flying in all directions.</p>
<p>Dust rose and covered the area.</p>
<p>For a moment, it seemed like everything had ended.</p>
<p>But then..</p>
<p>“Haahh!” the Beastman laughed loudly, and his voice echoed from within the crater while sounding almost insane.</p>
<p>Rathlos frowned slightly as he looked down, and his chest rose and fell as he tried to steady his breathing.</p>
<p>The Beastman slowly pushed himself up despite the missing arm, and blood continued to pour from the wound while staining the ground, yet there was no fear in his eyes.</p>
<p>Instead, there was a wide grin on his face.</p>
<p>“Do you think it’s over?” he said, and his voice carried a rough edge.</p>
<p>“No… this is just the beginning…”</p>
<p>His eyes burned with a strange and unsettling light.</p>
<p>“We are just scouts. There will be more reinforcement.”</p>
<p>As soon as his words ended, the air shifted again.</p>
<p>SWISH! SWISH! SWISH!</p>
<p>Several figures appeared in the sky one after another, and their presence pressed down heavily across the battlefield while multiple auras spread outward at the same time.</p>
<p>Divine Knights.</p>
<p>More than one.</p>
<p>Rathlos felt it instantly, and his injured body stiffened slightly while the pressure grew heavier around him, and even the ground beneath his feet seemed to sink.</p>
<p>His strength had already been pushed to its limit, and although he had managed to defeat one Divine Knight, he no longer had enough left to face what was coming next.</p>
<p>For a brief moment, it looked like the end had arrived.</p>
<p>But instead of fear, a faint smile appeared on his face, and it slowly deepened as he looked up at the sky.</p>
<p>Then something changed.</p>
<p>A sudden pressure descended from above, and it felt even heavier than before while forcing everyone to look upward.</p>
<p>The sky itself seemed to split apart.</p>
<p>A massive hand of light emerged from the sky, and it pressed downward while locking the entire area in place as if nothing could escape it.</p>
<p>“Want to trample the region under us?” a voice echoed, and it spread across the battlefield clearly.</p>
<p>It was calm, but it carried absolute authority.</p>
<p>“Have you asked me?”</p>
<p>SWISH!</p>
<p>A figure appeared in the sky, and he stood there while holding a spear that radiated a terrifying aura, and the weapon locked onto the Divine Knights below as if it had already chosen its targets.</p>
<p>His presence alone changed everything, and the pressure shifted instantly.</p>
<p>“Want to fight?” he said.</p>
<p>“Then this young master will accompany you.”</p>
<p>The Divine Knights froze for a brief moment as they sensed him, and even their expressions changed slightly while the pressure around them became heavier.</p>
<p>It was overwhelming.</p>
<p>The figure then glanced down toward Rathlos and Amber, and his tone became slightly lighter.</p>
<p>“Retreat,” he said.</p>
<p>“Leave this to me.”</p>
<p>Amber did not hesitate, and she moved toward Rathlos immediately while supporting him by the shoulder, and Rathlos gave a small nod without arguing as he steadied himself.</p>
<p>Both of them began to withdraw from the battlefield while stepping back carefully, and neither of them looked back again.</p>
<p>Behind them, the pressure in the air continued to rise.</p>
