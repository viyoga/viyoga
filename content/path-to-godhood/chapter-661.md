<p>They were currently marching through one of the most perilous regions on the continent. Battles could break out at any moment and casualties could rise rapidly if the army lacked strong experts. Because of that, the large number of potions and cultivation resources Ethan had just received were more than enough to raise the overall strength of his troops by a considerable amount.</p>
<p>More importantly...</p>
<p>He could also increase the cultivation realms of the people directly under him.</p>
<p>Thinking of this, Ethan immediately stood up and walked outside his tent.</p>
<p>"Randall."</p>
<p>"Noel."</p>
<p>"Maverick."</p>
<p>The three quickly arrived after hearing his call.</p>
<p>"Yes, My Lord!"</p>
<p>All three respectfully bowed before him.</p>
<p>Ethan quietly observed them for a moment.</p>
<p>Maverick had already reached the middle stage of the Divine Knight Realm through years of hard training and countless life and death battles. Almost every bit of his strength had been earned through his own efforts.</p>
<p>Randall and Noel were different.</p>
<p>Both of them had reached the peak of the Legendary Realm with Ethan’s help and had relied on many cultivation potions along the way.</p>
<p>However, their foundations were anything but weak.</p>
<p>Although their natural talents could not compare with true geniuses, they had followed Ethan into battle countless times. Every dangerous battlefield and every desperate fight had polished their combat skills. Their lack of talent had gradually been compensated by the tremendous amount of battle experience they had accumulated over the years.</p>
<p>Their cultivation might have advanced with the help of treasures, but their strength was absolutely real.</p>
<p>Ethan nodded slightly before taking several bottles from his storage ring.</p>
<p>Different colored potions quietly appeared in his hands.A rich medicinal fragrance immediately spread through the tent.</p>
<p>He handed the bottles toward them.</p>
<p>Randall and Noel looked at the potions before instinctively raising their heads toward Ethan.</p>
<p>Neither of them immediately accepted the gifts.</p>
<p>This was not the first time Ethan had given them cultivation resources.</p>
<p>However...</p>
<p>Receiving such valuable treasures in the middle of a war still surprised them.</p>
<p>Noticing their strange expressions, Ethan lightly coughed.</p>
<p>"Calm down, guys."</p>
<p>"I’ve had these potions for a long time."</p>
<p>"Mareti prepared them before the war."</p>
<p>"I simply didn’t give them to you earlier because your cultivation was still lacking."</p>
<p>He folded his arms while speaking calmly.</p>
<p>"Without enough accumulation, taking these potions would have been a grave mistake."</p>
<p>"You’ve all fought enough battles now."</p>
<p>"Your foundations are stable."</p>
<p>"So this is the right time."</p>
<p>A smile slowly appeared on Randall’s face.</p>
<p>Noel’s eyes also brightened.</p>
<p>Maverick silently accepted the potion while carefully placing it inside his storage ring.</p>
<p>Ethan looked at the three of them and nodded.</p>
<p>"Go."</p>
<p>"Break through as quickly as possible."</p>
<p>"I want all of you stronger before the next battle."</p>
<p>The three immediately straightened their backs.</p>
<p>"Yes!"</p>
<p>They saluted together with grateful expressions.</p>
<p>Just as they prepared to leave, Ethan suddenly called out again.</p>
<p>"Wait."</p>
<p>The three immediately stopped.</p>
<p>Ethan casually tossed another storage ring toward Randall.</p>
<p>Randall hurriedly caught it, although he almost fumbled because of the unexpected throw.</p>
<p>"What is this, My Lord?"</p>
<p>Ethan answered calmly.</p>
<p>"It contains cultivation resources, healing medicines, recovery potions, weapons, and supplies."</p>
<p>"Distribute everything among the troops."</p>
<p>"Do it according to merit."</p>
<p>"Reward those who fought bravely."</p>
<p>"Those who contribute more should naturally receive more."</p>
<p>Randall immediately nodded.</p>
<p>"I understand."</p>
<p>"I won’t disappoint you."</p>
<p>Ethan waved his hand.</p>
<p>"Go."</p>
<p>The three bowed once again before quickly leaving the tent.</p>
<p>Soon afterward, the resources began reaching Ethan’s personal troops. Excited voices occasionally echoed throughout the camp as soldiers received medicines, cultivation resources, and better equipment. The atmosphere became much livelier because everyone understood that stronger soldiers had a better chance of surviving the coming battles.</p>
<p>After arranging everything, Ethan finally returned inside his own tent.</p>
<p>He slowly sat down cross-legged.</p>
<p>With a wave of his hand, several ancient formation flags flew out and firmly embedded themselves around the tent.</p>
<p>An ancient array quietly activated.</p>
<p>Layers of transparent barriers appeared one after another before completely sealing the surrounding area.</p>
<p>Only after confirming that nothing could leak outside did Ethan slowly close his eyes.</p>
<p>His consciousness entered the system.</p>
<p>His gaze settled upon the reward.</p>
<p>EX-rank reward of 10% Law of Light Comprehension.</p>
<p>After taking a slow breath, he silently chose to absorb it.</p>
<p>The notification disappeared.</p>
<p>At the same moment...</p>
<p>The instant Ethan accepted the inheritance, an immeasurable stream of profound knowledge flooded into his consciousness. It was not information that needed to be memorized, nor was it a collection of techniques waiting to be learned. It was pure understanding. Every concept entered his mind as though it had always belonged there, allowing him to witness the very essence of light from its origin to its countless manifestations.</p>
<p>His consciousness drifted into an endless world where darkness stretched without boundaries, and within that endless void, the first ray of light quietly appeared. It traveled across immeasurable distances without hesitation, illuminating worlds that had never known brightness. It nurtured life, revealed hidden truths, guided countless beings through the unknown, and at the same time possessed enough power to erase civilizations if unleashed without restraint. Only at that moment did Ethan begin to understand that light had never been merely an element used for spells. It was one of the fundamental principles upon which existence itself was built.</p>
<p>Light was revelation because it allowed reality to be perceived. Without it, shape, color, distance, and direction became meaningless. It was guidance because every civilization throughout history had relied upon it to move forward. It was warmth because it sustained life, yet it was equally capable of becoming destruction when concentrated beyond its limits. It carried energy across unimaginable distances, connected stars separated by countless millions of kilometers, and silently recorded the history of the universe as it continued its endless journey.</p>
<p>Every beam of light contained countless mysteries hidden beneath its simple appearance. To ordinary cultivators, light was only something that shined or burned, but the Law revealed that its true nature was infinitely deeper. It was both gentle and violent. It could illuminate an entire world while remaining intangible. It possessed no mass, yet it carried momentum. It appeared continuous, yet it was composed of countless individual quanta. Every contradiction existed together without canceling the other, forming a perfect balance that ordinary logic struggled to explain.</p>
<p>Cultivators of this world spent centuries meditating beneath the sun, studying sacred scriptures, or seeking sudden enlightenment in hopes of grasping even the smallest fragment of this Law. Many exhausted their entire lives chasing that single moment of realization, only to die with nothing more than vague understanding. The reason was simple. They observed light only through cultivation, while Ethan unconsciously viewed it through another perspective as well.</p>
