<p>Early one morning he walked toward a secluded stone chamber located deep within the Blanks, a place that he often used for meditation, training, and occasionally for studying certain things that required complete silence.</p>

<p>The chamber was hidden behind several thick stone corridors and protected by multiple layers of security formations, which ensured that no one would disturb him unless something extremely urgent happened.</p>

<p>Ethan stepped inside the chamber and slowly closed the heavy stone door behind him while the faint echo of the door sliding into place spread through the room.</p>

<p>The chamber itself was dimly lit by several mana lamps fixed along the walls, their soft bluish glow illuminating the stone floor and the quiet, still atmosphere inside.</p>

<p>He walked calmly toward the center of the chamber and sat down cross-legged on the cold stone floor while placing both hands lightly on his knees.</p>

<p>For a moment he simply closed his eyes and took a slow breath as his mind gradually settled into a calm state.</p>

<p>Then he opened the system interface in his mind.</p>

<p>The notification from the birth of his child had still not been fully examined.</p>

<p>Ethan’s gaze slowly sharpened as he focused on the floating panel.</p>

<p>“Let’s see what the reward is.”</p>

<p>The moment he focused on the notification, the familiar sound appeared inside his mind.</p>

<p>[Ding]</p>

<p>A new set of glowing information slowly appeared before his eyes.</p>

<p>For a brief moment Ethan simply stared at the words as if confirming that he had read them correctly.</p>

<p>Then his eyes widened slightly.</p>

<p>The rewards appeared one after another.</p>

<p>[You have gained 1000 years of Knight Insight.]</p>

<p>[You have gained EX rank forging talent.]</p>

<p>[You have gained a Greatsword forging manual.]</p>

<p>[You have gained Draconic Bloodline Potion *1.]</p>

<p>[You have gained Divine Soul Flame.]</p>

<p>The chamber fell completely silent.</p>

<p>Even Ethan, who had already experienced many strange rewards before, could not hide the shock that appeared in his eyes.</p>

<p>“…1000 years of Knight Insight?”</p>

<p>He slowly leaned back slightly while staring at the glowing words floating in front of him.</p>

<p>That reward alone was already terrifying.</p>

<p>Knight Insight represented the accumulated understanding of countless battles, sword techniques, combat instincts, and the deeper principles behind martial power.</p>

<p>A thousand years of insight meant knowledge that normally required entire generations of warriors to accumulate through endless training, battles, victories, and failures.</p>

<p>Ethan quietly exhaled as he processed that thought.</p>

<p>Then his gaze moved to the next reward.</p>

<p>“Wait… what the hell is this?”</p>

<p>“Ex rank forging talent…”</p>

<p>“A talent?”</p>

<p>“System… now you can even give talents?” Ethan exclaimed in shock.</p>

<p>And that too an Ex rank talent.</p>

<p>That meant his natural ability in forging weapons had instantly reached the highest possible level.</p>

<p>Combined with the next reward, the forging manual, it clearly pointed toward a powerful path related to weapon creation.</p>

<p>“A Greatsword forging manual…”</p>

<p>Even without opening it, Ethan could already feel that the manual contained something extremely rare and valuable.</p>

<p>Then his eyes moved to the next reward.</p>

<p>“Draconic Bloodline Potion…”</p>

<p>A faint smile slowly appeared on his face.</p>

<p>Anything related to dragon bloodlines was never ordinary.</p>

<p>Finally his gaze settled on the last reward.</p>

<p>“Divine Soul Flame.”</p>

<p>Ethan’s expression slowly turned serious.</p>

<p>Even the name alone sounded extraordinary.A flame connected to the soul.</p>

<p>Possibly something capable of refining weapons, enhancing spiritual energy, or forging items beyond normal limits.</p>

<p>Also it can be used to cast attack.It can reside in his divine soul and can directly attack a person’s soul.</p>

<p>Ethan remained silent for several moments while carefully processing all the rewards.</p>

<p>Then he slowly spoke to himself.</p>

<p>“…Looks like this child really brought some terrifying rewards.”</p>

<p>His eyes gradually sharpened with interest.</p>

<p>“Let’s start with the talent one.”</p>

<p>He focused on the forging talent.</p>

<p>“Absorb.”</p>

<p>The next moment an enormous flood of information slammed directly into his mind.</p>

<p>“Ugh!”</p>

<p>Ethan instinctively grabbed his forehead as a wave of dizziness hit him.It had been a long time since he experienced something like this.</p>

<p>Endless streams of knowledge flooded his mind like a rushing river.</p>

<p>Information about metals, temperatures, hammering techniques, alloy structures, and forging processes appeared one after another.</p>

<p>He could see the structure of steel forming under intense heat.He saw impurities being burned away inside a blazing furnace.</p>

<p>He saw different methods of folding metal layers repeatedly in order to strengthen the internal core of a blade.</p>

<p>Basic forging techniques appeared first.</p>

<p>How to heat the metal evenly inside the furnace.</p>

<p>How to strike with precise force while maintaining the internal structure.</p>

<p>How to shape a weapon without damaging the balance of its core.</p>

<p>Then the knowledge moved deeper.</p>

<p>Complex rune inscriptions began appearing in his mind.</p>

<p>Forging was not simply hitting metal with a hammer.True weapon forging involved magical runic structures.</p>

<p>Each rune needed to be carved carefully while controlling the flow of mana.</p>

<p>And every rune needed to connect with the next one in perfect harmony.</p>

<p>Without magical knowledge, a blacksmith could never reach the highest ranks.</p>

<p>Fortunately Ethan already possessed a strong magical understanding.</p>

<p>After several moments the flood of information gradually stabilized.</p>

<p>Ethan slowly opened his eyes.His expression had changed.</p>

<p>He quietly looked around the chamber.</p>

<p>Immediately he began noticing things he had never paid attention to before.</p>

<p>Even the rune patterns engraved on the mana lamps around the chamber looked flawed.</p>

<p>Tiny distortions in the runic lines became clearly visible to his eyes.</p>

<p>Some connections between symbols were slightly misaligned.</p>

<p>Some mana pathways were inefficient.</p>

<p>Others leaked small amounts of energy without proper circulation.</p>

<p>Ethan rubbed his chin while observing them carefully.</p>

<p>“Haaa…”</p>

<p>“I can’t blame Otlo.”</p>

<p>“He is just a D rank forger who spent years climbing to B rank.”</p>

<p>Otlo was already one of the best smiths Ethan had managed to hire.</p>

<p>But compared to the knowledge now inside Ethan’s mind, the difference was enormous.</p>

<p>Ethan had tried many times to hire high level blacksmiths.</p>

<p>But most powerful human smiths worked under the Adventurer Guild.Even among them, those capable of reaching A rank or higher were extremely rare.</p>

<p>And most of them were disciples of dwarven masters.</p>

<p>Dwarven blacksmiths were legendary craftsmen.</p>

<p>But they were also extremely difficult to find.</p>

<p>Ethan slowly leaned back and looked at his hands.</p>

<p>A faint smile appeared on his face.</p>

<p>“Well…”</p>

<p>“It seems I don’t need to rely on others anymore.”</p>

<p>“I can even teach others and raise them..”</p>
