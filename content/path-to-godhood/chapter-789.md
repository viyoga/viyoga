<p>Ethan looked at the pillar for a moment before slowly reaching toward his back. His fingers wrapped around the handle of his greatsword, and he pulled it out with a steady motion. The heavy blade came into view, and a faint pressure spread through the underground chamber.</p>
<p>"Time to work Solaris!" Ethan spoke, pulling out his mana.</p>
<p>Swoosh!</p>
<p>The moment his mana surged through his body, a heavy flame blast erupted from him. Intense flames poured out from his body and quickly gathered around the greatsword. The blade began to glow as the flames wrapped around it, and waves of heat spread through the chamber.</p>
<p>The energy around Ethan continued to rise. Flames moved around his body and then gathered toward Solaris, forming a thick layer of fire around the greatsword. The ground beneath his feet started to heat up, and the nearby magic formations flickered from the sudden pressure.</p>
<p>Behind Ethan, a faint silhouette of a flame dragon slowly appeared.</p>
<p>Its huge shape hovered behind his back as flames continued to pour from Ethan’s body. The dragon’s form was not completely clear, but its presence made the air feel heavy and hot.</p>
<p>The flames concentrated around Solaris and spread across the chamber. The mages who were still lying on the ground were caught in the intense heat, and some of them began burning to ashes. Others screamed in fear and quickly tried to flee from the area.</p>
<p>"Run!"</p>
<p>"Get away from him!"</p>
<p>Ethan ignored them completely.His eyes remained fixed on the pillar.</p>
<p>He tightened his grip around Solaris and slowly raised the greatsword above his head. The flames around the blade became even stronger as he gathered his strength into one attack.</p>
<p>The faint flame dragon behind him seemed to move with his body as Ethan brought the greatsword down with brutal strength and power.</p>
<p>BOOOOOOOOOM!</p>
<p>BOOOOOOOOOM!</p>
<p>The greatsword struck the pillar with overwhelming force. For a brief moment, everything around Ethan seemed to freeze as the power of the impact gathered at a single point.</p>
<p>A violent wave of energy exploded outward from the point of impact. The force spread through the underground chamber and destroyed everything within its path. Stone, magic formations, equipment and the remaining mana stones were caught in the blast and were instantly torn apart.</p>
<p>The heat became intense as the flames around Solaris spread through the chamber. Everything that came too close to the center of the explosion was burned away and vaporized by the sheer power of the attack.</p>
<p>BOOM!</p>
<p>Another shockwave burst outward.</p>
<p>The ground shook violently, and cracks spread across the floor and walls. The remaining pillars trembled as the entire underground structure shook from the impact. Dust and broken stone were thrown into the air while the magic formations flickered and collapsed one after another.</p>
<p>The explosion continued to spread through the underground chamber, and the force pushed through the surrounding structures. The entire Capital seemed to tremble as the shockwave traveled through the ground.</p>
<p>Above the surface, people stopped in fear as the earth suddenly shook beneath their feet.</p>
<p>The city walls trembled.</p>
<p>Buildings shook.</p>
<p>The barrier covering the Imperial Capital rippled violently as the energy from below reached it.</p>
<p>For several moments, it felt as though the entire world had been struck by the attack.</p>
<p>At the center of the destruction, Ethan remained standing with Solaris in his hands. Flames still surrounded his body while smoke and dust covered the area around him.</p>
<p>Ethan then raised his chin to look only to be shocked</p>
<p>"What the helllll?"</p>
<p>....</p>
<p>Garran stood before the Capital with a darkened face. His eyes remained fixed on the city walls as the soldiers continued bombarding the barrier with attacks. Explosions kept appearing across the surface, but despite the constant assault, the barrier still stood firmly.</p>
<p>"Just what is going on?"</p>
<p>Garran clenched his fist and stared at the barrier with growing frustration. He looked at the people around him before speaking again.</p>
<p>"We have attacked with all our might, but still it isn’t enough to shake the place."</p>
<p>He stared at the barrier for another moment and then turned toward Lancelot.</p>
<p>"Duke Lancelot, what is this?"</p>
<p>Duke Lancelot also maintained a sombre expression. Even he had been taken aback by what he was seeing. As someone who had personally overseen the construction many decades ago, he knew the barrier was formidable, but he had never expected it to be this strong.</p>
<p>Several Transcendent Knights had already struck the barrier with their attacks, yet it was not enough to put even a proper dent in it. The barrier continued to shine as it absorbed the attacks and spread the force across its entire structure.</p>
<p>Lancelot slowly narrowed his eyes.</p>
<p>"Such a magic barrier arrangement. I wonder how they come up with this, and more than that, how much they have hidden."</p>
<p>Rathlos adjusted his glasses and looked at the barrier carefully. After watching the constant bombardment for a while, he slowly nodded.</p>
<p>"It seems this battle won’t be as easy as we expected."</p>
<p>"Hmmph!" Garran snorted and stared ahead.</p>
<p>"I don’t believe that this thing can hold infinitely. In such a case, I just need to bombard until it is torn apart."</p>
<p>He had hardly finished his words when a loud banging explosion suddenly shook the entire place.</p>
<p>BOOOOOOOOOM!</p>
<p>The ground trembled beneath everyone’s feet.</p>
<p>Everyone was taken aback and paused their attacks. Garran immediately turned his head toward the Capital, while Lancelot and Rathlos also stared ahead with serious expressions.</p>
<p>Inside the barrier, a huge fiery explosion suddenly erupted. The flames rose into the sky and lit up the entire Capital as the force of the blast tore through everything around it.</p>
<p>The remnants of the blast lingered for a few moments before slowly dispersing, leaving flames ravaging across the area. A portion of the city wall had disappeared, and parts of the structure seemed to have melted under the intense heat.</p>
<p>Behind the destroyed section, several large pillars could be seen supporting the remaining structure.</p>
<p>Then everyone noticed a figure floating near the pillars.</p>
<p>The man held a greatsword in his hand, and flames still surrounded his body. The heat around him caused the air to tremble, while the light from the flames illuminated his white hair.</p>
<p>Garran’s eyes widened.</p>
<p>"That figure... Is it Ethan?"</p>
