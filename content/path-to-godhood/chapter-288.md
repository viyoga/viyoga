<p>Sniff… sniff…</p>
<p>“Wuwuwuwuuw…”</p>
<p>“Ethan, she had such a terrible life. Don’t you dare treat her badly.”</p>
<p>“Yeah… Lia’s heart is breaking just from hearing this.”</p>
<p>“…”</p>
<p>Ethan just sat there and watched his wives roll around and cry after hearing Claira’s story. Their faces were red, eyes swollen, and tears kept flowing without stopping.</p>
<p>Maybe it was the wine.Maybe it was the sadness.Or maybe it was both.</p>
<p>Sophia and Lia grabbed Claira tightly and cried like children who had lost something precious.</p>
<p>“Wuwu… Sister, you’ve had it so hard… such a bitter life…” Sophia sobbed, her shoulders shaking.</p>
<p>“Why is God so unfair?” Lia cried loudly, burying her face against Claira’s shoulder.</p>
<p>Diana also raised her head, tears streaming down her face as she clenched her fists. “I knew it. That king is a bad guy… he’s toying with such a kind soul…”</p>
<p>“I wish he was cursed for his entire life,” Diana spat angrily and then rolled on the floor, still crying.</p>
<p>“Ahh… thanks… thanks for accepting me…” Claira cried heart-wrenchingly, her voice breaking as tears fell freely.</p>
<p>Seeing all this, Ethan covered his face with his hand.</p>
<p>He was starting to feel uncomfortable.</p>
<p>And for some reason, seeing them cry made him want to cry too.</p>
<p>’Ahhh… what is this thing affecting me?’</p>
<p>His chest felt tight, and his throat felt strange.</p>
<p>“Should I stop them?” Ethan muttered quietly.</p>
<p>“Nah… let them show their emotions,” Rina said calmly from the side while taking small sips from her glass.</p>
<p>Out of all of them, she seemed to have the highest tolerance for wine. Her expression was steady, and her eyes were clear.</p>
<p>“It’s good for Claira,” Rina continued. “Emotions kept inside for too long can choke a person.”</p>
<p>Ethan turned to her and asked, “Do you know anything about her past?”</p>
<p>“Are you asking about the witch thing?” Rina asked softly.</p>
<p>“Hmmm.”</p>
<p>Rina paused for a moment. Her fingers tapped lightly on the rim of the glass as she thought.</p>
<p>“I remember hearing something from the previous Count,” she said slowly.</p>
<p>“The King had loved a witch in his younger days, when he had just risen to the throne.”</p>
<p>“Ohhh!” Ethan’s eyes widened slightly.</p>
<p>“Is it a widespread tale?” he asked.</p>
<p>“No,” Rina shook her head. “Very few people know. I don’t even know how the Count found out. Who knows… this might even be the reason for his death.”</p>
<p>She took another sip and continued, “But I know one thing. After she was born, the King didn’t have any more children, and he didn’t take any wives either.”</p>
<p>“He also seemed to age very quickly after that.”</p>
<p>Ethan frowned deeply, thinking hard. There was something he couldn’t grasp fully, but it was eating at him from inside.</p>
<p>Also,weren’t the witches infertile so how was Claira born?</p>
<p>“The King also never appointed a Queen,” Ethan muttered.</p>
<p>“Haaa…” Rina sighed softly.</p>
<p>“Why does it feel like that old guy is a menace?” Ethan said with a bitter smile.</p>
<p>“He is,” Rina replied seriously. “Don’t ever underestimate him.”</p>
<p>“I wouldn’t,” Ethan said honestly. “That’s the reason I haven’t raised the issue of ennoblement yet. I really don’t even want to meet this guy.”</p>
<p>“I might be somewhat smart, but compared to those old experienced monsters, I’m not worth much. They can eat me alive and not even leave bones behind.”</p>
<p>Hearing this, Rina smiled faintly.</p>
<p>“That’s a very good quality you have,” she said. “Not overestimating yourself, and also not underestimating your enemies.”</p>
<p>Ethan was about to reply when he suddenly paused.</p>
<p>“Wait… why is it so peaceful?”</p>
<p>Rina also frowned slightly.</p>
<p>Both of them turned around at the same time.</p>
<p>They saw the women asleep on the small sofa, hugging one another tightly like children who had cried themselves out. Their faces were still stained with dried tears, but their expressions were calm now, soft and peaceful.</p>
<p>Ethan watched them quietly for a moment and then chuckled under his breath.</p>
<p>“Girls… what should I do with you, except love you.”</p>
<p>“Hahaha…”</p>
<p>He laughed softly as he saw them roll slightly in their sleep, mumbling and clinging to each other.</p>
<p>“I think you should take Claira and accompany her to sleep,” Rina suggested in a low voice.</p>
<p>Ethan nodded. He carefully lifted Claira away from Diana,Lia and Sophia’s grasp. She stirred a little but did not wake. He carried her gently to her room and laid her down on the bed, adjusting the blanket over her.</p>
<p>He turned around and began to loosen his clothes, but suddenly a hand grabbed his sleeve.</p>
<p>“Huh! Claira!” Ethan turned back quickly.</p>
<p>Claira was half awake, her eyes unfocused and hazy. She looked clearly tipsy, her cheeks flushed red. She tugged on his clothes and smiled foolishly.</p>
<p>“Husband.”</p>
<p>“Huh! What did you say?” Ethan asked, feeling a strange itch run through him.</p>
<p>“I said…. Husband husband.. Husband.”</p>
<p>“Yes, wife.”</p>
<p>“Husband, when will you spend the wedding night with me.”</p>
<p>“Wedding night?” Ethan stiffened slightly.</p>
<p>“Do you even know what a wedding night is!”</p>
<p>“Yes Sir!” she said and even gave a strange salute.</p>
<p>“Sister Sophia had explained everything to me..”</p>
<p>Ethan’s brows twitched.</p>
<p>“She even said that it was the day when you are going to make me yours, so please mark me as yours..”</p>
<p>Ethan’s lips trembled as he heard this.</p>
<p>’Fuck..Sophia what did you teacher?’</p>
<p>There was no way she would teach something good but when it comes to leading others astray if she claims second place no one would dare to compete for first.</p>
<p>“Tsk.. You don’t know anything.”</p>
<p>He sighed and tried to gently pull her hands away.</p>
<p>“Just go to sleep… We will talk about it after you sober up.”</p>
<p>“No.. No.. No!” Claira shook her head wildly. She wrapped her delicate arms around him and pouted, tears forming in her eyes.</p>
<p>“I want to do it today.. Pretty please..”</p>
<p>“Please… Please..” she started crying loudly, her voice was completely childish and unreasonable.</p>
<p>Ethan swallows hard.”We can’t.. You don’t even know anything..”</p>
<p>“No I know everything,” she said stubbornly and moved her hands downward.</p>
<p>“Sister Sophia said that I need to awaken the sleeping dragon first and then let it roar with all its might, so brother Ethan please show me your sleeping dragon.”</p>
