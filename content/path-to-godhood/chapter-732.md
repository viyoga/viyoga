<p>While the fight was taking place at front the,black robed figure who were summoning the hell spawn frowned.</p>

<p>The moment the gap appeared in the barrier, the entire battlefield erupted into chaos.</p>

<p>"They have broken the barrier!"</p>

<p>"Stop them!"</p>

<p>"Stop them at once!"</p>

<p>Angry shouts came from every direction as the black-robed figures finally lost their calm. Their bodies immediately burst with dark energy, and dozens of powerful auras rose at the same time.</p>

<p>Several of them ignored the demonic spawns and rushed straight toward Queen Ophelia, clearly intending to close the gap before the Emperors could enter. Their black robes fluttered violently as they moved, and waves of dark energy gathered around their weapons while they charged forward without caring about anything else.</p>

<p>The four Emperors reacted even faster.</p>

<p>"Clear the way!"</p>

<p>Emperor Ezekiel roared as his aura exploded outward.</p>

<p>Boom!</p>

<p>The ground beneath his feet shattered while he swung his greatsword with both hands. A massive sword arc swept across the battlefield and forced several black-robed figures backward. Emperor Aldric followed closely behind him while Emperor Caelus and the other Emperor attacked from both sides. Their accompanying Transcendent Knights also charged forward without hesitation, and the two groups violently collided in front of the barrier.</p>

<p>BOOOOM!</p>

<p>The first collision sent a powerful shockwave across the entire battlefield. Weapons clashed against one another while dark spells crashed against sword aura.</p>

<p>Black flames spread across the ground as several knights raised their shields and pushed directly through them. One black-robed figure appeared above Emperor Aldric and thrust a spear toward his head, but Aldric turned his body and blocked it with his sword. Sparks exploded between the two weapons before he twisted his wrist and sent the attacker flying with a heavy kick. Another group tried to rush toward Queen Ophelia from the side, but several Transcendent Knights immediately intercepted them.</p>

<p>"Don’t let them reach the barrier!"</p>

<p>Boom!</p>

<p>Boom!</p>

<p>Boom!</p>

<p>The entire area became filled with violent clashes. Black energy and brilliant sword light repeatedly crossed through the sky while the demonic spawns continued attacking from every direction.</p>

<p>The ground beneath the battlefield could no longer withstand the pressure and began collapsing piece by piece. Every few moments, another violent shockwave swept through the surroundings as the black-robed figures desperately tried to reach the opening while the Allied experts forced them back.</p>

<p>Amidst the fierce battle, several powerful auras suddenly approached from the distance.</p>

<p>The black-robed figures immediately turned their heads.</p>

<p>Several streaks of light crossed the ruined mountains before Ethan, Heliana, and the injured Transcendent Knights arrived above the battlefield. Although several of the knights were still wounded, the pressure coming from the group was enough to attract everyone’s attention.</p>

<p>Their appearance immediately caused quite a stir.</p>

<p>The expressions of several black-robed figures became distorted.</p>

<p>"Damn!"</p>

<p>One of them stared at Ethan with disbelief.</p>

<p>"What happened to the Crown Prince?"</p>

<p>Another black-robed figure immediately understood what Ethan’s arrival meant, and his face darkened.</p>

<p>"Is he dead?"</p>

<p>"He can’t even keep them busy."</p>

<p>"Useless."</p>

<p>The surrounding black-robed figures became even more anxious. Haster had been given one simple task. He only needed to delay Ethan and the others long enough for them to finish their preparations, yet Ethan had already returned with Heliana and several of the Transcendent Knights. Whether Haster was dead or still barely alive no longer mattered. It was obvious that he had failed.</p>

<p>On the opposite side, the four Emperors showed completely different reactions.</p>

<p>Emperor Ezekiel looked toward Ethan before giving a satisfied nod.</p>

<p>"Good."</p>

<p>"You came at the right time."</p>

<p>Emperor Caelus immediately looked toward the Transcendent Knights around him.</p>

<p>"Keep these black-clothed individuals at bay."</p>

<p>"Do not allow anyone to approach the opening."</p>

<p>The knights immediately answered.</p>

<p>"Yes, Your Majesty!"</p>

<p>The four Emperors wasted no more time. Queen Ophelia maintained the bypass while the others quickly moved toward the gap. Ethan and Heliana followed closely behind them while the remaining experts stayed outside to hold back the black-robed figures and demonic spawns.</p>

<p>"Stop them!"</p>

<p>One of the black-robed figures screamed after seeing their movements.</p>

<p>Several immediately tried to break away from the battle, but the Allied Transcendent Knights blocked them without hesitation.</p>

<p>Clang!</p>

<p>"Your opponents are us."</p>

<p>The knights attacked together and forced the black-robed figures backward. Powerful attacks exploded across the battlefield once again while the Emperors used that brief opening to rush toward the barrier.</p>

<p>The four Emperors passed through the narrow gap one after another.</p>

<p>Ethan and Heliana followed closely behind them.</p>

<p>The moment Ethan crossed the barrier, his expression changed.</p>

<p>The scene on the other side was completely different from what he had expected.</p>

<p>An enormous open space stretched before them. The ground had been covered with countless complicated channels and strange patterns that all led toward the center. At the very middle stood a huge oval-shaped structure that looked almost like a gigantic egg. Its surface was dark red and slightly transparent while countless vein-like lines covered it from top to bottom. Those veins continuously expanded and contracted while the entire structure slowly pulsed as though it were alive.</p>

<p>Thump!</p>

<p>Thump!</p>

<p>Thump!</p>

<p>Each pulse sent a strange vibration through the surrounding ground.</p>

<p>Countless streams of blood-red energy flowed toward the enormous egg from every direction. Ethan narrowed his eyes as he carefully sensed the energy and immediately realized that it was not ordinary mana.</p>

<p>It was the vital life force.</p>

<p>A massive amount of it.</p>

<p>The life force flowed through countless glowing channels carved deep into the ground before gathering beneath the enormous structure. Every few seconds, another powerful wave surged upward and entered the egg. The dark red veins covering its surface immediately brightened before carrying that energy deeper inside.</p>

<p>The amount was frightening.</p>

<p>Ethan could feel countless different traces within that energy.</p>

<p>Humans.</p>

<p>Beasts.</p>

<p>Other living creatures.</p>

<p>It felt as though the vitality of countless lives had been gathered from across the land and brought to this single place. The endless streams of life force continuously flowed into the enormous egg without stopping, making the entire structure pulse stronger with every passing moment.</p>

<p>Queen Ophelia’s beautiful face immediately became cold.</p>

<p>"So this is where all that life force went..."</p>

<p>The four Emperors stared at the enormous structure with grim expressions. Even with their experience, the sight before them was enough to make their hearts heavy. Whatever had been happening here had clearly been prepared for a very long time.</p>

<p>Suddenly, Emperor Caelus noticed something within the dense blood-red energy.</p>

<p>His eyes narrowed sharply.</p>

<p>"There he is!"</p>

<p>He immediately pointed toward the center.</p>

<p>Everyone followed his gaze.</p>

<p>Behind the thick layers of blood-red energy, they could barely make out a small silhouette sitting inside the enormous egg-like structure. The figure sat completely still with its legs crossed while its hands rested quietly upon its knees. Streams of vital life force continuously entered its body from every direction, and with every breath, more energy disappeared into the figure.</p>

<p>The surrounding egg pulsed along with it as though the two had become completely connected.</p>

<p>With every pulse, the aura around the silhouette seemed to grow slightly stronger.</p>

<p>The group fell silent as they stared at the figure hidden within the enormous structure.</p>

<p>Whatever was sitting inside that enormous egg was absorbing the massive amount of vital life force gathered from countless living beings.</p>
