<p>The demon girl refused to accept defeat.</p>
<p>With a furious roar, she forcefully rotated the remaining mana inside her body. Dark energy surged through her veins as cracks of black lightning spread across her skin. Gritting her teeth, she raised her blade and slammed it violently against the pillar of light surrounding her.</p>
<p>BOOM! BOOM! BOOM!</p>
<p>Each strike carried terrifying power. The ground beneath her feet shattered repeatedly while shockwaves rippled outward from the sealed space. The light pillars trembled under the assault, and fragments of energy scattered into the air.</p>
<p>Again and again she attacked.</p>
<p>However, after several strikes, her expression slowly changed.</p>
<p>The strength behind her attacks was weakening.Her breathing grew heavy at the sudden realization.</p>
<p>The demonic aura that once burned fiercely began fading little by little as if something was draining it away.</p>
<p>“I am getting weaker… Noooo!” she roared angrily.</p>
<p>Panic finally appeared in her eyes.</p>
<p>Without hesitation, she pulled out a scroll hidden beneath her armor and tore it open.</p>
<p>Instantly, spatial disturbance erupted.</p>
<p>The air twisted violently as a massive foreign intent descended upon the battlefield. Space cracked like fragile glass, and a terrifying summoning force pushed against Ethan’s sealing formation.</p>
<p>A shadowy figure began forming above her, roaring as it forced its way into reality.</p>
<p>Ethan’s eyes narrowed dangerously.</p>
<p>He immediately understood.</p>
<p>Reinforcement from a higher demon or a magical summon.</p>
<p>But how could he allow this escape?</p>
<p>Ethan raised his hand calmly and twisted his fingers, forming several ancient symbols in rapid succession.</p>
<p>BOOOOOOOM!</p>
<p>The Holy Sword of Judgment erupted.</p>
<p>A colossal pillar of divine light burst upward from the ground, expanding toward the sky like a descending beam of heaven itself. Golden radiance flooded the battlefield, washing away blood mist and darkness alike.</p>
<p>Runes of holy judgment rotated endlessly around the space as chains of light wrapped around the forming summon.</p>
<p>The half-materialized demonic entity struggled violently inside the brilliance, roaring with fury as holy flames burned across its body.</p>
<p>“Measly human! You cannot stop me with these petty tricks!”</p>
<p>Its voice shook the battlefield.</p>
<p>Ethan snorted coldly.</p>
<p>“You really think so?”</p>
<p>The next moment, his aura changed completely.</p>
<p>Flames erupted around his body as draconic scales spread across his skin. Crimson patterns ignited along his arms and shoulders while immense pressure burst outward.</p>
<p>Draconic Body activated.</p>
<p>Then—</p>
<p>BOOOOM!</p>
<p>Battle Frenzy erupted.</p>
<p>The mana inside Ethan churned wildly like a raging storm. The ground beneath him cracked while the surrounding air distorted under overwhelming force.</p>
<p>Even distant soldiers felt their hearts tremble.</p>
<p>Ethan slowly raised both hands again.</p>
<p>SWIIISH!</p>
<p>Darkness suddenly spread across the battlefield.</p>
<p>A bone chilling silence followed as light itself seemed swallowed. The temperature dropped instantly, and every sound faded as though the world had been muted.</p>
<p>Humans and demons alike froze.</p>
<p>It felt as if death itself had opened its eyes.</p>
<p>The darkness gathered behind Ethan, slowly condensing into a formless mass. Shadows twisted together and compressed until they began shaping into something solid.</p>
<p>A blade.</p>
<p>A massive dark sword formed behind him, floating silently in the air.</p>
<p>Its surface absorbed all light, and faint crimson lines pulsed across it like a heartbeat. Space warped slightly around its edge as though unable to endure its existence.</p>
<p>The moment the sword completed its formation—</p>
<p>Both the summoned demon and the assassin felt overwhelming dread.</p>
<p>A feeling they had never experienced before.</p>
<p>Death.</p>
<p>True death.</p>
<p>The summon roared desperately.</p>
<p>“HUMAN—!”</p>
<p>“Shut up!” Ethan cut it off coldly.</p>
<p>Without hesitation, Ethan swung his hand forward.</p>
<p>The dark sword shot out instantly, tearing through the air without making a sound. Space distorted along its path as if reality itself was being forced apart.</p>
<p>The summoned creature let out a furious roar the moment it sensed the incoming attack.</p>
<p>“You want to tear me with my power… Dream on!”</p>
<p>Darkness exploded from its massive body. Endless waves of black energy surged outward like a collapsing storm, spreading across the battlefield with terrifying pressure. The power of darkness gathered rapidly and expanded into a destructive tide meant to annihilate everything in its path.</p>
<p>Mountains could have been erased under such force.</p>
<p>The air cracked.</p>
<p>The ground beneath Frostvele trembled violently as the attack rushed forward to swallow Ethan’s blade completely.</p>
<p>For a brief moment, it looked as if the darkness had succeeded.</p>
<p>Then—</p>
<p>The summoned entity froze.</p>
<p>Its burning eyes widened in disbelief.</p>
<p>The dark blade moved forward calmly through the storm of destruction.</p>
<p>Its attack, capable of annihilating mountains, was cut apart like butter meeting a heated knife. Layers of condensed darkness split cleanly into two halves as the sword continued advancing without slowing even slightly.</p>
<p>Shock replaced arrogance.</p>
<p>The creature suddenly understood something horrifying.</p>
<p>“CHAOOOOSSS!”</p>
<p>It screamed in panic.</p>
<p>But it was already too late.</p>
<p>The blade pierced straight through the summoned entity and the sealed demon girl together.</p>
<p>Reality itself seemed sliced apart.</p>
<p>For a fraction of a second, everything became completely silent.</p>
<p>Then the world exploded.</p>
<p>BOOOOOOOOOOM!</p>
<p>A delayed shockwave erupted like the detonation of an atomic bomb. Blinding light burst outward as holy energy and absolute darkness collided violently before collapsing inward again.</p>
<p>A massive mushroom-shaped cloud rose into the sky.</p>
<p>The ground vanished.</p>
<p>A huge portion of the forest ahead of Frostvele was instantly wiped away. Snow-covered mountain peaks shattered and disappeared as if erased by an invisible hand. Trees, rocks, and frozen earth disintegrated into dust under overwhelming force.</p>
<p>The shockwave swept across the battlefield, throwing demons and human soldiers alike off their feet. Walls trembled violently while snow turned into steaming vapor under extreme heat.</p>
<p>Wind howled like a dying beast.</p>
<p>The summoned figure screamed in agony as opposing energies tore its body apart piece by piece. Holy radiance burned its existence while the abyssal darkness devoured whatever remained.</p>
<p>Its roar slowly faded into nothingness.</p>
<p>Below it, the demon girl’s eyes widened in pure terror as the darkness consumed her final resistance. Her body trembled helplessly within the sealing formation as cracks of light spread across her figure.</p>
<p>The seal flared brighter.</p>
<p>CRACK!</p>
<p>The spatial disturbance shattered completely like broken glass.</p>
<p>Light exploded outward once more before collapsing into silence.</p>
<p>The violent winds slowly weakened.</p>
<p>Smoke drifted across the ruined land while fragments of ash and snow began falling gently from the sky.</p>
<p>What once stood as forest and mountain had become a massive empty scar stretching far into the distance.</p>
<p>At the center of the destroyed battlefield stood Ethan looked ahead.</p>
<p>His breathing remained steady while his aura gradually calmed. Fading fragments of darkness disappeared behind him like dissolving shadows.</p>
<p>Ethan was about to sigh in relief but his eyes popped out looking down.</p>
<p>“The fuck is going on here?”</p>
