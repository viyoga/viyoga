<p>A few moments later, the cracked wall of ice slowly melted away.</p>

<p>Soft streams of cold mist drifted across the ruined battlefield before gradually disappearing into the air.</p>

<p>Heliana slowly walked out as though nothing had happened.Her appearance was once again flawless.</p>

<p>Not a single crack could be seen on her body anymore. Her silver-white dress looked neat, and her long purple hair gently swayed behind her. Her calm face remained as expressionless as ever while her crystal staff rested quietly in her hand.</p>

<p>The surrounding Transcendent Knights coughed awkwardly before quickly looking away.</p>

<p>A few of them quietly cleared their throats while pretending nothing had happened.</p>

<p>Ethan, however, did not look away.</p>

<p>His eyes remained fixed on Heliana.</p>

<p>His expression gradually became cautious.</p>

<p>’Why did she have cracks all over her body?’</p>

<p>’Is she even a human?’</p>

<p>The question continued circling through his mind.</p>

<p>Those cracks were unlike any injury he had ever seen. They did not look like wounds caused by an explosion. Instead, they looked as though her entire body had been breaking apart from within before somehow restoring itself.</p>

<p>The more he thought about it, the more suspicious it became.</p>

<p>’Could she be someone’s puppet?’</p>

<p>’Or is there something else behind this?’</p>

<p>Ethan quietly observed her while keeping those thoughts to himself.</p>

<p>The secret behind Heliana had suddenly become very interesting.</p>

<p>If she truly was some kind of puppet...Perhaps he could study the method of refining one.</p>

<p>If he could refine a puppet like that and leave it behind at home, many of his worries would become much lighter. At the very least, someone powerful would always remain behind to protect the people important to him.</p>

<p>He quickly pushed those thoughts aside.</p>

<p>This was not the time to think about such things.</p>

<p>Heliana calmly stepped toward the injured Transcendent Knights.</p>

<p>Her eyes slowly swept over everyone’s condition.</p>

<p>Several of them could barely stand while others still leaned on broken rocks to support themselves. Even after Ethan’s healing, their injuries remained serious.</p>

<p>She remained silent for a brief moment.</p>

<p>Finally, she spoke in her usual calm but stern voice.</p>

<p>"We don’t have time to relax."</p>

<p>"We need to go to the battlefield as quickly as possible."</p>

<p>The moment she finished speaking, her body slowly floated into the air.</p>

<p>Cold energy quietly gathered beneath her feet while her clothes gently fluttered in the wind.</p>

<p>The remaining Transcendent Knights looked at one another before silently nodding.</p>

<p>No one complained.</p>

<p>No one questioned the order.</p>

<p>They all understood that the battle ahead was far more important than their injuries.</p>

<p>Ethan also gave a small nod.</p>

<p>Without another word, he pushed off the ground and rose into the sky.</p>

<p>The others quickly followed behind.</p>

<p>Several streaks of light shot across the ruined wasteland as the group flew toward the distant battlefield where the Emperors were still fighting, leaving the destroyed valley and the ashes of Haster behind them.</p>

<p>Several streaks of light flew across the ruined sky before gradually slowing down.</p>

<p>Ethan, Heliana, and the remaining Transcendent Knights soon arrived near the main battlefield.</p>

<p>The moment they saw the scene below, everyone stopped in midair.</p>

<p>The entire battlefield had become complete chaos.</p>

<p>Violent explosions continuously echoed across the mountains while terrifying waves of spiritual energy shook the surrounding land. The ground had already collapsed in countless places, and enormous cracks stretched across the battlefield. Thick black miasma mixed with dust and smoke, making the sky appear dark and gloomy.</p>

<p>At the center of the battlefield, the four Emperors were fighting with everything they had.</p>

<p>Boom!</p>

<p>Boom!</p>

<p>Boom!</p>

<p>Their attacks continuously shook the heavens as sword light, aura, and powerful martial techniques exploded in every direction.</p>

<p>But what truly caught Ethan’s attention was not the Emperors.</p>

<p>It was their opponents.</p>

<p>Several gigantic creatures surrounded them from every direction.</p>

<p>Each one gave off an overwhelming evil aura that made even the surrounding space seem darker. Their bodies were twisted beyond recognition. Some had several arms while others possessed enormous horns, burning eyes, and long tails covered with black scales.</p>

<p>One creature had a wolf-like head with six arms, while another had a body covered with countless moving eyes. Black flames continuously burned around them while thick miasma poured from every part of their bodies.</p>

<p>The moment they moved, the surrounding air became heavy.</p>

<p>Even the ground beneath them slowly blackened.</p>

<p>Ethan frowned.</p>

<p>His eyes remained fixed on those horrifying creatures.</p>

<p>"What are those?"</p>

<p>Heliana quietly looked toward the battlefield.</p>

<p>For the first time, Ethan noticed a faint trace of fear within her usually calm eyes.</p>

<p>Her face became noticeably pale.</p>

<p>She slowly answered.</p>

<p>"They are the Spawns of the Devil."</p>

<p>"They come from Hell.A place ruled by the Demon Gods."</p>

<p>Her voice remained calm, but there was a heaviness in it that everyone could clearly hear.</p>

<p>"These creatures are not true Demon Gods."</p>

<p>"They are only their servants."</p>

<p>"But even as servants..."</p>

<p>"They are terrifying."</p>

<p>Ethan narrowed his eyes.</p>

<p>Even Heliana was showing such a reaction.</p>

<p>That alone proved how dangerous those creatures truly were.</p>

<p>Below them, the battle became even fiercer.</p>

<p>Boom!</p>

<p>Emperor Ezekiel swung his massive sword with both hands.</p>

<p>A gigantic sword aura split one of the demonic spawns in half.</p>

<p>The creature let out a horrifying scream before its body dissolved into black smoke.</p>

<p>Before anyone could breathe in relief, another demonic spawn immediately rushed forward from the side.</p>

<p>Its enormous claws tore through the air toward Queen Ophelia.</p>

<p>Emperor Caelus instantly appeared before her.</p>

<p>Clang!</p>

<p>His greatsword blocked the attack head-on.</p>

<p>The violent collision shook the surrounding mountains while a powerful shockwave spread in every direction.</p>

<p>At the same time, Emperor Aldric and Emperor Caelus continuously fought back the remaining demonic spawns. Their attacks never stopped for even a moment. Every time one creature got close to Queen Ophelia, one of the Emperors immediately intercepted it. They fought while constantly protecting her, refusing to let a single enemy reach her position.</p>

<p>Meanwhile,Queen Ophelia stood before a gigantic barrier covered with countless glowing runes and overlapping magic circles.</p>

<p>Her beautiful eyes moved rapidly across the complicated patterns.</p>

<p>Several small magic circles continuously appeared around her hands as she carefully examined every rune one by one.</p>

<p>Her fingers occasionally touched the glowing symbols before quickly moving to another part of the formation.</p>

<p>She was completely focused.</p>

<p>It was as though she could no longer hear the battle happening around her.</p>

<p>Boom!</p>
