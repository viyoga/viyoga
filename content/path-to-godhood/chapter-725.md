<p>The radiant giant continued rising while its holy light spread farther across the battlefield. Wave after wave of brilliant energy rolled outward, forcing the surrounding darkness to retreat.</p>
<p>Wherever the holy light reached, the poisonous miasma rapidly dissolved as though it could no longer exist beneath such overwhelming purity.</p>
<p>The mountains trembled continuously while the valley became divided into two worlds. One side was swallowed by endless darkness, while the other was illuminated by blinding holy light.</p>
<p>The enormous black hand pressing down on the radiant giant gradually reached its limit.</p>
<p>Crack!</p>
<p>Crack!</p>
<p>Crack!</p>
<p>White cracks rapidly spread across its entire surface. Thick streams of dark miasma leaked out through the widening fractures while the holy light continued growing stronger. The giant hand trembled violently before finally reaching its breaking point.</p>
<p>Boom!</p>
<p>The massive hand exploded into countless pieces of dark miasma.</p>
<p>The fragments scattered across the battlefield, but before they could even touch the ground, the surrounding holy light purified them one after another until nothing remained. A deep rumble echoed throughout the valley as the towering giant of darkness slowly took a heavy step backward. Even its enormous body seemed slightly shaken by the sudden burst of holy power.</p>
<p>At the same time, the dazzling pillar of light surrounding Ethan gradually began fading.</p>
<p>Everyone fixed their eyes on the brilliant figure emerging from within it.</p>
<p>As the light slowly dispersed, an enormous Holy Knight was revealed standing silently behind Ethan.</p>
<p>The gigantic figure towered over the battlefield like an ancient guardian that had slept for countless ages before finally awakening. Its body was covered from head to toe in magnificent silver armor decorated with flowing golden patterns.</p>
<p>Every piece of armor radiated a sacred aura while streams of pure light flowed continuously across its surface. A long white cloak gently swayed behind it despite the violent winds created by the battle.</p>
<p>Its face remained completely hidden beneath a closed helmet.</p>
<p>Only two brilliant golden lights shone quietly from within the visor.</p>
<p>Those glowing eyes calmly looked across the battlefield without showing the slightest emotion, yet everyone who met that gaze felt an indescribable pressure settle upon their hearts.</p>
<p>The Holy Knight remained motionless for several moments.Then it slowly reached toward its waist.Its enormous armored hand firmly grasped the hilt of a gigantic sword hanging beside it.</p>
<p>Shing!</p>
<p>The clear sound of steel leaving its scabbard echoed across the entire valley.</p>
<p>The sword was slowly drawn inch by inch until its dazzling blade was fully revealed. Brilliant holy light instantly burst from the weapon while countless ancient golden runes appeared across its surface.</p>
<p>The runes glowed one after another as streams of radiant energy flowed along the blade. The moment the sword was completely unsheathed, a powerful wave of holy energy swept across the battlefield like a tidal wave.</p>
<p>The surrounding miasma retreated even farther.</p>
<p>Many of the monstrous humans instinctively stepped backward.</p>
<p>Even those twisted creatures seemed to fear the terrifying holy power radiating from the giant knight.</p>
<p>The surrounding Transcendent Knights stared upward with expressions filled with disbelief.</p>
<p>"That..."</p>
<p>One knight slowly whispered while tightening his grip on his weapon.</p>
<p>"Is that his Knight’s Will?"</p>
<p>Another veteran knight quietly shook his head.</p>
<p>His eyes never left the towering Holy Knight.</p>
<p>"No..."</p>
<p>"It feels far beyond an ordinary Knight’s Will."</p>
<p>Several others silently nodded.</p>
<p>None of them had ever witnessed such a scene before.</p>
<p>Heliana quietly watched the giant Holy Knight.</p>
<p>The reflection of its brilliant light could be seen within her purple eyes.</p>
<p>A faint smile slowly appeared on her beautiful face.</p>
<p>"He really did it..."</p>
<p>Meanwhile, the giant of darkness standing behind Haster also began changing.</p>
<p>Its enormous featureless head suddenly twisted.</p>
<p>The thick black miasma swirling around it rapidly gathered together while gigantic black bones slowly formed beneath the darkness. The giant’s face became more horrifying with every passing moment.</p>
<p>An enormous skull emerged from the darkness while empty eye sockets slowly ignited with burning crimson flames. Rows of jagged black teeth appeared beneath the skull, and long curved horns slowly extended upward from both sides of its head. Black mist continuously poured from every gap in its massive skeletal body, making it appear like an ancient monster that had crawled out from the deepest abyss.</p>
<p>The horrifying transformation caused many soldiers to instinctively step backward.</p>
<p>The pressure released by the skeletal giant became even heavier than before.</p>
<p>The moment its transformation was complete, the giant skull opened its mouth.</p>
<p>ROAR!</p>
<p>A terrifying roar exploded across the battlefield.</p>
<p>The sound shook the entire mountain range while loose rocks tumbled down the cliffs. The thick miasma churned violently as though responding to its master’s anger.</p>
<p>Standing opposite it, the Holy Knight remained completely calm.</p>
<p>Without showing the slightest hesitation, it slowly raised its radiant sword with both hands.</p>
<p>The massive blade pointed directly toward the towering skeletal giant.</p>
<p>Neither giant moved.</p>
<p>One stood wrapped in endless darkness.</p>
<p>The other stood bathed in endless holy light.</p>
<p>The two towering figures silently faced one another while the battlefield below fell into complete silence. Every soldier, knight, and Transcendent expert instinctively held their breath. Even the ongoing battles around them gradually slowed as everyone’s attention became fixed on the impossible sight unfolding before their eyes.</p>
<p>Then Haster slowly lifted one hand.</p>
<p>His countless eyes burned with hatred as he stared toward Ethan and the Holy Knight.</p>
<p>"Kill it!"</p>
<p>The giant skeletal monster immediately obeyed.</p>
<p>It let out another deafening roar before raising its enormous fist high into the air. Endless darkness gathered around the massive arm as it swung downward with enough force to crush an entire mountain.</p>
<p>The Holy Knight calmly stepped forward.</p>
<p>Its movements were steady and without the slightest trace of panic.</p>
<p>The gigantic sword slowly rose before its body before being swung forward with overwhelming force.</p>
<p>Then...</p>
<p>Boom!</p>
<p>The radiant blade collided head-on with the giant skeletal fist.</p>
<p>The moment the two attacks met, a blinding explosion erupted across the battlefield. Holy light and endless darkness violently collided, sending enormous shockwaves in every direction.</p>
<p>The mountains trembled beneath the overwhelming impact while the sky itself seemed to split apart. Countless waves of light and dark energy spread across the valley as the battle between the two colossal figures finally began.</p>
