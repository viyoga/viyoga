<p>Rayon followed immediately.</p>
<p>Unlike Damor’s heavy and dominating approach, Rayon moved like a shadow slipping through the cracks. His rapier flickered in his hand as he lunged forward, his body leaning low and fast.</p>
<p>The thin blade pierced the air with sharp whistles, aiming straight for Ethan’s vital points. His movements were precise and vicious, every thrust calculated to kill with the least effort possible. His eyes were locked on Ethan’s chest and throat, searching for a single opening.</p>
<p>The two attacks arrived almost at the same time.</p>
<p>Damor’s sword came crashing down from above like a guillotine, while Rayon’s rapier shot in from the side like a poisonous fang seeking flesh.</p>
<p>CLANG!</p>
<p>A violent metallic clash echoed across the battlefield as Ethan raised his arm and deflected the sword’s edge with sheer force. Sparks burst outward as steel met hardened scales. At the same time, he twisted his body slightly, letting the rapier graze past his side and miss his heart by a narrow margin. The wind from the thrust brushed his clothes, but the blade failed to pierce him.</p>
<p>Rayon’s eyes widened.”Howw?”</p>
<p>The sound escaped his throat in pure shock as disbelief washed over his face. His mind raced as he tried to understand how someone could deflect such attacks with bare arms.</p>
<p>“Look!” Damor shouted sharply, drawing his attention.</p>
<p>Rayon’s gaze snapped back to Ethan, and only then did he truly see it. Ethan’s arms were covered in Draconic scales, reddish and dense, layered tightly like natural armor. The scales reflected faint light, and a low pressure radiated from them.</p>
<p>The revelation shocked both of them, but it did not stop their assault.</p>
<p>Rayon twisted his wrist and thrust again, faster this time. The rapier stabbed forward in rapid succession, each strike sharp and deadly, aiming for gaps, joints, and the throat. His feet danced lightly across the ground as he circled Ethan, attacking without pause. He moved in and out, retreating and advancing in flashes, refusing to give Ethan time to counter.</p>
<p>Damor did not give Ethan space to breathe either.</p>
<p>He stepped in hard and swung his sword horizontally. The wide arc forced the air to explode outward, pushing everything back. The blade was heavy and domineering, meant to overwhelm through sheer strength rather than finesse. His muscles bulged as he roared, pouring more power into the swing.</p>
<p>BOOOM!</p>
<p>The sword strike smashed into the ground when Ethan shifted aside at the last moment. The impact split the earth open, sending rocks and debris flying in all directions. Dust rose violently into the air, clouding vision and shaking the battlefield.</p>
<p>Rayon used that moment.</p>
<p>He burst out of the dust like a phantom and stabbed straight toward Ethan’s neck, his rapier moving in a straight, lethal line.</p>
<p>SWISH!</p>
<p>Ethan tilted his head at the last second. The rapier sliced past his skin, drawing a thin line of blood along his neck. A single drop slid down slowly. Rayon’s eyes lit up with savage delight as he sensed success, and he twisted his wrist immediately to follow up with another thrust.</p>
<p>Before he could complete the motion, Damor roared.</p>
<p>“DIE!”</p>
<p>His sword descended like a falling mountain, shadow swallowing everything beneath it. The weight of the strike pressed down with terrifying force.</p>
<p>Ethan raised his palm and caught the flat of the blade. The impact released a deafening boom, and the ground beneath them shattered further, cracks spreading outward in every direction. Damor’s arms shook violently as resistance far beyond his expectation surged back at him, numbing his hands.</p>
<p>“What?” Damor growled, his teeth clenched as disbelief and anger twisted his face.</p>
<p>Rayon seized the opening without hesitation and stabbed toward Ethan’s ribs, his movements sharp and desperate.</p>
<p>Ethan turned his wrist and punched sideways.</p>
<p>BANG!</p>
<p>The punch smashed into the rapier, bending its path and sending a violent shock through Rayon’s arm. Pain shot up to his shoulder, and he hissed, his face twisting. Still, he did not retreat. Gritting his teeth, he stepped in close, forcing himself forward as his rapier stabbed upward in a brutal and desperate thrust aimed at Ethan’s chest.</p>
<p>Damor also pushed forward at the same time, forcing his sword down with both hands, trying to crush Ethan through raw strength alone. Veins bulged on his arms as he roared, refusing to back down.</p>
<p>The three clashed at close range.</p>
<p>The clash rang again and again as sword and rapier attacked from different angles. Damor pressed with overwhelming power, each swing meant to break bones and shatter defenses. Rayon attacked with speed and precision, striking like a snake, retreating and advancing in flashes, never staying still for long.</p>
<p>The battlefield trembled under the combined pressure.</p>
<p>Trees nearby cracked and collapsed from stray shockwaves. The ground was carved with deep gashes from Damor’s sword strikes and pierced with countless thin holes from Rayon’s rapier. Dust and debris filled the air, and the sound of steel rang endlessly.</p>
<p>Sweat poured down Damor’s face as he roared and swung again, his breathing growing heavier.</p>
<p>Rayon’s breathing became ragged, his chest rising and falling sharply, but his eyes were wild as he stabbed relentlessly, refusing to slow down.</p>
<p>Yet through it all, Ethan stood firm.</p>
<p>Each time the sword descended, he blocked or redirected it. Each time the rapier struck, he dodged or deflected it by the smallest margin. His movements were calm, controlled, and terrifyingly efficient, as if he was reading their attacks before they even happened.</p>
<p>Damor and Rayon exchanged a brief glance.</p>
<p>This was wrong.</p>
<p>They were attacking together, yet it felt like they were the ones being hunted.</p>
<p>“AHhhhh!”</p>
<p>“Just die you bastard…”Rayon roared moving crazily.</p>
<p>Crimson Tyrant Abyssal Fall.</p>
<p>Heaven-Shattering Blood Ruin.</p>
<p>Void Rending Dragon Crush.</p>
<p>Scarlet Sovereign Descent.</p>
<p>One after another, countless attacks were unleashed within a short span of time. Blades of energy, crushing waves of power, and distorted ripples of force slammed toward Ethan from every direction.</p>
