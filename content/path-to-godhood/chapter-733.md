<p>The four Emperors stared at the enormous blood-red oval structure with grim expressions.</p>

<p>None of them needed to say anything. Whatever the person inside was trying to accomplish had to be stopped before the process was completed. The massive amount of vital life force still continued to pour into the structure from every direction, and with each passing second, the aura of the figure sitting inside became stronger.</p>

<p>"Attack!"</p>

<p>Emperor Ezekiel was the first to move.</p>

<p>BOOM!</p>

<p>His feet smashed against the ground as he shot forward like a cannonball. Both of his hands tightly gripped his enormous greatsword while a terrifying amount of aura gathered around the blade. The sword began trembling under the power before Ezekiel swung it down with all his strength.</p>

<p>"BREAK!"</p>

<p>SWISH!</p>

<p>A massive sword arc tore through the air and struck the blood-red structure head-on.</p>

<p>BOOOOOOM!</p>

<p>The entire underground space shook violently. The ground around the oval structure cracked apart while countless pieces of stone flew through the air. The blood-red surface sank inward under the terrifying force, but instead of breaking, countless veins across it suddenly lit up. A massive amount of vital life force surged through those veins and gathered at the point of impact.</p>

<p>The sword arc slowly disappeared.</p>

<p>Everyone’s expression changed.</p>

<p>The blood-red structure was still standing.</p>

<p>"What?"</p>

<p>Emperor Caelus narrowed his eyes before stepping forward.</p>

<p>"Again!"</p>

<p>The other Emperors immediately attacked with him.</p>

<p>BOOM!</p>

<p>BOOM!</p>

<p>BOOM!</p>

<p>Several terrifying attacks crossed the space at almost the same moment. Huge sword arcs, compressed waves of aura, and powerful spells crashed into the blood-red structure from different directions. The resulting shockwaves tore apart the surrounding ground while the entire chamber shook as if it were about to collapse.</p>

<p>Yet the blood-red oval continued standing.</p>

<p>Its surface twisted and trembled under the attacks, but every time a crack appeared, the endless vital life force flowing through the ground immediately rushed into it. The damaged section repaired itself before their eyes.</p>

<p>Ethan’s expression became serious.</p>

<p>"It’s using the gathered life force to repair itself."</p>

<p>Queen Ophelia had already noticed the same thing.</p>

<p>Her eyes moved across the glowing channels on the ground before returning to the enormous structure. Her expression suddenly changed when she sensed the energy inside growing stronger despite their attacks.</p>

<p>"Stop holding back."</p>

<p>Her voice immediately caught everyone’s attention.</p>

<p>Queen Ophelia tightened her grip around her crystal staff and shouted.</p>

<p>"Everyone pour everything at once."</p>

<p>The four Emperors immediately understood her intention.</p>

<p>If they continued attacking separately, the structure would simply keep repairing itself.</p>

<p>They needed to destroy it in a single strike.</p>

<p>Emperor Ezekiel stepped forward and lifted his greatsword above his head. His entire aura erupted without restraint, and the pressure alone caused the broken ground beneath him to sink. Emperor Caelus raised his weapon while an enormous amount of power gathered around his body. Emperor Aldric also stopped holding back as his aura spread outward and caused the air around him to shake.</p>

<p>Queen Ophelia lifted her crystal staff.</p>

<p>Countless magic circles appeared around her.</p>

<p>One circle formed.</p>

<p>Then another.</p>

<p>Within moments, hundreds of glowing magic circles covered the space above them.</p>

<p>"Everyone else, attack as well!"</p>

<p>The Transcendent Knights and court mages immediately reacted.</p>

<p>Ethan tightened his grip around his sword.</p>

<p>His Law of Light surged through his body before gathering along the blade. The sword rapidly became brighter until it looked as though he was holding a piece of the sun in his hand.</p>

<p>Heliana raised her staff beside him.</p>

<p>A freezing aura spread outward while countless pieces of ice formed in the air around her.</p>

<p>The entire chamber began trembling.</p>

<p>The amount of power gathering in one place was terrifying.</p>

<p>Even the blood-red oval structure seemed to sense the danger.</p>

<p>THUMP!</p>

<p>Its pulse suddenly became faster.</p>

<p>THUMP!</p>

<p>THUMP!</p>

<p>THUMP!</p>

<p>The countless veins covering its surface shone brightly as enormous amounts of vital life force rushed toward it. Layer after layer of blood-red energy gathered around the structure and formed a thick protective barrier.</p>

<p>Queen Ophelia’s eyes sharpened.</p>

<p>"NOW!"</p>

<p>The world seemed to explode.</p>

<p>BOOOOOOOOOOM!</p>

<p>Emperor Ezekiel swung his greatsword with everything he had. A gigantic sword arc tore through the air while Emperor Caelus and Emperor Aldric released their attacks at almost the same moment.</p>

<p>Queen Ophelia pointed her staff forward.</p>

<p>"Fire!"</p>

<p>Hundreds of magic circles flashed.</p>

<p>Countless beams of magic shot out together.</p>

<p>Ethan also swung his sword.</p>

<p>A brilliant arc of holy light joined the attacks while Heliana released a wave of freezing power beside him. The remaining Transcendent Knights and mages poured out everything they had without holding anything back.</p>

<p>For a brief instant, the entire space became covered by different attacks.</p>

<p>Sword light.</p>

<p>Magic.</p>

<p>Ice.</p>

<p>Holy light.</p>

<p>Pure aura.</p>

<p>All of them rushed toward the same target.</p>

<p>The blood-red barrier immediately reacted.</p>

<p>BOOOOOOM!</p>

<p>The first wave crashed against it.</p>

<p>The enormous oval structure violently trembled.</p>

<p>The blood-red veins across its surface flashed wildly while huge amounts of vital life force rushed through them to withstand the attack.</p>

<p>Crack!</p>

<p>A small crack appeared.</p>

<p>Everyone’s eyes immediately lit up.</p>

<p>"Keep going!"</p>

<p>Emperor Ezekiel roared.</p>

<p>"Don’t give it a chance to recover!"</p>

<p>More power poured forward.</p>

<p>CRACK!</p>

<p>The blood-red barrier began trembling uncontrollably. More cracks appeared across its surface while the endless vital life force desperately tried to repair them.</p>

<p>However, this time the damage was too fast.</p>

<p>The barrier could no longer keep up.The entire blood-red oval stumbled under the combined force.</p>

<p>The ground beneath it collapsed.</p>

<p>Its enormous surface twisted inward while the figure sitting inside finally moved slightly.</p>

<p>Queen Ophelia noticed it immediately.</p>

<p>"Almost!"</p>

<p>Everyone poured the last of their strength into the attack.</p>

<p>The cracks covering the blood-red barrier suddenly connected.</p>

<p>For a single moment, everything became strangely quiet.</p>

<p>Ethan’s pupils contracted.</p>

<p>"Back!"a voice emerged from one guy but it was late.</p>

<p>The blood-red structure suddenly collapsed inward.</p>

<p>The massive amount of vital life force stored inside compressed into a single point.</p>

<p>A blinding red light flashed.</p>

<p>The next instant...</p>

<p>BOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOM!</p>

<p>A world-shattering explosion erupted.</p>

<p>The blood-red barrier exploded apart as an enormous wave of energy swept outward in every direction. The ground was ripped apart while massive pieces of rock were blasted into the air. The surrounding walls cracked under the force, and the entire area shook violently as though the mountain itself was about to collapse.</p>

<p>"DEFEND!"</p>

<p>Queen Ophelia screamed.</p>
