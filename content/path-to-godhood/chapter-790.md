<p>Ethan stood in the middle of the destroyed area, staring at the pillar in shock. Flames were still burning around him, and pieces of the broken structure were scattered across the ground, but the pillar was still standing. Even after taking his attack directly, it had not collapsed.</p>

<p>For a moment, Ethan simply stared at it. He could doubt many things, but he had never doubted his destructive power. His power of offense was far above his league, and with the strength he had just used, he had expected the pillar to be completely destroyed.</p>

<p>"Damnnn?"</p>

<p>Ethan’s eyes widened as he looked at the pillar again. He slowly tightened his grip around Solaris while his expression became more and more disturbed.</p>

<p>"Just what kind of spell is this?"</p>

<p>He stepped forward and examined the pillar from a distance. There were several cracks around the ground beneath it, and the surrounding area had been destroyed, yet the pillar itself remained almost completely intact.</p>

<p>"Fuckk..I don’t believe it.."</p>

<p>Ethan roared in anguish and raised Solaris again. Flames gathered around the greatsword, and a powerful wave of energy spread through the area as he prepared to attack once more.</p>

<p>He swung the greatsword and sent another powerful attack toward the pillar. The flames crashed into the barrier surrounding it, but the force was pushed away and scattered across the room. The impact blew apart the remaining walls nearby, and chunks of stone flew across the chamber. Several magic circles were crushed under the falling debris, while the floor cracked from the force.</p>

<p>Ethan did not stop.</p>

<p>He changed his approach and sent several swords of light toward the same place. The swords struck one after another, but the barrier continued to hold. Every impact created a loud burst of light, and the shockwaves spread across the chamber, throwing dust and broken stones into the air.</p>

<p>The nearby pillars and walls shook from the repeated attacks. Pieces of the ceiling came crashing down, and the floor continued to break under the pressure. Flames spread across the destroyed area while the remaining magic formations flickered from the constant impact.</p>

<p>He then gathered a huge amount of energy around his body and released it all at once. The attack slammed into the pillar with enough force to shake the surrounding structure. A huge blast swallowed the area, and the walls around it were blown apart.</p>

<p>BOOOOOOM!</p>

<p>The explosion spread through the underground chamber. Stone was shattered into pieces, and the ground was torn apart as the force passed through the area. For several moments, nothing could be seen through the thick dust and smoke.</p>

<p>Then the smoke slowly cleared.</p>

<p>The pillar was still standing.</p>

<p>The barrier around it flickered once before becoming stable again.</p>

<p>Ethan’s expression slowly changed.</p>

<p>He tried again.</p>

<p>A powerful wave of energy crashed into the barrier and tore through the surrounding walls. A concentrated blade of light followed and cut through several structures nearby, leaving deep marks across the stone. Then another heavy strike from Solaris smashed down with brutal force, creating another deep crater beneath Ethan’s feet.</p>

<p>One attack after another crashed against the pillar, and every time the result remained the same. The barrier around the pillar would flash brightly and deflect the attacks before returning to its original state.</p>

<p>The area around Ethan had almost been completely destroyed. The floor was covered with cracks and broken stones, while parts of the ceiling had collapsed. Flames continued to burn across the wreckage, and the air was filled with dust and the remains of shattered magic formations.</p>

<p>Yet the pillar remained.</p>

<p>It stood quietly in the middle of all the destruction, as if everything Ethan had done had barely mattered.</p>

<p>Ethan finally stopped.</p>

<p>He stood there with Solaris lowered beside him and stared at the pillar in silence. His breathing was slightly heavier as he tried to understand what he was seeing.</p>

<p>He had already used several different attacks, but none of them had managed to break through.</p>

<p>Ethan rubbed his forehead and fell into deep thought.</p>

<p>"Just what the hell is protecting this thing?"</p>

<p>His gaze slowly moved across the pillar and the magic formations connected to it. He carefully looked at the energy flowing through the structure and tried to understand how the barrier was absorbing and redirecting his attacks.</p>

<p>There had to be something he was missing.</p>

<p>The strength of the barrier was already beyond what he had expected, but what truly bothered Ethan was the way it recovered after every attack. No matter how much force he used, the barrier continued to stabilize itself.</p>

<p>Ethan narrowed his eyes.</p>

<p>"This isn’t simply a defensive barrier."</p>

<p>He slowly raised his head and looked at the pillar again.</p>

<p>Ethan remained in front of the pillar and carefully studied the magic formation surrounding it. His eyes moved across every line and connection, following the flow of mana as it passed through the structure and continued toward the other parts of the barrier.</p>

<p>He tried to understand how each magic circle worked with the others, but the deeper he looked, the more complicated the entire structure seemed.</p>

<p>The more he looked, the more complicated it became. There were too many magic circles connected together, and each one seemed to have its own purpose. Some controlled the flow of energy, while others stabilized the barrier and redirected the force that struck it. Even if</p>

<p>Ethan managed to find one weak point, he could not be sure that breaking it would actually bring the entire structure down.</p>

<p>Ethan frowned and rubbed his chin. His eyes moved from one magic circle to another as he tried to follow the complicated flow of mana.</p>

<p>"This is going to take some time."</p>

<p>He looked toward the damaged chamber. There wasn’t much time. The fighting outside was still continuing, and the longer he stayed here, the more difficult the situation could become. He could not spend hours standing here and slowly figuring out every part of the magic formation.</p>

<p>Ethan’s expression became serious as he thought about what he could do. He looked at the pillar again, then at the magic circles, and finally at the energy flowing through them. His mind began to run through different possibilities, trying to find a faster way to break the barrier.</p>

<p>Countless thoughts appeared in his mind one after another, but none of them seemed fast enough. Every method required him to understand the structure first, and that would take too much time. Ethan slowly lowered his gaze as his mind continued searching for another answer.</p>

<p>Then suddenly, his eyes changed.</p>

<p>A thought flashed through his mind.</p>

<p>Chaos.</p>

<p>He stood still for a moment. The power of Chaos was different from his other abilities. It did not need to overpower something through pure strength. Chaos existed to break order and turn things into disorder.</p>
