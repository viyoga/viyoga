<p>Far away from the noise of Empires and the endless clash of battles, in a place hidden from the sight of ordinary beings, a vast and ancient palace stood in complete silence, as if it existed outside the flow of time itself.</p>
<p>The palace was grand yet oppressive, built from dark stone that seemed to absorb every trace of light rather than reflect it, while massive pillars stretched upward into a shadowed ceiling and faint magical runes glowed across the walls, forming an ever-present and quiet power that filled the entire structure.</p>
<p>At the center of a vast chamber, a woman sat calmly upon a raised platform, and although her posture was straight, it carried a natural ease, as if the entire space belonged solely to her presence.</p>
<p>Her long hair flowed behind her like a silent river, and her expression remained indifferent, almost devoid of emotion, yet there was an undeniable authority in her stillness.</p>
<p>In front of her, a heart floated in the air, and it emitted a faint crimson glow as it pulsed slowly, as though it still retained life within it, while multiple layers of intricate magic circles surrounded it, each rotating at a different speed as runes flickered continuously, binding, refining, and suppressing the immense power contained inside.</p>
<p>A heavy and ancient draconic aura leaked from the heart, and although it was restrained, it carried a suffocating presence that filled the chamber.</p>
<p>Beside the platform, another woman knelt respectfully, keeping her head slightly lowered as she spoke in a steady voice.</p>
<p>“Your Majesty, the Mage Tower appears to be overstepping once again and has resumed its experiments within the common mortal world.”</p>
<p>Her words echoed softly through the chamber, yet the seated woman did not respond immediately, and a brief silence lingered before her eyes narrowed slightly.</p>
<p>“Again,” she said calmly, yet the moment her voice fell, an overwhelming chill spread throughout the space.</p>
<p>The temperature dropped instantly, and the cold expanded outward like an invisible tide, moving through the palace in a silent wave until, within moments, the entire structure was swallowed in a freezing stillness, while even beyond its walls, the capital itself felt a sudden and terrifying drop in temperature.</p>
<p>Back within the chamber, the kneeling woman stiffened slightly under the pressure, yet she did not raise her head.</p>
<p>“These old coots seem to have forgotten the consequences of what happened in the last century,” the woman on the throne said quietly, and although her tone did not rise, the weight behind her words made the air itself feel heavy.</p>
<p>The kneeling woman hesitated briefly before speaking again.</p>
<p>“Should we dispatch a battalion and crush them?”</p>
<p>For a moment, silence returned as the seated woman’s gaze shifted slightly, as though she were considering the suggestion, and a faint flicker of killing intent appeared in her eyes, revealing that the thought had indeed crossed her mind.</p>
<p>Destroying the Mage Tower would not be difficult for her, yet reason prevailed as her expression returned to calm, and she slowly shook her head.</p>
<p>“No, they are not worth it, at least not now,” she said, and her voice remained steady.</p>
<p>The kneeling woman remained silent, awaiting further instruction.</p>
<p>As the seated woman’s fingers moved slightly, the magic circles surrounding the floating heart reacted instantly, and more layers appeared, overlapping the existing ones to form an even more complex and powerful structure.</p>
<p>At the same time, the heart responded.</p>
<p>It pulsed more intensely, and its glow deepened as the draconic aura surged outward.</p>
<p>A deep, earth-shaking roar echoed within the chamber, as though the soul of a dragon struggled within the heart, and the refining process began in earnest as energy surged through the circles, compressing, purifying, and reshaping the essence contained within.</p>
<p>The kneeling woman lowered her head further, as the pressure grew immense.</p>
<p>“The demons are not quiet, they are simply waiting,” the seated woman said steadily, her eyes fixed upon the heart.</p>
<p>“If they remain still, it only means their next move will be far worse,” she continued, and her expression grew colder.</p>
<p>She tapped her fingers on the armrest of the throne while calculating various possibilities.</p>
<p>“I hope whatever they are doing,Arcadia is prepared to take them on else..”her eyes narrowed slightly.</p>
<p>“If Arcadia falls to the demon,the Empires might suffer.”</p>
<p>“In such a situation I don’t wish to create chaos on two fronts,” she added, making her intent clear.</p>
<p>The kneeling woman nodded faintly and responded, “Understood, Your Majesty,” before silence once again settled over the chamber.</p>
<p>Only the steady beating of the heart and the faint hum of the magic circles filled the space as the refining continued, while the glow of the heart intensified and the draconic aura became increasingly concentrated.</p>
<p>Then, as the process continued, the woman’s hand paused mid-motion, and for a brief moment, her eyes shifted, as though a distant memory had surfaced.</p>
<p>It was a person, or perhaps only a presence, yet it lingered clearly enough to draw her attention.</p>
<p>“I wonder…” she murmured softly, her voice quieter now and tinged with thought.</p>
<p>“…if we will meet again, or if you will even be alive,” she continued, and although her tone remained emotionless, something subtle passed through her eyes.</p>
<p>The magic circles continued their endless rotation, and the heart pulsed steadily as the chamber remained silent, while far away, beyond sight and knowledge, fate continued to move forward without pause.</p>
<p>…….</p>
<p>A cold wind swept down from the northern peaks as the snow-covered plains leading to Frostvele trembled under a growing disturbance, and what first seemed like a distant rumble soon turned into a chaotic surge of movement.</p>
<p>From the shadow of the mountains, countless monsters rushed forward in disarray, and their movements were not coordinated like an army but frantic and desperate, as if something far more terrifying chased them from behind.</p>
<p>Large beasts crashed through frozen trees while smaller creatures darted between them, and even aerial monsters flew low and erratically, abandoning their usual territories as they fled.</p>
<p>Their roars and shrieks mixed together, creating a chaotic echo that rolled across the land and headed straight toward Frostvele.</p>
<p>High above, on a jagged cliff overlooking the valley, a lone figure stood silently, and his cloak fluttered slightly in the freezing wind as his gaze remained fixed on the distant tide of monsters.</p>
<p>He raised his hand slowly, and a faint glow gathered at his fingertips before a thin streak of light shot into the sky, disappearing into the clouds as a signal sent to somewhere unknown.</p>
<p>“It seems something has happened in the deeper region of the mountain, and it has frightened even these creatures enough to abandon their domains,” he said calmly, his voice carrying no urgency despite the unfolding chaos.</p>
<p>His eyes narrowed slightly as he continued to observe the scene, and although the monsters rushed forward like a living flood.</p>
<p>“It seems this is a perfect opportunity.Since the route is cleared we won’t encounter much problem while traversing through here.”</p>
