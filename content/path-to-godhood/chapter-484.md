<p>Ethan remained seated calmly in his private chamber while the auction continued below.</p>
<p>He did not make a single move.</p>
<p>From the outside, it looked as if he was simply there to observe everything like a spectator.</p>
<p>Rathlos, on the other hand, participated in a few rounds. He bid on several items that caught his interest, but each time the price rose too quickly and too high, forcing him to give up with a slight frown.</p>
<p>Ethan watched everything quietly.He was waiting.</p>
<p>Patiently.</p>
<p>It was not until the twenty-second item that his expression finally changed.</p>
<p>The auctioneer stepped forward again with a confident smile while a new item was brought onto the stage.</p>
<p>“The twenty-second item is one kilogram of Zeitatie ore.”</p>
<p>The moment those words were spoken, a faint stir spread across the hall.</p>
<p>The ore placed on display shimmered faintly with a silver-blue glow. Even from a distance, one could feel the dense magical energy contained within it.</p>
<p>“The uses of this precious high-grade magical metal are surely clear to everyone,” the auctioneer continued smoothly.</p>
<p>“Whether it is used for forging epic-level magical weapons or for creating advanced magic arrays and powerful secret magical objects, it is an excellent top-grade material.”</p>
<p>Inside the private chamber, Ethan, who had been leaning back casually, suddenly straightened his posture.</p>
<p>A glimmer of light flashed in his eyes.</p>
<p>He wanted it.</p>
<p>No.</p>
<p>He needed it.</p>
<p>He could use it to forge a powerful sword, or even create a magical staff for Rina and Diana.</p>
<p>But he was not the only one.</p>
<p>That much was obvious.</p>
<p>“The starting price of this item is one hundred million gold coins. Each bid must not be less than ten million.”</p>
<p>The moment the auctioneer finished speaking, the bidding exploded.</p>
<p>“One hundred and fifty million!”</p>
<p>“One hundred and eighty million!”</p>
<p>“Two hundred and thirty million!”</p>
<p>Voices echoed across the hall one after another.</p>
<p>Within less than two minutes, the price had already soared to three hundred and fifty million.</p>
<p>Ethan did not move.</p>
<p>He remained calm.</p>
<p>Bidding now was meaningless.</p>
<p>He wanted to finish it in one strike.</p>
<p>The price continued rising, but the speed gradually slowed.</p>
<p>After crossing four hundred million, fewer people remained in the competition.</p>
<p>When the price reached four hundred and fifty million, only two bidders were left.</p>
<p>Ethan leaned forward slightly.</p>
<p>The moment had come.</p>
<p>Through the sound transmission array, his voice echoed clearly across the hall.</p>
<p>“Five hundred million gold coins.”</p>
<p>The sudden jump silenced the hall for a brief moment.</p>
<p>A direct increase of fifty million.</p>
<p>It clearly showed his determination.</p>
<p>One of the bidders immediately fell silent.</p>
<p>But the other was unwilling to give up.</p>
<p>“Five hundred and ten million.”</p>
<p>“Five hundred and twenty.”</p>
<p>“Five hundred and thirty-five,” Ethan responded calmly.</p>
<p>“Five hundred and forty.”</p>
<p>“Five hundred and sixty,” Ethan said, his tone turning slightly impatient.</p>
<p>The other party paused.The auctioneer looked around carefully.</p>
<p>“The item will be sold at five hundred and sixty million. Anyone want to bid for five hundred and seventy?”</p>
<p>She slowly raised her hammer.</p>
<p>Just as it was about to fall…</p>
<p>“Six hundred!”</p>
<p>A sudden voice erupted from another private chamber.</p>
<p>The entire hall reacted instantly.</p>
<p>“What!”</p>
<p>Even Ethan frowned slightly.</p>
<p>“Six hundred and fifty,” he said without hesitation.</p>
<p>“Seven hundred.”</p>
<p>“Seven hundred and ten.”</p>
<p>“Seven hundred and fifty.”</p>
<p>The bidding continued.</p>
<p>Now even Ethan felt something was off.</p>
<p>550 million gold coins was already costly and going extra 200 million gold coins.</p>
<p>That was foolish.</p>
<p>The item was valuable.But was it really worth this much?His brows furrowed slightly.</p>
<p>“I quit.”</p>
<p>His voice echoed calmly across the hall.</p>
<p>The auctioneer paused for a moment, clearly surprised by the sudden withdrawal.</p>
<p>Then she shook her head lightly and brought the hammer down.</p>
<p>“Sold.”</p>
<p>Ethan leaned back in his chair.Something was not right.He frowned and decided to try his suspicion.</p>
<p>The auction continued.More items were brought out, each more valuable than the last.</p>
<p>Ethan began bidding again on a few items.</p>
<p>“Five hundred.”</p>
<p>“Five hundred and fifty.”</p>
<p>“Six hundred.”</p>
<p>“Six hundred and fifty.”</p>
<p>But every time he made a bid.Another voice followed immediately.</p>
<p>Without delay and without any hesitation.And it was the same person.</p>
<p>“Give it to him,” Ethan said once, his tone calm but carrying a trace of coldness.</p>
<p>He lightly slammed his hand on the armrest.</p>
<p>The next item came.</p>
<p>Ethan tried again.</p>
<p>“Five hundred.”</p>
<p>“Five hundred and fifty.”</p>
<p>“Six hundred.”</p>
<p>The same voice responded instantly.</p>
<p>Again.</p>
<p>And again.</p>
<p>Ethan’s expression slowly darkened.</p>
<p>“Damn… who is that bastard?” he muttered under his breath.</p>
<p>Rathlos stood beside him with a serious expression.</p>
<p>“My lord… this feels like a ploy,” he said quietly.</p>
<p>Ethan glanced at him.</p>
<p>“We do not remember offending someone recently,” Rathlos continued.</p>
<p>“And even if we did, who would be foolish enough to waste this much money just to compete with us?”</p>
<p>Ethan nodded slowly.The other party was not just bidding.They were targeting him deliberately.</p>
<p>Every time Ethan raised the price, they followed without hesitation, as if they had been waiting only for him.</p>
<p>By now, the other party had already spent nearly three billion gold coins.</p>
<p>That amount was absurd.</p>
<p>No normal noble house would spend money like this without a clear purpose.Ethan narrowed his eyes slightly.</p>
<p>“There is no way this is just a random noble,” he said quietly.</p>
<p>Rathlos nodded.</p>
<p>“Agreed.”</p>
<p>“Either they have an endless source of wealth…”</p>
<p>“Or this is something planned.”</p>
<p>Ethan leaned back slowly in his seat.</p>
<p>His gaze turned cold and sharp.</p>
<p>“Then let’s see how long they can keep this up.”</p>
<p>But deep inside, he already understood one thing clearly.</p>
<p>Someone was deliberately playing against him.Once or twice could be dismissed as coincidence.</p>
<p>But seven times?</p>
<p>Are you kidding me?</p>
<p>Is this a joke or he didn’t hold any value.If not for auction he might already started swinging his sword.</p>
<p>That was no longer something one could ignore.</p>
