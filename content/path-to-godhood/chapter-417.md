<p>Ethan stood with his arms crossed, glaring down at the white-haired boy standing in front of him. The room was silent, heavy with unspoken tension.</p>

<p>“So tell me, Ray,” Ethan said at last, his voice was calm but carrying an unmistakable weight.</p>

<p>“What do you have to say?”</p>

<p>Ray.</p>

<p>The man who had butchered frontline demons without batting an eye, the same man whom soldiers hailed as the bravest among them.</p>

<p>If only the Frontier soldiers could see him now.</p>

<p>Ray stood straight, his back stiff, his chin lifted, yet inside he was sweating so badly it felt as if he might melt into a puddle at any second. His heart pounded violently, and his throat felt dry no matter how many times he swallowed.</p>

<p>He opened his lips to speak, but before a single word could come out, a crunching sound echoed in the room.</p>

<p>Crunch…Crunch… Crunch.</p>

<p>Both Ray and Ethan turned their heads.</p>

<p>At the corner of the room, Herion and Nera were sitting side by side on a bench, casually munching flavored popcorn like this was some kind of street performance. Herion had one leg crossed over the other, while Nera leaned forward slightly, clearly enjoying herself.</p>

<p>“Why stop now?” Herion said with a lazy grin, shoving another handful of popcorn into his mouth.</p>

<p>“Carry on,” he added. “Don’t mind us.”</p>

<p>“Yes,” Nera chimed in cheerfully. “Finish the scene. Don’t focus on us.”</p>

<p>A thick vein bulged on Ray’s forehead.</p>

<p>He turned slowly, his gaze sharp enough to cut steel.</p>

<p>“What is this?” he asked coldly. “A show?”</p>

<p>Herion shrugged. “Kind of.”</p>

<p>“Do you want me to show you who is the elder—”</p>

<p>“Shut up!”</p>

<p>The voice echoed like thunder, shaking the hall. Ray flinched and instinctively stepped back, while Herion and Nera froze mid-bite.</p>

<p>Ethan turned back to Ray, his expression dark.</p>

<p>“Talk to me,” he said. “Why bully the younger ones?”</p>

<p>“But Father—” Ray started.</p>

<p>“Shut up,” Ethan snapped. “You said you would return within a year, but it has been almost two years. No, it might even be beyond two years.”</p>

<p>His words poured out like a flood that had been held back too long.</p>

<p>“And what is this brain of yours filled with?” Ethan continued, his voice rising. “Why were you jumping straight into that hellhole?”</p>

<p>Once Ethan started, he did not stop.</p>

<p>He scolded Ray with brutal eloquence, every sentence hitting like a hammer. Herion and Nera exchanged glances, their eyes shining.</p>

<p>This was better than any play.</p>

<p>“Worth it,” Herion whispered.</p>

<p>Nera nodded seriously. “Totally.”</p>

<p>Ray’s head lowered again and again until he was practically kneeling on the floor. His fists clenched tightly at his sides, his shoulders trembling.</p>

<p>“Say something,” Ethan demanded. “Why are you silent now?”</p>

<p>Ray snapped.</p>

<p>“What can I say?” he burst out, his voice filled with frustration. “I just wanted to help. I couldn’t stand seeing innocent kids and children dying out there.”</p>

<p>“I went to Frontier for a stroll, but who knew a calamity would strike it?” Ray continued, his voice shaking. “Now what did you expect me to do?”</p>

<p>“Run away? Ignore everything?”</p>

<p>Ray gritted his teeth and slammed his foot into the ground.</p>

<p>The floor trembled slightly, causing Herion and Nera to hiccup in surprise.</p>

<p>“Sorry,” Ray said firmly. “I don’t think I can do that.”</p>

<p>He took a deep breath.</p>

<p>“If you are upset because of this, I can’t say anything exce—”</p>

<p>TAAANG!</p>

<p>Before he could finish, Ethan appeared in front of him and flicked his head, not hard, but sharp enough to sting.</p>

<p>“Ahhhh!” Ray yelped, holding his forehead.</p>

<p>“Stop screaming like a madman,” Ethan snapped. “When did I say you were wrong?”</p>

<p>Ray froze.</p>

<p>“I just meant,” Ethan continued, his voice calmer but heavier, “why didn’t you run away when Lady Amber was fighting?”</p>

<p>He leaned forward slightly, his eyes burning.</p>

<p>“Don’t overestimate yourself, you little shit. When I was around fifteen, even your daddy had to run from a pack of rabid dogs coming after my life, yet you jumped straight ahead when there was danger.”</p>

<p>“Helping people depends on your capabilities,” Ethan said slowly. “Sacrificing yourself when it won’t make a difference is fucking foolishness, you idiot.”</p>

<p>He straightened.</p>

<p>“Is this what I taught you?”</p>

<p>The atmosphere turned cold.</p>

<p>Ray lifted his head and looked into his father’s eyes. They were not filled with anger.</p>

<p>They were filled with worry.</p>

<p>Deep sadness.</p>

<p>And a faint hint of disappointment.</p>

<p>Ethan was not furious, rather he was scared and Ray finally understood his feelings.</p>

<p>At that moment, Ethan’s heart had almost jumped out of his chest. If he had been one second late, he might have lost his son forever.</p>

<p>The cheat had given him a trump card, yes, but after saving Ray, a sudden thought had surfaced in his mind, sharp and terrifying.</p>

<p>What if…</p>

<p>What if one day both of his children were in life-threatening danger?</p>

<p>What would he do then?</p>

<p>The silence lingered.</p>

<p>At the side, Herion slowly leaned toward Nera and whispered, “He’s dead.”</p>

<p>Nera nodded solemnly. “Emotionally.”</p>

<p>Ethan shot them a glare.</p>

<p>“Stop making a fool of yourself..”Ethan scolded.</p>

<p>The only reason he allowed them to watch was because they have been under pressure since the past few days and seem to have gone crazy.</p>

<p>“Did you learn something?”he asked.</p>

<p>“Huh!”Herion froze and asked,”Were you also teaching us something!”</p>

<p>“Out.”</p>

<p>BOOOM!</p>

<p>The two were thrown out the next moment.</p>

<p>They jumped up instantly.</p>

<p>“Yes, Father!” Herion saluted.</p>

<p>“Good luck, Brother!” Nera added, giving Ray a thumbs-up before dragging Herion out.</p>

<p>The doors closed, leaving only father and son.</p>

<p>Ray swallowed and spoke softly. “I’m sorry… I didn’t think that far.”</p>

<p>Ethan sighed heavily and rubbed his face.</p>

<p>“I know,” he said quietly. “That’s exactly why I’m angry.”</p>

<p>He placed a hand on Ray’s shoulder, his grip firm but warm.</p>

<p>“Next time,” Ethan said, “think before you act. Be brave, but don’t be stupid.”</p>

<p>Ray nodded slowly.</p>

<p>“I will,” he said.</p>

<p>The tension eased, but the weight of that unspoken fear still lingered in the air.</p>
