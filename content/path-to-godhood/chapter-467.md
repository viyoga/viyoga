<p>“It will end quickly.”</p>

<p>Those simple words made Hamel’s expression darken slightly.</p>

<p>“Arrogant,” he muttered.</p>

<p>Pentos stepped forward calmly and placed a hand on Hamel’s shoulder.</p>

<p>“Calm down,” he said quietly.</p>

<p>Then he looked at Ethan.</p>

<p>“If you insist on such a condition, we will not refuse.”Garet slowly rolled his shoulders while loosening his sleeves.</p>

<p>“Well then,” he said with a faint smirk.</p>

<p>“Let us see what the famous Marquise Ethan is capable of.”</p>

<p>Duke Lancelot, who had been silently observing the entire scene, suddenly raised his hand slightly.</p>

<p>The priest immediately stepped back.</p>

<p>Servants and guests quickly moved away from the center of the courtyard while guards created a wide open space.</p>

<p>Within seconds, the wedding courtyard had turned into an improvised arena.</p>

<p>Ethan stepped forward calmly and stood in the center of the open ground.</p>

<p>His posture remained relaxed.His hands were empty.He did not even reach for the sword hanging at his waist.</p>

<p>That small gesture did not go unnoticed.</p>

<p>Garet’s eyes narrowed slightly.</p>

<p>“You are not drawing your weapon?” he asked.</p>

<p>Ethan shook his head calmly.</p>

<p>“There is no need.”</p>

<p>Hamel laughed coldly.</p>

<p>“Don’t regret it later.”</p>

<p>The next moment his aura burst out.A powerful wave of energy spread across the courtyard.</p>

<p>Several guests gasped loudly.</p>

<p>“Legendary rank!”</p>

<p>“He’s already at that level?”</p>

<p>Hamel took one step forward.The stone floor beneath his boots cracked slightly under the pressure of his aura.</p>

<p>At the same time, Pentos and Garet also released their own auras.</p>

<p>Three powerful pressures spread across the courtyard.The atmosphere instantly grew heavy.</p>

<p>Many weaker nobles instinctively stepped backward.Even some soldiers tightened their grip on their weapons while watching the three nobles carefully.</p>

<p>Amber frowned slightly while watching them.</p>

<p>Three Legendary rank fighters.</p>

<p>Meanwhile Ethan simply stood there calmly.</p>

<p>He stretched his neck slightly and rolled his shoulders as if preparing for a simple exercise rather than a serious fight.</p>

<p>Then he looked at the three men and spoke in a relaxed tone.</p>

<p>“Ready?”</p>

<p>Hamel’s eyes flashed with anger.</p>

<p>“Don’t act so relaxed!”</p>

<p>The next second his figure shot forward.</p>

<p>BOOM!</p>

<p>The ground exploded beneath his feet as he rushed toward Ethan with terrifying speed.</p>

<p>His fist cut through the air like a hammer aimed directly at Ethan’s chest.</p>

<p>Several guests gasped in shock.</p>

<p>“That speed!”</p>

<p>But Ethan did not move until the last moment.Just as Hamel’s punch reached his chest, Ethan stepped slightly to the side.</p>

<p>WHOOSH.</p>

<p>The punch missed him by a hair.</p>

<p>Before Hamel could react, Ethan’s hand suddenly grabbed his wrist.</p>

<p>“What—”</p>

<p>CRASH!</p>

<p>Ethan twisted his arm and slammed him directly into the ground.The impact shattered the stone floor.</p>

<p>A cloud of dust burst into the air while cracks spread across the courtyard.</p>

<p>The entire crowd gasped loudly.</p>

<p>“That fast?!”</p>

<p>Hamel coughed violently while struggling to get up from the crater he had created.</p>

<p>But Ethan had already let go of him.He stepped back calmly and dusted his sleeve as if nothing unusual had happened.</p>

<p>“Next,” he said casually.</p>

<p>Garet’s expression darkened immediately.</p>

<p>Without hesitation, both he and Pentos moved at the same time.</p>

<p>Two figures rushed toward Ethan from opposite sides.</p>

<p>Their attacks came together perfectly.</p>

<p>One blade aimed at his shoulder.Another fist moved toward his ribs.</p>

<p>The coordination between them was precise.</p>

<p>But Ethan suddenly moved.</p>

<p>His body twisted between the two attacks like flowing water.</p>

<p>CLANG.</p>

<p>His hand caught the blade.</p>

<p>At the same moment his elbow smashed into Pentos’ chest.</p>

<p>BOOM!</p>

<p>Pentos staggered backward while coughing heavily.</p>

<p>At the same time Ethan kicked outward.</p>

<p>THUD!</p>

<p>Garet was sent sliding several meters across the courtyard floor.Dust and broken stone scattered everywhere.</p>

<p>Ethan slowly straightened his posture.He looked calmly at the three nobles.</p>

<p>“You should attack together properly,” he said.</p>

<p>“Otherwise this will end too quickly.”</p>

<p>For a brief moment after the first exchange, the courtyard fell into a stunned silence.</p>

<p>Broken stone and dust still lingered in the air where Hamel had been smashed into the ground. Pentos staggered backward while clutching his chest, and Garet slid several meters across the courtyard floor before barely stopping himself.</p>

<p>Many guests stared in disbelief.</p>

<p>One noble whispered in shock, “He pushed all three of them back so easily?”</p>

<p>Among the soldiers standing along the walls, several of them grinned proudly.</p>

<p>But the three nobles were no longer smiling.</p>

<p>Hamel slowly pushed himself up from the cracked stone ground. His clothes were covered in dust, and his face had turned darker with anger.</p>

<p>“You bastard…” he muttered while wiping blood from the corner of his mouth.</p>

<p>Garet wiped his lips as well and slowly stood up.His earlier calm expression had completely disappeared.</p>

<p>Pentos straightened his posture and looked toward Ethan with a serious gaze.</p>

<p>They had underestimated him.</p>

<p>Completely.</p>

<p>Hamel clenched his fists and released a surge of energy.</p>

<p>BOOM!</p>

<p>His aura burst outward violently. A powerful wave of energy spread across the courtyard and forced many nearby guests to step backward.</p>

<p>Garet and Pentos did the same.</p>

<p>Three powerful Legendary Rank auras erupted together.The pressure in the air immediately grew heavier.</p>

<p>Amber’s expression tightened slightly when she felt the rising energy.</p>

<p>Duke Lancelot narrowed his eyes but still remained seated calmly.</p>

<p>Ethan simply looked at them.</p>

<p>“So now you’re serious,” he said quietly.</p>

<p>Hamel suddenly leapt into the air.His body shot upward like a cannonball.</p>

<p>Garet and Pentos followed immediately.Within seconds, the three of them rose high above the courtyard.</p>

<p>Ethan looked up and lightly bent his knees.</p>

<p>BOOM!</p>

<p>The ground cracked beneath his feet as his body shot upward into the sky.</p>

<p>The battle had now moved above the wedding courtyard.</p>

<p>Guests below raised their heads to watch while soldiers moved quickly to clear more space.</p>

<p>High above the estate, the four figures faced each other in midair.</p>

<p>Cold wind rushed across the sky while scattered clouds drifted slowly around them.</p>

<p>Hamel attacked first.</p>

<p>His fists ignited with blazing red energy as he roared.</p>

<p>“Flame Crushing Fist!”</p>

<p>His body turned into a streak of fire and rushed straight toward Ethan.</p>

<p>At the same moment, Garet drew his sword.</p>

<p>A sharp silver aura wrapped around the blade.</p>

<p>“Halcrest Wind Cut!”</p>

<p>He swung the sword and a massive crescent shaped blade of compressed wind tore through the sky toward Ethan.</p>

<p>Pentos extended both hands forward.</p>

<p>Several glowing magic circles appeared around him.</p>

<p>“Storm Spear Formation!”</p>

<p>Dozens of lightning spears formed instantly and shot toward Ethan from multiple directions.</p>

<p>The sky above the estate exploded with attacks.</p>
