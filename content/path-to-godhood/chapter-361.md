<p>Ethan forcefully suppressed the excitement rising inside his chest and tried to calm himself.</p>
<p>He could not afford to disappear into secluded cultivation immediately after every birth. If he did that too often, people would start to notice and question him. So this time, he waited.</p>
<p>Soon a month passed in the blink of an eye.</p>
<p>Only after everything settled did Ethan finally leave the main territory and head toward a specially constructed training chamber. It was built far away, deep in an isolated region, surrounded by layers of formations and natural barriers.</p>
<p>Here, he did not need to worry about destroying half his land by accident.</p>
<p>The chamber was quiet and heavy with dense mana.</p>
<p>Ethan sat down at the center, crossed his legs, and took several slow breaths to steady his racing heart.</p>
<p>“Alright,” he muttered. “Come on, system. Time for the bang.”</p>
<p>He clenched his fist lightly.</p>
<p>“Give me the rewards.”</p>
<p>“Open all of them at once.”</p>
<p>[DING]</p>
<p>The familiar clear sound echoed in the chamber like sweet music to his ears.</p>
<p>In the next instant, streams of light flashed before his eyes. Notifications poured down one after another, almost blinding him.</p>
<p>[You have gained 200 years of Mage Insight.]</p>
<p>[You have gained A-rank Combat Skill: Darkness Enroacher.]</p>
<p>[You have gained Growth-Type Combat Skill: Holy Sword Light Array.]</p>
<p>[You have gained Soul Bind Stamp.]</p>
<p>[You have gained 400 years of Knight Insight.]</p>
<p>[You have gained Master Knight Breakthrough Potion ×3.]</p>
<p>[You have gained the blueprint of Fire Rune Ballista.]</p>
<p>[You have gained B-rank Aptitude Potion ×5.]</p>
<p>[You have gained Troll Bloodline Potion ×1.]</p>
<p>[You have gained 200 years of Mage Insight.]</p>
<p>[You have gained an A-rank Flame Spell Set.]</p>
<p>[You have gained A-rank Aptitude Potion ×1.]</p>
<p>[You have gained the King Knight Breakthrough Potion.]</p>
<p>[You have gained Life Replacement Doll ×2.]</p>
<p>Ethan’s breathing grew heavy.</p>
<p>For a moment, his vision swayed as his mind struggled to process the sheer volume of rewards. He rubbed his face and let out a long breath.</p>
<p>“Damn…” he muttered. “That’s a lot.”</p>
<p>He did not rush.</p>
<p>First, he mentally set aside all the potions and blueprints. Those could wait. What truly caught his attention were the combat skills.</p>
<p>He focused on the first one.</p>
<p>[Darkness Enroacher]</p>
<p>A combat skill usable by both knights and mages. When activated, the user releases a spreading field of dark energy. Enemies within the area feel their bodies grow heavy and weak. Their stamina drains rapidly, and their strength fades. At the same time, fragments of their life essence are drawn toward the user, restoring vitality and mana.</p>
<p>The stronger the user, the wider the range and the deeper the suppression.</p>
<p>Ethan’s eyes narrowed slightly.</p>
<p>“A wide-area weakening skill that feeds me at the same time,” he murmured. “Perfect for crowd control.”</p>
<p>He moved on to the next one.</p>
<p>[Holy Sword Light Array]</p>
<p>A growth-type combat skill. The user forms an array of five swords made from condensed light and personal intent. These swords can move independently, attack from different angles, and respond to the user’s will.</p>
<p>Once an enemy is caught within the array, their movement is restricted. The pressure of the swords pins them down, making escape extremely difficult.</p>
<p>The power, speed, and sharpness of the swords grow along with the user’s rank and soul strength.</p>
<p>Ethan exhaled slowly.</p>
<p>“This one has huge potential,” he said softly. “It’ll only get stronger with time.”</p>
<p>Then his gaze shifted to the next reward.</p>
<p>[Soul Bind Stamp]</p>
<p>By placing this stamp on another person’s soul, the user establishes a binding contract. The bound soul cannot disobey the user’s direct commands. Any attempt to harbor malicious intent or betrayal will trigger punishment that directly affects the soul.</p>
<p>Warning: The user’s soul strength must surpass that of the target, or the binding will fail.</p>
<p>Ethan’s expression turned serious.</p>
<p>“…This is exactly what I needed right now.”</p>
<p>He immediately thought of the dark mage remnant sealed in the scepter. This was the safety measure he had been looking for.</p>
<p>Next, his attention moved to the spell rewards.</p>
<p>[A-rank Flame Spell Set]</p>
<p>A complete set of high-level flame spells. Includes offensive spells for large-scale destruction, concentrated flame lances for penetration, and sustained burning fields for battlefield control.</p>
<p>The spells scale with the user’s mana, affinity, and control.</p>
<p>“Good,” Ethan nodded. “I was lacking proper fire-based magic.”</p>
<p>Finally, his eyes fell on the Life Replacement Dolls.</p>
<p>As soon as he focused on them, two small wooden dolls appeared in his hands. They were palm-sized, carved with simple humanoid shapes, and covered in faint runes. They felt oddly warm.</p>
<p>A new set of notifications appeared.</p>
<p>[Life Replacement Doll]</p>
<p>Upon receiving fatal damage, this item will activate automatically. The doll will shatter, replacing the user’s death with severe injury and relocating the user to a nearby safe location.</p>
<p>One doll can be used only once.</p>
<p>Ethan’s fingers tightened slightly around them.</p>
<p>“…Two extra lives,” he whispered.</p>
<p>For the first time in a long while, a genuine sense of security settled in his heart.</p>
<p>He leaned back, letting his body relax.</p>
<p>Ethan took a deep breath and looked at the potions laid out before him.</p>
<p>“Breakthrough potion. Aptitude improvement potion and several others,” he muttered softly.</p>
<p>“Is this feeling of being rich!”</p>
<p>“Finally I can upgrade others’ strength..It’s time for everyone to fly too.”</p>
<p>A sense of relief washed over him.</p>
<p>“Thank God they came at the right time.”</p>
<p>With these, he could finally change something that had been bothering him for a long time.</p>
<p>Sophia, Lia, and Diana, along with his first four children except Ray, all had relatively low potential. It was like a small bone stuck in his throat. He could ignore it, but it was always there, reminding him.</p>
<p>Now, he could finally help them rise.</p>
<p>A gentle smile appeared on his face as that thought settled in his heart.</p>
<p>Then his gaze moved to the remaining potion.</p>
<p>His smile slowly stiffened.</p>
<p>“Huh?”</p>
<p>[Troll Bloodline Potion.]</p>
