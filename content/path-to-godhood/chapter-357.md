<p>BOOM!</p>

<p>SWOOSH!</p>

<p>A terrifying pressure exploded outward from Rathlos’s body. The ground flattened instantly, and the surrounding forest shook violently as a shockwave spread in all directions.</p>

<p>“Grymm!”</p>

<p>“Wha—!”</p>

<p>Joel and Randall were thrown backward, barely managing to keep their footing as the force slammed into them.</p>

<p>Bones rattled across the battlefield.</p>

<p>The corrupted energy resisted desperately, screaming and thrashing, but Rathlos stood firmly. His figure remained unmoving amid the storm of darkness.</p>

<p>“This is not your place,” he said coldly.</p>

<p>The darkness let out one final, piercing scream.</p>

<p>Then it was crushed.</p>

<p>The remaining will was completely subdued, dragged under Rathlos’s control. The black mist collapsed inward, and the staff’s glow dimmed sharply.</p>

<p>Silence followed.</p>

<p>Only broken undead remains and scorched earth were left behind as proof of what had happened.</p>

<p>Rathlos bent down and picked up the scepter and the chest beside it. He looked around and spoke firmly.</p>

<p>“Call the priests and cleanse this area.”</p>

<p>“I need to leave.”</p>

<p>With a loud blast, his figure shot into the sky, leaving behind a dark streak of light.</p>

<p>Randall and Joel stared at the scene, sweating heavily.</p>

<p>“When will the day come,” Randall muttered, “that we can act this badass?”</p>

<p>Noel’s lips twitched, not knowing what to say.</p>

<p>…….</p>

<p>Rathlos returned later and placed the items before Ethan.</p>

<p>“Hiss… I am shivering,” Ethan said, rubbing his arms as he stared at the scepter and chest.</p>

<p>“The atmosphere feels cold.”</p>

<p>“Is that really a dungeon?”</p>

<p>“I checked carefully,” Rathlos replied. “It is not. There was a remnant will of a dark mage sealed inside. I subdued it, but it still needs proper cleansing.”</p>

<p>“A dark mage?” Ethan asked, shocked.</p>

<p>“Yes,” Rathlos said, rubbing his chin. “It seems the mage died, but before that, he cut out and sealed a part of his soul into this.”</p>

<p>“Is that even possible?” Ethan asked.</p>

<p>“For mages, yes,” Rathlos replied calmly. “They do all kinds of nefarious research.”</p>

<p>“Damn…” Ethan cursed softly and reached out, gripping the scepter.</p>

<p>Just then, Rathlos’s voice echoed from outside.</p>

<p>“I have subdued the soul, but I wasn’t able to kill it completely.”</p>

<p>“Yeah, I understood that,” Ethan replied.</p>

<p>The moment Ethan grabbed the scepter, the world around him shattered.</p>

<p>His vision blurred, and in the next instant, he found himself standing in a completely different place. There was no ground, no sky. Only endless darkness, thick and heavy, like fog soaked in oil.</p>

<p>Then a deep, eerie voice rolled across the void, cold enough to crawl under his skin.</p>

<p>[Body… Give that body to me…]</p>

<p>The dark mist surged forward, twisting and writhing as it took on a vague humanoid shape. It lunged toward Ethan, clawing at his presence, trying to crawl its way into his soul.</p>

<p>But to Ethan’s senses, it felt pitiful.</p>

<p>Like a tiny ant trying to shake a towering tree.</p>

<p>Ethan merely straightened his posture.</p>

<p>His body erupted with bright, warm light.</p>

<p>[Ahhhhh… Ahhh?!]</p>

<p>[Stoooop! Stoooop!]</p>

<p>The mist shrieked in pain, recoiling violently. It scrambled backward, dragging itself into the far corner of the void as if burned alive.</p>

<p>Ethan watched it with calm eyes, more curious than alarmed.</p>

<p>From the way the Church had cleansed the remaining intent earlier, he had learned a few things.</p>

<p>First, to clear a will, one had to wash away the negative energy with light. Then, one had to overpower the will or intent itself. Finally, a direct soul attack was needed to truly erase it.</p>

<p>At that moment, he realized something.</p>

<p>“So this is how I killed the soul of that lich,” he murmured to himself.</p>

<p>For beings weaker than him, a single overwhelming strike was enough to do all three steps at once. But for beings stronger, or closer to his level, raw soul power was needed.</p>

<p>He had released the power of light just now. By all logic, the dark mage should have been erased.</p>

<p>Yet it was still here.</p>

<p>Screaming and cursing at him.</p>

<p>Ethan took a step forward.</p>

<p>The soul crawled backward in panic.</p>

<p>[No… don’t come… don’t… Ahhhh!]</p>

<p>Ethan slowly drew back his light and spoke calmly.</p>

<p>“Who are you?”</p>

<p>The trembling soul looked at him. For a brief moment, the hatred faded, replaced by something else.</p>

<p>Sadness and remorse.</p>

<p>[What does it matter…]</p>

<p>[I am going to die after all.]</p>

<p>“Even if you are dying,” Ethan said quietly, “won’t it feel better to talk before death?”</p>

<p>Silence lingered.</p>

<p>Then, a dry, hollow laugh echoed through the void.</p>

<p>[Hahaha… Good. Since you want to listen, then listen.]</p>

<p>[I am Mareti… One of the great dark mages from the Mage Tower.]</p>

<p>[I was hailed as the genius of the century. That praise stirred jealousy in many. However…]</p>

<p>The soul’s shape distorted and the darkness flickered violently.</p>

<p>[Who knew the one who would betray me was my own brother…]</p>

<p>[Not only did he kill me, he extracted my potential and all my knowledge.]</p>

<p>“Wait,” Ethan said sharply. “What did you just say?”</p>

<p>[It seems you don’t know.]</p>

<p>[There are ways to eat a person.]</p>

<p>[Ways that transfer their power, their potential, and even their memories to another.]</p>

<p>Ethan swallowed hard.</p>

<p>Cold sweat drenched his back.</p>

<p>That was horrifying.</p>

<p>Utterly horrifying.</p>

<p>If such things were possible, what else could these people do?</p>

<p>Mage Tower.</p>

<p>He had never heard of it before, but the name burned itself into his mind. He did not seek trouble, but he knew one truth very well.</p>

<p>As one rose higher, trouble would seek them.</p>

<p>And if he truly became one of the youngest to reach the upper stages, who was to say the mages would not target him?</p>

<p>“No,” he thought grimly. “I can’t afford to be unprepared.”</p>

<p>Ethan looked at Mareti again, his expression flat and unreadable.</p>

<p>“How many evil deeds have you committed?”</p>

<p>The soul froze.</p>

<p>[…]</p>

<p>[I don’t know if it can be called evil…]</p>

<p>[I played with corpses.]</p>

<p>[As a mage, research is necessary. People are tortured in research all the time, but my field was corpses. I didn’t need to actively hunt the living.]</p>

<p>[No matter where you go, people are always dying.]</p>

<p>[Aside from that, several knights, priests, nobles, and others died by my hands.]</p>

<p>[If you ask whether I am good or evil…]</p>

<p>[I am definitely evil.]</p>

<p>…….</p>
