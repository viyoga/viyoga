<p>"You look surprised," he said calmly.</p>
<p>"I am," Ethan admitted honestly after a brief pause.</p>
<p>Then he looked directly at the Crown Prince.</p>
<p>"Your Highness, with your status, I doubt friendship is something you can casually offer," he said carefully.</p>
<p>Haster smiled slightly after hearing that.</p>
<p>"And that is exactly why I am offering it seriously," he replied.</p>
<p>The atmosphere inside the room became quieter, and even the servants standing nearby lowered their heads further while not daring to interrupt.</p>
<p>Haster leaned back slightly against his seat while calmly swirling the coffee inside his cup.</p>
<p>"You are interesting, Ethan Blanks," he said.</p>
<p>"Far more interesting than most nobles inside this Empire."</p>
<p>Ethan remained silent and simply listened quietly.</p>
<p>Haster continued speaking slowly.</p>
<p>"You are talented."</p>
<p>"You are young."</p>
<p>"You have influence."</p>
<p>"And despite everything happening around you, you are still growing stronger instead of collapsing."</p>
<p>His crimson eyes narrowed slightly.</p>
<p>"That alone already makes you valuable."</p>
<p>Ethan gave a faint smile after hearing that.</p>
<p>"You are overestimating me, Your Highness," he replied calmly.</p>
<p>Haster immediately shook his head.</p>
<p>"No," he said.</p>
<p>"If anything, people are underestimating you."</p>
<p>Those words made Ethan’s eyes narrow slightly, and for a brief moment silence lingered between them again.</p>
<p>Then Haster suddenly laughed softly while waving his hand lightly.</p>
<p>"Relax," he said casually.</p>
<p>"I am not planning to threaten you or drag you into some troublesome political faction."</p>
<p>Ethan looked at him carefully.</p>
<p>"Then why?" he finally asked.</p>
<p>Haster took another sip of coffee before answering.</p>
<p>"Because I prefer capable people over useless flatterers," he replied calmly.</p>
<p>"Most people around me only know how to nod and agree with everything I say."</p>
<p>His expression became slightly colder.</p>
<p>"It becomes boring after a while."</p>
<p>Then his gaze returned toward Ethan again.</p>
<p>"But you are different.You are cautious."</p>
<p>"You think before acting."</p>
<p>"And despite pretending otherwise, you clearly dislike being controlled."</p>
<p>Ethan’s expression remained calm, but inwardly he felt slightly alarmed.</p>
<p>This Crown Prince was much sharper than he expected.</p>
<p>Haster suddenly smiled again.</p>
<p>"Do not worry," he said casually.</p>
<p>"I am not asking for blind loyalty."</p>
<p>"I simply want a proper relationship between us."</p>
<p>Ethan remained quiet for several seconds while carefully considering his words.</p>
<p>Rejecting directly would obviously be stupid, but accepting too easily was equally dangerous.</p>
<p>After a brief moment, Ethan finally spoke carefully.</p>
<p>"If Your Highness does not mind having someone troublesome as a friend," he said calmly.</p>
<p>"Then I naturally would not reject such honor."</p>
<p>Haster’s smile widened slightly.</p>
<p>"Haha..."</p>
<p>"That answer is much smarter than blindly agreeing," he said.</p>
<p>Then he slowly placed his cup down before looking directly at Ethan again.</p>
<p>"To celebrate this new friendship," he said calmly.</p>
<p>"I will give you a small piece of advice."</p>
<p>Ethan’s eyes sharpened slightly.</p>
<p>Haster’s expression slowly became more serious.</p>
<p>"The capital is not peaceful right now," he said quietly.</p>
<p>"There are many people watching you."</p>
<p>Ethan did not look surprised.</p>
<p>"That much I expected," he replied.</p>
<p>Haster nodded slightly.</p>
<p>"But some of them are not watching because of your talents," he continued.</p>
<p>"They are watching because of what you brought to my father."</p>
<p>The moment those words were spoken, Ethan’s heart tightened slightly.</p>
<p>So the information had already spread among the upper levels.</p>
<p>Haster observed Ethan’s reaction calmly.</p>
<p>"The war has reached a very delicate stage," he said.</p>
<p>"And your potion..."</p>
<p>He paused briefly.</p>
<p>"It has the potential to disrupt many things."</p>
<p>Ethan frowned slightly after hearing that.</p>
<p>"You speak as if ending the war would anger certain people," he said carefully.</p>
<p>For the first time since entering the room, Haster’s smile faded slightly.</p>
<p>Then he let out a quiet breath.</p>
<p>"You are smarter than most nobles," he muttered.</p>
<p>He slowly looked toward the window beside him while speaking calmly.</p>
<p>"Ethan..."</p>
<p>"This world is far uglier than you think."</p>
<p>His crimson eyes darkened slightly.</p>
<p>"And sometimes, peace is more dangerous to certain people than war itself."</p>
<p>Ethan’s fingers tightened slightly over the coffee cup.Then Haster continued calmly.</p>
<p>"Also, by trying to stop the war, you are provoking the people that stand high up in the sky, so you should think carefully about it."</p>
<p>His gaze slowly shifted back toward Ethan.</p>
<p>"Wonder whether you want to jump into the frontlines and bear the brunt..."</p>
<p>"Or stay behind and reap benefits."</p>
<p>The moment those words entered Ethan’s ears, a cold feeling crept across his back.</p>
<p>Because suddenly Mareti’s earlier warning echoed inside his mind once again.</p>
<p>"The cause of this war might not be as straightforward as you think."</p>
<p>The room became quiet again, and neither of them spoke immediately.</p>
<p>Then after several moments, Haster suddenly smiled faintly again as if nothing had happened.</p>
<p>"Well," he said casually while standing up slowly.</p>
<p>"That is enough serious talk for today."</p>
<p>He looked at Ethan with amusement.</p>
<p>"Otherwise you might start thinking I invited you here to recruit you into some conspiracy."</p>
<p>Ethan let out a helpless breath.</p>
<p>"...And here I thought this was only about dresses," he replied dryly.</p>
<p>Haster laughed openly this time.</p>
<p>"Haha!"</p>
<p>"Sadly, politics always ruins everything."</p>
<p>Then he looked toward Ethan one last time before speaking calmly.</p>
<p>"Enjoy your stay in the capital, Ethan Blanks."</p>
<p>"And be careful."</p>
<p>"Some eyes in this city are far more dangerous than enemies on the battlefield."</p>
<p>With that, Haster turned around and left alongside his guards while the sound of their footsteps slowly faded away from the room.</p>
<p>The atmosphere instantly turned colder and heavier after his departure.</p>
<p>Ethan remained seated quietly while staring ahead, and his expression slowly darkened as countless thoughts moved through his mind.</p>
<p>A few moments later, space beside him distorted slightly, and Rathlos appeared quietly before looking toward the direction where Haster had left.</p>
<p>Then he turned toward Ethan.</p>
<p>"What happened?" he asked.</p>
<p>"How did the meeting go?"</p>
<p>Ethan’s eyes remained fixed ahead for several moments before he finally spoke.</p>
<p>"The meeting went well however..."</p>
<p>He paused briefly.</p>
<p>Then remembering the subtle warning hidden beneath Haster’s words, Ethan slowly gritted his teeth.</p>
<p>"I think we just stepped into something far bigger than expected."</p>
