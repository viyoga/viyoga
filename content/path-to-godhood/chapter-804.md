<p>His face turned pale.</p>

<p>The attack wasn’t simply cutting his body.</p>

<p>It was entering inside him.</p>

<p>Just as the strange energy was about to cause more damage, Lancelot suddenly appeared beside him.</p>

<p>Lancelot slammed his palm against Garron’s back.</p>

<p>"Get it out!"</p>

<p>A powerful force entered Garron’s body and collided with the sword’s energy. The two forces fought against each other for a moment before Lancelot forcefully pushed the strange sword energy out.</p>

<p>A thin stream of sharp energy escaped from Garron’s body and disappeared into the air.</p>

<p>Garron took several deep breaths and looked at Lancelot with a grim expression.</p>

<p>Lancelot also stared at the green-robed figure.</p>

<p>"Who is this weird guy?" Garron asked in confusion and stared back at the three family heads, who had sombre expressions on their faces.</p>

<p>"Enough chit chat..He is dangerous. We should move to take him down at once." Lancelot spoke and raised his sword toward the green-robed figure.</p>

<p>He then looked toward the several Transcendent Knights around them and gave a firm command.</p>

<p>"Everyone..Attack him together..Don’t give him any chance to breathe."</p>

<p>Several Transcendent Knights immediately rushed forward. Their figures moved through the sky from different directions, and each of them released their full strength. Sword lights, energy attacks and powerful auras spread across the battlefield as they closed in on the green-robed figure.</p>

<p>The green-robed figure simply stood there.</p>

<p>He quietly watched the approaching figures.</p>

<p>Seeing this, Dorian’s expression suddenly changed.</p>

<p>He quickly took out a flute and brought it to his lips. The moment he started playing, a strange sound spread across the battlefield. The melody was not loud, but it carried a strange power that made the surrounding air tremble.</p>

<p>"Use all your power at once."</p>

<p>The Transcendent Knights heard his instruction and released their full strength.</p>

<p>The green-robed figure saw several figures moving toward him at once.</p>

<p>He slowly closed his eyes.</p>

<p>The next moment, his entire body began to glow.</p>

<p>A strange light spread around him, and an enormous ethereal figure slowly appeared behind his body. It rose into the sky like a giant shadow and covered the area behind him.</p>

<p>Rathlos stared at the huge figure and couldn’t help but ask.</p>

<p>"A humanoid like Ethan’s Knight.."</p>

<p>"No, it’s not a knight but a demon."</p>

<p>Lancelot’s voice became grim as he stared at the phantom.</p>

<p>The face of the phantom began changing.</p>

<p>Its once humanoid face twisted into something evil and monstrous. Its jaw stretched unnaturally wide, revealing rows of long and sharp teeth. Two huge eyes opened on its face, and a dark glow burned inside them.</p>

<p>Its forehead bulged outward, and strange horns curled backward from its head. Its expression looked almost like it was grinning, but there was nothing human about that grin.</p>

<p>The phantom’s enormous body was covered with dark lines that moved across its skin like living veins.</p>

<p>Then six massive arms slowly emerged from its body.</p>

<p>The arms appeared from different parts of its torso, twisting into place before spreading outward. Each hand was enormous, with long fingers that ended in sharp claws.</p>

<p>The sight made the surrounding Transcendent Knights slow down for a moment.</p>

<p>The green-robed figure suddenly moved his hands.</p>

<p>His fingers formed several strange signs one after another.</p>

<p>Then he raised both arms.</p>

<p>The six arms of the phantom moved.</p>

<p>BOOM!</p>

<p>All six hands slammed forward at once.</p>

<p>The air exploded.</p>

<p>The nearest Transcendent Knights were forced to dodge as enormous waves of force tore through the sky. One giant hand swept across the battlefield and smashed through several layers of energy barriers while another came crashing down from above.</p>

<p>The knights scattered in different directions.</p>

<p>One knight twisted his body in midair and narrowly avoided a massive fist that passed directly beside him.</p>

<p>Another raised his sword and blocked a giant palm.</p>

<p>CLANG!</p>

<p>The impact shook his entire body.</p>

<p>His sword bent under the pressure, and his body was forced backward.</p>

<p>Before he could recover, another hand suddenly came from his blind spot.</p>

<p>"Shit!"</p>

<p>He quickly raised his other arm.</p>

<p>BOOM!</p>

<p>The fist struck him and sent him flying across the sky.</p>

<p>At the same time, three other hands attacked the remaining knights.</p>

<p>One swept horizontally.</p>

<p>Another punched straight forward.</p>

<p>The third came from above and slammed downward.</p>

<p>The knights dodged the first two, but the third attack struck the ground beneath them.</p>

<p>BOOOOM!</p>

<p>The earth exploded.</p>

<p>A huge cloud of dust rose into the air as cracks spread across the ground. Stones and broken pieces of earth were thrown hundreds of meters upward.</p>

<p>The knights barely managed to escape the impact.</p>

<p>But the phantom didn’t stop.</p>

<p>Its six arms suddenly changed direction.</p>

<p>They moved together and then separated at the same time, attacking the Transcendent Knights from different angles.</p>

<p>One hand came from the left.</p>

<p>Another came from the right.</p>

<p>Two hands attacked from above while the remaining two suddenly moved from below.</p>

<p>The knights were forced to twist and turn constantly to avoid them.</p>

<p>Their faces became pale and the attacks were getting faster.</p>

<p>Much faster.</p>

<p>The six hands moved so quickly that afterimages filled the sky. For a moment, it looked as if dozens of giant arms were attacking at once.</p>

<p>A Transcendent Knight swung his sword and cut through one of the phantom’s hands.</p>

<p>But the moment the attack passed through it, another hand came from behind.</p>

<p>He barely turned around.The giant fist struck his chest.</p>

<p>BOOM!</p>

<p>His armor shattered, and his body was sent flying like a meteor.</p>

<p>Another knight rushed forward from the side and tried to attack the green-robed figure directly.</p>

<p>The phantom reacted immediately.</p>

<p>One of its hands caught the knight’s weapon.</p>

<p>Another grabbed his shoulder.</p>

<p>The knight’s expression changed as the enormous fingers tightened around him.</p>

<p>Before he could break free, a third hand punched him directly into the ground.</p>

<p>BOOM!</p>

<p>The impact created another crater.</p>

<p>Rathlos’s expression became increasingly grim as he watched the battle.</p>

<p>The phantom was not simply attacking with raw strength.</p>

<p>Every hand moved independently, yet they all worked together perfectly.</p>

<p>The Transcendent Knights could barely keep up.</p>

<p>The green-robed figure continued forming strange signs with his hands, and every movement made the phantom respond.</p>

<p>Six massive arms continued tearing through the battlefield.</p>

<p>The Transcendent Knights were forced to retreat again and again as the monstrous phantom continued its relentless assault.</p>

<p>For the first time, Rathlos and Garron felt a real sense of danger.</p>

<p>This strange green-robed figure was far more terrifying than they had imagined.</p>
