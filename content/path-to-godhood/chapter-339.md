<p>CLANG! CLASH! BOOOOM!</p>

<p>Ethan moved around emitting lightening flickers.</p>

<p>He blocked one strike, twisted his body to dodge another, and parried a third. Each collision sent violent shockwaves through the battlefield. Sacred light and cursed fire collided again and again, tearing the surroundings apart.</p>

<p>A cursed blade grazed Ethan’s side.</p>

<p>Dark flames clung to his armor, hissing as they tried to eat into his flesh. The curse spread instantly, black veins crawling along his skin.</p>

<p>Ethan gritted his teeth.</p>

<p>Light burst from his body, burning the curse away with a sharp hiss. The flames screamed as they vanished.</p>

<p>Agros’s eyes widened slightly.</p>

<p>“You can even burn curses and heal your wounds?” he snarled, his expression twisted with disbelief.</p>

<p>It wasn’t that he had never seen such people. Most who wielded such power were special beings, those blessed by the Goddess herself.</p>

<p>Yet at a glance, this man didn’t seem special at all. And still, he was hoarding far too many secrets.</p>

<p>“Whatever… a dead man leaves no tales.”</p>

<p>With that, he slammed the bone sword into the ground.</p>

<p>The earth itself responded in agony.</p>

<p>A deep, guttural groan rose from beneath the land as cracks began to spread in every direction and the ground split open like a wounded beast, and pillars of cursed flames erupted from below.</p>

<p>Entire streets were torn into the air and shattered. The stones melted and the remaining ruins of buildings warped and twisted. What remained of the capital burned as the hellfire swallowed everything in sight.</p>

<p>SWOOOSH!</p>

<p>The world seemed to burn as if hell itself had descended.</p>

<p>However…</p>

<p>“Damn… I am really lucky…”</p>

<p>A fire attack…</p>

<p>Now that was something he could take head-on.</p>

<p>Smiling, he slammed his feet on the ground.</p>

<p>BOOOM!</p>

<p>The explosion beneath him hurled him upward, as if the land itself had rejected his presence.</p>

<p>The next instant, pillars of fire burst and collapsed beneath his feet as he soared through the air. Heat clawed at his body, but sacred light wrapped around him like a second skin, keeping it at bay.</p>

<p>While airborne, he raised his greatsword high.</p>

<p>The power of light gathered along its blade.</p>

<p>It wasn’t gentle. It was heavy, sharp, and merciless.</p>

<p>Then he slashed downward.</p>

<p>A massive arc of sacred light fell from the sky like divine judgment.</p>

<p>BOOOOOOM!</p>

<p>The strike carved a deep trench across the battlefield.</p>

<p>The cursed flames were split apart as if sliced by an invisible blade. The earth screamed once more as the arc slammed directly into Agros’s chest.</p>

<p>Agros staggered back.</p>

<p>His massive body dragged through ruined structures, ploughing everything in his wake. Towers collapsed. Stone and metal were reduced to rubble beneath his weight. Dust and fire filled the air as he dug his feet into the ground, forcing himself to a stop.</p>

<p>He roared in fury.</p>

<p>“You dare!” he shouted, his voice shaking the very air around him.</p>

<p>After taking a moment to regain his balance, he charged again.</p>

<p>Each step shattered the ground beneath him, sending shockwaves rippling outward, flattening what little still stood tall.</p>

<p>He then swung his bone sword in a wide arc, releasing another storm of cursed flames, but this time, mixed with black lightning.</p>

<p>The attack spread like a tide, devouring everything that came in its path.</p>

<p>Ethan met him head-on.</p>

<p>But this time, Agros didn’t rely on just brute force.</p>

<p>His eyes burned as he roared strange words that twisted and shook the air itself.</p>

<p>“Undead Summon!”</p>

<p>The bone sword screamed.</p>

<p>And from its blade, thousands of bones burst outward. They twisted and snapped together midair, forming numerous humanoid shapes.</p>

<p>Hollow eyes burned with sickly green fire as the undead surged forward in a wave.</p>

<p>Ethan felt a chill down his spine seeing the world.</p>

<p>‘Damn!So it was a living shield…How can such a thing exist..’</p>

<p>Meanwhile Agros slashed again.</p>

<p>A black arc shot forward, carrying the weight of countless deaths. The ground it passed over withered instantly, turning everything around it gray and lifeless.</p>

<p>“Death Shot!”</p>

<p>A concentrated beam of pure decay was fired straight toward Ethan.</p>

<p>For a moment, a faint sense of unease crawled into his heart, but he shook his head.</p>

<p>“My people are standing behind me. I can’t step back or evade nor I can lose”</p>

<p>“I refuse to lose.”</p>

<p>“I won’t be defeated!” Ethan roared.</p>

<p>BOOOOOOM!</p>

<p>Light exploded behind him.</p>

<p>The air rippled as a humanoid figure of pure light formed behind him.And within its hand was a sword identical to Ethan’s, its edges glowing with an unbearable brilliance.</p>

<p>Then, a pillar of light descended from the sky.</p>

<p>The figure behind Ethan raised both his hands and grabbed it before swinging it like a weapon.</p>

<p>The pillar slammed into Agros.</p>

<p>The clash erased sound.</p>

<p>For an instant, everything within the range of several kilometers vanished. Buildings, rubble, fire, and even air itself, all vaporized.</p>

<p>The shockwave expanded outward, flattening the land and tearing the sky apart. Wind screamed as it shot across the battlefield, ripping everything in its path into nothing.</p>

<p>Ethan attacked with everything he had.</p>

<p>But a Death Shot, hidden within all that chaos, slammed into his side.</p>

<p>“Arghh!”</p>

<p>His body was sent flying backward like a rag doll until he smashed through ruined buildings and skidded across the ground for kilometers before finally coming to a stop.</p>

<p>“Cough!”</p>

<p>Blood spilled from his mouth as he forced himself upright and looked down, only to freeze.</p>

<p>Half of his armor had been blasted into tatters.</p>

<p>Burnt flesh exposed his ribs, and bits of bone could be seen beneath the charred skin.</p>

<p>“Fuckkkk!” Ethan roared, fury exploding within his chest.</p>

<p>“If this leaves a scar, Sophia is going to kill me!”</p>

<p>Even now, the fear in his voice was real.</p>

<p>But the pain didn’t last.</p>

<p>Golden light flowed from his chest. Soft holy hymns echoed as flesh knitted itself back together.</p>

<p>The burnt skin regenerated, and the exposed ribs were once again covered beneath the newly formed muscle.</p>

<p>Ethan exhaled sharply, wiped the sweat from his brow, and looked ahead.</p>

<p>He was starting to get pissed.</p>

<p>“You fucking piece of shit… I am gonna kill you.”</p>

<p>As he roared, terrifying power erupted from his body.</p>
