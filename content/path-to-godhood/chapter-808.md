<p>BOOOOM!</p>
<p>The impact exploded between them, but this time Ethan did not continue with another punch. He twisted his body and drove his shoulder forward.</p>
<p>The green-robed figure shifted his feet. His body moved strangely, almost like his bones had no weight. Ethan’s shoulder passed beside him.</p>
<p>The green-robed figure immediately turned his palm and struck Ethan’s chest.</p>
<p>BAM!</p>
<p>The palm strike pushed Ethan backward several meters. Ethan dug his feet into the air and stopped himself.</p>
<p>He looked at the man carefully.</p>
<p>"Different..."</p>
<p>The green-robed figure did not react. He simply raised one hand, his fingers relaxed and his palm facing Ethan.</p>
<p>Then he disappeared.</p>
<p>Ethan’s eyes widened. The man’s movement was completely different from before. Instead of rushing straight at him, he moved in a curved path, his body shifting from one position to another like a shadow drifting through the wind.</p>
<p>Ethan swung his fist, but the green-robed figure slipped past it. His palm struck Ethan’s ribs.</p>
<p>BAM!</p>
<p>Before Ethan could react, another palm struck his shoulder, then his chest, then his stomach. Each strike was short and precise, with no wasted movement.</p>
<p>Ethan’s body was pushed backward with every hit, but he simply endured them. His Draconic scales thickened across his skin before he suddenly grabbed the man’s wrist.</p>
<p>The green-robed figure’s eyes moved slightly.</p>
<p>Ethan pulled him closer.</p>
<p>"Got you."</p>
<p>He swung his other fist, but the green-robed figure bent backward and let the fist pass over his face. Instead of retreating, he rotated his body around Ethan’s arm and placed his palm against Ethan’s elbow.</p>
<p>With a sudden twist, he redirected Ethan’s own momentum.</p>
<p>Ethan was thrown sideways.</p>
<p>BOOM!</p>
<p>He crashed through the ground and rolled several times. The green-robed figure landed lightly, his robe barely moving.</p>
<p>Ethan slowly stood. His eyes became brighter.</p>
<p>"So you like using technique..."</p>
<p>Flames erupted around his body.</p>
<p>"Then let’s see how you deal with strength."</p>
<p>Ethan stomped the ground.</p>
<p>BOOOM!</p>
<p>He launched himself forward like a cannonball. The green-robed figure raised both palms as Ethan’s fist came down like a hammer.</p>
<p>The man blocked it with both hands.</p>
<p>The instant they collided, the ground beneath him collapsed.</p>
<p>BOOM!</p>
<p>The green-robed figure was forced downward, but he used Ethan’s arm as leverage and twisted his body. One foot landed against Ethan’s chest.</p>
<p>He pushed Ethan with the kick.</p>
<p>Ethan was thrown backward.</p>
<p>Before Ethan could recover, the green-robed figure followed. His movement was silent as he appeared behind Ethan and struck his back with an open palm.</p>
<p>BAM!</p>
<p>Ethan’s body lurched forward. He turned around immediately, but the green-robed figure was already moving again.</p>
<p>Ethan swung his fist, but the man stepped inside the attack. His elbow struck Ethan’s ribs, then his knee hit Ethan’s thigh before his palm slammed into Ethan’s chin.</p>
<p>Three attacks came almost at the same time.</p>
<p>Ethan’s head tilted backward, but instead of being pushed away, he grabbed the man’s waist. The green-robed figure’s expression finally changed.</p>
<p>Ethan lifted him.</p>
<p>"Gotcha!"</p>
<p>He slammed him into the ground.</p>
<p>BOOOOOOM!</p>
<p>The earth exploded. A huge crater formed beneath the green-robed figure.</p>
<p>Ethan didn’t give him time to recover. He jumped down and drove his fist toward the crater, but the green-robed figure rolled aside.</p>
<p>Ethan’s fist struck the ground.</p>
<p>BOOM!</p>
<p>A huge chunk of earth was blasted apart. The green-robed figure rose from the other side, his body moving like water.</p>
<p>He stepped forward and struck Ethan’s wrist, then his elbow and shoulder. Each hit was aimed at a different joint.</p>
<p>Ethan’s arm suddenly became numb.</p>
<p>The man followed with a palm toward his chest.</p>
<p>Ethan caught the palm.</p>
<p>The two stared at each other.</p>
<p>Ethan’s grip tightened. The green-robed figure’s fingers suddenly curled as his wrist rotated at an impossible angle.</p>
<p>A strange force traveled through Ethan’s arm.</p>
<p>Ethan’s expression changed. The man’s palm technique wasn’t simply using physical strength. The force was penetrating through his scales.</p>
<p>Ethan released him and jumped backward.</p>
<p>The green-robed figure didn’t chase. He simply stood calmly.</p>
<p>Ethan shook his arm.</p>
<p>"What was that force?"</p>
<p>"Fist intent?"</p>
<p>His flames grew larger.</p>
<p>Ethan lowered his stance.</p>
<p>The green-robed figure remained silent.</p>
<p>Ethan grinned.His body suddenly disappeared.</p>
<p>This time Ethan didn’t rush straight toward him. He moved unpredictably, and his fighting style had completely changed.</p>
<p>Instead of relying on clean sword movements or magic, he fought like a beast. He charged low, then suddenly changed direction.</p>
<p>His fist came from below.</p>
<p>The green-robed figure shifted his head and the fist missed.</p>
<p>But Ethan’s elbow followed.</p>
<p>The green-robed figure blocked it. Ethan grabbed his arm as the green-robed figure tried to twist free.</p>
<p>Ethan simply pulled harder.</p>
<p>Their bodies crashed together.</p>
<p>Ethan drove his forehead into the man’s face.</p>
<p>BANG!</p>
<p>The green-robed figure staggered. Ethan followed with a knee, but the man blocked it.</p>
<p>Ethan kicked his leg away and slammed his fist into his stomach.</p>
<p>BOOM!</p>
<p>The green-robed figure was pushed backward, but he immediately recovered. His feet touched the ground as he stepped forward.</p>
<p>His entire body suddenly became light.</p>
<p>He moved around Ethan in a strange circular motion.</p>
<p>One palm.</p>
<p>Two palms.</p>
<p>Three.</p>
<p>Ethan blocked the first. The second struck his shoulder, while the third hit his ribs.</p>
<p>Then the green-robed figure spun and struck him with his elbow.</p>
<p>Ethan raised his arm.</p>
<p>CRACK!</p>
<p>The elbow collided with his scales. The impact forced Ethan’s arm backward.</p>
<p>But Ethan caught the man’s sleeve and pulled towards him.</p>
<p>The green-robed figure stumbled forward.</p>
<p>Ethan’s knee slammed into his stomach.</p>
<p>BAM!</p>
<p>Before the man could bend over, Ethan grabbed the back of his head and smashed his forehead against Ethan’s own.</p>
<p>BANG!</p>
<p>Both staggered.</p>
<p>Ethan shook his head. The green-robed figure wiped blood from his nose.</p>
<p>For the first time, both of them were breathing heavily.</p>
<p>Then the green-robed figure raised his hand.</p>
<p>The air around his palm began twisting. Ethan’s expression became serious as he felt the difference.</p>
<p>The man’s previous attacks had been precise.</p>
<p>This one was completely different.</p>
<p>The green-robed figure stepped forward. His palm moved slowly, yet Ethan felt danger approaching.</p>
<p>Ethan clenched his fist.</p>
<p>Flames gathered around his arm.</p>
<p>The two moved at the same time.</p>
<p>One used a flowing palm.</p>
<p>The other used a straight, overwhelming punch.</p>
<p>Their attacks met.</p>
<p>BOOOOOOOOM!</p>
<p>The resulting force exploded across the battlefield.</p>
<p>The green-robed figure’s flowing technique tried to redirect Ethan’s strength, but Ethan simply forced his way through it.</p>
<p>His arm shook violently.</p>
<p>The green-robed figure’s palm trembled.</p>
