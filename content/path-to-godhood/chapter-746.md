<p>The moment the void sealed shut, the entire battlefield fell into complete silence.</p>
<p>The space around them turned pitch black as if the world itself had been swallowed whole. The sky, the ground, and even the air seemed frozen. Not even a ripple of spiritual energy could escape.</p>
<p>Everyone instinctively tried to move, but the surrounding space had become as solid as an unbreakable prison.</p>
<p>Crack!</p>
<p>Several Transcendent Realm experts immediately unleashed their full strength, trying to shatter the sealed space but nothing happened.</p>
<p>Their attacks disappeared into the darkness without leaving behind even the slightest crack.</p>
<p>An Ascension Realm expert might have been able to lock a small area for a short time, but locking an entire battlefield containing so many powerful experts was almost impossible.</p>
<p>Alderic and Caelus slowly lowered his sword.</p>
<p>His face had completely lost its color.</p>
<p>"So... this is it."</p>
<p>The others wore equally pale expressions.</p>
<p>Their hearts sank to the bottom.</p>
<p>They had already given up.</p>
<p>They were done for.</p>
<p>It’s fucking over.</p>
<p>At that moment, the darkness ahead suddenly began to twist.</p>
<p>A terrifying pressure descended upon everyone.</p>
<p>The black void slowly split apart, and a massive silhouette stepped out from within.</p>
<p>Every step he took made the sealed space tremble.</p>
<p>An enormous demon with crimson skin, curved black horns, and burning golden eyes emerged from the darkness. Black flames flowed around his body like living creatures, and his overwhelming aura crushed everyone present.</p>
<p>His gaze slowly swept across the battlefield before a cruel smile appeared on his face.</p>
<p>"I am Agros, the First Devotee of the Lord of the Second Layer of Hell."</p>
<p>His deep voice echoed throughout the sealed void like the judgment of death.</p>
<p>"I have finally descended to this world."</p>
<p>The moment Ethan heard those words, his entire body shook.</p>
<p>His heart nearly stopped beating.</p>
<p>’I know him...’</p>
<p>The memory instantly resurfaced in his mind.</p>
<p>During his battle against the King of Ruthiana, a projection of this very demon had appeared before him.</p>
<p>Back then, that projection had already been terrifying.</p>
<p>But compared to the being standing before him now it was nothing.</p>
<p>This Agros was incomparably stronger.</p>
<p>Before anyone could recover from the shock, a black-robed figure suddenly appeared before Agros.</p>
<p>Without the slightest hesitation, he dropped to one knee and lowered his head deeply.</p>
<p>"My Lord Agros."</p>
<p>His voice trembled with excitement and reverence.</p>
<p>"You have finally descended."</p>
<p>"It is this servant’s greatest honor to witness your arrival."</p>
<p>"Everything has been prepared exactly as you commanded."</p>
<p>"The path has been opened, and all obstacles have already been removed."</p>
<p>"Welcome, my Lord."</p>
<p>Agros merely gave a small nod.</p>
<p>His indifferent eyes looked at the kneeling figure for a brief moment before shifting away.</p>
<p>His gaze slowly moved across everyone standing there.</p>
<p>Every person he looked at felt their blood run cold.</p>
<p>Then...</p>
<p>His eyes stopped on a young man with white hair.</p>
<p>A smile slowly spread across his face.</p>
<p>"Finally found!"</p>
<p>Ethan’s heart instantly became frigid.</p>
<p>A cold chill ran through his entire body.</p>
<p>His fists unconsciously tightened as countless thoughts flashed through his mind.</p>
<p>Beside him, Queen Ophelia frowned deeply while staring at Agros.</p>
<p>"Just how many people did you offend?"</p>
<p>"First it was the Emperor and Crown Prince of Arcadia, and now it is someone from Hell."</p>
<p>Ethan forced out a stiff smile.</p>
<p>"Should we be worried about our life?"</p>
<p>"There is no need to worry about it," Queen Ophelia shook her head.</p>
<p>Ethan’s eyes immediately lit up.</p>
<p>’Do they have a trump card?’</p>
<p>Just as a small spark of hope appeared in his heart, Queen Ophelia calmly spoke again.</p>
<p>"Because we are already done for."</p>
<p>"..."</p>
<p>The little hope inside Ethan disappeared instantly.</p>
<p>The surrounding experts remained silent.</p>
<p>No one could refute her words.</p>
<p>Facing a being who could lock the entire battlefield inside a sealed void, even the strongest among them could only wait for whatever fate awaited them.</p>
<p>Agros slowly looked around at everyone standing before him.</p>
<p>His golden eyes were completely indifferent, as if he were looking at insects rather than living beings.</p>
<p>A faint frown appeared on his face.</p>
<p>"I don’t like people standing before me."</p>
<p>The moment those words left his mouth, a terrifying pressure exploded from his body.</p>
<p>BOOM!</p>
<p>The invisible force crashed down over the entire battlefield like an endless mountain.</p>
<p>"Ugh!"</p>
<p>Everyone’s faces twisted in pain.</p>
<p>Crack!</p>
<p>The ground beneath them shattered as countless experts were forced to their knees.</p>
<p>Some struggled desperately to remain standing, but their legs trembled violently before finally giving out.</p>
<p>Bang!</p>
<p>One after another, they slammed onto the ground.</p>
<p>Even Alderic Caelus, Queen Ophelia, and the other peak experts could not resist that overwhelming pressure.</p>
<p>Veins bulged across their foreheads as they gritted their teeth with everything they had, yet they were still forced down.</p>
<p>Only Agros remained standing calmly in the silent battlefield.</p>
<p>Without another word, he casually flicked his hand.</p>
<p>His figure instantly disappeared.</p>
<p>Before anyone could react, he was already standing directly in front of Ethan.</p>
<p>Ethan’s pupils shrank to pinpoints.</p>
<p>He could not even see how Agros had moved.</p>
<p>Agros slowly extended one hand toward him.</p>
<p>A terrifying power gathered in his palm.</p>
<p>The surrounding space twisted violently as black energy condensed into a beam filled with destructive power.</p>
<p>Everyone’s expressions changed.</p>
<p>"No!"</p>
<p>"Ethan!"</p>
<p>Their hearts nearly stopped.</p>
<p>The next instant, the beam of destruction shot straight toward Ethan.</p>
<p>BOOM!</p>
<p>The terrifying attack reached him in an instant.</p>
<p>Everyone closed their eyes.</p>
<p>They thought it was over.</p>
<p>But the moment the beam touched Ethan’s body, it silently disappeared.</p>
<p>There was no explosion nor there was any destruction.</p>
<p>The terrifying power simply vanished into nothingness as if it had never existed.</p>
<p>The battlefield fell into complete silence.</p>
<p>Everyone stared blankly at Ethan.</p>
<p>Their eyes were filled with disbelief.</p>
<p>Even Agros’ expression changed.</p>
<p>His eyes darkened as he stared at Ethan.</p>
<p>Then, he slowly raised his head and looked up at the sky.</p>
<p>"Who is it?"</p>
<p>A calm yet commanding voice suddenly echoed from above.</p>
<p>"Don’t bully too much, Agros."</p>
<p>The tone was calm, but it carried unquestionable authority.</p>
<p>CRANG!</p>
<p>A deafening sound echoed throughout the sealed void.</p>
<p>Everyone instinctively looked up.</p>
<p>The pitch-black sky above them suddenly cracked apart like shattered glass following which bright light erupted in the sky.</p>
