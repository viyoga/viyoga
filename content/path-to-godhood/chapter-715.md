<p>The moment Haster’s gaze landed on the ancient battlefield, his expression instantly hardened as if he had seen a ghost.</p>

<p>His pupils shrank.For a brief moment, even his breathing stopped.</p>

<p>The reason was simple.Leading the charge were the four rulers themselves.</p>

<p>At the very front stood Emperor Caelus of the Xylos Empire, Emperor Aldric of the Morvath Empire, Emperor Ezekiel Von Valorant, and Queen Ophelia of Eldoria. Behind them flew countless powerful experts whose terrifying auras covered the sky. Their momentum was overwhelming as they descended together like an unstoppable tide. The pressure alone caused the air to tremble while violent waves of energy spread across the battlefield.</p>

<p>Haster felt his scalp go numb.</p>

<p>He had expected an attack.</p>

<p>He had even prepared for one.But he had never imagined that all four rulers would personally lead the assault from the very beginning.</p>

<p>The Allied Army clearly had no intention of testing the waters.</p>

<p>They wanted to crush everything in one decisive strike.</p>

<p>Haster’s expression immediately became fierce.</p>

<p>He gathered his strength and let out a thunderous roar that echoed throughout the entire battlefield.</p>

<p>"Everyone!"</p>

<p>"Attack with your full strength!"</p>

<p>"I don’t care about your realm!"</p>

<p>"I don’t care about your cultivation!"</p>

<p>"Every single one of you..."</p>

<p>"Charge!"</p>

<p>The command instantly spread across the Arcadia camp.</p>

<p>Without the slightest hesitation, countless soldiers rushed forward.</p>

<p>Heavy infantry raised their massive shields before charging toward the enemy lines. Cavalry units urged their mounts forward while archers quickly formed ranks behind them. Mages began chanting one spell after another as brilliant lights gathered above their staffs. Commanders drew their weapons and personally led their troops into battle while nobles released their powerful auras and shot toward the approaching Allied experts.</p>

<p>The entire Arcadia Army moved at once.</p>

<p>War cries echoed across the battlefield.</p>

<p>The ground shook beneath the charge of countless soldiers.</p>

<p>Powerful figures streaked across the sky from both sides as they rushed toward one another at incredible speed. Waves of energy collided high above the battlefield while the space between the two armies rapidly disappeared. The momentum of the battle became greater with every passing second as more experts joined the charge without holding anything back.</p>

<p>Even so...</p>

<p>The Allied Army showed no signs of slowing down.</p>

<p>With the four rulers leading from the front, their formation remained perfectly organized as they continued advancing with overwhelming force. Their momentum resembled a massive wave crashing toward the shore, threatening to sweep away everything standing before it.</p>

<p>Seeing this, Haster’s expression became increasingly ugly.</p>

<p>His fists slowly tightened.</p>

<p>A Transcendent Realm knight hurried over and stopped beside him.</p>

<p>The man’s face had already turned pale.</p>

<p>He looked toward the approaching Allied Army before speaking with obvious panic.</p>

<p>"What should we do now?"</p>

<p>He gritted his teeth.</p>

<p>"Such fierce momentum..."</p>

<p>"We can’t keep up."</p>

<p>Haster remained silent.</p>

<p>His sharp eyes continued watching the battlefield while countless thoughts raced through his mind.</p>

<p>For several long moments, he did not say a single word.</p>

<p>Finally...</p>

<p>He slowly clenched his fist.</p>

<p>His face became filled with unwillingness and anger.</p>

<p>Then, through gritted teeth, he muttered in a frustrated voice,</p>

<p>"Retreat!"</p>

<p>"We need to retreat!"</p>

<p>The four rulers advanced at the very front of the Allied Army without slowing down even once.</p>

<p>Behind them were many Transcendent Realm experts along with countless Divine Knights, Saint Realm cultivators, commanders, and elite soldiers. Their formation remained perfectly organized as they pushed across the ancient battlefield like an unstoppable wall. Every step they took caused the ground to tremble, and the combined pressure of so many powerful experts made the surrounding air feel incredibly heavy.</p>

<p>Any resistance that appeared before them was quickly swept aside.</p>

<p>The Arcadia soldiers desperately tried to block their advance, but the difference in strength was simply too great. The Transcendent experts leading the charge broke through one defensive line after another while the elite knights behind them immediately widened the opening. Their advance was steady and relentless as they bulldozed their way toward the heart of the Arcadia camp.</p>

<p>Seeing the Allied Army rapidly closing in, the Arcadia commanders immediately reacted.</p>

<p>"Stop them!"</p>

<p>"Don’t let them advance any further!"</p>

<p>"Archers!"</p>

<p>"Mages!"</p>

<p>"Prepare the combined attack!"</p>

<p>The orders spread across the battlefield in an instant.Thousands of soldiers immediately moved into formation.</p>

<p>Rows of archers raised their bows while countless mages lifted their staffs toward the sky. Brilliant lights gathered one after another as enormous amounts of energy condensed above the battlefield. The air began vibrating violently as hundreds of spells merged together into a single overwhelming attack.</p>

<p>Within moments...</p>

<p>The sky itself seemed to light up.</p>

<p>Thousands of arrows wrapped in spiritual energy shot forward together.</p>

<p>At the same time, countless fireballs, ice spears, lightning bolts, wind blades, and massive stones surged toward the advancing Allied Army. The attacks merged into a gigantic wave that covered the sky as though it wanted to swallow everything beneath it.</p>

<p>The terrifying barrage crashed toward the Allied forces with unstoppable momentum.</p>

<p>At that very moment...</p>

<p>Queen Ophelia calmly stepped forward.</p>

<p>Her expression remained gentle, and there was not the slightest trace of panic on her beautiful face.</p>

<p>She slowly raised one hand.</p>

<p>Behind her, the elite mage squads of Eldoria immediately understood her intention.</p>

<p>Without wasting a single second, hundreds of mages simultaneously raised their staffs.</p>

<p>Their voices echoed together as they completed their spell.</p>

<p>Brilliant streams of light instantly gathered before Queen Ophelia.</p>

<p>A massive barrier rapidly expanded outward, covering the entire advancing force.</p>

<p>The enormous shield shimmered with countless magical patterns as it stood firmly before the Allied Army.</p>

<p>BOOM!</p>

<p>The combined attack slammed into the barrier.</p>

<p>Violent explosions erupted one after another.</p>

<p>The sky shook continuously while waves of energy spread in every direction. Fire, ice, lightning, and countless arrows struck the barrier without stopping, yet the enormous shield only rippled as it absorbed the terrifying impact.</p>

<p>Not a single attack managed to break through.</p>

<p>Not a single Allied soldier behind the barrier was touched.</p>

<p>The Arcadia soldiers looked on in disbelief.</p>

<p>Many of them had poured every bit of strength into that attack.</p>

<p>Yet it had achieved almost nothing.</p>

<p>The explosions gradually faded.</p>

<p>The battlefield became quiet for only a brief moment.</p>

<p>Then...</p>

<p>Emperor Ezekiel slowly stepped forward.</p>

<p>His blood-red eyes swept across the countless Arcadia soldiers standing ahead.</p>

<p>A cruel smile slowly spread across his face.</p>

<p>He raised his hand without the slightest hesitation.</p>

<p>"Flatten them."</p>

<p>His cold voice echoed across the battlefield.</p>

<p>"Leave nothing standing in our way."</p>

<p>The next moment...</p>

<p>The Transcendent experts behind him exploded forward.</p>
