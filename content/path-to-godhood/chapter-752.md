<p>If Ethan could call upon such a terrifying future self again, what would happen if he ever turned against them?</p>

<p>They would probably all die.</p>

<p>"The real work will begin now."</p>

<p>Ethan remained there for a moment, quietly watching them leave.</p>
<p>The battlefield had finally become silent.</p>
<p>But the silence would not erase what had happened here.</p>
<p>The mountains had been destroyed. The land had been turned into a massive wasteland. Ash and soot covered the ground while broken weapons and pieces of armor remained scattered everywhere. The traces of blood and energy had already soaked deep into the earth.</p>
