<p>The darkness surrounding Haster continued expanding without end.</p>
<p>The thick miasma rolled across the battlefield like a living ocean while countless strands of black mist gathered around him. His countless eyes slowly swept across Ethan before a twisted smile appeared on his face once again.</p>
<p>Without saying a single word, Haster slowly raised one hand.</p>
<p>The surrounding miasma immediately responded.</p>
<p>It violently churned and gathered together from every direction. The endless black mist rapidly compressed while the entire valley trembled under the overwhelming pressure. Within only a few breaths, the miasma began taking shape.</p>
<p>First came a pair of enormous legs.</p>
<p>Then a massive torso.</p>
<p>Long arms slowly emerged from the darkness, followed by a featureless head that towered high above the battlefield.</p>
<p>A gigantic humanoid figure made entirely of dark miasma stood behind Haster.</p>
<p>It looked like an ancient giant born from endless darkness.</p>
<p>Its body constantly shifted as black mist flowed across its surface while countless faint faces briefly appeared and disappeared within it. Its enormous form silently overlooked the battlefield, making everyone below feel incredibly small.</p>
<p>The surrounding Transcendent Knights looked up with shock.</p>
<p>"What the hell is that..."</p>
<p>Even Heliana’s expression became much more serious.</p>
<p>Haster slowly pointed toward Ethan.The giant immediately moved.</p>
<p>Boom!</p>
<p>One enormous foot crashed onto the ground, shaking the entire valley.</p>
<p>The next moment, the gigantic humanoid stretched out one massive hand toward Ethan.</p>
<p>The huge palm swept across the battlefield with overwhelming force, covering everything beneath it.</p>
<p>Ethan’s eyes narrowed.</p>
<p>Without hesitation, he pushed off the ground.</p>
<p>Boom!</p>
<p>His figure instantly disappeared from where he stood.</p>
<p>The giant hand slammed into the earth.</p>
<p>Boom!</p>
<p>The ground collapsed beneath the impact as countless rocks exploded into the air. A massive crater instantly appeared where Ethan had been standing only a moment earlier.</p>
<p>Before Ethan could regain his balance...</p>
<p>The gigantic humanoid suddenly moved again.</p>
<p>Its other arm was already descending from above.Ethan immediately looked upward.A huge black palm was falling straight toward him.</p>
<p>There was almost no room left to dodge.</p>
<p>Boom!</p>
<p>The enormous palm struck him head-on.</p>
<p>The terrifying impact slammed Ethan into the rocky ground below.</p>
<p>The earth exploded.</p>
<p>A violent shockwave swept across the battlefield while broken rocks and thick clouds of dust shot high into the sky.</p>
<p>The enormous hand remained firmly pressed against the ground.</p>
<p>Everyone’s expressions immediately changed.</p>
<p>"Marquise Ethan!"</p>
<p>Several Transcendent Knights instinctively shouted.</p>
<p>Heliana tightly gripped her crystal staff.</p>
<p>Her eyes remained fixed on the gigantic hand without blinking.</p>
<p>For a brief moment...</p>
<p>The entire battlefield became silent.</p>
<p>Then...</p>
<p>A faint white glow appeared beneath the enormous black palm.</p>
<p>At first it was only a tiny point of light.</p>
<p>Then it rapidly became brighter and brighter.</p>
<p>The holy light quickly spread through the cracks beneath the giant hand until dazzling rays burst outward in every direction.</p>
<p>Boom!</p>
<p>A pillar of brilliant light suddenly erupted from beneath the enormous palm.</p>
<p>The surrounding darkness was instantly forced backward.</p>
<p>The gigantic black hand trembled violently.</p>
<p>Crack!</p>
<p>A glowing hand suddenly burst through the center of the giant palm.</p>
<p>Its fingers tightly grabbed the dark miasma before powerful holy light rapidly spread across it.</p>
<p>The black hand immediately began breaking apart as white cracks spread throughout its enormous surface.</p>
<p>Everyone stared at the unbelievable scene.</p>
<p>The light continued growing stronger.</p>
<p>The pillar of holy light became brighter with every passing moment. It rose higher and higher while countless streams of radiant energy gathered together. The dazzling light slowly began taking shape, and what had once been a formless pillar gradually transformed into the outline of a gigantic humanoid figure.</p>
<p>First, a towering pair of legs formed from pure light.</p>
<p>Then a broad torso emerged.</p>
<p>Powerful arms slowly took shape while streams of golden-white energy continuously flowed across them. Finally, a noble head formed above the massive body, surrounded by countless rays of holy light.</p>
<p>The gigantic figure stood upright beneath the enormous black palm.</p>
<p>It looked completely opposite to Haster’s dark giant.</p>
<p>Where the giant of darkness radiated death and corruption, this radiant figure gave off warmth, purity, and an overwhelming holy presence. Its body shone like a small sun, and every ray of light that spread from it pushed the surrounding miasma farther away.</p>
<p>The giant slowly straightened its back.</p>
<p>Its glowing hand tightened around the dark palm pressing down upon it.</p>
<p>Crack!</p>
<p>Crack!</p>
<p>Crack!</p>
<p>More white cracks rapidly spread across the enormous black hand.</p>
<p>The dark miasma continuously leaked through the widening fractures while the holy light grew even stronger.</p>
<p>The surrounding battlefield shook violently.The two gigantic forces stood locked against one another.</p>
<p>Darkness pressed downward.Light steadily pushed upward.Everyone watched the unbelievable scene with wide eyes.</p>
<p>Even the monstrous humans stopped fighting for a brief moment.Haster’s countless eyes narrowed at once.</p>
<p>The smile on his face slowly disappeared.</p>
<p>For the first time since the battle had begun, a trace of surprise appeared across his twisted face.</p>
<p>Heliana looked at the towering figure of light and quietly tightened her grip on her crystal staff.</p>
<p>A faint smile slowly appeared on her face.</p>
<p>"So..."</p>
<p>"He finally reached this stage."</p>
<p>The radiant giant continued rising while its holy light spread farther across the battlefield. The surrounding darkness retreated wave after wave as though it could no longer withstand the overwhelming brilliance pouring from the giant’s body.</p>
<p>The radiant giant continued rising while its holy light spread farther across the battlefield. Wave after wave of brilliant energy rolled outward, forcing the surrounding darkness to retreat.</p>
<p>Wherever the holy light reached, the poisonous miasma rapidly dissolved as though it could no longer exist beneath such overwhelming purity.</p>
<p>The mountains trembled continuously while the valley became divided into two worlds. One side was swallowed by endless darkness, while the other was illuminated by blinding holy light.</p>
<p>The enormous black hand pressing down on the radiant giant gradually reached its limit.</p>
<p>Crack!</p>
<p>Crack!</p>
<p>Crack!</p>
<p>White cracks rapidly spread across its entire surface. Thick streams of dark miasma leaked out through the widening fractures while the holy light continued growing stronger. The giant hand trembled violently before finally reaching its breaking point.</p>
<p>Boom!</p>
