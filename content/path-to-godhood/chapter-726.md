<p>Ethan stared at the dark figure formed from the endless miasma and let out a cold scoff.</p>
<p>Using power gained through evil means to contend against him was nothing more than a pipe dream. If this had happened before, there might have been a chance. But now, everything was different. He had already comprehended the Law of Light. This was no longer a battle that could be decided by raw strength alone.</p>
<p>The Law of Light was one of the fundamental laws of Heaven.It was the source of life.</p>
<p>The hope that guided all living beings through darkness.</p>
<p>The power that protected life instead of destroying it.</p>
<p>It purified corruption, drove away evil, and illuminated every corner where darkness tried to spread. It was a force that resonated with Heaven itself, and it naturally rejected anything born from hatred, death, and corruption.</p>
<p>Someone with an impure heart might find it difficult to wield such power. Even if they forced themselves to use it, the Law of Light would never reveal its true strength to them.</p>
<p>But Ethan was different.</p>
<p>He never used this power for selfish reasons.</p>
<p>He fought to protect others.</p>
<p>He fought to clear away the lingering emotions of the countless dead trapped within the miasma. He wanted to free their souls from endless suffering and allow them to finally rest in peace.</p>
<p>As those thoughts filled his heart, the holy light surrounding him became even brighter.</p>
<p>Its radiance increased severalfold.</p>
<p>The entire valley was illuminated as wave after wave of pure holy light spread across the battlefield. The surrounding darkness was pushed back once again while the poisonous miasma rapidly dissolved wherever the light reached.</p>
<p>Behind Ethan, the towering Knight of Light slowly lowered its radiant sword.</p>
<p>Then...</p>
<p>It released the hilt.</p>
<p>Instead of drawing another weapon, the giant calmly extended one armored hand toward the dark figure.</p>
<p>Its fingers slowly curled inward.</p>
<p>With a single motion...</p>
<p>It beckoned the dark figure to come forward.</p>
<p>The golden lights within its helmet calmly stared at the giant of darkness.</p>
<p>The silent gesture was filled with complete confidence.</p>
<p>It was also a clear mockery.</p>
<p>Haster’s expression instantly twisted with rage.</p>
<p>His countless eyes became bloodshot while black veins spread across his monstrous face.</p>
<p>"ETHAAAANNNNNN!"</p>
<p>His furious roar shook the entire valley.</p>
<p>"BASTARD! I WILL KILL YOU AND HAVE YOUR BLOOD!"</p>
<p>His angry and menacing voice echoed throughout the battlefield while endless darkness burst from his body.</p>
<p>The giant dark figure immediately rushed forward.</p>
<p>Boom!</p>
<p>Each step caused the ground to tremble violently.</p>
<p>It raised its enormous fist and threw it straight toward the Knight of Light with enough force to flatten everything before it.</p>
<p>The Knight of Light calmly lowered its body.</p>
<p>Whoosh!</p>
<p>The gigantic punch swept just above its helmet.</p>
<p>The dark figure was completely missed.</p>
<p>Before it could react...</p>
<p>Boom!</p>
<p>The Knight of Light drove a heavy punch straight into its abdomen.The impact caused the dark giant to stagger backward.</p>
<p>Before it could regain its balance, another punch smashed into its chest.</p>
<p>Bang!</p>
<p>Then another struck its jaw.</p>
<p>Boom!</p>
<p>The three punches landed one after another without giving the dark figure a single moment to react. Every blow released a violent shockwave that spread across the battlefield while pieces of dark miasma burst away from the giant’s body.</p>
<p>The surrounding Transcendent Knights stared with wide eyes.</p>
<p>"It is... boxing?"</p>
<p>None of them had expected the gigantic Holy Knight to fight in such a direct and overwhelming way.</p>
<p>The dark figure angrily swung both fists toward the Knight of Light.</p>
<p>This time the Knight of Light moved with astonishing speed.</p>
<p>It stepped to the side with smooth footwork and completely avoided the attacks. Its movements were clean and precise while every step flowed naturally into the next.</p>
<p>Within a single breath, it had already circled behind the dark figure.</p>
<p>Before the giant could even turn around...The Knight of Light wrapped both powerful arms tightly around its waist.</p>
<p>The giant of darkness struggled violently.</p>
<p>Black miasma erupted from its body as it tried to break free.But the Knight of Light did not move even an inch.</p>
<p>It slowly bent its knees.</p>
<p>Then with overwhelming strength...It lifted the enormous dark figure completely off the ground.</p>
<p>The battlefield instantly fell silent.Everyone stared upward in disbelief.</p>
<p>The giant of darkness was actually being carried into the air.</p>
<p>The next moment...</p>
<p>Boom!</p>
<p>The Knight of Light violently slammed the dark figure onto its back.</p>
<p>The impact shook the entire valley.</p>
<p>A massive shockwave exploded outward while enormous cracks spread through the earth.Mountains trembled violently and countless rocks were thrown into the sky by the terrifying force.</p>
<p>The giant of darkness let out a furious roar as it struggled to stand.It never got the chance.</p>
<p>The Knight of Light immediately climbed on top of it.Then its armored fists came crashing down one after another.</p>
<p>Boom!</p>
<p>Boom!</p>
<p>Boom!</p>
<p>Each punch slammed heavily into the giant’s skull and chest. Every strike shattered large portions of dark miasma while the ground beneath them continued collapsing under the relentless assault. The giant of darkness struggled wildly beneath the Knight of Light, but it could not break free.</p>
<p>Finally...</p>
<p>The Knight of Light grabbed the dark figure with both hands.</p>
<p>It firmly held the giant in place.</p>
<p>Then an endless holy light erupted from its body.</p>
<p>Boom!</p>
<p>A blinding pillar of radiant light shot into the sky.</p>
<p>The entire battlefield was instantly covered in brilliant white light.</p>
<p>The dark miasma trapped within the giant rapidly began dissolving. Countless streams of corrupted energy were purified before everyone’s eyes while painful cries echoed from within the dark figure. The remaining miasma scattered in every direction before disappearing into nothingness beneath the overwhelming holy power.</p>
<p>Haster’s countless eyes widened.</p>
<p>His face was filled with shock and disbelief.</p>
<p>The dazzling holy light completely filled his vision.</p>
<p>For a brief moment the entire world before him turned white.</p>
