<p>However, the damage may not be very significant, but the humiliation he suffered was real. He had been full of confidence at the start, wanting to crush this insignificant human with ease. As a result, in the very first round of the battle, he suffered a small loss.</p>

<p>This was not just embarrassing. It was a disgrace for a mighty and ancient dragon like him.</p>

<p>The more he thought about it, the angrier he became. Volcanus’ massive body trembled slightly as his rage rose, and the lava around him surged violently in response.</p>

<p>“Insignificant human… you dare wound me?”</p>

<p>“I will erase your existence completely.”</p>

<p>As soon as the words fell, Volcanus carried a chilling killing intent and rushed straight towards Ethan.</p>

<p>It was obvious that he was ready to go all out. For beings like him, flame attacks and breath alone were not enough to crush someone with draconic blood. If he wanted to completely destroy Ethan, he had to tear him apart with his own claws.</p>

<p>Ethan narrowed his eyes as he saw the incoming dragon, and a faint smirk appeared before fading into a focused expression.</p>

<p>“You talk too much.”</p>

<p>“If you can kill me, then try.”</p>

<p>Facing the enraged Volcanus, Ethan wasn’t worried.He stepped forward in the air, his body flashing as he met the dragon head-on. His draconic aura surged, and faint crimson scales appeared across parts of his skin as he entered close combat.</p>

<p>For some reason, his blood was boiling with vigor.</p>

<p>The lava pool below did little to stop him. Instead, it seemed to enhance his strength, and waves of heat flowed into his body as if feeding his draconic bloodline.</p>

<p>Then the clash was instant.</p>

<p>Boom!</p>

<p>Ethan and Volcanus collided at an extremely fast speed, creating a shockwave that spread across the sky. The air cracked, and the lava below exploded upward like a storm.</p>

<p>Volcanus swung his massive claw, and Ethan barely raised his arm in time. The impact sent a sharp vibration through his bones, but he held his ground and countered with a punch infused with condensed energy.</p>

<p>At the same time, Volcanus twisted his body and slashed again. His claws tore through the air, grazing Ethan’s side and ripping apart part of his clothes along with flesh.</p>

<p>Blood scattered in the air.</p>

<p>Ethan gritted his teeth, his jaw tightening, but he did not stop. He grabbed onto one of Volcanus’ scales and drove his knee forward, striking the dragon’s body with force.</p>

<p>The two fought like mortal enemies.</p>

<p>Volcanus occasionally seized the opportunity and used his sharp claws to tear off pieces of flesh from Ethan, while Ethan relied on his speed and teleportation to avoid fatal strikes and counterattack from blind spots.</p>

<p>Amidst this, Ethan’s enjoyment reached its peak, and flashes of old scenes appeared in his mind. The memories of WWE moves and his childhood dreams surfaced for a brief moment.</p>

<p>His eyes sharpened.</p>

<p>His movements suddenly became quicker.</p>

<p>He rushed forward, his body lowering slightly, then he shot upward with force and threw a punch.</p>

<p>Superman Punch.</p>

<p>Boom!</p>

<p>The punch landed hard against Volcanus’ jaw, and even the massive dragon was caught off guard for a moment as his head jerked back slightly.</p>

<p>Ethan did not stop.</p>

<p>He twisted his body midair and grabbed onto the dragon’s horn, using it as leverage. With a sharp motion, he slammed his elbow down.</p>

<p>Elbow Strike.</p>

<p>The impact echoed, and sparks of molten lava scattered from the dragon’s scales.</p>

<p>Then he shifted again.</p>

<p>He pulled himself higher and slammed both fists downward with all his strength.</p>

<p>Boom!</p>

<p>The force pushed Volcanus slightly downward, causing the lava below to ripple violently.</p>

<p>Ethan’s breathing grew heavier, but his movements did not slow. He flipped his body and kicked forward with a spinning motion.</p>

<p>The spinning kick strike hit against the dragon’s face, sending another shockwave through the air.</p>

<p>For a brief moment, the scene looked unreal. A human figure was exchanging blows with a colossal ancient dragon, using raw physical combat mixed with skill and instinct.</p>

<p>However, the aftermath was clear.</p>

<p>Although Ethan’s attacks were powerful enough to shake mountains, they barely caused real damage to Volcanus. The dragon’s scales absorbed most of the impact, and only minor cracks and burns appeared.</p>

<p>The scene quickly turned brutal again.</p>

<p>The sky trembled with every collision, and the lava sea below churned violently. Heat waves mixed with shockwaves, turning the entire region into chaos.</p>

<p>They fought fiercely for more than ten minutes. However, due to the difference in strength and grade, and because Ethan’s control over his draconic power was still not as refined as Volcanus’, he gradually began to fall behind in this melee.</p>

<p>Ethan’s body flickered forward again, his speed cutting through the burning air. His eyes locked onto Volcanus’ head, and his leg tensed as energy gathered rapidly.</p>

<p>In the next instant, he twisted his body sharply and lashed out.</p>

<p>Super Kick.</p>

<p>Boom!</p>

<p>His foot slammed straight into Volcanus’ jaw with explosive force. The impact sent a shockwave rippling through the air, and even the massive dragon’s head snapped slightly to the side.</p>

<p>But Volcanus reacted immediately.</p>

<p>With a deep, furious growl, his enormous tail whipped around at terrifying speed.</p>

<p>Before Ethan could fully recover his stance, the tail struck him.</p>

<p>BOOOM!</p>

<p>The force was overwhelming. Ethan’s body was sent flying across the sky like a projectile, tearing through layers of heated air. The impact pressure alone made his vision blur for a moment.</p>

<p>He crashed toward a distant mountain.</p>

<p>At the last second, Ethan extended his arm and grabbed onto the rocky surface. His fingers dug deep into the stone, cracking it instantly as he forced himself to stop.</p>

<p>The entire mountain trembled under the sudden force.</p>

<p>Loose rocks broke apart and rolled down as Ethan hung there for a split second, his chest rising heavily.</p>

<p>Then his eyes sharpened.</p>

<p>Without hesitation, he twisted his body and pushed off the mountain with explosive force. His figure spun rapidly as he launched himself back toward Volcanus.</p>

<p>The air whistled around him.</p>

<p>In a blink, he closed the distance.</p>

<p>619.</p>

<p>His body spun midair, and his leg lashed out in a circular motion, carrying all the momentum he had built.</p>

<p>Boom!</p>

<p>The spinning kick slammed hard into Volcanus’ face. The impact echoed like thunder, and a visible shockwave burst outward as the dragon’s massive head was forced backward.</p>

<p>Lava splashed violently below, and the surrounding air shook from the force of the strike.</p>

<p>Ethan landed a short distance away, his feet skidding slightly in the air as he steadied himself, his gaze still locked onto the dragon.</p>

<p>His breathing became heavier, and more injuries appeared on his body. Blood ran down his arm, and faint burns covered parts of his skin.</p>

<p>Volcanus noticed it.</p>

<p>“You are reaching your limit.”</p>

<p>“In your dreams..” Ethan snorted, breathing heavily.</p>

<p>It would not be wrong to say he was exhausted.</p>

<p>They had been fighting for quite a long time, to the point that Ethan had even forgotten to keep track of time.</p>

<p>If not for his resilience and the Blessings of the Goddess of Light healing him slowly, this would have already turned into a dead battle.</p>

<p>Even though his punches carried terrifying might, enough to obliterate mountains, they hardly did any real damage to this huge dragon.</p>

<p>Ethan’s eyes flickered slightly as he moved back a little, creating distance.</p>

<p>He knew the dragon had a great deal of physical resistance against damage, so he had already been planning something in the background.</p>

<p>The treasure he looted and his earlier knowledge…</p>

<p>It was going to be useful now..</p>
