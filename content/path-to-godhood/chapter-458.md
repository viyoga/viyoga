<p>Their clash created a violent shockwave. Snow and dust burst outward in a circular wave and the surrounding stones cracked under the pressure.</p>
<p>Ethan felt it immediately.</p>
<p>The man had blocked his strike with only one hand.And he had done it effortlessly.</p>
<p>Even with the strength boosted by Battle Frenzy, Ethan’s attack had barely pushed the man half a step backward.</p>
<p>The masked man looked at Ethan with quiet curiosity.</p>
<p>“Interesting,” he said softly.</p>
<p>Ethan’s eyes narrowed.</p>
<p>He had already expected this.</p>
<p>A Mythic Rank opponent was far beyond an ordinary Legendary fighter.</p>
<p>But that did not mean he would simply give up.</p>
<p>Ethan twisted his body and launched another attack.This time his sword flashed forward.</p>
<p>CLANG! CLANG! CLANG!</p>
<p>A rapid exchange of strikes filled the night air.</p>
<p>Ethan attacked again and again while his sword cut through the air with incredible speed. Each strike carried enough power to break stone and tear through armor.</p>
<p>But the masked man avoided or blocked every attack with calm precision.</p>
<p>Sometimes he used his dagger.</p>
<p>Sometimes he simply stepped aside.</p>
<p>The difference in control was obvious.</p>
<p>Sometimes he simply stepped aside.</p>
<p>The difference in control was obvious.</p>
<p>After several exchanges, the man finally countered.His dagger flashed forward.</p>
<p>Ethan barely managed to tilt his body aside.</p>
<p>The blade grazed his ribs and cut through his coat again.</p>
<p>A thin line of blood appeared.</p>
<p>Ethan jumped back several meters and steadied his breathing.</p>
<p>The masked man lowered his dagger slightly.</p>
<p>“You fight well for someone at your level,” he said calmly.</p>
<p>“But this is pointless.”</p>
<p>Ethan wiped the blood from his side and gave a faint smile.</p>
<p>As he did, the wounds across his body slowly began to close.</p>
<p>The torn flesh knit together and the bleeding stopped.The masked man’s eyes narrowed slightly.</p>
<p>“Then why are you still standing there talking?” Ethan replied.</p>
<p>The masked man paused for a moment.</p>
<p>Then he sighed quietly.</p>
<p>He moved forward, but he had hardly taken a step when a sword cut through the air toward him.</p>
<p>The sudden attack forced him to dodge.</p>
<p>Ethan’s eyes flashed.</p>
<p>“Holy Sword Judgement!”</p>
<p>Brilliant light erupted around him.</p>
<p>Several glowing swords formed in the air, each one shining with intense holy energy. They shot forward and stabbed into the ground around the masked man.</p>
<p>BOOM! BOOM! BOOM!</p>
<p>The blades struck the earth in a circle and formed a radiant prison of light.</p>
<p>Chains of energy connected the swords and sealed the space inside.</p>
<p>For a brief moment the masked man was trapped.If the prison fully closed, the battle would end immediately.</p>
<p>But the man moved like a slippery eel.</p>
<p>His body twisted sharply and slipped through the narrowing gap between the swords. His movements were so precise and fast that he escaped just before the barrier completed.</p>
<p>He rolled away and landed outside the prison.</p>
<p>“Damn…” Ethan muttered under his breath.</p>
<p>This was the first time his sword judgement array failed.</p>
<p>Should I just use the bloodline blessing to gather strength?</p>
<p>If he used that power, the fight might end instantly.</p>
<p>But Ethan hesitated.</p>
<p>If more enemies appeared later, he could only use that power once, and using it too early could leave him vulnerable.</p>
<p>The masked man moved rapidly around the battlefield.He struck the light swords one by one while avoiding the glowing chains that connected them.</p>
<p>CLANG! CLANG!</p>
<p>Several blades shattered under his attacks.</p>
<p>“Those swords are dangerous,” the masked man said calmly while moving around the prison.</p>
<p>“But too bad you need to catch me first.”</p>
<p>As he spoke, the air around him suddenly changed.</p>
<p>Heavy pressure spread across the battlefield.</p>
<p>Even the wind seemed to freeze.Ethan felt it immediately.</p>
<p>This was the difference between their ranks.</p>
<p>The man had not even used his full strength yet.The masked man took one step forward.</p>
<p>The snow beneath his foot melted instantly under the pressure of his aura.</p>
<p>“You are strong for a Legendary knight,” he said.</p>
<p>“But you are still a Legendary knight.”</p>
<p>His figure vanished.</p>
<p>Ethan’s pupils shrank.</p>
<p>Too fast.</p>
<p>The masked man appeared directly behind him.The dagger moved silently toward Ethan’s neck.</p>
<p>But at the last moment Ethan twisted his body.</p>
<p>The blade missed his throat by a hair.</p>
<p>Ethan swung his elbow backward.The masked man blocked it again.But this time Ethan followed with a spinning slash.</p>
<p>The sword forced the man to step back slightly.</p>
<p>However the masked man turned around at the last moment and snapped his hand forward with heavy force.</p>
<p>The strike moved toward Ethan’s chest like a crushing hammer.</p>
<p>But before it could land—</p>
<p>An ancient hoarse voice suddenly echoed across the battlefield.</p>
<p>“Aren’t you ashamed dealing with kids who isn’t even one fifth of your age?”</p>
<p>The voice was old and calm.</p>
<p>But the power behind it was terrifying.An invisible force filled the air instantly.Both Ethan and the masked man froze in place.</p>
<p>The masked man suddenly felt a chill crawl down his spine.</p>
<p>A strange sense of déjà vu struck him.</p>
<p>Slowly, almost unwillingly, he lifted his head and looked toward the darkness beyond the ruined battlefield.</p>
<p>What he saw made his breathing stop.In the darkness of the sky, two eyes stared back at him.</p>
<p>They were deep, cold, and terrifying.</p>
<p>Those eyes carried a savage hunger that seemed capable of devouring everything.</p>
<p>The masked man’s knees weakened slightly.</p>
<p>“Those gazes…” he whispered.</p>
<p>“No… it can’t be…”</p>
<p>His voice trembled as realization struck him.</p>
<p>“Shouldn’t you be held up in Ruthiana?”</p>
<p>His breathing became uneven.</p>
<p>“So what is the demon of Abyssal doing here?”</p>
<p>For a moment the battlefield remained silent.</p>
<p>Then—</p>
<p>“HOHOOOO!”</p>
<p>A soft chuckle echoed through the night.</p>
<p>But the sound exploded like thunder across the frozen plain.The ground trembled slightly as the laughter rolled across the battlefield.</p>
<p>From the darkness beyond the broken pillars, a figure slowly stepped forward.</p>
<p>The man walked calmly, as if he had all the time in the world.</p>
<p>His long coat moved gently in the cold wind while his presence pressed heavily against the surroundings.</p>
<p>Even the air seemed to grow thicker as he approached.</p>
<p>Ethan watched carefully.The newcomer looked like an ordinary old man at first glance, but something about him felt completely different.</p>
<p>His aura was vast and ancient.</p>
<p>It felt like standing before a sleeping beast.</p>
<p>The old man placed his hands behind his back and looked toward the masked assassin with amusement in his eyes.</p>
<p>“Never knew someone remembered that old name of mine,” he said.</p>
<p>His voice carried a playful tone, but the power behind it was unmistakable.</p>
<p>Then he laughed again.</p>
<p>“Ahaha… what an honor to be remembered by those old times.”</p>
<p>The masked man expression tensed.</p>
