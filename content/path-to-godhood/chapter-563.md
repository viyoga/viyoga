<p>In his hand, a greatsword formed.</p>

<p>The blade hummed with power, and flames wrapped around it as if it were alive.</p>

<p>Solar Tyrant.</p>

<p>The moment he held it firmly, the flames intensified and spread wider, and a sizzling sound filled the air while heat radiated outward and distorted the surroundings.</p>

<p>His entire body erupted in flames, and every step he took created a fiery blast beneath his feet while shockwaves spread outward and the ground cracked and melted under the pressure.</p>

<p>BOOOOM!</p>

<p>He swung his sword, and a massive arc of fire tore through the air and slammed into the Orc Divine Knights while forcing them to retreat.</p>

<p>“Move!” one of them shouted quickly.</p>

<p>The five Divine Knights attacked together, and their movements were fast and coordinated as they surrounded Ethan from all directions while launching attacks one after another.</p>

<p>One struck from above while another came from the side, and the rest followed immediately while their attacks overlapped and aimed to crush him completely.</p>

<p>But Ethan did not fall back through the combined attack.</p>

<p>CLANG!</p>

<p>His sword collided with one strike, and sparks flew while the impact shook the surroundings, and then he turned immediately and slashed again while flames followed his blade and forced another Divine Knight back.</p>

<p>CRACK!</p>

<p>At the same time, lightning gathered around him again and burst outward suddenly, and the explosion pushed the others away while disrupting their formation.</p>

<p>He did not slow down.He kept pressing forward.</p>

<p>His sword moved continuously, and each swing carried both flame and force while cutting through everything in its path.</p>

<p>BOOOOM!</p>

<p>One Divine Knight tried to block, but the impact shattered his stance and sent him flying backward.</p>

<p>Another rushed in from behind, but Ethan turned instantly and struck with his fist, and the blow landed heavily while flames exploded outward from the impact.</p>

<p>CRACK!</p>

<p>The Orc’s body shook violently, and he was thrown aside like a ragdoll.</p>

<p>“Damn it!” one of them cursed loudly.</p>

<p>“This bastard—!”</p>

<p>They attacked again, and this time they went all out while their auras surged violently and their power reached its peak as they tried to suppress Ethan together.</p>

<p>But Ethan only became more intense.</p>

<p>His flames burned brighter, and his movements became faster while his attacks grew heavier with each passing moment.</p>

<p>He slashed and struck while casting magic without pause, and lightning bolts fell from the sky continuously while flames erupted from his blade, and the combination turned the battlefield into chaos.</p>

<p>BOOOOM! BOOOOM! BOOOOM!</p>

<p>The ground shattered again and again, and shockwaves spread in all directions while the surrounding area was torn apart completely.</p>

<p>One Divine Knight was struck directly and slammed into the ground, and another was forced to retreat while blood spilled from his mouth.</p>

<p>They were being pushed back.</p>

<p>Overpowered.</p>

<p>“This is insane…” one of them muttered, and disbelief filled his voice.</p>

<p>But then one of the Orc Divine Knights raised his hand and shouted.</p>

<p>“Enough!”</p>

<p>His voice cut through the chaos, and the others pulled back slightly while looking toward him.</p>

<p>“This is not working,” he said, and his tone turned serious while his expression hardened.</p>

<p>His body began to change.</p>

<p>His muscles swelled, and his aura surged violently while veins bulged across his body and his presence grew heavier.</p>

<p>The others followed.</p>

<p>Their bodies expanded, and their power increased as a strange energy wrapped around them, and their forms became larger and stronger while their eyes turned fierce and wild.</p>

<p>A different kind of power emerged.</p>

<p>They moved into position together, and their movements were precise as they formed a pattern around Ethan.</p>

<p>Five of them.</p>

<p>They decided to form an array.</p>

<p>The energy between them connected, and a dense pressure formed at the center while the ground beneath them cracked further and the air trembled under the force.</p>

<p>Ethan narrowed his eyes slightly as he looked at them, and his grip on the sword tightened while the flames around him flickered sharply.</p>

<p>And in the next moment, the array activated.</p>

<p>Ethan’s eyes narrowed the moment he felt the change, and his senses sharpened as he realized that he had been locked in place by the array that the Orc Divine Knights had formed around him.</p>

<p>The pressure around him increased rapidly, and a heavy force pressed down from all directions while cutting off his escape routes and limiting his movement, and even the air around him felt thick as if it was trying to crush him slowly.</p>

<p>This was dangerous.He could feel it clearly.</p>

<p>If he hesitated even for a moment, the situation could turn against him completely, and he knew that this was not the time to hold back or take things lightly.</p>

<p>Ethan did not dare to be careless, and his expression turned serious as his brows lowered slightly while he raised his hand without wasting even a second.</p>

<p>“Void Prison,” he muttered under his breath, and his voice was low but steady.</p>

<p>The next moment, the space around them twisted, and a dark ripple spread outward silently while covering the entire area, and the surroundings distorted as if reality itself had bent under his will.</p>

<p>Then everything froze.</p>

<p>The Orc Divine Knights felt it immediately, and their bodies stiffened as their movements slowed down unnaturally while a strange force wrapped around them and pressed from every direction.</p>

<p>It was not chains nor was it physical.</p>

<p>But it held them firmly in place, and the space around them itself felt locked as if they had been sealed inside something invisible.</p>

<p>The air turned heavy, and a dark barrier-like structure formed around the battlefield while trapping everyone inside, and the faint glow of their auras flickered as if being suppressed.</p>

<p>Their limbs felt heavier.</p>

<p>Their movements became sluggish.</p>

<p>Even their aura flow was disturbed and slowed down.</p>

<p>“What the hell is this?!” one of the Orcs shouted as his eyes widened in shock, and he tried to force his arm forward but it barely moved.</p>

<p>“Why can’t I move properly?!” another yelled while struggling, and his muscles tensed as veins bulged on his arms.</p>

<p>“This… this is not normal!” a third one roared while trying to break free, and his voice carried frustration and fear.</p>

<p>They tried to push through the restriction, and their bodies trembled as they released their strength, but the more they struggled, the stronger the pressure became, and it felt like the space itself was resisting them.</p>

<p>Their bodies slowed even more.</p>

<p>Their actions lagged.</p>

<p>They were completely trapped.</p>

<p>Locked inside a prison that they could not see clearly but could feel everywhere around them, and no matter how much power they used, they could not break free.</p>
