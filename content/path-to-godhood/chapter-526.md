<p>“Fuck… Are you stupid or what…” he muttered under his breath, while glancing at both of them with annoyance.</p>

<p>“Anyone comes and asks about me, and you both look at me at the same time…”</p>

<p>His voice was low, but the frustration in it was obvious.</p>

<p>The cloaked figure tilted his head slightly, as if he had already expected this outcome.</p>

<p>“So… you are the one who ruined our plans.”</p>

<p>Ethan frowned slightly as those words sank in, and a faint sense of unease rose in his chest. He tried to recall what exactly they were referring to, but before he could think further, something changed.</p>

<p>A loud, heavy sound echoed across the battlefield.</p>

<p>BOOM!</p>

<p>The space behind the cloaked figure twisted violently, and a massive silhouette slowly formed in the air. It was not fully clear, but it carried an overwhelming pressure that spread in all directions.</p>

<p>It looked like a divine figure.</p>

<p>Its outline was faint, but its presence was terrifying, and the moment it appeared, the entire battlefield trembled slightly.</p>

<p>Ethan’s pupils shrank as he stared at it.</p>

<p>“…This feeling…”</p>

<p>For some reason, it felt familiar, yet he could not remember where he had seen something like this before. That brief moment of confusion cost him.</p>

<p>The cloaked figure moved.</p>

<p>Without any warning or change in expression, he raised his hand and threw a spear forward. The motion was simple, almost casual, but the power behind it was anything but normal.</p>

<p>The moment the spear left his hand, Ethan’s body reacted on instinct.</p>

<p>“…Shit!”</p>

<p>A cold chill ran down his spine, and his heart skipped a beat as he felt the danger approaching. Without thinking, he turned and ran.</p>

<p>BOOM!</p>

<p>His body shot forward like a storm, and the ground cracked beneath him as he pushed his speed to the limit. The air behind him was torn apart, and his figure blurred as he tried to escape.</p>

<p>But the spear was already chasing him.</p>

<p>It carried a terrifying divine energy, and as it moved, the space around it began to split apart. The ground below was ripped open, and everything in its path was destroyed without resistance.</p>

<p>Ethan glanced back for just a fraction of a second, and his expression changed instantly.</p>

<p>“…No way…”</p>

<p>The spear suddenly vanished.</p>

<p>Before he could process what happened, it reappeared right in front of him as if space itself had folded.</p>

<p>“…Fuck—!”</p>

<p>There was no time to react, and there was no way to avoid it.</p>

<p>The spear struck directly.</p>

<p>BOOOOOOOOOOOOOM!</p>

<p>A massive explosion erupted, and the force of the impact tore through the land, shattering everything around it. The shockwave spread outward violently, flattening the surroundings and shaking the entire battlefield.</p>

<p>At the center of the explosion, Ethan took the hit directly.</p>

<p>There was no resistance.</p>

<p>His body burst apart instantly, turning into a cloud of blood and fragments that scattered into the air.</p>

<p>The sight was brutal and absolute.</p>

<p>Han’s eyes widened in shock as he stared at the scene in disbelief.</p>

<p>“…What the hell…”</p>

<p>Hale’s expression froze completely, and his body went stiff as if he could not process what had just happened.</p>

<p>“…No… way…”</p>

<p>For a brief moment, even the demons fell silent as they looked at the aftermath of that single attack.</p>

<p>Ethan was gone.</p>

<p>There was nothing left of him except a fading mist of blood in the air.</p>

<p>The battlefield grew even heavier, and a sense of fear slowly spread among the soldiers as they realized what kind of enemy they were facing.</p>

<p>For a few seconds after the explosion, the entire battlefield fell into a strange silence, and even the sound of weapons and screams seemed to fade as everyone stared at the spot where Ethan had been.</p>

<p>The air was still filled with traces of blood mist, slowly dispersing under the heat and pressure, and that sight alone made the hearts of the soldiers sink.</p>

<p>Han’s hand trembled slightly as he gripped his weapon tighter, and his eyes remained fixed ahead as if he could not accept what he had just witnessed.</p>

<p>“…No… this can’t be real,” he muttered under his breath, while his throat felt dry.</p>

<p>Hale stood beside him, and although he tried to remain calm, his face had already turned pale, and his breathing became uneven.</p>

<p>“…He… died just like that…” he said slowly, his voice barely steady.</p>

<p>Before either of them could react further, a burst of laughter suddenly echoed across the battlefield.</p>

<p>“Hahahaha…!”</p>

<p>The God Of Darkness Priest stepped forward slightly, and his voice carried a twisted excitement that made people uneasy.</p>

<p>“Did you think you can survive this… Even if you are a Saint, you would be killed today,” he said, while spreading his arms slightly as if admiring his own work.</p>

<p>“It’s a one-shot direct kill spear blessed by the God Of Darkness.”</p>

<p>He paused for a moment, then his tone turned colder.</p>

<p>“Daring to stand before us, you deserve to die.”</p>

<p>His gaze then shifted toward Han and Hale, and something in that look made both of them freeze for a brief moment.</p>

<p>A strange sense of déjà vu hit them, and without needing to say it out loud, both of them came to the same conclusion.</p>

<p>“RUNNN!”</p>

<p>That was the only thought that flashed through their minds.</p>

<p>Forget the war, forget everything else, because survival came first. Seeing Ethan, a mid-stage Mythic ranker, getting erased so easily had already shaken their confidence completely.</p>

<p>Their bodies tensed as if they were about to retreat, but before they could move—</p>

<p>A sudden change swept across the battlefield.</p>

<p>A bloody intent spread outward, thick and heavy, and it pressed down on everyone like an invisible weight. The temperature seemed to drop, and even the demons stopped moving for a moment.</p>

<p>Then..</p>

<p>Swoosh!</p>

<p>The scattered blood mist in the air began to swirl, slowly gathering together as if pulled by an unseen force. It rotated faster and faster, forming a vortex of red that drew everyone’s attention.</p>

<p>Han’s eyes widened.</p>

<p>“…What the hell…”</p>

<p>The swirling blood suddenly condensed.</p>

<p>And then a figure formed.</p>

<p>Standing there, completely intact, was Ethan.</p>

<p>“Marquise Ethan…!” someone shouted in disbelief, and that single voice broke the stunned silence.</p>

<p>Ethan stood calmly, his clothes untouched, and his expression cold as his gaze swept across the battlefield before stopping on the cloaked figure.</p>

<p>He looked at Han and Hale for a brief moment, then turned his attention forward again.</p>

<p>“That was my clone, I created it for backup,” he said calmly, while rolling his shoulders slightly as if nothing had happened.</p>

<p>Then his eyes hardened.</p>

<p>“Now…” he paused, and a faint killing intent leaked from his body as he stared directly at the cloaked figure.</p>

<p>“You have really pissed me.”</p>
