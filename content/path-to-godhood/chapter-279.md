<p>While a tense and deadly situation prevailed outside the mansion, the atmosphere inside was completely different.</p>
<p>It was bursting with excitement.</p>
<p>“Wooooow!” Nera jumped up from the couch and clapped her hands loudly.</p>
<p>“Big mother looks so cool!” she shouted, eyes sparkling.</p>
<p>Beside her, Miranda and Ray stood frozen, mouths slightly open, unable to blink.</p>
<p>“That’s so cool!” Leon shouted, sweeping his hands through the air like he was throwing spells himself.</p>
<p>“These kids…” Sophia muttered, pressing her fingers against her throbbing forehead.</p>
<p>The moment the explosions echoed outside, the children started howling that they wanted to see the fight.</p>
<p>Even though everyone knew it was dangerous, none of them budged. No matter what, they wanted to watch.</p>
<p>“It’s okay,” Diana said with a soft smile. “Everyone loves watching a fight.”</p>
<p>“Yes,” Lia added, her eyes glowing as she stared toward the rooftop. “Especially Sister Rina. She looks so cool.”</p>
<p>Sophia paused and glanced at Rina’s figure outside.</p>
<p>For a brief moment, jealousy bubbled up in her chest. Not because of Ethan’s love or anything like that.</p>
<p>She just wanted to look that cool too.</p>
<p>’Ahhh, Ethan… It’s your fault,’ she complained inwardly. ’Why didn’t you train me harder?’</p>
<p>If Ethan heard this, he would probably jump off his seat screaming that he had dragged her everywhere to train.</p>
<p>Sadly, Sophia was a knight, not a mage. She could not just read books and get stronger.</p>
<p>“Aahh! Look… fireworks…” Sally mumbled incoherently as another explosion lit up the sky.</p>
<p>…..</p>
<p>Outside, the night seemed to burn.</p>
<p>Dazzling flames burst into existence, swirling and condensing into a massive spear of pure fire. The heat distorted the air, and the ground trembled beneath it.</p>
<p>“You dared to disturb the peace of Blanks,” Rina said calmly, her voice carrying killing intent.</p>
<p>“Now you can die!”</p>
<p>She waved her hand.</p>
<p>The flaming spear shot forward with terrifying force, tearing through the night like judgment itself.</p>
<p>“Howwww?”</p>
<p>“Just howww?”</p>
<p>That was all the two assassins could manage to say.</p>
<p>Their minds felt like they had been smashed apart. Everything they believed made no sense anymore.</p>
<p>’How did the Blanks family produce three Master ranks?’ one of them thought in disbelief.</p>
<p>“FUCKKK!”</p>
<p>“Are you alright committing treason and trying to rebel?” one assassin screamed, unable to accept this absurd reality.</p>
<p>Rina did not care.</p>
<p>Unlike Hall and Noel, who had broken through using potions and still lacked stability, Rina had advanced under Emma’s guidance. Her power was refined, controlled, and deadly.</p>
<p>She lifted both hands.</p>
<p>A ring of fire bloomed behind her, splitting into dozens of flame lances that hovered silently in the air.</p>
<p>With a sharp flick of her fingers, they shot forward.</p>
<p>The assassins dodged desperately, twisting their bodies mid-air as flames grazed their cloaks and scorched their skin.</p>
<p>One lance exploded near their feet, blasting them upward.</p>
<p>Before they could regain balance, Rina stepped forward and slammed her palm down.</p>
<p>A wave of fire crashed into the ground, rolling forward like a burning tide. The earth cracked and melted as it chased them.</p>
<p>“Damn it!” one assassin cursed, rolling aside just in time.</p>
<p>Hall appeared from the left, his blade flashed as he struck. One assassin blocked, sparks flying, but Noel followed from the right, forcing him back.</p>
<p>The three of them moved in rhythm.</p>
<p>Rina controlled the battlefield, cutting off escape routes with walls of fire while Hall and Noel pressed from both sides.</p>
<p>Another spell formed above Rina’s head.</p>
<p>A spiraling flame sphere compressed until it screamed, then she released it.</p>
<p>The explosion sent one assassin flying into a wall, bones cracking loudly.</p>
<p>The second assassin tried to flee, feet pushing off the ground with desperate speed.</p>
<p>“You think you can run?” Rina said coldly.</p>
<p>She snapped her fingers.</p>
<p>A chain of ice burst from the ground, wrapping around both assassins’ ankles with a sharp crack.</p>
<p>They slammed to the ground, struggling, frost spreading rapidly up their legs.</p>
<p>The two exchanged a glance.</p>
<p>They clenched their teeth and began burning their life force, veins bulging as power surged wildly.</p>
<p>Before they could finish, Hall appeared behind them.</p>
<p>His blade rose and fell twice.Two heads rolled across the shattered ground.</p>
<p>The battlefield fell silent, and flames slowly died down.</p>
<p>Rina lowered her hand, her expression calm as if nothing extraordinary had happened.</p>
<p>…</p>
<p>Seeing the battle there was over, Duke Phillips turned toward the remaining assassin.</p>
<p>“So, what were you saying?” he asked calmly, his sword resting at his side.</p>
<p>“Hhhuhmm…” the assassin tried to speak, but his body was already twisted at an unnatural angle, bones crushed and joints dislocated.</p>
<p>“I am an experienced veteran, unlike them,” Duke Phillips continued coldly. “So do you really think you can commit suicide or take poison?”</p>
<p>The assassin’s eyes trembled in fear.</p>
<p>Phillips then turned his head slightly. “How is it over there?”</p>
<p>“Here!” Emma’s voice answered.</p>
<p>A figure was thrown across the ground, rolling several times before stopping. His mouth was sealed shut and both arms were bent backward at grotesque angles.</p>
<p>“I made sure he is completely broken,” Emma said flatly.</p>
<p>Two figures materialized beside them, kneeling briefly.</p>
<p>“Good work,” Duke Phillips said, still dazed. “For new Master ranks, you acted quite well.”</p>
<p>He shook his head slightly.</p>
<p>’Ethan hid things far deeper than I thought,’ he realized. ’What a frightening calculation.’</p>
<p>“Now inform him quickly,” Phillips ordered.</p>
<p>Hall nodded and pulled out a crystal ball communicator, channeling his mana.</p>
<p>“My Lord, we have taken care of the intruders,” Hall reported.</p>
<p>…</p>
<p>Inside the prison chamber, Simon stared at Ethan with a beast-like gaze.</p>
<p>“Aren’t you a little worried?” Simon sneered.</p>
<p>“Why would I—” Ethan paused and calmly pulled out a crystal ball. “Here. Listen for yourself.”</p>
<p>“My Lord, we have taken care of the intruders,” the voice echoed.</p>
<p>Simon’s eyes widened in horror.</p>
<p>He looked at Ethan as if demanding an explanation, but Ethan simply ignored him.</p>
<p>“Any remnants?” Ethan asked.</p>
<p>“Yes. His Highness Duke Phillips and Duchess Emma captured the ones they fought,” the voice replied. “The other two who came to assassinate us have been killed.”</p>
<p>“Good,” Ethan said calmly. “Now torture the captured one and see if you can extract any additional information.”</p>
<p>“Youuu!” Simon roared, veins bulging on his forehead as humiliation and rage surged.</p>
<p>Ethan cut off the connection and looked at the Adventure Guild Master.</p>
<p>“So, what do you want to do now?” Ethan asked softly.</p>
<p>“Guess,” he added with a faint smile.</p>
<p>That smile, which could charm any lady, felt terrifying to Simon.</p>
