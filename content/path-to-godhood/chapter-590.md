<p>The Emperor’s eyes narrowed slightly as he looked at the vial while falling into deep thought, and the faint glow coming from the liquid reflected within his cold eyes.</p>

<p>For a few moments, the entire throne room remained silent while everyone waited for him to speak.</p>

<p>"So you are telling me that this thing is capable of curing the disease of the Demons?" Alaster asked slowly while lightly rotating the vial between his fingers.</p>

<p>"And that we can exchange it for stopping the war?"</p>

<p>His expression changed slightly, and a deep frown appeared on his face while he looked first at the vial and then directly at Ethan.</p>

<p>"You are not joking with me, right?" he asked in a heavier tone.</p>

<p>Ethan immediately lowered his head slightly.</p>

<p>"How can I joke about such a grave matter, Your Majesty?" he replied calmly.</p>

<p>"You can ask some people to test it."</p>

<p>The Emperor continued staring at Ethan for a few moments while lightly tapping the armrest of his throne with his fingers.</p>

<p>Tap... Tap... Tap...</p>

<p>The sound echoed faintly through the silent hall.</p>

<p>Then he suddenly asked,</p>

<p>"Where did you get it?"</p>

<p>Ethan had already prepared his answer beforehand.</p>

<p>"When I was travelling, I found a certain dew near the Elven forests," he replied steadily.</p>

<p>"I preserved it and later used it for several experiments and checks."</p>

<p>He paused briefly before continuing.</p>

<p>"It works, but it can only be consumed by infected children. If healthy adults consume it without being infected, they might suffer side effects."</p>

<p>The Emperor narrowed his eyes slightly after hearing that.</p>

<p>"If your words are true..." he muttered slowly.</p>

<p>"Then this thing might truly change the current situation."</p>

<p>His gaze toward Ethan gradually became more curious.</p>

<p>Then he suddenly asked,</p>

<p>"For such a contribution, what do you want?"</p>

<p>The Emperor leaned slightly against the throne while continuing calmly.</p>

<p>"I know how to differentiate between punishment and rewards, so speak your mind."</p>

<p>Every official in the hall silently looked toward Ethan again.</p>

<p>Many of them thought Ethan would ask for wealth.</p>

<p>Territory.</p>

<p>Authority.</p>

<p>Or perhaps removal of the previous punishments placed upon him.</p>

<p>But Ethan remained silent for a brief moment before speaking calmly.</p>

<p>"I implore Your Majesty to take steps to finish this war," he said.</p>

<p>"Many people have suffered, and if this continues, then even the commoners will eventually be dragged into complete ruin, so I ask you to please take steps to stop this war."</p>

<p>The throne room became slightly quieter after those words.Alaster frowned slightly while carefully observing Ethan.</p>

<p>That answer was not what he expected.He had expected Ethan to ask for benefits.</p>

<p>Power.</p>

<p>Resources.</p>

<p>Or some grand reward.</p>

<p>But instead the man only requested for the war to end.</p>

<p>How strange and weird?</p>

<p>He looked at him briefly.</p>

<p>’Is it a sincere request or a pretense?’</p>

<p>And strangely...</p>

<p>That answer gave the Emperor an eerie feeling.</p>

<p>’So what exactly are you planning?’ Alaster thought quietly while narrowing his eyes slightly.</p>

<p>For the first time, the Emperor felt unable to properly see through Ethan.In his eyes, Ethan had always been a talented young man who recently learned how to crawl within the world of true experts.</p>

<p>But now...</p>

<p>That same young man was beginning to give him dangerous feelings.When Ruthiana joined Arcadia, Ethan had merely been a Legendary Knight.</p>

<p>But now he had already stepped into the Divine Knight realm.</p>

<p>The speed was fast.</p>

<p>Very fast.</p>

<p>Although not completely unheard of.</p>

<p>After all, many geniuses occasionally appeared in history, and some people remained stuck at certain realms forever while others advanced rapidly.</p>

<p>Still something about Ethan continued bothering him.The Emperor remained silent for several moments before asking again,</p>

<p>"Anything more you want to say?"</p>

<p>Ethan shook his head calmly.</p>

<p>"Nothing."</p>

<p>Alaster quietly stared at him before finally nodding.</p>

<p>"Very well," he replied.</p>

<p>"I will discuss this matter with my ministers before making a final decision."</p>

<p>He paused briefly before continuing in a calmer tone.</p>

<p>"You can rest assure...I will make your wish come true."</p>

<p>His gaze slowly shifted toward the vial again.</p>

<p>"This war, no matter what, has to end."</p>

<p>Ethan nodded respectfully before bowing once more.</p>

<p>"Then this subject shall take his leave."</p>

<p>Without lingering further, Ethan slowly turned around and walked out of the throne room while countless eyes silently followed his departing figure.</p>

<p>The massive doors gradually closed behind him.</p>

<p>RUMBLE!</p>

<p>The moment Ethan disappeared completely, the atmosphere inside the throne room subtly changed.</p>

<p>Then a shadow behind the throne slowly elongated before a figure quietly stepped out from the darkness.</p>

<p>It was Prime Minister Deus.</p>

<p>He calmly adjusted his sleeves before looking toward the Emperor.</p>

<p>"What do you think, my dear Prime Minister?" Alaster asked while lightly resting his chin against one hand.</p>

<p>Deus remained silent briefly before replying.</p>

<p>"Hard to say much."</p>

<p>He narrowed his eyes slightly.</p>

<p>"Either that fellow is quite crafty..."</p>

<p>"Or he is far too pretentious."</p>

<p>"Only time will tell."</p>

<p>He then looked toward the vial again.</p>

<p>"But for now, we need to seriously think about this matter."</p>

<p>Alaster also fell into deep thought.</p>

<p>This war...</p>

<p>Even if he hated admitting it—It had become a complete failure.And perhaps the greatest setback of his reign.Everything he once aimed for had nearly succeeded.</p>

<p>Arcadia had originally been the smallest and weakest Empire among the human nations, and he had spent centuries trying to change that.</p>

<p>And it had almost worked.</p>

<p>Almost.</p>

<p>Arcadia’s armies had repeatedly defeated the Demons and gained victory after victory.</p>

<p>Although there were losses, they still managed to push forward most of the time.</p>

<p>But then everything changed.</p>

<p>The Emperor’s expression darkened slightly.</p>

<p>"Those damned Demons..." he muttered quietly.</p>

<p>Then he slowly shook his head.</p>

<p>"No..."</p>

<p>"It is not just the Demons."</p>

<p>His eyes gradually turned colder.</p>

<p>"It is those Orcs and Beastmen who forgot their place."</p>

<p>"Those stupid mongrels dared to interfere."</p>

<p>A dangerous pressure faintly spread through the hall while several nearby officials instinctively lowered their heads further.</p>

<p>Then Alaster looked toward Deus again before speaking calmly.</p>

<p>"But you already know this."</p>

<p>"We are merely commanders standing on the surface."</p>

<p>His voice became heavier.</p>

<p>"The real people overseeing everything are the ones above."</p>

<p>"As long as they desire it..."</p>

<p>"Even if the entire human world descends into chaos, they will not flinch if it helps them achieve their goals."</p>

<p>The moment those words echoed through the throne room, even the atmosphere itself seemed to grow colder.</p>
