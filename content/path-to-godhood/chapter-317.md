<p>“Hey… hey, mister,” Ethan said, snapping his fingers to pull him back.</p>

<p>Rathlos froze and turned.</p>

<p>“I understand this is extraordinary,” Ethan continued, “but I do not think I am the only one.”</p>

<p>“You understand shit,” Rathlos cursed, startling Ethan.</p>

<p>“I may not have seen much of the world,” Rathlos continued sharply, “but from what I remember, only people born with special physiques, like a Chaos Body, can use chaos energy.”</p>

<p>Ethan blinked.</p>

<p>“Even then,” Rathlos went on, “they can only use chaos energy produced from their own bodies. They cannot convert other energies into chaos.”</p>

<p>Ethan was stunned.</p>

<p>’Chaos physique…’</p>

<p>’Oh my… do not tell me I have to deal with cultivation nonsense later.’</p>

<p>Ethan broke into a cold sweat as he remembered countless stories where protagonists reached the peak of their world, only to discover another cultivation realm above it.</p>

<p>’System… I will kill you if that happens.’</p>

<p>[Calm down, Host. I cannot reveal much, but the peak of your world will be equal to the peak of the cultivation world.]</p>

<p>[Also, your current stage is already comparable to the Void stage. If you reach the peak of this world, you can fight the peak of the cultivation world.]</p>

<p>’Oh, thank God,’ Ethan sighed inwardly.</p>

<p>He wiped the sweat from his brow.</p>

<p>Ethan wanted to ask more questions but the system stopped answering it.</p>

<p>So,he grabbed Rathlos by the shoulder.</p>

<p>“Enough,” Ethan said. “Stop going crazy and take this.”</p>

<p>Rathlos paused as Ethan tossed him a small bottle.</p>

<p>“It contains five percent Draconic Bloodline,” Ethan said.</p>

<p>Rathlos almost dropped it. He barely managed to catch the bottle and stared at it in horror.</p>

<p>He opened his mouth to speak, but before any words could come out, a loud scream tore through the air.</p>

<p>“ETHAAAAAANNNNN!”</p>

<p>“NOW WHAT STUPIDITY ARE YOU UP TOOO!”</p>

<p>The voice sent a chill down both Ethan’s and Rathlos’s spines.</p>

<p>…..</p>

<p>The next day, the Wyvern King and the King of the Maiden’s Heart stood side by side with their heads bowed.</p>

<p>Sophia stood before them, holding her throbbing forehead.</p>

<p>“If you were not enough,” she snapped, “now here comes another one.”</p>

<p>She glared at Rathlos.</p>

<p>“I can understand Ethan,” she continued, “but Sir Rathlos, you too?”</p>

<p>Ethan felt attacked and took a step forward, but Sophia’s glare froze him in place.</p>

<p>“How many times do I have to say this?” she shouted.</p>

<p>“First, you trained in the backyard and destroyed it. Then you moved to the garden and destroyed that too.”</p>

<p>“Then you caused a mental shock across the territory. After that, you trained in a separate building and still—”</p>

<p>She raised her hands in frustration.</p>

<p>“You destroyed a mountain!”</p>

<p>“Gosh! What am I supposed to do with you two? Argh!”</p>

<p>Rathlos, not knowing what to say, nudged Ethan slightly, signaling him to calm his wife.</p>

<p>“Darling…” Ethan tried.</p>

<p>“Shut up!” Sophia snapped.</p>

<p>“…”</p>

<p>Ethan stood there helplessly.</p>

<p>Just when it seemed there was no way out, Oliver stepped forward and coughed lightly.</p>

<p>“My Lady,” Oliver said carefully, “it is not that bad.”</p>

<p>Sophia turned sharply. “What do you mean?”</p>

<p>“We can use the destroyed mountain for construction,” Oliver explained. “We can develop a small facility of some sort there.”</p>

<p>Ethan immediately raised a thumbs-up toward Oliver.</p>

<p>’Your loyalty will not go unnoticed, Uncle Oliver,’ Ethan thought.</p>

<p>’I will reward you.’</p>

<p>Oliver nodded slowly, pretending not to notice Ethan’s gaze, while Sophia’s anger finally began to cool.</p>

<p>The crisis, for now, was narrowly averted.</p>

<p>……</p>

<p>Rathlos took the small vial and stared at it for a moment before opening the lid.</p>

<p>The instant it opened, a strong and pungent smell assaulted his nostrils. It was so intense that it almost burned his senses. Heat rushed up his throat, and his pupils shrank.</p>

<p>“It is Flame Dragon Bloodline,” Rathlos said slowly.</p>

<p>“Yes,” Ethan nodded.</p>

<p>Rathlos looked at him sharply. “From where did you get it? You do not seem to venture out much.”</p>

<p>Under Rathlos’s piercing gaze, Ethan thought for a moment before answering.</p>

<p>“I extracted some of my own essence and cultivated it,” Ethan said calmly.</p>

<p>Rathlos opened his mouth, but no words came out. For a long moment, he simply stared at Ethan, his thoughts in chaos. He did not know how to thank him.</p>

<p>After a while, he sighed.</p>

<p>“It is good,” Rathlos said quietly, “but it cannot last for generations.”</p>

<p>Ethan raised his brow. “Why do you need it to last for generations? I can get more.”</p>

<p>Rathlos froze and turned to him in shock.</p>

<p>“You mean…”</p>

<p>“Yes,” Ethan replied, rubbing his nose as if it were nothing. “I cannot give it every year, but I can produce it regularly.”</p>

<p>It was a lie, but Ethan said it with the confidence of someone who looked extraordinary beyond reason.</p>

<p>Rathlos stared at him deeply, then nodded.</p>

<p>“In that case, let me use it.”</p>

<p>“Hohooo!” Ethan waved his hand quickly. “Wait a moment. Do you not already have Shadow Dragon Bloodline?”</p>

<p>“I do,” Rathlos replied, “but it will not be a problem. The two will not conflict as long as I subdue them. There will be a strong clash, but I am sure I can suppress it.”</p>

<p>“And if you cannot?” Ethan asked seriously.</p>

<p>Rathlos smiled faintly. “Then it is better that I die. After I die, extract my remains and feed them to the next generation Wyvern King chosen by Wales.”</p>

<p>“…”</p>

<p>“Pfftt!”</p>

<p>Rathlos suddenly laughed. He stepped forward and patted Ethan on the shoulder.</p>

<p>“Do not be so serious. I am joking,” he said. “I am sure I will manage.”</p>

<p>Ethan finally relaxed and smiled back.</p>

<p>In the past few days, Ethan had unknowingly formed a sense of comradeship with Rathlos. Many people followed him, and many feared him, but very few treated him as an equal. Rathlos did. As a king of a nest, Rathlos spoke to him without fear or flattery.</p>

<p>Ethan enjoyed this rare feeling.</p>

<p>“Good,” Ethan said. “Then do it. I will protect you.”</p>

<p>Rathlos did not hesitate. He tilted his head back and drank the potion in one go.</p>
