<p>One commander stared with wide eyes as he spoke in shock.</p>

<p>The aura surrounding Ethan continued rising without slowing down. The energy inside his body began changing rapidly.</p>

<p>Every strand of energy became denser and purer as it flowed through his meridians with overwhelming force. His bones gave off faint cracking sounds as they were continuously strengthened, while his muscles, blood, and every part of his body underwent another transformation.</p>

<p>Every cell in his body was being refined by the surging spiritual energy, making him stronger with every passing second. His breathing gradually became calm, and his heartbeat settled into a steady rhythm. Yet the power inside him continued growing without showing any sign of stopping.</p>

<p>At the same time, a faint phantom slowly appeared behind him. It was not a martial spirit.</p>

<p>It was the reflection of his own Knight’s Will. The phantom stood silently with a sword in its hand while endless streams of light flowed between it and Ethan’s soul. It remained completely still, yet it radiated an unwavering will that seemed capable of cutting through anything.</p>

<p>Inside Ethan’s spiritual world, his soul began shining with a brilliant white light. The countless fragments of Knight Insight that had remained scattered until now rapidly merged together. One by one, they fused into a complete whole. His soul became clearer, stronger, and more stable. It was as though an invisible chain that had bound him for so long had finally shattered.</p>

<p>Boom!</p>

<p>Another terrifying shockwave erupted from Ethan’s body. The surrounding rocks were instantly blasted away while the miasma within hundreds of meters completely disappeared. A violent gust of wind swept across the battlefield, forcing countless people to shield their faces as their clothes fluttered wildly. The overwhelming aura spread outward like a raging storm, causing even the nearby Transcendent experts to tense up.</p>

<p>A powerful Transcendent Realm aura swept across the battlefield, and everyone present felt it deep within their hearts. Ethan had successfully stepped into the Transcendent Realm.</p>

<p>The sudden breakthrough shook everyone to their core. Even the four Emperors narrowed their eyes as they stared at Ethan. Haster’s countless eyes suddenly widened, and his face immediately twisted with rage as a dangerous killing intent burst from his body.</p>

<p>"Don’t let him break through!"</p>

<p>He roared at the top of his lungs, and his furious voice echoed across the battlefield.</p>

<p>The surrounding monstrous humans immediately obeyed his command. Dozens of powerful attacks erupted together as black beams, poisonous tentacles, blades of darkness, and countless other attacks rushed toward Ethan from every direction. They tore through the air with terrifying force, carrying enough power to destroy everything in their path. Their only goal was to kill Ethan before he could completely stabilize his breakthrough.</p>

<p>Just then...</p>

<p>A graceful figure suddenly appeared before him.</p>

<p>Heliana.</p>

<p>She calmly stepped forward while gently raising her crystal staff. Her long purple hair swayed softly in the cold wind, and her expression remained completely calm. There wasn’t the slightest trace of panic on her face even as countless deadly attacks rushed toward them.</p>

<p>"Absolute Zero."</p>

<p>The moment those words left her lips, an overwhelming wave of freezing energy burst across the battlefield.</p>

<p>The temperature dropped instantly.</p>

<p>The air itself seemed to freeze as an endless chill spread in every direction. White frost rapidly covered the rocky ground while countless snowflakes appeared from nowhere, dancing through the air before settling across the battlefield. Thick layers of ice spread outward at an incredible speed, swallowing everything before her in endless cold.</p>

<p>The incoming attacks collided head-on with the freezing aura.</p>

<p>One after another, the black beams froze solid. The poisonous tentacles were instantly covered in thick ice, and even the dark energy itself slowed before turning into countless frozen crystals. The terrifying attacks that had been charging toward Ethan lost all their momentum.</p>

<p>Crash!</p>

<p>Crash!</p>

<p>Crash!</p>

<p>The frozen attacks shattered one after another, scattering countless pieces of ice across the battlefield. Shards of crystal-like ice rained onto the ground, and not a single attack managed to reach Ethan.</p>

<p>At that very moment, Ethan slowly opened his eyes.</p>

<p>Brilliant light shone from within them.</p>

<p>His expression was calm, but the sharpness in his gaze made everyone who looked at him feel a chill. Without saying a single word, he tightened his grip on his sword. His Transcendent Realm aura surged around him as sword intent erupted once again.</p>

<p>Then he disappeared.</p>

<p>Boom!</p>

<p>The ground exploded beneath his feet as rocks and dirt were blasted into the air. His figure shot forward like a beam of light, leaving only a blurred afterimage behind. The monstrous humans barely had time to react before Ethan appeared in front of them and swung his sword.</p>

<p>A dazzling arc of holy light swept across the battlefield.</p>

<p>Slash!</p>

<p>The attack instantly cut through several monstrous humans. Their bodies were sliced apart before they could even raise their weapons to defend themselves. Blood sprayed into the air as the holy sword light continued forward without losing its momentum.</p>

<p>Without slowing down, Ethan continued charging deeper into the battlefield.</p>

<p>His sword danced continuously.</p>

<p>Each swing released another wave of radiant light.</p>

<p>The holy attacks tore through the thick miasma, forcing the darkness to retreat wherever they passed. The poisonous fog rapidly dissolved under the overwhelming light while the battlefield became brighter with every strike.</p>

<p>The monstrous humans desperately tried to stop him.</p>

<p>Yet Ethan continued advancing without hesitation.</p>

<p>Like a shining spear piercing through endless darkness, he carved a path straight toward Haster while the holy light behind him continued cleansing the cursed miasma covering the valley.</p>

<p>Ethan cut down another monstrous human before suddenly turning toward Queen Ophelia and the four Emperors. His voice echoed clearly across the battlefield despite the constant explosions and the sounds of clashing weapons.</p>

<p>"Leave this place to me.I will hold them back.You can advance."</p>

<p>The moment those words were spoken, the entire Allied Army fell silent. Many commanders instinctively turned toward Ethan while several Transcendent experts frowned deeply. Others hesitated as they looked between Ethan and the countless monstrous enemies surrounding him.</p>
