<p>“Go…”</p>

<p>“Stop!”</p>
<p>“I will leave, I swear!”</p>

<p>His body felt drained, and his mana reserves were nearly empty, while a deep exhaustion settled into his bones.</p>

<p>“You did a good job, and now let’s go.”</p>

<p>“Let’s go.”</p>

<p>He glanced at Lucien and then spoke again in a low, steady voice.</p>
<p>“Don’t you hate these people for taking your military points and pushing you to the back?”</p>
<p>Lucien froze for a moment, and his jaw tightened as those words hit him harder than expected.</p>

<p>He was supposed to be the hero here.</p>
<p>This battlefield should have been his stage, and his name should have been rising, spreading across the empire with glory and pride.</p>
<p>Alas .</p>
<p>Because of one mistake he had been pushed aside.</p>
<p>He was demoted and ignored.</p>
<p>The thought burned inside him.</p>
<p>It was too much to bear.</p>

<p>“I can’t betray humans.”</p>

<p>“I heard you have many brothers who are competing for positions,” he said calmly. “Amid all this, how many resources can you truly have?”</p>
<p>Lucien’s eyes shifted slightly, and his breathing slowed as he listened.</p>
<p>The Beastman took a step closer.</p>
<p>“Do you want to remain stuck at the Legendary Realm,” he continued, “or do you want to move forward?”</p>
<p>Those words lingered.</p>
<p>Lucien’s fingers tightened again.</p>
<p>The Beastman’s voice became softer, but it carried more weight.</p>
<p>“What have you gained by serving the human side?” he asked. “We promise that you will receive enough resources to push beyond Divine Knight.”</p>
<p>Lucien’s eyes paused for a moment, and a flicker of hesitation appeared.</p>
<p>“…You aren’t joking?” he asked, and his voice was lower now, but there was a trace of eagerness hidden within.</p>
<p>The Beastman shook his head slowly.</p>
<p>“I am not,” he replied. “Just follow our request, and you will have wealth and resources that will last you a long time.”</p>

<p>“…What should I do?” he asked.</p>

<p>“You don’t need to do much,” he said calmly. “Just allow us to cut off a portion of your forces and send a few false messages.”</p>
<p>Lucien’s expression remained still for a moment, and then slowly a smile appeared.</p>
