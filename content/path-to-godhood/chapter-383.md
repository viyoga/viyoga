<p>As the births concluded, the system activated. Ethan opened the rewards one after another without waiting. He did not hesitate because he was eager to see what he had earned this time.</p>
<p>Soon, lines of notifications chimed and flashed before his retina, making him gasp in shock. The text glowed brightly in his vision. The list of items was far more impressive than he had anticipated.</p>
<p>“Woow…Its totally new round of potions one after another..” he muttered under his breath, his eyes moving quickly as he read.</p>
<p>He stared at the descriptions of the items. They were different from the usual rewards.</p>
<p>“Holyshit…System, did you just upgrade yourself…” Ethan gasped, still reeling from the feeling of shock and déjà vu. It felt like the time he first discovered the system’s true potential.</p>
<p>Especially the defence weapons.It come at timely aid.</p>
<p>After calming down, Ethan walked in to greet the kids and the wives. He pushed the door open gently. The smell of blood and sweat hung heavy in the room, mixed with the scent of herbs.</p>
<p>He looked at the beds. His wives seemed to have passed out due to the turmoil of childbirth. They lay completely still with pale faces. Their chests barely rose and fell. Of course, they were not actually dead, but the exhaustion had drained every ounce of energy from their bodies. They looked like they had fought a great war and collapsed on the battlefield.</p>
<p>The experience of childbirth was quite unique and difficult, especially the first experience. Ethan looked at them with a complicated expression. Even as a warrior, he felt a chill thinking how something so narrow was able to stretch so wide to allow a child to pass. It was a physical feat that terrified him more than fighting monsters.</p>
<p>“Waaaaa…”</p>
<p>“Waaaaaa…”</p>
<p>Suddenly, the silence broke as the screams of children filled the room. The sound was loud and full of life.</p>
<p>Ethan shook his head when he heard the children cry and walked further inside. He moved past the beds to see the babies.</p>
<p>Inside the room, Sophia, Lia, and Diana were taking care of the children. They were moving back and forth with towels and warm water. Sophia held one baby close to her chest and rocked it gently. Lia wiped another baby’s tiny face. Diana adjusted the blankets and checked their breathing.</p>
<p>There were two boys and two girls. Ethan looked down at them. They were small, red, and wrinkled, with tightly shut eyes and tiny fists.</p>
<p>“And how can’t they be, after all whose children are they,” Ethan thought with a surge of pride.</p>
<p>He stood there for a long time, lingering at the scene, greeting them softly and playing with the kids. He touched their tiny hands and watched them sleep.</p>
<p>The were named as Rein,Kein,Kiea and Shelly.</p>
<p>For the next few days, he spent his time with his wives, moving from one room to another to take care of them. He did not focus on training or ruling during this time. Instead, he brought them food and water, helped them sit up, and made sure they were comfortable.</p>
<p>His genuine and sincere care really moved them, especially Mira. One evening, while he adjusted her pillow, she looked at him with warmth and asked softly, “You aren’t acting to steal my heart, are you?”</p>
<p>Ethan paused and looked at her with a slight smirk. “Aren’t you overestimating yourself?”</p>
<p>The air in the room froze instantly. Mira’s eyes widened, and she glared coldly. “What do you mean?”</p>
<p>Ethan realized he had made a mistake. Mira’s lip trembled, and her eyes filled with tears.</p>
<p>“Are you saying I don’t look good..Wuwuwuw…or have I become ugly after giving birth to a child so you don’t want me..”</p>
<p>Ethan started sweating and immediately moved closer to coax her while wiping his forehead. He waved his hands in panic.</p>
<p>‘Ahhh….A woman’s emotion is too unstable…Needed to tread carefully Ethan…’ he thought anxiously. He spent the next hour reassuring her that she was beautiful until she finally calmed down.</p>
