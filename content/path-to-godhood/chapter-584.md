<p>During these two years, Ethan had not only focused on strengthening the Blanks family from the outside, but his own growth had also reached a terrifying level because of the continuous rewards given by the system through his children.</p>

<p>As more children were born, their talents became increasingly monstrous, and many of the later generations possessed exceptional affinity toward magic, which caused the rewards Ethan accumulated to become more and more absurd with time.</p>

<p>By now, he had already gathered nearly eight hundred years worth of Knight Insight along with more than twelve hundred years worth of Mage Insight, and such a horrifying accumulation was enough to make even ancient monsters feel jealous.</p>

<p>Naturally, Ethan did not waste any of it.</p>

<p>He continuously refined and absorbed the insights little by little while carefully stabilizing his foundation so that his strength would not become unstable because of his rapid growth.</p>

<p>Because of that, his cultivation advanced at a frightening speed.</p>

<p>Not only had he completely stepped into the rank of Mythic Mage, but his knight cultivation had also broken through steadily until he reached the late stage of Divine Knight.</p>

<p>Such progress was already enough to shake countless powers if news of it spread outside.</p>

<p>However, the insights were only a small part of the rewards.</p>

<p>Over the years, the system had continuously granted Ethan all kinds of powerful things, including terrifying techniques, high level spells, advanced potion formulas, military tactics, forging methods, body strengthening arts, rare resources, and many other treasures.</p>

<p>Some rewards directly strengthened Ethan himself while others strengthened the overall foundation of the Blanks family.</p>

<p>But amidst all those rewards...There was one thing that truly shook Ethan to the core.</p>

<p>The Power of Law of Light Stone.</p>

<p>It was a special reward that allowed him to comprehend one percent of the Law of Light.</p>

<p>At first glance, one percent sounded insignificant, but the moment Ethan absorbed it, he immediately understood just how terrifying divine laws truly were.The holy power inside his body underwent a complete transformation.</p>

<p>Previously, the power of light he used relied heavily on accumulated faith and external sources, which meant its growth was rather slow and somewhat limited.</p>

<p>But after comprehending even one percent of the Law of Light, everything changed completely.</p>

<p>His control over light became natural and absolute, and the holy energy moved through his body smoothly as if it had become part of his existence itself.</p>

<p>Not only did his offensive power rise tremendously, but he also gained the ability to grant minor blessings of light to others.</p>

<p>Healing.</p>

<p>Purification.</p>

<p>Mental calmness.</p>

<p>Resistance against corruption.</p>

<p>All of those abilities became possible now.</p>

<p>And the increase in power was absolutely terrifying.</p>

<p>If Ethan compared himself to his previous state, then he was no longer stronger by just a small margin.</p>

<p>No...</p>

<p>He was at least two times stronger.</p>

<p>No...</p>

<p>Three times stronger than before.</p>

<p>But even after all this...</p>

<p>That still was not the most shocking reward.</p>

<p>Because what came next was enough to completely blow Ethan’s mind.</p>

<p>A son born from Amber had awakened with X-rank potential, and the moment the child was born, the system immediately granted Ethan a new technique.</p>

<p>[One Call From the Future.]</p>

<p>The name alone sounded insane.It was fucking badass.</p>

<p>It allowed Ethan to temporarily summon a future version of himself from the long river of time to aid him.</p>

<p>When Ethan first read the details, he almost thought the system had gone mad.</p>

<p>This was fucking insane.Insane in the truest sense.</p>

<p>A future version of himself...</p>

<p>For ordinary people, such a technique might not seem overly terrifying because many experts remained stuck at the same realm for centuries without improving much.</p>

<p>But Ethan was different.He was growing stronger continuously every single year.</p>

<p>With the birth of each child,his strength rise was nothing to be scoffed off. Who knew how terrifying his future self would become after several decades or centuries?</p>

<p>Just imagining it was enough to make his scalp turn numb.</p>

<p>What if his future version has already reached Godhood.That would be terrifying.</p>

<p>Who would dare to contend against him once he becomes God</p>

<p>With such a trump card in hand, whom did he even need to fear anymore?</p>

<p>Cough...</p>

<p>Of course, Ethan did not mean he would act completely unrestrained because he still understood how dangerous and unpredictable the world truly was.</p>

<p>The trump card was quite good but it also gave him a slight sense of deja vu.</p>

<p>What if the future god Ethan entering the past was caught by the Gods.The thought gave him a shiver but he shook it off.</p>

<p>’Think positively Ethan..Be positive.’</p>

<p>But for the first time in a very long while, he finally felt slightly at ease.Then another major reward arrived.</p>

<p>A daughter born from Cassandra granted him a Detoxifying Potion formula.</p>

<p>At first, Ethan did not think too much about it, but after carefully reading through the details, his expression slowly changed.</p>

<p>The potion could cure diseases, toxins, corruption, curses and several forms of bodily contamination.</p>

<p>And immediately...</p>

<p>One thought appeared in his mind.</p>

<p>Would this work on demons?</p>

<p>That single possibility instantly caught his full attention.</p>

<p>Over the years, several demons had escaped from battlefields and secretly infiltrated human territories where they slaughtered ordinary people purely for pleasure.</p>

<p>Some of those demons had eventually been captured alive by the Blanks forces.</p>

<p>Since then, Maverick had been secretly researching them beneath the estate.</p>

<p>And after several months of continuous experiments...</p>

<p>There was finally progress.</p>

<p>Inside the underground laboratory, Ethan stood quietly while looking at the potion in his hand, and deep thoughts flashed through his eyes.</p>

<p>"Mareti," he spoke slowly.</p>

<p>"Wind up things."</p>

<p>"I want to take this potion and offer it to the Emperor."</p>

<p>The moment those words left his mouth, both Hale and Maverick froze completely, and disbelief immediately appeared on their faces.</p>

<p>"The hell?" Hale blurted out while staring at Ethan with widened eyes.</p>

<p>"Ethan... have you gone insane or what?"</p>
