<p>"Yes... kill mmmmmeeee if you want, but stop humiliating me."</p>
<p>His voice was filled with anger and pain.</p>
<p>Future Ethan looked down at him with a cold expression.</p>
<p>"Didn’t I say to kill yourself if you want to die?"</p>
<p>Agros’ face twitched violently.</p>
<p>The humiliation had reached a level he could barely endure.</p>
<p>"Finish me!"</p>
<p>Future Ethan released his face and stepped back.</p>
<p>"Just kill yourself."</p>
<p>Agros slowly raised his head from the crater.His bloodshot eyes were filled with rage.</p>
<p>The bloody aura around his body began rising again, and the entire battlefield could feel his anger becoming heavier with every passing moment.</p>
<p>Agros slowly pushed himself up from the ground, but Future Ethan did not give him even a moment to recover.</p>
<p>He moved forward and struck him again and again, each attack sending Agros tumbling across the ruined battlefield.</p>
<p>Whenever Agros tried to stand, Future Ethan was already there. A punch sent him flying, a kick forced him into the ground, and another strike knocked him back into the air. Agros tried to defend himself, but every attempt was broken with ease. His bloody aura was scattered by each hit, and his body became covered with more wounds.</p>
<p>Agros’ expression slowly changed from anger to despair. He had tried everything he could think of, yet none of it worked. Future Ethan remained calm from beginning to end, and that calmness hurt Agros more than the attacks themselves. He could not understand how someone could stand before him and treat him like he was nothing. His breathing became heavy as his body shook, and the pressure inside him continued rising until even his own power became difficult to control.</p>
<p>Future Ethan finally stopped and looked at him. Agros was barely standing, with blood covering his body and his knees trembling under his weight. Future Ethan raised one hand and calmly pointed toward him. A thin circle of light appeared in the air around Agros. The circle quickly expanded and formed a large barrier around his entire body.</p>
<p>Agros looked around in confusion.</p>
<p>"What are you doing?"</p>
<p>Future Ethan did not answer.</p>
<p>The barrier suddenly tightened.</p>
<p>BOOM!</p>
<p>A huge force erupted from inside the circle. The ground beneath Agros was torn apart as his body was thrown upward. The destructive power continued rising into the sky while the barrier carried it higher and higher, far away from the battlefield.</p>
<p>Agros’ desperate roar echoed through the sky.</p>
<p>"ETHAAAAAN!"</p>
<p>Future Ethan simply watched from below.</p>
<p>The next moment, the barrier reached high above the battlefield.</p>
<p>BOOOOOOM!</p>
<p>A massive explosion erupted in the sky.</p>
<p>The entire area shook.</p>
<p>A huge wave of energy spread outward as the clouds were blown apart. A bright flash covered the sky while countless pieces of broken energy fell from above and disappeared before reaching the ground.</p>
<p>When the light finally faded, Agros was nowhere to be seen.</p>
<p>Everyone stared at the empty sky in silence.</p>
<p>The being who had appeared with such terrifying power had finally been destroyed by Future Ethan.</p>
<p>"Is it over?"Emperor Alderic asked in amazement as he looked at the long white haired man up above.</p>
<p>"Just what is their realm?"Emperor Caelus asked.</p>
<p>"I don’t know but it’s either Ascension Realm or at another stage above." Queen Ophelia concluded, giving others a shiver.</p>
<p>The black-robed man standing nearby suddenly turned pale.His eyes moved toward the sky before slowly shifting toward Future Ethan.</p>
<p>He understood what had happened.</p>
<p>Agros was dead.</p>
<p>His body trembled.</p>
<p>Without saying anything, he immediately turned around and tried to escape.</p>
<p>Future Ethan noticed him from the corner of his eye.</p>
<p>He sighed softly.</p>
<p>The black-robed figure increased his speed and tried to flee through the ruined space. His heart was beating wildly as he desperately tried to put as much distance between himself and Future Ethan as possible.</p>
<p>Future Ethan simply raised his hand.</p>
<p>"Too troublesome."</p>
<p>A strange chaotic mass of energy slowly gathered above his palm. It was not a simple ball of power. Dark and bright strands twisted around each other while several different forces mixed together in a violent and unstable form.</p>
<p>The black-robed man’s body suddenly froze.</p>
<p>His instincts screamed at him.</p>
<p>His face turned pale.</p>
<p>"What is that..."</p>
<p>He immediately reached into his robe and pulled out an ancient artifact.</p>
<p>The artifact released a powerful protective light that quickly formed a shield around his body.</p>
<p>Future Ethan calmly pointed his finger.</p>
<p>The chaotic mass of energy shot forward.</p>
<p>SWISH!</p>
<p>It crossed the distance almost instantly.</p>
<p>The black-robed man’s pupils contracted.He poured all his power into the artifact.</p>
<p>"Block it!"</p>
<p>BOOOOOOM!</p>
<p>The chaotic attack struck the protective barrier.</p>
<p>For a moment, everything disappeared beneath a blinding flash.</p>
<p>The force of the impact spread across the battlefield and shook the surrounding mountains. The ground cracked and collapsed while dust and debris were thrown high into the air.</p>
<p>No one could see what had happened inside the light.</p>
<p>Everyone could only wait.</p>
<p>Slowly, the blinding flash began to fade.</p>
<p>The dust settled and the battlefield had changed completely.</p>
<p>A huge valley had appeared where the attack had landed. The ground had been torn away for several kilometers, leaving behind a massive crater that stretched across the ruined land.</p>
<p>The surrounding mountains had been destroyed, and thick smoke still rose from the edges of the newly formed valley.</p>
<p>Everyone stared at the destruction in silence.</p>
<p>Future Ethan remained standing in the air as if nothing had happened.</p>
<p>His expression remained calm.</p>
<p>Future Ethan stared at the group that was slowly crawling away in the distance. Their bodies were covered with wounds, and none of them dared to look back at him. The terrifying battle had finally come to an end, and the pressure that had covered the entire area was slowly fading.</p>
<p>Future Ethan remained silent for a moment before speaking.</p>
<p>"It’s done."</p>
<p>His calm voice carried across the ruined battlefield.</p>
<p>"Handle the aftermath yourself."</p>
<p>After saying this, his gaze lingered on Ethan for a few moments. There was no need for more words between the two. Future Ethan simply looked at him before his body slowly became transparent. His divine glow faded away, and his figure finally disappeared into thin air as though he had never been there.</p>
<p>The moment Future Ethan disappeared, everyone turned toward Ethan.</p>
<p>Their gazes were stiff and complicated.</p>
<p>Nobody spoke for a while.</p>
<p>"Where did you get this power?"Queen Ophelia asked in amazement.</p>
