<p>Ethan froze for a split second with genuine confusion flashing across his face.</p>
<p>“Fuckkk!”</p>
<p>“This is why I waste time trying to understand hidden tricks, and those annoying guys still complain.”</p>
<p>Below, Kiea and Herios nearly collapsed as their knees weakened from fear.</p>
<p>“The hidden Commander of the Demon Army has made a move!”</p>
<p>The thought crossed Ethan’s mind instantly. This was no ordinary defensive scroll.</p>
<p>But before he could analyze further, his senses screamed danger.</p>
<p>“Crimson Night Slash!”</p>
<p>SWOOSH!</p>
<p>The world around Ethan suddenly darkened. Light seemed to vanish as a chilling crimson intent filled the air.</p>
<p>From within that darkness, an extremely agile figure appeared silently behind him.</p>
<p>The attacker held two curved blades wrapped in blood-like energy that pulsed like living flesh. The energy gathered together and formed something resembling a crimson moon.</p>
<p>Without hesitation, the figure slashed toward Ethan’s neck.The attack carried enough power to split a mountain in half.</p>
<p>The timing was flawless.</p>
<p>There was no warning, no killing intent beforehand, and no presence Ethan could detect earlier.</p>
<p>He had not even sensed the enemy approaching.</p>
<p>“Shield!”</p>
<p>Ethan reacted instantly.</p>
<p>A protective barrier flared around his body, glowing brightly as layers of mana condensed before him.</p>
<p>CRACK!</p>
<p>The curved blade struck the shield and produced a sharp ringing sound. The barrier shattered almost immediately under the terrifying cutting force.</p>
<p>But that single moment was enough.</p>
<p>Ethan twisted his body backward and retreated rapidly.</p>
<p>SWISH!</p>
<p>The blade descended and cut through where his body had stood.</p>
<p>The attack sliced apart only a fading afterimage.</p>
<p>Ethan reappeared several meters away, boots sliding across the frozen ground as his eyes narrowed sharply.Someone far more dangerous had entered the battlefield.</p>
<p>SWOOSH!</p>
<p>As Ethan reappeared several meters away, his boots slid across the shattered ground before he finally stopped. He paused suddenly when he felt something warm trickling down his throat.</p>
<p>He raised his hand and touched his neck.</p>
<p>Blood.</p>
<p>A thin cut had appeared across his skin.</p>
<p>His breathing grew slightly uneven for a moment.</p>
<p>That was quite close.</p>
<p>If his reaction had been even a fraction slower, that strike might have taken his head clean off.</p>
<p>Ethan slowly lifted his gaze and looked around the battlefield. Smoke drifted through the air while broken weapons and frozen blood covered the ground. However, no enemy could be seen.</p>
<p>The attacker had vanished again.</p>
<p>His eyes narrowed.</p>
<p>The opponent clearly possessed an extremely superior hiding technique. Even with Ethan’s heightened perception, he could not lock onto her presence.</p>
<p>Suddenly—</p>
<p>SWOOSH!</p>
<p>A sharp killing intent flashed behind him.</p>
<p>Ethan twisted his body instantly and dodged.</p>
<p>SWOOSH! SWISH!</p>
<p>Another strike followed immediately from a different angle. Twin blades sliced through the air repeatedly, each attack silent yet deadly.</p>
<p>For the next few moments, Ethan focused entirely on defense. He stepped, turned, and blocked while narrowly avoiding strike after strike. Each attack targeted vital points with terrifying precision.</p>
<p>The battlefield around him cracked under the pressure of their movements.</p>
<p>After several exchanges, Ethan’s eyes gradually calmed.</p>
<p>He had begun to understand.</p>
<p>After fighting for this long, he had more or less grasped the combat style and strength of this hidden figure.</p>
<p>Despite his numerous boosts and overwhelming physical ability, this opponent had managed to fight him evenly for a noticeable period.</p>
<p>That alone proved one thing.</p>
<p>This figure was far from ordinary.</p>
<p>Based on his observation, the attacker was most likely a famous Legendary Realm expert specializing in stealth assassination.</p>
<p>“This figure’s identity is definitely not simple,” Ethan thought quietly.</p>
<p>Another attack came.</p>
<p>This time, instead of dodging, Ethan deliberately slowed his movement.</p>
<p>The hidden figure seized the chance.</p>
<p>Twin daggers pierced forward toward his chest.</p>
<p>At the last instant—</p>
<p>Ethan moved.</p>
<p>His hand shot forward and grabbed the incoming blades directly.</p>
<p>The daggers pierced into his palm.</p>
<p>Blood burst outward instantly, staining both weapons crimson.</p>
<p>The assassin froze in shock.</p>
<p>Before she could react, Ethan tightened his grip and pulled forward violently.</p>
<p>BOOM!</p>
<p>He slammed his forehead straight into hers.</p>
<p>A powerful shockwave exploded outward as the invisible figure was forced into visibility.</p>
<p>Before him appeared a black-skinned demoness assassin. Her sharp crimson eyes widened in disbelief while dark markings flickered across her face.</p>
<p>“You!”</p>
<p>She clearly had not expected a human to catch her attack barehanded.</p>
<p>Without hesitation, she abandoned the daggers and retreated rapidly.</p>
<p>“Where are you running?” Ethan said coldly.</p>
<p>He casually pulled the embedded dagger from his palm and threw it aside.</p>
<p>To the demoness’ horror, the torn flesh of his hand regenerated instantly. Muscles reformed and skin closed as if nothing had happened.</p>
<p>Her figure dispersed again into shadows.</p>
<p>Hidden once more, she circled silently while taking a deep breath.</p>
<p>Yet something felt wrong.</p>
<p>For some reason, she felt as though the human was watching her.</p>
<p>“No… this isn’t possible,” she thought nervously. “The power of stealth is a divine blessing from the God of Darkness. It cannot be seen through easily.”</p>
<p>Suppressing her unease, she prepared her final attack.</p>
<p>This time she gathered divine shadow energy into her blades. Even if she suffered injury, she intended to kill Ethan in one decisive strike.</p>
<p>She moved silently toward his back.</p>
<p>Closer.</p>
<p>Closer.</p>
<p>Then suddenly—</p>
<p>Cold fear crawled up her spine.</p>
<p>“GOT YOU!”</p>
<p>The shout shattered her concentration.</p>
<p>Ethan turned abruptly and swung his sword.</p>
<p>Holy power erupted from the blade like a rising sun.</p>
<p>CLANG!!!</p>
<p>The collision exploded with thunderous force. Light and darkness clashed violently, creating shockwaves that blasted everything nearby apart. Snow, corpses, and shattered stone were thrown high into the air.</p>
<p>The demoness was sent flying like a broken arrow.</p>
<p>Her body crashed through the battlefield and carved a deep trench across the land before finally stopping miles away.</p>
<p>She struggled to rise—</p>
<p>BOOM! BOOM! BOOM! BOOM! BOOM!</p>
<p>Five massive swords made of pure light descended from the sky.</p>
<p>Each blade slammed into the ground around her, forming a perfect circle. The impact shattered the earth and raised towering pillars of radiant energy that locked space itself.</p>
<p>The glowing swords hummed loudly, creating chains of light that bound the surrounding area.</p>
<p>Before she could escape, Ethan appeared in front of her.</p>
<p>His fingers twisted rapidly, forming sealing signs.</p>
<p>“SEAL!”</p>
<p>A blinding flash erupted instantly.</p>
<p>Radiant light surged upward like a prison descending from heaven, completely engulfing the demoness assassin and locking her movements in place.</p>
