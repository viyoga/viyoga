<p>The beam collided.</p>

<p>For a brief instant, light swallowed everything.</p>

<p>The world went silent, as if sound itself had been erased. Then the impact detonated outward. The shockwave ripped through the land, flattening everything in its path. The ground split open like broken glass, mountains trembled, and even the sky twisted under the violent force.</p>

<p>Rayon’s scream was cut short.</p>

<p>The beam shattered and exploded. Ethan forced Rayon’s body straight through it. The energy dispersed wildly, exploding in all directions, but not a single strand of it reached Ethan as he casted a magical barrier around that deflected the attack.</p>

<p>He stood at the center of the chaos, unmoved, his grip still locked around Rayon as if nothing had happened.</p>

<p>The dust slowly settled, and what remained was silence.</p>

<p>Damor stood frozen, his sword still raised in the air, his eyes wide and hollow. His attack had failed and it had been erased.</p>

<p>Ethan stepped forward.</p>

<p>Rayon’s body dangled limply in his hand. Blood dripped steadily onto the shattered ground below, forming dark stains. His eyes were unfocused, filled with terror so deep that it had completely broken him. Half of his skull was cracked, and the skin over the front of his face had torn away, exposing ruined flesh beneath. Pieces hung loosely, threatening to fall apart at any moment.</p>

<p>And somehow, unbelievably, he was still breathing.</p>

<p>His lips trembled as broken words spilled out between sobs.</p>

<p>“I… I didn’t know… I swear… I didn’t know he was your son… please… please…”</p>

<p>His voice cracked, turning hoarse as panic consumed him entirely.</p>

<p>“I’ll do anything… anything… please don’t kill me…”</p>

<p>Ethan looked at him.</p>

<p>There was no anger on his face now.He just looked at Rayon as if he was looking at some pathetic guy.</p>

<p>“You already did,” Ethan said quietly.</p>

<p>He released his grip.</p>

<p>Rayon collapsed to the ground like discarded trash, coughing violently and clawing at the dirt as his body convulsed. Before he could even draw another breath, a foot slammed down on his head.</p>

<p>BOOOOM!</p>

<p>A crater erupted as his head was driven into the ground, the impact snapping his neck completely. His body went limp, twitching once before going still.</p>

<p>Damor’s hands began to tremble uncontrollably.</p>

<p>His sword suddenly felt unbearably heavy in his grip.</p>

<p>For the first time since stepping into the Legendary Realm, doubt crawled into his heart. His breathing became uneven, and cold sweat poured down his back as he stared at Ethan walking toward him.</p>

<p>Each footstep shattered the ground beneath Ethan’s feet and crushed Damor’s will even further.</p>

<p>“Y-you…” Damor tried to speak, but his throat felt dry, and no sound would come out.</p>

<p>He wanted to run, desperately, but his pride stood in the way like a chain around his neck.</p>

<p>Run.</p>

<p>Run.</p>

<p>Run.</p>

<p>The thought screamed louder and louder in his head, until fear finally won. He turned around to flee.</p>

<p>He barely managed to take a single step.</p>

<p>Six swords of pure light slammed into the ground around him, sealing every escape.</p>

<p>BOOOM!</p>

<p>Damor froze in place, his face drained of all color.</p>

<p>Ethan stopped in front of him and looked down.</p>

<p>Damor roared, forcing his fear down as he lifted his sword with everything he had left. Power surged violently around him, unstable and desperate, tearing at his own body.</p>

<p>“DIE!” he screamed, slashing downward with all his remaining strength.</p>

<p>Ethan raised one finger.</p>

<p>The sword stopped.</p>

<p>The blade trembled inches from Ethan’s skin, unable to move forward no matter how much power Damor poured into it. Cracks spread rapidly across the sword as the force reversed, screaming back at its owner.</p>

<p>Damor’s eyes widened in pure horror.</p>

<p>“No… No… this is impossible…”</p>

<p>Ethan flicked his finger.</p>

<p>CRACK!</p>

<p>The sword shattered into fragments that flew past Damor’s face, cutting his cheek and drawing blood. The shock sent him stumbling backward, his legs giving out as he collapsed to his knees.</p>

<p>Ethan grabbed Damor by the hair and forced his head up.</p>

<p>“Pride,” Ethan said coldly. “You keep saying that word.”</p>

<p>Damor gasped, his eyes bloodshot and unfocused, his body trembling violently. He raised his hand, trying to strike Ethan, but his arm suddenly felt empty.</p>

<p>“My power… What happened to my power?” Damor shouted in panic.</p>

<p>Ethan ignored him.</p>

<p>“You don’t even understand what it means.”</p>

<p>“Shut up… ba—KURGHHH!”</p>

<p>Before he could finish, Ethan grabbed his face firmly.</p>

<p>“Noisy.”</p>

<p>Ethan slammed Damor’s head into the ground.</p>

<p>BOOOM!</p>

<p>The earth caved inward, forming a deep crater. Damor screamed in agony, his voice breaking as blood spilled from his mouth and soaked into the dirt.</p>

<p>Ethan did not stop.</p>

<p>He lifted Damor again and threw him down with a heavy suplex slam.</p>

<p>BANGGHGG!</p>

<p>Damor’s head smashed directly into the ground, his body going limp as his consciousness flickered.</p>

<p>Ethan clapped his hands once, sharp and loud. The pressure eased slightly, making everyone else release a breath they did not realize they were holding.</p>

<p>Just as they were about to rejoice, Ethan’s figure slowly floated into the sky.</p>

<p>He looked sharply at Amber and spoke, his voice calm and terrifying.</p>

<p>“He isn’t dead. Be careful and don’t let him escape. I need to torture him.”</p>

<p>Amber nodded obediently, her face pale but resolute.</p>

<p>With those words, Ethan’s figure shot forward.</p>

<p>Ray stepped ahead and shouted, “Father, where are you going!”</p>

<p>“To finish digging the root.”</p>

<p>Ethan’s voice echoed through the land, followed by a deafening sonic boom as he vanished from sight.</p>

<p>….</p>

<p>Far away from the ruined battlefield, beyond shattered mountains and scorched plains, a massive demonic camp stood like a dark fortress carved into the land.</p>

<p>The air there was thick with corrupted mana, heavy enough to make breathing feel oppressive. Black banners marked with twisted symbols fluttered above tall iron poles, and the ground was reinforced with layered stone platforms and metal plates that showed signs of long use and careful maintenance</p>

<p>It was the base camp set by Demons.</p>
