<p>"What is this...?"</p>

<p>That was the only question flashing through everyone’s mind.</p>

<p>An indescribable fear quietly rose from the depths of their hearts. It was not something they wanted to admit, but the feeling of death had subconsciously appeared the moment Ethan stepped out of the pillar of light. His calm expression, the burning crimson armor covering his body, and the overwhelming pressure surrounding him made it feel as though they were not facing a single man, but a walking disaster.</p>

<p>Fear?</p>

<p>Death?</p>

<p>Ridiculous.</p>

<p>It was completely ridiculous.</p>

<p>Ethan was standing there all alone while they had tens of thousands of soldiers behind them, along with countless Saint Realm experts and even two Transcendent Knights. No matter how strong Ethan was, he was still only one person.</p>

<p>There was nothing to fear. There was absolutely no reason for an entire army to hesitate before a single man. As everyone continued convincing themselves, the uneasiness in their hearts gradually turned into anger.</p>

<p>Duke Valen was the first to suppress the fear growing inside him. He took a deep breath, tightened his grip around his weapon, and stepped forward with a determined expression.</p>

<p>"I refuse to believe one man can scare all of us."</p>

<p>BOOM!</p>

<p>The ground exploded beneath his feet as he rushed straight toward Ethan with astonishing speed. His aura erupted without restraint, causing powerful winds to sweep across the battlefield. Everyone immediately focused their attention on the Duke, believing he would at least force Ethan back.</p>

<p>However...</p>

<p>He had barely taken a single step.</p>

<p>Ethan’s figure suddenly blurred.</p>

<p>The crimson flames surrounding him scattered into the air as though his body had simply disappeared.</p>

<p>Duke Valen’s pupils violently shrank.</p>

<p>"So fas..."</p>

<p>Before the thought could even finish forming inside his mind...</p>

<p>THUD!</p>

<p>Something heavily struck the side of his face.</p>

<p>It was Ethan’s foot.</p>

<p>The kick landed with terrifying force. Duke Valen’s head violently snapped sideways while his entire body bent downward as though an invisible mountain had crashed into him.</p>

<p>BOOOOOOOOOOM!</p>

<p>His body slammed straight into the ground with a brutal impact. The upper layer of earth instantly shattered apart as a massive crater formed beneath him. Thick cracks rapidly spread across the battlefield while broken rocks and dirt exploded high into the air. The violent shockwave forced everyone nearby to stagger backward.</p>

<p>The surrounding commanders froze.</p>

<p>Their eyes widened in complete disbelief.</p>

<p>"Duke!"</p>

<p>Several shocked cries echoed throughout the battlefield.</p>

<p>"No!"</p>

<p>"How is that possible?"</p>

<p>"He couldn’t even react!"</p>

<p>The nobles stared blankly at the crater while their minds went completely blank.</p>

<p>The battle had only begun, yet Duke Valen had already been smashed into the ground with a single kick. Before anyone could recover from their shock, Ethan calmly stepped forward and stood over the fallen Duke as though nothing had happened. His face remained expressionless, and he did not even bother looking down at the man beneath his feet. To Ethan, Duke Valen no longer seemed worthy of another glance.</p>

<p>The sight made everyone’s hearts tremble.</p>

<p>"Attack!"</p>

<p>One of the Transcendent Knights suddenly roared.</p>

<p>The hesitation instantly disappeared.</p>

<p>BOOOOOOM!</p>

<p>The two Transcendent Knights exploded forward at the same time. Their terrifying auras swept across the battlefield while several Saint</p>

<p>Realm experts followed closely behind them without the slightest delay. Powerful sword energy burst through the air as countless magic circles rapidly appeared around Ethan. Blades, spears, palm strikes, and destructive spells rushed toward him from every direction, sealing every possible path of escape.</p>

<p>The battlefield instantly erupted into complete chaos.Facing the overwhelming assault, Ethan remained unusually calm.</p>

<p>He slowly raised one hand.</p>

<p>A crimson barrier instantly expanded around his body.</p>

<p>BANG!</p>

<p>BANG!</p>

<p>BANG!</p>

<p>Several attacks slammed directly into the barrier, creating deafening explosions one after another. The transparent crimson shield trembled violently under the barrage, yet it refused to shatter.</p>

<p>The two Transcendent Knights immediately changed tactics. One attacked from above while the other rushed in from Ethan’s blind spot. At the same time, the Saint Realm experts spread out and surrounded him from every direction. Their attacks came one after another without leaving even the smallest opening.</p>

<p>Ethan’s eyes calmly swept across the battlefield.</p>

<p>Instead of meeting every attack head-on, his body moved with astonishing precision.</p>

<p>SWISH!</p>

<p>He sidestepped one sword strike by the width of a finger.</p>

<p>WHOOSH!</p>

<p>He leaned backward just enough for another blade to whistle past his throat.</p>

<p>A spear thrust toward his ribs, but Ethan twisted his waist and let it pass by before taking another step forward. He slipped through the tiny gaps between attacks with smooth and effortless movements, almost as though he had already seen every attack before it happened.</p>

<p>The attacks that missed Ethan continued flying forward.</p>

<p>BOOOOOOM!</p>

<p>A massive sword slash tore through several military tents and reduced them to splinters.</p>

<p>Another attack smashed directly into a supply wagon, sending broken wood and burning supplies flying in every direction.</p>

<p>A blazing spear of energy crashed into the rear of the camp where groups of Allied soldiers were trying to retreat.</p>

<p>"Watch out!"</p>

<p>The soldiers screamed as they desperately scattered.</p>

<p>The explosion threw several of them into the air while others were knocked violently across the ground by the shockwave. Horses panicked and broke free from their reins, supply carts overturned, and fires rapidly spread throughout the camp. The orderly battlefield quickly descended into complete confusion.</p>

<p>"Stop!"</p>

<p>"You’re hitting our own men!"</p>

<p>One commander shouted in alarm while trying to reorganize the soldiers.</p>

<p>But nobody could stop anymore.</p>

<p>The two Transcendent Knights continued attacking without giving Ethan even a moment to breathe. The Saint Realm experts relentlessly followed behind them while powerful spells rained down across the battlefield. Yet Ethan continued weaving through every attack with unbelievable footwork while only raising his crimson barrier whenever an unavoidable strike came his way.</p>

<p>BOOM!</p>

<p>BANG!</p>

<p>CLANG!</p>

<p>Violent explosions echoed one after another while flames, shattered rocks, broken tents, and clouds of dust filled the battlefield. Even amidst the endless attacks, Ethan never stopped moving forward.</p>

<p>Every single step he took made the pressure in everyone’s hearts grow heavier.</p>

<p>Instead of feeling as though they were hunting one man...</p>

<p>It slowly began to feel as though one man was hunting an entire army.</p>
