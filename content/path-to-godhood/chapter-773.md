<p>She looked down at the documents again.</p>
<p>"People who might escape the net might harbor ill intentions toward us. They might not be able to harm us, but what about the kids?"</p>
<p>Her voice became more serious when she mentioned the children.</p>
<p>If this had been before, Mira might have rushed forward without thinking too much. She had once been driven by nothing but her hatred for Halcrest. But now she had children and a peaceful life. She had more things to protect, so she could no longer act blindly.</p>
<p>"Instead of finishing them outright, why don't you ask the Emperor to dismantle the head and support others to take up?"</p>
<p>Ethan's eyes lit up.</p>
<p>He had already been pondering about this.</p>
<p>The aristocratic families were huge and had many descendants spread across different places. It was almost certain that some of them would harbour resentment toward their own family. Mira herself was an example of this.</p>
<p>If they could remove the people at the top and support those who were willing to cooperate, they could weaken the families without destroying everyone connected to them.</p>
<p>"Good."</p>
<p>Ethan stood up and walked toward her. He placed both hands on Mira's shoulders and looked at her seriously.</p>
<p>"As expected from my wife."</p>
<p>"Mira, I want you to lead the Halcrest matters. I will also find others to help."</p>
<p>Mira looked at him quietly.</p>
<p>"You don't need to suppress your desire, and you can do what you want. Don't wipe out the hatred hidden inside you for the bigger picture."</p>
