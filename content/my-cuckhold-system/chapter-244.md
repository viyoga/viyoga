<p>They secured access to study it later.</p>
<p>After about fifteen minutes more of searching, they found a movement based art for bloodline users. This would be suitable for Nina no doubt because she was very slow when it came to close quarter combats.</p>
<p>...</p>
<p>Next, they proceeded to search for suitable battle arts for Jax.</p>
<p>It took over an hour to see anything that worked for both ice and katana. Most were just seperate battle arts.</p>
<p>Until finally they stumbled upon one...</p>
<p>~ Frozen Meridian Saber Art ~</p>
<p>A hybrid art merging swordplay with internal elemental flow... designed for ice bloodlines.</p>
<p>Jax whispered. "...This was made for me."</p>
<p>West nodded.</p>
<p>There were no arguments here. They scoped the wing deeper and soon spotted another.</p>
<p>Glacial Severance Steps</p>
<p>It was a movement battle art that amplified speed on frozen surfaces.</p>
<p>Jax was practically glowing as he thought about the multitude of ways he could apply it. "I’m learning this."</p>
<p>"You’re marrying it," Nina shook her head.</p>
<p>After a couple minutes more, then finally moved onto the summoner wing.</p>
<p>This section felt stranger like older secrets lived here.</p>
<p>Manuals for beast integration... summoned battlefield formations... multi-entity synchronization...</p>
<p>All sorts of arts in relation with summoner, lined the path.</p>
<p>West skipped a lot of arts until a particular title made him pause.</p>
<p>~ Triad Dominion Method ~</p>
<p>His eyes narrowed as he stared at the info. Three-summon resonance art...</p>
<p>"Resonance?" West looked further at the inscriptions on its surface and realized that it synced summoners with their summons in ways that the branches doesn’t.</p>
<p>When he grabbed it to check the contents, he realized they were incomplete. At the last page, it pointed out that the other part was located on a higher floor.</p>
<p>Unfortunately, that floor was restricted to them due to Shadow Reapers current rank.</p>
<p>Laughter suddenly rang out from nearby.</p>
<p>Four awakeners who seemed to have noticed them from their arrival here bumped into them while returning from higher floors.</p>
<p>Their gang jackets were marked with a flame insignia. Burning cloud gang... they were no doubt higher in rank.</p>
<p>One sneered. "Well well."</p>
<p>"Shadow Reapers."</p>
<p>Another laughed. "More like bottom dwellers."</p>
<p>The third added. "Didn’t know starter gangs got library cards."</p>
<p>Jax turned slowly with a smile. "Oh?"</p>
<p>He tilted his head. "You lot talk a lot for decorative traffic cones."</p>
<p>West immediately covered his mouth to stop laughing while the expressions of the four immediately turned offended.</p>
<p>"What?"</p>
<p>Jax continued. "You all look like the kind of guys who compare dick sizes in school bathrooms."</p>
<p>West snorted while Nina actually choked laughing.</p>
<p>One of the gang boys stepped forward furious. "You wanna say that again?"</p>
<p>Jax folded arms. "I said your whole squad looks like failed character customization."</p>
<p>The four erupted in anguish.</p>
<p>"Yours against mine, we’ll fold every last one of you," the tallest stepped forward.</p>
<p>Jax cracked his neck. "We don’t even need that."</p>
<p>"I alone can handle you four."</p>
<p>West muttered. "We can get banned... don’t fight in here."</p>
<p>Jax immediately backed down upon hearing that but it only made the four louder.</p>
<p>"Ohhh," one mocked.</p>
<p>"What was that?"</p>
<p>"Scaredy cat?"</p>
<p>"Yeah that’s what I thought."</p>
<p>"Weaklings."</p>
<p>One pushed closer, deliberately bumped Nina’s shoulder as they stepped away.</p>
<p>A hand clamped his shoulder from behind before they could take another step.</p>
<p>CRACK!</p>
<p>A bone shattering sound echoed causing the guy to scream out as his arm twisted.</p>
<p>Before anyone could process what was happening, West leaned close.</p>
<p>"Ohhh sorry."</p>
<p>"My bad."</p>
<p>"I just wanted your attention."</p>
<p>He looked almost concerned.</p>
<p>"I didn’t realize your arm was so weak."</p>
<p>Then stepped forward with a look of concern like he wanted to help and ’accidentally’ clipped the guy’s leg with his foot.</p>
<p>SNAP~</p>
<p>The knee folded in the wrong angle causing the man to collapse shrieking.</p>
<p>West blinked theatrically. "Oh... my bad."</p>
<p>"Didn’t even see you there."</p>
<p>Nina covered her mouth trying not to laugh while Jax held his ribs as his cheeks swelled.</p>
<p>The other three exploded. "You bastard!"</p>
<p>The moment they charged at West, a pressure wave hit.</p>
<p>BOOM.</p>
<p>A tall figure dropped between them with his voice resounding like thunder.</p>
<p>"ENOUGH."</p>
<p>It was a security awakener.</p>
<p>He looked at the broken man before staring at West with a somehow impressed look.</p>
<p>"What happened?"</p>
<p>West raised both hands. "Terrible accident."</p>
<p>Jax turned away pretending not to laugh while the security awakener stared.</p>
<p>He did not believe one word.</p>
<p>The three gang members pointed furiously. "He attacked him!"</p>
<p>West shook his head innocently. "Like I said terrible accident. Was only reaching out to inform that he bumped into my teammate... had no idea he had such fragile limbs."</p>
<p>Security’s eyes shifted to Nina and then to the aggressors.</p>
<p>"You need to be more careful Mr," his eyes moved back to West.</p>
<p>"Absolutely, please, here is my card, I’ll handle the hospital bills," West approached and handed his card over to one of them.</p>
<p>"You..."</p>
<p>Before they could protest, the security official interrupted. "Looks like it’s just a misunderstanding."</p>
<p>He proceeded to disperse everyone in order to avoid traffic and the four had to step out with their injured teammate, all furious and unable to do anything.</p>
<p>Jax burst laughing while Nina leaned against a shelf laughing too.</p>
<p>West looked down at the manual still in hand. "...Where were we?"</p>
<p>Jax wheezed. "You accidentally crippled a man."</p>
<p>West shrugged. "He had weak bones."</p>
<p>Nina pointed at him. "You are a menace."</p>
<p>West grinned. "And we haven’t even picked my battle art yet."</p>
<p>The aftermath of the encounter inside the Awakened Battle Arts Hall didn’t linger for long.</p>
<p>West finalized his choices as he stood before a hovering crystalline archive with its surface flickering with layered inscriptions.</p>
<p>"Alright," he muttered, scrolling through the projection. "Let’s lock this in."</p>
<p>First, he picked the Triad Dominion Method.</p>
<p>Although incomplete, even in its current state, it resonated with him in a way nothing else had.</p>
<p>He selected it and went for a perception-based art next.</p>
<p>Spectral Familiar Link: Ocular Sync Variant.</p>
<p>This one allowed the user to temporarily see through the senses of their summons.</p>
