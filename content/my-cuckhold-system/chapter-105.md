<p>The evening air was cooler than West expected.</p>
<p>Streetlights had flickered on, casting long orange shadows across the pavement. The faint buzz of traffic drifted from the main road as West walked back from the bus stop.</p>
<p>Nina had left reluctantly after three hot and passionate rounds. West didn’t even know where she got the energy from...</p>
<p>He thought all girls were like Aria who couldn’t handle anymore hard-core pounding after their brains had been fucked out... he thought wrong.</p>
<p>Fortunately, his stamina points were high.</p>
<p>West shoved his hands into his pockets, exhaling slowly.</p>
<p>Sunday nights were usually quiet and peaceful but peace rarely lasted long around him.</p>
<p>He had barely turned into the side street that led toward his apartment complex when a figure slammed into him hard.</p>
<p>West didn’t move.</p>
<p>The other person bounced off him like they had collided with a wall.</p>
<p>A gasp escaped soft lips.</p>
<p>"I’m sorry—I—"</p>
<p>West looked down and the face he spotted made his eyes narrow in response...</p>
<p>It was Lena.</p>
<p>Her hair was disheveled and her mascara faintly smudged. She was breathing unevenly like she had been running.</p>
<p>She froze when she realized who she had collided with.</p>
<p>"West...?"</p>
<p>Her shock shifted almost instantly into relief.</p>
<p>"Oh my God—West!"</p>
<p>She grabbed his arm.</p>
<p>"Please—please help me."</p>
<p>West’s expression didn’t change.</p>
<p>"...Help you?"</p>
<p>She nodded frantically.</p>
<p>"I’m being followed."</p>
<p>He tilted his head slightly.</p>
<p>"By?"</p>
<p>Her voice lowered.</p>
<p>"Caleb."</p>
<p>West stared at her blankly.</p>
<p>"You’re still rolling around with that loser?"</p>
<p>The words hit, but Lena didn’t argue.</p>
<p>"I know I don’t deserve your help," she rushed out. "I know that."</p>
<p>West folded his arms.</p>
<p>"Then why are you here?"</p>
<p>She swallowed.</p>
<p>"He begged me to come over today. Said he wanted to apologize. Said he regretted everything."</p>
<p>"And you believed him?"</p>
<p>"I didn’t think he’d lose his mind," she said, panic creeping back into her voice. "He started accusing me of going back to you. He got angry. Really angry."</p>
<p>West’s eyes sharpened slightly.</p>
<p>"And?"</p>
<p>"He wouldn’t let it go. I tried leaving and he followed me."</p>
<p>As if summoned by her fear, a familiar figure emerged at the far end of the street.</p>
<p>It was Caleb.</p>
<p>His face was twisted with rage... and when his eyes locked onto the scene before him that depicted Lena pressed up against West in desperation, something in him snapped.</p>
<p>"So I was right!" Caleb roared.</p>
<p>He pointed accusingly.</p>
<p>"You bitch! You really went back to him!"</p>
<p>Pedestrians on the sidewalk slowed while some stopped entirely.</p>
<p>Caleb’s voice echoed down the street.</p>
<p>"I’m gonna fuck your brains out today so you remember who the best man is!"</p>
<p>He charged forward blindly without hesitation... forgetting one crucial detail...</p>
<p>This was West.</p>
<p>West calmly stepped in front of Lena, moved her aside gently and threw a single punch.</p>
<p>His fist connected with Caleb’s jaw and a loud impactful sound rang out.</p>
<p>Bam!</p>
<p>Caleb’s body lifted off the ground as he flew backward and crashed straight through the glass panel of a nearby shop.</p>
<p>Bang!</p>
<p>Shards exploded outward as Caleb’s body tumbled inside and landed heavily against a shelf before going completely still.</p>
<p>Silence fell over the street as people stared.</p>
<p>Someone whispered—</p>
<p>"Is that...?"</p>
<p>"...An awakened..."</p>
<p>Phones were already out causing West to click his tongue softly.</p>
<p>He didn’t need this kind of attention.</p>
<p>He turned to leave but Lena followed immediately.</p>
<p>"Wait—West!"</p>
<p>He didn’t slow down.</p>
<p>"I told you to stay away from me."</p>
<p>"I know—I know!" she pleaded. "Please don’t leave me."</p>
<p>"I took care of your guy," West replied flatly. "Don’t bother me anymore."</p>
<p>"He’ll wake up!" she said urgently. "He could come to my place later when you’re not around. He could send people."</p>
<p>West stopped walking for a bit and slowly turned.</p>
<p>"I can’t go home yet," she continued desperately. "Please... just help me for tonight."</p>
<p>He studied her expression... noticing that she looked shaken with genuine fear.</p>
<p>He didn’t owe her anything, not especially after how things ended between them but still...</p>
<p>Since he had already gotten involved, he decided that he might as well just see this through to the end.</p>
<p>He exhaled quietly, thinking of a quick solution.</p>
<p>He couldn’t take her to his apartment.</p>
<p>If Mark was home—or returning soon—it would complicate things.</p>
<p>And Gor’thala or Seraphyra might make things even worse.</p>
<p>West glanced down the street and spotted a cheap motel sign a few blocks away.</p>
<p>"...Fine."</p>
<p>Relief flooded Lena’s face.</p>
<p>"Thank you."</p>
<p>"Don’t misunderstand," he added coldly. "This doesn’t mean anything."</p>
<p>She nodded quickly as they walked in silence.</p>
<p>---</p>
<p>The motel wasn’t glamorous.</p>
<p>Flickering neon sign... old carpets... dim hallway lighting.</p>
<p>West paid for a single room without hesitation.</p>
<p>The receptionist eyed him briefly but didn’t ask questions.</p>
<p>Moments later, they stepped inside the room.</p>
<p>It was small with only a single bed and a chair as well as a small desk.</p>
<p>Lena stood near the door with her arms wrapped around herself.</p>
<p>West glanced around once, satisfied it was secure.</p>
<p>"I’ll go now."</p>
<p>He turned toward the door but Lena moved quickly.</p>
<p>She grabbed him from behind, wrapping her arms around his torso and pressing her forehead against his back.</p>
<p>"I’m sorry... for everything... I should have never left you..." she whispered.</p>
<p>West’s chuckled humorlessly.</p>
<p>"You’re only saying that now because I’m awakened."</p>
<p>She flinched slightly.</p>
<p>"That’s not—"</p>
<p>He moved to pry her arms off with an utterly unimpressed look.</p>
<p>"Don’t bother me anymore."</p>
<p>"Please," she said again as her voice cracked. "Just stay a little longer. I’m scared of being alone."</p>
<p>Her grip tightened as a small sob escaped her lips and she began to cry.</p>
<p>West looked down at her hands around him.</p>
<p>Crocodile tears...</p>
<p>Or maybe not.</p>
<p>Either way, he wasn’t falling for it emotionally.</p>
<p>He turned around slowly.</p>
<p>"Did you break up with Caleb?"</p>
<p>She shook her head weakly.</p>
<p>"No."</p>
<p>"I will," she rushed out. "Next time I see him. Or—I’ll send him a breakup text right now."</p>
<p>She reached for her phone in her pocket but West’s eyes flicked down.</p>
<p>"No."</p>
<p>She hesitated.</p>
<p>"What?"</p>
<p>"Drop the phone."</p>
<p>She slowly lowered it onto the desk.</p>
<p>West stepped back and sat on the edge of the bed, resting his arms on his thighs.</p>
<p>’This is the perfect opportunity for that...’</p>
<p>Internally, West smiled deviously but on the surface he appeared completely calm.</p>
