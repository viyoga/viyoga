<p>As the body pieces were pulled together, a massive chunk of the collapsing ceiling fell.</p>
<p>CRASH!!!</p>
<p>It slammed directly onto the disk’s location, burying those pieces beneath tons of debris.</p>
<p>West let out a soft exhale.</p>
<p>"...That should slow you down."</p>
<p>Without wasting any more time, he grabbed his sword, turned and ran.</p>
<p>—</p>
<p>[ SKILL ACTIVATED: Radiant Acceleration ]</p>
<p>His body shot forward with a bright glow, turning the surroundings into a blur as everything dashed past him.</p>
<p>Behind him, the ruins collapsed faster with walls crumbling, ground splitting and entire sections falling apart.</p>
<p>West pushed harder, ignoring the pain and blood as another massive chunk missed him by inches while he ran.</p>
<p>"...Almost there."</p>
<p>What had once been a structured, albeit corrupted, underground world was now falling apart at an alarming rate.</p>
<p>At the surface, where the final level of the abandoned research facility was situated, the ground itself began to cave in.</p>
<p>Dust rose in thick clouds as concrete split and metal beams bent and snapped with ear-piercing screeches.</p>
<p>And at the center of it all, was the ruins entrance on the brink of shutting down.</p>
<p>Nina, Jax, Derek, Jared, and the others stood gathered around the collapsing entry point with tense looks.</p>
<p>Their eyes locked on the unstable opening below.</p>
<p>"...He won’t make it..." one of them muttered with a low shaky tone.</p>
<p>"He will," Jax shot back immediately, clenching his fists.</p>
<p>"But that collapse—"</p>
<p>"I said he will!"</p>
<p>Their voices overlapped as they waited around desperately, ignoring the fact that their surroundings were also being affected by the collapsing ruins.</p>
<p>Nina stood at the very edge with her hands trembling slightly. Her eyes never left the opening.</p>
<p>"West..." she whispered as the rumbling intensified.</p>
<p>Chunks of the surrounding structure began falling inward as the hole widened for a bit and then began shrinking.</p>
<p>"...Look!" Derek suddenly shouted.</p>
<p>Far in the distance, they spotted a figure moving at incredibly fast pace.</p>
<p>"West!"</p>
<p>His silhouette cut through the dust cloud as he dashed toward the collapsing exit.</p>
<p>Their faces were flooded with expressions of relief.</p>
<p>"He’s coming!"</p>
<p>"West!!!"</p>
<p>They all shouted at once with hopeful gazes.</p>
<p>Suddenly...</p>
<p>CRRRRRAAAASH!!!</p>
<p>The ground gave way completely and an entire section collapsed inward with a thunderous explosion of debris.</p>
<p>The entrance to the ruins was suddenly sealed shut under tons of concrete, steel, and rock as the rumbling continued for several more seconds before coming to a final stop.</p>
<p>Where the entrance to the ruins once stood, was now nothing but rubble.</p>
<p>"...No..." Nina eyes widened as her body began to tremble violently.</p>
<p>"...No... no no no..."</p>
<p>"He... he didn’t make it..." Jared muttered, stepping back slightly.</p>
<p>Jax’s fists clenched.</p>
<p>"...No..."</p>
<p>Nina staggered forward.</p>
<p>"WEST!!!"</p>
<p>Tears streamed down her face as she tried to rush toward the debris but Jax caught her.</p>
<p>"Stop!" he barked, holding her firmly.</p>
<p>"You’ll fall in!"</p>
<p>"Let me go!" she cried, struggling against him.</p>
<p>"Let me go! He’s—he’s still there—!"</p>
<p>Her voice broke completely.</p>
<p>"He can’t be... he can’t..."</p>
<p>Her body shook violently as she sobbed while the others looked on silently.</p>
<p>There was already one casualty but to add this one to the list? The one who stayed behind to make sure they all made it?</p>
<p>It left a heavy feeling on their chests even if they hadn’t known him for long.</p>
<p>A faint rumbling sound suddenly echoed at the far edge of the collapsed area.</p>
<p>No one took note as a small pile of debris shifted causing rubble to roll slightly because everyone felt that any disturbance in the environment was likely due to the collapse aftermath.</p>
<p>A shadow suddenly flickered and from within the darkness of the debris, a figure stepped out...</p>
<p>He phased out from the shadows like a ghost emerging from the void calmly with injuries all over his body.</p>
<p>He took a few steps forward before reaching out and tapping Nina lightly on the shoulder.</p>
<p>"...I’m fine."</p>
<p>Nina froze while slowly turning around.</p>
<p>"...West...?" Her tear-filled eyes widened in disbelief as she suddenly launched herself at him.</p>
<p>"WEST!!!"</p>
<p>She crashed into his chest, wrapping her arms tightly around him as if afraid he’d disappear again.</p>
<p>"You idiot! Don’t do that! Don’t scare me like that!"</p>
<p>West chuckled softly, placing a hand on her head.</p>
<p>"I said I’m fine."</p>
<p>He gently patted her hair while behind them, the others stared.</p>
<p>"...How..." Jared muttered.</p>
<p>"...How the hell did he survive that?"</p>
<p>Derek shook his head slowly.</p>
<p>"...That collapse would’ve buried anyone..."</p>
<p>Jax let out a breath he didn’t realize he was holding.</p>
<p>"...Of course he did..."</p>
<p>West had once again... defied expectations.</p>
<p>It was now over but not everything ended in relief...</p>
<p>A girl collapsed to her knees nearby with teary expression.</p>
<p>"So... its over?"</p>
<p>Beside her, another guy stood frozen, staring at the rubble with hollow eyes.</p>
<p>"He’s truly... gone..."</p>
<p>The girl’s name was Lila Vance and the boy beside her was Coren Hale.</p>
<p>They happened to be referring to the one who didn’t make it... whose head had been bitten off in the chaos below... Riven Cole.</p>
<p>Lila broke down completely with tears pouring down her face as she clutched her chest.</p>
<p>"He promised... he promised we’d all write our names in the awakened history books and climb the power ranking together..."</p>
<p>Coren looked away as his eyes turned red.</p>
<p>"...Damn it..."</p>
<p>West’s expression dimmed slightly but he didn’t say anything... there was nothing to say.</p>
<p>Loss was common in ruins and amongst awakeners but when it was someone you were close to... it certainly will hit different...</p>
<p>—</p>
<p>"...Just what is this place..." Derek muttered.</p>
<p>"What kind of research facility spawns something like that?"</p>
<p>"That creature..."</p>
<p>Jared shook his head.</p>
<p>"...Did they create it?"</p>
<p>No one had definite answers... only questions and unease because what exactly was their motive?</p>
<p>—</p>
<p>Lila suddenly stood up with grief filled eyes that burnt with determination.</p>
<p>"I’m not leaving empty-handed."</p>
<p>She moved quickly, heading toward what remained of the facility structures.</p>
<p>She broke into file cabinets, infiltrated nroken terminals and grabbed fragments of documents...</p>
<p>She began gathering whatever she could salvage while Coren followed silently.</p>
<p>"...We need answers."</p>
<p>Lila clenched her fists.</p>
<p>"...And when we find them..."</p>
<p>Her voice hardened.</p>
<p>"I’ll make sure those responsible for this... pay."</p>
