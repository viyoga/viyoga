<p>The man shook his head. "You need to see it yourself."</p>
<p>Hearing that irritated him even more.</p>
<p>Without another word, Bloodstain marched out of the office and his right-hand man quickly fell in beside him.</p>
<p>Several minutes later they arrived at one of Iron Vultures’ operational centers.</p>
<p>The moment Bloodstain entered, he knew something was wrong.</p>
<p>Everyone looked panicked. People were running around, multiple screens displayed were flashing notifications and operators were shouting at one another.</p>
<p>The atmosphere resembled a disaster zone.</p>
<p>Bloodstain slammed his hand onto a desk. "What happened?!"</p>
<p>Everyone immediately went silent while one of the senior operators stepped forward.</p>
<p>"Boss..." his voice trembled. "...ten ruins have been booked."</p>
<p>Bloodstain eyebrows rose in bafflement. "Ten?"</p>
<p>The operator nodded. "Ten."</p>
<p>For several seconds Bloodstain simply stared at him before letting out a short laugh...</p>
<p>The kind someone made when they thought they had heard something stupid.</p>
<p>"Ten?"</p>
<p>"Yes."</p>
<p>Bloodstain’s smile vanished. "How the hell did ten ruins get booked?"</p>
<p>The operators looked miserable. "We don’t know."</p>
<p>"You don’t know?"</p>
<p>"No."</p>
<p>Bloodstain’s eye twitched. "What do you mean you don’t know?"</p>
<p>The operator hurriedly pointed toward several screens. "It happened almost simultaneously."</p>
<p>Bloodstain’s expression immediately darkened as he stared at the screen.</p>
<p>Ten different ruin permits acquired and to make matters worse, most of them weren’t local.</p>
<p>His eyes moved down the list, noticing that one was nearly three cities away while another required air transportation.</p>
<p>Several were large-scale ruins that cost over two hundred thousand dollars just to acquire the permit.</p>
<p>The total amount spent quickly climbed from five hundred thousand to eight hundred thousand to one point two million to one point five million...</p>
<p>Bloodstain’s face became terrifying the moment he realized that nearly two million dollars had been spent trying to acquire these permits.</p>
<p>The room fell silent for an instant before he exploded in rage.</p>
<p>BLAM!</p>
<p>One of the nearby operators was backhanded so hard he flew across the room.</p>
<p>People screamed as computers toppled over.</p>
<p>The unfortunate man crashed into several desks before finally stopping and falling unconsciousness.</p>
<p>Bloodstain roared. "TEN?!"</p>
<p>"On a regular day we don’t book ten ruins!"</p>
<p>His voice echoed through the room.</p>
<p>"FIVE!"</p>
<p>"SIX!"</p>
<p>"Maybe seven if opportunities are exceptional!"</p>
<p>He pointed at the screen.</p>
<p>"TEN?!"</p>
<p>Nobody dared speak but after a while, his right-hand man finally stepped forward.</p>
<p>"Boss."</p>
<p>Bloodstain turned to look at him while the man spoke carefully.</p>
<p>"If we don’t clear them, the permits are wasted."</p>
<p>This only made Bloodstain angrier because it was true.</p>
<p>The money was already gone since the permits had already been purchased.</p>
<p>If they abandoned the ruins now, millions would be lost.</p>
<p>Several of those ruins could potentially generate huge profits so walking away wasn’t an option.</p>
<p>Bloodstain clenched his fists. "How many members do we currently have available?"</p>
<p>The right-hand man grimaced. "Not many."</p>
<p>Bloodstain already knew that this was due to increased security measures at certain areas outside of the base.</p>
<p>The double patrols, triple escorts, additional warehouse guards, surveillance teams, transport security business front protection and a bunch of other stuff.</p>
<p>The manpower requirements had exploded so a lot of force was currently being expended.</p>
<p>The right-hand man continued. "If we want to maintain current security..."</p>
<p>Bloodstain interrupted. "I don’t care."</p>
<p>The man nodded while pulling up figures. "If we send teams to all ten ruins..."</p>
<p>"...we can only afford about eight members per expedition."</p>
<p>Bloodstain cursed under his breath upon hearing that.</p>
<p>Normally fifteen to twenty awakened members would be assigned.</p>
<p>Anything below ten was considered risky especially for larger ruins.</p>
<p>Yet there was no alternative unless he wanted to abandon permits worth nearly two million dollars.</p>
<p>Bloodstain exhaled heavily. "Do it."</p>
<p>Orders immediately began flying, vehicles were mobilized, air transport was arranged and teams were assembled.</p>
<p>Members who had just finished assignments were immediately reassigned.</p>
<p>Some were pulled out of patrol rotations while others were redirected from ongoing operations.</p>
<p>Chaos spread throughout the organization and within hours, Iron Vultures became a giant machine scrambling to respond.</p>
<p>Convoys departed, teams left for the airport while some rushed toward distant locations...</p>
<p>Eventually, the base grew quieter due to the number of gang members that were sent out.</p>
<p>The right-hand man studied updated personnel numbers and his expression changed. "Boss."</p>
<p>Bloodstain looked over. "How many left at base?"</p>
<p>"Eleven."</p>
<p>Bloodstain cursed again.</p>
<p>Normally there would have been dozens or even over a hundred at the base but now only eleven awakened members remained inside headquarters.</p>
<p>Everyone else was occupied out there.</p>
<p>Bloodstain rubbed his forehead. He hated this feeling... something felt very wrong but right now the only thing he could do was adapt to the situation at hand.</p>
<p>Nearly two million dollars had already been invested but the profits could be substantial. Maybe this wouldn’t be so bad...</p>
<p>Eventually he addressed the teams preparing that were already on their way to the ruins through communication channels.</p>
<p>"I don’t care how difficult those ruins are."</p>
<p>"I don’t care what creatures are inside."</p>
<p>"You clear every single one."</p>
<p>His eyes were cold.</p>
<p>"I want my money’s worth."</p>
<p>The responses came immediately.</p>
<p>"Yes Boss!"</p>
<p>"We’ll clear them!"</p>
<p>"Leave it to us!"</p>
<p>Responses came in from all over while Bloodstain stood in place watching and waiting.</p>
<p>•••</p>
<p>•••</p>
<p>Elsewhere within the unusually quiet Iron Vultures headquarters, a figure stepped out of the darkness.</p>
<p>Glowing blue-white eyes opened beneath the shadows with a grin slowly spread across his face.</p>
<p>"Time to go hunting."</p>
<p>The figure vanished without a sound, leaving only darkness behind.</p>
<p>---</p>
<p>Several hours later...</p>
<p>Bloodstain sat behind his desk with his fingers pressed together.</p>
<p>It was nearly three in the morning and normally he would’ve been asleep by now or gone clubbing.</p>
<p>Instead, irritation had kept him awake here.</p>
<p>The entire ten-permit incident continued to bother him.</p>
<p>The more he thought about it, the less he liked it.</p>
<p>Nothing about it felt normal.</p>
<p>Ten ruin permits suddenly purchased at nearly the exact same time across multiple regions?</p>
<p>Something was very wrong.</p>
<p>His right-hand man stood nearby without making a sound and after a few more minutes, Boodstain finally broke the silence.</p>
<p>"I want answers."</p>
<p>"We’re trying."</p>
<p>"Try harder."</p>
<p>The right-hand man sighed.</p>
<p>"They’ve checked every terminal."</p>
<p>"Every account."</p>
<p>"Every authorization."</p>
<p>"And?"</p>
<p>The man shook his head. "The operators insist it was some kind of system malfunction."</p>
<p>Bloodstain’s eye twitched. "A malfunction."</p>
<p>"That’s what they’re claiming."</p>
<p>Bloodstain snorted. "Convenient."</p>
<p>The right-hand man didn’t argue because he honestly didn’t believe it either.</p>
<p>The chair scraped loudly across the floor as Bloodstain stood to his feet. "I’m taking a walk."</p>
<p>His subordinate frowned. "At this hour?"</p>
<p>Bloodstain looked at him. "Something feels off."</p>
<p>The man immediately nodded. "Understood."</p>
<p>...</p>
<p>That was the first thing Bloodstain noticed was how strangely quiet the headquarters were.</p>
<p>Usually Iron Vultures headquarters never truly slept... Just like any normal gang, it was always crazily loud.</p>
<p>Someone was always moving... someone was always training... someone was always arguing... someone was always drinking... someone was always causing problems...</p>
<p>But tonight, it was just silent.</p>
<p>His footsteps echoed through corridors and the few awakened still stationed around the compound saluted as he passed.</p>
<p>Bloodstain acknowledged them absentmindedly since his thoughts were elsewhere.</p>
<p>The gang compound was enormous with several uncompleted looking buildings. Apparently this was how Bloodstain liked it.</p>
<p>Nonetheless, there were training areas, storage sectors, vehicle garages, operations centers and a bunch of other structures.</p>
<p>Bloodstain walked past them all, silently observing how hollow the surroundings felt.</p>
<p>His frown suddenly deepened as something else occurred to him.</p>
<p>He stopped walking and looked around more keenly.</p>
<p>His right-hand man had said eleven members remained inside headquarters... then why had he only encountered eight so far?</p>
<p>Bloodstain was usually very observant so despite how tiny this detail was, he noticed it.</p>
<p>There were supposed to be three more around but he hadn’t spotted them.</p>
<p>Bloodstain immediately changed direction and started checking the vicinity.</p>
<p>He checked the first three buildings but he found nothing.</p>
<p>His expression darkened further as he checked the fourth and still found nothing.</p>
<p>Now he was actively searching for the three missing.</p>
<p>As the minutes rolled by, the compound seemed increasingly empty.</p>
<p>After some time, he reached one of the older storage sectors that was barely ever visited.</p>
<p>Massive containers filled the building. They contained equipments and materials awaiting transfer since this was a temporary storage location.</p>
<p>The lighting inside was dead, making the surroundings very dim. Several overhead lights flickered weakly while others had gone completely dark.</p>
<p>Bloodstain frown deepened as he stepped inside.</p>
<p>The darkness greeted him but since he was quite powerful, his senses were enough to get him through without stumbling into anything.</p>
<p>Rows upon rows of tall storage containers spread throughout the silent facility.</p>
<p>"Anyone here?"</p>
<p>There was no response.</p>
<p>Bloodstain kept scanning and listening for anything out of the ordinary till he reached the far end of the space.</p>
<p>A tilted gigantic storage unit blocked part of the path preventing further advancement.</p>
<p>Bloodstain reached out to grab it casually and moved it out of the way.</p>
<p>The moment it shifted off the path, Bloodstain froze.</p>
<p>He spotted three battered awakened members, tied up and gagged up ahead.</p>
<p>One of them immediately started struggling.</p>
<p>"HNNM!"</p>
<p>"HNNMM!"</p>
<p>Bloodstain’s eyes widened. "What?"</p>
<p>The man continued desperately.</p>
<p>"HNNMM!"</p>
<p>"HNNMMM!"</p>
<p>It took Bloodstain half a second to understand that the man wasn’t looking at him... he was looking behind him.</p>
<p>The moment Bloodstain turned, he spotted a figure standing within the darkness.</p>
