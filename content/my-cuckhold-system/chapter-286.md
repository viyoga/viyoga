<p>Vines suddenly began to erupt across her skin as she activated her transformation.</p>
<p>Leaves spread along her arms as her fingers elongated into claw-like appendages with fur covering parts of her body.</p>
<p>Her pupils became slitted as an extra set of eyes appeared and within seconds she resembled a strange fusion between a jungle predator and a living plant.</p>
<p>One of the remaining Iron Vultures members looked horrified.</p>
<p>"What the hell is that transformation?"</p>
<p>The girl charged. "I get that a lot."</p>
<p>Her claws slashed forward, splitting armor apart and slicing into his skin.</p>
<p>He screamed as vines immediately erupted from the wound and wrapped around him.</p>
<p>Nearby, two Verdant Pyre Behemoths tore through the battlefield.</p>
<p>The enormous creatures were terrifying to the already weakened opponents.</p>
<p>One grabbed an Iron Vultures member and hurled him into the ground, cracking reinforced concrete.</p>
<p>The other swatted another awakened across the face and slammed their paws into the ground, causing a pillar of green flames to erupt from below.</p>
<p>The impact sounded like a car collision as they got sent blasting into the sky with scalding burns.</p>
<p>Moments later, five of the eight Iron Vultures members were already unconscious while the remaining three backed away nervously.</p>
<p>One pointed a shaking finger. "You people are dead!"</p>
<p>Another nodded frantically. "You have no idea what you’re messing with!"</p>
<p>The third added. "Bloodstain is going to kill all of you!"</p>
<p>Hart cracked his neck. "Maybe."</p>
<p>The rookie grinned. "Maybe not."</p>
<p>Then proceeded to attack the remaining tro, ending the battle quickly.</p>
<p>Within less than a minute they were all unconscious.</p>
<p>The rookie stared down at them. "That’s it?"</p>
<p>Hart was already collecting loot. "What were you expecting?"</p>
<p>"A little more challenge."</p>
<p>Hart snorted. "Trust me. You’re going to get it."</p>
<p>Several minutes later, everything valuable had been loaded into a waiting vehicle positioned a short distance away.</p>
<p>Hart climbed into the passenger seat while the rookie jumped into the back.</p>
<p>One of the Verdant Pyre Behemoths squeezed into the cargo section while the other simply ran alongside as the driver kick-started the engine.</p>
<p>Hart activated the communicator. "Team Five. Loot successfully hijacked. Returning to base."</p>
<p>Static crackled briefly before Aria’s voice answered. "Good job."</p>
<p>The transmission ended as the vehicle immediately accelerated away.</p>
<p>•••</p>
<p>Several cities away, another ambush was reaching its conclusion.</p>
<p>An Iron Vultures convoy had been traveling across a highway unexpectedly ran into a hitch.</p>
<p>Currently, two vehicles were upside down while had been split open.</p>
<p>Debris littered the roadway... and standing calmly in the middle of the destruction was Aurethia.</p>
<p>The gold-haired summon looked completely untouched.</p>
<p>One surviving awakened staggered backward.</p>
<p>"T-That’s impossible... all our attacks had no effect..."</p>
<p>Aurethia didn’t say a word in response... Instead, she simply raised her sword.</p>
<p>The man screamed and attacked with a blast of greenish ring like energy but a second later he was sent flying through the air.</p>
<p>The battle ended shortly afterward because his other teammates were already down.</p>
<p>Aurethia proceeded to walk towards the vehicles next and picked up every single loot they had gathered before turning into a streak of glowing yellow light and speeding off.</p>
<p>•••</p>
<p>Elsewhere, at a different location far beyond Misty High City’s borders... a group of Iron Vultures members found themselves facing an entirely different nightmare.</p>
<p>A massive serpent-woman looked almost bored with five awakened members trapped within her coils simultaneously.</p>
<p>Their bodies groaned as they struggled desperately to free themselves. "Let us go!"</p>
<p>Seraphyra tilted her head. "Why?"</p>
<p>The man nearly cried while attempting to activate his branch.</p>
<p>Seraphyra squeezed slightly more and all five immediately stopped moving.</p>
<p>The giant serpent giggled. "Oh... I thought you were about to give an explanation. I guess it wasn’t that important?"</p>
<p>Nobody answered because they had all passed out.</p>
<p>Seraphyra sighed dramatically. "Master always gets the fun jobs."</p>
<p>She casually dropped them before slithering after the other three that were fleeing into the distance.</p>
<p>---</p>
<p>Across multiple regions, similar scenes continued unfolding. Shadow Reapers wasn’t attacking with numbers, they were attacking with quality.</p>
<p>Every single ambush had been carefully planned with every target selected beforehand.</p>
<p>Every ambush occurred while the Iron Vultures teams were exhausted from ruin clearing. This made them incapable of exercising much strength especially since they had cleared a ruin with less than usual numbers.</p>
<p>Jax, Nina and several involved newer recruits supported by Verdant Pyre Behemoths had all successfully ambushed Iron Vultures teams at their designated locations and were now returning with loot.</p>
<p>The operation was devastatingly efficient...</p>
<p>•••</p>
<p>•••</p>
<p>Meanwhile, back in Misty High City, West stood atop a nearby rooftop staring at a reinforced structure below reflecting moonlight.</p>
<p>The facility was large with massive security fences surrounding the property.</p>
<p>Multiple warehouses sat within the compound and surveillance cameras covered nearly every angle but there was barely any guards in place. The surroundings looked surprisingly empty.</p>
<p>West smiled. "Just as expected."</p>
<p>He activated his communicator and spoke through it. "Aria."</p>
<p>Her voice responded immediately. "Yes Gang Leader?"</p>
<p>West observed the facility below, noticing several vehicles abandoned.</p>
<p>Only about two to three awakened members remained visible... most of the personnel had clearly been recalled... just as planned.</p>
<p>West chuckled. "On site."</p>
<p>Aria immediately understood. "You found it?"</p>
<p>"I found it," West leaned against the rooftop ledge. "I knew the idiot would gather back all his manpower."</p>
<p>Aria laughed softly in response. "Which made breaking into the actual storage facility easier."</p>
<p>"Exactly."</p>
<p>There were barely any valuables kept at the Iron Vultures headquarters... all of it was stored in facilities like this one.</p>
<p>West’s eyes glowed faintly wbile blue-white footprints began appearing along the rooftop, leading directly toward the compound below.</p>
<p>Aria’s voice returned through the comms. "Need backup?"</p>
<p>West smirked. "They’ll be needing that when I’m done with them."</p>
<p>He looked down toward the unsuspecting storage facility. "I’ve got this one... I’m going to really enjoy doing this."</p>
<p>West dropped from the rooftop without making a sound.</p>
<p>The moment his feet touched the ground inside the storage facility compound, two awakened guards stationed near the nearest warehouse immediately noticed something was wrong.</p>
<p>One of them turned in disbelief while the other barely had enough time to open his mouth.</p>
<p>"What the—"</p>
<p>Bam!</p>
