<p>The building itself was tall, rectangular, and intimidating in that bureaucratic sort of way. Its glass façade reflected the cloudy afternoon sky while several security drones hovered quietly above the entrance.</p>
<p>West stood across the street with Jax and Nina, both of them staring up at the building.</p>
<p>Jax whistled softly.</p>
<p>"So this is it..."</p>

<p>"I didn’t think registering a gang would involve government offices."</p>

<p>"Well, its a sham. On the outside it seems operated by the government but the truth is that an awakened faction secretly runs things from underground. The government doesn’t have any real power... not in this day and age."</p>
<p>He looked toward the entrance.</p>
<p>"Let’s go."</p>
<p>The three crossed the street and stepped inside.</p>
<p>The interior looked like a mixture between a government office and a corporate headquarters. Beautiful floors, white walls, glass partitions, and a large digital directory listing different departments.</p>
<p>One of the walls had a large display showing the names of registered gangs operating within the city.</p>
<p>Some of them were massive organizations while others were smaller but all of them had one thing in common... they were official.</p>
<p>And soon, if everything went right, his gang would be among them.</p>

<p>"Welcome to the Gang Registration Bureau. How may I assist you?"</p>

<p>"We’re here to register a gang."</p>

<p>"Please have a seat while I verify your documentation."</p>
