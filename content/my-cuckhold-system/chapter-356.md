<p>West landed a clean uppercut as final attack, sending the gangster flying nearly thirty meters before crashing through an abandoned storefront.</p>
<p>BOOM!</p>
<p>The driver who had witnessed the entire encounter was left in awe. In less than thirty seconds, this young man had defeated four awakeners.</p>
<p>’Who in the world is he?’</p>
<p>West dusted off his hands as he returned to the passenger seat. "Drive."</p>
<p>The truck accelerated, speeding further ahead in a bid to flee this territory.</p>
<p>However, that brief battle had been far from quiet.</p>
<p>Lights flickered on throughout nearby buildings and voices echoed.</p>
<p>"Who’s fighting?!"</p>
<p>"The Black Jackals are under attack!"</p>
<p>Sirens fashioned from rusted steel began blaring and within seconds, dozens of gang members from the same Black Jackals flooded nearby streets.</p>
<p>"There!"</p>
<p>"A truck!"</p>
<p>"Stop them!"</p>
<p>Bullets struck the truck’s metal exterior.</p>
<p>PING!</p>
<p>PING!</p>
<p>PING!</p>
<p>The driver screamed. "They’re everywhere!"</p>
<p>West looked behind. "Keep driving."</p>
<p>Motorcycles began pursuing them, pickups joined in and from rooftops, several awakened leapt between buildings.</p>
<p>Meanwhile, miles away... inside the now-empty slave compound... one guard finally regained consciousness.</p>
<p>His head throbbed violently. "Huh...?"</p>
<p>He looked around and noticed that every cage was empty.</p>
<p>His heartbeat stopped. "...No..."</p>
<p>He immediately jumped to his feet and sprinted through the compound. "No!"</p>
<p>"They’re gone!"</p>
<p>"The kids are gone!"</p>
<p>Panic erupted instantly as the slave traders rushed from every building. "What happened?!"</p>
<p>"Where are they?!"</p>
<p>"The merchandise!"</p>
<p>The leader’s face twisted with fury. "Seal every exit!"</p>
<p>"Send word!"</p>
<p>"I don’t care who did it!"</p>
<p>"I want every road blocked!"</p>
<p>"Nobody enters the slums!"</p>
<p>"And nobody leaves!"</p>
<p>Messengers raced across the slums, flares shot into the night sky and different gangs began receiving the alert about dozens of stolen slaves.</p>
<p>Soon word circulated of a truck and within minutes, the entire slum district became a boiling hornet’s nest.</p>
<p>Back inside the speeding truck... the driver looked in horror at the growing number of headlights behind them.</p>
<p>"There are... there are more joining!"</p>
<p>West glanced through the rear window and could see members of the Black Jackals, slave traders and independent raiders.</p>
<p>Everyone was after the truck now. Some wanted money, some wanted revenge while ohers simply wanted whatever valuable cargo could unite so many enemies.</p>
<p>West smiled while slowly cracking his knuckles. "So..."</p>
<p>"It looks like we accidentally declared war on half the slums."</p>
<p>Behind them, engines roared louder while ahead, another roadblock was already beginning to form.</p>
<p>The truck roared through the shattered streets of Orizon City’s slums like a beast fleeing a burning forest.</p>
<p>Its engine groaned as it tore across broken asphalt, bouncing violently over potholes and piles of rubble.</p>
<p>Inside the covered cargo compartment, dozens of rescued captives huddled together in fearful silence. Every sharp turn sent them stumbling into one another, but not a single complaint escaped their lips.</p>
<p>They knew one thing... if the truck stopped, they would be dragged back.</p>
<p>Outside, chaos erupted.</p>
<p>"THEY’RE GETTING AWAY!"</p>
<p>"HIT THE TIRES!"</p>
<p>Gunfire continued to erupt from behind.</p>
<p>Bang! Bang! Bang!</p>
<p>Bullets ricocheted off the reinforced rear doors, leaving shallow dents across the metal plating.</p>
<p>The terrified driver gripped the steering wheel so tightly his knuckles turned white.</p>
<p>"S-Sir!"</p>
<p>"They’re gaining!"</p>
<p>"Just continue moving," West instructed while winding down the window.</p>
<p>He pushed himself through the open side window and placed his hand atop the vehicle.</p>
<p>Fwwhii~</p>
<p>With a sharp pull, he flipped onto the roof of the truck and stood atop it as though it were perfectly still despite racing at over a hundred kilometers an hour.</p>
<p>The fierce night wind whipped against his clothes and behind he could see the dozens of motorcycles and modified pickup trucks headlights.</p>
<p>He also spotted a few awakened sprinting across rooftops, using their enhanced bodies to keep pace with the convoy.</p>
<p>West exhaled slowly. "So many..."</p>
<p>Dark blue fur rapidly spread across his skin as he began to transform.</p>
<p>His muscles expanded, spiky fangs protruded from his mouth and his glowing eyes became narrow and predatory.</p>
<p>Within moments... the seven-foot-tall Voidfang Predator stood atop the truck.</p>
<p>One pursuing awakened pointed upward. "He’s transforming!"</p>
<p>"Take him down!"</p>
<p>Three awakened leaped simultaneously from neighboring rooftops.</p>
<p>One wielded twin hooked blades, another possessed elongated crystalline claws while the third had transformed his arms into massive stone hammers.</p>
<p>West’s lips curled upward as they descended together.</p>
<p>"So impatient," his chest expanded as a brilliant violet light gathered deep within his throat.</p>
<p>The first awakened noticed the surrounding air trembling and reacted swiftly. "...Move!"</p>
<p>Unfortunately...</p>
<p>BOOOOOOM!!</p>
<p>A concentrated beam of violet energy erupted from West’s mouth, carving through the night sky like a divine spear.</p>
<p>The rooftop beneath the attackers exploded into fragments.</p>
<p>The beam continued for nearly fifty meters before dissipating, leaving behind a smoking trench where concrete had once existed.</p>
<p>The three awakened had already been blasted backward.</p>
<p>One slammed through an apartment wall, another crashed onto the street below while the last spun uncontrollably through the air before disappearing into a collapsed building.</p>
<p>The pursuing convoy faltered briefly. "What kind of monster is that?"</p>
<p>"He fired that from his mouth!"</p>
<p>"Don’t slow down!"</p>
<p>The chase continued regardless with two motorcycles accelerating until they reached the truck’s sides.</p>
<p>Their riders jumped and both landed smoothly atop the vehicle.</p>
<p>One laughed while turning into a blur and sweeping his blade toward West’s neck at superspeed. "Got him!"</p>
<p>CLANG!</p>
<p>West caught it between two clawed fingers. "...That’s it?"</p>
<p>The gangster’s eyes widened as West squeezed.</p>
<p>CRACK!</p>
<p>The weapon shattered into dozens of fragments and before the awakened could react... West’s tail whipped towards his body.</p>
<p>BOOM!</p>
<p>The impact launched the attacker off the truck, sending him crashing through a parked vehicle before disappearing from sight.</p>
<p>The second awakened roared as his arms suddenly enlarged and changed color, resembling thick tree trunks.</p>
<p>He swung both fists downward but West met them head-on.</p>
<p>BOOOOM!!</p>
<p>The collision shook the entire truck causing the roof to cave beneath their feet.</p>
<p>The awakened grinned as roots grew out of his arms and wrapped around West, trapping him in place. "I’ve got you now!"</p>
<p>West smiled displaying his blue glowing fangs. "You’ve got... nothing."</p>
<p>His muscles swelled and with frightening ease, he forced himself free, ripping the roots apart.</p>
<p>The attacker’s confident expression instantly disappeared. "N-No..."</p>
<p>West grabbed both wrists and spun twice before hurling him like a rag doll.</p>
<p>The awakened flew across three buildings before crashing into a water tower, which immediately collapsed.</p>
<p>The pursuing gangsters collectively inhaled sharply. "Is he even Tier One?"</p>
<p>"He has been dominating every single awakened. Something is very wrong!"</p>
<p>West remained atop the truck with a calm expression, watching and waiting.</p>
<p>A few moments later, another barrage of bullets raced toward him.</p>
<p>Bang!</p>
<p>Bang!</p>
<p>Bang!</p>
<p>Bang!</p>
<p>&lt;[ Shadow Anchor Activated ]&gt;</p>
<p>He slipped into the shadow beneath him, causing the bullets to strike nothing.</p>
<p>A glowing footprint suddenly appeared atop one pursuing pickup and West emerged directly above it. "My turn."</p>
<p>BOOOOM!!</p>
<p>He slammed both fists into the vehicle’s roof causing the pickup to flip violently across the road, rolling several times before crashing into pursuing motorcycles.</p>
<p>The resulting pileup forced dozens behind them to scatter.</p>
<p>The driver inside the truck carrying the kids couldn’t help glancing upward through the cracked windshield. "...He’s fighting an army..."</p>
<p>West landed lightly back atop the truck. "We’re almost out of the slums."</p>
<p>Unfortunately... that was exactly when everything changed.</p>
<p>Ahead... a figure slowly stepped into the center of the main road.</p>
<p>Unlike the others, no one shouted around him... instead, the surrounding gang members instinctively kept their distance.</p>
<p>A long crimson coat fluttered behind him while his right arm glowed with brilliant orange light.</p>
<p>"So..." his voice echoed across the street. "You’re the fool causing tonight’s commotion."</p>
<p>West narrowed his glowing eyes. "A leader."</p>
<p>The man smiled. "I’ll make this quick."</p>
<p>A massive crimson branch erupted behind him and flames instantly exploded outward.</p>
<p>He thrust both hands toward the sky causing the fire sweep upward before crashing back down.</p>
<p>BOOOOOOOOM!!</p>
<p>An enormous wall of flaming spikes erupted across the entire width of the street, stretching nearly fifteen meters into the air.</p>
<p>Its blazing surface radiated enough heat to melt nearby streetlights.</p>
<p>The driver looked ahead and immediately panicked. "We can’t stop in time!"</p>
<p>"We’re going too fast!"</p>
<p>West quickly scanned their surroundings.</p>
<p>On the left, nearly fifty gang members emerged from side streets while on the right was another ambush force with rocket launchers, machine guns and even armed awakened.</p>
<p>Behind, multiple pursuing convoys rapidly closed the distance.</p>
<p>And ahead was the impenetrable inferno wall.</p>
<p>The driver screamed. "What do we do?!"</p>
<p>West stared at the burning wall and extended one hand. "...Keep going."</p>
<p>A glowing summoning circle unfolded before him causing the surrounding air to darken.</p>
<p>A ten feet tall figure suddenly materialized above the speeding truck, clad in long slited robes that danced within invisible currents.</p>
<p>Her smooth green skin shimmered beneath moonlight as her golden eyes opened slowly while the staff in her hand radiated immense magical power.</p>
<p>Gor’thala immediately bowed. "Your orders, Master?"</p>
<p>West pointed toward the wall. "Get rid of that."</p>
<p>She glanced forward. "...A simple task."</p>
