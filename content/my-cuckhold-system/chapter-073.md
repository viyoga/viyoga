<p>The blade throbbed with branch energy like it wanted blood.</p>
<p>Caleb lifted it with both hands, staggering slightly from his injuries, but driven by pure hate.</p>
<p>West was still on one knee breathing heavily.</p>
<p>Caleb’s lips curled.</p>
<p>"Die," he hissed and he thrust the glowing violet sword forward—</p>
<p>Straight toward West’s back.</p>
<p>CLANG.</p>
<p>The sound rang out like metal striking an immovable mountain.</p>
<p>The glowing violet sword had stopped.</p>
<p>Not because Caleb lacked strength...</p>
<p>Not because West moved...</p>
<p>But because something had appeared between them.</p>
<p>It was Gor’thala.</p>
<p>She stood tall and immovable with her massive ten-foot frame blocking the blade completely. The violet sword had managed to puncture her skin but only barely. The tip sank in less than half an inch, drawing a faint line of green blood that glowed softly before sealing almost immediately.</p>
<p>Her golden eyes turned downward slowly and what filled them was not pain...</p>
<p>It was wrath.</p>
<p>Caleb’s eyes widened in disbelief.</p>
<p>"What... what the hell—"</p>
<p>He never finished.</p>
<p>Gor’thala’s arm moved, sending a simple backhand slap forward...</p>
<p>But the force behind it—</p>
<p>BOOM.</p>
<p>The sound alone made the crowd flinch.</p>
<p>Caleb’s entire body left the ground instantly, spinning uncontrollably through the air like discarded trash. His transformation flickered and collapsed mid-flight as his seven-foot monstrous form shrunk rapidly back to his normal human size.</p>
<p>His consciousness abandoned him completely as his body flew over forty feet.</p>
<p>And then—</p>
<p>CRASH.</p>
<p>He smashed straight through the wall of a nearby structure, embedding halfway into it like a broken decoration.</p>
<p>Dust exploded outward as the concrete cracked around his limp frame and Caleb did not move again.</p>
<p>Not even a twitch.</p>
<p>The surroundings fell silent...</p>
<p>Phones that had been recording trembled in people’s hands.</p>
<p>Someone whispered weakly, "She... she just..."</p>
<p>"...slapped him..."</p>
<p>"...through a wall..."</p>
<p>Gor’thala stepped forward calmly, pulling the violet sword from her skin as if it were nothing more than a splinter. The wound had already closed completely.</p>
<p>Her staff materialized in her hand instantly and then morphed, transforming into a massive, elegant sword that buzzed with ancient magic.</p>
<p>She raised it.</p>
<p>"I am here," she voiced with a calm and absolute tone, "to vanquish the enemies of my master."</p>
<p>Her gaze locked onto Caleb’s unconscious body embedded in the wall.</p>
<p>"None shall survive."</p>
<p>She began walking toward him.</p>
<p>Each step carried the quiet inevitability of execution.</p>
<p>The crowd felt it.</p>
<p>That this wasn’t intimidation.</p>
<p>This wasn’t posturing.</p>
<p>This was real.</p>
<p>She would kill him.</p>
<p>Without hesitation.</p>
<p>Without remorse.</p>
<p>She raised the sword.</p>
<p>And swung but a loud voice rang out before she could strike.</p>
<p>"Stop."</p>
<p>West’s voice cut through the air.</p>
<p>The blade froze inches from Caleb’s neck.</p>
<p>Gor’thala turned her head slightly with her golden eyes settling on West.</p>
<p>"Master?" she asked.</p>
<p>West had pushed himself fully to his feet now. His body still ached, bruised, scorched, and battered but his eyes remained calm.</p>
<p>He walked toward her slowly.</p>
<p>"In as much as I wouldn’t mind Caleb being wiped out of existence," West said calmly, "I can’t allow it."</p>
<p>Gor’thala blinked once as a rare expression of confusion flickered across her face.</p>
<p>"Master... wants his enemies to live?"</p>
<p>West exhaled.</p>
<p>"This isn’t about mercy," he said. "It’s about consequences."</p>
<p>He glanced around at the broken courtyard. The destroyed pillar. The cracked wall. The unconscious bodies.</p>
<p>"Killing him would bring gang issues I’m not ready to deal with right now."</p>
<p>Gor’thala studied him and then lowered her sword.</p>
<p>"As you command."</p>
<p>She returned to his side instantly.</p>
<p>The crowd erupted into whispers.</p>
<p>"He just stopped her..."</p>
<p>"She listens to him..."</p>
<p>"Is that his summon?! He’s a summoner?"</p>
<p>"Bro... West is actually insane..."</p>
<p>A slow clap suddenly echoed from ahead.</p>
<p>"You won’t need to worry about consequences," a voice said calmly, "if you join Iron Chain."</p>
<p>Everyone turned and spotted three figures approaching.</p>
<p>At their center was Raze Calder.</p>
<p>His blazing iron chain tattoo burned faintly against his neck, glowing beneath his collar like a brand of ownership.</p>
<p>Two other Iron Chain members walked beside him, both Tier 1 awakened judging by the faint branch energy radiating from their bodies.</p>
<p>Raze surveyed the battlefield calmly.</p>
<p>His eyes took in everything.</p>
<p>The unconscious gang members.</p>
<p>The destroyed surroundings and finally...</p>
<p>Gor’thala.</p>
<p>"...Impressive," he said quietly.</p>
<p>The crowd murmured louder.</p>
<p>"That’s Iron Chain..."</p>
<p>"They’re actually here..."</p>
<p>"Not for Caleb this time for sure..."</p>
<p>West stared at him flatly.</p>
<p>"Why is Iron Chain here?" West asked.</p>
<p>Raze smiled faintly.</p>
<p>"You know why."</p>
<p>He stepped closer.</p>
<p>"Do you have an answer now?"</p>
<p>The meaning was clear.</p>
<p>Join them.</p>
<p>West opened his mouth to reply but another voice interrupted.</p>
<p>"So you’re a summoner..."</p>
<p>The voice was smooth, confident and powerful.</p>
<p>"...figures."</p>
<p>Everyone turned again and the moment they saw her—</p>
<p>The air itself seemed to grow heavier.</p>
<p>She walked forward with an authoritative bearing and her long dark hair flowing freely behind her. Her face was uncovered now...</p>
<p>She wasn’t wearing any mask or concealment and her beauty struck like a weapon.</p>
<p>She was cold, bossy and perfect... many male students in the background visibly gushed.</p>
<p>"It’s Miss Zu Li!"</p>
<p>"Leader of the Dragon Fang Gang."</p>
<p>"Oh my gosh, she is so hot I’d let her use me as a welcome mat!"</p>
<p>"Get in line buddy..."</p>
<p>Voices rang out from the background.</p>
<p>Being one of the most powerful gangs in the city, Miss Zu Li’s presence raised things to a whole new level.</p>
<p>Behind her stood several gang members and the familiar suited assistant.</p>
<p>The moment the Iron Chain members saw her, they faltered.</p>
<p>Even Raze’s expression tightened.</p>
<p>"...Dragon Fang," one of the iron chain gang members whispered.</p>
<p>"What is she doing here..."</p>
<p>Zu Li stopped several meters away from West and her eyes swept over him... then Gor’thala.</p>
<p>Then the unconscious awakened around him.</p>
<p>Her lips curved slightly.</p>
<p>"I was already interested in you," she voiced with sharpened gaze.</p>
<p>"But seeing you defeat four Tier 1 awakened without using your summon..."</p>
<p>Her smile deepened.</p>
<p>"...confirms it."</p>
<p>The crowd held its breath as Zu Li spoke clearly.</p>
<p>"I will make you my apprentice."</p>
<p>The words exploded through the surroundings like a bomb.</p>
<p>"WHAT?!"</p>
<p>"Apprentice?!"</p>
<p>"DRAGON FANG?!"</p>
<p>"That’s insane!"</p>
