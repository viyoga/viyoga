<p>The buses roared to life one after another as the doors hissed shut.</p>
<p>Students flooded in with backpacks, duffel bags, and far too much energy for eight in the morning.</p>
<p>Nina was already seated in the middle and on her left, sat Mira Han.</p>
<p>West was seated on her right, beside the window.</p>
<p>Mira glanced at him, unimpressed.</p>
<p>"You came late," she said flatly.</p>
<p>"Fashionably," West replied.</p>
<p>Nina smiled faintly, but her mind was elsewhere as the bus began to move.</p>
<p>Soon the lot faded behind them.</p>
<p>---</p>
<p>Inside the Bus, the atmosphere had transformed almost immediately.</p>
<p>What had begun as orderly seating devolved into laughter, shouting, and music within ten minutes.</p>
<p>One of the guys in the back named Darius, pulled out a massive portable MP3 speaker.</p>
<p>"ROAD TRIP MODE!" he yelled.</p>
<p>Music blasted through the bus.</p>
<p>The teacher supervising their bus, Mr. Lowell, turned in his seat.</p>
<p>"Lower it!"</p>
<p>Darius lowered it... for about thirty seconds... and then the volume crept back up.</p>
<p>Some students started singing along.</p>
<p>Others recorded stories for social media.</p>
<p>West leaned back in his seat.</p>
<p>This was how school trips were supposed to be.</p>
<p>Messy... chaotic... and unfiltered.</p>
<p>Mira crossed her arms.</p>
<p>"Remind me never to get on your nerves," she muttered toward West.</p>
<p>He smirked.</p>
<p>"Why? Afraid I’ll expose your secrets too?"</p>
<p>"I don’t have secrets."</p>
<p>"Everyone does."</p>
<p>She rolled her eyes.</p>
<p>"At least I don’t sabotage relationships for fun."</p>
<p>Nina shifted slightly at that.</p>
<p>The Lena scandal was still circulating.</p>
<p>Even now, some students were whispering about it.</p>
<p>Nina had seen the images and short clips just like everyone else...</p>
<p>Surprisingly, it didn’t make her feel jealous, angry or threatened because she knew Lena had betrayed West first.</p>
<p>And this was just payback. A well deserved one...</p>
<p>If anything, seeing what West had done to Lena stirred something inside her.</p>
<p>The way he had looked in those clips... dominant fucking the shit out of her...</p>
<p>Her pulse quickened just remembering it. Flashbacks of their time last weekend plagued her mind and she couldn’t help but sneak glances at West longingly.</p>
<p>As the bus drove out of Misty High City, Nina stared out the window but saw none of the scenery.</p>
<p>Her thoughts were entirely consumed by him.</p>
<p>West felt her gaze and he leaned closer slightly.</p>
<p>"Daydreaming?"</p>
<p>She swallowed.</p>
<p>"No."</p>
<p>"Liar."</p>
<p>Mira snorted.</p>
<p>"Can you two not be weird?"</p>
<p>West chuckled while turning toward Mira.</p>
<p>"So, Mira... still no boyfriend?"</p>
<p>She stiffened.</p>
<p>"That’s none of your business."</p>
<p>"Just checking. Wouldn’t want you falling behind everyone else."</p>
<p>"I’m not desperate like some people."</p>
<p>Nina lightly elbowed him.</p>
<p>"Stop teasing her."</p>
<p>"I’m not teasing. I’m concerned."</p>
<p>Mira shot him a look.</p>
<p>"You’re unbelievable."</p>
<p>The guys in the row behind them leaned forward.</p>
<p>"West, seriously," one of them said, "remind me never to cross you."</p>
<p>Another added, "Yeah, man. You destroyed Caleb."</p>
<p>West shrugged.</p>
<p>"Don’t give me a reason."</p>
<p>They laughed nervously.</p>
<p>Even joking about it, there was an edge beneath the humor.</p>
<p>Everyone had seen what he was capable of.</p>
<p>How the hell was his dick so big?</p>
<p>---</p>
<p>The first two hours of their journey was spent navigating through the familiar roads of Misty High City.</p>
<p>Tall glass buildings...</p>
<p>Dense residential blocks...</p>
<p>Crowded intersections...</p>
<p>Billboards advertising awakened recruitment programs...</p>
<p>Security patrol drones occasionally gliding above the streets.</p>
<p>After two hours went by, the bus crossed the city boundary.</p>
<p>The architecture began to change and Red Valor City came into view gradually.</p>
<p>Unlike Misty High’s smodern aesthetic, Red Valor felt heavier and older with wider roads.</p>
<p>Stone structures blended with newer reinforced buildings.</p>
<p>Large banners hung across intersections displaying the city’s emblem... a crimson shield crossed with two ancient blades.</p>
<p>The city had history.</p>
<p>It had once been the epicenter of awakened conflict decades ago.</p>
<p>That legacy remained in its architecture.</p>
<p>Some buildings still bore faint scars of patched stonework...</p>
<p>Tourism had softened its image in recent years.</p>
<p>Cafés and boutique shops lined the streets leading toward the historical district.</p>
<p>As they moved deeper into Red Valor, the skyline shifted.... soon lower buildings and more open space came into view.</p>
<p>Finally, in the distance, they could spot the Eternal Ruins.</p>
<p>Massive stone arches protruded above ground...</p>
<p>Fragments of ancient towers stood like broken teeth against the sky.</p>
<p>Even from afar, the scale was impressive.</p>
<p>The bus quieted slightly as students leaned toward the windows.</p>
<p>"Whoa..."</p>
<p>"That’s insane."</p>
<p>"It’s way bigger than I thought."</p>
<p>West’s eyes sharpened.</p>
<p>He wished Jax could see this and get an inkling of how ruins looked...</p>
<p>But they attended different schools.</p>
<p>Their bus soon turned towards a large tourist complex near the ruins.</p>
<p>---</p>
<p>The lodging facility was modern but built in harmony with the ancient surroundings.</p>
<p>There were stone exteriors, wide courtyards, wooden beams...</p>
<p>Teachers organized everyone quickly.</p>
<p>"Girls to the east wing!"</p>
<p>"Boys to the west!"</p>
<p>Groans and laughter followed.</p>
<p>West ended up in a four-person room with Darius, Felix who was quiet but observant... and Rohan, the class clown.</p>
<p>They tossed their bags onto the four single beds.</p>
<p>There were two desks and a shared bathroom.</p>
<p>"Claiming this one!" Darius announced, jumping onto the bed nearest the window.</p>
<p>Rohan flopped onto another.</p>
<p>Felix quietly took the corner bed while West chose the one closest to the door.</p>
<p>They unpacked quickly.</p>
<p>The atmosphere buzzed with anticipation as Felix glanced at West.</p>
<p>"You think anything interesting will happen here?"</p>
<p>West smiled faintly.</p>
<p>"It’s a ruin."</p>
<p>"That’s not what I meant."</p>
<p>A knock echoed down the hall.</p>
<p>"All students gather outside!" a teacher’s voice called. "We’re beginning our first tour of the outskirts!"</p>
<p>Darius groaned dramatically.</p>
<p>"Already?"</p>
<p>"Yes, already!" Mr. Lowell barked from outside.</p>
<p>The boys filed out and soon the courtyard was filled with Class 3.</p>
<p>Teachers stood at the front and led them out.</p>
<p>...</p>
<p>...</p>

<p>The Eternal Ruins loomed in the distance... up close, the scale was overwhelming.</p>
<p>Massive stone pillars covered in ancient carvings...</p>
<p>Broken staircases leading further downwards...</p>
<p>Fragments of walls etched with faded symbols.</p>
<p>The outer perimeter had been reinforced with safety railings and designated paths for tourists.</p>
<p>There was an entry point but the teachers didn’t lead the students there just yet...</p>
