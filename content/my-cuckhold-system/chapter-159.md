<p>Through the smoke, figures began walking in.</p>
<p>In just a few seconds, nearly twenty men stepped through the destroyed wall.</p>
<p>They all wore dark matching jackets.</p>
<p>Each jacket bore the same burning emblem stitched onto the back and many of them had the same tattoo visible along their necks or arms.</p>
<p>Anyone familiar with the city’s underworld recognized it instantly... the black Furnace Gang.</p>
<p>At the front of the group walked a man with an expression filled with hatred.</p>
<p>It was none other than Ross.</p>
<p>The moment he stepped into the auditorium, his eyes began scanning the crowd before locking onto West and Aria standing beside him.</p>
<p>Ross’s expression twisted instantly as the hatred in his eyes burned like fire.</p>
<p>He walked straight toward the stage while his gang members spread out around the auditorium.</p>
<p>Parents began pulling their children back while teachers froze in place and students whispered in fear.</p>
<p>Nobody dared challenge a gang of awakeners.</p>
<p>Ross jumped up onto the stage with a single powerful step and looked out over the terrified crowd before speaking loudly.</p>
<p>"Listen carefully."</p>
<p>His voice echoed through the vicinity.</p>
<p>"From this moment forward..."</p>
<p>"...this venue has been taken over by the Black Furnace Gang."</p>
<p>Panic rippled through the audience as Ross continued calmly.</p>
<p>"No one enters."</p>
<p>"No one leaves."</p>
<p>"Not without our permission."</p>
<p>Several gang members moved to block the exits.</p>
<p>The message was clear... everyone here was now trapped.</p>
<p>Students clung to their parents, tachers looked helpless while reporters quickly backed away.</p>
<p>No one dared to interfere because everyone knew that in this world, qwakeners made the rules.</p>
<p>And right now Ross and his gang were the strongest people present.</p>
<p>West stepped forward slightly with a calm expression but cold glint in his eyes.</p>
<p>He looked directly at Ross.</p>
<p>"What do you think you’re doing?"</p>
<p>Ross laughed bitterly when he heard his voice.</p>
<p>"So you finally decided to speak."</p>
<p>He pointed down at West from the stage.</p>
<p>"You really thought I’d just let you walk away?"</p>
<p>West didn’t respond while Ross’s voice rose with fury.</p>
<p>"You humiliated me."</p>
<p>"And then..."</p>
<p>His gaze shifted briefly toward Aria.</p>
<p>"You took my girl."</p>
<p>Aria flinched slightly as Ross clenched his fists.</p>
<p>"No."</p>
<p>"No, West Einstein."</p>
<p>He shook his head slowly.</p>
<p>"You don’t get to move on with your life after that."</p>
<p>His eyes burned with rage as he stared down at West.</p>
<p>"You’re going to pay."</p>
<p>"For everything you’ve done."</p>
<p>And judging by the twenty awakeners standing behind him...</p>
<p>Ross had come fully prepared to collect that debt.</p>
<p>The auditorium had turned into a prison as twenty members of the Black Furnace Gang stood around the perimeter, forming a loose circle that boxed everyone in.</p>
<p>Ross remained on the stage, looking down at them with burning hatred while several gang members began closing in.</p>
<p>West tilted his head slightly with a calm expression while Jax stepped up beside him immediately.</p>
<p>"Guess this is where things get messy."</p>
<p>Nina moved to West’s other side without hesitation.</p>
<p>"You’re not fighting alone."</p>
<p>The three of them now stood shoulder to shoulder.</p>
<p>Ross noticed the gesture and laughed.</p>
<p>"How touching."</p>
<p>Then his face darkened.</p>
<p>"But let me make something very clear."</p>
<p>He raised one hand and flames ignited along his fingers.</p>
<p>"If you don’t want these people to get hurt..."</p>
<p>His eyes locked onto West.</p>
<p>"...you will offer no resistance."</p>
<p>"Stand still."</p>
<p>"And let me deal with you however I please."</p>
<p>The surrounding crowd gasped softly while West stared at him for a second, without responding...</p>
<p>West suddenly began laughing hysterically.</p>
<p>Ross frowned.</p>
<p>"What’s so funny?"</p>
<p>West shrugged.</p>
<p>"Joke’s on you."</p>
<p>"You think I care."</p>
<p>The moment the words left his mouth, Ross’s hand shot forward.</p>
<p>A blast of blazing fire erupted from his palm and flew straight toward a nearby reporter.</p>
<p>The man didn’t even have time to react before the flames completely engulfed him.</p>
<p>"AAAAHHHH—!"</p>
<p>He ran blindly across the auditorium screaming in agony as fire consumed his body.</p>
<p>The smell of burning flesh filled the air causing students to scream and parents to turn away in horror.</p>
<p>Within seconds, the man collapsed and his body reduced to black ash.</p>
<p>Silence fell over the surrounding while Ross lowered his hand slowly.</p>
<p>"That," he said calmly, "was a warning."</p>
<p>His gaze returned to West.</p>
<p>"I know you."</p>
<p>"You act all cool, but you still have that annoying moral side."</p>
<p>He pointed toward the terrified students.</p>
<p>"Next time it’ll be one of them."</p>
<p>Then he smiled cruelly.</p>
<p>"Or one of your classmates."</p>
<p>He paused deliberately.</p>
<p>"Oh wait."</p>
<p>"Sorry."</p>
<p>"Your ex-classmates."</p>
<p>"Since you’re graduating today."</p>
<p>"Congratulations."</p>
<p>The crowd trembled and everyone waited to see how West would respond.</p>
<p>West didn’t even blink as he responded with a calm voice.</p>
<p>"You mistake something."</p>
<p>Ross frowned while West continued.</p>
<p>"Just because I don’t oppress others with my power..."</p>
<p>"...doesn’t mean I care about them."</p>
<p>A murmur ran through the crowd as West’s gaze hardened slightly.</p>
<p>"Kill every single person here."</p>
<p>"I wouldn’t be moved."</p>
<p>Gasps echoed everywhere.</p>
<p>"It wouldn’t be on me," West added calmly.</p>
<p>"I didn’t do it."</p>
<p>"So why would I care?"</p>
<p>Ross stared at him, searching West’s eyes for hesitation, doubt or guilt...</p>
<p>But no matter how much he checked, Ross couldn’t find any.</p>
<p>At this point, he couldn’t tell if West was bluffing or he truly meant it because there wasn’t a shred of hesitation.</p>
<p>Ross’s lips curled slowly.</p>
<p>"Fine."</p>
<p>"If that’s how you want to play it."</p>
<p>He raised both arms as flames exploded outward from his body.</p>
<p>Fire spread across the surrounding ground like wildfire, racing along cracks and debris.</p>
<p>Within seconds, a ring of blazing flames surrounded the entire crowd.</p>
<p>The heat became unbearable and students screamed again as smoke filled the air.</p>
<p>Ross looked at West.</p>
<p>"Then die with them."</p>
<p>West chuckled.</p>
<p>"Fool."</p>
<p>At that exact moment—</p>
<p>CRACK</p>
<p>Cold energy erupted beside him as Jax stepped forward.</p>
<p>His eyes glowed faintly blue as his branch activated.</p>
<p>Ice surged from his hands and slammed into the spreading flames.</p>
