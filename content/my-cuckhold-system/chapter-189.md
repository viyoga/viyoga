<p>~ Days Later ~</p>
<p>A commotion was currently ongoing at the Dragon Fang Gang Base...</p>
<p>An utterly terrifying figure that no one dared to look at directly was standing with a foot pinned onto someone’s face on the ground.</p>
<p>This figure had long hair cascading down her back with a cold yet beautiful expression on her face.</p>
<p>This was none other than Miss Zu Li and beneath her, the figure pinned to the ground, was Renzhi.</p>
<p>Her foot was pressed firmly against his face, grinding it into the cracked concrete beneath.</p>
<p>The impact alone had fractured the ground on contact and Renzhi’s face was swollen.</p>
<p>His once proud posture completely shattered.</p>
<p>Around them, several Dragon Fang members stood silently and no one dared to speak or interfere.</p>
<p>"You attacked him?"</p>
<p>Zu Li’s voiced with a tone that was too calm while Renzhi trembled in place.</p>
<p>"I— I was just—"</p>
<p>Her foot pressed harder, making his face sink deeper into the ground.</p>
<p>A muffled scream escaped him.</p>
<p>"What gives you the right?"</p>
<p>The question echoed heavily.</p>
<p>"I—I was trying to reprimand him... as his senior..." Renzhi’s voice broke as Zu Li took the pressure up a notch, causing cracks to spread further across the ground.</p>
<p>"You?"</p>
<p>Her tone dipped slightly.</p>
<p>"Reprimand him? He isn’t yours to reprimand..."</p>
<p>Renzhi’s body shook violently.</p>
<p>"I was wrong—!"</p>
<p>"I’m sorry—!"</p>
<p>"I didn’t understand—!"</p>
<p>"Please—!"</p>
<p>Her gaze didn’t change even slightly.</p>
<p>"You didn’t understand?"</p>
<p>Her foot pressed down again just enough to remind him.</p>
<p>"Let me make it clear for you."</p>
<p>She spoke each word with a clear and powerful tone.</p>
<p>"He is not your junior."</p>
<p>"He is not someone you correct."</p>
<p>Her eyes narrowed slightly.</p>
<p>"And he is certainly not someone you attack."</p>
<p>Renzhi could barely breathe.</p>
<p>"I’m sorry—!"</p>
<p>"I’ll apologize—!"</p>
<p>"I’ll do anything—!"</p>
<p>Zu Li finally eased the pressure slightly but her foot remained on him.</p>
<p>"You will apologize."</p>
<p>Her voice was absolute.</p>
<p>"On your knees."</p>
<p>"In front of him."</p>
<p>There was a beat of silence as she added.</p>
<p>"Because he is coming today."</p>
<p>Renzhi’s entire body stiffened.</p>
<p>"...Today?"</p>
<p>A cold sweat broke across his skin as the realization hit him like a truck... he had crossed the wrong person.</p>
<p>And now, there was no escaping the embarrassment that would come next.</p>
<p>Renzhi gasped in relief as Zu Li’s foot finally lifted. He coughed repeatedly as he struggled to breathe, barely able to lift himself.</p>
<p>But he didn’t dare stand fully or look up. There would be no saving face from this... only consequences.</p>
