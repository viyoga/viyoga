<p>West raised an eyebrow. "That somehow sounds even less convincing."</p>
<p>"Why would I be interested in anything you have to offer?"</p>
<p>Her smile widened. "Because..."</p>
<p>"...you stand to gain quite a lot from it."</p>
<p>West remained silent.</p>
<p>Seeing he wasn’t interrupting, she continued. "A once-in-a-century opportunity is approaching."</p>
<p>"It hasn’t been made public yet..." she paused deliberately before adding. "In three months..."</p>
<p>"...the Rift to Castle Extingo will open."</p>
<p>West frowned. "...Castle what?"</p>
<p>"Castle Extingo."</p>
<p>She folded her arms. "It’s similar to a ruin."</p>
<p>"...but only on the surface."</p>
<p>"Its scale is incomparable."</p>
<p>"Its treasures are beyond counting."</p>
<p>"And unlike ordinary ruins..."</p>
<p>"...it has never been cleared."</p>
<p>West’s interest visibly increased and she noticed.</p>
<p>"It appears only once every hundred years."</p>
<p>"But there’s another restriction."</p>
<p>"Only awakeners who have been awakened for one year or less may enter."</p>
<p>"The rift itself rejects everyone else."</p>
<p>West instinctively calculated. He had only been awakened for seven months which meant... he qualified.</p>
<p>She smiled after noticing the realization in his eyes. "I see you’ve already done the math."</p>
<p>West nodded slightly. "So..."</p>
<p>"What’s inside that’s so special?"</p>
<p>Her expression finally became serious. "There is one treasure."</p>
<p>"Just one."</p>
<p>"The treasure."</p>
<p>"The one every faction dreams about."</p>
<p>She slowly walked toward one of the windows overlooking the city. "Whenever an organization manages to obtain one..."</p>
<p>"...their future changes."</p>
<p>"Their influence explodes."</p>
<p>"Their prosperity becomes almost guaranteed."</p>
<p>West listened quietly. "For the past four hundred years..."</p>
<p>"No one has successfully brought one out."</p>
<p>"Every appearance of Castle Extingo ends the same way."</p>
<p>"Blood."</p>
<p>"Betrayal."</p>
<p>"Massacres."</p>
<p>"And before anyone succeeds..."</p>
<p>"...the castle disappears once again."</p>
<p>She turned back toward him. "My objective..."</p>
<p>"...is that treasure."</p>
<p>West studied her expression and could tell that she meant every word. "I’m assembling a team."</p>
<p>"The strongest first-year awakeners I can find."</p>
<p>"I want you among them."</p>
<p>"You may keep every other treasure you find."</p>
<p>"I won’t contest those."</p>
<p>"But..."</p>
<p>"That one treasure belongs to me."</p>
<p>"If I bring it back..."</p>
<p>"...my position within the family becomes unshakable."</p>
<p>"I could even contend for leadership."</p>
<p>Silence settled between them for a while as West lowered his head slightly, thinking.</p>
<p>This wasn’t simply another ruin expedition.</p>
<p>It would be a gathering of virtually every talented first-year awakener across the region.</p>
<p>Every ambitious rookie, rising genius, hidden monster... would be there.</p>
<p>It was... essentially a grand competition. And since it had countless valuable treasures, there was no doubt that the danger within would be great as well.</p>
<p>After several moments... West laughed.</p>
<p>Freya blinked in confusion. "What?"</p>
<p>West looked directly at her. "Why..."</p>
<p>"...would I join your team?"</p>
<p>He smiled confidently. "When I can simply build my own..."</p>
<p>"...and claim that treasure myself?"</p>
<p>She wasn’t surprised by his remark. In fact, she smiled even wider.</p>
<p>"I thought you’d say that."</p>
<p>She slowly walked closer again. "I may have forgotten to include one small detail."</p>
<p>"The Rift to Castle Extingo..."</p>
<p>"...opens only within mafia territory."</p>
<p>West’s expression changed slightly upon hearing that. "Meaning..."</p>
<p>She nodded. "Every gang."</p>
<p>"Every faction."</p>
<p>"Every independent awakener."</p>
<p>"Anyone wishing to enter..."</p>
<p>"...must first register through us."</p>
<p>She smiled sweetly. "And registration approval..."</p>
<p>"...falls under my authority."</p>
<p>She stepped close enough for only him to hear. "With a single command from me..."</p>
<p>"...you’re never getting inside Castle Extingo."</p>
<p>West remained silent for several seconds after Freya’s declaration.</p>
<p>The atmosphere inside the dimly lit lounge grew heavy.</p>
<p>He suddenly displayed a devious smile. "What if I just kill you right now?"</p>
<p>The words were spoken so casually that they almost sounded like he was discussing the weather.</p>
<p>Freya merely smirked while West continued. "I’m sure that authority would simply fall to Timothy."</p>
<p>He folded his arms. "And from what I remember..."</p>
<p>"I’m in considerably better graces with him than I am with you."</p>
<p>A laugh of genuine amusement escaped Freya’s lips. "I’ve missed that confidence."</p>
<p>She slowly circled around him with her heels clicking softly against the floor. "But would you really risk it?"</p>
<p>West’s eyes followed her until she stopped directly in front of him. "You’ve worked incredibly hard building your little kingdom."</p>
<p>"Shadow Reapers has finally begun making a name for itself."</p>
<p>"You’ve survived wars."</p>
<p>"You’ve defeated stronger enemies."</p>
<p>"You’ve gathered loyal followers."</p>
<p>She smiled. "...Would you really throw all of that away over me?"</p>
<p>She took another step. "Harming me..."</p>
<p>"...would mean declaring war against the Mafia."</p>
<p>Her voice became noticeably colder. "You may have defeated a gang like Iron Vultures."</p>
<p>"They were troublesome."</p>
<p>"They were established."</p>
<p>"But in the grand scheme of things..."</p>
<p>"...they were nobodies."</p>
<p>Her crimson eyes locked onto his. "The Mafia isn’t."</p>
<p>"If I disappear after walking into this headquarters..."</p>
<p>"There won’t be investigations."</p>
<p>"There won’t be negotiations."</p>
<p>"There won’t even be questions."</p>
<p>"There will simply be retaliation."</p>
<p>West remained expressionless while Freya chuckled. "You’re a smart man."</p>
<p>"You understand exactly what I’m saying."</p>
<p>"The moment I die..."</p>
<p>"...Shadow Reapers dies with me."</p>
<p>Silence followed because West knew she wasn’t exaggerating.</p>
<p>Iron Vultures had possessed hundreds of awakeners but the Mafia possessed far more to the extent where it was incomparable.</p>
<p>They had resources, influence, political connections, money, hidden elites, veterans...</p>
<p>Their reach extended throughout multiple cities.</p>
<p>It was precisely because of organizations like them that West had deliberately try to keep away from the eyes of upper circles.</p>
<p>His original plan had been to simply grow quietly and become powerful... only then could he attract attention.</p>
<p>Instead... attention had arrived much earlier than expected.</p>
<p>He let out a quiet breath. "...This is exactly what I wanted to avoid."</p>
<p>Freya’s smile softened. "For what it’s worth..."</p>
<p>"I’m not your enemy."</p>
<p>"Not today."</p>
<p>She slowly stepped closer, leaning toward his ear.</p>
<p>Her warm breath brushed lightly against his skin. "I’ll be in touch..."</p>
<p>As she spoke, her fingers briefly brushed the back of his head in a familiar, almost affectionate gesture before she withdrew.</p>
<p>Then, she simply walked past him.</p>
<p>West frowned and a second later, he turned.</p>
<p>"..."</p>
<p>The corridor behind him was completely empty.</p>
<p>Freya had vanished as though she’d never existed.</p>
<p>West sighed while rubbing his temples. "This woman... is trouble."</p>
