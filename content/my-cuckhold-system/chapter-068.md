<p>Lena shook her head frantically. "No! I swear—"</p>
<p>"You whore!" he snarled. "Just because he might be awakened, you’ll go back to him now, won’t you?!"</p>
<p>Her voice broke. "That won’t happen! Please, you’re hurting me—"</p>
<p>Students nearby froze.</p>
<p>They saw, heard, stood there witnessing the ordeal...</p>
<p>However, no one moved.</p>
<p>Because Caleb was awakened.</p>
<p>Somewhere nearby, West watched from the distance while walking over.</p>
<p>The crowd subconciously parted the way for him as he stepped forward with his school bag slung over his shoulders.</p>
<p>’It’s his ex, he’ll definitely intervene...’ They all had thoughts similar to this running through their minds.</p>
<p>Inside the summon domain, Gor’thala straightened.</p>
<p>"...Master," she said quietly, "that male appears unstable."</p>
<p>West didn’t respond...</p>
<p>He just kept moving.</p>
<p>Lena eyes seemed to light up hopefully when she spotted West’s approaching figure.</p>
<p>However, to everyone’s surprise... West walked past her.</p>
<p>Lena watched him from the corner of her eye as he moved by without slowing, without turning his head, without even a flicker of recognition.</p>
<p>There was no anger... nor pity... or any change in expression for that matter.</p>
<p>Just indifference...</p>
<p>And somehow, that hurt more than Caleb’s grip.</p>
<p>A hollow pit opened in her stomach as realization sank in.</p>
<p>He doesn’t care anymore.</p>
<p>Not even enough to hate her.</p>
<p>To West, that Chapter of his life was closed... Sealed... Completely burned.</p>
<p>Whatever was happening to her now was, self-inflicted. She had chosen Caleb so it was time for her to bear the consequences of that choice.</p>
<p>Inside the summon domain, Gor’thala observed quietly. She felt no moral outrage, no urge to interfere. She was not a savior, not a hero, not bound by human sentimentality.</p>
<p>Her only purpose was her master’s will.</p>
<p>West had chosen not to intervene so she did nothing.</p>
<p>The situation should have ended there... however, it didn’t.</p>
<p>Because someone else stepped forward.</p>
<p>"S-Stop it!"</p>
<p>A small, trembling voice rang out.</p>
<p>Caleb barely registered it at first, continuing on without a single reaction.</p>
<p>Mira Han stood a few steps away with her fists clenched so tightly, her knuckles were white. Her legs were shaking, but she forced herself forward anyway.</p>
<p>"Let her go," she said, louder this time. "You’re hurting her."</p>
<p>Caleb turned his head slowly with irritation flashing across his face like a spark hitting oil.</p>
<p>"...Who do you think you are?"</p>
<p>Mira swallowed, but she didn’t back away. She reached out, trying to grab his arm.</p>
<p>"Please—"</p>
<p>Caleb shoved her.</p>
<p>Unprepared for the strength behind that casual shove, Mira was sent stumbling backwards.</p>
<p>She cried out as she hit the ground, scraping her palm and elbow against the rough concrete. A sharp sting shot up her arm.</p>
<p>Gasps rippled through the nearby students.</p>
<p>Mira stayed down for half a second.</p>
<p>Then she pushed herself back up.</p>
<p>Her knees were shaking. Tears blurred her vision. But she rushed in again, grabbing at Caleb’s sleeve with both hands.</p>
<p>"Stop it!" she cried. "You can’t do this!"</p>
<p>That was when Caleb snapped.</p>
<p>His face twisted as fury overtook.</p>
<p>"How dare you touch me!" he roared and raised his arm, sending a backhanded strike towards Mira’s face.</p>
<p>As an awakened, if it landed, Mira would have been sent flying.</p>
<p>However, it never landed.</p>
<p>The earth shattering impact Mira expected even after her eyes closed halfway flinching, never came.</p>
<p>This was because, a hand had appeared out of nowhere and clamped around Caleb’s wrist mid-swing, stopping it cold.</p>
<p>The impact cracked through the air like a gunshot.</p>
<p>Caleb froze.</p>
<p>So did everyone else.</p>
<p>West stood there with his grip iron-tight and his eyes unamused.</p>
<p>"So now you’re a woman beater," West said calmly. "Didn’t think you’d sink that low."</p>
<p>Caleb’s pupils shrank.</p>
<p>Fear flashed across his face before he could stop it.</p>
<p>West.</p>
<p>He’d been relieved earlier when West walked away. Relieved enough to keep pushing, keep spiraling.</p>
<p>But now West was here.</p>
<p>And suddenly, Caleb couldn’t breathe properly.</p>
<p>"Mind your business," Caleb snapped, trying to sound confident.</p>
<p>His voice cracked halfway through but West didn’t let go.</p>
<p>Instead, he stepped forward and pulled Mira gently against his side, wrapping one arm around her shoulders in a protective gesture that felt instinctive.</p>
<p>"This is my business," West replied coolly. "Feel free to treat your girlfriend however you like—"</p>
<p>Lena flinched at the words.</p>
<p>"—but don’t go putting your STD-infected hands on my friend."</p>
<p>The surrounding students sucked in a collective breath.</p>
<p>Caleb’s face flushed red.</p>
<p>"You—!" he yelled. "An unawakened has no right to tell me what to do!"</p>
<p>West tilted his head slightly, unimpressed.</p>
<p>"Oh?" he said. "Funny. You sounded pretty confident last time too."</p>
<p>Caleb stiffened.</p>
<p>West leaned in just enough for only Caleb to hear.</p>
<p>"What if I gave you a taste of the same medicine you gave me back then in the cafeteria..."</p>
<p>Caleb didn’t even get to reply before West suddenly grabbed him by the hair and shoved him downward while raising one knee.</p>
<p>Bam!</p>
<p>A knee to the face, sent Caleb’s nose twisting the other way as blood burst out of his face.</p>
<p>"Arrghhh!"</p>
<p>He groaned in pain but West didn’t stop there...</p>
<p>West proceeded to pull him down by the hair and press his face down to the very ground.</p>
<p>"Now how does it feel to be the same as everyone else... trampled on the ground like a completely valueless piece of trash?"</p>
<p>Caleb was breathing heavily now... he spotted the looks of disgust from everyone in the surroundings and it scrambled his brain.</p>
<p>He momentarily forgot how to activate his branch as West kept his lips pressed directly onto the ground for a couple of seconds.</p>
<p>Caleb had never felt humiliation sting this badly.</p>
<p>"You are... nothing... I am... I am..."</p>
<p>Caleb struggled to form a single coherent sentence.</p>
<p>West strength was way beyond what he expected... he felt like he was being pressed down by a mountain.</p>
<p>Just when it felt like he was about to sink into the ground from the force, West suddenly released his grip.</p>
<p>"Oh right... I have somewhere to be," as soon as he stepped back, Caleb jumped to his feet with dust and blood smeared across his face.</p>
<p>He gritted his teeth in fury as he took on a battle stance. "How dare you?! You little piece of shit! I’ll teach you a lesson today!"</p>
<p>"Relax," West murmured upon noticing that Caleb was about to activate his branch. "I’ll give you a rematch later if you feel like replaying today’s humiliation but not right now. Some people actually have jobs... unlike you."</p>
<p>The fear came back instantly.</p>
<p>Caleb took another step back. Despite being free now, he wasn’t even confident that he could take West on even if he activated his branch.</p>
<p>"Tch," he scoffed, forcing a laugh and wiping his face with the back of his hand. "I’ll let you off the hook today."</p>
<p>No one believed him.</p>
<p>"Next time," Caleb continued, backing away, "I’ll teach you a lesson."</p>
<p>With that, he turned and stormed off.</p>
<p>Lena stood there, shaking with tears streaming down her face now that the danger had passed.</p>
<p>She turned toward West.</p>
<p>"Th-thank you," she said softly. "I—"</p>
<p>"I didn’t do it for you," West said coldly.</p>
<p>The words hit harder than any slap.</p>
<p>Lena’s lips parted, but no sound came out.</p>
<p>Without looking back, West walked away with Mira, leaving Lena alone in the middle of the gathered crowd.</p>
<p>-"Damn, Caleb really got his ass handed to him again today."</p>
<p>-"Brother is so shameless that he claimed to have let West off the hook."</p>
<p>-"Got it all recorded on video. Posting it on all social platforms right now."</p>
<p>-"Yo why is West so strong though? This shit ain’t normal!"</p>
<p>Voices of the crowd overlapped in the background even after West had already exited the school with Mira.</p>
