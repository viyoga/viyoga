<p>"Wouldn’t you like to know," West chuckled.</p>
<p>"Doesn’t matter!" Renzhi roared before exploding forward once again.</p>
<p>The distance separating them disappeared almost instantly as his fist tore toward West’s face.</p>
<p>"I’LL KILL YOU!"</p>
<p>Just before the strike landed, a glowing footprint suddenly appeared on the ground several meters behind Renzhi.</p>
<p>His fist passed straight through empty air as West vanished.</p>
<p>"...What?!"</p>
<p>Renzhi’s momentum carried him forward while his eyes darted wildly.</p>
<p>"Where..."</p>
<p>West appeared above the footprint behind Renzhi as though stepping out of thin air.</p>
<p>Renzhi’s pupils shrank. "How can..."</p>
<p>"...a Tier One evade my attack?"</p>
<p>Behind him, West whispered. "My turn."</p>
<p>Before Renzhi could even spin around, West’s body had transformed.</p>
<p>Dark blue fur had covered his skin and his height had reached seven feet with long razor-sharp fangs emerging from his mouth.</p>
<p>His glowing eyes burned like twin blue flames as his massive clawed hands hung sideways.</p>
<p>An overwhelming predatory aura flooded the surrounding causing every nearby bird to instantly take flight.</p>
<p>Renzhi instinctively stiffened as his insticts warned him of danger but all this had happened so quickly in less than the fraction of a second, that Renzhi hadn’t gotten the chance to turn around yet.</p>
<p>West’s beast-like grin widened. "You’ve been attacking for a while."</p>
<p>"I think it’s only fair..."</p>
<p>"...that I return the favor."</p>
<p>West’s powerful leg whipped forward while Renzhi was turning around.</p>
<p>BOOOOM!!</p>
<p>The kick landed squarely between Renzhi’s shoulder blades before he could raise his hand quick enough to block.</p>
<p>The impact detonated like an artillery shell as air burst outward in a visible ring.</p>
<p>Renzhi’s eyes widened in shock as his body was launched forward at incredible speed.</p>
<p>He tore through dozens of trees, snapping them apart one after another before crashing into the side of a distant hill with a deafening explosion.</p>
<p>The hillside fractured from the impact causing rocks to cascade down its slope.</p>
<p>For the first time since the battle had begun... the hunter had become the one sent flying.</p>
<p>Standing amid the ruined surroundings, the tall blue-furred beast slowly lowered his leg.</p>
<p>His glowing eyes remained fixed on the cloud of dust rising from the distant hillside.</p>
<p>"...You took that better than I expected."</p>
<p>The dust suddenly exploded outward as Renzhi shot back into the sky with a roar so furious it sent flocks of birds fleeing from the surrounding woodland.</p>
<p>"WEEEEEEST!!"</p>
<p>His wings burst open once more and every crimson eye covering them snapped wide open.</p>
<p>His aura swept across the surrounding violently.</p>
<p>West merely tilted his head. "Oh."</p>
<p>"Still got plenty left."</p>
<p>Renzhi stopped high above the battlefield with blood dripped from the corner of his mouth.</p>
<p>One of his wings trembled slightly from the damage but the hatred in his eyes only burned brighter. "You..."</p>
<p>"You monster..."</p>
<p>"You should’ve died already!"</p>
<p>The crimson eyes covering his body all began glowing simultaneously.</p>
<p>The amount of energy gathering around him became visible to the naked eye causing even the surrounding clouds to swirl unnaturally.</p>
<p>West glanced upward. "...That’s considerably stronger."</p>
<p>With a deafening roar, Renzhi flapped both wings.</p>
<p>SHHHHOOOOOOM!!</p>
<p>Dozens upon dozens of crimson beams erupted from the countless eyes decorating his wings.</p>
<p>Unlike before, these beams were thicker, brighter and far more destructive.</p>
<p>They blanketed the entire section of the neighborhood and everything beneath them disappeared beneath an endless barrage of explosions.</p>
<p>BOOM!</p>
<p>BOOM!!</p>
<p>BOOOOOM!!</p>
<p>Trees that had stood for decades vanished instantly as the earth was ripped apart.</p>
<p>West darted through the inferno, moving with impossible agility.</p>
<p>Every glowing footprint he left behind flickered for only the briefest instant before fading away.</p>
<p>One beam narrowly missed his shoulder while another sliced through the space his head had occupied less than a second earlier.</p>
<p>One detonated directly behind him, hurling chunks of earth into the sky.</p>
<p>Renzhi refused to let up. "I’LL KILL YOU!!"</p>
<p>His roar echoed across the district as his wings folded and he dove.</p>
<p>West looked up. "...Here he comes."</p>
<p>The distance between them vanished almost instantly as Renzhi’s right fist swung with enough force to crack the air itself.</p>
<p>The crimson eyes embedded throughout that arm flashed brilliantly and a strange scarlet beam enveloped West’s body.</p>
<p>Instantly... his body became unimaginably heavy.</p>
<p>The ground beneath him collapsed as cracks spread beneath his feet like an invisible mountain had been placed upon his shoulders.</p>
<p>West’s knees bent slightly. "...Gravity."</p>
<p>Renzhi’s expression twisted into a vicious grin. "Exactly."</p>
<p>"...let’s see you move."</p>
<p>Just as he swung, a glowing footprint materialized several meters behind Renzhi and West vanished.</p>
<p>Renzhi instinctively spun around as if expecting this and threw out a fist.</p>
<p>BOOOOM!</p>
<p>Both their fists clashed, causing a loud sound to detonated across the surrounding, sending them both back.</p>
<p>They both roared and charged at each other once more...</p>
<p>Fwwwwhooooommmm~</p>
<p>And from there... the battle escalated into complete chaos, leaving an ever-growing trail of destruction.</p>
<p>BOOOOM!</p>
<p>BAM!</p>
<p>BAM!</p>
<p>BANG!</p>
<p>BOOOM!</p>
<p>In just a two minutes, the battlefield had become almost unrecognizable.</p>
<p>What had once been a quiet residential district was now a landscape of shattered asphalt, uprooted trees, collapsed walls, and smoking craters.</p>
<p>Every exchange between the two awakened carved fresh scars into their immediate vicinity.</p>
<p>Windows continued to shatter from the resideual energy of their attacks.</p>
<p>Dust hung so thick in the air that visibility had dropped to only a few dozen meters.</p>
<p>Despite his heavy breathing, Renzhi beat his wings violently while unleashing another devastating volley of scarlet beams.</p>
<p>The attacks crossed the battlefield like crimson lances, vaporizing everything they touched.</p>
<p>West darted between the blasts with astonishing speed, causing Renzhi’s attacks to continously miss by mere centimeters.</p>
<p>BOOM!</p>
<p>Another explosion engulfed an abandoned intersection and West emerged from the smoke almost casually.</p>
<p>He rolled one shoulder. "Come on, Renzhi."</p>
<p>"I expected more from the great third grand captain of Dragon Fang."</p>
<p>Renzhi answered with another furious roar. "I’LL KILL YOU!"</p>
<p>West smirked. "You keep saying that."</p>
<p>"So far, you’ve mostly been demolishing your own neighborhood."</p>
<p>The taunt only poured fuel onto the fire causing Renzhi’s eyes to blaze. "You think this is funny?"</p>
<p>West shrugged. "You’ve certainly made it entertaining."</p>
<p>Renzhi vanished and when he reappeared, his right fist slammed toward West’s chest.</p>
<p>The crimson eyes covering that arm erupted with brilliant light causing the strange gravitational beam to strike West directly.</p>
<p>Immediately... the surrounding gravity multiplied by nearly a hundred fold.</p>
<p>The ground beneath West fractured into a spiderweb of cracks, causing chunks of concrete to sink into the earth.</p>
<p>Even the nearby wrecked vehicles were crushed due to the impossible weight.</p>
<p>This gravitational force was significantly stronger than before.</p>
<p>Renzhi’s face twisted into a savage grin. "Move now, I dare you. Your legs will snap like toothpicks!"</p>
<p>West’s knees bent slightly as his legs dug deep into the broken pavement.</p>
<p>For a brief instant, even he looked surprised. "...You’ve increased the output."</p>
<p>"Good."</p>
<p>Renzhi charged with a victorious look. There was no way any Tier One should be capable of moving under this level of gravitational suppression.</p>
<p>Not even an ordinary Tier two would be able to escape.</p>
<p>His fist tore through the air towards West, only for him to twist his body at the last possible instant.</p>
<p>Renzhi’s punch skimmed past his shoulder instead of crushing his chest.</p>
<p>"What?!"</p>
<p>Before Renzhi could recover, a glowing footprint materialized several meters behind him.</p>
<p>West vanished, escaping Renzhi’s gravitation supression but Renzhi instinctively spun around, throwing another fist forward.</p>
<p>Only for West to vanished again and appear directly above a luminous footprint that had just materialized in mid air.</p>
<p>As he appeared above Renzhi, his knee instantly came crashing downward.</p>
<p>BOOOOM!!</p>
<p>It struck the crown of Renzhi’s head, driving him straight into the ground.</p>
<p>The earth collapsed beneath him as a massive crater erupted outward while dirt and shattered stone blasted into the sky.</p>
<p>For several seconds... only silence remained.</p>
<p>West landed lightly at the edge of the crater and brushed dust from his sleeve. "Alright..."</p>
<p>He exhaled slowly. "Let’s just stop this."</p>
<p>The crater exploded as a bleeding Renzhi shot upward like an enraged beast. "NEVER!"</p>
<p>His shoulder slammed into West and the impact forced West backward several meters.</p>
<p>Renzhi’s left arm swung immediately afterward causing the gravity around West to change again.</p>
<p>His body suddenly became almost weightless but before the attack could connect, another glowing footprint appeared.</p>
<p>Renzhi’s fist struck empty air as West vanished.</p>
<p>"I’LL KILL YOU!" His roar echoed across the devastated district.</p>
<p>West reappeared many meters away atop the remains of a collapsed building.</p>
<p>His expression had become noticeably calmer as he folded his arms. "Renzhi."</p>
<p>For the first time since the battle began, his tone carried no mockery and he had a reason for this.</p>
<p>"Look around you."</p>
<p>Renzhi’s breathing remained heavy and his fists stayed clenched.</p>
<p>West gestured toward the destruction surrounding them. "This fight is getting bigger."</p>
<p>"Bigger explosions."</p>
<p>"More noise."</p>
<p>"More witnesses."</p>
<p>"It won’t be long before the media arrives."</p>
<p>Renzhi’s expression darkened while West continued evenly. "The longer we keep fighting..."</p>
<p>"...the greater the chance that everyone starts asking why a grand captain of Dragon Fang suddenly tried to kill the leader of Shadow Reapers."</p>
