<p>The moment Bloodstain turned, he spotted a figure standing within the darkness with glowing blue-white eyes, long glowing fangs.</p>
<p>Two massive storage chests rested across its shoulders as though they weighed nothing.</p>
<p>For a brief second neither moved... until the figure suddenly tilted its head.</p>
<p>"Oh," the voice sounded distorted. "You’re here."</p>
<p>Bloodstain exploded forward with pure rage erupting inside him. "Who the hell are you?!"</p>
<p>The floor shattered beneath his feet as his hand shot forward.</p>
<p>He moved with such a speed that most awakened wouldn’t be able to evade before they even realized they were under attack.</p>
<p>However, the figure simply smiled. "Too late."</p>
<p>Blue light encapsulated the figure and they vanished.</p>
<p>Only glowing footprints remained where it had stood.</p>
<p>Bloodstain eyes widened in shock as be stopped.</p>
<p>"Spatial movement?" His head swerved toward the empty storage area.</p>
<p>And now he finally noticed that the storage room was empty.</p>
<p>The storage compartments all had nothing within them anymore.</p>
<p>Loot, resources, materials... everything that was kept within this particular storage space was gone.</p>
<p>Bloodstain felt something inside him crack. "What..."</p>
<p>"...the hell?"</p>
<p>Without wasting another second, Bloodstain shot out of the building, leaving shockwaves in his wake.</p>
<p>Windows shattered nearby as he rushed toward another storage sector that contained recently acquired resources.</p>
<p>He arrived there and the doors were already open.</p>
<p>Bloodstain entered and just as he feared, empty shelves, crates and containers greeted him.</p>
<p>Nothing remained in here as well.</p>
<p>His face twisted in unbridled rage and anguish.</p>
<p>Usually Iron Vultures didn’t keep their true wealth inside headquarters.</p>
<p>That would’ve been foolish.</p>
<p>Their most valuable assets remained within heavily secured facilities elsewhere... same places he had doubled and even tripled manpower.</p>
<p>However, the same couldn’t be said for recently returned ruin loot and newly acquired materials awaiting transfer...</p>
<p>Usually when gang members returned from ruins, they’d get to the headquarters first so new stuff tend to be stored here before they get moved to the secure storage facilities.</p>
<p>And now... someone had scooped everything that was stored here.</p>
<p>Bloodstain let out a roar causing the entire building to shake.</p>
<p>Nearby walls cracked and several windows exploded.</p>
<p>He unleashed his senses wildly and felt the spatial energy ripple.</p>
<p>Bloodstain instantly turned. "There!"</p>
<p>He launched himself forward and the ground exploded.</p>
<p>BOOM!</p>
<p>He ascended across the air, scaling over multiple buildings in an instant.</p>
<p>Fwwwhoomsshh~</p>
<p>From there on he proceeded to cross rooftops at terrifying speed, leaping from one to the other.</p>
<p>His senses strained as he scaled through multiple streets before finally seeing glowing blue footprints in the distance.</p>
<p>Bloodstain used the footprints as an anchor, following a certain direction for one kilometer till he spotted another pair of glowing footprints.</p>
<p>He continued on and on till he kept spotting them one after the other but no matter how fast he moved, he didn’t catch a glimpse of that figure from the storage space.</p>
<p>At some point, it started to seem as though the footprints were mocking him.</p>
<p>Every time he arrived at the location where the spatial ripple were more prominent, he would find only another glowing footprint and this only fueled his rage.</p>
<p>The chase stretched for kilometers across district, neighborhoods and multiple sections of the city until the trail suddenly ended.</p>
<p>Bloodstain stopped atop a rooftop and could no longer sense any spatial ripple.</p>
<p>There were no more footprints or presence or any sight of the figure he once spotted at the base.</p>
<p>No matter how much he combed the surroundings, he found nothing.</p>
<p>The realization hit him that he had just been played... they had made him go on a wild goose chase.</p>
<p>Bloodstain roared in anguish before slamming his fist into the side of a nearby building.</p>
<p>BOOM!</p>
<p>Concrete exploded as the structure visibly cracked.</p>
<p>Pedestrians below screamed while rushing away as the side of a wall crumbled.</p>
<p>"I WILL FIND YOU!" his voice echoed throughout the area. "AND WHEN I DO... I WILL RIP YOUR HEAD OFF!"</p>
<p>For several moments he simply stood there breathing heavily.</p>
<p>All of a sudden, his communicator rang.</p>
<p>Bloodstain nearly crushed it. "What?!"</p>
<p>The voice on the other end immediately sounded nervous. "U-uh..."</p>
<p>Bloodstain’s eye twitched. "What?"</p>
<p>"Boss..." the voice swallowed. "We have a problem."</p>
<p>Bloodstain laughed dangerously nearly on the brink. "A problem?"</p>
<p>The employee immediately sounded terrified.</p>
<p>"O-or rather..." they paused briefly.</p>
<p>"...problems."</p>
<p>Bloodstain closed his eyes and for a moment he seriously considered throwing himself off the building.</p>
<p>Instead he pinched the bridge of his nose. "What happened now?"</p>
<p>The answer came hesitantly. "You need to hurry back to headquarters."</p>
<p>Bloodstain’s eyes narrowed because he knew that tone... It was the same tone people used whenever things had gone catastrophically wrong.</p>
<p>And judging from how tonight had been going...</p>
<p>He had a very bad feeling that whatever awaited him back at Iron Vultures headquarters...</p>
<p>Was somehow even worse than what he’d already discovered.</p>
<p>Without another word, Bloodstain launched himself into the night sky and began racing back toward the base.</p>
<p>For the first time in years... the mighty leader of Iron Vultures felt genuinely uneasy.</p>
<p>...</p>
<p>Minutes later, Bloodstain arrived back at Iron Vultures headquarters like a meteor.</p>
<p>The reinforced gates shook from the force of his landing as cracks spread across the pavement beneath his boots.</p>
<p>His expression was terrifying.</p>
<p>He had just discovered that somebody had somehow infiltrated his headquarters, looted enormous quantities of resources, humiliated his security personnel, and escaped right under his nose.</p>
<p>Now the call had implied things were somehow even worse.</p>
<p>The moment he entered the operations building, he met absolute chaos.</p>
<p>Operators were shouting over one another, communication officers were desperately switching channels while several screens displayed flashing warnings.</p>
<p>Others showed maps with multiple highlighted locations spread across several cities and regions.</p>
<p>Somewhere in the room, a communications terminal was continuously emitting static alongside screams.</p>
<p>Bloodstain immediately grabbed the nearest operator by the collar.</p>
<p>"What is happening?"</p>
<p>The operator nearly pissed himself.</p>
<p>"B-Boss!"</p>
<p>"Talk!"</p>
<p>The man pointed toward the screens.</p>
<p>"Teams are being attacked!"</p>
