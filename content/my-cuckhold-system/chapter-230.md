<p>West’s grip loosened slightly, lowering the man slowly till his feet touched the ground again.</p>
<p>For a brief moment, it looked like he was convinced causing the man to exhale slightly in relief. "...Good choice—"</p>
<p>"No," West refusal echoed like a thunderclap and before anyone could react, both hands swept in together from opposite sides.</p>
<p>It was as though he was going for a powerful clap... only that, the man’s head was the obstacle in-between his palms.</p>
<p>PAHH! KRRRYYYK~</p>
<p>A sickening crack echoed as the man’s skull compressed upon collision causing blood to burst outward violently.</p>
<p>The man’s eyes bulged before popping out of their sockets like golf balls shot into the air.</p>
<p>His body went limp instantly as everyone froze in place briefly.</p>
<p>"FIRE!" Someone suddenly yelled and in the next instant, gunshots erupted.</p>
<p>Bullets tore through the air but West was no longer at his initial standing point.</p>
<p>He charged forward with bullets slamming into his body but he didn’t slow down for even a second.</p>
<p>His fist shot forward—</p>
<p>BOOM.</p>
<p>One man’s face caved in instantly as his body launched backward into the wall.</p>
<p>Another turned to aim but West grabbed him before he could fire and hurled him upward.</p>
<p>The man smashed into the ceiling, tearing through it and causing panels to rip open.</p>
<p>The body stuck halfway through before dropping lifelessly.</p>
<p>West spun as another attacker fired point-blank and drove his fist into the man’s chest.</p>
<p>The impact folded him, crushing his ribs and collapsing his lungs. He dropped dead instantly.</p>
<p>At West’s current level of strength at 100 digits, normal humans stood no chance. He was already so strong that even a casual hit from him could send any one to the hospital with critical injuries.</p>
<p>Bang!</p>
<p>West grabbed a gun mid-fire and twisted, snapping the arm holding onto it.</p>
<p>Then drove his elbow into the man’s throat, crushing his windpipe.</p>
<p>Another tried to retreat but West didn’t allow it. He grabbed him by the collar and slammed him sideways into the plane wall, denting the metal.</p>
<p>The man slid down and stopped moving completely.</p>
<p>The last two fired wildly while West charged forward with astonishing speed, landing one punch on each of them.</p>
<p>With every single hitman down, silence finally returned to this part of the plane.</p>
<p>However, the plane interior was wrecked with bullet holes all over the walls.</p>
<p>Smoke permeated the surroundings and blood coated the floor along with bodies.</p>
<p>West stood in the center with a calm expression. His breathing was absolutely normal like this wasn’t even considered workout for him.</p>
<p>A voice suddenly crackled overhead.</p>
<p>"Mayday—mayday—mayday!" The pilots yelled with panicked tones.</p>
<p>"We’ve sustained internal damage—repeat—internal damage!"</p>
<p>"We are losing pressure—!"</p>
<p>The plane shuddered slightly while passengers began screaming in the distance.</p>
<p>West glanced in the direction of the cockpit. "...Great."</p>
<p>The cabin shook again violently while tilting sharply.</p>
<p>It suddenly began to drop causing screams to erupt from every corner of the aircraft as overhead compartments burst open and loose luggage tumbled down like rain.</p>
<p>Inside the cockpit, alarms screamed.</p>
<p>"Engine two failure!"</p>
<p>"Engine one is overheating—no—no it’s gone too!"</p>
<p>"Stabilize! Stabilize!"</p>
<p>The pilots fought desperately with the controls as their hands moved frantically.</p>
<p>Voices rose over the blaring warning systems and then they regained control again.</p>
<p>"Stabilizing..." one of the pilots let out a sigh of relief.</p>
<p>The plane then started to balance, straightening slowly and soon the screams lowered.</p>
<p>It appeared as though the clash from earlier resulted in the engines getting pierced by stray bullets but fortunately things seemed better now...</p>
<p>Or at least that’s what everyone thought...</p>
<p>Fwwwhooomm~</p>
<p>The plane suddenly lurched.</p>
<p>"What? Another engine failure?!" One of the pilots yelled as the nose dipped and then altitude began dropping faster.</p>
<p>"—We’re losing lift!"</p>
<p>"Mayday—mayday—this is Flight—!"</p>
<p>The transmission cut into static.</p>
<p>—</p>
<p>Back in the main cabin, chaos had fully taken over.</p>
<p>Passengers cried, prayed and clung to each other. Some were frozen in terror while others screamed hysterically as reality was sunk in... they were falling.</p>
<p>—</p>
<p>However, West remained still before Dr. Amos who lay slumped against the seat with blood still seeping faintly from fresh wounds sustained during the earlier gunfight.</p>
<p>Without hesitation, West pulled out one of the Chalice bottles.</p>
<p>"Drink."</p>
<p>Amos wasted no time in downing the liquid. Almost immediately, the bullets embedded in his body began to pop out one of the other.</p>
<p>Debra gasped as they pushed themselves out of his flesh.</p>
<p>Amos groaned in shock as his wounds began sealing. The skin knitted itself together rapidly and within seconds, the bleeding stopped.</p>
<p>"...Unbelievable..." Amos whispered while staring at his hands.</p>
<p>He looked up at West in astonishment. "...Thank you."</p>
<p>"I truly appreciate it."</p>
<p>However, his gaze drifted toward the shaking cabin with a disturbed expression.</p>
<p>"But unless you have a way to save the plane..." he displayed a hollow smile. "...it’ll all be for nothing."</p>
<p>West looked around at the tilt, vibration and flames flickering outside the window.</p>
<p>"...Yeah," he stood to his feet. "But no promises."</p>
<p>...</p>
<p>As West moved toward the cockpit, people tried to stop him.</p>
<p>"Sir, you can’t go there—!"</p>
<p>An air hostess reached for him while an air marshal stepped forward.</p>
<p>"Sir, return to your seat immediately or we will use force—"</p>
<p>West didn’t even slow down for even a second. He pushed the cockpit door open and stepped inside the room of panic.</p>
<p>The pilots barely spared him a glance as they worked frantically. "Who the hell are you? Get out of here!"</p>
<p>"We’re trying to keep this plane in the air!"</p>
<p>West stepped forward calmly. "Can you fix it?"</p>
<p>"What?!"</p>
<p>"Can you get it flying again?"</p>
<p>The co-pilot barked out a laugh. "Do you see what’s happening?! Both engines are gone!"</p>
<p>"Then answer me clearly. Is there any chance?"</p>
<p>The pilot snapped. "No! There’s no way!"</p>
<p>"...Alright," West turned but behind him, the air hostess and marshal were still shouting.</p>
<p>"Get back to your seat!"</p>
<p>"Sir, this is your final warning—!"</p>
