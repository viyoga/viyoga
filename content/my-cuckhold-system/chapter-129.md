<p>"What do you mean it wasn’t a gang?!"</p>
<p>"One person went in first."</p>
<p>Now genuine confusion spread.</p>
<p>"One person?"</p>
<p>"Yes... I do not know which gang he represents but he is an awakened."</p>
<p>Once the guard finished speaking, the men standing around the ruin entrance immediately broke into loud laughter.</p>
<p>"An awakened?" one of them scoffed. "So what?"</p>
<p>Another spat to the side. "Greedy idiot. Going into a multi-layer ruin alone?"</p>
<p>Everyone there understood what that meant.</p>
<p>A ruin with multiple layers was far more dangerous than a regular one. Each descending floor increased both the quantity and strength of creatures inhabiting it.</p>
<p>And this ruin had something even worse... a hidden third layer.</p>
<p>That alone changed everything.</p>
<p>One of the gang leaders folded his arms and said confidently, "He’s dead."</p>
<p>Several others nodded in agreement.</p>
<p>"To clear a third-layer ruin," another man added, "you’d normally need a Tier 2 Awakened leading several powerful Tier 1s."</p>
<p>"Exactly," someone else laughed. "And that fool went in alone."</p>
<p>The groups quickly came to a mutual understanding.</p>
<p>A third layer meant something else besides danger... lots of loot.</p>
<p>One of the gang leaders stepped forward.</p>
<p>"We all know the third layer will be massive. There’ll be creatures everywhere... and treasures too."</p>
<p>He glanced at the others.</p>
<p>"Instead of fighting each other outside, we go in together."</p>
<p>Another leader smirked.</p>
<p>"And split the loot?"</p>
<p>"As long as it’s fair."</p>
<p>A few moments passed and then, they all nodded one after the other.</p>
<p>"Deal."</p>
<p>With their temporary alliance formed, the gang members began preparing their weapons and equipment before moving.</p>
<p>Groups of awakeners descended through the ruin entrance, disappearing into the darkness of the underground structure as they headed toward the third layer.</p>
<p>Soon the area outside the ruin grew quiet again.</p>
<p>Only the guards remained.</p>
<p>One of the younger guards looked at the man who had told the gangs about West entering earlier.</p>
<p>"...Why didn’t you mention the other person who snuck in after him?"</p>
<p>The guard shrugged casually.</p>
<p>"Who knows who that was?"</p>
<p>He leaned against the wall lazily.</p>
<p>"Probably some misguided tourist."</p>
<p>The younger guard frowned slightly.</p>
<p>"You think they’ll survive?"</p>
<p>The older guard snorted.</p>
<p>"In a third-layer ruin?"</p>
<p>He shook his head.</p>
<p>"If an awakened can’t survive there themselves, then a regular person is most likely already dead."</p>
<p>"There was no point mentioning it."</p>
<p>—</p>
<p>Not far from the ruin entrance, hidden behind a large rock formation, Nina stood silently.</p>
<p>She had been watching everything and listening. What she heard made her heart sink.</p>
<p>"...dead already..."</p>
<p>The words echoed repeatedly in her mind as her fingers tightened around her phone.</p>
<p>"No..."</p>
<p>She quickly unlocked it and dialed West’s number.</p>
<p>Unfortunately, it didn’t go through.</p>
<p>Nina frowned and tried again but still it was pointless.</p>
<p>A small message suddenly appeared on her screen.</p>
<p>No Signal.</p>
<p>Her expression stiffened.</p>
<p>"...Of course."</p>
<p>Being deep underground meant the ruin likely blocked all outside signals.</p>
<p>Her grip on the phone tightened slightly as her worry increased. How was she supposed to contact him now?</p>
<p>Her chest tightened increasingly.</p>
<p>"...West..."</p>
<p>—</p>
<p>Meanwhile, deep within the third layer of the ruin, West stood in the middle of a vast cavern as the ground trembled...</p>
<p>Grrruuhhllllll~</p>
<p>A deep rumble spread across the cavern as cracks began spreading across the stone floor nearby.</p>
<p>West’s eyes sharpened as a section of the western cavern wall suddenly collapsed inward.</p>
<p>BOOM!</p>
<p>BOOM!</p>
<p>BOOM!</p>
<p>BOOM!</p>
<p>The sounds that followed echoed throughout the chamber like thunder.</p>
<p>West tilted his head slightly toward the collapsed section.</p>
<p>His brows slowly furrowed.</p>
<p>"...Those aren’t explosions."</p>
<p>The rhythmic thunderous noises were far too consistent.</p>
<p>In the next moment, realization dawned on him.</p>
<p>"...Footsteps."</p>
<p>A massive silhouette slowly emerged from the collapsing stone dust.</p>
<p>When the creature finally became fully visible, even West’s expression hardened slightly.</p>
<p>It was enormous with a height of fourteen feet.</p>
<p>Its body was incredibly muscular, wihth a strange smooth grayish looking skin.</p>
<p>Its limbs were an eerie metallic blue, reflecting the cavern’s light like forged steel.</p>
<p>Part of its face was covered by a strange armored plating, almost like a mask fused directly to its flesh...</p>
<p>And all of its eyes... glowed a different color.</p>
<p>Red...</p>
<p>Yellow...</p>
<p>Green...</p>
<p>Purple...</p>
<p>Four glowing eyes stared down at West with terrifying intensity.</p>
<p>Behind the creature, more of the four feet figures appeared.</p>
<p>They were over twenty in number this time, surrounding the strange throne positioned at the center of the cavern.</p>
<p>The being seated upon the throne leaned slightly forward, observing West with cold interest and then spoke with that strange tongue once more.</p>
<p>But the system within West’s mind immediately translated the intent.</p>
<p>『 "...Butcher." 』</p>
<p>The creature on the throne lifted a finger slightly and pointed at West.</p>
<p>『 "Get him." 』</p>
<p>The fourteen-foot monster immediately began moving.</p>
<p>BOOM!</p>
<p>BOOM!</p>
<p>BOOM!</p>
<p>Each step shook the cavern floor as it charged toward West.</p>
<p>At the same time, the twenty smaller creatures screeched and rushed forward.</p>
<p>West’s gaze swept across the battlefield in a split second and made a decision.</p>
<p>"Verdant Pyre Behemoths."</p>
<p>The massive flaming beasts behind him roared and charged forward to intercept the swarm of smaller creatures.</p>
<p>The cavern instantly erupted into chaos as claws, fire, and monstrous screeches filled the air.</p>
<p>However, West focused entirely on the giant approaching him.</p>
<p>The creature raised its massive arm and then swung downward.</p>
<p>The fist descended like a falling boulder but West did not dodge.</p>
<p>Instead, he stepped forward and threw his own punch.</p>
<p>BOOM!!</p>
<p>The collision was catastrophic.</p>
<p>A small shockwave erupted outward as their fists collided, sending cracks spider-webbing across the stone floor beneath them.</p>
<p>For a split second, the entire cavern seemed to tremble before West was sent flying.</p>
<p>His body shot backward through the air before crashing across the ground and sliding dozens of feet.</p>
<p>Stone shattered beneath him as he finally came to a stop.</p>
<p>West slowly pushed himself back onto his feet with his eyes narrowing slightly.</p>
<p>There was no doubt that ever since increasing his sub-stats, his physical capabilities had risen dramatically but this thing... was on a different level.</p>
<p>A thin layer of frost had spread across the arm he used to clash with the creature.</p>
<p>The cold crept along his skin like creeping ice.</p>
<p>West clicked his tongue and without hesitation he placed that arm against the ground.</p>
<p>Specifically—</p>
<p>Onto the blazing footprints left behind by the Verdant Pyre Behemoths as they fought nearby.</p>
<p>The flames quickly melted the ice causing steam to hiss as the frost evaporated.</p>
<p>Despite that, his arm felt slightly numb.</p>
<p>West rolled his shoulder.</p>
<p>"...Just like the others?"</p>
<p>Before he could analyze further—</p>
<p>BOOM!</p>
<p>The creature charged again with a speed that was ridiculously fast for something its size.</p>
<p>West moved this time.</p>
<p>He swerved sharply to the side as the massive fist slammed into the ground where he had been standing.</p>
<p>The impact shattered stone and sent debris flying everywhere.</p>
<p>West stepped in during the opening and his fist shot forward.</p>
<p>BAM!</p>
<p>The punch landed squarely against the creature’s torso but the impact felt strange.</p>
<p>It was like punching solid metal.</p>
<p>The creature slid back several feet from the force, yet it barely reacted.</p>
<p>West’s brows furrowed slightly.</p>
<p>"...Tough."</p>
<p>His punch was definitely strong enough to move it but he hadn’t truly hurt it due to its durability.</p>
<p>The creature slowly lifted its head locking onto West once again with its multicolored eyes as its lips curled into something resembling a cruel smile.</p>
