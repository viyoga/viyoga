<p>West stared at the screen silently because for the last few days, he had avoided him.</p>
<p>Because every time he thought about the truth regarding his sister, the anger resurfaced again.</p>
<p>But this time, it wasn’t just directed toward Debra... it was directed toward Mark too.</p>
<p>Eventually, West answered.</p>
<p>"...Yeah."</p>
<p>There was brief silence on the other end before Mark’s voice rang out softly.</p>
<p>"Finally."</p>
<p>West leaned back quietly. "You kept calling."</p>
<p>"I was worried."</p>
<p>West laughed lightly. "Funny you should say that."</p>
<p>"Uhm... you good son? Did something happen?" Mark questioned with a tone of worry from the other end.</p>
<p>"I wonder, Mark. Did it?" West responded with a dark tone.</p>
<p>Mark immediately understood the tone. "...Debra told you," he voiced in realization.</p>
<p>West’s voice lowered. "Tell me it’s not true."</p>
<p>"Tell me everything she said was a lie."</p>
<p>~Silence~</p>
<p>West’s voice hardened. "Tell me I never had a sister."</p>
<p>Mark finally exhaled quietly from the other side. "...I’m sorry."</p>
<p>That answer alone confirmed everything again.</p>
<p>West shut his eyes tightly. For some reason hearing it directly from his father hurt worse.</p>
<p>"You lied to me my entire life."</p>
<p>"West—"</p>
<p>"No."</p>
<p>West sat upright now. "You don’t get to do that. I gave you a second chance. You had every opportunity to tell me."</p>
<p>Mark’s voice became heavier. "She was falling apart."</p>
<p>"And that gave you the right to erase someone from existence?!"</p>
<p>"She wasn’t erased."</p>
<p>"Then why didn’t I know?!"</p>
<p>The shout echoed across the apartment sharply and even the summons went silent.</p>
<p>Mark remained quiet briefly before finally speaking again.</p>
<p>"...Because your mother was dying inside."</p>
<p>"She stopped sleeping."</p>
<p>Mark’s voice sounded tired now.</p>
<p>"Stopped eating properly. Started hallucinating. Crying randomly. Calling out your sister’s name in the middle of the night."</p>
<p>West stayed silent as Mark continued.</p>
<p>"I thought I was going to lose her too."</p>
<p>"She made me promise never to tell you because every reminder destroyed her. And breaking that promise was just something I never could bring myself to do even after so many years had gone by."</p>
<p>"There is no excuse... nothing excuses this sort of thing. I went my whole life never knowing," West’s voice lowered dangerously.</p>
<p>West stood from the couch and walked toward the massive window overlooking the city. "You had no right."</p>
<p>Mark sighed heavily. "...Maybe you’re right."</p>
<p>"I failed somewhere as a husband," he paused slightly before adding. "And maybe as a father too."</p>
<p>"But at the time..." Mark’s voice weakened faintly, "...I genuinely thought protecting her was the only thing I could do."</p>
<p>West stared out across the glowing skyline quietly.</p>
<p>There was a growing anger threatening to erupt deep inside of him but he didn’t know who to be angry at anymore.</p>
<p>He needed somewhere to direct all this anger at and he could only think of one solution...</p>
<p>•••</p>
<p>•••</p>
<p>The rain poured heavily across Misty High City.</p>
<p>Neon lights reflected across wet roads while distant sirens echoed somewhere in the lower districts.</p>
<p>A man with a grey left eye, walked slowly through the downpour with blood dripping from his knuckles.</p>
<p>The men kneeling before him were trembling badly.</p>
<p>One had both legs shattered already while another was missing teeth.</p>
<p>The third kept coughing blood while trying to crawl backward through puddles.</p>
<p>Darius calmly rolled up his sleeves. "You borrowed money from Eagles Print."</p>
<p>His deep voice carried no emotion whatsoever.</p>
<p>"And then disappeared."</p>
<p>The bleeding man cried immediately. "Please! Please! I just needed more time!"</p>
<p>Darius sighed softly. "You said that three weeks ago."</p>
<p>CRACK~</p>
<p>His boot stomped down onto the man’s wrist.</p>
<p>The scream that followed echoed across the alley and the Nearby Eagles Print members laughed while smoking.</p>
<p>One of them shook his head. "Boss Darius is in a bad mood today."</p>
<p>The other snorted. "He’s ALWAYS in a bad mood."</p>
<p>Darius ignored them completely and crouched calmly before the crying debtor.</p>
<p>"You know what the problem with regular people is?"</p>
<p>The man shook violently. "You all think gangs are banks."</p>
<p>Darius grabbed the man’s jaw firmly. "We are not banks."</p>
<p>He slammed the man face-first into the concrete causing blood to splatter across the wet ground.</p>
<p>"Collect the money from his shop," Darius said calmly while standing again.</p>
<p>"And break one more leg before you leave."</p>
<p>The gang members grinned. "Yes boss."</p>
<p>Darius lit a cigarette afterward and stepped back into the rain.</p>
<p>Darius Bentley was a forty-three years old tier 2 Awakened and a high-ranking enforcer of Eagles Print.</p>
<p>Eagles Print just happened to be one of the five strongest gangs in the entire city.</p>
<p>What had happened here was just a regular Tuesday to him... Debt collection, territory disputes, violence, threats and sometimes executions.</p>
<p>Eagles Print ruled several major commercial districts in Misty High City and Darius helped keep it that way.</p>
<p>People feared him... the heavy scar across his throat, his ability and the fact that he once crushed another awakened’s skull barehanded during a territorial war...</p>
<p>That fear made life easier.</p>
<p>•••</p>
<p>Elsewhere later that night, a ruin had opened near the industrial district.</p>
<p>Eagles Print vehicles flooded the streets and armed awakened members surrounded the perimeter aggressively.</p>
<p>Darius stepped out from one armored truck while cracking his neck slowly.</p>
<p>The atmosphere around the ruins already felt dangerous and several smaller gangs stood nearby arguing loudly.</p>
<p>One stepped forward angrily. "We arrived first!"</p>
<p>Darius glanced at him once and the smaller gang leader immediately swallowed his remaining words.</p>
<p>Darius spoke calmly. "You can leave now."</p>
<p>The man clenched his fists. "...And if we don’t?"</p>
<p>BOOOOOOM!</p>
<p>The ground beneath Darius exploded instantly as dark metallic spikes erupted upward around him while black steam-like energy leaked from his body.</p>
<p>His Tier 2 pressure flooded the surroundings brutally and some lower awakened nearby nearly collapsed immediately.</p>
<p>Darius stared at the smaller gang leader coldly.</p>
<p>"Then I’ll bury you here."</p>
<p>A few seconds later, the smaller gang retreated immediately.</p>
<p>Darius deactivated his ability afterward and lit another cigarette casually.</p>
<p>One of his subordinates laughed. "They folded fast."</p>
<p>Darius exhaled smoke calmly. "Bullying smaller gangs is usually fun some times. I enjoy doing this sometimes even though it is unnecessary."</p>
<p>The ruin raid began and hours later, the creatures inside the ruins had been ripped apart violently.</p>
<p>The others recounted the tale of how Darius fought like an entirely different being within the ruins.</p>
<p>"A massive monster charged toward him from the darkness, only for metallic black spikes to erupt through its body from beneath the ground instantly..."</p>
<p>SCHKKKKK~</p>
<p>"There was blood everywhere."</p>
<p>One Eagles Print rookie stared in awe.</p>
<p>"...That’s why he’s one of the strongest captains."</p>
<p>Another nodded quickly.</p>
<p>"Don’t ever piss him off."</p>
<p>But strangely, despite the violence... the blood... and the terrifying reputation...</p>
<p>There existed another side to Darius Bentley that almost nobody in Eagles Print ever saw.</p>
<p>The next morning, the same terrifying man who crushed awakened skulls for a living now stood in a kitchen wearing an apron, making pancakes.</p>
<p>"Dad! Mia stole my juice!"</p>
<p>A small girl with braided hair pointed angrily toward her older brother.</p>
<p>The older boy immediately protested.</p>
<p>"She wasn’t drinking it!"</p>
<p>"She was!"</p>
<p>"She wasn’t!"</p>
<p>Darius sighed tiredly while flipping pancakes expertly.</p>
<p>"Both of you sit down before I start charging rent."</p>
<p>The children laughed immediately and then his wife, Yelena, entered the kitchen afterward while tying her hair loosely.</p>
<p>"You’re threatening children before 8am?"</p>
<p>Darius kissed her forehead naturally. "Builds character."</p>
<p>She rolled her eyes. "You say that every time."</p>
<p>Despite the scar on his throat and his terrifying appearance... the way he looked at his family softened him completely, especially around his children.</p>
<p>At home, Darius wasn’t a gang enforcer or a Tier 2 awakened from Eagles Print, he was just, a dad.</p>
<p>After breakfast, he helped his daughter pack her school bag carefully, fixed his son’s tie and then drove both children personally to school.</p>
<p>Several parents stiffened slightly when they saw him arrive because everybody knew who Darius Bentley was.</p>
<p>Eagles Print wasn’t exactly subtle... still, Darius simply waved politely while holding his daughter’s hand.</p>
<p>"Dad," his son muttered awkwardly, "you don’t have to walk us all the way inside."</p>
<p>"Yes I do."</p>
<p>"...I’m thirteen."</p>
<p>"And still stupid."</p>
<p>"Dad."</p>
<p>"You heard me."</p>
<p>His daughter giggled loudly beside them.</p>
<p>Darius suddenly turned towards a pillar on the side with a look of curiosity. "Uh..."</p>
<p>"What are you looking at dad?" His son questioned.</p>
<p>"I thought I saw someone there..."</p>
<p>"There’s no one there old man. Already developing sight issues at your old age?"</p>
<p>His son teased him as they stepped past the pillar while he stared at it again with a look of confoundment.</p>
<p>•••</p>
<p>•••</p>
<p>~ Three Days Later ~</p>
<p>The massive holographic billboard floating above one of Misty High City’s busiest intersections flickered brightly before transitioning into a live news segment.</p>
<p>A female reporter appeared instantly and behind her was the crumbling entrance of a freshly collapsed ruin.</p>
<p>Emergency response vehicles surrounded the area while awakened gangs moved around carrying loot and monster remains.</p>
<p>But most importantly, standing directly at the center of the attention was West.</p>
<p>Dressed in the black and crimson Shadow Reapers jacket with his hands casually tucked into his pockets, he looked more like some arrogant celebrity than a gang leader.</p>
<p>The camera zoomed slightly. "Once again," the reporter spoke excitedly, "the rapidly rising newly awakened gang known as Shadow Reapers has successfully cleared another ruin."</p>
