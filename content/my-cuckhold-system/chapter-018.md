<p>The next two days passed quickly.</p>
<p>Too quickly.</p>
<p>Thursday blurred into Friday, and by the time West realized it, it was almost weekend again.</p>
<p>The system chimed.</p>
<p>&gt; [Quest Completed]</p>
<p>Objective: Acquire five phone numbers of girls with romantic partners]</p>
<p>West stopped walking.</p>
<p>The hallway around him buzzed with voices and footsteps as lockers slammed open and shut, but for a brief moment, it all faded into the background.</p>
<p>He had done it and even exceeded.</p>
<p>Five was required but be got six due to his newly acquired popularity.</p>
<p>The system responded immediately.</p>
<p>&gt; [Rewards Granted]</p>
<p>Cuck Points +50</p>
<p>All Sub Stats +3</p>
<p>Skill Upgrade: Adrenal Control — Level Increased]</p>
<p>West exhaled sharply.</p>
<p>His heart thudded hard in his chest from something dangerously close to exhilaration.</p>
<p>He opened the interface mid-stride and the translucent blue panels aligned neatly before his eyes as he continued walking.</p>
<p>---</p>
<p>[SYSTEM STATUS UPDATE]</p>
<p>Host: West Einstein</p>
<p>Age: 18</p>
<p>Tier: 0 — Dormant</p>
<p>Awakening Status: Locked</p>
<p>Affinity: 2%</p>
<p>Reputation: None</p>
<p>Available Menus:</p>
<p>SHOP</p>
<p>QUEST PANEL</p>
<p>INVENTORY</p>
<p>SKILLS</p>
<p>(( Adrenal Control - Lv.2 ))</p>
<p>Cuck Points (CP): 115</p>
<p>---</p>
<p>[Sub Stats]</p>
<p>Strength: 12</p>
<p>Speed: 13</p>
<p>Agility: 12</p>
<p>Seduction: 12</p>
<p>Confidence: 14</p>
<p>Charm: 11</p>
<p>Aura: 11</p>
<p>Mental Resilience: 11</p>
<p>Stamina: 12</p>
<p>---</p>
<p>[Skill: Adrenal Control]</p>
<p>Rank: Level 2</p>
<p>Duration: 90 seconds</p>
<p>Cooldown: 24 hours</p>
<p>Enhancement Effect: Adds 13 Points to all substats.</p>
<p>---</p>
<p>West stared at the numbers as he walked.</p>
<p>One hundred and fifteen Cuck Points...</p>
<p>That was more than enough.</p>
<p>More than he had imagined having this soon.</p>
<p>His fingers curled slowly.</p>
<p>Tier 1...</p>
<p>The thought alone made his pulse quicken.</p>
<p>Awakening wasn’t just power.</p>
<p>It was legitimacy.</p>
<p>It meant no one could casually put their hands on him again.</p>
<p>No one could force him to kneel.</p>
<p>No one could treat him like disposable trash.</p>
<p>West’s lips curved faintly. ’I can do it now.’</p>
<p>The system didn’t push him or urge him.</p>
<p>The option sat there, waiting. It was completely up to him to become an awakened and if he didn’t want to, he could simply keep increasing his substats.</p>
<p>As he rounded the corner of the hallway, someone stepped into his path.</p>
<p>Thump~</p>
<p>A soft collision between them caused books to fly into the air.</p>
<p>West reacted instantly.</p>
<p>His hand shot out as pages fluttered and covers spun while the books fell.</p>
<p>Swwhiii~</p>
<p>He caught every single book mid-air, moving smoothly with his fingers adjusting naturally as he stacked them neatly atop his palm.</p>
<p>He looked up and Lena stood frozen in front of him.</p>
<p>Her eyes were wide and her lips parted slightly.</p>
<p>For a second, neither of them spoke.</p>
<p>West handed the books back to her calmly.</p>
<p>"Be careful," he said.</p>
<p>His voice was neutral and detached.</p>
<p>Nothing like the boy she remembered.</p>
<p>Lena accepted the stack with trembling hands.</p>
<p>"...Thanks," she whispered.</p>
<p>West nodded once and stepped past her without looking back.</p>
<p>But Lena did.</p>
<p>She stood there, staring at his retreating back with her heart pounding painfully against her ribs.</p>
<p>How...?</p>
<p>How could someone change so much in just two weeks?</p>
<p>His posture was different...</p>
<p>His presence was more defining...</p>
<p>The way he moved so confidently...</p>
<p>He wasn’t the West she had left behind.</p>
<p>And that realization hurt more than she expected.</p>
<p>As West walked away, something else caught his attention.</p>
<p>He had noticed red markings around her wrists.</p>
<p>She tried to hide them by shifting her sleeves down, but she was not fast enough.</p>
<p>West noticed at the time and now remembering it... he paused internally.</p>
<p>"Huh."</p>
<p>He scoffed quietly.</p>
<p>"None of my business."</p>
<p>He didn’t slow down or turn.</p>
<p>He had already learned what happened when he involved himself in other people’s messes.</p>
<p>Behind him, Lena stood motionless long after he was gone.</p>
<p>For the rest of the day, she couldn’t focus.</p>
<p>Her mind replayed the moment over and over.</p>
<p>The way he caught the books.</p>
<p>The way he spoke without emotion.</p>
<p>The way he walked away without hesitation.</p>
<p>"Did I make a mistake?"</p>
<p>She shook her head.</p>
<p>"No," she whispered to herself. "He’s still unawakened."</p>
<p>That thought comforted her... briefly.</p>
<p>But it didn’t silence the ache in her chest.</p>
<p>---</p>
<p>Elsewhere —</p>
<p>Night fell over the city.</p>
<p>Far from the lights of schools and cafés, deep within a restricted industrial district, a group of men knelt in perfect formation.</p>
<p>They all wore matching crimson jackets with flaming chain tattoos etched into their necks.</p>
<p>This was the Iron Chain gang. A street level gang.</p>
<p>At the front stood a man who made everyone else look small.</p>
<p>He was six-foot-seven, broad as a wall with muscle hardened thick shoulders. His presence alone bent the atmosphere around him and his face looked like it was carved from the spirit of violence.</p>
<p>This was Magnus Vire, the boss of Iron Chain.</p>
<p>"Boss!" the kneeling men shouted in unison.</p>
<p>Magnus turned slowly and nodded sharply.</p>
<p>"Up."</p>
<p>They rose instantly.</p>
<p>Magnus walked forward, causing the sound of boots to echo softly as he stopped before an 18 year old youngster.</p>
<p>Raze Calder.</p>
<p>"Aren’t you supposed to be bringing in the new kid?" Magnus asked calmly. "The one set for initiation and awakened martial arts allocation?"</p>
<p>Raze lowered his head slightly.</p>
<p>"Yes, boss," he said. "But that kid is unworthy."</p>
<p>Magnus’s eyes narrowed.</p>
<p>"Explain."</p>
<p>Raze spoke carefully, recounting everything.</p>
<p>The school...</p>
<p>Caleb’s transformation...</p>
<p>The punch that led to his defeat...</p>
<p>"And then," Raze concluded, "the kid walked away. We can’t have trash awakened ruining the reputation of the gang."</p>
<p>Magnus was silent for a long moment.</p>
<p>Then he said, "Why not bring in the one who defeated him?"</p>
<p>Raze hesitated.</p>
<p>"...Because he’s unawakened."</p>
<p>The words landed like a bomb.</p>
<p>Magnus turned sharply.</p>
<p>"...What?"</p>
<p>Raze met his gaze steadily. "I’m sure. No branch activation. No aura. No awakening fluctuation."</p>
<p>Magnus’s eyes gleamed dangerously.</p>
<p>"That’s impossible," he said. "An unawakened cannot defeat an awakened. Either you were mistaken... or that kid is hiding something."</p>
<p>He turned away with hands clasped behind his back.</p>
<p>Then displayed a predatory smile.</p>
<p>"That boy," Magnus said, "has potential."</p>
<p>Everyone leaned forward unconsciously.</p>
<p>"For him to defeat another tier one awakened without so much as activating his branch, he must possess multiple branches," Magnus continued.</p>
<p>Murmurs rippled through the group.</p>
<p>Multiple branches.</p>
<p>At that age?</p>
<p>That was monstrous potential.</p>
<p>Magnus looked back at Raze.</p>
<p>"You must do what it takes," he said calmly, "to make him join."</p>
<p>Raze bowed deeply.</p>
<p>"Yes, boss."</p>
<p>Magnus’s gaze lifted toward the distant city lights.</p>
<p>"Our gang must become number one in Misty High City..."</p>
