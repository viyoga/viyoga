<p>One of his subordinates approached carefully. "The territory dispute near West Junction is getting worse."</p>
<p>Darius sighed tiredly. "Those idiots again?"</p>
<p>"They’re refusing to pay route tax."</p>
<p>Darius cracked his neck once. "Fine."</p>
<p>His expression hardened immediately. "Let’s go remind them who owns those streets."</p>
<p>The warmth on his face vanished instantly like it was never there.</p>
<p>~ Thirty Minutes later ~</p>
<p>SCREEEECH~</p>
<p>Vehicles drifted aggressively outside a large warehouse district and eagles Print members flooded out rapidly.</p>
<p>Several rival gang members immediately panicked.</p>
<p>"IT’S DARIUS!"</p>
<p>"Oh shit!"</p>
<p>"RUN!"</p>
<p>Darius walked forward calmly while loosening his gloves.</p>
<p>The rival gang leader stepped out shakily. "T-This is unnecessary—"</p>
<p>A single punch from Darius, sent the man flew through two warehouse walls instantly.</p>
<p>BOOOOOOM!</p>
<p>Dust exploded everywhere and everyone stiffened in horror.</p>
<p>Darius curled his fingers into a fist and unfurled afterwards. "You people really love wasting my time."</p>
<p>The remaining gang members immediately dropped their weapons while his subordinates cheered loudly.</p>
<p>"That’s our vice-captain!"</p>
<p>"Boss Darius!"</p>
<p>"Teach these dogs!"</p>
<p>Darius ignored the praise entirely, simply staring coldly at the terrified men.</p>
<p>"Next time you ignore Eagles Print taxes..."</p>
<p>Crackling energy surged faintly around his fist.</p>
<p>"I won’t stop at one punch."</p>
<p>...</p>
<p>Later that evening, Darius sat at a dinner table inside his home while wearing casual clothes instead of gang attire.</p>
<p>The atmosphere was completely different with warm lights, soft music and the smell of food.</p>
<p>His daughter sat beside him laughing while trying to force vegetables into his mouth.</p>
<p>"Dad! Eat it!"</p>
<p>Darius groaned dramatically.</p>
<p>"I already did."</p>
<p>"No you didn’t!"</p>
<p>His son immediately joined in.</p>
<p>"Mom! Dad’s cheating again!"</p>
<p>His wife burst into laughter from the kitchen.</p>
<p>"You’re a grown man losing to children again."</p>
<p>Darius leaned back dramatically.</p>
<p>"This house is oppressive."</p>
<p>The kids laughed loudly while his daughter climbed into his lap.</p>
<p>"Dad are you coming to school tomorrow?"</p>
<p>"PTA meeting?"</p>
<p>She nodded excitedly.</p>
<p>"You promised."</p>
<p>Darius immediately softened. "Of course I’m coming."</p>
<p>His son looked shocked. "But you missed the last one!"</p>
<p>Darius coughed awkwardly. "I was working."</p>
<p>His wife walked over carrying plates. "You mean throwing gang members through buildings?"</p>
<p>Darius looked offended. "Very important work."</p>
<p>She rolled her eyes before kissing his cheek lightly.</p>
<p>The massive terrifying awakener who could flatten buildings smiled like an idiot afterward.</p>
<p>...</p>
<p>The next afternoon at a large restaurant in central Misty High, business seemed to be booming as usual... until Eagles Print arrived.</p>
<p>The atmosphere immediately turned tense as customers looked away nervously while workers stiffened visibly.</p>
<p>Darius and six subordinates casually occupied nearly half the restaurant upon their arrival.</p>
<p>One subordinate snapped his fingers loudly.</p>
<p>"Oi."</p>
<p>"Bring more meat."</p>
<p>The owner hurried over nervously.</p>
<p>"O-Of course sir..."</p>
<p>Sweat covered his forehead as Darius leaned back lazily in his chair while eating.</p>
<p>No one had dared asked for payment because everyone knew better.</p>
<p>One of the younger workers accidentally delayed bringing drinks.</p>
<p>A subordinate immediately slammed his fist against the table.</p>
<p>BANG!</p>
<p>"Move faster!"</p>
<p>The worker nearly dropped the tray. "S-Sorry!"</p>
<p>Darius chuckled while sipping from his glass. "These new gangs are growing balls lately."</p>
<p>Another subordinate laughed. "They don’t respect seniors anymore."</p>
<p>"Seriously."</p>
<p>One man scoffed while chewing loudly.</p>
<p>"Back then newcomers knew their place."</p>
<p>Darius smirked. "That’s why I beat the leader of that new River Dragon gang."</p>
<p>The others burst into laughter immediately.</p>
<p>"Boss nearly folded him in half!"</p>
<p>"He was crying!"</p>
<p>"They need discipline!"</p>
<p>Darius pointed lazily with his fork.</p>
<p>"Exactly."</p>
<p>"These small gangs get one little victory and suddenly think they’re important."</p>
<p>One subordinate suddenly spoke up. "What about that Shadow Reapers gang though?"</p>
<p>Another nodded. "Yeah. They’ve been all over local news lately."</p>
<p>"They clear ruins insanely fast."</p>
<p>"I heard they only have like ten members."</p>
<p>Darius chuckled. "Shadow Reapers?"</p>
<p>He leaned back slightly. "We just haven’t run into them yet."</p>
<p>The men around him grinned while Darius continued casually.</p>
<p>"I’ll whip that pretty little gang leader running around like he knows what he’s doing."</p>
<p>The table exploded with laughter.</p>
<p>"Boss would crush them!"</p>
<p>"Especially their leader!"</p>
<p>"News made him look cold though."</p>
<p>"Cold doesn’t matter when your jaw gets broken."</p>
<p>More laughter erupted while the restaurant owner forced himself to smile as he served them.</p>
<p>...</p>
<p>Later that night at a movie theater downtown, bright fluorescent lights reflected across wet pavement outside while crowds slowly exited the building.</p>
<p>Darius walked alongside his wife and kids while carrying popcorn buckets under one arm.</p>
<p>His daughter held onto his massive hand happily.</p>
<p>"That movie was awesome!"</p>
<p>His son jumped excitedly.</p>
<p>"The dragon fight was crazy!"</p>
<p>Darius laughed. "You two nearly screamed the theater down."</p>
<p>His wife nudged him. "You screamed too."</p>
<p>"Only to show my support..."</p>
<p>She rolled her eyes. "You’re impossible."</p>
<p>The family continued walking together laughing warmly when suddenly, Darius slowed slightly.</p>
<p>His expression changed as his eyes drifted toward a dark alley across the street.</p>
<p>The laughter around him faded from his senses momentarily as that same feeling returned. For over a week now, he had felt it repeatedly... like someone was watching him.</p>
<p>Darius narrowed his eyes toward the darkness but there was nothing there except for shadows, rainwater and dim lighting...</p>
<p>Still, that strange feeling lingered.</p>
<p>"Dad!" His daughter dragged his pants. "Come on!"</p>
<p>Darius blinked once before looking back down at her smiling face.</p>
<p>"...Yeah." He smiled softly again. "Coming."</p>
<p>•••</p>
<p>•••</p>
<p>~ Two Days Later ~</p>
<p>Darius stepped into his house after returning from another ruin expedition and immediately, the hairs on the back of his neck rose.</p>
<p>Usually, by this time, his daughter would already be running around the living room while his son blasted games loudly from the television.</p>
<p>His wife would either be in the kitchen or curled up on the couch watching something while waiting for him to get back.</p>
<p>But at this moment, there was only silence.</p>
<p>The house was completely empty.</p>
<p>Darius frowned slightly. "Honey?"</p>
<p>There was no response. He walked further inside and turned the lights on to observe the surroundings better even though he could clearly feel everything through his awakened senses.</p>
<p>His expression slowly hardened as he immediately reached down to pull out his phone and dialed the driver he had hired specifically to pick up his children from school daily.</p>
<p>Ring~</p>
<p>Ring~</p>
<p>Ring~</p>
<p>There was no answer. He proceeded to dial the number again and still, the same situation repeated.</p>
<p>Darius’ eyes narrowed and he proceeded to quickly call the school next.</p>
<p>"Yes, Mr. Bentley," the receptionist said politely.</p>
<p>"My kids... where are they?" Darius inquired with a grim tone.</p>
<p>"Your children were already picked up over an hour ago."</p>
<p>Darius froze. "...What?"</p>
<p>"Yes sir. They left at the usual time."</p>
<p>His eyes narrowed instantly. "The drive home is twenty minutes and they still haven’t arrived."</p>
<p>The receptionist hesitated. "...Perhaps traffic?"</p>
<p>"No."</p>
<p>Something cold crept into his chest as he further inquired. "Who picked them up?"</p>
<p>"...The driver, I assume?"</p>
<p>Darius immediately hung up and dialed the driver again.</p>
<p>No matter how many times the phone rang, there was no answer. After calling for the umpteenth time, he stopped and dialed the school again.</p>
<p>When the school answered again, his voice had become terrifyingly calm. "I want you to check the cameras."</p>
<p>"...Sir?"</p>
<p>"The person who picked up my children. Was it the regular driver? Check now!"</p>
<p>There was silence for a moment before typing sounds rang out.</p>
<p>"...No," the receptionist sounded nervous now. "It wasn’t."</p>
<p>BOOOOOOM!</p>
<p>Darius punched the wall beside him instantly and concrete exploded apart.</p>
<p>The entire side of the living room cracked violently while dust and debris crashed everywhere.</p>
<p>"WHO DARES TARGET MY FAMILY?!"</p>
<p>"DO THEY HAVE A DEATH WISH?!"</p>
<p>His eyes glowed fiercely with amber light as aura pressure burst throughout the house.</p>
<p>Furniture trembled while windows cracked as he slowly stepped forward.</p>
<p>Someone had actually dared to touch his family... all he could feel at the moment was immeasurable bloodlust.</p>
<p>Darius immediately began making phone calls to the right people... powerful connections with tracking specialties.</p>
<p>His influence as a high-ranking Eagles Print executive immediately went into motion.</p>
<p>Within minutes, people all across Misty High were searching but still his driver’s phone remained unreachable.</p>
<p>His wife’s phone had been turned off but Darius called his wife’s closest friends next.</p>
<p>Unfortunately, none of them knew anything.</p>
<p>One even nervously asked if everything was alright but he hung up immediately.</p>
<p>His breathing had already become uneven and for the first time in many years, true panic began setting in.</p>
<p>He could handle any enemy... any gang... any ruin... any awakened.</p>
<p>But his children and wife... if anything ever happened to them... that was one thing he couldn’t handle.</p>
<p>And whoever took them somehow bypassed every safeguard he had...</p>
<p>His phone suddenly rang and Darius answered instantly.</p>
<p>"What?!"</p>
<p>"Boss," one of his men spoke quickly. "We got a trace."</p>
<p>Darius immediately stilled. "Where?"</p>
<p>"It’s strange... but the signal pinged repeatedly from a new resort center at the edge of the city."</p>
<p>Darius’ expression darkened instantly. ’A resort? What kind of kidnapping is this?’</p>
<p>"Send everyone there immediately."</p>
