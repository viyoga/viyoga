<p>All ten focused on West as he continued. "I actually have two options to offer."</p>
<p>"The first..."</p>
<p>"I can arrange for all of you to enter proper foster care."</p>
<p>"You’ll receive housing, education, medical treatment, counseling... everything you need."</p>
<p>"Professionals will look after you."</p>
<p>Several exchanged uncertain looks while West continued.</p>
<p>"The second option..." he smiled faintly. "You come back to Misty High City with me."</p>
<p>They all stiffened as West leaned against a nearby crate. "I’ll become your legal guardian until you’re adults."</p>
<p>"I’ll make sure you have somewhere safe to live."</p>
<p>"You’ll eat properly."</p>
<p>"You’ll go to school if that’s what you want."</p>
<p>"If you’d rather work... I’ll give you jobs."</p>
<p>"No one will ever sell you."</p>
<p>"No one will ever chain you."</p>
<p>"No one will ever own your life again."</p>
<p>The ten teenagers stared at him in disbelief but West wasn’t finished.</p>
<p>"And if any of you choose to walk the awakened path..." his eyes became firm. "I’ll train you."</p>
<p>"I’ll help you become strong."</p>
<p>"So strong..."</p>
<p>"...that nobody will ever drag you away against your will again."</p>
<p>Silence filled the warehouse until one girl slowly raised her hand. "...Why?"</p>
<p>West smiled. "Because everyone deserves the chance to choose their own future."</p>
<p>Another teenager lowered his head. "...Do we have to decide now?"</p>
<p>West shook his head. "No."</p>
<p>"I’ll be in the city for a few more days. You have until then to decide."</p>
<p>He gestured toward the adjoining rooms. "There are beds."</p>
<p>"Bathrooms."</p>
<p>"Fresh clothes."</p>
<p>"Sleep."</p>
<p>"When you’ve rested..."</p>
<p>"...then we’ll talk again."</p>
<p>The group quietly nodded.</p>
<p>As they slowly dispersed, Sammy walked beside West. "...You really meant everything you said."</p>
<p>West looked toward the sleeping quarters where the teenagers had disappeared. "As I mentioned, I have my own vendetta. But its not something I intend to brute force. If any of them are coming back with me to Misty High... it will be of their own volition."</p>
<p>Sammy smiled. "I think they’ll choose you."</p>
<p>West shrugged. "Maybe."</p>
<p>"Maybe not."</p>
<p>"I guess we’ll see..."</p>
<p>•••</p>
<p>•••</p>
<p>The events in Orizon City spread faster than West had anticipated.</p>
<p>Within twenty-four hours, every major news outlet was carrying some variation of the same story.</p>
<p>~ Mysterious Awakened Launches Raid on Slum Traffickers ~</p>
<p>~ Over Sixty Captives Missing ~</p>
<p>~ Multiple Criminal Organizations Blame One Another ~</p>
<p>No one knew exactly what had happened which was why the descriptions varied wildly.</p>
<p>Some witnesses insisted there had been an entire squad of awakened, others claimed they had seen an enormous beast breathing beams of violet light while some swore a legendary mage had frozen an entire street.</p>
<p>Many dismissed that as pure fantasy but the only details that remained consistent were of the mysterious awakened who wore a black hood and had his face completely hidden behind a mask.</p>
<p>They claimed he possessed a terrifying transformation ability and had somehow escaped the slums despite half the criminal organizations there trying to stop him.</p>
<p>Ironically, West’s greatest advantage was also the thing that confused everyone.</p>
<p>No one had seen his actual face.</p>
<p>The few who had fought him only remembered glowing eyes, blue fur, monstrous claws, and overwhelming strength.</p>
<p>His human appearance remained a complete mystery.</p>
<p>The gangs eventually reached another conclusion.</p>
<p>"It couldn’t have been one person."</p>
<p>"No way."</p>
<p>"There was a summon."</p>
<p>"There was a transformed awakened."</p>
<p>"They’re different abilities."</p>
<p>"It had to be at least two people."</p>
<p>That single misunderstanding completely changed the investigation.</p>
<p>Instead of searching for one individual, the slum gangs began suspecting rival organizations.</p>
<p>Rumors spread rapidly.</p>
<p>"Maybe the Iron Serpents stole them."</p>
<p>"No... this feels like the Crimson Wolves."</p>
<p>"I heard an outside syndicate entered Orizon."</p>
<p>"They’re preparing for war."</p>
<p>Paranoia spread like wildfire, resurfacing old rivalries.</p>
<p>Several gangs even attacked one another over unfounded suspicions.</p>
<p>West, however... couldn’t have cared less.</p>
<p>...</p>
<p>The safe house became surprisingly lively over the following days.</p>
<p>The remaining teenagers were settling into something they hadn’t experienced in years... a normal life.</p>
<p>Every morning began with proper meals ranging from fresh bread to eggs, warm soup, fruit, milk and so on.</p>
<p>West insisted they eat together around the same table.</p>
<p>At first, the atmosphere was awkward. Most of them ate in silence, as though expecting someone to suddenly take the food away.</p>
<p>However, after several days, conversations slowly began, laughter occasionally escaped and someone even argued over who got the last orange.</p>
<p>West considered that a victory.</p>
<p>...</p>
<p>Among the remaining ten, two stood out almost immediately.</p>
<p>The oldest boy introduced himself as Tom. He had been seventeen for quite some time and would be turning eighteen soon.</p>
<p>He was tall and lean despite years of malnourishment. Tom had short ash-brown hair and calm gray eyes. His left leg still carried an old injury that caused him to limp slightly, but he rarely complained.</p>
<p>He naturally looked after the younger ones.</p>
<p>If someone forgot to eat, Tom reminded them. If someone woke from a nightmare... he was usually the first to comfort them.</p>
<p>West noticed it almost immediately. "You’re used to taking responsibility."</p>
<p>Tom smiled weakly. "When you’ve survived places like that..."</p>
<p>"You grow up fast."</p>
<p>The oldest girl, Nadia, was also seventeen.</p>
<p>She had shoulder-length black hair that had finally begun regaining its shine after several proper meals.</p>
<p>Unlike Tom’s quiet nature, Nadia possessed remarkable spirit.</p>
<p>She teased people, laughed loudly and rolled her eyes whenever West tried making terrible jokes.</p>
<p>"You really think that’s funny?"</p>
<p>West looked offended. "It was hilarious."</p>
<p>"It absolutely wasn’t."</p>
<p>Several of the others burst into laughter while West pointed dramatically. "See? They’re laughing."</p>
<p>"They’re laughing at you."</p>
<p>West placed a hand over his chest. "Betrayal."</p>
<p>The room erupted with another round of laughter.</p>
<p>...</p>
<p>Not everyone remembered who they had once been.</p>
<p>One quiet girl spent most of her time staring through windows.</p>
<p>Whenever someone asked her name, she simply lowered her head. "I..."</p>
<p>"I don’t remember."</p>
<p>West never pressured her.</p>
<p>Several days later, while helping Sammy unpack supplies, he noticed her quietly watching birds land on the warehouse fence.</p>
<p>"They’re pretty."</p>
<p>She nodded. "They always come back."</p>
<p>West smiled thoughtfully. "...Then how about Robin?"</p>
<p>She blinked. "The bird."</p>
<p>"It always finds its way home."</p>
<p>She repeated the name softly. "...Robin."</p>
<p>A tiny smile slowly formed. "I like it."</p>
<p>She looked up at him with eyes that had finally regained a little life. "...Can I really use that name?"</p>
<p>West shrugged. "Names aren’t prisons."</p>
<p>"They’re something you choose."</p>
<p>Robin’s smile widened. "...Thank you."</p>
<p>She ran joyously to the others and told them. "Call me Robin from now on."</p>
<p>When she recounted how West gave her the name, some looked jealous but were still very happy for her.</p>
<p>Nearby, another teenage boy admitted he remembered nothing before arriving at the slums years earlier... not even his own name.</p>
<p>West studied him for a moment noticing how stubborn the boy was... always trying to help carry boxes despite a damaged shoulder.</p>
<p>Always insisting he could manage things himself...</p>
<p>West chuckled. "You remind me of an old oak tree."</p>
<p>The boy looked confused. "...An oak tree?"</p>
<p>"Strong."</p>
<p>"Hard to knock over."</p>
<p>"How about Rowan?"</p>
<p>The boy repeated it several times beneath his breath. "...Rowan."</p>
<p>His eyes gradually brightened. "I’ve..."</p>
<p>"I’ve never had anyone give me a name before."</p>
<p>West smiled. "Then wear it proudly."</p>
<p>Rowan bowed deeply. "I will."</p>
<p>...</p>
<p>Afternoons became unexpectedly enjoyable especially when West occasionally joined them outside.</p>
<p>One afternoon he organized an impromptu football match using an old rubber ball Sammy had found.</p>
<p>West deliberately exaggerated every mistake.</p>
<p>He pretended to trip over absolutely nothing, missed easy passes and even dramatically fell to one knee after Robin lightly bumped his shoulder.</p>
<p>"Oh no..."</p>
<p>"I’ve been defeated..."</p>
<p>"The mighty Robin has conquered me."</p>
<p>Robin covered her mouth while laughing. "I barely touched you!"</p>
<p>"I know."</p>
<p>"I’m very fragile."</p>
<p>Nadia immediately threw the ball at him. "Liar!"</p>
<p>West caught it effortlessly. "Betrayal again."</p>
<p>Even Tom couldn’t suppress a smile.</p>
<p>Sammy leaned against the warehouse entrance watching everything unfold. "...I’m surprised this side of you exists... awakeners are not usually like this..."</p>
<p>West continued juggling the ball. "What side?"</p>
<p>"The normal one."</p>
<p>West laughed. "I was normal before becoming an awakened too."</p>
<p>...</p>
<p>Evenings often ended with everyone gathered together.</p>
<p>Sometimes they watched old movies while sometimes West answered endless questions.</p>
<p>"What was school like?"</p>
<p>"Can awakened really jump across buildings?"</p>
<p>"Is Misty High City nice?"</p>
<p>"Do gangs actually have offices?"</p>
<p>West answered honestly whenever he could and tried to avoid glorifying violence but these kids had already seen plenty... perhaps even more than him.</p>
<p>...</p>
<p>Eventually... several more days passed and West’s return flight approached.</p>
<p>That evening everyone gathered inside the dining hall after dinner.</p>
<p>The atmosphere felt noticeably tense as West stood before them. "I’m heading back tomorrow."</p>
<p>They all stayed silent as he continued.</p>
<p>"So..."</p>
<p>"It’s time."</p>
<p>He folded his arms. "I promised I’d let each of you choose your own future."</p>
<p>"If you want to remain in Orizon..."</p>
<p>"I’ve already arranged placements with reputable foster programs."</p>
<p>"They’ll help you continue school, receive treatment, and build a life here."</p>
<p>"If..."</p>
<p>"You’d rather come back to Misty High City with me..."</p>
<p>"I’ll keep my promise."</p>
