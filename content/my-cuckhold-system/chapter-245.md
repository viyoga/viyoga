<p>It was perfect for battlefield control... or any unexpected situation. Being able to see through the eyes of his summons would definitely be a boon.</p>
<p>West smirked. "Oh yeah... this is mine."</p>
<p>Lastly, he picked something more grounded.</p>
<p>A universal combat art.</p>
<p>Foundation Combat Frame: Apex Form.</p>
<p>It was a baseline enhancement art usable by any awakener.</p>
<p>It refined stance, movement efficiency, strike flow, and reaction timing.</p>
<p>"Can’t always rely on flashy tricks," West stated.</p>
<p>Jax nodded."Smart."</p>
<p>Nina added, "Also makes you look cooler when you win."</p>
<p>West raised a brow. "That’s the real goal."</p>
<p>...</p>
<p>...</p>
<p>They returned to the Shadow Reapers base soon after and this time, they weren’t admiring the battle arts... they were using it.</p>
<p>Located in the inner section of the base, the Combat Development Arena spanned an entire wing.</p>
<p>The flooring was made from reinforced kinetic-dampening alloy, designed to absorb impact from high-tier clashes.</p>
<p>The walls weren’t ordinary... they were responsive surfaces embedded with energy diffusion matrices, preventing stray attacks from tearing the place apart.</p>
<p>Multiple sections divided the space:</p>
<p>• Open Combat Ring — for direct sparring and movement training</p>
<p>• Simulation Chambers — where terrain could shift between forest, ruins, desert, or urban</p>
<p>• Summoner Control Zone — wide space designed for multi-entity coordination</p>
<p>• Construct &amp; Creation Field — perfect for Nina’s abilities</p>
<p>• Elemental Impact Range — where Jax could unleash without restraint</p>
<p>Above it all, observation decks overlooked the entire area, allowing for monitoring, analysis, and future planning.</p>
<p>—</p>
<p>They wasted no time in choosing one art to focus on from the three they each acquired.</p>
<p>Nina took on Phantom Artisan Scripture, Jax chose Frozen Meridian Saber Art while West committed fully to Triad Dominion Method.</p>
<p>—</p>
<p>The first few days were rough, clumsy and a little frustrating.</p>
<p>Progress was slow.</p>
<p>Nina struggled with maintaining construct stability under rapid chaining.</p>
<p>Her drawings flickered and creatures collapsed mid-formation but she adapted quickly, increasing her speed.</p>
<p>By day three, she could manifest layered constructs in under a second.</p>
<p>By day five, she was chaining them.</p>
<p>By day seven... she was smiling while doing it.</p>
<p>—</p>
<p>Jax’s progress was more violent.</p>
<p>The Frozen Meridian Saber Art demanded internal synchronization with his bloodline.</p>
<p>At first, his strikes were powerful but inefficient.</p>
<p>There was too much force and too little flow but he trained relentlessly.</p>
<p>His katana began to turn frosty and his movements became cleaner.</p>
<p>By the end of the week, even when he wasn’t striking, every single movement of his blade left a thin line of ice in the air.</p>
<p>It was like the blade had a domain of ice surrounding it.</p>
<p>...</p>
<p>On the other hand, West’s training was entirely different.</p>
<p>The Triad Dominion Method required coordination beyond physical movement.</p>
<p>It demanded connection, alignment and synchronization.</p>
<p>He practiced hand signs, body positioning and summon coordination again... and again... and again... and again...</p>
<p>At first, nothing happened until something began to change within him.</p>
<p>A strange energy began forming along his spine, coiling upward like something awakening.</p>
<p>By the end of the week, it had completely form as a different source of energy seperate from the awakening branches.</p>
<p>He stood in the Summoner Control Zone, breathing calmly while Gor’thala, Seraphyra and Aurethia stood around him.</p>
<p>He moved through the final sequence of signs, gathering energy and then stopped.</p>
<p>West frowned. "...That’s it?"</p>
<p>He flipped to the final page and was hit with the reminder that this battle art was incomplete.</p>
<p>He exhaled sharply while pacing. "The rest is on the higher floors."</p>
<p>This of course meant it was still out of reach.</p>
<p>He clenched the manual thinking about the best possible way to tackle the situation because he definitely needed to finish this.</p>
<p>As frustration began to build, a familiar chime suddenly rang in his mind.</p>
<p>A system notification appeared before his eyes.</p>
<p>---</p>
<p>[ SYSTEM ALERT ]</p>
<p>Information incomplete...</p>
<p>Scanning...</p>
<p>Accessing complete info...</p>
<p>Complete info accessed.</p>
<p>[ Does Host wish to purchase complete version of: Triad Dominion Method? ]</p>
<p>[ YES ] × [ NO ]</p>
<p>---</p>
<p>West eyebrows rose in disbelief. "...What?"</p>
<p>He stared at the prompt before laughing. "You can do that too?!"</p>
<p>Without hesitation, West picked his answer. "Yes."</p>
<p>---</p>
<p>[ PURCHASE CONFIRMED ]</p>
<p>3000 Cuck Points Deducted</p>
<p>---</p>
<p>A new manual appeared in his grasp which was completely identical to the one he got from the hall.</p>
<p>Except for the addition of one extra page.</p>
<p>Thia was the missing piece.</p>
<p>West’s grin widened. "Now we’re talking."</p>
<p>Although West did wonder which sick bastard thought it was funny to keep 99% of the manual on the first floor and the final 1% on higher floors, he didn’t dwell on it.</p>
<p>He followed the instructions from the last page immediately, step by step until a strange burst of white energy erupted from his body.</p>
<p>Gor’thala, Seraphyra and Aurethia who were seated around him in a circle instantly felt a force increasing their bond.</p>
<p>A triangle of glowing red energy suddenly formed between them with West still at the center.</p>
<p>This energy connected them on a deeper level.</p>
<p>---</p>
<p>[ NEW SKILL LEARNED: Resonance ]</p>
<p>---</p>
<p>West’s eyes widened as he activated it instinctively.</p>
<p>The moment he did, everything changed. He could feel them in a different way... not just command them... he could be them.</p>
<p>His body suddenly transformed as Seraphyra’s biology integrated with his existence.</p>
<p>His body elongated as serpentine like scales appeared all over.</p>
<p>"Woah..." West muttered in disbelief as his body slithered and rose and suddenly he was able to see the world in ways a human never could.</p>
<p>He could feel what it was like to be Seraphyra... there was a powerful thirst for mischief brewing under the surface and something extra... but West didn’t let it linger for long.</p>
<p>He proceeded to return to his human form as his skin emitted a green glow.</p>
<p>Gor’thala’s power flowed through him as he grew to a height of twelve feet and a staff formed in his hand.</p>
<p>Raw magic swept from his being as he stamped the staff onto the ground.</p>
<p>Flowers and vegetation began to bloom across the surroundings.</p>
<p>West couldn’t help but chuckle excitedly at how cool this was. He could feel primal physical power flowing through his muscles which had grown twice in size.</p>
