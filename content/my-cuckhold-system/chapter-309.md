<p>The skeletal dragon descended next with blue flames erupting from its mouth.</p>
<p>The flames swallowed Gor’thala up entirely and the Iron Vultures members celebrated upon seeing that everything up ahead had been engulfed.</p>
<p>Suddenly, a glowing slash emerged from inside the inferno, splitting the flames apart.</p>
<p>However, the flames wasn’t the only thing that was split...</p>
<p>"KUUARRHHHHH!"</p>
<p>The dragon’s scream echoed throughout the battlefield as it’s left wing detached while Gor’thala burst from the flames unharmed with her sword emitting a bright green glow.</p>
<p>All eight fists of the giant ape descended simultaneously towards her direction.</p>
<p>BOOM!</p>
<p>BOOM!</p>
<p>BOOM!</p>
<p>The ground shattered repeatedly while Gor’thala danced through the barrage, evading the strikes.</p>
<p>Her blade flashed across the air and in the next instant, one arm fell...</p>
<p>But it didn’t end there...</p>
<p>Shiiinnnn~ Shiinnn~ Slassshhh~ Shiinn~ Shinnn~</p>
<p>More of the apes arms were severed as Gor’thala served across the battlefield, slashing at the descended fists.</p>
<p>Meanwhile Aurethia was operating on an entirely different level.</p>
<p>She remained completely untouched as tbe floating eye creature unleashed dozens of energy beams.</p>
<p>Aurethia walked through them swinging her saber at such a speed that it was invisible to the naked eye.</p>
<p>Every of her swing, intercepted multiple attacks, splitting the beams apart harmlessly.</p>
<p>The three-headed hound charged with three mouths opened and streams of fire erupted from each of them.</p>
<p>Aurethia vanished and reappeared behind it.</p>
<p>Three silver flashes engulfed the vicinity and in the next instant, three heads rolled across the battlefield.</p>
<p>The hound collapsed completely dead.</p>
<p>The giant mantis attacked, swinging its blades at impossible speeds.</p>
<p>Aurethia met every strike casually.</p>
<p>Clang~</p>
<p>Clang~</p>
<p>Clang~</p>
<p>The exchange lasted barely three seconds before the mantis split into six separate pieces.</p>
<p>The steel construct lasted nearly ten seconds but Aurethia’s saber carved through it eventually causing it to exploded apart.</p>
<p>Iron Vultures summoners began sweating heavily.</p>
<p>Their creatures were being dismantled efficiently. They stood no chance against West summons and it made no sense to them. West was only Tier 1 after all, just like they were.</p>
<p>Through the relentless attacks, Shadow Reapers members supported from the flanks.</p>
<p>Jax froze opponents who tried interfering, Vanya’s shot out glowing rings to wounded enemies, taking them out completely.</p>
<p>Other members launched abilities from safe positions.</p>
<p>West didn’t let any member head to the front line. He wanted the summons to tank all the damage so all Shadow Reapers members remained untouched.</p>
<p>The battlefield grew bloodier, louder and more chaotic.</p>
<p>Entire sections of pavement no longer existed.</p>
<p>The battlefield had completely devolved into carnage.</p>
<p>The neat roads surrounding Shadow Reapers Headquarters no longer resembled roads.</p>
<p>Entire sections had been ripped apart, buildings bore massive cracks, Vehicles burned in twisted heaps of metal and bodies littered the streets.</p>
<p>The ground itself looked as though a natural disaster had struck repeatedly.</p>
<p>In the middle of all that destruction... Bloodstain was rampaging.</p>
<p>The Iron Vultures leader looked less like a human being and more like a force of nature.</p>
<p>His clothes were tattered as blood energy rolled off his body in thick waves.</p>
<p>The crimson aura surrounding him had grown so dense that nearby awakened instinctively avoided standing too close.</p>
<p>Wherever he went, something died.</p>
<p>Another Verdant Pyre Behemoth charged toward him but Bloodstain didn’t dodge.</p>
<p>He simply punched forward and a massive spike of hardened blood erupted from his fist.</p>
<p>The attack pierced straight through the Behemoth’s chest.</p>
<p>The creature roared as Bloodstain grabbed the protrusion with both hands before ripping through.</p>
<p>The summon split apart from the inside with chunks of flesh exploding everywhere.</p>
<p>The massive body collapsed in two separate pieces.</p>
<p>Bloodstain kept moving without even looking back.</p>
<p>The crystal-antlered wolf glowing claws suddenly descended toward his neck.</p>
<p>Shiikkkkkkk~</p>
<p>Just when it was a few inches away from making contact, Bloodstain’s twisted while forming a giant crimson blade instantly.</p>
<p>SLASH!</p>
<p>The wolf’s shoulder exploded and the beast was sent tumbling across the battlefield.</p>
<p>Several Iron Vultures members cheered seeing their leader push back.</p>
<p>He looked unstoppable, like a god of slaughter.</p>
<p>Every step left destruction behind... every attack created another casualty.</p>
<p>The battlefield parted before him as he kept his eyes on a single target.</p>
<p>"WEST EINSTEIN!"</p>
<p>His voice boomed across the battlefield causing several ongoing fights actually paused for a brief moment.</p>
<p>Bloodstain’s fury caused the surrounding to rumble. "I’m coming for you!"</p>
<p>The declaration echoed through the ruined streets as he charged again.</p>
<p>A giant tower-bearer swung its colossal weapon down toward him but Bloodstain leapt, causing the tower smashed into the ground beneath him.</p>
<p>He landed directly on the giant’s arm, ran across it and plunged his hand into its shoulder.</p>
<p>Blood-red spikes erupted outward causing the giant to convulse voilently as its entire upper body exploded apart.</p>
<p>The enormous summon crashed into the battlefield.</p>
<p>Bloodstain emerged from the wreckage covered in splinters and debris.</p>
<p>He continued advancing, still heading toward West, leaving a hurricane of destruction behind him.</p>
<p>Even some Iron Vultures members had started avoiding his path.</p>
<p>Nobody wanted to be caught between Bloodstain and his target.</p>
<p>Meanwhile... since the battle began, West remained exactly where he was with his hands casually inside his pockets, relaxed.</p>
<p>He almost looked bored as screams, eplosions and the collapsing of Gor’thala’s summons rang out around him.</p>
<p>None of it seemed to affect him.</p>
<p>He simply observed and waited, watching Bloodstain tear through everything, forcing his way closer to him.</p>
<p>West smiled genuinely like this had all been expected.</p>
<p>Nearby Shadow Reapers members noticed Bloodstain carving a path through the battlefield to West.</p>
<p>"Uhh..." Jax scratched his cheek. "Boss."</p>
<p>"Yeah?" West muttered.</p>
<p>"I think angry grandpa is heading this way."</p>
<p>"Looks that way."</p>
<p>Jax stared. "Should we maybe stop him?"</p>
<p>Several nearby members immediately agreed, especially the newer recruits. They were eager to show their capabilities.</p>
<p>After all, anyone could see Bloodstain was on a completely different level.</p>
<p>Even while dozens of battles were taking place around them, his presence stood out.</p>
<p>The man was a monster.</p>
<p>Vanya stepped forward with her eyes glowing as energy gathered around her.</p>
<p>"We should intercept him."</p>
<p>Several others nodded in agreement.</p>
<p>"He’ll reach us."</p>
<p>"Let’s help."</p>
<p>"Everyone attack together."</p>
<p>The newer members were nervous since Bloodstain had already destroyed multiple powerful summons but nobody wanted him reaching their gang leader.</p>
<p>West finally sighed before shaking his head. "No."</p>
<p>Everyone paused in place with looks of confusion on their faces.</p>
<p>Jax blinked. "No?"</p>
<p>There was no way West thought he was taking that monster out without help, right?</p>
<p>This was most of them thought process.</p>
<p>But to their utmost shock, West pointed toward the battlefield. "Don’t get in his path."</p>
<p>The group looked confused as they watched Bloodstain had destroy another powerful summon.</p>
<p>The distance between them continued shrinking.</p>
<p>"Boss..." one recruit swallowed nervously. "He’s literally coming straight for you."</p>
<p>West nodded. "I know."</p>
<p>Jax frowned. "You sure about this?"</p>
<p>West’s smile widened. "Very."</p>
<p>Bloodstain’s roar echoed again as he blasted apart another Verdant Pyre Behemoth, causing the battlefield to tremble.</p>
<p>The distance shrank further but West remained unconcerned. "Let him come."</p>
<p>The words were calm, yet hearing them somehow made everyone even more nervous.</p>
<p>Vanya frowned. "Are you planning something?"</p>
<p>West chuckled. "Always."</p>
<p>Jax groaned. "I hate when you say that."</p>
<p>West eyes remained fixed on Bloodstain who had finally broken through the densest portions of the battlefield.</p>
<p>He had gotten close enough that individual features could be seen clearly.</p>
<p>Cuts covered his body, his clothing hung in tatters and blood dripped from multiple wounds, yet somehow... he seemed stronger than when the battle began.</p>
<p>The Iron Vultures members morale rose.</p>
<p>"Boss is almost there!"</p>
<p>"Kill him!"</p>
<p>"Rip him apart!"</p>
<p>The chants spread rapidly while Shadow Reapers members tensed.</p>
<p>Bloodstain displayed a vicious smile because he could finally see West clearly standing calmly in the distance with nothing in between them... waiting.</p>
<p>Bloodstain’s blood aura erupted once more, shattering the ground beneath his feet as he accelerated.</p>
<p>The burst of speed startled everyone watching as he turned into a crimson blur tearing across the battlefield, sraight toward West.</p>
<p>Jax instinctively took a step forward but West raised his hand. With a sigh, he immediately stepped sideways.</p>
<p>For the first time since the battle began, West slowly removed his hands from his pockets because after tearing through an army... Bloodstain had finally reached him.</p>
<p>West turned slightly. "Jax."</p>
<p>Jax immediately looked over. "Yeah?"</p>
<p>"I need a private room."</p>
<p>Jax blinked. "A what?"</p>
<p>West pointed at Bloodstain. "A private room."</p>
<p>The moment understanding dawned, Jax’s eyes widened.</p>
<p>Of course, only three people knew, West was the blue demon, including himself. Not even the new recruits at Shadow Reapers knew.</p>
<p>West had worked too hard keeping that identity hidden and he wasn’t about to expose it now.</p>
<p>Jax immediately grinned. "Ohhh... I got you."</p>
<p>His branch activated causing the temperature surrounding the battlefield to instantly drop.</p>
<p>Frost spread across the ground as massive walls of ice erupted upward.</p>
<p>BOOOOOOOM!</p>
<p>Tall walls nearly sixty feet high rapidly enclosed the area.</p>
<p>The ice was thick and dense, almost in a crystal-like way.</p>
<p>Nobody outside could see inside or outside, it was completely closed off.</p>
<p>The Iron Vultures members immediately reacted.</p>
<p>"What the hell?"</p>
<p>"Why did they block it off?"</p>
<p>"I can’t see!"</p>
