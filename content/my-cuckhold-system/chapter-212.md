<p>She held the phone closer to her face, inspecting.</p>
<p>"He is so hot."</p>
<p>"RIN!"</p>
<p>Mei finally caught up, grabbed her by the arm and snatched the phone back.</p>
<p>Without hesitation, she reached up and pulled Rin’s ear.</p>
<p>"Ow—OW—HEY!"</p>
<p>"Stay out of things that are not your business!"</p>
<p>Rin winced, trying to pry her hand away.</p>
<p>"Okay, okay, damn—!"</p>
<p>Meanwhile, at the head of the table Renzhi had been watching everything unfold quietly.</p>
<p>His brow lifted slightly.</p>
<p>"...A boyfriend?"</p>
<p>The room stilled again as Mei froze.</p>
<p>There was no escaping it now.</p>
<p>She sighed softly while straightening up. "...Yeah."</p>
<p>"Don’t make a big deal out of it, Dad. I’m over eighteen."</p>
<p>Renzhi opened his mouth but before he could speak, Lianhua’s gaze met his.</p>
<p>Without saying a word, she passed a silent message that said be gentle.</p>
<p>Renzhi was known for being hotheaded but Lianhua had always been able to put him on a leash. He was madly in love with his wife so he would never go against her wishes.</p>
<p>Renzhi exhaled lightly while leaning back.</p>
<p>"...Alright."</p>
<p>His tone softened. "You are eighteen... but you still need to be careful."</p>
<p>Mei crossed her arms slightly.</p>
<p>"I know," she stated before adding quickly—</p>
<p>"He’s not some random guy."</p>
<p>Renzhi’s eyes narrowed slightly.</p>
<p>"Oh?"</p>
<p>"He’s Mom’s personal fitness coach."</p>
<p>This revelation caught his attention causing Renzhi to slowly turn to Lianhua.</p>
<p>"...That true?"</p>
<p>Lianhua nodded calmly.</p>
<p>"Yes."</p>
<p>"And he’s good at what he does."</p>
<p>Renzhi blinked.</p>
<p>"...Why am I the last one hearing about this?"</p>
<p>Lianhua gave him a small look.</p>
<p>"Because you’d make a fuss out of it."</p>
<p>Renzhi opened his mouth before closing it again.</p>
<p>"Fair."</p>
<p>He leaned back slightly, processing.</p>
<p>"...Hmm... well... in that case..."</p>
<p>He glanced at Mei.</p>
<p>"At least invite him over."</p>
<p>Mei frowned immediately.</p>
<p>"...Why?"</p>
<p>"To meet him properly," Renzhi said simply. "Build a relationship."</p>
<p>Mei raised an eyebrow.</p>
<p>"Or interrogate him like the others?"</p>
<p>Renzhi waved his hand quickly.</p>
<p>"No, no—nothing like that."</p>
<p>He leaned forward slightly with a sincere look. "I promise."</p>
<p>Mei studied him with a suspicious look. "...You said that last time and then scared the other one away with your awakened power."</p>
<p>"That was different."</p>
<p>"How?"</p>
<p>Renzhi hesitated.</p>
<p>"...He deserved it."</p>
<p>Rin snorted from the side while Eric smirked slightly.</p>
<p>"Typical dad."</p>
<p>Lianhua sighed softly and Mei shook her head.</p>
<p>"You’re not doing that again."</p>
<p>"I won’t."</p>
<p>"I swear."</p>
<p>Mei stared at him for a few seconds longer before groaning. "...Fine."</p>
<p>"But if you start anything—"</p>
<p>"I won’t."</p>
<p>She pointed at him.</p>
<p>"I mean it."</p>
<p>"I know."</p>
<p>She leaned back into her seat while crossing her arms.</p>
<p>"...I’ll invite him."</p>
<p>Rin immediately perked up again.</p>
<p>"Ooooh! Dinner with the hot boyfriend—this is going to be interesting."</p>
<p>"Rin."</p>
<p>"Okay, okay."</p>
<p>However, her grin didn’t fade and across the table, Renzhi leaned back slowly with a thoughtful look.</p>
<p>~ Thursday~</p>
<p>West stretched his body, rolling his shoulders as he stood in front of the mirror in clean fitness outfit.</p>
<p>He was heading to Renzhi’s residence again ad this time, he already knew what he intended to do...</p>
<p>West smirked slightly.</p>
<p>"...Finally, time to complete this one month quest."</p>
<p>He grabbed his phone and suddenly paused with his brows furrowing.</p>
<p>There were more notifications from the same unknown number.</p>
<p>~ Son, please help me ~</p>
<p>~ They’re after me ~</p>
<p>~ You’re the only one who can ~</p>
<p>~ Please... ~</p>
<p>~ I don’t have much time ~</p>
<p>~ Son ~</p>
<p>~ Please answer me ~</p>
<p>West stared at the screen as his playful mood from earlier vanished.</p>
<p>His expression hardened slightly at the persistence of this so called prank.</p>
<p>His fingers hovered over the screen as he typed.</p>
<p>"...Who the hell is this?"</p>
<p>He had already confirmed his father was fine so why "son"?</p>
<p>West’s eyes narrowed but he didn’t press send immediately.</p>
<p>His thumb hovered briefly before he finally tapped send.</p>
<p>A reply came almost instantly.</p>
<p>~ Your mother ~</p>
<p>For a moment, West froze in place unmoving.</p>
<p>Something complicated and buried deep beneath years of indifference, resentment... and quiet longing he had long since trained himself to ignore, flickered across his face.</p>
<p>"...Yeah right."</p>
<p>His fingers moved again as he typed faster this time.</p>
<p>~ Don’t ever message this number again. ~</p>
<p>After tapping send, he block it instantly.</p>
<p>West exhaled slowly, tossing the phone onto the table as if it had suddenly become meaningless.</p>
<p>"There’s no way..."</p>
<p>His voice was low and almost dismissive.</p>
<p>His mother? After all these years?</p>
<p>That woman had walked out of his life without a single glance back, a call, a message or even a whisper of concern...</p>
<p>He had waited for so long but that version of him didn’t exist anymore.</p>
<p>"...Prankster."</p>
<p>That was the only explanation and to him it made sense.</p>
<p>—</p>
<p>West arrived at Renzhi’s residence about forty minutes later and the gates opened smoothly.</p>
<p>As expected, Mei was at work while Rin and Eric were at school.</p>
<p>Renzhi was away at the Dragon Fang base which left just one person at home as usual, if one didn’t count the silent, ever-present maids who moved like shadows through the mansion.</p>
<p>West stepped inside and a maid bowed slightly.</p>
<p>"Madam is waiting in the fitness room."</p>
<p>West nodded with a smile and went ahead.</p>
<p>—</p>
<p>The personal fitness room as usual was expansive and filled with high-end equipment that looked barely used... until recently.</p>
<p>Lianhua stood near the center waiting but today, she wasn’t dressed like usual.</p>
<p>West paused for half a second as he stepped in because she was dressed in a much tighter and revealing outfit.</p>
<p>Short athletic shorts hugged her lower body perfectly, leaving her toned legs and smooth, fair skin completely exposed. Her top clung closely to her figure, outlining every natural curve in a way that felt... intentional.</p>
<p>West’s gaze lingered for just a fraction longer than usual.</p>
<p>"...Damn."</p>
<p>The words slipped out of his mouth without resistance.</p>
<p>’Asians are a blessing from God...’ he voiced internally.</p>
<p>Lianhua noticed his lingering gaze and a satisfied smile bloomed across her face.</p>
<p>"Something wrong?" she questioned with a light tone.</p>
