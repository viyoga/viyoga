<p>West’s expression shifted again becoming more focused.</p>
<p>He closed his eyes and reached inward to the second untethered branch...</p>
<p>The one he had yet to contract.</p>
<p>Energy spread quietly beneath his skin as the world around him dissolved.</p>
<p>Jax watched as West’s body went still, standing upright with his eyes closed.</p>
<p>"...Okay that’s mildly terrifying."</p>
<p>Inside his consciousness—</p>
<p>West drifted once more into that strange realm.</p>
<p>Infinite space.</p>
<p>Dark yet luminous.</p>
<p>And floating before him—</p>
<p>Were hundreds of thousands of doors, bearing different shapes, colors and size.</p>
<p>West moved forward slowly, studying them carefully.</p>
<p>This choice mattered.</p>
<p>His second entity would define the balance of his arsenal.</p>
<p>Gor’thala was magic-heavy... elegant and dominant.</p>
<p>Perhaps this time...</p>
<p>He needed something different.</p>
<p>His hand hovered over a crimson door etched with spiky patterns... then he moved away.</p>
<p>A smooth silver door throbbed coldly... he ignored and drifted further... spotting a deep violet door that radiated quiet menace.</p>
<p>’Interesting... but not yet.’</p>
<p>Finally, he stopped before a door unlike the others.</p>
<p>It was matte black with no carvings or glow...</p>
<p>Just... stillness.</p>
<p>It didn’t call to him loudly or flash... it simply waited.</p>
<p>West stared at it and smiled faintly.</p>
<p>"I choose this one."</p>
<p>Outside in the real world—</p>
<p>Jax stood nervously.</p>
<p>"...West?"</p>
<p>The air around West began to distort slightly as powered gathered.</p>
<p>West’s consciousness snapped back into his body like a wire reconnecting to a live circuit as his eyes flew open.</p>
<p>"Gor’thala," he said immediately, "be ready."</p>
<p>The elegant entity behind him straightened with her aura tightening like drawn silk.</p>
<p>"You anticipate hostility, Master?"</p>
<p>"Yes."</p>
<p>West exhaled slowly.</p>
<p>"I’m taking the easy way out this time. Instead of fighting all by myself..."</p>
<p>The ground beneath them rippled.</p>
<p>The matte-black door he had chosen in the realm of doors had already opened and something had answered.</p>
<p>The summoning circle manifested beneath West’s feet causing dark sigils to spread outward like ink in water.</p>
<p>Jax, who was still standing nearby holding the awakening voucher, blinked.</p>
<p>"...Okay why does this feel way scarier than the train?"</p>
<p>A low, dragging sound echoed across the empty lot like scales scraping stone...</p>
<p>And then a massive serpentine body coiled outward in a slow, languid motion.</p>
<p>Ash-black scales shimmered under the fading evening light, edged with a faint metallic sheen. The body stretched at least thirty feet long with thickness, easily as wide as the torsos of two grown men side by side.</p>
<p>But it was her upper half that commanded attention.</p>
<p>Where the serpentine body rose, it transitioned seamlessly into the torso of a woman.</p>
<p>Her skin was pale with a faint gray undertone, smooth as carved marble. Dark curved horns arched elegantly from her temples, sweeping back like a crown. Long midnight hair cascaded down her back, contrasting sharply against the muted ash of her scales.</p>
<p>Her eyes glowed faintly gold... and they were bored.</p>
<p>The System interface flickered before West.</p>
<p>&gt; [ Summoned Entity: Seraphyra, the Dusk Serpent Sovereign ]</p>
<p>She looked around lazily with her tail coiling in a slow spiral.</p>
<p>Then her glowing eyes settled on West.</p>
<p>"Are you the one responsible for this, boy?"</p>
<p>Her voice was rich, velvet and laced with amused disdain.</p>
<p>Jax immediately let out a long whistle.</p>
<p>"Okay listen—if she wasn’t a thirty-foot snake, I’d definitely—"</p>
<p>Seraphyra didn’t even turn her head fully.</p>
<p>"Quiet, lesser being."</p>
<p>Her eyes flared and for a split second, golden light emanated from them.</p>
<p>Jax froze mid-sentence as his body stiffened.</p>
<p>Gray spread over his skin like frost crawling across glass and within seconds, he became a perfectly sculpted stone statue... mid-whistle...</p>
<p>West’s eyes darkened instantly.</p>
<p>"What did you do?"</p>
<p>Seraphyra examined her nails casually.</p>
<p>"I merely used Medusa Gaze on the lowlife."</p>
<p>West’s eyes turned fierce as the ground beneath his feet cracked faintly in preparation to attack.</p>
<p>Gor’thala stepped forward with magic gathering in her palm.</p>
<p>Seraphyra’s tail paused mid-coil and then she smiled.</p>
<p>"Oh relax. I will sign the contract."</p>
<p>West blinked.</p>
<p>"...You will?"</p>
<p>He had prepared himself for battle... so this was an unexpected turn of events.</p>
<p>She shrugged.</p>
<p>"I find this plane... mildly interesting. It has been some time since I’ve walked freely. I only have one condition..."</p>
<p>West narrowed his eyes.</p>
<p>"What’s the condition?"</p>
<p>Her gaze sharpened as a playful glint appeared in her eyes.</p>
<p>"Promise me that as my master, you will allow me to enjoy myself here."</p>
<p>Her tone softened, almost teasing.</p>
<p>"I wish to indulge. To roam. To taste the freedom of this realm. So long as I serve you, I will not be caged."</p>
<p>West studied her carefully.</p>
<p>"And what does ’enjoy’ entail?"</p>
<p>Her lips curved.</p>
<p>"Existing as I please. Exploring. Amusing myself. So long as I do not act against you."</p>
<p>Gor’thala’s voice cut in coolly.</p>
<p>"She seeks autonomy within the bounds of servitude."</p>
<p>Seraphyra’s golden eyes flicked toward Gor’thala.</p>
<p>"A perceptive one."</p>
<p>West considered for a moment...</p>
<p>He needed strength not chaos but so long as she was promising servitude... It was worth considering.</p>
<p>"As long as you do not step out of line," he said evenly, "and you obey when commanded, you may conduct yourself freely."</p>
<p>Her smile widened.</p>
<p>"Delightful."</p>
<p>The contract circle flared brighter as dark threads of energy wrapped around them both.</p>
<p>They both sealed the agreement with their blood and the system flashed...</p>
<p>&gt; Contract Established. Mutual Boons Granted.</p>
<p>West felt power infiltrate him alongside knowledge.</p>
<p>A catalogue of her abilities unfolded in his mind.</p>
<p>• Medusa Gaze.</p>
<p>• Poison Mist.</p>
<p>• Diamond Scales.</p>
<p>• Needle Breath.</p>
<p>• Size Magnification.</p>
<p>West’s eyes widened in astonishment.</p>
<p>Her combat potential was monstrous.</p>
<p>A new notification soon appeared in his line of sight.</p>
<p>&gt; You have acquired ability: Poison Mist.</p>
<p>West felt something shift in his lungs, opening a core that didn’t exist a while ago Now venomous vapor existed within him, to be used at his command.</p>
<p>Simultaneously, Seraphyra stiffened.</p>
<p>Her eyes widened as new notifications appeared before her.</p>
<p>&gt; +60% Boost to All Abilities</p>
<p>New Ability: Shadow Anchor</p>
<p>Humanoid Shift</p>
<p>The golden glow in her eyes flickered violently.</p>
<p>"...What?"</p>
<p>She looked at West again.</p>
<p>Then at the notifications.</p>
<p>Then back at him.</p>
<p>"Sixty percent?"</p>
<p>Her voice lost its lazy tone entirely.</p>
<p>"That magnitude of amplification... that is divine-tier enhancement."</p>
<p>Her serpentine body trembled slightly.</p>
<p>"How is this possible?"</p>
