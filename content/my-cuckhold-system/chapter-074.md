<p>Dragon Fang wasn’t just powerful...</p>
<p>They were elite.</p>
<p>Their members were feared citywide.</p>
<p>To be personally taken as apprentice by Zu Li herself—</p>
<p>That was beyond rare.</p>
<p>That was destiny-level opportunity.</p>
<p>Even the Iron Chain members looked shaken but Raze didn’t step back.</p>
<p>If anything, his resolve hardened.</p>
<p>If someone like Zu Li wanted West...</p>
<p>Then West’s value was even greater than they’d imagined.</p>
<p>"You can’t have him," Raze said firmly and all eyes turned to him.</p>
<p>Raze met Zu Li’s gaze directly.</p>
<p>"The Iron Chain Gang already has claim on him."</p>
<p>Zu Li raised an eyebrow.</p>
<p>"Oh?"</p>
<p>Her tone carried faint amusement.</p>
<p>"And when," she asked calmly, "did he agree to belong to you?"</p>
<p>Raze didn’t answer immediately because he couldn’t.</p>
<p>West hadn’t agreed.</p>
<p>But before the tension could escalate—</p>
<p>Another voice rang out.</p>
<p>"Well, well... looks like I came at the right time."</p>
<p>Everyone turned again.</p>
<p>This time it was Ross approaching.</p>
<p>He approached with several Black Furnace gang members behind him, their dark uniforms unmistakable.</p>
<p>Ross had originally come for a completely different reason.</p>
<p>He wanted to talk to West about Aria.</p>
<p>He wanted to ask West for advice because she had been avoiding him and acting distant.</p>
<p>Since West was supposed to be her brother, he believed a few tips could point him in the right direction.</p>
<p>However, when he arrived and saw the destruction...</p>
<p>Saw the unconscious awakened...</p>
<p>Saw Gor’thala...</p>
<p>Saw Zu Li...</p>
<p>He understood immediately.</p>
<p>West was valuable...</p>
<p>Extremely valuable.</p>
<p>Ross stepped forward confidently.</p>
<p>"I think," Ross said smoothly, "West will prefer to join family."</p>
<p>He looked at West.</p>
<p>"You are Aria’s brother after all."</p>
<p>He spread his arms slightly.</p>
<p>"You belong with Black Furnace."</p>
<p>The crowd lost its mind.</p>
<p>"BLACK FURNACE TOO?!"</p>
<p>"THREE GANGS?!"</p>
<p>"THIS IS INSANE!"</p>
<p>Iron Chain.</p>
<p>Dragon Fang.</p>
<p>Black Furnace.</p>
<p>Three gangs.</p>
<p>All standing there.</p>
<p>All claiming him.</p>
<p>All wanting him.</p>
<p>West stood there silently, watching three gangs argue over him like he was property.</p>
<p>All he wanted to do was vanish and get to where he was headed but he hadn’t found an opening yet.</p>
<p>Raze spoke first.</p>
<p>"Iron Chain discovered him first."</p>
<p>Ross scoffed.</p>
<p>"He’s family."</p>
<p>Zu Li didn’t raise her voice but when she spoke—</p>
<p>They both went quiet.</p>
<p>"He is mine," she said simply.</p>
<p>They both gritted their teeth... she was obviously way too powerful for them to contend with but the decision laid with West.</p>
<p>For a moment, no one spoke.</p>
<p>Raze from Iron Chain crossed his arms. Ross from Black Furnace adjusted his collar, trying to maintain composure. Zu Li stood with effortless authority, her calm gaze never leaving West.</p>
<p>And then, almost simultaneously—</p>
<p>They all spoke.</p>
<p>"Make your decision," Raze said firmly.</p>
<p>"You belong with us," Ross added smoothly.</p>
<p>Zu Li said nothing at first but her eyes alone carried the weight of expectation.</p>
<p>The pressure was suffocating.</p>
<p>Every eye in the courtyard turned toward West.</p>
<p>Students.</p>
<p>Gang members.</p>
<p>Bystanders.</p>
<p>Everyone waited.</p>
<p>Seconds passed.</p>
<p>Five seconds.</p>
<p>Ten seconds.</p>
<p>Fifteen seconds.</p>
<p>No response.</p>
<p>Zu Li frowned slightly.</p>
<p>Something was wrong.</p>
<p>Her instincts sharpened instantly as she turned around to look closer.</p>
<p>And then she realized, West wasn’t there.</p>
<p>Her eyes narrowed.</p>
<p>"...He’s gone."</p>
<p>"What?" Ross snapped while turning around.</p>
<p>Raze spun sharply too and sure enough—</p>
<p>West Einstein had vanished.</p>
<p>The exact spot where he had been standing was empty.</p>
<p>The crowd erupted instantly.</p>
<p>"WHERE DID HE GO?!"</p>
<p>"HE JUST DISAPPEARED?!"</p>
<p>"NO WAY!"</p>
<p>Raze cursed under his breath.</p>
<p>"Find him!"</p>
<p>Ross immediately barked at his men.</p>
<p>"Spread out!"</p>
<p>Zu Li didn’t shout.</p>
<p>She simply turned and then, moved.</p>
<p>—</p>
<p>Several blocks away.</p>
<p>West held tightly as wind roared past his ears.</p>
<p>He was on Gor’thala’s back and she was moving incredibly fast.</p>
<p>Her massive legs carried her across rooftops and streets with effortless power. Each step covered enormous distance and she wasn’t even using magic.</p>
<p>This was her natural speed.</p>
<p>West leaned low, gripping her shoulders tightly.</p>
<p>"Go, go, go!" he muttered.</p>
<p>Behind them, distant shouts echoed.</p>
<p>"They’re escaping!"</p>
<p>"DON’T LET THEM GET AWAY!"</p>
<p>Gor’thala didn’t look back.</p>
<p>"Master," she said calmly, "they pursue."</p>
<p>"I know," West replied.</p>
<p>He glanced behind them briefly.</p>
<p>Vehicles were already starting to appear in the distance.</p>
<p>Gang vehicles.</p>
<p>Fast ones.</p>
<p>Zu Li herself wasn’t using vehicles.</p>
<p>She was leaping from building to building.</p>
<p>Each leap carrying her across entire streets like gravity meant nothing to her.</p>
<p>West swallowed... she was too fast.</p>
<p>"Gor’thala," he said.</p>
<p>She nodded slightly.</p>
<p>"I understand."</p>
<p>Her body leaned forward slightly.</p>
<p>Magic gathered around her legs as faint green aura flared.</p>
<p>"Charge."</p>
<p>BOOM.</p>
<p>The world blurred as she accelerated instantaneously.</p>
<p>The ground shattered behind her as she launched forward like a missile, crossing entire city blocks in seconds.</p>
<p>Wind screamed past them violently as buildings became streaks of color.</p>
<p>West’s eyes widened.</p>
<p>"Holy—"</p>
<p>He barely finished the thought before they had already crossed several kilometers.</p>
<p>Behind them, the gang vehicles had only just begun to move.</p>
<p>Zu Li stopped mid-leap, landing on a rooftop.</p>
<p>Her eyes narrowed.</p>
<p>"...Acceleration skill," she murmured.</p>
<p>She watched the empty horizon.</p>
<p>West was gone... completely gone.</p>
<p>Her lips curved slightly.</p>
<p>"...Interesting."</p>
<p>—</p>
<p>~ Thirty minutes earlier ~</p>
<p>Aria stood outside West’s apartment door with her finger hovering over the doorbell again.</p>
<p>She pressed it.</p>
<p>Ding dong.</p>
<p>Silence.</p>
<p>She frowned.</p>
<p>"...He should be back by now."</p>
<p>She stepped back slightly, folding her arms.</p>
<p>She wore a tight cream-colored crop top that hugged her body perfectly, leaving a small strip of her smooth midriff exposed and shorts. Despite, the casualness of her dressing, she still looked incredibly beautiful and hot with her pink long hair swaying gently.</p>
<p>Her heart beat faster than usual...</p>
<p>West had been running through her mind all morning... She had anticipated it today long before it ever came... imagined it...</p>
<p>And now—</p>
<p>He wasn’t here.</p>
<p>She pulled out her phone and called him.</p>
<p>Ring.</p>
<p>Ring.</p>
<p>Ring.</p>
<p>No answer.</p>
<p>She sighed.</p>
<p>"Where are you..."</p>
<p>She leaned against the wall beside his door.</p>
<p>Five minutes passed...</p>
<p>Ten minutes...</p>
<p>Fifteen minutes...</p>
<p>Her excitement slowly began to fade, replaced by disappointment.</p>
<p>"...Did he forget?"</p>
<p>Twenty minutes...</p>
<p>Twenty-five minutes...</p>
<p>Thirty minutes....</p>
<p>She stared at her phone.</p>
<p>Still nothing.</p>
<p>Her chest tightened slightly.</p>
<p>"...He wouldn’t do that..."</p>
<p>Would he?</p>
<p>She exhaled slowly.</p>
<p>"...Maybe something came up."</p>
<p>She pushed herself off the wall.</p>
<p>"...I’ll just go—"</p>
