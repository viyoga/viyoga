<p>The guard looked down at the floor... there was nothing... No hole or crack or damage...</p>
<p>"Sir, there’s nothing—"</p>
<p>West on the other hand, didn’t say a word... he simply stepped toward the exact spot Mr. Ibe had pointed at.</p>
<p>He crouched slightly and placed his palm flat against the floor.</p>
<p>His eyebrows furrowed slightly as he felt something...</p>
<p>Something shifting beneath layers of rock and earth...</p>
<p>Cold radiated upward through the stone into his skin...</p>
<p>West stood abruptly.</p>
<p>"Stay back," he ordered sharply.</p>
<p>The authority in his tone made both the guard and Mr. Ibe instinctively retreat several steps.</p>
<p>West clenched his fist and punched.</p>
<p>Boom!</p>
<p>The impact cracked outward with a thunderous boom that echoed violently through the chamber.</p>
<p>The ground fractured slightly but did not cave.</p>
<p>He punched again...</p>
<p>And again...</p>
<p>Each strike sent shockwaves through the surroundings, stirring debris and loose dust into the air.</p>
<p>The ground vibrated continously but remained intact... like a sealed door without a visible handle.</p>
<p>West’s stood to his feet and called out...</p>
<p>"Gor’thala."</p>
<p>A green flare ignited beside him...</p>
<p>The air distorted as a ten-foot-tall figure materialized.</p>
<p>Gor’thala bowed slightly as her massive greenish frame radiated restrained power.</p>
<p>"Master."</p>
<p>"Attack the ground," West commanded.</p>
<p>She didn’t question it.</p>
<p>Arcane energy gathered in her hands and she slammed it downward.</p>
<p>A wave of destructive force crashed into the stone.</p>
<p>The chamber shook violently as cracks spiderwebbed outward but once again, the floor did not collapse.</p>
<p>She attacked repeatedly, layering spells atop spells...</p>
<p>Impact after impact...</p>
<p>Yet the surface refused to give way.</p>
<p>It was like striking the lid of something designed to only open from within.</p>
<p>West’s eyes narrowed upon realizing that brute force wouldn’t work.</p>
<p>He began scanning the area again with his gaze drifting from place to place as he touched everything in sight...</p>
<p>He unknowningly leaned againt the cracked statue for balance and then suddenly, the crack deepened...</p>
<p>A faint sound echoed as stone began to split...</p>
<p>West immediately stepped back.</p>
<p>The fissure along the statue extended downward, crawling toward the base and reaching the ground.</p>
<p>The floor suddenly began to split.</p>
<p>A thin curvy line formed at the base of the statue and raced across the stone beneath West’s feet.</p>
<p>"West!" Mr. Ibe shouted.</p>
<p>"Stay back!" West commanded without looking away.</p>
<p>The crack widened and almost instantaneously, a gap formed.</p>
<p>Moments later, elongated and slightly distorted hands began to emerge, phasing partially through the stone.</p>
<p>They reached upward, clawing at West’s figure.</p>
<p>Gor’thala reacted instantly and fired Arcane blasts from her palms, striking the limbs.</p>
<p>The hands splattered apart into dark, viscous fragments upon impact.</p>
<p>But as they disintegrated, the crack began closing again.</p>
<p>The ground sealing itself, almost as though, it was about to reshape everything back to the way it was like nothing had happened here.</p>
<p>"Gor’thala!" West barked.</p>
<p>Without hesitation, she thrust both massive hands into the narrowing gap.</p>
<p>Her fingers wedged between the splitting stone and then she pulled.</p>
<p>The crack resisted, trying to force shut but Gor’thala’s brute strength was out of this world...</p>
<p>The ground trembled and creaked continously, before widening as she pulled the crack apart.</p>
<p>More hands pushed upward, attempting to force it closed.</p>
<p>"Seraphyra," West immediately called out.</p>
<p>A surge of blue-white energy spiraled beside him.</p>
<p>A massive serpentine form manifested, elegant yet deadly.</p>
<p>Seraphyra’s luminous scales shimmered in the dim chamber light and without instruction, she understood.</p>
<p>Her long, powerful body slid forward, forcing itself between the widening fissure.</p>
<p>She braced against both sides and pushed.</p>
<p>The gap widened further, reaching ten feet in width and then more.</p>
<p>A rush of freezing air burst upward from below.</p>
<p>The cold was so intense that it felt unnatural.</p>
<p>A blue glow from beneath illuminated the chamber walls in ghostly light.</p>
<p>West stepped toward the edge.</p>
<p>He turned briefly toward Mr. Ibe and the guard.</p>
<p>"Stay here."</p>
<p>They could only nod, stunned beyond speech.</p>
<p>Without hesitation, West jumped.</p>
<p>Gor’thala and Seraphyra followed after him instantly, dropping into the abyss as well.</p>
<p>The moment they vanished below, the gap sealed shut.</p>
<p>Darkness swallowed them and for several seconds, they fell.</p>
<p>Cold intensified rapidly as the air grew thinner...</p>
<p>The blue glow beneath expanded, revealing depth far greater than expected.</p>
<p>The fall was at least a hundred feet down... maybe more due to how long they had been dropping...</p>
<p>Wind howled upward through the shaft and Seraphyra reacted immediately by coiling her serpentine body around West mid-air, cushioning him within her embrace.</p>
<p>"Master... I’ve got you," she winked at West with a playful expression despite the intensity of the situation.</p>
<p>Gor’thala descended faster than them with her body angled downward like a living projectile.</p>
<p>Boom!</p>
<p>A loud booming sound rang out as she landed with one knee pressed against the ground.</p>
<p>The ground beneath her cracked slightly and for a moment she didn’t move.</p>
<p>Seraphyra absorbed West’s landing entirely, lowering him gently to the surface before uncoiling.</p>
<p>West straightened slowly and looked around.</p>
<p>The sight before him was impossible to describe...</p>
<p>This was like being in another world...</p>
<p>"So the Eternal Ruins has more than just two layers... up to a century and no one ever discovered the third..." West mumbled in disbelief.</p>
<p>The cavern stretched outward beyond visible limits.</p>
<p>The ceiling soared high above, vanishing into a blue mist that glowed faintly like an artificial sky.</p>
<p>Everything was tinted in shades of blue.</p>
<p>The ground beneath them wasn’t normal stone... it was sculpted ice-like terrain but not frozen water... more like something crystalline.</p>
<p>Translucent pathways curved and twisted like rivers of hardened light.</p>
<p>Strange hills rose in uneven clusters with rough gliterring surfaces and at the peaks of these hills... bue flames burned.</p>
<p>Yet, they didn’t melt the terrain.</p>
<p>Rock formations surrounded the landscape, dark and angular and between them laid dozens of pits.</p>
<p>Each pit descended into deeper darkness and at the bottom of each, bue fire raged...</p>
<p>It would occasionally burst forth in waves, illuminated skeletal shapes along the edges with its glow.</p>
