<p>After school had ended for the day, West and Jax met back at work once again.</p>
<p>The café glowed warmly under amber lighting as the scent of roasted coffee beans blended with baked pastries and sweet cream. It was busy but manageable.</p>
<p>Jax leaned against the counter while wiping a glass.</p>
<p>"So," he muttered under his breath, "we’ve got school from eight to three, work from four to nine. That leaves... what? Late night training?"</p>
<p>West poured a latte with steady hands.</p>
<p>"We can’t train in open areas often," he replied quietly. "Industrial zones after closing hours. Weekends will be primary sessions."</p>
<p>"And mornings?"</p>
<p>West glanced at him.</p>
<p>"You want to wake up at five?"</p>
<p>"...I’ll consider it," Jax muttered.</p>
<p>They continued planning between orders.</p>
<p>Training cycles... mastery drills... branch stabilization... physical conditioning.</p>
<p>They needed structure...</p>
<p>Mina, who was restocking pastries nearby, narrowed her eyes at them.</p>
<p>"You two have been whispering all day," she said suspiciously. "What are you plotting?"</p>
<p>"Homework," Jax answered instantly.</p>
<p>"Group study," West added smoothly.</p>
<p>Mina crossed her arms.</p>
<p>"You hate homework."</p>
<p>Jax grinned.</p>
<p>"Growth arc."</p>
<p>She squinted at them, clearly unconvinced, but another customer called her attention and she walked off.</p>
<p>The moment she left, Jax leaned closer.</p>
<p>"She’s definitely suspicious."</p>
<p>"Then act normal."</p>
<p>"I am normal."</p>
<p>"You are not."</p>
<p>Before Jax could protest, the bell above the café door chimed.</p>
<p>Both of them instinctively looked up.</p>
<p>And Jax immediately whistled under his breath.</p>
<p>"Well damn."</p>
<p>A beauty had stepped in...</p>
<p>She had pink hair that extended down to her mid-back, catching the café lights with a glossy sheen. She wore a cropped black leather jacket over a fitted white top that hugged her figure, paired with a high-waisted skirt that accentuated her long legs. The skirt ended just above the knee, and smooth heeled boots added a subtle click to each step she took.</p>
<p>Silver rings glinted on her fingers and a delicate chain rested at her collarbone.</p>
<p>She looked effortlessly stunning and hot with her large chest bouncing every time she stepped forward.</p>
<p>Her eyes scanned the café and then landed on West.</p>
<p>A slow smile curved her lips.</p>
<p>Jax leaned toward West.</p>
<p>"Bro."</p>
<p>"I see her."</p>
<p>"She’s staring like she’s about to commit a felony."</p>
<p>West ignored him and focused on his register.</p>
<p>Aria approached the counter with her hips swaying subtly with each step. When she reached him, she leaned forward slightly, resting her elbows on the counter.</p>
<p>"Hi," she said softly.</p>
<p>"Welcome back," West replied with a smooth and composed voice.</p>
<p>Her gaze flicked over him.</p>
<p>"You missed me?"</p>
<p>West didn’t hesitate.</p>
<p>"You’ve been gone long enough to make the café noticeably less interesting."</p>
<p>Her lips parted slightly in amusement.</p>
<p>"Oh? So I’m the highlight of your day?"</p>
<p>"Among other things."</p>
<p>Jax coughed loudly from behind.</p>
<p>"Do you want to order?" West asked calmly.</p>
<p>Aria didn’t look away from him.</p>
<p>"Surprise me."</p>
<p>West tilted his head slightly.</p>
<p>"Dangerous."</p>
<p>"I like dangerous."</p>
<p>He turned to prepare her drink.</p>
<p>Throughout the time she stayed, Aria’s eyes rarely left him.</p>
<p>Whenever he walked past her table, she’d brush her fingers lightly against his wrist. When he set her drink down, she’d hold his hand a second longer than necessary.</p>
<p>And every time, West responded with flirtatious remarks.</p>
<p>"You look busy," she teased once.</p>
<p>"I make time," he replied.</p>
<p>"For customers?"</p>
<p>"For the right ones."</p>
<p>She bit her lip.</p>
<p>Jax shook his head while watching from the counter.</p>
<p>"This is criminal."</p>
<p>Mina rolled her eyes.</p>
<p>"Focus on the orders."</p>
<p>The evening eventually wound down.</p>
<p>Chairs were stacked... lights dimmed... and floors were mopped.</p>
<p>When they locked up, Jax stretched.</p>
<p>"So. Training?"</p>
<p>"Tomorrow," West replied.</p>
<p>Jax glanced toward Aria, who was waiting outside by the streetlight.</p>
<p>"Ah. Right. Personal development."</p>
<p>West shot him a look.</p>
<p>"Go home."</p>
<p>Jax grinned.</p>
<p>"Don’t do anything I wouldn’t do."</p>
<p>"That leaves too much room."</p>
<p>Jax laughed and left.</p>
<p>West stepped outside and Aria immediately linked her arm with his.</p>
<p>"You’re free now?"</p>
<p>"For a while."</p>
<p>They walked side by side through the quiet evening streets.</p>
<p>The city air was cool and streetlights cast soft golden halos along the pavement.</p>
<p>Their conversation flowed easily.</p>
<p>She talked about her day.... about a small argument she’d had at home... and about how bored she’d been.</p>
<p>"You really didn’t stop thinking about me?" West asked lightly.</p>
<p>She glanced at him sideways.</p>
<p>"You think you’re that unforgettable?"</p>
<p>"I don’t think," he replied. "I observe."</p>
<p>"And what do you observe?"</p>
<p>"That you’re holding my arm tighter than necessary."</p>
<p>She smirked but didn’t let go.</p>
<p>They reached his apartment complex sooner than expected.</p>
<p>As they climbed the stairs, the atmosphere changed.</p>
<p>When they stopped at his door, Aria turned toward him.</p>
<p>For a split second, neither spoke while West stepped closer...</p>
<p>Close enough that her back nearly brushed the door.</p>
<p>His hand slid around her waist and her breath hitched slightly.</p>
<p>"Someone might see," she whispered.</p>
<p>"There’s no one here."</p>
<p>"That’s not the point."</p>
<p>Yet she didn’t step away.</p>
<p>He leaned in, brushing his lips on the curve of her neck.</p>
<p>Her fingers tightened around his shirt as a soft shudder ran through her.</p>
<p>"West..."</p>
<p>Her voice trembled faintly in anticipation as he kissed her neck slowly, feeling her pulse quicken beneath his lips.</p>
<p>"You said you missed me," he murmured near her ear.</p>
<p>"I did."</p>
<p>He slowly unlocked the door while his lips traveled across her skin...</p>
<p>The moment they stepped inside and the door clicked shut—</p>
<p>Aria moved first.</p>
<p>She pushed him lightly against the wall and jumped into his arms, wrapping her legs around his waist as she pulled him into a deep kiss.</p>
<p>She was no longer hesitant or shy in the slightest...</p>
<p>Her fingers slid into his hair as she kissed him hungrily with their breath mingling and lips moving in heated rhythm.</p>
<p>"I hate you," she murmured between kisses.</p>
<p>"Why?"</p>
<p>"Because I couldn’t stop thinking about you."</p>
<p>His hands held her firmly, steadying her against him.</p>
<p>"That sounds like a you problem."</p>
<p>She laughed softly before kissing him again.</p>
<p>"I kept replaying the last time you were at my place," she admitted against his lips. "Every second."</p>
<p>West carried her toward the couch, still holding her effortlessly.</p>
<p>She pulled back just enough to look at him.</p>
<p>"You didn’t miss me?"</p>
<p>He brushed a strand of pink hair away from her face.</p>
<p>"I did."</p>
<p>Her eyes softened slightly at that and then she leaned forward and kissed him again—</p>
