<p>West stopped and turned his head slightly. "I’m trying to save your lives."</p>
<p>His voiced calmly before shrugging. "But if you’d rather die..."</p>
<p>"...be my guest."</p>
<p>He slapped the marshal’s hand off his shoulder and walked away.</p>
<p>His summons emerged behind him, phasing out from his summoner space like silent guardians.</p>
<p>Seraphyra stretched lazily. "I thought you said this was the safest way to travel, Master."</p>
<p>Gor’thala scanned the surroundings. "...This vessel is failing."</p>
<p>Aurethia simply observed while West scratched the back of his head.</p>
<p>"...Yeah but... It’s only falling because of me anyway."</p>
<p>Behind him the air hostess and marshal stiffened as a look of realization appeared on their faces.</p>
<p>"...Awakened..." sweat formed on their backs instantly.</p>
<p>—</p>
<p>West walked back down the aisle and this time, the stares he got were different.</p>
<p>Fear, awe and recognition flickered across their faces as his summons followed him.</p>
<p>"—It’s an awakened..."</p>
<p>"...We’re saved..."</p>
<p>"...Right...?"</p>
<p>"How naive... awakened don’t care about anyone else but themselves..."</p>
<p>"Let’s beg for help!"</p>
<p>"Fuck I don’t want to die!"</p>
<p>West ignored the background noise until he reached Debra and Amos again.</p>
<p>"Get up," he pointed at Seraphyra. "You’re riding."</p>
<p>Amos blinked. "...What?"</p>
<p>Seraphyra crouched slightly. "Get on."</p>
<p>Amos nodded and proceeded to climb on Seraphyra’s back without arguing.</p>
<p>Debra on the other hand, hesitated a bit before climbing onto Gor’thala’s back.</p>
<p>West turned toward the emergency exit and reached for it.</p>
<p>However, before he could open it, dozens of passengers came rushing in from the other compartment, begging.</p>
<p>"Please—!"</p>
<p>"Awakened sir, help us!"</p>
<p>"We don’t want to die!"</p>
<p>West stopped and turned slowly. "...Eh."</p>
<p>He shrugged slightly before responding; "That ship already sailed."</p>
<p>Their faces fell upon hearing that.</p>
<p>"I offered help earlier," his gaze flicked toward the air hostess and marshal. "You slapped my hand away."</p>
<p>The crowd murmured in confusion.</p>
<p>"We didn’t—!"</p>
<p>"Who did that?!"</p>
<p>The air hostess stepped forward quickly, bowing slightly.</p>
<p>"I—I’m sorry! We didn’t realize—please—!"</p>
<p>The marshal followed.</p>
<p>"...We apologize."</p>
<p>West watched them grovel and beg for some time before sighing.</p>
<p>"...Fine."</p>
<p>Hope returned instantly.</p>
<p>"Thank you—!"</p>
<p>"But," his word cut through the air. "It’ll cost you."</p>
<p>"How much?" someone asked.</p>
<p>West smiled faintly. "There’s about eighty of you."</p>
<p>"Two million dollars."</p>
<p>Gasps rang out immediately.</p>
<p>"That’s—!"</p>
<p>"We don’t have that—!"</p>
<p>West raised a hand.</p>
<p>"Divide it."</p>
<p>Simple.</p>
<p>"Otherwise..." he turned toward the exit again. "...I leave."</p>
<p>Panic spread as the crowd pleaded. "No—wait—!"</p>
<p>"You have five seconds to decide," he began counting.</p>
<p>"Five."</p>
<p>"Four—!"</p>
<p>"Yes! Yes! We agree!"</p>
<p>"Please—just save us!"</p>
<p>West nodded. "Good."</p>
<p>—</p>
<p>He moved quickly dividing them amongst his summons.</p>
<p>"Aurethia—twenty."</p>
<p>"Gor’thala—twenty."</p>
<p>"Seraphyra—twenty."</p>
<p>"...I’ll take twenty."</p>
<p>There were no arguments or hesitation.</p>
<p>—</p>
<p>West’s group sat strapped into their seats, attached to a large section of the plane.</p>
<p>"Don’t move."</p>
<p>He positioned himself at their center and pulled out his guardian sword.</p>
<p>Dark mist seeped out as he slashed swiftly around them.</p>
<p>BOOM!</p>
<p>The plane’s underside ruptured and the entire section detached.</p>
<p>They began to drop from above ten thousand feet in the air.</p>
<p>"AAAAHHHHHHH!"</p>
<p>Loud screams rang out but West remained calm all through as he stood in the center unstrapped unlike the others.</p>
<p>West slowly stepped forward off the ledge, grabbed it and stabilized himself beneath the falling section of the plane.</p>
<p>He proceeded to embed his fist into the underside of the structure as wind howled violently past them.</p>
<p>Meanwhile, on Aurethia’s side, she bound her group together tightly with a glowing leash and hurled them out.</p>
<p>They descended rapidly amidst yells while she followed.</p>
<p>—</p>
<p>Gor’thala on the other hand, summoned ten massive Pyre Behemoths.</p>
<p>"Hold on tight!" Gor’thala instructed as two passengers each sat on their backs before they jumped out into open air.</p>
<p>—</p>
<p>Seraphyra’s twenty were no longer twenty. Some were already sucked out by the gaps all over the plane. However, she didn’t seem bothered in the slightest.</p>
<p>Her eyes glowed and those who met her gaze turned to stone instantly.</p>
<p>After using her petrifying gaze on the ones still in the plane, she began to transform.</p>
<p>Her body expanded until she became monstrous in size. She turned into a colossal serpentine entity that was over two hundred feet long, causing the plane to burst apart even more, barely containing her.</p>
<p>She proceeded to swallowed the human statues whole and then launched herself out of the collapsing aircraft.</p>
<p>Her massive form coiled through the air as she caught more falling passengers, petrified them mid-air and devoured them for transport.</p>
<p>—</p>
<p>Above, the plane finally gave way, breaking apart.</p>
<p>Engines exploded, fire bloomed and metal ripped apart, dying the sky in trails of red.</p>
<p>The wind howled tearing at clothes and ripping screams from throats as West descended through the sky.</p>
<p>Beneath him, the ground became more visible as it drew closer.</p>
<p>West’s fists remained buried deep into the underside of the torn aircraft section with his entire body acting as a stabilizing anchor while twenty terrified passengers remained strapped to their seats above him, clinging to whatever sanity they had left.</p>
<p>Every inch of him felt like it was being crushed under the sheer force of gravity and velocity but still, he didn’t let go.</p>
<p>His eyes narrowed upon realizing that they weren’t dropping in the middle of nowhere.</p>
<p>"...Residential."</p>
<p>He noticed the buildings, streets, cars and people.</p>
<p>They were heading straight for the middle of a populated area.</p>
<p>The ground grew closer and closer as they fell uncontrollably.</p>
<p>"Brace!"</p>
<p>The passengers screamed as they closed in on dropping.</p>
<p>West on the other hand, activated Verdant Bloom over and over again.</p>
<p>[ Verdant Bloom Activated ]</p>
<p>[ Verdant Bloom Activated ]</p>
<p>[ Verdant Bloom Activated ]</p>
<p>Roots exploded outward from nothingness beneath.</p>
<p>He spammed it relentlessly, flooding the ground a few hundred feet beneath them with tangled vegetation. Layers upon layers of rapidly sprouting roots, vines, and thick fibrous growths filled the surroundings.</p>
<p>Each activation pushed more into existence, adding resistance and cushioning.</p>
<p>The ground was now only seconds away.</p>
<p>West roared under his breath. "Come on...!"</p>
<p>The roots thickened, spread and interlocked, forming a chaotic organic net.</p>
<p>And finally...</p>
<p>BOOOOOOM!</p>
<p>The entire section slammed into the street causing shockwaves to explod outward.</p>
