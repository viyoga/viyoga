<p>West quietly muttered to himself. "Whoever receives an Awakening Voucher has to be trustworthy... loyal... and capable."</p>
<p>He slowly looked out the conference room window toward the bustling headquarters below.</p>
<p>"This decision... can’t be rushed."</p>
<p>West said this but he knew there was no time. Three months was a very short duration for the numbers he needed... so he had to find a very amicable way to deal with this situation.</p>
<p>•••</p>
<p>•••</p>
<p>Meanwhile...</p>
<p>At the edge of Misty High, hidden behind enormous black iron gates...</p>
<p>Lay one of the principal estates belonging to one of the Mafia households.</p>
<p>Unlike ordinary mansions... this place resembled an ancient fortress.</p>
<p>Dark marble columns stretched toward impossibly high ceilings while massive crimson banners bearing the household emblem hung along every wall.</p>
<p>Everything about the architecture projected authority, power and legacy.</p>
<p>Within the grand council chamber... a meeting was already underway.</p>
<p>Long tables formed a semicircle around an elevated throne-like seat.</p>
<p>Numerous Mafia elders occupied the room and standing quietly beside the household head’s left shoulder... was Freya.</p>
<p>The room got heated with discussion as one elderly man slammed his fist onto the table.</p>
<p>"We have failed repeatedly."</p>
<p>"Every century..."</p>
<p>"The same outcome."</p>
<p>Another elder sighed.</p>
<p>"The Heart of Genesis remains beyond our reach."</p>
<p>"Four hundred years."</p>
<p>"Four failures."</p>
<p>"This cannot continue."</p>
<p>Murmurs spread throughout the chamber.</p>
<p>At the head of the room sat the Mafia Patriarch of this branch.</p>
<p>A broad-shouldered man with streaks of silver running through his otherwise jet-black hair.</p>
<p>His calm eyes swept across everyone present before he finally spoke. "It will be different this time."</p>
<p>The room immediately quieted as he slowly turned toward Freya. "Daughter."</p>
<p>Freya stepped forward gracefully and bowed respectfully. "I have prepared."</p>
<p>Several elders smiled approvingly as she straightened before speaking.</p>
<p>"I’ve spent the past months assembling exceptionally capable first-year awakeners."</p>
<p>"They have impressive potential."</p>
<p>"I believe..."</p>
<p>"...they possess the greatest chance our family has ever had."</p>
<p>"My team will retrieve the Heart of Genesis."</p>
<p>Several approving voices immediately echoed.</p>
<p>"Excellent."</p>
<p>"As expected of Lady Freya."</p>
<p>"The Patriarch chose well."</p>
<p>"Our hopes rest with you."</p>
<p>Freya smiled modestly. "You honor me."</p>
<p>Just then...</p>
<p>BOOM!</p>
<p>The enormous chamber doors suddenly swung open, resulting in the halting of every conversation.</p>
<p>Heavy footsteps echoed throughout the room as a massive figure entered.</p>
<p>Thud~</p>
<p>Thud~</p>
<p>Thud~</p>
<p>Seven months earlier...</p>
<p>West would recognise this face but now... he had changed dramatically.</p>
<p>His shoulders had broadened considerably with muscle pushing out beneath his fitted black attire.</p>
<p>A spiky scar crossed his left eye, forcing it permanently shut.</p>
<p>Rather than weakening his appearance... it made him look infinitely more intimidating, mature and dangerous.</p>
<p>If someone hadn’t known they were twins... Few would not believe he and Freya shared the same face.</p>
<p>Every step commanded attention, causing several elders to instinctively straighten.</p>
<p>Timothy stopped before the central table as his single visible eye slowly swept across the room.</p>
<p>"Aren’t you..."</p>
<p>"...all forgetting something?"</p>
<p>His gaze settled upon Freya as the surroundings turned completely silent.</p>
<p>"I’m also a first-year awakener."</p>
<p>One elder nervously cleared his throat. "Young Master..."</p>
<p>"When did you return?"</p>
<p>"We believed you were still completing the Patriarch’s assignment."</p>
<p>Without saying a word... Timothy lifted a thick stack of documents.</p>
<p>SLAM!</p>
<p>The papers crashed onto the table.</p>
<p>"I’ve finished."</p>
<p>"I successfully cleared one hundred ruins."</p>
<p>"...Exactly as Father instructed."</p>
<p>The room erupted in shock. "One hundred?"</p>
<p>"So quickly?"</p>
<p>"Incredible..."</p>
<p>Even several elders looked genuinely impressed. They didn’t think someone incapable like Timothy would have successfully cleared this number in seven months.</p>
<p>They believed it would take him at least a couple of years.</p>
<p>There was no doubt that he had changed a lot...</p>
<p>Freya quietly studied her brother with perfectly composed expression.</p>
<p>Only her eyes hinted that countless thoughts were already racing through her mind.</p>
<p>Timothy never looked away from her.</p>
<p>"I will participate in the Castle Extingo Expedition," his deep voice echoed throughout the chamber.</p>
<p>"And..."</p>
<p>"...I will bring back the treasure."</p>
<p>His declaration hung heavily in the air and for a brief moment... nobody spoke.</p>
<p>Finally... Freya smiled with a completely unbothered look. "Of course."</p>
<p>"It is your right to participate, brother."</p>
<p>She folded her hands politely. "But..."</p>
<p>"...there is one problem."</p>
<p>"You don’t have a team."</p>
<p>Timothy’s visible eye narrowed upon hearing that. "Then..."</p>
<p>"I’ll gather one."</p>
<p>Neither sibling looked away while the elders silently exchanged glances.</p>
<p>Everyone in the room understood...</p>
<p>Before Castle Extingo had even opened... a new battle... had already begun</p>
<p>•••</p>
<p>•••</p>
<p>Several days passed in the blink of an eye.</p>
<p>During that period, Shadow Reapers continued operating as normal. Expedition teams came and went, Aria remained buried beneath mountains of paperwork, Jax and the first-year squad began venturing farther outside Misty High in search of Affinity Stones, and West spent most of his time juggling gang affairs with preparations for Castle Extingo.</p>
<p>Today, however, was different.</p>
<p>Today had nothing to do with ruins, gangs, battles or Castle Extingo preparations...</p>
<p>Today was inspection Day.</p>
<p>A glossy surfaced black luxury SUV rolled through the front gates of Shadow Reapers Headquarters before stopping neatly in the courtyard.</p>
<p>The rear passenger door opened and out stepped Liz.</p>
<p>Several nearby members instinctively turned.</p>
<p>Even though they’d seen her before... she was still breathtaking.</p>
<p>Her silky black hair flowed almost to her waist, shimmering softly beneath the morning sunlight. She wore a cream-colored fitted blazer over a charcoal blouse tucked neatly into a pencil skirt that accentuated her curvaceous figure without appearing excessive.</p>
<p>Black heels clicked rhythmically across the pavement, while a leather document folder rested beneath one arm.</p>
<p>She looked every bit the accomplished real estate consultant.</p>
<p>Her naturally striking features were complemented by subtle makeup, making her seem less like someone headed for work and more like the star of a luxury magazine cover.</p>
<p>A few recruits whispered among themselves.</p>
<p>"So that’s the Miss Liz?"</p>
<p>"I finally understand what Jax meant."</p>
<p>"She’s unbelievably beautiful..."</p>
<p>Jax happened to overhear them and folded his arms dramatically.</p>
<p>"Children..."</p>
<p>"Beauty is temporary."</p>
<p>"You must look beyond."</p>
<p>Nina happened to walk by and looked at him. "...Says the guy who almost bought a sports car because it looked cool."</p>
<p>The nearby recruits burst into laughter while Jax pointed accusingly. "Why must you always attack me personally?"</p>
<p>Before Nina could respond, West emerged from headquarters with a smile.</p>
<p>"Morning."</p>
<p>Liz smiled back. "Morning."</p>
<p>"I hope you’re ready."</p>
<p>West laughed. "I spent twelve million dollars."</p>
<p>"I’d better be."</p>
