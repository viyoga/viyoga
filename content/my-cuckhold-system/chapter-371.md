<p>Jax’s head immediately snapped back toward him. “Bro, enough—”</p>
<p>West raised one hand and Jax immediately stopped.</p>
<p>West looked back at the camera and his voice became quieter. “I’m a disgusting cunt.”</p>
<p>Several Shadow Reapers members visibly flinched.</p>
<p>One of them muttered under his breath, “That bastard…”</p>
<p>But West kept going. “I’m a pathetic bastard.”</p>
<p>His fists clenched by his sides. “A powerless mutt.”</p>
<p>His eyes began to burn with restrained fury. “A worthless piece of shit.”</p>
<p>He took a slow breath. “The truth is…”</p>
<p>West’s voice became almost emotionless. “…I’m nothing.”</p>
<p>Jax could barely contain himself anymore. “Stop this.”</p>
<p>West didn’t look at him because he knew that if he looked away from the camera, even for a second, he might not finish.</p>
<p>“I’m just a coward who acts tough when I’m actually nothing.”</p>
<p>Every word felt disgusting coming out of his mouth.</p>
<p>West Einstein had never considered himself invincible since he knew there were stronger awakened capable of killing him… but to sit there and deliberately tear himself apart?</p>
<p>That was something entirely different.</p>
<p>“I should have stayed away from Matthew.”</p>
<p>His eyes lowered. “I’m sorry.”</p>
<p>“Matthew…”</p>
<p>His voice almost broke. “…I’m sorry for what I did to you.”</p>
<p>He stared at the camera. “I apologize.”</p>
<p>His eyes slowly narrowed. “I hope you can forgive this pathetic, disgusting, powerless mutt.”</p>
<p>The room was dead silent as West reached forward and… click~</p>
<p>The recording ended.</p>
<p>West remained on his knees with his phone resting loosely in his hand.</p>
<p>Jax immediately walked forward. “Don’t send that.”</p>
<p>West slowly looked at him.</p>
<p>Jax’s eyes were red with anger. “He’s playing with you. That’s all this is. He wants to see you break.”</p>
<p>West looked down at the video and for several seconds, he said nothing.</p>
<p>All of a sudden, his thumb moved and he clicked send. The file immediately began uploading.</p>
<p>Jax stared at him. “West…”</p>
<p>West slowly pushed himself back to his feet as his expression became calm again.</p>
<p>But beneath that calmness… something violent was brewing.</p>
<p>“He thinks he won,” West slipped his phone into his pocket. “Let him enjoy it.”</p>
<p>His eyes slowly darkened. “Every second Aria and Liz remain in his hands…”</p>
<p>His voice dropped. “…he’s buying himself another reason for me to make his death painful.”</p>
<p>A moment later, Angel Trigger received the video… and when he pressed play, his laughter echoed through the room.</p>
<p>“Look at him!” Angel Trigger threw his head back. “The great West Einstein!”</p>
<p>He replayed the section where West called himself a little bitch again and again and again…</p>
<p>His subordinates in the surroundings laughed along with him.</p>
<p>Angel Trigger leaned back, grinning. “Truly convincing.”</p>
<p>Only a few minutes passed before West’s phone rang…</p>
<p>He answered and from the other end, Angel Trigger was laughing…</p>
<p>“That was beautiful.”</p>
<p>“Truly convincing,” Angel Trigger chuckled. “You really would do anything for the people you love, wouldn’t you?”</p>
<p>West’s eyes narrowed. “Wouldn’t you?”</p>
<p>There was a brief pause beforr Angel Trigger voiced with a serious tone. “Perhaps.”</p>
<p>“But let’s not get sentimental.”</p>
<p>“What’s next?”</p>
<p>“Now…”</p>
<p>Angel Trigger deliberately paused. “The second demand.”</p>
<p>West’s expression became colder. “Go ahead.”</p>
<p>Angel Trigger smiled on the other end. “I want you to do something that will be a little more difficult.”</p>
<p>West listened.. and as Angel Trigger explained his next demand, West’s expression slowly changed.</p>
<p>“Do you understand?”</p>
<p>West remained silent for several seconds before answering. “…I understand.”</p>
<p>“Good.”</p>
<p>West lowered the phone as the call ended.</p>
<p>Jax immediately approached. “What did he want?”</p>
<p>West stared at the blank screen. “He wants me to go somewhere.”</p>
<p>“Where?”</p>
<p>West looked toward the window. “Black arrow gang.”</p>
<p>Jax frowned. “And?”</p>
<p>West’s eyes hardened. “And he wants me to do something there that could put me directly against their gang.”</p>
<p>West slipped his phone into his pocket. “Apparently, humiliating me wasn’t enough.”</p>
<p>His expression became cold. “Now he wants to multiply the number of enemies I already have.”</p>
<p>•••</p>
<p>•••</p>
<p>For the next week, West Einstein’s life became something he had never imagined it could become.</p>
<p>He had faced ruin monsters, fought high ranking gang members, walked into dangerous territories alone and stared death in the face more times than he could count.</p>
<p>Yet somehow, none of those experiences had been as frustrating as spending an entire week being treated like Angel Trigger’s personal errand boy…</p>
<p>Every morning, there was another call… every afternoon was a new demand…</p>
<p>And every evening, West returned to Shadow Reapers looking like he had aged several years.</p>
<p>The third demand had been relatively simple. “Go to the eastern district and collect a package.”</p>
<p>Then the fourth… “Deliver this to someone at Brist district.”</p>
<p>“There’s a media man who pissed me off. Go hang him upside down from the top of a 200 storey building.”</p>
<p>West had stared at the phone for several seconds.</p>
<p>“You have people for this.”</p>
<p>“I know.”</p>
<p>“Then why am I doing it?”</p>
<p>“Because I said so.”</p>
<p>West had clenched his teeth but went nonetheless. Angel Trigger seemed to derive some sick pleasure from being able to command a gang leader however he pleased.</p>
<p>The errands continued one after another.</p>
<p>Angel Trigger seemed determined to test exactly how far West would go.</p>
<p>At one point, West received a message containing nothing but an address.</p>
<p>When he arrived, someone handed him a ridiculous costume and a phone.</p>
<p>“Put it on.”</p>
<p>West stared at the outfit. “No.”</p>
<p>“Think about Aria and Liz,” Angel Trigger voice came from the other end.</p>
<p>West’s expression darkened and five minutes later, he was wearing the ridiculous outfit.</p>
<p>The recording lasted less than thirty seconds.</p>
<p>Angel Trigger apparently found it hilarious but West did not. However, he had no choice but to endure.</p>
<p>Every time his pride wanted him to fight back, he remembered two names… Aria and Liz.</p>
<p>—</p>
<p>One afternoon, Angel Trigger gave him another demand and West headed to the location.</p>
<p>West stood outside a warehouse. “What do you want me to do?”</p>
<p>“This is someone who got on my nerves,” Angel Trigger’s voice was completely casual. “Cut off an arm and a leg.”</p>
<p>West’s eyes hardened as he looked at the person Angel Trigger had indicated.</p>
<p>The man stood before him terrified…</p>
<p>“You’re joking,” West muttered.</p>
<p>“I’m not.”</p>
