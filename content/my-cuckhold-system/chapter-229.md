<p>Eventually, Amos leaned slightly toward West. "...Your awakening."</p>
<p>West glanced at him. "...What about it?"</p>
<p>Amos adjusted his glasses. "Was there a trigger? Something that caused it? A catalyst?"</p>
<p>West’s expression remained neutral. "...It just happened."</p>
<p>Amos frowned slightly. "Hmm, I started conducting this research because I wanted to help people obtain a shred of power that awakeners have. I’ve tried as much as possible to gather all I can on awakeners and you all seem to tell me the same thing as regards to your awakening..."</p>
<p>West shrugged. "I guess that’s all there is to it. People don’t choose to awaken, you know? It just happens to them..."</p>
<p>Amos didn’t press further but his curiosity about the matter lingered.</p>
<p>Of course, West couldn’t tell him he didn’t actually awakened naturally... he could never tell anyone.</p>
<p>...</p>
<p>...</p>
<p>The flight was supposed to be last an hour... but thirty minutes in, West stood.</p>
<p>"I’ll be back."</p>
<p>He walked toward the restroom, stepped inside, closed the door, took care of what he needed to, washed his hands and looked up.</p>
<p>His reflection stared back at him and in his eyes was a glint of danger.</p>
<p>He dried his hands, stepped out and walked back down the aisle.</p>
<p>However, when he arrived at their seats, Debra and Dr. Amos were gone.</p>
<p>West’s eyes narrowed slowly upon noticing that the seats were empty.</p>
<p>"...Tch... this is starting to get really annoying..."</p>
<p>Although the seats were empty, he didn’t worry much because they were in a plane mid-flight.</p>
<p>There was no way anyone was getting off a plane at this altitude.</p>
<p>West proceeded to head down the aisle, ignoring the curious glances of passengers.</p>
<p>His senses spread outward, searching for anything out of place. Soon, he turned toward the front section before stopping.</p>
<p>A faint scent of blood oozed into his nostril causing his gaze to sharpen instantly.</p>
<p>"...There."</p>
<p>He moved again, slipping past a half-closed service corridor, pushing the door open just enough to slide through.</p>
<p>—</p>
<p>Elsewhere, Debra stumbled forward slightly as the cold metal of the gun pressed harder against her back.</p>
<p>"Keep moving," the man behind her muttered with a tone of excitement.</p>
<p>Dr. Amos limped beside her, breathing roughly while the man with a axe shaped mark on his left cheek, laughed greedily.</p>
<p>"Yes... I hit the jackpot," he nudged the gun forward. "I knew staking out the airport with my guys would pay off."</p>
<p>Debra bore a terrified look while Amos grinded his teeth.</p>
<p>"You don’t know how much they put up for you two," the man continued. "Do you have any idea how valuable you are right now?"</p>
<p>He pushed them into a more secluded section of the plane... an area near storage compartments, away from most passengers.</p>
<p>"Hey—!" a voice voice suddenly rang out from behind.</p>
<p>"You’re not supposed to be back here—" an air hostess stepped forward, frowning.</p>
<p>Pfft!</p>
<p>A muted shot drizzle out and her body dropped instantly.</p>
<p>She was dead and thanks to the silencer there was almost no sound.</p>
<p>The man didn’t even spare her another glance. "...So."</p>
<p>He adjusted his grip on the gun. "Where were we?"</p>
<p>He stepped closer to Debra. "I need you to hand over all files and data on the assimilation research."</p>
<p>His tone sharpened. "According to reports... Dr. Debra has them... so hand them over."</p>
<p>The gun pressed harder.</p>
<p>"Or you both die. Right here. Right now."</p>
<p>Debra swallowed.</p>
<p>"I don’t—"</p>
<p>Pfft!</p>
<p>A shot rang out and Dr. Amos instantly screamed.</p>
<p>His leg gave out and blood burst from the wound as his body collapsed to the floor.</p>
<p>"Don’t make me ask again," the man voiced calmly.</p>
<p>Debra’s eyes widened as tears formed instantly. "I don’t have it!"</p>
<p>"Wrong answer."</p>
<p>Pfft!</p>
<p>He shot at the second leg causing Amos to cry out again. His voice broke as both legs exploded with agonising pain, causing blood to pool beneath him.</p>
<p>Debra dropped to her knees beside him.</p>
<p>"I’m not lying!" she cried. "I swear! It’s not on me!"</p>
<p>Her voice trembled.</p>
<p>"I have it stored somewhere—but it’s not with me!"</p>
<p>The man stared at her with a disappointed look. "...Oh well."</p>
<p>He sighed. "I guess that’s fine too."</p>
<p>He raised the gun again with a small smile on his face. "Even if both of you die... we’ll find it eventually."</p>
<p>He aimed while tightening his finger around the trigger... however, before he could pull it all the way down, a hand shot out from behind, gripping his neck.</p>
<p>He was instantly slammed sideways.</p>
<p>BLAM!</p>
<p>His face smashed into the side of the plane and blood exploded outward as the impact cracked bone and skin alike.</p>
<p>The gun slipped from his hand, clattering to the floor.</p>
<p>Before he could react, he was lifted again off the ground.</p>
<p>West stood behind him with a cold and deadly look.</p>
<p>"You shouldn’t have done that."</p>
<p>The man coughed violently with blood spilling from his mouth.</p>
<p>He then began to laugh in amusement. "...So it’s you."</p>
<p>West’s grip tightened. "...What?"</p>
<p>"The awakened..." the man rasped. "The one intelligence mentioned."</p>
<p>A grin formed through the blood. "The one who protected them... from Nightingale."</p>
<p>West’s eyes narrowed slightly. "...Who cares."</p>
<p>He raised his hand, ready to end their brief encounter but the man suddenly yelled out.</p>
<p>"Don’t... it’ll be in your best interest not to kill me."</p>
<p>West’s expression didn’t change as he slowed a bit.</p>
<p>"I didn’t come alone," the man added.</p>
<p>Suddenly, figures stepped out from corners, behind compartments, from blind spots and even the economic class up ahead.</p>
<p>They all had guns raised, aiming forward.</p>
<p>West’s eyes flicked around, counting them. They were no less than ten in number.</p>
<p>"You’re strong. I can see that," The man grinned wider as blood dripped from his chin.</p>
<p>"You’ll probably survive if the ten to one clash but what about them?"</p>
<p>His eyes flicked toward Debra and Amos.</p>
<p>"When bullets start flying..." his smile widened. "I can guarantee you...one of them dies."</p>
<p>"or maybe both," he added. "Just let me go and things don’t have to escalate any further."</p>
