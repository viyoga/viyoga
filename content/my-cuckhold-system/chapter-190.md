<p>As for West, despite being unaware of Renzhi thoughts, he wasn’t done either.</p>
<p>He still wanted to punish Renzhi properly, because as he walked beside Zu Li, a faint smirk crossed his face.</p>
<p>’Let’s see how you react... When everything you cherish... becomes mine.’</p>
<p>—</p>
<p>Zu Li and West arrived at one of the private training centers within the Dragon Fang base.</p>
<p>Unlike the usual training grounds, this was more advanced and refined, built for the higher ups.</p>
<p>The doors slid open with a soft hiss and inside was a vast, high-ceilinged chamber and smooth metallic walls lined with embedded energy panels.</p>
<p>Floating holographic interfaces hovered in the air and at the center was a massive open space designed for combat.</p>
<p>Surrounding it were multiple specialized zones:</p>
<p>— Gravity chambers, where pressure could be increased several times over to enhance physical training.</p>
<p>— Simulation pods, capable of recreating ruin environments and enemy behaviors in real-time combat scenarios.</p>
<p>— Energy resistance zones, where raw elemental attacks could be generated and endured.</p>
<p>— Weapon synchronization stations, designed to help awakeners better attune to their weapons and abilities.</p>
<p>Everything here was designed for growth.</p>
<p>Zu Li stepped forward.</p>
<p>"I heard about what happened at the ruins."</p>
<p>She voiced with her usual calm tone but her gaze was piercing.</p>
<p>"That you barely made it out."</p>
<p>She turned slightly toward him.</p>
<p>"Are you okay?"</p>
<p>West shrugged lightly.</p>
<p>"Yeah. I’m good."</p>
<p>"I had everyone retreat while I held it back."</p>
<p>Zu Li didn’t look completely convinced about the part where West said he was good.</p>
<p>"I know."</p>
<p>"I read the report."</p>
<p>West paused before chuckling slightly.</p>
<p>"Of course you did."</p>
<p>~Sigh~</p>
<p>"I don’t even know what that thing was..."</p>
<p>His expression darkened slightly.</p>
<p>"But I made sure it never saw the light of day."</p>
<p>West wasn’t glad that under his leadership, there was a casualty but nothing could be done about the situation... It was utterly unexpected and Zu Li understood this so she never blamed him.</p>
<p>"Its not your fault..." she placed a hand on his shoulder, meeting his gaze.</p>
<p>She held eye contact with him for a bit before adding. "You’ll be a great gang leader regardless... always remember, you can’t always save everyone under you. Lapses in judgement can occur, as well as inescapable situations... loss is something you will have to accept as you grow. As a leader, you’ll learn to adapt to unexpected situations better but even at that, some things can’t be salvaged. Just don’t beat yourself up about it."</p>
<p>"Yeah. Thanks..." West felt the weight he didn’t know was on his shoulders, ease up a bit.</p>
<p>He wasn’t too sentimental about the loss but it had created a bit of doubt in his heart as to whether or not he was fit to lead. Fortunately, Zu Li words had erased those little doubts that crept in.</p>
<p>She smiled at him and West returned the smile before they delved back into the matter.</p>
<p>"My people are already looking into it."</p>
<p>Zu Li’s tone suddenly turned colder.</p>
<p>"Especially since it claimed one of ours."</p>
<p>A brief silence followed before she continued.</p>
<p>"So far... we’ve uncovered something."</p>
<p>West glanced at her.</p>
<p>"The research facility...is tied to an organization."</p>
<p>She paused before revealing it.</p>
<p>"Frankincense."</p>
<p>West’s eyes narrowed slightly in recognition.</p>
<p>"...Frankincense..."</p>
<p>The name wasn’t unfamiliar to him at all.</p>
<p>"...That’s the same organization that runs energy plants across Misty High City, right?" He questioned.</p>
<p>Zu Li nodded.</p>
<p>"Yes."</p>
<p>"But it seems..."</p>
<p>Her eyes sharpened.</p>
<p>"They’ve been trying very hard to keep this facility hidden."</p>
<p>"To make sure it was never traced back to them."</p>
<p>West crossed his arms.</p>
<p>"What were they researching?"</p>
<p>His voice carried a faint suspicion.</p>
<p>"That creature...they made it, didn’t they?"</p>
<p>Zu Li exhaled lightly.</p>
<p>"We’re still gathering information... but from what we’ve seen so far..."</p>
<p>"Evolution."</p>
<p>"And replication."</p>
<p>The words lingered in the air with an unsettling feeling.</p>
<p>"But like I said," she continued, "we don’t have the full picture yet."</p>
<p>"But we will."</p>
<p>Her gaze hardened.</p>
<p>"I’ll make sure of it."</p>
<p>West nodded slowly but his mind traveled elsewhere because a memory resurfaced due to the name of the organisation mentioned.</p>
<p>’That’s where she worked...’</p>
<p>The woman who had disappeared from his life years ago...</p>
<p>The woman he hadn’t seen or heard from in ages... his mother.</p>
<p>His expression didn’t change but inside his thoughts swirled.</p>
<p>—</p>
<p>"Focus."</p>
<p>Zu Li’s voice snapped him back.</p>
<p>She had already stepped into the center of the training arena and energy began to gather around her.</p>
<p>"Let’s train."</p>
<p>West exhaled softly before stepping forward.</p>
<p>"Yeah."</p>
<p>But even as they began, his mind kept drifting back...</p>
<p>Back to a question that now refused to leave him.</p>
<p>’What exactly have you been involved in...? And why does it feel like I’m about to find out?’</p>
<p>...</p>
<p>...</p>
<p>~ Thirty Minutes Later ~</p>

<p>Swwhiiiii~</p>

<p>West blinked.</p>
<p>"...What?"</p>

<p>"You’ve been off since we started."</p>

<p>Zu Li raised a brow.</p>
<p>"Oh?"</p>
<p>She pressed down even more deliberately.</p>
<p>"Watch me."</p>
