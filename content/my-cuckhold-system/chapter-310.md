<p>Iron Vultures members attempted moving closer and several Shadow Reapers members did too.</p>
<p>Jax quickly raised another series of ice walls. "Private event."</p>
<p>"Tickets sold out."</p>
<p>"Please remain seated."</p>
<p>Despite the joke, nobody was laughing.</p>
<p>The pressure radiating from within the frozen enclosure was already beginning to rise.</p>
<p>Meanwhile inside... Bloodstain slowly looked around.</p>
<p>The gigantic frost walls surrounded both him and West, completely isolating them.</p>
<p>For a moment he looked confused before letting out a loud booming laugh. "You idiot."</p>
<p>West smiled while Bloodstain spread his arms. "You trapped yourself in here with me?"</p>
<p>The blood aura surrounding him intensified as his grin widened. "Nobody can help you now."</p>
<p>West’s shook his head while chuckling. "Oh... I think you misunderstand."</p>
<p>Bloodstain frowned as West slowly took a step forward.</p>
<p>The atmosphere changed, filled up with a strange predatory energy as West’s body started growing.</p>
<p>Bones cracking sounds rang out as his muscles expanded, stretching his cloth and dark blue fur rapidly spread across his skin.</p>
<p>Long glowing fangs extended from his mouth, bony spines erupted from his back and his eyes transformed into glowing blue-white orbs.</p>
<p>Dozens of glowing blue footprints appeared across the ground around them as he reached a height of eight feet.</p>
<p>Bloodstain’s eyes widened and his smile instantly vanished the moment the transformation was completed.</p>
<p>The Void Fang Predator that stood before him once more was the same beast responsible for the destruction of Iron Vultures.</p>
<p>The same beast responsible for everything...</p>
<p>For several seconds... Bloodstain simply stared as the silence between them grew.</p>
<p>All of a sudden he let out a broken almost relieved laugh. "HAHAHAHA!"</p>
<p>His shoulders trembled and his fists clenched as blood aura exploded outward from his being. "I knew it..."</p>
<p>Bloodstain’s voice became quieter. "I knew it was you."</p>
<p>The ground beneath his feet cracked, causing the ice walls to tremble as he let out more aura. "I KNEW IT!"</p>
<p>The roar shook the entire enclosure.</p>
<p>Outside the walls, both gangs immediately heard it but were still completely in the dark about what was going on in there.</p>
<p>Bloodstain pointed directly at West with an utterly deranged expression. "I’m not crazy after all."</p>
<p>"I’m not insane."</p>
<p>"It was you."</p>
<p>"It was always you."</p>
<p>Every loss and humiliation he had suffered in these past two months... every setback.... West Einstein was behind it.</p>
<p>West’s distorted voice echoed throughout the frozen arena. "Congratulations."</p>
<p>"You figured it out."</p>
<p>That confirmation only made Bloodstain angrier.</p>
<p>The Tier Two awakened finally unleashed everything without holding back.</p>
<p>The full pressure of a veteran Tier Two erupted outward.</p>
<p>BOOOOOOOOOOM!</p>
<p>Blood gathered, pooling from a couple corner of the battlefield with casualties before exploding from the ground.</p>
<p>Massive crimson spikes burst upward and the ice walls shook violently.</p>
<p>Even West could feel the pressure. Bloodstain was undoubtedly powerful. A tier 2 willing to go all out could become an almost unstoppable force...</p>
<p>Bloodstain eyes turned bloodshot as Veins covered his neck. "I will enjoy this."</p>
<p>The blood surrounding him condensed, forming scythes, spears, blades, axes and a dozen other weapons.</p>
<p>He displayed a savagery grin. "I will enjoy ripping you apart limb from limb."</p>
<p>West stared at him before slowly smiling, displacing his glowing fangs.</p>
<p>The smile looked deviously monstrous. "You had it coming."</p>
<p>His distorted voice echoed unnaturally causing Bloodstain’s expression to darken.</p>
<p>West took another step forward and more blue footprints spread across the ground.</p>
<p>The entire enclosure was now covered in them, like glowing trails left behind by a predator stalking prey.</p>
<p>Bloodstain could suddenly feel that something was different... the pressure surrounding West wasn’t Tier One anymore.</p>
<p>At least... not normal Tier One.</p>
<p>The creature standing before him felt dangerous. Why was he feeling danger from a tier 1?</p>
<p>West’s glowing eyes narrowed. "Just like you had this coming."</p>
<p>Bloodstain roared and attacked, sending dozen of blood weapons forward.</p>
<p>The ground exploded, spikes erupted beneath West and a massive blood scythe descended from above.</p>
<p>Bloodstain turned into a crimson blur, racing toward West.</p>
<p>However, just when he was a few feet away from reaching his target, a glowing footprint appeared behind Bloodstain.</p>
<p>He turned his head to the side, spotting the glowing footprint but the moment he peered back ahead, West was gone.</p>
<p>He appeared directly behind Bloodstain, instantaneously.</p>
<p>The Void Fang Predator’s fist shot forward.</p>
<p>BOOOOOOOOM!</p>
<p>The punch connected with Bloodstain’s side, generating a shockwave powerful enough to crack the surrounding ice walls.</p>
<p>Bloodstain was launched across the enclosure.</p>
<p>His body skipped across the ground, destroying multiple ice formations before finally crashing into a wall.</p>
<p>The entire frozen arena shook but fortunately, Jax was reinforcing it on the outside.</p>
<p>Both gangs paused and stared for a bit because despite not seeing the battle, they could hear that battle had truly began.</p>
<p>The gang leader slowly pressed himself forward from the wall after the attack.</p>
<p>For a brief moment, a faint glow flickered around his body before fading.</p>
<p>West’s eyes immediately narrowed upon seeing that Bloodstain was barely affected by the punch.</p>
<p>The punch had landed cleanly, yet Bloodstain had suffered far less damage than expected.</p>
<p>The only answer was, that the gang leader had an awakened equipment on that offered protection.</p>
<p>It only made sense. As the leader of a veteran gang, it would have been stranger if he didn’t possess expensive awakened gear.</p>
<p>Bloodstain slowly straightened with a grin across his face. "I’m glad you hit first."</p>
<p>West tilted his head slightly as Bloodstain dusted off his shoulder. "Now I know your current strength level."</p>
<p>The grin widened. "It’s absurd."</p>
<p>His blood aura intensified as he added. "Completely absurd for a Tier One."</p>
<p>West merely smiled while Bloodstain pointed at him. "But now that I know where you stand..."</p>
<p>The blood around him gathered. "I’m certain I can get rid of you here."</p>
<p>West chuckled. "Oh?"</p>
<p>Blue footprints lit up beneath him as he stepped forward. "We’ll see."</p>
<p>POOF!</p>
<p>He suddenly vanished.</p>
<p>Bloodstain’s eyes sharpened as West reappeared beside him with a fist shooting toward his ribs.</p>
<p>A wall of blood suddenly materialized between them.</p>
<p>BOOM!</p>
<p>West’s fist crashed into the barrier but instead of resisting completely, the blood suddenly dissolved and his arm passed through.</p>
<p>In the process of going through, the blood instantly solidified again.</p>
<p>West’s eyes widened as his entire arm became trapped.</p>
<p>"Got you," Bloodstain’s smiled savagely as Blood pooled around his right hand</p>
<p>A massive crimson gauntlet formed as he punched forward.</p>
<p>BOOOOOM!</p>
<p>The strike connected with West’s side, launching him across the battlefield.</p>
<p>Chunks of earth exploded beneath him as he flew across the air.</p>
<p>Bloodstain instantly leapt after him, shooting upward like a missile.</p>
<p>He arrived above West’s airborne form and immediately drove the massive blood gauntlet fist downward.</p>
<p>VHOOOM~</p>
<p>The punch was only inches away from contact when West vanished.</p>
<p>The fist missed him causing the ground below to explode.</p>
<p>BOOOOOOOM!</p>
<p>A crater appeared instantly below but before Bloodstain could recover, a glowing footprint appeared above him.</p>
<p>West materialized with his leg swing downward.</p>
<p>CRASH!</p>
<p>The kick connected with Bloodstain’s spine blasting towards the ground.</p>
<p>The newly formed crater doubled in size causing dust to erupt everywhere.</p>
<p>A shockwave rolled across the battlefield.</p>
<p>For a brief moment, Bloodstain remained buried at the center before slowly rising.</p>
<p>Once again, a glow flickered around his body but this time it seemed dimmer than the last time.</p>
<p>West noticed immediately and displayed a small smile.</p>
<p>He shot forward again while Bloodstain stretched his hand.</p>
<p>The blood surrounding him transformed instantly, forming a gigantic crimson scythe.</p>
<p>The blade swept forward, towards the approaching West.</p>
<p>Swwwhiiiiii~</p>
<p>Unfortunately, the scythe sliced through empty air.</p>
<p>He materialized from a footprint behind Bloodstain but another blood weapon was already waiting.</p>
<p>A giant hammer descended from above before he could attack Bloodstain.</p>
<p>West vanished again causing the hammer to obliterated the ground where he once stood.</p>
<p>He reappeared dozens of feet away and two enormous blood blades immediately swung toward him from opposite sides.</p>
<p>West vanished again and again and again as the battlefield became filled with glowing blue footprints.</p>
<p>Each time West appeared somewhere, another attack was already waiting.</p>
<p>Scythes, axes, hammers, spears and all sorts of weapons moved swiftly attacking him upon reappearance.</p>
<p>Bloodstain controlled them all with terrifying focus.</p>
<p>West continuously teleported across the battlefield from one footprint to another causing blue lights to flash continously.</p>
<p>The footprints were becoming dimmer the more he used teleportation and Bloodstain noticed it too.</p>
<p>The veteran’s grin slowly widened as he understood that there was some kind of limit.</p>
<p>West kept moving but the attacks kept coming nonstop.</p>
<p>Until finally, the last footprint dimmed completely as well.</p>
<p>The moment West’s teleportation entered cooldown, Bloodstain’s grin turned vicious. "There it is."</p>
<p>The blood surrounding him exploded outward. "You’re done now!"</p>
<p>Massive blood spikes burst from the ground from West sides and several from behind.</p>
<p>At the same time, dozens of floating blood weapons launched through the air while Bloodstain himself charged forward.</p>
<p>The giant scythe in his grasp swept through the battlefield as he turned into a red blur.</p>
<p>Every escape route was cut off... and every angle completely covered.</p>
