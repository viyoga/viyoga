<p>West stood directly behind Jax with arms folded.</p>
<p>Jax grabbed his chest. "What the hell man?"</p>
<p>West raised an eyebrow. "What?"</p>
<p>"How long have you been standing there?"</p>
<p>"Long enough."</p>
<p>The recruits immediately started trying not to laugh.</p>
<p>Jax pointed accusingly. "You move too quietly."</p>
<p>West ignored that. Instead, his eyes drifted toward the sports car.</p>
<p>Jax immediately brightened again. "Oh!"</p>
<p>He rushed toward the vehicle. "Check this out."</p>
<p>He slapped the hood with a bright expression. "Isn’t it beautiful?"</p>
<p>West stared at it without saying a word while Jax grinned.</p>
<p>"I know."</p>
<p>"It’s amazing."</p>
<p>West remained silent but Jax continued anyway. "I’ll take you for a ride."</p>
<p>He gestured dramatically. "Come on."</p>
<p>West expression remained deadpan while several recruits suddenly became very interested in the weather.</p>
<p>Nina smirked because she already knew what was coming.</p>
<p>West finally spoke. "You bought an expensive car."</p>
<p>Jax nodded proudly. "Yep."</p>
<p>"You like it?"</p>
<p>West looked at him before shifting his gaze to the car and then back at him.</p>
<p>"Return it," he stated.</p>
<p>Jax blinked. "What?"</p>
<p>"Return it."</p>
<p>The grin slowly vanished from Jax’s face while several recruits immediately stepped backward.</p>
<p>Nobody wanted to be involved.</p>
<p>Jax scratched the back of his head. "Uh..."</p>
<p>West’s expression remained calm as he added. "What did I say about laying low?"</p>
<p>Jax opened his mouth but closed it without saying a word as West continued.</p>
<p>"You know Iron Vultures are looking for any opportunity to blame us."</p>
<p>Jax winced as West pointed at the vehicle.</p>
<p>"How exactly do you expect people to believe that a member of a beginner gang can suddenly afford this?"</p>
<p>The realization hit Jax immediately, causing him to freeze.</p>
<p>Even the new recruits stopped smiling because that was actually a problem...</p>
<p>"Ah," Jax stared at the car before staring back at West.</p>
<p>"Ahhhh..." The realization fully settled. "Oh shit."</p>
<p>West nodded. "Exactly."</p>
<p>Jax covered his face. "Goddammit."</p>
<p>West’s tone softened slightly. "You can buy an even expensive one later."</p>
<p>"But right now?" he pointed at the vehicle. "We don’t need that attention."</p>
<p>After a few moments Jax finally sighed. "Fine."</p>
<p>West nodded. "Good."</p>
<p>There was no argument or complaints. Jax knew when he messed up.</p>
<p>About thirty minutes later, the return process had already begun.</p>
<p>Jax climbed into the driver’s seat and the engine roared beautifully, almost making him change his mind.</p>
<p>"Goodbye my love."</p>
<p>One recruit wiped away an imaginary tear while another started playing funeral music from his phone. Jax flipped them off.</p>
<p>The gates opened, the sports car rolled out and the journey toward the dealership began.</p>
<p>Several streets away, at a quiet corner... a figure stood partially concealed within the shade of a nearby building.</p>
<p>As the sports car passed, the figure watched it carefully with their eyes narrowed, focusing on the person in the driver’s seat... Jax.</p>
<p>The car disappeared around the corner while the figure remained still for several seconds.</p>
<p>The figure pulled out a phone and a number was dialed.</p>
<p>After the call connected, the figure finally spoke. "I just spotted one of Shadow Reapers."</p>
<p>The person listened and paused for the one on the other end.</p>
<p>"Yeah."</p>
<p>Another pause...</p>
<p>Their gaze remained fixed in the direction Jax had gone.</p>
<p>"And I think you’re going to find this interesting."</p>
<p>•••</p>
<p>•••</p>
<p>Dinner at the Renzhi household was usually one of the few moments where the family actually spent time together.</p>
<p>As usual expensive paintings decorated the walls while floor-to-ceiling windows overlooked the beautifully maintained gardens outside.</p>
<p>Yet despite all of that luxury, the loudest thing in the room remained Rin.</p>
<p>"Dad, listen to this!"</p>
<p>"No."</p>
<p>"You haven’t even heard it yet."</p>
<p>"I don’t need to."</p>
<p>"You do."</p>
<p>"I don’t."</p>
<p>"You do."</p>
<p>"I don’t."</p>
<p>"You do."</p>
<p>Renzhi sighed.</p>
<p>Across the table, Eric nearly choked trying not to laugh.</p>
<p>Mei rolled her eyes while Lianhua merely smiled and continued eating.</p>
<p>Rin pointed dramatically "Nobody appreciates my talent."</p>
<p>"You haven’t demonstrated any talent," Eric replied.</p>
<p>"That’s because you lack vision."</p>
<p>"I lack patience."</p>
<p>The siblings immediately started bickering again.</p>
<p>Normally Renzhi would have found the scene amusing but tonight... not so much.</p>
<p>His phone vibrated causing Renzhi to glance down.</p>
<p>His expression darkened immediately he saw who was calling. He instantly cut it.</p>
<p>A few seconds later it rang again but he cut it once more.</p>
<p>The third time, he didn’t even look before rejecting it.</p>
<p>His irritation steadily grew while across the table, Lianhua noticed. "Work?"</p>
<p>Renzhi forced a smile. "Something like that."</p>
<p>While dinner continued, the phone rang again and again and again...</p>
<p>Renzhi’s patience began evaporating.</p>
<p>Just when he was considering turning the device off entirely, the doorbell rang.</p>
<p>The entire table paused wondering who could here by this time.</p>
<p>Several moments later, one of the maids entered the dining room.</p>
<p>She bowed respectfully. "Sir."</p>
<p>Renzhi looked up. "What is it?"</p>
<p>The maid hesitated. "There is someone here to see you."</p>
<p>Immediately, a bad feeling settled into his stomach.</p>
<p>"Who?"</p>
<p>"They wouldn’t provide a name."</p>
<p>Renzhi’s eyes narrowed... Slowly pushing his chair back, he stood. "I’ll handle it."</p>
<p>He proceeded to walk out and the further he moved from the dining room... the worse his mood became.</p>
<p>By the time he reached the main reception hall, he already knew what he was going to find.</p>
<p>And unfortunately... he was right.</p>
<p>Bloodstain sat comfortably on one of the expensive couches as though he owned the place.</p>
<p>The moment he spotted Renzhi, he smiled.</p>
<p>Renzhi nearly lost his mind. "What the hell are you doing here?"</p>
<p>Several maids nearby immediately pretended not to hear anything.</p>
<p>Renzhi grabbed Bloodstain’s arm. "Outside."</p>
<p>Moments later the two men stood near the front gardens away from the house itself.</p>
<p>The moment they were alone, Renzhi exploded. "What the fuck is wrong with you?"</p>
<p>Bloodstain remained calm. "I’ve been trying to contact you."</p>
<p>"And I haven’t been answering."</p>
<p>"Exactly."</p>
<p>Renzhi rubbed his forehead. "Which should have told you something."</p>
<p>Bloodstain’s expression hardened. "Suddenly none of my friends want anything to do with me."</p>
<p>Renzhi groaned. "It’s not like that."</p>
<p>"Then what is it?"</p>
<p>"Don’t come around my family."</p>
<p>His voice lowered. "I don’t bring awakened business anywhere near them."</p>
<p>Bloodstain laughed. "Funny."</p>
<p>"What?"</p>
<p>"I remember saying something similar once."</p>
<p>Renzhi wasn’t amused.</p>
<p>Bloodstain’s smile disappeared. "I need help."</p>
<p>The words came out with a more serious tone than before and for the first time, Renzhi actually studied him carefully.</p>
<p>Bloodstain looked rough, exhausted and angry.</p>
<p>"My gang is falling apart."</p>
<p>Renzhi crossed his arms. "And what exactly do you expect me to do about that?"</p>
<p>Bloodstain stepped forward. "Help me."</p>
<p>"I can’t."</p>
<p>"Why not?"</p>
<p>"Because I shouldn’t even be associated with you."</p>
<p>Bloodstain’s eyes narrowed. "You’re the one who told me to go after that Shadow Reapers brat."</p>
<p>Renzhi immediately looked around. "Lower your fucking voice."</p>
<p>The last thing he needed was anyone overhearing this conversation.</p>
<p>Bloodstain continued anyway. "Now look where it got me."</p>
<p>Renzhi shook his head. "There is absolutely no way Shadow Reapers is responsible for everything that happened."</p>
<p>Bloodstain suddenly started laughing bitterly.</p>
<p>"That’s exactly the kind of thinking that got me here."</p>
<p>Renzhi frowned while Bloodstain continued. "I really underestimated them."</p>
<p>"Everyone underestimated them."</p>
<p>"Now look at the result."</p>
<p>For a moment neither spoke... then Bloodstain’s expression became cold. "I need to hit them back."</p>
<p>Renzhi immediately shook his head. "No."</p>
<p>"I need—"</p>
<p>"No."</p>
<p>Bloodstain stared at him and Renzhi met his gaze without flinching. "I cannot help you attack Shadow Reapers."</p>
<p>"I’ll get dragged into something I don’t want any part of."</p>
<p>Bloodstain voice dropped as he stepped closer. "You could get dragged into something much worse if I tell people who sent me after West in the first place."</p>
<p>A dangerous beat of silence as Renzhi’s eyes hardened. "Are you threatening me?"</p>
<p>Bloodstain didn’t answer but the tension between them became almost suffocating.</p>
<p>Eventually Renzhi looked away. If that information ever surfaced, it could create problems he didn’t need.</p>
<p>After several moments, he sighed. "I cannot help you directly."</p>
<p>Bloodstain remained silent while Renzhi continued. "I can’t touch Shadow Reapers."</p>
<p>"I can’t move against them."</p>
<p>"I can’t publicly support you."</p>
<p>He paused briefly before adding. "But perhaps I can assist financially."</p>
<p>•••</p>
<p>Meanwhile, back inside the house, dinner was still ongoing.</p>
<p>Completely unaware of the argument occurring outside, Rin was still talking.</p>
<p>In fact, she seemed physically incapable of stopping.</p>
<p>At one point she somehow managed to switch topics four times within a single sentence.</p>
<p>Even Eric looked impressed.</p>
<p>Lianhua eventually turned toward Mei with a playful smile on her face. "So."</p>
<p>Mei looked up from her phone. "Oh no..."</p>
<p>"How are things with your boyfriend?" Lianhua asked.</p>
<p>Rin practically teleported into the conversation. "YES!"</p>
<p>Eric groaned while Mei covered her face. "Mom."</p>
<p>"What?"</p>
<p>"Don’t."</p>
<p>"Why not?"</p>
<p>"Because she’s going to make it weird."</p>
<p>Rin pointed at herself. "Correct."</p>
<p>Lianhua laughed. "No, really."</p>
<p>She leaned forward slightly. "How are things with Smith?"</p>
<p>Mei’s expression softened. "They’re good."</p>
<p>"Just good?"</p>
<p>Mei immediately blushed. "Well... more than good."</p>
<p>Everyone immediately went; "Ohhhhhhh."</p>
