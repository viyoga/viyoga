<p>Bloodstain froze. "What?"</p>
<p>Another operator hurried over. "The ruin teams!"</p>
<p>Bloodstain’s eyes widened as a genuine look of disbelief appeared on his face.</p>
<p>The operator rapidly explained. "Six teams have already completed their ruin expeditions."</p>
<p>Bloodstain nodded. "And?"</p>
<p>"And they got ambushed while returning."</p>
<p>The room suddenly felt colder as Bloodstain stared at him.</p>
<p>The operator swallowed nervously. "They cleared their ruins successfully."</p>
<p>"They secured loot."</p>
<p>"They loaded everything into their convoys."</p>
<p>"Then while traveling back..."</p>
<p>His voice dropped. "...they were attacked."</p>
<p>A vein bulged on Bloodstain’s forehead. "No."</p>
<p>The operator nodded miserably. "Unfortunately."</p>
<p>Another screen lit up and convoy route appeared.</p>
<p>The location marker was blinking red.</p>
<p>~ LOST CONTACT ~</p>
<p>Bloodstain clenched his fists. "Who attacked them?"</p>
<p>"We don’t know."</p>
<p>"What do you mean you don’t know?!"</p>
<p>The operator flinched. "Survivors reported they were overwhelmed before they could identify everyone."</p>
<p>Bloodstain’s fury intensified while one of the communications officers raised a trembling hand.</p>
<p>"Boss..."</p>
<p>The woman immediately regretted speaking as soon as Bloodstain turned.</p>
<p>"Three teams have been successfully ambushed while three are currently under attack."</p>
<p>Bloodstain slowly looked toward the communications station and the room went silent.</p>
<p>A transmission was still active causing loud static noises to fill the speakers.</p>
<p>Loud panicked voices emerged next.</p>
<p>"LEFT SIDE!"</p>
<p>"DODGE!"</p>
<p>"AHHHHH!"</p>
<p>An explosion rang out causing everyone to stiffen.</p>
<p>Bloodstain rushed forward. "Report!"</p>
<p>The voice on the other end sounded exhausted as they rough breathing rang out. They were clearly injured.</p>
<p>"Boss!"</p>
<p>"Who is attacking you?"</p>
<p>There was no answer from the other end... only chaos as a loud impact rang out.</p>
<p>Loud screams followed causing Bloodstain to roar. "I SAID WHO IS ATTACKING YOU?"</p>
<p>An answer finally came from one of the other ends.</p>
<p>"We don’t know!"</p>
<p>"What?"</p>
<p>"We don’t know who they are!"</p>
<p>More explosions rang out and a man screamed.</p>
<p>"Randal!!!"</p>
<p>"He’s down!"</p>
<p>"Damn it! Who are these fuckers!"</p>
<p>"How dare you mess with Iron Vultur... arrghhh!"</p>
<p>Bam! BOOM!</p>
<p>As the crashing and explosive sounds continued to ring out, another voice shouted through the comms.</p>
<p>"It’s two awakened!"</p>
<p>"And two beasts!"</p>
<p>Another loud explosion went off and the transmission crackled.</p>
<p>Then the final voice came through. "ARRRGHHHH—"</p>
<p>The connection died afterwards, leaving Bloodstain utterly dumbfounded.</p>
<p>Nobody dared speak because everyone knew what that silence meant... that entire team had been defeated.</p>
<p>Which also meant that another batch of ruin loot had been stolen.</p>
<p>Bloodstain slowly lowered the communicator with shaky hands.</p>
<p>His hands weren’t trembling from fear at the moment... they were trembling from rage.</p>
<p>The room also began quaking as several terminals flickered resulting in one monitor cracking.</p>
<p>BOOM!</p>
<p>Bloodstain fist smashed through a reinforced workstation causing a bunch of computers to explode.</p>
<p>Operators screamed and jumped backward.</p>
<p>"What the fuck is happening?!" His voice echoed throughout the entire operations center.</p>
<p>Nobody had an answer because nobody understood what was going on.</p>
<p>Everything was well coordinated as though somebody had predicted every single move Iron Vultures would make.</p>
<p>Bloodstain suddenly remembered something.</p>
<p>The looted headquarters... the stolen resources... the mysterious infiltrator... the ten ruin permits... the ambushes.</p>
<p>One thought entered his mind at this moment... SHADOW REAPERS.</p>
<p>’Could it be them?’ The thought immediately made him angrier.</p>
<p>Because how could such a small gang cause him this much trouble? Impossible.</p>
<p>His right-hand man arrived moments later after being summoned.</p>
<p>The moment he entered, he could tell things were bad.</p>
<p>Bloodstain pointed toward the screens.</p>
<p>"Look."</p>
<p>The man did and his face immediately changed.</p>
<p>"Four successful ambushes?"</p>
<p>Bloodstain nodded. "Two ongoing."</p>
<p>The man cursed while Bloodstain slammed his palm against a table.</p>
<p>"We need to warn the others."</p>
<p>The operators immediately began to work.</p>
<p>Communications were attempted... channels were opened... signals sent.</p>
<p>But it was all for nought.</p>
<p>One operator nervously adjusted his headset. "Boss..."</p>
<p>"What?"</p>
<p>"The remaining teams are still inside their ruins."</p>
<p>Bloodstain already knew where this was going.</p>
<p>The operator continued. "We can’t contact them."</p>
<p>A curse escaped Bloodstain’s lips.</p>
<p>Of course they were well aware that Ruins blocked communication.</p>
<p>Once a team entered a ruin, they were effectively isolated from the outside world which meant every remaining expedition was walking blind toward whatever trap awaited them once they were done clearing their respective ruins.</p>
<p>Bloodstain knuckles whitened as he grabbed the edge of a table.</p>
<p>Somewhere out there, someone was systematically dismantling his organization and he couldn’t stop it.</p>
<p>His expression hardened. "No... I won’t let them run things to the ground for me."</p>
<p>Bloodstain pointed toward the operators. "Contact every available member outside."</p>
<p>"Every patrol."</p>
<p>"Every escort."</p>
<p>"Every warehouse guard."</p>
<p>"Every business front."</p>
<p>His voice became colder.</p>
<p>"Recall all of them."</p>
<p>The operators immediately began working sending calls and messages out.</p>
<p>The right-hand man frowned. "If we pull everyone back—"</p>
<p>"We need manpower." Bloodstain interrupted him. "Now."</p>
<p>The man reluctantly nodded. Bloodstain was the boss after all.</p>
<p>Several minutes later, responses started coming in.</p>
<p>Some teams acknowledged while others reported they were already en route but many couldn’t leave immediately and ohers were far away.</p>
<p>Gathering everybody would take time.</p>
<p>The realization only worsened Bloodstain’s mood but he would use whoever was available at the moment.</p>
<p>His eyes slowly drifted toward the display of maps with red markers all over.</p>
<p>Another transmission arrived and an operator immediately stiffened. "Boss."</p>
<p>Bloodstain turned. "What?"</p>
<p>The operator swallowed. "We’ve lost contact with another team."</p>
<p>•••</p>
<p>•••</p>
<p>Several hundred kilometers away, a young woman burst into laughter as she ducked beneath a flaming projectile.</p>
<p>"Damn!"</p>
<p>She rolled across the ground before springing back to her feet.</p>
<p>"This is insane!"</p>
<p>The woman couldn’t have been older than twenty.</p>
<p>She had short dark-green hair and an energetic face filled with excitement rather than fear. Even now, in the middle of a battle, she looked like she was having the time of her life.</p>
<p>"I just joined this gang!" She dodged another attack. "The first thing I get involved in is a gang war!"</p>
<p>A grin spread across her face. "This is awesome!"</p>
<p>A voice immediately barked from nearby. "Pay attention, rookie."</p>
<p>Hart slid beneath a blade before driving his shoulder into an Iron Vultures member’s chest hard enough to launch the man backward.</p>
<p>The newly recruited Shadow Reaper laughed. "Yes sir."</p>
