<p>West stopped after taking a few steps forward and looked around casually, taking note of the money, operations, workers...</p>
<p>"We had a deal," his voice echoed across the surroundings as he spoke.</p>
<p>"I paid for your land..." he took another step forward.</p>
<p>"...and yet you pulled this move..."</p>
<p>"...and restricted my construction."</p>
<p>The entire room remained silent for a bit but Viktor who had momentarily stiffened, quickly regained composure.</p>
<p>He removed the cigar from his lips and smirked.</p>
<p>"That’s how business works, kid."</p>
<p>He gestured lazily.</p>
<p>"You didn’t complete payment."</p>
<p>"So..."</p>
<p>"...pay up."</p>
<p>West chuckled softly and then began walking toward the tables where stacks of money was neatly arranged.</p>
<p>He ran his fingers lightly across one bundle to another.</p>
<p>"You have a lot of guts for an ordinary human."</p>
<p>His tone remained light but sounded almost amused.</p>
<p>He stopped before turning slightly.</p>
<p>"This is your last chance."</p>
<p>His eyes locked onto Viktor.</p>
<p>"Set things straight."</p>
<p>"Revoke what you did."</p>
<p>"Grant me access back to my land... and maybe..."</p>
<p>His lips curved faintly.</p>
<p>"I’ll leave you with just a light spank on the hand."</p>
<p>A brief silence ensued before loud laughter rang out from Viktor. Vane joined in and the others followed.</p>
<p>"You talk big, kid."</p>
<p>Viktor leaned forward.</p>
<p>"But until you pay five million..."</p>
<p>"...nothing changes."</p>
<p>West let out a quiet exhale before responding; "...That’s a shame."</p>
<p>His voice dropped slightly.</p>
<p>"Because now..."</p>
<p>His gaze hardened.</p>
<p>"...I’ll be sending every single one of you to the hospital."</p>
<p>He paused before adding; "And maybe..."</p>
<p>"...a few to the morgue."</p>
<p>His eyes swept the room as he smiled faintly.</p>
<p>"Oh well... I’m sure the doctors will be happy to receive you."</p>
<p>One of the guards charged forward furiously; "Enough of this—!"</p>
<p>However, before his hand could even reach West,</p>
<p>BOOM!</p>
<p>A single fist slammed into the man’s chest, sending him flying across the hall like a ragdoll and crashing into the wall with bone-shattering force.</p>
<p>This singular action was just the start of the incoming chaos.</p>
<p>West moved faster than the eye could perceive, like a storm of destruction, landing punches and blows across the surroundings.</p>
<p>CRACK! BAM! BAM! BOOM! BANG!</p>
<p>Bones shattered, bodies flew and limbs bent unnaturally as men dropped one after another.</p>
<p>Screams filled the air, followed by rapid gunfire as bullets tore through space—</p>
<p>Unfortunately, it didn’t matter. Some of the bullets missed completely while the others that hit, only bounced off, clinking uselessly to the ground.</p>
<p>West weaved through the barrage, effortlessly, grabbed one man and slammed him into another.</p>
<p>He drove both into the ground before kicking another, sending him crashing through a table of cash.</p>
<p>Money scattered everywhere causing blood to mix with bills.</p>
<p>Within moments, the entire hall was wrecked and broken bodies littered the ground.</p>
<p>Now, only three remained standing; Viktor, Vane and one other henchman.</p>
<p>Vane swallowed hard with a shaky voice.</p>
<p>"...Looks like you were wrong, boss..."</p>
<p>"...He’s an awakener."</p>
<p>West stepped forward slowly and with each step, his footsteps echoed across the surroundings, striking fear in the hearts of those who were left.</p>
<p>Viktor clenched his fists but forced a smirk.</p>
<p>"You don’t scare me," he spoke with a louder tone, trying to mask the tension.</p>
<p>"You better stop while you still can."</p>
<p>He leaned forward.</p>
<p>"Just because you’re an awakener... doesn’t mean you can mess with me."</p>
<p>He paused before adding; "I have awakeners under my command too."</p>
<p>He laughed again but clearly it was lacking the same confidence as before.</p>
<p>"Hahaha... you really don’t understand the world, kid."</p>
<p>"Whether you have super powers or not— Nothing beats the power of money."</p>
<p>He pointed at West.</p>
<p>"An awakener working for me is already on his way... He’ll deal with you."</p>
<p>West smiled in response before speaking in a cold tone.</p>
<p>"Oh?" He tilted his head slightly.</p>
<p>"...But can he get to you..." he took a step closer. "...before I do?"</p>
<p>Viktor’s laughter stopped instantly as his face froze... and for the first time, real fear set in.</p>
<p>In the next moment, Viktor turned to run, causing his expensive leather shoes to squeak sharply against the concrete floor as panic overtook whatever composure he had left.</p>
<p>The confident smirk, the arrogance and the belief that money ruled all things had all shattered in the presence of something far more primal... fear.</p>
<p>Behind him, West didn’t rush, he simply stepped forward.</p>
<p>However, Vane acted on reflex, yanking out his glock with trembling hands and raising it toward West.</p>
<p>"Stay—!"</p>
<p>He didn’t get to finish before West’s hand moved, clamping around Vane’s wrist mid-raise.</p>
<p>The gun never even aligned properly because no matter how much he tried, he couldn’t move the arm... West’s grip was just too strong.</p>
<p>There was a brief pause... before West suddenly twisted.</p>
<p>CRACK!</p>
<p>A loud crack echoed like a snapped branch in a silent forest.</p>
<p>Vane’s arm bent backward at an unnatural angle with the bone visibly jutting beneath the skin as his scream tore through the hall.</p>
<p>"AAAAAAGHHHHHHHH—!!"</p>
<p>His body collapsed instantly with his knees hitting the ground as the gun clattered uselessly away. Tears streamed down his face as he clutched his ruined arm, writhing in agony.</p>
<p>West didn’t spare him another glance because something else was happening up ahead.</p>
<p>When Viktor reached the wall behind, a hidden panel slid open with a mechanical hiss, revealing a concealed passage.</p>
<p>Viktor dove inside without hesitation and the door slammed shut just as West took a step forward.</p>
<p>THUD!</p>
<p>The reinforced steel barrier locked into place, sealing access.</p>
<p>Viktor’s voice suddenly echoed through hidden speakers, laced with relief and regained arrogance.</p>
<p>"Hahahaha! This is a reinforced room! Nothing can get in! Not even awakeners!"</p>
<p>Within the sealed space, Viktor leaned heavily against the wall, panting with a pale face that slowly twisting back into a grin.</p>
<p>Screens flickered on around him, displaying multiple camera angles of the warehouse floor.</p>
<p>West stood outside right before the wall while Viktor laughed again.</p>
<p>"Now what? You think you can touch me? The awakener under my payroll is already on his way! You’re finished!"</p>
