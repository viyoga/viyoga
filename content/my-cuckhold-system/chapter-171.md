<p>The interior of the Zu Li’s office was nothing short of luxurious. The walls were lined with ancient artifacts and modern displays alike as they entered her office.</p>
<p>It was spacious and elegant with a wide desk at the center and large glass panels behind it overlooking the entire estate.</p>
<p>Zu Li walked ahead and sat casually on the edge of her desk.</p>
<p>West leaned against a nearby pillar.</p>
<p>"...So?"</p>
<p>She looked at him.</p>
<p>"...You didn’t come for training this week."</p>
<p>West rolled his eyes slightly.</p>
<p>"I told you before."</p>
<p>"I’m not actually your apprentice."</p>
<p>"We’re just pretending, remember?"</p>
<p>Zu Li clicked her tongue.</p>
<p>"...Hmph."</p>
<p>"Fine."</p>
<p>She crossed her legs.</p>
<p>"Then how many now?"</p>
<p>West thought for a second.</p>
<p>"...Nine."</p>
<p>She raised a brow.</p>
<p>"The last one makes it nine ruins cleared."</p>
<p>"One more..."</p>
<p>"And I’m eligible."</p>
<p>Zu Li nodded slowly.</p>
<p>"...Ten ruins."</p>
<p>"That’s the minimum requirement to officially become a gang leader."</p>
<p>West shrugged.</p>
<p>"Which means until then..."</p>
<p>"Shadow Reapers stays on pause."</p>
<p>West didn’t sound bothered. If anything, he sounded patient because at the very least, he was gathering experience. There was still a lot about ruins that he was discovering along the way.</p>
<p>Zu Li watched him for a moment.</p>
<p>"...You’ve been busy."</p>
<p>West smirked slightly.</p>
<p>"Clearing ruins."</p>
<p>"Training newbies."</p>
<p>"Playing your ’apprentice’..."</p>
<p>"Working for your gang without actually being in it."</p>
<p>Zu Li chuckled.</p>
<p>"...You say that like you’re not benefiting."</p>
<p>West didn’t deny it.</p>
<p>"Fair enough."</p>
<p>She leaned back slightly.</p>
<p>"...You’ve grown."</p>
<p>West glanced at her.</p>
<p>"...I’d like to say you too but you still seem the same."</p>
<p>She laughed softly at that while West spoke again.</p>
<p>"For the next one..."</p>
<p>"I want to bring Jax and Nina along."</p>
<p>Zu Li’s expression changed a bit.</p>
<p>"...Hmm."</p>
<p>"They’ll slow you down."</p>
<p>West shrugged.</p>
<p>"They need the experience."</p>
<p>"And I’ll handle anything serious."</p>
<p>She studied him for a moment before sighing.</p>
<p>"...Fine."</p>
<p>"But—"</p>
<p>Her tone sharpened slightly.</p>
<p>"You’re coming for training this week."</p>
<p>"Whether you’re a fake apprentice or not."</p>
<p>"You still need it."</p>
<p>West groaned slightly.</p>
<p>"...You’re persistent."</p>
<p>"And you’re stubborn," she shot back.</p>
<p>A brief silence passed before West responded.</p>
<p>"...Alright."</p>
<p>"I’ll come."</p>
<p>Zu Li smirked.</p>
<p>"Good."</p>
<p>West pushed himself off the pillar.</p>
<p>"...Anything else?"</p>
<p>She shook her head.</p>
<p>"Go."</p>
<p>He turned and began walking toward the door.</p>
<p>But just before he left, her voice stopped him.</p>
<p>"Are you sure you’re ready to be a gang leader? It’s a whole different world... a treacherous path..."</p>
<p>West glanced back slightly.</p>
<p>"I know. Whatever may come... I’ll face it... as I always have."</p>
<p>She smiled while watching him walk out.</p>
<p>"And you have me as well..." She muttered with a low voice.</p>
<p>As West stepped out into the corridor, awakeners who passed by slowed, glanced and whispered expressions of envy.</p>
<p>...</p>
<p>...</p>
<p>West returned to his apartment later that evening, settling into the well furnished space.</p>
<p>It felt less like a temporary hideout and more like a base of operations now. Everything reflected a quiet kind of luxury.</p>
<p>But right now, none of that mattered.</p>
<p>With a low exhale, he loosened his collar and headed straight for the bathroom. The bathtub there was easily one of his favorite parts of the apartment.</p>
<p>It was a wide marble basin with smooth edges, large enough to stretch out completely.</p>
<p>He turned the tap and Warm water poured in causing steam to slowly rise and curl into the air like ghostly fingers. Within minutes, the room was filled with a soft haze.</p>
<p>West stepped in and the heat embraced him instantly, sinking into his muscles, washing away the fatigue from constant training, fighting, planning... everything.</p>
<p>He leaned back, closing his eyes.</p>
<p>For a moment, there was silence and peace until a faint ripple disturbed the water.</p>
<p>West didn’t open his eyes immediately... he already knew what was causing this disturbance.</p>
<p>"Can’t let me get one moment of peace, eh?" he muttered.</p>
<p>A soft, amused and sultry voice echoed beside him.</p>
<p>"You looked... too relaxed to resist," she voiced with a teasing tone.</p>
<p>When West finally opened his eyes, she was already half-submerged in the water with her serpentine features blending elegantly with her human form. As always, her facial expressions carried something dangerous... and something alluring.</p>
<p>Her fingers trailed lightly across the surface of the water as she leaned in closer.</p>
<p>"You push yourself too much," her gaze locked onto his as she added.</p>
<p>West smirked faintly. "And you’re here to fix that?"</p>
<p>"Maybe."</p>
<p>The space between them closed slightly as the atmosphere grew heavier and charged with tension.</p>
<p>Seraphyra fingers slowly trailed across West’s thighs underneath the water.</p>
<p>However, before things could deepen further, the door creaked open slightly.</p>
<p>Gor’thala stood there with her arms crossed, clearly unimpressed.</p>
<p>"If this is relaxation," she said bluntly, "you’re doing it inefficiently."</p>
<p>West laughed under his breath. "You want in too?"</p>
<p>She took a step forward but before she could take another.</p>
<p>Ting~ Ting~</p>
<p>All three paused as loud buzzing sound rang out.</p>
<p>West sighed, dragging a hand down his face. "Of course..."</p>
<p>He stepped out, quickly drying off and throwing on something casual before heading to the door.</p>
<p>When he opened it, he found his neighbor standing there.</p>
<p>She looked slightly flustered, brushing a strand of hair behind her ear.</p>
<p>"Oh—hi, um... sorry to bother you," Mrs Smith said quickly. "I just... I need help moving something and my husband isn’t home yet."</p>
<p>West leaned casually against the doorframe, a faint smile forming.</p>
<p>"Heavy?" he asked.</p>
<p>"Very."</p>
<p>He chuckled. "Alright. Lead the way."</p>
<p>—</p>
<p>Her apartment was just a few doors down.</p>
<p>As soon as he stepped in, West noticed the difference in atmosphere. It was more... lived-in. There were family photos, toys tucked into corners and the quiet signs of everyday life.</p>
<p>"The kids are still at school," she explained. "I didn’t want to wait too long."</p>
<p>West nodded while she led him to the object in question... a large, bulky cabinet.</p>
<p>Without hesitation, he lifted it.</p>
<p>The woman blinked in surprise. "W—wait, that easily?"</p>
<p>West just smirked. "Perks of working out."</p>
