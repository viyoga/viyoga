<p>Silence followed as dust hung thickly in the air.</p>
<p>The blue glow of the cavern flickered faintly through settling particles.</p>
<p>West stared at the rubble while slowly running a hand down his face.</p>
<p>"...Damn."</p>
<p>No going back that way.</p>
<p>He turned forward again toward the massive drop-off ahead.</p>
<p>The gargantuan cavern below still waited.</p>
<p>"Guess we’re moving forward."</p>
<p>He glanced at the two Verdant Pyre Behemoths.</p>
<p>Both were scorched in places from the corrosive splashes where the greenish creature had burned them.</p>
<p>The injuries weren’t fatal but West didn’t want to leave it be.</p>
<p>This was a large ruin... who knew what awaited?</p>
<p>West exhaled slowly and pulled out the Chalice of Vitality from his inventory.</p>
<p>The ancient cup gleamed faintly in the dim crystalline light. Its surface was engraved with winding sigils that that glowed and throbbed ike a heartbeat. Even holding it felt... alive.</p>
<p>He clicked his tongue upon recalling that the item required liquid... and there was none here.</p>
<p>There was no stream, n condensation... not even dripping cave water.</p>
<p>He looked around once, just to confirm.</p>
<p>"...Sigh..."</p>
<p>He needed to heal the Behemoths and himself.</p>
<p>He reached toward his side instinctively and felt the wetness on his finger...</p>
<p>He looked down at the dark red color that had dyed his fingers...</p>
<p>The spike from earlier had torn through his clothes after all. The wound hadn’t seemed deep during the fight, but now that adrenaline had faded, he could feel the burn.</p>
<p>Blood was steadily trickling down his waist.</p>
<p>West stared at it and then his eyes sharpened...</p>
<p>"...Liquid."</p>
<p>He let out a quiet laugh.</p>
<p>"Blood is liquid too."</p>
<p>The Verdant Pyre Behemoths rumbled low, sensing the shift in his aura.</p>
<p>Without hesitation, West slid his fingers into the wound, digging in.</p>
<p>His face tightened as he dragged his finger across the injury, reopening the half-clotted tear. Fresh blood welled up immediately, spilling faster.</p>
<p>Pain shot through his nerves but he ignored it.</p>
<p>He positioned the Chalice beneath the wound and dark red drops fell into it one by one.</p>
<p>Drip~</p>
<p>Drip~</p>
<p>Drip~</p>
<p>The engraved sigils along the Chalice began glowing faintly as the blood pooled within.</p>
<p>West waited as more blood flowed and even pressed harder against the wound to accelerate it.</p>
<p>After a short while, there was enough pooled at the bottom for a few small sips.</p>
<p>The Chalice made a low winding sound as West lifted it.</p>
<p>For a brief second, he wondered if this was insane... but nonetheless, he took a sip.</p>
<p>The blood had already transformed inside the cup.</p>
<p>What touched his lips wasn’t metallic or warm, it was cool and pure... like drinking distilled life itself.</p>
<p>Immediately, a warm current spread through his body, racing along his veins like a flood of golden light.</p>
<p>The torn flesh at his side knit together instantly as muscle fibers reconnected and skin sealed flawlessly.</p>
<p>Even lingering fatigue from the battle washed away like it had never existed.</p>
<p>Within seconds, he was whole.</p>
<p>West exhaled slowly, lowering the Chalice.</p>
<p>"...Worth it."</p>
<p>He walked toward the Verdant Pyre Behemoths.</p>
<p>Both beasts lowered their heads slightly in acknowledgment.</p>
<p>He held the Chalice up to the first one.</p>
<p>"Small sip."</p>
<p>The Behemoth carefully lowered its massive snout, surprisingly delicate for something twice the size of a mammoth.</p>
<p>It drank and the green flames along its paws flared brightly as the scorched patches along its hide faded.</p>
<p>Burn marks vanished. Its posture straightened as vitality surged back into its massive frame.</p>
<p>West moved to the second and the same result repeated after it took a single sip.</p>
<p>When he looked back into the Chalice, it was empty... not a single drop remained.</p>
<p>The glow faded to a dormant shimmer and West nodded approvingly before storing it back in his inventory.</p>
<p>Just when he was about to keep moving, something caught his peripheral vision.</p>
<p>A faint glint near the collapsed rubble sealing the tunnel behind them.</p>
<p>He frowned.</p>
<p>"That wasn’t there before."</p>
<p>He was certain of it.</p>
<p>The explosion had obliterated the green creature completely...</p>
<p>Or so he thought.</p>
<p>He approached the debris carefully.</p>
<p>There, partially lodged between fallen stones, was a tiny green stone no longer than a finger.</p>
<p>Unlike the creature’s corrosive sheen, this stone looked solid and crystalline...</p>
<p>West crouched and picked it up and the moment it left the rubble, A system notification flashed before his eyes.</p>
<p>---</p>
<p>[Item Acquired]</p>
<p>Name: Fragment of the Dissolution Core</p>
<p>Rarity: Rare</p>
<p>Type: Matter-Destabilizing Relic</p>
<p>Description: A condensed remnant left behind by an unstable phase-entity. Contains trace destabilizing properties capable of disrupting structural cohesion in both organic and inorganic matter.</p>
<p>Uses:</p>
<p>Crafting component for destabilization-based weaponry</p>
<p>Catalyst for advanced alchemical reactions</p>
<p>Potential integration into energy-conductive systems</p>
<p>May enhance lightning or vibration-based abilities</p>
<p>Warning: Prolonged direct contact may result in material degradation.</p>
<p>---</p>
<p>West’s eyes gleamed.</p>
<p>"So you did leave something behind."</p>
<p>He rotated the fragment between his fingers and smirked before storing it away.</p>
<p>Then he turned toward the massive drop-off ahead.</p>
<p>The ledge cut off abruptly, revealing a vast lower expanse at least seventy feet below. The terrain beneath looked entirely different with strange crystalline vegetation mixed with patches of dark stone plains.</p>
<p>West leapt lightly onto the back of one Verdant Pyre Behemoth, stand upright with his arms crossed coolly as wind from the cavern currents brushed his hair back.</p>
<p>The other Behemoth positioned itself beside them.</p>
<p>"Let’s go."</p>
<p>The massive beast took three powerful strides and jumped.</p>
<p>For a brief moment, they were suspended mid-air as they descended seventy feet down.</p>
<p>The cavern wind roared around them and then...</p>
<p>BOOM!</p>
<p>They landed heavily, cracking the stone beneath their paws and causing dust to explode outward in a ring.</p>
<p>Without slowing, they charged forward into the unknown stretch of land, disappearing into the vast crystalline wilderness.</p>
<p>---</p>
<p>Elsewhere in the third layer, Seraphyra hummed cheerfully to herself.</p>
<p>Unlike West’s path of violent confrontation, her route had been delightfully uneventful.</p>
<p>Her long serpentine body glided effortlessly through the cavern tunnels with her tail swaying lazily.</p>
<p>Every few steps—</p>
<p>"Oh~ Master will like this."</p>
<p>She plucked a glowing mineral from a wall.</p>
<p>A strange fang embedded in rock? Taken.</p>
<p>A glowing crystal? Taken.</p>
<p>A fossilized claw? Taken.</p>
<p>Her collection had grown... substantial.</p>
<p>Eventually, the narrow path widened into a massive chamber.</p>
<p>Seraphyra paused as her eyes narrowed slightly.</p>
<p>The chamber felt eerie...</p>
<p>Strange creature skulls were embedded into the surrounding walls. Some were elongated, some horned while others were zig-zagged with unnatural fangs.</p>
<p>At the center stood an altar and buried in its heart was a massive axe.</p>
<p>Its blade was half as tall as a man, etched with deep runic grooves. The haft was thick, dark, and looked forged from an unknown material.</p>
