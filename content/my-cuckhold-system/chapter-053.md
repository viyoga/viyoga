<p>West lay back on his bed with one arm tucked behind his head, staring at the faint glow of the system interface hovering above him like a second ceiling.</p>
<p>For the first time since the ruins incident, his mind wasn’t racing because of danger, fear, or adrenaline.</p>
<p>It was racing because of possibilities.</p>
<p>"...There’s a lot to do," he muttered in an excited, slightly unhinged way.</p>
<p>He mentally counted them off, lifting a finger for each point like he was organizing his life on an invisible whiteboard.</p>
<p>First.</p>
<p>He needed to make another summon. A proper one this time. Not a god with an ego the size of a continent who treated him like lint on her divine robe.</p>
<p>He needed a summon he could actually contract with.</p>
<p>Something that wouldn’t erase half a ruin chamber just to make a point.</p>
<p>Second...</p>
<p>The shop.</p>
<p>That cursed, beautiful shop.</p>
<p>He had over three hundred Cuck Points now.</p>
<p>Now he could get more skills, passives, enhancements, stat boosts... if he planned it right, he could push himself far beyond what a normal Tier 1 should look like.</p>
<p>And judging by how Raze and the Iron Chain boys reacted earlier?</p>
<p>He already was.</p>
<p>Third...</p>
<p>The inventory.</p>
<p>West’s eyes sharpened.</p>
<p>This one wasn’t just about power... it was about money.</p>
<p>He reached out mentally and tapped the familiar option.</p>
<p>&gt; [INVENTORY OPENED]</p>
<p>Items unfolded before him in neat, glowing rows, hovering slightly as if waiting to be judged.</p>
<p>West sat up.</p>
<p>"Alright," he murmured. "Let’s see what I dragged out of hell."</p>
<p>---</p>
<p>&lt;[ Inventory Overview ]&gt;</p>
<p>Rotars Florks ×13</p>
<p>Dusk Crystal ×5</p>
<p>Helo Metal Scrap – 6kg</p>
<p>Drifting Ash – 1kg</p>
<p>Affinity Stones ×2</p>
<p>Chalice of Vitality</p>
<p>Dark Menace (Ruin Guardian Weapon)</p>
<p>West whistled softly.</p>
<p>"Yeah... that’s a lot of loot."</p>
<p>Even without knowing exact prices of some of these materials, his instincts told him this was the kind of haul gangs killed each other over.</p>
<p>The Affinity Stones were the only things he was already confident about. Even the lowest-quality ones could sell for absurd amounts, and these had come straight from a residential ruin... meaning purity was likely high.</p>
<p>He planned to keep at least one but the other... would certainly fetch him insane money.</p>
<p>His gaze drifted to the Chalice of Vitality, hovering calmly like it knew it was special.</p>
<p>"...You’re staying with me," he said decisively.</p>
<p>That thing wasn’t just valuable... it was game-breaking. Continuous regeneration. Long-term bodily enhancement. Something like that wasn’t meant to be sold casually.</p>
<p>If it ever left his possession, it would only be in exchange for something equally absurd.</p>
<p>Then there was the sword.</p>
<p>Dark Menace.</p>
<p>Even just the name radiated menace.</p>
<p>West flexed his fingers unconsciously, remembering the weight of it. The way his arms had trembled. The way a single careless swing had cut a monster in half like paper.</p>
<p>"...Yeah. You’re not normal either."</p>
<p>But the truth was simple.</p>
<p>He couldn’t keep everything.</p>
<p>He needed liquidity.</p>
<p>He needed options.</p>
<p>And most importantly—</p>
<p>He needed information.</p>
<p>West closed the inventory and reached for his phone.</p>
<p>It was time to go down the Awakened Internet Rabbit Hole</p>
<p>The moment he typed keywords like "ruin materials value," "rotars flork uses," and "selling awakened loot legally," his screen exploded.</p>
<p>Forums... Private boards... News articles... Redacted guides... Shady listings... Half-illegal marketplaces hidden behind invitation-only networks.</p>
<p>West’s eyes widened.</p>
<p>"Oh... this is deep."</p>
<p>Deeper than he expected.</p>
<p>He quickly realized something important.</p>
<p>There was the public awakened economy—regulated markets, licensed brokers, official auctions.</p>
<p>And then there was the real awakened economy.</p>
<p>The one gangs used.</p>
<p>The one fixers whispered about.</p>
<p>The one where things didn’t always get receipts.</p>
<p>West clicked carefully, cross-referencing everything.</p>
<p>---</p>
<p>Item Breakdown of the information West managed to garner:</p>

<p>→ Biological ruin resource.</p>
<p>Primary Use:</p>
<p>Crafting Material</p>

<p>Rotars Florks are organic ruin-growths that naturally absorb ambient energy within unstable environments. When refined, they act as high-efficiency catalysts, amplifying energy flow during crafting, forging, alchemy, and ritual-based processes.</p>
<p>Also used in stimulants, sensory-enhancing drugs.</p>
<p>Applications:</p>
<p>Enhances success rate of weapon and armor crafting</p>
<p>Used as a power stabilizer for energy cores and devices</p>

<p>Commonly used in illegal enhancement serums due to their responsiveness to awakened energy</p>
<p>West’s eyebrows climbed.</p>
<p>"Good to know."</p>
<p>Dusk Crystals</p>
<p>→ Mid-tier energy crystals used in night-aligned constructs, shadow-tech, and illusion-based gear.</p>
<p>→ Five pieces could fetch a clean price if sold to the right buyer.</p>
<p>He nodded slowly.</p>
<p>"Okay... these are solid."</p>
<p>Helo Metal Scrap (6kg)</p>
<p>→ Extremely durable ruin alloy. Often reforged into weapons or armor cores.</p>
<p>→ Scrap quality, but quantity made up for it.</p>
<p>West grinned.</p>
<p>"That explains why it felt like lifting a dead fridge."</p>
<p>Drifting Ash (1kg)</p>
<p>→ High-risk, high-value material. Used in curse dispersal, entropy rituals, and certain forbidden enhancements.</p>
<p>→ Selling required permits... or connections.</p>
<p>West leaned back.</p>
<p>"...Yeah, I’m not touching that yet."</p>
<p>Then came the big ones again.</p>
<p>Affinity Stones</p>
<p>→ He already knew.</p>
<p>→ Auction-tier items.</p>
<p>→ Potentially life-changing money.</p>
<p>And finally—</p>
<p>Dark Menace (Ruin Guardian Weapon)</p>
<p>→ This one made him pause.</p>
<p>He searched carefully but information was sparse.</p>
<p>There were only fragments and rumors...</p>
<p>Screenshots of forum posts that vanished hours later...</p>
<p>Apparently, weapons that survived the destruction of their guardian were... rare.</p>
<p>Very rare.</p>
<p>They often carried lingering will, latent abilities, or compatibility requirements.</p>
<p>Some awakened couldn’t even hold such weapons without suffering backlash.</p>
<p>West swallowed.</p>
<p>"...Yeah. Definitely not selling you blindly."</p>
<p>He locked his phone and exhaled slowly.</p>
<p>"So yeah. I could make a fortune."</p>
<p>The realization settled into his bones but he’d have to make one move at a time. He couldn’t be brash about his next step.</p>
<p>After spending hours going down the rabbit hole, his gaze drifted back to the shop menu as he thought of the fourth thing he needed to try out.</p>
<p>His gaze drifted specifically, to one item...</p>
<p>The Awakening Voucher.</p>
<p>West stared at it longer than necessary upon realizing that it was still available for purchase.</p>
<p>"...This is insane," he muttered because the thought running through his mind was something catastrophic.</p>
<p>If the system truly allowed him to still purchase awakening vouchers even after he already awakened...</p>
<p>Then...</p>
<p>Perhaps he could use them on other people...</p>
<p>West’s pulse quickened.</p>
<p>"Why join a gang..."</p>
<p>When he could create one?</p>
