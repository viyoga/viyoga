<p>Bootsteps echoed as a group of gang members hurried into the chamber from one of the tunnels. They had just arrived from chasing Seraphyra.</p>
<p>The moment they stepped into the open chamber, they froze.</p>
<p>"What... the hell happened here?"</p>
<p>Their eyes swept across the floor. Bodies were everywhere with some torn apart and others crushed. The brutal aftermath of battle was unmistakable.</p>
<p>One of them crouched beside a corpse and turned it over.</p>
<p>"...These are natives..."</p>
<p>A chill crawled up several spines.</p>
<p>Even hardened gang members weren’t used to seeing so many dead at once but the shock only lasted a few seconds before anger replaced it.</p>
<p>Their gazes snapped toward the center of the chamber.</p>
<p>Seraphyra stood there calmly beside another person.</p>
<p>In her human form she looked almost serene with her black and purple hair resting over her shoulders.</p>
<p>"That’s her!" one of the gang members spat. "The bitch that interfered!"</p>
<p>Several others raised their weapons immediately.</p>
<p>"Doesn’t matter what happened," another growled. "We deal with her first."</p>
<p>West slowly stepped forward, positioning himself in front of Seraphyra as henarrowed his eyes.</p>
<p>"What happened?" He questioned.</p>
<p>"That bitch attacked us!" One shot back.</p>
<p>Seraphyra’s eyes flicked over the group with mild indifference.</p>
<p>"There is no need for assumptions," she said calmly.</p>
<p>Her voice echoed through the cavern.</p>
<p>"They were attempting to molest a school girl."</p>
<p>The gang members blinked.</p>
<p>"A... what?" West eyes narrowed while Jax scratched the back of his head.</p>
<p>"Hold up... school girl?"</p>
<p>Seraphyra didn’t answer verbally, instead she simply pointed.</p>
<p>A few feet away on the ground lay a large bundle. It was an enormous piece of shed serpentine skin tied together like a makeshift sack.</p>
<p>It was the same skin Seraphyra had used to carry the loot she collected along the way but now something inside it moved slightly.</p>
<p>West’s eyes narrowed before widening as he rushed forward.</p>
<p>The shed skin was thick and rubbery, but he tore it open quickly.</p>
<p>Inside lay a girl.</p>
<p>Her uniform was slightly torn from earlier struggle and her breathing uneven as she slowly stirred awake.</p>
<p>West froze.</p>
<p>"...Nina?"</p>
<p>At that exact moment her eyes fluttered open.</p>
<p>For a second they were unfocused before locking onto him.</p>
<p>"West...?"</p>
<p>Her voice trembled.</p>
<p>"You’re alive..."</p>
<p>Before he could react, she suddenly sat up and wrapped her arms around him tightly.</p>
<p>West stiffened in surprise as she hugged him.</p>
<p>"You’re alive," she repeated softly, almost in disbelief.</p>
<p>Jax blinked from the side.</p>
<p>"Well... this is unexpected."</p>
<p>After a brief moment Nina finally pulled back, though her hands still gripped his shoulders.</p>
<p>"What happened?" West asked. "Why are you here?"</p>
<p>"You shouldn’t even be in the ruins."</p>
<p>She looked down briefly before answering.</p>
<p>"I came to warn you."</p>
<p>West frowned.</p>
<p>"Warn me about what?"</p>
<p>"I overheard the guards talking to those gang members," she said quietly. "You were in danger."</p>
<p>"So you followed me into the ruins?" West stared at her.</p>
<p>"You’re not even awakened, Nina. How could you risk your life like that?"</p>
<p>She hesitated before displaying a soft smile.</p>
<p>"For you... I’d risk anything."</p>
<p>West didn’t know how to respond to that.</p>
<p>Seraphyra spoke again from nearby.</p>
<p>"When I found her," she explained calmly, "these gang members had already discovered she was following them."</p>
<p>Her golden eyes grew colder.</p>
<p>"They were manhandling her."</p>
<p>A brief pause followed.</p>
<p>"They intended to perform non-consensual acts."</p>
<p>West’s expression changed instantly as his eyes darkened.</p>
<p>He slowly turned toward the surrounding gang members.</p>
<p>Of course... gang members...</p>
<p>People like them had no morals.</p>
<p>They lived outside the normal rules of society. If something happened in the ruins, no one asked questions. Even on the surface, they could be brazen because they were awakened.</p>
<p>To them, a defenseless girl was simply an opportunity.</p>
<p>The gang members meanwhile had regained their confidence.</p>
<p>They were no less than fifty people in the chamber and they were all Tier 1 Awakened...</p>
<p>And at the back stood two figures whose aura was noticeably stronger.</p>
<p>Tier 2 bosses.</p>
<p>One of them stepped forward.</p>
<p>"What gang do you all represent?" he demanded.</p>
<p>His gaze moved between West and Seraphyra.</p>
<p>"Hand that bitch over."</p>
<p>His eyes then shifted to the scattered loot nearby and all the corpses of creatures in the surrounding.</p>
<p>"And hand over whatever treasure you’ve collected."</p>
<p>The rest of the gang members smirked.</p>
<p>They had numbers and strength so it didn’t matter which gang they represented.</p>
<p>The situation was even more dire because West was exhausted... he had barely recovered from his earlier battles.</p>
<p>On the surface the situation looked extremely unfavorable.</p>
<p>West slowly stepped forward fearlessly anyway.</p>
<p>Behind him Seraphyra let out a low giggle as her body began to change.</p>
<p>In seconds her human form dissolved into her true shape.</p>
<p>A massive serpentine body coiled across the stone floor with ashy emerald scales.</p>
<p>Several gang members jumped back instinctively.</p>
<p>"A summon?!"</p>
<p>Realization spread quickly.</p>
<p>"So this bitch is your summon," one of them sneered. "You’ll pay for her crimes."</p>
<p>Before they could move further, a heavy metallic step echoed.</p>
<p>The armored knight moved forward, placing herself beside West.</p>
<p>Her voice rang in a cold and absolute tone.</p>
<p>"Anyone who touches my master shall lose their limbs."</p>
<p>At the same time the ground trembled slightly.</p>
<p>Gor’thala lumbered forward from the other side. Her towering green structure scanned the crowd.</p>
<p>"The one who first makes a move," she rumbled, "shall die where they stand."</p>
<p>The air suddenly felt heavier as these three terrifying auras spread across the chamber.</p>
<p>Seraphyra’s coiled body radiated lethal venomous intent...</p>
<p>The knight stood like an immovable wall of steel...</p>
<p>Gor’thala’s monstrous presence felt like a crushing mountain...</p>
<p>And at the center of it all was West...</p>
<p>Several gang members instinctively stepped back.</p>
<p>Someone swallowed loudly.</p>
<p>"Wait... three summons?"</p>
<p>Another man eyes widened as he suddenly realized something.</p>
<p>"You have three summons?"</p>
<p>His gaze snapped to West.</p>
<p>"How is that possible? You’re just a kid."</p>
<p>Murmurs spread rapidly.</p>
<p>"You can’t be Tier 3..."</p>
<p>"Tier 3 summoners barely exist."</p>
<p>"There’s no way someone that young could be one."</p>
<p>The realization unsettled them.</p>
<p>If he really was Tier 3...</p>
<p>Then everyone here might die...</p>
<p>One of the bosses scoffed.</p>
<p>"Don’t panic," he said confidently. "Look at him."</p>
<p>He pointed at West.</p>
<p>"He’s too young to even be Tier 2."</p>
<p>"Don’t fall for the bluff."</p>
<p>West tilted his head slightly.</p>
<p>"Is that so?"</p>
<p>The boss smirked.</p>
<p>"If you were truly Tier 3, you would’ve wiped us out already. Powerful people erase others over minor inconveniences."</p>
<p>"Instead you’re standing there talking."</p>
<p>"Clearly you’re bluffing."</p>
<p>West suddenly chuckle echoed quietly through the cavern.</p>
<p>"Perhaps you’re right," he said calmly.</p>
<p>"But there’s something you haven’t taken into account."</p>
<p>The gang members frowned.</p>
<p>"What’s that?"</p>
<p>West’s smile grew slightly.</p>
<p>"The gang I represent."</p>
<p>A brief pause followed as he continued.</p>
<p>"I am from the Dragon Fang Gang."</p>
<p>The words alone caused several expressions to stiffen.</p>
<p>But he wasn’t finished.</p>
<p>"Miss Zu Li’s personal apprentice."</p>
<p>Complete silence instantly enveloped the surroundings as fear appeared across dozens of faces.</p>
<p>Even the two bosses stiffened.</p>
<p>They all knew that name...</p>
<p>Zu Li.</p>
<p>A monster.</p>
<p>Someone whose reputation alone was enough to make smaller gangs avoid conflict entirely.</p>
<p>If someone truly belonged to her inner circle... they were untouchable.</p>
<p>Suddenly the earlier arrogance vanished.</p>
<p>"W-wait...!"</p>
<p>Some of the gang members hurriedly lowered their weapons while others deactivated their branches.</p>
<p>"Apologies!"</p>
<p>"We didn’t realize who you were!"</p>
<p>"You can keep the loot!"</p>
<p>"Please don’t mention this to Miss Zu Li!"</p>
<p>Panic spread rapidly through their ranks.</p>
<p>Even though they technically had the advantage, no one dared take the risk.</p>
<p>If Zu Li discovered they harmed her apprentice...</p>
<p>They were dead men.</p>
<p>More terrifying was the thought that she might already know.</p>
<p>Surely someone that talented wouldn’t be sent alone.</p>
<p>They imagined hidden guards watching from the shadows, observing.</p>
<p>The thought alone made them sweat.</p>
<p>The thought that let a Tier 1 kid to clear the third layer of a ruin alone as training and he actually succeeded?</p>
<p>What kind of monster would he become later?</p>
<p>The gang members quickly began retreating.</p>
<p>"Sorry for the misunderstanding!"</p>
<p>"We’ll leave immediately!"</p>
<p>Within moments the chamber emptied as their footsteps faded down the tunnels.</p>
<p>Silence returned and then, Jax let out a long whistle.</p>
<p>"...That worked way better than I expected."</p>
<p>Seraphyra slowly returned to her human form while West exhaled quietly.</p>
<p>Truthfully, he had been bluffing but it had worked perfectly.</p>
<p>And although it did, he really felt a kind of way for pushing Miss Zu Li away so many times only to end up using her in a troubling predicament.</p>
<p>’I’ll have to repay her later...’</p>
<p>After a moment he looked down at Nina again.</p>
<p>"Can you walk?"</p>
<p>She nodded weakly.</p>
<p>"I think so."</p>
<p>"Good."</p>
<p>He helped her stand.</p>
<p>With the gang gone, there was nothing left to do down here.</p>
<p>It was time to return...</p>
<p>As the group began preparing to leave, West’s eyes drifted briefly to the battlefield behind them.</p>
<p>Bodies still lay on the circular platform where the butcher slaughtered humans...</p>
<p>For a moment he considered collecting the body parts of the fallen victims but then he shook his head.</p>
<p>Bringing those remains back would only traumatize the families even more.</p>
<p>Some things were better left here.</p>
<p>This raid had technically been a success but he already knew what awaited them above.</p>
<p>When news spread about what happened in the ruins tonight...</p>
<p>There would be mourning.... families would cry.</p>
<p>And the city would remember once again that the ruins always demanded a price.</p>
