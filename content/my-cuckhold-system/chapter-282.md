<p>The right hand man tilted his head slightly while Bloodstain answered the question himself.</p>
<p>"Zu Li."</p>
<p>That answer immediately shut down further arguments.</p>
<p>"The moment West dies inside my base, Dragon Fang will come knocking."</p>
<p>He tapped his fingers against the desk as he continued. "And unlike some people, I have no interest in picking a fight with that woman."</p>
<p>The right-hand man reluctantly nodded.</p>
<p>Bloodstain stood and walked toward the large window overlooking the compound.</p>
<p>"However..." his eyes darkened. "Running his gang into the ground is a different matter entirely."</p>
<p>The right-hand man smiled slightly while Bloodstain continued.</p>
<p>"Double the manpower following..."</p>
<p>"All transport routes."</p>
<p>"Every shipment."</p>
<p>"Every vehicle."</p>
<p>"Every warehouse."</p>
<p>The man frowned. "Isn’t that excessive?"</p>
<p>Bloodstain looked over his shoulder. "We underestimated them once."</p>
<p>His expression hardened as he added. "We won’t make that mistake again."</p>
<p>The right-hand man finally nodded. "I’ll get it done."</p>
<p>•••</p>
<p>•••</p>
<p>Back at Shadow Reapers headquarters, Aria had transformed one of the conference rooms into a temporary intelligence center.</p>
<p>Documents covered the table, maps filled several screens and personnel reports were stacked into neat folders.</p>
<p>West sat at the head of the table while Aria stood beside a large digital display.</p>
<p>Jax leaned against the wall nearby, Nina sat quietly reviewing information and Vanya moved in and out delivering additional reports.</p>
<p>Aria took a breath before beginning.</p>
<p>"Iron Vultures currently has four hundred and thirty-two awakened members."</p>
<p>The display changed, showing gang statistics.</p>
<p>"They’re ranked eighty-ninth in the city."</p>
<p>Another screen appeared displaying financial records, a few territories, known warehouses, business fronts, number of ruins cleared and estimated income.</p>
<p>West studied it carefully and for the first time, he was seeing the complete picture.</p>
<p>Iron Vultures wasn’t simply larger than Shadow Reapers, they were vastly larger.</p>
<p>Shadow Reapers had barely crossed into double-digit membership while Iron Vultures had hundreds.</p>
<p>Their resources dwarfed what Shadow Reapers currently possessed and yet, West wasn’t worried.</p>
<p>Because one truth remained... the bigger gang had more to lose.</p>
<p>The city currently had one hundred and forty-four registered gangs and the top one hundred were considered established organizations while the ones below hundred were considered street level.</p>
<p>Shadow Reapers had climbed rapidly but they still had a long way to go.</p>
<p>Jax crossed his arms. "Now that you’ve gone and confronted them directly..."</p>
<p>He looked toward West.</p>
<p>"...wouldn’t that just make them more cautious?"</p>
<p>West smiled. "Exactly."</p>
<p>Jax blinked in confusion. "That’s... good?"</p>
<p>"Very."</p>
<p>West leaned back in his chair. "The moment I walked into that office, Bloodstain started thinking defensively."</p>
<p>He pointed toward the map. "He’s expecting retaliation."</p>
<p>Nina slowly nodded in understanding. "Which means, he’ll reinforce everything."</p>
<p>"Correct," West stood and approached the display.</p>
<p>His finger moved across several locations.</p>
<p>"More guards."</p>
<p>"More patrols."</p>
<p>"More escorts."</p>
<p>"More surveillance..."</p>
<p>Jax scratched his head. "That sounds bad for us."</p>
<p>West shook his head. "No."</p>
<p>A grin slowly appeared as he added. "That’s exactly what I want."</p>
<p>The others looked confused while West continued. "When people prepare for an attack, they focus resources toward the places they think are vulnerable."</p>
<p>Understanding slowly appeared on Aria’s face as West pointed toward several highlighted locations.</p>
<p>"Transport routes."</p>
<p>"Warehouses."</p>
<p>"Convoys."</p>
<p>"Business fronts."</p>
<p>"He’ll reinforce all of those."</p>
<p>Jax’s eyes widened slightly. "Oh."</p>
<p>West nodded. "Which means manpower gets diverted."</p>
<p>Now even Nina was smiling slightly.</p>
<p>West folded his arms. "The more resources he dedicates toward defending expected targets..."</p>
<p>He tapped a final location on the map. "...the weaker he becomes elsewhere."</p>
<p>The display zoomed in at Iron Vultures Headquarters.</p>
<p>Jax immediately burst out laughing. "No way."</p>
<p>West smiled. "Yes way."</p>
<p>For several moments everyone stared at the image of the massive Iron Vultures compound.</p>
<p>Aria shook her head. "You walked into his office just to make him focus on the wrong thing."</p>
<p>West shrugged. "Partly."</p>
<p>Jax grinned. "Damn."</p>
<p>West’s smile widened. "The truck wasn’t the real objective anymore."</p>
<p>"Now the real objective, is to acquire way more than what was lost."</p>
<p>•••</p>
<p>•••</p>
<p>For the next several days, everything unfolded exactly the way Bloodstain wanted.</p>
<p>Transport trucks were no longer moving around with a handful of escorts. Convoys that previously had three or four awakened guards now had ten.</p>
<p>Warehouses received double security rotations, business fronts had additional personnel stationed nearby and known transport routes were constantly monitored.</p>
<p>Even some of their hidden storage facilities had received reinforcements.</p>
<p>If Shadow Reapers intended to retaliate, then Bloodstain was determined to make them bleed for it.</p>
<p>The first three days passed peacefully and nothing happened.</p>
<p>There was no sabotage, ambushes, mysterious accidents, stolen shipments or dead members.</p>
<p>By the fifth day, some of the gang members were starting to relax...</p>
<p>By the seventh day, many had already concluded that Shadow Reapers had backed down.</p>
<p>Even Bloodstain himself was beginning to wonder if perhaps West had simply realized how large the gap between their organizations truly was.</p>
<p>After all, Iron Vultures had more than four hundred awakened members while Shadow Reapers barely had a fraction of that.</p>
<p>"Maybe the kid had finally understood reality."</p>
<p>Even so, Bloodstain wasn’t foolish enough to completely lower his guard.</p>
<p>"Maintain current security."</p>
<p>That was the standing order and nobody argued.</p>
<p>The problem was that maintaining such heightened security was expensive.</p>
<p>Personnel were being stretched thin and people were working longer shifts.</p>
<p>Operations that usually required ten members now had twenty.</p>
<p>Everything was costing more money but Bloodstain viewed it as a necessary expense.</p>
<p>It was better to be safe than sorry.</p>
<p>...</p>
<p>On the tenth day, Bloodstain was seated in his office reviewing reports.</p>
<p>The massive desk before him was covered in paperwork and most of it was the usual.</p>
<p>Territory disputes, resource acquisitions, debt collections and several ruin reports.</p>
<p>He was halfway through reading one report when the office door burst open.</p>
<p>A young awakened practically stumbled into the room with a pale and sweaty face.</p>
<p>Bloodstain immediately frowned. "What?"</p>
<p>The man swallowed nervously. "Boss... we have a problem."</p>
<p>Bloodstain hated hearing those words especially lately.</p>
<p>His mood immediately worsened. "What problem?"</p>
<p>"It’s the logistics department."</p>
<p>Bloodstain rose from his chair. "Explain."</p>
<p>The man shook his head. "You need to see it yourself."</p>
