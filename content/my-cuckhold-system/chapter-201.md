<p>At the final stretch, their cars were neck and neck.</p>
<p>Heading for the last turn...</p>
<p>As they approached the snaking path, Mei orchestrated a perfect drift, drawing slightly ahead of West.</p>
<p>West tried to catch up, but the drift had put her slightly ahead, leaving his car a few inches behind hers just as they crossed the finish line.</p>
<p>WINNER: MEI</p>
<p>She exhaled, letting out a breath that she didn’t realize she had been holding.</p>
<p>"...I won."</p>
<p>West leaned back slightly, smirking.</p>
<p>"Yeah. You did."</p>
<p>He looked at her.</p>
<p>"So."</p>
<p>"What’s your wish?"</p>
<p>Mei held his gaze for a moment before stepping closer.</p>
<p>"...Close your eyes."</p>
<p>West raised a brow.</p>
<p>"That’s the wish?"</p>
<p>She nodded.</p>
<p>"...Trust me."</p>
<p>West paused briefly with a look of suspicion before he closed them.</p>
<p>The noise in the background of the arcade grew louder as he waited but West was powerful enough to feel movement in his surroundings so he knew she was drawing closer. He just didn’t know why.</p>
<p>All of a sudden, he felt something warm and soft pressed against his lips.</p>
<p>At first, it was light as if testing...</p>
<p>West opened one eye slightly, confirming what it was before closing it back as she lingered against him.</p>
<p>Their lips stayed pressed together for a few seconds and West hand came up lightly, resting against her waist as the moment deepened before she finally pulled away.</p>
<p>West opened his eyes slowly to see that Mei was already looking at him.</p>
<p>There was a faint blush on her face but her gaze didn’t waver.</p>
<p>"...That was my wish."</p>
<p>West smiled faintly.</p>
<p>"...Good choice."</p>
<p>She looked at him.</p>
<p>"...Was that okay?"</p>
<p>"Yeah, although a little warning next time. I might have bitten you cos your lips taste nice," West winked at her while speaking in a slightly hushed tone.</p>
<p>She chuckled shyly before punching his shoulder lightly. "Stawwpp..."</p>
<p>And from that point, the dynamic between them had changed.</p>
<p>It looked like West’s plan was finally moving forward.</p>
<p>—</p>
<p>Days later, West showed up at Renzhi’s house again unannounced.</p>
<p>The door swung open and Lianhua there stood in a short flair yellow gown with a look of surprise.</p>
<p>"Oh—"</p>
<p>She blinked in confusion.</p>
<p>"Our session isn’t until—"</p>
<p>West cut in casually.</p>
<p>"Yeah, I know... tomorrow."</p>
<p>She paused briefly before asking;</p>
<p>"...Then what are you doing here?"</p>
<p>West slipped his hands into his pockets.</p>
<p>"I’m not here for you."</p>
<p>Her brows furrowed slightly.</p>
<p>"...What?"</p>
<p>"I’m here for your Mei, your daughter."</p>
<p>Footsteps rang out from within as Mei appeared from down the hallway.</p>
<p>The moment she saw West, her face lit up.</p>
<p>"You came."</p>
<p>West smiled slightly.</p>
<p>"Of course."</p>
<p>Without hesitating, she walked straight up to him and kissed him right in front of her mother.</p>
<p>Her mother froze completely, watching as the two merged lips passionately.</p>
<p>West grabbed Mei’s waist, dominantly, pressed her body into his and took her lips between his intensely while keeping on eye open and locked onto Lianhua.</p>
<p>"...Mei—" Lianhua’s eyes remained wide in surprise.</p>
<p>Mei pulled back and grabbed West’s hand—</p>
<p>"Come on," she started dragging him toward her room.</p>
<p>"See you later, mom."</p>
<p>She dragged him up the stairs and after a few seconds, the sound of a door shutting, rang out, leaving the mother standing there speechless.</p>
<p>"They’re... Smith and my daughter are..."</p>
<p>A strange sensation welled up within her as she touched her chest.</p>
<p>"This feeling... am I..."</p>
<p>"Jealous..."</p>
<p>She couldn’t believe that such a feeling could exist within her... and it wasn’t the man she was married to that triggered such a feeling... it was her personal fitness instructor.</p>
<p>...</p>
<p>Upstairs, the atmosphere changed the moment Mei closed her bedroom door behind them.</p>
<p>Her room was... a reflection of her.</p>
<p>It was large and spacious, yet filled with personality in every corner.</p>
<p>Her soft pastel walls were decorated with a mixture of cute posters, fantasy artwork, and framed sketches she had clearly drawn herself. Shelves lined one side of the room, packed with collectibles ranging from small crafted figurines to neatly arranged books; novels, sketch guides, history books, e.t.c.</p>
<p>A gaming setup sat near the window with dual monitors while fairy lights draped across the ceiling gave the entire space a warm, cozy glow.</p>
<p>It was both girly and nerdy in perfect harmony.</p>
<p>West glanced around, mildly impressed.</p>
<p>"...Didn’t expect this."</p>
<p>Mei beamed, clearly proud.</p>
<p>"Of course you didn’t. People always underestimate me."</p>
<p>She walked ahead, barefoot, turning slightly to face him and that was when he really noticed what she was wearing.</p>
<p>She was clad in a pair of soft, fitted shorts that hugged her hips perfectly, stopping mid-thigh, paired with a light, slightly oversized top that slipped just enough to reveal her smooth collarbone and the subtle curve beneath.</p>
<p>The fabric clung gently to her frame, accentuating her figure without trying too hard.</p>
<p>She was dressed casually but undeniably attractively.</p>
<p>Her hair was loosely tied with a few strands falling around her face, giving her that effortless, at-home charm.</p>
<p>West’s gaze lingered for a second longer than necessary and Mei noticed it.</p>
<p>"See something you like?" a smile lit up her face as she asked.</p>
<p>West smirked faintly.</p>
<p>"Maybe."</p>
<p>She rolled her eyes playfully.</p>
<p>"Come on, let me show you something."</p>
<p>She dragged him toward her shelves, excitedly pointing out her collections that she had built over the years.</p>
<p>She pointed at some rare items she had hunted down and even some handmade crafts she had designed herself.</p>
<p>"This is a limited edition series... only ten exists in the whole world..."</p>
<p>"This one took me weeks," she added while holding up a small intricately carved piece.</p>
<p>"And this—don’t laugh—I tried to recreate it from memory."</p>
<p>West chuckled lightly.</p>
<p>"It’s actually good."</p>
<p>She paused before smiling.</p>
<p>"...Thanks."</p>
<p>Time passed easily between them as they talked, laughed and even ended up sitting by her gaming setup, playing a quick match of something she insisted he try.</p>
<p>"You’re terrible at this," she teased.</p>
<p>"I just started," West replied calmly.</p>
<p>"Excuses."</p>
<p>"You talk too much."</p>
<p>"And you’re still losing."</p>
<p>Their banter flowed naturally like they had known each other far longer than they actually had.</p>
<p>—</p>
<p>Downstairs, Lianhua paced back and forth cross the massive living room.</p>
<p>Her heels clicked softly against the floor as her thoughts ran wild.</p>
<p>’What could they be doing up there...?’</p>
