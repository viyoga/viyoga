<p>Vyre grinned while stretching his glowing violet sword toward West who was standing up ahead, staring at them with an unbothered expression.</p>
<p>He moved instantly with a burst of speed and slashed.</p>
<p>A glowing arc of violet energy tore through the air toward West.</p>
<p>Fwwhiiiii~</p>
<p>West leaned sideways.</p>
<p>The arc sliced through the ground behind him, carving a deep trench.</p>
<p>Vyre attacked again.</p>
<p>Slash.</p>
<p>Slash.</p>
<p>Slash.</p>
<p>Energy arcs flew relentlessly.</p>
<p>West moved, dodging the attacks to the best of his abilities.</p>
<p>Every strike missed by inches causing Vyre to narrow his eyes.</p>
<p>He raised his sword overhead, preparing a stronger attack.</p>
<p>West suddenly accelerated at this precise moment, vanishing from Vyre’s sight.</p>
<p>Vyre’s pupils shrank.</p>
<p>’Too fast...’</p>
<p>A fist appeared directly in front of his face.</p>
<p>He didn’t even see it coming.</p>
<p>BOOM.</p>
<p>His face imploded inward from the impact causing teeth to explode from his mouth.</p>
<p>Blood sprayed into the air as his body lifted and flew several feet across the air.</p>
<p>He spun repeatedly and crashed violently across the courtyard floor unmoving.</p>
<p>Silence returned as the students stared in absolute disbelief.</p>
<p>One awakened defeated.</p>
<p>Caleb beaten repeatedly.</p>
<p>And West...</p>
<p>Was still standing there calmly.</p>
<p>Not even breathing hard.</p>
<p>Icarus stared at him now with interest. The look of disdain had completely vanished from his face.</p>
<p>Phones were up with voices layered over each other, like the whole school had become one giant mouth that couldn’t stop talking.</p>
<p>"West is actually insane!"</p>
<p>"Bro, that other dude got folded!"</p>
<p>"Caleb got punched like a shopping cart!"</p>
<p>"Wait—so is West awakened or not?!"</p>
<p>Icarus didn’t rush in.</p>
<p>He stared at West, then at Vyre on the ground, then back at West again like his eyes needed to confirm reality.</p>
<p>His chains dangled loosely from his hands, glowing blue and crackling faintly, like they were impatient.</p>
<p>"...You awakened?" Icarus asked slowly, "No way you just beat two awakened up."</p>
<p>West didn’t answer the question.</p>
<p>He didn’t even nod.</p>
<p>He glanced at the crowd, glanced at the phones, glanced back at Icarus like the whole situation was a minor inconvenience.</p>
<p>Then he sighed.</p>
<p>"You lot are delaying me," he said while rolling his shoulders. "I have somewhere to be."</p>
<p>His gaze sharpened slightly, like a blade being pulled from a sheath.</p>
<p>"Don’t waste any more of my time. Come and get your share of ass whooping."</p>
<p>The crowd made a sound at once—half laughter, half shock.</p>
<p>Someone yelled, "HE SAID ASS WHOOPING!"</p>
<p>Another voice: "WEST IS HIM!"</p>
<p>A teacher’s faint scream came from far away: "WHO IS RECORDING THIS—STOP—"</p>
<p>Icarus’ face twitched.</p>
<p>That twitch became a scowl.</p>
<p>His pride didn’t like being spoken to that way, not by some guy the school still couldn’t even decide was awakened or not.</p>
<p>"Just because you managed to beat them by some luck," Icarus snapped, "doesn’t mean you’ll do the same with me."</p>
<p>West’s eyes said: Talk less.</p>
<p>Icarus’ eyes said: Bet.</p>
<p>The glowing chains snapped outward.</p>
<p>WHIP—</p>
<p>The air sizzled as they cut through it.</p>
<p>West spun sideways, barely dodging, and the chains slapped the ground hard enough to crack it. He took a step forward to close in—</p>
<p>But the chains bent back like they had minds of their own.</p>
<p>West’s brows lifted just slightly.</p>
<p>"Okay," he muttered. "That’s annoying."</p>
<p>The chains whipped again, curving mid-flight, trying to catch his ribs.</p>
<p>West twisted away and in the process, his shirt brushed against the crackling blue light by inches.</p>
<p>He tried to rush in with a kick to the torso after evading but Icarus flicked his wrist.</p>
<p>The chains snapped sideways at a ridiculous angle.</p>
<p>SLAP—</p>
<p>The metal-light lash smacked into West’s side even though West had already moved.</p>
<p>He barely got his left arm up to block at the last moment, but the impact still threw him.</p>
<p>He slid across the ground on his shoes with his arm raised and his teeth clenched.</p>
<p>When he stopped, a thick steamed bruise bloomed across his forearm like heat rising off it like a kettle.</p>
<p>West stared at his arm, then stared at Icarus.</p>
<p>"...So it’s like that," he said quietly.</p>
<p>Icarus smiled, satisfied, like he’d finally made West "look human."</p>
<p>"That’s right," Icarus said, lifting his chains again. "Now you’re paying attention."</p>
<p>West exhaled and then he launched himself forward again.</p>
<p>Icarus didn’t retreat.</p>
<p>He spun the chains vigorously, turning them into a circular blur of blue light, like a living barrier.</p>
<p>Every time West tried to enter—</p>
<p>WHIP. WHIP. WHIP.</p>
<p>The chains slapped his shoulders, grazed his ribs, caught his thigh once, struck his side again.</p>
<p>Every strike kept him back and bruised his body.</p>
<p>West’s shoes squeaked as he shifted and pivoted, trying to find an opening that didn’t exist.</p>
<p>The crowd flinched at every hit.</p>
<p>"OHHH!"</p>
<p>"NAH THAT CHAIN IS CHEATING!"</p>
<p>"WEST MOVE!"</p>
<p>West bit down a groan and snapped his head toward Icarus with a look that said: I’m going to make you regret being smug.</p>
<p>Icarus saw that look and laughed.</p>
<p>"Still confident?" he mocked, sweeping his chains out again, aiming to wrap West completely this time.</p>
<p>West ducked, rolled and shifted.</p>
<p>He was evading, evading, evading— but also drifting.</p>
<p>Unnoticeably...</p>
<p>Step by step...</p>
<p>He wasn’t just avoiding...</p>
<p>He was repositioning.</p>
<p>Icarus didn’t notice because he was too busy enjoying himself.</p>
<p>West backed toward a pillar near the edge of the courtyard. It was one of those thick concrete supports for a raised walkway. He stopped beside it, breathing evenly with focused eyes.</p>
<p>Icarus lashed again, sweeping his chains wide and nearly wrapping around West’s torso.</p>
<p>West ducked at the last instant.</p>
<p>The chains flew past him and—</p>
<p>CLACK.</p>
<p>They wrapped around the pillar, hooking and knotting on themselves, tightening like a snake constricting prey.</p>
<p>Icarus pulled his wrist back instinctively to retract them but the chains didn’t move.</p>
<p>His grin faded as he pulled harder but still nothing.</p>
<p>"...What?"</p>
<p>West lifted his head slowly, with that an irritating "gotcha" look.</p>
<p>"Thanks for borrowing me your leash," West said, and before Icarus could process the insult—</p>
