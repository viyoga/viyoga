<p>"Not everyone enjoys bloodshed and violence, Mr. Rain."</p>

<p>"But you could make way more money."</p>

<p>"Not everything is about money."</p>
<p>Then she added quietly:</p>
<p>"I enjoy the lack of constant competition."</p>
<p>"No betrayal."</p>
<p>"No backstabbing."</p>
<p>"Just work."</p>
<p>West looked genuinely surprised as she continued calmly.</p>
<p>"I think I’m fine here."</p>

<p>Most awakeners chased power endlessly but she seemed content with something simple.</p>

<p>"I want to craft a weapon with this."</p>

<p>It was an address and a name.</p>
<p>"A specialist crafter."</p>
<p>"Reliable."</p>
<p>She slid the paper toward him and West picked it up.</p>
<p>"Thank uhm..."</p>
<p>He paused slightly.</p>
<p>"...I never got your name."</p>
<p>She looked at him quietly for a moment before answering</p>
<p>"Becca."</p>

<p>"Thanks, Becca."</p>

<p>West stepped out of the warehouse district and into the fading evening light.</p>
<p>The streets here were quieter than the main city areas, but that didn’t mean they were safe.</p>
<p>Places like that establishment naturally attracted predators... ad predators often looked for easy prey.</p>
<p>West walked casually down the street with his hands in his pockets and the black mask still covering the lower half of his face.</p>
<p>He didn’t have to turn around to know what was happening.</p>
<p>Four footsteps followed him from behind.</p>
<p>They were keeping their distance, trying to look casual but their intent was obvious.</p>
<p>West sighed internally.</p>
<p>"Again?"</p>
<p>Last time this happened, he had deliberately avoided trouble but this time, West had less patience and didn’t care...</p>
<p>West rounded a quiet corner between two old industrial buildings.</p>
<p>The four men following him turned the corner a moment later and instantly froze because they didn’t find West standing there.</p>
<p>Instead, gwo figures stood waiting.</p>
<p>Gor’thala and Seraphyra...</p>

<p>They blinked in surprise.</p>
<p>"...The hell?"</p>

<p>BOOM</p>
<p>Gor’thala’s fist connected with the first man’s stomach like a cannon.</p>
<p>The man flew backwards and crashed into a dumpster.</p>
<p>Seraphyra slammed the other into the wall with her tail, then flung him around, crashing him to another person.</p>
<p>The remaining two tried to activate their branches but they were too slow...</p>
<p>Gor’thala grabbed one by the collar and slammed him into the pavement.</p>
<p>Seraphyra flicked her wrist again, sending the last one skidding across the asphalt.</p>
<p>The fight lasted less than thirty seconds.</p>
<p>By the time West casually stepped around the corner, all four attackers were groaning on the ground.</p>
<p>Seraphyra dusted her hands.</p>
<p>"That was disappointing."</p>
<p>Gor’thala snorted.</p>
<p>"Barely worth the effort."</p>

<p>"W-we didn’t know—!"</p>
<p>"Sorry!"</p>

<p>"Good."</p>
<p>Seraphyra giggled.</p>
<p>"Should we have killed them?"</p>

<p>"Not necessary."</p>

<p>"Oh."</p>
