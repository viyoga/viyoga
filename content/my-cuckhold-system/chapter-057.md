<p>The man hesitated. "Mistress... you came here to rest. You said all the candidates you screened were disappointing. Do you really want to bring work into this trip?"</p>
<p>She didn’t answer immediately.</p>
<p>Instead, she watched West disappear into the crowd.</p>
<p>"I have a feeling," she said quietly, "that if I let him slip through my fingers... I’ll regret it."</p>
<p>---</p>
<p>Meanwhile, West was completely oblivious.</p>
<p>He returned to his room far later than planned, collapsing onto the bed with a satisfied groan.</p>
<p>The resort, it turned out, did not shut down at night.</p>
<p>Arcades, combat simulators, VR arenas, glow-lit pools, sky lounges...</p>
<p>West sampled far too many... By the time he finally slept, it felt like his body powered down instead of drifting off.</p>
<p>When he woke again, sunlight was already flooding the room.</p>
<p>"...Why do I feel like I fought a dungeon boss in my dreams," he muttered.</p>
<p>Today was Sunday... which meant, they still had one more day of fun left.</p>
<p>After a ridiculously good complimentary breakfast—actual portions this time, thank the heavens—West leaned back in his chair, content.</p>
<p>"I’m heading deeper into the resort today," he announced. "Games. Attractions. Possibly poor life choices."</p>
<p>Mark folded his napkin calmly. "I’ll come with you."</p>
<p>West nearly choked on his drink. "You’ll what?"</p>
<p>"I’ll come with you."</p>
<p>West stared. "...Dad."</p>
<p>"Yes?"</p>
<p>"You realize ’games’ here include simulated combat, aerial courses, and things that may legally classify as ’near-death experiences’, right?"</p>
<p>Mark smiled faintly. "I raised you, didn’t I?"</p>
<p>West squinted. "Are you sure you’ll be able to handle it, old man?"</p>
<p>Mark scoffed. "Watch your tone."</p>
<p>West grinned, standing up and stretching. "Alright then. Don’t say I didn’t warn you."</p>
<p>It just so happened that the very moment West and his father stepped out of their room, the universe decided to be funny.</p>
<p>Because right there... standing in the passageway like she had been waiting... was Elina.</p>
<p>She froze for half a second when she saw them, then immediately brightened up.</p>
<p>"Oh! What a coincidence!" she said cheerfully. "I didn’t know you guys were staying on this floor."</p>
<p>West stopped walking.</p>
<p>Internally, his brain tilted its head. ’Huh? I’m pretty sure I mentioned the 159th floor yesterday...’</p>
<p>Outwardly, he kept his expression neutral. "Yeah... wild coincidence."</p>
<p>Mark, completely unaware of the internal alarms going off in West’s head, smiled politely. "Good morning."</p>
<p>Elina returned the smile warmly before her attention snapped right back to West.</p>
<p>"So where are you guys headed?"</p>
<p>West shrugged. "We’re going deeper into the resort. Games. Attractions. Probably embarrassing my dad in public."</p>
<p>Mark scoffed. "You’re assuming you won’t embarrass yourself first."</p>
<p>Elina’s eyes lit up. "That sounds fun! Can I tag along?"</p>
<p>West blinked. "Didn’t you come up here to get something done?"</p>
<p>She waved a hand dismissively. "Already did."</p>
<p>"...That was fast."</p>
<p>She smiled sweetly. "Efficiency is a virtue."</p>
<p>West stared at her for a second longer than necessary, then sighed. "Alright. You can come."</p>
<p>As they walked, West couldn’t help but notice that Elina looked... different.</p>
<p>Yesterday, she’d been cute in a quiet, nerdy way. Glasses, minimal effort, more brain than flair.</p>
<p>Today?</p>
<p>She was dressed very cutely.</p>
<p>She didn’t wear anything outrageous... just a light, well-fitted outfit, subtle colors that complemented her figure, and a soft shade of makeup that made her eyes pop without screaming for attention. She still wore glasses, but now they felt like an accessory instead of a shield.</p>
<p>West noticed the increase in attractiveness.</p>
<p>Unfortunately for Elina, West also immediately shut that line of thought down.</p>
<p>He mentally reminded himself of Aria, Nina, Mira, and the general landmine his romantic life had become.</p>
<p>’Let’s not add another...’</p>
<p>Elina who was blissfully unaware of the internal battle she was losing, happily tagged along as they explored.</p>
<p>The resort didn’t disappoint.</p>
<p>They passed through neon-lit game halls, interactive holographic zones, zero-gravity lounges, and even an area where people sparred using padded exo-frames. Mark tried a few things, to West’s surprise, although he complained loudly, he didn’t back out.</p>
<p>"Why does everything here try to kill you?" Mark muttered after stepping out of a balance simulator.</p>
<p>"That’s the fun part," West replied.</p>
<p>Eventually, they arrived at one of the resort’s advanced team games.</p>
<p>A staff member explained the rules while strapping them into floating gliders and equipping them with protective gear.</p>
<p>These gliders were disk-shaped platforms that hovered above an artificial ice-like terrain suspended in midair.</p>
<p>The objective was simple.</p>
<p>A glowing golden ball floated freely above the field.</p>
<p>Two teams with five players each.</p>
<p>First team to transport the ball to the opposing side won.</p>
<p>Each player had their own glider, but teamwork was mandatory. Obstacles drifted through the air and the ball itself moved unpredictably—sometimes accelerating, sometimes darting away like it had a mind of its own.</p>
<p>West, Elina, and Mark were placed on the same team.</p>
<p>Mark looked around skeptically. "I feel like I’m about to break something important."</p>
<p>"Just don’t fall," West said. "That’s usually step one."</p>
<p>Before the game began, West pulled them close.</p>
<p>"Alright," he said quietly. "Dad, you defend. Stay back, block anyone who gets through."</p>
<p>Mark nodded. "Got it."</p>
<p>"Elina, center support. Keep eyes on the ball and the opponents. Call out movements."</p>
<p>She nodded enthusiastically. "Okay!"</p>
<p>"I’ll strike," one of the other teammates with a bald head stated.</p>
<p>The other teammate who was a teenage boy with green hair, claimed he’d support.</p>
<p>West decided to stay around the West side as one of the strikers.</p>
<p>The countdown began.</p>
<p>Three...</p>
<p>Two...</p>
<p>One...</p>
<p>The game exploded into motion.</p>
<p>At first, it was smooth.</p>
<p>Their team coordinated well. Mark held the defensive line surprisingly solid, Elina kept calling out positions, and West moved strategically rather than aggressively.</p>
<p>The score climbed evenly.</p>
<p>1–1.</p>
<p>2–2.</p>
<p>Then 3–3.</p>
<p>That’s when things got... rough.</p>
<p>The opposing team started getting aggressive.</p>
<p>Too aggressive.</p>
<p>One of their players slammed into West’s teammate even after the ball had been passed, sending him spinning out of control.</p>
<p>The referee hesitated but didn’t call it.</p>
<p>Then it happened again.</p>
<p>A sharp elbow disguised as momentum, sent their main striker crashing off his glider.</p>
<p>His body spiralled downwards until the safety system kicked in and removed him from the match.</p>
<p>West frowned.</p>
<p>"Seriously?"</p>
<p>That left them short-handed.</p>
<p>West exhaled slowly. "Alright. I’ll switch to main striker."</p>
