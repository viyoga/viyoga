<p>West pushed himself off the pillar, stepping forward slightly.</p>
<p>"You met us here first."</p>
<p>The man frowned.</p>
<p>"And?"</p>
<p>West’s smirk widened.</p>
<p>"Shadow Reapers."</p>
<p>The five exchanged glances.</p>
<p>"...Never heard of it," another one said with a scoff.</p>
<p>"Must be some unofficial gang."</p>
<p>The first one crossed his arms.</p>
<p>"We, on the other hand, are officially registered."</p>
<p>"Setting Sun Gang."</p>
<p>"So do yourselves a favor..."</p>
<p>"And leave."</p>
<p>Jax slowly stood up, gripping his katana.</p>
<p>"...You guys serious right now?"</p>
<p>Nina expression tightened as she rose as well while West raised a hand slightly.</p>
<p>"I’ll make this simple," he stated with a strong tone.</p>
<p>"Leave now...Before things get ugly."</p>
<p>The five stared at him and then began laughing hysterically with looks of mockery.</p>
<p>One of them cracked his knuckles.</p>
<p>"Ugly?" he sneered. "You think three kids can threaten us?"</p>
<p>Another stepped forward while activating his branch.</p>
<p>"Looks like we’ll have to teach you how things work."</p>
<p>The atmosphere immediately turned incredibly tensed... and just as West was about to move...</p>
<p>"Hold on."</p>
<p>Jax stepped forward and Nina followed.</p>
<p>West raised a brow as they stood before him.</p>
<p>"...What?"</p>
<p>Jax didn’t look back.</p>
<p>"You sit this one out."</p>
<p>Nina nodded.</p>
<p>"For once."</p>
<p>West blinked.</p>
<p>"...Excuse me?"</p>
<p>Jax smirked slightly.</p>
<p>"You’re the gang leader, right?"</p>
<p>"Then act like it."</p>
<p>Nina added, "You can’t be the one solving everything all the time."</p>
<p>"Otherwise what’s the point of us being here?"</p>
<p>West looked at them and went silent for a moment before a small smile appeared on his face.</p>
<p>"...Alright."</p>
<p>He leaned back again.</p>
<p>"Show me."</p>
<p>Jax grinned.</p>
<p>"Gladly."</p>
<p>The Setting Sun members looked irritated now.</p>
<p>"...Done talking?"</p>
<p>Jax rolled his shoulders.</p>
<p>"Yeah."</p>
<p>"Now we’re starting."</p>
<p>What came next, happened really fast...</p>
<p>The moment one of the Setting Sun members lunged forward, Jax moved while unsheathing his katana.</p>
<p>A streak of ice followed as the blade came unsheathed, sweeping forward with intensity.</p>
<p>The attacker froze mid-motion and his arm encased in frost before Jax slammed the hilt of his blade into his chest...</p>
<p>Boom.</p>
<p>He went flying instantly, leaving the other four stunned.</p>
<p>They instantly activated their branches and two rushed at Nina, feeling like it would be easier to take down the woman first.</p>
<p>This was yet another mistake they made... underestimating another member of the Shadow Reapers...</p>
<p>She flicked her brush at incredibly swift speed and two constructs appeared mid-air... a reinforced shield and a long spear.</p>
<p>She grabbed the spear, spun and slammed it into the ground.</p>
<p>Thoooommmm~</p>
<p>A shockwave immediately blasted outward, knocking both attackers off balance.</p>
<p>Before they could recover, she drew again, making quick strokes in the air.</p>
<p>Two small beast-like creatures materialized and pounced, tackling them and dragging them down.</p>
<p>"...What the—?!" one shouted while struggling.</p>
<p>"What sort of power is this?!"</p>
<p>Jax dashed past with his katana glowing brighter as he slashed.</p>
<p>A wave of ice swept across the ground, freezing another opponent’s legs in place.</p>
<p>"Freeze its the police," Jax muttered with a mocking laughter before kicking him hard enough to send him crashing into a pillar.</p>
<p>Meanwhile, the remaining two tried to regroup.</p>
<p>One activated a transformation branch causing his arms to bulk up unnaturally, becoming large as cannons.</p>
<p>He reached a height of seven feet while both his arms were twice the torse of a full grown adult and nearly reaching the ground... they were gigantic and muscular.</p>
<p>He charged Jax who smirked in response.</p>
<p>"Finally... a worthy challenger."</p>
<p>Jax swung his blade, which clashed against the attacker’s hardened fists, letting out a loud CLANG sound.</p>
<p>The fist was strong enough to parry the strike but the frost coating the katana began to spread and the man’s arms started freezing over.</p>
<p>"...What the hell?!"</p>
<p>Jax twisted, using the momentum to flip and slash downward...</p>
<p>Fwwwhiiiii~</p>
<p>Ice exploded outward, creating a massive moving wall of frost that slammed straight into the man and rammed him onto the other end of the wall.</p>
<p>Boom!</p>
<p>The wall came crashing down and multiple ice spikes from the attack pierced into the assailant’s body at different positions causing him to spit blood before falling over.</p>
<p>The last one turned to flee only to find Nina already there.</p>
<p>Her brush moved once as she created a heavy construct like a solid, hammer-like object...</p>
<p>With a single swung...</p>
<p>WHAM~</p>
<p>He was sent flying across the warehouse floor, skidding to a stop.</p>
<p>Silence fell as the five members of Setting Sun Gang lay defeated, injured and groaning.</p>
<p>One of them staggered to his feet, glaring.</p>
<p>"...You’ll regret this," he spat.</p>
<p>Jax pointed his blade at him.</p>
<p>"Then come back stronger."</p>
<p>Nina added with a small smile, "We’ll be waiting."</p>
<p>Dragging their injured teammates, they quickly fled the warehouse.</p>
<p>"...This isn’t over!" one of them shouted before disappearing.</p>
<p>Jax exhaled.</p>
<p>"...That was it?"</p>
<p>Nina dusted her hands.</p>
<p>"Honestly... I expected more."</p>
<p>West pushed himself off the pillar, clapping slowly.</p>
<p>"...Not bad."</p>
<p>They both turned to him with looks of satisfaction.</p>
<p>"You’ve improved."</p>
<p>Jax grinned. "Told you."</p>
<p>Nina smiled brightly.</p>
<p>"Say it again."</p>
<p>West raised a brow.</p>
<p>"...You’ve improved."</p>
<p>She walked up to him.</p>
<p>"Commend me properly."</p>
<p>West blinked.</p>
<p>"...What?"</p>
<p>Before he could react, she grabbed his hand, guided it upward and placed it gently on her head...</p>
<p>From there, she slowly lowered it to her shoulder and then slightly above her chest...</p>
<p>West narrowed his eyes slightly as his fingers sank into her plush pillows...</p>
<p>"...Nina—"</p>
<p>She just smiled, enjoying the moment while Jax immediately turned away.</p>
<p>"Alright nah—this is where I leave."</p>
<p>He waved a hand.</p>
<p>"Get a room you two."</p>
<p>West chuckled before pulling his hand back.</p>
<p>"...You’re getting bold."</p>
<p>Nina winked.</p>
<p>"Gotta keep up somehow."</p>
<p>"Naughty girl... I’ll punish you properly when you come over later..."</p>
<p>...</p>
<p>...</p>
<p>~ Hours Later ~</p>
<p>The lights from nearby buildings cast a soft glow into West’s apartment, reflecting faintly off the glass panels and smooth surfaces as night settled over the city.</p>
<p>West sat on the edge of his bed with a phone in hand while he stared at the contact name for a moment before tapping it.</p>
<p>The call rang twice before it connected.</p>
<p>"...Hello?"</p>
<p>A soft and tired but refined feminine voice rang out.</p>
<p>West leaned back slightly.</p>
<p>"Didn’t think you’d pick up that fast."</p>
<p>There was a brief pause before a response came through.</p>
<p>"...West?"</p>
<p>"Yeah."</p>
<p>A faint exhale came through the line.</p>
<p>"Oh sorry, I do not have this number. New one? I’ve been meaning to call you," she said quietly. "Actually... I’ve been meaning to come see you."</p>
<p>West smirked faintly.</p>
<p>"With gifts, right?"</p>
<p>She let out a soft, embarrassed laugh.</p>
<p>"...You remember that?"</p>
