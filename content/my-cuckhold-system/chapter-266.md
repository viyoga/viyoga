<p>West lowered the phone slowly onto the table before leaning his head backward against the couch.</p>
<p>His eyes remained fixed on the ceiling.</p>
<p>"...I don’t even know what I’m supposed to feel anymore."</p>
<p>Inside the summon space, Seraphyra quietly watched him for once without teasing.</p>
<p>Even Gor’thala remained silent while Aurethia sat cross-legged nearby polishing her saber as she occasionally glanced toward him.</p>
<p>The summon bond made his emotions impossible to fully hide from them.</p>
<p>Confusion, conflict, anger, sadness... all flowed through the connection.</p>
<p>Eventually West exhaled heavily. "...I need sleep."</p>
<p>Meanwhile, elsewhere in Misty High City.</p>
<p>Jax stood outside the massive entrance gates of Misty High Metropolitan University while staring upward.</p>
<p>The place looked ridiculously expensive.</p>
<p>Tall silver-white structures stretched into the skyline while holographic banners floated overhead displaying enrollment announcements and academy rankings.</p>
<p>Students moved everywhere while security drones hovered calmly around the premises as administrative staff guided people toward registration halls.</p>
<p>Jax adjusted the collar of his Shadow Reapers jacket proudly.</p>
<p>The black and crimson jacket rippled with the wind, giving Jax a certain kind of charisma with the gang insignia rested sharply across the back.</p>
<p>He looked cool as hell... and he knew it.</p>
<p>Jax had made his choice with his parents concerns in mind.</p>
<p>He’d enroll in college but he wasn’t abandoning Shadow Reapers either.</p>
<p>A few nearby students whispered quietly while glancing toward him.</p>
<p>"Shadow Reapers?"</p>
<p>"Isn’t that the fast rising small gang?"</p>
<p>"I heard their gang leader’s insane."</p>
<p>"Yeah they made the news a bunch of times recently!"</p>
<p>Jax grinned slightly while entering the administrative hall casually.</p>
<p>The moment people noticed the gang attire, the atmosphere changed.</p>
<p>No one dared bother him or argued when he cut the lines.</p>
<p>Even the workers suddenly became extremely polite.</p>
<p>A receptionist with short silver hair immediately stood straighter.</p>
<p>"H-Hello sir. Registration?"</p>
<p>Jax left eyebrow raised. ’Sir? Damn. This awakened stuff really is life changing.’</p>
<p>"Uh... yeah."</p>
<p>"Of course. Right this way please."</p>
<p>The process went absurdly smoothly afterward.</p>
<p>Every form, document and process had no issues whatsoever.</p>
<p>One dude who originally tried complaining that Jax skipped part of the queue instantly went pale the moment he noticed the Shadow Reapers emblem.</p>
<p>Jax almost laughed. ’Not gonna lie... The respect feels addictive.’</p>
<p>Eventually he finished registration and exited the building with his new student ID spinning casually between his fingers.</p>
<p>"Wow," he muttered. "That was way easier than normal human life."</p>
<p>A familiar voice suddenly spoke nearby.</p>
<p>"You’re smiling too much."</p>
<p>Jax turned and noticed Nina leaning against the railing nearby with crossed arms.</p>
<p>Jax blinked. "...Why are you here?"</p>
<p>"West sent me to make sure you didn’t accidentally enroll into culinary school or something stupid."</p>
<p>"Rude."</p>
<p>Nina snorted softly as her gaze lowered toward the student ID.</p>
<p>"You actually did it?"</p>
<p>"Yeah."</p>
<p>"How do you feel?"</p>
<p>Jax looked toward the university campus briefly before shrugging. "...Honestly? Weird."</p>
<p>Nina leaned beside him quietly as Jax sighed.</p>
<p>"My parents aren’t completely wrong."</p>
<p>She was surprised at his admittance, after knowing how badly he was against higher institution initially. It was West who encouraged the thought of giving it a try without leaving the world of awakeners.</p>
<p>"I still wanna be awakened," he continued. "Still wanna be Shadow Reapers."</p>
<p>His fingers tightened around the ID. "But maybe they just wanted to know I’d still have a future if something goes wrong."</p>
<p>Nina remained quiet before softly adding; "You can have both."</p>
<p>Jax glanced toward her while she shrugged.</p>
<p>"We’re not some suicidal gang."</p>
<p>"...Tell that to West."</p>
<p>"Fair point."</p>
<p>They both laughed lightly afterward before leaving together.</p>
<p>•••</p>
<p>Meanwhile... the Iron Vultures base had become a complete disaster zone for the second time in less than two weeks.</p>
<p>The first incident involving the bowel catastrophe had already damaged morale badly enough.</p>
<p>Now the current situation was somehow even worse...</p>
<p>Because unlike diarrhea and vomiting, money problems threatened the actual existence of the gang itself.</p>
<p>Inside the large underground operations hall, dozens of members shouted over each other while technicians scrambled around holographic monitors and glowing system interfaces.</p>
<p>"Try it again!"</p>
<p>"I AM TRYING IT AGAIN!"</p>
<p>"Then why the hell is it still failing?!"</p>
<p>One of the finance officers slammed both palms against the desk angrily as another failed transaction warning flashed red across the monitor.</p>
<p>[TRANSACTION FAILED]</p>
<p>[ERROR CODE: INVALID ROUTING STRUCTURE]</p>
<p>"THIS MAKES NO SENSE!"</p>
<p>Another operator nearby cursed loudly.</p>
<p>"We can’t pay for supply runs!"</p>
<p>"Half our fuel requests are getting denied!"</p>
<p>"The weapon shipment didn’t go through!"</p>
<p>A third member nearly ripped his hair out.</p>
<p>"The Bureau rejected our ruins reservation again because payment verification failed!"</p>
<p>Meanwhile, Bloodstain stood in the middle of the chaos looking like a man approaching the edge of sanity.</p>
<p>The massive dark tattoo covering half his face twitched visibly while veins bulged slightly across his neck.</p>
<p>"...Explain this to me again."</p>
<p>The trembling finance officer swallowed hard.</p>
<p>"Boss... every time the system processes a transaction..." he clicked rapidly through several screens. "...something reroutes the payment structure."</p>
<p>Bloodstain narrowed his eyes dangerously. "What does that even mean?"</p>
<p>"It means the money exists but the system keeps flagging the transactions as invalid."</p>
<p>"Then fix it."</p>
<p>"We’re trying!"</p>
<p>Bloodstain slammed his fist into the nearest desk.</p>
<p>BOOOOOM!</p>
<p>The entire thing collapsed inward instantly causing everyone nearby to flinch violently.</p>
<p>"I AM SICK OF HEARING ’WE’RE TRYING!’"</p>
<p>The room fell silent and nobody dared speak for several seconds.</p>
<p>Then one nervous-looking technician slowly raised his hand.</p>
<p>"...Boss?"</p>
<p>Bloodstain’s murderous gaze snapped toward him.</p>
<p>"What?"</p>
<p>The man looked terrified already.</p>
<p>"...I think it might be a bug."</p>
<p>"A bug."</p>
<p>"...Yes."</p>
<p>Bloodstain stared blankly before slowly rubbed his face.</p>
<p>"...You’re telling me one of the top 100 gangs in Misty High City is currently being crippled..."</p>
<p>He pointed toward the screens flashing failed transaction warnings repeatedly.</p>
<p>"...By a damn software bug?"</p>
<p>Nobody answered because unfortunately, that was exactly what was happening.</p>
<p>They had already brought in specialists, system analysts, digital technicians and even a low-tier awakened hacker.</p>
<p>Unfortunately, nobody fully understood what was happening yet.</p>
<p>The bug was bizarre and well hidden... every time they thought they fixed one issue, another appeared.</p>
<p>Funds rerouted incorrectly, payment confirmations vanished, trade approvals corrupted, supply authorizations failed.</p>
<p>The gang couldn’t properly purchase resources or reserve ruins orconduct large-scale transactions.</p>
<p>The longer it lasted, the worse things became.</p>
<p>Bloodstain slowly sat back down heavily in his chair.</p>
<p>"...How long until it’s fixed."</p>
<p>The room remained silent again and that answer alone or the lack of it, was an answer in itself.</p>
<p>Bloodstain exhaled sharply before quietly muttering; "...Whoever is behind this..."</p>
<p>His eyes darkened dangerously. "...I’ll skin them alive."</p>
<p>None of them realized that the culprit was currently laying comfortably on a couch several districts away while eating chips.</p>
<p>West sat lazily inside his apartment with one leg resting over the other while a screen panels was laid out before him.</p>
<p>Thanks to Aria, the Iron Vultures bug had honestly turned out better than expected.</p>
<p>He originally only wanted to annoy them, instead, their entire operations structure was collapsing.</p>
<p>"...Maybe I overcooked a little," he muttered.</p>
<p>Seraphyra snorted from nearby while sprawled upside down across the couch.</p>
<p>"There’s no such thing as overcooking revenge."</p>
<p>"That sounds psychotic."</p>
<p>"Thank you."</p>
<p>West shook his head slightly before focusing back on the floating system panels before him.</p>
<p>Specifically, his sub stats. Something relating to them had been bothering him lately.</p>
<p>For days now, no matter how many Cuckpoints he attempted using... the numbers remained frozen at 120 points.</p>
<p>West frowned while selecting Strength again.</p>
<p>[SUB STAT LIMIT REACHED]</p>
<p>"...Again?"</p>
<p>He tried Speed and it displayed the same thing.</p>
<p>Every single sub stat displayed the exact same notification repeatedly and they were all currently at the 120 mark.</p>
<p>West leaned forward slightly now.</p>
<p>"...What the hell is going on?"</p>
<p>That wasn’t normal especially since his Cuckpoints balance was more than enough.</p>
<p>[CURRENT CUCKPOINTS: 29,320]</p>
<p>He should’ve been able to increase multiple stats easily.</p>
<p>Instead, everything remained locked.</p>
<p>West rubbed his chin thoughtfully and then his eyebrows furrowed as a memory suddenly resurfaced.</p>
<p>[ Threshold met ]</p>
<p>That strange notification from earlier after all his sub stats hit one hundred... he wondered if it had something to do with this.</p>
<p>But at the time, nothing happened afterward. He was still able to keep raising his substats until he got them to 120.</p>
<p>West slowly narrowed his eyes. "...Don’t tell me..."</p>
<p>The system interface shimmered softly before him but unfortunately, there were no additional explanations.</p>
<p>West sighed heavily while leaning backward again.</p>
<p>"This system seriously explains things in the worst possible way."</p>
<p>Inside the summon space, Aurethia looked over curiously.</p>
<p>"Perhaps your body has reached a temporary limit."</p>
<p>West frowned slightly. "Maybe."</p>
<p>Gor’thala spoke calmly nearby. "Growth rarely remains linear forever, Master."</p>
<p>Seraphyra stretched lazily. "Or maybe master is already too handsome."</p>
<p>West ignored that entirely while his thoughts drifted elsewhere again.</p>
<p>’Threshold met... What threshold? What changed?’</p>
<p>Before he could think deeper, his phone started ringing again.</p>
<p>West glanced toward the name displayed on the screen.</p>
<p>~ Dad ~</p>
