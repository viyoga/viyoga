<p>That night, after work, West didn’t sleep immediately.</p>
<p>As he lay back on his bed and stared at the dim ceiling above him, West felt something unfamiliar settle into his chest.</p>
<p>Satisfaction.</p>
<p>Not arrogance or relief... just satisfaction.</p>
<p>Satisfied from moving forward.</p>
<p>He reached for his phone.</p>
<p>There was only one name on his mind.</p>
<p>Aria.</p>
<p>West didn’t overthink it. Overthinking ruined momentum. He typed a simple message.</p>
<p>&gt; You home yet?</p>
<p>The reply came less than a minute later.</p>
<p>&gt; Yeah. Just got in.</p>
<p>Another pause.</p>
<p>Then—</p>
<p>&gt; Can we talk?</p>
<p>West’s lips curved upward slightly.</p>
<p>&gt; Call me.</p>
<p>The phone rang almost immediately.</p>
<p>"Hey," Aria said.</p>
<p>Her voice was softer than usual. Quieter. Like she was curled up somewhere alone.</p>
<p>"I’m listening," West replied.</p>
<p>That was all it took.</p>
<p>She poured everything out.</p>
<p>Ross being late again.</p>
<p>Ross leaving without explanation.</p>
<p>Ross choosing gang matters over her, over and over.</p>
<p>"He doesn’t even look at me the same anymore," she said bitterly. "It’s like I’m just... there. Waiting. Always waiting."</p>
<p>West stayed silent, letting her speak uninterrupted. He could hear the frustration breaking through her words, the loneliness she tried—and failed—to hide.</p>
<p>When she finally stopped, he spoke calmly.</p>
<p>"You know what that sounds like to me?"</p>
<p>"What?" she asked.</p>
<p>"It sounds like someone who’s been starved," West said.</p>
<p>She scoffed weakly. "Starved?"</p>
<p>"Yeah," he continued with a low tone. "Starved of attention. Of affection. Of being wanted."</p>
<p>The line went quiet.</p>
<p>Aria swallowed.</p>
<p>"...lonely."</p>
<p>"...Yeah," she admitted quietly.</p>
<p>West leaned back against the headboard, eyes closing briefly.</p>
<p>"That’s not something someone like you should ever feel... not even for a second..." he said. "Not when you’re that beautiful."</p>
<p>Aria laughed softly, embarrassed. "You always say that."</p>
<p>"Because it’s true," West replied. "And because it’s obvious you’re being wasted."</p>
<p>Her breath hitched audibly.</p>
<p>"You’re really bad for me," she murmured.</p>

<p>"I’d treat you like someone who’d been waiting too long," he continued slowly. "Like someone who deserves to be... appreciated properly..."</p>

<p>Then she laughed softly, clearly flustered. "You say things like that so easily."</p>
<p>"Because I mean them."</p>

<p>"And... more?" she asked, half-joking, half-testing.</p>

<p>"That’s..." she paused. "That’s dangerous."</p>
