<p>West and Liz occupied the spacious rear seats while a digital map floated above the tablet resting on Liz’s lap.</p>
<p>As the driver pulled away from Shadow Reapers Headquarters, she enlarged the first location.</p>
<p>"This is where we’re headed first."</p>
<p>West leaned over slightly. "So many."</p>
<p>She nodded. "You purchased warehouses, undeveloped land, commercial buildings and residential properties."</p>
<p>"You’ve basically become a real estate investor overnight."</p>
<p>West chuckled. "I honestly didn’t expect to."</p>
<p>"You’ll thank yourself later."</p>
<p>The driver continued navigating through city traffic and during the journey, their conversation naturally drifted away from business.</p>
<p>"So..." Liz smiled mischievously. "I thought younger guys preferred driving themselves instead of using a driver."</p>
<p>West looked out the window. "...About that."</p>
<p>"I can’t drive."</p>
<p>Liz blinked. "What?"</p>
<p>"I never learned."</p>
<p>She stared with a look of disbelief. "The leader of Shadow Reapers..."</p>
<p>"...can’t drive?"</p>
<p>West shrugged shamelessly. "I’ve always walked."</p>
<p>"Or taken public transport."</p>
<p>She covered her mouth before laughing. "I don’t believe this."</p>
<p>"It’s true."</p>
<p>"You’ve fought gang wars."</p>
<p>"Defeated veteran awakeners."</p>
<p>"Built an organization."</p>
<p>"...But can’t parallel park?"</p>
<p>West sighed dramatically. "Life is unfair."</p>
<p>She laughed even harder. "I’ll teach you one day."</p>
<p>West looked horrified. "You?"</p>
<p>"Absolutely."</p>
<p>"I value my life."</p>
<p>"You wound me."</p>
<p>"I’ve seen your parking."</p>
<p>"My parking is excellent."</p>
<p>"The lamp post disagreed."</p>
<p>She lightly smacked his shoulder. "I hit it once."</p>
<p>"You introduced yourself to it."</p>
<p>Their laughter soon filled the vehicle as they chatted.</p>
<p>...</p>
<p>...</p>
<p>~ Thirty Minutes Later ~</p>
<p>The first property stood within an industrial district.</p>
<p>A large warehouse surrounded by nearly five acres of unused land.</p>
<p>West slowly walked around the perimeter.</p>
<p>"What do you think?" Liiz asked.</p>
<p>He studied the area carefully. "Warehouse stays."</p>
<p>He pointed toward the empty land. "We build."</p>
<p>"What?"</p>
<p>"A logistics center."</p>
<p>"Shadow Reapers won’t remain this size forever."</p>
<p>She quickly noted everything. "Done."</p>
<p>---</p>
<p>The second property sat within a rapidly developing commercial district.</p>
<p>A four-story office building requiring extensive renovation.</p>
<p>West inspected cracked walls and aging interiors.</p>
<p>"This one?" Liz asked.</p>
<p>He nodded. "Renovate."</p>
<p>"Upper floors become offices."</p>
<p>"Ground floor gets rented out."</p>
<p>She smiled. "Generating income while expanding."</p>
<p>"I like it."</p>
<p>---</p>
<p>The third location overlooked a busy intersection.</p>
<p>It was a vacant land with traffic flowing constantly around it.</p>
<p>West observed quietly. "What would you build?"</p>
<p>Liz answered immediately. "A shopping complex."</p>
<p>"Mixed commercial."</p>
<p>"The foot traffic alone guarantees profit."</p>
<p>West looked impressed. "You already planned this."</p>
<p>She smiled proudly. "I hoped you’d ask."</p>
<p>---</p>
<p>By midday... they had already visited eight different properties.</p>
<p>Everywhere they went, West made adjustments.</p>
<p>Some lands would become business facilities while others would be left untouched until property values increased.</p>
<p>Several older buildings would be demolished entirely.</p>
<p>One abandoned factory caught his attention.</p>
<p>"This..." he smiled. "...could become another headquarters someday."</p>
<p>Liz nodded. "I was hoping you’d notice."</p>
<p>...</p>
<p>...</p>
<p>Many hours later... their final destination lay near Misty High City’s zoological reserve.</p>
<p>Unlike previous properties, this one bordered a quiet wooded section just beyond the zoo’s outer perimeter.</p>
<p>"It isn’t particularly valuable now," Liz explained while walking beside him. "But in five years..."</p>
<p>"...the expansion plans should dramatically increase land prices."</p>
<p>West looked around and noticed that the area was surprisingly peaceful.</p>
<p>The sounds of birds chirping and children playing could be heard somewhere beyond the trees.</p>
<p>"It has potential."</p>
<p>Liz nodded happily. "I’m glad you think so."</p>
<p>Just then...</p>
<p>WEEEEEOOOOO!</p>
<p>A deafening alarm suddenly echoed across the area.</p>
<p>Both of them froze in place.</p>
<p>"...What was that?" West frowned.</p>
<p>Several panicked voices followed. "Run!"</p>
<p>"Move!"</p>
<p>"Somebody stop it!"</p>
<p>Liz instinctively turned. "...West?"</p>
<p>Her eyes widened as she spotted a massive bear burst through a line of shrubs.</p>
<p>It was nearly ten feet tall while standing upright with thick brown matted fur.</p>
<p>Foam gathered around its mouth as it roared furiously, charging directly toward her.</p>
<p>Everything happened in an instant.</p>
<p>Liz’s mind went blank as her legs refused to move.</p>
<p>The distance between them vanished frighteningly fast as the bear raised one enormous paw.</p>
<p>She instinctively shut her eyes as the paws descended towards her.</p>
<p>THUD!</p>
<p>A loud sound of collision rang out but she felt no impact.</p>
<p>She slowly opened her eyes with a look of confusion... and standing right before her was none other than West.</p>
<p>His palm rested firmly against the charging bear’s chest area.</p>
<p>Despite the beast throwing its entire weight forward... it couldn’t move another inch.</p>
<p>The ground beneath West’s feet dented slightly... but otherwise... he remained perfectly still.</p>
<p>He turned slightly with a calm smile on his face. "Are you okay?"</p>
<p>Liz stared in complete disbelief. "...Y-Yeah..."</p>
<p>"I’m okay..."</p>
<p>The bear roared again with an enraged look.</p>
<p>It raised both enormous forelegs before bringing them crashing downward.</p>
<p>West sighed. "I was hoping you’d calm down."</p>
<p>The instant the paws descended, West’s open palm shot into the bear’s abdomen.</p>
<p>BOOM!</p>
<p>A small shockwave erupted, lifting the enormous creature off the ground entirely.</p>
<p>It flew backward over twenty meters before crashing through a metal fence and slamming into a thick concrete retaining wall.</p>
<p>The wall cracked and the bear immediately went unconscious</p>
<p>Everything became silent.</p>
<p>Moments later... several zoo security vehicles screeched to a halt nearby.</p>
<p>Uniformed guards jumped out carrying tranquilizer equipment.</p>
<p>One immediately shouted, "Everyone move away!"</p>
<p>"A dangerous bear escaped containment!"</p>
<p>"You need to evacuate—"</p>
<p>Liz pointed toward the unconscious animal. "...Is that the bear you’re talking about?"</p>
<p>The guards turned.</p>
<p>"..."</p>
<p>"..."</p>
<p>One slowly lowered his tranquilizer rifle. "...How..."</p>
<p>Another followed her pointing finger.</p>
<p>The moment they noticed West standing nearby, their eyes widened.</p>
<p>"...Wait."</p>
<p>"I know him."</p>
<p>"The young man from the news..."</p>
<p>Another guard looked twice and realization slowly dawned across his face. "...That’s him."</p>
<p>"...The leader of Shadow Reapers."</p>
<p>Several of the guards exchanged astonished glances.</p>
<p>One looked at the unconscious ten-foot bear and then back at West. "You..."</p>
<p>"...stopped it with your bare hand?"</p>
<p>West simply smiled awkwardly. "I guess. Tried to use as little force as possible. Sorry, still learning to control my strength."</p>
<p>The guards eyes nearly gouged out their sockets especially due to seeing how young West was.</p>
