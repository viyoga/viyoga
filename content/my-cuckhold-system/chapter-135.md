<p>The chamber that had once been a throne room of horrors slowly settled into an eerie silence.</p>
<p>Moments ago it had been filled with roars, explosions of power, shattering pillars, and the deafening echoes of combat. Now the only sounds that remained were the occasional drip of blood and the faint rumble of distant tunnels shifting after the brutal battle.</p>
<p>West stood at the center of it all with hundreds upon hundreds of those grotesque tentacled creatures corpses littered across the chamber floor. Pieces of shattered pillars, torn stone, and splintered organic structures were scattered everywhere.</p>
<p>The headless corpse of the Supreme Leader lay not far away, its body still twitching faintly as strange blue blood pooled beneath it.</p>
<p>West let out a slow breath as the earlier surge of power faded.</p>
<p>The ×2 boost and Adrenal Control pushing his body beyond its limits... all of it had begun to disappear.</p>
<p>And now the consequences arrived...</p>
<p>A sharp pain ran through his muscles causing West to grimace.</p>
<p>"Damn..."</p>
<p>Every fiber of his body felt like it had been violently wrung out. His arms trembled slightly and his shoulders burned while his ribs ached with every breath.</p>
<p>It felt like he had been beaten with hammers yet, he still straightened up and forced himself to move.</p>
<p>"We’re not done yet," he muttered.</p>
<p>Because the chamber... was filled with loot.</p>
<p>---</p>
<p>Jax was already moving through the battlefield.</p>
<p>"Holy hell..." he muttered, kicking aside a corpse.</p>
<p>He crouched down beside one of the fallen creatures and pulled something from beneath its body.</p>
<p>It was a glowing shard.</p>
<p>He whistled.</p>
<p>"Energy catalyst."</p>
<p>West walked past him and waved a hand.</p>
<p>The shard vanished immediately as he stored it inside his inventory.</p>
<p>"Keep gathering," West said.</p>
<p>Jax grinned.</p>
<p>"Oh I am."</p>
<p>And they got to work.</p>
<p>---</p>
<p>The next thirty minutes were spent clearing the chamber.</p>
<p>Anything that looked remotely valuable was collected.</p>
<p>Strange crystalline growths embedded in the walls.</p>
<p>Dense black metallic fragments from broken weapons.</p>
<p>Energy cores inside the tentacled creatures.</p>
<p>Several odd artifacts that had been scattered near the throne.</p>
<p>Every single item was handed over to West.</p>
<p>Jax tossed a glowing bone-like object toward him.</p>
<p>West caught it and inspected it briefly before storing it.</p>
<p>"Where’d you find this?"</p>
<p>"Inside one of those ugly things," Jax said. "Don’t ask which one."</p>
<p>West snorted and more items kept appearing. It made sense that the Eternal Ruins had this much... It was a three layered ruins after all.</p>
<p>Even chunks of rare mineral were embedded beneath the floor and Gor’thala pried them loose.</p>
<p>West stored everything but the most interesting item came from the Supreme Leader itself.</p>
<p>West crouched beside the corpse.</p>
<p>Its blue skin had begun to dull, its elongated limbs lying limp across the ground.</p>
<p>He searched its body carefully and surprisingly his fingers brushed something cold.</p>
<p>Upon pulling it free, he noticed it was a small object wrapped in thin blue crystal.</p>
<p>West frowned slightly.</p>
<p>"Interesting..."</p>
<p>The system flickered faintly in his vision as he stored it but he didn’t inspect it yet.</p>
<p>He would deal with that later.</p>
<p>For now, they continued clearing the chamber.</p>
<p>Eventually Jax straightened up and stretched his arms.</p>
<p>"Well..." he said.</p>
<p>He looked around at the absolute carnage.</p>
<p>"Not bad for our first ruins clearing together."</p>
<p>West snorted.</p>
<p>"You were at the rear the whole time."</p>
<p>Jax clutched his chest dramatically.</p>
<p>"That hurts."</p>
<p>"You hid behind Gor’thala most of the fight."</p>
<p>"That is called tactical positioning and besides, you told me to."</p>
<p>West shook his head.</p>
<p>Still... despite the teasing and near death experience, this had been a success...</p>
<p>A huge one.</p>
<p>---</p>
<p>West turned toward the knight.</p>
<p>The armored figure still stood silently nearby.</p>
<p>Their dark armor didn’t even have a scratch despite the heated battle. Only frost residue still clung to parts of the plating. The glowing saber remained sheathed at their side.</p>
<p>Jax walked up beside West.</p>
<p>"Bro... I still can’t believe what I saw earlier."</p>
<p>West raised an eyebrow.</p>
<p>"What?"</p>
<p>"You didn’t see it," Jax said.</p>
<p>"That butcher thing? The one that nearly sent you to the afterlife?"</p>
<p>West rolled his eyes.</p>
<p>"Yeah?"</p>
<p>Jax spread his hands dramatically.</p>
<p>"They literally stood there fighting it for a bit... then said something like battle pattern recorded or something."</p>
<p>West turned slowly toward the knight while Jax continued excitedly.</p>
<p>"Then their sword started glowing like the freaking sun... and they moved so fast I couldn’t even see them."</p>
<p>He shook his head in disbelief.</p>
<p>"They sliced that butcher into pieces."</p>
<p>West blinked once.</p>
<p>"...Pieces?"</p>
<p>Jax nodded vigorously.</p>
<p>"Like meat in a butcher shop."</p>
<p>West stared at the knight for a moment before walking forward.</p>
<p>The armored figure turned to face him and for a moment neither of them spoke.</p>
<p>Then West said simply,</p>
<p>"So."</p>
<p>The knight stood straight.</p>
<p>"You fought well," the knight replied calmly.</p>
<p>West folded his arms.</p>
<p>"Would you accept the contract?"</p>
<p>The knight was silent for several seconds before fighting.</p>
<p>"After seeing how your group fights... I have come to a conclusion."</p>
<p>West raised an eyebrow while the knight continued.</p>
<p>"Your methods are crude."</p>
<p>Jax coughed.</p>
<p>"Hey—"</p>
<p>"Your techniques are less refined," the knight added.</p>
<p>West folded his arms expecting another rejection like the goddess but then the knight said,</p>
<p>"However..."</p>
<p>Their helmet tilted slightly.</p>
<p>"I believe I will enjoy being a part of this group."</p>
<p>West smiled.</p>
<p>"Good."</p>
<p>A glowing formation circle appeared between them as the contract ritual began.</p>
<p>West bit his palm without hesitation and dark red blood dripped onto the contract sigil.</p>
<p>The knight removed a gauntlet and did the same.</p>
<p>Silver blood touched the circle and te moment both drops landed, the contract ignited.</p>
<p>A powerful energy spread through the air as the system activated instantly.</p>
<p>---</p>
<p>[Contract Successfully Established]</p>
<p>[New Entity Bound]</p>
<p>Name: Aurethia Sol’Vael</p>
<p>Classification: Radiant Execution Knight</p>
<p>Abilities:</p>
<p>Solar Execution Saber</p>
<p>Combat Pattern Analysis</p>
<p>Radiant Acceleration</p>
<p>Celestial Armor</p>
<p>Judgment Blitz</p>
<p>Sunflare Burst</p>
<p>Absolute Duel Protocol</p>
<p>Contract Benefits Applied</p>
<p>West Gains Ability:</p>
<p>Radiant Acceleration</p>
<p>Ability Description:</p>
<p>Allows the user to momentarily accelerate their body using condensed radiant energy, dramatically increasing speed and reaction time.</p>
<p>---</p>
<p>West’s eyes widened slightly.</p>
<p>"Speed ability..."</p>
<p>West couldn’t help but chuckle at this but the system wasn’t finished.</p>
<p>---</p>
<p>Contract Benefits Applied To Entity: Aurethia Sol’Vael</p>
<p>+50% Combat Power Increase</p>
<p>Shared Energy Regeneration</p>
<p>New Ability Acquired: Volt Manipulation</p>
<p>---</p>
<p>The knight suddenly froze while looking down at their hands.</p>
<p>Electric sparks crackled faintly between their fingers.</p>
<p>"...This..."</p>
<p>Even their normally calm voice sounded stunned.</p>
<p>"This level of enhancement..."</p>
<p>Then suddenly, the knight removed their helmet.</p>
<p>Jax eyes widened, West blinked and even Gor’thala stoic expression vanished momentarily...</p>
<p>Because the face revealed beneath the armor was not male.</p>
<p>It was female...</p>
<p>And not just any female... she was... breathtaking with smooth pale skin and golden yellow hair cascading down her shoulders as soon as the helm calm off.</p>
<p>But what stood out the most... were her eyes. She seemed to have two normal eyes but beneath them... were another pair.</p>
<p>All of her four eyes glowed faintly with radiant red and golden light.</p>
<p>Jax’s jaw dropped as his pupils practically turned into heart shapes.</p>
<p>"Holy—"</p>
<p>The knight suddenly knelt on one knee before West.</p>
<p>"I swear my blade to you," she said calmly.</p>
<p>West was still slightly stunned.</p>
<p>They thought it was a dude the whole time but before he could say anything, a blur shot into the chamber.</p>
<p>"MASTER!"</p>
<p>Seraphyra slammed into him like a missile.</p>
<p>West nearly lost his balance as she wrapped herself around him and began kissing his face repeatedly.</p>
<p>"You’re alive!"</p>
<p>"Kiss—"</p>
<p>"I missed you!"</p>
<p>"Kiss—kiss—kiss—"</p>
<p>West tried to pry her off.</p>
<p>"Okay— okay— I’m alive!"</p>
<p>Jax stared in absolute jealousy.</p>
<p>"Bro..."</p>
<p>He pointed.</p>
<p>"You get the hot fairy AND the hot knight?? And don’t get me started on the green mommy over there..."</p>
<p>Seraphyra hugged West’s neck tightly.</p>
<p>"What took you so long?" West asked.</p>
<p>Seraphyra puffed her cheeks.</p>
<p>"I told you! I had problems!"</p>
<p>She began explaining rapidly.</p>
<p>"I destroyed a lot of those gross cocoons!"</p>
<p>"Then when I was coming back..."</p>
<p>She crossed her arms.</p>
<p>"I ran into some very disrespectful and mean men."</p>
<p>West frowned slightly.</p>
<p>"Mean and disrespectful?"</p>
<p>Seraphyra nodded firmly.</p>
<p>"So I dealt with them!"</p>
<p>Jax scratched his head.</p>
<p>"What does that—"</p>
<p>At that exact moment, figures stumbled into the chamber.</p>
<p>A group of men with several of them injured.</p>
<p>One of them pointed immediately.</p>
<p>"There she is!"</p>
<p>His face twisted with fury.</p>
<p>"That bitch!"</p>
<p>"Get her!"</p>
<p>West slowly stepped forward, positioning himself in front of Seraphyra as he narrowed his eyes.</p>
<p>Because behind the first group, more figures appeared and even more after that...</p>
<p>Within a few seconds...</p>
<p>The massive chamber began filling with people bearing different tattoos, different uniform and possessing different weapons...</p>
<p>They were five separate gangs, all glaring toward the center.</p>
<p>The man who had shouted earlier pointed again.</p>
<p>"That little bitch attacked us!"</p>
<p>West sighed slowly while staring at the growing crowd. He already knew this was going to be troublesome but at least they had cleared the ruins completely.</p>
