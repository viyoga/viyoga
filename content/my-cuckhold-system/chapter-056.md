<p>The guide clapped his hands. "Alright everyone! Let’s begin the tour."</p>
<p>As they started moving, West leaned closer to his father and whispered, "So... how badly did you destroy that guy at work?"</p>
<p>Mark chuckled. "Accidentally, mostly."</p>
<p>West nodded solemnly. "That’s the best kind."</p>
<p>Behind them, Elina whispered loudly to her brother, "He’s even hotter up close."</p>
<p>The brother looked like he was reconsidering all his life choices.</p>
<p>West smiled to himself.</p>
<p>This weekend was already promising.</p>
<p>From the moment the tour began, it was clear, positioned herself beside West with the precision of a guided missile, matching his pace perfectly as the group followed the guide deeper into the resort’s sprawling interior.</p>
<p>If West slowed, she slowed. If he stopped to look at something, she stopped too. If he shifted slightly to the left, she somehow ended up already there.</p>
<p>It was impressive but mostly hilarious.</p>
<p>Meanwhile, Mark was a few steps ahead, engaged in forced workplace small talk with William and his wife. William smiled, nodded, laughed at the appropriate moments but internally, he was absolutely grinding his teeth.</p>
<p>West could tell.</p>
<p>The man had the same energy as someone politely chatting while mentally drafting an email titled "Why Mark Is Ruining My Life."</p>
<p>The guide led them first into a massive underwater dome, a transparent tunnel stretching through a man-made lagoon. Above and around them, massive aquatic creatures glided through the water... some were natural while some were awakened hybrids engineered for tourism.</p>
<p>As a gigantic manta-ray-like creature passed overhead, glowing faintly blue, the guide began explaining its biology.</p>
<p>Before he could finish—</p>
<p>"You know," Elina said suddenly, tilting her head toward West, "manta rays mate for life. They form deep emotional bonds."</p>
<p>West blinked. "Oh."</p>
<p>"They also swim together for years," she added cheerfully. "Side by side. Constant companionship."</p>
<p>Her brother made a choking sound.</p>
<p>William’s wife glanced over with her eyebrows raised.</p>
<p>West nodded slowly. "That’s... very loyal of them."</p>
<p>Elina smiled brightly. "Right?"</p>
<p>The brother leaned in. "Elina, remember what Dad—"</p>
<p>She shoved his face away with one finger without even looking at him. "Shh. Adults are talking."</p>
<p>"I AM ALSO AN ADULT—"</p>
<p>"You’re fifteen."</p>
<p>"I—"</p>
<p>"Shh."</p>
<p>West bit his lip to stop himself from laughing.</p>
<p>They moved on to the Ruin Exhibit Hall, where the skeletal remains of a massive creature discovered in a collapsed ruin were suspended mid-air by anti-grav fields. Its bones were spiky, twisted, and faintly gave off residual energy.</p>
<p>The guide explained the dangers of ruins, the unpredictability, the mortality rate.</p>
<p>Elina nodded thoughtfully. "You know, awakened couples who enter ruins together tend to have higher survival rates."</p>
<p>West turned to her. "...That so?"</p>
<p>"Yes," she said seriously. "Trust, coordination, emotional connection. It’s all very romantic. Dangerous love, you know?"</p>
<p>Her brother slapped a hand over his face.</p>
<p>West hummed. "Sounds... intense."</p>
<p>"Oh, very," her eyes sparkled as she responded.</p>
<p>They proceeded through a technological wing, where cutting-edge inventions were on display... adaptive combat drones, simulated battlefields, holographic training pods.</p>
<p>At one point, they boarded glass-bottom boats that glided silently across an illuminated canal. The water below shimmered with bioluminescent plants.</p>
<p>Elina leaned over the edge slightly too far.</p>
<p>"Oh—!"</p>
<p>She slipped.</p>
<p>West reacted instantly, grabbing her wrist and pulling her back against him.</p>
<p>Her face turned crimson.</p>
<p>"...Wow," she whispered. "That was... chivalrous."</p>
<p>West smiled awkwardly. "You okay?"</p>
<p>She nodded rapidly. "Yes. Yes. Very okay."</p>
<p>Her brother stared at them like he was witnessing a personal tragedy.</p>
<p>Later, they used compact glass submarines, descending briefly into deeper waters where massive shadows moved beyond the lights.</p>
<p>Elina, once again, found a way.</p>
<p>"Some deep-sea creatures only reveal their true colors when they feel safe," she said softly. "Otherwise, they stay hidden."</p>
<p>West glanced at her. "Is that so?"</p>
<p>She nodded. "Sometimes, you need the right person nearby."</p>
<p>Her brother groaned loudly enough that the guide glanced back.</p>
<p>Next came low-altitude gliders, floating just a few feet above the ground, sweeping them across hanging gardens and artificial cliffs. The wind rushed past and mist brushed West’s face.</p>
<p>He mentally bookmarked at least six places he wanted to revisit later.</p>
<p>’This place is insane,’ he thought. ’And I haven’t even explored properly yet.’</p>
<p>By the time the tour wrapped up, the brother had reached his limit.</p>
<p>"So," he said smugly as they disembarked, "what floor are you guys staying on? Bet it’s, like, somewhere mid-level."</p>
<p>West tilted his head. "159."</p>
<p>"...What?"</p>
<p>"159th floor," West repeated casually.</p>
<p>The boy’s jaw dropped. "That’s— that’s not possible. That’s VIP tier."</p>
<p>William’s smile cracked.</p>
<p>Elina’s eyes lit up. "Oh."</p>
<p>West shrugged. "Dad’s idea."</p>
<p>They parted ways shortly after as Mark and West headed back toward their tower.</p>
<p>Elina watched them go, quietly committing "159th floor" to memory like it was sacred scripture.</p>
<p>That evening, West and his father dined at one of the resort’s high-end restaurants.</p>
<p>The plating was exquisite, the flavors were complex but the portions were... insulting.</p>
<p>West stared at his plate.</p>
<p>"...Dad."</p>
<p>"Yes?"</p>
<p>"I’m not saying this isn’t good," West said carefully while poking at the food. "I’m saying if I blink too hard, it might disappear."</p>
<p>Mark laughed. "That’s fine dining."</p>
<p>West sighed. "Fine dining? More like fine fasting."</p>
<p>After dessert... which was roughly the size of a coin... West stood.</p>
<p>"I’m going to find actual food," he announced. "The kind that fills your soul."</p>
<p>Mark waved him off. "Don’t get lost."</p>
<p>West grinned. "No promises."</p>
<p>...</p>
<p>...</p>
<p>Mist drifted lazily between towering glass structures, lights reflected off the liquid-like stone floors and artificial waterways. Soft music buzzed beneath the sound of footsteps, laughter, and distant fountains.</p>
<p>It was the kind of place where money didn’t just exist... it floated... invisible but ever-present.</p>
<p>And walking through it like she owned the air itself was a woman with an intense, oppressive presence.</p>
<p>She was tall, long-legged with a posture straight enough to make surrounding people unconsciously step aside. Half of her face was concealed behind a black designer nose with a tiny red petal crafted along its surface.</p>
<p>The visible half of her face seemed stunning... One could spot sharp jawline, full lips pressed into a displeased pout, eyes hidden behind tinted glasses that still somehow felt like they were judging everything.</p>
<p>"This is unacceptable," she said coolly.</p>
<p>Beside her walked a man in a perfectly tailored suit, sweating despite the controlled climate. "Y-yes, Mistress... We’ll address it immediately. I’ve already spoken to the management—"</p>
<p>"You spoke," she interrupted with a flat tone. "But the service remains subpar."</p>
<p>The man nodded rapidly. "My fault entirely. I take responsibility."</p>
<p>She clicked her tongue, clearly unimpressed, and continued forward.</p>
<p>At the exact wrong moment, West came hustling up from the opposite side of the plaza.</p>
<p>His hands were filled with bagels, wraps, something fried and dripping oil into a paper bag, a drink precariously balanced on top.</p>
<p>He was mid-thought, mentally planning which attraction to hit next and whether his stomach could handle another round after last night.</p>
<p>One of the bagels slipped.</p>
<p>"Ah—!"</p>
<p>West instinctively crouched to grab it, completely disappearing from sight just as the woman descended a short set of stairs.</p>
<p>She didn’t see him.</p>
<p>So the moment he stood up, they collided.</p>
<p>Thump~</p>
<p>West staggered half a step back. "Oh—! I’m so sorry, I wasn’t looking—"</p>
<p>The woman didn’t move an inch...</p>
<p>She stood exactly where she had been with her heels planted to the ground like he’d bumped into a wall disguised as a person.</p>
<p>West blinked.</p>
<p>"...Uh. Sorry," he said again quickly, flustered. "Really. That was my fault."</p>
<p>He even tried to offer her one of the bagels. "Here—uh—as compensation?"</p>
<p>The man in the suit stepped forward instantly with a cold glare. "That won’t be necessary. Move along."</p>
<p>"Oh—okay," West shrugged. "Sorry again."</p>
<p>He nodded awkwardly and walked off, feeling slightly embarrassed but quickly brushed it off.</p>
<p>Behind him, the plaza noise resumed but the woman didn’t move.</p>
<p>She stood there, utterly still.</p>
<p>The man glanced at her. "...Mistress?"</p>
<p>Several seconds passed and then she spoke.</p>
<p>"He didn’t recoil."</p>
<p>The man frowned. "Pardon?"</p>
<p>"He bumped into me," she said slowly. "I was moving as well. Even casually... a normal person would have been pushed back. Hard."</p>
<p>The man stiffened.</p>
<p>"...Yes."</p>
<p>Her eyes narrowed beneath the shades as she turned her head slightly. "Yet he remained standing."</p>
<p>The man swallowed. "Even a Tier 1 awakened would have been sent flying. With your... natural output."</p>
<p>"Exactly."</p>
<p>His mind raced. "Then... Tier 2?"</p>
<p>She shook her head. "No."</p>
<p>"That’s impossible," he said quickly. "A Tier 2 would be older. Arrogant. Affiliated with a gang. Surrounded by noise and attention. That boy had none of that."</p>
<p>She was quiet for a moment.</p>
<p>Then she said, calmly, "He is Tier 1."</p>
<p>The man’s eyes widened.</p>
<p>"...Recently awakened."</p>
<p>His breath hitched. "How can you be sure?"</p>
<p>"I’m sure," she replied.</p>
<p>She turned slightly, following West’s retreating figure in the distance.</p>
<p>"That boy... find out who he is."</p>
<p>The man hesitated. "Mistress... you came here to rest. Do you really want to bring work into this trip?"</p>
