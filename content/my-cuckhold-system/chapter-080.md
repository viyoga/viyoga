<p>West leaned back against the wall, thinking.</p>
<p>He couldn’t equip Emotional Manipulation yet and Combat Instinct seemed like it could be a real powerful tool as well.</p>
<p>But none of them were worth replacing his current line up of skills.</p>
<p>Adrenal Control was essential for combat survival in dire situations.</p>
<p>Shadow Anchor gave him mobility and battlefield control.</p>
<p>And Silver Tongue...</p>
<p>He smirked slightly.</p>
<p>Silver Tongue was broken in social situations.</p>
<p>It made conversations effortless... seduction without resistance.</p>
<p>Fortunately, he could buy more buffs in the shop without having to worry because they worked differently. They weren’t skills, they were passive charms so they didn’t require slots.</p>
<p>They simply existed enhancing him permanently.</p>
<p>He navigated to his Sub Stats.</p>
<p>---</p>
<p>[SUB STATS]</p>
<p>Strength: 44</p>
<p>Speed: 43</p>
<p>Agility: 22</p>
<p>Seduction: 57</p>
<p>Confidence: 49</p>
<p>Charm: 31</p>
<p>Aura: 21</p>
<p>Mental Resilience: 21</p>
<p>Stamina: 22</p>
<p>---</p>
<p>West tapped the enhancement option, prepared to increase some of them by a few points.</p>
<p>He ignored Strength, speed, seduction, confidence, and Charm.</p>
<p>Those were already monstrous so he decided to focus on balance.</p>
<p>He added:</p>
<p>+10 Agility</p>
<p>+10 Aura</p>
<p>+10 Mental Resilience</p>
<p>+10 Stamina</p>
<p>[SYSTEM NOTIFICATION]</p>
<p>Cost: 200 Cuck Points</p>
<p>Remaining CP: 2,676</p>
<p>---</p>
<p>His body reacted immediately as he felt his mind turn clearer and his entire being feel lighter.</p>
<p>Even his posture shifted naturally.</p>
<p>He clenched his fist, feeling the undeniable improvement.</p>
<p>But he wasn’t done.</p>
<p>He opened the SHOP and began to browse the Buff Section.</p>
<p>Dozens of options appeared and he began purchasing selectively.</p>
<p>---</p>
<p>[BUFF PURCHASED: Predator’s Allure]</p>
<p>Cost: 50 CP</p>
<p>Effect: Increases physical attraction response from nearby targets by 15%</p>
<p>---</p>
<p>[BUFF PURCHASED: Touch Sensitivity Amplifier]</p>
<p>Cost: 60 CP</p>
<p>Effect: Physical contact increases emotional and hormonal responsiveness in target</p>
<p>---</p>
<p>[BUFF PURCHASED: Dominance Pressure]</p>
<p>Cost: 75 CP</p>
<p>Effect: Weak-willed individuals instinctively submit to presence</p>
<p>---</p>
<p>[BUFF PURCHASED: Combat Stabilizer]</p>
<p>Cost: 40 CP</p>
<p>Effect: Reduces physical shock and stagger during combat by 20%</p>
<p>---</p>
<p>[BUFF PURCHASED: Pain Resistance Charm]</p>
<p>Cost: 45 CP</p>
<p>Effect: Reduces perceived pain by 25%</p>
<p>---</p>
<p>[BUFF PURCHASED: Aphrodisiac Field (Passive)]</p>
<p>Cost: 80 CP</p>
<p>Effect: Physical intimacy dramatically increases emotional dependency and pleasure response</p>
<p>---</p>
<p>Total spent: 350 CP</p>
<p>Remaining CP: 2,326</p>
<p>West leaned back, stunned.</p>
<p>"...It feels like I have just achieved a higher state of existence..."</p>
<p>He could feel the difference in his presence...</p>
<p>He decided to navigate further and skills appeared.</p>
<p>He spotted an interesting one immediately but it was very expensive.</p>
<p>---</p>
<p>[SKILL: Kinetic Absorption]</p>
<p>Cost: 450 CP</p>
<p>Type: Active Combat Skill</p>
<p>Description:</p>
<p>Absorb incoming physical and energy damage, converting it into strength. The more damage you take, the higher your power.</p>
<p>---</p>
<p>West’s eyes widened.</p>
<p>"That’s broken."</p>
<p>If someone attacked him repeatedly, he could convert their attacks into power.</p>
<p>It could completely flip fights.</p>
<p>Unfortunately, he didn’t have extra skill slots.</p>
<p>He sighed and then he spotted another.</p>
<p>---</p>
<p>[SKILL: Volt Manipulation]</p>
<p>Cost: 120 CP</p>
<p>Type: Active Skill</p>
<p>Description:</p>
<p>Channel electrical energy through body to enhance attacks and paralyze targets.</p>
<p>---</p>
<p>Also useful... but again, no slots.</p>
<p>He kept scrolling until he came across a familiar item.</p>
<p>---</p>
<p>[T1 Awakening Voucher]</p>
<p>Description:</p>
<p>Grant Tier 1 Bloodline Awakening to chosen individual.</p>
<p>---</p>
<p>West’s suddenly recalled his plan to create his own gang.</p>
<p>Although, he didn’t know who to trust this on yet, neither was he sure if it would work, he purchased it instantly.</p>
<p>&lt;[ Tier 1 Bloodline Awakening Voucher ]&gt;</p>
<p>&lt;[ Tier 1 Transformation Awakening Voucher ]&gt;</p>
<p>&lt;[ Tier 1 Summoning Awakening Voucher ]&gt;</p>
<p>Victor picked Bloodline Awakening this time and it went on to provide the next set of options.</p>
<p>&gt; [Choose Awakening Branch Count]</p>
<p>1 Branch — Cost: 100 CP</p>
<p>2 Branches — Cost: 150 CP</p>
<p>3 Branches — Cost: 500 CP</p>
<p>Unlike the last time when he couldn’t afford it, this time he picked three branches.</p>
<p>&lt;[ Confirm your selection — 3 Branches ]&gt;</p>
<p>[ YES ] × [ NO ]</p>
<p>West tapped yes and almost instantaneous, 500 CP was subtracted from his total CP.</p>
<p>Remaining CP: 1826</p>
<p>The card materialized in his hand.</p>
<p>It was black and warm, emitting a very low glow.</p>
<p>He stared at it for a couple of seconds...</p>
<p>If this worked on other people beside him, he could create awakened beings.</p>
<p>He could create loyalty...</p>
<p>He could build an empire.</p>
<p>The thought of this was incredibly stimulating.</p>
<p>However, a seperate thought suddenly crossed his mind.</p>
<p>A dangerous one...</p>
<p>He looked down at himself.</p>
<p>"...What if..."</p>
<p>He paused.</p>
<p>"...I can awaken again?"</p>
<p>The system had never said it was impossible.</p>
<p>He swallowed slowly as curiosity overpowered his caution.</p>
<p>He lifted the card and placed it against his abdomen just like the first time.</p>
<p>The card reacted instantly, vibrating violently.</p>
<p>[SYSTEM WARNING]</p>
<p>West stared at the error notification floating in front of him with his pupils shrinking slightly.</p>
<p>---</p>
<p>[SYSTEM ERROR]</p>
<p>Secondary Awakening Failed.</p>
<p>Reason: Host has not reached required strength threshold.</p>
<p>Recommendation: Increase overall power before attempting again.</p>
<p>---</p>
<p>For a few seconds, West didn’t breathe.</p>
<p>His heart pounded violently in his chest.</p>
<p>"...It’s possible."</p>
<p>He whispered it out loud.</p>
<p>His fingers trembled slightly due to excitement.</p>
<p>He tried again immediately, pressing the Awakening Voucher against his abdomen once more.</p>
<p>The same result.</p>
<p>The same error.</p>
<p>The same limitation.</p>
<p>But now, he understood that this wasn’t a hard restriction... he just needed to meet the right requirement.</p>
<p>Meaning...</p>
<p>It wasn’t impossible.</p>
<p>He lowered his hand slowly, staring at the black voucher in awe.</p>
<p>"This system..."</p>
<p>He laughed quietly.</p>
<p>"...is insane."</p>
<p>He could awaken himself again.</p>
<p>Multiple awakenings...</p>
<p>The very thought shattered everything he thought he knew about awakeners.</p>
<p>In the world’s understanding, awakening was a one-time event.</p>
<p>Permanent.</p>
<p>Immutable.</p>
<p>You got what you got.</p>
<p>And that was it.</p>
<p>But the system...</p>
<p>Didn’t follow the world’s rules.</p>
<p>It had its own rules.</p>
<p>Then suddenly—</p>
<p>[SYSTEM PROMPT]</p>
<p>Host does not meet requirements for Secondary Awakening.</p>
<p>Alternative Option Available:</p>
<p>Increase Branch Count?</p>
<p>YES / NO</p>
<p>---</p>
<p>West froze.</p>
<p>"...Increase branch count?"</p>
<p>He blinked slowly.</p>
<p>He had never heard of such a thing.</p>
<p>Branches were permanent, determined at awakening.</p>
<p>That was the foundation of awakened hierarchy.</p>
<p>One branch meant limited growth.</p>
<p>Two branches meant superior potential.</p>
<p>Three branches meant elite tier.</p>
<p>If one awakened with two branches at Tier 1, they’d possess four branches at Tier 2.</p>
<p>If one started with three branches at Tier 1, they’d have Six branches at Tier 2.</p>
<p>Branches were destiny.</p>
<p>Branches were fate.</p>
<p>Branches could not be changed.</p>
<p>Yet...</p>
<p>The system was offering exactly that.</p>
