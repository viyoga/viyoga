<p>"Send everyone there immediately."</p>
<p>"Yes boss!"</p>
<p>The moment the call ended, Darius vanished.</p>
<p>BOOOOM!</p>
<p>The street outside exploded apart from the force of his movement while people screamed as a blur shot across rooftops.</p>
<p>He moved like a monster unleashed scaling buildings, traffic and roads... nothing slowed him down.</p>
<p>His thoughts spiraled violently the entire way. ’Who did this? Rivals? Debt collectors? A foreign gang?’</p>
<p>Were his children crying right now? Were they hurt?</p>
<p>Every possible scenario made his rage grow worse and by the time he reached the resort district, the pressure radiating from him was already terrifying.</p>
<p>The resort was absurdly luxurious with a gigantic glass structure overlooking almost the entirety of Misty High.</p>
<p>Waterfalls ran along the outer architecture while golden lights illuminated elegant balconies and rooftop gardens.</p>
<p>Ordinary people couldn’t even afford a single night here.</p>
<p>The moment Darius sensed the presence of his family, he moved.</p>
<p>BOOOOOOM!</p>
<p>The side of the skyscraper cracked as Darius launched upward.</p>
<p>In three leaps, he had already scaled more than seventy percent of the three hundred storey building.</p>
<p>And in five seconds, he practically scaled the entire building, landing at the very top.</p>
<p>THOOM~</p>
<p>The rooftop was beautiful with open-air and smooth marble floors.</p>
<p>Tall decorative lanterns glowing softly around shallow reflective pools while flower petals drifted gently through the evening breeze.</p>
<p>An infinity-edge view overlooked the entirety of Misty High beneath the night sky and at the center was an elegant dining table.</p>
<p>Seated around that table was his family, completely unharmed.</p>
<p>His daughter immediately lit up upon his arrival. "DADDY!"</p>
<p>She jumped up instantly and rushed toward him happily.</p>
<p>Darius caught her automatically while his wife smiled warmly as she stood to her feet.</p>
<p>"Honey..." She walked over and kissed his cheek lovingly before kissing his lips.</p>
<p>"Aww... you really shouldn’t have."</p>
<p>Darius blinked slowly in confusion. "...What?"</p>
<p>She laughed softly. "West from your gang told us everything."</p>
<p>His expression changed immediately. ’West? Who the hell is that?’</p>
<p>"The secret vacation," she looked genuinely touched. "I’ve been talking about wanting something like this for over a month..."</p>
<p>Her eyes softened lovingly. "You’re so sweet."</p>
<p>Darius slowly looked past her and spotted the masculine figure standing near the table with his back facing him.</p>
<p>The figure in black long coat finally turned around with a calm expression and a slight smirk.</p>
<p>Darius immediately recognised this person... ’The leader of Shadow Reapers.’</p>
<p>"Welcome boss," West said casually.</p>
<p>"As per your request, I brought them here safely and made sure they were comfortable."</p>
<p>Darius immediately stiffened as his branches instinctively activated beneath his skin.</p>
<p>Pressure exploded silently around him but his wife noticed nothing while West did.</p>
<p>He smiled slightly wider while looking over to the excited children.</p>
<p>"Dad! This place is awesome!"</p>
<p>"There’s a heated pool!"</p>
<p>"They brought us so much food!"</p>
<p>"And West is so cool!"</p>
<p>Darius stared at West while trying to control the suffocating pressure he was emitting.</p>
<p>"What..." he voiced in a dangerously low tone. "...are you doing?"</p>
<p>West simply walked closer casually and hugged Darius tightly like a loyal subordinate greeting his superior.</p>
<p>"Thank you boss," West smiled warmly for the family to see.</p>
<p>"This is the least I could do after you helped me blend into the gang as a newcomer."</p>
<p>The wife covered her mouth softly. "Aww..."</p>
<p>The children smiled brightly too while West leaned closer slightly and whispered directly into Darius’ ear.</p>
<p>"You don’t want to do this in front of the kids."</p>
<p>Darius’ body instantly tensed as West continued softly.</p>
<p>"Now be a good little boy and let me thank you properly for what you did all those years ago..."</p>
<p>The smile on West’s face sharpened slightly. "...if you don’t want your family getting hurt."</p>
<p>Pat~</p>
<p>Pat~</p>
<p>West gently patted the back of Darius’ head twice like calming an angry animal before adding one final thing.</p>
<p>"Oh," he whispered in a light tone. "Call off your gang members."</p>
<p>For a moment, Darius genuinely considered killing him anyway.</p>
<p>The rooftop trembled slightly from the pressure leaking out of his body but then, his daughter suddenly pulled his sleeve innocently.</p>
<p>"Dad?" her tiny voice instantly pulled him out of acting thoughtlessly.</p>
<p>Darius looked down at her and at his son, then his wife, realizing how unaware and vulnerable they were.</p>
<p>West had played this perfectly.</p>
<p>Darius slowly unclenched his fists while veins bulged violently along his arms.</p>
<p>His jaw looked like it might crack from how hard he was grinding his teeth but he had no choice.</p>
<p>He pulled out his phone and made the call. "Stand down."</p>
<p>His subordinates sounded confused immediately.</p>
<p>"Boss? But—"</p>
<p>"I said stand down."</p>
<p>Silence~</p>
<p>"...Yes boss."</p>
<p>West continued smiling pleasantly because to everyone else, it looked like a wholesome interaction between gang members but Darius knew the truth...</p>
<p>The only thing he didn’t know was why West was doing this.</p>
<p>Every second Darius stood there, he wanted to tear West apart but every time his eyes moved toward his children, he stopped.</p>
<p>So when West stepped back and casually said; "Oh, if you need anything I’ll be around. I’m here to make sure you all have a great time."</p>
<p>Darius immediately saw the opening. ’Good... leave... go... disappear... then I’ll find you later...’</p>
<p>He opened his mouth to further push the incentive. "I’m sure you have things to attend—"</p>
<p>But before he could finish, Yelena suddenly clapped softly.</p>
<p>"Oh no," she smiled warmly. "Let West join us."</p>
<p>Darius froze while the children immediately lit up.</p>
<p>"Yes!"</p>
<p>"Join us!"</p>
<p>"Please!"</p>
<p>West turned with almost suspiciously perfect timing.</p>
<p>"Oh?" he looked modest. "No no, I wouldn’t want to intrude..."</p>
<p>A soft smile instantly grazed his face. "...but if you insist."</p>
<p>He looked toward Yelena. "Thank you, Mrs. Yelena."</p>
<p>Yelena smiled in response. She was really a strikingly tall woman with long blonde hair that flowed over her shoulders. Even dressed casually, she carried herself with a certain regal composure.</p>
<p>Her sharp features were softened by warm eyes, smooth skin touched by sunset tones, and the sort of presence that made people unconsciously pay attention when she entered rooms.</p>
<p>She laughed softly. "Please. Just Yelena."</p>
