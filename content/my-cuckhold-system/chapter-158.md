<p>As West stepped away from the podium, several women in the audience stared at him with dreamy expressions.</p>

<p>"Man’s got game."</p>

<p>Handshake... Photo... Applause.</p>

<p>"West! Which gang will you be joining now that you’ve graduated?"</p>

<p>"Are the rumors true that you defeated another awakener during a school incident?"</p>
<p>"Do you plan to become a professional ruin explorer?"</p>

<p>West stepped outside the auditorium doors into the bright afternoon sunlight, checking for her but she was long gone.</p>
<p>He had hoped that seeing Freya inside the building had only been a coincidence but something about her sudden appearance unsettled him.</p>
<p>As he walked back into the auditorium, memory from that night in the alley resurfaced in his mind.</p>
<p>Freya pinning him against the wall... her fingers gripping his collar... her lips forcing their way onto his...</p>
<p>And the words she whispered afterward.</p>
<p>"You’re mine now."</p>

<p>"Crazy woman..."</p>
<p>He had dealt with many strange people recently, but Freya stood out in a different way.</p>
<p>She wasn’t just dangerous... she was unpredictable.</p>
<p>And unpredictability was something West preferred to avoid whenever possible.</p>
<p>Still, he pushed the thought aside.</p>
<p>Today was supposed to be a good day.</p>
<p>He wasn’t going to let Freya ruin it.</p>
<p>The celebration continued as students hugged their friends while parents snapped photos nonstop. Teachers stood nearby congratulating their graduates while reporters continued trying to gather comments from anyone interesting enough to interview.</p>

<p>"West!"</p>

<p>"It’s fine."</p>
