<p>The train horn blared drawing VERY close now.</p>
<p>"I’LL TALK!" Jax screamed.</p>
<p>The masked figure leaned closer.</p>
<p>"You have less than 30 seconds."</p>
<p>Jax’s voice cracked.</p>
<p>"OKAY OKAY OKAY."</p>
<p>The figure leaned in, ear inches from Jax’s mouth.</p>
<p>"Speak."</p>
<p>Jax inhaled deeply as the train thundered toward them while the masked figure waited.</p>
<p>"I sent it to..."</p>
<p>Jax suddenly grinned.</p>
<p>"Your momma."</p>
<p>The masked figure froze.</p>
<p>"What?"</p>
<p>"Your momma owns it."</p>
<p>The train horn SCREAMED.</p>
<p>Three seconds.</p>
<p>The masked figure panicked slightly.</p>
<p>"What did you just say?!"</p>
<p>"I SAID YOUR MOMMA!"</p>
<p>The masked figure let out a low sigh.</p>
<p>"Tell me who owns the money!"</p>
<p>Jax squinted.</p>
<p>"Still your momma."</p>
<p>The masked figure facepalmed but Jax kept going.</p>
<p>"And if not her, then probably your auntie. It runs in the family."</p>
<p>Jax leaned forward slightly despite being tied.</p>
<p>"Listen man. If you wanted financial advice, you could’ve just asked."</p>
<p>The masked figure growled.</p>
<p>"You think this is funny?"</p>
<p>"I’m literally tied to a railway. Humor is all I have."</p>
<p>The masked figure grabbed him by the collar.</p>
<p>"You will die here today..."</p>
<p>The sound of the train grew louder as the masked figure stepped back.</p>
<p>Metal grinding against metal rang out loudly accompanied by blaring sounds.</p>
<p>Jax stared at it with wide eyes and shaky breath. He was only using humor as a coping mechanism... he was very scared right now.</p>
<p>"Well," he muttered to himself, "this is unfortunate."</p>
<p>The masked figure stood several feet away with his arms folded.</p>
<p>The rumbling grew violent beneath Jax’s back. The track vibrated so intensely his teeth chattered.</p>
<p>He swallowed.</p>
<p>"Okay listen!" he yelled toward the masked figure. "If this is about the money, we can split it! Fifty-fifty! Sixty-forty! Okay seventy-thirty but I keep the emotional trauma compensation!"</p>
<p>The masked figure didn’t move.</p>
<p>Jax squeezed his eyes shut.</p>
<p>"...Your momma still owns it!"</p>
<p>The train roared over him as he braced for impact and the crushing pain.</p>
<p>’Looks like I’m dying a virgin... Fuck you West, see you on the other side brother.’</p>
<p>However...</p>
<p>Even after seconds had gone by... there was nothing.</p>
<p>No pain...</p>
<p>No pressure...</p>
<p>No bones snapping...</p>
<p>He cautiously opened one eye and was stunned by what he saw.</p>
<p>The train cars were phasing through him like mist through air.</p>
<p>The were transparent...</p>
<p>Ghostlike.</p>
<p>Each carriage slid harmlessly across his body.</p>
<p>"...Huh?"</p>
<p>The world around him began to blur.</p>
<p>The railway, trains and sky started dissolving like watercolor dragged through water.</p>
<p>The rumble faded and the horns dimmed until everything went silent.</p>
<p>The entire scene suddenly collapsed and Jax found himself no longer tied.</p>
<p>He stood in a wide open empty lot under dim evening light.</p>
<p>He blinked rapidly.</p>
<p>"What in the budget Inception was that?"</p>
<p>Slow clapping echoed behind him.</p>
<p>He turned to see the masked figure standing there, removing the mask.</p>
<p>Jax squinted.</p>
<p>"...West?"</p>
<p>West pulled the mask off fully with an almost amused expression.</p>
<p>Jax stared.</p>
<p>"You—"</p>
<p>Behind West, another presence materialized.</p>
<p>Gor’thala stepped forward with her magical aura spreading across the air.</p>
<p>Jax’s mouth dropped open.</p>
<p>"Okay I’m either dead or high."</p>
<p>West tilted his head slightly.</p>
<p>"It was an illusion."</p>
<p>Gor’thala’s voice flowed like silk.</p>
<p>"I constructed the scenario to test the resilience of his mind and loyalty to my Master."</p>
<p>Jax slowly looked between them.</p>
<p>"Test?"</p>
<p>West nodded.</p>
<p>"To see if you’d talk."</p>
<p>Jax blinked.</p>
<p>"You tied me to fake train tracks."</p>
<p>"Yes."</p>
<p>"You let me think I was about to die."</p>
<p>"Yes."</p>
<p>"You almost made me pee myself."</p>
<p>West considered that.</p>
<p>"Almost?"</p>
<p>Jax stared at him for a long second before bursting into laughter.</p>
<p>"You psycho!" he wheezed. "That was insane! I genuinely thought I was gone!"</p>
<p>He wiped at his eyes.</p>
<p>"But that was a top-tier prank. I ain’t even mad."</p>
<p>West watched him carefully.</p>
<p>"I believe you."</p>
<p>Jax tilted his head.</p>
<p>"Believe what?"</p>
<p>"That I can trust you."</p>
<p>Jax scoffed.</p>
<p>"Bro. Of course you can."</p>
<p>West shook his head slightly.</p>
<p>"No. I needed to be 100% certain."</p>
<p>His expression changed subtly, becoming more serious now.</p>
<p>"I intend to build my own gang."</p>
<p>Jax blinked in surprise.</p>
<p>"...Oh."</p>
<p>He processed that.</p>
<p>"...OH."</p>
<p>He took a step back.</p>
<p>"Like... Not just join one... Build one?"</p>
<p>"Yes."</p>
<p>Jax ran a hand through his hair.</p>
<p>"Okay that’s kinda hard."</p>
<p>He looked West up and down.</p>
<p>"You’d definitely be the cool boss."</p>
<p>West ignored the compliment.</p>
<p>"I don’t plan to recruit traditionally."</p>
<p>Jax frowned.</p>
<p>"What does that even mean?"</p>
<p>West’s eyes sharpened.</p>
<p>"It means I don’t need to rely on external factions."</p>
<p>Jax blinked.</p>
<p>"...Okay you lost me."</p>
<p>West reached into his inventory and then a small glowing voucher materialized in his hand.</p>
<p>It glowed faintly with strange violet energy.</p>
<p>Jax stared.</p>
<p>"What is that?"</p>
<p>"An item that can make someone into Tier 1 Awakener."</p>
<p>"...You’re joking."</p>
<p>West didn’t smile.</p>
<p>"Put this on your lower abdomen."</p>
<p>Jax froze.</p>
<p>"...Excuse me?"</p>
<p>"It will make you an awakened."</p>
<p>Jax stared at him in utter silence and then looked at the voucher before looking back at West.</p>
<p>"...You’re serious."</p>
<p>"Yes."</p>
<p>The humor drained from Jax’s face.</p>
<p>He stepped closer slowly.</p>
<p>"Wait. Wait. Wait. So you’re telling me..."</p>
<p>He pointed at the voucher.</p>
<p>"That thing?"</p>
<p>"Yes."</p>
<p>"...Turns normal people into awakened?"</p>
<p>"Yes."</p>
<p>"...And you have it."</p>
<p>"Yes."</p>
<p>"...And you’re offering it to me."</p>
<p>"Yes."</p>
<p>Jax stared in stunned silence.</p>
<p>A million thoughts raced through his mind.</p>
<p>Could West... create awakeners?</p>
<p>Was that even possible?</p>
<p>That wasn’t just rare.</p>
<p>That was revolutionary.</p>
<p>That was world-shifting.</p>
<p>"That’s why you tested me," Jax said slowly.</p>
<p>West nodded.</p>
<p>"If you can’t be trusted under threat of death, I cannot entrust you with power."</p>
<p>Jax exhaled.</p>
<p>"This is crazy."</p>
<p>He looked at the glowing voucher again.</p>
<p>"...You’re basically saying I can go from barista to battle unit."</p>
<p>"Essentially."</p>
<p>Jax blinked rapidly.</p>
<p>"I mean... I’ve always wanted superpowers."</p>
<p>He reached for the voucher but West raised a hand.</p>
<p>"Wait."</p>
<p>Jax paused mid-reach.</p>
<p>"What now?"</p>
<p>"You’ll likely go unconscious."</p>
<p>"...Like nap unconscious or hospital unconscious?"</p>
<p>"Probably nap. But you’ll need time."</p>
<p>Jax lowered his hand.</p>
<p>"Okay that’s fair."</p>
<p>West looked around the empty lot.</p>
<p>"We’ll activate it before we leave."</p>
<p>Jax nodded slowly.</p>
<p>"I still have something else to do..."</p>
