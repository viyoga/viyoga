<p>Aria followed behind West.</p>
<p>"...I tried handling it through proper channels," she said. "Legal, negotiation, everything. But they’re not budging."</p>
<p>West stopped again with his back still turned to her.</p>
<p>"...Of course they’re not," he paused before turning with a look of clarity.</p>
<p>"...This is what I get for trying to do things the right way."</p>
<p>Aria watched him closely as West’s lips curled slightly.</p>
<p>"I forget..."</p>
<p>His voice dropped into a very chilling tone.</p>
<p>"...the right way doesn’t work in this world."</p>
<p>A heavy and unsettling silence settled over the construction site as West looked around one last time.</p>
<p>West walked slowly through the partially completed structure, causing the sound of footsteps to echo across the surroundings due to the unfinished flooring.</p>
<p>Steel beams stretched overhead with glass panels already installed in some sections, giving the place a modern and almost futuristic aesthetic.</p>
<p>Despite being incomplete, it was beautiful... shaping up to look exactly as he had envisioned it...</p>
<p>A headquarters worthy of the Shadow Reapers.</p>
<p>His eyes traced the layout, scanning the training areas, command rooms, operational hubs and living quarters...</p>
<p>Everything was coming together perfectly but this interruption, threatened to put a dent on his plans.</p>
<p>His gaze hardened slightly.</p>
<p>"...I’m not making the mistake of trying to do things right ever again," he muttered.</p>
<p>Behind him, Aria stayed quiet because she could feel that quiet anger building beneath the surface.</p>
<p>Multiple heavy footsteps soon rang out from behind them.</p>
<p>West didn’t turn immediately because he already knew who the approaching figures were.</p>
<p>The same guard from earlier stepped forward again but this time, he wasn’t alone.</p>
<p>Ten others stood behind him, making them eleven in total.</p>
<p>They were all armed and to a normal person, they’d look intimidating... but West was far from normal.</p>
<p>"This is him," the guard from earlier pointed at West’s.</p>
<p>One of the others who was taller and broader, seemingly the leader of the group, stepped forward.</p>
<p>"You’ve been warned," he said coldly. "Leave now."</p>
<p>West finally turned slowly with a calm expression.</p>
<p>"I paid for this place," he said simply. "Which means..."</p>
<p>His eyes swept across them.</p>
<p>"...you’re the ones trespassing."</p>
<p>"Are you joking, mister?" Another guard scoffed while stepping forward, gripping his weapon tighter.</p>
<p>"Leave now... or we’ll make you leave."</p>
<p>Silence reigned for a few seconds before West exhaled lightly.</p>
<p>He reached for his tracksuit jacket and began to take it off.</p>
<p>The guards frowned.</p>
<p>"...What is he doing?"</p>
<p>West folded it neatly before handing it to Aria without looking.</p>
<p>"Well," he stated.</p>
<p>"I like this jacket."</p>
<p>"And I don’t want to stain it with blood."</p>
<p>Looks of confusion appeared on their faces.</p>
<p>"...What—"</p>
<p>Before they could blink, West vanished...</p>
<p>Fwwhooommssshhh—</p>
<p>One moment he was there, and the next, he was behind one of them.</p>
<p>Before the man could react, a firm hand clamped onto his head.</p>
<p>BOOM!</p>
<p>West lifted him clean off the ground and slammed him headfirst into a massive piece of machinery beside them.</p>
<p>Metal dented inward as a sickening crack echoed and blood splattered across the surface.</p>
<p>The man’s body went limp instantly but West wasn’t done...</p>
<p>He lifted him again and drove him into the ground this time.</p>
<p>SLAM!</p>
<p>The impact sent a dull shockwave through the floor as cracks appeared on its surface.</p>
<p>Blood pooled around West’s feet as he smacked his palms together like he was dusting dirt off them.</p>
<p>A brief silence accompanied with shock ensued before chaos broke lose.</p>
<p>"Attack him!"</p>
<p>The guards closest to him shot forward while swinging batons at his face, aggressively.</p>
<p>Unfortunately, from West’s point of view, they were as slow as sloths.</p>
<p>Each swing missed, cutting through empty air as he stepped between them effortlessly.</p>
<p>One of them swung widely but West caught the weapon mid-motion and yanked it clean from his grip.</p>
<p>CRACK!</p>
<p>He smashed it straight into the man’s face, sending him flying backward with blood spraying mid-air.</p>
<p>Another came from the side but West spun swiftly in response while swinging his arm out.</p>
<p>SMACK!</p>
<p>A single backhand collided with the guard’s face causing teeth and blood to fly out of his mouth.</p>
<p>The man slammed into a pillar over twenty feet away before collapsing to the ground.</p>
<p>All of a sudden... a loud gunshot rang out from behind.</p>
<p>Bang!</p>
<p>A bullet tore through the air and hit West, right at the neck.</p>
<p>They all froze in place, as looks of relief appeared on their faces.</p>
<p>West had been stopped before he could wreak any more havoc.</p>
<p>One of the guards held the shoulder of the other guard before praising him; "Good call."</p>
<p>Just as they were basking in relief, one of them seemed to notice that West hadn’t dropped.</p>
<p>He just stood there unmoving.</p>
<p>"Hey something’s not right..."</p>
<p>That was when they noticed that the bullet, stayed there on West’s neck, stuck in place like it had hit steel.</p>
<p>West reached up casually, pinched it between his fingers and pulled it off.</p>
<p>A small red mark remained on his skin but that was it. The bullet itself had flattened and crushed upon making contact with his skin.</p>
<p>Continous consumption of the contents of the Chalice Of Vitality had reinforced West’s skin to the point where bullets were no longer a threat.</p>
<p>West turned slowly.</p>
<p>"...Ouch."</p>
<p>That single word, sent a chill through them.</p>
<p>The remaining seven guards stepped back instinctively with widened eyes as their breathing turned uneven.</p>
<p>"...He’s an awakener..."</p>
<p>"We’re fucked—"</p>
<p>"...one of those monsters..."</p>
<p>One of them snapped.</p>
<p>"Hey—no one told me this job involved awakeners—I quit!"</p>
<p>He turned and ran.</p>
<p>This singular action triggered more panic causing two more to follow before another joined...</p>
<p>They broke formation and more started to turn around in order to flee.</p>
<p>West didn’t even bother chasing... he simply spoke in a calm but authoritative tone;</p>
<p>"If anyone takes another step forward..." his voice dropped, turning even colder.</p>
<p>"...I promise..."</p>
<p>"...it will be your last."</p>
<p>Some froze instantly as fear rooted them to the spot.</p>
