<p>Ross’s stomach twisted while the two gang members moved nervously beside him.</p>
<p>They were the only two still standing with him.</p>
<p>Which meant it was now three against three.</p>
<p>Ross and the two standing Black Furnace members.</p>
<p>Against...</p>
<p>West, Jax and Nina.</p>
<p>On paper, it looked balanced but after what Ross had just witnessed... there was no balance.</p>
<p>Ross felt a tightness creeping into his chest.</p>
<p>’Am I... scared?’</p>
<p>These people weren’t normal awakeners... they were monsters.</p>
<p>Before Ross could gather his thoughts, a shadow suddenly appeared in front of him.</p>
<p>Ross froze as he spotted West standing only a few feet away with an entirely different presence now.</p>
<p>His entire body radiated malice, pain, anger and a suffocating killing intent that made the air feel heavier.</p>
<p>Ross instinctively stepped back while West spoke in an ice cold tone.</p>
<p>"Where did you send them?"</p>
<p>Ross didn’t even have time to answer because the next moment, West’s fist descended...</p>
<p>BOOM!</p>
<p>Ross felt like the world exploded as the punch connected with his chest so violently that the shockwave alone cracked the ground beneath them.</p>
<p>Fwwwhiiiiii~</p>
<p>Ross’s body flew through the air like a broken missile and smashed straight through a concrete wall behind the auditorium.</p>
<p>Dust and debris exploded outward but West didn’t slow down for even a second.</p>
<p>He shot forward immediately, chasing Ross through the broken wall.</p>
<p>Inside the ruined hallway beyond, West grabbed Ross by the head and slammed him down.</p>
<p>BANG</p>
<p>Ross’s skull hit the floor but before he knew it, he was getting smashed through a wall... and then another wall right after...</p>
<p>West dragged him through the hallway like a rag doll, smashing him against every solid surface he could find.</p>
<p>Punch.</p>
<p>Kick.</p>
<p>Punch.</p>
<p>Kick.</p>
<p>The sound of bone meeting concrete echoed violently.</p>
<p>Ross tried to fight back but he couldn’t even free himself, neither could he focus well enough to channel his branch..</p>
<p>West’s attacks were relentless, brutal and unstoppable.</p>
<p>Blood sprayed across the floor and walls as Ross’s face quickly became unrecognizable.</p>
<p>His lips split, his nose shattered and one eye swelled shut instantly.</p>
<p>West’s voice echoed with fury between each strike.</p>
<p>"Gor’thala..."</p>
<p>BANG</p>
<p>"Seraphyra..."</p>
<p>BOOM</p>
<p>"Aurethia..."</p>
<p>His fist slammed down again.</p>
<p>"Where did you send them?"</p>
<p>Ross wheezed as blood poured from his mouth.</p>
<p>West grabbed him by the collar and slammed him into a pillar.</p>
<p>CRACK!</p>
<p>"Where?"</p>
<p>Another punch land...</p>
<p>"WHERE?"</p>
<p>Ross’s body barely responded anymore.</p>
<p>His face was swollen beyond recognition and blood dripped from his ears.</p>
<p>He let out heavy breaths as his vision turned white...</p>
<p>In the auditorium, the two remaining Black Furnace members stared at the destruction happening through the broken wall with pale faces.</p>
<p>"...We can’t fight that."</p>
<p>One of them whispered while the other nodded quickly.</p>
<p>Without another word both of them fled the scene immediately without even looking back.</p>
<p>Meanwhile inside the hallway, West raised his fist again while Ross’s head hung limply in his grip.</p>
<p>West was about to bring the next blow down when a firm hand caught his wrist.</p>
<p>A familiar voice accompanied it.</p>
<p>"Enough."</p>
<p>West turned his head and standing beside him was Zu Li.</p>
<p>Her expression remained composed as always.</p>
<p>"Unless," she continued calmly, "you intend to take his life."</p>
<p>West stared at Ross’s broken body for a moment before staring back at her.</p>
<p>"He should tell me where he sent my summons."</p>
<p>His voice still carried dangerous anger. He didn’t care if he took Ross life right here right now, he just wanted his summons.</p>
<p>Zu Li sighed slightly.</p>
<p>"They weren’t sent anywhere."</p>
<p>West frowned while she gestured toward the destroyed auditorium.</p>
<p>"That device they used is a summon siphoning tool."</p>
<p>"It temporarily drains the energy sustaining your summons causing them to return to the summon space which will also be unaccessible for a period of time."</p>
<p>West’s eyes narrowed slightly.</p>
<p>"They’ll come back?"</p>
<p>"Yes."</p>
<p>She nodded.</p>
<p>"Once your branch energy stabilizes again."</p>
<p>"They weren’t destroyed."</p>
<p>"They were simply forced back."</p>
<p>West’s anger eased slightly and the tension in his shoulders loosened.</p>
<p>After a few seconds, he let go of Ross.</p>
<p>The man collapsed onto the floor like a broken puppet.</p>
<p>West didn’t care that he not only stole Ross’s woman and humiliate him, he also beat him half to death... this was a day people would be talking about for a very long time.</p>
<p>West suddenly noticed people standing behind Zu Li.</p>
<p>Upon closer look, he realized that there were over a dozen awakeners all wearing the emblem of the Dragon Fang Gang.</p>
<p>Zu Li had arrived with reinforcements.</p>
<p>Apparently, she had come after hearing about the attack through the news broadcasts and seeing West school get taken over by a gang...</p>
<p>But when they arrived at the battlefield, she was stunned.</p>
<p>The entire auditorium was devastated and up to twenty experienced awakeners had been wiped out by three newly awakened individuals.</p>
<p>Her gaze slowly returned to West.</p>
<p>’This kid...’</p>
<p>She felt like she was looking at something dangerous... something that was growing at an alarmingly fast and powerful rate...</p>
<p>Something that could shake the balance of the awakened world in the future.</p>
<p>Zu Li turned toward the surviving Black Furnace members who were still crawling or barely conscious on the ground.</p>
<p>Her voice spread across the ruined auditorium like a blade.</p>
<p>"Listen carefully."</p>
<p>Every injured gang member froze.</p>
<p>"West Einstein is under the protection of the Dragon Fang Gang."</p>
<p>Her eyes hardened.</p>
<p>"If anyone from Black Furnace..."</p>
<p>"...or any other gang..."</p>
<p>"...tries to harm him again..."</p>
<p>Her voice dropped slightly.</p>
<p>"...they will be harming me."</p>
<p>"And the Dragon Fang Gang does not take that lightly."</p>
<p>The warning was crystal clear.</p>
<p>This was a message not only to the survivors here... but to the entire underworld.</p>
<p>And anyone smart enough to understand what just happened today would think very carefully before making West their enemy again.</p>
<p>"You didn’t have to do that. I don’t want to owe you any more favours," West muttered while pulling off his ruined suit and shirt to reveal a wonderfully sculpted lean muscled body.</p>
<p>Despite the injuries on his shoulders and chest, West still looked devastatingly attractive and Miss Zu Li couldn’t help but look the other way.</p>
<p>"Put on a shirt or something..."</p>
<p>"I would but its destroyed. Anyways, don’t come to my rescue any more in the future. Now I have two favours to repay," West voiced while turning away.</p>
<p>"No, just one. Just need you to show up next week based on our agreement... and your bleeding, should I..."</p>
<p>"No I’ll take it care of it. See you next week," West responded while heading in the direction of Nina and Jax to check on them.</p>
<p>The graduation ended with a bang... but not the type that came with excitement...</p>
