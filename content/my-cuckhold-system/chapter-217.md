<p>He scratched the back of his head. "My bad... got sidetracked."</p>
<p>Mei laughed softly. "Clearly."</p>
<p>"I’ll be there soon."</p>
<p>"You better be."</p>
<p>The moment the call ended, West stood up instantly.</p>
<p>"...Right."</p>
<p>No more thinking or distractions, it was time for action.</p>
<p>He stepped forward and jumped.</p>
<p>Fwwwhiiii~</p>
<p>The city blurred as his figure traveled in an arc across the air, causing wind to roar past him.</p>
<p>He landed on the next building rooftop effortlessly before charging forward and jumping towards another.</p>
<p>Each leap carried him dozens of feet across the skyline as his body moved with unnatural speed.</p>
<p>He didn’t slow down for even a moment, making the distance between buildings completely meaningless.</p>
<p>Within minutes, he reached his area and leapt from the top of an 80-storey building to his own 30-storey apartment.</p>
<p>He landed smoothly on the balcony, completely ignoring the stares of awe coming from down below.</p>
<p>And just like that, he was back in his own apartment.</p>
<p>...</p>
<p>...</p>
<p>West arrived at exactly 6:00 PM at Renzhi’s residence.</p>
<p>The gates of Renzhi’s estate opened smoothly, and he stepped out of the vehicle with the kind of confidence that didn’t need to announce itself.</p>
<p>His outfit struck that perfect balance between casual and standard. He was clad in dark fitted trousers that tapered cleanly at the ankle, a charcoal long-sleeve shirt with the sleeves slightly rolled to reveal his forearms, and a light tailored jacket draped over it that gave him a refined but effortless look.</p>
<p>His hair had been slightly tousled, purposely making it less neat than usual, giving him a more relaxed, almost roguish appearance.</p>
<p>The fake moustache sat subtly on his face, enough to distort recognition without looking ridiculous.</p>
<p>To anyone who didn’t know better, he was simply "Smith."</p>
<p>The door opened before he even reached it.</p>
<p>"Right on time," Mei voiced while leaning slightly against the frame with her lips curving into a pleased smile.</p>
<p>West smirked faintly. "Wouldn’t want to keep my girl waiting."</p>
<p>She reached forward and planted a kiss on his lips before stepping aside to let him in.</p>
<p>Her hand brushed briefly against his as she guided him inside.</p>
<p>"Everyone’s already at the table."</p>
<p>"Of course they are."</p>
<p>As they walked through the mansion, West’s gaze remained calm and observant with a relaxed composure.</p>
<p>By the time they reached the dining area, the entire table turned, setting their eyes on them.</p>
<p>Rin’s expression lit up instantly. "...Wow."</p>
<p>She didn’t even try to hide it.</p>
<p>"You’re much hotter in person."</p>
<p>The words came out bluntly, without hesitation, breaking the tension in the room immediately.</p>
<p>Before anyone could react, she was already on her feet, rushing over.</p>
<p>"Come, come—sit here!"</p>
<p>She grabbed West’s arm and pulled him toward the seat beside her, completely ignoring any formality.</p>
<p>Mei sighed while rubbing her temple. "Rin..."</p>
<p>West chuckled softly, allowing himself to be dragged along. "I like her already."</p>
<p>Rin grinned proudly. "Of course you do. Muhuahaha what’s not to like?"</p>
<p>West settled into the seat with a relaxed posture as he glanced around the table.</p>
<p>"Good evening," he greeted with a smooth and respectful tone.</p>
<p>Eric gave a small nod in acknowledgment, quiet as always.</p>
<p>Lianhua didn’t speak immediately but her eyes flickered toward him for just a fraction of a second before she looked away again, maintaining composure.</p>
<p>West turned toward Renzhi, stood slightly and extended his hand.</p>
<p>"Nice to finally meet you."</p>
<p>There was a long pause as Renzhi’s gaze lingered on West’s face, studying him carefully, almost as if trying to peel something back.</p>
<p>Then, he slowly reached out and their hands met.</p>
<p>Renzhi squeezed, putting a measured pressure into the handshake... the kind that would make normal people wince but West didn’t.</p>
<p>His expression didn’t change in the slightest... if anything, he even looked a little amused.</p>
<p>Renzhi noticed that West was unaffected but he hid it well, releasing the handshake with a neutral expression.</p>
<p>"...You look familiar."</p>
<p>West tilted his head slightly. "Do I?"</p>
<p>Renzhi narrowed his eyes just a fraction. "...Have we met before?"</p>
<p>West smiled lightly. "I just have one of those faces."</p>
<p>Renzhi leaned back slowly before speaking again. "...You’re not what I expected."</p>
<p>West rested his arm casually against the table. "How so?"</p>
<p>Renzhi’s gaze moved over him again. "You don’t look like the typical fitness instructor."</p>
<p>He was referencing it without saying it outright... West was clearly in shape, but not exaggerated. He was not the bulky, overly built type one would expect.</p>
<p>West shrugged before displaying a faint smile. "That’s kind of the point of fitness... balance."</p>
<p>"Having the right amount everywhere... instead of just being unnecessarily large."</p>
<p>Renzhi opened his mouth to continue but Mei’s eyes shot toward him.</p>
<p>Without saying a word, she was giving her father a warning to stop with the incessant questioning.</p>
<p>Renzhi paused and coughed lightly. "...Right."</p>
<p>Dinner began and if it wasn’t for Rin, it would have been painfully quiet but Rin had no intention of allowing that...</p>
<p>She spoke nonstop, moving from school gossip to activities to random social media trends and inevitably... to West.</p>
<p>"Smith."</p>
<p>"So you’re Mei’s boyfriend, right?" she questioned while leaning forward slightly. "Do you guys kiss? Like, a lot? Or do you do—"</p>
<p>"Rin!" Renzhi’s voice cut in sharply.</p>
<p>She blinked. "What? I’m just asking."</p>
<p>West chuckled under his breath as Renzhi’s grip tightened around his cutlery subconsciously.</p>
<p>Snap~</p>
<p>The utensil broke cleanly in his hand causing the maid to immediately step in, replacing it without a word.</p>
<p>"Rin," Renzhi called again. "can you keep quiet for one second?"</p>
<p>Rin huffed but leaned back.</p>
<p>"...Fine."</p>
<p>Meanwhile, West was thoroughly enjoying himself.</p>
<p>Every reaction... every flicker of discomfort from Renzhi... every time Rin pushed just a little too far...</p>
<p>It was... entertaining.</p>
<p>Because Renzhi had no idea that the man sitting across from him, smiling politely and answering questions, was none other than the same West he hated.</p>
<p>And was also...</p>
<p>Far more involved in his household than he could ever imagine.</p>
<p>Under the table, Mei’s foot brushed lightly against West’s as she shot him a sultry look.</p>
<p>She trailed slightly upward before retreating just as quickly.</p>
