<p>West pointed toward the projection. "So that’s everything I’ve managed to gather."</p>
<p>Jax whistled. "A castle the size of a city?"</p>
<p>"That’s insane."</p>
<p>Nina nodded. "And every first-year awakener is going to rush in there."</p>
<p>West folded his arms. "Exactly."</p>
<p>"Which means competition will be fierce."</p>
<p>Aria stepped forward. "I also prepared something."</p>
<p>She handed West another document. "Our current first-year awakened members."</p>
<p>West’s eyebrows rose as he looked through it. "...That’s it?"</p>
<p>Aria nodded. "I’m afraid so."</p>
<p>"Besides you..."</p>
<p>"There’s Jax."</p>
<p>"Nina."</p>
<p>"And..."</p>
<p>She pointed further down.</p>
<p>"Leo."</p>
<p>"Morgan."</p>
<p>West counted again. "Four."</p>
<p>Jax scratched his head. "...West."</p>
<p>"That isn’t enough."</p>
<p>West looked up. "We’ll need at least fifteen if we’re going to maximize our chances."</p>
<p>Nina nodded in agreement. "And that’s assuming everyone survives."</p>
<p>Silence filled the room.</p>
<p>West slowly began pacing from one end of the room to the other.</p>
<p>His footsteps echoed quietly as they all waited, simply watching him think.</p>
<p>Several long moments passed before West stopped.</p>
<p>A slow smile spread across his face. "I’ve been looking at this the wrong way."</p>
<p>The others looked at him. "Hm?"</p>
<p>West turned around. "If we don’t have enough first-year awakeners..."</p>
<p>He tapped lightly against the table. "...then we’ll simply make more."</p>
<p>Jax blinked. "...Boss?"</p>
<p>West’s smile only widened. "It looks like..."</p>
<p>"...it’s time to create some new first-year awakeners."</p>
<p>In the meantime, West sent for the other two first year awakeners in their gang.</p>
<p>About thirty minutes later, West stood before the holographic strategy table inside the conference room while Aria projected dozens of ruin reports across the air.</p>
<p>Jax sat lazily with his boots resting on another chair while Nina remained standing with folded arms.</p>
<p>Beside them stood the other two first-year awakeners currently in Shadow Reapers.</p>
<p>The first was a lean young man named Roy Wesker. He was nineteen years old with dark auburn hair cut short on the sides with the top slightly messy, giving him a perpetually carefree appearance.</p>
<p>Despite his relaxed smile, his emerald eyes were always scanning his surroundings. He possessed a Transformation Branch, allowing portions of his body to morph into hardened obsidian-like claws and armor.</p>
<p>Leo rarely spoke unless he had something useful to contribute.</p>
<p>Standing beside him was Morgana Perce, an eighteen-year-old woman with shoulder-length silver-blonde hair tied into a practical ponytail.</p>
<p>She wasn’t particularly tall, but she carried herself with confidence. Her amber eyes rarely betrayed emotion, and unlike the louder members of the gang, Morgan preferred observing before acting.</p>
<p>She also had a transformation Branch that allowed her morph into evolved versions of any animal she chose.</p>
<p>West looked around the room. "You four."</p>
<p>His tone became serious. "If Castle Extingo is truly as dangerous as every report suggests..."</p>
<p>"...then your current strength isn’t enough."</p>
<p>Jax scratched his cheek. "You’re not wrong."</p>
<p>Nina nodded as well. "We’re still too weak."</p>
<p>West pointed toward the holographic image of awakened branches.</p>
<p>"To survive in there, you need to increase the light across your branches."</p>
<p>"Which means..."</p>
<p>"...Affinity Stones."</p>
<p>Everyone sighed upon hearing that.</p>
<p>Leo rubbed the back of his neck. "Boss..."</p>
<p>"Those things are ridiculously hard to find."</p>
<p>Morgan agreed. "And ridiculously expensive."</p>
<p>West nodded. "I know."</p>
<p>Affinity Stones weren’t ordinary awakened materials...</p>
<p>They represented one of the primary methods through which awakeners cultivated their branches.</p>
<p>Every branch possessed dormant pathways stretching from one end to the other.</p>
<p>The more those pathways became illuminated through affinity, the stronger the awakened grew.</p>
<p>It wasn’t an overnight process and it required enormous quantities of suitable Affinity Stones.</p>
<p>West enlarged another projection which displayed blue, red and white stones.</p>
<p>"Jax."</p>
<p>"Nina."</p>
<p>"You both possess Bloodline Awakenings."</p>
<p>"You’ll require Blue Affinity Stones."</p>
<p>Both nodded as West then turned toward Leo and Morgan. "You two possess Transformation Branches."</p>
<p>"You’ll need Red Affinity Stones."</p>
<p>Morgan sighed. "I’ve only managed to absorb one since awakening."</p>
<p>Leo laughed bitterly. "I’ve not even managed a single one."</p>
<p>West wasn’t surprised.</p>
<p>Affinity Stones weren’t simply rare, they were outrageously rare.</p>
<p>Even gangs with enormous financial backing struggled to gather sufficient quantities.</p>
<p>West looked toward Jax and Nina and suddenly recalled another issue. "The two of you..."</p>
<p>"...have three branches."</p>
<p>Jax groaned dramatically. "Oh come on."</p>
<p>"Don’t remind me."</p>
<p>West chuckled. "Unfortunately..."</p>
<p>"You’ll require roughly three times the resources."</p>
<p>Nina pinched the bridge of her nose. "So we’re basically bankrupt before we even start."</p>
<p>Aria finally stepped forward. "I’ve already begun preparing for that."</p>
<p>Everyone looked toward her as she expanded another series of holographic reports.</p>
<p>"We have scanning database that estimates the probability of a ruin possessing affinity stones."</p>
<p>She highlighted several ruins scattered across neighboring cities. "These ruins have higher-than- 10% chances of containing Affinity Stones."</p>
<p>West smiled. "Exactly."</p>
<p>"From now on... you four will stop prioritizing Misty High based ruins."</p>
<p>"You’ll prioritize growth."</p>
<p>Jax raised an eyebrow. "So..."</p>
<p>"We’re traveling?"</p>
<p>West nodded. "Outside Misty High."</p>
<p>"Some of these ruins are several cities away."</p>
<p>Morgan looked impressed. "That’s going to be expensive."</p>
<p>"It is."</p>
<p>"But necessary."</p>
<p>Aria continued. "I’ll personally handle every booking."</p>
<p>"We’ll specifically reserve ruins with the highest recorded affinity yield."</p>
<p>Leo frowned. "And if we still don’t find any?"</p>
<p>West answered immediately. "Then we clear another."</p>
<p>"And another."</p>
<p>"And another."</p>
<p>Aria quietly added, "Statistically..."</p>
<p>"Even after clearing ten suitable ruins..."</p>
<p>"...finding even a single Affinity Stone isn’t guaranteed."</p>
<p>The room became noticeably quieter as everyone finally understood how difficult the coming months would be.</p>
<p>West crossed his arms. "I don’t care."</p>
<p>"We keep searching and if we can’t acquire from any cleared ruins, we’ll make purchases of any one we can find for sale."</p>
<p>"I refuse to walk into Castle Extingo underprepared."</p>
<p>He looked toward the projected team assignments. "You four won’t be going alone."</p>
<p>Another list appeared. "I’ve assigned ten additional Shadow Reapers members to accompany you."</p>
<p>"They’ll provide support."</p>
<p>Jax grinned. "So basically..."</p>
<p>"We’re becoming a dedicated expedition squad."</p>
<p>"Exactly," West nodded. "You leave tomorrow morning."</p>
<p>Everyone exchanged determined looks.</p>
<p>Three months was all they had. Three months to become significantly stronger.</p>
<p>Three months before one of the greatest opportunities in awakened history arrived.</p>
<p>As the meeting concluded and everyone began leaving... West remained behind.</p>
<p>His expression gradually became thoughtful.</p>
<p>Affinity Stones weren’t his biggest concern right now... creating awakeners were.</p>
<p>"I can’t just awaken random people..."</p>
