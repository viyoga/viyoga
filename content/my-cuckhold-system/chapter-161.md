<p>One of the attackers roared and unleashed their bloodline ability causing a blast of sonic waves to explode outward.</p>
<p>The invisible force slammed into West’s chest.</p>
<p>BOOM</p>
<p>His body shot backward through the air and crashed into a pile of broken concrete and several gang members smirked.</p>
<p>"Got him."</p>
<p>But the dust hadn’t even settled before West stood up, almost completely unfazed.</p>
<p>"...Seriously?" one of them muttered.</p>
<p>West cracked his neck slightly.</p>
<p>"You’ll have to do better than that."</p>
<p>Then he launched forward again like a missile.</p>
<p>The sonic-bloodline user barely had time to react before West’s fist slammed into his face.</p>
<p>CRACK</p>
<p>The man’s head snapped sideways violently as his body spun through the air while every tooth in his mouth flew out like scattered bullets.</p>
<p>He landed twenty feet away and didn’t get back up.</p>
<p>But West attack had created an opening... The remaining two awakeners rushed at him simultaneously.</p>
<p>One possessed another bloodline enhancement, boosting his physical strength to monstrous levels.</p>
<p>The other had who had purplish glowing claws swiped out...</p>
<p>They struck at the same time.</p>
<p>THUD</p>
<p>The bloodline user’s punch smashed into West’s ribs...</p>
<p>SLASH</p>
<p>While the transformed claws raked across his shoulder, drawing a line of blood.</p>
<p>West staggered slightly as he stared at the faint traces of purple in his torn flesh but before he could retaliate.</p>
<p>Boom!</p>
<p>His shoulder exploded, sending him flying again and crashing into the ground, rolling across it for a couple of feet.</p>
<p>Apparently, the traces of purple were explosives. Any direct contact would lead to an explosion moments later.</p>
<p>For the first time in the fight, he had been dealt proper damage.</p>
<p>Meanwhile, Ross was still kneeling on the ground, breathing heavily as two gang members helped him stand.</p>
<p>Blood dripped down his arm despite the crude bandage they were attempting.</p>
<p>One of them looked toward the battlefield.</p>
<p>"You said he was only a little stronger than regular Tier 1 awakeners because of multiple branches."</p>
<p>His voice trembled slightly.</p>
<p>"You didn’t say he was this monstrous."</p>
<p>Ross clenched his teeth and without answering turned to stare in West’s direction.</p>
<p>What he saw made his stomach drop...</p>
<p>West had jumped back into the fight with injuries and happened to just ducked beneath another attack from the purple glowing claw.</p>
<p>His body suddenly blurred as he activated an Acceleration skill.</p>
<p>[ Radiant Acceleration Activated ]</p>
<p>The sudden burst of speed allowed him to evade three incoming strikes simultaneously as he turned into a streak.</p>
<p>At the same moment, his hand sparked with electricity.</p>
<p>CRACK</p>
<p>A blast of lightning exploded from his palm, launching one attacker across the auditorium and slamming into a wall.</p>
<p>West pivoted sharply in the next instant as grabbed the head of the final attacker and his knee shot upward.</p>
<p>THUD</p>
<p>The jaw of this gang member shattered as he was launched backward.</p>
<p>Ross’s eyes widened.</p>
<p>"...No."</p>
<p>"...No..."</p>
<p>His voice trembled.</p>
<p>"He’s stronger than the last time I saw him..."</p>
<p>"Way stronger..."</p>
<p>He stared in disbelief.</p>
<p>"This shouldn’t be possible..."</p>
<p>For a moment, Ross looked like he might completely lose composure.</p>
<p>Then the gang member beside him grabbed his shoulder.</p>
<p>"Ross."</p>
<p>Ross blinked.</p>
<p>"What?"</p>
<p>The man leaned closer.</p>
<p>"...The tool."</p>
<p>Ross froze as his eyes widened in realization.</p>
<p>"Yes..."</p>
<p>"Yes!"</p>
<p>He turned toward the man urgently.</p>
<p>"Use it."</p>
<p>"Now."</p>
<p>The gang member nodded, reached into his jacket and pulled out a small metallic device.</p>
<p>It looked harmless, almost like a compact grenade.</p>
<p>West had just knocked down the last of the four attackers when the device was thrown.</p>
<p>West frowned as it rolled across the ground towards him.</p>
<p>"...What?"</p>
<p>TOOM~</p>
<p>A strange, low-frequency sound swept outward as the device activated.</p>
<p>It wasn’t loud, but the moment it spread across the surroundings, West felt something wrong.</p>
<p>A strange pressure formed inside his chest as his branches suddenly felt muted.</p>
<p>Behind him, Gor’thala’s body dissolved into particles of light... and a moment later, Seraphyra vanished as well.</p>
<p>Outside the auditorium and school itself... even Aurethia Sol’Vael disappeared instantly.</p>
<p>West’s eyes narrowed.</p>
<p>"...What?"</p>
<p>His connection to them had been cut off.</p>
<p>Ross slowly stood upright, clutching his injured arm as a cruel smile spread across his face.</p>
<p>"The Summon Siphoning Device."</p>
<p>He laughed bitterly.</p>
<p>"It drains the energy that sustains summoned entities."</p>
<p>West looked down at his hands, unable to sense his summoning branches within him.</p>
<p>Ross grinned wider.</p>
<p>"Well?"</p>
<p>He gestured toward the battlefield.</p>
<p>"Let’s see how you fight now..."</p>
<p>"...without your summons."</p>
<p>His voice carried a mixture of triumph and desperation, believing he had finally turned the tables.</p>
<p>But as the echo of his laughter faded, Ross slowly turned his head to observe the battlefield.</p>
<p>And the smile on his face began to disappear...</p>
<p>Bodies... were everywhere.</p>
<p>Black Furnace gang members lay scattered across the broken auditorium floor like discarded rag dolls.</p>
<p>Some were groaning in pain, some were barely conscious and a few were already dead.</p>
<p>Ross’s eyes widened and his breathing slowed.</p>
<p>"What...?"</p>
<p>The two beside him were the only ones left standing asides the other one in the middle of the battlefield already getting dealt with.</p>
<p>Jax stood a few meters away with one hand extended forward.</p>
<p>Ice had crawled up the man’s legs, freezing him solid from the waist down.</p>
<p>The gang member struggled desperately, but the frost refused to break.</p>
<p>Beside Jax, Nina raised her paintbrush, brushing furiously in mid air...</p>
<p>A shield materialized in front of her, glowing with magical energy... she grabbed the shield with both hands and charged forward.</p>
<p>"Move!"</p>
<p>CRASH</p>
<p>The shield slammed directly into the frozen man’s face and the impact shattered the ice holding him in place.</p>
<p>The gang member flew backward violently, slamming into the ground and skidding across it before finally coming to a stop.</p>
<p>Silence reigned as Ross stared in disbelief.</p>
<p>His brain struggled to process what he was seeing.</p>
<p>"They... they’re..."</p>
<p>His voice cracked.</p>
<p>"...They’re beat."</p>
<p>Almost all the fighters he had brought here with him had been taken down.</p>
<p>They were all experienced Tier 1 awakeners who had spent years cultivating their branches and yet they had been defeated by people who were newly awakened.</p>
