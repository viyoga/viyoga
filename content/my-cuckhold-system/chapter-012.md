<p>"Do you have a boyfriend?" West asked casually.</p>
<p>Her face flushed a deep crimson almost instantly.</p>
<p>Her thoughts spiraled.</p>
<p>’Isn’t this fast?’</p>
<p>’Did I misunderstand something?’</p>
<p>’Did I give off the wrong impression?’</p>
<p>"I—I don’t," she said finally with a jittery tone. "Why?"</p>
<p>West’s expression changed immediately, turning disinterested.</p>
<p>"Oh," he said flatly. "Meh. Pointless."</p>
<p>He stood, picked up his lunch and walked away.</p>
<p>Just like that.</p>
<p>Mira stood frozen with her mouth opened.</p>
<p>"Eh?" she whispered.</p>
<p>Then louder. "Eh? Eh? EH?"</p>
<p>She watched his back retreat up the steps and his long coat swaying slightly with each step. His posture was relaxed like he hadn’t just dropped a conversational grenade and left.</p>
<p>Her brain struggled to catch up.</p>
<p>’Pointless...?’</p>
<p>’What does that mean?’</p>
<p>’Did he only ask because—?’</p>
<p>The rest of the day, she barely heard a word in class.</p>
<p>Her mind replayed the scene again and again.</p>
<p>His calm question.</p>
<p>His flat reaction.</p>
<p>The way he’d walked off like she didn’t exist anymore.</p>
<p>’Does he only like girls with boyfriends?’ Her heart thudded strangely as she wondered.</p>
<p>She hated the thought and yet—</p>
<p>She couldn’t stop thinking about him.</p>
<p>---</p>
<p>After school, students streamed out of classrooms as laughter and chatter filled the halls. West slung his bag over his shoulder and headed for the exit, unhurried.</p>
<p>A presence suddenly blocked his path.</p>
<p>"Finally found you!"</p>
<p>Caleb’s voice cut through the noise like a blade.</p>
<p>West stopped, looked to the side and stepped towards the left before walking past him.</p>
<p>Caleb’s eyes widened in disbelief.</p>
<p>"What—hey—!"</p>
<p>He reached out, grabbing West by the collar, digging in with force meant to drag him back.</p>
<p>The contact lasted less than a second.</p>
<p>West’s hand snapped up, slapping Caleb’s wrist aside with enough force that knocked the grip loose without even turning around.</p>
<p>Caleb stumbled half a step back.</p>
<p>"What the—?!"</p>
<p>West kept walking.</p>
<p>That was when Caleb snapped. How dare an unawakened act so brazen towards him? Especially one that he had repeatedly put in his place.</p>
<p>"You think you’re slick now?" Caleb snarled.</p>
<p>West didn’t respond.</p>
<p>Caleb felt something ugly coil in his chest.</p>
<p>’He slapped my hand away.</p>
<p>Slapped.</p>
<p>Like it was nothing.’</p>
<p>Like Caleb was nothing.</p>
<p>The eyes watching them multiplied.</p>
<p>Caleb’s pride ignited.</p>
<p>"You think you can ignore me?" he growled.</p>
<p>West finally stopped.</p>
<p>Slowly turned his head.</p>
<p>Just enough to look at him.</p>
<p>Caleb flinched... just slightly.</p>
<p>West’s gaze was calm and unbothered.</p>
<p>"You were blocking the exit," West said.</p>
<p>That was it... the final straw that broke the camel’s back.</p>
<p>Caleb roared and stepped forward.</p>
<p>The air around him warped subtly as he activated his branch.</p>
<p>Power flowed through his body as his awakened ability came to life causing his veins to glow as pressure rolled outward.</p>
<p>Students nearby gasped and scattered.</p>
<p>Caleb’s lips curled into a savage grin.</p>
<p>"You should’ve stayed on the floor," he voiced while greenish markings appeared all over his body.</p>
<p>The ground beneath his feet cracked slightly as his eyes glowed up and a red line extended from the left to right.</p>
