<p>West glanced around briefly.</p>
<p>A few nearby gang members were pretending not to watch.</p>
<p>He reached into his pocket.</p>
<p>Of course, the item wasn’t actually in his pocket but he allowing it to appear in his grasp out of thin air wouldn’t be the smartest thing to do.</p>
<p>He pretended to retrieve something small and when his hand emerged, he placed it gently on the counter.</p>
<p>A Rotars Flork.</p>
<p>Its strange, organic-metallic structure shimmered faintly under the lights. Veins of dull green energy emitted from it in small waves like a living heartbeat.</p>
<p>The air around the counter changed and the woman’s eyes widened.</p>
<p>Even the bulky gang member nearby turned sharply.</p>
<p>"That..." she breathed.</p>
<p>West leaned back casually.</p>
<p>"Interested?"</p>
<p>The Rotars Flork wasn’t just junk.</p>
<p>It was used in crafting as an energy catalyst, so it happened to be really valuable and dangerous if mishandled.</p>
<p>Her professional mask slipped for a fraction of a second before snapping back into place.</p>
<p>"Please wait," she said before pressing a button beneath the counter.</p>
<p>Within seconds, two figures descended the staircase from above.</p>
<p>Unlike the others, these didn’t bother hiding their presence.</p>
<p>They were definitely awakened. They approached the counter slowly and one of them who had silver-rimmed glasses, picked up the Rotars Flork with gloved hands and examined it carefully.</p>
<p>He nodded once.</p>
<p>"Authentic."</p>
<p>The word rippled through the space and whispers broke out amongst a few gang members present.</p>
<p>After the Rotars Flork had been examined and confirmed authentic, the woman behind the counter adjusted her glasses and studied West more carefully.</p>
<p>The subtle change in her expression did not escape him.</p>
<p>She placed the Rotars Flork gently on a velvet-lined tray, scanning it with a handheld device that emitted faint blue beams across its surface. Data flickered across a small screen attached to her wrist.</p>
<p>"High purity," she murmured. "Energy veins intact. No structural fractures."</p>
<p>Her eyes lifted to him again.</p>
<p>"This piece could fetch anywhere between 150,000 to 200,000 dollars depending on the buyer and urgency of sale."</p>
<p>For a split second, West almost reacted.</p>
<p>’150k–200k?’</p>
<p>His heartbeat skipped.</p>
<p>Just a few weeks ago, he had been calculating whether he could afford an extra shopping spree. His entire savings sat at a pathetic three thousand dollars earned painfully from long shifts at Crème &amp; Ash, where the scent of roasted coffee beans clung to his clothes long after he clocked out.</p>
<p>And now a single item from his system inventory could earn him more than fifty times that?</p>
<p>He kept his face steady.</p>
<p>"What if," he said casually, "I had a whole batch to sell?"</p>
<p>The woman blinked and then chuckled softly.</p>
<p>"I’d probably tell you to fuck off," she replied without missing a beat. "Because I’d assume you were playing around."</p>
<p>West didn’t laugh because he was completely serious.</p>
<p>He had more than a whole batch...</p>
<p>Much more.</p>
<p>But now that he understood the value of what he possessed, his mindset shifted instantly.</p>
<p>This wasn’t just pocket change... this was the kind of thing gangs killed for.</p>
<p>He leaned back slightly.</p>
<p>"Yeah," he said lightly. "I’m just playing."</p>
<p>She nodded knowingly.</p>
<p>"I thought so. You’re not affiliated with any gang. It’s already shocking enough that you have such a high-quality Rotars Flork in your possession."</p>
<p>Her eyes sharpened faintly.</p>
<p>"But if you happen to acquire more in the future, we would be very interested."</p>
<p>West gave a vague shrug.</p>
<p>"Maybe I’ll get lucky again."</p>
<p>She didn’t press further.</p>
<p>That, more than anything, told him this establishment was disciplined. Curious, yes. Greedy, probably. But they understood boundaries—at least within their walls.</p>
<p>She began processing the transaction.</p>
<p>"Name?"</p>
<p>"Jax Monroe," West replied smoothly.</p>
<p>He had prepared that.</p>
<p>She typed it in.</p>
<p>"Affiliation?"</p>
<p>"Independent."</p>
<p>"Identification?"</p>
<p>He produced a forged ID he’d arranged earlier through certain... online channels. It was good enough to pass surface-level scrutiny.</p>
<p>She scanned it... the system accepted it and there were no further questions.</p>
<p>A few signatures were required. He signed with the alias. A small contract flashed across the screen confirming the sale terms. He agreed.</p>
<p>"Private room," she said.</p>
<p>The two suited Awakened from earlier escorted them upstairs. The room was minimalistic but secure with soundproof walls, reinforced door, and a table with a built-in scanning unit.</p>
<p>The Rotars Flork was placed into a containment case.</p>
<p>The tall Awakened with silver-rimmed glasses rechecked it once more.</p>
<p>"Final offer," he said calmly. "One hundred seventy thousand."</p>
<p>Right in the middle of the range.</p>
<p>West nodded immediately.</p>
<p>"Deal."</p>
<p>The man tapped a few commands into a tablet.</p>
<p>"Transfer destination?"</p>
<p>West provided Jax’s bank details.</p>
<p>He would not allow the money to hit his real account. If they saw his legal name, cross-referenced data, or connected him to anything suspicious, that could become a problem.</p>
<p>Jax could forward it to him later... It was much safer that way.</p>
<p>A moment later, the Awakened looked up.</p>
<p>"Transfer complete."</p>
<p>West’s chest felt light.</p>
<p>170,000 dollars... just like that.</p>
<p>He had never seen that amount of money associated with his life before.</p>
<p>Three thousand in savings used to feel like a cushion but this was an entirely different level.</p>
<p>However, he didn’t allow excitement to show.</p>
<p>He leaned back calmly.</p>
<p>"Pleasure doing business."</p>
<p>The man nodded.</p>
<p>"If you acquire more... high-quality items, you have our contact."</p>
<p>A smooth business card was slid across the table.</p>
<p>West picked it up, not offering one in return.</p>
<p>After a few more formalities, he exited the building.</p>
<p>The cool evening air hit his face as he stepped outside.</p>
<p>The loitering gang members were still there but their gazes were different now as they assessed him.</p>
<p>He walked calmly with his hands in his pockets and posture relaxed.</p>
<p>Behind him, he could faintly sense movement.</p>
<p>Four footsteps syncing subtly which meant four individuals.</p>
<p>They kept a slight distance, but it was not enough to stay hidden from West’s senses.</p>
<p>West’s eyes remained forward.</p>
<p>’So they target fellows who are alone...’</p>
<p>It clicked.</p>
<p>This was why gangs moved in packs. Safety in numbers. If someone walked out solo after making a deal, they became easy prey.</p>
<p>And judging by the aura he could faintly sense that they were Awakened.</p>
