<p>The guilt steadily ate away at Liz until eventually... friday arrived.</p>
<p>After one last sleepless night... Liz finally made her decision that she would meet West.</p>
<p>•••</p>
<p>•••</p>
<p>(( Shadow Reapers Headquarters ))</p>
<p>The atmosphere outside the main building was unusually lively.</p>
<p>Large production trucks occupied much of the space with lighting rigs, camera cranes and sound equipment set up.</p>
<p>Assistants rushed from place to place carrying props.</p>
<p>Upon arrival, Liz was dumbfounded. "...What’s going on?"</p>
<p>One of the staff members smiled. "They’re filming Mr. Einstein’s commercial."</p>
<p>She quietly followed the activity toward the main filming area.</p>
<p>Several large cameras pointed toward a specially constructed set while bright studio lights illuminated everything.</p>
<p>West stood near the center clad in a fitted black tank top that accentuated his athletic build, paired with dark cargo pants and combat boots.</p>
<p>A carton of Silver Crest milk rested casually in one hand.</p>
<p>His hair had been styled slightly differently for the commercial, giving him an even sharper appearance.</p>
<p>Even Liz found herself staring for a moment. "...He cleans up ridiculously well."</p>
<p>The director raised his hand. "Ready!"</p>
<p>"Cameras!"</p>
<p>"Rolling!"</p>
<p>"Action!"</p>
<p>A massive hydraulic crane positioned just outside the camera frame suddenly released its load.</p>
<p>A full-sized car dropped dramatically toward the ground and an actress stood directly beneath its path.</p>
<p>She looked upward in horror. "Help!"</p>
<p>Before the vehicle could crash, West shot forward into frame.</p>
<p>He planted one foot firmly against the pavement and caught the descending car... with astonishing ease using only one hand.</p>
<p>The actress looked up at him in amazement.</p>
<p>"Are you okay, miss?"</p>
<p>She nodded enthusiastically. "You..."</p>
<p>"...you’re my hero."</p>
<p>She leaned forward and kissed his cheek.</p>
<p>West smiled modestly before carefully lowering the vehicle back onto the ground.</p>
<p>He then turned directly toward the nearest camera with a confident grin on his face. "Wanna be as strong as me, kids?"</p>
<p>He lifted the milk carton. "Then make sure you drink..."</p>
<p>He took a dramatic sip. "...Silver Crest Milk."</p>
<p>The director immediately shouted, "Cut!"</p>
<p>Everyone applauded while the crew quickly gathered around reviewing the footage.</p>
<p>One cameraman laughed. "That looked fantastic."</p>
<p>Another nodded. "We’ll still need another angle."</p>
<p>The director pointed. "Reset everything!"</p>
<p>"We’re doing another take!"</p>
<p>West simply laughed. "No problem."</p>
<p>Liz watched quietly from a distance with a strangely fascinated gaze.</p>
<p>Seeing West joke effortlessly with the crew, listening patiently to every instruction and repeating scenes without complaint, made her even more drawn to him.</p>
<p>Even when one take failed because an extra accidentally looked at the camera...</p>
<p>He simply smiled. "No worries."</p>
<p>"Let’s do it again."</p>
<p>Takes were repeated with different camera angles, different dialogue delivery and different lighting adjustments, over and over again.</p>
<p>The process continued for nearly two hours.</p>
<p>Liz remained seated nearby the entire time watching and waiting.</p>
<p>Eventually... the director clapped loudly. "That’s it!"</p>
<p>"We’ve got everything!"</p>
<p>A small round of applause erupted from the crew.</p>
<p>West accepted a towel from one of the assistants before finally noticing Liz still waiting nearby.</p>
<p>His eyes widened slightly. "You’ve been here this whole time?"</p>
<p>She smiled faintly. "I didn’t want to interrupt."</p>
<p>West rubbed the back of his neck. "Sorry."</p>
<p>"I thought this would only take thirty minutes."</p>
<p>She shook her head. "It’s alright."</p>
<p>"I actually found it interesting."</p>
<p>He laughed. "Interesting?"</p>
<p>"I’ve never watched a commercial being filmed before."</p>
<p>"So..."</p>
<p>She smiled. "It was worth waiting."</p>
<p>West looked genuinely apologetic. "Still."</p>
<p>"Sorry for keeping you."</p>
<p>She held up the document folder. "I came because..."</p>
<p>"I have a business proposal I’d like to discuss."</p>
<p>West nodded. "Of course."</p>
<p>He handed the milk carton to one of the production assistants.</p>
<p>"Give me five minutes to change."</p>
<p>"I’ll meet you upstairs."</p>
<p>"Sounds good."</p>
<p>A few minutes later... West returned wearing a casual black shirt over his tank top.</p>
<p>He smiled. "Come on."</p>
<p>"My office’s a little quieter."</p>
<p>Liz quietly followed behind him.</p>
<p>The folder in her hands suddenly felt much heavier than it had when she’d arrived.</p>
<p>Every step toward his office made her feel even more conflicted about what she had come to do.</p>
<p>...</p>
<p>Few minutes later, the door to West’s office closed softly behind them.</p>
<p>Compared to the lively atmosphere outside where the commercial crew was still packing away equipment, the office was comfortably quiet.</p>
<p>West walked over to the minibar tucked into one corner and poured two glasses of cold water.</p>
<p>He slid one across the desk.</p>
<p>"You’ve been coming here so often lately..." he joked as he settled into his chair. "At this point I think I see you more than I see my own mother."</p>
<p>Usually, that would have earned him one of Liz’s bright laughs.</p>
<p>Instead... she smiled... but the smile wasn’t quite there. It didn’t reach her eyes.</p>
<p>West immediately noticed and leaned back in his chair, studying her for a moment. "...You okay?"</p>
<p>Liz looked up quickly. "Hm?"</p>
<p>"You don’t seem a little heavy."</p>
<p>"I’m fine."</p>
<p>West wasn’t convinced. "You’ve got that look."</p>
<p>"What look?"</p>
<p>"The one that says your brain is carrying around something heavy."</p>
<p>She tried to laugh again. "It must be the workload."</p>
<p>West folded his arms. "Or..."</p>
<p>"The stress from work."</p>
<p>"...and taking care of your fiancé every day."</p>
<p>Her fingers tightened around the folder resting in her lap. "It’s... been busy."</p>
<p>"I can imagine," West nodded sympathetically. "I honestly don’t know how you’ve managed it."</p>
<p>"Working."</p>
<p>"Looking after him."</p>
<p>"Still finding time to help me."</p>
<p>"I’d probably collapse after two weeks."</p>
<p>That almost made her genuinely laugh. "You’re exaggerating."</p>
<p>"No."</p>
<p>"I’m serious."</p>
<p>He smiled warmly. "I admire people who keep going when life keeps punching them."</p>
<p>Those words struck her harder than he realized causing her to look away. "...Thank you."</p>
<p>West noticed she still wasn’t relaxing which meant that whatever was bothering her... was bigger than simple exhaustion so he decided not to push.</p>
<p>Instead he smiled. "So..."</p>
<p>"What brings the city’s best realtor to my humble office today?"</p>
<p>Liz immediately reached for the folder and carefully placed the proposal on the desk.</p>
<p>She wanted to get this over with as quick as possible because the longer she stayed, the harder it became.</p>
<p>"I wanted to show you an investment opportunity."</p>
<p>West opened the file and she began explaining the project, the location, size of the land, its potential uses and possible long-term development.</p>
<p>She spoke professionally, listing points after points while West quietly turned page after page.</p>
<p>Eventually... he reached the purchase price. "...Twenty million?"</p>
<p>Liz swallowed. "...Yes."</p>
<p>West looked back at the documents. "...That’s a little pricey."</p>
<p>"It..." she hesitated. "...will still become valuable."</p>
<p>West continued reading. It was obvious he trusted her since he didn’t question anything else or check for hidden details.</p>
<p>That trust... made every word leaving her mouth feel heavier.</p>
<p>When she finally finished, West closed the folder.</p>
<p>"If you think it’s a worthwhile investment..." he smiled. "...then I trust your judgment."</p>
<p>Liz felt her heart drop as West picked up his pen. "Let’s get the paperwork signed."</p>
<p>He placed his signature on one page and then moved onto the next and did the same thing, over and over.</p>
<p>Each completed page felt like another pressure settling onto her chest.</p>
<p>The air conditioner continued filling the space with cool air, yet sweat gathered along her temples.</p>
<p>As she watched West sign, her breathing became shallow.</p>
<p>Not only had West saved her fiancée, had treated her better than any client she’d ever had.</p>
<p>He listened, respected her advice and never once tried to take advantage of her.</p>
<p>’And this... this was how I’m going to repay him?’ her vision blurred in guilt.</p>
<p>West reached the final signature page. "Just one more."</p>
<p>His pen hovered above the paper and just as he was about to sign, something inside her snapped.</p>
<p>Her hand shot forward and slapped the cup resting near West’s elbow.</p>
<p>It tipped over and water splashed across the desk, instantly soaking the documents.</p>
<p>West jumped back. "Whoa!"</p>
<p>"Liz—"</p>
<p>"I’m sorry!" her voice broke as she grabbed the dripping paperwork before West could react.</p>
<p>"No..."</p>
<p>"No..."</p>
<p>"I can’t..."</p>
<p>With trembling hands, she tore the soaked pages apart causing paper to scatter across the floor.</p>
<p>West stood there completely stunned. "Liz..."</p>
<p>Tears dribbled down her cheeks as she apologized constantly. "I’m sorry..."</p>
<p>"I’m so sorry..."</p>
<p>"Don’t sign it."</p>
<p>West stared at her. "What happened?"</p>
<p>"I..."</p>
<p>"I can’t tell you..."</p>
<p>"I’m sorry..."</p>
<p>Before he could reach her... she turned and ran, shutting the door behind her with a bang.</p>
<p>"Liz!" West stood in place utterly confused for some time.</p>
<p>After a couple of seconds, he hurried after her but by the time he reached the entrance of the base, she was already climbing into a cab.</p>
<p>"Liz!"</p>
<p>West watched the vehicle pull away and disappear into traffic in a short time.</p>
<p>His expression slowly hardened. "...Something’s very wrong."</p>
<p>...</p>
<p>...</p>
<p>Liz returned home in tears.</p>
<p>The moment she entered the apartment... her phone began vibrating.</p>
<p>~ Boss ~</p>
<p>She rejected the call but it rang again.</p>
<p>~ Boss ~</p>
<p>She rejected it once more and seconds later... it rang a third time.</p>
<p>~ Boss ~</p>
