<p>West watched Lila quietly, letting her gather whatever she wanted without hindering her.</p>
<p>After all was said and done, he turned around.</p>
<p>"...Let’s go."</p>
<p>—</p>
<p>The next day at the Dragon Fang Gang Base, four intimidating presences sat in a large meeting hall with a massive semi circle structured diamond like table before them.</p>
<p>At the center, West stood before four figures.</p>
<p>All four of them were Grand Captains... the rank just below assistant Gang Leader within the Dragon Fang Gang.</p>
<p>"You were responsible for that team!"</p>
<p>One of them with four ponytails slammed his fist against the table as his voice boomed.</p>
<p>"How could you let a new recruit die under your watch?!"</p>
<p>Another with a bald head and and two headed dragon tattoo on his neck, stood with blazing eyes.</p>
<p>"I told Boss Zu Li you weren’t fit for this! But she insisted!"</p>
<p>His glare sharpened.</p>
<p>"An outsider leading our people—this is the result!"</p>
<p>The pressure in the room skyrocketed while West’s expression remained calm and unbothered as he stood there silently.</p>
<p>One of the other Grand Captains, named Tenzin, spoke up.</p>
<p>"I’ve reviewed all reports. Not just his—but from every survivor."</p>
<p>He glanced at West.</p>
<p>"...What happened was beyond expectation."</p>
<p>He turned back to the others.</p>
<p>"And based on those reports—his actions prevented further casualties."</p>
<p>The room stirred but the opposing captains, Renzhi and Damien, weren’t having it.</p>
<p>"Prevented?!" Renzhi snapped.</p>
<p>"A leader who allows even one casualty has already failed!"</p>
<p>Damien nodded in agreement.</p>
<p>"He’s not fit to lead."</p>
<p>The fourth grand captain, Fred, remained silent, observing.</p>
<p>After their continous back and forth, their eyes suddenly turned to West.</p>
<p>"...Well?"</p>
<p>"What do you have to say for yourself?"</p>
<p>West remained silent for a couple of seconds, before finally speaking.</p>
<p>"Where’s Zu Li?"</p>
<p>Damien narrowed his eyes at the audacity of West... how could he call the name of their glorified leader so casually like that?</p>
<p>"...She’s out on official business. Won’t be back for two days."</p>
<p>West sighed lightly.</p>
<p>"Then I’ll speak to her when she returns."</p>
<p>After saying that, he turned around and began walking.</p>
<p>"I’ll take my leave."</p>
<p>—</p>
<p>"...Stop."</p>
<p>A hand came out of nowhere and suddenly grabbed his shoulder, restraining him from further movement.</p>
<p>"You don’t walk away from this."</p>
<p>Renzhi’s voice dropped, filled with restrained fury.</p>
<p>"You answer to us right now."</p>
<p>West glanced at the hand on his shoulder before shrugging it off.</p>
<p>"I’ve said everything in the report."</p>
<p>He voiced calmly.</p>
<p>"People die in ruins all the time..." he paused briefly before adding. "You should be thanking me the others made it out."</p>
<p>After saying this, he resumed walking.</p>
<p>The atmosphere exploded as energy swept out from Renzhi and the ground trembled slightly.</p>
<p>He had just unleashed his Tier 2 aura and it was very oppressive, stirring the air without him needing to move a muscle.</p>
<p>"If you take another step—"</p>
<p>He voiced with a deadly tone.</p>
<p>"I will cut you down."</p>
<p>The others reacted immediately.</p>
<p>"Hey—calm down—"</p>
<p>"This is unnecessary... the boss won’t be pleased—"</p>
<p>However, Renzhi didn’t listen. His four ponytails danced behind him as he continued to unleash his aura, expecting fear and reverence from West.</p>
<p>Unfortunatel for him, West didn’t even bother turning around.</p>
<p>He took another step forward and the Grand Captain’s face contorted in anguish as he vanished.</p>
<p>His figure blurred as he reappeared directly in front of West with his left arm already tranformed into a grotesquely reddish-black look, covered in glowing, twitching eyes with purple energy condensed at his fingertips.</p>
<p>Just as he appeared, a lethal cleave was already swinging towards West’s neck at a speed that anyone below tier 2 would be unable to follow.</p>
<p>CLANG!!!</p>
<p>A massive hand caught it right before it could make contact with West.</p>
<p>Gor’thala stood between them with her fingers gripping the grotesque arm and stopping it just inches from West’s neck.</p>
<p>The shockwave from the clash rippled outward causing the room to quake.</p>
<p>Renzhi’s eyes widened.</p>
<p>"...What...?"</p>
<p>He pushed harder but for reasons unknown, the arm didn’t move an inch while Gor’thala’s grip tightened slightly on it.</p>
<p>Behind her, West stood still as something stirred in his eyes which slowly lifted to meet the captain’s gaze.</p>
<p>There was a dangerous look in West’s eyes as he spoke.</p>
<p>"...You just tried to kill me."</p>
<p>There was no doubt about it, if that strike had met West neck, his head would have been separated from his body.</p>
<p>The tension in the hall turned very suffocating at this moment like something unseen was pressing down on everyone present.</p>
<p>Grand Captain Renzhi stood stiffly in place with his fists clenched at his sides before swallowing.</p>
<p>He couldn’t wrap his head around how the summon of a Tier one stopped his attack even though he didn’t use a lot of strength... still, he spoke.</p>
<p>"If you didn’t try to leave without permission..." Renzhi began, steadying his voice, "then I wouldn’t have had to attack you."</p>
<p>A murmur rippled through the surrounding grand captains while West tilted his head slightly.</p>
<p>"...Oh?"</p>
<p>Renzhi straightened his posture, trying to reclaim authority.</p>
<p>"You’re under the Dragon Fang Gang’s jurisdiction when you operate with us. Discipline must be maintained."</p>
<p>West’s gaze didn’t change even slightly as he refuted.</p>
<p>"I do not work for the Dragon Fang Gang."</p>
<p>The other captains immediately chipped in.</p>
<p>"Renzhi, you went too far."</p>
<p>"You know how close he is to the boss."</p>
<p>"What were you thinking?"</p>
<p>Their voices came one after another in critical tones but Renzhi didn’t budge.</p>
<p>Instead, his expression hardened.</p>
<p>"I was doing my job."</p>
<p>He stepped forward slightly.</p>
<p>"West is talented, yes."</p>
<p>"But that talent has gotten to his head."</p>
<p>A few brows furrowed.</p>
<p>"...Renzhi..."</p>
<p>He continued anyway.</p>
<p>"He’s a Tier 1 awakener... and yet he carries himself like he’s above everyone."</p>
<p>His eyes locked onto West as he added.</p>
<p>"If he’s not careful..."</p>
<p>"...he’ll meet his Waterloo sooner rather than later."</p>
<p>A dangerous silence followed.</p>
<p>"You should be thanking me for trying to correct you."</p>
<p>West stared at him for a moment before chuckling.</p>
<p>"I do not take lightly..." his voice dropped slightly.</p>
<p>"To anyone who threatens my life... no matter how powerful, they may think they are..."</p>
<p>He paused briefly before looking up again.</p>
<p>"...Renzhi, was it? Aren’t you married?"</p>
