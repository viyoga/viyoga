<p>"She’s been gone for two days," West placed the crumpled note on the table and flattened it. "Someone took her."</p>
<p>Several members exchanged uneasy glances. "Do we know who?"</p>
<p>"No."</p>
<p>"Where?"</p>
<p>"Unknown."</p>
<p>West’s eyes hardened. "But whoever did it deliberately left a message for me."</p>
<p>Immediately Jax picked up the paper and read it, his face darkened.</p>
<p>"So this is about you."</p>
<p>"Obviously."</p>
<p>West turned toward the gathered members. "Listen carefully."</p>
<p>"Every Shadow Reaper member is to keep their eyes open."</p>
<p>"Every contact we have."</p>
<p>"Every informant."</p>
<p>"Every street-level associate."</p>
<p>"I want word spread throughout Misty High City."</p>
<p>"If anyone sees Aria, even if they’re not sure it’s her, I want to know immediately."</p>
<p>One member nodded. "What about outside the city?"</p>
<p>"Do it."</p>
<p>West didn’t hesitate. "Contact any one you can in every neighboring city."</p>
<p>"Put out a description."</p>
<p>"Photograph."</p>
<p>"Clothing."</p>
<p>"Everything."</p>
<p>Another member raised a hand. "And if someone has information?"</p>
<p>"Bring them to me," West voiced with a surprisingly calm voice.</p>
<p>"Don’t interrogate them yourselves."</p>
<p>"Don’t threaten them."</p>
<p>"Bring them to me."</p>
<p>The members nodded and Within minutes, Shadow Reapers members began moving.</p>
<p>Phones started ringing, messages were sent and contacts were activated.</p>
<p>Information networks that normally took days to mobilize were suddenly operating simultaneously.</p>
<p>The disappearance of one woman had turned into an organization-wide search.</p>
<p>---</p>
<p>The first avenue of investigation was technology.</p>
<p>Aria always carried her phone but she also possessed a secondary communication device for emergencies.</p>
<p>And considering her position as West’s assistant, Shadow Reapers had equipped her with several tracking safeguards.</p>
<p>The problem was, none of them worked.</p>
<p>A team of digital specialists gathered around a bank of monitors. "Her primary phone was switched off."</p>
<p>"Secondary?"</p>
<p>"Nothing."</p>
<p>"Emergency tracker?"</p>
<p>"Last signal was forty-three kilometers from here."</p>
<p>West’s eyes narrowed. "Forty-three kilometers?"</p>
<p>"Yes."</p>
<p>"Location?"</p>
<p>A map appeared displaying an isolated roadside area. "Send people."</p>
<p>Within an hour, several Shadow Reapers members were combing the location.</p>
<p>They eventually found something... a damaged phone half-buried in a ditch.</p>
<p>West stared at a photograph placed beside the phone. "That’s hers."</p>
<p>Jax frowned. "So they left this here on purpose?"</p>
<p>"Possibly."</p>
<p>West stood beside the analysts. "Can you determine when it was destroyed?"</p>
<p>"Approximately two days ago."</p>
<p>"Before or after the person who took her entered her apartment?"</p>
<p>"After."</p>
<p>West’s eyes narrowed. "So they brought her there."</p>
<p>"Looks like it."</p>
<p>"Search the surrounding area."</p>
<p>"We already are."</p>
<p>"Expand it."</p>
<p>Immediately the order was given, sozens of people searched nearby roads, abandoned buildings, drainage systems, warehouses and surrounding properties.</p>
<p>Unfortunately, they found nothing. Not even the CCTV around here had a single footage from the last two days. It had all been scrubbed.</p>
<p>---</p>
<p>By evening, West had moved to his third avenue of investigation... his own memory.</p>
<p>He sat alone in his office with a sheet of paper before him, filled with names of people he’d fought, embarrassed and defeated.</p>
<p>People who had legitimate reasons to hate him.</p>
<p>West began crossing them out one after another.</p>
<p>Bloodstain.</p>
<p>He crossed it with a simple dash. "Dead."</p>
<p>Darius.</p>
<p>West stared at the name for several seconds.</p>
<p>Darius was currently trapped and under heavy restriction, he couldn’t have orchestrated this.</p>
<p>He crossed the name out.</p>
<p>Several others followed ranging from criminals he’d encountered to rival gangs.</p>
<p>A few were people who had suffered losses because of Shadow Reapers but eventually, only a handful remained.</p>
<p>West leaned back as his eyes settled on two names.</p>
<p>One was Renzhi and beneath it was Bloodstain’s right-hand man.</p>
<p>West tapped his fingers against the desk. "Renzhi..."</p>
<p>That possibility had been sitting in the back of his mind since the beginning.</p>
<p>The man’s entire life had been shattered because of him.</p>
<p>West had deliberately destroyed his marriage, fucked his daughte, humiliated him and defeated him in combat...</p>
<p>Renzhi had disappeared afterward.</p>
<p>West had no idea where he’d gone but he knew where the man had been last seen.</p>
<p>And if Renzhi wanted revenge... Aria would make an obvious target.</p>
<p>She was important to West and that alone made her valuable.</p>
<p>West looked at the second name... Arlott.</p>
<p>Bloodstain’s right-hand man was another possibility.</p>
<p>His death had undoubtedly left behind people who wanted revenge but West had fewer clues connecting that man to Aria.</p>
<p>West stood. "Renzhi first."</p>
<p>Jax entered the office just as West grabbed his jacket. "You found something?"</p>
<p>"I have a suspect."</p>
<p>"Who?"</p>
<p>"Renzhi."</p>
<p>Jax’s expression changed. "That Dragon fang guy?"</p>
<p>"Yes."</p>
<p>"The one whose wife—"</p>
<p>"Don’t."</p>
<p>West’s voice became colder.</p>
<p>Jax stopped. "Right."</p>
<p>West slipped the jacket on.</p>
<p>"Do you know where you’re going?"</p>
<p>"Yes."</p>
<p>He looked toward the window.</p>
<p>"The last place he was spotted."</p>
<p>Jax frowned. "You’re going alone?"</p>
<p>"Yes."</p>
<p>"Shouldn’t we send—"</p>
<p>"No."</p>
<p>West’s eyes narrowed.</p>
<p>"If Renzhi really has Aria, I don’t want a convoy showing up."</p>
<p>Jax understood. "Fine."</p>
<p>"But keep everyone ready."</p>
<p>"If I call..."</p>
<p>"...We move."</p>
<p>West nodded. "Exactly."</p>
<p>---</p>
<p>Minutes later, West left Shadow Reapers headquarters.</p>
<p>He didn’t bother using a car.</p>
<p>His body blurred, as a dashing sun like glow surrounded him.</p>
<p>&lt;[ Radiant Acceleration Activated ]&gt;</p>
<p>A burst of speed carried him across the city, turning the buildings around him to streaks.</p>
<p>Roads disappeared beneath him as he crossed entire districts within minutes.</p>
<p>His destination was an abandoned section of the city where witnesses had last reported seeing Renzhi after the destruction of his home.</p>
<p>West eventually slowed, arriving at a very quiet area.</p>
<p>Several buildings had broken walls, the pavement was cracked, there were burn marks and collapsed sections of roofing.</p>
<p>West landed silently and looked around. "So this is where you disappeared to..."</p>
<p>He walked deeper into the abandoned district and his senses expanded.</p>
<p>He searched for a while until he heard a faint sound.</p>
<p>West eyes narrowed as he sensed breathing not far away.</p>
<p>He turned toward a ruined building and slowly approached.</p>
<p>The doorway was partially collapsed.</p>
<p>West stepped through and spotted a dark figure sitting alone in the shadows with loose long hair and a tired posture.</p>
<p>There were alcoholic bottles scattered across the floor. They were too many to count.</p>
<p>West’s eyes sharpened. "...Renzhi."</p>
<p>The figure slowly raised his head and his eyes met West’s.</p>
<p>For several seconds, neither man spoke until Renzhi’s expression hardened. "Why are you here?"</p>
<p>West stepped forward. "Where is Aria?"</p>
<p>Renzhi’s brows furrowed. "Who?"</p>
<p>"My assistant."</p>
<p>"How the fuck would I know?"</p>
<p>West studied him carefully and noticed that there was no obvious deception... but that meant little right now.</p>
<p>"Someone took her."</p>
<p>Renzhi’s expression changed slightly. "And you think it was me?"</p>
<p>"You’re one of the possibilities."</p>
<p>Renzhi gave a bitter laugh. "After everything you did to me..."</p>
<p>"You think I’d waste my time kidnapping your assistant?"</p>
<p>West’s gaze remained cold. "You tell me."</p>
<p>Renzhi slowly stood. "You destroyed my life."</p>
<p>His voice trembled as he added. "You humiliated me."</p>
<p>"You turned my family against me."</p>
<p>"And then you walked away."</p>
<p>His fists tightened. "When I want revenge..."</p>
<p>He stepped forward. "...I wouldn’t hide behind your assistant."</p>
<p>West stared directly into his eyes and for a moment, the two awakened stood silently in the ruined building.</p>
<p>Until West finally broke the silence. "Then tell me where you were two days ago."</p>
<p>Renzhi’s expression darkened and for the first time, West noticed Renzhi wasn’t angry... he was exhausted, broken and almost hollow... still, West’s suspicion didn’t disappear.</p>
<p>Renzhi stared at West for several long seconds before scoffing. "I owe you no explanation."</p>
<p>His voice carried none of the desperation West had expected. Instead, there was a bitter, almost amused edge to it.</p>
<p>"I have absolutely no interest in your assistant."</p>
<p>West’s eyes narrowed as Renzhi continued. "I’ll destroy you personally when the time comes."</p>
<p>He paused briefly before adding. "Or..."</p>
<p>A strange smile crept across his face. "Who knows?"</p>
<p>He suddenly began laughing. It was low at first but grew louder and louder until his laughter echoed through the ruined building.</p>
<p>"Maybe I won’t even get the chance."</p>
<p>West remained expressionless while Renzhi’s eyes gleamed with something bordering on madness. "Someone might get to you before I do."</p>
<p>He spread his arms slightly. "For someone as young as you are, and someone who has only been awakened for a single year, you’ve already managed to accumulate an absurd number of enemies."</p>
<p>His laughter faded into a sinister grin. "You’re bound to go down eventually."</p>
<p>Renzhi stepped closer. "And unfortunately..."</p>
<p>His eyes became cold. "...the innocent people around you are doomed to go down with you."</p>
<p>West’s expression didn’t change but something inside him hardened.</p>
<p>He turned around at this point. "That’s enough."</p>
<p>Renzhi continued chuckling behind him while West stopped at the doorway before glancing over his shoulder.</p>
<p>"No matter how many enemies I accumulate..." he spoke with a calm look. "...I’ll always come out on top."</p>
<p>A faint smile appeared on his face as he added. "Fools never understand that until it’s too late."</p>
<p>With those words, West walked away.</p>
<p>The smile slowly faded from Renzhi’s face as he watched him disappear.</p>
<p>"We will see about that."</p>
