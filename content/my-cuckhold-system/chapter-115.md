<p>The walk back to the accommodation complex felt different.</p>
<p>The sky deepened into shades of amber and violet.</p>
<p>Lanterns around the outskirts began lighting up one by one.</p>
<p>The small town atmosphere grew warmer and almost romantic as street musicians played soft instrumental melodies.</p>
<p>The scent of food intensified as dinner crowds gathered.</p>
<p>Despite not entering the ruins, the day had still been full.</p>
<p>They had spent nearly two hours wandering and hadn’t even covered every establishment.</p>
<p>Time had slipped quickly.</p>
<p>Part of that was because they had arrived late.</p>
<p>They had left school at noon and arrived just past 3 PM. They spent over an hour sorting accommodation and then two more exploring.</p>
<p>Even without entry, the outskirts alone were overwhelming.</p>
<p>"We still have the whole weekend," Nina said reassuringly.</p>
<p>"Tomorrow we go in," Darius added dramatically.</p>
<p>"And nothing will stop us."</p>
<p>West glanced toward the ruins one more time before turning away.</p>
<p>Nothing... or something?</p>
<p>They reached the accommodation building as night settled fully.</p>
<p>Teachers gathered everyone in the courtyard for a quick headcount.</p>
<p>"No one wanders off alone," Mr. Lowell instructed sternly. "Curfew is strict."</p>
<p>Students nodded absentmindedly half listening and already planning mischievous deeds.</p>
<p>Mira stretched her arms overhead.</p>
<p>"Dinner first," she declared.</p>
<p>"Yes," Darius agreed immediately.</p>
<p>"Then maybe card games in our room," Felix suggested.</p>
<p>Nina looked toward West.</p>
<p>"You coming?"</p>
<p>He nodded.</p>
<p>"For now."</p>
<p>As they moved toward the dining hall, faint wind swept through the courtyard.</p>
<p>Somewhere in the distance—</p>
<p>A distinct but ominous sound echoed from the direction of the ruins.</p>
<p>Nina paused.</p>
<p>"Did you hear that?"</p>
<p>"Hear what?" Mira asked.</p>
<p>"Nothing," Nina said quickly.</p>
<p>West didn’t comment but his eyes shifted toward the dark outline of the Eternal Ruins.</p>
<p>...</p>
<p>...</p>
<p>That night, the accommodation building barely qualified as a place of rest.</p>
<p>Doors slammed, laughter echoed down hallways and someone blasted music low enough to pretend it wasn’t breaking curfew but loud enough to be irritating.</p>
<p>From West’s room alone, Darius and Rohan argued for twenty straight minutes over who had cheated in a card game before devolving into dramatic accusations that could be heard two corridors away.</p>
<p>Felix eventually muttered, "We’re going to get reported," but no one listened.</p>
<p>Across the hall, a group of girls were whispering loudly.</p>
<p>At some point, someone screamed over a prank involving a fake spider.</p>
<p>Teachers moved up and down the corridors issuing repeated warnings.</p>
<p>"Lights out means lights out!"</p>
<p>"Back in your rooms!"</p>
<p>"Anyone caught outside after this will lose excursion privileges!"</p>
<p>That warning didn’t stop a group of overly ambitious boys and girls from attempting something reckless.</p>
<p>Around 11 PM, Darius cracked the door open just enough to peek outside.</p>
<p>"Yo," he whispered, "some idiots are trying to sneak out."</p>
<p>West didn’t move from his bed.</p>
<p>"How many?"</p>
<p>"Like six of them. Rohan said they want to see the ruins at night."</p>
<p>"Of course they do," Felix sighed.</p>
<p>Within five minutes, raised voices echoed from the stairwell.</p>
<p>"What did we say about curfew?" Mr. Lowell’s voice thundered.</p>
<p>The sneaking group had been caught before they even made it halfway down the path leading toward the ruins.</p>
<p>The sound of disappointed groans followed as they were escorted back inside.</p>
<p>By midnight, the building finally quieted.</p>
<p>One by one, lights flicked off... laughter dwindled and footsteps faded as silence slowly settled.</p>
<p>But West didn’t sleep... at least not immediately.</p>
<p>He sat up in bed, back resting against the headboard with his eyes wide open in the dim room.</p>
<p>The Eternal Ruins had been cleared long ago, making it officially safe and tourist-approved.</p>
<p>But still... he decided to take precautions just in case.</p>
<p>He closed his eyes briefly and summoned his system interface.</p>
<p>It materialized before him, faint blue glow illuminating the dark.</p>
