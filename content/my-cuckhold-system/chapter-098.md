<p>The voices in the background continued.</p>
<p>-"Summoners aren’t built for speed."</p>
<p>-"They rely on their contracts."</p>
<p>-"That’s what I heard."</p>
<p>The starter raised the pistol.</p>
<p>"On your marks!"</p>
<p>West crouched.</p>
<p>"Set!"</p>
<p>Bang!</p>
<p>The moment the gun fired, runners exploded forward.</p>
<p>West on the other hand, pushed himself forward and the world slowed.</p>
<p>His first step tore into the track...</p>
<p>On his second step, he accelerated explosively.</p>
<p>By the time the others hit their third stride, West was already two meters ahead.</p>
<p>Gasps erupted from the stands.</p>
<p>"What the—?"</p>
<p>He leaned forward slightly, maintaining controlled breathing as his body shot forward by nearly eight feet any time he took a step.</p>
<p>His stride length was longer and by the first curve, he was five meters ahead.</p>
<p>By the second straight, the gap widened further.</p>
<p>The wind whipped against his face as he pushed harder.</p>
<p>To make things even more interesting, he didn’t even feel strain...</p>
<p>He crossed the finish line nearly an entire stretch ahead.</p>
<p>"WHAT?!"</p>
<p>"No way!"</p>
<p>"Summoners can’t run like that!"</p>
<p>"Was he even trying?!"</p>
<p>West slowed to a casual jog and turned around.</p>
<p>The second-place runner was still ten meters away.</p>
<p>He blinked in surprise...</p>
<p>Maybe he had gone a bit overboard. He didn’t even realize he was ever this quick.</p>
<p>Mira was screaming from the sidelines.</p>
<p>"WHAT ARE YOU?!"</p>
<p>West raised a hand casually and Class 3A went feral.</p>
<p>---</p>
<p>The next event was rope climbing.</p>
<p>There was a tall metal frame positioned at the center of the field with thick hanging ropes.</p>
<p>Daniel had been their rope specialist but since West was taking over in every sporting event that Daniel was supposed to participate in, he’d have to do this as well.</p>
<p>Students gathered closer.</p>
<p>"This one’s about upper body strength."</p>
<p>"Summoners definitely don’t have that."</p>
<p>"Watch him struggle."</p>
<p>West stepped forward and grabbed the rope.</p>
<p>The moment the whistle blew, he pulled.</p>
<p>His first pull launched him upward nearly a full meter... and his second pull was even stronger.</p>
<p>At this point, he wasn’t even climbing, he was ascending.</p>
<p>By his fourth pull, he was already at the halfway mark.</p>
<p>Students’ mouths hung open.</p>
<p>"Is he flying?!"</p>
<p>"No, that’s pure muscle!"</p>
<p>He reached the top in under six seconds, trapped the bell and the metal rang sharply across the field.</p>
<p>He descended just as smoothly, controlling his drop with ease.</p>
<p>The PE teacher stared at the stopwatch in disbelief.</p>
<p>"How is this possible?"</p>
<p>Mira shook her head.</p>
<p>"Perks of being awakened..."</p>
<p>Girls in the stands were openly swooning now.</p>
<p>"Did you see his arms when he pulled?"</p>
<p>"Oh my God..."</p>
<p>"Why does he look even hotter today?"</p>
<p>---</p>
<p>The next event was javelin.</p>
<p>West rolled his shoulders lightly as he stepped onto the field.</p>
<p>The other competitors had thrown decently, landing near the far marker, around seventy meters.</p>
<p>Respectable performances all across.</p>
<p>West picked up the javelin but it felt too light...</p>
<p>He inhaled, stepped back and ran forward with controlled momentum.</p>
<p>He planted his foot and threw, releasing it cleanly.</p>
<p>The javelin cut through the air like a missile, passing the seventy-meter marker in just a few moments...</p>
<p>The students stood in awe as it passed the eighty and ninety meter mark... but didn’t descend...</p>
<p>The javelin kept going...</p>
<p>CRASH!</p>
<p>A loud, distant impact echoed as the javelin pierced through the side of a small unused maintenance building beyond the field.</p>
<p>A second later, the tip punched out the other side.</p>
<p>Utter silence immediately spread across the field.</p>
<p>The PE teacher slowly lowered his clipboard.</p>
<p>"...West."</p>
<p>"Yes, sir?"</p>
<p>"Next time, aim... shorter."</p>
<p>"Understood."</p>
<p>The field erupted into chaos.</p>
<p>"HE PIERCED A BUILDING!"</p>
<p>"WHAT KIND OF SUMMONER IS THIS?!"</p>
<p>Girls screamed in excitement while phones came out.</p>
<p>Videos were already being recorded.</p>
<p>West exhaled slowly.</p>
<p>Maybe he’d miscalculated.</p>
<p>---</p>
<p>By the end of the events Daniel had been signed up for, Class 3A was dominating.</p>
<p>West had carried multiple points and now, he was the undisputed star of the day.</p>
<p>Wherever he walked, eyes followed.</p>
<p>Whispers trailed behind him.</p>
<p>"Dude is something else..."</p>
<p>Even students from other classes who had previously expected him to perform badly, were staring with interest.</p>
<p>West was standing near the inside edge of the track, sipping water and pretending not to notice the constant attention.</p>
<p>Meanwhile, Nina had her own event: a mid-distance sprint.</p>
<p>The whistle blew for Nina’s race and she took off cleanly.</p>
<p>She was pretty light on her feet despite the constant bouncing of the large watermelons attached to her chest.</p>
<p>But halfway through the curve, something strange happened.</p>
<p>Her stride faltered causing her to veer slightly off track.</p>
<p>Students gasped as she overcorrected too hard and her momentum carried her off the lane entirely...</p>
<p>And directly toward West.</p>
<p>He barely had time to register it as she crashed into him.</p>
<p>The impact would have knocked a normal student flat but West’s absorbed most of it.</p>
<p>He staggered a single step back while Nina bounced off his chest and nearly fell.</p>
<p>He caught her instantly by the waist and the field went silent again.</p>
<p>Her hands were pressed against his chest and his arm was around her.</p>
<p>Her face flushed red.</p>
<p>"I—I’m sorry," she stammered.</p>
<p>West looked down at her calmly.</p>
<p>"You okay?"</p>
<p>She nodded quickly.</p>
<p>West helped Nina steady herself while she stared at him with a strange longing like she was trying to keep something at bay.</p>
<p>...</p>
<p>...</p>
<p>The final whistle of the day echoed across the school grounds, cutting through the humid evening air like the closing note of a grand performance.</p>
<p>For a moment, there was silence and then, cheers erupted.</p>
<p>Laughter rolled across the field as some students shouted in triumph while others yelled in playful complaint. A few collapsed dramatically onto the grass as if they had just survived a battlefield rather than a sports festival.</p>
<p>The scoreboard flickered one last time.</p>
<p>Class 3A – 487 points.</p>
<p>Class 3C – 402 points.</p>
<p>Class 3B – 382 points</p>
<p>Class 3D – 356 points</p>
<p>"WE DID IT!" someone from 3A screamed.</p>
<p>West stood near the edge of the field, a towel draped around his shoulders and droplets of water still clinging to his hair from the pool event. His classmates swarmed him, slapping his back, pulling him into half-drenched hugs.</p>
<p>"You carried the relay!"</p>
<p>"That last sprint was insane!"</p>
<p>"You’re a monster!"</p>
