<p>West frowned. "Aria."</p>
<p>She continued speaking rapidly. "I used outside contractors."</p>
<p>"I hid every connection."</p>
<p>"I routed the transport through multiple channels."</p>
<p>"I made sure nothing pointed back to Shadow Reapers."</p>
<p>Her eyes became glossy. "I don’t understand how they found it."</p>
<p>West watched her quietly and placed a hand on her shoulder.</p>
<p>The panic in her expression eased slightly as he spoke. "This isn’t your fault."</p>
<p>"But—"</p>
<p>"It’s not."</p>
<p>She looked down as West gave her shoulder a reassuring squeeze.</p>
<p>"They were watching."</p>
<p>Aria eyebrows furrowed while West continued.</p>
<p>"This wasn’t random."</p>
<p>"They knew exactly what they were looking for."</p>
<p>He looked around the room. "They’ve probably been planning this for a while."</p>
<p>Jax slammed a fist into the table. "So what now?"</p>
<p>West’s expression turned cold... It was often the same expression people saw right before something unfortunate happened to someone else.</p>
<p>"So... what do we do?" Aria questioned as well.</p>
<p>West slowly smiled and leaned back against the table. "What do we do?" He repeated.</p>
<p>"Iron Vultures stole from us."</p>
<p>"They attacked our people."</p>
<p>"They interfered with our business."</p>
<p>West’s smile widened slightly as he continued. "They seem to have forgotten something."</p>
<p>"When you steal from someone..." his eyes gleamed. "...you should make absolutely certain they can’t steal back."</p>
<p>The room suddenly felt colder as West pushed away from the table.</p>
<p>"Get me everything."</p>
<p>"Routes."</p>
<p>"Names."</p>
<p>"Warehouses."</p>
<p>"Supply schedules."</p>
<p>"Assets."</p>
<p>He looked directly at Aria.</p>
<p>"I want every single piece of information we have on Iron Vultures."</p>
<p>Aria nodded immediately. "I’ll have it ready within the hour."</p>
<p>West smiled. "Good."</p>
<p>He then began walking toward the exit.</p>
<p>Jax stared at him suspiciously. "Where are you going?"</p>
<p>Without turning around, West answered. "To collect interest."</p>
<p>The heavy doors closed behind him and for some reason, every person inside the room suddenly felt sorry for Iron Vultures.</p>
<p>•••</p>
<p>•••</p>
<p>About an hour later, West found himself walking through the heavily fortified headquarters of Iron Vultures.</p>
<p>Although the place didn’t have the best looks on the outside, it still have great defenses.</p>
<p>Massive reinforced walls wrapped around the compound with surveillance systems covering nearly every angle. Armed awakened members patrolled constantly and many of them openly displayed their gang insignia on jackets.</p>
<p>The atmosphere was completely different from the newer gangs that were still trying to establish themselves. It was more raw...</p>
<p>Iron Vultures had been around for years so they had territory and had built up quite the influence.</p>
<p>Most importantly, over five hundred awakened members. Their numbers were nothing compared to a gangs like Eagle’s print or Dragon Fang who had over ten thousand members...</p>
<p>Still, Shadow Reapers was leagues behind.</p>
<p>West was escorted through several checkpoints before finally arriving at the private office of Bloodstain himself.</p>
<p>The room was large and intimidating with a massive black desk sat near the center while the walls were decorated with trophies from successful ruin expeditions. Rare monster skulls, expensive weapons, and preserved materials from high-level creatures lined display cases.</p>
<p>Bloodstain sat behind the desk and the dark ink-like markings covering half his face made him look even more imposing in person than in photographs.</p>
<p>For a few moments, neither man spoke.</p>
<p>After scrutinising West, Bloodstain gestured casually toward a chair. "Have a seat."</p>
<p>Bloodstain leaned back as West sat.</p>
<p>"I’ve been curious about you."</p>
<p>West smiled faintly. "And I’ve been curious about you."</p>
<p>The gang leader chuckled and for a brief moment, it almost looked like two businessmen meeting to discuss a contract.</p>
<p>Then West finally got to the point. "A truck belonging to Shadow Reapers was ambushed earlier today."</p>
<p>Bloodstain didn’t even blink. "Was it?"</p>
<p>"It was carrying several million dollars worth of merchandise."</p>
<p>"That’s unfortunate."</p>
<p>West nodded slowly. "Very unfortunate."</p>
<p>Bloodstain folded his hands together. "And what exactly does that have to do with me?"</p>
<p>West simply stared at him while Bloodstain returned the stare.</p>
<p>A brief silence enveloped the surroundings for a couple moments before Bloodstain laughed.</p>
<p>"Come on, kid. Surely you’re not walking into my base and accusing Iron Vultures of highway robbery."</p>
<p>West leaned back slightly. "The truck was intercepted."</p>
<p>"And?"</p>
<p>"The goods ended up here."</p>
<p>Bloodstain spread his hands. "Did the truck have Shadow Reapers written on it?"</p>
<p>"No."</p>
<p>"Did it have your gang logo?"</p>
<p>"No."</p>
<p>"Any official documentation connecting it to your gang?"</p>
<p>"No."</p>
<p>Bloodstain’s smile widened. "Then how exactly am I supposed to know those items belong to you?"</p>
<p>West couldn’t help but laugh. "Hahahahahaha!" The absurdity of it all was honestly impressive.</p>
<p>Bloodstain joined him. "Bwahahahaha!"</p>
<p>And foor a few seconds both men were laughing.</p>
<p>They both understood the game... of course Bloodstain wouldn’t admit to doing it.</p>
<p>Eventually West rose from his seat. "Well then."</p>
<p>Bloodstain simply just watched as West adjusted his jacket.</p>
<p>"Since Iron Vultures have decided to hold onto possessions that aren’t theirs, I hope they can handle the reverse."</p>
<p>The room suddenly became colder as several nearby awakened members stiffened.</p>
<p>A heavy pressure spread from Bloodstain as his eyes narrowed.</p>
<p>West merely glanced over his shoulder unaffecfed while Bloodstain spoke in a colder voice.</p>
<p>"Shadow Reapers have a lot of nerve making threats after everything my gang has suffered recently."</p>
<p>West raised an eyebrow. "Oh?"</p>
<p>"The bowel incident."</p>
<p>West looked confused.</p>
<p>"The system bug."</p>
<p>West still looked confused.</p>
<p>"The supply failures."</p>
<p>West blinked in utter confusion as Bloodstain glared at him.</p>
<p>West returned the glare and smiled. "Do you have any proof Shadow Reapers was responsible for any of that?"</p>
<p>~ Silence ~</p>
<p>West nodded. "Thought so. How could a small gang likes our possibly cause any issues for a big gang like yours."</p>
<p>Afterwards, West slowly started walking toward the exit. "This discussion is over."</p>
<p>As he stepped out, the doors closed behind him.</p>
<p>Bloodstain sat in silence for several moments after West left.</p>
<p>Eventually part of the wall rippled and a figure stepped out from it.</p>
<p>This was none other than Bloodstain’s right-hand man.</p>
<p>"You let him leave."</p>
<p>Bloodstain nodded. "Obviously."</p>
<p>The man frowned. "Why?"</p>
<p>Bloodstain looked at him as though he had asked something stupid. "Because I enjoy being alive."</p>
<p>The right-hand man sighed. "He was sitting right here."</p>
<p>"And?"</p>
<p>"We could’ve killed him."</p>
<p>Bloodstain laughed while shooting the man a look like he had just said something ridiculous. "Are you stupid?"</p>
<p>The man looked offended but didn’t say a word.</p>
<p>Bloodstain shook his head. "Do you know why Renzhi can’t touch him?"</p>
