<p>The lady on the other end, let out a soft, embarrassed laugh.</p>
<p>"...You remember that?"</p>
<p>"How could I forget?" West replied. "You made it sound like I was about to receive treasure."</p>
<p>She let out a lighter laughter this time before responding.</p>
<p>"...I’m sorry I haven’t come yet."</p>
<p>Her tone suddenly turned heavier.</p>
<p>"It’s just been..." she trailed off. "...a lot."</p>
<p>West’s expression softened slightly.</p>
<p>"Work?" he questioned.</p>
<p>"...Work, yeah. And..." she hesitated. "...him."</p>
<p>West instantly understood that she meant her fiancé. Without interrupting, he let her continue.</p>
<p>"It’s harder than I thought it would be. Taking care of him. Managing everything. Smiling at clients like nothing’s wrong..."</p>
<p>Her voice cracked slightly.</p>
<p>"...I didn’t expect it to feel this... lonely."</p>
<p>West sat up a little at this point.</p>
<p>"...You don’t have anyone helping?"</p>
<p>"Not really," she admitted. "Friends check in sometimes but... it’s not the same."</p>
<p>A brief pause ensued...</p>
<p>"...I just..." she exhaled slowly. "...I wish there was someone who could just tell me everything will be fine."</p>
<p>West didn’t hesitate.</p>
<p>"I can be that someone."</p>
<p>Another brief silence ensued before she reacted.</p>
<p>"...What?" she asked softly.</p>
<p>West’s voice lowered slightly.</p>
<p>"I said I can be that someone."</p>
<p>"You don’t have to go through it alone."</p>
<p>Her breathing hitched slightly on the other end.</p>
<p>"...You barely even know me."</p>
<p>West chuckled softly.</p>
<p>"I know enough."</p>
<p>"And besides..."</p>
<p>He paused just long enough.</p>
<p>"...I’m a pretty good listener."</p>
<p>A faint laugh slipped out of her.</p>
<p>"...You’re really something."</p>
<p>"I’ve been told," he replied casually.</p>
<p>There was a small pause before she spoke in an even softer tone this time.</p>
<p>.</p>
<p>"...Thank you."</p>
<p>West leaned back against the headboard.</p>
<p>"Don’t mention it... and believe me... everything will be alright," West added.</p>
<p>He could hear her let out a soft exhale from the other end.</p>
<p>"How was your day?" She poised a question that threw them into a conversational loop.</p>
<p>They kept talking, moving the conversation fromt just small things to work stories to annoying clients and little frustrations.</p>
<p>West listened attentively and responded occasionally, slipping in just enough remarks to lighten the mood.</p>
<p>"You know," he said at one point, "if I were there, I’d at least make sure both your upper and lower lips were curved upwards..."</p>
<p>She laughed lightly.</p>
<p>"...Oh really?"</p>
<p>"Yeah."</p>
<p>"And why does that sound suspicious..."</p>
<p>"Does it?" West smirked.</p>
<p>Another laugh rang out.</p>
<p>They talked for about fifteen minutes more and by the end of it, her tone had become lighter and warmer.</p>
<p>"...I feel a bit better," she admitted.</p>
<p>"Good," West said simply.</p>
<p>"...Thank you, West."</p>
<p>"Anytime."</p>
<p>There was a brief hesitation before she spoke again.</p>
<p>"...You said you’d visit?"</p>
<p>"Tomorrow," he replied.</p>
<p>"I’ve got business at a forge."</p>
<p>"On the way back, I’ll stop by."</p>
<p>"...That’s not too much trouble?"</p>
<p>West scoffed lightly.</p>
<p>"Not even close."</p>
<p>"...Okay."</p>
<p>She then added with a softer tone;</p>
<p>"I’ll be waiting."</p>
<p>West smirked slightly.</p>
<p>"Try not to miss me too much before then."</p>
<p>She laughed again.</p>
<p>"...Goodnight, West."</p>
<p>"Goodnight."</p>
<p>After the call ended, West stared at the screen for a moment before setting the phone aside.</p>
<p>"...Interesting," he murmured.</p>
