<p>Whatever fragile relief West had felt shattered at this moment as he let out a long, controlled sigh.</p>
<p>Something in that sigh shifted the atmosphere of the entire chamber.</p>
<p>He stepped off the back of the Verdant Pyre Behemoth slowly while the beasts growled as if responding to the darkening aura around him.</p>
<p>Over twenty of the red-furred, blue-limbed creatures pooled forward immediately, forming a loose semicircle.</p>
<p>Their eight eyes blinked in uneven patterns.</p>
<p>They emitted that same strange high pitched sound, but now it felt almost anticipatory.</p>
<p>Behind them, the blood pool bubbled violently as something large moved beneath the surface.</p>
<p>All of a sudden, an arm emerge which was longer than the arm of the others in the surrounding... it was still blue like theirs but much thicker.</p>
<p>And then, a torso rose from the crimson liquid... displaying the full figure of a being that was easily eight feet tall.</p>
<p>It resembled the smaller creatures but it was more refined...</p>
<p>Its fur was darker red, matted in places but arranged deliberately along its frame.</p>
<p>Its limbs were longer and more muscular... and its eight eyes were arranged symmetrically this time, glowing faintly.</p>
<p>Its posture was upright and confident.</p>
<p>A faint frost aura radiated from its blue limbs, causing the air around it to crystallize faintly.</p>
<p>It walked slowly through the pool with blood cascading off its body, and stepped onto solid ground.</p>
<p>Without looking at West, it moved toward the throne in the center of the chamber...</p>
<p>The smaller creatures lowered their heads as the eight-foot being ascended the steps and sat.</p>
<p>Its long blue fingers curled around the armrests and then... it spoke.</p>
<p>The language was a mix of both high and low pitch, layered with clicking undertones and harmonic vibrations that felt almost insectile.</p>
<p>West didn’t understand the sounds but the system did.</p>
<p>A faint overlay appeared in his vision.</p>
<p>[Translation Active]</p>
<p>"I must have this one."</p>
<p>The being’s eight eyes focused on West.</p>
<p>"He is worth one thousand of the ones you have brought so far."</p>
<p>Its gaze sharpened.</p>
<p>"My power will return even stronger... and then we can finally reclaim the surface..."</p>
<p>The smaller creatures screeched in response, as if celebrating the declaration.</p>
<p>West stared at the throne-bound creature in silence.</p>
<p>He had already felt anger seeing the platform... the heads...</p>
<p>But hearing that—</p>
<p>Hearing that the slaughter of his teacher and classmates had merely been... insufficient... left a bitter taste in his mouth.</p>
<p>He exhaled slowly.</p>
<p>"I am starting to understand the hierarchy of power... in this reality."</p>
<p>He spoke with a calm and cold tone.</p>
<p>"Those with more power have the authority to do as they please with those who are weaker."</p>
<p>The chamber grew quieter and even the bubbling pool seemed to dim slightly.</p>
<p>"And since you have exercised that authority..."</p>
<p>Electricity began to crackle faintly around his fingers while Green flames roared brighter behind him as the Verdant Pyre Behemoths took a step forward.</p>
<p>"I hope you’re ready to be on the other end of it..."</p>
<p>"I hope you won’t shrink back... and plead for mercy when you’re dealt with the same hand..."</p>
<p>The eight-foot being tilted its head slightly before making a sharp clicking sound.</p>
<p>All of a sudden an intense tremor spread across the surroundings as a section of the west cavern caved in instantly.</p>
<p>Boom!</p>
<p>Boom!</p>
<p>Boom!</p>
<p>Boom!</p>
<p>West narrowed his eyes while tilting his head slightly to stare in the direction of the caved in section...</p>
<p>His eyebrows furrowed upon realizing that the thunderclap like sounds weren’t explosions... they were footsteps.</p>
<p>---</p>
<p>Meanwhile—</p>
<p>At the surface of the Eternal Ruins, chaos was brewing.</p>
<p>Armored vehicles lined the perimeter.... motorcycles idled with aggressive revs... weapons and awakened abilities flickered into readiness.</p>
<p>Members of the Crimson Talon Syndicate were already arguing heatedly with the Iron Howl Brotherhood.</p>
<p>"We got the call first!"</p>
<p>"You got here first, not the call first!"</p>
<p>"That’s the same damn thing!"</p>
<p>Silver Crescent Union members stood nearby with their arms crossed, watching with calculating expressions.</p>
<p>Graveflare Assembly members lingered at the edges, ready to charge.</p>
<p>One of the Eternal Ruins guards stood nervously between them.</p>
<p>"I’m telling you," he insisted, "the ground split open. There’s a third layer. We saw it."</p>
<p>"You expect us to just trust that?" a Crimson Talon enforcer barked.</p>
<p>"You think we’d call five gangs for fun?!" the guard snapped back.</p>
<p>The Obsidian Vow had arrived shortly after and now stood slightly apart from the rest.</p>
<p>Their leader’s eyes remained fixed on a split in the earth where the third layer had revealed itself. It appeared that the increased tremors had opened up other areas that led below.</p>
<p>The leader wasn’t arguing, he was thinking.</p>
<p>But the others couldn’t come to an agreement.</p>
<p>They were all arguing about priority access, loot rights, territorial claims...</p>
<p>Voices escalated quickly which soon graduated into a shove and then...</p>
<p>BOOM!</p>
<p>An energy blast erupted from someone’s palm.</p>
<p>It struck the ground between two gangs, sending stone shards flying.</p>
<p>That was all it took...</p>
<p>The fragile standoff exploded.</p>
<p>Flames surged, bullets cracked through the air, kinetic blasts sent bodies flying backward...</p>
<p>An Iron Howl fighter was hurled into a ruined pillar...</p>
<p>A Crimson Talon member countered with a shockwave that flattened three opponents...</p>
<p>Silver Crescent finally joined in when one of their scouts got clipped by stray fire...</p>
<p>Graveflare responded with brutality, targeting whoever was closest.</p>
<p>Within seconds, the Eternal Ruins perimeter had become a battlefield.</p>
<p>Civilians and non-affiliated individuals scattered immediately, diving behind structures or fleeing entirely.</p>
<p>The guards scrambled for cover.</p>
<p>In the middle of the chaos, one guard tugged at the sleeve of a nearby gang lieutenant.</p>
<p>"They’re not the first."</p>
<p>The lieutenant frowned mid-fight.</p>
<p>"What?"</p>
<p>"They’re not the first ones here."</p>
<p>Several heads turned and blasts paused momentarily.</p>
<p>"What are you talking about?" someone demanded.</p>
<p>The guard swallowed.</p>
<p>"There was already someone who went down."</p>
<p>Silence rippled briefly across the battlefield as the Obsidian Vow leader’s eyes sharpened.</p>
<p>"When?"</p>
<p>"Over two hours ago."</p>
<p>The surrounding gang members stared at each other.</p>
<p>"Why didn’t you tell us that first?!" an Iron Howl fighter roared.</p>
<p>"You didn’t ask, sir!" the guard shot back desperately.</p>
<p>A heavy silence settled briefly amid the chaos.</p>
<p>"Which gang?" a Crimson Talon member barked.</p>
<p>The guard shook his head.</p>
<p>"It wasn’t a gang."</p>
