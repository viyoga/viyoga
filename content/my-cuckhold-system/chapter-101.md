<p>Zu Li opened her eyes slowly as a faint warmth spread across her cheeks.</p>
<p>She realized something that unsettled her even more... she was attracted to him.</p>
<p>His firmness... his immovable confidence in the face of absolute power... his charm... his presence...</p>
<p>She let out a quiet sigh.</p>
<p>"He really hates my guts..."</p>
<p>Outside the car window, the city blurred past.</p>
<p>"I’ll have to make amends."</p>
<p>---</p>
<p>Meanwhile...</p>
<p>Thirty stories above street level, a silhouette stood on the edge of a high-rise building with hands tucked behind their back.</p>
<p>They watched the black sedan move like a shadow through traffic.</p>
<p>The figure’s gaze followed it for a moment before shifting toward the direction of West’s apartment.</p>
<p>A faint voice carried through the wind.</p>
<p>"Interesting..."</p>
<p>The silhouette turned and disappeared into the shadows.</p>
<p>---</p>
<p>Back Inside, Mark stood in the living room, staring at his son.</p>
<p>"What is up with you?"</p>
<p>West exhaled slowly.</p>
<p>"Dad."</p>
<p>Mark rubbed his chin.</p>
<p>"She’s a big shot, West. You know how many people would beg for that opportunity?"</p>
<p>"I know."</p>
<p>"Then why?"</p>
<p>West hesitated briefly before speaking in a softer voice.</p>
<p>"Dad... I’m a grown man."</p>
<p>Mark blinked.</p>
<p>"I can make my own decisions."</p>
<p>He stepped closer.</p>
<p>"Just trust me."</p>
<p>Mark studied him and for a moment, he saw it...</p>
<p>He had spent so many years barely present that he didn’t realize that his son was no longer a kid...</p>
<p>But someone more grounded.</p>
<p>Mark’s expression shifted into pride.</p>
<p>"My son is an awakened."</p>
<p>He laughed loudly and grabbed West by the shoulders, tapping them repeatedly.</p>
<p>"An awakened! Do you know how insane that is?"</p>
<p>West chuckled slightly while Mark’s eyes gleamed.</p>
<p>"I don’t fully understand this world yet. Gangs. Ruins. Power. But I know one thing."</p>
<p>He squeezed West’s shoulder firmly.</p>
<p>"You looked like you knew exactly what you were doing just now."</p>
<p>West nodded.</p>
<p>"I do."</p>
<p>Mark smiled proudly.</p>
<p>"Then I’ll trust you."</p>
<p>He stepped back, still shaking his head in disbelief.</p>
<p>"My son..."</p>
<p>West watched his father walk back toward the kitchen, muttering excitedly under his breath.</p>
<p>...</p>
<p>...</p>
<p>Now that Mark knew, everything changed.</p>
<p>West no longer had to pretend.</p>
<p>No more hiding the subtle shifts in strength.</p>
<p>No more dismissing strange sounds in the apartment.</p>
<p>No more pretending the air didn’t ripple when something from another realm manifested.</p>
<p>Hours after Miss Zu Li left, Mark stood in the kitchen doorway, staring in awe.</p>
<p>Inside the kitchen, Gor’thala moved like she was a professional with her ten feet tall figure and her smooth greenish skin.</p>
<p>Her powerful arms that could likely rip steel apart was currently stirring a pot delicately.</p>
<p>Two elegant tusks protruded from her mouth, curving slightly upward. She wore a black-and-white maid outfit that looked comically small on her massive frame, yet somehow tailored to her proportions.</p>
<p>The frilled apron stretched across her broad torso, and despite her intimidating size, she handled utensils like a trained chef.</p>
<p>She looked incredibly hot and the back of her thick green thighs were on full display due to how short the maid outfit was.</p>
<p>The scent of garlic, herbs, and simmering broth filled the air.</p>
<p>Mark blinked multiple times.</p>
<p>"This... this is real?"</p>
<p>West leaned casually against the wall.</p>
<p>"Very."</p>
<p>Gor’thala turned slightly and bowed her head.</p>
<p>"Good morning, Father of Master."</p>
<p>Mark nearly choked.</p>
<p>"She talks?"</p>
<p>"Yes," West replied flatly.</p>
<p>"And cooks," Gor’thala added calmly.</p>
<p>Mark ran a hand over his head.</p>
<p>"In my kitchen..."</p>
<p>Gor’thala had spent weeks quietly absorbing information from the internet—recipes, cooking techniques, seasoning balances. She had taken to it with the same discipline she applied to combat.</p>
<p>Now she moved from stove to counter with confidence.</p>
<p>On the other side of the apartment, Seraphyra glided across the living room.</p>
<p>Today she had chosen her humanoid form.</p>
<p>Long black and silver hair cascaded down her back as she moved a cloth over surfaces that were already spotless, humming faintly to herself.</p>
<p>Every so often she would flick her fingers, and faint sparks of energy would clean dust from impossible corners.</p>
<p>Mark watched her pass.</p>
<p>"...And that one?"</p>
<p>"Seraphyra," West replied.</p>
<p>Seraphyra paused, turned slowly, and gave Mark a charming smile.</p>
<p>"Good morning, Father of Master."</p>
<p>Mark stared.</p>
<p>"My home..."</p>
<p>He looked around.</p>
<p>One towering green warrior-maid cooking...</p>
<p>One enchanting serpent-woman cleaning...</p>
<p>Both occasionally glancing toward West.</p>
<p>Both calling him master.</p>
<p>Seraphyra drifted closer and tilted her head.</p>
<p>"Master, are things to your satisfaction?"</p>
<p>Gor’thala turned as well.</p>
<p>"Is the seasoning adequate, Master?"</p>
<p>Mark’s head snapped toward his son.</p>
<p>"...Master?"</p>
<p>West shrugged.</p>
<p>"It’s a title."</p>
<p>Mark burst into disbelieving laughter.</p>
<p>"My house has turned into some kind of fantasy drama."</p>
<p>West smirked faintly.</p>
<p>"You’re adjusting well."</p>
<p>"I don’t think I’ve adjusted at all."</p>
<p>Still, Mark couldn’t hide the pride in his eyes.</p>
<p>His son commanded mythical beings and they served him willingly.</p>
<p>---</p>
<p>Later that afternoon, West left to meet Jax at the industrial site.</p>
<p>The abandoned structures offered privacy and space.</p>
<p>Jax was already waiting, frost curling faintly around his fingertips.</p>
<p>"You ready?" Jax grinned.</p>
<p>West nodded.</p>
<p>They trained until sunset. Jax practiced shaping frost constructs while West tested Volt Manipulation, letting thin arcs of electricity dance between his fingers and absorbing them from electrical outputs nearby.</p>
<p>Kinetic Absorption activated when Jax launched a dense ice projectile at him.</p>
<p>The impact thudded against West’s torso and disappeared.</p>
<p>The absorbed force converted into a subtle glow beneath his skin and when he sent a punch out, the force was amplified.</p>
<p>West smiled slightly... first week of training completed... and they were making progress.</p>
<p>---</p>
<p>Sunday arrived slower and Mark had plans.</p>
<p>"Heading to a friend’s party," he explained while adjusting his jacket.</p>
<p>He paused at the door.</p>
<p>"You sure you don’t want to come?"</p>
<p>West shook his head.</p>
<p>"Got things to do."</p>
<p>Mark nodded knowingly.</p>
<p>"Don’t burn the house down."</p>
<p>"No promises."</p>
<p>The door shut and silence settled over the apartment.</p>
<p>An hour later, West’s phone buzzed.</p>
<p>Nina:</p>
<p>~ Can I bring the art pieces over today? ~</p>
<p>West’s lips curved slowly.</p>
<p>~ Sure. I’ll be expecting you. ~</p>
<p>He looked up.</p>
<p>"Gor’thala."</p>
<p>She appeared from the kitchen doorway.</p>
<p>"Yes, Master."</p>
<p>"Prepare a couple of nice dishes."</p>
<p>Her eyes flickered briefly with understanding.</p>
<p>"It shall be done."</p>
<p>Seraphyra emerged from the hallway, smirking.</p>
<p>"Is Master about to engage in copulation with yet another female?"</p>
<p>West shot her a look.</p>
<p>"Mind your business."</p>
<p>She circled him slowly with amusement in her green seductive eyes.</p>
<p>"You are very busy lately."</p>
<p>West turned away.</p>
<p>"Both of you go patrol the neighborhood or something."</p>
<p>Gor’thala bowed and exited through the balcony with a single powerful leap.</p>
<p>Seraphyra lingered a second longer.</p>
<p>"Do not break her too badly, Master."</p>
