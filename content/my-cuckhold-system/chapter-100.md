<p>Weekend arrived quietly without alarms or school rush.</p>
<p>Just morning sunlight filtering through the curtains of West’s apartment.</p>
<p>He had woken up later than usual, planning to spend the day refining his new skills which included testing Volt Manipulation and maybe experimenting with Kinetic Absorption somewhere discreet.</p>
<p>Instead, the moment he walked into the living room, he froze.</p>
<p>Mark was seated comfortably on the couch.</p>
<p>Across from him, sitting elegantly with one leg crossed over the other, dressed in a tailored ivory blazer and slim black trousers, was Miss Zu Li.</p>
<p>The Miss Zu Li.</p>
<p>Leader of Dragon Fang Gang and one of the city’s most powerful awakened figures.</p>
<p>West stopped mid-step.</p>
<p>"...How?"</p>
<p>Mark looked up and grinned broadly.</p>
<p>"Ah, there he is!"</p>
<p>Zu Li turned her head slowly and a composed smile formed the moment her eyes landed on West.</p>
<p>"Good morning."</p>
<p>West didn’t return the smile but Mark stood up excitedly.</p>
<p>"Son... why didn’t you tell me you had awakened?"</p>
<p>West mouth hung slightly open for a brief second as his gaze shifted slowly from his father to Zu Li.</p>
<p>Zu Li held his stare without flinching but she could feel how piercing it was like he was triggered by the current scenario.</p>
<p>Mark continued, oblivious to the tension thickening the air.</p>
<p>"Miss Zu Li came to my workplace yesterday! Imagine that! To my workplace!" He laughed incredulously. "I nearly fainted when she introduced herself."</p>
<p>West’s eyes narrowed.</p>
<p>"She explained everything," Mark continued. "About your awakening and about opportunities."</p>
<p>Zu Li added gently, "I thought it was important to inform your father personally."</p>
<p>West nearly cursed but he held it in. He wanted to inform his father about it himself but not yet.</p>
<p>Mark’s voice carried excitement like a child’s.</p>
<p>"She said not only are you awakened, but she wants you to join Dragon Fang. Not just that—she wants you as her apprentice."</p>
<p>He turned to West proudly.</p>
<p>"Do you know how big that is? She’s always on television. Every other week there’s a report about Dragon Fang operations."</p>
<p>Mark’s excited was understandable. Which father wouldn’t want their son affiliated with someone like her? Every parent would want the best for their child, after all.</p>
<p>Mark clasped his hands together.</p>
<p>"I knew your future was set the moment she told me."</p>
<p>Zu Li’s smile softened.</p>
<p>"There are many benefits, West. Training resources. Protection. Guidance. You wouldn’t have to stumble your way through the awakened world alone."</p>
<p>West finally spoke with a calm voice.</p>
<p>"I didn’t expect you to stoop this low."</p>
<p>The temperature in the room seemed to drop.</p>
<p>Mark blinked.</p>
<p>"Son?"</p>
<p>West stepped forward slightly with his eyes locked onto Zu Li.</p>
<p>"Finding my father’s workplace. Showing up here."</p>
<p>His lips curved faintly but there was no humor.</p>
<p>"Now that you know my apartment... I suppose I should expect others as well."</p>
<p>Zu Li shook her head immediately.</p>
<p>"I came alone."</p>
<p>Her tone lost some of its polished sharpness.</p>
<p>"No one else will ever find out where you live."</p>
<p>West’s voice sharpened.</p>
<p>"Why involve my father?"</p>
<p>Mark looked between them, confused.</p>
<p>"What’s going on? She was just informing me—"</p>
<p>West cut him off gently but firmly.</p>
<p>"Dad."</p>
<p>He kept his gaze on Zu Li.</p>
<p>"You shouldn’t have brought him into this."</p>
<p>Zu Li’s fingers tightened slightly on her knee.</p>
<p>"It wasn’t my intention to coerce you."</p>
<p>"It feels like coercion."</p>
<p>A flicker of guilt passed across her face.</p>
<p>"I only wanted transparency."</p>
<p>West’s composure cracked slightly as anger bled into his tone.</p>
<p>"You could have spoken to me directly. You had my attention already."</p>
<p>Before Zu Li could remind West that he had been completely evasive, Mark frowned slightly.</p>
<p>"Where are your manners, West? This is Miss Zu Li."</p>
<p>West didn’t take his eyes off her.</p>
<p>"Father... Miss Zu Li was just leaving."</p>
<p>His voice hardened.</p>
<p>"Weren’t you, Miss Zu Li?"</p>
<p>The room went quiet as Zu Li held his gaze.</p>
<p>For a brief moment, she saw something in his eyes she hadn’t expected.... authority...</p>
<p>It unsettled her.</p>
<p>She exhaled softly.</p>
<p>"...Yes."</p>
<p>She rose gracefully.</p>
<p>"I was just leaving."</p>
<p>Mark looked baffled.</p>
<p>"But—"</p>
<p>Zu Li gently picked up her handbag and stepped forward.</p>
<p>She placed a smooth black card on the coffee table.</p>
<p>"My private number."</p>
<p>Then she paused in front of West and lowered her voice.</p>
<p>"I don’t know why you’re so adamant."</p>
<p>Her eyes searched his face.</p>
<p>"But eventually... you will have to join a gang. Nobody survives this awakened world alone."</p>
<p>West didn’t hesitate.</p>
<p>"I never said I was doing it alone."</p>
<p>A faint smirk touched his lips.</p>
<p>"Just not doing it with you."</p>
<p>The words struck harder than he intended.</p>
<p>He leaned slightly closer.</p>
<p>"If you had a chance before... congratulations."</p>
<p>"You just lost it."</p>
<p>For the first time since entering the apartment, Miss Zu Li faltered.</p>
<p>A small breath escaped her lips.</p>
<p>"I’m sorry... if I came off the wrong way."</p>
<p>Her tone softened further.</p>
<p>"I’ll leave now. I don’t want to further annoy you."</p>
<p>Mark stood there, stunned. Since when did the pompous and prideful Miss Zu Li turn into a composed and respectful kitty? Why were they talking like they already knew each other? What is really going on?</p>
<p>All sorts of thoughts plagued Mark’s mind as Zu Li walked toward the door.</p>
<p>---</p>
<p>Outside at the hallway, Zu Li remained still for a moment.</p>
<p>A strange sensation twisted in her chest.</p>
<p>It was guilt.</p>
<p>She hadn’t felt that since she became awakened...</p>
<p>When did she start apologizing?</p>
<p>When did she start... explaining herself?</p>
<p>She descended the stairs and exited the building.</p>
<p>A black sedan waited at the curb.</p>
<p>As she stepped into the passenger seat, the car drove off immediately.</p>
<p>In the seat, she leaned back, closed her eyes and replayed the scene.</p>
<p>His glare... his refusal... his refusal to bend.</p>
<p>Her lips parted slightly.</p>
<p>"When did I start becoming so humble in front of someone else..."</p>
<p>An 18-year-old boy.</p>
<p>She had faced veteran gang leaders... negotiated with officials... threatened powerful figures...</p>
<p>Yet, in his presence, she found herself shrinking.... choosing softer words... wanting to avoid displeasing him.</p>
