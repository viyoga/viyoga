<p>Thin streams of vapor seeped out from narrow cracks along the walls, curling into the air like restless spirits.</p>
<p>There were hundreda of triangular holes lining the walls, the ceiling and even parts of the floor.</p>
<p>Each one looked deep like something could emerge from them at any moment.</p>
<p>West stopped in the center of the chamber and his gaze moved slowly, analyzing the surroundings.</p>
<p>It wasn’t just a resting area... it felt like a nest.</p>
<p>He craned his neck slightly, looking higher and then he spotted something hanging from above.</p>
<p>A massive shape suspended from the ceiling by a thick, mucous-like strand.</p>
<p>West’s eyes narrowed slightly taken aback. The creature was enormous.</p>
<p>It was at least twenty feet long with a upper body that resembled a grotesquely overgrown gorilla. Its muscles bulged unnaturally beneath dark, armored skin but below was a far more disturbing lower body...</p>
<p>Connected to its torso was multiple long spiky spider-like legs. They extended outward, folding tightly inward as it hung dormant.</p>
<p>From its back, a pair of large, membranous wings rested, twitching faintly even in its stillness.</p>
<p>Its head was tilted slightly downward and all of its eight eyes were closed as it breathed slowly.</p>
<p>It was asleep.</p>
<p>West stared at it for a moment before exhaling lightly.</p>
<p>’Better to kill it now.’</p>
<p>His hand moved as he pulled something out of his inventory.</p>
<p>What appeared in his grasp was a massive black blade, far too large for a normal person to wield comfortably.</p>
<p>Dark mist seeped from its surface like a living thing as the air around it seemed to distort slightly.</p>
<p>West gripped it firmly and without hesitation, he swung.</p>
<p>A powerful arc of darkness tore through the air, cutting upward toward the suspended creature.</p>
<p>However, the moment the blade moved, the creature’s eyes suddenly sprung open.</p>
<p>Its entire body reacted instantly as the mucous strand attached to it snapped.</p>
<p>It dropped from the air rapidly...</p>
<p>WHOOSH—!</p>
<p>Evading the attack by mere inches.</p>
<p>The dark arc sliced through empty air and slammed into the ceiling, leaving a rough scar across the stone.</p>
<p>The creature landed with a heavy BOOM, cracking the ground beneath it.</p>
<p>Up close, it was even more terrifying, large and grotesque.</p>
<p>Its multiple eyes locked onto West as it moved swiftly.</p>
<p>The wings on its back flapped together violently.</p>
<p>THOOM—!</p>
<p>A silver wave of energy blasted forward, tearing through the air like a compressed shockwave.</p>
<p>West’s eyes narrowed.</p>
<p>"Radiant Acceleration."</p>
<p>&lt;[ Radiant Acceleration Activated ]&gt;</p>
<p>His body ignited as a sun-like glow erupted around him, bursting outward and skyrocketing his speed.</p>
<p>The wave passed through where he stood a fraction of a second earlier as he vanished, slamming into the wall behind him and detonating in a violent explosion.</p>
<p>West reappeared to the side... in motion.</p>
<p>He spun and in an instant, he was beside the creature swing the massive dark sword towards its legs.</p>
<p>CLANG—!</p>
<p>At the last moment, a strange crystallized coating formed over the creature’s limbs.</p>
<p>The moment the blade struck, it shattered through the crystal but slowed significantly due to weakened momentum.</p>
<p>Unfortunately, West was only able to deal a very shallow damage due to the crystallized coating.</p>
<p>The creature let out a deep growl that vibrated through the chamber.</p>
<p>Its massive arm rose and came crashing down.</p>
<p>West leapt back instantly as the fist slammed into the ground.</p>
<p>BOOM—!</p>
<p>The stone beneath shattered, causing cracks to spread outward in all directions.</p>
<p>West flipped mid-air and landed lightly over twenty feet away but the creature didn’t pause.</p>
<p>It charged at him with astonishing speed, swinging its fists wildly.</p>
<p>West swerved, zig-zagging across the terrain as he evaded the attacks which slammed into the ground and even walls, shattering them in the process.</p>
<p>He stepped in again after evading another attack, swinging at the creature’s legs.</p>
<p>The crystal formed again and shattered after impact, causing West to deal minimal damage.</p>
<p>"Tch."</p>
<p>The creature flapped its wings again.</p>
<p>THOOM—!</p>
<p>This time, West was a fraction too late and the silver wave struck him directly.</p>
<p>BOOM—!</p>
<p>His body was blasted backward as the force sent him flying across the chamber.</p>
<p>The Guardian Sword slipped from his grip mid-air as West flipped repeatedly—</p>
<p>He suddenly stabilized in the air before slamming his feet against the far wall and arching upon it.</p>
<p>CRACK—!</p>
<p>The stone dented under the impact as he instantly pushed off, launching himself forward again with his feet.</p>
<p>BOOM—!</p>
<p>The creature massive fist swung again as West’s figure shot across the air towards it.</p>
<p>West on the other hand, didn’t try to evade... instead, he swung back.</p>
<p>BOOOOOOM—!</p>
<p>Their fists collided and the impact exploded outward.</p>
<p>A shockwave rippled through the chamber causing the creature stagger three steps back while West landed smoothly.</p>
<p>Their eyes locked as he moved again, sweeping straight for his sword.</p>
<p>He grabbed it mid-run and turned before charging towards the creature again.</p>
<p>The creature steadied itself while its multiple eyes fell onto its fist tuat had clashed with West’s... its gorilla like fingers had twisted in strange directions and was bleeding.</p>
<p>It screeched in agony and suddenly opened its mouth...</p>
<p>Twwwhooommm~ Twwwhooommm~ Twwwhooommm~ Twwwhooommm~</p>
<p>Greenish, viscous webs shot outward rapidly as West approached.</p>
<p>West’s eyes sharpened as he dodged them one after the other.</p>
<p>But the webs kept coming faster until there was no space left to evade.</p>
<p>West’s grip tightened as he swung.</p>
<p>SHHHHK—!</p>
<p>The blade cut through the webs, slicing them apart mid-air.</p>
<p>He burst through them and closed the distance between him and the creature almost instantaneously.</p>
<p>Now he was right in front of it once again, swinging towards its legs.</p>
<p>Once again, crystal coating spread across its legs, creating a layer of protection.</p>
<p>However, this time, something was different.</p>
<p>A faint purple mist began to wrap around the blade.</p>
<p>"Poison Mist."</p>
<p>&lt;[ Poison Mist Activated ]&gt;</p>
<p>West had used an ability he got from Seraphyra to make the blade even more deadly.</p>
<p>He swung with all his might and as soon as contact was made...</p>
<p>BOOOOOOM—!</p>
<p>The crystal shattered completely like glass hit by a hammer and without slowing, the blade tore straight through the leg... and kept going.</p>
<p>In one unstoppable swing, West severed four legs causing the creature to scream as its body tilted violently.</p>
<p>Blood erupted outward in thick streams while it collapsed to the side.</p>
<p>In the process of collapsing, West stepped forward, leapt and drove the blade upward with everything he had.</p>
<p>The massive black blade pierced through the creature’s skull... ripping through flesh, bones and armor before bursting out the other side and splitting its head cleanly in two.</p>
