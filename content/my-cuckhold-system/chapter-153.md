<p>Next, West grabbed several empty plastic bottles and carefully, he poured the glowing water from the chalice into the bottles.</p>
<p>Each one filled with faint red liquid.</p>
<p>West repeated the process several times and soon he had multiple bottles containing liquid from the chalice.</p>
<p>He placed them back into his inventory before nodding in satisfaction.</p>
<p>"Perfect."</p>
<p>Now if he ever found himself inside a dangerous ruins again or injured in battle, he wouldn’t need to search for liquid to put in the chalice.</p>
<p>The healing supply would already be prepared.</p>
<p>Seraphyra glanced over from across the room.</p>
<p>"You look pleased."</p>
<p>West leaned back on the couch again.</p>
<p>"Just preparing for the future."</p>
<p>Gor’thala finished setting down a heavy cabinet while Aurethia adjusted a chair.</p>
<p>The luxurious apartment slowly transformed into a fully furnished living space.</p>
<p>...</p>
<p>...</p>
<p>Tuesday morning arrived quickly.</p>
<p>After the long process of moving into his new apartment and organizing everything the previous night, West finally returned to school. The week itself had already started moving at a fast pace again.</p>
<p>He had classes, personal training, gang planning and the quiet preparation for what he intended to do next.</p>
<p>Even though graduation was around the corner, West had far more important matters on his mind.</p>
<p>During breaks, he continued teaching Jax and Nina how to better control their branches. Both of them were progressing faster than expected, though they still lacked combat experience.</p>
<p>However, that could be handled later.</p>
<p>Right now, West was focused on something else entirely.</p>
<p>Money... or specifically, the lack of it.</p>
<p>After paying for the luxurious apartment and other expenses, West had roughly $70,000 remaining in his account.</p>
<p>This would normally be a lot of money for someone his age but not for what he planned to do.</p>
<p>To officially register a gang, there were several requirements.</p>
<p>One of them was financial proof...</p>
<p>The folks in charge demanded at least $100,000 in declared funds to prove the gang had operational capability.</p>
<p>West leaned back in his classroom chair while staring out the window.</p>
<p>"Thirty thousand short..."</p>
<p>He wasn’t worried though... bcause he had something far more valuable than cash.</p>
<p>Loot... materials from both the Residential and Eternal Ruins.</p>
<p>After school ended that afternoon, West headed straight back to his apartment.</p>
<p>He changed into different clothes and covered half his face with a black nose mask, concealing his identity.</p>
<p>Then he opened his inventory and examined the materials again.</p>
<p>After a moment of consideration, he chose what he would sell.</p>
<p>• Several Eternal Growth Crystals</p>
<p>• Blacksteel Ruin Fragments</p>
<p>• A few Eternal Ore Chunks</p>
<p>All of them were valuable crafting materials but they weren’t particularly useful to him personally so selling them made sense.</p>
<p>With his items selected, West left the apartment and headed toward a familiar part of the city.</p>
<p>---</p>
<p>About thirty minutes later, he arrived.</p>
<p>The place looked exactly the same as before.</p>
<p>From the outside, it appeared to be nothing more than a rundown warehouse wedged between two abandoned buildings.</p>
<p>Rust covered most of the metal panels, graffiti covered the concrete walls and the windows were so dirty it was impossible to see inside...</p>
<p>To any random passerby, it looked like an old building waiting to be demolished but those who mattered knew this was one of the city’s well known underground trading hubs.</p>
<p>A place where gangs conducted deals...</p>
<p>Where relics recovered from ruins changed hands and dangerous artifacts were exchanged for money, favors, or information.</p>
<p>Several men lingered outside, standing in groups smoking.</p>
<p>Their eyes constantly scanned the street amd most of them were awakened.</p>
<p>West walked past them without hesitation just as before and no one stopped him.</p>
<p>Inside, it was just as he remembered...</p>
<p>There was a large lounge area occupied one side of the warehouse, furnished with black leather couches where gang members casually discussed deals.</p>
<p>A metal staircase led to the second floor, likely containing private negotiation rooms.</p>
<p>And along the far wall stood a row of professional counters, each staffed by employees dressed in fitted business suits.</p>
<p>It looked more like a high-end bank operating inside a criminal warehouse.</p>
<p>West scanned the counters and noticed someone familiar...</p>
<p>The calm woman from last time who had kicked an aggressive awakener out of the building with a single strike.</p>
<p>She was sitting behind one of the counters, looking calm and professional as ever.</p>
<p>West approached just as two gang members finished their transaction and walked away.</p>
<p>He stepped forward and placed his hands on the counter.</p>
<p>"I’d like to sell materials."</p>
<p>The woman looked up and immediately assessed him with her gaze.</p>
<p>Even though he wore a mask and different clothes, something about him seemed familiar but she said nothing.</p>
<p>Instead she calmly pushed a transaction form toward him.</p>
<p>"Fill this out."</p>
<p>West began writing.</p>
<p>Name: Rain</p>
<p>Affiliation: Independent</p>
<p>Items for sale.</p>
<p>Then he listed the quantities.</p>
<p>When she read the form, her eyebrows slightly furrowed because she couldn’t see anything on him.</p>
<p>No bags, crates or any sort of container and yet he was claiming to sell multiple ruin materials.</p>
<p>Still, she remained professional.</p>
<p>"Follow me."</p>
<p>She stood up and led him toward the staircase.</p>
<p>Apparently she somewhat remembered him from last time.</p>
<p>They moved into a private inspection room upstairs, containing a reinforced table and scanning equipment used to examine relics.</p>
<p>The moment the door closed, West began unloading the materials.</p>
<p>One by one the eternal Growth Crystals appeared and after the Blacksteel fragments... lastly he placed loads of Eternal ore chunks on the table.</p>
<p>The woman’s calm eyes widened slightly but she tried not to show it even though she was shocked internally.</p>
<p>How does an independent awakener have this many materials?</p>
<p>Usually resources like this only appeared in the hands of established gangs.</p>
<p>To make things even crazier, he could somehow carry this many materials around without having any physical storage unit with him? How?</p>
<p>Despite all the questions swimming in her head, she asked no questions.</p>
<p>Instead she began the inspection process.</p>
<p>Each item was scanned and examined for authenticity.</p>
<p>Energy signatures were measured and quality grading was recorded.</p>
<p>The process took about fifteen minutes and when she was done, she finally looked up.</p>
<p>"These materials are high quality."</p>
<p>West nodded.</p>
<p>"I figured."</p>
<p>She calculated quickly on her tablet.</p>
<p>"These can be purchased in bulk for $520,000."</p>
<p>West leaned back slightly.</p>
<p>"Sounds fair."</p>
<p>She looked surprised.</p>
<p>Most people tried to negotiate for a higher deal but West simply accepted.</p>
<p>She finalized the transaction.</p>
<p>"Where should the money be transferred?"</p>
<p>West replied calmly.</p>
<p>"Same account as last time."</p>
<p>He of course picked Jax’s account again and the transfer process began.</p>
<p>While waiting, West casually said:</p>
<p>"Good kick."</p>
<p>The woman blinked.</p>
<p>"...Excuse me?"</p>
<p>West tilted his head slightly.</p>
<p>"The awakener you kicked out last time."</p>
<p>"Oh."</p>
<p>Her eyes softened slightly in recognition.</p>
<p>"Yes... Thanks I guess."</p>
<p>West chuckled lightly while she studied him more carefully now.</p>
<p>Most awakeners who came here were arrogant, entitled and violent but this one spoke calmly without throwing his weight around.</p>
<p>No ego or threats... just business.</p>
<p>After a moment West asked something unexpected.</p>
<p>"I know you’re awakened but..."</p>
<p>Her eyes flickered.</p>
<p>"How come you’re not out clearing ruins?"</p>
<p>"Why work behind a desk?"</p>
