<p>The creature’s body trembled once before completely going still.</p>
<p>Its massive form crashed fully against the ground and the chamber fell quiet.</p>
<p>West landed lightly as the purple mist faded and the glow around him dimmed.</p>
<p>He let out a lengthy exhaled as rushed footsteps echoed through the vicinity.</p>
<p>Tara, Derek and Mei burst into the chamber thinking West was in trouble due to the loud blasts they heard earlier.</p>
<p>They came in ready to help save him but what they saw, stopped them in their tracks.</p>
<p>A massive corpse with its head split in half and blood pooling across the ground.</p>
<p>And a figure standing before it calmly, holding a blade without so much as a scratch on his body.</p>
<p>Their minds went blank.</p>
<p>"...What the hell?" Derek whispered.</p>
<p>Tara’s eyes were widened as she locked onto the creature before sweeping towards West.</p>
<p>"...How?"</p>
<p>Tara, Derek, and Mei stood frozen with their eyes locked on the massive corpse that had just been split cleanly in two.</p>
<p>Tara stepped forward slowly, still staring at West.</p>
<p>"You’re a summoner..."</p>
<p>Her brows furrowed.</p>
<p>"So how did you—"</p>
<p>West didn’t reply. He simply rested the massive Guardian Sword against his shoulder with a calm and almost indifferent expression.</p>
<p>Mei, who had remained silent till now, spoke softly.</p>
<p>"...That wasn’t normal."</p>
<p>"No summoner fights like that."</p>
<p>Before anyone could push further—</p>
<p>RUMBLE—!</p>
<p>The ground shook violently.</p>
<p>"...What was that?" Mei questioned with a tense look as the rumbling intensified.</p>
<p>RUMBLE! RUMBLE!</p>
<p>The triangular holes lining the walls began to glow faintly as a low, eerie chittering sound echoed from within them.</p>
<p>West’s eyes narrowed slightly.</p>
<p>And in the next second, the triangular holes erupted as creatures poured out in waves.</p>
<p>They flooded out in dozens at first before growing higher in number, turning into an overwhelming swarm.</p>
<p>They looked like smaller versions of the boss creature West had just killed.</p>
<p>They were still grotesque with gorilla like upperbodies and arachnid like lower body but smaller.</p>
<p>They were roughly the size of large wolves and some were even bigger.</p>
<p>Only that now, the cavern was filled with hundreds of them.</p>
<p>Their screeches overlapped into a chaotic, deafening noise as they charged.</p>
<p>"...You’ve got to be kidding me," Derek whispered.</p>
<p>Tara immediately stepped back.</p>
<p>"We need to move!"</p>
<p>"Fall back!" West said calmly and this time, no one questioned him.</p>
<p>They retreated quickly, pulling back from the center of the cavern toward the narrower entrance corridor.</p>
<p>The swarm shot forward relentlessly and the first wave lunged at them.</p>
<p>Derek activated his transformation ability instantly and his body bulked up as he stepped forward to intercept.</p>
<p>Tara’s blade-like arms extended as she slashed at incoming creatures.</p>
<p>Mei darted to the side, pick off targets one by one as she swung spinning glowing blades forward.</p>
<p>But it was too much.... even for all three of them combined.</p>
<p>"They just keep coming!" Tara shouted.</p>
<p>"West—!"</p>
<p>She turned just in time to see him West stretch his hand forward.</p>
<p>"Come out."</p>
<p>A wave of energy rippled outward as the nd the air distorted while three figures appeared.</p>
<p>One had a serpentine like lower body that coiled elegantly with a strikingly beautiful humanoid upper body.</p>
<p>Her eyes glowed with green and gold as venomous energy gathered around her.</p>
<p>While the second was a ten-foot-tall orc warrior-mage, gripping her staff as raw power radiated from her presence.</p>
<p>The third was a knight with a golden saber at their side and their Armor exuding a faint glint.</p>
<p>The moment they appeared, the atmosphere changed.</p>
<p>Derek’s jaw dropped.</p>
<p>"...No way."</p>
<p>Tara’s eyes widened.</p>
<p>"...Three?"</p>
<p>Mei whispered.</p>
<p>"He has... three summons?"</p>
<p>Which meant... he had three branches.</p>
<p>Their minds struggled to process it but there was no time.</p>
<p>The creatures were already upon them and West’s summons moved.</p>
<p>Gor’thala slammed her staff into the ground.</p>
<p>BOOM!</p>
<p>Vines erupted outward, entangling dozens of creatures at once while Seraphyra inhaled slightly before exhaling.</p>
<p>A thick, poisonous mist spread across the battlefield, corroding anything that entered it.</p>
<p>Creatures screamed as their bodies began to deteriorate.</p>
<p>Aurethia suddenly vanished... she was so fast that no one saw her move.</p>
<p>In the next instant, over twenty golden lines flashed across the air.</p>
<p>Dozens of creatures froze before collapsing into pieces, cleanly cut.</p>
<p>West stepped forward as well and swung the heavy Guardian Sword like it weighed nothing.</p>
<p>Each strike obliterated multiple enemies at once as the battlefield turned chaotic.</p>
<p>Derek, Tara, and Mei found themselves with almost nothing to do.</p>
<p>Every time they engaged a creature, one of West’s summons had already dealt with three more.</p>
<p>"What... is this..." Derek muttered under his breath.</p>
<p>Tara watched as Aurethia moved through the battlefield like a phantom.</p>
<p>"...That knight..."</p>
<p>"He’s insane..."</p>
<p>They didn’t even know that Aurethia was female.</p>
<p>Mei looked toward West and noticed that he wasn’t just commanding them, he was fighting alongside them perfectly synchronized like they were extensions of him.</p>
<p>As the minutes passed, the swarm began to thin.</p>
<p>Wave after wave fell, until finally, the last creature let out a shriek and collapsed.</p>
<p>Silence returned and the cavern floor was covered in hundreds of corpses.</p>
<p>Tara exhaled heavily.</p>
<p>"...We didn’t do anything."</p>
<p>Derek let out a dry laugh.</p>
<p>"No... we didn’t."</p>
<p>Mei looked at West.</p>
<p>"...You’re a monster."</p>
<p>Without responding, West simply dismissed his summons.</p>
<p>They vanished back into the summons personal space as if they had never been there.</p>
<p>A few minutes later, footsteps echoed from the corridor.</p>
<p>Marcus and the others rushed in, clearly prepared for the worst.</p>
<p>However, what they found, stopped them in their tracks.</p>
<p>"...What happened here?" Lina whispered while staring at the signs of battle and bodies that littered the surroundings.</p>
<p>Kyle frowned.</p>
<p>"Are they... alive?"</p>
<p>Marcus side of the group had cleared the other section and in the process they realised that West’s group must have been on the part of the core section.</p>
<p>So after they cleared theirs, they rushed up as fast as they could to get here.</p>
<p>Heading further ahead, they spotted Tara, Derek, and Mei gathered around West... very much alive and unharmed.</p>
<p>Marcus let out a sigh of relief before approaching with a look of curiosity.</p>
<p>"...You cleared this?" he questioned.</p>
