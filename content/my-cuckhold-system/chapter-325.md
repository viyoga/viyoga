<p>Everything had become far more complicated.</p>
<p>Yet... another thought steadily pushed its way to the front of his mind...</p>
<p>Castle Extingo.</p>
<p>A ruin unlike ordinary ruins... appearing only once every hundred years and accessible exclusively to first-year awakeners.</p>
<p>A treasure that entire factions had slaughtered each other over for centuries.</p>
<p>He quietly muttered, "...What exactly are you?"</p>
<p>Without wasting another moment, he returned to his office.</p>
<p>---</p>
<p>The lights inside remained on until sunrise.</p>
<p>Computer screens illuminated the room showing digital archives, old expedition records, forum discussions and ancient newspaper articles.</p>
<p>West searched everything he could find but unfortunately... very little information actually existed.</p>
<p>Almost everything regarding Castle Extingo had either been classified or deliberately erased.</p>
<p>What little he managed to gather only deepened the mystery.</p>
<p>Every account described the castle differently.</p>
<p>Some called it an endless kingdom, others described a floating fortress.</p>
<p>One witness claimed the hallways constantly rearranged themselves while another insisted entire cities existed inside.</p>
<p>Some reports spoke of impossible beasts. Others mentioned ancient guardians that had never been defeated.</p>
<p>One recurring detail that appeared again and again said the castle closed exactly one month after opening.</p>
<p>Anyone still inside vanished alongside it, whether they survived or not... no one knew.</p>
<p>West leaned back in his chair. "...Interesting... very interesting."</p>
<p>By the time the first rays of sunlight entered through the windows... he realized he had never gone to bed.</p>
<p>His coffee had gone cold hours earlier but his mind remained active.</p>
<p>Kon~</p>
<p>Kon~</p>
<p>Kon~</p>
<p>The sound of knocking echoed from up ahead.</p>
<p>West looked toward the door. "Come in."</p>
<p>The office door opened and Liz stepped inside carrying a neat stack of folders alongside a tablet.</p>
<p>She smiled warmly. "I told you I’d be back."</p>
<p>Her eyes slowly narrowed as she stopped walking. "...Wait."</p>
<p>"You..." she looked him up and down. "...You’re still wearing yesterday’s clothes."</p>
<p>West scratched the back of his neck. "...Yeah."</p>
<p>She blinked. "Don’t tell me..."</p>
<p>"...you stayed up all night."</p>
<p>He laughed tiredly. "Gang leader duties."</p>
<p>She sighed sympathetically. "I was joking."</p>
<p>"You actually did."</p>
<p>West shrugged. "It happens."</p>
<p>Liz placed the folders onto his desk before looking at him again.</p>
<p>A trace of concern crossed her face. "It must be exhausting."</p>
<p>West leaned back in his chair. "You have absolutely no idea."</p>
<p>She walked closer and for a brief moment, she simply studied his face.</p>
<p>Then she reached up naturally, brushing a stray lock of hair away from his forehead before lightly touching his cheek.</p>
<p>"You’ve got tired eyes," her thumb gently brushed beneath one of them. "You should rest."</p>
<p>West smiled. "I have endless energy. I’ll survive."</p>
<p>She smiled back. "I won’t keep you long then."</p>
<p>"I know you probably want to collapse into bed after this."</p>
<p>West chuckled. "Only bed I’d agree to collapse into, is one with you on it."</p>
<p>Her cheeks slightly flushed. "You..."</p>
<p>West quickly brushed past that statement, continuing on normally. "This is nothing... I’ve literally handled worse."</p>
<p>She coughed twice, regaining her composure. "I somehow believe that."</p>
<p>Opening the first folder, she spread several documents across the desk.</p>
<p>"Everything we discussed yesterday has been prepared."</p>
<p>She pointed toward different sections.</p>
<p>"The commercial warehouse acquisition."</p>
<p>"The residential investment package."</p>
<p>"The industrial land purchase."</p>
<p>"The office complex."</p>
<p>"The legal documentation."</p>
<p>"The tax projections."</p>
<p>"I’ve also negotiated slightly better pricing than yesterday."</p>
<p>West looked up. "Already?"</p>
<p>She nodded proudly. "I made a few early morning calls."</p>
<p>"I told you..."</p>
<p>"...I could get you better deals."</p>
<p>West couldn’t help smiling. "You really don’t waste time."</p>
<p>"I can’t afford to."</p>
<p>She slid another document toward him. "If we move quickly..."</p>
<p>"Most of these properties can be secured before competing investors notice."</p>
<p>West picked up the pen and without hesitation, he began signing one document after another.</p>
<p>Liz watched him with a small smile. "You trust me quite a lot."</p>
<p>West continued signing. "I trust competence."</p>
<p>"And you’ve earned mine."</p>
<p>She looked genuinely pleased. "I won’t disappoint you."</p>
<p>"I know."</p>
<p>The final signature dried across the last page.</p>
<p>West closed the folder. "There."</p>
<p>"Business concluded."</p>
<p>Liz gathered the paperwork neatly.</p>
<p>"I’ll have everything processed immediately."</p>
<p>She paused before smiling. "And then..."</p>
<p>"You are going to sleep."</p>
<p>West laughed. "I’ll think about it."</p>
<p>"No," she folded her arms. "You’ll do it... even if I have to force you to bed myself."</p>
<p>West smirked. "Now wouldn’t that be delightful..."</p>
<p>She grabbed his left ear and pulled it. "Don’t be stubborn."</p>
<p>•••</p>
<p>•••</p>
<p>Over the next several days, one particular name occupied almost every free moment West had.</p>
<p>Castle Extingo.</p>
<p>Ever since Freya’s unexpected visit, the mysterious once-in-a-century phenomenon had refused to leave his thoughts.</p>
<p>Whenever he wasn’t attending to Shadow Reapers business, sparring, handling paperwork or checking on the gang’s rapid expansion, he was seated in his office with a mountain of files spread across his desk.</p>
<p>Aria had noticed his sudden obsession almost immediately.</p>
<p>Without asking too many questions, she simply delivered everything Shadow Reapers had managed to gather regarding Castle Extingo over the years, including personal testimonies from century old first-year awakeners who had somehow survived previous expeditions.</p>
<p>Unfortunately... much of the information contradicted itself.</p>
<p>Even so, a general picture slowly began forming.</p>
<p>West leaned back in his chair while flipping through another thick folder.</p>
<p>"So that’s how it is..."</p>
<p>He finally understood why he had never heard of Castle Extingo before.</p>
<p>It wasn’t because it was secret... itwas because it only mattered to awakeners.</p>
<p>Before becoming awakened himself, he had been an ordinary high school student. Discussions about first-year awakened opportunities never reached ordinary civilians.</p>
<p>Within awakened circles... Castle Extingo was practically legendary. Everyone knew about it.</p>
<p>It was one of the greatest opportunities a newly awakened person could ever hope to encounter.</p>
<p>West opened another file, spotting a detailed architectural sketch.</p>
<p>"...No way," his eyebrows rose. "So it’s literally a castle."</p>
<p>The illustration depicted an impossibly gigantic fortress stretching far beyond what should have been physically possible.</p>
<p>Its walls resembled mountains while its towers disappeared into the clouds.</p>
<p>Countless bridges connected enormous sections together.</p>
<p>According to the estimated scale... the castle wasn’t simply large, it was approximately the size of an entire city.</p>
<p>West whistled. "A city disguised as a castle..."</p>
