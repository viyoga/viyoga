<p>West’s body stiffened slightly.</p>
<p>"...Whoa—"</p>
<p>It wasn’t painful but it was intense, infiltrating his core swiftly.</p>
<p>The energy spread rapidly through him, reaching his branches.</p>
<p>Inside him, he could feel the three branches responding...</p>
<p>Light began to spread, starting from the base and slowly rising upward.</p>
<p>---</p>
<p>[ BRANCH CULTIVATION IN PROGRESS ]</p>
<p>---</p>
<p>West’s breathing slowed as he focused inward, feeling the glow climb steadily.</p>
<p>From the bottom, to a quarter and then higher...</p>
<p>The sensation intensified, making his body feel lighter and more responsive.</p>
<p>The energy continued pushing upward until it stopped just below the center.</p>
<p>---</p>
<p>[ BRANCH STATUS UPDATED ]</p>
<p>Progress: Mid-Level Advancement Achieved</p>
<p>---</p>
<p>The glow stabilized as the energy slowly faded.</p>
<p>West opened his eyes and noticed that the stone in his hand had turned completely white.</p>
<p>"...Used up already."</p>
<p>He rolled it between his fingers and could tell that there was no more energy within.</p>
<p>Right now, it was just a shell.</p>
<p>"...Worth it."</p>
<p>West exhaled slowly because the difference was obvious.</p>
<p>He could feel that his branches had gotten more powerful but it didn’t affect only him...</p>
<p>A faint ripple passed through the room as three presences stirred... Gor’thala, Seraphyra and Aurethia.</p>
<p>West didn’t summon them but he could feel their internal state due to their connection to him.</p>
<p>"...So it affects them too."</p>
<p>West smirked slightly upon realizing they had gotten stronger due to this increment.</p>
<p>"That’s a bonus."</p>
<p>He stretched his arms slightly, feeling the boost in strength to his body.</p>
<p>"...Not bad at all."</p>
<p>He glanced at the system interface.</p>
<p>---</p>
<p>[ AFFINITY: 0% ]</p>
<p>---</p>
<p>"...Back to grinding."</p>
<p>He shrugged.</p>
<p>"Fine by me."</p>
<p>His expression turned sour when he took a glance at the clock.</p>
<p>"...Shit."</p>
<p>Over three hours had passed since he got the Affinity Stone. West moved immediately, heading for the bathroom.</p>
<p>Cold water ran down his body, washing away the lingering heat from the absorption.</p>
<p>By the time he stepped out, he felt even more refreshed. Without wasting time, he put on dark jeans, light jacket and a few accessories, like the dark earrings...</p>
<p>In no time, he was on his way to the Divine Forge...</p>
<p>—</p>
<p>By the time West arrived, it was already around 2 PM.</p>
<p>The building was the same as before... tall, futuristic looking with a well structured interior, filled with all sorts of awakened items.</p>
<p>West walked in casually, heading towards the workshop section.</p>
<p>The moment he stepped in, a familiar voice rang out.</p>
<p>"Oi!"</p>
<p>West looked up and there he was... Orion Voss.</p>
<p>His hair was messy but his eyes were full of excitement and his gloved hands were stained.</p>
<p>"Look who finally showed up!"</p>
<p>West smirked slightly.</p>
<p>"Didn’t think I’d miss this."</p>
<p>Orion walked over quickly, wiping his hands on a cloth.</p>
<p>"You didn’t have to come all the way here, you know? I would have wired your percentage to the same account as last time..."</p>
<p>West shrugged.</p>
<p>"I wanted to."</p>
<p>Then he added;</p>
<p>"...Also wanted to check something."</p>
<p>Orion grinned.</p>
<p>"Good timing then."</p>
<p>He turned and gestured toward a nearby table.</p>
<p>"It sold."</p>
<p>West raised a brow.</p>
<p>"...Already?"</p>
<p>"Yep!"</p>
<p>Orion looked almost proud.</p>
<p>"Second weapon. Gone."</p>
<p>West nodded slowly.</p>
<p>"...Price?"</p>
<p>"Same range," Orion said casually. "Around four-fifty."</p>
<p>West let out a quiet breath.</p>
<p>"...Nice."</p>
<p>Orion leaned closer slightly.</p>
<p>"Transaction’s already being processed."</p>
<p>"Your share’s coming through."</p>
<p>West nodded.</p>
<p>"Good."</p>
<p>West suddenly reached into his inventory while Orion’s eyes immediately sharpened as one item after the other appeared.</p>
<p>"...Wait—"</p>
<p>Soon a small pile had formed on the table</p>
<p>There were metallic shards and energy-infused materials... all necessary items for crafting.</p>
<p>Orion froze.</p>
<p>"...You serious right now?"</p>
<p>West glanced at him.</p>
<p>"I want more weapons."</p>
<p>Orion blinked.</p>
<p>"...More?"</p>
<p>West nodded.</p>
<p>"Yeah."</p>
<p>Then added—</p>
<p>"I’m thinking ahead."</p>
<p>Orion crossed his arms, studying him.</p>
<p>"...Explain."</p>
<p>West leaned slightly against the table.</p>
<p>"My gang will be official soon."</p>
<p>"And when that happens..."</p>
<p>"...People will join."</p>
<p>Orion’s eyes narrowed slightly.</p>
<p>"...And you want to arm them."</p>
<p>"Exactly."</p>
<p>Not just basic gear but real awakened weapons with boons that gave an edge.</p>
<p>West tapped one of the materials lightly.</p>
<p>"I don’t want dead weight."</p>
<p>Orion’s grin slowly spread.</p>
<p>"...I like that."</p>
<p>He picked up one of the fragments, inspecting it.</p>
<p>"You’ve got good stuff here."</p>
<p>West smirked.</p>
<p>"I know."</p>
<p>Orion looked up.</p>
<p>"So what are we making?"</p>
<p>West didn’t hesitate.</p>
<p>"Variety."</p>
<p>"Different types."</p>
<p>"Different styles."</p>
<p>"Something for everyone."</p>
<p>West wanted blades, spears, light armor and even specialized tools. He wasn’t thinking small...</p>
<p>Orion chuckled.</p>
<p>"...You’re building something serious."</p>
<p>West’s expression remained calm.</p>
<p>"I don’t do things halfway."</p>
<p>A brief silence passed between them before Orion extended a hand.</p>
<p>"Alright then."</p>
<p>"Let’s build your army."</p>
<p>West shook it firmly and just like that, the foundation of Shadow Reapers’ future arsenal... had begun.</p>
<p>...</p>
<p>...</p>
<p>The late afternoon sun cast a warm glow across the city as West stepped out of Divine Forge, leaving the grinding of machinery and faint scent of heated metal behind him.</p>
<p>His business with Orion Voss had gone smoothly and with more weapons in production and money steadily flowing in, things were beginning to align perfectly for what he had planned.</p>
<p>But there was one more stop to make before heading back home. West approached a standard supermarket by up ahead and smiled before stepping in.</p>
<p>...</p>
<p>...</p>
<p>About thirty minutes later, West glanced at the small shopping bag in his hand.</p>
<p>Inside were a few carefully chosen items which seemed simple and thoughtful enough ...</p>
<p>However, there was one particular addition... a flower.</p>
<p>He smirked faintly.</p>
<p>"...This should be interesting."</p>
<p>—</p>
<p>After another thirty minutes, he arrived at a quiet residential area which was well kept and clean. Apparently, this was different from the section that the Mafia owned.</p>
<p>West stepped out of the car and walked up the path toward a small bungalow house.</p>
<p>Before he could knock, the door opened, revealing an enthralling feminine figure... the realtor.</p>
<p>And just like the first time he met her, she looked breathtaking with long dark hair cascading over her shoulders and a few strands falling gently across her face.</p>
<p>Her corporate attire had been replaced with something more casual today, but it only made her look softer... and more approachable.</p>
