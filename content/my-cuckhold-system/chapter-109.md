<p>The other thief eyes widened in shock and terror as be pulled the trigger.</p>
<p>Bang!</p>
<p>The entire hall went up in gasps while the thief stared ahead in confusion.</p>
<p>"You missed..." a voice resounded behind him.</p>
<p>The moment the thief turned around, a fist was already shooting towards his face.</p>
<p>Bam!</p>
<p>West’s punch collided with the armed robber’s face causing a bone crushing sound to ring out as his entire being got lifted into the air and sent flying towards the other end of the hall.</p>
<p>It was unclear whether the thief was alive or dead as he lay in place unmoving with blood seeping through the mask.</p>
<p>Within minutes, all the masked thieves had been dealt with.</p>
<p>Only one man remained standing.</p>
<p>The unmasked leader.</p>
<p>He adjusted his cuffs calmly despite the carnage surrounding him.</p>
<p>"Well," he muttered, "that escalated."</p>
<p>One of the Verdant Pyre Behemoths charged straight for him.</p>
<p>At the last second, the air around him distorted as the eins along his neck darkened and his eyes emitted a crimson glow.</p>
<p>A surge of blood-like energy burst outward from his body.</p>
<p>The charging Behemoth was struck mid-charge and sent skidding backward with its flamimg green claws carving grooves into the marble as it regained balance.</p>
<p>Now West finally understood why he hadn’t worn a mask.</p>
<p>He wasn’t afraid of being identified because he was an awakened.</p>
<p>Specifically...</p>
<p>A bloodline awakened.</p>
<p>A dark aura surrounded him as crimson energy swirled across his arms.</p>
<p>"Did you really think," he said coldly, "I’d lead this operation without insurance?"</p>
<p>Being an awakened meant law enforcement treaded carefully. The police rarely intervened directly against awakened unless absolutely necessary and even at that, they couldn’t do much of anything.</p>
<p>The awakened were above the law.</p>
<p>Disputes between awakened often resolved themselves in blood long before paperwork got involved.</p>
<p>His lackeys were regular men who were expendable which was why they had to hide their identity but he was different. It wasn’t entirely uncommon to find an awakened leading a gang of normal unawakened criminals.</p>
<p>The only difference in this case was that they’d just be an unofficial gang, incomparable to even a street level awakened gang.</p>
<p>Gor’thala stepped forward with her staff glowing.</p>
<p>Seraphyra slithered closer from the opposite side with her lips curved.</p>
<p>"Cute," she said in her silky voice. "But how does that help you?"</p>
<p>The leader’s eyes darted briefly between the two monstrous summons.</p>
<p>He had watched them slaughter his men effortlessly.</p>
<p>Even with his awakened branch activated, he was outmatched.</p>
<p>In a bid of desperation, he lunged sideways suddenly and grabbed a young guest by the collar, yanking her upright and pressing a blood-infused blade of energy against her throat.</p>
<p>"Don’t move!" he barked. "One more step and she dies!"</p>
<p>The hall froze while the girl trembled violently.</p>
<p>West who was standing beside his father, turned to face the leader.</p>
<p>The leader smirked slightly.</p>
<p>"Back off," he warned.</p>
<p>West didn’t respond verbally.</p>
<p>Instead, he reached out with his hand and activated Verdant Bloom.</p>
<p>&lt;[ Verdant Bloom Activated ]&gt;</p>
<p>The marble floor beneath the leader cracked as Vines erupted upward from the ground like living serpents.</p>
<p>They wrapped around his legs, torso and arms, pinning him before he could react.</p>
<p>The hostage stumbled free and collapsed to the floor, scrambling away.</p>
<p>The leader roared in fury as his blood energy flared violently.</p>
<p>He tried to tear free but the vines only tightened.</p>
<p>Gor’thala lifted her staff high and ancient runes ignited along its length as she thrust it forward.</p>
<p>A torrent of verdant flame erupted and struck the bound awakened head-on.</p>
<p>The magic infused flames engulfed him instantly.</p>
<p>"Arrrrgggghhhhhhh!"</p>
<p>His agonizing scream tore through the hall.</p>
<p>The blood energy tried to free him but the vines held firm as the flames intensified.</p>
<p>Guests turned away, unable to watch while the smell of burning flesh filled the air.</p>
<p>His screams soon weakened before fading completely.</p>
<p>When the flames finally died down, only charred remains stood bound in blackened vines.</p>
<p>Then those too crumbled to ash.</p>
<p>Silence followed.</p>
<p>Seraphyra flicked ash from her nails while Gor’thala lowered her staff.</p>
<p>Guests slowly began to rise as whispers spread.</p>
<p>"That boy..."</p>
<p>"He is an awakened..."</p>
<p>"He saved us..."</p>
<p>The host of the party who was a wealthy middle-aged man in a torn tuxedo, hurried over.</p>
<p>"Are you alright? Do you need anything? I’ll call an ambulance—security—anything!"</p>
<p>Others gathered around West, offering their gratitude and praise.</p>
<p>West barely heard them.</p>
<p>His attention was on one person only.</p>
<p>He knelt beside Mark again.</p>
<p>"Dad. We’re going home."</p>
<p>He carefully lifted him.</p>
<p>With a subtle gesture, one Verdant Pyre Behemoth lowered itself slightly.</p>
<p>West placed Mark carefully atop the creature’s broad back.</p>
<p>The guests watched in stunned silence as the summoned beast moved toward the shattered rooftop opening.</p>
<p>Gor’thala leapt upward first.</p>
<p>Seraphyra followed in a silver blur.</p>
<p>West glanced back only once and then he ascended with them.</p>
<p>...</p>
<p>Meanwhile, on a skyscraper opposite the event center, a silhouette stood after watching the entire exchange.</p>
<p>"Very interesting... now I know why..." the voice of this figure echoed silently through the rooftop.</p>
<p>---</p>
<p>~ One Hour Later ~</p>
<p>Mark lay on the couch, still pale and weak.</p>
<p>West stood over him holding the chalice.</p>
<p>He tilted it gently and guided the glowing liquid toward his father’s lips.</p>
<p>"Drink."</p>
<p>Mark obeyed and immediately a small glow of light spread across his skin.</p>
<p>The bruises faded, cuts sealed and broken tissue knitted together within seconds—</p>
<p>Mark sat upright, breathing clearly.</p>
<p>He touched his face in disbelief.</p>
<p>"I... I feel fine."</p>
<p>He looked at his hands and observed other parts of his skin. All of his injuries were gone.</p>
<p>The chalice vanished quietly back into West’s inventory.</p>
<p>Mark stared at him for a long moment and then laughed weakly.</p>
<p>"I’m glad I called you."</p>
<p>His eyes softened.</p>
<p>"You’ve become a much more capable young man than I thought."</p>
<p>West didn’t smile but was glad to see his father was okay.</p>
<p>Mark reached forward and gripped his shoulder.</p>
<p>"I’m proud of you."</p>
<p>West finally let out a faintest exhale.</p>
<p>For the first time he had killed this night and he didn’t feel bad about it...</p>
<p>In a way, that bothered him.</p>
<p>He could tell, something inside him was changing. He was becoming less sympathetic, the stronger he grew...</p>
