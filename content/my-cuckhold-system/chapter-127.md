<p>One creature suddenly leapt high from a rock on West blind spot with both arms extending toward his face...</p>
<p>He twisted while still on a Verdant Pyre Behemoth and slammed an electrified elbow into its torso.</p>
<p>Electricity spread through its body, causing its fur to ignite slightly as it convulsed.</p>
<p>He finished it with a kick that crushed its head.</p>
<p>Unfortunately, West didn’t come out unscathed.</p>
<p>One limb brushed against his thigh while another grazed his ribs.</p>
<p>Frost injuries accumulated, slowing his movement slightly as patches of ice crept along his skin.</p>
<p>The Verdant Pyre Behemoths proved invaluable.</p>
<p>Their flames countered the frost-based attacks, and the roots erupting from their scorched footprints tripped several enemies, making them easier targets.</p>
<p>Eventually, the last of that cluster fell and the surrounding returned to tranquility.</p>
<p>West exhaled sharply, brushing ice shards from his arm.</p>
<p>"Annoying little things."</p>
<p>He surveyed the strange establishment ahead and realized that it wasn’t a single structure...</p>
<p>It was a network filled with holes.</p>
<p>Countless large openings dotted the stone walls with some being circular and others just rough and shapeless. Due to the size variations, some were wide enough for a Behemoth to squeeze through while others were narrower.</p>
<p>There was no clear main entrance, just too many options.</p>
<p>West frowned.</p>
<p>"Great. A maze inside a maze."</p>
<p>He had no way of knowing which path might lead to the captives.</p>
<p>So he chose the only logical approach, he started checking them one after the other.</p>
<p>The first hole led upward, spiraling back toward the surface region he’d already passed.</p>
<p>The second looped around and connected to another hole nearby...</p>
<p>The third descended deeper and as soon as he entered it, the path twisted sharply.</p>
<p>The Behemoths squeezed through with difficulty and at the end of this path, another group of the eight-eyed creatures waited.</p>
<p>Another battle broke out and this time, there were up to twenty in number.</p>
<p>West already confirmed at this point that this was some sort of hive for the native creatures of the third layer.</p>
<p>He pushed deeper, checking hole after hole.</p>
<p>Some led to a dead end, some looped while some descended. Each deeper route meant more resistance and battles broke out occasionally, leaving West with more minor injuries as time passed.</p>
<p>Eventually, he entered a passage that widened steadily.</p>
<p>The number of creatures increased to thirty along this path and were no longer roaming randomly.</p>
<p>They were stationed at different positions as though they were guarding the place</p>
<p>When over thirty charged at once, West wasted no time in pulling out an Implosive Disk.</p>
<p>"Three seconds," he muttered.</p>
<p>He activated it and threw it into the center of the charging swarm.</p>
<p>The disk hit the ground and the air warped violently as a localized gravitational implosion formed within a three-meter radius.</p>
<p>All the creatures were yanked inward toward a compressed focal point.</p>
<p>Their elongated limbs flailed uselessly as they were dragged together, unable to free themselves from the force that bound them to that specific position...</p>
<p>Having only three seconds of time, the Verdant Pyre Behemoths roared and slammed both flaming paws onto the ground simultaneously.</p>
<p>A massive pillar of greenish flame erupted from beneath the compressed mass, shooting upward like a volcanic blast.</p>
<p>The creatures screamed with a high-pitched and distorted tone.</p>
<p>When the gravitational pull released, charred bodies rained down from above, filling the passageway with smoke and the stench of burnt fur.</p>
<p>West stepped over the remains.</p>
<p>"Let’s finish this."</p>
<p>The tunnel ahead widened dramatically and soon he emerged into a massive chamber.</p>
<p>Immediately he got here, he knew that this was likely the location that mattered the most in the entire third layer...</p>
<p>To the right, a dark pool rippled faintly with opaque liquid that reflected no light.</p>
<p>In the center stood a throne-like structure carved from the same alien stone as the walls.</p>
<p>Strange pillars rose around the chamber, etched with unfamiliar blue glowing symbols.</p>
<p>At the far end stood an exalted circular platform that was at least four feet tall and the creatures were densely concentrated here...</p>
<p>There were dozens upon dozens scattered around here but they didn’t charge immediately...</p>
<p>They stood in clusters, watching.</p>
<p>West’s gaze shifted to the circular platform and the moment he realized what was on it... he froze...</p>
<p>There were body parts ranging from arms to legs to torsos and even decapitated heads.</p>
<p>Fresh blood glistened across the surface... like the ones atop were recently butchered...</p>
<p>West stepped forward slowly and finally, he saw the body parts clearer...</p>
<p>The decapitated heads... one belonged to his teacher with eyes still wide open... frozen in both shock and despair...</p>
<p>Another belonged to Emily... and another belonged to Calvin...</p>
<p>He spotted the guide’s close to the left ledge...</p>
<p>They were all decapitated and displayed on this platform like offerings.</p>
<p>The frost in the air suddenly felt suffocating as West’s expression changed.</p>
<p>The playful greed and detached composure vanished.... replaced by something darker.</p>
<p>The eight-eyed creatures began emitting a low synchronized screeh while the dark pool by the side rippled more violently.</p>
<p>West’s eyes moved frantically across the circular platform again... the teacher, the guide, the students...</p>
<p>He frowned when he noticed that the boy whose mother had begged him personally wasn’t there.</p>
<p>West scanned the severed heads again carefully, forcing himself to look past the blood and the frozen expressions of horror.</p>
<p>Truly, the boy’s face wasn’t among them.</p>
<p>A small, fragile sense of relief slipped into his chest.</p>
<p>Maybe...</p>
<p>Maybe at least one of them had survived.</p>
<p>His gaze shifted slowly toward the pool at the side of the chamber.</p>
<p>It churned unnaturally, thick and dark.</p>
<p>He squinted and ten his stomach tightened when he realized that it wasn’t water or some strange abyssal liquid.</p>
<p>It was blood...</p>
<p>A vast, churning pool of blood.</p>
<p>The metallic scent he had subconsciously ignored now slammed into him fully.</p>
<p>Above the pool, rusted and stained chains dangled from the cavern ceiling... and attached to those chains were body parts...</p>
<p>Arms, legs, torsos, heads with blood dripping steadily from them into the pool below.</p>
<p>Drip.</p>
<p>Drip.</p>
<p>Drip.</p>
<p>The sound echoed through the chamber like a metronome of cruelty.</p>
<p>West’s face contorted when he finally spotted the boy.</p>
<p>His head hung from one of the chains, slightly tilted with eyes half-open as if frozen in disbelief.</p>
<p>Blood still trailed from the severed neck...</p>
