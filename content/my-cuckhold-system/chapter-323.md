<p>Liz stared at him for several quiet seconds before laughing softly. "...Thank you."</p>
<p>"You don’t know how much I needed to hear that."</p>
<p>She looked toward the floor. "I’ve lost several major contracts over the past few months."</p>
<p>"Some clients made it clear they expected... sex."</p>
<p>"I refused."</p>
<p>"It’s been difficult."</p>
<p>West nodded quietly. "I know its hard but you made the right decision."</p>
<p>She smiled. "I hope so."</p>
<p>She reached for the doorknob and paused one final time. "If my life had been different... If I didn’t have a fiancée."</p>
<p>She smiled warmly. "...I think someone like you would’ve been very hard not to fall for."</p>
<p>West simply smiled back. "I’ll take that as a compliment."</p>
<p>"You should," she gave a small wave before leaving.</p>
<p>West watched the closed door for a few moments before leaning back in his chair.</p>
<p>A thoughtful smile crossed his face. "Seed planted..."</p>
<p>...</p>
<p>Beyond the Shadow Reapers headquarters...</p>
<p>Nearly a hundred feet above the surrounding streets...</p>
<p>A solitary feminine figure stood atop a neighboring rooftop.</p>
<p>The night breeze gently stirred her long hair.</p>
<p>From her vantage point, the brightly lit Shadow Reapers compound was clearly visible.</p>
<p>She quietly observed the celebration below with a faint smile on her face. "So..."</p>
<p>"...you’ve really grown since the last time I saw you."</p>
<p>The mysterious woman remained standing there, silently watching the headquarters below as the city lights glittered beneath the night sky.</p>
<p>...</p>
<p>...</p>
<p>Meanwhile, within the Shadow Reapers headquarters, the celebration gradually came to an end.</p>
<p>Hours earlier, the Shadow Reapers headquarters had been overflowing with laughter, music and influential guests from all across Misty High City.</p>
<p>Now... the grand hall looked like the aftermath of a festival.</p>
<p>Half-finished drinks rested atop tables, colorful decorations still hung from the ceiling and several chairs had been moved out of place after enthusiastic conversations and games.</p>
<p>Only the soft instrumental music that still drifted through the speakers remained to remind anyone that the place had, only a short while ago, been bursting with life.</p>
<p>The outsiders had all gone home.</p>
<p>The visiting gang leaders, businessmen, sponsors, representatives... pretty much all outsiders, had already left.</p>
<p>Only Shadow Reapers members remained.</p>
<p>Some of the recruits who had been drinking far beyond their limits had already begun stumbling toward the residential lodges inside the headquarters compound.</p>
<p>Others weren’t nearly as successful.</p>
<p>"...I’m telling you..." a new recruit pointed dramatically toward a decorative statue. "...that guy blinked."</p>
<p>Another recruit stared at the statue for several seconds. "...I think you’re right."</p>
<p>Jax walked over and smacked both of them lightly on the back of their heads.</p>
<p>"The statue didn’t blink."</p>
<p>"It doesn’t even have eyelids."</p>
<p>The first recruit frowned. "...Then explain why it’s judging me."</p>
<p>Jax sighed. "They’re completely gone."</p>
<p>Nearby... another member had somehow managed to fall asleep standing against a wall.</p>
<p>West couldn’t help laughing. "Jax."</p>
<p>"Hmm?"</p>
<p>"I’m promoting you."</p>
<p>Jax’s eyes lit up. "Really?"</p>
<p>"...To drunk babysitter."</p>
<p>His smile immediately disappeared. "Boss..."</p>
<p>"...that’s not a promotion."</p>
<p>"It is tonight," West patted him on the shoulder.</p>
<p>"Get everyone to their rooms."</p>
<p>Jax looked around, noticing over a dozen thoroughly intoxicated members.</p>
<p>"...I suddenly regret joining this gang."</p>
<p>West laughed. "You’ll survive."</p>
<p>Among the group sat Nina but unlike the others, she wasn’t causing trouble.</p>
<p>She simply sat on one of the sofas with her arms folded and her cheeks slightly flushed from the alcohol.</p>
<p>Her words were beginning to slur. "I’m..."</p>
<p>"...perfectly fine."</p>
<p>She then immediately leaned sideways.</p>
<p>Jax caught her before she slid onto the floor. "...Sure you are."</p>
<p>West smiled. "She isn’t going home tonight."</p>
<p>Jax nodded. "I figured."</p>
<p>West looked toward one of the female recruits. "Get one of the guest rooms ready."</p>
<p>"We’re not risking her wandering home in this state."</p>
<p>The recruit nodded. "Leave it to me."</p>
<p>One by one, the headquarters slowly quieted.</p>
<p>The music volume lowered... more lights were switched off and within another half hour, only a handful of members remained cleaning.</p>
<p>Satisfied that everything was under control, West finally stretched. "What a day..."</p>
<p>He quietly walked toward his office.</p>
<p>The corridors had grown noticeably darker now that most of the decorative lighting had been turned off.</p>
<p>Only the emergency lights remained, casting long shadows throughout the headquarters.</p>
<p>As West rounded a corner leading toward the lounge outside his office, he suddenly stopped... someone was here.</p>
<p>A feminine silhouette sat comfortably upon one of the sofas near the back of the room.</p>
<p>Most of the lights had already been turned off so she almost looked as though she had materialized directly from the darkness itself.</p>
<p>She smiled. "...Long time no see."</p>
<p>West’s eyes narrowed.</p>
<p>His vision had long surpassed that of ordinary awakeners so even within the dim lighting... he could clearly make out her features.</p>
<p>His relaxed expression disappeared immediately. "...You."</p>
<p>His voice became noticeably colder. "What are you doing here?"</p>
<p>The feminine figure slowly rose from the sofa with a confident smile that never faded. "I needed to see my man."</p>
<p>She began walking toward him with slow steps. "You’ve grown so much in seven months."</p>
<p>West didn’t move from his standpoint. "That’s close enough."</p>
<p>She ignored the warning and took another step.</p>
<p>West hand suddenly shot forward.</p>
<p>He seized her by the throat before shoving her backward several feet.</p>
<p>She slid across the smooth floors before effortlessly regaining her balance.</p>
<p>He spoke with a cold tone. "This isn’t going to be like the last time."</p>
<p>"The days of pinning me against a wall are over."</p>
<p>"Try anything..."</p>
<p>"...and I’ll rip your head off."</p>
<p>The feminine silhouette laughed almost affectionately. "You’ve gotten hotter too."</p>
<p>She raised both hands peacefully. "I come in peace."</p>
<p>Her crimson eyes slowly wandered across him.</p>
<p>"As much as I’d love to have you..." she smiled almost obsessively. "...body, mind and soul..."</p>
<p>"...I’m here for a different reason this time."</p>
<p>West remained guarded. "I’m listening."</p>
<p>"I came with a proposition."</p>
<p>West folded his arms. "Hm."</p>
<p>"Why would the mafia have a proposition for me?"</p>
<p>"I haven’t climbed high enough to end up on your radar yet."</p>
<p>She laughed softly. "Oh..."</p>
<p>"Don’t flatter yourself."</p>
<p>"It’s not the mafia."</p>
<p>She tapped her own chest. "It’s me."</p>
