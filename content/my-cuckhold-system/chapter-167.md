<p>West glanced at the wall before reaching up to his ear to touch the black earring on it.</p>
<p>A moment later, Marcus’s voice echoed softly in his mind.</p>
<p>"Can you hear me?"</p>
<p>West smirked slightly.</p>
<p>"Loud and clear."</p>
<p>On the other side, Marcus let out a small breath of relief.</p>
<p>"Good."</p>
<p>"We’ve been separated."</p>
<p>West leaned casually against a piece of broken stone.</p>
<p>"Yeah. I noticed."</p>
<p>Marcus continued.</p>
<p>"There are two sections left in this ruin."</p>
<p>"One to the left."</p>
<p>"One to the right."</p>
<p>West’s gaze shifted toward the path ahead.</p>
<p>"I see."</p>
<p>Marcus’s tone became serious.</p>
<p>"One of those sections is the core zone."</p>
<p>"The one that likely houses the strongest creature here."</p>
<p>"And the best loot."</p>
<p>He paused briefly.</p>
<p>"If you encounter that section..."</p>
<p>"Don’t engage."</p>
<p>"Wait for us."</p>
<p>West didn’t respond immediately while Marcus continued.</p>
<p>"You’re only four."</p>
<p>"You won’t be able to handle it alone."</p>
<p>"But if it’s the other section—"</p>
<p>"The standard one—then it’s fine to clear it."</p>
<p>"Problem is..."</p>
<p>"We don’t know which is which."</p>
<p>West pushed himself off the stone.</p>
<p>"...No problem."</p>
<p>"We’ll head forward."</p>
<p>Marcus hesitated.</p>
<p>"...Be careful."</p>
<p>West chuckled lightly.</p>
<p>"When am I not?"</p>
<p>The connection faded and just like that, both groups began moving again.</p>
<p>---</p>
<p>On Marcus’s side, the six of them moved cautiously through the newly formed corridor.</p>
<p>The atmosphere felt different and more tense now but that wasn’t what occupied their minds.</p>
<p>"...Did you guys see that?" Kyle finally said.</p>
<p>"No," Lina replied immediately.</p>
<p>"Because that wasn’t normal."</p>
<p>Mei wasn’t there to calm them this time so the group grew louder with confusion.</p>
<p>"That slab..." Eric muttered.</p>
<p>"It was huge."</p>
<p>"And he just—kicked it?"</p>
<p>Derek wasn’t here to add fuel, but Kyle took his place.</p>
<p>"There’s no way that was raw strength."</p>
<p>"Right?"</p>
<p>Lina frowned.</p>
<p>"...Did he summon something?"</p>
<p>"No," Eric shook his head.</p>
<p>"I didn’t see anything."</p>
<p>Kyle held his chin in contemplation.</p>
<p>"Then what the hell was that?"</p>
<p>"A summoner can’t be that strong."</p>
<p>"That doesn’t make sense."</p>
<p>The group fell into a heated discussion as theories flew around while Marcus walked ahead silently.</p>
<p>He shook his head slightly when he heard one of their theories. ’These kids...’</p>
<p>They truly had no idea.</p>
<p>---</p>
<p>Meanwhile on West’s side, silence reigned.</p>
<p>Derek, Tara and Mei were still staring at him, processing and trying to understand to understand what they had just witnessed minutes ago.</p>
<p>Finally, Derek spoke up.</p>
<p>"...How the hell did you do that?"</p>
<p>West didn’t answer.</p>
<p>"You’re a summoner," Derek continued.</p>
<p>"That kind of strength—"</p>
<p>West turned and for the first time since they entered the ruins, his demeanor shifted.</p>
<p>"Stay behind me."</p>
<p>He voiced with a calm but authoritarive tone.</p>
<p>All three of them paused.</p>
<p>"...What?" Derek frowned.</p>
<p>"It might get dangerous ahead," West added.</p>
<p>Tara blinked in submission... He was a summoner but something about his tone made it hard to argue.</p>
<p>Derek opened his mouth briefly before closing it.</p>
<p>"...Fine."</p>
<p>Tara hesitated for a moment before stepping forward slightly.</p>
<p>"...Hey."</p>
<p>West glanced at her while she looked away briefly before speaking.</p>
<p>"...Thanks."</p>
<p>Her voice was quieter than usual.</p>
<p>"For earlier."</p>
<p>West stared at her for a second before shrugging.</p>
<p>"Don’t mention it."</p>
<p>His tone was completely neutral and almost distant.</p>
<p>"I owe you one. I’ll make it up to you later."</p>
<p>"Do you have a boyfriend?" West question came out of the blue, taking her by surprise.</p>
<p>Tara didn’t know how to respond without stammering for a bit.</p>
<p>"Uunh yeah sorry I do. You can ask for anything else but not that. My boyfriend and I are two years in now."</p>
<p>She blushed a little while saying that. It was like her bad teenage attitude suddenly vanished.</p>
<p>"Good. I’m glad you have a boyfriend," West responded a tone of interest.</p>
<p>"Uh?" Tara was completely bamboozled.</p>
<p>Why would he be glad she had a boyfriend? Was he not asking to know if she was single so he could shoot his shot?</p>
<p>West suddenly turned to her and smirked. "Let’s focus on the task at hand first. We’ll come back to this later..."</p>
<p>She simply nodded still confused as the group continued forward.</p>
<p>---</p>
<p>They had barely walked twenty minutes when the environment changed again.</p>
<p>The corridor opened up into a massive new environment.</p>
<p>West stopped as his eyes narrowed slightly.</p>
<p>"...We’re here."</p>
<p>The others stepped forward behind him and what they saw, Immediately told them that this wasn’t a normal section.</p>
<p>A vast cavern stretched out before them with its ceiling so high it disappeared into darkness.</p>
<p>Strange green webs covered parts of the walls and structures, glowing faintly in the dim environment.</p>
<p>Massive stone pillars stood crookedly throughout the area. Some were broken while others were intact.</p>
<p>And in the center was a crystallized pillar.</p>
<p>Inside it, some strange dark looking object was trapped.</p>
<p>Derek swallowed.</p>
<p>"...This is it."</p>
<p>Tara nodded slowly.</p>
<p>"The core zone..."</p>
<p>Even without seeing the most powerful creature in the ruins, they could feel it.</p>
<p>The atmosphere here was oppressive and heavy like something powerful was watching them.</p>
<p>Derek stepped back slightly.</p>
<p>"We should wait."</p>
<p>"Marcus said—"</p>
<p>"I know what he said."</p>
<p>West stepped forward.</p>
<p>His gaze fixed on the cavern ahead.</p>
<p>"There’s something here."</p>
<p>Tara frowned.</p>
<p>"Obviously there is."</p>
<p>"That’s the problem."</p>
<p>West didn’t respond.</p>
<p>Instead, he started walking forward.</p>
<p>"Stay here."</p>
<p>The three of them froze.</p>
<p>"...What?"</p>
<p>West didn’t look back.</p>
<p>"I’ll check it out."</p>
<p>Derek stepped forward immediately.</p>
<p>"Are you insane?!"</p>
<p>"That’s the boss zone!"</p>
<p>West kept walking while Tara clenched her fists.</p>
<p>"...That’s suicide."</p>
<p>"Just stay here," West completely ignored them and walked forward alone.</p>
<p>The moment he crossed into the cavern, the atmosphere changed again.</p>
<p>The air grew warmer and each breath carried a faint, metallic taste, mixed with something damp and organic.</p>
<p>The faint glow that illuminated the outer cavern gave way to a more concentrated light source ahead.</p>
<p>West slowed his pace as his eyes sharpened when he entered the chamber.</p>
<p>However, this was not just any chamber... this was a hibernation chamber.</p>
<p>The walls curved inward in a semi-circular dome, towering high above. They emitted a sickly green glow, like veins of energy running through the surface.</p>
