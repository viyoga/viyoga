<p>West’s boots slammed against the ground as he thrust the massive blade forward with everything he had left from the Supreme Leader’s rear.</p>
<p>The attack came with terrifying force.</p>
<p>Even boosted, even empowered nearly threefold beyond his original strength, West still knew the being before him was not something that could be underestimated...</p>
<p>The blade tore through the air and at the last possible moment, the Supreme Leader turned around with its blueish fingers shooting forward like lightning.</p>
<p>They grabbed the sword just inches before it could plunge through its chest.</p>
<p>For a brief second, the entire chamber went still... then a deep, unnatural icy glow spread from the creature’s fingers, creeping across the metal of the massive sword.</p>
<p>Crystalline frost rapidly spread along the blade.</p>
<p>West’s eyes narrowed but he wasn’t surprised... he already knew what this creature was capable of.</p>
<p>However, in the next instant th dark mist constantly leaking from the giant weapon suddenly reacted violently...</p>
<p>It hissed, churned and then the frost began to rot away like acid eating through fragile glass.</p>
<p>The creeping frost cracked apart, dissolving into blackened vapor.</p>
<p>For the first time, the Supreme Leader showed shock.</p>
<p>West took this milliseconds shock as an opportunity and he pushed.</p>
<p>"RAAAAAAH!"</p>
<p>His muscles expanded ripping through his uniform as the sheer strength behind the push caused both of them to explode across the chamber like a launched missile.</p>
<p>Stone shattered beneath their feet and pillars splintered as dozens of the surrounding creatures were sent flying while the two streaked across the room in a blur.</p>
<p>BOOOOOM!</p>
<p>They smashed into the far wall and the impact cracked the entire chamber.</p>
<p>Before the creature could react, West forced the blade forward with a savage roar.</p>
<p>The massive sword plunged through the Supreme Leader’s shoulder and embedded itself deep into the wall behind it, pinning the creature in place.</p>
<p>A horrific, distorted shriek erupted from its throat.</p>
<p>The sound echoed through the chamber like metal tearing apart and every creature in the room reacted immediately, vibrating as if they felt the pain as well.</p>
<p>Without hesitation, West’s fist came down.</p>
<p>BOOM!</p>
<p>The punch smashed into the creature’s abdomen causing the wall to shake.</p>
<p>BOOM!</p>
<p>He sent out another strike and cracks spread across the rough and uneven wall behind.</p>
<p>BOOM!</p>
<p>He struck again...</p>
<p>Each punch was like a detonating cannon causing shockwaves to ripple through the chamber.</p>
<p>The Supreme Leader’s body jerked violently with every hit but the surrounding creatures were already moving.</p>
<p>They dove forward in dozens with their tentacles whipping through the air as they descended upon West.</p>
<p>Even though Gor’thala, the ten Verdant Pyre Behemoths, and Jax fought desperately to keep them back, there were simply too many.</p>
<p>Several slipped past the defensive line and in the next moment, they arrived before West.</p>
<p>Tentacles tipped with clawed hooks lashed toward West from every direction.</p>
<p>"Tch,"</p>
<p>West clicked his tongue in annoyance as he yanked one of the writhing tentacles out of the air and spun.</p>
<p>The creature attached to the tentacle was instantly pulled from its initial position as West rotated with speed.</p>
<p>The creature became a living weapon as he swung it around like a helicopter blade.</p>
<p>WHAM! WHAM! WHAM!</p>
<p>Its body smashed into the others around him and the sounds of bones shattering and heads bursting apart rang out.</p>
<p>Several creatures were flung across the chamber like ragdolls and soon the creature he held was nothing more than a bloody mess, missing almost every body part.</p>
<p>West let go and then suddenly leapt into the air.</p>
<p>He traveled nearly thirty feet across the air and grabbed one of the creatures by the head.</p>
<p>Instantly, he spiked it downward.</p>
<p>BOOOOM!</p>
<p>The impact crushed it against a nearby pillar and its skull exploded apart, showering the floor in fragments.</p>
<p>Two tentacles managed to strike him from behind.</p>
<p>SLAM!</p>
<p>The hits landed against his back and West staggered a step as more tentacles lashed forward.</p>
<p>Nearly sixty attacks were raining towards him at once.</p>
<p>Fwwhhiiiii~</p>
<p>West body blurred as he twisted between strikes, ducking, swerving and pivot to evade.</p>
<p>His fists became weapons of devastation as he swung them powerfully.</p>
<p>CRACK!</p>
<p>One creature’s head collapsed inward.</p>
<p>RIP!</p>
<p>Another was torn in half.</p>
<p>SMASH!</p>
<p>A third was sent flying across the room.</p>
<p>His movements were inhumanly fast.</p>
<p>In mere seconds the creatures that had broken through were reduced to mangled corpses and twitching limbs scattered across the ground.</p>
<p>West exhaled slowly as he turned.</p>
<p>He instantly froze in place upon noticing that the wall was empty.</p>
<p>The Supreme Leader was gone.</p>
<p>The massive sword had fallen to the floor with a metallic clang and dark blood pooled beneath it.</p>
<p>West’s head snapped in the direction of the throne and he spotted the Supreme Leader sprinting.</p>
<p>Its wounded body dashed toward the pool of blood beside the throne with chains dangling body parts above it.</p>
<p>West’s eyes narrowed upon realizing what this meant..</p>
<p>"Not happening..."</p>
<p>He bent down and grabbed the massive sword.</p>
<p>In the past, he could barely lift it with one hand but now it felt weightless...</p>
<p>West launched forward causing the floor to crack beneath his feet as he accelerated.</p>
<p>Fwwwhoooommmm!</p>
<p>Anything in his path died as the giant blade slashed again and again.</p>
<p>Creatures were sliced apart...</p>
<p>Bodies fell in halves...</p>
<p>Limbs scattered across the floor as he closed the distance rapidly.</p>
<p>The Supreme Leader was one step away from reaching the blood pool when West swung.</p>
<p>A massive arc of black energy erupted from the swing, cutting through the air with terrifying power.</p>
<p>The Supreme Leader turned upon sensing the danger but it was too late.</p>
<p>BOOOOM!</p>
<p>The arc slammed into it and its body was blasted off the ground as it flew backward like a cannonball.</p>
<p>The supreme leader smashed straight through the far wall and stone exploded outward.</p>
<p>The creature vanished into a newly formed tunnel beyond the chamber.</p>
<p>West didn’t pause, he jumped straight forward through the hole, arriving inside the tunnel—</p>
<p>The Supreme Leader finally stopped retreating as its blueish fingers elongated once more.</p>
<p>They stretched like spears, striking at West.</p>
<p>West ducked the first strike, swerved to evade another which slashed past his face and parried the third with the massive sword.</p>
<p>The fingers collided with the blade and frost instantly formed across the metal, only to rot away again under the dark mist.</p>
<p>The Supreme Leader hissed and attacked relentlessly like a mad wounded dog that had been forced against the wall.</p>
<p>Its fingers lashed through the narrow tunnel like serpents.</p>
<p>West dodged and countered as their battle turned into a blur.</p>
<p>Rocks shattered and the tunnel walls cracked as chunks of rock collapsed while their fight pushed deeper into the passage.</p>
<p>Despite its injuries, the Supreme Leader was still terrifying.</p>
<p>West knew one thing clearly... if those fingers touched him, the frost would cripple him again.</p>
<p>So he stayed just outside their reach, making sure that the fingers never made contact with his skin.</p>
<p>Meanwhile, back in the chamber, Gor’thala stood before Jax with her staff in her hands.</p>
<p>She planted it firmly against the ground and a burst of fiery energy erupted outward.</p>
<p>Spell after spell surged from her position, blasting apart the approaching creatures.</p>
<p>Jax fought beside her using his Glacial Sovereign bloodline.</p>
<p>Frost storms burst from his hands sending shots of ice spears through the air.</p>
<p>Creatures were punctured and froze solid before shattering apart.</p>
<p>Nearby, the Knight faced the Butcher.</p>
<p>The monstrous creature swung violently, smashed down its axe like weapon again and again.</p>
<p>Each strike released waves of freezing power causing frost to spread across the Knight’s armor but it did nothing.</p>
<p>The frost simply cracked apart and fell away while the Knight staggered back by half a feet each time, watching silently.</p>
<p>After a few exchanges, they nodded slightly.</p>
<p>"Battle pattern recorded successfully..."</p>
<p>They spoke with a calm and strange voice beneath the helm.</p>
<p>"...neutralising target."</p>
<p>The saber in their hand suddenly erupted with a brilliant sunlike glow and in the next instant, a streak of blinding light shot forward...</p>
<p>And then another...</p>
<p>And then another...</p>
<p>The Knight moved around the Butcher at impossible speed causing lines of glowing to motion flash across the battlefield.</p>
<p>Suddenly, the Knight returned to their original position and calmly sheathed the saber silently.</p>
<p>A second later, the Butcher’s body fell apart as dozens of clean slices appeared across its form...</p>
<p>Its entire body exploded into chunks, collapsing into diced flesh across the ground.</p>
<p>Jax stared in disbelief from a distance.</p>
<p>"...yeah."</p>
<p>He swallowed.</p>
<p>"I’m really glad they’re on our side."</p>
<p>The Knight simply turned toward the remaining creatures and joined the fight.</p>
<p>Together, they began wiping out the remaining swarm.</p>
<p>Meanwhile inside the tunnel, the Supreme Leader suddenly stopped retreating.</p>
<p>Its body trembled as power began to radiate from it.</p>
<p>West immediately felt it as the creature’s aura began to climb.</p>
<p>Something dangerous was happening like the being was about to unleash something and this reminded West of the butcher using that charge which raised its power level...</p>
<p>Only that in this case, the Supreme Leader body features were writhing and transforming...</p>
<p>West’s eyes sharpened instantly realizing as he raised his hand.</p>
<p>"Verdant Bloom."</p>
<p>&lt;[ Verdant Bloom Activated ]&gt;</p>
<p>Green light erupted beneath the Supreme Leader’s feet as very thick vines burst from the stone floor.</p>
<p>They wrapped around the Supreme Leader instantly, each being twice as wide as a full grown adult’s torso.</p>
<p>The bindings were far stronger than usual thanks to West’s boosted state...</p>
<p>The creature struggled but the vines held it in place for just a moment... and that moment was enough.</p>
<p>West body turned into a streak as he shot forward with unprecedented speed with his blade extended.</p>
<p>The tunnel echoed with the sound of a blade cutting through flesh and in the next instant, silence returned.</p>
<p>West stood still with blood dripping slowly onto the rough tunnel floor.</p>
<p>In his hand, he held the severed head of the Supreme Leader.</p>
