<p>West stared at the dark, reflective pool at the far end of the chamber that he had noticed earlier but ignored.</p>
<p>Its surface rippled and then, a massive reptilian head rose slowly from the water.</p>
<p>Yellow glowing eyes opened causing West’s heart to skip.</p>
<p>"...Uh oh."</p>
<p>The creature pulled itself free of the pool with water cascading off its body in heavy sheets.</p>
<p>It stood upright, towering over West and completely bathing his entire figure in a massive shadow.</p>
<p>The creature was at least thirteen feet tall with its physique carved from dense, corded muscle. Its skin was dark emerald and black, layered with thick scales.</p>
<p>A long tail dragged behind it and along the tail were multiple eyes.</p>
<p>Small, glowing yellow eyes that blinked independently.</p>
<p>West’s stomach dropped.</p>
<p>The creature inhaled deeply and spoke.</p>
<p>"Exu vaue rao masu litu..."</p>
<p>Its voice echoed in strange, layered tongues as the words folded over each other like overlapping whispers.</p>
<p>West raised both hands instinctively.</p>
<p>"Okay, listen," he said quickly while backing away a step. "I don’t want any trouble. I’ll just—uh—leave."</p>
<p>The system made a soft chime.</p>
<p>&gt; [ Item Stored in Inventory: Chalice of Vitality ]</p>
<p>The moment the chalice vanished from his hands, the creature’s eyes narrowed.</p>
<p>Slowly, deliberately, it extended one clawed hand.</p>
<p>Reality rippled.</p>
<p>From thin air, a black sword emerged.</p>
<p>It was long and spiky along the edges. Dark mist coiled around its blade like smoke with intent.</p>
<p>West’s slowly stepped back with a cautious look on his face.</p>
<p>"Of course you have a sword," he muttered.</p>
<p>The creature lifted the blade effortlessly.</p>
<p>Then swung.</p>
<p>A dark arc tore through the air.</p>
<p>West immediately dove sideways.</p>
<p>The arc slammed into the far wall and carved a three-inch-deep gash straight through solid ruin stone.</p>
<p>Dust exploded and cracks spiderwebbed outward.</p>
<p>West rolled to his feet with widened eyes.</p>
<p>"...Yeah. That’s why gangs exist."</p>
<p>This thing wasn’t meant to be fought solo.</p>
<p>Not at Tier 1.</p>
<p>Not by someone without proper combat training or awakened techniques.</p>
<p>The creature stepped forward, dragging the sword against the ground.</p>
<p>Sparks flew.</p>
<p>Spikes of black energy erupted with each scrape.</p>
<p>West turned around and bolted.</p>
<p>He sprinted toward the chamber entrance, weaving around the rough stony ground as another dark arc slashed past him, missing his head by inches.</p>
<p>His heart pounded as he increased his speed. The only thought running through his mind at this moment was to create distance between himself and this creature.</p>
<p>The moment he closed in on the entrance of the chamber, a massive stone slab descended from above with a thunderous crash.</p>
<p>BOOM.</p>
<p>Dust erupted as it slammed down, sealing the exit completely.</p>
<p>West skidded to a stop. "That’s not good..."</p>
<p>Slowly, he turned, watching the creature advance.</p>
<p>Its sword scraped the ground again as it stepped forward slowly like West fate was already determined.</p>
<p>Spikes burst upward.</p>
<p>West clenched his fists.</p>
<p>"...Looks like I don’t have a choice."</p>
<p>The moment he steeled himself, the world sharpened.</p>
<p>His awakened body responded instantly as he shot forward at astonishing speed.</p>
<p>His muscles coiled as his feet struck the ground with explosive force, pushing him multiple feet forward with every step.</p>
<p>The creature swung again but West leapt.</p>
<p>Fwhooomm~</p>
<p>His body ascended over the arc of darkness as he flipped forward midair, and drove a punch straight toward the creature’s face.</p>
<p>His fist connected and the impact sent a shock through his arm but the creature’s head snapped back slightly.</p>
<p>It staggered backward by half a step.</p>
<p>West didn’t hesitate.</p>
<p>He pressed the advantage.</p>
<p>He darted in, landing another punch—then a kick—then another strike to the ribs. His movements were instinctive and with the help of his new speed and strength, achieving this sort of precision was easy.</p>
<p>’This is... amazing...’ West never considered himself to be a violent person but he was enjoying every moment of this.</p>
<p>Seeing him body capable of things he never knew would ever be possible for him.</p>
<p>He ducked under a counter-swing, rolled, and slammed his elbow into the creature’s knee joint.</p>
<p>The reptilian guardian snarled but then it adapted.</p>
<p>Its left arm moved.</p>
<p>One casual swing came flying at West and he didn’t even see it properly.</p>
<p>Bam!</p>
<p>Pain exploded through his body as he got launched across the chamber like a ragdoll.</p>
<p>He slammed into the wall, causing stone to crack beneath the impact.</p>
<p>Air burst from his lungs as his vision swam.</p>
<p>He slid down the wall, coughing as blood trickled from the corner of his mouth.</p>
<p>The creature stepped towards him, pulling his sword forward again.</p>
<p>West wiped his mouth with the back of his hand and pushed himself up slowly.</p>
<p>"...Okay," he muttered. "So you hit harder..."</p>
<p>West didn’t dare let it hit him again.</p>
<p>That single blow earlier had rattled his bones, shaken his organs, and left his vision swimming for precious seconds. He knew it in his gut now, with a certainty that bordered on terror.</p>
<p>If that sword touched him even once... it would be over.</p>
<p>The thought sharpened his focus.</p>
<p>West moved.</p>
<p>He didn’t think in terms of techniques or styles. He thought in terms of survival. His awakened body responded instinctively as his muscles coiled.</p>
<p>The guardian’s sword cleaved through the air again causing a crescent of darkness to shriek past where his head had been a heartbeat earlier.</p>
<p>West slid low with his boots scraping stone, then sprang back up and drove his fist into the creature’s ribs.</p>
<p>The impact reverberated up his arm.</p>
<p>The creature’s body was dense, like punching reinforced steel wrapped in muscle.</p>
<p>Nonetheless, West punch packed enough force that the creature subconsciously let out a grunt and staggered half a step backwards.</p>
<p>West didn’t waste a single second.</p>
<p>He darted sideways, narrowly avoiding a backhand swing, then pivoted and slammed his elbow into the creature’s side.</p>
<p>Then followed up with another hit... and then a kick to the thigh... and another punch to the abdomen.</p>
<p>He would hit, evade..</p>
<p>Then, hit again and evade continously.</p>
<p>The guardian was powerful... overwhelmingly so... but it was also slower.</p>
<p>It wasn’t incredibly slow... it was just slower than him.</p>
<p>And that difference was everything.</p>
