<p>The battle had already turned brutal, but now it escalated into something far more desperate.</p>
<p>The fourteen-foot Butcher moved with terrifying speed despite its massive size, advancing toward him, completely unfazed by the damage it had taken so far.</p>
<p>West rolled to the side just as the monster’s fist smashed into the ground where he had been standing.</p>
<p>BOOM!</p>
<p>The impact shattered stone and sent shards flying in all directions.</p>
<p>West sprang up and immediately countered, driving a heavy punch into the creature’s side.</p>
<p>THUD!</p>
<p>The impact forced the creature to slide back slightly but that was all....</p>
<p>There was no visible injury.</p>
<p>West clenched his teeth. "Damn it..."</p>
<p>Against most creatures, his raw strength alone would have been overwhelming but not this one.</p>
<p>The Butcher snarled and lunged forward again.</p>
<p>West quickly activated Volt Manipulation, electricity erupting around his arm in crackling blue arcs.</p>
<p>The lightning wrapped around his fist like a living serpent as he drove it straight into the creature’s chest.</p>
<p>CRACK!</p>
<p>Electricity exploded outward across the Butcher’s body.</p>
<p>For a moment the chamber lit up in blinding blue light but when the sparks faded, the creature was still standing there, completely unaffected.</p>
<p>A deep rumbling sound escaped its throat before its entire body suddenly began to glow with a violent charged aura.</p>
<p>West’s eyes widened.</p>
<p>"Oh shit—"</p>
<p>The creature leapt high into the air and swung its massive fist downward.</p>
<p>The moment the fist struck the ground—</p>
<p>BOOOOOM!!!</p>
<p>A terrifying wave of force exploded outward like a detonating bomb.</p>
<p>The shockwave tore through the chamber.</p>
<p>West was blasted backwards through the air along with the two Verdant Pyre Behemoths battling the smaller four-foot creatures.</p>
<p>The entire cavern trembled.</p>
<p>West slammed violently against the rough stone near the upper entrance tunnel before crashing back down onto the ground.</p>
<p>Pain exploded through his body and before he could even inhale properly—</p>
<p>THUD! THUD! THUD!</p>
<p>The Butcher was already charging again.</p>
<p>West barely had time to lift his head before the massive creature reached him.</p>
<p>A huge hand shot forward and grabbed his hair.</p>
<p>"Ghk—!"</p>
<p>With a brutal yank, the creature flung him across the chamber like a ragdoll.</p>
<p>The world spun violently as West’s body flew across the air.</p>
<p>West was still mid-air when one of the four-foot creatures suddenly stretched one of its limbs.</p>
<p>The arm elongated unnaturally like elastic and swung downward toward West like a giant whip.</p>
<p>But before it could hit, a massive flaming figure shot upward.</p>
<p>One of the Verdant Pyre Behemoths leapt into the air and caught West.</p>
<p>The elongated limb smashed into the beast’s back instead.</p>
<p>The Behemoth growled but held West tightly before landing heavily on the ground.</p>
<p>West slid out of its grasp, coughing violently with blood dripping from the corner of his mouth.</p>
<p>His vision blurred slightly.</p>
<p>Across the chamber, the fourteen-foot Butcher was already charging toward them yet again.</p>
<p>The Verdant Pyre Behemoth roared and unleashed a wave of greenish flames.</p>
<p>The fire swept forward like a raging tide but the Butcher didn’t slow down.</p>
<p>It burst straight through the flames with the green fire exploding around its body yet doing nothing to halt its momentum.</p>
<p>A second later—</p>
<p>BAM!</p>
<p>Its leg slammed into the Behemoth’s torso.</p>
<p>The force of the kick sent both the beast and West flying across the chamber again.</p>
<p>West rolled violently across the ground while the Behemoth crashed against the stone wall.</p>
<p>Frost rapidly spread across the creature’s body from the point of impact.</p>
<p>It growled in agony as icy crystals crawled across its flaming hide while the Butcher was already charging at them again.</p>
<p>It didn’t give its prey even a second to breathe...</p>
<p>Just as it reached striking distance, Vines suddenly burst from the cavern floor.</p>
<p>Dozens of thick roots wrapped around the creature’s legs and torso causing the butcher to stumble as its movement halted for just a second.</p>
<p>Within that one second of breathing space, West forced himself to his feet.</p>
<p>His chest rose and fell rapidly as he reached into his inventory and a heavy object appeared in his grasp.</p>
<p>It was a massive black blade nearly as tall as he was with dark mist slowly seeping from its surface...</p>
<p>The Guardian Sword...</p>
<p>The weight was still monumental but his current level of strength made it much easier to lift compared to the past times.</p>
<p>Without hesitation, West swung.</p>
<p>Swwhiiii~</p>
<p>The enormous blade sliced forward in a brutal horizontal arc but the Butcher reacted instantly...</p>
<p>With a violent roar it tore the vines apart and shifted its body at the last moment.</p>
<p>Instead of cutting through its torso as originally planned...</p>
<p>SHHHK!</p>
<p>The blade carved straight through its left arm.</p>
<p>The limb separated completely and dropped to the ground with a heavy thud.</p>
<p>The Butcher let out a thunderous scream as dark blood sprayed across the stone while it clutched the stump of its shoulder.</p>
<p>West exhaled sharply.</p>
<p>Finally... something that could cut through that steel of a body...</p>
<p>He tightened his grip on the sword and charged before swing again and again.</p>
<p>Each strike carried terrifying power capable of cleaving through anything but the sword was enormous and heavy...</p>
<p>Although, West was much stronger now and could swing faster than before, there was still a bit of a delay everytime he did...</p>
<p>The Butcher quickly adapted, moving with surprising agility...</p>
<p>West’s attacks sliced through empty air again and again.</p>
<p>The creature twisted and ducked around the blade as its glowing eyes burnt with rage.</p>
<p>Then suddenly, it vanished from West’s front, appearing behind him.</p>
<p>Its remaining fist shot forward like a cannon.</p>
<p>West reacted swiftly by spinning around and rraising the Guardian Sword to block.</p>
<p>CLANG!!!</p>
<p>The moment the fist made contact, a tremendous force blasted through West’s arms sending the heavy sword flyimg straight out of his grasp.</p>
<p>It slammed into the cavern floor dozens of meters away and slid across it for a few more.</p>
<p>West staggered back as the Butcher attacked again.</p>
<p>He swerved sideways evading but the single arm hammered down relentlessly, swinging at him again and again.</p>
<p>However, now that it only had one arm, its attack patterns were easier to read.</p>
<p>West began evading more effectively, making the attacks harder to hit but unfortunately, he was starting to get exhausted due to his injuries.</p>
<p>While looking for the perfect opportunity to launch a counter attack, West suddenly noticed that the creature began glowing again.</p>
<p>He expression sank upon realizing what this meant...</p>
<p>The charged aura intensified around the monster’s body as it prepared to unleash that devastating shockwave attack once more.</p>
<p>West immediately tried to create distance but the creature didn’t give him space.</p>
<p>It pursued him relentlessly and in this charged state, its speed had drastically increased.</p>
<p>Dodging was nearly impossible.</p>
<p>West could feel death closing in.</p>
<p>Even if the fist didn’t land directly on him, the shockwave alone would cause incredibly damage.</p>
<p>Inside his mind he desperately reached out.</p>
<p>’Gor’thala... Seraphyra... hurry...’</p>
<p>Unfortunately, even though he had been sending signals to them all this time, the third layer was massive...</p>
<p>There was no way they would arrive in time...</p>
<p>The Butcher charged and threw its glowing fist at West.</p>
<p>West reacted by jumping back but the creature followed him vehemently, not leaving any room between them.</p>
<p>West’s eyes widened upon realizing that he wouldn’t be able to avoid this attack... to make matters worse, he had taken the one time redirect pendant off hours earlier...</p>
<p>Fwwwhiii~</p>
<p>The charged blow increased in his field of vision and was about to land when a massive flaming body suddenly leapt in front of him.</p>
<p>The other Verdant Pyre Behemoth placed itself between West and the incoming punch as it landed.</p>
<p>BOOOOOOM!!!</p>
<p>The shockwave detonated and a violent force erupted across the chamber.</p>
<p>West was blasted backward along with the Behemoth as the creature’s fist punched straight through the beast’s chest.</p>
<p>A massive hole now existed where its core should have been and frost rapidly spread across its entire body.</p>
<p>The Behemoth let out one final weak growl before freezing completely and turning into a frost statue.</p>
<p>CRACK.</p>
<p>Its frozen body shattered into pieces snd the fragments dissolved into glowing particles before disappearing entirely.</p>
<p>West slammed against the cavern floor and his vision nearly went black but he was still alive.</p>
<p>The Behemoth had absorbed most of the impact.</p>
<p>Across the chamber, the Butcher’s charged aura slowly faded as its speed returned to normal.</p>
<p>It began charging toward West again but suddenly, green flames erupted in its path.</p>
<p>The remaining Verdant Pyre Behemoth unleashed a barrage of fiery blasts straight at the creature’s face.</p>
<p>Flash after flash exploded across its vision.</p>
<p>The monster roared as the flames temporarily blinded it.</p>
<p>The fiery barrage continued for several seconds before suddenly coming to a stop.</p>
<p>The Butcher blinked through the fading flames and noticed West was gone.</p>
<p>In confusion, the creature turned around and the moment it did, it froze.</p>
<p>West stood behind it with the Sword back in his hands and dark mist flowing around the blade.</p>
<p>Without hesitation, he swung and a massive vertical slash descended with unstoppable force.</p>
<p>From the blade erupted a dark crescent of energy and before the creature could react—</p>
<p>SHRAAAAAK!</p>
<p>The arc tore through its body.</p>
<p>For a moment, it seemed as though nothing happened.</p>
<p>But a second later, the Butcher noticed its vision was tilting.</p>
<p>One section of its eyes saw the cavern floor while the other saw the ceiling.</p>
<p>Its hands instinctively tried to hold its face together but it couldn’t.</p>
<p>A thin black line slowly spread from the top of its head... all the way down its body... ending between its thighs.</p>
<p>All of a sudden, its entire body split apart and the two halves collapsed to the ground with a thunderous crash.</p>
<p>The Butcher had been completely cleaved in two...</p>
<p>West let out a sigh of relief as he dropped onto one knee while using the massive sword as support.</p>
<p>As his chest heaved up and down in heavy breaths, he suddenly noticed something...</p>
<p>Up ahead... the throne was empty...</p>
