<p>Bloodstain had turned West’s surrounding into a perfect kill zone.</p>
<p>The attacks closed in with West standing in the middle.</p>
<p>Everything suddenly slowed as West’s Voidfang Predator heightened senses activated fully.</p>
<p>His transformed eyes glowed brighter as his brain processed distances, angles, timing, velocity simultaneously.</p>
<p>Each attack trajectory and possible path was perfectly calculated as the world before him entered hyper focus.</p>
<p>He suddenly activated a skill.</p>
<p>&lt;[ Radiant Acceleration Activated ]&gt;</p>
<p>Golden light exploded from his body causing a sun-like glow to surround him.</p>
<p>Bloodstain’s eyes widened as West moved at a speed so ridiculous it looked like teleportation.</p>
<p>He swerved around the first spike, twisted around the second, ducked beneath a flying axe, stepped around a descending hammer and leapt between two blood blades.</p>
<p>Not a single attack managed to touch him.</p>
<p>Bloodstain struggled to follow his movement even with Tier Two perception.</p>
<p>West became a golden blur racing through the battlefield as he suddenly soared over Bloodstain’s head.</p>
<p>The veteran immediately spun around but he wasn’t fast enough.</p>
<p>West had landed behind him with his leg swinging out.</p>
<p>CRAAAAASH!</p>
<p>The kick connected from the side turning Bloodstain’s body into a projectile.</p>
<p>BOOOOM!</p>
<p>He smashed into one ice wall, ricocheted off and shot across the battlefield before crashing into the opposite wall.</p>
<p>BOOOOM!</p>
<p>He bounced across it again from one end to the other like a ball being thrown between giants.</p>
<p>The entire frozen enclosure shook violently as cracks spread across them.</p>
<p>West suddenly appeared before him while he was richocheting towards the next surface, grabbed his head and leapt.</p>
<p>The veteran barely had time to react before West drove him downward.</p>
<p>BOOOOOOOOOOM!</p>
<p>The impact expanded the crater again causing the earth below to collapse.</p>
<p>Cracks spread everywhere as West landed on top of him with a grin on his monstrous face.</p>
<p>"Your protective gear has a limit doesn’t it?"</p>
<p>Bloodstain’s expression changed as he realized that West had figured it out.</p>
<p>That expensive awakened armor had been reducing the force behind every strike but the glow that would usually flicker anytime Bloodstain got hit had reduced by a whole lot.</p>
<p>Without waiting for his answer, West’s fists descended upon him.</p>
<p>BOOM!</p>
<p>A dimmer glow flickered as a loud booming sound reverberated across the surrounding but before it could even settle, another strike descended.</p>
<p>BOOM!</p>
<p>The glow appeared again but slightly dimmer as more strikes descended</p>
<p>BOOM! BOOM! BOOM! BOOM! BOOM!</p>
<p>The barrage continued repeatedly, deepening the crater, causing the ground to shatter even more.</p>
<p>Every impact forced Bloodstain further downward and yet West hadn’t even given him any real damage.</p>
<p>But his repeated barrage was targeting the armor and Bloodstain realized it too late. Unfortunately, he couldn’t even bring it to a stop because every powerful strike, kept him down.</p>
<p>The glow continued appearing, weaker each them.</p>
<p>The armor was undoubtedly reaching its limit.</p>
<p>BOOM!</p>
<p>BOOM!</p>
<p>BOOOM!</p>
<p>BOOOOM!</p>
<p>The impacts continued until finally... the protective glow appeared one last time, barely visible before vanishing completely.</p>
<p>Bloodstain’s eyes widened as West’s next punch descended fiercely toward Bloodstain’s face.</p>
<p>BOOOOOOOOM!</p>
<p>The impact detonated across the enclosed battlefield.</p>
<p>The ground beneath them shattered further as cracks spread outward like a spiderweb across the crater floor.</p>
<p>Bloodstain’s head snapped violently to the side with blood bursting out of his ear, nose and mouth.</p>
<p>For the first time since the battle began, there was no flicker.</p>
<p>The awakened expensive armor absorbing the force had finally reached its limit.</p>
<p>The gang leader’s expression darkened as he gritted his teeth in pain.</p>
<p>West grinned. "There it is."</p>
<p>His glowing eyes narrowed. "You’ve been relying on that thing a lot."</p>
<p>Bloodstain spat blood to the side before smiling. "Good."</p>
<p>West frowned slightly. "Good?"</p>
<p>Bloodstain suddenly slapped both hands onto the ground and nlood-red spikes exploded upward from every direction.</p>
<p>West instantly leapt backward as several spikes narrowly missed him.</p>
<p>Others punched through the space where he had been standing a split second earlier.</p>
<p>Bloodstain rose from the crater with his clothing was in terrible condition.</p>
<p>His face was bruised, cuts covered parts of his body, yet the pressure radiating from him had somehow become even more dangerous.</p>
<p>The entire enclosed battlefield began trembling.</p>
<p>The blood scattered across the battlefield, outside of the ice enclosure started moving.</p>
<p>Tiny droplets, crimson stains and even areas with patches of blood from fallen comrades... every trace of blood drifted toward Bloodstain.</p>
<p>BOOM!</p>
<p>It blasted through a section of the ice wall, burrowing through to reach him.</p>
<p>However, the ice wall was closed back instantly by Jax on the outside, preventing anyone from managing to get an inside view.</p>
<p>Bloodstain spread his arms and the floating blood gathered around him before condensing into over thirty large scythes.</p>
<p>Dozens of crimson weapons floated behind him, resembleing an arsenal, like a floating execution ground.</p>
<p>West whistled. "Trying to compensate for losing your armor?"</p>
<p>Bloodstain laughed. "You talk too much."</p>
<p>The moment every weapon was launched simultaneously, West’s heightened senses immediately kicked in.</p>
<p>The world slowed as he tracked the incoming attacks.</p>
<p>Fwwwhooommmm~</p>
<p>West twisted, evading a blade before ducking to avoiding spear passed overhead.</p>
<p>A hammer-shaped construct shattered the ground beside him as he suddenly swerved sideways.</p>
<p>Over twenty more attacks were still incoming, approaching from every angle.</p>
<p>Unfortunately, even though his perception was absurd, Radiant Acceleration was still under cooldown so avoiding them all with his normal speed was going to be an insurmountable challenge.</p>
<p>Swwwhiihhh~ Fwwhwiii~ Swerveee~ Sweerrvee~</p>
<p>While he was in the process of evading, Bloodstain suddenly appeared while sweeping the massive blood scythe in his grasp horizontally.</p>
<p>West immediately crossed both arms but the collision launched him backward.</p>
<p>BOOOOOOM!</p>
<p>His feet tore trenches through the ground before he stabilized himself but a large bleeding gash had appeared across his arms.</p>
<p>Bloodstain immediately followed up with no intention of allowing breathing room.</p>
<p>West rushed forward as Bloodstain swung again, ducking beneath the attack.</p>
<p>A punch shot toward Bloodstain’s ribs but the gang leader blocked with the scythe, sliding backward immediately after impact was made.</p>
<p>The ground cracked beneath his feet as West followed with a succession of strikes, swinging his fist multiple times.</p>
<p>Each strike landed with explosive force but Bloodstain defended most of them, except for the last two which struck his chest and face, sending him crashing backwards.</p>
<p>Bloodstain flipped once across the air and then suddenly landed with a powerful stomp.</p>
<p>The ground beneath West exploded as a massive blood spike erupted upward.</p>
<p>West jumped away but another spike followed and soon the battlefield transformed into a deadly maze.</p>
<p>Bloodstain used the opportunity to make his floating arsenal attack again.</p>
<p>West found himself weaving between blood weapons and spikes simultaneously.</p>
<p>Any Tier 1 would have been long skewered but West’s agility was off the charts.</p>
<p>"Stop moving!" Bloodstain exploded, charging forward again while unleashing overwhelming power.</p>
<p>A giant blood scythe expanded, growing larger until it exceeded twenty feet.</p>
<p>He proceeded to swing and the entire battlefield split.</p>
<p>Thrrriiiiiiihhhhhh!</p>
<p>A crimson crescent tore across the enclosure causing West’s eyes to widen slightly.</p>
<p>&lt;[ Radiant Acceleration Activated ]&gt;</p>
<p>Golden light exploded around him as he swerved towards the left as fast as he could, causing the crimson crescent to miss him by inches.</p>
<p>BOOOOOOOOM!</p>
<p>It struck the ice wall instead, instantly obliterating a portion of it.</p>
<p>However, even though West had evaded the attack, the residual force from the attack still sent him flying, crashing into the other side of the ice wall.</p>
<p>Outside the enclosure, both gangs jumped as the pressure from the clash leaked through.</p>
<p>"What the hell are they doing in there?" One Shadow Reapers recruit asked nervously.</p>
<p>Jax simply folded his arms while unleashing a fresh ice wall to cover the hole. "No clue."</p>
<p>Meanwhile, Bloodstain appeared before West who had blasted into the east ice wall and sent a fist towards his belly.</p>
<p>BOOM!</p>
<p>It sounded as though a bomb had detonated as he continued to repeatedly swing his fist.</p>
<p>BOOM! BAM! BAM! BOOM!</p>
<p>West who was on the receiving end of these repeated barrage suddenly opened his mouth wide.</p>
<p>Bloodstain felt the hair on his body rise as his senses alerted him of danger but he wasn’t fast enough.</p>
<p>A blueish white beam suddenly shot out of West’s mouth, slamming into Bloodstain and sending him flying.</p>
<p>Bloodstain crashed into the ground and rolled repeatedly, forming a deep trench as blood gushed out of a massive burn mark on his chest.</p>
<p>West suddenly reappeared above Bloodstain before he could recover with a descending kick.</p>
<p>Bloodstain crossed his arms to block but the collision still drove him into the ground.</p>
<p>Before he could blink, a punch was already headed for his face.</p>
<p>BOOM!</p>
<p>Bloodstain saw stars as the punch landed and before he could even process it, another slammed into his chest and the next onto his jaw.</p>
<p>A barrage of fists rained down upon him, barely giving him the chance to evade or block.</p>
<p>Before Bloodstain knew it, there were bloodstains all over his tattered clothing... and these bloodstains, didn’t belong to his enemy... they belonged to him.</p>
<p>The absence of his armor became painfully obvious.</p>
<p>West’s attacks were like the force of a moving mountain.</p>
