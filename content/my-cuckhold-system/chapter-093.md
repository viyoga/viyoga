<p>Far from schoolyards and industrial ruins, a sprawling estate sat behind massive black gates carved with dragon motifs.</p>
<p>Stone pillars towered at the entrance, each wrapped in sculpted serpents. The driveway curved through manicured gardens, past fountains shaped like coiled wyrms spewing arcs of water into ornamental ponds.</p>
<p>The main building resembled a fusion of modern luxury and ancient fortress. It was made of glass, steel, and carved charcoal stone blending into a structure that radiated authority.</p>
<p>This was the headquarters of the Dragon Fang Gang.</p>
<p>And today—</p>
<p>The front courtyard was packed.</p>
<p>A long line of young awakeners stretched down the driveway with some in school uniforms, others in casual clothes. All of them looked nervous and hopeful.</p>
<p>Whispers filled the air.</p>
<p>"I heard they only take fifteen awakened this cycle and only one gets to be her apprentice..."</p>
<p>"Did you see the evaluation arena? It’s insane."</p>
<p>"If Miss Zu Li notices you, you’re basically set for life."</p>
<p>Suddenly, the side doors of a secondary building opened.</p>
<p>Silence rippled down the line as Zu Li stepped out.</p>
<p>Her black and crimson attire with a slit that reached her left thigh, flowed elegantly as she walked. Her presence was sharp yet refined, like a blade hidden within silk.</p>
<p>Behind her followed several high-ranking gang members. Among them was a well-dressed man in a tailored charcoal suit and hands clasped behind his back. West would recognize him if they ever crossed paths again.</p>
<p>The line erupted in hushed gasps.</p>
<p>"It’s Miss Zu Li."</p>
<p>"She looks even better in person."</p>
<p>"Oh my God..."</p>
<p>"She’s terrifyingly beautiful."</p>
<p>Zu Li didn’t acknowledge the gushing.</p>
<p>She walked forward calmly with jer heels clicking against stone in rhythmic successions.</p>
<p>Suddenly, a young man from midway down the line broke formation.</p>
<p>"Miss Zu Li!"</p>
<p>Gasps erupted as he sprinted forward.</p>
<p>Gang members moved instantly to block him, but he ducked past one and slid to a stop a few meters from her.</p>
<p>"Make me your apprentice!" he shouted. "I promise I’m talented enough!"</p>
<p>He puffed out his chest.</p>
<p>"I’ve trained for the past year! I’ve studied dragon resonance theory! I have a very active branch!"</p>
<p>The surrounding gang members moved to shove him back but Zu Li raised a hand.</p>
<p>They stopped as the courtyard went silent.</p>
<p>She tilted her head slightly.</p>
<p>"Show me."</p>
<p>The line collectively gasped.</p>
<p>"That worked?!"</p>
<p>Even the overzealous applicant blinked.</p>
<p>"...Really?"</p>
<p>Zu Li’s expression didn’t change.</p>
<p>"Show me."</p>
<p>The young man’s face hardened as he closed his eyes and activated his branch.</p>
<p>A wave of energy rippled outward as his body began transforming.</p>
<p>His arms thickened with claws extending from fingertips. Scales formed across his shoulders as a draconic tail snapped out behind him. His jaw elongated slightly with fangs protruding upwards.</p>
<p>The crowd murmured in awe.</p>
<p>"Partial dragon morph."</p>
<p>"Not bad..."</p>
<p>The young man roared and inhaled deeply.</p>
<p>Green flames erupted from his mouth in a wide arc, scorching the courtyard’s stone tiles ahead.</p>
<p>The heat forced several people in line to stumble backward in panic.</p>
<p>The flames crackled and spread briefly before dying out.</p>
<p>Smoke lingered.</p>
<p>The applicant panted slightly, looking proud.</p>
<p>Zu Li’s expression remained utterly unchanged.</p>
<p>"...You’ll have to do better than that."</p>
<p>His smile faltered.</p>
<p>He growled and activated another another layer of transformation.</p>
<p>His scales darkened... and his muscles expanded further as he shot forward, swiping his claws in rapid succession at a nearby stone pillar.</p>
<p>Chunks of carved dragon sculpture shattered and fell.</p>
<p>He turned back toward her but still she looked completely unimpressed.</p>
<p>Zu Li blinked once.</p>
<p>"Disappointing. You’re nothing compared to him."</p>
<p>Her words cut deeper than any blade causing his face to flush red in embarrassment. But only Zu Li knew the ’him’ she was referring to.</p>
<p>"Then what would impress you?!" The applicant questioned in desperation.</p>
<p>Zu Li’s gaze sharpened slightly.</p>
<p>"Come at me."</p>
<p>The entire courtyard went still.</p>
<p>"If you can so much as lay a finger on me," she continued calmly, "I will personally make you my apprentice."</p>
<p>The line erupted in shocked murmurs while the young man’s eyes gleamed.</p>
<p>"You’re really powerful, Miss Zu Li... but laying just a finger on you? That’s not mission impossible."</p>
<p>He smirked before charging at her.</p>
<p>Swwhiiiii~</p>
<p>His claws sliced through the air as he dashed forward at full speed.</p>
<p>For a split second— it genuinely looked like he might reach her.</p>
<p>All of a sudden, a sharp crack echoed across the courtyard.</p>
<p>Zu Li’s arm moved so fast it blurred, connecting a heavy slap with the side of his face.</p>
<p>Bam!</p>
<p>The sound was brutal, followed by the sound of clattering teeth.</p>
<p>His body twisted midair as he spiraled violently, smashing through the outer wall of one building...</p>
<p>Bang!</p>
<p>He completely broke through it and then slammed through the inner wall of a second building.</p>
<p>Dust and debris exploded outward and a drawn silence reigned across the surroundings.</p>
<p>A distant groan could be heard from somewhere inside the wreckage.</p>
<p>Zu Li shook her hand once, as if brushing off dust.</p>
<p>"Next."</p>
<p>She voiced with a cold and utterly unmoved tone.</p>
<p>The line collectively stiffened.</p>
<p>"Fuck..."</p>
<p>"That’s what you get for jumping the line..."</p>
<p>"He got folded..."</p>
<p>No one else stepped out.</p>
<p>Zu Li continued walking and her entourage followed immediately.</p>
<p>As they moved toward the main building, she spoke without looking back.</p>
<p>"Report."</p>
<p>The suited man beside her adjusted his glasses slightly.</p>
<p>"We have not located West’s current residence."</p>
<p>Her eyes narrowed faintly.</p>
<p>"But?"</p>
<p>"We confirmed the coordinates of his old neighborhood. Reconstruction underway."</p>
<p>She continued walking.</p>
<p>"And?"</p>
<p>"We have identified his father’s workplace."</p>
<p>She stopped for a moment and her gaze shifted toward him.</p>
<p>"Details."</p>
<p>The man in the suit handed her a tablet.</p>
<p>"Employment records confirmed this morning."</p>
<p>A faint smile touched her lips.</p>
<p>"Good."</p>
<p>Behind her, the broken applicant was being carried away on a stretcher.</p>
<p>Zu Li’s voice remained calm.</p>
<p>"Although he is not a minor... I believe every father has a say in their kid’s life. So long as we are able to convince him that Dragon Fang is the perfect choice for his son... I want him found before the other gangs escalate."</p>
<p>"Yes, Miss Zu Li."</p>
