<p>Shadow Reapers technically only had sixty-seven combatants present including West, his three summons, the members currently outside the base and the massive summoned army.</p>
<p>Iron Vultures had over a hundred awakened present, yet somehow... it felt like Shadow Reapers were the larger force.</p>
<p>Everywhere one looked, there were towering monsters, colossal beasts and multitude of Verdant Pyre Behemoths.</p>
<p>The battlefield looked completely overcrowded with Shadow Reapers forces.</p>
<p>A transformation user from Iron Vultures leapt into the air while his body enlarged into a muscular beast nearly ten feet tall.</p>
<p>He roared. "I’ll crush these thi—"</p>
<p>Before he could complete a sentence, one of the giants swung.</p>
<p>BOOOOOOOOM!</p>
<p>The enormous tower in its grasp came down like divine force, flattening everything in its path.</p>
<p>The transformation user disappeared beneath the attack as three vehicles behind exploded, concrete shattered and the road caved inward.</p>
<p>Nearly ten Iron Vultures members were sent flying through the air.</p>
<p>Some crashed through nearby walls, others bounced across the pavement while a few simply disappeared beneath rubble.</p>
<p>The giant slowly lifted its tower again, and a crater was visible underneath.</p>
<p>Several Iron Vultures members stared in horror. "What the hell is that thing?!"</p>
<p>Another giant attacked, sweeping its towet horizontally this time, obliterating everything in its path.</p>
<p>BOOOOOM!</p>
<p>Barriers shattered and defensive abilities collapsed as awakened members scrambled desperately to avoid being turned into paste.</p>
<p>The giants were demolishing the surroundings but that wasn’t Iron Vultures only problem.</p>
<p>The Verdant Pyre Behemoths shot at very quick pace.</p>
<p>One slammed both flaming paws into the ground and Immediately...</p>
<p>BOOOOM!</p>
<p>A pillar of green fire erupted beneath an Iron Vultures member.</p>
<p>The man screamed as he was launched thirty feet into the air.</p>
<p>Before he even landed, three more pillars exploded beneath nearby awakened.</p>
<p>Unlike ordinary fire, the green flames carried magical force.</p>
<p>They stuck to barriers, burned through armor and continuously spread across the battlefield.</p>
<p>Another Behemoth swiped its claws and five awakened leapt away, trying to evade but one got caught.</p>
<p>The claws carved directly through his defensive equipment before launching him across the battlefield.</p>
<p>Meanwhile more pillars of emerald flames continued erupting.</p>
<p>The battlefield looked like a forest of green infernos.</p>
<p>Iron Vultures immediately tried adapting.</p>
<p>"Focus them!"</p>
<p>"Bring them down one at a time!"</p>
<p>"Stop spreading out!"</p>
<p>Veteran members began barking orders.</p>
<p>The Iron Vultures members immediately formed groups of fours, five and even six in some cases.</p>
<p>Each group concentrated their attacks onto a single Verdant Pyre Behemoth, raining down, fire, ice, lightning, kinetic blasts and explosions...</p>
<p>One particular Behemoth became the focus of five awakened at once as electricity rammed into its side while another Iron Vulture member repeatedly slammed blue glowing bolts into its chest.</p>
<p>An earth manipulator followed up causing stone spikes to erupt beneath it and then came a transformation awakened who attacked from the rear and then a fire user...</p>
<p>The attacks struck continuously as a gravitational manipulator held it down in place.</p>
<p>The creature roared while enduring the bombardment as it fur cracked and chunks of its body exploded away.</p>
<p>The Behemoth finally collapsed after nearly thirty seconds of relentless assault while the five awakened breathed heavily with sweat covering their bodies.</p>
<p>Before they could even recover, another Verdant Pyre Behemoth had charged them.</p>
<p>"Oh come on!" One of them nearly cried.</p>
<p>The beast crashed into their formation like a runaway truck, scattering the entire group</p>
<p>One was sent tumbling through the air, another disappeared beneath its claws while a third got caught by an eruption of green flames.</p>
<p>Everywhere on the battlefield similar scenes played out.</p>
<p>The Verdant Pyre Behemoths were absurdly durable.</p>
<p>They weren’t opponents that could be defeated by one or two awakened.</p>
<p>It took coordinated attacks, relentless pressure and multiple abilities working together to take a single one down... And even then casualties still occurred.</p>
<p>The more the Iron Vultures members fought, the more horrified they became.</p>
<p>Because every time they managed to destroy one summon, three others seemed to appear nearby.</p>
<p>Another giant crushed the east section of battlefield, allowing the elite beasts continued advancing.</p>
<p>Bloodstain quickly realized that if the giants were allowed to continue rampaging unchecked, the battle would be over before it truly began.</p>
<p>The colossal tower-bearers were simply too destructive.</p>
<p>Every swing flattened portions of the battlefield... every step crushed vehicles, walls and defensive positions...</p>
<p>One giant alone was occupying nearly twenty Iron Vultures members while four others were still wreaking havoc elsewhere.</p>
<p>Bloodstain’s eyes narrowed as he ripped another Verdant Pyre Behemoth apart with his bare hands and charged forward.</p>
<p>The ground exploded beneath his feet as crimson aura erupted around him while blood-red energy gathered into his hands.</p>
<p>The energy rapidly stretched outward and in the next instant, a giant scythe materialized.</p>
<p>Its blade was nearly twenty feet long with dark red veins spreading across its surface.</p>
<p>A single swing from Bloodstain, separated a giant enormous arm.</p>
<p>SLAAAAASH!</p>
<p>The severed limb spun through the air before crashing into the battlefield below.</p>
<p>Iron Vultures members immediately erupted into cheers.</p>
<p>"Boss!"</p>
<p>"That’s our leader!"</p>
<p>"Take it down!"</p>
<p>The giant staggered but before it could recover, Bloodstain’s right-hand man made his move.</p>
<p>His body suddenly shrank, becoming smaller and smaller, until he was barely larger than a mouse.</p>
<p>He vanished from sight and then reappeared atop the giant’s remaining arm.</p>
<p>The giant attempted to swat him but the man phased through the attack entirely by becoming even smaller.</p>
<p>His body slipped through tiny cracks in the giant’s bark-like exterior as he entered the giant.</p>
<p>A few seconds later...</p>
<p>BOOOOOOOOM!</p>
<p>The giant’s chest exploded outward.</p>
<p>Wooden armor, stone flesh and magical organs erupted into the air.</p>
<p>The right-hand man emerged from the opening and immediately began growing.</p>
<p>His body expanded larger and larger and due to that expanding force, everything around him was ripping apart...</p>
<p>In this case... the giants innards.</p>
<p>The giant staggered as Iron Vultures members unleashed their strongest attacks simultaneously.</p>
<p>BOOOMM!</p>
<p>BOOOMM!</p>
<p>BOOOMM!</p>
<p>BOOOMM!</p>
<p>The barrage hammered the giant relentlessly before the massive creature finally collapsed.</p>
<p>Its enormous body shook the battlefield upon impact causing several nearby awakened stumbled from the force.</p>
<p>Cheers erupted again but the celebration didn’t last long because there were still four giants.</p>
<p>To make matters worse, those weren’t even the biggest problem anymore...</p>
<p>The true nightmare were the five elite beasts.</p>
<p>The crystal-antlered wolf, leapt over an entire battlefield section in a single bound, barely touching the ground with its paws.</p>
<p>An Iron Vultures member tried to intercept it but the wolf simply passed by him.</p>
<p>In the next instant, his body suddenly split apart.</p>
<p>The awakened was dead before he even understood what had happened.</p>
<p>The wolf’s claws were that extremely fast and super sharp.</p>
<p>Every movement left glowing emerald trails hanging in the air.</p>
<p>The six-legged obsidian panther was even worse.</p>
<p>It practically teleported across the battlefield, whipping its thorn-covered tendrils around like living spears.</p>
<p>One tendril pierced through a defensive barrier while another wrapped around an awakened’s waist.</p>
<p>The poor man barely had time to scream before being hurled into the path of a giant’s descending tower.</p>
<p>The impact painted the surrounding pavement red.</p>
<p>The enormous forest-backed tortoise was a moving catastrophe.</p>
<p>Iron Vultures members continuously attacked it but nothing worked.</p>
<p>Fire abilities, energy blasts, kinetic attacks and everything they could muster struck its shell but nothing accomplished anything significant.</p>
<p>The creature simply kept walking and every step caused gigantic roots to erupt from the earth.</p>
<p>The roots burst upward beneath awakened, wrapped around limbs, crushed bones, dragged victims into the ground and even ripped some apart, limb by limb.</p>
<p>Entire groups found themselves trapped before other summons arrived to finish the job.</p>
<p>Above the battlefield, the leaf-winged predator ruled the skies.</p>
<p>Every flap of its sixty feet wide wings, eleased thousands of glowing razor leaves that cut through armor, sliced skin and shredded barriers.</p>
<p>Iron Vultures ranged attackers found themselves completely suppressed.</p>
<p>The battlefield had become a nightmare.</p>
<p>The Iron Vultures summoners finally unleashed their summons.</p>
<p>Massive magical circles erupted throughout the battlefield.</p>
<p>One creature emerged resembling a gigantic rhinoceros covered entirely in black iron plating.</p>
<p>Another looked like a skeletal dragon with glowing blue fire burning inside its ribcage.</p>
<p>A third was an eight-armed gorilla standing nearly twenty feet tall.</p>
<p>There was a three-headed crimson hound, a giant scorpion with crystal stingers, a floating eyeball creature covered in hundreds of smaller eyes, a serpent made entirely from bones, a humanoid construct formed from living steel, a</p>
<p>giant armored mantis and a monstrous bat whose wings generated sonic shockwaves.</p>
<p>Ten summons immediately rushed Gor’thala and Aurethia.</p>
<p>The summoners knew exactly who the greatest threats were. Once Gor’thala was dealt with, her summons would definitely vanished...</p>
<p>The battlefield around them exploded as the iron rhinoceros charged first.</p>
<p>The creature lowered its head and accelerated, causing the ground to crack in its path.</p>
<p>Gor’thala smiled while transforming her staff into a sword.</p>
<p>CLANG!</p>
<p>The collision generated a shockwave, throwing nearby fighters off their feet.</p>
<p>The rhinoceros roared while Gor’thala slid backward several feet.</p>
<p>For the first time in the battle she looked slightly challenged.</p>
