<p>Debra shoulders trembled slightly as she lowered her head.</p>
<p>West sighed tiredly. "Leave."</p>
<p>The single word felt cold.</p>
<p>"I handled your situation. Dragon Fang won’t come after you. Frankincense is too busy fighting for survival to chase you anymore."</p>
<p>He stepped backward toward the apartment again.</p>
<p>"Your life is no longer in danger. There’s no reason for you to still be here."</p>
<p>But as soon as he moved to enter again, Debra grabbed his hand once more.</p>
<p>This time West’s irritation visibly surfaced. "You’re really starting to get on my nerves."</p>
<p>Debra looked up at him with watery eyes. "...Did you ever stop to think about how I knew you could help me?"</p>
<p>West paused with a slightly thoughtful expression.</p>
<p>The question piqued his interest because truly those texts asking for protection felt like she already knew he was awakened.</p>
<p>But how could she when she hadn’t been in his life for years.</p>
<p>West frowned slightly while Debra slowly wiped her tears.</p>
<p>"I knew because even though I stayed away..." her voice softened painfully, "...I still kept tabs on you."</p>
<p>West immediately rolled his eyes.</p>
<p>"Oh wow," he muttered dryly. "Am I supposed to be touched by that?"</p>
<p>"No."</p>
<p>Debra shook her head weakly.</p>
<p>"I’m not asking for pity."</p>
<p>West turned again, clearly done with the conversation but Debra stepped forward hurriedly.</p>
<p>"Please."</p>
<p>Her voice cracked badly this time.</p>
<p>"Please just hear me out. Just this once."</p>
<p>West stopped for a moment while Debra continued before he could interrupt.</p>
<p>"If after hearing everything... you still decide you never want anything to do with me again..." tears streamed down her face again, "...then I’ll leave and never bother you again."</p>
<p>West exhaled slowly with a look of fatigue... he was tired and needed rest but she clearly wasn’t going anywhere.</p>
<p>And maybe... a part of him wanted answers too... even if he hated himself for it.</p>
<p>He finally stepped aside from the doorway with a hesitant expression.</p>
<p>"...Fine."</p>
<p>Debra looked up slightly.</p>
<p>"But after this," West said coldly, "we’re done."</p>
<p>She nodded immediately. "Okay."</p>
<p>West let her inside the luxurious apartment and the beautiful city skyline immediately came into view beyond the massive windows while early sunlight illuminated the living room in soft gold.</p>
<p>Debra stepped inside slowly like she didn’t belong there while West tossed his jacket aside before sitting heavily on the couch.</p>
<p>"...Talk."</p>
<p>Debra sat opposite him with her fingers intertwined nervously.</p>
<p>For several long seconds, she couldn’t speak.</p>
<p>But finally, she summoned up the courage.</p>
<p>"...You weren’t an only child."</p>
<p>West eyebrows instantly furrowed in confusion and disbelief. "...What?"</p>
<p>Debra looked down at her hands.</p>
<p>"My first child... was a girl."</p>
<p>"She was four years old... almost five... when you were born."</p>
<p>"I had a sister?" West eyes widened slightly, completely confounded.</p>
<p>Debra nodded weakly. "You were still a baby then. That’s why you don’t remember her."</p>
<p>West leaned forward slightly now as the irritation on his face had been completely replaced by confusion.</p>
<p>"...Then where is she?"</p>
<p>The moment he asked, Debra broke... fresh tears poured down her cheeks instantly.</p>
<p>West’s chest tightened slightly without understanding why.</p>
<p>"She died."</p>
<p>West stared at her blankly. "...What?"</p>
<p>"She was killed."</p>
<p>Debra’s voice shattered.</p>
<p>"...By an awakener."</p>
<p>The apartment suddenly felt unbearably quiet as West sat completely frozen.</p>
<p>Because for the first time since this entire conversation began, his face was starting to display a look of anger.</p>
<p>"...No."</p>
<p>Debra nodded while crying openly now as West slowly stood to his feet.</p>
<p>"How?" he asked quietly.</p>
<p>"How did I never know this?"</p>
<p>Debra hugged herself tightly. "Because I made Mark promise never to tell you."</p>
<p>West mind spun violently as he stared at her.</p>
<p>He had a sister... and his father never said a word. ’How could he have hidden this from me?’</p>
<p>Debra continued shakily. "She died because of an awakener incident... a blunder during a territorial conflict."</p>
<p>Her breathing became uneven. "They were fighting in the city."</p>
<p>West remained completely still, listening to Debra recall the incident.</p>
<p>"She was just... there."</p>
<p>Debra covered her mouth while sobbing now.</p>
<p>"They said it wasn’t intentional. They said collateral damage happens during awakened battles..."</p>
<p>Her voice turned bitter. "But how do you tell a mother her daughter was collateral damage?"</p>
<p>West looked away slightly as something twisted uncomfortably inside him.</p>
<p>Debra continued crying softly. "She died in my arms."</p>
<p>"I still remember how cold she was becoming..."</p>
<p>The room fell silent again except for Debra’s quiet sobs while West slowly sat back down.</p>
<p>His expression had become distant now as various thoughts surfaced in his head.</p>
<p>"She had your eyes," Debra whispered.</p>
<p>"She used to hold your tiny hands after you were born. She adored you."</p>
<p>West swallowed slightly. "...Stop."</p>
<p>Debra shook her head weakly. "I need you to understand."</p>
<p>She wiped her face again. "After she died... I completely broke."</p>
<p>West remained silent as Debra continued.</p>
<p>"We mourned her for months... Years, honestly."</p>
<p>Her voice sounded hollow now. "I couldn’t function anymore."</p>
<p>She laughed weakly through tears.</p>
<p>"I’d hear her voice sometimes. I’d think I saw her running through the house."</p>
<p>West lowered his gaze slightly.</p>
<p>"We had pictures of her everywhere at first..." Debra whispered. "But eventually I couldn’t handle seeing them anymore."</p>
<p>Her breathing trembled again.</p>
<p>"So I made Mark throw everything away."</p>
<p>"Every picture..."</p>
<p>"Every video..."</p>
<p>"Every toy..."</p>
<p>"Every trace..."</p>
<p>West suddenly understood why there had always been a strange emptiness in old family albums growing up.</p>
<p>He never questioned it before but it made sense now.</p>
<p>Debra continued quietly. "I made him promise never to tell you."</p>
<p>"Why?"</p>
<p>"Because I wanted to protect you from that grief."</p>
<p>West laughed bitterly.</p>
<p>"So instead I just grow up not knowing I had a sister."</p>
<p>Debra lowered her head again.</p>
<p>"I know... and it was also the only way I could leave her completely buried because I needed to be a mother to you."</p>
<p>Silence reigned for a while before Debra spoke again.</p>
<p>"For a while... things got better."</p>
<p>Her expression softened faintly. "You were little. Funny. Loud. Always running around the house."</p>
<p>"You made life feel normal again."</p>
<p>A painful smile appeared on her face."But eventually..."</p>
<p>The smile disappeared. "The fear came back."</p>
<p>West noticed how haunted Debra’s eyes looked.</p>
<p>"I became terrified every single day."</p>
<p>Her voice lowered. "Terrified that awakeners would take you from me too."</p>
<p>"I started having episodes again," she admitted quietly. "Panic attacks. Hallucinations. Depression."</p>
<p>Her hands trembled visibly now. "I stopped sleeping properly."</p>
<p>She laughed weakly.</p>
<p>"I’d check your room five times every night just to make sure you were still breathing."</p>
<p>West’s expression changed slightly.</p>
<p>"The marriage started falling apart because of it."</p>
<p>Debra closed her eyes. "I wasn’t me anymore."</p>
<p>The room felt painfully heavy now.</p>
<p>"And then..."</p>
<p>She looked back at him.</p>
<p>"The assimilation research project appeared."</p>
<p>West’s gaze sharpened slightly while Debra nodded slowly.</p>
<p>"They wanted to create something that could allow ordinary humans to stand against awakeners."</p>
<p>Her eyes hardened slightly for the first time.</p>
<p>"And to me..." She smiled bitterly. "It sounded perfect."</p>
<p>"Somewhere to channel my grief."</p>
<p>"...And somewhere to create something strong enough to protect my son."</p>
<p>Silence swallowed the room whole afterward... and for the first time since she arrived... West truly didn’t know what to say anymore.</p>
<p>...</p>
<p>...</p>
<p>~ Hours Later ~</p>
<p>The apartment had become painfully quiet after Debra left.</p>
<p>West still sat on the same couch in the same position with the same expression.</p>
<p>The untouched breakfast she brought rested on the table nearby, probably cold now just like the coffee sitting beside it.</p>
<p>The city lights outside the massive windows had slowly transitioned from warm sunrise colors into deep blue evening tones, but West barely noticed.</p>
<p>His thoughts were elsewhere...</p>
<p>’I had a sister...’ the realization kept replaying in his head over and over again like an endless loop.</p>
<p>A sister who died because of an awakener...</p>
<p>West leaned back deeper into the couch while rubbing both hands down his face slowly.</p>
<p>"...What the hell..."</p>
<p>For years he thought the story was simple... that his mother abandoned them and his father stayed... end of story.</p>
<p>That was easier to process... it made it easier to hate her... But now, everything felt complicated.</p>
<p>Because as much as he hated what Debra did... he couldn’t completely ignore what drove her there anymore.</p>
<p>The image of her crying while talking about her daughter lingered stubbornly inside his head.</p>
<p>~ "She died in my arms..." ~</p>
<p>West shut his eyes tightly... annoyed... because part of him didn’t want to empathize.</p>
<p>It was easier being angry... it was easier to blame her but now he knew things he was never supposed to know.</p>
<p>And somehow... this made it difficult to feel the earlier resentment.</p>
<p>Brrtzz~</p>
<p>His phone suddenly buzzed.</p>
<p>West glanced toward it lazily, as the illuminated screen displayed a name.</p>
<p>~ Dad ~</p>
<p>Normally, West always answered immediately but this time, he stared at the phone silently while it rang before declining the call.</p>
<p>The apartment turned quiet again afterward.</p>
