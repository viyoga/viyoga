<p>&gt; [Activation condition met: Romantic Replacement + Emotional Trauma.]</p>
<p>[Host psychology compatible.]</p>
<p>"Compatible?" West scoffed. "That’s one way to put it."</p>
<p>He leaned forward wiyh elbows resting on his thighs, eyes locked onto the interface now.</p>
<p>"Explain it," he said. "All of it."</p>
<p>The system didn’t respond immediately.</p>
<p>Then—</p>
<p>The screen expanded.</p>
<p>Lines of light unfolded like pages being turned.</p>
<p>&gt; [Cuckhold System — Full Interface Unlocking...]</p>
<p>West sucked in a breath.</p>
<p>&gt; [World Truth:]</p>
<p>This is a hierarchy-driven reality.</p>
<p>Power defines worth.</p>
<p>Romantic value is an extension of personal power.</p>
<p>West swallowed.</p>
<p>"Bullshit," he muttered.</p>
<p>&gt; [Demonstrated false.]</p>
<p>The cafeteria incident replayed again—only this time, slower. Every face. Every reaction. The way Caleb stood taller. The way Lena gravitated toward him without hesitation.</p>
<p>West looked away.</p>
<p>&gt; [Cuckhold System Function:]</p>
<p>You gain power by reversing the dynamic.</p>
<p>By becoming the replacement.</p>
<p>By taking what others believe is secure.</p>
<p>His chest tightened.</p>
<p>"You want me to do... what was done to me."</p>
<p>&gt; [Yes.]</p>
<p>Silence stretched.</p>
<p>West laughed again, sharper this time. "You’re sick."</p>
<p>&gt; [You did not receive mercy.]</p>
<p>[Why should you offer it?]</p>
<p>The words hit harder than any insult Caleb had thrown at him.</p>
<p>West stood abruptly, pacing the small room.</p>
<p>"So this is my ’opportunity,’ huh?" he said bitterly. "Become the thing I hate."</p>
<p>&gt; [Become the thing that survives.]</p>
<p>He stopped.</p>
<p>That... felt different.</p>
<p>&gt;[ Should you choose to follow this path, you shall rise in Power, Tier, Society. You shall become capable of clearing ruins]</p>
<p>West dragged a hand down his face.</p>
<p>"All this power stuff," he said. "Tiers. Awakening. Gangs. Ruins. You’re saying this is my way in?"</p>
<p>&gt; [Only method available to Host.]</p>
<p>He glanced back at the interface.</p>
<p>"What if I don’t do it?"</p>
<p>The screen dimmed slightly.</p>
<p>&gt; [System will remain dormant.]</p>
<p>[Host will remain Tier 0.]</p>
<p>West already knew what that meant.</p>
<p>Invisible.</p>
<p>Replaceable.</p>
<p>Background.</p>
<p>His phone buzzed again.</p>
<p>This time, he picked it up.</p>
<p>A message from an unknown number.</p>
<p>Caleb: She’s staying over tonight. Just thought you should know.</p>
<p>West stared at the screen.</p>
<p>His hands stopped shaking.</p>
<p>He locked the phone and tossed it onto the bed.</p>
<p>"...Fuck it," he whispered.</p>
<p>The blue interface flared.</p>
<p>&gt; [Decision Registered.]</p>
<p>The screen transformed again, becoming cleaner, sharper.</p>
<p>&gt; [Cuckhold System — Active]</p>

<p>"And if I succeed..."</p>

<p>Levels.</p>
<p>He thought of Lena’s indifference.</p>
<p>Upgrade.</p>
<p>He clenched his fists.</p>
<p>"Nobody gave a damn when it happened to me," he said.</p>
<p>The system throbbed softly, like agreement.</p>
<p>West looked at the quest again.</p>
<p>Someone else’s girlfriend.</p>
<p>His mind drifted automatically to school, to the café, to faces he’d never looked at twice.</p>
<p>"...You’re serious," he murmured.</p>
<p>&gt; [Entirely.]</p>
<p>West leaned back, staring at the ceiling again but this time, the fan wasn’t what he saw.</p>

<p>And for the first time since Awakening had reshaped the world, West Einstein realized something terrifying.</p>
<p>He wasn’t locked out anymore.</p>
<p>He just had to climb.</p>
