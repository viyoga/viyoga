<p>After seeing the luxurious space at his new place, the current apartment felt cramped.</p>
<p>He looked around slowly taking note of the couch, the television, the bed, he kitchen appliances... pretty much, everything he owned.</p>
<p>Moving all of it would normally take hours.</p>
<p>He walked over to the couch and placed his hand on it when an idea popped into his head.</p>
<p>"...Wait."</p>
<p>He frowned thoughtfully.</p>
<p>"What if I tried that...Could it work on furniture?"</p>
<p>West focused slightly, mentally targeted the couch with his hand still placed upon it.</p>
<p>For a split second nothing happened... and then...</p>
<p>WHOOSH</p>
<p>The couch vanished.</p>
<p>West looked down at the empty spot where the couch had been in awe.</p>
<p>"...No way."</p>
<p>He opened his inventory and sure enough... there it was.</p>
<p>[Item Stored: Living Room Couch]</p>
<p>West burst out laughing.</p>
<p>"You’ve got to be kidding me."</p>
<p>He walked over to the TV and touched...</p>
<p>WHOOSH</p>
<p>Stored.</p>
<p>Next came the coffee table, then the dining table and chairs...</p>
<p>They vanished into his inventory one after the other.</p>
<p>West couldn’t stop grinning.</p>
<p>His system inventory had always been used for items he found in the ruins. He had no idea he could use it to store regular stuff till now.</p>
<p>"This is insane."</p>
<p>He moved through the apartment like a vacuum cleaner touching bed frames, mattress, kitchen appliances, shelves, cabinets...</p>
<p>Every item he could lift or touch was stored instantly.</p>
<p>The apartment began emptying rapidly... until suddenly—</p>
<p>[Inventory Full]</p>
<p>West stopped.</p>
<p>"...Seriously?"</p>
<p>He opened the interface and noticed that all the slots were filled.</p>
<p>He looked around the apartment realizing he barely reached the halfway point.</p>
<p>There were still several pieces of furniture and other stuff left.</p>
<p>He scratched the back of his head.</p>
<p>"Well... that’s annoying."</p>
<p>Then another notification appeared.</p>
<p>[ Would you like to expand inventory space? ]</p>
<p>&lt;[ YES ]&gt; × &lt;[ NO ]&gt;</p>
<p>West raised an eyebrow.</p>
<p>"...Expand?"</p>
<p>He tapped YES.</p>
<p>Immediately another notification popped up.</p>
<p>[ -2000 Cuck Points Deducted ]</p>
<p>West winced in disbelief.</p>
<p>"...That’s expensive."</p>
<p>A new notification appeared in the next line.</p>
<p>&lt;[ Inventory Capacity Increased ]&gt;</p>
<p>[ +30 Slots ]</p>
<p>West nodded slowly.</p>
<p>"Okay... that’s actually worth it."</p>
<p>Thirty extra slots was huge.</p>
<p>He immediately resumed collecting items.</p>
<p>More furniture disappeared, bookshelves, kitchenware, bedroom cabinets, lighting stands.</p>
<p>Within another ten minutes, most of the apartment had been cleared but eventually the inventory filled again.</p>
<p>West glanced around, noticing that few items were still left behind.</p>
<p>A large wardrobe, some heavy boxes and a few smaller appliances but he shrugged.</p>
<p>"Good enough."</p>
<p>Whatever remained could be handled later.</p>
<p>"I’ll just send Gor’thala and Seraphyra tonight."</p>
<p>They could carry the rest easily.</p>
<p>With a satisfied expression, West locked the door and headed toward his new apartment building after checking taking a bunch of precautionary measures to ensure he wasn’t followed.</p>
<p>---</p>
<p>The luxury tower looked even more impressive at night.</p>
<p>Soft golden lights illuminated the lobby and the elevator ride upward felt smooth and quiet.</p>
<p>On his way down the hallway toward his apartment, West passed several residents.</p>
<p>A couple walking their dog, an elderly man returning from groceries, a young woman carrying shopping bags...</p>
<p>West gave polite nods as he walked.</p>
<p>He suddenly heard loud voices echoe from one of the nearby apartments.</p>
<p>"...You never listen!"</p>
<p>"You’re exaggerating again!"</p>
<p>West glanced toward the source of the argument.</p>
<p>A man and a woman stood outside their apartment door arguing loudly.</p>
<p>The man looked furious while the woman looked equally irritated.</p>
<p>The moment she noticed West approaching, her expression softened slightly.</p>
<p>She was quite beautiful with long brown hair and elegant features.</p>
<p>Her slender figure was wrapped in a casual home flower patterned dress.</p>
<p>West stopped briefly to greet his neighbours.</p>
<p>"Good evening."</p>
<p>The woman smiled politely.</p>
<p>"Good evening."</p>
<p>West took her hand gently and gave a playful gentlemanly kiss to the back of it.</p>
<p>"Pleasure meeting you, I’m West, your new neighbor."</p>
<p>"Nice to meet you West, I’m Mrs Richards," she responded gleefully.</p>
<p>The husband immediately turned red with rage.</p>
<p>"HEY!"</p>
<p>But before he could say anything, the wife shot him an annoyed look.</p>
<p>"See?"</p>
<p>"You should learn to be more like him. A gentle man."</p>
<p>The husband looked like he might explode while West simply smiled calmly and continued walking and waving.</p>
<p>"...Goodnight..."</p>
<p>He reached his apartment door and unlocked it.</p>
<p>The moment he stepped inside, the massive empty space greeted him again.</p>
<p>West walked to the center of the living room before opening his inventory.</p>
<p>"Alright. Time to unpack."</p>
<p>One by one, items began reappearing across the surroundings.</p>
<p>Couch, table, television, kitchen appliances, beds...</p>
<p>Furniture quickly filled the apartment but West didn’t bother arranging anything.</p>
<p>Instead he snapped his fingers and three summoning circles appeared.</p>
<p>Gor’thala, Seraphyra and Aurethia Sol’Vael materialized inside the apartment.</p>
<p>Seraphyra looked around the luxurious space with interest.</p>
<p>"Oh?"</p>
<p>She smiled.</p>
<p>"I like the upgrade master."</p>
<p>Gor’thala crossed his arms approvingly.</p>
<p>"Good lair."</p>
<p>Aurethia simply nodded.</p>
<p>"Orders?"</p>
<p>West stretched lazily and dropped onto the couch.</p>
<p>"You three arrange everything."</p>
<p>Seraphyra laughed.</p>
<p>"You summoned us... to do interior decorating?"</p>
<p>"Exactly."</p>
<p>Gor’thala instantly began lifting furniture while Aurethia moved chairs into positions efficiently.</p>
<p>Seraphyra on the other hand organized the shelves while turning back to face West. "Master you’d better reward me properly by redecorating my insides later..."</p>
<p>"I’ll just pretend like you didn’t just say that..." West relaxed on the couch while watching them.</p>
<p>"Hmph! No fair," Seraphyra pouted her lips.</p>
<p>Another thought suddenly crossed West’s mind at this moment and it had to do with the Chalice of Vitality.</p>
<p>That artifact had saved him a number of times and even during the Eternal Ruins incident, it still helped him.</p>
<p>However, it had one major weakness... he couldn’t always use it during combat since it required liquid.</p>
<p>And filling it in the middle of a battle wasn’t practical.</p>
<p>West sat up.</p>
<p>"...Wait."</p>
<p>As the idea formed, he opened his inventory and pulled out the Chalice before walking to the kitchen sink.</p>
<p>He filled the chalice with water and the liquid inside immediately began glowing with a dull red light.</p>
