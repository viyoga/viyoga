<p>The moment Nina flicked the brush forward, a glowing line appeared mid-air but she didn’t stop there... she kept flicking and flicking...</p>
<p>Within seconds, she had drawn a small creature which looked like a mixture between a bunny and a fox. It seemed cute and almost harmless looking.</p>
<p>Until it appeared into reality alive with flesh and bones.</p>
<p>It looked around momentarily before dashing forward at surprising speed and slamming into a metal crate, knocking it over.</p>
<p>Jax blinked.</p>
<p>"...Okay, that’s new."</p>
<p>Nina grinned proudly. "Second branch!"</p>
<p>She spun again, drawing two more creatures which were slightly larger this time with sharper features.</p>
<p>They appeared instantly, moving with more aggression.</p>
<p>"First branch is still objects," she explained quickly. "Second branch lets me create living things... kinda."</p>
<p>West watched carefully.</p>
<p>"Limitations?"</p>
<p>"Size and complexity," she replied. "And how fast I can draw."</p>
<p>She demonstrated again, drawing a clean and simple shield.</p>
<p>It materialized instantly in her hand without a second wasted. Then she drew a spear and a cluster of small floating platforms.</p>
<p>Her speed had improved drastically.</p>
<p>What used to take her several seconds now happened in moments.</p>
<p>"Third branch though..." she voiced with a lower tone.</p>
<p>West raised a brow.</p>
<p>"...Go on."</p>
<p>She hesitated.</p>
<p>"Only once a day."</p>
<p>She proceeded to lift the brush again and drew a massive incomplete outline that radiated intensity...</p>
<p>She stopped halfway.</p>
<p>"...I could make something huge."</p>
<p>"How huge?" Jax asked.</p>
<p>Nina glanced at West.</p>
<p>"...Like... a building."</p>
<p>She paused.</p>
<p>"...Or worse... nuclear..."</p>
<p>West understood immediately.</p>
<p>"...Don’t use it unless you absolutely have to."</p>
<p>She nodded quickly.</p>
<p>"Yeah, I figured."</p>
<p>Despite her lower physical strength compared to Jax, her versatility was terrifying.</p>
<p>She could create objects, creatures, structures...</p>
<p>She was practically a walking arsenal.</p>
<p>"Good," West pushed himself off the pillar while they both turned to him.</p>
<p>"You’re ready."</p>
<p>Jax grinned instantly.</p>
<p>"For what?"</p>
<p>West smirked.</p>
<p>"To join me on the next ruin."</p>
<p>That got their full attention.</p>
<p>"...Seriously?" Nina asked.</p>
<p>West nodded.</p>
<p>"It’ll be the last one."</p>
<p>"For me with Dragon Fang Gang."</p>
<p>They both froze.</p>
<p>"Wait... that means—"</p>
<p>"Ten ruins," West said simply.</p>
<p>"After that, I meet the requirement."</p>
<p>"And Shadow Reapers becomes official."</p>
<p>"Finally!" Jax laughed in excitement. "It’s about time."</p>
<p>Nina practically bounced on the spot. "We’re really doing this..."</p>
<p>West nodded.</p>
<p>"Yeah."</p>
<p>Then added calmly—</p>
<p>"So be ready."</p>
<p>—</p>
<p>Later, as they took a break, West leaned back slightly, pulling out his phone.</p>
<p>"Tomorrow, I’ve got something to handle."</p>
<p>Jax raised a brow. "What now?"</p>
<p>"Divine Forge."</p>
<p>That got Jax’s attention immediately.</p>
<p>"...More weapons?"</p>
<p>West nodded.</p>
<p>"Remember your katana?"</p>
<p>Jax glanced at it instinctively.</p>
<p>"Yeah?"</p>
<p>"There were leftover fragments from the Cryoheart Shard."</p>
<p>Jax’s eyes widened.</p>
<p>"No way..."</p>
<p>West smirked.</p>
<p>"Orion used them."</p>
<p>"Made two more frost weapons."</p>
<p>Nina blinked. "Wait... like yours?"</p>
<p>"Not exactly," West said. "More on the weaker side but nonethless valueable."</p>
<p>"One already sold."</p>
<p>"For around $450,000."</p>
<p>Jax nearly choked.</p>
<p>"...Excuse me?"</p>
<p>West shrugged.</p>
<p>"The second one’s about to sell."</p>
<p>"I’m going to finalize it tomorrow."</p>
<p>Nina stared at him.</p>
<p>"...How much money do you even have now?"</p>
<p>West thought for a second.</p>
<p>"...Close to ten million."</p>
<p>"...BRO WHAT?" Jax eyes widened as he shouted.</p>
<p>West chuckled. "Relax."</p>
<p>"That’s startup money."</p>
<p>"For the gang."</p>
<p>And he wasn’t even exaggerating because behind the scenes, Aria had been working relentlessly setting up offshore accounts, creating untraceable transaction pathways, structuring finances in a way that couldn’t be linked back to them... Investments... Allocations... logistics... data... everything...</p>
<p>"She’s been handling a lot," West stated.</p>
<p>Jax nodded slowly. "Yeah... she’s legit."</p>
<p>Nina smiled. "She really wants this to work."</p>
<p>West’s expression softened slightly.</p>
<p>"...Yeah."</p>
<p>"She does."</p>
<p>—</p>
<p>As the sun began to set, the training session wrapped up.</p>
<p>They were tired but excited because everything was finally coming together.</p>
<p>Nina suddenly turned to West.</p>
<p>"Oh—wait!"</p>
<p>West glanced at her.</p>
<p>"What?"</p>
<p>She smiled sheepishly.</p>
<p>"I... uh... made something."</p>
<p>"For you."</p>
<p>West raised a brow.</p>
<p>"...A weapon?"</p>
<p>She shook her head quickly.</p>
<p>"No no—art."</p>
<p>"I drew you... again."</p>
<p>Jax immediately grinned. "Yo—let me see—"</p>
<p>"No!" Nina snapped.</p>
<p>"It’s not for you!"</p>
<p>West chuckled slightly.</p>
<p>"...When do I get it?"</p>
<p>"I’ll come by this week," she responded.</p>
<p>"You’re always welcome," West voiced with a smirk while Nina smiled brightly.</p>
<p>"Good."</p>
<p>The late afternoon sun filtered through the broken ceiling panels of the abandoned industrial warehouse, casting long beams of golden light across the dusty ground as they rested a little in preparation to call it a day.</p>
<p>Jax sat on a rusted crate with his ice katana resting across his lap and faint wisps of cold still radiating from the blade.</p>
<p>"The ruin better not be some weak stuff. I need a real fight this time," Jax stated with a look of confidence.</p>
<p>Nina rolled her eyes while twirling her glowing brush between her fingers.</p>
<p>"You say that now, but when something massive shows up, you’ll be the first to shout ’West help me!’"</p>
<p>Jax scoffed. "Please. I’ve grown, okay?"</p>
<p>West leaned back against the pillar, folding his arms and watching them with mild amusement.</p>
<p>"...You’ve grown," he echoed.</p>
<p>Jax smirked. "See? Even boss agrees."</p>
<p>Nina snorted. "He didn’t say how much."</p>
<p>Jax was about to fire back when footsteps echoed from the entrance.</p>
<p>All three turned as five figures walked in casually.</p>
<p>They didn’t even glance at West, Nina, or Jax at first.</p>
<p>Instead, they spread out slightly, scanning the warehouse, looking around as if inspecting property.</p>
<p>"...Yeah," one of them voiced with hands in his pockets. "This works."</p>
<p>"Plenty of space," another added. "Hidden enough too."</p>
<p>"Perfect location for our base."</p>
<p>Jax blinked.</p>
<p>"...Uh?"</p>
<p>Nina frowned slightly while West said nothing.</p>
<p>One of the five finally turned toward them and walked forward before stopping a few feet away.</p>
<p>His gaze swept over them briefly and dismissively before speaking.</p>
<p>"This place is now the property of the Setting Sun Gang," he declared with a flat tone.</p>
<p>"You can leave now."</p>
<p>There was a brief silence before low chuckling sounds rang out from West.</p>
<p>The man’s brow twitched.</p>
<p>"...Something funny?"</p>
