<p>Debra didn’t like hearing that one bit but she knew he wasn’t wrong.</p>
<p>Eventually she nodded. "We understand."</p>
<p>Mark nodded too without arguing because both had already seen enough of the awakened world to understand how dangerous it could be.</p>
<p>The important thing was that they were talking again... they had access to him again... since he had given them another chance.</p>
<p>For Debra especially, that meant everything.</p>
<p>As the conversation continued, she found herself smiling more than she had in years.</p>
<p>There were no more episodes... or nightmares.. or endless guilt... at least not to the same degree.</p>
<p>Knowing that the man responsible for her daughter’s death was gone had given her a form of closure she’d never thought possible.</p>
<p>For the first time in nearly two decades, she was finally starting to heal.</p>
<p>•••</p>
<p>•••</p>
<p>Elsewhere...</p>
<p>Iron Vultures Headquarters looked like a corpse of what it once was.</p>
<p>Many members had left, others had become inactive and several divisions had collapsed entirely.</p>
<p>The once-bustling base now felt hollow, empty and broken.</p>
<p>Bloodstain himself looked years older than he had only weeks earlier.</p>
<p>Dark circles sat beneath his eyes and stress had become a permanent part of his appearance.</p>
<p>Today, however, his attention wasn’t on the state of his gang, it was on the guests who had arrived.</p>
<p>Nearly fifteen awakened stood throughout the room from Eagle’s Print.</p>
<p>Even standing casually, they radiated confidence and authority.</p>
<p>At their center stood a broad-shouldered man with short dark hair and a star shaped tattoo on one side of his face.</p>
<p>His name was Victor Severus and he was one of Eagles Print’s high-ranking leaders who shared the same rank Darius once held.</p>
<p>The room remained silent for several moments as bloodstain maintained a respectful posture.</p>
<p>This was something he rarely did but even if Iron Vultures hadn’t been hit with their most recent ordeal, Eagles Print still operated on a completely different level.</p>
<p>Victor finally spoke. "We heard rumors."</p>
<p>Bloodstain nodded as Victor continued. "Interesting rumors."</p>
<p>"What kind?"</p>
<p>"The kind involving a furry blueish creature."</p>
<p>Bloodstain immediately stiffened and Victor caught it. "Good."</p>
<p>"You know what I’m talking about," Bloodstain slowly nodded while Victor’s gaze sharpened.</p>
<p>"A creature capable of teleportation."</p>
<p>"Blue footprints."</p>
<p>"White glowing eyes."</p>
<p>"Glowing fangs."</p>
<p>Every word made Bloodstain more certain that they were talking about him...</p>
<p>Victor folded his arms. "Sound familiar?"</p>
<p>Bloodstain swallowed. "It does."</p>
<p>The Eagles Print members exchanged glances as Victor stepped forward.</p>
<p>"We believe the same individual is responsible for Darius Bentley’s death."</p>
<p>The room became silent at the mention of Darius’s name.</p>
<p>Victor’s voice hardened. "Eagles Print does not forget its own."</p>
<p>"We do not forgive attacks against our people."</p>
<p>"And we absolutely do not forgive murder."</p>
<p>A dangerous pressure filled the room causing several Iron Vultures members to instinctively step backward.</p>
<p>Victor continued. "We need everything."</p>
<p>"Every report."</p>
<p>"Every encounter."</p>
<p>"Every witness statement."</p>
<p>"Every scrap of information."</p>
<p>"We will find this vermin."</p>
<p>"We will destroy him."</p>
<p>"And we will avenge Darius."</p>
<p>For a moment Bloodstain simply stared as something he hadn’t felt in a long time enveloped him... hope.</p>
<p>His gang was collapsing since their most recent ordeal and his reputation was ruined.</p>
<p>But now that Eagles Print... a force capable of crushing Shadow Reapers entirely had entered the picture... things were looking up.</p>
<p>For the first time in weeks, Bloodstain smiled.</p>
<p>He instantly instructed his subordinates to compile everything they had so far on the incident.</p>
<p>Files were brought out filled with reports, photos, investigation documents and the likes.</p>
<p>Victor accepted them before asking one final question. "Who is responsible?"</p>
<p>Bloodstain smile widened as he spoke the name without hesitating for even a second, "The man you’re looking for..."</p>
<p>He tapped one of the files. "...is West Einstein."</p>
<p>The room became completely silent as Victor slowly looked down with a photograph on top of the young leader of Shadow Reapers.</p>
<p>A rising gang... a newcomer... a nobody.</p>
<p>Victor’s eyes narrowed. "West Einstein."</p>
<p>Bloodstain nodded. "Shadow Reapers."</p>
<p>A look of curiosity appeared on Victor’s face. "Interesting."</p>
<p>Bloodstain had not felt this hopeful in weeks.</p>
<p>For the first time since the collapse of Iron Vultures began, he genuinely felt like things were finally about to turn around.</p>
<p>Victor Severus stood in the center of the room flipping through report after report while several high-ranking Eagles Print members gathered around him.</p>
<p>Bloodstain watched with growing excitement.</p>
<p>Every file was there... every witness statement... every sighting... every surveillance image... every blurry piece of footage... every report from the members who survived the ambushes.</p>
<p>The files showed the same thing repeatedly... an image of a strange beast-like humanoid with glowing blue-white eyes, long glowing fangs, monstrous physical strength, impossible speed... and most importantly... the glowing footprints.</p>
<p>The glowing footprints confirmed that this was truly the same creature who had murdered Darius Bentley even though some of the images weren’t particularly clear.</p>
<p>Most of the images been captured from a distance... several were blurry while others were partially obscured.</p>
<p>But there was enough to identify the creature... enough to know it was responsible.</p>
<p>Bloodstain folded his arms now confident that he could completely destroy Shadow Reapers once Eagles Print got involved...</p>
<p>West would die... his stolen loot would be recovered and iron Vultures would reclaim their reputation.</p>
<p>Everything would begin moving in the right direction again.</p>
<p>Victor continued reading with an increasingly serious expression.</p>
<p>Bloodstain mistook it for concern and anger directed at West until Victor suddenly threw the entire stack of files onto a nearby table.</p>
<p>The sound echoed throughout the room causing Bloodstain to blink.</p>
<p>The atmosphere instantly changed as Victor glared at him with a furious look. "Utter nonsense."</p>
<p>Bloodstain froze. "What?"</p>
<p>Victor turned toward him. "I didn’t realize Iron Vultures invited us here to waste our time."</p>
<p>The smile vanished from Bloodstain’s face immediately. "What are you talking about?"</p>
<p>Victor pointed at the reports. "This."</p>
<p>His voice rose as he added. "All of this."</p>
<p>The Eagles Print members around him looked equally annoyed.</p>
<p>One woman with crimson hair folded her arms while another bald giant of a man looked ready to leave.</p>
<p>Victor stepped forward. "You expect us to believe this?"</p>
<p>Bloodstain frowned. "It is the truth."</p>
