<p>"No wonder people call it Castle Extingo."</p>
<p>West continued reading and discovered that the castle consisted of numerous floors.</p>
<p>However... unlike ordinary buildings where one climbed upward... Castle Extingo worked in reverse.</p>
<p>Everyone entering the rift appeared on the highest floor.</p>
<p>From there... the only path forward was downward.</p>
<p>Every descent brought explorers closer to richer treasures and greater danger.</p>
<p>The lowest floor... according to every available source, contained the greatest rewards in the entire castle.</p>
<p>It was also believed to house the legendary treasure Freya had spoken about.</p>
<p>West tapped the table thoughtfully. "So the deeper you go..."</p>
<p>"...the greater the reward."</p>
<p>"But also..."</p>
<p>"...the greater the chance you die."</p>
<p>He wasn’t surprised. Nothing valuable ever came easily.</p>
<p>Another report that caught his attention, explained why Castle Extingo remained undefeated despite appearing every hundred years.</p>
<p>Its greatest obstacle wasn’t merely the monsters... it was also the restriction.</p>
<p>Only awakeners who had been awakened for one year or less could enter.</p>
<p>That meant... everyone participating was effectively still a rookie.</p>
<p>They were all tier one awakeners, with majorly a single branch and limited combat experience.</p>
<p>Normally... Veteran awakeners could brute-force through many dangerous situations.</p>
<p>Castle Extingo removed that possibility entirely.</p>
<p>It forced inexperienced awakeners to rely on teamwork, intelligence and adaptability.</p>
<p>West quietly nodded. "No wonder everyone forms teams."</p>
<p>Reading further, he discovered the castle itself wasn’t a single uninterrupted structure.</p>
<p>Every floor contained multiple districts, residential-like sectors, ancient libraries, collapsed cathedrals, underground prisons, gardens, laboratories, massive banquet halls and so on.</p>
<p>Every section housed different dangers, puzzles and rewards.</p>
<p>Some explorers spent an entire month exploring only one floor before the castle disappeared again.</p>
<p>West frowned. "So absurdly huge..."</p>
<p>He opened another expedition journal.</p>
<p>The author had survived nearly eighty years earlier.</p>
<p>The handwriting grew increasingly frantic toward the final pages.</p>
<p>"...Never underestimate anything..."</p>
<p>"...The smallest creatures kill just as efficiently..."</p>
<p>"...Our strongest member died to insects..."</p>
<p>West blinked. "Insects?"</p>
<p>He continued reading and the journal described tiny ant-like creatures no larger than a thumb.</p>
<p>Just a single one had stripped a Tier One awakener to bones within seconds.</p>
<p>West slowly closed the journal. "...Right."</p>
<p>"Noted."</p>
<p>Another report documented invisible pressure fields... another described corridors that rearranged themselves... another warned of mirrors capable of trapping consciousness...</p>
<p>Some traps couldn’t even be detected until they activated.</p>
<p>The more West read... the more he realized something.</p>
<p>Castle Extingo danger surpassed even two-layered ruins.</p>
<p>Considering its impossible size, endless floors, countless unknown creatures and environmental hazards... its difficulty level bordered on insanity.</p>
<p>And yet... every appearance attracted thousands of first-year awakeners.</p>
<p>This was because the rewards were equally absurd.</p>
<p>Even explorers who failed to descend very far often emerged carrying treasures worth millions.</p>
<p>Many gangs funded rookie teams specifically for this expedition.</p>
<p>According to Aria’s reports... registration through the Mafia cost approximately one million dollars per team.</p>
<p>West initially thought the amount ridiculous until he continued reading.</p>
<p>Successful expeditions routinely generated ten to twenty and sometimes even a hundred times that amount in profit.</p>
<p>No wonder organizations practically fought over promising first-year awakeners.</p>
<p>West finally reached the section discussing legendary treasures.</p>
<p>His fingers stopped atop a grainy photograph, taken over three centuries ago.</p>
<p>It depicted a perfectly spherical object suspended above an elaborate pedestal.</p>
<p>The sphere looked almost ordinary with smooth white features.</p>
<p>Yet... something about it made West continue staring.</p>
<p>"...So that’s it," he picked up the accompanying document. "The Heart of Genesis."</p>
<p>"Interesting name."</p>
<p>The moment he began reading, his expression slowly changed. "...Capable of awakening latent potential..."</p>
<p>"...Allows ordinary humans to awaken..."</p>
<p>"...Extremely unstable outside Castle Extingo..."</p>
<p>"...Historically impossible to secure..."</p>
<p>West reread that paragraph again and again before leaning back. "...Whoa."</p>
<p>"So that’s why everyone loses their minds over this thing."</p>
<p>If the information proved accurate... yhe Heart of Genesis could awaken ordinary people.</p>
<p>Which meant that, a faction possessing it could gradually create its own awakened force.</p>
<p>It was a terrifying thought to behold. Entire organizations could reshape the balance of power if they possessed it.</p>
<p>It made sense why Freya wanted it. The mafia would technically crown her queen if she could acquire such a treasure for them.</p>
<p>No wonder four centuries of bloodshed had revolved around this single treasure.</p>
<p>West couldn’t help laughing. "What an unbelievable prize..."</p>
<p>He looked toward the ceiling. "...Too bad it’s practically useless to me."</p>
<p>His system already allowed him to purchase Awakening Vouchers.</p>
<p>He already possesed the power to create awakened allies from the very beginning.</p>
<p>Compared to the Heart of Genesis... his system accomplished the same objective without requiring him to fight half the awakened world.</p>
<p>The legendary treasure everyone desired... held almost no personal value to him.</p>
<p>He rubbed his chin thoughtfully. "I could simply refuse Freya."</p>
<p>The idea lingered for several seconds before he shook his head. "No."</p>
<p>"Castle Extingo itself is worth entering."</p>
<p>The Heart of Genesis may not be his objective but everything else was.</p>
<p>The castle contained countless treasures.</p>
<p>Surely the greatest prize wasn’t the only extraordinary object hidden within.</p>
<p>There had to be other relics, ancient materials and perhaps even resources that his system couldn’t replicate.</p>
<p>Walking away wasn’t an option.</p>
<p>However... another realization surfaced. "I don’t like being controlled."</p>
<p>West stood and walked toward the enormous city map mounted upon his wall. "If Freya wants one team..."</p>
<p>"...I’ll bring two," his eyes sharpened. "An independent team led by Jax."</p>
<p>If one group became delayed...</p>
<p>It significantly increased Shadow Reapers’ chances.</p>
<p>There was only one problem left now.</p>
<p>West frowned. "...First-year awakeners."</p>
<p>The eligibility wasn’t optional. Anyone awakened for more than one year would simply be rejected by the castle.</p>
<p>Currently... him, Jax and Nina qualified.</p>
<p>Beyond that... very few Shadow Reapers members met the requirement.</p>
<p>Many recruits had already been awakened for years before joining.</p>
<p>That left Shadow Reapers surprisingly short-handed.</p>
<p>West looked toward the calendar noting that exactly three months remained.</p>
<p>Three months to prepare for what many considered the greatest opportunity of an awakened’s first year.</p>
<p>A slow smile spread across his face. "Looks like..."</p>
<p>"...the next big adventure has already begun."</p>
