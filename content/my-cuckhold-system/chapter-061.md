<p>Zu Li watched him for a second longer, like she was savoring the fact that he hadn’t folded.</p>
<p>Then she said, "I was intrigued after we bumped into each other yesterday."</p>
<p>West’s brows rose. "People get intrigued over bumps now?"</p>
<p>"Yes," she said smoothly. "Because you didn’t recoil. A regular person would have been sent flying... a tier 1 awakened wouldn’t have been much better off either... but you..."</p>
<p>West remembered that casual collision from yesterday and the fact that she hadn’t moved at all.</p>
<p>So she wasn’t just "an awakened."</p>
<p>She was different... The kind of person whose casual body strength made other awakened look like regular gym bros.</p>
<p>Zu Li continued with an almost conversational tone.</p>
<p>"Today... despite the hurdles you faced, you still didn’t reveal your awakened power. You didn’t activate your branch."</p>
<p>West’s eyes narrowed slightly.</p>
<p>"You were watching me."</p>
<p>"Yes."</p>
<p>"You followed me around all day."</p>
<p>"Yes."</p>
<p>"And you’re saying—" West’s voice sharpened, "—you saw what happened?"</p>
<p>Zu Li nodded. "I saw everything."</p>
<p>West’s stomach tightened.</p>
<p>Elina’s near fall.</p>
<p>The weird aggression in the games.</p>
<p>The cyclone in the water.</p>
<p>The track vanishing.</p>
<p>All those "freak accidents" that were too freak to be coincidence.</p>
<p>Zu Li spoke like she was commenting on the weather.</p>
<p>"You relied on strength and agility. You escaped every sticky situation without activating your branch. That caught my eye."</p>
<p>West didn’t respond... he listened without a suspicious gaze.</p>
<p>Zu Li tilted her head slightly as her eyes shone with interest.</p>
<p>"However," she continued, "it made this difficult for me."</p>
<p>West’s expression didn’t change, but his blood pressure did.</p>
<p>"Because I need to know what kind of awakener you are," Zu Li said, "before I can decide whether to take you under my wing."</p>
<p>The assistant’s eyes lit up like fireworks.</p>
<p>There it was.</p>
<p>The grand fortune.</p>
<p>The "your life is made" moment.</p>
<p>Zu Li—of all people—was offering apprenticeship.</p>
<p>The assistant looked like he wanted to scream: BOW. NOW. KID. BOW.</p>
<p>But West’s mind had tuned out everything except one part.</p>
<p>All the hurdles you faced today.</p>
<p>His eyes sharpened as the realization hit fully now like a door slamming.</p>
<p>He stepped forward half a pace.</p>
<p>"Wait."</p>
<p>Zu Li’s brows lifted.</p>
<p>West pointed with a dangerously calm voice.</p>
<p>"All of those... the cyclone... the people ramming us... the track vanishing..."</p>
<p>His face contorted.</p>
<p>"That was you?"</p>
<p>Zu Li didn’t even deny it.</p>
<p>"Yes," she said simply. "I sabotaged every activity you engaged in so I could see what you were capable of."</p>
<p>The assistant nodded like this was reasonable.</p>
<p>Like endangering civilians was a harmless hobby.</p>
<p>West’s face went still.</p>
<p>Very still.</p>
<p>His fingers curled as anger silently condensed underneath his calm persona.</p>
<p>And when he spoke, his voice was quiet.</p>
<p>"If you weren’t a woman," West said, "I would have hit you."</p>
<p>The assistant snapped, finally finding his voice. "How dare—!"</p>
<p>West turned his head slightly with a cold look.</p>
<p>"Shut. Up."</p>
<p>The assistant actually shut up.</p>
<p>His body did it before his pride could argue.</p>
<p>Zu Li’s gaze sharpened, but she didn’t look offended.</p>
<p>If anything... she looked more entertained.</p>
<p>West took a breath through his nose.</p>
<p>"I don’t care much about my life," he admitted bluntly. "I’ve been through worse. I’d survive."</p>
<p>His eyes flashed.</p>
<p>"But you put my father in jeopardy. And my friend."</p>
<p>His voice rose slightly now.</p>
<p>"People could’ve died. Not just me. Not just Elina. Other guests. Kids. Workers. Random people who came here to relax."</p>
<p>The assistant’s lips parted in disbelief.</p>
<p>No one talked to Zu Li like this.</p>
<p>No one accused her.</p>
<p>No one scolded her like she was a reckless teenager messing with fireworks. That was the ultimate power of not just being in a gang but owning one... It meant you didn’t have to live by the rules... and she didn’t.</p>
<p>She never expected that anyone would ever stand up to her, because no one ever did.</p>
<p>West stepped closer, just enough to make it clear he wasn’t afraid to stand in her space... though he didn’t touch her this time.</p>
<p>"You’re powerful," West stated. "Congratulations. You can ruin a day for fun. But don’t ever do that around me again."</p>
<p>Zu Li’s eyes gleamed.</p>
<p>West pointed toward the corridor where his father had gone.</p>
<p>"Stay away from me."</p>
<p>Then his finger moved slightly, indicating the resort at large.</p>
<p>"And stay away from everyone around me."</p>
<p>The assistant looked like his world was collapsing.</p>
<p>This kid had just rejected a gang leader’s interest.</p>
<p>Not politely...</p>
<p>Not carefully...</p>
<p>He didn’t bow...</p>
<p>He didn’t flatter...</p>
<p>He didn’t beg for forgiveness...</p>
<p>He outrightly scolded her and then turned around and walked away just like that.</p>
<p>He simply left like he’d just finished telling off a rude cashier.</p>
<p>The assistant stood frozen with his mouth open.</p>
<p>Then he whispered, horrified, "He’s... he’s insane..."</p>
<p>Zu Li didn’t respond.</p>
<p>She watched West’s back disappear down the hallway.</p>
<p>And the more she watched...</p>
<p>The more something in her eyes sharpened into certainty.</p>
<p>’Even after knowing exactly who I am... he still gave me a piece of his mind.’</p>
<p>Her lips curved into a slow, satisfied smirk.</p>
<p>"I must make him my apprentice," she murmured with a soft but dangerous voice. "no matter what."</p>
<p>The assistant finally found his breath.</p>
<p>"Mistress... he insulted you. He threatened you. He—"</p>
<p>Zu Li lifted a hand lazily.</p>
<p>The assistant stopped immediately.</p>
<p>Her gaze stayed locked in the direction West had gone, as if she could still see him.</p>
<p>"No," she said, almost pleased. "He didn’t threaten me."</p>
<p>She smiled wider.</p>
<p>"He drew a boundary."</p>
<p>The assistant looked even more confused. "That’s... worse."</p>
<p>Zu Li’s eyes glittered. "That’s rarer."</p>
<p>Then she turned with her mask still down and her beauty on full display.</p>
<p>"Find out who he is," she ordered.</p>
<p>The assistant swallowed. "We already know—"</p>
<p>Zu Li’s eyes flicked to him.</p>
<p>He corrected himself instantly.</p>
<p>"I mean... everything. Family. Background. Friends. Schedule. Work. School. His habits."</p>
<p>Zu Li’s smile returned.</p>
<p>"Exactly," she said. "If I let him slip away... I’ll regret it."</p>
