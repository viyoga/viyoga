<p>The three summons immediately dashed through the swamp toward where the assassin had crashed.</p>
<p>The ground was torn apart and the trees were broken with mud displaced across the ground.</p>
<p>They arrived at the position of the toppled tree but there was no body.</p>
<p>Only a trail of blood across the ground that lead deeper into the forest could be spotted.</p>
<p>"...She escaped."</p>
<p>Meanwhile, the glowing bindings dissolved, melting into particles of light that scattered into the damp swamp air before vanishing completely.</p>
<p>West rolled his shoulders the moment he was free, testing the feeling.</p>
<p>"...Annoying ability."</p>
<p>He stepped forward without wasting time.</p>
<p>By the time he reached the spot where the blood trail had ended, his summons were already there.</p>
<p>Gor’thala stood still with her blade lowered.</p>
<p>"...Master... I failed."</p>
<p>She lowered her head slightly. "It is my fault she escaped. Punish me."</p>
<p>Before West could respond, Seraphyra let out a soft chuckle while swaying her tail lazily behind her. "Kinky."</p>
<p>Gor’thala only kept her head lowered while Seraphyra grinned wider. "I didn’t know you were into that sort of thing, Gor’thala."</p>
<p>Aurethia, as usual, said nothing.</p>
<p>Her glowing saber remained in hand as her gaze scanned the swamp ahead, searching for even the faintest sign of movement.</p>
<p>West stepped up beside them, sweeping his gaze over the broken terrain, crushed foliage and the faint blood traces that had led them here.</p>
<p>"...No one is getting punished," he stated with a firm tone while Gor’thala lifted her head slightly.</p>
<p>"But—"</p>
<p>"I said no," West tone left no room for argument. "Fan out."</p>
<p>His eyes sharpened. "She couldn’t have gotten far."</p>
<p>Gor’thala moved first, vanishing into the trees to the left while Seraphyra slithered in the opposite direction with her tail flicking as she disappeared into the undergrowth.</p>
<p>Aurethia didn’t waste a second either, dashing straight ahead with the quickest pace.</p>
<p>West remained where he was for a moment before moving as well.</p>
<p>Minutes passed as they scanned the terrain but the forest remained eerily quiet with only the sounds of insects and the occasional ripple of stagnant water.</p>
<p>"...She’s gone," West finally turned back after nearly fifteen minutes of searching.</p>
<p>One by one, his summons returned to the original spot.</p>
<p>Gor’thala landed lightly, Seraphyra emerged from behind a cluster of trees while Aurethia stepped out of the shadows.</p>
<p>All three shook their heads, returning with the same results.</p>
<p>West exhaled slowly. "...Oh well."</p>
<p>His gaze drifted toward the direction where the blood trail had vanished.</p>
<p>"I’m sure this won’t be the last time we see her."</p>
<p>"We’ll just have to be more careful next time."</p>
<p>With that, he turned around. "Let’s go."</p>
<p>...</p>
<p>The car sat exactly where they had left it.</p>
<p>Debra and Dr. Amos were still near it, both looking visibly tensed and crouched due to the earlier battle.</p>
<p>The moment West stepped into view, Debra rushed forward slightly.</p>
<p>"West!"</p>
<p>Her eyes scanned him quickly. "Are you okay? You’re not hurt, are you?"</p>
<p>The concern in her voice was genuine but West didn’t look impressed.</p>
<p>"My wellbeing is none of your concern."</p>
<p>The words were more chilling than ice causing Debra to freeze in place.</p>
<p>The faint hope in her expression dimmed slightly but she nodded.</p>
<p>"...Alright."</p>
<p>West reached the car. "We’re leaving."</p>
<p>Without delaying for another second, yhey all got in.</p>
<p>Dr. Amos hesitated slightly before taking the driver’s seat, glancing briefly at the back where Gor’thala, Seraphyra, and Aurethia stood behind the car.</p>
<p>"...Uh..."</p>
<p>He cleared his throat awkwardly. "What about... them?"</p>
<p>West leaned back into his seat. "Don’t worry about it."</p>
<p>A moment later, the three summons vanished as if they had never been there.</p>
<p>Dr. Amos blinked. "...Right."</p>
<p>He started the engine and they left.</p>
<p>As they drove, the forest faded behind them.</p>
<p>The swamp gave way to rough terrain, then to proper roads, then finally to the distant glow of civilization.</p>
<p>Neither Debra nor Amos spoke much and West didn’t either.</p>
<p>His gaze remained fixed outside the window as his thoughts ran quietly beneath the surface.</p>
<p>—</p>
<p>Nearly an hour later, they arrived at the airport.</p>
<p>Tickets had already been arranged before they left the shack and fake passports. They both had scarfs covering their faces slightly... all that remained was to wait.</p>
<p>They took their seats in the waiting area, blending into the crowd of travelers moving through the terminal.</p>
<p>For a while, everything was calm until a sultry voice echoed in West’s mind.</p>
<p>"...Master."</p>
<p>Seraphyra’s head peeked out from the edge of his summoner space with a slightly pouty expression.</p>
<p>"I’m hungry."</p>
<p>West stared at her for a moment before sighing. "...Of course you are."</p>
<p>He stood up and walked toward one of the nearby snack shops, grabbing a few items without much thought.</p>
<p>When he returned, he subtly transferred them into his summoner space.</p>
<p>A moment later, Seraphyra’s eyes lit up.</p>
<p>"Oh—this is good."</p>
<p>Gor’thala took one silently while Aurethia examined hers before eating with quiet curiosity.</p>
<p>"...Human food in a bag is always delicious," she murmured.</p>
<p>West shook his head slightly. "...You three..."</p>
<p>—</p>
<p>Another hour passed before boarding time arrived.</p>
<p>They moved with every one else headed for Misty High, filed in and took their seats.</p>
<p>The plane taxied before finally lifting off into the sky after all the necessary proceedings.</p>
<p>In the sky, the buzz of the engines filled the cabin as the city below shrank into distant lights.</p>
<p>Inside West’s summoner space:</p>
<p>"Master!" Seraphyra pointed excitedly. "What sort of bird is this?"</p>
<p>West rubbed his forehead.</p>
<p>"...It’s not a bird."</p>
<p>"Then why does it fly?"</p>
<p>"...Because humans built it to."</p>
<p>Gor’thala tilted her head slightly.</p>
<p>"...So it is a construct designed for aerial traversal."</p>
<p>"Yes."</p>
<p>Aurethia nodded slowly.</p>
<p>"...Efficient."</p>
<p>Seraphyra leaned closer to the edge of the space.</p>
<p>"...Can it breathe fire?"</p>
<p>"No."</p>
<p>"...Disappointing."</p>
<p>West let out a sigh.</p>
<p>"...I regret answering that."</p>
<p>The conversation continued turning strange but carrying a lot of amusement.</p>
<p>Debra and Amos watched from their seats, occasionally exchanging glances.</p>
<p>If they didn’t know better, they would have thought he was talking to himself.</p>
