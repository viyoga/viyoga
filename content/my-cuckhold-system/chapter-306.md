<p>One young man swallowed hard. "There are so many of them..."</p>
<p>Nobody disagreed because it was true.</p>
<p>There were over a hundred Iron Vultures mob outside while on the inside, only around twenty members were currently present.</p>
<p>Even if every Shadow Reaper was here, their total numbers would barely exceed fifty. The difference was enormous.</p>
<p>A young lady nervously looked toward the gates. "What do we do?"</p>
<p>Nobody could come up with an answer because nobody knew the present way forward.</p>
<p>The walls around the compound were impressive and the defensive systems were very much active.</p>
<p>However, everyone understood a simple reality... if a full-scale battle began, those walls wouldn’t mean much.</p>
<p>Awakeners weren’t ordinary people... buildings, roads and even entire city blocks got destroyed in the face of a battle.</p>
<p>Walls certainly wouldn’t survive forever.</p>
<p>Jax stood near the center of the gathering with his hands resting casually in his pockets.</p>
<p>Oddly enough, he looked completely relaxed while everyone else looked ready to panic...</p>
<p>He looked mildly annoyed as one recruit pointed outside. "Why aren’t you worried?"</p>
<p>Jax shrugged. "Because West has never failed us before. Besides, panicking doesn’t solve anything."</p>
<p>Another recruit spoke. "They outnumber us five to one."</p>
<p>Jax nodded. "Yep."</p>
<p>"And you’re calm?"</p>
<p>"Yep."</p>
<p>The recruits stared while Jax stretched lazily. ’Trust me... or better still, trust in our gang leader."</p>
<p>"Everything will be fine."</p>
<p>The confidence in his voice felt strangely reassuring.</p>
<p>Nearby, Vanya folded her arms and unlike many of the others, she wasn’t particularly afraid either.</p>
<p>Mostly because she’d seen enough of Shadow Reapers already to understand one thing.</p>
<p>Nothing around here was normal... West definitely wasn’t normal and whenever things looked impossible... he somehow made them work.</p>
<p>Outside the walls, Bloodstain’s patience finally ran out.</p>
<p>His voice boomed across the compound. "WEST EINSTEIN!"</p>
<p>The sound echoed. "COME OUT HERE!"</p>
<p>His face twisted after waiting for seconds with no response. "You coward!"</p>
<p>The gathered Iron Vultures members began shouting insults, threats, challenges and everything they could think of.</p>
<p>Bloodstain pointed toward the compound. "Tell your precious gang leader to come out and answer for his crimes!"</p>
<p>The crowd roared again.</p>
<p>"He stole from us!"</p>
<p>"He attacked us!"</p>
<p>"He ruined our gang!"</p>
<p>"We’ll destroy them!"</p>
<p>Bloodstain raised his hand and silence slowly returned.</p>
<p>A dangerous smile appeared on his face as he spoke again. "Very well."</p>
<p>"If he won’t come out..."</p>
<p>His blood-red aura began spreading. "...we’ll go in."</p>
<p>The pressure around him intensified causing several nearby members to step back instinctively.</p>
<p>Bloodstain pointed at the gates. "I’ll give him ten seconds."</p>
<p>His voice echoed. "Ten."</p>
<p>Inside the base, nervous swallowing could be heard.</p>
<p>"Nine."</p>
<p>Several recruits tightened their grips on weapons.</p>
<p>"Eight."</p>
<p>The AI voice within the base continued monitoring external threats.</p>
<p>"Seven."</p>
<p>Some members began preparing abilities.</p>
<p>"Six."</p>
<p>The pressure outside increased further.</p>
<p>"Five."</p>
<p>One recruit looked like he might throw up.</p>
<p>"Four."</p>
<p>Jax sighed.</p>
<p>"Three."</p>
<p>The crowd outside began chanting again.</p>
<p>"Two."</p>
<p>Bloodstain’s bloodline activated fully causing a dark crimson energy to wrap around his body.</p>
<p>"One."</p>
<p>There was a brief silence before...</p>
<p>"ZERO!"</p>
<p>BOOOOOOM!</p>
<p>Bloodstain unleashed a massive blood-based strike directly into the main gate.</p>
<p>The attack exploded forward, distorting the air and filling it with crimson energy.</p>
<p>DHIIIINNNNN~</p>
<p>The moment the attack hit... it deflected instantly.</p>
<p>The entire blast reversed direction causing Bloodstain’s eyes to widen in disbelief.</p>
<p>"Oh shi—"</p>
<p>BOOOOOOOOM!</p>
<p>The explosion swallowed him completely as the shockwave blasted outward.</p>
<p>Iron Vultures members were sent flying like bowling pins.</p>
<p>"Kiaaarrrhhhh!"</p>
<p>Loud screams rang out as several people crashed through vehicles while others rolled across the ground.</p>
<p>When the dust settled, Bloodstain was lying in a crater.</p>
<p>The Iron Vultures members stared in astonishment as Bloodstain slowly sat up with an absolutely murderous look on his face.</p>
<p>Embarrassment practically radiated from him as he climbed out of the crater and brushed dust from his shoulders, pretending like nothing happened.</p>
<p>Nobody dared laugh... they could only stare.</p>
<p>How could a small gang like Shadow Reapers have such sophisticated piece of technology as a defense mechanism.</p>
<p>Bloodstain coughed before pointing toward the gate. "A simple trick."</p>
<p>His voice sounded forced as he added. "How long will such tricks survive against true power?"</p>
<p>His eye twitched as he grabbed the nearest member by the collar. "What are you waiting for?!"</p>
<p>The poor man nearly died of fright.</p>
<p>"Attack!"</p>
<p>Immediately abilities began flying.</p>
<p>Fire... Ice... Wind... Summons... Projectiles... Explosions...</p>
<p>Hundreds of attacks crashed toward the defensive barriers.</p>
<p>At first many reflected causing several Iron Vultures members to suffer injuries from their own attacks...</p>
<p>But eventually the sheer volume became overwhelming. The barrier began absorbing damage instead.</p>
<p>Within the base, alarms sounded as the central AI system activated.</p>
<p>A calm female voice echoed throughout the compound.</p>
<p>"Warning."</p>
<p>"External attack detected."</p>
<p>"Defensive Shield Capacity: 91%."</p>
<p>Several recruits paled as another explosion shook the walls.</p>
<p>"Defensive Shield Capacity: 85%."</p>
<p>The atmosphere became more tense as the assault intensified.</p>
<p>The entire front section of the base glowed beneath continuous bombardment.</p>
<p>"Defensive Shield Capacity: 72%."</p>
<p>"We’re fucked!" Someone cursed while another recruit began pacing.</p>
<p>"Defensive Shield Capacity: 55%."</p>
<p>As the panic increased, one young recruit looked toward Jax. "Shouldn’t we do something?"</p>
<p>Jax scratched his cheek. "Probably."</p>
<p>"PROBABLY?"</p>
<p>"Relax."</p>
<p>"RELAX?!"</p>
<p>The recruit pointed toward the ceiling. "They’re trying to kill us!"</p>
<p>Another explosion shook the structure causing dust to fall from above.</p>
<p>The AI continued.</p>
<p>"Defensive Shield Capacity: 48%."</p>
<p>Several recruits visibly paled and even Vanya’s expression became serious.</p>
<p>Jax finally sighed while looking around. "Okay."</p>
<p>Everyone turned toward him while he turned towards Aria. "Where even is West?"</p>
<p>Aria frowned, about to dial West’s number again when a single glowing blue footprint appeared, right behind Jax.</p>
<p>Everyone stiffened as the footprint glowed brighter and then another appeared... until the room was filled with multiple glowing footprints.</p>
<p>The air warped and space twisted as blue light gathered.</p>
<p>Suddenly a figure materialized from nothing, right above one of the footprints.</p>
<p>Several recruits nearly screamed causing Jax to jump. "DAMN IT!"</p>
<p>Everyone turned and standing casually behind him was West with one hand in his pocket and the other holding a drink.</p>
<p>He looked completely calm and relaxed as though a hundred awakened weren’t currently trying to destroy his headquarters.</p>
<p>Jax glared. "You trying to give me a heart attack?"</p>
<p>West took a sip. "Possibly."</p>
<p>The recruits stared with looks of relief while others looked confused.</p>
<p>West glanced toward the status display.</p>
<p>"Defensive Shield Capacity: 43%."</p>
<p>He nodded. "Not bad."</p>
<p>Aria stared. "Not bad?!"</p>
<p>West smiled. "Bought us plenty of time. Didn’t even know it would work this effectively. Gotta thank Viktor properly next time I see him."</p>
<p>Outside another barrage struck causing the walls to shake again.</p>
<p>Bloodstain’s voice echoed across the compound. "WEST EINSTEIN!"</p>
<p>West cracked his neck before displaying a dangerous smile.</p>
<p>The kind his members had learned to recognize... the kind that usually meant someone else was about to have a terrible day.</p>
<p>He looked toward the gates and then toward his gathered members before shifting back to the display.</p>
<p>"Defensive Shield Capacity: 39%."</p>
<p>West’s smile widened. "Good."</p>
<p>Jax raised an eyebrow. "Good?"</p>
<p>West nodded. "Now they’re all gathered in one place."</p>
<p>A grin spread across West’s face. "Let’s go welcome our guests."</p>
<p>...</p>
<p>...</p>
<p>West stepped through the opened gates alone.</p>
<p>The massive reinforced doors behind him slowly rumbled shut again.</p>
<p>BOOOOOM!</p>
<p>The sound echoed throughout the surrounding and Immediately, all eyes landed on him.</p>
<p>Over a hundred awakened stared at the young man walking casually toward them with his hands in his pockets.</p>
<p>Many of them had never seen him before in person and their reactions showed his stunned they were.</p>
<p>"That’s him?"</p>
<p>"That’s West Einstein?"</p>
<p>"He’s a kid."</p>
<p>"That’s the Shadow Reapers leader?"</p>
<p>"He looks younger than my nephew."</p>
<p>Several Iron Vultures members exchanged confused glances.</p>
<p>This was the person who had supposedly run a top hundred gang into the ground?</p>
<p>This was the monster Bloodstain had spent weeks obsessing over?</p>
<p>The expressions on their faces only made him smile wider.</p>
<p>Bloodstain stepped forward with a face twisted in rage. "You finally decided to come out."</p>
<p>West tilted his head. "Funny."</p>
<p>"What?"</p>
<p>"I was about to ask what a big prestigious gang like Iron Vultures is doing outside the headquarters of a newly established gang."</p>
<p>Several nearby members shifted uncomfortably while Bloodstain’s eye twitched.</p>
<p>West continued. "Bullying the little guys?"</p>
<p>"Tsk."</p>
<p>"What a terrible look."</p>
<p>A few Iron Vultures members looked away because they couldn’t deny how ridiculous this looked.</p>
<p>A Misty High top hundred gang surrounding a rookie gang.</p>
<p>Bloodstain immediately pointed at him. "Stop pretending you don’t know why we’re here."</p>
<p>West blinked innocently. "Why are you here?"</p>
<p>The vein on Bloodstain’s forehead visibly bulged. "You know exactly what you did."</p>
<p>West shrugged. "I genuinely don’t."</p>
<p>The surrounding members began muttering.</p>
<p>Bloodstain growled. "You sabotaged my gang."</p>
<p>West laughed. "No."</p>
<p>"You robbed my facilities."</p>
<p>"No."</p>
<p>"You stole our resources."</p>
<p>"No."</p>
<p>"You attacked our teams."</p>
<p>"No."</p>
<p>"You ruined Iron Vultures."</p>
<p>"No."</p>
<p>"No."</p>
<p>"No."</p>
<p>"No."</p>
<p>Every denial came instantly without hesitation.</p>
<p>It was so shameless that several people almost admired it.</p>
<p>Bloodstain almost exploded seeing how West denied so casually.</p>
<p>West proceeced to point toward the gathered crowd. "Do you have proof?"</p>
