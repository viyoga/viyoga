<p>At 8:00 AM, West stood in front of a massive gated residence with lush greenery surrounding the property.</p>
<p>The surrounding walls were tall with security cameras mounted upon.</p>
<p>The entire structure was elegantly designed showing just how rich seasoned awakeners were. Renzhi wasn’t even the gang leader and yet he could afford this type of lifestyle.</p>
<p>West realized he still had a long way to go.</p>
<p>He adjusted the small fake moustache sitting neatly above his upper lip.</p>
<p>It wasn’t a big disguise but it was enough to slightly alter his appearance, leaving him still very attractive since this was exactly what he had on when he purposefully "accidentally" bumped into Mrs Lianhua Kuro.</p>
<p>He glanced at his reflection briefly in the mirror-smooth gate surface.</p>
<p>"...Good enough."</p>
<p>The gate opened shortly after and he stepped in.</p>
<p>The surroundings was breathtaking with a long driveway lined with perfectly trimmed hedges led to a grand mansion. The building itself was modern yet luxurious with large glass panels, marble finishes, and wonderful architecture that screamed elite wealth.</p>
<p>Water features decorated the front with soft flowing fountains that existed just to remind you that money wasn’t a concern here.</p>
<p>West was led inside by a maid and he noticed that the interior was even more impressive.</p>
<p>The ceilings were high with chandeliers and smooth marble floors.</p>
<p>The funiture were on the minimalist side but every piece looked expensive and refined.</p>
<p>"This way, sir," the maid said politely.</p>
<p>West followed calmly until they arrived at a spacious indoor training area which was fully equipped with top-tier fitness machines.</p>
<p>There were weights and an open space with mirrors lining the walls.</p>
<p>At the center stood Renzhi’s wife, Mrs Lianhua Kuro.</p>
<p>She was dressed in form-fitting workout attire that hugged her figure perfectly. Her body was toned, elegant, with just enough softness to make it captivating.</p>
<p>Her hair was tied back neatly with a few loose strands falling in front of her face.</p>
<p>She turned as West entered and for a moment, her eyes lingered.</p>
<p>"Oh..."</p>
<p>A faint smile formed.</p>
<p>"Welcome. You’re right on time."</p>
<p>West gave a polite nod.</p>
<p>"Time is of the essence."</p>
<p>He answered calmly.</p>
<p>"I’ll be working with you from today."</p>
<p>She stepped closer, subtly observing him once more.</p>
<p>"...Are you sure you’re 27? Now that I take a closer look at you, you seem much younger than that."</p>
<p>West smiled faintly.</p>
<p>"I get that a lot. Being fit helps to keep you looking young and healthy."</p>
<p>Her lips curved slightly.</p>
<p>"I’ll be looking forward to our sessions."</p>
<p>West nodded and they proceeded to start immediately without wasting any more time.</p>

<p>His eyes stayed attentive and observant as he demonstrated the movements while she followed his instructions.</p>
<p>West stepped closer occasionally, adjusting her posture and guiding her form.</p>
<p>"Relax your shoulders..."</p>
<p>"Good..."</p>
<p>"...that’s it."</p>
<p>His voice dropped slightly when he spoke with a low tone and every correction came with a subtle compliment.</p>
<p>"Your flexibility is impressive."</p>
<p>She blinked slightly.</p>
<p>"...Thank you."</p>
<p>A faint blush appeared on her face every time and West took note of it.</p>
<p>They moved to more active drills ranging from squats to core work to light resistance...</p>
<p>Each time she completed a set, he acknowledged it.</p>
<p>"Perfect form."</p>
<p>"...You’re doing better than most beginners."</p>
<p>"Your stamina is surprisingly good."</p>
<p>Each word was carefully chosen and delivered naturally.</p>
<p>He wasn’t excessive with the compliments, he offered just enough to keep things smooth.</p>
<p>Her breathing grew heavier as the session progressed and sweat formed lightly along her skin, making her look even hotter and glamorous.</p>

<p>Lianhua movements slowed slightly as she started getting fatigued but she didn’t stop.</p>
<p>And every time she pushed through, West was there, encouraging her.</p>
<p>"Good."</p>
<p>"Keep going."</p>
<p>"You’ve got this."</p>

<p>"I could say the same about a good student."</p>
