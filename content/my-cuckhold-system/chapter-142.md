<p>SPLASH!</p>
<p>Bright red paint exploded across the ground as it slammed down.</p>
<p>West and Nina both froze.</p>
<p>"...Did that just..."</p>
<p>"...Become real?"</p>
<p>They stared at the spilled paint in shock.</p>
<p>Nina slowly looked down at the brush in her hand.</p>
<p>"...I can draw things into reality?"</p>
<p>West let out a low whistle.</p>
<p>"That’s..."</p>
<p>He shook his head.</p>
<p>"...ridiculously powerful."</p>
<p>Even Jax’s Glacial Sovereign abilities weren’t this absurd.</p>
<p>Reality creation through drawing? Looks like three branches awakened were always on another level compared to other awakeners.</p>
<p>It made sense why they’d vye over him even when they didn’t know he had two branches.</p>
<p>If and when gangs finally discovered Nina, there was no doubt that every single one of them would try their very best to recruit her...</p>
<p>The potential was insane.</p>
<p>Nina looked equally stunned.</p>
<p>"...I think I got something crazy."</p>
<p>West grinned.</p>
<p>"Looks like it."</p>
<p>---</p>
<p>Over the next several days, their routine settled into a rhythm of training after school and work.</p>
<p>West trained with Aurethia, Jax practiced his ice abilities against Gor’thala and Nina learned to control her strange brush power.</p>
<p>The more she experimented, the more they realized how flexible the ability was.</p>
<p>She could draw objects, tools, materials and even weapons.</p>
<p>Each drawing temporarily became real but she noticed that the more sophisticated a drawing was, the more energy she expended.</p>
<p>A bucket of paint wasn’t anything special but when she drew a blazing saber, she felt the toll.</p>
<p>One evening, as they rested after training, West leaned back against a broken wall.</p>
<p>"Well," he said casually.</p>
<p>"We’ve got three members now."</p>
<p>Jax nodded.</p>
<p>"Which means?"</p>
<p>West smiled.</p>
<p>"Which means we can finally make it official."</p>
<p>"A gang."</p>
<p>Nina’s eyes lit up.</p>
<p>"Seriously?"</p>
<p>"Yeah."</p>
<p>West had already begun making inquiries through awakened networks.</p>
<p>Apparently, forming an awakened gang required a few steps.</p>
<p>First, they had to register officially which would also require some grant and a few other steps that West felt wouldn’t be a problem.</p>
<p>Second, they needed recommendations from two established gang leaders.</p>
<p>If they succeeded, they would officially become an Awakened Gang.</p>
<p>That meant access to:</p>
<p>• Awakened Battle arts halls</p>
<p>• Training facilities</p>
<p>• Ruin expedition permits</p>
<p>• Official awakened resources</p>
<p>West looked toward the skyline of the city with ambition gleaming in his eyes.</p>
<p>Step by step, he was making progress...</p>
<p>Soon, the world of awakened gangs would learn a new name.</p>
<p>...</p>
<p>...</p>
<p>On Thursday, West walked along the busy road carrying two grocery bags while massive digital billboards flickered above the buildings, displaying the same type of news that had become increasingly common.</p>
<p>Awakened news...</p>
<p>Ruins...</p>
<p>Power...</p>
<p>Danger...</p>
<p>Opportunity.</p>
<p>The large screen mounted above a shopping complex shifted to a live broadcast. The logo of a global news network appeared before the camera focused on a well-dressed reporter standing in front of a research institute.</p>
<p>"Recent global statistics have shown that the number of ruins appearing around the world has increased by nearly fifteen percent in the past year alone."</p>
<p>A graph appeared beside her on the screen, displaying the alarming rise.</p>
<p>Pedestrians slowed down to watch while West glanced up briefly before continuing to walk.</p>
<p>The reporter continued.</p>
<p>"Experts are still unsure what is causing this sudden surge, but many believe it is connected to the mysterious force responsible for the awakening phenomenon."</p>
<p>The screen shifted again.</p>
<p>Now it displayed a man standing calmly in front of several microphones.</p>
<p>Even people who didn’t follow awakened politics knew who he was.</p>
<p>One of the most powerful awakened alive... a global celebrity.</p>
<p>A man whose true tier remained unknown.</p>
<p>West recognized him immediately to be the man known as King Fisher. When you reached a particular level of power, no-one regarded you by your birth name anymore... there was always a sort of nickname like a title of power that the public bestowed upon you.</p>
<p>The man spoke in a calm, composed voice.</p>
<p>"We are currently living in a time where the concentration of the energy responsible for awakenings appears to be increasing."</p>
<p>His gaze remained steady as cameras flashed.</p>
<p>"Our researchers are still studying the phenomenon."</p>
<p>He paused briefly before continuing.</p>
<p>"We know very little about the origin of the ruins or the powers that have begun appearing in our world."</p>
<p>Another flash of cameras.</p>
<p>"But one thing is certain."</p>
<p>He folded his hands behind his back.</p>
<p>"The source of these awakenings is becoming more active."</p>
<p>"We intend to find out why."</p>
<p>The broadcast ended as the screen switched to another news segment.</p>
<p>West shifted the grocery bags in his hands and kept walking.</p>
<p>The city traffic roared around him as people continued their daily routines.</p>
<p>But the news about ruins and awakenings followed everyone everywhere now.</p>
<p>Another large street screen flickered ahead but this time the headline was different.</p>
<p>BREAKING AWAKENED NEWS</p>
<p>West raised an eyebrow slightly as he approached.</p>
<p>The reporter on screen looked almost excited.</p>
<p>"Today we have shocking news regarding the Eternal Ruins incident that occurred this past weekend."</p>
<p>Several pedestrians stopped walking while West slowed slightly.</p>
<p>"Sources have confirmed that a mysterious awakened individual cleared the entire third layer of the Eternal Ruins alone."</p>
<p>Gasps rippled through the small crowd watching the screen.</p>
<p>"Even more surprising..."</p>
<p>The reporter leaned slightly toward the camera.</p>
<p>"This awakened has reportedly become the newest apprentice of the famous gang leader—Miss Zu Li of the Dragon Fang Gang."</p>
<p>The screen displayed an image of Zu Li.</p>
<p>Her cold expression and long beautiful hair gave her an intimidating presence even through a digital display.</p>
<p>West resisted the urge to sigh while the reporter continued.</p>
<p>"According to rumors, the mysterious apprentice possesses three awakened branches."</p>
<p>The crowd exploded into chatter.</p>
<p>"Three branches?!"</p>
<p>"That’s impossible!"</p>
<p>"I heard there hasn’t been an awakened with three branches in over a century!"</p>
<p>West adjusted his groceries and quietly continued walking.</p>
<p>People around him were already discussing the rumor loudly.</p>
<p>"Did you hear that? Three branches!"</p>
<p>"Who is this guy?"</p>
<p>"They’re saying he’s still really young!"</p>
<p>"If Zu Li picked him up, he must be a monster."</p>
