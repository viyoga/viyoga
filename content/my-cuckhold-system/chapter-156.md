<p>West nodded calmly.</p>
<p>"We recently awakened."</p>
<p>"That’s common around this time of year," Halden replied.</p>
<p>He pulled up the registration requirements on his screen.</p>
<p>"First, we need confirmation that all founding members are awakened individuals."</p>
<p>The three of them each placed their hands on a small scanning device.</p>
<p>The machine made soft beeping sounds and a moment later the results appeared on Halden’s monitor.</p>
<p>He raised his eyebrows slightly.</p>
<p>"All three confirmed."</p>
<p>"Good."</p>
<p>"Next requirement."</p>
<p>He tapped the screen again.</p>
<p>"A gang must begin with a minimum of three founding members."</p>
<p>He glanced at them.</p>
<p>"You meet that requirement."</p>
<p>Jax leaned back in his chair slightly.</p>
<p>"So far so good."</p>
<p>Halden continued.</p>
<p>"Next is financial verification."</p>
<p>"A new gang must show a minimum operational fund of $100,000."</p>
<p>West pulled out his phone.</p>
<p>"No problem."</p>
<p>He transferred the funds immediately and the terminal beeped.</p>
<p>Halden looked at the confirmation.</p>
<p>"Payment received."</p>
<p>He nodded.</p>
<p>"Now we move on to the official registration."</p>
<p>He opened a form.</p>
<p>"Name of gang?"</p>
<p>West had been thinking about this for a long time, so without hesitation, he answered.</p>
<p>"Shadow Reapers."</p>
<p>Halden typed the name while Jax grinned slightly.</p>
<p>"That sounds badass."</p>
<p>Nina nodded approvingly.</p>
<p>"Yeah... I like it."</p>
<p>Halden finished entering the name and then looked back at West.</p>
<p>"Now we reach the final requirement."</p>
<p>His tone shifted slightly.</p>
<p>"For a new gang to be recognized, you must provide two recommendations from existing gang leaders."</p>
<p>Jax and Nina both stiffened slightly.</p>
<p>They hadn’t known about that part.</p>
<p>Halden folded his hands.</p>
<p>"Most new gangs struggle here."</p>
<p>"Without connections to existing organizations, this requirement can delay approval for months."</p>
<p>But before he could continue, West calmly slid two documents across the desk.</p>
<p>"Here you go."</p>
<p>Halden blinked while picking them up.</p>
<p>Then his eyebrows slowly rose higher and higher as he read.</p>
<p>Jax leaned toward West and whispered.</p>
<p>"...Wait."</p>
<p>"You actually had those?"</p>
<p>Nina looked equally shocked while Halden looked up at West again.</p>
<p>"You’re eighteen?"</p>
<p>"Yes."</p>
<p>"And you already have two gang leader recommendations?"</p>
<p>West shrugged.</p>
<p>"I’ve met a few people."</p>
<p>Halden looked down again at the documents.</p>
<p>The first one made his eyes widen.</p>
<p>"Recommendation from..."</p>
<p>"...Zu Li?"</p>
<p>He looked up sharply.</p>
<p>"The leader of Dragon Fang Gang?"</p>
<p>Even Jax nearly choked.</p>
<p>"WAIT WHAT?!"</p>
<p>Nina stared at West.</p>
<p>"You know her?!"</p>
<p>West rubbed the back of his neck slightly.</p>
<p>"We met once."</p>
<p>That was a massive understatement since they had bumped into each other multiple times due to Zu Li’s obsession.</p>
<p>Halden shook his head slowly.</p>
<p>"That’s... quite impressive."</p>
<p>He then looked at the second recommendation.</p>
<p>This one surprised him in a different way.</p>
<p>"Iron Chain Gang."</p>
<p>He glanced back up.</p>
<p>"Not one of the major organizations, but still an established street gang."</p>
<p>Halden leaned back slightly.</p>
<p>"You’re full of surprises, Mr. West."</p>
<p>West didn’t reply.</p>
<p>Instead, his mind drifted briefly to the moment he obtained that second recommendation.</p>
<p>---</p>
<p>He recalled two days ago when he visited the Iron Chain Gang territory.</p>
<p>It wasn’t exactly a pleasant place.</p>
<p>There were old factories, broken roads and graffiti everywhere.</p>
<p>West had walked directly into their territory alone.</p>
<p>Naturally, that attracted attention.</p>
<p>Several gang members surrounded him quickly and one of them stepped forward.</p>
<p>It was a youngster no older than 19 with dark hair and a scar across his cheek.</p>
<p>"Raze."</p>
<p>"What are you doing here?" Raze looked annoyed.</p>
<p>"Didn’t we already try recruiting you like five times?"</p>
<p>West nodded.</p>
<p>"Yes."</p>
<p>"And you rejected every offer."</p>
<p>Raze crossed his arms.</p>
<p>"So why are you here now?"</p>
<p>West got straight to the point.</p>
<p>"I need a recommendation."</p>
<p>Raze stared at him.</p>
<p>"...For what?"</p>
<p>"To start my own gang."</p>
<p>There was a brief silence before Raze burst out laughing.</p>
<p>"You serious?"</p>
<p>He shook his head.</p>
<p>"You might be talented."</p>
<p>"But you’re a Tier 1 awakener who just awakened recently."</p>
<p>"That’s suicidal."</p>
<p>West simply stood there calmly before answering. "That’s my decision to make. I’d like to speak to your boss."</p>
<p>After a few minutes of back and forth discussion, Raze sighed.</p>
<p>"...Fine."</p>
<p>"I’ll take you to the boss."</p>
<p>About a minute later, West found himself standing before the Iron Chain leader...</p>
<p>This man was... massive.</p>
<p>He was nearly seven feet tall, with arms thick as tree trunks.</p>
<p>When West explained his request, the man laughed loudly several times before leaning forward.</p>
<p>"Alright."</p>
<p>"I’ll give you the recommendation."</p>
<p>Everyone in the room looked shocked but the giant man raised one finger.</p>
<p>"There’s a condition."</p>
<p>West already knew what was coming.</p>
<p>"You owe me one."</p>
<p>"One day..."</p>
<p>"...I will collect that favor."</p>
<p>West nodded.</p>
<p>"Fair."</p>
<p>The man grinned.</p>
<p>"That’s how the awakened world works, kid."</p>
<p>"Nothing comes free."</p>
<p>He leaned back in his chair.</p>
<p>"If you want to survive out there..."</p>
<p>"...learn to use others."</p>
<p>"Or you’ll end up being used."</p>
<p>West remembered those words clearly.</p>
<p>---</p>
<p>~ Back To The Present ~</p>
<p>West’s thoughts returned to the bureau office as Halden finished reviewing the documents.</p>
<p>"Well."</p>
<p>"Everything seems to be in order."</p>
<p>He typed a few more entries into the system.</p>
<p>Then he printed a document and slid it across the desk.</p>
<p>"Congratulations."</p>
<p>"You have successfully submitted the registration for the gang..."</p>
<p>"Shadow Reapers."</p>
<p>Jax grinned widely while Nina looked excited.</p>
<p>West simply nodded calmly as Halden continued.</p>
<p>"However, approval isn’t immediate."</p>
<p>"The review process usually takes two to three weeks."</p>
<p>"During that time, your documentation and recommendations will be verified."</p>
<p>"If everything checks out..."</p>
<p>"...your gang will officially be recognized."</p>
<p>West stood up.</p>
<p>"Understood."</p>
<p>They left the bureau building together as the afternoon sun began to set.</p>
<p>Jax stretched his arms.</p>
<p>"Man..."</p>
<p>"That actually worked."</p>
<p>Nina smiled.</p>
<p>"Shadow Reapers..."</p>
<p>"I like the sound of that."</p>
<p>West looked up at the skyline.</p>
<p>In two or three weeks, everything would change... and the real journey would finally begin.</p>
<p>For now, he had to get ready for his graduation tomorrow.</p>
<p>...</p>
<p>...</p>
<p>That night, he briefed Aria on the current progress through chats and she congratulated him non stop.</p>
<p>"Have you thought about where the gang base will be located?" She asked West.</p>
<p>"I have something in mind..."</p>
