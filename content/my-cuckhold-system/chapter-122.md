<p>"Right," West said to Gor’thala.</p>
<p>She nodded but her eyes lingered on him with a look of concern.</p>
<p>"You should not move alone."</p>
<p>"I’ll be fine," West stated but Gor’thala wasn’t having it.</p>
<p>She extended one hand and the air warped as two massive shapes began forming from swirling green flame on the floor.</p>
<p>They appeared in the next instant and it was none other than the Verdant Pyre Behemoths.</p>
<p>Each was colossal—twice the size of a mammoth.</p>
<p>Dense muscle rippled beneath thick hide that looked scorched yet alive.</p>
<p>Green flames burned steadily around their massive paws, branding the crystalline ground with smoldering marks that hissed and crackled.</p>
<p>From each burning imprint—</p>
<p>Gnarled roots burst forth instantly, writhing like living serpents before retracting slowly into the stone.</p>
<p>Their eyes burned with intelligent green infernos.</p>
<p>Three massive tails lashed behind each beast, ending in spear-like tips that gleamed ominously.</p>
<p>They lowered their heads slightly toward West in acknowledgment.</p>
<p>Gor’thala spoke calmly.</p>
<p>"They will guard you."</p>
<p>West nodded once.</p>
<p>"Thanks."</p>
<p>Telepathic threads connected him to both Gor’thala and Seraphyra so if either found the missing individuals, he would know instantly.</p>
<p>Without further hesitation, they moved...</p>
<p>Seraphyra had taken the left, Gor’thala the right path while West stepped into the center passage with the two Behemoths flanking him like moving fortresses.</p>
<p>The cave narrowed initially, then gradually expanded.</p>
<p>Blue veins pulsed faintly through the walls like arteries beneath translucent stone.</p>
<p>Behind them, silence reclaimed the intersection.</p>
<p>However, high above the cavern wall where a rough spiky rock protruded downward like a fang, something moved.</p>
<p>A drop of viscous greenish substance formed at its tip and fell.</p>
<p>The droplet hit the ground below without sound.</p>
<p>Instead of splattering, it rippled and expanded.</p>
<p>The surface undulated like disturbed water and then it began reshaping itself.</p>
<p>Elongated limbs extruded as a torso formed from the transforming mass.</p>
<p>A featureless head rose last with indentations slowly forming where eyes might be.</p>
<p>The green mass stabilized into a strange creature that was semi-translucent with its body constantly changing slightly, like it could never fully decide on its shape.</p>
<p>It turned slowly, facing the center path where West had gone.</p>
<p>Then it began moving... gliding silently toward the middle cave.</p>
<p>...</p>
<p>...</p>
<p>~ Outside The Ruins ~</p>
<p>The phone rang three times before someone picked up.</p>
<p>Inside a high-rise building in Red Valor City, a man in a black shirt with a crimson insignia on the chest leaned back in his chair with his boots resting on a metal desk scarred by years of weapon cleaning.</p>
<p>"Crimson Talon Syndicate," he answered lazily. "State your business."</p>
<p>On the other end of the line, a voice trembled slightly.</p>
<p>"This is security from the Eternal Ruins perimeter. We are requesting immediate awakened assistance."</p>
<p>The man blinked in confusion and then laughed.</p>
<p>"Is this a prank call?"</p>
<p>"No, sir. We have—"</p>
<p>"The Eternal Ruins?" he interrupted. "The tourist site that’s been fully cleared for over a century?"</p>
<p>"Yes, but—"</p>
<p>"That ruin was sterilized long before I was born," he scoffed. "We don’t respond to ghost stories."</p>
<p>"This is not a joke!" the guard snapped, losing composure. "Multiple individuals have gone missing. There are tremors. We suspect an undiscovered section—"</p>
<p>"Impossible."</p>
<p>The representative sat upright now.</p>
<p>"You’re telling me the most studied ruin in Red Valor City suddenly decided to grow a new floor?"</p>
<p>"We are seeing activity beneath the second layer!"</p>
<p>"That’s not how ruins work."</p>
<p>"They are shifting!"</p>
<p>Silence.</p>
<p>Then a sharp exhale from the Crimson Talon rep.</p>
<p>"If this is someone’s attempt to bait our resources, I’ll personally track the call origin and—"</p>
<p>The guard cut him off.</p>
<p>"Call whoever you need to. But if you ignore this and something surfaces inside city limits, that’s on you."</p>
<p>The line went dead.</p>
<p>The representative stared at the phone for several seconds.</p>
<p>Then muttered, "Idiots."</p>
<p>But he didn’t look entirely convinced.</p>
<p>---</p>
<p>Crimson Talon wasn’t the only gang receiving a call.</p>
<p>Across Red Valor City, in a dimly lit underground bar serving as headquarters for the Iron Howl Brotherhood, another phone rang.</p>
<p>In a rooftop office overlooking the industrial district, the Silver Crescent Union received the same warning.</p>
<p>Inside an abandoned train depot converted into a fortified base, members of the Graveflare Assembly paused mid-training to listen as their communications officer relayed identical information.</p>
<p>Eternal Ruins... Tremors... Missing persons... Possible undiscovered layer.</p>
<p>Some dismissed it outright and some cursed the guards for incompetence... but not all.</p>
<p>Inside a heavily reinforced estate on the eastern side of Red Valor City, a man in his early thirties stood before a large holographic map projected across a circular war table.</p>
<p>He wore a long dark coat with subtle silver lining and his hair was tied back neatly.</p>
<p>His presence commanded silence even when he wasn’t speaking.</p>
<p>A subordinate approached cautiously.</p>
<p>"Boss... we just received a call from the Eternal Ruins perimeter."</p>
<p>The man didn’t look up immediately.</p>
<p>"Another tourist scuffle?"</p>
<p>"No, sir. They’re claiming there is movement beneath the second layer. Tremors. Missing people."</p>
<p>Hearing this, made him pause.</p>
<p>Slowly, he raised his head.</p>
<p>"What did you say?"</p>
<p>"They suspect an undiscovered section."</p>
<p>The room grew still as the man’s eyes sharpened.</p>
<p>"Repeat that."</p>
<p>"They believe there may be a third layer."</p>
<p>There was a brief silence before his lips parted slightly...</p>
<p>"So it’s true?"</p>
<p>The subordinate blinked.</p>
<p>"Sir?"</p>
<p>"My father always suspected there was a third layer," he said quietly. "He spent years studying geological inconsistencies in the Eternal Ruins. Everyone called him insane. Said the site was fully mapped. Fully cleared."</p>
<p>His eyes glinted now.</p>
<p>"After all this time... it has finally appeared."</p>
<p>He turned sharply.</p>
<p>"Gear up."</p>
<p>The room instantly shifted into motion.</p>
<p>"We’re heading to the Eternal Ruins."</p>
<p>This was the headquarters of the Obsidian Vow.</p>
<p>And unlike the others... they weren’t hesitating.</p>
<p>---</p>
<p>Within the hour, vehicles began moving across Red Valor City.</p>
<p>Black armored vans bearing the insignia of Crimson Talon... Motorcycles from Iron Howl... An unmarked convoy belonging to Silver Crescent.</p>
<p>Graveflare members moving quietly in civilian transport... and Obsidian Vow’s heavily equipped team leading the surge.</p>
<p>Some gangs were going to confirm whether it was a hoax while others were going because if it wasn’t, they did not intend to arrive late to a newly discovered ruin layer.</p>
<p>Word spread fast in awakened circles, and although, it looked like a rumor... it was potential fortune.</p>
<p>And fortune meant war.</p>
<p>---</p>
<p>Meanwhile — Beneath the Ruins</p>
<p>Seraphyra glided effortlessly through her assigned path with her serpentine body coiling elegantly around sharp turns.</p>
<p>Despite the oppressive cold and alien architecture, she seemed thoroughly entertained.</p>
<p>"Ohhh," she murmured playfully, lifting something from a wall with her tail. "Master will like this."</p>
