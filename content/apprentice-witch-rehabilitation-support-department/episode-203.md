<p>"Silence is basic etiquette in an art gallery."</p>

<p>"You must be well-educated. I'll forgive you, so try checking your voice."<br>"...Ah, aah. Thank you."</p>

<p>"Thanks? How amusing. A guy who bows his head even while getting his money back."</p>

<p>But if that's the case, isn't the witch being not so quiet herself right now?<br>More than that, the other spectators around us are having quiet chats at about this level too.</p>

<p>"Judging by the looks of it, you must be the one. The gossip topic among brats these days."<br>"...Are you talking to me?"</p>

<p>"Annoying thing. Who else is here besides you? That cow-like girl over there?"<br>"……."</p>

<p>She kept her eyes fixed on the work before her instead of me, and asked a question.<br>I belatedly looked at Orca, but she hadn't seemed to dispel her magic yet, merely pointing at her throat with her hand and making an embarrassed expression.<br>Since it seemed only I could speak for the moment, I eventually finished a light bow and replied.</p>

<p>"...You flatter me. I just got lucky and became a topic of conversation.... Ah, I'm Nam Do-won."<br>"Eden Ineba. There's no need to bother remembering it. Our connection will be nothing more than a passing encounter."</p>

<p>"Ahaha...."<br>"Still, it's curious how different you look from what I imagined. Those punk bastards usually share a certain common look."</p>

<p>She's definitely far from demure.<br>Suppressing that stray thought, I just diligently nodded my head.</p>

<p>"It wasn't me who was chattering away in front of the fragment, but the two of you."<br>"...I'm sorry."</p>

<p>"I have a mountain of things I want to see, so I don't want to waste any more time standing here."</p>

<p>Since I didn't want to be scolded any longer, I gladly saw her off as well.<br>It seemed she really didn't have any separate business with me after all, as Eden faded away into the crowd.</p>
