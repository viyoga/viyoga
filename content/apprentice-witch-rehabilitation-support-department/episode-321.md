<p>- Dorothy Showcase Fancam, 1 Million Views in Just 3 Hours... "Her Face Is the Genre"</p>

<p>- [Reporter's Notebook] Rubia, Attention Focused Solely on Leader Dorothy's Visuals... Concerns of a 'One-Man Team' Also Raised</p>

<p>- Ad Industry Love Calls Just One Day After Debut?... Industry Attention Concentrated on Dorothy</p>

<p>.<br>.<br>.<br>.</p>

<p>Dorothy's debut showcase, which I was earnestly told I absolutely had to watch, ended successfully.</p>

<p>Before it got any later, I returned home and spent a while just browsing entertainment news plastered with Dorothy's face.</p>

<p>I've never once looked up the news myself in my life, but it was kinda fun since a familiar face popped up.<br>And just Dorothy's face itself is the kind you can just stare at and lose track of time.</p>

<p>Maybe that's why entertainment articles were pouring in by the bucketload.<br>But everyone was saying pretty much the same thing.<br>All the members are pretty, but among them, the leader Dorothy is in a league of her own.<br>Looks like they prepared hardcore. But Dorothy is pretty.<br>Especially Dorothy, she's fucking gorgeous.<br>I thought the editing was way too heavy at the pre-release, but in person she's also insane-that-makes-no-sense-fucking-gorgeous and so on and so forth...</p>

<p>In personal showcase reviews rather than news, there was a lot of praise for the debut song too, but eventually, as if promised, they all moved on to praising, marveling at, worshipping, and declaring Dorothy's looks as their ultimate bias after talking about the music.</p>

<p>Well, Dorothy isn't an ordinary human but a witch apprentice hiding her identity, so I guess it's an inevitable phenomenon.</p>

<p>"……."</p>

<p>It is a bit of a foul, just as Orca pointed out earlier.<br>A trainee witch butting into a game of house where humans compare who's more attractive.<br>It's no different from a boxing match between mismatched weight classes...</p>

<p>Perhaps that's why there were some articles likening Dorothy's appearance to a witch, but fortunately, no one has seriously suspected or tried to dig into it yet.</p>

<p>This, too, is because as Orca pointed out, the debate ends the moment someone counters with, 'Why would a witch do something like this?'</p>

<p>The public image of a witch is simply women crazy about magic.<br>The logic goes: why would magic-philes, whose only reason for helping humans dispose of beasts is magic, waste their time on idol activities?<br>It's not like they gain anything from it, either.</p>

<p>...The fact that there's something to gain,<br>is a secret known only to a very few, including myself.</p>

<p>"Wow, a lot of photos were taken..."</p>

<p>Even as I scrolled down through the articles, Dorothy's photos, which I hadn't seen before, kept pouring out endlessly.<br>Were there that many reporters who visited the scene?<br>As I looked more closely, there were also many photos where the surrounding situation was subtly different, as if taken at different times.</p>

<p>I'm not sure, but maybe there was a separate time to talk with reporters before the showcase....<br>It's not important, so I roughly guessed and moved on.</p>

<p>Before I knew it, it was midnight, time to get some sleep for tomorrow.</p>

<p>- Brrrrrr</p>

<p>A call came in late at night.</p>

<p>"No way..."</p>

<p>Sure enough, the name displayed on the screen was the one I predicted the moment I heard the vibration sound.<br>Dorothy Kihased.</p>

<p>I heard that towards the end of the showcase, there was a separate impression broadcast on the fan community afterwards. Since it's so late, that must be all over now.<br>Starting from signing up, there were various things needed, so I couldn't check that far... but it'll probably be fine.</p>

<p>I, who had been lying down comfortably until now, deliberately sat up and answered the phone.</p>

<p>"Hello?"<br>- Are you busy?</p>

<p>"No. I'm not usually busy at this hour."<br>- I was wondering if you might be charging mana with another apprentice witch. If so, is it okay to talk on the phone with me right now?</p>

<p>"Sure. As long as you want."<br>- Good.</p>

<p>"Looks like you're resting now that everything's done."<br>- That's right. I'm in the room you came into directly last time. The room where Nam Do-won pinned me down in the stifling blankets and came inside me over and over, *thrust, thud*.</p>

<p>"...What if another member accidentally hears you talking like that?"<br>- It's fine. Unlike that day when the magic briefly malfunctioned, my illusion magic is perfect right now.</p>

<p>Lately, Dorothy's been so busy we barely exchanged messages.<br>It's been a really long time since I heard her voice.</p>

<p>Instead of the frail voice I heard last time, Dorothy continues the call in the stiff, monotone voice I know well.</p>

<p>- Anyway, I'll give you a question.<br>"...Huh?"</p>

<p>- A question. I'll give you one.<br>"That thing you mentioned last time, are you really doing it right now?"</p>

<p>- If you watched the live stream, they're things you can answer easily. And if it becomes tomorrow, your memory will get fuzzy, won't it.<br>"I don't think that's wrong, but..."</p>

<p>She unilaterally started the test.</p>

<p>- At the start of the showcase, a simple profile photo of me would have come out. At that time, what outfit was I wearing?<br>"It was white, with pink as an accent color... and I think it was a crop top that showed the stomach."</p>

<p>- Correct. It seems you did at least watch it.<br>"To be honest, I didn't watch it with total focus, but I did watch it all the way to the end. ...Ah, I didn't watch the review broadcast, though."</p>

<p>- Is that so. Then I'll have to exclude a few of the prepared questions.</p>

<p>...Did I really have to watch even that?</p>

<p>A cold sweat trickled down, but fortunately, judging by Dorothy's reaction, it didn't seem to be mandatory.<br>Though it's not totally okay. More like a 'use your head and do well' kind of vibe.</p>

<p>After this call ends, I'd better hurry up and sign up even then.</p>

<p>- Next question. Please tell me what our second song was.</p>

<p>It was the title track.<br>The long English title didn't roll off the tongue easily, but since I got it roughly right, I passed.</p>

<p>- Do you remember the greeting slogan? I won't ask about the movements in detail.</p>

<p>I couldn't get this one exactly right either, but I gave an answer worth about 8 out of 10 points.</p>

<p>...Dorothy then said that it might not seem like a big deal, but every time she does it, she hears a barrage of camera shutter sounds, so it's secretly embarrassing.</p>

<p>Seeming to understand how she felt, I let out a small laugh, and Dorothy, after grumbling a bit more, posed the next question.</p>

<p>- I made a small mistake during the choreography. Did you see it, Mr. Nam Do-won?<br>"A mistake? ...Hmm. I didn't get the feeling that anything went wrong at all."</p>

<p>- Is that really so?<br>"Uuuum... Wait a moment. Just give me a little time to think..."</p>

<p>A mistake, huh.<br>I wondered if there really was such a thing and racked my brain, but even so, nothing in particular came to mind.</p>

<p>...Besides, honestly speaking, the moment I watched most intently during the showcase was when Dorothy was in close-up.</p>

<p>I didn't get the feeling of 'That's definitely a mistake' either.<br>There wasn't even a sense of a slip-up.</p>

<p>A small mistake...</p>

<p>"I'm not sure. To put it bluntly, this doesn't seem like a problem that can be answered easily..."<br>- Hmph. It makes one wonder if you really watched properly.</p>

<p>"I, I really saw it properly. Especially the part Dorothy mentioned... well, since I'm a man. I watched it even more intently."<br>- ...Hmm.</p>

<p>"But I still don't know what mistake you made."</p>

<p>Dorothy falls silent for a moment.<br>The sound of rustling bedding is briefly heard over the receiver, and then she continues speaking.</p>

<p>- Was my appearance attractive enough for you to watch intently?<br>"……."</p>

<p>- If that's true... then at least I didn't waste my effort. That's a relief.</p>

<p>There was no particular sense of her reproaching him for failing to solve the problem.</p>

<p>Rather, even more warmly than before.<br>Dorothy speaks with a sound like pat-pat, tapping a blanket.</p>

<p>- Ah, for the record, the answer is 'I didn't make a mistake.'<br>"...Huh?"</p>

<p>- It means that Nam Do-won answering that he didn't know until the end was the correct answer. Well done.<br>"Th-thank you."</p>

<p>- To add an explanation, since I practiced the choreography and even saved it all with illusion magic, there is absolutely no chance I will make a mistake.<br>"...I guess that makes sense. You're right."</p>

<p>- Of course, we must consider the possibility of other members making mistakes, so we can't rely solely on illusion magic.</p>

<p>...It seems it was a trap question meant to pressure me and draw out a wrong answer after all.</p>

<p>- Then I'll ask the final question now.</p>

<p>You haven't gotten anything major wrong so far, and you've got just about everything right.<br>So I thought that proved well enough that I'd seen the showcase, but it looks like Dorothy wants to ask the last question she prepared after all.</p>

<p>It's a bit past bedtime, but it's not too late either.<br>Above all, it's been a while since we talked on the phone.</p>

<p>...It's kind of funny saying this to a witch, but.<br>I guess it's okay to just let her do whatever she wants for today at least.</p>

<p>- It's a slightly different tone from the questions asked before, but hmm.</p>

<p>After making a light rustling sound, Dorothy slowly added her next words.</p>

<p>- When the members said their goodbyes at the end, didn't I roughly say thank you to the fans?<br>"...The way you put it makes it sound like you weren't particularly grateful to the fans."</p>

<p>- That's actually the case, so what can I do. Anyway.<br>"……."</p>

<p>- The cheers from the scene at that time. And the applause. Did you hear them?<br>"...Yes."</p>

<p>I heard them.<br>The other members, of course, received plenty of cheers as well, but I remember the roar when it was Dorothy's turn being a bit louder.</p>

<p>That is why, acknowledging it calmly, Dorothy, after a brief pause, spat out the 'real question'.</p>

<p>- Without all those people knowing, I plan to try having 'bestiality' with Mr. Nam Do-won often from now on.<br>"……."</p>

<p>- How is it? Does this kind of thing suit your taste?</p>

<p>...A fan who became interested in Dorothy from the pre-promotion.<br>A fan who bought a ton of albums and came to the debut showcase.<br>And even the fans who will fall for Dorothy's looks and scrape together her photos from now on.</p>

<p>Making them all into fools,<br>Does that suit your taste?</p>

<p>"...It's fucking hot."<br>- Then that's a relief.</p>

<p>When I answered honestly, a short laugh was heard from the other end of the line.</p>

<p>...Not just anyone.<br>It was the laughter of Dorothy Kihased.<br>That rare sound.</p>
