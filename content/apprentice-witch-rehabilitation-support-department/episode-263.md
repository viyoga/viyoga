<p>It's been a full 17 years since I started working for a cleaning company exclusive to celebrities.<br>A cleaning lady who turned forty-five this year was having this thought as she chatted with a celebrity she happened to meet today.<br>…As expected, nothing beats a daughter, she thought.</p>

<p>"Oh my, you didn't have to go to the trouble of preparing even this."<br>"You might get thirsty while working."</p>

<p>"I brought my own water. Cold water."</p>

<p>Of course, most celebrities treated the cleaning lady kindly.<br>A few only went so far as to treat her like she wasn't there, but fortunately, she had no memory of being mistreated while doing this job.<br>Would it cause a huge scandal in this day and age? Or was it perhaps to spread good rumors around?<br>She didn't know the truth, but she had lived assuming it was roughly that.</p>

<p>Thanks to that, she was a cleaning lady accustomed to such kindness, but the part that moved her this time was.<br>Honestly speaking, just the appearance right before her eyes.</p>

<p>Since the trainee, who was said to be debuting soon, had an appearance that drew nothing but admiration, the cleaning lady had been struggling to manage her gaze for a while now.</p>

<p>In the case of celebrities relaxed at home, their beauty often collapses more than expected.<br>Still, it's an appearance that can't be compared to ordinary people, but that's only in a relative sense.<br>No matter how much of a celebrity they are, they can't live 24 hours a day all dolled up, can they?</p>

<p>But this trainee here, even as the cleaning lady stared blankly, not a trace of makeup could be found. None at all.<br>An appearance that made one wonder if it was really the same person kept catching the eye.</p>

<p>Even though she seemed to be resting because she was a bit sick, giving off an overall languid atmosphere.</p>

<p>"Besides, you look like you've caught a bad cold or something, so don't worry about me and just go inside and get some rest."<br>"I'm fine. I took some medicine."</p>

<p>"What if it gets worse..."</p>

<p>She did have a son of a similar age, but far from being filial, he was the kind of guy she worried wasn't even studying properly.<br>How nice it would have been to have a daughter this pretty and sharp.</p>

<p>The cleaning lady lowered her gaze, hoping her son would grow up quickly.</p>

<p>Since this is a service job, it's right to try to make sure the other person feels as little discomfort as possible.<br>This trainee has probably lived her whole life under these kinds of gazes and is used to it, but you never know.</p>

<p>"The general cleaning is all done, so please just go in and rest. Or should I run to the nearby mart and make you some honey water? It works wonders for a sore throat."<br>"...Ah, I appreciate the sentiment, but you really don't need to go to that much trouble."</p>

<p>"You're going to say it's fine because you took medicine again, right?"<br>"No. In a little while..., um..."</p>

<p>"……?"<br>"Anyway, I'm fine. A similar treatment is scheduled."</p>

<p>A similar treatment?<br>Did a member who finished their schedule promise to make honey water?<br>Not knowing for sure, the cleaning lady answered with a shrug of her shoulders.<br>Since the employer says it's fine, it would be strange for a subordinate to force the issue.</p>

<p>After checking the dorm condition one last time, the cleaning lady gathered her supplies and left the dorm.</p>

<p>Then, she took the elevator down and headed for the parking lot for a moment.</p>

<p>"Huh?"</p>

<p>Instead of going straight to the underground parking lot, the elevator stopped briefly on the first floor.</p>

<p>But even when I poked my head out to look, no one was visible in front of it.</p>

<p>"...Did they press the button for the elevator next door, too, and just take whichever one arrived first?"</p>

<p>But that didn't make sense, because the elevator next door wasn't operating or heading down to the parking lot with it; it just stayed idle on standby.</p>

<p>The cleaning lady, who didn't believe in ghosts, thought it must have just been a momentary glitch and headed for the underground parking lot again.</p>

<p>"……."</p>

<p>Only then did the button for the elevator right next to it light up.</p>

<p>*****</p>

<p>…That scared me.<br>I thought the elevator came down because I pressed the button, but I guess the timing just happened to overlap by chance.<br>I dodged to the side to avoid bumping into the woman, and only after seeing the elevator doors close again did I let out a sigh of relief.</p>

<p>Fortunately, it seems Dorothy's illusion magic is still working perfectly.<br>This time I was feeling sick from a cold, and when I came in I saw there were a lot of CCTV cameras around, so I wondered if it would really be okay.<br>Judging by the result, there doesn't seem to be any need to worry.</p>

<p>The other elevator that arrived late was fortunately completely empty.<br>I was going to just take the stairs if this looked risky too, but this works out well.<br>I got on and pressed the 6th floor button firmly, just as Dorothy had told me.</p>

<p>"Haa..."</p>

<p>I heard they moved to a better place recently, and there's even someone who looks like a cleaning lady coming and going.<br>I wonder how much that kind of service costs per visit.<br>While searching on my smartphone, I thought, 'What, it's cheaper than I thought?' and before I knew it, we arrived at the 6th floor.<br>She said it would be visible if I turned right right here...</p>

<p>Ah, found it.<br>Room 601.</p>

<p>"You've come."<br>"...Doesn't that sound like something a gangster would say?"</p>

<p>"It's a misunderstanding. I was just speaking politely."</p>

<p>I pressed the doorbell, and the front door opened immediately, as if someone had been waiting right there.<br>It's a common sight with Neria, but since it's Dorothy, it feels somehow different. Awkward, even.</p>

<p>Taking the doorknob, I carefully closed the door behind me and stepped inside before anyone could see.</p>

<p>"Hmm. By the way, you really did come wearing a mask, Mr. Nam Do-won."<br>"...I heard someone say to wear one so I don't pass on my cold."</p>

<p>"It's been a while, so it's a bit disappointing not to see your bare face. I suppose it can't be helped for the sake of hygiene and health, though."<br>"Then couldn't you just use illusion magic to paint a face over mine?"</p>

<p>"That's quite a splendid idea, but wouldn't that defeat the purpose? It would just be an illusion, after all."</p>

<p>Today's Dorothy looked exactly like a cold patient through and through.</p>

<p>Loose and roomy. Dorothy is wearing soft clothes that look like winter pajamas.<br>Under the cute pajamas that a little kid would like, thick pastel-toned sleep socks also caught the eye.<br>This, too, is a rather cute outfit that doesn't match her figure, like something a little kid would wear.</p>

<p>Whatever the case, thanks to her face being 'Dorothy,' she pulls off whatever she wears in the end.</p>

<p>"Don't just stand in the entryway; come in. Is what's in Nam Do-won's bag the flowers you mentioned this morning?"<br>"Ah, yes. I also heard how long to steep them for the best result. Shall I make it right away?"</p>

<p>"Sounds good."</p>

<p>Compared to the old dorm I saw once before, this place has definitely gotten bigger in many ways.<br>Whether the agency is paying more attention, the interior overall feels more polished.</p>

<p>But judging by the still-messy entryway, it seems a few of the same friends from last time remain among the members who've confirmed their debut.<br>I took off my shoes among the ones rolling freely on the floor and headed to the kitchen, guided by Dorothy.</p>

<p>What immediately caught my eye was an island table slightly protruding from the center of the kitchen.<br>Along with a small sink, neatly arranged salt, pepper, and even a mysterious Nuclear Fire Chicken sauce stood out.<br>Seeing as this is also tidily organized, it seems there's at least one person with that kind of personality among the members.</p>

<p>"You said the other members still have a long way to go on their schedules, right?"<br>"Yes. It will take at least three hours."</p>

<p>"That's plenty of time. Then..."</p>

<p>First, I'll try making the flower tea I received from Lady Mari. It would be best to bring up the story about Lady Lynn after that.<br>Having finished my plan, I picked up a coffee pot that was nearby.</p>

<p>"Is there anything I can help with?"<br>"You must be having a hard time with the body aches. Wouldn't it be best to just rest? If you overdo it, it could get worse."</p>

<p>"...Hmm."</p>

<p>At those perfectly reasonable words, Dorothy sat down, resting her chin on the table without any particular rebuttal.<br>While I placed my bag across from her and was handling the coffee pot, she spoke to me again.</p>

<p>"Nam Do-won."<br>"Yes."</p>

<p>"But right now, don't we look something like newlyweds?"<br>"……."</p>

<p>...That was a genuinely out-of-the-blue remark.</p>

<p>"By any chance, Lady Dorothy, are you saying that because you're reminded of when you helped Neria last time?"<br>"Are you talking about the wedding dress? Of course I remember, but I didn't say that with that in mind."</p>

<p>"But you got super excited about netorare sex last time too... Something's suspicious..."<br>"It's a misunderstanding. It seems a strange image has been formed somehow."</p>

<p>Well, you do weird things often, so it's only natural that kind of image would stick.</p>

<p>Bestiality play and all that, there was even a time you buried your face like a dog eating kibble while keeping that expressionless face.<br>You even committed public exposure in a park in the middle of the night, with illusion magic as a cheat key.<br>She's even the Witch who used condoms as props—completely unnecessary for a witch—for more realistic netorare sex.</p>

<p>So it's only natural to be suspected just for bringing up a newlywed-like story.</p>

<p>"Suspicious..."<br>"It's just that the thought suddenly occurred to me. Since we're on the subject, do you by any chance have a fetish for the term 'honey'?"</p>

<p>"I don't know. Probably not."<br>"Then how about we use this opportunity to test it out?"</p>

<p>"...Do as you please."<br>"Honey. Honey. Honey. ...How is it?"</p>

<p>"...I understand even less."<br>"Hmm. I guess you prefer ordering me to put a collar on my neck and call you Master."</p>

<p>"That's not it either... ...No, it's not that it isn't..."<br>"So you do prefer it."</p>

<p>"...Yes."</p>

<p>Unless they were actually doing something like a real married couple, saying this while waiting for the water with flowers to boil would only sound like a joke.</p>

<p>As she was responding with a troubled expression, Dorothy let the arm she had been resting her chin on slip down smoothly.</p>

<p>She rested her cheek on her collapsed forearm and lay prone, speaking after a brief silence.</p>

<p>"I like it. This atmosphere."<br>"……."</p>

<p>"Come to think of it, isn't it similar to the atmosphere when we lived together? Exchanging trivial small talk. Resting. That kind of atmosphere."<br>"...That's true."</p>

<p>"So, I strongly recommend that you, Nam Do-won, make an effort to develop similar tastes from now on."<br>"I'll keep that in mind, but a person's tastes don't change that easily..."</p>

<p>"They do. After all, didn't you completely change my tastes?"<br>"……."</p>

<p>"You made me into a woman with a weird taste, feeling loved by a handsome puppy."</p>

<p>Does it work like that.</p>

<p>I wanted to point out the expressions and vocabulary used, but unfortunately, the timer started ringing right on cue.<br>It means it's been steeped just enough by now, so finish it before it gets too strong.</p>

<p>Therefore.</p>

<p>"Here, it's still very hot, so cool it down well before drinking."<br>"Thank you for preparing this. Then..."</p>

<p>As Dorothy poured the flower tea into the cup with a trickling sound and took short sips to taste it.</p>

<p>An incident broke out.</p>

<p>- Beep, beep-beep, beep.</p>

<p>One of the members had returned to the lodging earlier than expected.</p>

<p>"...Didn't you say it would definitely take three hours?"<br>"Ah. It seems my memory was wrong."</p>

<p>"But the Witches definitely have pretty good memories..."<br>"Still, it's fine. I have my magic, don't I?"</p>

<p>Dorothy's words were right.<br>I knew her magical skill well, and trusted it, so even though things had gone a little awry, there was no anxiety.</p>

<p>I could just cover it up with an illusion, finish what I was doing, and then go back.</p>

<p>That's why I was resting easy even when I heard the front door opening.</p>

<p>"Dorothy unnie! Are you resting well? Or are you still asleep?"<br>"......Wait, hold on. Mr. Nam Do-won."<br>"Huh?"</p>

<p>That was when things went wrong.</p>

<p>"...The magic, it's not working well."<br>"……?"</p>

<p>"It's not that it fails to manifest entirely. But, it feels like something is interfering... grittily..."<br>"Huh?"</p>

<p>"Y-your bag. Hide your bag first. You mustn't make a sound either."<br>"Ah..."</p>

<p>"And even if not your whole body, you must hide at least up to your ankles..."</p>

<p>The magic not working well.<br>One might wonder if it's because of a cold, but when I came up from the first floor, I had perfectly succeeded in hiding it.<br>It probably has nothing to do with a cold.</p>

<p>Then that means Dorothy's magic malfunctioned for a different reason.<br>...In that case, there's one thing that immediately comes to mind as suspicious.</p>

<p>The flower tea I just drank.</p>

<p>The one I brought using 'neighborhood elders' as an excuse.<br>Lady Mari probably thought in human terms, that's for sure.</p>

<p>"Unnie? Are you really in a deep sleep...? Something smells good..."</p>

<p>For now, I hurriedly hid both my bag and my feet behind the dining table.</p>

<p>"Oh, you're here? I remember hearing you'd be coming in around evening..."<br>"Dorothy unnie! One of our schedules got cancelled... The others will be back soon!"</p>

<p>"...Ah."<br>"I wondered what that scent was. You were brewing tea in your kettle, unnie."</p>

<p>"Yes. I heard it's good for a cold."<br>"It smells really good. I'm curious about the taste too, I wonder if it'd be okay to steal a sip of yours..."</p>

<p>"There's plenty, so it should be fine..."<br>"Really?"</p>

<p>Probably around high school age.<br>A member with a youthful appearance pulled out the chair right next to Dorothy and sat down.<br>Judging by her attitude, she seems to be a close member who likes Dorothy a lot.</p>

<p>But anyway, putting that aside,<br>What am I supposed to do now?</p>

<p>"Huh? It tastes bad!"<br>"I guess it's because it's a tea used as medicine."</p>

<p>"Still, it's edible-bad...? There's a strange addictiveness to it..."<br>"……."</p>

<p>"Ah, right, I didn't wash my hands. Just a sec."</p>

<p>While I was awkwardly reading the room, fortunately, she said she'd go wash up first and stepped away for a moment.<br>As expected of someone close with Dorothy, she even walked off casually taking her socks off.</p>

<p>In that gap, Dorothy quickly spoke to me.</p>

<p>"Nam Do-won. If you go out the front door right now, you might be able to hide the sound..."</p>

<p>- Beep, beep beep.</p>

<p>...It's over.<br>The rest of the kids should be back soon, and I guess it's happening now.</p>

<p>As soon as I heard the story, I tried to run away, accepting that I'd be caught on CCTV, but at this rate, I won't even make it past the front door.</p>

<p>In the end, as I tried to hide behind the wall again, Dorothy stepped in front of me, shielding me further, and spoke.</p>

<p>"...It can't be helped, so, um, the furthest room. It's my room, so please go inside."<br>"……."</p>

<p>"It should be fine. It's my private room, and as such, no friends come and go as they please..."</p>

<p>For now,<br>that seemed like the only way.</p>
