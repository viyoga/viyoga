<p>"My, my. What have you two been up to?"<br>"Ah, we didn't do anything..."<br>"...Yes. We just chatted a little."</p>

<p>"Hmm. Well, it was only about two minutes, after all."</p>

<p>The brief time for showing off ended in just that short moment.<br>What more could I ask of Neria, who had already turned into a tomato?<br>I didn't particularly want to, either. And there wasn't time for it.</p>

<p>So, after waiting for Neria to calm down, we returned to the Rehabilitation Support Division office together, and there were the two witches, still lounging in their office chairs as if they'd enjoyed the gossip.</p>

<p>They were witches, but dressed in attire far removed from the witch stereotype we usually imagine.</p>

<p>One of them, the witch who had been sitting in the chair chatting and hugging a tube like a doll, stretched widely and stood up.</p>

<p>"Since both of you are here, shall we get going then...?"<br>"Is everything you mentioned earlier finished? Like the coordinate calculations."</p>

<p>"Naturally, the calculations didn't take long at all... I was just chatting with Erzel and the youngsters these days for a bit..."</p>

<p>Ughhh. With a refreshingly languid voice, the Team Leader even does a brief stretch.</p>

<p>As her sparkling golden eyes turned toward us, I naturally took Neria's hand and headed toward Lord Erzel's side.</p>

<p>"Hm? Oh? Huh?"<br>"...Why are you looking at me like that, Team Leader?"</p>

<p>"No, no. I just remembered the conversation I was having with Erzel earlier. It's nothing major."<br>"Is that so..."</p>

<p>"More than that, you don't need to huddle together like that. My magic isn't exactly the size of a 4-person elevator."<br>"Ah, I thought it might be more convenient for teleporting."</p>

<p>"That's not entirely wrong, but I have no intention of going by teleport?"<br>"……?"</p>

<p>"If I was going to use teleport, I would've left right away. There's a reason it took a few minutes."</p>

<p>With those words, the Team Leader put the tube she had been holding back on her belt.</p>

<p>If I had to pick the person most excited about the deserted island story among the four of us, it would have to be our Team Leader.<br>As that obvious guess crossed my mind, the Team Leader walked toward the office wall.</p>

<p>In terms of the organizational chart, a department where just two boxes for names would be enough.<br>The Rehabilitation Support Department office may be notoriously cramped and shabby, but this is the relatively spacious and clean wall among them.</p>

<p>I wondered what she was going to do there, when the Team Leader's outstretched index finger poked the upper right corner of the wall.<br>It was the upper right corner at a pretty high position, higher than not only the Team Leader's height but mine as well.</p>

<p>At this point, I recalled one of the Team Leader's spells I'd seen before.</p>

<p>"Heave-ho."</p>

<p>A pathetic little shout.<br>Right after that, the Team Leader drew a straight line using her finger touching the wall.</p>

<p>"This way, it'll be easy to go back and forth when needed~"</p>

<p>The first direction was left.<br>The Team Leader's index finger extended smoothly to the upper left, a length about the size of two beds placed vertically end-to-end.</p>

<p>Next, without stopping her stride, downward.<br>In Go terms, the lower left corner.</p>

<p>Next, naturally, to the right, lowering it until it almost touched the floor.<br>Then, up, so it could return to where the finger had first started.</p>

<p>Though it looked like it was drawn sloppily, it formed a clean rectangle as if measured with a ruler.</p>

<p>"Hehe."</p>

<p>Knock, knock.<br>As the team leader knocked playfully, the space marked by the finger toppled over like acorn jelly.</p>

<p>...Whenever you suddenly visited our house, you'd often burst through the wall.</p>

<p>It's probably the same principle as this magic.</p>

<p>"Is this the place? Erzel? The time zone matches Seoul's based on the location, too..."}<br>"Even if you ask me, I don't know much with just this. At the very least, I'd need to scout the whole coastline to get a feel for it."</p>

<p>"Really? Well, it does feel like a deserted island. It's nice and quiet."<br>"Better than being dropped in the middle of the ocean, I guess. The odds of hitting this place are probably 99%. If you really don't know, there's always GPS."</p>

<p>"Hmm."</p>

<p>A widely stretched white sand beach.<br>A sea so blue it hurts the eyes.</p>

<p>I had expected this much from the start, so to be honest, I felt a bit indifferent.<br>Frankly, you can see sandy beaches and the ocean at just about any coast, right?</p>

<p>But beyond that, faced with the transparent emerald color spreading beneath the sea, my gaze was helplessly stolen.</p>

<p>I'd definitely heard the description 'coral reef island,' but this is my first time actually seeing one.<br>Following the surreal blue-green light stretching out endlessly, the coral reefs ended and the sea was coldly dyed a deep, dark blue.</p>

<p>That must mean it connects straight to the deep sea from there.</p>

<p>To think I'd see a view like this with my own eyes, the kind that'd rack up a massive plane ticket price...</p>

<p>"I'll go take a look myself. Eve, you rest. Do-won, you stay here and look after Neria."<br>"Understood."<br>"...I'm going to stay put anyway, so there's no need to fuss over me..."</p>

<p>While Erzel spoke, the humid, muggy heat of the equator gently enveloped the office.</p>

<p>The open equator might be better than Korea's murderous midsummer heat, but that's only a comparison based on midsummer standards. After all, Korea is still in the cool early summer.</p>

<p>Soon after, an unfamiliar salty sea smell touched the tip of my nose.<br>It wasn't a pleasant scent. A wind laden with sticky saltiness—really not great.<br>Still, thanks to the fresh fishy smell, reality felt a bit more vivid.</p>

<p>On top of that, the sunlight pours down endlessly like lasers.</p>

<p>It's the kind of heat that would tan you dark in an instant if you don't apply sunscreen properly.</p>

<p>"...Neria. By any chance, do witches not need to apply sunscreen or anything?"<br>"We do. We just live a bit longer; we didn't use an invincibility cheat code or anything."</p>

<p>"Ah. Right."</p>

<p>Then does that mean I could apply sunscreen on Neria's back later?<br>As I stared at her blankly, a gaze that was clearly saying 'What are you looking at' returned immediately.<br>Not just the gaze, but she even kicked my shin with her foot wearing Crocs as a bonus.</p>

<p>They say she played that many adult games, yet it seems she hasn't experienced the beach event at all.<br>Or maybe it's because lately when people say summer, it's all about summer vacations in the countryside, so the beach scene has rather become a minor thing.</p>

<p>As I was convincing myself of that alone, I heard the sound of shuffling footsteps right next to me and naturally turned my head in that direction.</p>

<p>"Aaah... Sunscreen is an absolute must..."</p>

<p>It was none other than the Team Leader.<br>The Team Leader, stepping on the hot sand heated by the sun, wearing pure white sandals.</p>

<p>After playing around like that for a moment, the Team Leader came back over to the office side and grabbed my wrist.</p>

<p>Her body temperature was the same as ever, but sand had scattered, making the office floor a bit dirty.</p>

<p>"Do-won. Let's just do one thing for work."<br>"What kind of work is it?"</p>

<p>"Here, it's really, truly a raw, natural beach, you know? Because of that, there's absolutely nothing we need. Not a parasol, not a sunbed, not even a simple table..."<br>"...That's certainly true. Then where should I get it from and set it up?"</p>

<p>"Ah, the item is right here!"<br>"……?"</p>

<p>She thrust her hand into empty air, and a moment later.<br>From the rippling void, the Team Leader's hand emerged, dragging out a genuinely heavy parasol with a strained grunt.<br>Unlike its usual appearance, it was neatly folded like an umbrella, which made it feel strangely awkward as a bonus.</p>

<p>"I'll do it."</p>

<p>From there, I took it and managed to pull it out somehow.<br>I pulled it out, but still... the Team Leader, who only got the call this morning, had a parasol prepared in advance...</p>

<p>"I brought it from the beach warehouse?"<br>"...No, I haven't said anything yet, though."</p>

<p>"But you had that 'Where on earth did you get that?' look on your face."<br>"……."</p>

<p>"There's a lot more to bring out, so just put that in the corner for now."<br>"...Understood."</p>

<p>"Ah, and it'd be better to quickly go buy some drinks too..."</p>

<p>...An extremely clean theft.</p>

<p>But the Team Leader is a witch, and as long as I put it back properly before getting caught, it shouldn't be a big problem.</p>

<p>I didn't think any deeper about it.</p>

<p>*****</p>

<p>"It's my island. Standing at the far end and looking inward, it does look like the shape I remember."<br>"Really?"</p>

<p>"The GPS coordinates check out, too. It's floating in the middle of the ocean, so it's hard to see... anyway."<br>"Hm?"</p>

<p>"What are the things Do-won brought? A parasol?"<br>"I thought we might need them to hang out here!"</p>

<p>Taking Lady Erzel's approach, driven by curiosity, as my cue, I set down the beach supplies I had 'borrowed' on the sand for a moment.</p>

<p>A parasol. A sunbed. A snorkeling mask. Fins. A cooler (though, the snorkeling mask onwards, you bought them yourself) and so on......</p>

<p>I'll assemble the sunbeds one by one later. But the parasol's wide shade seems necessary right now, so I unfolded this one first with a snap.</p>

<p>"It's nice to have some shade. Good work, Do-won."</p>

<p>Perhaps because the sun was truly stinging, Lady Erzel stepped inside the moment I unfolded the parasol.<br>Following behind her, the Team Leader entered, and then Neria, who had been hesitating, came in too. Even under the wide parasol, it felt more crowded than expected.</p>

<p>I brought a spare, too, so it should be fine if I unfold that one later as well.<br>As I wiped the cold sweat from my forehead with my wrist, Lady Erzel cleared her throat.</p>

<p>"Neria's test will be conducted on the opposite side of the island. Since it's water magic, it would be difficult to do it around here."<br>"Ah... I understand. We'd likely get swept up if we're not careful."</p>

<p>"Of course, I can control it all, so it won't come to a dangerous moment, but we didn't come here for a water park experience, did we? If you really want to try it, we can do it separately later when we have time."</p>

<p>Erzel's gaze moved slowly between me, standing there blankly, and the Team Leader, who was smiling bashfully for some reason.</p>

<p>"For now, it'll be Do-won and Eve here. You two can just enjoy the beach."<br>"Me and Do-won. Okay~"</p>

<p>"Neria and I will go to the other side of the island. It'll probably take about two hours at most, so..."<br>"Two hours, huh... Hmm, hmm."</p>

<p>And then even the Team Leader looked at me and spoke.</p>

<p>"She said two hours! Do-won."<br>"...I heard it too."</p>

<p>"Hehehe..."<br>"……."</p>

<p>Since you said that while still smiling.</p>

<p>In the end, I had no choice but to smile back.</p>
