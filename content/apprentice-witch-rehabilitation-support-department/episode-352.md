<p>How should I describe this melancholic, totally messed-up physical state?</p>

<p>"Rather than a cold coming on... hmm..."</p>

<p>About six hours past the time I usually fall asleep.<br>I drank a little alcohol, too, controlling it just enough so it wouldn't affect tomorrow.<br>Add to that the specific physical condition of those old men who used to complain, 'My joints ache when it rains'...?</p>

<p>"No, no... that's not enough..."</p>

<p>What else could I add to that?<br>It feels like a reflux-esophagitis level of annoying discomfort has lodged itself appropriately all over my body, making life miserable.<br>If I could, I'd just quit everything and spend 365 days a year recuperating at home—that's the kind of condition I'm in.</p>

<p>...The problem is, I have to wait until tomorrow in this uncomfortable state.</p>

<p>"Help me..."</p>

<p>I have to meet Hayes in this state and even steer the conversation in my favor.<br>Isn't the difficulty way too damn high, more than I thought?<br>Grumbling and complaining, I eventually turned off the lights and burrowed deep under the covers.</p>

<p>The appointment with Eden was set for after 8 PM tomorrow, accepting her suggestion.</p>

<p>For me, the earlier the better, but looking back, I've been summoned by Eden pretty often lately, haven't I?<br>Even tomorrow, I'm scheduled to meet her again.</p>

<p>Since Eden has been supplied with mana this frequently, she has to consume mana for it to make sense...<br>We deliberately delayed the time to create a natural atmosphere.<br>Eden, who consumed mana during the day, calling me to recharge... I guess it'll turn into a cliche scene like that.</p>

<p>That's why I have to endure looking like this until tomorrow evening.</p>

<p>"Haa..."</p>

<p>It would be one thing if I could rest well at home since it's the weekend, but tomorrow is a weekday.</p>

<p>It's the day I have to face Neria and Orca in turn, who must be furious because I've canceled schedules in a row recently.</p>

<p>To get knocked around like a soccer ball by them, stamina is more important than anything.<br>Of all times, something like this had to overlap.</p>

<p>Since it's come to this, maybe I should just clear one more day...<br>Hmm...</p>

<p>"……."</p>

<p>Ah,<br>I don't know.</p>

<p>"...It'll work out somehow."</p>

<p>Fine.<br>My head hurts.<br>I'd rather just go to sleep quickly.</p>

<p>Sighing in my shitty physical state, I groaned and forced myself to try to sleep.</p>

<p>*****</p>

<p>Needless to say, a situation like 'I woke up the next morning feeling perfectly fine!' didn't happen.</p>

<p>Rather, my condition is even worse than yesterday.<br>Maybe it's because I feel just as lousy even though I think I slept pretty well.<br>Or maybe it's because the subway is unusually packed with people today.</p>

<p>As always, rush hour has long passed, so why are there so many people at this hour...<br>My head feels like it's splitting even though I'm just standing here...<br>I wonder if there's some festival going on around here that I don't know about?</p>

<p>That was the train of thought I was on.</p>

<p>"……?"</p>

<p>I made eye contact with a short-haired little kid standing there holding their mom's hand.</p>

<p>Age... hmm.<br>I don't know since I don't have a younger sibling that age.<br>Three? Four?<br>Just a little kid whose crown barely reaches their mom's waist.</p>

<p>For some reason, they're not avoiding my gaze at all, as if we're having a staring contest.</p>

<p>They're even pretty good at it.</p>

<p>'Maybe it's because they're a baby and their eyes are moist...'</p>

<p>A trivial thought, briefly.<br>Looking around, everyone else was absorbed in their smartphones.<br>Even the mom holding their hand tight was leaning against a pillar, busy on her phone, so the kid must've wanted to have a showdown with me, the one they happened to make eye contact with.</p>

<p>If I'd been in top condition, I might've been able to win, but right now I'm dying of exhaustion...<br>I decided to act like an adult and let her win, giving a bright smile before naturally trying to avert my gaze.</p>

<p>And then, ironically, the only thing left to do was fiddle with my smartphone... well, what can you do.</p>

<p>Anyway, I thought that was the end of it.</p>

<p>"……."</p>

<p>But when I smiled, the little kid slipped behind her mom's legs to hide.<br>Her face was still visible, but her body was hidden.</p>

<p>Up until now, whenever something similar happened, I only remember babies bursting into laughter, so she's the first one to react like that.</p>

<p>It's just this much, but it stings a little.</p>

<p>- This stop is...</p>

<p>If there's anything that's particularly changed, it's that my physical condition is trash right now.<br>I wonder if I was unconsciously making a bad expression...?</p>

<p>This trivial worry continued until I got off the subway and arrived at Neria's house.</p>

<p>"Hmm. You really do look more listless than usual."<br>"...What?"</p>

<p>"You, Nam Do-won. I said you look listless."</p>

<p>I thought I'd forget about it appropriately as time passed since it wasn't a big deal,<br>but because Neria pointed it out the moment she saw my sorry state at the entrance.</p>

<p>"Well, I guess it's natural since the poison makes me hurt..."<br>"And the poison... ...Ah. Don't tell me the Team Leader told you?"</p>

<p>"That's right. He said something about you having a place to use it later, so he had no choice? Anyway, that was the nuance he gave it to me with."<br>"I see..."</p>

<p>"Are you really in that much pain? Feel like dying?"<br>"Not to the point of dying, just enough to want to lie down somewhere and sleep deeply."</p>

<p>"Hmm."<br>"Even so, I didn't think I'd have to stay in this state for another half a day..."</p>

<p>When I asked back if it wouldn't be okay to be in pain after stopping by the mansion, the answer came back that there were a few variables, so he didn't like it.<br>Because Hayes might come to pick me up directly like this time.<br>Even if that's not the case, on the days I visit, Hayes is always waiting for me from the entrance.<br>Before that, if you were to inject poison into me separately, Eden, who acts as the syringe, would have a hell of a hard time.</p>

<p>Well, it'd probably be easier for Eden to just get it all over with and rest now rather than going back and forth again with the Team Leader's space magic...</p>

<p>Can't argue with that.</p>

<p>"So, do I look that drained to you too, Neria?"<br>"Yeah. Your eyes look kinda sunken, like a freshly dead, fresh zombie."</p>

<p>Zombie.<br>So that's why that little kid earlier happened to have a staring contest with me.<br>And she scurried off and hid when I smiled... was that because she was flustered that a zombie smiled?</p>

<p>As I was making that plausible guess, Neria spun around and showed me her back.<br>A small back clad in a pitch-black tracksuit.</p>

<p>And that posture...</p>

<p>"...What are you doing? All of a sudden?"<br>"Get on my back."</p>

<p>She really intends to piggyback me.</p>

<p>But since it was obviously impossible with Neria's strength, I said while neatly arranging the shoes I had just taken off.</p>

<p>"If I get on your back, I might accidentally bleh on your shoulder."<br>"Then I'll just tape your mouth shut and carry you."</p>

<p>"...Are you kidnapping me?"<br>"I could."</p>

<p>"Where are you taking me?"<br>"The bedroom. You said you're sick."</p>

<p>Come to think of it... when was that?<br>A day when I was sick and resting at home.<br>I remembered Neria coming all the way to my house to take care of me.</p>

<p>Back then, my house was the setting, but today it's Neria's house.<br>Besides, unlike back then, today there's no risk of passing the illness to Neria.</p>

<p>Even as Neria tugged insistently on my wrist and walked, I followed silently behind.</p>

<p>And where we arrived was, as expected, Neria's bedroom with the bedding still unmade today.</p>

<p>"Alright, take off your clothes before you lie down to rest. It'll be more comfortable than staying in your shirt."<br>"...You were worried if I was hurting at the entrance, but I guess you really are planning to take my mana."</p>

<p>"Take them off before I change my mind and actually eat you up, idiot."</p>

<p>There have been times I fell asleep from accumulated fatigue while replenishing mana with Neria.<br>But this is the first time I've used it with the intention of falling asleep right from the start.</p>

<p>Still, it would've been better if I were the one nursing Neria... but instead, I'm the one being nursed.</p>

<p>Sitting on the bed in an atmosphere I rarely experience...</p>

<p>"I can't believe the time has come to bring this out again."</p>

<p>With those words, she scanned the computer desk, put something on her head, and strutted over with a smug, triumphant expression.</p>

<p>Turns out it was the maid headband I'd seen briefly before.<br>The one with white frills fluttering about.</p>

<p>The fact that she was still wearing her black shut-in outfit made it awkward, yet strangely fitting.</p>

<p>"Why a maid? Are you trying to use it in the same sense as a nurse?"<br>"Huh? You said you're sick because of the poison? How am I supposed to cure that?"</p>

<p>"...What's with you getting serious all of a sudden."<br>"I just remember your reaction being pretty good last time when I did the maid cosplay for you."</p>

<p>"...Was my reaction really that good."<br>"Pfft. Acting all tough... You were busy fucking me in that maid outfit while in heat, even though I was busy playing games."</p>

<p>Neria flipped me the bird playfully, then stepped out of the room and disappeared for a moment.<br>Before long, she returned to the bedroom carrying something that looked like a towel.</p>

<p>"Here. Keep this on your forehead."<br>"...You say it's because of the poison, but a wet towel like this won't make any difference whether it's on my forehead or not, would it?"</p>

<p>"Who doesn't know that? Cut the crap and just take it. Don't kill the mood with boring talk."</p>

<p>And only after forcing a warm wet towel on me did she smirk with satisfaction...</p>

<p>"...Thanks, Neria."</p>

<p>Is it because I haven't seen her face in about two days?<br>She looks even cuter today.</p>

<p>I just let the trivial stuff slide.</p>
