<p>Back at the old dorm, she had a single room all to herself, and it looks like it's the same deal this time around.<br>Her distinctive looks... are naturally a reason, but since idols are all so young, I wonder if it's because Dorothy, at twenty, gets treated as the eldest.<br>With that brief speculation, I took a quick look around her room.</p>

<p>Should I say it's so very Dorothy? Her room resembles its owner, looking like it doesn't know the word 'makeup' very well.</p>

<p>It's not completely empty, but it has the feel of a model home interior done by a pro?<br>A bedroom that's a spitting image of Dorothy, who only gets her makeup done by a pro.</p>

<p>"Through the window... obviously I won't be able to escape."</p>

<p>Turning my gaze toward the window, a forest of buildings of all shapes and sizes spread out beyond it.<br>Whether it was tinted, the somewhat dim and dusky color tone stood out particularly.</p>

<p>You can feel it even at eye level from right here, but...<br>Room 601. That is, the 6th floor.<br>To escape through the window from here, you'd need the skill to scale walls.<br>Even if you did have it, there's a high probability pedestrians would be snapping photos, so you absolutely couldn't attempt it.</p>

<p>Then, there are exactly two ideas left that come to mind.</p>

<p>"Hmm."</p>

<p>1. Send an SOS to the Team Leader, who can use spatial magic, asking her to save me.</p>

<p>2. Since there's no schedule left anyway, wait it out like this and slip away once everyone's fallen asleep.</p>

<p>Ah, and this might be a different story, but figuring out the exact side effects of the flower I just received from Lady Mari?</p>

<p>"It's not like I got a curse that lasts a lifetime..."</p>

<p>Witches all seem to be on friendly terms with each other, so it's hard to believe they'd deliberately cultivate such a scary flower.<br>Besides, she's a witch who knows my job well, and she gave me quite a large amount, as if telling me to use it when needed later.<br>In other words, unless there's some terrifying conspiracy, the side effects shouldn't be that severe.<br>I think it'll be fine if I wait a bit.</p>

<p>But that 'bit' could be 5 minutes, or it could be 5 hours.<br>For now, I should contact the Team Leader first.</p>

<p>[Nam Do-won]<br>- Team Leader, could you teleport me somewhere urgently?</p>

<p>[Eve Lycaon]<br>- Huh?<br>- All of a sudden?<br>- Where to?</p>

<p>[Nam Do-won]<br>- It's Dorothy's quarters, but things went a bit wrong so...</p>

<p>I happened to have the address to Dorothy's quarters saved from the way over here.</p>

<p>I copied it as is and sent it to the Team Leader, and even reported the trivial detail that no one but Dorothy should've been there at this hour, but the schedule fell through so the members returned early.</p>

<p>Then, after a brief silence, the Team Leader sent an unexpected reply.</p>

<p>[Eve Lycaon]<br>- So, you mean Do-won is hiding in a specific room right now?<br>- So I can't just teleport anywhere in that house?</p>

<p>[Nam Do-won]<br>- Yes.</p>

<p>[Eve Lycaon]<br>- Then I'm in a bit of a bind too.<br>- It's not entirely impossible, but the amount of information needed to set the coordinates increases drastically...<br>- It might be difficult...</p>

<p>After delivering those unsettling words, the team leader asked me to wait a moment while she searched for something.</p>

<p>Even amidst that, I remembered the few voices coming from outside, and the words about having to hide below the ankles, so I just sat down plop on the floor next to the bed for a moment.</p>

<p>A conclusion was reached.</p>

<p>[Eve Lycaon]<br>- Hey, Do-won.<br>- To put it bluntly, it might be a bit difficult...</p>

<p>[Nam Do-won]<br>- Is it completely impossible?</p>

<p>[Eve Lycaon]<br>- If there's a building floor plan posted online, it'd be super easy, but I searched by address and it doesn't seem to exist.<br>- The clothes and underwear you wore in the sea this time, those seem to be on the drying rack.<br>- Or, I could first look at something like a fire escape map in the building hallway and then 'pinpoint' the coordinates to go there, but...<br>- You mean every time the pinpointing fails, a verification object will get awkwardly stuck in a wall or furniture?<br>- That's too much of a gamble...</p>

<p>[Nam Do-won]<br>- ...Ah.</p>

<p>[Eve Lycaon]<br>- It might be a bit difficult...</p>

<p>Even with the Team Leader's magic, it seems it'll be difficult.</p>

<p>Then in the end, the only option is to wait until night at the earliest, or hide until Dorothy's magic works properly...</p>

<p>- Click</p>

<p>While I was thinking, I suddenly heard the sound of a door opening, so I unconsciously twisted my body toward the back while still in a sitting position.<br>The movement was so fast I almost smashed the back of my head on the floor.<br>But fortunately, the one who walked up with big strides was the face of an apprentice witch I know well.</p>

<p>"You hid well. I'll give you praise."</p>

<p>Dorothy, who had brought my shoes that must have been at the entrance, casually set them down with a thud on the floor next to me.</p>

<p>...If you do that, the bedroom floor will get dirty.<br>Hoping it would be a little less so, I laid them on their side, and at the same time, Dorothy plopped down onto the bed.</p>

<p>"That was close. I had included Mr. Nam Do-won's shoes within the range of the illusion magic, but it wasn't applied."<br>"...We survived because the entrance was a mess."</p>

<p>"I truly mean it. Since they were sneakers, the design didn't stand out too much, but a single, long shoe on its own... It's really fortunate we weren't discovered."</p>

<p>Sighing along with the recollection, Dorothy looked down at me sitting on the floor and continued speaking.</p>

<p>"By the way, what were you doing? Were you perhaps comfortably playing a smartphone game?"<br>"Ah, no. I was contacting you wondering if our team leader might be able to rescue us."</p>

<p>"...Come to think of it, spatial magic..., ah, that method..."<br>"But they said setting the coordinates is difficult, so it's close to impossible."</p>

<p>"Is that so?"</p>

<p>...What.<br>Her tone of voice suddenly seems to have brightened.</p>

<p>Eh, must be my imagination.<br>I quickly forgot about it and handed over my smartphone so Dorothy could see it well too.</p>

<p>It's the screen where I just sent a reply to the team leader saying, 'Understood, I'll figure out a way to escape on my own...'</p>

<p>"They said it would be difficult because of the reason written there. It seems best to hold out with our own strength..., no, with Dorothy's power, and then slip away quietly."<br>"I think I'll probably be fine by the time everyone's washed up and gone into their rooms. Hmm."</p>

<p>"Is there something on your mind?"<br>"There is."</p>

<p>Dorothy handed me back my smartphone and whispered.</p>

<p>"My magic, why did it end up like this?"<br>"……."</p>

<p>...There's nothing I can say except that I'm sorry.</p>

<p>"Thanks to you, my cold seems to have gotten worse. Nam Do-won."<br>"I'm sorry...."</p>

<p>"Even after thinking it over again, the flower tea I just drank seems to be the culprit. What is your opinion, Mr. Nam Do-won?"<br>"...I have nothing to say, even if I had two mouths."</p>

<p>"...Hmm. The part about my cold getting worse was a joke, but it's true that my body feels lighter right now. However, since it was given to you by the Witch of Flowers, I suppose the side effects that appear on a witch weren't anticipated."<br>"Yes.... Probably...."</p>

<p>Dorothy looked down at me, gently shaking her crossed legs, as I answered on my knees properly bowed.<br>She cleared her sore throat with a few coughs and spoke.</p>

<p>"Um, my legs hurt a bit."<br>"……."</p>

<p>"You said your legs hurt. Mr. Nam Do-won."<br>"……."</p>

<p>Right. After causing this much trouble, a leg massage is the least I can do.<br>Still on my knees, I pushed against the floor to adjust my position bit by bit, then carefully took hold of one pale, smooth calf before me.</p>

<p>...But since I don't really know how to give a proper massage, it was inevitably a bit clumsy.<br>With half-assed hand movements, wondering if this would even work.<br>Press. Press.</p>

<p>I pressed and pressed into this soft, supple skin.</p>

<p>"Please focus a bit more on the lower part of the calf."<br>"...Is here okay?"</p>

<p>"Hmm. Not bad. I have some complaints about the details, but they're hard to explain, so I'll let it go."<br>"That's a relief. I'm not good at this kind of thing."</p>

<p>"Please do the other leg as well."</p>

<p>I have sins to atone for, so I have nothing to say.<br>So I obediently gave her a massage, and Dorothy favored me with a faint smile that was hard to notice.</p>

<p>She, who had been teasing me about how it felt to become 'the first male masseur for Dorothy,' then delivered the relatively fortunate news.</p>

<p>"I've once again asked the members in the living room not to enter the bedroom unless it's a truly urgent matter. …Though, honestly, it would be more accurate to say the members asked me not to leave the bedroom."<br>"So you only go out for meals?"</p>

<p>"I've also asked for meals to be delivered to the door starting tonight. Other than that... ah, the extent of it is just pretending to go to the bathroom for the sake of naturalness."</p>

<p>She said she didn't have any friends who would just barge into her bedroom normally, so now that she has a cold, she'll be even more careful not to spread it.<br>Dorothy, having asserted this confidently, eventually lay down on the bed with a light stretch.</p>

<p>Well, she is a patient with a bad cold and body aches, after all.<br>Even if she feels a bit better thanks to the flower tea, she's not completely recovered.<br>She must want to rest comfortably.</p>

<p>"You have three layers of blankets on."<br>"I couldn't help it because of the chills. I sweated a tremendous amount; it's embarrassing."</p>

<p>"You witches always smell good anyway. On the other hand, if a guy like me did that, it'd be a huge problem."<br>"...Mm."</p>

<p>"By the way, if you fall asleep like this, what happens to the illusion magic you cast on me...?"<br>"It will unravel gradually. Not all at once, but starting with the parts that require continuous additional calculation."</p>

<p>"So that's how it works."</p>

<p>As I tucked the blanket in neatly along with my words, everything but Dorothy's tired-looking face disappeared under the thick covers.</p>

<p>...Maybe it's because the bed and blanket are both pretty big. It gives off a strangely cute vibe.</p>

<p>I was sitting right next to her, gazing out the window at the scenery.</p>

<p>"Nam Do-won."<br>"Yes."</p>

<p>"How about you just lie down next to me as well, Nam Do-won?"<br>"...Suddenly?"</p>

<p>"It's just... I'm worried about what happens if I actually fall asleep. If you look around right now, there's nowhere to hide, is there? We should prepare for any unforeseen circumstances."<br>"Well, that's true, but..."</p>

<p>"It's not exactly a suitable hiding spot, but still. If you say you're buried under a thick blanket, even if someone else sees, they probably won't find it suspicious."<br>"Hmm..."</p>

<p>"You must be tired from producing semen for the Rehabilitation Support Division work, so take this opportunity to just sleep comfortably here."</p>

<p>...Well. Let's just say being tired is one thing.<br>Honestly, isn't it hard to tell if someone else is lying next to you?<br>Whether it's the left or right, one side would feel pretty unnatural.</p>

<p>Viewing it as a game of hide-and-seek was one thing, but since that wasn't the only point to be mindful of, I threw a short question at Dorothy.</p>

<p>"But you'll definitely catch a cold if you do that, Dorothy."<br>"Aren't you staying in my bedroom too long to be worrying about that now? You idiot, Nam Do-won."</p>

<p>"That's..."<br>"……."</p>

<p>"...Yes. That's right."</p>

<p>Because I had nothing to say.</p>

<p>In the end, I raised the white flag.</p>
