<p>A light drizzle had been continuing since yesterday.</p>

<p>They said it was nationwide rain, didn't they?<br>I don't usually watch the weather forecast closely, but that's probably what they said.<br>So everywhere from that mountain valley to the heart of Seoul must be a mess with rainwater.</p>

<p>Stopped at a red light glowing crimson, I wiped the rain-soaked visor.</p>

<p>Thanks to that, I'm driving safely today, unintentionally.<br>It's been a really long time since I've driven obeying the speed limit stitch by stitch.<br>Well, with this many people around, I guess it can't be helped.<br>At this rate, I wonder if I'll suffocate to death.</p>

<p>Still, in a few days, when I go back deep into the mountains, it'll be like usual...<br>...No, wait. A few days? What few days.<br>To loosen Master's stubbornness, I'll have to stay in Seoul for at least a month or so.</p>

<p>Maybe I'll have to keep running at this pace for longer than I thought.</p>

<p>"Haaah..."</p>

<p>If it weren't for the 'sudden order' Master gave me, I would've been taking a walk at my usual pace today, too.</p>

<p>If it was going to be like this, I should've just left the bike and come on foot.<br>I was leaning against the handlebars, waiting for the light to change.</p>

<p>"Uh, hello!"<br>"……?"</p>

<p>Just then, the putt-putt-putt of a motorcycle engine sounded from right behind me.<br>A stranger grabbed the clutch lever and pulled to a stop right next to me.</p>

<p>...Are city people usually this friendly with their greetings?<br>Even the countryside valleys known for their warm hearts don't suddenly exchange greetings while riding bikes like this.</p>

<p>While I was dumbfounded, the purring engine noise unpleasantly mixed in.</p>

<p>"Wow... You look like a woman, but you ride quite a tough bike! The model name is... ah, I really know this. This cyberpunk-like appearance... hmm..."<br>"……."</p>

<p>"Do you happen to live around here? If you rode a motorcycle like this around, you'd definitely stand out. Or is this your first time coming out this far today?"<br>"……."</p>

<p>"You're a quiet one~ Don't tell me there's a misunderstanding? I'm really not a weirdo! I'm a law-abiding citizen!"<br>"……."</p>

<p>"Or are you perhaps a foreigner?"<br>"……."</p>

<p>He was just a noisy person.<br>Setting aside that impression, my gaze went to the small camera attached to the side of the helmet.</p>

<p>...What do they call that in city speak? An action cam?<br>But isn't that something a person holds directly in their hand?<br>Then is that a dash cam?</p>

<p>But the equipment looks pretty good for just a dash cam...</p>

<p>Maybe it's a city trend...</p>

<p>"Hm? Oh, my camera?"</p>

<p>I hadn't touched the visor at all, but it seems my gaze was read because of the direction my face was turned.<br>The man, who noticed with good timing, started rambling on.</p>

<p>"I do YouTube, too! It's not a big channel or anything... I just upload pretty scenery I come across while doing this day laborer gig~"<br>"……."</p>

<p>"Or maybe~ a good connection~"<br>"……."</p>

<p>It's a story I have no interest in.<br>Besides, if it's YouTube, isn't that a type of that 'idiot box' Master mentioned?</p>

<p>An employee participating in the idiot box would surely be closer to the 'good-for-nothing' type of person.<br>Master told me never to get close to people like this. Yeah.</p>

<p>Just then, the smartphone vibrated briefly.</p>

<p>[Master]<br>- Where are you?<br>- I know the weather's foul, but you seem to be running later than I expected.</p>

<p>There's still a long way to go until the appointed time.<br>Not just in words, but actually, there's a little over an hour left.<br>Asking where I am is one thing, but it's far too early to be saying I seem late.</p>

<p>But that's just how Master is.<br>I accepted it as usual and replied.</p>

<p>[Hayes Ineba]<br>- I've almost arrived.<br>- I'll contact you again when I reach the front of the building.</p>

<p>[Master]<br>- Hm.<br>- Come safely.<br>- Your health comes first.</p>

<p>Just as I finished checking that far, the traffic light happened to turn green.<br>As I naturally gripped the clutch lever tight, the man still beside me blabbered on.</p>

<p>"By any chance, where are you headed? If it's your first time on this route, I could help you—"<br>"Don't follow me."</p>

<p>"Huh?"</p>

<p>It'd be nice if he just got the hint here so things don't get annoying.<br>That's what I'd like, but expecting anything from an inferior human is even more foolish.</p>

<p>Tsk. Clicking her tongue, Hayes deliberately sped up and put distance between herself and that spot.<br>Even though the road surface was wet and slippery from the spring rain, I've got enough experience to be confident up to a point.</p>

<p>As they drifted apart like that,</p>

<p>"...Hah! Geez! I treated you this kindly, and you tell me not to follow? Tsk... kids these days..."</p>

<p>The man, who belatedly noticed the honking, rode his motorcycle slowly, pondering for a bit.</p>

<p>...The woman he'd run into by chance just now, the bike she was dragging along, and her figure. Both were no joke.<br>Aside from the fact that he hadn't seen her face at all, those two things alone meant the thumbnail clickbait would probably pull insanely well.</p>

<p>Of course, the conversation itself had been completely ignored, so there was no video angle to speak of.<br>But if he just used it moderately for the thumbnail, that alone would make for a pretty sweet side income...</p>

<p>"……."</p>

<p>But judging by the reaction just now, she was incredibly stiff and cold.</p>

<p>"...Ah, geez. What a waste."</p>

<p>It's just a hobby YouTube channel anyway.<br>It's better to just not care about it rather than leaving open the possibility of unnecessary noise arising.<br>It's a shame, but, hmm, this is the right call.</p>

<p>Nevertheless, since what he'd just seen was a physique rarely seen around, he let out a deep sigh of regret, but only for a moment.</p>

<p>To forget the thought, the man deliberately turned the steering wheel in a different direction.</p>

<p>*****</p>

<p>"Looks like the story worked out well in the end."<br>"Yep. It's all thanks to Do-won working so hard."</p>

<p>"I didn't really... do anything, though."<br>"Nothing?! Thanks to you, Do-won, three elder witches have already left rave reviews saying they were completely satisfied!"</p>

<p>"Ahaha..."<br>"Hehe. Anyway, about Lady Eden, I reported to her this morning, and she coolly replied, 'I shall give it a try and believe in it.' She even called her disciple to Seoul."</p>

<p>"To Seoul... meaning she was originally staying somewhere else..."<br>"I don't know the details either. The fact that Lady Eden had a young apprentice witch was news to me."</p>

<p>"Ah, you mentioned that last time."<br>"So I only know her name and face. Hayes Ineba."</p>

<p>"Hayes..."</p>

<p>Hayes Ineba. Hayes Ineba.</p>

<p>I chewed and swallowed the name over and over so I wouldn't forget it, then accepted the nicely grilled samgyeopsal the Team Leader handed me and bowed my head.</p>

<p>The place I ended up at as a result of [Do-won, you like meat, right?] was unexpectedly the Team Leader's house.<br>Even inside, it was a small room of about 8 pyeong temporarily constructed with magic.</p>

<p>The Team Leader said that here, we could grill meat to our heart's content without worrying about oil splattering.<br>True to those words, there wasn't even a single sheet of newspaper laid out around us.<br>Whether to call it tough or just reckless, we're just grilling meat with a single portable burner placed there.</p>

<p>For space magic that showed infinite possibilities, it's a pretty cheap use, but.<br>Oh well. As long as the samgyeopsal is delicious, that's all that matters.</p>

<p>I dipped the meat in just the right amount of ssamjang, balanced it with white rice, chewed and swallowed, then asked one thing I was curious about.</p>

<p>"I'm a bit curious about the situation over there."<br>"The situation over there?"</p>

<p>"Yes. I wonder if you called your disciple in while just casually telling your story, or if you made up some other excuse to reduce her resistance..."<br>"Hmm... Probably the former? I remember Marie's request was the latter, but since that was a special case, it's right to view it as such."</p>

<p>That makes sense.<br>On top of that, if possible, it'd be great if she were a case like Dorothy, full of motivation...</p>

<p>As expected, I guess it's right to give up on that.<br>After all, the witch who requested this is a witch of an age far beyond even the Team Leader.<br>If even she couldn't solve it, the probability is high that it's a really tough, knotted problem.</p>

<p>Maybe this time the witch is actually a lesbian... or something like that.<br>The problem might have arisen for a reason you'd see in an erotic novel rather than reality.</p>

<p>"……."</p>

<p>But if that's really the case, then how exactly are we supposed to solve this?<br>Do I have to start by drinking alcohol like Dorothy...?</p>

<p>While I was seriously pondering the 'method for dealing with lesbians' all by myself, the Team Leader, who had been grilling meat diligently, opened his mouth with an 'Ah-'.</p>

<p>"Come to think of it, remember when Lady Eden filed a complaint?"<br>"A complaint? Ah, yes. That complaint."</p>

<p>"There was a slightly concerning expression at that time.... Just a moment!"</p>

<p>With those words, the Team Leader rummaged through the pocket of the coat he had taken off and set aside.<br>She soon took out her smartphone and began scrolling through her gallery.</p>

<p>Fortunately, she seemed to have found it quickly, as the screen turned toward me in less than 10 seconds.</p>

<p>"Look here. Right here. The last part of the petition."<br>"The last..."</p>

<p>Though you said 'the last,' I reflexively looked upward upon hearing those words.</p>

<p>It wasn't anything else, but because Hayes's photo happened to be there alongside it.</p>

<p>It's a far cry from a neat, stiff ID photo.<br>Nor is it a photo capturing her usual appearance.<br>As if she'd hurriedly taken a photo seizing this moment, a picture of Hayes asleep with the blanket pulled up to her chin.</p>

<p>Perhaps due to the composition, I couldn't make out many details, but there she was in the photo, looking like she'd taken her master Eden's pale green hair and made it a slightly brighter shade.</p>

<p>"Hmm? Do-won, are you looking at the photo right now?"<br>"...S-sorry."</p>

<p>"It's okay. There's nothing to be sorry about. You'll be her handler soon, so you should know her face."</p>

<p>The reason she was entrusted to the 'Apprentice Witch Rehabilitation Support Division' with such a peaceful expression lies below.<br>With a slightly nervous feeling, I lowered my gaze.</p>

<p>And sure enough,<br>At the end of the petition, there was a concerning phrase written, just as the Team Leader said.</p>

<p>- It seems there will likely be problems with future mana charging if this continues, so re-education by a specialist is deemed necessary.</p>

<p>- Absolute confidentiality required.</p>

<p>"Re-education...?"</p>

<p>...Just what was the first education like to warrant that?</p>
