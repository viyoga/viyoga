<p>"Shall I go get you a glass of water?"<br>"...That would be th-thankful."</p>

<p>Leaving Hayes's bedroom, back into the hallway.<br>Only after properly closing the door behind me did I finally manage to let out a breath.<br>Pressing my palm firmly against my still-twitching lips for no reason.</p>

<p>I never expected the reaction to be this good.</p>

<p>When I think of the image of an apprentice witch in my head, there's Neria and Orca, the expressive witches.<br>And then there's Dorothy and Hayes, the witches with flat emotional expression. I thought I could categorize them like that.<br>But the Hayes I just saw had a much more innocent and pure vibe than I thought?</p>

<p>Suddenly that hit me hard, and my lips twitched so much I thought I'd die.</p>

<p>Fortunately, she turned her head away first, looking embarrassed... Otherwise, I would've been treated like some crazy person.<br>Or I would've been cleanly poisoned to death.</p>

<p>"...Ah, right. Water."</p>

<p>Anyway, the atmosphere is good.<br>It's uncertain whether I'll be able to have sex with Hayes as Eden wanted, but at least doing what we did yesterday again seems like it won't be a problem.</p>

<p>So now I just need to bring her some water and then wait with a grin on my face until dawn...</p>

<p>"……."</p>

<p>...Wait with a grin?</p>

<p>Are you sure that's okay?</p>

<p>"Like Eden said, weird things have been happening every dawn. But since a lot of it happened inside Hayes's dream, isn't it pretty ambiguous?"</p>

<p>Surely he didn't keep me trapped in the dream to use me as a masturbation tool, did he?<br>We've already pleasured each other's genitals with our tongues, so there's no need to do that in a dream.</p>

<p>That being the case, if there's a place that's immediately concerning, it'd have to be the kitchen where I frequently ran into Hayes...</p>

<p>So I have to come to the kitchen at dawn pretending to drink water or something?</p>

<p>But 'dawn' covers too wide a range, doesn't it?<br>Do I just have to sit tight in the kitchen?</p>

<p>Even when a similar thing happened recently, Hayes didn't stop masturbating and crawl out until nearly 3 AM, right?</p>

<p>"Hmm..."</p>

<p>It was a situation where, far from staying awake until then, it was obvious I'd pass out from sleepiness first.<br>Holding a clean glass, I pondered for a moment, then decided to wet my throat first and think things over.</p>

<p>For one thing, Hayes must have some ulterior motive.<br>That's why she tried to keep me here until tomorrow morning, right?</p>

<p>But since I couldn't look inside her head, I had absolutely no clue what kind of picture she had drawn up.</p>

<p>Because of that, there was a chance that even a trivial action of mine could end up pouring ink all over her picture.</p>

<p>"Then, number 1."</p>

<p>If I decide to go with the approach of not acting rashly, it would be better to wait quietly in the guest room.</p>

<p>Option 2.<br>Considering everything that's happened so far, regardless of being tired or whatever, waiting in the kitchen all night seems like the right answer.</p>

<p>Well, both just differ in location; I have to wait for Hayes either way, so having to fight off sleep is the same.</p>

<p>"What should I do..."</p>

<p>I gulped down the remaining cold water in the cup and pondered for a moment.</p>

<p>Sadly, the only option visible was [3. Stop the foolishness and ask Hayes herself where she'll be waiting], so I returned to the bedroom with the cup filled to the brim with water.</p>

<p>"Is your hiccup okay now?"<br>"...Well."</p>

<p>As I offered the cup of water along with my words, Hayes fidgeted and accepted it.<br>True to form, there was no word of thanks.</p>

<p>The succulent, a prop for forced conversation, was already showing off its freshness in its original spot.<br>Only Hayes sits alone, upright, at the bedside.</p>

<p>But whether she hadn't noticed me yet, or simply sat there out of habit.<br>She hasn't sat off to one side, but has taken the center spot....</p>

<p>Was even this too difficult for Miss Zero-Friends Country-Girl Apprentice Witch?<br>Or does it mean this human bastard shouldn't dare think of sharing a seat and just stay standing?</p>

<p>As I briefly pondered this, Hayes pulled her mouth from the cup and let out a breathless gasp.</p>

<p>"Haa..."<br>"Give me the cup. I'll put it back on my way."</p>

<p>There was still a little water left in the cup, but Hayes carefully handed it over without finishing it.<br>Fortunately, the hiccups seem to have been resolved.</p>

<p>Then now...</p>

<p>"……."</p>

<p>...What do I do?</p>

<p>I just jokingly thought of the option [Ask Hayes directly], but since it doesn't protect a witch's pride, dignity, and so on, even though it's not complicated, I'm conflicted.</p>

<p>She takes after her master, so she's especially sensitive about that kind of face-saving.</p>

<p>The master is too embarrassed to revise the sex education he spouted himself into a revised edition, so he's calling in a proxy.<br>And the disciple who learned under him is staying by my side, constantly spouting lines like, 'How could a witch do such a filthy thing.'</p>

<p>Even knowing all this, if I were to bring up something like, '[Honestly, you were thinking of masturbating to me, weren't you?]' the losses would outweigh the gains...<br>Which ultimately means I have to take the inefficient detour despite knowing everything.</p>

<p>"...Ah, right."</p>

<p>With my hamster-sized brain, this was the best I could do.</p>

<p>"I think I'll turn in early today since I'm tired. Must be because the rain's been pouring down outside."<br>"...Is that so."</p>

<p>"Have a good night, Lady Hayes."</p>

<p>What the hell does he mean going to sleep in this situation? I'll set that aside for a moment.</p>

<p>The important point is this.<br>I'm going to sleep, so I'll stay holed up in the guest room.<br>So, Hayes, you come find me whenever you want.</p>

<p>Anyway, it was a given I'd have to stay up until dawn; the only difference was whether I waited in the kitchen or the guest room, right?<br>Hayes would never bring this up outright, so I casually dropped the hint from my side.</p>

<p>As a result, Hayes let out a big breath as if the tension had eased, even though I was still standing right in front of her.</p>

<p>...It was absurd how she only now started checking my expression, but anyway, I trust the Witch's intelligence was enough to understand.</p>

<p>- Click</p>

<p>I put the used cup back in the kitchen properly.<br>Then, straight to the usual guest room.<br>I threw myself onto the bed, which I've become quite used to by now, and let out a sigh containing the day's fatigue, just like Hayes did a moment ago.</p>

<p>...Come to think of it, this is the first time I'm staying the night without using Lady Mari's flower, isn't it?<br>I've come all this way, but I wonder if Hayes will really come to do naughty things at dawn while Master is asleep... that thought crosses my mind too.</p>

<p>Along with that, melting my brain with various short videos, before I knew it, it was deep into the night.</p>

<p>- Thud, thud</p>

<p>Around 1 AM.<br>A presence began to be felt from outside the guest room.</p>

<p>If it's those footsteps, I'm already familiar with them.<br>The sound of Hayes walking in soft slippers.<br>Eden never wears that kind of indoor footwear, so it's a sound that can't be mistaken.</p>

<p>It slowly approached the front of the guest room.</p>

<p>"……?"</p>

<p>Only pausing for a moment.<br>Hayes, starting to make footsteps again before long.</p>

<p>In that direction... it's the kitchen side...</p>

<p>'...Is she checking to make sure we don't miss each other?'</p>

<p>Or does it mean I should read the room and come out to the kitchen on my own?<br>Ugh, groaning, I turned off even the smartphone screen, the last remaining light in the guest room.</p>

<p>Either way, the latter is more likely than the former.<br>Or maybe she's heading there thinking I'd naturally be in the kitchen.</p>

<p>When people talk person to person, misunderstandings arise even when conveying words with their literal meaning.<br>In the midst of that, since I spoke hoping she'd interpret it well, it's only right to consider the probability of failing to interpret it the other way around, too.</p>

<p>...In the meantime, the fact that I arrived later than Hayes bothered me a little.</p>

<p>- Thud, thud</p>

<p>Footsteps returning this way again, contrary to expectations.<br>Lightly opening the guest room door.<br>In the end, she stepped inside this room.</p>

<p>- Thud, thud</p>

<p>Since I wasn't in the kitchen, she must have turned back and entered the guest room.<br>I could immediately guess Hayes's train of thought up to this point.</p>

<p>But the timing was the problem—it was right before I was about to get up from bed.</p>

<p>...Hayes ended up opening the door and coming in just like that, so even on a day like this, I became the crazy bastard who was actually sleeping soundly.</p>

<p>Since the best timing has already passed, I don't know when would be good to let her know I'm awake.</p>

<p>"……."</p>

<p>Since it's come to this, I have no choice but to get up right away if I sense her trying to wake me.<br>If I then apologize, saying I accidentally dozed off, there shouldn't be any major problem.<br>As I was belatedly deciding on a second-best option, Hayes kept approaching closer to my side.</p>

<p>...Wait, she's not planning to hit me to wake me up, is she?<br>I flinched at the presence that had drawn strangely close, but the action that followed was even more bewildering.</p>

<p>"Hmm..."</p>

<p>Hayes, who wouldn't have been out of place pinching my cheek lightly to tell me to wake up.</p>

<p>Poke, poke,<br>Was because she was gently pressing on my cheek with her slender index finger.</p>

<p>"...I remember you saying you were tired..."</p>

<p>Hayes muttering quietly to herself, as if the words were lingering in her mouth.<br>Her hand gestures poking my cheek once again are infinitely gentle.<br>It feels like she has no intention of forcibly waking someone who's sound asleep.</p>

<p>...That's strange.<br>Hayes must have had her own plans for this early morning, so is it really okay not to wake me?<br>Or does it mean it's okay for me to just keep sleeping like this?</p>

<p>No matter how I think about it, in a situation where the plan has practically fallen through,</p>

<p>Hayes muttered quietly once more.</p>

<p>"...Still......, I need to apologize for the underground workshop incident......"<br>"……."</p>

<p>"Since you said you liked this kind of thing last time...."</p>

<p>For some reason.<br>While carefully touching the blanket near my lower body.</p>
