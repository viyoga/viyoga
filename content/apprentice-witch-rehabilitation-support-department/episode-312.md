<p>…A morning where my whole body feels stiff and achy.<br>I opened my eyes without a smartphone alarm, rising along with the dim morning sunlight.<br>At the same time, the blanket I kicked off slowly slid down under the sofa and lay sprawled on the floor.</p>

<p>Even though I still feel tired enough to die, the fact that I woke up… makes me wonder if it's because the sleeping spot was too uncomfortable.<br>Pressing my palm firmly against the old sofa belatedly, it's definitely not cut out to be used as a bed.<br>Orca's sofa at her place was fluffy and really nice, though.</p>

<p>There's no guarantee this won't happen again in the future….<br>Should I use the bonus-like thing I got from Dorothy's master last time to replace the furniture one by one?<br>Get a wider, better bed too….</p>

<p>"Haaam..."</p>

<p>Orca, who had taken a seat on the opposite end of the sofa, was fortunately still traveling in dreamland.<br>Unlike me, who had barely woken up yesterday evening, she must have been tired from various things since morning, so I understand.</p>

<p>So, I carefully slipped away from my seat, trying not to wake her, and headed to the kitchen.<br>I wet my parched throat from the night with cold water, and also sneakily checked on Neria's condition in my room.</p>

<p>...She's sleeping soundly here too, just the same.<br>Even though she did all that yesterday, she left the bedsheets as they were.<br>As expected of the Witch of Water, it seems she took care of the bed herself, made it all fluffy and dry, and is sleeping soundly.</p>

<p>Having finished all my checks, I walked quietly back towards the living room sofa.<br>The time checked via smartphone was only just 6 AM.</p>

<p>It's way too early compared to my usual routine,<br>and since I'm up early, there's nothing in particular I need to do.<br>At the same time, I feel like I'm gonna die of exhaustion.<br>I'll turn off all my alarms and sleep a little longer.<br>Just as I made that decision, last night's message flashed through my mind.</p>

<p>It was none other than a message from the Team Leader.</p>

<p>"Just a mistake, right?"</p>

<p>Unless there was additional contact, which there wasn't, it's highly likely a mistake.<br>Follow-up messages came saying it was sent by accident, and to rest well with Neria, reassuring me.</p>

<p>But when you see someone else's mistake, you can't help but get even a little curious.</p>

<p>Since I was all set to catch some more sleep, I closed my eyes and let my imagination run wild wondering, 'Just what kind of message did they send?'</p>

<p>"……."</p>

<p>...Because it's none other than the Team Leader.<br>A dirty joke...?</p>

<p>Who knows.</p>

<p>Or maybe a message meant for another witch,<br>was sent to me by mistake.</p>

<p>*****</p>

<p>Again.<br>A morning where my whole body feels stiff and achy.<br>I opened my eyes just the same, without something like a smartphone alarm, but there were a few differences from dawn.</p>

<p>The morning sun had risen much brighter, and.<br>...It was damn noisy.</p>

<p>The two apprentice witches who had been sleeping soundly in their own spots until dawn,<br>were bickering in the kitchen.</p>

<p>"Do-won doesn't like stuff like this."<br>"H-has he ever told you directly that he hates it?"</p>

<p>"Not exactly, but my gut says so. He chomps down on vegetables just fine, but I've never seen him eat things like sam? (ginseng)."<br>"...Then maybe he eats it well? Isn't it just that since he eats with you, who has a childish palate, there was never a need to order it specifically...?"</p>

<p>"He likes that childish menu, too. Not that milkit samgyetang."<br>"Mmm..."</p>

<p>Orca stood in front of the pot, poking at it hesitantly with her chopsticks.<br>It seemed Neria, sitting in the dining chair right behind her, was kibitzing about the breakfast menu.</p>

<p>Orca must be thinking of making a restorative meal since I had a hard time yesterday....<br>...I don't know if this analogy fits, but it feels like watching a dog and a cat.</p>

<p>A dog wanting to do anything more for its owner,<br>And a cat interpreting and enjoying its master on its own terms.</p>

<p>Of course, I didn't say it out loud.</p>

<p>"...I like samgyetang. It's just not something I order often."<br>"Wh-what? When did you wake up?"</p>

<p>"Just now."</p>

<p>Throwing off the blanket, I got up from the sofa and moved to the seat next to Neria.<br>As I approached, tapping my throbbing forehead from fatigue, Orca's face brightened as she spoke.</p>

<p>"Do you like samgyetang?"<br>"If someone else even prepares it for me, I'll bow and eat it. ...No, I'll bow and eat it. Like I said earlier, it's not something you eat often normally."</p>

<p>"That's a relief.... Neria kept saying bad things, so I was worried I'd just spend money and hear complaints...."<br>"Did you go out and buy it?"</p>

<p>"Yes. I thought I should feed you something good for your body today."<br>"Ah..., thanks for taking care of me. You must've been tired since morning."</p>

<p>"...Hehe."</p>

<p>Orca points that out and then glances over at Neria.<br>Following her gaze, I turn my head too, and see Neria standing with her arms crossed tightly under her chest.</p>

<p>The large white shirt she borrowed last night catches the eye, all crumpled and wrinkled.</p>

<p>"Nam Do-won, how come you never ate stuff like that when you were with me?"<br>"Because there wasn't really any particular reason to eat it?"</p>

<p>"Aha. So when it was just the two of us, it wasn't to the point where you needed a stamina booster...?"<br>"...Does it work out like that?"</p>

<p>"You'd lie down and rest saying it was hard each time, so I guess it wasn't *that* hard. Hm."</p>

<p>Usually, I only had to satisfy you alone to begin with.<br>Yesterday, the two apprentice witches took turns alternating between rest and sex.<br>It's an unavoidable part, isn't it.</p>

<p>Logically, that train of thought would be natural, but Neria's proposal that followed immediately was...</p>

<p>The moment I heard it, I was dumbfounded.</p>

<p>"I wonder if living together and wringing me out all night like yesterday would make a difference..."<br>"……."<br>"……."</p>

<p>A lot of things I want to say come to mind, but I don't know which one to blurt out first.<br>Living together, honestly, I think it's an act of confirming each other's flaws in spades rather than merits.<br>Instead of worrying about this, would asking her if she's seriously serious be the first thing to do?</p>

<p>Not knowing how to react, I just kept my mouth shut for a moment.</p>

<p>Orca, who had been checking the pot since a while ago, spoke up with a frown.</p>

<p>"Besides you, Neria, there are other witches who need to be supplied with semen. Aren't you being a bit too greedy?"<br>"Well, the others can just each try their best to get Do-won worked up on their own. Right?"</p>

<p>"You were saying you'd milk Do-won dry all night. That's already unfair from the start..."<br>"Unfair? Why is seizing an opportunity for myself unfair?"</p>

<p>"That's..."<br>"Standing at the front of the waiting list like right now isn't unfair; it should be seen as being diligent."</p>

<p>Having explained it that way, Neria leaned back comfortably against the chair's backrest.<br>This time, she turned her head toward me.</p>

<p>"But, if I really say I'll move in... Orca and Dorothy here are bound to butt in without fail..."<br>"……."</p>

<p>"From the perspective of someone watching from the sidelines, it's pretty damn annoying. That is."</p>

<p>Especially with Dorothy, I have experience actually living together for a short period.<br>Even after that cohabitation period was completely over, Neria's grumbling and complaining is still vivid in my memory.</p>

<p>Whether it was because that memory came to mind, Neria stared at me with an unreadable expression.<br>Mixing in a humming sound like 'Hmm~', for a long while.</p>

<p>After making eye contact with me like that, a short sigh eventually slipped out.</p>

<p>"Sigh. Unless you just secretly sleep over once in a while, there's no other way."<br>"Secretly?"</p>

<p>"This dairy cow here, and the beast witch who's already had the cohabitation experience. If I make a move to cohabit first, they're all going to set their sights on this spot, right?"<br>"...I don't think it'll go that far."</p>

<p>"That's about right. So it's probably best to just leave the position vacant."<br>"……."</p>

<p>"If you add Dorothy here, that makes three, and if you add that girl you're trying to rehabilitate, that makes four. Do you think you can handle living together with all of them?"</p>

<p>I couldn't handle it.<br>One night with four apprentice witches could be wrapped up as a good memory, but if it dragged on for days.<br>Even now, after sleeping with just half that number—two people—I'm this exhausted. If I really did that, it wouldn't take more than a few days before I'd be wasted away and looking haggard.</p>

<p>...In the end, Neria's words all seemed right, so I nodded.</p>

<p>Just then, Orca finished cooking and brought the pot over this way.</p>

<p>"Living together... we can talk about that another time. For now, try a bite. See if it suits your taste, Do-won."<br>"Ah, thanks."<br>"Honestly, this is just a meal kit. Wouldn't it taste the same if I made it?"</p>

<p>"Well, it's made with sincerity."</p>

<p>Thanks to the restorative meal she cooked, I managed to regain some vitality, even if I was still tired.</p>

<p>The problem is.</p>

<p>"Hey, Nam Do-won. You're gonna recharge mana with us today anyway..."<br>"...Th-that's right. Then rather..."</p>

<p>...a little replenished vitality.</p>

<p>It means it was taken away again immediately.</p>

<p>They say everything can be done by phone or the internet these days...<br>Perhaps because she's an old-fashioned person, she had an unavoidable stubbornness.<br>A feeling, roughly like this: there absolutely must be a person at the post.</p>

<p>In contrast to that rigid demeanor, she would ignore the civil servant vacation system and let her subordinates rest to their hearts' content.</p>

<p>Especially.<br>She skipped work today.</p>

<p>"Erzel! Over here, over here."<br>"What's this all of a sudden? You want to consult with me?"</p>

<p>"Ah, that's... Umm......"</p>

<p>A cafe in Seongsu-dong.</p>

<p>Eve, who had been waiting for Erzel in that corner seat, went straight to the point instead of beating around the bush.</p>

<p>"Actually..., ………. …."<br>"...You want to know how to look like an attractive woman to Do-won?"</p>

<p>"Don't say it so loud...! Whisper, whisper..."<br>"Ah..., well..., I get the gist of it..."</p>

<p>"……."<br>"What should I do. My advice won't be of any help."</p>

<p>Then Erzel said playfully.</p>

<p>"I was just standing still next to Do-won, and he was all flustered and fidgety?"<br>"...Huh?"</p>

<p>Actually, rather than being flustered, I'd only seen him act with manners.</p>

<p>As a result of Erzel teasing her because she was curious about her friend's reaction, Eve's expression darkened slightly, not knowing the truth.</p>

<p>"Really...? Just by being next to him?"<br>"Yeah."</p>

<p>"...Then I guess my charm is just..."<br>"...Pfft."</p>

<p>...The truth comes a little later.<br>I'll let you know quietly after the counseling is all over.</p>
