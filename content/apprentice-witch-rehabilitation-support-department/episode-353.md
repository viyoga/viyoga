<p>...I dozed off for a moment.</p>

<p>A warm, fluffy bed and a languid body.<br>It was the perfect environment for falling asleep, so I expected this would happen, but I never thought I'd fall asleep so suddenly without any warning.</p>

<p>At this rate, it's not like I was hit with sleep magic or something.<br>I lightly rubbed my eyes, threw off the blanket tucked up to my chin, and woke up.</p>

<p>Because of that, the wet towel placed on my forehead for good measure fell off with a thud.<br>She said it wouldn't mean much, but it was an item Neria forced me to buy in the end.<br>Judging by the fact that it hasn't dried, either Neria took good care of it, or not as much time has passed as I thought.<br>It must be one of the two.</p>

<p>I folded it neatly again and set it aside.</p>

<p>"Ugh..."</p>

<p>Even though I'd woken up, the poison in my body remained the same, so I didn't feel recovered at all.<br>I just wanted to stay lying down forever, but I checked my smartphone anyway...</p>

<p>Hmm. Exactly 2 hours.<br>It was just the right time for lunch.<br>Since all I did today was toss and turn and take a catnap, I wasn't as hungry as I thought I'd be.</p>

<p>Is Neria not in the bedroom because she's preparing lunch?<br>I tried twitching my nose to check, but I couldn't tell.</p>

<p>The only things she can cook are microwaving frozen food and making mandu-guk...<br>There's absolutely none of that smell.<br>Only the characteristically sweet scent of witches is faintly detectable.</p>

<p>Just where and what on earth is she doing?<br>Wondering if she might be taking a shower, I forced my body up.</p>

<p>It was right after I left the bedroom.</p>

<p>"……!"<br>"……?"</p>

<p>Neria was lying comfortably on the living room sofa, holding her smartphone.<br>Since I had taken over the bed and was sleeping soundly without permission, she must have been considerate not to wake me... or so I thought.<br>If it were just that, it would have been a rather heartwarming sight.</p>

<p>As I came out of the bedroom, Neria made a somewhat surprised expression for some reason.<br>She then pressed her lips tightly together, her lip corners trembling faintly.</p>

<p>...What is it?<br>Is she holding back laughter?<br>Feeling an inexplicable suspicion, I approached Neria.</p>

<p>"Were you here because you were worried I'd wake up?"<br>"Y-yeah...! That's right. Probably."</p>

<p>"You could've just stayed by my side. Like when you and Orca came to find me last time."</p>

<p>Did she say it was a hugging pillow?<br>Anyway, we ended up sharing the bed with some weird excuse.</p>

<p>I sat next to Neria's crown with a tired expression, telling that story.<br>As I said earlier, it's because this kid was lying horizontally on the sofa.</p>

<p>"...Ahem, hmm."<br>"……?"</p>

<p>But,<br>This time, she even deliberately turned her body so her feet were facing me.</p>

<p>The suspicion I've been feeling since a moment ago is turning into certainty.</p>

<p>"...Why are you suddenly giving me your feet?"<br>"Ah, I just wanted you to give me a massage. It's been tough nursing you all this time."</p>

<p>"Nursing? You really did?"<br>"I... I kept the towel moist with water."</p>

<p>It was indeed wet.<br>Leaving aside whether that's the kind of nursing that would actually improve my condition, it's a fact that Neria took care with the towel.</p>

<p>But what is this unpleasant feeling, like grains of sand in my shoes...?</p>

<p>"Hmm..."</p>

<p>I tried to find a clue through deduction alone, but unfortunately, my head lacks that kind of talent.<br>In the end, I started using a different method.</p>

<p>"Something's suspicious."<br>"Su-suspicious my foot... ...Kyaa...?!"</p>

<p>It just so happened that Neria's small bare feet were on my side.<br>When I lightly tickled her soft soles, Neria tried to pull her foot away as if to escape.</p>

<p>But even so, Neria, a shut-in witch, couldn't beat me in strength.<br>She tugged my hand holding her ankle just a tiny bit, but lacking strength, it soon returned to its original spot.</p>

<p>Then, torture again.</p>

<p>"Kyahat..., hey, wait a sec...! It tickles......"<br>"Speak honestly. What did you do wrong. You know, don't you?"</p>

<p>"I'll tell you! I said I'll tell you...!"</p>

<p>In less than 10 seconds, Neria waved the white flag and fumbled to hand me her smartphone.<br>To be precise, with the camera turned on.</p>

<p>"……."</p>

<p>I wondered what she meant by handing it over like this, but the moment I saw my face, I understood why.</p>

<p>...It wasn't that she was trying to talk about my condition or anything.<br>It was because black letters, written as if with a brush, were clearly inscribed on one of my cheeks.</p>

<p>The act itself is childish, but the content of the writing is even more childish.<br>'Neria got fucked and went home,' seriously, even middle school boys wouldn't write something like this.</p>

<p>Not a kid, but what the hell...</p>

<p>"...That's why the towel's damp. You just scribble and get it wet at the same time, and you're done."<br>"I really did nurse you. I prayed to the Moon Lady like crazy for you to get better fast."</p>

<p>"If we just draw out the poison by evening, it'll be over..."<br>"Then I guess my prayer's about to be answered."</p>

<p>"...Fine. Let's say that's true."</p>

<p>It's absurd, but it's not exactly a prank worth chewing someone out over.<br>As long as Neria didn't have malicious intent, no matter how much she scribbled, it'd wash off with water easily enough.</p>

<p>She must've been bored while I was sleeping.<br>If anything, the one who deserves to be cursed at right now is me, for being unable to work at the Rehabilitation Support Division because my body feels like shit.</p>

<p>That's why I feel sorry, and<br>I understand how Neria feels...</p>

<p>I was trying to let it slide.</p>

<p>"Here, your phone...... wait."</p>

<p>Bottom left of the camera app.<br>A photo of someone sleeping soundly caught my eye.</p>

<p>"......Ah."<br>"……."</p>

<p>Who else would it be.<br>It's a picture of me.</p>

<p>A photo with a wet towel on the forehead, and even a strange victory declaration still intact on the cheek.</p>

<p>…Honestly, I did want to delete it.</p>

<p>"You, you take a picture of me too."<br>"…What kind of photo?"</p>

<p>"Uh, um, uh…."<br>"……."</p>

<p>"Creampie, and drawing a tally mark on the side…?"<br>"…That thing you did last time when Lady Erzel was watching beside you?"</p>

<p>"Doing it multiple times, and taking a photo every time you add another tally mark… that's not bad either."</p>

<p>If this is also considered one of Neria's memories,<br>I can't just thoughtlessly delete it.</p>

<p>"...Want to take a picture?"<br>"……."</p>

<p>In the end, only the remaining capacity decreased.</p>

<p>*****</p>

<p>- I will come this evening, so know that.</p>

<p>...Master, haven't you been meeting Nam Do-won too often lately?<br>A pointless thought crossed her mind, but Hayes didn't let it show.</p>

<p>The ultimate reason for doing that is because it's the most efficient means of recharging magical power.<br>Besides, Master, as seen from the side, is someone who buries herself in the books of her study and never stops researching, except when she steps out for a breath of fresh air.<br>She might even be going out and dealing with monsters of severe danger every time she leaves!</p>

<p>When someone like that wants to recharge her magical power, what could her disciple, Hayes, possibly say?<br>Even if they can't help, they shouldn't hold us back.</p>

<p>"Haa..."</p>

<p>It's been quite a while since I holed up in the underground workshop.<br>Hayes, leaning comfortably against the chair's backrest, stretched out fully and then checked the time.</p>

<p>6:00 PM.<br>It feels like only a moment since I last checked the clock, but it's already time to prepare dinner.<br>Right on cue, the smartphone alarm started ringing.</p>

<p>So, just as I was finishing up a quick cleanup before leaving and catching my breath.</p>

<p>"...Ah, right."</p>

<p>Remembering what Master had said during the day, I examined the right wall of the underground workshop.<br>I wasn't sure what to use as the reference point for 'right,' but intuitively, it's probably based on the entrance.</p>

<p>Thinking that way, I found it.<br>The ventilation equipment Master had mentioned.<br>Hayes's gaze caught on a square vent, about the size of two palms, on the upper part of the right wall.</p>

<p>Since she had never felt the need for ventilation until now, to Hayes, it was just a piece of interior decor that occasionally caught her eye.</p>

<p>Finding a switch at waist level on the lower part of the wall, she muttered quietly.</p>

<p>"Is this it...?"</p>

<p>This seems to be the switch.<br>It looks like it'll be incredibly noisy if I turn it on.<br>But since Master specifically mentioned this, maybe she means for me to turn it on regularly?</p>

<p>She told me not to worry about it, but things like this are exactly what you're supposed to handle on your own initiative.</p>

<p>- Kwaaaa</p>

<p>Just as I thought. That's why it's usually kept off.<br>Even the sound echoes a bit in here, so at this volume, the noise would probably reach all the way up the stairs...</p>

<p>Hayes, scratching her ear for a moment because of the racket, looked at her smartphone clock with a frown still on her face.</p>

<p>The initial plan was to finish everything from meal prep to dinner with this turned on, but it seems better to change the plan.<br>Let's wait just about 3 minutes and then go up.</p>

<p>Since the prep time was scheduled with plenty of leeway anyway, wasting about 3 minutes here won't cause any issues for dinner.</p>

<p>"……."</p>

<p>...But.<br>Why did Master suddenly bring up ventilation?</p>

<p>"I haven't handled a poison that sensitive recently, though..."</p>

<p>Besides, since those kinds of things are isolated and handled separately, ventilation wasn't strictly necessary either.</p>

<p>Still, I wonder why Master brought up the subject of ventilation?<br>Could it mean I might have to handle such poison soon?</p>

<p>"Unless the toxicity in the air threatens a witch, that's not it either..."</p>

<p>Just what could be the reason.<br>Hayes's eyes, which had been narrowing her brow as she said that, stared blankly at the wall for a moment.</p>

<p>"...Nam Do-won."</p>

<p>It could be a problem for Mr. Nam Do-won, who is an ordinary human.</p>

<p>"Could it be that symptoms have already appeared, and he's contacted Master...?"</p>

<p>Sadly, if so, the pieces roughly fit together.</p>

<p>The very fact that Master suddenly brought up the topic of ventilation itself means it was because of a contact from Nam Do-won.</p>

<p>He arrived home and suddenly started vomiting blood... or something like that.</p>

<p>"...No. If the symptoms were that serious, Master would have rushed over immediately..."</p>

<p>Even if not that, he would have sent Hayes to go detoxify him.<br>But Master simply stayed holed up in the mansion's study today, immersed in research.</p>

<p>He produced enough discarded poison that he deemed failures to easily amount to several liters.</p>

<p>Then, have only relatively mild symptoms appeared?<br>So that's why I contacted Master, and Master was planning to detoxify him if Nam Do-won came by this evening...</p>

<p>"……."</p>

<p>Is Hayes<br>not responsible at all?</p>

<p>Hayes, who left the ventilation equipment to gather dust,<br>has she not created another reason to apologize to Nam Do-won?</p>

<p>...Even so,<br>is that too harsh a judgment?</p>

<p>It's a problem Nam Do-won himself should have paid more attention to,<br>is it really okay for Hayes to bear no responsibility at all?</p>

<p>"……."</p>

<p>…A bit of the responsibility,<br>seems to lie with me.</p>
