<p>"I can't make someone who says they're in pain work at a flower shop... Just rest. Go over there to the chair."<br>"Is that okay?"</p>

<p>"Do I look like a bad witch who works even sick people like slaves?"<br>"Well, not exactly..."</p>

<p>"Unless I'm so busy I absolutely need to borrow your hand, that's not the case. So rest when I tell you to rest."<br>"Haa... Thanks for understanding, Orca. For helping me today, when my body's back to normal next time..."</p>

<p>"No need to pay me back next time. ...I have something simple I want to do in a little bit."<br>"Later?"</p>

<p>"Because I have a photo I just received from Neria."<br>"……."</p>

<p>After finishing all the daytime schedules, it was already evening.<br>Walking out of Orca's flower shop, raindrops that I didn't know when they started were hitting the asphalt and breaking apart.</p>

<p>Not that heavy. Thin raindrops that couldn't even make proper puddles.<br>The timing was coincidentally suspicious enough that the thought 'Could this be a bad omen?' brushed past my mind...</p>

<p>After all, I've seen plenty of rain when Neria and Lord Erzel were by my side.<br>Superstition is just superstition.</p>

<p>On my way out, Orca tried to lend me an umbrella, so I gratefully accepted it, saying I'd put it to good use.<br>Then, because I was still tired even though I'd slept during the day, I pressed my eyelids with my fingertips for a moment.<br>A taxi stopped with perfect timing, I got in the back seat, and leisurely arrived at Eden's mansion.</p>

<p>"Thank you."</p>

<p>The light rain was still falling here, too.<br>The only difference was that instead of the cozy alleyway in front of the flower shop, a cold, massive gate blocked my view.<br>It was like seeing Hayes's attitude toward me.</p>

<p>...And if that's what I have to do today.</p>

<p>"...Haa."</p>

<p>Using the opportunity Eden created.<br>Trying to change Hayes's attitude, even just a little.</p>

<p>For that, I pressed the doorbell firmly, hypnotizing myself that today was my first and last chance.</p>

<p>- Beep</p>

<p>Without even the process of asking, "Who are you?"<br>The lock disengaged, and the side door opened.<br>It seems she was waiting out front for me again today, timing it for when I'd arrive.</p>

<p>I tilted my umbrella to pass through, then cut straight across the garden center, which was growing wet with rain.</p>

<p>Then, folding my umbrella at the entrance, I twisted the doorknob.</p>

<p>"……."<br>"...Ah, Ms. Hayes."</p>

<p>Hayes was immediately visible, leaning lightly against the wall.</p>

<p>A worn cardigan.<br>And a slightly stretched-out T-shirt.<br>Hayes, wearing the same combination of clothes she always favored, stared at me with a bland expression.</p>

<p>It was hard to say she was 'waiting for me'—her attitude was lukewarm at best, as if she were only here because of the Master's orders.</p>

<p>"……."<br>"……."</p>

<p>...Maybe it was because of what happened yesterday.<br>The air surrounding us felt strangely awkward.</p>

<p>No, not just strangely awkward.<br>It was suffocating, even more so than our first meeting a couple of months ago.<br>Back then, Hayes was businesslike, but at least she spoke to me, didn't she?</p>

<p>Anyone could tell it was because of yesterday's caressing...<br>...but bringing it up would be tantamount to suicide.<br>For now, it would be best to try and break the ice with a different topic.</p>

<p>It's not exactly the atmosphere to demand another apology right away, just as Eden ordered.</p>

<p>"...Ah, where should I put the umbrella? People like me, lowly ones, just spread them out in front of the house to dry."<br>"You can just handle it. Do I have to explain even things like that one by one?"</p>

<p>"Then... can I just spread it out at the entrance?"<br>"Sure, whatever."</p>

<p>I thought a rich person's house would have an umbrella stand that dries them automatically, but I guess not.<br>Or maybe they could just use a simple drying spell.</p>

<p>I'm not oblivious enough to ask Hayes to do even that.<br>I set the umbrella down with an awkward smile.</p>

<p>"Where is Lady Eden right now?"<br>"Well, she's probably in the bedroom. Or the study. She'll be in one of those two places."</p>

<p>"Yes. I'll go look for her."</p>

<p>...No, that's not it.<br>Shouldn't I have forced the conversation with Hayes to continue, even if artificially?</p>

<p>After easing the mood a little, I should have brought up that pathetic excuse... 'Lady Hayes, actually, I've been feeling unwell since yesterday... Unbelievable, there's a trace of poison in my body...'</p>

<p>"Nam Do-won."</p>

<p>Just then, as I was about to walk away with no other choice, Hayes stepped slightly in front of me, blocking my path.<br>The gap is wide enough that if I wanted to, I could just ignore her and walk right past.</p>

<p>"What is it?"<br>"……."</p>

<p>The answer didn't come immediately.<br>On top of that, Hayes, who had been staring at me blankly just a moment ago, had even turned her gaze slightly to the side.</p>

<p>After maintaining her silence for a moment longer, she spoke in a tone that sounded like she was nitpicking point by point.</p>

<p>"You shouldn't answer that you'll look for it thoroughly."<br>"...Huh?"</p>

<p>What kind of sudden nonsense is this?</p>

<p>"Shouldn't you be questioning why I don't know Master's exact location?"<br>"……?"</p>

<p>The following words were just as absurd.<br>Usually, Hayes always pinpointed Eden's location accurately, but one out of every three or four times, she'd speak as if she 'didn't know well.'</p>

<p>When I answered back then that I'd find out on my own, she said nothing.<br>Why suddenly now?</p>

<p>Do I really have to question it?</p>

<p>"...Why don't you know? Is there some special reason?"</p>

<p>The situation itself is hard to understand, but still, it's a good opportunity to exchange a few more words with Hayes.</p>

<p>That's why Hayes, forcing out a curiosity that didn't exist even a single gram, cleared her throat with a 'ahem, hmm-' and continued speaking.</p>

<p>"It's because I was alone in the underground workshop after dinner was completely over, and I just came up now."<br>"Ah... something like that happened..."</p>

<p>"I need to help with Master's research and raise my own achievements, too. I came up after experimenting with a whole bunch of various poisons."<br>"...You're diligent. I'll cheer you on."</p>

<p>I see.<br>Thanks to that, I do understand why Hayes didn't know her master's location.</p>

<p>...Honestly speaking, it makes you wonder what the hell you're supposed to do about it.</p>

<p>It's not a particularly important story, is it?<br>You're used to Eden being in the bedroom or study with 99.9% probability, aren't you?<br>Was I just hoping he'd understand the reason?</p>

<p>I was standing there dazed, not knowing what kind of reaction to give.</p>

<p>Hayes, adjusting her cardigan slightly, spoke in a lower voice than before.</p>

<p>"... Mr. Nam Do-won, are you okay?"<br>"……?"</p>

<p>... Wait a minute.<br>It's good that I'm somehow managing to converse with Hayes, but.<br>Why is the flow of conversation like this?<br>Doesn't it feel like something was suddenly skipped over in the middle?</p>

<p>"Okay? What do you mean by that all of a sudden..."<br>"Y-you went to the underground workshop, didn't you. Yesterday."</p>

<p>"That's right."<br>"Back then, I perfectly controlled all the poison I accidentally released... but it was hard to perceive and control all the various poisons spread in the air, especially those that act through inhalation or skin absorption."</p>

<p>A thoroughly drunk middle-aged man.<br>Or the sensation of talking to a senile old man, for a moment.</p>

<p>Listening to Hayes's rambling voice, I finally started to see her purpose.</p>

<p>And right on cue, Hayes finally brought up the main topic.</p>

<p>"By any chance..., were there any painful spots after you returned home...?"</p>

<p>Finally realizing her true intentions, I barely held back the urge to ask, 'Did you beat around the bush all this time just to say that?'</p>

<p>...She's a witch who's not as articulate as I thought.<br>Well, she did say she lived for a long time in a mountain village with hardly any people.</p>

<p>Telling long-winded stories, or...<br>Subtly steering the conversation toward a specific topic... that might be an advanced technique for Hayes.</p>

<p>"Come to think of it, her condition did seem a bit strange."<br>"How exactly was it strange?"</p>

<p>"My body aches all over... and I'm tired... I just thought it was my condition, but listening to you, Lady Hayes, it does seem a bit suspicious."<br>"Then..."</p>

<p>The flow is good.<br>I was going to bring up the poison anyway, but thanks to Hayes, I saved myself the trouble.</p>

<p>No wonder the reaction when I was caressing her was better than I thought.<br>Unlike how she tried to push me away with her mouth saying it was weird, it seems the pleasure itself was right up Hayes's alley.</p>

<p>I'd guessed as much from the start, but this confirms it.</p>

<p>As I was cheering internally, Hayes held out her hand to me.</p>

<p>"Wanna give me your hand? I'll check if it's really because of the poison."<br>"Here you go."</p>

<p>This is that thing I experienced through Eden yesterday.<br>I don't know the principle well, but just by holding my hand for a moment, she checked my body's condition and even finished controlling the poison.</p>

<p>"...There really is poison. Even if it's a trace amount."<br>"It's not like I'm going to die from this, right?"</p>

<p>"It's woefully insufficient for that, but if I leave it alone, your body will keep feeling unwell. I'll extract it, so wait."</p>

<p>Since I'd already seen her skills, I decided to trust and leave it to her; in a short time, Hayes instantly grasped my physical condition.<br>After that, every ten-odd seconds, my condition began to improve step by step.</p>

<p>Naturally, she couldn't match Eden, who finished things in the blink of an eye, but considering the few hundred years' difference in experience, it's not strange at all.</p>

<p>"How is it now? Are you okay?"<br>"Much better. I was staggering all day, worried I'd show an unsightly appearance even in front of Lady Eden. So it was simply because of the poison."</p>

<p>When I answered while pretending not to know, Hayes was easily fooled and nodded.</p>

<p>Since things are wrapping up like this,<br>What comes next is,<br>WWE time with Hayes.</p>

<p>"Thank you, Hayes. Then I'll be on my way to see Eden..."<br>"...Ah, wait a moment."</p>

<p>As I tried to pass by Hayes as if I were really leaving, she urgently grabbed my hem, hesitatingly opening her lips.</p>

<p>"Before you go, that... I have one thing I'm curious about."<br>"What is it?"</p>

<p>"...Um, well."<br>"……."</p>

<p>"Tomorrow morning... we're going back, right? You're always like this when you come late."<br>"I suppose that's likely. Though who knows, if my schedule with Lady Eden ends early."</p>

<p>"Hmm..."<br>"...Are you asking because you want me to leave a bit early?"</p>

<p>"That's not it, it's just..."<br>"……."</p>

<p>"...Never mind. Your master is waiting, so just hurry up and go."</p>

<p>With those final words, Hayes vanished toward the underground workshop.</p>
