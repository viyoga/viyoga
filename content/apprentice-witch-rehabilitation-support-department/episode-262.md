<p>Line 2, where quite a few people are still visible at this hour, same as always.<br>Lucky enough to snag a seat, I leaned back comfortably against the backrest and let out a sigh.<br>Even while waiting for the subway, I saw a few people who were pretty drunk, but there were none around me, so I was at least a little relieved.</p>

<p>A. Dorothy has recently found herself enjoying idol activities!</p>

<p>B. Since Dorothy's come this far, she's going to try a real idol experience for a bit before quitting!</p>

<p>Aha. So this is what being a real idol feels like.<br>It's getting boring.<br>I should go study magic hard.</p>

<p>Caught on a secret date with an unidentified man.<br>Photos were taken in front of the motel.<br>Then, deliberately caught again in the lobby of some OB-GYN clinic.<br>And even after that, the paparazzi spotted a car shaking violently, screech—screech—.<br>Dorothy, expelled from the idol group.<br>Now I have to go study magic hard.</p>

<p>Quitting like this.<br>Both of them, anyway, the flow of events itself is similar.</p>

<p>But the key is whether Master Rin will permit her disciple's brief deviation.</p>

<p>That's what I think, probably.</p>

<p>'Mmm...'</p>

<p>...It doesn't seem like it'll work!<br>Since she's already let it slide, and let it slide, and let it slide, and eventually even called me, it seems.<br>If I tell her to wait again, I might just end up taking the blame for her frustration.</p>

<p>'This is difficult.'</p>

<p>Even if I were to chime in with 'let's increase quantity instead of quality since she's being obedient,' there's too much I'd have to give up.<br>Honestly, I kind of want to do all sorts of 'special things' with Dorothy too, so...<br>For example, Dorothy's stage outfit from the fancam that just got uploaded, or the outfit from the photo she posted on social media... no, anyway.</p>

<p>I have things I want to do too, so it's a real shame to just give up obediently.</p>

<p>How on earth can this be resolved?</p>

<p>"……."</p>

<p>...I don't know...</p>

<p>*****</p>

<p>That's why, as I was greasing the frying pan with butter, it had already been 30 minutes since I sent Dorothy a message asking if she was free to talk for a bit.</p>

<p>"...She hasn't read it."</p>

<p>I didn't know the schedule would be this packed even before debut.<br>It's this hard for her, a witch herself, so I don't know how the other debut group members are holding up.<br>Maybe it's because, unlike me at twenty, there are many members whose age still starts with a 1... and so on.<br>I remember having conversations roughly like that.</p>

<p>So I guess she's probably fast asleep right now and can't see the message.<br>Since I've seen scenes on TV variety shows a few times where celebrities sleep in the car on the way to their schedules.</p>

<p>If it was going to be like this, I should've just sent a message last night.<br>I got up from the dining table with a belated regret.</p>

<p>I'll probably check it in the middle of getting through the morning schedule.</p>

<p>"Still no contact?"<br>"Yeah."</p>

<p>"Hmm hmm hmm. At this rate, did she lose her smartphone? Or maybe the company confiscated and is managing all of them."<br>"Could be..."</p>

<p>Because I still hadn't received Dorothy's contact.<br>Lying comfortably on the sofa with Neria, I served as a backrest for her as she gamed, fiddling with my silent smartphone.</p>

<p>...Or not? Am I thinking this way because I don't know the entertainment industry well?<br>Maybe Neria's words were really right...</p>

<p>"...Huh."</p>

<p>It disappeared.<br>The 1 behind the message.</p>

<p>"Just saw the message..., ...what?"</p>

<p>Just as I was about to tell Neria, a call came in within that short time.<br>She really called the moment she saw the message; even thinking that way, it was an absurdly fast speed.</p>

<p>The one thing I'm worried about is whether Dorothy's side is in a situation where she can be contacted right now - though she did answer.<br>She must have called because there's no problem. Yeah.</p>

<p>"Hello?"<br>- Hellooo..........</p>

<p>"Lady Dorothy, um... are you okay? You sound like you just woke up."<br>- I'm fine.... Didn't you send the KakaoTalk, Do-won, three hours ago....</p>
