<p>"Orca. Are you at the shelter right now?"<br>"Yes. And Hayes is with me."</p>

<p>"Lady Hayes...?"</p>

<p>Fortunately, the burning heat subsided immediately.<br>Of course, she hadn't confirmed it for certain with a mirror in this situation...,<br>When she pressed the back of her hand to her cheek, she felt only a normal body temperature, contrary to her worries.</p>

<p>Thanks to that, Hayes let out a sigh of relief and quietly steadied her breathing.</p>

<p>While she acted as composed as possible, Nam Do-won opened the flower shop door and entered, heading to the shelter with familiar steps.</p>

<p>...Meeting that man two days in a row,<br>At best, it only happened occasionally, when it got late and she stayed the night, leaving the next morning.<br>There hadn't really been a case like this before.<br>Since our schedules overlapped and we ran into each other like this, it feels strange.</p>

<p>Would 'awkward' be the right word?<br>Or 'uncomfortable'?<br>Or maybe 'self-conscious'?</p>

<p>No suitable word came to mind to accurately describe my current mood.</p>

<p>"This is the first time seeing you here, Miss Hayes. I wonder if I'm interrupting the two of you..."<br>"Th-that..."<br>"No! We were just talking about the succulent you gifted last time, and we just finished."</p>

<p>"Ah, the succulent."</p>

<p>It had been taken out on the table until just a moment ago, but the timing wasn't good.<br>Hayes happened to have stored it with inventory magic, so Mr. Nam Do-won asks without knowing the reason.</p>

<p>Hayes, who had been quiet until Orca finished answering, glanced around and then brought out the succulent pot again.</p>

<p>"You've raised it really well? It's not mushy at all, and it hasn't gotten leggy..."<br>"I wonder if I already explained that. Mr. Do-won, you fool."</p>

<p>"...Hmm, come to think of it, you said it was all over. So, Lady Hayes, are you going home now?"<br>"Probably? Hayes has her own schedule, after all."<br>"……?"</p>

<p>But then.<br>One thing that grated on Hayes's ears.<br>Nam Do-won's way of speaking.</p>

<p>Of course, Mr. Nam Do-won spoke comfortably like this even in front of Neria.<br>And the way he spoke in the video Orca filmed was similarly lacking in manners.</p>

<p>But didn't she definitely not use honorifics when chatting casually with Orca?<br>Besides, Orca seems like the kind of girl who's comfortable with honorifics, just like Hayes herself, so there must have been a burden to dropping them.</p>

<p>Hayes, rewinding her memories, hesitated and then asked while putting the flowerpot away again.</p>

<p>"Um, Mr. Nam Do-won. I'm sorry to suddenly ask this."<br>"Huh?"</p>

<p>"...Since when did you speak so comfortably? To Orca?"<br>"Ah, it hasn't been long. I think it's been about a month now."</p>

<p>"A month..."<br>"Since we speak comfortably anyway when charging mana, you gave me permission to do so normally as well."</p>

<p>A month ago overlaps roughly with the time Hayes had conducted a short 'test'.<br>It seems something happened with Orca around that time.</p>

<p>Since it wasn't something to think deeply about, Hayes brushed it off lightly and continued with what she wanted to say.</p>

<p>"Just in case... You... That..."<br>"……?"</p>

<p>"Hmm..."</p>

<p>But after only a few words, she struggled for a long while before barely managing the next one.</p>

<p>"...Step outside for a moment. It's a topic that's awkward to discuss where Orca can hear. ...Sorry, Orca. I'll borrow Nam Do-won for a bit."<br>"Sure... For a little while is fine..."</p>

<p>Hayes immediately stood up and headed outside the flower shop.</p>

<p>*****</p>

<p>...What? What is it?</p>

<p>Dragged outside by Hayes without knowing why, I ended up heading to a convenience store at the end of an alley, far from the flower shop, along with her.<br>When was it? It's that convenience store I stopped by briefly to buy food when there was a blackout in this area.</p>

<p>The reason being, Orca is the Witch of Flowers, so there's a chance she could eavesdrop even from a distance like in front of the flower shop, or so Hayes claimed.</p>

<p>Knowing Orca's personality, I don't think she'd pull a stunt like that, though....<br>Hayes looked like she wanted to talk as secretly as possible.</p>

<p>I followed her lead, but.</p>

<p>"……."<br>"……."<br>"That'll be 2,500 won."</p>

<p>So what's this really about?</p>

<p>Hayes, who'd been busy avoiding me for a while, suddenly started talking to me a lot more ever since she saw the article photo of Dorothy yesterday.</p>

<p>I handed her one of the 500ml 1+1 coffees I'd just bought and asked.</p>

<p>"What is this regarding?"<br>"...It's a thought that occurred to me after seeing what you did earlier."</p>

<p>"Yes."</p>

<p>As I walked out of the convenience store with Hayes, I could feel the gazes of the people walking nearby snap sharply in our direction.<br>And the way they quickly pretended not to notice the moment our eyes met.</p>

<p>...I've gotten somewhat used to it while hanging around with Neria, but it seems like it'll take a long time before I become numb to these exhausting reactions.</p>

<p>Unlike me, Hayes turned back toward the convenience store as if it were nothing new and opened her coffee lid.</p>

<p>"Don't tell me you speak informally to your master, too?"<br>"......? Huh?"</p>

<p>"Informal speech. Like you did with Orca earlier."</p>

<p>Would I do that?<br>I want to answer that so badly it's killing me, but this is a rare chance for conversation.<br>In a situation where I should be even more polite than usual, I can't just blurt out my true feelings.</p>

<p>"I use honorifics diligently when I'm with Lady Eden. Just like I'm doing now in my conversation with Lady Hayes."<br>"Hmm..."</p>

<p>"Speaking comfortably with other witches like Neria or Orca is, of course, because the witches themselves permitted it. ...Honestly, isn't that only natural?"<br>"Right. You wouldn't have just started speaking informally out of nowhere."</p>

<p>Hayes answered nonchalantly, wet her throat with a sip of coffee, and then glanced at my face again.</p>

<p>...It might be a strange way to put it, but it felt similar to the gazes of the passersby earlier.<br>Even the way she hesitantly averted her eyes first when our eyes met was exactly the same.</p>

<p>In that state, she muttered quietly.</p>

<p>"For the record, I have absolutely no intention of permitting it."<br>"...Is that so."</p>

<p>"We should at least keep a minimum boundary. I'm an apprentice witch. You're an ordinary human."<br>"Y-yes..."</p>

<p>"I know that a witch compromising a little to fulfill a man's fantasy is quite efficient, but even so, that should stop at recharging mana. Abusing it normally could rather result in lowering the stimulation when it really matters..."</p>

<p>Since she was stating something so obvious unnecessarily, I pretended to listen while taking a sip of the coffee I'd just bought.</p>

<p>While doing so, Hayes finished her roughly one-minute nagging session refreshingly and tilted the coffee again as if her throat was parched.</p>

<p>Then, clearing her throat loudly, she,<br>As if just passing by.</p>

<p>"By the way, Mr. Nam Do-won."<br>"Yes. I was wrong. I'll be more careful from now on."</p>

<p>"No, not that. I just wanted to ask something since we're on the subject."<br>"...Go ahead."</p>

<p>"That is..."</p>

<p>He threw it out casually.</p>

<p>He brought up a different topic from the nagging just now.</p>

<p>"S-sex... you know, speaking informally to a witch during it."<br>"...Yes."</p>

<p>"......Does it feel that good?"<br>"……."</p>

<p>"In the video you filmed with Orca too..., you spoke in a really rude tone."<br>"I... guess I did."</p>

<p>"The result was that I came so much that semen was trickling back out through the gaps..."</p>

<p>It had already been over a month since I filmed the video for Hayes.<br>Not a single trace remained in my memory of what I had said to Orca back then.</p>

<p>But judging by the words I usually spat out...<br>...the intensity is probably highest when I'm recharging mana with the Team Leader.<br>With Neria or Orca, it feels like things just go with the flow.</p>

<p>I doubt I said anything too extreme.<br>Figuring as much, I lowered my voice so it wouldn't reach the passersby's ears and replied.</p>

<p>"There isn't really a huge difference, but still, honorifics feel too stiff while our bodies are overlapping. That's why it ends up feeling like we're doing... work, you could say."<br>"……."</p>

<p>"Since it's an act that involves pleasure, isn't it better if we both feel good?"</p>

<p>Strictly speaking, it is work.</p>

<p>Hayes must not know this story, so I swallowed it down.</p>

<p>"...I see now. So that's the excuse you've been using all this time to speak informally to witches."<br>"Perish the thought. If that were true, I would have said something insolent to Lady Eden as well, since she is the most remarkable witch I know."</p>

<p>"Unlike other apprentice witches, isn't it because you're afraid of our master?"<br>"She is scary... but she's also someone who did a bunny girl cosplay for me. If I had harbored strange intentions, I might have made other demands."</p>

<p>"Hmm..."<br>"For example, like I did with Orca, laying Lady Eden down and having sex..."</p>

<p>...I will never have that kind of thing with Eden in my lifetime.<br>She's probably doing that every single day with her secret boyfriend, the one she even hid from her disciple.</p>

<p>Right after I blurted out something that even I couldn't tell was a joke or the truth.</p>

<p>Hayes, who had been fidgeting with a convenience store coffee, spoke with a surprisingly serious air.</p>

<p>"Anyway, going by your logic, that means speaking informally is 'mutually' pleasant, right?"<br>"...Right?"</p>

<p>"It's not just you feeling good; the witch feels good too. Am I right?"<br>"……."</p>

<p>Could this be,<br>[Since it's unavoidable for the sake of semen, I'll permit you to speak informally to me too] kind of development?</p>

<p>Since I was working my brain hard for the first time in a while, my answer came a beat late.</p>

<p>"I can't say for certain, but at least the witches I've known were all like that."<br>"...Is that so."</p>

<p>"If you're curious about the effect, I think it'd be good to set aside some time later and run a test like last time..."<br>"No. There's no need for a grand test like back then."</p>

<p>Hayes, cutting it off in a single stroke.<br>While I was inwardly disappointed, she poked me with the bottom of the convenience store coffee she was holding.</p>

<p>"Let's just try it lightly right now. I'll be the judge of whether it really works and whether it's rudeness I can tolerate."</p>

<p>...Proposing it as if it were nothing, unlike Hayes, who used to growl at everything.</p>

<p>"Ah, no swearing. Swearing feels bad."<br>"Have you ever been sworn at by a human?"</p>

<p>"...Oh, when I was riding a motorcycle. There was a weird person who did that."<br>"Something like that happened..."</p>

<p>I only realized after asking, but that's highly likely a lie.<br>Being sworn at by a human... she's probably talking about when I entered Hayes's dream.</p>

<p>Back then... I remember swearing at the Team Leader, but with the intensity lowered a lot.</p>

<p>So let's just go with the concept of talking to a friend I've known for a day or two.</p>

<p>"Then, do we do it right away?"<br>"...Try it."</p>

<p>I stopped speaking because a passerby happened to walk past us and enter the convenience store,</p>

<p>I briefly scanned the surroundings.</p>

<p>And spoke casually.</p>

<p>"...Hayes."<br>"……."</p>

<p>"Uh..., how was the cof..."<br>"Stop."</p>

<p>In terms of time, 5 seconds.<br>In terms of syllables, nine syllables.</p>

<p>"...It's not that great. I don't feel good either."<br>"R-really?"</p>

<p>"Keep using honorifics with me from now on. Got it?"<br>"Yes..."</p>
