<p>It would be perfect if it were a hobby as addictive as a game.<br>But there are truly all sorts of hobbies in the world besides that.</p>

<p>If you got close to Orca, you might pick up the hobby of growing flowers.<br>If you got close to Neria, it would be the game I mentioned earlier.<br>And if you got close to Dorothy... um, hmm... watching a performance? Coffee?</p>

<p>Whatever it is, you'd be able to experience something extraordinary and out of the ordinary.</p>

<p>"...Friends?"<br>"Yeah. Your name came up while the two of us were talking."</p>

<p>Right next to me, standing with hands clasped behind his back, keeping his post at ease.</p>

<p>Eden, having finished charging her mana and changed into different clothes, called Hayes over and spoke listlessly.</p>

<p>"Wasn't there something you consulted me about a while ago? That compared to the past, you feel like your skills have stagnated lately and you're just running in circles."<br>"...Yes."</p>

<p>"I wouldn't know since I've never really experienced that, so it was hard to empathize, but this man, no, Nam Do-won gave a good idea."</p>

<p>The impression that the title had become 'Nam Do-won' passed briefly.</p>

<p>From what I heard, Hayes had a worry common to anyone, and conversely, this grandmother seems to be a monster who never once hit a wall while growing up.</p>

<p>Maybe that's why she's still living researching magic even after eating over 500 years...<br>Not knowing the detailed customs of witches, I listened, roughly speculating.</p>

<p>At that moment, Eden snapped her fingers as if calling a subordinate.</p>

<p>"You give the detailed explanation."<br>"Ah, yes."</p>

<p>Taking the baton from her, I began to recount the story we had just been editing together.</p>

<p>"I know a few other apprentice witches, and they went through something similar to you, Lady Hayes."<br>"……."</p>

<p>"The common thread in the solutions I heard back then was... friends."<br>"Friends..."</p>

<p>Was it because a human, not even a fellow witch, had started the story that she found it untrustworthy?<br>Hayes, who had been listening with her mouth firmly shut for a moment, quietly repeated the word, her gaze shifting slightly away when it came to 'friends'.</p>

<p>Even if she's not showing great interest, she doesn't seem to be completely ignoring my words either.<br>For now, that's enough.<br>Even I would just brush it off if some passing monkey or chimpanzee started chattering away.<br>A witch's perspective on humans probably isn't all that different from this.</p>

<p>"Just studying hard all day long isn't the be-all and end-all. There were cases where people fully recharged by just resting for days with a friend. Or some would up and go on a trip with a friend. Some even picked up a new hobby through a friend."</p>

<p>A few of those were just made up, and Neria, who's one of the similar examples, hasn't just rested for a few days but has been resting on a scale of months, even years.<br>Anyway, that's not what's important right now.</p>

<p>To guide her pouting attention to one place, I deliberately dug deeper into the topic of hobbies.</p>

<p>"How about you try picking up a new hobby through a friend, Lady Hayes? If you change your mood like that, you might unexpectedly find a solution to what's been blocking you."<br>"……."</p>

<p>And at that moment, along with those words, Hayes's gaze turned blankly toward her master.<br>Eden, receiving the gaze, yawned "Haaam-" and answered.</p>

<p>"It doesn't matter as long as you don't reveal your real name. There are other families besides our Ineba that handle poison magic. Use them as an excuse, and I'll contact them directly later."<br>"But..."</p>

<p>"Are you worried that the meaning behind me consistently hiding you will be lost?"<br>"...Yes."</p>

<p>"You worry so much. Hmph."</p>

<p>Come to think of it, something comes to mind.<br>This job right now had the condition of absolute secrecy attached to it.</p>

<p>At the time, I leaned more toward the idea that it was a story related to 'incorrect sex education,' but judging by the atmosphere, it felt like Hayes's very existence was a secret.</p>

<p>But even an ordinary human nobody like me knows about Hayes because of some trivial matter, right?<br>So when you actually think about it, it might not be that important of a secret after all...</p>

<p>"Hooh. You're curious about the reason too? You perverted little thing."</p>

<p>Eden, glancing this way as I quietly pondered.<br>Since this atmosphere was also lighter than expected, I honestly nodded after watching the situation for a moment.</p>

<p>"Yes. Honestly, I am a little curious. Even though I'm a human with little connection to you witches."<br>"That's exactly why I can tell you."</p>

<p>"...Ah, right. I guess that makes sense."<br>"It's nothing that grand. I was just being careful because if word got out that I'd taken on another apprentice witch at this age, it would cause a stir."</p>

<p>Eden ran her thumb over her immaculate nails and continued speaking.</p>

<p>"Haven't I already raised several apprentice witches? But disciples who were mired in 'personal feelings' left much earlier than I did. So, taking on another apprentice here would obviously become gossip."<br>"……."</p>

<p>"Even among witches, in their cramped community, a tiny bit of gossip often keeps them talking for days on end. What woman would want to end up on that chopping block? Tsk."</p>

<p>...Perhaps.<br>Could it be that witches aren't as old as one might think, even with eternal youth, because of sentimental reasons like that?<br>In that sense, perhaps a witch with a personality like Lady Erzel's—dissolute yet enjoying it briefly and ending it cleanly—had the advantage in survival of the fittest.</p>

<p>As the puzzles I hadn't thought much about began to fit together one by one, Lady Eden's folding fan unfolded softly, fanning a gentle breeze.</p>

<p>"In other words, it's just that it would be troublesome and unpleasant if many witches found out."<br>"……."</p>

<p>"Anyway, I hear Cadet Nam Do-won has a few 'acquaintances who are apprentice witches.' I'd like to hear your opinion on that. Hayes."</p>

<p>At this, Hayes closed her lips and fell silent for a moment.<br>Fortunately, the wait wasn't long.</p>

<p>"...Since Master suggested it, I'll give it a try."</p>

<p>*****</p>

<p>Fortunately, this morning I had practically canceled my entire usual schedule for Hayes, so even if it suddenly changed to something else, there would be no problem.<br>In fact, when I told Neria, [I think I'll need your help for this and that reason], the reaction to a new schedule popping up was even positive.</p>

<p>So, let's try having her talk with Neria first, and if she doesn't seem to take any interest in the game, we'll move on to the plan of getting her a succulent to raise as a hobby from Orca.<br>Around the time I finished that rough outline, Hayes, having finished getting ready to go out, walked lightly to the entrance.</p>

<p>When she's at home, she always wears shabby clothes similar to what Master wears.<br>But this time, she's wearing an outfit that actually looks like she put some effort into dressing up.<br>Especially that charcoal-colored drab t-shirt and black rider jacket caught the eye.</p>

<p>Though, leaving the outfit aside.<br>As someone attending to Hayes for the day, the thing that's really nagging at me is that expression of hers.</p>

<p>It was because of that strangely annoyed expression, the kind you'd only see in a dream, that I ended up striking up a conversation for no good reason.</p>

<p>"It's the height of summer now; are you sure you'll be okay wearing a jacket like that?"<br>"You'll get hurt if you fall."</p>

<p>"Ah. So that's why you're deliberately wearing a jacket like that..."<br>"And because you cool down faster if you go fast. Does that explain it a bit?"</p>

<p>"Yes. Of course."</p>

<p>I asked a question because I felt like I should say something, but as expected, I got a reply with a tone so awkward it made me want to die.<br>She's not completely rejecting me and keeping her distance, so I guess I should consider that a relief...<br>Even though her appearance is that perfectly put together, it feels like there's a guarded high school girl inside.</p>

<p>Still, there won't be a bigger chance to build rapport than right now, right?<br>Even if I plan to borrow another apprentice witch's power, in the end, I have to get close to Hayes to be able to do the 'next' step.</p>

<p>I opened the front door and kept trying to strike up a conversation.</p>

<p>"I asked around to see if there were any apprentice witches with a free schedule right now, and it turns out there's one who likes games and is free."<br>"Hmm..."</p>

<p>Besides, this guy doesn't fit the word 'social circle' in the slightest, so even if he saw Hayes, there was a high chance he wouldn't think of Eden's face.<br>In the first place, the only people I could call friends are me and Dorothy, right?<br>That part is a bonus point.</p>

<p>"Do you happen to like games, Lady Hayes?"<br>"I've never tried."</p>

<p>"Ah..."<br>"……."</p>

<p>She said she grew up without a single friend, and sure enough.<br>A tone returned that didn't seem to have even 1mg of intention to hold a conversation this time either.</p>

<p>There's a possibility that because her favorability toward me is close to zero, that kind of tone just naturally popped out.<br>Whatever the cause, it's a tone with far too many shortcomings to keep up the haha-hoho chatter any longer.</p>

<p>On top of that, with motorcycle motion sickness overlapping, we couldn't manage a proper conversation until we got on the apartment elevator.<br>At most, it ended with an exchange of 'Are you feeling okay?' 'Yes, I'm fine...'</p>

<p>That long, drawn-out silence was finally broken only when the front door to Neria's house opened.</p>

<p>"You're here earlier than I thought? I figured you'd come around lunchtime."<br>"Lady Hayes knows how to ride a motorcycle."</p>

<p>"A motorcycle? Ooh..."</p>

<p>Today, Neria wasn't wearing her usual black unemployed shut-in look, but just a loose, comfortable T-shirt.<br>It'd be a stretch to say she dressed up more than this, so she probably wanted to show that meeting me is just that comfortable.</p>

<p>Or maybe... it'd be more accurate to call it Unemployed Shut-in Look Ver. 2, with a bit more skin exposure.<br>Neria, a little different from usual, stared at Hayes, who was standing a step behind me, and spoke.</p>

<p>"Is this the Hayes you mentioned earlier...?"<br>"...It's Hayes Velaine."</p>

<p>"Velaine~.... If it's Velaine, then probably poison...?"<br>"Th...that's right. Poison magic."</p>

<p>"Oh.... Poison magic...."<br>"……."</p>

<p>"……."<br>"……."</p>

<p>As for lying about her affiliated family, I heard the reason, so I'll let that slide.<br>I thought it'd be fine given Neria's noisy personality, but it's really awkward here too, so the conversation doesn't flow well.<br>Honestly, I brought her along expecting things to work out somehow, but there's a slight variable.</p>

<p>Still, um, I've already told Neria everything about why this happened via message.<br>Even if it's awkward now, won't it keep getting better if we spend time together a few more times?</p>

<p>While watching the creaky first meeting right next to me, Neria poked my forearm and asked a question.</p>

<p>"You said you're not in a sexual relationship with this kid, right?"<br>"……!"</p>

<p>With my back turned to Hayes, whose shoulders were flinching beside me.<br>...So that only I can see it.<br>With an expression that was 100% joking.<br>As if asking something completely obvious.</p>

<p>"...Yeah. We don't do that."<br>"I see~ Then, well, I guess there's no need to decide on an order."<br>"……."</p>

<p>Neria, having closed the front door herself, changed the subject as if what she just said was nothing.</p>

<p>"Nam Do-won said on KakaoTalk that you've never played games at all, is that true?"<br>"...Yes."</p>

<p>"Then let's start with something super easy first..., and if it suits your taste, then next~..."<br>"……."</p>

<p>"Ah, and I'm Neria Heka~..."</p>
