<p>The more I mulled it over, the more I wanted to sigh, but it felt a bit too early to just say, 'Okay, I give up,' and politely go through the refund process.</p>

<p>Of course, to put it bluntly, part of it was that I liked Hayes's looks.<br>Born a man, how could I dislike a witch's appearance?<br>That's why there's also a desire to try and forcibly fix this frosty relationship we just had.</p>

<p>But it's not *only* because of that.</p>

<p>A few days ago, when the Team Leader came to our house.<br>The reason you rushed things with Orca was because the Team Leader wanted to receive this grandmother's petition, right?<br>Which means... connecting it to wanting to look good to her... wouldn't be much of a stretch.</p>

<p>Whatever her personality, this grandmother seems to be quite a famous figure in the witch world, so it wasn't strange at all.</p>

<p>But what if I give up on this right away and report it to the Team Leader?<br>...Even if she wouldn't show it on the outside, she'd be pretty disappointed on the inside.</p>

<p>"...Hah."</p>

<p>Come to think of it, I received a lot from the Team Leader too... well, 'grace' might be the rough word for it.</p>

<p>Anyway, there's quite a lot of that kind of thing.<br>It's the duty of a human being to repay both grace and grudges by inflating them as much as possible.</p>

<p>I couldn't hold back the sigh, but I decided to try and raise the odds even a little.</p>

<p>"Then, Lady Eden. Would it be alright if I asked you a few things before I talk to your disciple? I did hear an explanation, but it was only the rough outline. I have a lot of questions."<br>"Permission granted. I'm not asking as a joke, either."</p>

<p>Eden, sitting in a chair and reading leisurely, even though I'm standing right next to her.<br>Her slender fingers turned the worn pages gently.</p>

<p>I silently dragged over the remaining chair beside Eden and sat down.<br>From a distance, it probably looks like a delinquent bothering a model student.</p>

<p>"Your disciple... I mean, I'd like to know about Hayes's personality. Or maybe her hobbies. Would that be okay?"<br>"Personality and hobbies. Personality and hobbies..."</p>

<p>Judging by her drawn-out answer, I briefly wondered if she didn't know her disciple well...<br>She adjusted her sitting posture slightly and answered in a soft, gentle tone.</p>

<p>"She has a more naive side than she looks. Should I say she trusts people easily?"<br>"Aha."</p>

<p>"I've managed to fix that scatterbrained head of his since childhood, but his nature doesn't change."<br>"Naive... That's a clue that'll help with re-education."</p>

<p>"Probably. Even if your case isn't certain, he's a kid who believes my words without a doubt."<br>"...Then, wouldn't it be easier and faster for Lady Eden to handle the re-education directly instead of me?"</p>

<p>"Oh my, dear..."</p>

<p>But I'm right.<br>I asked carefully, but all I got back was a witch's sigh.<br>Then I saw eyes glancing at me as if I were pathetic.</p>

<p>There are types like Neria or Dorothy who don't take after their masters much, but here, master and disciple are spitting images of each other, like Orca.<br>Only the atmosphere built by time differs.</p>

<p>"You inferior thing. Do you truly not understand the deeper meaning behind why I would bother using a servant to do something?"<br>"...Pride...?"</p>

<p>"Yes. That rude guess is correct. I've paid the price of my pride, so you must render service befitting of it."<br>"Y-yes, that's right."</p>

<p>"Enough of this foolish question and answer. I shall accept the next question."</p>

<p>As expected, my previous guess was right.<br>So you want to use me because of your pride.</p>

<p>Since the topic came up anyway, I decided to ask everything I was curious about.</p>

<p>"I'll keep your personality in mind. Next, do you happen to have any hobbies?"<br>"She takes after me and likes books. And she likes taking walks in the breeze."</p>

<p>"Reading... Walks...."</p>

<p>She seems to live a life completely different from mine, but still, walking together seemed like something I could somehow manage to arrange.<br>Pretending to be a reader requires a lot of preparation, but a walk is just an extension of exercise.</p>

<p>If I keep trying, will there come a day when I can walk alongside her?<br>For now, I set that as my only goal.</p>

<p>"If it becomes necessary later, would it be okay to use Lady Eden as an excuse to create some pretext? Like pretending it's an errand..."<br>"Hmm."</p>

<p>Eden pondered briefly, then nodded.<br>She may be a cranky old woman, but it seems she's willing to allow a certain degree of convenience.<br>It must mean she's truly taking this matter seriously.</p>

<p>The more so, the more grateful I naturally am.<br>From my perspective, I can't help but be grateful, but...</p>

<p>On the other hand, one 'trivial' question comes to mind.</p>

<p>"Um, then I have a personal question."<br>"What is it?"</p>

<p>"By any chance, does Lady Eden still use the same old method? The one you used when teaching Lady Hayes sex education."<br>"……."</p>

<p>"……?"</p>

<p>As I blurted out the question, Eden, strangely enough, just stared silently at the worn book instead of answering right away.</p>

<p>A clearly different attitude from before.</p>

<p>Only after a moment, with a *ahem, ahem!* did her belated reaction emerge, and then her voice was heard.</p>

<p>"...Why should I answer to the likes of you? Give me a reason I can accept from the bottom of my heart."<br>"Ah... if Lady Eden is in the middle of not changing the old ways, do I really have to change the disciple's ways? That's what I was wondering."</p>

<p>"……."<br>"Whatever the method, the mana will charge properly in the end... or is it that the 'crime' part has been weighing on your mind long-term...?"</p>

<p>It's fundamentally weird, isn't it?<br>Why the sudden rush to change a method that's been working fine all this time?</p>

<p>For that reason, after speculating with the Team Leader yesterday, we concluded that it's probably because it's a crime.<br>It's the part I expected to be 99% correct.</p>

<p>"I-I could just do it... no, ugh..."<br>"……?"</p>

<p>However, instead of answering right away, Eden just grumbled and glared at my face with dissatisfaction.</p>

<p>...That's strange.<br>The witch had been keeping her eyes glued to the book since a moment ago.<br>Was the question I just threw out that annoying?</p>

<p>While I was counting things up in my head.</p>

<p>- Brrrrrr</p>

<p>A small vibration sound started coming from the cardigan pocket Eden was wearing.</p>

<p>It's not particularly important, but it's an outfit that feels quite similar to what Hayes was wearing earlier.</p>

<p>Just like their similar appearances, the priest's taste seems remarkably alike.</p>

<p>"……."<br>"……."</p>

<p>Judging by the continuous vibration, it seems a call... is coming in.<br>Eden, having checked her smartphone screen briefly so I couldn't see, then let out a long sigh and started glaring at me.</p>

<p>At first, with an inexplicable viciousness.<br>A moment later, with an equally inexplicable hesitation.<br>Finally, with a sigh mixed with resignation.</p>

<p>I tried to interpret that strange shift in mood, but gave up early on and asked carefully.</p>

<p>"Should I step outside for a moment while you take the call?"<br>"...No need. If my disciple happens to see us, the cleanup will be even more of a hassle, won't it? You idiot."</p>

<p>"Ah... I guess so. Since you'd know I'm recharging my mana."<br>"Besides, doing it this way makes explaining way easier... ...Tch."</p>

<p>"……?"</p>

<p>Eden clicks her tongue bluntly and swipes her thumb across the screen.<br>She tucked her long hair behind her ear and hesitantly parted her lips.</p>

<p>"...He-hello?"<br>- ――, ―――. ――――?</p>

<p>...Hey there.<br>Who the hell is this?<br>It's loud, so contact me later.<br>Not something like that.</p>

<p>She'd said 'Hello-' in a shy voice.</p>

<p>Did I mishear that?</p>

<p>"M-me? I'm fine... I was just reading a book..."<br>- ―――. ――?</p>

<p>"What book? Uh, by an author named Marilynne Robinson..."</p>

<p>...Leaving aside the fact that what Eden had just been reading wasn't even in the vicinity of a novel, but an incomprehensible, baffling book.</p>

<p>What?<br>You could speak straight?</p>

<p>"Mhm. That book you mentioned is good too. As expected, you knew about it..."</p>

<p>Judging by the upturned corners of her mouth, she seems to have a favorable impression of the person on the other end of the call.<br>However, her face is excessively red to just dismiss it as simply being in a good mood.</p>

<p>It's not just a flush from feeling good; it's an apple-red hue that screams she'd kill anyone who spoke to her out of sheer embarrassment, looking like she wants to die of shame.</p>

<p>On top of that, the voice leaking from the smartphone has a fairly deep, pleasant baritone...</p>

<p>Mixing that with this unfitting, 100% estrogen-filled tone of voice right now, one could roughly surmise...</p>

<p>"But I'm kinda busy right now... Yeah. There's a student... Uh-huh."<br>"……."</p>

<p>"...Huh? You ask if I was reading a book...? ...Ah! Uh, I read that book a little while ago... Ahaha..."<br>"……."</p>

<p>"I'll call you back later..."<br>"……."</p>

<p>"...Me too, I... that is..."<br>"……."</p>

<p>"...L-love......"<br>"……."</p>

<p>Should I just bury myself in the corner of the study right now so I can pretend not to see the Archwitch's disgraceful behavior?<br>But if I show that I'm this conscious of it now, wouldn't I get seriously beaten for real after the call ends?<br>I guess it's best to just stay here acting like nothing's wrong.</p>

<p>While I was debating, the call ended.</p>

<p>"……."<br>"……."</p>

<p>I observed a brief moment of silence.<br>Fortunately, Eden, who knew the value of life, calmly spoke first.</p>

<p>"…The method I used for sex education. I've discarded it now."<br>"…I see."</p>

<p>"Goodness, the way young people talk these days is just so cringey…"<br>"……."</p>

<p>"……."<br>"……."</p>

<p>"…Just watching and reading the room is more infuriating, so hurry up and ask your question. It'll only take 5 minutes."</p>

<p>I asked immediately.<br>Exactly what kind of relationship do you have with the person who just called?</p>

<p>At that, Eden squeezed her eyes shut tight, then opened them and answered.</p>

<p>"...Boyfriend. Hayes doesn't know this yet either, so keep your mouth shut tight."<br>"You hid it from your disciple too?"</p>

<p>"Damn it... Then how was I supposed to say it...!! I've been drilling it into you with threat after threat after threat not to give your heart to a man uselessly like your seniors, but to just take their semen...!"<br>"C-calm down. Sorry for asking, Lady Eden."</p>

<p>"You wicked bitches, I taught you so many times that the smartest thing is to just use men and throw them away... Haaa..."<br>"……."</p>

<p>"...Now I have nothing to say either."</p>

<p>I wondered how old her boyfriend was.<br>That tactless question spun around in my mouth, but I grabbed hold of my sanity and swallowed it down.<br>If I let a joke like that slip, I might end up helping with her clinical experiments right away.</p>

<p>Instead, I decided to ask something like this.</p>

<p>"Then, to summarize the request Lady Eden mentioned once more..."<br>"……."</p>

<p>"I hope that, at the very least, her young disciple can enjoy the happiness Lady Eden went hundreds of years without knowing... before it's too late."<br>"……."</p>

<p>"But to do that, you'd have to completely deny the old teachings yourself. And you'd even have to explain the reason, so you found someone to say it for you because it was utterly embarrassing...."<br>"……."</p>

<p>...I added briefly to Lady Eden, who was just fiddling with the corner of the book without a rebuttal.</p>

<p>"Come to think of it, I suddenly remembered something. Eden, don't you have a boyfriend?"<br>"What. Why. What else are you so curious about? Do you really intend to use up all the promised five minutes?"</p>

<p>"As Eden kept pointing out, a manly man who is the complete opposite of me..."<br>"...Since it's true, just shut up..."</p>
