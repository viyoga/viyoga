<p>Today marks the third time in total.</p>

<p>The 'Operation: Pluck Flowers and Hide Them in Hayes' Pillowcase,' which was clumsy at first, has now become quite seasoned.</p>

<p>Eden makes up a suitable excuse to keep Hayes occupied,<br>and while resting in the guest room, I receive the signal and quietly hide the flowers,<br>then pretend nothing happened, rest, and wait for Hayes to fall asleep. That's it.</p>

<p>As expected, humans are creatures of adaptation.<br>It was around the time the second step, [Quietly hide the flowers], was wrapping up, along with the saying that gets quoted whenever this situation arises.</p>

<p>Just as I slipped out of Hayes' room, the sound of small footsteps was heard from the far end of the hallway.</p>

<p>- Thud, thud</p>

<p>If it were Eden's footsteps, there'd be no problem, but unfortunately, that's the sound of Hayes walking in soft slippers.<br>Eden couldn't be bothered with that kind of indoor footwear and had zero interest in it, but Hayes, I've noticed, occasionally wears them.</p>

<p>The fortunate thing is that this isn't a straight corridor.<br>There are corners scattered in between, so we can't see each other... well, technically.</p>

<p>To put it coldly, that just means we avoided getting caught right this second.<br>Hayes's room is at the very end of the corridor, so hiding in another room in a hurry would 100% get me caught by the sound of the door opening.</p>

<p>"Tch..."</p>

<p>I wonder if it's because this is the end of the corridor.<br>Right nearby, there was a large square window perfect for viewing the outside scenery, so I decided to make use of this immediately.</p>

<p>It's better than being treated like an idiot just staring at the scenery out of nowhere, rather than sneaking into the apprentice witch's bedroom and getting interrogated, right?</p>

<p>...But when I actually looked out the window, it faced Bukaksan, so with the late hour overlapping, only the mountain ridge, even blacker than the sky, stood alone in the darkness.</p>

<p>"...What is it? Nam Do-won, why are you here?"</p>

<p>While I was researching a slightly more natural posture, Hayes's characteristically blunt voice flew this way, accompanied by footsteps that had grown quite close.</p>

<p>At the same time, I turned my head to see Hayes's appearance....<br>Somewhat embarrassingly, she was wearing cute pajamas that didn't match her usual image at all.<br>Especially when you think of Hayes riding her motorcycle vroom-vroom in a leather jacket, you wonder if those tastes can really come from the same person at the same time.</p>

<p>They say she lived secluded in the mountains for a long time, so maybe her childhood tastes and habits just remained as they were?</p>

<p>I was curious, but I didn't bother asking.</p>

<p>"I was just staying in my room and got stiff, so I wandered around the mansion wherever my feet took me... and the view here happened to be pretty."<br>"The view...?"</p>

<p>Fortunately, the excuse that I 'walked around because I was stiff' seemed to work well enough.<br>But more than that, as expected, Hayes reacted to the words 'the view is pretty,' tilting her head to look out the window beside my shoulder.<br>In the process, the ponytail hidden behind her back swayed gently beside Hayes's figure.</p>

<p>"Is that pretty?"<br>"W-well, at my house, I never get to see mountains out the window like this."</p>

<p>"Hmmm..."<br>"And since you've lived in a mountain valley for a long time, Lady Hayes, maybe you've become a bit desensitized to things related to mountains?"</p>

<p>"Before it gets dull, that's just pitch black. I honestly don't see what's pretty about it."<br>"Th-... the night sky is hazy with city lights, but the mountain is dyed even darker, giving it a bit of an ink wash painting... vibe..."</p>

<p>"Ink wash painting...?"</p>

<p>Ah. Wait.<br>Is this too much bullshit?</p>

<p>While I was regretting blurting out just anything to smooth things over, Hayes clicked her tongue softly.</p>

<p>"Anyway, we don't need to watch that here, do we?"<br>"...Right. The mansion has plenty of windows."</p>

<p>"You know well. Unfortunately, this is in front of my bedroom, so let's admire it from another window. I get distracted when there are other people near my bedroom."</p>

<p>And with that, Hayes issued an order to leave.<br>She even stepped aside to clear the path, as if telling him to scram immediately, and waved her hand dismissively in the direction she had just come from.</p>

<p>It felt a bit haphazard, but it was a relief that he'd managed to hide the fact that he'd snuck into the bedroom.</p>

<p>"...Ah, Lady Hayes."<br>"...What is it?"</p>

<p>Since he'd already succeeded.<br>He decided to push his luck a little.<br>Just one more thing before he went.</p>

<p>"About that informal speech you mentioned earlier."<br>"……."</p>

<p>"That's also an easy and efficient way to provoke the male side, just like we 'tested' last time..."<br>"Nam Do-won."</p>

<p>While deliberately bringing up that story to test Hayes, Hayes replied without any change in expression.</p>

<p>"I told you clearly that I thought that was terrible."<br>"Ah..., right. You did."</p>

<p>"I have no intention of changing my mind. Let's go."</p>

<p>*****</p>

<p>"...This is difficult......"</p>

<p>Sightseeing my ass.</p>

<p>After drinking a glass of cold water and returning to the guest room, I lay down on the plush bed and mulled over the situation from a moment ago.</p>

<p>First, I still think Orca's argument has merit.<br>If it had just ended with her showing a weird reaction immediately after I spoke to her casually, that would be one thing, but before that, Hayes was already having a ton of dirty talk with Orca, wasn't she?</p>

<p>Orca, being a woman herself, understands a woman's heart better.<br>...It's just that it'd be more convenient for me if Hayes is into that kind of thing.<br>I do hope Orca's guess is right, though.</p>

<p>The problem is that the reaction I just saw was even colder than the last time in front of the convenience store.</p>

<p>Unlike witches with expressive faces like Neria or Orca, Hayes was a blunt witch, even towards Dorothy...</p>

<p>She had almost never answered as coldly as she did just now.</p>

<p>If she reacts that stiffly, there's a possibility she really hated the informal speech.<br>Compared to a witch, my hamster-like small brain capacity gives me a headache.</p>

<p>...Should I have asked Eden if there's a poison that forces one to speak only the truth?<br>Anyway, that's what I do in dreams.</p>

<p>The belated regret was only momentary.<br>Loosening my stiff shoulders, I sat up and checked my smartphone clock with a heavy sigh.</p>

<p>11:30 PM. Going by Hayes's upright daily schedule, it was well past the time she would've left for dreamland.</p>

<p>"...It's a shame, but it can't be helped."</p>

<p>Just like the casual speech that had been on my mind this time around, if there were a 'bait' that perfectly suited Hayes's taste, it would naturally be more convenient and better, but...</p>

<p>Just because it doesn't exist doesn't mean the operation is completely ruined.<br>After all, the work is the same as always.</p>

<p>Since this was my third infiltration today, I brought the flower and sat leaning against the wall with my eyes closed, just like last time.<br>It was an action I had been doing consistently since the first attempt, trying to get as close to Hayes's bedroom as possible.</p>

<p>Now, if I just enter a state similar to a lucid dream, it will be over.</p>

<p>"……?"</p>

<p>But today,<br>Why isn't it working?</p>

<p>"What?"</p>

<p>Just in case, I waited about a minute longer, but nothing in particular changed.<br>Just a young man sitting with his eyes closed, holding a flower.</p>

<p>Having given up on the first attempt, I looked at the flower in my hand in a bewildered state.</p>

<p>A flower that didn't wither even without water, as if it had been slathered in preservatives.<br>Right now, it's a flower with its petals torn off, leaving just a little under half remaining in my hand.</p>

<p>The way to use this flower is simple.<br>You just tear an identical flower haphazardly, put some on Hayes's pillow, and hold the rest in your hand.<br>There's already a track record of succeeding twice like this.</p>

<p>But the usage method was wrong starting today?</p>

<p>"There's no way that's it."</p>

<p>I can't say for certain, but the probability of that being the cause isn't high.<br>Then the next thing to check would be 'what I did differently from last time,' I guess.</p>

<p>But even thinking hard about it, there was absolutely nothing like that.</p>

<p>Location?<br>It's that same spot where I always sit with my back against the wall.</p>

<p>Time?<br>I heard when Hayes falls asleep from Eden, and I've already succeeded twice.</p>

<p>Did I pick the wrong flower?<br>But Mari said it was fine to just pluck it roughly?</p>

<p>Then what, the clothes I'm wearing?<br>If that's the cause, then how did those two times work before?</p>

<p>"Hmm..."</p>

<p>Still, just in case, should I try moving to a different spot?<br>Getting up from my seat, this time I lay down on the bed to get comfortable and closed my eyes.<br>Of course, I held the flower tightly between both hands and placed it on my solar plexus.</p>

<p>About 3 minutes in this princess-like pose.</p>

<p>...I almost fell asleep waiting this time, too.</p>

<p>"What kind of bug is this...?"</p>

<p>Muttering the line Neria always says whenever she makes a mistake during a game, I got up and headed for the mansion's bathroom.</p>

<p>You said you almost fell asleep earlier?<br>Given the hour, I couldn't help but get a little sleepy myself.</p>

<p>So I scrubbed my eyes with cold water and was on my way back to the guest room.</p>

<p>I wondered what the problem could be today of all days that things weren't working out...</p>

<p>"……."</p>

<p>Hypothesis one.<br>What if Hayes discovered the mysterious flower inside the pillowcase and pulled it out?</p>

<p>It wouldn't just end there; the mansion would be turned upside down to identify it.</p>

<p>A report would likely go to Eden immediately, and she'd brush it off with, 'I-it's a flower good for sound sleep~', 'I got it from a friend I know~'.</p>

<p>If that kind of situation arises, we've already agreed with Eden on how to resolve it.</p>

<p>But judging by the quiet, this doesn't seem to be the case.</p>

<p>Next, hypothesis two.</p>

<p>"……."</p>

<p>What if it's simply because Hayes hasn't fallen asleep yet?</p>

<p>"...This one's actually pretty plausible?"</p>

<p>Honestly, no other cause came to mind besides this.<br>Besides, this one fits the front and back the best.</p>

<p>Other than this, there's only hypothesis three: the flowers have actually passed their expiration date!</p>

<p>So, before returning to the guest room.</p>

<p>Just a quick recon.</p>

<p>"……."</p>

<p>Silencing my footsteps, slowly.</p>

<p>...opening the door is absolutely out of the question, after all—just as I was worrying about things like that, I arrived near Hayes's bedroom.</p>

<p>Fortunately,<br>the need to have such worries completely disappeared.</p>

<p>- ...Ugh, ...Urgh...</p>

<p>"……."</p>

<p>The unmistakable moans,<br>kept leaking out from beyond the door.</p>
