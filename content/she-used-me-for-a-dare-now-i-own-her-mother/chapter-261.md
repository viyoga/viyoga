<p>Edward walked toward the east wing, his footsteps measured against the marble floor.</p>

<p>He closed his eyes. Let the sounds wash over him. Let the thrill peak and settle.</p>

<p>He raised his hand.</p>

<p>A heartbeat.</p>

<p>The door opened.</p>

<p>Richard stood there, a sheet wrapped hastily around his waist, chest still heaving, skin flushed with exertion. His face was composed... impressively so... but his eyes betrayed a flicker of something beneath the mask.</p>

<p>Annoyance. Suspicion. The faintest edge of guilt.</p>

<p>"Yes?" Richard’s voice was cool, controlled. "What seems to be the matter, Mr. Steele?"</p>

<p>Edward smiled warmly.</p>

<p>"Forgive the interruption, Mr. Blackwood." His tone carried nothing but polite apology. "But I’m afraid something urgent has come up."</p>

<p>He paused, letting the words settle.</p>

<p>"Something that cannot wait."</p>

<p>Richard’s jaw tightened. For a moment, irritation flickered openly across his features.</p>

<p>"I’m coming," he said curtly.</p>

<p>The door closed.</p>

<p>Inside, Richard exhaled sharply, running a hand through his disheveled hair.</p>

<p>"What happened?"</p>

<p>Natalia’s voice came from the bed... languid, curious, tinged with frustration. She lay tangled in the sheets, skin still flushed, watching him with half-lidded eyes.</p>

<p>"It’s Jonathan." Richard reached for his shirt, pulling it on with sharp, efficient movements. "It must be something serious. He wouldn’t interrupt otherwise."</p>

<p>Natalia’s lips curved into a pout.</p>

<p>"Now? Of all moments?"</p>

<p>"I have to go."</p>

<p>Natalia rose from the bed, the sheet falling away as she crossed to him. She didn’t bother covering herself.</p>

<p>Her hands found his chest, smoothing the fabric of his shirt.</p>

<p>"We’ll have other moments," she murmured, eyes lifting to meet his. "Many of them."</p>

<p>She rose on her toes and kissed him... slow, lingering, a promise wrapped in warmth.</p>

<p>Richard allowed it. Even returned it, briefly.</p>

<p>Then he pulled back, a flicker of thought crossing his features.</p>

<p>"Do you think Edward knows?" His voice dropped lower. "I mean... he must have heard. We weren’t exactly... quiet."</p>

<p>Natalia laughed softly, her fingers playing with the collar of his shirt.</p>

<p>"You were too excited to notice," she whispered. "But don’t worry. That old man heard nothing he could recognize."</p>

<p>She smiled.</p>

<p>"I made sure of it."</p>

<p>Her eyes glinted with mischief.</p>

<p>"And what if he did hear?"</p>

<p>Richard’s jaw tightened, uncertainty flickering.</p>

<p>Natalia traced a finger down his chest.</p>

<p>"What can he do, Richard?" Her voice dropped to a whisper. "You’re the future head of House Blackwood. That old man needs you far more than you need him."</p>

<p>Richard’s posture shifted. Straightened. The doubt fading, replaced by something harder.</p>

<p>"You’re right."</p>

<p>Natalia smiled... the smile of a woman who knew exactly which strings to pull.</p>

<p>"I’m always right."</p>

<p>Richard exhaled. Nodded once.</p>

<p>"I should go."</p>

<p>Natalia’s fingers traced his jaw one last time.</p>

<p>"Don’t keep me waiting too long," she said, a smile curving her lips. "Or I’ll have to punish you next time."</p>

<p>Richard huffed a quiet laugh. Nodded once. And turned toward the door.</p>

<p>He didn’t look back.</p>

<p>But Natalia’s eyes followed him... watched the door close, watched the silence settle.</p>

<p>Then her lips curved into a slow, knowing smile.</p>

<p>Mischievous. Triumphant.</p>

<p>She returned to the bed, stretching like a satisfied cat, and stared at the ceiling.</p>

<p>***</p>

<p>Richard entered the study with measured strides, his composure fully restored. Only the faint flush at his collar betrayed anything at all.</p>

<p>Jonathan stood by the window. Edward sat in his chair, tea untouched, watching with polite curiosity.</p>

<p>"What is it?" Richard’s voice was clipped. Businesslike.</p>

<p>Jonathan crossed the room and leaned close, voice dropping to a whisper.</p>

<p>A name. A detail. Brief. Clipped.</p>

<p>Richard’s expression didn’t change.</p>

<p>But his body went still.</p>

<p>"Who?" The word came out quiet. Dangerous. "How?"</p>

<p>"I don’t know yet, sir." Jonathan’s tone was grim. "But we need to investigate immediately. This is serious."</p>

<p>A pause. Jonathan glanced toward Edward, then back to Richard.</p>

<p>"We can also use this. Position the Blackwells as allies in resolving the situation. Turn the mess into leverage."</p>

<p>Richard absorbed the words. Calculated. Then nodded slowly.</p>

<p>He turned toward Edward, his mask sliding back into place... smooth, grateful, apologetic.</p>

<p>"Mr. Steele." He inclined his head respectfully. "I’m afraid an urgent matter requires my immediate attention. We’ll have to continue our discussion another time."</p>

<p>Edward rose, spreading his hands graciously.</p>

<p>"Of course, Mr. Blackwood. Duty calls. I understand completely."</p>

<p>Richard’s gaze drifted... just for a moment... toward the corridor leading to the east wing.</p>

<p>Toward the room where Natalia waited.</p>

<p>Then he turned and walked toward the entrance, Jonathan falling into step beside him.</p>

<p>Edward accompanied them to the gates, ever the gracious host.</p>

<p>The limousine waited, engine purring softly.</p>

<p>Richard paused at the door, offering a final nod.</p>

<p>"Thank you for your hospitality, Mr. Steele. As always."</p>

<p>"The pleasure is entirely mine, Mr. Blackwood." Edward’s smile was warm. Fatherly. "Do take care of yourself. And please... don’t be a stranger."</p>

<p>The door closed. The limousine pulled away, gliding through the gates and disappearing down the long drive.</p>

<p>Edward watched until it vanished from sight.</p>

<p>Then his smile changed.</p>

<p>Warmer faded. Fatherly dissolved.</p>

<p>What remained was something sharper.</p>

<p>Darker.</p>

<p>Hunger.</p>

<p>His breath quickened. His pulse stirred with an ache he’d been suppressing since he stood outside that door, listening to every moan, every cry, every sound of another man claiming what was his.</p>

<p>He turned and walked back into the mansion.</p>

<p>His footsteps quickened as he crossed through the halls. The servants had vanished... trained well enough to know when to disappear. The corridor to the east wing stretched empty before him.</p>

<p>He didn’t slow down.</p>

<p>He didn’t knock this time.</p>

<p>The door swung open.</p>

<p>Natalia lay tangled in the sheets, exactly where Richard had left her. Flushed. Disheveled. The bedsheet pulled loosely across her body, hiding nothing of consequence.</p>

<p>She looked up at him with a slow, knowing smile.</p>

<p>"That was faster than expected."</p>

<p>Edward’s eyes moved over her... the marks on her neck, the swollen lips, the evidence of another man’s hands written across her skin.</p>

<p>His breath quickened.</p>

<p>He crossed the room in three strides, shedding his jacket, his shirt, his restraint.</p>

<p>Natalia laughed... low, throaty, welcoming.</p>

<p>"Eager today, aren’t we?"</p>

<p>He reached the bed. Reached for her.</p>
