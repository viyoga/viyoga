<p>Her lips pressed against his in a sudden, reckless surge of heat.</p>

<p>Driven by days of suppressed longing, she didn’t merely brush against him; she leaned into the contact with eager desperation, parting her lips to drink in his scent and taste.</p>

<p>For several breathless seconds, every shred of her professional discipline vanished beneath the sheer hunger she had tried so hard to deny, her fingers digging deeper into the expensive fabric of his jacket.</p>

<p>Then, like ice water, reality came crashing down.</p>

<p>The reminder of Karl, her contract with the Mercers, and the armed ambush waiting just floors above pierced straight through the intoxicating fog.</p>

<p>A sharp gasp tore from her throat as she abruptly broke the kiss, pulling her mouth back against the cool glass of the mirrored wall. Her chest heaved heavily against the strained silk of her blouse, her pupils dilated with sudden shock and horror at her own complete lack of restraint.</p>

<p>Alex didn’t move away. He remained standing over her, looking down with a slow, wicked amusement that made her face burn with intense shame.</p>

<p>Lifting one hand from the glass wall, Alex brushed the pad of his thumb across the swell of her parted lower lip, slowly smearing a trace of her crimson lipstick.</p>

<p>"So you really came all the way up here just to get ruined by me all over again, Mrs. Shaw?" Alex asked in a low drawl, his dark eyes gleaming.</p>

<p>Naomi could only stare at him, her throat parched and her breath shuddering under his touch. Her face was flushed bright red, and every rational thought in her head seemed to scatter the longer his thumb traced the contour of her mouth.</p>

<p>A quiet chuckle rumbled in Alex’s chest.</p>

<p>"Well," he murmured, his gaze darkening with raw intent, "who am I to keep such a gorgeous woman waiting?"</p>

<p>Before she could form a single word of defense, Alex surged forward and captured her lips with crushing authority.</p>

<p>A muffled gasp caught in Naomi’s throat. Her hands came up against his chest, her palms pressing firmly against his tailored shirt in an instinctive, frantic attempt to push him away.</p>

<p>Every lingering rational thought in her head screamed at her to stop, warning her that giving in now would completely destroy her remaining professional distance.</p>

<p>Yet the moment his mouth claimed hers with unyielding heat, that fragile barrier crumbled.</p>

<p>The stiffness in her arms dissolved within seconds, and her flat palms curled tightly into the expensive fabric of his lapels. Instead of shoving him back, she ended up pulling him closer. A helpless whimper escaped her throat as she parted her lips, answering his demanding rhythm with an even fiercer, reckless eagerness that abandoned all caution.</p>

<p>Alex slid his hands down to her waist, his grip unyielding as he lifted her effortlessly off her heels, raising her up against the mirrored wall to match his height. Naomi wrapped her arms tightly around his neck, meeting his mouth with a frantic, breathless hunger.</p>

<p>The fear, the guilt of her betrayal, and the cold reality of the ambush awaiting upstairs all burned away beneath the suffocating heat of his presence.</p>

<p>DING.</p>

<p>The crisp chime of the elevator echoed sharply through the cabin, announcing their arrival at the forty-eighth floor.</p>

<p>The sound cut through the haze like ice water, forcing their breathless mouths apart.</p>

<p>Naomi stared up at him, her gaze dazed and hungry, her pulse racing wildly against her throat. Her lips remained slightly parted, swollen and flushed, as she struggled to draw air back into her lungs.</p>

<p>"Alex..." she whispered, her voice trembling and faint.</p>

<p>Her eyes flicked toward the parting elevator doors, where cold reality waited, before snapping right back to him.</p>

<p>Panic and desire tangled violently inside her chest. She couldn’t bring herself to step back; her heels still dangled an inch above the floor, and her trembling hands remained clutched tightly around his suit collar, refusing to let him go.</p>

<p>Alex merely smiled, entirely unhurried, as if the world outside the cabin didn’t exist.</p>

<p>With effortless ease, he lowered her back down until her slender heels touched the floor. His hands lingered on the curve of her waist, steadying her trembling frame as she found her footing.</p>

<p>Leaning down until his lips brushed the shell of her ear, his voice dropped to a low, velvet murmur that carried an unmistakable promise.</p>

<p>"Patience, Mrs. Shaw," he whispered, a wicked spark of amusement dancing in his eyes. "Let us go inspect my new office first. And once I finish clearing the floor... I might just let you inaugurate my new desk."</p>

<p>Alex smoothed the front of his jacket with casual elegance, completely unfazed by the darkness beyond the threshold.</p>

<p>"Come along, Mrs. Shaw," he said smoothly, turning to step forward. "Let’s go take a look."</p>

<p>The sight of the open corridor snapped Naomi out of her sensual daze like a slap to the face.</p>

<p>To her, that doorway was not an office entrance; it was a firing line. The sudden image of suppressed rifles tearing through his body flashed violently behind her eyes.</p>

<p>Terrified by the thought of him taking a single step past the safety of the cabin, she lunged forward without thinking, her trembling hands latching onto his wrist with desperate force.</p>

<p>"No, please... don’t go out there," she stammered, her voice shaking so badly she could barely string the words together.</p>

<p>Alex paused, glancing down at her pale fingers digging into his cuff before looking back up at her. His expression remained entirely placid, tinged with a mild, deliberate confusion.</p>

<p>"What’s wrong?" Alex asked, his tone light and conversational. "You were eager enough to bring me up here a minute ago. It’s just an empty floor, Naomi. Don’t tell me you’re afraid of a little darkness?"</p>

<p>"You don’t understand," she pleaded, her heart hammering against her ribs so violently that it made her chest ache. Her knuckles turned stark white as she tried to pull him back. She was on the very brink of confessing everything — the Mercers, Karl’s orders, the armed squad — anything to keep him from stepping into the crossfire. "Wait... we shouldn’t be here. Please, just press the button and go back down. You can’t go out there!"</p>

<p>Instead of listening, Alex gently twisted his wrist, slipping effortlessly from her grasp.</p>

<p>"Nonsense," he murmured with an easy chuckle, turning away and crossing the threshold into the dark lounge. "A landlord doesn’t run away from his own property."</p>

<p>"No! Alex, stop!"</p>

<p>Panic completely overtook her survival instincts. Forgetting her mission, her cover, and even the danger to her own life, Naomi rushed out of the elevator right behind him.</p>

<p>"Don’t! There’s—there are—"</p>

<p>Her words choked in her throat.</p>

<p>She skidded to a halt on the dark marble floor, her wide eyes darting frantically across the massive open-plan executive reception.</p>

<p>There were no gunshots.</p>

<p>No black-clad mercenaries stepped out from the architectural pillars. There were no crimson laser sights converging on Alex’s chest, nor was there any sound of safeties clicking off in the shadows.</p>

<p>The entire floor was silent, vast, and completely deserted. Bright morning sunlight streamed through the panoramic floor-to-ceiling windows, spilling across the polished marble floor and illuminating the sleek leather couches and minimalist glass displays.</p>

<p>Alex came to a stop in the center of the foyer, hands resting casually in his trouser pockets.</p>

<p>He turned around slowly to face her, an amused, indulgent smile curving his lips.</p>

<p>"See?" Alex said, tilting his head as his dark eyes locked onto her trembling figure. "There is nothing here at all. What on earth were you so afraid of, Mrs. Shaw?"</p>

<p>Naomi stood frozen, her chest heaving as the bewildering sight sank into her frantic mind.</p>

<p>Confusion tore through her thoughts. Where are Karl’s men? Did they miss the signal? Did Karl call it off?</p>

<p>Before her rational mind could piece together an explanation, an overwhelming wave of dizzying relief washed over her. He wasn’t dead. There was no blood, no slaughter, and no execution.</p>

<p>Her shoulders sagged, and she let out a long, trembling breath she felt like she had been holding for an eternity, a faint, fragile smile of pure relief touching her lips.</p>

<p>BANG!</p>

<p>A deafening gunshot cracked through the dead silence, the violent blast shattering the quiet like a thunderclap.</p>

<p>Naomi screamed, her hands flying to her ears as the sound echoed off the reinforced glass.</p>

<p>A dull, heavy thud struck the polished marble right beside her feet.</p>

<p>Trembling violently, Naomi pulled her hands from her ears and forced her gaze down. The breath violently tore from her lungs in a strangled gasp.</p>

<p>It was Alex.</p>

<p>Or rather, his limp, motionless body sprawled across the cold stone, unmoving. No teasing smirk. No sovereign arrogance. Just dead, terrifying stillness.</p>

<p>"No... no, no!" Naomi shrieked, her knees buckling as she collapsed to the floor beside him.</p>

<p>Her trembling hands frantically grabbed his jacket, shaking him in blinding terror. "Alex, wake up! Please, no... this can’t be happening!"</p>

<p>Frantic, she snapped her head up, searching the shadows for the shooter, for Karl, for anyone at all.</p>

<p>?There was no one.</p>

<p>?Only the hollow, suffocating silence of the dark, empty floor... leaving her completely alone with his body.</p>
