<p>Vivienne turned toward the mirror and stopped.</p>

<p>Helena was right.</p>

<p>She was a mess.</p>

<p>Her hair hung loose, strands clinging to her flushed neck. Her blouse sat crooked, wrinkled where his hands had gripped. And her lips... swollen, bruised deep red... screamed exactly what had happened in that hallway.</p>

<p>She reached up, smoothing her hair back with sharp movements, straightening her collar, erasing the evidence.</p>

<p>’Did Helena suspect something?’ The thought flickered through her mind, accompanied by a small flutter of anxiety.</p>

<p>Vivienne paused, studying her reflection.</p>

<p>Then she exhaled slowly, her shoulders relaxing.</p>

<p>’Let her know.’</p>

<p>She wasn’t keeping it a secret anyway. Helena would find out eventually. Besides, it wasn’t as if she’d done anything wrong. She’d simply... tested the merchandise before the official presentation.</p>

<p>Vivienne’s lips curved into a small, private smile.</p>

<p>But as the adrenaline faded, a different sensation made itself known.</p>

<p>A cold, sticky heaviness between her thighs.</p>

<p>She had almost forgotten. In the rush to escape him, in the panic of the swap, she had ignored the physical reality of how hard she had responded to him.</p>

<p>She was soaked.</p>

<p>It wasn’t just a little dampness. It was a relentless, viscid reminder clinging to her skin, the aftermath of his finger pushing inside her and her body flooding to welcome him.</p>

<p>She bit her lip, a fresh wave of heat climbing her neck. She couldn’t go down to dinner like this. She was dripping.</p>

<p>Her eyes darted nervously toward the heavy mahogany door.</p>

<p>It was closed.</p>

<p>’I hope she hasn’t noticed this too.’</p>

<p>Quickly, needing to assess the damage, she hitched up her tight pencil skirt, bunching the fabric at her waist.</p>

<p>The reflection in the mirror was crude. Erotic.</p>

<p>The black lace tops of her stockings cut starkly against her pale thighs. And there, right at the precipice of the lace, the skin glistened... slick and undeniable.</p>

<p>Vivienne snatched a tissue from the vanity.</p>

<p>Her breath hitched as she reached down, dabbing at the sensitive skin of her inner thigh, trying to wipe away the mess he’d made of her.</p>

<p>She closed her eyes for a second, the friction of the tissue sparking a ghost of the pleasure she’d felt just minutes ago.</p>

<p>"Helena."</p>

<p>The voice came from the hallway, sharp and impatient, followed by the turning of the door handle.</p>

<p>Vivienne’s eyes snapped open.</p>

<p>Panic, cold and electric, spiked through her. She couldn’t be found like this... hand between her thighs, skirt hiked up to her waist.</p>

<p></p>

<p>In a blur of motion, she crumpled the tissue into her fist and shoved her skirt down, the fabric swooshing back into place just as the door swung open.</p>

<p>She spun around, her heart hammering against her ribs, her hand hiding the evidence behind her back.</p>

<p>Helena stood in the doorway. Arms crossed. Chin lifted.</p>

<p>Her eyes swept over Vivienne... taking in the flushed face, the slightly breathless stance, the way Vivienne was pressing her back against the vanity as if guarding a secret.</p>

<p>"Don’t waste time primping," Helena continued, her tone carrying the exact dismissive edge Vivienne herself used when subordinates moved too slowly. "Our guest has been waiting long enough. Let’s not keep him hungry anymore."</p>

<p>Vivienne let out a breath she didn’t realize she’d been holding. The tension in her shoulders unspooled, a wash of pure, dizzying relief crashing over her.</p>

<p>She didn’t see.</p>

<p>The secret... the wetness, the shame, the crude reality of what she had been doing... was safe. Helena just thought she was vanity-checking her hair.</p>

<p>But as the adrenaline faded, it left something else in its wake.</p>

<p>Something sharp. Uncomfortable. Twisting in her chest like a burr she couldn’t dislodge.</p>

<p>Irritation.</p>

<p>Helena wasn’t suggesting they hurry. She was ordering.</p>

<p>And the tone was pure Vivienne Vanderbilt at her most imperious. The voice she used to end arguments. To dismiss incompetence. To remind people exactly who held the power in any given room.</p>

<p>Hearing it directed at her felt... wrong.</p>

<p>Vivienne wasn’t accustomed to being ordered around. Not by board members. Not by competitors. Not by anyone.</p>

<p>Certainly not by her own cousin.</p>

<p>’This bitch is getting too into character,’ Vivienne thought, her jaw tightening.</p>

<p>But even as irritation flared hot in her chest, another part of her... cooler, more strategic... approved.</p>

<p>That’s good.</p>

<p>If Helena could own the role this completely, Alex would believe every second of it.</p>

<p>Which meant Vivienne could sit back, play the nervous assistant, and watch the game unfold exactly as she’d planned.</p>

<p>’Let her have her moment,’ Vivienne decided, forcing the irritation down. ’Let her play CEO for one night.’</p>

<p>’I’ll see how you behave when he is actually in front of you,’ she promised silently, the thought dripping with menace.</p>

<p>Vivienne straightened, dropping the crumpled tissue discreetly into the bin behind her, and walked toward the door.</p>

<p>"Of course," she said, her voice carefully neutral. "I’m ready."</p>

<p>Helena’s eyes swept over her one more time... assessing, judging, cataloging every detail... and then she nodded, satisfied.</p>

<p>"Good. Follow me."</p>

<p>And without waiting for a response, Helena turned and walked out of the room, the emerald silk of her gown whispering against the floor with every confident step.</p>

<p>Helena’s breath caught.</p>

<p>Just slightly.</p>
