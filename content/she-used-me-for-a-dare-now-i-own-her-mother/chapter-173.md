<p>Alex stepped out of the shower, water cascading down a body that no longer felt entirely mortal. His skin gleamed faintly under the light, his muscles moving with effortless precision.</p>

<p>Even his hair... thicker, darker, alive with sheen... looked stronger than it had half an hour ago.</p>

<p>He’d already been healthier than any mortal alive... but now, his very biology had ascended beyond that definition.</p>

<p>He grabbed a towel... the fabric felt rougher against his enhanced senses, every thread distinct... and wrapped it around his waist with hands that moved with supernatural precision.</p>

<p>The bathroom mirror was fogged with steam, but a single swipe of his palm cleared it instantly.</p>

<p>And then he froze.</p>

<p>The man staring back at him... was him. And wasn’t.</p>

<p>His face was the same. Same features, same bone structure, same eyes.</p>

<p>But everything else...</p>

<p>He’d thought he was in good shape before. Peak human, the system had said. 20 in Body.</p>

<p>He’d been proud of that...</p>

<p>But now?</p>

<p>Now he realized how shallow his understanding had been.</p>

<p>His physique hadn’t bulked up, but refined... every line sharper, every contour precise. Muscles looked sculpted rather than grown, balanced in perfect symmetry, strength without excess. His chest and shoulders carried divine proportion, power and grace fused into something almost unreal.</p>

<p>Even his skin looked different... alive, radiant with a vitality that made the light seem to bend toward him.</p>

<p>And his eyes...</p>

<p>They held an intensity he’d never seen before. A depth that seemed to see beyond surfaces, beyond the mundane physical world into something deeper.</p>

<p>Alex turned slightly, watching muscles move beneath skin with mechanical efficiency.</p>

<p>No wasted motion. No excess. Just pure, controlled power contained in a form that looked almost too perfect to be real.</p>

<p>"Holy shit," he breathed. "I thought I looked good before. I thought I was at my peak. But this..."</p>

<p>He flexed experimentally, watching muscle fibers respond with accuracy that seemed impossible. His previous "peak human" body now felt like a rough draft compared to this masterpiece.</p>

<p>"I was so naive," he murmured, unable to look away from his reflection. "I had no idea what ’peak’ even meant."</p>

<p>"Magnificent, isn’t it?"</p>

<p>Lilith’s voice drifted from the doorway, rich with satisfaction and something that might have been pride.</p>

<p>Alex’s eyes flicked to her reflection in the mirror. She was leaning against the doorframe, her eyes tracking over his transformed physique with obvious appreciation.</p>

<p>"That’s what transcendence looks like, darling," she continued, her smile turning knowing. "What you were before? That was the absolute ceiling of human potential. Impressive by mortal standards."</p>

<p>She pushed off the doorframe, moving closer with that liquid grace that made his enhanced perception struggle to track.</p>

<p>"But this?" She gestured to his reflection.</p>

<p>"This is what happens when you break through that ceiling. When your body becomes more than flesh and bone."</p>

<p>Her eyes met his in the mirror, gleaming with satisfaction.</p>

<p>"Tell me... how does it feel? The power?"</p>

<p>Alex turned to face her, towel still wrapped around his waist, and felt the raw strength humming through every fiber of his being.</p>

<p>"I could take ten of my previous self," he said, and it wasn’t arrogance... it was simple truth.</p>

<p>"Easily. Without breaking a sweat."</p>

<p>His hands flexed, remembering how they’d cracked tile with gentle pressure.</p>

<p>"My old body was strong. But this?" He looked down at hands that could crush or caress with equal precision. "This is something else entirely. It’s like I was fighting with training weights on, and someone just removed them all at once."</p>

<p>Lilith’s smile widened, absolutely delighted.</p>

<p>"Ten of your previous self," she repeated, clearly savoring the statement. "Oh, darling. You’re still underestimating."</p>

<p>She circled him slowly, her gaze assessing, appreciating.</p>

<p>"Your Body stat went from 20 to 30. That’s not a 50% increase... it’s exponential. The foundation doubled, and everything building on that foundation grew accordingly."</p>

<p>She stopped directly in front of him, close enough that he could smell cinnamon and night-blooming flowers again.</p>

<p>"You could handle fifteen. Maybe twenty. And that’s just raw physical capability." Her eyes gleamed. "We haven’t even tested your enhanced Mind or that delicious supernatural Charm of yours."</p>

<p>Alex felt something shift in his chest... not quite pride, but a dawning realization of what he’d become.</p>

<p>Then another thought struck him, and his eyes widened slightly.</p>

<p>"Wait," he said, looking at Lilith with sudden comprehension. "Is this... is this the same realm Victor and his team were in? When I first met them?"</p>

<p>Lilith’s smile turned into something more knowing. Almost a smirk.</p>

<p>"Yes," she said, her voice carrying a note of satisfaction. "They were Enhanced Stage beings. Early Enhanced, most of them. Victor himself..." She paused deliberately.</p>

<p>"Victor was at the peak of Enhanced Stage."</p>

<p>The words hit Alex like a physical blow.</p>

<p>His mind flashed back to that first encounter... the night he’d crossed paths with Damien and Dimitri.</p>

<p>The way they’d moved... effortlessly, with a kind of supernatural grace that didn’t belong to mortals. The precision in every gesture, the way the air itself seemed to react to their presence.</p>

<p>Back then, he’d thought it was arrogance... two men too confident for their own good, playing at superiority.</p>

<p>But now he understood.</p>

<p>It hadn’t been arrogance. It had been power.</p>

<p>The kind that came from standing a stage above everyone else. From existing on a plane he couldn’t have even comprehended at the time.</p>

<p>And he... he’d actually stood against them. Spoken to them like equals.</p>

<p>A faint, incredulous laugh escaped him. "I really was clueless back then," he muttered.</p>

<p>He ran a hand through his damp hair, the memory replaying with terrifying clarity.</p>

<p>"I actually thought I could take them on. I was ready to fight them if necessary, thinking I had a chance because of my enhanced abilities and skills."</p>

<p>He laughed, but there was no humor in it... just the sharp edge of someone who’d narrowly avoided disaster. "If you hadn’t warned me in time, if I’d actually tried..."</p>

<p>He couldn’t finish the sentence. Didn’t need to.</p>

<p>The consequences would have been catastrophic. They would have crushed him without effort. His peak human stats would have been a joke.</p>

<p>And the more he thought about it, the more the truth sank in... he’d been lucky.</p>

<p>Lucky they’d come to him seeking help that night instead of confrontation.</p>

<p>Because if Damien or Dimitri had even a trace of ill intent... if they’d seen him as an obstacle rather than an ally... he wouldn’t have stood a chance.</p>

<p>No defense. No warning. Just silence... and the end.</p>

<p>"But if Victor is at the peak of Enhanced Stage," Alex said slowly, his strategic mind already working through the implications.</p>

<p>"Victor was at peak Enhanced Stage," Lilith interrupted, emphasizing the past tense with deliberate clarity.</p>

<p>Was.</p>

<p>The single word landed like a thunderclap.</p>

<p>Alex’s eyes widened as understanding crashed through him. "You mean... he broke through? Advanced to the next stage?"</p>

<p>"Indeed," Lilith confirmed, her wine-dark eyes gleaming. "Your little help... which was anything but little for them... pushed him forward. Saving his team, altering their fate... it became the spark he needed. He broke past the limits of the Enhanced Realm and stepped into the Apex Realm."</p>

<p>Apex Realm.</p>

<p>One full Realm above where Alex stood now.</p>

<p>But instead of feeling intimidated, Alex felt something else entirely.</p>

<p>Opportunity.</p>

<p>His mind flashed through their brief interactions... the way Victor had carried himself, the respect he’d shown despite Alex’s lower power level, the genuine gratitude in his voice.</p>

<p>"That’s..." He paused, mind racing through possibilities. "That’s actually perfect."</p>

<p>Lilith’s eyebrow arched, clearly intrigued by his reaction.</p>

<p>"I saved his life," Alex continued, his strategic thinking crystallizing. "His entire team’s lives. They were facing certain death, and I pulled them back from the brink."</p>

<p>He met Lilith’s eyes directly.</p>

<p>"He owes me," Alex said quietly. "Not in the petty sense of a favor owed, but in the profound sense of a life preserved. And men like Victor? They don’t take that lightly."</p>

<p>Lilith’s smile turned absolutely predatory with satisfaction.</p>

<p>"Oh, darling," she purred. "Look at you. Already thinking like a true Master."</p>

<p>She moved closer, her presence almost overwhelming.</p>

<p>"You’re absolutely right. Victor is a man bound by honor and gratitude. The kind of man who, when shown genuine kindness at his darkest moment, becomes unshakably loyal."</p>

<p>Her eyes gleamed.</p>

<p>"And you saved not just him, but his entire team. His people. His responsibility." She leaned in, voice dropping to something more intimate. "He doesn’t just owe you a debt, Alex. He owes you everything."</p>

<p>"Convincing him to serve me..." Alex said slowly, working through the strategy. "It wouldn’t even require manipulation or coercion. Just... acknowledgment of what already exists between us."</p>

<p>"Precisely," Lilith confirmed. "You showed him grace when he was powerless. That kind of selfless intervention?" She smiled.</p>

<p>"That’s worth more than any contract or binding oath. It creates loyalty that runs deeper than magic or compulsion ever could."</p>

<p>Alex looked at his reflection again... at the Enhanced Stage being staring back at him, at the power thrumming through enhanced muscles, at the strategic mind already mapping out alliances and possibilities.</p>
