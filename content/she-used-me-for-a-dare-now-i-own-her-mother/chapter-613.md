<p>Karl stared up at the blood-slicked blade, his chest heaving with shallow, ragged breaths.</p>

<p>Even with the corpses of his six operatives cooling only feet away, a stubborn, desperate glimmer of denial flickered in his bloodshot eyes.</p>

<p>He was her husband. They had spent years navigating the corporate underworld together, bound by contracts, shared secrets, and legal ties. In his mind, Naomi was simply trying to scare him, exerting leverage to punish his pride.</p>

<p>She was an attorney and a calculating operative, not a cold-blooded butcher who would casually slaughter the man she had taken vows with.</p>

<p>Yet as he searched her face, that fragile certainty began to crumble.</p>

<p>Naomi didn’t look angry, conflicted, or hurt. Her golden eyes were utterly flat, carrying the calm, detached indifference of someone deciding whether to crush an annoying beetle under her shoe.</p>

<p>A wave of pure, icy terror washed through Karl’s veins, finally drowning out his arrogance.</p>

<p>"N-Naomi... wait," Karl choked out, blood and spittle bubbling past his broken teeth as he scrambled for words. "You can’t do this... We’re married! How can you blame me for what I said? Look at what you did! You cheated on me right before my eyes! What husband wouldn’t lose his mind seeing his own wife giving herself up so shamelessly in another man’s lap?!"</p>

<p>He strained his neck upward, his voice rising in frantic, pleading desperation.</p>

<p>"I was just angry! I swear, Naomi, it was just the heat of the moment! I didn’t mean any of it... I would never have actually killed you! You know me, I was just emotional! Don’t let that monster turn you against me!"</p>

<p>Naomi didn’t bother offering a verbal reply.</p>

<p>Instead, she drew her right foot back and drove the hard, pointed tip of her black pump straight into the side of his battered face.</p>

<p>CRACK.</p>

<p>The violent kick snapped Karl’s head sideways, slamming his cheek against the marble tiles. A muffled howl tore from his throat as fresh blood splattered across the stone, his remaining teeth rattling in his gums from the brutal impact.</p>

<p>"Still trying to play the righteous husband, Karl?" Naomi murmured, stepping forward until the sole of her shoe rested firmly against his ear, pinning his head to the cold floor. "Have you already forgotten whose idea this was from the very start? Who was the one that begged me to put on my most flattering dress and go to that lounge? Who was the one that told me to use my body to wrap Alex Hale around my finger for Mercer money?"</p>

<p>She looked down at him with quiet, unadulterated disgust.</p>

<p>"You pushed your own wife into the clutches of another man just so you could secure your little promotion," she continued, her voice cold, smooth, and razor-sharp. "And now that the tables have turned and you’re the one on the floor, you suddenly remember your vows? You accuse me of cheating? How many cheap women have you dragged into your bed over the years behind my back? Did I ever plot to put a bullet in your head for it?"</p>

<p>Karl groaned beneath her heel, his hands feebly scraping against the stone as her words stripped away his pathetic excuses.</p>

<p>"And so what if I gave in to him?" Naomi said softly, a dark, lingering warmth flickering in her eyes for a fleeting second before turning to ice.</p>

<p>"Have you looked at that man, Karl? Do you truly believe anyone in this city, no matter how disciplined or strong-willed, could ever resist someone of his caliber? The truth is, I wanted to run straight to his penthouse the very next morning. The only reason I stayed away... the only reason I resisted that temptation... was because a foolish, sentimental part of me still respected the fact that you were my husband."</p>

<p>Her voice dropped an octave, dripping with pure venom.</p>

<p>"And how did you reward my loyalty? By threatening to unleash your dogs on me and plotting my murder."</p>

<p>A low, mocking chuckle escaped her lips as she casually nudged the side of his battered face with the pointed toe of her pump, forcing his head back against the stone with cold disdain.</p>

<p>"Did you really take me for a fool, Karl? You thought I wouldn’t notice the offshore accounts you quietly set up, or the neat little escape route you mapped out behind my back? You honestly believed you could use my name to cash in with the Mercers, toss my corpse away like trash, and fly off to live like a king on your own."</p>

<p>Karl’s face drained of what little color it had left.</p>

<p>Knowing his deepest, darkest plan had been exposed tore away the very last shred of his composure. He shook his head frantically, his bloody chin smearing across the floor as he begged with everything he had left.</p>

<p>"No... no, Naomi, please!" he sobbed out, terror completely breaking his voice. "I was muddled! I wasn’t thinking straight, I swear to God! Forgive me... please, for the sake of our years together! For our marriage’s sake, Naomi!"</p>

<p>Naomi looked down at him without a single flicker of warmth or hesitation. Her golden eyes remained completely flat, the blackened blade in her hand angling downward toward his exposed throat.</p>

<p>Seeing that cold, unmovable resolve, Karl realized with sickening certainty that his tears and begging meant nothing to her.</p>

<p>Panic instantly morphed into wild, venomous desperation. His bloodshot eyes widened, and he bared his bloody teeth in a frantic bid to play his absolute final card.</p>

<p>"Wait! You stupid bitch, don’t do it!" Karl rasped frantically, spitting blood across the marble. "You think you’re safe just because you crawled into Hale’s bed?! You have no idea what the Mercer family is hiding! The Old Patriarch... the previous leader who went into secluded cultivation years ago... he succeeded! He broke through into the Ascended Realm!"</p>

<p>Karl stared up at her, his voice trembling with manic conviction as he tried to claw back any shred of control.</p>

<p>"Do you hear me?! A genuine Ascended Realm powerhouse! Nobody in this city knows yet, but he is completely unmatched! A walking disaster! Neither you nor that arrogant bastard can ever escape the Mercers once they come for you! Kill me, and you’re signing your own death warrant! But keep me alive... let me live, Naomi, and I can still plead your case to the family! I can tell them you were coerced! I’m the only one who can save you from being torn apart!"</p>

<p>He stared up at her, chest heaving, desperately waiting for the sheer weight of an Ascended Realm terror to force her to drop the knife.</p>

<p>Naomi simply tilted her head, a soft, indifferent shrug lifting her shoulders.</p>

<p>"Is that your big secret?" she murmured, her voice smooth and entirely unbothered. "Marriage, and an old man in seclusion?"</p>

<p>Stepping forward, she planted the slender stiletto heel of her shoe directly against his collarbone, pinning his upper body flat against the floor. Crouching low with lethal poise, she brought the blackened steel blade descending toward his neck.</p>

<p>Karl squeezed his eyes shut, bracing for the searing bite of cold metal. He waited for the agony, his breath hitching in his chest, his entire body rigid against the stone.</p>

<p>Yet the fatal strike never came.</p>

<p>Instead, the sharp edge came to an abrupt, dead halt right against the side of his neck, resting lightly against the frantic, visible thrumming of his carotid artery. The steel merely grazed his skin, chilling his flesh without drawing more than a tiny bead of crimson.</p>

<p>A heavy, breathless silence hung between them.</p>

<p>Karl slowly cracked his swollen eyelids open.</p>

<p>Right above the gleaming steel, Naomi’s lips had parted into a soft, radiant smile. Her golden eyes curved with what looked like genuine gentleness, washing away the arctic chill that had filled them just seconds before.</p>

<p>A sudden, desperate spark flared inside Karl’s battered chest.</p>

<p>She stopped.</p>

<p>His heart hammered with manic, frantic hope. He had broken through her ice. The threat had worked, or perhaps, after all these years, the memories of their marriage and the vows they took had finally made her hesitate. Underneath all that ruthless ambition, she was still his wife.</p>

<p>She was still soft for him.</p>

<p>"N-Naomi...?" Karl choked out, a trembling, fragile gasp of relief escaping his lips. "You... you still care..."</p>

<p>"Hush, Karl," she whispered, her voice low, velvety, and tender, like a lover sharing an intimate secret. "Don’t ruin it. I just realized I was being terribly ungrateful."</p>

<p>Karl stared up at her, clinging desperately to the warmth in her expression, completely oblivious to the hollow void behind her golden eyes.</p>

<p>"Before you go, I really ought to say thank you," Naomi murmured, her smile widening into something breathtakingly cruel. "Truly, Karl. If your pathetic greed hadn’t pushed me straight into his arms... if you hadn’t sent me to that lounge to seduce him... I never would have known what a real man feels like. Because of you, I was dismantled, claimed, and conquered by a true god. For giving me to him... I’ll always be genuinely grateful."</p>

<p>The realization hit Karl like a physical blow.</p>

<p>The fragile warmth in her smile twisted into a vicious mockery that made his blood run completely cold. His jaw dropped open in pure, suffocating horror, his throat working desperately to let out a scream.</p>

<p>He never got the chance.</p>

<p>Without a single tremor in her hand, Naomi drove the blackened blade deep and dragged it across his exposed throat in a clean, effortless arc.</p>

<p>Karl’s scream died as a wet, violent choke.</p>

<p>A thick crimson geyser erupted from the severed artery, painting hot across the marble floor, spraying across Naomi’s ivory blouse, and leaving heavy red streaks across her charcoal skirt.</p>

<p>His body convulsed in violent, frantic spasms. His hands flew to his ruined neck, fingers clawing desperately against the gaping wound in a futile attempt to hold his life inside.</p>

<p>Naomi didn’t flinch or pull away. She remained crouched over him, watching with cold, serene eyes as the thrashing slowly weakened, the frantic light in his dying pupils dimming into dull, glassy nothingness.</p>
