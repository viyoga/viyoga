<p>Catherine lay motionless, afternoon sun warm on her exposed skin, feeling Alexander Hale’s hands settle on her neck.</p>

<p>Rough. Masculine. Warm.</p>

<p>Her breath caught.</p>

<p>’Control yourself.’</p>

<p>She’d survived Senate interrogations. Boardroom ambushes. Political assassination attempts disguised as dinner parties.</p>

<p>’One man’s touch shouldn’t... ’</p>

<p>His fingers traced the curve where her shoulder met her neck, pressure firm and deliberate, and sensation detonated through Catherine’s nervous system like electricity finding ground.</p>

<p>’Oh God.’</p>

<p>Her hands clenched against the bench, knuckles white, every muscle in her body suddenly taut with effort not to react, not to show how his touch was unraveling her with methodical precision.</p>

<p>’This isn’t normal.’</p>

<p>’Touch didn’t feel like this.’</p>

<p>Not from massage therapists who’d trained for years. Not from lovers who’d spent months learning her body. Not from anyone.</p>

<p>But Alexander Hale’s hands... calloused, confident, moving with clinical precision that somehow felt more intimate than seduction... were doing something Catherine couldn’t identify and couldn’t resist.</p>

<p>’Stop.’</p>

<p>’Tell him to stop.’</p>

<p>’You’re Catherine Blackwood. You don’t lose control. You don’t... ’</p>

<p>Suddenly his thumb pressed against a point just below her ear... firm, deliberate, precise... and the world whited out.</p>

<p>Not pain.</p>

<p>Not even pleasure in any recognizable sense.</p>

<p>Just sensation so intense it bypassed thought entirely, short-circuited every defense Catherine had spent decades building, and tore a sound from her throat before consciousness could intervene.</p>

<p>"Ah—hn..."</p>

<p>The moan escaped before she could stop it... sharp, involuntary, shocked by its own intensity.</p>

<p>Raw. Needy. Completely, catastrophically unguarded.</p>

<p>’No.’</p>

<p>Catherine’s eyes snapped open, horror flooding through her chest as reality crashed back.</p>

<p>’I just... ’</p>

<p>’I just moaned.’</p>

<p>’Like some desperate... ’</p>

<p>Her gaze found his, and what she saw made her breath stop entirely.</p>

<p>Alexander Hale wasn’t surprised.</p>

<p>Wasn’t shocked.</p>

<p>Wasn’t even pretending professional obliviousness.</p>

<p>He was smiling.</p>

<p>Not smug. Not crude.</p>

<p>Just... knowing.</p>

<p>Like he’d been waiting for exactly that sound. Like he’d orchestrated every touch specifically to extract it. Like Catherine Blackwood losing control had been inevitable from the moment she’d spread her legs and invited him between them.</p>

<p>Shame flooded her face... heat that had nothing to do with afternoon sun and everything to do with the reality of what had just happened.</p>

<p>’I should stop this.’</p>

<p>’Should order him away.’</p>

<p>’Should prove I’m still in control by ending this before it goes further.’</p>

<p>The words formed in Catherine’s mind with crystal clarity.</p>

<p>But they didn’t reach her lips.</p>

<p>Didn’t transform into sound.</p>

<p>Didn’t do anything except hover uselessly in her thoughts while Alex’s hands remained on her body and Catherine lay frozen beneath his touch, unable to speak, unable to move, unable to do anything except stare at his reflection and wonder when she’d lost the ability to say no to someone she’d met twenty minutes ago.</p>

<p>Because beneath the shame, beneath the horror, beneath every rational thought screaming that this had to stop...</p>

<p>Some traitorous part of Catherine wanted his hands to move again.</p>

<p>Wanted to feel that sensation tear through her one more time.</p>

<p>Wanted to surrender completely and see where it led.</p>

<p>’How did it come to this?’</p>

<p>The question flickered through Catherine’s mind with uncomfortable precision.</p>

<p>The Catherine Blackwood — untouchable, unconquerable — lay vulnerable before a man who was supposed to break.</p>

<p>And worse — far worse — reacting to his touch in ways she couldn’t control, couldn’t explain, couldn’t rationalize away as simple physical response.</p>

<p>His presence somehow more overwhelming than it should be.</p>

<p>His perfect, maddening composure making her want things she had no business wanting.</p>

<p>Making her feel things she’d spent decades learning not to feel.</p>

<p>Making her question everything she thought she knew about control and power and what happened when Catherine Blackwood decided to test someone.</p>

<p>And she had absolutely no idea how it had happened.</p>

<p>No clear moment where she’d lost control.</p>

<p>No obvious mistake she could identify and correct.</p>

<p>Just a series of escalations that had seemed reasonable at the time, each justified by logic that now felt flimsy and transparent, leading inevitably to this moment.</p>

<p>This impossible, dangerous, completely inexplicable moment.</p>

<p>Everything had started with a single argument with Victoria — an argument that left Catherine determined to prove a point. To prove that he was no different.</p>

<p>That Alexander Hale, regardless of his wit or charm or whatever spell he’d cast on Victoria, would still fall the same way every man did when faced with her, Catherine Blackwood.</p>

<p>The initial plan had been simple.</p>

<p>Elegant, even.</p>

<p>Meet him. Test him. Demonstrate that beneath whatever clever responses he’d prepared, beneath whatever strategy he’d employed to seduce Victoria, he was ultimately just another man who would react to Catherine exactly like every other man did.</p>

<p>With desire that overwhelmed judgment.</p>

<p>With obvious interest that proved he wasn’t special, wasn’t different, wasn’t worth the risk Victoria was taking.</p>

<p>But nothing had happened the way she’d planned.</p>

<p>Not once had she seen his composure slip.</p>

<p>The woman who could make the most powerful men in the Enhanced community nervous with a single glance. Who controlled every interaction through sheer force of presence and the uncomfortable awareness that she understood exactly what effect she had.</p>

<p>Politicians stumbled over their words around her.</p>

<p>Business leaders made poor decisions trying to impress her.</p>

<p>Even other Enhanced — people with abilities that should have made them immune to such base reactions — found their focus wavering when Catherine Blackwood walked into a room.</p>

<p>But Alex didn’t.</p>

<p>And that fact — that infuriating, fascinating fact — made something shift in Catherine’s chest.</p>

<p>Something dangerous.</p>

<p>Something she should have recognized earlier but had dismissed as simple curiosity, simple desire to protect Victoria, simple need to vet the man who’d somehow captured her best friend’s attention.</p>

<p>I need to push further, she’d told herself when he’d first resisted her provocations.</p>

<p>Need to test him fully.</p>

<p>Need to find where he breaks.</p>

<p>Because everyone broke eventually.</p>

<p>Didn’t they?</p>

<p>But with each escalation — each boundary she’d pushed, each test she’d designed — Alex had simply... adapted.</p>

<p>Turned her interrogation into conversation.</p>

<p>Made her blush with a single frank assessment.</p>

<p>And now, standing between her deliberately spread legs with his hands on her skin, he remained so perfectly controlled it made Catherine want to scream.</p>

<p>Or do something far more dangerous than screaming.</p>

<p>***</p>

<p>Alex stared at her flushed expression... eyes wide with shock, cheeks burning with shame, lips parted in breathless silence that spoke louder than any words could.</p>

<p>But what he saw went far deeper than surface emotion.</p>

<p>Overlaid across Catherine’s body like a living map visible only to his Enhanced perception, Serpent’s Whisper painted her vulnerabilities in breathtaking detail.</p>

<p>Deep crimson zones pulsed along the curve of her neck where he’d just touched—primary sensitivity areas still resonating from contact, glowing brighter with her elevated pulse. The side of her throat. The hollow behind her ear. Places that would make her gasp if he touched them again.</p>

<p>Amber shimmer traced her collarbone, the inner curve of her wrists where they pressed against the bench, the small of her back visible where her bikini dipped low.</p>

<p>Soft gold highlights marked her temples, the tension points along her shoulders, the base of her spine.</p>

<p>And beneath it all, barely visible but unmistakably present — pale silver traces pulsing in rhythm with her racing heartbeat. Psychological vulnerability points. Emotional weak spots that his skill was already identifying with surgical precision.</p>

<p>Fear of losing control.</p>

<p>Need for validation from someone who matches her.</p>

<p>Desire to surrender to someone strong enough to take what she wouldn’t give freely.</p>

<p>Catherine Blackwood lay before him completely exposed... not just physically, but emotionally, psychologically, stripped bare by abilities she couldn’t see and didn’t understand.</p>

<p>Perfect.</p>

<p>His lips curved into something between amusement and satisfaction.</p>

<p>"How does it feel?" Alex asked quietly, voice carrying undertone that transformed the simple question into deliberate provocation.</p>

<p>Catherine’s breath caught, her gaze snapping to his with mixture of shame and something far more dangerous.</p>

<p>Desire.</p>

<p>Alex’s smile widened fractionally, fingers still resting on her skin with casual possession that suggested he had no intention of moving unless she explicitly asked.</p>

<p>"Should I stop?" he continued, the offer sounding less like genuine concern and more like a challenge.</p>

<p>Go ahead. Tell me to stop. Prove you still have control.</p>

<p>His eyes held hers with focused attention that made it clear he already knew her answer.</p>

<p>Catherine’s lips parted — the beginning of words that should have been easy, should have been automatic — but nothing emerged except shallow breath that betrayed exactly how thoroughly she’d lost this particular battle.</p>

<p>Alex waited, patient and knowing, watching her struggle with something that felt remarkably like satisfaction.</p>
