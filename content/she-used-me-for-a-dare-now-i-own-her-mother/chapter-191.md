<p>Victoria’s luxury sedan pulled smoothly through increasingly wealthy neighborhoods, morning light sharpening into late morning as they drove toward the exclusive district where Catherine’s estate waited.</p>

<p>Massive estates began appearing... separated by acres of grounds, surrounded by walls that spoke not of wealth but of power and the necessity to defend it.</p>

<p>Each property was a statement: We belong to a world you cannot enter uninvited.</p>

<p>Alex settled into the passenger seat, eyes on the printed report instead of the passing streets.</p>

<p>This was the security briefing for Victoria’s manor... delivered barely thirty minutes ago... no small feat, considering getting accurate security details on someone tied to House Blackwood bordered on impossible.</p>

<p>Alex paused and glanced into the side mirror.</p>

<p>A black SUV followed at a comfortable distance... silent, unremarkable to anyone else.</p>

<p>Viktor and his team.</p>

<p>Not hovering.</p>

<p>Just there.</p>

<p>A reminder that he wasn’t walking into Thornhaven alone.</p>

<p>He exhaled slowly, his gaze returning to the report again.</p>

<p>Six to eight Early Enhanced.</p>

<p>Four to five Mid Enhanced.</p>

<p>"Impressive," he murmured... not intimidated, just acknowledging reality.</p>

<p>Then his eyes narrowed slightly.</p>

<p>"But manageable."</p>

<p>Not overconfidence.</p>

<p>A calculated assessment.</p>

<p>Alex continued, the faintest hint of a smirk tugging at his mouth.</p>

<p>Three Peak Enhanced—Adrian Blackwell, Seraphine Vale, Lucan Graves.</p>

<p>Adrian Blackwell wasn’t just the youngest of the three... he was their leader.</p>

<p>A prodigy with the fastest recorded rise to Peak Enhanced in the last decade, rumored to be less than a year away from breaking into the next realm.</p>

<p>Born into the Blackwell lineage, a prestigious vassal family tied to House Blackwood, Adrian carried power the way other men carried entitlement.</p>

<p>Rude. Arrogant. The kind of rich heir who never hid the belief that he was the chosen one in any room.</p>

<p>And unlike the others, he wasn’t assigned to Catherine Blackwood’s security detail... he put himself there.</p>

<p>Under the guise of protection.</p>

<p>In reality?</p>

<p>Everyone knew he was pursuing her.</p>

<p>Romantically and strategically.</p>

<p>A future bet on power. A man who wanted the next head of House Blackwood not as someone to serve... but as someone to stand beside when the world eventually bowed.</p>

<p>Alex’s eyes paused on the paragraph a second longer than necessary.</p>

<p>"...interesting," he murmured, a slow smirk pulling at the corner of his mouth.</p>

<p>Not irritation. Not concern.</p>

<p>Amusement.</p>

<p>So that’s the competition.</p>

<p>A self-appointed guard dog with a crush and a superiority complex.</p>

<p>It’ll be fun watching your face when this falls apart, Alex thought, the smirk deepening.</p>

<p>Victoria glanced over from the driver’s seat, catching the expression.</p>

<p>"What?" she asked. "What did you just read?"</p>

<p>Alex didn’t hide it.</p>

<p>"Adrian Blackwell, You know him?"</p>

<p>Victoria’s brows lifted immediately.</p>

<p>"Oh him... how could I not?" Victoria scoffed... half laugh, half annoyance.</p>

<p>"The precious heir of the Blackwell family... the highest-ranked vassal under Blackwood, and he never lets anyone forget it."</p>

<p>Her tone sharpened.</p>

<p>"He’s been circling Catherine for years. Strategically planted himself on her security team just to get near her."</p>

<p>She rolled her eyes.</p>

<p>"Don’t be fooled. His intentions aren’t pure. He doesn’t want to protect her... he wants to attach himself to her before succession politics unfold."</p>

<p>A beat.</p>

<p>Then her lips curved, slow and wicked.</p>

<p>"But if he thinks Catherine is going to fall for entitlement wrapped in Peak Enhanced muscle..."</p>

<p>She glanced at Alex, eyes gleaming.</p>

<p>"...he’s about to learn what a real threat looks like."</p>

<p>Three Peaks... manageable, he thought.</p>

<p>If something went wrong, it wouldn’t be easy...</p>

<p>but it wasn’t suicide either.</p>

<p>Then his eyes caught a blank space...</p>

<p>not a sentence, but an absence.</p>

<p>No mention of anything above Peak.</p>

<p>No Apex.</p>

<p>No confirmation.</p>

<p>No denial.</p>

<p>Alex’s grip tightened slightly on the report.</p>

<p>Either Viktor’s team didn’t detect one...</p>

<p>or Thornhaven was hiding something powerful enough</p>

<p>that even Apex-level operatives couldn’t confirm it.</p>

<p>The relief in his chest cooled into focus.</p>

<p>If there’s an Apex inside that manor, he thought,</p>

<p>I’m not walking into a meeting... I’m walking into a throne room.</p>

<p>"Alex," Victoria said, breaking his thoughts, her voice calm but edged with anticipation,</p>

<p>"We’re two minutes out."</p>

<p>He lifted his gaze just as her car turned onto a private road lined with ancient oaks, their branches weaving together overhead like a natural archway:</p>

<p>Thornhaven Manor.</p>

<p>Alex’s Enhanced perception took in the scale, and even knowing the intelligence reports, even having studied the architectural diagrams and satellite imagery, the reality exceeded every expectation.</p>

<p>Even more beautiful than I imagined, Alex thought, genuine appreciation mixing with tactical assessment. And more intimidating.</p>

<p>This isn’t wealth on display... this is power that doesn’t need to announce itself because it’s self-evident in every stone, every sight line, every calculated detail.</p>

<p>Victoria’s car approached the main entrance, and immediately... as if they’d been monitoring the private road... two figures emerged from a security station integrated seamlessly into the landscaping.</p>

<p>Both wore dark suits that managed professional appearance without being obvious security uniforms. Both moved with coordination that suggested enhanced reflexes and situational awareness beyond normal human capability.</p>

<p>Alex’s Enhanced perception engaged automatically, analyzing movement patterns, the way they carried themselves, the subtle indicators of realm status that most people would never consciously notice.</p>

<p>Early Enhanced, Alex confirmed within three seconds of observation. Both of them. Exactly as the intelligence reports indicated.</p>

<p>The confirmation settled something in his chest... Viktor’s team had been accurate.</p>

<p>Which meant everything else in their briefing was equally reliable.</p>

<p>Both men straightened the moment the car stopped.</p>

<p>"Welcome back, Mrs. Blackwood,"</p>

<p>They saluted Victoria with crisp precision... respect reserved for someone far above their pay grade... then gave Alex a brief, assessing nod. Not dismissive. Not welcoming. Neutral acknowledgment of someone they had not yet classified.</p>

<p>One security officer gestured smoothly toward a parking area to the left of the main entrance, guiding Victoria’s vehicle with professional efficiency.</p>

<p>The other maintained position at the entrance checkpoint, his attention tracking their car with alertness that never quite crossed into obvious suspicion but missed nothing.</p>

<p>They’re good, Alex noted. Not just Enhanced realm... professionally trained with years of experience. They know exactly what they’re doing.</p>

<p>Victoria parked smoothly in the designated area, and as they exited the vehicle, a third figure approached from the main entrance.</p>

<p>This one was immediately different.</p>

<p>Late thirties, dressed in a formal suit—not standard security attire—moving with the kind of steady, unhurried confidence that came from authority, not muscle.</p>

<p>His posture didn’t say assigned here.</p>

<p>It said this place belongs to me.</p>

<p>Victoria’s elbow brushed Alex’s arm—subtle, but intentional.</p>

<p>"Adrian Blackwell," she murmured.</p>

<p>Alex looked at him again.</p>

<p>The System didn’t even need a full second.</p>

<p>Peak Enhanced Realm.</p>

<p>Much stronger than the others. And close... too close... to breaking through.</p>

<p>The kind of man who didn’t just guard a fortress.</p>

<p>He expected to inherit it.</p>

<p>And as he neared, his posture shifted into a slight bow... respectful but not subservient, precisely calibrated... directed specifically toward Victoria.</p>

<p>"Mrs. Blackwood," He said, his voice carrying cultured professionalism with undertone of authority. "Welcome to Thornhaven Manor. Miss Catherine is waiting for you."</p>

<p>Victoria nodded acknowledgment, as Adrian straightened from his bow.</p>

<p>Then his attention shifted... briefly, professionally... to Alex, and his gaze carried assessment that spoke of years evaluating potential threats.</p>

<p>He acknowledged Alex without interest, not even bothering to inquire who this unknown man was.</p>

<p>"Miss Catherine requests that you join her immediately," He continued, still addressing Victoria. His tone remained respectful, but there was clear instruction beneath the courtesy.</p>

<p>"She wishes to speak with you first."</p>

<p>He paused, and his next words were delivered with careful precision:</p>

<p>"She specifically requests your presence alone for the moment."</p>

<p>Victoria’s expression flickered... surprise quickly masked by professional composure, though Alex caught the micro-expression with his Enhanced perception.</p>

<p>She wasn’t expecting that, Alex noted. Which means this is Catherine’s decision, not something Victoria and Catherine discussed beforehand.</p>

<p>"Of course," Victoria said smoothly after barely perceptible hesitation. She glanced at Alex, and something apologetic entered her expression. "Would you mind waiting just a few minutes? I’ll come back for you shortly."</p>

<p>"No problem," Alex replied, keeping his voice casual despite the tactical recalculation his Enhanced mind was already performing at superhuman speed.</p>

<p>Catherine wants Victoria alone first. Why? To verify my credentials without me present? To get Victoria’s honest assessment where I can’t hear? To establish control by dictating meeting terms from the very start? To test how I handle being made to wait?</p>

<p>Probably all of the above. This is exactly how someone politically sophisticated operates... gather information first, control the environment completely, never let unknown elements dictate any terms whatsoever.</p>

<p>Victoria followed the security officer toward the main entrance, her posture confident despite the unexpected separation, and within moments they’d disappeared through the massive oak doors.</p>

<p>Leaving Alex standing alone in the parking area.</p>

<p>With the third person... Adrian... still positioned between him and the entrance with professional precision.</p>

<p>Not blocking access overtly. Not threatening in posture or demeanor. Just... present. Watchful. Positioned exactly where he could observe Alex while maintaining control of the approach to the entrance.</p>
