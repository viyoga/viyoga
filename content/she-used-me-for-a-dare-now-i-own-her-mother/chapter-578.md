<p>Morning light filled the master bedroom, casting a warm, gentle glow across the polished floorboards.</p>

<p>In front of the full-length mirror near the walk-in closet, Victoria turned to the side, smoothing the fabric of a soft beige dress over her hips. Her brow furrowed with mild uncertainty as she studied her reflection, tilting her head before turning back toward the sitting area.</p>

<p>"Alex," she called, her tone caught between excitement and quiet worry. "What do you think of this one? Is the color too dull, or does it look proper enough?"</p>

<p>Alex sat back on the leather sofa, already dressed in clean, casual clothes, watching her with a slow, amused smile. A cup of coffee rested on the small table beside him, steam rising quietly into the cool morning air.</p>

<p>"You look stunning, Victoria," he said, the warmth in his voice entirely genuine. "Just like you did in the last three dresses."</p>

<p>Victoria let out a soft, exasperated sigh, though a faint blush touched her cheeks as she smoothed her sleeves. "You’re not taking this seriously. I can’t just wear anything."</p>

<p>Seeing her this flustered was a rare sight, and Alex couldn’t help but enjoy every second of it.</p>

<p>After their serious conversation late into the night, they had ended up falling into each other again. One round had quickly turned into another on the dark silk sheets. Victoria had wanted to keep going, completely lost in the heat, but Alex had laughingly stopped her, pulling her close and telling her she needed to rest if she wanted to be able to walk properly in the morning.</p>

<p>When she asked why, and he casually told her he was taking her to Danny’s house to see his parents and Nina, her entire mood had shifted.</p>

<p>From that very moment, Victoria had been awake with quiet excitement.</p>

<p>It wasn’t that she was meeting them for the first time. She had already visited Nina at Memorial Hospital, sat by the bedside, and spoken with David and Linda to make sure the family had everything they needed. But back then, she had arrived as Madame Blackwood—the powerful, untouchable figure offering aid from behind an official title.</p>

<p>Today was entirely different.</p>

<p>Today, Alex was taking her through their front door, into their living room, to introduce her openly as his woman.</p>

<p>For someone who spent her life dealing with clan politics, cold negotiations, and people bowing out of fear, standing before the only family Alex truly cared about carried far more weight than any high-society banquet ever could. She wanted everything to be perfect.</p>

<p>"You don’t need to overthink it so much," Alex said, rising from the sofa and crossing the room toward her.</p>

<p>He stopped behind her, meeting her gaze in the mirror. Resting his hands lightly on her waist, he leaned down until his chin brushed the soft curve of her shoulder.</p>

<p>?"You already know how they are," Alex murmured, his thumb tracing a gentle line against her hip. "They aren’t the kind of people to judge you by your dress or your name. Linda has been talking about making you a proper home-cooked meal since the day you walked into that hospital room. They’re not going to care about any of that, Victoria. They’re just excited to have you in their home."</p>

<p>Victoria looked at their reflection, watching the calm, steady smile on his face. The knot of nervous tension in her chest began to ease, though she still reached up, placing her hand gently over his.</p>

<p>"I know," she murmured softly, leaning her head back against his chest. "I just... I want them to like me. Not because of my name or what the Blackwoods can do for them, but because I’m the one standing beside you."</p>

<p>Alex turned her around in his arms, his smile deepening as he looked into her dark, earnest eyes.</p>

<p>"They’re going to love you," he promised, leaning in to press a soft, unhurried kiss to her lips. "Now grab your coat. Nina is probably already waiting by the window."</p>

<p>Victoria lingered in the warmth of the kiss for a heartbeat longer before pulling back just enough to search his face.</p>

<p>The quiet, unshakable certainty in his eyes did what dozens of mirror checks couldn’t... it uncoiled the last knot of nervous tension resting in her chest. A faint, helpless smile broke through her composure, softening the aristocratic edge of her features completely.</p>

<p>"Fine," she breathed, giving in with a small, fond shake of her head. "I’ll trust your judgment."</p>

<p>She turned and reached for the light-colored coat draped over the armchair, slipping it over her shoulders with practiced, effortless grace. As she smoothed down the lapels, a subtle, playful glint returned to her dark eyes.</p>

<p>"Though if Linda takes one look at me and thinks your taste is questionable, I’m holding you entirely responsible."</p>

<p>Alex chuckled, offering her his arm with an easy grin. "Fair deal."</p>

<p>Victoria slipped her fingers around his arm, leaning into his side for a brief second as they turned toward the door. The lingering worry was gone, replaced by a steady, quiet warmth as she let him lead the way out.</p>

<p>Alex walked beside her with his usual easy rhythm, his fingers resting reassuringly over hers. He could sense the slight tension lingering beneath her calm facade, and it only made him look at her with greater fondness.</p>

<p>Taking her to Villa 6 this morning wasn’t just a brief walk across the estate grounds. It was a quiet declaration... bringing the woman who shared his bed and his ambition into the heart of the only family he recognized.</p>

<p>At the bottom of the staircase, the grand foyer was bathed in clean morning light.</p>

<p>Emma and Madison were already waiting near the front entrance, their postures neat and attentive. The panic from the previous evening had completely washed away, replaced by an earnest, respectful ease now that they understood Victoria’s place beside their master.</p>

<p>"Good morning, Master. Good morning, Madame Victoria," Emma said with a small, polite bow as they approached.</p>

<p>"The car has been brought around to the front driveway, Master," Madison added with a gentle smile. "The climate control is already running, and the tank is full."</p>

<p>Alex offered them both an appreciative nod.</p>

<p>"Thank you. You two did well."</p>

<p>?He paused near the archway, looking at them with a calm, steady gaze.</p>

<p>?"I’ll be out for the morning with Victoria," Alex said, his tone measured and reassuring. "Take care of the house, but make sure you take care of yourselves first. If anyone comes looking for me, simply tell them I’ve stepped out on personal matters. And remember... no matter who knocks on those gates, you don’t need to shrink back or live in fear. You work for me now. For Alexander Hale. No one gets to look down on my people."</p>

<p>The words settled over the foyer with quiet, immovable weight.</p>

<p>?For a moment, both Emma and Madison stood frozen, taken aback by the casual certainty in his voice. In their line of work, domestic staff were usually treated as invisible or disposable... the first to be blamed and the last to receive protection when influential figures clashed.</p>

<p>Hearing their master offer his own name as an absolute shield washed away the last traces of their anxiety.</p>

<p>?Emma’s shoulders squared, a bright, unmistakable pride showing in her eyes.</p>

<p>"Understood, Master."</p>

<p>?Beside her, Madison’s hands clasped neatly over her apron, a radiant, genuine smile breaking across her face as she dipped her head. "We will guard the villa, Master. Have a wonderful morning with Madame Victoria. You don’t have to worry about a thing."</p>

<p>"Good. Have a peaceful morning, both of you," Alex said.</p>

<p>With the estate matters settled, Alex pushed open the heavy front doors, stepping out into the fresh, brisk autumn air. The gravel driveway sparkled faintly under the morning sun, where his dark coupe sat idling with a low, smooth purr.</p>

<p>Alex guided Victoria down the shallow stone steps and walked around to the passenger side.</p>

<p>He pulled the door open for her with an effortless, unhurried gesture, offering her a reassuring look.</p>

<p>Victoria looked back at him, the last traces of her hesitation melting into a soft, genuine smile.</p>

<p>Gathering the hem of her coat, she slid comfortably into the leather passenger seat.</p>

<p>Alex closed the door with a solid thud, walked around the hood, and slipped behind the steering wheel.</p>

<p>The drive through the estate grounds was brief and quiet, the paved road winding past manicured lawns beneath the cool morning shade. Within barely three minutes, the car came to a smooth stop in the driveway outside Villa 6.</p>

<p>Alex cut the engine. Through the front windows, the soft glow of the living room lights was already visible, and even before getting out, the faint, comforting scent of fresh baking seemed to linger in the quiet air.</p>

<p>He stepped out and walked around to open the door for Victoria. As she placed her hand in his and stepped onto the stone walkway, she gave his fingers a brief, subtle squeeze.</p>

<p>"Ready?" Alex asked with a faint smile.</p>

<p>Victoria took a quiet, steadying breath, letting her natural composure settle back into place.</p>

<p>"Ready."</p>

<p>They walked up the stone steps side by side to the front entrance. Reaching the double doors, Alex lifted a hand and pressed the small brass bell.</p>

<p>The soft chime echoed inside, immediately followed by the sound of light, hurried footsteps rushing toward the door.</p>
