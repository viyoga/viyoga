<p>Alex slipped out of Victoria’s private entrance into the cool night air. Behind him lay twelve thousand square feet of marble and crystal. Ahead of him waited his beat-up Honda and a studio apartment that could fit inside Victoria’s closet.</p>

<p>The drive across the city took him from manicured lawns and iron gates to flickering streetlights and cracked sidewalks. Every mile reminded him why he was doing this.</p>

<p>His apartment building sat between a laundromat and a convenience store. The elevator was broken again, so he climbed three flights to his apartment, exactly what five hundred dollars a month bought you in this part of town. A Murphy bed, a wheezing mini-fridge, and a bathroom you had to step sideways to enter.</p>

<p>But it had reinforced walls that blocked external scanning. Essential for what he was about to do.</p>

<p>Alex locked the door and activated the white noise generator in his desk drawer. Only then did he let his mask slip away.</p>

<p>Time for a progress report.</p>

<p>He sat on his couch and closed his eyes, focusing on the neural pathway that connected him to the system. The apartment faded, replaced by a digital environment that existed only in his mind. Holographic displays materialized around him, showing data in soft blue light.</p>

<p>At the center, a progress bar hovered:</p>

<p>```</p>

<p>TARGET: Victoria Blackwood</p>

<p>Status: 89% Captured</p>

<p>Timeline: EXTENDED</p>

<p>```</p>

<p>Alex’s pulse quickened. Eighty-nine percent was higher than he’d hoped, but still not complete. And what did "timeline extended" mean?</p>

<p>"Lilith," he called out. "Explain, please!"</p>

<p>Lilith’s voice slithered into his mind, velvet-smooth and soaked in amusement.</p>

<p>"Well, well," she purred, her voice playful, amused, and just a little wicked.</p>

<p>"Eager, aren’t we? Can’t even enjoy Sunday morning after such a good night?"</p>

<p>Alex felt his cheeks warm. "I need to know where I stand. The deadline...."</p>

<p>"Oh, darling," Lilith laughed like breaking crystal. "You’ve been worried about that countdown, haven’t you? Lying in that woman’s arms, thinking about ticking clocks instead of enjoying your success?"</p>

<p>"Is she fully captured or not?"</p>

<p>"Direct. I like that." She waved her hand, and charts appeared around them. "You’ve done beautifully, Alex. The dependency is established, look at these neural pathway changes. The emotional hooks are set so deep she probably dreams about you."</p>

<p>Alex studied the data. Victoria’s brain scans showed dramatic changes in her reward centers and decision-making processes. The woman from a month ago was neurologically different from the one lying in silk sheets across town.</p>

<p>"But," Lilith continued, "one hundred percent capture? Not quite yet."</p>

<p>Alex’s stomach dropped. "What do you mean? She’s addicted, she’s planning our meetings, she’s rationalizing everything...."</p>

<p>"She’s infatuated," Lilith corrected. "Obsessed, even. But complete capture means she’ll sacrifice anything for you. Her company, her reputation, her family...everything. Right now, she still has boundaries."</p>

<p>"She’s protecting her daughter’s feelings, maintaining her public image, keeping James in the dark. A fully captured target would burn it all down if you asked."</p>

<p>"So I failed?"</p>

<p>"Oh, sweetheart, no." Lilith’s laugh was genuinely warm. "You’ve accomplished something remarkable. The good news? That seven-day deadline that’s been haunting your dreams? Gone."</p>

<p>Relief hit Alex like a physical force. "Really?"</p>

<p>"Really. You’ve established sufficient control that the emergency protocols are deactivated. No more ticking clock, no more termination threats. You can take your time with the final phase."</p>

<p>The weight he’d carried suddenly lifted. That countdown had driven every decision, haunted every quiet moment.</p>

<p>"But," Lilith’s voice sharpened, "you only get your reward when she hits one hundred percent. Complete devotion. The kind of love that overrides every other consideration."</p>

<p>"What does that look like exactly?"</p>

<p>"She chooses you over everything," Lilith said simply. "Her business empire, her social standing, her family, when push comes to shove, you come first. Always."</p>

<p>Alone again, Alex processed what he’d learned. The countdown was gone. That constant pressure had finally lifted.</p>

<p>For the first time in these 2-3 days, he could breathe freely. Sleep without nightmares. Plan properly instead of rushing every interaction.</p>

<p>But Victoria wasn’t some naive college student. She was brilliant and, despite her vulnerability, fundamentally strong. Getting her from eighty-nine to one hundred percent would require finesse.</p>

<p>She still had boundaries. Still protected parts of her life. Still believed she controlled their relationship.</p>

<p>Time to change that.</p>

<p>Alex opened his laptop and began planning his next moves. Victoria was addicted to him now, but addiction wasn’t surrender. He needed to find her breaking point, the moment she’d choose him over everything else.</p>

<p>Her company was her pride, built from nothing into a billion-dollar empire. Her reputation mattered. Her relationship with Sophia, dysfunctional as it was, still carried weight.</p>

<p>Those were his pressure points.</p>

<p>Alex smiled as plans formed. Victoria thought she was managing their relationship, keeping it contained. She had no idea her careful boundaries were about to be tested.</p>

<p>Soon, she’d face a choice that would reveal whether she truly belonged to him.</p>

<p>And Alex intended to make sure there was only one possible answer.</p>

<p>But first, he had other business to handle.</p>

<p>Tomorrow was Monday. Classes would resume, and Alex realized he wanted to face the people he’d wronged. Danny. Sarah. Mike. His real friends who’d tried to warn him about Sophia, who he’d screamed at and called jealous when they were just trying to protect him.</p>

<p>Alex reached for his phone to call Danny, then remembered....it was shattered. And he barely had money for a new one.</p>

<p>"Oh, Alex," Lilith’s voice purred from the digital space still lingering in his mind. "Check your bank account, darling. You’ve received a little gift. One hundred thousand dollars from an... anonymous benefactor."</p>

<p>Alex’s eyes widened. "Victoria?"</p>

<p>"One of her spare accounts, I’d imagine." Her laugh was musical with mischief. "Congratulations on gaining a sugar mommy, sweetheart."</p>

<p>```</p>

<p>System Note: Target dependency stable at 89%. Final phase protocols now available. Estimated timeline for complete capture: 2-4 weeks.</p>

<p>```</p>
