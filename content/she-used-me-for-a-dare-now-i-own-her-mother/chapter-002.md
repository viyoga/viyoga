<p>Alex’s eyes snapped open in the sterile hospital room. The mechanical voice hadn’t been a dream - it was still there, echoing inside his skull like an unwelcome guest.</p>

<p>"What the hell?" he whispered, sitting up despite the sharp pain in his ribs.</p>

<p>The room was empty. Just him, the beeping heart monitor, and the smell of disinfectant that made his stomach turn. But the voice came again, clearer this time.</p>

<p>[Ding! Trauma analysis complete. Subject shows optimal psychological damage for system integration.]</p>

<p>Alex spun around, looking for speakers, cameras, anything that could explain the voice. His movement sent lightning through his cracked ribs, but he didn’t care. "Who’s there? Show yourself!"</p>

<p>Instead of an answer, his vision filled with something impossible - a translucent blue interface that looked like it belonged in a science fiction movie. Text scrolled across it in sleek font:</p>

<p>```</p>

<p>[DOMINION PROTOCOL ACTIVATED]</p>

<p>Subject: Alexander Hale</p>

<p>Age: 22 years, 4 months, 12 days</p>

<p>Psychological Profile: Betrayed, Humiliated, Seeking Vengeance</p>

<p>Compatibility Rating: 94.7% - EXCEPTIONAL CANDIDATE</p>

<p>```</p>

<p>"This isn’t real," Alex muttered, squeezing his eyes shut. "Concussion. That’s all this is."</p>

<p>But when he opened them, the screen remained. Worse, a new voice joined the mechanical one - female, sultry, with an accent he couldn’t place.</p>

<p>"Oh, but it is real, darling. More real than anything you’ve experienced in your pitiful little life."</p>

<p>Alex’s hands trembled. "I’m losing my mind."</p>

<p>"You’re gaining it for the first time," the female voice purred. "Let me introduce myself properly. I am Lilith, architect of the Dominion Protocol. I specialize in transforming broken men into apex predators."</p>

<p>The interface shifted, showing what looked like a business card floating in his vision:</p>

<p>```</p>

<p>[LILITH - DOMINION SYSTEMS CONSULTANT]</p>

<p>"Turning Victims into Victors Since Time Immemorial"</p>

<p>Success Rate: 97.3%</p>

<p>Client Satisfaction: Guaranteed</p>

<p>```</p>

<p>"Every civilization has had men like you, Alex. Betrayed by those they trusted, destroyed by those they loved. Most die broken and forgotten. But a selected few..." The screen flashed. "A selected few become legends."</p>

<p>Alex felt something stirring in his chest - not hope, exactly, but something sharper. "What do you want from me?"</p>

<p>"Wrong question, sweetie. What do YOU want? Because I can smell your rage from here, and it’s absolutely delicious."</p>

<p>___</p>

<p>The interface expanded, filling Alex’s vision with detailed schematics that looked like a cross between a video game menu and a corporate presentation.</p>

<p>"Here’s how this works," Lilith explained as images flowed across the screen. "You will hunt. Specifically, you’ll hunt the women in the social circles that destroyed you. Mothers, sisters, wives, daughters - anyone connected to your tormentors."</p>

<p>Alex’s stomach clenched. "Hunt them? You mean kill them?"</p>

<p>Lilith’s laugh was like silk over steel. "Kill them? Oh, darling, you think so small. Death is mercy. I’m talking about complete psychological capture. You will make them fall so deeply in love with you that they’ll abandon everything - family, fortune, reputation - just to be near you."</p>

<p>The screen showed a flowchart with disturbing clarity:</p>

<p>```</p>

<p>[PHASE 1: TARGET IDENTIFICATION]</p>

<p>[PHASE 2: PSYCHOLOGICAL MAPPING]</p>

<p>[PHASE 3: SEDUCTION PROTOCOL]</p>

<p>[PHASE 4: EMOTIONAL DEPENDENCY]</p>

<p>[PHASE 5: TOTAL DOMINANCE]</p>

<p>```</p>

<p>"Each successful conquest rewards you exponentially," Lilith continued. "Money transfers directly to offshore accounts I control. Enhanced physical capabilities - strength, stamina, recovery speed. But most importantly, the ability to read people like open books."</p>

<p>Alex touched his swollen face. Even through the painkillers, everything hurt. "This is insane."</p>

<p>"Insane? Marcus Steele breaking your ribs for sport was insane. Sophia making you fall in love with a lie was insane. I’m offering you sanity - the kind that comes with power."</p>

<p>The interface showed a new screen:</p>

<p>```</p>

<p>[CURRENT ABILITIES:]</p>

<p>Seduction: 12/100 (Pathetic)</p>

<p>Manipulation: 8/100 (Non-existent)</p>

<p>Wealth: 3/100 (Poverty Level)</p>

<p>Physical Prowess: 15/100 (Weakling)</p>

<p>Social Status: 1/100 (Invisible)</p>

<p>[ENHANCED POTENTIAL]</p>

<p>All stats can reach 100+</p>

<p>Unlock supernatural charisma</p>

<p>Perfect psychological reading ability</p>

<p>Unlimited financial resources</p>

<p>```</p>

<p>"The rules are simple," Lilith said. "Complete missions, gain power. Fail missions, lose everything - including me. And Alex? Some missions will test your moral boundaries. The question is: do you care more about being a good person, or about making them pay?"</p>

<p>Alex thought of Sophia’s cruel smile as he lay bleeding. "What kind of boundaries?"</p>

<p>"You’ll seduce married women away from their husbands. You’ll turn daughters against mothers. You’ll create scandals that destroy political careers and business empires. You’ll become the chaos agent in their perfect lives."</p>

<p>"And if I refuse?"</p>

<p>The screen flashed red:</p>

<p>```</p>

<p>[SYSTEM REJECTION CONSEQUENCES:]</p>

<p>- Permanent return to baseline abilities</p>

<p>- Memory wipe of this encounter</p>

<p>- Guaranteed life of mediocrity</p>

<p>- 99.7% probability of suicide within 2 years</p>

<p>```</p>

<p>"Your choice, sweetheart. Become the predator, or remain the prey."</p>

<p>Before Alex could respond, alarms started blaring across the interface. Red warning text scrolled rapidly:</p>

<p>```</p>

<p>[URGENT: TIME-CRITICAL MISSION DETECTED]</p>

<p>[FAILURE TO ACCEPT WITHIN 60 SECONDS RESULTS IN PERMANENT LOCKOUT</p>

<p>59... 58... 57...]</p>

<p>```</p>

<p>"What the hell is this?" Alex demanded.</p>

<p>"Perfect timing, actually," Lilith purred. "Your first target just became available. Accept now, or lose your chance forever."</p>

<p>The countdown reached 45 seconds when a detailed profile materialized:</p>

<p>```</p>

<p>[PRIMARY TARGET: Victoria Blackwood]</p>

<p>Age: 42</p>

<p>Status: CEO, Blackwood Industries</p>

<p>Net Worth: $15.2 billion</p>

<p>Marital Status: Legally married, emotionally abandoned</p>

<p>Current Location: Home office, working late</p>

<p>Psychological State: Exhausted, lonely, questioning life choices</p>

<p>```</p>

<p>"Sophia’s mother?" Alex’s voice cracked.</p>

<p>"The woman who raised the bitch that destroyed you, yes. But also the woman who noticed you, Alex. She genuinely liked you during those three months. Thought you were refreshing compared to the vapid rich boys usually orbiting her daughter."</p>

<p>The timer hit 30 seconds.</p>

<p>"Victoria Blackwood works 16-hour days building an empire while her politician husband campaigns across the country. She hasn’t had meaningful physical intimacy in two years. She craves authentic connection with someone who sees her as more than a bank account."</p>

<p>A new screen appeared:</p>

<p>```</p>

<p>[MISSION OBJECTIVES:]</p>

<p>- Seduce Victoria Blackwood within 7 days</p>

<p>- Make her choose you over family loyalty.</p>

<p>- Bonus: Make her confront Sophia about the bet</p>

<p>[SUCCESS REWARDS]</p>

<p>- System permanently activated</p>

<p>- Enhanced charisma and physical capabilities</p>

<p>- Access to Level 2 missions</p>

<p>- Offshore account with $50 million starter funds</p>

<p>[FAILURE PENALTIES]</p>

<p>- System deactivation forever</p>

<p>- Return to broken, powerless state</p>

<p>- Guaranteed destruction by your enemies</p>

<p>```</p>

<p>20 seconds.</p>

<p>"She’s alone right now," Lilith whispered seductively. "Working late in her home office while her family ignores her. This is your window, Alex. Your chance to start the conquest that will give you everything."</p>

<p>15 seconds.</p>

<p>Alex’s mind raced. Seducing his ex-girlfriend’s mother was beyond twisted. But the alternative was returning to his dorm room, to his broken life, to the whispers and pitying looks from classmates who knew about his humiliation.</p>

<p>10 seconds.</p>

<p>"What makes you think I can seduce a billionaire CEO?" he asked desperately.</p>

<p>"Because you already did it once, darling. You seduced her daughter - admittedly it was fake on her end, but Victoria watched you charm Sophia for months. She knows you have something special."</p>

<p>5 seconds.</p>

<p>Alex thought of Sophia’s laugh. Marcus’s fists. The promise ring lying broken on marble floors.</p>

<p>"Time’s up, Alex. Choose now."</p>

<p>His finger moved toward the "ACCEPT" button floating in his vision.</p>

<p>"Yes."</p>

<p>The interface exploded with golden light.</p>

<p>```</p>

<p>[MISSION ACCEPTED]</p>

<p>[ENHANCEMENT PROTOCOL INITIATING</p>

<p>GOOD LUCK, DARLING. YOU’LL NEED IT]</p>

<p>```</p>

<p>___</p>

<p>The golden light faded, leaving Alex feeling... different. Not physically yet, but mentally sharper, like someone had cleared fog from his brain.</p>

<p>What have I just agreed to?</p>

<p>But even as the question formed, Alex found he didn’t care about the answer. For the first time since the hospital, he felt something other than pain and humiliation. He felt purpose.</p>

<p>"Victoria Blackwood," he said aloud, testing how her name felt on his tongue. The woman who’d smiled warmly at him during family dinners, who’d asked thoughtful questions about his business ideas, who’d treated him like a human being instead of a curiosity.</p>

<p>’She enabled Sophia’s cruelty by not seeing through it,’ Alex told himself. ’If she really cared about me dating her daughter, she would have noticed something was wrong. She would have protected me.’</p>

<p>The rationalization felt hollow, but Alex grabbed onto it anyway. He needed something to justify what he was about to do.</p>

<p>Standing up from the hospital bed, Alex was surprised to find the pain in his ribs had diminished to a dull ache. His face still looked like hamburger in the bathroom mirror, but his left eye was opening more than before.</p>

<p>’Enhanced healing. The system is already working.’</p>

<p>Alex studied his reflection. Broken nose, split lip, bruises covering half his face - he looked like exactly what he was: a victim. But underneath the damage, something new flickered in his eyes. Something hungry.</p>

<p>"They thought I was weak," he whispered to his reflection. "They thought I was nothing."</p>

<p>His phone buzzed with a text from an unknown number: "Black sedan waiting outside. Driver will take you to prepare for tonight. -L"</p>

<p>Alex grabbed his discharge papers and headed for the door. As he walked through the hospital corridors, he caught nurses staring at him - not with pity this time, but with something else. Curiosity? Interest?</p>

<p>’It’s already starting.’</p>

<p>In the elevator, Alex’s reflection showed a man with purpose. Still broken, still battered, but no longer just a victim.</p>

<p>He was becoming something else entirely.</p>

<p>___</p>

<p>The black sedan was exactly where Lilith had promised - a Bentley with tinted windows and a driver who didn’t ask questions. Alex slid into leather seats that probably cost more than his tuition.</p>

<p>"Where to, sir?" the driver asked.</p>

<p>Before Alex could answer, the car’s navigation system activated by itself, showing a route to an address in the city’s most expensive district.</p>

<p>The Blackwood estate loomed ahead - thirty acres of manicured grounds surrounding a mansion that looked like a fortress. Lights glowed in a single third-floor window.</p>

<p>"Victoria’s office," Alex murmured.</p>

<p>The system’s voice whispered in his head: "She’s working late again, as always. Her husband is campaigning in Nevada. Sophia is spending the night at Marcus’s penthouse. You have a clear field."</p>

<p>Alex straightened his borrowed suit and walked toward the gates, his reflection in the car window showing someone new - someone dangerous.</p>

<p>"Time to begin the hunt."</p>
