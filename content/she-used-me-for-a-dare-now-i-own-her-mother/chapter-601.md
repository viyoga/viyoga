<p>Beyond the security turnstiles, the true scale of the property revealed itself.</p>

<p>The central commercial tower rose with imposing architectural precision, its glass and steel atrium stretching upward toward the skylights. Through the floor-to-ceiling glass corridors, the view encompassed the entire city block.</p>

<p>The deed didn’t cover a mere office high-rise; it secured the whole surface perimeter bordered by North, East, South, and West Avenues.</p>

<p>Lush green courtyards, ornamental water features, and several surrounding commercial pavilions encircled the main tower, while massive subterranean levels extended deep underground for private logistics, operations, and secured archives.</p>

<p>Complete ownership of the sector, from the reinforced foundation to the rooftop helipad.</p>

<p>Alex walked through the grand gallery, taking in the clean geometry of the stone and glass with genuine satisfaction.</p>

<p>"To think I secured this entire block for what amounts to pocket change," Alex remarked aloud, his voice carrying easily through the quiet space. "Raymond really gave away an absolute fortress."</p>

<p>Walking half a step behind him, Naomi kept her eyes fixed on his back.</p>

<p>Seeing his relaxed stride and the casual confidence in his posture stirred an uncomfortable warmth beneath her ribs. He looked completely at home, moving with the easy grace of a man who naturally commanded everything around him.</p>

<p>For a fleeting second, a reckless, irrational thought surfaced in her mind, whispering how cruel it was to lead someone so undeniably striking and capable into a slaughter.</p>

<p>Naomi bit the inside of her lip, forcing the thought down.</p>

<p>She reminded herself that she was an operative first. This was her career, her survival, and her livelihood. She could not afford to let sudden, shameful desires cloud her judgment.</p>

<p>What she felt in the club was nothing more than raw physical lust, an unexpected lapse in control that had temporarily compromised her senses.</p>

<p>She could not betray her contract with the Mercers, nor could she throw away her standing with Karl, over a dangerous attraction to a man marked for death.</p>

<p>"Which way to the main office?" Alex asked, turning around and cutting cleanly through her inner spiral.</p>

<p>His dark eyes settled on her, catching the slight pallor on her face and the stiffness in her posture.</p>

<p>?A light, mocking smile curved his lips. "You look rather down today, Mrs. Shaw. You should cheer up. Transferring ownership of a magnificent place like this ought to bring a smile to anyone’s face."</p>

<p>?</p>

<p>He tilted his head slightly, his gaze sharpening with playful malice.</p>

<p>?"Or could it be... you’re simply not happy that I was the one who bought it?"</p>

<p>?Naomi snapped out of her daze, a cold bead of sweat running down her spine. For a terrifying second, she wondered if he had seen through her entire cover.</p>

<p>She quickly smoothed her expression into a polite, professional mask to hide the flutter of panic in her chest.</p>

<p>?"Not at all, Mr. Hale," she managed, keeping her tone measured. "A transaction of this scale is merely exhausting. The executive penthouse and central operations terminal are on the upper tier. The express lift will take us directly there. Right this way."</p>

<p>Turning away, Naomi led him down a secluded corridor toward a private elevator bank. She swiped an encrypted keycard against the reader, and the doors parted smoothly.</p>

<p>?Alex followed her inside, his imposing presence instantly making the spacious cabin feel narrow.</p>

<p>The heavy brushed-metal doors slid shut, sealing the two of them inside the private express elevator. With a soft, muted chime, the lift began its swift ascent, the digital display rapidly ticking upward as the ground floor fell away beneath them.</p>

<p>In the enclosed space, the silence felt deafening.</p>

<p>Naomi stood rigid beside the control panel, her knuckles turning white against the leather portfolio clutched to her chest. Her heart was hammering violently against her ribs, so loud she feared he could hear it.</p>

<p>She couldn’t stop her mind from picturing what was happening dozens of floors above them.</p>

<p>By now, the six masked men would have slipped out of the freight lift and moved into position, spreading silently across the executive lobby with their suppressed weapons trained on the elevator entrance.</p>

<p>In less than a minute, these doors would open, and Alex would be torn apart before he could even take a step.</p>

<p>And she would be the one who walked him into it. The thought twisted like a cold blade in her stomach. Fine beads of sweat pricked along the back of her neck and her temples, and no matter how much she tried to force her breathing to stay even, she couldn’t calm the panic rising inside her.</p>

<p>"You look far too nervous for someone of your expertise, Naomi."</p>

<p>The low, velvet voice shattered the silence.</p>

<p>Alex didn’t remain on his side of the cabin. He stepped directly into her space, cutting off her breath before she could step back. Raising his arms, he planted a hand on the mirrored wall on either side of her shoulders, trapping her completely beneath his shadow.</p>

<p>His broad frame loomed over her, shutting out the rest of the elevator and leaving her with nowhere to look except his calm, unbothered eyes.</p>

<p>"You certainly weren’t this timid in the club," Alex murmured, his gaze dropping slowly to her parted lips before rising back to her eyes. "You were quite bold back then. But right now, you look like a scared little cat."</p>

<p>He tilted his head slightly, a faint trace of mocking disappointment coloring his expression.</p>

<p>"You’re really turning out to be quite a disappointment, Mrs. Shaw. Don’t tell me all that fire was just the alcohol talking?"</p>

<p>His taunts struck straight through her fragile composure.</p>

<p>Naomi’s breath hitched, her chest rising and falling in rapid, unsteady gasps. Her rational mind screamed at her to pull away, to remember her training, her duty, and the mission.</p>

<p>’Why do I even care?’ she cursed herself desperately. ’Why do I care if he dies? He’s just a target. A stranger.’</p>

<p>Yet the heat rolling off his body made her head spin. The phantom memory of that night flared back to life... his hands on her skin, the effortless way he had overpowered her, and that dark, lingering ache that had haunted her ever since.</p>

<p>’Was that truly all it was? Was she losing her mind simply because she had never actually gotten to taste him completely?’</p>

<p>The elevator display flashed past the fortieth floor. The floor indicator was climbing toward their execution point, second by second.</p>

<p>Driven by a reckless wave of panic and pure, suffocating desire, her last shred of restraint snapped.</p>

<p>Naomi let go of her portfolio, reached up with trembling hands, and grabbed the lapels of his suit jacket. Pulling him down with sudden, desperate strength, she surged forward and captured his lips with her own.</p>
