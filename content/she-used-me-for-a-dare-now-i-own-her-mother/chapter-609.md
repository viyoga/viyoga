<p>The concussive blast tore through the heavy frame, sending shattered oak, twisted brass, and a cloud of burning cordite smoke roaring into the office.</p>

<p>In that exact fraction of a second, Alex moved.</p>

<p>With effortless, unhurried strength, he wrapped his arms around Naomi and shifted his body, using his broad, iron-sculpted back to shield her completely from the flying splinters and the shockwave.</p>

<p>Naomi stayed right where she was... locked securely on his lap, her bare thighs clamped tight around his hips, her face buried deep into the warm curve of his neck and chest.</p>

<p>He hadn’t pulled out. If anything, the violent detonation seemed to feed the untamed fire running through his veins.</p>

<p>Deep inside her slick, trembling core, his heavy length gave a slow, powerful throb, pulsing with hot energy as if the impending danger only heightened his arousal. It was buried so profoundly deep that every breath she took felt completely claimed by him... a sensation so overwhelming and unforgettable that it branded itself into her very soul.</p>

<p>"Clear left! Clear right!"</p>

<p>The sharp, disciplined tactical commands barked through the settling dust just a few feet away.</p>

<p>?Buried against the crook of Alex’s neck, Naomi heard every single syllable.</p>

<p>She didn’t tense. Instead, a slow, wicked smirk curved her reddened lips against his warm skin. Her arms simply tightened around his broad shoulders, her hips sinking down just a fraction deeper against his lap in pure, brazen defiance.</p>

<p>Through the swirling haze of gray smoke and pulverized drywall, heavy combat boots stepped over the splintered threshold.</p>

<p>Karl strode in first with his tactical sidearm raised, backed tightly by six heavily armored operatives sweeping the shadows with suppressed rifles.</p>

<p>They had breached the room ready for violence.</p>

<p>They had expected to find Alex’s corpse bleeding out on the rug, or Naomi frantically scrambling behind the desk, weeping and cowering in terror.</p>

<p>Instead, as the draft from the shattered window swept the smoke aside, the scene revealed before them froze every single man in his tracks.</p>

<p>The bloodthirsty grin on Karl’s face instantly died. His jaw went slack, his tactical discipline evaporating into thin air as the reality before him shocked him and his entire squad to their very cores.</p>

<p>For three long seconds, the ruined executive suite was dead silent.</p>

<p>?The six tactical operatives lowered their suppressed rifles by an inch, their eyes darting between the mahogany desk and their commanding officer.</p>

<p>?They all recognized Naomi Shaw. She was known to them as the pristine, untouchable corporate attorney who doubled as the Mercer family’s most lethal shadow operative... a cold, calculating woman who had never once lowered her guard or shown a flicker of weakness.</p>

<p>?To see her completely naked, flushed crimson, and shamelessly joined with the man they had been dispatched to assassinate completely shattered their tactical focus.</p>

<p>?"She really is a damn beauty..." one of the men muttered under his breath, the words slipping out completely unconsciously.</p>

<p>?Beside him, the other operatives nodded without a second thought. Their fingers remained loosely curled around their triggers, but their professional discipline had melted away, replaced by open, intoxicating lust burning in their eyes as they stared at their commander’s wife.</p>

<p>?Beside them, Karl stood completely frozen.</p>

<p>?Hearing his own elite squad openly drooling over his naked wife felt like scalding oil poured directly into his ears.</p>

<p>Every drop of blood in his veins rushed to his head, the veins in his neck and temples bulging into thick, violent cords. His hands shook so violently that the frame of his pistol rattled against his knuckles.</p>

<p>?His own wife was sitting brazenly in the lap of Alex Hale — the one man he hated down to his very marrow — while his men openly lusted after the sight.</p>

<p>?"Shut up!" Karl screeched, his voice cracking into a rabid, humiliated roar. "Shut the fuck up!"</p>

<p>?He threw his arm forward, pointing the trembling muzzle straight at the desk.</p>

<p>?"Shoot! Shoot the hell out of them! Tear them both to pieces right fucking now!"</p>

<p>?The six operatives violently snapped out of their daze. Shaking the haze from their minds, they brought their suppressed rifles to bear and held their triggers down.</p>

<p>?RAT-TAT-TAT-TAT-TAT!</p>

<p>A deafening storm of suppressed automatic fire erupted across the room.</p>

<p>Muzzle flashes strobed frantically against the shadows, unleashing a hail of armor-piercing rounds aimed directly at the desk. Plaster shattered from the back wall, and a thick haze of cordite and pulverized drywall flooded the air.</p>

<p>Yet, through the violent barrage, neither Alex nor Naomi made a single move to take cover.</p>

<p>Naomi didn’t scream. She didn’t flinch. Her face remained comfortably buried against the broad, solid wall of Alex’s shield-like chest, completely hidden from the shooters and the blinding strobes of gunfire. She remained entirely sheltered in his embrace, treating the deafening roar of automatic fire as if it were nothing more than a harmless breeze.</p>

<p>The rifles ran dry. Six empty magazines dropped onto the carpet, while brass casings scattered across the floor.</p>

<p>?In the sudden, ringing silence, Alex didn’t even shift his weight. A lazy, wicked smirk curled the edge of his lips, his fingers idly stroking the smooth curve of Naomi’s bare hip as though a hail of armor-piercing rounds were nothing more than falling rain.</p>

<p>?"Such noisy pests," Alex murmured, his deep, resonant rumble vibrating directly against her cheek with pure, effortless disdain. "Interrupting us right when things were getting good. What do you say, Naomi... should I clear this trash out of our way?"</p>

<p>?Naomi didn’t hesitate for a single second.</p>

<p>?Still sheltered safely against his chest, she gave a firm, deliberate nod against the warm curve of his collarbone... her agreement silent, absolute, and completely stripped of mercy.</p>

<p>The thick cloud of smoke and plaster dust slowly drifted aside, swept away by the morning air pouring through the shattered panoramic window.</p>

<p>The enforcers squinted, expecting to see a mangled pile of shredded flesh and shattered mahogany.</p>

<p>Instead, their eyes widened in total disbelief.</p>

<p>Sitting in the exact same position, untouched and pristine, were Alex and Naomi. Not a single bullet had grazed their skin. Not a single splatter of blood marred the mahogany desk.</p>

<p>Around them, dozens of flattened, deformed lead slugs lay scattered harmlessly on the carpet, their kinetic force completely erased before they could come within two feet of the couple.</p>

<p>Karl’s jaw dropped.</p>

<p>A cold, greasy sweat broke out across his forehead, instantly soaking through his collar. His skin lost every trace of color, turning an ashen, sickly gray.</p>

<p>The sniper round that vanished into thin air. The bullet that left no impact crater in the lobby. The dead, unyielding wall that had just stopped hundreds of armor-piercing rounds without a scratch.</p>

<p>It wasn’t body armor. It wasn’t luck.</p>

<p>A suffocating, primal terror seized Karl’s throat, squeezing the breath right out of his chest. His knees trembled, his weapon slipping from his numb fingers to hit the floor with a hollow thud.</p>

<p>Only a single, trembling word managed to crawl past his dry lips.</p>

<p>"...Apex."</p>

<p>An Apex Cultivator.</p>

<p>The realization hit him like a physical blow. Survival instinct entirely obliterated his pride, his rage, and his desire for vengeance.</p>

<p>Without giving a single order, and without uttering another word to his bewildered men, Karl spun violently on his heel and bolted into the dark corridor, running for his life like a terrified dog.</p>

<p>Karl didn’t look back. His boots pounded frantically against the polished marble, his breath tearing in ragged, burning gasps.</p>

<p>Every survival instinct screamed at him to reach the elevator shaft, to crawl down the maintenance vents — anything to put distance between himself and the monster in that room.</p>

<p>He didn’t even make it past the threshold.</p>

<p>A sudden, freezing gust of wind whipped through the corridor. The air pressure plummeted, and before Karl’s boot could hit the broken door, a dark shadow rose directly in front of him.</p>

<p>Alex was already there.</p>

<p>There was no sound, no warning, and no time to react. Before Karl could even process the shadow suddenly blocking his path, a storm of blinding, brutal strikes hammered into his body. It was a blur of violence delivered in a fraction of a second... so inhumanly fast and seamless that Karl couldn’t even tell where he was being struck.</p>

<p>?A muffled, sickening series of cracks snapped through the hall.</p>

<p>?Karl’s vision flashed white. The strength tore out of his muscles all at once, his limbs turning into dead weight while he was still in mid-air. His momentum collapsed instantly, and he crashed face-first onto the hard marble floor with a heavy, hollow thud. He lay there limp and paralyzed, unable to twitch a single finger or let out more than a choked, wheezing gasp, his eyes bulging in silent, suffocating horror.</p>

<p>Inside the doorway, the six operatives froze, their faces draining of color.</p>

<p>Seeing their commanding lieutenant struck down like a stray dog in the blink of an eye shattered the last ounce of their composure. The terrifying weight of the word Karl had muttered finally crashed over their minds with full, suffocating reality.</p>

<p>Apex.</p>

<p>Panic set in. With trembling, clumsy fingers, the men scrambled to draw their secondary sidearms—</p>

<p>It was already too late.</p>

<p>Alex didn’t give them the space to blink. He flickered into their midst like an ink stain spreading across water.</p>

<p>Thwack. Thwack. Thwack.</p>

<p>One by one, the elite enforcers dropped where they stood. Their rifles clattered across the floor, their armored bodies collapsing like marionettes whose strings had been cleanly cut.</p>

<p>In less than two seconds, all six men were sprawled across the floor around the shattered doorway, wide-eyed, completely paralyzed, and drowning in helpless terror.</p>

<p>Stepping over the paralyzed heap of bodies as though they were nothing more than discarded trash, Alex walked back into the office. His bare, sculpted chest rose and fell in a calm, unbroken rhythm, his obsidian eyes dark with sovereign satisfaction.</p>

<p>Naomi was still waiting at the edge of the mahogany desk. Naked, flushed crimson, and trembling with electric excitement, her golden eyes shone with absolute adoration as she watched him return.</p>

<p>Alex closed the distance, his hand reaching out to tilt her chin upward, his thumb lightly stroking the flushed warmth of her cheek. A slow, wicked smile curved his lips.</p>

<p>"Now then, darling..." Alex murmured, his deep voice smooth and laced with dark amusement. "Where were we? I believe we have some unfinished business to attend to."</p>
