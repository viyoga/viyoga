<p>Time stopped.</p>

<p>One second. Two. Three.</p>

<p>Each heartbeat stretched into eternity as Lila remained frozen... naked, straddling Alex.</p>

<p>Her orgasm died like a gutted flame, pleasure ripping away so violently it left only hollow, aching emptiness.</p>

<p>No. No no no no...</p>

<p>Mike stood in the doorway, chest heaving, eyes wide with shock.</p>

<p>And Lila felt...</p>

<p>Rage.</p>

<p>Pure, white-hot, incandescent rage.</p>

<p>Not at being caught. But at Mike’s presence.</p>

<p>At the interruption.</p>

<p>You fucking idiot, she thought venomously, her mind still half-drunk on pleasure even as reality crashed back in. Why NOW? Why right FUCKING now?</p>

<p>She’d been so close. So perfectly, exquisitely close to drowning in this moment... to carving the feeling of Alex inside her into her memory so deeply it would never fade.</p>

<p>And this fool... this terrible-timing, oblivious FOOL... had destroyed it.</p>

<p>The fury burned through her veins like acid, so intense that for one dangerous heartbeat she almost screamed at him. Almost told him exactly what she thought of his intrusion.</p>

<p>Then... like ice water dumped over her head... clarity hit.</p>

<p>Stop.</p>

<p>The command sliced through her spiraling thoughts like a blade.</p>

<p>Stop. Think. ACT.</p>

<p>Sophia’s voice echoed in her skull, sharp and unforgiving: ’Don’t you dare fuck this up, Lila. One mistake and you’re done.’</p>

<p>Her body was still frozen, still straddling Alex, still exposed and vulnerable and caught in the worst possible position.</p>

<p>But her mind... her mind snapped into cold, ruthless focus.</p>

<p>Sophia.</p>

<p>The name sliced through her rage like a blade.</p>

<p>The mission. The plan. This is...</p>

<p>Her mind reeled, scrambling to catch up.</p>

<p>This is it. This is the moment. This is what she’s here for.</p>

<p>The cameras. The setup. The careful orchestration of bringing Alex here, getting him inside her, and making sure Mike would...</p>

<p>Oh God.</p>

<p>The full weight of the situation crashed over her like a tidal wave... but her frustration and panic didn’t lessen; they surged higher, tightening around her like a vise.</p>

<p>For one wild, desperate moment, a thought slammed into her... screw every scheme, every plan, every lie. She wanted to make it right, to tell Alex everything before it was too late.</p>

<p>Maybe... maybe if I tell him the truth right now... maybe I can still keep Alex. Maybe...</p>

<p>But then Sophia’s face materialized in her mind.</p>

<p>Not the charming, mischievous she showed the world. The other one. The real one.</p>

<p>Cold. Ruthless.</p>

<p>Those dark eyes gleaming with something that wasn’t quite human, boring into her like knives.</p>

<p>Do it if you dare.</p>

<p>The unspoken threat hung in the air between past and present, wrapping around Lila’s throat like a noose.</p>

<p>She shivered, the tremor running bone-deep despite the warmth of Alex still inside her.</p>

<p>Abandoning the plan wasn’t an option.</p>

<p>It never had been.</p>

<p>Sophia didn’t forgive failure. Sophia didn’t accept excuses. And Sophia never let anyone walk away.</p>

<p>Lila had seen what happened to those who tried.</p>

<p>Her gaze slid sideways... slowly, carefully... to Alex beneath her.</p>

<p>And her breath caught.</p>

<p>His face... God, his face.</p>

<p>Disbelief. Panic. Those dark eyes wide, pupils blown, chest heaving beneath her.</p>

<p>He looked... he looked exactly like a man caught in the worst moment of his life. Exactly like someone who’d just realized his world was ending.</p>

<p>Perfect.</p>

<p>The thought stabbed through her panic, cruel in its clarity... it tore at her, yet she knew there was no other choice.</p>

<p>Her mind shifted, clicking into place like puzzle pieces snapping together.</p>

<p>Okay. Okay. I can work with this.</p>

<p>She would play the script. Follow Sophia’s plan to the letter. Let Mike believe what he was supposed to believe. Let the confrontation explode exactly as Sophia had orchestrated.</p>

<p>And then... after... when the dust settled and Mike was gone and the cameras stopped rolling...</p>

<p>She would go to Alex.</p>

<p>She would explain everything. Make him understand. Use every tool in her arsenal to pull him back to her side.</p>

<p>I did it once. I can do it again.</p>

<p>She’d made him want her before, hadn’t she? Made him cross every line, break every rule, throw away his friendship for one night with her.</p>

<p>She could do it again.</p>

<p>She would do it again.</p>

<p>Because Alex... Alex was hers now. He just didn’t know it yet.</p>

<p>All she had to do was survive the next ten minutes.</p>

<p>Then she could have everything.</p>

<p>***</p>

<p>Her body was still frozen, still straddling Alex, but her mind snapped into ruthless focus.</p>

<p>Perform. Now. Perfectly.</p>

<p>She moved.</p>

<p>One gasping breath, and then she was scrambling backward off Alex’s lap with a cry that was half-shock, half-manufactured horror.</p>

<p>Her hands flew to the tangled sheet at the foot of the bed, yanking it up against her chest with desperate, clumsy movements.</p>

<p>"Mike..."</p>

<p>His name tore from her throat... broken, raw, soaked in shame and terror.</p>

<p>Perfect.</p>

<p>Her legs shook as her feet hit the floor. Real trembling... adrenaline and performance melting together until even she couldn’t tell the difference.</p>

<p>She backed away from the bed. From Alex. The sheet clutched so tightly against her naked body that her knuckles went white.</p>

<p>"Mike, I..."</p>

<p>A sob caught in her throat. She let it. Let the sound crack and splinter.</p>

<p>"Oh God, Mike..."</p>

<p>Tears welled in her eyes. She’d learned this trick years ago... how to tap that hidden reservoir of emotion and pull it to the surface at will.</p>

<p>The tears spilled over, hot tracks down her flushed cheeks.</p>

<p>She let her breathing go ragged. Let her shoulders shake.</p>

<p>This is what he expects to see, she thought with cold precision even as her sobs grew louder.</p>

<p>A victim. Violated. Destroyed.</p>

<p>Mike stood frozen in the doorway, his face a mask of shock and devastation.</p>

<p>Her hand shot out, finger pointing directly at Alex.</p>

<p>Trembling. Accusing.</p>

<p>"He..."</p>

<p>The word came out strangled, desperate, and she forced her voice to crack beautifully on it.</p>

<p>"Mike, he... he tried to force me... "</p>

<p>Silence crashed over the room like a wave.</p>

<p>Even Alex went still.</p>

<p>Lila kept her finger raised, shaking violently, tears streaming down her face.</p>

<p>"He threatened me..." Her voice climbed higher, threading hysteria through every syllable. "I said no, I told him no but he... he wouldn’t listen..."</p>

<p>She choked on the words. Let her whole body shudder with sobs.</p>

<p>"I tried to stop him but he was too strong... when I kept saying no he just..."</p>

<p>The word caught in her throat.</p>

<p>Deliberate.</p>

<p>Devastating.</p>

<p>"He ra..."</p>

<p>She couldn’t finish it.</p>

<p>Let the accusation hang there, incomplete but unmistakable. More powerful for what she didn’t say.</p>

<p>Her hand dropped, covering her face as she collapsed to her knees on the floor.</p>

<p>The sheet pooled around her waist. Her shoulders shook with violent, wrenching sobs.</p>

<p>"I’m so sorry... " The words came out garbled, barely intelligible through her crying. "I’m so sorry, Mike... I didn’t want this... I tried to fight him but I couldn’t... "</p>

<p>She peeked through her fingers, just enough to see Mike’s face.</p>

<p>Still frozen. Still devastated.</p>

<p>Got you.</p>
