<p>Alex’s eyes tracked down the golden interface, taking in the familiar categories... Status, Skills, Missions... all things he’d grown accustomed to over the past months.</p>

<p>But then his gaze caught on something new.</p>

<p>Something that made his enhanced mind pause.</p>

<p>★ NEW FEATURES</p>

<p>The text seemed to shimmer slightly, as if the system itself was drawing his attention to it. Waiting for him to explore.</p>

<p>He glanced toward the living room, where Lilith had settled onto the couch with that knowing smile playing at her lips. She was watching him, eyes gleaming with anticipation.</p>

<p>"Go ahead," she encouraged, gesturing toward the interface.</p>

<p>Alex felt his pulse quicken... not from nervousness, but from genuine excitement.</p>

<p>With a thought, he focused on NEW FEATURES.</p>

<p>The interface expanded instantly, responsive to his intention in a way the old system never had been. Golden text cascaded across his vision, revealing options that made his breath catch.</p>

<p>[NEW FEATURES]</p>

<p>━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━</p>

<p>[1] MASTER’S PRIVILEGE</p>

<p>Status: ACTIVE</p>

<p>Effect: All Shop purchases reduced to 50% of original cost</p>

<p>Justification: True Masters command resources efficiently</p>

<p>Note: Applies to skills, items, enhancements, and services</p>

<p>━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━</p>

<p>[2] STAT ENHANCEMENT PROTOCOL</p>

<p>Status: UNLOCKED</p>

<p>Function: Convert Conquest Points directly into stat points.</p>

<p>Exchange Rate: 500 CP = +1 Stat Point</p>

<p>Note: Enhancement is permanent and immediate.</p>

<p>━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━</p>

<p>[3] TACTICAL SCAN</p>

<p>Status: UNLOCKED</p>

<p>Function: Analyze target’s power level and threat assessment</p>

<p>Range: Visual contact required</p>

<p>Output: Power tier, realm stage, threat level, basic capabilities</p>

<p>Limitations: Cannot scan beings more than 2 realms above host.</p>

<p>Warning Display: [DANGER] indicator for overwhelming threats</p>

<p>━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━</p>

<p>Alex stared at the interface, his enhanced mind processing each feature at superhuman speed.</p>

<p>Holy shit.</p>

<p>Master’s Privilege meant everything in the Shop cost half price. Every skill, every enhancement, every resource... cut in half.</p>

<p>He found himself wondering how many missions it would take before he could just get whatever he wanted from the system instantly.</p>

<p>He knew he’d need an absurd amount of points to reach that level... but with this new benefit, the thought didn’t feel as impossible anymore.</p>

<p>He exhaled a quiet sigh of relief. At least this was something... something that made the climb ahead feel a little less impossible.</p>

<p>But that was just the beginning.</p>

<p>The Stat Enhancement Protocol... he could buy stat points directly with Contribution Points. His mind raced through the mathematics.</p>

<p>500 CP for +1 stat.</p>

<p>That meant 5,000 CP would give him +10 across a stat... the equivalent of advancing from one stage to the next without the need for divine catalysts or breakthrough moments.</p>

<p>It was... incredible.</p>

<p>But the third feature...</p>

<p>Alex felt his eyes lock onto the Tactical Scan with almost predatory focus.</p>

<p>Information. Real-time power assessment. Threat evaluation.</p>

<p>He’d been operating blind for months, guessing at people’s capabilities, relying on Lilith’s warnings to avoid catastrophic mistakes.</p>

<p>The memory of almost confronting Damien and Dimitri flashed through his mind... how close he’d come to disaster because he couldn’t perceive the gap between their power and his.</p>

<p>Never again.</p>

<p>With Tactical Scan, he’d know. He’d see power levels, threat assessments, capabilities. The [DANGER] warning would keep him from walking into situations that would get him killed.</p>

<p>It was survival. It was strategy. It was information warfare made manifest.</p>

<p>"You’re thinking very loudly, darling," Lilith’s voice drifted from the couch, amused and warm. "I can practically hear your mind calculating possibilities."</p>

<p>Alex looked up from the interface, meeting her eyes.</p>

<p>"These features..." he said slowly, his voice carrying a mixture of awe and excitement. "They’re not just upgrades. They’re force multipliers."</p>

<p>Lilith’s smile widened, clearly delighted by his assessment.</p>

<p>"Exactly," Lilith confirmed, her wine-dark eyes gleaming with satisfaction. "You understand. These aren’t just conveniences, Alex. They’re the tools that separate Masters from those who merely have some powers."</p>

<p>She stood, moving toward him with that liquid grace.</p>

<p>"For now, these three are yours. Master them. Use them wisely. And remember..."</p>

<p>Her hand came to rest on his shoulder... warm, alive, thrumming with divine energy.</p>

<p>"The system will serve you," she continued, voice low and deliberate, "but only as well as you serve your own purpose. Power without wisdom is destruction. But power wielded strategically..."</p>

<p>Her smile curved into something sharp, predatory.</p>

<p>"That’s dominion."</p>

<p>Alex understood perfectly.</p>

<p>The system wasn’t just about strength... it was about choices. Every feature, every reward, every tool depended on how intelligently he used them.</p>

<p>Reckless power would burn him out. Calculated power could change everything.</p>

<p>He glanced down at his hands... the faint hum of energy running beneath his skin, the golden interface hovering before him, alive with promise.</p>

<p>Master’s Privilege. Stat Enhancement Protocol. Tactical Scan.</p>

<p>Three new tools. Three new paths forward.</p>

<p>And if Lilith was right, this was only the beginning.</p>

<p>He pulled the interface back up, golden light flickering across his eyes as his thoughts raced.</p>

<p>Status.</p>

<p>He needed to see it... needed to feel how much he’d actually changed.</p>

<p>"Status first," Alex murmured, a hint of anticipation slipping into his voice as he focused on the option.</p>

<p>The command responded instantly, the golden display shifting and expanding before him.</p>

<p>A single line of text appeared, simple and direct.</p>

<p>[SYSTEM INPUT REQUIRED]</p>

<p>He frowned slightly, reading the prompt that expanded below it.</p>

<p>Lilith gestured to the display.</p>

<p>"The system needs to understand what kind of Master you intend to be. Not what you are right now, but what you’re building toward. Your path. Your ambition."</p>

<p>The interface expanded.</p>

<p>[WHO ARE YOU BECOMING?]</p>

<p>[Define your path:]</p>

<p>★ CONQUEROR</p>

<p>Ascend through combat, accumulate supernatural strength, dominate hierarchies</p>

<p>Missions focused on: power accumulation, territorial control, direct confrontation</p>

<p>★ SEDUCER</p>

<p>Path of Charm and Connection</p>

<p>[Missions, rewards, and growth will align with your chosen identity]</p>

<p>Alex stared at the options, feeling the weight of the question.</p>

<p>This wasn’t just picking a class in a game. This was defining who he wanted to become.</p>

<p>"Why does it need to know?" he asked.</p>

<p>"Because Level 2.0 doesn’t test you anymore," Lilith explained, her voice soft but serious. "It serves you. But it can’t serve you effectively if it doesn’t understand what you’re trying to build."</p>

<p>She placed a hand on his shoulder.</p>

<p>"The system isn’t asking you to limit yourself, Alex. It’s asking you to be honest about your ambitions. About who you see yourself becoming in the years ahead."</p>

<p>Alex looked at each option again.</p>

<p>Businessman. He had a billion dollars and the intelligence to build something extraordinary.</p>

<p>Conqueror. He’d just reached Enhanced Stage and hunger for more power thrummed through him.</p>

<p>Seducer. Victoria’s devotion, Tisha’s loyalty... he couldn’t deny the appeal of that path.</p>

<p>But the truth was...</p>

<p>"I’m not just one thing," Alex said slowly. "I never have been."</p>

<p>He thought about Victoria and her business connections. About the supernatural power now flowing through his veins. About the relationships he’d built and the empire he could create with a billion dollars.</p>

<p>"I want all of it," he admitted. "The wealth, the power, the connections. I don’t want to choose just one path."</p>

<p>Lilith’s smile was knowing.</p>

<p>"Then choose Hybrid," she said simply. "It’s the hardest path... no specialization bonuses, no focused growth. But it’s also the most honest. You won’t be constrained by a single identity."</p>

<p>Alex met her eyes.</p>

<p>"Hybrid," he confirmed.</p>

<p>The interface pulsed.</p>

<p>[PATH SELECTED: HYBRID]</p>

<p>[Master Identity: Confirmed]</p>

<p>[System optimization: Complete]</p>

<p>[Dominion System fully initialized]</p>

<p>Dominion System’s core remains Seduction, but evolution has expanded its recognition of multiple forms of conquest... Emotional, Strategic, and Physical.</p>

<p>The nature of Dominion has not changed.</p>

<p>Only its reach has.</p>

<p>[Welcome, Master of All Paths]</p>

<p>The golden display settled, and Alex felt something shift... not physically, but in how the system responded to him. It felt... aligned. Like it finally understood who he was.</p>

<p>"Perfect," Lilith said, her satisfaction profound. She turned to him, wine-dark eyes gleaming with something between pride and anticipation.</p>

<p>"Now the real work begins."</p>

<p>She gestured to the interface, where new options were already appearing... missions, opportunities, paths forward.</p>

<p>"You have wealth, power, skills, and a system that knows who you are." Her smile turned wicked. "So tell me, Master..."</p>

<p>She leaned closer, her presence overwhelming in the best way.</p>

<p>"What do you want to build?"</p>

<p>Alex looked at the interface... at the possibilities spreading before him like a map of futures he could choose.</p>

<p>And for the first time since this whole journey began, he felt like he was finally in control.</p>

<p>Not just of his power.</p>

<p>Of his destiny.</p>
