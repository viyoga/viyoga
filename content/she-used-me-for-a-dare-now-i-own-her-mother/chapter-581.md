<p>Linda’s face lit up with radiant maternal pride, a warm laugh bubbling up as she waved a hand dismissively.</p>

<p>"See? Good food cures more troubles than any executive boardroom ever could. Eat as much as you like. There is plenty more where that came from."</p>

<p>Across the table, David set down his coffee mug, the ceramic clicking softly against the saucer.</p>

<p>The easy humor in his face settled into something much quieter and deeply sincere as he looked across at Victoria.</p>

<p>"It really is an honor to have you under our roof, Victoria." David said, his voice measured and full of heartfelt gravity.</p>

<p>"We never had the chance to properly thank you. Back when Nina took a turn for the worse at Memorial, when we felt completely helpless... you stepped in without hesitation. You cleared the private wing, brought in the top specialists, and made sure our little girl received the best care in the city. There aren’t enough words in the world to repay a debt like that."</p>

<p>The dining room quieted for a moment, the weight of those dark hospital days lingering softly in the air.</p>

<p>Victoria set her fork down with practiced grace, looking directly at David and Linda. Her gaze held none of the cold, unyielding pride that usually kept high society at arm’s length.</p>

<p>"You really don’t owe me anything, Mr. Morrison," Victoria answered softly, shaking her head with an earnest smile.</p>

<p>Reaching out, she gently smoothed down a stray lock of Nina’s hair, letting her hand rest with natural affection on the little girl’s shoulder as Nina leaned happily against her side.</p>

<p>"Nina is such a sweet, resilient girl," Victoria continued, her voice warm and sincere. "Seeing her healthy, energetic, and laughing at this table is the only reward anyone could ever ask for."</p>

<p>"Besides, if we are being completely honest... I only made a few phone calls to open the hospital doors. Alex was the one who moved heaven and earth to save her. If anyone brought Nina back to you, it was him."</p>

<p>David and Linda exchanged a quiet, emotional glance.</p>

<p>Hearing a woman of Victoria’s immense stature speak with such genuine modesty, openly placing Alex at the center of everything, touched them more than any formal courtesy ever could.</p>

<p>"That boy has always been like that," Linda said softly, breaking the heavy stillness with an affectionate sigh as her eyes drifted to Alex.</p>

<p>"Ever since Danny first brought him through our front door, he never knew how to put himself first. Whenever our family hit a rough patch, whenever money was tight or one of us fell ill, he was always the first to quietly step forward and take the weight onto his own shoulders."</p>

<p>Linda paused, setting her coffee cup down as her gaze lingered on Alex with an overflowing maternal warmth forged through years of shared hardships.</p>

<p>"He’s always been stubbornly protective that way... he would endure anything in silence just so the rest of us wouldn’t have to worry, never once asking for a single thing in return."</p>

<p>Alex gave a quiet, helpless groan, resting an elbow against the table and rubbing his temple with one hand as the spotlight shifted entirely onto him.</p>

<p>"Mother, please," Alex murmured with a wry shake of his head. "We were having such a nice morning. Do we really need to turn breakfast into an emotional retrospective about me?"</p>

<p>"We certainly do," Linda replied with a triumphant tilt of her chin, utterly undeterred by his protest. She turned an affectionate, conspiratorial glance across the table toward Victoria. "Victoria deserves to know what kind of boy you really are. Behind that calm, composed face, you’re just a stubborn young man who never learned how to stop shouldering everyone else’s troubles. Don’t you agree, Victoria?"</p>

<p>Victoria’s lips curved into an amused, radiant smile, her eyes glittering with quiet delight as she looked from Linda to the thoroughly cornered man beside Nina.</p>

<p>"I couldn’t agree more," Victoria answered smoothly, leaning forward just a fraction. "In fact, I would love to hear everything."</p>

<p>Alex glanced across the table toward Danny for any shred of backup, but Danny only raised his coffee mug with an unapologetic, teasing smirk, silently letting him know he was entirely on his own.</p>

<p>Turning her head, Victoria took in the rare, embarrassed look on Alex’s face. A faint, knowing smile curved her lips, her dark eyes softening with a quiet warmth and a deep sense of understanding.</p>

<p>She was well aware of his incredible strength and the immense burdens he carried, but hearing about the quiet, fiercely stubborn boy who had clawed his way through poverty and hardship without a word of complaint touched something deep within her. Her respect for him, already profound, settled even deeper into her heart.</p>

<p>Beneath the linen tablecloth, Victoria’s slender fingers reached out and lightly brushed against Alex’s knee, offering an unspoken, intimate reassurance that she understood every sacrifice he had ever made.</p>

<p>Alex caught her fingers in his hand, giving them a firm, grounding squeeze before looking back at Linda with a warm, resigned smile.</p>

<p>"Alright, alright. I surrender, Mom. Just leave a little bit of my dignity intact before Nina starts taking notes."</p>

<p>That earned an immediate burst of triumphant giggles from Nina and a hearty chuckle from David.</p>

<p>Breakfast continued with that same lively, infectious energy.</p>

<p>By the time the meal wound down, the platters were nearly bare. Seeing the empty dishes, Victoria instinctively picked up her plate and silverware, preparing to carry them toward the kitchen sink.</p>

<p>Linda gasped in mock horror, instantly waving a checkered dish towel to block her path.</p>

<p>"Put that down right this second!" Linda scolded affectionately. "You are our honored guest, Victoria. If I let you wash a single dish under my roof, I wouldn’t be able to look at myself in the mirror. Go on, sit back down."</p>

<p>Before Victoria could even protest, two small hands latched firmly onto the sleeve of her coat.</p>

<p>"Mom said you can’t do chores, Aunty Victoria!" Nina announced triumphantly, tugging with all her might. "Come on, come to my room! I have three whole drawing pads and I drew a picture of our new garden with the big pond!"</p>

<p>Victoria looked down at the energetic little girl, completely disarmed, and let out a soft, amused laugh. "Alright, lead the way. I want to see this garden."</p>

<p>As Nina eagerly dragged Victoria down the hallway toward the stairs, Danny leaned back in his chair, watching the retreating figure of the formidable Blackwood matriarch being happily led around by an eight-year-old child. He slowly shook his head in pure disbelief.</p>

<p>"If anyone in the downtown financial district saw this," Danny muttered under his breath, "they’d think they were having a collective stroke."</p>

<p>Alex chuckled, standing up from his chair. With the women upstairs, the atmosphere shifted into something calmer, giving Alex the exact window he needed to fulfill the quiet promise he had made to Danny earlier that morning.</p>

<p>David rose to clear the remaining cups, but Alex reached out, resting a gentle hand over David’s wrist to stop him.</p>

<p>"Dad, leave those for a minute," Alex said quietly.</p>

<p>David paused, adjusting his wire-rimmed glasses as he looked at the serious, steady composure settling over the young man’s features. Beside them, Danny straightened up, sensing the shift in tone immediately.</p>

<p>Setting the mugs back down on the wooden surface, David pulled out his chair and sat back down. He rested his hands flat on the table, studying Alex with the calm, observant patience of someone who had seen his son grow through hardships into a man of quiet conviction.</p>

<p>?"What is it, son?" David asked gently. "You’ve got that look in your eye. Something on your mind?"</p>

<p>?Alex didn’t beat around the bush. He met David’s gaze directly, speaking clearly so there was no misunderstanding.</p>

<p>?"Victoria isn’t just dropping by for breakfast today, Dad," Alex said, his voice even and steady.</p>

<p>"From now on, she will be staying here at Roland Estate. In Villa 1, with me."</p>

<p>David’s brow furrowed, his fingers tightening slightly against the edge of the table.</p>

<p>Even with his limited involvement in upper-district affairs, David was far from naive. He knew exactly what the name Blackwood stood for in this city... ruthless power, ancient wealth, and a tangled web of political influence.</p>

<p>A woman of Victoria’s position didn’t simply walk out of her family compound to live in someone else’s villa without ripples spreading across the entire city.</p>

<p>Danny swallowed quietly, leaning forward in his chair. He had suspected things were moving in this direction, but hearing Alex lay it out so definitively still gave him chills.</p>

<p>David exhaled a slow, heavy breath, leaning across the table as his gaze locked onto Alex.</p>

<p>"Alex, a woman of her standing doesn’t take a step like that lightly. The circles she walks in... people will talk, and families like hers don’t take kindly to things they can’t control. Are you prepared for what follows a decision like this?"</p>

<p>"I am, Dad," Alex replied without a hint of hesitation.</p>

<p>David listened closely, his sharp eyes searching Alex’s face for any sign of recklessness. He found none... only cold, absolute confidence.</p>

<p>"I know what you’re thinking, Dad," Alex said, his voice calm, steady, and earnest. "You’re worried that bringing someone with Victoria’s background into our lives is inviting a storm we don’t need. But you know me. I would never take a reckless step, especially when it comes to the safety of this family. I’ve thought this through from every angle and prepared for every possible outcome. There won’t be any fallout, no sudden threats, and nothing that I haven’t already accounted for. You have my word on that."</p>

<p>A profound silence filled the kitchen.</p>

<p>David looked at Alex for a long moment. He didn’t know the exact extent of Alex’s mysterious power, nor did he understand the shadowy battles being fought beyond the estate gates. But he knew the boy in front of him. He saw the steady, unbreakable resolve in Alex’s eyes, and that was more than enough.</p>

<p>Slowly, David stood up from the table. Reaching out, he placed a heavy, calloused hand on Alex’s shoulder, giving it a firm, proud squeeze.</p>

<p>"You’ve never lied to us, Alex," David said softly, his voice thick with quiet emotion. "We don’t need to know every little detail about your business or your world. As long as you stay safe, and as long as you know that this house will always be your home, Linda and I trust you completely."</p>
