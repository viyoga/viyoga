<p>"Where exactly is the evidence, Mr. Pierce?"</p>

<p>Miranda stood from her seat with the fluid grace of someone who had spent years commanding courtrooms and academic panels.</p>

<p>Her movement was so deliberate, so confident, that the entire chamber seemed to shift its attention to her like iron filings drawn to a magnet.</p>

<p>Pierce blinked, clearly caught off-guard by the directness of her challenge.</p>

<p>"Evidence? Dr. Whitman, we’ve presented... "</p>

<p>"Have you?" Miranda’s interruption was seamless, professional, devastating.</p>

<p>"Because from where I sit, you’ve wasted considerable time on emotional speeches. Attempted to manipulate them into confessing to crimes they maintain they never committed. You even threatened them."</p>

<p>She moved from behind the panel table, positioning herself where she could address both Pierce and the other panel members.</p>

<p>"But actual evidence? Concrete proof of these alleged assaults? I’m still waiting."</p>

<p>Pierce’s face flushed slightly, but he rallied with the practiced authority of decades in administration.</p>

<p>"Are you suggesting all of this is fabricated, Dr. Whitman?"</p>

<p>His voice rose with apparent incredulity, gesturing toward William and Brad.</p>

<p>"Are you claiming these injuries are somehow staged? That these young men inflicted such trauma upon themselves?"</p>

<p>His tone carried wounded disbelief, as if Miranda’s questions were an assault on the victims rather than legitimate procedural inquiry.</p>

<p>"Oh, no... no." Miranda’s voice softened immediately, carrying genuine concern.</p>

<p>"I’m not denying their injuries exist. Quite the contrary... I’m deeply sympathetic to what they’ve endured."</p>

<p>She turned toward William and Brad, her expression showing authentic care.</p>

<p>"These young men have clearly suffered significant trauma. The medical evidence is undeniable on that point."</p>

<p>Her voice carried the professional sympathy of someone who had seen real victims and understood their pain.</p>

<p>"My concern is not with the reality of their injuries, Mr. Pierce. My concern is with the complete absence of evidence connecting these injuries to the students you’ve accused."</p>

<p>Miranda’s tone became more pointed, more focused.</p>

<p>She gestured toward William and Brad.</p>

<p>"Mr. Thompson, you testified that your attackers broke car windows during this assault, correct?"</p>

<p>"Y...Yes," William replied weakly, his voice barely audible.</p>

<p>"And this occurred in a campus parking lot with multiple vehicles and clear lines of sight?"</p>

<p>"Yes."</p>

<p>Miranda’s voice grew stronger, more confident.</p>

<p>"Well then, we are fortunate, aren’t we? That parking lot is equipped with CCTV surveillance."</p>

<p>"Which means the assault, if it happened as described, would be on record... every moment of it. Footage that could identify the perpetrators in minutes and allow us to deliver a fair judgment."</p>

<p>Her eyes swept the room, then returned to Pierce with a blade-like precision.</p>

<p>"So why, Mr. Pierce... after your passionate speeches and repeated appeals to emotion, have we not seen a single frame of this supposed evidence?"</p>

<p>The chamber grew still. Panelists straightened in their seats, their gazes locking on Pierce.</p>

<p>Even the audience leaned in, attentive. William and Brad shifted uneasily, while every eye now waited for his answer.</p>

<p>Pierce’s jaw tightened, but his voice remained controlled, carrying just the right note of administrative frustration.</p>

<p>"Dr. Whitman, as I’ve already informed this panel, the campus surveillance system has been non-operational for the past three days due to equipment malfunction."</p>

<p>He shrugged slightly, as if this were merely an unfortunate coincidence beyond his control.</p>

<p>"Our facilities management team has been working around the clock to restore functionality, but these systems are complex. The timing is regrettable, but hardly unprecedented."</p>

<p>"Oh my, " Miranda’s voice carried a note of mock surprise that barely concealed her skepticism.</p>

<p>"How remarkably convenient. The entire surveillance system fails just one day before this alleged assault occurs."</p>

<p>Pierce’s voice sharpened with defensive authority.</p>

<p>"What exactly are you implying, Dr. Whitman?"</p>

<p>Miranda’s response was light, almost playful, but with steel underneath.</p>

<p>"Oh, I’m not implying anything at all, Mr. Pierce. Merely observing the rather fortuitous timing of this technical failure."</p>

<p>She paused, allowing the implications to settle before continuing with renewed focus.</p>

<p>Before she could speak again, Alaric Langford, Vice President for Administrative Affairs, cleared his throat. He leaned slightly forward, his expression calm but deliberate.</p>

<p>"For the record," Langford said, his voice measured, "as the administrator responsible for facilities oversight, I can confirm that I reviewed the systems report myself. The surveillance grid did experience downtime due to a hardware fault. Our technicians verified it, and the repair logs are on file."</p>

<p>He folded his hands neatly on the table, his tone seeking to close the matter rather than fan suspicion.</p>

<p>"The failure was unfortunate, yes, but not fabricated. I take full responsibility for the oversight, and I assure this panel it was a matter of technical malfunction, nothing more."</p>

<p>A murmur rippled through the room... his statement offered reassurance on the surface, but the weight of his personal responsibility only deepened the tension.</p>

<p>"Very well," she said evenly. "Let us set aside the surveillance issue for the moment and turn our attention to other aspects of this incident."</p>

<p>Miranda turned her attention back to the heart of the matter.</p>

<p>"Mr. Thompson, Mr. Martinez, this assault allegedly occurred in a busy campus parking area during morning hours when students and faculty arrive for classes."</p>

<p>Her voice carried genuine curiosity rather than accusation.</p>

<p>"Surely someone else witnessed this extended altercation? Ten minutes of violence with weapons and property damage... that would certainly attract attention."</p>

<p>The silence stretched uncomfortably. William and Brad exchanged uncertain glances, but neither offered a response.</p>

<p>Miranda waited, allowing the silence to emphasize the absence of corroborating witnesses.</p>

<p>"No one saw anything? No one heard the commotion? No one reported suspicious activity."</p>

<p>Again, silence.</p>

<p>"How remarkably isolating this assault must have been, despite occurring in such a public location."</p>

<p>Miranda moved to her table and retrieved a folder of photographs.</p>

<p>"Mr. Pierce, you’ve submitted photographs of property damage... specifically, the vehicles belonging to William and Brad, with their windows broken."</p>

<p>She held up the photos for the panel to see.</p>

<p>"But I’m curious about something. In these images of the alleged assault scene, I don’t observe any glass fragments on the ground."</p>

<p>Pierce responded quickly, his tone carrying reasonable explanation.</p>

<p>"Obviously, the area was cleaned immediately to prevent injury to other students. Broken glass poses a significant safety hazard."</p>

<p>Miranda nodded thoughtfully.</p>

<p>"Absolutely logical. Safety must always be our priority."</p>

<p>She paused, consulting her notes.</p>

<p>"But surely photographs were taken of the scene before this cleanup occurred? Documentation of evidence before its removal would be standard procedure for such a serious incident."</p>

<p>Pierce’s response came too quickly, suggesting he’d anticipated this line of questioning.</p>

<p>"The immediate priority was student safety and medical care for the victims. Documentation was secondary to human welfare."</p>

<p>Miranda’s smile was gentle but pointed.</p>

<p>"Of course. Though I notice you’re answering questions I directed to the victims themselves."</p>

<p>She turned back to William and Brad.</p>

<p>"Gentlemen, were you present when these scene photographs were taken? Did security or administration document the area before cleanup?"</p>

<p>William shifted uncomfortably in his chair.</p>

<p>"I... we were being treated for our injuries. I don’t know about photographs."</p>

<p>"Naturally," Miranda replied with understanding sympathy.</p>

<p>"Medical care would certainly take priority over documentation."</p>

<p>She looked back at Pierce.</p>

<p>"So we have no surveillance footage due to equipment failure. No witness testimony despite a public location. No scene documentation before evidence removal."</p>

<p>Her voice remained professional, but the implications were devastating.</p>

<p>"This presents certain... challenges in establishing the facts of this case."</p>

<p>The other panel members began shifting uncomfortably in their seats.</p>

<p>Dr. Brennan frowned as he reviewed his notes. Dr. Carlisle exchanged a meaningful glance with Mr. Langford.</p>

<p>The systematic absence of evidence was becoming impossible to ignore.</p>

<p>Pierce’s confidence was visibly wavering, but he maintained his authoritative demeanor.</p>

<p>"Dr. Whitman, these procedural questions don’t change the fundamental reality. We have victims with severe injuries and clear testimony about their attackers."</p>

<p>"Ah yes," Miranda said, her voice carrying renewed focus as she turned her attention to the cafeteria witness.</p>

<p>"That testimony does indeed deserve the most careful examination possible."</p>

<p>Her slight smile suggested she was just getting started with her systematic demolition of Pierce’s case.</p>
