<p>The kiss lasted only seconds, but time seemed suspended in Nina’s quiet hospital room.</p>

<p>When they finally broke apart, both Alex and Linda stood frozen, breathing heavily, crushed under the weight of what had just happened.</p>

<p>Alex’s eyes were wide with shock, his body rigid, his mind struggling to process it. What just happened?</p>

<p>"Mom, what... we just..." His words fractured in his throat, unable to name what had transpired between them.</p>

<p>Linda’s hand flew to her mouth, horror spreading across her features. The impulse had been sudden, overwhelming... she’d acted without thought.</p>

<p>And now, seeing his panicked expression, reality hit her like a thunderbolt.</p>

<p>"Oh God," she whispered, stepping back as if distance could erase the moment. "Alex, I... I didn’t mean... that was..."</p>

<p>What have I done? Why couldn’t I wait for the right moment? Will he hate me? Look at me with disgust? Have I ruined everything?</p>

<p>The thoughts spiraled, sharp and cutting, threatening to undo her.</p>

<p>But even as panic consumed her, she noticed something crucial.</p>

<p>She’d expected anger... outrage at a boundary crossed. She’d braced for disgust. She’d feared rejection.</p>

<p>But that wasn’t what she saw.</p>

<p>He wasn’t angry. He wasn’t disgusted.</p>

<p>He was panicked, yes... chest rising and falling too quickly, hands flexing nervously at his sides... but it wasn’t fear or shock. It was the panic of someone who’d done something forbidden.</p>

<p>Even in this moment, she found him unbearably... captivating. So fragile yet so alive. So handsome. So achingly human.</p>

<p>A dangerous thrill raced through her, heat spreading she could hardly control. This wasn’t the time. Not now. Not while the shock was still written across his face.</p>

<p>"I’m so sorry," Linda said quickly, her voice trembling despite her attempt at control. "I don’t know what came over me. I was just so... emotional seeing you again, and I..." She pressed her hands to her burning cheeks, trying to contain the fire inside her. "Please don’t be angry with me."</p>

<p>Alex ran a hand through his hair, rough and agitated, still stunned as if struck by lightning, trying to remember how to stand.</p>

<p>"I’m not... Mom, I’m not angry," he said slowly, carefully. "I’m just..." His jaw worked, searching for words. "That was..."</p>

<p>"Completely inappropriate," Linda finished for him, though her heart hammered at the absence of anger in his eyes. He’s not rejecting me. He’s not pushing me away.</p>

<p>"You must think I’ve lost my mind," she added, voice small, vulnerable.</p>

<p>"No," Alex said quickly, then caught himself. "I mean... you’ve been under so much stress with Nina. Emotions are running high... after everything that’s happened..."</p>

<p>He was giving her an out, a reason to retreat. The stress, the fear, the emotional exhaustion... it all made sense. But they both knew it was more than that.</p>

<p>They stood in awkward silence, eyes darting away, air thick with unspoken words.</p>

<p>"Where... where is everyone?" Alex asked suddenly, scanning the room as if noticing they were alone for the first time. "Danny? Mike?"</p>

<p>"They went to get food," Linda said, voice steadier now. "They thought you were taking longer than expected, so they ate while waiting."</p>

<p>Alone.</p>

<p>The word hit him like a physical blow. His eyes flicked to the door, then back to her, panic returning.</p>

<p>"I should... I should go find them," he said abruptly, moving toward the door. "Make sure they’re not worried."</p>

<p>"Alex, wait..." Linda started, but he was already halfway there.</p>

<p>"I’ll be right back," he said, not meeting her eyes. And then he was gone, the door clicking shut behind him.</p>

<p>Left alone, sank into her chair, a slow smile spreading across her face. He ran. He actually ran.</p>

<p>The panic in his eyes, the careful distance he’d maintained... it spoke of a man wrestling with something he couldn’t control. Not disgust. Not anger. Just the impossible pull between them.</p>

<p>It wasn’t as bad as she’d imagined. In fact... it was enlightening.</p>

<p>She touched her lips, still warm from his kiss, and felt that dangerous thrill again. Brief, impulsive, but telling. He wasn’t immune. Whatever had been building between them... it wasn’t one-sided.</p>

<p>This changes everything.</p>

<p>***</p>

<p>Alex practically fled down the hospital corridor, his heart pounding as he tried to escape the suffocating intimacy of Nina’s room.</p>

<p>His heart hammered against his ribs, each beat a reminder of the impossible thing that had just happened.</p>

<p>What the hell just happened?</p>

<p>He found himself in a forgotten alcove near the service elevators, the kind of space hospitals kept for staff breaks or quiet phone calls. Empty now. Quiet.</p>

<p>He pressed his back against the cold wall and tried to breathe.</p>

<p>His hands were shaking.</p>

<p>The kiss replayed in his mind with vivid clarity. The softness of Linda’s lips, the way she’d melted against him, the desperate hunger in the way she’d pressed closer...</p>

<p>It wasn’t an accident, he realized with crystalline certainty. At first, maybe, but then... then she was really kissing me. She wanted it.</p>

<p>The knowledge sent a complex mix of emotions through him... guilt, panic, and something darker that he didn’t want to acknowledge.</p>

<p>"No." The word came out harsh, scraping his throat raw. He pressed the heels of his palms against his eyes. "No, no, no."</p>

<p>This couldn’t be happening. Linda was family. She’d been kind to him when no one else was. She’d opened her home, treated him like a son.</p>

<p>And now...</p>

<p>Why? The question burned through his mind. What changed? She’s been like a mother to me for years. Why now? Why suddenly...</p>

<p>His mind raced backward through recent weeks, searching for the moment everything went wrong. Nina’s hospitalization. Linda’s gratitude.</p>

<p>The way she’d started looking at him differently, touches lingering just a fraction too long, her gaze holding his with an intensity that made something in his chest tighten.</p>

<p>He’d dismissed it. Stress, he’d told himself. Emotional exhaustion. A mother clinging to the person who’d saved her child.</p>

<p>But that kiss...</p>

<p>That kiss had been anything but grateful.</p>

<p>His thoughts were interrupted by a familiar, silky voice that seemed to whisper directly into his consciousness.</p>

<p>"Struggling with something, darling?"</p>

<p>The voice slithered into the alcove like smoke under a door... velvet-soft, amused, intimate in a way that made his skin crawl.</p>

<p>Alex’s blood turned to ice.</p>

<p>"Lilith." Her name left his lips as an accusation.</p>

<p>She didn’t manifest visually. She never did unless she wanted to. But her presence filled the empty space around him, a weight pressing against his consciousness, warm and suffocating.</p>

<p>"Its you...You did this." His voice shook with fury and something darker... fear. "You made... "</p>

<p>"Oh, Darling." Lilith’s laughter was gentle, almost maternal. "I didn’t make her do anything. That would be crude. Inelegant."</p>

<p>"Then what the hell just happened in there?"</p>

<p>"What happened... ," Lilith said slowly, savoring each word, "...is that Linda Morrison acted on feelings she’s been suppressing for days. Feelings you’ve been too blind... or too unwilling to see."</p>

<p>"That’s not... she wouldn’t..."</p>

<p>"Wouldn’t she?" The question hung in the air, soft and poisonous.</p>

<p>"Tell me, Alex. When did you start becoming someone different to her? When did the boy she once knew transform into something... more?"</p>

<p>Images flickered through his mind unbidden. Linda’s face when he’d assured her Nina would survive. The way she’d clung to him in the hospital hallway, trembling. The grateful tears, the whispered thank-yous, the looks that had started carrying weight he’d refused to acknowledge.</p>

<p>"After your enhancement," Lilith continued, her voice a gentle current pulling him deeper.</p>

<p>"After you became stronger. More capable. More... magnificent. You saved what mattered most to her, Alex. You became her hero. Is it really so surprising that gratitude might blossom into something else?"</p>

<p>"She’s married," Alex said desperately. "She has a family. She’s... "</p>

<p>"Unhappy." The word dropped like a stone into still water. "Oh, she loves her family. But love and desire aren’t always the same thing, are they? David is safe. Predictable. Comfortable. But you..." Her voice curled around him.</p>

<p>"You’re extraordinary. And she feels it every time you walk into a room."</p>

<p>Alex’s jaw clenched. "You’re twisting this. Making something wrong sound... "</p>

<p>"Natural?" Lilith suggested. "Because it is, darling. A woman attracted to a powerful man who’s shown her kindness. There’s nothing twisted about that. It’s one of the oldest stories in the world."</p>

<p>"She’s like a mother to me!"</p>

<p>The words tore out of him, raw and desperate. The truth he kept circling back to, the foundation of everything he believed about this relationship.</p>

<p>Silence stretched between them.</p>

<p>When Lilith spoke again, her voice had shifted... softer now, almost compassionate.</p>

<p>"Alex... she was never your mother. She was your friend’s mother who showed you kindness. There’s a difference." A pause.</p>

<p>"That’s not... this isn’t..." Alex’s thoughts fragmented, unable to find solid ground. "Why are you doing this? Why her?"</p>

<p>The system interface blazed to life before his eyes without warning, translucent text hanging in the air with terrible finality:</p>

<p>[SYSTEM MISSION]</p>

<p>[SEDUCE LINDA MORRISON AND MAKE HER YOUR WOMAN]</p>

<p>REWARDS:</p>
