<p>Lilith.</p>

<p>Alex’s mental call was quiet, almost casual, but he felt her presence stir immediately.</p>

<p>The connection between them thrummed with awareness, and within moments, her voice slithered into his thoughts... smooth, amused, carrying that eternal hint of condescension.</p>

<p>"Yes, darling?"</p>

<p>He kept his expression neutral, his gaze still locked on Catherine’s face as she processed his offer. Internally, he focused his thoughts with deliberate precision.</p>

<p>"What medicine should I purchase for her? The most suitable option for her specific situation."</p>

<p>There was a pause... Lilith seemed to be considering, or perhaps savoring the moment. When she responded, her voice carried a note of satisfaction.</p>

<p>"Oh, how delightful. You’re finally thinking strategically."</p>

<p>Alex resisted the urge to roll his eyes. ’Just answer the question.’</p>

<p>"The Elixir of Absolute Restoration," Lilith purred, her tone turning almost instructive. "The same one you gave to your dear Viktor."</p>

<p>Alex’s brow furrowed slightly. "Why that specifically? I thought it was primarily for healing."</p>

<p>"Darling, use that pretty head of yours," Lilith chided, though her voice held genuine instruction beneath the mockery. "Catherine Blackwood has been cultivating for years using House Blackwood’s resources. Impressive by mortal standards, certainly... but still fundamentally impure."</p>

<p>Alex listened, his focus sharpening.</p>

<p>"She has accumulated countless impurities over the years... microscopic flaws that build up through any conventional cultivation method. Even the Seven Great Families, with all their wealth and expertise, cannot create truly pure advancement resources."</p>

<p>Lilith’s voice took on a more serious edge.</p>

<p>"And those impurities? They become shackles at higher realms. What seems insignificant now will bottle up her potential later, creating invisible ceilings she’ll never break through.</p>

<p>Lilith’s voice dropped lower, almost intimate.</p>

<p>"The Elixir will purge all of that. Every toxin, every dormant impurity, every cellular flaw accumulated through years of imperfect cultivation. It will strip away the compromises her body has made and rebuild her from the foundation up. She won’t just be healed... she’ll be reborn."</p>

<p>Alex’s pulse quickened slightly. That made perfect sense. Catherine’s foundation was already strong by mortal standards, but if it carried years of accumulated microscopic flaws...</p>

<p>He nodded slowly, the logic undeniable. "Alright. That makes sense. I’ll purchase it."</p>

<p>But then a thought occurred to him, and he paused.</p>

<p>"Wait. How exactly does the Harem Support System work?"</p>

<p>There was a brief silence, and Alex could almost sense Lilith’s satisfaction at the question.</p>

<p>"Oh, good. You’re learning to ask the right questions before making mistakes," she purred, approval evident beneath the condescension.</p>

<p>"Just explain it," Alex thought impatiently.</p>

<p>"Very well, darling. Listen carefully," Lilith said, her voice taking on that instructive edge again. "When you purchase items through the Harem Support System, you simply specify which harem member it’s for. The system handles the rest automatically."</p>

<p>"That’s it?"</p>

<p>"That’s it," Lilith confirmed. "Just state the item you want and who it’s intended for. The moment the purchase is confirmed, the resource becomes attuned specifically to that person. It will work perfectly for them and only them."</p>

<p>Alex’s mind worked through the implications. "And if someone else tries to use it?"</p>

<p>"Completely inert," Lilith said flatly. "No effects whatsoever. If you tried to use it yourself, or gave it to someone outside your harem, the medicine would be nothing more than expensive colored water. Useless."</p>

<p>A warning edge entered her voice.</p>

<p>"So choose carefully, darling. Once attuned, the resource is locked to that specific person. No transfers. No do-overs. If you waste a 10,000 CP elixir on the wrong person, that’s your mistake to live with."</p>

<p>Alex absorbed this, understanding both the protection and the limitation. The system prevented theft or misuse, but it also meant he couldn’t stockpile resources for himself or distribute them freely.</p>

<p>Alex took a mental breath, then made his decision.</p>

<p>"I want to purchase the Elixir of Absolute Restoration for Catherine Blackwood."</p>

<p>"And keep it stored in the system space until I’m ready to retrieve it."</p>

<p>"Confirmed," Lilith purred, satisfaction evident in her tone.</p>

<p>Immediately, Alex’s vision shimmered as the familiar translucent interface materialized before his eyes... invisible to everyone but him. The system window expanded, displaying the item in crystalline detail.</p>

<p>[ELIXIR OF ABSOLUTE RESTORATION]</p>

<p>[Description: A rare alchemical creation, unmatched even by the Seven Great Families. This elixir fully restores the mortal body, healing all visible and hidden injuries, purging toxins, dormant poisons, and cellular damage. Beyond recovery, it upgrades the body to a higher tier of capability, unlocking its full potential and preparing it to handle greater transformations.]</p>

<p>[Base Cost: 10,000 CP]</p>

<p>[Harem Discount (90%): 1,000 CP]</p>

<p>[Attuned to: Catherine Blackwood (Exclusive Use)]</p>

<p>[PURCHASE CONFIRMED - STORED IN INVENTORY]</p>

<p>[Warning:] This item can only be used by the designated harem member. All other users will experience zero effects.</p>

<p>Alex’s lips curved into a faint smile, though his expression remained neutral to the outside world.</p>

<p>The entire internal transaction... from question to purchase to storage... had taken perhaps ten seconds of real time.</p>

<p>To Catherine and Victoria, Alex had simply gone quiet for a moment, his expression thoughtful as if considering how to proceed.</p>

<p>Now his focus returned fully to the present, to Catherine’s shocked face and Victoria’s delighted grin.</p>

<p>"Ready to see what I’m actually capable of?" Alex asked softly, his voice carrying easily through the silence</p>

<p>Catherine’s hands had gone numb. Her breath felt shallow in her chest. Every instinct screamed that this was impossible, that she should laugh it off as a joke or a test or temporary madness.</p>

<p>But when she spoke, her voice was steady.</p>

<p>"Show me."</p>

<p>The two words carried weight... challenge, desperation, hope, and fear all compressed into a single demand.</p>

<p>Alex’s smile widened fractionally. Then, with deliberate casualness, he reached beneath the table.</p>

<p>His hand disappeared into what appeared to be his jacket pocket, though the angle was slightly awkward, as if he were retrieving something from an inner pocket or perhaps a bag at his feet that Catherine couldn’t see.</p>

<p>When his hand emerged, it held something that made Catherine’s entire world stop.</p>

<p>The vial seemed to capture light that didn’t exist in the room.</p>

<p>It rested in Alex’s palm, neither glass nor crystal, yet it appeared to hold the luminescence of distant stars.</p>

<p>The container itself was impossibly perfect... no seams, no imperfections, crafted from a material that Catherine’s trained eye couldn’t identify. It looked ancient and futuristic simultaneously, as if it had been pulled from both the distant past and the far future.</p>

<p>But it was the liquid inside that truly defied comprehension.</p>

<p>It shifted colors as she watched, flowing from deep gold to silver to hues that had no names... colors that shouldn’t exist, that her mind struggled to process. The substance pulsed with its own inner radiance, a warm glow that seemed alive, conscious, aware.</p>

<p>Catherine’s breath had stopped entirely.</p>

<p>She stared at the vial with the kind of intensity usually reserved for witnessing miracles or horrors. Her analytical mind tried desperately to catalog what she was seeing, to fit it into some framework of understanding.</p>

<p>It failed completely.</p>

<p>This wasn’t medicine as she understood it. Medicine came in standardized medical vials, clearly labeled, with precise dosage instructions and chemical compositions.</p>

<p>Even the most expensive healing compounds from House Blackwood’s private laboratories looked clinical... sophisticated but ultimately mundane.</p>

<p>This...</p>

<p>This looked like someone had bottled the essence of creation itself.</p>

<p>The light from the vial played across her face, casting shifting colors across her features.</p>

<p>She was so utterly transfixed, so completely lost in the impossible beauty of what Alex held, that she’d entirely forgotten to question how he’d produced it.</p>

<p>Where had it come from?</p>

<p>Had it been in his pocket the entire time?</p>

<p>That seemed impossible... something this extraordinary couldn’t just be casually carried around.</p>

<p>And the angle had been wrong, as if he’d pulled it from somewhere that didn’t quite make physical sense.</p>

<p>But her mind couldn’t focus on those questions.</p>

<p>Couldn’t hold onto the logical inconsistencies.</p>

<p>Because the medicine itself demanded her complete attention, drowning out everything else with its sheer, overwhelming presence.</p>

<p>"What..." Catherine’s voice came out as barely a whisper. She had to clear her throat and try again. "What is that?"</p>

<p>Alex turned the vial slowly in his hand, letting the impossible light dance across the walls. When he spoke, his voice carried the weight of absolute certainty.</p>

<p>"The Elixir of Absolute Restoration."</p>

<p>"It’s a medicine unmatched even by the Seven Great Families. It will completely restore your body... healing every visible and hidden injury, purging all toxins, dormant poisons, and cellular damage accumulated through years of imperfect cultivation."</p>

<p>Catherine’s eyes widened further with each word.</p>

<p>"But beyond simple restoration," Alex continued, his gaze holding hers with intense focus, "it will upgrade your body to a higher tier of capability. It will strip away every microscopic impurity you’ve absorbed through conventional advancement resources."</p>

<p>Her breathing had become shallow as she processed his words.</p>

<p>"And most importantly," Alex said, his voice dropping to carry absolute certainty, "this will push you through to Apex realm tonight... cleanly, safely, with zero risk of foundation damage or cultivation deviation."</p>
