<p>The leather seats of Victoria’s Mercedes-Maybach whispered luxury as they glided through the city’s elite district. Victoria had been talking for twenty minutes straight, her excitement infectious and slightly manic... the behavior of a woman about to debut her most precious possession.</p>

<p>"Alex, you have to understand," Victoria said, her manicured hand resting possessively on his thigh, "this isn’t just a fundraising party. This is the Blackwood family’s annual demonstration of power. We’ve hosted this event for thirty-seven years."</p>

<p>She gestured toward the glittering skyline. "Tonight, you’ll meet senators who decide federal budgets, judges who shape constitutional law, CEOs who control market sectors, and Hollywood royalty who influence culture itself."</p>

<p>Alex listened intently, but part of his attention was on Victoria’s transformation. Gone was any trace of the neglected political wife. In her place sat a woman radiating confidence and predatory satisfaction.</p>

<p>"The guest list reads like a Forbes power ranking," she continued. "Senator Patricia Hamilton... she controls the Armed Services Committee and hasn’t smiled genuinely since her husband died three years ago. Judge Morrison’s wife Diana Morrison... she runs the city’s largest private foundation and hasn’t been properly fucked in a decade."</p>

<p>Victoria’s language had become deliciously crude since their affair began, her CEO persona bleeding into their private moments.</p>

<p>"Then there’s Congresswoman Elena Torres... just divorced, controls the Hispanic vote, and has a reputation for... acquiring young male staffers. Ambassador Sarah Chen handles all Pacific trade negotiations... arranged marriage, completely loveless. Federal Reserve Board member Rachel Goldman’s daughter Jessica... banking dynasty heiress, twenty-six, and bored out of her mind with trust fund boys."</p>

<p>Alex smiled. "You sound like you’re presenting a menu."</p>

<p>Victoria’s laugh was sharp and possessive. "Maybe I am. These women have been circling each other like sharks for years, all of them starving for something real. Something... substantial."</p>

<p>Her hand moved higher on his thigh, her touch electric even through the fabric.</p>

<p>"But tonight, they’re going to see what I have. And Alex..." Her voice dropped to that husky whisper that made his blood sing. "I want them to want you. I want them to see exactly what kind of man I’ve claimed."</p>

<p>The Metropolitan Club: First Impressions</p>

<p>The Blackwood family’s private club rose thirty stories into the night sky, its Art Deco architecture a monument to old money and older power. Valets in white gloves moved like choreographed dancers, handling Bentleys and Ferraris with practiced reverence.</p>

<p>"Jesus," Alex breathed, taking in the marble facade and golden lighting.</p>

<p>"Language," Victoria teased, but her smile was proud. "This is just the lobby, darling."</p>

<p>As they stepped from the Mercedes, Alex felt the familiar whisper of Lilith’s voice:</p>

<p>"Welcome to the feeding ground, beloved. Tonight, you’re not just attending their party... you’re hunting in their sacred space."</p>

<p>The Elite Presence Mastery skill activated automatically, his enhanced charisma radiating like a magnetic field. Alex could feel it working... the way conversations paused, how heads turned with seemingly involuntary interest.</p>

<p>Victoria noticed immediately. "My God, Alex... the way people are looking at you..."</p>

<p>She wasn’t wrong. From the valet who held eye contact too long, to the hostess whose professional smile became something hungrier, to the cluster of younger women near the entrance who simply stopped talking... Alex’s presence was having exactly the effect Lilith had designed.</p>

<p>"I’m not used to this much attention," Alex admitted, his enhanced reflexes making him aware of every gaze, every subtle body language shift around him.</p>

<p>Victoria’s arm slid through his with territorial satisfaction. "You better get used to it. Tonight’s just the beginning."</p>

<p>The elevator to the thirtieth floor felt like ascending to Olympus. When the doors opened, Alex stepped into a world he’d only seen in movies... crystal chandeliers casting prismatic light across marble floors, floor-to-ceiling windows offering panoramic city views, and nearly three hundred of the most powerful people in the state mingling with practiced elegance.</p>

<p>System Alert: [HIGH-VALUE ENVIRONMENT DETECTED]</p>

<p>Elite Social Gathering: 7 Primary Targets Identified</p>

<p>Recommendation: Strategic introduction sequence to maximize impact</p>

<p>But it was the immediate response to Alex’s entrance that made Victoria’s breath catch.</p>

<p>Conversations didn’t just pause... they fractured. Senator Patricia Hamilton, mid-discussion about defense appropriations, lost her train of thought entirely when her gaze fell on Alex. Diana Morrison, explaining foundation budgets to a circle of donors, found herself trailing off as Alex moved through the crowd.</p>

<p>"Victoria, darling!"</p>

<p>James Blackwood approached with the broad smile of a successful politician, arms spread in welcome. He kissed his wife’s cheek with practiced affection, then turned to Alex with genuine warmth.</p>

<p>"And you must be the young entrepreneur Victoria’s been raving about. Alexander Hale, isn’t it?"</p>

<p>"Senator Blackwood, it’s an honor," Alex replied, his Silver Tongue Protocol making his voice resonate with exactly the right mix of respect and confidence.</p>

<p>James’s handshake was firm, his politician’s instincts reading Alex as someone worth cultivating. "Victoria tells me you’re revolutionizing sustainable technology. We need more young visionaries like you in this country."</p>

<p>The irony was delicious... James proudly introducing his wife’s lover to the political elite, completely oblivious to the sexual tension crackling between them.</p>

<p>"Senator, Ma’am has been invaluable in helping me understand how innovation intersects with policy," Alex said smoothly. "Her insights have been... deeply penetrating."</p>

<p>Only Victoria caught the double meaning, her cheeks flushing as she fought back a smile.</p>

<p>Victoria’s eyes glinted with quiet satisfaction as she guided Alex deeper into the sea of power players, their evening unfolding like a chess match with stakes far beyond money, reputation, influence, and secrets waiting to be weaponized.</p>

<p>Tonight was the opening move. And Alex was ready to claim his place.</p>
