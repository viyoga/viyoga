<p>Sophia’s hands tightened on the steering wheel as she turned onto the tree-lined drive leading to Aunt Catherine’s estate.</p>

<p>Finally.</p>

<p>After weeks of being stonewalled by that insufferable bitch Amanda..."Ms. Blackwood is unavailable," "Perhaps try again later," "She’s in important meetings"...her aunt had finally called her directly.</p>

<p>Like she always did when Sophia truly needed her.</p>

<p>Because Aunt Catherine understood.</p>

<p>And unlike that smug, condescending assistant of hers, Aunt Catherine actually took Sophia seriously.</p>

<p>Amanda.</p>

<p>Just thinking about that woman made Sophia’s blood boil.</p>

<p>The way she’d dismissed every call, every message, with that fake-polite voice that barely masked her contempt.</p>

<p>Like Sophia was some annoying telemarketer instead of Catherine Blackwood’s niece.</p>

<p>Well, that was going to end today.</p>

<p>First order of business... making sure Aunt Catherine knew exactly how her precious assistant had been treating family.</p>

<p>How Amanda had deliberately blocked Sophia’s attempts to reach her.</p>

<p>How she’d been dismissive, disrespectful, borderline insubordinate in her refusal to put calls through.</p>

<p>Sophia could already picture it... Aunt Catherine’s face hardening with that CEO coldness when she realized how her assistant had been acting.</p>

<p>"She did what?"</p>

<p>And then the inevitable firing.</p>

<p>Because nobody disrespected a Blackwood.</p>

<p>Not even someone who worked for one.</p>

<p>Sophia smiled at the thought of Amanda packing her desk, that smug superiority finally wiped from her face.</p>

<p>Good riddance.</p>

<p>Aunt Catherine had always understood her.</p>

<p>Unlike her mother.</p>

<p>That manipulative, selfish, disgusting woman who’d stolen...</p>

<p>No.</p>

<p>Sophia forced herself to breathe.</p>

<p>Stay calm.</p>

<p>Stay focused.</p>

<p>She’d rehearsed this a thousand times in her head over the past weeks.</p>

<p>What she’d say.</p>

<p>How she’d explain.</p>

<p>The exact words that would make Aunt Catherine see the truth about what Victoria had done.</p>

<p>What Victoria was doing.</p>

<p>With Alex.</p>

<p>Her Alex.</p>

<p>Sophia’s jaw clenched at the thought of his name.</p>

<p>When had it started?</p>

<p>When had he stopped being the nobody she’d used for a dare and become... this?</p>

<p>This obsession that kept her awake at night.</p>

<p>This constant, gnawing need that she couldn’t shake no matter how hard she tried.</p>

<p>She’d catch herself thinking about him at the worst times.</p>

<p>And the worst part... the absolutely infuriating part... was that she’d had him.</p>

<p>He’d been hers.</p>

<p>Worshiped her. Would have done anything for her.</p>

<p>And she’d thrown him away like trash because that’s what he was supposed to be.</p>

<p>Disposable. Forgettable. Nothing.</p>

<p>But Now...</p>

<p>Sophia had realized something terrifying.</p>

<p>She wanted him back.</p>

<p>No... worse than that.</p>

<p>She needed him back.</p>

<p>Needed to prove she could still have him whenever she wanted.</p>

<p>Because she was Sophia Blackwood.</p>

<p>She could have anyone.</p>

<p>So what if she’d kicked him aside? So what if she’d let Marcus beat him bloody? So what if she’d humiliated him in front of everyone?</p>

<p>She was Sophia Blackwood.</p>

<p>She could snap her fingers and have him back.</p>

<p>That’s how it was supposed to work.</p>

<p>That’s how it had always worked.</p>

<p>Except her mother... her own mother... had somehow gotten involved.</p>

<p>And now...</p>

<p>Now Victoria Blackwood was fucking him.</p>

<p>Her daughter’s ex-boyfriend.</p>

<p>A man decades younger than her.</p>

<p>And she wasn’t even hiding it anymore.</p>

<p>She’d cut off Sophia’s money. Kicked her out of the house. Claimed that James knew, that her father was okay with this sick, twisted affair.</p>

<p>Lies.</p>

<p>All lies.</p>

<p>Her father would never accept this.</p>

<p>Her mother was just manipulating everyone like she always did, playing her corporate games, thinking she could control everything and everyone.</p>

<p>Well, not anymore.</p>

<p>Sophia pulled up to the manor and killed the engine, checking her reflection in the rearview mirror.</p>

<p>Perfect.</p>

<p>Hair styled just right. Makeup flawless but not overdone. The picture of a concerned, hurt daughter seeking her beloved aunt’s wisdom and support.</p>

<p>She’d tell Aunt Catherine everything.</p>

<p>How Victoria had seduced Alex right after the breakup... probably even during the relationship, knowing that bitch.</p>

<p>How she’d thrown Sophia out for objecting to this... this perversion.</p>

<p>How she’d claimed James supported it... an obvious lie meant to isolate Sophia.</p>

<p>How Amanda had been blocking Sophia’s calls, probably on Victoria’s orders, trying to keep her from reaching the one person who could help.</p>

<p>Aunt Catherine would be horrified.</p>

<p>Aunt Catherine would fix this.</p>

<p>And once her mother was properly punished for this betrayal, once she was humiliated and forced to end things with Alex...</p>

<p>Then Sophia could have him.</p>

<p>Just for herself.</p>

<p>The way it should have been from the start.</p>

<p>Sophia grabbed her purse and stepped out of the car, smoothing her designer dress.</p>

<p>She walked up to the front door and rang the bell, a small smile playing at her lips.</p>

<p>’I’m coming for you, Mother.’</p>

<p>’And this time, you won’t be able to charm your way out of it.’</p>

<p>***</p>

<p>The gate opened and Sophia swept through without waiting, ignoring the guard at the door with a dismissive wave.</p>

<p>Aunt Catherine would be in her office at this hour.</p>

<p>She always was.</p>

<p>Sophia walked through the familiar halls, heels clicking against marble, already rehearsing her opening lines.</p>

<p>Then she saw her.</p>

<p>Amanda.</p>

<p>Standing near the staircase, tablet in hand, that same professional expression that made Sophia’s blood boil.</p>

<p>Their eyes met.</p>

<p>Sophia smiled.</p>

<p>Cold.</p>

<p>Knowing.</p>

<p>’Enjoy your last days here, bitch.’</p>

<p>Amanda’s expression didn’t change, but Sophia caught the slight confusion in her eyes before she turned and continued toward the office.</p>

<p>The door was open.</p>

<p>Empty.</p>

<p>Sophia frowned, stepping inside and looking around.</p>

<p>Where was she?</p>

<p>Catherine was always in her office at this time.</p>

<p>Always.</p>

<p>For a moment, Sophia considered going back to ask Amanda.</p>

<p>Then dismissed it immediately.</p>

<p>She wasn’t giving that woman the satisfaction.</p>

<p>Besides, she could find her aunt herself.</p>

<p>If not the office, then...</p>

<p>The bedroom.</p>

<p>Of course.</p>

<p>Maybe Catherine was resting, or changing, or...</p>

<p>Sophia turned and headed for the stairs, climbing toward the second floor where she knew her aunt’s private rooms were.</p>

<p>She’d find her.</p>

<p>Tell her everything.</p>

<p>And finally, finally, everything would be set right.</p>

<p>Sophia reached the top of the stairs, turning down the hallway toward her aunt’s bedroom.</p>

<p>Then she heard it.</p>

<p>A moan.</p>

<p>Distant but unmistakable.</p>

<p>Sophia froze mid-step.</p>

<p>What...?</p>

<p>Another moan. Louder this time. Definitely female.</p>

<p>Her mind raced.</p>

<p>Aunt Catherine?</p>

<p>The thought was shocking, unexpected, but...</p>

<p>So what if it was?</p>

<p>Her aunt was a powerful, beautiful woman. Unmarried. She had needs like anyone else.</p>

<p>Probably a boyfriend. Some wealthy executive or...</p>

<p>Sophia should leave.</p>

<p>Come back later.</p>

<p>This was private, and she’d already intruded enough just by coming upstairs uninvited.</p>

<p>But then another sound.</p>

<p>A different voice.</p>

<p>Lower. Male.</p>

<p>And something else beneath it.</p>

<p>Sophia’s feet moved before her brain could catch up.</p>

<p>Curiosity overpowering common sense.</p>

<p>Just a quick look.</p>

<p>Just to confirm it was what she thought and then she’d leave quietly, wait downstairs, pretend she’d never...</p>

<p>She reached the bedroom door.</p>

<p>Wide open.</p>

<p>Not even closed, let alone locked.</p>

<p>Sophia’s hand touched the doorframe as she leaned forward slightly, just enough to see inside.</p>

<p>And froze completely.</p>

<p>The scene before her was...</p>

<p>Her aunt.</p>

<p>Naked.</p>

<p>Eyes closed, head thrown back, mouth open in pleasure.</p>

<p>Sitting on someone’s face.</p>

<p>And she wasn’t alone.</p>

<p>Another woman... also naked... was beside her.</p>

<p>Riding a man beneath them both.</p>

<p>Hard.</p>

<p>Desperately.</p>

<p>Sophia’s brain stuttered, trying to process.</p>

<p>A threesome.</p>

<p>Her elegant, composed Aunt Catherine was in the middle of a threesome.</p>

<p>The shock was so complete that for a moment Sophia couldn’t move, couldn’t think, couldn’t...</p>

<p>Then the woman riding the man moaned.</p>

<p>Loud.</p>

<p>Desperate.</p>

<p>And turned her head slightly.</p>

<p>Her profile visible in the afternoon light.</p>

<p>Sophia’s blood turned to ice.</p>

<p>That face.</p>

<p>Those features.</p>

<p>No.</p>

<p>No, that wasn’t possible.</p>

<p>That couldn’t be...</p>

<p>"MOM?!"</p>

<p>The word tore from Sophia’s throat before she could stop it.</p>

<p>A scream.</p>

<p>Raw.</p>

<p>Horrified.</p>

<p>Disbelieving.</p>

<p>Everything stopped.</p>

<p>Froze.</p>

<p>Victoria’s eyes snapped open, head whipping toward the doorway.</p>

<p>Catherine’s eyes flew open, body going rigid.</p>

<p>And beneath them both...</p>

<p>Sophia’s gaze dropped.</p>

<p>To the man.</p>

<p>The man her mother was riding.</p>

<p>The man her aunt was sitting on.</p>

<p>She couldn’t see his face from this angle, but she could see his body.</p>

<p>Strong.</p>

<p>Young.</p>

<p>Familiar in a way that made her stomach drop.</p>

<p>No.</p>

<p>Please, no.</p>

<p>Victoria was still staring at her, frozen mid-movement, still straddling him.</p>

<p>Catherine had turned her head, still positioned over his face.</p>

<p>And then the man moved slightly.</p>

<p>Adjusted.</p>

<p>Enough for Sophia to see.</p>

<p>To recognize.</p>

<p>Alex.</p>

<p>Her Alex.</p>

<p>The man she’d thrown away.</p>

<p>The man she’d decided she needed back.</p>

<p>The man she’d come here to reclaim by destroying her mother.</p>

<p>Was underneath both of them.</p>

<p>Fucking her mother.</p>

<p>His face buried between her aunt’s legs.</p>

<p>Both women naked and clearly in the middle of...</p>

<p>Sophia’s hand flew to her mouth.</p>

<p>Her vision swam.</p>

<p>This wasn’t happening.</p>

<p>This couldn’t be happening.</p>

<p>"Sophia... " Victoria started, voice wrecked and breathless.</p>

<p>But Sophia couldn’t hear anything over the roaring in her ears.</p>

<p>Couldn’t process anything beyond the image burned into her brain.</p>

<p>Her mother riding Alex.</p>

<p>Her beloved Aunt Catherine with him too.</p>

<p>Both of them.</p>

<p>At the same time.</p>

<p>The aunt she’d come to for help.</p>

<p>The aunt who was supposed to save her from her mother’s perversion.</p>

<p>The aunt who was supposed to fix everything.</p>

<p>Was fucking the same man.</p>

<p>Together with her mother.</p>

<p>Sophia’s world shattered.</p>
