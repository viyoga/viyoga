<p>Luis let out a whoop, arms in the air. "We actually did it! We beat Apex XI!"</p>

<p>Danny stumbled toward Alex, grinning, face streaked with dirt and sweat. "You... you made it look too easy, man!"</p>

<p>Alex allowed himself a faint smile, knees bending as he clutched the ball. His voice was calm but carried through the chaos like a command:</p>

<p>"Look at yourselves. This... this is what happens when you trust each other, when you refuse to break. This is ours. Every second, every pass, every tackle... it all mattered."</p>

<p>The team surged forward, pulling Alex into a group hug. Laughter, cheers, and shouted congratulations collided with the fading boos from the stands. For once, the roar of admiration drowned out everything else.</p>

<p>Phones flashed, fans high-fived strangers, and the stadium buzzed with disbelief and joy.</p>

<p>Alex’s team had not only survived... they had triumphed in style, against the odds, and against the arrogance of Apex XI.</p>

<p>As they raised their fists in unison, Alex let himself exhale fully... not in relief, but in satisfaction.</p>

<p>They had done it. They had earned this victory.</p>

<p>Only he himself knew how close he had been to snapping... to letting loose the power simmering inside him and ending it all in one brutal sweep.</p>

<p>But this... this felt different. Harder, yes. And yet infinitely more satisfying. It was earned, not stolen by impulse.</p>

<p>A low purr stirred in his mind.</p>

<p>"Well, well... congratulations," Lilith’s voice slithered through, velvet and mocking. "For not acting on your impulse. You’ve passed the first evaluation of restraint."</p>

<p>Alex frowned. "First? There’s more?"</p>

<p>Her laugh was a soft ripple, half amusement, half warning. "Who knows?"</p>

<p>But behind that playful lilt, her thoughts were far from careless. She remembered others... bright, burning candidates who had seemed promising at first, only to crumble.</p>

<p>Men who mistook raw power for mastery, who gave in at the first taste of temptation.</p>

<p>They had lost everything.</p>

<p>Her smile thinned in the dark of her mind. "This one... his real test is yet to come. Only then will I know if he is truly capable... or just another failure."</p>

<p>Meanwhile, unaware of her unspoken judgment, Alex opened the system interface to check his task completion.</p>

<p>[ MINI QUEST — DOMINION SYSTEM ]</p>

<p>Objective 1: Dominate and Win the match — Completed.</p>

<p>Reward: +2,000 CP</p>

<p>Objective 2: Exercise restraint even if provoked — Completed.</p>

<p>Reward: +3,000 CP</p>

<p>Total Point Gained: +5,000 CP</p>

<p>***</p>

<p>Marcus slammed his palm against his thigh, jaw tight, sweat dripping down his temple. The heat of the stands... boos, chants, and flashing cameras... made it feel hotter.</p>

<p>"How... how did they pull that off?!" one teammate hissed, voice tight with disbelief. "We set it up perfectly! Every signal, every trap... and they still..."</p>

<p>"They still won?" another muttered, shaking his head. The crowd’s anger crashed over them, sharp and relentless. "We did everything to make Hale act rash... to make them crumble..."</p>

<p>Marcus’s fists clenched. He had orchestrated the entire betrayal...William and Brad had followed his plan to the letter... but somehow Alex had turned it all against them. Their trap had backfired.</p>

<p>The audience erupted, shouting "Traitors! Traitors!" directly at William and Brad.</p>

<p>Camera flashes caught every twitch, every grimace, every flicker of regret. Marcus shot them a glare, teeth clenched.</p>

<p>"I swear..." Marcus growled. "They won’t just get away with this. Not today. Not ever."</p>

<p>William and Brad exchanged panicked glances, disbelief and guilt written across their faces.</p>

<p>They had thought they could manipulate the game... and Marcus... but Alex’s calm precision had undone everything.</p>

<p>Their meticulously planned ascent... along with the connection they had hoped to forge with the titan... had slipped away.</p>

<p>***</p>

<p>From the sidelines, Mike jogged over, concern etched across his face. "Hey, everyone alright?" he asked, crouching beside Danny, brushing a stray strand of hair from his sweaty forehead.</p>

<p>Sarah followed, clipboard still tucked under her arm, her eyes sharp with worry yet softened by affection. She crouched beside Danny, tilting her head as her voice slipped into that familiar mix of care and firmness.</p>

<p>"Danny... let me see your ankle," she said, her voice gentle yet firm... the kind of tone only someone who cared too much could have.</p>

<p>Danny hesitated for a fraction, then lifted his foot. Sarah’s fingers traced gently over the joint, inspecting.</p>

<p>"Hmm... looks fine. But... keep it iced for a while. No heroics." Her eyes flicked up to meet his, and though her words were professional, the faintest softness tugged at the corner of her lips, a hint of care that didn’t need saying.</p>

<p>Mike checked in on the others quickly, running hands along shoulders, backs, and knees, making sure bruises and strains were minimal.</p>

<p>"You guys pushed hard... impressive," he said, giving them encouraging nods.</p>

<p>Then Tisha stepped in... clipboard tucked to her side, expression composed. She glanced over the group, then fixed on Alex.</p>

<p>"Mr. Hale," she said in that steady, faculty voice, "well executed. How did you keep your unit composed under that pressure? Briefly."</p>

<p>Alex, still catching his breath, allowed a small grin. "Focus, trust, and reading their patterns. We didn’t let anger set the tempo."</p>

<p>Tisha nodded once, approval contained. "Good. Debrief tomorrow. For now... hydrate, stretch, ice."</p>

<p>She swept her eyes across the group, making sure each face met hers, no one pretending to be tougher than they were. "Don’t wait until the pain sets in. I want you moving smart, not limping by nextday."</p>

<p>Her tone left no room for argument... it wasn’t just suggestion, it was instruction.</p>

<p>Her gaze lingered on Alex a beat longer than necessary... something unspoken there... before she turned, professor-mask firmly in place.</p>

<p>Danny, Sarah, and Mike traded quick glances... the kind of knowing smiles that weren’t meant for anyone else.</p>

<p>They’d seen enough between Alex and Tisha to recognize the undercurrent beneath her professional tone.</p>

<p>Luis and the others, meanwhile, just took it as a rare moment of praise from a faculty member, nodding respectfully.</p>

<p>"Wow... that was something else," Luis said, brushing sweat from his brow with a grin. "Honestly, I didn’t expect it to go that way. That was... intense, but fun."</p>

<p>Danny laughed, slinging an arm around Luis’s shoulder. "Yeah, not gonna lie... I’d do it again. Maybe next time, we’ll even give them a harder challenge."</p>

<p>With nods and chuckles, the group gradually made their way toward the tunnel, exchanging light-hearted jabs and laughter, the tension of the match slowly dissolving into camaraderie and the shared thrill of a hard-fought game.</p>

<p>***</p>

<p>From his elevated seat near the VIP section, President Pierce watched the chaos unfold with a sharp, calculating gaze.</p>

<p>He had been waiting for Alex to snap, to show a flash of raw power or to make a reckless move... anything that would give him a clue as to why someone like Blackwood had taken an interest in the boy.</p>

<p>He leaned forward slightly, hands clasped on the railing, eyes narrowing as he scanned the field.</p>

<p>Marcus had executed every part of his trap with precision... signals, feints, psychological manipulation, and Pierce allowed himself a faint nod of approval. The strategy was brilliant... professional.</p>

<p>But Alex? The boy had not given him a single hint. Not in anger, not in frustration, not in a subtle twitch of impatience.</p>

<p>Every movement was deliberate, every decision measured. His composure never wavered.</p>

<p>His lips curved into a thin, almost imperceptible smile. Interesting. Here was a student who could hold himself under pressure, who could resist the very impulses that toppled others.</p>

<p>He exhaled slowly, letting the tension in his shoulders ease. "Huh," he murmured under his breath. "Not a single clue... yet."</p>

<p>***</p>

<p>The team started filing back toward the tunnel, voices still buzzing with leftover adrenaline.</p>

<p>Alex fell into step with them, shoulders finally loosening... until movement in the crowd caught his eye.</p>

<p>Madison.</p>

<p>She was cutting across the sideline, slipping through clusters of students as if they weren’t even there.</p>

<p>Instinctively, Alex’s gaze darted around... William and Brad were nowhere in sight. Of course they weren’t.</p>

<p>He exhaled through his nose. Knew this would happen... the timing’s perfect, though. ’I’ll give them that. They played their part like pros.’</p>

<p>Madison reached him without a word, slid a folded slip of paper into his hand, and moved on so smoothly that to everyone else it looked like she’d just brushed past him.</p>

<p>Alex slowed his steps, thumb pressing open the crease. One line in neat handwriting stared back at him:</p>

<p>"Last section of the library. Fifteen minutes."</p>

<p>"She knows how to pick the timing," Alex muttered under his breath, voice low enough to drown under the tunnel noise. The words tasted equal parts thrill and danger.</p>
