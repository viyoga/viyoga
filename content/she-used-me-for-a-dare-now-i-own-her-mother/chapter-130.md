<p>"Alex... I want you as a man."</p>

<p>He froze, eyes widening, voice catching in his throat. "But... you’re my mom... I mean... I... we... this—it’s... impossible."</p>

<p>He fumbled for words, trying to reason with her, to make sense of the impossible emotions crashing between them.</p>

<p>Linda held his gaze, heart pounding, a hint of defiance in her voice. "Am I... really?"</p>

<p>Her hand still resting over his racing heart. "You’re not my son, Alex. You’re not blood. You’re a man... a powerful, kind, devastatingly attractive man... who became part of our family because we chose each other."</p>

<p>The distinction hit Alex like lightning. Not son. Not family by blood. A man.</p>

<p>"But... we can’t... we shouldn’t..." he murmured, his voice tight, as if the words themselves were a struggle against everything he’d been taught to feel.</p>

<p>Linda held his gaze, unwavering. "I tried to fight it," she whispered, leaning closer until he could smell her perfume, feel the warmth radiating from her skin.</p>

<p>"But I can’t fight myself anymore. When I’m near you, I don’t feel like a mother guarding her son. I feel like a woman who’s alive again... wanting, needing, aching... for you."</p>

<p>Alex’s eyes were wide, his breathing ragged.</p>

<p>"And I think," Linda whispered, leaning closer until their faces were only inches apart, her breath warm against his skin, "you feel it too, don’t you? This pull between us... something that isn’t just family or gratitude... it’s something neither of us can explain."</p>

<p>Her gaze flicked down to his lips, lingering there, trembling with desire she could no longer hide.</p>

<p>The space between them thinned until even air seemed to hesitate, charged with something dangerous and undeniable.</p>

<p>Her hand rose to his face, fingertips tracing the sharp line of his jaw, the warmth of his skin calling to her in ways words couldn’t explain.</p>

<p>She leaned closer... breath meeting breath... the faintest brush of her lips almost touching his.</p>

<p>And just then, Alex’s hands came up, catching her wrists gently but firmly, halting the moment on the edge of collapse.</p>

<p>"Mom... wait," he whispered, the word slipping out before he could stop it... half habit, half desperate plea. "Please... I just... I need time."</p>

<p>Linda froze, her heart hammering against her ribs. For a moment, hurt flashed across her features... the sting of rejection, the fear that she’d misread everything.</p>

<p>"Time?" she asked, her voice small and vulnerable.</p>

<p>Alex’s hands were still touching hers, his eyes pleading. "I need to understand this. To figure out what it means. What we’re risking. What we’re doing to everyone we care about."</p>

<p>He looked down at their intertwined fingers, his voice breaking slightly.</p>

<p>"This isn’t just about us. There’s Danny, Nina, David... if we cross this line, there’s no going back. And I need to be sure... absolutely sure... that we both understand what we’re choosing."</p>

<p>Linda studied his face, seeing the war raging behind his eyes.</p>

<p>He wasn’t rejecting her... he was protecting them both from a decision made in desperation and desire.</p>

<p>"You’re right," she said finally, though every fiber of her being screamed against pulling away. "This is too important to rush into."</p>

<p>"But think about it carefully, Alex," Linda said, her voice steady, but the warmth and intensity behind it impossible to hide.</p>

<p>"Think about what you feel when you look at me. Think about what you want. What you truly want."</p>

<p>She straightened, smoothing the folds of her blouse with a deliberate, almost ritualistic care, her fingers lingering for just a moment over her heart as if anchoring herself.</p>

<p>Then she took a step toward the door... and stopped.</p>

<p>Her eyes met his one last time, unflinching, holding both promise and patience, daring him to meet the truth between them.</p>

<p>"Whatever you decide, I’ll respect it," she continued, her words deliberate, unyielding. "But I won’t pretend anymore that I don’t want you. I like you, Alex. Freely, without guilt, without shame."</p>

<p>She inhaled softly, letting the weight of her confession hang in the air, her hand brushing the doorframe.</p>

<p>Her voice softened, but the resolve behind it didn’t waver.</p>

<p>"And nothing will change that, Alex."</p>

<p>With that, she stepped through, the door clicking softly behind her, leaving Alex alone... his chest tight, his thoughts a storm, and her lingering presence, the faint scent of her perfume, a reminder of everything he could no longer ignore.</p>

<p>***</p>

<p>Alex remained on the bed for a long time after she left, his mind a battlefield of conflicting emotions and desperate rationalizations.</p>

<p>Why are you hesitating? a voice in his head asked, and Alex wasn’t sure if it was his own thoughts or something else entirely.</p>

<p>Because it’s wrong, he tried to answer, but the words felt hollow now.</p>

<p>Is it? the voice pressed. She’s right, you know. She’s not your mother. She’s a woman... a beautiful, passionate woman who wants you. And you want her too, don’t you?</p>

<p>Alex pressed his palms against his eyes, trying to shut out the images that flooded his mind. Linda’s face inches from his.</p>

<p>The warmth of her body beside him. The way she’d looked at him like he was everything she’d ever wanted.</p>

<p>She’s a woman who wants you, the voice continued relentlessly. You’ve seen it in her eyes. You felt it when she was in your arms. She’s not fighting this attraction... why should you?</p>

<p>Alex’s chest heaved. The voice wouldn’t stop. The more he tried to push it away, the louder it grew — whispering her name, replaying her words, her nearness.</p>

<p>She wants you, Alex. She chose you. You felt it—</p>

<p>"Stop it," he muttered under his breath, gripping his hair, nails biting into his scalp.</p>

<p>You want her too.</p>

<p>"Aaghh... stop it!" His voice broke. "Fucking stop!"</p>

<p>The sound bounced off the sterile hospital walls, a jagged echo of his own weakness. For a moment, everything inside him trembled... his breath, his hands, his resolve.</p>

<p>He stared at the floor, chest rising and falling like he’d just fought something he couldn’t see. Maybe he had.</p>

<p>Silence fell again, heavy and merciless.</p>

<p>His resolve... that firm moral certainty that had defined him for so long... felt like sand slipping through his fingers.</p>

<p>And that realization made him angry. Frustrated. Confused in ways he’d never experienced.</p>

<p>He shot to his feet, the sudden movement making the narrow bed creak in protest. His hands clenched into fists so tight his knuckles went white, every muscle in his body coiled like a spring ready to snap.</p>

<p>The small hospital room felt like a cage designed specifically to torture him... too small to pace, too quiet to escape his thoughts, saturated with Linda’s lingering presence that made rational thinking impossible.</p>

<p>I can’t think here, the realization crashed over him with desperate clarity. Not surrounded by her scent, her memory, the echo of her confession.</p>
