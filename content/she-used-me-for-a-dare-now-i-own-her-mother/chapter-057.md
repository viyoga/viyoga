<p>Saturday – Night.</p>

<p>The club was an oasis of dim light and muted luxury. Velvet chairs hugged the corners. Jazz drifted through the air like smoke, weaving with the scent of aged liquor and expensive perfume.</p>

<p>Sophia sat alone in a shadowed corner, posture perfect, champagne flute balanced between her fingers.</p>

<p>She looked as if she belonged to this place... elegant, untouchable, and self-possessed. Yet beneath the calm façade, her thoughts worked like sharp blades.</p>

<p>A phone buzzed softly against the marble table. Without rushing, she lifted it to her ear.</p>

<p>"Yes?"</p>

<p>The voice on the other end reported progress... plans advancing as expected, small pieces already shifting into place.</p>

<p>A smirk curved at her lips. She tapped her nail against the crystal glass, each sound a measured beat of satisfaction.</p>

<p>"Carry on," she said, voice cool and crisp. A few taps sent a reward through to her contact. "Good work. Keep it moving."</p>

<p>The call ended. Sophia leaned back in her chair, letting the crystal glass rest lightly against her lips.</p>

<p>A slow, satisfied smile curved her lips.</p>

<p>’Perfect...’ she thought, fingers drumming lightly against the table. ’Everything is falling into place exactly as it should. I can’t wait to see that day... when all the pieces come together, and they finally realize the game has already been set.’</p>

<p>Her eyes drifting lazily toward the club entrance. She drummed her fingers against her glass.</p>

<p>"Where are they?" The faint tightness of her jaw betrayed her growing impatience. She had no intention of waiting long.</p>

<p>Then, finally, she saw them. Robert and Kyle.</p>

<p>The men stepped inside together, familiar silhouettes cutting through the smoky air.</p>

<p>Her expression shifted in a heartbeat... scheming to impatient to... vulnerable.</p>

<p>A single glimmering tear slid free, catching the low light like a jewel.</p>

<p>Robert noticed first.</p>

<p>"Sophia?" His voice was taut with alarm.</p>

<p>Kyle was at her side in moments, brushing his hand against hers as if to anchor her. "Why are you crying?"</p>

<p>She let the performance bloom. Her shoulders trembled just so, lips quivering as though her heart were fracturing. She whispered, soft, broken, "It’s... Marcus."</p>

<p>Their attention sharpened instantly.</p>

<p>"Yesterday... he almost hit me." Her voice cracked, and she clutched her glass as if it were the only thing keeping her together.</p>

<p>The story tumbled out, carefully calculated to evoke fear, doubt, and protective instincts.</p>

<p>Marcus had confronted her after hearing about Alex. James Blackwood’s interference had set him off.</p>

<p>Sophia painted him as volatile, unreasonable, almost monstrous, her eyes glistening with the faintest hint of tears.</p>

<p>Their eyes burned with concern. Yet beneath it, something else stirred.</p>

<p>She leaned forward slightly, her perfume a soft current wrapping around them. Her fingers brushed theirs lightly, not enough to be bold, just enough to linger.</p>

<p>Every sigh from her lips, every tremor of her voice was a seed... fear, sympathy, and forbidden desire tangled together.</p>

<p>Robert’s jaw tightened. Kyle’s breath came shorter. Both knew their loyalty belonged to Marcus, but the sight of Sophia... tear-streaked, vulnerable, too beautiful in her fragility... was impossible to resist.</p>

<p>For Robert, his hand lingered too long over hers. For Kyle, his gaze dropped to her lips when she exhaled, slow and aching.</p>

<p>For her... I’d do anything. Even against Marcus. The thought pulsed unbidden, treacherous, impossible to silence.</p>

<p>Sophia let the silence stretch, just enough for their minds to betray them. Then she murmured:</p>

<p>"Things aren’t right between us. I don’t know how much longer I can stay with him..."</p>

<p>She didn’t need to say more. The suggestion was enough. Their imaginations did the rest.</p>

<p>Now they believed there was a chance... if they stayed close, if they protected her, if they played their cards right... Marcus might lose her. And one of them might gain her.</p>

<p>Exactly as she intended.</p>

<p>They fussed over her, Robert muttering reassurances, Kyle brushing away her tears, both desperate to soothe her.</p>

<p>She let them. She leaned on them. She let them believe she needed them.</p>

<p>But when their eyes turned away for just a second, Sophia’s mask slipped.</p>

<p>A flicker of a smile curved her lips... sharp, triumphant.</p>

<p>Both men are leaning exactly where I want. Marcus will follow soon enough. None of them even see the threads I’ve already set in motion.</p>

<p>She lifted her champagne flute. The golden light of the chandelier shimmered across the glass, catching the curve of her smirk like a crown.</p>

<p>The room itself seemed to bow toward her, every note of jazz, every flicker of gold, echoing the truth.</p>

<p>Sophia wasn’t waiting to win. She already had.</p>

<p>***</p>

<p>Sunday Evening – Victoria and Alex</p>

<p>The city wore the soft, amber glow of the sinking sun, painting the streets with warmth.</p>

<p>Inside Victoria’s sleek black car, the hum of the engine was steady, almost hypnotic.</p>

<p>Leather seats, the faint scent of her perfume lingering in the air, and the soft hum of traffic outside created a cocoon of privacy.</p>

<p>Victoria’s posture was composed, confident, every movement precise.</p>

<p>Her hands gripped the steering wheel with control, but there was an unmistakable lightness in her expression, a satisfaction that radiated from every feature of her face.</p>

<p>Alex sat beside her, leaning slightly back, letting the vibrations of the car meld with the lingering heat between them.</p>

<p>The weekend had left its mark... not just physically but emotionally, mentally, in ways that left them both raw, exhilarated, and craving more subtle moments like this.</p>

<p>They had explored every hidden, primal desire, every secret fantasy they’d dared to suppress, in rooms that had become sanctuaries of indulgence.</p>

<p>Stairs, swimming pool, garden, every corner of their house had been witness to their shared surrender, their unspoken understanding.</p>

<p>And now, after an entire Sunday spent in languid sleep and quiet recovery, they were here, navigating the city streets, still wrapped in the afterglow.</p>

<p>Victoria’s eyes flicked toward Alex, a knowing glint in her gaze. "You’re awfully quiet," she murmured, voice low, teasing, yet intimate. "Still replaying... everything?"</p>

<p>Alex let out a soft chuckle, fingers brushing over his jaw. "Yeah... I can’t stop. You’ve... made it impossible to think of anything else."</p>

<p>Her laughter was soft, a throaty, teasing melody that wrapped around him. "Good," she replied, tilting her head in that subtle, deliberate way that always made his pulse spike. "That’s the idea."</p>

<p>The streets slid past in streaks of gold and shadow, the wind through the slightly open windows carrying the scent of night blooms and city life.</p>

<p>Victoria maneuvered the car with effortless grace, each turn precise, controlled, like an extension of herself.</p>

<p>Finally, she slowed, pulling up in front of a polished dealership, its lights reflecting off the clean glass and shiny exteriors inside.</p>

<p>After their long conversation, they had arrived at last, standing before the dealership where Alex’s new car awaited.</p>

<p>"So," Alex said, leaning slightly toward her, "your turn. I want you to choose. I trust your taste."</p>

<p>Victoria’s lips curved in a small, playful smile. "Hmm... not too flashy," she said, her eyes scanning the rows of vehicles.</p>

<p>"But it should have... presence. People notice, but not in the way that screams for attention. Something sleek, practical, confident... undeniable."</p>

<p>Inside, the air smelled faintly of new leather and polished metal. Victoria’s hand brushed against his occasionally, casual but deliberate, sending sparks along his arm.</p>

<p>Alex felt the warmth of her presence, the subtle command in the way she led him, guided him toward her chosen car.</p>

<p>She stopped in front of a deep metallic gray sedan, its lines sharp but understated, elegant without screaming extravagance.</p>

<p>Victoria stepped back, hand sweeping toward the car as if presenting a prize. "This one. It’s strong. Confident. And... it suits you."</p>

<p>Alex ran a hand along the hood, feeling the smooth metal beneath his fingers, sensing the care she’d taken in selecting it.</p>

<p>"You really know what you’re doing, don’t you?" he asked, his voice low, a mix of admiration and lingering desire.</p>

<p>Victoria leaned closer, lips brushing his ear. "I know you," she whispered, her voice like velvet. "And that’s all that matters."</p>

<p>The purchase was smooth, swift. Paperwork signed, keys handed over... and just like that, Alex held the car.</p>

<p>A gift from Victoria, a token of her taste and influence, it reflected not just practicality but her quiet authority, her hand guiding the shape of his world in subtle, undeniable ways.</p>

<p>Back in the car, Victoria slid into the driver’s seat again, her hand finding his on the gear shift.</p>

<p>Fingers intertwined briefly, almost possessive, almost tender. "Ready for the next adventure?" she asked, her tone teasing, confident, and full of unspoken promises.</p>

<p>Alex’s lips curved into a slow grin, his heart still hammering from both the weekend and the subtle tension radiating from her.</p>

<p>"Always," he said, the word carrying a weight of trust and desire.</p>

<p>Victoria’s eyes glimmered in the reflection of the dashboard lights, a playful tilt to her head.</p>

<p>The city stretched before them, every streetlight dancing off the car’s surface, mirroring the quiet thrill of power and indulgence between them.</p>

<p>She glanced at him briefly, letting her gaze linger a beat too long, sending shivers down his spine.</p>

<p>For Alex, the weekend had unlocked something profound: desire, vulnerability, trust... all perfectly intertwined.</p>

<p>And as Victoria started the car and merged into the city streets, he realized this was only the beginning. The car was more than a vehicle—it was another stage, another Chapter of their connection, another playground for the subtle games they were only beginning to explore.</p>

<p>Victoria’s hand brushed his again, this time resting lightly atop his. "You know," she murmured, a small smirk tugging at her lips, "some adventures... are only meant for two."</p>

<p>Alex caught her gaze, that confident, challenging look, and leaned closer. "Then I’m ready... for whatever comes next."</p>

<p>Her laughter, soft and teasing, filled the car as the city lights blurred past.</p>

<p>For a moment, it felt like time itself had slowed, letting them linger in the charged, unspoken tension of everything they had shared... and everything they had yet to discover.</p>

<p>Then Victoria’s expression shifted, a flicker of unease crossing her otherwise calm features.</p>

<p>Her fingers traced idle patterns on the gear shift as she spoke, voice low but deliberate: "Catherine called me today. She wants to meet."</p>

<p>Alex stiffened instantly, a jolt running through him. He swallowed. "Catherine... did she... see us?" His voice was tense, betraying the unease he had tried to bury.</p>

<p>Victoria’s eyes narrowed slightly, the glint in them sharp and assessing. "From the way she spoke... I get the feeling she knows more than she’s letting on. I’ll only know when I see her in person."</p>

<p>Alex exhaled, the hum of the engine suddenly louder, the city lights blurring past like a pulse of warning.</p>

<p>His mind raced back to the party... the bathroom, the stolen moment, the abrupt interruption. And now Catherine’s call, her subtle knowing tone, made everything feel precarious.</p>

<p>Victoria leaned back, fingers brushing his hand again, just enough to remind him that despite the tension, she was in control. "But... don’t worry. I’ll tell you everything after I see her."</p>

<p>Alex’s chest tightened. For the first time that evening, he realized that not every adventure with Victoria was predictable.</p>

<p>Some were dangerous. Some had stakes he wasn’t yet ready to measure.</p>

<p>And Catherine... Catherine might already know more than they had imagined.</p>
