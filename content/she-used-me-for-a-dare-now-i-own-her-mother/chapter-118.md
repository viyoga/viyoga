<p>The afternoon sun cast long shadows across the master bedroom balcony as Alex sank into the wide cushioned chair, letting the expensive furniture cradle his weight.</p>

<p>From this vantage point, the view stretched endlessly... manicured gardens rolling outward like green waves, the six surrounding villas standing sentinel in their perfect circular arrangement.</p>

<p>It should have been peaceful. Instead, his mind churned with relentless momentum.</p>

<p>He closed his eyes briefly, trying to organize the chaos of the past week.</p>

<p>Nina’s crisis had come first... that pale, fragile little girl fighting for her life while her family watched helplessly.</p>

<p>Then the trial. Marcus steele’s smug face across the courtroom, the carefully orchestrated theater of justice, and his own calculated performance on the stand.</p>

<p>He’d won that battle, but the war was far from over. Marcus and Pierce wouldn’t simply accept defeat... they’d regroup, recalibrate, strike from a different angle.</p>

<p>And now Viktor.</p>

<p>The dying man’s revelation haunted him more than he wanted to admit. Seven Guardian Gods. Seven Sacred Houses. An eighth god who’d supposedly killed them all. Conspiracy woven through the very foundations of modern society.</p>

<p>Too much. It’s all happening too fast.</p>

<p>But wasn’t that what he’d wanted? Power, influence, the ability to reshape his circumstances? He couldn’t complain about the pace when he’d been the one pushing forward so aggressively.</p>

<p>His thoughts drifted sideways, landing on something softer... Tisha’s offer.</p>

<p>Her voice surfaced in his memory... "Move in with me."</p>

<p>She’d asked him to move in with her, that genuine warmth in her voice making it clear she meant it.</p>

<p>The invitation had been simple, uncomplicated by ulterior motives or strategic calculation. Just... affection. Love, though neither of them had said the word yet.</p>

<p>He refused her because he already planned her to let her move in with him.</p>

<p>But the only issue is Victoria, he didn’t introduced her with Victoria, yet.</p>

<p>Both women were seasoned enough to avoid chaos. He trusted their control, even their unspoken dependence on him.</p>

<p>But jealousy was woven into the nature of women; it was a current he’d have to navigate carefully, never underestimate.</p>

<p>But that created problems he needed to solve.</p>

<p>Alex opened his eyes, his gaze sharpening as he studied the surrounding properties with new intensity.</p>

<p>Six villas, each smaller than his central mansion but substantial in their own right, arranged in a perfect circle around his position of prominence.</p>

<p>Whoever had designed this layout understood symbolism... the center villa was unmistakably the seat of power, with the others orbiting like planets around a star.</p>

<p>He stood, moving to the balcony railing to get a better view.</p>

<p>The architecture was deliberate. Each villa had clear sight lines to his own, but they were positioned far enough apart to provide privacy while maintaining the sense of a unified compound.</p>

<p>The shared gardens connected them all, creating both beauty and vulnerability... anyone in those six properties would have easy access to his space.</p>

<p>That was the problem.</p>

<p>If he brought Tisha here... and eventually Victoria, if that complicated arrangement ever solidified... he couldn’t afford unknown neighbors.</p>

<p>He couldn’t have strangers living fifty meters away, potentially observing his movements, noting his visitors, reporting to whoever might pay for such information.</p>

<p>The Steele family would love that kind of access. So would the Sacred Houses, if Viktor’s revelations proved true and they learned of his involvement.</p>

<p>Privacy equals security. Security equals control.</p>

<p>The solution was obvious, if expensive: buy them all.</p>

<p>If he owned all six surrounding villas, he could transform this entire area into a private sanctuary... a fortress disguised as a luxury compound.</p>

<p>The strategic advantages multiplied as he thought it through.</p>

<p>First, security. He could station Viktor’s team... assuming he healed Viktor and recruited them successfully... in the first villa at the entrance.</p>

<p>They’d serve as both guards and early warning system, their professional capabilities ensuring nothing approached his residence without detection.</p>

<p>Their presence would be explicable too; wealthy people employed security staff all the time.</p>

<p>Second, separation. If both Tisha and Victoria were going to be part of his life, they’d need their own spaces within his territory.</p>

<p>Dedicated villas would provide independence while keeping them close. It would also reduce friction... each woman could maintain her own domain, her own autonomy, rather than feeling like they were competing for position in a shared household.</p>

<p>Third, future expansion. He had no idea where his path would lead, but more resources were always better than fewer.</p>

<p>Guest accommodations for allies, safe houses if enemies struck, research facilities if he needed to investigate Viktor’s evidence more thoroughly... the possibilities were endless.</p>

<p>The only question was cost.</p>

<p>Alex pulled out his phone, quickly calculating. His central villa had cost twenty five million.</p>

<p>If the surrounding properties were priced at 10M... and Manager had indicated they were... he was looking at approximately forty-eight million dollars to acquire all six.</p>

<p>Sixty million.</p>

<p>The number should have been paralyzing. A year ago, he’d been scraping together money for textbooks and ramen.</p>

<p>But that was before Victoria. Before the system. Before Lilith had opened doors he hadn’t even known existed.</p>

<p>He had resources now. Not unlimited, but substantial. Victoria’s patronage provided access to financial instruments most people never learned existed. The system’s rewards had given him liquid assets and investment opportunities.</p>

<p>And if he played his cards right with Viktor’s situation, he might gain access to capabilities that transcended mere money.</p>

<p>I can do this. I can make this work.</p>

<p>Alex’s hands tightened on the balcony railing as his vision expanded.</p>

<p>Not just six villas... an empire. Not just a home... a headquarters. A place where he could operate freely, protected from observation and interference, surrounded by people he’d chosen to trust rather than strangers thrust upon him by circumstance.</p>

<p>The afternoon sun continued its slow descent, painting the gardens in shades of gold and amber.</p>

<p>Somewhere in the city, Viktor lay dying while his loyal team waited for Alex’s decision. Somewhere else, Sophia and Marcus plotted their revenge. And somewhere... perhaps closer than he realized... the Sacred Houses pursued their own mysterious agendas.</p>

<p>But here, in this moment, Alex allowed himself to see past immediate crises to something larger.</p>

<p>He was building more than security measures or relationship logistics. He was constructing the foundation of genuine power... the kind that couldn’t be easily threatened or dismantled.</p>

<p>A compound. A sanctuary. A fortress.</p>

<p>His fortress.</p>

<p>"Ambitious, darling," Lilith’s voice purred into his consciousness, amused and approving in equal measure. "But then again, why settle for a single mansion when you can claim an empire?"</p>

<p>Alex smiled, still gazing out at the six villas that would soon be his.</p>

<p>"Why settle indeed."</p>
