<p>The air in the meeting room was thick with a heavy, reverent silence. Damien, Dimitri, Andre, and Pavel stared at the crimson pill in their palms like it was the most precious thing in existence.</p>

<p>The memory was written clearly across all their faces... the elixir Alex had given to Viktor. The miracle medicine that had pulled their commander back from death’s doorway and pushed him beyond anything they’d thought possible.</p>

<p>Viktor hadn’t just been cured.</p>

<p>He’d broken through to Apex Realm.</p>

<p>From dying to transcendent in the span of hours.</p>

<p>And now Alex was giving each of them something similar.</p>

<p>Alex’s gaze swept across all four of them.</p>

<p>"Try them," he said simply. "Right now. See what they do."</p>

<p>The four men exchanged glances... brief, wordless communication that spoke of years fighting together, trusting each other with their lives.</p>

<p>Then, without hesitation, all four raised the pills to their lips.</p>

<p>Damien first. Then Dimitri. Andre. Pavel.</p>

<p>They swallowed simultaneously.</p>

<p>Viktor leaned back in his chair, watching with the calm assessment of a commander observing his troops under new conditions. His Apex-enhanced perception tracked every micro-expression, every subtle shift in their physiology.</p>

<p>He wasn’t shocked anymore.</p>

<p>Not by Alex’s mysterious sources, not by his impossible resources, not by medicine that shouldn’t exist appearing from jacket pockets like casual gifts.</p>

<p>Viktor had stopped questioning where Alex’s power came from.</p>

<p>He’d simply accepted that it existed... and that aligning himself with it had been the best decision he’d ever made.</p>

<p>For several heartbeats, nothing happened.</p>

<p>The four men sat still, eyes closed, clearly feeling for internal changes with the kind of focus only cultivators possessed.</p>

<p>Then Andre gasped.</p>

<p>His eyes flew open — and they were different.</p>

<p>Not physically changed. Still the same brown eyes he’d always had.</p>

<p>But there was something in them now. A brightness. A clarity. Like stars had been lit behind his irises.</p>

<p>"I can feel it," Andre breathed, his voice thick with wonder. "It’s... it’s working. Right now. In real time."</p>

<p>Dimitri’s eyes opened next, carrying that same inner luminescence.</p>

<p>"My meridians," he rumbled, his massive chest expanding as he drew a deep breath. "I can feel them opening. Pathways I didn’t even know were blocked... clearing."</p>

<p>Pavel’s hands came up to his chest, eyes wide and shining.</p>

<p>"I feel lighter. Like I’ve been carrying weight I didn’t know existed and someone just lifted it off my shoulders." His voice cracked with emotion. "How is this possible?"</p>

<p>Damien opened his eyes last, and the professional composure that usually defined him had vanished completely.</p>

<p>"My pores," he said, almost laughing with disbelief. "I can feel them opening. Feel the impurities being pushed out. This is..." He shook his head, unable to find words adequate to the experience. "This is incredible."</p>

<p>All four of them stood there, breathing like they’d just discovered air for the first time, feeling changes happening in their bodies that conventional cultivation would have taken months to achieve.</p>

<p>And they knew... knew with absolute certainty, that this was just the beginning.</p>

<p>Damien moved first.</p>

<p>He crossed the distance to Alex in three strides and dropped to one knee, his head bowed.</p>

<p>"Thank you," he said, voice rough with emotion that went beyond gratitude into something deeper. "Thank you for seeing us. For investing in us. For..."</p>

<p>"Don’t."</p>

<p>Alex’s voice cut through the moment, gentle but firm.</p>

<p>Damien looked up, confusion flickering across his face.</p>

<p>Alex reached down, gripping Damien’s shoulder and pulling him back to his feet with Enhanced strength that made the movement effortless.</p>

<p>"Don’t kneel," Alex said quietly, his gaze sweeping across all of them. "Not to me. Not anymore."</p>

<p>He stepped back, addressing all four men now.</p>

<p>"You decided to be my people. That makes you my family. And family doesn’t kneel to family."</p>

<p>The words landed with weight that had nothing to do with authority and everything to do with genuine connection.</p>

<p>Pavel’s tears came harder. Dimitri’s jaw worked as he fought his own emotion. Andre looked away, blinking rapidly.</p>

<p>Even Viktor’s expression softened, something profound passing through his eyes.</p>

<p>Alex turned back to his inventory, reaching in again.</p>

<p>When his hands emerged, they held four small containers... each one filled with nine more crimson pills.</p>

<p>He moved through the room systematically, placing one container in each man’s hands.</p>

<p>"Ten pills total," Alex explained. "One every three days. Proper cultivation between doses. Let each pill integrate fully before taking the next."</p>

<p>He paused, meeting each of their gazes in turn.</p>

<p>"If you practice diligently... if you dedicate yourselves completely to cultivation over the next month... I’ll have all of you reach Peak Enhanced Realm in this one month."</p>

<p>The shock on their faces was immediate and absolute.</p>

<p>"A month?" Andre whispered.</p>

<p>"Peak Enhanced?" Dimitri’s voice carried disbelief mixed with desperate hope.</p>

<p>Alex’s expression remained steady, certain.</p>

<p>"A month. Maybe less if you push harder. These pills will accelerate your progress exponentially. But you still have to put in the work. Still have to cultivate properly, manage your energy circulation, endure the discomfort as your bodies transform."</p>

<p>He let that sink in.</p>

<p>"Do that... prove your dedication... and I promise you..." His voice dropped, carrying absolute conviction. "I’ll make each of you Apex Realm."</p>

<p>The room went completely silent.</p>

<p>Not just quiet. Silent in the way that follows revelations too large to process immediately.</p>

<p>Apex Realm.</p>

<p>Not just Peak Enhanced... the threshold they’d been fighting toward for years.</p>

<p>Apex Realm.</p>

<p>The level Viktor had reached. The level that separated mortals from legends. The level that made you valuable to Sacred Houses, that gave you genuine power in the hidden world, that transformed you from expendable asset into irreplaceable force.</p>

<p>But beneath the shock was something else.</p>

<p>Belief.</p>

<p>Because they’d seen what Alex could do.</p>

<p>If anyone could deliver on a promise this audacious...</p>

<p>It was him.</p>

<p>Viktor remained seated for a long moment, his intense gaze drifting over the faces of his men. He watched the raw excitement, the starlight in their eyes.</p>

<p>For the first time, the hardened commander’s tactical mask completely shattered, and a single, silent tear of happiness traced a path down his scarred cheek.</p>

<p>Viktor stood slowly, the movement drawing every eye in the room.</p>

<p>His Apex presence radiated outward... not threatening, but undeniable. The kind of power that made the air feel heavier, that reminded everyone present exactly what they were being offered.</p>

<p>He walked toward Alex with measured steps, his expression carrying depths of emotion that his tactical discipline could no longer fully contain.</p>

<p>When he reached Alex, Viktor placed a hand over his heart.</p>

<p>"Thank you," he said, his Russian accent thickening with emotion. "Not just for the medicine. Not just for the resources."</p>

<p>His eyes swept to his team... Damien, Dimitri, Andre, Pavel... all of them still holding their pills like sacred treasures.</p>

<p>"Thank you for taking care of these men. My people. My brothers."</p>

<p>His voice dropped lower, carrying weight that made it feel like an oath.</p>

<p>"From this moment forward, you can send me anywhere. Ask me to face anything. Even certain death." His eyes locked onto Alex’s with absolute conviction.</p>

<p>"And I will not complain. I will not hesitate. I will go gladly, knowing that you’ve given us more than we ever dared hope for."</p>

<p>The words hung in the air, profound and binding.</p>

<p>Alex felt something shift in his peripheral vision.</p>

<p>The system interface materialized, visible only to him:</p>

<p>[LOYALTY UPDATE]</p>

<p>Viktor Reeves: 99% → 100% (MAXIMUM LOYALTY ACHIEVED)</p>

<p>Status: ABSOLUTE DEVOTION</p>

<p>Bond Type: Beyond Contract - Family</p>

<p>Alex looked at Viktor... really looked at him... and saw past the Apex practitioner, past the skilled operative, past the professional soldier.</p>

<p>He saw a man who’d lost everything serving House Blackthorne. Who’d been betrayed by those he’d bled for. Who’d watched his team hunted like animals with nowhere to turn.</p>

<p>And then found salvation in a college student who’d appeared in a hospital room with impossible medicine and genuine compassion.</p>

<p>Viktor’s loyalty wasn’t transactional anymore. Wasn’t strategic.</p>

<p>It was absolute. The kind of bond that transcended employment or even friendship into something that looked like devotion.</p>

<p>And these men... Damien, Dimitri, Andre, Pavel... were all Viktor had left. His team. His family. The only people in the world who’d stood with him when everything collapsed.</p>

<p>Alex had just promised to elevate them beyond their wildest expectations.</p>

<p>Had given Viktor’s family a future.</p>

<p>Alex stepped forward, closing the distance between them.</p>

<p>He placed his hand on Viktor’s shoulder—not as employer to employee, but as equal to equal.</p>

<p>"Listen to me very carefully, Viktor," Alex said, his voice carrying quiet intensity. "I will never send family to certain death."</p>

<p>He let that statement settle.</p>

<p>"You’re not expendable anymore. You’re not soldiers I’m deploying to die for objectives. You’re my people. My investment. My family."</p>

<p>His grip on Viktor’s shoulder tightened slightly.</p>

<p>"I’m building something that will last. Something that will change everything. And I need you... all of you... alive, strong, and standing beside me when we reshape this world."</p>

<p>His eyes held Viktor’s without wavering.</p>

<p>"So no. I won’t send you to certain death. I’ll send you to victory. Because that’s what family does."</p>

<p>For a long moment, Viktor just stared at him.</p>

<p>Then the Apex practitioner’s composure finally cracked completely.</p>

<p>Viktor pulled Alex into an embrace... brief but fierce, carrying emotion that words couldn’t adequately express.</p>

<p>When he stepped back, his eyes were bright but his expression was steady.</p>

<p>"Then we will deliver that victory," Viktor said simply. "Whatever you’re building... we’ll help you build it."</p>

<p>He turned to his team.</p>

<p>"You heard him, gentlemen. We have one month. One month to prove we’re worthy of this investment. One month to reach Peak Enhanced Realm."</p>

<p>His voice carried command that made them all straighten instinctively.</p>

<p>"I expect absolute dedication. Proper cultivation. No shortcuts, no half-measures. You will earn what he’s given us."</p>

<p>"Yes, sir!" The response came simultaneously from all four men, military discipline overriding emotion.</p>

<p>Viktor’s lips curved into a slight smile.</p>

<p>"Good. Then get to the cultivation chambers. Begin immediately. I want progress reports every three days."</p>

<p>They moved as one... professional efficiency taking over as they filed toward the door, each man clutching their pills with reverent care.</p>

<p>But Pavel paused at the threshold, looking back at Alex one final time.</p>

<p>"Thank you, Mr. Hale," he said quietly. "For everything. For believing in us. For giving us a future."</p>
