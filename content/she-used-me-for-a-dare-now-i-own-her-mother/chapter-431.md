<p>Six days had passed since that night with Elyria.</p>

<p>Yet, no matter how hard he tried, Alex couldn’t forget it.</p>

<p>He could still feel the overwhelming, crushing force of her palm strike. It hadn’t felt like being hit by a person. It felt like being struck by a cataclysmic wave that had swept him off his feet and slammed him into the wall like he weighed nothing.</p>

<p>One single attack. That was all it took.</p>

<p>In that moment, Alex had truly understood the terrifying gap between realms. The difference between Enhanced and Apex wasn’t just a matter of strength. It was like the difference between a candle and the sun.</p>

<p>And he wanted to bridge that gap as fast as possible.</p>

<p>He had dedicated every waking hour to training. Morning, afternoon, and night... he pushed his body to its limits inside Viktor’s basement facility.</p>

<p>The only thing that mattered to him now was Battle Sense.</p>

<p>And it had paid off.</p>

<p>[Battle Sense: 5%]</p>

<p>His Battle Sense had jumped from 1.5% to 5% in just six days. It was still far from where he needed to be, but the progress was undeniable.</p>

<p>Tonight, however, was different.</p>

<p>The atmosphere in the underground training hall was heavy... thick with tension. The air itself felt charged, like the calm before a storm.</p>

<p>Alex stood in the center of the large sparring area, shirtless, his body glistening with sweat. His breathing was steady, but his eyes were sharp.</p>

<p>Surrounding him in a loose circle were four men.</p>

<p>Damien. Dimitri. Andre. Pavel.</p>

<p>They weren’t standing casually. They had taken positions like predators circling prey. Their stances were low, balanced, and ready. Their eyes never left Alex for even a second.</p>

<p>No one spoke.</p>

<p>The only sound was the faint hum of the lights above and the occasional creak of the reinforced floor.</p>

<p>Alex could feel it... the pressure. The weight of four trained killers focusing entirely on him. This wasn’t training anymore. This was a hunt.</p>

<p>And he was the target.</p>

<p>From the edge of the ring, Viktor stood with his arms crossed, watching silently. His expression was unreadable, but his eyes were sharp.</p>

<p>Alex took a slow breath and glanced at everyone.</p>

<p>Then, he turned his gaze toward Viktor.</p>

<p>"Viktor..." Alex said, his voice calm but firm, carrying a quiet weight. "I hope you will honour our agreement."</p>

<p>Viktor smiled.</p>

<p>A slow, knowing smirk spread across his face as he looked directly at Alex.</p>

<p>"Yes," he said calmly, his voice carrying a hint of amusement. "I will... if you defeat them all together."</p>

<p>Alex wanted to increase his Battle Sense in the fastest way possible.</p>

<p>After thinking deeply, he came to a clear conclusion... the fastest method was the same one that had worked with Elyria.</p>

<p>Getting beaten.</p>

<p>He believed that facing overwhelming power head-on, surviving it, and letting his body adapt from the damage was the quickest path to real growth. That’s why he had directly asked Viktor to hit him with full power.</p>

<p>But Viktor had refused.</p>

<p>He told Alex that he was still not ready. That taking a full-powered strike from him right now would only break him, not help him grow.</p>

<p>Instead, Viktor gave him a different challenge... defeat all four of his men together in a single fight.</p>

<p>Only then would he even consider facing Alex with his full strength.</p>

<p>The air in the room grew even heavier.</p>

<p>Alex’s eyes slowly moved across the four men. Damien. Dimitri. Andre. Pavel. All of them were watching him with calm, focused expressions.</p>

<p>He had already defeated each of them one by one over the past few days. He knew their styles. Their strengths. Their weaknesses.</p>

<p>But fighting them all at once?</p>

<p>That was a completely different story.</p>

<p>Viktor’s voice cut through the silence.</p>

<p>"No cultivation enhancement. No extra abilities. No tricks. Just pure skill. If you can do that... then maybe, just maybe, I’ll consider hitting you myself."</p>

<p>Alex stared at Viktor for a few seconds.</p>

<p>Then a slow, dangerous smile spread across his face.</p>

<p>"Fine," he said quietly.</p>

<p>He rolled his shoulders and dropped into a fighting stance, eyes locked on the four men around him.</p>

<p>The tension in the room spiked to its peak.</p>

<p>Damien cracked his knuckles.</p>

<p>Dimitri shifted his weight.</p>

<p>Andre’s eyes narrowed.</p>

<p>Pavel gave a small, approving nod.</p>

<p>Viktor’s voice echoed through the hall.</p>

<p>"Begin."</p>

<p>The moment Viktor gave the signal, the air in the training hall turned razor-sharp.</p>

<p>Damien was the first to speak. His voice was calm, almost respectful, but carried a clear warning.</p>

<p>"Be careful, sir," Damien said, rolling his shoulders as he took a step forward. "I’ve improved a lot since our last fight. Last time you beat me easily. Tonight... it won’t be that easy."</p>

<p>Pavel smirked from Alex’s left side. "He’s not lying. We’ve been training harder than you think."</p>

<p>Dimitri cracked his neck on the right. "Four against one. Even if you’re stronger, numbers change everything."</p>

<p>Andre stayed silent, but his eyes were locked on Alex like a hawk, calculating every breath he took.</p>

<p>Alex didn’t reply. He simply adjusted his stance, knees slightly bent, hands loose at his sides. His eyes moved slowly across all four of them.</p>

<p>Then it began.</p>

<p>Damien moved first... a sharp jab aimed straight at Alex’s face. It looked real. Fast. But Alex’s instincts screamed feint.</p>

<p>He didn’t bite.</p>

<p>Just as he expected, Damien’s real attack came from below... a low kick aimed at his knee. Alex stepped back, narrowly avoiding it.</p>

<p>At the same moment, Pavel attacked from the left with a spinning backfist. Alex ducked, only for Dimitri to rush in from the right with a knee strike. Alex twisted his body at the last second, letting the knee graze past his ribs.</p>

<p>Andre didn’t attack. He simply moved.</p>

<p>The four of them began circling him like apex predators, constantly changing positions.</p>

<p>Every few seconds, one of them would shift... left, right, behind... forcing Alex to keep turning. They weren’t attacking blindly. They were testing. Probing. Looking for the smallest opening.</p>

<p>Alex’s eyes narrowed.</p>

<p>’This is... completely different.’</p>

<p>Fighting them one by one had been difficult, but manageable. He could focus on one opponent, read their rhythm, and counter.</p>

<p>But now?</p>

<p>Four different fighting styles. Four different rhythms. Four different threats attacking from multiple angles at once. It was overwhelming.</p>

<p>Every time he focused on one, another would move into his blind spot. Every time he tried to create distance, they would close in like a pack of wolves.</p>

<p>’This is much harder than I thought...’</p>

<p>A cold sweat formed on his back, but Alex forced himself to stay calm. His breathing remained steady. His mind sharpened.</p>

<p>’Focus. Don’t panic. Read them.’</p>

<p>Damien came in again with a combination... jab, cross, low kick. Alex blocked the first two and stepped over the kick. But the moment he did, Pavel was already there, throwing a sharp elbow toward his temple.</p>

<p>Alex raised his forearm just in time. The impact stung.</p>

<p>Before he could recover, Dimitri swept low from behind, trying to take his legs out. Alex jumped, barely avoiding it, only for Andre to appear in front of him like a ghost, throwing a devastating spinning heel kick toward his chest.</p>

<p>’Too fast—!’</p>

<p>Alex crossed his arms in front of his chest at the last second. The kick landed hard, sending him sliding backward across the mat. His arms burned from the impact.</p>

<p>The four men didn’t give him a second to breathe.</p>

<p>They moved again... repositioning instantly. Damien took the front. Pavel moved to his left. Dimitri circled to his right. Andre stayed behind, waiting for the perfect moment.</p>

<p>Alex’s chest rose and fell faster now. His mind raced.</p>

<p>’They’re not just attacking... they’re coordinating. Every move forces me into the next one’s range. This is teamwork at its highest level.’</p>

<p>He clenched his fists.</p>

<p>’But I’m not the same person I was six days ago.’</p>

<p>Alex took a deep breath and dropped even lower into his stance. His eyes became calm. Focused. Dangerous.</p>

<p>"Come on then," he muttered under his breath. "Let’s see how long you can keep this up."</p>

<p>The four men exchanged quick glances.</p>

<p>Then they attacked again... this time with even more coordination.</p>

<p>The four men moved like a single organism.</p>

<p>Damien came from the front with a lightning-fast combination... jab, cross, elbow. Alex blocked the first two, but the elbow slipped through and clipped his shoulder.</p>

<p>At the same time Pavel attacked from the left with a low sweep. Alex jumped over it, only for Dimitri to appear on his right and throw a sharp knee toward his ribs. Alex twisted mid-air, taking the hit on his forearm instead.</p>

<p>Pain flared up his arm, but he didn’t have time to react.</p>

<p>Andre was already behind him.</p>

<p>A spinning back kick slammed into Alex’s lower back, sending him stumbling forward.</p>

<p>Before he could regain his balance, Damien was there again... this time with a powerful roundhouse kick aimed at his head.</p>
