<p>"Mr. Hale," Dimitri said, his deep voice carrying genuine warmth. "Welcome."</p>

<p>Alex nodded once. "Good to see you, Dimitri."</p>

<p>Dimitri stepped aside, holding the door open with a smooth, almost practiced movement.</p>

<p>"Please. Everyone is waiting for you inside."</p>

<p>Alex crossed the threshold.</p>

<p>Immediately, the change hit him.</p>

<p>The villa felt alive.</p>

<p>Not chaotic.</p>

<p>Not tense.</p>

<p>But alert.</p>

<p>Grounded.</p>

<p>Dangerous in the way only trained operatives could be... like wolves resting but capable of tearing apart threats in a heartbeat.</p>

<p>Dimitri walked ahead, guiding him through the entryway. Alex followed silently, taking in the environment.</p>

<p>The interior was clean and elegant in a way that spoke of quiet wealth, not showmanship... polished flooring, minimalist corners, tasteful art.</p>

<p>But what stood out wasn’t the decor.</p>

<p>It was the signs of life:</p>

<p>A rack of neatly arranged tactical gear.</p>

<p>A table with multiple news screens running.</p>

<p>An open doorway where he glimpsed training mats.</p>

<p>A whiteboard with operational notes in three languages.</p>

<p>They hadn’t just found shelter here.</p>

<p>They had built a base.</p>

<p>Dimitri led him to a pair of tall double doors.</p>

<p>"They’re waiting in the hall," he said, and pushed them open.</p>

<p>Alex stepped inside...</p>

<p>...and four men straightened instantly.</p>

<p>The hall was spacious, sunlight streaming through tall windows. The furniture was arranged for meetings rather than relaxation... comfortable leather seats, a central table with maps and notes spread across it.</p>

<p>Damien.</p>

<p>Andre.</p>

<p>Pavel.</p>

<p>And at the center...</p>

<p>Viktor Reeves.</p>

<p>Their presences hit Alex all at once... and so did the Dominion System.</p>

<p>Holy shit.</p>

<p>The information cascaded through Alex’s Enhanced mind, each detail clicking into place with tactical precision that made his breath catch.</p>

<p>Viktor—Apex Realm. Lilith was right. He’s actually transcended. One full realm above me.</p>

<p>A mixture of emotions flooded through him:</p>

<p>Shock at the scale of transformation.</p>

<p>Acknowledgment that his investment had paid off exponentially.</p>

<p>His eyes move from Viktor to others...</p>

<p>Damien—Middle Stage Enhanced. He’s progressed too. Half a realm above where I am now.</p>

<p>The others—all Early Stage Enhanced. Every single one of them at my current level.</p>

<p>Pride—fierce and undeniable—because this is my team. I built this.</p>

<p>And beneath it, confidence settling into his bones like armor.</p>

<p>Four Enhanced-realm operatives, led by an Apex practitioner, all bound to me by loyalty deeper than contracts.</p>

<p>With them backing me, Catherine’s family politics can’t touch me. Marcus and his crew? They’re nothing. Sophia’s social games? Irrelevant.</p>

<p>I’m not just playing in their world anymore. I’m operating on a completely different level.</p>

<p>The assessment overlay faded as quickly as it had appeared, leaving Alex staring at the five men who’d arranged themselves in informal formation... not military rigid, but clearly organized.</p>

<p>Damien flanking Viktor slightly behind. Andre and Pavel to the sides.</p>

<p>Creating natural respect without forced hierarchy.</p>

<p>Alex let his gaze sweep across them, taking in the transformation with his own eyes now that the system data had confirmed what his instincts were screaming.</p>

<p>The last time he’d seen these men, they had been broken... haunted, hunted, barely clinging to life.</p>

<p>Now...</p>

<p>They all looked healthy. Strong. Purposeful. The desperate fugitives from the hospital were gone. These were warriors who’d found their footing again.</p>

<p>Viktor stepped forward, his presence steady and commanding.</p>

<p>"Alex," He said, his voice deep and resonant, carrying natural authority without aggression. His Russian accent was subtle but present, particularly on the hard consonants.</p>

<p>"Welcome to the home you’ve given us. We’ve been waiting for this moment... to thank you properly, to show you what your grace has built."</p>

<p>"Thank you, Viktor," Alex replied, noting how Viktor’s eyes tracked his movement... assessing, recognizing. "You look... well."</p>

<p>Viktor’s lips curved into a knowing smile. "An understatement, I think. But appropriate."</p>

<p>Viktor glanced over his shoulder at the men standing behind him, a flicker of pride passing through his eyes. He lifted a hand in their direction, inviting Alex’s attention toward them.</p>

<p>"You already know every member of my team... but allow me the honor of introducing them to you properly."</p>

<p>He gestured toward Damien.</p>

<p>"This is Damien—my second-in-command. When I’m not present, he runs operations."</p>

<p>Damien extended his hand.</p>

<p>"Good to see you again, Mr. Hale."</p>

<p>His grip was firm, respectful, familiar... not the awe-filled uncertainty from their first meeting.</p>

<p>Next, Viktor motioned toward a tall, calm figure.</p>

<p>"Dimitri. Logistics, surveillance, and forward security."</p>

<p>Dimitri nodded once, shaking Alex’s hand with quiet warmth.</p>

<p>"We’ve been looking forward to your visit."</p>

<p>"Andre," Viktor continued, "communications, intel, and tactical planning."</p>

<p>Andre offered a professional smile.</p>

<p>"Mr. Hale. Always an honor."</p>

<p>Then Viktor’s gaze shifted to the last member of the line.</p>

<p>"And Pavel... the youngest of us."</p>

<p>Pavel stepped forward... but instead of offering his hand immediately, he paused. His shoulders tensed, breath unsteady, as though something was pushing up his throat.</p>

<p>"Mr. Hale, I..." Pavel’s voice cracked slightly. He swallowed hard, forcing himself to continue. "I need to apologize. When Viktor was dying, when we thought we had no hope, I... I blamed you."</p>

<p>His hands clenched into fists at his sides.</p>

<p>"Said terrible things. Doubted you when you were our only chance." He dropped his gaze, jaw tight with shame. "You owed us nothing. We were strangers. Fugitives. And you still helped. I should have trusted that. Should have believed."</p>

<p>Pavel’s voice dropped lower, thick with emotion he was barely controlling.</p>

<p>"Instead I... I cursed your name. Called you false hope. Said you’d abandoned us to die."</p>

<p>He forced himself to look up, meeting Alex’s eyes directly despite the obvious cost. "I’m sorry. Truly. Whatever you need, whenever you need it... I will never doubt you again."</p>

<p>Alex stepped closer, his Enhanced body moving with unconscious grace that made his authority feel natural rather than forced.</p>

<p>"Pavel," he said, his voice calm and measured, carrying that weight his Enhanced capabilities gave it. "You were desperate. Your leader was dying. Your team was facing extinction. Desperate men say things they don’t mean."</p>

<p>He stopped directly in front of Pavel, close enough that he had to look up slightly to meet his eyes.</p>

<p>"I don’t hold it against you," Alex continued, letting each word land with precision. "You’ve seen now what happens when you trust me. When you give me time to deliver."</p>

<p>Viktor stepped forward the moment Alex finished speaking to Pavel.</p>

<p>"Alex," he said, his voice calm but carrying the unmistakable authority of a man who now stood at Apex Realm, "before we continue, allow me to speak for my team."</p>

<p>He rested a firm hand on Pavel’s shoulder, steadying him... not shielding, but acknowledging.</p>

<p>"If, during my absence... if in the chaos of that night... any of my men spoke out of fear or acted out of desperation," Viktor said, "I take responsibility."</p>

<p>Viktor’s gaze swept across his entire team before returning to Alex.</p>

<p>"They are good men. Loyal men. Skilled men who have bled for causes far less worthy than what you represent." Viktor’s voice took on an edge of fierce protectiveness.</p>

<p>"But they are also human. And humanity breaks under enough pressure. Fear makes us cruel. Desperation makes us unfair. The shadow of death makes us say things we would never speak in clarity."</p>

<p>He released Pavel’s shoulder and took a half-step forward, closer to Alex, his presence radiating sincerity that no amount of power could fake.</p>

<p>"Whatever was said in that hospital room, whatever doubts were voiced, whatever blame was cast..." Viktor’s voice carried absolute conviction.</p>

<p>"It was said by men who had lost everything except each other. Men who had been betrayed by those they served. Men who had every rational reason to believe that hope itself was a lie."</p>

<p>His eyes never left Alex’s face.</p>

<p>"So I ask you, as their commander, as the one responsible for their discipline and conduct..." Viktor’s voice softened, but lost none of its intensity. "Please. Forgive them for their humanity. Forgive them for breaking under impossible weight. Forgive them for being what we all are beneath the training and the power... just men trying to survive one more day."</p>

<p>The silence that followed was profound.</p>

<p>Viktor—Apex Realm, capable of crushing Enhanced practitioners with ease, positioned to potentially command respect from Sacred Houses themselves... stood before Alex asking forgiveness for his team with the kind of humility that made the request impossible to dismiss or diminish.</p>

<p>Alex felt something shift in his chest... recognition of what this moment actually meant.</p>

<p>Viktor wasn’t just apologizing on behalf of his men.</p>

<p>He was demonstrating leadership.</p>

<p>Taking responsibility for his team’s conduct even when he’d been unconscious and powerless to control it.</p>

<p>Showing Alex that loyalty flowed both ways... that Viktor would stand between his men and consequences just as fiercely as he’d stand between them and enemies.</p>

<p>It was a test, Alex realized. Not of him, but for him.</p>

<p>Viktor was showing him what real leadership looked like. What it meant to command people rather than just deploy assets. What it took to earn the kind of loyalty that transcended contract and obligation.</p>

<p>Alex held Viktor’s gaze for a long moment, letting the weight of what was being offered settle fully.</p>
