<p>As soon as they sat down at the table, Luis couldn&#x27;t help but reveal a lecherous smile.</p>

<p>Glancing around the room in the evening, he saw that Hela was dressed beautifully and strikingly, but she was too busy attending to relatives to be able to get away, so Luis didn&#x27;t go over to disturb her.</p>

<p>At this table, however, there was another familiar face, Brook, a stunning woman whom Nancy would have to call sister-in-law.</p>

<p>The most crucial part was that her husband, her cousin Darian, was sitting right there beside her, and Luis couldn&#x27;t help but observe this cuckold.</p>

<p>Darian wasn&#x27;t tall either. It seemed like either a genetic mutation or a bloodline issue in their family. The previous generation were all big and tall, but by the next generation, regardless of gender, none of them were tall; in fact, they could even be called short.</p>

<p>Daniel wasn&#x27;t exactly physically impressive either, but at least he was slick and well-groomed. Coupled with his lazy demeanor, he was every inch a rich young master, which was how he managed to deceive his sister-in-law.</p>

<p>But this Darian was a different story. Calling him sly and rat-like wasn&#x27;t an exaggeration at all. There wasn&#x27;t a single hint of menace about him. Just sitting there, you could tell he was a contemptible sort.</p>

<p>As soon as he sat down, his speech was laced with a mindlessly arrogant and provocative tone, the kind that made it hard not to notice him, even if you tried.</p>

<p>Some time ago, this idiot got into a fight with someone after drinking, and with no sense of restraint, he ended up getting locked up. At least they managed to bail him out in time for this big housewarming day, though who knows how much money that cost.</p>

<p>Brook sat next to him, acting affectionately, but in reality, she paid him no attention at all.</p>

<p>According to Hela, the couple&#x27;s relationship had long since fallen apart. Whether they had children or not was irrelevant. The main issue was that this cousin was a piece of work himself.</p>

<p>Equally idle and lazy, he was particularly adept at stirring up trouble. Over the years, all the money his father had earned had been squandered by him. If it were just eating, drinking, whoring, and gambling, that might be one thing, but the constant troublemaking was unbearable.</p>

<p>It was said his father was also extremely wealthy, a shrewd businessman dealing in the jadeite trade.</p>

<p>People in the jadeite business were all tough cookies, but being tough didn&#x27;t necessarily mean you made money. His father was one of those who was both ruthless and clever.</p>

<p>In the early years, he ran a gambling rough stone business. Honestly, in that line of work, anyone who could successfully cut open a promising stone was a real expert, and his father was one of them. Plus, he ran the business well and didn&#x27;t gamble himself, so he indeed raked in a fortune.</p>

<p>When it came to building this manor, his father contributed the largest share. Although the business isn&#x27;t doing well now and nobody really gambles on rough stones anymore, a lean camel is still bigger than a horse.</p>

<p>The most consistent thing was definitely this son, a standard prodigal son who had done every kind of absurd thing imaginable.</p>

<p>They had originally hoped he&#x27;d settle down after marriage, but now it&#x27;s clear they were completely wrong. The guy was always out eating, drinking, and whoring. Brook not divorcing him was already a face-saving gesture for her in-laws.</p>

<p>Darian took a big gulp of his drink and said with a hint of disdain, &quot;Daniel, look at the business you&#x27;ve chosen. What&#x27;s the point of opening some lousy bar? You work yourself to death, swallow your pride, and for what?&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;That little bit of money you earn, what can it even do?!&quot;</p>

<p>His family was the wealthiest. In the past, Daniel might have just tolerated it.</p>

<p>But now, Daniel&#x27;s temper wouldn&#x27;t let him indulge this. He gave a cold smirk and said, &quot;Big brother, it&#x27;s still better than having nothing to do, isn&#x27;t it? At least I run my own business and manage to make a bit.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;That&#x27;s not even real money! Blood money or just hard-earned cash?&quot;</p>

<p>Darian said boorishly, &quot;Only useless people earn that kind of money. I&#x27;ve always said you just don&#x27;t get the importance of connections and vision.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;Come on, come on, have a drink!&quot;</p>

<p>Seeing an argument brewing, Brook quickly lifted her glass to intervene, a helpless, wry smile on her face.</p>

<p>Daniel wasn&#x27;t about to give her any face, but then she looked towards Luis. Luis casually raised his glass, &quot;Let&#x27;s just drink, let&#x27;s just drink. We&#x27;ll set all that aside for now.&quot;</p>

<p>Seeing this, Daniel did shut up for his sake, but Darian just gave a disdainful snort, &quot;So this is your brother-in-law, huh? That live-in son-in-law, right? What kind of pathetic thing is that?&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;What real man would become a live-in son-in-law? That&#x27;s even worse than a eunuch. At least eunuchs served the emperor.&quot;</p>

<p>Luis was certain he had never done anything to offend this guy, but the guy&#x27;s mouth was unbelievably foul. Launching into such a blanket attack was uncalled for.</p>

<p>At this point, it was clear which side everyone was on. Luis and his brother-in-law Daniel were in the same boat, and there was no way they were going to swallow this insult.</p>

<p>The moment Darian said this, the table fell into an immediate, tense silence. Everyone frowned, thinking he had gone too far.</p>

<p>Luis, however, raised his glass, maintaining a smile as he said, &quot;Cousin is right. But to be a live-in son-in-law, you at least need to look presentable. Looking at your, shall we say, &#x27;unique&#x27; features, it&#x27;s clear you just don&#x27;t have the looks for the job.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;As for me, I&#x27;m lucky enough to have a decent face and body. When I was younger, I was almost tricked into becoming a male prostitute. Luckily, I didn&#x27;t go down that wrong path, but thinking back, I guess I do have some assets.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;Life&#x27;s not too bad now. I don&#x27;t rely on my parents or my in-laws, and I still have some decent money to spend.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;Honestly, I do envy you a bit. You have nothing to do but never lack for cash. Having good parents really is important. As for whether the son is actually human or not, well, that&#x27;s not really my place to say.&quot;</p>

<p>A snort escaped Nancy, who burst out laughing. Her sister-in-law&#x27;s straightforwardness had always been her best quality.</p>

<p>This remark was a direct insult to Darian&#x27;s appearance. Normally, by a certain age, people might not care about such things, but to disregard wealth and target that was a real low blow.</p>

<p>Coupled with the jibe about living off his parents, which was deeply insulting to his pride. If it bothered Daniel in the past, it certainly bothered Darian now. Darian&#x27;s face immediately turned incredibly ugly.</p>

<p>Brook was even more embarrassed, but the look in her eyes clearly blamed her own husband for his reckless and offensive words.</p>

<p>&quot;That one&#x27;s a no-go. Mainly, importing is a hassle. Money isn&#x27;t the issue, but things like license plates are a real pain.&quot;</p>

<p>Brook, on the other hand, seemed completely indifferent, just anxious and afraid her husband would cause more trouble, her face fixed in a helpless, bitter smile.</p>
