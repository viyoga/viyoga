<p>Luis was serving them with great enthusiasm, originally thinking that his sisters-in-law were still young and, not having been stimulated by society, their vanity wouldn&#x27;t be that strong.</p>

<p>He was showing might not have much effect, but it turned out he was overthinking it; they were no longer children, and he couldn&#x27;t treat them as such.</p>

<p>Besides, what Blanche said was very true: while the city&#x27;s best high school was indeed excellent, the culture of comparison was even more severe, even though over half of the students were academic elites.</p>

<p>But honestly, aside from students from humble backgrounds, many of those top students came from well-off families. Their families could afford all sorts of tutors, using money to pile up their grades.</p>

<p>And there were quite a few sponsored students whose grades were a bit lacking, but their families were simply wealthy.</p>

<p>So while the school was strict, the atmosphere wasn&#x27;t necessarily simple. Luis remembered Blanche saying that the kids studying there didn&#x27;t wear a single pair of shoes that cost less than 50 dollars.</p>

<p>It just so happened that his sisters-in-law and Avery hadn&#x27;t crossed that line, so Luis specifically bought them both sneakers costing several hundred dollars, further closing the distance between them.</p>

<p>&quot;Brother-in-law, you really know how to set up the lighting.&quot;</p>

<p>After the two young girls finished taking pictures with the wine bottles, his sister-in-law Leah was overjoyed, flashing her brother-in-law a sweet smile.</p>

<p>&quot;Godfather, you&#x27;re so great. If it were my mom, she&#x27;d kill me for spending this much on a meal and taking pictures like this.&quot;</p>

<p>Avery was also visibly moved as she spoke.</p>

<p>Leah stuck out her tongue in embarrassment, giving her brother-in-law a sweet smile, and said, &quot;My mom would yell even worse. This is purely delaying her from eating a hot meal.&quot;</p>

<p>Before they knew it, the dark beer and Angel&#x27;s Kiss cocktails were finished. A charming flush of wine had already appeared on their faces, making them look even sweeter and more endearing under the lights.</p>

<p>The dishes were almost all served, and they ate to their hearts&#x27; content, chattering away and posting on their Moments at the same time.</p>

<p>The location tag was correct, and the first picture was particularly showy: a hand holding a dark beer mug with the caption: &quot;This cup is almost 6 dollars and so bitter, I really don&#x27;t know what&#x27;s so great about beer.&quot;</p>

<p>It seemed no woman could escape this kind of vain flexing.</p>

<p>The effect of Luis was obvious. Young girls couldn&#x27;t resist this kind of meticulous gentleness and consideration.</p>

<p>Once he opened the red wine and poured it for them, they didn&#x27;t show any sign of objection at all. Instead, Leah said coquettishly with a hint of allure, &quot;You perverted brother-in-law, making us drink so much. You&#x27;re not trying to get me drunk so you can do bad things with Avery, are you?&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;Careful, or I&#x27;ll tell my second sister and have her come catch you in the act.&quot;</p>

<p>Of course, this was a joke, the kind that even Avery, the one involved, wouldn&#x27;t believe.</p>

<p>Luis grabbed his sister-in-law&#x27;s soft, jade-like hand, caressing it as he chuckled wickedly, &quot;Brother-in-law wants to do bad things with you too. If your second sister catches us, I&#x27;ll get a divorce and take you both far away, how about that?&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;You wouldn&#x27;t dare... I don&#x27;t want to run away with you.&quot;</p>

<p>Leah glanced at her blushing, excited best friend, then at her brother-in-law, and lowered her voice, &quot;Brother-in-law, don&#x27;t make jokes like that. You know how much my sister loves you. You can&#x27;t abandon her.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;Of course not. Your brother-in-law isn&#x27;t that kind of person.&quot;</p>

<p>Luis used his silver tongue to flatter both of them until they felt light-headed. After all, they were young girls without much social experience, so they were relatively easy to please.</p>

<p>Added to that was the luxurious environment, the romantic music, and the meticulous attentiveness; it was the kind of situation where the wine alone wasn&#x27;t intoxicating, but the atmosphere made them feel tipsy.</p>

<p>They cursed their classmates and teachers, and Luis cursed right along with them, even finding new angles to criticize even more harshly.</p>

<p>This approach worked best. They were as happy as if they&#x27;d found a kindred spirit, chatting more and more with Luis, thoroughly delighted.</p>

<p>Before they realized it, the entire bottle of red wine was gone. Split three ways, it wasn&#x27;t actually that much, but they had already drunk the dark beer and cocktails earlier. Now, their eyes held a misty, dazed look.</p>

<p>Their stomachs were full, but their spirits were still high. In fact, their enthusiasm for drinking seemed even greater than Luis&#x27;s, which was a pleasant surprise for him.</p>

<p>&quot;Let&#x27;s go back to the room to drink!&quot;</p>

<p>His suggestion was shyly accepted. After all, since they had come this far, they might as well enjoy themselves thoroughly.</p>

<p>The manager led the way to the ocean-view suite. As soon as they entered, they couldn&#x27;t help but gasp in amazement. The room was huge, at least around a thousand square feet.</p>

<p>In the center was a custom-made king-size bed, almost as big as two double beds combined. The bathroom was also exceptionally spacious, bright, and luxurious.</p>

<p>&quot;This is so amazing!&quot;</p>

<p>They ran to the floor-to-ceiling windows cheering, then sat down in the chairs by the window, happily taking pictures of the night view with their phones.</p>

<p>All evening, Luis had been subtly appealing to their budding vanity, and it seemed quite successful. Before long, food ordered from the restaurant was also brought in on a service cart.</p>

<p>Western-style dishes came on large plates with small portions, emphasizing delicacy over quantity. They weren&#x27;t really full, so continuing to eat and drink wasn&#x27;t a problem.</p>

<p>&quot;So beautiful.&quot;</p>

<p>In addition, there was an even more extravagant seafood platter. The seafood platters found elsewhere usually contain ordinary items available at any market.</p>

<p>But this one had king crab, giant spider crab, mantis shrimp, huge tiger prawns...</p>

<p>Leah said a bit nervously, &quot;Brother-in-law, that&#x27;s so much food. It must be really expensive. What if we can&#x27;t finish it?&quot;</p>

<p>Luis seized the opportunity to hold her hand, looking at her with affectionate eyes. &quot;As long as you two can take even one bite of these things, they have value. You are the priceless treasures in my heart.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;So annoying! You&#x27;re such a liar!&quot;</p>

<p>His sister-in-law shyly lowered her head, but this time, she didn&#x27;t pull her hand away.</p>

<p>&quot;Hey, hey, if you two are going to hook up, don&#x27;t do it right in front of me.&quot;</p>

<p>This time, Avery pouted, as if jealous, and said, &quot;Don&#x27;t you know about first come, first served? I was here first.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;What, do you think this is the olden days? My second sister still has to agree whether she&#x27;ll take you as a concubine.&quot;</p>

<p>They joked around happily, still taking pictures, selecting angles with great excitement.</p>

<p>They could post to their Moments many times. Posting everything all at once would be a waste, so they chattered seriously about the strategy.</p>

<p>&quot;Leah, Avery, cheers!&quot;</p>

<p>Luis raised his glass. This time, they were drinking red wine again; he had ordered two bottles. They raised their glasses joyfully and clinked them.</p>

<p>&quot;Oh no... I forgot to block my mom!&quot;</p>
