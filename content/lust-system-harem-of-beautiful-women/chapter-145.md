<p>This time, Ruth slammed the table and said, &quot;Daniel said your in-laws already know about this. What are you planning to do about it?&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;Mom, I can handle it myself. Why are you bothering with her?&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;I won’t bother? You’re my daughter; how can I not bother?&quot;</p>

<p>Ruth became even angrier: &quot;Today, I called your mother-in-law, originally intending to confront her, but she brought this up right away. Mom has lost all face and couldn’t even stand up for you.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;The key is, Mom received many calls, and their tone was very aggressive, just like debt collectors.&quot;</p>

<p>Nancy had a headache and couldn’t bear to listen any longer. She turned around and headed upstairs.</p>

<p>Ruth shouted, &quot;What are you going upstairs for?&quot;</p>

<p>Nancy said angrily, &quot;To sleep, obviously! There’s nothing to say about this matter. I’ll handle my own affairs.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;Nonsense! Little Sister’s classmate is here; there’s no room for you to sleep.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;None of your business.&quot;</p>

<p>The mother and daughter argued, parting on bad terms. What Luis appreciated more was the beautiful sight of his mother-in-law breathing heavily in anger, her chest rising and falling.</p>

<p>&quot;Let me go up and take a look!&quot;</p>

<p>Lily quickly followed.</p>

<p>For a moment, only Luis and his mother-in-law were left in the living room. Mother-in-law Ruth: favorability meter 88%, emotions: angry, stifled, depressed.</p>

<p>At least she waited until her younger sister and the classmate weren’t around before making a scene. Probably Yana had also advised her; otherwise, with his mother-in-law’s temperament, she wouldn’t have been so reasonable.</p>

<p>Luis immediately grabbed her hand. Under his mother-in-law’s shy and reproachful gaze, he kissed her jade-like hand and said softly, &quot;Mom, don’t be angry. I’ll go up and see what’s going on.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;Big Sister is right. If you two with your tempers sleep in the same room, you’ll tear the house down.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;I wish they were as worry-free as you are.&quot;</p>

<p>Ruth sighed.</p>

<p>Luis looked at the beautiful, charming mother-in-law before him and said hoarsely, &quot;Mom, wait for me. I’ll come to you in a little while.&quot;</p>

<p>It seemed the sister-in-law’s mission couldn’t be completed tonight. This was a heaven-sent opportunity; he must conquer his mother-in-law tonight. Luis had secretly made up his mind.</p>

<p>Upstairs, the sister-in-law must have heard some noise. To avoid being implicated, she closed her door tightly.</p>

<p>Luis pushed open the master bedroom door. Nancy was lying on the bed in the spot that originally belonged to Luis, sulking. Lily stood nearby, somewhat at a loss and unsure what to do.</p>

<p>An eldest sister is like a mother. Whether Nancy has a mother’s nurturing heart is unclear, but she certainly has the overbearing aura of the Mother-in-law. At the very least, her two younger sisters are especially obedient under her domineering influence.</p>

<p>&quot;Big Sister, don’t overthink things. If there’s anything to discuss, we can talk tomorrow.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;Lily, you sleep with Big Sister tonight. Mom is also very upset. I’ve already persuaded her to go back to her room.&quot;</p>

<p>Lily thought this was the best way to handle the situation. What if both parties were still angry and started arguing again? Especially since her mother had drunk quite a bit of alcohol.</p>

<p>&quot;Honey, let me prepare some bedding for you.&quot;</p>

<p>In her mind, her husband was going to sleep on the sofa again.</p>

<p>Luis simply shook his head and said, &quot;No need. I have to go to the internet cafe tonight. You all should go to bed early.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;Oh, alright. Then make sure to rest. Tomorrow is a holiday for everyone else. I’ll ask Big Sister to accompany me to buy some things.&quot;</p>

<p>Lily didn’t think much of it, but as soon as Nancy heard this, she immediately turned around and gave Luis a meaningful look.</p>

<p>Luis secretly responded with a lewd smile, then pretended to put on his clothes and went downstairs. Mother-in-law Ruth sat in the living room in a daze for a moment, and Lily followed him down to explain that Luis had something to attend to.</p>

<p>Luis put on his shoes and went out, but he didn’t go far. Instead, he hid in the fire escape stairwell, waiting.</p>

<p>He sent a message to his Mother-in-law: &quot;Don’t lock the door. Open it for me once Lily goes upstairs. Mom, I want to sleep with you tonight.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;I know...&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;What, is Big Sister jealous??&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;Then please take good care of her, Big Sister. An elder sister is like a mother. You have to tell Lily not to be so conservative and to learn more skills. Otherwise, I’ll really suffocate to death.&quot;</p>

<p>Nancy was probably too upset now to even respond to Luis’s teasing.</p>

<p>This message made Luis overjoyed. He quietly pushed the door open and found it was indeed unlocked. Luis immediately tiptoed inside, closed the door, and locked it.</p>

<p>Carrying his shoes and walking on tiptoe like a thief, he passed through the living room, dimly lit only by a nightlight. At that moment, his Mother-in-law’s door seemed like the gates of heaven.</p>

<p>As soon as he opened it, he saw Mother-in-law Ruth sitting at the head of the bed, wearing cool and sexy pajamas. She looked at him with a smile on her face, but there was also a hint of melancholy that was hard to detect.</p>

<p>Mother-in-law Ruth: Favorability 88%, Emotions: conflicted, helpless, confused.</p>

<p>None of the three indicators showed anything related to lust. Although she definitely knew what her son-in-law intended to do, Ruth, who had drunk half a bottle of red wine, was clearly preoccupied with her eldest daughter.</p>
