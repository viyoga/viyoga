<p>With his mother complex... he was no longer bothered by Deewa blocking him, because he’d started figuring out how to make his dreams a reality.</p>

<p>&quot;Hello, Manager Chen!&quot;</p>

<p>Following Iron Ox’s instructions, the young influencer pressed him about the matter during pillow talk—while also blowing him under the covers. The contempt in her eyes was fleeting and well-hidden.</p>

<p>David made the call, then his eyes widened. &quot;What? Four days? Are you kidding me? I thought the sales office was officially opening on Tuesday!&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;That’s correct. But Mr. Zhang has given orders. No one is allowed on site for the first two days. His friends have booked it for an exclusive shoot.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;Seriously? What the hell!&quot;</p>

<p>After confirming the details, they’d be able to shoot in four days.</p>

<p>David didn’t think much of it and passed on the news. But this wasn’t something Mira could accept—not even Iron Ox.</p>

<p>The reason was simple: in the influencer industry, you’re riding the wave of the times. A special theme or catching the right trend can make you blow up overnight.</p>

<p>But trends are fickle. Unless you deliberately create one—which costs a fortune with no guaranteed return—no one in their right mind tries to force it.</p>

<p>At Vivid Media’s office, Iron Ox was anxious when he got the call. &quot;Young Master David, you’re joking, right? Another four days? Didn’t you say the opening was tomorrow?&quot;</p>

<p>Tiger Real Estate had been running nonstop local TV and mall ads for the past few days.</p>

<p>The only villa district in the old city had generated a lot of buzz. They’d sent people out to hand out flyers everywhere—everyone knew about it.</p>

<p>Villas in new developments are usually in the suburbs. But City Garden’s Phase 1 and 2 combined already made it the largest community in the old city.</p>

<p>Adding a villa district now had everyone amazed—Tiger Real Estate was still the same powerhouse. It was also building hype for their upcoming IPO.</p>

<p>Behind City Garden was Zhongshan Park. Nearby were the prestigious Xiongzi Primary School and Bathek City No. 1 Primary School.</p>

<p>Across the street was the bustling Wanda Plaza. To the right were a bank and a wet market—the most complete and convenient living amenities.</p>

<p>Phase 1 was a bit noisy since it was a redevelopment area, but that didn’t affect the tranquility of Phase 2 and the villa district.</p>

<p>Both Iron Ox and Deewa had chosen this location because of Tiger Real Estate’s massive promotional push.</p>

<p>&quot;Young Master David, four days is way too long!!&quot;</p>

<p>Iron Ox pleaded. &quot;Can you talk to them? We could shoot at the same time without interfering with each other.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;I’ll ask.&quot;</p>

<p>David hung up. On the other end, a muffled &quot;oh&quot; suggested he’d just been blown.</p>

<p>Mira’s face was ice-cold. She gritted her teeth. &quot;That little pervert. Rabbit slept with him last night, and after getting what he wanted, he still says we have to wait four days.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;Bastard. Yesterday he was all promises, turns out he’s just a spoiled brat with no real pull at home.&quot;</p>

<p>Iron Ox was seething too.</p>

<p>The most basic rule in this industry is understanding that the internet has no memory.</p>

<p>Trends come and go unpredictably. Most are fleeting—like beachcombing, cooking tutorials, or outdoor barbecues.</p>

<p>Memes like &quot;Subject Three&quot; or &quot;You’re So Beautiful&quot; lasting that long are the rare exceptions.</p>

<p>Though there are many niches, other trends can blow up overnight with no warning, and you never know how long they’ll last.</p>

<p>But as an industry professional, the key is to follow the trend. It’s like swimming upstream—fall behind and you’re left in the dust. Follow and you have a chance to blow up.</p>

<p>Mira gritted her teeth. &quot;That little bastard. But who booked it? Could it be Blossom Media?&quot;</p>

<p>At that, Iron Ox’s expression darkened. &quot;Could be. There’s no other real competition in the city right now.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;Damn it. We’ll go check it out tomorrow and see for ourselves.&quot;</p>

<p>Iron Ox was so furious he slammed the table. Who could afford to wait four days? Who knows how long this trend will last?</p>

<p>It takes a full day of shooting and an all-nighter to edit enough videos, especially with so many small influencers waiting for their turn to appear.</p>

<p>If your videos come out after the trend has passed, you’d be crying tears of blood.</p>

<p>&quot;Hey, I asked. They said no—too many people. Nothing I can do.&quot;</p>

<p>After David hung up, Iron Ox erupted in curses. Mira grabbed her bag and stood up. &quot;Mr. Wang, now’s not the time to be angry.&quot;</p>

<p>He calmed down and reluctantly called another smaller real estate company.</p>

<p>Four days was absolutely too long to wait. He’d have to settle for an ordinary residential project to shoot. It was frustrating to miss out on Tiger Real Estate’s buzz.</p>

<p>On the other side, the mood was completely celebratory.</p>

<p>At the Taiga family’s private restaurant, Dominic Jr. was pouring drinks with such enthusiasm that his guests felt almost overwhelmed.</p>

<p>Luis had brought Deewa and Nancy along, with three cameramen in tow.</p>

<p>At the dining table, the restaurant manager and cameramen were busy filming each dish. Dominic Jr. raised his glass with a smile and said, &quot;Then we’ll be counting on your company to help promote our private kitchen.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;Not at all. The Taiga family’s private kitchen is already famous—reservations need to be made at least a week in advance. We’re hardly in a position to promote it.&quot;</p>

<p>Deewa raised her glass with a smile and said, &quot;We should be thanking you, Mr. Zhang, for giving us the opportunity to broaden our horizons.&quot;</p>

<p>Compared to her social grace, the aloof Nancy was less articulate, but she gave a soft, charming smile and clinked her glass as well.</p>

<p>Since they were local influencers, their content naturally included restaurant reviews of all kinds.</p>

<p>They didn’t do the kind of promotional fluff that only praises; they provided objective reviews to attract viewers.</p>

<p>The Taiga family’s private kitchen wasn’t widely known to the public, but its luxurious decor and the chef’s skill would definitely catch people’s attention.</p>
