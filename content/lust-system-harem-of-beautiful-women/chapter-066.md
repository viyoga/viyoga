<p>Daniel’s face was already stormy with irritation. The restaurant owner quickly followed after Luis, carrying out a big platter of prime rib strips for the grill, laughing warmly:</p>

<p>&quot;Ruth, your son-in-law really saved me today. Without him, I’d have been in a real mess. This one’s on the house—please, don’t turn it down.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;Aiyo, we’re neighbors, how can you call it a favor.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;Not at all. Which family around here doesn’t have a college graduate? But how many of them are truly capable?&quot;</p>

<p>Aunt Fatty put down the complimentary tripe, laughing heartily as she said, &quot;But your son-in-law’s skill level is just too awesome. Someone was criticizing his education background before—what bullshit. Lack of a diploma isn’t important; lack of culture is what’s scary.&quot;</p>

<p>After all this commotion, the way the mother and three daughters looked at Luis had changed. They increasingly felt that Luis was exceptionally cultured.</p>

<p>Daniel, however, was furious watching Luis steal the spotlight and couldn’t help but say, &quot;If Luis is so capable and has earned so much money, how come he didn’t think of buying a car back when Mom was being wronged?&quot;</p>

<p>This statement sounded very reasonable. The problem was, after this incident, the attitudes of the mother and three daughters were no longer influenced by any preconceived notions.</p>

<p>Especially the look in the Mother In Law’s eyes when she glanced over—it held no doubt anymore, as if she was certain her second son-in-law would provide a satisfactory answer.</p>

<p>Luis lit a cigarette and said lightly, &quot;Brother-in-law, all these things that happened today—you didn’t know about them either, but that doesn’t mean I did. How many years has it been since I married into the family?&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;I didn’t understand the wrongs Mom suffered. A live-in son-in-law like me has to keep his head down; how could I inquire about so much?&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;As for things like cars, no one ever told me.&quot;</p>

<p>Luis added with a chuckle, &quot;But you, Brother-in-law, as the favored eldest son-in-law, you should have reminded me about these things.&quot;</p>

<p>This statement, using retreat as advance, was almost pointing a finger and cursing him outright: You, the eldest son-in-law from a well-off family, didn’t handle this, and now you have the nerve to ask me?</p>

<p>It’s hard to generalize about women when it comes to major right and wrong, but when it comes to trivial matters, they will absolutely keep things crystal clear.</p>

<p>Sure enough, Nancy’s delicate eyebrows furrowed. &quot;Daniel, this can’t really be Luis’s fault, can it? Even I didn’t know the specific situation at home after I married out.&quot;</p>

<p>Seeing even his wife defect, Daniel almost felt like vomiting blood. He immediately said, &quot;That’s not what I meant. I meant that since he has money now, how could he let your mom suffer these wrongs and only bring up buying a car now?&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;It’s been two years, right? With that money, why pretend to be poor...&quot;</p>

<p>This once again seemed to hit a key point. No matter how foolish Daniel might be, his ability to grasp the main point was relatively strong.</p>

<p>However, he underestimated one thing: Luis, who had always lived under someone else’s roof and endured constant mistreatment, might seem like a quiet gourd, but in reality, he had far more cunning than Daniel. Their different upbringings dictated this; it was impossible for someone like Luis to harbor the naive notions of those who’ve lived privileged lives.</p>

<p>Moreover, Luis knew how to cater to their preferences. His method of dealing with Daniel was like using a blunt knife to cut meat—ensuring Daniel felt uncomfortable every time he picked a fight.</p>

<p>Luis sighed and said, &quot;When I got married, it wasn’t that I had no money. At that time, policies in various countries were changing rapidly, and many blockchain assets were experiencing violent fluctuations. It wasn’t a good time; I had to wait and observe.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;Besides those, there were also overseas investments. The amount wasn’t huge, but it was all real money.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;Just like this current 50,000 dollar withdrawal. If I didn’t cash it out, it might only be 40,000 dollars by the time we finish this meal.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;Those investments from a few days ago were sitting there. Plus, some contracts can’t be withdrawn; they have time limits and involve leverage. Brother-in-law probably doesn’t understand these things.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;To put it simply, that stock I have is worth 400,000 dollars now, but in six months, it’ll be 600,000 dollars.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;As for cashing this out, if I don’t sell, 50,000 dollars could drop to less than 10,000 dollars in a month.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;Some are more complicated. Two years ago, it was worth less than 10,000 dollars, and now it’s about 360,000 dollars, and I can’t bear to sell it.&quot;</p>

<p>Adopting a tone of admitting fault, Luis immediately said with a chuckle, &quot;This might be a bit hard to understand. In short, I’m a money grubber. The past few years were a stable period where I couldn’t move things.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;It’s all about profit margins, really. Actually, the money in the stock market isn’t that much either...&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;Mainly blockchain, various contracts...&quot;</p>

<p>Luis pretended to blank out for a moment, then immediately said with a laugh, &quot;Let’s not talk about this anymore. It’s too exhausting. Some things involve accumulating little into much, others involve making money from foreign exchange rate differences—it’s all a bit messy.&quot;</p>

<p>Hearing this, Ruth, who already held him in high esteem, directly slammed the table. &quot;What are you asking all this for? What do any of you understand? How many times have I told you all? It’s not that we’re saying women can’t do things, but if you can’t earn money yourself, don’t stand to the side talking nonsense.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;Luis had no money before, right? But he invested all his hard-earned money and didn’t squander it. From the time he married Lily until now, he hasn’t been to a bar even once.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;Now that he has money, he’s spending it on his wife, on me, his Mother In Law, what’s the problem with that? So much nonsense.&quot;</p>

<p>After this continuous output, Daniel picked up his car keys and left, without even saying goodbye to his Mother In Law.</p>

<p>Ruth directly cursed, pointing at Nancy and saying, &quot;You see? This is what you call a highly educated family? He left without even saying a word to me. Is he mentally ill? Does our whole family have to fawn over him?&quot;</p>

<p>Furious, Nancy called him, but he hung up immediately.</p>

<p>She was also extremely angry and immediately tried to soothe her, &quot;Mom, don’t be angry. He’s just been spoiled rotten. He’s the petty type.&quot;</p>

<p>Ruth snorted and said, &quot;I can see that. Really no upbringing. Tell him to learn more from Luis in the future. smart, low-key, and knows how to care for people.&quot;</p>

<p>Nancy suddenly asked, &quot;By the way, Luis, how do you know so much?&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;Stock Education is foundational. Useless without it. But it’s also exhausting. I have to get up in the middle of the night to look at all kinds of data.&quot;</p>

<p>By this point, Luis naturally couldn’t avoid showing off a bit.</p>

<p>After eating and drinking their fill and paying the bill, Nancy seemed to look at him differently.</p>

<p>Annoyingly, the mission related to her still hadn’t been triggered. What the hell? Luis was eagerly looking forward to putting a green hat on Daniel, to properly vent the resentment he’d accumulated over the years.</p>

<p>As they were leaving, the table of foreigners at the entrance was drinking joyously. They insistently pulled Luis into a very lively conversation. After having a few drinks and exchanging contact information for the future, Luis finally left.</p>

<p>Nancy was staying at her parents’ house today. Given Daniel’s attitude today, with her temper, she wouldn’t indulge him.</p>

<p>Luis and Lily were already habitually holding hands, fingers interlaced. Lily, looking blissful and intoxicated, hugged her husband’s arm, chatting and laughing happily with her mother.</p>

<p>Nancy suddenly asked, &quot;Luis, what were those foreigners saying earlier that got them so excited?&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;Oh... they’re here on a business inspection trip. The translator their group sent turns out he’s half-baked and completely useless—got chewed out and sent back.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;They asked if I could be their translator. One thousand dollars a day.&quot;</p>

<p>Ruth’s eyes lit up. &quot;Goodness, that’s 30,000 dollars a month then...&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;That’s way better than being some crappy accountant! And still needing connections? Pah! Really treating the three of us women like fools.&quot;</p>

<p>Stimulated by alcohol, Ruth didn’t hold back even with her eldest daughter. &quot;Nancy, you’ve been fooled by him. That guy’s mouth can really say anything.&quot;</p>

<p>Nancy sighed but didn’t refute it. One’s own matters are only known to oneself.</p>

<p>Seeing his sister-in-law’s melancholy and somewhat helpless appearance, perhaps she also felt some regret. Luis was inwardly anxious, wondering why the mission still hadn’t been released.</p>

<p>Damn it, his heart was utterly sincere in its eagerness to cuckold Daniel.</p>

<p>Upon arriving home, Nancy wiped the sweat from her little nose and said, &quot;It’s so hot, Mom. We really should install an air conditioner in the living room. Our living room isn’t that big, and if we just close the kitchen sliding door, it’ll get nice and cool.&quot;</p>

<p>The Wood family’s oddly shaped duplex was indeed quite small. A low-power standing air conditioner would definitely be sufficient.</p>

<p>After hearing her daughter’s words, Ruth rolled her eyes directly and said, &quot;Things don’t just appear by flapping your lips. How about you and Lily split the cost, half each?&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;I’ll have to wait until the end of the month. I told you I’m short on cash right now.&quot;</p>

<p>When the topic turned to money, Nancy became a bit irritable, her face showing clear helplessness.</p>
