<p>Soon, teachers from nearby came over to intervene, including Harvey Zhang.</p>

<p>&quot;This, this, it was just a pat on the butt. What’s the big deal?!&quot;</p>

<p>The fat woman stammered after watching the footage.</p>

<p>Principal Ma said grimly, &quot;And there’s more. You all remember this yourselves.&quot;</p>

<p>The screen switched to the surveillance footage from the first-year disciplinary office. This time, there was not only video but also audio, clearly capturing everyone’s conversation.</p>

<p>&quot;Director Zhang, how can you blatantly lie like this!&quot;</p>

<p>Principal Ma slammed the table, furious. &quot;As a teacher at this school, this is how you educate our students?&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;Openly covering for your friend’s child—you’re quite something. Next, will you be covering for school bullying as well...&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;No, Principal Ma, I didn’t see what happened at the beginning!&quot;</p>

<p>Harvey Zhang, pale and sweating profusely, quickly said, &quot;I didn’t mean to. I would never try to frame Leah.&quot;</p>

<p>Principal Ma said indignantly, &quot;You can get out now. I won’t make a decision on your punishment unilaterally. I’ll report this to the district education bureau for a meeting to decide.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;This, this, isn’t that too much?!&quot;</p>

<p>Harvey Zhang was dumbfounded.</p>

<p>He would have preferred to sweep this under the rug, even take the beating without complaint, as long as things didn’t escalate further.</p>

<p>If it were handled internally, it would have been a slap on the wrist. He never expected the reputation-conscious Principal Ma to voluntarily report it.</p>

<p>In such cases, the consequences were usually severe—he could even lose his position.</p>

<p>Principal Ma glared at him and said, &quot;Go back now and wait for the result. Are you trying to interfere with my work by staying here?&quot;</p>

<p>That was a serious statement. Harvey Zhang broke out in a cold sweat, quickly agreed, and tried to leave.</p>

<p>At that moment, the fat woman spoke up anxiously, &quot;Hold on! So he just gets away with beating people up?!&quot;</p>

<p>Gasle remained silent. A young officer produced a document and said, &quot;Director Zhang, this is our police report. We’ve classified this as an altercation between both parties.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;If you have no objections, please sign it.&quot;</p>

<p>At this point, Harvey Zhang had no objections. Seeing that the report stated each party was responsible for their own medical expenses, he quickly signed and left.</p>

<p>The fat woman panicked. &quot;We’re both injured like this, and you’re not even arresting him?!&quot;</p>

<p>Gasle, still pretending not to know Luis, cleared his throat and said seriously, &quot;Ma’am, we’ve reviewed the footage. The verbal provocation started on your side.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;But he threw the first punch!&quot;</p>

<p>The fat woman was completely unconvinced.</p>

<p>Gasle explained patiently, &quot;Both sides were initially at fault. You also retaliated, and there were three of you. Your son Zion also threw objects and attacked.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;If we’re going to press charges, Director Zhang’s head injury is partly his responsibility.&quot;</p>

<p>The fat woman was furious. &quot;So what? We’re the ones who got hurt!&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;Enough!&quot;</p>

<p>Principal Ma, usually refined and composed, slammed the table. &quot;Being injured doesn’t make you right. Your son sexually harassed a female student and then lied to slander her.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;The school will hold a meeting to decide whether to expel your son.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;No. 1 High School is a place for education. We will not tolerate such bad apples!!!&quot;</p>

<p>Hearing the word &quot;expulsion,&quot; the fat woman’s face showed clear panic. &quot;You can’t do that! He has exams next year! And when we got him in through the back door, we spent our life savings!&quot;</p>

<p>A sponsored student... Principal Ma narrowed his eyes and said, &quot;Is that so? Who did you give your money to? I welcome you to report it.&quot;</p>

<p>Realizing she’d misspoken, the fat woman quickly waved her hands. &quot;No, no, I misspoke. What I meant was my child...&quot;</p>

<p>Principal Ma’s presence was indeed commanding—she immediately started stammering and rambling.</p>

<p>Gasle glanced over and subtly signaled. The officer walked over and said, &quot;Ma’am, this is our police report. Take a look and sign it if there are no issues.&quot;</p>

<p>Seeing that each party was responsible for their own medical expenses, the fat woman said resentfully, &quot;You’re all heartless and biased! Why aren’t you arresting him when we’re the ones hurt like this?&quot;</p>

<p>Gasle finally stepped forward and said matter-of-factly, &quot;This is classified as a mutual altercation. This is a standard mediation result.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;Besides, the medical expenses were covered by the school. You didn’t pay anything.&quot;</p>

<p>The fat woman continued to argue, &quot;So what? I’m bruised, my son is hurt like this. He has to take responsibility!&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;That’s beyond our jurisdiction. If this is a financial dispute, I suggest you take legal action.&quot;</p>

<p>The officer beside her was also getting impatient and said coldly, &quot;This is our ruling. If you’re unsatisfied, you can appeal or file a complaint against us.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;Oh, you think I’m afraid of you? Who do you think you’re putting on airs for?!&quot;</p>

<p>The fat woman flew into a rage, tore up the agreement, threw it at the officer’s face, and shoved him. &quot;I’ve got you figured out! You’re all in cahoots, out to get me, aren’t you!&quot;</p>

<p>Luis couldn’t help but snort. This bitch had some nerve.</p>

<p>Leah had stopped crying. She wiped her tears, her red-rimmed eyes stealing glances, trying hard to hold back a laugh.</p>

<p>During the Old Peter incident, and the time Luis fought with David, she’d already met Gasle several times.</p>

<p>She wasn’t face-blind—she recognized him as her brother-in-law’s friend. But Luis quickly grabbed her and signaled with his eyes.</p>

<p>The sharp little girl immediately played along, pretending not to know him, acting pitiful and innocent. It was the smartest and most devious move.</p>

<p>The officer, shoved by the woman, immediately changed his expression, assuming a standard stance and snapping, &quot;Stop right there! Do you know you’re assaulting a police officer?!&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;Oh, you’re scaring me? Who do you think you are, strutting around in that uniform...&quot;</p>

<p>The fat woman was aggressive, not the type to roll on the ground and throw a tantrum. She stepped forward again, reaching out to shove him.</p>

<p>The young officer didn’t let her get away with it. He pulled out his pepper spray and sprayed her directly. The fat woman immediately clutched her eyes and screamed, rolling on the ground, &quot;Ah! My eyes! My eyes...&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;Police brutality! Police are killing people!&quot;</p>

<p>Her shrieks were as piercing as a pig being slaughtered. Principal Ma’s face darkened—he hated this kind of hysterical commotion the most.</p>

<p>Gasle immediately ordered, &quot;She assaulted an officer. Cuff her!&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;Yes, sir!!&quot;</p>

<p>Two officers rushed over, grabbed her arms, cuffed her, and pulled her up.</p>

<p>&quot;Principal Ma, have someone else come pick up this child.&quot;</p>

<p>Gasle looked around and added, &quot;You all witnessed the assault on the officer. Please make a copy of the surveillance footage from your office and give it to me.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;No problem!&quot;</p>

<p>Watching the fat woman being taken away, Principal Ma immediately called someone to make arrangements.</p>

<p>They took the pale-faced Zion to another office. After copying the surveillance footage, Luis, along with his sister-in-law Leah and Principal Ma, went to give their statements as witnesses at the scene.</p>

<p>Principal Ma finished his statement first, then said very solemnly, &quot;Mr. Luis, Leah, rest assured, I will give you a resolution very soon.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;We will not tolerate Harvey Zhang’s behavior. No. 1 High School has no room for such bad apples.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;Leah, you’ve been wronged today. I’ll have a good talk with your homeroom teacher when I get back.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;Tomorrow is Friday. I think you should take the day off and rest. If your studies are affected, I’ll arrange for a teacher to give you make-up lessons.&quot;</p>

<p>The attitude was surprisingly good. Leah felt a bit flattered and quickly said, &quot;Thank you, Principal Ma.&quot;</p>

<p>Principal Ma let out a long sigh of relief, looked up, and said earnestly, &quot;Mr. Luis, I’ll take my leave now.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;Bye bye!!!&quot;</p>

<p>As soon as he left, Leah said happily, &quot;Principal Ma is usually so stern and strict. Everyone’s afraid of him. I didn’t expect him to be so fair and just.&quot;</p>

<p>Luis rolled his eyes. &quot;Come on. He’s just afraid of the trouble if I make a big deal out of it.&quot;</p>

<p>Hearing that, Leah realized he was right. Without her brother-in-law, she would have been bullied badly today, and Principal Ma probably wouldn’t have handled it so seriously.</p>

<p>After they finished giving their statements, Gasle walked them all the way to the car. Luis handed him a cigarette. &quot;Chief Gasle, what’s going to happen to that fat woman?&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;Not enough for criminal charges. The prosecutor would probably kick it back, or if her family gets a half-decent lawyer, they’d drop it.&quot;</p>

<p>Gasle took the cigarette and lit it, smiling. &quot;But we’re still filing a case. She’ll be locked up for at least a month or so.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;Thanks a lot!!!&quot;</p>

<p>Luis nodded with a smile.</p>

<p>Gasle said with a teasing grin, &quot;Mr. Luis, Principal Ma was pretty cooperative too. Looks like that phone call was your doing.&quot;</p>

<p>Leah, obediently holding her brother-in-law’s arm, had been panicked and confused earlier, but now that he mentioned it, she remembered a detail.</p>

<p>Principal Ma’s attitude hadn’t been nearly as accommodating at first. It wasn’t until he got a phone call that he completely shifted sides.</p>

<p>Though he’d said little, she’d clearly heard him say, &quot;Yes, yes, I’ll handle it. Harvey Zhang is at the hospital. I’ll deal with him severely.&quot;</p>
