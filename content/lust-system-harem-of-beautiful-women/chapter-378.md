<p>Bella, an extraordinary woman from the newly emerging powerful Leon family in Bathek City, a family of highly educated intellectuals.</p>

<p>She possessed progressive, even radical, ideas, a true wealthy heiress, and was an opponent that even the seasoned tycoon Dominic found troublesome.</p>

<p>Even though the Leon family was slightly inferior to the Taiga family in terms of connections and deep-rooted influence, with her personal capabilities, she could still fight to a standstill.</p>

<p>This absolutely counted as being outstanding among people. Luis had heard about it back when he was injured, this boss’s wife was highly capable and also had an exceptionally strong temper.</p>

<p>Even with Sebastian Ye backing her up, every time she was confronted like this, Bella seemed not to resent it but was rather immersed in it. Luis even began to suspect she might have a masochistic streak that just hadn’t been awakened yet.</p>

<p>&quot;You’re joking. I remember both your cars were smashed, and you’ve only bought one now.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;With so many people in the family, it probably isn’t enough. How about another 911...&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;No need...&quot;</p>

<p>Before she could finish, Luis directly hung up the phone. He truly had little interest in this particular... scenario.</p>

<p>A strictly, rigidly straight lesbian really offered little hope. Sometimes people’s prejudices were just fucking stupid. As someone clear-headed about the world, Luis didn’t want to do anything foolish.</p>

<p>Honestly, every time her call came, Luis once again refreshed his understanding of Sebastian Ye’s power. If he weren’t staying clear-headed, he might even start to despise Bella’s attitude.</p>

<p>But from another perspective, being able to make both major local powers lower their arrogant heads...</p>

<p>The terrifying extent of Sebastian Ye’s influence was something unimaginable to a person at the bottom of society like Luis. That so-called &quot;father hack&quot; now seemed to be worth its price.</p>

<p>&quot;Luis, why didn’t you go for a follow-up check?&quot;</p>

<p>Just as he was thinking about this, Hail’s call came through. In a warm, gentle voice, he said, &quot;Even if the wound is superficial, you still need to pay attention to hygiene and other aspects.&quot;</p>

<p>For a moment, Luis felt incredibly flattered. He hadn’t expected Hail to be so attentive to his condition.</p>

<p>Previously, Luis had no real concept of the word &quot;secretary.&quot; Now, upon closer understanding, this Hail was certainly no easy character either.</p>

<p>To say he was second to one and above ten thousand was no exaggeration at all. To put it bluntly, if Mayor Waller wasn’t around, he was the mayor.</p>

<p>&quot;I had a check-up at the community clinic. It’s been disinfected and is almost ready for suture removal.&quot;</p>

<p>Luis quickly explained. On the other end, Hail gave a slow, gentle laugh and said, &quot;When are you free in the next couple of days? I’d like to see how your wound is healing. A major hospital is still more reassuring.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;In a couple of days. There seems to be a lawsuit needing pre-trial mediation tomorrow or the day after. I still need to go inquire about the situation!&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;A lawsuit? What lawsuit?&quot;</p>

<p>Hail was clearly taken aback upon hearing this.</p>

<p>&quot;Just a minor dispute. My sister-in-law got tricked by someone. It’s not a big deal...&quot;</p>

<p>Before Luis could even finish his sentence, Hail pondered for a moment and said, &quot;Let’s meet first. I want to understand the situation.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;It’s like this... I can’t really explain it clearly myself. When would you have time? I’ll have the lawyer speak with you.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;My schedule is quite packed today. At six, I have an hour free. Send me the address.&quot;</p>

<p>Luis had just received notice about that bastard Anglo’s matter. His teacher’s wife, Winslet’s, assistant had informed him professionally out of duty. He actually didn’t need to make a special trip.</p>

<p>This was clearly a nuisance lawsuit, best ignored. The main thing was he had other matters tonight. This was an excuse Luis had come up with off the top of his head. He hadn’t expected Hail to become so concerned upon hearing it.</p>

<p>After some thought, Luis first called his teacher’s wife, Winslet, and then headed over to the law firm.</p>

<p>In the simple yet cluttered office, Teacher’s Wife Winslet was still discussing matters with other lawyers.</p>

<p>As soon as he entered, the assistant lowered her voice and said, &quot;Brother Luis, your teacher’s wife is in a bad mood. You should comfort her in a bit.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;What happened to Teacher’s Wife?&quot;</p>

<p>Luis asked with concern.</p>

<p>In Luis’s impression, Teacher’s Wife Winslet had always been a cheerful and gentle woman. Even though her profession was law, she never gave people an aggressive feeling.</p>

<p>&quot;Ah, it’s about your master!&quot;</p>

<p>The assistant lowered her voice even further, gossiping, &quot;I think their divorce is pretty much imminent.&quot;</p>

<p>Before he could inquire further in detail, Winslet had already returned. She probably noticed the two whispering and immediately asked, &quot;What are you two doing standing so close? Talking about me behind my back?&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;Teacher’s Wife, what are you saying? How could we possibly speak ill of you?&quot;</p>

<p>Luis immediately smiled an honest smile.</p>

<p>The assistant, however, stuck out her tongue and said playfully, &quot;Master, I was just asking Brother Luis if he’s going to treat us to a big feast tonight.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;Hmph, you little gossip. Finish your work first.&quot;</p>

<p>Winslet called Luis into her office, then stretched lazily and said, &quot;That little gossip was talking about your master, wasn’t she?&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;We were just about to when you came back.&quot;</p>

<p>Luis lit a cigarette and sat down casually, grinning as he said, &quot;Teacher’s Wife, I’m curious too. What’s actually going on?&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;You’re such a busybody too! Why don’t you go ask your master!&quot;</p>

<p>Winslet shot him a charming, sidelong glance, then passed him an ashtray, sounding somewhat puzzled as she said, &quot;I’ve long known no man is any good, but even so, it gets annoying sometimes.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;My master can’t possibly be stirring up any major trouble, right!&quot;</p>

<p>Luis wore a look of concern.</p>

<p>&quot;Ugh, it’s so. I’ll tell you later.&quot;</p>

<p>Winslet glanced at the time and teased, &quot;My beloved disciple, it’s rare for your teacher’s wife to have a free evening. What grand feast are you going to treat me to?&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;How about seafood? Let me properly show my filial respect to you today.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;Great I’m going to fleece you thoroughly today. The disciple shall repay the master’s debt.&quot;</p>

<p>At six-oh-five, Hail appeared punctually at the law firm, still impeccably groomed, looking very refined, cultured, and dignified.</p>

<p>Upon sitting down, he first understood the case situation from the assistant. Without saying much more, he advised Luis, &quot;Luis, although it’s a small matter, you should have informed me about such minor troubles.&quot;</p>

<p>With that, he presented a business card to Winslet and said gently, &quot;You are the acting attorney, correct? This is my card. After my people have looked into the case details, they will communicate with you.&quot;</p>

<p>Hail spoke entirely in an official, businesslike tone. His powerful aura made Winslet feel somewhat pressured and also a bit puzzled.</p>

<p>Luis quickly explained, &quot;This is not a hired lawyer. It is my teacher’s wife, who is also a relative.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;Oh, oh, my mistake. I thought it was a hired, unlicensed lawyer. Apologies.&quot;</p>

<p>Hail immediately chuckled and said, &quot;It is even more convenient to communicate with one’s own people. Attorney Hail is truly young and accomplished. I will leave now and contact you later.&quot;</p>

<p>With that, he departed first. Winslet picked up the business card and saw only a name and phone number. Her delicate eyebrows instantly furrowed: &quot;An official??&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;Yes, the deputy mayor’s secretary.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;How do you know such a person??&quot;</p>

<p>What puzzled Winslet was that people of that sort appeared very approachable, yet in reality, they held themselves aloof, keeping others at a respectful distance.</p>

<p>Yet this Mr. Hail’s attitude toward Luis was exceptionally warm. It was not the professional demeanor one would display at work, but rather an expression suggesting a close personal friendship.</p>

<p>&quot;It is a long story, Master’s wife. Please do not inquire too deeply. I am not close with him, but one of my relatives is familiar with his superior.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;Oh, oh... Alright, then I will see what he has to say later.&quot;</p>

<p>As they spoke, the sky had already grown dark. The little assistant had plans to have dinner with her boyfriend that evening and left early. Luis got into his teacher’s wife’s car, and together they arrived at the Ocean Hotel.</p>

<p>The rooftop garden restaurant offered a view of the city’s dazzling night lights. The decor was luxurious and exceptionally exquisite, making it very suitable for couples to visit. Its most prominent feature, naturally, was its sheer expensiveness.</p>
