<p>Chloe was full of curiosity. &quot;Sister, so you got the vice president position you wanted. That should be a happy thing, right? No matter what, Luis helped you out.&quot;</p>

<p>Hela took a deep breath. Watching this scene play out before her eyes, she felt a twinge of sourness.</p>

<p>She thought to herself, maybe her willpower wasn&#x27;t as strong as her sister&#x27;s. She had been too guilty to go to the Central Garden today, afraid to face her in-laws and also afraid to face Luis&#x27;s wife.</p>

<p>Now, even meeting her daughter-in-law made her feel a bit uncomfortable, which was why she had specifically asked her sister to go in her place today.</p>

<p>But her sister was perfectly at ease, chatting and laughing with the man&#x27;s wife, building a pretty good relationship. Just based on that mental fortitude, Hela felt she had to concede defeat.</p>

<p>&quot;Yeah, he really did help a lot.&quot;</p>

<p>Taking this opportunity to clear the air with her sister was probably for the best. At least this way, if something suddenly happened, their sisterly bond wouldn&#x27;t be damaged over a man.</p>

<p>That bastard was a lecher, and there was nothing she could do about it. Whether his legal wife found out was a problem for another day.</p>

<p>Hela had come to a realization. The biggest source of risk for this wretched man, skillfully juggling a relationship between them, the biggest chance of them being caught in the act, actually came from her own sister, Chloe.</p>

<p>With this thought in mind, Hela took the initiative to speak. &quot;It wasn&#x27;t just handing over the evidence to keep me safe. He also worked behind the scenes the whole time, helping me find evidence to take down my competitor.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;Baby, that&#x27;s amazing!&quot; Chloe beamed with pride, hugging Luis and giving him a kiss.</p>

<p>Hela felt a little pang of jealousy, gritting her silver teeth as she said, &quot;Really. I was almost at my wit&#x27;s end back then, completely helpless against Kai. Honestly, I was mentally prepared to give up.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;At that point, I even started thinking it didn&#x27;t matter if I became vice president or not. Just being able to protect myself would have been the best outcome.&quot;</p>

<p>Chloe let out a surprised &quot;Wow,&quot; her face full of admiration. &quot;Sister, so that means Luis helped you twice.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;First, he happened to have evidence that could hurt you, and instead of giving it to the other side, he gave it to you, ensuring you were safe.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;Second, he helped you take down your competitor, letting you get the vice president position you wanted.&quot;</p>

<p>Chloe giggled. &quot;Sister, you must be really happy about your rapid promotion.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;You were under so much pressure back then, you looked completely worn out. Your endocrine system was so out of whack your period was a mess, or even stopped. You don&#x27;t know what you were like back then.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;You were like a powder keg, absolutely fierce and radiating killing intent, a real live shrew...&quot;</p>

<p>Hela couldn&#x27;t help but interrupt her, rolling her eyes. &quot;You&#x27;re quite eloquent, aren&#x27;t you? Where do you get all these weird descriptions from?&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;That&#x27;s because you don&#x27;t know. Just saying one word to you and you&#x27;d snap at someone. Everyone kept their distance back then.&quot;</p>

<p>Chloe playfully stuck out her tongue. &quot;Sister, you were like you wanted to eat people alive. Not like now, all glowing and radiant.&quot;</p>

<p>Hela was a bit stunned, thinking it over, as Chloe continued teasing, &quot;Sister, judging by this, it&#x27;s not an exaggeration to say my man saved your life.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;I guess so!&quot; Hela said with a wry smile.</p>

<p>That smile confused Chloe. Luis hugged her then, a wicked, mischievous grin on his face. &quot;Chloe, if someone saves your life, wouldn&#x27;t it be only right to offer your body in return?&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;Of course it would be right, it&#x27;s only natural...&quot;</p>

<p>Chloe stopped mid-sentence as realization dawned. She stared wide-eyed at her sister, then at her own man.</p>

<p>Her usually dignified sister, Hela, was just smiling helplessly, lowering her head to avoid her gaze, showing such a guilty look for the first time.</p>

<p>And when she looked at her man, his smile was lewd and evil, full of that familiar, obscene implication.</p>

<p>Chloe wasn&#x27;t stupid. Stunned, she pointed at her sister, then at her man. &quot;Sister... you... you two!!&quot;</p>

<p>Luis immediately grabbed her hand, kissing it gently. &quot;Your sister and I are adults. We were in the same boat, facing danger together. She was under immense pressure.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;You know her relationship with your brother-in-law isn&#x27;t good. So, some things just happened naturally.&quot;</p>

<p>Chloe was completely dumbfounded, her voice trembling. &quot;Sister... so... the man who... who made you glow like that... it was him...&quot;</p>

<p>Hela let out a long sigh. The secret was finally out. At this moment, she felt an immense sense of relief.</p>

<p>The two sisters fell silent simultaneously, their breaths quickening. Seeing that they weren&#x27;t angry, Luis lit a cigarette and started smoking.</p>

<p>He carefully observed their reactions. Confirming the situation was still under control, he said calmly, &quot;That&#x27;s right. Your sister was so depressed back then she could hardly cope. I guess it was also a way to vent pressure.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;Back then... why didn&#x27;t you tell me!&quot; Chloe suddenly yelled, her eyes reddening with unshed tears.</p>

<p>Hela didn&#x27;t know how to explain. Luis, however, was perfectly composed, gently holding her hand. &quot;How could I have told you? At that time, we had just gone through your husband&#x27;s ordeal.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;Well...&quot; Chloe couldn&#x27;t argue. Looking at the timeline, it was true.</p>

<p>Luis continued, &quot;Chloe, that&#x27;s why I say fate is strange sometimes. Meeting both you sisters were beautiful mistakes, right?&quot;</p>

<p>Chloe looked weakly at her sister, not knowing what to say for a moment.</p>

<p>In fact, her sister&#x27;s thoughts were probably similar to hers. Calculating the timing, both sisters believed it was a beautiful misunderstanding.</p>

<p>After all, neither of them believed in the existence of such an incredible thing as a System. Besides, they didn&#x27;t tell each other everything. Shame and reserve made them hide things from each other.</p>

<p>Her sister Hela certainly wouldn&#x27;t dare say she was threatened at first, trained like a slut, taken in every hole until she climaxed repeatedly, and only succumbed after being thoroughly fucked into submission.</p>

<p>Their psychology now was strange. They didn&#x27;t feel the need to confront Luis about this absurd situation of having both sisters. Not at all.</p>

<p>On the contrary, they were both worried that in a moment of excitement, this man might let something slip, revealing secrets that would make them lose all face. So, their attitude became strangely hesitant and timid.</p>

<p>Deep down, shame and tradition were at play. Their first reaction to this absurdity wasn&#x27;t anger at being fooled, because looking at the timeline, everything was coincidence, or fate.</p>

<p>All they felt was the awkwardness of not knowing how to face it, helplessness, and utter disorientation.</p>

<p>They were both panicked, silent, and without a word, they secretly glanced at the man they shared. Over this time, they had gotten used to seeing him as their pillar of strength.</p>
