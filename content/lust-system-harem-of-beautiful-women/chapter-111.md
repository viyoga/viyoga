<p>&quot;Besides, I can’t let her manage it. If she did, forget this car, she wouldn’t even let me buy that bracelet.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;I’m stingy enough, but Lily’s even worse. You know what your sister’s like.&quot;</p>

<p>By the time they finished talking, they were already home. Luis stretched again and said, &quot;Big Sister, I’m going to wash up first. Call Mom and Lily. Let’s just grab a quick bite downstairs tonight instead of cooking.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;Okay!&quot;</p>

<p>Even back home, Nancy still felt dazed. But the fact remained: she now had a hundred thousand-dollar Porsche Cayenne under her name. It was surreal.</p>

<p>Right after getting home, Nancy received a call from Teacher Blanche, who chuckled suggestively. &quot;Nancy, that 2,000 dollar kickback from your top-tier man, I’ll let you pass it to him.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;What makes him top-tier?&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;Pays for a hundred thousand-dollar luxury car without blinking, gets himself dirty for a bit of kickback, saves where he should, spends where he should. Isn’t that top-tier?&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;Nancy, you’re not a kid anymore. I know you’ve got high standards, but if a man like this wanted a mistress, I guarantee women would be lining up for him.&quot;</p>

<p>Nancy didn’t know how to explain anymore. Suddenly, a thought drifted through her mind: If only this car were mine...</p>

<p>&quot;This money usually comes in cash, by the way. I’ll treat you to dinner after work and hand it over then.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;How can I let you treat me, Teacher? I’ll cover it.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;Alright, see you then. You pick the place.&quot;</p>

<p>After hesitating for a moment, Nancy went upstairs and stopped outside the bathroom. Listening to the sound of running water, she softly called out, &quot;Brother-in-law, Teacher Blanche asked me to dinner to hand over that kickback. Do you want to come along?&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;Haha, she is reminding you that it is time to give her a gift to compensate her.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;Then, how much should I give her?&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;Two thousand is enough, but that is your teacher, and you cannot let her look down on you. Big sister, please wait a moment.&quot;</p>

<p>Nancy’s phone rang. She picked it up and saw a transfer message for 5,000 dollars.</p>

<p>You know, two thousand dollars might not sound like a lot, but for someone as desperate as her now, it was almost a huge sum of money.</p>

<p>&quot;Why is there so much?&quot;</p>

<p>Nancy accepted it without hesitation but still asked a question.</p>

<p>&quot;Just transfer two thousand to her. The rest will be considered Big Sister’s reward. I cannot let you pay for her dinner yourself.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;As for dinner, I will not go. You two can just catch up on old times.&quot;</p>

<p>It would be inconvenient for him to go himself. Anyway, this BUG level skill of knowing everything was like God’s vision. Luis completely trusted the big data information collected by the System.</p>

<p>Nancy also changed her clothes and went out. She first transferred two thousand to Blanche. As expected, Teacher Blanche was very polite and said it was too much. However, after some polite hesitation, she still accepted the money.</p>

<p>The meal was scheduled at the seafood restaurant in the Ocean Hotel. As soon as Blanche sat down, she asked with concern, &quot;How is it? Did you find the card?&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;Found it. Luckily it was not lost. Otherwise, it would be very troublesome to report it lost.&quot;</p>

<p>Nancy handed over the menu and said with a smile, &quot;Teacher, take a look at what to eat.&quot;</p>

<p>Blanche was not polite at all. She flipped through the menu and said with a smile, &quot;I am happy for you today. I will not be polite. Let us have a good meal to celebrate.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;Salt roasted crab, fish fin soup, and a serving of Momo.&quot;</p>

<p>With some roast meat and vegetables added, the price would be over a three hundred dollars, which was considered a normal consumption level here.</p>

<p>As soon as the dishes were served, Teacher Blanche smiled and said, &quot;Very good, Nancy. You are so charming. You really made me jealous today.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;Teacher, please stop teasing me.&quot;</p>

<p>Nancy shook her head. It was hard to put into words.</p>

<p>At this time, she was thinking how lucky her sister was. It would be great if this were her husband. Why did he not buy this car for her?</p>

<p>&quot;How dare I? Where did you find such a top notch man? He is so generous. Both of his cards had enough money for the full payment. Honestly, I finally understand why you cannot judge a person by their appearance.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;And let me tell you, if you buy a high end Porsche, you would probably have to pay in full. Otherwise, you would have to take out a mortgage and only have the down payment.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;I have been working here for half a year, and this is the first time I have seen someone pay full price for a Cayenne. They did not even blink an eye. What a rich guy!&quot;</p>

<p>Teacher Blanche blinked and smiled as she enjoyed the food, &quot;I wanted to tell you something today, but now it seems unnecessary.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;What is it?&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;Poke, including many of your classmates, when they find boyfriends, they buy a car and are satisfied with the down payment. But when they have to pay the car loan with their own money, they are all in a mess.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;I originally thought you would only give the down payment, so I wanted to remind you of this. If he does not pay off the car loan, I can find a way to help you get some cash from the car to save you unnecessary burden.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;Now it seems that I overthought it. You have found a truly rich man.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;Poke is relatively okay, but Fianna is even more foolish. She paid a 6,000 dollar down payment for a BMW 3 Series and slept with him for a year. When he kicked her out and she could not pay the loan, she did not even think of a solution. In the end, the car was auctioned off.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;Not a penny left. She was screwed for a year for nothing.&quot;</p>

<p>Teacher Blanche sighed earnestly and said, &quot;Do not blame me for being long winded. I have seen a lot of these things. If you are foolish and unworldly, you will easily lose both your money and yourself.&quot;</p>

<p>Fangfang was someone Nancy still vaguely remembered. She had posted photos of her BMW on her Whatsapp Add To Day for a while.</p>

<p>Nancy was quite envious at that time because the other person’s car registration was in her own name, while her second hand car had to be registered under two names.</p>

<p>&quot;Nancy, seize the opportunity. Even if you do not want a divorce, that is fine. We cannot let this kind of truly rich person get away.&quot;</p>

<p>Teacher Blanche smiled ambiguously and said, &quot;Given his conditions, if your classmates knew about this, it would be like sharks smelling blood.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;He is rich, generous, and willing to spend money on you. Is that not top notch?&quot;</p>

<p>After finishing the meal, she returned home in a daze. In the living room, Luis was lying on the sofa playing with his phone.</p>

<p>Nancy took off her shoes and asked, &quot;Where are Mom and Lily?&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;Walking downstairs. They said there was nothing in the refrigerator, so they went to buy some.&quot;</p>

<p>Nancy came to Luis and put down the envelope. Inside the envelope was 2,000 dollars in kickbacks. The finance department always gave cash.</p>

<p>Luis did not even glance at it, still playing with his phone and said, &quot;Just leave it there.&quot;</p>

<p>Nancy nodded, hesitated for a moment, and wanted to say something. In the end, she went back to her mother’s room without saying anything, took her clothes, and went to take a shower.</p>

<p>Her mind was in chaos. She did not know whether it was because the house was completely renewed, or because the living room now had air conditioning and she was not used to it, but the constant debt collection calls and messages every day had tortured her to the point of breakdown.</p>

<p>After showering and coming downstairs, the scene she saw made it even harder for her to conceal her jealousy.</p>

<p>Luis casually tossed the envelope containing 2,000 dollars in cash to Lily, who had just returned home. Lily, however, shook her head and refused, &quot;Husband, the 5,000 dollars you gave me last time hasn’t been spent yet. Why are you giving me so much money?&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;You’re pregnant now, you need to focus on nutrition. If you dare to be stingy again, I’ll teach you a lesson.&quot;</p>

<p>Knowing that her older sister had come down, and to further provoke her, Luis threw the envelope to his mother in law Ruth.</p>

<p>&quot;Mom, this is for household expenses. You take it.&quot;</p>

<p>Ruth blushed and said, &quot;The household allowance you gave last time is still there. You should give your money to Lily.&quot;</p>

<p>Calling it household expenses sounded better. Luck was a mysterious thing. Last time, when her son in law gave her money, she had cash in her pocket and felt confident. Recently, she had been on a lucky streak and hadn’t touched a single penny of the 2,000 dollars.</p>

<p>Although two thousand dollars was not a huge amount, the back and forth refusal seemed particularly ironic, because for Nancy now, two thousand was a lot of money.</p>

<p>Just then, Nancy walked over, feeling a pang of sadness, but jokingly said, &quot;There are actually people who refuse money. Your awareness is too high now.&quot;</p>

<p>Luis then casually tossed the envelope to Nancy and said with a laugh, &quot;Big Sister, then you keep this money.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;Don’t joke around, it’s not my turn to take it.&quot;</p>
