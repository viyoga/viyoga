<p>As Luis was speaking, the car gave another jolt.</p>

<p>Ruth looked back and noticed the tires of the car behind them, which had been rigidly still, were now rolling. She said in slight astonishment, &quot;Son-in-law, how is that car able to move? Wasn&#x27;t the handbrake already engaged?&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;The brake parts have snapped. With that level of quality, even a slight tug is too much force for them.&quot;</p>

<p>Luis spoke smugly as he drove, towing the car all the way to the entrance of the community. With a cold smirk, he pulled the car to a stop just outside the underground garage, got out, and untied the tow rope from the rear.</p>

<p>&quot;Parking it here means no one else can get out!&quot;</p>

<p>Ruth instantly understood what her son-in-law intended to do.</p>

<p>Luis chuckled darkly. &quot;That&#x27;s right. Since he wasn&#x27;t willing to come move his car, we can&#x27;t exactly let him move it easily now, can we?&quot;</p>

<p>As he spoke, a car pulled up. It was an apprentice from Fatty&#x27;s repair shop.</p>

<p>&quot;Thanks for the trouble!&quot;</p>

<p>The apprentice dropped off the supplies and left. Luis efficiently deflated all four tires of the battered car, then fastened locking wheel clamps onto them.</p>

<p>He then wrapped an iron chain around the axle and bearing area underneath. His movements were so swift and seamless that Ruth watched with sheer satisfaction.</p>

<p>&quot;All done!&quot;</p>

<p>To prevent the property management from pulling any more stunts, Luis drove his Mercedes over to a temporary parking spot in Phase Two, then circled back and headed home with his mother-in-law.</p>

<p>The property management company for Phase Two belonged to the Taiga family, so parking there was a safer bet.</p>

<p>It wasn&#x27;t the theft itself that was frightening, it was the fear of being targeted. Luis certainly wasn&#x27;t going to wait around for someone to come knocking. As soon as he got home, he told his mother-in-law to go shower first, then dialed Bella.</p>

<p>He got straight to the point. &quot;President Bella, don&#x27;t tell me you didn&#x27;t know what was going on with that property management company.&quot;</p>

<p>The Phase One property was under the Leon family&#x27;s control. There was no way Bella could claim ignorance, she was far too well-connected at a critical moment like this.</p>

<p>&quot;I only just found out,&quot; Bella said, her tone laced with resignation. &quot;It&#x27;s nominally our family&#x27;s asset, but in reality, Leon&#x27;s brother put his wife and her relatives in charge of it. So I don&#x27;t really have any say over what happens there.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;His wife?&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;Her name is Kiara. She manages the property company and also runs a small real estate agency in your neighborhood.&quot;</p>

<p>That clicked everything into place. Luis let out a cold laugh. &quot;President Bella, looks like you&#x27;re trying the old &#x27;kill with a borrowed knife&#x27; trick again. Leon&#x27;s brother&#x27;s got a hard-on for picking a fight with me, and you&#x27;ve been enjoying the show from the sidelines, haven&#x27;t you?&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;I&#x27;m being wronged here. That man&#x27;s brain is full of shit.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;You saw what happened that night. We&#x27;re in the middle of a fight over the inheritance now. You really think he&#x27;d listen to anything I say?&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;The more I try to talk him down, the more stubborn he gets, alright?&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;Speaking of borrowed knives, though... I&#x27;ve got a pretty good idea. Haven&#x27;t the property management teams for Phase One and Two been at each other&#x27;s throats lately?&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;Alright, alright. We&#x27;ll talk another time.&quot;</p>

<p>Luis rolled his eyes and hung up. Still, he knew he couldn&#x27;t let this slide. It wasn&#x27;t the crime that scared you, it was the obsession.</p>

<p>&quot;Hey... Don&#x27;t get cocky just because I&#x27;m being polite.&quot;</p>

<p>An unfamiliar number flashed on the screen. It was Leon&#x27;s brother. His voice was low and venomous. &quot;You think you can protect that bitch? Don&#x27;t make this your fight, or I&#x27;ll make sure your family doesn&#x27;t have a single peaceful day.&quot;</p>

<p>Luis mulled it over. He&#x27;d already arranged to meet Nina for drinks that night, he wanted to understand what was really going on, why Leon&#x27;s brother was coming after him with such obsessive rage.</p>

<p>Nina had agreed without hesitation. They decided to meet at Hans&#x27;s bar.</p>

<p>By the time Luis arrived, the bar wasn&#x27;t too crowded. At the small counter, Daniel was grinning ear to ear, deep in animated conversation with a young boy.</p>

<p>The boy looked like a freshly of-age college student. Fair-skinned, delicate features, classically handsome, the kind of boy-next-door with an irresistibly cute smile and little fangs when he laughed. Definitely a certified baby-faced heartthrob.</p>

<p>&quot;Luis, meet our future sales champion.&quot;</p>

<p>The moment Daniel saw Luis, a flicker of unease flashed across his face. He quickly sent the handsome young man off to take care of some work.</p>

<p>Luis raised an eyebrow. Could it be that Daniel had already crossed that line? He pulled up the Persona Tags and stared, stunned.</p>

<p>Cuckold. Green-hat enthusiast. Both tags were still there, unchanged, same old. Just the font was a little darker, that was all.</p>

<p>But they were completely overshadowed by the third tag: Homosexual. Not only did it have an absurdly bold golden background, but the font was thick and heavy, almost aggressively prominent, like it had fully manifested, saturated with completion.</p>

<p>This was probably the final stage of the Persona Tag system. Among the three, this one had been fully realized.</p>

<p>A purebred gay man. Throw him into a military barracks or a prison cell, and he&#x27;d swim like a fish in water. Those were dream environments for men like him.</p>

<p>And Hans&#x27;s bar? It was famous throughout the surrounding cities, even the capital knew of it as a premier gay hotspot.</p>

<p>Most of the clientele were wealthy, sophisticated, attractive men. Of course Daniel would lose control here. He couldn&#x27;t resist grazing on his own pasture.</p>

<p>He was thriving here. That was why the homosexual tag had completely activated. Chances were, he&#x27;d already slept with that boy.</p>

<p>&quot;Brother-in-law, business been okay the last couple of days? My sister hasn&#x27;t come by to help?&quot;</p>

<p>Luis suppressed his exasperation and sat down to chat.</p>

<p>Daniel shook his head. &quot;She went to get a facial with Blanche. They&#x27;ve been exhausted these past few days, they need the rest.&quot;</p>

<p>Daniel&#x27;s marketing strategy for the bar was simple: seduction as the core appeal. So his fairy-like sister-in-law, and married women like Chloe, would dress to kill and drop by.</p>

<p>Blanche did the same. Hell, Daniel even threw himself into it, once suggesting that Luis should dress a little sexier and come hang out too.</p>

<p>The whole point was to lure people in. That&#x27;s why the budget for entertaining Nancy and her college alumni had ballooned recently.</p>

<p>And then Nina arrived, gliding in like water. She wore a sleek black dress that made her look both delicate and devastatingly seductive. Her hair fell loosely, silver earrings catching the light and sparkling with every subtle turn.</p>

<p>The moment she walked in, all eyes were on her. The female regulars, the straight men nursing their drinks, everyone swallowed hard.</p>

<p>&quot;I&#x27;ll leave you two to talk.&quot;</p>

<p>Daniel&#x27;s gaze at Nina was unnervingly innocent. He smiled politely and excused himself.</p>

<p>Luis smiled and was about to order a drink when Nina took the initiative, looping her arm through his. Her voice was soft. &quot;I haven&#x27;t eaten yet. Will you take me to get something to eat?&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;Sure.&quot;</p>

<p>She was driving Bella&#x27;s ostentatious red Ferrari. Luis had always thought it suited Nina better, this was what a real beauty and a luxury car should look like.</p>

<p>Behind the wheel, her cheeks slightly flushed, Nina murmured, &quot;I told her you invited me out. Do you know what she said to me?&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;What?&quot;</p>

<p>Luis asked almost instinctively.</p>
