<p>Ruth stepped aside for a moment. The young couple wiped off the massage oil, got dressed, and went downstairs with her.</p>

<p>Normally, their card games ended at midnight, but today they finished a bit earlier because someone had to leave unexpectedly due to personal matters.</p>

<p>Originally, everyone would have just gone home, but now Yana had become unexpectedly generous.</p>

<p>In the past, she had lived rather frugally, but now that her household had a steady backbone, she was much better off financially. As soon as the game ended, she started calling out, insisting on treating everyone to a late-night snack.</p>

<p>As a newly minted landlady, Yana was a very responsible landlord. Even though that Northeast-style barbecue place was doing great business, she still made a point of going to show her support.</p>

<p>The tenants were so moved they could almost cry. Yana worried about them like a real mother hen.</p>

<p>At first, when they just opened, everything was chaotic, but now things ran smoothly and orderly.</p>

<p>Late-night snacks were a lively affair. A bunch of married women gathered around gossiping, while Luis and Lily sat obediently to the side, focusing on eating.</p>

<p>When the topic turned to the conflict with Old Peter, Lily’s mother was in high spirits, feeling proud and vindicated.</p>

<p>She drank one glass of wine after another, thoroughly delighted. The way she looked at her son-in-law was full of deep satisfaction and smugness.</p>

<p>On the surface, her husband Arthur was the one standing in front, but the truth was, it was Luis who had come up with the idea. Without her son-in-law, the property management wouldn’t have cooperated at all.</p>

<p>Poor Arthur had been used as a tool with no recognition whatsoever. Luis had stayed in the background handing out ideas, and that alone had made his mother-in-law’s heart nearly melt with joy.</p>

<p>&quot;Ah, I have to say, Yana is the lucky one!&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;Exactly. Her husband died so young... I’m so jealous I could die.&quot;</p>

<p>The conversation among these middle-aged women was absolutely wicked. In their eyes, Yana was the happiest of them all.</p>

<p>Her husband died early, so she didn’t have to take care of him. What incredible luck. Now she lived a carefree life and was doing pretty well.</p>

<p>&quot;Oh, come on, you guys. What’s there to be jealous about?&quot;</p>

<p>As Yana spoke, she secretly glanced at Luis. With her husband gone, having a suitable lover by her side was wonderful. Her only regret was little Lily, whom she had raised with her own hands.</p>

<p>But ever since she discovered that Lily had issues with her sexual orientation, that guilt and sense of wrongdoing had lightened considerably.</p>

<p>Occasionally letting Lily take advantage of her during breast massages or other times when she played dumb, in Yana’s eyes, was a form of compensation that made her feel better.</p>

<p>Late into the night, after eating and drinking their fill, everyone went home. Yana had left with some other women, so there was no chance to make a move.</p>

<p>They got home honestly, but as soon as Luis opened the door, he noticed something was wrong.</p>

<p>The floor was vibrating with a buzzing sound, like construction work downstairs with a power drill, but a drill couldn’t run that continuously and steadily.</p>

<p>&quot;What’s going on?&quot;</p>

<p>Ruth asked, her eyes hazy with drunkenness and her voice filled with confusion.</p>

<p>Luis closed the door behind him, thought for a moment, and said, &quot;It should be one of those ’building-shaking devices.’ You’ve probably seen them online.&quot;</p>

<p>Few people had actually seen one, but many had heard of them. The news usually reported satisfying outcomes. No wonder it felt so powerful, like the whole building was shaking.</p>

<p>&quot;Damn that old bastard.&quot;</p>

<p>Ruth couldn’t help but curse. Lily furrowed her delicate brows and said, &quot;How are we supposed to sleep like this? Mom, you should sleep upstairs tonight. Tomorrow we’ll figure out how to deal with this.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;I’ll come up after I shower!&quot;</p>

<p>Ruth cursed her bad luck and went to take a bath.</p>

<p>The young couple also took a romantic shower together. Afterward, Lily wore only her panties and a little camisole, while Luis sat in front of the computer in just a pair of boxers.</p>

<p>Ruth came upstairs drying her hair, wearing a sexy silk nightgown. Now she didn’t even bother with a bra. With every step she took, the sway of her cleavage had an utterly shocking impact.</p>

<p>Before Luis had moved in as a live-in son-in-law, the women in the house had worn even skimpier clothes than this. Now things were gradually getting back to that point.</p>

<p>Lily didn’t mind at all. Even if her husband looked at her mother with lustful eyes, she wasn’t jealous.</p>

<p>After all, at home, you wear whatever feels comfortable.</p>

<p>As a woman herself, she knew how uncomfortable it was to wear a bra in the summer. If your skin was sensitive, you could even get heat rashes. There was no need to be so restrained at home.</p>

<p>Since the women didn’t mind being looked at, Lily didn’t mind her husband looking. It wasn’t like he was losing anything by enjoying the view.</p>

<p>&quot;Minda’s really efficient, isn’t she?&quot;</p>

<p>Ruth stood beside Luis, watching the surveillance clips he had pulled up.</p>

<p>The fact that Minda had gone from being a mistress to a firmly established concubine proved she was no mere trophy wife.</p>

<p>After all, Dominic had had so many affairs that he probably couldn’t even remember how many women he’d slept with.</p>

<p>The events of the day were temporarily over, but Minda had taken the matter to heart.</p>

<p>She was clever and had people watching Old Peter’s movements. Within the community, setting up surveillance was no challenge for her.</p>

<p>The first clip she sent showed the hallway downstairs and the elevator entrance, now equipped with a high-definition camera that could also record audio.</p>

<p>In the footage, Old Peter’s apartment door stood wide open. The father and son were having a fierce argument.</p>

<p>Old Peter shouted, &quot;Who told you to pay the property management fee? Why spend that kind of ill-gotten money? Have some self-respect. What have I always taught you?&quot;</p>

<p>Pete, furious and frustrated, yelled back, &quot;If I don’t pay, we’ll end up in court! How will Lanta’s parents see me then?&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;You have a grudge with them, go fight with them yourself! Why drag her parents into it?&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;Just to save a little money, you had to start a fight right in front of them. You’ve completely disgraced us.&quot;</p>

<p>Old Peter was the type who never backed down, always aggressive even when wrong. He roared, &quot;So what? I didn’t do it on purpose.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;What kind of wealth do they have? How dare they look down on me? That dinner I treated them to cost over twenty dollars!&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;So what if I saved some card money? What’s the problem?&quot;</p>

<p>Father and son were locked in an explosive argument. Pete, absolutely livid, shouted, &quot;We’re about to get married! Are you doing this just to spite me?&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;Bullshit, it’s no big deal. Quit making a mountain out of a molehill.&quot;</p>

<p>Pete, utterly furious, said, &quot;Then what about my car? My boss needs the vehicle! I can make excuses for a day, but if I don’t pick him up tomorrow, he’ll get suspicious.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;If I lose my job, what do I do?&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;My boss has been so good to me. If he finds out the truth, I won’t be able to show my face.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;Bullshit! So you’ve got a wife and forgotten your father, huh?&quot;</p>

<p>Old Peter, always unreasonable, pointed a finger at Pete’s nose and cursed, &quot;If you lose your job, find another one. You’re a grown man with hands and feet. Even if you beg on the streets, you won’t starve.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;Stop with the nonsense, begging? How would Lanta and I get married like that?&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;Marry the rooster and follow the rooster. If she changes her mind over something like this, what good is a woman like that?&quot;</p>

<p>Watching the father and son argue, the kind-hearted Lily sighed and said, &quot;I kind of feel sorry for his son.&quot;</p>

<p>Luis said casually, &quot;Yeah, the harm of a toxic family is huge. When parents are shitty, the children are the ones who suffer.&quot;</p>

<p>Even Ruth’s heart softened a bit. She said, &quot;Son-in-law, I don’t think his son is that bad. If he were willing to admit his mistake, maybe we shouldn’t be too harsh.&quot;</p>

<p>Pete’s face turned green with rage as he cursed loudly, &quot;Enough! If you can’t help me, then don’t make things worse for me!&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;It took so much effort for Lanta’s family to finally accept me, and her father is willing to support our new family. Are you trying to destroy me?&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;If you’re not filial, why are you even alive? You’re going to turn against your own father for some worthless woman, aren’t you?&quot;</p>

<p>Luis agreed, then sneered, &quot;What a fucking idiot. He’s absolutely hated by gods and men alike!&quot;</p>
