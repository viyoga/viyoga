<p>Master Mav, with a face full of fierce and brutal features, was already seething with murderous intent. He rushed forward and hoisted Kai up like a chicken, angrily cursing, &quot;You bastard Kai, damn it, look at the mess you’ve made, keeping me in the dark for so long.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;Master Mav, what’s wrong?&quot;</p>

<p>Before Kai could finish speaking, he was slapped again. Master Mav, his eyes red, pulled out his phone and said, &quot;I always thought you were a decent man, but you’re nothing but a scoundrel, damn it...&quot;</p>

<p>The video was still set in his office, but this time there was no suggestive content.</p>

<p>A beautiful girl said anxiously, &quot;Husband, what do we do? If their family really finds out, I’m done for.&quot;</p>

<p>Kai walked up behind her and embraced her, comforting her, &quot;What’s there to be afraid of? Their family will be overjoyed, having such a big, healthy grandson.&quot;</p>

<p>The girl spoke in a tone that sounded on the verge of tears, &quot;But I don’t even know whose child it is...&quot;</p>

<p>Kai lifted her chin and kissed her, chuckling with obvious smugness, &quot;Don’t worry, the Mav family are all careless. Right now, they’re just thrilled to have a grandson.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;If it comes down to it, we can check a paternity test when the time is right...&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;You just gave birth, you should focus on resting properly. Even if you’re here to visit me, don’t stay too long.&quot;</p>

<p>On the surface, it seemed like concern, but in reality, it gave the impression that he had already grown tired of her.</p>

<p>Master Mav grabbed Kai and slapped him hard across the face, roaring furiously, &quot;My daughter-in-law is such a good woman, and you dared to take advantage of her? You’re going to pay for this, I’ll sue you...&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;I didn’t, Master Mav, please calm down.&quot;</p>

<p>Before Kai could finish, another burly man charged over, it was Master Mav’s son, the cuckolded husband.</p>

<p>He immediately tackled Kai to the ground, hitting him while weeping and cursing, &quot;You son of a bitch, bullying my wife just because she’s honest, I’ll kill you...&quot;</p>

<p>The scene descended into chaos. At the corner of the stairs, Hela watched everything unfold with a cold gaze, unable to help but marvel at how outrageously scandalous this bastard’s games were.</p>

<p>Once she opened those videos, she understood why the cuckolded husband was so enraged, it wasn’t just about the infidelity.</p>

<p>One video showed Master Mav’s daughter-in-law sitting nervously in the office, like a child who had done something wrong.</p>

<p>Kai held a few documents in his hand, shaking his head as he spoke, &quot;Niece-in-law, we’re not strangers here. I’ll be blunt, you’ve seen these reports. You were involved in running the cafeteria, so you should know whether what I’m saying is an exaggeration or not.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;Three hygiene failures, purchasing meat from unlicensed slaughterhouses, and those so-called homegrown vegetables from the countryside without any pesticide testing.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;There have already been two or three complaints about food safety. The main issue is that you know whether there’s a problem with the rice you’re using.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;Also, employees have spotted rats through the glass several times...&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;And your big barrel of dish soap is even worse. Not only is it an unlicensed product, but you got caught red-handed.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;Look at these yourself, how many complaints have come from within the hospital and from higher-ups?&quot;</p>

<p>The girl looked at them, trembling, and said, &quot;Uncle, I... we didn’t realize the problem was so serious. That dish soap comes from a major factory, it must be fine...&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;Fine? The contract isn’t just for show. This is a legitimate corporate cafeteria, not some roadside stall.&quot;</p>

<p>Kai rapped his knuckles on the table and sneered, &quot;Niece-in-law, your husband and his family might be simple folk, but you’re a proper university graduate. You should understand the severity of this.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;Protecting them won’t be easy, I might even end up in prison myself...&quot;</p>

<p>With that, Kai pounced on her and began tearing at her clothes as the girl struggled weakly in fear and panic.</p>

<p>The verbal threats combined with his forceful demeanor, a scene like this was something this inexperienced young woman had never faced before.</p>

<p>The video cut off abruptly at that point. The young husband, now wearing the horns of a cuckold, was naturally beside himself with rage, no longer caring who this so-called benefactor was.</p>

<p>The security team reacted swiftly, quickly arriving with riot gear to intervene. The incident caused a huge uproar, and someone even reported it to the police. The city hospital was thrown into disarray and unease that day.</p>

<p>To restore order, all parties involved were eventually taken away by the officers. The final outcome was unlikely to be determined anytime soon.</p>

<p>Having witnessed this entire farce, Hela was in an excellent mood.</p>

<p>Inside the office, the pretty assistant had prepared the freshly cleaned tea set. She closed the office door and locked it behind her.</p>

<p>The assistant wore a coy, charming smile and said, &quot;Director, this is perfect. With this commotion, Kai is definitely finished.&quot;</p>

<p>Although the two had been locked in a back-and-forth struggle during this period, overall, Hela had been the one at a disadvantage.</p>

<p>Naturally, her subordinates had also endured their fair share of humiliation along the way, so it was difficult not to feel a sense of schadenfreude now.</p>

<p>Most importantly, after today’s incident, even if Kai faced no legal consequences, the hospital would certainly punish him, possibly even dismiss him, to save face. At the very least, his career here was over.</p>

<p>Once Kai fell, Hela would rightfully become the number one Vice President.</p>

<p>Her swift rise meant her trusted allies would also benefit greatly. The assistant was already dreaming of a future where she could do as she pleased.</p>

<p>Even if they vary in age and specialization and might not know each other personally, once they enter the workplace, they become a cohesive unit, it’s almost like a culture of medical clans.</p>
