<p>Lily smiled gently at this time, stood up and poured tea for Daniel, and said softly:</p>

<p>&quot;Brother, my husband has low education but that does not mean he has no ability. Besides, university graduates are everywhere in society now.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;If we really need to find this type of job, nothing else, my women’s hospital is still hiring nursing assistants and security guards. The salary also starts at 4000 dollars. There should be no need for you to specifically ask for favors to find such a job.&quot;</p>

<p>After saying this, Lily sat down. On the surface she smiled very calmly, but in reality she was nervous to death.</p>

<p>For her, her mother, father, older sister, and even later her older sister’s husband were all absolutely authoritative figures.</p>

<p>Gathering the courage to say these words, she was actually panicking terribly, her palms were full of cold sweat. But after sitting down, when her husband held her hand, her face was full of tenderness, and she felt everything was worth it.</p>

<p>The young couple’s affection was visible to the eye. Ruth felt both comforted and full of guilt, but Nancy’s expression was a bit strange.</p>

<p>Daniel suddenly felt his status collapse. His authority in this family was also challenged. His mother in law was one thing, but even the usually unnoticeable Lily dared to challenge him???</p>

<p>&quot;Mom, where did you buy this bracelet?&quot;</p>

<p>Nancy suddenly noticed the bracelet worn on Ruth’s hand.</p>

<p>The Summer Cicada series gold bracelet was the most popular, all the rage for a while, even spreading to campuses. As a fashion enthusiast, Nancy could not have failed to notice.</p>

<p>In fact, Nancy also bought one, but it was not the style costing over thousands. She originally planned to take it out later to show off, but she was surprised to find that not only her mother, but even her second sister Lily had one on her hand.</p>

<p>Most importantly, just by looking at the thickness, it was definitely the most expensive model. Some gossipy women had shown off in front of her before, so she had long remembered it.</p>

<p>&quot;Luis bought it for me.&quot;</p>

<p>Ruth said with a proud smile: &quot;Not just this one, there is another one at home but i am too lazy to wear it.&quot;</p>

<p>Nancy naturally would not hold back. She grabbed her sister’s hand at once to take a closer look.</p>

<p>Daniel, who had just been suppressed by his mother in law until he could barely breathe, immediately found a new topic. He immediately said displeasedly: &quot;Luis, this is too much.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;Brother, how am I too much.&quot;</p>

<p>Knowing he was looking for an excuse to start something, Luis ate meat skewers and asked unhurriedly.</p>

<p>Daniel, seeing this as looking like a guilty conscience, immediately put on a stern face, adopting the air of an elder, and said:</p>

<p>&quot;Luis, this is not right. I know this series is popular now. Even if you want to please your wife and please Mom happy, you should grit your teeth, borrow a credit card, take out an online loan, and buy two entry level models.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;Two top tier ones add up to over seven thousand dollars. Where did you get that kind of money.&quot;</p>

<p>After saying this, Daniel turned to Ruth, both to curry favor and seemingly meaningfully said: &quot;Mom, I think you should not wear this bracelet. Wearing a fake out and getting exposed would be so embarrassing.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;What if you pass through some security equipment, and your bracelet gets stuck as soon as it goes through. That would be so humiliating. What difference is there from a gold necklace floating on water.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;By the way, Luis, where did you buy it? The workmanship looks very good. The gold plating process is indeed good.&quot;</p>

<p>Daniel’s face was already completely full of mocking smile. He said earnestly: &quot;Luis, if you have no money, then live a stable life. Do not play with these flashy and insubstantial things.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;Flashy my ass!&quot;</p>

<p>Ruth’s face darkened. She directly scolded: &quot;The invoice from the mall is right there. I know more people from the specialty store than you do. Do you suspect I am wearing a fake to show off?&quot;</p>

<p>Her scolding not only stunned Daniel, even Luis was a bit stunned. Seeing she was so confident, she probably even checked the authenticity of the invoice.</p>

<p>Luis narrowed his eyes and smiled, looking at his charming mother in law. Ruth lowered her head guiltily for the first time all night, not daring to meet Luis’s eyes.</p>

<p>&quot;Then it is even more wrong!&quot;</p>

<p>Daniel suddenly slammed the table, righteously said: &quot;Luis, what is going on with you? Your job earns just that little money, and now you have spent so much. How many times have I told you to be practical. You swiped those credit cards and online loans. Have you considered how to pay them back later?&quot;</p>

<p>Saying this, Daniel felt extremely comfortable. He had seen too many of this type of person, swiping credit cards and taking online loans for advance consumption.</p>

<p>Even he himself had those, but unlike others who ended up failing, when things blew up, someone else always cleaned up the mess for him. That was the benefit of leeching off the family.</p>

<p>&quot;How much did you rack up on things like credit cards and payday loans?&quot;</p>

<p>Daniel said with increasing confidence: &quot;Mom, even if this thing is real, after spending money to buy it, how to pay it back later? The interest is sitting there. Is it something he can afford?&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;When he cannot pay it back, you receiving collection calls every day will be even worse...&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;I tell you, repaying loans with loans only leads to a dead end. When he is buried in debt, it will drag your whole family down.&quot;</p>

<p>The more Daniel spoke, the more excited he became, completely unaware that his wife Nancy next to him looked slightly nervous.</p>

<p>According to normal southern etiquette, if smoking, at least offer him one.</p>

<p>But Luis lit a cigarette himself and smoked it. Smiling, he asked: &quot;Brother, if there is a problem at home, as the oldest brother, how much money would you contribute to help solve it?&quot;</p>

<p>Although Daniel was excited, he was not foolish and would not fall into this trap. He immediately said, &quot;You are the one who married into the family, why should I spend this money.&quot;</p>

<p>Although Daniel was excited, he was not foolish and would not fall into this trap. He immediately said, &quot;You are the one who married into the family, why should I spend this money.&quot;</p>

<p>Luis slowly laughed and said, &quot;You only realized that now? So when Father In Law is not at home, I am the head of the household. Why should you be the one to criticize and comment?&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;You...&quot;</p>

<p>Daniel was so angry that he slammed the table and directly pointed at Luis’s nose, saying, &quot;You’re just a live in son in law, what are you acting so arrogant for!!&quot;</p>

<p>Having endured so much humiliation, now that Mother In Law was on his side, and Nancy could be forgiven because of her long beautiful legs.</p>

<p>The problem was Daniel, you slick and oily guy, what right do you have? Luis did not want to endure his humiliation anymore and sneered, saying:</p>

<p>&quot;What’s wrong with being a live in son in law? You Daniel, are the only son in your family, but when you got married, it was quite shabby too. Was there any glory in that?&quot;</p>

<p>As soon as this was said, Nancy’s expression subtly turned displeased. It was true that the Wood Family parents were sharp and harsh, but they were reasonable and not selling their daughter. Seeing that the family was well matched and not poor, they did not have many demands when marrying off their eldest daughter.</p>

<p>There was no bride price and no dowry. They bought Nancy a bag, and the gold accessories added up to just over 4,000 dollars. At that time, Nancy did not think much about it, but after comparing with people around her, she felt she had married too shabbily. This was a sore point for her.</p>

<p>&quot;Speaking of which, after marrying my wife, I have spent much more money in the wife’s family than you, Young Master Daniel.&quot;</p>

<p>Luis said lightly, &quot;My money, how I want to spend it is my business. At least I spent it on my wife and the family.&quot;</p>

<p>Hearing this, Ruth seemed to have her fighting spirit awakened and immediately scolded, &quot;That’s right, Luis doesn’t hesitate at all to spend money on me and my daughter.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;He himself is reluctant to buy clothes that cost a few tens of dollars, but he didn’t even blink when buying gold bracelets for the two of us that cost over 3,000 dollars each.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;Daniel, you’re not young anymore. You’re married now but still not sensible, always spending money on things like game consoles, drinking, and singing. How can that do? People who live properly aren’t like this.&quot;</p>

<p>After this exchange, Daniel’s face turned green and pale.</p>
