<p>Seeing Luis dodge, Elara Ye held him even tighter, speaking in a fierce, threatening tone: &quot;Little brat, why are you dodging? You dare to look down on your big sister?!&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;You’ve grown up and got some nerve, I think you’re asking for a beating.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;Getting married means forgetting your roots, huh? I’ll beat the crap out of you later, believe it or not.&quot;</p>

<p>Carter Peng, however, remained calm and said with a smile, &quot;Uncle really should have introduced Luis to me earlier. Come to think of it, I’ve never seen him at any family gatherings over the years.&quot;</p>

<p>If this illegitimate son identity had come from Elara Ye’s own mouth, he might have suspected she was covering for a lover.</p>

<p>After all, this was a political marriage, their relationship so lukewarm that they’d agreed they could each do their own thing, but on the surface, they absolutely had to maintain a perfect image.</p>

<p>But in the end, what man could truly accept that in his heart? Knowing about an affair was one thing, but seeing it with his own eyes was entirely another.</p>

<p>The reason he was so composed was because it came from his uncle Sebastian Ye.</p>

<p>That night when they met, the loving look in that father’s eyes for his son couldn’t be faked. As a man, he fully trusted his instincts.</p>

<p>Besides, as a distinguished elder and someone of high status, his uncle was respected by everyone. He wouldn’t fabricate such a fact to tarnish his own reputation and that of the family.</p>

<p>Given Sebastian Ye’s credibility, there could be no doubt about this illegitimate son’s identity, and no one would dare to deceive the Ye family.</p>

<p>Carter Peng figured that these two siblings had known each other since childhood, and had simply grown a bit distant after not seeing each other for a long time. Now that they were living together, they were regaining their former closeness.</p>

<p>The reason he’d never known was that for a family like theirs, an illegitimate son was also something that couldn’t be brought into the open.</p>

<p>For the sake of reputation and future prospects, they’d remained tight-lipped, and no one had ever mentioned it to him.</p>

<p>So seeing this man and woman dressed like that together didn’t make Carter Peng think twice. After all, they were siblings.</p>

<p>Just as Carter Peng finished speaking, Elara Ye shot him a glare.</p>

<p>Carter Peng realized he’d misspoken. That was a classic case of saying exactly the wrong thing, how awkward.</p>

<p>Elara Ye immediately signaled to him and said, &quot;Uncle said that this New Year, he’ll probably bring Luis back to stay for a couple of days.&quot;</p>

<p>Since Luis’s surname was Xu, they could bring him back and introduce him as a godson. After all, these people were somewhat superstitious, and finding someone with a compatible birth chart wasn’t unusual.</p>

<p>&quot;Ah, that’s wonderful news!&quot;</p>

<p>A man like Carter Peng would never let embarrassment show on his face. He immediately laughed and said cheerfully.</p>

<p>Luis smiled and said, &quot;It’s not set in stone yet. That guy’s so busy he doesn’t even know if he’ll have the time himself.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;Let us know once you’ve settled on a date for your trip back!&quot;</p>

<p>Carter Peng complained, &quot;Your family can really hold their liquor. Usually, during work, nobody touches a drop, but when it comes to holiday gatherings, every single one of them...&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;Oh, so marrying into my family is such a hardship for you?&quot;</p>

<p>Elara Ye rolled her eyes at him.</p>

<p>Carter Peng laughed heartily and said, &quot;Which son-in-law of your family hasn’t been puking their guts out? Which one hasn’t gotten dead drunk?&quot;</p>

<p>Elara Ye was the sole exception, a complete female alcoholic.</p>

<p>As they were talking, Carter Peng’s phone rang. He answered it, then stood up and said, &quot;I’ll head out first. Can’t be late for a drink with your boss.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;Thanks for your hard work!&quot;</p>

<p>Elara Ye was in a great mood and saw him out the door.</p>

<p>Once she sat back down, she opened another bottle, raised it with a grin, and said, &quot;Come on, Luis, you’ve worked hard too. Now that the mole’s been caught, that’s one less thing to worry about.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;Not hard at all. It’s my honor to take care of my big sister.&quot;</p>

<p>Luis clinked his bottle with hers.</p>

<p>Before they knew it, they’d polished off an entire case of twenty-four small bottles of bottled Budweiser.</p>

<p>The female alcoholic Elara Ye still wanted more. She grabbed a few more bottles from the fridge and said, &quot;Oh, by the way, there’s something I need to tell you. You don’t need to keep following Xeno anymore.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;Don’t follow him anymore?&quot;</p>

<p>Luis asked, a bit surprised.</p>

<p>Elara Ye said, &quot;There’s a TV drama our station is investing in. He’s going to visit the set tomorrow. His plane ticket for noon tomorrow is already booked.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;For the time being, we all need to keep a low profile. We can’t make any rash moves.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;This tacit understanding is pretty much reached. Besides, in such a short time, we probably won’t be able to dig up any more useful dirt.&quot;</p>

<p>At that, Elara Ye sighed, &quot;In the end, we’ll just have to let fate decide. At least your brother-in-law helped out, so we should be looking at a fifty-fifty chance.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;Who’ll end up on top? Even we don’t have the confidence or certainty anymore.&quot;</p>

<p>Luis narrowed his eyes and asked softly, &quot;Sis, what if something happened to Xeno and he was no longer fit for work? What if he needed a break?&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;What kind of accident?&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;Like a car crash, needing to be hospitalized for surgery and unable to work for a short time.&quot;</p>

<p>After Luis finished speaking in a low voice, Elara Ye was stunned at first, then said anxiously, &quot;Luis, don’t do anything rash! If something happened to you, I couldn’t face Uncle.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;And going to such extremes, if it got found out, it would cause a lot of trouble. Xeno’s family isn’t exactly a pushover either.&quot;</p>

<p>Seeing her flustered for the first time, Luis laughed and said, &quot;Sis, I was just joking. There’s no way I’d drive my car into him myself. I’m not that crazy.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;Don’t scare me like that!&quot;</p>

<p>Elara Ye said with a coquettish pout.</p>

<p>Her hair was slightly disheveled, her face flushed with drink, and her beautiful eyes held a hint of intoxication. She looked utterly captivating.</p>

<p>&quot;Don’t worry, big sis. I have my own methods. I can guarantee you’ll win this time for sure.&quot;</p>

<p>Luis declared confidently.</p>

<p>&quot;Alright, alright, as long as you don’t do anything reckless, I’m relieved. I believe you.&quot;</p>

<p>Elara Ye treated it as a joke.</p>

<p>&quot;You don’t have to clean up after you finish. The cleaning lady will come tomorrow afternoon to take care of it. My head’s a bit dizzy, I’m going to bed.&quot;</p>

<p>After saying that, Elara Ye swayed slightly, yawned, rubbed her eyes, and went back to her room.</p>

<p>Watching her graceful figure, Luis thought to himself, damn it, just when things were finally getting a little intimate that evening, the brother-in-law had to come back and ruin everything. At this rate, when would he ever complete his mission?</p>

<p>Damn... looks like he’d have to rely on his acting skills. Luis opened his phone and, using the information she’d provided, booked a first-class seat on the same flight as that Xeno.</p>
