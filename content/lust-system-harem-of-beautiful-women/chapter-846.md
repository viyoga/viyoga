<p>For Elara Ye, she only cared about two things: first, the extent of Xeno’s injuries, and second, whether Luis had covered his tracks perfectly, leaving no loose ends.</p>

<p>From a selfish human perspective, as long as it didn’t implicate her, there was nothing to worry about.</p>

<p>But ultimately, she had to consider her uncle’s side of things, from a purely interest-based standpoint.</p>

<p>And on a personal emotional level, after learning about this, she was utterly shaken. She never expected that this newly acquainted younger brother would be willing to take such a huge risk for her.</p>

<p>In the eyes of a normal person, if this wasn’t an accident but a meticulously planned conspiracy, it would be terrifying.</p>

<p>Thinking it over calmly, how did he bribe the driver, and how could he ensure the driver wouldn’t slip up under police interrogation?</p>

<p>The source of the car, and Xeno’s whereabouts—he’d have to have an informant within the crew. Was that informant trustworthy?</p>

<p>Elara Ye thought it through and realized that she herself wouldn’t have the courage to do something like this. She wasn’t confident she could make it flawless.</p>

<p>Even though children of powerful families had a certain pride, they also saw and knew more. Generally, those who’d seen the world wouldn’t be so arrogant.</p>

<p>That’s why she was so worried. The most important factor was that this wasn’t the Ye family’s traditional territory.</p>

<p>If there were any clues, they couldn’t easily intervene. So she kept thinking about trying to find connections over there, to use the guise of concern to gather more information and be ready for any unexpected developments.</p>

<p>&quot;When are you coming over!!&quot;</p>
