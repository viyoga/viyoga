<p>The young boy, eyes red with rage, grabbed a bookshelf off the desk and hurled it at him.</p>

<p>Luis dodged to the side, and Harvey Zhang, already beaten to a pulp, took the hit instead. He grunted and collapsed to the floor, unable to get up.</p>

<p>&quot;Brother-in-law...&quot;</p>

<p>Leah was terrified too. After a moment of stunned silence, she tried to rush in and help.</p>

<p>Though his sister-in-law was usually well-behaved, she was going through a rebellious phase and was the most restless one in the family.</p>

<p>She grabbed a pair of scissors from the table and lunged at the boy. Luis quickly wrapped his arms around her waist, holding her back, even as the boy grabbed a heavy book and slammed it into his back.</p>

<p>&quot;Be good. Just call the police. Let your brother-in-law handle this.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;Don’t cry now...&quot;</p>

<p>He carried her outside the door and gave her a gentle, loving smile.</p>

<p>&quot;Ah...&quot;</p>

<p>Leah was so startled she forgot to cry. Her shriek of surprise drew attention from others.</p>

<p>The boy grabbed a stool and brought it down hard on Luis’s back. Luis winced in pain but smiled at her and gave her a gentle push.</p>

<p>As soon as the back door closed, his expression turned ferocious, and his voice was filled with rage: &quot;You little punk! I was already throwing hands while you were still playing in the mud! You dare to sneak-attack me...&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;Ah... no!!!&quot;</p>

<p>Soon, the only sounds were the screams of the boy and his mother.</p>

<p>&quot;Fuck you! You come here spewing shit? You really think I’m some pushover?!&quot;</p>

<p>Leah dialed the police with one hand while pounding on the door with the other, crying, &quot;Brother-in-law! Brother-in-law! Open the door! Let me help you!&quot;</p>

<p>Right now, she could only think about the fact that there were three people on the other side. What if her brother-in-law got hurt? And that hit to his back had been solid.</p>

<p>Screams continued to echo from the office. By the time security realized something was wrong and several guards rushed over with riot gear, a patrol officer also arrived. They had gotten there very quickly.</p>

<p>The commotion was so loud that nearly every student knew about it.</p>

<p>The principal was naturally alerted as well. When they finally opened the door, they all swallowed hard.</p>

<p>Luis was sitting in a chair, smoking, looking utterly defiant. On the surface, he didn’t have a scratch on him.</p>

<p>No surprise there—given his physique, Luis was tall and muscular, built to take down his opponents as easily as chopping vegetables.</p>

<p>On top of that, he had a combat specialty skill unlocked. Not only could he handle these three weaklings, he could go up against special forces without breaking a sweat.</p>

<p>&quot;Brother-in-law! Are you okay?!&quot;</p>

<p>Leah was crying her eyes out. She rushed to him, checking him all over, completely ignoring the strange looks from everyone else.</p>

<p>The principal of No. 1 High School was a middle-aged man surnamed Ma. Principal Ma looked at the office, which looked like a tornado had hit it, and his face turned dark.</p>

<p>He immediately ordered the teachers to take all students back to their classrooms and pull the curtains so no one could watch any longer.</p>

<p>Then he turned and said with a smile, &quot;Officer, let’s send them to the hospital next door first. Since this happened on school grounds, if it’s convenient, we can mediate here in a little while. How about that?&quot;</p>

<p>Clearly, Principal Ma didn’t want to blow things up. After all, No. 1 High School was the best in the city, and he wouldn’t tolerate any damage to its reputation.</p>

<p>Gasle glanced at him and nodded, saying, &quot;That works. A peaceful resolution would be best.&quot;</p>

<p>The reason he’d arrived so quickly was that Luis had started feeling uneasy on the way and had called him in advance. Gasle hadn’t hesitated and had come straight over with his men.</p>

<p>It took about an hour to go back and forth. By then, the three of them had their wounds treated and had been examined.</p>

<p>Director Harvey Zhang had been beaten into a pulp. He had multiple cuts on his lips, two missing teeth, soft tissue damage across his body, and a neck sprain.</p>

<p>He was the most pitiful because he couldn’t even tell what injuries were from Luis and what were from the bookshelf the boy had thrown.</p>

<p>The boy, Zion, was also beaten badly, his face as swollen as Harvey’s. He was black and blue, had a broken rib, and multiple fractures.</p>

<p>Luis had done it on purpose, targeting his right hand in particular. He’d broken several bones there as punishment for the boy’s wandering hands.</p>

<p>His fat mother wasn’t doing much better either—her face was swollen too, making her already ugly appearance even worse.</p>

<p>In the principal’s office, the air was thick with smoke as soon as the door opened.</p>

<p>Gasle lit a cigarette, and Luis followed suit without hesitation. Principal Ma didn’t make a fuss about it, even though he didn’t smoke himself. He had someone bring him an ashtray.</p>

<p>Having witnessed Luis’s brutality, the three of them entered without daring to look up, too afraid to even make eye contact with him.</p>

<p>Leah sat quietly beside him, but her expression toward them was fierce and angry.</p>

<p>As soon as they came in, Principal Ma’s face darkened. He turned his laptop screen around and said angrily, &quot;Director Zhang, Zion, Zion’s mother, take a look at this yourselves.&quot;</p>

<p>Modern surveillance cameras were high-definition, capable of clearly identifying faces.</p>

<p>Whether for easier management or to shift blame, everything had been captured clearly.</p>

<p>The footage showed them in the cafeteria. Leah, Avery, and a few other classmates were laughing and talking as they waited in line. Suddenly, Zion walked past, said something with a lecherous look on his face.</p>

<p>Leah seemed to curse at him angrily, but Zion just shrugged it off.</p>

<p>Just as he was passing by, he suddenly turned around and reached out to smack Leah on the butt.</p>

<p>Leah shrieked and turned to kick him. But Zion responded with a backhanded slap that sent her stumbling to the ground.</p>

<p>The scratches on his face came from Avery and another girl who had jumped in to grab him. If they hadn’t stopped him, it looked like Zion would have kept hitting Leah.</p>
