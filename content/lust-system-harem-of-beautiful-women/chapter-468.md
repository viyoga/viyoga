<p>As evening fell, Luis slowly arrived at the bar with his wife. The backyard of the bar was already bustling with activity.</p>

<p>The barbecue restaurant on the third floor, which served late-night snacks, was free during dinner time, making it the natural venue for tonight’s banquet.</p>

<p>Father-in-law Arthur and Darlan shared a common trait: they were both traditional, rigid, and deeply concerned with face. With their son and son-in-law launching such a significant business, asking them to keep quiet and low-key would be worse than killing them.</p>

<p>So, after Old Bard and Arthur discussed it, they decided they should at least host a banquet to invite close relatives and friends.</p>

<p>Even if it wasn’t a grand celebration, it had to be somewhat presentable. They wouldn’t accept monetary gifts; it was just about treating everyone to a lively gathering.</p>

<p>Truthfully, Daniel and his ethereal sister-in-law were rather reluctant. They had been utterly exhausted during the opening period, struggling to even find a moment for their clandestine affairs with the brother-in-law.</p>

<p>Luis wasn’t particularly enthusiastic about it either, but with the two elders in such high spirits, he couldn’t bring himself to dampen their mood.</p>

<p>They hired a local cook, decorated the venue a bit, and as night descended, the banquet commenced in a lively fashion.</p>

<p>Mother-in-law Ruth was also very diligent. After all, her two sons-in-law were the bosses now, and not showing off the family business simply wasn’t her style.</p>

<p>Yana and her daughter, along with several close card partners, were invited, including her younger sister Eloise and her family.</p>

<p>At the main table where the men sat, Arthur and Darlan were overjoyed, raising their glasses and toasting everyone, running around more energetically than they had on their own wedding days.</p>

<p>Daniel and Luis also had a tough time, especially in managing the mood-spoiling presence of Uncle Logan. The difficulty of that task was unimaginable.</p>

<p>Before long, Luis’s face was flushed red from drinking. He felt somewhat better only after washing his face with ice water.</p>

<p>&quot;Why are you drinking so hurriedly? You haven’t eaten anything. Drink this bowl of soup first.&quot;</p>

<p>The mother-in-law doted on her son-in-law, especially this second son-in-law. The affair was one thing, but what mattered most was this son-in-law’s filial piety.</p>

<p>Ruth grabbed Luis and pulled him over to the women’s table. Luis obediently drank the abalone and stone olive soup, glancing up briefly but not daring to utter a word.</p>

<p>This table filled Luis with both pride and anxiety, fearing he might accidentally ignite some spark.</p>

<p>The two sisters, his mother-in-law and her sister, his wife and her older sister Lily, Hela and Chloe who came carrying her child, plus Yana—all of them had been intimate with him...</p>

<p>&quot;So adorable. When I have a daughter in the future, I want her to be this beautiful too.&quot;</p>

<p>Most troubling was that his wife Lily and Chloe were chatting and laughing merrily, hitting it off instantly, and even exchanged contact information.</p>

<p>Not only did his sister-in-law Nancy look rather strange, but Luis himself was a bit worried. If sparks were to fly, he would undoubtedly be the first to perish.</p>

<p>Blanche and her daughter, along with Maria, also came, sitting with Ariana and the others to drink juice.</p>

<p>Close relatives and friends from both sides had also arrived. Luis glanced around and saw Brook and her group there too. Hela’s trusted aides from the hospital had also taken a table.</p>

<p>After greeting everyone around, Luis returned to the main table. It was then that Arthur took out a list and said, &quot;Son-in-law, your father is counting on you this time.&quot;</p>

<p>The list included over a hundred cases of mineral water and over a hundred cases of locally produced, somewhat well-known soda water.</p>

<p>&quot;My dear in-law, when did you change your line of work?&quot;</p>

<p>Darlan asked with a laugh.</p>

<p>&quot;Ah, don’t mock me. Making money these days is harder than eating shit. The payment for last year’s project still hasn’t come through. It wasn’t until this year that I got these items to offset part of the debt.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;Just this batch alone offset 100,000 dollars for me.&quot;</p>

<p>Daniel nearly spat out his soup. After coughing a few times, he widened his eyes and said, &quot;Dad, were you cheated? These two items together are worth at most 20,000 dollars.&quot;</p>

<p>It was true he used to be idle and live off his parents, but now he was bursting with ambition. A quick mental calculation told him exactly how much these things were worth.</p>

<p>Arthur sighed, &quot;What choice do I have? Whatever I can recover, I will. If I don’t accept this, once the project department shuts down, I won’t even know who to look for.&quot;</p>

<p>Daniel couldn’t bring himself to refuse. He said, &quot;Dad, have someone bring the stuff over in the next few days. Just hand it over to Auntie.&quot;</p>

<p>After a satisfying meal where everyone ate and drank their fill, the old-fashioned ones, like Logan, left first.</p>

<p>Those who were interested stayed to tour the bar and have another drink. It wasn’t until past ten, when more customers started arriving, that the army of relatives finally withdrew.</p>

<p>Mother-in-law and his wife Lily also left first, mainly because the atmosphere here was too normal, which left his yearning wife quite disappointed.</p>

<p>In the bar’s backyard, father-in-law Arthur pressed his car key, and the lights of a black Mercedes-Benz C-Class flashed.</p>

<p>Sister-in-law Nancy said with a charming smile, &quot;Not bad, Dad. You finally gave up on that old relic. This car looks quite nice.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;The car’s condition is good, but it’s ridiculously expensive.&quot;</p>

<p>Arthur gave a bitter smile.</p>

<p>He then explained the real purpose of his visit. The mineral water and soda were trivial matters.</p>

<p>This Mercedes was what truly gave him a headache. It was also part of last year’s project payment, used to offset a debt of over 70,000 dollars.</p>

<p>&quot;Over 70,000 dollars.&quot;</p>

<p>Luis was dumbfounded. &quot;Even if this car were brand new from a Store, the total cost for a fully loaded model with all fees wouldn’t reach 50,000 dollars.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;Right. This car is three years old, and these things depreciate terribly.&quot;</p>

<p>Arthur said, on the verge of tears, &quot;I’ve asked around everywhere. The highest offer I got was only 20,000 dollars. Selling it at that price would mean a total loss for me.&quot;</p>

<p>Moreover, he needed funds to keep his operations running. Given last year’s several hundred thousand dollars in unpaid debts, if this trend continued, his construction company would likely go bankrupt this year.</p>

<p>In the end, Luis took over the Mercedes for 40,000 dollars. Of course, this had to be kept secret from mother-in-law Ruth. If she found out, Arthur would definitely face severe consequences.</p>

<p>The weekend passed quickly. His wife and mother-in-law returned to the capital to continue their studies. Once his sister-in-law and the others went back to school, it became exceptionally quiet.</p>

<p>Luis spent a day with his wife in the capital before slowly returning to the city to prepare for the next phase of their plan.</p>

<p>On Monday morning, Luis first went to his teacher’s wife Winslet’s office. The items here were almost all packed up.</p>

<p>Inside the office, Winslet massaged her own shoulders and said, &quot;My body really feels worse with each passing year. Just this little bit of work leaves me sore and aching.&quot;</p>

<p>Luis walked straight behind her and placed both hands on her shoulders.</p>

<p>Winslet stiffened all over, her face slightly flushed. She said, &quot;Why are you being so attentive now? It’s your teacher’s wife who should be trying to please you.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;Hehe, it’s only right to be filial to my teacher’s wife.&quot;</p>

<p>Luis began massaging her shoulders and asked, &quot;Teacher’s wife, is it comfortable?&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;Comfortable!&quot;</p>

<p>Winslet closed her eyes, enjoying the moment, a slightly shy smile on her face.</p>
